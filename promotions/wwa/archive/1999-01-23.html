WWA — 23 Jan 1999

23 Jan 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saturday January 23,1999 The screen opens up to the WWA banner. The camera shows the outside of the complex, revealing it to be the Bryce Jordan Center. Yellow and red pyrotechnics explode down the ramp and out of the four ringposts. The WWA banner fades in again and is instantly stamped with the word "Extreme"! The screen fades into the arena where thousands of fans are jumping up and down and screaming. As the camera pans over the audience signs such as "New Star = X-treme", "Flynn's freaks are nothing but Geeks", and "RAVAGE RULES". The camera makes it's way to the announcers table where Eric Pembrook, Juan Guevesos, and John Douglass are sitting. Eric : Hello, and welcome ladies and gentleman to another exciting edition of Saturday Night Extreme! I'm your host Eric Pembrook and with me as always are Juan Guevesos and John Douglass. John : By the way folks, this show is brought to you by an extreme specialist himself, Lawrence Mack. Big Larry promises not to take anything away from the show and hopes to only make it better, and it definetly looks that way with the great match-ups he's given us tonight. Juan : That's right, John. Tonight we're going to see ex-heavyweight world champ Randy Cross trying to gain back some gold for himself and the HOP. Eric : We will also see the young superstar, Kristopher Kasanova, in action against Zack Crockett. John : Top that off with an extreme title bout and a very promising newcomer and you've got yourself a great show! Eric : With that, let's get to the first match of the evening pitting the newcomer Lantern against Annihilation.... John : Didn't Mr. Mack promise us good matches. This is going to be a doozy, jeesh! Juan : Alright, let's go to Gary Trudeau with the introductions. The camera flashes to the ring where Gary Trudeau is standing armed with his microphone. Gary Trudeau : Welcome Ladies and Gentlemen to the Bryce Jordan Center. The first match of the evening is set for one fall and has a twenty minute time limit. First, making his way to the ring, he stands for everything that is right and just. He hails from Calgary...he is Lantern!!! Lantern walks out to "Indestructable" by Matthew Good Band. As he walks to the crowd many cheer for him. He rolls into the ring and takes off his trenchcoat. Gary Trudeau :And his challenger, standing 7-4 and weighing 405 pounds, from Chicago..we have Annihilation!!! The sound of three gongs is heard over the PA system as Annihilation enters the arena. He's wearing a "CPE sucks" shirt and he walks confidently towards the ring. Leonard follows him and begins handing out t-shirts and beer to the fans. Eric : Lantern wasting no time getting this match started...Off the opposite ropes with Annihilation still on the outside. John : Through the ropes with a sliding kick, knocking Annihilation on his back. Juan : Lantern is going to have to pull off some nice moves like that if he wants to win this match, Annihilation out-weighs him by almost 200 pounds! John : You can say that again, we don't know much about Lantern here tonight, but we do know that Annihilation has proven himself rather well in the WWA. Eric : Lantern up on his feet quickly and stomping down on the big guy's chest. Juan : Lantern picks Annihilation up by his shoulders and dropkicks him over the steel steps! John : Lantern back up on his feet and on the attack. Eric : Running start, jumps off the steps towards the fallen Annihilation! Juan : Ouch! Annihilation lifted his knees upon impact, they went straight into Lantern's chest. Eric : Annihilation now up to his feet. Picks Lantern up by his hair and drops him right on his back. John : Has the bell even rang yet guys?! This match hasn't even started! Juan : I think that Annihilation is sensing that, he just made his way into the ring. Eric : Lantern is now stirring around....He too is making his way into the ring. John : Both wrestlers are in the ring, the ref signals for the bell. Eric : And this one in now officially underway... Juan : Lantern off of the ropes and attempts a clothesline on Annihilation. Didn't even move the big guy! Eric : Lantern again off of the ropes, but Annihilation with a clothesline of his own this time! John : That clothesline should wake the rookie up. Lantern's down on his back. Eric : Annihilation slowly over to Lantern and Lantern with a Russian leg sweep. Juan : Lantern wasting no time, he picks up Annihilation's right leg and slams the knee down hard into the mat. Eric : Lantern picking the leg up again and driving it back down into the ring. John : It looks like this Lantern guy isn't that dumb of a character after all! Juan : Annihilation up on his feet now, he is favoring that right knee. Eric : They lock up in the middle of the ring, Annihilation easily taking advantage, has Lantern in a side-headlock. John : Annihilation with a suplex on Lantern. Juan : Lantern slow to get up, probably should have stayed down, a big boot to the face of Lantern by Annihilation. Eric : Annihilation picks Lantern up over his head into a guerilla press and tosses him over the top rope! John : I thought that we were going to see the crippler there for a second... Juan : Annihilation to the outside. What's he doing?! He just took Gary Trudeau's chair! Eric : Annihilation running at Lantern with the chair, Lantern sees this coming and avoids the chair shot. Juan : Lantern with a hard punch to the back of Annihilation's head, as he was stooped over after that whiff. John : Lantern now on the right of Annihilation, grabs his head....Running bulldog on the chair! Juan : Annihilation's face just drilled that chair! He's out! Eric : Lantern's not done just yet. He picks the chair up and goes up on the ring apron. John : Lantern tucks the chair under his left arm, running start, connects the chair with Annihilation's right knee! Juan : Lantern is really doing a number on Annihilation's knee! Eric : Annihilation now sitting up, grasping his right knee in pain. John : Lantern tries to kick Annihilation's knee again, but his foot was caught by Annihilation. Juan : Instead, Lantern kicks Annihilation right in the face with a fallaway type kick! Beautiful! John : Lantern now picking up the chair and throwing it into the ring. Juan : Lantern somehow gets Annihilation on his feet and kicks him into the ring. Eric : Annihilation dazed just got drop kicked into the turnbuckle and is hanging all over it. John : Lantern positions the chair where he wants it and walks over to Annihilation. Juan : Lantern quickly up on the second ropes to the left of Annihilation... John : Lantern grabbing ahold of Annihilation's head. A swinging DDT! Annihilation's head connecting with the chair! Eric : A beautiful move by the rookie. The cover...1, 2, 3!!! Lantern is your winner! Juan : An impressive win for the youngster by knocking off Annhilation in his first match here in the WWA. Lantern's arm is raised by the referee and he heads backstage. John : Annihilation gets up and walks back to his lockerroom shaking hands as he heads out with Leonard. Eric : This next matchup should be a great one. We get to see Kristopher Kasanova again, a young kid who's status is quickly rising to superstardom. Here we go! The camera flashes to Gary Trudeau in the ring with his microphone. Gary Trudeau :The next match of the night is between two of the WWA's top lightweights. First, representing the House of Pain, we have Zack Crockett!!! "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter plays over the loudspeaker as Zack Crockett comes out from backstage. He walks down the ramp towards the ring to a chorus of boos. Gary Trudeau :And his opponent. Hailing from Seattle, Washington we have...Kristopher Kasanova!!! As "Tourette's" by Nirvana begins to play Kasanova walks out from behind the backstage curtain. Tonight he is escorting two beautiful blondes to the ringside area. He kisses each of his women and slides into the ring. Eric : Kasanova and Crockett are circling each other, patiently waiting for the bell to start their match. Juan : We have what's looks like what a good matchup right here for us tonight. John : There's the bell, and this one's under way!!! Eric : Kasanova and Crockett locking together in the middle of the ring, with Crockett gaining the early advantage. John : Crockett sends Kasanova off of the ropes with an irish whip. Juan : Kasanova comes off of the ropes and ducks the clothesline, he's coming off the ropes again, and connects with a beautiful dropkick! Eric : That move really didn't seem to phase Crockett. He's back up on his feet. Juan : Kasanova quickly over to Crockett and theres the tie up. Kasanova whips Crockett off of the ropes and connects with a powerful clothesline. The crowd's going crazy! John : Crockett a little bit slower getting up from that hit. Kasanova wraps his arms around Crockett, in an attempt to flip him over his back. Juan : Crockett saw this coming however, and gives Kasanova a big elbow to the face. Eric : Crockett doesn't gain much momentum however. Kasanova just crushed Crockett's back with a double ax handle. John : Kasanova kicked Crockett in the butt and sent him on his face! He's going up the ropes. Eric : Could we see the Kiss of Death this early in the match?! Listen to this crowd Juan! Juan : Crockett is back up on his feet now, with Kasanova standing on the ropes....Crockett just fell into the ropes! John : Kasanova really didn't want that to happen. Straddling the ropes like that is good sometimes, but! Not in his case! Eric : Crockett pulls Kasanova off of the ropes and sets him up for a neck-breaker. Crockett lands it. Juan : Crockett up celebrating, but Kasanova isn't out just yet. He's up on his feet, much to Crockett's disbelief. John : Kasanova running full speed at Crockett, who has his back turned, and connects with a huge drop kick, right in the middle of Crockett's back. Juan : Crockett is barely standing now, he is hanging all over the turnbuckle, anything to keep from falling down... Eric : What's this?! Kasanova is tucking Crockett's legs into the ropes. Crockett is now stuck upside down by the turnbuckle. John : Kasanova gaining speed off of the opposite rope, and connects with a huge elbow to the face of Crockett. Eric : That move got Crockett untangled in a hurry! Wow! Did you see the impact off of that hit?! Juan : Kasanova's not done yet, he's standing Crockett back up, and..What's he doing?! Kasanova just jumped to the outside of the ring. John : Kasanova is still on the apron and he grabs Crockett's head from the outside of the ring and is facing this roaring crowd. Eric : What is Kasanova going to do here? Juan : Kasanova is pointing to the crowd with his left hand and the crowd errupts, they are really loving this young gun. Eric : Kasanova just did a backflip back into the ring and planted Crockett's face into the mat on impact. What a move! John : What a move is right! What should we call that move?! A backflip face slam? That was incredible! Juan : A very nice move indeed, and what do great wrestlers do after very nice moves? The go for the cover....1.. 2... 2 count only! John : How did Crockett manage to get his shoulders up?! Eric : Nevermind that! Crockett also managed to muster up some energy. He just tied Kasanova up. John : Crockett with an irish whip, sending Kasanova's chest right into the turnbuckle. Juan : Crockett following close behind Kasanova, and he turns Kas around. Eric : Huge chest chops from Crockett. 3, 4! 4 chest chops. That was heard all throughout the arena! John : Kasanova with a rake in the eyes. The ref didn't catch that one.... Eric : Kasanova just grabbed Crockett by the back of his pants and threw him through the ropes. Crockett's on the outside now. Juan : Kasanova's gaining speed off of the opposite ropes....SUICIDE DIVE! Connected perfectly on the fallen Crockett! Beautiful move. John : Kasanova is up in a flash and looks at the crowd for approval. Eric : Kasanova just dragged Crockett back in the ring?! What's this all about? Juan : You've got to be kidding me. Kasanova is climbing the ropes. Eric : Kasanova ontop of the ropes now. Arms pointed in both directions, this crowd is going absolutely nuts! Juan : Kasanova with THE KISS OF DEATH! Off of the top ropes!!!! AND TO THE OUTSIDE!.... John : BINGO! Executed Perfectly, Kasanova landed right on top of Crockett. I don't even think Crockett saw it coming. Eric : This crowd is absolutely chaotic right now. John : Kasanova slowly up to his feet and pulls up Crockett by his hair. He slides Crockett's motionless body into the ring.... Eric : Kasanova with an easy cover, This one's over! 1, 2, 3! Kasanova your winner! Juan : Not to mention a 450 Splash on the outside of the ring. That was one heck of a gutsy move by the youngster. John : Kasanova now with a microphone, out of breath in the center of the ring....... Kasanova : Bob!!! Have you ever had a crowd cheering for you this loud?! Have you ever fully experienced anything even remote to what I experience every night with my women?! (crowd responds even louder to that comment..) Hahaaaa. I'm ready for you Bob, but unfortunately for you....there's no way for you to be ready for me! Ohhh yeah Bob, that right there was nothing near to what I'm going to do against you.... Kasanova drops the mic and the crowd yet again explodes. He walks up the rampway shaking hands every ten steps or so. John : Wait, who in God's name is that?!?! Eric : Spidero just jumped the guardrail! El Porto Spidero starts harassing Kris with some fists. Juan : I guess Spidero wanted to give him a personal message man to man. John : AHH!! Damn!! Spidero giving Kristopher a quick knee to the groin and MY GOD, not on the concrete!!!! Eric : Spidero dropping him with his Fisherman's Buster. Juan : The Mexican appears to have a mic in hand. El Porto Spidero : My dear friend. Why? Why must you be such a cocky fool? I didn't see a reason to forcefully awaken you, but as you can now feel, I just did. Kasanova....pretty boy....keep your hands to yourself and just prepare to feel that little punishment again. We will meet...sooner than you could ever possibly wish. Juan : And there goes Spidero as quick as he came in. Eric : Whoa....is he alright. That could have cracked his skull wide open. Juan : The medics are out checking Kasanova but as quick as Spidero left, Kasanova's up and running backstage looking for the CPE member. John : And there you have it! Kristopher Kasanova with an impressive win over Zack Crockett and facing a little constructive criticism from Spidero. Kasanova didn't look happy leaving! Eric : No, he sure didn't. We have to go to commercial ladies and gentlemen. We'll be back with a TV Title Match that you can't miss! Don't go away! The camera fades in and "The Irish Assassin" Gregory O'Hara is in a fake jail. He grasps the bars showing off his massive biceps. He is wearing an orange jumpsuit that reads "WWA Correctional Institution" on it's back. O'Hara :When you're locked up life is bad. When you're locked up with an enemy life is hell. On January 31, the WWA's top superstars will all go through hell at the WWA's second annual "Rage in the Cage". Watch as superstars duke it out in a cage and FEEL THE REVOLUTION!!! The camera fades back in to the broadcast area at Saturday Night Extreme. Eric :Welcome back ladies and gentlemen. I believe we are ready to get to the TV Title Match putting Randy Cross up against Ken Steele, a rookie, the WWA TV Champ. Juan :We all know what kind of luck Randy has with rookies. This should be interesting. John :Hey, shut up man! Randy's one of the best....What the heck is this? "Great American Nightmare" by Rob Zombie starts up over the PA. Ken Steele steps out through the curtains and stops suddenly. Duke Daring steps out with a sling on his arm and right next to him is Nick Bruiser. They step up next to Ken, and right as they do ChiKylin and Mantis step out all decked out in their new look. ChiKylin heaves the belt up onto his shoulder as he and Mantis join the other three. Finally Flynn Steele steps out of the curtain and the crowd gives him a very mixed reaction. The six men stand still for a few seconds and than walk down to the ring and all enter. Duke has a little trouble getting into the ring as his leg looks hurt, but he finally gets in. Flynn walks over and grabs the mic from the mat and than walks back towards the middle. Flynn : Welcome ladies and gentleman. What you see before you........ Suddenly Mantis starts cracking up behind Flynn, and he stops and looks back. Mantis starts to fall to his knees and lifts his arm, pointing at Chi. The camera swings over to Chi and zooms in onto his arm. The tattoos covering him appear to be peeling. Mantis falls completely to the mat and starts rolling around. Duke and Nick start to point and chuckle as well. Chi rubs his arm and every single tattoo peels off as he removes his hand. Flynn looks at Chi as he looks back and both finally break down and laugh. As Mantis continues to roll around his purple and yellow mask pulls off revealing his old black and green mask, with his long hair still attached to the other mask. Finally Flynn calms down and raises the mic back to his mouth Flynn : I guess the jig is up. Look, why did we do this..well, just a reminder to all, gimmicks don't make the wrestler..talent does. We did this just to draw some heat. No one mentioned any of the members of FEAR till we put on this gag. Now look. Everyone wants a peice of us. Good. We like being hunted. We are use to being hunted. Let it begin, and see just how many of you fall to the wayside. The FEAR strikes in your heart..then we rip it out. Flynn drops the mic as the members of FEAR surround the ring, leaving Ken in the ring, ready and pumping the crowd up to a roar. Eric :That was quite interesting...it was all a hoax. What an announcement from the FREAKS or FEAR I mean. I'm all confused, let's get to the match... The camera flashes to Gary Trudeau in the center of the ring with his microphone. Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, the next match of the night if for the WWA Television Title. First, already in the ring, he is the TV Champion....Ken Steele!!! Steele raises his arms to the crowd. The crowd gives a mixed reaction. Gary Trudeau : And his challenger, he is the leader of the House of Pain and master of the figure four leglock...Randy Cross!!! Cross walks out from behind the backstage area as "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter begins to play. He slowly walks down the ramp, walks up the ringside stairs, and enters the ring. Eric : There's the bell and this match is officially under way. Cross looks really uninterested in this rookie. Juan : He should be fearing his life. We don't want him to blow his chance at this belt like he did with O'Hara at Season Beatings. John : Shut up Juan. That was a fluke. We all know that. Steele and Cross in the ring and the two lock up. Eric : Cross with the quick advantage and puts Steele in a camel clutch type position. Juan : Steele out with a jab to the ribs. He runs and gains momentum off the ropes. He flies and hits Cross with a thai kick. John : Chi must have taught him that move. The youngster going to the ropes. He gets to the second and flies for an elbow drop. Eric : Cross sticks up a boot and Steele's head lands in it. The youngsters inexperience showing very much right there. Cross up quickly and nails Steele with a legdrop. John : There's the Randy Cross I grew up watching. Randy picks Steele up into a fireman's carry and slams him down into a sort of inverted samoan drop. Great move by Cross. Juan : Randy going to the legs of Steele showing signs of maybe a figure four?! Yes, he's going for... Steele kicks out sending Randy back into the turnbuckle. Eric : Steele up quickly and whips himself on top of Cross into the turnbuckle. Steele throws Cross through the ropes to the outside of the ring. John : Steele trying to plant Cross's face into the metal steps leading up to the ring but Cross holds his ground and instead sends Steele's head bouncing off the metal. Juan : It's all good outside the ring, though, because this is Saturday Night EXTREME!! Cross rolls Steele back into the ring. Eric : Cross with the pin. 1..2..what is he doing?! Cross just released his cover. What the...?! Here come Mr. Business, Zack, and Aric Crockett sprinting to the ring. John : Cross has this easily won. What is he doing? Business and the Crockett's in the ring and start thrashing Steele. Juan : Cross locks in the figure four on Steele as Business, and the Crockett's stomp boots on this youngster. Eric : What is the purpose in this? I have no idea!! John : This should get more interesting, FREAKS in the form of Bruiser, Daring, and Flynn Steele come down the ramp and slide into the ring. Juan : They're not FREAKS anymore, they're FEAR. Bruiser and Daring clear the ring of Crockett's and begin sparring with the brothers outside. Flynn is going for Cross and nails him with a massive clothesline. Eric : Business continues to torture the young Steele. Business scoop slams the kid. John : Cross doesn't look happy and starts throwing fists towards Flynn. He's connecting a lot of those shots. Juan : Business sees his stablemates being beaten by FEAR outside the ring and leaves the nearly unconcious Ken Steele. Eric : Flynn and Cross really going at it inside the ring and Flynn nails Cross with a haymaker. Cross responds with an elbow to the gut and a russian leg sweep. John : Ken Steele is starting to get up and sees his father and Cross going at it. He gets to his feet quickly and goes to aid his father. Juan : Wait, some fan just jumped the guardrail. He slides into the ring and....EVIL KILLER on Ken Steele, that's no fan, that's Denrol!!! Eric : All chaos has broken loose here in the WWA! Good grief where is Cantley and his men?! Juan : Denrol out of the ring and back into the stands. That was smooth hit and run. John : Finally, Cantley and his men are out here! It's about time! We've got to go to commercial while this mayhem is being cleaned up by Cantley's security forces. Eric : The Extreme Title Match is next, you don't want to miss it! Don't go away! The camera fades out and after a few boring and useless commercials fades back in again. Eric : We're back ladies and gentlemen and ready for our main event. Juan : That's right, Eric. Shaman vs. New Star in his quest to get some gold here in the WWA. Let's get this party started. The camera goes to Gary Trudeau in the ring. Gary Trudeau : Ladies and gentlemen, the next match of the night is our main event and for the WWA's Extreme Title! First, the challenger. He stands 6-7 and weighs 310 pounds...Shaman!!! "Devil Man" by White Zombie begins to play as Shaman slowly approaches the ring. He has a wheel barrow filled with all kinds of stuff. He appears to be totally focused on the ring. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent. He is the leader of Star Station X. He stands 5-10 and weighs 217 pounds...The WWA Extreme Champion...New Star!!! New Star walks out alone to the sounds of "Chi" by Korn. Halfway down the ring New Star gets a smirk on his face and sprints and slides into the ring. Eric : This match is underway and Shaman has a surprise waiting for New Star in the ring. Shaman swings and connects with paint can from his wheel barrow. John : New Star looks unphased as he goes at Shaman as if nothing happened. New Star is pummeling Shaman with fists. Juan : New Star is off the ropes and nails Shaman with a well executed face buster. Now New Star sends Shaman off the ropes.... Eric : Tilt-a-whirl back breaker by the extreme champ. He slides outside the ring looking for treats in Shaman's wheel barrow. Juan : And treats are what he finds. He pulls out a 2x4 and slides it back into the ring. John : He sets the 2x4 down by him and pulls Shaman up by the head. He sends Shaman off the ropes again with an irish whip and quickly picks up the 2x4...oh no! Eric : New Star swings the 2x4 at Shaman's head but luckily the leader of Warrior's Pride ducks out of the way. John : Shaman off the opposite ropes and dropkicks the 2x4 right into the chest of New Star. Eric : Shaman slides the 2x4 out of the ring and goes to work on New Star. Shaman showing his strength advantage here as he picks New Star into the air and throws him down into the mat with a sidewalk slam. Juan : Shaman pulls New Star out of the ring and dumps the contents of the wheel barrow out onto the ground...What is he going to do?! John : Shaman throws New Star's head into the cornerpost on the outside of the ring, stunning the lightweight even more. Juan : Shaman wheels the wheel barrow up the ramp to the top platform next to the backstage curtain. Eric : The big man back down to the ringside area quickly and picks New Star up and onto his back. He's carrying him up the ramp! John : Shaman arrives to his destination and sets New Star into the wheel barrow. Shaman's wheeling him down the ramp. He let's go halfway down the ramp....and.... Juan : New Star just flew out of the wheel barrow and hit the side of the ring with full force. This kid's got to be unconcious. Half of Star's body is under the ring. Eric : Shaman slowly makes his way back down to New Star. Shaman's working the crowd now. This isn't a very smart move by the big man. We all know how tough New Star is! John : Shaman starts pulling New Star out from under the ring by his legs and.... New Star has his barbwire covered bat and drills Shaman with it. He must've hidden it under the ring before this match! Eric : New Star and Shaman both look really dazed. New Star somehow rolls the big man back into the ring. New Star with a cross leg takedown on Shaman in the middle of the ring. Juan : New Star's going to the ropes now. Could this be....it is!....the New Star Press! But somehow Shaman manages to get out of the way! New Star's down! John : Shaman picks up New Star and is going for the Thunder Clap, a spinning powerbomb.... He lands it!! Eric : Shaman with the cover. Here come Outlaw and Todd Logan. Shaman releases his cover as the two HOPers slide into the ring. Logan goes straight to Shaman and pummels him with a clothesline. Juan : Outlaw giving New Star some elbow drops to the chest. Hey, I guess this means Outlaw finally bailed Todd out of jail! John : See how smart the HOP are?! They're protecting Outlaws number 1 contender spot at Rage in the Cage. Outlaw and New Star will meet at Rage in the Cage for the Extreme Title and the #1 spot for Darken's World Title! Eric : The ref just called for the bell. This match has been thrown out and Outlaw has done his job. This makes me sick. John : This is great, HOP are the greatest! Juan : Yeah, you still can't explain why Randy threw away his chance for the TV Title can you?! Eric : Well folks, for John Douglass and Juan Guevesos, I'm Eric Pembrook, we'll see you next week! John : And remember, if you're not mean, you're not extreme.... Return to Homepage
