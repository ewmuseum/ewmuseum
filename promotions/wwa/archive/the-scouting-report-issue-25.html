The Scouting Report - Issue 25

24 Sep 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings and salutations WWA fans, it’s your esteemed and valued Director of Talent back with all the dirt and opinions you can handle with a new Scouting Report. -First we got some news from one returning superstar and two former. -As most of you have seen on Meltdown, and for those of you that didn’t shame on you, Liam Jenson is back on the roster. Matt Warr’s new bodyguard was taken out at the beginning of the year because of a broken neck. Of course after some recovery and rehab he’s back and ready to work. -Jackson Kraven has apparently been approached by a movie studio with an interest to his playing the villain in a new Universal Soldier sequel. "They want me to play one of a new breed of super-soldiers who have been altered genetically as well as cybernetically," Kraven said to E! Entertainment tonight, "I throw a guy out of a helicopter at one point, and that was a whole lot of fun." -Kraven would not be drawn on the subject of wrestling, but did also say that the injury that has kept him sidelined for so long may be on the mend. "My doctors say I probably shouldn't ever wrestle again, but that if I do I should at least wait until September or October of this year." -Former WWA Cruiserweight Champion Ramsey Kaine made headlines for the wrong reasons again this week, being picked up for a speeding violation in his home state of Maine. -Now on to the recap from Fiesta. -The Intercontinental Tournament got off to a great start on Monday. In the first round American Eagle faced the aerial artist Jimmi Zion. Eagle took it to him from the beginning but lost thanks the effect of Keru no Akiro. The former Fiesta enforcer distracted Eagle with a challenge. This just added to the hatred between the two. -Of course this wouldn’t be the only time that the Eagle would be seen. He also interfered in another match of the tournament. He came and cost Keru no Akiro the match with Venny LeTrav. It was a real fun match with such a contrast of styles till the Eagle came done, knocked out Akiro, and then pulled of his shirt to reveal a referee shirt. He counted the three count for Venny. -This of course lead to a backstage game of cat and mouse that ended with Eagle making a mad dash out of the arena parking lot in his car. -In one of the best matches of the night rookie Matt Keith and the veteran Wes Sanders. I thought it really proved how far Keith will go in this fed. Even though Wes came out on top I see good things in the future for both men. -Unfortunately the match between Joey K and Alex Knight did not take place. But to see the rational for that we have to go back earlier. -Last Fiesta Enigma called out Kash. Well Kash was there that night but laid in the back from a mystery assailant. At first Ricky Harrington tried to blame Enigma but then decided that Joey K had something to do with it. Of course Joey tried to pawn it off on Wes. In the end though Ricky striped Joey of his Intercontinental hopes and gave Knight a free slot in the next round. -In other big news on the “Night So Late Show” with Harley and Somers it was announced that when Raine is away he’s left a watchdog to make sure Troy Steffy behaves. And that man is X-Corp trainer Bridge. Needless to say Troy was not pleased. -In the main event Homo/Phobic from the UWF defended their PTC Tag-Team Title against out boys in X-Corp. Dave and Harley put up a valiant effort but came up short. Of course congrats for out to the champions although they are a bit…strange. -Now on to thoughts for the next Meltdown. -In the opening match of the night Eddie Van Dorn will be facing the Chuckster. Chuck has been impressing people lately even garnering himself a spot in Cold Pain Enterprises. However Eddie got the duke last week over Chuckster’s stablemate Shaman. Can he get the same results this week? -CPE will also be busy in a tag match. WWA TV champion David “Sniper” Wattsoner will be joining forces with Shaman to face Devil’s Own members Tommy Starr and Lockdown. You have to know that Lockdown will be wanting revenge after losing the TV title last week. And you know that you don’t want to make that convict mad. -Later on Grunge will face Leviathan for another time. The winner of this match will get to pick the stipulations for their match at Labor Day Brawl. Grunge has been throwing out stipulations all over Insider, not able to make up their minds. Levi has decided to play it closer to his vest. One has to think that the two are trying to play mind games with each other. -And in the main event “The Almighty” Damian Thorne will be facing “The Rainmaker” Nick Adams in the battle of the man who would be a god and the main who would be a force of nature. Last week Thorne outsmarted Nick’s manager Fate to get the victory. The DQ happened when Fate whacked Thorne with a chair thanks to Thorne’s urging. Now the two hope to settle their differences and you know Kash has to be watching. -Yes Kash, the World Heavyweight Champion will be there as will Tracer Bullet. Tracer will be there to continue his interrogation of who is the White Ninja. Tracer seemed to have it last Monday but we’ll have to wait and see. Well until next time this is Josh Wilson. Be sure to watch Meltdown and Fiesta. You won’t be disappointed. Ciao.
