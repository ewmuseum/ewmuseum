WWA — Feb 2001

Feb 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera moves down to the ring where Gary Trudeau is standing with a microphone. Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... The next match of the evening will be for the Extreme Title! Introducing first from Los Angeles California and the leader of the Star Station-X! We have New Star! "Chi" by Korn starts over the PA. New Star steps out from behing the curtains and is immediately hit with a chorus of boos. New Star mearly smiles and walks to the ring with the slight swagger only New Star can do. As he approaches the ring he looks right into a camera and arches his eyebrow and smiles, saying "That was for you Watson". He mockingly kisses the camera and proceeds to the ring with confidence. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent... Hailing from right here in New York City... And leader of the Cold Pain Enterprises! We have The Sniper! "Saturday's Alright for Fighting" starts over the PA as the Sniper enters the arena to a HUGE crowd pop for the hometown wrestler! He slaps a few hands on his way to the ring. His focus is on the ring and New Star. Johny : Whoa! New Star leaps over the top at the Sniper to get this going and is caught as the referee calls for the starting bell! Terry : Bodyslam on the concrete by the Sniper! This is turning into a brawl already! Tony : And why not? This is hardcore and that's what the fans came to see. Sniper now whips New Star into the steel guardrail and charges him..... CROWD : OOOOHHHH!!!! Johny : The Sniper just raced up the timekeeper's lap and off the timekeeper's table to try a spinning bulldog against New Star but New Star groined him chest and crotch first into the ringpost! Can we get a replay? Terry : The referee is having trouble controlling this, but New Star rolls Sniper into the ring, and wow! Springboard legdrop and the first cover, only a one, way too early for either of these guys to win. Tony : But you got to wonder about their health. They've been fighting in bouts like these for some time and it's bound to take it's toll. New Star applying good effort with that reverse chinlock, but the Sniper shoots him off, drops down, New Star vaults back into a powerslam by Sniper! Johny : Only a one count as well, and the CPE head now throws New Star through the ropes to the floor. He's up on top! Tony : Oh my! Johny : WHOA! What a flying bodypress! Both men lay on the floor as they're battling all over the ring. The Sniper is up, and he pulls New Star up by his crewcut, but New Star with a low blow! Terry : Plus the DDT on the concrete! This kid doesn't show any mercy, I like that! Tony : New Star grabs the guardrail and a springing moonsault off the guardrail onto the prone Sniper! The ref wants it back in the ring, but New Star says no and throws Sniper into the crowd! Johny : The fans eat it up as they trade chops in the fourth row! A fan hands New Star a cup of beer and New Star yells have a drink on me, on you! and smashes the beverage over Sniper's head. Terry : Yeah there we go! Sniper with his own low blow now, and he's taking New Star up the steps! He just slammed him on the aisle stairs! New Star gets up, and a flying bulldog onto the stairs! Johny : That hurt the Sniper who's holding his hip and New Star is holding his head in pain.... Terry : No he's trying to stop the flow of blood...We got juice! Tony : Both men are giving it there all and now Sniper batters New Star with well educated fists and there's a beel throw into the fans! Johny : Who thankfully moved, but not such a good thing for New Star. I wonder if landing on chairs back first raises your insurance premium? Terry : It doesn't matter though, Sniper has gone up to the next level to line up New Star... Tony : He launched! CROWD : OOOOHHHHHHHHH!!!! WWA! WWA! WWA! Johny : New Star got out of the way and the Sniper just dove chest first into standing folding chairs! Tony : Oh my GAHD! Terry : I think he's broken. Johny : Both men aren't stirring and the ref is checking on them, geeze New Star is wearing a crimson mask. Tony : New Star is up first somehow, and he's crawling back to the ring....He just found a chair and tossed it ino the ring, and what's he looking under the apron for? RANDOM FAN : BARB WIRE!!!! Terry : Ut-oh! Johny : New Star just pulled out a ten foot length of barb wire! Now that's thrown in the ring! He's going below for more? Tony : He should hustle if that's the case, Sniper is up and the fans are chanting for him as he stumbles towards The Future... Terry : New Star just pulled out a ladder! This kid's on a shopping spree! Johny : And here comes Sniper, whom New Star deflects with an upwards smash of the ladder! Tony : Now New Star sets the ladder down and tries to whip Sniper into the guardrail but no! A reversal! Terry : And Sniper hits a big clothesline knocking New Star over and into the crowd! Johny : Will they ever get back in the ring? Tony : Maybe...? Sniper is pulling something out from under the apron, it's a folding table! Terry : Sniper may have set it up, but having your back to New Star isn't wise.... Johny : German suplex on the floor! That took something out of both men! Tony : The ref again is having to check if New Star can go on, then he goes to check on Sniper who at least isn't bleeding... Terry : New Star's up first, and he scores with a few stomps....Now New Star has rolled into the ring and pulled the ladder in with him... Tony : Why's he setting that thing up? This isn't a ladder match! Johny : Who knows with these guys, they're all a little sick if you ask me. Terry : Regardless, New Star has brought that folding chair and himself up the ladder and wait a minute..I see, Sniper's on the apron, and down comes New Star off the ladder with the steel chair! OHHH! Tony : New Star now hooks a face lock over the ropes and suplexes Sniper back into the ring....I guess we'll see some 'rassling after all! Terry : New Star up on the second rope, and he misses with the elbow drop! Now Sniper shows he was playing possum by getting up and showing off to his fans! That sickens me! Tony : So do bouts with no interference, so we'll pretend you're twice as ill and take your mic! Terry : Hey! Johny : Enough guys, The Sniper has New Star and whipped him into the side of the ladder, and New Star drops like bricks beneath it's joint and the ladder falls sideways onto the ropes near it and falls back the other way..... Terry : Onto the head of Sniper! HA HA! That's what you get for kissing up to the fans, bad karma! Tony : Brother....New Star now seizes the momentum and brandishes that strand of barbwire... Johny : I was hoping he'd forget about it..... Terry : The kid is holding it in one fist and now....He's wrapping it around his arm? Johny : What's he thinking? Tony : Sniper is up and shoots a single leg, but no! New Star sprawled his legs back locks the whizzer and oh I'm going to be sick! CROWD : OOOOHHH!!!.....OOOOHHHHH!!!!....OOOOHHHH!!! Terry : New Star just crossfaced Sniper three times while his arm was wrapped in barb wire! Good god man! Johny : New Star has cut a gash on Sniper's forehead at least as bad as his own! Tony : Now he steps back and charges for a clothesline, but Sniper ducks, and a stun gun! Johny : The crowd is on it's feet, this bout is showing why both men are worthy of gold.... Terry : The Sniper now has the ladder and he's wrapping it over New Star's arm.... and he stomped it shut! Tony : The crowd may be roaring for that, but geeze, this is sick! Johny : No....This is EXTREME! Terry : Now Sniper sets the ladder up again, and forces New Star up it, on the same side as him.... Tony : Is he going to try a superplex off it? Johny : Looks that way, and wait, if he does, New Star will crash over the top and through the table on the floor! Terry : Sniper, lifts up on that contraption, but New Star is refusing and ...... Johny : OOOOOOOHHHH! New Star just uppercutted Sniper in the groin with his barbwire wrapped arm! The Sniper is wobbly! Tony : New Star just shoved him! Terry : He's down--- CARUNKTUNKT! CROWD : OOOOOOOOOHHHHH!!!! WWA! WWA! WWA! WWA! WWA! Tony : The crowd is a riot, New Star's arm and head are bleeding and Sniper just fell through a table onto the floor off a ladder. Now New Star is at the top of the ladder and he puts his arms out as if being crucified! Johny : He leaped! Tony : He dove onto the Sniper from all the way up there! That eighteen year old's insane! Terry : Anything to win my man, however with him holding his knee, I don't think The Future looks all too good at the minute... Johny : The ref is exercising his option to count, and both men look exhausted.... Tony : Bloodied, battered, what more can they do? Terry : Both have rolled into the ring and now are squaring off from their knees, firing shots, but neither gets completely knocked over... Johny : Seems like a lucha moment eh? Terry : Please don't go foreign now! The Sniper hits two fists at once instead of one, and New Star rolls over, drained. Sniper drops an elbow, and now applies a spinning toe hold.... Johny : Ah, good old wrestling does live.... Tony : The ref is checking New Star, and New Star looks out, so he raises the arm, and it drops once....twice,...thr, no New Star brings it up and small packages Sniper.... Terry : ONE! TWO! THR! DAMN IT REF COUNT! Johny : You placed a wager on this bout didn't you? Terry : My gambling problems are none of your concern..By the way can I borrow five bucks? Tony : Sniper on the rebound first and hits New Star with a backslide, and only a two count. Johny : Sniper with a double leg and jack knife pin, the ref counts...ONE! No New Star bridges up, where is he getting this from? New Star spins around and tries his backslide but Sniper with a backward roll! Terry : New Star was waiting! A kick to the midsection and it's the NOVABO- Johny : NO! Sniper falls behind New Star with a reverse rollup, but New Star kicks his man off into the turnbuckles and as he staggers back New Star's kip up allows him to catch Sniper in a russian legsweep onto the folding chair that's been lying around! Terry : The crowd has popped and New Star climbs the ropes... Tony : Sniper's up though and falls into the top rope and New Star may sing differently tonight! Johny : WAIT A MINUTE! Sniper has stood up the chair and asks the fans for approval and they roar! Tony : He's up there with New Star, could be a hurricarana.... Terry : He jumps and no! New Star just powerbombed him onto a standing chair! CRANKT! Johny : THAT'S NOT RIGHT! Tony : The chair is broke and New Star is covering Sniper...... Johny : ONE! Terry : TWO! Tony : THREE! We have a winner and Sniper kicks out a breath too late! Johny : He honestly did it, that punk kid took Sniper past the limit! Gary Trudeau : Here is your winner and first WWA EXTREME HARDCORE WRESTLING CHAMPION.....New Star! "Chi" by Korn begins to play, but New Star grabs a mic... Return to Homepage
