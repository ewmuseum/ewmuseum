February 12, 1999 Final Friday: The Apocalypse The screen opens up to a large parking lot where all the wrestlers for Final Friday are waiting. Most are in their cars or have company with them, and all of them do not look too happy about having to wait. Insults are shouted back and forth between some such as O'Hara and Denrol, who are being held apart by their respective stables. Some people are encouraging one side of an argument to "be a man". Suddenly a large black bus with covered windows drives up. The door opens and a little man with real big hair steps out. YES : Ya be doin' good ta be hurrinin'. The wrestlers are gather up their things and try and head for the bus, all trying to get there first. Yes speaks up again. YES : Hold dat right dere. Dose folk wid da competors kin just git now. None but da competors. And ifins I've gots ta seats yas, den I will. Otherwise, be adults. The wrestlers with friends and managers all start adding things to their own bags, and debating over other objects. Others rush to get in the bus acting manly so as to ignore how they were just spoken to on television. Big Eliem is doing his best to keep Big Mama Jama from kicking the bus driver's ass. Scene cuts to the Horsemen cabin. The wrestlers are filing off the bus, all looking around with some awe or suspicion in their eyes. Some look even a little afraid, with some large animal roasting over the fire pit and Raven Crow prodding it with a large poker to test it. He grins at the group rather evilly in a sarcastic way. A couple men, wearing "t-bird security" shirts rush off into the woods shouting for Lone Wolf. Quickly they come back with him, him with a dead squirrel already caught. He tries to cook it in Raven Crow's fire, but is dragged inside after the other wrestlers. Jak is shaking his head. Then.... World Wrestling Alliance flashes through the screen and leaves the WWA Banner. Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. Final Friday: The Apocalypse Lightning strikes from the ceiling onto the four ringposts as the lights go out. One bolt yellow, one white, one red, and one purple. As the thunder clap echoes into nothingness, the sound of hoofbeats can be heard, as though it's a wild herd galloping across an open field. The sounds subside as "Sad but True" by Metallica takes up the darkness. The dark form of Darken rides to the ring on a great pale stallion. He climbs into the ring and slaps his horse into running back down the aisle. He stands in the center of the ring for a long time, the crowd of wrestlers all assembled throughout the benches quietly watching, very few daring to break the silence with a mumble.
