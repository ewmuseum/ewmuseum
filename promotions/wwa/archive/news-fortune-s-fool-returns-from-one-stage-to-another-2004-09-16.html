Fortune’s Fool Returns From One Stage To Another

16 Sep 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a two week absence from the WWA, Chance returned to the ring last night against SurReal, claiming a victory in his first match back. But, where has “The Good Guy” been in the last two weeks? WWA-online.com caught up with him last night after the show and it was revealed that Chance was in Edinburgh, indulging his two passions besides wrestling and women, acting and music, at the Edinburgh Fringe Festival. In the afternoon Chance performed in a five-star sell-out production of Arthur Miller’s “Incident at Vichy”, asked to comment on the play, Chance said “It’s very moving, emotional and powerful. It has a lot of relevance to modern times and I think it could teach a great to certain people in power at the moment. Of course, as it’s Arthur Miller, it’s gloomy as hell.” Chance’s band “Forlorn Hope”, featuring former EWA star and sometime WWA wrestler Jarod Crow on lead guitar, played at night. Playing a mixture of funk and rock, audiences responded well, Chance was especially pleased to play alongside “The Love Gods”. Saying “The Love Gods are a really exciting new band, they won Radio 1’s Unsigned Band of the Year competition this year in the UK, and they play some really great rock. Other bands worth a mention are Breakmaus, an excellent funk and jazz band, and Audioporn, just for the name.” Asked for further comments Chance replied “Edinburgh is a great city, especially during the festival, I advise everyone to go. Not only are there great plays and music, but the stand-up comedy is great, wear a bright shirt like I do and I guarantee you’ll get picked on, which is a great laugh. Plus I met Christian Slater who was performing in an adaptation of One Flew Over the Cuckoo’s Nest, turns out he’s a casual WWA fan, if any of you are in London in the next few months he’ll be performing in the West End soon, too!” Now back in the WWA, fans can expect to see Chance even more motivated and full of energy than before after this brief detour into the amazing Edinburgh Festival.
