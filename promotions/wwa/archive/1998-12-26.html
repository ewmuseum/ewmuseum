Saturday December 26, 1998 The screen opens up to the the WWA banner. Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows the Fleet Center. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Eric Pembrook, John Douglaus and Juan Guevesos sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Eric : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the another episode of Saturday Night Extreme! I am your host Eric Pembrook. Tonight we are coming to you live from the Fleet Center in Boston Mass. John : Tonight we see who will go for the Extreme title at Seasons Beatings tomorrow night! Juan : That's right the semi-finals should be great! Eric : I'll say! We have The Sniper going up against the Lone Wolf and then New Star the self titled EXTREME ICON will be going up against the Crippling Canuck Brent Watson. John : I think we should just save everyone time and give the extreme belt to the Sniper! Eric : I take it then John that you are making a prediction on the outcome of the Tourney then? John : Sure! Why not! Yeah! The Sniper will win the Extreme Belt! Juan : I wouldn't count out the Light Heavyweight Champ or New Star for that matter. John (with an annoyed look): Whatever Juan... And again the musician says "Save Yourself...Self...Self"... "Self" echoing into nothingness as the music stops and the lights come with the Mimic standing in the ring. Juan : WOW! The crowd is cheering wildly for this apparant rookie. John : What the hell is the Mimic wearing...?! Eric : It looks like the same as what Bomber is wearing... All except the Tiger striped mask. I would say 255 or maybe a little more... Eric : Vinnie has his direction now as he is up and the two men circle each other. Collar and elbow tie up. Vinnie easily pushes the Mimic to the ropes. The referee asks Vinnie for the break. Juan : Vinnie swings with a haymaker! The Mimic ducks and moves out of the way. John : Vinnie turns and gets nailed with a dropkick from the Mimic. Juan : This time the Mimic is on the offensive bringing Vinnie to his feet and sending him across the ring with an irish whip. He lifts the big man up and THE BRONX BOMB! Eric : The Mimic just hit Vinnie's own finishing move on him. Vinnie looks to be in trouble now. The Mimic with the cover and the three count! Also a little embarrassing for the Bomber as his own Bronx Bomb was the reason for the Mimic's win! Eric : Nevertheless... The Mimic gets his first win in the WWA! "Save Yourself" by Stabbing Westward starts over the PA again as the crowd starts cheering for the rookie. The Mimic leaves the ring slapping hands with the fans. The camera closes and a commercial starts.... The camera fades back into the ring... And to Gary Trudeau. Gary Trudeau : This match is set for one fall. Now he's heading over to the timekeeper's table. John : He picks up the ring bell. He's going to hit Spidero with it. Juan : DENIED! Spidero kicked the bell right into Misery's face. There's first blood. Eric : Yes, a cut over his eye, that looks bad for Misery. He could become tempory blinded in one eye. John : Spidero picks up a chair and whacked Misery in the head. He goes for the cover. Juan : Two and three quarters. The ref was just a tad slow to count there. Eric : 15 minute mark now. Still no falls registered. John : Back into the ring. Spidero sets up a slightly dazed Misery on top. Wraps his head for a top rope Frankstenier.
