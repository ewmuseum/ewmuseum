WWA — 21 Nov 1997

21 Nov 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday November 21, 1997 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows Lehman College's The Apex. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey and Terry Valentine sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the Apex in the Bronx, New York. This is the Innagural Episode of Friday Nite Fights. I am your host Tony Bailey. And to my right the "honorable" Terry Valentine. Terry looks at Tony with an annoyed look Terry : Thanks, Tony! This is a very exciting night for Northeast Championship Wrestling. After tonight we will have a new NCW New York City Champion. I would just like to say that... Tony :Thank you, Terry! Again, Terry flashes Tony an annoyed look. And then just shakes his head with a scowl on his face. Tony Bailey : Tonight is the NCW New York City Championship Tournament. It should be exciting... Terry. Terry : Whatever! Tony : OK. Well anyway the Tournament will start right after this short interview. Johny... The Camera flashes to Johny Morehouse. Who is having a hard time hearing.... Johny : Wow! I'll tell you what Tony, I can barely hear in this place. The fans are going crazy! I have two working men coming out here... "The Blue Collar Men". Johny turns towards the locker room area. Dillan Krueger and "The American" Jed Smith walk out of the locker room as the theme to Bladerunner blairs throughout the arena. Dillan Kruegar steps up to Johny Morehouse. Dillan Krueger : Cut the stinkin music! The music stops. Dillan Krueger : Me and Jed Smith here are what is happening in the NCW!!! And if you don't like it.... OH WELL!!! Now if you think we came here just to wrestle for the helluvit, you're dead wrong!! We're here to break your bones and spit on your body after we beat the Bloody Heck out of ya!!! See, I, "Sundance Kid" Dillan Krueger, am a specialist in what I do, an innovator if you will, and I don't play around I'm here to beat these pieces of scum that call themselves wrestlers!!! I will be around the corner at every match, in your face at every interview, I will even be in your momma's bedroom if you don't watch it.. HAHAHAHA Cause I'm that kinda guy!!!! Now what comes with Perfection, which is me if you don't grasp it, is Semi Perfection, which is my good buddy "The American" Jed Smith that is standing beside me. Together we're to good for anyone, and that's why we are forming "The Resistance"!! HAHAHAHAHA!!!! Jed Smith : Well said Dillan, you heard right we're here to whip up on your asses and that's the way it'll be. I'm tougher than any scum in here, and that's the way it'll be. Anyone who wants to join "The Resistance", go for it, we just say who stays and who gets cut. If you can' tell now, we take no crap from noone and we'll be your best friend, or worst enemy, and if we're your friend, we'll stand up for you as long it's no everyday basis, and for you slap tub of lard fans, we really don't care if you boo us or cheer us, it don't matter, we're here to wrestle, AND THAT'S THE WAY IT'S GOING TO BE!! The crowd boos.... "You Suck" is heard as "The Resistance" backs away from Johny. The "Sundance Kid" flips off the crowd as he and "The American" turn to go into the Locker Room. Johny : Well, you heard it here first. Back to you Tony. The camera goes back to the broadcast area. The camera zooms in on Tony and Terry. Tony looks to Terry Tony : Well, that was a very bold statement from the "Blue Collar Men". Terry : "The Resistance" you ninny! I really LIKE those guys. I think I have a favorite to win the whole Tournament! I think the "Sundance Kid" will leave here with the gold. As a matter of fact you should just give the belt to Kruegar and we can all go home early. Tony : Are you finished! Terry nods.. Terry : Yep. Go ahead. Tony : Thank You. We will go now to the Ring Announcer, Gary Trudeau, for the first match intro. The camera goes down inside the ring and zooms to Gary Trudeau Gary Trudeau : Ladies and gentleman, welcome to the Apex in the Bronx. In the first match of the New York City Title Tournament..... "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts over the PA. The crowd starts the boo. Gary Trudeau : We have: Hailing from St. Paul, Minnesota standing 5 foot 10 inches and weighing in at 239 pounds, from the "House of Pain", Zack "The Enforcer" Crockett. The crowd keeps the boos going as the camera goes to the Locker Room Exit. "The Enforcer" walks out of the back. He walks down the isle jawing at the fans that, obviously dislike him. Terry : Listen to this crowd, they love this guy! Tony : You are obviously DEAF as well as DUMB! Zack Crockett gets into the ring and hold his hands out to show off for the crowd. This is met with a greatly displeased crowd. The Camera goes back to Gary Trudeau as Filter is replaced with "Pepper" by The Butthole Surfers. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent: Hailing from Chicago, Illinois standing 6 foot 2 inches and weighing in at 220 pounds, we have Johny Blaze. The crowd goes wild... As Johny Blaze enters the Arena he plays the crowd as he walks down the isle. He is giving high fives as he enters the Ring area. He climbs the turnbuckle and jumps into the ring off of the top rope. Gary Trudeau removes himself from the ring. The bell rings. Tony : We are joined, now, by Johny Morehouse. Johny : The introductions have been made, and both men are in the ring. Tony : This should be a great technical matchup. Both men are incredible wrestlers. Terry : Yeah, it's too bad that Blaze doesn't have a chance against the Enforcer. Tony : Only time will tell. Johny : Both men are circling each other. Look at the expressions on each of their faces. Tony : What expressions? They both look like they have ice running through their veins. They lock up and feel each other out. The Enforcer gets the leverage, and pushes Blaze back a few steps. Terry : See what did I tell you? Blaze can't match up with The Enforcer's power. Johny : Keep in mind Terry, The Enforcer may have the power, but Blaze has the height advantage, and I would say the speed as well. Tony : They lock back up again. This time Blaze gets the advantage, and takes The Enforcer down with a legsweep. The Enforcer hits hard on the mat. He looks dazed. Johny : Blaze now following up, he grabs Enforcer's legs. What's he going for? The Figure - 4? An airplane spin? Tony : No it looks like he's trying for... Yes, it is, he's going for the airplane spin. Round and round The Enforcer goes where he stops no one knows. Terry : Blaze releases him and sends him sliding across the mat into the corner. Oh No! The Enforcer just rammed the cornerpost shoulder first. The ref's checking on him. This match could be over. Alright, here comes Randy Cross down the aisle, this can't be good for Blaze. Johny : The ref says let it continue. Blaze goes to continue his offense. He reaches down to pick up The Enforcer. Cross has the ref's attention. Tony : The Enforcer just nailed him with a low blow! Where's the ref! Terry : Ha! The ref didn’t see it. Blaze is down! Tony : The Enforcer is now in total control. He piledrives Blaze through the mat. Johny : Enforcer picks up Blaze and piledrives him again. He throws Blaze outside, right in front of Cross. Terry : Look at the Enforcer. He's distracting the ref. Cross just DDT'd Blaze on the concrete. Cross picks up Blaze and throws him back in. Tony : Someone is coming down the aisle. It appears to be Griffin. Terry : What business does he have down here? He has no business here! Johny : He's either a new member of House of Pain or he's here to protect Blaze. Tony : Back in the ring Enforcer gives the sign for his finisher. He whips Blaze in and - wait Blaze reverses it and floors Enforcer with a flying forearm smash. Terry : Cross is on the apron. Griffin goes over and drop-kicks Cross to the concrete. Johny : Meanwhile Blaze has Enforcer down. He gives the sign for his finisher. Blaze executes his patented Hell's Fire. Terry : The Enforcer submits. Blaze advances, but wait Cross is in the ring. So is Griffin. Johny : Cross attacks Blaze. Griffin attacks Cross. Blaze regains his composure and he and Griffin whip Cross into the ropes and take him down with a double drop kick. Tony : Enforcer is up and he get's knocked out the ring by a double clothesline. The House of Pain retreats to the locker room. Johny : Blaze and Griffin shake hands and head back to the locker room. Terry : Did you see those cheap shots that those, those two losers pulled on the House of Pain. I don't believe it! Harrington better fine those creeps for CHEATING. Tony : I sense a little bit of animosity between the House of Pain and Blaze and Griffin. At any rate, Johnny Blaze advances and Crockett doesn't. Johny : That's right, Tony. Let's go down to Gary Trudeau for the next Match. The camera goes down inside the ring and zooms to Gary Trudeau Gary Trudeau : It is time for the second match in the New York City Title Tournament. The theme to "Psycho" starts over the PA. Gary Trudeau : Hailing from Richmond, Virginia standing 6 foot 5 inches and weighing in at 250 pounds, we have Erik "Painmaker" Payne. The crowd gives up sort of a mixed reaction as the Painmaker enters the Arena. He confidently walks down towards the ring. The Painmaker gets into the ring as his song stops and "Harvester of Sorrow" by Metallica begins. The Harvester of Sorrow enters the arena as the crowd meets him with a few cheers. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent: Hailing from the Frayed Ends of Sanity standing 6 foot 5 inches and weighing in at 272 pounds, The Harvester of Sorrow. Gary Trudeau leaves the ring. Tony : This match jumps to the opposite side of the spectrum than the previous match. Johny : That's right, Tony. We go from two technicians to two brawlers. Terry : Harvester just hit the ring and jumps Payne before the bell rings. Johny : Payne never saw him coming. Harvester kicks him in the midsection and piledrives him. Tony : That'll leave a mark. Terry : Harvester is relentless. He picks up Payne and whips him into the ropes. Payne practically gets his head taken off with a vicious clothesline. Johny : Payne is helped to his feet by Harvester who tries to whip him into the corner. Tony : Harvester charges the corner, but Payne lifts his leg and kicks Harvester in the head. Terry : Harvester is reeling. Payne runs out of the corner and takes him down with a football tackle. Payne is pummeling him with a barrage of punches. Tony : Harvester sits up and knocks Payne for a loop with a massive haymaker. Johny : Both men to their feet. Payne kicks Harvester in the stomach, and takes him down with a double underhook suplex. Terry : Now that takes serious power. Payne follows up with a bow and arrow submission. Johny : This could be over already. Tony : Wait! Here comes the entire stable of House of Pain. "The Painmaker" Randy Cross, Aric Crockett and Zack "The Enforcer" Crockett. Johny : They all enter the ring and start pounding on Payne. Harvester just stands back and laughs. Tony : The ref calls for the bell. Payne is down and out. The House of Pain continues to beat on Payne. Terry : Look, the Harvester is joining in on pounding Payne. Does this mean that the Harvester is part of the House of Pain? Tony : What a dispicable act. I can't believe that the House of Pain is doing this. This is disgusting, even for the House of Pain! Randy Cross come up to the camera and yells, "I'm the only Painmaker here, BABY!" Johny : The House of Pain gets out of the ring and walks out of the Arena. The Harvester leaves with them. Terry : So who won the match? Tony : Erik Payne won by Disqualification. He will move on to the next round! Terry : It sure doesn't look like he won. Laying there in the middle of the ring. Johny : The Paramedics have taken Payne out on a stretcher. We are ready for the next match. The camera goes down inside the ring and zooms to Gary Trudeau Gary Trudeau : It is time for the third match in the New York City Title Tournament. The theme to "Bladerunner" starts over the PA. Gary Trudeau : Hailing from Tampa, Florida standing 6 foot 1 inches and weighing in at 219 pounds, we have the "Sundance Kid" Dillan Krueger. The crowd gives a rather negative response as the "Sundance kid" make his way to the ring. He confidently walks down towards the ring. He gets into the ring and takes off his sunglasses and Hat. "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts up on the Public Address. The fans break into a fit of pure hatred, with booing and the like. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent: Hailing from St. Paul, Minnesota standing 6 foot 2 inches and weighing in at 252 pounds, "The Painmaker" Randy Cross. Gary Trudeau leaves the ring. Tony : Listen to the hatred the fans already have for Cross. Terry : I love it. Too bad that Cross and Krueger have to fight each other. I hate to see two such model citizens go against each other. Johny : Who knows what to expect from this match. Tony : Both men are hardly fan favorites, but both are very skilled in the technical aspect of wrestling. Terry : This could be a rerun of the Blaze-Enforcer encounter. Johny : I wouldn't count on it. I expect both of these men to go tooth and nail until there is only one man left standing. Tony : The bell sounds and Cross charges Dillan. Dillan takes him down with a drop toe hold. Johny : Dillan attempts to put a Boston Crab, but he can’t get him over. He tries for an elbowdrop instead but Cross rolls out of the way. Terry : Both men lock up, and Cross backs Dillan into the corner. Cross now unloads a series of back knifehand chops across the chest of Dillan. Tony : Wow those chops are echoing through the arena. Cross attempts a cross corner whip, but Dillan reverses it. Wait, Cross re-reverses it and Dillan hits hard into the corner. Johny : Cross follows up with a monkey flip in the corner. Cross slaps on a kneelock submission. Terry : Cross is trying to soften Dillan up for a quick submission and also for his finisher Figure Four leglock. Tony : Dillan gets to the ropes and the ref makes Cross break the hold. Cross takes Dillan to his feet and nails him with a knee breaker. Dillan is hurt. Johny : Cross goes back to work on the left leg with another grapevine, this time in the center of the ring. The ref is asking for the submission, but Dillan won't give up. Terry : Cross breaks the hold and drops three consecutive elbows to the left knee. Even if he can't get the submission, Dillan will be impaired with only one good leg. Tony : Well you know what they say, no man stands tall without 2 legs to support him. Johny : Dillan struggles to get to his feet, and Cross drop-kicks Dilan outside of the ring. Dillan lands on his knee on the concrete. He’s not getting up. Terry : He maybe playing possom. Johny : The ref is up to 5 in his count. Cross goes out and throws him back in. Tony : Cross goes for a cross corner whip, but the ref got in the way. The ref is out! Terry : And look here comes Aric Crockett of the House of Pain. Johny : Big surprise. And look he’s got a chair. Tony : Aric hits Dillan then rolls outside and revives the ref. Cross covers Dillan and the ref groggily counts to 3. Johny : It looks like House of Pain is taking no prisoners. Terry : I told you that Cross would win. I knew it the whole time! Tony : You said that Dillan Krueger would win the Tournamnent! Terry : Huh, I don't recall. Anyway I told you! Johny : Well, whatever you said, Terry, Randy Cross is in round two. Down to Gary Trudeau. The camera goes to the ring and Gary Trudeau Gary Trudeau : It is time for the four match in the New York City Title Tournament. An Oriental song start up on the PA. Gary Trudeau : Hailing from Somewhere in the Orient standing 5 foot 11 inches and weighing in at 215 pounds, we have the White Ninja. The crowd gives a positive response as the White Ninja enters the Arena. He gives a few high fives as he walks down towards the ring. He gets into the ring and begins a Martial Arts routine. The Theme to "Bladerunner" begins. The fans start a "Ninja", "Ninja" chant as The American" Jed Smith makes his way to the ring area. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent: Hailing from Tampa, Florida standing 6 foot 1 inch and weighing in at 220 pounds, "The American" Jed Smith. Gary Trudeau leaves the ring. Tony : In the final first round match, we will see the battle of Technician versus Martial Artist. Johny : Is it me or is almost everyone in this fed primarily or secondarily a Technician. Terry : Not everyone . . . just the majority. Johny : Ninja takes his stance as the American enters the ring. The bell sounds, but the Ninja has not moved an inch. Tony : The American doesn’t know how to approach this. He mocks the Ninja with his crude impersonation of The Karate Kid Crane Kick. Terry : The Ninja launches with a flying shoulderblock. American side steps and waits til Ninja gets to his feet. Tony : American nails him with a beautiful standing dropkick. Terry : I like this guy, but the fans don't. I don't hear any of those peons chanting U-S-A. Johny : Ninja to his feet again, is met with a kick to the midsection. Tony : American screams something about the new revolution and drives Ninja to the mat with his modified DDT. He hooks the leg and gets the three count. Terry : Wow. That was impressive. I think "The American" will win the Tournament. Tony : How many times are you going to change your mind, Terry. Anyway, Jed Smith, will advance to the next round. Johny : We’ve just gotten word from the back that "Painmaker" Erik Payne is unable to wrestle for the remainder of the tournament, so Johnny Blaze will goto the finals via default. So let's go to the Ring. The camera goes to the ring and Gary Trudeau Gary Trudeau : It is time for the only match in the New York City Title Tournament round two. "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts up. Gary Trudeau : Introducing, from St.Paul, Minnesota standing 6 foot 2 inches and weighing in at 252 pounds, we have "The Painmaker" Randy Cross accomponied by Aric Crockett. The crowd, again, tries to boo The Painmaker out of the Arena. He enters the Arena and basks in the glory of the Booing crowd. Aric Crockett, also, comes out of the back with Cross. He gets into the ring and does a little strut around the ring. He takes off his robe and hands it to Crockett. The theme to "Bladerunner" starts as "The Resistance" comes out of the back. The crowd gives a little bit of a positive reception as "The Resistance" come down to the ring. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent: Hailing from Tampa, Florida standing 6 foot 1 inch and weighing in at 220 pounds, "The American" Jed Smith accomonied by the "Sundance Kid" Dillan Krueger. Gary Trudeau leaves the ring. Johny : Well I almost feel sorry for the winner of this match, because they’ll have to face a fresh Johnny Blaze who pulled off an incredible victory despite the House of Pain's interference. Tony : The bell sounds and we are under way, in the only semi final round of this tournament. Terry : They lock up and immediatly Cross takes Smith down with a legsweep. He follows up with an elbow to the knee but Smith rolls away. Tony : Cross manuevers behind Smith and takes him down with a belly to back suplex, and attempts to grapevine the leg. Johny : Smith counters with a legsweep of his own and gets to his feet. Cross is up. Tony : Surprisingly neither man has cheated. Terry : Smith kicks Cross in the stomach. It looks like he could go for his finisher. Cross nails him with a low blow. The ref didn’t see it. Johny : Dillan jumps on the apron and argues with the ref. Look at Aric. He just slipped something to Cross. Tony : Smith turns to Cross. Cross leveled him with whatever object Aric gave him, and then threw it back out to Aric. Terry : The ref spins around and Cross has Smith down. The ref starts to count. Dillan tries to get in the ring to stop the ref but Aric levels him with a lariat on the outside. Tony : Cross gets the pinfall and Aric rolls in the ring. House of Pain begin beating Smith. Dillan rolls in and begins attacking Aric. Smith fights back against Cross. They’re fighting all the way to the locker rooms. Terry : Blaze must be loving this. If Cross gets hurt, he'll get the belt by default. Johny : Just got word from the locker room that security has seperated the 4 men and Cross WILL be in the final match. Johny : Ok folks, here’s the match we've all been waiting for, the main event. Let's go down to the ring for the introductions. Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen. This next match is the final match in the New York City Title Tournament. It is scheduled for one fall, with no time limit. Coming down first . . . "Pepper" by Butthole Surfers blares through the arena and the crowd erupts in cheers as Blaze and Griffin step out from behind the curtain. Both men greeting the fans along the way Gary Trudeau : He is lead to the ring by Griffin. Weighing in at 220 pounds, and hailing from the streets of Chicago Illinois. He is Johnny Blaze . . . Blaze jumps from the floor to the apron and does a springboard somersault into the ring. "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter begins to play and the arena is filled with jeers and boos Gary Trudeau : He is the cornerstone of the House of Pain, and is being led to the ring by his stablemates Aric and Zack Crockett. Weighing in at 252 pounds, and hailing from St. Paul, Minnesota. He is "The Painmaker" Randy Cross. Johny : The intros have been made, and the ring is cleared except for the ref and the final two combatants. The refereee holds the title belt in the air Tony : Both men are great technicians, but let's see how well Blaze can handle the distraction of House of Pain. Johny : He's not alone out there though, he's got Griffin by his side. Terry : Still it's still 3 against 2, maybe more if Harvester is part of the crew. Tony : Only time will tell. The bell sounds and we are under way. Johny : Blaze forces Cross into the corner and unloads a series of knife-edge chops across his chest. Tony : Those chops are echoing throughout the arena. Johny : Blaze attempts to whip Cross cross corner but Cross reverses it and Blaze is sent into the corner. Tony : Cross charges but Blaze steps out of the way. Cross manages to put on the brakes before hitting the turnbuckle. Terry : Cross turns around and is leveled by a running lariat. Tony : Leveled? He was just sent airborne. Terry : What does it matter he's down isn't he? Johny : Blaze hooks Cross's arms and legs. He's got him in the Hell's Fire. Tony : He doesn't have it hooked in all the way. Cross struggles, and rolls into the ropes. Johny : Both men to their feet. Blaze charges, and Cross counters with a backdrop that sends Blaze over the top rope to the concrete. Terry : Blaze looks like he wrenched his knee on the fall. Griffin is there to help him up. Tony : Cross rolls out and pushes Griffin aside and throws Blaze back in. Griffin leaves ringside and heads back to the locker room. Terry : Hah, Griffin is Chicken! He is deserting Blaze after a little push. Johny : Who knows. Cross is on the top rope and lands an incredible fistdrop onto Blaze's injured leg. Tony : Blaze is in pain. Cross goes for the figure four. He gets it. Terry : Yes. It's over. It's all over for Blaze. Johny : Blaze isn't giving up. He's trying to roll over but look at Cross, he's using Aric for leverage. Tony : The ref looks up and Cross releases Aric just quick enough to not get caught. But, look Blaze has the move half reversed. Just a little more. Johny : He's got it. Blaze reversed the Figure Four. Cross is in agony. He breaks the hold. Tony : And look here comes Griffin again, this time he's with someone. It looks like - yes it's Michael Blackheart. Terry : What business does he have down here? He doesn't have a manager's license. Tony : Neither do any of the House of Pain members. Johny : Back in the ring Cross has Blaze set up for a suplex, but Blaze blocks it. Blaze tries to reverse it. Tony : Oh my lord! Blaze's knee gave out while he had Cross up in the air and it turned the suplex into a very devestating brain buster. Cross is down and busted open. Blaze is down with quite possibly a broken leg. Johny : Zack is on the apron distracting the ref. Aric rolls into the ring behind the ref and takes that object from earlier and nails the ref in the back of the head. The ref is out. Tony : Griffin rolls in and nails Aric with a Frankensteiner. Meanwhile Zack has gotten a chair and climbed to the top. Terry : Zack dives off the top and lands on Blaze's face with the chair below him. Arabian Facesmash. Blaze is unconscious. Johny : Blackheart has had enough. Blackheart rolls into the ring and choke slams Zack onto the chair. Tony : Cross is beginning to stir. Blaze is busted open. Either man can win this if the ref ever comes to. Terry : Griffin and Aric are now brawling on the outside. Blackheart throws Zack through the ropes onto his brother. Johny : The ref is starting to stir, and here comes The Resistance Kruegar and Smith. They rush Aric and Zack. Tony : Cross crawls over and collapses on Blaze. The ref begins to count. 1 - 2 - 3. Terry : YES!!! Cross wins, Cross wins. I told you, I told you! Johny : But the fight is far from over. Blaze is down. Cross is down. Griffin and Blackheart are pounding away at Aric. The Resistance are fighting with Zack. Tony :Who is that coming in the Arena. Is that the Black Scorpian! We are out of time. Tune in next card.
