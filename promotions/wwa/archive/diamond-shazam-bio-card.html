Diamond Shazam Basic Information Nicknames | The Mastodon of Fashion Real Name | Brian Kohne Height | 6' 5" Weight | 279 Date of Birth | 13 September 1979 Home Town | The Deepest Recesses of Hell by way of Osaka Japan by way of Hollywood California by way of Hawaii... it's complicated Theme | The Edge of Glory by Lady Gaga More Details Appearance Bloated 80's style muscular physique (I'm implying steroids here) on a light-skinned mixed blood Hawaiian frame. In a sense, he's quite genetically gifted in that he posseses both his father's European facial features and the strong athletic body typical of Islanders; he'd be quite the attractive athlete if he weren't so intent on wearing as much make-up as a mutant Kabuki performer. Despite effete behavior, thick gnarled hands and an obviously reset nose speak of his difficult past. Hair is dyed bright plum and wrapped around short pins shaped like devil horns. Ring Attire Traditional black wrestling briefs underneath a light green and white pleated Japanese hakama reaching down to his ankles, with a large belt featuring a red fanged demon head covering the lower belly. Wears black studded leather bracers, studded leather bicep bands and a dark kukui nut lei necklace. Wrestles bearfoot without the benefit of knee or elbow pads (although he does tape his ankles). Uses indigo facepaint, dark purple lipstick and elaborate mascara patterns around his eyes. Nails (hand and feet) are expertly manicured and painted purple. Personality Aloof and condescending, Diamond Shazam is a diva of a man who is convinced that wrestling should be as shocking as any nouveau cinema. Which he does through inordinate amounts of over-the-top stiff violence. There is nothing he enjoys more than drawing disgust and revulsion from his audience, which is reflected by his gaudy appearence and drawing blood from a well-loved personality. Despite his love of movies, Diamond Shazam has much respect for professional wrestling, considering it the modern-day American theater. As such, he endeavers to make wrestling as grotesque and off-putting as how he see's the American public; art reflecting life as he'd say. History Born in picturesque Hawaii, Brian Kohne spurned the sea and surf in favor of movie houses, eventually using his father's money to move to Hollywood and study professional cinema. Despite a gentle nature, Brian drove away potential friends with his abhorrent opinions on what constitutes "good cinema"; until classmates led him towards a course in modern theater (and far away from them), which by some trick of fate landed him on the West Coast wrestling scene. Already as loud and outlandish as the Hawaiian shirts of his homeland that do not actually originate from Hawaii, Brian Kohne quickly gathered the attention of a visiting Japanese promoter eager to add oversized Americans to his fledgling promotion. In something that could best be explained as a straight-up mash of UWF-style shoot wrestling with HUSTLE-like gimmickry, fighters would hit each other with obscenely stiff blows while dressed up in ridiculous outfits such as condom coveralls with purple afros. It was in that environment that Diamond Shazam was born; originally this Hell-spawned wizard who turned children into goats and opponents into so much mush, Brian lasted over three years getting kicked in the head by the more experienced wrestlers. For you see, naive Diamond Shazam learned at his expense that the Japanese were a xenophobic and closed-minded lot; finally figuring out that his employers were interested in him only because his natural size made him that much more impressive to destroy by the smaller native talent. He worked long and hard to earn the title of Baka Gaijin, only to learn that these words didn't exactly mean what he thought they meant. After constant brow-beating and servitude, Diamond Shazam broke down hard. A backstage fight with the promoter who hired him ended with criminal charges and an exile from Japan, not to mention a fair bit of bitterness towards pro-wrestling... Two things he appreciated from the experience, however; first being exposed to the advanced power moves known as "strong style wrestling". After having his theories mocked and ridiculed back in America, it was refreshing having a weapon by which to vent his frustrations towards those who never appreciated his genius. Secondly were the totally sweet threads they gave him. Yes, taste-challenged Diamond Shazam not only kept the name but the whole entire gimmick, relunctantly admitting that the Japanese were the only ones to understand proper fashion. Now wiser and with all trace of innocence lost, Diamond Shazam returns to force his ideas upon the American audience, believing that since all performers should suffer for their art, it is only fair that art causes suffering in return.
