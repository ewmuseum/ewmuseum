Thursday April 12th, 2001 The screen opens to the familiar WWA Studios in East Rutherford, New Jersey. Sitting at the Blast Off! desk is Eric Pembrook and Terry Valentine. The lights are still down, but you can see they are dicussing something. The lights turn to the correct brightness, both men orginize their scripts in front of them in unison. Behind them the screen comes to life showing the Space Shuttle taking off, then the new Blast Off logo appears. The camera zooms back out to show both men with smiles on their face. Eric : Welcome everyone to the revamped and first edition of the WWA Blast Off! With me as always is "honorable" Terry Valentine. Terry : Thanks Eric.... Great to be back in the WWA Studios here in New Joysey! Eric : We are on the Heels of one of the better Pay Per Views of the WWA in recent history. It was 3 hours of pure, Madness! Eric : Owner and CEO Mark Harrington is struggling to replace him, but as always he will think of something. Along with the loss of Johny Morehouse, we are also losing the staple of the WWA, Friday Night Fights. Terry : It is indeed a sad day for the WWA. But again, we will keep moving on into uncharted waters... MONDAY NIGHT! Eric : It should be fun! We are moving head to head with the Big Fed! The first ever Monday Night Meltdown line-Up is shown.
