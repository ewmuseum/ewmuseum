WWA — 18 Nov 1998

18 Nov 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WWA SLAM!! WORLD WRESTLING ALLIANCE... The Ultimate in Wrestling Entertainment! A video of the Earth rotating in space. The camera zooms through the atmosphere down to the various countries..pass Big Ben..pass the Eiffle Tower..the Sphinx..into Orlando..to the Universal Studios..Lot 24..Set 3B..The WWA Set is there..a logo appears.. Along the back wall are images of some of the top superstars..Cross..Outlaw..Steele..Denrol..Tambo. Sitting next to the video monitor, behind a desk, is the WWA Vice President Stan Jackson. Stan : Welcome to the NEW SLAM! Yes I know that we only had one show but things are vamped up cause you asked for it. May I introduce my cohost. Flynn Steele. Flynn : I am glad to be part of this show. I've done some commentary before. Not bad being back behind the mic. Stan : Let's start with a recap of what happened Friday. Flynn : Friday we saw Kris Kassanova beat Watson with the help of O'hara and some salt. O'hara is trying to make himself known as a force in the WWA, Maybe he is trying to get his own stable together. Stan : Sniper and Butler had a visitor in thier match. Eliem. He tried to take them down and out alone, almost did, but the CPE stopped the carnage. Sniper was spitting mad to get at the big guy. Flynn : Speaking of the CPE, they gained a new force. El Porto Spidero. After fighting to a time limit draw, and then Star Station X attacking, CPE came down and saved the mexican superstar. I hate to admit it, but the Cold Pain Enterprises is getting stronger. Soon, if not now, they are a real force to be careful of. Stan : Denrol recieved another title shot. This time against Payne for the TV belt. This one ended when Black Lotus came down to talk with Alexandra. Things got ugly. A catfight began and.. Flynn (snears): Then Denrol made a big mistake, he touched my wife. So I came down and showed him what's what. Stan : That aside. Denrol had Payne beat with the Evil Killer. Harrington made the right choice by having a rematch. Flynn : Denrol better watch himself..lightning could strike twice. Stan : That a warning. Flynn : That's a fact. Stan : Flynn here then faced three of the best the House of Pain has to offer. In the guantlet match, Steele took a tremendous battle. First Business, Logan then the former champion. Flynn : I take no satisfaction beating Cross. He is a shell of who he was. Doesn't matter. I move on, but he better get back on track or step aside. Stan : In the final, well, let's just say both House of Pain and Star Station simply fought. Flynn : That is the easy way to put it. Nothing was settled in this stable war. This looks to be a strong feud in the making. Stan : When we return, we will have an interview from the TV champion and the first of our two main events. The camera closes and a commercial starts.... The camera fades back into Johny Morhouse walking in front of a camera. He turns to the face it and begins to speak. Johny : Hello ladies and gentlemen. I am going to try and get an interview with the TV Champ. Johny turns to the locker room door and knocks on it, softly announcing who he is and his intent. The door slowly opens and Major Havok's face can be seen. He motions for Johny and the camera crew to enter. As the camera enter the room, you see Sgt Payne sitting with an ice pack on his neck. He sees Johny and quickly puts the ice pack down and stands up, putting his "face" on. Johny : Well Sgt Payne... Can I call you Matt, or perhaps Matthew. Payne : No Sir! I am currently on duty. SO i would prefer you call me Sgt or Sgt Payne. Johny : Ok... Well, Sgt Payne what are your comments on your match this evening... And more to the point Denrol. Payne : Well Mr Morehouse... It is simple you see. I was beaten in the middle of the ring. Denrol executed his "Evil Killer" and that is that. Denrol is a great competitor, and I guess I must thank Mr Steele, more to the point Mrs Steele, for a second chance at Denrol. Johny : So you must have heard Harrington's desicion about already assigning a re-match between you two. Payne : Yes Sir. Johny : As TV Champion, do you have any concerns this time around about the Cold Pain Enterprises? Payne : I have no reason to believe that Denrol and his cronies will try anything. They didn't last time afterall. But I must say... Denrol, you bring your boys into this match and interfere. It will be a bad day for you and yours. That goes for all of you "STABLES" out there. You ladies are just lucky I don't bring in my Cpl in charge and my Squad leaders. We would detroy everyone of you. So consider yourselves LUCKY. Johny : Well... Sgt, Do you have a prediction for this Friday? Payne (i>looks to the camera and gets a sneer on his face): WAR! The camera cuts back to Stan Jackson and Flynn Steele. Stan : Payne seems ready. Flynn : As much as I hate it, Denorl's time is prime to finally win gold. Denrol had too many tough breaks. Everyone gets lucky sometime. Stan : Let's go to our main event. Stormtrooper is going to show what he brings to the table. Flynn : Just power. The image swithes to a full WWA arena. Tim Powers is in the ring with Stormtrooper Terry : Just look at that guy! Stormtrooper towers over this punk Powers. Johny : Powers with the early attack. He's punching away at the gut of the German. Terry : Who is just laughing. It's not even effected by them. Powers off the ropes. Johny : One clothesline attempt. Nothing. Another Still nothing! It's obvious Tim can't even come close to harming this monster. Terry : third att..noo. Stormtrooper has him by the neck! Johny : Chokeslam! Powers is planted. Terry : Stormtrooper with the pin. He pulled Powers up at two. I love it! Johny : The ref is repremanding him for it. Trooper with the whip into the ropes, plows right through with a clothesline, almost removing the kid's head. Terry : The German taunting the crowd, who are showing thier distain for the German fighter. Johny : He just waves them off, lifting Powers to his feet. Terry : Inverse DDT. just going through the motions now as He finally pins Powers. Tim was totally desimated. Johny : Back to the studios. The camera flashes back to the set. Stan : The Stormtrooper is going to be a powerhouse that I am not to sure can be derailed. Flynn : Odd that someone like that is a SS-X member. He doesn't fit into thier childish attitude, but to each thier own. Stan : Now for an announcement. Three weeks from now, December the 11th..we will be having our Pay Per View event, Season Beatings. Matches will start being announced next week right here. Flynn : Tis the season for giving, and the WWA is set to give out some ass-whoopings. Stan : Also do not forget to check out the weekly column No Hold Barred..which I remind you does not represent the opinions of the WWA. Flynn : Or any sane person, whoever writes that is a bit of a whiner. Don't like what happens in the ring, then deal with it there. Stan : Well that is the end of our show, for Flynn Steele, this is Stan Jackson saying Come on back ya hear. Return to Homepage
