The Gamblers Table — Birthday Bash

12 Jan 2004

Bylined "The Gambler" (Kevin/Vic Sage), the fed's own in-house prediction columnist persona.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome, WWA faithful, to another edition of the Gambler’s Table. This time, we celebrate six glorious years of WWA action with the 2003 Birthday Bash. We have some great matches for this one, not to mention the Birthday Brawl itself, which is always unpredictable. Allow me, Kevin Sage, former Las Vegas bookie, to share my picks with you. Cross-Roster Four-Way Elimination Tag Team Match Chuckster & Fuel vs. David “Sniper” Wattsoner & Khaled vs. Keru no Akiro & Brad Polaris vs. Jimmi Zion & Desolation Winners Get IC/US Shot In this unique match, the teams are composed of members of the WWA who are on opposite rosters. For example, Chuckster is a member of the Meltdown roster, while his partner, Fuel, is a member of the Fiesta roster. Both members of the winning team will earn a title shot on their respective shows. So, if Jimmi Zion and Desolation were to win, then Zion, as a member of Fiesta, would receive a shot at the Intercontinental Title, and Desolation, as a member of Meltdown, would receive a shot at the US Title. Needless to say, this is a great opportunity for the eight men involved. But, there’s more to this than just a title shot. Chuckster and Desolation have had an ongoing rivalry for quite some time, and have taken every opportunity to get in each other’s way, including an exceptionally pungent impromptu fight in a dumpster. Of course, David “Sniper” Wattsoner is part of this match as well. Sniper trained one of the WWA’s own legends, Denrol, and is swiftly making a name on his own merits here in the WWA as the former TV Title champion. Sniper also reformed Cold Pain Enterprises, a stable that spanned both shows. Unfortunately, Shaman and Chuckster turned on Sniper before he left for Fiesta, and CPE: Meltdown was disbanded. CPE: Fiesta is alive and well though, with three members, Jimmi Zion, Sniper, and Fuel, representing CPE interests in this match. Keru no Akiro was also offered a position with CPE, but declined after gaining a victory over Zion. One has to wonder what kind of odds Chuckster will be up against when he is facing a long-time rival in Desolation, former stablemates in CPE, and even being forced to team with Fuel. Considering Fuel’s temper, I don’t expect that tag team to last long. However, Chuckster and Fuel have both been mentored by the WWA’s extreme icon, Shaman. Khaled has found himself on Shaman’s bad side as of late, with a series of brutal attacks between the two, and Khaled’s attempt to cripple Shaman. Will Chuckster and Fuel put aside their CPE differences to show Khaled the error of angering their mentor? Will CPE set aside their indignation at Akiro’s refusal to join? To add even more intrigue, a masked man attacked Brad Polaris during his debut on Meltdown. Will that masked man become a factor in this match? And we can’t forget about Desolation’s ever-present partner, Agony. This is shaping up to be a fantastic match, and I wouldn’t miss it for the world. It’s a tough call, but my money’s on Zion and Desolation. Desolation is an impressive rookie brawler, and Zion has the speed and experience to bring the victory home. Texas Dogcollar Match Liam Jenson vs. The Mysterious GAROU Another promising young EWA graduate, The Mysterious GAROU, will meet Matt Warr’s personal bodyguard and enforcer, Liam Jenson, at the Birthday Bash in a Texas Dogcollar Match. The two men will be wearing dog collars and will be tethered together with a length of heavy chain attached to those collars. The only way to win this match is to literally choke one’s opponent into submission. GAROU has impressed many in the WWA with his high-flying skills, and his deep commitment to the warrior way. Unfortunately, this match is one of Liam’s choosing, after earning the right to do so on Meltdown in a six-man battle royal, and highlights his own sometimes-sadistic nature. Furthermore, Liam has gone so far as to name a special referee for this match, Tommy Starr. With Jenson and Starr’s recent alliance, this is certainly a tough situation for GAROU. Jenson holds the edge in size, standing half a foot taller, and weighing eighty pounds more than his opponent. The only factor that I can see as being in GAROU’s favor is the fact that Jenson has only recently returned from a broken neck. Nonetheless, as much as I would like to believe that GAROU’s honor and warrior spirit could overcome the odds, I have go with the safe bet and wager my money on Liam Jenson getting the win. WWA World Title Kash vs. Enigma Gate To Hell Match Enigma called Kash out long ago, challenging him to defend his World Title, even before Labor Day Brawl. As the leader of the Forsaken Souls, Enigma got his answer when he expelled Joey K from his stable. In a macabre ceremony, Enigma even went so far as to bury Joey K and hold a mock funeral, complete with a priest in attendance. Much to Enigma’s surprise, this priest turned out to be Kash in disguise. Soon after, Kash began a series of attacks on Enigma, assisting X-Corp in their battles with the Forsaken Souls. On the 10/20 Fiesta, Enigma attacked one of Kash’s Thirteen, Victoria. During Kash’s non-title match with Dave Harley, Enigma lowered Victoria from the rafters, crucified on a large iron cross. With that distraction, Dave Harley was able to get the win over Kash. Before Enigma’s match against Venny LeTrav that night, the Thirteen were able to rescue Victoria from the clutches of the returning member of the Forsaken Souls, Se7en. Enigma claimed the Intercontinental Title that night, but was forced to defend his title against Dave Harley immediately after. To make things worse, Commissioner Bridge named a special guest referee for that match, Kash. Not surprisingly, in consideration of the odds stacked against him, Enigma lost the IC Title to Dave Harley. Two weeks later, Enigma revealed his army of henchmen to counter Kash’s coven of thirteen followers. The ensuing brawl between Kash’s Thirteen, and Enigma’s army of thugs paved the way for Kash to drop from the rafters and attack Enigma. This brawl was interrupted by Commissioner Bridge. With neither man satisfied by the results of that fight, this epic running battle continued to escalate. During the Bridge Barnstormer match on the most recent Fiesta, Enigma taunted Kash by using the Kashmission on Sniper. He took things further however, when his minions attacked Kash, allowing Enigma to climb the ladder and snatch the contract that would allow him to participate in the Birthday Brawl. Later that night, Kash teamed with Mal Somers to take on Dave Harley and Enigma. With such an unusual pairing, it’s no surprise that Harley turned on Enigma, allowing Kash and Somers to pick up the win. After that match, with Enigma enraged, Kash offered Enigma a shot at the World Title at Birthday Bash in a Gate To Hell match. Enigma refused the match, citing the fact that he couldn’t be expected to accept a match when he didn’t know the rules involved. Commissioner Bridge involved himself again to book the match, despite Enigma’s objections. Now, Enigma, the architect of darkness, gets his chance to wrest the World Title away from the WWA’s resident Wiccan champion, Kash. With Kash’s Thirteen nearby, and Enigma’s new army of minions ready to do his bidding, I don’t expect this match to be as simple as a one on one battle. Easily the most dangerous villain that Fiesta has ever known, Enigma combines incredible agility and speed with a devious mind. His deadly submission finisher, the Hypnotic Conundrum, has garnered innumerable wins for Enigma. However, he’s giving up five inches in height and a little over thirty pounds in weight against a man who has proven his ability to work in any style. Moreover, Kash has his own unstoppable submission finisher, the Kashmission. We all know that Enigma is the master of planning, but with the rules for the Gate To Hell match being unannounced, he’s likely to find himself at a disadvantage. For me, Kash is money. Birthday Brawl In this huge event, every belt in the WWA is up for grabs. The methods by which participants win each belt vary widely, as do the competitors themselves. In this historic event we have some of the best that the WWA has to offer, such as Kash, Enigma, Dave Harley, Mal Somers, Grunge, Leviathan, Tracer Bullet, Damian Thorne, Venny LeTrav, The Psycho, Shaman, Bonafide, Chance, Matt Keith, and one wildcard entrant who has not yet been revealed. Word has it that other WWA superstars of the past and present have been clamoring for an opportunity to get into this match. And why wouldn’t they? The Birthday Brawl provides a great chance for anyone, absolutely anyone, to walk away from Madison Square Garden with a prestigious WWA Title. The current champion doesn’t even have to be pinned to lose his title. The titles will remain with their respective shows though, so if a Fiesta talent were to win a Meltdown title, that talent would then move to Meltdown. Keeping that in mind, let’s take a look at the likely playing field for each belt individually. The Television Title, from Meltdown, is currently held by “Psycho” Steven Murphy after a timely intervention by Matt Warr and Leviathan allowed him to defeat Sniper on his last night on Meltdown. The TV Title will be won by gaining the first pinfall of the night. Psycho, a powerful technical wrestler, will have his work cut out for him if he wants to retain that title. There are any number of wrestlers who would love to get their hands on the TV Title, not the least of which is the number one contender, Shaman. Of course, we can’t overlook some of the other great competitors that may be after this belt, such as one of the WWA’s hottest rookies, Matt Keith. Perhaps we’ll even see Venny LeTrav or Bonafide step up to take the TV Title. When the dust settles though, I expect to see the WWA’s extreme veteran, Shaman, get the first pin of the Brawl. Fiesta’s GenX Title will be awarded to the first wrestler to make an opponent bleed, whether by intention or accident. Venny LeTrav, the reigning GenX Champion, will have to fend off all of the competitors that may set their sights on this belt. I believe that any of the folks going after the TV Title could just as easily set their sights on the GenX Title, making both belts hotly contested. At the top of the list is Bonafide, the number one contender to the GenX Title. Despite his questionable mental state, Bonafide has the technical skill necessary to get the job done on any given day. Venny’s unique and energetic style may prove to be too much for Bonafide to overcome though. On the other hand, Matt Keith’s violent, hardcore style may give him an advantage in trying to get his opponents to bleed. When the blood flows, I expect to see Matt Keith with the GenX Title in hand. Another Fiesta Title, the Intercontinental Championship will be claimed by the first man to exit the arena. The current IC Champion, Dave Harley, picked up the belt when he challenged Enigma only moments after Enigma won the belt. Interestingly, this was also the moment when Harley had sent his long-time partner and friend, Mal Somers, on an errand to get him out of the arena. Mal, who had already made it known that he wanted to be the first to have a shot at Enigma’s IC Title, was understandably shocked by Dave’s actions. Factor in Mal’s recent disagreements with and reliance on Rookie, the now-defunct Rookie Title belt that Mal has somehow personified, and one has to wonder about the potential fate of X-Corp. Another member of X-Corp, Chance, won his slot at the Birthday Brawl in Bridge’s Barnstormer match on Fiesta. But then, Chance seems to be losing patience with his partners as of late, casting even more doubt on the future of this stable. Any other man in this Brawl could be the first to exit the arena as well, but I think that we’ll see Mal Somers step foot outside of the arena to win the IC Title. But, will Rookie get jealous of the new belt and drive Mal to even more unhinged behavior? Meltdown’s own US Title will be won by the first man to make an opponent submit. The reigning champion, Grunge, certainly has an advantage in this type of match with his submission finisher, the Grunge Movement. This move has served him well, winning him numerous victories. The number one contender for the US Title, the green-eyed monster called Leviathan, may not have the technical arsenal that Grunge does, but he more than makes up for it with his incredible strength and brutal killer instinct. Still, any man in the Brawl could claim the esteemed US Title, perhaps even a member of X-Corp. I’m certain that the ongoing battles between Leviathan and Grunge will reach an apex here. I’m equally certain that Leviathan’s recent partnership with Damian Thorne will play a role, and we’ll see a new US Champion in Leviathan. And, of course, we have the most sought after, and most celebrated belt in the world of wrestling, the WWA World Title. The World Title will be earned under the rules of the Mile High Match, suspended above the WWATron. In order to reach the belt, one must scale the frame of the giant video screen, erect the ladder on top, and climb that ladder to grab the belt. As always, the competition for that belt will be intense this year. Both Kash and Enigma will be involved, even after surviving a grueling battle earlier in the night. The arrogant, and some would even say smarmy, Tracer Bullet is likely to make his own bid for the World Title, but the presence of the WWA’s own personification of remorseless evil, Damian Thorne, may cause his focus to be less than perfect. Damian Thorne’s apparent vendetta against all women has left Tracer’s wife, Melissa, in danger, and Tracer will be looking to protect his interests by eliminating the threat. We also know that there will be one unannounced wildcard in this spectacular event. Whoever that wildcard is, I’m sure that he’ll make his presence known here in the World Title scene as well. Interestingly, if Enigma should beat Kash earlier in the night, then fail to claim the belt here, then he will have earned the distinction of the shortest WWA World Title reign in history, finally lifting that stigma from Denrol. Could Kash possibly walk out of the Birthday Brawl as the champion, after defending his title against Enigma and fighting in a mass brawl, with others vying for the World Title? As much as I hate to say it, I believe that it will be too much, even for the most popular hero of the WWA, Kash. Just as in the US Title situation, I think we’ll see the partnership between Leviathan and Damian Thorne play a major role here, earning the World Title for a member of this Unholy Alliance. My money says that “The Almighty” Damian Thorne walks out of the Birthday Brawl as the WWA World Champion. There’s one more stipulation in the Birthday Brawl, allowing one competitor to secure a World Title shot of his own by eliminating, whether by pinfall or submission, the most opponents. Because no one can possibly prepare for the wildcard entrant in this match, I’m betting that he’ll be the one to score the most eliminations and gain that shot at the World Title. What an incredible night we have ahead of us. Six years of history will be celebrated with blood and sweat. All five WWA titles will be on the line, and the heated rivalries, and downright blood feuds, that are running rampant through the WWA will insure that the action is extraordinarily intense. Don’t miss this one folks. Call your local cable or satellite providers and set your VCRs. The WWA’s Sixth Annual Birthday Bash is going to blow us all away! Until next time, this is Kevin Sage reminding you of the words of Thomas Jefferson. I'm a great believer in luck, and I find the harder I work, the more I have of it.
