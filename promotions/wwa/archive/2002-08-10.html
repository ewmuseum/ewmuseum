Kraven picks up the ring steps once again. He charges Moore, smashing him with the stairs and knocking him backwards over the top rope. The referee begins to stir. Kraven tosses the steps over the top rope. Mikal: That was brilliant strategy by Jackson Kraven. He is utilizing every opportunity to dissect Moore. Chase: Moore is out! The referee looks from Kraven to Moore and back. He begins to count. At three, Moore clutches desperately at the guardrail. Blood oozes from under his long black hair. At six, Moore makes it to his knees. At eight, Jackson Kraven slides under the ropes and begins to attack Moore again. Chase: He was 2 counts away from winning this match! Mikal: He doesn't want to win it on a countout! Kraven "helps" Moore to his feet, lifts him and drops him throat-first across the guardrail. The Tomorrow Man grabs a fan's sign that says "Gimme Moore" and tears it in half. He grabs a handful of Moore's hair and begins to drag his foe's face down the slats in the guardrail. Each metal bar is left with a sticky crimson sheen. Kraven lets go of Moore's hair and the man from Texas falls limply to the floor. Kraven rolls back into the ring to break the count. Mikal: This is why the Tomorrow Man is the most feared competitor in the WWA today. He is merciless and unrelenting. Chase: He's a sick SOB, that's for sure! Kraven climbs to the top turnbuckle. His shadow falls across Moore's battered form. Kraven raises an elbow and dives off. Mikal: OH YEAH! Chase: That was the worst Savage impression I've ever heard! With a sickly wet thud, the Tomorrow Man crashes onto Moore. The crowd goes silent. Kraven drags Moore's limp body toward the ring and tosses him in. Kraven follows Moore in and places a single foot in the center of Moore's chest. Mikal: That is all! 1.. 2.... Before the ref's hand can fall a third time, Moore pushes Kraven's foot off his chest, spinning the Tomorrow Man. Moore forces himself up beneath Kraven and rises unsteadily. Kraven finds himself, ever so briefly, in the Electric Chair Drop position. Moore staggers under the weight of his opponent and then sends him crashing, throat first, across the top rope. Jackson Kraven's head whiplashes off the top rope. Mikal: What the - ? Chase: That was the biggest hot shot I've ever seen! Both men are down. Moore's face and chest are covered with blood. The Tomorrow Man gasps for air. Chase: Fans, you may never see a bigger match than this. This one could very well be for the soul of the WWA! Moore is first up. He pulls Kraven up. Kraven lands a quick left to the side of Moore's face. Moore takes a step back. Kraven takes control, whipping Moore into the ropes. Moore ducks under Kraven's clothesline and bounces off the far ropes. He extends his arm for a clothesline. Kraven wheels around and both men connect with their clotheslines. Both men hit the mat hard! Mikal: Neither of these men can get a significant advantage. Moore and Kraven both push up to their knees. They start throwing punches again. This batch is much weaker than the first. None of them hit very hard, but they still take their toll. Moore swings wildly, but Kraven avoids the blow. The punch hits the ref, flooring him. Chase: Tough night for the ref. Moore lunges at Kraven. The Tomorrow Man grabs Moore trunks and catapults him through the middle rope. Kraven rolls outside. He jams an elbow into the back of Moore's head, knocking him down. Kraven reaches under the ring and pulls out a table. The Tomorrow Man sets up the table and grabs a handful of Moore's hair. Mikal: Kraven has wood! Chase: That's weak! Mikal: Like your chin comrade! Kraven drags Moore to the ring apron and sets him up for a piledriver. Kraven grabs Moore's trunks and pulls Moore down, right through the table. The wood splinters under the impact. A collective gasp goes up in the crowd. Chase: He's trying to cripple Craven Moore! Mikal: That sounds like a good idea to me. Chase: Pinko bastard! Kraven isn't done. He drags Moore to the English announcers' table. The announcers there are wearing vintage WWI tin helmets. Something about an air raid can be heard as Kraven slams Moore down onto the table. Moore starts to climb the corner buckle again. Chase: It's like Kong climbing the Empire State Building! Mikal: King Kraven? I like that. Kraven dives off the top turnbuckle, aiming an elbow drop right at Moore's heart. At the last possible moment, Moore rolls off the table and the Tomorrow Man crashes through it all alone. Another table's life is extinguished in a blown spot. A moment of silence. Chase: NOBODY HOME! The referee starts to count. Both men attempt to rise. Moore is once again favoring his neck. Kraven pulls a jagged piece of wood from his right arm, which hangs limply at his side. Moore rolls into the ring at 6. Kraven follows in at 8. Mikal: I wish Jackson Kraven would stop toying with Moore and put him away! Chase: He'll be hard pressed to do that with one arm. Kraven swing a roundhouse left at Moore. Moore blocks the block and wraps his other hand around Kraven's throat. With tremendous effort, Moore raises Kraven off the mat and hits a chokeslam. Wembley Arena explodes with cheers. Chase: He's still got something left! Mikal: Stop pandering. Moore stands over Kraven. He looks out into the crowd, and slowly begins to pull off his elbow pad. Mikal: He wouldn't! Chase: He just did! Denrol Elbow! Moore slings his elbow pad into the crowd and begins to bounce himself off the ropes. After the second bounce, he stops, gyrates and drops a huge elbow on Jackson Kraven. Wembley Arena comes alive with flashbulbs. Chase: Denrol Elbow! Moore covers Kraven and gets a 2 count. Chase: I can't believe Kraven no-sold that! Mikal: Come on! No one should be forced to sell THAT move! Moore climbs to the second turnbuckle. He jumps off, landing both feet squarely into Jackson Kraven's mid-section. You can almost hear the air rushing from his lungs. Mikal: Did he just hit "Steppin' Out"? Chase: Moore sending a message in there. Moore reaches down for Kraven. The Tomorrow Man grabs him and rolls him up. The ref drops to make the count. 1.. 2.... KICKOUT! Once again the two titans climb to their feet. Moore reaches for Kraven only to take a European uppercut. Kraven grabs Moore and, with surprising speed, hits the KravenBomb. Mikal: KravenBomb! The Tomorrow Man is about to win this one! Chase: Not in this match! Mikal: Damn stipulation! Kraven covers Moore. The referee taps Kraven to remind him of the match stipulation. Kraven slaps the mat and then begins to yell at the referee. This is a tactical mistake because it allows Moore time to climb to his feet. Moore gets a short running start and hits a flying Diamond Cutter on the Tomorrow Man. The Arena erupts in cheers. Chase: Moore is surprisingly sudden for a big man! Mikal: Bah! Moore walks to the corner and pats the top turnbuckle. The crowd goes bananas. Moore slowly makes his way to the top turnbuckle. During his climb Jackson Kraven rises to his feet. Moore turns around into a haymaker from Jackson Kraven. Moore sits on the top turnbuckle. Chase: These two behemoths are slugging it out on the top turnbuckle. Mikal: This will be like Sputnik crashing! Moore punches Kraven. Kraven punches Moore. Kraven punches Moore a second time. Then a third. Moore sags. The camera catches that demonic look on Jackson Kraven's face once again. Mikal: This ends now comrade! Chase: I'm afraid you're right. Kraven drapes one of Moore's arms over his shoulder and attempts to hit a superplex. Moore blocks it and flails at Kraven. Kraven tries to pull Moore off again. Moore blocks it again. Kraven releases the hold and swings at Moore. Moore blocks the punch and lands a double thrust squarely in Kraven's throat. Mikal: Where did that come from? Chase: Who knows? At this point I think these guys are channeling former selves! Kraven instinctively grabs for his throat. Moore slams the point of his elbow between Kraven's eyes. The Tomorrow Man tumbles limply onto the mat. Moore finishes his climb to the top, and perches himself unsteadily on the turnbuckle. Chase: I don't like the looks of this! Mikal: He's never hit this in his entire career. Tonight won't be any different! The arena is filled with the strobing light of thousands of flashbulbs as Craven Moore pushes off of the turnbuckle and stretches out for a frog splash. Chase: OHMIGOD! Mikal: I've seen everything now! Chase: HE HIT IT! HE HIT IT! Moore hooks the leg. The referee counts. 1.. 2.... 3! Chase: HE DID IT! Mikal: NO! THIS IS WRONG! SO VERY WRONG! Moore rolls off of the Tomorrow Man to the outside. The referee raises his arm and hands him the belt. Brooke Prescott: Ladies and Gentlemen, your winner - and NEW - WWA WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, CRAVENNNNNNNN MOOOOOOOOOOORE! Bloody and battered, Moore backs up the ramp clutching the belt to his chest. Jackson Kraven slumps against the ropes, looking on in disbelief. Moore raises the belt above his head and the crowd cheers wildly Mikal: This is not right!! Chase: Folks, don't forget to tune into Meltdown!!
