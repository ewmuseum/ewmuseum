A 3rd Brother? Birthday Bash 2006 has taken shape and we now know the majority of the card (save for a few surprises of course) as the promotional material has been sent out. One interesting wrinkle in the line-up is the tag team title match. What's interesting about it isn't the fact that it's a 4 way singles match. Or that it is the in-ring return of David Black after a long lay-off. What's interesting is that it includes a man that isn't even on the roster. This mystery man is none other than "Ty" Ballard. WWA Insider has done its homework, and we can inform you with 99.9% accuracy that Ry and TARIN Ballard do not have another sibling who has signed with the WWA. The announcement of Ty Ballard in the tag title match is simply a minor typographical oversight by the WWA brass. Of course, no one is perfect, and these little errors happen from time to time. It's completely understandable. Even still, given the rivalry that has heated up between the Elements of Opposition and True Living Colors, one can imagine that the EofO aren't too pleased to have this mistake involve their name. Already a ripple of laughter has been heard backstage, with the biggest ripple coming from the champs themselves. Can it be too much longer before the "so pathetic they can't even bother to get your name right" jokes start? Rest assured though, it is TARIN Ballard competing in the tag title match at Birthday Bash, with an opportunity to cash in on the single's environment his brother Ry couldn't a few weeks ago. Winning that match will be one way to ensure a mix up like this never happens again. Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
