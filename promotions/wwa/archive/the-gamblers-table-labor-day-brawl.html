The Gamblers Table — Labor Day Brawl

16 Aug 2003

Bylined "The Gambler" (Kevin/Vic Sage), the fed's own in-house prediction columnist persona.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As we move toward Labor Day Brawl in beautiful Tampa, Florida, it’s time for another installment of The Gambler’s Table. Here’s where I, Kevin Sage, impart my observations and picks for the spectacle that is a WWA Pay-Per-View. With so many great matchups to cover, including the WWA World Heavyweight Title Tournament, let’s jump right in. WWA World Heavyweight Champion Craven Moore vs. Tracer Bullet Falls Count Anywhere, No Interference As both men are quick to point out, these two have never met one-on-one in the ring. Their only experiences against each other are in tag team matches. That means that we’re starting out LBD with a historic match in more ways than one, as this is the first World Title defense of the night, but certainly not the last. This match is a Falls Count Anywhere match, meaning that nearly anything could happen. With the unearthly power of Craven Moore bearing down on the indomitable talent and determination of Tracer Bullet, this Labor Day Brawl is going to start big! One would think that Craven Moore’s size would be a factor in a falls count anywhere bout, but Tracer’s creativity could nullify that advantage. We know that both will be prepared for this match, but I believe that Tracer’s edge in experience will be enough to carry the day. My bet goes to Tracer Bullet to win the World Title from Craven Moore. Jackson Kraven vs. Denrol Steel Cage, Win by Pinfall or Submission Only, No Interference What do you get when you cross the Tomorrow Man with The Greatest of All Earth Time and a steel cage? I’m not sure, but I know that it will include bloodshed. Jackson Kraven is perhaps the most vicious man in the WWA today, maybe ever. Kraven would love to get his hands on Denrol again after defeating him at March Madness. As a stipulation of Kraven’s victory over him, Denrol was supposed to retire. Being addicted to the world of competition however, Denrol just couldn’t stay away. Kraven would love to show him just why he should have stayed retired. Somehow, I don’t think that Denrol’s ability to enrage his opponents is as effective with Jackson Kraven as it is with so many others. Most opponents, when angered, become prone to mistakes. Kraven just gets more dangerous. My money is on Jackson Kraven to go on to meet Tracer Bullet. Harley Trapper vs. We The People (Spike, Joey Edwards, Crimson Warrior, Mr. Internet, and Tim Powers) Handicap Match Harley Trapper has strung along the members of We The People with promises of turning them into an elite unit for the last couple of months. Trapper may have even succeeded if the members of We The People had not come to the realization that they don’t want to be an elite unit of winners; they like being the losers and slackers that they are. To that end, they turned on Trapper one by one. Angered by their betrayal, and obviously discounting his own shoddy treatment of their ranks, Trapper challenged the lot of them to a handicap match. As such, Harley Trapper will face off against Crimson Warrior, Joey Edwards, Spike, Mr. Internet, and Tim Powers. Normally, I would go with the safe bet that five on one is a nearly unconquerable obstacle. But, then I remember that We The People like to lose. They want to lose this match to reclaim their place at the bottom of the barrel. I don’t claim to understand it, I just put my money down for Harley Trapper to put the members of We The People back where they want to be. WWA Rookie Champion Christyan Payne vs. Justin “Air” Bourne It seems fitting that two of the hottest young rookies in the WWA should square off for the Rookie Title at what may be the hottest Pay-Per-View of 2002. Chistyan Payne was making a name for himself before the Horsemen bestowed the mantle of War upon him. Since that time however, he has only intensified. As yet undefeated, “The Jackal” seems to be destined for greater things in the future. Of course, Justin “Air” Bourne stands in his way. Justin Bourne defeated James Beam in a submission match on Fiesta to earn the right to go on to face both Ramsey Kaine and Kash on the same night. In winning that three-way match, despite injuries suffered during training, Bourne staked his claim to the title shot at LDB, as well as his fair share of respect. Both of these men are incredible athletes with aerial arsenals that would make the Royal Air Force jealous. If I were sure that Bourne were at one hundred percent, I might be likely to choose his determination and spirit over Payne’s grim ruthlessness. Unsure of the status of Bourne’s injuries however, and factoring in the knowledge that the other Horsemen will be in the building, I have to believe that “The Jackyl” Christyan Payne will retain his Rookie Title. Michael Stryfe vs. Wes Sanders Ladder Match After losing his Television Title to Ekosyde in a tremendous upset last week on Fiesta, Michael Stryfe will be out for blood. In Birmingham, England, “The Rage” took a wrench to the head from Michael Stryfe, ending in a disqualification for Stryfe. Since the belt cannot change hands on a disqualification, Stryfe retained his hold on the coveted Television Title belt. In Wes’ struggle to wrest the belt from Stryfe’s iron grip, these two awesome men battled to a no-contest decision after brutalizing each other in a steel cage in London. With amazing strength and a capacity for violence that is matched only by Jackson Kraven, Michael Stryfe will be sure to bring everything he has to knock Wes Sanders from his undefeated perch. Furthermore, Stryfe proved that Ladder Matches fall within his realm of dominance with his victory over Troy Walcott for the TV Title. Will Wes Sanders’ extensive martial arts experience, coupled with his unyielding drive to succeed, be enough to stop the runaway train that is Michael Stryfe? Or will Matt Warr’s haunting statement that Sanders can’t win them all become prophesy? In the end, I think that Wes Sanders’ obsession with victory will allow him to walk away with yet another mark in the win column. *Note: At the time of this writing, no changes have been made to this match. With the recent change in the ownership of the TV Title though, there may be modifications to the card. Please remember to look at the final booked card before placing any bets, and don't blame me if you place a bet without all of the information. WWA Tag Team Champions Havoc vs. Grunge and Venom Despite having faced each other numerous times in the ring, neither of these two teams has managed a clear victory over the other. In their first meeting with Havoc, the interference of the Human Condition cost Grunge and Venom the number one contender spot. Since that time, so many months ago, Havoc went on to win the belts from The Human Condition in a vicious, no-disqualification battle, wherein the interference of Rick Venom might have been the deciding factor. Now, Grunge and Venom have the opportunity to show the world, and Havoc, who the real champions are. With the rivalry running high, something else developed over the past couple of weeks that makes this match an emotional one. Deimos’ infatuation with Tiffani Andrews has prompted him to send flowers and write poetry. Tiffani spurned his advances with a slap across the cheek, enraging Deimos. Unable to accept the rejection, Deimos attacked her with his Panic Attack finisher right in front of Rick Venom! Now, both Venom and Deimos are out to utterly destroy the other. Tiffani’s presence may be a very important factor here, preventing Deimos from focusing on the match. On top of this, Grunge has been riding a wave of accomplishment in the WWA, not the least of which is the Cruiserweight Title belt. Will Phobos be able to deal with Grunge while keeping his brother in the match? I’ve been in love before folks, and I can tell you, no man can focus through a broken heart. My money’s on Grunge and Venom. WWA Extreme Champion Eddie Van Dorn vs. Damian Thorne Triple Cage Match - Top Cage Filled With Weapons If Eddie Loses, He Drops To The Bottom Of The Ladder, And Can't Get An Extreme Title Shot Until He Has Earned It Every now and then, I wonder if the WWA goes too far. This match is an example of just that. In a three-tiered cage with the top cage filled with weapons, Eddie Van Dorn and Damian Thorne will do everything in their power to maim and cripple their opponent. Truth to tell, watching a match like this makes me cringe. Watching EVD and Damian Thorne fall from the balcony when “Mighty” Joe proved that he could fly, made me cringe. Damian Thorne and EVD were interrupted in that extreme match by a double countout after Joe unceremoniously disrupted the proceedings. After a submissions-only, extreme battle on Meltdown, Damian Thorne wrested the contender spot from Joe’s Grilling Machine-pressed hands. Damian Thorne is certainly fitting of the moniker, Baddest Mofo on the Show, but he’s facing EVD. Regardless of Damian’s impressive muscle mass advantage over him, EVD has come to embody the extreme spirit of the WWA. No matter who wins this match, I know that I’ll cringe several times. I only hope that the last cringe involves EVD pinning Damian Thorne, because that’s where I’m putting my hard-earned money. The Horsemen (Death, Famine and Pestilence) vs. The 7th Layer (Steve and Kaleb Edmunds, and Jay Jay Da Silva) Survivor Series The 7th Layer has managed to catch the attention of the Horsemen, and that may be a bad thing. The 7th Layer formed soon after “The Nefarious One” Steve Edmunds dropped the Big Bad Animal moniker. Since that time, they have made their presence felt in the WWA by decimating anyone that they could get their collective hands on. “The Hellraiser” Kaleb Edmunds could have been pulled from the darkest imagination of Clive Barker, and Jay Jay Da Silva’s talents can never be underestimated. It’s really no surprise that this dark brotherhood has attracted attention. Shaman, who appears to be the mouthpiece of the Horsemen, made the challenge on Meltdown for this match. According to the card, three of the Horsemen will be in this fight; Shaman fulfilling the role of Famine, Magician a.k.a. Pestilence, and [drumroll, please] Death. Yes, that’s right. After all these months of waiting and speculation, we’ll finally get to see Death in the ring. Everyone is eager to see Death in action and, hopefully, to see him unmasked. Who is Death? I certainly have no idea, but I’m willing to bet that we’ll find out at Labor Day Brawl after the Horsemen defeat the 7th Layer. WWA US Champion Shane Dorian vs. Ian Tense No Disqualification At May Mayhem 2002, Ian Tense took the US Title from Shane Dorian in a three-way, elimination street fight. In that brutal match, which also included Harley Trapper, Dorian and Tense nearly destroyed each other. In the end, Ian Tense walked away victorious. On the July 30th Meltdown, Dorian got his US Title back from Ian Tense and also took a measure of revenge by nearly crippling his nemesis. Now, Ian Tense has returned with a new attitude, but without his doctor’s approval. During the time that Ian Tense has been out, Shane Dorian has run roughshod over the WWA, piling up victories and handing out career-threatening injuries. Every time we see these two get together, all Hell breaks loose, and this time will be no different. Shane Dorian is in the midst of a hot streak since returning to WWA television and reclaiming the US Title. In fact, it appears that this may the prime of his career, even garnering an opportunity to be a contender for the World Title. Inversely, Ian Tense is injured. His doctor has not cleared him for in-ring action, and a bad fall could end his career permanently. Ian has more to lose in this match than Dorian. As much as I like Ian Tense, I can’t help but wonder if he’s jumped into something that he is simply not ready for. I'll take the cautious bet on this one, and throw my money behind Shane Dorian to retain the US Title. Tracer Bullet or Craven Moore vs. Jackson Kraven or Denrol World Title Tournament Finals, No Interference If my previous predictions come to pass, then we’ll be seeing Jackson Kraven take on Tracer Bullet for the most prestigious prize in the sport, the WWA World Heavyweight Championship. This will certainly not be the first time that these men have met in the ring. If past experience is any indication, we can expect a lot from Tracer Bullet. His technical skills and determination have proven to be too much for Kraven in the past. On the other hand, Jackson Kraven seems to become more malicious and sadistic with each passing day. Has Kraven reached a point where Tracer’s skill will be unable to cope with the sheer brutality? I don’t think that’s likely to be the case. My money, when the betting opens on this one, will be on Tracer Bullet. On the other hand, my predictions could be wrong. In the interest of thorough speculation, let’s take a look at the other combinations that could occur. If Craven Moore should beat Tracer Bullet, then we could see another epic clash between the two mammoth powerhouses, Craven Moore and Jackson Kraven. There is nothing but bad blood between these two, and either man would be happy to crush the dreams of the other by snatching the World Title away from the other. So far, Craven Moore has been lucky enough to get the upper hand in overall wins against Jackson Kraven. However, the scales could be balanced in this match. Has Moore been getting by on borrowed time or is he truly the superior athlete? Either way, this matchup would be one for the hall of fame. If this match comes to pass, I’ll place my bet on recorded history and back Craven Moore. If Denrol, the greatest of all earth time, should get past Jackson Kraven, the fans could be treated to an instant classic as Denrol takes on Tracer Bullet. This time, without the alcohol. Both of these men are monumental competitors who will always be remembered as two of the greatest wrestlers this sport has ever known. In fact, under different circumstances, I could even see these two being best of friends. When the World Title is on the line though, neither man will allow their mutual respect to cloud their resolve. As much as I respect Denrol’s talents, his comedic distractions may be his undoing at the hands of the Private Eye, Tracer Bullet. I’d bet on Bullet. If I am wrong on both of my predictions, as unlikely as that seems, then we could end up with Craven Moore and Denrol locking up in the squared-circle for the honor of celebrating with that gold belt. Craven Moore’s indomitable power and size may appear to create an unfair advantage, but Denrol is a master of causing mischief. Not to take anything away from Moore, but he can be distracted, as witnessed when Jackson Kraven caused him to lose the triple threat on Meltdown. Denrol’s most potent weapon may be his ability to enrage his opponents with his antics, forcing them to make mistakes. In my view, this would be the deciding factor in this fantastic battle. If this is the final match, I’ll be betting the farm on Denrol. Regardless of the outcomes, Labor Day Brawl 2002 could possibly be the greatest Pay-Per-View in the history of the WWA, and possibly in the history of wrestling. I wouldn’t miss this for any sum of money, and neither should you. This is Kevin Sage, signing off until the next Pay-Per-View, Birthday Bash. Until then, remember this: A gambler with a system must be, to a greater or lesser extent, insane. Design and Graphics © World Wrestling Alliance 2003
