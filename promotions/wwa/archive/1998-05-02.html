WWA — 2 May 1998

2 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saturday Night Main Event Saturday May 2, 1998 Camera focuses in on Terry and Tony as they look at the camera smiling.... Tony : Welcome to the Saturday Night Main Event. I am here along with Mr. Terry Valentine. Let's get to the first match and get this thing started tonight. Camera zooms in on Gary Trudeau as he introduces the first match.... Gary Trudeau : And approaching the ring now is Vinnie " the bronx " Bomber.... Camera focuses opn Vinnie " the bronx " Bomber as Stone Temple Pilots...Plush Blasts over the P.A.......as the crowd screams wildly at his introduction.....and we see signs that say Vinnie is da man..... Gary Trudeau : And his opponent is GUTTER from the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse.... Camera zooms in on Gutter as he makes his way to the ring as Gutter Ballet plays by Savage over the P.A.... Terry : Well lets see what Vinnie can do.... Tony : Well he has established himself in the ncw already.... Eric : Ok Vinnie grabs Gutter and throws him to the ropes followed by a clothesline.... Johny : Vinnie with a quick jab to the throat.... Terry : Gutter laughs the jab to the throat off... Tony : What would you expect for a bronx war.... Terry : Or from a guy who doesn't know the meaning of taking a bath.... Eric : Vinnie with a modified version of the bronx bomb..... Terry : Lets just call it a less powerful move ok... Johny : Vinnie with the spear.... Tony : Vinnie looks to be in control of this match.... Eric :Vinnie with the B-52 Bomber... Terry : Vinnie with the 1....2....oh wait he pulls him up.... Eric : Whats he doing ...oh no the Bronx Bomb.... Johny : He tells the referee to count to 5.... Tony : The ref counts 1...2...3...4...5.... Terry : Vinnie wins the match via the count to five...what a schmuck....What a grimy stymie move..whatever that means.... Tony : Ok lets get to the next match.... Camera focuses on Gary Trudeau as he gets ready to speak again.... Gary Trudeau : And to the ring first followed by The New Regulators BOB.... Camera Focuses on BOB as he tosses cookies to the fans and we hear BOB's theme..... Gary Trudeau : And his opponent Big Daddy Butler...... Camera focuses on Butler as we hear don't fear the reaper.... Terry : The time has come for the cookie monster vs the cookie crunch.... Tony : If Bob's Driver were here he would bash you... Terry : But he's not hahaha... Eric : Butler attacks BOB.... Johny : BOB smacks Butler and sends him sprawling across the ring... Tony : BOB knocks Butler Down... Terry : And steps on him... Johny : The ref counts 1....2......3......WOW BOB kept his word at stepping on Butler like a bug.... Tony : ( Barely trying to breathe from laughter) BOB gets the win so lets go to Gary once again.... Camera focuses once again on Gary Trudeau.... Gary Trudeau : And our next match will be Fire Storm and his lovely manager Blaze accompanying him... Camera focuses on Fire Storm as he walks to the ring and we hear fire sirens blaring in the background... Gary Trudeau : And his Opponent The fifth Element.... Camera focuses on The Fifth Element as he appraoches the ring and we hear Can you deliver by Armored Saint blast over the P.A...... Tony : Well this should be interesting... Terry : Fire and Paper what else could it be... Eric : Elements got Fire Storm in a front face lock quickly... Terry : but Fire Storm relenquishes the hold nailing him in the stomach.... Tony : Element looks as if he is in pain... Johny : Storm grabs him and body slams him to the mat... Terry : So much for the grand element... Tony : Shut up terry...Element gets slammed to the mat again... Eric : Storms showing he has some talent in the fed... Terry : Element with a quick knee to the mid section on Fire Storm.... Johny : But it doesn't seem to Phase Storm... Tony : It seems Elements quick run is coming to an end.... Terry : Fire Storm with a quick pile driver and the cover up... Tony : 1.....2.....3.....Fire Storm is victorious.... Eric : Well lets get back to Gary.... Gary Trudeau standing close to the ring pointing to a pit where the next match will take place.... Gary Trudeau : The next match is purely a grudge match as we lower both men into the pit Mad Coil vs Rocky Stevens..... Camera focuses on the nervous Rocky Stevens as Mad Coil Smiles evily to his own delight.... Terry : The ref just paddlelocked the cage shut.... Tony : Mad Coil knows what he wants.... Eric : Mad Coil Grabs Stevens and slams him into the wall... Johny : And again as he slams him into the walls of all of the four sides... Tony : Well i think we just saw Rocky Stevens get his shoulder dislocated... Eric : Mad Coil With a quick arm bar take down causing further pain to Stevens shoulder... Terry : Where did my Gummy Bears go to... Tony : Mad Coils wrenching and twisting Stevens shoulder .... Eric : Mad Coil applies his finisher not once but four times....The avalanche Mad Coil Style.... Tony : Stevens is bleeding from his forehead.... Johny : Mad Coil Climbs the rope and exits the pit.... Eric : Mad Coil grabs the mic... MAD COIL : Its time Total Anarchy for you to realise what we do to people who betray and become our enemies.... Terry : That was short and sweet like me... Tony : Oh please ... Johny : To the next match quickly please... Camera focuses in on Gary Trudeau.... Gary Trudeau : Well let me apologise we are having technical difficulties to the sound system..so in this corner Talon....and in the other corner Griffin.. Camera focuses on the two men squaring of in the ring as Talon screams Obscenities at Griffin... Terry : Griffin with a side snap suplex... Tony : Griffin with a gillatine leg drop...But he misses... Eric : Talon with a snapmare to griffin.... Terry : this could be considered the king of the skies match.... Johny : Griffin is definitly favored in this.... Tony : Griffin gets up dazed as Talon attacks him from behind..... Eric : Talon looks up to the locker rooms for support... Terry : Well i don't think anarchy is stupid enough to send anyone to the ring with BOB standing there with a table.... Tony : Griffin with a sleeper hold... Johny : Griffin with a flying clothesline.... Eric : Griffin with a round house kick upside the kisser.... Terry : Griffin grabs Talon and applies a finisher move that is from the late great Griffin from times gone by.... Tony : Griffon for the 1...2...3...and the win lets get to the next match soon.... Camera focuses on DeadBolt as he watches the curtains move as he speaks to them.... DeadBolt : Come on Mr. Steele lets see what you got so we can show you how we handle the situations with Total Anarchy........ Terry : Total Anarchy is cool but so is The House of Pain..... Tony : Oh well lets get to the match... Eric : Look Steeles coming out of the crowd... Johny : Steele with a few moves that seem technically impossible to do but he has done them... Terry : DeadBolt Quickens the pace as he slams Steele down quickly.... Tony : Steele and DeadBolt start nailing each other with rights and lefts.... Eric : Its time to see who is the real loser of this match... Johny : Well it seems that Steele has the tin plated gold for his actions in other feds but what about Dead Bolt... Terry : Dead Bolt attacks Steele with a flurry of massive powerslams and bombs... Tony : Look its Chaos and Raiden charging the ring quickly... Eric : Shaman and Mad Coil come out from nowhere to attack them also.... Johny : Well Anarchy seems tobe fouled tonight at every corner... Terry : Steele has the upper hand as he puts down Dead Bolt in his own finisher the A-Bomb wow That takes some nerve... Eric : Steele with the one ...two...Three... Tony : Steele with the win.... Tony : There seems to be some after math of the match... Terry : Steele seems to be running and arguing with Shaman... Tony : Shaman walks away with little thought guess he was right a warrior knows when to walk away... Eric : Now to Gary Trudeau for the Main Event.... Camera focuses on Gary Trudeau as he prepares for the main event.... Gary Trudeau : Tonights match is a one fall count match... Non-Title bout.... Camera focuses in on Randy Cross as he walks ringside with The House of Pain.... Terry : Cross looks prepared with the house doesn't he... Tony : yeah figures.... Cameras then focuses on Angel Reaper as he prepares for his match with Randy Cross as he walks to ringside...with the Total Anarchy.... Tony : They both attack each other as do both the Stables ... Terry : Hey i don't see Rocky Stevens ... Eric : the report says he has sustained massive shoulder dislocation and tendon damage.... Johny : Both sides arent giving in.... Terry : The ref rings the bell and starts counting... Tony : The ref is calling both men to the ring but he is being ignored... Terry : The ref calls for the bell... Eric : Double elimination... Terry : WHAT?? Randy Cross should have beat that clown... Johny : Guess thats what happens when people start feuds.... Eric : They don't seem to be stopping fighting, This could go on all night... Tony : Well thats all the time we have lets give you the rest if the happenings next time we see each other have fun and see ya soon....
