[Commerical Break End.] [Scene opens with a tape of Joe's interview with Outlaw earlier in the day, in a darken studio.] Joe : Thanks for coming Outlaw. First question. How do you feel after that brutal match with New Star? Outlaw : As you can see (The Outlaw holds up a black cane with a silver tip) I am still feeling the effects of that match. First, the deal was with Darken, but I believe as the World Champion any contract, verbal or otherwise, he enters into anyone holding that belt must honor. The other of course is the simple fact that the HOP currently holds the title so I may choose to wait. But, I will under no circumstances request my shot prior to Darken's Birthday Bash. All of my energy over the next two weeks will be going into repeating as champion. Joe : What do you feel is in the future for House of Pain? In my opinion we are stronger than ever before.....we again hold the World Title, we have the top two contenders for the belt in Randy and myself. We have the extreme title, etc,etc. The House of Pain will again reign supreme.....trust me.....we are always moving ahead. Joe : What were you thoughts when New Star actually stood up after falling off the roof off the cage? Outlaw : I couldn't believe it......I thought to myself "there is no way he will get up from that". Then I saw him crawling back in the ring and I asked myself "Do you have anything left......are you sure you can keep going" and without hesitation I answered yes. Joe :Is there anyone you dream of facing in the ring soon? Outlaw : I don't dream of facing anyone. I look forward to every match and every match brings something different. I never underestimate an opponent, therefore, every opponent is a great challenge. But (The Outlaw smiles) if there was one person and only one that I could face tomorrow......without a doubt it would be Darken. Darken and I have faced on three occasions and I have not one win to show for it. Two losses and one double countout. Although the two losses were not straight up they are still losses...... Joe :Last question. Who do you see as your greatest threat as Extreme Champion? Outlaw: Anyone with the desire to win. To me Extreme is not this silly act of using as many outrageous weapons you can......it's about having a will to win, no matter what the cost. But, if I had to name one person it would be Jak Lancer, my fellow HOP member. Jak is whacked out man......and he will most definitely do anything to win with no regard to the cost. Joe : Any last comments? Outlaw: My last comment will be a bit of advice to my fellow wrestlers. If you want to make it in this federation, if you want a chance to move up the ladder, get recognized, then I strongly suggest you enter your name in Darken's Bash. You are looking at the defending champ and since winning that tournament I have gone on to capture 3 titles. In fact, I've just decided to offer a title shot for the extreme title for whoever becomes the runner-up. The winner already gets a World Title shot, which is going to be me anyway, so, I will give the runner-up a shot at my title. So be there......but prepare to go down.
