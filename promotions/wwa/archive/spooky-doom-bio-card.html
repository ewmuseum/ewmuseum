Spooky Doom Basic Information Nicknames | the Deadkid, the Grim Avenger Real Name | Unknown Height | 5'7" Weight | 192" Date of Birth | 13 September 1992 Home Town | Death Valley, CA Theme | "What's Up people" by Maximum the Hormone More Details Appearance Small and prominently muscular. Spooky Doom hits the gym regularly and it shows. With a bull-like neck, a well-defined upper body and muscular arms; Spooky Doom could pass for a bodybuilder if his build weren't so axed towards agility. Well-tanned caucasian with no distinctive markings. A more muscular take on the classic cruiserweight build. He's still very short at 5' 7. Ring Attire Wears a sleek bone-colored bodysuit open at the shoulders with lime colored crescent-shaped shoulder openings and a black Pac-Man ghost design across the chest, along with matching pleather pants. Wears a hood stitched together to his mask: the hood is white with a lime trim and extends down to the shoulders while the mask is black and horseshoe-shaped around the mouth, with green flames emblem wrapping around the brow like a crown. Uses white wrist tape and flexible black slip-on sneakers to aid in his high-flying. He also sports large white bicep bands, each marked by a black Pacman ghost. Personality Spooky Doom describes himself as some manner of lil' Grim Reaper-like thingy, the nephew of a big shot superstar redneck zombie shootfighter sports entertainer never referred to by name. Growing up in awe of his supernatural uncle, Spooky wanted nothing more than to follow him into professional wrestling, except that the original Grim Reaper of wrestling won't allow any free rides in this business. Denying his nephew access to his company until Spooky proved himself in battle, the Deadkid ended up using his uncle's very famous name so as to open doors to wrestling schools worldwide; training at an American wrestling school before moving to Mexico to ply his trade; his small size and propensity to hit high flying moves being better appreciated there. Since then, Spooky Doom's uncle died. Again. In a manner less embarrassing than the first four times that he died, certainly, but he's dead nonetheless and SOMEBODY needs to take his place. Making up for his inability to teleport in darkness or shoot lightning bolts from his fingertips by putting on rad amazing matches filled with tons of high spots, Spooky Doom joins the wrestling business knowing that Death isn't an option for him. We mean it, he can't afford the vacation days at this point.
