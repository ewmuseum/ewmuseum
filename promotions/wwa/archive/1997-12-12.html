Friday Dec 12, 1997 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows the RIC Athletic Complex. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey, Terry Valentine and Johny Morehouse sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the RIC Athletic Complex on the campus of Rhode Island College in Providence, Rhode Island. We have another great card for you tonight folks. Terry : Yes we do Tony. Tonight is the conclusion of the Northeast Championship Tournament. Tony : That's right; After tonight we will have the Number one wrestler is the NCW. And probably be the number one contender for the CWC National Title. Johny : Well Tony, we certainly do have a great card tonight, as we conclude the NCW's Northeast Championship Title. I'm putting my money on "Wild Thing" after his upset victory over Matt Sanders. Terry : You call it an upset, and I'll call it what it really was. Cheap! There's no way he'll get past Steel Cold unless that idiot Black Scorpion interferes again. Tony : Well my pick is Michael Blackheart, who stunned the NCW with a tremendous bout with "Lobo" Tony Ruiz last week. It's only a matter of time be.....
