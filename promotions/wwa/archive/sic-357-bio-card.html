Sic(357) (bio card)

What this is Roster bio card for the WWA wrestler Sic(357), giving measurements, hometown, entrance theme, style, a description, an entrance narrative, a move list, finishers and a history.
Sic(357) Name: Sic(357) Weight: 246 lbs. Height: 6'2'' Hometown: Detroit, Michigan Entrance Theme: Skin Ticket by Slipknot Primary Style(s): High Flyer/Technical Alignment: Face Description: 357 is lithe in build, and of medium height. He looks more or less like a normal person, like you or me. He has very dark brown/almost black hair which is spiked and at some points shaved to a very short buzz cut. On his upper left arm he has a tatoo of a barcode with the number "357" scrawled in the center of the pattern. The tatoo seems to have been branded into his flesh, as the scar tissue around the pattern would suggest. He has dark brown eyes and a dyed blue, two part braided goatee which reaches down to the bottom of his rib cage. He wears a metal mask, metallic silver in color, that covers the entrire front side of his head, with leather straps connecting around the back. For the mouth, vertical slits are cut into the mask, forming a strange, twisted smile. Generally, in the ring he wears no shirt, coupled with baggy, dark green draw string pants on which a large red dragon symbol is pressed on the upper left leg, and a smaller identical symbol is pressed on the right leg near the bottom, as well as a pair of jet black Dickies steel toed work boots. While entering the ring he wears a black towel over his head and part of his face. Out of the ring he usually wears similar clothing with the exception that he most often wears a pair of dark green cargo pants and an Adidas coat on which an emblem of a jackass over an emblem of an "A" is sewn on the right breast. Entrance: Skin Ticket blasts over the p.a. as the lights go completely out and the crowd goes ape shit. The intro plays out and the intricately complicated percussion of Slipknot is evident as it's timpanies boom over the speakers. After several repetitions of the drum beat, 6 pyrotechnic explosions burst at the head of the ramp leading to the ring in sync with the drums, continuing in this pattern with explosions occuring in different areas several more times. Once the smaller explosions cease, 2 huge jets of flame erupt on opposite sides of the stage at the head of the ramp as the roar of vocals begins, and simultaneously a spot light shines directly on the center of the stage, where 357 stands, his arms extended to the side, his fists clenched in balls and his head tilted back, his face looking to the sky, holding this position for several seconds before lowering his head. The crowd erupts again in cheers and the house lights rise. 357 strides to the ring and leaps onto the ring apron. He takes ahold of the top rope with his hands and hoists his body over them with a side vault. He climbs the nearest turnbuckle and flings his arms into the air, an effort for which the crowds reaction is excellent. In sync with the crowd he motions with his four word slogan. He cuts across his throat (Don't) with his thumb (Fuck) and then points to his chest twice (With This). He jumps off of the turnbuckle backward and backs up to the ropes opposite those closest to the stage. He crouches down with his hands on his upper thighs, cracking his neck from side to side as he awaits the next entrance. 19 most commonly used moves: 1. Death Rattle (Hangman's sitout piledriver,) 2. Romp Stomp (running spinebuster through a table in the corner,) 3. tumbling flipping guillotine leg drop, 4. tornado face buster (180 degree spinning face to knee, like Justin Credible's spinning DDT, but it ends with Triple H's chin crusher) 5. single leg heel kick (occasionally performed with a chair,) 6. forearm shots, 7. hurracanrana, 8. crossface punches, 9. german suplex, 10. tornado DDT into a bulldog (from the turnbuckle,) 11. slingshot somersault senton, 12. chair assisted springboard leg lariat (Air Sabu,) 13. spinning spinebuster (Rhyno spinebuster,) 14. running dropkick to the face (when opponent is sitting at the bottom of the turnbuckle, in tree of woe position, etc.) with a chair thrown into opponent's face before the dropkick connects, 15. lariat (like Stone Cold's clotheslines where he ends on his knees,) 16. corkscrew chokeslam (Show Stopper, swinging chokeslam,) 17. Kataha Jime (Tazzmission,) 18. running knee to the midsection, 19. STO backbreaker into a Downward Spiral/Flatliner Finishers: Finisher(1): 357 Justice A pedigree into a jumping inverted DDT (Wham Bam Thankyou Ma'am) Finisher(2): Terminal Velocity Toad Splash A high elevation, high impact inverted frog splash (357 holds his arms and legs behind his back and grabs his ankles with his hands before he lands the splash) Finisher(3): Straight Edge Razor A reverse twist of fate/reverse DDT into a diamond cutter/Roll the Dice History: 357 was born and raised in the mean streets of Detroit. At a young age he was taken in by a pastor when his parents were seperated from him due to drug problems. His adopted father taught him the ways of God and told him that he knew that 357 was to bring the Word of God into the world of professional wrestling. 357 knew then that it was true. He immediately went to California upon turning 18 so that he could study in the Asylum wrestling school. He learned the basics of wrestling there. Soon after, he trained extensively with the hardcore legend, Terry Funk. He developed his unique extreme-high flying style on his own by facing the likes of persons such as Sabu, Rob Van Dam, and the Sandman. His short and what appeared to be an unpromising wrestling career was abruptly halted after he was brutally mugged and then hit with a high impact finisher by his former best friend ......or so we thought. 357 has returned to the wrestling business to seek retribution for the damage caused by his former close friend by showing the entire world his true potential as well as to clean up the business and world that he once deeply respected and loved. He has dropped the man of God gimmick for the time being, realizing that he must solve the problems that lie before him in his own way if they are to be overcome in his own time. As of late, 357 has become more focused, more intense. He calls himself a "machine" and a "mechanized man," and rightly so. As of now he works as a well oiled machine, operating purely on adrenaline and rage, living for the moment and going by the motto "go big or go out." 357's name is derived of one law, 357's Law: 357; being in nature both man and machine of superior physicality, can reach terminal velocity, which is almost certainly followed by the Toad Splash, a move known to drive opponents deep into the mat, in the following amount of milliseconds.....357
