The Scouting Report - Issue 28

28 Nov 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings and salutations WWA fans, it’s your esteemed and valued Director of Talent back with all the dirt and opinions you can handle with a new Scouting Report. -First up some news on new GenX Champion Venny LeTrav. -At a Detroit house-show WWA Fiesta Superstar Venny LeTrav had a small confrontation with a drunken fan that emptied his tobacco spit cup onto The Cajun's head after he was thrown into the barricade by none other than Jimmi Zion. The Cajun immediately lunged at the man and yanked him over the railing where officials and even Jimmi Zion himself had to hold the 29-year-old wrestler from attacking the young man, who was only in his mid-twenties and was also intoxicated. Security removed the fan from the building and Venny got on the mic to apologize to the audience for the delay. The match was abruptly restarted after Venny emptied a bottle of water on his head. The fans seemed to have enjoyed the near-beating as well as the match. -Of course you have to remember that Venny is a volatile guy but that brings me to my next point. Everyone who can should try and check out our WWA house shows. If you can’t get the EWA then that’s the best place to check out the WWA’s new talent. Keep on the look-out for tickets going on sale. -Speaking of new talent, congratulations to GAROU who is the most recent superstar to get called up from the EWA. The new-comer has been impressing lately and will join other call-ups such as Desolation, Chuckster, Khaled, and Tommy Starr on Meltdown. -Now on to Fiesta. -Life just hasn’t been that great for Joey K lately. After being put in the hospital thanks to his recent altercations with Dave Harley he was attacked last Monday by his own partner in Enigma. He’s been on the outs with the Forsaken Souls leader from some time so Enigma thought he’d give Joey his own special version of a funeral. However the surprise came when it was Kash serving as the Priest. Enigma and Kash don’t like each other and it should come as no surprise that the two are on a collision course. -Troy Steffy has not been in a good mood lately. He blamed his man the American Eagle for losing the match at Labor Day Brawl on purpose to help Bridge. Eagle denied it of course but got fired for his innocence. -Next Keru no Akrio took on Jimmi Zion in which showed an amazing match between both men. Keru no doubt thought he had Jimmi beat thanks to the strength advantage but Jimmi gave it is his all but submitted to Zion at the end. Keru then laid an envelope at Jimmi’s chest. One has to wonder if that’s Keru’s reply to whether he will join CPE or not. -“Rage” Wes Sanders had his long awaited match against his little brother Chivo that night. Chivo tried his best but thanks to Mal trying to help out the younger Sanders Wes got hold of Rookie and used it to get the win. This has set up a match between Sanders and Somers for next week which should leave the fans drooling. Of course Sanders probably shouldn’t have messed with X-Corp as it gave Kash a target and he used that opportunity to the fullest, choking out Sanders. -It seems Troy wasn’t done yet as he keeps pawning the blame in his conspiracy theory that Bridge is why neither he or Raine is the commissioner. What’s more disturbing is that Nate is starting to believe him. If I was Raine I’d never turn my back on Troy because he’s always got something planned. -In the shock of the night the two rookies Matt Keith and Alex Knight, who hate each other I might add, beat the veterans Mal Somers and Chance in a tag-team contest. Of course Keith’s flunky JC Swinger had helped with a chair-shot right to Somers. I hate to agree with Mikal but he’s right the two are the Sting and Lex Lugar of the WWA because they started a Pier 6 right after their match. -The Tokyo Showdown is my nomination for match of the night as we saw Dave Harley’s brainchild come to light. The match took place on a scale replica of Tokyo and in order to win the WWA GenX Title, you had to climb up to the top of Tokyo Tower and pull it down. And of course they had to wear costumes, Venny as Godzilla, Dave as King Kong, and Bonafide as…a giant chicken. Lot’s of laughs with that. In the end Venny got the win and the GenX title. -At the end of the night we saw Enigma advance to the Intercontinental Title Tournament with a win over Fuel. Still the CPE member put up a valiant effort and upped his stock in the locker-room’s eyes. Enigma brings new meaning to the term sore winner as after the match he took out Fuel with a steel chair. One has to wonder if that will work when he goes against Venny LeTrav in the finals next week. -Now on to the next Meltdown -David “Sniper” Wattsoner has put head booker Matt Warr between a rock and a hard place. Wattsoner has been announced in a talent trade to Fiesta but he’s also the reigning TV Champion. If he wins against “Psycho” Steven Murphy on Monday then the title goes with him. If I know Warr he’ll have a trick up his sleeve to make sure that doesn’t happen. -Tracer Bullet and Damian Thorne hate each other. That’s evident. After his blatant attack on Tracer last week the match has been set up. Now these will attempt to tear each other apart. Although Tracer has had a new attitude these last weeks he still has some sense of honor. That’s not true when it comes to Thorne. Thorne has attacked women, fans, and every wrestler in the locker-room. But don’t count Tracer out. -And in the main event of the night we get to see Wattsoner’s Brawl. In a match that Wattsoner will referee we have four sets of team; Grunge and Leviathan, Nick Adams and Liam Jenson, The Chuckster and Desolation, and Tommy Starr and Shaman. Wattsoner’s brawl is an old set-up that seems like Warr games with two rings surrounded by a cage. Once there is only one team standing the two partners will brawl until one is thrown in a tub of beer. The winner gets an automatic entry into the Birthday Brawl. Nick Adams and Liam Jenson would be my bet as they seem to be the only two who don’t hate each other but with Nick hurt who knows. It could be anyone’s game. Well until next time this is Josh Wilson. Be sure to watch Meltdown and Fiesta. You won’t be disappointed. Ciao.
