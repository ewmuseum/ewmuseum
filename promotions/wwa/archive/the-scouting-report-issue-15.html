The Scouting Report - Issue 15

28 Nov 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome back to the Scouting Report all you rabid WWA fans. Your beloved Director of Talent Josh Wilson is here to once again give you the scoop on the goings on of the WWA. -Last week I reported that Justin Bourne is on his way back to the WWA but he’s chosen to take a pit stop by the EWA to work off some of his ring rust before his WWA début. I caught his match on EWA television and I can safely say that he doesn’t have much of it. -Speaking of the EWA there is some real talent there. Soon everybody here in the states will be able to catch all the action Europe is enjoying. In the next Fiesta the EWA roster will invade with the first Pain Games and will crown a new WWA European Champion. Check it out. -We have just gotten finished with March Madness and I can safely say that the PPV was one of the best I’ve seen in quite some time. I’m going to give a rundown of the matches and what I thought of the outcomes. -The Tag-team number one contendership was fought between the rookie team of the Brother Delucci's and the Fiesta enforcers of Keru no Akiro and Evan “Loco” Motive. I can’t say I was surprised with the Italians picking up the victory because they have really been impressing lately. -The question is can the rookie team keep up with the big boys of Havoc, the Cell, and X-Corp. With all these teams vying for the gold the tag-team ranks are picking up more than ever. -The GenX title match between the champion James Beam and Jimmi Zion promised to deliver high-flying, hardcore action with it being a Tables, Ladders, and Chairs Match. The action was great and James Beam is really coming up in the division. However I was a bit disappointed that the match couldn’t have ended without outside interference. -James Curzon and Showtime are extremely funny but who was that guy who is being called “The American Male.” No it’s not Scotty Riggs or Marcus Bagwell. The question you have to ask is “Isn’t that name still trademarked?” -The TV Title is quickly becoming the most sought after belt in the WWA next to the World Title with this many viable contenders. Grunge managed to pull out the victory over Joey K, Venny LeTrav, and Nick Adams. Still this feud is far from over with all four men deserving of titles. -In that match is what many are calling one of the shocks of the match with Nick Adams accidentally hitting Grunge’s wife Julie with the chair. I can assure you that I spoke with Grunge after the match and Julie is fine. Even though it is disturbing to see something like that happen to a women remember they are still professionals. They know what they are doing. -Many are calling the X-Corp win over the Cell the match of the night. Of course how could it not be with the comedy of Rookie’s commentary and the hellish action that happened in the cage? These two teams have taken the Tag-Team Division up a notch and Dave Harley and Mal Somers are worthy Tag-Team Champions. -Speaking of that match many have said even though it was a great sight, the cage buckling couldn’t really happen. Well I recently talked to a friend of mine who happens to be a physicist and he assured me that metal does expand when heated and this could happen, although like said in JFK, “physics could explain how a elephant can hang off a cliff with his tale rapped around a small plant.” -Mal Somers looks like he’ll have a lot of troubles on the horizon what with the heated Tag-Team division and now Eddie Van Dorn on his back. His stealing rookie was a welcome surprise. -Havoc and the Veteranz match was what it was booked to be, to prove which team is the best in WWA history. Havoc came out on top, which was a bit of a surprise. I thought with it being the Veteranz last match they might pull out the win. -I really do hope that this isn’t the last we’ll see of the Veteranz. They give a rich history to the WWA Tag Division and in a way, are the elder statesmen of the lockroom. -Chance and Denrol seemed to serve as a springboard for Chance to get to the main event. Although it made me stand up and notice Chance even more it also made me notice two things about Denrol. He truly deserves the name of the WWA Franchise and he like to help the rookies out a bit. -I hope Denrol doesn’t stay away for too long. Things won’t be the same without him and I’ll miss the pizza that he sometimes brings around from his restaurants. -Tracer Bullet and Enigma showed why Bullet is a future hall of famer. His way around the I Quit match was creative. He used mind games the way Enigma likes too. -I think after this Tracer should get back in the main event picture and send Enigma to the US Title. Both are very deserving. -Vik Ventressa vs. Jackson Kraven promised to be a blood bath and that it was. Jackson came out on top, which doesn’t surprise me too much. This was Vik’s last match and he will be missed. I think he could have had one last World Title run in him. -Speaking of that match the spot with Troy Steffy was pure comedy. At least the WWA writer remembered to flush. -The World Title match was a great match with Ian Tense tricking Kash and Damian Thorne out of the belt. Many have accused Ian of backstage politicking but I can assure you that that stuff does not go around in the WWA locker-room. We aren’t the WWE people. -And with the outcome of that match we have a World Title race heating up. Wes Sanders was on the show and made it clear that after his injury he wants another stab at it. And now Jackson seems to have stuck his way into the picture. Add Kash and Thorne and Tense might be in trouble. Well the card for Meltdown isn’t out yet but next week I will give a rundown of that night and will talk more about the going ons of the WWA. Ciao.
