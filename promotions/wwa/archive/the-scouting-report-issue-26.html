The Scouting Report - Issue 26

28 Nov 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings and salutations WWA fans, it’s your esteemed and valued Director of Talent back with all the dirt and opinions you can handle with a new Scouting Report. Although normally a TSR would have been out last week I had to take a special trip to Texas. I was actually shopping for a specially designed ring and I’m not talking a Wrestling Ring. Major upheavals in the personal life of your good friend here. Last week was also special because of the start of college football and I’m happy to say my beloved Sooners came out on top. And unlike that announcer on a show I will remain nameless about I actually went to school there so bully for me. -This weekend will be huge for all WWA fans as we have the first PPV of the successful WWA Roster Split. Hey we show the WWE how to do it right fans. And since it’s so close to that time I’m going to give you the rundown, no not the Rock movie, of all the matches. -In the first match of the night we have a treat for all of you EWA fans. A first for the WWA as the European Title will be settled on a WWA PPV. Desolation recently came out on top in a match with former champion Mystical Illusions and the Muppetman. The same night Brad Polaris beat new WWA Superstar Khaled to earn the right to face the champion in a huge WWA night. Desolation brags that he’s the one who sent former EWA star Chuckster packing to the WWA but Brad thinks he’s got his number. -“Psycho” Steven Murphy is a man who thrives on controversy. And recently for assaulting a man on Insider no less he was sent to prison. This is what has spurned him to challenge his long-time rival The Chuckster to a Prison Match with the loser having to be locked away with UWF’s Bum Boozler. Although Murphy was the one to come up with this dangerous match The Chuckster has been on a role lately beating such veterans as Eddie Van Dorn. Also you have to remember Chuck did beat Murphy for the European Title when both were in the EWA. One has to wonder who will come out on top…and who will come out on bottom. Yes a bad pun. -Fiesta has had a power struggle as of late. The assistant commissioner Troy Steffy has made it clear that he wants the top spot over his boss Nathanial Raine. He stuck the first blow having the returning American Eagle assault the boss. While he was out Troy ran Fiesta with his own sickening desires till an order from Raine caused X-Corp trainer Bridge to be named “Assistant Assistant Commissioner.” Not only that the hatred between Eagle and Raine’s friend Keru no Akiro has been bubbling over. CEO Harrington has had enough of this mess and has ordered this to be settled with a contest between Akiro and Eagle to solve who will be the commissioner and who will be let go. Eagle is a former TV Champion but remember Akiro is a wild card with his emphasis on chaos. -Next is an event threatening to bubble over. David “Sniper” Wattsoner must put his newly won TV Title on the line against three men, one being his stablemate and fellow veteran Shaman. DO member Tommy Starr has also thrown his hat in the ring and since Meltdown head booker Matt Warr hates Cold Pain Enterprises so much he’s thrown his man Liam Jenson in the match to make sure CPE doesn’t walk out with the title. This cage match is special though. One has to pin his opponent then exit the cage to win. One would think CPE has the advantage but Starr and Liam have been working well together lately. -Dave Harley has promised to put the X back in the GenX division but one man has made it his mission to make sure that doesn’t happen. Troy Steffy hates X-Corp and has been trying in vain to get the title off Harley. Mr. Too Hot for Monday Night has risen to occasion each time however causing Steffy to unveil a huge challenge, a GenX gauntlet match. First is an Inferno match with Fiesta’s resident pyro Fuel. Next Harley would have to get back the first ever GenX champion Jimmi Zion in a ladder match and we all know his expertise in that. Finally the winner will have to face Harley’s arch enemy Joey K in a cage match. It looks good for Joey on paper but never count out X-Corp. -The Forsaken Souls will face 2/3rds of X-Corp in a graveyard match. We Sanders hates Mal Somers. And it’s extremely well documented how much Chance and Enigma hates each other. That being said recent events have made people wonder how much Enigma and Sanders are on the same page. They’ll have to be to get back at their rivals. All men have excelled at hardcore with Sanders, Chance, and Enigma all holding the Extreme Title at one point in their careers. And Mal held the former EWA Extreme Title. Still Enigma is the only one to have ever been in a graveyard match back when he lost to Se7en. And the real question, will Rookie be there? -Many are calling the Clockwork Orange match between Grunge and the challenger Leviathan the stealer of the night. Its real simple Grunge has the United States title and Levi wants it. Grunge chose this unique weapons match after a stipulations match last Meltdown in which Levi walked out saying he doesn’t care. These two men hate each other and on paper it looks like the advantage goes to Levi, after all the Devil’s Own leader is one of the most blood thirsty cruel men to have participated in the sport. But if that’s true then why did Grunge choose this match? And why has Grunge been trying to research Levi’s past? One thing is for certain this will be a blood bath. And remember there have been no pins in this long feud. It’s really anybody’s game. -And in the main event of the night WWA Champion Kash will put the title on the line against N.I.A. Nick Adams who won the right at the Independence Day Rumble. Nick has all the tools needed to be a champion and Kash is mad at the disrespect he’s been shown towards challengers. Still there are so many factors in this match. One is that the title is still missing thanks to the White Ninja and Tracer isn’t any closer to finding the culprit. Nick has also been distracted lately what with the new look and his hatred of Damian Thorne. Both men will have to put aside these other factors if they have any hope of winning. -Also don’t forget fans that the PPV isn’t all. We also have a pre-show with Damian Thorne facing Fate, Venny LeTrav and Bonafide squaring off, and Matt Keith and Alex Knight hoping to squash their problems. Before I go I’d just like to give a shout-out to old friends [name withheld] and [name withheld] who both have birthdays this weekend. Have a good one guys. Well until next time this is Josh Wilson. Be sure to watch Meltdown and Fiesta. You won’t be disappointed. Ciao.
