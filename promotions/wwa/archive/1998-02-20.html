Terry : Segier catches his arm, and drives him down into an armbar submission. Johny : Shades of the Crockett and Havok match from earlier. Tony : Cross rolls out of the armbar, and applies a short arm scissors submission. Terry : Segier into the ropes and both men get to their feet. Johny : Well this match is for the use of the figure four, but it looks like this match will show more submissions then just that. Tony : I agree, Johny. These two men are out to prove that they don’t need to rely on the figure four, but it will be the only way to win. Terry : Segier and Cross lock up and Cross applies a half chicken wing. Tony : Segier with a standing switch loops behind Cross, reversing the hold. Johny : Cross swiftly turns the hold into a hip toss, taking Segier to the mat. Terry : Outside the ring you can hear the stable members shouting for their respective teamates. Tony : Cross looks like he’s going for the Figure Four! Johny : Instead Cross slingshots Segier across the ring. Terry : Look at the twisted smile on Cross’s face. Tony : He’s acting as though this is a walk in the park. Johny : Cross approaches the fallen Segier. Terry : Segier leaps to his feet and dazes Cross with a tremendous European uppercut, and another one. Tony : Cross is dazed. Johny : Segier executes an inverted atomic drop, and follows it up with a scoop slam. Terry : I guess the walk in the park is starting to get rained on. Tony : Segier grapevines the right leg of Cross, and wrenches back. Johny : Segier grabs the ropes for extra leverage. Tony : A page right out of Cross’s book. Terry : Segier breaks the hold. Johny : Segier holds down the right leg of Cross and nails Cross’s knee with a double knee smash. Tony : Segier backs off and Cross slowly gets to his feet. Terry : Segier tries for a single leg takedown, but Cross counters with a Brazillian armlock, similar to Aric Crockett’s Punisher hold. Johny : Talk about pulling a move out of thin air. Tony : He pulled the move from somewhere but it wasn’t from thin air. Johny : Easy Tony this is a family show. Terry : Segier reaches the ropes and Cross breaks the hold. Tony : Segier to his feet. Johny : Cross executes a beautiful single leg takedown, and immediatly follows up with a knee lock submission. Terry : Cross grabs the ropes for leverage. Tony : I guess turnabout is fair play when your trademark move is on the line. Johny : How can you call it a trademark move? Tony : What do you mean? Johny : Neither man made the move. There is no special modification on it. They just ripped it off whoever first used it. Terry : What are you saying? Johny : I’m saying that this match is a waste of time. Who cares what hold a man uses as long as you win. Tony : Wow, I never would’ve expected to hear that from you. Terry : I’m impressed. Tony : Cross breaks the kneelock and pulls Segier to his feet. Johny : Segier stuns Cross with a thumb to the eye, and follows it up with a knee breaker. Terry : That idiot Segier just dropped Cross on the knee that was just worn down in the hold. Johny : I have no idea. Terry : Look at the shirt the Outlaw is wearing! It says House of Pain. Does this mean... Tony : Back in the ring Cross has gotten to his feet first and he tries for the figure Four! Outlaw is down to ringside. Johny : Cross has the Figure Four on Segier. Terry : It’s only a matter of time before Segier submits. Tony : No Segier is turning Cross over to reverse it. Johny : Segier has Cross over! Tony : The ref is checking to see of Cross submits. Terry : What is the Outlaw doing? Johny : Outlaw has his New england Title. Tony : Outlaw just cold cocked Segier with the Title! The ref didn't see it! Terry : Outlaw drop back to the floor and taunts the UKA cage. Johny : Cross rolls Segier back over and his shoulders are to the mat. Tony : This could be the end. Terry : 1-2-3. Cross wins. Johny : The Outlaw back up into the ring. Cross is smiling and says something to Outlaw. Terry : The two champions shake hands and give a quick hug. I think I am going to cry.
