WWA — 19 May 1999

19 May 1999

Wednesday May 19, 1999 ARE YOU READYYYY.. I CAN'T HEAR YOU.... I SAID ARE YOU READY FOR THE...... The logo shatters as the camera scans about the New Orleans Arena. Various signs can be seen..'There a Doctor in the house?', 'Denrol is like hockey..NO BALLS!', 'HAMMER TIME', and 'Tell it Preacherman!'. Finally the camera pans down on Rick Harrington and Steve Colon. Rick : Welcome WWA FANS! We must say sorry for last week but that god damned dog show pre-empted us! Steve : I'd kill that damn pink mop! Rick : That was a prize winning poodle. Steve : Earmuffs! Rick : Our main event should be classic..The self proclaimed Simply the Best Flynn Steele against an old foe..the House of Pain's Todd Logan. Steve (sniffing about): I don't think they cleanned it all up. But the best match has to be Washington against Preacher, a pair of great rookies trying to get higher up the charts. Let's go straight to the first match. Rick : Well, for our first match up, we have newcomer, Chad Goodman making his WWA singles debut. Steve : It'll be interesting. Let's go down to Gary for the introductions... Gary Trudeau : The following contest is scheduled for one fall, with a TV time limit of ten minutes. Introducing at this time, already standing in the ring, weighing in at 250lbs, Mr. Internet!!!! Mr. Internet sneers at some of the fans as he paces around the ring. A few scattered boos are heard. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, making his way to the ringside area from Virginia Beach, Virginia, weighing in at 225lbs, Chad Goodman!!!! "Anyway You want It" by Journey pumps over the PA as Chad Goodman makes his way from behind the curtain. Goodman wildly pumps his fist to the crowd at the entrance way before beginning to trot down the aisle. He smiles widely as he makes it point to slap as many hands as he can. Goodman steps up into the ring and does a quick run of the ropes. Finally he goes over to a corner and climbs up to the second turnbuckle. Facing the fans he rapidly pumps his fist. Rick : Well, you can say one thing. The kid is psyched. Steve : No doubt. Looks like we are ready to go as Mr. Internet comes charging out of his corner. *Ding*ding*ding* Rick : Mr. Internet charges at Goodman and immediately starts throwing wild haymakers. Goodman is under assault as Internet takes him and hip tosses him out of the corner. Mr. Internet in control from the bell. The computer geek following up with another hip toss.. Chad's clutching his back in pain as his lands hard onto the mat. Steve : Welcome to the big time, rookie. Rick : Third time for Mr. Internet as he goes for the hiptoss. Chad with great agility! He flips out of the toss onto his feet. As Mr. Internet spins about he gets could by a vicious clothesline. Steve : The tide could be turning in this one. Goodman runs over into the ropes. High running dropkick. Man, check out his head bounce back like on of those bobbing head toys. Rick : High risk move as Chad hops up onto the top rope. Nice looking spingboard moonsault, goes for the cover. Two only as he forgets to hook the leg. Steve : Typical rookie mistake. The crowd's getting behind him, though! Give me a break..that fist pumping went out with Arsenio. Rick (makes the annoying fist pumping): Woof Woof Woof! Anyways, looks like he wants to end this now. Steve : Mr. Internet starting to stir as he runs across to the ropes..springboard..Springboard guillotine legdrop! That's the Flashback!! Rick : Woah! What impact!! Did you see Mr. Internet's body go flying off the mat?!? Steve : Goodman makes the cover 1....2...3!!!!! This one's over. Gary Trudeau : Here is your winner in a time of 2:33, Chad Goodman!!! Goodman grabs the mic from Gary... Goodman (breathing a little heavily): There's plenty more where that came from baby!! Rock on!! Goodman drops the mic as "Anyway you want it" by Journey cranks up over the arena. Rick : Well, there you have it. Rookie, Chad Goodman looking very sharp in his singles debut. When we return, we'll have the second of our rookie showcase matches as the Rookie Ring champion, Joey Styles against Spike. A pan out over the crowd fades into a commerical. Rookie Chad Goodman will be signing autographs tomorrow at the New Orleans Wal-Mart from 12 noon until 4. Bring all the kids out to see this rising young star. Half of all the proceeds go to the United Way. It should be a fun afternoon for the entire family! And then the camera fades in.... Rick : The next match has the former WWA rookie champion, "Rolemodel" Paul Styles wrestling Spike. Spike is already in the ring. Gary Trudeau : The following match up is schedualed for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Introducing first.. from Unkwon places, weighing in at 219 pounds .. the "Rolemodel" Pual Styles! "Rolemodel" Paul Styles walks out in baby-blue tights with white trim, the style of Bret Hart, under an unbuttoned Kansas City Royals jacket. He also has on a pair of small, round sunglasses and short, neatly combed blond hairs comes out from the locker room. On his right is Paul's longtime girlfriend, Jessica, who is slightly shorter then the man, probably about 5'8. She is wearing a light blue T-shirt and jeans, and a pair of small, retangular sunglasses with tranparent baby-blue tint, the same as Paul's tights. She doesn't have the look of a supermodel like a Sabel or Debra, but more of a girl nextdoor look, like a Stephanie McMahon. They both walk to the ring, Paul with a smug arrogant look on his face, Jessica just looking at Paul and holding his right arm as the crowd just watches them. Once they reach the ring Jessica goes up the ringsteps and down the apron first and hold the middle and top rope apart as Paul gets in. The ref then comes over and holds the ropes open for Jessica as Paul watches. Jessica then removes Paul's jacket and sunglasses and leaves the ring and goes to Paul's corner. Ding Ding Ding Rick : Well Spike charges right at Paul, who simply side steps and Spike flys into the corner. Paul jumps right on the situation hammering Spike in the back with fists. Steve : But Spike turns around and nails Paul with a right hand, and it launches Paul halfway across the ring. Paul isn't the toughest wrestler in the WWA is he, Rick? Rick (laughing a little): No, I don't think so after seeing that. Steve : Spike is pulling Paul up to his feet, and just drove him back down witha clothesline that almost took Paul's head off. Rick : Spike is all over Paul, now peppering him with shots to the face. But now he pulls him back to his feet and whips him into the ropes, and a HUGE backdrop from Spike. Steve : This match isn't working out too well for Paul. Rick : Spike is all over him and he pulls him back to his feet again, and Paul snuck in a low blow when the ref had his back turned talking to Jessica on the outside. Steve : Paul really took some rough shots, but he looks to have things back in control as he throws Spike into the ropes and connects with a knee to the midsection, followed but a nice Russian leg-sweep. Paul is just looking down at Spike now and has a big smile on his face as he just nudges him in the ribs with his left boot, as though he was checking to see if he's dead. Rick : And he drops a knee into the throat of Spike, and now Paul is trash talking with a few fans as Spike rolls around holding his throat. Steve : Paul looking to be having fun now. He is waiting for Spike to slowly get up, he walks up behind him, AND THERE'S HIS FINISHER, THE HARDCORE EXTREME DREAM! Rick : What? That's his finisher? It's a damn sleeper! Steve : He told me earlier it was his finisher and he would put Spike away with it. I dunno whats Hardcore or Extreme about it, but it his finisher all the same. Rick : Well so much for the finisher, Spike just nailed Paul with a jawbreaker, that hit so hard Paul stumbled backwards right out of the ring. Steve : Jessica is rushing over to comfort Paul, but Spike reaches out and pulls Paul back in by the hair before she can even get there. Spike pulls Paul to the middle of the ring and slams a forearm across Paul's head that sends Paul to the mat. Rick : The ref is talking to Spike about the hair pull but Paul is reaching into his right boot and just pulled out what looks like a pair of brass knuckles and puts it on his right hand. Steve : Spike and the ref didn't see it, as Spike comes over and sets Paul up for something, POWERBOMB... NO! Rick : As soon as Spike got Paul all the way up for it, Paul just nailed Spike right between the eyes with those brass knuckles and Spike fell like a bag of rocks with Paul on top, and the ref didn't see it, 1.... 2.....3!!!!! Ding Ding Ding Gary Trudeau : This winner of this match, "Rolemodel" Paul Styles! Steve : Paul pulls out a cheap win but Jessica seems happy enough as she rushes into the ring and is helping Paul to his feet, and Jessica just took the brass knuckles out of Paul's hand and put them in a pocket of her jeans. Rick : Paul and Jessica are leaving as this crowd starts to boo them pretty loudly after the cheap win, but they really don't seem to the care. The ref is still helping Spike who is still out of it. We will return to the SLAM after this message.... A few short car lot commercals and SLAM returns... Rick : Folks! you missed what happened in the back during the break.. A video replay comes on in the back. the Sitcom Fathers rush into Joey Style's locker. ALF comes and blocks off Jessica. Mr. Brady has Styles over his knee..he's spanking him!! can we show this on TV?. Seever : Well Mr. Styles you cheated to recieve your Rookie Ring, and like we say, people who cheat get their due. This is for all the children out there who think that cheating makes a winner. Now count along with me. The crowd chants up to eight before he lets Style up. The Rolemodel is SHOCKED! As the tag team leaves, Jessica comes and helps the enraged Styles up. Rick : Wow..that was..um.. Steve : Interesting. Rick : Yeah. Ok fans, next up..Ant Banks against Dr. Slash. Gary Trudeau : The next match of the eveing puts two Rookies together in a 20 minute time limit match, with one fall. Introducing first hailing from Vancouver BC. He is Doctor Slash Hayes! "Doctor Love" by Kiss starts over the PA. Skid and Hayes enter to a mixed reacion from the fans.. The duo walk to the ring. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent... Haiing from Compton, CA he is Ant Banks! "Straight Outta Compton" by NWA starts over the PA. The crowd boos the Rookie on his way to the ring. He mearly smiles an arrogant smile and procedds to the ring. Rick : This should be a good match here Steve. Steve : Definately! Wait.. It looks like Ant Banks is calling for a mic. Ant Banks : Shut up pukes! I was talking to my bud the Doctor over here. And we have decided to NOT WRESTLE tonight! (The crowd boos) SHUT UP! Harrington! Thisngs are going to change around here! You don't call the shots anymore! WE DO! Banks throws the mic down. Banks and Hayes get out of the ring, joining them is Skid... Rick : Damn it! What the hell is this! Ricky drops his head set and runs up to Dr Slash Hayes, Skid and Ant Banks, yelling! Steve : You would think that he would learn. Ricky! Get back here! Rick (Walking back and turning to talk): Just remember who pays your check, you bums! Steve : Yeah Rick, your Uncle... Rick : I wasn't talking to you! Who's next! Uh.... The trio leave to the booing of the crowd. They simple yell right back at them! Steve : Indeed. Our next bout is looking good. It features two young, up coming combatant. Rick : That's right. Fredrick T. Washington will step up against the Preacher of Pain. Steve : This should be one hell of a match, Ricky. Rick : Knock it off, Stevey. Oh, looks like we're ready. Take it away Gary.. Gary Trudeau : Thanks guys. Okay. Our next match for you lovely fans this evening, is shedualed for one fall, with of course .. a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first. He comes from the city of 'Brotherly Love'. He weighs in at 255 pounds ... Fredrick T. Washington! "Walk" by Kilgore begins to play of the sound system. Fredrick Washington steps out from behind the curtain. His name appeares on the big screen in blue boldfaced letters. He makes his way to the ring with a mixed reaction in the background. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent. He brings his Technical Martial Arts skills to us from Atlanta, Georgia. He weighs in at 320 pounds .. the Preacher of Pain!! "In-A-Gadda-Da-Vida" By Iron Butterfly starts to play over the PA system. Preacher makes his way to the ring, as the lights are dimed. He gets to the stop of the steps by ringside, and raises the lights. He steps into the ring.. Steve : What a copy off that other guy.. Rick : Anyway, both men seem to be ready. ding...ding...ding Steve : Yes! Rick : What...? Steve : I don't know. (stands up) Hey BEER MAN! Rick : Oh my god... Anyway. Fredrick off to the attack. Fist of fire .. none stop. The both are returning lightening quick punches. These guys are damn good! Steve (sits down with a cold beer in hand) This is the life. (takes a sip) Lets go Freddy! Rick : No! Don't call him when he's in combat! Steve (fredrick leaning over the ropes): You're nothing, you damn drunk. Where's Betty Ford when you need her?! Rick : Quick roll up by Preacher! Only a two. You shouldn't do that, man! Steve : Preacher took a Side Kick to the face! Preacher able to shake that off, however. Poke to the eye, Knee Thrust to the midsection .. Beautiful Butterfly Suplex! Rick : Tremendous combo by Preacher. Preacher back to work.. Misses with the Fist Drop from the second turnbuckle! Steve : Fredrick .. Oh Damn! Missile Drop Kick to the face! He did that on the reboun from the rope, Ricky! Rick : Fredrick not done yet .. he's taunting Preacher to get up.. Spin Hell Kick to the face .. as Preacher was getting up! Steve : Slingshot into the tournbuckle by Frdrick! Preacher is holding his forehead. He may have been busted open.. Rick : NO! It was a prank! Precher with a thumb to the eye, and a Spinning Leg Sweep! Preacher right away, Full Nelson locked on .. good! Steve : Look at the drunk kick and scream ... Damn! He got the ropes.. Rick : Fredrick struggling to his feet, and is met by a Fist to the midsection .. Knee Lift to the face!... Upper Cut!... Fredrick is straggering .... Side Kick! Fredrick drops like a ton of bricks! Steve : Yes! Preacher cirles his pray! He got him, Yes! The Decent .. and there it is! Boom! Preacher for the cover .. Hooks the leg.. Rick : 1...2...3!! Preacher takes the "W"! Gary Trudeau : Here is your winner... Preacher of Pain! "In-A-Gadda-Da-Vida" By Iron Butterfly starts to play over the PA system again. Preacher of Pain lifts his arms high above his head for a crowd pop out of respect. He leaves the ring, and walks back to the locker rooms, slaping hands of many fans. Steve : Damn, that was a great match! Rick : Yes, very exciting! ... Time for a commercial break, I'm told. We'll be right back! The screen fades to black and then opens to a black screen. The following announcement has been paid for by the House of Pain. "Thunderstruck" by ACDC can be heard in the background as the screen opens to show Randy Cross and Nikodemous Segier standing with a black and red HOP shirt on. Nik : DAMN! I look good! Randy : Yeah... Its gotta be the shirt! Randy does a sort of thumb point thing to his and Nik's Red and Black shirt. The screen fades to black and the shirt appears... Voice Over : Get your House of Pain red and black t-shirt now! Only $19.95 plus a small $5.00 House of Pain, "Be like us" charge. The screen opens back to Randy and Nik who are modeling there Red and Black shirts. Nik : What are you waiting for..? Randy : Order now! DO it! Just then Jennifer Segier enters with her very tight black and red shirt on. She comes up and takes Nik's arm... Jennifer : You know you want it. Jennifer blows a kiss to the camera as the screen fades to black... And then the voice over... The Preceeding announcement has been paid for by the House of Pain. Gary Trudeau : Ladies and gentlemen... It is now time for the main event of the evening... It is the renewal of a fued that spans over a year... Introducing first hailing from Pittsburgh PA! He is the former United States Champion, he is Flynn Steele! "Great American Nightmare" by White Zombie and Howard Stern starts over the PA. Flynn Steele enters the arena to a great pop from the fans. Flynn slowly walks to the ring slapping hands of the fans... He jumps into the ring and looks ready. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, hailing from Dublin Ireland and weighing in at 275 pounds... Representing the House of Pain, the former World TV Champion Todd "The Irish Hammer" Logan! Logan, wearing black jeans and a black HOP T-Shirt, is seen running down the rampway. "Jump Around" by House of Pain begins to blare over the PA System, but is cut short because Logan has already reached the ring. Logan grabs a chair and tosses it through the ropes. Rick : Whoa! Gary Trudeau bails out of the ring as Flynn charges the entering Logan. Hey! What is that on Logan's arm! Steve : It looks like a cast Ricky... Oh! Logan swings the cast but Steele ducks and slams a forearm shiver into the head of Logan rocking him. Logan comes back with a left of his own... This is turning into a brawl early here as the ref sweeps the chair from the ring with his foot. Rick : There is alot of commotion near the rampway... Steve : It looks like.. Griffin, Shaman, Gutter, The Mimic, Sir Micheal and Master Xavier are all coming down to the ring... They are forming the WWA WALL! Rick : Let's see the HOP get through that! HAHA Inside the ring Logan has since beaten Steele down with that cast of his.. The ref is yelling at him to stop! Steve : Logan pushes the ref away... Rick : You will be fined for theat Logan! Steve : Do you honestly think the House of Pain cares Ricky? I wonder how much they have in that HOP fund of theirs...? Logan lifts Steele up and sends him across via Irish whip. Rick : Steele ducks a clothesline attempt by Logan with that cast. Flynn off the opposite ropes and LOU THESZ press! 1 count only! Steve : Logan is up with a look of rage... OH! Steele looks out! Logan just layed him out with that Cast! The ref looks confused! Logan rolls out of the ring and grabs that chair... HE tosses it into the ring and the ref is warning him. Rick : HUGE FINE! Logan pushes Larry Crosier by the face to the corner... Steve : Logan picks the chair up an- Rick : Don't do it Logan! Steve : Logan slams the chair into Flynns back!
