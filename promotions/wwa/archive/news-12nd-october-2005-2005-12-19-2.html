Mick Vain on the Shelf Nearly two weeks removed from Labour Day Brawl, the WWA has learned that Mick Vain has indeed suffered an injury that will keep him out of action for an undetermined amount of time. After numerous tests, WWA medical officials have decided to keep Mick away from active competition for the next few weeks. Mick’s own doctor, Dr. William Holding was quoted as saying, “It’s more precautionary than anything at this point. The injury may not be a major problem right now, but my colleagues and I agree that this type of injury could worsen in such a severe way that Mr. Vain would be forced to miss much more time than the few weeks we are recommending. Given regular treatment and some time to heal properly, Mr. Vain should make a full recovery in a short amount of time.” Dr. Holding would not elaborate on the nature of the injury and neither would WWA officials. Given Mick’s performance at a recent Meltdown, this news comes as no surprise. It was said by a few people that it looked like there was something wrong with the former tag team champion. “He just looked a little off,” said one WWA employee. As it is now, there are rumours that Mick suffered a slight concussion or some ligament damage at LBD or a previous event. Though not even Mick Vain could say for sure. We caught up with him for these comments. “It’s very disappointing,” a very disheartened Mr. Vain said, “I had a feeling that something wasn’t right but I thought it was nothing too severe so ignored it. I was heading into my biggest match at LBD and wasn’t going to miss it. The WWA and the fans were counting on me and I wasn’t going to disappoint. Things didn’t improve a whole lot last week and after my match with Vex on Meltdown, I felt worse. So I decided to listen to my body and get checked out. When the doctors gave me the news, well, for lack of a better term, it sucked!” Mick would also not comment on the nature of his injury but assured us that he should only be out for a few weeks before resuming his in-ring career. For the time being, Mick will be touring some of the cities where the WWA will be appearing. He will be signing autographs at local Wal-Marts, arena box offices and other venues.
