Cody Williams Basic Information Nicknames | The Reflection of Perfection, Second to None, 220, CCXX, God's Gift to Professional Wrestling, The Hollywood Rockstar, Mr. Pound for Pound Real Name | Cody Preston Williams II Height | 6'2" Weight | 235 Date of Birth | November 18, 1979 Home Town | Beverly Hills, CA Theme | "Head Like A Hole" (Clay Remix) by Nine Inch Nails More Details Appearance Cody is a blue eyed caucasian male with a well defined muscular build. He has shoulder length brown hair that he ties into a ponytail when outside the ring, sometimes sporting a braided tail. He is usually seen with diamond stud earrings in both of his ear lobes, and a designer watch of some sort at all times. He wears custom tailored designer suits outside of the ring, at events and appearance, and backstage. He has stubbly facial hair, his moustache and beard connecting to his sideburns. Ring Attire Cody Williams wears a ball chain necklace that is fitted around his neck. He ears black elbow pads and has his left wrist taped up in white tape while his right wrist and hand are taped up. He wears black biker short tights, with a gold waistband and gold bands on the bottom of each leg opening. He has various star designs on his trunks and the word "Perfection" on the back. He wears different color combinations on his tights and changes them at each event. He wears black kneepads and black patent leather boots. Over his boots he wears japanese style kick pads that are black in color and the padded part is gold. They have various star designs on them and along with his tights, he wears different colored kick pads to correspond with his tight color combinations. Personality He is a cocky, arrogant son of a bitch that thinks he is better and greater than everyone else. He believes that he is the center of everything and that the world revolves around him. Growing up in a rich family where everything was handed to him on a silver platter, Cody felt he needed something in his life to strive for, something to work hard for, instead of having everything come with ease. He felt that he should take a former childhood fantasy and make it a reality: become a professional wrestler. Cody excelled in wrestling school and soon found that professional wrestling was indeed his true calling. After making it through the ranks of organization after organization and having won title after title he finally made it to the "big show". During the last stop on a tour in Japan, a botched powerbomb left him with a serious neck injury that took him out of the wrestling scene in late 2002. Doctors said that he'd never be able to do physical activities ever again, they said that he'd never be able to wrestle again. Look who's laughing now. After five year hiatus from the wrestling scene "The Reflection of Perfection" Cody Williams is back, and in better shape than ever and is ready to tackle his next greatest endeavor.. Showing the world once again, why he is God's gift to professional wrestling!!! He now lives life a day at a time and lives by the saying "Carpe Diem" or seize the day and never takes his life, the opportunities given to him, or his friends and family for granted. A natural in the ring and in the mic, he is easily hated but his undeniable talent and skill has garnered him a plethora of fans and haters alike.
