The Scouting Report - Issue 23

24 Sep 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings and salutations WWA fans, it’s your esteemed and valued Director of Talent back with all the dirt and opinions you can handle with a new Scouting Report. -Well fans we’ve just come off Independence Day Rumble and to say it was off the charts would simply be an understatement. Namely not just because of the matches but also the two big surprises that happened that night. I’ll get to those later but first let’s start with the matches. -After a fun entrance by WWA Champion Kash we had our first match of the night. Toot McBiggins, Myzery, and Christopher Vice all squared off for the rights to keep their jobs in a “Loser Leaves Town” match. Luckily we were spared from too much pain thanks to the WWA European Champion the Chuckster. After giving them a beating Chucky just eliminated all three of them, effectively removing them from the WWA. -Next we have the match that many have called the “ratings war” of the WWA between Joey K and Dave Harley. Both having “talk shows” and each claiming theirs was better. In the end though the TV Title came between the two, the title which was in the possession of Joey. Of course that all changed after a Harley Bomb and we crowned a new champion. After losing the belt it seems that Joey was even moved to tears. Sad really. -In an Extreme Match, David “the Sniper” Wattsoner faced off against Shaman. The match took place out on the streets of Utah and was basically a mini version of the Bourbon Street Brawl. This match seemed to prove that both of the legends can still go. After hitting each other with everything that wasn’t nailed down Shaman got the win. Although both men weren’t in the best shape of their careers after. I’m not even sure Shaman knew were he was. -Chance and Enigma’s feud came to a head at the Independence Day Rumble. After months of waring it seems that both men became tired and wanted to settle this once and for all. Unlike other matches this wasn’t a gimmick match. It was just a plain knock out drag out affair with both men trying to kill each other. In the end that leader of the Forsaken Souls came out on top with a Fall From Grace. Still if Chance can take the WWA Title with the shot he earned at BSB then I can tell you for sure that these two will meet again. -In one half of the main event Kash and Tracer Bullet faced off. A lot was on the line that night. Kash wanted to prove that he earned the World Title and that it wasn’t a fluke. Tracer wanted to finally get a foothold in beating Randy Cross’s record of being a four time World Champion. The two started early that night with Tracer and Kash trading barbs, mainly about Tracer’s wife Missy and Kash’s Mother. When the two fought it was a true event with kickouts many times. In the end though, Tracer fell pray to the Kashcade. -Of course that wasn’t the only news in that match as someone wearing the White Ninja uniform stole the World Title Belt during the match. One would have to assume that Kash cannot be happy about this. -And now in the main event of the night, the Rumble itself, there was quite a surprise. Nick Adams came in a number eight and managed to outlast twenty-nine other men. He won after eliminating fellow YU stable-mate Grunge. After that elimination Adams celebrated as only he can, with tons of water bottles. Now Adams finally has what every WWA superstar wants, a shot at the gold. I always said it was only a matter of time. -And of course we also had the return of the monster Damian Thorne. After being engineered by Matt Warr Thorne made his return as only he can. He solidified his breakup with Lady Stephanie with a violent display that I won’t go into detail. A truly sick sight. -But the biggest shock of the night was the news of a roster split. The WWA Talent will be split into two pools and wrestle every other week with Fiesta one Monday and Meltdown the next. Matt Warr will be in charge of Meltdown and Commissioner Raine in charge of the other. I have been told that we will find out who goes were on this coming Meltdown. -This Meltdown will be the last with the full roster. As such it will be a show that you will not want to miss. -In the first match of the night Se7en will face off against Jimmi Zion. The two have a large contrast of styles. Zion is mainly a highflier while Se7en is more of a power wrestler. Plus with Se7en in the ring can the Forsaken Souls be too far behind? -In the next match Dave Harley will have to put his newly won TV Title on the line against the GenX champion Lockdown. The first fall of the match will be for the TV Title and the second will be for the GenX title. This match seems a bit unorthodox. What could be the motives behind the match? -Damian Thorne will make his return against Reverand Tweak. Tweak made the mistake of challenging Thorne in the rumble Sunday. Now it seems that Mr. Nasty is out for blood and he’s already not in a good mood. I sure wouldn’t want to be Tweak right now. -Wes Sanders and Enigma will get a good chance at hurting X-Corp further when they will get Mal Somers in a handicap match on Meltdown. We all know that Mal is a great competitor but can even he stand the onslaught of these two men? -After weeks of talking Grunge will put his United States Title on the line in a match with Leviathan. After hurting Grunge’s friend Dusk last night at the ppv we know Grunge is mad. Not only that Levi used that to sucker Grunge into a match of Levi’s choosing at Labor Day Brawl. This match will be another contrast of styles not to be missed. -And in the main event the question is who can Kash trust? The World Champion will be teaming with the man he beat last Sunday in the form of Tracer Bullet to take on Chance and Nick Adams. Chance won the Bourbon Street Brawl to get a shot at the World Title and Adams won the right for a shot at Labor Day Brawl. And of course one can only guess that Tracer is not done with his quest for the gold. Still Tracer and Kash have had uncanny luck when they have tagged. Either way this will be a war. Well until next time this is Josh Wilson. Be sure to watch Meltdown and Fiesta. You won’t be disappointed. Ciao.
