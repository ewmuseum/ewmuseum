WWA — 22 Sep 1999

22 Sep 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The opening seqences runs, showing clips of Outlaw, Steihl, Kristopher Kasanova, Nik Seiger, and other WWA superstars to the sound of "Fly Away" by Lenny Kravitz. Then the scene flashes into the WWA Studios... The camera pans over the inside, and then the camera finds Ricky Harrington and Steve Colon, who are staning behing the podium-type table. The music dies as they start to speak.] Ricky : Welcome to Slam! It has been awhile since we've had this show air, but now with the large influx of new talent, thanks to the new and GREATEST Commissioner in the history of the WWA, this show demands to get back on the air. Steve : Ricky with another shameless plug for his run at being hired as the REAL commish! Ricky : I am the real commish! Steve : Ok Ricky... Why is it that whenever Mark Harrington addresses you, it is as Interim Commissioner...? Ricky : Semantics Steve! Merely semantics! Steve : Do you even know what you are saying Ricky? Ricky : Shut up dude! Anyways... Tonight is a great night to come back on the air. Tonight we get to see the Flamer in action against Rattlesnaks Ryan! That should be a good one.... Steve : The Flamer is one of the hottest things, if not the hottest thing in the Rookie Ranks, pardon the hottest/flame pun. But he is a rising star in the WWA, that is for sure! Ricky : The Preacher of Pain seems to have turned the crosshairs right onto the back of the Flamer though. This rise to flame, may be extinguished before it gets started... (snickers at his wit). Steve (looks at Ricky, shaking his head): Wow Ricky... Good one. Ricky : It's hard to be humble wh- Steve (interupting Ricky, much to Ricky's chagrin): Anyways... On top of the Rookie Ring match, we get to see the other rookie phenom in Shaku Endbringer! In my humble opinion, he is the best thing in the Rookie Ranks! Ricky : What? Flamer beat him already! Steve : Shaku Endbringer dominated that match from start to finish. I am taking nothing away from the Flamer, he is a great athlete, but I just think that this Shaku guy is the real deal! Ricky : But he is facing a tough one in Inoki Hotaka! This guy will send this Shaku guy packing! Which brings us to our beginning match, putting the demonspawn "Lord of Darkness" Doomsday against the Inspector Max Quinn, the Mountie! Let's get to the ring.... Gary Trudeau : Ladies and gentlemen... Welcome to the World Wrestling Alliance studios in East Rutherford, New Jersey! (crowd pops) The first match of the evening will be set for one fall.... Introducing first hailing from the Darkside... He weighs in at 324 pounds... He is the "Lord of Darkness" Doomsday! "Scythe, Rage And Rose" by Dark Tranquility starts over the PA. Doomsday enters the studio to a mixed reaction, as the fans do not know what to do with this guy... He walks to the ring, but before he enters the ring he pauses to stare at some of the fans at ringside. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent.... Hailing from Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada and weighing in at 280 pounds... He is Inspector Max Quinn... The Mountie! "O Canada" rings from the loudspeakers as Inspector Max Quinn, the one known as The Mountie, appears at the top of the ramp. He is met with a mixture of cheers and boos, but The Mountie reaction to the crowd is indifference. He walks to the ring in his full Royal Canadien Police Uniform, peeling off his jacket as the Mountie remains focused on the ring. He slowly moves to ringside. Ricky : I'll tell you what.... This Doomsday guy creeps me out a little... Steve : Yeah... He is truely an odd duck... Max Quinn is up on the apron taking his time in getting into the ring. Is Quinn looking to take the Lord of Darkness out of him game by waiting... Ricky : I don't think that is a very good idea.... Trying to take anything from the Lord of Darkness! Look at 'em! Sheesh! Steve : The Mountie finally enters the ring... The Lord of Darkness is simply standing there starring at the Mountie. Max Quinn doesn't know what to think... He feints an attack, but Doomsday doesn't even flinch. Ricky : The Mountis shrugs and attacks! Oh! Doomsday just took his head off with a clothesline! The Mountie is reeling on the mat now as Doomsday snaps his hair back while rising to his feet. Max Quinn is starting to get to his feet now... Doomsday pushes him back into the ropes and then across the ring with an irish whip.... Steve : Doomsday raises the foot hitting The Mountie in the chops... Quinn goes down to the mat, this time holding his face... Doomsday looks to the crowd, who immediately boo him. Ricky : The fans sure don't like this monster! Doomsday stalks over to the now rising Mountie... Mountie with a right to the stomach... The Lord of Darkness doesn't budge, but he lowers the boom with a giant forearm to the back of Mountie. Steve : Te Mountie quickly rakes the eyes of Doomsday, as Doomsday was reaching down to bring Mountie up... Doomsday staggers a little bit and Quinn pops up and runs agains the ropes... Clothesline! Ricky : WOW! Max Quinn takes the big man down! Steve : This Max Quinn, Mountie fellow shows a great deal of strength to actually take Doomsday down... The Mountie scoops Doomsday up and... Bodyslam! Ricky : No doubt about it... The Mountie is strong... And now the Mountie fires the Lord of Darkness into the ropes... Belly to belly! Steve : Nicely done there by Quinn! Quinn, taking no prisoners, immediately gets Doomsday to his feet... Canadian Back breaker! The Mountie really feeling it now.... Ricky : The Mountie gets Doomsday up one more time... He sets him up for a... Yes! Powerbomb! The Mountie turns to the camera and mouths the word "Massacre"... As I thought... The Mountie is hooking the legs, locking in the "MAPLE LEAF MASSACRE"! Steve : It's academic now! The ref is down checking on Doomsday, who taps out! Ricky : What?! Doomsday just tapped out?! Amazing Steve! The Inspector Max Quinn gains the victory in the first match of the evening.... Steve : I'll tell you something... This Mountie guy is really opening some peoples eyes comeing from the Rookie ranks... He is truely a force to be reckoned with! The Mountie rolls out of the ring with little fanfare, as he entered. Ricky : Let's take it to the back with "Ring rat" Mary Bingham! Mary.... [The screen flashes to the back, somewhere in the locker room area... Mary Bingham is standing there with a big smile and a very lowcut white outfit. Inaki Hotaka joins her as the camera man is seen pointing his fingers in five, four, three, two, one fashion and points to Mary...] Mary Bingham : Mr. Hotaka what is this interview about?? Inaki Hotaka : Well its about this Mary. I'm sick and tired of hearing Shaku Endbringer brag about how great he is. I think he is about as great as any rookie and well tonight I'm gonna show him how competitive the rookie ranks are. Is there anything you wish to know Mary. Mary : Well Inaki you made an impact into the rookie ranks, do you plan on going further and gain heavyweight title shots and the such? Inaki Hotaka : Eventually I will have to prove to the other wrestlers that I can and will handle my own. In fact I would like to extend and offer to the veterans.... If anyone out there that is a veteran, I would be glad to accept anyone who is willing to teach a rookie some things about the sport and I would be willing to gain an ally in this. Mary : Well there you have it Inaki Hotaka in a search or quest to better himself. [Camera focuses on Inaki Hotaka as he smiles at Mary... Then he leaves... The screen flashes to the studio, the ring and Gary Trudeau.] Gary Trudeau : The next match of the evening will be set for one fall, it will have a 15 minute time limit. Introducing coming to the ring at this time, Inoki Hotaka! ["Primal Scream" by Motley Crue starts up over the PA. The crowd starts to throw paper streamers as Inoki enters the arena to decent fanfare. Inoki Hotaka rolls into the ring and prepares for the match.] Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, hailing from parts unknown, he is Shaku Endbringer! [The lights dim out and the sounds of "Counterfeit" by Limp Bizkit start to play as the crowd hushes over slightly. The lyrics done by Fred Durst are song softly, whispering over the keyboarding effects.] Freakin' me out, you wear a mask called Counterfeit Freakin' me out you wear a mask... Freakin' me out, you wear a mask called Counterfeit Freakin' me out you wear a mask... Freakin' me out, you wear a mask called Counterfeit Freakin' me out you wear a....Freak... [As the guitar hits on the last word, fireworks explode from either side of the entrance in a straight line up in the air and then crossing, alternating upwards and crossing for a few shots, then they cease while the lyrics play out. The song plays out and then it comes to just before the chorus and the song is cranked up.] ARE YOU SICK OF YOURSELF? WELL I'M SICK OF YOU TOO!!! [The lights come on with Shaku Endbringer standing in front of the entrance, his back arched over and he is running his thumb across his throat, screaming out into the air, being drowned out by the cheers of the fans of the spectacle that they just witnessed. Shaku is dressed in black cutoff jeans and a black vest, unbuttoned in the front exposing his muscular body. Shaku holds his pool cue high into the air, angling it much like Sandman does with his Kendo Stick. Shaku walks to the ring slowly, gripping his pool cue in this right hand and walking to the ring. He gets to ringside and a fan is holding a sign that signs "Shaku, the way of the future". Shaku walks over and looks down at it, the fans all screaming around him. He gives the kid his anarchy sunglasses and slaps a few hands at ringside before climbing the apron. He climbs the apron and stands on the side of the turnbuckle, raising his pool cue up into the air again and running his thumb across his throat. Shaku moves over and sits on the top turnbuckle, taking off his trench coat and his silver chains. He hops down from the turnbuckle and grabs the ropes with his arms, stretching himself for the match.] Ricky : Ok... Quite the entrance for a rookie and for Slam. The fans are digging it though... Steve : I'll say Ricky. Hey.. Look who the ref is... Ricky : HA! Joe Stock... He used to be the host of this show until the Prez got a clue! Steve : Or maybe a call from his SISTER! Ricky : Shutup! Steve : Sorry... Inoki charges... Inoki with a spinning OH! Shaku caught him and hits a t-bone suplex! Nice coutner there by the big man!
