Steve : Both men break cleanly and they start to circle again... Another collar an- NO! Mr. Internet fired a boot to the midsection... Irish whip from Mr. Internet and big back drop sending Sir Micheal onto the mat... Impressive start by Mr. Internet. Ricky : I'll say! Mr. Internet gets Sir Micheal up an immediately locks in another standing side headlock. This time Mr. Internet lead Sir Micheal to the JOB Squad's corner. There's the tag and Tim Powers is in... Steve : Big forearm to the exposed ribs of Sir Micheal... Powers pushes Sir Micheal into the ropes and sends him across the ring ala irish whip... Sir Micheal ducks a clothesline from Powers.... Ricky : Sir Micheal comes off the ropes and hits a flying shoulder block sending Powers to the mat... Sir Micheal is up and Powers is regaining his feet also... Drop kick from Sir Micheal sends Powers into the JOB Squad's corner.. The tag is made and Mr. Internet charges into the ring... Steve : Clothesline from Sir Micheal takes Internet's head off... Sir Micheal gets Mr. Internet up and irish whips him HARD into the corner right next to Master X! A place Mr. Internet DOES NOT want to be... Ricky : Sir Micheal charges and hits a big corner splash... Mr. Internet falls out from the corner and the tag is made and Master X enters the ring... Master X sends Mr. Internet into the ropes and Boot Boot to the face of Mr. Internet... Master X again picks Mr. Internet up and tags Sir Micheal in. Ricky : Sir Micheal climbs up the ropes... They proved to be just to much for the JOB Squad... Ricky : Well then... Shall we keep this night moving with the next match of the evening... On the back of his vest and pants says "SUPERSTAR" Johnny Griggs in black lettering. Griggs is paying for his showboating now! Steve : Griggs is still down... He really got smashed by that clothesline. The Mountie stomps on the right leg and knee of Griggs... Again.. And another by the Mountie. Wow... The Mountie really focusing on that right knee and leg of Griggs as he snaps that leg back pulling the hamstring of Griggs. Ricky : Great strategy employed here by the Mountie as he targets the legs of Griggs... You can't fly if you can't stand. Steve : Good point Ricky... The Mountie now gets Griggs up and executes a nice swinging neckbreaker.... The Mountie showing some signs of technical expertise now, instead of sheer power. Ricky : I'm telling you! He is the real thing! The Mountie now showing us his power as he POWERBOMBS Griggs through the mat! The Mountie drops for the cover and this one is over.... Steve : NO! Griggs throws his shoulder up at the last second! Ricky : Slow count from the ref! Steve : Maybe... But Griggs got his shoulder up. The Mountie shrugs off the near fall and picks Griggs up whipping him to the ropes.... Missed clothesline from The Mountie and Griggs ducks and goes against the opposite ropes... Ricky : POWERSLAM from The Mountie as he caught Griggs off the ropes! The Mountie in complete control of this match now again gets Griggs to his feet. And again Moutnie irish whips Griggs to the ropes... Steve : Griggs with a baseball slide through the legs of the Mountie! Dropkick to the knee of Mountie! Now it is the Mountie's turn to feel the pain in the legs... Griggs getting his second wind here drops an elbow across the knee of the Mountie! Ricky : Hey... Have look who is in the crowd Steve... Steve : What...? Ricky : Up there in the crowd.. It is Troy Walcott, Tommy Lazer, Blake Daniels and Chad Goodman... It looks like Chad Goodman is taking notes or something? Steve : You are right Ricky... I wonder if they are taking notes for their enemies or if they are looking to rush another member into the Frat? Springboard leg drop onto the Mountie... Steve : Nice agility there by Griggs with that nice springboard move from the ropes.... Uh Oh! Griggs is going to the top now... Ricky : No worries... The Mountie is stirring now... Griggs is perched on the top as the Mountie regains his feet. The Mountie turns and NO! Steve : Clothesline from the top by Johnny Griggs! The Mountie in trouble now! Again Griggs non stop up to the top on the other side now... And.. The SUPERSTAR! A frog splash from the top! And he hooks the leg and 3! Ricky : Damn! Johnny Griggs with the win here on Slam.. We will be right back... [The screen closes to a commecial... I try my best to keep in touch all the time, but that isn't always possible. When I can though, I use AT&T. They have the cheapest rates around. Believe me, I've called from almost everywhere, and no other company can measure up to the prices of AT&T. ["Suicide" walks over to the wall and picks up a phone.] Hi honey. The next match here this evening will be a loser leave town match! The loser will say goodbye to the WWA forever! Introducing first... Ricky : That can't be a boost for his confidence here tonight. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent... Making his entrance hailing from the Pit! And weighing in at 233 pounds he is VIPER! And finally I got my revenge for all you put me through [pointing to Banks]. How does it feel to be screwed Banks, how does it feel? [Viper takes one last long look at Ant Banks and leaves the ring. Thornhill gets slowly to his feet and this time Spidero with a head scissors takedown.. ["Breathe" by Prodigy starts blasting over the sound system...] Ricky : What is this! Steve : Music Ricky! Ricky : Thornhill bursts into a flying clothesline, taking Spidero's head off... It appears that the music helped Thornhill get pumped for this match... Steve : Thank god that the music stopped! Ricky : What's wrong...? You know what they say.. If it's to loud... You to- Steve : OK! Spidero realing now as Thornhill goes to the top... Missle drop kick! Thornhill on fire now! Ricky : Hey!! Wh- Why are they out here...? Steve : James Wish and Johnny Griggs are both out here and diving into the ring! Griggs tackles Thornhill simultaneously of Wish tackling El Porto Spidero! Ricky : What the hell is this about! These two men are attacking Spidero and Thornhill! Steve : Both Thornhill and Spidero are down now... And both Wish and Griggs are up on top, on opposite side of the ring... "Shooting Star" from Griggs onto Thornhill and Wish hits his "Last Wish" on Spidero! Ricky : Get security out here! This is terrible! ["Superstar" Johnny Griggs puts his arm on James Wish's shoulder as both men lean in towards the camera, for a CLOSEUP.
