Theme | "What I've done" by Linkin Park More Details Appearance (To be honest, I don't know how to describe a sweet mask or awesome wrestling pants or even a pretty sweet looking character. This is more just to get your wheels spinning and to release your artistic freedom. Try some things out. We'll see how they look. Have fun.) MASK: Friar wears a mask. The mask is plain but strong and intimidating. It has stitching in the back. His eyes and mouth can be seen. (I haven't included colors because I would not want to limit what you can do or what ideas you might have for the mask.) PHYSICAL: His eyes are blue and hair is long and blond. Friar is muscular and well-built. A long blond ponytail falls from the mask down to the middle of his back. A rich thick chin beard can be seen under the mask also. His skin is slightly tan. He has slight chest hair and some scaring on his left ribs. TATTOOS: He has a rosary tattoo that runs the length of his left arm. It wraps around at the bicep and then winds down the arm to the forearm, where it meets another tattoo of a medium sized cross. On his left chest is a large cross tattoo and on his right ribs is a tattoo of a rampant lion. Ring Attire RING ATTIRE: He wears a pair of light blue pants much like Sabu would wear (all shiny and loose) and they have a thick, black waist wrap. His boots are white and black. He has black tape on his wrists and a black band on his right bicep. He has white tape on his fingers, also. Personality Vigilant, brave, and reckless in his ways, Friar takes the fight to whoever needs it. His night could be wide open and he’ll end up in a Hardcore Rules, 2-on-1 match during the Main Event to raise money for the Children’s Hospital. Or he could end up in the adult hospital from the massive brawls he finds his way into backstage. It’s all up in the air. He’s been called a “loose cannon with reckless abandon” and a “hero with no boundaries.” He hits the Gift DDT all the time. LOVES to sneak up, hit the Gift, and bail without anybody – even his victim – knowing he did it. History WWA brings Friar in to help bolster ratings and test their top superstars and champions. Will he make his mark on WWA fans by standing up to the competition? Or will the masked enigma fall, proving once and for all, WWA is where the best in the business call home? Only time will tell.
