The Scouting Report - Issue 7

28 Nov 2003

Bylined Joe Stock - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey everyone. This is a packed edition of the Scouting Report, but before I get started, I want to say a few things. While I think the WWA provides some great entertainment, sadly it seems the WWE doesn't anymore. After weeks of disappointing shows, I can no longer watch. So instead of seeing Monday Night Raw, for maybe the first time in years, I skipped it and watched Monday Night Football. I did catch some bits and pieces on commercials breaks, but what I saw wasn't very impressive. Sadly it seems the WWE has forgotten what the second "w" means. Don't worry, the World WRESTLING Alliance hasn't forgotten and never will, at least as long as I am around. On to the report. -First off, let me say the WWA remembers 9/11. We wish all those who have lost love ones the best life can give them and that we as a country promise we will never forget the thousands who died for freedom on that day. God Bless America. -Labor Day Brawl 2002 has come and gone and was a huge success in my opinion. From top to bottom we had some amazing matches. -Moore Vs Bullet was a one of a kind brawl and I think everyone was shocked by the ending. The only issue was the reuse of some spots (We've seen the "swirly" before.0 and the unnecessary special guest referee. I have been involved with the WWA for a number of years, but I can't remember us having so many guest refs in a short time span. Maybe our zebras should unionize so they can't be replaced so easily. Plus the ending was a little two lame for a World Title Match. -Kraven & Denrol had a great match; the two seem to bring out the best in each other. Of course I personally think we could have done without the table spot, but what do I know? And what was with the false three counts? I'm all for false finishes, but that should be left to the wrestlers to include, not the official. -I didn't expect much out of the Trapper/We The People match and I wasn't disappointed. Through we could have done without the lame celebrity showing. At least this angle seems to be over; I hope Trapper moves on to bigger and better things. -I think Bourne and Payne could have been better, but since they weren't given much time, the match ended up a spotfest with little psychology. Plus, why did the ref allow Payne to use a steel chair? -While I think Ladder Matches are now a very overused gimmick, Sanders and Stryfe had a great one. I think Sanders has a good future in the US Title scene and I'm sure Stryfe will find himself up there soon too. My only issue was the one, two, THREE table spots. That puts the count at four for the night. -One of the best tag team title matches we have had in a while happened between Havoc and Rick & Venom. Hopefully we can get some more of that in the near future. -Eddie and Thorne was off the charts. The two haven't had a long feud, but what we've gotten was two ***** matches. I also like that while we had some great spots, not were so insane they were not believable. Good luck to both men, they have great futures in front of them. -The 6-man was... there. It's only purpose was to unveil Darken and it did. I don't know what's in the future for either stable, but it should be fun to watch. Thank goodness it was a great match. -While Dorion/Tense match wasn't long, it got the job done. Ian tense looked great in the match and the style of the match was able to hide that Tense wasn't 100% quite yet. But it was also clear this feud isn't over and the two will battle once again very soon. -Moore/Denrol may have not been a great match on paper, but it was in the flesh. The two men, despite being tired from earlier matches, gave 125% and tore the roof off the place. The only things hurting the match were the ending and Randy Cross. While I respect the man, he was a distraction. The run-in could have been done after that match. I see no reason Cross had to soak up the spotlight from these two great athletes. -I do think the new House of Pain has potential. That is if Cross let's Thorne be the wrestling leader, since I'm not sure randy can deliver anymore in the ring. He would work much better as the mouthpiece. Hopefully HOP can also pick up some more talent. -Looking ahead to next week, the lineups for Meltdown and Fiesta are... unique. -I don't think Kaine has earned his shot yet, but nothing can be done now. Of course if he wins, that's another issue. Through I've been pushing the division for some time, so I shouldn't talk. -Eddie Vs Rick Venom doesn't seem to have a purpose, but I could be missing something. I still expect a good match out of both men. -The Texas Longhorns have been too quiet lately, but the tag team division needs to be pushed, so I guess they are the sacrificial lambs for the slaughter. -Ian Tense should be at the top of his game for Meltdown I'm told, so expect an even better match than the one from LDB. -Not sure what to make of Bullet Vs Black, but both men need a big win, so expect a good match. -Fiesta is going to be a great night for rookies. 6 of them debut and I can't wait to see all of them in action for the first time in a WWA ring. Through the one I look most forward to is Chance. -I was surprised as anyone when Barrett Sinclair came to WWA offices and asked to be put back on the road. He makes his big return on Fiesta, set your VCRs to record for this bout. -Shaman and Kash should have a good match. A win for Kash could push him huge. -The main event is a bit questionable, but the men involved could surprise us all. -Nothing to report on the injury side right now, the WWA came out of LDB a-okay. -On to talent relations. I recently got a chance to speak to Justin Bourne and he express happiness that we're back in the states. According to him, well I'll quote him. "There just shouldn't be a country that doesn't have root beer!". He also can't wait for Fiesta and promises a surprise for his fans. He also is working on his own web site, I can't wait to see it. -Wallace Coleman's segments on Fiesta have been going down well. His anxiety to explore the business further is being shown in these segments. I wouldn't be surprised if he tried his hand at something different soon, as he is very keen and a very hard worker. -I'm told a number of WWA superstars have been busy at WWA Offices. Some of thme are the Four Horsemen, who have been asking something about stock options. No idea what's going on there. -Another person who's been lurking around is Denrol. He's been bugging Warr to book a Canadian tour soon. -IT'S SHOWTIME! -There are talks going on right now, to reopen the EWA. I can't say much, but... well I can't say much. -Look for a tag team or two very soon, we're in negotiations right now. -As for the side project, be patient, it's coming guys. From underneath the New York Yankees cap, this is Joe Stock.
