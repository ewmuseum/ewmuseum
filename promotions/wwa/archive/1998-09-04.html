Friday Night Fights — 4 Sep 1998

4 Sep 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday September 4, 1998 The screen opens up to the the WWA banner. Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows the Rose Garden. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey, Terry Valentine and Johny Morehouse sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Welcome once again everybody to Friday Night Fights. I am your host Tony Bailey and to my right is Johny Morehouse and on my left is Terry Valentine. Tonight we are live coming to you from the Rose Garden in the lovely Rose Quarter in Portland Oregon. We've got a great for you tonight. Johny : That's right! We sure do! Tonight we will see the First round of the Tv Title Tournament. It will be in the Lethal Lottery format. Terry : Along with that, we get to watch the Black Scorpian destroy little Nikki Segier. Tony : That remains to be seen Terry. Lets go to the first match of the evening. "Saturday's Allright for Fighting" by Spectrum starts over the PA as David "Sniper" Wattsoner enters the arena to a mixed reacation. The veteran walks to the ring slapping a few hands. In the ring the Patriot is waiting for the match to start. Tony : This is the first time Mr. Wattsoner is in action here in the WWA. I have been looking forward to seeing him. Johny : Well Tony, let me tell you... He is a GOOD one. He has been around a long time. I think even Flynn Steele has an elder in the WWA ring. Terry : In that case we better get the oxygen over here STAT! Tony : ANYWAYS! There is the bell. Wattsoner CHARGES Patriot and unloades an arsenal of rights and left. Johny : Wattsoner has the Patriot in the corner. Patriot slumps as Wattsoner plants his head in the turnbuckle. Terry (sacastically): BOY! Wattsoner with a nice display of TECHNICAL moves. Tony : There was your technical move Terry. Johny : Wattsoner with a PRETTY belly-to-back suplex. He gets up quickly and stomps at the Patriot in the stomach. Tony : Wattsoner seems to be in charge here. He picks Patriot back up and delivers a DDT. Wattsoner quickly up and a big leg drop. Terry : Wattsoner picks the Patriot up again. This time he tosses him into the ropes and delivers a move....??? Johny : Samaon drop Terry! Tony : Why does Harrington PAY you! Terry : I am the BEST announcer in this business. Tony : Right... Wattsoner is apparently waiting for the Patriot to rise to his feet. The Patriot staggers up and Wattsoner with a series of fast jabs, then... OH! Patriot falls lifelessly to the mat. Johny : That was the "ICE PUNCH". May a foe has lost to that move! The ref counts the three and the Sniper gets a win in his debut here in the WWA. Tony : Well ladies and gentlemen lets take you to the next match of the evening. This should be a good one. Terry : Nikki doesn't stand a chance. The Black Scorpian will take him apart. Tony : Maybe Terry! Lets go find out.... "Tub Thumping" by Chumbawamba starts over the PA. The crowd starts to cheer as Nikodemous Segier comes out of the back and down the ramp towards the ring. Nik slaps hands and talks with the crowd on his way to the ring. Tony : Wow... Nik is pretty popular here in Portland. Terry : He sucks! The fans are idiots! "Snap your fingers, Snap your neck" by Prong starts over the PA. The crowd boos as the Black Scorpian enters the arena with the Fan right behind him. The Black Scorpian walks to the ring without taking his eyes from Segier, whereas the Fan yells and threatens the crowd. Terry : The Black Scorpian gets up on the apron and... HEY! The match hasn't even started yet! Johny : Segier charges and knocks the Scorpian form the apron and onto the concrete. Uh Oh the Fan starts moving towards the ring. Tony : Who is that! Johny : That is Elixir coming from the second row. He nails the Fan in the head with... A Purse? Terry : Real men carry purses... (laughs). Tony : Maybe so... The Fan is down! The ref calls for the bell and this match is officially under way! Johny : I see now... Elixir came from the second row. It looks like Segier's wife is sitting there as well as Elixir's manager... GI Jane. Terry : The Black Scorpian is up. Now Segier will PAY! Tony : Wow... The Black Scorpian stands face to face with Elixir. It looks like the Black Scorpian smiled as he jumps up on the apron again. This time Segier lets him into the ring. Terry : Big mistake on the part of Segier. Johny : Don't think so Terry... Segier nails Scorpian with a drop kick to the knee. Tony : The Black Scorpian slumps to the ground and Nik follows up with a few more kicks to the knee of Black Scorpian. The Scorpian in the ropes and the ref asks for Segier to back away. Segier obliges and moves back. Terry : The Black Scoprian is up on his feet trying to shake off the pain in his knee. Tony : The two men to lock up... No! Segier feints for the Scorpian's knee and then grabs a wristlock. The Black Scorpian backs him into the ropes and sends him across with an irish whip. Johny : Scorpian attacks, but Segier with a baseball slide under the attack and Segier up and DROPKICK sending the Black Scorpian to the mat. Great counter by Segier. Black Scorpian up immediately and charges again. Arm drag by Segier and a reverse wristlock. Terry : I think that Segier pulled the Scorpian'ss hair or soemthing...? HA HA... See the Black Scorpian is on his feet now. You can't keep him down. Tony : Segier with the standing wristlock. Ouch.. Segier drives his shoulder into the Scorpian's shoulder. Again, and again... Segier witha burst of quickness set up and SNAP SUPLEX. Segier jumps up and raises his hands in the air and the fans are cheering! Uh... We have word from the back that there is something going on? The camera flashes to the back parking lot and we see David Wattsoner laying face down on the concrete. A crowbar is lying next to him. The camera moves up to show New Star getting in Wattsoner's car and drives away. Medical people enter the scene and begin to attend to Wattsoner. The camera flashes back to the ring where we see Black Scorpian kicking and stomping Segier on the mat. Tony : Wow! New Star apparently attacking David Wattsoner. Terry (laughing): NEW STAR has just demonstrated a LOS ANGELES car jacking! Johny : Well fans while you were gone, the Black Scorpian has capitalized on a mistake by Segier and is now in control of the match. Tony : Black Scorpian with a handful of hair sends Segier into the ropes and CLOTHESLINE! Segier flips in mid air and falls to the mat in a heap. There is the sign from the former champion. Terry : That's RIGHT! Time for the EQUALIZER! The Black Scorpian has it locked on. Wha- Johny : GREAT counter by Segier! He is trying to sit down. This increases the pressure on his neck and throat, but the Black Scorpian can't drop him. Tony : So it appears that the Black Scorpian is modifying the hold. Dragon Sleeper! Terry : Segier is flailing around! It's over! HA HA! Johny : The ref is checking Segier! Segier is trying to fight it. He reaches the ropes! Tony : The ref asks for the break. The Black Scorpian lets Segier drop to the mat and stands up and gets right into the refs face. Johny : Segier is trying to regain his feet as the Black Scorpian turns to face him. Scorpian moves in and sets up a suplex. Blocked by Segier and countered into a swinging neck breaker. Terry : Both men are down now. Get up Scorpian! Tony : Segier is up and he brings Scorpian up by the hair. He sets him up for a suplex. NO! Brainbuster! The Black Scorpian is in trouble now. Segier follows up with a CHOKE! Terry : DQ him ref! Johny : He gets a five count Terry... Segier breaks at 4 and applies the choke again. Again breaking at the 4 count. Segier up and drops an elbow across the Scorpian's neck area. Nikodemous gets the Black Scoprian up to his feet and executes a PILDRIVER! Tony : Segier brings Scorpian back to his feet and Short ARM Clothesline! The Black Scorpian looks to be in severe trouble here. Nik locks on a top wristlock and a takedown. Nik then lock on a sort of scissors headlock submission. Johny : That's right! Segier calls this the "Twist". Terry : NO! Get to the ropes! Tony : No such luck Terry. The Black Scorpian is tapping out. The match goes to SEGIER! Segier beats the House of Pain AGAIN! Terry : Shut up! It was a lucky match. Johny : Luck or not... Segier with a big win here against the Black Scorpian. Tony : Well folks... The moment we have been waiting for. The Lethal lottery starts next! The camera closes and a commercial starts.... The camera fades back onto the commentators. Tony : Welcome back... let's go to the ring for the first match of the Lethal Lottery. The camera goes to the ring where Gary Trudeau is standing with a jar of names in his hands. Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... It is now time for the LETHAL LOTTERY! The first name in the Lethal Lottery will be... Mantis! "Great Milenko" by Insane Clown Posse starts over the PA as Mantis enters the arena to a mixed reaction from the crowd. Mantis stops on the ramp as his music dies down and the camera zooms back to Gary Trudeau in the ring. Gary Trudeau : And his Partner... Sgt Matthew Payne! "The Marines' Hymn" starts over the PA as Sgt Payne enters the arena to a large crowd pop. Mantis immediately walks up to Payne and holds out his hand. Payne looks at him and the to men shake hands. The two men wallk to the ring together. Tony : Wow... That is a good team! It looks like they will be able to work to gether with that handshake. Terry : Someone should step on that bug. Gary Trudeau : Their first opponent will be... Mad COIL! "Feed my Frankenstein" by Alice Cooper starts over the PA as Mad Coil enters the arena to a round of boos. The would be House of Pain member walks to the ring by himself. Gary Trudeau : And Mad Coils partner will be... Matt "The Missle" Pepper! "Push" by Matchbox 20 starts over the PA. The crowd starts to cheer. Matt Pepper starts his approach to the ring from the crowd. He is pushing his way through the crowd. Tony : Well, this will be an interesting match here... Pepper has gone through a couple changes the past few weeks. Johny : So has Mad Coil. I wonder if he and Pepper can work together? Terry : Doesn't matter! Look how big Mad Coil is to everyone else. He doesn't even need a partner. Tony : And the Match begins with Mantis shaking Pepper's hand. Matt tries to hook up with Mantis, but the masked wrestler steps behind for a waistlock. Johny : Pepper is trying to break the hold..works his way to the rope. Pepper gets his feet on the second rope and flips back out of the hold! Slingshot off the ropes, Mantis cross crosses of the other ropes and catches Pepper with a flying forearm. Terry : Mantis tags Payne who locks an armbar on the dazed Matt. Mad Coil is calling for Pepper to get the tag. Tag him you loser! Tony : Pepper drops and sinks his feet into Payne's gut, flipping him across the ring and breaking the armbar. Tag to Mad Coil who charges Payne slamming like a frieght train into the suplex master. Terry : Oh YEAH! That's what I am talking about! Johny : The blow knocks Payne back some, but Coil is not letting up, several clublike forearms to the side of his head before whiping him across. Payne ducks the clothesline and nails the gutwrench suplex. Tony : Payne tags Mantis who goes to kicking Coil. The STB member locks a Scorpian Death lock on Mad Coil..this should be all. Terry : You gotta do more than that! Tony : Coil to his credit is not submitting. Pepper is on the top turnbuckle. Payne yelled to Mantis. Mantis gets out of the way half a second before Pepper hits a body splash. Johny : Instead of saving his partner..I think Matt just took Mad Coil out. Mantis standing dropkick sends Pepper out of the ring. Mantis tags Payne and tells him something. The Masked arielist is climbing the turnbuckles. Payne has Mad Coil hooked and back with a double arm suplex. At the same time Mantis hits the Nutcracker! Tony : Payne bridges, Pepper charges but is leveled with a clothesline from Mantis... three! Total domination by Payne and Mantis! Terry : Mad Coil will have to do more than that to impress the HOP! Johny : Payne and Mantis shake hands once again and then leave the ring. Tony : Let's keep the action going and get to the second match. The camera goes to the ring where Gary Trudeau is standing with a jar of names in his hands. Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Now for the second match in the Lethal lottery. The first name in the next Lethal Lottery match will be... Gregory "The Irish Assassin" O'Hara! "Fuel" by Metallica starts over the PA. The Irish Assassin enters the arena to the boos of the crowd. O'Hara just smiles as he walks to the ring ignoring the crowd. Gary Trudeau : And his Partner... Vinnie "The Bronx" Bomber! "Plush" by The Stone Temple Pilots starts over the PA as Vinnie Bommber enters the arena to a fairly postive crowd responce. Vinnie smiles towards the ring and slaps some hands on the way to the ring. Vinnie enters the ring and points at O'Hara. The two men start to argue. Tony : Uh OH... It looks like this team is having problems already. Terry : Vinnie needs to realize that this is the Irish Assassin, not Todd Logan! Tony : Apparently, Vinnie understands this and calms down. Gary Trudeau : Their first opponent will be... Big Daddy Butler! "Don't Fear the Reaper" by Blue Oyster Cult starts over the PA. Big Daddy Butler enters the arena to a positive crowd reaction. He looks towards the ring staying focused... He stands waiting for his partner. Gary Trudeau : And his partner will be... Big Eliem! Terry : What is this a JOKE! Tony (laughs): This is completely random Terry! Terry : Uh Oh!.... Here comes Butler over here... Butler : (grabbing a mic) Hey Eleim why don't you get your big black carcass out here so we can get this sh** over with. I don't like anymore than you but lets get past tonight and then I'll beat the hell out of ya on Sunday. "Body Count" by Body Count starts over the PA as Big Eliem enters the arena to a round of boos. Big Eliem just yells at the fans. Spits at a few of them as he walks to the ring! Terry : Now you did it you little smurf....here comes Eleim and he doesn't look happy. Tony : Well O'Hara and The Bomber are in the ring and they appear to be discussing how to handle the match as we watch the arrival of Butler and his partner Eleim. Johny : That is the important thing for these guys like Eleim to remember...its just one match...tag up and get to the second round. Terry : Eleim could beat these guys alone. Tony : Well Eleim has walked to ringside and he and Butler are having words. Terry : You tell him Eleim...just blast him. Johny : Real smart Terry...cost yourself the match over some stupid feud. Tony : Well Butler just waved Eleim off and has entered the ring where O'Hara was waiting and jump right on him. Johny : O'Hara is all over Butler. Butler can't get off the mat. O'Hara with drops and elbow. Terry : Eleim has made his way to the apron. I guess he will participate after all. Tony : O'Hara backs Butler into the corner and whips him across to the other side and follows up with a body splash in the corner. Johny : O'Hara picks the downed Butler up and drags him to the corner where he tags in The Bomber. The Bomber climbs to the second turnbuckle and down with and elbow to the out stretched arm of Butler. Tony : The Bomber whips Butler into the ropes and goes for a dropkick, but Butler grabs the ropes and the Bomber misses. Terry : Butler goes for a tag but Eleim turned his back...He won't tag the little smurf. Tony : Better watch it Terry. Johny : Butler just got nailed from behind by the Bomber driving Butler into the corner and knocking Eleim of the apron. Terry : He attacked Eleim while he had his back turned.. Johny : He did not... Tony : Bomber picking Butler up.... Johny : Wait a minute Eleim just entered the ring and nailed Bomber. Terry : I guess he decide to be tagged in afterall. Tony : No...Eleim just grabbed Butler and powerbombed him. Now he is picking him up and delivers a chokeslam. Terry : He just spit on the smurf. He's leaving the ring. Johny : Bomber quickly covers Butler and gets the 1 2 3 and he and O'Hara will advance. Terry : Did you see that....Eleim smash papa smurf. Johny : All yeah...that was smart...now he is out of the tournament. Tony : Lets go to the next match. OH! Wait O'Hara just attacked Bomber from behind. O'Hara is unleashing rights and lefts. Kicking and stomping on the now prone Bomber. Look at O'Hara... He has a smile on his face as he leaves the fallen Bomber behind. Terry : I guess that will teach Vinnie to that again??? Tony : Let's go to the next match and Gary Trudeau... The camera goes to the ring where Gary Trudeau is standing with a jar of names in his hands. Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Now for the third match in the Lethal lottery. The first name in the next Lethal Lottery match will be... Shaman! "Devil Man" by White Zombie starts over the PA. Shaman enters the arena to the cheers of the crowd. Shaman walks to the ring slapping hands. Gary Trudeau : And his Partner... The Hoser! "Staying Alive" by The Bee Gees starts over the PA as The MAN enters the arena to a large Fan POP. The Hoser walks to the ring slapping hands and dancing with the crowd. The disco is cut short as the camera moves back to the ring Gary Trudeau. Gary Trudeau : Their first opponent will be... "The Punisher" Aric Crockett! "Hey Man, Nice Shot" starts over the Pa. Aric comes out to the boos of the crowd. Aric just smiles and walks to the ring yelling right back at the fans. Gary Trudeau : And his partner will be... Bob! Terry : WHAT! How unfair is that! "Bob's Theme" somes on as Bob's Driver encourts Bob's carcass to the ring. The crowd is chanting "BOB", "BOB", "BOB", "BOB". He just grins and walks to the ring Tony : Boy! Aric looks very upset! Terry : I wonder why! His partner is that Hurman Munster reject! No Fair! Tony : You know the pairings were totally random. Terry : But...BOB!! Johny : Aric is glaring at his partner and screaming at him. Terry : Oh yeah..this is going to be a good team. Tony : There is the bell and looks like Aric and Hoser to start. The two lock up, headlock by Aric. Johny : Hoser shoves him off to the ropes. Drop down as Aric hops over to the other side. Leapfrog over Aric, then drop onto his back for a monkey flip. Terry : But Aric being the experienced wrestler saw it coming, dropping the knee to the face. Crockett goes to tag Bob.... Where is Bob? Johny : Bob is down at the guardrail smiling at a little girl. Terry : Why isn't he paying attention! Tony : Aric is ticked! He's yelling at Bob who is looking up to the ring with a childlike grin. Johny : Oh, Shaman just slammed into Aric with a running clothesline. Hoser shook off the knee and had made the tag. Terry : This isn't fair! Teaming with Bob is like going into a handicap match..3 on 1! Tony : Shaman lifts Aric by the neck and tosses him into the corner. Aric is stunned while Shaman lays a big boot to the face. And Down Crockett goes. Tag to Hoser. Johny : And Bob is finally back on the ring apron. Hoser on the top rope..Air Hoser! Terry : Aric let that air out fast bringing the knees up. Aric slowly getting to his feet and locks a standing full nelson on the smaller Hoser. Johny : The HOP member drags Hoser in the nelson to his corner. He is yelling a Bob to hit Hoser. Tony : Bob isn't going to do that. Terry : HIT HIM DAMN IT! Bob is just standing there blinking at Crockett. Tony : Aric lets go of Hoser and slaps Bob. Johny : And Bob just unleashes a hellashious right cross to Aric's jaw. Aric is out! Hoser is hooking the leg.. three! Shaman and Hoser advance! Terry : What the hell was that! Tony : Shaman is over with Bob talking to the gentle giant. Terry : GENTLE! He hit his own partner! I demand a rematch! Johny : Maybe when Aric wakes he will want one too..he is still out as the three men head to the back. Terry : I don't believe this! F@(*ng BOB! Tony : TERRY! Watch it! Let's go to the next match... The camera goes to the ring where Gary Trudeau is standing with a jar of names in his hands. Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Now for the final round in the Lethal lottery. The first name in the match will be... Zack "The Enforcer" Crockett! "Hey Man, Nice Shot" starts over the Pa. Zack comes out to the boos of the crowd. Zack just smiles and walks to the ring yelling right back at the fans. Gary Trudeau : And his partner will be... "Stealth" Shane Warner! Terry : What...you've got to be kidding. Denrol walks to the ring splashing "holy" water on the crowd. Gary Trudeau : And his partner will be and the LAST Lethal Lottery competitor... "Hollywood" Johny Tambo! "More Human Than Human" blasts over the speakers as HJT walks out from behind the curtain. Jennifer walks out next, dressed in a skin tight Catwoman suit. She walks in front of HJT and cuddles up against him. the duo walk down to the ring, Tambo and Jen jaw with the fans along the way. Tony : Well HJT had some derogatory remarks with regard to Denrol earlier this week. Terry : Maybe he will drop him like Big Eleim dropped Butler Johny : I think HJT is a little to smart for that. Terry : What does that mean. Insert Entrance for Father Denrol Tony : Well Denrol entered the ring with caution and these two men are staring each other down. Johny : It appears Denrol is beginning to talk to HJT...possibly explaining the pros and cons of working as a team as opposed to against each other. Oh...he just ran right into Warner knocking both men to the mat. Johny : Zack jumps on Warner and begins pounding away. Tony : What is he doing... Terry : He has lost it.
