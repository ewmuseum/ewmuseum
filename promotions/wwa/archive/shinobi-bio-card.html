What this is Roster bio card for the WWA wrestler Shinobi, giving measurements, hometown, style, ring attire, entrance music, physical appearance, background, manager, finishing and signature moves.
Shinobi Name: Takashi Miura Height: 5'8" Weight: 202 From: Kyoto, Japan Style: Martial Arts Aerialist Ring Attire: He wears green shorts with two black stripes down each side. He wears green kneepads, green elbow pads, black wristbands and black boots. Out of the ring he wears a black t-shirt with Shinobi written on it. Entrance Music: "Zero" by Smashing Pumpkins Physical Appearance: He is a little Japanese guy. He is well-built and has well-defined muscles. He has died blond hair down to just below his ears. I guess he looks most like Matsato Tanaka. Background: In Japan Shinobi is already legendary for his incredible high-flying, hardcore matches. However this is not enough for him, he wants to famous everywhere and has joined the WWA to make sure that everyone knows his name! He was brought up in a very wealthy family who sent him to the best school. He is very well-spoken and extremely intelligent. He is a handsome guy and is every Japanese schoolgirl's fantasy man. However several years of extreme matches have taken their toll, although he seems perfectly normal Miura occassionally he will say or do something totally random and no-one will be entirely sure why. However, apart from random insanity, Takashi Miura is superior athelete, he takes hsi anme from the greatest group of ninjas of all time and he aims to be the greatest wrestler of all time! Manager: HAKEEM: He is a 6'6'', 500lbs Black guy. He is Miura's personal assitant and bodyguard. He is not the smartest guy in the world but he is very loyal and will do anything for his boss. He was hired to keep control of Miura's screaming fans but now he also accompanies Shinobi to the ring. He takes no crap from nobody. He rarely gets involved in matches unless instructed to do so by his boss. Finishing Moves: SHINOBI DRIVER: Spinning Cradle Piledriver SHURIKEN: Flying Flip-Over Leg Drop Signature Moves: 1: 202: Flying Crucifix Cradle 2: Frankensteiner 3: Huricanrana 4: Tornado DDT 5: Flying Karate Kick 6: Springboard Bulldog 7: Tarantula 8: Handspring Heel Kick 9: Tiger Driver 10: Northern Lights Suplex
