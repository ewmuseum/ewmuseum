Kraven falls forward knocking down Ventressa as he goes. Keane stands in the center of the carnage with his hands on his hips and his chest puffed out. Mighty Joe: Alright evil-doer! Let's take this back outside and away from all these innocent bystanders. Joe grabs Kraven by the collar and slowly, with great effort, drags the bloody Tomorrow Man toward the door. The hostess opens the door and the two combatants disappear into the muggy New Orleans night. Ventressa and the co-eds help Moore to his feet. Tears stream down his face as he tries to blink away the hot sauce. The foursome find an empty table and sit down. Ventressa: Alright folks, show's over. The next round is on the big guy! The meager crowd cheers. Moore: Thanks! Ventressa: Oh, like you can't afford it, Mr. Movie Star! The camera now cuts to Harley Trapper riding his red Harley-Davidson slowly down an empty back street. He swings round a corner and sees EVD on his stolen black Ducatti motorcycle. Fat Tony: So many motorcycles! It's like a Hell's Angels convention! Jimmy: Not really, as there are only two bikes... Fat Tony: Welll... it could be a small convention? The two men spot each other at the opposite ends of a deserted road. They both look determined and unwavering. They rev up their bikes as the tension mounts. Jimmy: Oh my god! What are these two going to do? Fat Tony: You ever see that movie "Knight Riders"? They stare at each other and then rev up their bikes one last time. They speed towards each other. Jimmy: This is insane! Please don't try this at home! The bikes narrowly miss one another, speeding to opposite ends of the street before screeching to almost simultaneous stops and turning back to face one another. Trapper unslings his baseball bat from the scabbard-type device on his back and grins, before gunning his engine. EVD does the same, and the two men speed towards each other again. Jimmy: Someone is going to get killed here! Fat Tony: Cool isn't it? As the two bikes pass, Trapper swings for Van Dorn's head and only narrowly misses. Jimmy: That was a close one for EVD, it could have been over right there! Fat Tony: If that had connected his life could have been over, let alone his match! Jimmy: Those bikes are travelling at least 90 miles an hour, this is extremely dangerous! Fat Tony: Stop whining about danger! These two are insane, they don't care about the danger, let's see who the most insane is! Again the two men swing their bikes around and speed towards each other. Trapper readies his bat again. As they get closer Eddie lets go of the handle bars and leans forward on his bike. Jimmy: What is EVD doing? Fat Tony: I don't know but I think it will be cool. The bikes are almost together. Trapper swings his bat round his head. Eddie grins like a maniac, then jumps forward off his bike! Fat Tony: I can't watch! EVD jumps into Trapper and spears him off the bike. Trapper flies backwards through a shop window with Eddie landing next to him. They are both bleeding from cuts on their arms and faces. We watch a replay of the incredible stunt, and can hear the fans assembled around the commentary desk chanting "Holy $hit!" Jimmy: Holy mother of God! That is incredible! Fat Tony: For once I am lost for words. Jimmy: EVD with a huge spear from his bike onto the other and then they both crashed through the window of that hardware store. I think they may both need medical help! We come back to the action as EVD somehow flops a weak arm over Harley Trapper, the ref counts. Ref: 1... 2... 3!!! Jimmy: Eddie Van Dorn eliminates the King of the Streets with an incredibly dangerous move! Fat Tony: Incredibly dangerous but incredibly awesome! Jimmy: Eddie Van Dorn is an incredible athlete. He just hit a spear from a moving motorcycle! Fat Tony: It looks like EVD may be an incredibly stupid athlete, too! Eddie Van Dorn might have eliminated himself in the process! We now cut to another replay of the incredible stunt, and as we come back neither man has moved and a couple of EMTs are tending to them both. It may appear for once that Tony's diagnosis is correct. We now cut away, and find that "Mighty" Joe and Jackson Kraven are still battling it out, and are heading down an alleyway. Jimmy: These two men are incredible athletes. Kraven’s been bleeding for almost the entirety of this match! The alleyway leads to a warehouse on a wharf. Kraven blocks a left and then a right before grabbing Keane by the hair and hurling him through the doors of the warehouse with a massive clang. We follow Kraven into the warehouse and take the scene in. The warehouse is almost empty, but in the middle of the huge room is a giant circular pool, filled with oysters and freezing water. Kraven’s nose wrinkles- it is obvious that the smell is horrendous. We now cut to a split-screen replay of EVD and Harley Trapper’s earlier antics. On the other side we see Venny LeTrav and Tracer Bullet, watching the TV in the bar we saw them in earlier. Venny counts on his fingers and realisation dawns on him. Venny: You know, I think we probably shouldn’t have drunk quite so much. There’s only you, me, Kraven and Keane left in this match. LeBeaux nods, finishes his drink and gets off his barstool, getting into a fighting stance. Venny finishes his drink too and gets up. Venny: May the best Cajun win. The two men lock up in the middle of the bar. Tracer wins the lockup, circling into a waistlock and then goes for a German Suplex. Venny isn’t as drunk as you’d have thought, and manages to flip around and land on his feet on the bar! Jimmy: What a display of agility! Before Tracer has time to turn around, LeTrav leaps off the bar and onto Tracer’s shoulders, hitting a victory roll! There is no ref to make the count, but it doesn’t matter, because Tracer rolls through the pin and makes a cover of his own. He realises that there is no referee and lets go of his fellow Cajun, helping him to his feet. LeTrav looks confused. Venny: What? Tracer: No ref. Venny: Ah. Outside? Tracer: Outside. Venny: Deal. The two head out of the bar, but Tracer comes back in, leaving a small pile of change on the bar as a tip. We now cut back to the Oyster Factory, where we see that Kraven and Keane are no longer on the floor level, but on one of the gantries that criss-cross the factory. Kraven goes for a short-arm clothesline but Keane ducks under it and goes for a schoolboy rollup. There is no ref to make the count, but it doesn’t really matter as Kraven swiftly reverses the rollup into a small package! We now cut back to the announcers’ desk, where Fat Tony and Jimmy have been joined by former WWA Head Booker John Arthur. The former Head Booker looks very stressed and wears a headset walkie-talkie. Fat Tony: John, what are you doing here? John: Where are they? Jimmy: Well, LeTrav and Tracer are outside a bar and Kraven and Keane are busy in an oyster factory… why? John: I need to know! There’s no referees when they’re needed and Matt’s throwing a fit! Fat Tony: Well, I think the oyster factory’s quite near the Acme Oyster House that Kraven and Keane wrecked… John: It is? Thanks! The former Head Booker sprints off out of shot, and we cut to a split-screen again. A little “live” symbol appears in the corner of both shots. On one side we see Tracer Bullet get hit with an Enziguri by Venny LeTrav. On the other we see Craven Moore and Vik Ventressa watching the match. Moore: Ladies, we have to go. Vik: Yeah. Tex’ll pick up the bill. We cut to full-screen again as Venny now tries to hit Tracer with a second enziguri. Tracer is too smart, however, and turns the move into a Dragon Screw Legwhip! The crowd who are watching them fight cheers loudly, and cheer even more loudly as a referee with a headset walkie-talkie turns up! We now cut back to the oyster factory. We watch as Keane gets the upper hand, knocking Kraven down with a neckbreaker variation and moving in, mounting the two-time former World Champion and punching him repeatedly. Now we cut back to Venny and Tracer. Tracer picks up the fallen LeTrav and tries to whip him into a nearby wall. LeTrav reverses the whip and sends Tracer down an alleyway instead! Jimmy: What will we see next!?! We cut back to Kraven VS Keane. Down on the floor which is now quite far below the two combatants we see John Arthur and a referee run into the factory. Arthur gestures upwards, and the referee begins the ascent. Almost two storeys up and still some way from the top of the warehouse , Keane picks up Kraven and tries for a Tornado DDT, but Kraven has scouted the move and slams Joe to the gantry with a modified BearHug Slam. The gantry shakes and the crowd gasp. Keane rolls away as Kraven tries to drop an elbow on him, but gasps in horror as he nearly rolls off the gantry! Fat Tony: These two men are dicing with death. Jimmy: Mighty Joe and the man who destroyed his title shot... Jackson Kraven. Could it be any more fitting that two larger-than-life superstars are still fighting in these, the undoubted closing stages of this larger-than-life match? Kraven gets up a second after Joe, who heads up another set of stairs to the very top of the gantry. The crowd gasp as every punch thrown and every step taken shakes the flimsy metal structure. We now cut back to LeTrav and Tracer Bullet’s portion of this match, which we see has ended up on the wharf again! LeTrav tries for a Hangman’s Dropkick, but Tracer drops into the splits again. Fat Tony: OK, doing it once is impressive. Doing it twice is just showing off. As LeTrav gets back up, Tracer grabs him and… Jimmy: Pounding The Pa… Jimmy doesn’t finish. The impact of Tracer’s finisher on the wharf, following the abuse it took during War VS Sanders and Van Dorn VS War is enough to crack it into pieces, dropping both men through the wood and into the murky waters below! The crowd watching around the announcers’ desk and the crowd who followed the two from outside the bar break into the by-now-familiar “Holy $hit!” chant. Fat Tony: They destroyed the wharf! These two men destroyed the wharf! Jimmy: This is terrible! Are they okay! The ref yells into his walkie-talkie to someone. We now cut back to the oyster factory, where Joe Keane rocks Jackson Kraven with a series of right-handed jabs to the jaw, Marquess of Queensberry-rules-style! Jimmy: May I remind everyone that these men are fighting eighty feet up in the air!?! We cut back to the wharf. The lights attached to the camera glare off the face of the referee. He gulps and begins to speak. Referee: Because we are unsure of the well-being of both Tracer Bullet and Venny LeTrav, we have decided to eliminate both men from this match. I repeat, we have eliminated both men in the interests of their own safety. We’re sending some lifeguards down there to see if they’re okay. We now cut back to the announcers’ desk. Jimmy looks shocked. Jimmy: Folks, I… Venny Letrav and Tracer Bullet have been ruled out of this contest because they may have been seriously hurt during that fall of the wharf! I- Fat Tony: interrupting Who cares!?! We still have Jackson Kraven and “Mighty” Joe Keane in the oyster factory! Let’s get back there quick! Can we- We cut back suddenly as “Mighty” Joe hits another punch and then another. He steps back, kisses his hand and lets fly, staggering Kraven to the very edge of the gantry! Sensing that he has the upper hand, Joe catches Kraven in the position for a final Truth and Justice on his huge foe as the crowd cheer, but Kraven reacts too fast and elbows him hard in the face, breaking up the move and his nose! Joe falls back clutching his bloodied nose, but goes to face Kraven again despite his half-blind state. Fat Tony: “Mighty” stupid idea, buddy! Jackson kicks Joe in the midsection and places his head between his legs. They are now directly above the pool of oysters, with Moore and Ventressa on the floor at the opposite side of the building, too far to do anything but be spectators to the horrifying scene which may be about to unfold. John Arthur yells frantically into his walkie-talkie. The crowd’s cheers begin to fade out as the bloodied Tomorrow Man lifts Joe into a powerbomb position as the crowd screams and the ref begs him to let go. He then lifts Joe even higher, and holds him in the air for about 4 seconds hang-time. Jimmy: No, for the love of God! He’s a young man! Don’t do this! You don’t need to do this, Kraven! You could end Joe Keane’s career right here! We have a marvellous shot of the huge Jackson Kraven holding Joe in the air from Craven Moore’s point of view. Kraven then releases Joe and completes the Kravenbomb. We hear the crowd at the announcers’ desk scream, cheer, gasp, look away and roar all at the same time as Joe falls approx eighty feet into the oyster pool, landing with a tremendous splash which sends oysters high into the air. The ref slowly and reluctantly raises the Tomorrow Man's hand, realising that whatever Kraven does next, “Mighty” Joe won't be getting back up. Ventressa holds back Moore on the ground whilst up on the gantry Jackson Kraven smiles evilly at the big Texan. He has his title shot... Or does he? Up the ladder to the gantry comes a man no-one expected to see again in this match- Eddie Van Dorn! Every move Van Dorn takes seems to send him fresh agonies, and he is covered by tiny cuts from that amazing motorcycle-assisted Kraven looks as though he’s ready to spear Eddie off the ladder up to the gantry, but Van Dorn motions for him to stay put until he’s up there as well. Once there EVD looks down at the pool and pantomimes disgust before turning back to Kraven. EVD: Congratulations, bud. You're going to the Rumble as the number one contender. Kraven is rather apprehensive, as he knows Eddie's history of pulling devious swerves at any given moment. Kraven: Why should I believe you? EVD: First of all, I was in here for the fight, not the win. Kraven looks incredulous and steps towards the much-smaller Van Dorn. EVD, to his credit, doesn't back down. EVD: Yeah sure, I could just as easily kick your ass as give you the victory right now, but I won't. Why? Well, not only was I in this more for the fight itself, but there's also the fact that if I take the title shot at IDR, I'll miss participating in the Rumble. I would much rather go on to the Rumble and get the title shot THERE. You understand. Kraven: Oh, I understand alright. I'll take the win. No skin off *my* nose. Eddie turns to go, but an evil grin crosses Kraven's face. Kraven: Oh, Eddie? Van Dorn turns a second too late to stop the hands that shoot towards his throat. Kraven hoists his far-smaller foe off his feet, his face a mess of smeared paint and blood. As Van Dorn chokes and writhes mid-air, Kraven smiles, spitting a mouthful of blood at his foe before speaking. Kraven: I'll take your win... and a little revenge for last year. Kraven drops EVD with a huge No More Tomorows right onto the steel grilled floor that shakes the entire gantry. Kraven drops onto Van Dorn with a lateral press, and the ref knows better than to not make the count. 1! 2! 3! A little WWA logo appears in one corner of the screen with show credits. We see Craven Moore helping to hoist a near-unconscious "Mighty" Joe out of the oyster pool, concern written all over his face for the younger man. John Arthur yells frantically into his headset walkie-talkie. Fat Tony: We have a winner! We have a winner! We now cut to Kraven, still holding EVD in the lateral press position and grinning, his teeth covered in his own blood, ice-blue eyes burning with feral rage in the crimson mask that is now his face. Fat Tony: That man is going to Independence Day Rumble! Jimmy: I pity the WWA if he makes it three title reigns! I really do! Craven Moore has the destiny of the entire federation in his hands! Truth and Justice or No More Tomorrows... it's all up to him! Fat Tony: It sure is! Jimmy: Folks, we're out of time! I've been Jimmy Rose... Fat Tony: And I've been having a whale of a time! Jackson Kraven is going to Independence Day Rumble! Jimmy: Good night everybody! We'll see you in Boston! We fade to black.
