The Gamblers Table — Rage in the Cage 2003

27 Nov 2003

Bylined "The Gambler" (Kevin/Vic Sage), the fed's own in-house prediction columnist persona.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone, and welcome back to the Gambler’s Table. With Rage In The Cage 2003 less than a week away, it’s time for me, “The Gambler” Kevin Sage, to share my thoughts and predictions for one of the most highly anticipated events in professional wrestling. WWA’s Rage In The Cage is certainly a unique event. Every match, from the opening to the main event, takes place within the foreboding confines of a steel cage. This Pay-Per-View event is not for those with a weak constitution. With eight incredible matches to cover, let’s dive right in. Mal Somers and Dave Harley vs. Leviathan and Lockdown No Disqualification Saturday Night Fiesta has been turned upside down by the emergence of the tag team called the Cell. Leviathan and Lockdown’s unholy alliance with Commissioner Antonio Cruz has given them ample opportunity to display their cold brutality. The Extreme Corporation, Mal Somers and Dave Harley, have risen recently as fan favorites, and perhaps the only thing standing between The Cell and total domination. Leviathan and Lockdown are two of the most vicious competitors to enter the WWA in a long while, as evidenced by the way that they have run roughshod over Fiesta as Cruz’ personal enforcers. This No Disqualification match would seem to be in their favor, but we can’t overlook Mal Somers and Dave Harley. The Extreme Corporation, as they call themselves, are no strangers to world of the extreme, and this match will provide them with the opportunity to show us all what the word “extreme” is really about. If their previous battles in the EWA are any indication, these four men will open RITC with a bloodbath. In the end, I’m willing to bet that we’ve only seen the tip of the iceberg for the Extreme Corporation. WWA Television Champion Christyan Payne vs. Shaman vs. Nick Adams vs. Grunge WWA Television Title This heated battle has been growing since Christyan Payne stole the Television Title from Nick Adams with help from the tag team, Mack Tenn. Since that time, Mack Tenn has interfered on numerous occasions to help insure that Payne retains the title. Nick Adams has also kept his eye on the belt, jumping through whatever hoops the Commissioner has placed before him to get his title shot. The race for the TV Title got more interesting when Payne and Grunge faced off on Meltdown. In that match, Payne brought a chair into the ring, but Grunge managed to avoid it and got the pin. Payne pointed out the chair to the referee, who then disqualified Grunge from the match, despite the fact that Grunge never touched the chair. Enraged by Payne’s underhanded tactics, Grunge took matters into his own hands, interfering in Payne’s most recent match on Meltdown. Fed up with this state of affairs, President Matt Warr insured that Grunge would not interfere in the title match at RITC by making Grunge part of it! Commissioner Cruz had his part in the booking of this match as well. When Nick Adams questioned Cruz’ ability to run a show like Fiesta, Cruz acquiesced to allow Adams his title shot at RITC, but also added Shaman to the mix! This is one wild ride that I would not want to miss. Christyan Payne is as devious as he is talented in that squared circle. His manipulations have allowed him to retain the title for quite a while. On the other hand, Nick Adams is a quick learner. After having suffered through some of Payne’s manipulations, I’m sure that Adams will be prepared for any eventuality. Grunge, in many opinions, is poised on the edge of greatness. Winning the TV Title here would be only the beginning for Grunge. Of course, one must never underestimate Shaman, one of the most psychopathic monsters ever to spill blood in the WWA. By far the largest competitor in this bout, Shaman brings an element of unpredictability that could change everything. In the end, despite the hard-fought road to victory that this match entails, I believe that Adams’ focus and grit will carry him to victory. My money’s on Adams. Jimmi Zion vs. Chance vs. Enigma WWA Cruiserweight/Extreme Titles The war between Chance and Enigma started at Birthday Bash, where Chance won the Extreme Title from Enigma. On Meltdown, we witnessed Enigma’s symbolic burial of his alter ego, and Cruiserweight Title holder, Ekosyde. This left Enigma with both titles, and a host of individuals eager to strip them from him. Then, Jimmi Zion, the original Cruiserweight Champion made his return to the WWA. Zion, with his beautiful wife Mikalia at his side, immediately set out to reclaim the prestige that was once his. This led to a handicap match with Chance and Zion against Enigma. In a very controversial decision, the referee in that match declared Chance and Zion to be joint champions of both titles! Needless to say, no man involved in the decision was pleased with it, and all three men have made it their personal mission to unify these two prominent titles. Although he is a tremendous athlete, I can’t go with Zion in this one. His wife was recently injured at the hands of Cruz’ despicable Cell, and I believe that this will prove to weigh too heavily on him. Chance and Enigma are evenly matched competitors, both with incredible talents and determination. In the end though, I think that the steel cage will play into a competitor’s crueler instincts, and that’s why I’ll be betting on Enigma. Tag Team Champions The American Luchadores vs. Havoc vs. ??? Venny LeTrav and Justin Bourne, the original American Luchadores, managed to claim the Tag Team Titles at the Birthday Brawl. When Havoc stepped forward for their chance at the titles, Venny found himself alone because of Justin Bourne’s contract troubles. Luckily for Venny, Mighty Joe stepped up to the challenge and, together, they successfully defended the Tag Team Titles as the new incarnation of the American Luchadores. Havoc took revenge after the match, but the Texas Longhorns interrupted them, saving Venny and Joe from their wrath. Havoc vented their rage on the Longhorns in a contender’s match, absolutely demolishing the Lone Star legends. The next week, Havoc and the American Luchadores agreed to meet at Rage In The Cage. Furthermore, the Luchadores suggested inviting the Longhorns to participate. TXL declined however, announcing their retirement on Meltdown. To make things interesting, they suggested that another team, to be named at RITC, take their place in the cage. Both teams agreed, despite the mystery of the third team. As a thank-you for their years of work, Matt Warr allowed TXL to choose the stipulations for this match, and they chose Tables, Ladders, and Chairs. The American Luchadores are incredibly talented wrestlers, able to go toe-to-toe with anyone in the WWA. Unfortunately, signs indicate that Venny and Joe may be experiencing a bit of friction. Without total teamwork and commitment, that cage can become a deathtrap real quick. Havoc has been named as the overwhelming odds favorite, but Havoc’s recent string of losses before their victory over the Longhorns, makes me reticent to put my money on them. This mystery team, regardless of who they are, will have their hands full trying to overcome the phenomenal talent of the Luchadores, and the astonishing power and dominance of Havoc. In the end, I would be a fool to bet on an unknown team. Therefore, I’ll go with the crowd and put my money on Havoc. Tracer Bullet vs. Shane Dorian I Quit Match The war of words between Shane Dorian and Tracer Bullet has escalated in recent weeks to become a powder keg of hatred. In the beginning, they were only exchanging barbs. Those barbs became angry, and things broke out into a vicious locker room brawl that left Tracer Bullet bloodied and broken. Not satisfied with that, Dorian continued taunting Tracer Bullet at every opportunity. Bullet, the picture of self-control, seemed to take it all in stride, even slinging a few of his own subtle taunts back. But the outlook changed when Shane Dorian handcuffed Tracer to the ring, and made him watch, helplessly, as Dorian powerbombed his wife, Melissa to the canvas. Anyone who knows Tracer Bullet knows that he is protective of his friends and his family beyond anything else in life. Shane Dorian may have opened a proverbial Pandora’s Box with this heinous act. Dorian’s size and strength will present a formidable obstacle to the Cajun Detective. Despite his strength and cruelty, it looks like Dorian’s last vile act may very well herald his downfall. Everyone knows that Bullet’s stalled career is the result of a lack of drive, a lack of focus on Tracer’s part. Now, Dorian has given him someone to focus on, and that may be the biggest mistake of Dorian’s career. I’ll put my money on Tracer Bullet. WWA US Champion Kash vs. Damian Thorne WWA US Title As seems to be a recurring theme for RITC, here is another example of the extremes of hate. These men have tried their hardest to cause grievous injury to the other, with no thought as to their own well-being, on more occasions than I count. They’ve even gone so far as to play chicken in speeding automobiles, nearly ending both of their promising careers. Damian Thorne, the undisputed leader of the House of Pain since his victory over the legendary Randy Cross at Birthday Bash, is one of the most lethal and ruthless men in this business, perhaps second only to his stablemate, Jackson Kraven. They call him Mr. Nasty for a reason, folks. Kash, on the other hand, has risen quickly in the WWA with a combination of power, speed, technical knowledge, and unwavering instinct. Many are expecting great things from Kash, after his victory in the Birthday Brawl to claim the US Title. There’s no doubt that the occultist, Kash, is one of the hottest new superstars of the WWA, but does he have what it takes to overcome the “Baddest MoFo on the Show?” Regardless of the outcome, I hope the EMTs are quick, because someone won’t be walking away from this one under their own power. When all is said and done, and the carnage has abated, I’ll expect to see Damian Thorne with the US Title in hand, and that’s where I’ll put my money. Denrol vs. Jackson Kraven Cell in a Frozen Hell Jackson Kraven, undoubtedly the most sadistic monster in the WWA, seems to have made it his personal mission to invoke the ire of Denrol. He tried to retire Denrol at March Madness 2002, only to see Denrol make a comeback. At Labor Day Brawl, Denrol defeated Jackson Kraven in a cage to move on to the finals in the WWA World Title tournament. Incensed, Kraven got his chance for retribution on the October 7th edition of Meltdown. In that epic confrontation, it looked as though Kraven may have finally ended Denrol’s career with a running powerbomb onto the concrete floor. What would have crippled a lesser man only served to strengthen Denrol’s resolve, prompting him to return at the Birthday Brawl and take Jackson Kraven’s chance at the World Title away. Since that time, Denrol and Kraven have exchanged heated words and threats, and their mutual hatred has exploded into absolute abhorrence. This all culminated on this most recent Meltdown, when Jackson Kraven viciously attacked Denrol’s pregnant wife. One can only imagine the bitter wrath that Denrol feels toward Kraven after such a horrible event. At RITC, they’ll face off in a hideous three-story construct of steel and barbed wire, with catwalks, and a barbed wire kendo stick, exposed to the harsh elements of winter near Calgary, to settle this once and for all. To insure that interference will not be a factor, each of the two doors that lead into this hellish contraption will be chained and padlocked five times over. This match can only end when one man is unable to respond to a ten count after a pinfall. If the pinned combatant is able to go on, then the match continues. This is the only way to win, no disqualifications, no submissions, and no countouts. One has to wonder just how far these two men will go to destroy the other, and if anyone will be able to walk away from this horrendous clash. Normally, I try not to let my outrage get in the way of rational thought, but my bet goes with Denrol, just because I want to see Kraven get his just desserts. *Note: This match will be fought on the Saturday preceding RITC, with every WWA superstar in attendance, so make your bets early. And while your at it, you may want to say a prayer for the men that are putting their lives on the line in this horrifying match. WWA World Champion "The Prototype" Ian Tense vs. "The Rage" Wes Sanders WWA World Title Iron Man Match This battle has been brewing for a long time. The devious champion, Ian Tense, will finally meet up with “The Rage” Wes Sanders in a steel cage, with Iron Man rules. The man to score the most pinfalls in one full hour of grueling combat will walk away with the most prestigious title in the world of professional wrestling, the WWA World Title! “The Prototype” Ian Tense has proven, time and again, that he is more than capable of fighting any man on any front. His technical skills are second to none, and his calculating strategies border on genius at times. And yet, here comes Wes Sanders. Tense has done everything possible to assuage Sanders’ relentless pursuit. He has thrown Sanders into the path of the tag team Havoc, he has placed Sanders in the hands of the sadistic Jackson Kraven, and he has pulled out every taunt that he knows in an attempt to shake Sanders’ relentless quest. In spite of it all, Wes Sanders has overcome every plot, walked away from every trap, and bested every taunt. His hate for Ian Tense has burned unabated for months, and now Tense will be trapped in a cage with a man that has only two things on his mind, claiming the WWA World Title, and destroying Ian Tense. With his remarkable martial arts prowess, and in-ring ability, Sanders may just be the man to do it. One should never underestimate the cunning of Ian Tense, however. Tense’s victories over superstars like Shane Dorian and Troy Walcott, as well as his amazing victory in the Birthday Brawl, show that Ian Tense has what it takes for a long reign with the World Title. This will be Ian Tense’s first real title defense since winning the belt, and it may be the most challenging match of his career. After the hour has passed, I’m putting my money on “The Rage” to capture the gold, although I’ll be on the edge of my seat for the entire time. With the animosity running deep throughout this event, the moniker “Rage In The Cage” has never been more appropriate. There you have it, the first WWA Pay-Per-View of the new year, and the first since signing a television contract with Primetime Central. With the increased fan base that has come from this lucrative contract, this is likely to be the most watched event in WWA history. Until the next mind-blowing WWA PPV event, this is Kevin Sage, reminding you that gambling is the surest way to get nothing from something. Design and Graphics © World Wrestling Alliance 2003
