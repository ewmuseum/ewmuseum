Now we watch incredulously as Kraven grabs the referee's arm and slams it down on the mat. 1! 2! 3! Kraven gets up and kicks Moore's body off Keane. He mouths something at the timekeeper, who rings the bell! DING, DING, DING! Gary Trudeau: Your winner by pinfall and STILL WWA World Heavyweight Champion... CRAVEN MOORE! The crowd do not cheer. Instead they seem confused, and they are even more confused as they watch Kraven help Keane to his feet. Jimmy: Folks, I'm at a loss here. Would somebody please tell me what's going on?!? Kraven calls for a mic as he dusts Keane off. He receives one, and grins at the smaller man. Kraven: Sorry about that, Joe. I couldn't very well let you win my title, now could I? The crowd boo, and Kraven motions for silence. Kraven: I know I said I'd be your equalizer, and I know I haven't been all that great at it, but... there he is. Knock yourself out, he's all yours. Keane looks suspiciously at Kraven. Kraven gestures at Moore. Keane grins evilly. Jimmy: What the Hell? The crowd boo as "Mighty" Joe extends the hand of friendship to the evil Tomorrow Man. Jimmy: What the... this was a set-up?!? This was a set-up all along! Joe Keane has joined forces with Jackson Kraven! Joe Keane has sold his soul to Satan! Fat Tony: This is great! Keane and Kraven shake hands centre-ring over the fallen Texan. The crowd boo. Jimmy: I'm appalled! Absolutely appalled! Kraven tries to break Keane's grip, but Keane won't let go. Kraven: Excuse me? Keane's grin widens yet further. Realisation dawns in Kraven's eyes a second too late, and the crowd roar as Joe summons up every ounce of strength in his body to take down the Tomorrow Man with a Yuranage! Fat Tony: What the... God-damn it! He tricked him! Jimmy: Truth and Justice! Truth and Justice! That's what he calls that move! We see a replay of "Mighty" Joe using his new finisher on Kraven. As we cut back to real life Kraven has rolled out of the ring and is making his way back up the ramp, holding the small of his back and staring daggers at Keane. In the ring, "Mighty" Joe offers a hand to the fallen Moore and helps him to his feet. The crowd cheer as the now-revived referee hands the WWA World Title belt to Moore, who holds it up over his head to a wave of flash photography as "Voodoo Chile" plays. Jimmy: Truth and Justice has prevailed! Fat Tony: Yeah, but Jackson Kraven has the last laugh! He's protected Craven Moore's title reign, and his title shot at Independence Day Rumble! Jimmy: Kraven will have to get through the Bourbon Street Brawl first, Tony. And the twenty-or-so other men in that match will certainly have something to say about that! A little "WWA" logo appears in the bottom of the screen. Jimmy: We're almost out of time! I've been Jimmy Rose... Fat Tony: And I've been Tony Valentino... Jimmy: What will happen on next week's Meltdown? Fat Tony: Forget Meltdown, how about Fiesta? That's coming our way too! Jimmy: It is indeed! Goodnight, everybody! We fade out to black on an image of Craven Moore giving Joe Keane a friendly pat on the back...
