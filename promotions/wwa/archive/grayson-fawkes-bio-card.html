Grayson Fawkes Basic Information Nicknames Real Name | He got rid of it long ago Height | 6' 6" Weight | 286 lbs. Date of Birth | Unknown Home Town | Unknown Theme | "Everything I touch" - Stabbing Westward More Details Appearance A large, imposing figure. Fawkes always wears a full-faced mask that is half black and half white. He is built like an SUV and while not overly muscular his strength is apparent just by looking at him. Ring Attire In the ring he wears a simple black wrestling singlet. On the chest are the letters "GF" in white lettering. He wears simple black boots with white trim and black MMA gloves. During his walk to the ring, he wears a black hooded ring vest with the hood up; on the back of the vest is the image of his mask with the same "GF" from his singlet underneath the picture. Outside of the ring Fawkes can be seen wearing long sleeved button up shirts, dress slacks, dress shoes and a black button up vest. Personality Unpredictable yet stoic, mind numbingly violent yet frighteningly intelligent. The man is a walking contradiction who wants nothing more than to make a name for himself by destroying as many people as he needs to. At WWA events, he has a manager, Joseph Dawes, who does all of the talking for him. Dawes is more or less your atypical smooth talking manager, though he does appear to have a sinister side to him.
