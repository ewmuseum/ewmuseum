The Scouting Report - Issue 18

28 Nov 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s time for another action packed edition of The Scouting Report with yours truly. After a small hiatus I’m back. Things have been going on recently with the WWA and I’m here to give you the lowdown. -Perhaps one of the biggest shocks came recently with the announcement that former TV, Extreme, and US Champion Damian Thorne is retiring. -Damian has sustained a series of what are known as "cranial fissures" very similar to the condition which ended Brett Hart's career. Basically, there are a series of hairline fractures which cris-cross most of the rear of Damian's skull, and some of the left side, and it has been clearly stated that any further blows to his head could result in the complete collapse of those damaged sections and hence possibly ending his life. -The WWA wishes to remember all the great matches of Damian Thorne’s past. Matches such as Throne’s first TV Title win, the Triple Cage with Eddie Van Dorn for the Extreme Title, Thorne retiring the legendary Randy Cross and taking the name of the House of Pain and who can forget his win over Kash for the US Title. -We hope Damien the best in all his future endeavors. We won’t forget the man know as “Mr. Nasty” and “The Badest Mofo on the Show.” -In other news Meltdown finally returned to the network on April 28. Circumstances beyond our control meant a key piece of equipment blew up at Meltdown HQ, and couldn't air. However things have been back on track and we have had another Meltdown since then. -However unfortunately Fiesta has seemed to have fallen prey to the same misfortune that plagued Meltdown. The April 26 show has not aired yet but the bosses have assured me that our Commissioner is doing everything he can to get the show up and running. -We have recently had to let someone go as well. Evan “Loco” Motive has asked for some time off and we have given it to him. His tag-team partner, Keru no Akiro, is still active though. -Most would think that the only recent import from the EWA would be former Tag-Team Champion, Justin Bourne but that’s all changed last week. -The WWA Talent Committee has recently selected two EWA wrestlers to be promoted to the WWA recently. This are not the only people on the list, just the ones the WWA has slots for. The two superstars are Tommy Starr and Reverend Tweak. -Both men will be booked on WWA TV as soon as possible. -Other things happening in the WWA: -Last Meltdown the match that was supposed to take place between Eddie Van Dorn and Mal Somers could not take place due to EVD getting glass in his eye. Don’t worry Eddie is fine. The doctors just seemed to think it would be better if Eddie not jump into the match that night. -Also it seems that Ian Tense has joined forces with the Cell. This seems to make sense as The Cell are the only two men in the WWA that have the same disrespect for life that Tense does. -Speaking of strange alliances what about Enigma coming out with Joey K in his match for the TV Title last night? Either way it didn’t seem enough to allow Joey to pick up the victory and the title, if that’s even what it’s about. Maybe we’ll find out Monday. -Now on to thoughts about the nest Meltdown. -In the first match of the night we have Se7en facing off against James Curzon. Does anybody else but me think this is a mismatch? I mean we have the perennial coward in James and the guy who doesn’t get a lot of sun in Se7en. Either way it should be really funny to watch. -Next we have Justin Bourne, making his return to Meltdown, teaming up with Jimmi Zion to take on the team of Shaman and former GenX Champion. -It seems that in this match Zion is the underdog. After all Shaman can’t stand the guy for claming to be extreme. Zion cost Beam the GenX Title and even his own partner Bourne must be a little sore about the questionable way Zion won. Jimmi better watch his back. -In a Eight Man Elimination Match we have four of the hottest tag-teams in the WWA competing today. We have the Tag-Team Champions, X-Corp facing off against Havoc, The Brothers Delucci, and The Cell. -After the pier six brawl that erupted between all four of these teams last week it’s anybody’s guess who could come out the winner. -One has to wonder though, will the person responsible for the recent troubles between the Tag-Team champions. After all he said he would unveil himself at the next Meltdown. This seems to be trouble for Dave Harley. -Speaking of mysteries one has to wonder what will be the rules in the next match. Grunge challenged Nick Adams to a match Monday in his home state of Oklahoma. One has to wonder what the match will be and judging by last Monday if Nick will even show up. -Whatever the match is Grunge assured me that it will be something the fans have never seen before. So far only he, Nick Adams, and President Warr know the rules of the match with Grunge is calling the Oklahoma Metal Match. -In the Main Event of the night the contenders for the World Title are about to explode. In one corner we have number one contender Kash teaming with former three-time champion Tracer Bullet to face World Champion Ian Tense and the other former three-time champion Jackson Kraven. -We already know that Tracer and Jackson hate each other as well as Tense and Kash but you have to remember even the teammates aren’t crazy about each other. Tracer recently sent a IOU to Kash for not helping to prevent the mugging Tracer’s wife got a few months back. And Kraven and Tense have tried the team thing before. It didn’t go well. -This match is like a car-wreck. You want to watch even though you know your probably shouldn’t. Well until next time this is Josh Wilson. Be sure to watch Meltdown and Fiesta. You won’t be disappointed. Ciao.
