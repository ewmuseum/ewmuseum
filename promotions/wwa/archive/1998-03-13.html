WWA — 13 Mar 1998

13 Mar 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday Mar 13, 1998 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows the RIC Athletic Complex. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey, Terry Valentine and Johny Morehouse sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the RIC Athletic Complex on the campus of Rhode Island College in Providence, Rhode Island. We have another great card for you tonight folks. Johny : Thats right. Tonight we will have the first round of the CWC Northeastern Tag Title Tourney. We will also see Jak "The Boil" Lancer have his first title defense against the former champ. Along with many new faces in the NCW.... Tony : Let's get down to the ring for the first match of the evening..... The camera flashes to the ring, and we see Gary Trudeau standing in the middle of the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to Rhode Island College... The First match of the eveing will be a match for the first round of the CWC Northeast Tag Title Tourney. It is set for one fall... Introducing first coming to the ring... Weighing in at 252 pounds and hailing from St. Paul Minnesota, we have the former Northeastern Heavyweight champion "The Painmaker" Randy Cross.... And his tag partner weighing in at 225 pounds from Houston Texas, and Reigning CWC New England champion Outlaw. "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts over the PA. The Outlaw and Cross come oout of the back to a chorus of boos. The duo just smile and make fun of the crowd as they approach the ring. Trudeau : And their opponents, first weighing in at 255 pounds and hailing from New York City, we have the former New York City Champion Angel Reaper. And his partner from Chicago, Illinois and weighing in at 220 pounds and current CWC Rhode Island Champion Johnny Blaze. "Pepper" by Butthole Surfers starts over the PA. Angel Reaper and Blaze enter to the extreme opposite of Cross and Outlaw. The crowd is on their feet. Cheering for the New Alliance tag team. Tony : What we have here is one of the most heated feuds of the NCW wrapped into one match. Johny : And I would have to give the advantage to the former Northeast champ and current New England Champ to take this match. Terry : Of course. Johny : It looks like Angel Reaper will be starting things off against Cross. Terry : Yeah that’s because Blaze is too chicken to get into the ring with either of the men who beat him. Tony : Shut up, Terry. Johny : Angel locks up with Cross. Angel takes down Cross with a quick sweep. Tony : Angel stomps on the chest of the fallen champ. Terry : Angel tries for another stomp but Cross rolls out of the way, and trips Angel to the mat. Johny : Cross is going for the figure four! Terry : Angel kicks off, and Cross is sent flying back to a neutral corner. Tony : Angel kicks up to his feet and sends Cross from corner to ropes, and levels him with a big boot. Johny : What is Outlaw doing? He’s taunting Blaze to come in. Tony : Outlaw enters the ring as does Blaze. Blaze gets cut off. Terry : Outlaw has that New England title. Johny : Outlaw coldcocks Angel with the belt. Tony : Cross goes for the cover. The ref spins around. Johny : Blaze tries to make the save but he’s cut off by Outlaw. Terry : Cross and Outlaw will advance. Tony : That was cheap! Terry : Whatever it takes! Regardless the House Of Pain moves on. Johny : Let's go to the next match.... The camera flashes to the ring, and we see Gary Trudeau standing in the middle of the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, the second match this evening with be another first round match for the Norhteast Tag Titles. Introducing first from the Navajo Desert and weighing in at 310 pounds we have Shaman. And his partner from somewhere in the Himylayas and weighing 380 pounds we have Mad Coil. "Devil Man" by White Zombie atarts up over the PA. Shaman and Mad Coil stride in to the arena to a good crowd reaction. Shaman and Mad Coil stride to the ring appering to be all business. Trudeau : And their opponents, weighing in at a total combined weight of 489 pounds. We have Da Killer Connection. "Gangsta's Paradise" starts over the PA. Da Killer Connection walks to the ring to the obvious displeasure of the fans. Tiburon and Talon walk to the ring threatening the crowd with bodily harm. Johny : Well the last time we saw DKC in action they got a major upset thanks to the help from newcomers to NCW Anarchy. Terry : Well Anarchy is making a name for themselves fast in the ranks, but it doesn’t mean a thing if they don’t wrestle any matches. Tony : You’ve got a good point there. Johny : Well it looks like Shaman is starting things off against Tiburon. Tony : They lock up and Shaman slams Tiburon to the mat. Terry : Shaman whips Tiburon in and floors him with a big backdrop. Johny : Shaman tags in Mad Coil. Mad Coil off the ropes flies at Tiburon with a cross body block. Tony : What the heck is going on. The lights just went out. Terry : There seems to be some commotion in the ring but we can’t tell it from here. What is going on? Tony : The lights are flickering back on. Look! Johny : Mad Coil is down in the ring. Shaman is down on the outside. Terry : Mad Coil has a red A painted on his chest which can only mean one thing. Tony : Anarachy strikes again. Tiburon goes for the cover. 1-2-3. Harrington must put a stop to this. Terry : Tiburon and Talon advance! Johny : Let's go to the next match.... The camera flashes to the ring, and we see Gary Trudeau standing in the middle of the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, the third match this evening will be set for one fall, with a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first from Pullman, Washington and weighing in at 221 pounds we have Apocalypse. "End of the World" by R.E.M. starts up over the PA. Apocalypse walks to the ring with a mixed reaction from the crowd. He remains focused on the ring. Trudeau : And his opponent, weighing in at 287 pounds and hailing form parts unkown, we have accomponied by Dr. Edgar Von Harrisburg The Psychotic. "Welcome to the Jungle" by Guns and Roses starts over the PA. The two men enter the ring area to a mixed reception from the fans. Tony : This should be an interesting match. Johny : Both these men are relatively the same height, but Psychotic has a sixty-six pound weight advantage. Tony : The bell sounds and we are under way. Terry : Apocalypse charges forward and Psychotic staggers back after a huge shoulderblock. Johny : Psychotic charges forward and sends Apocalypse to the mat after a shoulderblock of his own. Tony : Psychotic pulls Apocalypse to his feet and tries for a scoop slam. Johny : Apocalypse rolls him up. Psychotic kicks out at 2. Terry : This Apocalypse doesn’t waste any time. Tony : Both men to their feet. Terry : Psychotic is stunned by a thumb to the eye, and Apocalypse executes a tremendous stomach breaker. Tony : Apocalypse lifts Psychotic to his feet and whips him into the corner. Johny : Psychotic hits hard and is nailed as Apocalypse follows him in with a massive forearm. Terry : Apocalypse is pummeling Psychotic in the corner. Johny : Apocalypse goes for a cross corner whip, but Psychotic reverses it. Tony : Psychotic follows him in with a massive clothesline that sends Apocalypse to the outside. Terry : Psychotic follows him outside and rams Apocalypse’s head into the ring steps. Tony : Psychotic goes for it again. Apocalypse blocks it and slams Psychotic’s head into the steps. Johny : The ref is up to five on his count. We could have a double count out. Terry : Apocalypse charges Psychotic with a clothesline. Psychotic elevates him with a back drop. Tony : Apocalypse lands hard on the concrete, Psychotic rolls in just as the bell sounds. Terry : Psychotic is raising his hands in victory but I don’t think he’s won. Tony : The ref is pulling his arms down. The official decision is a double countout. Johny : Psychotic is enraged. Terry : Well better to enraged than in pain, Apocalypse is still down. Tony : Let's go to the next match. The camera flashes to the ring, and we see Gary Trudeau standing in the middle of the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, the fourth match this evening will be set for one fall, with a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first from Pittsburgh and weighing in at 256 pounds we have led to the ring by Eric Stanley "Untouchable" Ryan Lee. "Voodoo Child" by Jimi Hendrix starts up over the PA. The two men walk to the ring to a rather negative responce. The two men are yelling back at the crowd as they walk to the ring. Trudeau : And his opponent, weighing in at 275 pounds and hailing from Dublin Ireland, we have Todd "The Irish Hammer" Logan. "Jump Around" by House of Pain starts over the PA. Logan strides to the ring ignoring the crowd. He appears to be a man on a mission. Johny : I hear that Logan has dedicated this match to The Outlaw for reasons of libel or something. Tony : Isn’t libel when you use someone’s name without permission and it ruins their reputation? Johny : Yeah something like that. Terry : Well Ryan Lee is in the ring and here comes Logan. Tony : Logan rushes the ring and tackles Lee to the mat. Johny : The ref calls for the bell. Terry : Logan buries an elbow into Lee’s face. Tony : Lee is bleeding. Logan may have broken his nose. Johny : The Irish Hammer is on fire. Terry : Logan yanks Lee up to his feet and nails him with a high knee. Johny : Lee is dazed. Tony : Logan locks in The Irish Sleeper. Terry : Lee is trying to fight it, but that will only use up whatever energy he has. Johny : Logan adds more pressure and takes Lee to the mat. Tony : The ref is checking Lee’s arm. It drops once. Terry : The fans are actually cheering for Logan, Lee’s arm drops a second time. Johny : Look at the look of determination on Logan. Lee’s arm drops a third time. Tony : This match is over. The ref calls for the bell. Terry : Logan won’t release the hold. The ref is trying to pull him off. Johny : Logan breaks the hold and kicks Lee in the ribs while he’s down. Terry : Talk about payback being a bitch. Logan leaves the ring to the cheers of the fans. Tony :Let's go to the next match.... The camera flashes to the ring, and we see Gary Trudeau standing in the middle of the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, the fifth match this evening will be set for one fall, with a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first from Trenton, New Jersey and weighing in at 275 pounds we have "Rock Hard" Rocky Stevens "Enter Sandman" by Metallica starts up over the PA. Stevens walks to the ring and is welcomed to the NCW with a pretty good reception. Trudeau : And his opponent, weighing in at 300 pounds and hailing from the frozen North of Canada, we have the Fifth Element. "Can you deliver?" by Armored Saint starts over the PA. The Fifth Element strides to the ring ignoring the crowd. He is focused on Stevens and the task at hand. Johny : Both of these men are reknown masters of submission. Terry : I would have to say the power edge goes to Rock, but the speed and agility clearly go to Element. Johny : The ref calls for the bell and the men immediately lock up. Tony : The Fifth Element with the early advantage. Terry : Element with an armdrag takedown on Rocky, Rocky reverses. Johny : Rocky levels Element with a clothesline. Element rolls out of the way of an elbow drop. Terry : Element off the ropes. Rocky with a backdrop. Johny : Element lands on his feet and floors Rocky with a dropkick. Tony : Element goes for the cover. Rocky kicks out with authority. Johny : Rocky sends Element into the ropes. Element hooks the ropes. Terry : Element charges Rocky. Rocky tries for a clothesline. Tony : Element catches him in a sunset backslide. Johny : Rocky barley kicks out at two and a half. Tony : Both men to their feet lock up. Rocky slips behind Element and tries for a sleeper. Johny : Element drops to the mat and stuns Rocky with a kick up to the jaw. Terry : Element kicks to his feet and whips Rocky into the ropes. Tony : Element executes The Warshadow! The Rock is locked into a leg scissors submission. Johny : Rocky taps out. Tony : That was a good match, let's go to the next match... The camera flashes to the ring, and we see Gary Trudeau standing in the middle of the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, the sixth match this evening will be set for one fall, with a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first from Miami FL and weighing in at 245 pounds we have Kue. "Unforgiven II" by Metallica starts up over the PA. Kue walks to the ring with a pretty good ovation. He slaps a few hands on his way to the ring. Trudeau : And his opponent, from the Navajo Desert and weighing in at 310 pounds we have Shaman. "Devil Man" by White Zombie atarts up over the PA. Shaman strides into the arena to a good crowd reaction. Shaman has a bandage on the back of his head. Johny : These two men have gone back and forth in a verbal war, but now they match wits in a one on one battle. Terry : There’s the bell and Shaman comes out fighting. Johny : Shaman floors Kue with a shoulderblock takedown. Tony : Shaman hits the ropes, and leaps over Kue’s drop toe hold. Terry : Shaman off the ropes again ducks around Kue’s leapfrog, and Shaman puts on the brakes. Johny : Kue turns around and is met with a massive forearm. Kue is dazed. Terry : Shaman executes a tremendous belly to belly suplex. Johny : That will knock the wind right out of you. Tony : Shaman continues his offense, dropping an elbow from the second turnbuckle. Johny : Shaman goes for the cover. Kue kicks out at 1. Terry : Shaman picks Kue up and sets him up for a suplex. Tony : Kue blocks it. Kue counters with a jumping DDT. Johny : This could be over. Tony : Kue goes to the top. This could be his patented Corner Pocket manuever, the top rope bulldog. Johny : Shaman springs to his feet and catches Kue off guard with a punch to the midsection. Terry : Shaman doubles over Kue with another shot to the stomach. Tony : Shaman locks Kue into a spinning powerbomb, Shaman’s finisher! Johny : Kue has just fell victim to the Thunder Clap. Wow what a move. Tony : Shaman goes for the cover. 1-2-3. That was a good Match. Let's go to Gary Trudeau for the next Tag team match.... The camera flashes to the ring, and we see Gary Trudeau standing in the middle of the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, The next match of the eveing will be a match for the first round of the CWC Northeast Tag Title Tourney. It is set for one fall... Introducing first coming to the ring... Weighing in at a total combined weight of 498 pounds. We have the Minnesota Wrecking Crew. "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts over the PA. The Crocketts come oout of the back to a chorus of boos. The duo just smile and make fun of the crowd as they approach the ring. Trudeau : And their opponents, first weighing in at 241 pounds and hailing from Memphis Tennessee, we haveRU Retty... And his opponent.... Is a mystery.... "Strutter" by Bob Seager starts over the PA. RU Retty comes out of the back to the mixed reation of the crowd. He enters the ring. Tony : Does anyone know who R.U. Retty's partner is? Terry : I bet I'll know who it is when I see him. Johny : Very impressive there Terry, your parents must be so proud. Terry : Why are these IDIOTS booing, the Minnesota Wrecking Crew are such honorable men and great athletes to boot. Tony : When have the Crockett's ever been honorable? Johny : Maybe in his warped mind, bad is good Tony. Here come Retty's partner. Terry : That's Britainicca, I knew it all along, you see that's what makes me the best commentator in the NCW, I've got all the scoops. Johny : Oh, you're definitely scooping it all right Tony, and doing a very nice job of it I might add. Tony : Easy boys, the match is about to begin. It looks like the "Enforcer" Zack Crockett and the newcomer R.U. Retty will start things off. Terry : The Wrecking Crew has this one in the bag, not only do they have the style and grace of champions but they are far more experienced then the two bums across the ring from them. Johny : I will agree that the MWC definitely have more experience but then again one could say Randy Cross had twice as much experience as Nik Segier and he still LOST Terry! Terry : Randy Cross didn't lose he was ripped off by a corrupt system and we all know it. Tony : You just don't give up do you? Anyway, Retty and Zack lock up, Crockett clamps on a side headlock and he's really grinding it in. Terry : He just may twist this guy's head off if he keeps doing that, and I think it's great. (Laughs) Retty with a reversal into an armbar, and an Irish whip into the far side ropes and he just dropkicked the "Enforcer" right down to the mat. Johny : Retty is dragging Zack to the corner and he tags in the Brit, who locks on a reverse chinlock. Zack is rising to his feet though and delivers two quick elbows to the gut, getting him the seperation he needs and a clothesline by Zack takes him down. Tony : In comes the "Punisher" and he grabs Britainicca up by his masktop before powerslamming him down to the mat. A cover, 1...2...and a kickout by Britainicca. Terry : That was a very slow count, the Crew should have won already. Britainicca looks a bit injured and the Punisher just tossed him halfway across the ring with a hiptoss. What's that? Terry : What's going on?? Tony : In the ring Britainicca is ripping off his mask and oh my god, it's NIK SEGIER! Aric turns around and he looks like he just saw a ghost, clothesline by Segier stuns Aric and he just connected with a flawless piledriver. He goes for the cover...1...2.. Zack comes in and kicks Nik in the back, breaking up what would have definitely been a 3 count. Terry : Aric was already lifting his shoulder, tell it like it is Tony, not how YOU see it. Johny : Segier tags in Retty as Zack gets tagged in by Aric. Zack just leveled R.U. with a fierce clothesline and he's lifting him up, Spinebuster and Retty is out. Terry : Segier tries to make the save but is stopped by Aric and the Minnesota Wrecking Crew advances to round 2. Johny : That was a great match but the Crockett's will advance despite the big shocker Retty & Segier had planned. Terry : I told you so, I told you so! Tony : Shut up Terry! Let's go to the last Tourney match tonight... The camera flashes to the ring, and we see Gary Trudeau standing in the middle of the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, The next match of the eveing will be a match for the first round of the CWC Northeast Tag Title Tourney. It is set for one fall... Introducing first coming to the ring... Weighing in at a total combined weight of 651 pounds. We have the team of Black Scorpian and The Fan. "Iron Man" by Black Sabbath starts over the PA. The Black Scorpian and the Fan walk to the ring. The crowd is cheering for the team. Scorpian slaps hands as the fan just walk to the ring. Trudeau : And their opponents, weighing in at a total combined weight of 632 pounds we have the current CWC National Tag Team Champions, the Overlords of the Dark. "Sad but True" by Metallicca starts over the PA. The Overlords come out to a pretty good responce. The stride to the ring staring at their opponents. Tony : This is also a first round match in the CWC Northeastern Tag Title Tournament, guys is it just me or does Darken look bigger then the last time we saw him? Terry : It must be all that raw meat that him and Steihl eat, I think these guys should be banned from the CWC. Johny : Why's that Terry? Is it because none of your House of Pain guys will beat them when it counts? Terry : Who asked you for an opinion there Morehouse? I can get those Anarchy guys out here with the snap of my fingers tough guy! Tony : This one is almost to close to call, but I know that the Overlords have been partners for much longer so I guess they will probably win this one. Terry : Who do I dislike more, the Overlords and their refusal to acknowledge the HOP as the premier stable in the NCW? Or is it that meddlesome Black Scorpion and the Fan? Hmm, this is a tough one. Johny : While Terry is lost in thought the match has begun and Darken starts off against Scorpion. Black Scorpion raises his arm in a request for a test of strength. Terry : What is this guy nuts? Darken's forearm is as big as Scorpion's waist, where does Harrington find these guys? Tony : Darken raises his arm and a boot to the gut by Scorpion a smart move and he picks up Darken and slams him to the mat! But Darken pops right up and boy does he look mad. Terry : Darken is now raining punches all over Scorpion who does his best to cover up at first, and now he's fighting back. Johny : But Darken is just too big and a massive right hand drops him. Darken easily lifts the Black Scorpion and drops a big elbow to the ribs, but Scorpion kicks out. Tony : Darken just tagged Steihl in and Steihl leaps to the top rope before dropping a sledgehammer blow to the insertion point of the shoulder which causes Scorpion to grimace in pain. Terry : Steihl whips Scorpion to the ropes, but it's reversed and here comes Scorpion with a clothesline that staggers Steihl he crawls over to his corner and here comes The Fan for the first time in the match. Johny : The Fan is now taking it to Steihl who can't seem to shake the big man off, The Fan applies a sloppy headlock and Steihl can't seem to escape it. Tony : The Fan releases the hold and bodyslams Steihl to the mat before dropping an elbow and firing some punches to Steihl's midsection. Terry : It looks to me like The Fan doesn't know what he wants to do with Steihl as he's lifting him up. The Fan goes for a short clothesline but Steihl ducks it and muscles him up before thrusting him down on his neck in a modified German suplex. Johny : Here comes Darken and look at the power as he lifts the Fan straight up above his head before press slamming him down. Darken tags in his partner as he lifts the Fan again, before dropping him down in a brainbuster. Tony : Steihl's climbing up top and he just leaped off the top rope over Darken's shoulders hitting a massive splash. Terry : The Fan looks like he's out and Black Scorpion thinks better of coming in to make the save, the Overlords win and move on to round two as well. Tony : That was a great match. The Overlords on their quest to be Northeat champs. Terry : Give it a rest, Tony! Johny : It is time for the main event.... The camera flashes to the ring, and we see Gary Trudeau standing in the middle of the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, it is time for the MAIN EVENT. This match will be for the New York City Title. It is set for one fall with a thirty minute time limit. Introducing first, the challenger from New York City and weighing in at 255 pounds we have Angel Reaper. "Antichrist Superstar" by Marilyn Manson starts up over the PA. Reaper walks out to a good reaction. The Reaper slaps a few hands and gets into the ring. Trudeau : And his opponent, weighing in at 233 pounds and hailing from Scranton, PA we have accompanied to the ring by Mr. Agony the current New York City Champion Jak "The Boil" Lancer. The Theme to Psycho starts and Lancer enters the arena to a loud chorus of boos. HE merely smiles and proceeds to the ring. Johny : It is time for our main event. Tony : There’s the bell and here we go. Johny : Angel with a show of strength scoops Lancer up and slams him down. Tony : Lancer from the mat takes Angel down with a leg sweep. Johny : Lancer springs to his feet and follows it up with a quick elbow drop. Tony : Lancer attempts to grapple with Angel on the mat, but Angel monkeyflips Lancer off of him. Terry : Both men back to their feet, and Angel comes off the ropes with a vicious lariat. Tony : Lancer flies head over feet into the corner. Angel hooks him up in the Tree of Woe. Johny : Angel backs to the opposite corner and charges Lancer who’s still tied up. Tony : Angel buries a knee in Lancer’s midsection, and Lancer slumps to the mat. Terry : Angel drags Lancer to the center of the ring and climbs to the top rope. Johny : Angel launches off the turnbuckle for a flying headbutt. Tony : Lancer rolls out of the way in a hurry, and Angel flies head first into the canvas. Terry : That’s gonna leave a mark. Terry : Lancer picks Angel up off the mat and executes a textbook backbreaker. Johny : Lancer goes for the pin, but Angel kicks out at 2. Tony : Lancer picks Angel up and goes for a piledriver. Terry : Angel blocks it and counters with a backdrop. Johny : Angel stomps away at Lancer, Lancer catches a foot, and takes him down with a sweep again. Angel Reaper covers him 1 - 2 - 3 Johny : Outlaw can't believe it! He is beside himself... We are out of time.
