Outkast Basic Information Nicknames | The Hand of God Real Name | Marc Maron Height | 6'1 Weight | 222 Date of Birth | 2/21/1978 Home Town | San Francisco, CA Theme | "Rose of Sharyn" by Killswitch Engage More Details Appearance Outkast is heavily tattooed on his chest and arms, has short dark hair slicked back, scruffy facial hair. When outside the ring he wears a lot of torn jeans and Affliction t-shirts. Ring Attire Black and purple trunks with black boots and black knee pads. Hands are taped. Personality Outkast believes he is the answer to all of lifes questions. He feels he is better, smarter, and above everyone. He manipulates others to get what he wants. Outkast plays mind games and will go to any lengths to get what he wants. He will also go to any lengths to win. History Outkast has been in the business since he was 19 years old and has worked his way up through the industry from being a grunt to being one of the most well knowned names in the business. He has worked for companies such as XFC, UiWF, and now NEWera Wrestling.
