The Gamblers Table — Independence Day Rumble 2003

27 Nov 2003

Bylined "The Gambler" (Kevin/Vic Sage), the fed's own in-house prediction columnist persona.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome back to the Gambler’s Table. I’m Vic Sage, and I want to apologize up front for not providing the WWA faithful with an article for Independence Day Rumble 2003. Family issues conspired to insure that I would be unable to fulfill my responsibilities to the fans, and I sincerely apologize for that. Nonetheless, the eagerly awaited Labor Day Brawl is coming quickly, and we have a lot of incredible action to discuss. Let’s get to it. Desolation vs Brad Polaris EWA European Title The EWA European Title will be on the line in our opening match at Labor Day Brawl. Desolation, one of the most unhinged individuals ever to step foot in an EWA ring will face Brad Polaris. It was on EWA’s Glory that Brad Polaris defeated Khaled to garner the number one contender spot. On that same night, Desolation, with assistance from his brother, Agony, defeated Mystical Illusions to claim the European Title. EWA Commissioner Andy Seeley announced soon after that their title match would take place at the prestigious Labor Day Brawl. Both of these Detroit natives have turned a lot of heads in the EWA, impressing the fans and the head office with their talents. Desolation, one half of The Pain Syndicate with Agony, is much larger than his opponent, outweighing him by nearly sixty pounds. Despite this difference in sheer muscle mass, Brad Polaris has an extensive amateur background, and a vast knowledge of technical wrestling. With his long-time girlfriend, Mandy Monroe, in his corner this young man has propelled himself through the ranks of the EWA in short order. Unfortunately, I foresee trouble for Mandy in this match, as Agony would certainly not be above using her to distract her boyfriend from the match. As much I would like to see Brad Polaris with the great honor of carrying the European Title, I have to put my money on Desolation. Keru No Akiro vs American Eagle Commissionership Match As the Commissioner of the WWA, Nathaniel Raine has been in charge of Fiesta for quite a while, and has brought great success to the show. After the roster split however, Matt Warr sent Troy Steffy to Fiesta as the Assistant Commissioner. Unfortunately, this did not bring about the peaceful leadership that Ricky Harrington, the current WWA president, would like. Not surprisingly, Troy Steffy started causing problems right away. After enduring a three-hour layover in Hong Kong, courtesy of the Assistant Commissioner, Nathaniel Raine confronted Troy on Fiesta, prepared to fire him. Unfortunately, the returning American Eagle attacked Raine and revealed himself to be Steffy’s personal enforcer. After the cameras stopped however, Keru No Akiro came out to chase away both Steffy and the American Eagle. On the next Fiesta, Keru No Akiro cost American Eagle his match against Jimmi Zion and his place in the Intercontinental Title tournament. In retaliation, Eagle interfered in Akiro’s match with Venny LeTrav, causing him to lose his place in the tournament as well. With hatred at the forefront of their minds, Eagle and Akiro have fought a running battle backstage ever since. Tiring of the state of affairs on Fiesta, Ricky Harrington made a stunning announcement on the most recent Fiesta. Due to the sniping and backstage politics, he suspended both Troy Steffy and Nathaniel Raine. Furthermore, he declared a match for Labor Day Brawl between American Eagle and Keru No Akiro with a special condition. Either Raine or Steffy will be fired after LDB, depending on who wins this match. If Akiro wins, Steffy will be fired. But, if Eagle wins, then Raine is out the door. Harrington had one more bombshell to add, the referee for this match will be the interim Commissioner, Bridge. With so much riding on the outcome of this match, both Akiro and Eagle will be fighting like their own careers depend on it. Keru No Akiro has shown a tremendous resilience and an uncanny ability to rise to the occasion. A powerful wrestler, standing at 6’2, and weighing 295 pounds, Keru No Akiro has a distinct size advantage against the 220-pound American Eagle. On the other hand, despite the fact that he represents all of the worst stereotypes about Americans, Eagle has a deadly arsenal of technical skills, the most deadly being his infamous and deadly Wingspan submission hold. This match could go either way, but I think that we have to take the referee into account in this one. Raine brought Bridge in as the Assistant-Assistant Commissioner, so we have to wonder if a loyalty will exist between the two that will turn the tide of this match. Although I know that Bridge will try to call this one fairly, I think we’ll see Keru No Akiro save Nathaniel Raine’s job. Chuckster vs “The Psycho” Steven Murphy Prison Match The rules for this Prison Match are simple: There are no rules. With Psycho currently serving time in prison for assault, he would normally be unable to compete at LDB. But, with the cooperation of the authorities, Chuckster will step into that prison with him and battle in a no-holds-barred contest with a unique prize on the line: Freedom. The loser of this match will be forced to finish out Psycho’s sentence, a period of one week. In addition, Chuckster added yet another stipulation to the match: The loser must complete that sentence with Bum Boozler, the sexually deviated monster from UWF who groped Needless to say, both men will be giving everything that they have in this match, knowing that their own freedom is on the line. Because this match takes place in a secure prison locale, neither Chuckster’s stable mates, Cold Pain Enterprises, nor Psycho’s stablemates, Devil’s Own, will be able to assist their teammates. The history between these two goes all the way back to their time in the EWA, battling over the European Title. Chuckster eventually came out on top of that exchange, but it was a hard fought battle. The EWA has turned out an impressive list of new talent for the WWA, and these two men are the cream of the proverbial crop. The Chuckster, the master of the Powerbomb, is comfortable in a variety of wrestling styles, from the hardcore to the technical. Psycho, on the other hand, is a highly talented technical wrestler with a mean streak. When all is said and done in this brutal match, I believe that we’ll see Psycho walk away from prison. David Wattsoner vs Shaman vs Tommy Starr vs Liam Jenson TV Title Cage Match Since his debut in the WWA at Bourbon Street Brawl, David “Sniper” Wattsoner, the man who trained the five-star champion Denrol, has made a huge impact, reforming Cold Pain Enterprises and claiming the TV Title with a win over Lockdown. After this, Sniper teamed up with his indomitable stablemate, Shaman, to take on two members of Devil’s Own, Tommy Starr and Lockdown. Unfortunately, due to personal issues, Lockdown had to take a leave of absence. Warr filled his position in that match with the newly returned Liam Jenson, formerly known as Rick Venom. The four men fought a great tag team match that ended indecisively with a double count-out, no contest decision. Realizing that the current state of affairs left the TV Title contendership in doubt, Matt Warr declared a four-way cage match for the TV Title at LDB. The wily veteran Sniper, has already declared this latest run in the WWA to be his last in the world of wrestling, and would like nothing more than to successfully defend his title against some of the toughest TV Title contenders that the WWA has to offer. With his experience, technical expertise, and ability to take a beating, he has a great chance at it. On the other hand, his fellow CPE member Shaman is no stranger to titles in the WWA, having held just about all of them at one time or another. Easily the most physically dominating figure in the match, Shaman has shown his own exceptional technical expertise in his long and storied career. One has to wonder if his alliance with CPE, and with Sniper particularly, will be a factor in this match. If the past is any indication, I think we’ll find that Shaman will put all of his effort into this battle, insuring a very difficult time for all of his opponents, whether friend or foe. Tommy Starr, since graduating from the EWA, has impressed the WWA with his skills and his phenomenal in-ring skills. Although he is the smallest man in the cage, we know that his arrogance and skills will more than make up the difference. Nonetheless, Starr is still a rookie to the WWA, and will be hard pressed to be able to defeat the level of talent that is arrayed against him. With a long road to recovery from a very serious neck injury, and a constant battle with alcohol addiction, Liam Jenson has returned to the WWA as Matt Warr’s personal enforcer. As we saw in the tag match, Jenson has returned with newfound skills and his ring skills have improved dramatically. The pessimism that often plagued Rick Venom seems to have been embraced by Liam Jenson, and his dark outlook fits the enforcer role quite well. Although considered by many to still to be an unproven commodity, there can be no doubt that Liam Jenson is one to watch. This is a tough match to call, but I’ll go with a long shot. If I’m right, I stand to make a lot of money from Liam Jenson’s victory. GenX Title - Gauntlet Dave Harley vs Fuel (Inferno Rules), Jimmi Zion (TLC Rules) and Joey K (GenX/Cage Rules) When Dave Harley won the GenX Title Meltdown, he promised to bring back the extreme in the GenX Title. For the uninitiated, this refers to the lineage of the GenX Title being an amalgam of the previous Extreme and Cruiserweight titles. The rivalry between Dave Harley and Joey K, not to mention between X-Corp and the Forsaken Souls, has raged for months. When X-Corp revealed their WWA show, the wildly popular Not So Late Night With Dave And Mal, Joey K unveiled his own WWA show, the short-lived K Moments. At Independence Day Rumble, Dave Harley defeated Joey K for the TV Title, and Joey has been on the hunt for more gold ever since. When his chance at the Intercontinental Title was stripped away by Ricky Harrington for an alleged attack on the World Champion Kash, Joey turned his attention to the GenX Title. Disgusted by the treatment of his fellow Forsaken Souls member, Enigma approached Troy Steffy to resolve the situation. In his last official act before his suspension, Assistant Commissioner Steffy set this Gauntlet. In keeping with his “putting the extreme back in the GenX Title”, Dave Harley has risen to meet the challenge with determination. Troy Steffy has made sure that the odds are against Harley, by insuring that each match falls within the strengths of Harley’s opponents. In the first match, Harley will face Fuel, the WWA’s resident pyromaniac, in an Inferno match. The man that is set on fire first loses, and the other moves on to face “Sky Flyin’ Jimmi Zion” in a Tables, Ladders, and Chairs match. The winner of that match moves on to face the eagerly waiting Joey K in a GenX Cage match. Fuel is certainly at home in an Inferno match, having no fear of fire. Harley’s weight advantage may prove to be important in this match. I expect to see Dave Harley collect the win, but Fuel’s arsenal of submission holds will certainly take their toll. Jimmi Zion is one of the most fearless and innovative high-flyers in the WWA today. If Harley makes it this far, he’ll find that his hardcore brawling style will be tested to its limits. Both men have nearly indomitable wills and I don’t believe that either will come out of this match healthy. These TLC matches are dangerous, and people like Zion and Harley will push the boundaries. Nonetheless, Harley has battled through some of the most impossible situations, never allowing the odds to dictate his fate. I think he’ll get past Jimmi Zion, although only barely. In the last match, Joey K will have a distinct advantage. His brawling skills, mixed with his technical expertise, makes Joey K perfectly suited to a cage setting. Not only that, but Joey K will be fresh while Dave Harley, if he makes it past both Fuel and Jimmi Zion, will have gone through hell to reach this point. If Dave Harley can survive through all of this and still retain his GenX Title, I’ll be amazed. Few men could accomplish such a feat. Just for that fact, I have to put my money on Joey K to claim the GenX Title. Graveyard Match Wes Sanders/Enigma vs Chance/Mal Somers The war between X-Corp and the Forsaken Souls continues, this time in a tag team match in a graveyard. Sanders has had his sights set on Somers for months, pressuring him at every turn by threatening and attacking his friends. Weeks ago on Meltdown, Enigma and Wes Sanders took on Mal Somers in a tag match. In that fight, the unbalanced Mal brought out his tag partner, the former championship belt, Rookie. The referee allowed the inanimate belt as the tag partner, allowing Mal to use it to take out both members of the Forsaken Souls. With the locker room jeers that come with being pinned by Rookie, Wes and Enigma have been out for revenge. The last straw for Mal came on Fiesta when Wes Sanders dented Rookie on Mal’s head with a top rope Frog Splash. Now, Mal is out to avenge the injury to Rookie. Enigma has a further issue on his mind though. He wants a piece of Chance. Chance earned a shot at the World Title at Bourbon Street Brawl. Having beaten Chance at Revolution, and again at IDR, Enigma claims that he deserves the title shot instead of Chance. The hatred between these two men has been burning white hot for most of the year, and this is just one more opportunity for the two get their hands on each other. It’s difficult to place a bet on a match that has such awesome competitors as these. Enigma, the leader of the Forsaken Souls, is one of the most devious and conniving men ever to walk the arenas of the WWA. Combined with being one of the premier aerialists of the WWA, this makes Enigma very dangerous. Teamed with the incredible kickboxing skills of Wes Sanders, Forsaken Souls will be tough to beat. Chance is no stranger to the high-flying style himself though, amazing the fans at every turn with his innovative and unpredictable acrobatics. With the versatile and unpredictable Mal Somers at his side, not to mention Rookie, X-Corp is certainly up to the task. This is going to be an astounding match. I’ll be recording this one, because I’m sure that I’ll miss some of the high-flying action every time that I have to blink. When all is said and done, or gone and buried in consideration of the locale, I believe that the lethal duo from the Forsaken Souls will stand tall. Grunge vs Leviathan US Title Clockwork Orange Match The current US Champion, Grunge, and the leader of Devil’s Own, Leviathan, have been playing a dangerous game of mental chess. Ever since Grunge locked Leviathan in a room with a tiger at the Bourbon Street Brawl, Leviathan has made it his personal mission to strip Grunge of the US Title. At IDR, After IDR, Leviathan and his stablemates attacked Dusk, Grunge’s friend, and used him as a bargaining chip to force a title match at LDB. Soon after, Grunge and Leviathan battled for the US Title in a brutal match on Meltdown wherein Grunge managed to retain the title, but only by disqualification when Leviathan tried to end his career with a Depth Charge through a table. With Devil’s Own, Leviathan even threatened Grunge’s wife, Julie. Outraged by this, and in an escalating pattern of retaliation, Grunge destroyed Leviathan’s motorcycle with some gasoline and a match. On the latest Meltdown, Grunge and Leviathan again met in the ring with the stipulation that the winner of that match would be able to choose the stipulations for their match Labor Day Brawl. In yet another incredible contest, it looked like Leviathan had it won with a Crossface submission. Surprisingly though, Leviathan released the hold and walked away. The referee counted him out, and Grunge got the win. It didn’t take long for Grunge to choose the Clockwork Orange match for LDB. Above the ring will be a variety of weapons, supported by poles at the four corners. These weapons, obviously, are legal for all uses in this match. Needless to say, this one will be bloody. Grunge is a great athlete and a determined competitor, who never fails to give it his all. With skills in both the technical and aerial styles, Grunge is often unpredictable in the ring. Leviathan on the other hand, is one of the most physically powerful men in the WWA. Although he often relies on this prodigious strength, he has also shown that he is no stranger to the technical arena. As much as I would like to see Leviathan fail in his quest, I have to believe that he’ll walk out of LDB as the US Champion and that’s where I’ll be placing my bet. WWA World Champion Kash vs Nick Adams WWA World Title With his spectacular win at Independence Day Rumble, Nick Adams earned a shot at the World Title, and cemented himself as a main event wrestler. But, the road to the LDB has been a difficult one. Even after his win at IDR, naysayers began questioning his ability to carry the main event mantle. During this time, his longtime girlfriend, Anna, left him, adding to his mounting difficulties. The number of people that doubted Nick’s qualifications was pared down at EWA’s Truth or Consequences when Nick Adams took the World Champion, Kash, to a no contest decision in an incredible match. Then came Damian Thorne, perhaps the most evil and sadistic man in the WWA. Thorne, the self-proclaimed god of wrestling, claimed that Nick was unworthy of the title shot. In a match that can only be described as career defining, Nick Adams pinned the nearly unstoppable Damian Thorne in the center of the ring. With his critics resoundingly silenced, his new manager, the incomparable Fate, at his side, and his New And Improved identity, Nick Adams has risen to the challenge at hand is ready for the greatest opportunity of his life, the WWA World Title. Throughout it all, Kash has remained patient, waiting for Nick to prepare himself and bring his best to Labor Day Brawl. Of course, perhaps Kash has been preoccupied with other matters. He has been beset on all sides by challengers to his title, and his championship belt, the most precious icon in all of professional wrestling has been missing since it was stolen at IDR by an unknown ninja in black. Although Tracer Bullet has been searching valiantly for the perpetrator of the theft, Kash’s rage has continued to grow and he has promised the ninja a very gruesome end. Both Kash and Nick Adams are among the very best that the WWA has to offer, and we the fans are in for a treat. Both are well-rounded competitors, dedicated to bringing their best to every match. Kash holds the edge in size, outweighing Adams by thirty-five pounds, but Nick Adams’ extensive martial arts background may help him to overcome that difference. Either man deserves the title, but I can only choose one. Despite his unpredictable nature, for me, Kash is money. Of course, we have also have Tracer Bullet tracking down the ninja who stole the WWA World Title. This mystery has seen accusations fly, and many an armchair detective has posed their own theory. I’ll leave the detective work up to someone who knows what they’re doing. I believe that Bullet will provide us with the answer at Labor Day Brawl, and that answer may shock us all. Labor Day Brawl is shaping up to be one of the greatest events in recent memory. Whatever happens, don’t miss out on the opportunity to order this spectacular event from your Pay-Per-View provider.
