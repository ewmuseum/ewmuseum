Lamprey Basic Information Nicknames Real Name | Feliz D'Souza Height | 6'0" Weight | 213 lbs Date of Birth | March 10, 1990 Home Town | Sao Paulo, Brazil Theme | "Hell March" by composer Frank Klepacki More Details Appearance Lamprey's facial features are all obscured by his mask. Physically he is lithe and flexible, not unlike the jaw-less aquatic creature of his nom de guerre. Ring Attire Lamprey wears a dark purple unitard with "Lampreia" in dark green lettering down both sides. His mask is a black featureless mesh which obscures his face. Around the rim of his face a ring of teeth similar to a lamprey's mouth are airbrushed. Personality Lamprey is an angry, reactionary revolutionary. He does not specifically state what he is rebelling against, but when asked, he'll reply : "que você começ?" History Lamprey is a relative newcomer to the sport, though he was a well known lightweight MMA fighter in his native Brazil. After leaving the sport, disgusted with the criminal elements that have infiltrated it, he decided to try his hand at professional wrestling. After a year of training, D'Souza has donned the masked of Lamprey, and is set to invade the WWA with General Mayhem's Militia.
