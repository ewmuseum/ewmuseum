Meltdown - September 24th 2001 The World Wrestling Alliance Presents...... The logo fades away into images from the World Trade Center and Pentagon disasters on September 11, 2001. "Imagine" by John Lennon plays, along with sound bytes from that day. As the song ends, we fade to the Gund Arena in Cleveland, Ohio. Gary Trudeau and all the GLWF superstars stand in the ring, with their hands over their hearts. Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, please rise and be silent, for the traditional ten bell salute to those who lost their lives on September 11th. Ding... Ding... Ding... Ding... Ding... Ding... Ding... Ding... Ding... Ding... Gray Trudeau : We will never forget. The crowd starts a chant of "USA" as the GLWF Monday Meltdown beginning starts. "When Worlds Collide" by Powerman 5000 plays as images of GLWF stars in action like Jackson Kraven, Trent Lebeaux, Shaman, Kahn, and WWA World Champion Shawn Cage flash past the screen. They end on the Monday Night Meltdown logo and than it goes to break. COMMERCIAL BREAK The camera crew catches Eddie van Dorn just as he is arriving at the building. Wallace Coleman : Eddie, a few words, if you please? Eddie, once again trying to look like a badass and failing just because he's 5'11" and 174 pounds, walks right up to the camera. EVD : I don't. All I'll say is that if I don't win this match, there is something dreadfully wrong. Other than that, just watch later tonight, you're sure to see something unforgettable. Eddie walks off quickly, leaving the camera crew high and dry. We cut to Jimmy and Fat Tony sitting at the announcer's desk. Everything is as usual, except for one thing: the desk has a nice big GLWF logo on it! Jimmy : Friends, the folks here are about to go wild! Welcome to the first GLWF card EVER! Fat Tony : You said it! These guys are going nuts, and it's only our first match! Who knows what they'll be like for the main event? Jimmy : For once I have to agree with you, Tony! The fans are jacked tonight! Fat Tony : Let's see if Gary Trudeau bought a new hairpiece in the layoff, shall we? We cut to Gary Trudeau in the ring. Gary is resplendent in an all-white suit with a stars-and-stripes tie. He beams out at the fans as if he's fit burst. Gary Trudeau : Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, children of all ages... are you ready to go insane? The crowd roar, and Gary's smile grows wider by the second. Gary Trudeau : This opening bout is a non-title tag-team match, and is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Las Vegas, Nevada... weighing in at two-hundred and fourty-five pounds... The Joker! The opening strains of the Steve Miller Band's "The Joker" are met with rapturous crowd response, and the purple pyro that explodes around the stage is met with even more. The Joker strides out onto the stage and salutes the fans before slowly sauntering down to the ring, slapping the hands of a few lucky fans as he goes. Jimmy : The fans showing The Joker how glad they are that he's back! Fat Tony : Bah, they're not cheering for him. They're cheering for his theme! The Joker is a waste of the GLWF's money, even if they're paying him as little as they are TJ! Jimmy : Quiet, you. As Joker mounts a turnbuckle and salutes the crowd, Gary Trudeau pipes up again. Gary Trudeau : His partner, from Buffalo, New York... The crowd go berzerk, causing Gary to pause for the reaction to die down before continuing. Gary Trudeau : ...Weighing in at two-hundred and ninety-one pounds... the current WWA World champion... SHAAAWN CAAAGE!!! The WWA world champion strides out onto the stage with the WWA title draped over his shoulder and a confident grin on his face. As Mudvayne's "DIG" roars out over the arena's sound system, the champ heads to ringside, also slapping the hands of those fans lucky enough to have rampside seats. Once in the ring, Cage shakes his partner's hand and they retreat to their corner to await their opponents. Gary Trudeau has vacated the ring by now, but his voice still rings out over the packed crowd. Gary Trudeau : Their opponents, the current WWA Tag Team champions... The Veteranz! Two purple pyros hurtle rocket-like out from the base of the screen at the back of the stage and the Manic Street Preachers' "You Love Us" blasts out over the arena sound system. With a screech of tyres far too spectacular for the vehicle it purports to belong to, the Vetzmobile appears on the stage and trundles down the ramp, accompanied by a wave of purple pyros on both sides of the ramp. Jimmy : The Veteranz turned down the request for this to be a title match, but they certainly look focused! Fat Tony : How do you know? I can hardly see them through all that damn pyro! The Vetzmobile comes to a halt by the ring, and out come Trapper and Jonny Shrapnel. The WWA tag-team champions go into a two-man huddle, and then break, with Trapper being the man who elicits to enter the ring first. Cage and The Joker confer for a second, with the smaller man deciding to start things off for his team. The two approach the referee, who speaks briefly to them before giving the signal to the timekeeper. DING, DING! Jimmy : Ladies and gents, we are underway! Fat Tony : We may have been gone, but we're back, baby! Jimmy : Trapper circling The Joker warily... maybe Joker was right when he accused the Veteranz of being afraid to put the belts on the line? Fat Tony : Nah, not afraid. Just too dumb to understand what they were doing, more like! Trapper and Joker lock up centre-ring, with the somewhat larger Trapper gaining the advantage, dropping his foe to the ground with a scoop slam. He drops an elbow on The Joker. Jimmy : Quick start by Trapper here. Fat Tony : He's an idiot! He'll tire himself out like this! Trapper picks up his foe and whips him into the ropes, only for The Joker to leapfrog him! On the rebound, The Joker levels the Veteran with a clothesline!. Jimmy : And Trapper goes down! Fat Tony : See? What'd I tell ya? The Joker now goes for a legdrop... but misses as Trapper rolls out of the way! The crowd cheer as Trapper signals for the ManTrap! Jimmy : Looks like he wants to finish this one already! Fat Tony : Like I said, he probably wore himself out already! Trapper goes for the ManTrap, but only manages to grab one foot before The Joker boots him in the face, before doing a nip-up back to his feet, much to the crowd's delight. Joker then performs a beautiful standing dropkick, knocking Trapper into the corner. The Joker moves to the opposite corner and for the baseball slide dropkick... but misses, crotching himself on the ringpost! Jimmy : Yowch! That's gonna ruin your night! Trapper again signals for the ManTrap. He manages to sinch it in this time, but being in the corner Joker can easily reach the ropes. Trapper lets go, but as soon as Joker is off the ropes he goes for it again! Fat Tony : What's this idiot playing at now? The ref jaws at Trapper, who moves away long enough for The Joker to get up this time. The two lock up again, and again Trapper manages to come out on top, whipping Joker into the corner and following up with an avalanche! Jimmy : The Veteranz in control at the moment... Fat Tony : But Trapper is going for the ManTrap again! What the Hell does he think he's doing? It's a bad idea whatever he's thinking. He must not realise where he is, because it's easy for The Joker to tag out to Shawn Cage, who simply shoves Veteran off his partner! Cage grins as he enters the ring, and the fans laugh as Trapper tags out to his partner rather than face the champion. Jonny Shrapnel looks just as scared, but has no time to think, as Cage rushes him! Jimmy : Yowch! Cage squashes Shrapnel in the corner! Fat Tony : Now Trapper might have been okay with that. Shrapnel? Cage outweighs him by seventy-three pounds! That's like dropping a housebrick on a melon! Cage steps back and Shrapnel staggers out of the corner. He is easy meat for a Cage bulldog! The Veteran goes down, and Cage flexes for the crowd before picking him up and going for a press slam! Jimmy : Here we go! First cover of the match! Fat Tony : One, two... close. Trapper breaks up the pin at two-and-a-half. He does indeed, but he jumps out of the ring just as fast to avoid the champion's wrath. As Cage turns around to continue his assault on Shrapnel, he is met with a low blow! Jimmy : That was... questionable, at least... The ref didn't see it, however. He was too busy getting The Joker out of the ring. Shrapnel capitalises with a swift fistdrop, before sinking in a rear-chinlock. Fat Tony : Bor-ring! Jimmy : I think he realises the only way of stopping Cage is by slowing this match down to a crawl! Cage fights the hold every step of the way, eventually managing to get himself back to a vertical base! Shrapnel clings on to his hold for dear life, wrapping his legs around the champion, but Cage still stands! Jimmy : Look at the power of the WWA world champion! Cage stomps backwards across the ring, slamming Shrapnel hard into the turnbuckles! As he steps out, Shrapnel falls flat on his face! Fat Tony : Anyone order Shrapnel pancakes? Jimmy : Cage now going for a powerbomb... Fat Tony : How about Cage-And-Shrapnel pancakes? Jimmy : Cage with the cover... Fat Tony : Will you quit ignoring my jokes? Jimmy : If only I could... 1... 2... Trapper manages to break up the cover just in time to avoid the three count, and just as quickly as last time vacates the ring when it looks like Cage might get his hands on him. Jimmy : Trapper runs like a scalded dog! Fat Tony : He can't hope to compete with Shawn Cage. Cage tags out to The Joker, who showboats a little, pantomiming being afraid of his tag-team partner, much to Trapper's disgust. Joker mocks Trapper some more before hitting an Irish Whip on Shrapnel. The Veteran crumples into the corner, and Joker hurtles in. However, with what little strength he has left, Shrapnel dodges and Joker hits nothing but turnbuckles! Shrapnel goes for the schoolboy rollup! 1... Joker manages to kick out, but looks very embarassed to have been covered the first place. Shrapnel takes this as the opportunity to level the man Las Vegas with a short-arm clothesline and tag out to Trapper. Jimmy : Trapper not so scared now! Fat Tony : It's not Cage, he can afford not to be scared! With Joker lying on the mat, Trapper now slowly climbs to the top for a rare aerial move. However, his slowness proves his undoing, as it gives Joker time to tag Cage in. As Trapper turns to leap, Cage now towards him! A look of terror on his face, Trapper makes a leap from the top turnbuckle onto the top of the Vetzmobile. Having landed, Trapper drops his pants and shows his ass to Cage, and the crowd roar with laughter! Jimmy : An astonishing lack of respect for the world champion here! Fat Tony : Ah, lay off him. Just be glad it isn't Patterson or Brisco. Jimmy : True... As the crowd laugh, Cage shrugs, then climbs the turnbuckle and leaps off, hitting a spectacular plancha onto Trapper! The crowd roar their approval as Cage hops off the top of the VetzMobile and rolls Trapper back into the ring. Things look bad for the Veteranz now as Cage tags in The Joker, who now goes to the top rope, and hits the Super-Trooper Splash on Trapper! 1... 2... The count is only just broken by Shrapnel, but Cage swiftly gets a measure revenge, knocking him down with a big boot to the face! As Cage vacates the ring, Jonny rolls out onto the apron. The Joker picks up Trapper and signals for the Trump Card! Fat Tony : Hasn't he already hit the Trump Card? Jimmy : I guess not... but we're about to see it now! The Joker whips Trapper into the corner, running in and going for an Aftershock. However, Trapper still has enough left in him to push Joker off, sending the man from Las Vegas flying straight into the referee! Fat Tony : The ref is down! It's chaos! I love it! With the ref down, the match quickly degenerates into a brawl, and as Trapper picks up his fallen foe, Cage clotheslines Jonny off the apron and out of the ring! However, something goes horribly wrong as Cage's momentum carries him over the top rope: his head hits the ring apron hard and he falls to the ringside matting! Jimmy : That looked like it hurt Cage as much as it hurt Shrapnel! In the ring, the Joker has the upper hand, winning the lockup and delivering a bodyslam! A wicked grin on his face, the Joker now applies the ManTrap to Trapper, and the Veteran taps out! However, the ref is still out and so Joker carries on with the hold regardless. Outside the ring Shrapnel notices Cage is not moving so he goes over the VetzMobile and drives it round to where the champ is lying. The Veteran revs the engine, an insane grin on his face as Cage gets groggily to his feet. Fat Tony : He's gonna run Cage over in that stupid VetzMobile! I love it! Johnny guns the engine and speeds towards Cage. At the last second, Cage leaps out of the way! The purple-and-black cart drives into the barricade, and Shrapnel flies out of the cart into the crowd! Jimmy : Folks, this is why you should always wear a seat-belt! In the ring, Joker is still holding the Mantrap on Trapper. The ref comes around and sees Trapper tap out, and signals for the bell! DING, DING, DING! Gary Trudeau : Your winners... Shawn Cage and The Joker! Cage shakes his head as he looks at the crashed golf cart, but then a grin crosses his face. As The Joker jumps out of the ring, Cage gestures at the cart, and the two men jump into it! The crowd laugh as the Vetzobile trundles back up the ramp. Trapper has a look of utter disgust on his face as the ref helps him to his feet. Shrapnel, only just getting up, looks dazed... but then, he always does! Jimmy : Folks, that was pretty wild! Fat Tony : You can say that again! But I have a feeling tonight's only gonna get wilder! Jimmy : You said it! The dejected Veteranz walk to the back as we go to break. COMMERCIAL BREAK As we return, Jackson Kraven walks through the locker room. A group of WWA wrestlers are gathered in a circle. He stops. Jackson Kraven : What the hell is going on in here? The Joker, Viper, Kahn and Villain step back from the circle. In the center of the circle sits Craven Moore. He is holding a dog-eared copy of Playboy. Jackson blanches. Craven Moore : That Jenny, she's a nice piece Jackie. Kraven reaches for the magazine. Craven Moore snatches it away. Craven Moore : Easy Jackson. It's not like she's trying to hide it. Eddie Van Dorn : Not bent over like that she's not. Jackson Kraven backhands Van Dorn who flies across the room. Craven Moore : Just one question? Jackson Kraven rubs his temples. Jackson Kraven : What? Craven Moore : How much did the implants cost? Jackson Kraven roars and takes an angry step toward Craven. Craven pushes the chair back and stands. He tosses the magazine to Kahn. Craven Moore : You really don't want to do that Jackie. Save it for the ring! The gathered wrestlers step between the two men. Jackson walks away mumbling to himself. Satisfied, Craven smiles. We return Jimmy and Fat Tony. Jimmy : I don't know how smart that was. Jackson Kraven already hates Moore, does Craven want to make it worse? Fat Tony : Well it's too late now. But that big dumb idiot will learn. Jimmy : Anyway, up next is the first Windy City tournament match, and it's the first ever interfederation bout. Gary Trudeau : This is a first round match in the tournament to crown the GLWF Windy City champion. Introducing first, representing nPw, accompanied by Becca, he weighs in at 247 pounds, Justin Vincible!!! "Bombtrack" by Rage Against the Machine starts up over the PA. Justin and Becca enter the arena to a mild amount of boos. Justin stands at the top of the ramp staring at the ring. Becca stand to his right with her hand on his bare chest. As the chorus to the song hits, they begin walking to the ring. Justin looks at a couple fans giving them the "I am above you" look, then disregards them. Justin leans in and gets some advice from Becca, then slides into the ring under the bottom ropes. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, representing the GLWF, hailing from Paterson, New Jersey and weighing in at 253 pounds, Riley "Kahn" O'Neil. The lights slowly dim down. The arena billboards finally become the only light within the place. Slowly a guitar is turned on and it sits humming for a few seconds. As it's humming a huge cloud of white smoke begins to bellow out through the curtains. As soon as the cloud is too thick to see through a single sound joins in with the guitar. POOOOFFFF!! As soon as the exhaling sound gives way, the room explodes as the drums kick in and "Part of Me" by Tool rips through the speakers. I know you well. You are a part of me. I know you better than I know myself. I know you best, better than anyone. I know you better than I know myself. You are a...part....of....me!! As the song continues three huge flames of fireworks blast from the entrance; green, white and orange respectively. As the sparks from the fireworks rain to the ground a man stumbles out through the curtain. Immediately it is clear who he is. His smile reaches from ear to ear, as the blood in his eyes becomes quite clear. He takes a step, slightly stumbles, but quickly regains his balance and looks to the ground as if to blame it. From his waist, Kahn grabs and lifts up a shinny gold plated belt. He raises it above his head as the crowd cheers for his "Windy City Title". He slumps it over his shoulder and proceeds to the ring. He climbs in, again raises the belt high and then kisses it before handing it...or attempting to hand it...to the ref. The ref however looks at Riley with a confused look and mouths a few words before Riley finally takes the belt and places it in the corner. YOU ARE A PART OF ME!!! Justin charges Kahn and nails him with a clothesline. Kahn takes two staggered steps backwards, but doesn't fall. He shakes his head to clear the cobwebs and then shakes his finger at Justin. Jimmy : And this one is underway. Fat Tony : I can't believe Stock let Vincible in the building tonight. Isn't he fired? Jimmy : Pay attention to the match! Justin and Kahn lock up in a collar-and-elbow tie-up. Kahn gets the upper hand and whips Vincible into the ropes. Vincible bounces off and gets nailed with a clothesline from Kahn. He spins 360 degrees and lands on the mat. Jimmy : Somebody check his vitals. Fat Tony : Who taught Justin to wrestle? Marty Jannetty? Kahn goes right back to work. He pulls Justin off the mat and onto his feet. Then Kahn grabs just by the neck and the right arm and tosses him over his back. Justin hits the mat hard and bounces. Fat Tony : That was one hell of a head and arm suplex. Jimmy : You can say that again. Fat Tony : That was one hell of a… Jimmy : Never mind. Outside the ring Becca cheers Justin on. Justin manages to pull himself up using the ropes. He lets go and staggers back a step. Unfortunately that step takes him into the waiting arms of Kahn. Kahn clamps a full nelson on Justin and bridges back. Jimmy : Dragon suplex! Fat Tony : Here comes the count! 1.. 2.. At the last possible moment Justin pushes out of the pinning combination. Kahn once again pulls Justin up from the mat. He puts Justin's head under his right arm and slings Justin's left arm over his shoulder. Then Kahn powers Justin straight up and holds him there. Several long seconds pass as Justin just hangs there suspended by the strength of Kahn. Jimmy : Look at the power of Kahn. He's had him up for a good 15 seconds now. Fat Tony : Look at Vincible's face. All the blood has rushed into his head. Then, unceremoniously, Kahn drives Justin's head into the mat with a picture perfect brainbuster. Justin rolls around on the mat clutching his head and neck. The crowd begins to chant "KAHN! KAHN! KAHN". Feeling the electricity from the crowd, Kahn wastes no time. He pulls Justin up again and puts him in a double underhook position. Kahn lifts Justin up and slams him down again with a devastating double underhook backbreaker. Justin doesn't move. Jimmy : This one is about over. Fat Tony : If we're lucky. Kahn reaches down for Justin once more. This time he is met with a pair of stiff rights that stagger him back a step. Justin rises to his feet, still clutching his neck. Kahn moves in again. Justin swings wildly, but Kahn ducks the punch. Kahn goes under Justin and lifts him onto his shoulders. Kahn spins Vincible down with a huge Death Valley Driver. Fat Tony : Hey Jimmy, is that your wife? Jimmy : Where? Fat Tony : I could swear I hear a fat lady singing. Kahn looks down at Justin and shakes his head. He grabs Justin by the tights and pulls him up. Justin hangs there limply. Kahn ducks down and once again drapes Justin across his shoulders. He releases Justin's legs and spins them behind him. Justin's head bounces off of Kahn's shoulder. Jimmy : SOBERED! SOBERED!! SOBERED!!! Fat Tony : Forget about it. Kahn covers the unmoving form of Justin Vincible, not even bothering to hook the leg. The referee makes the count. 1.. 2.. 3! Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of this match... KAHN! Kahn staggers to the corner and grabs his belt. He begins to celebrate as if he just won the WWA Title. The fans cheer him wildly. Jimmy : The GLWF goes up 1-0 on the nPw. Justin didn't look too good in his first match as a nPw wrestler. Fat Tony : Yeah, he couldn't be anymore of a loser than if he was a computer nerd who spent all day making fun of his coworkers that don't know how to use a simple database. Jimmy : That was a weird, inside reference that only one person got. Fat Tony : Don't look at me, I got told in my headset to say that. Jimmy : Up next, more GLWF action, with Trent Lebeaux Vs EVD. COMMERCIAL BREAK Shaman is waiting near the locker rooms as Kahn walks out of the locker room. Shaman takes a cord he is holding and wraps it around Kahn's neck in a twisting fashion choking the man down to the ground. He then slams Kahn against the wall. Shaman : Hey Kahn... Trust me you will never understand me again boy. You seem to think that you're invincible. I just kicked your ass here tonight. You want a piece of me you know where to ask for the challenge. Sleep well punk I'll see you in the future. Shaman punches Kahn a few times in the face directly and then leaves him there with the cord wrapped around Kahn's throat and a note on Kahn's chest that reads. Shaman : This time I'm making the first move!!! The scene goes to Jimmy and Fat Tony. Jimmy : Shaman making a big statement. He has really changed his attitude recently. Fat Tony : I kind of like this side of Shaman. He's still a dumbass, but maybe he's a cool dumbass. Gary Trudeau : This is an Windy City Tournament Match. Introducing first, coming to us from Truth or Consequences, New Mexico and weighing in at a whopping 174 pounds, Eddie van Dorn! Most of the crowd boo ceaselessly as "Another Brick in the Wall, Part 2" begins to play. However, some people are actually cheering, whether it be for the song or for the young man's brash attitude toward life. Either way, they're far outnumbered by those opposing them as soon as Eddie himself makes his way through the curtains with a look of utter disdain on his face. It's almost as if he doesn't really want to be there. Be that as it may, he gets quickly to the ring and waits for his hated rival. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent... Gary doesn't even get those three words out of his mouth before the fans move to their feet in unison. Rather than competing with the deafening roar of the crowd, Gary just says the name and goes to sit down. Gary Trudeau : Trent LeBeaux! Incubus's "Stellar" permeates the crowd. As mellow as most of the song is, it does nothing to calm them as they await the arrival of who some... mostly the man himself... claim to be a legend. They don't have to wait long as Trent LeBeaux steps through the curtains and makes a solemn walk toward the ring, never taking his eyes off of the devious young adversary he has to face tonight. The end of the song comes and pyro goes up from the ringposts one at a time for each time the lead singer says, "How do you do it?" until all four posts are lit. The pyro dies down and Trent turns toward Eddie, who is giving a sarcastic golf clap to the former champ's elaborate entrance. Jimmy : And this ought to be a war, Tony! Fat Tony : I'm looking forward to it myself, Jimmy. Seeing Trencer trounced by such a green grappler will be a wonderful way to spend my Monday. Jimmy : You been reading Shel Silverstein recently? The ref checks each man for any foreign objects and then signals for the bell to be rung. Jimmy : van Dorn wastes no time as he charges right toward Trent with a shoulderblock. He hits it, but it's not Trent who falls to the mat. Laughter echoes throughout the arena as Eddie falls flat on his ass after the attack. Fat Tony : Dammit, Trent's what... 234 pounds? That's not exactly some superheavyweight the kid ran into there. Jimmy ( still laughing ): I'll bet Trent has never had a sixty-pound weight advantage in any match, EVER. Eddie is undaunted as he gets back to his feet and tries the shoulderblock again. Trent is again unwavering, but at least Eddie stays on his feet this time, quickly following up with a footsweep, which is blocked. Trent just grins. Jimmy : LeBeaux seems to be enjoying himself out there. Fat Tony : He'd better not underestimate Eddie. He did take out both Jackson Kraven and Craven Moore at the Bourbon Street Brawl. Jimmy : Yeah, after making a sneak attack on two men who had been fighting in the streets for at least 30 minutes. Fat Tony : Good point. Jimmy : Eddie van Dorn now goes for a dropkick, but Trent is two steps ahead of the Rookie Ring holder... Fat Tony : Can we actually still call him that? It's been discontinued, you know... Jimmy : Well, he still holds it... anyway, Trent put a stop to the dropkick attempt by grabbing one of Eddie's feet. Trent now has one of Eddie's feet, while Eddie is hopping on the other facing in the opposite direction. He jumps up and kicks backward with the other one, nailing Trent in the midsection and forcing him to let go in addition to moving a few steps backward. Jimmy : And we have the first REAL contact of this match! Eddie back up quickly and kicks Trent in the stomach, doubling the man from New Orleans over. Fat Tony : Let's hope he can follow through. Jimmy : You seem to have taken a shine to this kid, Tony. Fat Tony : Any guy attempting to remove LeBeaux from the world of wrestling has my support. Eddie does indeed follow through with the kick by hitting Trent with a brainbuster DDT, then performing a picture-perfect flip splash onto his prone opponent. Fat Tony : There you go, Eddie! You've got him! Jimmy : Already? Fat Tony : Sure! Eddie doesn't seem to share Tony's sentiments however, as he brings LeBeaux to his feet and hits a few violent forearm shots which stagger the former champ. Jimmy : Those looked surprisingly good for such a young superstar. Fat Tony : He may be young, but he's had over two and a half years of experience already. Jimmy : And that experience is shining through with that abdominal stretch. That's right, Eddie has caught Trent in an abdominal stretch. Unfortunately for him, though, his opponent seems just as well-versed, if not more so, in this profession. Trent uses his unlikely weight advantage to his... advantage... as he pulls at Eddie's planted foot with his free arm, then falls back onto him in a pinning position. Ref : 1... 2... kickout. Jimmy : And Eddie knew he had better let go of that hold, or this match would have been over sooner than expected. Fat Tony : Come on Eddie! You can get the upper hand again, I know it. Jimmy : Looks like you're wrong, Tony. Tony IS wrong. Because when Eddie backed into the ropes and attempted to take Trent out with a cross bodyblock, Trent caught his much lighter opponent. Jimmy : Don't see THAT every day. Fat Tony : What's with that grin? Jimmy : Trent does have a rather self-satisfied look on his face... Fat Tony : Please don't tell me... Trent curls Eddie up and over his head. Fat Tony : The Big Easy? Please, not now! Unfortunately for Trent and his fans, he overestimated the power it would take to lift such a small opponent up for his finisher, and ended up merely hitting a fallaway slam. Fat Tony : Phew. Jimmy : Maybe not The Big Easy, but it still packed a pretty good punch. And Trent is quick to capitalize on the fallen youth by cinching in a Sharpshooter. Jimmy : Trent going for a submission here, it looks like. Fat Tony : But Eddie's not having it. Come on Eddo, break the hold! Jimmy : He can't, Tony! They're not close enough to the ropes, and he's too scrawny to power out of it! Fat Tony : But he's not giving up, either! And Trent knows this, as well. So while keeping Eddie's legs tied up with his knee, he does a quarter-turn and pulls his opponent up from the ground at the shoulders. Fat Tony : What the hell is that? Jimmy : I believe that's called a Queen Angelito Stretch, Tony. Eddie is screaming in pain, but still refuses to give up. Jimmy : This kid's got some huge... Fat Tony : Watch it... Jimmy : Pain tolerance. Fat Tony : They always say that it's not the size of the dog in the fight, but the size of the fight in the dog that's important. Jimmy : And 25 cliche points for Tony... Reluctantly, Trent releases the hold and brings Eddie to his feet, then whips him toward the turnbuckle. Eddie runs into it facefirst and leans into the buckle, limp as a dishrag. Jimmy : Trent now comes from behind and attempt to smash Eddie's face into the turnbuckle, but Eddie puts a stop to it by putting his foot on the middle turnbuckle. Fat Tony : That's my boy! Jimmy : Another attempt and another block... Eddie now throws a few elbows to Trent's abdomen and quickly jumps to the top turnbuckle. Fat Tony : This is where he shines! Trent comes toward the turnbuckle, but Eddie kicks him in the head to stagger him backward, then leaps off and tries to catch the New Orleans native in a flying hurricanrana. Fat Tony : All right! Jimmy : Not so fast, Tony... Not so fast is right, as Trent is having none of this. He has prevented the completion of the hurricanrana and tries to pull Eddie back up into a powerbomb, but Eddie punches him several times on the top of the head before sliding off of Trent with a vicious DDT. Jimmy : Ooh! Fat Tony : And both men are down, attempting to catch their breath. Jimmy : This has been a great match so far, it could go either way. Fat Tony : Either way, huh? Then tell me who just got up? Eddie apparently caught his breath a lot quicker than Trent did, as he nipped up just seconds after landing on the mat.
