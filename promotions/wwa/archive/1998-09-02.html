Wednesday September 2, 1998 The screen opens up to the the WWA banner. Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows the Memorial Coliseum. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey, Terry Valentine and Johny Morehouse sitting behind the table in the broadcast area.
