WWA — 19 Sep 1998

19 Sep 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
W.W.A World Wrestling Alliance Welcomes You To Saturday Main Event.. Camera opens as Terry Valentine announces the opening ceromonies. Terry : Well welcome to Saturday night Main Event. It's about time they put a real person at the mic. Tony : Hey why did you unhook our mics Terry. Eric : Thats it, I'm gonna beat him. Johny : Nah.. He ins't worth it. Terry : Hey don't blame me. Tony : Our first bout of the night should be interesting between two of our newest newcomers. Johny : Yes it seems sanity does not benifit these two but i guess it will be interesting to see. Eric : Well lets get to the ring and see what Gary Trudeau is about to do. Camera Opens as we see Gary Trudeau preparing to announce the first match. Gary Trudeau : Making his way to the ring the self proclaimed master of fire.. FIRE! Camera focuses on the ring as FIRE makes his way to the ring as Pyrotechnics blast off all around him and "Fire" by Scooter blasts over the PA.... Gary Trudeau : And his Opponent.. the insane, the funny Malkav Amaranth. Camera focuses on Malkav Amaranth as he is telling Gary how to properly announce his name. And all of the sudden pushes a pie into his face. Terry : Wow that took guts to do that to Gary he is a no nonsense man. Tony : Well he took it. And he is laughing too. Eric : There is the bell and the two men lock up and FIRE with a quick chop to Malkav. Johny : Malkav with a poke to the eyes and a three stooges move as he pops him and grabs Fires nose.. Terry (laughing): That's funny.. Eric : Fire tries to break free with no avail.. Tony : Malkav slaps down on his other hand releasing the hold on Fire. Johny : That has got to hurt, Fire with a boot to Malkav and a slap to his face. Eric : Malkav just kicked him in the family jewels . Johny : The ref wasn't looking, Huh thats odd. Tony : Whats odd. Johny : The small trail of fire burning towards the ring slowly. Tony : I see that... Maybe its nothing.? Eric : Malkav with a russian leg sweep. Terry : A little barbeque sauce from Malkav. Tony : Well it was funny when he was chewing on Terry's leg. Johny : Malkav with control again but it seems that he has control by a wedgie this time. Eric : Malkav with a quick bite to the ears. Tony : He has taken lessons from Mike Tyson I see. Eric : Fire points to his ears and points to the ref.. Johny : Malkav barks at the ref and the ref steps back. Tony : Hmmm.... That fire is right next to the ring. Terry : oh my god! The fire just burst into a figure of a man, its Steihl. Eric : Fire turns and sees Steihl. Fire is leveled by Malkav and Malkav rolls him up. Tony : 1.2.3.Malkav with the win. Terry : Steihl just climbed into the ring. Tony : Steihl and Fire are facing off. Eric : Fire lights the ring up again. Terry : Steihl holds his arms up and the flames turn into some sort of twister of fire.. Tony : Malkav is roasting marshmellows in the ring. Terry : Hey where did he get marshmellows.. Eric : Fire is stepping back from Steihl. Johny : Oh my god a ball of fire is falling from the ceiling as Steihl lowers his hands. Terry : It hits the ring and all the fire is gone including Steihl. Eric : Fire is searching around and is confused...? He jumps out of the ring and heads back to the locker rooms. Tony : Down to you Gary for our next bout.. Camera zooms in on the ring as Gary Trudeau Announces the next match. Gary Trudeau : Our next match is sheduled for a 20 minute time limit. First making his way to the ring is GUTTER. "Gutter Ballet" by Savage blasts over the PA as the former Horseman , now current member of Warriors Pride heads to the ring. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent hailing from the Himalayas.... Mad Coil! He comes to the ring as "Feed my Frankenstein" by Alice Cooper blares over the PA. He gets in the ring and attacks Gutter right away. Terry : Thats a man who is gonna be HOP real soon. Tony : Mad Coil seems to be punishing Gutter .. Eric : He seems driven to hurt the man. Johny : Gutter seems to be fighting back a bit. Terry : Mad Coil with some sort of karate kick just leveled him again. Tony : Gutter better get out of the ring or he's gonna get hurt. Eric : Mad Coil just power slammed Gutter. Johny : Mad Coil with a power bomb. Terry : Gutter pie can we say it together Gutter PIE!!!! Tony : Oh my god Gutter is not even responsive enough to move and Mad Coil is still attacking him. Eric : Mad Coil with his finisher the Avalanche.. Johny : Mad Coil is covering for the win.1.2.3. Mad Coil Wins. Terry : Look he is attacking Gutter again and continueing after the bell has already been rung.. Tony : He won the match leave. Gutter be damn get him out of there. Eric : Man call security! Mad Coil is unleashing on Gutter. Terry : Make him pay, make him pay. Johny : Look a large man just stepped over the railing from the fans hes wearing cowboy boots , blue jeans and a white t-shirt and war paint?? Eric : It's Shaman! He enters the ring and sends Mad Coil to the floor outside the ring. Terry : Mad Coil has a mic. Mad Coil : Shaman it seems you have finally showed up! The time has come to get BEATEN! Eric : Shaman also has a mic.. Shaman : Mad Coil... Times up, big mouth. Bring the noise and i will quiet you down for good. Terry : Shaman just through the mic at Mad Coil and is motioning him to get in the ring. Tony : Security is here and they are pushing Shaman and Mad Coil further apart from one another. Eric : Gutter is up... But he looks beaten but good. Johny : Well lets get to our next match which should be a good one. Camera focuses on the ring again as Gary Trudeau announces the next bout. Gary Trudeau : Making his way to the ring at this moment is Vinnie " the bronx " Bomber . The crowd get quite loud as "Plush" by The Stone Temple Pilots blasts over the PA. Vinnie makes his way to the ring with his manager Bill Dove. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent making his way to the ring now is "The Punisher" Aric Crockett. Aric is walking to the ring as a circus theme music comes on and we hear someone laughing over the PA. Voice : Hey what are you doing up here! Get out! The camera zooms in on the control room and we see Malkav Amaranth messing with equipment. There is another man trying to pry a control of some sort out of his hand. Terry : Aric doesn't look happy at all. Tony : Vinnie and aric lock up, but Vinnie powers out quickly.. Eric : Aric with a chop to Vinnie and Vinnie staggers. Johny : Vinnie is reeling from a series of blows from Aric. Terry : Teach you to mess with the HOP doesn't it Vinnie. Tony : Aric with another chop and Vinnie steps up and gives him a right cross and knocks Aric back. Johny : Both men are just throwing fists and kicks . Eric : This has turned into a brawl rather quickly. Terry : Crockett with a kick to the mid section and sends Vinnie to the ropes. Tony : Oh he nails him with a clothesline. Eric : Vinnie is getting up slowly only to be kicked in the back of the head. Johny : Crockett seems determined to beat Vinnie. Terry : Hey he runs his mouth at the HOP too, Beat his @$$. Tony : Crockett is working on the shoulder of Vinnie. Eric : It seems he is breaking him in for the finisher of his. Johny : Typical HOP keep menuevering for the kill. Terry : This is wrestling coward. Tony : Crockett still kicking Vinnie . Eric : Abdominal stretch by Aric. Johny : He is using the ropes for leverage without the ref seeing him. Terry : Hey ref Vinnie has hold of his tights. Tony : No he doesn't. Eric : Crockett Nails Vinnie with a double axe handle to the back of the head sending him to the mat. Johny : Vinnie is down Aric with the Brazilian Armbar. Terry : Vinnie is toast go HOP! Tony : Vinnie is not giving up he actually is powering to the ropes. Eric : The ref breaks the hold and Aric is in the refs face. Johny : Giving Vinnie recovery time. Not good for his chances of winning. Terry : Get him again Aric he is getting up. Tony : The ref just moved to the side as Vinnie speared Aric from behind and starts to kick and stomp on him. Eric : Vinnie whips Aric into the ropes and oh my the bronx bomb. Johny : He picks him up again into the b-52 bomber. Terry : NO NO NO !!!!!! Tony : Vinnie with the cover 1...2...3... Vinnie with the win tonight. Eric : Now back down to the Interview area where New Star stands waiting for his interview to be conducted by none other than the former NYC, New England champion of the NCW... Angel Reaper! Camera focuses on the interview platform as Angel Reaper begins to speak with New Star. Angel Reaper : Welcome New Star.. what have you got planned here in the WWA. New Star : What have i got planned...? I'm the best here and the best there ever was and that includes even better than you old man. Angel Reaper : Don't hold your breath kid, now you seem to be bent on taking down some of the extremists in this sport such as Shaman, Vinnie " the bronx " Bomber and of course David Wattsoner. New Star :: Hey... I'm the FUTURE of wrestling , I'm the EXTREME ICON and those guys are just old news. Shaman better be ready for me, when i beat him on his own ground in the desert. Angel Reaper : Hmmmm that won't be so easy New Star. New Star : Well I'm the best. I will do what I do best, and that's to win. Angel Reaper : Well thank you for your interview. Well back to ringside for thye main event , Take it away Gary. Camera pans over the crowd as it circles slowly to ringside and Gary Trudeau to announce the main event. Gary Trudeau : The challengers from the United States Marine Corps. Hailing from the Tun Tavern in Philadelphia PA, The Commandants Own. Camera focuses on the ramp way as the Commandants Own makes their way to the ring under the Marine Corps Hymn blasting over the PA. Gary Trudeau : And their opponents the WWA World Tag Team Champions Anarchy.. Camera focuses on Anarchy as they make their way to the ring with out their manager Havok. Terry : Wheres Havoc? Tony : They Lock up Raiden and Havok lock up in the ring. Eric : Raiden with the power and size advantage here. Johny : Raiden throws Havok to the ground. Terry : Maj Havok to his feet and kicks Raiden. Tony : Havok with a quick tag to Payne.. Eric : Commandants Own with tag team perfection as they beat down Raiden and knock Chaos out of the ring. Johny : Payne clamping down on Raidens shoulder with an armbar. Terry : Hey whats going on somethings on the monitor. Tony : Look its Havoc... Anarchy's manager. Havoc : Hey Marine boys! Terry : The Commandants Own are looking up at the LARGE monitor. Havoc is holding a lighter under the Marine Corps colors... Havoc : Well boys, You have till the count of ten to prevent me from disgracing your colors again..one.two. Tony : The Commandants Own are leaving the ring and running back to the locker rooms. Eric : The ref is counting them out. Johny : Anarchy wins and look Havoks coming out of the crowd with a remote in his hand and he is laughing. Terry : The crowds booing Anarchy. Tony : Look its The Commandants Own their running back to the ring ticked off. Eric : Anarchy running for the side exit with The Commandants Own on their path. Johny : Anarchy has a limo waiting there in and it speeds off. Tony : The Commandants Own are mad they are kicking stuff and throwing stuff. Eric : Well thats all the time we have this week see you next week.
