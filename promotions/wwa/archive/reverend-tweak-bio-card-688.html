What this is Roster bio card for the WWA wrestler Reverend Tweak, giving height, weight, birth date, hometown, entrance music, physical appearance, ring attire and personality.
Biography Wrestler's Name: Reverend Tweak Real Name: William B. Tweakson Height: 6' 1" Weight: 267 Date Of Birth: June 4, 1978 Hometown: Fort Wayne, Indiana Entrance Music: "Fixxxer" by Metallica Wrestler's Quote: Physical Appearance: Standing at 6'1", the Reverend isn't the tallest, nor the shortest, person in the world; yet, he still can stand there with the best and still look intimidating. He weighs about 267 and has an average-esque build: slightly muscular but with a little gut. Reverend's skin tone was originally just white, but now he's been tanning a lot lately so he has a somewhat tanned complexion, but not too tan so that is offsets his original skin color too much. The Reverend's hair is long, straight (not curly), and dark blond. It reaches down to just above the middle of his back, and it is usually always tied back in a ponytail. He has no facial hair. The most notable feature of his face would be his eyebrows, which are near perfection: nice, fine, thick, but neatly trimmed. His eyes, which are a light brown, seemed aged beyond the Reverend's years with some very thin wrinkle lines cornering the outsides of them. The nose starts from right in between the eyes and smooths down his face to a round-ish point that rises even (straight forward). The mouth and chin compliment each other in their fair shape and curvature. In total, the shape of his head seems to be somewhat fair, yet still shows definite signs of humanity. As we said, him being somewhat muscular applies mainly to the Reverend's chest, shoulders, arms, and legs. His stomach isn't perfect, as in washboard, but it's about average shaped -- not really round or thin or having too much weight, but about average. Also, he makes sure his chest and underarms are shaved since one day he plans on putting feathers there (or so he says...). Ring Attire: In the ring, Reverend Tweak wears a pair of somewhat bright-red pants-length tights with a white symbol of a bird carrying a ice cream cone on the behind. The boots worn over the tights are black with the laces having the same color red as the tights. His upper body consists, first and foremost, of no shirt of any kind. His elbows have black tape underneath, and on top are red elbow pads of the same color of red in the tights with slits on the pads on the inside of the elbow so the black tape is visible. Also some white tape is wrapped around some of his fingers. While making his entrance to the ring, the Reverend wears an open black robe with only a white collar holding together around his neck. With him, the Reverend carries a book of some sort and features the Reverend Tweak logo, that of a bird carrying a ice cream cone, very visibly on the front cover. Personality: Reverend Tweak, firstly, obviously has some mental problems. Certain event(s) during his adolencence has had a definite effect on his personality. It also lead to his certain religion we speak of now, with as disturbing and abnormal as it is. Despite this, the Reverend is a very caring person who loves to entertain and make people happy. Through all the mental misconfigurations and disturbing facts, deep down he is warm-hearted and good natured.
