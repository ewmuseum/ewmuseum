Meltdown

29 Oct 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Meltdown - October 29th 2001 The World Wrestling Alliance Presents...... We begin with the voice-over of Johny Morehouse. Johny : In 6 days... Scenes from past battle royals in the WWA. Rookie Battle Royals, others to decide number one contenders. Even the massive World War III Match at Pain Games. It is than replaced by still shot of the newest GLWF members like Mad Dawg, The New York Slugger, Sully Apathy, "The Punisher" Damien Thorne, and even the more established J.C. Swinger. Johny : Men will compete in a battle royal. One will "BreakOut". One will win a GLWF Title Shot. One will become a star. Blackness. Johny : In 6 days... Scenes from the past great tag teams. House of Pain, Commandment's Own, Kasanova & MidCarder, and of course, Overlords of the Dark. Than stills of the Rebel Angels and The Vetranz. Johny : Two teams keep alive the great tag team legacy of the WWA. It may not be for the titles, but that does not mean they fight any less. Blackness Johny : In 6 days... Scenes from the famous FWA invasion of the WWA, including Jim Black winning the WWA World Title. Than we get still shots of Villain and Kevin Pop. Johny : Inter-federation warfare rages. Two men from very different worlds meet to find out who the better man is. Blackness Johny : In 6 days... Scenes from Randy Cross' Four World Title Reigns, Denrol becoming the Grand Slam Champion, and Segier's long title reign as World Champion. Than still shots of Vic Ventressa and Eddie van Dorn. Johny : History will be made. The first time one man will ever defend two championships in one night in the WWA. The odds are stacked against him. Can he survive? Blackness Johny : In 6 days... Scenes from the big brawls in WWA History. The Escape-The-Arena Match, the Mall Brawl, and the Bourbon Street Brawl. Than stills of the dozen men in the Birthday Brawl. Johny : The biggest match in WWA History. Twelve men, four titles, one match. Blackness Johny : In 6 days... A simple shot of a ring in an empty arena. Johny : The World Wrestling Alliance turns four. The Monday Night Meltdown opening begins, with news scenes from Ventressa, Kahn, Shawn Cage, and others. All supported by the playing of "When Worlds Collide" by Powerman 5000. It than flashes into the Brown County Veterans Memorial Arena Expo Centre Complex in Green Bay, WI, where pyro is shooting off and the fans are going nuts. We pan over to Jimmy Rose and Fat Tony. Jimmy : Welcome to Monday Night Meltdown. We are live from... Fat Tony : Go ahead, say it. Jimmy : Ummm, The Brown County Veterans Memorial Arena Expo Centre Complex. Fat Tony : Wow, I couldn't tell you had it written down in front of you. Jimmy : Your sarcasm is noted. Anyway, this is a special edition leading to WWA Birthday Bash. Tonight, just one match. Fat Tony : Justin Vinicble faces the rookie 357 to find out who's going to be in the Birthday Brawl. Jimmy : But first, after these messages, we're have a State of the GLWF Address from GLWF owner, Denrol. COMMERCIAL BREAK We come back at the entranceway. "Full Nelson" by Limp Bizkit plays as GLWF owner Denrol comes out in his classic leather jacket and black jeans. Upon entering the ring, Gary Trudeau gives him the mike. Denrol : I hope everyone is having fun ( crowd pop ). While our fans at home are only seeing one match, all of you live are getting a full slate of dark matches after we go off the air. But I think it best if the entire GLWF audience listens to what I have to say today. The GLWF is going very strong. We have of course your favorite like Lebeaux, Ventressa, Kahn, and others. We also have a great batch of new talent like 357, Damien Thorne, Mad Dawg, and others. I would not be surprised if our Breakout Battle Royal is the sleeper hit of Birthday Bash. But let's not forget the amazing talent in the PWF. Tommy Michaels, Shane Dorion, and Kevin Pop are all great wrestlers and are part of the future of the WWA. With that in mind, I spoke to PWF President Matt Warr. Him and I came to an agreement and while the legal paperwork has to be finished, I can make this announcement. There will be a number of regional federations in the WWA. The NCW is the newest and I'm sure soon we'll have more. Warr and I thought it would be to our advantage if the GLWF and the PWF were not enemies, but friends. Thus, we have made an alliance. One for mutual profit and gain. Matt Warr has bought 30% of the GLWF. And I have bought 30% of the PWF. Now this does not mean we're going to become involved a lot in PWF affairs. What it means if we going to work together more. You will see not just more Inter-Region matches between us, but much more. GLWF/PWF tag teams like Kahn & Nelson. Big feuds between wrestlers in both feds. Stables made of members from both feds. And most of all, pure GLWF matches on PWF Mayhem and pure PWF matches on GLWF Meltdown. Yes, that means that people in the Great Lakes region will not get to see the PWF superstars and the west coast will get GLWF guys as well. The first sign of this new agreement, is the Light Heavyweight Title. I'm proud to announce we are renaming the PWF Light Heavyweight Championship, the PWF/GLWF Cruiserweight Championship. The first champion will be crowned at our first co-PPV, PWF/GLWF Ruptured. The tournament to decide the two men who are going to Ruptured will be a Mixed one. Meaning 8 guys from the GLWF and 8 from the PWF. The matches will take place on Mayhem and Meltdown. Now this doesn't mean it's all friendly. I can't make the GLWF guys like the PWF and I don't want to. This means Villain is STILL going to kick Kevin Pop's ass on Sunday. Crowd cheers Denrol : It only means it'll be easier for the guys to settle their issues right in the ring. That's the way I think everyone wants it. With this address, I also bid farwell for a while. My duties behind the scenes are taking up too much of my time and thus I can't be on screen anymore. President Joe Stock will be the face of the GLWF for the near future. Now before I go, a final topic is Vik Ventressa. He has made a number of comments and I want to clear up something. I have no issue with ventressa. He may believe what he wants, but if he thinks that I'm doing my best to hold him down, he's mistaken. If anything, I want him to be the best he can be. I chose him when I came back not out of hate. But because I saw in him the chance for greatness. Vik could be the Greatest of All Earth Time. But he's going to have to prove it. Winning the WWA World Title helped. Becoming the first dual defending champion helped. However, there is a key step he is missing. I hope he opens his eyes and sees it. Goodnight folks. "Full nelson" plays as Denrol leaves. COMMERCIAL BREAK Gary Trudeau : ladies and gentlemen this match is scheduled for one fall and is the lethal lottery finals to determine the final Birthday Brawl place! The crowd pops shamelessly Gary Trudeau : Making his way to the ring, he weighs in at 211 pounds...... Three Five Sevennnnnnnnnn!!!!!!!! The crowd cheer for the rookie as "I'm gonna get mine!" blares over the speakers and "Conceit/Walk" by Pantera begins to blast in succession. After several moments 357 enters at the head of the rampway. He leaps into the air and spins with a twisting roundhouse kick, recieving a huge cheer from the crowd. As he approaches the ring the crowd chants with the music "Re-Spect Walk!" He arrives at the ring and slides in under the bottom ropes. He leaps onto the turnbuckle nearest him and holds his arms in the air. In sync with both his music and his large mass of fans he points to himself four times with his thumbs pointing in "DON'T FUCK WITH THIS!!" He leaps off of the turnbuckle and cracks hits neck from side to side, then bounces from foot to foot, waiting in tense anticipation for the match to begin. Jimmy : This rookies first match was only a couple of weeks ago Fat Tony : That's right and he sucked then too. I dont believe he even got this far. Jimmy : Give the kid a chance Valentino Fat Tony : When he proves it in the ring, I'll give him a chance....... Knocked Straight the F@(k out! "Full Nelson" by Limp Bizkit errupts over the Public Address. Justin emerges from the back with Rebecca Lynn Schafer right next to him. The crowd errupts in boos as this duo emerges, both are wearing pure arrogance on their faces. Justin stops at the top of the ramp and Becca places her hand on his chest. They stay there as the music plays, as it hits the chorus and the musicial says "You'll get knocked the f@(k out!", Justin and Becca begin their walk to the ring. Justin simply ignores the fans as he is so much better than they! Justin gets to ringside and mocks 357, by jutting his thumbs at himself. Justin ( with annoying voice ): Don't F@ck with this! Fat Tony : Don't you just love this guy. Every week he comes out here and puts someone down. Jimmy : The guy can wrestle... But I do not like him. no. DING, DING, DING Jimmy : Well both men are in the ring now, perhaps this match will get started! Fat Tony : Ha! look at Vincible... simply mocking 357! It takes a confident man to do that..... All of a sudden, 357 explodes from the blocks, taking out Justin Vincible with a clothesline. he gets back up and bounces off the rope before jumping high for a leg drop. He then picks up Vincible and snap suplexes Justin, right toward the corner of the ring Jimmy : 357 making an impression! Fat Tony : Leaving Justin on the floor! 357 runs into the corner and springs himself on the corner before dropping a little splash on Vincible. 357, obviously pissed off by Vincible's mocking stands back and starts motioning violently for Vincible to get up Fat Tony : Hey 357... can I call him 7 for short? Jimmy : No you can't. Fat Tony : Hey, the man will get up when he's good and ready! Vincible eventually gets up, as 357 runs toward him for a clothesline, Vincible ducks as 357 hits the ropes, then Justin Vincible reverse kicks 357 right in the chops! 357 goes out onto the apron! Fat Tony : That's better Justin... keep it going boy..... Just as 357 gets up, Justin runs for the ropes and sends 357 flying. becca runs around to talk some smack but sees Justin running to the turnbuckle.... he springboards off the middle rope, and dropkicks ofer the top rope to send 357 to the floor again! Jimmy : That's two of Vincibles signature moves in a row, and you have to ask if 357 is gonna last a whole match the way this is going! Fat Tony : yeah, my boy Vincible taking the trash out! Vincible gets back up, a little shaken. Becca comes round to see if he was alright, and he seems to be as he shakes her off. he picks up 357 and bangs his head into the security rail. He bangs it again... he goes for it again, but 357 puts his hand in the way and bangs Vincibles instead! He then takes a hold of Vincible neck before quickly dropping a neckbreaker! Jimmy : Good defense from 357, and now he's bringing it back into the ring.... 357 picks up Vincible and hooks his leg for a fishermans suplex, but Vincibles weight and size make this difficult.... so when 357 stops struggling, Vincible rolls it all up into a small package!! 1... 2.. KICKOUT!! Jimmy : Justin Vincible almost taking a sneaky win from 357, levelling this match back up. Both men are stood, 357 takes Justin down with an arm drag. Justin thinks better of this opportunity and rolls out of the ring. The fans boo Jimmy : Justin Vincible seemingly wussing out on the match! Fat Tony : Nah, its just smarts. justin Vincible is a smart man. Theres no other word for it. Jimmy : well, 357 looks just as disappointed at this act of cowardice from JV Then 357 runs for the ropes and executes a suicide plancha OVER the top rope!!!! Jimmy : Oh my god! What a risky move from 357! Fat Tony : And we're back outside the ring... i thought the WWA was waiting for Halloween for violence? Either way, 357 picks up Vincible and tosses him back in the ring. 357 stands on the apron and flips over the ropes into a splash right onto Vincible! He hangs on for the cover!! 1... 2 Vincible with a lucky escape hooks his leg on the ropes Fat Tony : See, I told you he was smart! Jimmy : Either way, that was a great athletic achievement for this young high flyer 357 gets up, picking up Vincible with him. he whips him to the corner and goes for the avalance. he connects, but his small size has little impact, so when Vincible locks up straight after he is a little surprised!! Even more so when he gets planted with a belly to belly suplex!!! Jimmy : What a great manouver from Justin Vincible!!! Fat Tony : I told you!! The guys got skills! Jimmy : he seemingly pulled that one out of nowhere.... Fat Tony : Much like David Blaine... Vincibles pain is all an illusion..... Vincible looks pumped after that one, and picks up 357. After a quick punch in the face, Vincible ties up from behind and goes for a german suplex, but instead gets an elbow to the head! 357 pushes Vincible to the ropes, but Justin grabs 357s arm and reverses the move. When 357 returns, Vincible does a Thesz press followed by the punches! Fat Tony : Yeah baby! Beverly Hills beatdown! Vincible gets back up and picks up 357 with it. he shoots 357 to the ropes and Justin catches him with a overhead release belly to belly suplex. Justin jumps up and points to himself with his thumbs and yells, "Don't F#ck with this!". This enrages 357, who jumps up and Justin drops him with a drop toe hold. Justin jumps on top of 357 and pushes his face into the mat hitting the back of his head with slaps. Fat Tony : Ha! Jimmy : That's hardly fair is it?! Fat Tony : Funny as hell though! 357 rolls off and out of the ring. Vincible stood up and laughs Jimmy : how can he do that when he himslef did it not 10 minutes ago! 357 starts to walk up the ramp.... Vincible leaves the ring and starts to follow... then with a sudden sharp and abrupt turn, 357 charges Justin Vincible down with a spear!!!! Fat Tony : C'mon, he's cheating! That's like throwing in the towel! Jimmy : Don't be stupid, he was playing possum! 357 still have his head under Justins Arm, so he struggles to get him back up before going for a northern lights suplex. Unfortunately, his size beats him again, and Vincible tries to reverse it to a DDT... but he fails too, and 357 manages to pick up Vincible just enough to muster an atomic drop! Vincible falls off and immediately begins to hold his crotch Fat Tony : LOW BLOW!!! Jimmy : The atomic drop is a legit move Tony.... Fat Tony : Not when you do it that badly.... how are Becca and Justin going to have kids now? 357 picks up the injured Justin Vincible and heads for the announcers tables Fat Tony : Aw man, can't we have just one week without going through the damn spanish announcers table? 357 walks right to the ENGLISH/AMERICAN announce table.... Fat Tony : oh sh*--- Tony gets cut off as they start brawling.... Vincible gets angry and makes a lunge, but 357 gets angrier and.... Fat Tony : --Uh oh!!! Jimmy : 357 just hiptossed Vincible ON to our announce table! Fat Tony : But now wheres he going?? 357 climbs up the turnbuckle... all the way to the top before POKING HIS THUMBS IN THE AIR!!!! Jimmy : 357 is about to Frog Splash Justin Vincible Through the announce table!!! Fat Tony : My God!!!! 357 makes the leap, leapfrogs the air but VINCIBLE ROLLS OFF! Jimmy : MY GOD! 357 Just put himself through a table! Fat Tony : And it looks like Vincible is out cold too! Surely enough, we see a replay of the incident....a monitor flying off the table and hitting Vincible in the head Fat Tony : That HAD to knock him for six. Jimmy : Neither man is keeping up and the ref is counting. 1... 2... 3... 4... Justin starts to get up 5... 6... 7... 357 starts to move, but not much. 8... 9... Justin slides into the ring. 10!!! Gary Trudeau : Your winner of the match by countout, JUSTIN VINICBLE!!! Jimmy : Wow, what a surprise ending. A great match cut short by an amazing high risk that cost 357 the match. Fat Tony : Doesn't matter, Justin is going to Birthday Brawl! Justin gets his hand raised, but he doesn't seem happy. He steps out of the ring and grabs the still hurt 357 and throws him into the ring. Jimmy : Wait, what is he doing? Fat Tony : A little extra fun for the fans. Justin puts 357 down with a DDT. He than runs to the ropes and comes off with a Lionsault-like move. He than covers the out cold 357 and Becca counts to three for him. Jimmy : That move is a new one from Justin he's calling Just Insane. But this is just unnecessary. He got the win. Fat Tony : The rookie needs to learn a little respect. Plus it's a nice way to show off a new move. "Full Nelson" by Limp Bizkit plays as Justin Vincible and Becca celebrate in the ring. Jimmy : So Justin Vinicble is the twelfth man in Birthday Brawl and 357 is going on to the Breakout Battle Royal. And we're out of time, we'll see you all at Birthday Bash.
