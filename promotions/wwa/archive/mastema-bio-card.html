Date of Birth | Unknown Home Town | Lee County, KY Theme | "Spirit in the Sky" by Norman Greenbaum More Details Appearance His brown hair is untidy and shoulder length. He has no visible tattoo's but he does have several scars. The most predominate is a scar in the shape of an upside down pentagram on his right bicep. Ring Attire A huge man decked out in a white outfit much like the outfit Kane used to wear when he first appeared. He wears a White Luchadore like mask, the eye screens are blood red, and a Gold cross is over his forehead. Personality He stands silent, permitting only his manager to speak for him. Almost like a light switch, once the bell rings he becomes a demon in the ring and doesn't stop until he is called off by James. History The legend of Mastema is that he was a wild mountain man from Kentucky, living like a hermit in the mountains. After his young wife died, he began practicing dark arts trying to resurrect her. After many false promises from demon's, he made a pact with Satan where he would allow a demon to possess him and to do whatever the demon wanted for the resurrection of his wife. The newly possessed man went on a six-month rampage where it is rumored he committed murder several times but was never arrested. An old time Tent Revival came to town and Mastema was ordered by the demon to burn the revival to the ground. As the behemoth attempted to trap everyone inside, the Reverend leading the revival, Allen T. James identified that he was a man possessed. It took 8 men to hold Mastema down and an Exorcism was performed. At some point during the deliverance, James formulated a plan to bring money to himself using Mastema. Did I mention James is a shyster? Now controlling the monster, James sought out for the riches of Pro Wrestling, knowing that Mastema could cause the destruction he sought and it not being illegal.
