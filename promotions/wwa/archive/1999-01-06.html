WWA — 6 Jan 1999

6 Jan 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WORLD WRESTLING ALLIANCE... The Ultimate in Wrestling Entertainment! Scene opens with the new WWA Wednesday Slam logo. A black and white clip film from Season's Beatings starts. It shows highlights from all the matchs, including New Star diving from the ladder onto Sniper with barb wire wraped around his arm, Big Eliem tackling Ungentle Giant, FEAR getting dropkicked into the flaming cage, and the ending to the World Title Match. Then it goes into an opening seqeunce involving shots of wrestlers such as New Star, Shaman, Outlaw, Denrol, FEAR, and Spidero doing amazing stunts. "Paradise City" by Guns & Roses play during this time. Then it cuts to the studio, which looks like a newscast room, complete with glass desk and a large wall screen behind that table. Sitting at the desk is Joseph Stock. Joe : Hello everyone and welcome to the new and improved WWA Wednesday Slam. I'm your new host Joseph Stock. You know me well from the infamous No Holds Barred column. Well now I'm on TV too. We have a great card for you this evening. We are going to run down the results of Season's Beatings, give you some hot rumors, tell you what's ahead for the WWA, and even give you a hot interview with the World and US Champ, Gregory O'Hara. Then there's our wrestling action. In lightweight action, Outlaw vs Matt "The Missile" Pepper, Shaman vs Damien "The Wolf" Smith and our main event, a non-title match between FEAR and the Commandant's Own. Let's head over to the arena, for our opening matchup. Scene flashes over to inside a studio arena. A ring with a large WWA Slam in the center stands in the middle of a small crowd of cheering fans. Shaman and Damien "The Wolf' Smith are already in the ring. The bell rings. Johny Morehouse : Hello everyone in TV land. This is our first ever match under the new Slam format. Both men are already in the ring and we are underway. Eric Pembrook : Yes and Shaman takes control right away. He bounces Smith off the ropes and delivers a massive bodyslam. Johny : Here's a nice followup to a with a knee the face. Now a chinlock by Shaman. Eric : Smith manages to escape, but gets slamed back down with a brainbuster. He picks up the fallen Damien. Johny : Tombstone piledriver by Shaman. He covers. 2 and a half. Smith rolls to the outside for a breather. Eric : Shaman follows him to the outside and crushes his head against the guardrail. Johny : Wait a second, here comes Black Scorpion down the ramp. Eric : I'm scared what he's up to. The action has returned to the ring. Damien out of nowhere with a dropkick to Shaman's knee. Johny : Shaman holding his knee in pain. Now a front sulpex by Smith. He covers. Just two. Shaman gets up. Eric : Kick to midsection by Damien is caught. Now Shaman spins him around and delivers an atomic drop. Johny : Great followup to that move with a spinebuster. Now Shaman climbs the turnbuckle. Wait I think-- Eric : Yes it's a Thunder Clap! Black Scorpion comes in. He picks up Shaman and delivers a reverse DDT! Johny : The ref has called for the bell. Shaman has won by DQ. Now Black Scorpion leaves to the boos of the fans. Eric : A disappointing win for Shaman, because he had Smith beat. Back to the studio and Joseph. After these commericals. Commerical break starts. 10-10-9000's Slam of the Week: New Star flying off a ladder to hit Sniper with a barbed wire covered arm. Commerical break end. Scene returns inside the Slam studio, Joe scanning over a packet of papers. He smiles at the camera. Joe : Welcome back to Wednesday Slam. Season's Beatings was a great Pay-Per-View and don't forget to catch the encores all month long on Direct TV. We started off the show with Duke Daring taking on Mr.Business. The match was going fine till Jack II showed up. Flynn Steele confronted Jack, leaving us very confused. Duke got the win. Jack II then enetred the ring and started beating on Mr.Business. Outlaw came out to help his fellow HOPer. Darken finally showed up and left with Jack II. Stills run as Joe talks. Joe : After that mess, we had the Extreme finals. Sniper vs New Star. This was really just a brawl. And we'll show it in entirly in just a few minutes, because it is our Match of the Week. Next was Shiny vs Shiny, Denrol vs Lone Wolf. All were disappointed when HOP came down and interfred. A double DQ was the result, spoiling what had so far been an excellent Pay-Per-View. We can only hope these two go at it again. Then it was time for Anrachy vs FEAR. Or aleast it was scheledude to be. Anrachy pulled a fast one and Briceland with New Star took on the tag champions. New Star was very hurt after his brawl with Sniper and was not much help. The flaming cage was not harmless either. FEAR had special firesuits protecting them. Close calls filled the match. New Star got pinned and FEAR retaiend the gold. But New Star was seriously hurt and we hoped to have an update tonight, but no luck. Things ended with Steele vs O'hara, Denrol special guest Ref. CPE was out to "protect" Denrol, but ended up brawling with HOP. Then Logan showed up, but was stoped by a masked man. We'll discuss that later. Denrol proved his bias when he refused to finish a three count when Steele had the match won. Here's the ending, because it's beyond describation. Scene switchs over to a tape of the ending of the main event. Tony : Someone's coming down. Sniper! Terry : He pulls Denrol's leg, breaking the count. Johny : Steele has been screwed! Denrol unbiased my ass! Tony : Steele in Denrol's face about it. Terry : O'Hara gets up and climbs the top rope on the other side. Johny : Steele pushes Denrol. Denrols pushes him back. Tony : Steele gets shoved right into the Leapranchun Leap! Terry : O'hara goes for the pinfall. 1..2..3! Johny : Hold on a second, Steele got his shoulder up! Tony : The bagpipes are playing. They have annouced O'hara as the winner. Terry : Where's the replay? Johny : I don't know, but I swear Steele's shoulders were up. Tony : Flynn is leaving slowly with a look of complete and utter disgust, shaking his head as he leaves the areana. Denrol has a mic. Denrol : Steele, I promised you, that you would hold the World Title over my dead body. Well I'm still kicking! Terry : Steele just ignores him and continues the walk out of the arena. Denrol : As for you! He turns around and points at O'Hara. Denrol : I can't believe how dumb you really are. That for one moment you thought the elite CPE would have a drunken idiot like you in it's ranks. Here's my message from Sniper. Johny : He just slaped O'hara!!! CPE is running down. Tony : They pull Denrol out of there quickly and they're running to the back, Denrol amoung them. Terry : Look! Denrol has soemthing in his hands. He's leaving with O'hara's belts! Johny : O'hara just saw that and now he's chasing after CPE. Back to the studio. Joe : As you can see, it was a fun night. Don't forget those encores. Let's go back to the arena, for Outlaw vs Matt "The Missle" Pepper. When we come back, we'll have the Tag team tourney brackets! The scene flys over to the studio arena. Outlaw and Pepper are already going at it. Eric : We have started already. Outlaw gets surprised by a franksteiner by Pepper. Matt goes off the ropes. Johny : Flying shoulder breaker by Pepper! The underdog showing some great moves on the wily vet, Outlaw. Eric : Now Pepper goes to the top ropes and flys off with a moonsault. Outlaw can't seem to get up with all these fast moves. Johny : Off the ropes again. Swinging neck breaker by Pepper. He runs over to the ropes. Eric : He delivers a spingboard moonsault, but misses badly when Outlaw moves. Now Outlaw in control. Johny : Outlaw locks on an armbar. Pepper is fighting it. He makes it to the ropes. Eric : Outlaw knocks Missle with a hard clothesline. Now he delivers a elbowdrop to Matt's stomach. Johny : He covers Matt. Somehow Matt kicks out at two. Outlaw picks him up to countine the assault. Eric : Hold on, Matt with a hard slap that catchs Outlaw off guard. He gives Outlaw an underarm piledriver. Johny : Matt climbs up the turnbuckle. He waits for Outlaw to rise. He flys off at him with a missle piledriver. Eric : Outlaw catchs him midair, gives him a hard powerbomb and quickly covers. 1..2...3! Outlaw and HOP with the win. Johny : Here's more bill paying for WWA. We'll see you soon with our main event. Commerical break. Return to studio with Joseph Stock. Joe : You guys wanted it and we are going to give it to ya! A Tag Team Tourney, winner getting a title shot. If you have been holding your breath, release it now, here's our brackets. A brackets screen fills your TV moniter. Lone Wolf/Jak Lancer vs Outlaw/Denrol ????? vs Flynn Steele/Gregory O'Hara MidCarder/Krisopher Kassanova vs David Briceland/El Porto Spidero Duke Daring/Jack II vs Stehil/Darken Scene switchs back to Joe in the studio. Joe : As you can see, it should be a great tourney. As for the myserty spot, that's going to be deiced with a triangle match between Hot House, Anarachy, and Spirtual Warriors, the normal tag teams of the tourney. In other news, tickets just went on sale for WWA's next Pay-Per-View. January 24th,1999 from American West Arena in Phoenix,AZ Joe : No matchs signed yet, but that's hasn't effected sales of tickets. You better get them now because they'll be gone in a few more days. Some quick rumors for you all. O'Hara has been spotted talking to many WWA superstars backstage. Insiders believe these may have something to with the myserty man that appeared at Season's Beatings. Also CPE is going to have a very big annoucement in the next week or so. Now it's time for our match of the week, Sniper vs New Star for the Extreme Title from Season's Beatings. Tape of match starts to run. Click here to see match Tape ends. Joe : We'll be right back with an interview with Greogry O'Hara, Interview of the week and than our main event. Commerical break. Return to studio. Joe : And now an interview with our World Champion, Gregory "The Irish Assassin" O'Hara, taped eariler. Tape starts, O'Hara and Joe sitting in chairs in a dark set, spotlights only on them. Joe : Thanks for coming Mr. O'Hara. First off, maybe some background. Can you tell us anything about yourself we don't know? O'Hara : Yeah, there's plenty. I grew up in Kilroy, Ireland. That's probably why I'm such a mean bastard these days. When I was about 9, a man you all know too well brought me into the IRA. I thought that man was my friend but he just corrupted me. When I was about 16 I left Ireland and came to the U.S. to get away from the IRA and to start a new life. Ummm....I'm an aries, and my birthday is April 1. And I'm quite the ladies man....oh wait you all know that, you saw my Playboy shoot. Joe : Why did you become a wrestler? O'Hara : Well, when I got to America I learned a valuable lesson. You need money for everything, even happiness. I learned I could fight in a ring and get money for it. Fighting is what I've always known and I figured if I could get a salary for it I might as well jump all over the opportunity. Joe : Have any heros you idolized as a kid? O'Hara : Yeah, there was the dwarf I knew back in Kilroy. He could lick his own back. It was the coolest thing I had ever seen and I definetly idolized him. He was my hometown hero. Joe : Did you ever expect to get so far, so fast? O'Hara : Yeah, actually I did. I have high expectations for myself. I look around the WWA and see a lot of good wrestlers. But I look at myself and see a great wrestler, that's the difference. I make things happen, I make people FEEL THE REVOLUTION!!! Joe : This is a fan question. What's your favorite music band? O'Hara : Personally, I enjoy all kinds of music. From rap to metal. But there is something about Korn that is different. The way they play makes me feel kind of funny inside. I definetly enjoy there music the most out of any. In fact, they are in the C.D. player in my car right now. Joe : You aren't very luckly with stables. First House of Pain, than Cold Pain Enterprises. Any thoughts? O'Hara : Well, I'm the one who decided to stay away from the HOP. Todd influenced me as a youngster and I didn't want him making decisions for me again. As for Cold Pain Enterprises, Sniper begged me to join many times, but I kept telling him NO,NO,NO! Then at the Pay-Per-View he made it sound like he didn't want me in CPE. That is so wrong, he was just ticked I didn't want to have anything to do with Cold Pain Enterprises. By the way can anybody tell me want Cold Pain is, is it frost bite? Joe : Who's your toughest oppunent yet? O'Hara : Definetly Randy. That guy is an outstanding wrestler in my opinion and I decided on the right move at the right time and won. It was the greatest day of my life. Joe : Anyone you ever dream of facing one-on-one? O'Hara : There are many people that have dreamed facing me one-on-one. Don't you hear them b!tch and complaining. I'm only one champion, I don't have the time to give everyone a title shot at the same time. Oh wait, a person I dreamed of facing. Nope, not really. I've faced the almighty Randy Cross, and showed him my skills. Who else do I need to prove myself to. Joe : Rumors are flying on who that masked man was who stoped Logan from attacking you. Wish to clear it up? O'Hara : You think I know who that man was? I wish he would've let Logan in the ring. I could've beat his ass along with that old fart Flynn....that's just my style, though! Joe : You have been bracketed in the tag tourney to team with Flynn Steele. Any thoughts? O'Hara : Wow. If Flynn gets over his recent loss to me, we could be one helluva team. I mean, the current WWA World and U.S. champion and the former WWA U.S. champion together on one team. We could rip it up...and make our opponents FEEL THE REVOLUTION! We got some good chances if Flynn can put the past behind us. If he doesn't though, that's cool. I'll just kick his @$$ again. Joe : What's next for The Irish Assassin? O'Hara : Actually, I have an important announcement at the next card concerning the future of "The Irish Assassin". I'll answer all the questions there. Joe : Thanks for coming, any last comments? O'Hara : Yeah, Logan, you want a piece of me. Set it up, brother. I'll sit your ass down. You used to be the one who bossed me around, well the rolls will be reversed when we fight....you'll be my b!tch!!! That's just my style!!! Tape ends, back to the studio. Joe : Thanks to O'Hara again for letting interview him. Next week we'll have the president himself on the hot seat. And you can help out. Got questions for Mark Harrington? Rumors or news to be given. Thoughts, Suggstions, Comments? All can be sent to [detail withheld] And now to our interview of the week and right after that, our main event. The camera pans down from a clear blue sky filled with light and fluffy clouds. The ground is a lovely green field, children are running around playing, flying kites, and families are picnicking. The birds are chirping in the background. All in all there is a sense of peace, security and love. The kids running all fall at the same instant, the kite strings snap. Everyone pauses and this all happens in the span of a second. A nuclear blast rips from the field, the sky turns dark and cloudy, and lightning strikes from the sky to touch base at the edge of the permanent mushroom cloud. A dark seems to rise in a shadow form of a man through the blast. More and more becomes noticeable, until from cloak to boots to gloves are all noticeable. A great scythe held in his hand, Darken begins to shrink to human size in the once field. Ripping from the fading cloud, where the lightning has struck, comes a large castle of a cabin. It stands at least eight stories high and has towers on the four corners. Each tower looks as though it is a lookout or battle tower, and in each rests a giant gong, all different colors- black, red, yellow, and one of a sickly skin color. Around the tower grows a wall, a large wall, at least six feet thick and four stories high. The stone wall towers over the man to be towered over by the cabin castle. Around the cabin, the desolate land has turned half lively as massive trees begin to grow all around it, none closer than ten feet, and none within thirty of one another. Little else grows, but a scarse grass and a few weeds. All in all, the area still looks dead. Darken walks up to a section of the wall where the mighty wooden gates stand. The reenforced wood and steel opens easily for him when he pushes it in. The camera follows inside to a sight much like the old cabin. There is a giant fire pit in front of the main of the building, a towering blaze already alight and what looks like spitted cows roasting inside it. Around this fire rests giant rocks and other objects which might be used as lifting materials. There are also weapon racks holding staves and arrows and bows all around the front, neatly stacked. Darken continues until he stands before the massive door to the cabin which stands as high as a single story. Darken reaches out and knocks on the door, the sound echoes and carries through the entire fortress. Upon recieving no answer, Darken smiles and sits down. He tosses back his hood, uncharacteristically, and combs his hair with his right hand, covering most of the right hand side of his face with a dragging motion. Darken : 'Tis been so long since the WWA has been moving that I thought it fitting to return to the New Year in style. Many of the wrestlers are beginning to lack in the interviews and offerings of words, allowing themselves to sit back and become lazy as they offer up their tributes to all their competitors. I thought it a good motivational idea to show the WWA what can be done with a computer and a few dollars. It may not be the most businesslike show for the period, but it does show that I am still a dedicated member of the ranks, unlike others... topping the list, the disembodied voice. I think we can leave him alone for this time though. Darken grins almost impishly as he tosses his head and hair back, though the hair on the right side falls simply down his face once more. Underneath it seems the eye is closed. Darken : 1999, the New Year and the Last Year, for the Apocalypse is prediced to come forth, and his riders as always stand ready. The Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse are ready for the New Year in the WWA and the events which come with it. I have heard some criticism about the Horsemen lately and I will say this and only this: The Horsemen are not a group to gather together to win a match, but a group of deadly opponents who gather together to protect the interests of the others, and go about our own. Darken seems proud during his speech of his brothers and reaches his feet. He takes a some steps and stands back towards the camera in front of the fire. Darken : 1999 brings a new Extreme Champion, the first of many. 1999 brings new life into the ranks, and 1999 will bring a new WWA Heavyweight champion. Gregory, Irish Assassin, you have spoken before to the masses and agreed to my challenge. Though you have been forced into a title defense against Once Steihl at January's Rage in the Cage, you have agreed to defend against me in the first Friday Night's Fights following your defense against Flynn Steele. One of those Friday's is coming, O'Hara, and with that Friday will come your last day of reign as the WWA Heavyweight Championship falls into the hands of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. Come Rage in the Cage, Once Steihl will be afforded the chance to not attempt to take my Heavyweight belt from me, as I will offer him the chance to not once more humiliate himself. He has beaten me fairly, once. Once in only he and I knows how many times, and it will be his last. Darken reaches into the fire, and pulls back his hand holding a burning log, not yet coaled, but charred easily. Darken : The fire is a friendly place in winter, and no fear could come from it. The Horsemen are ready for 1999, I am ready for 1999, the other wrestlers of the WWA? It has yet to be decided. House of Pain, the Horsemen will find you as you have seen, but your demise will come down the way you could least stand it, one by one by one...... Darken turns to the camera and glares his red eyed glare with evil intent. The film melts under that glare. Scene flashes for the last time over to the arena.
