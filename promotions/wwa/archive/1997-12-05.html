WWA — 5 Dec 1997

5 Dec 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday Dec 5, 1997 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows the Bryce Jordan Center. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey and Terry Valentine sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the Bryce Jordan Center on the lovely campus of Penn State in State College PA. We have a great one for you tonight folks. Terry : That's right, we do Tony. Tonight is the First round of the Northeast Championship Tournament. Then the main event is Randy Cross - Johnny Blaze in a Steel Cage for the New York City Title. Randy Cross should.... Tony : That's right; the last time these two men met up, interference cost Blaze the title. To settle the score tonight they will face one on one in an enclosed steel cage. We've got a great card tonight. Terry : That's right no way in, no way out. Cross will prove once and for all he deserves that title. Tony : Not if Blaze takes it from him. Anyways, let's go down to the ring where the Commish Mark Harrington has an announcement... The camera goes down to the ring where a table with two chairs is already set up. Mark Harrington is already in the ring with a microphone in his hand. Harrington : Ladies and gentlemen. It is my pleasure at this time to introduce to you a man, who is hopefully going to join the ranks of our NCW stars. He is the international star and is well known to all of you. Please welcome along with me DAN "THE RUSSIAN BEAR" SOKOLOV !!! "Enter Sandman" (with no words) starts to play and a man wearing black jeans and a t-shirt with his own picture on it comes out and walks to the ring. He ignores all the fans trying to slap his hands. Sokolov gets in the ring and ignores a hand extended by the commissioner. He walks pass him and sits down in one of the two chairs provided. Mark Harrington also sits down in the remaining chair. Harrington : Welcome Mr. Sokolov ..... Sokolov interrupts him and grabs the mic out of his hand. Sokolov : I don't need all these crapy talks and introductions. I have had it so many times in my life that I can't even remember. Let's get down to the business. Did you put what I wanted in the contract or you are just wasting my time? Harrington(taking out a contract): Here it is .... Sokolov ripps it out of his hands and begins to read it. Sokolov : I don't see an immediate title match which I have requested. Harrington : Well, you see Mr. Sokolov, NCW is a highly competitive federation with many great stars in it, so I can't just give you a title shot. There are many more people ahead of you... You must prove yourself first. Sokolov : I can't believe all this crap comeing out of your mouth. I have to prove myself ?! I have had more titles in my carrer than all those other wrestlers of yours combined. You know what i am going to do? Sokolov gets up. All of a sudden Sokolov grabs a pen and signs a contract. Sokolov : You wanted it? You got it! Now give me something I want. I want you to go down to the lockerrooms and get me any of your little superstars right now so I can give them a good lesson and show all of you that I don't need to prove anything to nobody. Sokolov gets up, leaves the ring and storms back into the lockerroom. Mark Harrington gets up, as well, and walks towards the announcers area. Tony : Wow! That was quite a statement. Terry : I like him! Tony : You would. Ladies and gentlemen, the Commissioner just handed me the Tournament brackets. Solokov's first match is against the current New York City Champion "The Punisher" Randy Cross. Wow, Sokolov has his work cut out for him right off the bat. Terry : Yep, Cross is clearly the best in the NCW so, I think Sokolov's debut might be tarnished. Tony : Joining us now is Johnny Morehouse. Welcome Johny. Johny :Great to be here. Let's get to the matches. The camera goes down to the ring and to Ring Anouncer Gary Trudeau. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Welcome to the Bryce Jordan Center. The first match in the Northeast Heavyweight Championship Tournament is set for one Fall... "Mars the Bringer of War" starts over the loud speakers. Trudeau : Introducing first, from South Boston, Mass weighing in at 220 lbs we have led to the ring by his head cheerleader Tammy, "Merciless" Mark Lynch. The crowd starts to boo as Tammy and Mark make their way towards the ring, ignoring the crowd. As Mark step into the ring, the American National Anthem starts over the speakers. The crowd stands up and gives up a little cheer. Patriot comes out of the lockerroom area and walks towards the ring. Trudeau : And his opponent, from the Bronx, New York weighing in at 245 lbs we have the Patriot. Johny : Well, there are the ring announcements for the First match. It is pretty obvious that the fans do not like Lynch. Tony : I think you are right. Tonight we will see many of NCW's wrestlers for the first time. Johny : And what a way to start off your NCW career than with a shot at the Northeast Championship title. Terry : Will you two stop yacking, so we can call the match? Johny : The bell sounds and we are underway. Tony : These two wrestlers are both great mat technicians. Terry : Technicians? Geez how many technicians are in this fed? Tony : Um . . . oh look the Patriot makes his move. Johny : Patriot takes Lynch down with a dropkick, and follows up with a quick elbowdrop. Tony : Lynch is out of the way and kicks up to his feet. Terry : Patriot rushes in, and kicks Lynch in the midsection. Patriot drives him into the mat with a powerbomb. Johny : He's not done yet. Patriot pulls Lynch to his feet and attempts a snap suplex. Tony : Wait. Lynch blocks it and reverses it. Terry : You don't want to try to match up suplexes with Lynch. That's his territory. Tony : Patriot gets up and Lynch hooks him into a German suplex but he doesn't let go. Lynch pulls Patriot back up and nails him with a second German suplex. Terry : I think Patriot is unconscious! Johny : Lynch pulls him back up and nails him with a gutwrench suplex. Terry : Lynch is incredible! Tony : He's going for his finisher "Cry Out for Mercy". Johny : Patriot taps out. Lynch will advance to Round 2. Terry : Did you see that display of Suplexes. He is awesome! Tony : Ok. That was a great match. Terry : It looks like it's "Showtime" for Harvester as Kruegar nails him with his finisher. Tony : Kruegar goes for the cover and gets the three count. Kruegar advances. Terry : Uh...
