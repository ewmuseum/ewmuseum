Friday Night Fights — 16 Jan 1998

16 Jan 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
January 16, 1998 Friday Night Fights The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows the Bryce Jordan Center. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey and Terry Valentine sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the Bryce Jordan Center on the lovely campus of Penn State in State College PA. We have a great one for you tonight folks. Terry : That's right, Tony. We have a Lumberjack Match for the New England Title and a New York City Title match. As well as other great matchups. Tony : Let's not waste anytime, here tonight. Let's go straight to the Outlaw and the Outlaw's Saloon. We goto the backstage area where we see The Outlaw dressed like a lumberjack. Behind him is a moonshine still with scattered bottles with the name "White Lightening" printed on them. The Outlaw : Ladies and gentlemen, tonight I plan on putting an ass kicking on Johnny Blaze and the only burning anyone will feel will be this moonshine going down. Tonight, my guest on the Saloon are the three members of the Triple Threat, assuming they are still together. The camera catches Triple Threat as they walk out to the saloon and The Outlaw extends his hand to each member. All three members ignore the extended hand. The Outlaw : Now, I would like to start by congratulating you, even though we have had our differences, Brian Cario and Demented Drake in a good match last Friday for the tag belts. Fortunately my boys won, but we consider you guys the number one contenders. Drake cuts off the Outlaw. Drake : We don't give a damn about those stupid belts you horses' ass. We can beat those two clown's anytime we want. The Outlaw : Well, we aren't here to debate that issue. I would just say that if you could beat them you would've done last Friday, but you didn't. Now, what I want to know is what the hell is going on with you three. A few weeks ago, Mark Lynch, you were acting as if you were leaving Triple Threat and then last week you're all together in the ring putting a beating on the poor Harvster of Sorrow. What's going on? Lynch : We've never been more together. As for me leaving that's a load of B.S. I was just showin respect for Blackheart and Blaze. It's morons like you who blow things way out of propotion that make people believe rumors. The Outlaw : Mark Lynch, tonight you have a big match against Blackheart. Do you think you can regain gold in this federation and are you saying that we will see a united Triple Threat as you enter the ring? Lynch : Your damn right I'm gonna win. I will be champion again. Like I been saying all week he's gonna be 3 inches shorter and his gym bag is gonna be 15lbs. lighter. As for Drake and Brian being there, your damn right they'll be at ringside. We are a team. We will never break up and that's that. They'll be at rinside with me as always. The Outlaw : Well guys I hate to cut this short but with my big match tonight I must go and prepare. I wish you luck tonight against the little arogrant punk Blackheart. And Brian I don't no where you and I stand right now but if you still want to get on sometime just sign the contract. Cairo : Outlaw, any time any place. I fear no man. Don't Mess. The Outlaw begins to leave when "Back in Black" begins to blare over the PA. He shakes his head in disgust. Tony : Well there you have it. The Triple Threat is still together. Terry : I thought it was pretty obvious that they were back together after last Wednesday Night War. Tony : We are joined by Johny Morehouse. Good evening Johny. Johny : Great to be here, Tony. Tony : Let's go down to the ring and Gary Trudeau for the first match of the evening. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Welcome to Friday Night Fights! Our first match this evening will be set for one fall with a Ten minute time limit. Introducing first.. The Theme to "Psycho" starts over the arena. Trudeau : Accomponied to the ring by his manager Mr. Agony, hailing from Scranton, PA and weighing in at 233 pounds, we have Jak "The Boil" Lancer. The crowd starts to boo as Jak Lancer enters the arena. The Theme to "Psycho" is replaced by "Gutter Ballet" by Savage. Trudeau : And his opponent, from parts unknown and weighing in at 312 pounds, we have Gutter. The crowd gives Gutter a mixed reception as he walks down to the ring. Johny : Jak hits the ring, and Gutter immediatly levels him with a flying clothesline. Tony : This guy is wasting no time in making a name for himself. Terry : Yeah and a reputation as a cheat, attacking a man before he's ready to fight. Johny : Jak gets to his feet dazed, and Gutter knocks him through the ropes with a standing clothesline. Tony : Jak doesn't know where he is right now. Johny : Gutter hits the ropes and launches at him with a suicide dive. Terry : Does this guy have any regard for his body? Johny : Not apparantly. Tony : Jak moves out of the way and Gutter hits the guard rail and lands to the concrete. Terry : Jak picks Gutter up and rolls him into the ring. Johny : Jak whips Gutter into the far ropes. Gutter reverses it. Tony : Gutter hits the perpendicular ropes and turns it into a criss cross. Terry : Which man will make the move first. Johny : Gutter dives with what appears to be an attempt at a claw, but Jak somersaults out of the way. Tony : Jak slaps on his patented Boil Lancer. Terry : Gutter holds out for as long as he can, but gives up. Tony : Jak continues to win here in the NCW. Johny : No doubt about it, Jak Lancer is one of the top contenders. Let's go to the ring for the next match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Ten minute time limit. Introducing first, already in the ring, we have Tim Powers. Tim Powers raises his hand to the rather unenthuised crowd. Hunter Hearst Helmsly's theme starts over the Arena. The crowd goes to their feet and starts to boo. Trudeau : And his opponent, from Brighton, England and weighing in at 241lbs, we have Christopher Cunningham. The crowd continues to boo as Cunningham enters the arena with a beautiful woman on his arm. Cunningham generally ignore the crowd as he walks down the aisle. Tony : Tonight the Blue Blood Cunningham matches wits and skill against Tim Powers. Terry : And look at the beauty that escorted him to the ring. Johny : What’s this? The next chapter in Cunningham’s Book of Love? Terry : You’re just jealous because no woman would give you a second glance. Tony : The bell sounds and we are underway. Johny : Cunningham jumps Powers and brings him to the mat with an armdrag takedown. Terry : That didn’t take him long, now Powers is in trouble. Johny : Cunningham follows it up with a kneedrop to the back of Power's head. Tony : Cunningham has seemed to start up a fire with Nikodemous Segeir, it may be something he will later forget. Terry : Oh, you don’t know what you’re talking about. Cunningham will dispose of him just as easy as he’s taking care of Powers in the ring. Johny : Cunningham doubles over Powers with a punch into the midsection. Tony : Cunningham off the ropes drops Powers with a tremendous kneelift. Terry : Call the fat lady to sing, because this one is over. Johny : Cunningham yanks Powers up to the mat and sets him up for his patented Royal Slumber. Tony : He drives Powers down with the modified piledriver-front suplex combination. Terry : He goes for the pin and it’s a dominating victory for newcomer Cunningham. Johny : Very impressive for the man from England. Let's go to the next match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Ten minute time limit. Introducing first, already in the ring, we have the White Ninja. The White Ninja throws a few kicks and punches in the ring as the crowd gives a little response to the White Ninja. "Wild Thing" starts up over the Arena. The crowd goes to their feet and starts to cheer and sing along to the song. Trudeau : And his opponent, from Lakeland, Florida and weighing in at 240 pounds, we have Chris "Wild Thing" Rogers. The crowd continues to cheer as Rogers enters the arena. Rogers ignores the crowd and walks straight to the ring. Johny : Well this is strange. Usually Wild thing greets his fans. Tonight he just walked to the ring as if they didn't exist. Tony : This could be a new attitude completely for Wild Thing. Terry : I like him better if he's focused on the match rather than these idiot fans. Johny : There's the bell and Wild Thing levels Ninja with a sickle clothesline. Terry : Wow he practically took his head off. Tony : Wild Thing piledrives Ninja in the center of the ring. Johny : We are seeing a much more aggressive Wild Thing. Tony : I wonder what has driven Rogers to this state. Terry : Probably tired of people overlooking him. Johny : He's going to the top. Tony : You know what this means! Psychedlic Splash. Terry : He nails it and hooks the leg. Johny : 1-2-3. Tony : The fans are half for, half against Rogers. Terry : Rogers is walking back, and a small kid is asking for his autograph. Tony : Rogers grabs the paper and rips it up. Johny : What a jerk. Terry : I like him. Tony : Let's go to the next match The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Twenty minute time limit. Introducing first.. "Freak" by Silverchair starts over the arena. Trudeau : From the great country of Canada weighing in at a total combined weight of 514 pounds, we have the Canadian Connection. The crowd cheers as the Canadian Connection enter the arena. The two wrestlers slap the hands of the adoring crowd. Silver Chair is replaced with the Marine's Hymn. The crowd gives a mixed reaction. Trudeau : And their opponents, from the Tun Tavern in Philadelphia PA and weighing in at a total combined weight of 476 pounds we have the Commandant's Own. The crowd gives a mixed reaction as Sgt Payne and Major Havok enter the arena. The Commandant's Own are in full dress uniforms and they remain focused on the ring. Johny : Well the Canadians haven't been vrey active in the fed as of late. Tony : Mix that with the fact The Commandant's Own are new, we have no idea what to expect in this match. Terry : There's the bell. Johny : It's going to be Bret starting things off against Havok. Tony : Havok whips Bret into the ropes and catches him in a big backdrop. Johny : He follows up with a quick leg drop and tags in Payne. Terry : Payne piledrives Bret and goes to the second turnbuckle. Johny : He fistdrops Bret right between the eyes. Tony : Payne now runs across the ring and nails Travis off the apron. Terry : Travis attempts to slide into the ring, but the ref cuts him off. Johny : This is great teamwork by The Commandant's Own. Tony : They've cut the ring in half, and kept Bret from tagging out. Johny : Payne sets Bret up for the Ground Assault and drills Bret into the match. Terry : Payne tags back to Havok, who climbs to the top and delivers Terror From Above, a beautiful splash. Tony : Havok pins Bret, and Payne levels Travis who tries to prevent the pin. Johny : That was an impressive showing for newcomers Commandant's Own. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Ten minute time limit. Introducing first.. "Who Do You Love?" by George Throughgood and the Destroyers starts over the arena. Trudeau : From New York, New York weighing in at 300 pounds, we Johnny Dice. The crowd boos as Johnny Dice makes his way to the ring. He just smiles and walks about his business. "Who Do You Love?" is replaced with "Black Widow" by Alice Cooper. The crowd cheers. Trudeau : And his opponent, from Pittsburgh PA and weighing in at 350 pounds, we have the "Demolisher" Cain Graves. The crowd continues to cheer as Graves comes down to the ring. Johny : It's like Invasion of the Attitude Snatchers or something tonight. Tony : First Rogers comes out and turns on his fans, and now Dice comes out and doesn't berate or insult the fans. Terry : Something is definitly screwy here tonight. Johny : And we haven't even gotten to the main events. Tony : Dice is strutting around the ring with the arrogance of a young Scott Hall. Terry : Maybe Dice and Rogers have been hit in the head too many times. Johny : It's possible. Tony : Well there's the bell and Dice is keeping his distance. He seems to be mocking Cain. Terry : Dice is standing on one leg in the Crane Kick stance, and Cain is not appreciating the humor. Johny : Cain steps forward and takes a swinging haymaker at Dice. Tony : Dice ducks and grabs Cain by his moustache and monkey flips him. Terry : Well that was a risk Cain had for leaving his moustache unkempt. Tony : Dice rolls outside and pulls Cain's legs around the ringpost. Johny : Dice slams Cain's leg around the post. Terry : Well that's one way to keep a man from mounting an attack. Tony : Dice rolls back in and slaps on a figure four. Johny : Dice is using the ropes for leverage. Terry : The ref doesn't see him! Cain submits! Johny : This night is definitly crazy. Tony : What could possibly happen tonight that could be more weird? The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Ten minute time limit. Introducing first.. "Sweet Dreams" by Marylyn Manson starts over the arena. Trudeau : From parts unknown and weighing in at 260 pounds, we have Nemesis. The crowd boos as Nemesis makes his way to the ring. He ignores the crowd and continues on to the ring with a look of determination. "Sweet Dreams" is replaced with "Iron Man by Black Sabbath. Trudeau : And his opponent, also from parts unkown and weighing in at 255 pounds, we have the Black Scorpian. The crowd is on their feet as the light go out. A red scorpian forms in the middle of the ring by laser lights. The lights come on and the Black Scorpian is in the ring. Johny : There's the bell. Tony : Nemesis charges Scorpion. Terry : Nemesis attempts a crucifix. Johny : Scorpion nails him with The Equalizer! Tony : This could be the shortest match ever. Scorpion locks in the Scorpion Death Lock. Johny : Nemesis submits. Terry : What happened, I blinked. Tony : What happened wa the Black Scorpian just made Nemesis submit in about thirty seconds. Johny : I would say that was pretty impressive. Terry : He sucks! Let's go to the next match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Twenty minute time limit. This is a NON-Title match, Introducing first.. "Hey Man, Nice Shot" by Filter starts over the arena. Trudeau : Weighing in at a total combined weight of 489 pounds, accomponied by the Northeast Heavyweight Champion "The Painmaker" Randy Cross and representing the House of Pain, we have Da Killer Connection. The crowd boos hysterically as the Killer Connection enter the arena. The Killer Connection are yelling and threatening the fans as they proceed to the ring. Randy Cross is dressed in a Gray suit. Filter is replaced with "Sad but True" by Metallica. The crowd starts to cheer. Trudeau : And their opponents, the current CWC National Tag Team Champions and weighing in at a total combined weight of 632 pounds, we have the Overlords of the Dark. The crowd continues to cheer as the Champions make their way to the ring. Terry : Joining us for commentary in this match is leader of House of Pain, Randy Cross. Tony : Cross, let me welcome you to the announcer's booth. Randy : Thanks for having me. Well I get to watch Da Killer Connection take out The Lords of the Dance. Terry : There's the bell and it looks like Darken will start things off with Tiburon. Randy : Well here we have Darken, going up against one of the most under rated wrestlers in the NCW, Tiburon. Tony : Darken and Tiburon lock up and Darken gets him in an armbar. Terry : Darken follows up with a vicious short arm clothesline. Randy : And look at Tiburon he gets right back to his feet and continues fighting. Tony : Darken charges, and Tiburon ducks. Talon catches Darken with a kick to back when he hits the ropes. Randy : Beautiful team work displayed. Tony : Teamwork, that's blatant cheating. Terry : Oh shut up, Tony. Randy : Yeah shut up, Tony. Terry : Tiburon tags in Talon. Randy : Talon is am impressive newcomer, but once again overlooked by the fans. Terry : Talon whips Darken into the corner, but Darken reverses it. Tony : Darken tags out to Steihl. Randy : Oh great The Nutcracker Princess. Tony : Steihl attempts to cross corner whip Talon into a neutral corner. This time it's Talon who reverses it. Terry : Steihl just hit heads with the ref. Randy : Talon tags in Tiburon, who goes for the pin. 1-2-3-4-5-6-7-8 Tony : You can count all you want. The fact remains that the ref is out. Terry : The ref is starting to stir. Tony : He's groggy but he's going to count. 1-2 Terry : Steihl kicks out with great authority. Randy : The punk was clearly beaten in the middle of the ring. Tony : Steihl tags out to Darken who hits the ring like a fireball. Terry : Here comes Talon who is met with a vicious powerslam. Tony : Darken tosses Talon to the outside and sets up Tiburon for his infamous Dark Pogostick. Randy : What a stupid name for a move. Tony : It may not be a good name but it is very effective. Terry : Darken gets Tiburon airborne and bounces on Tiburon's skull five times before releasing the hold. Tony : Darken goes for the pin. Terry : 1-2-3. Randy : Well The Overlords were lucky tonight. When they meet the rest of the House of Pain their luck will run out. I'm out of here. Terry : It was a pleasure having you here, Randy. Great guy that Cross. Tony : I guess. Nevertheless, let's go to the first of the two main events. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Thirty minute time limit and will be for the New York City Title. Introducing first.. "Mar's the Bringer of War" by Holst starts over the arena. Trudeau : The challenger and former Northeast Champion from South Boston, Mass and weighing in at 220 pounds, accomponied by the rest of Triple Threat we have "Merciless" Mark Lynch. The crowd cheers as the Triple Threat makes their way to the ring. They play with the crowd as they walk towards the ring flashing the Triple Threat sign. Holst is replaced by "Kick Start my Heart" by Motley Crue. Trudeau : And his opponent, from Portland Oregon and weighing in at 195 pounds, and current New York City Champion we have accompaonied by Velvet, Micheal Blackheart. The crowd is on their feet cheering as the Blackhearts make their way to the ring stapping hands and enjoying themselves. Tony : Well here we go for the third time. Blackheart versus Lynch. Johny : This is part one of our tremendous double main event. Terry : And as we all know, Lynch’s lucky number is three, so this could be the end of Blackheart’s title reign. Tony : With Griffin gone, The Regulator’s seem to be fighting in a new light. They aren’t taking anything from anybody. So I don’t think Blackheart will just lie down and die for Lynch. Johny : Well I have to go with Lynch on this one, he’s been to the top and he wants back there. Tony : Blackheart gets halfway into the ring, and Lynch charges him. Johny : The ref rings the bell while Lynch is clubbing Blackheart with rights and lefts. Terry : Blackheart is already down on the mat, and Lynch is stomping a mudhole in him. Tony : Blackheart sweeps Lynch and kicks up to his feet. Johny : Lynch gets to his feet, and Blackheart charges him. Tony : Lynch counters with a backdrop, and sends Blackheart goes over the top and lands feet first on the apron. Terry : Lynch goes after him and tries to suplex him back into the ring. Johny : Blackheart buries a fist in his midsection, and attempts a sunset flip. Tony : Lynch catches Blackheart in midair and tries to slam him to the mat. Terry : Blackheart counters with an armdrag takedown. Both men crash down to the mat. Tony : What a match! Johny : A match? We’re only five minutes into it and we’ve seen more action than most wrestling feds see in an entire program. Tony : Blackheart kicks up to his feet. So does Lynch. I’m impressed. Terry : Blackheart hits the ropes and launches at Lynch with a flying forearm smash. Tony : Lynch ducks and grabs Blackheart from behind when he hits the mat. Johny : Lynch explodes with a Northern Lights Suplex. Terry : 1-2-kick out. Tony : Blackheart was kicking on instinct there. Johny : Lynch yanks Blackheart up by the hair and is admonished by the ref for hair pulling. Tony : I don’t think Lynch cares right now. Terry : It looks like Lynch is setting him up for a vertical suplex. Johny : He gets him up and look at the power of Lynch, he’s holding Blackheart up causing all of the blood to rush to his head. Tony : Lynch falls back and Blackheart hits hard. Terry : That took the wind out of Lynch as well but he has plenty of time to catch it. Johny : Blackheart will be lucky to walk out of this match, much less retain his title. Tony : Outside the ring Velvet and Tammy are having words. Johny : If a catfight ensues I have to put my money on Velvet. Terry : Back in the ring, Lynch is applying the modified Boston Crab, known worldwide as The Lion Tamer. Tony : Blackheart reaches out for the ropes, he’s almost within reach. Johny : Blackheart, with a tremendous show of endurance and strength, pushes up and works his way to the ropes. Terry : Both men are reaching the point of frustration, but I have to believe that Lynch has more control at this point. Johny : Although I hate to say it, I would have to agree. Tony : Lynch holds up the Triple Threat sign, and sets Blackheart up for the infamous Phoenix Plex. Terry : He nails it, and Blackheart is down in the center of the ring. Johny : Velvet is screaming for Blackheart to get up. Tony : Lynch looks like he’s ready to finish Blackheart off. Johny : He’s got him set up for a powerbomb. Tony : Lynch yanks him up into the air and pow- No! Terry : I can’t believe it. Blackheart countered with a Frankensteiner into a pin. Johny : 1-2-3. Unbelivable! Blackheart wins! Tony : Lynch is just starting to stir. He doesn’t know what just hit him, but you can be sure that he is not happy about it. Johny : That was an amazing match, to bad there had to be a loser. Terry : The wrong guy won that is for sure! Tony : Let's go to the ring for the main event tonight. Our next match this evening will be set for one fall with a Thirty minute time limit and will be for the New England Title and will be under Lumberjack rules. Introducing first.. He looks very determined and is ignoring the crowd. ACDC is replaced by "Pepper" by the Butthole Surfers.
