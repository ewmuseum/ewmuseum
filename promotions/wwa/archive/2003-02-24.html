Jimmy: Kash doesn't look happy! EVD: I've got something for you. He reaches into his pocket and pulls out nothing. He has lost the letter! EVD: I'll be right back! Eddie slides back out of the ring and starts searching for his lost letter. Meanwhile Jackson Kraven is choking Chance with the bottom rope and Kash has reapplied the Kashmission to Nick Adams. Fat Tony: A whole lot of strangulation in that there ring! Adams finally reaches the ropes and Kash lets the hold go. Adams drops to the mat, gasping for breath and Kash goes off in search of other prey. Jimmy: A lucky escape for Nick Adams there! EVD is still searching for his letter. He is picking through what is left of the table he went through. Suddenly he sees a bit of movement out of the corner of his eye. James Curzon is sneaking off, in his hand is a piece of paper, Eddie chases after him. Crowd: 3…….2……1! V/O: IIIIIIIIIIIIIIITTTT'S SH... what do you mean "I have to use the team entrance?!" Jimmy: Oh no. I thought it was him earlier I prayed this wouldn't happen. Fat Tony: God hates you Jimmy. Showtime is pushed down the ramp unceremoniously by the backstage crew. At the same time James Curzon is running away from EVD up the ramp. Jimmy: Better watch out! Curzon is backing away from EVD and Showtime is pushed down the ramp by the Ring Crew. They back into each other Showtime: ARRRRGGHHH!!!!! Curzon: ARRRRRGGGH!!!!! They scream, Showtime faints and Curzon falls over. EVD takes the paper out of Curzon's hand and runs back to the ring. Jimmy: Eddie has got his letter back! Kash is watching from a distance, talking to himself, as Kraven and Chance fight it out. Eddie gets back in and hands the paper to Kash, he reads it. Kash: Escape Routes from Carver Hawkeye Arena? Interesting. Jimmy: EVD has stolen Curzon escape plan! EVD starts looking all over again. Kash continues his conversation with himself. Back on the ramp Showtime and Curzon have recovered. They look warily at each other and then at the ring. Neither of them trusts the other. Jimmy: Neither of them wants to go first in case they get stabbed in the back! The start edging very slowly towards the ring. Suddenly A picture of the globe slowly spinning is shown on the tron, it stops on the British isles which are covered with a Union Jack. The Union Jack grows larger and large until it has covered virtually the whole planet. Suddenly Red, white and blue sparks fly out across the stage. RULE BRITANNIA! Suddenly as Politik by Coldplay blasts out of the speakers and a line of bright white pyros explode across the back of the stage. Damian Thorne appears at the top of the ramp. Jimmy: It seems Thorne couldn't wait till the proper time to enter! Curzon and Showtime stare up the ramp in horror as Thorne walks towards them. They look down the ramp and see Eddie Van Dorn also advancing on them. Curzon: AAARRGGHH! Showtime: AAARRGGHH! The run around in a circle and then run into each other, banging heads. They fall of either side of the ramp and fall nearly 2 feet onto the concrete floor. Fat Tony: Haha! Those dumb schmucks! Eddie sees his letter and picks it up he runs into the ring and hands it to Kash. He pockets it without a word. EVD: Not even a thank-you? Kash: We could say thank-you, we won't though! EVD flies into a rage he punches Kash, knocking him down to the floor and then slides out of the ring. He picks up a chair and smashes it down on Kash. The bell rings. Jimmy: EVD is disqualified! He shouldn't have done that, The Southern team are gone! Eddie is disgusted. He throws the chair away and walks back up the ramp. Offering a half-hearted kick to Curzon on the way past. Fat Tony: Only six men left now! Kraven is being attacked by Adams. He is fed up with it, he picks Adams up and tosses him out of the ring. He follows him out and starts smashing their heads against the ring-post. The ref tries to stop him but Kraven pushes him away, the Referee lands in a crumpled heap. Fat Tony: The Ref is down and here comes the baddest mofo on the show! Jimmy: Well, Damian Thorne certainly took his time getting to the ring! Fat Tony: Just good tactics, thats all! Thorne stands in the ring and faces up to Kash across the ring. The rest of the British team, Chance and James Curzon get into the ring and line up with their team-mate. Fat Tony: This looks bad for Kash! Suddenly Havoc appear on the ramp, they march into the ring. Jimmy: Havoc are here to help their Team-mate against The Brits! Fat Tony: They should have let him face a beating on his own. Deimos, Phobos and Kash stand facing the three Brits. Suddenly Thorne rushes towards Kash. Havoc make no attempt to stop him. They grab the other two and toss them onto the outside. Jimmy: Havoc made light work of those two! Fat Tony: Watch out Damian! Havoc advance on Thorne, who is stamping on Kash. Suddenly they join in, also pounding away on the helpless Kash. Jimmy: Havoc aren't here to help their Team-mat from The World but their team-mate from Hop!! Kraven also gets into the ring. He picks up Kash and powerbombs him over the top rope onto the outside. The boos are deafening as the House of Pain gaze arrogantly out over the crowd. Fat Tony: House of Pain had this planned all along! Fantastic! The four men beckon on the others outside the ring. Curzon, Showtime, Adams, Kash and Chance. They pile in and a mass brawl breaks out. HoP have the size advantage and it shows as the men battle in the middle of the ring. Jimmy: HoP are just too big and strong! Fat Tony: I know, isn't it great? Jimmy: Someone ought to do something! A cheer goes up from the crowd and Johnny Shrapnel runs in to the ring. He hits a superkick that sends Deimos over the top rope, he takes down Phobos with a running bulldog before Kraven gets to him and throws him out of the way but the damage is done. Chance manages to get onto the turnbuckle and hits a missile dropkick that knocks Phobos out of the ring. Jimmy: Shrapnel has stopped another HoP victory! Havoc get to their feet and see the small Veteran heading back up the ramp. They give chase and leave the legal men to get on with the match. Jimmy: Now the odds are stacked against HoP! Adams takes down Thorne with a German suplex and Showtime flys through the air for a Showboat, but misses and lands on his ass. Adams covers Thorne anyway. Fat Tony: Why don't his British team-mates help him? Jimmy: He abandoned them, now they abandon him! The ref has just recovered enough to make the count. Ref: 1…….2…….3! Jimmy: Nick Adams eliminates Damian Thorne! Meanwhile the other two Brits are taking on Jackson Kraven. Chance and Curzon hit the Tomorrow man with a double snap suplex. Jimmy: They've taken him down, but can they get the pin? Fat Tony: Nope. Chance covers Kraven so does Curzon, then Kash and even Kraven's Team-mate Showtime pile on. You can barely see the big guy from under the pile of bodies. Ref: 1…….2……3! Jimmy: Kraven is gone! Fat Tony: Four teams and five men to go! Thorne and Kraven are dumped out of the ring. They shout obscenities at the other wrestlers before deciding they are not worth it and walking away sullenly. Jimmy: That's suprisingly good behaviour from the House of Pain! Fat Tony: They've already hijacked this event for themselves, maybe they don't want to push their luck! In the ring Curzon looks around nervously, he stands up and runs off, only to walk straight into Nick Adams' back. He staggers backwards and right into Kash. Jimmy: The Kashmission! Kash locks in his finisher but Chance manages to knock Curzon free of the hold. Jimmy: Chance rescues his team mate! Fat Tony: But gets no thanks for it! Curzon has decided it is far too risky to hand about and runs off up the ramp. Adams grabs Showtime and whips him into the corner. Showtime hits the turnbuckle face first, he spins around three times and falls on his face in the middle of the ring. Jimmy: Showtime was knocked out by the turnbuckle! Fat Tony: How did he manage that? Adams takes advantage and covers Showtime. Ref: 1…….2…. Jimmy: Showtime rolls over! He's covering Adams! Ref: 1…….2……..3! Fat Tony: Showtime got a pinfall? Jimmy: I don't think that's ever happened before. Fat Tony: And he has eliminated the last member of the West Coast Team! Only 1 American left now! Come on, Showtime! I never thought I'd say that. Jimmy: Where has James Curzon got to? Suddenly the Tron springs to life. We see James Curzon running through the backstage area. He pushes through the fire doors and keeps on running. Jimmy: That answered my question! I've got a feeling that he's not coming back! Fat Tony: I wouldn't either if everyone was out to get me! Chance leaps off the turnbuckle and takes Kash out with a moonsault. Then he flips onto his feet to face the oncoming Showtime. Fat Tony: Showtime is all that is left between the greatest nation in the world and failure! That is not good news. Showtime ducks his head and tries to spear Chance. The Brit leapfrogs over Showtime and he hits the ringpost head-first. Chance climbs the turnbuckle and executes a perfect somersault plancha onto Showtime. Kash laughs loudly. Kash: That wasn't very good, now was it? Chance: Well, you do it better then! Kash: OK Kash climbs the turnbuckle and goes for his own somersault plancha. He leaps forward, lands on his feet, then does a forward roll and lies over Showtime. Jimmy: I think Chance did it better! Fat Tony: I think you would have done it better! The ref drops to count the pin Jimmy: Surely that's the end for the USA! Ref: 1…….2……3! Fat Tony: No! No! No! A Canadian or a Limey is going to win! What a tragedy! Showtime is unmoving so the referee just rolls him out of the ring as unceremoniously as he was pushed to the ring at the beginning of this match. His body slumps on the ringside floor. Fat Tony: I've never seen such disrespect! From the entrance ramp, the tinny sound of dozens of small feet stomping their way to the ring can be heard. Fat Tony: It's the Mini Showtimes! They will make everything all right! Jimmy: Oh no. I was hoping I dreamed those guys. Fat Tony: This IS a dream come true! The Mini Showtime's make a show of checking Showtime's vital signs at ringside. They all shake their heads in dismay. Jimmy: Must be alive then. The Mini Showtimes all hoist Showtime onto their diminutive shoulders and proceed to haul him up the ramp as would pall bearers. Jimmy: I don't suppose they'll toss him out like they did Payne and Mack Tenn. Fat Tony: Perish the thought! Istn't this is reminiscent of The Undertaker's druids from all those years ago. Jimmy: Kind of like a freaky Disneyland version of it. The two men circle each other. Kash lunges forward and grabs Chance. He hugs him and bites him on the neck. Jimmy: Did Kash just bite him? Fat Tony: Yeah and why not? They do it in boxing. Chance is a little freaked out by that and backs off from Kash. Then Chance runs into the ropes and springs back with a dropkick. Kash steps aside and gets behind Chance. He locks in the Kashmission. Jimmy: The Kashmission! Again! Fat Tony: One move, again and again! He's turning into The Rock! Oh no, the Rock doesn't have that many moves! Chance reaches for the ropes but can't quite make it. He flaps wildly trying to reach them but Kash is just too strong for him. Chance is left with just one option. Jimmy: Submission! Chance has submitted! Fat Tony: The World wins! Like I always said they would! Jimmy: Kash is the victor of Global Warfare! Kash stands on the turnbuckle and the fans go wild. He jumps back down and raises Chance's arm. They crowd cheer again Jimmy: That is a true picture of sportsmanship!
