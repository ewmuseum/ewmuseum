The Scouting Report - Issue 8

24 Sep 2003

Bylined Joe Stock - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That's right, it's back. The Scouting Report has returned and is packed full of info. It's bee a wild time here in the WWA and we got a lot of stuff to cover, so let's just dive right in. -The big news of course is that the WWA no longer has a television contract. This is a shock to the entire federation. In a board meeting this week, Warr has told the staff that unless we find a new network by December 1st, than the WWA will be shutting it doors. Warr has been talking to many companies, looking for some time slot. Through I must wonder how well he can look when we find him every night drunk in his hotel room. -I can't get a hold of the WWA Owner Mark Harrington to find out his take on all this, he has taken a vacation to Europe and never left a way to get a hold of him. -I have been told to act as if we will get a new contract and keep on the lookout for talent and make sure to keep resigning WWA wrestlers. I just resigned a number of our wrestlers, details later in the report. -Birthday Bash was an amazing event, with a super hot crowd. From top to bottom, we delivered. If this was our very last televised event, than we went out with a bang. -Mack Tenn Vs Texas Longhorns was a good opener, through it has become more and more clear the Longhorns can't deliver in the ring. I thought Mack Tenn looked very impressive and hopefully they will bounce back and get a victory. -The Rookie Fourway could have had a bit more action, but in the time they had, they managed to do well. Mal Somers has atough mantle to carry, because the Rookie Champion is always looked to make a quick impact in the WWA. Many have fail; hopefully Somers will be an exception. -My God, Grunge Vs EkoSyde was one hell of a match, best of the night if you ask me. The wrestling was off the scale and it had the fans on the edge of their seats the whole time. I don't know how either man will top this, but I can't wait to see. -There's a long story about the Shaman Vs Magician match and why it was cancelled. I can't really discuss the details, but I will say what happened didn't not improve Matt Warr's mood. -Enigma Vs Chance has the bad luck to follow the possible match of the year that was the two out of three falls match, but I don't think they disappointed. A nice brutal math with good creative use of weapons. When it's reveled who Enigma is, the WWA will be shocked, believe me. -I wasn't sure how good Cross Vs Thorne could be, since Cross really is past his prime, but the two men, realizing they couldn't match the isnane wrestling of the last two matches, went for pure brutality. Cross did the right thing and passed the torch properly. -I'm not sure what to make of Birthday Brawl. I'm not a fan of the match, I think it's too much stuff and too many high spots, that elicit only so much of a pop, but have a very real chance of ending careers. Still I thought all the men involved did a great job. I'm proud to see that so many rookies walked out with belts. The American Lucahdores could single-handily save the tag division. Nick Adams shows a lot of potenical and the constant exposure involved with the Television Title can realy help him out. I was shocked when Kash won the United States Title, that newcomer has really risen very quickly in the WWA. Hope he doesn't crash and burn. -What can be said about our new champion Ian Tense? Finally the main event has a new star. It's funny how the sidekick always becomes the true star here in the WWA. Just look at Denrol who was only suppose to be backup for David Wattsoner, look at Tracer Bullet, who was in Nik Segier shadow for so long, Jackson Kraven who was Brian Jones' lackey, and now Ian Tense, who was the sidekick to Troy Walcott. You never know where you'll find the next Headliner. -I spoke with the new champion shortly after he won. He was still in shock that he was given the chance to be on top, talked about what a roller coaster the match was, and that he knows 30 other guys in the back want his spot now. I think Tense has a long and profitable future here in the WWA. -It's always tough to put together a match with so many people and the agent John Arthur did as good a job as can be expected. In that type of match, some people's psychology gets lost of course. Birthday Brawl isn't really the best match to tell a story in. -I'm not sure who will get the big shot for the WWA World Title at Rage in the cage, if that event happens, but both Wes Sanders and Tracer Bullet have a claim to make for a title shot. We'll see who gets the big chance. -Injuries are a requirement of a match like Birthday Brawl and this was no exception. Almost every wrestler is at least sore, but a few have more serious injuries. -Showtime hurt himself BEFORE the match, in a sparring session. Thus he got eliminated very quickly. I can't get details on the injury because the medical report was lost in a mysterious fire. Showtime will be out for an unknown amount of time. -His partner Jim Black has asked for personal time and has been granted it. We hope to see Reborn back as soon as possible. -I caught Venny LeTrav right before he left the arena. He had quite a few bumps and bruises, but was proud of how well he did in the match. He even called it the best night of his life. Him and Bourne will make some great tag champions I think. -Damian Thorne managed to rack up quite a number of injuries in the Hell in a Cell and is expected to only do lite workouts until the WWA is back on television. -Jackson Kraven has suffered a broken rib, but is expected to be back in action in a short time. He is one tough guy. -Tracer Bullet and Ian Tense both landed safely from their big match ending bump, through they were using a lot of ice after the show. -Denrol's neck is causing him a lot of problems; I'm told he is on a number of painkillers. He is very hopeful he'll get clearance to return for Rage in the Cage. -Onto contracts and other talent stuff. -Ramsey Kaine has entered drug rehab. He had a lot of potential; I hope he works out his personal problems. We in the WWA wish him the best of luck. -We have signed the rookies Chance and Nick Adams to full contracts. The two have shown great potential and we hope they have a bright future. -Grunge & Rick Venom will be sticking around for a while. -The World Champion Ian Tense has signed a new yearlong contract with the WWA. The man is now the highest paid wrestler in the federation. -Jackson Kraven and Damian Thorne have both signed new contracts. Kraven's has some nice incentives if he manages to get back the World Title, while Thorne's include a buyout clause in case he manages to make it to the main event in 2003. -Randy Cross and the MWC are no longer with the WWA, they have decided to hang up the boots. Cross has left a huge mark on this federation and he will never be forgotten. If we create a Hall of Fame, he should be the first one named in it. -Craven Moore and the WWA have parted ways. He is looking to further his career in Hollywood and we are okay with that. We wish you all the luck in the world Tex and you will be missed. Hope to see him back someday. -Barrett Sinclair has decided the WWA isn't for him. It's too bad, he was impressive I think. -The status of The Magician's contract is up in the air, but I think he may be released. -We are not currently accepting talent because of the TV situation, but that will hopefully change. -We have what could be the final set of house shows coming up in the great state of Iowa next weekend. I expect a number of the top WWA stars to be in action in those cities, tickets are flying out the door as we speak. -Tickets for upcoming episodes of Meltdown and Fiesta are being sold, but are subject to cancellation and refund of course. I am very hopeful we'll pull out of this, we didn't spend 5 years to end it all like this. That's all for now, I'll be back no matter what next week, in what I admit, could be the very last Scouting Report ever. From under the New York Yankees baseball cap, this is Joe Stock, over and out.
