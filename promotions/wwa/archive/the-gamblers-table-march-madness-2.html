The Gamblers Table — March Madness

12 Jan 2004

Bylined "The Gambler" (Kevin/Vic Sage), the fed's own in-house prediction columnist persona.

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another huge event from the World Wrestling Alliance is swiftly approaching. The newly named May Revolution promises to be huge, with the top stars of the WWA doing what they do best. I’m Kevin Sage, former Las Vegas bookie, here to provide my thoughts on the fantastic action that we’ll all be privileged to witness soon. In addition, I’ll be providing my own betting preferences, sharing my reasons for the bets that I’ll be making. Of course, don’t take my word as gospel, and don’t blame me for any losing bets that you make. Gen X Champion MyZery vs. Prometheus vs. Reverend Tweak vs. Tommy Starr Gen X Title Four of the hottest newcomers to the WWA meet in a Four Way match for the WWA Gen X Title. Since his debut on the April 19th edition of Fiesta, MyZery has had an impressive series of victories in the WWA, including a win over Showtime to become the current WWA Gen X Champion, and defeating both Showtime and Curzon in a handicap match. The undefeated MyZery has shown an ability to adapt to any situation. This would certainly be a big win for Prometheus, named after the Greek legend of the titan that gave man the knowledge of fire, and he has the prodigious strength, combined with a heavy dose of in-ring technical skills, to pull out an upset. Reverend Tweak, despite his strange, biblical fascination with birds and ice cream, is a great technician and brawler, and is an alumni of the EWA. Angered by Tommy Starr, who stole his bible on this past Fiesta, we may see a more vicious side of Tweak, that could turn the tables in this match. Tommy Starr, the newest member of Devil’s Own, has been impressive both in the EWA and now in the WWA, with a wide-ranging arsenal of both traditional and high-flying attacks. This is one match that I’m looking forward to seeing. With these four young competitors battling it out under Gen X rules, I expect great things. In the end, I think we’ll see MyZery continue his undefeated streak, and that’s where I’ll put my money. Keru No Akiro vs. Fuel Boiler Room Match Brought into the WWA by Shaman, Fuel is one half of the Horsemen. With some remarkable submission skills, and a penchant for fire, Fuel has already made a name for himself in the WWA under Shaman’s tutelage. Keru No Akiro, Japanese for The Rage That Runs Blood Red, has an impressive run of victories under his belt, including two previous wins over Fuel. Of course, the stakes have been raised, making this a Boiler Room match. The only way to win this one is to be the first man to exit the boiler room. This requires that one’s opponent be unable to stop one’s egress. Although Fuel knows how to dish out pain, I’ll be betting on Keru No Akiro’s power and brawling tactics in this one. Venny LeTrav vs. Se7en Cage Match This feud has become one of the most brutal and destructive battles in a short time. In the ring and out, these two men have gone after each other with cruel abandon. Se7en has brutalized Venny at every turn, at times with assistance from Venny’s former partner, Joey K. In fact, on the May 20th edition of Meltdown, these two nearly ended each other’s career. Now, they’ll meet in a steel cage, with no outside interference. Se7en is a frightening individual, combining incredible power with a sadistic streak. Venny LeTrav is normally an easy-going young man, but his hatred for Se7en has brought out the most violent temper in this innovative luchadore. Without a doubt, these two are going to tear each other apart in that cage, but when the dust settles, my money says that the Ragin' Cajun, Venny LeTrav, comes out on top, showing us all why we never want to make a Cajun angry. Jimmi Zion vs. Shaman Himalayan Pit Match Jimmi Zion’s claims to being “extreme” caught the attention of none other than the veteran extreme icon, Shaman. Since then, Shaman has made it his personal mission to show “Sky Flyin’” Jimmi Zion exactly what it means to be extreme. He caused Zion to be eliminated in the huge elimination match on the May 10th Fiesta, and has ferociously attacked Zion at every turn, spilling his blood with sadistic glee. Even when Zion came up victorious in a one on one contest, Shaman brutally attacked him afterward. In this Himalayan Pit match, these two men will be trapped in a pit twenty feet deep, twenty feet long, and twenty feet wide, covered by a locked gate. Somewhere within the six inches of sand at the bottom of the pit, the key to the gate is hidden. The winner will be the first man to find that key, climb the twenty-foot rope and unlock the gate to escape. Needless to say, this match is incredibly dangerous, requiring the winner to beat his opponent into unconsciousness. Shaman’s “Got Blood” chant should be more than satisfied in this one. I have to give credit to Zion’s heart and determination. He hasn’t backed down yet and despite the beatings that he has suffered, keeps coming back for more. Unfortunately, this is Shaman’s territory, he created this match and he knows it better than anyone. When all is said and done, I’ll bet on the extreme experience of Shaman to take this match. Nick Adams vs Grunge US Title Nick Adams has been clamoring for a chance at the vacated US Title since March Madness. Only one thing has stood in his way, Grunge. There’s already plenty of bad blood between these two. Adams lost at Rage in the Cage when Julie, Grunge’s wife, came to Grunge’s assistance with a pair of bolt cutters. Then, at March Madness, Adams accidentally injured Julie with a mistimed chair shot. All along the way, these two have been developing a seething anger toward the other, and now they’ll settle it in the ring, one on one, vying for the US Title. These two competitors are very similar. Both stand at six feet tall, and weigh 220 pounds. Both have great technical skills and are very quick in the ring. Realistically, either man would make a great US Champion, but only one can come out of this with the belt. My money says that Nick Adams will finally get his wish, and will earn that US Title that has sat vacant since March Madness. Enigma vs. Chance Casino Match On the March 3rd edition of Meltdown, Chance, Enigma, and Jason Wainwright took on the WWA Franchise players, as part of Chance’s feud with Denrol. After the match, Denrol ran from the arena, leading Chance to the parking lot. There, Chance was nearly run down by a red Silverado, with Denrol in the back. Since that time, we’ve all wondered who was trying the truck. Well, we got our answer on May 12th when Joey K confessed to being the driver. More surprising, we discovered that Enigma had orchestrated the entire thing! He had been laying the groundwork, and planning his revenge since Rage in the Cage. Recently, he gave Chance the opportunity to join him and Joey K, to stand at his side, rather than be destroyed. Chance refused the offer and suffered yet another beating for it. Now, these two will meet in a Casino match at Revolution. Although the smallest man on the roster, Chance is also among the fastest and most innovative. Enigma is also a high-flyer with an impressive mean streak and a calculating mind. Unfortunately, at the time of this writing, the rules of the Casino match are unknown. It’s tough to make a bet without having that information, but I don’t think that Joey K will be far from Enigma’s side. Because of that, I think that Enigma’s plans will come to fruition, and that’s where I’ll put my hard-earned money. X-Corp vs. The Cell vs. Havoc vs. DeLuccis Tag Team Titles King of the Hill match The reining Tag Team Champions X-Corp have had their hands full since winning the titles at March Madness. Their feud with the Cell has continued unabated, and have had their friendship tested by Chance. Chance set out to save Mal Somers from what he believes to be the inevitable betrayal that will occur, in much the same way that Dave Harley turned on his former partner, Johnny Carnage. Although X-Corp is still together, one has to wonder how much Mal Somers really trusts Dave Harley. The Cell has run rampant over Meltdown, just as they did Fiesta for so long. Having recently formed Devil’s Own with WWA World Champion Ian Tense, Leviathan and Lockdown have become more dangerous than ever. Havoc, coming off of their win against the Veteranz, driving the greatest tag team in WWA history back into retirement, have made their presence known in the Tag Team Title race, as well. Tommy and Tony DeLucci earned their right to the number one contender spot at March Madness, but have not had the opportunity for a fair chance at the belts, due to the interference of the Cell and Havoc. With a series of no contest decisions and utter brawls leading into Revolution, anything could happen. In this King of the Hill match, the tag belts will be suspended above a four-tiered scaffold in the arena parking lot, dressed to appear as a wasteland, complete with junked cars and bonfires. The three challenging teams need to reach those belts before being eliminated by pinfall or submission. X-Corp is not required to reach the titles. If they can eliminate all of their opponents before any of them can reach the belts, then X-Corp retains. This is going to be one hell of a match, folks. Mal Somers and Dave Harley are both accustomed to this kind of extreme match, and they’re tough enough to make a strong showing. Lockdown and Leviathan have shown their own familiarity with the hardcore style, and they’ve got plenty of cruelty to back it up. Phobos and Deimos are the biggest men in the match, and as three-time Tag Team Champions, they’ll be one of the favorites going into this one. The DeLuccis have surprised a number of people in the WWA with their swiftly growing skills, but they’ve been strangely quiet as of late. All things considered, I think the Cell is likely to walk, or limp, away with the victory, and that’s how I’ll be betting. WWA World Champion "The Prototype" Ian Tense vs. Kash WWA World Title Ian Tense has secured his place as one of the greatest World Champions in WWA history, holding the World Title for an astonishing seven months. Ian Tense, with a combination of incomparable ring skills, masterful machinations, and his Ian-Tensifier finishing move, has retained his title despite the efforts of some of the WWA’s best challengers, such as Wes Sanders, Damian Thorne, Jackson Kraven, and Craven Moore. On the April 28th Meltdown, Ian Tense aligned himself with the Cell to form Devil’s Own. In the process, the three men viciously attacked Kash, causing irreparable damage with a hammer to Kash’s face. Since then, Kash has set his sights squarely on Ian Tense, while hiding his face behind a gothic mask. With the assistance of Devil’s Own, Ian Tense has tried to dissuade the attention of Kash, through various cruel attacks. Through it all, Kash has persevered, never wavering from his hatred of Ian Tense, returning again and again. With a plethora of in-ring skills, his willingness to do whatever is necessary, and his unbeatable Kashmission finisher, Kash has become one the most popular wrestlers in the WWA. Either man could pull a victory from anywhere. Kash may be larger than Ian, but Ian has shown numerous times that he can overcome any size difference. Either way, this match will be incredible, fired up with intensity and rage. One has to wonder just how much of a factor the other members of Devil’s Own will be in this match. Despite this, and please pardon the pun, my money is on Kash to overcome all odds and win the World Title. As you can see, Revolution is going to be an incredible night. Don’t miss this one, no matter what happens. All eight of these matches are guaranteed to be spectacles. So, this is one year of the Gambler’s Table, and I’ve enjoyed every moment of working with the WWA staff and speaking with the fans worldwide. Thank you all for your support and hard work, especially the backstage folks that make it all work, President Matthew Warr, Commissioner Nathaniel Raine, Director of Talent Josh Wilson, Director of Public Relations [name withheld], Director of Promotions Mike Dale, and the man who started it all, the WWA Owner Mark Harrington. Until next time, remember the words of Oliver Wendell Holmes, Jr.: Controversy equalizes fools and wise men -- and the fools know it.
