Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Full script of the World Wrestling Alliance’s 2006 Bourbon Street Brawl supercard from New Orleans, with the Bar Room Brawl, the US title match, the three-way grudge match, the masked-title match, the Bourbon Street Brawl itself and backstage segments.
Many men have passed through this road before… Clips are shown from the past, starting from the first ever Bourbon Street Brawl, gradually finishing with last year’s Brawl. Many men lived to fight the next day… Clips are shown of Dave Harley, Shaman and Mal Somers competing in the BSB’s of old. For some, this winding road is slowly coming to a stop… Flashes are shown of Tracer Bullet and Denrol in tag action. For others, the journey is only just beginning… We see Vista holding the Television title, followed by Jonathan Winters, and finally Khaled raising the United States title. But be it at the start or at the end of their journey… Momentary snap shots are shown of New Orleans and the Infamous Bourbon Street. Every man makes a stop at this particular street.
