Ember burning out? Papers leaked from within the WWA head offices have revelead that the WWA World Champion, Ember, is coming close to the end of his original contract. An unknown rookie at the time of his signing to the WWA, December 2004, Ember was only signed to an eighteen month contract - which is set to expire in June 2006, only a few short months away. It has been revealed that contract neogitaions have stalled, with Ember unwilling to negotiate at all before RITC - where he is set to face Mal Somers in defence of his title - and refusing to set a date at all to settle the matter. The WWA are obviously eager to not only renew his contract but get him on the books for a much longer term but Ember's refusal has led some to question if it is just part of the games that he plays ... a ploy to ensure that his contract negotiation goes his way ... or if the firey trail that Ember has burnt through the WWA could be burning out? Ember himself was 'unavailable' for comment ... or so he said when he hung up on this reporter. Designed By [name withheld] | © World Wrestling Alliance 1997 - 2005
