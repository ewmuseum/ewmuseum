The Scouting Report - Issue 6

24 Sep 2003

Bylined Joe Stock - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome everyone to the Pre-Labor Day Brawl edition of TSR. We've got a lot of stuff to cover, so let's get right down to it. -The WWE just put up a great Summerslam show. Believe me the WWA plans to top that with Labor Day Brawl. From top to bottom we have an excellent card and I just know the WWA stars will deliver. -Fiesta was the last show before the Pay-Per-View. I thought it was a good show. Here are some random thoughts on the event. -The opening promo was more a WWE-style promo than WWA, but I think it did what it needed to do. The hype for this Title Tournament is just growing by the day, it will not be easy to live up to it. -The Horsemen may have saved us all from a substandard match. While I'm impressed the Longhorns have been working hard, their matches could use some speeding up. -The Kash and Se7en match was short and sweet. As reported last week, Se7en is on his way out, but we do hope to see him back very soon. Kash meanwhile has surprised a number of people backstage with his efforts. I expect him in the title scene soon. -A nice brawl between Stryfe and EkoSyde. I really liked the figure four spot. The EkoSyde win is certainly surpise and changes the face of the Labor Day Brawl card. -The tag match nicely set up the rookie title match. You know, next to the World Title, the Rookie belt may be the most hotly contested belt in the WWA. With great stars like Bourne, Payne, Venny, Williams, Kash, and others in the hunt, it should stay that way. -Talking about rookies, the WWA has decided to temporary put a halt on singles wrestler applications. With some EWA superstars and one other I'll reveal next week, we feel we have enough singles. What really needs help is the tag team roster. We're looking for about three good teams. Check the web site to find out how to contact us. -From the EWA, we have added Dave Harley, Mac Maloy, and "The Chairman" Mal Somers. I wish all three men best of luck in their new home. -On the injury side, there isn't much to talk about. Venny LeTrav's ribs are feeling better. I'm told by him that his minor injury will not stop his high-flying ways. There is no house shows this week, as we want all our stars to be 100% for Labor Day Brawl. Troy Walcott is going to have his final doctor's appointment on Tuesday and it's expected he will be cleared than to return to action. I have asked our own Dr. Taber to be on hand in Tampa, because I expect we will see some serious injuries from that event. -Most of all our superstars have been doing apperance in Florida for our show. Venny LeTrav is one of them and at a recent radio show, he is quoted as saying, when talking about main eventing two Fiestas in a row... ""It's great on Fiesta, although I do miss Meltdown. I'm ready to see what happens at LDB, which is gonna be one helluva show. Hopefully I can come see it, if not, watching it in my hotel room is just fine with me" -On the subject of Fiesta, expect some changes after Labor Day Brawl. The show will become the showcase for our Mid- and Undercarders. That includes our brightest stars, our rookies. Meltdown will be treated as the flagship it is, so expect our biggest superstars on that show. -In other appearances, Ramsey Kaine took part in a charity in-line skating contest. He came in second, but chose to threaten the winner with being powerbombed off the stage instead of being a graceful loser. I guess, "Arrogance is Bliss". -Our superstars are always being contacted for roles in Hollywood, since they learned how good of actors wrestlers can be. Just look at Moore in Return to the Roadhouse. Moore's own archrival Jackson Kraven is in talks for a TV show spinoff of Suburban Commando. No info yet on if Hulk Hogan will get involved too. -Here's what you've all been waiting for, my predictions for Labor Day Brawl. -Kash Vs Jim Black: A rematch from a few weeks ago, these two could have a good show to get the crowd ready for the pay-per-view. I see Black getting the win here, but both men should look good. -Trapper Vs We The People: I think this may be a throwaway match. It does conclude an angle, but it's not one of our better angles. Hopefully afterward we can find something to really do with Trapper. So much talent, just being wasted. Winner Trapper of course. -Justin Bourne Vs Christyan Payne: I'm really looking forward to this one, it's the sleeper on this card. I hope we let these rookies go 15-20 minutes. Be prepared to sit on the edge of your seat. Bourne will win, but I think it'll be very close. -Wes Sanders Vs Michael Stryfe. My only issue is that Ladder Matches are overdone right now. Hopefully these two men can add some creativity to a match that really needs it. I see Sanders finally get what he deserves. -Rick Venom & Grunge Vs Havoc: This is one of the quester matches on the card, but it could be a classic. The tag team scene needs help and I think this match could be it if all four men try their best. Havoc will retain I believe, mostly because Grunge already is the Cruiserweight Champion. -Damien Thorne Vs Eddie van Dorn: Wow, this no doubt could be the match of the show. I'm upset these great feud may get blown off here, but we're going to get an amazing match no matter what. Thorne will become the champion and Eddie will move on to bigger and better things. -Four Horsemen Vs The 7 Layer: This match serves one purpose, that is reveling Death. As one of the handful who know who this wrestler is, I'm sure he'll get a huge reaction in Tampa. Hopefully we can just get him reveled and move on. Horsemen of course. -Ian Tense Vs Shane Dorion: A heated feud blow-off match. Expect a brutal bout. I think both men want to show they are ready for the main event. I see Ian tense winning the title here and maybe Troy Walcott showing his face. -Tracer Bullet Vs Craven Moore: I'm waiting for this one. The two have never met one-on-one and if their tag match was a preview, than we have one hell of a match coming our way. I think Tracer Bullet has destiny on his side and will prevail. -Jackson Kraven Vs Denrol: This is a great sequel to their March Madness match. Expect them to pick up right where they left off. I'd be shocked if we don't see blood in this bout. I see Jackson Kraven once again beating Denrol. -Jackson Kraven Vs Tracer Bullet: These two havn't faced each other in a while, but there is some serious history here. Except that Tracer has won every match. That is a jinx I see Jackson Kraven overcoming on Sunday. After a great match, Jackson Kraven becomes a four-time World Champion. That's all for now, I'll see you after Labor Day Brawl. From under the New York Yankees cap, enjoy the show.
