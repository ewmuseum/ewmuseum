Saturday November 20, 1999 WORLD WRESTLING ALLIANCE... The Ultimate in Wrestling Entertainment! Most Improved Wrestler or Tag Team Shaman. Shaman has been one of the longest running wrestlers in this fed. He was always on the forefront of the action. But it wasn't until May 9 of this year did Shaman get his first taste of Gold, when he defeated New Star in a brutal war. Alpha Omega. Both members of this team: 'Pretty Boy' Troy Walcott and 'The Crusader' Tommy Lazer were struggling in their young WWA Careers. However when they formed this team, they really took off in their singles career as well. Mantis. Mantis was a household name in the Tag Team ranks. But it wasn't until this year, after joining the House of Pain, did his Singles career take off. He held the TV title twice before getting injured in July. And the winner for the Most Imporoved WWAmmy goes to... Shaman! Shaman : Well thank you for the Awards. I owe this to the fans. They have been with me since day one and as always they are the most important factor in my future and career. It has been this way since the begining and will be this way till the end. Thank you for the honor and with this such award i will maintain the top performances which have always been the epitome of the WWA and all the greats who have come before me and are yet to come. Thank you once again. Best Fued Fraternity vs The Crusade. This fued started May 26 of this year... This fued led to many battles between leaders Mimic And Walcott. The most memorable ones where simply the stable vs stable brawls that ensued almost everytime any member of the opposite stable was scheduled to wrestle. This Fued ended at the Labor Day PPV (September 5). Mimic vs 'Pretty Boy' Troy Walcott. This fued was really fueled by the stables of the Crusade and The Fraternity fueding, in which these two were the leaders. This fued led to 4 matches against each other. These matches were normally clinics on how to wrestle, most of the time very technical. But both men had been know to really throw down and fire a few punches. The Mimic won 2 and Walcott won 2. However, Walcott threw the last blow when he defeated the Mimic at the Labor Day PPV (September 5), in a Mask vs Career match. Tracer Bullet vs Chad Goodman. This fued started up on June 18 of this year. Tracer Bullet, self proclaimed 'Master of Disguise', came out dressed as Chad Goodman making a complete mockery of the young star. Many battles took place in this disagreement. A couple of note would a Steel Cage match, in this the great match was interupted by some outside interference. Another great match was the Bourbon Street Brawl, in which the two men actually fought on Bourbon Street. Tracer came out on top with an assist from the Preacher of Pain. This fued ended Aug 27th with Tracer Bullet finally beating Chad fairly. Overlords of the Dark vs House of Pain (Tag Team). This fued started July 28 of this year. The Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse attacked Logan and the Magician, after they hard fought victory over the two-time tag champs Commandant's Own. This War quickly materialized into an Overlords against the newest Tag team of the House of Pain in Logan and Magician. A number of matches against each other, one of which appeared the House of Pain had beaten the Overlords, but the desicion was overturned by the President in a controversial call. This fued has persisted and is still to this day not over, perhaps at the Birthday Bash?! And the winner of the Best Fued WWAmmy goes to... Tracer Bullet and Chad Goodman. Tracer Bullet and Chad Goodman both stand up at the same time. They make their way to the podium from opposite ends of the stage, then receive their award.
