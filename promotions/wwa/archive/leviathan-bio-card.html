Basic Information Nicknames Real Name | Thomas Connor Height | 6'5 Weight | 315 Date of Birth | 1/10/70 Home Town | Clearwater Florida Theme | Hells Bells by AC/DC More Details Appearance | Thomas Connor is bald. He has green eyes and sometimes wears contact lenses to change them. His physical appearance resembles a fully conditioned Kane. His is a solid built man and unlike most big men he is fully proportioned to his height. Opponents would be awestruck when standing toe-to-toe with Leviathan as he is chiselled to perfection. A virtual Greek God. Ring Attire | His wrestling trunks are long light blue with large white tridents on either side. His boots are white with lightning bolts down the side. He wears a white trench coat to the ring, which has the words “Extreme Best” on the back. Personality | Due to his appearance, Thomas Connor, oozes confidence. He is overly cocky because of his size. History | Thomas Connor grew up in a middle class suburb in Clearwater. Given the most his parents could offer, Thomas was never satisfied as he was always considered a freak or abnormal due to his size. Being larger than any other children in his neighbourhood. He played football and enjoyed the adrenalin associated with throwing his body and hurting the opposition. Academically he never achieved anything like his football status. He could never control his aggression and soon this made him a liability to any team he was on. Getting penalised continually throughout the game for unnecessary roughness. Eventually being thrown out of most games, with some schools boycotting his high school if he played. Not wanted by the football team, shunned by most of his peers, he believed there was only one avenue to take. He then became the poster child for Jail’s are us. As a juvenile he made a home in many detention centres. Most of the time he spent in these centres was due to aggravated assault without provocation. The reason being is that Thomas was larger and stronger than teenagers older then him. This lasted throughout his school years and became Thomas’s profession when he left. Due to his lack of learning, he started work as a bouncer at nightclubs. His over zealous aggression to rowdy patrons landed him in hot water with the local authority. Twice sending drunks into intensive care after spearing them through the windscreen of parked cars. The second assault landed him in jail for five years. While in jail, Thomas started to read about Greek Mythology. Becoming obsessed with learning all about it. Here is where he started calling himself Leviathan and resented anybody using his real name but close friends calling him that. He studied it religiously, believing he was a descendent of Herakles. He also started his rigorous weight-training regime. During his prison holiday he was noticed, by wardens and prisoners alike and with little coercion he enhanced his reputation with victory in the prison’s unofficial UFC style tournaments. With each opponent learning what it is like to step in the ring with the now “Extreme Beast”. He left prison with an undefeated record and a list of corpses in his wake. After Jail, Leviathan could not settle down and drifted across the USA. Working odd jobs but always his temper and aggression landed him in direct confrontation with every local police department. This led to him to either fleeing the area or escaping punishment due to a technicality. Leviathan then saw an advertisement for security for a wrestling promotion, the EWA. An affiliate of the powerful WWA. He enjoyed the ability to be rough while working as security, he was noticed by the owner due to his size and ability to throw would-be disruptors back over the security rail. Before his in ring debut, Leviathan gained a mysterious benefactor, that filled his pockets full of cash. So now he had all he needed money and free reign to bloody anybody that stepped in the ring with him. One house show, Leviathan got into an argument with a local Indy wrestler and ended up putting him in hospital after launching the victim down a flight of stairs. Realising his potential for brutality, the EWA President offered Leviathan a chance to become a member of the roster. Leviathan was hesitant as the President wanted to him to be his hired gun after the invasion of the IPWA. Seeing it as an opportunity to get paid for beating people up and a way to let out his aggression, Leviathan accepted the role with gusto. Learning his technic from one of the President’s chosen few. He debuted and produced a dominant display against the IPWA crew. This also brought him in direct confrontation with the then EWA champ Mac Malloy. The two did not see eye to eye and despised one another. Leviathan did what many people thought was unthinkable decimating the IPWA and punishing the IPWA behemoth and owner, Rick the Brick ( a man mountain in his own right). Leviathan destroyed opponents when in the ring with them, as recipients of the Depth Charge. Just when he was about to enter the main event, the EWA suffered financial trouble and had to shut its doors down indefinitely. Yet Leviathan retained his benefactor and sought a larger audience. The WWA, the supreme wrestling association.
