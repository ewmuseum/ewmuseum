WWA Glory 10.20.08 WWA Glory October 20, 2008 Live from the Sovereign Bank Arena,in Trenton, NJ We start the show out in the parking lot, with Wallace Coleman standing by. He looks to his left as a long black limousine approaches. It pulls up in front of where Wallace is standing. The driver gets out of the car and walks around to open the door. Wallace looks on as Seth Copeland emerges from the limousine holding his sledgehammer and wearing jeans and a black, gold and white Affliction "American Psycho" t-shirt, to a chorus of roaring boos coming from inside the arena, courtesy of the sold out Trenton, New Jersey crowd. His mouth turns up into a smirk and he puts on his sunglasses as Wallace Coleman immediately shoots him a question. Coleman: Seth Copeland. Why are you here at Glory tonight in Trenton, New Jersey? Copeland looks at him and his mouth shapes into an annoyed smirk. Copeland: I'm here because this is the last stop before Day of the Dead and the brutal Halloween House or 'Town' of Pain, as it's being called this year. I'm here to send a message to every single man in that match. I want to give them a preview of what they're all about to fall victim too and take care of some business at the same time. Those who aren't tough enough to withstand my fury don't deserve to be in my presence. Copeland places the head of the sledgehammer into Wallace's sternum and pushes him back several feet.
