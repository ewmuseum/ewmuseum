WWA — 22 Aug 1999

22 Aug 1999

Sunday August 1, 1999 The screen opens up to the WWA banner. Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The camera shows the outside of the complex, revealing it to be the Cummberland County Civic Center. The image of the arena fades, and the scene changes to the backstage area. Various wrestlers, announcers and managers mill about getting ready for their matches. The screen quickly takes the view of Juan, John, and Eric, who are the announcers for Sunday Night Extreme. The three seem pretty happy to be getting some work. Eric : Welcome again to the WWA's Sunday Night Extreme and it's been a while since we've had one of these. Tonight we're coming to you from the Cumberland County Civic Center in Portland Maine. It's a smaller arena, but we're a smaller show than Friday. And we've not done much with the great state of Maine. So let's go see Gary Trudeau and get this card going. Gary Trudeau : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the WWA's Sunday Night Extreme. I'm Gary Trudeau and let me say it's a pleasure to be here in Portland Maine tonight. We've got an action packed extreme card for you tonight, so let's get things moving. Introducing first, "the Superstar" Johny Griggs. The crowd gives the rookie a little reaction as "Heart Shaped Box" By Nirvana begins to play... Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, "Suicide" James Thornhill! The crowd gives a little more positive reaction for Suicide as 'Fuel My Fire' by Prodigy begins. Suicide tries to pump the crowd and it seems to be working, as he makes his energetic way to the ring. Juan : Well this match gets underway and these two promising superstars are going to see who's going to be first into the upper rankings Eric : Well this could be a maker match, and Suicide nails Griggs with a spear and has him down. He's just delivering punches to the man. John : He's going for the quick advantage and this is the way to get it. Eric : But Griggs rolls him over and backs off. He dives on Suicide as he gets up and nails those inside forarms. Juan : Griggs with the irish whip and Suicide goes down to a high crossbody, but manages to kick out easily. Griggs follows up with a springboard backelbow. Eric : Griggs possibly looking to become one of the great hgih fliers in WWA history, and he goes up for a second rope splash. Juan : Suicide gets the knees up and Griggs hits hard. Suicide rolls outside and grabs himself a chair. He's misses his swing. John : Griggs with a jumping back kick and he dents the chair on the head of Thornhill. Thornhill kicks out of the cover at two, and Griggs picks up the chair. Juan : He wait's for Thornhill to get up, and he too misses. The chair bounces off the top rope and hits him in the face. Thornhill capitalizes and grabs the chair. Eric : Thornhill with a legdrop with the chair, and he hooks the leg. Griggs kicks out. John : Griggs is bleeding a little from over his right eye, and Thornhill is gouging it. Juan : Thornhill with a kick to the groin and he climbs the ropes. He does a flip with the chair. Eric : Griggs moves and kicks Thornhill right in the face. Thornhill almost got put back on his feet with that one. Juan : Griggs grabs the chair, but thornhill still has it, and gives it to him, right in the gut. John : Thornhill is the brawler type, he's more in his element here. Eric : Good point, and he just puts Griggs back on the match with the chair. John : He picks him up, and there's the Adrenaline rush, right on to the chair. This match is over. Juan : Good match for a couple of rookies, but I am sure Griggs would have won it if it wasn't extreme. High flying skills beat out brawling any day. Eric : Thornhill is getting a beating for his troubles. Look at Griggs kick him to the mat now that he's back up. John : That's what Thornhill get's for posing after a win. But the fans aren't too happy about this. John : And here comes the Fraternity. I wonder what they've got planned. Juan : Well we've seen them keeping an eye on Griggs at another match, I can't help but wonder if they were scouting him. It makes sense, and the four men circle him. He'd better hope it's a good thing and not the Frat upset about his attack on Thornhill. John : Here it comes, the moment of truth. Juan : And the Fraternity all rush Griggs. It's a giant bearhug ceremony.... "Superstar" Johnny Griggs : Thornhill you not only got a beating by the best wrestler in the rookie bracket but you also have seen the newest member of the FRAT! And now that I'm a Frat brother I'll be able to showcase my skill with the best wrestlers and stable the WWA has to offer! And as a Fratbrother my first order of duty is to talk to Viper... Viper you and I more myself than you are the best Rookies in the WWA so I'm giving you a challenge Wish and SEAL have left the rookie rankings so Viper how's about you and I have a match next FRIDAY NIGHT FIGHTS for the Rookie Ring! And better yet I'll add a stipulation if your man enough to step into a no holds barred match with myself... Now Viper I hope you accept this challenge because damnit you're going to make me famous and that's because I'm the next Rookie Ring Champion! Now one last thing I'm the ONE.... the ONLY... SUPERSTAR! And I'm here to help the Frat be the best stable the WWA has ever seen even though it already is! He then passes the mic to Goodman... Goodman : Yeah. I got some words for one Tracer Bullet. ( mixed crowd reaction ) yeah, well, Tracer, I know you're quite the ambitious fellow there. Always going for the gold, and the word on the street is that you've weaseled your way into a cushy little PPV spot for the US title.... Well, I don't like that.. Not one bit... You see, me and you... We still have unfinished business. Tracer Bullet, you crotch goblin, if you think you can get one cheap win over me and then duck me, well think again. I still want you in this ring, and I still want you in this ring on Labor Day!! ( crowd pop ) So here's what I'm proposing.... This Friday... a little show we call Friday Night Fights here in the WWA... Me and you for the number one contendorship spot. ( crowd pops ) And after I beat you.. I will claim your spot in that three way.. buuuuuut, I'll abandon it. There are things in life that are more important then gold. One of those things happens to be the joy I'm gonna get when I beat you at the PPV. 2 out of 3 falls. And the world will finally see you exposed. Tracer Bullet. You can't duck me forever, sucka. The fraternity leaves the ring to a mixed reaction, with their new member Johnny Griggs. Eric : The Fraternity with a new member, and Johnny Griggs looks to have a good future here. I'd put him against Thornhill in a non extreme match any day. But of course, sometimes the dice don't fall your way. John : And Goodman has some big words for Tracer Bullet, we'll just have to wait and see if he accepts. He'd be stupid to put up his title shot though. Juan : Goodman has a right to be upset with him. I don't think we've seen the end of this, though I'm sure some people would say otherwise. But Suicide James Thornhill still has the win here tonight on Extreme over Johnny Griggs, and I'm sure we'll see a lot from the two of them in the future. Let's see the next match between Suniel Sanhga and the Mountie. commercial break Gary Trudeau : First introducing in a ten minute bout ...First hailing from the great northern country Canada is The Mountie... Camera focuses on the ramp way as the crowd seems to not respond as well as The Mountie has expected as he goes down and still slaps a few fans hands on the way to the ring.... Gary Trudeau : And his Opponent weighing in at 310 lbs is Suneil Sangha... Voodoo Child by Jimi Hendrix blasts over the PA as Suneil Sangha steps out and poses on top of the ramp way. Red and white spot lights flash all over him and the arena. Then he begins making his way to the ring high fiving fans on the way and steps over the top rope with ease... Juan : Well theres the lock up Sangha with the easy break and power out. John : Sangha has the size over Mountie. Eric : Mountie seems to be shuffling outside of the ring. Juan : Sangha is looking at the ref pointing at Mountie. John : Mountie is throwing chairs into the ring. Eric : Mountie in and slamming fists into Sangha's kidneys. Juan : Mountie with a quick single arm DDT on Sangha. John : Mountie with quick control of the match. Juan : Mountie sends Sangha to the ropes drop toe hold take down. John : That had to hurt Sangha landed on a chair face first. Eric : Yep that will leave a mark. Juan : Mountie helps Sangha up and sends him over the ropes and quickly follows. John : Sangha into the post and Mountie sends himback in the ring. Juan : Sangha climbs to his feet. John : Mountie pointing at fans and slapping hands then heads into the ring. Eric : Sangha has had enough as Mountie slides into the ring and drops a knee to the lower back. Juan : Mountie felt that as Sangha helps him to his feet and quick shoulder to the back. John : Sangha strutting around Mountie and a elbow drop to the back. Eric : Sangha focusing on Mounties lower back. Juan : Mountie with a roll over and a few quick punches to the midsection. John : Sangha has enough of that quick kick to the head and grabs a chair. Eric : Sangha slams the chair into the back of Mountie. Juan : Sangha is setting chairs up. John : Guerrilla press and Mountie drops on the chairs. Eric : Sangha up again and setting up a chair again. Juan : Sangha helps Mountie up and a Russian Leg Sweep down on a chair. John : Sangha has Mountie in The Canadian Death Lock. Eric : Mountie gives up and Sangha with the win. Juan : Sangha is standing there gloating and posing. commercial break Gary Trudeau : Our first opponent weighing in at 256 lbs and representing The Fraternity is Blake Daniels.... Camera zooms up the rampway as Blake Daniels makes his way to the ring at the crowds mixed reactions. "Cumbersome" by Seven Mary Three blasts over the PA system... Gary Trudeau : And his opponent representing The House of Pain weighing in at 234 lbs is Tracer Bullet... Camera zooms around the crowd as Tracer Bullet appears from the corner near the rampway. He makes his way to the ring as "Jeremy" by Pearl Jam blares over the PA as the crowd Chants HoP HoP HoP.... Tracer has a mic... Tracer Bullet : Look here, Blake, YOU saw the match that took place last Friday, didn't you? You saw the kind of punishment all eight of the participants took, and, well... it's taking a little longer than I expected to recuperate from that. So what I'm saying is that I'm not really up to fighting you... not tonight. Maybe some other night, though. Besides, I don't really have any personal vendetta against you anyway, so it wouldn't be worth risking a further injury. Blake nods and starts to leave the ring when Bullet clubs him over the head with the microphone. The feedback resounds throughout the arena. Tracer : Gullible bastard... you're in the Frat, that's enough reason for me to choke the life out of you. Ring the damn bell! Ding Ding Eric : Bullet choking Daniels on the bottom rope. Juan : Tracer helps Daniels up and slams him quickly. John : The cover 1..2 no Daniels kicks out. Eric : Bullet should have known that was to quick. John : Daniels with a quick clothesline but misses. Eric : Bullet wraps the trench coat around Daniels neck. Juan : Daniels with a chin buster. John : Nice counter for a choke like that. Eric : Daniels inwrapping the coat from his throat. Juan : Daniels with a quick suplex. John : Bullet witha quick small package. Eric : 1..2.. no Daniels kicks out. Juan : Daniels to his feet only to be clotheslined by Bullet. John : Guillitine legdrop y Bullet on Daniels head. Eric : Bullet motions for the mic as Gary Trudeau tosses it to him. Juan : Bullet goes to say something then slams it down hard into Daniels head. John : Bullet rolls outside the ring and grabs a table. Eric : Bullet setting the table up. Juan : Bullet back into the ring sets up Daniels on the top rope. John : Bullet with a quick hurricurana outside onto the table. Eric : That was suicidal Juan : It was great. John : Bullet gets up and grabs Daniels and rolls him into the ring. Eric : Bullet with a quick snapmare. Juan : hey isn't that Goodman making his way to the ring. John : he;s trying to get Bullets attention. Eric : Bullet still focusing on Daniels. Juan : Bullet with a blatant choke on Daniels. John : Bullet has taken complete control of this match. Eric : Goodman still trying to get Bullets attention to no avail. Juan : Bullet dragging the hurting Daniels to the corner. John : Could it be yes The Interrogation. Eric : Daniels giving up quickly . Juan : Bullet pulls back harder not breaking the hold. John : Goodman in the ring now charging the corner. Eric : Bullet jumps down and jumps the gaurd rail and shakes his rearside to Goodman sticking out his tongue and disappears by a sign that says Fear HoP.. Juan : Bullet with the win and Blake Daniels has got his first taste of HoP.. John : Now I remember why I like that guy. He's cool. But we've got another commercial and the first real extreme match of the night. commercial break Juan : What's this? Three guys just wheeled a big dumpster out here... Eric : I guess we'll find out in a minute! Gary Trudeau : Our next match is a no holds barred, falls count anywhere match that has a thirty minute time limit. Introducing first, from Hartford, Connecticut, and weighing in at 242 pounds...representing the Fraternity... "PRETTY BOY" TROY WALCOTT!!! "If You Think I'm Jiggy" by the LOX starts up as the fans begin to cheer a bit. Walcott steps out and makes his way to the ring, in his usual ring attire. He waits in the ring for his opponent. Gary Trudeau : And his opponent, from the Navajo Desert, and weighing in at 310 pounds...SHAMAN!!! The lights go out and on the JumboTron appears the word "FEAR" as the crowd chants "IS NEVER BORING." Shaman walks out, wearing blue jeans, a t-shirt, and combat boots with his hands taped up like a boxer's hands. But suddenly he turns and walks back to the locker rooms. John : Where the hell'd he go? Eric : I have no idea! As "Super Charger Heaven" by White Zombie blasts over the P.A. system, white pyrotechnics start lighting their way to the ring in two lines on the sides of the aisle. Suddenly, a thick fog rolls up from under the ring as Walcott looks completely confused. Juan : THERE'S SHAMAN! HE WAS UNDER THE RING! Eric : Shaman jumps up and starts hammering away on Walcott. John : Shaman is beating the hell out of Walcott. Clothesline over the ropes! Eric : Shaman follows him out and rams him into the dumpser head first! Juan :Shaman reaches in and brings out a CAR BUMPER! HE SWINGS AT WALCOTT! John : Walcott ducks and grabs the bumper. He clocks Shaman with the bumper! Juan :Walcott on the offense now and he smashes him with a chair! They're making their way to the back. John : Walcott rams Shaman head first into the steel ramp on their way to the loading bay! Eric : Look, there's a big rig truck. Walcott tries to whip Shaman into the side, but Shaman reverses it! Shaman kicks Walcott in the gut. Juan :POWERBOMB ON THE CONCRETE! John : What's he doing? Shaman hauls Walcott to the roof of the cab and sets him up for a powerbomb! Eric : NO! PILEDRIVER RIGHT OVER THE ENGINE! John : Shaman covers him. One, two, thr- NO! KICKOUT! Juan :Shaman is pissed! He pulls Walcott up and launches him off of the hood of the cab and down about six feet to the concrete! Eric : Shaman follows up with an axe-handle smash on Walcott. Where are they going now? John : They're outside! There's traffic! Juan :This doesn't look good for Walcott! Eric : Walcott is fighting back! He's trading punches with Shaman! John : Walcott stuns the big man with three well placed right hands and then hits a dropkick, sending him back first into a lamp post! Juan :Looks like Walcott is going to follow it up with a, YES, FACEBUSTER! He hooks the leg! One...two...thre- NO! SHOULDER UP BY SHAMAN! John : Walcott pulls Shaman to his feet and smashes him face first into the lamp post again. Juan :He runs him over to a highway barricade, with cars whipping past no more than eight feet away! Eric : Walcott smashes Shaman face first on the barricade. NO! Shaman reversed it! Walcott goes reeling! John : Shaman punches Walcott and drags him back to the parking lot. Juan :There's a nice inverted atomic drop to stun the man! Eric : Shaman pulls Walcott over to a parked car...a Yugo? Juan :Shaman lifts Walcott onto the hood ONCE AGAIN! John : He joins him up there...another piledriver? POWERBOMB RIGHT THROUGH THE WINDSHIELD! THAT'S GOT TO BE IT! Eric : Shaman covers. One...Two...THREE! Shaman wins the match! John : What a match. I don't know if Spike and the Preacher can compete with this. Eric : I'm sure the Preacher can, but we'll just see about Spike. commercial break Camera is in the locker room. Shaman is seen walking back after his match with Troy Walcott. He's showing signs of his match. He walks up to Chad Goodman. Goodman : Good match out there, Shaman. You gave it your all, bud. Goodman extends his hand. Shaman takes him down with a haymaker punch and Blake Daniels and Tommy Lazer attack Shaman from behind and begin to pound on him. Shaman throws them off with a burst of strength and turns around to nail Blake with a low blow, followed by a rabbit punch to the nose of Lazer. Goodman delivers a shot to the back of Shaman's head with a chair, and Shaman goes down as the cameras cut to ringside. Gary Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen, this next bout is scheduled for the WWA Extreme Title. Introducing first... Hailing from right down the road in East Rutherford he is Spike! "Loser" by Beck starts over the PA. Spike enters the studio and the fans give him a great pop as he walks to the ring slapping hands. Spike goes through a variety of poses much to the fans approval. Gary Trudeau : And now your WWA Extreme Champion! Hailing from Atlanta, Georgia he is the Extreme Being...The Preacher of Paaiin! The lights dim down and the fans begin to applaud already as The Preacher of Pain, slowly, with a cocky expression crossed on his face comes out from the back curtain. A few tinkerings of notes are heard, from what sounds to be an organ. The Preacher looks into the crowd with an embraced feeling of arrogance. His eyes and face show no emotion, as if the people in the stands mean absolutely nothing to him. From behind the Preacher emerges a smaller figure, wearing a brown robe. The robe drapes over his head nicely. The Preacher's head tilts from one side of the arena to the next, as the guitar chords of "No Quarter" begins to be played. Slowly walking to the ring, The Preacher motions for the microphone. As the mic is given to him, the music is cut. The Preacher starring up at Spike, begins to talk to a chorus of boo's from the crowd. P.o.P : Spike, we've all waited a very long time for this match. You wouldn't believe the anticipation I felt, as I heard your theme music "Loser" by Beck being played. This is a match WWA wrestlers dream about...and when I say that, I mean...a match with The Preacher of Pain. I'm sure some of the WWA's finest are watching this backstage...this is going to be a real specimen of a match. Forget about all the others who have came before us...all who claim greatness. Spike is one of the best wrestlers here... The crowd begins to laugh, and boo. The Preacher looks at the crowd, and quickly disregards them by not listening to their reaction. P.o.P : Lets face it, the talent here in the WWA...very lackluster to say the MOST. And you, brain-dead...SINNERS watching this...lackluster performance need to get a purpose...a calling in life, and you need to get one very quickly. The Preacher pushes the mic away from his lips. And this time, seems to enjoy the boo's from the crowd. A popcorn container is flung at The Preacher, and popcorn spills all over the aisle. The Preacher begins again.... P.o.P : This planet is doomed. Filled of filth and disgust. Morals lacking within every soul. Let me tell you something my friends...your time is just about up. Its time to get off your carriage and do something with yourselves. Instead of watching grown men, battle it out...soon your own battles will come...Oh yes! Watch Spike closely as he falls into the fate of me, The Preacher of Pain, The Extreme Being. For your fate is NEXT! The Preacher whips the mic into the guardrail, creating a negative pop and stir into the pa system. Eric : Spike running to the far rope as fast as he can...oh! He comes down on The Preacher with a missile drop kick. Juan : Like a highway accident these two wrestlers are sprawled along the aisle here. John : I think Spike really wants to shut The Preacher up here... Eric : By their reactions...I think the fans do too John. Spike is the first to his feet. Spike seems to be just as dazed as The Preacher though, who is also rising to his feet... John : Spike nails The Preacher with a kick to the midsection. Oh, and down with a DDT. Juan : The Preacher doesn't seem very focused in this match, thats for sure... Eric : Well, he is fighting Spike here. Preacher trying to roll away from Spike. The Preacher grabbing underneath the ring...The Preacher with a broom... John : Spike is to his feet with a fury...ooh..The Preacher with 1...2...3...4 damaging blows to Spikes head. Juan : Spike is definitely in a daze now. Looks like The Preacher is taking this one alittle more seriously...as Spike took the brief advantage early on. The Preacher to his feet now, as he cracks that broom stick over Spikes head one more time... Eric : That thing just snapped in half. The Preacher disregards half of it, and now using it as a choking device against Spike. John : Its always good to see Spike out here at Extreme... Juan : Good to see him getting choked half to death? John : Spike with a backwards elbow to The Preacher, and another...Spike breaking free. Oh! The Preacher of Pain ends that escape with a devastating clothesline. Eric : Spike is once again down. Jaun: He should probably just stay there.... John : The Preacher utilizing this time nicely, by pulling out and now assembling a table near ringside. Eric : Spike really doesn't give enough credit though...you have to admit he is tougher then many... Juan : Yeah tougher then a male nurse...maybe... John : Look at this loyal fan giving his own chair for Spike. The Preacher with his back turned...CRACK! With the chair! Eric : The Preacher stumbling around the table trying to get his balance here, he's not finding it very well. Juan : A good majority of the fans here, seem to enjoy seeing The Preacher take a hit from that chair. Spike nailing him ONCE again, taking The Preacher straight down to the concrete.
