Jimmy : He hit it. Rocky hit it. Both men stay down but ten seconds or so later Rocky moves over and makes the cover. One … Two … Thre … Jimmy : There's Quietus … didn't somebody get rid of him! Officials now chase off Quietus who gets a few kicks into Rocky before being forced away. Quietus : For Devils Own, Muppet! Jimmy : Adams had this won until Quietus appeared. Adams still looks like he's going to be first to get to his feet when all of a sudden a running Quietus reappears and takes him down with a big clothesline. Jimmy : Get him out of here. As officials struggle to deal with Quietus, Muppet is getting back to his feet and as he sees Rocky doing the same he runs at him and hits his three quarter nelson bulldog. Jimmy : Welcome to Muppet Land! He drops into the cover but there are no referee's. Jimmy : Rocky has taken so much. Suddenly one referee sees the cover being made, he's about one hundred metres away as Quietus has been pushed back that far. It takes fifteen seconds, but the count is made. One … Two … Thre … Jimmy : How in the hell did he get his shoulder up? Muppet doesn't react to this, he's had so much taken out of him he can't. He waits near enough half a minute before bringing himself and Rocky to his feet. He takes him over to the Bourbon Street Bank. He sets him up for a powerbomb against the stairs of the bank after walking him up about twelve stairs. Jimmy : Don't do this! He has him up for a powerbomb, but somehow Rocky rotates in mid-air and gets Muppet in a three quarter nelson. He then delivers the Welcome To Muppet Land off the stairs all the way to the floor. Jimmy : … Both men don't move. Eventually, Rocky drapes an arm over the lifeless Muppet Man. One … Two … Three! Muppet Man is eliminated! Rocky Adams wins the Bourbon Street Brawl. Neither man moves as the referee lifts up the arm of Rocky Adams. EMT's run out for both men, and for about a minute we don't know what's going on. Jimmy : These two men have really taken themselves to a new level here tonight, and credit where credit is due, Muppet Man took an absolute pasting but he kept going and even without his Devils Own comrades showed what a powerful force he will be for years to come. Fat Tony : I can't believe one of those SCWF guys won! Jimmy : I'm a little shocked, but the performance that Rocky Adams put forward tonight … well he may be a little cocky but he earned this victory and he deserves the title shot that will come his way. Fat Tony : I just hope these guys can walk away and see their careers rise, tonight they may have enforced a peak on their careers! Rocky is getting up now on his own as fans gather around. There's a rather large contingent of people now and one of them throws the megaphone that Rocky came to the Brawl with. He picks up the megaphone and looks set to say something in it but instead wipes the blood of Quietus off his face and a tear from his eye and just raises his hand in a moment of pure pride. Jimmy : What a sight, to see a man humbled not just by the reception he receives, but the knowledge that he did it. He did the impossible. Rocky Adams is your 2004 Bourbon Street Brawl Winner!
