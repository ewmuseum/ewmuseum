Fault Line - February 9, 2005

9 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A preview and card for the Meltdown special Fault Line on Monday 9th February 2005 live from Los Angeles, headed by a two-out-of-three-falls World Championship match.
Fault Line Live from Staples Arena Los Angeles, California Monday, 9th February 2005 WWA FAULT LINE Last year, it was ‘Total Meltdown’ which set the tone leading in to Rage In The Cage. This year, Fault Line promises to set the bar at an all new level as we begin the countdown to Rage! In just one action-packed night scores will be settled and new battle lines drawn as the WWA prepares for one of their biggest PPVs of the year. Mal Somers and Tracer Bullet will tear the house down one last time – and this time, if Mal wins Tracer has promised to reopen Mal’s case. Everything rests on Mal winning – if he does, perhaps Tracer Bullet can discover the real truth behind Mal’s parent’s deaths. Also, Shaman aims to settle the score once and for all with recent Devil’s Own turncoat Matt Warr. Will the WWA veteran show the WWA President who the real boss is? Hell Itself turned on his mentor Khaled last month choosing to align himself with Matt Warr rather than his friend. At Fault Line, Khaled has promised to return and repay the favour owed to Hell Itself! Also at Fault Line, Joey Edwards must prove himself against the silent rookie…no, not the belt – Silence! And Brian Calvin faces one of his toughest challenges as US Champion to date – by facing both Grunge and recent X Corp dropout Chance. Can Calvin hold on to his belt and remain joint US/IC Champion? The Embrace The Power Tournament has had both fans and superstars captivated in recent months. With the end finally in sight, who can walk away as victor? And what exactly will their prize be? Craven Moore looks to not only settle his differences with Dave Harley but also claim another World Championship victory. In a unique two out of three falls match, both men have chosen a stipulation of their own…but the final stipulation (if needed) has been left up to the fans! Visit the WWA website to register your vote…it could very well decide who walks in to Rage In The Cage as WWA World Champion!
