What this is Roster bio card for the WWA wrestler Kahn, giving measurements, hometown, birth date, style, ring attire, entrance music, appearance, history, finishing, signature and trademark moves, and personality.
Name: Kahn Real Name: Riley O'Neil Height: 5'10" Weight: 253 From: Paterson, New Jersey Birth Date: November 15, 1978 Age: 22 Style: He's a powerhouse/technician against most people. The giants of the world who outweigh him by a good 100lbs or so get to see his martial arts skills however. His main style though is to get his ass kicked and simply outlast his opponent...hell, he's Irish. Ring Attire: He wrestlers shirtless. White pants with green flames around the bottoms of his legs is his only attire. A large 4-leaf clover centers the flames on each pant leg, and a small Ireland flag is on each side of his hips. Entrance Music: "Part of Me" by TOOL Physical Appearance: He resembles Tazz a lot, both in height and physical mass; although he is larger in build. Kahn's head is shaved and he has a black goatee that covers only his chin. His upper body is huge, and also covered in slicing scars. His back shows more scars, and on his right shoulder is an evil looking Leprechaun tattoo. Just from looking at him you can see the Irish in him and you can see the faint resemblance to a Leprechaun in his face. Oh yeah....he drinks a lot and smokes some of the finest greenery on the market. History: There is little history of this young man. He was born in Ireland, and moved to the states when he was 10. He's wrestled in small indy feds throughout the country but has never found a place to make his home. Hopefully this will place will be able to survive him. Finishing Move: "Sobered" (It's a TKO style maneuver (ala Marc Mero), but instead of leaning back and dropping his foe to the mat after spinning him, Kahn drops his foe's head and neck down across his own (Kahn's) shoulder. So it ends up like a Stunner, but the added force from the spin and the extra height add all the pain in the world....for those that aren't knocked out.) Signature Moves: 1. Dragon Suplex 2. Stalling Brainbuster 3. Spinning Death Valley Driver 4. Hangman's Neckbreaker 5. Head & Arm Suplex (Taz Plex) 6. Release Cradle Suplex 7. Gargolye Suplex 8. Double Underhook Back Breaker 9. Over-the-head Release German Suplex 10. Double Arm DDT Trademark Moves: 1. "Clutch Slam" - Single Hooked Leg Choke Slam 2. "Backdrop DDT" - Backdrop Bomb with foe's head under Kahn's arm to put the force of the move on the neck 3. "Drink Up" - Canadian Backbreaker dropped into a Dominator Slam Personality: He's a fun loving guy. Of course that's only when he isn't drunk or stoned, which is about 90% of the time. He has made it a custom to wrestle under one of the above circumstances, and often times even both. They make it so he feels little to no pain even when he is all but dying. Other then that he's cocky and arrogant as someone with skills should be. He's also frequently called the "Crazy Little Leprechaun", as those who mock him tend to find out why.
