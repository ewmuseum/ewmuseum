The Scouting Report - Issue 21

24 Sep 2003

Bylined Josh Wilson - Director of Talent, in the fed's own kayfabe voice.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings and salutations WWA fans, it’s your esteemed and valued Director of Talent back with all the dirt and opinions you can handle with a new Scouting Report. -Well we just had a new Meltdown, the first one after Revolution. Man was it a show. -We only have a few weeks till are next pay-per view Independence Day Rumble. As such we’ve been rushing to give the fans the best ppv buildup that’s possible. -WWA owner Mark Harrington made his successful return to Meltdown this Monday much like the prodigal son returning to home. Mr. Harrington claimed to have some new ideas that would revolutionize the WWA product. -But as of now that is not to be as President Matt Warr refused to sign the contract to put these changes into affect. After a temper tantrum by the boss Harrington vowed to get the contract signed when Mr. Warr least expects it. Well as long as I get to keep my job I’m happy. -As far as changes the staff and I have been kicking around some ideas for the EWA and WWA roster. We’ve had some more wrestlers join the roster and this is just the beginning. Stay tuned for more info. -Speaking of changes in the staff Troy Steffy has rejoined the Fiesta team. The former head booker has become Commissioner Raine’s new assistant and is already driving him up the walls. Time to cue the Odd Couple theme. -Next on Meltdown Prometheus, Reverend Tweak, and Fuel fought for the number one contenders spot for the GenX Title currently held by Myzery. I’ve often said that Tweak is going places and this is just more proof of the facts. -X-Corp premiered their new not so late show on Meltdown this week with Chance as their special guest. Those boys are crazier than a pet coon. And just because JR said that doesn’t mean he’s the only guy from Oklahoma. Nick Adams is going to be the guest next week on the show. -Next up on Meltdown Venny LeTrav, Lockdown, and Jimmi Zion locked up in a high impact affair. Zion ended up the winning this time but Lockdown proved that he’s a psycho by beating the crap put of both men afterwards. -Of course the Devil’s own wasn’t down yet. Leviathan issued a very violent challenge to Grunge for the United States Title with Mal Somers looking on. Of course if I know one thing about Grunge he is not going to take this lying down. -And to make matters worse Levi managed to show that he is the green eyed monster by beating Mal Somers in the Sweet Sixteen Match with a score of 19 to 11. It looked like Mal had the match in the bag when Imperceptus came to the ring and clobbered Mal. You know that Youth United isn’t going to take that defection lightly. -The tag-team division is in turmoil with Havoc’s retirement on Monday. They didn’t say why but we now know the belts are suspended. So what’s going to happen there now? -Next Enigma managed to beat out Grunge, Nick Adams, and Dave Harley in a fatal fourway match. It seemed that the leader of the Forsaken Souls was doomed as the other three men seemed to only be interested in beating Enigma. Still the dark one managed to sneak a win. -But the big news came after as Grunge offered a spot in Youth United to his former enemy. Nick accepted and they almost blew the roof off with the reaction. -Of course that wasn’t the only addition to stables that night as the returning Wes Sanders joined Enigma’s crew after a beat down on Chance. Could a confrontation with Ian Tense’s boys be not far down the line? -And Tracer Bullet made the announcement of the Bourbon Street Brawl for the week before the ppv. He extended twenty invites minus Grunge, Kash, and himself. Namely because Tracer and Grunge are guaranteed a title shot and Kash is the champion. -He also extended a invite to EWA member Chuckster and a unnamed man not on the roster. Who could it be? -But what about next week’s Meltdown? -In the first match of the night Myzery will be facing Joey K for the right to were the TV Title. Still with Joey’s membership in the Forsaken Souls Myzery will be facing almost four men instead of one. The odds don’t look to be in his favor. -After a confrontation last week Reverend Tweak will be facing Enigma. Enigma is the veteran and is not one to fight fair but Tweak has the faith of ice-cream and birds on his side. Not only that but Tweak said Enigma would be sorry. Still one has to wonder who will be the sorry one. -In a high impact affair the Cell will be facing the new allies Nick Adams and Grunge. The Cell have been working together for a long time but Grunge and Adams compliment each other well. Still will Grunge and Levi be blinded by their hatred with each other to win the match? -And in the main event new stable mates Wes Sanders and Se7en will be facing off against Chance and Mal Somers representing X-Corp. Sanders is a former number one contender for the World Title and Se7en is a powerhouse but Chance and Somers are no slouches either. Well until next time this is Josh Wilson. Be sure to watch Meltdown and Fiesta. You won’t be disappointed. Ciao.
