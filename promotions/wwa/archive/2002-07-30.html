Several EMTs rush down to see to Ian Tense. It takes four of them to moves the huge speaker off his arm. Jimmy: I don’t think we’ll be seeing Ian Tense in action anytime soon! He’s going to need serious medical help. Just as the ring is cleared, the house lights all fade to black. An ominous drum beat, similar to a heartbeat pounds on the area sound system. Fat Tony: Don't be frightened Jimmy! Jimmy: Don't be...? YOU'RE the one under the table! Fat Tony: I dropped my pen. Jimmy: What's going on? The crowd murmurs in the dark. Some inventive folks in the crowd begin holding up Bic lighters. Jimmy: This is creepy... The drumbeat is now accompanied with a low, eerie pipe organ tone. Suddenly, words appear on the WWATron, in a font that looks like it is being scrathed on the wall with a knife. Jimmy: What does it say? I can't quite... oh no. Oh no indeed. Scratched in white on a black background is one word: DEATH. Fat Tony: Uh oh, there goes my pen again. Jimmy: Is this it? Do we finally find who Death is? The scratching continues. Now a circle is drawn around the word. And then a diagonal line crosses DEATH out. Jimmy: What? And the drum and organ screech to a halt. The music is replaced by "I am a Japanese Sandman" by the Cellos. The house lights come back on, and a single man dressed in black stands on the entrance ramp. Fat Tony: can it be? Jimmy: It can't be? The man wears black cargo pants and boots. He wears a black T-shirt with white letters reading "THANK YOU." He carries a broom handle wrapped in black electrician's tape. He wears black sunglasses, with silver frames just to mess up the colour scheme. Jimmy: It's Jim Black! Fat Tony: JIM BLACK!! JIM BLACK!!! JIM BLACK!!! The music dies down, Jim Black gives the mildly stunned crowd a wave, and lifts a microphone to his lips. Fat Tony: Bouncing with glee Jim Black Jim Black Jim Black Jim Black Jim Black Jim Black Jim Black Jim Black Jim Black! Jimmy: Would you keep qui... Fat Tony: Shhh!! He's gonna talk! Jim Black opens his mouth and is about to speak. then he lowers the mic and the house lights again fade to black. Jimmy: What the...? The house lights come back up. Jim Black is gone. the crowd is still buzzing. Most of them are annoyed by the interruption. Fat Tony: Oh goody goody goody! Christmas came early for the WWA! Jim Black is back! Jimmy: But what does that mean? We'll be right back... Commercial Break Jimmy: Folks, weclome back, and If you blinked you may have missed it, but i think That Magnificent Bastard jim Black, has come back to the WWA! Fat Tony: The former WWA Champion, leader of the FWA invasion, And general all around nice guy to boot! Jimmy: Nice guy? Whatever.... Fat Tony: Hey, show the man some respect... He could kick your ass..... Jimmy: Indeed he could, but I have just one question... what does this mean for the WWA? Fat Tony: Who knows! He killed the New HOP, beat the undefeatable Nik Seiger, and still looked cool in the process! Jimmy: Well I guess we'll find out on insider, but until then were gonna have to wait! Fat Tony: I'm sure we won't be waiting long.... Jimmy: But whats to wait for when we have Tracer against Denrol for the Cruiserweight title.. right now! We now cut to a panning shot of the arena, taking in some of the fans in to the arena tonight. A big fat bearded guy in a Hawaiian shirt and big, goony Christian-style sunglasses holds a sign that says "WARR FEARS BIG DADDY G". A skinny guy in a "WWA UK Tour '02" t-shirt holds up a sign saying "MAKE THE COMMONWEALTH GAMES A HARDCORE MATCH!" Someone is even dressed up in a giant chicken suit, holding a sign that says "EVEN I COULD KICK HARLEY TRAPPER'S ASS!" As we cut back to the commentator's desk, Fat Tony is chuckling. Fat Tony: Remember back when Denrol was good? Remember the halcyon days of the "chicken suit" match? Jimmy: All too well, I'm afraid. I'll bet that Denrol… "YOUR MOUTH IS WRITING CHECKS YOUR ASS CAN'T CASH!!!" "Full Nelson" by Limp Bizkit cuts Jimmy off as red and gold pyrotechnics shoot off into the air from the stage. Denrol steps out of the entrance to a giant-size pop. He is dressed in a classic sky blue Ric Flair-style robe with "Pure Greatness" in gold on the back. He struts across the stage and than bows at the crowd. Jimmy: We got quite a match coming up here. With the announcement that Moore will battle both Denrol and Shane Dorion next week for the WWA World Title, we could be looking at a preview of Labor Day Brawl. Fat Tony: Have you listen to Denrol's promos? He's already acting like he's the champion. Jimmy: Indeed he does. I think he forgets that Commissioner Cruz signed up a WWA World Championship match on Fiesta for whoever wins that match against Jackson Kraven! Fat Tony: And I think we all know what Kraven thinks of Denrol. Denrol steps into the ring and removes his robe, revealing black Angle-style tights with red trim and random gold triangle designs on the side. He climbs each of the turnbuckles, giving the fans a chance to get their pictures in. Denrol than waits in the middle of the ring for Tracer. Some sleazy sax music, typical to the streets of the Big Easy, plays over the PA system as a picture of a handgun slowly fades into view on the WWATron. The hammer on the gun is pulled back just before the sax holds a long, high note. While this note is being held, a voice-over is heard over the PA. Voice-over: I'm a private eye... The high note ends abruptly after this statement, and the gun on the WWATron fires at the exact same time that an explosion occurs on the stage. The sax music is then overtaken by Pearl Jam's "Jeremy." At home... Drawing pictures... Of mountaintops... With him on top... Lemon yellow sun... Arms raised in a V... And the dead lay... In pools of maroon below... The entire arena goes dark, save for four spotlights that hit the ramp at strategic points to make them look like a series of streetlights. The whole place gives off an eerily black and white feel. The bullet that came out of the gun on the big screen is now seen zipping through various highlights of Bullet's career, almost as if we're watching a personalized version of KoRn's "Freak on a Leash" music video. These highlights include three different World Title victories, dropping the private eye gimmick, and then picking it back up again. Reclaiming the gimmick is the scene that is playing just as Tracer himself appears at the top of the ramp in full detective gear. Tracer walks slowly down the ramp, a big grin on his face. As he hits each of the four spotlights on the ramp, they turn blue. Finally, he makes it to the ring and stands directly in the centre of it. He reaches into his trench coat and draws a pistol, then aims it straight up into the air. As Tracer fires a blank round, white fountain pyrotechnics fire off from each of the four ringposts. As soon as the pyro dies down, the lights return to normal. Tracer then takes off his trench coat, revealing the WWA Cruiserweight Title, which is removed also. He hands these two items, his shoulder holster, and his hat to a ring attendant and waits for the bell to ring. Jimmy: Looks like we're ready to begin... Suddenly a female voice yells over the PA system. Voice: Don't ring that bell! This match isn't legal! "Of Wolf And Man" by Metallica plays as Alexandra Korth, wife of Denrol, steps out with a microphone. The fan reaction to the raven-haired woman is mixed. Some are cheering her hot navy blue business suit with short skirt. Some are pissed she is stopping the Main Event. Either way, she ignores the crowd as she glares at the two men in the ring. Alexandra Korth: Gordon, I warned you before. But as you always do, you didn't listen to me. Well now you're going to pay. It seems when you decided you'd rather wrestle than stay with me, you forgot that I still am your manager! Jimmy: What?!? Alexandra Korth: That means only I can sign contracts for matches for you, and that means this match isn't legal! The crowd boos this announcement. Denrol is yelling something at Alexandra, but it can't be made out. Alexandra: I wouldn't want to disappoint your fans however, so I have signed a replacement! Ladies and gentlemen, give a big hand… for Father Denrol! The lights go out. After a moment, the WWATron comes alive with dark clouds rushing over a full moon. Father Denrol's voice speaks over the PA. Father Denrol: Tracer Bullet… long have I watched you. I, unlike so many here today, remember your time in the House of Pain. I remember the evil that was HOP, or as I called it, the Army of Darkness. You may be the only remaining member of that army, but that just means you must be the last to be destroyed. Prepare to pay for your sins Tracer, and prepare to meet your creator! Evil laughter fills the arena as a pyro "lightning bolt" shoots out and hits the entrance, igniting a huge fireball. The arena lights suddenly come back on and standing in the ring behind Denrol and Tracer Bullet is Father Denrol, looks just as we last saw him: bald with a red cross tattooed on his forehead, dressed in a priest's outfit. Tracer and Denrol realize the laughter is coming from behind them and turns around. Father Denrol is still laughing at them when they charge and give him a double clothesline. Fat Tony: Father Denrol paying for his mocking. Now the real Denrol rolling out of the ring as we start this match. Jimmy: Folks, I cannot believe what Alexandra Korth has just done. She's robbed us of our Main Event! In the ring, we see Tracer pick up Father Denrol and whip him into the ropes. As Father Denrol comes back, he is met with a thunderous backdrop. Outside the ring we see that Denrol is heading off up the ramp with a furious look on his face, but Alexandra just drops her mic and heads off to the back without a backward glance. Denrol says something that lip-readers will find very offensive and turns around to watch what is happening inside the ring. As we watch, Tracer nails an Ace Crusher on Father Denrol, who sells the move as though hit with a bomb. On the ramp, Denrol shakes his head and heads off under the WWATron. Back in the ring Tracer whips Father Denrol into the turnbuckles. The crowd pop as Tracer grabs the staggering Father Denrol and nails him with a Pulp Fiction reverse Samoan Drop. Jimmy: This is going to be the fastest Main Event ever. Alexandra Korth has a lot to answer for. Tracer goes for a cocky one-handed cover and the referee makes the count. 1! 2! Somehow Father Denrol kicks out at two and three-quarters. Tracer shrugs and picks up his fallen foe, before… Jimmy: Pounding the Pavement! Pounding the Pavement! This one is over, no doubt about it! The referee drops and counts. This time the three-count is academic, and the crowd pop loud for their hero as the referee raises his hand in the air. Gary Trudeau: Your winner, and STILL the WWA Cruiserweight champion… Tracer Bullet! Tracer helps up Father Denrol and mouths the words "too bad" at him. Father Denrol shrugs and looks disappointed as "Jeremy" plays. Fat Tony: I don't think this match lasted two minutes! Jimmy: Alexandra Korth has a… Pauses Folks, I'm getting word we can go backstage! Fat Tony: Let's see what Denrol has to say to his lovely young wife! I bet it ain't nice! Whoo, eh!?! Jimmy: Honestly… The scene now cuts to the parking lot. Denrol is running towards a limo. The back door of the limo is down and Alexandra is sitting there. She turns to Denrol as he approaches. Denrol: Alex, what's going on here? Why did you do that? Why did you ruin my match? Why? Denrol runs a hand through his hair. Alexandra's voice is cold when she eventually deigns to answer her husband. Alexandra: You deserved it, Gordon. You made your mistake. You chose wrestling, the fans, and the fame over me. Now you have to live with that mistake. Denrol: You didn't need to do that! I know you're upset right now, but you asked the impossible… I can't give this up… even for the woman I love! Alexandra: Fine. I wish you best of luck next week. I hope you become the World Champion for a second time. Because when you do and when you go to Labor Day Brawl, I will do everything in my power to make sure Tracer Bullet once again defeats the "Greatest of All Earth Time". Go, driver. The limo drives off, leaving a stunned Denrol in its wake. Fat Tony: Did you hear that? Did you hear that? Alexandra Korth wants to screw Denrol over! She wants to see him fail! How great is this! Jimmy: Folks, we're almost out of time! We focus on the limo as it drives away, and a little "WWA" logo appears in the bottom corner of the screen. Fat Tony: Alexandra Korth has screwed Denrol out of his chance at the WWA Cruiserweight gold and now wants to screw him out of the World Title, too! Jimmy: How this will affect Denrol is anyone's business, but we're out of time! I've been Jimmy Rose… Fat Tony: And I've been laughing my ass off at Denrol and Alexandra Korth's marital problems! I love it! Jimmy: We'll see you on Meltdown, folks! Don't forget to watch Fiesta! Goodnight everyone! We fade out on a picture of Denrol staring after the departing limo, looking stunned.
