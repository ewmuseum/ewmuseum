WWA — 14 Jan 1998

14 Jan 1998

Wednesday January 14, 1998 The screen opens up to the the NCW banner. Then.... Northeast Champion Wrestling flashes through the screen and leaves the NCW Banner Then firework type explosions go off around the Banner. The Banner fades... then the screen shows Lehman College's The Apex. The screen then goes to the inside to show the fans jumping and screaming at the Camera. The camera pans over the the Complex and then the camera finds Tony Bailey and Terry Valentine sitting behind the table in the broadcast area. Tony : Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the Apex in the Bronx, New York. Which I might add in the "Homebase" for the Northeast Championship Wrestling. This is the Innagural Episode of Wednesday Night War. I am your host Tony Bailey. And to my right the "honorable" Terry Valentine and to my left the "Talkman" Johny Morehouse. Terry looks at Tony with an annoyed look Terry : Thanks, Tony! This is a very exciting night for Northeast Championship Wrestling. This is the first Wednesday Night War for the NCW and we got a good one. Johny : Thank you, Tony. Tonight we have a double Main Event. The Executive's Club is putting their title on the line against Matt Sanders and Canuck. And we have a grudge match 2 months in the making putting the House of Pain against the Triple Threat, the Harvester of Sorrow and "Brutal" Brian Cairo are going head to Head. Tony : That's right, let's go down to Gary Trudeau for the first Wednesday Night Match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... Welcome to Wednesday Night War! Our first installment of NCW on Wednesday Night. Our first match this evening will be set for one fall with a Ten minute time limit. Introducing first, already in the ring, from somewhere in the Orient, weighing in at 220 lbs we have the White Scorpian. The White Ninja throws a few kicks and punches in the ring as the crowd gives a little response to the White Ninja. "Gangsta's Paradise" starts up over the Arena. The crowd goes to their feet and starts to boo. Trudeau : And his opponent, lead to the ring by Aric Crockett, from Alcatraz and weighing in at 260lbs, representing the House of Pain, we have Tiburon. The crowd continues to boo as Tiburon and Aric Crockett come out to the ring. Tiburon and Aric are yelling profanities back at the crowd. Johny : Tiburon is winless in his last three attempts. His fortune should change tonight against a much smaller White Ninja. Terry : He win simply because the House of Pain is in his corner! Tony : There is the bell and the first match of Wednesday Night War in underway. Johny : The two men lock up and Tiburon throws the ninja to the ground. Tony : Tiburon definataly has the strength advantage as shown by that exchange. Terry : The two men go for a lock up again. This time the White Ninja gains the advantage with a side headlock. Tony : Tiburon with a display of power picks the Ninja up and drops him on his back with a pretty Belly to Back suplex. Terry : Tiburon is up and brings the Ninja up with him. Tiburon sends him into the ropes and executes a clothesline. Nicely done. Tony : Tiburon grabs the Ninja and set him up for a Pildriver. Up and DOWN! The White Ninja is in serious trouble, now. Johny : It looks like it is time for the Tiburon Chomp. We haven't seen this move before, it is a Sunset Flip Powerbomb. Tiburon is up and over, Boom! And the cover 1 - 2 - 3. Terry : Did you see that move, such athletic prowess. Now I see what the House of Pain was thinking when they recruited Tiburon. "Gangsta's Paradise" starts over the Arena as the duo of Tiburon and Aric Crockett leave the ring. Tony : That was a impressive display put on by Tiburon. That match will go done in history as the first match on Wednesday Night! Johny : Yes, It will. Both the White Ninja and Tiburon have a place in NCW history books. Now lets go to the second match of the evening. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... The second match on the card for this eveing is a singles match. It is set for one fall and a ten minute time limit. Introducing first, already in the ring, we have the Patriot. The Patriot raises his hand and coninues to warm up in the ring as the crowd gives a little response to the Patriot. "The Unforgiven" by Metallica starts up over the Arena. The crowd gives a pretty positive reception. Trudeau : And his opponent, from Sweetwater, Texas and weighing in at 198lbs, representing the Triple Threat, we have "Demented" Drake Blackthorne. The crowd cheers a little more as Drake Blackthorne comes out to the ring. Drake is holding the "Soul Pole" up in the air and waving it around. He points it at the ring and at the Patriot. The crowd cheers at this. Johny : This is the first action that Drake Blackthorne has had in the NCW as a singles competitor. Tony : Drake is a fierce competitor and should do just fine in his NCW singles debut. There's the bell and we are ready to go. Johny : As soon as the bell sounds Drake charges and tackles the Patriot. Drake is flailing away at the Patriot face, arms and chest. The Referee admonishes the count. Terry : Drake gets off of the Patriot and pulls the Patriot up and floors him with a standing clotheline. The Patriot goes down in a heap. Tony : It looks like Drake is going up to the top turnbuckle. The Patriot is getting to his feet. Drake jumps and nails the Patriot with a Flying Drop Kick. Terry : Drake flashes the Triple Threat sign, which means it is time for the "Dementia Plex". He nailed it. 1 - 2 - 3. Johny : Boy, Drake Blackthorne didn't even let the Patriot have a chance in this match. He controled the whole match. Very impressive indeed. Terry : I would say that "Demeted" Drake Blackthorne's NCW singles debut showed that he is a force, not only in the Tag Team ranks, but in the singles as well. Tony : Good Call Terry. Now let's go down to Gary Trudeau for the next match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... The next match on the card for this eveing is a singles match. It is set for one fall and a ten minute time limit. Introducing first, already in the ring, we have the Tim Cavanaugh. Tim raises his hand and coninues to warm up in the ring as the crowd gives a little response to the Tim Cavanaugh. "Rock Hard, Ride Free" by Judas Priest starts up over the Arena. The crowd starts to cheer. Trudeau : And his opponent, from Chicago, IL and weighing in at 267lbs, accomponied to the ring by Dean Ritman, we have Scotty Kilborn. The crowd continues to cheer as Dean and Scotty slap hands and talk to the crowd as they enter the ring. Tony : It has been awhile since we have seen Scotty Kilborn in action. Terry : It hasn't been long enough! Johny : There is the bell and we are underway. Tony : The two wrestlers lock up in the middle. Tim Cavanaugh is a big strong guy. Johny : Finally Scotty gains the advantage with a side headlock. Cavanaugh pushes him back to the ropes and sends Scotty for a ride. Cavanaugh leap frogs Scotty and then Cavanaugh drops down to the ground and Scotty rebounds off the ropes and drops an elbow on Cavanaugh's back. Terry : Cavanaugh really telegraphed that one. Scotty picks Cavanaugh up and sends him in the corner. Tony : Scotty runs after him and nails Cavanaugh with a clothesline in the corner. Scotty holds Cavanaugh up in the corner. Scotty backs up into the middle of the ring with Cavanaugh in tow. Scotty set up what appears to be a Northern Light Suplex, but changes it into a Brainbuster type move. Johny : Scotty calls that the "Motor City Driver". He covers, 1 - 2 - 3. Terry : Isn't Kilborn from Chicago? Why does he have a move named after Detriot? Tony : Shut Up, Terry! He can call the thing whatever he wants. Johny : OK. That was a great match for Scotty Kilborn. He should be a power in the singles division for sure. Tony : Let's go to the ring for our next match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... The next match on the card for this eveing is a singles match. It is set for one fall and a fifteen minute time limit. Introducing first, from parts unknown and weighing in at 260 lbs, we have Nemesis. "Sweet Dreams" starts up over the PA as Nemesis come down to the ring. The crowd is giving Nemesis a good response. Nemesis slaps a few hands and enters the ring. "Body Count" by Body Count starts up over the Arena. The crowd starts to boo. Trudeau : And his opponent, from Dupont, PA standing over 7 feet tall and weighing in at 327lbs, we have Big Eliem. The crowd continues to boo as Big Eliem makes his way to the ring. Tony : This match is going to be an interesting match-up. We have the sheer size of Big Eliem and the quickness of Nemesis. Johny : Nemesis won his debut a few weeks ago, so he will be looking to continue his winning ways. Whereas Big Eliem lost his debut to the huge Bob. Terry : Big Eliem is going to win this match! Tony : Why! Terry? Terry : He's bigger. Tony : That is all you could think of, he's bigger? Size doens't always win. Johny : Alright, there is the bell, let's go to the action. Terry : The two men lock up in the middle of the ring. Big Eliem pushes Nemesis clear into the corner. Tony : Nemesis get's up and they lock up again. Oh.. Nemesis catches Big Eliem in the gut with a kick. Nemesis back him up into the ropes and sends him for a ride. Johny : Nemesis backs into the ropes and lauches into a cross body block. Big Eliem catches him and lifts him up over his shoulder. Big Eliem takes a few steps and DOWN with a powerslam. Terry : Ouch! See I told you that Big Eliem would win. Tony : Big Eliem picks up Nemesis and throws him into the ropes, Nemesis catches Big Eliem with a dropkick to the chest. Big Eliem staggers back a few steps... but doesn't go down. Terry : It looks like Nemesis is going to try it again. Nemesis nails Big Eliem with another drop kick that sends Big Eliem over the Top rope and onto the concrete. Johny : Nemesis go to the ropes and climbs up to the Top Turnbuckle. He jumps and hits Big Eliem on the back with a double axe-handle. Big Eliem goes down. Tony : Nemesis rolls in and then back out of the ring to break the count. He lifts up Big Eliem and throws him into, No it is reversed....Nemesis goes headlong into the metal stairs. Terry : Big Eliem shakes the cobwebs out and goes and picks up Nemesis. He runs Nemesis into the ring post. Nemesis is down and he looks hurt. Tony : Big Eliem picks Nemesis up and helps him into the ring. Johny : Big Eliem sends Nemesis into the ropes and hits with a big Boot to the head. Nemesis again falls to the ground. Terry : Big Eliem picks Nemesis up again and lines him up for his finisher. Big Eliem takes Nemesis up and then DOWN. Powerbomb! Nemesis isn't moving. Tony : Big Eliem drops to the cover. 1 - 2 - 3. Terry : I told you that Big Eliem would win. Johny : I guess that win puts Big Eliem back on track for the Big matches. Tony : I guess it wouldn't hurt his chances at all. Let's go to the next match. The camera zooms down to the ring were we see Gary Trudeau in the center on the ring with a microphone. Trudeau : Ladies and Gentlemen... The next match on the card for this eveing is a singles match. It is set for one fall and a fifteen minute time limit. Introducing first, from Houston, Texas and weighing in at 225 lbs, we have The Outlaw. "Thunderstruck" by ACDC starts up over the PA as The Outlaw comes down to the ring. The crowd boos as the Outlaw threatens bodily harm to the fans. "Iron Man" by Black Sabbath replaces ACDC over the PA. The crowd stands up and starts to cheer. Trudeau : And his opponent, from parts unknown and weighing in at 255 lbs, we have Black Scorpian. The crowd continues to cheer as the the lights go out and "Iron Man" blares over the PA. The lights come on and the Black Scorpian is standing across from The Outlaw. Tony : This match is going to be a barn burner. There is no love between these two combatants. These two wrestlers have been at each others throats since day one. Johny : There's the bell and the Outlaw charges the Black Scorpian. The Outlaw catches the Black Scorpian off guard and begins to unleash two months of anger on the Black Scorpian. Right, lefts, kicks and elbows. Terry : Yeah! Squash the Black Bug! The Black Scorpian in down and the Outlaw is still kicking and beating on the Black Scorpian. Tony : The referee admonishes the five count and the Outlaw backs off. Johny : Wow! That was quite a flury by the Outlaw. I guess he means business tonight. Terry : The Outlaw helps the Black Scorpian to his feet and sends him into the ropes and catches the Black Scorpian with a drop kick that sends the Black Scorpian to the mat in a heap. Tony : Great drop kick by the Outlaw. The Outlaw picks up the Black Scorpian once again. This time the Outlaw locks in an abdominal stretch. Terry : The Outlaw finally gets a chance to display his superior wrestling abilities. The Black bug doesn't have a chance. Tony : Look the Outlaw is using the ropes for leverage. The ref looks up and sees the Outlaw and make him break the hold. Johny : The Outlaw picks up the Black Scorpian and drop him in a Body Slam. Tony : The Outlaw picks up the Black Scorpian and sends him to the ropes and nails him with a jumping spin Kick. The Black Scorpian looks like he is in trouble. The Outlaw goes for a quick pin, but only get a two count. Terry : Who is that coming out of the crowd... Oh no it's that giant from the other night. Johny : His name is officially The Fan. Tony : What is he going to do out here? It looks like he is just standing and watching the match. Terry : The Outlaw picks up the Black Scorpian and GOOD, he sees the Fan guy. He walks over to the ropes and starts yelling and cursong at the Fan. Tony : The Black Scorpian sees the Outlaw is distracted and rolls him up, No a two count! That caught the Outlaw offguard. He won't forget about the Black Scorpian again. Johny : The Outlaw throws a few punches to gain the advantage again. He sends the Black Scorpian into the rope and goes foe clothesline, but the Black Scorpian duck and rebounds off the opposite ropes. The Black Scorpian hits The Outlaw with a drop kick. The Outlaw goes down. Terry : The Black Bug pulls the Outlaw's hair to bring him up, the black bug send him into the rope and Oh, hits with a flying clothesline. Terry : Yes! Get him. The Outlaw charges into the corner. Tony : The Black Scorpian moves and the Outlaw hits hard in the corner. He staggers back a few steps and the Black Scorpian is there and DOWN! The "Equalizer". The Outlaw is down! The Black Scorpian slumps into the corner apearently to rest. Terry : Yes! Here comes the rest of the Executive's Club. Mr. Business and The Judge both hit the Fan from behind. The Fan goes down. Tony : The Executive rushes the ring and is met with a clothesline by the Black Scorpian. The Judge and Mr. Business are both in the ring now. Black Scorpian knocks the Judge out of the ring with a drop kick, but Mr Business grabs him in the Claw. Terry : LOOK at the black bug! Mr Business is making him pay. Terry : The Executive's Club said that they would take care of this Black Scorpian business. It looks like the Black Scorpian is taken care of! Tony : Maybe so, but it took them four against two, too do anything! Johny : Well, The Black Scorpian wins by disqualification at any rate. Tony : Let's go to the ring for the Tag Title match.
