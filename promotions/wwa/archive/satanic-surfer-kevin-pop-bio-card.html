"Satanic Surfer" Kevin Pop (bio card)

What this is Roster bio card for the WWA wrestler "Satanic Surfer" Kevin Pop, giving style, measurements, hometown, entrance music, appearance, ring attire, background, finisher and move list.
Wrestler Name: "Satanic Surfer" Kevin Pop Style: All Rounder Height: 6'1" Weight: 232 Hometown: Newport, England Entrance Music: "Bloodclot" by Rancid Appearance: Looks like he's been sleeping in a chair for a week, which he probably has. Kevin's pretty muscular body is set off with a weak english tan. His long blonde hair hangs just below his shoulders, and always looks messed up. has a tattoo on each arm, one which reads Surfers Against Sewage and another of Pop; the rice crispies cereal mascot. Ring Attire: Wears baggy denim jeans and blue or black boots underneath. Always wears a hawaiian shirt to ring which he takes off before wrestling. Has wrists are taped with blue tape. Background: Grew up in the seaside town of newport, and was surfing from the age of 4. After school he went onto college and dropped out after the first day, due to extreme pressure from work. Realisng he needed cash to supply his surfing needs, he signed up at a seaside wrestling show as an extra. Showing flair and talent, he was scouted by a local federation to wrestle for them on a regular basis. Clocking up a good bit of fame, and a fairly good income..eventually the travelling got to him when he was banned from a manchester swimming pool for trying to surf in the deep end. Now he seeks fame and fortune in the GWF. Finisher: The wipeout Description: A stalled brainbuster DDT Moves: 1. the shorebreak (second finisher) - arm wrench guillotine face crusher 2. jump swinging DDT 3. jawbreaker 4. high angle back drop 5. hip toss 6. belly to belly release suplex 7. japanese arm bar 8. hang ten - salute to crowd then a frankensteiner 9. back elbow 10. sunset flip powerbomb 11. top rope double leg drop
