What this is The founding pitch for a promotion that, per an exhaustive Wayback check (see the BBW wing), never launched — served here whole because it's the entire surviving record, not a fragment of a larger one. Designed by Sean Simon Williams (1985–2018).
BUCK BROTHERS WRESTLING (BBW) Buck Bros Wrestling is an independent wrestling promotion headquartered in Wheeling, West Virginia. It is run by two brothers, Zachary and Elijah Buck, who own the Buck Brothers Flea Market & Auction on the outskirts of town. Business has been slow for the past few years, so the brothers want to drum up interest in the auction and get new vendors. How to do so, you ask? Well, that rasslin’ game is pretty popular in the South, or so they here, so Zach and Elijah decided to have monthly wrestling events at the market. PROMOTION CONCEPT: Z-tier indy fed. This isn’t a company for anyone who is remotely professional about their wrestling career. This is for weekend warriors: the factory worker who works five days a week and wrestles once or twice a month on the side. This is for a fifteen or sixteen year old kid with two weeks of wrestling training who thinks he’s good enough to work in front of (a very small number of) people. The ENTIRE GOAL of BBW is to get people to come to the flea market and drum up more business. BUILDING: Buck Brothers Flea Market and Auction. It’s a medium sized building in the middle of nowhere. The wrestling ring is set up in the middle. It’s actually a third-hand boxing ring, and is stiff enough that half of most matches take place outside the ring. There are enough chairs for 160 patrons (four rows of ten on each side), though the amount of asses in the seats at any one time are highly unlikely to ever reach that amount. The Bucks will count almost anyone who walks into the market during the show as part of the attendance and still probably not hit triple digits most times. CHAMPIONSHIP: BBW World Heavyweight Championship. The belt itself is a WWE replica title. Literally a WWE replica title. No alterations. It has John Cena side plates, for the record. Calling it a world title is a joke, but BBW is a joke, so it all works out. That’s all they can afford for now. PAYOFFS: Wrestlers for WWC receive as payment for their services $5 cash and a food voucher worth $10 at Fat Larry’s House of Bar-Be-Que, convieniently located on the opposite end of the Market. This is actually probably the best part about working for WWC - Fat Larry’s pretty good at barbecue, and the ten bucks gets you a brisket or pulled pork sandwich, potato salad, cole slaw, and a big thing of sweet tea. SHOWS: Once-monthly, almost always the third Saturday of every month. Referred to as “Wrestling at the Flea Market” followed by a number. Not Roman numerals, Just a number. DISTRIBUTION: The company has, God help us, a local television deal on a public access station. It airs from 2:30-3:30 every Saturday morning. Thirty minutes of the show are wrestling, the other thirty is basically an infomercial for the Buck Brothers Flea Market and Auction. Zachary and Elijah also upload the raw footage from the show to YouTube on a monthly basis. ENTRANCE MUSIC: Wrestlers do not get to pick their entrance music; instead it is randomly selected from one of 20 songs on a CD that Zachary burned to play on a boom box. The Soviet National Anthem IMPORTANT PEOPLE: Zachary Buck: Co-owner of BBW. The company’s lead heel manager. Is utterly horrible at his job. Whether or not this is on purpose is up for debate. Elijah Buck: Co-owner of BBW. Has no actual wrestling training, and thinks pro wrestling is real. His brother convinced him that he was tougher than everyone else so he’s on the roster. He shoots against all of his opponents, but never wins because he’s 5’7” and 169 lbs. “Fat” Larry Wilson: Owner and operator of Fat Larry’s Bar-Be-Que, the most popular tenant of the Buck Bros. Flea Market and Auction. The roster is paid primarily in vouchers to his place, and the restaurant doubles as the backstage area for the company. Larry loves the wrestlers, but hates the Bucks, largely because they take forever to pay him. Jacob Manson: The portly half of BBW’s intrepid commentary team. Manages to get Slim to give him his food voucher every week, but no one’s sure how, and no one really wants to ask. Slim Buttons: Pimply faced and scrawny half of BBW’s commentary team. He goes to high school with Jacob. His greatest ambitions are to wrestle for BBW one day and get into Pocahontas County College. Barista: “The Coffee Making Animal” and retired pro wrestler. Has found himself in West Virginia and runs the Darkest Timeline Coffee Shop alongside his business partner, fellow former pro wrestling and Samoan anime fan Umanga. CHARACTER CREATION ADVICE: - THIS IS NOT A SERIOUS E-FED. No RPs will be required; if you want to, though, go ahead! No segments will be required, but if you want? Knock yourself out! I will book all manner of ludicrous things for your characters to do, so once a month you can sit back and read the ridiculousness. This is a place to have fun first and foremost, for everyone. - Remember, this is a SHITTY fed. Attendance will be 50-150 tops, your character makes almost nothing, and you work in a flea market,for God’s sake. This is important. Please do not apply generic millionaires or I will have to reject your application. :( Keep the setting in mind. - Please feel free to have your character be somehow involved with the Flea Market and Auction if you want. I’m more than willing to work with everyone interested to make this ridiculous place even more ludicrous. - My final piece of advice? Make a new character here. Don’t take someone you have association with. IMO, the fun of BBW is just how ludicrous it is. Roll with that. ------------------ APPLICATION: If, god help us all, there’s enough interest, I will get forums together and start this ridiculous place. A blogspot page is also an option.
