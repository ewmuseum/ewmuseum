Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Buck Brothers Wrestling exists, in full, as one pitch document: a founding invitation for a promotion that was never meant to be taken seriously by its own design — a “Z-tier indy fed” run by two flea-market owners to drum up foot traffic, paying its wrestlers five dollars and a barbecue voucher, drawing entrance music at random from a boombox CD. The pitch's own closing line hedges the launch on “enough interest” to justify a forum or a Blogspot page.
