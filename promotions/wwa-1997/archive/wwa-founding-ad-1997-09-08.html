R-wrestling Come on BABY!!!! I know you want to!!!!

8 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-Wrestling is basically your chance to live out your dream of becoming a proffessional wrestler. You will deal with your own ego, the conflicts that come with working with others, and the challenge of becoming a success in the world of proffessional wrestling.
Send back to [detail withheld] The R-wrestling fed you will be participating in will be called the "Wild Wrestling Alliance". It will be an organization that will start out as an indy and rise to prominence with you as one of the top stars, if you choose to enter it.
You can have a maximum of 2 guys. For tag teams, you must run them like 2 individual wrestlers. REAL NAME (Your e-addy): WRESTLER NAME: LOOKS (Not attire, but what he basically looks like [weight, height included]): GIMMICK: ANY PRE/POST MATCH RITUALS HE DOES: ENTRANCE: ANYTHING ELSE THAT SHOULD BE KNOWN: FINANCIAL STATUS: Dirt poor (Can't change it) CARD STATUS: MIDCARD (Can't change it) OVER POINTS: 0 (Negotiable) CONTRACT: 15 shows, $50 an appearance (Negotiable) RING ATTIRE: * Vest ($40) * Tights ($70) * Boots ($100) * Jewelry ($300) * Leather jacket ($150) * Mask ($50) * gloves ($50) * hat ($50) * Knee pads ($70) * Elbow pads ($60) FINANCIAL STATUS: DIRT POOR - You have $500 to start - Live in a studio apartment that costs $350 a month (Rent due at the end of every month)
NOT SO BAD - $5,000 - NICE apartment that costs $900 a month ON YOUR OWN - $50,000 or above - Shitty house in the hood that costs $10,000
UPPER MIDDLE CLASS - $500,000 or above - Damn cool house that costs $100,000
FILTHY RICH - $1,000,000 or above - VERY nice house with all the cool stuff that costs $700,000
CARD STATUS: You will all start out as Midcarders, but then as points fluctuate, things will change based on point percentages.
0% - 25%: JOBBER 26% - %75: MIDCARDER 76% - 90%: MAIN EVENTER 91% - 100%: SUPERSTAR OVER POINTS: These are points that you will get after appearances that will judge how over with the crowd you are. When you reach a certain # of points (Which can't really be judged seeing as though the points will keep rising), you will become a top draw and you will get more money in your future contracts.
You get more points by doing interviews, which you will get paid for and you will have to type up yourself. Judging by how good the interview is, you will get extra points, which will put you over with the crowd.
SIGNED AGREEMENT:_____________________________ By signing this, you agree to appear when scheduled and not get out of line, as judged by the WWA Board of Directors.
