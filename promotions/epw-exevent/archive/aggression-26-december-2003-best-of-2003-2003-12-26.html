BEST MOMENTS - #2
9-11 Tribute (9/11/03)

{The camera focuses on the ring as Outlaw, Masked Marvel, Madman Luke Bishop, Big Bad John and Show Stopper stand in the center of the ring.}

{Outlaw holds up the microphone that is in his right hand and begins to speak. The crowd goes silent.}

Outlaw: Two years. It has been two years since that fatefull day. September 11, 2001, the day that a terrorist attack brought down the world trade center towers. Not only ending the lifes of civilians that lived and worked around the area and in the building, but ending the lives of many brave individuals that took on the responsibility of attempting to save those people's lives.

{Outlaw hands the microphone to Madman Luke Bishop.}

Madman Luke Bishop: Tonight, in memory of those who lost their lives two years ago, the names of all who lost their lives in the incident shall be honored.

{Madman looks towards the entrance stage. There is a large screen on the right side that is covered by a dark sheet. An XEW staff member walks up to the screen and pulls off the sheet. Madman hands the microphone over to Masked Marvel.}

Masked Marvel: Throughout the show, this screen will scroll through and display the names of each and every person that lost their lives in the terrorist attack of two years ago.

{Masked Marvel hands the microphone over to Big Bad John.}

Big Bad John: Although I am American, just as Show Stopper and Outlaw are, Luke Bishop and Masked Marvel are not. No matter where we live or what country we were born in, we are all one thing, human. So, with that said, we ask that you please observe a moment of silence as we remember those who lost their lives.

{The crowd is silent. After about a minute, the superstars raise their heads and Show Stopper is handed the microphone.}

Show Stopper: We would like to give our con..........

{Show Stopper is interrupted when a strange middle eastern song plays throughout the arena. All of the sudden, two men wearing full body tights and masks step out from behind the curtains. Covering their tights are the colors of the Iraqi flag. One of the men holds up a microphone and begins to speak.}

Masked Man #1: You people are ridiculous! How can you come out here and say that this event was a tradegy? You Americans got what you deserve!

{The crowd boos.}

{The camera switches to the ring where Show Stopper has the microphone.}

Show Stopper: Are you looking for a fight? If you are, then you're in luck! Why don't you two come down here and say that again! You want to disrespect the people that lost their lives and the families who lost their brothers, sisters, fathers, mothers and friends? Come down to the ring and do it!

{The two masked men run down the ramp and slide into the ring. The five superstars in the ring jump the two and then throw them back out of the ring. The masked men then slowly get to their feet and stumble back up the ramp.}

{Outlaw grabs the microphone from Show Stopper.}

Outlaw: Hey, if you don't like it here, you can get the hell out!

{The crowd cheers.}

{The camera shows the masked men holding their heads. The camera then fades to black and the footage end. The camera then changes to Cole Porter back in the studio.}

Cole Porter: That was the 9-11 tribute which was interrupted by the two Masked Iraqi men. Since then, both men have competed in the Chamber From Hell Match but as for what they did on that show, I think it was ridiculous, and I think that they got what they deserved. Speaking of the Chamber From Hell Match, here it is. The match comes in at number two with 26% of the vote.

{The camera fades to black. The footage then plays.}

Michael Sharpe: LUKE BISHOP HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!

Erik Channel: Oh, I didn't even realize that Leatherface was still in there.

[Hudman walks over to Leatherface and the two begin to exchange punches as the referees on the outside close the cage door. Tom Conway and King Hardkore continue to go at it in the center of the ring while Psycho Dan has Ace on the ropes on the east side of the ring. The referees on the outside of the ring open the cage door as Psycho Dan attempt to eliminate Ace. Meanwhile, Richard Rush stands in the northwest corner of the ring, watching the action.]

Erik Channel: Hudman is a sitting duck. Why doesn't anyone go over there and eliminate him?

[Hudman kicks Leatherface in the gut and hits him with a DDT near the south side of the ring. Hudman then grabs onto the cage door on the south side of the ring and pushes it open. Hudman steps over Leatherface and begins to kick him, trying to eliminate him.]

Cole Porter: Hudman takes Leatherface down with a DDT.

[King Hardkore grabs onto Tom Conway and irish whips him into the ropes on the west side of the ring. King Hardkore tries for a clothesline, but Tom Conway ducks under it and stands behind King Hardkore. Hardkore turns around and is hit in the face with a superkick by Tom Conway. King Hardkore falls to the mat and rolls to the north side of the ring. Tom Conway walks behind Psycho Dan and grabs him by the head, pulling him off of Ace. Conway turns him around and kicks him in the gut. Tom Conway grabs onto Psycho Dan and then irish whips him into the ropes on the north side of the ring, closer to the northeast corner. Psycho Dan comes off of the ropes. Tom Conway steps around Dan and hits him with a falling neckbreaker. Tom Conway gets back up and walks back over to King Hardkore, who is getting to his feet on the north side of the ring.]

Cole Porter: King Hardkore is still going at it with Tom Conway.

Erik Channel: Yeah, and Conway is still beating the crap out of him.

[Ace gets off of the ropes and walks over to where Psycho Dan is on the mat. Ace begins to hit Psycho Dan with a series of stomps, keeping him on the mat. Ace grabs Psycho Dan by the head and helps him to his feet. Ace then back him into the northeast corner and begins to hit him with a series of shoulder thrusts. Meanwhile, on the south side of the ring, Hudman isn't able to eliminate Leatherface, who gets back to his feet on the ring apron with his back facing the outside.]

Erik Channel: Hah. Hudman can't get rid of Leatherface so easily.

[Hudman kicks Leatherface in the gut and lifts him up in a front suplex. Hudman lets go of him, causing him to fall to the outside and hit his face on the ring apron.]

Cole Porter: Oh yes he can!

Michael Sharpe: LEATHERFACE HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!

Erik Channel: What is going on here? Somebody needs to stop Hudman!

[Hudman turns around as the referees on the outside of the ring close the cage door. Hudman leans against the ropes on the south side of the ring and watches the action. Ace pulls Psycho Dan out of the corner and switches positions with him. Ace kicks Psycho Dan in the gut and hits him with The Spade. Meanwhile, Tom Conway kicks King Hardkore until he ends up sitting down in the northeast corner of the ring. Richard Rush moves out of the way as Tom Conway begins to hit King Hardkore with a series of stomps in the corner. Richard Rush turns towards Hudman and the two stare at each other for a few seconds before Ace runs over and clotheslines Hudman. Ace then begins to stomp on Hudman, who is hung up on the bottom rope on the south side of the ring.]

Erik Channel: There we go! Eliminate him, Ace!

[Ace grabs onto Hudman and helps him up to his feet. Ace lifts Hudman into the air and hits him with a backbreaker. Ace turns toward Psycho Dan to see that he is slowly trying to get to his feet on the east side of the ring. Ace walk over to Psycho Dan and grabs him by the head. Ace helps him up and then throws him into the cage on the north side of the ring. Psycho Dan hits the cage face first and falls back to the mat. Ace helps Psycho Dan up once again and walks him to the door on the east side of the ring as the referees on the outside begin to open in. Ace props Psycho Dan up on the top rope and attempts to throw him out of the ring. Meanwhile, on the south side of the ring, Hudman grabs onto the ropes and quickly gets back to his feet.]

Erik Channel: What? He wont stay down!

Cole Porter: Hudman is determined to win this thing.

[Hudman runs over to the east side of the ring and clotheslines Ace, sending both Psycho Dan and Ace over the top. Psycho Dan falls to the outside first, immediately followed by Ace.]

Cole Porter: Did you see that?!

Erik Channel: Yeah, how did he just eliminate two guys at once?!

Michael Sharpe: PSYCHO DAN HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!

Erik Channel: This is ridiculous!

Michael Sharpe: ACE HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!

[Ace gets back to his feet and stares at Hudman, who is on the inside of the ring as the referees close the cage door. The two continue to argue as Richard Rush and Tom Conway both stomp on King Hardkore in the northwest corner of the ring. Richard Rush and Tom Conway grab King Hardkore and lift him to his feet. Tom Conway walks Hardkore to the door on the west side of the ring while Richard Rush opens the door. The two grab onto King Hardkore and throw him through the second rope and to the outside of the ring.]

Cole Porter: There goes Hardkore.

Michael Sharpe: KING HARDKORE HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!

Erik Channel: There is no way that Hudman can win now!

[Richard Rush closes the door as Tom Conway turns around towards Hudman, who hits him with a punch, causing him to take a step back. Hudman then walks over to Richard Rush and hits him with a clothesline, causing him to fall to the mat. Hudman then turns around and grabs onto Tom Conway. Hudman runs to the east side of the ring and throws Tom Conway into the cage. Tom Conway falls onto the apron between the cage and the ropes. The referees on the outside of the ring open the cage door. Hudman grabs Tom Conway by the arm and pulls him across the apron and in front of the opening in the cage. Hudman takes a step back and hits Conway with a huge stomp, causing him to fall off of the apron and to the outside of the ring.]

Erik Channel: What? How can this be?

Michael Sharpe: TOM CONWAY HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!

Erik Channel: I'm not going to worry, Richard Rush will take out the trash right here.

[Hudman leans against the ropes, staring down at Tom Conway who is on the outside. On the other side of the ring, Richard Rush gets to his feet and runs at Hudman. Just as Richard Rush gets to him, Hudman just happens to move out of the way, causing Richard Rush to send himself over the ropes and out of the ring.]

Erik Channel: NO!

Michael Sharpe: RICHARD RUSH HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!

Cole Porter: He did it! He's going to Last Resort!

{Erik Channel stands up and throws down his headset.}

Cole Porter: Hudman pretty much eliminated everyone and is now the number one contender.

{The bell sounds.}

Michael Sharpe: Here is your winner and number one contender for the EPW World Championship, HUDMAN!

{The camera shows Hudman as he looks down at Richard Rush, who is lying on the outside of the ring. He looks up in disbelief as he hears Hudman's theme music. The camera then fades to black and the footage ends. The camera changes back to Cole Porter in the studio.}

Cole Porter: That was truly a great match. We are going to take a short break, but when we get back, we will be showing the number one moment and match of this past year on Aggression. Don't change the channel.

{The camera fades to black as the show goes to a commercial break.}

COMMERCIAL BREAK

{The show comes back from the commercial break to Cole Porter in the studio.}

Cole Porter: We are back and ready for the number one moment of this past year. Coming in at number one with 35% of the vote, here is the debut of John Adams.

{The camera fades to black. The footage then starts.}

BEST MOMENTS - #1
John Adams Debut (8/28/03)

{Hudman's music plays as he stands in the center of the ring.}

Cole Porter: We are back from the commercial break and we are just about ready for Hudman vs. the Mystery Opponent.

Erik Channel: I think that I might know who it is.

{"Highway To Hell" by AC/DC plays as Richard Rush steps out from behind the curtains and onto the stage with a microphone in hand.}

Richard Rush: Cut the music.

{Richard Rush's music fades out.}

Richard Rush: Let me get to the point. Hudman, you want to know who your opponent is tonight? Well, here he is.

{A tall man with long hair, wearing a trench coat and dark sunglasses steps out from behind the curtain.}

Richard Rush: You know this man Hudman! This is The Prophet!

Cole Porter: What?

{The camera switches to Hudman.}

Hudman: That's not Coleman Dowell!

{The camera switches back to Richard Rush.}

Richard Rush: I never said it was. This isn't The Prophet that you know from XEW. This isn't the man that you followed in The Prophecy, this is a man that you screwed over!

{The camera switches back to Hudman.}

Hudman: I don't know what you are talking about.

{The camera switches back to Richard Rush as the man takes off his sunglasses.}

Richard Rush: Look at this man Hudman. Look at his face. Does he look familiar? He should, you screwed him over! I'm not talking about XEW, this happened way before you ever got here. Does SWL ring a bell?

{The camera switches back to Hudman.}

Hudman: Yeah, I used to work for an independent promotion called Southern Wrestling League. That was a long time ago when I first came to the states. What does that have to do with anything?

{The camera switches back to Richard Rush.}

Richard Rush: Ha. Don't act like you forgot, Hudman. It was you that screwed this man over. You held this man back. If it wasn't for you, John Adams would have been a superstar.

{The camera switches back to Hudman.}

Hudman: What are you talking about? John Adams is already well-known.

Cole Porter: What is he talking about?

Hudman: Anyone that knows their history would know that John Adams was president of the United States of America.

{The camera switches back to Richard Rush.}

Richard Rush: You don't get it Hudman. This isn't a joke. This is serious. This man was pretty much living on the street. This man almost fell through the cracks. Why was this? Because of you Hudman. It was you that went behind his back to the promoter's office. You would go in and put him down. Everything that you always suggested was in your best interest. This man is 7' 3" and 350 pounds. He is a monster and he is far more talented than you ever were. If it wasn't for you going behind his back and spreading lies about him, he would be the biggest superstar in the business today.

{The camera switches back to Hudman.}

Hudman: You know what? If he is half as talented as you say he is, why doesn't he drag himself down here and get an ass kicking?

{The camera switches back to the stage.}

Richard Rush: Fine, you got it pal.

{The Prophet runs down the ramp and slides into the ring.}

Cole Porter: That was strange. Do you think what Rush said was true?

Erik Channel: I have no doubt.

{The camera fades to black as the footage ends. The camera then changes back to Cole Porter in the studio.}

Cole Porter: The entire face of EPW has changed since the debut of John Adams. He is now the current EPW Champion and has helped lead the company to where it now is. Another man who has made great contributions to EPW is none other than Hudman. The man is a former World Champion and now has his sights back on the gold. It is only fitting that Hudman be is the match that has been voted number one. With 37.5% of the votes, here is The Destroyer vs. Hudman.

{The camera fades to black. The footage then starts.}

BEST MATCHES - #1
The Destroyer vs. Hudman (9/4/03)

XEW WORLD TITLE MATCH
THE DESTROYER VS. HUDMAN

[Hudman and The Destroyer walk to the center of the ring and shake hands. Afterwards, Destroyer starts to walk away, but Hudman keeps ahold of him. The Destroyer turns back towards Hudman and is kicked in the gut. Hudman then hits The Destroyer with a vertical suplex.]

[The Destroyer gets to his feet and faces Hudman. Destroyer goes for a clothesline but Hudman ducks and hits him with a back suplex. Hudman helps The Destroyer to his feet and then hits him with a series of punches, sending him into the southwest corner. Hudman continues the assault in the corner and then irish whips The Destroyer into the opposite side of the ring. Hudman runs across the ring and tries to hit The Destroyer with a clothesline. The Destroyer gets his foot up and kicks Hudman in the gut.]

Cole Porter: Hudman is taking this shot at the title seriously.

Cole Porter: Destroyer finally slows Hudman down.

[The Destroyer hits Hudman with a tornado DDT. Destroyer then goes for the pin. Referee, John Adams starts the count. .1 .2. Hudman kicks out.]

Cole Porter: That count was fast.

Erik Channel: Was it?

Cole Porter: Yeah, Hudman was almost lost right there because of it.

Erik Channel: I don't really care who wins as long as it's not Hudman.

[The Destroyer gets up and faces John Adams with a look of confusion on his face. Hudman gets to his feet and stands behind him. The Destroyer turns around and is kicked in the gut. Hudman lifts him onto his shoulders and hits him with the Final Twist.]

Cole Porter: There's the Final Twist.

[Hudman goes for the pin. John Adams ignores the pin and leans up against the ropes. Hudman gets to his feet and then begins to argue with John Adams.]

Erik Channel: You can't argue with him, he's the referee!

Cole Porter: If he is the referee how come he didn't count the three?

Erik Channel: I think that Destroyer might have had his shoulder up.

{Richard Rush runs down from the crowd and climbs over the barricade with a steel chair in hand. Richard Rush slides into the ring behind Hudman. Rush runs at Hudman and swings the chair. Hudman ducks and Richard Rush hits Adams with the steel chair.}

Erik Channel: No!

Cole Porter: He finally got what he deserves.

{Adams falls down to the mat and rolls into the northeast corner of the ring. Richard Rush turns around and is kicked in the gut by Hudman, causing him to drop the steel chair. Hudman lifts Richard Rush onto his shoulders and then hits him with the Final Twist.}

Cole Porter: Another Final Twist! This time to Richard Rush!

[The Destroyer gets to his feet and stands behind Hudman. Hudman turns towards Destroyer and is kicked in the gut. The Destroyer lifts Hudman up and hits him with the Destruction Device. The Destroyer goes for the pin. ... ... ... ...]

Erik Channel: The referee is knocked out. Thanks to Hudman. Destroyer should have had the win there.

Cole Porter: It was Richard Rush who hit the referee with the chair.

[The Destroyer rolls off of Hudman and tries to get to his feet. Destroyer grabs onto the ropes on the north side of the ring for leverage.]

Cole Porter: The Destroyer is worn out and trying to get to his feet.

{Referee, Ted Sinegal runs down to the ring and slides in.}

Cole Porter: Finally we get a real referee out here.

[Hudman rolls over and grabs The Destroyer from behind, putting him in a school boy. Referee, Ted Sinegal starts the count. ...1 ...2. John Adams barely gets over to stop the count by kicking the referee.]

Cole Porter: I can't believe this! Hudman had the title won.

[John Adams then grabs Ted Sinegal and throws him out of the ring on the east side. John Adams helps Hudman to his feet. Adams lifts Hudman up and hits him with a huge powerbomb in the center of the ring.]

Erik Channel: There's his move, Cole.

Cole Porter: This is ridiculous!

[John Adams grabs The Destroyer and rolls him onto Hudman. John Adams is about to start the count when The Destroyer stumbles to his feet. The Destroyer starts to argue with Adams.]

Erik Channel: What is he doing?

Cole Porter: He has pride, Erik! He doesn't want to win like this.

[John Adams clotheslines Destroyer and rolls him back onto Hudman. Referee, John Adams starts the count. .1 .2 .3. The referee calls for the bell.]

Cole Porter: Hudman has just been screwed!

{The Bell Sounds.}

{On the outside of the ring, Richard Rush gets to his feet. Rush signals for John Adams to lift Hudman up.}

Cole Porter: Now what is going on?

{John Adams helps Hudman to his feet and drags him out of the ring. Adams exits the ring and begins to drag Hudman up the ramp. Richard Rush slowly follows the two men from behind.}

Cole Porter: Somebody needs to come out here and stop this! The match is over!

{The Destroyer rolls out of the ring and slowly follows the other three as they make their way to the stage. John Adams grabs Hudman by the throat and walks to the left side of the stage. The Prophet is about to lift him into the air when The Destroyer gets to the stage.}

Cole Porter: No, don't do this!

{John Adams turns towards The Destroyer while holding onto Hudman. The Destroyer runs at John Adams and tries to hit him. Adams moves out of the way and The Destoyer runs into Hudman, knocking him off of the stage a through a table.}

Cole Porter: Oh my god!

{John Adams and Richard Rush walk away laughing as The Destroyer climbs down to check on Hudman.}

Cole Porter: Is Hudman alright?

{The Destroyer walks up to Hudman who is screaming in pain and clutching his back.}

The Destroyer: Somebody get out here! We need some help!

{The Destroyer continues to yell for help while trying to help Hudman.}

{The camera fades to black as the footage ends. The camera then changes back to the studio with Cole Porter.}

Cole Porter: Well, that's it. This year has been great for the company but there is no doubt that this coming year will be just as good if not better. With the current alignment of Richard Rush and Hudman and the brothers, John Adams and Big Bad John, teaming up to go up against, one can't help but wonder where this is going to go and what effect it is going to have on EPW. You have just witnessed the Evolution of EPW but it has just begun. Don't let it pass you by.

{The Aggression theme song, "Liberate" by Disturbed plays as the camera zooms out and then fades to black as the show ends.}
