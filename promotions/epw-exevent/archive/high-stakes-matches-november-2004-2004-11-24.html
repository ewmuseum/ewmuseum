High Stakes: matches (November 2004)

24 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.


Deadly Draw World Title Match
Chapel vs. Tom Conway vs. Ace vs. Madison Killjoy

After a many controversies surrounding the World Title within the last few weeks, John Adams made the decision that the only way to settle things was to put together a four-way match. This won't be any normal match though, it will be a Deadly Draw Four-Way Match. The way things work is that two people will start off the match in the ring and if there is no winner before the five minute time limit is reached, a third participant will then enter the match. If after another five minutes, a decision is still not rendered, the last participant in the match will enter. After all four participants are in the ring the match will continue until a decision is rendered. What this means is that the match could end before two of the participants in the match even enter. The match goes with the theme of the event going on the lines that the luck of the draw will give an advantage. The question is, will luck be enough?
Ladder Match
John Adams vs. Psycho Dan

Over the past few weeks, Psycho Dan has caused chaos all over the place in EPW. He has gone to great lengths to make everyone's lives a living hell. Why? Because he believes that he is the rightful owner of EPW. After purchasing stock which gave him 1% ownership of the company, Dan has gone around acting as if he owns the company. Taking exception to his actions, John Adams has tried to take care of Dan by putting him in various matches but due to Dan's continuous efforts has Adams has thus decided to take matters into his own hands. John Adams decided that the only way to settle this was to set up a match where if Psycho Dan did not come out the winner, he would have to give up his stock. This match being the ladder match. In this match, the person who climbs up the ladder and grabs the briefcase suspended above the ring will be the winner and thus end the feud once and for all.
