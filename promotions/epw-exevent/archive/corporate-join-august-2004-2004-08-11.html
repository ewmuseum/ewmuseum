What this is The fed’s own Join page: how to apply as a created singles wrestler or tag team. The group mail address given for sending applications is removed under the privacy rule.

Below is information on how to join Evolution Pro Wrestling.

Before joining EPW, there are a view things that you should take care of.

First of all, it is recommended that you read the rules and expectations of the federation to get an understanding of how things are run here.

Secondly, take a look at the roster page before you try to join to make sure that the your character's name isn't already taken. Evolution Pro Wrestling only accepts applications as created wrestlers. To join EPW as a created singles wrestler or a created tag team, click the appropriate link below. Make sure that you fill out all of the required information if you join as a created wrestler. You should either download the text file, fill it out and send it via email to [detail withheld] as an attatchment or you could copy and paste into an email, fill it out and then send it.
