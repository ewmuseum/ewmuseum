WARFARE: A SPECIAL NIGHT
This week, Warfare will be broadcast live at the location of the High Stakes pay-per-view at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas, Nevada.

On this special occasion, Warfare will provide an inside look into the pay-per-view during the second hour of the event. We will provide backstage interviews with EPW superstars before and after their matches as well as a very special main event match that will take place in a stage built in the arena parking lot.

For a full preview of the event, click here.


June 19, 2004

King Hardkore held the EPW Hardcore Title for a total of 131 days, but due to various strange rulings, had only one successful title defense.
