Aggression results, 6 August 2004 (introduction segment)

6 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EPW Aggression - August 6, 2004
ALLTEL Arena - North Little Rock, Arkansas



Introduction
[A special opening video plays, chronicling the past two Toy Chest Matches as well as showing the events leading up to tonight’s three title matches. The video ends and then the normal opening footage is shown, while the theme music to Aggression plays in the background. As the video ends, the camera shows the entrance stage as fireworks begin to go off. The camera then begins to pan around the electric crowd as the announcers speak.]

Cole Porter: We are live tonight from the ALLTEL Arena in North Little Rock, Arkansas for the Aggression event of the year!

Erik Channel: Yeah, it is going to be great! Three title matches!

Cole Porter: That’s right, the first of which will be Tom Conway vs. Ricky Summers for the EPW Legacy Title.

[The camera changes focus to the announce table as the fireworks die down.]

Erik Channel: You mean “The Perfect Fraud”.

Cole Porter: Well, that’s what Conway says. Tom Conway personally attacked the character of Summers on a recent occasion and then challenged him to a match for the Legacy Title tonight on Aggression.

Erik Channel: Yeah and when Conway is done with him, he will send him back to the Pop Warner League or wherever the hell he played ball.

Cole Porter: I thought you didn’t like Conway anymore.

Erik Channel: I don’t agree with him siding with Chapel and all of that but I guess a little bit of the old Tom is still there.

Cole Porter: Unfortunately.

Erik Channel: Yeah, for Summers.

Cole Porter: Last week on Aggression, Khaos won a Battle Royal, giving him a shot at the Intercontinental Title and tonight he will get his shot when he faces Ace one-on-one.

Erik Channel: Yeah, I can’t wait to see the drunk versus the demon guy or whatever the hell he is.

Cole Porter: The final title match of the evening will take place in a Toy Chest Match. The World Title will be on the line when Chapel faces Ricky Summers in the third Toy Chest Match in EPW history and the first ever to take place on Aggression.

Erik Channel: Yeah, I am actually looking forward to that match. There is no way that Chapel is going to be able to cheat in this match and he will finally be exposed for the fraud he is. Just like Ricky Summers was.

Cole Porter: Well, I don’t know if I agree with you.

Erik Channel: Just admit it Cole, you know that it’s true.

Cole Porter: All three titles will be on the line tonight, but there is much more as well. Stay tuned to find out.
