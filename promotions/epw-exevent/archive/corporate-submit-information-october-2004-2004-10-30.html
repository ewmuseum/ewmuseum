Corporate: Submit Information (October 2004)

30 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The fed’s own guidelines for members sending in news items and segments.

Below are the guidlines that should be followed when submitting information.

SUBMIT NEWS
If you would like to send in news about your superstar or have an idea for another superstar, there are a few guidelines that you must follow.

1. Don't send in a bunch of news everyday.

2. Make sure that your submission is somewhat intellegent and isn't something ridiculous.

3. Don't get mad if your news isn't posted.

If you still don't understand how you should submit news, here is an example of what it could look like.

"As you all have probably heard by now, reports have been indicating that Mick Foley will not be working this year's WWE Summerslam PPV, this August. Many were also saying he would headline the PPV with none other than Triple H. Foley is on tour for his book, Tietam Brown which will continue on for two more months. With him being on tour, he will not be able to get back into wrestling shape. Although he won't be at Summerslam this year, some are saying that he will be at WrestleMania XX. The one question that remains to be unanswered is who his opponent will be." - From NoDQ.com

SUBMIT SEGMENT
If you would like to submit a segment to be placed within one of the EPW events, there are a few guidelines that you must follow.

1. Please don't send fifty-six thousand segments per show. Usually one or two is good, or maybe a segment that continues throughout the night.

2. Make sure that it is at least somewhat intellegent. Don't send an email saying that you want to run around the arena shooting people with paintball guns while eating a jar of pickles.

3. Don't send the same angle in every show.

4. Don't cry like a three-year old baby at an all-you-can-eat resturant who just dropped his lollipop and sat on a thumbtack if your angle isn't put in the show. Sometimes there isn't enough space, they are sent too late, or they just aren't any good.

5. Make sure that your email has all the information needed to insert the segment into the show. For example, if you go in detail with what you want to do on the show, then there is a greater chance that your segment will be added to the show.
