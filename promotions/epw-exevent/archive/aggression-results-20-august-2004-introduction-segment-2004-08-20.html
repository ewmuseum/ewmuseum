Aggression results, 20 August 2004 (introduction segment)

20 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EPW Aggression - August 20, 2004
Skyreach Centre - Edmonton, Alberta, Canada



Introduction
[The opening video for Aggression plays and the theme song can be heard in the background as the show begins. As the video ends, the camera focuses on the entrance stage as fireworks begin to go off. The camera then starts the pan around the crowd as the announcers begin to speak.]

Cole Porter: We are live here tonight from the Skyreach Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. We are north of the border here in Canada.

Erik Channel: Unfortunately.

Cole Porter: I am sorry that you feel that way, but we are going to be around Canada for the next two weeks or so for some huge events, including the Breaking Point pay-per-view this Sunday!

Erik Channel: Forget about Sunday for a minute; tell us what is scheduled for tonight.

[The camera changes focus to the announce table.]

Cole Porter: Well, scheduled for tonight is a match between Khaos and Alias to begin with. This match was set up after Alias accidentally threw a handful of salt into the face of Khaos. It should be an interesting match seeing as how Khaos has a huge size advantage over Alias. It will be interesting to see what Alias’ strategy is against this man.

[Cole Porter slides a piece of paper over to Erik Channel.]

Erik Channel: What’s this?

Cole Porter: Don’t you want to announce the next scheduled match?

Erik Channel: What? Is that what this is?

Cole Porter: Yeah, go ahead.

[Erik Channel picks up the piece of paper and reads it.]

Erik Channel: After the assault on Total Mayhem by Shawn Singleton a match has been set up, pitting him against the tag team in a handicap match.

Cole Porter: The third match scheduled for the card is not actually a match, but a contest. It’s a Football Passing Challenge between Tom Conway and Ricky Summers. It is the second in the series of three and if Tom Conway wins, he will win the series.

Erik Channel: Yeah, and Summers will admit that he is in fact a fraud.

Cole Porter: Well, the other match scheduled for the show is the main event Kyle Pearl will take on Ace.

Erik Channel: Speaking of Ace, what is going on with him and this Clarence Carmichael guy? More importantly, what is going on with him and Chapel? More importantly than that, what is going on with Chapel and Hudman?

Cole Porter: Calm down. All of those questions will be answered tonight. At least I hope they will.

Erik Channel: There is just so much craziness going on lately.

Cole Porter: There sure has been. Stay tuned.
