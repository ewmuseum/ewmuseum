DETAILS ON WARFARE TELEVISION DEAL
Details on the television deal that EPW has signed with the WGN Superstation for Sunday Night Warfare have finally become available. The deal was signed back in the beginning of June but it wasn't until now that the details ahve become public.

  • Warfare will air on Sunday Nights at 10/9 EST.
  • Warfare will be broadcast live on Sundays in which there is also an EPW pay-per-view.
  • EPW may use any and all footage from the show aired on the WGN Superstation on any other television show or product in which they see fit.
  • The first show will air on Sunday, June 13, 2004.
  • If at any time EPW cannot produce a Warfare show on it's scheduled time slot, EPW must provide suitable alternative programming.
  • If advance warning of four or more weeks is given, WGN Superstation may allow EPW to reschedule the show to a special time for the week specified.
  • If at any time EPW cannot produce a suitable program or cannot receive the ratings expected from the WGN Superstation, the contract may be terminated.
  • Any contract negotiations may not begin until the last 30 days of the specified contract.
  • The contract is valid through February 29, 2005, but the WGN Superstation may cancel the show and terminate the contract if it is apparent that any of the terms of agreement have been violated.
