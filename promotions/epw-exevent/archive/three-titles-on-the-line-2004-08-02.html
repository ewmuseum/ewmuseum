THREE TITLES ON THE LINE
Aggression will be live this week from the ALLTEL Arena in North Little Rock, Arkansas for a show that could quite possibly be the best yet. On this week's show, Maize will compete against Chris Clarance in a Singles Match. Both men will be looking for a win in order to boost themselves up in the rankings.

Four wrestlers will debut in a single match when the team of Total Mayhem go up against two other debuting superstars, Alias and Kyle Pearl.

This past Sunday on Warfare, Tom Conway challenged Ricky Summers to a match for the Legacy Title. The match has been booked and thus "The Pefect Package" will face "The Con" for the newly created Legacy Title.

In the second title match of the night, Ace will defend his Intercontinental Title against Khaos. Last week on Aggression, it was Khaos who won a 4-Man Battle Royal, which gave him the chance to face Ace. Will Ace be able to hold onto his title while struggling to hold onto his sanity?
