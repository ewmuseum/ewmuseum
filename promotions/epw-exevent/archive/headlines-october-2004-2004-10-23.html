DESTROYER TO RETURN TO EPW?
Rumor has it that former EPW World Champion, The Destroyer may be returning to the company very soon. It has been rumored that he has been in discussions with EPW officials over the last few weeks and could sign a deal as soon as next week.

We will keep you posted on any updated information we receive regarding The Destroyer's return.


August 21, 2004
WE NEAR THE BREAKING POINT
This week on Aggression, the Breaking Point card for this Sunday was finalized with such matches as Tom Conway vs. Ricky Summers in a Kicking Challenge and Ace vs. Chapel for the World Title being added to the event.

Other than serving as a promotion for Breaking Point, this week's Aggression offered up answers to recent questions, but more were brought up as well.

August 20, 2004
TWO DAYS BEFORE BREAKING POINT
This week's Aggression will take place from the Skyreach Centre in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada just two days away from the big Breaking Point event.

Other than the further defining of the Breaking Point card, much more is scheduled to take place this week.

After Alias accidentally hit Khaos with a handful of salt to the face last week, a confrontation between the two is scheduled to take place this week to settle the score. It must also be in the back of Khaos' mind that he is set to defend his title against both Hudman and Shawn Singleton just two days later.

The next match scheduled for the show is yet another match that was made after what happened on last week's event. Shawn Singleton assaulted Total Mayhem for seemingly no reason and thus a Handicap Match was made to allow them a chance at redemption. Shawn Singleton may have gotten himself in too deep going up against two men that are so much larger than he.
