FIRST SHOW ON USA NETWORK IS A SUCCESS
The first Aggression show on the USA Network turned out to be a rousing success. The show featured many matches, including the main event match between Ace and Madison Killjoy.

Psycho Dan got on John Adams' bad side and thus earned himself a Steel Cage Match against Shawn Mathers on the debut edition of Warfare.

Ace and Chapel continued their gambling ways, but this time raising the stakes with each other so to speak.

Would the bet pay off, or are the odds against him?

To find out, click here.


June 11, 2004

Big Bad John and John Adams are half-brothers. Both men shared a father, who passed away in the fall of 2003.
