Tonight I Get Mine!

15 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up in the locker room of Jason Slade, he is scene sitting on a chair and resting his arms on the back of the chair, he has a distraught look on his face, it seems as him he is thinking of something. Something must be on his mind because he brow is beading with sweat and his hand begins to rub his chin. Amylee is seen putting stuff into a suitcase, a couple shirts, a pair of pants, and other miscellaneous items. Amylee speaks up] Amylee: Jason are you okay? You seem a little out of it. [Jason still staring off into the distance ignores what Amylee is saying] Amylee: Jason? [She walks up to Jason and starts to snap her fingers in his face, Jason shakes his head and looks up at her] Jason Slade: Oh! What's up baby? [Walking back to her suitcase] Amylee: You okay, you have been staring at the same spot on the floor for over an hour now. Jason Slade: Yeah I'm cool, it's just I've gotten off to a really rocky start here in EPW. I mean last night I really blew it when I walked under that damn ladder, I'm so stupid, I guess that crazy hocus pocus shit is really true! [Amylee laughs as Jason leans back in his chair stretching his arms] Amylee: Well it's okay, don't you have a match tonight on Warfare? Jason Slade: Yeah I'm going up against some no name wrestler trying to make it up in the ranks, so John Adams stick him in a match with me. I'm just hoping I can a win out of my bag of tricks tonight. Amylee: Well baby, be honest with yourself, in your last two matches you have dominated and you lost to a fluke, I mean Xian was getting owned by you all throughout the match and then you just happened to trip and fell right into Xian finisher. And hell last night Kable just flat out cheated! [Jason smirks, as Amylee throws down her shirt] Amylee: I mean he exposed the turn buckle and Tony Mount saw it. He smashed your head in exposed steel, what kind of shit is that? [Jason gets up trying to calm down Amylee] Jason Slade: Amy, it's okay, I'm not worried about all of that, I mean of course its hitting my Breaking Point, but no to less Kable cheated yes, he with the FBI, who gives a flying f***. All I want is to get out in that ring tonight and take my revenge out on this jobber John hired to take me on. I will take out my frustrations on his sorry ass tonight. [Amylee looks shocked] Amylee: But what if he is like seven foot tall, and four hundred pounds?? You have no idea who this guy is, doesn't that scare you Jason? Jason Slade: Scare me? No! I've been through hell with giants in the past, I've been through hell with crazies. Whatever and whoever it is he will go down tonight! [Jason walks to the bathroom and looks into the mirror] Jason Slade: Next Sunday is Breaking Point, and I want a match, I want to be on a pay per view event, because I deserve it dammit. Sure I've only been here for two weeks, but I've takin Xian to the limit and I pretty much had Kable crying for me to stop showing him up. I am going to confront Mr. Adams and demand a match for Breaking Point. Amylee: But what if he doesn't give you a match Jason? [Jason looks up into the mirror and smiles] Jason Slade: Oh he will, I guarantee it! [The scene fades out with an evil smile on Slade's face, not the cocky playboy look we are used to seeing, but I believe tonight Slade has some bad intensions for his opponent] fade out
