The End Draws Near!!!!

25 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The End Draws Near!! {The scene opens up to a deserted apartment house building on top of a rocky hill overlooking the ocean in the middle of the night. The building looks like it hasn’t been touched for years. The walls surrounding the building are old and cracked with age. The night sky is not undisturbed for long as a motorcycle comes into view. The bright moon light glistens of the hog as it drives to the abandon building.} {The driver steps of the bike after it stops in front of the building. The driver has been known by many as a gladiator, phenomena, unstoppable, brother, and friend. But this Sunday as he goes for his newest obstacle he is baffled. The man’s name is John Adams of EPW. And the obstacle is Psycho Dan in a scaffold match. But the question still remains, “Why a Scaffold Match?” Does he have a death wish? Does he like the pain and torture? And why has he chosen me? Was it that I took away his pathetic 1% stock? Was it because I tormented his lawyer J.B. Daniels? Or was the answer in here? Inside this abandon building.} {John Adams goes inside the building. The inside is dark, gloomy, and very dimly lit. But to the right north end corner of the building is a lit area. As we move closer to the lit area that is inviting us into the foretelling of the destruction that is coming this Sunday, we hear the muffled movement coming from the light.} {When we arrive into the full light of the north right end of the building, it is revealed that a man is tied and gagged with a oil lamp as the only thing to protect him from the terror of the night. John Adams walks into the light as if he was greeting an old friend.} John Adams: So Preacher Ledge I assume? How are you doing? Comfortable, can I get you a pillow? Ledge: Hmphng! John Adams: What was that Ledge? Yes I will destroy the career of Psycho Dan this Sunday! And yes I will send him straight to hell!! {The muffled scream was so loud that the air from the yell almost put out the flame of the Oil Lamp. John Adams lets out a devilish grin.} John Adams: I want to thank you Ledge for the information you gave me about Psycho Dan! Believe me it will come in handy this Sunday. Ledge: Hmmmmmm!!!!! John Adams: What was that Ledge? Yes you are right, life isn’t fair. As you can see Ledge you can never be in EPW, and Psycho Dan will never see the light of a living day! {A laugh befalls Adams uncontrollable showing Ledge that Adams has truly lost it. The fear Ledge is experiencing is unmanageable as he is scared what might happen next.} John Adams: Psycho Dan has no idea what I have in stored for him, and I have you to thank Ledge. The end is near for the Psychotic one! I have destroyed the careers of some of the best in the business, Hudman, Richard Rush, Show Stopper, The Destroyer, and soon your little friend Dan is going to be no different! {John Adams pulls out a bag from the darkness. John Adams slowly passes the moving bag pass Ledge as to taunt him on his upending doom.} John Adams: I have a friend for you to meet Ledge! I felt sorry for leaving you here all by yourself so I brought you a friend to play with! This way you won’t be so board! {John Adams pulls a long slender snake out of the bag. The snake hisses at Ledge as Ledge begins to scream.} John Adams: Now you don’t want to do that! You may scare him. {John Adams walks closer to Ledge who is screaming hysterically.} John Adams: Hey Ledge! Does this Snake look sleepy to you? He does? Well will you sleep with him? Ledge: HMPHMMM!!!!!!! John Adams: You will! Great, thanks a bunch Ledge! {John Adams sets the rosy boa down on Ledges lap as he heads to the light.} John Adams: Goodnight you two don’t let the bed bugs bite! {John Adams blows out the light as he heads outside the building. When he gets on his bike the screams of Ledge get louder as John Adams takes off in his bike. The end is near.} {The scene fades to black.}
