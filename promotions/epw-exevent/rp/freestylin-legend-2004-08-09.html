Freestylin' Legend

9 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens with a TV viewing the Jay Leno show] Jenna: I'm so glad I get to see my baby on TV [The camera goes to the TV] Jay Leno: And now introducing the newest addition to the EPW Roster..Alias! [The scene goes to the set of Jay Leno] Jay: How are you Alias? Alias:. Please call me Shane, and I am great..the career with EPW is going all well despite a loss last week at Aggression. Jay:Yeah, I saw that..any thoughts on the upcoming match that you have with this Skull guy? Alias: It should be an entertaining match, I have some thoughts on him already, I have not seen him, nor heard about him yet. Jay: Well that's good lessens the stature of the competition that could be upcoming. Alias: Ahh..not quite I would like a challenge win or lose, it is just always fun to have a challenge. Wow, that name Skull, doesn't it sound like a rapper's name or something?.. [Jay begins to laugh] Jay: Well, now that I think of it, that might just be true. Alias: What band is playing here tonight? Jay: A band called Taking Back Sunday, their new CD just came out, they are trying to promote it. Alias:Uh, well cancel them, I will pay them for whatever, I'd like to perform tonight. [Alias winks at Jay] Jay:Shane, you are a sick man..but hey I guess I have to let you. [Alias walks over to the stage and begins to talk to the lead singer of Taking Back Sunday] Alias: Hey Adam, I am a big fan of you guys and all but I'm sorry to say that you won't be playing tonight Adam: Dude..Damn..how much you payin' us? Alias: We will work that out later, as for now I will give you guys a little plug for your new CD, great job on it by the way. Adam: Thanks bro.. [Alias grabs a mic off the stage] Alias:This is a remdtion I like to call.. "SKuLL"... [Alias begins to Rap] Alias: Yo yo!..this is Alias in the house!! Alias: Well my name's Skull and I got a big fro.. Alias: I think ill beat Alias at Aggression but I don't really know.. Alias: Alias might use a chair, and mess up my hair.. Alias: Alias might put me through a table, and that might hurt. Alias: He might make me cry, but ill just say it was a lie... Alias: He may embarrass me, then I might go Pee.. Alias: shouting - IN MY PANTS! Alias: But in the end, Alias will pin me.. 1..2..3 Alias: Thank you thank you!!..I'm Alias the Freestylin' Legend.. [Crowd busts out laughing at Alias] Jay Leno: Shane, you are the biggest fool ever... Alias: Ahh, it was fun though, maybe it will send out a message, this is how worried I am about the match at Aggression. I am confident that this character.."Skull" will be nothing but a joke, just like that rap. Skull, are you ready to witness the most extreme man in EPW?..I hope your watching. Jay Leno: Well that's all the time we have for you Shane, I hope to have you back again man, you are a riot. Alias: Thanks Jay, it was a pleasure I'll be sure to have a chat with you after I beat Skull at Aggression. Jay Leno: Good Luck man, and next on the show we have Tom Cruise with scenes from his new film Collateral. [Alias walks out of the building and hops into his GTO as his cell phone rings] Alias: Hello? Jenna: Shane, I am dying, you are killing me. Alias: What!? Jenna: Your rap sweetheart Alias: Oh, that was kinda funny wasn't it [Alias laughs] Jenna: You totally made a fool of yourself but I still love you.. Alias: Alright sweetheart I am on my way home, love you bye. [Alias hangs up phone and begins his drive home] [Fade Out]
