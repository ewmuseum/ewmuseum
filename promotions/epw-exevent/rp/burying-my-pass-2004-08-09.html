Burying My Pass

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name: Maize Location: 18th street in Chicago,IL Date:august 9,2004 2:45AM Burying My Past Prelude An underdog.... What a sick excuse of a label to call yourself. This term orignated from some asswipe who needed an excuse to lose.So that, in the rare case he may win, he can try to use that to give him major push and exploitation. Definitions are as followed: 1.One that is expected to lose a contest or struggle, as in sports or politics. 2.One that is at a disadvantage. Two out of the two definitions isn't a bad way to describe yourself Kyle Pearl. But what is worse is that you don't try to improve yourself. You just sit at home and waiste away like those cans of corn in your kitchen cabnit. Or that brand of ceral your wife bought that no one never eats. So it just sits there till the "best by" date expires. Well that's exacutally what you are doing. You are just sitting on the couch bitching and moaning to yourself like it's going to change anything. Then you fuss about how you were born in the streets, and that you had a bad life growing up!!! Well Kyle Pearl,you are not the only person in this company that has been through some shit and hard times in life. YOU ARE JUST THE ONLY BASTARD WHO HASN'T MOVED ON! The Scene [ The scene shows Maize walking around a rugged,unprepossessing nieborhood in Chicago.Maize is wearing a whit shirt with blue shaggy jeans and some Temberland boots. The camera zooms into a street sign. It said 18th, prehaps one of Chicago's more dangerous areas. Maize continues walking untill he is stopped by a skinny young man with a red bandana around his head. The young mans eyes are as red as blood and his lips are as black as tar. His hair,as you can see from the top, are nappy braids that go criscross. He grabs Maize by the shirt and then suddenly he falls to one knee and vomits on Maize's boots. Maize face looks distraught other than antagonizing. Maize shakes his head and pull the young man's arm over his shoulders,and assists him in walking.] Young Man:(Sounding weak and anxious) You got some money for me dawg.....I need to buy some dru...I mean some medicine for my minor cold...(coughs in a very sick manor) Maize:Man it looks like you have more than a cold. You need a doctor. Besides, what have you been doing with the money I've been giving to you. I pray to God you weren't still buying "Crack" with it, was you?! Young man:...Naw dawg.....Listen dawg. I'm clean now dawg. Can you see it. Dawg, I've been putting that money in church man.(starts to sing in an awful voice,R.Kelly-he save me)"He....Saved me...Ohh. Gave me a second chance... He sa.... [The young man vomits again, but this time he is able to turn his head opposite of Maize. Maize stops walking so the young man can get a chance to get it all out.] Maize: Yo listen cuz,[The young man turns out to be Maize's cousin Milton Mabin Jr.]I got to get you to a hospital man. You don't look to good at all. C'mon! [Maize tries to put Milton's arm back on his should but Milton snatches it away.] Milton Mabin Jr.: Man, I don't need no damn doctor. I am clean man! You don't believe your own cousin dawg.(Coughs. You can see little drops of blood on his ashy lips). Maize: Jesus Christ! You have been doing Crack! You really need a medic! Milton Mabin: Dawg, what I need is you to give me some more money....C'mon......I owe "Big Balla" money! Even you couldn't match up to Big Balla..... Now is my chance to bury my past, other than digging it up and letting it take over my soul. Yes, compared to Big Balla I "WAS" an underdog. But things have changed....As matter of fact "I" have changed! It was time I did something about my past. Not for myself Kyle, for one in need. Thats where I draw my powers from. Those in need of entertainment to get away from there problems. Those in need of dire help. Those in need of a breakthrough!! Innocent people falling victim to men who have nothing to do with there life but to threaten the welfare of others!! In my point of view you and Big Balla were the same kind of people. A DOG WITH NO BARK NOR BITE. Just lame backgrounds that make them seem tough!!!!! Like a pitbull,it just stares, it doesn't even have to move to scare a innocent by stander.The by stander is scared because he or she has heard bad things about pitbulls! But inside of that pitbull is a little bitchy ass mutt,namely Kyle Pearl! You claim you are from the streets of Harlem. You use that as proof you can survive in a street match and as an excuse why you are the way you are. Let me tell you a little about me. Not Maize,but Carrington Banks!! I was born in the streets Chicago,Il. Namely 17th street. I use to sell drugs for Big Balla when I was 16. The first person I sold to was my cousin Milton. He got addicted. I was in debt to Big Balla because I always gave Milton free drugs. I never could find a way to make enough money. I was married and had a kid when I was 19. I worked in as a camera man for "Fact or Fiction". Later on when I was 22, I was fired because I powerbombed one of the white staff members for calling me a useless "Nigga". My uncle Michael Banks found that out and introduced me to the owner of TAMW. He said I can get the 200,000 dollars plus more and I could pay off Big Balla. Thats why I started wrestling. So I can pay off a debt. But if it wasn't for those trail I wouldn't be wrestling right now and I wouldn't be a millionair. And I thank God everyday I was put through that situation! Now today was that day when my pass comes back with revengance.Now It was up to me to let it haunt me for the rest of my life like Kyle Pearl. Or bury it forever.... So it was my time to approach a well known Drug Lord named Big Balla. TO BURY MY PAST, and for my cousin's FUTURE! [Maize has a scowl on his face as he remembers Big Balla. He walks across the street to his $52,000 Presidental Lincoln. He carries Milton with him. They get in the car and with one angry step on the peddal, Maize peels out! The scene arrives at what looks like an abondoned house. Maize signals for Milton to stay in the car as walks up to the building.There are two big bouncers on each side of the door. Both are no were as built as Maize. Maize peers into both of their eyes and walks in unannounce. When he walks in A big black guy in his early 40's about 6'10 and 345lb of muscle is just about to sit down in a chair behind his desk while he is talking on his cell. As soon as he sees Maize he stops and stands fully up, hangs up his cell and begins to smile.......] Big Baller:(Speaking in 3rd person)Well look who has come back to pay Big Balla a visit! Little Carrington! How many years it been, Two,Three? Maize:(Smiling)You know Big Asswipe, that would of been kind of intemidating and funny 9 years ago. But now it's just fucking pathetic!!! I am here to pay Milton's debt with my fist!! [Big Baller then swipes all the mess off his desk in anger. He then walks in Maizes face. Maize peers into his eyes with a cocky smile on his face.] Big Balla: Looks like your little ass gained a dirty mouth since the last time we talked....(smiles) You see how your cousin is doing thanks to you.(starts to laugh) It's all because of you!! Maize: It is because of punk ass pussy like you who intemidate young kids so they can so your bidding....I am here to stop that. Meet me outside bitch!!!! [Big Balla picks Maize up and throws him outside the door and starts to kick him down the sidewalk. Soon it begins to rain and people start to gather around. Milton has a look of consern on his face as he looks out Maize's car window.] Big Balla: Never sass me again boy.(kicks Maize again) [Before Big Balla can kick Maize another time, Maize grabs his feet tripping Big Balla off his feet with the help of the wet sidewalk. Maize stands up and take off his shiry revealing his wet,ripped, and well trimmed body. Maize waits for Big Balla to get on his feet. Maize then approaches Big Balla and Punches him in the gut multiple times. Maize then cock hit fist back and punches Big Balla in the jaw so hard that Balla spits out blood.] Maize: Whats wrong now. I thought you owned 17th street huh? Looks like you are getting your....Ugh!! [Big Baller smacks Maize in his face with a pistol causing the long scar on Maize's eye to bleed. The blood mixed with the rain makes it look like Maize is crying blood.People start to spread out screaming and running. Maize then throws a hay maker and hits Balla. Balla hits the ground and rolls into the street and quickly gets up.The gun that was in his hand slides by maize's car. Maize runs to him at full speed but as soon as he gets close ,Big Balla pulls out another gun and points it at Maize. But Maize does not stop and a gun shot is heard....] Damn....who would of ever thought it would of ended like this..... [The camera is focused in on Maize's face.The rain and blood drips down Maize's face like water on the windsheild of a car when it is raining. Maize's eyes are bucked and they looks down then look back up. Sirens are heard in the back ground as the camera slowly back up. Big Balla is on the ground shaking and barely alive with his gut spilling blood like it's a pitcher pouring red cool-aidin some little kids glass. Also there is Milton almost 10 feet away from the almost dead body, holding pistol in his hand. it seems that Milton picked the pistol up when it slid by Maize's car. Not to long after the police come out and snatches Milton and puts him in the police car. As Milton is pushed into the car he looks at Maize. Milton mouths move and it looks like he is saying "Thank You".The police car drives off. Maize then looks up at the crying night sky and smiles. It suddenly stops raining. Maize walks to his car not paying attention to non of his surrounds. He just smiles and enters his car and drives off. If we keep hope and endure God will find ways to work things out. See Kyle, I bet anything you don't understand why I was smiling after all that happened. I was smiling because now my Cousin is off the streets and can finally get the treatment he deserve. Big Balla probaley survived but he will spend the rest of his life in jail. I will pay for Milton's Rehabilitation programs and lawyer. If push comes to shove I will also pay for his bail. After two long weeks I can finally go home to my caring wife and son. I can also shoot another episode of a "Jobber's Life". As for the rest of my anger inside......I'm saving that for Friday, so I can unleash it on YOU....KYLE PEARL!!!!! {End of Scene}
