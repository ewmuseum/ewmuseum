So it is writen, so it shall come to pass...

27 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*the screen flickers at first, showing distorted images and static, every once in a while a singular word appears but it disappears again before we have a chance to read it, the screen continues to flicker… eventually the image comes clear and we see a pitch black room with a single spot-light shining down, we hear footsteps in the background coming closer, the person steps into the light and we see that it is Chapel, he is wearing a dark red suit and tie, his blood red hair is combed back and he is wearing a pair of red tinted sun-glasses… he looks very professional, and a little bit scary…* Chapel: The Dead Sea Scrolls… *An image of an old worn book appears briefly on screen* Chapel: Nostradamus… *An image of the great ‘soothsayer’ appears briefly before us* Chapel: Coleman Dowel and John Adams… *Both ‘Prophets’ appear on screen* Chapel: *smirks* Hell, even Crystal balls, Tarot cards and the Magic 8 ball… *All three objects appear before us on screen* Chapel: All these things and people can apparently do the same thing, they can ‘Foresee the Future’… I can tell you all one thing though… *the camera does a fast zoom on Chapel, focusing on his face* …They didn’t see this one coming. Whispered Voice: (OS) "Legacy" *the word Legacy flashes on screen* *cut to Chapel sat in a black leather chair under the spotlight, he has his legs crossed and his fingers peaked together under his nose* Chapel: We have the power… *cut’s to a different camera angle of Chapel* We have the attitude… *cut’s to another different camera angle of Chapel* Soon we will have the belts… *cuts back to the first camera angle* And soon, you will all have no where to hide… Whispered Voice: (OS) "Legacy" *the word Legacy flashes on screen* *cut to Chapel shuffling his trademark park of cards… he draws three cards* Chapel: This Sunday I have a triple threat Toy Chest match for the World and IC titles… In this match I * he holds a card up to the camera, It is the "King of Hearts" he places it back into the deck* not only have to face the fool that is Khaos *holds another card up to the camera, It is one of the "Jokers" he places it back into the deck* but I also face the "Wild Card" that’s is Madison Killjoy *holds another card up to the camera… all that appears on this card is a large red ‘Question Mark’ he places it back into the deck* …I can promise you two only one thing, you will never leave the chest the same man who entered it, this match will test you beyond your limits… I should know, I created it. Whispered Voice: (OS) "Legacy" *the word Legacy flashes on screen* *cut to Chapel standing in the spotlight hands in his pockets* Chapel: The Prophecy has ended, the Legacy has begun… nothing is going to be the same. *The light fades out and the word Legacy fades in, it stays there for a few seconds then is replaced by the numbers 03/28/04 The screen fills with static again*
