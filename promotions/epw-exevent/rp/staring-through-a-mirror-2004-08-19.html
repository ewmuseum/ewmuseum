Staring Through a Mirror

19 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What is the meaning of Aggression anyways? Aggression - The act of initiating hostilities or invasion. The men of EPW do not realize that this is a characteristic that all of them lack. There is one man I can think of in particular..Yes, you guessed it..Khaos. [The scene opens as Alias and Jimmy Janes are sitting in a bench in a park with the lights overhead glaring down on them] Jimmy Janes: Shane..2 weeks in the EPW and you're already fighting one of the big boys of the fed. So I hear they call the guy Khaos, and he is the Intercontinental Champion of EPW. Alias: Yeah, He is quite a superstar in the EPW very well known. It's kind of sad that I am going to have to walk into the ring and kick his pyschotic ass around..Hell, Ill be like that fan who kicked the shit outta him so bad that he called himself Khaos, Ill beat his ass so bad he is gonna refer to himself as Big Bad John again. [Jimmy Janes laughs] Jimmy Janes: I see you've remembered Lesson 1:Never Overestimate the opponent.. Alias: I'll never forget it, Dad. The lessons of the ring stay clear in my mind always..I must be getting home to Jenna though.. Jimmy Janes: Gettin' too good for your old dad eh?.. Alias: Never..I'll give you a call later and we can discuss this whole Khaos thing. Jimmy Janes: Sure enough. [Alias and Jimmy hug and part ways] [Alias jumps into his GTO and starts the engine driving away] Alias: Khaos you really are some sort of freak. You think that I am some sort of pussy, you think that your schizophrenia scares me? I may be a rookie but your scare tactics are senseless and ultimately no threat to me. Khaos you are more pathetic than I had expected, your attempts at using words but ultimately slaughtering them, is obliterate one of those big words you just learned to use and the soverigns of malice..what are you trying to make a desperate attempt at being Gollum from Lord of the Rings? I mean everyone sees the way you look at that half-assed excuse for a title you have..MY PRECIOUS MY PRECIOUS!!! [Alias chuckles to himself] Alias: Khaos, look at you..You are singling out every superstar in the EPW? I mean c'mon bro you can do better than that. It was pretty foolish of you to announce this to everyone, you are going to have to show these men up now, I don't think you have the potential to do that anyways..not to mention show me up. You talk about how your gonna kick my ass all over the ring its a funny matter actually. You act as if you are EPW. You run around barking orders to your brother.."Give me inferno match brother!! I must burn opponents!" You actually think your brother is going to accept to these terms? I will have to die laughing when you get turned down. You were showing more fight when Jule Mercadel was asking you questions about the new dude in EPW. Hell, I bet Jule could even kick your ass! [Alias turns onto the street where his house is located] [Alias sees a big character standing under the light on the corner] Alias: Ooo!..Big Bad John must've come to get me..Hold On I'll give him the directions back to your house so he can go haunt you again..Watch out Khaos, BBJ is gonna choke you out again!..The expression on your face when he did that was priceless. The fear and emotion shown in your face..you looked as if you were gonna cry..I guess I might have to call BBJ out to our match this Friday to teach you a real lesson..Hell.. I dont need the other half of you to win this fight. You are probably scared of the whole roster just as you are scared of BBJ thats why you are acting like you are all hard on..Listen Khaos..None of us cower in fear with your desperate attempts to scare us..your destruction will be set to a fast downfall with the "newcomer" beats your ass all around. Knowing you, you will probably have to go home to your dear Cain and shout some orders at him for you to feel better after getting your ass beat by me. Cain you poor demented soul, you are a slave to this man.. [Alias laughs as he gets out of his car and walks up the stairs] Alias: Ahh..Khaos you shall be the most fun in the ring considering you are already scared shitless by Big Bad John, I am going to love just laying you out cold to have another dream about your other self... [Alias unlocks the door to his room and enters] Jenna: Shane! Alias: Hey baby.. Jenna: Ready to watch movies.. Alias: Sure enough my dear..just one moment I need to do something.. [Alias walks to the bathroom] [Alias looks into the mirror] Alias: Something isn't right here.. *Alias punches the mirror* [The mirror falls to pieces] Alias: Khaos, you see all of the pieces of this mirror on the floor? Khaos, put them all back together how they were before..it's nearly impossible..correct?..Those are the chances of you beating me.. Jenna: Shane, What are you doing!? You know that's seven years bad luck Alias: What is bad luck?..I don't get that..I mean look at the lady I've got here.. [Jenna Hugs Alias] [Fade Out]
