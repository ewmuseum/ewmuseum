Hell's Fury!

22 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hell’s Fury ::::: Everybody has that point of no return, a point where somebody says enough is enough, a point of no return, where even ones sanity is even put to the test. That is the human nature of everybody’s Breaking Point. For this man in particular his breaking point has been building for quite some time. Years of fighting for a cause that resurfaces it’s ugly head every time it gets. His father, Hudman, Richard Rush, Psycho Dan, his brother John Black, and even Chapel all has faced him, and all of them have failed. But once again a new adversary has risen. A warrior, who is cheered and praised, a gladiator, that is respected and admired, and a man, that is well liked and respected. That used to be him that used to be his life long ago. But that time has passed and now that warrior has what does not belong to him in the first place. ::::: 12:30 AM, Saturday ::::: In a hotel near the Canadian Border, room 75 he waits. He waits for the most grueling match of his career Guarida de Diablo Match, The Devils Den. The pinnacle of John Adams and Xian’s career…… two men, one platform and only one survivor as the other is stuck inside the Devils Den. Damnation, purgatory, and Hell……..it’s funny if you think about it, a place to spend all eternity while you pay for your sins. Yes tomorrow is the biggest match alright, but why can’t John Adams get any sleep? ::::: (Guarida de Diablo Match is going to be quite literally hell on earth! The passion that I feel for this sport is by far less than ordinary and at this match I will make my presence known! Just like I have so many times in the past PPV against the best our business has and had to offer. Total Chaos against The Destroyer and my Brother, Last Resort when I faced Hudman, Anarchy when I faced Hudman again and thought I put an end to his career, and Overthrone when I faced Richard Rush and took over this business!) ::::: As “The Prophet” reminisce of days long past he is reminded of one thing, and that is this match. Two of the best competitors in this business today, having one hell of an epic battle where the loser will go straight to hell. John Adams heads to the TV set and turns it on. ::::: {Live this Sunday it's EPW's PPV event Breaking Point!} ::::: That’s the story on everybody’s mind. Come see EPW this Sunday as tempers flare and emotions rise as everybody on the roster finally reaches that Breaking Point. The thought of a relaxing shower comes to mind as Adams starts to make his way to the hotel shower. :::::: (Day in and day out I have fought to bring the very best to EPW and my work will all be decided Sunday in a match. I have never lost a PPV match and I’m not to about to start either. They, the roster of EPW have no idea the importance of this match. I will prevail, I must. Not only for the sake of the fans and everybody on the roster but for EPW it’s self!) ::::: Words that John Adams lives by everyday. Words that inspire him to get up in the morning everyday and deal with guys like Xian. After his shower John Adams heads to the lumpy hard bed that the good people of the hotel has provided and falls asleep. John Adams has to get plenty of sleep for tomorrow not only will he be involved with one hell of a match but his sanity will be put to the test as it reaches its Breaking Point. ::::: {The Scene Fades to Black.}
