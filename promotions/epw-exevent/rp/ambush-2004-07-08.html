Ambush

8 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( Tha camera opens up to Psycho Dan sitting in a brown 1986 Ford 350 Lariat king cab pickup truck parked with the engine running. He is in the underground parking area at the RBC Center, Raleigh, North Carolina sitting in the dark as if awaiting some one). Psycho Dan: This will be real good yah wait till the bastard gets out of his limo and run him down like a dog! This will teach him to mess with me I will show everyone who I really am! This will make John Adams pissed and I like it, soon he will be here soon......Who is this.....no its a limo....not my target.... ( A white limo pulls past the truck and stops infront of the back door area and the passenger door opens and a man and a woman walk out and enter the arena as Dan waits with a sick looking smile on his face). Psycho Dan: Who are these people taking limo's like they own the place or something, naw just big shots who think they are somebody....they too will see what I am in time...must focus on my target..... ( Psycho Dan seems nervous and excited at the same time as he waits for the right person to drive up to the back of the arena. Dan lights a cigar and puffs on it his eyes darting around keeping a watchful eye out). Psycho Dan: Were the hell is he....he is never late! This plan is going to be my best one ever....Someones coming? ( Dan ducks down as a red mini van pulls up and a few wrestlers from EPW's line up step out and start unpacking bags from the back of the van). Psycho Dan: What the hell these guys have no matches tonight? Damnit they better get out of the way when he gets here or I will run em over too... ( The wrestlers gathers there bags and go inside the arena and the mini van drives off and parks out of view, as a Shelby Cobra GT500 rolls up to the back door area and stops, its break lights still on and the driver seems to be gathering something from the passenger seat area. Just as the drivers side door opens Psycho Dan rev's the engine and peels out heading strait towards the drivers side of the car as screams are heard from the parking lot area and the driver of the Shelby Cobra GT500 dives into the passenger area of the car just as the heavy truck side swipes the drivers side door off the car. And the truck excelerates and tears off into the night as witnesses come from the parking lot area and from the back stage area of the arena. They pull the scared driver out of the car and it is Berry Buster Mayor of Raleigh N.C. and ask him if he is ok). Witness: Are you ok Mayor? Berry Buster: Yah who was that maniac? He almost killed me? Witness: I don't know it was dark all I saw were headlights! It looked like a truck and the driver was trying to kill you? Berry Buster: Call the cops!! I want this investigated! ( The scene fades as Psycho Dan pulls the truck to a stop in a near by ally and steps out of the truck, brushing his jacket with black leathered gloves. Dan puffs on his cigar and smiles). Psycho Dan: Lets see Chapel get up from that hehehehe.... ( Psycho Dan gets on his motorcycle and rides off into the darkness thinking he ran down Chapel as the camera fades).
