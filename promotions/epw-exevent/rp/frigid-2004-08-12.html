Frigid.

12 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Vengeance. An unbridled feeling of retribution. The emotion, or act thereof, that precipitates occurrences of violence and spite. Fury. The feeling of uncontrollable anger and vehemence that must be channeled through no other course but immediate, and often, impulsive courses of battle. Revenge. The reprisal of any deed of wrongdoing. The sense of being that encompasses those who suffer from the negative effects and causes of such conducts. Often, coupled with the uncontrollable fury, unrestrained vengeance, and brutal nature of revenge, a man can forego the decision-making process that he would normally succumb to. Intelligent, reasonable, and sympathetic behaviors become null and void. There is nothing more severe than the wrath of the scorned. He who has been turned back has nothing left to lose. Nothing left to leave behind. An uphill battle must be fought by the strong against the weak. Coming back, time and time again, the sign of persistence, determination and vitality…it embodies and fulfills the will of the warrior. He has no recourse left but to continue, not until his own death, but until the death of everyone before him. The spirit of the warrior knows no end. You can’t kill Shawn Singleton. He will kill you. ***** [The mall’s automatic doors slide open, as we are taken inside. The carpet inside the entrance is damp, a little bit displaced from the wet footsteps having trodden on it from the afternoon’s rain. The food court is buzzing with aroma, the french fries mixing with the smell of freshly cooked pizza, hot dogs, and chicken simmering behind the counter of the Chinese food stand. Benjamin Ketchum moves in front of the camera, wearing a black EPW polo shirt with red letters, and a pair of khaki shorts. He looks at the camera.] “I can’t believe I agreed to do this, but…Hello, folks, this is Benjamin Ketchum, reporting from the lovely Iowa City mall, and the question I’m here to ask…who the fans think is going to take home the win in the insane main event tag team double jeopardy title match.” [A young asian woman walks by, wearing a white blouse and blue jeans. Ben awkwardly approaches her, tapping her on the arm, and motioning her towards the camera.] “Ma’am, are you a professional wrestling fan?” [She shakes her head, and walks away. Ben looks at the camera, disappointed.] “Why do I feel like they gave me this assignments just for fun?” [As he finishes his statement, a man rolls past, a bag in his lap. Ben notices the man, turns back to the camera, and does a double-take. He motions for the camera to follow him, as he speeds up his walking pace to find the man who has made his way to the exit.] “Hunter?” [The wheelchair stops outside of the doors, and turns to the side. The man in the chair, The Hunter, addresses Ben.] “What?” [His tone sounds annoyed and cold, but Ben persists, attempting to salvage the trip. He moves forward, rushing to sit on the bench near the entrance, a few feet from The Hunter.] “Can I just get a word with you? I know you and Shawn haven’t done any interviews, and I was just trying to get a quick word with you. Speaking of which, where is Shawn?” [Hunter looks Ben in the eye, his pupils seeming to shrink Ben in half.] “Shawn is at the hotel, preparing.” “Preparing…for what? The match, I presume.” “It’s about way more than just this match, Ben.” [Hunter throws the bag in his lap into the trashcan beside the bench.] “What do you mean?” Ben asks. “You ever watch a caged lion get unleashed in the wild?” [Ben’s facial expression changes, mixing between confusion and bewilderment.] “No.” [Hunter nods, acknowledging the inevitable answer] “Then you have no idea what’s about to happen.” [Hunter turns from Ben, rolling around the corner of the mall. The camera turns back to Ben, still seated on the bench.] “Not one straight answer, and the ones I do get are sick riddles. Um…This is Ben Ketchum, reporting for EPW, take care, folks.” [Ben drops his hands to his knees, slapping them with frustration.] ***** The Hunter’s persona, his demeanor, his heart, his spirit. All cold. Shawn has adopted those techniques and characteristics. Frozen. Remember the old adage? Chapel should. What dish is best served cold? Revenge.
