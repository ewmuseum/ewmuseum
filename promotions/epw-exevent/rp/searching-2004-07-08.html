8 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The camera fades in to Tom Conway, who is sitting on the edge of the bed in his motel room. Conway stares at a yellow flyer that is in his hand. The camera pans around and zooms in on the piece of paper to reveal that it is an old flyer for BDW, featuring Emilio Dervante. The camera begins to zoom out as Tom Conway folds up the piece of paper and slips it into his pocket.} Tom Conway: What time is it? {Tom Conway turns around and looks at the alarm clock that is set on the end table next to the bed. The clock shows that the current time is 1:17PM.} Tom Conway: I only have a few hours to get this done, so I better get going. {Tom Conway stands up and grabs his suitcase which is lying near the edge of the bed. Conway pulls his sunglasses out of his pocket and puts them on. He then walks out of the hotel room and onto the second floor balcony, wearing a pair of jeans and tanktop. Conway walks towards the stairs and then begins to walk down to the ground. The sun shines on his shoulders as he begins to step out from the shade of the building. Conway then walks to the back of the building and towards the parking lot. He stops at a fairly new, black, chevrolet truck and pulls his keys out of his pocket. He then unlocks the door to the truck and places his suitcase on the passenger seat. Conway then climbs into the truck and closes the door. Conway reaches into his pocket and yet again pulls out the BDW flyer.} Tom Conway: It says here that the headquarters is not too far from this area. I should be able to go down there and talk to someone about finding this Emilio guy. {Conway sets the flyer on the suitcase next to him and starts up the truck. Conway backs out of the parking spot and then leaves the motel lot. Conway drives down the street and quickly gets to the building that is referenced on the flyer. He parks on the side of the road, exits the truck and walks up to the building with the flyer in his hand. Conway walks up to the building but doesn't see the BDW logo anywhere.} Tom Conway: What is the deal here? I thought this was the BDW building. {Tom Conway looks at the flyer and looks back at the building address and confirms that they are the same.} Tom Conway: Well, I guess that this is the right building. {Tom Conway opens the door to the building and walks into the lobby. He walks up to the receptionist and shows her the flyer.} Tom Conway: I am looking to find out how to get ahold of this man. {The woman looks at Tom Conway with a surprised look on her face.} Receptionist: You're Tom Conway, aren't you? Tom Conway: Yeah, why? Receptionist: My boyfriend watches EPW. Tom Conway: Cool. Receptionist: So, what did you want? Tom Conway: I am looking for Emilio Dervante or someone who may be able to point me in his direction. {The receptionist hands the flyer back to Conway.} Receptionist: I know that Emilio lives not too far from here. Tom Conway: Oh, really? Receptionist: This is no longer the BDW headquarters but I can write his address down for you. He is one of our clients here. Tom Conway: That's cool. {The receptionist writes down the address of the man onto a piece of paper and hands it over to Conway. Conway takes the paper and looks it over.} Tom Conway: Thanks a lot. {Tom Conway slips the piece of paper into his pocket and then exits the building. On his way out he notices a sign that he didn't see on the way in. The sign reads: "Billman Agency".} Tom Conway: I guess that this is one of those Hollywood agency things. Good for Emilio, he is looking to have his agent set him up with some gigs. I already have my agent. I don't need to go to any agency. {Tom Conway opens the door and leaves the building. He walks up to his truck and enters. Conway looks at the clock and notices that the time is 1:44PM.} Tom Conway: I got to get to this place and get things done before three. {Conway starts up the car and drives to the address that is listed on the piece of paper. He gets to the building and is surprised to see that the house is fairly large and there is a locked gate at the front of the drive-way.} Tom Conway: This guy has got a nice setup here. {Tom Conway stops the truck and steps out. He walks up to the intercom and presses the button to talk. Before he is able to say anything, a ladie's voice is heard over the speaker.} Woman: Who is this? Tom Conway: This is Tom Conway. Woman: I don't know of any Tom Conway. Tom Conway: I need to speak to Emilio about EPW. Woman: I don't know anything about EPW but Mr. Esteves is not home right now. Tom Conway: What? I am looking for Emilio Dervante. I must have the wrong house. Woman: I would say that you do sir. Tom Conway: Shit, I better get going then. {Tom Conway walks up the truck and steps in.} Tom Conway: I guess that I will have to wait until Aggression. I heard that he may be backstage and if he is, I have a little something to discuss with him. {Tom Conway starts up the car and drives off. The camera fades to black.}
