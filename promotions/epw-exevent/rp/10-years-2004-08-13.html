10 years...

13 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Flashback] The blurry memory from nearly 10 years ago. As this boy Shane Carbon walks through the stores of Wal-Mart, he hears gunshots fired. In fear, he runs quickly to where his parents are located only to see his mother lying on the ground and the gunman standing above her with a glock in his hand. The gunman is tackled by Shane's father and the gun flies near Shane's feet, Shane picks up the gun and pushes his father away then firing the glock at this man, shoots him and kills him. His fathers face depicted a story, of life and death. The night of the incident Shane's father had taken his own life. [Flashback] [The sound of rain can be heard as Alias travels in his car, Alias sticks a CD into the CD player. Alias finally arrives to his destination, The sign reads "Saint Louis County Jail"] Alias: Bad things always happen to someone in their life. [Alias walks into the Jail and speaks with the Guard] Guard: Yes Shane I would be glad to take you to him. Alias: Thank You, Jason. Jason: You have 15 minutes. [Alias sits in a small room with another well built man] Alias: Listen, here is the deal man. I have made it big finally in wrestling I have made my way to EPW. I just want to know what you were thinking when you took the lives of those people. My opponent this week is extremely unique, he has a sense of insanity too him, and he is definatly mentally disturbed, so please Jack tell me this. Jack: Shane, you know what happened, I just..shot the kid you know? Alias: Jack, I need this.. Jack: Ok, Shane... It was the night of November 13th, this boy was named Scott Smirenov. He was 18 at the time of the murder. Me and Scott had gotten pretty trashed that night, like me and you had once done. Well, he told me that he had been with my girlfriend when her and I were together, and you know that she meant more than anything to me, it was all in rage bro..I smashed him in the head with a beer bottle then drug him on the back of my car and just left him there. Then, I drove home to my house got out my 12-Gauge and took the life of Jessi. She may of been the world to me but she should have never done that. I will kill again if they ever let me out of here. The feeling I receive from it is amazing.. Alias: Jack, Have you ever heard of a man named Skull, I believe he was in here at one time. Jack: No..wait..I think I might have. It was known that you musn't mess with him if you are here. Even a lot of the crazy black guys ain't mess with him. There was one guy, his name was Antonio, he beat Skull pretty badly, although in the end Skull did kill him. Antonio found some sort of mental weakness in him, and took advantage of it and all Skull did was hold his head until Antonio stop hitting him, then SKull delivered a big blow to the temple of this man and he just fell to the floor. [Alias looks in Aww] Alias: Do you know what it was that go to Skull?.. Jack: I think I have an idea, but it can't be sure..considering Antonio was the only man to know..but he was killed I talked to him briefly though. Alias: Please tell me... Jack: Alright.. [Jack begins to whisper as he does not want the other men around to hear what they are talking about] Jack: I have heard that the thing that gets him the most is when you mention his family, He is very sensative on the subject and will refuse to listen to you. Try it, it may be the key to victory. Alias: I am not threatened by this guy, his desperate attempts to intimidate me were at all else a failure. The key to victory may be there but the beating that I will dish out will obtain the vitory itself. This man may have killed some people but I have killed some souls. Jack: Good Luck, Shane..you should do the trick.. Alias: Thanks bro, I'll be back to see you. [Alias hops back into his car and begins thinking to himself] --Can this be true, this man is a cold blooded killer, It can't be it is some kind of intimidation tatics..This man is a liar. His attempts to intimidate me will fail. There is no harming Alias. There will be nothing he can do when he steps into the ring tonight. The time has come for me to show him who the true killer is..Alias..bustin' some skulls.--
