Dan's been Training!!!

19 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The camera opens to the Las Vagas, Nevada in the Mirage Casino the Worlds Martial Arts 23rd Annual Power Breaking Championships. We see the announcers of the shows thats being televised on ESPN2 this evening Walter Kenning and Sam Powel both are at there announce table sitting, awaiting for the competition to begin ). Walter Kenning: Welcome folks to the 23rd World Martial Arts Annual Power Breaking Championships in Las Vagas, Nevada in the Mirage Casino. This championship shall be great we have competitors from all around the world. One guy from EPW named Psycho Dan the six foot ten inch tall, 360 pound giant is here competing tonight. Sam Powel: Yah this guy Psycho Dan is a professional wrestler and has just earned his black belt two years ago, and he is only 23 years old how about that. Walter Kenning: Well also in this championship is a man from Japan Kimo Senkei a 6 foot eight inch tall 300 pound of a man. He has been competing in these championships for the last 6 years now and is the fan favorite. Sam Powel: Yah he is a fan favorite and a very power man at 35 years old. Walter Kenning: Tonight we start of with breaking concrete slabs one inch thick, five inches wide and two foot long. and starting up first tonight is Howard Dunn from Chicago at five foot six and 180 pounds. He looks ready for a 12 break set up. ( Howard Dunn looks real nice in his red and black uniform his dark brown hair neatly parted to the right, he stands ready breathing and focuses on breaking his pile of concrete slabs. Howard readies a downward elbow strike a common form in slab breaking. Howard take a few practice his strike before the break and one final Ki he comes down hard with his elbow strike and 10 of the 12 concrete slabs break and the fans cheer for him. Howard bows to the judges and the crowd and gives a short wave and goes back stage). Sam Powel: That was a good showing for Howard Dunn he broke 10 out of 12 slabs and he takes number one slot in this championship. Walter Kenning: Yah he always gives the fans the credit they deserve, he is donating his winnings to an Orphanage he grew up there in Chicago in his youth. Sam Powel: He is a great man I hope he wins tonight those children need the money. Walter Kenning: Are next competitor is a young man from New York City, Carl Bradshaw a five foot seven inch 190 pound man, who in his spare time teaches martial arts to kids free at the Y.M.C.A. He is going for a 12 slab break, lets see if he does it? ( Carl Bradshaw walks out wearing a white uniform trimmed in black bows to the judges and the crowd with a kind smile. Carl takes a few practice fore arm strikes and yells a huge Ki. and smashes through all twelve concrete slabs with little effort and the crowd goes wild and cheers for Carl. Carl bows again to the fans and judges and jogs back stage). Walter Kenning: That was impressive taking Carl Bradshaw to the lead over Howard Dunn. Sam Powel: Yes this contest is proving to be one of the best I have seen in my years of covering this championship. Walter Kenning: We have up next from Japan, standing in at six foot eight inches tall and weighing in at 300 pounds! Kimo Senkie!!!! Sam Powel: I bet size and weight have the advantage in this kind of contest Walter? Walter Kenning: Yes size and weight equal power in this event and I know this man has it all! ( Kimo Senkie walks from back stage to the cheers of many of his fans from Japan and around the world. Kimo is wearing a red uniform with an oriental style dragon emblem on the back and front of his shirt. Kimo's long black hair is tied in a neat pony tail and his short goatee waxed and strait. Kimo bows to the judges and the fans and eyes the 15 slabs of concrete he has chosen). Sam Powel: Kimo has chosen 15 slabs tonight if he can do this he takes first place! Walter Kenning: Yes this should be good? ( Kimo takes his time concetrating on his breathing and focusing his power to break these slabs, giving a loud final Ki Kimo jumps in the air with an elbow smashing almost through the entire stack leaving only one slab unbroken. Kimo stares in shock as only 14 of the 15 slabs have broken). Sam Porter: Awww tuff luck Kimo but 14 out of 15 puts you in first place I believe? Walter Kenning: Yes this puts Kimo in first place with only one opponent left Psycho Dan, and he might have a chance at breaking Kimo's record? Sam Porter: That may be so, Dan still has to break 15 slabs in order to beat Kimo. I just don't think Dan can do it? (Psycho Dan comes out to his music, Meaning of Life by Disturbed, Dan raises his hands in victory and looks right at Kimo and gives him the double bird. Making Kimo get angry and disgusted with Dans actions. Dan walks to the stack of 15 slabs still eyeing Kimo and punches the slabs with a downward closed fist shattering all 15 concrete slabs, debris falling to the floor at Dans feet. Dan looks at Kimo and gives him the double bird again further making Kimo angry and Dan walks back stage with a smile on his face). Sam Powel: Holy Cow! Dan showed no respect to Kimo after shattering 15 slabs!!! Dan just took first place in this championship and insulted the Japanese competitor? Walter Kenning: Wow Dan has great power I never saw that kind of power in his wrestling in EPW lately Dan's been working out!!! Sam Porter: Did you not see Dan disrespect Kimo? Walter Kenning: Thats just Dan's hype he psyched out Kimo and Kimo knows it! Sam Porter: Dan has no respect for anyone, why don't you go interview him? Walter Kenning: I think I will, you stay here and don't get in any trouble! ( Walter Kenning gets up from his announce table and walks back stage to find Psycho Dan to interview him. We see Dan sipping a cup of coffee, leaning against a wall with a sports bag at his feet as Walter Kenning walks up mic in hand). Walter Kenning: Psycho Dan can I get a word with you? ( Psycho Dan looks up and tosses his cup of coffee away and smiles ). Psycho Dan: Sure why not....what do you want to know? Walter Kenning: Well you did wonderful tonight in the championships? What is your opinion of Kimo from Japan? Psycho Dan: Kimo is just some rice eater from Japan that came here to America to try and prove he is superior to Americans. I beat him tonight just like America beat Japan back in World War 2. Kimo go back to Japan and eat your rice, American know how will only beat you! ( Out of now were Kimo comes out and side kicks Dan in the side of his head,Dan holds his temple were he was kickes covering up, followed by several kidney thrusts from his hands. Kimo then thrusts a left knee into Dans stomach, but Dan does not seem to be effected by the massive blows and Kimo stops in his tracks. Dan gives a right chop to Kimo's throat stunning him. As Kimo tries to back off and breath, Dan gives a heart punch to Kimo that stops him in his tracks, Kimo falls to the floor out cold laying on his back not moving. Dan looks at Walter Kenning and walks to him taking his mic in hand ). Psycho Dan: Aggression I got a punk named the Freak!...He fancies himself a superstar in EPW but a Hardcore match is my style. Special Referee John Adams...Great I get a chance to get my hands on that bastard and finish what I started!....Freak your going down! John Adams!...at Aggression I will deal with you and beat you like the bitch you are! Then I will get even more personal Adams! By laying you out in my ring and humiliate you like never before! After your interference last time! Its payback Time!!! ( Psycho Dan reaches over and picks up his sports bag and walks to the pay window and collects a $50.000 grand prize check. Dan then walks out of the casino and hops on his Harley Davidson and rides off into the night to await this weeks Aggression ).
