The End Is Near

24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Footage of Chapel being attacked on last week’s Aggression is shown in slow-motion. As the footage gets to the portion where Chapel is shown being attended to, the footage runs extremely slow. The screaming of Chapel can be heard for almost a full minute as the footage is slowly shown. As the footage begins to end, the screen color becomes pure red and then fades to black. As the camera fades back in, it is focused on a large, glass orb being held in someone’s hands. The camera zooms out and focuses on the back of this man. Darkness fills the room, this making any features of the room difficult to view. The man turns towards the camera and it is revealed that it is none other than Madison Killjoy. Killjoy begins to smile as the camera slowly zooms in on him. The camera stops at a full close-up and Killjoy begins to speak.] Madison Killjoy: Your fate is sealed. There is no turning back. The end is near, but it seems like forever. The torture and the agony of having to go through the anticipation of what is to come, is unbearable. Chapel, you may not admit it but deep inside, you know where the truth lies. You know that what I say is the truth. I hear your soul cry, Chapel. I hear your silent screams of redemption. You truly do want to change. You want to do the right thing. Well Chapel, it’s just not that simple. You see, you already had your chance to do what needed to be done. You had your one and only chance at salvation. It’s just too bad that you didn’t do the right thing. Madison Killjoy drops the glass orb that he is holding in his hand. The camera follows it as it hits the ground and shatters into hundreds of pieces. The camera then pans up to Killjoy as he looks down at the remains of the orb.] Madison Killjoy: Chapel, it’s over. You shall be freed from this torture soon enough. This life will be over but I assure you that you will only see the beginning of your damnation this Sunday. As for this week, I am paired with the man that broke you down, Chapel. I am going to be standing next to the man that put the beloved Chapel out of commission. I personally don’t know the man, but I am sure that he does not measure up to a man of such divinity as I. He did the right thing by taking matters into his own hands, but this week on Aggression, I will take matters into my hands; just as I will with Chapel at Soul Survivor. Madison Killjoy chuckles.] Madison Killjoy: Dervante, you are a very funny man. You do a very good job of making light of my statements. Do you think that my messages are jokes? Do I make you laugh!? You are just another one of the thousands of lost souls that have not seen the light. You do not see the truth of my words as you are emulating the life of an infidel such as Chapel. Instead of prancing around and making jokes, what you should be doing is praising “him” for the simple fact that my powers are not focused on you past this Friday. You should be thankful that my attention is adverted towards the one that they call Chapel. Trust me; your time will come soon enough. Madison Killjoy glares into the camera with a look of annoyance on his face.] Madison Killjoy: Then I am brought to the boy wonder, Tom Conway. What can I say that hasn’t already been said about the so called “man”? Conway, I don’t understand your motives. I don’t know why you are trying to suck up to Chapel, but I don’t like it. I already showed you what happens when you step up to Madison Killjoy and I taught you the same lesson twice. I never would have guessed that you would have been back for more. You can go ahead and make all of the fake titles that you want, but the true championship shall be around my waste soon enough. Just two days away from my reign. How does it feel Conway; to know that I will do what you could never do? How does it feel Dervante; to know that what I preach is the truth no matter how much you don’t want to admit it? How does it feel Chapel; to know that the end is near? The camera fades to black as Madison Killjoy stares into the lens with an evil looking smile on his face.]
