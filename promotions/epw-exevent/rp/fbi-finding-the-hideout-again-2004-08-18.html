FBI- Finding The Hideout (AGAIN)

18 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opened to the windy streets of Hawaii where all three of us were speeding down the road to get to Joey Campbells hideout. The main mountain that was in Hawaii was about 230 minutes out of town. It wouldn't tak that long though because we were already half to the mountain. We were getting to the outskirts of town, and we could altready see most of the mountain. We were getting closer, I could feel it. Finally we had skidded to a stop at the very bottom of the mountain. We could see that Joey had must have put a camoflouge door to get in or something just so we wouldn't find it. All three of us started feeling around the walls to try and find the secret door. Suddenly Stephen got sucked into the wall.) Christina: What was that? Jackson Kable: I have no clue but I bet Joey saw us and put a trap up on the wall. (Suddenly at the far end of the wall of the mountain, I found a little hatch that probably led to the inside of the mountain. I pulled the hatch and a huge door opened from the wall. Looking into it it was pitch black. I stepped into the door first and then Christina came in behind me. Right when both of us got into the place the door behind us shut really loud. The sound echoed throughout the whole mountain. Me and Christina held our hands oout in front of us just in case we ran into a wall. Suddenly a big light lit up the whole inside of it. It was just a large cave. We probably weren'y even in the core of the mountain where the hideout probably is.) Christina: This is kind of scary. Jackson Kable: No kidding, but we have to find where they took Stephen. (Suddenly a few guards came from behind us.) Guard 1: Get on the ground and put your hands up. (Me and Christina dropped to the ground and put our hands high into the air. The guards surrounded us with weapons in there arms. We got off the ground and stood there hoping that the guards wouldn't shoot us. Suddenly Christina put her hands down and kicked two of the guards in the side of the face. They twisted around and fell to the ground. The other two, I turned around and punched both of them. All of them were down in a matter of seconds. We saw a door so we walked over to it and walked through. We were in a room this time it was not a cave. It was just an empty room with nothing in it but a chair in the centre of the room.) (Suddenly I just remembered that I was in a match on Breaking Point. I twas the next PPV for EPW, the federation I was in. Jason Slade challenged me to a no dq barbed wire ropes match. He wanted his ass beat again. I beat him last week on Aggression after I tripped him into the exposed turnbuckle. At Breaking Point I'm going to pound on this little runt till hes bloody.) Jackson Kable: Where are they keeping him? Christina: Who knows. This hideout is probably huge. It might take awhile to find him. (We kept walking through doors as the scene started to fade.)
