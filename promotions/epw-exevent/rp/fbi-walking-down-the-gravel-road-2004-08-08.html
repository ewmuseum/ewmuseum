FBI- Walking Down the Gravel Road

8 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opened up to the empty gravel road that me Stephen, and Christina were walking on. My forehead was full of sweat, and I'm pretty sure that Stephen's was too. I wasn't too sure if girls could sweat so I didn't bother asking Christina if she was sweating. All of us were walking with our heads down towards the ground. I had seen that there was a few interesting rocks I had walked over when walking. We saw the skyline of Hawaii, so we figured that we were getting pretty close to the city. My legs were really starting to hurt, and there was so much sweat on my forehead that I could fill a glass of water with all of it. No one would drink the water of course, because that would be kind of sick.) Jackson Kable: Is it just me, or is it really hot out here? Stephen: I can't breathe, so it is not just you. Christina: I'm dying. I need to get out of this weather. Jackson Kable: Just face it, when we get to hawaii maybe we could go a little off task and take a swim in the beach or something. *25 Minutes Later* (We had finally made it to the city of Hawaii. All three of us were out of breath. I was sitting at a picnic table that was on the sidewalk on the side of the road. Stephen and Christina kept standing, but I didn't know the reason. After a little bit we had regained our breath and started walking down the sidewalks. The city was so big, it would be impossible to find Joey Campbell. With all of these people around, it would be hard to find him. But, we would just need to use the mini tv on my belt to find our main man who tells us where Joey's hideout and everything was. I grabbed the mini tv and turned it on. A face came on the screen.) Man: Hey Kable, what's up? Jackosn Kable: Mark, I need you to tell us where Joey is hiding out here in Hawaii. This city is so big it would take us a year to find him. Mark: Just a second and I'll check. (In 5 minutes mark's head came back onto the screen.) Mark: Well, we have found out that he has a very large hideout in Hawaii's biggest mountain. We haven't got the name of the mountain, but you should be able to see it from the place your standing. The mountain is so big, that it covers most of Hawaii's skyline. Jackson Kable: Shit, that's big. Mark: No kidding. So I assume that your good from here? Jackson Kable: Yup were perfect, thanks mark. (I pushed the off button on the mini tv and put it back onto my belt. Stephen and Christina walked over to me.) Stephen: So, what did Mark say? Jackson Kable: He said that Joey Campbell has a very large hideout in the largest mountain that's in Hawaii. He didn't know what the mountain was called, but he said that we could probably see the mountain from the skyline of Hawaii. Christina: Okay, so technically we don't have to goto the mountain right away. We could have a little break. Stephen: Yeah, but I don't think that we should go swimming. I'm still wet from swimming udner water from that island. (All three of us started walking down the sidewalk again. I saw a coke machine a little bit ahead of us, so I started walking a little faster so I could get a drink. I got to the machine and stuck a dollar into the machine. A pepsi popped out of the bottom of the machine. I took the bottle of pepsi and started drinking it. Stephen and Christina got to the machine and also got a drink. I twisted the cap back onto the bottle, and sat down on a bench that was right beside the pop machine. We all sat as the scene started to fade.)
