The door opens, and we enter the house. Inside, the house is full of smoke. Further in, we find the living room, where "Who Made Who" by AC/DC is drowning out any conversation. Sitting on the couch is a couple dudes who look like they're half asleep. Two chairs line the other wall, Freak is sitting in one, and Dominique is in the other. He then makes up his mind, and grabs a Mountain Dew, and a Diet Coke. "Calling Dr. Love" by Kiss begins to play. He smirks and sits down. He hands the Dew to Dominique. Dominique: Thanks hun.
