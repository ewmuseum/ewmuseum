Name: Maize(Carrington Banks) Location: Chicago,IL Date Taped: July/28/2004 A Jobber's Life Episode One Network Voice:Next on USA is the season premiere of "A Jobber's Life" The scene shows the opening credits for "A Jobber's Life". Images of Maize,his wife, and six year old child flashes across the screen. Then they finally show the executive producer's name and begin the show. Then the cameras shows Maize,referenced as Mr.Banks, in his private interview area, ready to give his thoughts on why starting an reality show of his life. A man whose head only can be seen is interviewing "Mr.Banks" who is sitting in a black swade chair. {Let be know that the all shows were taped and the dates correspond day shown on t.v} Interview Person: Why, Mr.Banks, why start an reality show about your life in and outside of wrestling. Mr.Banks: Well I think the fans would appreciate wrestling more if they see the true person behind the entertainer. By watching my show they will get a better idea of what a wrestler like myself go through on a daily basis. You see I am consider famous by definition, and I think the fans have a distorted view of a wrestlers life. They pay more attention to the more famous wrestlers like Chapel,Ace and Tom Conway, giving them the state of mind that wrestler's lead glamorous lives. Now I'm not saying my life isn't great, because as you may know I have a beautiful wife and wonderful son. What I am saying it's difficult juggling wrestling, being an husband, and being a father without dropping at least once. Interview Person: Nicely put, well here you go folks...... Mr.Banks:(Scowling) Hold on just one minute. You don't start my show, Maize starts his own shows! Well this is what the a-maizing fans been waiting for. This is a "Jobber's Life" The scene then splits to the actually show already in progress. Mr.Banks, now being referenced to as carrington or daddy, is waiting by the phone on the couch in the living. Then his wife, whose name is Jessica but might be referenced as snuggle bear or honey, walks in and sits next to him. Jessica: Whats wrong Carrington? Are you still waiting for that phone call from that EPW? They probl... Carrington:(cuts her off)Don't even say it! I know they'll call! I gave them the best tryout they problem seen in weeks! Man do you know what this will do to my career if they sign me? They... Jessica:(cuts him off)Yeah it'll make your days away from home even longer. You know, you wasn't even at the hospital when Paris, your own son, was born. I just wonder how far it going to go. Especially if you get sign to a big promotion like EPW. Carrington: We need this to be financially stable. I thought we talked about this already.......(Jessica starts laughing) And what are you laughing for? Jessica:(smiling) I just remembered we are on T.V.!!(chuckling) Carrington covers his face in embarrassment of what Jessica just said. Carrington:Well at least you're not dumber than that other Jessica! Jessica:What do you mean by that!? Carrington laughs,gets up and walk to the bathroom followed by the camera crew. Not aware that the camera crew is following him Carrington enters the bathroom and takes a leak. Carrington then realizes someone is breathing over his shoulder and turns around. Carrington: What in Sam's hell are you guys doing in the bathroom with me? You perverted excuses for human beings. One of the cameramen: Sir we are just doing our jobs. By the way my name is Chuck I'm a big fan of yours!!(extends his hand and then Carrington shakes it) Carrington: Well I'm going to let you slide since you're a fan, but next time don't follow me when i go piss, thats just gay! Chuck: Understood Mr.Maize SIR! The camera crew then exits the bathroom. Chuck is the last person leaving then Carrington calls his attention.... Carrington: Oh yeah by the way....I didn't wash my hands.(Laughing) Chuck walks out and shakes his head without saying a word. Soon after, while Carrington is washing his hands his son, Paris, burst into the bathroom. Carrington then jumps out of unespectancy. Carrington: What in tha......They made you a perverted gay guy too?(smiles) just kidding what do you want? Paris: Daddy, a man named L. called and said he wanted to speak to the guy you play on T.V.,Maize! Carrington, without hesitation rushes to the phone with the camera crew,lead by Chuck follows him. Jessica hands him the phone and then everyone crosses their fingers(even chuck). Carrington slowly puts the receiver up to his ears. Carrington:(trying to sound calm and collective)Hello.....This is Maize! L.: Hello we've reviewed your tryout session and i just called to tell you that.......... Commercial break is shown.....A variety of commercials and advertisement are shown the last one is the following Guy with suit in a chair: I have good news Employee:(Smiling) Well Tell me! Guy in suit in a Chair: Your Fired Employee: But i though you said you have good news!!! Guy in suit in chair: I do, I SAVE MONEY ON MY CAR INSURANCE BY SWITCHING TO GICCO!! Your dismissed Voice: Save 15% or move on your car insurance by switching to Gicco! The show resumes where it left off L.: Hello, we've reviewed your tryout session and I just called to tell you that.......... The scene shows Maize, his wife, and his son in a suspenseful order of camera shots. L.: We want you here with us here at the EPW!!! Maize smiles trying to keep his cool. He then makes a dumb up sign to his wife Jessica. Jessica then smiles. You can tell in her facial _expression that she was faking it because this she wanted Maize to settle down. Paris has no idea what is going on so he is emotionless. Chuck, the camera guy, smiles himself. Maize puts his finger up to his mouth to keep them from talking loud so he can hear L.. L.: I want you to come down to my office for some contract negotiations. I'm not gonna lie to you, the main reason why I i signed is to promote my company on your up coming reality show. So I've already made reservations for you flight at American Airlines and I want you at the office by tomorrow afternoon!!! Carrington: Yeah I gotcha, but American Airlines? Come on, thats almost guarantee that I'm going to be late. Also the camera crew is obviously allowed anywhere i go.(looks over at Chuck. Chuck shrugs his shoulders) L.: Whatever! Just make sure you here by noon or my camera crew and my body guards I'll kick your ass! Carrington:(talking under his breath) Just for that I'm coming at 11:59. L.: (Clears Throat) Just be here!!! L. hangs up the Phone......Then a commercial break follows. A promotional video for the EPW pops up on the screen... Network Voice: USA just gets better every week. But we all know one thing............EPW Aggression is the hallmark of our network. What will happen this week, Will The Legacy reguvenate it's reign... Highlights of Chapel Holding up the World Title, Ace holding his Intercontinental Title, and a couple of match highlights are shown on screen. Network Voice: Well watch Aggression live on Fridays 10/9 EST and find out what all the fuss is about. Only on USA, the Network for the masses! Mintos freshness,Blahh,BLahh The scene picks up where it last left off in the living room with everyone waiting for the news. Carrington: I got the Job! Paris frowns up and runs to his room, Chuck, the Camera Guy, Is the only one who gave verbal reply by saying "yes"! Jessica looks over at Maize and shakes her head! The Camera Is now on Chucks Point of View. It shows Paris run up the stairs sniffling. Carrington: What is up! Why are you all acting this way just because the cameras is on!! Jessica: You know...you didn't even tell your son that you were gonna start wrestling again! What are you going to do now, Huh!? Mr. Goodwill Ambassador my ASS! You can do whats right for your fans but not you own family!!! Carrington: Well without these FANS your ass wouldn't have that big stone on your fingers, hell you would be in this $800,000 house with your $100,000 Gucci purse!!!!! Jessica: That has nothing to do with the fact your son will grow up without having a personal relationship with his father!!!! Carrington: Growing up without a father! He'll see me on T.V. all the time and he can say....Hey thats my father!!(trying to lighten' up the mood) Jessica: (trying to keep from smiling) Your sarcasm isn't going to get you out of this! Your son wants a normal life not this glamorous fairytale rich kid life!! Carrington: Too bad you can't say the same for yourself!! Jessica, without responding,walks away as the camera crew,lead by Chuck, follows her, she then puts her hand in the way of the camera lens and tells them to cut the cameras off. They do not and before she can close her bedroom door you can see a tear run down her left cheek. Maize then runs up to the door and try to open it but its lock. Carrington: Don't do this the fans are watching and they'll think differently of me! Jessica: (Can vaguely hear her through the door) It's always about the fans(sniff) Thats all you care about. Go marry them! Go do what you want, but don't talk to me about it, apparently I don't understand talk to your son about it! Maize then puts his head down and shakes it. As he walks up the stairs he begins talking into the camera. Carrington: Man.....this is bad folks......but i can't let the people down, they've been waiting two years for me to return!! I'm about to convince my son that I'll be able to see him more often. Man I hate lying to my son. Maize enters his son's room. His son, Paris, is face down on his bed crying and sniffling, Maize then sits down next to him on the bed..... Carrington: I.... Paris: Are you going away like when you was touring with Uncle Mark(Young Gunz Banks)? Why do you always leave me!!! Carrington:(sighs)Yeah....it's true I was hired by EPW....but I'll be able to see you!!! But i am going away tomorrow to..... Paris: Well quit wrestling and become a REAL father!!!!!!(runs out the room) Maize is hurt so much that he didn't even go chasing him after that he goes into his room in which he finds his cloths already out the door, the dirty work of Jessica. He then chuckles and turns to chuck who the camera perception is in. Carrington: (trying to sound happy) YOU still on my side? Chuck: yes I..... Carrington: That was a rhetorical question!!!!!(looks through cloths and Items on the floor) Honey Can you throw me my bags and tooth brush!!!!!??? I'D MIGHT AS WELL MAKE IT WORTH WHILE!!!!! {commercial Break} Network Voice: This Week on EPW's Aggression, Chapel retained his title at Soul Surviver in a legendary match aginst MDK, but now he has Kable to face will Kable make his presence known when Chapel faces Shawn Singleton. So Watch EWP Aggression 10/9PM EST and find out what the fuss is about.....ONLY ON USA, the Network for the masses!!!!!!! So remember, if you deposit you time and money in Carrington Banks, he'll make sure you get love with the intrest you deserve!!!! Network Voice: Now back to the season premiere of a Jobbers life...... The scene shows Maize at the airport checking in through the metal detector. Maize puts his bags on on the tread and walks through the metal detector. It doesn't go off but a guard near by walks up and and puts his hand near Maize's Chest..... Guard: You stop right there Mr..... Maize: Whats the problem...??? Guard: (smiling) Becuz the metal detector didn't go off and you are gold.Can i have your autograph?? Maize enters his hotel room and talk to the cameras for a while.... Maize: Well, this is it folks, this is where a wrestler makes his first impression.....the interview with the boss! So imma take a quick nap and CHUCK will wake me up at 11:15...... Maize then falls back on the bed and closes his eyes! Lady:Hello! How may i help you? Maize: Yes my name is Mai....I mean Carrington Banks, and I have an appointment with Gary L...... Lady: Hold on....... Maize: Hey I bet your arms are hurting......Want me to hold the camera? Chuck tries to give the camera to Maize but he starts laughing... Maize: HA! Who you think I am man....This is my show...I don't hold cameras for anybody.... Another camera is focused on Chuck...Chuck looks pissed..... Maize: Man I'm just kiddin'!! Lighten up!!
