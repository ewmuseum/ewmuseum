Getting Into Shape

12 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The camera fades in to Tom Conway as he enters a gym in the middle of the night. The clock shows 3:13AM as he closes the door and begins to walk towards the weight machines. He stops at a leg machine and sits down in the chair. Conway sets the weight to 200 pounds and begins to work out. Conway does a few reps until a man walks up next to him. The man, who is nearly the size of Conway, steps on screen and it is revealed that he is Andre Callendar. Conway lets go of the weight and turns in the seat.} Tom Conway: Hey, what’s up? Andre Callendar: You told me to meet you here. Tom Conway: Yeah, that’s right. Andre Callendar: What did you want? Tom Conway: What do you think? I got a challenge set up with Ricky Summers this week and I wanted you to help train me and get me ready. Andre Callendar: Well, I guess that’s cool. {Andre Callendar walks around the leg machine and adds on an extra 100 pounds. Try that.} Tom Conway: Sure. {Conway struggles at first, but then begins to push the weight easily.} Tom Conway: This isn’t hard. Andre Callendar: So, what’s up with this challenge? Tom Conway: Well, it’s a series of challenges. The first will be a punting challenge. Andre Callendar: Punting? Tom Conway: Yeah, well Ricky Summers doesn’t know what he has gotten himself into. Andre Callendar: How is that? Tom Conway: Well, I have played football before. I was a star athlete on my high school football team. Andre Callendar: Yeah? What position? Tom Conway: I played punter. I was one of the best punters in the country at the time. Andre Callendar: Well, you probably have got Summers beat with this punting thing then. Tom Conway: Probably? I will smoke his ass. Andre Callendar: Hey, I don’t doubt you. Tom Conway: Hey, we all know that Summers is a fraud anyway. {Conway lets go of the weight and relaxes. Andre Callendar’s cell phone rings.} Andre Callendar: Sorry, that’s my phone. Tom Conway: Who the hell is calling you at this time? Andre Callendar: Hold on. {Andre picks up his cell phone and begins to speak. A few words are said and he then hangs the phone up.} Tom Conway: Who was that? Andre Callendar: Nevermind that. {Andre walks around the weight machine and adds another 100 pounds, setting the weight to 400 pounds.} Andre Callendar: You know what? I got to get going. Do a few reps with it on 400 pounds and then work up to 500 pounds and above, I have to get going. {Tom Conway begins to push the weight and really struggles.} Andre Callendar: See you later. {Andre quickly walks away and leaves the gym. Just as the door closes, Conway drops the weight and begins to hold his legs.} Tom Conway: Damn! {The camera zooms in on Conway and then fades to black.}
