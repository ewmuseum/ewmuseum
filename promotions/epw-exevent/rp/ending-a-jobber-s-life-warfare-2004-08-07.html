Ending a jobber's Life(Warfare)

7 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name:Maize Location:N. Little Rock,Ar (on the way from Aggression) Date Taped:Aug. 7,2004 12:45am EST(Inside of his Limo) Ending a Jobber's Life [The scene shows Maize sitting in his Limo wearing a white suit with no tie and a blue dress shirt underneath. Maize just left the ALL-TEL Arena. He has a confident look on his face. He is holding a glass cup in his right hand and is clutching it tightly. His left hand is raised to his ear occupied by a cell phone. The sound hasn't kicked and yet so you can only see Maize moving he mouth. Finally the sound kicks and and you can hear the emotions of the streets and highways. You can also begin to hears Maize's phone conversation....] Maize: So who is the unlucky bastard? Voice on the other end: We are unaware of that at this point. The authorities say that he is a "Developmental Superstar" who's name will be revealed later on. Maize:Damn, later on when? Like later this evening after my match. Is he a damn surprise guest or some sh*t? Voice on the other end: I don't know sir the only thing I know is what I've already told you... Maize:So you are telling me that I have a match at Warfare against some asswipe "jobber" and you can't even find out who he is!? You see, I let Michael talk me into hiring your ass again and the only thing you can give me is what they put on the table!? Man i want your ass to find out who my opponent is.. Not only that, but i want to know his birthday, his mother name, his fathers name, his ancestors name, I even want to know his favorite food, so get to it Ron!!! [The glass that Maize has clutched in his hand is shattered by the force of Maize's hand squeezing it. Maize's hand doesn't even has a scratch on it! ] (Turns out to be Maize's Agent)Agent Ron:(Sounding embarrassed) Okay I'll get to it right away Maize..... Maize:........(Clears throat in a cocky manner) Ron:(fake coughs)I mean ....SIR!....I mean Mr.Banks....I mea.....(hears dial tone) [Maize hangs up the cell phone,kisses it, and puts it in his pocket that is located on the upper right corner of his suit. He then looks directly at the camera and begins to talk..] Maize: Well kid,whoever you are. I hope you are ready for a beating of a life time....You see I just came from Aggression and did what I had to do best and thats whooping ass and talking' trash!! I whipped a little country cowboy's ass by the name of Chris Clarence. You see, including you, I will have had 3 matches in less than 5 days. So you best believe I am more than ready! You come out here and want to be a mystery guy....Shoot,I probably be wrong.....they probably told you the same thing they told me, but quite frankly I can give a rats ass about who I am facing at Warfare. I don't care if you are a rock star,a pop star,twinkle twinkle little start, Hell even the little engine that could!! The fact of the matter is you will enter the ring unknown and leave known as the asswipe who got slaughter at the Convocation Center in Jonesboro, Arkansas by Maize!! You see I've faced many different kind of opponents in my short 2 year career of wrestling. I have faced former champions, I have beaten former champions. Hell......I am a former champion!! But you my friend were describe as a "Developmental Superstar"!!(laughs) That description of you will no longer be relevant after Warfare because you my friend will become an "OBLITERATED SUPERSTAR"! You probably be that type that, as soon as you step into that ring, you start to second guess yourself....You will feel the nervous signals channeling down your spinal chords! But for me, when I hear the fans chant my name, the only thing that I feel in that square circle is electricity!! And best believe what I have in store for you will be shocking!!!! Damn talking about you bores me...Especially when I don't know who I am talking about!!! [Maize pulls his cell out of his suit pocket and dials a number.......] Maize:(Mocking Tom Conway) Hello is this Andre... [Maizes starts to laughs hard the person on the other ends laughs hard too. It sounds like a familiar voice....In fact it is Maize's Uncle Michael Banks again on the other line] Michael Banks: What up chief?! Heard you have yet another match Sunday at Warfare. Who's ass are you whooping now? Maize: Now you want to be on my side...(Gives a brief laugh). Well thats the thing I don't know......he's supposed to be...well...somebody I guess! Michael Banks:Well we can go down the roster and see if they might be your opponent..Want to try that! Maize: Yeah It'll give the viewers something to watch while I am wasting air time..... Michael Banks:Well how about Xian, he is classified as a level 1 jackass... Maize: Na, giving him a match against me would be a sin. That guy don't deserve to get in the ring with the A-Maize-Ing One!! I was thinking along the lines of Tom Conway... Michael Banks: The guy doesn't even know how to cover up a lie! So how can he cover you for the pin? Maize: Well they did say it was a "Developmental Superstar" . He need lots of development,but he is no superstar(Laughs)! Michael Banks: I hear you there man....How about Jason Slade? Or Alias? Maize: Jason Slade probably is still getting use to the ropes, so he might still be bruised. Doubt if they let him wrestle Sunday! I just help Alias out so it wouldn't make sense if I faced him. Michael Banks: Well could it be....(gets cut off) Maize: Whoa! Hold ya horses nut sack! You talked out of turn!(sarcastically speaking)> See thats what I'm saying, you try to give the fans something organized and this what happens!! I mean....(gets cut off) Michael Banks: How about Chapel? Maize: Na, he and Hudman probably are thinking of other ways to screw Singleton!Man that guy gets so close!!! Michael Banks: Yeah, he probably had to get his hair dyed again. All damn day for those split ends!!! Maize: For a second there you sounded like a hairstylist!!.....I didn't know you were..... Michael Banks: Don't even play with me like that!!!!!!!!! Maize: ANYWAYS....Maybe it could be Kyle Pearl...Na he is the type to ambush a brother when he is not paying attention. Sore losing sum' Bi'atch!! Michael Banks: I know who it is.....ACE!!!!.....No he is having a hell of a hang-over....C'mon anyone who loses to Khao has to be drunk!!! Maize: Hey i wasn't drunk during that match, just after the match!!!...Man.....I give up...SHUT UP!!! Get off my damn cell!!! Michael Banks: You know it could be...(dial tone) [As soon as Maize hangs up on MMX the cell phone rings to the glorious tone of Amazing Grace...Maize looks at the caller ID.....It says Agent Ron.....Maize presses talk on his cell phone.] Maize: Hello.... Agent Ron: This is Ron..... Maize: I know who the hell this is...What? You think I am stupid?......You think I can't read my caller ID? HUH?(Laughs)......Don't piss on yourself Ron...whatcha got for me! Agent Ron: Well you are not facing an Active EPW Superstar......In fact you are facing a jobber.....By the name of.............BOB VAN DAM!!! Maize:(Laughing and talking at the same time)HOLY SHAWN MATHERS IN HELL WITH A PITCH FORK STUCK UP HIS ASS RUNNING AROUND SINGING "CRY ME A RIVER" FOR A SATANIC TALENT SHOW......WHO IN THE HELL IS BOB.... VAN .....DAMN!!!!!! Agent Ron:(Trying to hold in his laugh)Well thats what they told me! Maize: Well damn.......bye! [Maize looks toward the camera] Maize:Damn, I thought I had a challenge on Sunday.....But I guess I just will have to end this Jobber's Life!!!!
