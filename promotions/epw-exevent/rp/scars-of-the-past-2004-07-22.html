Scars of the past...

22 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Prelude] 3 months ago {The camera fades in on Khaos just moments after his defeat in the toy chest match at Evolution Chapter I. There Dennis Dorman is trying to get a few words with The Khaotic One himself.} Dennis Dorman: Excuse me Khaos can we get a few words regarding your defeat at the hands of Chapel at Evolution? *Khaos turns eyes at Dorman but gives no response and looks back down at the ground* Dennis Dorman: Anything? *Khaos gets up takes his duffle bag and without even giving Dorman a look he leaves the locker room wounded and all* Dennis Dorman: Well I guess we will not be getting a word in after all well that wraps up Evolution this is Dennis Dorman signing off! World Wide Wrestling magazine reports Khaos has quit EPW! *A distant voice echos...* Coward... KHAOS: I lost becuase I was a coward? NO! I was too focused on the world title rather on my mission to cause nothing but pain and sorrow to EPW and its superstars! *The voice speaks agian...* Then what are you waiting for? Your wounds have healed right? Then it's time you got your revenge on those who have humiliated you and you know who I am talking about... KHAOS: ACE! That bastard, yeah he would do something like that just to get a laugh or two. My wounds may have healed but that day when he put that crap in my beer will always be fresh in my mind! Then you know what to do so all thats left is to WAKE UP! *KHAOS wakes up in a cold sweat and is breathing heavily as thoughts of the dream are still fresh in his mind.* KHAOS: I know what I must do now but I am going back to basics I am going back home to New York! *Khaos decides to sell his property in Japan and fly back home to what was once his stepfathers wrestling school which has a for sale sign on the front door* KHAOS: I will start with this building in which will be my stronghold known as the KHAOTIC KORNER! 3 months later *Nighttime stars fill the New York sky as there is a slight chill in the air. In the building where his stepfather used to own there is a line of people mostly gothics and punks of all kinds waiting to get in the club known as THE KHAOTIC KORNER, Khaos has transformed his stepfathers building into one of the hottest clubs in town. THE KHAOTIC KORNER features a huge stage that the local punk, rock, metal bands alike play their souls out all night and it is perfect for mosh pits, most of time people will get hurt but as Khaos says: As long as they paid their money I could give a crap less what they do to themselves. Once a month a world famous metal, punk, rock band will perform at the KHAOTIC KORNER. In their first month they had Cradle of Filth play there for one night only they played their single "Tortured Soul Asylum" as their finale they also plan to have other bands such a Slipknot and Godsmack in the months to come.THE KHAOTIC KORNER has been a huge for success for Khaos however thoughts of 3 months ago still loomed in his mind, he has been training all this time to keep himself in shape just in case if another promotion wanted his services although he would like nothing more than to return to EPW to get his revenge on the people who made him look a fool on national television. But he knew that would never happen.....till one day while when he finished working out his assistant know as Troy a man who has been khaos's assistant since he opened the club 3 months ago hands him a letter from none other than EPW themselves, he's about the same height as Khaos but excels in accounting and is in charge of the clubs fiscal budget he wears a black suit black shirt and gray tie he has white skin and black hair he always wears black glasses and becuase of his mysterious appearance and knowledge of accounting Khaos has hired him for his services and he has yet to make a mistake.* Troy: Sir I have a letter from a promotion that I think you might want read. *Khaos tilts head then give a disgusted look to Troy.* KHAOS: Read. Troy: Very well *ahem* "We here at EPW ask you John Black aka Khaos if you would consider joining us once again, we have recently closed down but reopened and we have heard that you have moved back to the U.S. from Japan and had set up your club here in New York we could really use the help and with your talent we could take ourselves back to the top again oh and yes here's the new roster, Ace Chapel Emilio Dervante Jackson Kable John Adams Karl Jackson Madison Killjoy Psycho Dan Shawn Singleton The Freak Tom Conway *Just then Khaos's eyes widen with delight* KHAOS: Ah so Chapel and Ace are still here, excellent! The funny thing is my worthless brother still owns EPW but I know for a fact he would not bring me back then who did is the question... *Just then Troy sees something fall out of the envelope in what appears to be a contract.* Troy: Looks to be a contract sir. *Swipes the contract away from Troy.* KHAOS: Well now what do we have here? A EPW contract how fascinating oh it also says that your first opponent will be none other than Ace in a first round match in the Soul Survivor Tournament winner gets a world title shot at a date to be announced later. Heh this is rich Ace is going to wish he and the legacy had finished me off when they had the chance HAHAHAHAHAHAHA! *Khaos signs his name on the dotted line then gives it to Troy for him to deliver it to the EPW offcials who are currenty at a house show not that far from the KHAOTIC KORNER. As he leaves he notices some writing on the back of the letter* This contract will go though no matter what your brother says... KHAOS: The hells going on here? Who is setting all this up? Never the less I would like to thank this person then slam his or her face into the concrete HEHEHE just because. Hmm a lot has changed since I left EPW three months ago a lot of faces I see, some that are new and some that are still here like for instance my dear worthless brother John Adams how's it going big bro I see you managed to let weaklings like Psycho Dan run all over you tisk tisk. Its sad really to think almost almost year ago you showed up in EPW beat the legendary Hudman not once but twice and now look at you out of shape and in turmoil with your own inner demons I suggest you just lay down and die for good your rotten pathetic good for nothing jackass! *Khaos calms down a bit sips some water then continues.* KHAOS: Speaking of Psycho Dan just who in the hell do you think you are huh you come in to EPW saying your some kind monster BAH your just a cheap rip off of some 1970's horror flick then you go around pretending to kill people and taking on 1500 lbs bulls? GIVE ME A DAMN BREAK YOU DUMBASS PIECE OF CRAP OFF A HORSES ASS! You think just by pulling these little stunts people are going to be afraid? No, you want to know what fear is? You want to know what REAL torment is all about? Well when you're done with your little business with my brother come see me in the ring I will gladly show you what REAL torment is if your not a chicken shit that is HAHAHA! *Khaos walks over to the glass wall where he can see the stage of THE KHAOTIC KORNER in its entirety.* KHAOS: There seems another moron who needs to learn a lesson around here and I mean you "The Freak" if there was a belt for consistent babbling you would be the champion ten times in a row, lets get one thing straight kid I can give a rats ass about your pathetic insignificant life no one cares that your a fucked up loser and you need get to get a fuckin life and what's worse halfway though your ridiculous segment I shook my head in disgust this just proves that your a complete waste of life and you got what you deserved last week by getting your teeth kicked in by Shawn Singleton. Speaking of him....a man so little words BUT he needs no words to prove he can already take down anyone he desires...I am only going to say this...ill be keeping a very close eye on you. Madison Killjoy its been a while hasn't it yes you did beat me three months ago but my business is not with you it is with Ace however I will say this stay out of my way I will take down The Legacy myself understand. *Khaos then walks down to the spiral staircase leading to the stage he takes a look around like hes admiring the place he jumps up on stage looking at the floor acting like he is speaking before an audience.* KHAOS: Tonight will be a another busy night for this place much like its going to be for me this coming Friday. Ace your time has come for this is your judgement day this will be the apocalypse of your career and I will personally see to it that you burn in hell like Shawn Mathers I will make you suffer for all the sins your have committed and your punishment will be severe and exetreme! Whats going though your mind right now? Fear? Sorrow? Hate? Perhaps anger because of the fact that your life is in pieces and I am the one responsible, well as they say Ace paybacks are a bitch and this Friday I am going to pay you back for everything that you have sinned for! Ace it's over you life your career EVERYTHING! I will put an end to the Ace of Spades once and for all! *Khaos jumps off the stage then walks to the front door opens it but pauses for a second then turns his head to speak.* KHAOS: Oh that leaves me with you Chapel it will be a while before we go at it agian but I will say this next time we meet if I am going down? I AM TAKING YOU WITH ME! *Khaos walks though the door as the camera fades to black.*
