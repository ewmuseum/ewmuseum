Checkmate, Bitch

29 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XP hasn’t been in EPW for long. He’s go his kicks out on some people, Ace, Russell Wayne, Jackson Kable, Paige… and John Addams has felt the raging youth’s anger on more than one occasion. See, Addams had a problem with the youngster, more so his attitude. Grow up to be 7’3 and 350 pounds of threatening power, and you tend to get a bit of an ego problem. Everyone’s to fear you because you’re large as hell. That’s not the case with Xian. Xian’s the type that if you’re the biggest, baddest, most extreme son of a bitch around, he’s gonna test your meddle to make sure you’re not full of shit. Those fluke hypes… there’s a number of them going around wrestling these days, and it looked like a good number of them liked to work for EPW. On an issue of irony, let us welcome Emilio Dervante. “Yo.” This week, Emilio had to go one on one with The X himself. They said it was Xian’s Aggression debut… but much like Emilio’s hopes of winning, that was a lie. It was about a week or two ago that XP was on Aggression, where John Addams ran in and interfered against Xian for Jackson Kable. This time there wasn’t anything that would stand in Xian’s way, Emilio, Addams, whoever had the fucking balls to step out there at the same time XP is out in the ring. And as hard luck would have it, Emilio would be that man to step out in front of the people at the same time Xian is out there. Addams was another matter though. John Addams, the owner of the joint, he had something against the Hard Luck Kid. Bullies usually had some nonsensical excuse for picking on a smaller person, toying with them like they have the right. He’s the boss, but fuck that. He hired Xian, and now he has to deal with him. Like the lore says, open Pandora’s box, and prepare to get fucked up along with the rest of the world. Bring Xian Phillipse into your federation, and you better hope you have insurance, because people are getting hurt, and shit’s getting busted. Addams will keep on throwing guys in XP’s path to wear him down, but all he’s doing is lowering the roster count week by week. Xian will drop anyone in his path Addams puts in front of him… and then when there’s only 2 people left in EPW, and Xian holds all the belts… Addams will be the final fight, the one that’ll destroy the Prophet. “Oh yeah… I know.” Xian leaned against the wall of the subway with his cell phone to his ear. Dressed in a pair of faded blue jeans with white Chuck Taylor’s and a white retro T, he kept his eyes cast down at the ground as the train sped through the underground warrens. The cabin was filled somewhat, majority of the seats taken, but no one really standing up except for XP. He didn’t like to sit on the subway… because he found himself falling asleep most of the time. You fall asleep on the subway and you leave yourself open to a mugging, and XP wasn’t down for beating anyone’s ass today. He liked to save all that for Emilio. Listening to the person on his phone, he smirked a bit, pushing off the wall slightly and resting back against it. “Well, if they wanna say it’s my debut, fuck it. I got like, 3 matches on my back; Wayne, Kable, and Paige, an’ I ain’t been legally pinned once. I ain’t down wit’ breakin’ tha streak wit’ some damn Dervante.” He reached up to scratch his head, his arm resting down against his stomach as he leaned to the side against one of the door’s guard panels. “Yeah, I heard of him before. I think tha guy was in LOW or DWO for a minute or two, then dropped off tha face of tha fuckin’ planet for a while. At least I ain’t heard of him or seen his ass. I know I had a shot ovah there in DWO… I remembah visitin’ tha HQ and talking wit’ Bane about a contract or some shit, but I went back to tha Indies… I mean Bane was fuckin’ hot for a big girl, but I got scared she’d try an’… rape me or some shit. I mean tha broad kept starin’ n’ called me pretty… that shit ain’t cool, ya know?” The speaker said something that made Xian smile chuckle lightly, the smile widening on his face. “Yeah, well I gots no problem wit’ a babe rapin’ me… I mean ya can’t rape tha fuckin’ willin’ ya know? But I was talkin’ ‘bout tha ‘no good’ kind of rape… you know, stickin’ shit in holes that Emilio’s familiar wit’ an’ I ain’t. I heard Callisto was down wit’ that shit… so I said fuck it an’ went back to where I was havin’ fun.” His brow raised as he listened to the next response. “Well I dunno…” he said, looking up at the ceiling of the train with the tip of his tongue peeking past the corner of his lips. “I guess I’m havin’ fun here. I mean… I’ma sure as fuck enjoy myself when I get my hands on Addams. I’ma whip up on that tall ass one of these days… an’ when it happens he bettah not act surprised. He’s a fuckin’ prophet he says… he should see that shit comin’. An’ Emilio, yeah I think I’ma have some fun wit’ Em Fridy night. Me against that Aztec warrior.” The subway began to slow, preparing for a stop. Xian didn’t pay much attention though to the flow of passengers entering, his arm leaving his stomach and slipping into his pocket to contract himself for people to squeeze past. “Oh yeah, I think I noticed that too.” He said, voice lowering as a few people now stood closer to him. “I mean, if ya seen one arrogant aristocratic asshole, all tha rest of tha Triple A’s look like replicas. Tossin’ around cash, an’ broads ‘fallin’ at their feet like they went cripple or some shit… that type of shit is played out. I mean if you’re gonna be cocky, at least bring somethin’ new to tha table… look at me for instance.” A pause as the train started back up. Xian smiled at the speaker’s response. “I know I give ‘cocky’ anothah meanin’, but that’s neither here nor there. I meant that when ya look at me, hell yeah ya think ‘Man, this is one superficial son ova bitch!’ but that’s not even tha case. I mean you know me… am I cocky?” Once he heard the speaker’s answer, his smile spread again across his boyish face. “Please,” he said, holding back a small laugh, “can we move past my extremities? I’m trying ta make a point.” A short pause… “Thank you. No, I’m not coc- arrogant, I’m one of tha coolest dudes you’d evah meet. You see tha fans out there? They fuckin’ adore me… and that’s ‘cause I don’t go out there an’ alienate ‘em. I’m right there in tha crowd, slappin hands and kissin’ babes like I’m runnin’ for office. I’ma fuckin’ people pers—“ An interruption stopped his statement, but he returned to it after a quick remark. “I know campaignahs SHOOK hands an’ kissed BABIES… does it look like I have that much time? Every wrestlah gets ‘bout a minute an’ a half to two, so I ain’t got time ta search through tha thousands of people just to shake a few hands an’ kiss tha infants. I improvise shit… that’s why most people ain’t seen my finishah yet in EPW. If I can fuck someone up wit’out usin’ tha Hard Luck, then fuck it… use that othah shit. I doubt Emilio even gets locked in that bitch… I’ll probably bounce his head off tha mat wit’ Tha X or my One Hittah Quittah… drop ‘im like I dropped Addams that time on air.” The subway stopped again, and a good swell of people exited. As that happened, Xian’s phone beeped; he looked down at it and put it back to his ear. “To be continued,” he pressed the button and answered “Hello?” The new caller spoke… and what they had to say brought his brow to wrinkle up, eyebrows slanting downwards. “Who is this?” He didn’t seem to get the answer he wanted, but whatever answer he got brought his eyes up, staring ahead through the open door into the crowds. He moved forward, but the door slid shut on him before he could step out. He peered out of the door; eyes narrowed trying to scan for anyone on a cell phone. As great as the numbers would be perceived to be… he couldn’t find a one. The train moved on, and Xian kept looking. As the train sped off into the tunnel, Xian could’ve sworn he saw an arm held up in the crowd… holding a cell phone. The call disconnected… and Xian lowered it from his ear slowly. Someone was playing games with him… and fuck if he liked playing games he didn’t want to play. Just like Addams, someone wanted to fuck with him, but instead of being a bitch and sending innocents to get slaughtered by the bad boy himself, this new person was bringing the game a lot closer to home. Emilio was but a pawn in the game Addams was trying to play with Xian. A pawn… quite worthless… and a fitting representation for Emilio… but if Addams persisted to send pawn after pawn his way, Xian would eventually tip the whole fucking chessboard over and it’d be game over for Addams. So Emilio… Friday night… King takes Pawn. You’ll be discarded off this game boards of Addams’… and sooner or later, it’ll be checkmate for the Prophet… as well as the rest of EPW.
