Training Day...

21 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This Sunday, tomorrow night, Ricky Summers Vs Tom Conway in the final bout for the football challenge series. I Ricky Summers did exactly what I said I was going to do last night on Aggression. I said I was going to defeat that junior high school, peewee football player in the passing contest. He thought he had me cornered when he received training from none other than New York Jets Hall of Famer Joe Namath. Not only receiving help throughout the week, but bringing that pervert on the field. Well, I decided to fire back with my own weapon of choice. I man that I know close, a man that lead the Boston College Eagles to the championship as a kicker. A man that did not come from the New York Yets. I call them the Yets, because frankly there are yet to show anything impressive, much like that little puke Tom Conway. The man I will be receiving some extra training from is Jake Miller, not like I truly need the training anyway. After I get all the right training I will defeat Tom Conway in the kicking contest, then I will finally get to meet him face to face in the ring for the Evolution Pro Wrestling Legacy Championship title. Tom Conway is going to see me kick footballs, and he's going to see my kick some ass. That title he holds around his waist is as good as mine come this Sunday. It's noon time here in Boston at the practice field. The wind is blowing hard through the air, making difficult to train. Jake Miller and I are dressed both in sweat pants and our old Boston College football jerseys. He is placing the football on the tee, and kicking it off over and over again. Multiple footballs scatter the field as he keeps pulling them out from a sack. I watch trying to study. "Now basically Rick it's all in the hips. I know that sounds like it came from some dancing trainer but it is true. Your hips will set your balance, and aim." Jake said swinging his thigh. "Oh well that great, I feel like I'm in dance class. I should have no problem defeating Tom Conway." I replied. "Alright I'm going to set you up and I want you to try." "Alright." I said anxiously. Jake grabs another football from the sack and sets it up on the tee. I take a few steps back and the football falls off the tee due to the wind. Jake sets it up again after squeezing the ball trying to soften it up a bit. The ball is set, then I step back a few steps. I wait for his signal. "Alright now look at where you are about to kick it. Just like you've watched me, right through the uprights. Remember it's all in the hips, lets see what you got." Miller said. "Yea right, I'll be able to get this, I'm that damn good. There is no challenge I can't defeat." I replied heavily concentrating on the goal posts. I take a few seconds to loosen up then I jog a few steps towards the ball. I take a swing with my leg and kick the ball towards the uprights, I just barely knick the bottom of the uprights, I did not get any height on the lift. "Nice try, but remember you have to come up from underneath the ball to give it that lift." Miller said. "Alright, that was just because of the wind pushing it down." I said biting my bottom lip. "Um, Rick. The wind lifts the ball, besides where on the South side of the field. The wind is in our favor." Jake said pointing to the other side of the field. "I'm telling you the wind brought it down, I'm right believe me." "Well, lets try this one again shall we?" Miller sets up the ball again, I take a few steps back. I set myself up, loosen up my arms and take a deep breath. After a slight pause I take off charging the ball quickly. I kick the ball and land on my ass, as the ball flies high into the air, and falls in front of the uprights. Miller begin to laugh as I get frustrated. I can't believe this, I'm having an off day or something, All I could think was I am not paying attention to the training. I picked myself up and brushed off. This jersey is an off field jersey, I am not ruining it. I fix my hair and move back. "That was the funniest thing I have seen in a long time." Miller said with a quick chuckle. "Yea, yea, yea, whatever. Everyone has there moments." I replied rolling my eye's. "Alright remember this. I'm gonna set it up and kick, remember these words." Miller explained. Miller sets up the ball again and licks his index finger putting it in the air. That kinda freaked me out, I'm not sure if I'm getting training from a star kicker or Watson himself. Miller sets up and goes through the process. "Set, Aim, charge, swing. Set, Aim, charge, swing. Set, Aim, charge, swing." Miller repeated. Jake Miller went through the process one more time as he goes through the steps, then kicks the ball perfectly down center of the uprights. The red flags on the top of each poll are blowing wildly in the wind. "Alright you try." Jake said. I set myself up and get ready I repeat tom myself. "Set, aim, charge, swing." I kick the ball getting underneath it, the ball hangs a little to the right, and just barely hooked away from the uprights. "Well, you got some nice air off it, and you were only a little off accuracy. Try it again." Miller said. I set up another ball, the last ball in the sack. I brush my face off as sweat pours down my face. I loosen up and repeat to myself the process once again. "Set, aim, charge, swing!" I yelled. I kicked the ball hard, and right when it left my foot I knew it was going good. It when straight down center of the uprights and I threw my hands in the air. ow I know what Adam Vinetieri felt like when he won both superbowls for the New England Patriots. I jumped up and down a bit, then drop to my knees as if I just won the championship. Jake Miller looks at me like I have just went nuts, but he continues to clap. "Nice kick Champ." He said. "Thanks, its all in the hips." I replied laughing. "Yea, you see I told you." "Alright, I think I'm done for one day, the wind is starting to get to me." I said. "Alright, well pick up the balls and put them, in this sack." Miller said pointing the lose balls. "What? I don't think so." I said putting my hands in the air. "Hey, I didn't kick them out there." "Son of a bitch." I said, knowing he had me pinned. I grabbed the net sack and walked over to the footballs and threw them in the sack. Miller picks up the tee and throws it in the back of his pickup truck. "You owe me Summers." Miller said. "When ever you want to learn how to play a real football position you just let me know." I said with a grin and a wink.
