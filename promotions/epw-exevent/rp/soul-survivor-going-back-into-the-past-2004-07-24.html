[Soul Survivor] Going back into the past

24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Uninformed is one thing, stupid is another…” Ever since KJ was a youngster he was given the path of someone who would be destroyed by depression. His parents were murdered and to add to that he was already going to be raised in a troubled neighborhood. From there KJ was placed in a home where peopled were PAID to care for him. “Money doesn’t buy love.” Whoever said that should obviously get out of the fairy tale he or she is living in. Of course KJ continued to fit under the stereotype so well especially after he gathered a wish…a wish that turned into a dream. Fact is however is that KJ was told numerous time by those who “loved” him that he should thank about a real occupation. Those being paid to be a parent really weren’t doing their job well since they gave him that speech at the age of seven. First off when kids are that young all they can do is dream, but then again KJ's road was one way and their was no light at the end. Also what idiot uses a word like occupation when talking to a kid at the age of seven, oh and lets not forget the lack of proper education at that time in his life. It wasn’t hard for a young KJ to understand and like all things during that time period…it hurt. The pain at the time was unbearable and night after night were ended with sounds of crying and weeping. All at the tender age of seven. KJ was feeling emotions that he shouldn’t have felt at the time. He was young still getting a grip of the world in front of him even after being delt with the obstacles he tried to overcome. Yet each day led to more anger and sadness the combination being difficult to neutralize let alone kill. Even as KJ matured the pain continued and even though nullified by more access to the TV and the outside world. Still though the cards handed down to KJ continued to spell a certain four-letter word… H-U-R-T! The negative about maturing is that you begin to understand. That’s right understanding can be a negative just as it can be a positive. Those random feelings actually start to mean something. The end results could be that of happiness, which is basically the English dream. White picket fence, two kids, and farm animals are vastly overrated. KJ however wouldn’t mind something so overrated cause at this point he isn’t even close to the English dream. He was just the exact opposite with all his emotions coming to a head. A breakdown perhaps would have been on the way if not for one thing. That one thing being that KJ wasn’t only depressed and angry. KJ was also known to show signs of being one thing that people either like or for the most part dislike. That is right KJ was slowly becoming an… Egomaniac… KJ's ego grew each year he aged and was especially noticeable during his pre adult years when he moved away from what brought him the hurt and the pain. After leaving the home where people’s “occupation” was to love a kid that quite frankly they never gave a damn about. Even after his departure KJ knew that he had to find someway to take his mind off of all the bad things in his past and to focus on all the good things that would be in his future… And he did just that. KJ started to learn more and more about professional wrestling that he even took his ego to another level and decided that he would force himself into the toughest wrestling school he could get into. It all started with a simple phone call and the persistence of one Karl Jackson. “Hello, I would like to sign up for a year of training in your school.” KJ was straight to the point as the person on the other line found out when they lifted the phone to their ear. Even though seriousness was a good trait to have the school itself was just harsh. “Oh really let me guess you must be some three-hundred pound slob who thinks that he can get into a ring and make a living, well that isn’t going to happen you piece of crap.” Woah…they were quick to jump to conclusions. KJ was slightly angered by the comment, but was able to keep his cool. “Your not even close on that estimate, how about you give me a chance and let me prove that to you.” Once again Jackson was straight to the point and knew that if he was ever going to complete his dream he would have to start at that school. Not any other school it had to be THAT school in particular. Of course he would have to get past this over the phone conversation first. “Oh is that a fact well I’ve had many nobodies come through this line telling me the exact same thing as you so what makes you so different?” Failure was not an option for KJ and he quickly jumped at the chance to convince this person across the receiver that he could compete in the school. “A nobody huh, give me a break man you don’t have a clue…” KJ paused and realized he was losing his cool and was going to try and get back to conversing, but wasn’t able to before he was interrupted. “You know what kid, fine I’ll give you a chance. You be here at noon tomorrow and we will go from there.” And with that and a clicking noise later KJ was off the phone. A couple steps were taken in a few different levels. One was the step that would lead toward his eventual employment in EPW. The other level being KJ's ego which went up another notch by not taking no for an answer. Finally KJ was in a way getting exactly what he wanted, which also helped the arrogance grow. KJ now feeling all high and mighty took a seat on his bed and just let the feeling flow through his body. He was on cloud nine as some random ESPN news report came across his TV. Jackson let his eyes wonder off and focused them directly on his alarm clock… 11:30 AM Now the stage was set. There was no way KJ would miss his first day at school. Of course this wasn’t you regular school, this school where people train to be a professional. Now unlike other athletic sports wrestling wasn’t viewed as a REAL sport, but KJ was too clever to let that get to him. He knew that wrestling was a sport and like all sports it would take training, hard work, dedication, and of course just a little bit of luck. The school itself was pretty high class with all the regular work out equipment and also included an actual ring. KJ was excited to know that he would get a chance to not only get in an actual ring, but in due time work an actual match. Yet before he could do any of his dream stuff he had to make sure his realistic lively hood was paid off. Minus $150.00 KJ finished paying off his monthly apartment fee and now he could finally rest easy. Resting is exactly what KJ did since he figured unlike real school he would at least rightfully prepare himself for what was to come. As Jackson lay silently in his bed he had “those” memories. “Those” memories being of his life before that do or die phone call where he would only accept what he wanted to hear. Yet before he had no choice, but to take in all the little things life gave him and put up with everything life took away. Memories of his childhood faded as did KJ himself who went off into a deep slumber dreaming off all the possibilities tomorrow has in store for him… BEEP BEEP The alarm went off, and continued to go off as KJ took his oh so precious time at getting to his feet. KJ's body ached all over for some reason, but when finally awaken KJ leaped off the bed. He knew today was the beginning of his time in the controversial yet profitable world of wrestling. Before he could join those ranks however… SMACK The alarm clock came to an immediate stop as KJ went off and did the usual mourning wake up routine except he did it much quicker than usual. Excitement was definitely in the air, and before KJ knew it he was inside the school where he would learn the tricks of the trade… “And you are?” KJ was immediately greeted by a decent sized man who looked like he could be a trainer of sorts. “I’m Karl Jackson.” KJ said with some pride in his voice as the man grew a grin from ear to ear. The man then got a little bit closer to KJ and began to size him up. “Oh so you’re the fool who called me yesterday, well I’m John the master trainer of this facility.” The man known as John waved his hand to the side showing off some of objects in the building. These included weight lifting accessories, stationary bikes, and yes an actual wrestling ring. KJ pretty much ignored the “fool” comment and scanned the facility and noticed that the place seemed to be ran quite well. Before KJ really got a grip of the place John interrupted his train of thought. “Ok kid come over here I want to show you something…” Jackson listened and for once did what he was asked as he and John went over to the ring where there were two nobodies training to be somebody’s. KJ watched closely as the two unknowns “worked” a match ending with the two taking an outside “bump.” “Now that is how it’s done, this isn’t for fun or show this is work.” KJ's eyebrow rose a little as the trainer known as John continued. “So big shot you want to learn this art form…fine, but it will be on one condition.” KJ looked at John and listened closely since this was a vital part in KJ's career and life in general. “If you so happen to slow my production down I will not only drop you as a student, but I can assure you an ass whipping before you leave…got it.” KJ just nodded his head and echoed a word that would stick to his memory like a fly in a spider’s web.
