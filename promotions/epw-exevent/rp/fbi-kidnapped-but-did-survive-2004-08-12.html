FBI- Kidnapped, but did survive.

12 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opened up to the picnic bench we were sitting at drinking our pepsi's, taking our break from finding Joey Campbell. I finished the last gulp of the bottle, and then hucked the bottle into the garbage can that was right beside table. After a few minutes Stephen and Christina finished there bottle's and chucked them into the garbage can. We all started walking down the sidewalk. Suddenly, a few guys came out of a back alley that we walked past, and pulled us into them. They held all three of us against the wall, with the hands covering our mouths so we wouldn't be able to talk. I tried to turn my head so I could see if Stephen and Christina were alright. The last thing I would want is Stephen and Christin to be hurt. The guy that was holding me, punched me in the nose. Some blood started coming out. The guy kicked me in the leg also. Then we saw some more guys coming from the dark side of the alley.) (Christina was pinned against the wall by two guys, because she was kicking and trying to fight her way out of them. But it was no use. They pinned her really hard, so she could'nt even move her neck. Christina got her legs free, and stated kicking the guys in the legs. But one of the kicks went a little high, and nailed the guy in the johnson's! The guy fell to the ground, as the other guy that was holding her wound up for a punch on Christina. I knew that it was very wrong to hit a girl, but this guy really didn't care who he hit. He went for the punch, but Christina ducked and the guy hit the brick wall that was behind Christina. A huge crack went throughout the back alley. The guy started holding his hand, letting go of his grasp on Christina. Christina, using her gymastics skills, ran up the wall and did a backflp off and kicked two guys straight in the stomachs. They flew back into the brick wall and fell to the ground. There was still two guys covering Stephen, and only one covering me. One of the guys that was pinning Stephen, let go and started running after Christina. She started running down the back alley and out of sight.) (The one guy that was holding Stephen, who was staring at Christina run, was not even worried about Stephen getting out of his grip. Stephen, wound up, and punched the guy in the side of the head. The guy fell to the ground, without making a sound. There was one last guy, who was trying to hold me back. I powered out of his grip, and then put some of my shirt up. I started flexing my muscles super hard. The vains started popping out of my skin. The guy was staring at them. And in one second, he fell to the ground. Me and Stephen started running down the back alley, the way where Christins was running with the guy on her tail. At the end of the back alley, we started to slow down. Both of us lost our breath, so we were huffing and puffing to get our breath back. Which way would Christina have gone? Suddenly, we turned around and Christina was hiding behid a dumpster. She walked over to us.) Jackson Kable: Where is that guy that was chasing you? Christina: Oh, when I started running. I hid behind this dumpster and he went past. Then I came out and beat him. He's over there. (We all looked to the corner of the back alley, and that guy was laying there with a little blodd coming from his mouth. He was laying on his head in the corner.) Stephen: Ouch. That has to hurt. Christina: No kidding. He won't be getting up for awhile. (Suddenly, something started lowering from the sky. Or else it was just coming from the top of the building, but whateevr it was, it was getting weird. It was a very large platform, with three things on it. I couldn't tell what they were. Suddenly, the radar on my belt started to beep. Taht meant that Mark had wanted to talk to me. I reached onto my belt, and saw Mark's face with a huge grin on it. The platform lowered right to the ground, and finally we saw what had been on the platform. They were our motorcycles, that we had left at the airport when we left from Chicago. But how did they get them here? Suddenky, makr started to talk.) Mark: Hey guys, what's up? Anyways, surprised to see your bikes there? Stephen: You bet we are. Christina: How did you get them here? Mark: I had your special airplane pick them up and bring them there. We had some guys in Hawaii right now, monitoring what you guys do, and then when we get something that's yours, we get them to hook them up with you. Christina: That is awesome. Jackson Kable: Thanks man. We owe you big time. Mark: No you dont. This is what I get paid to do. (We all started laughing as Mark's face faded from the screen. Instantly, I hopped onto my motorcycle, and started the engine. I drove off the little platform it was on, and then waited for Stephen and Christina to get where I was parked with my motorcycle. All of us got off the platform as it started to lift into the air. We all started going down the street. We turned and then the scene started to fade.)
