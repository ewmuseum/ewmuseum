Sad Time...

22 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up just outside the Winnipeg Arena, the parking lot is all too quite except for one ominous car sitting diagonal in the lanes. The engine has stopped and the windows are down, Jason Slade is seen sitting down with the cell phone too his ear, he looks out the window and begins to talk] Jason Slade: I know, I understand and I am so sorry this is happening to you, but you have to be here with me tonight, your my good luck charm, with out you I will loose. [Jason opens the car door and walks outside, he begins to rub his piercing under his lip and look to the sky] Jason Slade: I know that, she means the world too me also, but I just I could get you here tonight so you can be by my side, I don't want to do this match with Kable because I know how much it pains you too watch. [Jason kicks the tire on the car and rests his hands on the roof] Jason Slade: Well I didn't realize it was that bad, so just stay there with your mother and i don't really know, but she needs you right now more than I do. [Jason runs his hand through his hair] Jason Slade: Love you too. [Jason close his phone and gets back into the car as the scene fades to black] fade out [The scene opens up inside a hospital room in Battle Creek, Mi. The sounds of heart monitors are heard in the background Amylee is seen sitting down holding here mothers hand, she is crying with her head resting on the bed. Cards and flowers cover the hospital room, the television is on CNN and muted, the window over on the other side of the room is open enough o let in a slight breeze] Amylee: Mom I wish you where here right now to give me some advice, because I really don't know what to do at this point, I just wish you where here right now to help me out.. [Mom begins to tighten her grip on her daughters hand. her eyes open as Amylee looks up toward her mother] Mom: Amylee....my darling Amy... Amylee: Oh mom!! I love you so much. Mom: I love you too honey. [The two exchange a kiss and a sentimental huge, Amylee comes back to her chair and kisses her mothers hand] Amylee: Mom these past few day's I have needed you so badly, so much bad stuff is happening to me and I just don't know what to do. Mom: *coughing* What do you mean Amy? Amylee: It's Jason. I love him to death but he is becoming a danger to himself in the ring, he is a match at Breaking Point... Mom: Breaking Point..? [Amylee smiles] Amylee: Yeah it's a pay per view event that he is apart of and he is fighting this man Jackson Kable and the match they are in is so dangerous. Mom: Well I've known Jason for a long time and he does do stupid things sometimes, but how can this be any different? [Amylee is fighting trying to hold back the tears] Amylee: Mom this match can kill Jason if he isn't careful, the ring ropes are being replaced by barbed wire and the turn buckles are exposed to reveal cold hard steel. [Amylee's mom starts to cough uncontrollably as Amylee hands her some water, her mom takes a sip and settles back down while Amylee takes the cup and sets it back down on the counter] Amylee: I just need some advice mom, I'm so scared right now, I don't know what to do. Mom: I have tried to be there for you all through out your life, there may have been sometimes where I failed you as a mother, or as a friend. But you can't let Jason slip away from you, you two are meant for one another and when I am gone, you have to have somebody with you in your life. Amylee: *crying* Stop talking like that mother, you are the greatest mother in the world and you deserve the best. Mom: This cancer is killing me every time I take a breathe, I can't hold on much longer, I just wanted to tell you I love you and please help Jason concern his match, he needs you AL... [Mom begins to loosen her grip a little as her eyes fade fast, Amylee gets a look of panic on her face as she suddenly hears the sound of the heart monitor making a continuous beep noise. Amylee looks at the machine and starts to cry and scream for the nurse] Amylee: NURSE!!!! NURSE!!!! [Just then a large black lady comes running into the room, she runs over to Amylee's mom and check her pulse and she gets nothing. The nurse looks over at Amylee whose head is down crying into the bed] Nurse: Sugar? [The nurse walks over to Amylee and picks her up so they are face to face] Nurse: I'm sorry to tell you this, but your mother has passed on. Amylee: No!! [Amylee falls into the Nurse as she begins to hug Amylee, the nurse rubs her hair and holds her close to comfort her] Nurse: Just understand one thing dear. Amylee: *sobbing* Yes... Nurse: Your mother loves you with all of her heart, she could not stop talking about you yesterday when your where on your way to see her, she was the happiest I have ever seen her. Amylee: Really? Nurse: Why yes sugar, she loves you and wants what's best for you, so you need to get out of here and go get your man, he needs you more than anything right now. Amylee: But my mother? Nurse: She wants the best for you and Jason is the best for you now, your mother has joined your father up in heaven right now, she is as happy as can be, you don't need to worry about your mother. Go on get out of here. [Amylee is still crying a little bit, but a smile somehow forms on her face] Amylee: Thank you so much for helping my mother through these times, and for the advice, I've got to go, thank you so much. [Amylee starts running down the hallway as the scene fades to black] fade out
