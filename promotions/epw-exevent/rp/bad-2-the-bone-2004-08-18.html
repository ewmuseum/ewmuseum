Bad 2 The Bone

18 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"I make a rich woman beg, I'll make a good woman steal. I'll make an old woman blush, and make a young woman squeal. I wanna be yours pretty baby, yours and yours alone. I'm here to tell ya honey, that I'm bad to the bone. B-B-B-B-Bad. B-B-B-B-Bad. B-B-B-B-Bad. Bad to the bone!" The stereo in my new Porch 911 plays loud as a continue to sing to the lyrics. It's extremely muggy and wet out as I whip down the streets of Boston, Mass I can hear each puddle. Today I decided to give myself a new look for a Wednesday. I great look at that, wait, no, a prefect look. Everyone likes to underestimate my power and abilities, well damnit that ends today. I pull into a bar on the South side of town. I'm not a big drinker, hell I hardly drink at all. Once I stepped out of the car everyone began to point, and laugh. "What a freak!" One lady said wearing slacks, and a short black top. "Hey, nice ass baby!" A big biker guy said laughing. I didn't think there was a problem with what I was wearing. Hell gotta fit the occasion I figured. I walked through the double doors into the bar as everyone couldn't help but to take a glance over at my tight leather pants, big black cowboy boots, and leather Harley Davidson vest. More people began to snicker, but I ignored them. As I walked up to the bar I sat down next to a lovely blonde girl. I figured I would be smooth. "Can I buy you a drink?" I asked the lady. The lady turned around and gave me a quick smile, I thought for sure she was falling for me. Her grin then went down to a frown as quick as that. "Get lost ass hole." She said with pitty. "Listen, I know it must be hard speaking to such a man of my talent, and virtual popularity, but are you from Memphis?" I asked. "That has got to be the oldest one in the book. No, I'm not from Memphis, and I'm not the only ten you see." "Well then, I am very appalled. I wasn't asking that, I actually have a grandmother from there, and I might have seen you on the streets there pretty lady." I said, completely lying through my teeth. "Yea right. I said get lost ass hole." The lady said angerly. "Can I just buy you a beer? How about some rum and coke. I was told Captain Morgan's and coke wasn't so bad." Just then a guy walked up to me and put his large hands on the bar table and hovered above me. He looked at me with a mean face. He was indeed another biker dude. Big long beard, black and white bandana, hasn't bathed in weeks. The smell practically drove me ugly. "Do you mind? I'm having an A-B conversation here." I said. "Oh let me guess, you want me to C my way out huh?" "Actually let me rephrase that punk. I'm having an A-B-C-D-E conversation. And yes I would like you to get the F out!" I said, now completely aggrevated. The guy didn't take to keene to that remark. He took a pool stick and broke it over his knee trying to impress the girl, and intimidate me. I took an empty beer bottle and smashed it over the bar stool. Now to my suprise it shattered in pieces. "Definitely not how they do it in the movies." I said to myself. The guy then grabed me and tried to strangle me with on half of whats left of the pool stick. All the bikers gathered around like it was some pig fest. I was not about to be humiliated. I kicked back as far as I could and got the man in the sack. He let his grip go and bent over, I then smashed a beer bottle over his head and through him to the floor. I jumped up onto the bar table as more and more bikers gathered around, about ready to tear my head off. Just then a voice yelled over in the back. "Wait a second! It's Ricky Summers!" The whole crowed went silent as they began to peer at me. I didn't know if this was a bad thing, or a good thing. But all I knew was I needed to get out of here. "Hey Ricky! Is that you? Hey everyone, it is, it's Ricky Summers! Stop, stop the commotion!" The man yelled. The biker got up and extended his hand to me. I didn't know whether this was a trick or not, but I went for it. I shook his hand and everyone gathered around. I sat up on the bar table and called for a round of beers. I man wearing blue jeans and a sweatshirt walked over to the crowed. "Summers, tell us about the football game where you sacked Florida States quarterback five times." The man said. "All right guys, gather around, here is how it went." I said. Suddenly things to a dramatic change for the best I guess. Just like that, I beat a biker and get recognized. I took off my bandana, because I knew it wasn't me. I am who that guy knows me as. Ricky Summers, best damn linebacker around. Perfection at its finest. "Alright it was October of the year two thousand two. It was a pretty cold day for the Gators and the Eagles to play, but we fought hard. I picked up four sacks into the game. This game was all tied up in the fourth quarter. Finally with three minutes and fifteen seconds remaining Boston College scored a touchdown to break the tie twenty-eight to twenty-one." The bikers, and drunk all peered at me as if I was telling a bunch of kindergartner a lunch time story on the magic carpet. "Somehow Florida State was thirteen yards away from the endzone with eleven seconds left. The quarterback passes to the endzone, and NO! It's deflected by the cornerback. It's now fourth down, I am sweating heavily. I already had four sacks, I could here the crowed. Ricky! Ricky! Ricky! The quarterback drops back, I rush in and get knocked down. The quarterback throws the ball, and completes it at the one yard line. A quick time out is called." I continued. I call for another beer and pound it till it's halfway full. I don't drink much, so I was getting quite a glow if you know what I mean. I started to slur my words, and the tables began to bounce heavily. "Alright now where was I?" I asked. The biker dude with his rag over his head trying to stop the bleeding spoke up raising his hand. "It's fourth down, they just got it at the one yard line, a timeout was called." He said. "Thank you. Anyway A timeout was called. One-second left on the clock. I thought for sure they were going to run it in to tie the game. The quarterback snaps the ball, goes to hand it off to the running back, but fakes it. It's now a play action pass underway. The quarterback goes for the throw, but pump fakes. I rush in and get knocked down. The quarterback begins to scramble out of the pocket. The crowed goes wild. Ricky! Ricky! Ricky! I get up and charge the quarterback. The QB is at the five, the four, the three, the two, then smash! I sacked the quarterback." I said in excitement. I couldn't believe it. These drunk guys were taking it like it was live or something. Reliving the moment, they jumped up and down with there beers in hand. And began to cheer. I knew I was drunk, I knew I over exaggerated a bit. But I was loving it. "What about this Tom Conway character in Evolution Pro Wrestling?" And then it happened. They mentioned the man I hate more than anyone in the world. I man that screwed me out of last weeks competition. He set me up on the finally punt but deflating it. "Tom Conway? The-Con? You mean the punk that needs to cheat to win? The bitch that has my soon to be Evolution Pro Wrestling Legacy Championship title?" I said angered. "Yea the guy who beat you in the punting contest. Not much of a punter eh?" The guy said snickering a but. "No, infact I play a real position in football. And by the way there skippy, the ball was deflated on my last punt. That punk cheated. But it's all good. Because this Friday night I will win the next competition and face him at Breaking Point for the legacy title. Tome Conway is a little bitch, that has a lot to learn about the game of wrestling. And I will be the one to personally make sure he knows that wrestling, is not his thing. He isn't in the same league as me, and he isn't even wrestling material." I said. "Ricky! Go out there and win this competition. Not for you, but for Boston!" One man yelled. The whole crowed in the bar began to yell and chant. It was like playing football all over again. But there drunk so what do they know. I dropped down from the bar and staggered, I fell but my good old buddy with the bloody head caught my fall. I gave a last wave and walked out the door. I sat in my car and took off. While trying to go forward I messed up and went in reverse knocking over about twenty bikes all line dup. I could see one biker come out and yell shocked. Then the whole crowed came out. The bikes dropped like dominos. I took off quickly as the bikers began to throw some bottles at my car.
