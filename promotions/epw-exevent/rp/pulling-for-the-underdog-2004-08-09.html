Pulling for the Underdog

9 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Coming to grips with a loss is hard enough as it is, but when somebody sticks their head in business where it doesn’t belong then we have ventured into totally different playing fields. I had that chair held high getting ready to clear up some things that I thought needed to be cleared up. Then Maize came to the rescue. The only gratitude that Maize got for coming to the rescue of Alias was a street fight with me. I plan on making him think twice before he ever tries to play hero with someone again. The scene opens up with Kyle Pearl sitting on the couch in his house. He is wearing a wife beater and some jean shorts, and is showing no interest at all in moving from the spot that he is in. Molly on the other hand is rushing around seemingly rushing around for something. Every time she passes Kyle she gives him a look of disgust, but continues to rush around without saying a word to the idle Pearl. She continues doing this for about 10 more minutes before letting her emotions lash out at Pearl. Molly: Look Kyle, you lost one match, and you are moping around here like your best friend died. Get your ass up and get ready you have to take me to my doctors appointment and I can’t be late. I’m sure if you plan on sticking around in EPW for as long as you say you are then you will get another shot at Total Mayhem, but until then you can’t just sit here and mope. It’s not good for you. Kyle: You wouldn’t know anything about it. And if you know anything about me then you no losing the match wasn’t the half of it. If we were watching the same match I had it won until that fuck up went and got himself pinned, and just when I was going to get my revenge for getting my first loss. Maize had to come down to the ring. I don’t think that it is Alias who needed to be put in his place that night. Molly: You know what they are saying about Maize don’t you? They’re saying that he could be the future of EPW. They are saying that he could be what knocks Chapel off of his throne. Do you think that you are up for such a task? You might take smaller bites of your food pal. You’re in no position to be fucking with World championship caliber wrestlers right now. Kyle: I don’t give two shits who he is. You don’t interfere in something that isn’t any of your business. I’m going to show him why on Friday. It’s going to be just him, and me in a street fight. The first person to bleed loses the match. If I have to, I will shock you and the rest of the world, and show you all why Maize isn’t anything but just another hopeless superstar that’s going to get lost in the midst of all the hype surrounding him. Molly: I’m just warning you. If you think like that all the time then pretty soon you will be in for a rude awakening. One day your going to meet the guy that loves to brawl, and get banged up just like you, and that is when you will learn that thinking you can beat anyone’s ass will only get you so far in the business. Enough of this chit chat though, you need to get up, and bring me to the doctors. Kyle reluctantly gets up off his ass, and escorts Molly to Civic. They get into the car, and Kyle begins to drive towards the city. On the way to the doctors there are very little words being exchanged between the two. Kyle seems to be thinking a lot, and it looks as if he is having trouble focusing on the road, and the tasks that are at hand. I don’t care what anyone says. I like being the underdog. It makes winning that much easier. When it comes right down to it though, I know that Street Fights are my environment. I’m used to fighting in the street, and I take enjoyment out of beating the shit out of my opponent until I see blood. That’s just the way things are. I hope that Maize is ready. Not just physically, but mentally, because that is the only way that he is going to defeat me. They pull into the parking lot of the doctor’s office, and get out and walk into the building. There are a few people in the waiting room, and a couple of kids on the ground playing with action figures of some sort. The two take the two open seats, and Molly waits for her name to be called to see the doctor. Kyle picks up one the Sports Illustrated magazines, and begins reading it. After Molly’s name gets called she leaves into the back room. Kyle, not paying any attention before, starts listening in on what the little kids were talking about. Kid #1: I’m sick of playing ninjas. Hey, did you see EPW Warfare last night. It was so crazy, get this, Ricky Summers and Tom Conway are in a Punting Challenge match. And Maize whooped this nobody, but that’s not all, after he was done he made a change to his match with that Pearl guy. He changed it to a First Blood Street fight. Kid #2: No way dude, I can’t believe I missed that. My stupid sister had a date downstairs and I couldn’t watch T.V. Girls, who needs them? Maize is the man, he is going to totally whoop on that new comer. What did you say his name was again? Pearl? That’s kind of a cheesy name don’t you think? Kid #1: Forget talking about wrestling; let’s pretend these ninjas are the real wrestlers. My wrestler will be Maize, and yours can be Pearl. We can use these toys as our weapons, and have our own little street fight. (The other kid shakes his head no) aw c’mon why not? Being Pearl isn’t really that bad. I promise I won’t whoop up on you to much. Kyle gets up from his chair, and steps on one of the action figures, and throws the other one across the room. It hits the wall and loses its legs and arms. There are no sounds except for the whimpering of the little kids, and the gasps from the surrounding adults. Pearl leaves the doctors office, and sits down into his car. He doesn’t move or make any signs of anger. He just sits there, and thinks. I’ll show them. I’ll show everyone. They all think that Maize is hot shit. I’m going to knock this guy off of his throne, and show everyone who the real future of EPW is. They will all learn to never underestimate someone of my caliber again. The only troubles that I am going to have with this street fight is when I finally see Maize’s blood I don’t know if I have it in me to stop. I am going to beat away at him until there is nothing left. Kyle thoughts are interrupted by Molly. She gets into the passenger side of the car, and just stares at Kyle with a look of humility. She looks down at her hands, and then back up at Kyle and still seems to be at a loss for words. Kyle on the other hand continues to think, and pretends to pay no attention that Molly even exists. Molly, no longer holding back her frustration, bursts out in anger. Molly: What the fuck was that all about? I come out of the doctor’s office, and I hear all this talk about some psycho that has nothing better to do with his life, but ruin little kids’ fun. What in the hell could little kids quite possibly have done to you to piss you off that much? I don’t know where you get off getting angry at the world, but you need to get that shit in check. Kyle: You just don’t get it do you? I don’t have to explain myself to nobody. I’m bring you home, because I have shit to do to prepare for my match. If you don’t like that then I can leave your ass here, but your definitely not coming back to my place. Kyle peels off out of the parking lot in the direction of Molly’s place. Molly, still at a loss for words, doesn’t say anything else to piss of Kyle anymore, and watches him all the way back to her place. When the pull up Molly opens the door, and steps out of the car, and Kyle peels off and doesn’t look back once.
