Whatever it takes.

19 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A cornered animal is the most dangerous animal. More dangerous than an angry one, a frightened one, a cornered animal presents the biggest threat to safety because it is forced to do whatever it has to do to survive. It forgets about reasoning, common sense, its own well-being. It would rather leave the situation bruised and battered than not leave it at all. You push something close to the edge, and it will do what it must to not fall. Shawn Singleton is on the edge. Backed into a corner, he is going to whatever it takes. Survival was never glamorous or pretty. Shawn doesn't intend for it to be. ***** [The conversation hadn't really been about anything for the past ten minutes. The two teenage boys sit on the bus stop, alone, in the mid-afternoon heat. The rain had already subsided, leaving the sun to provide the raw, dry heat that is customary in this region. The blond, crew-cut kid, no more than 16, wipes his hands on his orange shirt. His friend, a black teen with noticeable peach fuzz, adjusts his white t-shirt, and uses his wifebeater to wipe his forehead. He reaches in his pocket, and pulls out a mesh trucker hat with his name, "Darren", sprayed on it in black letters. They both lean back on the aluminum bench, awaiting their transportation.] "You watch Aggression last week?" he says, patting his pants pockets in search of that starburst he saved from the mall. "Naw, I was over Trent's house, what happened?" Brian replies, still trying to wipe the chocolate off of his fingertips. "Aww, man, it was actually really good. Your boy went off, whooped these two guys' asses. It was pretty brutal," Darren says, finding the cherry-flavored starburst he desired. "Wait...who?" Brian asks. "Singleton," Darren responds in between juicy chews. "Oh, yeah, he's cool as hell, dude just kicks ass for no reason. Just out of the blue, he'll go off," Brian says, almost admiringly. "Well, you should've seen what he did to these two tall guys, I think they're some new tag team. The show starts, and The Hunter is watching Shawn pace around, getting angrier and angrier the more he walks. And you know how Hunter is like a sick freak, but he's in a wheelchair, so he's Shawn's mentor. So, he's liking what he sees, so he's like 'Shawn, go out there and don't come back until somebody's blood is on your hands.' So, obviously, something cool is going to happen..." "Obviously." "...so like a few seconds later, the two guys, I think they're Total Khaos or...Total Mayhem, that's it. So, yeah, they're walking out of their locker room, and as they get near the corner, the first one gets nailed with a chair shot to the head. I'm talking, Shawn hit him so hard, the chair like broke on the guy's head. So, the guy falls and is bleeding, and Shawn uses what's left of the chair on the other guy, then takes him by the back of the head and slams his face through the glass of a vending machine. It was sick. Like, you know how I used to talk about wrestling...but this dude...there's something really wrong with him. Like, Hunter makes him do whatever he wants, and Shawn just goes out there and gets it done. Dude is like some sort of caged animal, and Hunter just lets him loose." ***** The cage door is unlocked, and the animal within is missing. When will he show up? How much damage can he create? However much it takes.
