Rewind..

21 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens as Alias is sitting on his couch viewing the tape of Aggression.] [Alias watches as Khaos hits The Gateway to Anarchy] Alias: I escaped it once..I thought the mace would have done it..This man is just as sick as I had though..The Gateway to Anarchy two times..I had Khaos..his so called destruction turned into a great match..the match went back and forth noone ever really was ahead.. [Alias stops the tape and looks at the camera] Alias: Khaos..if you ever step into the ring with me again..the next time The Gateway to Anarchy will be closed. On other matters of business..this week I will be facing a Mystery Man..of some sort in the EPW at Warfare. This man will probably be just as laughable as the last. This man will probably act like some nutcase like Khaos thinking he can beat me. I am not going to dwell on this loss last night though. The frustration after the match will be the shock felt during the match at Warfare..Its my time..Alias is going to come to rise.. [Alias removes the tape from the VCR] Alias: I will not dwell in the past!!! [Alias smashes the tape] Alias: Come Warfare no matter who steps into that ring whether it be the biggest legend in this fed ever..or the biggest jobber in this fed they are going to feel another sort of wrath in the ring. The likes of the Malachi Special will be felt..the suffering is inevitable. [Alias stares blankly into the camera with no emotion] Alias: I have come to rise..I will be noticed.. [fade out]
