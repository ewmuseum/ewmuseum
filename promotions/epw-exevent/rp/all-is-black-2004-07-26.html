All Is Black!

26 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Madison Killjoy is shown looking out the window of an old style, dark hotel room. From the outside, nothing can be see but immense fog and rain. As Madison Killjoy puts his hand on the glass window, the camera focuses on his reflection on the glass.] Madison Killjoy: Is this it? Is this what life is all about? Yes it is. Life is nothing but a large cycle of events. Before you are born……you are nothing. After you die……you are nothing. That is the case for most beings, although there are a few exceptions. Chapel, you are one of these exceptions. You see, not only were you nothing when you were born and you will be nothing when you die, but you have been nothing your entire life. You are nothing but shallow human being who is not aware of what is really going on. [Madison Killjoy pulls his hand off of the window.] Madison Killjoy: You were right about one thing. When you said that I didn’t want the title, you were right. I don’t care for the gold. It just angers me that a “man” like you has the title. You are a disgrace to the company that I spend my life, working hard for. I spent my whole life training to get where I am. How did you get what you have? Was it luck? Was it deceit; or was it both of these things? The world is a mysterious place. It boggles the mind how a “man” such as you can cruise through life on nothing but pure luck. The true men are people who work for what they have, unlike you. [Madison Killjoy turns around and looks into the camera. The side of his face can barely be made out by the faint light outside of the window.] Madison Killjoy: You said yourself that you are questioning the existence of a higher being. Why is that? Is it because you have gone through so much in the last few weeks? Is it because you haven’t gotten your way? Poor Chapel; did you ever stop to think that the one that causes your problems is the one that stares back at you in the mirror? My prophecies have proven true. I told you that you would be punished for your sins and everything is already starting to take shape. That’s not the half of it because this Sunday at Soul Survivor, things will get that much worse. [Madison Killjoy rests his arm on the ledge of the window.] Madison Killjoy: Just in case anyone thinks that they are going to interfere in this match, they better think twice. I spoke to John Adams about my concerns and he agreed with me, stating that if anyone is to interfere in this match, the will be fired from EPW. No Singleton. No Ace. No nothing; Just Madison Killjoy and the one that they call Chapel one-on-one in the center of the ring. Chapel, you will see the light soon. Oh yes……you will. When it’s all said and done, you will be lying down in the center of the ring, looking up at the arena lights. You will think that you have reached the heavens but in an instant, will be brought down to the lowest level of hell. Looking into the crystal ball at the future of the EPW World Champion, all is black! [The camera fades to black.]
