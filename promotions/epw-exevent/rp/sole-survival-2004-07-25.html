Sole Survival

25 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*A sole survivor is a person that has beaten all the odds stacked against him, a person that has survived the impossible, the unstoppable, the unthinkable and the unbelievable. This is what makes a sole survivor, some call him a fool others call him a hero and some ignore him while others praise him. I am a survivor as far as I can remember since I was ten years old I have learned to survive on my own I was neither praised nor called a hero but ignored or called a fool. This Sunday will I survive? Will I beat the odds such as the impossible, the unstoppable, the unthinkable and the unbelievable? Only fate knows that answer all to well...but I do know this...I have nothing to lose which makes me....the real sole survivor.* {The takes place at the KHAOTIC KORNER where as usual there is always a long line to get in, inside a sea of people can be seen as far as the eye can see headbanging to the pounding of the music letting all their cares wander away like nothing. The scene shifts to Khaos who is watching them all from afar like a god over a continent of mortals as he sits on a throne like recliner swirling a glass of blood red wine in his hand as Troy walks up to the side of him. KHAOS: This Sunday Troy at Soul Survivor I will go against three other superstars for the right to not only become the Soul Survivor but to earn the right to be the #1 contender for the EPW World Title and challenge whoever maybe the champion at Total Chaos regardless if its Chapel or MDK or perhaps Shawn Singleton....you know something Troy MDK, Singleton and I have something in common we hate Chapel and we would like nothing more than to see him fall by one of our hands, so who will it be only fate knows that answer for sure. *Just then Khaos takes a whiff of the air and comes to the conclusion that its perfume.* KHAOS: We seem to have ourselfs a visitor Troy. Troy: Indeed we do, shall I call in the boys? KHAOS: No need I got this one. Troy: As you wish sir. *Khaos turns his head to see a very beautiful but dangerous looking woman behind him, she is wearing black leather shorts and a sleeveless halter top and boots that go up to her knees she has black hair with purple streaks deep blue eyes and white skin and devil red lipstick. After seeing what's before him Khaos has a slight grin to his face.* KHAOS:Well now its been a long time.....Yukina. How's your grandfather doing? Yukina: John it's been ages since you left Japan and please do not call me by my real name I prefer "Nightshade". KHAOS: HAHAHA fair enough my dear I was just about to suggest the very same. You look very lovely and ever so dangerous like a black widow it astounds me. *Khaos begins to check her out up and down and is very impressed. Yukina is the granddaughter of Ukyo Misato his sensei in Japan and one of his three trainers.* Nightshade: As for grandfather Ukyo he sends his best regards. But thats not im why here Khaos im here to be your assistant not an assistant for here Troy's got that covered I am talking about around the ring a.k.a. your manager. KHAOS: Did Ukyo set this up? Or perhaps you were bored in Japan. Nightshade: Hmm I guess you could say it's a little of both... KHAOS: Very well then you may accompany me to the ring and do as you please. *Nightshade nods to him in a positive manner* KHAOS: Excellent simply excellent now that the stage has been set for this Sunday I will be the soul survivor! These three other individuals have the unfortunate task of taking me on I will start with you Psycho Dan this Sunday Dan we will not have to wait to see who's more dangerous becuase as far as I am concerned taking on you would be a waste of my time once you get into the ring I will throw your skinny ass right back out of it but then again when you lose to my brother you will not be able to make it here becuase your contract will be terminated HAHAHAHA! Jackson Kable is it? A former FBI agent who quit and said that it was not paying you enough? Well that was the stupidest career mistake anyone could ever make you chose the wrong sport to be in becuase here is where I beat the crap of people who think that very same thing and here you are undefeated because you beat a couple of nobody's woopdeefuckindoo. Fact of the matter is all good things must come to an end just ask Ace who's drowning his sorrows in alcohol as we speak, Jackson get ready becuase this is the last pay per view you are ever going to see PERIOD! *Khaos gives a slight pause then closes his eyes as if hes in a serene state of mind then resumes speaking once more.* KHAOS: Finally we come to you Karl Jackson....a man who thrives on competing each and every week man who despite being a 7'4" man has a soft side to him that makes me sick everytime I see it on your segments a man who has a impending rage inside of him a monster if you will, but you cannot let it go becuase of your good guy stature am I right? Of course I am, your wife and that pictrue of your father are barely holding that barrier that holds your anger in. You know it will not be long before you let it all go and end up almost killing someone...what a interesting thought...oh wait thats right you kill someone that weakling Shawn Mathers of course you could have hurt him but you did it cleanly instead of letting your anger out and killing him that way. KJ let it go all your frustration your anger your pent up rage I want to see it for myself because your going to need every fuckin ounce of anger you got to take me down because I don't care if you are 7'4" I will take you out and I will win Soul Survivor becuase I am a survivor I rely on no one unlike you who depends on his wife to get him by BAH your just shell of a man KJ why even show up the pay per view! You realize that the only way to get to the world title is to go though me and I realize that the only way to get to the world title is to go though you let us forget about the other two losers and be the last two standing Karl Jackson vs. KHAOS rage vs. rage and anger vs. anger SHOW ME WHO YOU ARE KJ SHOW ME THE MONSTER INSIDE OF YOU I DARE YOU! Chapel you say its end for me? That's fine by me because it's going to take more than fuckin tap dancing to stop me this time! *Then without warning he picks up the glass of wine and throws it at a picture of Chapel holding the world title on his shoulder you can see wine going down the picture as if it were blood instead of wine as the scene fades to black.*
