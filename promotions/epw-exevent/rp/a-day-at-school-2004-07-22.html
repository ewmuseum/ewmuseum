A day at School!

22 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A day at the school! {The scene opens up to the middle of the day in New York City by the acclaimed newly reopened school Khaotic Korner. Where we see a man stumbles into the school. The inside of the school is being redecorated for the reopening and there is a secretary in the middle of the room behind a desk answering phone calls. The man stumbles a bit as he heads to the desk. The man is revealed to be EPW’s Ace.} Secretary: Yes may I help you sir? Ace: Of course (Hic…) you can help me pretty lady! (Hic….) I… (Hic…) I want to sign up for your school! Where do I (Hic…) sign up? Is there a secret password for this club or what (Hic…Hic…)? I mean my brother isn’t a little owner fag (Hic…) could I still get in? Secretary: I’m sorry I don’t think… Ace interrupting her: (Hic…) Well what if I change my name? (Hic….) I can do that change it to “The deuce of Spade” What do you think? (Hic…) Or how about, Karnage? (Hic…) Come on here give a little! (Hic…) I can go to one of the jobber guys in EPW and say he took my sanity! (Hic…) I know I can quit the Legacy and join with him where we can call ourselves AOL! (Hic…) The Alliance of Losers! {The secretary is seen pushing a button underneath her desk. And within seconds of Ace’s senseless babble two, security guards come barging through. They attempt to take on Ace, but within seconds Ace delivers them both a “Blackjack” after he turns around and grabs the woman by the shirt.} Ace: (Hic…) you tell that son of a bitch (Hic….) that this fry day, fly day, I mean Friday I will (Hic…) finish what I still have to finish because I need to start it before I can finish it! (Hic…) Secretary: I…I don’t know what you are talking about! Ace: (Hic…) Tell Big Bad Kahn or whatever his name is that I will dominate him in this Soul Survivor Tournament (Hic…) I will be at the final dance not him! {He heads in to the practice area where the students practice their moves.} Ace: Khaos! (Hic…) Khaos! (Hic…) {The other students look at Ace with amazement that a drunken guy is making an ass out of himself.} Ace to one of the students: Hey (Hic…) Where is he? Where is the big bad Krapper? Student: Listen he’s out at the moment! Here is $20 bucks if you need to get back on your feet? {Ace takes the money and delivers a “Blackjack” to him.} Ace: Do you think I need your sympathy? (Hic…Hic) Well I do need your money! (Hic…) Khaos! Since you’re too chicken shit to face me (Hic…) I’ll just have to beat that ass of yours at Aggression! {He then let’s her go as he stumbles around some more. As he heads out the door he stops near a thing of plants near the front entrance. Unzips his pants and let’s his bodily functions go inside the plants. After he is done he zips his pants up and goes outside.} {Will he be victorious in his match against Khaos? Will he even be sober for his match? Will he get over his misfortune? For this and more stay tuned for Fridays Aggression to find out.} {As Ace leaves we notice a shadowy figure from behind one of the walls. As we try to take a closer look we notice the figure is gone but a rose is left in it’s place as rain starts to pour down as if it was magic.} {A Storm is coming!} {The scene fades to black.}
