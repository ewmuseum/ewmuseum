Friendship

29 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He walks down the street’s of New York City, it is the middle of summer but he has his jacket zipped up, his eye’s are covered by a pair of expensive sunglasses, his face is emotionless, litter blow’s by carried on the breeze of a passing car… he doesn’t notice, he has come to this city to try and save the life of someone he considers a friend… he hopes that he isn’t too late. He stops outside an apartment building, he looks up at the entrance and then enters, he walks up two flights of stairs as the elevator is broken and reaches the apartment he wants, he stares at the door number and then knocks… "The Man of Chance" has come to try and save the "Ace of Spades" Ace: *muffled, behind the door* wha- WHO THE HELL IS IT?!! Chapel: Ace, it’s me Chapel… open the door will ya’ Chapel leans into the door and tries to listen at what’s going on behind it… all he can hear is loud rustling, the odd banging and the occasional curse word… he straightens up when the door is opened a fraction, Ace can be seen peering through the crack… Ace: …the hell do you want, man? Chapel turns his head away from the stench of alcohol radiating from Ace’s mouth Chapel: We need to talk… can I come in? Ace: *shrugs and opens the door wider* you cassa et me cassa… or something like that. Chapel enters the apartment and takes stock of the place… there is nothing but trash on the floor, empty liquor bottles line every available space, he looks towards the table near the door and sees a smashed picture frame, he picks it up and looks at it… inside the broken glass is a picture of Pamela Frost, Chapel shakes his head and puts the picture back on the table… Chapel: *mumbles to himself* …I hope you are happy now Pam Ace: *closes the door and turns towards Chapel* Was’ that? Chapel looks up at his friend and sees him properly for the first time, he has bag’s under his eyes that you could carry luggage in, his face is grimy and un-kept, his clothes are dirty… in short, Ace looks like a shadow of his former glory Chapel: *sighs* It’s nothing man… *takes he puts his shades inside his pocket and takes his jacket off* …anywhere I can hang this? Ace: *walks over to his couch* Just throw it somewhere… *He sits down heavily and picks up the bottle of whisky in front of him* Chapel: *looks round for a suitable, he thinks it better to keep his jacket on* I’ll keep it on… Ace: Suit yourself… *takes a swig out of the bottle* (hic) …so what can I do you for? Chapel: *walks to the chair opposite Ace and tips the rubbish out of it, he sits down and looks at his friend* Well… I came here to talk about you Ace: *takes another swig* W-what a-a-about me? (hic) Chapel: well for one thing, you are a total bloody wreck… Ace: Heh, you crazy B-brit’s and your accents… Chapel: I’m serious here man, look at yourself… when was the last time you had a wash or a change of clothes for that matter? Ace: *thinks* well now… that would be the day that I found my girlfriend in the arms of another man… Chapel: …what the hell happened to you? What’s become of the guy I respected? Where’s the guy who took down names and took no crap? Ace: *takes a swig* …I’m sorry he doesn’t live here anymore Chapel: Bullshit, I know he’s down there inside of you… you just buried him under a ton of emotional baggage. Ace: Heh, I’ll drink to that… Ace lifts the bottle to his lips, Chapel reaches forward quick as a flash, grabs the bottle and throws it against the far wall where it shatters satisfactorily Ace: …hey, I was drinking that Chapel stands up quickly and slaps Ace hard across the face Chapel: WHAT THE HELL IS WRONG WITH YOU!! Ace: *touches his face where the stinging is slowly fading* …what’s wrong with me? You wanna know what’s wrong with me? HOW ABOUT I GIVE YOU A FUCKING LIST?!! Ace rises out of his sitting position, Chapel takes a step back and readies himself Ace: NUMBER ONE, I LOST MY NETWORK SHOW!… Chapel: *Calmly* …show’s get cancelled all the time, it was nothing personal Ace: NUMBER TWO: MY GIRLFRIEND SHACKS UP WITH ANOTHER GUY BECAUSE OF ME… Chapel: …Pam was a slut, she fooled us all with that innocent act, it was better you found out now then in the long run Ace: Number three, and this is my favourite… my cousin, my blood, he dies in the ring… possibly from a drug overdose fed by one of the assholes in the locker room Chapel: *leans in close to Ace* …Shit happens, deal with it Ace glares up at Chapel with a scowl on his face, he brings his fist back and smacks him in the gut Ace: How dare you? Ace picks Chapel up by the hair and punches him in the face Ace: HOW DARE YOU? Ace throws him against the wall and holds him up by his shirt Ace: WHO THE HELL DO YOU THINK YOU ARE? Chapel: *spits out some blood* …I’m the last friend you have Ace lets go of Chapel and drops to the floor, Chapel wipes the blood from his mouth and looks down at his friend Chapel: You couldn’t have done anything about your show, it was the networks decision to cancel it… I was right about Pam, she would have cheated on you in an instant even if she had a ring on the finger of hers, and as for Shawn… I know he was your family, I know you loved him but getting drunk in your home and screwing up at work is no way to go about honouring his memory… do you think he would have wanted to see you like this? Ace says nothing, he just slowly shakes his head Chapel: *crouches down and looks at his friend* drinking away your memories is not a solution, it’s a cry for help… I want you to know that I’m here for you, anything you need I will help you get… but you’ll have to give up the booze, I can’t do any thing unless you take that first step. Ace still stays silent looking at nothing, Chapel straightens up and sigh’s Chapel: I have a match this week against Shawn Singleton, I would have liked it if you could have come down to the ring with me… to watch my back, but if you stay like you are now then you are of no help to me Chapel turns and heads for the door Chapel: I want to help you Ace, but first you have to help yourself… Chapel closes the door behind him as he leaves, Ace still sits on the floor and stares at nothing, as if it was just a reflex, Ace’s hand reaches for a half full bottle of scotch that is next to the chair… FADE OUT
