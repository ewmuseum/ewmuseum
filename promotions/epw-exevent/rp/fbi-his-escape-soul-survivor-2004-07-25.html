FBI- His Escape(Soul Survivor)

25 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We had finally caught up to him. By the look on his face he was not to happy when he saw all of us already beside him. He tried kicking and everything he could do to get us away but it didn't happen. We had caught him. Suddenly from above us we saw all of the guards that were in the plane gliding don on there parachutes. They got to us and started punching. Joey, while all of this was happening just flew away safetly because we had been so busy with these guards. After we were done with the guards again we had saw that oey had gotten away yet again. We were pretty much at the bottom of a safe landing. We came down and hit the ground a bit harder than I thought. We quickly un hooked our parachutes and started runnnig towards Joey.) Stephen: Get back here! Jackson Kable: Nah, just let him go. Christina: But he's getting away and we might not find his hideout. Jackson Kable: Well first thing is that I put a radar on his jacket when we were fighting him. And second of all is that we are on a island that isn't very big, so its impossible to get a hideout. Stephen: I guess your right. And we need a rest before we do anything else crazy here. (We all went and saw a really nice spot to sit under a really big tree. It was hard to figure out what the tree was, but there was lots and lots of shade under it so that's where we went. We really didn't bring anything to eat because we had our bags full already. All of us heard each other's stomach's growling but there was no food to eat. We sat there and started talking about everything we had done on this adventure so far.) *15 Minutes Later* (We got off the grass and stretched. We had been talking for 15 minutes already. I pulled the radar panel off my belt and looked at it. It showed a picture of Joey walking through the beaches shore. He had a whole bunch of sticks piled together spelling the word DOWN HERE! He was crying when his face was shown on the radar panel. I put the panel back onto my belt and we started walking towards the area where Joey was. We got there, and saw that a ship had stopped to see what it was. Joey Campbell was seen entering the boat and shaking hands with the man that owned the boat.) Jackson Kable: Oh no! We have to get on that boat. Its the only way we'll get off this island. Stephen: But its already leaving. Jackson Kable: We gotta catch up to it. (We all started running down the sandy area. It was kind of hard because the sand would sink into our shoes and make it harder to run. The boat was already pretty much gone. We dived into the water and started swimming. Our utility belts were soaking wet and some of it was probably ruined. I took out my extra hook gun and shot it at the boat. It hooked into the side of the boat. Stephen and Christina all jumped at me. We were all hanging on. Now all we had to do is pull ourselves up to the boat and became un noticeable.) Jackson Kable: Good job everyone. Now we gotta pull ourselves up one by one to get to the boat. (I was the first one to pull myself up. It was really easy for me because of all of my muscles. I quietly snuck onto the boat behind a large barrel. Stephen and Christina had a hard time pulling them selves up but after a few minutes they did. I looked at me utility belt and saw that everything was soaking wet. I was surprised that the radar panel and everything wasn't wrecked. I looked at the back and it said that these items were waterproof. We all were sitting behind a large barrel as we heard Joey and the man who owned the boat talking.) Stephen(whispering): Good work everybody. (I looked barely out of the barrel to see what was happening. The guy who owner the boat was giving Joey a diving suit with a huge oxygen tank and mask and everything for diving. He was going to dive off the boat and swim to where ever he'll get. Joey put the suit on, and in a instant he sat off the ledge of the boat and fell into the ocean. We jumped out as the guy who owned the ship was frightened to see us.) Guy: What do you want? how did you get on my ship. Jackson Kable: We were stranded on that island to so we snuck onto your boat. Can we ask a favour from you? Guy: Like what? Jackson Kable: Can we have three diving suits with three oxygen tanks and masks and everything? Guy: I guess so. Stephen: Thank you very much. (We all took the suits and put them on. We hooked the oxygen and everything up. All three of us sat on the ledge of the boat and dropped backwards into the ocean as the scene started to fade.)
