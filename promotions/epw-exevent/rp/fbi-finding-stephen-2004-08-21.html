FBI- Finding Stephen

21 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Me and Christina kept walking through all of the rooms looking for Stephen. If some of you didn't know, Stephen got captured by Joey Campbell's guards, and now we were set out to find him and get him back to us. We opened one more door, and saw a few guards in the corner of the room punching someone. We got a glimpse of the guy they were punching. It was Stephen. We started running at the guards. They all turned towards us as Stephen started to smile to see that we were here. I punched two of the guards out and Christina did a in air spin kick to the other two guards. I walked over to Stephen and untied him from the chair he was stuck on. He also had a dirty sock in his mouth. I took that out. He spit on the ground two times to get that bad taste out of his mouth. He got off the chair and all three of us hugged.) Jackson Kable: You ok man? Stephen: I'm ok. That sock was starting to make me choke. Christina: No kidding. (All three of us started wlaking towards the door that was at the end of the room. Before we went into the door we looked through the window to see if anybody was in this room. There were a few guards, but this time it was a huge room with tractors and everything driving around the huge room. There were lots of computers that men were sitting at probably working on stuff for thr bomb Joey planned to kill the world with. Beside the door that we were stansing beside, we saw another door that said employees only. We walked into that door, and saw come of the costumes that the men in that room were wearing. All three of us put a costume on, and then walked into the big room with all of the people in it.) Christina: This is getting kind of scary. What if we get caught? Jackson Kable: That's the thing. We won't. Christina: Ok, but if we get caught its all your fault. (We walked by a few of the guys that were working there and they just looked at us and then turned away and did whatever they were doing before. We walked up a few set of stairs and saw on the wall that there was a sign on the wall that said Joey Campbell's office, and it had an arrow pointing to the right side. We went that way to find him. We saw a huge door with a name plate on it that said Joey Campbell. We knocked on the door and then walked inside. Joey Campbell was there sitting in a leather chair writing something on a piece of paper. He looked up from his desk and looked at us.) Joey Campbell: What do you want. Jackson Kable: I want you to put your hands up! (I ripped the costume off and pulled the gun out of my belt. He put his hands up, but some guards came from behind me and both hit me in the back. They started stomping on me with hard black boots. Some more guards came into the room, and got Stephen and christina pinned down to the floor. All of us tried to get free but the guards were too strong. They had captured us. Two guards had to hold me down because I was the strongest person in the room.) Joey Campbell: Haha. You actually thought you could get me that easy? Well think again. (Joey got off the seat he was sitting on and opened the window. I heard a helicopter coming towards the mountain. The helicopter stopped right beside the window of the mountain. Joey jumped onto a rope ladder that was coming from the top of the helicopter. The helicopter left leaving us with all of his guards. The guards pulled out ropes from there back pockets and started tieing us to chairs and anything they could see in the room. Then, the guards left leaving us alone in the room tied up to chairs and desks.) Christina: Now what are we going to do? We are stuck here. Stephen: And we can't even move our fingers. Jackson Kable: Don't worry guys. I have a plan. (I could move my fingers a little bit but not that much. I reached to my belt and pulled out the mini tv that I could use to contact Mark. I pressed the button and sudenly Mark's face came onto the screen. I smiled at him.) Mark: What happened to you guys? Jackson Kable: We were inside Joey Campbell's room, but his guards came and tied us up. Joey went in a helicopter and escaped. Stephen: We need help fast. Mark: Well, I'm sending a team in a helicopter right now to the location that your at. They can come into the room and untie you guys and then you can go with them and pick your motorcycles up and put them into the helicopter. Then they can take you to where Joey plan's on going next. Jackson Kable: Ok, how long the wait for that team? Mark: About 10 minutes. Jackson Kable: Ok, we are going to wait here until they come, ok? Mark: Ok, see you guys later. (Marks face went off the screen and it was blank. Now we had to just wait until that team came to untie us so we could go and get Joey Campbell.) *10 Minutes Later* (Suddenly, we heard a helicopter sound coming from the window. A whole bunch of people came through the window and ran over to us to untie us. We all got free and then hopped out the window back into the helicopter. We flew downwards because we had to pick our motorcycles up from where we had parked them. The helicopter stopped on the ground and all of the people got out to load them onto the helicopter. We got them on and then flew away as the scene started to fade.)
