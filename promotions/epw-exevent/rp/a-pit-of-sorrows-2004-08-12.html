A Pit of Sorrows!!

12 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Pit of Sorrow {The scene opens up to an abandon, empty, and condemned warehouse in Roswell New Mexico. Inside this condemned warehouse, lying amongst the broken crates and scurrying rats exists a man that on this day has lost all hope. A man that on this day has decided to sit in a broken down old building to drink his sorrows away. The only thing filling up his soul is the elixir he is consuming and the sorrow he is enduring. Some may ask “What kind of sorrow would cause a wealthy man as this drink?” The answer is simple really, his cousin is dead, his girlfriend cheated on him, his talk show is cancelled, he lost his IC belt, and his career is going down hill on the fast track. But if we would ask a question here, is that the only thing on this man’s mind?} [Why after all these years am I still haunted by the accident? It wasn’t my fault…..It wasn’t my fault!] {The pitiful man still traumatized by his own demons takes another swig out of his bottle. With thoughts flooding his mind he throws his bottle with all of his rage in his heart. The bottle smashes into tiny fragments off in the corner scaring all of the rats from there current meal they found. He’s had a long career this Gladiator of the ring, but what has it gotten him?} [I thought I could restart my life! I thought if I could run away from the problems of my life then there would be no way it could ever rear its ugly head again! Oh God the rain! I can still hear the rain! He was so young and full of life, and I took it away from him!!!!!!] {The man gets up and hits a nearby wall causing it to crack, but in the process breaking his knuckle causing it to bleed. With each drop of blood that drops on the ground he is reminded of his past, present, and the future he is headed. How could it of gotten this bad? Was the Almighty punishing him? Is he damned to live this live of pain and regret?} [The rain still haunts me to this day as a reminder of the travesty that I have endured! Melissa forgave me, so why can’t I forgive myself?] {The man looks up as if he is looking past the roof of the condemned building, up to the heavens above.} [There is no forgiveness for the crime that I committed! If I wasn’t so damn irresponsible then my cousin would still be alive, I would still have my show, and I would still have my true love Melissa. How could she forgive me!!!! Her son is dead and I’m responsible for that!] ::::: Flash Back::::: {The scene reopens to a time long ago in the middle of the night at Fort Carson 7th Infantry Division of Colorado. A man is seen leaving a bar with a child holding an ice cream cone in his hand get into the mans car and drives off. Moments later the car crashes into a nearby lake reportedly killing the kid instantly. Even though there was no body found the man was still charged with a DUI and manslaughter.} ::::: Flashback Ends ::::: {The scene comes back into view with the man in the abandon warehouse still drinking away his sorrows with a new bottle. As the hours go by so does the liquid in the bottle.} [My fault……..It’s all my fault………….It’s all my damn fault!!!!!! How could I be so damn stupid? I had a promising Military Career, a fiancé and the respect of my family and it’s gone…….all gone!] {The man walks around the building thinking about the ghost of the past. After awhile he decides to go outside where his limo is waiting for him.} Limo Driver: Home Mr. Jefferys? Ace: Yes Jeeves (Hic…) {The man now revealed as EPW’s Ace goes inside of the black limo with nice black leather seats. While inside the phone in the limo starts to ring…..} Ace: Hello? (Hic…) {No answer from the other line but the sounds of rain with heavy breathing.} [What the hell is this?????] {With pain and sorrow in his heart for what the rain reminds him Ace yells at his limo driver to take off.} {As the limo speeds off into the horizon a silhouette of a man can be seen but that’s all that can be seen as rain begins to pour down out of nowhere.} {The scene fades to black.}
