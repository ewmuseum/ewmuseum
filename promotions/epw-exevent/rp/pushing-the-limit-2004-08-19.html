Pushing The Limit

19 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What's it like being perfect? A lot of people always asked themselves that, mostly the average, and below average. Well, being perfect may have it's good times, but it also carries some rough times. Being perfect is being the best at everything, and anything, like myself. Tom Conway decided he thought he was better than myself, well he's wrong. I'm going to prove him wrong, and show him why The Perfect Package is everything he isn't. I didn't get this perfect from sitting on my ass. I worked hard. Determination has a big part to do with being the best. I don't like to lose, because face it I never lose. I've got the talent, the body, and the looks to be Mr. America. Friday Night on Aggression it will be "The Perfect Package" Ricky Summers and "The Opposite of Perfect" Thomas Conway battling it out not in the ring, but on my turf. On the field throwing footballs. Footballs to me are like the average Americans bread and butter. Conway challenged me to my own game, and now he will lose to me at my own game. One thing I learned was never to challenge a ram to a charging contest. Conway will learn never to challenge Ricky Summers to a football contest. It's around three O'clock in the afternoon and the rain continues to poor on the streets of Worcester, Ma. Me and my old high school friends decided to take a trip over to the old cabin where we use to hang out. I'm driving down the road in my Poche 911, Billy Mason is int he passenger seat, and Robert Huffer is in the back. Billy isn't that bad of a kid he just has some anger issues. Robert on the other hand loves to challenge, he's a mini me. The whole trip Robert continuously bugged me to speed down the streets, trying to make me push the speed limit. "Come on Rick, lets see what this bad boy has, huh? You have a Porch 911, now lets use it." Robert said. "Do you want to get pulled over today? Come on Huffer, think for a second. I have to be at Aggression tomorrow night, I really don't feel like calling Gary from a jail cell saying I won't be able to make it." I replied with a little bit of sarcasim. "What a bitch. Come on Rick, now that we know the brakes work, how about we test the accelerator?" Billy Said peering at me. Now normally I would let all them comments pass, but this time I felt a little eager to prove them wrong. I'm no bitch damnit, I'm the Prefect Package. Not only the greatest looking man on the planet, but the most perfect. If I'm perfect o the field, I'm perfect on the road. I stepped hard on the accelerator as it damn near puts me in the back-seat. I could see Billy jerk his head back from the speed. Billy is cheering in the back, he gets high of this stuff. I swerve a bit, but keep control. It's pouring hard out, the windshield whippers keep a steady motion. I see brake lights in front of me, but I just pass them on the other side of the road. Huffer is in the back freaking out in excitement, and Billy is griping his seat belt looking like he will never let go. "All right guys, are you ready for this? Huffer Buckle up, this is gonna be a wild ride." I said doing up my seat belt. with one hand while I steered the wheel with the other. "Oh Jesus, this is gonna be bad. You do know it's raining right? And you do no we could hydroplane at any point in time, right?" Billy said. "Forget that bitch, come on Rick push it baby, push it!" Huffer yelled. I thought about it for a few, but then I just laid off the gas and slowed the car down the speed limit. I could see Billy sigh in relief knowing he's now safe, and not going to be in a casket any time soon. Huffer is in the back upset because he wanted to see us crash I guess. No one ever knows what goes on through his head. I took a quick left off the off ramp squealing the tires, then pulled onto the dirt road where the cabin is. "That was pathetic Rick, I bet I could beat you in racing." Huffer said crossing his arms. "Now that's funny. Are you shitting me, you mean Mr. Perfect himself over here driving. Are you going to take that from him Mr. Perfect Package?" Billy Said laughing. "Yea right, perfect package my ass. That's not what your girl said last week." Huffer said trying to piss me off. "What the hell are you talking about?" I asked. "Lets just say your girl said you weren't so perfect in every way. Like say... Down below." Huffer said laughing. "Yea right, lets just say I damn near split her in half every time" I replied. I pulled into the parking lot, then turned off the car. Billy opened his side door than got out. I opened the drivers side then got out stretching my arms and legs. Huffer gets out and rushes for the door because it's raining. The door is locked, and only I have the key. I figured I would be a dick, and let him wait in the rain. Billy and I took a good look at eachother and knew what we were both thinking. We both jumped into the car and locked the doors. Huffer ran to the car trying to open the door, getting wet from the rain. He bitched a little bit, than we finally got out of the car. I walked over tot he door and unlocked it, opening it up. Huffer charged in and ran for a towel. Billy and I walked in and say on the leather couch and turned on the television set. I turned it over to the Prime-time Central news station. Right when I flipped to the channel a lady was reporting the EPW news. "Now it is not clear who, or what Ace is talking about when he says his son is hear. Ace seems pretty flushed for words, and scared. In other Evolution Pro Wrestling news Ricky Summers loses the Punting challenge to Tom Conway. It was said that the last punt from The Perfect Package was a fluke. Summers claims the ball was deflated, explaining the non bounce." The reporter continued. "It's true, you guys seen it. I punted the ball and it landed and didn't even budge." I said. "You would figure you would notice the ball was deflated wouldn't you?" Billy asked laughing. "I couldn't tell, I think there was a tack or something on it and when I kicked it, it just deflated." "Excuses, excuses." Huffer said. *TO BE CONTINUED*
