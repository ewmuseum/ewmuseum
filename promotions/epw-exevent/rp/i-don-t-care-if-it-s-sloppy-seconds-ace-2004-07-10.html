I don't care if it's sloppy seconds Ace

10 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Russell Wayne's hotel suite Columbia, South Carolina July 10, 2004 6:00 AM Russell Wayne is just waking up, he is wearing nothing but boxers. Pamela Frost is under the covers sleeping quietly as an angel. Russell gets up and puts on some sweatpants and a t-shirt. He looks at Pamela and smiles. He then goes to the kitchen and opens the refridgerator and takes out some eggs and bacon he takes out a couple of pans and a bowl. He turns on the stove and puts the pan on it and then puts the bacon on it. He takes the eggs and cracks them and scrambles them and puts some salt and pepper and sonme butter in the pan. Cooks the bacon and eggs and puts them on 2 plates, he makes toast and puts butter on it and also puts butter on it. Russell brings Pamela's plate to her along with his. He sets down the plate next to her and gives her a kiss on the ear and she wakes up and smiles. She grabs a shirt and puts it on. Pamela: Hey sweety Russell: Hey baby, how are you? Pamela: I'm good, I had good dreams last night. Russell: Really what about? Pamela: Just about me and you. Russell: Man, what did you find in Ace anyway? Pamela: He was good in the beggining but not only did he put work before me he also sucked in bed. Russell: Ha, yeah go figure. Pamela: Yeah, but lets not worry about him though. You can take care of him later.Let's just concentrate on us. Russell: Of course, sorry. What are you doing today? Pamela: Well, I am probably going to go hook up with one of my girlfriends who I know down here and go to the mall and shop. I might also pick up something special for us. Russell gives her a kiss and she giggles. Pamela: What are you doing today sweetheart? Russell: Oh I'm probably going to go work out and hang out at a bar. Pamela: Well, don't get to drunk. Russell: I won't honey don't worry. Pamela: Well, I'm going to get in the shower. Wanna join me? Russell gets up and smiles as the scene fades to black Street before a bar Columbia, South Carolina July 10, 2004 11:04 PM Russell Wayne is walking outside of a bar wearing jeans, boots, a t-shirt and a black leather jacket. He walks by an alley and hear's a girl scream. He sees five guys and a girl against a back wall of the alley and the five guys are surrounding her and prodding at her. He grabs a trash can lid and runs at the five guys and hits the one in the center in the back of the head. He crumples to the ground. The lady crouches in the corner. Another guy punches at him and Russell grabs his arm and hits him on the elbow causing it to go the opposite way. Another guy runs at him and Russell jumps up and kicks him in the face. The other 2 guys run off. Russell helps the lady up. Russell: You ok? Lady: Yeah, just a little shaken. Russell: You OK? Do you want me to get you a ride home? Lady: Sure, if you want. Russell takes out his cell phone and calls his limo driver. He waits with the woman for a few minutes and the limo pulls up. Russell: Phil, take the woman anywhere she wants to go. The lady looks at Russell Wayne in awe. Russell: What? You can help yourself to anything in there and don't be afraid to give Phil a hard time....also Don't feed Phil. Russell smile's at Phil and Phil laughs. The lady steps into the limo and waves goodbye. Russell walks into the bar and grabs a stool. Russell: BARTENDER BEER ME The bartender looks at Russell and his eyes widen. He grabs a Budweiser and pours it in a frosted mug. Russell: Thanks, make a tab for me for the night. Russell starts downing the beer as the screen fades to black. "South Texas Deathride" by Union Underground hits the PA system and Russell Wayne comes to the ring to the screams to the crowd. He's wearing his usual black Armani suit and Rayban sunglasses. Russell climbs in the ring and grabs a microphone. Russell: Well, as you all know.....I lost....not a great way to start a career but hey, win some lose some....now it's time for me to win some. This week I face some guy named Xian something of another....nobody cares who he is so why do a promo on him? I know I will beat him yada-yada-yada. Right now I am going to cut something on that idiot Ace. Ace, you have no idea what you lost. You didn't lose your coveted Intercontinental championship but your girlfriend. You see, she is now mine. She is my trophy and my girlfriend. You lost her and I gained her and there is nothing you can do about it. You were a poor excuse for a boyfriend and from what she's told me a poorer excuse for a lover. You are pathetic. I don't know how you have that Intercontinental championship but you better enjoy it while you can because I am coming up in the rankings and soon, Ace, soon I will hold the EPW Intercontinental Championship. Now Ace, do you think you can handle it? "South Texas Deathride" hits the PA system as Russell Wayne drops the microphone and the screen fades to black.
