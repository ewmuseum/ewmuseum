Raindrops

22 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Trust can't be established based on intial contact. Much like greatness, it needs to be tried and tested, proven before it can be intact. An undying and unquestioned faith in something or someone, a bond that can go unscathed and unbroken. A partnership has been forced, as a tag team main event has been scheduled for Aggression. Shawn Singleton and Madison Killjoy. An unlikely duo. With The Hunter at the helm of Shawn's ship, he has begun to adopt the concepts and policies of his mentor. Accepting The Hunter's mannerisms as his own, he is now the physical extension of Hunter's mind. That's downright disgusting. The Hunter is a man who once knocked a man unconcious backstage during a pay-per-view match, and attacked the referee as his opponent was loaded onto a gurney. He then took control of the gurney and ran him through a glass door. You put the sadistic mind of The Hunter into the physical package of Shawn Singleton, and you have a ticking timebomb on your hands. A man who sees the entire world as an enemy. And the EPW staff trusts this man to have a partner, and place him in the main event? Emilio Dervante, Tom Conway, even Madison Killjoy... Good luck. ***** "Who wanted the buffalo chicken tenders and the ceasar salad?" asks the server, a twenty-four-year-old hispanic man. His hair is slicked back, a little too much mousse, and he has some stubble left on his chin from a bad shave this morning. "That's mine," Shawn Singleton replies from his side of the booth, raising his right index finger to visually cue the waiter to leave the food in front of him. Ian, the name displayed on the tag of his TGIFriday's shirt, places the two plates in front of Shawn. "And the Friday's chicken sandwich goes to..." "Here," Hunter says, motioning for his food. Ian puts the plate in front of him, folds up his food tray and stand, and walks back into the kitchen. Hunter lifts up the top bun to his burger, and examines it. He takes a deep breath through his nose, sucking the top row of his teeth with his tongue. "I said no mayo," he says coldly, the tone of his voice noticeably more hostile than before. Shawn shrugs his massive shoulders, having already stuffed two bleu cheese-covered tenders into his mouth. His black sleeveless t-shirt has a spot of wing sauce on it, and he wipes it up with his napkin before taking a sip of his lemon-less water. "You said you had something to tell me?" Shawn asks, removing the croutons from his salad and putting them on an appetizer plate. "Yeah, I do. I must admit, you did a damned good job last week. I expected nothing less, but you did what I told you, and you achieved success. You even dealt with that little spat I had with their so-called champion," Hunter says, before taking a bite out of his sandwich, the mayo having been scraped off with his knife. A woman in a tight red tubetop and a black mini-skirt walks by. She is very well-proportioned, a cute face to go along with it. She and, presumably her friend, walk past The Hunter and Shawn's booth, heading towards the bar area. "You even, during your destruction of your opponent, had the crowd behind you, cheering your actions. Did you like that? he asks. Shawn looks up from his empty plate of chicken tenders, wiping his mouth with his napkin. "Didn't really care for it here or there, but, it wasn't bad." "See, that's your problem!" Hunter says, moving closer into the table, while intensifying his voice. "You don't need to listen to those people, you don't need to listen to the commentators, the interviewers, the other wrestlers. Not even the men who sign your checks. Only person in this goddamn industry who has any say is me!" A few nearby table occupants pause in mid-bite, looking over at the heated discussion. Ian comes back, a pitcher of water in his hand. "Everything alright, fellas?" he asks, unsure of how to handle whatever response comes from the two large men sitting in front of him. The Hunter looks up at him, mulling over the details in his face, his expressions. He extend his glass, and allows it to be refilled before turning back to Shawn. Ian walks away, unsure of what just transpired. "You take into account what I tell you, no one else should factor in, you got me?" Hunter says, returning back to his normal position, taking another bite of his sandwich. Shawn nods his head, looking down at his salad. He picks up the fork, and stabs into it, eating the remnants. "Tomorrow night, we're in Florida. They thought enough of your debut to put you in the main event. But, they still don't know what to make of you, unsure of your motives. Keep them on their toes, listen to what I tell you, and you have nothing to worry about. There is a path set before you, a trail to success that you must follow. I know this path, because it is mine. I can only lead you to water, Shawn, I can't make you drink," Hunter says. ***** You ever notice that you can smell rain before it arrives? The heat in the air, the salty residue of a forthcoming drizzle or downpour seem to be a dead giveaway of the weather's intentions. Yet, despite all of the predictions and assurance that it will arrive, nothing can be done to alter or stop the inevitable. No matter what, the rain will fall, the thunder will erupt, the lightning will electrify. Mother Nature doesn't care, no matter how much good or how much bad her forces bring. She does her job and lets the chips fall as they may. ***** Hunter sits in his wheelchair outside of Friday's, looking at his watch. 6:03 PM. Shawn walks out, done using the restroom. "You didn't tip?" Hunter looks up at him, shaking his head. "I said no mayo," comes the cold response. Shawn takes out one of the mints in his pants pocket, slipping it into his mouth after discarding the wrapper into his pocket. Hunter looks at him, knowingly. "Let's head to the car, Shawn," he says, firmly. "Why?" Shawn inquires. "It's about to rain," Hunter says in a sinister tone. ***** On Friday, the chips will fall.
