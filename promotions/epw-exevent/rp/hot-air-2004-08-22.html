Hot Air...

22 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Yeah, I got th’ job done. What, you thought I wasn’t even gonna show up that night? Not after that bastard wanted ta put his hands on me… yeah, right.” Xian Phillipse is known for always having something to say. You snap, he snaps back. You call him a bastard, he calls you a rat-faced pencil dick mother fucker. You put him through a table… well, he comes back and beats the shit out of you with a chair while you’re handcuffed to the stall. He was one for paying people back in spades and aces… and in this case, Ace still had a little payback coming his way for that first match Xian had here. Xian wasn’t one to hold a grudge… but like it was mentioned above, You snap… he snaps back. And now he’d have to snap the big man himself, John Adams, in half. Right now Xian was in his car on his way to Manitoba for the show. Most people liked to fly… Xian liked to drive. It was the way the old school wrestlers did things, and it was the way he was brought up in the business. He was trained by an old school wrestler from the late 70s and 80s, but everything that Xian did inside the ring was state of the art, updated, and original only to him. It seemed like most people these days had all the same moves, all the same talk, all the same look. Xian just didn’t care who it was at times, as long as they weren’t boring. This Guarida de Diablo match with Adams… it definitely made things hell of a lot more interesting. If it was just a standard match that he and Adams settled their business in, then Xian might just fall asleep during the match and Adams would pick up a win. We don’t want that to happen, now do we? But Xian’s black Delorian sped through the highways towards its destination. He was on his cell phone hands free, Ill Nino playing low on the stereo. “But yeah, that was some good work ya did there. I gotta commend ya for that… but… how’d ya even get backstage wit’out a pass?” he listened for a moment, and then nodded with a smile. “Ahhh, yeah see, I would’ve thought that would happen. Security ain’t what it used ta be. Slip em one lil lie, an’ they fall like the Prophet… heh.” He passed by a moose on the side of the pine tree-laced highway, glancing at it in the corner of his eye. “So where are ya now, babe?” Some ways up, Xian looked up and saw… another moose on the side of the road. He looked at it, brow wrinkling, but shook it off and started to mess with the stereo. “Damn Canada… oh, oh you’re there now? Good, if you’re gonna hang around there for a while mind bookin’ me a room? I uh… I forgot ta make reservations anywhere. I don’t care where it is as long as it’s a bed, an’ its dry… an’ there’s a place ta eat in walkin’ distance.” After turning a bend in the road Xian looked down to switch CDs… and he either saw a huge stump on the side of the road… or another moose. He barely saw it, but he did kind of a half double take to look back before keeping his eyes on the road. “Crazy place… what’s that?” he asked, lowering the sound. “Oh, you HAVE a place?” he listened again as Brand New played. “Oh, you have YOUR place? Well now… I dunno, we just met an’ all… how do I know you’re---ok, it’s fine wit’ me.” Xian grinned at his playful change of heart, and as he saw his turn coming up on the right he shifted lanes and prepared. “Oh yeah… did you see any moose on your way up here? I swear I saw like…” she stopped him on the phone, responding with something. “Ohhh, ok. I thought I was goin crazy or some shit… I KNOW Adams ain’t gettin’ ta me like that.” Xian chuckled, leaning back in his seat and enjoying the breeze against his face. Canada sure was a breath of fresh air… but Adams… … he was nothing but hot air… and Xian was gonna empty all that out of his lungs…
