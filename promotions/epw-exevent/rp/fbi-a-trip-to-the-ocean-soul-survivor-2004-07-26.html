FBI- A Trip To The Ocean(Soul Survivor)

26 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(All three of us looked around the ocean as we floated to the bottom. It was the best sight we have seen on this trip so far. There was little fish dancing on a big rock. Crabs and everything were swimming at the bottom of the ocean. We were so caught up at looking at the fish we forgot where Joey Campbell had went. We looked around but all we could see were fish swimming around and crabs laying down. We started kicking our feet making us go faster and farther where we had wanted to go. Luckily that man that owned the boat gave us the special ocean helmets that had a walkie talkie built into it so we could talk to anyone. And the best part about it was that the walkie talkies wouldn't get broken because they were waterproof too. This trip was actually becoming worth while. We were swimming a the bottom of the ocean and it was awesome. All the colours and everything was so beutiful. Well, I guess that the colours of the ocean were pretty because they were a green grungy type of colour, but you get the picture.) Jackson Kable: This is awesome you guys. Stephen: No kidding. Christina: I loved the dancing fish on that rock back there. (Suddenly all three of us looked ahead, and through the shitty colour of water we could see something moving. But it was not a fish it was a human. We started kicking our feet really fast so we could catch up to the human. It was probably Joey Campbell but we had to make sure just so we wouldn't lose him again and have to do everything over to find him. We were right on his tail. From the view I was at I could see that it was Joey Campbell's face behind the diving mask. We got to the side of him and he looked at us and panicked. He started kicking really fast. He got a little bit ahead of us and with him kicking water it came straight into our masks. The dirt from the water clogged up the masks makingg it very hard to see. All of us struggled to get the dirt of the mask. But it was no use, Joey had escaped because I pulled my mask off. My black wavy hair went all around the water. I cleaned my mask of and so did Stephen and Christina. By the time we had all put our masks back on we had lost sight of Joey.) Jackson Kable: Damnit. We lost him because of this stupid water. (We started kicking our feet towards the head of the water. We got out of the water and saw that the sun was shining really bright. We looked around us and saw that we were really close to Hawaii. We dove back under the water and started kicking. Joey had probably been really close to Hawaii so we had to get there to get him. We started kicking really fast.) *15 Minutes Later* (I'm pretty sure we got to Hawaii because we got onto land. We dragged our bodys onto the sandy beach. All of us took out maks and suits off and saw that our normal day clothes had not been wet. The diving suit must have been some kind of waterproof so it wouldn't get our clothes wet. We started walking through the sand to get to the city of Hawaii. We looked ahead of us and saw a whole bunch of skyscrapers in the view. We were there just from swimming. We looked behind us and saw that the island we swam from was barely visible. We swam all of that way. We goto onto a gravel road so it would be easier to walk.) Stephen: We swam a lot of the way. Christina: It didn't seem that far. Jackson Kable: I guess time goes by when your having fun. (We kept walking as the scene started to fade.)
