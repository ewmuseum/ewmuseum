Dan's Speaks his Mind

24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Psycho Dan is seen sitting in his locker room just before Soul Survivor, Dan looks in deep thought as if he were troubled. Dan stands and wipes his face with a towel and tosses the towel into a bin near by. After abit of stretching Dan looks up at the camera and gives an insane looking smile to the camera ). Psycho Dan: Soul Survivor I get John Adams in a Scaffold Match. A match I asked for and got, a match that I excel in me being the psycho one, Hard-core Matches are my specialty. John Adams you accepted this match as a Scaffold Match that can end ones career. As I have ended many careers in these kinds of matches. You have made my day by accepting this match. ( Dan smiles and picks up a bag near by, dust falling from the bag as Dan empties the contents on the floor scattering human bones at his feet. ) Psycho Dan: The bones tell me John, that your suffering has only just begun and after Soul Survivor you will know what suffering is truly about. I like the pain and suffering John, it feeds me and gives me purpose. As for my career, my purpose has only been to make others suffer and feel pain. I will embrace are match with all my strength and ability to give every one of my fans what they want, the pain and misery I put upon you John Adams your suffering will be great. And this I promise to everyone in EPW, the total mutilation and humiliation of John Adams! ( Dan slides his foot over the bones and smiles as he seems to be reading some sort of sign in the jangled pile of human remains. Dan goes on one knee picking up a rib bone and moves a few more bones with it and smiles once again. ) Psycho Dan: Freak you seem to like to talk an awful lot, you run your mouth off like a 10 cent whore and make threats and promises. Talk is all you are, like we really wanted to know your whole life about how you met some cheap slut. You come to EPW calling people names and spouting crap and slandering super stars as if you know them. You been here only a short time and already people are tired of your crap, I promise this....You my cheap friend will suffer pain and humiliation. But the bones say not at my hands but at the hands of a man named Chapel. He will teach you what it means to be in EPW, title shot or not you will pay for your loud mouthed antics. As well as boring the hell out of every fan in EPW with all your nonsense with these cheap whores your hooking up with. ( Dan seems to smile slightly as he moves a few more bones and looks up ). Psycho Dan: Seeing as Kaos has returned and has caught my attention with his words I will deal with you as well since my greatest fan attacked you time and time again driving you insane, you have not learned your lesson yet . Yet your suffering has brought me to EPW and you left with your mind scared. Soon Kaos we will handle are unfinished business as I finish destroying what mind you have left.. (Dan starts collecting his bones and placing them in the bag once more. Dan stands up carrying his bundle and walks to the doorway and stops. He turns around his face stern and emotionless. ) Psycho Dan: As far as my career ending, well no one will end my career until I am dead and buried! Soul Survivor will be an event that will make or break the minds of men! And I intend to stay around for a long, long time, EPW will see a change and many will suffer as I make them remember that pain and suffering that will be the future of all in EPW. ( Dan opens the door and walks out of his locker room, the camera follows Dan as we see him walking down a darkened hallway and down a flight of stairs. The camera can just make out another figure in the shadows as Dan stops and seems to be talking to the person ) Psycho Dan: Ahhh you have arrived, ready to begin? Dark Figure: Yes they will not even know what will happen, are plan will be a big surprise to EPW.... Psycho Dan: Yes...lets go to the basement and talk somewhere more private? (We see Dan and this dark figure disappear from view down the stair's leading to the basement. The camera man follows but looses sight of the two men as they walk into the darkness, leaving many to think who this stranger with Dan is). ( Psycho Dan is seen walking from the basement with out his bag of bones and heads back to his locker room. In Dans hand is a yellow envelope with what appears to be money popping from the edge. Dan enters his locker room and puts the envelope in his locker and Dan picks up the phone and hits re-dial and the phone rings ) Psycho Dan: Yah.....this is Dan is Ledge in? Ledge: Yes....this is he... Psycho Dan: Yah everythings set up here, I got a big match tonight and after that I will head to the gym there... Ledge: Ok buddy, We will be waiting for you... (Dan hngs up the phone and remembers his mentore Preacher Ledge from his old IWZ days. Ledge trained Dan in the ways of wrestling and instilled his psychotic ways that Dan has always followed. It will be great to see his old friend again after so many months. Ledge has always been there when Dan needed him the most. Last time Dan faced Ledge was in a match that lead to there seperation and this meeting shall be a reunion that Dan will remember, only true friendship can be this strong. Dan heads off into the shower to prepar for his up comming match and the camera fades ).
