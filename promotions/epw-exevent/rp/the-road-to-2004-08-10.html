[The scene opens up inside a car cruising down the highway, Jason Slade is scene sitting in the passenger seat and Amylee driving her car. The weather is so beautiful, the sun is shinning, not a cloud in the sky. The windows are down and the sound of "The Only" by Static X is heard coming from the car, Jason is bobbing his head, meanwhile Amylee's phone rings. Her hands are a tad busy as she has a drink in one hand and the wheel in the other, she looks around while her phone continues ringing, she sets the sets the drink between her legs and reaches for her phone. Jason turns the radio, just to be a nice guy, Amylee rolls up her window] Amylee: Hello? [The  voice speaks up on the other side of the phone, we don't get to hear what the other person is saying due to the fact that Jason's window is still down and the wind is blowing at a fierce speed] Amylee: Oh no! I'm fine, everything is going great. Mom can you hold on one sec.... [With one hand on her phone and the other on the wheel, Amylee looks over at her window and its rolled up, the gum she has been chewing for the last three hours is scene between her teeth] Amylee: Jason give me your hand please. Jason Slade: For what? Amylee: So I can spit my gum out. Jason Slade: Umm what's wrong with your hand? Amylee: There a little busy, can you just take my gum and toss it out your window? [Jason gets this horrid look on his face] Jason Slade: Not a chance! Amylee: Oh come on! You've had my spit other places on you before, what do you care? Jason Slade: I care a lot, that's disgusting and down right nasty, just roll down your window, use your elbow! Amylee: I'm driving you goof, I can't reach the window button....I have an idea. [Jason's eyebrows raise] Amylee: I'll spit my gum out your window! Jason Slade: Oh hell no, that's even worse than putting it in my hand. Amylee: Now what's wrong? Jason Slade: Well if you spit your gum out your mouth, all that saliva will get all over my face, because face it Lee, you don't have the greatest aim baby! [Amylee rolls her eyes as Jason starts laughing] Jason Slade: Here... [Jason leans over and rolls down her window, she spits out the gum as Jason rolls up her window so she can finish her conversation with her mom] Amylee: Thanks Jason....So mom how is everything? [Her face turns into a expression of confusion] Amylee: Mom....mom.....why do you want to talk to Jason? [Jason leans against the door waving his arms, in a sign of he don't want to talk] Amylee: Yeah sure, he is right here. [Jason has a pretty pissed off look on his face as Amylee hands over the phone to Jason, all of a sudden one of them fake smiles form on Jason's face] Jason Slade: Hi mom!!! [Making a gun and putting it to his head] Jason Slade: Oh yeah everything is great, I started my new job....No, no I'm a wrestler...no a wrestler, not a messenger. [Jason's hand hits his face in disbelief] Jason Slade: Yes ma, I'm wrestling with EPW now, me and your daughter are on our way to Iowa City now for my match on Friday...No ma, not for a bunch of cats... [Jason pulls the phone away from his ear and cups the mouth piece with his hand. Jason looks over to Amylee] Jason Slade: You've got to be kidding me!! [Amylee can't stop laughing, she just clutches the steering wheel and looks straight ahead, her face is turning beat red from all the laughter] Jason Slade: Alright ma, we got to go, aliens have landed and what all our body fluids, talk to you later, hugs and kisses from Amy, bye! [Jason shuts the phone and tosses it into the center console of the car] Jason Slade: I love your mom like my own, but that woman really needs a hearing aide! [Amylee is still laughing] Amylee: You over exaggerate too much Jason, she isn't that bad.. Jason Slade: Baby its like talking to a crazy kid on a chocolate addiction! [The both start to chuckles as Jason looks at the next mile sign, it reads] Iowa City Washington West Branch
