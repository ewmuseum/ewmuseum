Finding myself,My Breaking Point

2 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name:Maize Location:Various Date Promo made:August 2,2004 Date Tape made: Right after Aggression(7/30/04) *The scene shows a darkroom filled with abyss. The only thing that can be seen is a vague shimmer from a 14th century chandelier...Suddenly the cameras focuses in on a dark figure in the middle of the darkness the cameras get closer and closer. Revealed only by the light emitted from the camera is the face of Maize. The camera then scans down the scar on his left eye that transcends from the eyebrow all the way till it reaches his cheek. The look on his face is everything but ecstatic or exultant. It's more like he's irascible and exasperated. He begins to talk with his voice sounding intense. Maize:It wasn't too long after my defeat that I realized something...I realized I can't be really wrestling for the fan's entertainment..can I?..because if I was wrestling for the fan's entertainment...I wouldn't had those feelings tangled inside the pit of my stomach.....but those feelings were ones that I didn't recognize.I kept asking myself,"Can it be defeat? Can It be failure?". NO!... I needed to stop trying to cover up the truth with kind gestures and and false confusment! I knew this feeling very well....It wasn't until now that I chose to accept it....this feeling that conquered my soul...this feeling that has been for many, a doorway to blind anger...This feeling that overwhelms even the strongest of men....This feeling was MORTIFICATION!!..... First,I thought it was ironic that I kept saying Freak would throw himself over the top ropes, but instead that what I end up doing!! Was this fate...Was this destiny....Is there a vex on my soul that I can't even defeat the evilest of men!! It seems that way! As Khaos peered through my eyes with that demonic glance I felt that same tension when Mysterious,from the UPWA, defeated me and I hurled at him with out thinking....because for that moment, I wasn't myself....I wasn't Maize...I wasn't Carrington Banks......I wasn't that man who gives to charities.....but I knew that side of me.............It was the part of me that doesn't look out for the welfare of my son...It was the part of me that causes my eye to twitch............It was that part of me that wanted to INJURE KHAOS!!!!!....When he looked into my eyes and smile, it felt like Satan himself called to me saying"attack him.....he has embarrassed you......he has took your "PRIDE"....CURE HIM FROM THIS VIRUS CALLED LIFE"! And that confirmed it, I'm not who I think I am...I don't even think I'm a "who"....Then "what" am I......And how can I change myself......eventually I found out the only thing I had to do was.....FIND MYSELF!!!Chris Clarence, at aggression, you won't be wrestling the man who appeared on the screen last Friday...You will face a man who has found himself!You will face a man who after aggression,had a supernatural experience.Chris, its kind of hard to explain. So just in-case you need evidence, i got it all on tape. You see Clarence you entered that ring a rookie and came out a rookie! You let an opportunity go to waste and now you face me!....But you don't know what you are up against. Roll the tape Chuck!!! *The camera clicks off then cuts back on.Static fills the screen, but soon the images on the screen begin to get less vague until they can be vividly seen...It shows Maize outside of the Williams Arena waiting on his Limo to pick him up. The camera roams around the empty parking lot with nothing but the wind pushing little slithers of trash across the pavement.Maize then lays down his bag,unzips it and grabs his cell phone out. He dials a number and pace back and forth in the empty parking lot.. [Narrator Maize: I will guide you through this just in-case a stupid piece of crap like you start getting confused...even today people think I made this up....Humph!The camera never lies.....] Maize:.......Yeah where are you with my Limo.......Who the hell you think this is......goddamn this is Carrington Banks......Maize!!.........Forget about apologizing because I lost my match, I need my Limo.......Kevin Garnett what?!!!...So you pick Kevin Garnett up with my Limo.....Sorry my ass!Just bring my Limo to the arena!!....How long? An hour!!! *Suddenly a police officer walks up from the the hallway that leads to the parking lot. [Narrator Maize:Till that day.....I never believed in bad luck.....Like some old man once said, first time for everything.] Police officer: You're going to have secure the premises. We have a charity NBA basketball game tonight.....Midnight madness they call it....I guess you got to tell your ride to pick you up at the Texaco over yonder..... *Without even debating Maize hangs up his cell, picks up his equipment bag, and walks off to what seems a path of darkness with light at the end. Indeed it was the Parking lot exit way. At what seems to be an act of bad luck, when Maize stepped out of the parking lot....it began to thunder storm. [Narrator Maize: You know Chris Clarence...when I look at that night when it rained now...I could swear it wasn't rain that was rolling down my cheek, but I can promise blood will be running down yours!!] Maize:(Trying to sound like nothing happened) Keep the cameras rolling...this might prove to be the headline for "A Jobber's Life" next week......You other guys stay here just in-case the Limo shows up. Chuck get that camera from him,you're coming with me..... The camera points upwards, showing the dark ominous cloud as Chuck is given the camera.......Then camera is then pointed towards Maize face as he quickly turns away and start walking down the street toward a gas station... [Narrator Maize: I was depressed...I let my defeat take over my body and personality......Every thing and person I saw I contradicted it in my mind.] *They continue to walk across those dark,cold, and wet stepping stones we call streets until they came up to the gas station. The gas station was a Texaco with six dark black and white gas pumps. There is also a flash market marked Tiger Mart that serves as the host of the gas station. Maize and chuck walk in the store. As soon as Maize enters,he walks up to the store clerk. The clerk looks like a thirty-five year old Arabian male with a positive aura about himself....Maize cloths dripped to the ground like blood from a slaughtered animal. Arab Man:Welcome to Ti-Ger-Mart, oh you Maize.......Maize favorite wrestler of my son,how you do there...what may I get you..... Maize:Give me the cheapest bear ya got! Arab Man: I got just the thing.... *The Arabian man then bends down and grabs a bottle of some light gray liquid. He then slides it across the counter toward Maize. Arab Man: Homemade beer....mother's special..On the house for son's favorite wrestler...oh by way...can I get autograph...for son.......here is paper you can write on.... *The Arab man hands Maize a piece of paper that is white on one side and red on the other.....Maize signs the white side..... Arab Man: Nice choice...I mean thank you, come again!!! *Maize walks out of the store with a confused look on his face..He stands outside of the store and drinks all of the "Grey liquid" that was given to him...suddenly his phone rang to the tone of nobody knows...Maize didn't set his phone to that tone...If you can remember, earlier that day it was set to amazing grace. [Narrator Maize: I don't know whether it was the free beer or I was still suffering from that lost...............but this is where some weird shit started to happen....Another old man once said"God works in mysterious ways"...I was about to find out first hand...] *Maize looks at the monitor and it says I.M.U.....At first, Maize is hesitant to answer the call......but he slowly and cautiously raise the cell to his ears and pressed talked..... *Maize:Hello? Voice: I know the thoughts that been running through your head.....You are confused.....but it's because you don't remember!! *Maize: Remember what?.......What the fuck are you talking about!!!! Voice: Look to your right....what do you see? *Maize: A jackass camera man by the name of Chuck, hey if you want airtime you gotta pay buddy!!! Voice:(Laughs VERY hard),You are still funny as the last time you remembered!!! Now do you see that abandoned house. *The cameras scans the old run down house with a soulless white texture. Voice: There........YOU WILL FIND YOURSELF!!!!! *The phone hangs up leaving Maize confused as hell! Maize:What in Sam's Hell was that!!? Chuck, I think I may be drunk...So I have an excuse..SO follow me to this one more place... Chuck: Hold I need to Recharge the camera battery... Maize: Well do it... *The camera cuts off then cuts back on. They are in a poorly kept house.....Maize looks around....The only thing he sees is a desk and a chair turned around so. Camera points toward the ground and points back up....It appears that there is someone sitting in the chair. Maize walks toward the chair...He gets closer and closer...before he got to close the person in the chair raises his hand as to tell Maize to halt. Man: Don't come any closer....why are you here? Maize:(Sounding a little nervous)Too..find... my..self? Man:(burst out in laughter)....You actually believe that crap!! (Maize looks kind of confused...He starts to step closer and closer) Man: Don't tell me you took a sip of that gray stuff from the Tiger Mart!!(bursts out into laughter yet again)That always happen...Kid leave here.....HA(mocks Maize)"I'm here to find myself!Bah! Maize:Look here, I have no time to be mock by some ass wipe who is too shame to show his face!! Someone called and told me I will find myself or the answer to my problems here! Anyways who are you!! Man:Didn't you read your caller ID on your cell phone? Maize: Yeah it just said I.M.U........ [Narrator Maize: Pause the tape......(The Images on the screen stop moving)...Now by this point a guy of your IQ,Chris Clarence,is probably more lost than a dead pet fish flushed down the toilet!!So, I'm giving you time to take in what you have seen so far......Buzz!!!,Times up ass wipe....continue the footage! Man: Well then why ask who I am.... Maize: What is your name......I don't want to waste my time playing the name game...Who are you? Man:(laughs really hard) You are the one playing the name game. I've already told you to look at your caller ID! Besides if I told you my name,you would believe me, now would you? Maize: Of course I would!!Tell me!!! Man: My name is Carrington Perea Banks!!! [Narrator Maize: Man, if I didn't try to run my black - out of there as fast as I could...] Maize,along with Chuck, strikes toward the door, but suddenly Maize stops and laughs He walks closer and closer to the man in the chair(supposedly himself)... Man: Don't come any closer.......I have some questions to ask you!!! Maize stops in curiosity Maize: Hurry up you sick bastard before I put a foot up your -!!! Man: Oh like you failed to do to Khaos?(laughs) Maize: Well technically if you are who you say you are,then you lost against Khaos also you dumb -wipe!!!! Man: Well technically, if you want to put it that way, you didn't wrestle tonight!!! You wanted to find yourself and here I am....."I AM YOU"(I.M.U) Maize: You are sick! Why shou....(Maize's eyes lights up in disbelief) The man stood up and turned around. He "Kind Of" resembled Maize, but he has no scar on his left eye. Maize runs again but in the process drops to his knees. The camera moves very fast and when it focuses on Maize it seems the camera is too far away to tell who is who.Maize, then gets up on his feet... The voices are slightly different, you can only hear the dialog..... Man(Maize supposedly):YOU don't remember us.....you forgot....Ever since Zero broke our arm and took the YGP World title away from us....we've never been the same!!! I know that was the first time we lost a title defense match, but "YOU" took it too seriously. Instead, "YOU" left me in that ring...."I" was still down for that three count all theses years....You left me, that part of "YOU"! You were injured for two years!!! You left wrestling an athlete.."A Legend" in your own right!!!!.... Maize: Stop it...who are you really....are you just some sick fan who happens to look like me!!!!!!STOP PLAYING WITH ME!!!!!(Maize starts holding his hear as he starts to shake violently!!!) Man: You wanted to find yourself didn't you.....I'm here to make you remember that side of you that cheated death.You remember don't you?....When Commissioner Slater ran you over with an 18 wheeler!! Somehow you survived...that was because I was apart of you then....I am that intensity that you use to have.....To make it simple we were two parts of a whole....I was the Sports and you were the entertainment...together we were unstoppable....This is the biggy right here..."NOW A DAYS YOU ONLY LET ME OUT AFTER YOU ARE DEFEATED!!!!!"......{Camera shuts off} The camera is focused back on Maize's face in that dark ominous room with the chandelier's reflection giving off it's flair....... Maize:Thats when I started to believe Chris Clarence.......Every single word he said was true...I did lose that side of me....But that day....July 30,2004....After a 2 year losing streak.....I'VE FINALLY FOUND MYSELF!!! And you can bet when I enter that ring in Friday night, you will get you ass handed to you by the new intense Maize!!! It's not going to be a one-on-one Chris......It's gonna be a slaughter!!!And now when I tell you once you step into the ring with Maize there because there is NO WAY OUT! It means so much more!! Because I have reach something that these other EPW wrestlers,including Chapel and Hudman, haven't reached.... A fist flies towards Maize's face. As soon as contact is made...Maize's face shatters like glass...The whole time, Maize was looking in a mirror hosting this Promo....The Camera Zooms towards Maize's fist...A river of blood is dropping down his hand ....The camera zooms in on Maize's face......... Maize: AND THAT IS MY BREAKING POINT!!! {Broadcast Ends}
