Lucky 13

12 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Friday the 13th the day that supposedly means bad luck and superstition black cats and broken mirrors all the while to me its just another day but this Friday the 13th is going to be different some how, something very interesting that only fate knows what's in store for me. I will be teaming up with Shawn Singleton this Friday against Chapel and Ace but this is no ordinary tag match.....for the person who defeats Chapel wins the world title and if Ace or Chapel defeat me I will lose the Intercontinental title....however only one can win it....who will it be? The answer lies in victory.....* {The scene fades in on Khaos in the basement of the KHAOTIC KORNER which is surrounded by a circle of candles lit around him as he stares at his newly won Intercontinental title.} KHAOS: So we meet again....its been three months since I have held this title and yet so little has changed.....I was obsessed with this belt that is what caused my downfall, I did not do things right and therefore I lost it to Chapel. I was thinking "sure just get your belt back and everything will be okay again' WRONG it was not OKAY everything fell apart then after consistently getting attacked by a psycho fan then losing to Chapel almost every week, the AOV falling apart....I could not take it anymore. so I lost it.....my sanity....EVERYTHING....but I soon realized who's to blame for all this it's you Chapel your the reason I came back to settle an old score yes have beaten me multiple times but the only way your going to get rid of me is by pointing a gun to my face and pull the fuckin trigger because it is going to take a lot more than luck to stop me this time. *Just then Khaos hears footsteps from the right of him and a tall man can barely be seen but still remains hidden* ???: Khaos do not be so hasty you will get your revenge on him soon enough.... KHAOS: What the? That voice...could it be..Cain? Cain: Heh I see you have figured it out Lord Khaos. KHAOS: Cain its been..... Cain: One month. KHAOS: Ah yes it has been. What's with the "Lord" crap? Cain: Hahaha I figured you would hate that despite these people that come here every night worshiping you like royalty it is quite amusing I say. KHAOS: Hey I am here to give the people what they want and that is salvation from this filth we call life nothing more, nothing less. By the way who brought you here? That would be me. *Just then a woman like figure appears before Khaos to his delight it is Nightshade.* KHAOS: Well now look who is here Nightshade back from your trip to Japan? Nightshade: Yes the reason was to find Cain and bring him here to sign with EPW. KHAOS: Cain I never knew you wanted to sign with EPW so what's come over you all of the sudden? Bored or perhaps you would like to kick someone's teeth in or maybe give them a lesson in pain. Cain: All the above plain and simple however I will let the victim come to me regardless he will be begging for salvation when I am done with him, however long I choose to take with their suffering then I will give him "The Cure". KHAOS: I see you have already planned on what you are going to do with your prey. As for me my target is Chapel as far as I am concerned Ace is not a factor in this match because I OWN ACE always have always will. My partner is Shawn Singleton a man who I know little about but respect his talent in the ring and if it were not for Hudman he would have been the World Champion a long time ago him and I don't like each other never have never will that is the way it has been for me all my life I trust no one not ever you Cain nor my weakling of a brother BUT we do know that Chapel must be made to suffer by all costs we will stop at nothing in making sure that Chapels suffering lasts an eternity that he will not forget! Failure is NOT an option period! As for the world title I could care less about it, Shawn it is all yours all I want is Chapels head on my wall. So Chapel bring all the luck you can because this Friday the 13th is going to be the worse day in your career and there not a thing you or anyone in EPW can do about it. *Just then Troy walks in and annouces the following.* Troy: Sir shall I inform the boys to start letting people in the KHAOTIC KORNER? KHAOS: I am feeling a little genorus tonight so sure why not let the people though and let them escape reality and enter my realm. Troy: As you wish my lord. Cain: There is that "lord" crap agian hehehe. KHAOS: Yes, but I am starting to like it, this Friday will be Lucky 13 for me. Now let us go and enjoy ourselves for the rest of the night shall we? *Khaos walks upstairs along with Nightshade to the stage while Cain stays behind and moves the camera towards his barely visible face and has it zoom in closer to him only saying this in a cold quiet ghostly like voice.* Cain: Everyone will suffer....Chapel your time as come and the end is at hand so I will ask you the question that you have asked your opponents for years. How do you like YOUR chances? *Cain blows out all the candles then pushes the camera back down as the scene fades to black.*
