And I do want to take chances on this mission. So I guess I will do this. Stephen: That's the spirit. (By the time we were done talking, Stephen had half the wall tooke out. Stephen made sure that it was safe, and then jumped through the hole in the wall. I took my gun out, and also shot it. It landed on the other airplane, just beside where Stephen shot his. Christina shot hers, and it went to the head of the plane. We all jumped out of the hole in the wall, and started dangling from the ropes. With all of my strength I pulled myself up onto the back of the airplane. I started pulling Stephen up to. Then Christina. All had went as planned. We all high fived.) Jackson Kable: It worked!
