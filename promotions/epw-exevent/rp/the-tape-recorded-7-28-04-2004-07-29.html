The Tape(Recorded 7/28/04)

29 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name:Maize Location:Inside a Limo Date Taped:July 28,2004(3:00 P.M. Central) THE TAPE [This tape was sent to you by Maize. You put this tape into your VCR. You then press "Play". You sit down on your cofitable couch. You wonder what contents occupy this tape. The tape finshes tracking and it begins....The scene shows Maize sitting in a Limo with a buisness suit on. It looks like he just left from somewhere important. Maize also has a contract in his hand. He puts the contract in front of the camera and the camera zooms in at the bottom halve just above the signatures. It states:Carrington Banks will debut July 30,2004 in a 4-man battle royal aginst Paul Brown,John Black,and Chris Collins for IC Title #1 contendership. The camera zooms back to the angle it was in showing Maize's full body sitting in the Limo....] Maize: If you are watching this tape,then you must be one of the a** wipes I have to face this Friday night at Aggression. Well let me tell you a little about myself. I am neither friend or foe we I enter that square circle. I am a competetor!....I am not merciful to the merciless like you...Khaos!! You see I am a man, who in 25 years of living, experienced more hell than most people do in their entire lives. I am a man who, even though face with turmoil and domestic dispute, never stray away from those who love me and those support, namely my family and fans! I am a man who, at this Friday night at Aggression, will walk out of the Williams Arena in Minneapolis, Minnesota with both my head and my hand held high!! I have a point to prove and quite frankly, I plan to prove in this 4-man battle royal!! I am surprised your still watching this tape. You may be smarter than I thought. Especially you Khaos, I thought you would've ejected this tape and burn it as some kind of demonic sacrafice(laughs to himself). I won't be too long. Please forgive me if I delayed your plans of intercepting Cupid's arrows and beating the living hell out of every guy who looks like that fan who beat you a** and made you an satan obsessed anarchist!! Talk about low self-esteem(knods his head in dissapointment). Then we have a fellow debuter by the name of Chris Collins. I don't know much about you kid so I'm not going to make any judgments, but I'll tell you this...."Bring your best and lose in an honorable defeat!!!". So you need to pause this tape and at least "TRY" some one handed push ups because it's a long way down getting thrown over the top ropes kid! Then there is the Marlynn Manson reject with a keen scense of humor who calls himself Freak. By now I thought you probley ran your narrow a** head first into your T.V. just for your own amusement and pain(laughs to himself again)!! But I guess you were occupied slithing you wrist with a plastic butter knife. Well listen Freak.....Um....I'll just call you Paul....You see Paul, me and you have something in common. We'll do anything for the entertainment of the fans and that makes us unpredicatable. But you lack in one area like the rest of these a** wipes.....You don't harness the adrenaline from the fans when they cheering you on in a match. At Soul Surviver you limited yourself.....you thought you were the underdog, that just because there were men two and three times your size, that you had an excuse to lose.....When really you had the biggest advantage and that was the fans!!!! Instead, you lost and now you have to settle for 2nd place...thats if you win the battle royal and beat the IC champ!! [Maize shakes his head and laughs. He pulls out a transparent glass cup from the cup holder in his Limo. He then grabs a pitcher of water from the mini fidge. He pours the water in the glass till it reached the middle. Maize then hold the glass of water up to the camera....] Maize: Now I have a question for you all.... Is this glass of water I'm holding half empty or half full??? [Maize waits awhile before speaking again] Maize: The answer is.....there is no answer! You see if you even came up with an answer in your mind, you are not prepared to face me. Because you see whether you thought it was half empty, or whether you thought it was half full...... [Maize drinks the glass of water] Maize: In the end.....it doesn't even matter(smiles)!! You see Chris,Khaos, and Paul(Freak)....No matter how many championships you may have won and no matter how many matches you came out the victor....This Friday night at Aggression we all start new...It's a clean slate......every match is a new beginning!! The only thing that can be factored in is the hype from our A-Maize-ing fans, or lack there of, and our passion and LOVE for our sport!! And thats exactly what I have!! From the moment my theme hits the arena, till the final bell rings and my hand is raised in victory, the love for the fans electrifies me as they cheer me on! When they chant my name there is no pain,no fatigue, no fear of losing. Just me whooping your two cent a**es all over Minnesota!!! So Friday at Aggression I will be the victor, not because I'm the best, not because I'm the fastest, and not because I was the most prepared..no,no,no.....You see I will win because I will endure and overcome! Not by choice.....because I must.....because I have to.....It is my "DESIRE"......IT'S FOR MY FAMILY.....IT'S FOR THE FANS!!! So my final question for you is.....ARE YOU READY?....."BECAUSE ONCE YOU STEP INTO THE RING WITH MAIZE......THERE IS NO WAY OUT!!!!!!!" {Tape End}
