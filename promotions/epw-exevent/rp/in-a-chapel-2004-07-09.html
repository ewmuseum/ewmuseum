In a chapel

9 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera fades in to Madison Killjoy who is standing in front of a white, dirty building. Madison Killjoy stands with his back towards the camera, staring at an old, dried out bush. Madison Killjoy pulls a branch off of the plant as he begins to speak. Madison Killjoy: Dead. This was once full of life. This plant was a vibrant green and full of leaves. Now look at it....dead! Madison Killjoy drops the branch on the ground as he begins to look up onto the roof of the building. The camera pans upward and focuses on a cross on the top of the building. Madison Killjoy: This building is abandoned. This church is no longer in service. I guess that this building would classify as a chapel. The camera pans back down towards Killjoy as he begins to walk to the entrance to the building. Madison Killjoy: This chapel has been all but forgotten. As Killjoy walks closer to the doors to the building, we can see that the doors have been boarded up. Madison walks up to the door and pounds his fist on the plywood. Madison Killjoy: I knew that this place was going to be boarded up. That is why I brought this. Madison walks a few steps to the left and grabs a crowbar that he had previously placed there. He then takes the two boards off of the doors with no problem. He throws the pieces of wood to the side and then opens the doors, walking into the building still carrying the crowbar. As Madison walks into the building a strong odor of mold emits as he looks around the trashed room. Seats have been turned over, glass broken and carpet torn. Madison Killjoy: This place is a mess. This chapel is a mess. Chapel is a mess. Madison Killjoy finds a seat that is on the ground and sets it upright. He then sits down in the chair and pokes at the ground with the crowbar as he continues to speak. Madison Killjoy: I never look past anyone. Even someone such as Tom Conway, but I have been wronged too many times. I know that it is my time to win and win I shall. I will defeat Tom Conway and in a few weeks, finally get my shot at the man that has corrupted our youth. He is a man that assumes that he is in the right although he has never seen true good. Madison pushes another chair over with the crowbar. A clanking sound is heard as the hits the floor. Madison Killjoy: This isn't any normal match against Conway this week, no it's a steel cage match. I know about being confined. I have been locked within the walls of a prison before. Some even say that life is a prison. It is true what they say. Life is a prison for some. Those are the ones that I like to refer to as the lost. For the ones that see the light, they are the ones that do not live in confinement. They are the ones that I prefer to the think of as the enlightened. I am one of the enlightened. Chapel is not, nor is Tom Conway. Hell, I would even go as far as to say that no one else on the roster is truly enlightened. Madison Killjoy stands up and gets out of the chair. He then begins to look around the building once again. Madison Killjoy: You see, I came here to get a first hand look at what people like Chapel have gone through. This place used to be pure. It used to be a place of study and worship. Somewhere down the road, it lost its way. This place is now nothing more than another abandoned building. Just like Chapel he lives but his soul has abandoned his body. His conscious has escaped him. He is lost and is now leading a life that has been perceived as righteous by others that have lost their way. I will not fall for it. I will not fall for his false values and his choice of ignorance. I will not subject myself to the lifestyle that he lives. Why is that? For I have a conscious......and a soul. Madison Killjoy lets out a laugh under his breath. Madison Killjoy: It is funny. At Soul Survivor, only one soul shall survive the battle. Just as the good book says, eventually, good comes out on top. I have been wronged far too many times in recent weeks. This time, I shall not waste the opportunity to rid the world of a man such as Chapel. Chapel, it’s for your own good. You just don’t realize it yet. The camera fades to black as Madison Killjoy walks away from the camera and towards the door.
