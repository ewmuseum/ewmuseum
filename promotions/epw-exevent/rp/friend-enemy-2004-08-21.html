Friend/Enemy

21 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*We enter a familiar looking room, it is a room we have seen countless times before and will see countless times again, it is an office belonging to a man who’s life seems to have taken a slight turn for the worst… the world is out to lynch him, a man from his past wont leave him alone and now… his best friend has turned his back on him. This office belongs to Chapel… a man who’s world is crumbling down around him. We walk over to the desk that is shrouded in darkness and take a seat in front of it… we wait, we know we wont have to wait long… our host is a very punctual man.* Ting…thud… *We turn our head towards the noise coming from the far corner of the room… we listen for it again…* Ting…thud… *The noise is a familiar one to us, it is a noise we have heard limitless times, but it is a noise that still seems alien to us… the voice of our host comes out of the shadows* Chapel: Head’s they win… Ting…thud…Tails we loose… ain’t that always the way? *he walks over to us… the strange noise follows him ting…thud…as he draws near we realise why the noise is so familiar… a coin shines in the limited light as it is tossed in the air ting…thud…* Chapel: But you knew that already didn’t you Ace… the odds were always stacked against us but we still found a way to piss everyone off and have a good time *chuckles* *The man of chance passes our chair and takes a seat behind the desk… we sit up and listen intently* Chapel: But, that’s behind us now… gone are the day’s of mirth and merriment, now it’s time for misery and misfortune… this week I fight my team-mate, this week I fight my friend, this week… a legacy end’s *Chapel sits in contemplative silence… the noise from the coin is the only sound in the entire room… Our host takes a deep breath and sigh’s* Chapel: …Let’s look at the consequences of this match… *he flips the coin, ting…and catches it, thud…he doesn’t open his hand* Head’s… I win the match, the championship belt is still around my waist BUT to keep it I have to demolish one of the few people I care about in this world and a friendship is over. *he flips the coin, ting…and catches it, thud…once again he doesn’t open his hand* Tail’s… I loose the match, Ace has the satisfaction of victory and regains his confidence to become a dominant force once again BUT for that to happen I have to loose the belt, I loose my title, I loose my dignity and again a friendship is over. *Chapel stands up from his seat and turns his back to us* Chapel: There will be no winners in this match, only losers… think you can handle that Ace? As hard as it may be, I know I can… this friendship was over the second you picked up that bottle and drank your life away… you are the blame for all of this, not me… this Sunday a Legacy end’s and a new era dawn’s, but who will the light shine upon? They won this time Ace, we lose… How do you like your chances now? Ting… thud… FADE OUT
