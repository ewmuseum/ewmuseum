The scene opens up on a grave lightly lit by a flickering candle placed on top of it. Lightning strikes and the camera catches a glimpse of the inscription…. ”Hudman” Thunder rolls and lightning strikes once more….. “January 24, 2003” “ANARCHY” Thunder booms across the cemetery and once more lightning strikes this time enough to show that the grave is empty and dirt piled to the side….. A graphic for Breaking Point hits the screen and a voiceover ensues… Voiceover: Everyone has their breaking point… now the superstars of EPW have theirs in your front room. Will Chapel defeat Ace? What will happen in the triple threat match for the Intercontin….. The screen minimizes to a small white dot and the camera pans back to show Hudman in his dressing room after Aggression watching the television… Hudman: Too many questions, not enough answers…. Hudman stands up and walks to the fridge and opens an ice cold coke….. Hudman: Ahhhh….. Answers….we all want em…but only a few of us got em. And have I got a few for you guys. Khaos. The mere thought of stepping into a ring with you fills me with such disgust that I may be physically sick but hey If I have to lower myself to that level to win a belt again. Then so be it. As I said on Aggression. You have never been a contender. You aren’t one now and you certainly wont be one on Sunday. Singleton on the other hand. There’s a lot of mystery around you for me. Why does a man who has had so many shots at the title have so much trouble attaining it. Answer: You are just not good enough. I on the other hand fought from the very bottom of this federation beating greats like The Prophet Colman Dowell and The Destroyer to take my place among the higher echelons. And it is there I wish to return. Could I ask for a better platform than at Breaking Point? So the date is set, events are in motion. There are plenty of questions but only one answer….Hudman.. Scene fades to black
