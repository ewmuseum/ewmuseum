(Un)Lucky Omen's

12 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Chapel leans against the wall in the back rooms as the crew set up all the technical stuff for Friday’s show, a buffet table is set up next to him for the hungry teamsters, Chapel takes a passing glance over it…* Chapel: …Ooh, Muffin’s *Chapel reaches across for a chocolate muffin and accidentally knocks a salt shaker over, sending the white crystals everywhere… Chapel takes a bite into the muffin and surveys the mess he accidentally caused* Chapel: *while eating* …meh, someone will clean it up Voice: Hey Chapel!! *Chapel turns his head as his name is called out, he gives a heavy sigh as he sees EPW interviewer Dennis Dorman running over to him, Chapel puts his muffin down and gets ready to meet Dennis… who, undoubtedly, has some very interesting questions for him* Chapel: Hey Dennis, what can I do for you? Dennis: *composes himself before speaking* I was… I was just hoping to get a few words about your up-coming tag match this Friday Chapel: *picks up his Kit-bag and nod’s in a not-unfriendly way* Sure… lets walk and talk *Chapel takes a few steps forward and almost trips over a black cat that runs out in-front of him* Chapel: What the? *he turns to the teamsters* Hey, Who let a cat into the set? Teamster: *watches the black cat dash into the shadows* Dunno’ chief , maybe it’s a stray wandered in off the streets… Chapel: *turns his attention back to Dennis and continues walking* Well you might as well fire away… Dennis: *tries to keep up with Chapel and conduct his interview* Okay (ahem) Chapel this Friday sees you and your friend Ace in the first Tag-team match you have had in ages… what are your thoughts on that? Chapel: *turns his head and gives Dennis a sarcastic look* …Ya’ reeeaaallly put a lot of thought into these questions didn’t you… (sigh) Alright, the way I see it is, I’ve beaten pretty much every guy to enter this organization, everyone from Madison Killjoy to Khaos… I’m at the top of my game, so where is the next step in my career? Tag wrestling obviously, I know at the moment we don’t have a Tag division, but with these new faces coming in almost daily it’s bound to happen… I just want to be on top of that and add another "W" to my tally Dennis: You just mentioned Khaos… you and Ace are going to be facing him and Shawn Singleton this week… do you have any strategy? Chapel: Sure do, I plan to rush in, crack skulls and get Ace to pin Khaos to win back the IC title… Ace has been lacking confidence as of late and I’m hoping that a win will be a big shot in the arm for him. Dennis: And what about your opponents? Chapel: HA! The newbie and the washed up wannabe? Oh let’s see what I can say about them… *Chapel lowers his eyes to the ground as he thinks for a second, Dennis Glances up and notices an obstruction in the hallway… it is a set up ladder leaning against the wall, Dennis tries to call Chapel’s attention to it* Dennis: Er… Chapel? *points to the ladder* Chapel: *continues to think of a suitable answer* … Dennis: Chapel… the ladder? Chapel: Quiet Dennis I’m thinking here… *Dennis and Chapel reach the ladder, Dennis moves sideways and walks around the ladder… Chapel walks under, oblivious to the fact that a ladder was there* Chapel: *Snaps his fingers and looks up* Now I got it… Dennis: *glances back at the ladder and then turns to Chapel* Got what? Chapel: An answer… God Dennis, try paying attention to the world around you for once… remember you asked me about my opponents? Dennis: Oh yeah… and what can you say about them? Chapel: Well, lets start with the old news first… Big Bitch John… "Been There, Done That" hell I’ve beaten this guy more times then he’s had hot dinners, just because he gets a face lift, a name change, an obviously fake tattoo and a dye job he thinks that he’s gonna run rough-shot over the entire roster… well John-John, it aint gonna happen, enjoy that IC belt while you can because my boy Ace is gonna take it from you this Friday. Dennis: …and of Shawn Singleton? Chapel: You mean Shawn Single-For-Life? Well I give credit where credit is due… thank God Shawn isn’t due any… he’s a whipping boy, a servant, a lackey if you will to that gimp in the wheel chair, Shawn is no real threat to me… and I’ll prove it one way or another. *Chapel and Dennis reach the locker room door, Chapel turns to Dennis* Chapel: Any more questions before I go in here and shower this terrible conversation from my body? Dennis: Er… just one, Hudman has been seen interfering in a lot of your matches lately, some are even begin to suggest that you can no longer win without his assistance, any comment? Chapel: Yes I have a comment, Hudman has nothing to do with me… for all I know he’s trying to win favours off of me so he can back-stab me later on, well that ain’t flying with me… I said it before and I’ll say it once more for the hard of hearing… Hudman, keep your weird mind-games away from me, I don’t want anything to do with you… let’s keep that chair-shot I gave to you in the old XEW day’s as our last pleasant memory of each other, in short… STAY THE HELL AWAY FROM ME!! Dennis: *slightly perturbed* erm… thanks for that Chapel, I think I have everything I need Chapel: *regains his composure* Good… now if you will excuse me I need a shower, this interview has made me feel all dirty *Chapel enters the locker room and nod’s hello to the guy’s that are there… he set’s his kit bag down on a bench, he hears a slight ‘tinkling’ noise from within it, he opens the bag and rummages around… eventually he pull’s out a mirror… it’s got a crack right down the middle* Wrestler #1: *looks over to Chapel* Uh-oh man, you just gone and broken a mirror… Wrestler #2: Oops, that’s seven years bad-luck right there man… and it’s Friday 13th this week, wow what an omen… wouldn’t want to be in your shoe’s when the bad mojo starts happening *The two wrestlers start laughing, Chapel turns and gives them a weak smile* Chapel: Ha comma Ha comma Ha fella’s, I don’t do omens… *he looks back to the broken mirror, his face turns serious for a second as if he’s filled with doubt… he shakes his head and grins again* …Nah, nothing but the good stuff in my future *he drops the mirror into the bin and continues to empty his bag* FADE OUT
