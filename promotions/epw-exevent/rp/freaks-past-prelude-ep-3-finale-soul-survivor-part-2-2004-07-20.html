Freaks Past... Prelude EP 3 - Finalé [Soul Survivor] Part 2

20 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“What do you want to do?” Freak asks “Just be here with you.” Dominique says… Elsewhere… A Semi stops on the side of the highway… The passenger door opens, and the Trucker sees the bloody mess that is "The Dude". “What the hell happened to you?” The Trucker asks. "The Dude" smiles as he raises his Desert Eagle. A shot rings out in the desert. "The Dude" steps into the passenger side of the Semi, and opens the driver’s door. The dead trucker falls out of the door as it slams shut. The Semi begins moving through gears, heading for town. Elsewhere… “Paul…” Freak wakes up, and sees Dominique looking into his eyes. “My Love.” Freak says, smiling. “Can we get out of here? I don’t feel safe.” Dominique says “Alright. Get your things together.” Freak says without missing a beat. Freak hops out of bed, as does Dominique. Dominique walks to her bag on the floor and bends down to go through it. Freak looks on at Dominique doing this with a kid at a candy store look on his face. Dominique spins around, smirking at Freak. “What’cha looking at?” Dominique asks sweetly “Oh nothing.” Freak says, turning red. “Sure…” Seth, Freak, and Dominique all emerge from the hotel, with bags in tow. They load up into the Challenger. Freak fires up the Challenger, and looks across the way at Dominique. “We’re going to go some place safe… Some place where I can see the guys coming.” Freak says. “Alaska?” Dom asks “You guessed it.” Freak says “Alaska!?” Seth sounds pissed “You’ll dig it, I’ll teach you how to snow machine.” Freak says “What’s a snow machine?” Seth asks… “You’ll see.” Freak replies. --------------------- You see... and that was it... to this day... I have lost contact with Dom, the love shattered into a million pieces... the dude I later found was a little punk called Nitro... he sadly was my brother... the one my family decided to keep... throw me away... and keep him... that son of a bitch... thanks to him, and my so called Father... I lost what was important to me... the only women to ever truely love me... and now I'm lost....
