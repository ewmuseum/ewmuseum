Lost Soul

24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The wind is howling and the rain pours down, it is a hellish night, it is a night where everyone should be at home, warm and safe. On nights like this only a few places are left open, bar’s stay open for the drunks that can no longer feel the rain on their inebriated faces, Shelters stay open for the homeless who have no-where else to stay and finally, Churches stay open for those that want peace, solitude and sanctuary… we focus in on one such church, the light from inside make the images on the stained glass windows seem alive, we enter the place of worship to find it virtually empty, there are candles illuminating the floor in front of us, there is a smell in the air that we can’t seem to place our finger on, we look up and see a statue of Christ on the crucifix… we feel slightly humbled at the sight of him, our eye’s drop to the alter in front of us and we see a man reading aloud from the bible… Man: "…Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me; your rod and your staff, they comfort me…" We walk nearer to the alter to listen in on the man but he has seem to grown silent and looks to be reading ahead… the man makes a noise, it seems to be a mix of a laugh and a curse. Man: What a cock of shit… He picks up the bible and throws it against the back wall, he turns his attention to us, his head is heavily bandaged, his face is bruised and mouth is bloody… this man has been through the wars, this man is Chapel, his happy-go-lucky demeanour is gone… all that is left is pure rage. Chapel: Forgive my appearance gentle viewers, but I had the shit kicked out of me last week, but I’ll go into that in greater detail later on… He walks from be hind the alter and comes down the aisle, he turns his back to us and looks up at the image of Christ… he speaks but it seems to be more to himself then to the world Chapel: …why did you let this happen? How could all that has happened be part of the greater scheme? Why do this to me and my friends? He lowers his head and strokes back his hair then faces us once again Chapel: I and my friends have been through a lot this past couple of weeks… we lost a friend to drugs, another one of us has reached rock bottom and seems to be taking up residence there, and I get jumped from behind by a man who is too cowardly to challenge me face to face… now would a caring God let this happen to his people? No, I think not, which leads me to believe that God has left us, he doesn’t care about us any more… YOU HEAR ME GOD? IF YOU’RE HERE, STRIKE ME DOWN AND SHOW ME THAT YOU CARE!! He stands there for a few seconds, seeing if anything will happen… nothing does. Chapel: Heh, thought so… He turns back to us… Chapel: If god doesn’t exist then that means there is no hope for salvation, no matter what I do it’s going to all end up the same… He bends over and picks up a lit candle from off the floor, he looks into the flame and continues to talk Chapel: *never raises his eyes from the flame* A familiar face has returned to us, he is a man who I used to beat on a regular basis, he was a man who I defeated to become the holder of two championships, I found it quite a laugh when he left the federation at my hands… it was quite a power rush, but now he is back, *looks up at us* Welcome Back Khaos, I can’t wait to tap-dance all over your face again. But next time we meet, when I put you down… you are staying down. He plays with the flame with his fingers, he winces in pain slightly then continues to talk Chapel: Now this is to the man that was to cowardly to challenge me face on, he is the one who from behind jumped me and proceeded to beat the living crap out of me… Shawn Singleton, you made an enemy that day, you want to beat me up for ignoring your pal "Wheel’s" then go ahead… but at least be a man about it and do it in the ring, be a man because I don’t have time to mess around with a child like you. He walks over to a pillar and leans against it, the flame from the candle flickers slightly Chapel: Madison Killjoy… you have had a problem with me from day one haven’t you, Now why is that? Do you have a problem with my superior ability? is it the fact that no matter what you do I always come out on top? or is it because of the fact that no matter how hard you try you will never be accepted like the rest of us have? Face it Maddy, you have this inferiority complex 10 miles wide, I mean why else would you push yourself so hard to beat me, I know it’s not for the belt… In fact, Maddy, I believe that deep down, you want to be me… you want to be "The Man of Chance", it’s okay, I understand completely, you’re a loser and you want to become a winner… well, this Sunday is your chance, beat me and take my belt… but be warned Maddy, after this Sunday you will never get another chance again. You think me evil, don‘t you Madison… I just have to say, you can’t even comprehend the word yet… He throws the candle down on the ground and the floor begins to burn, we realise that the smell that was bothering us earlier is gasoline… the church starts to become ablaze. Chapel: *walks through the flames* THERE IS NO GOD, NO MESSIAH, NO HEAVENLY BEING THAT CAN SAVE OUR SOUL’S!! He exit’s the church as the flames break through the windows Chapel: …in the end, we only have ourselves to rely on. Khaos, Shawn, Madison… your time draws near, How do you like your chances? He leaves the scene as the sound of fire engines draw nearer… FADE OUT
