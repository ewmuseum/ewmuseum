My recognition....

22 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*A breaking point, there comes a time when a man reaches their breaking point when they have reached that point they go into a change, a change like no other they become a different person and their mind suddenly becomes clear that their only goal is to get the job done by any means possible even if they take a persons life, this is what's called a breaking point thus the name of the pay per view says it all. This Sunday I have reached my breaking point with Shawn and Hudman, after this Sunday they will never be the same again....EVER!* {The scene fades in on The Sovereigns of Malice members Cain and Nightshade as they are already here at the Winnipeg Arena in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada just one day early from Breaking Point. Cain sits in a remote corner in the locker room and Nightshade leans against the empty lockers deep in thought.} Cain: Well now you can tell that Lord Khaos is ready don't you agree Nightshade? Nightshade: Oh yes most definitely, he's ready to pound some heads in I can tell you this much, Hudman and Shawn have no clue of what Khaos is really capable of and this Sunday they and the whole EPW will know the wrath of The Khaotic One is real and not just some childish boast. Cain: Agreed, this Sunday will be day that Khaos gets the recognition that he deserves! The recognition that he is the most dominate force since the John Adams World Title days. Nightshade: This Sunday will be Lord Khaos's rise to power... Cain: Yes... *Meanwhile Khaos is sitting in the middle of the ring with a look of anticipation on his face, most of the lights in the stands are dimmed and some are off only the dimmed lights are focused on the ring as Khaos awaits his match with udder delight.* KHAOS: The day has come....Sunday August 22, 2004 will be the day that I Khaos will rise to power and no longer be shunned by the entire EPW roster as a fool who makes childish boasts. Hudman what gives you the right to tell me that I am not a contender and saying that you will have stoop to my level to get my title well got news for you Hudman this Sunday I am going to the next level and you will be left behind and who do you think you are anyways telling me such words huh your in no position whatsoever to tell me this bullshit! Do you really think you're in any shape to compete let alone throw a punch? No I think not your still living in the past Hudman you might as well look yourself in the mirror and say "he is right I am past my prime and I am living a stale dream I might as well just give up this foolishness and retire for good". But did you? No you simply got bored and decided to make an appearnace for the past month and four times save that piece of crap Chapel from losing his title. Face facts Hudman your yesterday's news and your comebacks are as stale as ever do yourself a favor and stay away from the ring better yet stay away from this sport period becuase as far as I am concerned you no longer belong here anymore. *Khaos then sits up and looks up with a focused look on his face then he looks down and at the camera and begins to laugh hysterically.* KHAOS: Shawn that leaves me with you...since when is it right to look ahead rather than what's in front of you huh? Do you think that I am no challenge to you at all? That's were you are dead wrong Shawn you should ALWAYS look at what's in front of you when your in this sport didn't The Hunter teach you any better? Guess not, this Sunday Shawn your going to see a side of me that you have never seen a side in which I am at my breaking point and I say that is not very good news for you my friend for you see when it is all said and done...I will be the victor and then you will see why they call me Khaos becuase in the end Shawn there are winners and there are losers and I am afraid that you are on the losing end. This Sunday Hudman and Shawn the gateway to anarchy will open and I will send you both though it and once you enter there's NO.....WAY.....OUT! It all begins now... Sunday everyone will in EPW know thy name... KHAOS!
