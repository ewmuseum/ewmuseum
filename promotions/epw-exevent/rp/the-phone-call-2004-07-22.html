The Phone Call

22 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( - The scene opens on a small ranch in Amarillo Texas. The Sun is setting over in the west and as it sinks below the trees we begin to zoom in on the ranches front porch. On the porch is a man with a white cowboy hat, flannel cut sleeved shirt, a pair of blue denim jeans with a large Texas flag belt buckle and a pair of black cowboy boots. The man is sitting in his rocking chair with his hats brim down and it appears he is taking a nap. Just then the silence is broken by the sound of a putting engine of a 1985 Ford F150. The rusty old automobile makes a stop in front of the porch and out steps a obese man in overalls and a John Deere tractor hat. he makes his way up the porch steps and begins to shake the sleeping man. - ) Obese Man: Wake up Chris! Wake up! ( - The man gives Chris a shake and Chris lifts his head to look at him. - ) Chris: God danmit Bubba! I'm trying to catch some z's out here on the porch and your fat ass has to come wake me up. This better be good, cause last time you woke me up to show me what tricks your left breast can do. Bubba: Oh its better all right! Look what we got in the mail today! ( - Bubba reaches into his back pocket of his overalls and removes a white envelope. He wedges his finger under the small opening and rips it open]. He pulls out a letter and begins to read it to Chris. - ) Bubba: It reads, Du, Du, Du...Dear Mr. Chris Clarance. That’s you. And Mr. Big Bubba Redneck, ha ha shucks that’s me! Chris Clarance: God Danmit Bubba you shit kicking retard I know who I am and I know who you are, give me that! ( - Chris Clarance rips the letter out of Big Bubba Rednecks hands and begins to read it out loud. - ) Chris Clarance: Our head of talent and recruiting here at the EPW (Evolution Pro Wrestling) has expressed great interest in signing you and your Manager to wrestling contracts. If you are interested please reach me at anytime at the number below. Sincerely, Gary L. EPW C.E.O. Big Bubba Redneck: Well Ill be a monkeys uncle! That dare is some greaaaat news! I knew it was something big Chris! Chris Clarance: This is some pretty great news Bubba, go fetch my phone for me. Lets give this Mr. L. a call. ( - Chris places the letter down on a small table next to him as Bubba walks inside the screen door to retrieve the phone. While Bubba is in the house Chris reaches into his pocket and pulls out a bag of Levi Garrett chewing tobacco. He reaches in and grabs a fat wad. He packs it up and slams it in his mouth. Just then Bubba returns with the cordless telephone.) Big Bubba Redneck: Here ya go Chris. Chris Clarance: Thanks Bubba. ( - Chris then looks down on the paper and dials the number listed into his phone. The phone rings 3 times before someone on the other end answers - ) Chris Clarance: Yeah hi is this Mr. L.? Hey Gary its Chris Clarance, how are things? No I'm doing fine myself, just hanging out working on my farm. Yes I did receive your letter. Uh huh. Right. No I'm defiantly interested. This Friday on Aggression? Sure not a problem. When would you need me to leave. Uh huh. No problem We will be there tonight. Ok thank you very much Mr. L. you wont regret this. You too. Bye. ( - Chris hangs up the phone. - ) Big Bubba Redneck: What'd he say Chris?! Chris Clarance: He’s offered us a contract, we leave for Ft. Lauderdale tonight! He said Ill be fighting this Friday on Aggression against some guy named Jackson Kable for my debut. Big Bubba Redneck: Jackson Kable? Is that there guy the one who they say gonna show up to my house between 9am-9pm and he dont show? Chris Clarance: No Bubba, thats the cable company. Not too sure about anything with this Kable guy. But we’re going to throw down on Friday night for sure. Hope that sum bitch likes the site of his own blood cause unfortunately he’s going to have to be an example to all the other numb nuts on the roster of what carnage I can create. Go pack your bag Bubba, we got a plane to catch! Big Bubba Redneck: Ok Chris Ill be ready in 10. ( - Bubba waddles into the house again, while Chris stands up out of his rocking chair and spits a big wad of saliva onto the floor. Chris walks into the house and turns into his bedroom. His walls have frames with all his previous championships from other federations stored inside. Chris opens up his closet and grabs his suit case. He unzips it and begins digging threw he dresser for some clothes to bring with him. He lines up some blue jeans over alls and a few t shirts. Chris then reaches under his bed and grabs a brown cardboard box. He sits it on the bed and opens the top. Inside sits his wrestling attire. Chris looks down at his black chaps and shorts and a smile appears on his face. - ) Chris Clarance: It’s been too long, I’m glad to see you guys again. ( Chris grabs his gear and throws it in the suitcase with the rest of his clothes. He zips the suitcase and grabs it as he exits the room. - ) Chris Clarance: Bubba you ready!? ( - Big Bubba Redneck exits his room with his duffle bag of clothes and walks towards Chris and the door. Chris hits the lights and grabs his keys from his pocket as they step outside the door. Chris closes the door and locks up the ranch. He then heads down over to his white GMC Seirra pickup truck and throws the bags in the bed of the truck. Chris unlocks the doors with his key and hops in. He places the keys in the ignition and starts up the diesel fuled automobile. Bubba straps on his seatbelt in the passenger side seat as Chris throws the car into reverse. He backs out of the driveway and shifts to drive and peels off down the dusty dirt road off to the airport. - ) width="150" height="50" type="audio/x-pn-realaudio-plugin" loop="
