The Hour of Deliverance

24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Hour of Deliverance! {The scene fades to Riverside where a once powerful man used to live. A once devious, ruthless, and powerful man. A man that during his time on this earth he was the epiphany of business………..and evil. This man I speak of is Frank Adams the once father of John Adams and John Black.} {His death was sudden but not mourned. His memory was great but not honored. His passing was something of rejoice and praise. Why not? He was the one man everybody in Riverside respected out of fear. His death will always be remembered as the day tyranny died in Riverside County.} {The scene moves in closer to the old location in the middle of the night. The scene looks devastated after the fire that was done by John Adams after his brother inherited it. A closer look reveals that John Adams is there rummaging through the debris. The man is heavy with thought and grief for the tyranny lives again.} John Adams: This is where it all began for me. The lies, deceit, fear, and the tyranny that I endured on a day to day basis. Even though my Father is dead, his legacy lives on! Psycho Dan has dug my father out of his resting place and has invoked the spirit of the man that I despise. The spirit of my father guides him. The spirit of my father is fueling him with the tools he needs to beat me, John “The Prophet” Adams! Or so he thinks. {John Adams continues his walk through the graveyard of a house that once was.} John Adams: You see Dan I have taken the liberty of booking our match before the 4-way competition. You see after I am done with you there will be no way for you to compete in the 4-way. The Freak on the other hand, if he proves to me that he can make you feel the pain and anguish of my wraith then he will take your place in the 4-way match. Unfair you may say! But my dear Dan life is full of things that are unfair. Life, friends, loved ones, and the things you work very hard for is the most unfair. Your career here is pathetic and useless, and this Sunday on PPV I will prove to everybody how pathetic it is! {As John Adams walks to one end of the burnt burial of the home, a gentle wind starts to blow in revealing a picture of John Adams Brother.} John Adams: Now to my dear old Brother John Black, a.k.a. Khaos! Welcome back brother, I see you got my letter in the mail with an EPW contract! Surprised? Don’t be, you amongst others are part of my ultimate prophecy. One that has been written in the stars for a millennium! So sit back dear brother for your redemption is near. And I know your justice will be swift and severe! {The memories of an EPW of long and forgotten start to flood his mind. The AOV, Hudman, Richard Rush, and all of the long and forgotten people that was in EPW. He heads back to his bike and heads to a hotel to catch some sleep before his plane ride. The hotel is nothing fancy just some run down Motel 6 near Ontario Airport. As he checks into his room, sleep rushes over him almost instantly as he hits the hard cold bed.} {John Adams Dream} {The dream opens up to where the last one left off. Adams is now by his father’s grave site. The sun is shining and the birds are chirping. Adams feels all alone in mind, body and soul. With a sudden explosion his father’s casket comes bursting out of his grave. John Adams pulls away from the uncertainty as he sees the casket still smoking from the explosion. The casket opens with a boney hand as a boney skeleton comes out and stares at John Adams.} Skeleton: John Adams the time has come to take your redemption. To take my redemption! {The skeleton starts to take form and starts to grow flesh as John Adams is seeing his father being formed. Frank Adams laughs as we see closer a evil grin on the face of John Adams.} {End of the Dream.} {John Adams wakes up in a cold sweat but also with a revelation. The time has come and at Soul Survivor John Adams will have his reckoning as he takes Psycho Dan up to that scaffold and send his condemned soul to the depths of hell.} {The scene fades to black.}
