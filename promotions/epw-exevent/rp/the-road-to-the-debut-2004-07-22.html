The Road To The Debut

22 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( - A cloud of dirt and dust trails off the back of a white GMC Sierra pickup truck as it races down the back roads of Amarillo Texas. Its destination, the Amarillo International Airport. In the passenger side seat sits Big Bubba Redneck, and behind the wheel is none other than the “Cowboy” himself Chris Clarance. They appear to be singing some sort of song together as we listen in. - ) Chris Clarance: Plowing these fields in the hot summer sun . Over by the gate, lordy here she comes. With a basket full of chicken and a big cold jug of sweet tea. I make a little room and she climbs on up. Open up the throttle and stir a little dust. Just look at her face she ain't fooling me!!! Big Bubba Redneck: She thinks my tractor's sexy, It really turns her on! She's always staring at me, while I'm chugging along!! She likes the way it's pulling, while we're tilling up the land, she's even kinda crazy about my farmers tan! She's the only one who really understands what gets me.... Chris And Bubba: SHE THINKS MY TRACTORS SEXY!!! ( - Kenny Chesney’s “She Thinks My Tractors Sexy” continues to play in the background as Bubba does his little shimmy dance in the passenger seat and Chris taps on the steering wheel to the beat. -) Big Bubba Redneck: This song just always puts me in a good mood Chris, it just makes me wana dance and let out a good ole YEEEHAW!! Chris Clarance: Well we got reason for celebration and joy my good friend, we are going to sunny south Florida to debut in the EPW!! And there anit a danm thing that can bring me down at this moment. After we get in and go talk to Mr. L., we are hitting the town boy and we are gonna have us a good ole big city shin dig. Big Bubba Redneck: Its been a while since we’ve had a good shin dig Chris. But hot dang we are back in business baby and Big Bubba Redneck is gonna tear up the town and drive them there city slickers wild! Chris Clarance: Bubba you better believe it. I plan on signing the dotted line and then busting open a bottle of jack and getting fucked up. Then, come Friday night we can celebrate again after I kick Jackson Kables ass up and down the sunshine state. I actually almost feel bad for this poor bastard, I have to debut against him and make him my first victim. All his lifes goals and achievements will be crushed right there in that ring in front of the world. Sorta melon colly when you think about it. Big Bubba Redneck: You feel bad for him!? He should’ve known what he was getting himself into when he accepted that match. Chris Clarance: I said almost! You and I both know once I lace up those cowboy boots and throw on my hat and walk down that isle that I’m gonna enjoy every single second of pounding that poor shmucks face in and humiliating him on national television. It’s my time to shine Bubba, I didn’t come out of retirement to become some federations curtain jerker. I’m the anti hero of the century and I’m here to establish dominance and further my career as a champion. I’ve held world titles in every single federation I’ve ever competed and I didnt need the help of anyone, I did it all on my own. I didnt have any connections inside the business, I didnt have the most popular fan following, I just had myself and I did it all on my own. I never tried to be anyones hero and I really don’t give two shits about these fans Im just out there to break someones skull and not get put in jail. Come on, your telling me I can fuck someone up and not go to jail, and get paid!? This is the best job on the planet hoss! Big Bubba Redneck: I know where your coming from Chris, and thats why I’ve stuck around for this wild ride. Chris Clarance: Bubba who you kidding you just stuck around cause you don’t have a house or a life and you like the free food. Big Bubba Redneck: Well.... yeah you got me there, the food is great. Oh yeah Chris don’t miss the next exit. Chris Clarance: Already on it. ( - The pick up winds down the interstate exit ramp and pulls up to Amarillo International Airport and Chris slows down the truck and gets in the long term parking lane. The truck pulls up to the ticket machine and Chris reaches out and presses the button so he can recive his parking ticket but the ticket does not dispense. Chris pushes again.... and again..... and again. Until finally he gets fed up and punches the machine. Still no ticket and the bar wont go up and give him access to the parking garage. Chris then puts the truck in reverse and revs the engine and then slams on the gas and blasts the bar right off and he plows right into the parking lot. - ) Chris Clarance: God danmit I hate it when shit like that happens! Big Bubba Redneck: Ohhhh no Chris hope we don’t get in any trouble for that. And Im sure that didnt help out your paint job. Chirs Clarance: I don’t care I just wana get the fuck out of here and make our flight. ( - Chris pulls into a spot and puts the pick up into park. The two both hop out of the front seat and head to the bed of the truck to grab their bags. Chris presses his key arlarm and they walk off towards the terminal. As they walk through the airport terminal Big Bubba begins to sweat profusely and they are forced to stop for him to take a rest. - ) Chris Clarance: Bubba you ok? Big Bubba Redneck: ( - Panting - ) Ye... Yeah... Im good... Whew... This is a work out! Chris Clarance: God danm Bubba, you’ve walked 50 feet and you look like you’ve ran a marathon... you need to lose some wait hoss. Come on we’re gonna miss our flight, get your fat ass up. Big Bubba Redneck: Com... Com... Coming Chris. ( - Finally the two reach terminal D-4 and check in just before boarding begins. - ) Flight attendent: We are now baording flight 2243 with non stop service to Fort Lauderdale/Hollywood international airport now boarding our first class customers and young children traveling alone and those who may need assistence. Chris Clarance: Thats us Bubba lets roll. ( - Chris and Bubba grab their carry on bags and walk down the ramp way. The door closes and the secene fades. - ) width="150" height="50" type="audio/x-pn-realaudio-plugin" loop="
