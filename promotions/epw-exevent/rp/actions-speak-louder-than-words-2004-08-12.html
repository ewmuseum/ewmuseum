Actions Speak Louder Than Words

12 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name:Maize Location:Iowa City,Iowa Date:August 12, 2004 1:30PM Actions Speak Louder Than Words PRELUDE Hmph.....You thought he wouldn't ever speak again after he witness what happened. I guess the phase I can show him better than I can tell him doesn't apply to Kyle Pearl. Kyle Pearl will have to EXPERIENCE what I had just experienced first hand, by my hands. He will have to bleed a river of blood before he can sail into reality. He still doesn't realize what he is getting into. He is so obsess with lingering in his past that he doesn't realized how much pain he is about to experience Friday 13,2004 at Aggression. Now ain't that a bitch ? Just when he thought his luck couldn't get worse, he faces "The Amazing One" on Friday the 13th. The day of bad luck!! The day that Satan himself feel a atmosphere of fear. The day Kyle Pearl loses to Maize in a First Blood Street Match! THE SCENE The scene shows Maize doing one handed push ups in his hotel room, located in Iowa City,Iowa. He is wearing a black jogging out fit with gold lining. He has on some Reebok running shoes that are all white. He has a pissed off look on his face as he watches The TV and do his one handers at the same time. The camera gets a quick glimpse of the TV. The images click off as soon as the camera focus in on the TV. Before it went off you could see a quick image of Kyle Pearl. At this point Maize start hitting his push ups harder and harder without stopping..... I don't know what pissed me off more: The fact that he contradicted my past, or the fact that he still revisits his past even if it means harm to his love one. I realized I wasn't facing a regular man. In fact, I realized I was facing a cold heart monster! Maize eyes have a fire in them that we have not seen before. His eyes do not move from that TV screen. His one handed push ups are in constant rhythm. Sweat soon glides down Maize's face and as soon as some sweat hits the scar on Maize's left eye he flinches in minor pain. Never the less, his eyes are still staring forward at the TV and he is still in the same rhythm. Suddenly with one quick motion he pushes himself back on his feet with one hand. He grabs his cell and his sun glasses off the bed. His face is still pissed off looking when he slowly apply the sun glassed to his face. He puts his cell phone in his jacket pocket and walks out of the hotel door. As soon as he walks out three kids around 12 and 13 come from out of no where. The have Maize vs Pearl posters in their hand. It seems like they were waiting on me. They depend on me to give them happiness for this one day.....That is a reason why I don't walk around with body guards. They hinder me from happiness. When I saw the smile on those kids faces, it made me feel a little bit better. For that brief moment, I forgot about the situation on 18th and 17th street. I forgot about the ignorant decisions of Kyle Pearl. BUT I couldn't forget about our match....The match that i created......The match.....That I........Created! Kid#1: Look It is Maize. Wow the amazing one is in the same hotel as me. Kid#2: I told you Jarvis. I told you thats was him. Kid#3: No you didn't you said that was Nelly! No one listens to Nelly no more!!! I said it was Maize!!(Looks at Maize)....Can we get your autograph? Please? Without saying a word, Maize pulls out his special pen and signs the kids posters. They walk away excited you can here their comments... Kid#1: Yeah! I got the biggest one! Kid#2: Nuh-un, mine looks bigger than yours.(sighs) I wish I could of got Kyles autograph It would of been perfect.... Kid#3:I don't know about that! He might think we were the guys who try to take his wife in his "old" hood. Kid#2: Yeah those guys did look about our age. Besides, he might tear our posters up! You know he doesn't like Maize!! I didn't have to say a word. Those kids spoke for me. Kyle showed us his "Hood" and all we got were teenager trying to spit game to his wife. Yeah that is tough, sarcastically speaking of course. Maize continues to walk down the stairs of the hotel. As he pass people by you can hear them whispering..."Is that that guy from that TV show","Thats the Charity guy","Thats the wrestler guy...um Maize". Maize pays no attention to those comments and walks out the hotel door. When he walks outside it looks like a perfect day, not cloud in the sky. As he approaches his Limo he stops, looks around and sees that the Carver-Hawkeye Arena isn't that far ahead. Even though he can't see the arena, he sees a sign that says Carver-Hawkeye Arena this way -----> down the street. He backs up from the Limo, and walks down the sidewalk to the sign. Chuck,as always, follows him all the way to the sign. He looks to his right and see the arena is about a good mile down. He continue to walks. He still has the same look on his face than when he was in his hotel room. At this point while he is walking it seems like he goes into deep thought. "Still i don't understand the actions of Kyle Pearl. What does he wants me to realize, that he was the school bully or some shit." Thoughts like that ran through my head as I walked down those streets.They continued: "Really, if I can be honest with myself, where I grew up makes where he grew up look like Sesame Streets!! But he still insists on trying to prove a point that isn't sharpened. Don't he get what is going to happen to him tomorrow. He is going to come into that match with a false sense of reality and walk out as a bloody loss soul. Is he that blind?" Before Maize could finish his thought process he was at the arena. He walked in through the VIP door, after a security check of course. He walked in to a Beautiful arena with gold and black interior,Maize's wrestling colors. They have the wrestling ring 3/4th set up. Only the apron and decorations are not up. Only 10 people are in the arena. They seem to be the set up crew. Maizes grabs one of the fold up chairs, black with a Hawkeye basketball logo on it, and slides into the ring. The Set-up crew looks at him for moment, then they go back to work. Maize unfolds the chair in the middle of the ring with the same look on his face. Maize takes off his sun glasses and catches a view of the arena himself. He sits in the chair and stairs at the half done Aggression Logo near the entrance way.Once again Maize goes into deep thought as the cameras slowly zoom back and gradually fade away. You may have seen empty seats and a couple crew-men. But what i saw was all the fans throwing their cares away for a moment of entertainment and happiness. While Kyle is probably still focusing on his past, I am "In-visioning" the future. The only guaranteed future. Me vs Kyle Pearl in a first blood street match.... This is what I do for a living. I don't care where I or Kyle Pearl grew up, we both will have to face off in the streets of Iowa. A street that belongs to neither of us.But.... I have walk those streets today.....I said nothing verbal today.......And won't say anything else until after my match....Because i would just be waisting my breath like Kyle Pearl. Kyle Pearl tries to force someone to understand him, now he is weak from irritation. Whereas I meditated the whole day and physically pre-pared for my match. Now like I said before the phase "I can show you better than I can tell you" doesn't apply to Kyle Pearl. But there is one phase that is Uncontested in the amount of truth it contains......And that phase is: "ACTIONS SPEAK LOUDER THAN WORDS" {Fade out Completely} You ask me was I ready Kyle Pearl......I'll show you!!!
