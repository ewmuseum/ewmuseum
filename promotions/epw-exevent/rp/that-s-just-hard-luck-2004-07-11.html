That's just Hard Luck

11 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(OOC: Hey, I stayed up till 1am waiting for my password, but it didn't show. So I wake up this morning and find it in my mailbox, so I'm posting now hoping you understand the situation) The screen is black at first, but a voice cuts through it like a knife in butter. "Right... let's go 'head and do this." The darkness fades away to reveal a lovely scene, the sunrise shining down over the city of Columbia, South Carolina. The city's not as bustling as it usually is, but there are those few who are moving along their way to the early services of their churches. One young man didn't have time for church... at least not this day. He had something more on his mind... like the match he was booked in so suddenly for EPW Warfare. Against a man named Russell Wayne... who didn't seem to think this was a match at all, but just something to make him look good. That... was not the case. Think more of the opposite, Russ. Standing on the balcony of his hotel room, the young upstart Xian Phillipse looked out at the city as he leaned against the railing with his arms. Dressed in black jeans with white boots and a white tank top showing ripped muscles in his arm and shoulders, the black haired youth kept his eyes out on the streets as he spoke to the camera. "You know... normally I don't do this type of shit, talkin' to the camera... but I saw somethin' that made me wanna talk. So..." He looked over to the camera, leaning on his left side against the railing. "Russell Wayne... just what tha FUCK are you thinking?" His face frowned up as he asked that question, eyes turning stern as he stared into the camera. "I mean... damn, was that the best you could do? Cut some bitch ass 2 minute promo wit' enough dead air between each scene to make it look like ten minutes? I'm new to the company, expect them to give me a test to make sure they didn't book a jobber to their show, and instead of a challenge they give me a jobber to go over on? I appreciate lookin' good 'n shit, but I do that on me own, fuck handouts. I've come too far wit' too much work involved for things to get easy now." Signing, he looked away, shaking his head as he looked back down at the street. Then he looked back up at the camera, showing he wasn't done venting his frustrations. "An' on top of that, this guy brushes me off like I'ma insignificant no-name punk that couldn't possibly have any talent... and goes off to talk about someone else! Hey yo... you're cuttin' that piece of shit promo against ME... not against Ace, Spade, Club, King, or the rest o' the fuckin' deck of cards, ya hear me? You're fighting me tonight, so I think it's best you worry about me, and not what this Ace guy is doin' Otherwise you're gonna find yourself with another L on your record, and I'm gonna start my career in EPW off to a nice start with a win over you." He gripped the railing of the balcony and leaned up, looking up at the sky for a while. He definitely had something on his mind, a gentle breeze picking up and blowing through his hair a bit. Pushing away a few locks from his eyes, he looked into the camera, hesitating for a short moment. "This is a new day, can't you see that? Sun's up, birds chirpin' people up and about gettin' their shit done before the day gets all clustered wit' everyone else and their bullshit. This new day is the beginnin' of a new life... more mines here, and I'm gonna start shit off right here by dealin' with ya Russ. Tonight... here in Columbia... it's gonna be Xian Phillipse beginnin' his legacy in EPW by handlin' this bitch right proper. I mean, after that weak ass promo you produced, I feel kinda insulted by not just you, but by EPW." Smoothing his hair back behind his ear, he cut his eyes after giving the camera a short glance. "I mean... first Russell completely blows me off like I ain't shit... then EPW books this shit like it's supposed to be a tryout match... when it's more like a blowout match. I'm startin' to think they booked me around here to be made a fool of... and that's somethin' I just don't appreciate... not one damn bit." Xian turned around to lean back against the railing, folding his arms across his toned chest. "Shiiiiiit, heh, I may be new here, but it seems like me an' this Ace dude already got somethin' in common. We're both gonna kick that ass... but this time you don't have to sleep with my girl to earn this ass-whipping either. Consider it a souvenier... 'cause you'll look back on this night when I walked into the ring and made you feel smaller than that cock Pam Frost has to fake it with every night. But hey... that's what the slut gets for leavin' Ace for ya. I mean she had a decent 'Ace in the hole' every night, and then she goes off with this guy who loses his first round... that's kinda downsizin', ya know? But hey... I'm not gonna beat around the bush here like Russell does with Pam, I'm doin' this promo to address the issues." He held up one finger as if he were counting. "First, Russell Wayne, if you ever face me again, I pray that you put more effort into stepping in the ring with me, or I won't be responsible for what happens to you. You bettah be hella glad there's insurance for wrestlers... because if you cut another tiny-dick ass promo like the one you did again... I'll crush your windpipe." The second finger came up, which was also counted. "Second, EPW... hey, how you doin? EPW... next time you cheat the fans out of givin' them a decent match wit' 'The Hard Luck Kid' Xian Phillipse, I'ma go hunt down whoevah the fuck the boss is and smack him right in his butt ugly face. You book me, you do it so that the fans aren't cheated outta their money. See, you're puttin' the health of Russell Wayne at risk. I hate when I'm thrown the bottom feeders, and so I tend to get a bit of a reputation as a hard ass when the wuss they send my way gets their nose broken or some shit like that. But... you know what?" He looked up at the camera as if he just had an epiphany, a slight smile coming onto his face slowly. He held up a finger, wagging it at the camera before closing his hand into a fist. "That's how shit is in the real world. You get chopped steak when you think you asked for prime rib, and you get some hermaphrodite in drag instead of the hot broad you spent all night talkin' to in the club. That's reality... and Russell Wayne, don't get pissy when you find yourself embarrassed and beaten in that ring. That's how life is... nothing... but Hard Luck." Xian Phillipse's smirk faded from his face as he glared into the camera, his handsome face cutting to static as the promo ended.
