Times Change....People Die....

20 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up inside a hotel room, clothes are all over the floor and the chairs, tables are over turned, the lamp on the dresser has been broken and tossed to the floor, the mirror to the dresser has been shattered. The only light that seems to be on is in the bathroom. The door to the hotel room opens up slowly and laughter is heard. In from the bright light come Jason and Amylee, Amylee flips on the light and they both stop dead in their tracks and look around. Amylee brings her hands to her mouth in shock, Jason proceeds to look around the room] Jason Slade: Damn baby! You really must of pissed off the maids. [Amylee pointing to the counter next to the bed] Amylee: I tipped her eight dollars!! Jason Slade: Damn this place is a mess, I wonder what happened in here? [Amylee walks into the bathroom where a piece of paper is tapped to the wall, just above the toilet. She pulls it off the  wall and walks out and towards Jason] Amylee: Jase? I found this. [Jason looks at her face and grabs the piece of paper from Amy's trebling hand] Jason Slade: Calm down baby, everything will be okay, I promise. [Jason opens up the piece of paper, it reads] ...Dear big shot...You've called yourself the Real Deal...You live by your saying  "Pain is Inevitable, Suffering is Optional"  ...Sunday you will find out the credo you live by, will be the one you die by... ???
