Relenquish Your Efforts.

24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There wasn’t much to his appearance. Most people would overlook him as well as his position. Despite his small frame and outlandish appearance, most of those who passed by him paid him little or no attention. That was their mistake. His legs were folded up like a pretzel, limbs intermingling into a seated knot. With large hands positioned at his side, his body was at ease. His almond eyes were closed, his breathing slow and paced. Around him the clanking of iron against iron seemed consistent, along with the strenuous grunts of other human beings straining their bodies. They were more concerned more on getting what they wanted, not on their surroundings and who may be in them. That was their mistake. Xian had no care for them. None of those upstart egotistical imbeciles were worth his trouble. These older ones who wished to be all that they could be…they were lost in their own decorated past. All they saw within this company was a way to become rich and famous…they cared nothing of the thrill of beating another man’s ass. True, he was the new generation of fighters…arrogant, cocky, absurd, mediocre, and overall a total anarchist. One sight of this man in uncontrollable action and he would bring the great saint of battle to his knees in sorrow. As he sat over in the darkest corner of the gym, he could also see each and every one of the new breeds. Youthful, energetic, with a fresh fire to rise up into stardom, they were. Inspirational? Not really…their motivation was flawed. Upon entering the field of battle called wrestling, these individuals all looked upon it as one thing. A CAREER. Only a career, where they provide a service, and they get paid well for it with added perks of being in the public’s spotlight; this is their JOB. Nothing more…nothing less. That…IS… their mistake. “Battle: a combat between two persons. I fully agree with that definition. My combat his existed with this wretched man ever since I arrived here. And he actually thinks he can surpass my power, what a fool. I knew from the very first day I laid eyes upon this man that he would serve to be trouble for us in the future. I can foresee these things, just as I can foresee both him and all the other’s defeat at my hands come Unleashed and beyond. I have to make sure that there is nothing left of them before I step into utter combat once again with this man who thinks he is my superior. I don’t like loose ends left lying around.” “Battle: a general encounter between armies, ships or war, or aircraft. Hah, this also is true, except that my encounter with these two people representing the new generation will be nothing but general. My encounter will be EXTREME…carnage and blood will pave my way to victory over my enemies. My armies…consisting of two single entities, Xian and Addams, in some way the more lightweight Xian is the aircraft of this war, where the Addams exists as the ship of war, Xian would say SHIT of war. In simple realization, aircraft, they have the advantage by being both faster, strike first, and keep moving. Doomed they will be when they come to realize that there is no stopping the war machine that I am…but by then it will be too late. The sound of the bell declaring their defeat will snap them out of their self-hypnosis. Of course all of their ego stroking and confidence does nothing only but to entrance their own minds in order to somehow devise an impetus to fight back in a blatantly hopeless fight.” “Battle: an extended contest, struggle, or controversy. Extended is the correct word. Time and time again I have been haunted by mindless foes that think that they may come back into the light and propel themselves off of my glorious fighting skills. Sure, taking a beating from me will get them noticed, but are they looking for the attention of the bookers…or the EMTs? Most likely the latter…why else would they endanger themselves by stepping up to face me? Controversy has nothing to do with Addams and my battle. My battle is nothing more than pure and unadulterated hatred between two people, and soon…I will see who holds the more hatred between us. Though hatred is sometimes something that clouds the minds of people, I use it to stimulate my fight. It will be upcoming…that my test of mettle will occur.” “Victory…is not so sweet until you thrash your opponent into unconsciousness. I have tasted the sweet nectar that comes with it when referring to that man Xian was to face…and the taste seems so familiar. It is a taste that has you craving for more. It is a tease that had you thirsty again after your first drink. It is a flavor that had you famished again after your first meal. It is a savor that takes over your mind. It becomes a…lust. Ah…my lusts…there are so many that I soon forget which is the greater. One has a tendency to reappear through us time and time again, and that one is my dominating lust for battle.” “Battle, it has been my whole life, ever since birth. My destiny was already set into motion from that day… to fight forever…with weaklings who have no better common sense but to challenge us because I are forced to remain in the background. However, some have made it clear to make fun and jest of my mental situation. Have these men no idea of the eternal struggle within? They should know of it…they go through it every day when they say words…words like, “ I can defeat Xian. He is a peasant, a lower form of life… I have full confidence in my abilities Xian prides himself upon his skills, and it will be his undoing. What the heck is a Xian??”…Words echo forth with the sound of idiocy, yet they cannot help themselves for being fools. I know that they can hear them floating around their head now. Each time they try to build up something within themselves, the internal struggle inside of them begins. They see those words as their mind reassuring them that they are indeed well prepared to face us. Unbeknownst to their minds…the darkness exists within them all.” “Evil exists in all of us as I have said before on countless occasions. It takes some time for some to embrace their inner darkness. That is why the struggle takes place. The two forces of light and dark within living things battle back and forth, like two rival deities fighting for the position of the mortal realm’s supremacy. It is human nature to choose what side suits their pleasure the best…human nature is a selfish object. So selfish that it chooses to demean one person of the proper understanding by holding onto false hopes in order to have some control of their fighting ability. In contrast to human nature, the darkness within us is the true path, and there are few beings around us that can truly understand this. In the outcome of this battle between face versus heel…good versus evil…the Aura versus the Void…bet your money on the here and now, Xian…not some Prophet.” Some of the younger guys noticed the man meditating in the corner. They have their comments, being narrow-minded like the majority of humanity. Pointing and snickering like the post-pubescent ego monkeys that they are. You know how Xian is about mockery towards him; it usually doesn’t last long before they find themselves with their arm bending in the other direction. This time, however, the future EPW champion just sat there in his black tank top and black sweats and sneakers in silence. He didn’t have time for punks, especially when he had to deal with the queen of the punks and his jester on Warfare . The same two guys were making jokes about the appearance of Xian still. Just like the uneducated to make comments on what they know nothing of. They were both in gray sweats, though the blonde man was in a white shirt, and the brunette was wearing a gray tank top. Both of them took one last snicker at him before walking away towards the showers. At their lockers, they both rummaged through their belongings for their shower equipment. Just then, the blonde’s head was smashed into the back of his locker before being sandwiched in by the door of the locker. The brunette turned around to find his friend’s body hanging limp outside of his locker, his head caught within the locker door. Friends know how to stick together. He went to check over on his buddy, though the damage has already been done. Then he heard it: the metal slamming approaching him quickly like thunder. He pulled his friend out of the locker and looked up just to find the set of lockers behind him toppling down upon him. Like an iron set of giant dominoes, the row of lockers tipped against one another before finally crashing into the wall. The dust settled, and from that dry mist stood the image of Xian Phillipse. His face was as blank as a sheet of paper, but the vein on his forehead throbbed madly like a tiny individual heart. With a turn of his massive frame, Xian was back on his way out of the locker room, going against the flow of nosey people wondering what just occurred. Within Xian's head, only one thought ran. “Addams…train…learn…then relinquish your efforts in defeat to me…”
