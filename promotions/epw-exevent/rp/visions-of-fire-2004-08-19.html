Visions of fire

19 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*A lesson is taught by those who have had bad experiences with a certain point in there life thus they teach these lessons so is to prevent that same mistake from happening again to another and so on. Last Friday a certain someone forgot to teach their pupil a lesson on how to choose their enemies wisely and to finish the job for as the old sane goes..."what goes around come comes around" but what I am about to experience is a vision, a vision that will teach me the lesson that I have forgotten for so long the lesson of ignorance.* {The scene fades in on Khaos who is in a undisclosed room surrounded by images, images of the people he as encountered throughout his career people he has defeated and people he as met and people he has lost to, although most of the images are faint and hard to make out only three images can be made out one is Shawn Singleton another is John Adams and finally the last one is Chapel. Looking at the three images Khaos is baffled at what he is seeing and wonders who is showing these visions to him and why.} KHAOS: Whats going on here is this some kind of fucked up joke or what ANSWER ME who are you and what the hell is going on here! *The three images say nothing as Khaos is getting fed up with the silence, but just as he is about to speak another image comes between the three it is faint at first but it looks like the image of what he used to be the image of Big Bad John.* KHAOS: What is this? The hell is going on here this cannot be? You're me...but how.... I thought I got rid of you long ago! Big Bad John: So you thought....but what you do not realize YOU have forgotten something. KHAOS: Oh yeah what is that? Big Bad John: Oh how the mind tends to slip. You want to know? Fine I will tell you what it is, the lesson of ignorance John the lesson that you left behind along with me! KHAOS: The lesson of ignorance? Do not be a fool I have not forgetten that! Big Bad John: Oh really? Tell me something if that is the case then why are you not the world champion better yet why have you not beaten Chapel or our brother yet hmm? *Khaos does not say a word as he looks down in disgust.* Big Bad John: Your face explains it all John. You are too ignorant you take your opponents way too lightly and thus you get sloppy and make careless mistakes. Tell me something do you remember your last match with chapel not the one from evolution but the ones before that? I do every single week he would school us like there was tomorrow becuase of the fact we have forgotten this lesson. Now do you remember the very last match you had with our brother? KHAOS: Yes I do...all to clearly...it was my last shot at the title. Big Bad John: We came within a eyelash of winning the damn thing since then you have stopped being consistent and have gotten soft on your opponents even though you have won the Intercontinental title from Luke Bishop you were still sloppy and that eventually led to your defeat by Chapel and thus you lost the Intercontinental title and got worse from there! KHAOS: But I.... Big Bad John: But what! There is NO EXCUSE for your bullshit what happened to the animal that is inside of you huh? *BBJ then grabs Khaos by the neck and starts the yell the following in his face.* Big Bad John: WHAT THE FUCK HAPPENED JOHN ANSWER ME! Where is the fire John, where is the fire that brought you within a eyelash of winning the world title! KHAOS: Ugh..it is still here....the fire...yes it's here...I can feel it again yes that feeling, it is as I remember...I can see it all clearly now... *BBJ lets go of his neck as the images of Chapel and John Adams disappear in a swirl of flames and the image of Alias appears along side of Shawn Singleton Then walls slowly catch fire as BBJ walks towards Khaos setting his hands on fire in the process.* Big Bad John: Look at my hands and tell me what you see. Do you see the animal you once were? Do you see the fire that you once had? Have you learned anything for this encounter John? Most importantly have you learned your lesson about ignorance. KHAOS: Yes it has become clear to me now I am to obliterate every fuckin superstar in EPW period and with the help of Cain we from a tag team like no other we will be called THE SOVEREIGNS OF MALICE! Big Bad John: Good now you are ready to take EPW by storm once again! *The Images of Shawn and Alias disappear in a swirl of flames as BBJ walks towards Khaos engulfed in flames and says one last thing.* Big Bad John: Unleash the animal, unleash the rage let loose the fire that is known is KHAOS! The Anarachist of Anarchy! *The dream ends in a flash of white as Khaos wakes up for what seems like a long nitemare he gets up and sits at the edge of his bed with a demented look on his face.* KHAOS: Time to burn everyone in hell starting with Alias. Cain how long have you been sitting there? *Cain takes a few steps forward but stays in the dark corner of the room.* Cain: Well now by the movements in the bed I say you were having a little nitemare or am I wrong? KHAOS: I call it a wake up call to realize I have been doing things all wrong all this time Cain, I have been ignorant, sloppy and careless in the ring, yeah I am Intercontinental champion but I won it from a drunk not a real competitor if I am going to turn heads and make these halfwit superstars in EPW notice me then I am going to have to start making examples out of everyone one of my opponents each and every week! Cain: Well now don't we have goals...I do as well one is to get into EPW and kicking some teeth in these so called "superstars" here need a lesson in pain and I am the only who can end their suffering with The Cure hehehe. KHAOS: Have you turned the contract in yet? Or are you waiting for me to "make a move". Cain: Oh I turned it in do not worry I will get a response sooner or later if not blood will be spilled I can tell you this much. KHAOS: Regardless you know the plan for Breaking Point right? Cain: Yes it will be executed with ease and little effort so there is no need to worry. KHAOS: Excellent! However there is one final task I wish for you to do Cain. * Cain says nothing as he nods to Khaos.* KHAOS: Sell this place then wait for the sale to go though then....burn it down this was my stepdads building no one will use it but me and besides he would have wanted it that way anyway HAHAHAHA! Cain: Heh I see, if I may ask why do you wish this place destroyed? KHAOS: It is boring now and that I am sick and tried of serving the masses. One more thing bring a EPW camera man and Jule Mercade into here I have a announcement concerning my match this Friday. Cain: Consider it done. *Cain walks off into the darkness and leaves the room.* 2 HOURS LATER Jule Mercadel: Hello I am Jule Mercadel here to get a exclusive interview with The Khaotic One himself, today he will tell us that he has a special announcement concerning his match at Aggression and he will discuss his opponent Alias who he is taking on this Friday in a non-title match! So Khaos since coming back you do realize that you have only lost once and that you are undefeated on Aggression so far, your thoughts? KHAOS: Simple I have plowed though my opponents with ease and I will not stop until I get what I want and that is the world title and if I have to go though EVERY damn superstar here in EPW then so be it! Jule Mercadel: Ok, moving on to the next question the announcement you wish to make for your match this Friday, what do you have planned for Alias? KHAOS: I am glad you asked Jule. Brother listen up here's what I want you to do your going to make this Fridays match.....AN INFERNO MATCH! Jule Mercadel: An Inferno match!? That has not been seen since Total Chaos do you think your brother will do it and make it an Inferno match? KHAOS: Of course he will I know my brother all to well he will do it. Jule Mercadel: I have been told as well that the Breaking Point card has been set it will be an EPW INTERCONTINENTAL TITLE THREE-WAY MATCH Khaos vs. Shawn Singleton vs. Hudman! KHAOS: Hudman? But how he is not under contract in EPW! Jule Mercadel: According to the rumors your brother plans to sign him as of this Friday. Your thoughts on that as well? KHAOS: Hmph so he wants to come back eh? I will just have to do somthing about that then. Jule Mercadel: Fair enough, about our newcomer Clarence Carmichael what do you think about him? KHAOS: What do you mean "what do I think about him" bah what kind of dumbass question is that? All I have to say is this Carmichael you stay on your side and I will stay on my side get it? Good! Jule Mercadel: One last thing Khaos, do you have any words for your opponent Alias who you will be facing this Friday in a non-title match? KHAOS: Ah I almost forgot about you Alias this Friday will be delightful for me as I take my first step to domination but not as delightful you for you see, you have the privilege of taken me on in a inferno match did you honestly think that you would get away with what you have done this past Friday? No I am sorry kid accident or not you must pay for your sins because in this world when you fuck with Khaos your playing fire and when you play with fire you get burned! This Friday I am going to make an example out of this little bastard and I want everyone in EPW to pay special attention to this match because I am going show this little punk what happens to people who take me lightly he suffer a horrible fate like no other and I will make sure this message gets across the entire roster I HAVE COME FOR YOU ALL so once I am done with Alias if any of you want a piece of me your more than welcome to try but the result will be same I will make you suffer a horrible fate just like the one Alias is going to suffer from and there is not a damn soul left on this planet who can say otherwise! So Alias bring all you have your prayers, your hopes and your dreams because this Friday the games are over this time Khaos is for real and I am going to throw all those prayers, hopes and dreams in the bowels of hell and as for you Shawn don't think that things between me and you are over oh no its just getting started and Hunter listen very carefully you will suffer as well, that I promise! This interview is over escort him out of my face Cain. Cain: Right. Lets go moron The Khaotic One Has Spoken! *Cain escorts Jule of the room as the scene fades to black.* Soon The Sovereigns of Malice will reign supreme!
