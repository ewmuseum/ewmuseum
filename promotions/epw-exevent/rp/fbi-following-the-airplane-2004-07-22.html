FBI- Following the Airplane

22 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up to the airplane we had just landed on from jumping from the other one we were on. We were sitting on the back of the wings on the plane. It was very dangerous, so none of us moved at all except for when we talked. The wind was blowing hard against us almost taking us off, and letting us drift through the air like rag dolls. I had just been informed that I was against Chris Clarence in a match on Aggression. What made it even worse, was that at EPW's PPV that was this Sunday, I was in a four way match for the winner of the Soul Survivor tournament. My first match ever in EPW I was against Joey Dangerously, for a shot to be in the finals of the Soul Survivor at the PPV. It was me against Karl Jackson, Psycho Dan, and the winner of the Ace/Khaos match that was scheduled for Aggression. The winner of this four way match will not only be crowned Soul Survivor but will also get a chance at the World Title two months later at the EPW PPV. If I won that four way I would get my dream chance of winning a shot at the World Title, which was currently helf by Chapel.) Jackson Kable: Damn its windy up here. Stephen: No kidding. Christina: How do we get into the airplane? Jackson Kable: Of course we just cut open the roof of the airplane and drop in. Christina: Oh that helps. This roof is like pure metal how are we supposed to break through it and just drop in out of now where. Stephen: We could just be really careful, and climb to the side where the door is, and break that window to get into the plane. That would always work. Jackson Kable: I think that would be easier. I'll break the window with my fist. (We started carefully climbing around the top of the airplane, searching for the side that had the door on it. We found it, and I started climbing down to it, ready to break the glass with my fist. I punched it as hard as I could and shattered pieces went everywhere. I unhooked the latch from the inside and all of us walked into the plane. Joey Campbell was caught completely off guard because when we got into the plane he was masturbating. He looked up at us and quickly zipped up his pants. We all started laughing as hard as we could, as Joey's face went brick red.) Joey Campbell: Oh its you dickwads again. What the hell do you want with me. You want to kill me? well first get past my guards. (We turned around and saw 10 guuards standing there cracking there knuckles with some having there arms crossed. I looked at the smallest one and started laughing. He was a midget. The little midget guard came running after me. I tripped it and then picked it up for a military press slam. I chucked the midget into the wall of the airplane. That was one down, and 9 to go. All of the guards started rushing us like we were little kids eating lollipops. All of them jumped at us and started punching, they didn't even know who they were punching. I grabbed two of them by the shirts, and choked them out till they were not breathing. I took one and chucked him out of the door of the airplane. I could see him flying through the air. The rest we took care of like nothing. All of them were down. We turned back to Joey, but he wasn't there. I looked out the window and saw that he had taken a parachute and jumped out the exit door when we were beating on his guards.) Christina: We need parachutes. Jackson Kable: Pilot, we need parachutes. Pilot: There in the back cupboard over there. (We walked over and each strapped on a parachute. I was the first one to jump. I jumped out the door and then Stephen and Christina did. We were all flying through the air towards Joey Campbell. We must have went really fast because we were just above him.. Something must've have been wrong with his parachute. We starte gaining on him quick as the scene started to fade.)
