The Release

13 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Part 1 ::The camera begins to tape and slowly fades into three jail cell bars a dark misty jail cell. The cell bars have a rusted bronze look to them with the water dropping down from the walls and bars created by the condensed steam flowing throughout the entire jail. The camera slowly zooms out from the bars exposing the rest of the jail cell. The inside of the cell is very dark because of the jails dying lights and it being night. The camera pans over the corner of the cell where the outline of a man’s body can be seen created be the dim light created by the full moon coming in through the bared window of the cell. The man appears to be very muscular with a strong man build. Aside from the outline of the man’s body, the rest of the man is covered by darkness. The camera slowly begins to zoom closer into the man as he begins to speak.:: Unknown Man: For years now I have been sitting, rotting in this jail. I have been paying the price for the crimes I have committed over the years. The stealing, hurting, murdering I have done has landed me in a place of no return. A place where all are the enemy. A place where the society has forever cast me. A place where evil men are supposed to break down. Well I for one have not broken down. Captivity has made me stronger. Though I killed, I still felt bits of guilt that were my weakness. Confinement has erased those thoughts and feeling from my mind. Now I have a greater thirst to kill as the more pain, hurt and suffering I cause, the more I crave to do it again. This craving has pushed me to my mental and physical limits. I have trained to become stronger mentally and physically then I ever was before. Society thought they will killing the monster that I am but in reality they have killed my weaknesses and made me even stronger. ::The man suddenly pauses and turns his head toward the bared window of the jail cell. The camera pans over to the window where the man is staring into to. Outside a few drops of water begins to drop from the sky quickly followed by a hard shower of rain. As the speed and amount of the rain increases, huge dark black begin to form in the sky and the crashing sound of thunder is heard. The pans back over to the man who now has his head elevate upwards and his arms spread open as the outside rain begins to seep through the concrete roof of the cells. As the rain begins to built up on the roof, the water begins to heavily poor into the cell. The man stays in position as he embraces the huge amounts of water now pouring down on top of his from the roof.:: Unknown Man: You may think you are safe because I am confined to a jail cell keeping me away from the weak and innocent, but soon I will be out. Soon I will be out of this cell, on the public streets you walk on each and every day with your friends, family and complete strangers. The wrestling career and life that was taken from me when I am brought here will once again be in my grasp. I will once again know how it is to live a life of freedom. I will have a career. And once I am out, those who has the luxury of living there lives while a rotted in jail will feel my rather. This Friday I am out. My first victim? ALIAS!!! ::Lightning begins to fill the jail shell and a glimpse of the sadistic man’s face can be seen as the in the quick flashing light. The camera begins to slowly zoom out of the jail cells and fades into darkness as the signal cuts.:: Part 2 ::As the man finishes the promo, a tall Southern jail guard dressed in a grey and black security suit and black security had walks up the at camera man is now beginning recording some extra footage of the jail with a young blonde interviewer/reporter instructing the man on what to specific things to capture on tape. As the security guard reaches the camera man, he places his hand over the camera lens blocking the cameras view.:: Jail Guard: What do you two think your doing? You were only supposed to record the promo. That’s it. Now get out of here! Interviewer/Reporter: Umm sir, we are just recording some extra footage of the jail just in case we want to use it for later or throw it into the last edit of the promo. Jail Guard: Well I’m sorry missy but ya’ll have to leave now. We are loading SKuLL into the back of the jail security truck to bring him over to the Carver-Hawkeye Arena in Iowa City. Aggression is tomorrow night you known. Interviewer/Reporter: SKuLL? Is that the man’s name? Well could we record some of that? Jail Guard: Hell No!!! Missy, SKuLL is most dangerous in situations like this. Why just last year he killed two guards shipping him off to his jail. I am saying no for your protection. Interviewer/Reporter: Please, I will pay you. How’s $2000 sound? ::The lady pulls out the 20 freshly printed $100 bills out of her purse and hands it over to the guard. The man takes the money and glances at it for a few seconds before stuffing it into his pocket.:: Jail Guard: Missy, I’ll let you tape but don’t blame me if you wined up dead. Interviewer/Reporter: I’ll be fine. Now get your man over to the cell so we can start recording. ::The jail guard takes his hand off the camera lens and calls for his men to come over to the cell with the equipment needed to move SKuLL. Within a few minutes, 6 men dressed in security suits walk over to SKuLL’s cell. Four of the men are heavily armed with assault rifles and a hand gun on each side of them. All four men are also wearing black protective helmets with a half shield protecting their faces. The other 2 men are not armed. Instead, one of them is holding a straight jacket and chains and the other men is holding a mouth harness, leg chains handcuffs. The men slowly open SKuLL’s cell as he awaiting them. The four armed men quickly walk in and aim their guns at SKuLL’s head in case he tries any tricks. SKuLL decides to keep cool and allowed the two other men to get the equipment of him. The men spend a few minutes securing the harness, handcuffs, leg chains and straight jacket, locked the chain sitting over the straight jacket in place and begin to move SKuLL down the cell way halls to the security truck sitting outside. The interviewer/reporter and camera man run and catch up the SKuLL as he is being escorted down the hall. The interviewer/reporter flips on her microphone.:: Interviewer/Reporter: SKuLL, for about a week or so now there have been rumors that you would be released from jail and coming back to wrestling which we all known that your going to EPW. How did you get a deal that got you released from prison? ::SKuLL turns his head toward the lady who is now pointing her microphone in the direction of SKuLL’s mouth. SKuLL gives a faint smirk.:: SKuLL: Who the fuck are you? Interviewer/Reporter: Oh, I am just some random person trying to get a permanent job with EPW. Now, can you please just answer the question. SKuLL: My agent made a deal with some people. He knows some people in high places. Interviewer/Reporter: Ok, well then what are your plans in EPW. SKuLL: I plan to go for the Heavyweight Title. No matter how long it takes. For years I have been sitting at the absolute bottom of society, which is prison, and I am going to do what it takes to make it back on top of the world. ::The interviewer goes for another question to in pushed aside now by the security guard as the men have reached the security truck. The two unarmed men open the back door of the truck and press the flashing red button sitting of the inside of the back door. Suddenly, a cold mist begins to pour from the back of the truck as a silver metallic chair slowly begins to from out from inside the truck. Once the chair is completely out, the men sit SKuLL down in the chair. On the chair is a leg, waste and neck strap. The men rap the straps around SKuLL and tighten then securing SKuLL to the chair. Once secure, one of the security guards presses the side button and the metallic chair is pulled back into the truck. Then men lock the door and head into the truck. The one jail guard walks over to the interviewer/reporter and camera man.:: Jail Guard: Well this is where the trip end. We are closing up for tonight so it’s time for you two to leave. See ya’ll later. Interviewer/Reporter: But… Jail Guard: But Nothin’. Ya’ll go on home now. Interviewer/Reporter: God damnit…Ok cut the signal we’re done here. ::The two walk off the jail grounds and get into an EPW Camera Van where all the camera equipment is being kept. The camera signal is cut as the camera man begins to it into the van.::
