What is wrong with this crompany?

24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{A camera follows Tom Conway as he walks down the streets of a fairly populated town not too far from the site of this week’s Aggression. Tom Conway repeatedly glances back at the camera as he walks down the busy streets in mid-day. The sun shines into the lens of the camera as the sounds of people chatting, cars zooming by and footsteps can be heard. Conway carefully makes his way around the other people walking down the sidewalk as the camera follows. Tom Conway looks back at the camera with a slight smile on his face.} Tom Conway: I can’t believe that I agreed to do this stupid thing. {Conway questions the cameraman.} Tom Conway: What am I supposed to do now? Talk to the fans or something and allow the camera to follow me as I go about my business as if it were any other day? Cameraman: Yeah, that’s about right. Tom Conway: Screw this. This sucks. {Tom Conway bumps into a middle-aged lady.} Tom Conway: Sorry… Lady: Watch where you are walking, sonny. {The lady walks past the cameraman as Tom Conway looks on. During this time, a young, African-American kid walks up to Conway and stands next to him. He then begins to yell into the camera.} Boy: EPW is da-bomb-diggity-bomb, sucka! {Tom Conway turns towards the boy and looks at him with a look of surprise.} Tom Conway: What’s up? Boy: Hey man, this is Robert. My name is Robert Klicko. {The boy puts out his hand, looking for Tom Conway to give him a handshake. Tom Conway pulls his hand back and looks at the boy with a look of discomfort on his face.} Tom Conway: You know what, I better not. I am about to eat in a few minutes. {The boy looks at Conway with a confused look on his face.} Robert Klicko: Well, I guess that it’s all good, homey. {Robert looks back into the camera and yells once again.} Robert Klicko: Man, we all know that Tom Conway is the bomb, but I gots a question for you guys there; what da brothers at in EPW? {Tom Conway nudges the boy.} Tom Conway: So, what do you think of what is going on right now in EPW? {Robert looks at Conway with a disappointed look on his face.} Robert Klicko: Oh, don’t even get me started about the crompany. Tom Conway: Crompany? Robert Klicko: You heard me. The problem is wit the guys in the office. Tom Conway: What do you mean? Robert Klicko: We need to see Tom Conway on the top here in the EPW. We need to see The Con up there winning the titles. Tom Conway: Well, Chapel is the current World Champion. {Robert looks at Conway with a disappointed look on his face.} Robert Klicko: Don’t even get me started about Chapel. Tom Conway: Why? Robert Klicko: Just because. Tom Conway: I guess that is a valid reason. Robert Klicko: It sure is. You know what else is valid, the World Championship. Tom Conway: Do you mean valued? Robert Klicko: No, I mean valid. Tom Conway: Well, words escape me. Robert Klicko: How come you didn’t escape that Cage Match? Tom Conway: You mean, escape from the cage to win? Robert Klicko: Yeah, of course. Tom Conway: Well, I would prefer not to talk about that. Robert Klicko: Yeah, I see what you mean. {Tom Conway and the cameraman begin to walk away from the fan.} Robert Klicko: Hey, where are you guy going to? Tom Conway: I have to go get something to eat at a restaurant down the street. Robert Klicko: Hey, you want to go to Gorilla Hut’s? {Tom Conway takes a step towards the fan and the camera focuses on the confused look on Conway’s face.} Tom Conway: What the hell is Gorilla Hut’s? Robert Klicko: It’s my favorite pizza place. Tom Conway: I don’t eat pizza. Robert Klicko: Everybody eats pizza. Even dogs eat pizza sometimes. I have a dog that loves pizza. I found him on the street one day. Tom Conway: Good for you. Robert Klicko: Yeah, good for me. Tom Conway: Well, I have to get going. {Tom Conway steps away from Robert.} Robert Klicko: Can I go with you? {Tom Conway pauses and looks back at Robert.} Tom Conway: I would let you come along, but you need to have reservations. Robert Klicko: Oh, well thanks anyway. {Tom Conway begins to walk away once again. As the camera starts to pan towards Conway, Robert is seen walking a few feet down the street and talking to strangers that pass him by. The camera focuses on Tom Cownay as he continues to walk down the street away from Robert. He then looks into the camera with a slight smile on his face.} Tom Conway: What the hell was that? Cameraman: I don’t know. {The camera fades to black.}
