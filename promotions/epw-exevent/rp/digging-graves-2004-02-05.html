Digging Graves....

5 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene starts in a misty dark grave yard. Xion is stood with his manager Alcazar they are both digging a grave. The camera zooms in a the grave and on the grave stone it says “Shawn Mathers R.I.P”. Alcazar looks up at Xion and begins to laugh he then looks at the camera and laughs even more) Alcazar~ Shawn Mathers as you can see we are saving your family from digging your grave. The reason being is because after tomorrow nights match when Xion rips you apart the only decent play for you will be 6 ft under ground. Xion will stop at nothing to destroy you, tomorrow especially because he wants to show EPW what he is all about. This man……or beast as some of you would say is going to put you through pain like never before, see Xion does not give a damn about what he does to his opponents as long as he inflicts huge amounts of pain that is all he cares about. So are you sure you want to step into the ring with a beast who’s only concern is inflicting pain on other people. Xion does not care whether you live or die and there is a high risk you will not be living after the match. Hear is a little sample of what Xion can do. (Alcazar picks up the heavy thick grave stone, then Xion walks over slowly and puts his hand through it in one punch the grave stone breaks into little pieces) Alcazar~ As you can see Xion is a very powerful man! (The camera zooms in on Xions mask he smiles and laughs) Alcazar~ If one punch does that to a thick grave stone just imagine what it will do to your face when his big strong fist lands on you nose first. The pain that you are going to go through is just unbearable even for a normal person to even think about, the very worst of your nightmares are going to come true tomorrow because Xion is one big living nightmare and all of EPW better know what they are in for. All you fools who call your selves superstars better be afraid because you never know when Xion will strike. Speaking of so called superstars take a look at thing. (The camera points at another grave that is to the left of Shawn Mathers. It is a big hole and on the grave stone it says “Mephisto”) Alcazar~ Xion does not take kindly to people calling him a “over grown luchadore” tomorrow you will see that Xion is anything but a luchadore. Mephisto just because you see Xion wearing a mask that does not mean he is a Mexican. So Mephisto I think you are worthy of a punishment and in the next few weeks you shall receive your punishment and believe me it’s going to be quite a painful punishment. So be scared, after what you see what happens to Shawn Mathers I have a feeling that you will be leaving the country with a brown stain on your under wear. No body talks about Xion like you did and gets away with it maybe after every one sees what happens to you then maybe people will keep there mouth shut in future. Any way enough talking for today……..Shawn Mathers tomorrow you’ll be seeing Xion in the ring and believe me its going to be HELL! Ha Ha Ha Ha (The screen fades and goes to a commercial)
