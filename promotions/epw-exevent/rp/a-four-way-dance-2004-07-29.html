A four way dance

29 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Fate has looked away from me and thus I did not win the match at Soul Survivor to gain the #1 contendership of the world title. However fate seems to have other things planned for me as I was booked in a four way battle royal for the #1 contendership of the Intercontinental title, the winner of this would go on to face Ace on next weeks show. Will I be victorious or will fate look away saying "You are not worthy." the answer lies in victory.* {The scene fades in on a cold night as Khaos stands on the rooftop of the KHAOTIC KORNER thoughts of the loss at Soul Survivor are fresh in his mind but also must realize that it is in the history books and must move on. A cold breeze runs though the night sky as he stands in the moonlight with thoughts of the pay per view running though his head like a river.} Nightshade: You know Khaos thinking of the past gets you nowhere. *Khaos turns around only to see Nightshade behind him.* KHAOS: You sound just like Ukyo when you say that hehe. But there will be other opportunities for world title soon enough but for now it seems I have a chance to become a two time Intercontinental champion of course once I win this four way battle royal or as I call it a four way dance. Nightshade: A four way dance....sounds acoustic. KHAOS: Heh indeed it does but what I am going to do to the three fool's this Friday is going to be anything but acoustic. Nightshade: Indeed. *Khaos then puts his hand on his chin and goes into deep thought.* KHAOS: So Chapel has done it again I see, but sooner or later his luck WILL run out after all he cannot keep this up forever he will fall. But enough about that fool let's change the subject, KJ you did everything I asked of you and more when I looked into your eyes I saw raw power a monster if you will I want to see that again so I will tell you what once I win the Intercontinental belt I will give you the first shot all I ask is you bring the same attitude you brought to me at Soul Survivor the attitude of a monster but if you do not I have "other ways" of awaking the beast inside of you. *Just then Nightshade's cell rings.* Nightshade: Nightshade here....yeah what's up Troy? Oh really now then I will relay this message to Khaos. *Nightshade hangs up her cell.* KHAOS: So what the hell was that all about? Nightshade: Troy has an interesting promo that he thinks you should see. KHAOS: Really now...oh joy another boring promo well let's go down see what all this fuss is about. *Both Khaos and Nightshade head back downstairs to Khaos's study where Troy awaits them both.* KHAOS: So...what's this "promo" I am going to see. Troy: This is from the newcomer named Maize I believe has a few words for you so I skipped the boring parts and stopped it at this. *Troy puts the tape in as Khaos watches the promo* Especially you Khaos, I thought you would've ejected this tape and burn it as some kind of demonic sacrafice(laughs to himself). I won't be too long. Please forgive me if I delayed your plans of intercepting Cupid's arrows and beating the living hell out of every guy who looks like that fan who beat you a** and made you an satan obsessed anarchist!! Talk about low self-esteem(knods his head in dissapointment). *Troy stops the tape and notices Khaos's sadistic smile* Troy: Hmm I already know what that means so I do not have to ask. KHAOS: Indeed, this just proves how much of a jackass this kid is, are you in that much of a hurry to get your face shoved into the concrete Maize? A "demonic sacrifice and cupids arrows and beating up every fan"? Give me a fuckin break Maize since when do you know anything about me in the first place pal huh and why do you name yourself after a vegetable? Ah I know why because your some fucked up loser who cannot even take care of his own son now that shows me how pathetic and weak minded you really are and if that's what your going to give me in the ring you might as well not EVEN show up I have wrestled with the best in this company AND I WILL NOT BE MADE FUN OF BY A SCREWED UP LITTLE FAGGOT SUCH AS YOURSELF! In short Maize this is a mans sport this is where people like me beat the shit of people like you who THINK just becuase they have got in to EPW they think they are all high and mighty so this coming Friday Maize get ready to put your career on the line becuase it looks like the school of pain is going to be in session HAHAHAHA! As for you Chris Collins I do not know you and I do not give a damn but do yourself a favor do not be anything like Maize he is a little bastard who needs to get his teeth kicked in so If you do not want the same fate I suggest you do not even show up to ring. *Khaos then has a seat in his throne like recliner and puts hand on his face like he is disgusted with something.* KHAOS: What is with these kids today? They think by saying a few words they can walk in here and start making boasts and retarded wishes? This Maize kid really needs to be put down in bad way PERIOD! Heh I bet you right after watching this he will start blabbing again and speaking of blabbing I get to go against the babbleweight champion of the world The Freak so I finally get a chance to shut that big mouth of yours for good I knew there would be a time when we would meet and this Friday I am going to show you what happens to those who underestimate me! Freak this Friday I am going to show you and that little vegetable Maize a thing or two about knowing your place here in EPW below my feet, childish boasts like this need to be punished! So Freak, Maize and Collins you think you got the balls so show your puny asses out in the ring this Friday I hope so becuase do not make me go and hunt your sorry asses down in the arena then drag you guys out to the ring just show up and get your asses kicked because I am not in a good mood after what happened at Soul Survivor and I am ready show all of you The Gateway to Anarchy once you get past those gates there's NO.....WAY......OUT! *The scene fades to black on Khaos's angered look.*
