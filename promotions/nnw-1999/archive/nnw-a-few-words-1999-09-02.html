A Few Words on NNW

2 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys, You may remember me from about two months ago when I was trying to get a new e-fed, NNW, up and running. Then again you may not.
Anyway after alot of hard work and newly grown grey hairs NNW is running and running very well. We have around 15 active members and have a rumour board which gets around 7 messages a day.
The fed is co-run by myself and two other bookers but soon due to increasing pressure from school I'm handed my third over to another friend.
We just staged our first pay per view supercard and it should have just been posted below (SuperBash '99). At the pay per view we had our first world champion crowned.
The fed is completely handwritten and since the 18th July we have had 3mb worth of cards written, mostly by myself. Hence the grey hairs on my young head.
And, most importantly, we have an easy to remember website address. www.nnw.cwc.net If you're interested in joining the fed then fill out an app from the page and send it to [detail withheld] Feel free to leave any comments on the rumour forum or maybe even post a rumour. Thanks for listening to me and being a very helpful resource when I was at my wits end trying to get my dream federation started.
Thanks Michael NNW creator, co-booker and card writer.
