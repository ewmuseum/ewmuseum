Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

OWO staged itself as the “Old World Order,” run by a single operator under the on-air authority-figure name the Inquisitor, with a rival authority figure, the CEO, holding standing power to overrule him. Its two surviving broadcasts run 28 and 30 December 1997: Sunday Night Six-Pack, prerecorded at its own television studios in Manhattan under Hell's Angels security hired for the night, and Tuesday Night Titans, live from the Target Center in Minneapolis.

Six-Pack opened on a masked stable revealing itself as the Storm Stable, feuding for seats at ringside with the promotion's own Darkside's Domain faction, and closed on the villainous stable ICE storming the ring: Icicle attacked the Inquisitor over a stalled match booking, and Icicle's allies piledrove him through a block of ice and a stack of bricks before the Hell's Angels beat all three ICE members in the parking garage. Meanstreak won the World Heavyweight Title for the second time on the same card, cracking the Crow over the head with the ring bell in a First Blood match.

Tuesday Night Titans ran fifteen matches, opening on a wrestler being counted out cold and declared dead by the promotion's own paramedic to a cheering crowd. The Wrecker won the Ironman Title from Rigomortus across a full hour of falls; the Xtreme Triangle Title was renamed the World Television Title mid-show by the promotion's own commissioner, handed to the winner of a newly announced thirty-two-man tournament; and Terminator beat Raven by countout in the main event without taking the Xtreme Heavyweight Title, since a countout cannot change hands.

No document past 30 December 1997 survives to say what became of this fed's own roster or titles after that card.
