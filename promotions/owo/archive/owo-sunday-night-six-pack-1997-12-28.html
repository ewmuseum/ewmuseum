OWO Sunday Night Six-Pack

28 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday Night Six-Pack - Prerecorded at the OWO Television Studios in Manhattan New York (12/28/97) Fireworks blast off! Merv Albert: "Welcome wrestling fans to the highest rated wrestling show on the weekends, Sunday Night Six-Pack! Here's my broadcast colleagues Jimmy Sick and the Stump!" Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "Thanks Merv. We have a big night of action lined up tonight plus a special interview with Icicle at the end of tonight's show. The OWO President The Inquisitor has hired the Hell's Angels to do security. There's at least a hundred bikers here tonight. And these guys are rough customers too." Jimmy Sick: "The OWO prez has a long history with the Hell's Angels. They've always been there when The Inquisitor needed a favor. Oh no!" The lights go off in the arena as fans can be heard screaming and yelling. Then the Song "Tubthumping" by Chumbawumba comes over all the loudspeakers. Then two large red and white fireworks go off and blast in the arena! Now smoke fills the top of the arena and a gloomy red light fills the ring. The song is still heard in the background. After a couple of more minutes four masked men come through the walk way and make there way to the ring. When each of them get in the ring they all go to a separate corner, then raise there hands up in the air, and slam them down, while more fireworks go off in the air. Then they all line up in the center of the ring, and sit down on the OWO logo over the middle of the ring. The Mystery Man #1 comes over to the ring side area, and takes a microphone from OWO announcer Paul Miles. Mystery Man #1: "OWO, our presence has been felt for the past week or so now.. Many of you may know who we are, some of you know why we are here, and then there is the other ones who don't have a clue about our extraordinary powers, those who cross are path shall be sentenced to a penalty, those who don't shall be left alone till further notice. It has been a while sense the UXW league closed down now, but if I am not mistaking there was a man there who held the Triangle title for the longest imaginable time considering how hard it is too hold on to it. That man is standing right here in the center of this ring, that man is me. Now some of you where not in the UXW or you just didn't pay any attention, so before I reveal myself to everyone here is some of my past to present history. I am a two time XWL X-treme champion, I am a two time TWD IC champion, Me and my flock here also won the TWD Stable titles and Triple Tag Team titles two times, I just held the RWL IC title twice, ! I also held the RWL tournament title twice, I was the first ever NWOWO IC champion, first person chosen into the NWOWO hall of fame, And I am the Current eIWL eXtreme champion. I am the one and only TWISTER!" Twister takes his mask off and walks over to the turnbuckle, he goes to the top rope and he raises his fist, he brings them slamming back down has another firework takes off and makes a huge bang. Then Twister just sits up on the rope. He signals his hands up and the lights go out. Then a bunch of fireworks take off and the lights come back on. In the center of the ring are all the mystery men with their masks off...they are THE STORM STABLE! Storm Master: "OWO, now many of you will remember the greatness we had over the UXW. That greatness is to carry over to the OWO with us back, and we are better then ever. On behalf of Twister, and the Action-X-Treme Team I would like to consider our selves part of the greatest league on earth, all because of us, Revolution X and my main man Johnny Sick!" The lights go back out and The Storm Stable can be seen headed to where the Darkside's Domain usually sits. After some more fireworks explode the lights come back on and The Storm Stable is sitting in the Darkside's Domain's seats. Merv Albert: "Wow! I can't believe it! The Storm Master has returned with Twister." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "I've heard of Twister's reputation and I've seen him wrestle on tape. This guy is talented." Jimmy Sick: "The Storm Master is a bum! He's an out of work TV weatherman who could not get a job so he went into pro wrestling instead. The Storm Master and his cronies are pathetic losers. I cost his wrestlers plenty of matches when I was a referee in the UXW. Sure they used to be good friends with my derelict brother when they where in the Bomb Squad but I don't give a rat's ass about Twister or the Storm Stable!" Merv Albert: "Let's get to our first match!" ------------------------------------------------------------------------ First Blood Match: Meanstreak beat The Crow in 0:06:43. Meanstreak won the OWO World Heavyweight Title Merv Albert: "Wow! Meanstreak wins the OWO World Heavyweight Title for the second time. He's been a great champion since arriving to the Old World Order." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "Meanie won this match after he cracked the Crow over the head with the ring bell. Blood started flowing from his head and it was all over!" Jimmy Sick: "Meanstreak doesn't deserve to wear that belt! He's not a great champion. Sure, he's held 4 major titles since arriving but he's also lost three of those titles. We'll see how long he can hold onto this one. And it won't be very long if I can help it!" Merv Albert: "I understand that Meanstreak will be teaming with Twister now that Hot Body Chipp N. Dale is on leave. I don't think this will hurt Revolution X at all." Jimmy Sick: "Oh yeah! The two biggest doofus losers in the OWO teaming up together in Revolution X? I can't wait to see them wrestle!" Merv Albert: "Hold on! Here comes the new streamlined version of the Darkside's Domain to the ring. And Moon Mulligan is coming down too!" Fireworks blast off as the Darkside's Domain come down the ring. Assassin: "First Moon I would Like to apology for calling you Max but the Darkside was making fun of you and I realized the Darkside is headed in the wrong direction Moon so we are turning it around and if anyone in the OWO has a problem to bad but we will claim all the gold in the OWO tell them Raven." Raven is holding the OWO Xtreme Heavyweight Title over his shoulders. Raven: "OWO we will rain terror on you we will dominate all comers and takers it has come to be said so it shall come to past. 'Quote The Raven Never More!'" Raven, Assassin, Sting and manager Perry Saturn head for their usual front row seats but Twister, Thunder Storm, Avalanche and The Storm Master are already sitting there. They start to argue with each other. Merv Albert: "This looks bad!" As the argument over the front row seats ensues, the Hell's Angels come out to separate The Darkside's Domain from The Storm Stable before a brawl breaks out. The Hell's Angels lead The Darkside's Domain away from the ring area as the fans pelt them with debris. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ LynchMob and Zombie beat The Dominators when LynchMob made Ryan O'Quinn submit to an armbar submission in 0:16:00. Merv Albert: "The new tag team of Zombie and OWO Xtreme Triangle Champ LynchMob get the victory over the up and coming team of the Dominators." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "The Wrecker is obviously not healthy. I understand he's been nursing some minor injuries lately. Maybe this contributed to the loss." Jimmy Sick: "Wrong! LynchMob and Zombie just got lucky. The Dominators will be the next tag team champs. I guarantee it." ------------------------------------------------------------------------ BaseBrawl Match: Terminator made Virus submit to a figure-four sleeper in 0:07:47. Merv Albert: "Virus beat The Atomic Punk last Tuesday but he could not beat the top ranked Terminator here tonight. Let's get to the replay of this match." Terminator runs into the ropes. Genny trips Terminator. Danny Davis warns Genny. Terminator nails him with a bodyslam. Terminator takes him down with a spinebuster slam. There is no referee to count. Terminator gets back up. Terminator runs into the ropes. Virus misses with a clothesline. Terminator takes him down with a flying forearm. There is no referee to count. Terminator gets back up. Danny Davis is back on the job. Terminator climbs to the top turnbuckle, but Virus nails him in the stomach. Terminator falls onto the top turnbuckle. Terminator falls back into the ring. Virus goes for a ropeburn, but Terminator blocks it. Terminator takes him down with a flying forearm. Danny Davis counts: One, two, shoulder up. Terminator complains about a slow count. Terminator executes a figure-four sleeper. Virus submits immediately. The winner is Terminator. Time of match: 0:07:47. Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "Manager Genny tried to get involved with this match but veteran ref Danny Davis was right there!" Jimmy Sick: "I was hoping Virus would beat this loser they call the Terminator but Danny Davis does not accept bribes. Maybe next time!" ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Handicap-Match: The Greg Foundation beat The Outsiders and Curt Hennig when Tommy Richards made Curt Hennig submit to the Say the Words in 0:17:21. Merv Albert: "Kevin Nash got a big win against Mr. Insanity at the pay-per-view but the Greg Foundation was too technically sound tonight!" Jimmy Sick: "The Greg Foundation are the OWO Xtreme Tag Team champs and they showed why tonight!" Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "Kevin Nash is the glue that holds the Wolf Pack together but Curt Hennig looked awful in this one!" Jimmy Sick: "You're right Ray. Mr. Perfect was not perfect tonight. Let's see the replay on this one." Curt Hennig and Kevin Nash whip The Icon Tommy Richards into the ropes. They hit The Icon Tommy Richards with a double clothesline. Kevin Nash leaves the ring. Curt Hennig nails him with a full nelson. The Icon Tommy Richards reaches the ropes after being trapped for 5 seconds. Curt Hennig nails him with a cobra clutch. The Icon Tommy Richards breaks the hold after 14 seconds. The Icon Tommy Richards goes for a Northern Lights suplex, but Curt Hennig blocks it. Curt Hennig goes for a back heel kick, but The Icon Tommy Richards blocks it. The Icon Tommy Richards executes a sleeperhold. Kevin Nash makes the save. The Icon Tommy Richards whips Curt Hennig into the turnbuckle. The Icon Tommy Richards charges into the corner. The Icon Tommy Richards catches him in a Northern Lights suplex. The Icon Tommy Richards executes the Say the Words. Curt Hennig submits after 9 seconds. The winners are The Greg Foundation. Time of match: 0:17:21 Merv Albert: "Let's get to our next match!" ------------------------------------------------------------------------ "Iceman" Josh Cagle pinned Edward Glide after a fisherman suplex in 0:05:26. Merv Albert: "The Iceman has dominate the series with long time rival Edward Glide since they both signed with the OWO a few weeks ago." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "Cagle usually has the odds stacked against him but he always seems to come out on top. This should give him some momentum going into the TNT when he wrestles for OWO Xtreme Triangle Title." Jimmy Sick: "Edward Glide has been getting the shaft since his arrival in the OWO. This guy is a great talent but Cagle keeps cheating to win these matches. It's only a matter of time before Glide and his associates The Dominators get their hands around Cagle's scrawny neck and beat the tar out of him!" Merv Albert: "The OWO technical staff is putting up the Thunderdome for the next match between Assassin, Raven and Sting vs Meanstreak, Terminator and Tommy Marvelous. This should be a good one!" ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Special Six-Man-Tag No-DQ-Match in the Thunderdome: Darkside's Destruction and Sting beat Meanstreak, Terminator and Tommy Boy Toy Marvelous when Assassin pinned Meanstreak after a tiger suplex in 0:26:19. Merv Albert: "Assassin has been in a bit of slump but comes out of it in a big way by pinning reigning OWO World Heavyweight Champ Meanstreak!" Jimmy Sick: "I'm a big fan of Assassin. This guy is so devious and nefarious. He was responsible for bringing in Raven and Sting. I think he's the real leader of the Darkside's Domain, not Raven." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "I think...wait a minute! It's Icicle's music courtesy of the Doors!" "5 to 1" by The Doors plays as Icicle slowly walks to the ring. Moon Mulligan hands him a mic, and Icicle stands with a pissed off look on his face. Icicle: "Well, Moon, it seems to me that there is a certain 'Prick Stable' member who decided to take a little leave of absence. Am I right?" Moon Mulligan: "If it is Hot Body Chipp N. Dale you are referring to yes you are correct sir!" Icicle: "He knew I was going to go after him, and so in fear that I'd break every little bone in his flabby body he ran away, and hid so he wouldn't get knocked around by me. Now I want to have a little talk with The Inquisitor. So he better get his 12th century ass out here so we can discuss business." The Inquisitor walks down with a frown on his face. Moon Mulligan hands him the microphone. The Inquisitor: "Icicle what is it that you want to talk to me about? Hot Body Chipp N. Dale is injured so you need to walk back to the locker room, and wait till he has recovered. I will then sign a match between you, and him." Icicle: "So what your saying is that me, and Hot Body aren't gonna fight tonight?" The Inquisitor: "Com'on, Hot Body is injured. He can't wrestle tonight." Icicle: "You know that pisses me the 'bleep' off! Now you've got exactly 30 seconds to find Hot Body, and send him out here for a wrestling match or something really bad is gonna happen." The Inquisitor: "I'm not gonna risk the lawsuit from Hot Body. This would put a man's life on the line, and I believe that my time is up so if you'll excuse me." The Inquisitor walks away. Then Icicle nails him from behind with a clothesline! Icicle is enraged he tears the security railing off it's hinges, and slams it onto the back of The Inquisitor! That hurt! Icicle reaches into his tights, and pulls out a pair of brass knuckles. All of the sudden former OWO Superstars Sub-Zero, and Ten Degrees Below Zero run in through the crowd. Sub-Zero is carrying a block of ICE, and Ten Degrees Below Zero is carrying 4 bricks. They set the bricks, and Ice block up, and Icicle grabs The Inquisitor, and piledrives him through the block of ICE and onto the 4 bricks. Wow! The Hell's Angels are coming out of every exit in mass numbers! Icicle throws down a smoke bomb, and when everything clears. The lights go out, and when they come back on the members of ICE are gone. Merv Albert: "Holy Moly! Icicle and former OWO Tag Team Superstars 'Frozen Solid' made a cameo appearance! The fans really went wild when the ICE stable appeared! The fans even cheered the attack on The Inquisitor! What's the world coming too?" Jimmy Sick: "I'm not so sure if attacking The Inquisitor was a good idea or not? Wait a second Merv! OWO producer Ron Reinhardt has some action in the parking garage!" OWO cameraman Danny Santos is filming in the parking lot. The Hell's Angels are in a big circle. In the middle is Icicle and former OWO Superstars Sub-Zero and Ten Degrees Below Zero. The Hell's Angels are beating the three wrestlers from Iceland to a pulp! Merv Albert: "The Hell's Angels must have caught the ICE stable members in the garage!" Also present are original Old World Order stable members Minister Thomas Paine and White Trash. These two are leading the brutal attack on the members of the ICE stable. This is awful! As the Hell's Angels surround the members of the ICE stable White Trash and Thomas Paine go to work. Paine nails Sub-Zero and Ten Degrees Below Zero with a double DDT onto the concrete! White Trash does his finisher "The Trailer Court Trashing" onto the hood of ICE's limousine! Merv Albert: "We're out of time fans. OWO paramedic Ted Welby is looking at the OWO prez right now. He looks like he's going to be okay..." The picture fades as the satellite uplink goes down...
