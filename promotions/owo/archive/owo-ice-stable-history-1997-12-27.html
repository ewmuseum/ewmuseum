ICE

27 December 1997

So you wanna know what the hell the ICE Stable is. Here is everything you'll need to know about the whole stable. Before    The    Stable In the days before ICE was formed they were all just 7 brothers. The only non-family member of ICE is Cold Shoulder. It started in 1963 when the first of the brothers was born Known only as Winterblast now, then only 2 years later came Sub-Zero, and in 1968 Ice Cold Ninja 2 years later came The Frozen One, and a year later ICE Demon. Then in 1975 Icicle, and Ten Degrees Below Zero were born. The twins, but at first glance you would never think so because of the enoromous difference in size. The older brothers decided to take the younger ones away from their abusive father so they would not have to live through the hell that they did. Slowly each brother came into the wrestling business starting with Sub-Zero back in 1991 then came Winterblast, and Arctic Blast. They formed the stable known now only as ICE. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ The    Peak    of    the    Stable In 1993 the ICE stable brought in the legendary Frozen One, and the Martial Arts master Ice Cold Ninja. The stable soone began to dominate all competition. With The Frozen One, and Ice Cold Ninja annihilating the singles competition and Sub-Zero, and Arctic Blast dominating tags, and the masterful Winterblast as the brains behind the brawn. Soon enough they were in 5 different organizations in which they held 16 titlesthey were kings of the mountain, and above all else they had the only thing that matters, but like the old saying goes nothing lasts forever ------------------------------------------------------------------------ The Downfall and Ressurection Soon enough as new younger and bigger men arose the domination of the ICE stable came to an abrupt halt. Ice Cold Ninja, and The Frozen One went into retirement. They found the gap and clogged it up with something so powerful, so strong it cannot be described. They filled that gap with a 7' 4" 400 lb MONSTER by the name of Icicle, and then the betrayal. Winterblast went behind the stable's back and so Icicle confronted him and ended his career with ICE. They found another Big Man in ICE DEMON. Then they developed a feud with the EHA, but then they left, and came face to face with Winterblast again. After this ICE DEMON was killed in a car accident so ICE brought up their very small brother Ten Degrees Below Zero. Now another member is going into retirement, and a fighter who may not be ready will have to take his place. ICE has strengthened by joining up with THe Hell Warriors, and of course they have recently won the o.W.o. Stables titles. Unfortunately a tragic car accident seemed to end their premature OWO careers with Icicle being the only remaining member. Then the ICE Stable made an unbeleivable return at PFW Wrestlemas '97 where they showed how bad they are. The ICE Stable has also won multiple most hated awards with feds and after you get to know them you'll hat them to.
