OWO Tuesday Night Titans

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tuesday Night Titans - Live from the Target Center in Minneapolis, MN (12/30/97) ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Special Dark Match: Terminator defeated Blizzard by the Termination in 0:00:07. After the match was over every wrestler in the OWO came down and started to kick the unconscious Blizzard. They took turns beating the Hell out of him. Merv Albert: "That was some match but Blizzard looks hurt. Here comes the OWO paramedics to check him out." Jimmy Sick: "What a loser." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "This guy Blizzard is a sorry excuse for a human being." Merv Albert: "We're getting word from OWO producer Ron Reinhardt about Blizzard. OWO paramedic Ted Welby as just pronounced Blizzard dead. He's not going to be back." The OWO fans start to cheer as they take the dead Blizzard out on a stretcher. The OWO wrestlers spit on him as they take his corpse out. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Fireworks blast off! Merv Albert: "Welcome wrestling fans to the greatest show on earth! Tuesday Night Titans! We have fifteen great matches to bring to you tonight. Oh no! Here comes the Greg Foundation!" DeGeneration X's theme music plays as the OWO Xtreme Tag Team Champions The Greg Foundation make their way to the ring. The trio chases Moon Mulligan out of the ring and Paul Miles hands "The ICON" Tommy Richards a microphone as Jimmy Sick makes his way down to the ring. Tommy "The Icon" Richards: "Well first I'd like to congratulate Icicle on stepping up to the plate, and showing the prez that this fed isn't worth anything without the wrestlers, and that he better get it straight in his mind that he's got one very, very, very, large X on his 12th century ass. Next order of business. Cagle you beat Eddie G, but lord knows you can't beat me so you can come out here, and walk the walk, but you sure can't talk the talk. Now how about me, and you right here next week." Greg Allocca: "Next thing I'd also like to add that thanks to Jimmy Sick the Greg Foundation has been signed to 3 year contract extensions. Since our original contract ran out on Sunday Jimmy here convinced the ratings risers to stick around, and now we'd like to bring up the man himself." Jimmy Sick walks up to the ring, and Tommy Richards shakes his hand. Mr. Insanity: "Your a great man Jimmy, and that's why we bought you a present. It's a little late for Christmas, but we thought it was in the best interests." Mr. Insanity hands Jimmy Sick a box. He opens it inside is a dart set with the bullseye being a photo of Johnny Sick, and a punching bag with Johnny Sick's portrait on it. Jimmy Sick: "You shouldn't have guys. This is the best Christmas present I've ever gotten." The OWO President The Inquisitor walks out. His head is still bandaged from the attack by Frozen Solid this past Sunday. He orders The Greg Foundation, and Jimmy Sick to leave the ring. Jimmy Sick leaves, but The Greg Foundation has different plans. Greg Allocca hits The Inquisitor with a Superkick! The Inquisitor counters it with an Crucifix submission hold. Now Jimmy Sick is back in the ring. He pulls a can of mace out of his pocket. He starts to spray mace in the face of manager Greg Allocca! Here comes The Inquisitor's former tag team partner from the GIW The Minister Thomas Paine! Paine and The Inquisitor are battling the Greg Foundation while announcer Jimmy Sick cheapshots Greg Allocca! Wow! Merv Albert: "This is wild! Hold on Ron Reinhardt has some action in the back!" Danny Santos is filming in the OWO service entrance. A large group of Hell's Angel's bikers are blocking the entrance. The former OWO superstars Sub-Zero and Ten Degrees Below Zero and trying to bum rush the door but aren't having any luck. Merv Albert: "Woah! It looks like the Hell's Angel's are still on duty!" As Jimmy Sick nails Greg Allocca with another limp wristed punch The Storm Master runs down. He has his Lightning Rod and nails Jimmy Sick in the back of the head! Jimmy Sick is down and unconscious. Now the OWO Commissioner Tony Tullas is coming down with Monty Gates. They flood the ring and separate the brawlers. The Storm Master makes his way to the announcing booth. Merv Albert: "Jimmy Sick is out cold, The Storm Master is coming the announcing booth, The Greg Foundation attacked the OWO President, Frozen Solid tried to break into the building...what else can happen tonight?" Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "Well Merv, I think the Greg Foundation wanted to 'divide and conquer' by attempting to sway Jimmy Sick against the OWO President with luxurious gifts. I don't think Jimmy is going to bite the hand that feeds him though. It's The Inquisitor who gave him a chance to get revenge on his brother. It's also The Inquisitor who pays him a hefty paycheck every week, not the Greg Foundation." The Storm Master sits in Jimmy Sick's empty seat as the OWO paramedics attend to Jimmy Sick. The Storm Master: "I'm taking that little punk Jimmy Sick's place in the announcing booth tonight. How did you like the way It hit him with my trusty lightning rod?" Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "To be honest I thought it was a cheapshot, but on the other hand maybe he had it coming to him after criticizing you and the Storm Stable this past Sunday night." Merv Albert: "Let's get to our first match! Wait a second! Here comes the Darkside's Domain!" Assassin, Raven and manager Perry Saturn make their way down to the front row. They beat up some teenage fans and take their usual front row seats. Raven grabs the microphone from OWO announcer Paul Miles. Raven: "Storm Stable, on Sunday you took our seats and the Hell's Angels wouldn't let us kick your asses but tonight is a different story. So Storm Stable, if you have the guts to come out right now we don't have to wait until the next Tuesday. We can do this match right now you bunch of punks. It has come to be so it shall come to pass 'Quote the Raven nevermore.'" Merv Albert: "Well Mr. Storm, who do you react to this?" The Storm Master gets up from the announcing booth and goes to the ringside area. He starts to yell at Assassin and Raven as they sit there mocking him. The fans are cheering as Twister, Avalanche and Thunder Storm come down the steel entrance ramp to the ringside area but the OWO security staff is right behind them! Merv Albert: "Well it looks like the OWO security staff is not going to let anything happen tonight. I guess will have to wait till next Tuesday to see who wins that big 6 man tag match between The Storm Stable and The Darkside's Domain." The Storm Master comes back to the announcing booth as the OWO security staff leads Twister, Avalanche and Thunder Storm back to the lockeroom area. The Storm Master: "Those punks better watch it!. They're lucky security came out before a natural disaster occurred!" Merv Albert: "Let's get to the ring!" ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Bed-o-Nails Match: Bloodbath defeated Ryan O'Quinn in 0:39:43. Merv Albert: "Bloodbath continues his winning ways by beating Ryan O'Quinn unconscious and placing him on the bed 'o nails for the victory. The OWO paramedics are carrying O'Quinn out right now." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "This is a big victory for Bloodbath as he marches towards the top ten." The Storm Master: "Bloodbath and the members of the Brotherhood are talented but they will crumble when they feel the force of the Storm Stable upon them. We will come to dominate the Old World Order in short time. Mark my words!" ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Non-Title Match: Post and Squala beat Art of War when Squala pinned Pillage after a dragon suplex in 0:20:38. After the match was over Assassin and Raven jumped over the guard rail and jumped up on the ring apron and stared down the tag team champs as they left the ring after the loss. Merv Albert: "What a win for the newcomers! They beat the current OWO World Tag Team champs in this non-title match! Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "This is a major upset. This will cause some waves in the tag team ranks. Post and Squala will be a force to be reckoned with. It looks like Assassin and Raven still have a grudge against their former stable mates Sack and Pillage. Raven and Assassin gave them the 'evil eye' as they left the ring." The Storm Master: "Sure they beat the Art of War but wait till they face my monsters. The Action-X-Treme Team will put these jobbers in their place if we cross paths." ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Icicle pinned Tiburon with the Glacier in 0:10:28. The Darkside's Domain surrounded the ring and stared down the Superstar from Iceland as he celebrated his victory. Sting jumped up on the ring apron and pointed a bat at Icicle and the lights went out. When they came back on a few seconds later The Darkside's members were gone. Icicle looked like he had seen a ghost! Merv Albert: "I don't know what the Hell that was about? But Icicle gets another victory against an old nemesis and newcomer to the OWO." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "This Tiburon is big and mysterious. At 6' 10" and 350 pounds he could be a force once he hones his skills here in the OWO." The Storm Master: "Yeah but Twister is 7' 1" and weighs almost 400 pounds. He's a monster. He could easily beat Tiburon or Icicle. These guys are losers compared to my stallions. I can't even begin to list how many titles Twister has held...lets see UXW, XWL, TWD...oh we might run out of time. Oh speaking of Twister here he comes now!" A loud noise comes over the loudspeaker. It sounds like a storm. Now it starts to sound like a train overhead. No...that's the sound of a tornado! Fireworks blast off and Twister comes out of the entrance. The fans go crazy! ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Twister pinned Hardcore Harry Hatemonger with the Last Rites in 0:13:16. Merv Albert: "Well that's a new finisher for Twister. As I recall he used to use the 'Wild Water' back in his UXW days." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "I never wrestled Twister but the thing about him is that he is big and extremely agile. He can beat you with quickness as well as strength." The Storm Master: "Very good analysis Stump. I'm sure Twister would have whooped your ass the way that he's gonna whoop every other minimum wage wrestler here in the OWO." ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Submission Match: Action-X-Treme Team beat The Atomic Punk and Stretch Cunningham when Avalanche made The Atomic Punk submit to a sleeperhold in 0:22:47. Merv Albert: "Well, The Storm Master's wrestlers get another victory tonight against fan favorites The Atomic Punk and 'Stretch' Cunningham." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "The Atomic Punk is one rough customer but once Avalanche got him in that sleeperhold it was all over." The Storm Master: "This was such a great match. Let's see the replay of the end of this gem." 21 minutes have gone by. The Atomic Punk is busted wide open. Thunder Storm nails him with a bow-and-arrow submission. Thunder Storm goes for a scorpion deathlock, but The Atomic Punk blocks it. The Atomic Punk reenters the ring. Thunder Storm follows him back in. Thunder Storm brings the baseball bat into the ring. Earl Hebner is back on the job. The Atomic Punk goes for an abdominal stretch, but Thunder Storm counters it with a hiptoss. Thunder Storm tags Avalanche. Thunder Storm nails The Atomic Punk with a legdrop. Avalanche executes a jumping DDT and goes for the pin. Avalanche goes for a Boston crab, but The Atomic Punk blocks it. The Atomic Punk nails him with a knee to the back. The Atomic Punk goes for a power bomb, but Avalanche blocks it. Avalanche goes for a reverse neckbreaker, but The Atomic Punk blocks it. The Atomic Punk goes for a jumping side kick, but Avalanche counters it with a duck-down move. 22 minutes have gone by. The Atomic Punk is bleeding profusely. Avalanche nails him with a sleeperhold. The Atomic Punk submits immediately. The winners are Action-X-Treme Team. Time of match: 0:22:47 The Storm Master: "Wow! I'll never have to work at the Weather Channel again with this kind of talent!" Merv Albert: "Let's get to our next match as the Champ LynchMob puts up his title against the Iceman and Assassin." ------------------------------------------------------------------------ No-DQ-Triangle Match: "Iceman" Josh Cagle defeated Assassin and LynchMob: "Iceman" Josh Cagle pinned Assassin with the IceBreaker in 0:07:44 "Iceman" Josh Cagle won the OWO Xtreme Triangle Title Ryan O' Quinn, Edward Glide and The Wrecker attacked The Iceman as he celebrated his victory. LynchMob got in on the action too. Edward Glide nailed Cagle with a snow shovel. Then LynchMob did his finisher the "Hangman's Drop" on Cagle onto the cement floor. OWO security came out to stop the massacre. Merv Albert: "How about that! 'Iceman' Josh Cagle wins his first title but pays the price after the match is over. LynchMob lost his belt and was not even pinned." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "But that's the same way he won the belt. He didn't pin King Bad Ass to win that title! What's this?" The OWO Commissioner and former OWO Ironman Champ Tony Tullas comes down to the announcing booth. He takes a microphone. Tony Tullas: "I have a major announcement concerning the OWO Xtreme Triangle Title. For now on the Triangle title will be known as the OWO World Television Title. Any wrestler of any rank can challenge for this title. And next Tuesday night we will have a 32 man single elimination tournament. The competitors will consist of non-ranked wrestlers hand picked by the OWO Executive Board. The brackets will be posted at the OWO Website. The winner will face the reigning OWO World Television Champ later in the card. So get ready Cagle." Merv Albert: "Well you heard it first here wrestling fans. The Triangle Title is now the OWO World Television Title. We'll see who wins that tournament next week." ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Loser-Leaves-Town Match: The Icon made Carnage submit to an armbar submission in 0:12:58. Rigomortus "The Icon Killer" came down the ring with a grave digger's shovel. He jumped up on the ring apron to attack The Icon as The Creator distracted the ref. Rigomortus took a swing but The Icon reversed Carnage's hold and Rigomortus hit Carnage instead. The Icon was able to put Carnage in an armbar submission and won the match. Merv Albert: "This is a big upset. The Icon comes in and upsets a top ranked contender here in the OWO." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "Here comes the OWO security staff to escort Carnage out of the building. There handing him a ticket to board an airplane to leave town. He won't even get a chance to shower and change clothes. I feel sorry for whoever has to sit next to this big sweathog on the airplane." The Storm Master: "The Brotherhood is an inferior wrestling stable. Carnage lost this match because The Icon is more talented than Carnage in every way. But the Storm Stable is better than the Icon and Carnage combined. Don't ever forget that!" ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Cage Match: Tommy "Boy Toy" Marvelous defeated "The Icon" Tommy Richards by escaping the cage in 0:50:56. Rigomortus attacked Tommy Richards as he was coming down the steel entrance ramp. Mr. Insanity was right behind to make the save and started brawling with Rigomortus. OWO security came down to separate the two wrestlers as the match began. Merv Albert: "What a match! The Boy Toy gets the win in his hometown. He got quite a pop from the audience. His new manager Naughty Kitty looked great in the skimpy outfit." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "Naughty Kitty was the difference here. Did you see her grab Tommy Richard's leg as he was trying to escape from the cage?" The Storm Master: "Naughty Kitty is hot...What's this!" As Tommy Richards is leaving Assassin and Raven appear in the entrance. Raven surprises Tommy Richards and nails him with a DDT onto the steel entrance ramp! Now Assassin is setting up Richards for a piledriver onto a steel chair! Merv Albert: "This is going to be bad!" "The Icon" Tommy Richards is upside down. Assassin is about to drop him on his head! Merv Albert: "Wait a minute. Manager Greg Allocca went back to get Mr. Insanity and here he comes!" Mr. Insanity comes flying out! He tackles Assassin and knocks Assassin and Tommy Richards to the ground. Now manager Greg Allocca superkicks Raven! Merv Albert: "Here comes Sting!" Sting comes out from the back and does a Scorpion Deathdrop on manager Greg Allocca! This is havoc! A brawl breaks out as OWO security floods the entrance. Merv Albert: "The party is over folks. OWO security is not going to let this thing go any further. Let's get to our next match as they clean this mess up." Omen's music starts as he makes his way to the ring. All of the sudden Sting breaks free of the pack of security guards and heads straight for Omen. Sting attacks Omen as he is entering the ring and the bell sounds! ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Sting pinned Omen with the Scorpion Death Drop in 0:04:11. Merv Albert: "Well Sting got the jump on Omen by attacking him before the bell rang." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "It didn't take too long after that. Sting schooled Omen and then did his patented Deathdrop move and won the match." The Storm Master: "The OWO Sting stinks. I'd like to see the real WCW Sting come here and face this punk!" Merv Albert: "Here comes the Powers of Doom for our next match!" As Sting waits in the ring with his baseball bat his partner Assassin comes out. Sting then turns and points his bat at Raven. Raven stares back at Sting and then crawls out of his front row seat and over the guard rail. He enters the ring and stares straight at Sting. They stare each other down as a rope comes down. Sting attaches himself to the rope and is lifted up to the rafters. Merv Albert: "What's with the Powers of Doom?" Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "I've been informed that the Powers of Doom are no more. Assassin and Raven are in a new tag team known as 'Darkside's Destruction.'" ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Darkside's Destruction beat The End when Raven pinned Vulture after the Darkside's Drop in 0:23:03. Merv Albert: "The Champ got the win for the new tag team of the Darkside's Destruction." The Storm Master: "Darkside's Destruction are going to be jobber team." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "I don't know about that. Raven is already the OWO Xtreme Heavyweight Champ. Who's to stop him and Assassin from winning tag team gold?" ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Iron Man Match: The Wrecker beat Rigomortus 12 falls to 7 : x The Wrecker beat Rigomortus via disqualification in 0:03:01 x Rigomortus beat The Wrecker via a foreign object in 0:7:35 x The Wrecker beat Rigomortus via disqualification in 0:9:23 x Rigomortus beat The Wrecker via a Frankensteiner in 0:10:55 x The Wrecker beat Rigomortus via the Wrecking Ball in 0:13:31 x Rigomortus beat The Wrecker via the Six Feet Under in 0:15:33 x Rigomortus beat The Wrecker via the Six Feet Under in 0:22:24 x The Wrecker beat Rigomortus via the Wrecking Ball in 0:23:38 x Rigomortus beat The Wrecker via a Frankensteiner in 0:24:43 x The Wrecker beat Rigomortus via the Wrecking Ball in 0:27:44 x Rigomortus beat The Wrecker via a foreign object in 0:29:29 x The Wrecker beat Rigomortus via a full nelson in 0:31:40 x Rigomortus beat The Wrecker via a spinebuster slam in 0:32:27 x The Wrecker beat Rigomortus via a roundhouse right in 0:39:15 x The Wrecker beat Rigomortus via a chop in 0:42:12 x The Wrecker beat Rigomortus via the Wrecking Ball in 0:46:40 x The Wrecker beat Rigomortus via the Wrecking Ball in 0:47:57 x The Wrecker beat Rigomortus via the Wrecking Ball in 0:50:22 x The Wrecker beat Rigomortus via the Wrecking Ball in 0:52:23 x time limit expired in 1:00:00 The Wrecker won the OWO Ironman Title Merv Albert: "The Wrecker dominated Rigomotus to win the title." The Storm Master: "The Wrecker is complete idiot! This guy is a pushover. Pillage stopped him in the Ironman Tournament last week. He got lucky tonight." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "It's too bad the dead man had to lose the title so quickly. But the Brotherhood are still the OWO Stable Champs and will take on the Darkside's Domain later in the card. Hopefully Rigomortus and company can retain their titles." ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Death Match: Revolution X beat The Dominators 3 falls to 1 : x The Wrecker beat Meanstreak via the Wrecking Ball in 0:04:35 x Meanstreak beat The Wrecker via a clawhold in 0:17:48 x Twister beat The Wrecker via a flying senton in 0:24:55 x Twister beat The Wrecker via a headscissors submission in 0:28:30 Merv Albert: "Twister filled in for Hot Body Chipp N. Dale and was the difference in this match as he beat the current Ironman Champ for two falls to give Revolution X the win." The Storm Master: "Revolution X has Twister. A new age is upon the OWO." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "Revolution X looked good tonight. These two guys are talented wrestlers." ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Special Six-Man-Tag Match: Enchantment, Carnage and Rigomortus beat Darkside's Destruction and Sting when Enchantment defeated Assassin via the Kiss of Death in 0:25:37. The Brotherhood retained the OWO Stable Titles OWO World Television Champ "Iceman" Josh Cagle came down to the ring and attacked Assassin as The Creator distracted the ref. He nailed Assassin with a chair and quickly left the ring area. Enchantment put the Kiss of Death on Assassin and got the win. Merv Albert: "That was some match. Enchantment is a great athlete. She is agile and deadly at the same time." The Storm Master: "Enchantment is an enticing morsel but she better not try any of her magic on the Storm Stable." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "Ron Reinhardt has given me an update on Jimmy Sick. Teddy Welby has taken him the hospital as a precautionary measure. He'll probably just need a few stitches and an aspirin." ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Texas Death Non-Title-Match: Meanstreak pinned "Iceman" Josh Cagle after a German suplex in 0:08:38. The Iceman attacked Meanstreak as he was making his entrance. The bell sounded and the match began. Ryan 'O Quinn and Edward Glide attacked "Iceman" Josh Cagle with snow shovels during the match. Assassin, Raven and manager Perry Saturn also came down and attacked "Iceman" Josh Cagle with leather straps. Meanstreak also got attacked by Icicle but was able to come back to win the match after security cleared the area. Merv Albert: "It was Champ vs Champ in this non-title match. The OWO World Heavyweight Champ came out on top." The Storm Master: "Meanstreak is the dominant force in the OWO along with Twister and the Storm Stable. Johnny Sick always associated with the best. It's too bad he's going to prison. If you're watching Johnny good luck in your upcoming trial." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "Cagle really didn't have a chance after being attacked by four wrestlers during this match. I'd like to see these two in the Thunderdome." Merv Albert: "Here comes the Terminator for our next match." The Terminator's music starts as the fans cheer this Superstar. The Terminator makes his way down to the ring as fireworks blast off! Raven is still in his seat waiting to enter the ring as Terminator jumps on the ring apron. Merv Albert: "Hold on! What's this?" The Terminator hurls himself off the ring apron and onto Raven, Assassin and manager Perry Saturn. He's taking them all on! Merv Albert: "The Terminator is a lunatic!" Terminator is brawling with the Darkside's members as the match begins. The bell rings as the OWO security staff comes down to keep Assassin and Raven at bay. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Extreme Match: Terminator defeated Raven by countout in 0:09:07. Raven retained the OWO Xtreme Heavyweight Title Merv Albert: "Wow! Terminator beat the tar out of Raven. He put him through a table on the outside of the ring and Raven was out cold. He could not make it back to the ring for the ten count and the ref gave the match to the Terminator. But you can't win the title on a disqualification or countout so Raven is still the champ." The Storm Master: "I've have a lot of respect for the Terminator. I know Johnny Sick praised him right before leaving. It's too bad he did not win the title." Ray "The Stump" Stevens: "That's the way it goes. The Terminator will get another shot this Sunday in s special tag team match. Raven may walk out of that match with both belts. We'll see what happens this Sunday. See you next week wrestling fans!"
