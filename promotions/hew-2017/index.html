A self-declared comeback of a defunct, historic promotion, staged around a one-night Barbwire Gauntlet at the old Hardcore City Arena that this corpus never shows taking place. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The promotion's own comeback vignette, “Return of the Mack Part 1,” posted 8 April 2017, stages two returning figures, Mr. Smooth and Simon, at the disused Hardcore City Arena in Charleston, South Carolina, reminiscing over the venue's own past legends — Triple XXXstasy, Hector Ruiz, Semper Fi, Zero, Eli Flair, and Kevin Riggs — while agreeing between themselves to keep one figure, named only “HIM,” out of the comeback.

A second vignette the next day, “Into the Fray,” introduces a hooded, voice-modified newcomer, Code Name: Reaper, who throws his own name into the comeback's centerpiece event, “The Barbwire Gauntlet,” challenging “legends like Eli Flair, Sean Stevens” and taunting a wrestler named Ice Tre, warning that his own entry would keep anyone else from signing on.

Two wrestler applications round out what survives: Wayne, an easygoing brawler off a small farm in Ontario, and Kirk Rogers, a heel billed as a “failed standup comedian” and “manipulative creep” out of Valley Stream, New York.

No results document survives for the Barbwire Gauntlet or any other show. The last dated document on record, a brief June 2017 reply, notes only that recruitment was “moving slower than we thought.”
