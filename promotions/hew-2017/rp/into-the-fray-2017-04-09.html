Into the Fray

9 April 2017
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stalker — Apr 9, 2017 at 2:31 AM Fire The scene is an obvious burning hot orange, the letters HEW in the background. Location is unknown, the room is dark and ominous except for the hanging banner. A red glow appears in the distance, throwing a hue on to the letters making them truly look brought to life. Footsteps follow that and a cloaked figure appears in front of the camera. As he turns to face it the presence is clear. Code Name: Reaper has entered the fray.
Reaper: [voice is modified] Supposedly this is a place where legends were created. Some would say this was what you would call a ground breaking federation, possibly even historical.
Now they are coming back, for one last night, and with this come back it will feature 'The Barbwire Gauntlet'. There is no other place I would rather be than to step into the HARDCORE CITY ARENA that night and destroy all of the other competitors.
So.... that is what I am going to do. I am throwing my name into that ring, consider this my challenge... my entry.
I will take on all comers, whether they be supposed legends like Eli Flair, Sean Stevens or even pussy ass wanna be's like Ice Tre.
Sorry to do this to you... HEW. Now that I am in, no one else will join. Because there is one thing greater than the fear of losing and that... is the fear of the unknown.
You all have been warned. Code Name: Reaper's eyes glow a sapphire red as he glares into the camera. For a brief moment they turn to a bright fire orange and the camera cuts to black.
