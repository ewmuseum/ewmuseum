Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Wrestling Anarchy Organization opened in May 2000 under a fictional cable network's own backing, its founding handbook staging a kayfabe boardroom hire alongside its own non-kayfabe introduction from the operator behind it. A small executive cast anchored the promotion's early press conferences and broadcasts, and a periodic newsletter carried the roster, rankings and event calendar between shows.

June brought the promotion's first pay-per-view, an eight-part card built around a tournament for its Icon Heavyweight Championship, followed weeks later by a supercard billed as No Remorse I, reposted in full a month after its first broadcast. A power struggle over the company's own presidency ran through the year's cards, staged as an in-ring angle between two of its own on-screen executives.

August brought a self-described re-birth after a quiet stretch, and by September a revised handbook and a fresh recruitment drive kept the roster turning over. From October into November the promotion ran a string of cards under a recurring name, Reckless Abandon, each posted across several parts and drawing new arrivals to a roster that never stopped recruiting.

The last surviving card ran the day after Thanksgiving 2000, alongside a newsletter column still soliciting talent. No closing notice survives, and how the promotion's own story ended isn't in the record.
