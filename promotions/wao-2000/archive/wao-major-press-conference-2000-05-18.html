WAO — Press Conference

Major Press Conference

18 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens inside a conference room of the Los Angeles Hilton. The room features a long black table featured with WAO executives featuring owner Trevor
Fleming, President Richard Perry, and other executives. Some future WAO broadcast personalities such as Gordon Nicks, Wesley Wade, and Stevie C are sitting at the prestegious table as well. The mass media are stirring around the newest press conference like buzzards on their prey. WAO commentator Gordon
Nicks climbs out of his chair and heads towards the podium in which the WAO logo is hanging upon.]
[Gordon Nicks appears to be in his mid thirties, short brown hair, goatee. He is wearing a three-piece Armani suit. Nicks tests the microphone and begins to speak.]
GN: Ladies and gentlemen ... welcome to this pressconference we have ordered to occur on this wonderful evening. I'm Gordon Nicks, the Master of
Ceremonies for the evening. I would like to welcome GEN-X TV chairman ... and the owner of the Wrestling Anarchy Organization ... Trevor Fleming.
[The media begin to clap and gain shots of Trevor Fleming heads towards the podium. Gordon Nicks walks back to his seat as Fleming begins to speak.]
TF: Hello, and welcome. I have called this pressconference tonight because in the recent weeks ofWAO Headquarter's being open ... we at the WAO have worked some deals out with top stars, pay per view broadcasts, and many other future ventures. But this, without a shadow of a doubt ... has to be the biggest
agreement ever to occur in WAO's short history. I, Trevor Fleming ... in order to make the WAO the _number one_ promotion in all of wrestling, is willing to offer fifty percent of the company to this man. You may know him as a kingpin in this business, ladies and gentlemen I give you ...
_Robert Barnes_. [The crowd is literally in shock. The wrestling fansin attendance begin to scream from the top of their lungs and slowly a ... "LAW" chant becomes profound in the conference room.]
[The camera tilts to the left in which Robert Barnes stands at the stairs leading up to the podium. He is sporting the brightest smile in modern day history. Armed with paperwork, his reading glasses, and a buzz cut. He walks to the podium and smiles, clears his throat, and speaks.]
RB: I bet your all shocked to see me here. Truth is, I wouldn't be here right now, except, I'm a sucker for a good deal like this - that and I can't resist that many zero's.
[Barnes smiles.] RB: LAW was a great franchise, I had fun running it. But let's face it, it's over with. WAO is the new thing. It's over-the-edge, it's hip, and after the PPV, it's gonna be the talk of the town. Trevor has made contacts, I have made contacts, and we are set for a very _Huge_ roster of talented athletes who will put the Aye in Anarchy.
[Barnes pauses, flips through some paper work, and then speaks once more.] RB: The exact date for the show is being worked on by the committee, however, the first show's being entitled WAGING WAR, in which you will witness a Survival Of The Fittest Match, between the top in the business. For those who missed the LAW conference, it's simple rules. A Gates Of Hell cage will be set up, in which a top is placed over the cage, creating a cell like atmosphere. The chosen superstars will wrestle inside and outside of the cage, and the only way to obtain victory is to climb to the top of the cage, climb the fifteen foot high ladder and retrieve the WAO Icon Heavyweight Title.
It's a previlage working with Trevor on this project, as it appears we have a lot in common, and you fans will benefit from it greatly. Thank you.
[Robert Barnes begins to walk his seat as Trevor Fleming begins to head to the podium. As the two meet, they smile and shake hands. As Fleming reaches the podium, Fleming begins to speak.]
TF: That's right ladies and gentlemen ... Waging War, coming to pay per view television ... we promise to bring the most exciting, entertaining, spectacular as the WAO will become the paragon of wrestling. Thank you ladies and gentlemen for attending, and have a good night.
[The media begins to shout out questions as the flash photography runs rampant. Robert Barnes, Richard Perry, Gordon Nicks, Wesley Wade, and Stevie C. begin to walk off of the staging area of the press conference out the door. The camera slowly fades.]
