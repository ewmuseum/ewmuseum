Introduction /=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/ The break is finally over! I'm now settled in High School, and ready to resume the great fed known as the WAO! Some new things have come up, as Virus is no more, and the Icon Heavyweight Championship is vacant! Virus has been revamped to "Reckless Abandon" which will be an exciting, action -packed card pulling out all the stops! So, it's a brand new day here in the WAO and let's get it started! /=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/ II. Headlines /=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/ ROBERT BARNES WILL GIVE A "STATE OF THE WAO" ADDRESS AT RECKLESS ABANDON! Live, this Sunday night on October 8th, on location from the Mirage night club in New York City, Robert Barnes will address the public with the "State of the WAO Address"! We last saw him celebrating in which he won control over the company a month ago! What can we expect this Sunday night? Tune in to find out! /=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/ III. Reckless Abandon Lineup /=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/ CARD: Reckless Abandon DATE: October 8th, 2000 WHERE: Mirage night club -- New York City, NY - MAIN EVENT! TO BE ANNOUNCED! WAO President Robert Barnes has stated that during his State of the WAO Address, he will announce the main event of the evening! What will it be? Who will be involved? The only way to find out is to tune in! - NEW YORK STREET FIGHT! "Kaotic Nightmare" Sid Savage vs. A Mystery Opponent Sid Savage has been tearing up the wrestling independent scene! Now that he's signed under a WAO contract, he has vowed to tear up the competition bit by bit as he's challenged anyone to step in the ring with him this weekend in a New York Street Fight! Who will answer his challenge? - SINGLES ACTION! "Simply Golden" Jeffrey Keenan vs. "Brutal" Brian Floyd This match let alone on paper looks like a doozy! Both wrestlers have a strong following. Both men will meet in the squared circle at the Mirage and it will be a classic! - OPENING CONTEST! "Good Guy" Dawson Walsh vs. "Simply Perfect" TJ Masters "Simply Perfect" TJ Masters is technically still the Wrestling Anarchy Organization United States Champion! Yet the championship committee has officially made an announcement, Masters still holds onto that strap! But he has to meet the "Good Guy" Dawson Walsh this Sunday night! *** ALL THIS AND MORE THIS SUNDAY NIGHT! *** DEADLINES: - Strats are due: Thursday October 5th, 2000 6PM CST. - Flashes are due: Friday October 6th, 2000 11PM CST - In-Rings are due: Saturday October 7th 7PM CST /=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/=/ IV.
