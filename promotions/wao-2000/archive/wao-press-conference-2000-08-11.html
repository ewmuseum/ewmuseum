WAO — Press Conference

Press Conference

11 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The screen opens inside a press room in which the camera is focused upon a black WAO backdrop and a podium. Overheard in the background is the conversation between the press and fans of the pro-wrestling industry. Suddenly marching upon the podium is new WAO President and Part Owner Robert Barnes. He is decked in casual attire, and with a few sheets of paper in his hand. As he reaches the podium, he adjusts and taps the mic as he begins to speak.]
RB: [Clears throat] Ladies and gentlemen of the media, may I have your attention please. I have called this Press-Conference for one reason. To address the current state of the Wrestling Anarchy Organization.
[The crowd begins to settle as photography begins to flash.] RB: Last week at No Remorse, we saw two competitors .... [name withheld] and
King Apples square-off in what fans call the "greatest match in WAO history." Apples came out the winner, giving me this job as not only as a
Part Owner, but as this company's President. [Pause.] RB: Virus was pre-empted last week because me being the President and Part-Owner decided that the WAO needs to refocus and head into a new era of professional wrestling. And I start the refocusing here tonight.
[Pause.] RB: As of August eleventh of the year two-thousand ... Christian "Spotlight" Madison is no longer employed by the Wrestling Anarchy Organization. [The uprising in the crowd begins apparent as they are in shock.] RB: His lack of enthusiasm, tardiness, and bad professionalism will _not_ be tolerated any longer. I hope Mr. Madison achieves success in his future endeavors. Which brings me to my _next_ point ...
_ the state of the Icon Heavyweight Title _ . [The crowd listens on.] RB: I have constructed possibly the most violent, vehement event to ever grace Pay Per View television. That's right. The Icon Heavyweight Championship belt will crown a _rightful_ owner at our Pay-Per-View Final Justice, which will now be held September tenth.
[Pause.] RB: I deem this PPV the "Era of Tribulation." Why you ask? Thirty men will go through literally _hell_ to gain what is the top prize in the company.
[Pause.] RB: Thirty men will compete in two matches total. Ten in Tribulation Match number one, and twenty in Tribulation Match number two. You are asking yourself, "What the hell are these Tribulation Matches?". Tribulation Match number one is a five versus five double elimination match. Meaning, that both team's objective is to eliminate all their opponents. Once that step is completed, that team will battle each other. For example, that could make it a five way, four way, three way, etc., between their own team. This match is sanctioned under "Anything Goes." The sole survivor will head into the "Final
Tribulation" match. [Pause.] RB: Tribulation Match number ... two. A twenty-men Inverted Cage Battle Royal. You're damn right. Twenty men will battle outside the ring and their objective is climb up the cage and into the ring. The first two to do so will battle in a First Blood match. The winner will meet the winner of the previous
Tribulation Match in the "Final Tribulation". [Pause.] RB: Final Tribulation. Two men have went through just a small fraction of the hell to be proclaimed the Icon Heavyweight Champion. Two men will compete in a ... "Hellraiser's Fury Match." The same cage in the second Tribulation Match will be filled with various weapons and will be ... [clears throat]
_set on fire_. [The crowd reacts in shock as the speculation begins as a demonic grin comes upon the face of the new President.]
RB: As I was saying, a weapons-filled cage will be set afire, and the opponent's objective is to burn their opponent. A major body part. Not their foot or some other crap. The winner will be our _new_ and _undisputed_ WAO Icon
Heavyweight Champion of the World! Now, I declare this press conference over, and you all have a nice day.
[Robert Barnes raises both arms in celebration and walks away as the media and press ask questions in which he basically ignores. The flash photography runs rampant as Barnes makes his exit and the camera slowly fades to black.]
[name withheld]: The most underrated talent in e-wrestling today. WAO Co-President Handler of: "Racy" Riley Bradd (AGW)
