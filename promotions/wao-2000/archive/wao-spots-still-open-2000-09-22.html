Spots Still Open!

22 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-- PRESS RELEASE -- (AP) SILICON VALLEY - GEN X-TV ... known for its hot original series such as "Bill and Jerry in San Francisco", "The Life and Times of Silkk E. Smooth" , and hit game show "DUH!" has released their lineup for the new fall season, and one happens to be the reformation of the WAO (Wrestling Anarchy Organization).
The WAO had a previous trial run on the network during the months of June and July to test the waters so to speak, and got very impressive viewing numbers. They featured many stars such as [name withheld], King Apples, "the Extreme
Goddess" Kieran Erotikka, Christian "Spotlight" Madison and many others. Also a
"new star" was on the horizon by the name of "Street Cinema" Meursault Simmons. The WAO, headed by owner of the GEN X network gave full power of the "day to day" operations of the company to Robert Barnes... a promoter who had an amount of success in the Los Angeles wrestling circuit. He helped in promotion a free-TV cable supercard known as "No Remorse", which recieved the highest rating on the network for that month.
Now the question is, who will return this fall? Sources indicate that there will be a new breed of athletes coming into the organization that will be more hungry than their predecessors. There are slight rumors that Meursault Simmons and the powers that be are negotiating some sort of contract for his eventual return.
This fall, the GEN X-TV network has the possibility to be a force in the cable television industry, with the WAO leading the way. More details will be released!
