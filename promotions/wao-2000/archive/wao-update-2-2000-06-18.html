We've just come off a _great_ PPV, and a great way to start off the WAO! We have the first Icon Heavyweight Champion crowned, and many twists and turns occuring along the way! We would like to thank you, the people because without your participation, the card wouldn't have been anything! Also, a side note ... we are now going to the "2+1 / 3+1" rule regarding flashes. What that means you are limited to two flashes a week, and the one optional, yet preffered in-ring. If you are iin the main event of the card, you're allowed three flashes, and one optional yet preferred in-ring interview. Hope this clears up everything. =+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+= II. [Latest News] FIRST EVER ICON HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION CROWNED! Christian "Spotlight" Madison overcame the odds this past Sunday night on the Waging War PPV! He went through two challenging tag matches with his partner "Priceless" Anthony Gold and came out on top in the grueling Survival of the Fittest match! Congrats go out to Madison! WAO PRESIDENT RICHARD PERRY PROMISES PAYBACK THIS SUNDAY! Richard Perry, WAO President, was pushed around and beaten up by part owner of the WAO, Robert Barnes. Richard Perry has vowed revenge as he says it will come to pass this Sunday on the very first Virus! Stay tuned, this could be _BIG_! WHO IS "SAINT" JONAS HARDCASTLE? "Saint" Jonas Hardcastle arrived here in the WAO as a virtual unknown, in which he was revealed the mystery spot in the lottery tag tournament. What's the deal between Hardcastle and "Street Cinema" Meursault Simmons? Only time will tell. WAO'S UNITED STATES CHAMPION TO BE CROWNED AT NO REMORSE! The WAO United States Championship ... which is currently vacant, will be up for grabs at No Remorse. In which an 8-man single elimination tournament will be held starting on the first Virus, concluding with the Semi-Finals and Finals at the No Remorse supercard in Detroit. =+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+=+= III.
