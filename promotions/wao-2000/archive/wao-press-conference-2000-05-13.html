WAO — Press Conference

Press Conference

13 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[From darkness ... the camera slowly fades inside of a conference room filled with news reporters, photographers, and other forms of the media. The camera gets a horizontal shot of a long table in which executives and the higher ups are sitting at. Behind the table, is a huge backdrop which reads: WAO - Wrestling
Anarchy Organization.] [Chatter is heard all over the conference room. A man slowly gets of his chair in the center of the table as he walks toward the podium. On that podium is the WAO logo. The man is Trevor Fleming, owner of GEN-X TV, and chairman of the WAO. Fleming taps the microphone signalling for the crowd to settle down as he begins to speak.]
TF: (clears throat) Good afternoon. For those who don't know, I am Trevor Fleming ... Cheif Executive Officer of
GEN-X Television. A channel bringing you some of the most controversial programs around. We offer syndicated re-runs such as Married with Children, Roseanne, and many others.
We also produce original movies in which the critics call "raunchy". But now, we're bringing another audience to the GEN-X family ... professional wrestling.
[Trevor Fleming smiles as the flash photography begins to go wild. Fleming resumes.] TF: Many of you are asking what promotion will GEN-X TV carry? EMWC ... no. UWF ... no. Both are fine promotions I might add, but it's time for the world of wrestling to gointo a different direction for the millenium. As we bring to you ...
the Wrestling Anarchy Organization. You may call it the WAO. The WAO will hold a weekly broadcast on Sunday nights entitled, "Virus" ... in which the most athletic, charismatic athletes will _all_ want to be a part of. A fresh, new breed of the sport is on the horizon. Please welcome the WAO President, Richard Perry.
[The crowd begins to calmly clap as Richard Perry walks up to the podium. Perry and Fleming shake hands in which Fleming takes his seat at the table and sips on his glass of water.]
RP: Good afternoon ladies and gentlemen. I am Richard Perry, WAO President. My goal here is to proivde the best action and entertainment in the world of professional wrestling, and take the WAO to the very top. We at the WAO are in negotiations with a huge array of talent, all across the globe. I can't mention any at the time due to legal reasons.
One of the reasons this press conference was called to publicly announce the WAO. Another reason is to announce the first event the Wrestling Anarchy Organization will hold. In which on June 18th, two thousand only on pay per view television ... the WAO will present you ... _GENESIS_. It will be a first in the sport in many ways as
Genesis will eminate _live_ from Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles, California! [The crowd begins to stir.] Now all of you are thinking to yourselves, "what's so special about this pay per view?". The answer to that very question is that we will crown ...
[The camera tilts to the right as some executive walks up to Perry. In the executive's hand is a title belt. It has a very large gold plate with diamonds around the edges and in the immediate center is the
WAO calligraphic logo etched in red. The belt also has two side domes with the red WAO calligraphic logo in the center as well.
The executive hands Perry the belt as he walks away.] RP: Our very first ... [raises the belt in the air] _Icon Heavyweight Champion_. [The crowd claps along with the executives.] RP: This title will not be up for grabs in some passe' tournament ... or some half done battle royal ... but it will be on the line in an original, WAO exclusive. The Icon Heavyweight Title will be up for grabs in a "Anarchy "Aslyum". And here are the rules. The twenty men will draw numbers backstage in which the even numbers will battle in cell one, and the odd numbers will compete in cell two. In cell one, the only way you can be eliminated is by pinfall. In cell two, the only way to be eliminated is if you are handcuffed to the wall. The two winners of each cell will battle one on one in cell one for the [raises belt] WAO
Icon Heavyweight Championship. The winner will truly be known as the
_man_ to lead the WAO to the top of wrestling world. But the question is, are _you_ ... the people, ready for what we are to embark on? I say you are.
[The crowd claps as Trevor Fleming walks up to Richard Perry as they shake hands and hug. They both raise each others arms as the flash photography makes the room look like a strobe light. The camera slowly begins to fade.]
