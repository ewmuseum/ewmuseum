Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

All Desert Wrestling is southwest lucha-hardcore-comedy where the production values are the joke themselves — a steel cage made of 4x4s, chicken fencing, and baling wire that collapsed mid-match and made the show, not ruined it. It's a tabletop campaign, run live in 2021 under the World Wide Wrestling system, part of a wider network its players called the NWF. Two sessions survive, served in full below.

Its legends are drawn tight: El Triton the Lucha Mountain (a mountain in a lucha mask who speaks only in screams and carries his baby into the ring), Rory Borealis (a hillbilly from Michigan's Upper Peninsula who got high, got lost, and somehow ended up in the southwest), and El Professor (a thirty-year veteran of the lucha circuit who walks to the ring in a tweed jacket). Its title is the Baja Blast Championship Sponsored by Taco Bell — the sponsorship is part of the legal name, and the surviving record says it in full, every time, without exception. Its own title history opens on a vacancy: Hot Mask held the belt first, then exited the territory suddenly enough that no in-fiction loss was ever staged — the title, and a place in ADW's own lineage, left open for him before El Triton won it back out of the resulting three-way, dragged over the fallen El Professor by a furious The Void, who wanted the pin for himself. El Triton spent the season defending the belt against Gavin Firebrand, the Right Reverend, while The Void and El Professor fought their own rivalry to a Steel Cage draw and back.

Storylines

Two sessions survive, and between them three arcs run start to finish — every beat below linked to the session it was staged on.

El Triton and Gavin Firebrand — a title feud fought over a kidnapped son Written by AI, 2026

Season Recap opens the feud in one scene: with the vacant title just won, the Right Reverend Gavin Firebrand calls El Triton out in the name of the Lord, and the two go back and forth until Firebrand rolls from the ring and steals El Triton's own son off the announcer's table, taking a thrown chair to the leg on his way out. Firebrand returns later that same session with the boy and four of his deacons in tow, offering a trade: the child and the title, on the line together at the Run for the Border PPV. El Triton, wielding the backstage interviewer as a weapon, destroys all four deacons left behind to guard the deal.

Run for the Border pays it off twice over. First, outside the arena, El Triton — mid-song on a donkey, tequila bottle in hand — is confronted by Firebrand and the surviving deacons; the brawl that follows is broken up only when Commissioner Ed Johnson comes out to wrangle it into the ring. Then, in the title match itself, Firebrand hands El Triton's son off to a deacon and fights with his own leg still hurt from the chair shot — landing a sleeper hold and demanding, microphone in El Triton's face, a pledge of service to God. El Triton answers "I pledge my service to Guadalajara!!!!", fights up, and ends it with a backdrop off the top rope, keeping the Baja Blast Championship Sponsored by Taco Bell and winning his son back in the same fall.

El Professor and The Void — a rivalry that never resolves clean Written by AI, 2026

The two are teammates against each other from the start — El Professor's own team wins the season-opening 6-way tag when he pins The Void, and the two meet again minutes later in the three-way for the vacant title on Season Recap: El Professor has the match won when a furious Void, denied his own rematch, runs in from outside and drags El Triton over the fallen Professor for the pin instead. The Void calls Professor out again later the same session, is snubbed as beneath a "Technical Teacher," and attacks him from behind anyway, setting up a Steel Cage match on the spot. The cage — built, per the wing's own placard, from 4x4s, chicken fencing and baling wire — tears itself apart when an El Professor hurricanrana sends both men through a collapsing wall; the match is called a draw, the #1 contendership to the Baja Blast Championship still unsettled.

Run for the Border closes the book on it, and only it: a properly rebuilt cage holds together this time, and it's The Void himself who tears a wall loose in the end — sending both men tumbling into the crowd, where El Professor, first man out through the wreckage of a diner table, claims the win and the #1 contendership. No challenge for the Baja Blast Championship itself has occurred within the surviving record.

Rory Borealis and Dag Boomer — a comeback that doesn't land Written by AI, 2026

Dag "The Yellow Terror" Boomer, a former five-time Baja Blast Champion Sponsored by Taco Bell, challenges Rory Borealis on Season Recap — the record notes only that the match "solidified Rory as a fan favorite," its own result never stated. The rematch opens Run for the Border outright: Rory drives Boomer from the ring into the IHOPaCC's own kitchen, spends part of the match doing dishes and cooking orders while Boomer looks on in confusion, and finishes him clean with the Pasty Plunge for the three count.
