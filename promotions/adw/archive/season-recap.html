Season Recap — crowning a new champion

2021
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. The museum serves it whole because pretending it didn't happen would be a worse dishonesty than being embarrassed by it. See Curation.
What this is A multi-episode summary, not a single card — the promoter's own session notes covering the run of weeks between the Baja Blast Championship going vacant and the Run for the Border PPV that follows it. No exact date survives; 2021 is a year-precision attestation (see the ADW wing), not a date this document itself carries.
A Summary of the current season of All Desert Wrestling. In our first session, The Baja Blast Champion Sponsored by Taco Bell, Hot Mask, had died suddenly of Covid. In order to crown an new champion, a 6 Way Tag Match was held between the team of El Triton (played by Ryan), a mountain in a Lucha Mask who speaks only in screams and carries his baby with him into the ring; Rory Borealis (played by Chris), a hillbilly from the Upper Penninsula of Michigan who got high and lost and somehow ended up in the south west; El Professor (played by Loz) a 30 year veterain of the luchador circuit who walks to the ring in a tweed jacket; and the team of The Void, a 6'4"Brick shithouse; Gail Storm, a technical Wrester; and Ras Trent, a high college kid. Each member of the player's team was able to show off thier wrestling accumen, with El Professor taking the victory for their team by pinning The Void. This led to a three way fight to first pin between El Professor, El Triton, and Rory Borealis. El Professor put on a clinic, handlily besting both El Triton and Rory with his expertice and accumen. Just when El Professor was ready to seal the deal with a pinfall, The Void rushed to the ring! Furious that the man who pinned him would get the Baja Blast Championship sponsored by Taco Bell he landed the Darkness Powerbomb on El Professor. The Void then drug El Triton, who's only move in this match had been to attempt to jump over the ropes and fail, over the veteran for the win. With Championships comes challengers, and El Triton's first was The Right Reverend Gavin Firebrand. The Fire and Brimstone preacher called the Lucha Mountain out, invoking the name of the lord and demanding the Champ face him. El Triton accepted the challenge, facing off against the Soldier of God. The match went back and forth, with Triton eventually gaining the upper hand. When hope seemed lost, Gavin Firebrand rolled out of the ring and stole El Triton's son off of the announcer's table! On his way out, El Triton chucked a chair at Gavin, hitting him hard in the leg, but Gavin was able to escape. Rory Borealis' challenger came in the form of Dag Boomer, former 5 time Baja Blast Champion sponsored by Taco Bell. Their match solidified Rory as a fan favorite and the Pastie as a deadly weapon and tasty treat. In the latest Episode, El Professor was called out by a furious Void. The Void challenged El Professor to fight him "right here, right now". El Professor casually walked out to the ring, appearing to be ready to fight but snubbed the Darkness Eternal, saying the opening match wasn't a fitting place for the Technical Teacher. As El Professor made his way back up the ramp, The Void attacked him saying if this wasn't good enough then they could fight in the Main Event inside of a Steel Cage!!! Betweeen matches, the God Blessed Bastard Gavin Firebrand came to the ring with El Triton's son and four of his deacons. He berated the Lucha Mountain, saying that he had claimed Triton's Son for God. El Triton rushed to the ring carrying the backstage interviewer, whom he weilded as a weapon to take out two of the deacon's on his way to the ring. Gavin, flanked by his remaining two deacons, commented that a match right now wouldn't be fair since his leg was still injuired. But he would be happy to have a match at the Run for the Border PPV next week in Guadalahara! The stakes, Triton's Son and the Baja Blast Championship sponsored by Taco Bell! Triton agreed and Gavin escaped, leaving his four deacons to face the Lucha Mountain! Triton made short work of the four, destroying the deacons and pinning the last one with his boot. In the Main Event, due to budget cuts, the Steel Cage was an amalgamation of 4x4's, Chicken Fencing, and bailing wire. The Technical Teacher faced the Darkness Eternal in the rickety shakey cage/shack. After a number of bumps off the cage by each competetor and the structural integrity of the slap dash structure compromised, BOTH competitors fell out of the cage when the wall collapsed due to an El Professor hurricanrana sending both of them through the cage! The whole structure collapsed, leaving debris in the ring and outside. The match was called a draw and now El Professor and The Void will face eachother at Run for the Border facing off for the #1 Contendership of the Baja Blast Championship sponsored by Taco Bell!
