Run for the Border

2021
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. The museum serves it whole because pretending it didn't happen would be a worse dishonesty than being embarrassed by it. See Curation.
What this is ADW's own PPV, held in Guadalajara, Mexico — the promoter's own session notes, following directly on from the Season Recap. No exact date survives; 2021 is a year-precision attestation (see the ADW wing), not a date this document itself carries.
This week, All Desert Wrestling had their PPV Run for the Border in Guadalajara, Mexico at the International House of Pancakes and Convention Center! The opening match was Rory Borealis vs Dag Boomer! After a pretty rough back and forth, the Terror from the Yoop drove Dag "The Yellow Terror" Boomer from the ring and into the kitchen. The Mexican audience was not wholly on board for the unusual nature of Rory, who spent at least some of the time in the back doing dishes and cooking orders while Dag gazed on in confusion. In the end, the Northerner finished Dag with a Pasty Plunge and covered him for the three count. After the match, the Baja Blast Champion sponsored by Taco Bell, El Triton, was spotted outside of the IHOPaCC riding a donkey and badly singing while playing a pawn shoppe guitar and drinking Poncho Villa Tequila. His lonely song was interrupted by the presence of one Gavin Firebrand and his deacons who had kidnapped his son weeks ago. The Reverend spoke to El Triton about redemption in the eyes of the lord, about joining forces to conquer the ADW, nay conquering the whole NWF! El Triton offered out his hand, grasped Gavin's, and beaned him hard across the face with his bottle of tequila! One of the Deacons was struck by Triton's guitar and the other three would have been laid low by a swinging donkey if Commissioner Ed Johnson hadn't come out to wrangle the fight into the ring. Gavin Firebrand handed the child off to one of his deacons and entered the ring, his leg still injured from their earlier meeting. The Lucha Mountain seized the Reverend's cane and proceeded to beat the holy man across his chest and back, laying the minister low. The fight was back and forth, with Gavin landing the classic thumb in the eye to set up a missile drop kick, only to have El Triton throw Firebrand into the audience! In the end, Gavin Firebrand applied a laying sleeper hold to the Baja Blast Champion sponsored by Taco Bell. He stuck a microphone in Triton's face and demanded the Lucha Mountain say "I pledge my service to God!" El Triton turned to the microphone and shouted, "I Pledge my service to Guadalajara!!!!" With this act of defiance Triton stood up, carrying Gavin Firebrand to the top turnbuckle rope and crushed the Minister of Pain with a huge backdrop! The Referee counted three and that was the end! In the final match of the night (not counting the South West title match that was simmed), The Veteran El Professor faced off against The Void in a Steel Cage match that would determine the Number One Competitor for the Baja Blast Championship sponsored by Taco Bell! The Mexican Crowd fell firmly behind the 30 Year veteran of the Lucha, shouting for the Tenured Teacher of Torture as he made his way into the steel cage. The two competitors each displayed massive prowess, The Void in his sheer strength and brutality, El Professor in his cunning and technical ability. El Professor was able to turn a charge by the End of All Things into a drop toe hold faceplant into the middle turnbuckle! But El Professor couldn't escape from the Void forever. The Void caught El Professor and threw him bodily into the steel chain-link, this time properly put together and solid. In the end, El Professor was able to lay the monster low long enough to make a start up the cage. Just as the Teacher of the Technical's hands touched the top of the cage, the Void finally got to his feet. With a rage borne from some dark place, the Void shook the cage so hard, the walls tore from their moorings and sent the cage wall and El Professor falling into the crowd! El Professor fell through a diner table and ended up covered in the Rooty Tooty Fresh and Fruity, but as the first man out of the cage, El Professor had won!
