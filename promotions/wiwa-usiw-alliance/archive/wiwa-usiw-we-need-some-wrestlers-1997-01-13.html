[USIW] We need some wrestlers!

13 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mr. Bungle ( [detail withheld] ) wrote: : RSPWF: : I'm the president of a fed called USIW, United States Internet : Wrestling. We are currently trying to build the fed into a larger : one. I've recently signed up a few more wrestlers to contracts and I : am still in need of five to ten more good ones. : We are also needing tag teams in the worst way. Our tag champs have : beaten every other team and some of them twice. I hate that it had to : happen that way, but it did. Now they're getting bored with the lack : of competition. So, are there any good teams that might wanna join? Biggest the two FAMOUS matchs have been screw job endings :). : Well, in case you aren't sure, here are some details. We are a role : playing fed. That means ROLE PLAYING, not sending three line insults : and getting mad when you lose. I write all the cards as of now, but I : am looking for good writers who may be interested in helping out. : Here's our weekly schedule: : Monday: Monday Night Dynamite! : Tuesday: "Inside USIW" update show : Wednesday: House show : Thursday: Thursday Night Thunder! : Friday: "Inside USIW" update show : Saturday: House show : Sunday: House show : Interview of the Week : Rankings : So, as you can see, there's somethign happening every day of the week. : I like it because it keeps everyone's interest on the days we don't : have cards. We have Pay Per Views once a month. We may possibly : start having two a month soon as we work on an e-fed alliance with : some other great feds. We are currently attempting to set up an : alliance called WIWA (Worldwide Internet Wrestling Alliance). : It will include: : USIW MCW NIWF : BWF IFW AAWF : So, if anyone is interested in seeing a card or getting an : application, just e-mail me and let me know. Thanks. : -Chris
