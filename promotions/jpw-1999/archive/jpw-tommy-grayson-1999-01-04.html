Tommy Grayson: New Year's Day

4 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[SCENE: Cameras open on Times Square, 42nd street where the New Year's celebration has taken place. It's an extremely cold night and the streets are completely empty as people have gone home to get some sleep...but only to get hangovers in the morning. Several people stumble by, drunk as skunks. The camera closes in on a man sitting on the edge of the sidewalk. It appears to be
"Loose Cannon" Tommy Grayson. He has a quite joyful look on his face and he doesn't seem the cold.]
[Grayson is wearing a thick Nautica jacket, which is zipped up, not allowing us to see what other tops he's wearing under it. He's also wearing a pair of
Levis blue jeans, and a pair of suede Timberland boots. Grayson also has on a red Yankees baseball cap backwards. It has the Yankees logo on it embroidered in white. He also has on a pair of black, leather gloves.]
[Grayson looks around the empty streets and smiles. He looks at the ground for a second, and then looks up at the camera. The smile is somewhat sadistic, but at the same time, we can see that he's quite stable at the moment. Grayson begins to speak.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Ah yes, another new year. 1999. Of course, we know that every year comes with a unique problem. Something for everybody to fear each and every single day of their hopeless, continuous lives. Well, this is the year of
"The Loose Cannon." This is the year of Tommy Grayson. You're looking at your newest fear. Your newest problem. [Chuckles] You can damn well bet that THIS problem ain't gonna be solved anytime soon. Not even by "The Highlight" Dylan
Ryder. Cute name, Dylan. Your nickname fits you...after all, you ARE going to be "The Highlight" come January 7th. You're gonna be in the main event with me.
I'll tell you something, Ryder...you're not gonna be the highlight during the match. You're gonna be the highlight after the match. A highlight of what happens when you mess with a guy like me. I doubt you have any idea of my capabilities in the ring, Ryder. Hell, I'll admit it. I don't even have any idea of your capabilities in the ring. I've never heard of you, Ryder...but that doesn't matter. Why? Because anybody who enters the ring with me always gets one hell of an ass-kicking.
[Grayson gets up from the cold sidewalk and begins to walk slowly across the street. He only looks left and right once or twice, considering the street is empty. The camera follows him along the way.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - I would imagine the odds would be fifty-freakin'-fifty. If you beat me, you get a damn chance at glory. You get to go around yelling, "YO
ADRIAN!! I DID IT!!" Then again, if I beat you...I can move up the rankings here in JPW, and I can aquire the goal of getting the JPW World Heavyweight title. It ain't a simple task...but I know, and everybody knows that I can do it. [Grins] Damn, when they say it's freezing...YOU BETTER BELIEVE IT!! Anyway,
Ryder, I know that you're gonna try your best to beat me...
[Grayson looks like he's gonna try to hold in some laughter.] TOMMY GRAYSON - Whatever, Ryder! Like I said, this is MY year, and I will rule the whole God damn world...I am mother[BLEEP]kin' Lex Luthor! Lobo! Hell, you could say that I am the major supervillain in professional wrestling. You,
Ryder, will be nothing but a PUNY human being to MY SUPERIOR ASS!!
[Grayson begins to imitate a corny, monotone laugh.] TOMMY GRAYSON - AH HA HA. HA HA HA HA. You're going down, Ryder. Plain and simple. [Grayson begins to near the other side. He pauses for a moment and looks at the camera with his cold, brown eyes.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Well...I see that Sensuous Samantha is doing some of her
"dirty deeds" here in JPW as well. Nice to see you again, Sammy. Did I tell you that I've always had the hots for you? Of course I did. You and me, we've got a lot in common. I'm the Devil in some respects...and you're a bitch in a lot of respects. That goes together very well. I'll be your schmuckleberry any day,
Sammy. I think you should meet..."The People's Tongue."
[Grayson sticks his tongue out and makes a slurping noise. He quickly puts it back in his mouth.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Damn, it's cold! Anyway, Sammy...you will eventually experience it. I guarantee that.
[Suddenly, a drunk guy bumps into Grayson.] DRUNK GUY - [Slurred] HEEEYYY!!!! AREN'T YOU TOMMY FRAYSON...HICCUP...?!? TOMMY GRAYSON - Go puke somewhere else, man. I don't need you staining my new
Timberlands! DRUNK GUY - YOU KNOW DAT YOU SUCK, RIGHT? TOMMY GRAYSON - Piece of crap...GET OUTTA HERE! [Grayson shoves the guy off camera. The man simply stumbles away. Grayson looks back at the camera.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - You know, it's peons like that who are ruining the world today! Along with the Republicans, Hanson, N-SYNC, The Backstreet Boys, and rednecks! NOT TO MENTION DRUNKS!! I'm proud to be a Democrat, damn it...and
Dylan Ryder, your ass is gonna get whooped...DEMOCRATICALLY!! You're gonna live...
...LIKE... ...A... ..._SUICIDE_!! [Grayson walks off along the cold streets. Fade to black.] -Handler of: "Loose Cannon" Tommy Grayson [EFW, EWA, ICW, JPW, L.A.W] -Voted as ICW's "Biggest Lunatic" -Voted as ICW's "Most Underrated" -Career dedicated to [name withheld], R.I.P. :) -SEXIEST VOICE ON RSPWF!! (Ask Sensuous Samantha)
