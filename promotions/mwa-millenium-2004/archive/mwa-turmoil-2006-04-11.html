MWA Turmoil, 11 April 2006

11 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The road to Revolution is long and brutal and it stops at MWA KAOS! KAOS, which will be seen live on pay-per-view is a Taboo Tuesday pay-per-view where the FANS choose the matches and the stipulations! However, we still have a few weeks before KAOS and last night was a jam-packed edition of Turmoil to say the least! April 11th, 2006 card: The following is the line-up for MWA Turmoil from Kansas City, Missouri at the Kemper Arena: Triple Threat Match: Jody Franklin v. Kristyn Paige v. Stefani - This match is a Welcome to MWA match as three newcomers all have their first match of their MWA career! Kristyn Paige and Stefani. are two divas just looking to make it big but unfortunatly for them...they have to match up to Jody Franklin...a man who has already had a few words for the MWA crew. So who will walk out the winner in their very first match? I guess you'll just have to tune in! Tag Team Match: Cody Casey/Dustin Williams v. Political Machine (Rennings/Kenner) - The re-formation of one of PWSF's legendary tag-teams S.I.N? It looks like it as Casey ($ik) and Williams (Griffin) take on an EFWO legendary tag-team in the Political Machine! With rumors of tag-belts on the horizon, which team will make a stand and march one step closer? Singles Match: Stomp v. Red Star - PWSF legend takes on last EFWO Heavyweight Champion Red Star. Both men have suffered from less then successful careers in the MWA. Maybe one of them will finally step it up and start turning things around. Tag Team Match: David Bable/Karma v. Trent Hawk/Darren "The Darkness" Galazin - "The Snake" (Bable) and the most Dominant man in the MWA (Karma) take on the dissapointing Trent Hawk and the newcomer Galazin! Singles Match: "Prime Time" Jake Mosley v. Tanya Preston -"Sin-Sational" Steve Sovey special guest referee. - A budding feud has been forming between Sovey and Mosley and last week it took another turn as Sovey pinned Mosley with the "Simply Sin-Sational" thanks to Tanya! This week, thanks to Sovey's request to Showtime, Tanya takes on Mosley in her debut match with 'the Sin-Sational One' as the special guest referee! Tag Team Match: "The Gothic Icon" NoMaD/Keith Smith v. Uprising (Scorpio/RC Tucker) - On one team, 'X' Champion NoMad on the other the number one contender to the 'X' Championship...Scorpio! Oh yea, and Keith Smith and RC Tucker are in the match as well. Singles Match: Kato Osric v. James 'The Python' Reaver -Two tough men fast approaching the top of the MWA. The question is who will continue their ascension and who will suffer a fall from grace! Singles Match: Mason Cell v. Hardcore Assassin - The number one contenders to the MWA World Heavyweight Championship...and they face off this week in a battle of PWSF legends!
