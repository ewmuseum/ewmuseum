MWA Turmoil: Episode 50

14 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Singles Match: The Cool Kid v. "Notorious 462" Neta - Two debuts will open up the 50th episode of MWA Turmoil. TCK, The Cool Kid, will take on PWE legend Neta! Rumor has it the winner of this match will be in for a special something...if they impress that is. Triple Threat Match: Quintessence v. Tanya Preston v. The Fizz - Who will win this match? Oh, the fans that's for sure! Quintessance and The Fizz both make their debut this week and Tanya Preston will have to rebound from being on the recieving end of "The Kiss of Death" from 'the Sin-Sational One'! Which diva will win? Will it be one of the new girls? Or will Ms. Preston show just why she has been the top diva since her arrival! Singles Match: David Bable v. The Messiah - Two stars who have fallen without much grace. Bable seemed to be nearing the top of the MWA just a mere month or two ago. He was in a tight competition for the 'X' Championship and was going toe to toe with 'Sin-Sational' Steve Sovey! But now...he faces the Messiah. A PWSF legend who just can't see to get things right in the MWA! One of them has to turn things around and quickly! The question is...who will it be? Singles Match: Deuce Jackson v. David Jones - Last week Deuce Jackson recieved a message from a mysterious unknown. This week he'll try to send a message to David Jones. Jones, who was fired from the MWA but then re-hired will take on quite the challange in the MWA's beast! Oh, and this one is a tad bit personal. Tag Team Match: Outlawz (Cody Casey/Dustin Williams) v. Hardcore Assassin/James 'The Python' Reaver - The Outlawz, who have been one of MWA's hotter tag teams as of late, will take on two of the most Hardcore individuals around: the former number one contender in Hardcore Assassin and EFWO Hardcore legend James Reaver! Singles Match: Nicky Nightmare v. Skitzo - Last week SKovey got the better half off Team Revolution, this week the opponents are switched up and it will be SKiTZo taking on Nicky Nightmare instead of Chris DeLion! Will Team Revolution's luck change? Or will SKovey keep on rolling? 6-Man Tag Team Match: "Fabulous" Fabian Fairchild/Fred Rennings/"The Gothic Icon" NoMaD v. Kato Osric/T Jay/Triple C - Odds and ends. Fairchild and NoMaD have been dissapointing as of late and Rennings is a question mark as he is no Kenner. The other side however? Three of the hottest acts in the MWA. However, how will Kato and Triple C co-exist after Triple C's words on the last Turmoil!? Singles Match: Christopher DeLion v. "Sin-Sational" Steve Sovey - Sovey has been hot as of late but a win over the number one contender would be huge for DeLion. Rumor has it Sovey has alot on his mind and this will be the match where the MWA is flipped upside down. Non-title No-holds Barred Singles Match: Scorpio v. Kevin Frost (c) = Scorpio called him out. Scorpio called out the MWA's World Heavyweight Champion. Will Scorpio surprise and prove to everyone that he deserves a place in the main event or will Frost shut the young man up and concentrate on Ground Zero. Just one week away from what will sure be a hot pay-per-view, this is the 50th episode of Turmoil and who knows just what will happen!
