Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

No founding statement for the Millenium Wrestling Alliance survives. What its own record opens with instead is a member's own recruiting post of 27 February 2004, pitching the fed to the newsgroup as “a strong fed, 30 members, always looking for more. Top roleplaying talent” and pointing readers to its own site, then at mwa-wrestling.com. Two years on, its address had changed to milleniumwrestlingalliance.com, and its record picks back up mid-run with three build cards for its own weekly Turmoil broadcast, taped out of Kansas City's Kemper Arena and Rosemont's Allstate Arena.

By April 2006 its own roster ran to something like forty names, built around the 'X' Championship, held by NoMaD against a standing challenge from Scorpio, and the MWA World Heavyweight Championship, held by Kevin Frost, who turned back a non-title callout from Scorpio on the fed's 50th episode of Turmoil rather than risk the belt. Its own cards named tag teams like the Outlawz (Cody Casey and Dustin Williams) and Political Machine (Rennings and Kenner), framed feuds between newcomers and its own established acts — “Sin-Sational” Steve Sovey against “Prime Time” Jake Mosley, David Bable against the Messiah — and traded on other promotions' own history in its own billing, introducing arrivals as a “PWSF legend” or an “EFWO Hardcore legend.”

Every card the fed served itself as a build for the coming week's show, never as a results report — no match across the three carries a stated winner in its own surviving text. How the promotion ended isn't in the surviving record; its own last held document previews an unnamed approaching pay-per-view that no other surviving document attests.
