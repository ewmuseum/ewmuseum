One continuous fed under three successive names per its own banner -- OWA (1996-97), WWC: World Wrestling Carnage (1997-2001), UGA (2001 on) -- an annual three-level Carnacage cage structure match its own signature event across two of those eras. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

One promotion under three successive names, per its own banner: "OWA/96-97 - WWC/97-01 - UGA/2001 AND ON!" No document survives from the OWA era itself in the held corpus; its WWC and UGA eras both do.

The WWC World Heavyweight Championship's own served title-history page runs an eighteen-champion chain across the fed's WWC years. "The Franchise" Shane Stud won it a fifth and, in the held corpus, final time at Carnacage III (31 December 1999, billed the last-ever Carnacage), regaining the belt from Disturbed Youth weeks after losing it to him. Under the later UGA name, Chainsaw Charlie won the promotion's own separate UGA Heavyweight Championship in its own "Ultimate Quest" tournament at Summer Breakout 2001.

Carnacage itself was the fed's own signature annual event: a three-level steel-cage structure match, its final instalment run alongside a full undercard. Rancid won it, defeating twenty-nine others across three levels for $1,000,000 and a title shot of his own choosing.

Wings are linked, not merged: this wing's own registry row (founded 1997, a single served debut broadcast) and uga's own row (its own WWC-era title history and Carnacage broadcasts) stay separate records for the same continuous promotion's own two different eras/holdings.
