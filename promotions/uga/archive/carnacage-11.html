On the side of the third level, Youth and Kelly are brawling with Punk and Gionet.] WWC Carnacage 1999 Copyright 2000 EnterprisePro Productions CarnageSports Entertainment Now, here is the full recap of Carnacage 99! The opening match was Red Rose Disaster versus Abnormal Behavior in a Career Match. The match ended when Street Punk ran in and laid out both men, and promised to take the WWC Hardcore Title by the night's end. Mark "The Amateur" Patterson then defeated Grendel in an upset via pinfall in the second match of the evening. Following the match, Grendel was laid out by a mysterious person and covered in black. (We would later find out this man was Revelation). Ace Fireside and Rick Frost, reformed as Attitude Adjustment then made their pay-per-view teaming return to defeat James Ramon and Luke Golden via pinfall, in part three of the event. Following this, Dave Morrison, The Mad Hatter and Fan Boy, three former WWC superstars were shown in attendance. Former WWC wrestler Antonio Taveraz was then shown backstage complaining about the ovation that Dave Morrison recieved. He then swore that if Morrison recieved an ovation like that again, he would nail him straight in his face. In the first of the Triple Hardcore Challenge, Chainsaw Charlie defeated Joe Snow. It was a brutal matchup, but a non-title one as well. Following this, the WWC International Champion Larry Gionet defeated The Jester via disqualification. This came after Jago Ripley and The Beast came in and attacked Gionet, resulting in the DQ. They then turned their attention to the Jester and the three men attacked The Jester until Insanity Incarnate made the save. Insanity Incarnate then battled The Super Destroyers and were able to defeat them via pinfall after some mis-communication between Ripley and the Beast. Following this match, Ripley propositioned Gionet into joining the Trio of Terror. Gionet had a better idea though, and told Ripley that if he and The Beast could defeat Street Punk and Joe Snow at the next tv show, they would be the tag team of the Trio of Terror and Punk and Snow would be out. In the second of the Triple Hardcore Challenge matches, Frantic defeated Street Punk via pinfall. It was an absolutely brutal and bloody battle and Punk recieved help to the back following the matchup. Jon Massey then came out and was about to make a huge announcement when Eric Davidson and Kevin Beck came down to the ring. The two men said that they were the ones who shot Jon and Jon and Storm Richards attacked them both. They then revealed that they weren't the ones to really do it and their lives were threatened if they did not come out and say they did it. Massey then asked them who it was, and with that, JaMeS RioT stormed out from the back shocking the world. Massey left abruptly and disturbed, and RioT attacked Storm Richards and left him laying before leaving the scene. The third and final match in the Triple Hardcore Challenge took place with a four way dance for the WWC Hardcore Title. It was an absolutely brutal and bloody battle and Street Punk defeated all the odds and won the matchup after pinning Joe Snow. He won, becoming the new WWC Hardcore Champion. The next match was a WWC World Television Title match with Mad Dog Mortimer defending against Mighty Ryan Kelly. Frat Boy, originally scheduled to be the referee was announced as not being at the show prior to the match and was also announced to be suspended. However, he came out during the match and helped Kelly win, and counted the three count, apparently giving Kelly the win, and apparently making him the new WWC TV Champion. Kelly was confused while Mortimer was angry at the conclusion of the matchup. Rancid defeated THP in a Hell in a Cell battle that was very brutal! The two men battled all over, but Rancid was able to defeat the big man via pinfall. Shane Stud defeated Disturbed Youth in an epic World Heavyweight Title matchup to once again become the WWC World Heavyweight Champion! Rancid defeated twenty nine other men to win the Carnacage three-level matchup, taking home $1 million and a title shot at any title, any time! Advancing to level two were fourteen wrestlers, including: Rancid, Mighty Ryan Kelly, Shane Stud, Disturbed Youth, Joe Snow, Street Punk, Larry Gionet, THP, Frantic, Chainsaw Charlie, The Jester, Storm Richards, Jago Ripley and Mark "The Amateur" Patterson! Seven of those fourteen advanced to the top and third level, including: Rancid, Chainsaw Charlie, Frantic, Disturbed Youth, Mighty Ryan Kelly, Larry Gionet and Street Punk! Rancid took home the money and the title shot in the finale of the event!
