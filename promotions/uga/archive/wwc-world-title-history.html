WWC World Heavyweight Championship — title history

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Current WWC World Champion "The Franchise" Shane Stud WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP 1ST WWC World Champion: "The Franchise" Shane Stud Shane Stud defeated Doom in Tournament finals. 2ND WWC World Champion: Doom Doom defeated Shane shortly after the tournament. 3RD WWC World Champion: "The Franchise" Shane Stud - 2nd Reign Shane once again defeated Doom at Carnage in a Cage '98. 4TH WWC World Champion: The Hottest Prospect The Hottest Prospect defeated Shane in a Hell in The Cell Match. 5TH WWC World Champion: "Mighty" Ryan Kelly MRK defeated THP in their second match to become champ. 6TH WWC World Champion: The P.I. P.I. defeated MRK in an One Hour Iron Man Match. 7TH WWC World Champion: The Hottest Prospect - 2nd Reign THP defeated PI, MRK and Frat boy in a 4-Way Ladder Match. 8TH WWC World Champion: Rancid Rancid defeated THP to win his first WWC World Title. 9TH WWC World Champion: Mad Dog Mortimer Mortimer defeated Rancid at First Fight 99 on January 19th to win the World Title. 10TH WWC World Champion: Chainsaw Charlie Charlie won the title in a four way dance for the belt after pinning THP! He won the belt on April 1st, ending Mortimer's 72 day reign as champion! 11TH WWC World Champion: Mad Dog Mortimer - 2nd Reign Mortimer won the title at WWC Shattered Dreams in a three way dance after pinning Charlie following "The Bite" Powerbomb twenty two minutes into their match. The win came on May 2! 12TH WWC World Champion: Shane Stud - 3rd Reign Stud won the title for the third time in his career after defeating Mad Dog Mortimer and Larry Gionet in a Three Way Dance, eighteen minutes into the match, at Summer Breakout 99 on June 20! 13TH WWC World Champion: Perry Tanhammer Tanhammer defeated Stud for the title in an epic battle that lasted 43 minutes! It was the main event at Beyond the Limits 99 on August 29th, and saw Perry Tanhammer pick up his first WWC World Title ever! It happened at the Kawasaki Stadium in Japan! 14TH WWC World Champion: The Hottest Prospect - 3rd Reign THP defeated Perry Tanhammer, Shane Stud and Mad Dog Mortimer in an epic battle that saw the return of Rancid at the late September 99 pay-per-view, Carnage 99! It was an epic battle that ended Perry Tanhammer's one month reign! 16TH WWC World Champion: "The Franchise" Shane Stud - 4th Reign Shane Stud defeated THP in an epic 21 minute Hell in a Cell match at Rush Hour on December 6th in Scranton, Wilkes-Barre, PA. It was an incredible battle that gave Shane Stud his 4th WWC World Title reign and ended THP's over two month reign. 17TH WWC World Champion: Disturbed Youth DY shocked the world on a cold friday night, December 17th in Toronto! Not only was it the first ever WWC World Title change in Canada, but it was Youth's first World Title win, and the first ever Three Way Dance with Stud/Kelly/Youth! Kelly pinned Stud thirty one minutes into the match to become the new champion! He shocked the world and ended Stud's eleven day run as champion. 18TH WWC World Champion: "The Franchise" Shane Stud - 5th Reign Stud won his illustrious fifth WWC World Heavyweight Title at Carnacage 99, on December 26th! He defeated his Sex and Violence friend and partner Disturbed Youth in an epic battle to regain his title belt!
