RSWA Match: Ladieees and Geeeeentlemeeen

25 January 1990
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
> 11) Nikita Koloff (bald version) v. Lex Luger --US Heavyweight title > > Nikita caught Luger with a Russian Sickle and it was all over for the > Total Package. That's funny, I thought I saw a different match: Koloff came to the ring with uncle Ivan. Luger came to the ring strutting and posing.. Koloff challenged Luger to a test of strength, and then kicked Luger in the midsection. After punching, kicking, and throwing Luger around,, Koloff threw Luger to the ropes, with the idea of giving him the Russian Sickle. Luger ducked, and went for a shoulder block. It knocked Koloff back, but not down. Luger, intimidated, left the ring. Ivan, with chain in hand, got Luger back into the ring. Koloff continued the assault, with a powerful slam. He tried for a second, when Luger rolled him up in a small package for a two count. Koloff put Luger in a headlock for a few minutes, which was broken when Luger dropped from a standing position, causing Koloff's chin to hit the top of Luger's head. Luger then went to the attack, with punches and chops. A powerslam hurt Koloff. As Koloff staggered to his feet, Luger mounted the second turnbuckle, and delivered an elbow to the head. Luger then threw Koloff into the turnbuckle, again and again, working on the back. Luger then threw Koloff out of the ring. Luger followed to the apron. First, a bodyslam ("On the pavement!" screams Lance Russell), then Luger rammed Koloff's head into the barrier. Ivan ran Luger off with the chain. Luger, as Ivan helped Nikita back into the ring, snuck around the ring with a chair. As Nikita reentered the ring, Luger hit Ivan in the back of the head with the chair, which put Ivan out. Luger continued the assault on Koloff, with a series of well placed dropped elbows into the chest, then the small of the back. He followed that with another throw into the turnbuckle and a backbreaker. Then, as Koloff squirmed in pain, Luger, with a smile on his face, climbed to the top turnbuckle, telegraphing a fist off the top rope. But Ivan, who had somewhat recovered, shook him off, to the floor. Nikita, who had seen Ivan getting up, grabbed the referee's shirt in an 'attempt' to get up. (Yes, I am making them both heels. It is more fun that way.) Ivan then followed with a punch to the head with the chain, which opened a cut on Luger's head. He then punched him again, this time in the back of the neck. Nikita, somewhat rejeuvanaed, went to the floor, and threw Koloff into the ringpost. Then, he tried to drop Luger on the barrier, but his back was too injured. So he brought Luger back into the ring, threw him off the turnbuckle a few times, gave him the back body drop, and a reverse neckbreaker (the Rude Awakening, for those not in the know). He went for the cover, but Luger kick out at two. Then, Koloff tries to suplex Luger, but his back gave out mid move, causing Luger to land on top of Koloff. Koloff was able to kick out. Both wrestlers got to their feet feeling groggy. Luger ran to the ropes, and nailed Koloff with a shoulderblock.. He tried again, but Koloff dodged, and put Luger into a sleeper. Luger was caught. The ref was picking up Luger's hand a second time, when Mike Rotundo (Remember who beat Koloff for the NWA TV title) ran to ringside and attacked Ivan from behind. Koloff released the hold to help his uncle. He hit Rotundo a few times, and returned to the ring. He went for the pin on Luger, who was able to kick out. The match continued, while Ivan and Rotundo fought outside. Koloff threw Luger off the ropes to go for the Russian Sickle, but Luger ducked. On the rebound, Luger was able to hit Koloff with a flying bodypress. Unfournately for Luger, the ref was occupied with the brawl outside, so he didn't see Koloff pinned for a four count. Luger went for an elbow, but missed. Koloff got up, and after a lot of punching and kicking, was able to nail Luger with a suplex. He went for the pin, but Luger was able to get his foot on the bottom rope. By this time, Ivan was on top of Rotundo. The ref ran out to save Rotundo, and was accidentally knocked out from Ivan winding up to hit Rotundo again. Ivan tried to wake the ref up, but was hit from behind by Rotundo. Koloff went out to save his uncle again, and was beating Rotundo, when Luger hit Koloff square in the back with a chair. He then brought Koloff into the ring, and slapped on the Human Torture Rack. He couldn't put the hold on full strength, because in the torture rack, Luger must support his victim's weight on his neck and lower back. His neck was too hurt to make Koloff submit, and since the ref was still on the floor, he relinquished the hold. Another ref came out to wake the first, and to shoo Rotundo away. Luger, holding his heck in pain, kicked Koloff. Then he threw him off the ropes to deliver a back body drop. Koloff dropped an ax handle right on the neck, which hurt Luger. He then got Luger in swinging neckbreaker (or whatever that move the Honky Tonk Man does.) By this time, the first ref was crawling back into the ring. Koloff was able to nail Luger with the Sickle, and end the match as the new U.S. champion. -whew- Well, O.K., it was the same match, but a lot of things were left out. Hope you liked it. Tony
