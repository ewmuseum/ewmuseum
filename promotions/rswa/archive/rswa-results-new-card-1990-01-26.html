RSWA results...new card

26 January 1990
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Results of the first RSWA card from Memphis... 1) Bill Dundee v. Eddie Gilbert Gilbert wins the CWA title in the Coal Miner's Glove Match 2) Freebirds v. Gordy/Hanson BamBam and The Lariat win a controversial match 3) Rock-n-Roll Express v. Rockers Time limit draw 4) Bushwackers/Duggan v. Midnight Express/Sullivan Bushwackers/Duggan win handily in Barbed-wire cage match 5) Flair v. Curt Henning Henning wins via countout when Genius interferes: Genius' leg broken by an irate Flair (with Figure 4). 6) Carlos Colon v. Hulk Hogan Colon by DQ...Colon swears revenge for Hulk attack that caused DQ 7) Road Warriors/Sid Viscious/Bruiser/Crusher v. Demolition/Skyscrapers/ King Kong Bundy Warrior team wins: Dan Spivey injured and in hospital for week. 8) Dusty Rhodes/Dick Murdoch v. Funk Bros. (Terry, Dory Jr.) Rhodes/Murdoch win a bloody double bullrope match. 9) Dr. Death v. Ted Dibiase Steve Williams wins in a breeze 10) Von Erich Bros. v. Ivan Koloff/Bruiser Brody/Kamala/Abdullah the Butcher/Original Sheik "A barbeque in the cage!" Chris and Kevin burned by Sheik...Abdullah then snacked on Chris' charred flesh. Mike and Kevin submit at same time. 11) Nikita v. Lex Lugar Nikita uses the sickle to crush the Total Package 12) Bill Watts v. Fritz von Erich The Cowboy wins quickly as Fritz worried about his sons.. MAIN EVENTS... 13) For the RSWA World Title... Bruno Samartino v. Andre the Giant Bruno scored a stunning victory after slamming the big Frenchman 14) Gagne/Gagne v. Bockwinkle/Stevens Ray and Nick proved that cheating will overcome purity on most occasions in the loser leave the RSWA cage match. But who are those new masked wrestlers...the Amazing G-men??? 15) Jerry Lawler/BamBam Bigelow v. Tommy Rich/Austin Idol The King and his partner win this inclosed Thunderdome cage match (no referee) and send Rich and Idol to the hospital for extended stays. ============== This new card takes place at the Tampa Stadium. Gordon Solie and Mike Graham call the match. 1) Superstar Billy Graham v. Wahoo McDaniel--Indian Strap Match (both men claim to have never lost this type of match) 2) Lex Lugar v. Kerry Von Erich--for Lugar's US title 3) Eddie Gilbert v. Jeff Jarrett--for Gilbert's CWA belt...if Jarret wins, he dates Miss Hyatt 4) Steiners v. Hart Foundation 5) Valliant Bros. v. Stan Hanson/Terry Gordy--for RSWA tag titles...in a cage! 6) Harley Race v. Dusty Rhodes--for the Florida State Title 7) Ric Flair v. Dory Funk Jr.--World title elimination 8) Ken Patera/John Studd v. Road Warriors--cage match 9) Al Perez v. Tito Santana 10) King Jerry Lawler v. Larry Zbysko--Memphis Chain Match 11) Sheik v. Abdullah the Butcher--No DQ, No Time Limit, No Stopping for blood! There must be a pinfall or submission! 12) Roddy Piper v. Bob Backlund--title elimination match Double Main Event-- 13) For the RSWA World Title-- Bruno Samartino (champion) v. Curt Henning 14) Grudge Match-- Hulk Hogan/Bruiser Brody v. Ultimate Warrior/Andre The Giant --Triple Dome of Terror Cage Match! -------------- "Violence is the last refuge of the incompetent." --Salvor Hardin -------------- [detail withheld] |One of these sigs should work.
