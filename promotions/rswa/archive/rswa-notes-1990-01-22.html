RSWA Notes...

22 January 1990
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The RSWA has taken the role that the NWA used to handle, that of being the overseeing organization within wrestling. They are the only organization that may sanction the World Champion. The WWF, NWA, USWA, and other regional and local organizations have agreed to recognize the RSWA champions (singles and tags) as the World Champions. Each organization maintains its list of champs, but they are only recognized as Federation champs and not as World Champs. The Federations have stopped calling their champs world champs: They call them (exp. The WWF Champion, The NWA Champions). At the beginning of this fantasy series, these are the RSWA Champions: World Heavyweight Champion: Bruno Samartino (who else...really?) World Tag-Team Champions: The Vallian Brothers (not at Memphis card due to injuries received at hands of Road Warriors after retaining titles due to interference by Mgr. Capt. Louis Albano: BTW, Albano is listed as serious but out of danger from the beating he took at the hands of the Warriors at that match! The Valliants should be back within 2-3 weeks.) Federation Champions: WWF: Hulk Hogan NWA: Ric Flair USWA: Jerry Lawler Federation Tag Champs: WWF: Colossal Connection NWA: Steiners USWA: Simpsons Regional: US: Lex Lugar IC: Ultimate Warrior AWA: Larry Zbysko CWA: Bill Dundee Others may be added as you want. The various titles may change as you want, but I think the Federation and regional titles should remain with wrestlers in that organization...they can be merged in title v. title except for the federation titles.
