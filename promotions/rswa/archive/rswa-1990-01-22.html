RSWA!

22 January 1990
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OK...what we've all been waiting for! The RecSport Wrestling Association! The idea is simple. If you wish, post your results to the following fantasy card, including an annotated match or two. I think the best way to go about this is to have the various wrestlers be at the peak of their careers. This way you could match some of the famous old-time wrestlers (such as Bruno or Bruiser) against today's top stars. I'll try to keep a tally and post after a week or so. You can even create your own card! Remember to try to keep this fun (I mean, don't have Dusty Wolf beating Flair or Lee Scott beating Hulk...). Maybe one of the higher ups will read it and realize that inter-federation matches might be very popular...you never know! On with the matches... =================== It's Monday night, so it must be Memphis! "Hello everybody, this is Lance Russel along with my partner Dave Brown from Mid South Coleseum right here in Memphis Tennessee! Tonight's card features the stars from all over the wresting world, including the different major federations. Let's go to Dave for a list of tonight's big card. Dave?" "Thank you, Lance. This is Dave Brown, and here's the card: 1) Eddie Gilbert v. Superstar Billy Dundee --Coal Miner's Glove Match for the CWA title. 2) Terry Gordy/Stan Hanson v. The Freebirds (Hayes/Garvin) 3) Rock-n-Roll Express v. The Rockers 4) Bushwackers/Jim Duggan v. Midnight Express/Kevin Sullivan --Barbed-wire Cage Match 5) Ric Flair v. Mr. Perfect (World title elimination match) 6) Carlos Colon v. Hulk Hogan (World title elimination match) 7) Road Warriors/Sid Viscious/Bruiser/Crusher v. Demolition/ Skyscrapers/King Kong Bundy --Wargames Cage Match 8) Dusty Rhodes/Dick Murdoch v. Terry/Dory Funk Jr. --Double Texas Bullrope Match 9) Dr. Death Steve Williams v. Ted Dibiase 10) Kerry/Kevin/David/Mike/Chris Von Erich v. Ivan Koloff/Bruiser Brody/ Kamala/Abdullah the Butcher/Original Sheik --Wargames cage match 11) Nikita Koloff (bald version) v. Lex Lugar --US Heavyweight title 12) Cowboy Bill Watts v. Fritz Von Erich --Promoters grudge match 13) Tri-Main Event: For the RSWA World Heavyweight Title: The Challenger: From Grenoble in the French Alps, The 8th Wonder of the World...Andre The Giant! The Champion: From Pittsburgh, PA via Italy, The Living Legend Bruno Samartino! One fall, 1 1/2 hour time limit. 14) Tri-Main Event: Verne/Greg Gagne v. Nick Bockwinkle/Ray Stevens --Losers leave the RSWA Cage Match 15) Tri-Main Event: King Jerry Lawler/BamBam Bigelow v. Tommy Rich/Austin Idol--Thunderdome Cage Match--No Rules--No Referee--Roof on Cage--Door Locked ============== Remember, each wrestler is the the peak of his talent!
