OWL Live - May 6, 1997 Live from Kannta, ON [The words OWL Live appear on a black screen.] Kyle: "Welcome everyone to OWL Live! We are live from Kanata, Ontario, and just one week away from OWL's PPV extravaganza, Midnight Mayhem!
