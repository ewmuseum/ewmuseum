LOW — Front page

Front page, April 2003

captured 17 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ortiz For Mayor? Rumors have been flying around LOW headquarters about Jason Ortiz signing up for the next Mayor election in Miami, Florida. Sources tell LOW officials that Jason is wants the job, but Jason says other wise when we caught up and talked to him about the Rumors. [Knock on Jason's locker room door. Their was silence as you could hear Jason stand up and scream.] Jason Ortiz: Hold on a second, I am changing. [Their was a few more minutes of silence as one of Low's many interviewers sat next to Jason's locker room door. The door opened, out came Jason Ortiz, clean clothes on and all.] Jason Ortiz: WOW! Didn't expect you here. [Jason quickly shrugged his shoulders] Jason Ortiz: What do you need, if this is about me doing drugs, the accusations are not true. LOW Interviewer: No, it's nothing like that I want to talk to you about another rumor. [Ortiz stood motionless with his hair sticking straight in the air.] Jason Ortiz: Another rumor, what kind of *BLEEP* is that? This *BLEEP* is starting to piss me off, first a rumor about my leg and now another one? What is LOW thinking? LOW Interviewer: So the Rumor isn't true? [Jason's eye lids began to lower] Jason Ortiz: I don't even know what the *BLEEP* the RUMOR IS ABOUT! LOW Interviewer: Well sources tell LOW that you want to become the next Mayor for Miami. Isn't that the city you live in? Says your name is signed up on the list. Jason Ortiz: That's *BLEEP* bullshit! I never signed up to be the Mayor. What the hell does the Mayor do anyways, he is probably some panzy ass bitch who sits on a computer typing all day. WELL I AM A WORKING MAN! I MAKE MONEY THE RIGHT WAY. So NO the Rumors are not true. Who signed me up for the list cause I sure in the hell didn't. This just makes me sick, Jason Ortiz as the next Mayor of Miami. So people are stupid you know that. Who in their right mind would believe me, Jason Ortiz, head leader of the Hoods as a Mayor. [Jason spits on the ground, turning around and heading into his locker room again.] Jason Ortiz: Get a real *BLEEP* story and stop harassing me god damn it! LOW Interviewer: Wait- [Jason slammed the door shut, locking it to make sure no one would bother him.] All content and images are property of LGM1 Web Media. Copyright 2002-2003.
