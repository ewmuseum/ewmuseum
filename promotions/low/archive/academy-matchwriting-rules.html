Academy: Matchwriting Rules

It is a tedious task, and should not be taken lightly. But in enrolling in the matchwriting school of the Lights Out Academy, you are taking the first steps to becoming a better matchwriter, which will assist you as a handler helping out in your fed, as a staff member, or as a fedhead yourself. Matchwriters are highly appreciated in Lights Out Wrestling, and will gain you quicker acceptance to the fed. Therefore, this portion of the academy will be run by Trigger, owner of LOW. There are a few simple rules, and the word 'simple' is stressed. Do not ask for your matches to be graded - When the graders get to them, they'll get to them. It is a service to you that they are doing, and you should give them all the time that they need to thoroughly read your work and give it an accurate score. Do not complain about your grades - When you complain to a grader or a staff member, it only makes us feel frustrated that our hard work to teach you has failed. This is a place to learn the fundamentals of eWrestling, and complaining is NOT one of them. Strive to perfect yourself - If you are getting Ds and Fs on your grades, there is nothing to be ashamed of... But we do want to see an effort. If you've gotten an F on your last two matches, and both graders have said it had too many grammar errors, follow their advice and run your submission through a spell check before you post! Do not give up - Once again, this is a tedious process. You probably will not succeed on your first try. As long as you are making an effort, and doing everything you can to improve yourself, you will come out of the program a better matchwriter, and a better person as a whole. Things in the matchwriting section of LOA run very easily. Every week, a match will be posted on the Matchwriting forum. You will then have the week to write this match, and post it as a new thread. Within a short amount of time, our graders will rate your work, give you feedback, and criticisms. You will thus improve, until you are as skilled in matchwriting as any. These are the simple rules to be followed in the matchwriting section of the Lights Out Academy, and we hope that you follow them well. They will lead you to great success and happiness in the future, so keep them in mind as you embark on this journy. We hope to see you posting in no time.
