JD: What the hell is going on. Oh please, God, no. CS: But...why...? JD: Someone help him! After wrapping it around his neck, he secures the clip to the wire, creating a tightening effect when pulled on. His face remains still yet you can see the fear in his face. JD: Kevin, you have the match won, please get down and enjoy the after party. CS: This is the most horrible thing that I have ever seen. What is he trying to accomplish? As Annihilation stands there with the shiny piece of gold that took him years to grab hold of around his waste he stares, his face looking cold. He reaches into his back pocket and pulls out a small piece of paper and stares at it. Suddenly his eyes grow big as he realizes where he is right now. JD: Get out of that Kevin. You are a hero we need you here. Annihilation quickly goes to unclip the wire, but in a flash, the platform gives from the damage taken during the match, and Annihilation's body goes limp with one hand between his neck and the wire. His teeth clench and he struggles for air. The crowd is absolutely silent and Annihilation wobbles trough the air like the most helpless thing on the face of the planet. JD: Someone get him down. Oh my God. CS: Cut the fucking wire. He has a better chance to live the fall than he does right now. JD: Somebody fucking hurry, NOW!!! The sounds of depression are heard through the commentators voice and the sound of people crying fulfill the arena. After about forty-five seconds Annihilation seems to have lost all energy and air as suddenly his hand falls to his side and the noose-like wire tightens around his neck with great force. His fists clench and a tear slowly rolls down his cheek, all the way down to the rubble of the cages and seconds later, there is no movement. JD: ... CS: ... And there he hangs...the true hero of Lights Out Wrestling. Here he hangs above the men that have tried to destroy the home of Lights Out Wrestling times before, who find themselves underneath the mess of the cages...the destruction...the fate. The scene is depressing. One man hangs numerous feet above ground dead and nothing is heard but the cries of the people around. Slowly, something falls from the sky and it is seen that it is the small piece of paper that Annihilation pulled out of his pocket. As it slowly floats its way down, it lands on top of the platform that was the last ground that Annihilation ever stepped on. As the view zooms in onto the piece of paper, you can read the words that were scribbled on it... "I've Defeated The Tyrants That Once Ruled Lights Out Wrestling. But It Wasn't Lights Out For Them... ...It Was Lights Out For Me."
