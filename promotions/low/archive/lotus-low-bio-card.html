Lotus Real Name: “T'ain’t none ya DAMN bizness!” Birthdate: “Don’t worry bout it… you ain’t buyin’ me nothin’ anyways” Hometown: “Representin' dat F-U son! Fukuoka fo' life!” Height: “My license say 5’3… I guess it’s right…” Weight: 'Who cares? I ain't swole up like yo fat ass… patnah!' (123) Nicknames: “Me people call me Lo… but anyone who ain’t cool wit’ me like dat get stuck fo’ callin’ me dat. On tha streets I got tha tag “Kogeru Grrl”, you’ll see dat on walls around tha hood.” Status: Neutral Entrance Music: 'Material Jungle' by Ozrosaurus Information Wrestling Style: “I go HARD playa! Straight GANGSTA! DAT’S MY style!” (Lo’s style revolves around stiff strikes and speed. Always being the lightweight underdog, no one dares have a problem when she works extremely stiff with her attacks. With her background in Wing Chun and Wushu strikes from Lotus done at full force are the same as a 200 pound man’s hits, though she has to let off with a few of her moves that would normally knock someone out. Lo’s style involves working the body over with stiff strikes, and when their base is lowered she takes them down by their legs or locks in a submission to ground them. She hits some acrobatic maneuvers when compliant. When it comes to weaponry, Lotus is a prodigy. In the environment of extreme wrestling, Lotus is a savant, the future, as it’s known. She also possesses the Tenzan blood in her, the same warrior threshold for pain in her. Setup Move: None (“Pfft, why am I gonna warn tha bastard dat I’m about ta whip they ass huh? Dat’s just dumb, man…”) Finisher: 'S-Unit' / 'Lotus Clutch' ('S-Unit' (impact finisher) (Saikyo-Unit, but she calls it the “S-U-NIT!”) A modified Shining Wizard using a knee to the jaw followed by another knee to the temple or the side of the head. Lotus whips this move out of nowhere, and can perform this move in any situation. 'Lotus Clutch' (submission finisher) An inverted reverse full nelson clutch. Lotus can lock this on in various situations, by either taking the opponent down onto their stomach and locking it on, a DDT into a rollover into the clutch, or the popular counter-clutch: the opponent runs at Lotus to swing with a lariat, but she hops up and takes the brunt of the attack to the chest, sacrificing herself a bit for the opportunity to lock in the Lotus Clutch in a standing counter position, where she starts to kick into the legs and smaller back to drop the opponent to their knees for grounding.) LOW titles: 'I ain't got none yet... I just got here...' LOW Awards: 'Man, what I just say?' Other Federations: The X Other Federations Awards: Pretty much the baddest bitch in The X at the time.
