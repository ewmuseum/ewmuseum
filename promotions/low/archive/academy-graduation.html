Academy: Graduation

>>Navigation Main • Graduation • Tutorials • Forums Roleplaying • Rules • Grading Matchwriting • Rules • Grading Segment Writing • Rules • Grading Graduation The ultimate goal in Lights Out Academy is to improve your schools as an eWrestler. Whether it be in roleplaying, segment writing, or matchwriting, it is more than likely that you will improve if you allow yourself to learn. However, there is a tangable goal to this academy as well. Graduation. What does this mean? This means that you have proven yourself worthy, and that you are beyond what the academy can teach you. It means that you are Lights Out Wrestling quality, and not only will you be accepted into LOW, but you will also have the benefit of already earning responsibility and trust from the management. In addition to this, you will automatically be put on the Poser list, and your wrestler's image will be completed at the next available opportunity. So just how do you graduate from Lights Out Academy? Simple. There are two ways. Plan A You must have at least 7 A grades in roleplaying, and 5 A grades in segment-writing. This is not too difficult. As long as you follow the grading criteria, you could be done in less than two months. Moreso, you will have one of the best eWrestling educations out there. Plan B Don't have the time to write twelve peices of work, without much variation in style? That's okay. Plan B is definitely for you. In Plan B, you only need 4 A grades in roleplaying, 3 A grades in segment-writing, and 3 A grades in matchwriting. That's only ten peices of work, in three different categories! You could realistically do this in one to two months. If you graduate with Plan B, you will earn a spot on the LOW roster although you will be required to write one match a week. Very different opportunities, for very different people. One thing that should be made clear is that it doesn't matter how good or bad you are! This is an academy, things are supposed to start out tough. Thus, we hereby retract the previous statement that you will more than likely improve. We garuntee that you will improve. So dive right in. It doesn't matter what you're getting into. See what lies before you, and see how you can make the best of it. To join Lights Out Academy, simply head on down to the forums and post your first submission. Let's see where you go from there.
