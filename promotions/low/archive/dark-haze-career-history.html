Dark Haze — LOW career history

captured 20 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Career History Anthony Eng a.k.a. ”Dark Haze” grew up on the streets of Northwest Atlanta. . Peter Eng was his father, but he was also a Mafioso in Yakuza or “Japanese Mafia.” His father, half African/half Japanese, left his mother just two weeks after she became pregnant forcing her to take care of her children. Peter is now nowhere to be found and they are no longer legally married. His mother, Tonya, had already begun to raise three other children with a different father before Ant was born. Both of his older brothers began to look to the streets as a way to make money, and for them to try and support themselves and their only sister. His older brothers, Jamal and Mike, both joined a gang called the Crips. After, being two of the most active members in the Atlanta chapter they quickly gained respect and rose to the top. Growing up Anthony took it upon himself to be like his brothers but even more so like his father, whom he only has stories to reminisce of. In the fourth grade Anthony began to sell weed to kids during school, and he continued this until the 6th grade, where he made it into a full time job. He would wake up, go to school and sell marijuana, and then he’d hit the streets after school to sell the drug. Anthony began to realize why his brothers were doing the same. In middle school him and some of his friends started their own gang, fighting or “warring” with other groups at the school and on the streets. With his body growing and maturing fast he joined the Crips at the age of 14. After going into high-school, he quickly received a reputation of a thug and a very feared drug dealer for his age. In and out of school kids began to call him “Dark Haze,” because the weed he was selling was very dark, almost purple, he called it dark haze. Ever since, the name stuck. Along with his brothers he rapidly went up in the rankings and soon his “boys” joined the gang, and he became a leader in the Crips. He called his little branch the' North Atlanta Crip Gang.' Dark Haze is one of the biggest drug lords and most feared people in the northwest and all throughout Atlanta. Despite his street fame, he has been shot, hospitalized, jailed, and is hated just as much as he is liked on the streets. After going to jail several time his brothers demanded him to get out of the gang and do something productive, and because of his and his brothers’ status he has a chance to do so. Dark Haze took his anger, want of money, and mostly his want of fame to the wrestling ring. Haze joined a small fed called TXW and was a main-eventer starting from his first match. He quickly began to stack on titles and belts. After he finally won the World Championship TXW was forced to close due to the excess fighting outside of the ring. Dark Haze then got a chance and was signed into a larger federation called RUSH Wrestling. In RUSH he was a great competitor and fought well, but then his old federation decided to re-open under the name Lights Out Wrestling, or LOW. Haze ended his contract with RUSH and hit the rings of LOW. A few weeks into LOW’s birth Dark Haze was arrested on charges of Conspiracy to Distribute, Conspiracy to Murder, Attempted Murder, Possession of Marijuana, and Conspiracy to Gang Related Activities, without bail. Dark Haze was admitted to the County Jail “CJ” and was being held until his trial, which could be several months. He gained credit for serving time in jail and the charges were dropped. After getting out of jail Haze found LOW is now closed down and he has been left jobless. Today, Anthony Eng a.k.a. “Dark Haze” was looking to get back in the ring and make money the legal way, although it may be hard, seeing how he just can’t totally leave the streets alone. He found XWF and DW, two well respected federations, and joined them. After a few months Haze realized that having two jobswas no joke, so he decided to take a break and fufill his dreams. Haze went into a temorary retirement and bouht a small island. Now, Dark Haze has decided to return to the wrestling world and found no place better to return, Lights Out Wrestling.
