Academy: Segment Writing Rules

captured 2 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
>>Navigation Main • Graduation • Tutorials • Forums Roleplaying • Rules • Grading Matchwriting • Rules • Grading Segment Writing • Rules • Grading Rules We will state the guidelines/rules and the ‘what we want to expect’ from you below. Follow these guidelines/rules accordingly and you will find yourself trouble free and with great in-cards as well too. First off we’d like to tell you that you may not use a wrestler with out his or her consent! We can not stress this enough, and we don’t like the idea of a complaint coming up to us about this rule broken and changing the course of the wrestler in any which way. So please, before you even think about using a wrestler besides yours, please get the handlers position. Failure to do so will result in a firing. This as well goes for the staff too. Another rule we like to enforce is the rule of writing on in-card every week. No questions asked. At the end of each week a in-card must be present in the in-card board, or you will get a warning. If this continues on the next week, you will be suspended or fired from LOA. No questions asked as I said. No OOC (Out Of Character, as if you were speaking to a friend personally in the real world) on the in-card forums! I’ve never seen this happen, and I hopefully never will! There is no reason to post OOC on the in-card forums when they there is a OOC forum as well. That is what you use. This rule has been carried out already, and failure to follow this will result in a suspension or firing. Do not, and I repeat, do not complain about your grade! The grade you receive is the official one, unless ruled out and discussed later on. If you believe you have been getting unfair grades, please contact a staff member and talk to them. In other words, justice will be carried out to keep you happy. If you would like to how to improve your self, please ask for help from a staff or a friend. If we find out that, that the in-cards you are using for LOA are being used in a different federation and/or we see in the wording of where LOA should be, and we find a different say such as XWF, you will be fired no matter what the reasoning is at all. ALL in-cards must be and will be for LOA primary use, no questions asked! Period! If you are to contact an owner or a staff member about your in-card not being graded, you will be fairly warned! The next time this happens, you will be fired from LOA! In LOA we are taking time out of our lives to help you, and the least you can do is wait patiently. If you can not wait patiently, we might as well ask you to leave LOA. Nothing else! We will get to your in-card as soon as we have time; if it takes awhile it will take awhile, but we assure you your in-card will be graded! You may not reply to your in-card post! If you need to do so, take it up with the grader or post it on the OOC forum. This should clearly be understood! Nothing else! Your in-cards must have a reason to be posted; just not to have one up if you’ve been warned! We only allowed in-cards for the following; feuds; interviews; storylines; and angles. Nothing else! If you show your wrestler buying a pop, that will not cut it and it will not be graded! You must have a reason, or it is gibberish. For an in-card to be posted in an event, the in-card must maintain a C- average or it will not be posted. A C- average is 70 points or above. Anything else dealing with 69 points or below, will not be posted! Yes, even if is i69 points. So if you would like your feud or storyline to come across, work hard for 70 points and it will. These are the guidelines/rules for in-cards in LOA Follow them and you will be successful with your in-cards and move onto better things as well. Work hard and you will earn everything you would like in LOA. Work will be involved, but in the end there will be fun!
