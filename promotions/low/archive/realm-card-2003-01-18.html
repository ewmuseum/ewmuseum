Realm Card, 18 January 2003

18 January 2003

Saturday, January 18th, 2003, 11:59 PM Pacific Time STIPS RESULTS Realm Archives Aftermath Match vs.vs. Zurick vs. Chris Kinsley vs. Miller Magnum Three brand new wrestlers have been signed to Lights Out Wrestling. Their names have different values. From superstardom, to stardom, to unnoticed talent, they are all trying to prove themselves in LOW. But they don't know what they're in for. Before they can face the Lights Out Wrestling warriors... they have to face each other... Singles Match vs. Iron Lung vs. Canyon Thunders After a much needed break to heal his injuries, Canyon Thunders is back, and ready for action. But as we have seen in the past, even a week off can mean catastrophe for one's popularity in Lights Out Wrestling. Thunders worked hard for his spot in LOW, and now it seems there's only one way to keep it. Stopping the incredible streak of Iron Lung. Non-Title Match Warrior Match vs. Luke Justice vs. Judgement Judgement has been shooting for the top ever since he entered this company. One by one his victims fall. But Luke Justice has been working much longer for the spot that he feels he deserves. Is he gonna let Judgement take main event status before he does? It doesn't seem likely... it doesn't seem likely at all Ultimate Tapei Deathmatch vs. Jason Kage vs. Matt Robinson As of late, Matt Robinson has been on a terrible losing streak, fumbling the ball in the most important places. But he's still got talent. And he still keeps striving to be the best. The best? That's just suitable for Jason Kage, who belongs to THE BEST faction in LOW. Not suitable for Jason Kage, who sees Robinson as only another falling star... Non-Title Match Singles Match vs. Pax vs. Annihilation Pax is a record breaker. A record maker. A title holder. A brand new strap around his shoulder. The Aftermath Champion. Undefeated since it's creation. But now here's Annihilation. Dying to get a peice of the gold. Refusing to stop, until he has annihilated all. Pax must be stopped. Pax must be beaten. Pax is just another opportunity to get at the gold... Lightning Dome Match vs. CADE vs. Tristan Belmont Tristan Belmont is running with the big dogs now. The Shadow Riders. They've got his back. And he's got something for them as well. He's got his rising status in LOW. His slow ascend to main event level. But is he really ready for it? He wants to prove himself to the Riders. He wants to prove himself to LOW. But CADE has proven time and time again: nothing can be proved against him. Streetfight vs. Lotus vs. PoGo He's back. There's no denying it. Even if we want to. He's come. Come with a new message. A new fight. More desperate than the last. He must rid LOW of evil. He must, before time runs out. And now, he faces the man that single-handedly called him back from the Nethervoid. The man that single-handedly brought Lotus upon a new quest. The man that single-handedly is pure evil. Pure evil... that must be stopped.
