LOW — Federation paperwork

The History of Lights Out Wrestling

captured 18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Prologue “You’re making a mistake, man. You’ll regret this.” The message clicked off, and Trigger took another sip of his drink; sighing. Beep. “…The fuck is goin’ on, homie? You ain’t gonna let this shit get to you, I know you won’t.” Beep. On the contrary, the caller knew all too well. Trigger himself had seen Dark Haze’s free agency listing at the commissioning office when he turned in his papers earlier that day. Yes, it was official… True Xtreme Wrestling was no more. The company he had poured his soul into for nearly seven months was no more. Trigger’s dream was no more. And for what? Beep. “Hey buddy. It’s Dan.” Trigger hesitated, his trembling hand quivering over the phone. Sadistic. His mentor. His trainer. His champion. “Please don’t do this. I’ve taught you better, haven’t I? How can you throw away what you worked so hard for? How can you throw away what we all worked so hard for? Please tell me there’s another answer. Please tell me that this isn’t all because of Meyhu…” Meyhu. Mike Meyhu. How could it not be because of the so called Number One Brawler in the Business? The number one backstabber in the business; that’s what he was to Trigger. The man who never let vengeance die. The man who always needed to have the last laugh. Meyhu had destroyed TXW… brick by brick, stone by stone. He used every means necessary, and in the end his quest prevailed. He made Trigger regret the very day that the two had met. It was all over now. No going back. But as the night died, and his spirit died as well, The Infinite Warrior Trigger had one vow. One promise. Never trust a Meyhu. Never again. Chapter 1 – “New Beginnings” He told himself this was just temporary. He told himself this was just a favor for his friends. He told himself he wasn’t lying. But he knew deep down that he was. “Lights Out Wrestling.” Trigger looked baffled. “That’s what it’s going to be called? Lights Out Wrestling?” “Yes sir,” replied Emil Niemi, the head chairman. “Lights Out Wrestling…” The Infinite Warrior rolled the name around on his tongue. “Lights Out… That was the name of TXW’s television show, wasn’t it? Tuesday Night Lights Out.” More nods from the room of savvy businessmen. Another minute passed, and once again Trigger wondered how he had gotten into this position. He had sworn he was done, that his days in wrestling were over. Even now he didn’t know what was going on. Even now he regretted his every movement. Yet he wasn’t going anywhere. He might as well give it a shot. The chairmen had made it seem so simple over the phone. A group of ex-TXW wrestlers wanted to start up a new federation, a reunion of their histories together. Yet as Trigger himself knew, passion didn’t automatically equal success. They needed a name for the contracts. They needed reassurance for the public. They needed a guarantee. The Infinite Warrior happened to be it, and in a way, he didn’t mind. If there was one thing he didn’t want, it was an uneventful future for a group of competitors that he had trained. Something did seem fishy, however. “This federation,” started Trigger, “… Who will be running it?” It was Mr. Niemi’s turn to chime in again. “Well, Trigger sir,” he started, “we were thinking that it would be you…” Chapter 2 – “And So It Began” They were all there. Nearly every one of them. From Silk to Showtime, Jesse Jamester to Brandon Brokeyaoff, even Mike Meyhu joined in the festivities. This last fact proved to be a difficult one for Trigger to swallow. Mike Meyhu… The very man who had ended his joy for the hobby. The very man whose name he loathed, whose face he spat at, and whose actions he feared. “He’ll follow me to the grave, won’t he?” Trigger asked himself as he sat in an office-room, preparing for the debut of Lights Out Wrestling Realm, the LOW’s weekly telecast. “Maybe I should just leave now. It’s not like they’ll care.” But he knew in his heart that it wasn’t true. He was doing what humans like to do best: avoid their problems at all costs. “…Not anymore…” he vowed, and stood to his feet. No, Trigger decided that he was done being afraid. That he was done basing his life on the actions of another. It was time to stand up to the music… It was time to face his fears. One step… Two… Down the hallway he trotted, slow at first, but then at a steady pace. Down the hallway with his eyes focused dead ahead. “Hey hey! Trigger, my man!” He turned, and saw Brandon Brokeyaoff coming from the door of a locker room. The Infinite Warrior pulled the handle, and cleared his eyes from the smoke that filled its area. There, on the couch sat Brandon Brokeyaoff and Dark Haze, smoking an unmentionable substance. “Yo Trig, I’m sorry I can’t be here officially tonight, but I’m here in spirit, you know that.” Haze was barred from competing in Lights Out Wrestling as his contract with RUSH had recently been renewed. While he missed the presence of the last TXW World Champion, he was proud of his ‘warrior’ for making it to the big leagues. After a few moments of chatting, Trigger took to the halls again, his confidence in himself building. It was then that another voice beckoned him into a locker room. Who would it be but Jesse Jamester, a long time follower of the Infinite Warrior. “Thanks for the opportunity, Trigger. You won’t regret it.” That evening Jamester was set to face CADE and the son of Trigger’s mentor, making his debut as Silence. It was the main event, and a qualifying round in the tournament to crown the first LOW World Champion. Trigger knew perfectly well what he was doing when he gave Jesse this opportunity… And no, he didn’t regret it one bit. Time flew by, and before he knew it, the taping of Realm was almost at its beginning. Trigger strutted down the hall, happiness in every step, feeling as if nothing could bring him down. No sooner would such an idea be thought that such an idea would be broken, as a shadow cast itself over the Infinite Warrior. He tilted his head upwards, but he knew the sight awaiting him. The black goatee… The bald skull… The arrogant complexion… He tilted his head upwards, face to face with “Mean” Mike Meyhu. “Congratulations, Trigger,” he said, and then proceeded to walk onwards. No attack. No insult. No threat. Just “congratulations.” He stood there, in the hall, confused for several moments. In the depths of his imagination, the Infinite Warrior could not determine what his foe meant by it. But then all at once, it hit him… Meyhu hadn’t meant anything. Meyhu hasn’t tried to mean everything. Meyhu had turned over a new leaf. Trigger’s killer instinct kicked in. He should go after the enemy now, while his back was turned. Meyhu would never know what hit him. But in the back of his head, a new thought awakened. Things didn’t have to be the way they always had. Meyhu had gotten his revenge, and that story was over. But another one was now beginning. This was a new era. This was a chance to start over. Chapter 3 – “No Chance For Survival” “Ugh.” As the knife cut into his flesh, taking the Infinite Warrior by surprise, Trigger fell to the floor. Straining, his energy drained. Reaching out… But no hand reached back. Only another slash of the knife, stabbing mere centimeters away from his jugular. Crawling towards the door… Another stab. His groaning subsided. When the mysterious attacker entered his locker room, only one thought entered his mind. Meyhu. He had been naïve to trust the Number One Brawler in the Business. Now, his mistake was coming back to haunt him. How could he have been so stupid? Perhaps it was the glitz and glamour of it all. Over the past six weeks, LOW had been doing stupendous, crowning champions such as Lotus in the Xtreme Division and CADE as the World Champion. The Shadow Riders had debuted, and raged a short war against Jesse Jamester and Silence, and stables ignited into flames as the RiP’s Rick Static turned his back on the group he had created to found Broken Reality along with Tristan Belmont and Pax. Even Dark Haze had found a way to return, and for a while, everything seemed good. But not now. Not after this mysterious man, Kevin Miller, had hired the assassination of the Infinite Warrior. The assassination that seemed to be a success. The attacker stepped on Trigger’s skull, turning it to the side. Now, as he blacked out, the Infinite Warrior caught a glimpse of his face. Not the face of Mike Meyhu. Perhaps he wasn’t so naïve afterall… Chapter 4 – “False Idols” Trigger was gone. There was no doubt about that. There was doubt about the very fact that he was alive. But for the rest of Lights Out Wrestling, life went on. But in the heart of LOW… A part of it had died. Storylines still progressed, however, with Broken Reality coming to a split, Lotus ascending, and Sean Dahmer capturing attention like never before. The Realm title was introduced, and was vacated by Rick Static, who decided to leave his past behind and venture out into the future. Internally, another battle raged. The battle for control. Kevin Miller, the CEO of Lights Out Wrestling, had a firm grasp on the situation at the current time. Though his face was never revealed, and his name was thought to be fake, he had a devastating effect on LOW wrestlers and staff, through his henchman Justin Malice. The man who first destroyed Trigger, and then proceeded to rid the Vice President and Commissioner away. Shockingly, The Shadow Riders joined with Miller and Malice in a group that came to be known as The Winning Team. In the following weeks, such fighters as Sean Dahmer and Luke Justice joined as well. All that stood in their way was chairman Emil Niemi and his team of wrestlers known as Black Mist. But even they were taken down… And for a while, it seemed as if The Winning Team truly was the winning team. For a while, until a new alliance joined in the fight. Unholy as it was, the two enemies banded together to destroy the greater evil. Lotus… And none other than “Mean” Mike Meyhu, the World Champion of Lights Out Wrestling. And so, things stayed steady. At the pay per view, entitled Infinite, Matt Meyhu fought Justin Malice for the presidency of LOW, and his brother Mike defended his World Championship against Lotus. It was a match booked to tear LOW apart, but in reality, it only made the federation stronger. Also at Infinite, a Vice President was hired. Jack Sherwin joined up, and he joined up firmly on the side of LOW. The first battle of the war for Lights Out Wrestling had ended. But another one was beginning. And this time around, the sides would be even… Chapter 5 – “The Shot Heard Around The World” No sooner had Jack Sherwin come to Lights Out Wrestling that Kevin Miller pulled more strings to have the Vice President fired. But Sherwin was not going out without a fight. A simple phone call was all it took, and a new plague had come to LOW… Yet this time, it was on the real winning team. The home team. This plague? None other than former PCW star Rich Sherwin, who was destined to unite LOW and wage war against Miller and his goons. But this was not as easy as he had originally thought. Whatever Team LOW through at Miller was thrown back right in their faces. Mike Meyhu, Jesse Jamester, Lotus, and Sherwin were a formidable force, but just as formable was their opposition, which included Jason Kage, Eric Russo, Luke Justice, Sean Dahmer, and the ever-fearsome Justin Malice. Something needed to happen, and something needed to happen quickly. LOW needed a change, no matter which side that change would come for… And a change came. The Equalizer they called him. Hyped for weeks, both sides stood on end, wondering if this equalizer would be for them or for the enemy. And in the end, they never found out. Because CADE returned as this very Equalizer, but the first World Champion of Lights Out Wrestling had no distinction in his actions. First he saved Meyhu from an ambush attack. Then he took out Lotus, costing him the World Championship in a match against Rich Sherwin. At least the title stayed with Team LOW, right? Wrong. For right when his hands grasped the gold title belt, Rich Sherwin became a new man. Filled with hatred, deceit, and greed, Rich Sherwin became the poster boy for Kevin Miller and The Winning Team. All was to be settled soon… And soon all was settled… Soon came the ferocious battle cry… Chapter 6 – “The Journey Home” Justin Malice sat in his locker room, in utter darkness, focused in on the television screen. Tonight was a very important night. Tonight, everything was on the line. The pay per view was entitled BattleCry, and it was truly the beginning of a war. Title defenses here and there, grudge matches, and even a royal rumble to top it off. But none of those encounters meant anything when in comparison with the main event. Mike Meyhu. Lotus. Sean Dahmer. Rich Sherwin. CADE. Two men from each side, and one oddball thrown into the mix. Tonight, the World Championship was on the line. Tonight, everything was on the line. It had been an incredible evening already, with Justice Incorporated, Luke Justice, and Tristan Belmont all winning respective titles. But now, that was a distant past. What lay ahead was the future. The main event started off well for The Winning Team, as the savior of LOW, Lotus, did not show up. “That whiny bastard. One loss and he disappears,” exclaimed Malice, smirking to himself. “We’ll show them.” Things continued along this path. CADE was eliminated. Meyhu was badly injured. Though Sherwin was down and out as well, Dahmer continued to lay on the punishment. It seemed like things couldn’t get any better for The Winning Team. They didn’t. In fact, they got much, much, worse. A new hope arrived. A new savior arrived. Lotus arrived. And in a shocking speech, he declared that his work in Lights Out Wrestling was done. That his work on this planet was done. With hesitant words, he claimed that he had rid the world of all the evil he could… Yet the only one that stood in his way was Sean Dahmer. “The Nightmare” as he called himself was the last strand of evil left in LOW. And with this statement, Lotus announced that he would take Dahmer back with him… When he returned to the Nethervoid… Nobody knew where this Nethervoid was, or what it was for that matter, but within moments Dahmer and he vanished, never to return that evening. It was down to two men. Meyhu and Sherwin. Malice’s heart was beating a mile a minute. An attempt… A reversal… A close call… A final finisher. The Meyhu Meltdown prevailed. Mike Meyhu prevailed. Lights Out Wrestling prevailed. And the Winning Team staggered even more. “ That bastard Sherwin! I'll tear him up! I'LL FUCKING KILL HIM!!! I—” He didn’t have time to finish. A blunt metal object came down on the back of his head, and he fell to the ground with a thump. Standing in his place, stood a shadowy figure. He spotted the camera and covered it with his hand, but not before the fans got a glimpse of his face. …Of the face of LOW President Trigger… Chapter 7 – “Fall Of An Empire” Every wall fell, with only one left standing. The Winning Team had lost this battle. The Shadow Riders and Luke Justice broke away, looking for more fame and fortune with less busy-work. Justin Malice was destroyed by a raging Trigger. All that remained was the mysterious Kevin Miller… And Rich Sherwin. Rich Sherwin, the only one left. Rich Sherwin, the devil’s only hope. Rich Sherwin, the most hated man in LOW. The cancerous cell that must be removed. And Team LOW was happy to do so. “I don’t think this is a good idea, Mike.” Trigger sat in his office, counseling the World Champion. “I can’t honestly believe that Miller would risk getting rid of his only henchman. I can’t risk getting rid of you.” Meyhu smirked. “There’s no risk involved, Trig. Me versus Sherwin, one on one? World title on the line, and the loser leaves LOW! How could it get any better?” Trigger was not moved. “Mike, do you remember who it was that made the matchup? It was Miller. I’ve never met him in person, I’ve never even seen his face, but I think I know him well enough to say that he wouldn’t take a chance like this. He’s got too much to lose.” “It’s not his stupidity that’s in question,” retorted the champion. “Why do we need to worry about his mistake? Trigger, you were almost murdered by this man. Isn’t it about time we get some revenge?” “I’m not about revenge. Revenge clouds vision. Clouded vision leaves one helpless, prone to vicious attacks. I can’t risk another vicious attack, Mike. You know I can’t.” “Then maybe we’re not on the same page after all.” With that, Meyhu left. He did go through with the match, and he did win. It seemed for a while as if the Infinite Warrior’s suspicions were unfounded. …If only he had kept with his gut instinct. Chapter 8 – “The Unkindest Cut Of All” Tonight was the night that all would change. Tonight was the night that an era would end. Tonight was the night that a new one would begin. Tonight was the night… And as tension mounted, it began to unfold. Half of Trigger knew what would happen before it ever did. Knew how Miller would surprise the audience with a voice interview near the beginning of the show. Knew how Miller would seem calm, despite the collapse of his kingdom. Knew how Miller would promise to reveal himself before the night was over. The show was War Wounds… And the stage was set for a memorable evening, whether good or bad. Kevin Miller had lost everything. All of it. Nothing was left for him in LOW, and so it was time to pull out the final assault. Even his booking for the main event was a desperation move. Placing Meyhu against Jamester against Lotus all at once. Yes, Lotus had returned. It seemed Miller held a lot more evil than appeared at first sight. The three fought, but didn’t give in. They weren’t torn apart by the greed for glory; if anything it stuck them together stronger than ever. Once again, Meyhu successfully defended his championship, yet nobody was angry. Well… Perhaps nobody with the exception of Kevin Miller. He was furious, and no sooner had the victory been announced that the last few henchmen under Miller’s spell ran out to obliterate Meyhu, but this time, they would not succeed. This time, one man swore on his life that he would not let them succeed once more. This time, one man would defend a nation. Trigger took a deep breath, and sprinted out into the arena. This time, things would end well. Down went the henchmen, one by one, until the ring was cleared. Trigger and Meyhu embraced, to the fans’ delight, and their friendship was official at last. But the good times would not go on forever. “Trigger? Trigger! Didn’t think I’d lie to you, did you?” All gaze was directed up towards the LOW Tron, where Kevin Miller, cast in shadows, began to speak. “The show’s almost over, Trigger. It looks like it’s time to make due on my garauntee. It looks like it’s time to reveal myself to the world.” The fans were alert as ever, waiting on the CEO. “Trigger, do you even realize how stupid you’ve been? How stupid you’ve been to not see who I am when I have been right under your nose the whole time? The game is over, Trigger. You have lost. And now, you will be over as well.” In the darkness, a grin comes over his face. “Trigger… Say goodbye to LOW… You no longer have any power here.” The lights then changed, filling the rest of his office in shadows while giving a clear close-up of the face… The face of Kevin Miller… …The face of Mike Meyhu. Not believing his own eyes, Trigger turned around to face his friend… His friend who smirked, before lifting him carelessly into the air, and dropping him with the Meyhu Meltdown. The fans were in shock. The world was in shock. Trigger was in shock as he slipped into unconsciousness… With the recording of “Kevin Miller” laughing all the while.
