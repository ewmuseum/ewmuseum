Realm hits the air, and the next show goes up... March 5, 2005, 12:10 pm After coming off of a week of many twists and turns, where not only newcomers were pushed, but the wings of LOW spread out a bit, causing for some new strides to be taken. On the past week of Realm, The Killer Pirates of the Outer Carribbean came in and beat the Flying Vergomovs for a shot at the tag team titles at the next PPV. After thus, CJ Tyler, a new guy whos' not so shy to the camera, especially with his surfer tan, defeated Tony Morrison for the #1 Contendership of the Realm Title, which is held by The Demond! Speaking of the Demond, it was one for the ages as The Demond and Donny Diamond duked it out hardcore style in the Pittsburgh Dump for the Realm Title. However, to the fans dislike, and surely to the dislike of the wrestlers themselves, they ended the match in a draw, causing the Demond to retain his title... Last but not least, John Adams faced off against Dark Haze, in what was drastically the only match in LOW History to make two non-World Title Contenders go at it, with no title on the line, in the Main Event of an LOW Realm! Was it dissapointing? HELL NO! Haze and Adams gave it their all, but in the end, it was the Haze's willingness to do anything to win that gave him the edge. Aside from the matches a few things happened backstage. Richard Rush (LOW's current owner) had been called by someone who appears to be apart of the LOW company, or is going to be soon. The details are unsure, but the fact remains that something is going down, and the story is only but beginning. S.I.N. has marked it's territory in LOW as it's leader John Adams sent S.I.N. to attack Dark Haze, but to no avail, the Haze of LOW kept on agoing, and defeated the odds, even when attacked from behind. Then their was one... extreme title belt that is! After the vicious fight between Adams and Haze, the former Extreme Title was ignited and then scorched black as John Adams lit it on fire. He may not have won, but he left his name all over the title for all to see. .... That and more happened on Realm 71, but for the full happenings, go read it yourself! Now onto next week, the show in which will be the heater for the Pay-Per-View, and will definitely lead some of LOW's newer talent into gold-deserving opputunities. Make sure not to miss it. Card for Realm 71 Goes Up! February 22, 2005, 10:33 pm 8 matches, 4 Dark, 4 Aired. It's a HUGE card, displaying a lot of LOW's new talent. This one's sure to be a blockbuster, so make sure to check out who's facing who, and get your tickets early! Realm 70 hits airs! February 22, 2005, 10:32 pm It was everything we asked for and more! Well... except a match that had to be cut short due to airing time, but with that slight glitch out of the way, LOW, is running full steam as the engines are working their best ever, and the kaboose isn't dragging us down, but speeding us up. If you haven't seen the show, make sure to do so now, cause it's one to remember! Whats in store for Realm 70? February 18, 2005, 6:07 pm After a long hard week and what was an exploding show, the fans are in for another treat Monday Night on February 21st, 2005. The show will open up at 8:30 PM with 2 dark matches. The first of the two being a triple threat between Deliverance, J.J. Sunshine, and Tony Morrison. The three of them all relevantly new to the LOW scene, and all have a shot to gain a show spot, shall they impress the LOW personals. The other dark match is between Danny Amazing and Scottywood. Danny Amazing called for a challenge last week, and in return, he’s getting his challenge answered by the one and only Scottywood. In recent weeks Scottywood has been on a decline with his matches, showing little to no admiration. A possible firing is in order shall the LOW employee not put forth more effort in coming weeks. Hopefully, he’ll do so here. As the show meets the 9:00 PM time, the show will then come on air and our match ups for air time are quiet well also. A lot of heat has added to these matches causing them to make the roster members want to prove to Philly fans why they’re going to win, and move up in the ranks of the LOW. First match of the night is the Flying Vergomovs versus the Killer Pirates From the Outer Caribbean. It’s two of LOW’s TOP teams going at it for what will truly be a spectacular finish. They both have fought tooth and nail to gain their shot at the Tag Titles, and now that they’ve achieved success, they will either flatten one another, or leave little left for the Champions to pick at. It’s a tag match made in heaven, or hell if you can think of what two pirates will do when they spot gold at bay… The next match is a singles match between a newcomer, Drake Alexander, facing off against one of LOW’s fastest uprising stars in CJ Tyler. In recent weeks CJ has been the talk of many LOW issues, mostly rumors, but doubt none he’s one of the many future stars in the business. As Drake gets to take first crack at CJ, we are only sure of how this match looks. Fans are depicting whether to like CJ or if he’s merely a push over. If he beats Drake, a star that LOW scouts say ‘has a lot of potential…’ then it may be a match nobody wishes to miss. Further on we have the No Falls Count Handicap match between Khaos and Chapel taking on The Demond, the new Realm Champion. Chapel and Khaos are friends of John ‘The Prophet’ Adams, and have a set history with the big man that not all know about. Last week we got a taste of what they wanted out of LOW via a cameraman sneaking in on them. After Adam’s lost his title in what was a very controversial match, one can only expect that this match was assigned by Adam’s buddy, and owner, Richard Rush, to make up for the humiliation that John Adams went through. With Adams on the bench, and the Demond taking on two men at once, what does it mean if Chapel and Khaos beat the Demond? Does it give them the chance to fight for the title? Or is it merely a victory of strength for S.I.N.? Finally, we get to the main event. World Heavy Weight Champion, Jason Kage faces off against Extreme Champion, Dark Haze. It’s a long time match that once resulted in a bloody beating, but now may be the ultimate push for a mid-carder, that being Haze. A long time ago these two faced off on Realm, and gave the fans a great match, but that was then… now, their older, and their better… which means? Their going to give them an even better match! Though neither title is on the line, Haze would like to prove he deserves a shot at the World Title. On the other side, Kage is rebuilding strength as he is coming back to the ring for the first time since the Pay-Per-View, due to injury. With not titles on the line, no care for their bodies, and only the top prize in the business and what this win will mean to it, what must these two be thinking? We’ll find out on Realm 70! Well obviously it wasn't scheduled for this week. Instead Realm 69 will be live on February 13th, in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania. The show will feature 4 matches, and 2 un-aired matches, that start half an hour before the show. If you wish to catch the action of some possible up-an-comers, make sure to buy a ticket and get to the arena by 9:30 PM. Otherwise the show starts at 10:00 PM, and will go on till Midnight, due to it's late night hardcore mature action that the FCC (oh god don't bring them back into the picture) have rated LOW and it's extreme nature. Yet, for those who are too poor to buy a ticket, even though they only cost $20, then you can sit back, relax, flip the channel to 1001 on your Prime Time Central Network reciever, and watch some hardcore, in your face, action as LOW brings the hard knocks back to the ring on this special Day-Before-Valetines-Day edition of Realm. Late Breaking News January 19, 2005, 1:40 pm After talking it over with Prime Time Central executives, Lights Out Wrestling will now be holding the first PTC Global Title defense on Realm 67, this upcoming Sunday. The match will feature champion Seymour Almasy, going up against the challenger Latch Key Kid. Seymour has been known for his overwhelming success in PTC sponsored events, such as the Global Title Tournament, the Cruiser Weight Tournament, multiple inter-promotional events over the past year, and multiple Global Title defenses. All in all, he’s proven to make his name worthy of holding that title, and he’s coming to fight. Latch Key Kid on the other hand is a new comer almost, though he did survive PTC’s sponsored event “GTT4” as the second to last person in the match, and so this is how he gained a shot at the PTC Global Title. However, to say he’s 100% in for the title, would be an overstatement by far. We’ll see just how good these two men can perform in an LOW ring, this Sunday. When asked of what an ‘anonymous LOW superstar’ thought of this event they responded, “It’s all the same as what we do every weekend. So what if they have a Global Title around their waste, it doesn’t give them any more respect than it would if I held the LOW Xtreme title, or the LOW Tag Belts. The title isn’t what makes the wrestler, it’s the wrestler who makes the belt worth something… I don’t see how Seymour can be labeled a great wrestler when he’s facing off against wannbe’s like the Latch Key Kid. Now come on, what kind of moron names themselves that?” After the interview the wrestler said he’d rather not be named, as he wouldn’t want Seymour to be worried if he may attack or whatever during the event, and that any exposure of LOW at the time is good, so he wouldn’t want to ruin that for his company either. For now, I’m Mike Pegani, good day folks. Vacation over... Back to the ring. The Saikyo Around LOW? April 20, 2004, 10:16 pm As the days draw near to the debut of the Saikyo System, everyone backstage in LOW is buzzing about Raizo Tenzan and Lotus coming onto the show. Word is buzzing around about the methods and motives of the Saikyo System, be they either fact or fiction. Yet who’s word can you trust? A number of statements have been gathered from various wrestling names who’ve learned of the S-Unit or have encountered them in the past, thus giving the fans something to expect from the Strongest System. Jason Marah knows the Saikyo System almost as well as anyone else; he was forced from his ownership position in a hostile takeover of The X by Raizo Tenzan and his Saikyo System. Having worked alongside Raizo in PURE, he was familiar with his style, and in The X he watched them rise up to dominance of the entire company. “The Saikyo System...fan-fuckin-tastic athletes and better "businessmen". But, as people they're complete and utter bastards. They're a perfect example of waste of lives. Maybe that’s why they've been so successful though. These lads ruined my dreams and tried to ruin me and I fucking despise the pillocks. But I've gotta respect them as wrestlers and suits...there's no denying them there. Still scum, though.” On Raizo Tenzan: “I first crossed paths with this bloke in PURE and we had two things in common. We both could wrestle and we both looked good bald. He's one of the most dangerous people not only in wrestling, but in the world. He could destroy lesser men than me in a ring...outside the ring he was worse. If ya made him look bad in the ring or took the last Coke from the vending machine I'm sure you'd probably be sleeping with the fishes in a week or so.” On Lotus: “Lotus...well, if I was ten years younger. *cheeky smile* Talented grappler, great lookin' girl but fuckin hell, she is such a bloody bitch!” Former PCW wrestler Rent-A-Hero is another who’s familiar with Raizo’s methods. In The X it was Raizo who got RAH’s friend Marissa hooked on the illegal aphrodisiac, Big O, and prostituted her on the streets of Tokyo. Despite his acts of revenge against Raizo, RAH was defeated when the nVg Akiyama was brought in to crush him. Tracking RAH down to his place of retirement (location disclosed for protection from super villains), he merely had one thing to say: “More evil and violent then all issues of the Maximum Carnage series combined” Iron Lung, current LOW superstar, was caught backstage the last Realm and given a brief synopsis of the Saikyo System, and afterwards had these words: “Well ill say that they seem to be up and coming stars, they have a lot of potential, and also offer a lot to the future of the company. I look foreword to seeing what they can do” Recently crowned PTC Global Champion, Jesse Jamester, has always been a known fixture in LOW, so when he heard word that the Saikyo System were coming into the promotion he had this to say: “These two are like a wind storm. They're going to tear LOW up and down shaping it a whole new look! I expect to see some killer action and possibly gold around their waists in no time! Whether you have Raizo going singles or Lotus taking on someone, both of them will show that they've got the talent to send shockwaves through any company. This is the reason why LOW's so good, a little bit of spice added in with a bunch of skill, and you've got the newest caliber of greatness running through the tag ranks, the Saikyo System.” Of course not all feedback was positive, a few members of the backstage locker room staying true to their colors with their comments. Jason Ortiz: “System? What's that? Something I can smoke right? If it has anything to do with the SYSTEM of CRACK...count me in!" It’s Casey Sanders’ profession to give his opinion on things, being one of the head commentators of LOW alongside Joey Donaldo. He got the memo straight from the boss about their entry, and had a bit to say about the System. "What I think about them coming to LOW? Well, what can I say? They have proved in other federations that they can handle their own, whether they be alone or together. I personally can't wait for Tenzan to come in. I have heard great things about him, and I hope he will not let down. The little Asian better be hardcore to the bone and either bleed or make others bleed, because we all know that, that's the only thing I really enjoy (nervous laugh). “As for the other ... what's her name? Lotus, yeah, well she's hot, I think that is the first thing that pops to anyone's mind. I mean, did you see the jugs on her? (makes hand sign that suggests large breasts) She can sure pin me any night, you know? Ha ha ha, in all seriousness, if she is anything like Kimi Aikohana, that other fine Asian, then I have nothing to worry about. I have heard great things from my partner Jerry Donaldo, but I am still trying to figure out why he never told me she had such a fine ass. Ha Ha Ha... You think it will piss Tenzan off? I should shut up, yeah? Bah, all in all, they are a great add to our roster, and hopefully, they can strip the god awful super hero nerds of their titles and give the tag belts some dignity. Fucking super-galactic pencil pushers!” Upon the arrival of the Saiko System to Lights Out Wrestling a few superstars have given their thoughts and feelings of this new tag team. One of those men was Cyrus Skar, when asked how he felt he gave this statement: “The Saiko System? They interest me not, I have a lot on my mind at the moment; I need not spend my time wondering about trivial things. I have an important match against Jason Ortiz to prepare for and I have other personal issues to deal with. Whether they have talent or not also does not enter into any equation with me involved. For I do not see myself finding a partner and becoming a tag team in the future, I am and always will be a singles wrestler. Now if you’ll leave me be, I have important matters to attend to.” It was clear that Cyrus did not wish to comment much on the Saiko System, but it was obvious that he showed a hint of admiration for the tag team, as he knew what they were capable of. He may have made out that he did not care, but he watched everybody closely. He trusted nobody and liked to know his enemies. He’d keep his eye on them for now… In conclusion, one cannot make it into LOW alone without the personal opinion of the boss themselves. Chrystal Dawn Fellini, the new owner of LOW, hired the Saikyo System personally sight unseen, knowing of their abilities immediately. Her words are high of the duo, not a surprising thing at all. "What do I think of the Saikyo System? Heh... Well, y'know, it's always a different perspective when you know people in this business both inside and out of the ring. I've done a bit of *ahem* dealing with these guys during my marraige to Kazuyin, and all I can really say is that true brutality is not so much a sin, as it is an advantage. I suspect these two will be a great asset to the LOW atmosphere, especially the tag team ranks." "Raizo... Hmm... Well, he's charismatic, always knows what he wants, and is willing to go to an extreme to obtain his goals. A man of such attitude could indeed be trouble, but I don't think his trouble will lie in the foundation of LOW, as much as it will in opposing teams coming out on top." "I've not had the chance to personally meet Lotus. I have seen her in action before though. It's really kind of scary, that I see an anger in her that I may have been the only one to hold inside. I may be wrong though, but if she applies that anger to productive manners, she may go far." There you have the reviews of the debuting Saikyo System. It’ll be soon that we see what exactly they’re capable of once they hit the LOW ring. Saikyo System Omens? There’s a few backstage in LOW that suspect they might be here for the same reason. Word’s already spreading that there’s solid connection between the Saikyo System and the Akiohana clan in Japan, and their business here in LOW expands further than the ring.
