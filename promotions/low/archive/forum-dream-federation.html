No longer caring about what the fans think of his actions, Tristan Belmont has sworn to end Almasy’s career so something like this can never happen again. Jesse Jamester vs. Luke Justice- After it was decided that these two men would compete for the #1 contender ship to the World Title, a draw in the match re-sparked a previously thought dead feud between the two. This time around however there is more then just hatred for the other driving them on, this time there is a shot at the World Title hanging in the balance. Jason Warrior vs. Jason Ortiz- After failing to pick up key wins in tag matches, the two fan favorites of the LOW decide to call it quits as a team. Like most tag team break-ups however, this one is not pretty. Warrior blames Ortiz for tarnishing his flawless record due to his undisciplined ways and habitual drug use. Ortiz however sees this as Jason Warrior trying to control him and make him change his ways. This begins a gigantic feud that divides the fan’s in half as some of them cheer for the rebellious Ortiz while others root for the admirable ways of Warrior. Homo/Phobic vs. The Metallo Zords- In a stroke of ingenuity, the Metallo Zords are able to gain a win over the tandem of Homo/Phobic when the Bum Boozler goes for his very unusual raping finisher only to connect with a metallic chastity belt of one of the Zords. This causes the unsuccessful Boozler to become obsessed with completing his mission in some way, shape, or form. Of course Shane Stone wants no part of this but the Boozler can be very persuasive and brings him along for the ride anyway. Callisto Bane vs. Terry Woods- The co-GM’s of the LOW product, both have a history of being very controversial. As both attempt to make the show the best under the LOW name, a feud sparks as to which one can come up with the most controversial program ever witnessed on broadcast television. Will one of the two come out on top or will the FCC shut down the LOW before that happens?
