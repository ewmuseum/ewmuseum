Match Stipulations (April 2003)

captured 18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Warrior Match The first wrestler to be not moving for thirty seconds loses Hell's Fury Match Takes place in the Boiler Room, with gasoline spread everywhere. The first wrestler to find matches that are hidden in the boiler room, and then light the room on fire, and then escape the boiler room, wins. C-4 Explosives Match At a random interval in the match, the ring will explode with C-4 Explosives. Junkyard Brawl Match takes place in a junkyard...anything goes. Scaffold Minefield Match above the ring hangs a scaffold the size of the ring, except there is a big square hole in the middle of the scaffold. in the ring are c-4 explosives and if any wrestlers falls onto the mat they will detinate the explosive they hit. No Rope Barbed Wire Deathmatch There are no ropes on the ring and around the outside of the ring lay barb wire. Thumbtac Board Match Outside the ring are boards filled with thumbtacs. King Of The Mountain Match Takes place in a junkyard. A 60 foot metal "mountain" is assembled, with one person starting out on top of it. The other opponent(s) try to climb the mountain, and knock the person on top off. The person who is on the top after 30 minutes becomes The King Of The Mountain Cliff Hanger Match Takes place on a cliff, all wrestlers are wearing mountain climbing gear. The point of the match is to climb up to the next ledge of the cliff, and grab the briefcase or title hanging there. Wrestlers can also throw opponents off the cliff(they are attached to a cord). Barbed Wire Flaming Tables Match Object of the match is to wrap opponent in barbed wire, set a table on fire, and throw the opponent through the table. Extreme Warrior Match Used only for Triple Threat Warrior/Xtreme championship matches. It is a two falls match. The objective is to render your opponent helpless for 30 seconds(as is the Warrior Match). The first fall, there is glass, tacs, and several other weaponds in the ring. The second fall, it is the same, but now the ropes are electrified, and electricute those who touch them. First fall awards the Warrior championship, second fall awards the Xtreme championship. No Rope Barbed Wire Cage Match There are no ropes on the ring, which is inside a cage. The cage steel is lined with barbed wire. First pinfall, submission, or knockout wins. Tables And Ladders Xtreme Match Inside the ring are three ladders, a ten foot ladder, a forty foot ladder, and a forty five foot ladder. There are also five tables. Pinfall wins. Lightning Dome Match Takes place on a giant slab of cement. Covering the cement, is a huge dome of barbed wire. When the match begins, the barbed wire is lit on fire, and continues to burn throughout the matchup. Ultimate Ironman Match The main object of this match is the same as any-whoever can score the most vicories in sixty minutes is the winner. But in the Ultimate IronMan match, that 60 minutes is broken down. For the first 15 minutes, anything goes, pinfalls, submissions, etc. Then, after the 30 minute mark, only submissions are allowed. Another 15 minutes later, only knockouts are allowed. And with 5 minutes to go, everything goes once again. Ultimate Tapei Deathmatch There is sharp glass glued to the ring ropes, as well as shattered glass everywhere outside the ring. Scaffold Deathmatch The scaffold is covered with a dark sheet. Somewhere(unknown to the wrestlers) in the scaffold is a hole. The first person to fall through the hole loses. The ring below is filled with tacs, all facing points up. Lights Out Match The wrestlers wrestle in a glass cube above the ring. The object of the match is to throw your opponent through the glass wall, and let them fall down to the ring below. Thirty minutes into the matchup, the cube is released, and falls to the mat below, and the match is declared a draw. Aftermath MatchTakes place in the boiler room. At random increments of time, smoke bombs and explosives will fall from the ceiling. Also, the room is filled with boxes, some containing weapons, some containing more explosives. No way to escape, winner is declared only by knockout. Tower Of TerrorTakes place in the Three Tier Cage. 10 minutes into the match, the top cage explodes. 20 minutes into the match, the middle cage explodes. Bottom cage is made of bamboo. After 30 minutes, it is lit on fire. Winner is declared by pinfall, submission, or TKO. Pinfall, submission, or TKO must take place inside the bottom cage.
