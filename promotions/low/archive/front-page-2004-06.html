Cut back on television time... and Realm 52! June 6, 2004, 1:15 am After a long two weeks of LOW being absent from television marketing, it’s finally back. However for the next couple of weeks LOW’s feature show, REALM will only appear for one hour on your local broadcasting network. We here at LOW would like to inform you that we’ll be getting to our regular routine in no time. So if you would, please hold in their as LOW promises to give you the same intense action but with more explosiveness. With that said, Realm 52 is to take place on June 12, 2004. It’ll feature four matches, a six man tag, a match for the extreme title, and a match for the number one contender ship of the extreme title. It’s definitely going to be a blockbuster to see so make sure to tune in and find out what happens! Realm 51 Finally Airs... June 3, 2004, 7:40 pm Due to a problem with the connections of LOW and it's television network provider, LOW's Realm 51 was delayed 2 weeks and aired finally on January 03, 2004. New champions crowned, including the PTC Global Title, and sparks flying everywhere from the match made by Chryste of the 2 falls match at the PPV that will decided who is the World Champion and who is the new LOW Owner. Now however, it’s time for the aftermath of Realm. All that and more may take place, but for now, check out the card to see what will happen in the ring. Just click on the TV dropdown and checkout LOW’s recent online update of the next REALM! On a night where the Realm, eXtreme and Tag Team Title were defended, who came out on top? Who came back to LOW on this very special night? Who left LOW? What happened you ask? Check it out! The 50th Edition ... just a click away! Realm 50 on the way! April 26, 2004, 9:37 pm About two years ago, the very first edition of Realm went up. LOW was born on a cold winter night, and next week, LOW celebrates its life by celebrating half a century of shows. Many have speculated as to what would happen on that night, and the new president Chryste has made sure that this night would be a memorable one, booking every champion to defend their title belts, as well as a very special Hall of Fame Inductee Ceremony. Chryste promised that this card would shake up LOW, and she made sure it would. In the very first match of the night, she pits two enemies with one another against two LOW newcomers. Indeed, in a Tornado Tag Match set to happen in a Cage, Chastity will be paired up with the Hangman Aaron Matthewson against Legacy and Joseph Magnus. Will the power struggle between both men continue, or will they be able to cooperate and beat the newcomers. Chryste has promised great things for the newcomers, and has warned the other two that a loss could results in termination of their Pay Per View match. When asked what she meant by that, the president simply replied: “I have no place for slackers. Either you work, or you can take the curb” Returning Tyrone Johnson will go one on one against Jason Warrior in a rematch for their stellar Pay Per View match just before LOW closed. No one really knew who was the best, and this week, we will find out. Following that will be a match that was booked last week by none other than Chryste, Luke Justice will take on Ty Baron in a Loser Leaves Town Lumberjack Match. The Pretty Hate Machine did not like the comments made towards her by both individuals and decided to pit them both against one another in an all or nothing type of deal. Will the Shadow Rider return to what he once was, or will the former BDW champion send the Rider packing? The very first Title match of the night will happen when the Metallo Zords will defend their tag team titles against the team consisted of Lotus and Raizo Tenzan, collectively known as the Saikyo System. Both teams have made quite the impression on Chryste, and now, she has decided that she would say what would happen if they were paired up against one another. With the Platinum Ranger and Luke Justice lurking around, the Metallo Zords are in no way safe, but what can be said about the System’s problem? You have to be certain that Angelus Sorrow will be watching closely. All the elements point to disaster … so let the disaster be! Then, the new Number One Contender to the Realm Title will take on the champion Cyrus Skar. A lot of hype has been forwarded towards Malik Katsuya, most of it stemming off from his weird actions towards Jackie Bust and Alicia Assets. One thing is for certain, the man who banished the team to the ‘Shadow Realm’ is one demented person. This week, he takes on one of the most vile person on the roster in Cyrus Skar. Skar has gone through too many people to lose his title now, but could his time be near? The Realm Title is on the line… And in the Main-Event, will Iron Lung ever get a rest? Last week he beat the man that became the Number One Contender in Fathom, and this week, by order of Chryste, he has to beat the man that beat the Number One Contender in Angelus Sorrow. Again, Sorrow has to deal with the Saykio System, but Lung has to deal with Skar. Do not think that just because it has been calm between both men that the storm has settled. Angelus Sorrow and Iron Lungw ill go head to head in an Ultimate Tapei Deathmatch to determine the eXtreme Champion. Chryste promised to bring back Tristan Belmont, and this week, she pulled out all the stops as she announced that on the 50th Edition of Realm, she will hold a very special ceremony, bring Belmont into the Hall of Fame along names like Lotus and Mike Meyhu. When asked about it, the president only answered with this: 'Not only is he the epitome of what LOW once was, but how could a Hall of Fame induction keep him away from all these fans?!" The question remains, will Belmont break his vow of silence and step out into the arena he once made so great? Or will he remain in the shadows and continue to ignore the plea of his fans and of our new found President? The Saikyo Around LOW? April 20, 2004, 10:16 pm As the days draw near to the debut of the Saikyo System, everyone backstage in LOW is buzzing about Raizo Tenzan and Lotus coming onto the show. Word is buzzing around about the methods and motives of the Saikyo System, be they either fact or fiction. Yet who’s word can you trust? A number of statements have been gathered from various wrestling names who’ve learned of the S-Unit or have encountered them in the past, thus giving the fans something to expect from the Strongest System. Jason Marah knows the Saikyo System almost as well as anyone else; he was forced from his ownership position in a hostile takeover of The X by Raizo Tenzan and his Saikyo System. Having worked alongside Raizo in PURE, he was familiar with his style, and in The X he watched them rise up to dominance of the entire company. “The Saikyo System...fan-fuckin-tastic athletes and better "businessmen". But, as people they're complete and utter bastards. They're a perfect example of waste of lives. Maybe that’s why they've been so successful though. These lads ruined my dreams and tried to ruin me and I fucking despise the pillocks. But I've gotta respect them as wrestlers and suits...there's no denying them there. Still scum, though.” On Raizo Tenzan: “I first crossed paths with this bloke in PURE and we had two things in common. We both could wrestle and we both looked good bald. He's one of the most dangerous people not only in wrestling, but in the world. He could destroy lesser men than me in a ring...outside the ring he was worse. If ya made him look bad in the ring or took the last Coke from the vending machine I'm sure you'd probably be sleeping with the fishes in a week or so.” On Lotus: “Lotus...well, if I was ten years younger. *cheeky smile* Talented grappler, great lookin' girl but fuckin hell, she is such a bloody bitch!” Former PCW wrestler Rent-A-Hero is another who’s familiar with Raizo’s methods. In The X it was Raizo who got RAH’s friend Marissa hooked on the illegal aphrodisiac, Big O, and prostituted her on the streets of Tokyo. Despite his acts of revenge against Raizo, RAH was defeated when the nVg Akiyama was brought in to crush him. Tracking RAH down to his place of retirement (location disclosed for protection from super villains), he merely had one thing to say: “More evil and violent then all issues of the Maximum Carnage series combined” Iron Lung, current LOW superstar, was caught backstage the last Realm and given a brief synopsis of the Saikyo System, and afterwards had these words: “Well ill say that they seem to be up and coming stars, they have a lot of potential, and also offer a lot to the future of the company. I look foreword to seeing what they can do” Recently crowned PTC Global Champion, Jesse Jamester, has always been a known fixture in LOW, so when he heard word that the Saikyo System were coming into the promotion he had this to say: “These two are like a wind storm. They're going to tear LOW up and down shaping it a whole new look! I expect to see some killer action and possibly gold around their waists in no time! Whether you have Raizo going singles or Lotus taking on someone, both of them will show that they've got the talent to send shockwaves through any company. This is the reason why LOW's so good, a little bit of spice added in with a bunch of skill, and you've got the newest caliber of greatness running through the tag ranks, the Saikyo System.” Of course not all feedback was positive, a few members of the backstage locker room staying true to their colors with their comments. Jason Ortiz: “System? What's that? Something I can smoke right? If it has anything to do with the SYSTEM of CRACK...count me in!" It’s Casey Sanders’ profession to give his opinion on things, being one of the head commentators of LOW alongside Joey Donaldo. He got the memo straight from the boss about their entry, and had a bit to say about the System. "What I think about them coming to LOW? Well, what can I say? They have proved in other federations that they can handle their own, whether they be alone or together. I personally can't wait for Tenzan to come in. I have heard great things about him, and I hope he will not let down. The little Asian better be hardcore to the bone and either bleed or make others bleed, because we all know that, that's the only thing I really enjoy (nervous laugh). “As for the other ... what's her name? Lotus, yeah, well she's hot, I think that is the first thing that pops to anyone's mind. I mean, did you see the jugs on her? (makes hand sign that suggests large breasts) She can sure pin me any night, you know? Ha ha ha, in all seriousness, if she is anything like Kimi Aikohana, that other fine Asian, then I have nothing to worry about. I have heard great things from my partner Jerry Donaldo, but I am still trying to figure out why he never told me she had such a fine ass. Ha Ha Ha... You think it will piss Tenzan off? I should shut up, yeah? Bah, all in all, they are a great add to our roster, and hopefully, they can strip the god awful super hero nerds of their titles and give the tag belts some dignity. Fucking super-galactic pencil pushers!” Upon the arrival of the Saiko System to Lights Out Wrestling a few superstars have given their thoughts and feelings of this new tag team. One of those men was Cyrus Skar, when asked how he felt he gave this statement: “The Saiko System? They interest me not, I have a lot on my mind at the moment; I need not spend my time wondering about trivial things. I have an important match against Jason Ortiz to prepare for and I have other personal issues to deal with. Whether they have talent or not also does not enter into any equation with me involved. For I do not see myself finding a partner and becoming a tag team in the future, I am and always will be a singles wrestler. Now if you’ll leave me be, I have important matters to attend to.” It was clear that Cyrus did not wish to comment much on the Saiko System, but it was obvious that he showed a hint of admiration for the tag team, as he knew what they were capable of. He may have made out that he did not care, but he watched everybody closely. He trusted nobody and liked to know his enemies. He’d keep his eye on them for now… In conclusion, one cannot make it into LOW alone without the personal opinion of the boss themselves. Chrystal Dawn Fellini, the new owner of LOW, hired the Saikyo System personally sight unseen, knowing of their abilities immediately. Her words are high of the duo, not a surprising thing at all. "What do I think of the Saikyo System? Heh... Well, y'know, it's always a different perspective when you know people in this business both inside and out of the ring. I've done a bit of *ahem* dealing with these guys during my marraige to Kazuyin, and all I can really say is that true brutality is not so much a sin, as it is an advantage. I suspect these two will be a great asset to the LOW atmosphere, especially the tag team ranks." "Raizo... Hmm... Well, he's charismatic, always knows what he wants, and is willing to go to an extreme to obtain his goals. A man of such attitude could indeed be trouble, but I don't think his trouble will lie in the foundation of LOW, as much as it will in opposing teams coming out on top." "I've not had the chance to personally meet Lotus. I have seen her in action before though. It's really kind of scary, that I see an anger in her that I may have been the only one to hold inside. I may be wrong though, but if she applies that anger to productive manners, she may go far." There you have the reviews of the debuting Saikyo System. It’ll be soon that we see what exactly they’re capable of once they hit the LOW ring. An explosive Realm April 10, 2004, 1:03 pm The show had to be delayed due to major complications, but when it aired, how it shocked the fans and shook the foundations that Lights Out Wrestling stood on. Fans came in groups to find the answer to one question “Who was the president?” and they got their answer. In what can be labeled as the biggest surprise to ever air on LOW television, former DWO World Champion and Mega-Superstar Chryste made her appearance for the first time on LOW soil. Jaws were dropped before she could even state her business. Also, a former 4WW made his debut on Realm, and even though he did not have a match, that did not stop him from being on the receiving end of a beating, from the unlikeliest of all perpetrators. And in the main-event, for the second time in two weeks, Iron Lung squared off against Cyrus Skar. Could the eXtreme Champion prove that the week before was a fluke, or did Skar prove dominance again? All that and much much MUCH more happened on that week’s edition of Realm. April 6, 2004, 1:28 pm With the anticipated debut of the Saikyo System in LOW, many people are starting to wonder the outcome of having the ruthless pair of Raizo Tenzan and Lotus in the company will be. Their track record speaks for themselves; opponents left in their aftermath, opponents not showing up for work the next week, and companies financially drained while their accounts swell like the black eyes they dish out. There’s a few backstage in LOW that suspect they might be here for the same reason. Word’s already spreading that there’s solid connection between the Saikyo System and the Akiohana clan in Japan, and their business here in LOW expands further than the ring. At this point no one knows except Raizo Tenzan himself… New Breed of Talent Comes to LOW April 6, 2004, 1:27 pm On this upcoming edition of LOW Realm, Light’s Out fans will bear witness to the LOW debut of the pairing of Raizo Tenzan and Lotus, the Japanese independent duo known around as the Saikyo System. Normally when one hears that the Saikyo System is coming to their promotion, most people get edgy. The Saikyo System is synonymous for in ring domination due to its talented numbers and industrial genius thanks to their mastermind Raizo Tenzan. In PURE the Saikyo System dominated the ring with Raizo at the helm, and when the company closed many rumors floated about that Raizo Tenzan had a hand in the financial collapse of the company. And when the gang ventured into American and into the rising independent promotion The X, it was there that they stomped the competition to the ground and then ran off with both the ownership to The X as well as its money. Things are different now that Raizo Tenzan and Lotus are in LOW, however. No longer does the self-admitted yakuza gang travel in numbers, but only as a pair, trio at most with Raizo’s girlfriend and former porn star turned director Yuka Hamada by his side. Their presence inside LOW will be a great asset to the company, no doubt. Raizo Tenzan is an accomplished technician and a mighty powerhouse, standing 6’4 and 289 pounds of solid muscle. And opposing his might is his cousin, the 5’3 123 pound “Kogeru Grrl” Lotus, not to be mistaken by LOW’s own Lotus. Some have called her a hardcore prodigy, doing things with weapons that many have never seen before, and taking damage that most men have shied away from. Alongside this thirst for violence is her ability in the martial arts, her stiff-limb strikes enough to stagger larger men and it’s those qualities that make her a savant in this business at the young age of 17. Little is known about this family pair except their talent, their cunning, and their ruthlessness. But LOW will now witness them with their own eyes as they take on Legacy and Joseph Magnus in tag team action. Will these two be the first to be made examples of by the Strongest System? The Past Becomes the Present March 12, 2004, 5:03 pm Tonight many people lived a long time remembrance of the federation that once brought them warriors to a ring to fight it out in honor of the people. Blood was poured in the past, guts were twisted and turned with the drop of a finisher, and in the end tears would be shed over these warriors passion. It was a time of power and a time when men and women of the LOW walls would do anything to win.
