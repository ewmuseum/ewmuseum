Tristan Belmont Real Name: Remy Belmont Birthdate: February 19th 1973 Hometown: Montreal, Quebec, Canada Height: 6 feet 8 Weight: 308 pounds Nicknames: The Demonic Warrior Status: Heel Entrance Music: 'Bring Me To Life' by Evanescence Information Wrestling Style: Technical and Powerhouse Setup Move: Darkness Come (Irish whip to the corner. Belmont grabs his opponent and sits him ontop of the third turnbuckle in the corner. Belmont steps out of the ring and stands on the ring apron. He grabs the man by his neck and chokeslams him from the third rope to the outside of the ring. Belmont himself jumps off with the man, never releasing the neck, adding more weight. ) Finisher: Reocurring Nightmare (Kick to the gut. Belmont then places his opponent up like he would do an Outsider's edge, having his opponent rest like he were on a cross. Belmont then pushes back with all his strenght, sending his opponent in the air. While the man is coming down, Belmont grabs the back of his neck and applies a backwards Stunner, practically breaking the man's neck. Finisher#2: The Prophecy: Belmont grabs his opponent by the neck and starts choking him, while both men are standing. He brings two of his fingers under the Adam's apple, and then touches both those fingers from under the Adam's apple. Just as his opponent is about to tap out from lack of air, Belmont raises him up in the air, and brings him down hard with a high impact chokeslam. He does not let go of the hold until the man has crashed down hard into the canvas and has stopped moving. Finisher #3: Battle Cry: Tristan Belmont throws the man between his legs and hooks his arms up as if he would perform a Pedigree. He then pulls back with all his strength and pulls the man up so he comes on Belmont's shoulders, before slamming him down to the mat with all his might with a Power Bomb (Variation between Pedigree and Power Bomb).)
