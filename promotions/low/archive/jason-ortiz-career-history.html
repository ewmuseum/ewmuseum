Career History Jason Ortiz's Entrance: “Dirt Off Your Shoulder” hits the air waves, and immediately, the crowd stands up, as they realize that it is once again, everyone’s favorite Crack Head. Once the first verse hits, everyone goes wild. I probably owe it to y'all, proud to be locked by the force Tryin to hustle some things, that go with the Porsche Feelin no remorse, feelin like my hand was forced The lights begin to dim down to a light yellow color- the fans still screaming out of the top of there lungs, the women screaming even louder than the men. Everyone looks at the ramp, there is no one person in the arena that is not staring at the ramp way. Jason Ortiz walks out, smile on his face. Middle finger to the Lord, nigga grip I'm a boss Stab the ladies they love me, from the bleachers they screamin All the ballers is bouncin they like the way I be leanin All the rappers be hatin, off the track that I'm makin But all the hustlers they love it just to see one of us make it Came from the bottom the bottom, to the top of the pots Now to his right he turns and poses. The yellow lights begin to spin in different directions, and once he steps foot out of the back, the side of the ramp way shoots out bright yellow pyro’s that go screaming in the air, and only a few seconds later, white ones follow those. The pyro’s play over and over a few times, until Jason gets halfway onto the ramp. Nigga London, Japan and I'm straight off the block Like a running back, get it man, I'm straight off the block I can run it back nigga cause I'm straight with the Roc You gotta get, that, dirt off your shoulder You gotta get, that, dirt off your shoulder Finally he turns forward and looks down the ramp and poses with his right arm in the air, flipping off the crowd. They love it when he does that. The pyro’s shoot down, and a big white spot light shines down on Jason Ortiz. He continues to walk down the ramp, the fans doing everything what it takes to get his attention. Some try and yell cuss words at him. Some yell for Ortiz to look at there signs. They try everything. You gotta get, that, dirt off your shoulder You gotta get, that, dirt off your shoulder Your homey Hov' in position, in the kitchen with soda I just whipped up a watch, tryin to get me a Rover Tryin to stretch out the coca, like a wrestler, yessir He gets down to the ring, and turns back around, his body facing towards the ramp way, once he does that, yellow and white sparks fly up from the middle of the ramp way, splashing the fans with delight. Jason turns back around, as his face expression turns into a grin. He slides in the ring, and moves around there a few times, not touching the ropes with his body. Keep the Heckler close, you know them smokers'll test ya But like, fifty-two cards when I'm, I'm through dealin Now fifty-two bars come it, now you feel 'em Now, fifty-two cars roll out, remove ceiling In case fifty-two broads come out, now you chillin with a boss bitch of course S.C. on the sleeve At the 40/40 club, ESPN on the screen I paid a grip for the jeans, plus the slippers is clean No chrome on the wheels, I'm a grown-up for real
