Adam Stryker — LOW career history

captured 13 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Career History Stryker was born Adam Lukas Dyas. He was born into a very rich family. His father was a successful lawyer as was his grandfather. Born in LA Adam grew up in the nice neighborhoods and went to the better schools of the area. He began his wrestling career at the age of 10. He used it as a means to vent his frustrations. He would never fight anyone on the playground or after school but he would always make the kid he was wrestling feel the pain. Adam was a very good and successful wrestler. He had won numerous tournaments in the LA area and was considered one of the top wrestlers in the state upon entering HS. But his parents had other plans for him. They pulled him from the public school system and placed him in a very prestigious private school. He was crushed and he thought that his wrestling career was over before it even had the chance to begin. Adam soon got his chance as the wrestling coach saw him around the campus. Being only 100 pounds it made him very useful for the struggling team. He agreed to wrestle and was very good. He wrestled all four years he was in the school and put up a very impressive 159-2 record. He won four consecutive state championships at 103, 119, 145 and 189 while beating charges of steroids from other wrestlers in his league on more than one occasion. When his HS career came to end Adam received a letter from the coach of the UCLA team. He was offered a full scholarship to the school and happily accepted. But before his first match for UCLA he was involved in a fight with an upper classman. Adam suffered a cut that wen down the middle of his right eye causing a scar that was later removed by surgery. The other kid landed himself in the hospital. The coach siding with the upper classman kicked Adam off of the team and he was eventually expelled from the school. His dreams were crushed. This is when he met JL Knight. The two became friends and Knight convinced Adam to try his hand at professional wrestling. It was also Knight that convinced him to drop his real last name and pick up Stryker. JL got Stryker his first real break in the MEWO. But shortly after that fed closed down and Stryker was left with no where to go. He dropped Knight and decided to go it alone. He first took a tour of Japan and watched and learned from the hardcore wrestlers of the Japanese federations. He spent a couple of years there and came back here with a new drive and a new desire. But he came home to terrible news; both of his parents had been killed in a car accident. He slowly slumped into a terrible depression that ultimately ended with an attempt at taking his own life. He tried cutting the veins in his forearms but was discovered by his family's butler who got him to a hospital just in time. Adam has since gotten matching tattoos over the scars that were left behind. Adam, at his lowest with nowhere to go, was befriended by a man known as Neo Titan. Neo Titan, as he was known in the EHWF his real name being Simon, took Adam under his wing and got him into the EHWF where Adam flourished in the extremely hardcore setting. After a couple of years in the EHWF, he moved onto the AWR and spent a good year and a half there spreading his name and making his fan base grow. After leaving the AWR and feeling burnt out he took sometime off of wrestling to rest his body and his mind. He took another trip to Japan and finally relaxed for the first time since his parent's sudden and tragic death. Upon his return from Japan he was given an offer from Callisto Bane to wrestle in her upstart new promotion the DWO. He happily accepted and with a renewed vigor he entertained like never before. He, along with a few others, helped to build the DWO up to become one of the major players of the PTC. But Adam, at the age of 27, felt that it was time for him to move on. He had mastered the DWO capturing the World Title and proven himself to be the man on top. He started looking elsewhere and that is when the PCW came calling. Adam left the DWO for the ranks of the PCW. He had limited success in PCW, capturing the Extreme Title, but soon lost his drive for the business and left. He attempted a comeback in the DWO under the name of Kaos, playing a masked mute managed by Jaslene Myst. The DWO closed it's doors before Adam could reveal himself though. Soon after the DWO closed down, Adam decided it was time to take a break. He walked away and thought he wouldn't be looking back. It wasn't until Adam was approached with an offer to join the FAW that he considered coming back. He saw that the FAW was in a place similiar to the DWO when he first arrived there. Never one to pass on a challenge Adam decided to join the FAW in hopes of proving to himself that his stints in the AWR and the DWO were not flukes. The FAW closed it's doors before Adam could get his first match which has led him to LOW. And thus a new chapter in his professional career is about to unfold.
