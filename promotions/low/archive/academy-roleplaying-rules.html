Academy: Roleplaying Rules

captured 11 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
>>Navigation Main • Graduation • Tutorials • Forums Roleplaying • Rules • Grading Matchwriting • Rules • Grading Segment Writing • Rules • Grading Rules In the roleplay-section of LOA (Lights Out Academy), there are two different sets of rules. The first set and most important are Laws. The Laws are those in which you must follow at all time or else you'll be fired on the spot. Guidelines are less important and are looked at as a side source to keep up to date on and focus around the promos/role-plays themselves. The rules may change at given times, so please check in, however the head chief of LOA will usually inform when rules change. These are the Laws of LOA. When writing promos/role-plays you must always use your character at an In-Character (IC) stand point. (Ex: Your character hates a man named Logan, you do not talk as though he is your friend like you would on AIM.) Never post IC messages on the OOC board; as well never post OOC messages on the Promo's Board. This is a STRICT policy! First time is a warning; second time your fired. Never use the Staff Officials such as Owner, President, Vice President, Commissioner, VIP, or any other staff official of these importance's throughout the LOW or LOA community in your promos/role-plays. If so you will be fired on the spot no matter how you have used them. Do not use promos previously made in past federations. Your first promo/role-play should be made specifically for LOA. If in fact you do this we will result in termination of your stay in LOA. We feel you should never do this, for the efed you did the promo/role-play for has possession of it and it'd be considered stealing. Result of not obeying is automatic termination of LOA stay. If at any time you contact the owner asking them for the following you will be warned, then banned from the LOA. Subjects: Posers, Results, Card, Grading of Role-play/promo, Site Updating, Recruitment, to be a staff member, and/or Title Shot. Anything that can be answered on the OOC board should be posted there. Things that you may contact the staff for include personal notes, getting help with promos, personal problems, and anything related with your character and others. We stress this rule quite much for if you do not follow you will upset the graders and those who grade do it on there own time, so respect there decisions and efforts. Never complain about your grade. That's basically the bottom line. If you feel you have a valid point then you may contact that grader through PM or Email but do not ever come off with a nasty temper or they have the right to contact a higher official and have you banned. As stated before, those who grade role-plays do it on there own time; so respect their decisions and efforts. Respect your peers. Each handler has their own style, own opinion, and own right to do as they wish within the boundaries of these rules. So for that you shall respect their efforts. We only want helpful people in LOA. If you insult or put down another I guarantee you will not get far. Therefore, check your attitude at the door. These are the Guidelines of LOA. Promos/Role-plays may be any length but we stress that you are graded upon the length of your promo/role-play so you should aim to get your point across and not ramble on. The grade you get is permanent unless a valid fact proves that the grader was wrong, or his math had been calculated incorrectly. We wish that you not do In-ring, Arena, or any promo/role-play that has to do with the LOA arena area what so ever. These are considered HIGHLY unoriginal and we only inform this so you get the best out of your promos/role-plays. IF you desire to, do them as an in-card, however those two are graded. You must post a promo/role-play once a week at least or you'll be fired. No exceptions. (NOTE: You may re-join LOA up to 5 times before being banned unless fired for reasons under the Laws section.) No Replies to people's post on the Grading or the Promo/Role-Playing boards. This applies to members only, staff officials allowed. Do not ask for Posers, you will get one when you are accepted into LOW and have proven to be loyal. Try to work hard. The harder you work the better you'll get. This is not really a rule it's just a note. Those who have fun and help out get far, those who want others do to the work for them end up losing. Some of the best people in an academy are brought together because they share an understanding for this game. I like it and I will say it's fun. Sometimes it is work, but when it's done it's worth the effort. You'll love LOW if you enjoy the Academy. We put on a regular show using a simulator however sometimes we will edit it. Good luck EVERYONE!
