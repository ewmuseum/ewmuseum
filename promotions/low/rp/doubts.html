Doubts

22 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Doubts. Yeah, even I have doubts. Who to thought that a man of my caliber would have them? Well, I do have a big match coming up at the pay-per-view called Pulse, LOW’s first pay-per-view since the federation opened its doors earlier this year. I have a Triple Threat match for the number one contendership for the eXtreme Championship. Oh, this should be fun...I’m going up against Blue Coral and the returning Fathom. Oh, this should be a big day for me. Most people think I won’t win. Fuck them...I don’t need their comments or opinions about this match. A. Kreed lost his first match, ladies and gentlemen, and he’s pissed! Shut up... My doubts have resurfaced when I lost my first match, a humiliating defeat, I might add. Master Hoshi trips Kreed on the outside! Tatsumi Rishimon takes the advantage! Damn that master... Rishimon Special! What a move! One...two...three! A. Kreed lost his first match, and he’s pissed! We get the picture! Sorry. Anyway, that’s where my doubts surfaced after losing to one of my sensei’s proteges. Like myself, Tatsumi Rishimon is one of many of Kenji Iwakura’s “prized students.” The difference between him and me is that I’m the first American to graduate under Sensei Iwakura’s tutelage. I think it’s time for me to fly out to Kenji’s estate, where I need to destroy my doubts and become the number one contender for the eXtreme title. These “doubts” are killing me. After fourteen hours on the plane, I finally touched down in Tokyo, where Sensei Iwakura and Tohru Yoshimoto were to pick me up. It was about mid morning by the time I got there. I took a late flight. I exited the plane when I saw both gentlemen. Kenji is dressed in black, of course, and Tohru is draped in the largest trench coat you’ll ever see anyone wear. To this day, I wonder how a man who is over seven feet tall, pushing towards the seven hundred-pound mark fit into that limousine fit for a man who is of average size (in Japan, anyway). Sensei Iwakura greets me in the same fashion when we meet: he bows. “Greetings, my prized student,” he said to me. Being the gentleman that I am, I return his bow. “As always, sensei. It’s been a long flight and I’m exhausted. Remind me not to take a late flight again.” “I bet you are, my son. Let’s go to my estate where you can rest. Let’s go, Tohru.” Tohru nodded and opened the door for Kenji and myself. We entered inside the plush interior of the limo, while Tohru loaded my luggage into the trunk. Oh course, everything is black, since Kenji Iwakura is rather fond of the color. “So, what brings you here to Tokyo, Simon?” “I have my doubts, sensei,” I replied. Kenji looked at me rather surprisingly. “Doubts? Whatever do you mean?” “I have a big pay-per-view match coming up this Sunday and I feel I am not ready. A Triple Threat match for a spot at the eXtreme title. I haven’t had a match of this caliber since the Extreme Iron Man match I had against Chris West and Fnord in 4 Winds Wrestling. Most people believe that I won‘t win.” “Perhaps you need some training, my boy,” Kenji said, smiling. “By the time I’m through with you, you’ll be ready.” “Excellent.” I felt relieved after he told me that. “I need a little warm-up anyhow. The matches LOW gave me the past few weeks were too easy.” “And my training won’t be. You’ll be going against six of my graduates later this afternoon. I guarantee that you’ll be winning that contendership.” He rolls down the window. “Are you almost ready, Tohru?” “Just about, sir,” he replied, then slammed the trunk shut. “We’re ready to go.” “Excellent. Take us away.” And Tohru just did that. He scrunches himself inside the small limousine, started the engine and drove off towards Sensei Iwakura’s estate. “That kid was nowhere in my league, sensei,” Tatsumi told Kenji Iwakura after his match against me. “I felt he faired well,” Kenji replied. Back in 1999, I was known as “Technically Personified” A. Kreed, an annoying kid who believes that he is the greatest technical wrestler of all time. On this night, I was thwarted by a man who used his size, his Puroresu background, his martial arts background and highflying abilities to defeat me. Ever since Tatsumi defeated me, I’ve always had a weakness against highflying wrestlers. I could never keep up with them and it’s difficult for me to ground them when I have the advantage. They’re like the Energizer Bunny, they keep going and going and going. Ahem...back on track. I was rounding the corner to get to my locker room, with a white towel on my head and a white towel around my waist. “Now I feel human,” I said. “Hey kid!” I heard voice call out to me. I see a smaller man with Tatsumi Rishimon bringing up the rear. He bowed to me and extended his hand. “My name is Kenji Iwakura. I was impressed with your match against my protege tonight.” I shook his hand and returned the bow. “I know who you are, Mr. Iwakura. You’re one of my favorite wrestlers. It is an honor meeting you. What can I do for you?” “It’s not what you can do for me, it’s what I can do for you.” I looked at him quizzically. What can he do for me? “Come again?” “How would you like to be trained my me?” Whoa...Kenji Iwakura, a man who is a legend all over the world wants to train me? Did I hear that right? “I’m sorry, what did you say?” “I would like to take you underneath my wing.” Tatsumi Rishimon was as shocked as I am. “Sensei you shouldn’t---” “Silence!” Kenji held up a hand to Tatsumi. “This is between the boy and myself. You can wait in your locker room.” Tatsumi looks down at his master then me. For a man who’s six feet, six inches tall and weighs two hundred and twenty-six pounds, he was and still an intimidating man. “As you wish, sensei.” He politely gives Kenji a bow, gives me a dirty look then retreats to his locker room. “He doesn’t like me, I can tell,” I said as I watched Tatsumi round the corner towards the locker room. I looked down at Kenji, who is a mere five feet, seven inches, but weighs about one hundred and ninety-five pounds. Kenji balances himself on his black, marble cane, topped with a platinum dragon’s head. “Don’t mind him,” he replied. “He means well most of the time. But my concerns aren’t about him at this moment, my concerns are about you, Mr. Kreed. I feel you have the precise potential and I would be honored to train you under my tutelage. Give me six months to a year, and you will improve your wrestling abilities. What do you say?” I can’t believe this is happening. One of my favorite wrestlers is asking me to be his student. “I would be honored to be trained by you, Mr. Iwakura.” Kenji smiled. “Then it is settled.” He reaches into his pocket to produce a plane ticket. He hands it to me. “Your flight leaves next Wednesday for your flight to Tokyo. Don’t be late.” I took it. “Thank you, sir.” I gave him a bow and went on my way. The afternoon arrived quicker than I thought. I was still in bed resting when Sensei Iwakura awoke me. “It’s time for your training. See you downstairs.” I looked up at him and nodded. “I’ll be there.” Kenji smiled and left the room, closing the door behind him. I sat up in bed and looked out of the mirror that is to my right. What a beautiful view of the countryside of Tokyo. What I like about the location of Kenji’s estate is that it’s pretty much a private area. Kenji owns almost a staggering one thousand acres! He has all the land and doesn’t do much with it, other than the fact that his training grounds are there for use, and is heavily guarded by the top security of Tokyo... Technology. Kenji has an array of stealth weaponry, lasers, reinforced confinements, and the whole shooting match. “What a beautiful day,” I said to myself. “Too bad that I’m going to get my ass handed to me here in a few minutes.” A few minutes later, I arrive downstairs, where Kenji trains all of his students. There are six students standing next to each other and I recognize them all. Standing first is Kaijinbo Himura Jr., son of the innovator of the Death match, Kaijinbo, who recently passed away late last year, I believe. Standing at square five feet, nine inches tall and weighing about two hundred and forty pounds, he is one of two students to graduate under Kenji’s tutelage. Already, Jr. is following in his father’s footsteps to become the next Innovator of the Death match. Hell all those scars on his body are a dead giveaway. Other than having a strong hardcore background, he has a good Puroresu and mat-wrestling savvy as well, taught well by Kenji Iwakura, who was a master of his Puroresu, technical and mat-wrestling skills. He is in his mid-twenties. Next to Jr. is Bravura Kawada. Now, Kawada is a bit of an oddball. He’s not exactly a man of sound mind, much like myself, but he takes his matches to higher levels than I could ever take them. He was nicknamed “The Kamikaze Kid” because of his reckless highflying ability. He does everything in his power to win matches, even if it means severely injuring his opponent and/or himself. He’s about Kenji’s size, except for a more athletic tone. Over the years, Kenji has put on some bulky muscle to stay in shape, but tries to stay as athletically fit as possible. He is in his late twenties. Standing next to Kawada is Shiva Katsumoto. For a man who is about six feet, three inches tall and weighting two hundred and fifty pounds, he has one of the most lethal martial arts background you would ever see in a wrestler. He also has several grappling and bone breaking arts; not to mention that he’s a powerhouse in the ring as well. The man is impossible to defeat in a fight. The man is about thirty-two. He is the oldest man to graduate under Kenji’s tutelage. Next in line is Kenji’s prized student, Tatsumi Rishimon, a man that I know very well. He is the man that gave me one of my first defeats in the sport of professional wrestling. He has it all: the size, the speed and the ability to defeat opponents of any size. Kenji trained him well. For a man who is twenty-six years old, he’s on his way to become the best in Japan. Next is another powerhouse by the name of Akira Udajiri. What makes him different than Shiva is that Akira is a brute with more muscle than you can image. He looks like a mini Batista, standing six feet, one inch and weighing two hundred and eighty pounds of muscle. He graduated under Kenji’s tutelage about three years ago when he was twenty-two. And finally, we have Julie Onimusha, the only female to graduate under Kenji’s tutelage. You remember Julie, don’t you? She made an appearance in Realm a couple of weeks back. Kenji is sitting on a chair next to the ring, smiling at me. “Simon, meet your competition. I believe you remember Tatsumi Rishimon and Julie Onimusha.” I nodded. “That I do.” I look up at Tatsumi. “It’s been a long time, my friend.” “Perhaps too long,” he commented. There was a hint of distaste in it. “And I still see you don’t like me. Fair enough. It doesn’t matter either way. I’m not much of a fan of you neither.” “Now that’s settled,” Kenji butted in. “Let’s start with your training. I‘m pairing everyone up in a group of two. Akira and Shiva, Jr. and Bravura and Tatsumi and Julie. Let‘s start with Jr. and Bravura first. Julie, you‘ll be the referee.” Akira, Shiva and Tatsumi leave the ring. I enter the ring, shaking Jr. and Bravura’s hands. “I’m sorry about your father, Jr.” “Arigato,” he said, bowing. All right. Gentlemen, are you ready? “Hai,” said Jr. “Yes, sensei,” said Bra. I simply nodded. “Excellent.” He rings the bell. “Let’s begin.” Out of nowhere, I see Bravura dropkick me in the side of the head. I never saw him coming. Jr. picks him up, hurls me into one of the corners of the ring, then crushes me with all of his two hundred and forty pounds, executing a painful Avalanche Splash. I fall to the mat, gasping for air. “Jesus Christ, my ribs,” I said to myself. “Is everything alright, Simon?” Kenji asked. He noticed that I was in a great deal of pain. I gingerly get up. I nodded. “I’m alright.” “Good. Continue.” I see Jr. coming at me with a full head of steam. I evaded his attack and threw him out of the ring. Next thing I feel is that Bravura is on my back, applying a sleeper. I break his sleeper, lift him up in an inverted Crucifix Power Bomb set-up, then driving him down head first with a Michinoku Driver II layout. “Excellent move, Simon,” Kenji said. I looked over at Kenji and smiled. I saw Jr. reentering the ring from the corner of my eye. I Super kicked him right in the face. “Thank you, sensei.” “You’re welcome. Keep going until there’s a pin fall. or submission decision.” I get Jr. to his feet and whip him into the ropes. I bounce off the opposite ropes, drilling Jr. down with my Somersault Lariat. I go for the cover and Julie’s there in position. “One! Two---” Bravura dropped a leg across the back of my neck. He attempts to pick me up, but I drove my elbow into his gut, picked him up over my head and threw him out of the ring. Next, I applied my finisher, Possessed, on Jr. Without thinking twice, Jr. tapped. Kenji rang the bell. “All right, Simon wins by submission. Jr., out of the ring. Akira and Shiva are up next. Julie, you can still ref.” Jr. leaves the ring. Akira and Shiva enter. Kenji rings the bell. “Let’s begin.” Before I could get to my feet, Akira Udajiri drops an elbow on the back of my neck. He picks him up, Gorilla presses me and plants me hard on my back! “Ow, motherfucker!” I bellowed. Shiva stands over me, dropping a “flashing” leg drop across my throat. When someone who weighs two hundred and fifty pounds drops a leg on you, it’s going to hurt. He goes for the cover. “One! Two! Thr---Kick-out by Simon!” Julie said. “Focus, Simon!” I heard Kenji yell from the floor. Shiva gets me to my feet, then dropped me to my knees with one of the most fierce spinning back kicks I have ever felt in my life. I coughed up blood and spat it onto the mat after such a hard kick. “Stop!” Kenji yelled out. “Simon, can you continue?” I gingerly get to my feet and kicked Shiva hard in the stomach, then executed a spine-tingling sit-out power bomb. I kipped back up. “Of course,” I said, wincing a bit. “All right. Continue.” Akira clobbers me in the back of the head with a running clothesline. I rolled on my back, kicked him in the knee, grabbed a hold of his leg and set him up for my finisher, but Shiva kicked me in the back of the head before I could pull it off. He put me up into an Argentine Rack Back Breaker, swung me around and plants me with a Michinoku Driver II-B layout, a move he calls Serpent Smasher. He pins me: one, two, three. The match is over. Kenji calls for the bell. “All right. Shiva Katsumoto with the pin fall. Shape up, Simon, this is your final spar. Akira, out of the ring. Tatsumi and Julie, you’re up. Shiva, you can ref since you won the pin fall.” Akira Udajiri leaves the ring and Tatsumi enters. He wastes no time by executing a springboard somersault leg drop across the back of my neck. Kenji rings the bell. “Let’s begin.” Julie is about to go in for an attack, but Tatsumi stops her. “He’s mine. You can sit and watch.” “All right. Don’t be too rough on him.” Tatsumi drives an elbow into my stomach, then picks him up over his head into a fireman’s carry then drives me head first with the MD II, a move he calls the Bushido Driver. But Tatsumi isn’t satisfied. He picks me up again and drills me with another Bushido Driver, and this time, I felt my head collide to the canvas. He places a bare foot on my chest: one, two, three. The match was over. Kenji rings the bell. He looks at Tatsumi with disgust a look of disgust on his face. “Tatsumi, get the hell out of the ring. I want to talk to you later. Shiva, get Simon back into his room.” Shiva nods and helps me up. Tatsumi leaves the ring. Kenji gets up and grabs Tatsumi by the arm. “Don’t pull a stunt like that again.” “Why? I didn’t hurt him.” “You could have broken his neck.” Kenji lets out a frustrated sigh and shakes his head. “Go home. Get out of my sight. I will think of a punishment when you come back.” Tatsumi jerks his arm from Kenji’s grasp. “Perhaps I’m better off not being here. I’m not coming back. Good bye, sensei!” Tatsumi takes his leave from Kenji’s estate, never to come back. Shiva walks past him, helping me back to my room. Kenji walks over. “Are you okay, Simon?” I couldn’t answer. I was too beat up and exhausted to even say anything. Kenji looks up at Shiva. “Instead of taking him to his room. Take him to the spa room so he can soak. He’ll need it.” Shiva nodded. “As you wish, sensei.” He takes me out of the room. Kenji shakes his head. After today, Kenji has decided to have nothing to do with his former student, Tatsumi Rishimon. Just because I was the first American to graduate under Kenji’s teachings doesn’t mean that Tatsumi can take this frustrations out of me. Perhaps it’s the simple fact that Kenji took me under his wing that made Tatsumi upset. Tatsumi wouldn’t attack Kenji because he has too much respect for him. Either that or the fact that either Tohru or Kenji would dispose of him. Kenji leaves the wrestling room and heads over to the spa room to check on his best student. Hopefully, soaking in that spa will do Simon some good, Kenji. I really do.
