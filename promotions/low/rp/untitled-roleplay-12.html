captured 21 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eric Russo vs. Sean Dahmer :: Anti-Christ Superstar is blaring out of the PA system as Sean Dahmer is already in the ring, jumping from side to side. He is getting ready for his big match as he runs into the ropes and bounces off of them as Marilyn Manson screams his theme. :: Well here we go folks. This is the semi-main-event for tonight’s Realm, as Nightmare Sean Dahmer, who is currently in the ring, goes one on one against the Realm Champion, against a man that has been on a HUGE roll as of late, getting a win over the mister Meyhu last week, Eric Russo! You are right on that one, Eric has been on a roll as of late, but it has not been an easy week for him. Last week, after his win, he was cut short of his celebration by Tristan Belmont who came out and beat both of them, but mostly Eric as Mike Meyhu rolled away from the pain soon. Russo must be hurting from that beat down, but I am pretty sure, as much as I hate the son of a bitch, that he will be ready for this one! :: The fans turn towards the entrance as Anti-Christ Superstar fades into silence. The fans boo loudly, as : All of the lights in the arena go out as the entryway fills with a bright shower of sparkling gold fireworks. As this happens, “I'm a Dog” by Kid Rock begins playing throughout the arena. A bright spotlight centers on the entryway as “Superstar” Eric Russo steps into sight. He is wearing a leather vest over his entrance gear. The spotlight glares off his sunglasses and slicked back hair as well as the gold horse’s head which sits atop the black cane he carries in his right hand. Russo walks down to ringside and begins to climb the steps onto the apron. Russo walks to the center of the apron and turns to face the crowd. He proceeds to taunt the fans into a frenzy before entering the ring. He makes his way around the ring taunting the crowd as he removes his leather vest and awaits the opening bell. He does not have to wait for the bell though, as Dahmer is fast to strike him down with a fury of stomps and kicks. Russo tries to battle out of this predicament, but he is not able to, so being the smart one that he is, he rolls out of the ring, to a huge chorus of boos from the fans. Russo turns around to them and flips off the fans he once “loved” minutes ago. Dahmer stays in the rings and screams at Eric Russo to come back in the ring:: This match is already under way, and Sean Dahmer is taking it as it stands right now! You are right! Eric Russo seems to want none of Sean Dahmer right now. He is out taking a quick breather! :: The breather is over as Eric Russo slides in the ring, and once again, he is stomped at by Sean Dahmer. Dahmer chooses to pick up Russo and whips him immediately in the ropes. Eric bounces off of them and comes back, knocked down with a thunderous clothesline. He gets back up though, but he is knocked out down again by another clothesline. Russo gets back up, but this time ducks a third attempt, and kicks Dahmer in the gut. Dahmer doubles over, and Russo kicks him right in the chest. His body swings backwards, and he runs right into a power slam by Eric Russo! Dahmer holds his back as the referee comes for the count. Referee’s Count: 1 … Kick Out! Dahmer gets a shoulder up. Russo gets back up and picks Dahmer up by the head. He grabs him by the neck and pushes him back into the ropes, connecting with a knee lift, followed by a hip toss. Dahmer gets back to his feet and walks into a punch by Eric Russo, who continues the punches until he has pushed him into the corner. Russo continues pounding on Dahmer, who tries to block as many shots as he can. Russo stomps Dahmer/s foot with his heel, and then clotheslines a prone Dahmer into the turnbuckle. Dahmer is about to fall, but he holds himself up by hanging to the top ropes. Russo begins climbing the ropes and punches Dahmer in the face. He punches along as the fans count “1 … 2 … 3 … 4 … 5 … 6 … 7 … 8 … 9 …” At 9, Dahmer pushes himself back and low blows Russo and carries him out of the corner, dropping him down with a Manhattan drop. Russo bounces off Dahmer’s knee, holding onto his crotch. He stumbles a few steps and steps into Dahmer’s arms, who throws on top of his shoulder. He places him, so that Russo’s head is between his legs, and his legs are around Dahmer’s head. And in a swift moment, Dahmer drops down to the mat with a Tombstone Piledriver! The fans react with a great pop as Dahmer drops Russo and gets up himself, looking down at a hurt Russo. He gins as he goes for a simple pin. Referee’s Count: 1 … 2 … Kick out! Dahmer looks on in shock as he thought he had this match won :: He should’ve hooked the leg, he might have gotten that pin if he had! :: As if he had a thought connection with Jerry Donaldo, a frustrated Dahmer hooks the leg as the referee drops his hands against the mat again. Referee’s Count: 1 … 2 … Kick Out! Dahmer picks Russo up, looking mighty pissed off, and he sends him into the ropes. He doubles over, and when Eric comes, he shoots him high above his head. Russo crashes down on the mat and immediately; he is picked up by Dahmer who throws him again into the ropes. This time, Russo ties himself to the ropes and does not come back. Dahmer comes at him for a clothesline, but Russo ducks the shot and low blows Dahmer, who walks around the ring, hurting, holding his crotch. Russo smirks as he grabs him by the arm pits and throws him over his head, connecting with a picture perfect Northern Lights Suplex. The referee sees this, and immediately, he begins the count Referee’s Count: 1 … 2 … Kick Out! Russo breaks the hold and gets back up slowly as Dahmer gets back up himself. The two of them are back up, and they now grapple in the middle of the ring. No one seems to get the advantage, until Russo knees Dahmer in the gut. Dahmer doubles over in pain, as Russo smashes both his forearms on his back. Dahmer does not fall however, as he punches Russo in the gut twice. Russo stumbles backwards, and takes a huge swing at Dahmer, who ducks again, and connects with a hard low blow. Russo doubles over, holding his crotch, as Dahmer throws Russo’s hand around his head, and lifts him up in a suplex position. However, he does not let him go, and walks around in the ring. :: Look at the strength of Dahmer! This is not a normal man’s strength! He is carrying Russo like he was nothing but a feather, and lord knows that it takes large sums of strength for a man as small as 6 feet to do that to someone! He might be one of the strongest in the federation Jerry. He sure as hell is proving that you do not need to be huge to be the best, and in my mind, pound for pound, whether I like him or not, Sean Dahmer is the best wrestler that LOW has, and if he is not THE best, he sure as hell is one of them. The same can be said about Eric Russo. You said it! :: Back in the ring, Dahmer drops Russo down on his back, connecting with a picture perfect stalling suplex. Dahmer immediately turns himself around back to his feet and picks Russo up. He smashes his forearm in Russo’s face two or three times before throwing him into the ropes. Russo comes back and is driven straight into the mat by a thunderous spinebuster from Sean Dahmer, who immediately goes for the pin. Referee’s count: 1 … 2 … Kick Out! :: Oh My God! How did he kick out of that? This is the Realm Champion Jerry, he is not anybody! :: Dahmer slides out of the ring and kneels down in front of the apron. He throws the apron up and begins looking under the ring, to find a big arsenal of weapons. He pulls out a lead pipe and a Singapore cane. He throws both items in the ring, and continues looking beneath the ring. Finally, he almost crawls under the ring, and pulls out a HUGE cinder block from under the ring, which he has pain in lifting. The thing must weight 150 pounds or something, as it is HUGE! He slides it in the ring and rolls under the third rope back before he is counted out. Slowly, Dahmer picks up the lead pipe, and stalls for Russo to get up. Russo eventually gets back to his feet, Dahmer runs at him and smashes the pipe in his gut, causing the Realm champion to bend around the pipe and gasp for air. Russo stumbles backwards, almost knocked out of air, and Dahmer slams the pipe beneath Russo’s kneecap, which sends the champion down on the mat, face first, and holding his knee. The fans create a ruckus :: Damn! Dahmer is taking this to the next step! I’m loving it! :: Dahmer slowly makes his way towards the turnbuckles which he slowly climbs, not aware that Russo is beginning to pull himself back up with the aid of the ropes. Dahmer reaches the top and looks at the fans. He signals that he is crazy, and in a big leap, he jumps towards Russo, and performs a 720-degree flip!!! That’s a double back flip, and that is a double Shooting Star Press, which sadly, ends with Dahmer’s face smashing face first into a terrible soccer kick from the rising Russo, who is pushed back by the intensity at which Dahmer is coming! DID YOU SEE THAT?! DID YOU JUST SEE WHAT I SAW?! DID HE FUCKING DOUPLE BACKFLIP?! He sure as hell did Casey! He sure as hell did. That move was practical suicide, and he took it into thought, as he is LOW’s suicide! Nightmare Sean Dahmer just performed a 720 degree Shooting Star Press, which has to be the craziest aerial move we have seen so far! Dude! Russo kicked him straight in the face! Look at Dahmer, even after such a crazy fucking move, he is down on his stomach, blood flowing from his face. This man came so fat, and Russo’s kick came so strong, it might have destroyed his skull! :: Fans: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! The referee looks on in concern at the Nightmare who rolls around in the ring, screaming and kicking like a baby, in great pain. Eric Russo leans against the topes, taking the time to get his energies back. He pushes himself away from the ropes and begins walking for Sean Dahmer, who is still on the mat, blood flowing freely from his most then likely, broken nose. Eric laughs at him, as he slowly picks him up. Dahmer barely is able to stand on his own, as he immediately falls forward into Russo’s arms, which grabs him around the waist and throws him over his shoulders and head, with a belly-to-belly Suplex. Dahmer crashes down on the mat, as Eric Russo limps his way to the Singapore cane, holding on to his kneecap, still hurting from that shot, and kicking Dahmer real hard did not help at all. He picks the cane up and slowly swings it around freely in the air. Dahmer tries to get up, but he is immediately shot down by a shot of Singapore Cane to the face. The crack is huge! Dahmer gets up!!!! The fans are in awe, and Russo backs up three of four steps, with a look of sheer terrification on his face :: Ok … Jerry, is it me, or did Sean Dahmer just rise? He just rose my friend Jerry? Yes Casey? HOW IN THE HELL DID HE GET UP AFTER ALL THOS FUCKING SHOTS? HOW! I was about to ask you the same question. This man has taken so much damage over the years, it seems that nothing hurts him anymore. That Singapore Cane bent around his head, he was kicked in the face, and still he is up! :: Russo charges towards Dahmer and whacks the cane in his ribs, but Dahmer does not budge, as he slowly makes his way towards Russo and grabs him by the neck. Russo smashes the cane twice in his gut and pushes him away. He swings again, but this time, Dahmer blocks it off and kicks him in he gut, following through with a thunderous DDT. Dahmer gets back to his feet and walks to the corner of the ring. He hides from our sight slowly, as Russo begins to get back to his feet. He is dazed. Dahmer comes back towards him and kicks him in the gut again. He whips him into the ropes and connects with a high flapjack on Russo, who bounces off the mat. Dahmer loses no time and continues his assault, and grabs on to Eric’s left lag and puts him into a single Boston Crab. He pulls Russo up and applies one of his favorite submissions, called the Brain Scan. Russo screams in pain, but he is able to drag himself to the ropes and break the hold. Russo tries to stumble back to his feet, and just as he is back up, Dahmer shoots out a red mist right into Russo’s eyes. Russo screams in pain, as Dahmer’s mouth is flooded with this red mist. :: He just shot that red mist in Russo’s eyes! Russo cannot see a damn thing, and these fans here love it! :: Russo tries to wipe as much mist as he can off, but to no avail, as sooner then later, Dahmer comes running at him and knocks him down with a hard shoulder tackle, followed by another one, and another one, before dropping an elbow drop right into Russo’s heart, and turns himself for a straight pinfall. Referee’s Count: 1 … 2 … Shoulder Up! Dahmer gets up, picking Russo back up at the same time. He throws him on top of his shoulders, and places him for a tombstone pile driver. Just as he is about to drop it, Russo weighs himself in the opposite direction and reverses the hold. Now he is about to drive Dahmer’s head into the mat. He slowly chuckles, shaking his head form side to side. He lifts Dahmer up, and then throws him on top of his shoulder, in a fireman’s carry position! :: WHAT A FUCKING COUTER! :: And then, in an instant, a blinded Eric Russo connects with his finisher; The Falling Star! Still dazed, Russo falls over Dahmer Referee’s Count: 1 … 2 …3! The bell rings as “ I’m a Dog by Kid Rock ” blares out of the PA system, with Eric Russo being named the winner of the match :: Eric Russo once again showing what a great athlete he is. He took out a possible world title contender. This man is one of the greatest LOW has to offer even though he is a total and complete jackass! Dahmer thought he had control of this match, hell we all thought that, but Russo proved him wrong with a great counter. This match ruled, it was simply the greatest! ::Russo raises his hands in victory but the tron lights up to reveal the face of one, Big Daveylicious. He still has an angry look on his face wearing dressed in a black Armani suit. :: Big Daveylicious: Hello, Russo. I see you pulled up another cheap victory over some jobber. Too bad...Luke Justice won't be their to celebrate with you. ::We back out to reveal Big Daveylicious is standing right next to a hosptial bed with poor Luke Justice's lifeless body wearing a neck brace, a bandage wrapped aroun his head, in a hospital gown with the bed covers covering his waist. Big D shakes his head at the injured Shadow Rider.:: Big Daveylicious: It's a shame. I thought the Shadow Riders were like brothers. Apparently, not. Look around in this room. Tell me if you can see something missing from this hospital room. ::We look around see medical equipment, a vistors chair by Justices bed and a monitor.:: Big Daveylicious: You should be ashamed Russo. I could have possibly ended this man's career just because he is associated with you and he doesn't get a telephone call. Nor, a freaking get well card! You disgust me. ::He takes off his Oakley sunglasses staring off at us in a serious look.:: Big Daveylicious: This Shadow Rider crap must be around so they can cover your ass? Looks like you'll be fighting me alone. Too bad I gave Justice half of the punishment you'll recieve. What's it like Russo seeing your men helpless against me? At my mercy, begging me to stop? Doesn't that scare you? ::Big D reaches inside and pulls out a card entitled 'GET WELL, JOBBER'. He lays it on the lifeless Shadow Riders stomach.:: Big Daveylicious: ...think about it he could be you. This could be you! HE COULD BE! ::Suddenly, Luke Justice's eyes awaken tiredly.:: Luke Justice: Nu...nurse? ::Big D looks back to Justice with a sick look in his eyes. Sadly, Big D leans close to the injured Justice for him to realize who it is.:: Big Daveylicious: Sorry, this isn't my shift! ::Justice spits in the face of Big D.:: Luke Justice: SOMEBODY HELP ME! Big Daveylicious: You won't need help where your going... :: Big D grabs ahold of the hospital bed and flips it over making Justice's body fall onto the floor with the bed collasping on top of him.Big D smiles devilishly at his actions and looks to us on more time. We cut away from the tron to reveal a look of shock from Russo.:: Big Daveylicious: Remember Russo, he could be you. Too bad he won't make it to Shell Shock...BUT YOU WILL! ::Big D walks off out of our view as we move in to Justice lying on the cold floor with the hospital bed above him screaming in pain as the scene fades to black.:: Outcome: Victory over Sean Dahmer Show URL: http://
