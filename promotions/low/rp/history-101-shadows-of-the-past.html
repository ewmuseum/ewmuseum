History 101- Shadows of the Past

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. Slowly the logo fades out to black. For a moment, nothing. Then the sound of Russo’s voice speaks out as he continues his recount of the past.) Russo (VO): “Kage and myself entered the NRW together, but certain other circumstances pulled me away. During my absence from the federation, Kage began laying the foundation for our planned attack upon my arrival. Then at the last moment, our plans would be pushed off track by a man that I still hold contempt for to this day. That was my first encounter with the man that had by this point become known as Jesse Jamester. I couldn’t believe my luck, I had not even made my long awaited return and I already had found my new mark.” (The scene fades in to a shot from the past as Russo’s flashback begins. The scene opens in a parking lot. In the lot, Jamester begins walking towards the back door of an arena. Out of nowhere an unidentified person comes running up behind him and cracks him with a cane that has a silver horse’s head at the top of it in the back of the knee. Jamester drops to the ground holding his knee writhing from the pain. Finally we get a clear shot of the attacker’s face.... It’s “Superstar” Eric Russo! As Jamester lies on the ground holding his knee, Russo stoops down beside him and whispers into his ear. ) Russo: “I told you I would be here and this is only the beginning of the carnage that I’m gonna cause.” (At this point, Russo’s voice picks up in a voice over.) Russo (VO): “Hey, it’s not like I didn’t warn him now is it?” ( Russo then pulls off Jamester’s mask. As he stand up again, he holds the mask high above his head as a type of souvenir of what he just did. Security now comes running out from inside the arena towards Russo. Russo not wasting any time takes off running and jumps a fence to escape the security. The camera shot returns to Jamester who is still contorting his body on the ground holding his knee with one hand and covering his face with the other. He is surrounded by paramedics and it looks as though Jamester’s knee is hurt bad. They load Jamester onto a stretcher and wheel him over to the ambulance. Once inside, the ambulance speeds away from the arena headed towards the hospital. The scene fades out as Russo’s voice picks up once again.) Russo (VO): “What do you know, only my return night and I make a bigger impact then Kage did in his entire year. Well anyway, that was the first shot fired in the war that would come to be known as the Riders-Jamester feud. Jamester went on to have his back badly burned later on that night as well. Due to all the damage, Jamester ended up spending some recovery time in the hospital. Being the nice guy that I am, I decided to pay him a little visit there.” (The scene opens to a shot from the past. We are now in a large parking lot. Eric Russo sits on one of the small stone space dividers with a fully buttoned trench coat on. Russo stands up bringing a sign that says hospital into view. He begins unbuttoning the trench coat. Once it is unbuttoned, we can see that Russo is wearing a pair of hospital scrubs under it. He removes the trench coat from his shoulders and tosses it to the ground. Russo then removes a doctor mask from the pocket of the scrubs and puts it on. He enters the hospital and begins walking down the hall. Finally, he comes to an elevator which he rides to the third floor. As Russo steps out of the elevator, we see a sign for the burn unit. Russo follows this sign into the burn unit and begins searching for Jamester. He finally finds the helpless Jamester in room 327. Jamester is lying face down on the bed so as to not hurt his burns. He is also wearing a cast on the leg that Russo injured only a few days prior to this. Jamester also has a wrap on his head that covers his face. Russo enters the room and acts as though he is Jamester’s therapist, he helps Jamester to his feet and begins walking him down the hallway. As the two men enter the physical therapy room, Russo locks the door behind them. He sits Jamester down on a bench, and proceeds to remove the covering over the burns on his back. Then in a sudden movement, Russo rakes his fingers down Jamester’s back in one swift vicious movement. The sudden rush of pain causes Jamester to jump up from his seated position only to fall flat on his face due to the cast on his leg. Russo now grabs a used metal bed pan and slams it onto Jamester’s head. Russo lets out a little snicker as he proclaims that Jamester is now truly a $h!thead. He now begins stomping on Jamester’s already injured leg until the cast on it shatters. Russo pulls Jamester back to his feet and slams his head into the wall. He then lifts him up into a fireman’s carry. With a swift turn, Russo drops the Falling Star. He then takes Jamester’s limp body and stuffs him into the soiled linen basket. Russo unlocks the door of the room and begins pushing the basket down the hallway until he reaches the stairwell. Russo opens the stairwell door and proceeds to shove the soiled linen basket with Jamester in it down the stairs. The basket tumbles down the stairs only making it about two flights before crashing against the wall and toppling over. Seeing this, Russo comes down the steps and lifts the basket back up and shoves it down the stairs again. Once again it hits a wall and topples over but this time when it falls over Jamester falls out and rolls down what is left of the stairs. Russo now goes down to where Jamester is and kicks him a few times before leaning down next to his ear and speaking.) Russo: “You just got your ass kicked again you dumb $h!t. Enjoy your holiday vacation and have a happy New Year.” ( Russo then stands up and gets in one last kick to the ribs before exiting the stairwell and leaving the hospital. The scene fades out as Russo’s voice starts up again.) Russo (VO): “Things between the Shadow Riders and Jamester seemed to get progressively worse as time went on. We knew that if Jamester was still around, we would never be able to extend ourselves to our full potential. So, we concentrated our full attention on him until he finally broke and ran for safety. Once Jamester was gone, the sky was the limit. It was at that time, that Kage and myself decided to begin building the foundations of our newfound empire.” (The scene opens to a shot of Russo and Kage standing on a snowy rooftop. They are accompanied by a large hulk of a human and a voluptuously built brunette. In front of the group stands a camera crew. We listen in as Russo begins to speak.) Russo: “Christmas, when most people are sitting at home having dinner with their loved ones I am here. I am spending my holiday with my family, the people that care about me. Now we are not a family in the traditional sense but we still act as a family to each other. We watch out for each other, we take care of each other, and we would kill for each other. Raven and myself are both a product of the same man, we have both walked down the same paths in this life. We have both been guided down those paths by the Snake and we have both learned the lessons that he was willing to teach us.” (As the Russo’s voice on screen fades down, a voice over takes it’s place.) Russo (VO): “I truly felt that they were my family. We were always there for each other and nothing could change that. Even though I would later learn what a little jealousy could do, I spoke what I truly felt at the time. The Snake had made both Kage and myself what we were, that fact made Kage my brother. Or a distant relative at least. But little did everyone know that in just a few short weeks, the final piece of our plan would fall into place.” (The scene reopens to a shot from the past as Russo reminisces. The scene opens to a small rundown apartment. To one side of the room, the Snake sits in a rickety rocking chair. On the other side, Russo paces along the wall. He begins speaking.) Russo: “The Shadow Riders want you back Snake. We feel as though with you there, we can accomplish our goals in only half the time.” Snake: “I don’t know, I’m kind of happy being retired.” (Russo looks down, sullen. He then glances over at the Snake.) Russo: “Snake, we’ve never asked you for anything from you. Can’t you just help us out this once? We’d really appreciate it.” (The Snake pauses in thought for a moment. His expression then breaks as he responds.) Snake: “Fine, I’ll do it.” (As the scene fades out, we hear Russo’s voice once again.) Russo (VO): “The Snake had taught me to well how to manipulate people. Now it was time to manipulate an entire federation.” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
