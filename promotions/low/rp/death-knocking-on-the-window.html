Death Knocking On The Window

captured 23 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Enigma stands in the dimly lit street as the rain drizzles down his face and onto his trenchcoat, he stands there staring straight ahead, looking as though he is deep in thought. Alias, I told you that I would win that match for you, the match tested my will. Ricky Summers claimed he was The Perfect Package, the unbeatable. Alias, you gave me the strength in the match to cease the victory. I will not stop at Ricky Summers. This week, I square off against not one, not two, but three individuals in a ring. First, 'The Prophet' John Adams, a man who I have reguarded with great respect all of my career, now is the time that I show this man to respect me, through EPW, John Adams always tried to push me to limits I was not ready for, it may have benefitted in the end, but now it was a mistake. John Adams will feel the wrath of the little monster he has created. Second, Matt Meyhu, the self proclaimed 'Marvelous One', I am so much reminded of Ricky Summers by this man. Hell, I kicked Ricky Summers pretty boy ass, why don't they just call me the Pretty Boy Killer because Matt Meyhu you are next on the list. Lastly, Cobi Deekins..The Demond trampled him for the Realm Title. The Demond will not be the first to destroy Cobi Deekins. Enigma walks down the street as he continues to think about the huge 6-man tag team match this week. What is LOW thinking? Pairing me with this Tessa. They could atleast give me suitable partners for this match. Although, on my team the tension amongst the teams will rise too, hell I might have to take out my team after we secure victory. Enigma stop at the corner of the street, he presses the button on the stop light for the walk sign to go. Cars fly through the stop light and finally the Walk logo pops up and Enigma walks across the street, making his way to a broken down building. He walks into the building and glares down the hallway, watching as a child crys alone in the hallway. The father walks out of the apartment door 'You little son of a bitch kid! Get the fuck away from me.' he screams this at the small 3 year old looking child as he hits the child in the face. 'What the fuck is your problem you jackass.' I said as I began to walk down the hallway. 'You have the fuckin nerve to hit a kid?' I glared at him with another sort of evil in my eyes. 'Yeah, you mother fucka what you gon do about it?' He said as he glared into my eyes. I walked up the the man and began screaming at him, I went into a state of which I had nothing running through my head. I grabbed the man by his throat and slammed him against the wall, I began punching him in the face, he began to bleed all over his wife-beater. He fell to the floor, disorented and with a broken nose. I persisted to punch him, I lifted him up and knee'd him in the ribs multiple times, he fell to the floor screaming in pain. I looked at the child, the child had tears streaming down his face.' 'Daddy!!' the child yelled 'What did you do to my daddy?' the child screamed at me as he hit me in the leg. 'Your daddy got what he deserved son, come with me.' I picked up the child and began to walk down the hallway with him. He stared into my face looking in wonder. I knocked on a door and then entered the apartment. 'Shari?' 'What?' she said. 'It's me' 'Alright, who the hell is that' she said as she walked into the living room. 'Some kid from down the hallway his father was beating him, sadly he did it in front of me so I had to show him what the kid was feeling, could you take him to child services for me?' 'Sure.' 'I have to go now, I forgot what this visit was about I was just in the neighborhood.' I walked out of the room and down the hallway kicking the man one more time as he laid in the hallway. I began thinking about the beating I had given to him. If I execute like that when it comes to Catastrophic, noone will be able to handle me. I must find a fuel to feed on, Alias last week, the thoughts of that father beating his son this week.. 'Catastrophy will be witnessed as I walk down the ramp.' Just as I said that my cell phone rang..I answered 'Hello?' 'Alias is dead, Enigma is next' [Fade Out]
