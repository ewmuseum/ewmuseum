Love Part 3 Archive - 6.5.2003

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Hey, how are you?' Cyrus inquired. 'I'm fine, what happened the other night, the blackout, the whole getting rid of me quickly thing, what was that?' Replied Hannah from the other end of the phone line. 'It was just something to do with my mental condition, don't worry about it.' Cyrus didn't want to talk about it right now. He wasn't too happy, he'd lost his title shot and was still feeling a bit depressed. This was evident in his voice. 'What's the matter? You don't seem yourself, you seem depressed. Want to talk about it?' Hannah's friendly, caring voice lulled Cyrus into a sense of comfort. He gladly obliged. 'Well, it's just that, now don't laugh.' However, Cyrus did feel slightly embarrassed. 'I lost my title shot against Iron Lung.' Cyrus waited for Hannah to laugh, but alas it did not come so he continued. 'That match was really important to me, I honestly thought I could win it as well. But yet I didn't, I knew in my heart I had the ability to win, but I couldn’t do it. Maybe I'm not ready to move up the ranks yet…' Cyrus's voice trailed off. 'Honey, cheer up, its one loss. I mean how many other matches have you lost?' Hannah was doing her best to cheer him up. 'Well, just one other one.' Replied a solemn Cyrus. 'See! I mean you've been there a while now and only lost two that's not that bad now. I'm sure that you are good enough to be up there it's just that you had a bad night. I mean they wouldn't be booking you up there if they didn't think you were good enough to be competing with them.' Hannah was making a lot of sense to Cyrus. 'You've got to dust yourself down, carry on believing in yourself and get on with your life, you're not going to win every single match you're in, but as long as you realize that you'll be fine.' Hannah's sweet voice was finally helping Cyrus out of the depression he'd been feeling for the past few days. He was starting to realize that winning every single match isn't important, it's how you perform that counts. Cyrus knew even though he had not won, he'd put out a damn good effort. 'Thanks, you're incredibly intelligent.' Cyrus smiled but Hannah could not see it, though from the pause in the conversation she guessed it. 'I'm so lucky to have you. I need to see you, do you think you'd be able to meet me at the park in about twenty minutes?' Cyrus was desperate to see her again, after how well things were going last time. 'I'd love to!' She proclaimed with an extreme sound of joy in her voice. 'I've missed you so much…' Cyrus cut Hannah off mid-sentence. 'I know and I've missed you too…' With that they both said their good-byes and that was the end of their phone conversation. However, somebody had been listening in. One person had listened to that entire phone call. That person was not happy at all, that person was jealous. That person was spiteful, that person was Cyrus Skar! 'So then, you'll be seeing her soon I take it?' The voice was full of jealousy, it hated the fact Cyrus was not alone anymore. It wanted Cyrus all to its self. 'Yes I am, I'm going meeting her in the park in about twenty minutes…' Cyrus was cut off by a very angry and emotionally fuelled voice. 'Shut up!' Screamed the voice. 'Why? Cyrus why?' The voice went to the other extreme of whispering now…'I don't understand. Why do you push me out of your life for, some, some, girl? I mean look at her, she doesn't care about you, she's using you just to get your money. She said she doesn't like wrestlers, you're a wrestler, why are you so special? Why doesn't she hate your guts?' The voice was on a rampage attacking Cyrus from every conceivable angle. 'I hate you sometimes, you're so selfish, it's always what you want, well I'm sick of it Cyrus, think about me for once in you life. Think about what I want as well. I have to live here too you know?' Cyrus was not just about to take sympathy on the voice though. Cyrus was just as angry, if not more so with the voice. 'How dare you! How dare you suggest that she hates me.' Cyrus was fuelled with rage, the voice had angered him, even if angering Cyrus was not his purpose. 'You think she doesn't care about me? She loves me, just like I love her. You can't accept that though, can you? No! You can’t, you just think that it should be you and me against the world, because the world thinks we're freaks. Well not the entire world is so narrow minded as you led me to believe. There are good people out there like Hannah, don't ever try and tell me different.' Cyrus's eyes were bulging due to the ferocity of his mood. 'Cyrus, I'm sorry.' The voice had calmed down and was regretting a few of the things it had said in the heat of the moment. 'I didn't mean any of that. I can see that she cares for you and you care for her, but I just don’t want you getting hurt, can you understand that?' The voice was lulling Cyrus into a false sense of security. 'Yes I can understand that, I'm sorry for going off my top at you as well, but those things really hit home. I'm not standing for no shit now, I know in the past I've been known to let things just go over my head, well not any more!' Cyrus seemed like a changed man to the voice, he had a new sense of confidence. 'Where has this new found confidence come from?' The voice was intrigued. 'Only the other day you were wallowing in self pity due to your defeat at the hands of Iron Lung. What's changed?' 'Hannah.' Replied Cyrus with a love struck gaze. 'Hannah's changed?' Asked the voice sounding confused. 'No!' Snapped Cyrus, breaking out of his euphoric trance. 'That conversation we had before made me realize why I shouldn't be moping. She helped me regain my confidence. It's all thanks to her. She was my savior.' The voice was not impressed, it thought it was the reason that Cyrus was more confident. 'Oh right…' Said a rather subdued voice. 'Well, lets talk about the matter in hand shall we? Statuz Quo…' The voice tried to shrug off the earlier comments and talk to Cyrus about what it thought the more important things in Cyrus's life were. 'You've got a one on one match against him, you think you're ready.' Cyrus smiled. 'Oh I'm ready.' He said confidently. 'I'm more ready than I've ever been. I'm taking each match as it comes now. Now more presuming winning, no more taking things for granted. That's in the past now. I'm going to focus all of my attention on Statuz Quo, then after him…who knows what'll happen? I might even get another title shot if I'm lucky.' Cyrus laughs. 'But then again do I want another Aftermath Title shot?' 'Indeed Cyrus…' The voice saw where Cyrus was going with the previous question. 'You'll be worthy of more than that title soon. Within a few more matches people will have to recognize you're ability. Then, we'll start to get the big shots…Cyrus, like Hannah said…confidence is the key…' The voice shuddered at the thought of agreeing with Hannah, but she was right. 'I'd better go and start getting ready, I said I'd meet her at the park in about fifteen minutes. I'll talk to you later.' Cyrus was trying to end his conversation with the voice now. He wished to hurry up and meet Hannah. 'I understand when I'm not wanted.' The voice laughed. 'Ciao Cyrus, I hope yours and Hannah's meeting goes ok…' With that the voice returned to the depths of Cyrus soul. Cyrus then ran up stairs and whipped off his top as he quickly walked into the bathroom to prepare for his date.
