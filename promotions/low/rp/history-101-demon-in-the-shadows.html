History 101- Demon in the Shadows

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. Slowly the logo fades out to black. For a moment, nothing. Then the sound of Russo’s voice speaks out as he continues his recount of the past.) Russo (VO): “We were soon to find out that Kage still had no eye when it came to picking out talent to fill our ranks. Kage had his eyes set on another of the LOW’s up and comers. A man who went by the name Tristan Belmont.” (The scene opens to a shot from the past as Russo begins to reminisce. We are taken to the time when Tristan Belmont first pledged his allegiance to the Shadow Riders. The Shadow Riders and Broken Reality are locked in a stare down in the middle of the ring with neither team giving any ground to the other. Both teams begin to inch closer and closer as the tension mounts. Suddenly Tristan Belmont turns his back to the Shadow Riders and levels both Ezra and Annihilation with a heavy double clothesline. Russo, Kage, and Justice just stand back with ear to ear grins and watch as Tristan Belmont single handedly destroys his fellow compatriots of Broken Reality. After he is satisfied with the sheer carnage that he has caused to his partners, Belmont turns and faces the Shadow Riders. Slowly a twisted grin slides across Belmont’s face. Belmont approaches the group where he is welcomed with handshakes and smiles. A voice over by Russo begins.) Russo (VO): “Even though I welcomed him into the group at Kage’s request. I never trusted Tristan Belmont as far as I could throw him. While everyone saw what Belmont wanted them to, I saw him for what he really was . . . a opportunist. This however was not the time or the place to make my thoughts known. So I patiently waited and continued with what was planned. That being me putting over yet another name that I did not trust as I welcomed it into a group that I had made famous.” (The scene continues as the cage rises. Russo slides out of the ring and grabs a microphone from the announce table as well as a t-shirt from under the ring. He now re-enters the ring and walks over to Belmont. The two men stand face to face for a moment. Russo then hands Belmont the t-shirt. Russo pipes up in a voice over.) Russo (VO): “And there goes the neighborhood.” (The scene resumes as Belmont unfurls the t-shirt revealing the logo of the Shadow Riders that is printed on the front. Belmont pulls the shirt over his head and raises his arms in triumph. Now all four men turn ad face the camera as Russo raises the microphone to his mouth and begins speaking.) Russo: “We promised that tonight Lights Out Wrestling would be turned upside down and once again we have delivered exactly what was promised. Today marks a new era here in Lights Out Wrestling, today marks the era of the Shadow Riders. What you see here before you is the greatest assembly of talent to ever step foot inside of a wrestling ring. You have Luke Justice the current Xtreme champion, Tristan Belmont the most dominate Realm champion of all time, and you have Kage and myself the greatest tag team walking the face of the earth today. What else could you ask for? This is our time. No more Mike Meyhus. No more Lotus or Cades. A dark cloud has now eclipsed their glory. It is time that this federation is purged of it’s impurities. A purge that will allow the true cream of the crop to rise to the top of the mountain while leaving all others knowing exactly why they just like the rest are nothing but another falling star in the dust of the Shadow Riders.” (Russo’s voice continues in a voice over as the scene fades out.) Russo (VO): “I can’t say that the time with Belmont was all bad though. While it may be true that he was larger then the average man and therefore much harder to carry. Tristan did do a lot of good for the Shadow Rider name while he was still with us.” (The scene flashes as it reopens to the present day. Russo walks towards the table where Jerry is sitting and takes a seat across from him once again. Jerry begins to question the superstar.) Jerry: “What kind of stuff did he do?” Russo: “Well he . . .” (Russo quickly pauses as he thinks of something.) Russo: “Oh and he . . . wait a second, that was me. Oh well, I guess Belmont really didn’t do much of anything for the Shadow Riders. I guess it was us who did everything for him.” (The scene fades out as Russo continues in a voice over.) Russo (VO): “During his stay, The Shadow Rider name raised Belmont to a level that he could have never dreamed of reaching on his own. Although he may argue differently these days from under the thumb of his new mentor, he still knows the truth deep down inside of him. Just like I made Jason Kage, I made Tristan Belmont too. And I will break him just as easily. Unlike Belmont, I know my purpose. My purpose is revenge.” (The scene reopens to a shot form the past. We are now in an LOW ring where Russo slides back into it and grabs onto Belmont’s hand to celebrate a victory. He raises Belmont’s hand, as both Riders stand tall over their broken opponents whom are now being loaded on stretchers, and having neck braces put on them. Belmont looks down at them as Russo starts trash talking them, his back turned to Belmont. Belmont walks over to one part of the ring and grabs the Realm title, which he throws on the ground. Russo turns around and is kicked in the gut by Belmont! Russo’s voice starts up in a voice over.) Russo (VO): “Do you know the feeling when someone you have done everything for turns their back on you?” (Belmont throws Russo in between his legs and hoists him high into the air only to quickly drop back down with the Recurring Nightmare!) Russo (VO): “Well I do.” (The voice over dies off as the scene continues. The fans go absolutely nuts as Russo holds onto the back of his neck, rolling around in pain as Belmont slowly gets back to his feet. Belmont picks up the Realm title, which he looks at for a moment before throwing on top of Russo’s helpless body, which EMT’s are now rushing too. Belmont pushes the two EMT’s and picks Russo back up. He throws him in between his legs again and hoists him up on his shoulders. He walks over to the ropes and powerbombs him out of the ring, to the concrete! Russo voice picks up into a voice over once again.) Russo (VO): “Belmont should have finished me off while he had the chance though. That was one mistake that I will never let him forget.” (The scene continues. The fans break out in a holy shit chant as Linkin Park’s In the End blasts out again, while Belmont looks down at the broken Rider …… his former partner. Belmont walks over to the ring announcer and is tossed a microphone. The fans quickly settle down as Belmont brings the microphone to his mouth.) Tristan Belmont: “Eric…… take this as my resignation from you and the Shadow Riders ……” (Russo’s voice picks up once again.) Russo (VO): “Belmont started a war that night and I will be the one to finish it.” (The scene continues with Belmont rolling out of the ring and slowly making his way up to the entrance of the aisle. He looks as walks at the three men being loaded on stretchers and laughs, as he disappears to the back! Russo’s voice can be heard.) Russo (VO): “And once I do, there will be nothing left of the so called Demon.” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
