The Battle Begins As the Metallo Zords continue to celebrate in the ring, the scene switches to one of their arch nemesis the evil Queen Mogombu and the Platinum Ranger watching the Metallo’s in their crystal ball from the evil queen’s moon palace. Queen Mogombu begins to speak. Queen Mogombu: Those metallic morons may have been able to win tonight my precious Platinum Ranger, bu now it is time for them to fall! The evil queen pause for a moment as she turns her head to look at the Platinum Ranger. She then continues. Queen Mogombu: Now that you know their weakness when it comes to broom sticks, the time has come for you to attack. The time has come for the demise of those two goody two shoes. The time has come for me to finally take over the earth! Queen Mogombu now throws her head back in a fit of diabolical laughter. As she does so, the Platinum Ranger speaks. Platinum Ranger: Destroy the Metallo Zords . . . it would be my plizeasure my queen. The Metallo Zords will fizall and then the earth will be yours! At this the Platinum Ranger kicks his back and joins Queen Mogombu’s sinister laugh with his own robotic laugh. With both villains now laughing at the horror they are set to unleash on the duo of the Metallo Zords, the scene fades out. Show URL: http://
