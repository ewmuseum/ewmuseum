Christianity’s Beast Slayer!

17 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The hour is upon us, and the sands of time are slowly drifting away! This Saturday is not only Realm 59, but the dawning of a new era! An era of new talent in LOW……an era of enlighten minds in LOW……an era of…..The Prophecy!] -----The scene opens up to St. Paul's Roman Catholic Cathedral in Pittsburgh, PA. The time is the middle of the day approximately 12:05 PM. Outside of the church it’s sunny and the birds are out chirping away while the old folks decide when to have there bingo game. The Cathedral itself is a tall building with two tall towers and is an example of the Scholastic, or Decorated, Gothic style of the 14th Century. Inside the old Gothic style church a crowd forms and gets into a line for there annual confession of there sins. Among them are Cindy Clarentine-her sin is she stole some money out of the collection plate, Billy Bob Valentine-his sin is he had unclean thoughts about his neighbor and cheated on his wife, Belly Rose-her sin was she was the neighbor and cheated on her husband, and many more who have gathered here to be forgiven for their sins.----- -----Over in the corner of the church is a man heavy into prayer as he is kneeling down by a statue of the Virgin Mary. A closer look reveals that he is praying with a pearl beaded rosary. A near by Priest comes by to the individual. ----- Priest: Excuse me my son, but is there anything I can help you with? -----The man begins to laugh a quite diabolical laugh leaving the Priest in quite a confused state. As the Priest clutches his crucifix around his neck, he begins to wonder why a man so indulged into his prayers can clearly be filled with so much wickedness. ----- Priest: My son what ails you today? Have you come to confess your sins? What is your name my son? The man: Some in my days has called me a phenomenon, some have called me a monster, and some has even called me the next antichrist! But you can call me a messenger, a messenger with a message for you and this congregation! Your sign of hope that you call Christianity will be slain this Saturday! For you see I John “The Prophet” Adams must go up against your “Christian Beast” in what you may call an epic battle between good and evil! -----The man now known as John Adams gets up off of his knee and towers the Priest. The Priest backs off, startled in fear from the giant of a man before him. He clutches his crucifix harder and starts saying an Our Father. ----- John Adams: Why do you pray? Is it because the fears you feel is consuming your soul so much that you hope God will come down to save you? Or is it simply that you feel I will take pity on a pathetic worm like yourself? There is no one in this church who could stop me! Not you, not this people, and certainly not your almighty “Christian Beast”! -----The people inside of the church decide to flee out of the area for the fear of what The Prophet may do next, leaving The Prophet and the priest left inside.----- Priest: W..What do you want? John Adams: What else do would I want Padre? But like I said, to deliver a message! A message of hate, war, chaos, and the fact that Max Roswell “The Christian Beast” will be the first sacrificial lamb to my legacy in LOW! -----All of a sudden with one fluent motion John Adams delivers “The Prophecy” to the Priest with authority. And with a wicked smile and black heart he kneels down by the priest. His hand strokes the priest hair back a couple of times. ----- John Adams: The cleansing is complete, and now your soul is free! That is the Prophecy that I deliver! -----John Adams leaves through the back of the church pausing a bit to see the churches mission statement: {The Catholic Community of Saint Paul Cathedral Parish, empowered by the Holy Spirit and nourished by the Eucharist, proclaims witnesses and shares the Good News of Jesus Christ. The Mother Church of the Diocese of Pittsburgh is uniquely situated in the midst of academic, medical and cultural centers. We invite all to join us as we live our faith and reach out in charity to those in need. As we move into our future, we affirm our baptismal call to holiness by deepening our faith and loving one another, all for the honor and glory of God.}As he begins to laugh he heads towards his bike. With the bright sun beating down on his back he gets on the bike and takes off. ----- ~ A couple of days later. ~ Location: Philadelphia, Motel 6 near the LOW Underground Arena. Time: 12:00 AM -----The scene reopens too late at night in Philadelphia at a Motel Six near the arena. At the Motel in Room Thirteen lies a restless John Adams. With thoughts going through his head it makes it real hard to concentrate or sleep. As John Adams tries to sleep, all kinds of feelings start to burst into his mind, about his Father, his days in EPW, and his time so far in LOW. Max Roswell was at once one of GW’s finest cruiserweights and a man that believes he has superpowers. ----- ~The Alarm Rings! ~ -----John Adams gets up from a long restless night. Still thinking about his match this Saturday he decides to get showered and dressed. An interview has been set up for him by LOW officials to talk about his match. ----- -----After he gets showered and dressed he pays his bill and heads out for his interview by the arena. When he gets there he is met up with Mike Pegani one of the LOW interviewers. ----- Mike Pegani: Hello Prophet might we get a few words from you? John Adams: Make it quick Mike! Mike Pegani: Well this is a two part question, first all the fans want to know how you are adjusting since the closing of EPW. And second is how mentally prepared are you when you have to go face to face with “The Christian Beast” Max Roswell? John Adams: Well as far as EPW goes it is a thing of the past, ashes to ashes dust to dust! The transition from EPW to LOW is not a big deal; to me it is just another battlefield to conquer! And Secondly the question you should ask is how mentally prepared is Max? You see Max this isn’t GW boy! This is LOW and this Saturday I will not only test the bounds of your sanity but the depths of your soul! I am no mere man my friend, and I’m not meant to be taken lightly! Mike Pegani: Uhhh great! Well that’s all the time we have for this interview! I’m Mike Pegani for LOW! ----- As Mike and the camera crew leave John Adams is reminded that this is a new environment, some faces are the same but the purpose has changed! The purpose to take back the respect he deserves in this company……….Even if it’s by force. ----- ~FIN~
