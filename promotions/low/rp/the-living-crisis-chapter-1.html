The Living Crisis (Chapter 1)

24 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Prologue: What if all hope ran short? What if every last drip of energy you had was drained from you? Could you muster the fact of being a prune? Could you remain a bag of dried up bones? Some people say death is the same thing. Others expect another live afterwards. One man has experienced such and his name is The White Panther. In the early days of wrestling he was known as The Kid, the underdog. Everyone wanted to beat him down and take him out for he was a rookie, but no one could for they’re cockiness had become they’re main reason for loss. Jesse Jamester proved many wrong. He proved the generation of extreme wrong when he took a fatal fall from the rafters and went through 23 tables. With three broken ribs, a cracked shoulder blade, and three misplaced discs in the neck Jesse looked to be on the shelf for life. However the kid fought back with a passion and desire, and even though still injured went on with his career proving many doctors wrong. In early 2003 Jesse had been the abuser of drugs, alcohol, and other illegal goods. His abuse of these good became the reason for him being put in a rehabilitation center. Though rough he had felt it was a relaxing place after the first three weeks until he was examined after a self-suicide attempt. Jesse had been diagnosed with cancer. A section by his heart and in his left lung had the cancer cells and the surgery would be near impossible. However Jesse had gone into surgery to do the impossible, and prayed for the best. Jesse had no idea what his future would hold in just wrestling. Seeing how he had been manipulated, teased, and toyed with, he felt he had to do something now that would get his life back on track. Something that would play a part in his life after wrestling… That something was music. Jesse planned to be the vocals on stage as well playing bass alongside Ryan who played the guitar, and two old friends by the names of Steve Pavlick who would be playing drums and Mike Lere who would be playing another guitar. All 4 were thrilled of the idea especially since they once were a band right after IFF closed. Jesse was tuning his voice as he and the other members sat down to discuss the band’s name. The Story: Friday June 13, 2003 (5:00 PM ) “Well what’s our name going to be boys?” Jesse starts off showing he’s eager to get this band rolling, “what shall we throw in to the world of music?” “I was thinking Death on Arrival?” shoots Steve looking at the floor. He knows this is big and this will determine how many fans interupt the music or if they even want to listen to it. “Ohm, how about Holy Sins?” questions Mike now writing down the names on a piece of paper. “I like Holy Sins… Death on Arrival is a band or sounds like a band already out there, I think, so that’s out. Ah, what about the Hallucinations?” states Jesse now wondering what the heck he just said. “Hey that doesn’t sound to bad!” mutters Steve. “I think Unholy Beer would work,” sighs Mike. “How about we wait a while and find out whats going to happen. If we wait, ideas will come. Okay so first, does everyone agree we have a solo song for each of us?” asks Jesse. “I agree,” states Mike. “I agree,” follows Ryan. “I don’t care if I have one, I just want to do what I do best,” answers Steve. “Great, we all will do one vocal song with the others in the background, alright,” Jesse mutters now righting down the ideas he has generated on a piece of paper and a check beside them. A check means good, an “x” means bad, “Now how about interviews. Should we let everyone or just one?” “I think you should do it Jesse, you and Ryan,” claims Steve. “Eh I don’t care, as long as we look good. So Jesse or Ryan, you two have to be the leaders of this band, you’re the brothers that founded whatever our name shall be,” follows Mike. Mike is a 6’2” man with short shaved black hair, a peirced eyebrow, nose, both ears, and lip. He has a skiny face, and usually wears a shirt with the middle finger on it flicking you off. Steve is short, about 5’8” and has spiked red tiped hair and a red goatee. Usually wears leather. Both great friends of Jesse from the teen years in Dallas. Steve had always wanted to be a wrestler but his size had made him unable to, so being with someone who was made him feel better about his choices in life. Mike had been a wrestler and loved it all. A former tag team champion with Jesse in New Revolution Wrestling, Jesse had believed in Mike since they met. “So were okay with either Ryan or me doing interviews and articles. How about a website, should we have one?” questions Jesse. “Nah, were just getting started and it’d slow us down. I think we should work slowly with what we got, do a show for Lights Out Wrestling after our first single, and then see where we can go or what we can do,” replies Steve. “Ah I got it! We can call ourselves the Metal Lords!” says Mike and looking around for some sign. “So what do you say?” “I love it! Metal Lords! Bad ass man! Bad…” starts Steve but soon interrupted by Jesse, “Hey man, you don’t need to swear.” Steve looks at Jesse and nods agreeing. The senseless swearing only brings out the teen in him. The band kept talking remembering how it felt to be back together again. Ryan now a monster hovering a good 7 feet and Jesse only half a foot smaller, they were the leaders of the pack and had big things planned ahead of them. “Well boys. I have to get ready but Friday I’ll be back up here and we can begin writing some lyrics for our first single. Sound okay?” boasts Jesse waiting for a reply. “Sure man, whatever suits you. We’re going to come up with some ideas while your gone, and we’ll discuss them Friday,” answers Steve as he writes down something on paper. “Later man,” replies the rest of the group. Walking out the door Jesse knew everything would be looking up from there. His life was becoming a lot better, after rehabilitation and the testing of Jesse being on multiple drugs he was bliss that life had took a turn for the better. [I]Monday June 16, 2003 (12:43 PM) [/I] A bright sunny morning flows throughout the sky. The clouds are burst open from the sun forming white, red, orange, and yellow colors throughout the sky. In the backseat of a bus lounging beside his daughter sits Jesse Jamester waving his hair out of his face as he reads a magazine entitled “Heart Surgery” and what the chances are of surviving. The magazine is much like a guide of choice and it helps people who are unsure to get a grip on what’s going on and how they can focus on the good. Jesse worried most likely is into the magazine as his daughter Samantha whose only three years old sits there beside him staring at an older woman who’s near 60 or so. Samantha just keeps staring like the absent-minded child she is. Jesse turns his head revealing the eyebrow ring he just had pierced and sees Samantha doing this. As an adult he taps her shoulder and waits as she slowly turns her head towards him still admiring the old woman. “Sam, have I not told you never to stare at people, it’s rude,” whispers Jesse kindly knowing she’s just a little girl. “Why don’t you look at the books I got you?” Jesse hands her two books. One is a ‘Find Waldo’ and the other is a talk book where you click a button and the sound plays of it as you read. Samantha begins looking in the ‘Find Waldo’ book as the bus continues on to the next stop. Monday June 16, 2003 (4:28 PM) The bus comes rolling into the parking lot of a bus station. Jesse taps Samantha on the shoulder and tells her it’s time to get off the bus. Jesse gathers the things they have and grabs the bag that he stuffed above them then gets off the bus with Samantha. The first breathe of fresh air is a refreshing moment for the two, since the bus wasn’t a very uncomfortable place to sit for three days. “Lets’ get a cab sweetie, okay? We have to hurry,” says Jesse kindly as he holds her hand and the two walk over onto the sidewalk where the cabs pull in. Jesse gets the first cab he can. Loading it up he puts Samantha in the seat and the two go off. The drive a simple drive that Jesse can ponder over, however not as simple as he thought. As the cab comes to a red light a shot is heard. From the right side of the car comes a man rushing, he breaks the window and unlocks the door. Jumping in he puts the gun to the cab drivers head and demands him to drive. Frightened for his life the cab drives off and doesn’t think twice about it. “What are you looking at? Huh? You got a problem boy?” says the African American male in his early twenties. “Boy you got a problem? Want some of this?” Jesse just looks at his daughter who is frightened and tells her to lie down and close her eyes. As she does, he puts his hand over here ear and looks at the future convict. “I don’t want any trouble,” starts Jesse quite though afraid for he is bleeding sweat at this moment. “I just ask don’t harm my daughter.” “You know fool, I don’t want to hurt you. I also didn’t want to hurt that fool back at the register but he pulled a gun on me and that bitch got a bullet in return. So what you going to do boy? You going to be tight or try something on me?” mutters the man again. “I’ll be tight,” says Jesse frightened though trying to hide it. The car driver turns the car and speeds past an intersection. Jesse knowing danger is ahead braces himself and protects his daughter. The cab slams head on head with another cab shattering the glass and nearly killing the driver of the cab Jesse’s in. The thief jumps out and runs from the scene as quick as he can with a hurt leg. Jesse blacks out after a few moments. Monday June 16, 2003 (9:58 PM) Dizzy he wakes upon a cushioned bed drowsy and can’t stay up properly. Feeling the back of his head Jesse realizes he has a patch on the his neck. He blinks a few times trying to clear the blurry view he has. Finally a doctor walks in and sits down to explain the happenings. “Hello Mr. Jesse, I’m Dr. Hadad. As you may remember or not, you were involved in a car accident earlier today and there are certain injuries that have come over yourself because of it. Now I know this may be hard for you,” mumbles Dr. Hadad as he continues on, however Jesse interrupts. “Wait… Just tell me whats wrong with me and wheres my daughter. Okay?” “Well Jesse, you have suffered a cuncussion and will keep expierencing black outs for a week or two,” states Dr. Hadad trying to ease his next words. Jesse speaks up still curious, “What about my daughter Samantha?” “Your daughter… ohm… she… lost… uh… well, she lost… her hearing in both ears temporarely,” mutters Dr. Hadad looking down. “What do you mean temporarely? How long are we talking about?” Jesse says questioning Dr. Hadad. “1 day, 1 week, 1 month, unknown really,” says Dr. Hadad just waiting for a reply. Jesse sits there try to except the situation for what it is however he cannot. Everything had been going right and with the operation he was going to have the following day he was sure it would be a perfect new start. However, everythin is turning around and heading in the same direction his past had been, a disaster. Jesse just sits there dumbstruck.
