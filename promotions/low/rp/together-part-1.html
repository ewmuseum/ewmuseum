Together - Part 1

27 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I made, a mistake? 'Everybody does at some time or another.' So I hear, but I never make mistakes. 'Don't worry about it, all is right now.' Yes, things seem to right themselves in the end. Go now Cyrus, you have much longer left. Do not waste it. I'll see you later. With that Cyrus awoke, his last dealing with Death was complete. That was it, he would not seem him again in his sleep. At last Cyrus's nightmare was over. He could now focus, he could concentrate on the really important matters in his life now. LOW being his top priority. He turned onto side and checked his new alarm clock, '04:30' it read. Cyrus slid out of his bed and went over to his bathroom to get ready for the first day of his new life. The first day of his life with him. The first day of his life as a team. The first day of his life, together. ---------- 'Cyrus it's been a long time since you did a press conference. What made you decide you wanted to do one today?' The reporter quizzed Cyrus with an interesting opening question. A smile broke out on Cyrus's face the next few moments would reveal a lot about Cyrus. It would reveal that there had been some changes. Some things were going to be different from now on. It had begun at Realm. 'I must say with the death of Hannah, things have changed. I am no longer trapped or bound. The only force stopping me from complete control was Cyrus's love for his girlfriend. I'm not Cyrus, though that is what you shall call me. I do not wish to confuse you too much, as you're all probably well aware I'm his other personality. The other soul trapped inside his body. From now on, I run things. Cyrus attempted to live his life well and he failed. He made some bad choices, like demoting his wrestling career over a woman. I have my head on the right way round, I shall do what is best for me. Now, any more questions?' All that could be heard for a few seconds, were the scribbles of the reporters jotting down as much of what Cyrus had just said as possible. They couldn't believe what they were hearing. Was this true? Or was it just a rouse? Cyrus did sound different, he sounded like himself, but as if somebody else was talking on top of his voice. It unnerved a few of the reporters, but still they did their jobs and asked him questions. 'So what are we to expect from the new Cyrus Skar?' The reporter was only a small man, but the bravest of all the reporters. His voice wavered with fear as he spoke. Somehow Cyrus was able to scare the whole room just by speaking normally. He had changed and the reporters could sense it. 'What to expect? Expect the unexpected. That's all I can really say concerning LOW, I'm involved with a number of things at LOW at the moment one of them is only the beginning of something huge. With time you'll see how big, it's going to open it's mouth and swallow LOW whole. LOW won't know what's hit it. If you want to know what to expect from me in the ring, well lets just say I won't be holding back now. When that pussy Cyrus was in charge of his body, well he didn't always give it his all. That stops right now, I'm not going to go easy on anybody. This is it now, it's time for me to shine in LOW, my first opportunity being my match against Jason Ortiz at 'Wake Up Call'.' Cyrus's final sentence caused a few minds in the room to come up with good questions, thus causing three hands to shoot up into the air. Cyrus looked carefully at the three possibilities and chose a rather fat man. He pointed to him and awaited his question, no doubt about the PPV. 'I've got two questions Mr. Skar. Number one, are you hoping to beat Ortiz? He's beaten you many times before. How will you make this time different? Also why have you aligned yourself with the demonic Jade?' Cyrus took a sip of water from a beaker and cleared his throat. He prepared himself for a rather long reply. He coughed once again and then began to speak in his new tongue. 'Jason Ortiz is one fucked up individual. He is a drug addict, a man on the edge, a man who can be awesome one day and shit the next. We won't know if Jason's going to be at his best or not at the PPV due to the factor that he takes drugs. I on the other hand will be at my very best, I'd say I've got a good chance if Jason comes to the ring at his best. I've got an awesome chance if he doesn't.' Cyrus stops to take a breath and another sip of water. He then places the beaker back down on the table and answers the second question the reporter asked him. 'I can tell from the anticipation on every single face in this room that you all want the answer to this question the most. This is the question that's on everybody's lips but their too scared to ask. Why have I chose Jade? Why her? Why now? Well it's nice that you're all so interested. But I'm not saying anything. It's nothing to do with any of you people and it won't effect anything if I do tell you. All you need to know is that yes we are on the same page and yes we are going to take over LOW, just wait and see how…' Cyrus broke off in mid-sentence much to the shock of the half a dozen reporters in the room. He clutched at his head and then a burst from his old voice was heard. 'No, I won't let you take my body…' Cyrus tipped the table in front of him up causing a pitcher of water to shatter on the floor and causing all the reporters to scatter like flies. Cyrus let out another blast, this time from his new voice. 'Stay where I put you! It won't be long, soon you won't be able to fight, not even a little bit!' With that the pain in Cyrus's head stopped. The other personality was back in charge again. He straightened his jacket and walked out of the conference room as quickly as he could. He stepped behind a curtain to see somebody was waiting for him. 'You? I thought you didn't like me? You wanted to kill me? How come you're here?' The man stayed in the shadows not revealing himself, he then spoke gently and softly reassuring Cyrus that his intentions were indeed true and honest. 'I'm here because I said I would help you. I never wanted to kill you, I was always on your side. I'll explain later, but now we must go. We have business to attend to.' Cyrus smiled and followed the man into the shadows and out of the building. Not only had he made an alliance with Jade, it now looked like Cyrus was making an alliance with somebody else. But who? Only time would tell. ----------- He looked around his prison cell, was this all he was going to have for the rest of his life? Would he ever break out? He seriously doubted it now. His only possessions being his toilet, sink and bed. He felt poor, he felt used, he felt betrayed. How could his other personality trap him inside his own body like this. Cyrus knew there must have been something he could do, but what? He needed a plan, especially now he had made an alliance with Jade. She would make him even stronger, she would strengthen the prison that was around Cyrus now. But that man, was he really there to help? Or was he there to free Cyrus? Cyrus wished it was the latter, he hoped and prayed it was the latter. But for now he might as well get some rest. He was tired, he'd been thinking a lot. It's surprising how thinking can tire you out. Especially when you have no body. ----------
