A Day In The Life- Bloody Hands Jason Ortiz LOW Roleplay #1027 Date: April 16th, 2003 Location: Miami, Florida ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ A Day In The Life- Bloody Hands ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ Date: April 15th, 2003 Place: Outskirts of Miami, Florida Time: 2:23 p.m. Jason jumped up, smacking the face of his long time friend Hector Lopez. Jason’s angry was becoming more intense, his body moving quicker than he could think. Jason looked down at his BMW; blood splattered everywhere on the clean black paint job. Hector was sitting on the ground, his face laying almost on the ground, blood running off the top of his Mexican head. Jason’s BMW was trashed. Totaled again like it was nothing. He pulled the door open, climbing inside of the trashed beamer. Jason quickly rolled down the window, hoping that the blood would run off the side windows. He quickly looked down, noticing that he too had blood on him. “What the fuck!” Jason Ortiz exclaims when he climbs back out of his black BMW again, his anger still showing. “I told you mother fuckers to say in line, I told you not to come in this area when I was sleeping. I have told you a thousand fucking times Hector and you knew what I was talking about. Jesus fucking Christ man, now my car is fucked and everyone is in a pool of blood.” “What’s the big deal Jason? I didn’t mean to hurt anyone,” Hector screamed out loud. Jason took a deep breath, looking at the dead body on the side of the road. “You didn’t mean to hurt anyone, look at that guy right their, he is fucking dead!” Jason was referring to Mark Ventrilla, the man that Jason was to speak to the day prior but things has held Mark back and he was forced to ask Jason for his help. Jason’s eyes were glued on one thing in particular, Mark’s dead body. Jason couldn’t get over the fact that not only was some guy dead but his car was FUCKED. Jason looked toward the other side of the road. “We can’t just leave his fucking body here,” Jason sigh. He was right, in no means would they leave the body behind. This was a dead guy that they were talking about. Sure they could go to the cops, but what would that do, Jason would be looking at Manslatuer, and would be sent to prison and he wasn’t down with that shit. Ortiz was becoming nerves; his palms began to sweat as he thought of something to do. Where would Jason stick the body? “Just leave him here, no one will know Jason!” Steven burst out, hoping that the rest of the gang would agree with him. Just leaving him on the side of the road, in a pool of his own blood would be like the same thing as turning yourself in to the police. “Well,” Jason started. Jason’s face was bright red, still thinking of his next move. “NO! WE CAN’T LEAVE HIM HERE!” There was a few seconds of silence in the area. “Do you have a better idea?” Steven insisted with a quick wink. “I am going with Jason’s plan now; I am changing my mind.” Jason looked back at his BMW, what was left of it. Jason could drive it; it must be able to drive! Jason walked over to the broken down car as it was turned sideways in the middle of an old highway on the outskirts of Miami, Florida. He looked down, seeing the reflection of his dirty face, almost crying at the reflection of his own face. Jason continued to look at himself, knowing that he would never forgive himself for this. “Put him in the trunk,” Jason snaps, looking at Mark’s lifeless body as Hector and Steven Scott stand over it. “We need to dump the body...before anyone finds out about this!” ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ Date: April 15th, 2003 Place: Outskirts of Miami, Florida Time: 3:13 p.m. Just leaving the area and already Jason didn’t feel as if this wasn't on of his brightest ideas. With his head pressed against the window in he back seat, he begins to think hard on the matter of his life. He was looking out the tinted window; the car riding like shit but who could blame him, the thing was a complete total now. Looking down at the long ditch that followed the old highway, Jason imagined Mark’s body laying there, dead as doorknob. “Are you sure no one saw us Steven?” Hector asked as he turned around, noticing that his boss, Jason Ortiz had his head against the window. Hector knew that he didn’t want to address Jason, not right at the moment that is. Sitting in Jason’s car with his head against the warm window almost made Jason sick, the smell of Mark’s body was coming through the back vents. It had only been around an hour and already Mark was begging the stink, but Jason didn’t think it was the body in general, he just thought that Mark always smelled like that. “I didn’t see anyone,” Hector replied with a quick nod, trying to keep his eyes on the road. “Well...I don’t think I saw anyone.” “What do you mean by that,” Jason nodded. “You didn’t see anyone, how could you not see anyone, we are in the middle of know where.” Jason rolled down his window, hoping that the smell of the dead body in the trunk would soon fade out the window. It was a cold day on this afternoon, actually making it a lot better for Jason. He wasn’t in the mood for a hot sweaty day, today; it was rather nice for Jason Ortiz as he sat in the back seat of his own car with Hector driving him around. “How much longer do you think Hector?” Jason asked as the car speed up a little bit more. “Eh, I wouldn’t know Pretty Boy, about thirty more minutes,” Hector shrugged. “Why do you need something Jason? You seem like you are not doing so well back their.” Hector had hit it right, Jason wasn’t feeling good, he felt like shit. Just the smell of the body in his car made him almost puke on the floor, but he was holding back, trying not to mess up the car anymore than it was already was. Jason opened his mouth, the puke began to rush up his mouth, Jason could hardly talk, but a few words did come out of his mouth. “STOP THE CAR,” Jason whispered, more like a scream but it was hard for him to do that. Hector looked up and noticed that Jason was about to ralph, he slowed down the car, dramatically. Everyone in the car could hear Mark’s dead body hit the back of the trunk as the car pulled to a halt on in the middle of the road. Jason propped open the door, kicking it out of his way. He pushed his head out of the car, puke going every where on the ground. Steven tried not to look, knowing it was his is boss and all, but just seeing Jason puking would make him do it as well. “Let it all out Pretty Boy,” Hector protested from the front seat of his boss’s car. Hector Lopez kept his foot on the break, jacking the car into park, knowing that they wouldn’t go anywhere for a little while. Jason lifted his head up; you could smell the puke inside of the car. The instant reaction of the two men in the front was to roll down the window, but they tried to bare through the smell of Jason’s own puke. “How do you feel know Jason?” Steven sounded surprised, looking back at his friend- boss if he wanted to call Jason that. Ortiz’s face was bright red; a little bit of the puke was on his lip and some more on his brand new white shirt. The shirt could be cleaned, but Jason’s pride...couldn’t. “Ahhh...” Pretty Boy, Jason Ortiz mooned, his stomach not feeling that good after the puking. Jason closed one eye, trying to hold the pain back. Hector started the car again, slamming the thing into drive. “Damn this fucking sucks,” Jason continued. His face became pale. At the bottom of Jason’s shirt was a clean spot, a place to wipe the rest of the puke off with. Jason reached down, lifting the bottom up, wiping his lip off. The car speed up to around 90 miles per hour, Hector knowing that they had to get the body out as quick as possible. “Calm yourself Hector,” Jason said quietly. His face glued back on the side of the road. “What ever you say man, just trying to make time.” the Mexican friend of Jason said, ignoring what he said. Jason had realized what he had done. Jason had commented a crime of a lifetime, killing another human being. To top off the whole situation Jason didn’t even know the guy. Mark was an old friend of Steven’s but Jason couldn’t understand his friend. Jason wondered about this big Nazi, the guy had no feelings. He knew what Steven was thinking, Jason knew that Steven Scott was hurting inside...or was he. “STEVEN!” says Jason Ortiz sharply. Steven wasn’t paying attention to his boss; he was paying more attention to the old country side view. Jason tried to stand up, but more pain began to fill his stomach. It was even hard for Jason to move; Jason wondered how he would battle through this. “Steven...” Jason repeated. Jason tried not to move. Steven turned his head back, looking down with his large eyes, the powerful ones that could stare a hole right through you. “WHAT THE FUCK DO YOU WANT,” Steven cursed. Never once had Steven Scott backed talk his boss....but there is a first for everything right? Jason shrugged, not saying a word as the car slowed down, reaching the end of the old highway. ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ Date: April 15th, 2003 Place: Outskirts of Miami, Florida Time: 2:23 p.m. “COME ON YOUR PUSSIES,” Jason said with vengeance. He was sitting on the hood of his own car, the car that had been smashed into pieces. Steven and Hector were digging, hard at work. On the ground next to the car was a bloody, horrified body that scared Jason. Jason tried not to look at the thing, but his mind tried to tell Jason to look. Jason tried to fight it, but he couldn’t hold it in. Jason looking down on Mark’s body as it laid face down the ground. All of the clothes were dirty, mostly because the ground was turning into mud. The rain began to fall, first time in over 2 months. It was a change for Jason, no more sun in the sky, only dark black rain clouds. “Dig! Hurry,” Jason said, wiping the water off his brow. “Hell I could dig twice as fast as you fuckers.” “Do it then Jason; don’t keep shooting off at the mouth damn it,” Steven shouted from afar. Jason wasn’t in the mood for digging at the moment, to much chaos was running through his mind. And not only was it that, his stomach was still hurting, making it hard for him to even talk. Finally after much anticipation the whole was dug. Steven and Hector dropped the shovels next to Mark’s pale white body. Hector winked at his best friend Jason Ortiz. “Are you sure about this Jason?” Hector quickly wondered. “I mean, there is other options.” There wasn’t time for other options this was the only thing that popped into Jason’s mind. Sure their were other options, probably better ones that Jason could think of, but they had to stick with the plan and burying Mark Ventrilla was the plan. Jason lifted his head up, the water dripping down from his forehead. “Sure as I will ever be,” Jason cried, clutching his stomach again.
