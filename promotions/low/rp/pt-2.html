{Pt. 2}

27 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The mustang drives smoothly passing over the speed humps as though they were no longer there. Time has no meaning of a car of this quality and being as it is a well manufactured car there is very little to fault it but still there is always something. The air conditioning of the car is none existent and Samuel is required to keep the windows opened out continuously. Samuel looks around and gazes out at those other cars sharing the dust filled highway with him. None are as powerful or command as much respect as the 1954 blue Mustang yet still they glide with a certain grace yet to be acquired by such an untamed beast. In some of the strangest ways Samuel misses his bike already, the seat had been warn into what could be referred to as a 'ass curve' and this newer mustang had yet to acquire his. After ten miles of hard driving Samuel spots a lay-by, inside it a burger place sits and pulling into it for a rest and something to eat he smiles upwards and stares at its name. 'Coral Cove' Strange enough was the name but the building was painted in two colors, one the color of his fighting garb, the dark blue of a blue coral snake and the other white, the color of water as it flows free. He smiles outwardly but his expression inside is much more poisonous if the people here were his fans then surely they wouldn't mind providing him with food without asking for payment. Yet if they were not his fans the question was to be raised as to how he would pay for the meal and what reception would he get. He walks into the diner confidently, looking around the occupants hoping to find a man that knew who he was. None as he looked around many of them seemed to be watching intently at four figures upon the television screen to the right hand corner of the entrance. The four men rung bells within Samuel's mind, he could not place it but he had the feeling of deja vu and it wouldn't leave him. Taking his seat at the in front of the till he gazes up at the fight, two men garbed in what seems to be the same outfit as he within the ring seemingly fight their opponents with no let up at all. Samuel watches also, his face stern and concentration complete as he watches the screen. These two within the outfits move like angels their moves coming together like he had witnessed within the Realm division and when fighting with people such as Josh Starwind. On and on they fight until Samuel is knocked on the shoulder making him loose his concentration and missing the ending of the fight and the announcement of their names. 'You ain't from round here are you sonny' It was a statement not a question and Samuel could do nothing else but shake his head in acceptance of the fact. Where in the hell was he anyway, there had been no signpost to tell him where they were and no real names apart from the name of the diner. 'Thought not, can I sit you a meal?' Samuel nods his head appreciatively and extends his arm gesturing that the older man (looking around his mid 50's) to take his seat. As he does so Samuel smiles, the man reminds him of Jimmy, his curling grey hair and metallic green eyes giving his face a slimmer appearance. Samuel smiles at him and asks for his name. 'Rupert... my friends call me 'Holocaust', don't ask me why, I have no idea' The two share a laugh as though agreeing that the name was strange but brushing it off as something friends call one another. The man asks for Samuel's name and nods his head knowingly when it is spoken. 'At one time Sam people round here would love to wrestle. Do you enjoy wrestling?' Samuel laughs and hands to Holocaust his Lights Out ID. Holocaust looks at the name for a moment and shakes his head quickly as though to deny something. He smiles at Samuel and speaks out again. 'We've not watched wrestling since the older days. Not since Xtreme Wrestling closed down around here' Samuel smiles and the two converse for a while longer talking of this old company 'xWe' and its apparent closure. Holocaust seemed reluctant to talk about the owner of the company and seemed even more reluctant to continue their conversation on any longer than it was necessary. After a while of talking Holocaust suggests to Samuel that he may wish to stay over night here at the diner but Samuel kindly refuses and standing up from the seats they share leaves the diner and Holocaust behind. Samuel walks out from the building refreshed and no longer hungry yet still confused about the things surrounding his brother. Getting outside he looks up once more admiring the building that now is behind him and getting back into his Mustang he drives away back onto the open highway.
