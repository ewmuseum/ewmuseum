Fixing the Problem

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by a voice over message which says . . . “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to a shot of the interior of a stretch limousine. The interior has all the amenities that one could ask for in a car from a small television set right down to a fully stocked mini bar. We can see “Superstar” Eric Russo sitting at the very back of the limo. He is wearing a black suit with a white shirt that buttons at the collar. He is also in possession of his trademark black cane with the golden horses head at the top. Suddenly a phone begins ringing in a catch electronic tone. Hearing this Russo reaches into the suit’s breast pocket and pulls out a small cell phone. He then flips it open as he raises it to his ear.) Russo: “Eric Russo speaking.” (Russo pauses for a moment as a garbled voice comes from the other end of the line.) Russo: “Jason, just calm down for a second. Now tell me what do you mean we’re in trouble?” (Russo listens as Kage’s voice on the other end of the line becomes more frantic. As Kage explains the situation to Russo over the phone, Russo’s demeanor slowly becomes tenser and tenser.) Russo: “What the hell we’re you thinking!? I can’t believe you did this! Look I gotta go, I’m gonna see if I can smooth this thing over.” (Russo snaps the phone shut and places it back in his pocket. He then speaks up as he begins talking to the driver.) Russo: “Driver, take me over to Bellevue Hospital on 1st Avenue.” (The driver speaks over his shoulder as he responds.) Driver: “Right away sir, we should be there in about 10 minutes.” (Russo lets out a sigh as he sinks back in his seat. He raises his hand to his head and begins massaging his forehead. As he is doing this, he begins muttering to himself.) Russo: “What the hell could he be thinking . . . as if we don’t have enough problems already.” (Russo continues muttering to himself as the few minutes of time it takes to get to the hospital seems to drag on for hours. Finally the limo reaches it’s destination as it pulls into the ambulance docking bay at the front of the hospital. The driver quickly opens his door and jumps out of the car. He races around to the back door of the limo on the passenger side and grabs hold of the handle. The driver then pulls the door open and waits for Russo to exit the car. Russo places his legs outside of the car and pulls himself out to a standing position. He takes a moment to get himself situated before he begins his walk into the emergency room. The interior of the metropolitan hospital has a unfriendly and surgical feel to it. The bleak white walls give off an imposing feel as Russo walks towards the front desk. When he reaches the desk he is greeted by a wrinkled nurse who casts a twisted smile in his direction. She slowly begins talking in a deep cackly voice.) Nurse: “Can I help you?” Russo: “Yeah, I’m looking for a Lance Adelson.” Nurse: “Well let me just check.” (The nurse turns her attention to a small computer screen at her side as the sound of fingers clicking against a keyboard can be heard coming from behind the desk in short spurts. After a few moments, she turns back to Russo with a saddened look on her face.) Nurse: “It appears that your friend is in critical condition up on the sixth floor. He’s in room 612.” (The nurse raises her hand from behind the desk and points towards a dreary hallway. She then continues.) Nurse: “You can take the elevator at the end of that hall. I hope your friend is alright.” Russo: “Me too . . . me too.” (Russo turns from the desk and starts towards the long dimly lit hallway. He makes his way down the hall without a thought in his head other then the fear that the Shadow Riders will lose their TV deal. He finally comes to a pair of rusted steel doors with a small panel that has two arrows marked on it. Guessing that this is the elevator, Russo presses the button with the arrow that is pointing upwards. The doors slide open with a deafening screeching sound. Wary, Russo steps into the rickety old elevator as the doors close behind him. He then presses the button marked 6 as the elevator begins to climb through the shaft in a haphazard way. Russo watches on anxiously as the numbers in the elevator blink in succession floor by floor. As the elevator reaches the 6th floor, the doors slide open once again. Quickly Russo jumps out of the elevator with a sigh of relief. As he surveys his surroundings, the thing that stands out to him the most is the quietness of the floor. He slowly begins his walk down the eerie hallway, he begins looking for room 612. He finally comes to a door with the number 612 sprawled across the top of it in black paint. Russo raises his hand and lightly knocks on the door. The door now creeks open as E! Network CEO David Adelson peeks his head out from within the room. Judging by the look on his face, we can tell that he has been in tears fro the better part of the night since he has heard about his son. Seeing Russo, Adelson steps out of the room and into the hallway where he begins to speak.) Adelson: “Eric, it’s good of you to come.” Russo: “No problem, how is he?” Adelson: “He slipped into a coma while they were transporting him here.” Russo: “Really, do you have any idea what happened?” Adelson: “Not really, all I’ve been told is that he was attacked outside of The World downtown. They also told me that before he slipped into the coma, he kept on muttering something about how it doesn’t matter.” Russo: “Really? Wow, that’s strange.” Adelson: “They said he was probably delirious by that point.” Russo: “I’m really sorry to hear that. I’m sure he’ll pull through though. He’s a tough kid.” Adelson: “Thank you Eric, that means a lot. I won’t forget this.” Russo: “As I said, it’s not a problem. Just being a good friend.” Adelson: “Well I really hate to cut you short friend but . . .” Russo: “Don’t worry about it, get back to your kid. He needs you right now. I’ll call you later.” Adelson: “Ok, I’ll talk to you then.” (Adelson extends his hand and Russo reaches out and shakes it. Russo then gives him a pat on the back as he enters back into his son’s room. After the door to the room shuts, Russo starts back towards the elevator. As he does this, he reaches into the suit’s breast pocket and pulls out his cell phone. He opens it and presses a button before raising it to his ear. As Russo continues towards the elevator, the phone begins to ring the number that he called. After two rings, a voice that we can clearly make out to be that of Jason Kage picks up.) Kage: “Hello.” Russo: “You are one lucky son of a bitch.” Kage: “Why what happened.” Russo: “The kid is in a coma. That bought us some time to deal with this situation.” Kage: “You have any ideas on what to do?” Russo: “I have one, but it’s not gonna be pretty. Call up Hayes and Castle and I’ll meet you three at the apartment in two hours.” (With that the picture cuts back to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again. The logo is accompanied by a voice over message which states “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The screen now fades to black.)
