Obligatory Training Pt. 1

captured 2 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In his career, “The Loose Cannon” Jason Kage has accomplished many things. He’s been a multi-time World Champion in numerous federations, he’s held the tag team titles uncountable times with his partner in crime Eric Russo, and he’s ended the careers of such combatants as John Starr, Rob Synn, and Oblivion. Hell, he’s ended their lives, literally. He’s sparred with the likes of Lotus, Tristan Belmont, and the Meyhus, and he’s completely dominated feuds with others like Annihilation and Jesse Jamester. One thing that’s never been questioned is Kage’s tenacity in and out of the ring, along with his obvious lacking of morals and ethics. Kage will stop at nothing to win, or to even slightly benefit himself, regardless of what the means are. These are just a few reasons why fans and peers alike questioned his disappearance from the LOW scene when he was sidelined with an injury earlier this year. After all, it’s unlike Kage to let something like that pass up opportunity for personal gain. Recently, however, the Loose Cannon resurfaced, showing the world that he had far from hung up his boots in LOW. In the midst of a war being waged between LOW and the FCC, this Shadow Rider has reared his head, and made his presence known. Where will he stand in the combat? Will he be the one to lead LOW into the new year? Only time will tell, and that time has come. Infinite II. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ::The scene opens to a shot of downtown New York. The bustling streets are covered with a thin sheet of ice as the traffic crawls along. Men in Santa suits ring their bells next to red tin collection buckets on the street corners and busy pedestrians speed along the sidewalks with their shopping bags. The holiday season is upon us, and it’s evident by the scenes of the city. We pan through a wide variety of shots, showing the surrounding area, the department stores and the apartments that tower around. The scene quickly pulls up, showing one specific building. As the view continues to elevate, we finally reach the top of the building. There, on the roof of this apartment building, sits Jason Kage. Kage, who is dressed in an all white suit with a silk red shirt, sits in a beach chair on top of the complex. He, in his pony tail and black sunglasses, sits with his legs elevated on the ledge of the building, resting on the top of a gargoyle. He sits comfortably, although somewhat out of place, sipping a mixed drink. The hand that doesn’t hold the drink holds a cell phone, which is pressed to Kage’s ear. We are soon able to listen in on his conversation.:: Kage: “Yeah, yeah, I know . . . I don’t care what measures you have to take, just see to it that it goes through . . . No shit, I’m well aware you want Eric in on this . . . All right already, I’ll call him later to check . . . Yeah, sure, whatever, I don’t care, just don’t fuck this one up, all right? All right, goodbye.” ::Kage closes the phone and shakes his head in disgust. He now pulls himself out of his chair and stands up. He mutters under his breath, presumably about the phone call that just took place, and begins walking away. He makes his way over to the roof entry to the building and enters the door. Our view follows over his shoulder as he makes his way down a floor to his penthouse. He enters his penthouse and almost immediately takes off his white jacket, hanging it on the back of his door. He walks into his kitchen and stands at his countertop. He reaches into the cabinet below and pulls out a bottle, then goes scrounging in the overhead cabinet for some other things. He begins mixing himself another drink when he is caught off guard by a female voice.:: Voice: “Drinking alone, are you? Ah, I guess some things should never be expected to change.” ::Startled, Kage spins around to see Lexus standing in his doorway. Lexus, valet and love interest of Eric Russo, looks disappointedly at Kage. She wears a pair of black leather pants and a red half top, and she whips her blonde hair to the side as she begins to speak again.:: Lexus: “Big surprise th . . .” ::Kage, obviously startled by her appearance, cuts her off and raises his voice with his inquiry.:: Kage: “And just what the fuck are you doing here?! How did you get into my penthouse?!” ::Lexus, in response to Kage’s hostile questioning, keeps her cool and nonchalantly responds.:: Lexus: “Eric asks me to swing by and check in with you to see how things were going. He said he wanted to come himself, but he had other engagements. He wanted to make sure you were getting ready for your big match, and by the looks of things . . . ::her voice grows sarcastic:: . . . You’ve certainly been training.” ::Lexus looks smugly down at Kage’s cup, implying that his taste for alcohol is impeding his ability to properly train for the match. After her point is conveyed, she continues on with her explanation.:: Lexus: “And thank you for greeting me so kindly to your humble abode, I appreciate your hospitality. I’m glad that you’re so warm and welcoming to visitors too, you really made me feel at home.” ::Kage, either being a smart ass or just completely oblivious to the sarcasm, answers.:: Kage: “Yeah, no problem. So what’s the deal, I still don’t get why you’re here. I can take care of myself, I don’t need to be checked in on. I’m prepping for this match plenty, thank you very much. Now, correct me if I’m wrong, but you know where the door is, so you can see yourself out.” ::Lexus walks out of the doorway into the kitchen. The Loose Cannon looks at her as to question why she’s not leaving, but his wondering goes unanswered. Lexus walks right up to Kage, nearly pressing herself up against him. With one finger, she grabs his collar, and softly speaks.:: Lexus: “Now Jason, I know this is your home, and I would appreciate it if you would let me stay here a little while. After all, I’m not so bad, right?” ::Lexus speaks very suggestively to Kage, who just looks disgusted with the whole act. Lexus, however, was not sincere in this dialog, as her hand quickly forms a fist around his collar.:: Lexus: “Not to mention, if you put me out, it’s a sure bet that Eric will have a bounty on your head faster than you can blink. I’m a very persuasive girl, Mr. Kage, and I don’t think I’d have any problem convincing him that big bad Jason Kage needs to be dealt with.” ::Lexus releases her grip on Kage’s collar and walks away. She walks around the counter and enters the living room of the penthouse. She takes a seat on a big black leather chair that sits against the wall and adjusts herself comfortably. Kage, looking on with a grimace, fixes his collar and shakes his head. He looks down at the drink that he’s mixed himself, then at the bottle of rum that sits next to it. He quickly downs the drink then chases it with a swig of rum before making his way into the living room. He takes a seat on the matching leather couch that sits adjacent to the chair. Once seated, he and Lexus begin to converse.:: Kage: “So just spit it out, what’s going on. Russo know I can handle myself, I’m not sure I follow this little check up.” Lexus: “It’s quite simple actually, I think even you might be able to understand. Psymon put Eric on the shelf, it’s only natural that Eric question your ability to prepare for such a match. He said something about not wanting you to make the same mistake he did, blah blah, you know. But yeah, so he sent me to make sure you were doing everything you could to get ready for this match. Not surprisingly though, what do I find? You, in your penthouse alone, drinking. I knew I’d find something like this. I didn’t want to say anything to Eric, but I’ve known this about you for a long time, Kage. You never take your career seriously, you always have a drink in your hand, and you . . .” ::Kage interrupts, not liking the barrage of verbal insults he is facing.:: Kage: “Yadda yadda yadda, ya stuck up bitch. I get the job done, that’s what counts. Its not even like I have a big challenge ahead, I’m facing Psymon. Pshhh, I was working the main events with Lotus while this guy was opening up Friday night shows in high school gyms. Sorry to burst your bubble, but I don’t really have a lot to worry about. Speaking of Lotus, this Psymon character kind of reminded me of him a little bit with the way he talks. He has the same speech pattern of repeating everything he says a thousand times that Lotus used to have.” ::Kage, mocking Psymon, begins to talk in a slow, deep, stupid sounding voice:: Kage: “There is a King of Wrestling. That King is me. There is a God of Wrestling. That God is me. I am the King of Wrestling. I am also the God of Wrestling. The God of Wrestling is what I am. As is the King of Wrestling, because I am that as well. LOW has a King, and that King would be me. Along with being LOW’s King, I am also LOW’s God. I am the King and God of LOW. I am also the King and God of Wrestling. The God of Wrestling and the King of LOW is . . .” ::Having heard enough of his pointless mockery, Lexus cuts Kage off.:: Lexus: “See, this is why we worry. In the time that you could be training, you’re making fun of the way your opponent talks. So he repeats himself. I’ll tell you this, when you guys step into the ring it’s not going to matter who speaks better. It all comes down to whose better prepared. I wish I didn’t have to say this to you, but somebody’s got to . . . You’re not prepared. You never lift a finger to train, all you do is lounge around, eat, drink, and sleep. It’s important . . .” ::Kage interjects:: Kage: “I also fuck.” ::Lexus ignores the Shadow Rider and continues her speech.:: Lexus: “It’s important that you take some time to get yourself ready for this. It’s a World Title match, Psymon’s no joke. He’s been training.” ::Kage grows indignant as he responds.:: Kage: “Yeah, with Street Fighter characters! He’s off training with some martial artist Jap named Ryu?! Haha, that’s classic. He might have Ryu, but that’s no match for my forces. M. Bison and Blanca have got my back one hundred and ten percent, so if he thinks he’s got this made in the shade he’s in for a rude awakening.” ::Lexus rolls her eyes, impatient with Kage’s immaturity and lack of concentration. She makes one final plea at accomplishing her reason for being sent.:: Lexus: “All right, look, I don’t want to be here any more than you want me here. You’re the most immature, irresponsible, and downright revolting person I’ve ever dealt with, and if there was a God somewhere in this universe, I’d never have to see your ugly mug again. But look, as long as Psymon’s out there and you’re in the title picture, Eric’s going to want to make sure you’re doing all that you can. In turn, he’s going to keep sending me to coach you through things until you get it right because he’s laid up in the hospital. So, the sooner you get this Psymon problem taken care of, the sooner we can cease contact. Now I know you’re not easily motivated, but that has to mean something to you. So do you think that maybe, just maybe, you can work up some kind of drive to get this out of the way, or is your feeble little brain unable to process what I’ve just said to you?” ::Kage sits in deep thought for a minute. He looks to be contemplating a lot, puzzled and confused. Finally, he speaks.:: Kage: “So wait . . . You think I’m ugly? Really, you think I’m ugly? I knew you were dumb, but I had no idea that your taste was that bad too.” ::Kage speaks in complete seriousness, not realizing how shallow he is seeming.:: Lexus: “Have you even heard a word I’ve said?” ::Kage completely ignores Lexus’ question and it goes unanswered. He pulls himself off the couch and walks past Lexus, then re-enters the kitchen. Once there, he walks over to the bottle of rum he left on the counter top and picks it up. Alarmed by it’s light weight, he downs the few drops that are left and then sets the bottle back down on the counter. Annoyed with his action, Lexus speaks up from the other room.:: Lexus: “I asked you a question.:: ::Kage, rolling his eyes, replies in a very low and disappointed murmur.:: Kage: “There is not enough alcohol in the world for this conversation . . .” ::Kage shakes his head and leans one arm on the counter. He stands in his kitchen, debating his next move, as Lexus sits comfortably in the living room, no intention of leaving. With that, the scene begins to fade out. What will become of The Loose Cannon? Will he shake the annoyance that is Lexus? If he does, will he offend his fellow Shadow Rider Eric Russo? Will Kage in fact be the next World Champion? And if so, will he have M. Bison and Blanca to thank? We’ll find out next time, when we join our heroes, the Metallo Zor . . . errr . . . The Shadow Riders.::
