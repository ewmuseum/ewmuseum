Metallic Mayhem pt. 4

8 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~{When we last left our heroes, it looked as though they were helpless against the invading army of the evil Queen Mogambu. With the newly created evil ranger around, the Metallo Zords powers were stripped and the tandem was left as mere mortals in helmets. To top off their already existing problem, they had to face the fact that there were two monsters destroying the down town area of their city. With these monsters on the lose, now was not a time to be powerless. It was up to the Rangers however, whether or not they would risk their own lives in an attempt to save the lives of millions of innocent people. Would the Metallo Zords make the ultimate sacrifice? }~~~ ::We open to a scene of the Metallo Zords standing in their control center, where everything is still acting up. With the computer systems down, the small robot known as Bobbalu is scurrying about trying his best to fix them, seemingly without much success. Meanwhile, the Rangers discuss their options with their leader, Bobbabu.:: Bobbabu – Yes, lives are in danger in the city, however, I cannot recommend that you go there to try and save them. Without your powers, you would be putting yourself at a great risk. Gold Ranger – Yes, we are aware, but someone needs to rush to the city’s rescue, Bobbabu. Silver Ranger – And it surely is not going to be a super hero Kangaroo, Bobbabu. Gold Ranger – He is correct, no Kangaroo could do the job that we are going to do, Bobbabu. Bobbabu – Then so be it. I must warn you to be careful Rangers. I would not want to see you harmed. ::The Metallo Zords nod in acceptance of the warning. They then grasp each other by the arm and wait to be beamed to the city. No light beams come however, and the Zords are left standing there arm in arm.:: Gold Ranger – I do not understand. Where are our beams with which we bid adieu, Bobbabu? Silver Ranger – Yes, where are the beams that cast our particles askew, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – Without your powers, you cannot beam anywhere. You must travel like normal people, by means of foot. ::We now cut to a scene of outside of the control center, where the Metallo Zords now stand. They discuss their game plane.:: Gold Ranger – Which way do you suppose the city is, my silver chum? Silver Ranger – I believe it is this way, to the East, my golden companion. Gold Ranger – Funny, because I believe it is this way, to the West. Silver Ranger – Well I happen to know that the city is this way, to the East. Gold Ranger – This truly is a puzzling predicament, because I am sure that the city is this way, to our West. Silver Ranger – I would bet you that if I went East and you went West, I’d make it to the city before you. Gold Ranger – Really? Because I would be willing to wager that if I walked West, I’d make it to the city before you. Silver Ranger – There’s only one way to find out the correct answer. Gold Ranger – Fine then, let’s find out who’s the more intellectual intergalactic once and for all. Silver Ranger – Fine, you go that way, and I’ll go this way. Gold Ranger – Fine. ::The Metallo Zords turn their backs to one another and begin pacing off in opposite directions, set to meet at the city. But who will make it there first? The scene fades over to a shot of the moon, more specifically, Queen Mogambu’s palace. There we see the Queen staring into her crystal ball, surrounded by her subjects.:: Queen Mogambu – Yes, yes! We have them just where we want them now! What a perfect little wedge we’ve driven between those meddling Metallo Zords! It’s time we see to their demise once and for all! Lulu de Blu – Yes, zee to deir demise we will! Queen Mogambu – I want to see it first hand. Raziel! Dispatch two teams of Ich Van Herbes! I want the Metallo Zords brought to me! ::The scene fades back over to a shot of the Gold Ranger, who is walking through the desert alone. He kicks the sand up with his feet as he treads through. He mumbles to himself as he walks.:: Gold Ranger – ‘I’ll bit I can get there faster going East’. Stupid Sterling Silver Salmon Slab. The city is West, I am sure of it. ::The Gold Ranger looks up to see that he is surrounded by Ich Van Herbes!:: Gold Ranger – Huh? ::A patrol of four Ich Van Herbes land around him, two on either side.:: Gold Ranger – Aw man, I do not have time for this! I need to beat the Silver Ranger to the city! ::Gold Ranger removes the canteen he had around his neck and now holds it with both hands. Ich Van Herbe #1 throws a punch at him and he uses the bag to block. The two of them trade low kicks and the Ich Van Herbe collapses onto the sand. Ich Van Herbe #2 leaps over its comrade's body. Gold Ranger bashes the canteen up into its face, then whirls it around and smacks it into the Ich Van Herbe's side. Ich Van Herbe #2 stumbles away.:: Gold Ranger – Hiya, take that! ::Ich Van Herbe #3 rushes over to the Gold Ranger and tries to pull the canteen away, perhaps recognizing it for the deadly weapon that it is! Gold Ranger holds onto the strap of the canteen with his right hand and throws a punch with his left. The Ich Van Herbe dodges his attack and succeeds in yanking the canteen away. Gold Ranger stumbles for a moment.:: Gold Ranger – No, I will not fall! I need to save those people! ::The Gold Ranger doubles over and yells out in pain. Another Ich Van Herbe kicks the Gold Ranger in his weakened state, dropping him face down in the desert sand. The scene now cuts away to a similar looking stretch of desert, where the Silver Ranger is walking. He too mumbles to himself as he plods through the sand.:: Silver Ranger – ‘It’s West, it’s West, the city’s West.’ Gear Gripping Golden Grape Guzzler. Who does he think he is? ::A sudden noise alerts the Silver Ranger. He looks up only to see a group of Ich Van Herbes standing before him.:: Silver Ranger : Come on, Ich Van Herbes! You want some? Come get some! ::The first Ich Van Herbe mid-kicks at Silver Ranger. Silver Ranger grabs its foot and throws the evil-doer to the ground. He raises his left leg and stomps down at the fallen Ich Van Herbe; it rolls aside in the nick of time.:: Silver Ranger – Yeah, take that, you Ich Van Freak! ::Another Ich Van Herbe kicks Silver Ranger in the upper back, collapsing itself in the process. The Silver Ranger hits the sand facedown, and spins onto his back as another Herbe closes in. Planting both feet on its stomach, he boots it into the air twice, then rolls clear as it plummets down. Scrambling to his feet, The Silver Ranger backs away from a spinning Ich Van Herbe. He ducks a trio of kicks and springs back up, unleashing a battle cry.:: Silver Ranger – Ayyyyoooowa! ::Nearby, an Ich Van Herbe is boosted into the air by its comrade. The airborne soldier soars towards the Silver Ranger, landing its right foot squarely upon his chest and knocking him down. Silver Ranger gets to his feet and goes into defensive stance, regarding the surrounding Ich Van Herbes. Silver Ranger scans the battleground, sizing up the opposition.:: Silver Ranger – You’re not so tough, Herbes! Let’s go! ::Silver Ranger runs and kicks down an Ich Van Herbe. He leans back to avoid a kick from another, grabs its arm, and flips the soldier down with a quick arm drag. Finishing it off with a punch, he turns back towards the East and starts walking again. He walks along steadily, and even seems mesmerized for a moment ... until he is alerted to the presence of another Ich Van Herbe. His face contorts with battle rage; he hurtles towards the last soldier and springs, legs wide apart, locking them around its neck. He flips to the sand, toppling the Ich Van Herbe head over heels. The Ich Van Herbe collapses in a heap and the Silver Ranger seems to be vanquished of all his foes.:: Silver Ranger – Phew, that was close. ::The Silver Ranger begins walks again when all of the sudden something crashes down on his back. It’s an Ich Van Herbe and it locks the Ranger in a sleeper hold. The Silver Ranger struggles and struggles, but unfortunately for him, he cannot break free. Soon the Silver Ranger drops to his knees, then forward onto his face. The Ich Van Herbe gets up, leaving the Silver Ranger face down in the sand. We now fade back up to the moon and into Queen Mogambu’s Palace, where she is seen laughing.:: Queen Mogambu – Yes, we have them now! Those Metallo Zords can’t stop us! ::She continues laughing and we fade to another scene within the palace of the evil Queen. We can presume that some time has passed. We are taken inside a room that appears to be a dungeon. There we see the Metallo Zords strapped up and shackled to a war, their arms stretched straight up above them. They stand suspended by chains, apparently unconscious. As the Rangers begin to stir, the commander of the evil army and second in command to Queen Mogambu, Tim, enters the room. He approaches the Zords, who are just awakening.:: Tim – I see you are finally joining us, Rangers. I can’t say it comes as a surprise. ::The Metallo Zords both immediately try to break free of their restraints and attack Tim, however they soon learn that they chains are too strong.:: Gold Ranger – What did you bring us here for? What do you want? Tim – What do I want? I think the question is more like what does he want. ::Through the doorway steps the metallic clad villain that sent the Metallo Zords into shock before. Standing to the right of Tim, he gazes at the Metallo Zords, who let out audible gasps. With this, the scene closes. What is the significance of this masked man? What is it he wants? Will the Metallo Zords escape? How will they save the city from the monsters? Will Lulu de Blu ever take a shower or will he remain true to his French roots? All this and more, next time we meet up with the Metallo Zords.::
