New Breed- Introductions

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. Slowly the logo fades out to black. For a moment, nothing. Then the sound of Russo’s voice speaks out as he launches into a voice over.) Russo (VO): “Well another week, another story. This week I go one on one with the “Nightmare” Sean Dahmer. Now I am not going to be the guy who sits around and berates his opponent with trash talk because god knows if I did that, I’d have enough material to go on for hours upon hours. No, this week Sean Dahmer is not my main concern. My undivided attention rests solely on the shoulders of Jason Kage. Kage tried to deal a death blow to my career when he turned his back on me and stole the name of what I created. He should have known better then that though. He should have known that as the saying goes . . . we don’t die we multiply. I have already taken back the name that I built, now it is time to rebuild it with a new generation. It is time to prepare the new breed of Shadow Riders.” (The scene opens in a dark alley, a group of four well built men follow two midgets down the dark damp alley. As the midgets reach the midpoint in the alley, they turn and open a thick metal door. As they enter the door, the four men follow behind them. The continue down a dark hallway as Russo’s voice is heard in a voice over.) Russo (VO): “Knowing that just as it does with any other wrestler, my time would come to an end some day as well. The Snake had taught me well though. He taught me to always be prepared and so I was prepared for this as well. Over my many years in the sport I have watched for emerging talent that could be used to carry on what I have built. Ones that would know and hold their place unlike my former acquaintances. Now it was time to express my interest to them.” (We are quickly brought back to the present scene as the two midgets begin speaking to each other.) Midgets: “Ompa Lumpa . . . Dompaty . . . Doo” Matt: “What the fuck are these guys saying?” Ray: “I don’t know, but it’s pretty obvious they want us to follow them.” (The group starts walking down a long tunnel that has what appears to be a wrestling arena at the end of it. The arena is made of stone walls giving it a very dungeon like feel. The group begins to feel a little uneasy about their present predicament as they begin to vice their feelings to each other as the two midgets continue forward.) Ray: “Hey . . . what is this place?” (Using sarcasm to cover up his apparent fear, Matt responds.) Matt: “Man, it looks like the Temple of Doom. Now all we need is Indiana Jones.” (Hearing this, Chuck blurts out.) Chuck: “Eww . . . I hated those movies. I never liked snakes.” (Wanting to investigate a little further, Rob speaks up trying to get the rest of the group to keep up.) Rob: “C'mon guys, I think we better follow them.” (Steam begins to rise out of a steel grating on the floor surrounding the ring. Matt recoils in fear as he speaks out.) Matt: “Woah! I think we woke it up!” (Chuck softly speaks to the rest of the group.) Chuck: “Goodbye, folks.” (He then turns and starts running for the door as he calls back over his shoulder.) Chuck: “Let me know how it turns out guys.” (Ray reaches out and manages to catch the back of Chuck’s shirt.) Ray: “Wait! Chuck come back here . . .we need you!” (As Ray pulls on Chuck’s shirt fighting not to let him get away, Rob speaks up.) Rob: “C'mon guys, we gotta keep going.” (Finally Chuck gives in and the four continue further into the arena. With the tension now building, Ray begins to nervously speak.) Ray: “What's gonna happen next?” (Rob turns to Ray and responds.) Rob: “If you don't ask, maybe nothing.” (Rob lets out a nervous chuckle as Matt begins to question him.) Matt: “Well, what if it does Rob? What then? I don't about know about you, but I'm all for leaving. I think we should get out of here . . . .” (Suddenly the corner ring posts light up as a tower of fire raises out of the top of each one for a short second. In awe of the display, the group responds.) Rob, Ray, Chuck, & Matt: “Wow!” (We now hear Russo voice boom through out the arena as it echos over the pa system.) Russo’s Voice: “Shut up ye jackasses! Who dares enter the almighty and awe inspiring presence of the true King of the World, “Superstar” Eric Russo!?” (Not sure what to make of this, the group collectively responds.) Rob, Ray, Chuck, & Matt: “Eric Russo?” (Russo now enters into the empty arena through a side door. He is wearing a black suit with a red button down shirt under the jacket. He approaches the group as he begins to speak.) Russo: “So just as everyone else, you have heard of me?” (In almost a state of shock, Rob responds.) Rob: “Heard of you? Who hasn't heard of Eric Russo?” (Almost as if scripted, Ray continues.) Ray: “Eric Russo, the un-crowned world champion!” (Chuck now joins in.) Chuck: “Eric Russo, the superstar!” (Not wanting to feel left out, Matt decides to jump in.) Matt: “Eric Russo, the man who built the Shadow Riders!” (The scene pauses as we hear Russo’s voice.) Russo (VO): “I knew there was a reason I liked these four.” (The scene continues as a now smiling Russo, obviously from the ego stroke, responds.) Russo: “Please, please, don't make a fuss young ones. Mr. Superstar will do just fine.” (Still taken aback, Rob continues.) Rob: “But you're the one . . . .” (Russo however beats him to the punch and cuts him off.) Russo: “Yes, it is true. I am the keeper of a great power. A power known throughout the universe. A power known as . . . .” (Matt speaks up.) Matt: “Warrior-ism?” (With a look of confusion at the stupidity of Matt’s statement, Russo turns to him and responds.) Russo: “No . . . I’m not Trigger you jackass.” (Russo then looks back to the rest of the group as he continues.) Russo: “I possess the power of the Shadow Rider name!” (In awe, the group speaks up.) Rob, Amanda, Lindsey, & Matt: “The Shadow Rider name?” (Smiling at the groups interest, Russo responds.) Russo: “Yes . . . the Shadow Rider name.” (Russo flashes the breast of his shirt that is concealed by the suit jacket to reveal a small embroidered Shadow Rider logo. Still curious as to what they are all doing there, Rob begins to question Russo.) Rob: “But, Mr. Superstar, what is this place? What is that you do here?” (With a slight laugh, Russo responds.) Russo: “Funny you should mention that, because that is why I have brought you four here. You see, I have found that there are few in the industry today that share the same moral convictions that I was taught to live by when I was trained. So in a hope of rekindling an era that has been long forgotten, I have decided to take an active part in shaping the future. I have decided to begin training of a new breed of wrestler . . . a new breed of Shadow Rider.” (Russo pauses to clear his throat. He then continues.) Russo: “I have watched the four of you as you have developed your skills. In you four, I see the qualities that are needed to carry the Shadow Riders name long after I have hung up my boots. I am here to offer my help in developing those skills at this facility. Now understand, this is not a direct offer into the Shadow Riders. No, think of this as more of a tryout. If any of you manage to wether through what I have planned for you, then and only then there may be a place for you in my ranks. Before you give me your answers though, let me move on to what else we do here. Aside from training the future here, we do something much more important . . . we merchandise.” (Now confused, Matt responds.) Matt: “Merchandise? What's that?” (Seeing an opening, Russo perks up.) Russo: “Merchandising. Follow me I'll show you.” (Russo looks to the two midgets and speaks.) Russo: “Open up the door.” (The midgets open a door to another room on the wall. In the room, is a whole bunch of “Shadow Riders” merchandise lined along the walls. The group continues into the room with Russo leading the charge. He begins to speak as he does so.) Russo: “Ha, ha, ha, come on. Walk this way. Take a look. We put the team’s name on everything. Merchandising. Merchandising. This is where the real money from the your wrestling career is made.” (Russo continues around the room holding up various products and announcing what they are to the group as he does so. The first thing he holds up is a t-shirt with the Shadow Riders’ logo plastered on the front.) Russo: “The official Shadow Riders’ T-shirt.” (Russo drops the t-shirt in favor of a brightly colored book.) Russo: “Shadow Riders- the Coloring Book.” (Russo tosses the book to Matt.) Russo: “Here . . . amuse yourself jackass.” (Russo continues down the line of products. He now picks up a lunch box.) Russo: “Shadow Riders- the Lunch box.” (He tosses the box to Lindsey.) Russo: “Here, put some of your food in that. You could stand to lose a little weight.” (Russo now continues along picking up a box of cereal.) Russo: “Shadow Riders- the Breakfast Cereal.” (He tosses the box to Amanda.) Russo: “You can eat this, you’re a little on the scrawny side.” (Russo moves along and now picks up a Gameboy.) Russo: “Shadow Riders- the Video Game.” (Russo turns on the small apparatus which lights up and begins making noises. Hearing this, the midgets speak.) Midgets: “Ooooooo.” (With a smile, Russo continues.) Russo: “As you can see, the little people love this one.” (Russo tosses the game to Rob.) Russo: “Here.” (Russo continues along. He then suddenly stops and begins to speak without turning to the group.) Russo: “Last, but not least, Shadow Riders- the Action Figure.” (Russo turns around and holds up an action figure of himself.) Russo: “Me . . . who else did you expect?” (He now pulls a small string on the figure’s back. The figure speaks.) Russo Figure: “You jackass!” Midgets: “giggle.” (With a smile Russo responds.) Russo: “Adorable ain’t he?” (Before the group has a chance to respond to Russo’s first question, Russo manages to slip a second one in.) Russo: “Oh and what about my offer?” (Before the group even has a chance to consider what their doing, they agree.) Rob, Ray, Chuck, & Matt: “Yeah . . . we’re in.” (As the scene begins to fade out, we hear Russo’s voice once more.) Russo (VO): “Hey Trigger made Belmont a star like this so how hard could it possibly be? Mwahhhh!” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
