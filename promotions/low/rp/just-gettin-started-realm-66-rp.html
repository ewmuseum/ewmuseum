Just Gettin started....(Realm 66 RP)

captured 8 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:::We open our scene inside the magnificent home of AJ Palmer. AJ is nowhere to be seen at the moment but it appears we are inside of some kind of boardroom. The walls are a nice blackish red color with red carpets. There is a beautiful conference table made of what seems to be oak in the center. There is one man sitting at the table. A tall blond man who is eating a sandwich (We’ll call him blondie for now). As he continues to get his munch on, another man, also not AJ walks into the room. Now none of you know this man, so we’ll introduce him. He is the right hand man for AJ, the name he goes by…Big J, Or J for short. He is also a tall man, and fairly overweight.::: Big J: Where’s AJ at? Blondie: (Putting down his sandwich) Out on the balcony. Big J: In the rain? Blondie: Yeah, you know how he is. Why? Big J: Oh I got the damn card for him this week. Blondie: You mean he’s got a match? Big J: Yeah, and just like he’s been saying. We got the PERFECT cover now, money’s just gonna be rolling in left an right……. AJ’s Thoughts: ….Back to wrestling again? I never thought I’d go back. Especially after what happened back home. I know we got it made. That is if no one finds out my secret. :::AJ is standing out on his balcony. No shirt so you can see on his back a pair of black angel type wings tattooed into his skin. On his wrists, blue and black flames up his forearm. If it was a nice day it would be a beautiful view. Over looking the beach and the ocean. A place many people would kill for…and AJ has. However on this day, it’s dark, cloudy, and rainy. The rain soaking AJ from head to toe. He’s just leaning up against the railing, looking out towards the ocean.::: AJ’s Thoughts: I got to keep it together, if I fail with this, no telling where I could end up. It’s either run again, or face prison, and there is no way I’m doing that. :::As AJ continues to think to himself, Big J comes in from the sliding glass doors. He starts out into the rain but then thinks better of it.::: Big J: Yo, AJ, come on in out of the rain man, you don’t wanna get sick before this weekend. We got big plans remember? :::AJ doesn’t move an inch, just continues staring out over the clear water of his personal beach.::: Big J: I got the fax from LOW man, you got yourself a match. A big one too. :::Still no movement from AJ. He looks as though he is deep in thought, the kind of life changing thought.::: Big J: I took the liberty of looking up the people your facing, some tough competition. I got the papers right here man, if you wanna look at them. AJ: Yeah just set them on the table. Big J: You aight man? I know you get like this from time to time but remember man, we got your back. :::AJ turns around facing his friend. His face showing no emotion what so ever. He just wipes the rain water from his face and crosses his arms: AJ: Yeah man I know, and thanks. Hey get the guys together and get the car ready. I’m expecting the phone call any time now. Big J: Aight man, will do. I’ll set these papers on the table. Make sure you take a good look at them. ::: AJ nods and Big J goes back into the house. AJ looks up at the sky into the pouring rain and starts talking.::: AJ: If you got any big plans for me, might as well tell me now. Once we start there ain’t no going back! :::Walks looks down at his feet and uncrosses his arms. Slowly he begins walking into the house. Once inside he grabs a towel from a chair. He wipes his arms down and his face before hanging it over his shoulders::: AJ’s Thoughts: Come man, let’s get this going. Give me the damn call all ready. :::AJ walks around to the head of the table and pulls out the leather chair to sit in. He sits down and starts going through the papers Big J left for him.::: AJ’s Thoughts: OK, let’s see what we got. An 8 man Octagon match? :::AJ sets the papers down for a second::: AJ’s Thoughts: I knew it, I knew I was getting into more than I could handle. An 8 man Octagon? (AJ picks the papers back up.) Ok, who we got in it. Ken Kaze, OK not sooo bad. (AJ flips through the papers a little.) 5’11, 198 pounds, and a good guy. Eh, not so bad at all. Calls himself “LOW’s Resident Moron”. What in the hell is up with this guy. Ain’t he got more self-respect than that. But of course who am I to say. I put on a good front but I got less self-respect then anyone I know. :::AJ rubs his eyes and flips through the papers some more::: AJ’s thoughts: Cobi Deekins….ENIGMA?!?! (AJ takes a deep breath and leans back in the chair) I saw the damn fool last week fight that big bastard what’s his name. If he’s all ready been fighting for a damn title, then what kind of chance do I got against him? He’s about the same size but I been out for 3 damn years. No way in hell I can contend with him, no way!!! :::AJ slams the papers down on the table and kicks the chair back leaping out of the seat::: AJ’s Thoughts: I knew it, I knew I was in trouble. (He picks the papers back up and begins to pace around the table as he looks at them). JJ Jarone, Max Roswell, Myles Lecroy….( As he reads he takes a double look at the paper) Scottywood? Damn he’s still around? I never faced the guy but heard his name around. If a guy stays around this long he’s got to be good. Damn good too. Ahhh, and he’s bigger too. PERFECT!! What the hell have I done? I mean I know I can handle my self in a fight but am I still good enough to fight back in the ring? I know I told the guys the win doesn’t matter, it’s about selling our stuff but DAMN!!! I can’t make myself look like an idiot. Then we got a guy named Taynell Cooley….not a profile on him? Great, just, great. A man with no past, reminds me of someone. :::AJ tosses the papers back down on the table letting some of them slide onto the floor. He opens the glass doors back up listening to the rain pound down on the marble balcony::: AJ’s Thoughts: Ok so let me get this right, not only do I have 7 other men to worry, but two men are probably gonna be one of the hardest people I have faced and one I don’t know anything about!! But on top of that, it’s gonna be an Octagon match? A god damn Octagon? This ain’t no underground fight, I thought this was a wrestling match? Not some kind of kill or be killed match. :::One of the papers that slid on the floor was now directly below AJ, he looks at it with a puzzled look on his face as he bends over and picks it up::: AJ’s Thoughts: (AJ begins to get a smile across his face) The winner will receive a Realm title shot at the next PPV. This could be very interesting, a win at the PPV would be big for business. Means more public appearances, more publicity, more clients! More Clients equals more money…more money is more POWER!!! This could be big, this could be very big. (AJ’s smile quickly turns to a scowl) Well, it also means more people seeing me, the more chance of getting recognized. DAMN!!! Why does everything have to go bad, why does everything good, seem to have something bad backing it up. :::AJ takes a step back and with a quick, stiff jab he puts a hole in one of the walls.::: AJ’s Thoughts: Just once I want something to go my way, just once I want to be able to say I WON, I made it through all the bad. I don't know if it will ever be like that again. It’s weird how the choices I have made in life all have come back to screw me over in the end. :::As AJ continues to ponder the choices he has made in his life, Big J returns to the room with a black phone in his hand::: Big J: AJ man, it’s him…. :::AJ stops looking out the window and quickly goes over to get the phone. Once he receives it he covers the speaker::: AJ: Thanks. Everything all ready to go? Big J: Yeah we been ready for a while, the guys are just getting they’re pieces ready to go. AJ: Good. When I’m off the phone we need to go so make sure the cars are out front and we got everything. This is the start man, we gonna make some big time cash. Big J: Aight AJ, I’ll take car of it. :::AJ pounds fists with Big J before leaves the rooms and AJ again starts pacing around the table::: AJ’s Thoughts: Ok AJ, here we go….. AJ: (Placing the phone to his ear) Boss…how we doing today? Boss: Cut the chit chat AJ, you know we got business and you know I’m a VERY busy man. AJ: Sorry boss, just a bad day outside thought I’d try and lighting the mood. Boss: AJ, you’re very good at what you do, but you still got a lot to learn. The shipments comming in tonight. Gonna be at the Imperial Landing bout 11:35. Gonna be one boat and one boat only. AJ: I thought it was gonna be two man? The deal was two! Boss: AJ, you know not to use that tone with me!! Besides we couldn’t risk it. Not this early in the plan anyway. Now you done what I said and got into that LOW? AJ: Yeah all set, been in for a couple weeks now. Got a match this week. Boss: AJ, you damn well know this isn’t about you wrestling again. All this LOW stuff is just a front, you ain’t there to win matches just to cover up and make more clients, ok? AJ: Yeah yeah man, I get it. Boss: AJ I’m serious. You screw this up and we’re all in trouble you know? AJ: I said yeah man, it’s all taking care of. Boss: Make sure you bring the RIGHT money. There’s gonna be bout 6 of these guys fully equipped. They’re not gonna take any bullshit. We don’t want what happened last time to happen this time as well. What I’m trying to say is keep your guys in check AJ…or I will!!! AJ: Don’t worry about it boss. They’re all under control. Nothing to worry about on this end. Boss: Better not be AJ. I got my eyes on you. :::AJ press the end button on the phone. He looks down at the floor and then effortlessly smashes the phone on the table::: AJ’s Thoughts: Watching ME?!? ME!?! That son of a bitch thinks he’s got all the power? I’ll handle him when the time comes…for now though….. :::AJ stands straight up and cracks his knuckles against the side of his face. He grabs his gun holster and slings it over his shoulder.::: AJ’s Thoughts: …Do it up… ::: We leave with AJ walking out the room door knowing all to well he has a full plate in front of him. With dealing with his “Boss” and the upcoming Octagon match, no telling when this guy is gonna crack!:::
