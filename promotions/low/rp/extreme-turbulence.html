Extreme Turbulence

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders along with a voice over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to a shot from inside of the Shadow Riders private jet which is headed towards the location of this weeks Realm. Inside the jet, Eric Russo is in the middle of conducting an interview for the newest SRtv reporter, the curvaceous blonde, Lexus and an LOW cameraman. Kage is sitting towards the rear of the cabin drinking a whiskey on the rocks and Luke and Belmont are playing a game of cards at a small table to the front of the cabin. As Russo continues answering the questions of the beautiful reporter, the cameraman signals that he needs to change tapes. As the cameraman turns off the camera and begins to change the tapes, Russo strikes up a conversation with Lexus.) Russo: “So, what are you doing after Realm tonight? You maybe wanna come hang out with us and go to the clubs to party a little bit? We’re probably gonna fly up to New York and head on over to Scores. Last time we were there, we met up with Howard Stern for a few drinks . . . I could introduce you if you want. Maybe he’ll put you on his show or something.” (Knowing that this could be the chance of a lifetime, Lexus responds doing her best to control her excitement over such an offer.) Lexus: “Well . . . um . . . I guess that would be fun. I always wanted to meet Howard. You’d really introduce me?” Russo: “Sure, why not?” (Suddenly a jolt of turbulence rocks the entire plane startling everyone in the cabin. Trying to play off the fact that the jolt had shaken him up, Russo continues talking to Lexus in a cool, calm, and collected tone.) Russo: “So, it’s a date then?” (Luke pipes up from his seat still shaky from the turbulent jolt.) Justice: “Will you be quiet for a second!?” (Sensing Luke’s uneasiness, Russo calls over to him.) Russo: “Luke, just relax . . . it was just a freaking air pocket. Don’t be such a wuss.” (Not wanting to look bad, Luke responds.) Justice: “I wasn’t scared . . . my neck is still in bad shape from my match with Pogo last week.” (Russo and Belmont let out a slight chuckle at Luke’s cover. Their smiles however are cut short by yet another jolt of turbulence even bigger then the first. The jolt is so strong in fact that Kage’s glass of whiskey rises off the tray in front of him and pauses briefly in mid-air before crashing to the cabin floor and shattering. The large jet takes another fierce jolt from the turbulence. With a sinking fear in the pit of his stomach, Luke calls out.) Justice: “We should have just drove to Realm . . . we were only about a ten hour drive away! I knew I hated flying for a reason!” Belmont: “You’re right! We could have just taken the car!” (Showing his utter terror in the situation, Luke drops his head down trying his best to keep from launching into an all out panic attack. As he does this, the pilot’s voice comes crackling over the speaker system in the cabin.) Pilot: “Ok guys it looks like we’ve run into a bit of a problem in the form of an electrical storm we were trying to avoid. Just fasten your seatbelts and we’ll do our best to get us all out of this without any further problems.” (The plane’s turbulence worsens as everyone in the cabin makes sure that they are securely fastened into a seat. Finally Kage opens his mouth as he speaks.) Kage: “How the hell could they not see an electrical storm coming right in front of us?” (Fastening his seat belt as if he was strapping in for some kind of rough ride at the amusement park, Russo speaks out in an excited fashion.) Russo: “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.” (The light coming through the cabin windows begins quickly fades out as it is replaced by dark rain clouds on all sides of the jet. Luke looks up once again, his tension mounting, and stares straight ahead in an almost trance like state. The jet suddenly takes a sudden drop only to stabilize moments later. Nobody utters a word except for Russo who still seems somewhat at ease trying to play off his true feeling of complete and total fear towards the situation.) Russo: “Oh yeah . . . it’s gonna be a bumpy ride!” (Moments later, the cockpit door bursts open as the co-pilot enters the cabin using his hands on the wall to stabilize himself in the shaky aircraft. Once he has a good enough balance, he begins speaking to the passengers of the jet.) Co-pilot: “Ok, were gonna try and land in Charlotte, North Carolina. To do this, we’re gonna have to cut off the lights in this cabin in order to get all the power we can to the engines. We’re gonna try and set the jet down in a large field that we found on the map.” (By this point Russo notices that Tristan is busily rubbing a small cross in between his fingers. Hearing the plan, Kage calls out from the back of the cabin.) Kage: “A field!? We’re gonna land in a frigging field!?” (Giving way to his panic, Luke speaks.) Justice: “I can’t breathe!” (There is a plethora of abnormal sounding electrical buzzes and zaps as the jet cuts through the thick clouds haphazardly. As it does this, the co-pilot continues trying to reassure his passengers.) Co-pilot: “Now this landing may be extremely rough but we should all be fine when it’s over aside from a few bumps and bruises. However in a situation like this, it’s always best to get anything you want to say to each other out in the open just in case something goes wrong. So, if there is anything you would like to say to each other that you’ve been holding back, now would be the perfect time to lay everything out on the table to make sure there are no regrets. Just hang in there though, we’ll get you out of this.” (The co-pilot returns into the cockpit closing the door behind him as the wether outside of the aircraft worsens. Hail begins to click against the exterior of the jet echoing the sound throughout the cabin. The lights now abruptly cut off leaving the fearful passengers in almost total darkness. Russo stares straight ahead into the seemingly endless darkness as the cockpit door swings open. Inside the cockpit, there is a state of disarray as the pilot and co-pilot scramble to try and get th jet under control. Seeing that the door is open, the co-pilot quickly reaches out and shuts it once again. Allowing his fear to take control, Kage blurts out mocking people from the LOW locker rooms ususal comments.) Kage: “Yeah . . . it’s so cool to have a jet to travel in. I wish I could go from place to place in a jet. It must be so nice. Yeah, they don’t tell you you’re gonna die in it!” (Still in a trance like state, Luke mutters under his breath.) Justice: “That guy just told us we’re gonna die. He said we’re gonna die.” (In a state of panic, Belmont speaks out to Luke in a tone filled with both fear and annoyance.) Belmont: “Yeah we’re gonna die . . . and we’re gonna die in Ric Flair’s hometown. How sad is that?” Russo: “Shut up Tristan!” Belmont: “We can’t even die somewhere good . . . what the hell!” Russo: “C’mon . . . get us somewhere good! At least let us die somewhere that they aren’t gonna burry us in crappy wooden pine boxes because they can’t afford good wood. Stupid hick jackasses.” (Everyone in the cabin lets out an uneasy laugh at Russo’s attempt to keep the mood light as the jet carries them to their death. Suddenly another crack echos through the jet as they are hit by more chunks of hail. Finally cracking, Lexus blurts out.) Lexus: “Oh my God! We’re all gonna die!” (Luke is now severely shaking with fear as his eyes well up with tears. Luke begins taking quick shallow breaths as a full on panic attack sets in. Coming to terms with the fact that this may very well be the end, Russo begins to speak to the entire group in the cabin.) Russo: “Guys . . . look, just in case we don’t make it through this. I want all of you to know that it’s been an honor working with you guys. I love all of you . . . you’re my brothers no matter what. I just wanted to tell you guys that.” (The entire aircraft shakes again as a bolt of lightning nearly connects with the jet. The entire cabin quickly lights up with a flash before returning to complete darkness once again. Belmont now begins to speak through the darkness to his teammates.) Belmont: “Guys, I just want to let you know that I’ve really enjoyed my time as a Shadow Rider. I never knew that there was so much fun to be had. I thank all three of you guys for making me feel welcomed into the group.” (In utter fear due to the Shadow Riders making preparations to die, Lexus blurts out once again.) Lexus: “Oh my God! We really are gonna die!” (The jet hits another air pocket in it’s path causing it to jerk upwards and back down abruptly. The jet settles for a moment as if there in no trouble whatsoever. This feeling is short lived however as the jet resumes it’s turbulent shaking once again. Due to the brief moment of calmness, Luke has worked up enough nerve to make his statement to the group.) Justice: “Guys, I love you all too. You are my brothers . . . you’ve always been there for me. Especially when Kat walked out on me. I just want all of you to know that I really appreciated it even if I never really showed it.” (Timidly, Russo responds.) Russo: “Uh Luke . . . I kind of slept with Kat while you were still going out with her.” (Piping up from the back of the cabin, Kage joins in.) Kage: “I kind of slept with her too Luke.” (Hearing the other two Shadow Riders confess, Belmont speaks up as well.) Belmont: “Well it was after you broke up with her but I slept with Kat too Luke . . . sorry.” (Kage pipes up once again from the back of the jet as he lets out another fact.) Kage: “Eric . . . promise you won’t get mad.” (Unsure about what Kage is about to say, Russo tries to remain calm.) Russo: “I won’t get mad.” (Reluctantly, Kage continues.) Kage: “I kind of slept with Rachel too.” (With his fear giving way to a fit of anger, Russo exclaims at Kage.) Russo: “You slept with my sister!?” (Backpedaling, Kage tries to explain himself.) Kage: “Yeah but it didn’t mean anything. It just kind of happened . . . we were both drinking one night at the bar and one thing just kind of led to another.” (Furious with Kage’s admission, Russo flips out.) Russo: “I can’t believe you slept with my sister! How could you do something like that!? And you call yourself my brother!” (Taking offence to Russo’s reaction, Kage begins speaking his mind.) Kage: “I’m not you’re brother. I never wanted to be! Hell, I don’t even like you!” Russo: “Ok, enough!” (Paying no attention to Russo’s request for him to stop, Kage continues his verbal assault.) Kage: “None of us like you Eric. You’re stuck on yourself and you act like you’re somehow better then all of us. That’s the way it’s always been!” (Hearing this, Belmont pipes up in his seat.) Belmont: “Way to go Kage . . . it’s about time somebody told him.” (Kage continues with his onslaught.) Kage: “You just kept your presence held above us so we couldn’t say anything. Threatening us that you would walk out on the Shadow Riders. Acting like it was a privilege for guys like us to even be associated with someone as great as you. We had to put up with that bullshot from you since day one. Hell, I’ve had to put up with living with you for seven years and I’m probably gonna have to put up with dying with you and I don’t like it one bit. Damn you Russo, damn you straight to hell.” (Luke Justice just watches on as Kage and Russo argue back and forth locked ack into his trance like state. Russo now calls out to Luke and Belmont.) Russo: “Luke . . . Tristan . . . you guys don’t like me? I can’t believe you guys don’t like me either.” (Luke just stares at Russo unresponsive in his catatonic state while Belmont just turns his head away so he doesn’t have to look Russo in the eyes. Seeing this through the darkness, Russo speaks out.) Russo: “Oh . . . I see how it is. I give you guys everything. Everything I do, I do it for you three and you guys don’t even like me. Well then fuck all three of you . . . I don’t need the Shadow Riders. You guys need me!” (Kage turns his attention to Lexus who is nearly frozen in fear in the seat next to Russo and calls out to her.) Kage: “Hey Lexus . . . you don’t wanna go anywhere with Russo. He’s an asshole and he doesn’t even know Howard Stern. He’s just trying to get in your pants. Don’t listen to anything he tells you . . . more often then not he lying.” (The plane jerks sideways and begins to drop through the sky like a rock. This sensation seems to kick start Lexus who yells out to both Russo and Kage.) Lexus: “Both of you shut up! I don’t wanna hear anymore of this! Shut up! Shut up! Shut up!” (Paying little attention to Lexus’ request, Russo yells back to Kage.) Russo: “Yo Jason, if we make it through this you’re still a dead man!” (Calling back up to Russo, Kage responds.) Kage: “Ooh . . . I’m real scared. The big Mr. Superstar is gonna get me. Don’t hurt me Mr. Superstar. I know I couldn’t match up to someone as great as you. I mean a someone like me isn’t even on your level. Big bad Eric is gonna beat me up for blowing his cover. I’m so sorry for telling her that you’re nothing but a lying, self centered, asshole. Please forgive me . . . I don’t know what I was thinking.” (Russo tries to get out of his seat in order to attack Kage but the turbulence keeps him from being able to get to his feet. Knowing that he won’t be able to get to Kage, Russo lets out a yell of frustration. This pushes Lexus over the edge as she screams out once again.) Lexus: “That’s it! I quit! I don’t want this job!” (The shaking intensifies knocking Luke out of his trance. Luke finally interjects himself into the argument between Russo and Kage.) Justice: “Damn both of you! Just get over yourselves, you’re Shadow Riders! I’m sick and tired of all this in-fighting between us! We’re about to die and all you two can do is argue back and forth . . . what the hell! You guys are always talking about the group this . . . the group that! Well this is the group and you two are ripping it apart at the seems! If you can’t see that, I don’t even wanna be in this group! I’d be better off on my own then in a group that can’t get along!” (Russo turns around in his seat as him and Kage lock eyes in a hate filled stare. The jet is trembling and shaking in the air harder and harder with each passing second as the ground moves closer and closer. Lexus is in her seat crying profusely. Suddenly, the silent cameraman realizes that this may be his last chance to say anything. He calls out as loud as he can.) Cameraman: “I’m gay!” (Everyone in the cabin turns to the cameraman with a look of shock on their faces. Suddenly, the jet levels out as it breaks through the clouds. Streams of sunlight gleam through the windows piercing through the darkness and lighting the interior of the cabin as the jet comes in for a landing in Charlotte, North Carolina. The interior of the cabin is flooded by an menacing silence. None of the people in the cabin can bring themselves to look at one another. The cockpit door swings open once again as the co-pilot steps into the doorway with a smile spread across his face from ear to ear. He begins to speak in a joyous tone.) Co-pilot: “We did it! We’re saved! We’re gonna live!” (With the passengers still in a state of shock, the jet touches down on the ground at a small airport. The jet pulls into one of the open gates as the co-pilot opens the cabin door. Sullenly, the passengers of the jet get up out of their seats and exit the aircraft into a long hallway. The group plods down the hallway in silence. The group is deep in thought about the events that have just transpired. None of them wanting to so much as utter a word to another. In an attempt to break the silence, Kage speaks.) Kage: “Well that was interesting . . .” (Hearing Kage’s comment renews Russo’s anger at him. Russo jumps at Kage and takes him down to the floor. They are quickly pried apart however by Luke and Belmont. After cooling Russo and Kage down again, the group continues down the hallway. When the group finally reaches the terminal, the group splits into three smaller groups without so much as a word. Luke and Belmont break off and head towards the baggage claim area. Kage and the Cameraman walk off to find a taxi, and Russo and Lexus head off towards a small bar located inside the airport. Each group heads off in a different direction towards Realm. As the picture fades out to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again where we hear a voice-over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The picture then cuts to a black, blank screen.)
