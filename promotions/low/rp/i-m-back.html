I'm Back

15 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The sun is rising, birds are chirping, the breeze is kicking up, and waves are crashing into the shores. The aqua blue water is crystal clear making it translucent to see the underworld of the sea. Zooming in, you see no more than an absolutely exotic paradise. Palm trees, coconuts, wild berries, and colorful birds make up the scenery. Dolphins jump in and out of the water, and the air smells purely like vacation. In the distance, a mansion complements the small island. It is rather large containing a basketball court, pool, and even a small airstrip. Getting closer, panning in a circular motion, around the pool a man is shown laying down in a reclining chair. He has on a blue bathing suit, dark black glasses shade his eyes, and he is smoking on a fine hand rolled cigar with a well-known unmentionable substance. His complection is a darkish brown-black in color. His face is made up of average features, and a thin goatee lines the edge of his mouth and face. Covering his bald head is a blue du rag tied behind the back of his head. He removes his shades and sets them onto a small glass table beside him. “Chitty, Chitty, Bang, Bang, Ain’t Nothin’ but a Crip Thang ” the familiar face, Dark Haze, makes his presence known. “How’s it going? How is the U.S.?” He is smiling with no care, “So where has Haze been? What has Haze been doing? I am about to fill everyone in.” Dark Haze sips his alcoholic ‘Whiskey Sour’ before speaking, “Dark Haze left wrestling, took a little break, and he even left the country. I wanted to see what else this world has to offer. I’ve been many places and seen many faces. I’ve thugged on the streets of Atlanta, and I banged in the hoods of Decatur. I have faced the best boxers that the world has to offer. I’ve spit on microphones across the nation, and I’ve even fought many topnotch wrestlers from the Meyhu’s to ‘The Jamester,’ of TXW and LOW, to Status Quo of RUSH Pro Wrestling. Competition remained high no matter what the job was, and only one other thing was similar between every on of my jobs; it was always about a dollar. Hell, I live for money, and money lives for me. A young thug grinding the streets for every cent I could get or take, I matured and found a spot in a business that would be around forever. I believe I was seventeen when I landed a spot on the roster of True X-treme Wrestling. I would face stars like Sadistic and the Meyhu Brothers. Believe it or not they were like family; they beat my ass around the ring, and they molded me into a man. A man with power, a man with force, a man with strength, and a man with the ability to knock out any wrestler. As we all know, TXW would soon come to an end, and my title as the reigning World Champion would become a worthless ornament for one of my walls, along with the other trophies I took home. I would move to a new federation by the name of RUSH Wrestling or RUSH Pro Wrestling. I also spent time in LOW, XWF, and DW, and I competed in two at the same time. Becoming extremely run down from the two federation wrestling career I decided to chill out for a while and live out a dream. After all, I had accomplished much more than I ever imagined and had plenty of money to enjoy life. For one, I was able to have a short career at Tha Row records, formerly known as Death Row. But now, now it was time to move on, time for bigger, better things. My one dream was to have an island of my own. I left my careers behind and fulfilled the only open obligation I had, make my dream come true.” Haze levitates his arms in display, “Look what I got. I own paradise; paradise is my home. From a gang banger to a superstar athlete, to a connoisseur of cigars, buds, and alcoholic beverages life has changed a great deal. Despite few similarities, I kind of like the new life. Yet, I must not let it go unmentioned, that I have almost been bored by living in this paradise. I still write rhymes, and I still have endorsements to make my cash flow in, but I miss the other aspect of the streets, or even wrestling. I miss the physical and emotional part, as well as the action. I have no worries here. I miss the anticipation of an upcoming fight. I miss the beef and drama, and most importantly I suffer from the lack of competition and fighting. I want to be out there, whether it’s in the ring or on the street I want to be beating the dome of my opponent, physically leaving them lifeless. On occasions I may even have my ass handed to me, leaving me the reminder that there will always be someone who can out do me, but thats just part of the excitement. I must be prepared for anything.” Haze rips the flavorful cigar and washes it down with his mixed drink. “Well, Lights Out Wrestling I have been preparing; I’ve trained to the max. My potential may just be peaking, and I am ready. I’ve gained strength, knowledge, and perhaps become a better person. I changed my life, became successful, and saw everything. It’s time for round two. This is my second shot, and I do not plan on blowing it.” A door sounds and a lady is shortly present. She holds a tray containing a well-balanced, home cooked, southern meal. Grits, eggs, bacon, cornbread, and biscuits with gravy are only part of the tray’s makeup. Along with the food, the tray contains a cigar just like the one previously smoked and a new Whiskey Sour. She rests the tray on the table. “Can I get you anything Mr. Haze?” she asks. “I’m fine,” Haze replies as he sparks up the cigar torching the tip of it. He continues puffing, and clouds of smoke billow from his mouth leaving a fresh aroma in the air. He grabs a remote and presses a button to turn on a stereo. Before you can even imagine his food is gone. Haze wipes his face clean with a napkin, and then he looks at his watch. “Hmmm...to go in, or not to go in?” A couple seconds later you find Haze running towards his pool. From a full sprint comes a double back flip followed by an enormous splash. Haze head pops out of the water similar to a turtle’s head. He then hops onto a floating chair and begins to relax. “So they say they’ve booked the matches already. They say that they are giving me a shot to prove myself. They say Infinite II will be Xtreme.” Haze pauses, “Well I guess they called upon the right person. Xtreme title, warrior title, hell even the world title, I was there when they were born. Yeah, many people don’t remember the beginning of LOW, but I sure do. I was there; I was most certainly there. How I reminisce upon the old days, when the name Dark Haze represented power, glory, and fame. The days when the ‘Tru Blue Superstar’ actually had some significance in meaning. The day when my name could be mentioned and everyone knew who you were speaking of. The days when I had respect, no matter what my personality was like. Those were the good old days.” He sips a drink that his maid brought to him. “Yet the birth of these titles wasn’t when LOW was opened it was even before that. It was when True Xtreme Wrestling was around. The first ever Xtreme federation. The federation that was always imitated, but never duplicated. The federation that booked the most Xtreme matches with the most Xtreme stipulations. It was TXW; the fed which made my career.” Haze turns his attention the other way, and calls out “Can you bring me another cigar?” His attention is focused again, and his expression looks as if he is completely obliterated. Although he is Dark Haze. “Now where was I? Oh yeah, Xtreme. They say that they’ve put me into a tournament with four contenders including myself. Round one: Dark Haze v. The Demond and Cyrus Skar v. Ken Kaze.” “Here you go sir.” Haze’s maid hands him the cigar and a lighter. While she waits, Haze lights up the cigar and puffs the flame up making sure it’s lit well. “Thank you mam,” he hands her the lighter, and she goes on her way. “Anyway, first things first...” Haze holds in a hit. “...first round, I’m paired up with The Demon, how cliche. A 7'3 almost 300 pound monster. A powerhouse, brawler who calls himself Hell’s Guardian. Need I say more?” Haze cracks up, as he takes another hit and holds it in. “Demon, Demond, are you sure your not Diamond? I believe sometime along the line there used to be a wrestler named Diamond Dallas, of WCW or something like that. No affiliation huh?” Haze pauses, “I’m getting off track again. So, I have to fight a demon of a Demond, how nice? He had a troubled life as a child, was institutionalized, broke out, moved away, and came back to reek havoc. He’s dominated two feds in his life and is going for a third. My background may not be quite as interesting, but as some famous person has said before, “that doesn’t mean shit.” If you want to talk about backgrounds, I’m an experienced pro boxer, pro wrestler, shit I was a gang banger, my brothers banged too, my father was in the Yakuza, the Japanese Mafia, and plain and simple I’ve had tougher challenges to face. Supposedly, Hell’s Gaurdian has been running around Lights Out, week in and week out, tearing people up and leaving there broken bodies as a show of his abilities. Well I have been out of the game for a while but as far as I know many of us could accomplish feats like that, just we choose to stop when it’s over. Well all joking aside, I was here the other night when The Demond took on...” a pause interrupts his speech. “Shit, I forget, but I did see your match last week. I saw all seven feet three inches and all 300 pounds of beast. I saw my competition, and frankly I wasn’t impressed. You definitely aren’t flawless, because I saw your opponent straight split your head wide open. I saw that man roll all over you, manhandling you like a rag doll. Yep, all seven plus feet. I mean you may have a great, winning record, but there wasn’t an impressive win. I mean, you almost lost last week, he reversed your finisher for crying out loud. All he needed was a little more experience. I have come to LOW to reclaim the fame I once had, and I will stop the unstoppable. I have that experience. After all, I am only about a foot shorter than you, which is about a foot or more taller than the Japanese people you used to fight. I think I’m in pretty good shape for this match, and I wouldn’t be surprised to see the train, Demond, turn into a train wreck” Haze keeps smoking, and his part Asian eyes grow smaller and smaller. “As for Cyrus Skar, he’s a great athlete. He’s somewhat a veteran like me. I’ve never fought him, so I find that it would be a great opportunity, for me and him, to face each other for the title. His opponent Ken Kaze, I haven’t heard much of him besides for how he thinks he deserves this shot. I just hope that he can make a good match of this and wear down Cyrus; I’d hate to have to waste all my energy fighting someone at full strength when I’m only at half or three quarters. The Demond should be flattered if he can make me use more energy than that. Yet I can’t get ahead of myself; I must focus on the one they call The Demond. Although, don’t expect me to not be ready for the second match of the night; I was born ready.” Haze continues his drinking and smoking. “This is but the beginning; you’ll always be able to expect dark haze to be around when the lights are out. Chitty, Chitty, Bang, Bang. True blue for life.” Dark Haze throws up a few hand signs, as the view slowly fades out to a picture of Dark Haze’s private island.
