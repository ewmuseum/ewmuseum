Rising From the Ashes II

captured 4 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's not true that life is one damn thing after another; it is one damn thing over and over. This is mindset. This is where my heart lies. Fed up. I am a caged animal. A wild animal. At least that’s where I’ve been these past few months. No longer will I grumble over the situations that I have plagued me. No longer will I allow them to ride my thoughts like shadow riders always behind me. And like that wild animal, I will stalk my prey. And ultimately… Unleash the Beast. It’s a raining day in Tampa. The black drapery of night has been hanging for hours as the twilight moon has taken center stage. The stars glisten as the purple clouds somberly mope over the sleeping citizens. Yet, there is one whom is awake. One who hasn’t slept for what seems like five months now. A man whom has been deprived of the very life force that kept his spirit within alive. It’s feeding time. Yes, this man is a man starving of something that was unexpectedly, deliberately, and maliciously taken away from him within the blink of an eye. In the outskirts of Tampa lay in ruins a rundown, bullet-holed, rat infested church. Atop this mighty structure of decay is a bell tower, but there is no more bell. Just a man and his thoughts…His nightmares…His passions. A light mist begins to fall from above. A distant roar of thunder protrudes from miles away. A flash of lightning without a sound illuminated the entire view of the city. We see this man in the bell tower. His hair is black and dangles in his face; a face covered in black and red. His eyes are like that of lightning, blazing and consumed with adrenaline. Though he wears the costume of subtlety, his heart is racing like no other within. He knows that right now is not the time to unleash what he has found within this last year. He has learned to manipulate an entity that has haunted his dreams; this force that is unwilling to let his mind rest at ease. What is this entity one may ask. What could have plagued this man’s dreams for so long, and now, as it appears, is one in the same with him? How can a man take a terrorizing enemy and convert it into his ally? This man’s enemies of old know all-to-well what this spirit is that lurks within the domicile of his body. It has a specific name, but no face. It has a heart, but no body but that which it consumes. This thing has festered within his mind, heart and soul for as long as he can remember. Death and sadness were its parents. He adopted it and made it his child. This is a Beast. A Beast with no remorse. No mercy. No forgiveness. This spirit is his skill against those who oppose him. It isn’t used for mere pleasure, but only for necessity… The Evil Necessity. But does one truly, truly, understand the concept of the situation Warrior has faced his entire life? His family empire has fallen…His parents…His brother…His wife…His best friend. All gone. Every last one of them. Some are dead, some are alive and there is one in between. All Warrior has left is himself and God. Who needs anyone more than that? Warrior is just that…A warrior. And he needs no other than the Almighty for his strength. The situation with Warrior is this: Mind-games. For Warrior, it’s all psychological. He has another persona within him, the Beast, and he is now just learning how to co-exist with it. How was the Beast created? A plethora of emotions, situations, people and experiences all wrapped into one. That’s what the Beast is. When it is summoned all morality fades from Warrior’s senses. For such a long time Warrior has been haunted by this demon within. The entity constantly calling his name in his sleep. Even while he is awake. He tried to ignore it, but the forces soon became to strong for him to bare. And now, with Alla and his best friend Isaac Tyron out of the picture, Warrior is totally consumed with the animal dwelling within him…Wild, and ready to be let go. Now the time has come for Jason Warrior to align himself with one Jason Ortiz – World Heavyweight Champion. The two will go head to head with one of LOW’s notorious Tag Team Legends…The Shadow Riders…Ego-maniacs who think they’re gods among men. Boys who think they can just “trick” all of LOW and expect to be worshiped. Not going to happen. Ortiz and Warrior have put their differences aside…For good. What’s in the past is in the past. What’s in the future is most important now. Warrior knows the importance of this main even come Realm. And he isn’t about to fail. Warrior has been able to obtain a long undefeated streak here within LOW…And frankly, that’s what he holds closest to his heart. Not the gold, the friendships, the business nor the women…Warrior takes pleasure in his true purpose. To dominate. And all will come to pass… Warrior is headed for the World Heavyweight Championship, regardless of he holds the strap at that time. Warrior has learned to respect people like Jason Ortiz…But what is important now is to be the king of the mountain. The Shadow Riders, truly a bunch of girls hiding behind a logo of steadfast horses, will come to a violent halt…A rude awakening, this coming weekend. The cocky attitude, the asinine arrogance will all fall down. They always do in the end. I’ve beaten one of ‘em before…When they were the Metallo Zords…So Warrior at least has an understanding of what to expect this Saturday. The jackasses will meet a true wild animal in due time. The heart racing within him is not biological, but the pounding footsteps of the creature chained within. The chains of solitude and loneliness. The key was lost, but is now found once again. This key will unlock these chains and so unleash a force holy and unholy simultaneously. This key, however, will not present itself for another day. This key will arrive in the form of a competition. This key has a name… This key lies within a Realm of battle and war… This key is Lights Out Wrestling. The battle has begun again. The lightning continues to flash as the thunder begins to protrude its low and hollow sounds. But do we remember this soul? Do we remember what he stands for? Do we remember what he has done for such a company as Lights Out Wrestling? He is the Warrior of past, present and future. He is the one that will deliver the greedy, selfish, deceitful and dark from their hellish lives. He is the one that will overcome the stepping stones that lie within grasp of what he originally set out to accomplish in the first place. This is no place for heroes. This is no place for masked men in tights to perform tricks simply for attention. This is about not a sideshow set to amuse those with no lives. This is a damn Realm of war…Bloody and unrelenting. This place has no flashy lights and glamour. This place is nothing more than a dark ally, consumed with blood, sweat and tears. The day is upon us. The day that a new era begins. Not just any new era, but one that will be lead by a wave of passion and desire. …All that is this business is embodied in one man… …One soul… …He lives again… …It has awaken… …The Beast… …Has been unleashed… Enough said.
