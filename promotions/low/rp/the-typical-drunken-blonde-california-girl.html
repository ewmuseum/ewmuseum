The Typical Drunken Blonde California Girl

captured 6 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens overlooking what looks to be an old wooden cabin on the banks of a dark river. A single light post illuminates the night sky, and sends its reflection shimmering across the shimmering water. Loud music can be heard coming from inside the cabin and the camera slowly fades into it. We see CJ Tyler, bandages and scratches spread across his body like he’d just gone through a garbage disposal. He isn’t alone, as a single adult female, probably around Tyler’s age if not younger accompanies him. The woman has a very nice, curvaceous body, with long blonde hair tossing and turning and she dances with Tyler to the music. As they dance, the bottles of beer in their hands slosh about, dropping bits of the liquid onto the carpet. Finally, the music stops and both people plop down onto the couch next to them. “Whew! Can I throw a party or what, baby doll?” Tyler exclaims as he chugs the rest of his beer and belches loudly. Tyler tosses the empty bottle to the ground and watches it roll out of sight. “Like, yeah, this is, like, so much fun,” says the Typical Drunken Blonde California Girl (TDBCG) as she slams down the rest of her beer. “Hey baby, you mind gettin’ me another beer out of the fridge in there?” Tyler says as he tilts his head back and closes his eyes, possibly trying to clear his head from the drinking, the car crash, or the brutal Junkyard Brawl he went through last week. TDBCG gets up from her spot on the couch and dizzily struts her way into the kitchen. Tyler rubs his forehead a bit, squinting his eyes. “Heh, I told Card I could see the future. I saw the ending of our match just like it happened. Still can’t figure out why he punched me in the car though.” Suddenly, a bottle of beer flies through the air, landing squarely between Tyler’s legs. Tyler lungs forward, letting out a high-pitched squeal. Tyler clutches his groin area in pain as TDBCG comes back into the room, laughing drunkenly. “Thanks for the beer,” Tyler says between moans of pain. “You’re, like, welcome.” TDBCG takes a seat next to Tyler and places her hand on his inner thigh, trying to arouse the pain out of him. Tyler smiles and grabs the beer by his hand, twisting off the cap with a “pop”. Tyler takes a swig and starts to lean in towards TDBCG, when a little electronic jingle begins to play. The jingle sounds like “Californication” by Red Hot Chili Peppers. Tyler looks down, trying to find where the sound is coming from. He reaches his hand through the cushion between him and TDBCG. He searches through there for a second until his hand emerges, clutching a small, silver cell phone. Tyler flips the phone open and presses it into his ear. “Hello? Yes, this is CJ. Yea, I’ve got a minute,” Tyler gets up from the couch and walks away from TDBCG. TDBCG looks at him with confusion, wondering what could possibly pull him away from her. Tyler walks out of the cabin and onto the front patio. He shuts the front door behind him. “So, I’ve got a match against Max Roswell this week? You couldn’t book me against someone more talented?” Tyler asks into the phone. “Alright, but I’m sure it won’t be a long match, can’t promise you that I’ll make him last more than a few minutes. Alright, later.” Tyler moves the phone from his ear and snaps it closed. He pushes the front door open and goes back into the cabin. He grabs a pad of paper and a pen and jots something down quickly, then stuffs it into his pocket. Tyler walks back to the couch, but TDBCG had seen Tyler write something down and is now eager to see what it was. “Like, what did you just, like, write down?” TDBCG asks as Tyler jumps onto the couch next to her. “Nothing.” “Sure, what is it?” “I told you, it’s nothing.” “If you want to get anything from me tonight, you’re gonna have to show me what it is.” Tyler lowers his head and reluctantly points to his left pants pocket. TDBCG slides her hand into his pocket, looking into Tyler’s eyes as she does. She grabs something and squeezes…hard. Tyler lets out a very loud yelp. Tyler looks at TDBCG with anger, but she just smiles back at him. “You better be careful down there, or there’s not gonna be anything for me to please you later.” TDBCG laughs as she finally grabs the right thing and pulls out the slip of paper. She unfolds it with an eager smile on her face. As she reads it, her smile fades and is replaced by confusion. “Why does this say ‘Maxi-pads in Pittsburgh’?” Tyler stares at TDBCG very seriously, looking sternly into her eyes. “Are you stupid or something? That’s were you gotta go to get the best ones.” “No way! I always just went to Longs at the corner.” “Well, that’s why you’re always so cranky.” “Wait a minute, why do you, like, know about Maxi-pads?” “Maxi-pad is a wrestler I’m fighting this weekend in Pittsburgh.” “Oh,” TDBCG says with a chuckle. “Is it a Maxi-pad match?” Tyler gives her a look that symbolizes, “You gotta be kiddin’ me”. “What?” TDBCG asks with annoyance. “Nothing.” “So, you gonna beat this guy, or what?” “Heh, of course. Wait! I think I’ve got another vision of the future!” Tyler exclaims with excitement. TDBCG looks at him with astonishment, eager to here what the future holds. “What do you see?!” “I see you. You’re in a dark room, and your taking off your clothes,” Tyler says with one eye closed, the other one peeking out at TDBCG. “Hmmm, I’m thinking you really can see the future,” TDBCG says as she gets up and pulls Tyler towards the bedroom. The camera goes back to the shot of the lake and the light post as we hear a zipper unzip and a deep voice, not Tyler’s, say: “Open up, big boy!” “What the ----?!” The scene fades out.
