Obligatory Training Pt. 2

captured 2 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When we last saw LOW’s savior, “The Loose Cannon” Jason Kage, he found himself in quite the predicament. Not one to over work himself, Kage had been kicking back and enjoying his New York penthouse along with all the luxuries that come with it. It was much to his dismay however, that he found his fellow Shadow Rider Eric Russo’s valet, Lexus, waiting for him when he arrived home. Lexus, who looks down on characters such as Kage, was apparently sent by Russo in order to make sure that Kage was taking his upcoming match at Infinite II seriously and not just living the extravagant Shadow Rider live style. Obviously, Lexus did not see what she wanted to. Disappointed with his drinking and lack of preparation, she demanded that he begin training. Not one to bend to anybody’s requests, Kage ignored her demands and remained in his penthouse unfazed. Lexus, however, was just as tenacious with her will as Kage was with his, and refused to leave until she made some headway. The last we saw of the duo they were trapped in a stalemate in the penthouse, neither one backing down. Did Kage cave in and listen to her requests? Usually unlikely, however if I did not, did he alienate himself from his best friend and partner, Eric Russo? Did Lexus give up and leave the penthouse? She may have, but if she did would she only be sent back later to finish the job?
