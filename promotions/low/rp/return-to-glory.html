Return to Glory

captured 20 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::The scene opens to a daytime shot of Biscayne Bay in Miami, Florida. An overcast fills the sky as some light drizzle falls onto the calm surface of the water where a plethora of boats are anchored. The shot then centers on the screened door of an expensive looking beach house as it slowly opens. From the door emerges a shirtless Eric Russo. From his appearance, it is obvious that Russo has been keeping himself in shape since his retirement. The former LOW champion starts toward a small dinghy tied to a dock in front of his house. Russo unties the boat and climbs into it. Russo steers the boat out into bay as he navigates around the gigantic sailboats and yachts. Finally the boat comes to a stop next to a twenty-five-foot yacht as Russo cuts the engine. Almost as if rehearsed to the point of perfection, Russo ties the small boat rear rail of the yacht and climbs aboard the larger ship. As he climbs onto the deck of the yacht, Russo is startled by a voice.:: Voice: Well if it isn’t the Superstar himself. ::Russo spins to see where the voice is coming from when he locks eyes with a well-dressed man sitting on one of the deck chairs. As Russo continues to study his surprise visitor, the man goes on.:: Man: It’s been a long time Eric. ::Russo nonchalantly reaches down and opens a cooler, snatching a soda from inside.:: Man: Do you know who I am Eric? ::Russo nods his head as he responds.:: Russo: I remember you from LOW’s backstage. We talked right before my match against Psymon. ::The man smiles at Russo’s remembrance of him and continues.:: Man: That was a hell of a match you had with Psymon. I thought you had the win so many times during that one. It really could have gone either way that night. ::Without missing a beat, Russo responds.:: Russo: But it didn’t Man: Well you win some and you lose some. Sometimes those losses can really stick with you though can’t they? They can just stop any and all momentum that you got going. ::Russo cockily fires back.:: Russo: That’s the way it would seem. ::Reassuringly, the man continues on.:: Man: I’ll still say that you were one of the best in that ring though. You had all of the tools. You were fast, strong, had a killer instinct. The only problem is you always wasted too much time in finishing the job. ::Russo nods his head in agreement as he responds.:: Russo: Yeah well, I have a bad knee to remind me of that now. ::The man glances toward Russo’s knee then back Russo’s face as he goes on.:: Man: Well injuries always were a part of the game. ::Annoyed at his comment, Russo looks to the man whose face is now filled with a smile. Growing impatient, Russo cuts the man to the quick.:: Russo: So did you just come down here to break my balls or is there a point to this visit? ::The man replies in a dry and to the point fashion.:: Man: LOW wants you back in the ring. ::Russo pulls back in confusion at the statement as the man continues to explain.:: Man: The King of the Deathmatch tournament is about to take place and we’d like to see you in it. Your return match would be against Donny Diamond on this weeks’ Realm. This could really be a great match. ::Not even considering the offer, Russo responds.:: Russo: I’m retired. Man: Yeah and it looks like things are going great for you. ::Russo shrugs his shoulders as he responds.:: Russo: I don’t have any complaints. It’s quiet around here. Nobody bothers me for autographs or pictures. Man: You know, that’s the great thing about going out on a losing note. It gives you a great excuse to just fade away. ::Pushing a little harder now, the man continues.:: Man: Eric do you know what separates a winner from a loser? Russo: A three count? ::The man lets out a slight chuckle at Russo’s remark before continuing.:: Man: Getting back to your feet after you’ve been knocked off em’ . . . I’ve watched you fight back from more adversity then this in the past. You have what it takes to be a leader. You have what it takes to push LOW back to where it used to be. ::The man pushes himself up from his seat and starts toward Russo as he continues.:: Man: A mediocre title run and a loss at the hands of Psymon. Is that really how you wanna be remembered? ::Russo looks out over the bay as he responds.:: Russo: I really don’t care if I’m remembered at all. ::The man moves in closer to Russo as he speaks.:: Man: You still got a few good years left in you. You still have bags of talent and can run circles around a lot of other talents out there. If you enter your name into this King of the Deathmatch tourney, who knows what the future holds for you? ::Russo looks to the man once again as the man continues.:: Man: I can’t make you any promises Eric, but wouldn’t you rather take a chance at reaching your full potential instead of just wasting away down here wondering what could have been? ::The man looks into Russo’s eyes.:: Man: Think it over Eric. ::With that the scene fades out.:: --------------------LATER THAT NIGHT-------------------- ::The scene opens in the master bedroom of Russo’s house where Russo quietly slips in and sits on the side of the bed next to his girlfriend Lexus. Russo pauses for a moment as he studies her peaceful face as she sleeps. Suddenly she stirs, looking up to Russo as she does so.:: Lexus: I was having a dream. ::Interested, Russo questions her.:: Russo: Was it a good one? ::Lexus’s face scrunches in confusion as she responds.:: Lexus: I’m not really sure. In my dream you were sky diving and I was on the ground watching you. You were refusing to open your parachute though. ::Russo chuckles at the dream as questions further.:: Russo: Did you catch me before I hit the ground? Lexus: No. ::Taken aback by this response, Russo questions Lexus.:: Russo: Why not? ::Lexus sits up and pulls Russo in close to her in an embrace as she responds.:: Lexus: I’m not sure . . . maybe because you’ve never really needed my help before. ::Russo smiles as he responds.:: Russo: You really had me going there for a second. You didn’t have a dream like that. ::Lexus smiles as she teases Russo a little more.:: Lexus: Maybe I did . . . ::Russo and Lexus share a kiss but it is cut short as Russo pulls back from her.:: Russo: Honey . . . ::Russo struggles to push the words out of his mouth, knowing what Lexus’ reaction to them will be.:: Russo: . . . If I had to do something to get some closure . . . You would want me to do it right? . . . No matter how crazy the idea sounded? ::Lexus studies Russo’s face for a moment before pulling away from him.:: Lexus: Oh god . . . you wanna start wrestling again! ::Russo tries to calm the situation.:: Russo: Only one more run. I want you to come on this run with me too. This is really important to me. ::Russo’s words do little to stem Lexus’ anger.:: Lexus: You said you were done with wrestling! You said you were going to stay here with me! What could possibly be so strong to pull you back to that life?! ::Russo looks away from Lexus as he speaks.:: Russo: It’s just something that I have to do if I wanna be right with myself. ::Lexus shakes her head in disgust as she screams out.:: Lexus: You want me to give you my blessing on this!? You want me to tell you that I support you!? Fine then, go! Get out! ::With her fury now exploding, Lexus shoves Russo out of the bed and points to the door. With a sunken expression, Russo pushes himself to his feet and leaves the bedroom, softly closing the door behind him as the scene fades out.::
