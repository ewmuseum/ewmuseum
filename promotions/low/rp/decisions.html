March 20th, 2004- Miami Florida. His head had popped up from the couch as he realized that he had yet again, fallen fast asleep in the middle of his Miami Beach House. Rolling over, with his hair messed up, and blowing his hair through the windows. Jason scratched his eyes. He actually had told Steven Scott, earlier to make sure that he wouldn’t fall asleep. Did that happen? No. The Nazi had giving Jason Ortiz his own word to make sure that he would wake him up, but again, his word wasn’t shit anymore. As Jason looked around the room, he could tell from just looking at the reflection of the sun on his windows that it was a little past noon. He should have set an alarm or something. Steven Scott, was actually no where in the living room? Jason yelled for him. “STEVEN!” He shouted, his voice echoing off his white walls that needed to be repainted very badly. “STEVEN!” There had been a few seconds before Jason even said anything. He had finally realized that Steven was probably outside, or went for a walk. Who fucking knows? As long as Steven wasn’t here, Jason was all good. It wasn’t like Jason was going to kill the poor Nazi bastard, but the fact had remained- he didn’t wake Jason up from his nap. For a while now, that had been the first time that Jason had shut his eyes for more than an hour. Miracle. Miracles do happen to Crack Heads too... Pretty Boy Jason Ortiz shifted his body. He sighed. “Well I guess it’s just me...and,” he looked down towards the floor next to his white couch. His faithful dog was lying there. Asleep. Doing nothing- like usually. That thing wasn’t good for anything. The motherfucker would just sleep all day, and at night, when Jason heads to bed, the bastard will keep you up with his barking. Sometimes Jason loves that dog. Sometimes he wants to murder it. That’s the beauty of having an animal in your house. He pulled himself up of the white couch. Amused, he lifted his arms up and swung them towards his dog’s body, but for some reason. It didn’t flinch. “You lazy motherfucker,” Jason growled. Looking towards the table, he noticed that he had slept pretty late. How he found that out was not by looking at the clock, but at another way. The mail was neatly stacked on the table, and told him that the mail comes after 1:00 p.m. His body told him not to pick up the mail. But it also told him to search through it. Jason was going to do that. Picking his body up off the dirty couch he suddenly had sharp pains in his back. He fought through it. As he walked to the neatly folded bills sitting on his table, the one that he has hit his knee on so many fucking times. He bends over and picks up the top letter. The name sounds familiar. Jason shrugs. “Larry Vaughn?” he wonders. Saying the name again. “Larry Vaughn.” As most of you may have remembered Larry Vaughn was the creator the television show staring Jason Ortiz called. “No Place Like Home.” From the beginning Jason thought it would be a splendid idea, but towards the end, he found out something else. The show as using him. Turning Crack Head against the family . He didn’t want any part of that. Knowing that he had put the family through enough made him sick. Jason grabbed the letter and tore it open. It read. Dear, Mr. Jason Ortiz. As you may have remembered, I am Larry Vaughn, the man that used to sign your paychecks in the past. You remember the show we had last time? No Place Like Home. For weeks the thing was a hit Jason. You remember the ratings. Sure that’s all I wanted. Ratings. But you know damn well I was making you money and you were making me money. So I had a proposition for you. I have heard that recently you have left Rehab and are on to a better life. When you left the show, and decided to quit and throw everything in my face... it hurt Ortiz. It really did. I didn’t want it to end like that. We only had about ten great shows. You could have pushed for more. When I read that letter it was like a slap in the face. What did you think the show was supposed to be about? You being friends with the Burke’s? Fuck no. It was about you doing everything to make their lives a living hell, and all the way up until the end, you realized that. That was the purpose. You don’t have to make a decision now. Maybe never. But I had an idea. I noticed that you might be running low on money, and trying to start you life back together. So I had a great idea that could make both of us fucking happy. What is it? It may not be what you are thinking. It has nothing to do with the second season of No Place Like Home. That show is old news. I have a new idea. This will be a whole new show...starring everyone’s favorite CRACK HEAD. I am not here to fuck you over Jason Ortiz. That is the last thing I would do. This is just an opportunity for you. Think about It Jason, you think you can spend all the rest of your life in that tiny house in Miami. Spend the rest of your life wrestling at Lights Out Wrestling? Think about it. This show will give you a new start...and a new look on life. I can’t explain what the show is about, but if you are interested. You know where my office is. Thanks for you time Jason Ortiz. -Larry Vaughn “Owner of Reason Television.” Was it actually pretty wise for Jason Ortiz to take this man’s offer. Like he had pointed out, this would be a new start for Jason’s shit hole life. It wasn’t going anywhere, and every time he sits in this Miami Beach House, he wonders when his life will turn around. This could be it. Was Larry Vaughn really interested in a new show with Ortiz? “Decisions...” stuttered Pretty Boy Jason Ortiz. “Decisions.” A decision he would have to make really soon. March 20th, 2004- Miami Florida. † In the Mind of a Crack Head† The late nights. The dark nights. It had been another night with Jason sitting there, in bed, trying to gather his thoughts, as he knew, that the next day would he the same thing. Over and over. He noticed from the outside of his bedroom window that it was again pitch-black, and once again dark and gloomy. He rolled over, and squinted. Jason Ortiz had seen it again. It was something that he wanted to see, as it had disappeared from him in the past. His eyes looked around the room, and he just sat in his bed. He wanted answers. The strive to be the best had turned Jason’s life to fucking hell. If only for one day... his life would be actually. Normal. But it would never be normal. The things that happened in his past just really fucked it over for him. But that is what he would have to do. Take the pieces back and try to put it back like a puzzle. That’s what his life was now. A puzzle. He didn’t know how he would take it. He didn’t know which direction to take. As each day goes by, it’s inevitable that it may be until the day that he dies that Jason regains his dignity. His heart and everything inside of him still hurt. The thoughts of his former girlfriend Kelly, handing back the ring a few nights back was a sign of something. But what was this so-called sign. But this night... she had returned. Maybe with a message. Maybe with an explanation. .......Why...... Jason lifts his head up and tries to make out what his former girlfriend is saying. It is dark, and the visions of seeing her again, made him instantly wake up. He cringed. He tried to speak, as he seen her body move forward. Pretty Boy Jason Ortiz’s lips shivered with a cheer of pain. It had been a while since he cried. “Why can’t you tell me what is wrong?” cried Jason Ortiz, letting out a large sigh after that. “What does this entire mean?” .......Why...... Sure he really didn’t have here ring in real life from his past dreams a few nights ago. After tonight he would wake up, he would look back on this. These were the only dreams, in the morning that Jason Ortiz still remembers. He remembers them very clearly. He can point out every mistake and every word that comes out of these dreams. Jason leans against the bed, his body shifts in an uncomfortable position. Ortiz holds out his hand. “Please, take the ring back...” Kelly moves back just a little bit. He will do whatever it takes this time to keep her here for a while. The sight of seeing her for the second time since she died was enough to just satisfy him, but knowing, that she would be gone in a matter of minutes, or even less... seconds. This was his chance to gain some answers. Answers into the past. Answers into the fucking future. She smiled at him. He smiled back at her. “... All I have asked for in life now Kelly was to bring you back. I am not God. I can’t shoot down a fucking bolt of god damn lighting to bring your ass back,” he continued, his bottom lip moved faster than the top. “GOD DAMN IT! LISTEN!” March 21st, 2004- On The Road to Lights Out Wrestling. “This week will be different,” protested Jason Ortiz. Why did it have to be like this? Another loss that he had to cope with. He had tried so hard to make it back to the top of Lights Out Wrestling. Maybe one day, hoping to capture that Lights Out Wrestling, World Title. But last week, it was shut down. The chance for him and Jason Warrior to gain something that he hasn’t done in a while. Something that he always wanted to do since the day that he entered Lights Out Wrestling. The Tag Team Championships. It had passed, and after the fall of the match, Jason Ortiz lost more respect for Jason Warrior. If only Jason Warrior would have let him have the pin- so both of them would continue their friendship as the LOW Tag Team Champions. Did that happen? Fuck no. Both of them. The Beast. The Crack Head had different issues on their minds that day. Both had major EGO problems, and none of them realize that. It is like both of them blame their problems on everyone else. It is very true. For Jason Ortiz, blaming everything was due to the fact that again, he still didn’t have his life back together. For Jason Warrior, it was for him to stay undefeated. He wanted the win, to prove that he was the best thing to come into LOW in a long fucking time. This week, things were going to change. Jason Ortiz had stopped thinking about THE BEAST, and started to think to the future. What would hold for the Crack Head in Lights Out Wrestling? What was ahead in his path to glory and fame? “What the hell is stopping me!” Jason screamed, grabbing his head with both hands and shaking it rapidly. Nothing was stopping him; it was just the fact that he still hadn’t put his problems aside in the ring. That was his problem, and these next few weeks, he would have to do that. This week, he had a singles match against a man the he has never heard of. A newcomer to the world of Lights Out Wrestling. He would fall. Jason would gain the victory. End of story. It wasn’t like Jason wanted the match, but deep down inside of his body... he actually did. The strive for being the best started here. He wasn’t going to let it go to waste this week. He was going to make something of his Wrestling Career. He would get it back on track and just maybe. Just maybe, win a match. Finally. If he wins. He will be happier than a drug addict buying a sack of his favorite weed. If not... That was a WHOLE different ball game. He would see how things would work out... In the end.... March 21st, 2004- Miami Florida. † In the Mind of a Crack Head† For the past few nights, Jason Ortiz has not got a wink of any kind of sleep. He tired everything. Sleeping pills. Staying up all night, then go to sleep early in the morning. He tried everything, but nothing seemed to be working. He was about feed up, just thinking about his former girlfriend, Kelly was giving him a heart attack. He can’t stop thinking about her, and as the days go by... it seems inevitable that he misses her much more than when she first died. What was all of this about? The dreams about here. Everything about her. It had to make sense. Everything in this world makes sense, but this was down right confusing Jason Ortiz. As he lies in bed, his dark green eyes looking at the top of his ceiling, he realizes something. The whole situation why she had come back. He finally got it. He finally realized what this was all about. “She wants me to move on.” That was fucking it. All she wanted was that.
