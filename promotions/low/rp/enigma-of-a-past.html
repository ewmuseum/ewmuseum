Enigma of a Past!!!

28 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~ Well what do we have here….Alias? Or is it Enigma….I can never tell, for you see we know learn that Alias and Enigma are in fact one and the same!! Funny isn’t it? What’s even funnier is that I John “The Prophet” Adams is responsible for the mix up….for I gave birth to Enigma….I gave him life! Intentionally, no but do I regret it? No…there is no way that I regret the thing I have done! There is no way that I regret what I did for the man who I owe so much for!! Confused??? Don’t be……………. ~ ~ I have come to believe that the whole world is an enigma, a harmless enigma that is made terrible by our own mad attempt to interpret it as though it had an underlying truth. Umberto Eco (1932 - ) ~ ~ Never tell a man you can read him through and through; most people prefer to be thought enigmas. Marchioness Townsend ~ Time: 4:51 PM Date: August 21st, 2004 Location: EPW Head Quarters before the Bankruptcy. ~ The evening sky was clear and tranquil with only a small sense of urgency of the upcoming doom that was going to happen. As we look closer, we see the building that has produced shows like Aggression and Warfare quite empty for all to see. Except for one lone man inside, one lone man who was responsible for the company EPW to go bankrupt. That man John “The Prophet” Adams, he was sitting in his office alone and depressed of the fact that his business, a business he had worked so hard for is no more, once the proud owner of a company that has produced so much, now only an empty shell of a man. The door at the far right hand corner knocked as it has for so long, but will for the very last time. ~ ~ The once warrior of a kingdom now in ruins…… ~ John Adams: GO AWAY!!! ~ He didn’t want to see anybody right now, nor did he care too. His dreams of EPW were finally laid to rest and there was nothing anybody could do about it. The door flung open to reveal EPW Superstar Alias at the door. Filled with frustration and rage inside his heart, he enters the office. ~ ~ With darkness that blankets his eyes……. ~ Alias: NO, no I’m not going away without an explanation Adams! I was hired by you for one goal in EPW and that is to make money and be the EPW World Champion!! Now I’m going to give you to the count of three Adams and after I better have an answer!! 1…. John Adams: No Alias I don’t have the answer you seek, the bank foreclosed on us just yesterday!! Hell son, even I have to go look for a job! All of the blood, sweat, and tears that I have put into this company and for what? For what Alias, so I can always give guys like you a shot at there dreams? So I can always give guys like you a shot at that top of the mountain? If not that then what Alias…..why don’t you tell me what for then!!!!!!!!! ~ The tension inside the office was well past the boiling point of hatred. Now all that was left was a possible violent solution to be made. As Alias stares down at the eyes of the man who had given his shot he wondered if he actually had what it takes to finish this confrontation that he had started. ~ ~ Blind to the man he once was…… ~ 2…. John Adams: I warn you Alias, I’m not in the mood right now to be taken any shit from you!! You stupid idiot, do you actually believe I was ever going to give you a title shot? Do you actually believe a Jobber as yourself was going to be pushed to that grand stage for the title? ~ John Adams started to laugh hysterically hearing the news. How can this guy….Alias be any good? How can he, there was no way I was going to push a man who clearly had no talent what so ever to get a shot at the belt. ~ ~ Ready for redemption into the land of the damned……. ~ 3…. ~ And in that instant both wrestlers’ lives were changed forever as John Adams lunges at Alias and delivers a spear to the upper body sending Alias down to the ground. John Adams then picks Alias by the head and delivers his most devastating move, “The Prophecy” straight into the desk breaking John Adams office desk into two. ~ ~ A mad man he must face to reclaim his life………. ~ John Adams: You want to know what you can do Alias, do you? You bastard, you and the rest of EPW can go straight to hell!!! I’m sick and tired of all the losers in this fed crying to me why I can’t have a title shot and why the fed is closed! Well it is you fucking loser!!! ~ As Adams was saying this with all the rage and anger built inside of him, he proceeded to grab Alias’s head and pound it to the floor below. ~ ~ Unknowing that the mad man is his creator………. ~ John Adams: Some wrestler you are Alias, you dumb piece of shit!!! You are more of an enigma of wrestling! ~ And with a sudden jolt back to the reality around him John Adams finally realizes the carnage that he has done. ~ ~ And that this mad man is lead by a higher power…… ~ John Adams: My God!! What the hell did I do, Alias….Alias speak to me!!! Shit, I have to call 911!!! ~ John Adams was very lucky that day….lucky that he was able to cover up his outburst of rage…..lucky that no one else was in that building…..also he was lucky that he found his hate, he had found that anger that once drove him to be great. I guess in a morbid sense John Adams was grateful for Alias bursting into his office that day….for he had found his hate and “The Prophecy’s” rebirth. ~ ~ The higher power of “The Prophecy” to lead him into victory!!! ~ ~ I cannot forecast to you the action of Russia. It is a riddle wrapped in a mystery inside an enigma: but perhaps there is a key. That key is Russian national interest. Sir Winston Churchill (1874 - 1965), Radio speech, 1939 By keenly confronting the enigmas that surround us, and by considering and analysing the observations that I have made, I ended up in the domain of mathematics, Although I am absolutely without training in the exact sciences, I often seem to have more in common with mathematicians than with my fellow artists. M. C. Escher (1898 - 1972), Quoted in To Infinity and Beyond, E Maor (Princeton 1991) It may well be doubted whether human ingenuity can construct an enigma... which human ingenuity may not, by proper application, resolve. Edgar Allen Poe, The Gold Bug ~ ~ So Alias, there you have it!! The once enigma of a joke in EPW has finally gotten his shot at the Realm title! But can you handle it? Can you face me Alias or will it be Enigma? (Laughing.) How does it feel Enigma that I, John Adams have given you life? How does it feel to embrace the dark side of humanity at the hands of the Prophecy? Into the Realm of chaos we go stopping at nothing and no one till we finally get what we desire!! Is that it Enigma my boy? Is that what you desire….to rip me into shreds for taking the life of Alias and giving you yours? The end is near Enigma….and this time I will finish the job!!!! ~ ~ FIN ~
