** I’m not the one who’s so far away… ** :: We open in a blank field. Farmers and their families call them dry spots, but in nature they are called dead spots. It’s a spot where the Earth, no matter how toiled and how cared for, just refuses to grow or allow anything to survive at all. These spots are often considered sabotage or just plain vandalism of the ground by other farmers, but it actually a very natural occurrence. It’s not someone who’s into farming wants to see, but it is amusing to people who disapprove of farming in general, but now we’re off topic. The occurrence of dead spots is not so natural lately, and thus we reach our point. Recently dead spots have been occurring due to the negligence and the mistreatment of the Earth, and they have been known to reach a circumference of up to three hundred and fifty feet, in cases where the people owning the land have no clue what they are doing, nor do they toil and treat the land the way they should. So basically the point is negligence and the lackadaisical attitude some people have toward their profession can lead to not only their downfall itself, but can also harm the business itself. Recently the LOW has been plagued by individuals that seem to be “ dead spots “ themselves on the LOW roster. With the competition thinning and the talent decreasing, what is there left to do but to impress the LOW fans enough to keep them in seats? Not a thing, that’s what there’s left to do, and so it shall come to pass. The King of the Death Match Tournament is underway, and with round one starting at Realm… someone has something to say. Edging the Dead Spot in our field we see The Demond, tall, imposing, outright a massive piece of humanity. He skims the still live corn stalks that surround the dead spot as he walks, the dry leaves crackling in his hands. He wears a long trench coat that drags the ground, and we can see Gilgan crouched in the stalks near by, calmly sitting there out of the way, most likely at the demand of our host. :: The Demond: “ Dead Spots Gilgan, that’s what these are called. “ Gilgan: “ Interesting… really… “ The Demond: “ It is indeed interesting you simple minded fool. Think about it a moment, no matter how hard this ground is worked on it will never be any better than it is at this moment. No matter what is done to it, no matter how flashy someone makes it look, no matter what’s planted under the surface, it will always just be a waste, a piece of nothingness. “ Gilgan: “ Oh see, how wrong was I? Nothingness is SO interesting. “ The Demond: “ Your petty remark and childish use of sarcasm is noted… but see if you think about it a moment, this isn’t the first time we’ve been around something like this in our lives. “ Gilgan: “ Examples? “ The Demond: “ Well take CJ Tyler for instance. I had to face him this week at Realm in round one of the King of the Death Match Tournament and what happened? “ Gilgan: “ He canceled so you got a bi. “ The Demond: “ Indeed, he canceled. And do you know why? “ Gilgan: “ Because he’s being whinny and bitchy like all these other homo’s leaving the LOW? “ The Demond: “ Not so, not so at all. He left for a simple and logical reason. “ Gilgan: “ And what was that? “ The Demond: “ He left because he remembers Gilgan. “ Gilgan: “ Remembers what? “ The Demond: “ He remembers exactly what I did to him the last two times we meet in a ring. He remembers that he was made the Realm Champion, BY me. He was given the title because I allowed it and only for that reason. He knows at any moment I can end things for him. I have the power, the ability, and the where-with-all to do something like that as well, and that’s an important step these days. Everyone makes all these promises about how they WILL win, and how they WILL accomplish what they want in life, but no one delivers anymore. “ :: Gilgan stands and makes his way into the circle of dead earth. We now notice a large red mark on the side of his face, almost as if he’d been slapped at such a high rate of speed the mark just stained itself on his skin. :: The Demond: “ Like Albert for instance. “ Gilgan: “ Al? What about that fuckin hoe cake? “ The Demond: “ Well, yesterday we ran into him at that restaurant, and he said right then and there that you wouldn’t leave the place without a little gift from him to you. You were so worried about your car getting damaged… again… that you never saw what he had in mind. When you heard the Prelude Engine and looked up, and got bitch slapped at 45 mph, you should have realized then that he’s smarter than you, and that is why he was my friend, because he’s in the same category in life I am. We both realize what needs to be done and when, and don’t look back on it and think, we just do it because we both have the ability to know its right the first time we do it. Al doesn’t say something and then not do it, he’s a man of his word, even if it pisses people off, that’s Al, all action, little talk, and I like that about him. “ Gilgan: “ Well I don’t like him… and I should like him… “ The Demond: “ I didn’t associate myself with him so YOU could like him… “ Gilgan: “ But still… I should call him, and tell him I’m going to blow him up… “ The Demond: “ Yeah… wait… did you say ‘ blow him up ‘? And just how do you intend to do that? “ Gilgan: “… I meant to say I was going to blow up his car… but I fucked up… leave me alone. “ :: Demond chuckled, breaking off a corn stalk and twirling it in his fingers like a drumstick. :: The Demond: “ But at any rate… I’ve got a bi this week and they said my match is going to end up being against whoever wins between Ron Diamond and Juvian Ramorez… “ Gilgan: “ That bum is wrestling again? “ The Demond: “ Indeed he is, and he seems pretty fired up and ready to go. “ Gilgan: “ Yeah, yeah, yeah, I think the only thing he’s been able to fire up the last several months was the grill, he’s so ring rusted he’ll have to wait before his matches start so the medical guys can give tetanus shots. “ The Demond: “ I’ll take him seriously none the less, it is the fool who enters a battle unprepared that losses a thousand times, to prepare once and dominate. “ Gilgan: “ Can we go now, I think a spider just bit me… “ The Demond: “ Sure it wasn’t an alien like from that movie… what was it… Signs? “ Gilgan: “ … … … … … … … … Dude you know that shit creeps me out, not cool. “ The Demond: “ What was that? “ :: Demond points, Gilgan freaks, and tears off into the corn. Demond just chuckles and walks off. HAHAHAHAHAHHA! I WIN! HAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHHA “ Amy: “ Ok MANDARK, see you later. “ :: Amy leaves the back door of the house as Al laughs over the Dexter’s Lab reference… then Dilgear enters. :: Al: “ Oh… it’s YOU! “ :: The sarcasm could be cut with a knife… or ridiculously large pair of scissors… :: Dilgear: “ Indeed it is. “ Al: “ And what do YOU want? “ :: The at least mild friendship they’d achieved was obvious now, much more comfortable with one another than the last time they spoke to each other. :: Dilgear: “ I was not purposely eavesdropping on you from the living room and need to talk to you. “ :: After the long, long… LONG moment it took Al to figure out how someone could NOT PURPOSELY eavesdrop on someone, he shook his head and sat down. :: Al: “ Go. “ Dilgear: “ Well you see, that look on D’s face recently, it is defeat, you’re exactly right. That’s what Gilgan does to people. “ Al: “ Well we need to fix that shit, I ain’t about to see D like that forever, this little deal of his can last however long it needs to but I ain’t putting up with it for long. “ :: Dilgear smirked :: Dilgear: “ I’m glad to hear you say that… I’ve got a plan… and you’re the only one that can pull it off. “ Al: “ People always relying on me… damn… “ :: Al leaned it to hear the plan. :: ----------------------------------------------------------------- Two days later ----------------------------------------------------------------- :: We open late in the afternoon, the sunlight bleeds from the Earth, and the sky is illuminated a bright orange and pink. The Demond sits on the top of a picnic table, several thousand trees behind him. A Rowan tree sits directly behind him, completely out of place with the tall Pine trees towering behind him. On a lengthy branch that hangs low down the trunk a lone Raven sits, looking down at The Demond. :: The Demond: “ Once upon a midnight dreary, while I pondered, weak and weary, Over many a quaint and curious volume of forgotten lore-- While I nodded, nearly napping, suddenly there came a tapping, As of some one gently rapping, rapping at my chamber door-- ''Tis some visitor,' I muttered, 'tapping at my chamber door— Only this and nothing more.' Ah, distinctly I remember it was in the bleak December; And each separate dying ember wrought its ghost upon the floor. Eagerly I wished the morrow;--vainly I had sought to borrow From my books surcease of sorrow--sorrow for the lost Lenore-- For the rare and radiant maiden whom the angels name Lenore-- Nameless here for evermore. And the silken, sad, uncertain rustling of each purple curtain Thrilled me--filled me with fantastic terrors never felt before; So that now, to still the beating of my heart, I stood repeating, ''Tis some visitor entreating entrance at my chamber door-- Some late visitor entreating entrance at my chamber door;-- This it is and nothing more.' Presently my soul grew stronger; hesitating then no longer, 'Sir,' said I, 'or Madam, truly your forgiveness I implore; But the fact is I was napping, and so gently you came rapping, And so faintly you came tapping, tapping at my chamber door, That I scarce was sure I heard you'--here I opened wide the door;---- Darkness there and nothing more. Deep into that darkness peering, long I stood there wondering, fearing, Doubting, dreaming dreams no mortal ever dared to dream before; But the silence was unbroken, and the stillness gave no token, And the only word there spoken was the whispered word, 'Lenore?' This I whispered, and an echo murmured back the word, 'Lenore!'-- Merely this and nothing more. Back into the chamber turning, all my soul within me burning, Soon again I heard a tapping somewhat louder than before. 'Surely,' said I, 'surely that is something at my window lattice; Let me see, then, what thereat is, and this mystery explore-- Let my heart be still a moment and this mystery explore;-- 'Tis the wind and nothing more!' Open here I flung the shutter, when, with many a flirt and flutter, In there stepped a stately Raven of the saintly days of yore; Not the least obeisance made he; not a minute stopped or stayed he; But, with mien of lord or lady, perched above my chamber door-- Perched upon a bust of Pallas just above my chamber door-- Perched, and sat, and nothing more. Then this ebony bird beguiling my sad fancy into smiling, By the grave and stern decorum of the countenance it wore, 'Though thy crest be shorn and shaven, thou,' I said, 'art sure no craven, Ghastly grim and ancient Raven wandering from the Nightly shore-- Tell me what thy lordly name is on the Night's Plutonian shore!' Quoth the Raven 'Nevermore.' Much I marvelled this ungainly fowl to hear discourse so plainly, Though its answer little meaning--little relevancy bore; For we cannot help agreeing that no living human being Ever yet was blest with seeing bird above his chamber door-- Bird or beast upon the sculptured bust above his chamber door, With such name as 'Nevermore.' But the Raven, sitting lonely on the placid bust, spoke only That one word, as if his soul in that one word he did outpour. Nothing further then he uttered--not a feather then he fluttered-- Till I scarcely more than muttered 'Other friends have flown before-- On the morrow he will leave me, as my hopes have flown before.' Then the bird said 'Nevermore.' Startled at the stillness broken by reply so aptly spoken, 'Doubtless,' said I, 'what it utters is its only stock and store Caught from some unhappy master whom unmerciful Disaster Followed fast and followed faster till his songs one burden bore-- Till the dirges of his Hope that melancholy burden bore Of 'Never--nevermore.'' But the Raven still beguiling my sad fancy into smiling, Straight I wheeled a cushioned seat in front of bird, and bust and door; Then, upon the velvet sinking, I betook myself to linking Fancy unto fancy, thinking what this ominous bird of yore-- What this grim, ungainly, ghastly, gaunt and ominous bird of yore Meant in croaking 'Nevermore.' This I sat engaged in guessing, but no syllable expressing To the fowl whose fiery eyes now burned into my bosom's core; This and more I sat divining, with my head at ease reclining On the cushion's velvet lining that the lamp-light gloated o'er, But whose velvet violet lining with the lamp-light gloating o'er, She shall press, ah, nevermore! Then, methought, the air grew denser, perfumed from an unseen censer Swung by Seraphim whose foot-falls tinkled on the tufted floor. 'Wretch,' I cried, 'thy God hath lent thee--by these angels he hath sent thee Respite--respite and nepenthe, from thy memories of Lenore; Quaff, oh quaff this kind nepenthe and forget this lost Lenore!' Quoth the Raven 'Nevermore.' 'Prophet!' said I, 'thing of evil!--prophet still, if bird or devil!-- Whether Tempter sent, or whether tempest tossed thee here ashore, Desolate yet all undaunted, on this desert land enchanted-- On this home by Horror haunted--tell me truly, I implore-- Is there--is there balm in Gilead?--tell me--tell me, I implore!' Quoth the Raven 'Nevermore.' 'Prophet!' said I, 'thing of evil--prophet still, if bird or devil! By that Heaven that bends above us--by that God we both adore-- Tell this soul with sorrow laden if, within the distant Aidenn, It shall clasp a sainted maiden whom the angels name Lenore-- Clasp a rare and radiant maiden whom the angels name Lenore.' Quoth the Raven 'Nevermore.' 'Be that word our sign in parting, bird or fiend!' I shrieked, upstarting-- 'Get thee back into the tempest and the Night's Plutonian shore! Leave no black plume as a token of that lie thy soul hath spoken! Leave my loneliness unbroken!--quit the bust above my door! Take thy beak from out my heart, and take thy form from off my door!' Quoth the Raven 'Nevermore.'
