Coming Next Week ::The screen fills with a backdrop of a black starry sky, obviously in outer space. The scene has a calm silence around it until rolling drums start up and an amazing march begins playing. As the classical music plays on, scrolling yellow text (Star Wars style) starts rolling over the screen, starting at the bottom and moving up, getting progressively farther and farther away.:: Not too long ago, in a galaxy not too far away from here . . . There was a noticeable unrest sweeping through the several hundred solar systems in the universe. Unrest due to the tyranny of the evil Queen Mogambu. On this day however the winds of change course through the air as the rumbling of a new power passes from person to person. A power the likes of which no living soul has ever witnessed. A power that will defend their rights against all of the evil forces that would attempt to oppress them. A power known as the Metallo Zords. An intergalactic team of heros set to defend the earth from any and all forms of evil . . . ::A quick flash of an intricately designed silver helmet crosses the screen briefly.:: What does the future hold for your planet? Will the evil Queen Mogambu prevail and make the planet earth nothing but a barren wasteland like so may other planets before. Or will your world be the first to be saved from the terrible fate that awaits it? Time will tell, time and the Metallo Zords . . . ::A quick flash of an intricately designed gold helmet crosses the screen briefly.:: For once the battle for the Earth has begun, no living soul will be able to stop it except the Metallo Zords . . .
