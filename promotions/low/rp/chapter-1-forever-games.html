Chapter 1: Forever Games

captured 10 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Forever Games Name: Kasper Sky Date: 15th May 2004 Darkness falls, the disappearance of the sun beneath the never-ending horizon. The fallen kings over the generations, spirits gleaming in an orange indescribable by the words of mere mortals, captivated in one of the unquestionable wonders of the universe. Grey clouds enveloped the gods of the sky, the blue vanishing, overcame by the dull gloomy gray. And day turns to night, all in a matter of time. The second hand ticks by, now only a mere statistic in the eyes around. The world, never really understood. A cycle now accepted without question. Lives revolved, now without heart, honor, spirit and love. Surprising. I think not. Eternal blue the color of the sky, how the evil wings of the night always seem to fly… Look around, and see the revolutions of the circle of life, closing in on us, choking the very life of us, yet nobody cared. Put their hands up, ignored, and finally despaired. Cowards, all of them. A series of robotic mortals, walking in a line, obeying orders and following instructions. The voices in their minds disregarded. Maybe even disrespected. The scent of the air, breathe it in, take it in slowly. Feel it, the mixture of the moisture and dryness, a compelling combination. Question the forces of nature if you must, but believe in it. For mortals like you will always live in the shadows of time. However, immortals, we own time. We bask in eternity. The hourglass slowly but surely trickles away, like the deep red blood will continue to drip until the end of the day… Temptations, the very inner souls of mortals fill with them. Greed over love, money over honor, life over spirit. Examples, all of them, but true enough for the sight to see the building blocks of human life. A form of species that can never be really understood. Instructions obeyed, without even the mind working its way towards thought. Mindless fools, even cowards at their own game. The game of life, the roll of the dice where it’s either dead or alive. Why must the end draw so near, yet so far the wind whispers in fear… Maybe one day you mortals will understand, that not everything seems simple as it’s meant to be. Words of destiny and faith, mere words to captivate your souls, burning you deeper and deeper into the depths of heartfelt sympathy, thus making you weaker towards your cold fearless desires. I shake my head in disbelief, at your lack of evil emotions. Evil is what drives us towards redemption, to atone for our sins in the past, to make sure that in the end, hellfire will not burn us alive. But yet, when is in the end? Unlike you, we immortals drown in eternity, a timeless medium that knows no end. For us immortals, there is always tomorrow. The glittering eyes of the night it may seem, yet on closer look it’s just a symbol of mortal dreams… There is nothing inside you mortals, only the weakness of emotions. You have been living a lie all your lives, dreaming of the heavenly sky, not yet never once have you mortals conquered the heavens. Your failures were made from your own, and yet you blame the gods. Disgrace, the middle name of your species. Extinct, you will have been if we immortals have gotten our way. Either way, time was never your friend, and never will be. The clock ticks, and yet I continue to smile. The second turns, and yet I smirk in your face. The look of concern in your eyes, all the more I’ll laugh and spit in your eyes. Gleaming bright the naked eye sees, yet full of pain down on their knees… Lights Out Wrestling. A new home for me, maybe a new burial ground for men who had prided themselves with mixing with the best. How I snicker at you guys, thinking that I’m just another statistic at an already crowded place. Think yourself right, all you want. You can never be more wrong. The fear in your aura I can already feel, practically seeping through your pale, dry skin. Yet your arrogance defies your heart, making you lie to your already weakened state of mind. You think you can stand up and be counted, I doubt that’ll ever happen. Life is but a game, a game that you can’t win. Bad have my dreams been of late, shadows and gloom awaiting their fate… Anarchist, a guy who thinks he owns the world and that people should just fear him just because of his name, almost certainly borrowed from his high school dictionary. Here’s a challenge my friend, come close to even beating me, and I will congratulate you in person. I’ll shake your hand even, and buy you two presents instead of one for Christmas. I bet you’ll like that, won’t you? I can almost see you grinning from ear to ear, waiting for Santa Claus to come down the old dusty chimney. You know what, just the very thought of you makes me smile. That’s good, just in case you don’t know. It’s been awhile since I last meet a guy who thinks he’ll have a chance beating me, and I doubt you’ll end up better than the last one. But never mind, at least you can take comfort that you won’t be the first, and certainly not the last. With time maybe I’ll wither and die, yet all I’ve learnt are sadness and cries… Maybe right now, confusion is setting deep in your hearts. Maybe you don’t even know what I’m talking about, after all, all of you are just mortals. The very weak in the seemingly endless food chain. Look around, and observe the shadows looking down on you. Notice the darkness of the sky in the eternal nights, and you will know what I mean. If you don’t, then I guess I overestimated your mind. But one thing I’m sure of. That today, you are a target. Tomorrow, you will be a statistic. In the future, you will be a casualty. Gone were the days where nothing goes wrong, maybe everything good in this life is gone… Signed with heartfelt affection, Kasper Sky.
