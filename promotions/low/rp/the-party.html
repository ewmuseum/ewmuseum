The Party

13 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Pathetic as the upper class is. I still would love to dine with them' The party itself had been great. The meal was first class and the wine was second to none, yet there was something missing. May be it had been because he had been missing from the party that had all but ruined my night yet I still had anything but a real reason as to why he hadn't been there. We have been together on and off now for six weeks, the sex is pretty good and he knows exactly how to handle himself when around others. I'm not upset that he hasn't contacted me its just that I had expected him to turn up, what with him and me almost going stable now, and he had said that he would be here. Could he just have gone off me? If that is the reason then what is it that brought it on? I will willingly change for him, all he has to do it tell me. I walk to his car; the large blue mustang is still empty. He had left it here yesterday night and it hasn't moved since my senses tell me I should be more concerned with what may have happened to him rather than the fact that he has stood me up but I can't seem to understand that he is in trouble... why should I understand anyway! I look around the car park checking that the other guests have gone, covering my elbow and adding a few extra layers of protection I attempt three or four times to shatter the glass but proceed only to hurt myself. I take out my phone from my small white bag and attempt to call him on the cell and realize that his own is still locked in the mustang when I begin to hear it playing from outside. I turn and allow my blonde hair to brush against the car as I stubbornly walk away, if he didn't wish to talk to me most men would have just told me that I was a fuck and leave but he had insisted that I was more, well more fool me. I walk out of the car park and straight onto the main road, my long leather jacket is beginning to stick to my wet legs and soon three drivers have offered me a lift to where ever I wish to go, so long as I let them take me to their 'place' first. The first two drivers I don't even bother looking at, I walk away before they finish their sentences and laugh to myself as the pull away annoyed. The third and final man I smile at and listen closely as he uses his romantic voice to lure me into his car. I shiver with delight as he strokes my face and for the second time ever I make the first move, heading to kiss him. Strangely he brushes me away saying something about never taking another mans woman and continues to drive onwards to his 'special' place before heading to drop me home. We take a dead left at some trees then a right at the signpost for the old train station. I close my eyes for a moment and remember the good times that I had there, sharing my first passionate moments with another of the same sex here in the toilets and loosing my virginity the second way only two cubicles down from the mirror. Suddenly he is ruff though, he grabs me by my hair and covers my eyes with a large piece of transparent material. I don't bother screaming as this for once is a game I enjoy playing, my blood races to my head and I feel light headed as we move out from the car and I am thrown back first into a wall. I shudder and laugh and lift my hands to be tied, for a while the man plays with me toying with how I am to be tied and finally throwing me on top of another body. I try to make some kind of contact with them but there is only silence. I close my eyes and wish of the warmth making the place around me warm with my panic. I close my eyes and because tired, its over. I rest.
