Goals of Sucess

captured 6 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The roleplay enters with clips of the 'disappearance' of enigma rolling on the screen. Intently Enigma watches the tape, no thoughts running through his head.The truth is the nightmares of Jenna are still there, the discontent of his emotions are there..and quite honestly his fear of losing his wrestling career is amongst the true list of his worries. Death sometimes seems like the only option, the option that will make closure in this world, the option that will result in him seeing Jenna again, a chance for him to apologize, a chance for him to express his true emotions because he finally knows who he is and what he needs. The television screen goes to a blank screen.] 'You reap what you sow..' Enigma said quietly as he sat in the dimly lit room. '..You see..Mister Cobi 'The Idol' Deekins what you do not understand is that my worry for the other men was because you could not prove to me that you were worth beating, 2 months ago I was at the top of my career, Creme De Le Creme, so to speak, but now I have fallen to the bottom of the ladder. I was in contention for the Realm title and later would move to bigger and better things.' [Enigma picks up another tape and slides it into the VCR, footage of his loss the past week on Realm 67.] 'Deekins, maybe I should of prepared myself better yet maybe I should have came to fight you. Life is a series of regrettable moments, moments that will never leave your mind. Cobi, the moment that the refs hand went down for the 3 count. Time Stopped, Life mattered nothing anymore, what happened was a fluke..you beat me..I was prepared in no way for a match, mentally and physically. Deekins you see now you've just gone and pissed me off now is the time for this to end, this time Deekins you don't come out of the match. The emotions will run high and your dreams will be Obscured.' [Enigma turns off the TV and walks towards his desk, sitting down he picks up a pencil and begins to write something down on the paper] Dear Enigma, I am sad to inform you that I will not be returning to this apartment. I will be leaving this place as soon as Realm 68 is over. The time has come for me to move on, life shall go on and I will prevail as a sucess. Whether my fate lies in the wrestling world is still undecided. I know that my life will become something more than it is, I feel as though I am a blind man walking. I am filled with anger, sadness, and regret. Life must have some meaning to me, it is not about beating Cobi Deekins in a Dark match to find my way back onto the Television of the LOW wrestling world. My emotions run high as to which I will be wearing my emotions on my sleeve when I walk into Realm 68, Anger will be the main emotion..finding a way to beat the living hell out of Cobi Deekins will be the only goal as I focus on the match. Out of the wrestling ring, I promise to myself that I will become a sucess..I will set goals, I will acheive goals. Life will go on Enigma, Life will go on. Love, Your Friend, Enigma [Enigma stands up in a world of confusion, yet his goals are so clear..Sucess. I have fallen into the vanquishes of Anger and until they are released there is no telling the actions that will take place. To sum it all up, Cobi Deekins has fate knocking on his doors and his fate isn't that of good it is the forces of evil coming to get him] 'I'm climbing the ladder again, and there's no stopping me now'
