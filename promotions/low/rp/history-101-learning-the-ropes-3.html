History 101- Learning the Ropes 3

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. The scene opens to a shot from the past where Eric Russo is lying in a hospital bed. We assume that this is due to the effects of the fire. To the side of his bed, the Snake sits in an cheaply manufactured arm chair watching over his injured pupil. As Russo slowly begins to come to, he begins to question his current location.) Russo: “W . . . wh . . . where are we?” (The Snake perks up at the sign of conscience. He quietly answers the question in a calm almost soothing tone.) Snake: “We’re in the hospital Eric. We had quite a little predicament back there. I guess you were right about it being dangerous for you to hang around in this territory.” (Russo slightly chuckles as he thinks back to the previous events. He quietly begins to speak.) Russo: “They almost got me huh?” Snake: “They probably did get you Eric, but no one took you cause heaven didn’t want you and hell was afraid that you would take over.” (The two men share another quiet laugh as the scene fades out. We are soon greeted by the now familiar voice of Russo as he continues his tale.) Russo (VO): “Hell did want me though as I could feel it clawing at my soul. Through my first lesson, the Snake implanted a seed of evil inside of me. Under his guidance that seed blossomed and consumed all traces of morals that I once had. For the first time, I truly understood what the Snake was teaching me. I no longer had any fear of consequences because I finally learned that in my case the rules did not apply. After I was released from the hospital, the Snake and I decided to hang around it the territory for a few more weeks. The reason for this was just to show all the doubters that there was nothing that could stop the one man onslaught that I had become. Once I had become fully accustomed to my new found outlook on life, I decided to let the Snake in on my revelation.” (The scene re-opens to a scene a few weeks down the road from the last. We are located inside of what appears to be a private dressing room. In the room, Russo sits lounging on a large leather couch positioned against the wall. As he does this he begins plucking grapes from a plate on the table and nonchalant popping them in his mouth. The Snake paces back and forth in front of him as he goes over the night’s game plan.) Snake: “Ok Eric, tonight I want you to hurt this guy and I want you to hurt him bad. I want you to make them remember your name when all of this is said and done.” (Russo pauses in his grape eating activity long enough to look up to the snake, with a smile on his face, and respond.) Russo: “No problem.” Snake: “No, I don’t think you’re understanding me. I want you to make sure he doesn’t get back up . . . ever.” (Russo looks up at the Snake once again with a smile as he responds.) Russo: “Oh, you want paralysis. Ok, you got it.” (The Snake looks at Russo with a confused look on his face.) Snake: “And you don’t have any problems with this?” (Seemingly carefree, Russo answers.) Russo: “No, not really.” Snake: “Could it be that you’ve finally gotten it?” (The Snake looks at Russo examining his laid back demeanor. As he does so, a smirk slowly creeps across his face. He continues.) Snake: “You did . . . after all this time, you’ve finally gotten it. It’s time for you to move on to the last phase. After your match tonight, I’ll tell you what I have planned for you. I’m gonna make sure that you’re for real though.” (The scene begins to fade out as Russo voice begins again.) Russo (VO): “That night I wrestled with more intensity then I ever imagined possible. I held nothing back on my opponent. Every time I saw an opportunity to inflict more pain, I seized it with a grin on my face. The torment on my opponent finally ended when I made sure that he would never be able to step inside of a wrestling ring again. Hell if the people wanted to hate me, I was going to give them a damn good reason. That night, I found that I truly did enjoy the misery of others . . . especially when it was brought about by me. Sure it is an acquired trait but once you get a taste for it, it can be as satisfying as any drug on the market. Just like a drug, I developed an addiction to pushing my opponents to their limits and making them scream for mercy. Those that could manage to physically, we soon to find out that I could wage a mental just as strong. With the Snake’s lessons guiding me, I became unstoppable. Little did I know that the Snake was about to through me a major curve ball that I definitely would have never seen coming.” (The scene re-opens as Russo returns to the backstage area after his match. The arena almost vibrates from the boos coming from the crowd as the medics brush by Russo and head out to the ring to attend to the superstar’s latest victim. The Snake follows closely behind Russo, he breaks into a slow paced jog to catch up to his pupil. As he comes up beside Russo, the Snake slows back down to a normal pace as he begins to excitedly speak.) Snake: “That was great Eric! I knew you had that mean streak somewhere in there, it just needed a little coaxing to show itself.” (Russo turns to look at the Snake with a sinister smile as the duo continues towards Russo’s dressing room. He responds.) Russo: “You know what, I think I can get used to this, I was born for it. I was chosen as a shepard of man, and as that a shepard it is my duty to thin the herd.” Snake: “That’s good to hear, but about what I was saying earlier . . .” (Russo quickly interrupts.) Russo: “Yeah, I can’t wait to hear what you have in mind for me next.” Snake: “Well what I was thinking is that with how great you are on your own, you could be that much better if you were to learn how to function as a unit as well. You see, you have already managed to master your singles style. If you can manage to master a tag one as well, you will truly be a total package.” Russo: “That’s true, but I mean who am I gonna team up with. I don’t know anyone who will be able to work alongside my style. It’s one of a kind.” (Wryly the Snake smiles as he responds.) Snake: “Actually I already have someone in mind who I think you’ll fit perfectly with. He’s another one of my students and it’s about time that you two meet each other.” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
