Match to become Realm contender Part 1: The beginning of a superstar

captured 4 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RECORD 0-0-0 ROLEPLAY NUMBER -1- SHOW Realm People Mentioned All of LOW OPPONENT(S) Aaron Rain, The Demond, Cobi Deekins, Matt Meyhu, Profusion TITLE HISTORY None Match to become Realm contender Part 1: Beginning of a superstar Match to become Realm contender Part 1: Beginning of a superstar ::Sunday, August 1st, 2004 at 5:30 PM in an arena in Pittsburg, Pennsylvania:: *The scene opens up outside the arena where LOW's Realm will take place. The camera zooms out and turns and you see a man outside looking at the arena. He smirks and walks in. He walks straight toward the ring. The camera switches to the one out at the ring. The lights go pitch black, a very small red light dims into the arena. '40 Oz' by D-12 hits the P.A. system and the lights turn on with colorful lights after an explosion erupts. Swift Payne walks down the ramp looking angry. He enters the ring and goes on all the turnbuckles raising his arms Austin style. After he is finished, he grabs a mic and looks into a dead silent crowd and begins to speak as the lights come on and the music fades* Man: Let me begin by stating that I am Swift Payne. *The crowd boos and cheers to the name remembering the same man in other federations* Swift Payne: Since when does a federation announce cards on t.v.? Well it's actually a good idea. Better than just driving down to the arena just to see a damn card. It's actually kinda sad. Well I noticed that I take on 5 other people and out of the 5, I only know one person, Matt Meyhu. Let me just start off with Aaron Rain. Swift Payne: Aaron I don't have a single clue who you are. All I know is your a nobody. I would know who you were if you weren't such a loser....Oh wait, you just joined. You a newby? I mean yeah I am new to LOW but I am not new to wrestling. Are you? Well if you were hopin this was going to be rastling, then you came to the wrong place. Your listenin to a man you should not mess with. Yes I know you will sit here makin fun of my name but guess what? When all is said and done, your ass will be made fun of cause the truth is, pain will be given and it will be given by me. Swift Payne: Ok next we got The Demond. Just to make sure, is it Demond or Demon? Just wondering I mean you know we mustn't fuck with a Demon cause they are so scary you know. Enough of that. Show me what you got. As I see it, you are also a newby. I haven't seen your name anywhere so I am guessin you are new to wrestlin too right? If not, you better not wrestle like you are. I need some good competition. I want someone to make me run for my money. Show me what you have got to offer. Do NOT disappoint me or else you will go for the ride of....Wait either way, you WILL go for the ride of your life! *The crowd goes a little nuts over the quote that hasn't been heard for years. Swift Payne walks over to a turnbuckle and rests his arm on it* Swift Payne: Anyway now we are onto Cobi Deekins. Another newby. Wait why am I in a newby match? Yeah I am new now in LOW but I have been in the older LOW. This is weak. It's a waste of my time. Oh well who knows. Maybe these guys are tougher than I will expect so I need to shut my mouth. Cobi my man how's it goin? You sound like you should go to football. Deekins? Weird man. Anyway onto the point here. Can you whoop my ass? Of course not. Can you TRY to whoop my ass? Of course not. Can you GET your ass kicked? Of course you can. The point is Cobi, your ass will get kicked one way or another. This match is going to get rough and when it does, blood flies and I hope you arn't like those bitches who get scared of blood. I mean we don't need to have you runnin around the Hexagon ring sayin 'OH MY FUCKING GOD IT'S BLOOD!!!'. I mean if you will, go home while you still can. I will make you bleed until you won't bleed anymore. Swift Payne: Profusion has been here before. Show me what ya got. Show me if you can beat the shit outta a newby like me. If not, then beg me to NOT put you in the hospital. I need to face a tough guy like you. I am a powerful man. Just ask me if I am and I will show ya. I will show ya the true power of a real superstar. I mean do you really think you or anyone scares me? I hope not. Thinkin that would cost you big time. Anyway I got nothin more to say to you. Swift Payne: Oh man. My next guy I have known since the start of my career. This man was Commish of Fantasy Wrestling Federation and I don't think he liked me much until the end of FWF Matt Meyhu. Now I know for a damn fact this motherfucker is going to be tough. But then again, he lost to a damn girl....Wait wait nevermind those damn Shadow Riders came to save the whore so Matt was fucked. They do it to Matt this time, I will personally whoop their asses. Matt, I want a good match. Me and you and the end Matt. Show me if you still got it in ya or if you should just retire. I expect to be bleeding by the end of the night. So show me what you got and still have. Lets make this the match to remember and the match of the night. Fuck the main event, this SHOULD be the main event. Lets prove it. *The crowd goes nuts as Swift goes back to the center of the ring* Swift Payne: Ok so I may have offended many and not got a damn friend but thats ok. I am not afraid of any damn LOW superstar in this federation. Let me say somethin to the Shadow Riders. You step one foot near the ring, I will beat your punk asses after I gain #1 contendership for that Realm title. You may be pissed at me now and thats fine. I will let you know when I care. I will make an impact in the LOW, one that will shock everyone when I rip that title away from the bitch. Yeah thats right Brody Dahmer, your a whore who does a little somethin somethin to get those Shadow Riders to attack people. Bring 'em. I will scare their asses. I will make sure they don't fuck with anyone ever again. Enough of those losers. All you LOW wrestlers watch out, there will be a new leader in this federation and I am that man. You want to try me, then bring it. I will show you exactly what I am all about. On a final note, anyone who wants a piece of me will end up going for the ride of their life! *The lights go out as '40 Oz' by D-12 hits the P.A. system. A little bit of a dim red light is shown in the arena as Swift Payne leaves. The fans boo and cheer as the camera fades with a black background and the letters SP appearing and then fading* $Next RP Title: Match to become Realm contender Part 2: The rise to stardom is almost present$ Yo Swift Payne here, IM me at MNEminemM or e-mail or MSN messenge me at [detail withheld] to talk, give me feed back or to talk about my character and maybe give me ideas how to change him a bit.
