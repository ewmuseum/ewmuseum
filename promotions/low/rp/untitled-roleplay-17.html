Untitled roleplay

captured 22 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dawn of Destruction :: As Jesse Jamester lays motionless in the ring, the announcers are left in awe of what they’ve witnessed. :: What a match that was, Casey, I don’t believe my own eyes! And just think, Jerry, this night isn’t even over, who knows what could happen next?! :: Jamester remains unconscious in the ring as the camera pans around the audience. The Bloodhound Gang’s 'Boom' blares throughout the arena now and the crowd gets to their feet, not knowing what is going on. After a moment, 'The Loose Cannon' Jason Kage appears from behind the curtains, still in his wrestling attire. Behind him is Big Daveylicious, who also remains in his ring gear. The two Shadow Riders quickly make their way down to the ring and step through the ropes. Davey plays to the fans a little bit while Kage calls for a microphone. As he waits to be handed one, he looks at the downed Jamester and shakes his head. He is handed a microphone and begins yelling. :: Kage: Russo! Russo! Get your ass out here, Russo! We’re not finished yet! :: Kage pauses and waits for Russo, but has no luck, as there is no sign of the Superstar. :: Kage: Russo! Russo! I’m not moving until you get your egotistical ass out here! I’m not done with you! What the hell is going on! Kage obviously isn’t happy with the outcome of his match! Well I’m not happy with it either! Russo better get out here fast! :: The jumbo screen flickers on and Eric Russo is seen sitting backstage in his wrestling tights in a chair in his locker room. He smirks as he begins speaking. :: Russo: You really expect me to come down to that ring? Look, you got a goat humping Canadian in there with you. I mean what’s that all aboot, eh? I’m not coming down there while he’s still in the ring, get him out and then we’ll talk a little more. :: Kage begins stomping around in the ring angrily. He frustratedly turns to Jamester, who is now stirring, and walks over to him. Kage lifts his boot and begins kicking the Canadian Cowboy. He pushes him out of the ring with his feet, and turns back to the monitor. He waves his hands for Russo to come down to the ring, while Davey also prepares for a fight. :: Russo: Yeah, you still got that ass jockey Davey in there with you. What, are you afraid to go one on one with me. Get him out of the ring and then maybe I’ll come out. :: Russo hold up the NRW World Heavyweight title and waves it around. :: Russo: Maybe I’ll even bring this down with me. :: Big D begins flipping out in the ring, wanting to go back to the locker room to fight. Kage grabs him and tries to talk him down. After a brief meeting between the two, Kage convinces Daveylicious to step out of the ring. Davey climbs to the apron and reluctantly drops down to the floor. The screen now shows Russo push himself out of his chair and begin walking. A moment later, the arena fills with the sounds of 'King of My World' by Saliva. Eric Russo appears from behind the curtains with the NRW World Title hanging over his shoulder and begins walking to the ring. He takes his time getting there, as he taunts the fans on his way. Once he finally makes it to the ring, he stops on the ring steps and gives a quick smile in the direction of Big D. Davey cringes, but knows that he has to remain on the outside. Russo steps through the ropes and slowly walks over to Kage. They go nose to nose in the middle of the ring. :: This match could get resumed right here and now, Casey! We may get a winner after all! I don’t believe my eyes, Jerry! This is amazing! Russo may still get what’s coming to him! :: As the two original Shadow Riders face off in the ring, the roar of the crowd becomes deafening. After a moment of extreme tension, the stale mate is broken. Russo shoves Kage lightly, causing him to lose his footing for a quick moment. In response, Kage fires back with a heavier shove, staggering Russo. Russo takes a moment to chuckle at the situation, then shoves Kage hard enough to take him off his feet. Kage tumbles backwards towards the ropes, landing on his back. :: It’s on, Casey! Russo and Kage are going to finish their epic encounter right here and now! I can’t wait, Jerry! Kage is going to drive the nail into Russo’s coffin! :: This shove prompts Big D to hit the ring. He slides under the bottom rope and charges at Russo, but Russo dives into him with a double leg take down. Russo begins landing punch after punch on Big D, but is soon stopped when Kage gets back to his feet and yanks Russo off of him. He shoves Russo back into the ropes, allowing Daveylicious to escape the beating. Kage pulls a microphone to his mouth and shouts at Russo. :: Kage: You don’t attack my partner like that! :: By now, Big Daveylicious is back up to his feet. He takes a stand behind Kage, and the two Shadow Riders stare down Russo. Russo begins timidly backing away, as he realizes there is no where to run. Davey and Kage begin stalking their prey as Kage finishes his dialogue. :: Kage: Nobody attacks my partner like that! You attack him like this!! :: With a quick flowing motion, Kage spins around and connects with a stiff kick to the gut of Big D! Daveylicious doubles over and Kage wastes no time in hooking his head under his arm and dropping him with the Timebomb DDT! :: What’s going on?! I don’t understand! WHAT THE HELL?!?!? :: Kage springs up to his feet now and looks down at what he’s done. He smirks at Davey’s fallen body and turns again to face Russo. He begins walking towards Russo again now, as Russo just stares at the carnage that just took place. :: My God, if Kage did that to his own partner, just think what he has in store for Eric Russo! This is about to get ugly! :: Kage, who is grinning a sadistic smile, creeps his way over to Russo, who is now backed against the ropes. Kage walks right into him, so that they are chest to chest. The two men begin yelling obscenities at each other, tearing apart the others’ character and ability. As they do, the crowd watches in awe. :: This is it, Casey, the moment of truth! Shhh...I want to savor this moment forever. :: After a little bit more of a yelling contest, Russo busts out in a wild fit of laughter. His voice becomes audible as he bursts out to Kage. :: Russo: I can’t keep this up! :: Russo stumbles forward to Kage the two embrace! Russo and Kage laugh their way back to the middle of the ring, where they pat each other on the back and raise they’re hands in the air for the camera to see. :: WHAT?!? NO!!!! NOT THIS!!! NOT NOW!!!! I’M SICK! THOSE DAMN SHADOW RIDERS! THEY TRICKED US AGAIN!! ROT IN HELL, RIDERS!! ROT IN HELL!! :: Russo and Kage celebrate in the ring with great joy as they soak in the crowd’s boos. The ring starts to fill up with beer cups and empty bottles as the two TRUE Shadow Riders stand triumphantly in the center. :: It can’t be! THIS CAN’T BE! The Shadow Riders CAN’T STILL BE TOGETHER! DAMN IT!! DAMN THEM! DAMN YOU! DAMN LIFE! :: As Russo and Kage celebrate in the ring, Russo begins laughing even harder. He motions to Kage to hold on for a minute then he walks over to the corner of the ring. There, he picks up the NRW World Title that he brought out with him and carries it over to the far side of the ring. Kage joins him as they taunt Jesse Jamester, who remains on the outside of the ring. Russo waves the belt around while yelling. :: Russo: You want this, Jesse? You want NRW? Come and get it! :: With a seemingly miraculous burst of energy, Jamester drags himself to his feet and darts into the ring! He charges at Russo and Kage, flailing his arms like a man possessed! He is heard audibly growling as he charges in! As he does, Russo and Kage both side step and toss the Canadian Cowboy over the top rope on the opposite side of the ring! As he flies over the top, his head and neck get tangled in the top and middle ring ropes, creating a noose like effect. Russo and Kage look at Jamester, then at each other, then back at Jamester again. The two begin smiling and laughing uncontrollably. :: We’ve seen this before, Casey! We’ve seen this exactly one year ago to be exact! I remember! Our first show went off the air with Jamester being hung by these damned Shadow Riders! History is repeating itself, and I don’t like it one bit! :: Russo and Kage walk over to the hanging Jamester! They can’t stop themselves from laughing as they point at him. Jamester hangs there like a fat kid on a pull up bar as his face begins changing colors! Eric Russo climbs onto the ring apron, while holding the NRW title in his hands. He stands next to Jamester and rubs the belts in his face in a cheese grater like fasion, scraping skin off as he does. As this is going on, Big Daveylicious begins to make it to his feet on the other side of the ring! He runs at Kage, who turns around and spots him before it’s too late. Kage ducks down just as Big D makes it over to him, and he hoists Davey up in a flapjack like position! He drops back with it and Russo, on the apron, grabs Davey’s neck and drapes him over the top rope with the South Texas Deathride! South Texas Deathride on Big Daveylicious! :: I had forgotton just how rotton these two can be! I hate those Shadow Riders! I hope they die a slow and painful death! I want to see them burn! Burn, Riders! BURN!! :: Daveylicious’s neck whiplashes as he falls backwards onto the mat. Kage looks down as a smile creeps across his face. Russo, who is on the floor now, walks over to the announcers table. He grabs Jesse Jamester’s cowboy hat from on top of it and places on his head. While at the table, he also grabs something from underneath of it. He tosses the item up to Kage and walks back over to Jamester, who is still hanging. Russo steps back up onto the apron and, while wearing Jamester’s own cowboy hat, begins taunting the Dark Warrior. He starts swinging the NRW title around over his head like a lasso as he exclaims. :: Russo: Yippie-kia-oh-kia-ay, Jackass! I’m a Canadian Cowboy! Yey-haw, eh? :: Russo smiles as he continues taunting Jamester. Back inside the ring, Kage holds up the object that was handed to him by Russo. As he shakes it around, it becomes evident that it is none other than a can of black spray paint! He grins ear to ear as he kicks Davey over onto his stomach with his foot. He opens the can and leans over Big D! After a moment of the spray can hissing, Kage stands back up to reveal five letters scrolled on the back of Big Daveylicious. OWNED. :: NO! NOT AGAIN! IT’S BAD ENOUGH SEEING THIS HAPPEN TO JAMESTER ALL OVER AGAIN, BUT NOT DAVEY TOO! These Shadow Riders know how to rub people the wrong way. There’s a strategy in what they’re doing, but that doesn’t change the fact that they’re sadistic sons of bitches! :: Kage laughs at what he’s done and turns around to face Russo. What he finds when he turns around is Russo on the floor jumping up and down on top of Jamester’s cowboy hat. Seeing this only serves to make Kage laugh even harder as he calls Russo into the ring. Russo steps up onto the apron and places the squashed hat back on top of Jamester’s now blue face. He steps through the ropes and sees Kage’s artwork, causing him to laugh some more. Kage tells him to put the finishing touches on it and hands Russo the spray can. Russo shakes the can up and prepares to spray Davey again, but thinks better of it. He shakes his head with a 'nah' and clears his throat. Without hesitation, Russo spits a wad of phlegm onto the OWNED written on Davey’s back! Russo and Kage embrace again and Russo gets a microphone. :: Russo: See Davey, you’re not a Shadow Rider, you never were. For that matter you were never even truly a member of LOW’s roster. Hell, nobody even likes your sorry ass around here! Go back to where you came from, jackass! We don’t take to kindly to jobbers in these parts. So why don’t you just make like Belmont’s dad and beat it. :: Russo throws the microphone down to the mat and the Shadow Riders raise each other’s hand in victory. They wait for their music to que, but to their dismay it doesn’t. Music does play however, only this time it’s Evanescence’s 'Wake Me Up'. Russo and Kage exchange panicked glances as the crowd gets to their feet in anticipation! Tristan Belmont comes from behind the curtains now and runs down to the ring! :: Belmont is on fire! He must have taken exception to those derrogatory suggestions from Eric Russo! Belmont’s my new favorite wrestler, Jerry! Kick their asses, Tristan, kick their asses! :: Belmont slides into the ring and charges at the Shadow Riders, but suffers the same fate as Jamester and Big D as he is caught with a double superkick from the most dominant team in LOW history! Belmont slams down onto the mat with a thud. By this point, EMTs are rushing to the ring to check on Jamester’s condition! Russo spots them and heads over, grabbing the one EMT and tossing him into the ring over the top rope. The EMT’s head smacks the canvas as Russo walks over to him and starts undoing his belt. He rips the belt off of the medical worker before kicking him back out of the ring. Russo now points at another EMT and yells to him. :: Russo: Make this easy, give me your belt! :: The EMT hands Russo his belt, and Russo walks back over to the middle of the ring. He takes one belt and hands it to Kage, who smiles another evil grin and walks over to Tristan Belmont. Kage stands behind Belmont, who is laying face down, and begins getting the belt ready. He sticks the buckle of the belt right in Belmont’s mouth and ties the rest of the belt behind his head! He starts pulling back, choking Belmont! :: He’s choking the life out of Tristan Belmont! We heard Belmont’s candid confessions about his childhood, there’s no reason for Kage to exploit them! Kage is a sick bastard! He’s a twisted soul who deserves to die! :: As Kage chokes Belmont, Russo begins yelling. :: Russo: What’s the matter, Tristy, not the man you’re father was? Who’s your daddy now? Huh, who’s your daddy now?! THIS IS SICK!!! :: Belmont begins whimpering now and flailing his arms about trying to reach Russo. In response, Russo whips Belmont with the second belt across the back of his neck. After another moment, Kage releases the choke and lets Belmont drop. :: There’s no call fo this! This is revolting! These Riders are going to burn in hell! :: Kage walks away from Belmont now and slaps hands with Russo. Kage walks over to the corner and grabs a microphone. He begins pacing around the ring, finally stopping to speak. :: Kage: What a difference a day makes! Yesterday, everybody in LOW was looking to me to drive the big bad Superstar out of the company. Today, look at you, you hate me. Why? I didn’t change anything. I’ve always been this Kage. How stupid are you people? After seven years together, you think that the Shadow Riders are about to split just because some bleached blonde bitch wants to try and break us up? That jobber isn’t even worthy of cleaning my Jamester shaped toilet. Jamester, Davey, Ortiz, Annihilation, Judgement, Matt Robinson, all of you, all of you fools. You all bought into this. The sweetest one of all though, has to be . . . you . . . :: Kage walks over to the fallen Belmont, who has now rolled onto his back. Kage stands over Belmont and begins pointing in his face. :: Kage: Tristan Belmont! Of everyone that has made asses of themselves by listening to us, you have to be the worst one! You think you would know never to trust us, Belmont! And now, you stupid, Canadian, ape-looking, hairy ass, vampiric, French jigglefuck, you’ve proven to everyone that you’re nothing but a fool! You proved to everyone . . . :: Kage is cut off when Tristan Belmont sits straight up, putting him eye to eye with 'The Loose Cannon.' Tristan Belmont reaches forward and grabs Kage’s throat! He pulls himself to his feet and brings Kage to a standing position! He preps him for a chokeslam but Russo runs towards him now! Belmont catches Russo as well now and holds each of them by their throat! He readies himself to pick the two Riders up for a double chokeslam, but his plans are foiled when Russo and Kage simultaneously lift their boots and kick Tristan Belmont square in the groin! :: DAMN IT! SO CLOSE! Don’t worry, Jerry, these Riders will get what’s coming to them, it’s just a matter of time! :: Belmont releases the Riders and drops to his knees, then falls forward to the mat holding himself. The Shadow Riders duck out of the ring as quickly as they can and begin backing up the entrance ramp. As Belmont crawls to the ropes and Davey begins to stir, Jamester is now sitting on the apron with the aide of EMTs. Russo and Kage continue backing up the ramp as they observe the havoc they left in the ring. As the two REAL Shadow Riders grin at their trickery, Russo pulls a mic from the side of his tights and the duo stop at the stage. :: Russo: I’m sure all of you would like to get your hands on us right now. But that chokeslam Belmont just attempted is the closest any of you will ever get to retribution on us. And that’s not ego talking . . . :: Kage leans over into the microphone. :: Kage: That’s reality. Oh, and by the way, one more thing . . . :: Russo and Kage speak together into the microphone now. :: The Shadow Riders: WE’RE RETIRED! :: The Shadow Riders raise their arms in the air at the top of the ramp as 'Neckbone' by Powerman 5000 blasts over the speakers! The Riders bow to the chorus of boos they receive, soaking in the hatred of the fans. As they celebrate at the top of the ramp, the scene cuts back to the broadcasting table. :: This is a dark day in LOW’s history. Dark day? The Shadow Riders are gone! But look what they left behind them! And there’s no way anybody’s going to be able to seek vengeance on them! I thought we saw the Riders rear their ugly heads for the last time, but I was wrong! These damn Shadow Riders just turned LOW on it’s head! :: The scene fades with workers desperately trying to clean up the scene for the main event. Indeed, it is upon us. :: Show URL: http://
