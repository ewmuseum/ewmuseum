Metallo Madness pt.2 The Metallos sit in their locker room unwinding from their match. As they do so, the two intergalactic superheros strike up a conversation. Silver Ranger: Wow, that match was a lot more challenging then I expected. Gold Ranger: I would have to agree. We must still be feeling some repercussions of the number that mysterious evil Ranger did to our power supply. The Silver Ranger stands up and heads towards a set of lockers where he opens one and begins loading his gear into a duffel bag. Silver Ranger: I never knew being an intergalactic superhero had so much involved in it. If only there was a way to go back to the way it was before. Things were so much simpler then. Gold Range: Ah the good old days. I miss our acrobatic displays...the roar of the crowd when we made those death defying flips in the air. It is no use thinking about that now though. Our life as the Amazing Flutter Brothers has come to an end. The Gold Ranger now stands up and walks over to where the Silver Ranger continues to load stuff into the Metallos’ duffel bag. He begins to help the Silver Ranger out. Silver Ranger: We can not argue though, we chose this life and there is no going back now. It is our duty to save this world and we will not falter. Thinking about the past will only hinder us in our completion of the task at hand. That is something we can not afford with this new evil metallic menace running around. Gold Ranger: Indeed, we must keep our focus on the problem at hand. Let us be off so that we may continue our quest for truth, justice, and interplanetary safety. With that the two Metallos hoist up the duffel bag, each grabbing hold of one of the handles. They then turn and walk to the locker room door where they exit the room. The door slowly creaks shut behind them. As it does however, the camera spins just in time to catch a view of the mysterious evil Ranger that has been plaguing the Metallos as he steps out of the shadows. The light in the room glares off the black visor in his helmet. In a deep robotic voice, strongly reminiscent of the Metallos, he speaks. Mysterious Evil Ranger: The Amazing Flutter Brothers. Now I know your secret and the games shall begin. With that, the mysterious evil Ranger grabs a firm hold of his belt buckle. In an instant, he is engulfed in a bright beam of light and smoke as he transports out of the room with a sinister evil robotic laugh leaving the locker room empty. The picture fades out.
