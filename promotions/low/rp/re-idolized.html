Re-Idolized!

captured 28 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
- I Walk A lonely road - - The only one that I have ever known - - Don't know where it goes, but it's only me and I walk Alone! - 'Taking that beating a couple of weeks ago brought me back down to earth you know doc!... I mean I held the Realm Championship for two weeks but in those two weeks I thought I was invincible... then The Demond and his friend beat some sense in to me... it's all coming clear to me now. I joined the L.O.W still thinking I was number 1! And when I won the title on my first match... I completley lost control of my ego.' As the scene slowly comes clear we can see The Idol Cobi Dee laying down on a weird shaped chair, with his head resting in his arms. He's looking up at the ceiling with his back facing his psychiatrist. 'It's funny when you think about it... I got my ass kicked by some giant stringbean... he's over 7ft00 tall and yet he only weighs just under 300lbs... can you believe that? When you're that tall you'd expect them to be like... well over 400lbs right? Ahh what does it matter... what happened... happened... I was screwed, humiliated. It's happened before... I just gotta let this one go past now right?' Cobi pauses for a split second. In that split second he was expecting the his doctor to get a word in. But Cobi is quick to continue... 'Anyway... I didn't have a match last week. I was given time off to recover. And boy did I need it. You see... I'm in the opening match this monday night. 6 man tag match... against two guys and a chick. I don't really wanna face this chick because she's quite hot... I think I might have a thing for her you know... how can you hit someone you like man tell me?' Finally Cobi pauses and allows his doc to answer his questions... 'So you're worried about this match and what could possibly happen to this young lady you like? Is this a different lady from the other 4 you have spoke about? Or not?' 'No it's a different one... Tessa Windsor... I've never spoke to her... just see her every now and again. She smells so beautiful... her perfume just makes me float in the air... You don't think she's putting some sort of hypnosis shit in there do you?... I wrote a song about her you know, you wanna hear it?' Cobi turns his head slightly toward his doc and waits for an answer. You can tell by his face that he doesn't want to listen to Cobi singing... 'Ermmm... no, maybe you should just keep speaking to me... tell me Cobi. Do you have any problems with anyone in L.O.W? Anyone there that's making your life in the company a little difficult?' 'Only that little bastard that the Demond calls his friend. He's not even apart of the company and he's getting involved in my business... He'll pay for what he did on Monday if I see him... But other than that... no I don't have any problems... hell nobody even talks to me man. Some Idol huh? I'm like that kid that always sits at the back of the class room on his own. You know... the one you use to see on the play ground kicking that small stone all on his own... Not that I care anyway, I mean I think I'm a cool guy... that's all that matters right? OH... actually I sort of had a little fling with one of the female interviewers... she's the only one that does talk to me... I think she likes me.' The Psychiatrist collects his thoughts together, then begins to write something down on his note pad. Cobi turns around again to try and see what his doc is doing... after a while Cobi turns back around and continues to talk. 'Another one of my opponents has also taken a disliking to me... I don't know if it will develope in to a feud between us... It seems like all my opponents can think about is destroying me... I mean I haven't even said anything yet and they just wanna rip my head off... I keep telling myself to remain cool y'know. Keep it together... I could make fun of them all if I wanted to... but I've grown out of that now. This is serious.. you gotta back your words up 100%... otherwise you'll just look like a fool infront of thousands of fans.' 'Why do you think all of your opponents seem to hate you so much? You just said you don't have any problems with anyone...' Cobi raises his eyebrowe and begins to bite his lower lip, thinking of the perfect answer to his docs questions. 'No I don't have any problems with anyone... I don't know if they have a problem with me or not... they don't talk to me... just about me... doesn't bother me or anything... Maybe it's because they're all jelous of me? I don't know... I'm a handsome man... I attract a lot of beautiful women... And all the guys are ugly and only attract the ugly women. I mean The Demonds fiancee... I'm pretty sure she's green and just covers her face in a shead load of make up... she's got weird ears too... and she's quite fat... I've got a picture if you wanna see her...' Cobi reaches in his pocket and pulls out a folded up picture of Shrek. He hands it to his psychiatrist who seems a little amused. 'But anyway... I haven't even seen this Enigma dude or Arron Rain... So I don't know what's eaten at them two.... Maybe they're jelous of me because I'm cool and they're not? This Enigma dude seems to have a few personal problems which I'm guessing he just wants to take it out on everyone that steps in the ring with him... you know he's going the wrong way about things. Hell maybe he should come here and speak to you... just let it all out you know? I'm feeling pretty good about myself now. Just what I needed... but what am I going to do about Tessa? What if we're both in the ring together? What if she hates me and thinks I'm an idiot? What if she likes me and wants me to fix this match for her? I don't know what to do about that dude.' 'Maybe you should give her a call Cobi... talk to her and see if she does like you... She probably doesn't even know who you are? But I guess she'll find out Monday night huh?' 'Anyway Doc... I gotta run, got a hot date with this chick I met the other day... You know how it is with women these days... going for a meal... I'll think of what I could say to Tessa... you know, practise on this chick.' Cobi pulls himself up off the chair. He winks at his doc who seems a little shocked by Cobi's feelings for all these women... before he dissappears out of the office.
