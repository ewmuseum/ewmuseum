Up Close and Personal with Eric Russo

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. Slowly the logo fades out to black. The scene opens to a shot of Eric Russo sitting backstage in his locker room as he prepares for Realm. Suddenly there is a knock at the door. Russo looks up from lacing his boots and calls out.) Russo: “Yeah . . . come in.” (The door to the locker room opens to reveal LOW reporter, Sarah Camion. Camion closes the door behind her and approaches Russo. As she does so, she begins to speak.) Sarah: “Hey . . . what’s up? Someone over in production said you wanted to see me for something.” (Russo looks up at the curvaceous woman and responds.) Russo: “Have a seat . . . I have a few things I wanna say.” Sarah: “Oh . . . the camera crew should be here in a few minutes. Do you wanna run though the interview once as a sort of practice while we’re waiting?” (As Camion pulls up a chair across from Russo, the wrestler responds.) Russo: “Sure, why not.” (Camion’s face fills with a smile as she launches into her mock interview.) Sarah: “Ok, I’ll open with the normal stuff then I’ll hand it off to you with something like . . . “Welcome Mr. Russo, why don’t you tell us what’s on your mind tonight.”. Does that sound good? What’s your response gonna be?” Russo: “Well I was gonna go with . . . “As is customary the night of my match, I stand here before all of you to talk a little bit about my opponent.”.” (Camion smiles as she responds.) Sarah: “That’s good, now let’s move through the interview real quick. I’ll just play off of what you say. Ok with you?” Russo: “Sure, whatever.” Sarah: “Ok, you start off. Let me know where you want this thing to go. Ready . . . start.” (Russo leans towards Camion as he begins to speak.) Russo: “Now as you may know, tonight I step into the ring with a man that I have chased from place to place for over a year now. Now understand, I do not take this match as lightly as I do some of my others.” (Camion responds.) Sarah: “Well tell me, why is that? Do you . . .” (Russo cuts Camion off before she can finish as he continues.) Russo: “Now before you go jumping to some stupid conclusions as so many of the Low roster likes to do, allow me to elaborate. The reason I am taking this match so seriously is not because I fear what my opponent can do. No, the reason I am taking this match seriously is because this is my chance to succeed where so many others have failed. Tonight I finally get my chance to rid the world of a piece of trash like Jesse Jamester.” Sarah: “But Eric, do you think it’s really fair to lab . . .” (As if he didn’t even here Camion’s comments, Russo continues.) Russo: “After over a year of him running from and dodging me, he has finally been forced into a match against me. Oh and this will not be any ordinary match, this match will take place within the confines of a steel cage.” (Breaking from the interview for a moment, Camion gets sidetracked.) Sarah: “Ooh a steel cage! Are the bars on it straight?” (With a look of slight confusion on his face, Russo answers.) Russo: “Yeah . . . why?” Sarah: “I was just wondering how you were gonna get Jamester anywhere near a straight bar?” (Russo lets a slight smirk cross his face at the comment by Camion, but it is quickly removed as he continues.) Russo: “Even though he will not admit it, Jamester knows his time is now limited. The time for talking is over and now there is nothing but me and him. No Belmont, no Kage, no Shadow Riders, and no Silence. This time around, he comes face to face with the man that has haunted his dreams for so long. The man who shut down his precious SCW. The man that Jesse Jamester knows he can’t beat.” Sarah: “Well Jesse is known for beating other men. Wait . . . that didn’t come out right. Just keep going.” (Russo nods his head and continues.) Russo: “Now knowing Jesse, after our match he’ll make up some kind of excuse after I whip him from pillar to post as to why it was unfair. Deep down inside however he’ll know that he got beat by the better man. After all, that’s what I am.” (Camion finally manages to break in.) Sarah: “Better man!? That’s a pretty big statement there now isn’t it Eric?” (Russo smirks as he responds.) Russo: “What . . . don’t agree? Well think about it this way, what has Jesse Jamester ever done? Has Jesse ever pinned Mike Meyhu? No . . . how about Tristan Belmont? Maybe Cade . . . wait, no he didn’t beat him either. So I ask you, what has Jamester done to get his title shot? I mean I know the LOW wants to promote themselves as a diverse company, but giving an undeserving has-been a shot just because of his sexual preferences. That my friends is just down right wrong.” (With a little laugh, Camion responds breaking the interview.) Sarah: “Jamester’s getting his title shot because he’s gay!?” (Not one to get off track, Russo just continues as planned.) Russo: “No matter though, it’s not like he actually has a chance. You see, Jesse Jamester is nothing but a filler. A filler to hold my spot as the #1 contender to the title as I take care of something a little more important. He’ll realize that fact soon enough though. And when he does, he will realize that he just like the rest is nothing but another falling star.” (Camion begins to close up the interview. She is quickly cut off however by Russo.) Russo: “Oh and Sarah . . . that’s not ego, it’s reality.” (Camion smiles as she begins to speak.) Sarah: “That’s gonna work great. Now all we have to do is perform it again in front of a camera.” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
