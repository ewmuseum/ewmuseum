Nightmares

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders along with a voice over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens up to a shot from inside the bedroom of LOW’s Realm champion, “Superstar” Eric Russo. Russo is lying on the bed at the center of the room fast asleep. From the darkness and stillness of the room, we can assume that it is sometime in the mid-hours of the night. Suddenly a shrill scream pierces through the darkness jolting Russo from his peaceful slumber. In fear of what is happening, Russo springs from his bed and rushes to the door. He reaches out and yanks the door open as he exits the room into the hallway. At this point, another scream cuts through the air. Russo races down the hall as he begins to call out.) Russo: “Jason . . . Jason . . . what’s wrong!?” (Russo reaches the bedroom door of Jason Kage’s room and quickly pulls it open. He then reaches in and flicks the light switch on only to get no response. As Russo’s eyes begin to adjust to the darkness of the room, he spots Jason Kage who is lying stiffly in his bed. Kage’s blanket is bunched up around his neck allowing only a view of his head. A fright-filled look fills Kage’s eyes as he stares out of his bedroom window and into the night. As Russo enters the room, Kage becomes aware of his presence and subsequently relaxes a little. Russo starts towards the bed as he begins to speak.) Russo: “Jason . . . what’s wrong? What happened? Did Jamester try and break into one of the bedroom windows again?” (Kage forces a smile out as Russo pulls up a small wooden chair and takes a seat at Kage’s bedside. Russo continues . . .) Russo: “Seriously, what’s the matter . . . nightmare? Do you want me to tell you a bedtime story so you can maybe fall back asleep?” Kage: “Ok . . .” (Russo rolls his eyes knowing that he now has to make good on his offer to tell Kage a story. He then pauses for a moment as he tries to think something up. Suddenly, his eyes light up as he begins speaking.) Russo: “Once upon a time there was a prince named Big Daveylicious who was trying to make a name for himself by fighting with the king . . . we’ll call him King Eric. Now Prince Davey, well he wanted the precious treasure that King Eric had. Prince Davey tried and tried to get what Eric had, but he just could not seem to get the job done no matter what he did. He would pass the many defeats off on other people and things because Prince Davey was an egotistical son of a bitch. Now Eric . . .” (Russo is cut off by Kage who speaks up from under his fortress of blankets.) Kage: “Eric?” Russo: “Yeah . . . what?” Kage: “Have you ever told a story before?” Russo: “No . . . what the hell do I look like? A freaking babysitter?” Kage: “I’m just saying that you should make the story a little more interesting. Like you could have Prince Davey steal the treasure from the king.” Russo: “Steal something from the king eh? Well I think the story would be more interesting if King Eric kicked your ass for giving his stupid suggestions.” (Russo shakes his head in a disapproving fashion. The two men sit in silence for a few moments until Kage pipes up once again.) Kage: “Look, forget about what I said. Tell me a story about when King Eric and Lord Tristan teamed up to slay the 2 mighty legit dragons.” Russo: “Do you think I wanna tell that story?” (Kage nods his head signaling his response is yes.) Russo: “What makes you think I would wanna tell that story now?” Kage: “I can see it in your eyes.” Russo: “I’m tired, I’ll tell you that story another time.” (Kage smiles as he looks up at Russo and responds with genuine interest.) Kage: “No . . . please tell me now.” (Russo leans back in his chair for a moment as he debates what to do. Finally he gives in to Kage’s request as he leans forward in his chair and begins to speak.) Russo: “Once upon a time, the great and powerful King Eric found himself in a very bad position. Now Eric had to rid his kingdom of two dangerous fire breathing dragons. As great as Eric was though, he knew he could not accomplish the feat on his own. So Eric headed north up towards Canada where there was a fierce band of Vikings.” (Russo pauses and smiles at the way his story is shaping up and then continues.) Russo: “When Eric got to Canada, he went in search of the legendary warrior of all the Vikings . . . Lord Tristan of Belmont. Now when Eric finally came face to face with the man he had searched for, he was surprised to find that the legends did not do the hulking monster justice in way of his description. Lord Tristan was nearly seven feet tall and weighed over three hundred pounds of sheer muscle.” (Russo pauses as he thinks for a moment of how to continue the story. He then resumes his speaking.) Russo: “Now after Lord Tristan heard of the great King’s plight, he readily agreed to join up with him in his battles against the two evil dragons. So with Lord Tristan now at his side, King Eric began his journey back towards his kingdom. Once they arrived at the kingdom, King Eric and Lord Tristan found that the dragons had begun proclaiming themselves as the greatest tandem of hell raisers on the planet. Now this didn’t sit well with king Eric because he knew that it was him and his good friend . . . Sir Jason that were the best tandem to ever step foot on a battlefield. Sir Jason however was busy taking care of other business in the name of the Riders of the round table.” (Russo pauses once again as he looks down at Kage. Kage is looking on captivated by the story that Russo is weaving. Russo smiles at this and continues.) Russo: “Now King Eric and Lord Tristan were not about to stand for this. So, they took up arms and went into battle with the 2 dragons from the mountains of Legit. The battle raged on well into the night as they fought in the valley of Upper Barbey Wire. The four combatants bled profusely due to the wounds they received from the sharp jagged rocks that surrounded them. The battle went on for days and days with either side having only a small advantage at any given time.” (Russo leans in towards Kage as he speaks in a whisper like tone.) Russo: “I’m not sure how this story is gonna end . . . I sure do hope that the team of King Eric and Lord Tristan pull through and slay those evil dragons.” (Kage looks up still enthralled.) Kage: “I hope so too.” (Kage stares up at Russo’s unwavering glare for a couple of minutes until he finally works up enough nerve to speak in a whisper.) Kage: “Eric . . . I wanna tell you why I was screaming earlier.” (Russo leans in closer as he whispers back.) Russo: “Okay Jason . . .” (Tension begins to mount in the room as Kage takes a long pause. Finally he speaks up but still keeping his voice in a whisper.) Kage: “. . . I see wrestlers.” (Russo just stares at Kage with a confused look on his face at this statement. Kage now elaborates.) Kage: “I see WO wrestlers . . . some of them scare me.” Russo: “Do you see them while your sleeping?” (Kage shakes his head signaling no.) Russo: “When you’re awake?” (Kage nods his head yes.) Russo: “WO wrestlers on TV, like in PCW and OSW?” Kage: “No, walking around PTC locker rooms, like regular PTC wrestlers . . . They can’t see each other. Some of them don’t know their WO.” Russo: “They don’t know their still considered WO?” Kage: “No, they think their PTC . . .” (Russo does not move as he tries his best to hide his shock over Kage’s admission. Russo’s eyes lock with Kage’s as the two men stare at each other for what seems like an eternity. Kage now continues.) Kage: “They tell me stories about their past glory days . . . things that have been long forgotten by everyone but them . . . things that nobody really cares about here and now.” (Russo tries to act as though he is unaffected as he continues to probe at Kage’s statements.) Russo: “How often do you see these WO wrestlers?” Kage: “All the time. They’re everywhere.” (Kage pauses for a moment as he stares at Russo expressionless face.) Kage: “You won’t tell anyone will you?” (Russo looks down at Kage sympathetically, his eyes almost showing some sort of relief, as he responds.) Russo: “No, because I see them too . . .” (With that, the picture fades out to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again where we hear a voice-over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The picture then cuts to a black, blank screen.)
