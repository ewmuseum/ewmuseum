Rp 1 for Aftermath Archive - 16.3.2003

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Slowly I place the receiver back upon its base. A smile starts to break out on my face. I try to resist but I can’t help it. It’s started. I can now officially say that I am a member of LOW. My first match being on Aftermath. I relish the thought of being in a federation again. I live to wrestle. Without wrestling I would cease to exist. I walk over to my small, but ample kitchen and open the fridge. I see a carton of milk and some butter. From the smell I can tell the butter needs to be thrown away. I quickly open the bin and throw the butter away. There is a knock at the door. I turn round and walk up to the door and unbolt it. Before me stands a small man, from the formal clothes he is wearing he seems to be an official of some kind. He speaks in a quiet yet confident tone. Man: Excuse me…Mr. Skar I presume? I wonder who this man could be. I spot the tape recorder in has hand then realize why he is here. Cyrus: Yes…and you would be? Man: Clarke Brent. I’m a reporter for a small wrestling magazine, I was wondering if you would do me an interview about your recent move to LOW. I think to myself. Would this benefit me? It would get me more exposure in the press. But do I need anymore? Could this man portray me as something am I not? Am I willing to take that risk? I finally decide that any publicity is good publicity. I move out of the way of the entrance and let him in. I sit down on my brown leather sofa and he sits down in the chair opposite. He starts his tape recorder and pulls out a notepad and pen. Clarke: Ok Cyrus. Let’s get straight into this. Why did you leave NGW? He starts with an easy question. One that needs to be answered and one that for me, is easy to answer. There are no secrets about why I left NGW. I clear my throat then begin to speak. Cyrus: I left NGW for a few reasons. I found the staff were unprofessional and I also heard that NGW would be leaving PTC very soon. PTC is my life. I could not be in a federation that wasn’t in PTC. I also thought it was time for me to join a federation where I belonged. I never really belonged in NGW or FAW. I may sound big headed here, but those federations did not deserve somebody of my abilities. I was underused and underrated. Now that I am in the best federation in LOW, I believe I will get the exposure I deserve. Clarke jots a few notes down on his pad and then carries on speaking. Clarke: So, what made you choose LOW? Cyrus: I had a few offers off various federations, LOW is the best federation in PTC. Everybody knows that. I decided it was time I went to a federation that suited my ability. Rather than being in some small federation nobody cares about like FAW or NGW I decided it was time to go to the next level. LOW was the only federation that could do that for me. Clarke: Ok, so how long do you think you’ll stay here? You weren’t in NGW very long… Cyrus: Due to unforeseen circumstances. NGW was a shambles. I got out as quickly as I could. Clarke: Right, recently you’ve been causing quite a stir with a few other people in PTC. You were one of the founding three of FTS. Now FTS has gotten a lot of exposure off other PTC superstars. Now, most hate FTS, it’s like PTC has been split in two. The people who like FTS or are with FTS and the people who hate you. Why did you decide to start up FTS? Cyrus: It was only going to be an alliance between Fox, Belmont and myself. We never knew it would get the exposure it did. We didn’t know so many people would want to join us. All we knew was that it would make an impact in PTC and it would cause trouble. That was our aim along with being the most dominant force in PTC. I believe we have already achieved our goals. Clarke: So what’s next for you in LOW then? You have a match with… Cyrus: Seth Araya. Clarke: Yes…how do you feel about this match? Cyrus: I feel confident. I feel that starting at the bottom of LOW can only be good for me. As I slowly work my way up the ladder I will earn respect. Being thrown in at the deep end where I belong would be wrong. I may have paid my dues in other companies but I’m here in LOW now. It starts all over. I may be worthy of being in the top half of the LOW roster, but that doesn’t matter until I prove it here. Clarke: Indeed, now then Mr. Skar. LOW hold many, many talented superstars. Do you honestly believe you belong here? Cyrus: What are you trying to say? Are you trying to say I have no talent? No skill? No ability? Clarke: Well, no…but… Cyrus: Shut up. You reporters are all the same. You think you know how the wrestling world works, when really you know jack shit. I get sick and tired of you guys coming here and acting like you know what you’re talking about. You try getting in a ring week after week and putting your body on the line. Then come back to me and interview me. Until then, you have no respect for me and if you have no respect for me I have no respect for you. I get up out my chair. This man has shown his true feelings. I glare at him and slowly feel the saliva welling up inside my mouth. I spit right in his face. He looks shocked but he can do nothing about what I have just done. I help him out of his seat and go and unlock the front door. I push him out and he lands face first in the mud. Clarke: You’ll be hearing from my lawyer! I just laugh at his petty threat as he tries to clear some of the mud from his face. He slowly walks off my drive muttering to himself under his breath. I slam the door shut and sit back down. I pick up a magazine and begin to read.
