“At The Doctor’s”

30 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My macaroni’s sitting in the toilet and I don’t know what to do. I made a bit of cream after I ate my Yankee beans and now I think I have the flu. ::Mail day. Derek goes to his mail box and checks his mail. Bill, bill, bill, Far Below Legal re-subscription, and a notice from Doctor Willipants Nickelbag that Derek is WAY over due for a check up. Derek, fearing for his health, rushes to his cordless phone and dials up the doctor office and schedules an appointment for… dammit, I forget.:: [Two Days Later] ::Derek’s sitting in the waiting room of the doctor’s office. “God I hate the fucking waiting room, “Derek politely thinks to himself. Finally, the doctor calls Derek in.:: [Examination Process: Physical, Mental and Emotional] ::Derek is sitting on one of those tables in a hospital gown waiting for the doctor. He does what every other normal American does waiting for the doctor; he looks around the room and twiddles with his thumbs and fingers. Finally, the doctor comes in reading over some papers.:: Doctor: Mmhhmmm, mmhhmmm. Derek, I have good and bad news. Now, you’re in great shape. You haven’t been on drugs lately and your body is really showing an improvement. You’ve become more intelligent and more resistant mentally to certain things. You’ve seemed to recover well from lots of things… but… Derek: But what? Doctor: There’s no good way of saying this. Derek, you’re literally insane. The part of your mind that controls happiness is corrupt. Your brain, according to these CAT scans in very abnormal. According to these tests that you’ve taken, you have a rage problem also but you could never show it. So, if you don’t seek help for that soon you’re going to snap and kill and awful lot of people. I’m surprised you’re not jumping off the walls as we speak. See, you’re insane but it’s kinda controlled by you. Most people have no control over their insanity but for some reason, as insane as you are, you’re perfectly sane. Hard to describe and talk about. I never really saw this before. I have heard of it plenty of times but it’s never crossed my path. I haven’t been around that long. Derek, you have three choices to do with your condition. One, you can take pills. The pills will only sedate your problems. Thus, you will have your problems for the rest of your life, you’ll just pay for very expensive pills to help you through life. Two, you can go into a mental institution and seek the help you need and you will be guaranteed your sanity once you leave. Three, you can see a psychiatrist. The psychiatrist will be expensive and may recommend you to expensive medication. Don’t get me wrong; with your income you will be able to afford it. Seeing the psychiatrist will allow you to lead a normal, well, almost normal life except once a week or more you will be required to see the psychiatrist. You have one of those three choices now. If you don’t choose I will call up the mental institution and you’ll stay there. Well, what do you say? ::Derek looks around the room a bit. He gazes out the window and sees the demons in the sky. Their eyes are windows to their fiery souls and they bare their charcoal teeth. The sky goes dark green as it rains bloody piss. The demon’s wingspan increases with every drop of piss hits a human soul. Derek can feel the anguish eating his heart. He smiles at the doctor and takes a pen from him to sign a form. The windows begin secreting tar. Only the shadows of the demons can be seen through the windows. Ten stories up. Derek closes his eyes and remembers the black and white shores of Venice Beach. When he opens his eyes again there isn’t any tar on the windows, there’s a thunderstorm outside. The sky is gray and he can hear the rain hit the window. He glances at the doctor then at the paper to sign ‘Derek’. He looks out the window at a dark green sky, gray clouds spitting piss, three demons grinning their charcoal teeth and glaring through the glass. Derek looks back at the smiling doctor and jams the pen right behind the doctor’s chin and throws him through the glass. Derek and the demons watch as the man falls ten stories to his death on top of a moving car. The demons begin beckoning him to come outside. Derek walks out onto the ledge of the building and gazes down. He then looks to the sky for pity and walks off the ledge. Derek hits the ground on his feet. He lands dressed with black pants, black boots, black shirt, chains hanging from the pants and shirt, black trench coat and a pair of sunglasses that only a coke head would wear. He cracks his neck and knuckles then looks around the parking lot. The sky goes from dark green to very clouded and gray. The rain goes from bloody piss to black water. Derek looks up at the gray sky, his wings rip from through his shirt and coat. He stretches them and takes off through the clouds.::
