SS RP 3 Nightmare Part 2 Archive - 15.4.2003

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Voiceover: Ever closer does the Pay Per View draw…bringing the fate of nine men’s careers closer and closer. One of them has the chance to become the Number One contender for the Aftermath Championship. All nine men want that title shot, they need that title shot, the can sense they are close to it, just from the sheer fact that they have been booked in the match. All of them feel that they shall win, eight men will be going home disappointed. Gutted because they let such a golden opportunity pass them by. One man however will be glad. Glad that he took his chance and grabbed it by the throat. This Sunday, it will become evident. The future of LOW will be competing. The man, who wins, will be the best of the future. The best… The taxi driver slammed his foot on the breaks causing the vehicle to stop abruptly, nearly sending Cyrus through the windscreen. Cyrus took of his seat belt and passed the driver a handful of bills. “That should cover it.” Muttered Cyrus as he got out of the taxi. He had other things on his mind now. He was focused on his training, he knew he’d have to train harder than normal so that he’d be ready for his first LOW PPV. Cyrus walked towards the huge complex that was in front of him. Shining steel reflecting the suns rays into his eyes, causing him to squint. He smiled. This was a training facility to be proud of; Cyrus had come to the right place. He cracked his knuckles, he was ready for action. He strolled up towards the automatic doors and stepped into the practically brand new facility. To his left was a reception desk where you could book certain things for set times, straight ahead of him was a corridor, along either side were doors with plaques on. He casually walked down the corridor until he came to a door that read ‘Punch Bags.’ He placed his hand around the doorknob and went in. Before Cyrus was eight punch bags. Cyrus went over to a little table and chair in the corner. He took his long, black leather jacket off and placed on the chair carefully. He then walked over to one of the punching bags and started lightly jabbing it. “Excellent Cyrus.” Came the voice. “I’m trying to train…” Replied Cyrus sounding agitated. “Ok, I’ll come back in a few minutes when you take a rest then, Ciao Cyrus.” With that the voice went back to the depths of Cyrus’s soul. Cyrus then started to hit the punch bag harder, hitting it with combinations of different punches and then throwing in the odd kick. Cyrus was hitting the bag with conviction, causing it to sway violently with every blow landed upon it. A drip of perspiration fell from Cyrus’s forehead to the floor, only now was his work out starting to test him. He continued for a few minutes, left hook, right hook, upper cut, kick! The bag swung violently again after the final blow. Cyrus decided he deserved a break. He went over to his coat and pulled out some loose change. He went over to the vending machine and purchased a bottle of mineral water. He then sat down at the table and started to drink from the bottle. Cyrus stopped drinking for a second. “Mind if I but in?” Said the voice almost laughing as it spoke. “Go for it. I’m only having a break for a couple of minutes though so make it quick.” Cyrus spoke quickly and hurried. He did not want to sit around for too long. “Well I was just going to ask you how your training was going? Seems to be going ok…” Said the voice trying to sound innocent. “You can see how it’s going. It’s going fine. It’d only go wrong if I got injured or something.” Replied Cyrus sounding very annoyed. “If you haven’t got something interesting to say go away. Cyrus then got to his feet, as there was silence. The voice had taken a hint and stopped talking. Cyrus walked back over to the punch bags. He was just about to start his work out again, only this time more intense. But he stopped. He just stood there, still as anything. “I want to be somewhere I belong.” Came the familiar voice of Chester Bennington. “What?” Cyrus sounded perplexed, confused and shocked all in one. “That music again…” Cyrus looked around but could see nowhere, where the music could be coming from. Then he realized it was coming from inside of him again. However, this time it did not stop. It continued on and on mocking Cyrus. Cyrus shut his eyes clutching his head as he fell to the floor. “No!” Screamed Cyrus who was now curled up in agony on the floor. “Why won’t it stop? Please make it stop!” Pleaded Cyrus, but alas nobody was there to help him. He was all-alone. Or was he? He looked up to see all the members of the Gauntlet match stood in a line. Seth Araya, Chris Wrestling, everybody was there. He blinked, the pain had subsided, but the music was still playing. He walked over to Chris Wrestling. Chris did not move a muscle. “Why are you here?” Asked Cyrus, now taking his hands off his head as the pain faded. He got no answer though. Chris just stood there staring a hole through the wall. Cyrus repeated himself, this time with more of a sense of urgency in his voice. “Why are you here?” Still there was no answer. At this point Cyrus lost his cool. He lashed out at Chris Wrestling punching him in the stomach again and again and again. Harder and harder Cyrus punched, but yet Chris did not move a bit. He did not curl over in pain, he just stood there staring. The music stopped. Cyrus fell to the floor feeling week. He shut his eyes again. When he reopened them the eight men had gone, the punch bag in front of him was swaying. He walked over to it and with one final kick knocked it off it’s fixture in the ceiling. “Temper, temper!” Said the voice, mocking Cyrus. “No need to get angry.” “Why?” Asked Cyrus, he wanted to know the answer. The voice knew it. “Because, like I said, the alcohol makes you hallucinate as well as have nightmares. I would have been surprised if you hadn’t hallucinated. It wouldn’t have made sense.” Said the voice with a ‘know it all’ attitude. “So now there’s going to be no more hallucinations? No more nightmares?” Cyrus was sounding quite relieved. “Indeed, unless…” The voice stopped. “Unless what?” Said Cyrus getting impatient. “Unless you drink again…that will trigger it all again.” The voice laughed. “But that won’t happen again will it?” “No it won’t” Cyrus walked over to one of the punch bags that was still hung up and carried on training. The voice chipped in every now and again with encouraging comments. Cyrus finished after a few minutes and sat down with a huge smile on his face. “One more obstacle. I’m halfway over it. My preparation is nearly complete. All I’ve got to do now is win the match.” Cyrus laughed. “I make it sound so easy don’t I?” “It is easy Cyrus, if you do what I say, it’ll be very easy….” The voice and Cyrus both laughed. When they both stopped the voice returned to the depths of Cyrus’s soul.
