captured 21 July 2004

Eric Russo vs. Mike Meyhu Jerry, next up we have one hell of a match planned for all of you fans out there. You will see two fighters battle it out, non-title style. That’s right Casey. Sharp glass is going to be glued to the ropes around the inside of the ring, and there’s also shattered glass all over the outside. It’s a formula for physical scars and a whole lot of crimson. And my two favorite wrestlers are in it. Mike Meyhu and Eric Russo will do battle in this match, and you got to like Meyhu’s chances in this match. Indeed, Meyhu has the World Championship under his name. But Russo is a former Tag Champion with the Shadow Riders and he might just have more tricks up his sleeve. You can’t count anyone out in this matchup, because when everything is on the line you can expect both of these men to try and pull out all the spots to insure their victory. Even if it isn’t a title match, I’m looking for Meyhu to come out strong. It won’t be a sure thing to guess the winner, that’s for sure. So let’s get to the match right about now and figure out who has what it takes to survive a tetanus shot! :: The camera swoops from the commentary area to the ring where the official stands, ready to officiate the match as both men are already in the ring. The crowd is loud and standing on their feet, making a lot of noise before the match can even start. :: Listen to this crowd, the match hasn’t even started yet and they are on their feet here! They can’t wait to see what kind of madness this match may have in store! They also can’t wait to see these two evil men beat the hell out of each other! :: The bell tolls inside of the ring, and both men start to move towards each other. They swing around each other like vultures waiting for their pray, but none of them seems to be giving the okay for their opponent to make the first move. Their defense stances are about the same, their hands are up just awaiting to block a shot. Their feet stay moving as they pace around the ring, the crowd still on their feet. :: The bell has tolled, but neither man has tried to take an advantage yet. Trust me, this match is going to be close. Whoever makes the first impact move is going to take this thing, even though I hate to say those kinds of things sometimes. Yes, but the spaces between the moves could be nothing but anticipation. Their eyes look ready and willing, thirsty for a win. Who wants it more? It’s still up for grabs until somebody makes that first intense move! :: Mike Meyhu and Eric Russo finally lock horns in the middle of the ring, trying to push their way into power. Mike takes Russo’s head, sticking it under his arm and providing a head wrench. Russo throws Meyhu’s body into the ropes, and when Meyhu comes off Russo throws him down with a sharp hip toss. Meyhu’s back has some blood on his back from the glass. :: There’s a hip toss by Russo, and we’re having a very basic start to this match. It’s best to start off basic. You get to try out traditional things, and really try to predict what your opponent’s next move will be. :: Meyhu gets back onto his feet, and the two lock things up again. This time Russo is the one who takes the headlock to Meyhu, and Meyhu shoves Russo into the ropes. Russo hits the ropes hard, grunting because of the glass hitting his back. Meyhu telegraphs a ducking motion and Russo just plainly takes his head in the palm of his hand, pushing Meyhu’s head right into the mat and taunting the crowd. They boo at Russo as he starts to smear his boot into Meyhu’s forehead, then he starts to stomp away at his chest. :: And now the match has officially started, so to speak. Sharp boots are being given to Meyhu, and he seems to be shaking in his boots! He’s not shaking, he’s being booted into the mat! Those big boots are just digging into Meyhu’s body, and he seems to be reeling in the ring! Russo isn’t stopping, he just continues to stomp on Meyhu and ignoring the official. :: Russo finally gets to Meyhu’s head, pulling him up onto his feet but getting a poke in the eyes as a thank you. The official warns Meyhu as he delivers a sharp knee into Russo’s stomach, throwing him into the ropes. Russo grunts with the glass hitting him, and when Russo comes off Meyhu delivers a stiff arm to his throat, knocking him right down to the mat and driving the wind right out of him. Meyhu goes for a quick cover while the referee slides in to count the three. :: one – two – - no! Close, but only a two count as Russo gets a shoulder up! What a stiff arm by Meyhu! Knocked the wind right out of Russo’s sails and he still doesn’t look to have recovered from it! That was just a great shot delivered by Meyhu. He ran Russo over like a freight train! :: Meyhu gets to his feet, pulling Russo up but getting an eye poke right back. The referee warns Russo as he delivers a haymaker punch to Meyhu, sending him closer to the corner. Another clenched fist punch, and Meyhu’s back hits the corner. Russo delivers some sharp kicks to Meyhu’s chest, making his breath run short. He then stops for a minute, and delivers some rabbit punches. He grabs Meyhu’s arm, trying to whip him to the other corner hard. Meyhu reverses, sending Russo into the corner this time! Russo looks groggy as Meyhu charges, only to get a boot to his face! :: Russo plays possum, and Meyhu gets his just desserts for charging! Smart move by Russo and now Meyhu is blinded! Or is he? I don’t think he’s messing around, he’s holding his head and wincing pretty bad! :: Russo charges at the blinded Meyhu, taking him down with a harsh boot and going for a pin while the referee slides in to count. :: one – two - - no! Another close call, this time on Russo’s end! But Meyhu gets the shoulder up! Both men are doing extraordinarily well, and this match is defiantly living up to my expectations! Both men have been cut a little bit on the back by that sharp glass, and now there looks more to be coming soon! :: Russo shakes his head in discomfort, picking Meyhu up and providing a sleeper hold. The crowd boos as Meyhu looks totally out of it, and the referee goes over to lift his wrist three times to signify a knock out. :: Could Meyhu be out of it? He certainly looks to be on Dream Street! I doubt it very much, but you never know. Russo could have put him out cold! :: The official gathers Meyhu’s wrist, and it drops once. He gathers it again moments later, and it drops once more as the crowd shouts “two!” The third time, Meyhu makes a fist and doesn’t let the wrist drop. Meyhu delivers a sharp elbow to Russo’s gut, making him release the hold. Meyhu then bounces off the rope, grunting with pain and gathering more blood on his back while providing a running elbow to the head of Russo with another pin attempt. :: one – two - - no! I can’t believe this. Both men have proved themselves worthy enough to survive in this match, and they indeed get my kudos! This has been a match unlike any other. I’m surprised that either man hasn’t passed out by now due to a lil slit on their back each time they hit those ropes with that glass on them! Neither man is afraid of their blood either. I remember when I was a kid that I almost passed out just of the sight of my blood. You aren’t the only one, but let’s get back to the match! :: Mike Meyhu pounds on the mat and picks up Russo once more, getting a poke to the eyes. Another warning is passed to Russo as he gives Meyhu a DDT to the mat, covering. :: one - - two - - no! Slow count, and Meyhu gets his shoulder up yet another time! This is amazing, I can’t believe what I am seeing tonight! :: Russo uses the ropes to pull himself up, and he waits for Meyhu to get to his feet. Meyhu does, and Russo hooks on executing a belly-to-belly suplex! He waits for Meyhu again, signaling to the crowd that the match may be over soon. :: What a suplex, and Meyhu is in some deep trouble right now! Russo wants to end it, he wants that “Falling Star” to hit! He may get his wish upon that star Jerry! :: Russo picks Meyhu up one last time, throwing him up into a Reverse Fireman’s Carry. Russo hits a sit-down neck breaker while the crowd goes insane, and Russo covers for the win! :: one - - two - - three! :: The bell tolls as Russo stands up, the referee raising his hand in victory. Russo pulls it away with anger as the crowd boos, and Russo stomps on Meyhu once more before leaving the ring. He gets a couple more cuts from the ropes on his back and he scrunches some more with his boots as he goes up the ramp and through the curtain. :: What a match, Russo hits the Falling Star and takes the champion. But he doesn’t win the crown, but this can indeed be a sign for the future of that belt! Meyhu is still lying on that mat, obviously upset at the outcome. But it was a decent and well officiated match, he shouldn’t be so depressed. He’ll have other shots at Russo, this doesn’t look to be over between these two just yet! :: The sounds of piano’s are heard now, as both men in the ring towards the entrance. The lights go down as a woman’s voice, begins singing. Eric Russo, and a staggering Mike Meyhu turn to the entrance, as only a white spotlight illuminates it. Red strobe lights go on and off How can you see into my eyes like open doors. Leading you down into my core where I've become so numb. The sounds of guitars, drums and bass now come on as the woman begins singing over those instruments. Eric and Meyhu begin looking worried, as they do not know what to expect. They stand at opposed sides of the ring, doused in darkness. Without a soul my spirit's sleeping somewhere cold until you find it there and lead it back home. Nothing! Everything goes dark. No more music. Nothing. And then a second later, the Music blasts out again, with a man’s voice joining in with the woman’s! Pyros explode in the entrance as the music becomes louder and stronger and every light switches on and off. Out of the back, Belmont steps out, with a look of demonic determination on his face. Wake me up. Wake me up inside. I can't wake up. Wake me up inside. Save me. Call my name and save me from the dark. :: OH MY GOD! TRISTAN BELMONT IS HERE! :: Belmont now slides in the ring as both men look at him and jump him. Eric Russo begins stomping away at a sliding Belmont, but to no avail, as Belmont pushes back with his arms and flattens him with a clothesline. He turns his attention to Meyhu and begins walking towards the cowering champion. Meyhu is in the corner now as he pushes back and runs into Belmont with a high knee to the gut. Belmont does not even move! Meyhu looks on in shock as Belmont shows a sign of pleasure, as he slowly wraps his arms around Meyhu’s neck, and throws him way over his head with a belly-to-belly over head suplex! Belmont begins walking over to Meyhu when Eric Russo comes from behind and shames both his forearms into Belmont’s back, which makes him stumble forward. Meyhu jumps back up to his feet and smacks Belmont in the face before kicking him in the nuts! :: Nutcracker! Damn it! :: Eric Russo looks on as Meyhu throws Belmont over his shoulder and drills him down with the Meyhu Meltdown! :: MEYHU MELTDOWN! Belmont is finished! :: Or is he? Just as Casey finishes those words, Belmont upper body comes up as the Undertaker once did! Meyhu looks on in total; shock as Eric cannot believe his own eyes. Belmont gets up and grabs Meyhu by the neck. Eric comes running and he is knocked down with a big boot from Belmont, who raises Mike Meyhu high in the air and drives him down hard into the mat with a choke slam! The fans go absolutely nuts as their hero is getting revenge! :: Payback is a bitch isn’t it boys! Meyhu and those damned Riders stole the Realm title win from Belmont last week. And now … they pay! And now Eric is alone with Belmont! :: Belmont stalks his way towards Eric Russo with the same demonic look on his face. He backs Eric into the corner and begins beating the living hell out of him with a variation of uppercuts and straight jabs. Eric stumbles back and forth, dazed and practically knocked out, and just as Belmont is about grab his neck too, Eric slides out of the ring and rolls out of danger’s way. Eric and Meyhu look at Belmont and both men nod their heads simultaneously as the slide back in the ring and run towards Belmont. Eric Russo misses with a clothesline and is leveled with a lariat by Belmont. Meyhu runs right into a big boot, which sends him over the third rope and to the ground. He begins getting back up to his knees and just as he is about to slide back in the ring; he shakes his head from side to side, leaving Belmont alone with Eric Russo. Meyhu is bailing! There is nowhere to run now! Eric Russo is trapped in the ring with Belmont! I LOVE IT! Run Meyhu, but soon enough, you will have to face this man, and you will not run anymore! Tristan Belmont is coming for you! :: Belmont picks Russo up and throws him on top of his shoulders in a crucifix position. Belmont follows by pushing him in the air, and grabbing Russo’s neck in mid-air, and driving it down hard with a sit down neck breaker! RECURRING NIGHTMARE!! Russo rolls out of the ring, in pain. The fans go absolutely nuts as Belmont is the only man remaining in the ring. The PA system blares out Belmont’s new theme song “Bring me to Life” by Evanescence. Belmont looks on with a crazed look on his face as we go to a commercial break.:: Outcome: Victory over Mike Meyhu Show URL: http://
