Eric Russo vs. Sean Dahmer :: Ring Announcer: Ladies and Gentleman, the next match is scheduled for one fall and is the Main-Event of tonight’s Realm. First, from Last Vegas Nevada, weighing in at two hundred and forty five pounds, he is the Leader of the Shadow Riders, Superstar Errrrrrrrrriiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiic Russo!!!!! The fans boo royally as they hear the name of the protagonist in this match. :: Main-Event time! Main-Event time! Yes Casey, it is indeed the time of night where two stars are given the opportunity to headline the stellar show that is Realm, and to make it even better then it already has been, which is going to be a hard task to do quite frankly! Hard or not, these two guys who are coming into the ring are hated by all, and to see both of them in a match, main-event or not, will be awesome. We get to witness their brutality put into work, against one another! And seeing as it is a main-event, both guys will work even harder to win this, which means they will hurt each other even more! Ha ha! This will be great! I hope this match to be a technical one, seeing as this will be a normal single’s match. You think that egotistical prick that is Russo will play a technical match? Well he can do it; he is a very good mat wrestler! Ha, a very good mat wrestler on a two main-event losing streak! :: All of the lights in the arena go out as the entryway fills with a thick red smoke. As this happens, 'Anger Management' by Methods of Mayhem begins playing throughout the arena. A bright spotlight centers on the entryway as “Superstar” Eric Russo steps into sight through the smoke. He is wearing a leather vest over his entrance gear. The spotlight glares off his sunglasses and slicked back hair as well as the gold horse’s head which sits atop the black cane he carries in his right hand. :: Look at the bastard! Just seeing him makes me so mad, I want to run down and beat the crap out of me! But of course, you are holding me back Jerry, so I will let Dahmer do that to him. I am not holding you back … Erm … shut up Jerry. :: Russo walks down to ringside and begins to climb the steps onto the apron. Russo walks to the center of the apron and turns to face the crowd. He proceeds to taunt the fans into a frenzy of hatred before entering the ring. He makes his way around the ring taunting the crowd even more as he removes his leather vest and waits for his opponent to come down to the ring. Anger Management by M.o.M slowly dies down into nothing, and now, the major jeers and boos of the fans are the only thing in the arena. Russo looks form side to side, laughing at the masses of people screaming for him to die and other obscenities.:: Listen to this Jerry? Listen to the reaction this pick is getting> No one likes him? Who cares? Who cares if anyone likes him? I do not like him myself, I will admit, but the guy is a great wrestler, and that is all that matters. How can you even start defending that fucking egotistical bastard? It makes no sense? Don’t you wish he were out of LOW as much as all the fans do? No I do not … beyond his big mouth is awesome ring ability, and that is what we are here to do, bring a wrestling show, and he helps a lot! Whatever you say. :: The jeers stay the same as Anti-Christ Superstar blares out of the PA system. The lighting closes down, and only a few dark red strobe lights go around the arena. Finally, as the song reaches it’s chorus, the lights all come back on, and form the back comes Nightmare Sean Dahmer, soaking wet, ready to fight. :: These fans don’t like him more then Russo, so why are you not going crazy on him and wishing he was fired and all that you wished a few seconds ago? Because Sean Dahmer, Sean Dahmer is not bad! Sean Dahmer just does not care about the fans, and that is why they hate him. Russo is just an ass everywhere he goes! So is Dahmer … But Dahmer is not an ass to every one in LOW. Rarely do you ever see him start a fight for no apparent reason. Because he rarely talks … :: Dahmer rolls under the third rope and quickly gets back to his fee, but he allowed Russo one opportunity, and already, he pays dearly for that, as he is stomped on the back by Eric Russo. The ring bell tolls, and the beginning of this match is officially announced. Russo lays in a few good kicks to the back of Dahmer and then picks him up by the back of the head. With no hesitation, he throws him into the ropes with an Irish whip, and catches a running Dahmer with a beautiful dropkick that sends Dahmer crashing to the mat. Dahmer springs back up, and Eric Russo catches him with a hip toss. Angered, Dahmer gets back up and runs back towards Russo, who attempts another hip-toss, only this time, Dahmer blocks it, and delivers a hip-toss to Russo. Russo gets back up, and a clothesline knocks him down. Eric Russo gets back up and looks from side to side, as the fans heavily boo him, and seem to be cheering for Dahmer here. Dahmer signals for him to come over, and Eric Russo obliges. The two men grapple in the middle of the ring. Russo pushes forward, seemingly gaining the advantage, but as soon as Dahmer is pushed back, he breaks the hold by lifting his knee in Russo’s gut, and following through with a snap Suplex to Russo. :: Beautifully executed there by Dahmer! This match is still in it’s beginnings, and already we have seen some good offense by both men. Not good enough … Would you stop being so negative damn it? You are ruining a good match, don’t you see that? Would you stop getting on my fucking back? I hate Eric Russo, you get that? I fucking hate his guts, so I want him to get hurt, is that so fucking hard to understand? Shut the fuck up! I want him down, I want him hurt and I will criticize a much as I have! Too bad if you don’t like it, and if you fans don’t like it at home, well fuck you too! … :: Russo holds his back as Dahmer gets back up to his feet and drops an elbow right to the heart of Eric Russo who lets out a huge curse upon contact. Dahmer rolls onto Russo for the very first pin of the night:: I doubt this will do it :: Referee’s Count: 1 … Kick-Out! An easy kick-out by Dahmer who just lifts his left shoulder with ease. Dahmer gets back up, and Russo soon follows him. Dean tries a few punches but all his attempts are blocked by Russo, who now connects with his own series of punches. Russo backs Dahmer into the corner by smashing his fist into Dahmer’s face and stomach. Once in the corner, Russo lays in a few shots to Dahmer’s chest, follow by a high knee that connects straight between Dahmer’s legs. The Nightmare tilts forward, holing onto his crotch, screaming in pain, as Eric Russo throws him in between his legs and hoists him up onto his shoulder in power bomb position, instants before he power bomb’s him into the turnbuckle! Dahmer’s neck connects with the third rope, and his body falls limp, sitting down now, against the turnbuckle. :: What a move! What a move! Yeah Yeah … I’ll give him that! That move might’ve broken Dahmer’s neck right there! Will he pin him now? :: Russo smiles as the fans boo him. He grabs onto Dahmer’s leg and pulls him a few feet away from the corner. Wasting no time, Russo applies a beautiful figure four-leg lock to Dahmer who now cries out in pain as Eric Russo applies much pressure and weight to Dahmer’s knee. The referee asks Dahmer if he is willing to stop the match, but Dahmer pushes him away, screaming for him to get lost. Dahmer wrenches his arm as far as he can, wishing to get to the bottom rope, but only a few inches are missing. Dahmer springs his body forwards, and now, in a sitting position, he strikes Russo to the gut two or three times. Russo keeps the move on, but loses some steam, which allows Dahmer to pull Russo along with him, and grab onto the third rope. The referee tells Russo to break the hold, and the Superstar does as he is told, quite confusing enough. :: Though things seemed to have been going well for Dahmer in the beginning, I think the tide has turned and now, Russo is taking complete advantage, and complete control. He has been able to apply two high impact and definitely hurting moves to Dahmer, who has yet to get that opportunity. Just wait … this match is not over! Dahmer cannot lose this … he just can’t :: Russo gets back up and immediately he goes back to Dahmer. He grabs both of Dahmer’s legs and pulls them up to about his waist. Dahmer tries to fight back, but just as he does, Russo falls back, which catapults Sean Dahmer forwards. His neck crashes down straight into the third rope, and his body stays at the same place. Dahmer holds onto his neck and screams out in pain as Russo quickly gets back up to his feet and taunts for the booing crowd. Dahmer helps himself back up with the aid of the ropes abut he is immediately charged at by Eric Russo who clotheslines him. Dahmer backs a few steps and comes back right into Russo’s arms who delivers a picture perfect Northern Lights Suplex, right into a bridge pin. Referee’s Count: 1 … 2 … Kickout! Dahmer springs up, breaking up the bridge pin. :: God damn it! Dahmer, do something you moron! Hurt him! This match has been all technical so far, and Eric Russo has showed himself to be the best between the two on that aspect, so far I say, because nothing is done yet. :: Russo gets back up angered that the match was not over yet. He picks up Dahmer by the back of the head and clubs him back down with his forearm, smashing it behind Dahmer’s neck. He picks Dahmer up again, but this time, the Nightmare breaks the hold and rushes into Russo, tackling him down with a hard-pressed spear. Nightmare lands on top of Russo and he begins to lay in some vicious punches in Russo’s face with no mercy. The referee tries to restrain Dahmer, but to no avail, as Dahmer busts up Russo’s nose with his fury of punches. Russo’s attempts to fight back are futile and pitiful. :: YES YES YES! THAT’S RIGHT! FUCK HIM UP GOOD! FUCK HIM UP! Casey, calm down! Quiet Jerry, finally that fucking bitch is getting what he deserves, Dahmer is taking it to him, and he’s putting down the hurt on Russo. He just broke his nose, I’m sure of it, it has to be! Look at it; it’s losing gallons of his blood! Ha ha ha! Go Nightmare! Continue! Break his neck now! Dear God, you have lost it! :: Filled with a burst of anger, Dahmer jumps up to his feet and picks up a bloody Russo. He whips him into the corner, and comes running towards him, crashing a hard clothesline into Russo’s chest. Dahmer loses no time to continue his onslaught, as he catches Russo with a swift DDT that spikes Russo’s head into the mat. Dahmer jumps back to his feet and climbs the ropes. He looks down at Russo and pushes himself upwards, jumping in the air, and connecting the Suicidal Intentions on Russo, a beautiful double Shooting Star press. Fans: Holy shit! Holy shit! Holy shit! Holy shit! Holy shit! Holy shit! Holy shit! Holy shit! Holy shit! :: Holy shit is right! What a move! What a fucking move! Yes! Dahmer is taking it home! He snapped out of the pain and now he is delivering that pain to Russo! Suicidal Intentions, this is all over! Cover him Dahmer, give him his third straight main-event loss in three weeks, humiliate him like the bitch he is! :: Dahmer rolls on top of Russo. Referee’s Count: 1 … 2 … Kick Out! :: What? No! no! How did he do that? God Damn It! I don’t know but he did it, and that’s all that matters! :: Dahmer looks on in shock as His chance of winning has just slipped between his fingers. He looks at Russo in shock that he kicked out after such a high impact move. Dahmer springs back up to his feet and waits for Russo to get up. Russo wobbles back to his feet, and just as he is up, Dahmer spits out the Blood Mist into his eyes. :: Blood Mist! Blood Mist! That’s right! You know what that leads too? No, what? THE ETERNAL SLUMBER! :: Russo wobbles back and forth, wrenching at his eyes, trying to see clear, but in a split second, he is thrown in between Dahmer’s legs, and on top of his shoulders! :: YES YES YES! THE SLUMBER ARRIVES! :: In an instant, Russo throws himself off, and catches Dahmer with a swift low blow, followed by a school. Boy pin, while holding the tights, without the referee knowing. Referee’s Count: 1 … 2 … 3!!! :: WHAT?! NO! NO!!!!!!!! WHAT THE FUCK!?! Watch your language! SHUT THE FUCK UP! The bell tolls and Anger Management by Methods of Mayhem sounds off. The fans boo loudly as Dahmer looks on in shock, and tries to explain to the referee that Russo held his tights! The referee hears nothing of it and goes to raise Russo’s hand, who can finally see. He has a slight smile on his face, and says he just outsmarted Dahmer. :: THAT BASTARD! HE DID NOT DESERVE IT! HE DID NOT EVEN GIVE HIM HIS FINISHER! :: Dahmer runs towards Russo, who kicks him in the gut and delivers the Falling Star, his finisher. Dahmer crashes down hard into the mat as Russo rolls out of the ring, victorious:: Happy now? Outcome: Victory over Sean Dahmer Show URL: http://
