Redemption of the Damned!

captured 1 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~ So now the battle lines have been drawn and the forces that are against us are now known. Raping and pillaging the very existence of our souls! But the Prophecy is strong within my troops as it flows through there blood, and with great anger we shall smite all of our enemies and then and only then we shall have our redemption…….my redemption! This is the passion that I feel, and this is the truth that I speak!!!! ~ ~ Khaos ~ {Hours have passed where it only should take minutes, weeks have passed where it should have been days, and years have slipped away where it only should have been months. And yet where has all the time gone? Those were the questions that the young John Black had to ask himself as he was sitting down to some coffee in a nearby Starbucks in downtown Philadelphia. The people inside seemed to be hyped on some type of drug because of the speed they were moving to get there coffee. “Has it really been that long?” he thought as he was contemplating his and Chapel’s lost to The Demond this last Realm. “All this time…….was I really riding the Adams train of success?” he took another sip of his double shot espresso cappuccino, lingered a bit engulfing the sweet aroma of a Starbucks coffee bean. This week it was to be him and Chapel going up against Danny Amazing and Michael Blair in a tag match but yet he wonders if he will be enough, is he the weak link or will he be one of the strong players?} ~ Anger, hatred, greed and power is some of the everyday events that go on……… ~ ~ Phone Rings! ~ Khaos: Yes……..got it, yeah I’ll be there! ~ Chapel ~ {On a lonely side road right by a local tavern in Pennsylvania there was a man who had at one time so much going for him….friends, success, and lady luck on his side. But now he is playing cards in a tavern for petty cash while he tries to reclaim his once glory with LOW. As we see him sitting in the dark smoke filled room we wonder if he is going to recover from his shaky start in LOW?} ~ Rebirth of the old to reshape our destiny……….. ~ Chapel (as he sits and plays cards): Read them and weep boys Royal Flush! One of the men who lost: Damn Chapel, that’s 3 straight in a row!! I say your dumb ass is cheating!!! Chapel (with a smirk on his face): Boys….Boys, I never cheat Lady Luck….especially when she is on my side! {Chapel rakes in the money as his cell phone rings.} Chapel (as he answers the phone..) : Hello……yeah…..but…..it wasn’t my fault……I understand……no……shit get off my back I’ll be there ok! (Slams the phone on the ground and storms out of the tavern.) ~ John Adams and S.I.N. ~ {Darkness is a funny thing really…lots of people have judged it for evil and others have judged it as a source of comfort like a blanket. A blanket of security or maybe a blanket that gives peace of mind, it doesn’t matter with the night sky because with Adams he calls it home. A home that has always been there, a home that he has always embraced and a home that will always be there in his mind….body and spirit, that’s the mind set of The Prophet as thunder is heard over head in a cemetery near by the arena where Realm 71 will be held. Adams has always been a man that has seen the evil in others as himself but as lightning lights up the night sky, he is troubled about the chair shot he received in Realm 69 by Haze. Because it was that shot, a shot heard all around the world of Professional Wrestling that started to change people’s perspective on Adams. It was that chair shot that was the turning point in the fans of LOW that told them “Maybe Adams is only human?” Well he is more than that; he is a survivor of a hellish existence of his childhood. But the doubt of the LOW fans and Hazes undefeated record is about to change…….it all will change.} ~ A Prophecy was foretold from the depths of hell……. ~ {As Adams waited under the night sky in the cemetery he noticed up in the sky that the clouds started to move in quick, blocking out the light from the moon. But yet the man who is a phenomenon to most and a mystery to all continued to wait. He looked on towards where his bike was as he noticed a white 2005 Lincoln Town car that was turned into a limo pull up into the nearby parking lot by Adams bike. As the occupant came out of the limo the sound of thunder got louder as it was revealed to be Richard Rush, owner of LOW, as he walked closer to Adams the lightning in the sky got more and more intense followed closely by the booming sound of the thunder.} ~ A destiny to collect all of the sins of the world……. ~ Rush: Adams…..I’m sure that all of the plans are set in motion? John Adams: What the hell do you think? Of course they are…..after this Haze will regret the day he ever crossed paths with me! {As the two masterminds talked about there plans of redemption a Black Lamborghini pulled up to the parking lot, and the occupants are revealed to be none other then The Devils Angels….S.I.N., Khaos and Chapel.} ~ And release them upon the world of man………. ~ Khaos (getting out of the car.): That’s the last damn time I have you drive me Chapel! Chapel (getting out of the car.): What’s your problem man, if you would have just listened and asked for directions…..but no you just wanted to talk to that girl! Khaos: So what, she had a damn good body! Chapel: SHE WAS 15! Khaos: No she wasn’t, she couldn’t have been man. I could tell she was 19 years old! Chapel: Who cares, were here! There’s Adams and Rush! {The two partners walked unto the hollow ground past the tablets of people who once was in the land of the living towards Rush and Adams who are still talking.} ~ A infestation that will surly take no prisoners………. ~ Rush (to the tag team partners as they walk up.): Well boys finally you two decide to show up! Oh by the way, nice match last week! Chapel: Listen, The Demond just got a lucky shot on us, I have a question though Mr. Rush! Rush: Yes? Chapel: I don’t need this loser! Khaos is the guy who is holding me back! Khaos: Me holding you back???? John Adams (interrupting them.): Shut up! You two have only to worry about one thing……..and that is if Donny doesn’t join I want you two numb nuts to challenge for the tag titles so get along or else! {The sound of Adams voice was enough to quiet the bickering Tag Team. And after they plotted and planed against Haze and the rest of there enemies, we could see that the heavens above released a down pour of water as if God himself was crying, and crying he may have been for the group of S.I.N. were some of the evil and cunning people around. What diabolical plan were they constructing? And how is Adams handling the lost of his Realm title? The truth of things were started to unravel as the chess pieces were all set out let the games begin.} ~ A Prophet and followers will lead into a new world, his!!! ~ ~ FIN ~
