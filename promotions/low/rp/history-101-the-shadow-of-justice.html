History 101- The Shadow of Justice

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. Slowly the logo fades out to black. For a moment, nothing. Then the sound of Russo’s voice speaks out as he continues his recount of the past.) Russo (VO): “With the Shadow Riders once again purged of all their impurities, we were free to continue without any further distractions. Or so we thought. It seems that someone else had ideas for our little group. Someone you do not refuse an offer from.” (The scene opens to a shot of the present point in time, Russo pushes himself away from the table that he has been sitting at fro so much of his story. As Jerry watches on, Russo begins pacing the length of the room as he continues his tale.) Russo: “In hopes of a quick and easy process, Mike Meyhu under the guise of Kevin Miller saw the advantage that he could gain by siding with Kage and myself. We had a history of siding with the highest bidder, and Meyhu knew that.” (Jerry pipes in.) Jerry: “How much did he pay you guys?” (Russo responds with a slight chuckle.) Russo: “As much as we wanted.” (Russo lets out a sigh then continues his story.) Russo: “It was during this time that Kage and myself reunited with an old friend from our days in the NRW. Luke Justice was an up and coming talent during our days in the NRW. Now, he had just broken into the LOW as one of the most promising future talents on the market. It was not long before our intersecting pasts nurtured a strong friendship. Luke may not have been a Shadow Rider at the time, but he was the closest anyone had ever come to displaying the attitude that we prized so much.” (The scene fades out with a series of squiggly lines. It quickly reopens to a shot from the past. The scene reopens on the ground floor in a fancy hotel lobby. The shot moves through the crowded lobby. Slowly the camera moves to the large doors of the elevator as they slide open. Russo, Kage, and Justice step out of the elevator and head over to the front desk. Behind the large oak desk sits a well-dressed man who is writing something in a book with a black leather binding. As the three men approach the desk, the man flips the book closed to reveal an insignia in the cover which says 'Ritz-Carlton'. As the men reach the front desk, Russo begins talking to the man sitting behind it.) Russo: 'Yo, we wanna check out.' (The man behind the desk looks up into the face of Russo as he responds.) Man: 'Oh yes, no problem sir. You're with the Miller party right?' Russo: 'That's right, you're looking at one half of the greatest tag team ever to step foot in a wrestling ring. . .' (Russo looks over his shoulder towards Luke.) Russo: 'You hear that Luke? The greatest tag team EVER to step in a wrestling ring.' Justice: 'That's only because you never faced Xtreme Justice.' Russo: 'Sure, sure. . . you just keep telling yourself that.' Justice: 'Will you just shut up and get us checked out.' Russo: 'I'm doing it, I'm doing it.' (Russo turns back to the man behind the desk as he begins speaking again.) Russo: 'Look, is there anyway we can speed this up?' Man: 'You'll be out of here in just a moment, I'm sorry for the wait.' (As Russo begins to turn to face Luke and Jason, the man behind the desk speaks up.) Man: 'Excuse me sir. It seems as though you have a charge on you're account.' (Looking a little surprised, Russo begins to question the man.) Russo: 'Charges for what!?' Man: 'Well it says right here that you ordered some movies on pay-per-view during the night.' Russo: 'Movies!? What movies?' Man: 'It says here that you ordered the film 'Dreamquest' starring Jenna Jameson at 4 o'clock in the morning.' Russo: 'Oh, that movie. No problem.' Man: 'There will also be a charge for the mini-bar and the damage that was done to the room.' Russo: 'Fine, how much do we owe?' Man: 'Let's see, damage plus movie plus mini-bar plus room charge. You're total comes to $15,472.' (Russo's jaw drops when he hears the price.) Russo: 'You know what, I just remembered that one of our friends is picking up the tab. Just send the bill to Tristan Belmont over at Lights Out Wrestling's home office. Here, I'll write down the address for you.' (The scene fades out as Russo’s voice starts up again.) Russo (VO): “It was not long before Luke grew on us. Through our time together in the Winning Team, I took notice of how well he functioned within the parameters of our group. That is why I finally decided to offer him official membership into the folds of my group.” (The scene now flashes back to the present day. Jerry excitedly begins speaking.) Jerry: “Wow, you really managed to find someone who fit the group that well?” Russo: “Strange as it may be, we did.” (Jerry smiles as he responds.) Jerry: “How lucky can you get? I mean what could the chances of that actually happening be?” (Russo lowers his head and shakes it from side to side in regret of his past mistakes. He begins speaking.) Russo: “Unfortunately, we were about to find out the answer to that question. We were about to find out just how bad the odds actually were.” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
