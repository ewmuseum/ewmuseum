Origins pt. 2

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::We open to a shot of the inside of some sort of control room. Inside the room, a large talking head sits inside along cylindrical shaped tube. Next to the tube featuring the giant head, a small robot that somewhat resembles a trash can scurries back and forth on the floor in front of a large computer. Just then, the room fills with light as gold and silver beams shoot through the ceiling and onto the floor simultaneously forming a cloud of smoke. As the smoke dissipates, we are greeted by the familiar masked faces of the Amazing Flutter Brothers. As the Flutter Brothers aware of their new surroundings, they begin survey the area as they try and quell their confusion. Before they have a chance to accustom themselves however, they are taken by surprise as the giant head in the tube begins to speak. His voice echoing and bouncing off the walls thought the metallic control room.:: Bobbabu - Welcome to my home young ones. My name is Bobbabu and I bet you are questioning why you are here. More importantly, I bet you are questioning how I am talking to you since I am little more then a head and have no voice box. Do not worry though, I will answer all of your questions without any further adieu. ::The Flutter Brothers sit back and watch intently as the head in the giant tube disappears and gives way to various images as he begins his story.:: Bobbabu - Recently in a galaxy not to far from this one, the evil forces of the evil Queen Mogombu have been progressively invading world after world, taking the resources of each planet and using them for their own benefit. These actions have caused many native populations to die a slow and agonizing death at the hands of these villainous forces. Sadly for us, thus far their evil reign of tyranny has been faced by the futile opposition of the Starship Enterprise crew. ::The Flutter Brothers speak up finishing each other’s thoughts.:: Flutter Brother 1 - How does this involve us? Flutter Brother 2 - The point of the story is out of view Bobbabu. Bobbabu - Don’t be so impatient, I was getting to that . . . phew! ::The Flutter Brothers quiet down as Bobbabu continues his story.:: Bobbabu - Recently, these forces of evil have ran out of planets to invade in these other galaxies. This has forced these evil forces to find sustenance elsewhere, so they were forced to move onto the next galaxy . . . this galaxy. They're first target has been chosen, and it is the planet called Earth. They wish to invade this planet and harvest your population, starting with the fittest of the fit. In other words, you. ::The Flutter Brothers’ demeanor obviously cracks at this comment. Fearful to hear what lies ahead of them, they bravely seek more information about their situation as they speak continuing each others thoughts once again.:: Flutter Brother 1 - How can we prevent this monstrosity from taking place. Flutter Brother 2 - What can we do Bobbabu? ::Bobbabu’s face re-emerges inside of the giant tube as a look of grave concern fills his face. He answers.:: Bobbabu - There is one thing you can do. I will however need the complete and total cooperation of both of you. ::The Flutter Brothers look questioningly at each other then almost as if they communicated with each other telepathically, they look back to Bobbabu.:: Flutter Brother 1 - You have our complete and undivided attention. Flutter Brother 2 - I hope your idea is not too taboo Bobbabu. ::With a smile on his face now, Bobbabu begins speaking to the Flutter Brothers who stand in attention listening intently.:: Bobbabu - I am glad to hear that. For it is you two that I have chosen to become the defenders of the planet Earth. You two will become my Metallo Zords. As them, the evil Queen Mogombu you will pursue. ::Hearing their team name for the first time, the Flutter Brothers speak out in unison.:: Flutter Brother 1 - The Metallo Zords? Flutter Brother 2 - That’s a name we’ll have to look in to Bobbabu. ::Bobbabu begins to talk.:: Bobbabu - There will be no discussion about the name. There is a long history behind the name of the Metallo Zords. That however is another story for another time. For now, you should remain content in the knowledge that along with the Metallo name comes a certain amount of power thanks to my special home brew. ::Now interested, the Metallos / Flutters speak out.:: Flutter Brother 1 - What kind of powers do we get when we become the Metallo Zords? Flutter Brother 2 - Can we swing canes made of bamboo Bobbalu? ::Smiling, Bobbabu responds.:: Bobbabu - Yes you will learn how to swing sticks of bamboo, but that is not all you can do. As the Metallo Zords, you will be able to tap into a universe of powers and skills the likes of which noone has ever seen. Anything that you need to know can be transmitted directly to you in less then an instant by my personal assistant, that little robot there, Bobbalu. ::A small robotic hand with a white glove on it raises out of the top of the robot. The hand then begins to shake from side to side as Bobbalu waves hello to the two Metallo Zords. Bobbabu begins talking again.:: Bobbabu - Before we go any further, I must warn you. Once you accept the role of the Earth’s protectors, there is no turning back. All of you past accomplishments in the circus will be gone. All of your hopes and dreams of a normal life will be lost forever. If you accept these duties, you must accept everything that goes with them. It’s not about you two, it’s about the world view. ::With a slight hint of reluctance, the Metallos / Flutters take a deep gulp and nod their heads in agreement to the terms that have been laid out in front of them. When he sees their acceptance of the offer, Bobbabu calls to Bobbalu.:: Bobbabu - Get the Metallo brain stew Bobbalu! ::The small robot moves along the floor until he reaches a small refrigerator. Hands come out of the top of the robot once again as he uses them to open the refrigerator door. Bobbalu then reaches inside and removes two cups filled with some kind of purple liquid. The robot then glides across the floor until he comes to a stop in front of the two Metallos / Flutters. Bobbabu starts talking again.:: Bobbabu - All you have to do is drink the liquid from those cups and the transformation will commence. Remember though that from this point forward, you must remain tried and true. ::The Metallos / Flutters nod their heads reassuring their understanding of the consequences of their decision. With a deep breath, they both reach out and grab the cups. Slowly they raise the cups to the location of their mouths under their masks and proceed to pour the liquid through their masks and into their mouths. With a loud audible gulp, the men swallow the liquid and seal their fate. The room flashes with a bright brilliant light that temporarily blinds anyone within view. As vision returns, we can see that the Flutter Brothers are no longer in the room. In their place stands two men, the one on the right is wearing a metallic looking silver vest along with an intricately designed silver helmet. Too his left, the other man wears a similar attire but his is colored gold. Both of the men are also wearing a pair of baggy black karate pants. The two men now grip hands briefly showing signs of an unsaid comradery as the Metallo Zords are born.::
