Delta Force (PPV RP)

16 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera opens a wide panoramic shot of a stately red brick two-story house, situated beneath a beautiful arbor canopy of oak and pine that serves to shade it from the harsh midday sun. The driveway comes straight from the quiet collegiate street a hundred yards or so from the house and then splits in a circular drive in front of the house. On it sit two vehicles—a black Jeep Wrangler and a garnet Ford Expedition. The Expedition is absolutely spotless and has a black vanity plate on the front with “CK” written in gold block lettering. The Wrangler is almost completely covered in caked mud, so much so that its confederate flag vanity plate is barely visible beneath the grime. The house itself is brick, and the white trim work stands in stark contrast to the deep red of the structure itself. The lawn is very well kept, without a single blade of grass overlapping into the driveway or street. As the camera moves in tighter, we can see a brand new bench press and weight rack on the front porch of the estate. Sitting on the bench, with his forearms resting on his knees, is one half of the LOW Tag Team champions—Hank Cobb. Wearing black mesh athletic pants and tan sandals, Cobb is breathing heavily, as the sweat has completely soaked through his gray tanktop. The reason for the exhaustion is readily apparent, as the barbell is loaded with four 45lbs. weight plates on either side. Grabbing a gallon of water from beneath the bench, Cobb takes a big gulp and then wipes the sweat from his forehead with the back of his right hand. “Man, it’s hotter than blazes today Korv. I’m fixin’ to pass out after that last set.” The comment is addressed to his good friend Colby Korver, who is reclining comfortably on a black leather recliner about ten feet to Cobb’s left. With only white mesh basketball shorts on, Korver appears the model of comfort. Sitting directly under a porch ceiling fan, Korver takes another sip from an almost empty bottle of Corona before answering his tag team partner. “It’s just another beautiful day here in sunny Florida. This shit’s right off a postcard man. You southern boys can always find something to complain about.” “Now wait a gosh darn minute, I’m over here sweating like a whore in church and you’re sitting over there sipping Corona and lecturing me?” Korver takes the final swig of his Corona and puts it down next to a half dozen other empty bottles beside his chair. Opening a medium-sized cooler on the ground to his right, Korver pulls out yet another fresh one along with a metal bottle opener. Opening the beer, Korver tosses the bottle opener and Corona cap back inside the cooler. Shutting the lid, he responds in turn. “Yeah, that sounds about right.” Cobb sighs audibly and flops back onto the bench, letting his arms extend out to his sides. “Alright, you win. I ain’t gonna sit here and argue with you when we have a much bigger issue at hand. One more day man, and then it’s our turn to shine. National TV boss, it doesn’t get much better than that.” “I hear ya man. It feels like forever since we beat those wannabe thugs on Realm a few weeks back. Ever since then it’s just been day after day of sitting around.” “Well some of us have been doing more than sittin’ CK, I feel stronger now than I ever have. Well…since the injury at least. But even the ol’ knee feels good right now, it’s all coming together man. We’ve got our titles, so now we just gotta go out and slap them boys around tomorrow night. It’s gonna be a brawl boy.” “Absolutely, but I still can’t believe these two jokers made the pay-per-view. I mean, have either of them done anything since we got here? Now I haven’t exactly done extensive research on their match history, but I’ve looked over their bio’s and it’s not much to look at. Neither of these guys have ever even sniffed title gold, and they somehow weaseled their way into a match for our tag team gold? Unbelievable.” “Ah, give ‘em a break. Whatever the reason, they’re our opponents, so we gotta go out there and get the job done tomorrow.” “No doubt. I’m ready brah. Soma and Reality, they have no idea what’s coming. These are singles wrestlers, and they’re stepping into the ring and our world. Tag team wrestling, they don’t realize how big a difference there really is. But come tomorrow, we’re going to show these yahoos exactly how harsh a reality it is. Because whether it’s you dropping Southern Comfort on them, me hitting the Keg Stand or us committing Fratricide, we will run right over these guys. Come Monday morning, they’ll be nothing more than road-kill in our rearview mirrors. Because this Delta Upsilon Iota mack truck is running on all cylinders…and we’re going straight to the top.” “Amen brother, now get off your lazy ass and spot me over here.”
