Agitated Volatility: Femme Fatale

10 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[OOC: Let me try to explain the premise of the last roleplay I put out called Happens To The Best Of Us which was the fifth part of the mini series ’Agitated Volatility’ and clear some confusion. Annihilation has not in fact changed his name…he’s merely developed a second personality. Annihilation destruction and all, and it is the focal point of his immeasurable strength and rage. However, Civitas Dei is a term used to describe the afterlife, and represents Annihilation’s intellect and ability to both read the minds of others and see events before they happen. Anni-Dei uses both sides of his persona in order to survive long enough to figure out just what mission he’s supposed to be carrying out. In context, Anni-Dei is very much a superhero, and the rest of this series is going to increasingly resemble an ongoing comic book-like saga. If you are lost in any of these, you may want to read from the start. Enjoy! [Rehash:] [A new sinister figure referred to only as “Mr. Black” appears just as Annihilation is developing his powers. Spurred on by his mission as an assassin to seek and destroy, Mr. Black searches for Annihilation but finds no luck. The mystery man seen in Annihilation’s dreams plays an important role in keeping him alive, but the time comes when Annihilation meets Mr. Black in an abandoned church. Using his new ability to harness his rage and send shock waves through the air, he causes the banister Mr. Black is leaning on to crumble and for Mr. Black to topple off. Presuming him to be dead, Annihilation flees. After receiving a call from the mystery man, Annihilation comes to realize that the second half of his persona, that of the enigmatic Civitas Dei, has been unlocked, and that his training is complete. Annihilation becomes known as Anni-Civitas Dei, with Anni or Annihilation representing his strength and rage and Civitas Dei representing his mind. Mind and body, Heaven and Hell, Creator and Destroyer – however you organize it, Anni-Dei now has all the makings of a superhuman figure. When Mr. Black resurfaces, Anni-Dei needs all his abilities to kill the assassin by luring him onto a rooftop and crushing him with a falling water tower. However, the New York State Insane Asylum captures Anni-Dei after his agent had leaked information that he had been acting very strange, and he’s lead away to the Asylum, bringing us to today…] ----------------- **Taking small sips from his bulky coffee mug, security guard Larry McCallister enjoys the start of yet another calm evening at his desk. With the unadulterated pleasure of watching the latest 24-hour marathon of Cops lying in wait before him, Larry takes another sip from his coffee and smiles subtly. Just then, the door swings open, and a petite woman donning a pair of black sunglasses and a tight, leather outfit struts in. Edwin stands up to greet the woman.** Larry: Excuse me, Miss, please step through the metal detector. **The woman steps through the metal detector, and the overhead lights instantly turn to a deep red and begin to flash incoherently. A loud crescendo of a beeping sound illuminates the otherwise dull and dreary front waiting room at the New York State Insane Asylum. Tired of the daily grind, Larry proceeds to go through the same procedure as if it were any other casual occurrence.** Larry: Miss, do you have any objects that are metallic in nature on your person? **Just then, the woman grabs Larry by the tie and forcefully flings him over his desk some ten feet away. While the woman proceeds to saunter away, Larry reaches gingerly over his desk and snatches his walky-talky, then screams into it…** Larry: I have a breach, I need backup! **The security alarm sounds, and the door inside the asylum is sealed before the woman can react. From a side door comes a team of three security guards, and they form a perfect triangle around the woman. The first man confidently lunges in, but the woman delivers an uppercut kick that hits him on the bottom of the jaw and sends his head cracking upward. The second man dives in to tackle her from behind, but the woman simultaneously dissolves from the head down until she’s nothing but what appears to be a cloud of smoke. As the man dives through this ominous cloud, she gradually reconstructs from the foot up behind him, then turns around and executes a cartwheel kick that delivers a blow to the side of his face and snaps his neck. The third security guard draws out his pistol and, with his hands growing increasingly shaky and, in the process, sending bits of sweat across the room, prepares to fire at the woman. Just then, she spins until she becomes an immeasurable blur, and the security guard fires at her and empties his cartridge. The woman then stops directly in front of him, and he comes to the shocking realization that he hit nothing but air. He drops his gun just as the woman clutches him by the throat, then tosses him upward so that his head barrels through the ceiling. With his head stuck in the gaping hole he created, he struggles a bit before he falls deadly still. The first security guard stands up and bolts toward the door, thrusting himself inside and, in doing so, slamming the door shut and locking it behind him. Larry stands up and draws out his pistol from his desk, and the woman darts over to him and grabs his wrist. Just then, a gaping hole opens in her stomach so that there’s nothing but air left at her midsection, and she places aims the pistol head at it and empties his cartridge. The hole closes promptly, and she snaps his wrist, then grabs him by the shirt, lifts him over her head, and tosses him through the front door of the Asylum, shattering the glass.** Officer: Dr. Rutherford, we’ve got a problem in the front office. **Standing at attention, Dr. Rutherford looks at the officer with a displeased manner and pouts his cheeks.** Dr. Rutherford: I’m about to see a patient, what on earth could be the problem? Officer: We had an intruder kill three guards. We had Mr. Mickelson return to tell us about it. He was pretty frantic, sir, and to be frank, I’ve never seen him this worked up. Dr. Rutherford: Three guards? What on earth is Mr. Mickelson talking about? There’s no way any man could do this! Officer: Sir, it’s a woman, and I’ve already had the opportunity to review the security tapes. This just happened maybe three minutes ago, and I need clearance to send in our armed officers. Dr. Rutherford: How many do we have stationed? Officer: Three officers trained in automatic weapons, however, I’d have to unlock the main door first. Dr. Rutherford: Do whatever you need to do, but don’t let this woman get in here. I’ve got a very important patient here who simply cannot be tampered with! Do you understand me!!! Officer: Y-y-yes sir. **The woman sits calmly as she watches the main door intently. She has pulled up Larry’s old seat and placed it in the center of the lobby, giving her the perfect vantage point. Just then, the main door is unsealed, and three heavily-armed officers toting automatic weapons and bulletproof vests file in. They form a uniform row guarding the door, and they aim their weapons at the woman and begin firing. The woman leaps up out of her seat and vanishes into a cloud of smoke, which hovers over the men. She reconstructs above them and then lands on top of the middle man’s head; leaping back, she executes a double back flip and lands on her feet on her chair. The chair tilts on its axis and rolls toward the back of the lobby as the other two men continually fire. Finally, one man rips a grenade out from his vest, tears the pin off, and whips it forward. The grenade explodes and tear gas flows out; the officers place their visors on to shield their eyes as they venture in with caution. The woman comes from nowhere and leaps forward, catching one man off guard and knocking him down. She sprints forward and vanishes into a cloud of smoke right before the main door, and the smoke seeps in through the cracks in the linings of the door. The woman reconstructs at the other side, leaving the three armed officers stranded and locked out.** **Gasping for breath, the officer scrambles into a heavily padded room containing Dr. Rutherford and a patient of his strapped to a stretcher. Just as Dr. Rutherford prepares to administer a drug to the patient with a shot, the officer stops him.** Officer: She got through the main door! **Dr. Rutherford drops the needle and screams at the officer.** Dr. Rutherford: What did I tell you?! Don’t let her through the main door!!! Listen, I need 5 minutes alone with this man, to at least extract from him what he knows. Now, you get security out there and you stall her until then! Officer: Sir, we have no security left. **As the officer continues to ramble on, a cloud of smoke oozes through the wall behind him and then molds together to form the woman. She grabs the officer and crushes his skull, then somehow detaches her hand from the rest of her body. The hand floats through the air, grabs a glass container hanging on the edge of a small desk placed next to the patient, and smashes it across the back of Dr. Rutherford’s head, knocking him unconscious. The hand then returns to the woman and re-attaches itself to her, and she leans in close to the patient and gently pulls off his mask, revealing him to be Annihilation. Woman: You don’t know who I am, but you will soon. My name is Aura and I’ve been instructed to save you Kevin, but we must move quickly if I am to do so. **The woman pries the straps off of Annihilation and allows for him to sit up.** Anni/Dei: Huh…what? Aura: I see they’ve kept you medicated. Quickly, we have to get going before they know I’m here. They will send in another assassin if they realize you’re alive. **Aura pulls Annihilation to his feet, and she helps him out of the room in which he was held.** --------- [Later On That Night] **After sneaking Annihilation into her car stationed at the rear of the building, Aura took all necessary precautions in making a swift getaway. Now, as they reach the outskirts of the city, the drugs’ stranglehold on Annihilation’s mind begin to wear off, and he awakens in the passenger’s seat.** Anni/Dei: W-w-where am I? Aura: Let me try to explain this as best I can. You were brought to the New York State Insane Asylum after there were complaints lodged about your behavior being a bit ”eccentric”. However, the assassins are still after you, and they tracked you down at the Asylum and paid off a Doctor to fill you with drugs and pick apart your brain to gather all the information that they need. I’m like you Kevin, but I’m also very different. **Aura’s left arm grows thinner around the wrist and her pinky finger grows until she’s able to turn the radio off without shifting her hand from the wheel. As she returns to her usual figure, Annihilation lets out a shrill shriek and shrinks back into his seat.** Aura: Just as you’re able to harness your strength, I too have an ability that supercedes all logical thinking. You see, I can shift my cells around at will. I can detach and reattach my body at will, and it opens the door to limitless possibilities. I can move through any space, shift my body accordingly, and even float. Collectively, a person is far too big to be suspended in air, but a single cell is entirely different. Anni/Dei: I don’t understand, why are you telling me this? Aura: To show I’m an outsider, much like you are. I also need your consent with what we must do next. Anni/Dei: What is that? Aura: It’s time that you visit the man from your dreams, Kevin. It’s time for you to learn of why exactly you were chosen and what you were chosen for. It’s time for these questions that have plagued you to find the answers you’ve yearned for. Anni/Dei: Where does he live? Aura: We’re close now, it won’t be more than twenty minutes. [As the car speeds off into the distance, the scene fades to a close.]
