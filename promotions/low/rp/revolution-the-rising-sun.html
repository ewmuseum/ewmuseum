Revolution: The Rising Sun

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The hot Saturday sun in Calgary is a pool of warmth for anyone outside. It warms their necks and makes the people feel better while working outside. Much like ice cream on a hot day the sun is a sensation to this town. At the local bar Jesse is just glancing around as he takes a drink. Jesse has long brown hair that is tied in a ponytail and a brown goatee. Jesse's blue eyes were told they make him stick out so when he walks around he looks down. A distraction is something he doesn't need so he keeps it cool and hangs low. Jesse takes a seat at the bar and asks for a Budweiser as he scopes out the bar looking for someone. As he turns left he jerks his head back noticing something or someone. They're in the center of the room sitting at a table is Steve Andrews. Steve is drinking a beer with his woman Terri. She's a nice blonde girl near 5'6' with a pink belly top and a pair of leather jeans with mesh strings hanging down the sides. Her face is real smooth with a freckle just above the right side of her lip. Green eyes and a small face, she makes bunnies look like second rate models. Jesse notices Steve and grabs his beer heading over towards him. As he approaches Steve looks up and with a sudden glance he jumps up and is hugging Jesse. The two immediately are doing some handshake representing friendship. Terri smiles as Jesse kisses her on the cheek and takes a seat next to the two. Steve is in shock from the stranger. He hasn't seen Jesse in near 2 years. Seeing as how the two split up after NRW they have not talked and have either not crossed paths or chose there routes to avoid stuff like this of happening. Jesse takes a swig of his beer and starts talking. 'So what the hell are you doing up in here?' says Jesse smiling like he doesn't know. 'You know man, when an old enemy hits the place I live in, its like dogs pissing on a tree and marking their territory. We all here for TBC,' replies Steve still surprised. 'When you say all who do you mean?' asks Jesse a bit confused. 'Myself, you, Nick Slater, Bladez, Corey Taylor, Omar, and I think another… not sure. You know ever since me being thrown from that second story apartment building I been having backlashes right? I had this fucked up think where I remember the worst things from the past. I have to inject some shit into my arm every night to keep calm. It's so messed,' says Steve. 'That is man. Playing it still? You know the guitar? I have been practicing my ass off on vocals. It's hard like hell doing something you have no background music too. However, I am hoping Bladez and you are up to it,' replies Jesse. 'Yeah I'm cool with that. I've been practicing here and there. It's cool. I got to catch up with Bladez here in an hour. So I'll be here all night. How about you?' asks Steve. 'Nah I got work to do. Someone was mentioning an old name that I have to check out, may be trouble for me later. Talk to you two later, okay?' says Jesse. 'Okay,' replies Steve. 'Sure thing,' says Terri. Terri kisses him on the cheek as Steve shakes his hand. Jesse guzzles the rest of his beer and sets the beer down with the 4 that are already there. He walks out as Steve goes over to the jukebox and puts a quarter in playing 'Sweet Home Alabama' a real nice song they listen to in Calgary. [Block Away] Bladez is seen in the house as he walks into the Ice Cream shop. He has an old T-shirt on that says 'Don't be Played, Be the Player!' and on the back 'Mystical Mix' in big blue letters that are outlined in white in some scratch text. Bladez chuckles as he sees a picture of him, Steve, and Jesse on the shelf of the Ice Cream shop. The clerk is an old man who's been working there for near 60 years. Seeing as how Jesse lived here a while ago the picture is old and the three are very young. The clerk smiles as he sees Bladez and extends a hand that is taken with gratitude by Bladez. Bladez looks at the list real hard but the man already has his ice cream ready. 'You always knew what I wanted,' says Bladez. 'Yeah well after 15 years it wasn't hard to remember an order I'd taken every day. So how's the group?' asks the Clerk. 'Not bad Mr. Herko,' replies Bladez. 'That's great news Ryan. Great news is always a good thing to hear around here, since you guys left we have only dropped in everything. You three were young but a life around here. The only three kids who did everything to get in trouble,' says Mr. Herko. 'Yeah we were a hand full. I remember that time when…' says Bladez. As he is about to say something two guys enter the shop. Ones real tall about 6'9' and has a beard that goes all the way around his cheeks connecting with his fade cut hair style. He has brown eyes and is muscular. Wearing a pair of black jeans and a white tank top that stands out only because of the large chain necklace that hangs from his neck over the chest area. The other guy is only 6'3' and not as muscular. He has extensions and is more thug-like than the other guy. Wearing a white sweater with a hood and a pair of baggy tan jeans he looks up at Bladez and smiles. Bladez looks closely and then after a few seconds' sees what he hasn't for near 3 and a half years, the face of Raptor who's an old nemesis, who also fought alongside the Heart Breakers against the Mystical Mix in the beginning. He was the first person to beat Jesse Jamester in IFF for the World Title. Bladez is in complete disbelief. He steps backwards as the two come closer. 'If it isn't an old friend, is that you Bladez?' asks Raptor. 'I believe it is,' says Viper. 'I guess I am then huh?' says Bladez. 'So what you doing up in here?' asks Viper 'I live up in here. Why you here?' replies Bladez. 'That's for us to know, and you to find out,' says Raptor with a snotty voice. Bladez stares down his archenemy. These 3 have been the crown jewels of the main reason for the closure of IFF. Bladez drops two dollars on the counter and walks around Viper and Raptor as he leaves. [Rooftop of Hotel] For sure it was none other than Corey Taylor upon the rooftop of Calgary's finest hotel, Snowfall Resort. The hotel guaranteed top-notch quality and sure did it deliver. It has a sweet that contains a bedroom for kids, master bedroom, kitchen, living room, dining room, 2 bathrooms, and a closet the size of a bathroom to fit your clothes. More than what's needed for a family of 4 and too much for just a honeymoon couple. Their average rooms are just a bedroom, kitchen, and a bathroom. Still without any doubt it's a bargain for near $100 a week. The locals who move in and out stay they're quite often so they earn their share of money. Above on the rooftop is Corey Taylor a visitor to this fine place, yet he fines no peace inside so he lets his troubles go upon the roof. Corey is sitting on the edge looking down. The townspeople walking around getting stuff or just having a nice day. He grabs some snow from a near by pile and forms a snowball that he throws at a woman down on the corner. It hits her in the back and she turns around to see who it was. Corey laughs as he throws another at an old man knocking the rug upon his head off. Corey just smiles as he sits there. Growing up where he did it was like hell compared to this place. Corey has a home, friends, and even someone to look up to. His idle for a superstar is Omar. Omar's a real big fellow who beat Jesse Jamester in a cage match during a World Title bout in EWF. Both were main superstars there and had achieved accomplishments beyond what many would have wanted. Corey was a manager at the time and was watching the match from a locker room. It was Omar who moved when Jesse went for the frozen fear making the ending result be Omar crawling out the open cage door to obtain the prize possession that Jesse had held. That match was something that opened the eyes of all superstars in EWF and became the reason for a superstar such as Corey to fight again like he did in IFF. As Corey sits there he hears a noise. From the side of the building where the stairs are comes three men. Two are large and very round, almost twins if described closely. The other man is short, yet muscular, and all three are wearing black suits. Corey jumps up and starts to walk quickly along the side of the building. However the big men cut him off and the little guy comes up to him looking ready to fight. 'You owe me Mr. Taylor! Why do you run eh? Why do you make it so complicated for yourself? I am not your enemy. I am your friend, but when friends are mistreated they become your enemy and that's what I feel like, mistreated. Do you want to be a victim of the Mafia eh?' says the little man. 'Johnny I didn't mean to. Really, I have been working my ass off to get that money but it's been real tough. With me being up here and all, my mother dying, and losing my house. I am near flat broke,' moans Corey as the large men hold him by the shoulders. 'You think I am stupid? This isn't work. Sitting on a roof throwing snowballs at women isn't work. Does this look like work to you Mikey?' asks Johnny. 'No sir,' grunts the large man to the left who must be Michael. 'That goes for me too sir,' says the other who has a wristband on that says Thomas. 'You have a week to get me half of what you owe, or I'll have to pay a visit to your wives house. Understand?' says Johnny. 'Understood,' replies Corey. The large men toss Corey to the ground and walk away with Johnny. Corey is thankful for the offer and gets up quickly preparing for some attack but none follows. [Fades to night] Let the earth be shattered… Let dreams drift on… Let life ponder… Let it go beyond… From heaven to high water… We cry for our well being… We care for no one but ourselves… We are selfish beyond our years… Like rich and greedy pigs we fight… Until we fall… Then all hope is over… And life means nothing to us anymore… It's going to be a ride… One that will last an eternity… So buckle your seat belt baby, Cause were hitting the highway to our souls... Life is but a mystery, let's keep it that way.
