Progressing towards Realm

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders along with a voice over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to a shot outside of the airport where we last left “Superstar” Eric Russo after his near death experience. Still slightly shaken from the incident, Russo walks out of the airport carrying his bag followed by his newest “friend”, Lexus. Russo walks towards the street and plops his bag down on the curb. In a somewhat annoyed tone, Russo begins speaking to Lexus as he surveys the pick-up area.) Russo: “I don’t get it, the limo was supposed to be here ten minutes ago. Just like LOW to strand it’s biggest star in the middle of North Bumblefuck with no way to get to the arena.” (Grateful that he is not leaving her behind, Lexus turns to Russo as she responds in an appreciative tone.) Lexus: “I really want to thank you for the ride. The last guys I had a job with would have just left me sitting in that airport with no way to get home. Kage really does have you all wrong.” (Enjoying the ego boost that Lexus is giving him, Russo turns to her with a slight smirk on his face.) Russo: “Finally, someone sees that I’m not a bad guy. I’m a very caring pers . . .” (Russo is cut off by the sound of screeching tires as a long, sleek, black limo barrels towards him and Lexus. Russo quickly jumps back from the curb pulling Lexus back as well. The car comes to a sudden stop with the back door lined up perfectly in front of Russo and Lexus. The driver’s door does not open but rather just sits closed as the car trembles with the sound of the still running engine. Russo reaches out and opens the back door of the car allowing Lexus to get inside. After Lexus has moved in and gotten comfortable, Russo sticks his head in the car and calls up to the driver in an angered tone.) Russo: “Yo . . . what the hell is wrong with you? Not only are you late, but you didn’t even get out of the car to open the door. I do believe that is part of your job description jackass.” (Getting even more annoyed by the driver’s total lack of penitence for his actions, Russo calls up to him again.) Russo: “You do realize that my bag still has to go in the trunk don’t you?” (Suddenly the driver’s attention is taken as he turns in his seat to look at Russo. The driver is a middle aged man with an old fashioned 90's style bowl hair cut. As he stretches his lips into a large smile, he exposes a large gap between his two front teeth. In an almost ridiculously moronic tone, he speaks.) Driver: “Oh . . . sorry about that.” (With that the driver reaches out and triggers the latch for the trunk. He then turns back to Russo with an expectant look on his face as he speaks once again.) Driver: “There you go.” (Taken aback by the driver’s total ineptitude for his job, Russo pulls his head out of the car and bends down as he grabs hold of his bag. In one swift motion, Russo tosses the bag into the trunk of the limo triggering the sound of some type of glass breaking. Shaking his head, Russo then slams the hatch of the trunk shut once again. Still slightly confused about how this driver has managed to keep his job for this long, Russo slides into the car to join Lexus slamming the door shut behind him. The car peels out of the pick-up area and onto the highway as it heads towards the arena. As the car moves along the highway in a peaceful fashion, Russo moves in closer to Lexus in order to try and get a better “thank you” for his generosity of giving her a ride. As this is happening, the driver of the car keeps taking quick glances at the couple in his rear-view mirror. The driver continues this action for a couple of minutes until finally, he destroys the moment as he begins talking to the couple.) Driver: “So, what were you two doing at the airport? Did you fly in from somewhere?” (Almost in shock the driver’s utter stupidity with his comment, Russo glances up at him to respond.) Russo: “Good guess . . . we were at the airport because we flew in from somewhere.” (Russo now focuses his attention back on Lexus. As he does this, he mumbles under his breath to her.) Russo: “What a jackass.” (Russo and Lexus continue with their previous activity trying not to let the driver ruin their good time. This however is short lived as the driver pipes up again.) Driver: “Well I saw that you had some bags with you. Then I saw that you had a ticket from the airport sticking out of your back pocket. I just added up the facts . . . it doesn’t take a genius or anything.” (Refusing to take his attention off of Lexus, Russo mumbles a response the best he can due to his face being buried in the nape of Lexus’ neck.) Russo: (Muffled)“That’s nice.” (Russo continues working on Lexus until the driver cuts in yet again.) Driver: “So, where you two headed?” (Disgusted, Russo pulls away from Lexus realizing that the driver isn’t going to let the couple be. With a sigh of submission, Russo answers.) Russo: “We’re going to Norfolk . . . I’m a wrestler for the LOW and I have a match there.” Driver: “Ah, you’ll love it there. Florida is nice and warm around this time. I don’t understand why you’re going to Virginia first though. You want me to just turn around and take you South now?” (Russo rubs his forehead trying to cope with the headache that is mounting due to the idiotic line of questioning that the driver is putting forth. Reluctantly, he answers not wanting the driver to make a decision on his own accord.) Russo: “No . . . that’s fine. Just tack me where the sheet says.” (Russo turns his attention to the window where he watches the other cars on the road wiz by one after the other. The driver glances back at him once again in the mirror as he introduces himself.) Driver: “The name’s Christmas . . . Lloyd Christmas.” (With a sigh of despair, Russo responds.) Russo: “I’m Eric and this is Lexus.” (Still looking back in mirror, we can tell the Lloyd senses Russo’s utter disinterest in what he has to say. So in an attempt to capture his curiosity, Lloyd begins speaking to him once again.) Lloyd: “You know Eric . . . this isn’t what I normally do for a living. It’s just for now.” (Still not interested in what he is saying, Russo responds. Had it of been any other day, Russo would have already reached his limit but due to his experience earlier in the day, he was feeling abnormally generous. He can’t however resist letting his wit shine through.) Russo: “Is that so . . . well good for you. What do you normally do . . . work for telemarketing companies?” (Letting out a laugh, Lloyd responds.) Lloyd: “No . . . me and my buddy Harry are saving up our money so we can open our own pet store one day.” (Feigning interest in hopes of shutting Lloyd up, Russo responds.) Russo: “Good for you, hope everything works out well.” (Lloyd’s eyes light up with Russo’s apparent new found interest spurring him on even more. He turns in his seat and looks back at Russo and Lexus with an ear to ear grin as he speaks once again.) Lloyd: “I got worms.” (Without explanation, Lloyd turns his attention back to the road. Lexus’ expression turns to one of fear and disgust as Russo responds with utter disdain in his voice.) Russo: “You what!? I don’t wanna know that you jackass!” Lloyd: “No, that’s what we wanna call it. I Got Worms because that what we’re gonna mainly do. We’re gonna sell worm farms . . . just like ant farms.” (Russo’s look of disgust turns to one of confusion as he responds actually interest fro the first time. Although te interest is for all of the wrong reasons.) Russo: “You’re selling worm farms?” (Overjoyed that Russo is asking questions, Lloyd decides to further explain.) Lloyd: “Yeah . . . worm farms! Isn’t that a great idea!?” Russo: “I guess.” (Hearing them underlying tone of doubt in Russo’s voice, Lloyd decides to explain why his idea is so ingenious.) Lloyd: “It really is a good idea . . . see most people don’t realize that worms make great pets. Much better then ants in fact. Their quiet, loveable, they look neat, they don’t have any teeth so they don’t bite, and the kids love the way they squirm all over the place.” Russo: “Aren’t you supposed to leave them in the farm just like the ants?” (Realizing that Russo’s observation makes sense, Lloyd responds.) Lloyd: “Well yeah . . . I guess you are. Worms are still better though, their at least three times more fun. Besides, if you chop an ants head off it doesn’t grow back it just kind of wiggles around and dies.” Russo: “Oh . . . I didn’t know that.” (Unfazed by Russo’s interjection, Lloyd continues with his praises of worm farming.) Lloyd: “Not to mention there’s a lot of money to be made when it comes to farming worms. I wouldn’t mind getting some of it either if you know what I mean. I could be a rich man.” (Shaking his head, Russo turns his attention back to the window as his thoughts wander back to his upcoming title defense at Realm and the emanate destruction of the Shadow Riders. Lloyd seeing the concern on Russo’s face pries at the cause.) Lloyd: “What’s wrong, still a little tense from your flight?” Russo: “Yeah . . . something along those lines.” (Lloyd turns his body sideways leaning his right arm on the back of his seat while keeping his left hand on the wheel to keep the car steady in order to look at Russo. As he does this, he begins speaking.) Lloyd: “It’s ok, you’re on the ground now. Besides, it’s not flying that you really have to worry about. In fact riding in a car has been proven to cause more deaths in one year then flying does in ten. So, your more likely to be killed off a plane then on. You know like in a car crash that mangles your body because you go through the windshield and slide twenty feet away from the accident on the hard gritty cement.” (Russo looks up at Lloyd realizing that he is not even remotely concerned with the road in front of him. Lexus is paralyzed in fear once again as she has her second close encounter with death today. Russo quickly yells out Lloyd.) Russo: “What are you doing!? Keep your eyes on the road you idiot!” (Realizing what he is doing, Lloyd quickly turns his attention back to the road in a state of hurried panic. Once he is sure he has the car firmly in his control, he speaks over his shoulder to Russo.) Lloyd: “Good idea! You never know what kind of maniacs are behind the wheel these days.” (Russo lets out another agonizing sigh as the car continues on it’s way towards the location of Realm. Slowly, the picture fades out to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again where we hear a voice-over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The picture then cuts to a black, blank screen.)
