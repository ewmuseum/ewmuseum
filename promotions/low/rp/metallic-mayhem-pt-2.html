Metallic Mayhem Pt. 2

2 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~{ The last time we saw our heroes, they had just been beeped on their com links while they were enjoying an afternoon out with their friends. Meanwhile, the evil Queen Mogambu was busy sending her henchmen to work concocting a very special ‘surprise’ for the Metallo Zords. After demanding it not just be any monster, the Queen gave special instructions to Raziel as to what she wanted sent to destroy the Metallo Zords. What is in store for our intergalactic friends? Just what is Queen Mogambu having made? Hopefully we’ll find some answers. We now join our heroes as they answer their call to save the world. }~~~ ::We start with a scene of the Metallo Zords sitting at the table in the recreation center with their four friends. The group sits in silence as they watch their Metal clad friends.:: Gold Ranger – All right, let’s do it. ::The Metallo Zords look down intently at their com links and press in the buttons to receive their urgent message. Bobbalu’s voice is broadcast through the com link.:: Bobbalu – Rangers, Rangers, come in Rangers! We have an emergency! Gold Ranger – We are here, what is troubling you, Bobbalu? Bobbalu – It’s Queen Mogambu! Gold Ranger – Queen Mogambu? Whatever did she do, Bobbalu? Bobbalu – You need to get to the control center, Rangers! She’s constructing something, or someone, I don’t know who! Gold Ranger – We will be right there, us two, Bobbalu! ::The Metallo Zords stand up from the table and walk about three feet away from it. They then lock hands and address their friends.:: Silver Ranger – I am sorry we have to part on such terms, but the world needs us, friends. Gold Ranger – Yes, my sentiments exactly. Thin n’ Trim Tammy – Don’t sweat it, guys, we understand. Afro Otis – Yeah, saving the world is something you cats have gots to do. We can dig it. Brainiac Benji – Affirmative. Pleasantly Plump Patty – If you have to leave, can I have the rest of you’re food? Gold Ranger – Good, I am glad you understand. Silver Ranger – We will return. ::The Metallo Zords grasp on to each others' hands and wait. Two beams of light shoot from the sky, one gold and one silver. Each beam surrounds the appropriate Ranger, and as quickly as they appeared, the beams are gone. With them, the Metallo Zords disappear as well.:: Pleasantly Plump Patty – Does that mean I can have their fries? ::The scene cuts to a shot of the moon, and then to the inside of Queen Mogambu’s palace fortress. Inside, the Queen stares at her crystal ball. She then pivots around and begins looking over a railing, down at her workroom.:: Queen Mogambu – What is the progress of our creation, Raziel? Raziel – It is almost complete, my Queen. He will be ready to fight by this evening. Queen Mogambu – Yes! Oh, yes yes yes! Victory is mine! Muahahahaha! ::We are given a few quick glimpses of the workroom now. All we can see is sparks flying every which way as Raziel leans over the creation. A bright metallic glare seems to be being reflected from the body of the new creature. The glare is so bright that it fills the screen with a white light, blinding the viewer temporarily. The scene now cuts to inside of the Metallo Zords Control Center. Here, we see the Gold and Silver Rangers standing in front of Bobbabu, the giant, all knowing, talking head in a tube. His partner, Bobbalu, scurries about the floor worrying franticly.:: Bobbabu – Rangers, there is an emergency in the park. ::Bobbabu directs the attention of the Metallo Zords to the Orb of Knowledge. The Zords turn around the view the Orb, where a scene of the park is displayed.:: Bobbabu – It seems as though the despicable Queen Mogambu has dispatched a pack of her evil Ich Van Herbes to terrorize the masses! Gold Ranger – Gasp! No, she will not get away with this! Silver Ranger – How dare she ruin these poor people’s picnics! Bobbabu – I know, this is vile and disgusting. Gold Ranger – We must put an end to her reign of terror! Silver Ranger – No deed this nefarious goes unpunished! Bobbabu – My sources tell me however, Rangers, that there is a storm brewing. Queen Mogambu has something big in the works, and I fear it could spell the end of this planet. Gold Ranger – Nonsense! But first, we must stop those Ich Van Herbes! Bobbabu – So be it. ::The Metallo Zords lock hands once again, and just like before, two beams of light come down around them. The light surrounds the Zords, and within an instant, the light and the Metallos are gone. We cut to a view of the park. Moments later, the Gold and Silver beams of light dart through the sky and zap down to the grassy hills of the park. The Metallo Zords stand where the light connected and survey the area.:: Gold Ranger – It does not appear that there are any Ich Van Herbes here, my Silver amigo! Silver Ranger – But I do not understand, there were Ich Van Herbes running around like sugar in maple syrup a minute ago! Gold Ranger – Well it looks like they are all gone. Our job here is done. Silver Ranger – I suppose you are right, Gold Ranger. It is time that we . . . ::A lightning bolt hits the ground in between the two Metallo Zords. The Metallo Zords are sent flying back by the charge of electricity and land on the ground about ten feet apart, each about five feet from where the lighting hit. A puff of smoke forms where the lighting stuck, and when it clears, a figure is standing there. The Metallo Zords try to make it to their feet as they mutter in disbelief.:: Gold Ranger – No . . . it can not be. Silver Ranger – It . . . it is impossible. ::The Rangers look on in disbelief. We are left with one final flash of the figure, who is glimmering in the sunlight as if he is wearing a suit of Metallic armor. Who is this mysterious figure? Why does he have Metallic armor on? Have the Metallo Zords met their match? Did Patty get to eat those left over fries? Find out this and much, much more as the Earth hangs in the balance, next time we join the Metallo Zords!::
