Before the Kill

6 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Life had been different since Donny took me to my father and they both showed me the error of my ways. I found out the true meaning of my existence. My reason for existing was just this, to deliver all sinner’s souls to what would be their new home, Hell. I didn’t understand it at first, but it became clear to me over time. Donny’s voice still rings clear in my mind, “You owe me your life brother. If it were not for me, your head wouldn’t be on your shoulders.” I didn’t understand what he meant at first, so I had to ask him what he meant by that and he answered me with this, “When I took you from Realm and from the Living Realm and took you to Hell, our father was going to take your head. You have failed him time and time again, he wasn’t going to let that happen again. But I begged our father to give you the same second chance that he gave me. I told him that I would take full responsibility of you.” I kind of had to laugh at what Donny was telling me. I didn’t laugh about the fact that I could have lost my head but at the fact that Donny was going to take full responsibility of myself and my actions. Hearing that took me into the past, way into the past. I remember living with our foster family and it seemed like no matter what I did, Donny took the punishment for me. At that time I had complete respect for my brother, I even so much as loved him at that time. But men grow, and love fades. Hate began to grow inside me, hhatrednot just for my brother, but for everyone. But now it seems as though that is changing. I don’t love my brother but I have grown to respect him again. He earned the respect by asking our father to spare my life, but at what cost to the both of us. I feel so different now. Life, trust, and even love use to fill my body but it has been pushed out of me and only darkness remains. The more I try to love the more the darkness grips me. I still remember Dark Specter telling me to go back to her. That her can only be one person, my wife and my only lover, Dominique. But how can I go back to her? I am not the one who Dominique fell in love with. I am now nothing but a shell, no soul to call my own. No love fills these bones, only darkness, and I had my brother to thank for this. He help free me from the things that were my weakness. Without my brother I would still be the weak fool that I once was. When I was capable of love I could never cconcentrateon my match. I would always be thinking of Dominique, hoping she wouldn’t get involved in the match some how. And what if she did get involved? I had to worry that if she did she would get hurt. But I am free of that now. There is but one thing on my mind now, only one goal that is so clear to me. That goal is the job that was bestowed upon me, to deliver sinner’s souls. The day of our first job had finally come. I still remember that day well. I sat somewhere in the depths of Hell only waiting for the word that I would be doing some killing. I remember telling my brother that when the job was first given to me. I told him that I couldn’t wait to kill again and it angered my brother. “This isn’t just some mear killing!” he screamed at me. “We are simply sending souls to our father. Even in death there is still life, we are simply taking them on the jjourneyto the next faze of their lives.” I didn’t understand this at first. I always thought that when your soul left your body you were dead. I thought that no matter if you went to heaven or hell, you were dead just the same. But the more I thought about it the more I understood. Your physical body may be dead but all it was was a shell to house your living soul. Even when your physical body has died your soul still lives. As I sat there my brother came along and said, “The time has come.” I knew right away what he meant. It was time to send our first victim’s soul to Hell. He then told me what this mission was. “We must go to a little town in Pennsylvania called Cookport. Two teenagers there continue to sin day in and day out. It is our job to send their souls where they belong,” Donny told me as I became excited, the first real emotion I have felt since the darkness took my body. Cookport, that place sounded familiar to me somehow. Then I remembered. I remember my life somewhat from when me and my brother had got separated. I lived in Pennsylvania for a short time and I remembered going to Cookport a few times to a fair. I could not wait to return there only to see what had become of the shit town that I once knew. We had finally returned to earth, to the town of Cookport, and thus, our job began.
