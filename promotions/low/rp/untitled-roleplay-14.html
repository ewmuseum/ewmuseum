Untitled roleplay

captured 21 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eric Russo vs. Jason Ortiz JD: We have finally made it to tonight’s Main Event! Jason Ortiz will defend his LOW World Championship against a man who was granted a title shot by Vice President Yuka, “Superstar” Eric Russo, the mastermind behind the Shadow Riders. CS: That’s right. This match has been in the making for months and the match is finally here. I have a feeling that this match will be one to remember when Eric Russo is crowned the new LOW World Champion. I can feel it in my bones! JD: I don’t know about that. Jason Ortiz won’t go down without a fight. Let’s go down to the ring where Senior Referee Tom Bentley, and Ring Announcer Rusty Monarch, are standing by. Take it away, Rusty! RM: The following contest is scheduled for one fall, and it is for the LOW World Heavyweight Championship! “Devil Without a Cause (Superstar Remix)” by Kid Rock starts to play as “Superstar” Eric Russo slowly steps onto the stage, being greeted by the Pittsburgh crowd. The crowd jeer loudly, but it doesn’t effect the leader of the Shadow Riders. Dressed in his wrestling attire and complete with a Shadow Riders T-shirt, he makes his way down to the ring.| RM: Making his way to the ring from Las Vegas, Nevada. Weighing in at a total of eight hundred and thirty-two pounds when you include his ego, but weighs in at two hundred and forty-five pounds. This man is the creator of the Shadow Riders. This man has the largest ego in Sports Entertainment today. He is the one and only...“SUPERSTAR” ERIC RUSSO! JD: Sheesh, such a long entrance for that egomaniacal man! CS: Hey, it strokes his ego. His ego will be thoroughly stroked when he becomes the next LOW World Champion. JD: For some reason, that sounded very wrong. RM: His opponent... “Dirty Off Your Shoulder” by Jay-Z starts to play and the Pittsburgh crowd went absolutely nuts for the LOW World Champion. When Jason Ortiz steps onto the stage with the World title belt around his waist, the fans go into a bigger frenzy, about to blow the roof off the arena. He makes his way down to the ring, smacking hands with the fans and the arena is filled with “Crack Head” chants. RM: Making his way to the ring from Miami, Florida. Weighing two hundred and seventy-nine pounds, he is the LOW World Heavyweight Champion - he is everyone’s favorite Crack Head - JASON ORTIZ! Jason Ortiz enters the ring, removing his World title belt and handing it to Tom Bentley. Both men walk towards each other, staring each other down while Bentley goes over the rules. TB: All right, gentlemen, this match is for the LOW World Heavyweight Championship. I want a good clean match - and I do mean clean, Russo. Pin falls and submissions will determine the winner and World Heavyweight Champion at the end of the match. Are there any questions before we begin? ER: Can I use a lifeline? TB: Don’t be a smart-ass, Russo. Since there are no question, shake hands and let’s get it on. Both men are hesitant, not even wanting to extend their to one another. Being the man that he is, Jason Ortiz extends his hand to Russo, keeping his eyes on him. Now Russo is the one being hesitant. He looks down at the champion’s hand, then back at his eyes. A grin curls the right side of his face and shakes Ortiz’s hand, then unloads a massive loogie in Ortiz’s face. The fans immediately boo as the champion stands there with a large snot ball on his cheek. To pay “Superstar” back, he unloads with a hard right hand, knocking Russo to the ground. Tom Bentley calls for the bell. JD: After a disrespecting start, the match is on! CS: You mean Ortiz’s sucker punch? JD: Why do I even bother with you? The LOW World Champion wipes the large loogie from his cheek and gets Eric Russo to his feet. He whips Russo into a corner and unleashes a massive Avalanche Splash in the corner, followed by right hands and kicks to the gut, breaking down “Superstar” in the corner. Senior Referee Tom Bentley is there to break up the onslaught and gives Crack Head a warning. Ortiz picks up Russo and gets a thumb to the eye of his troubles and a stiff Clothesline afterwards. Russo drops to his knees and does a blatant choke in front of the referee and Bentley goes for a five count... One... Two... Three... Four - and Eric Russo broke the choke before the count of five. Tom Bentley warns Russo for his antics and Russo waves him off and puts the boots to the World Champion. Afterwards, he drops an elbow and goes for a cover, digging his right forearm in Crack Head’s face... One...and an immediate kick-out after the count of one. “Superstar” continues with the momentum in his favor and locks in a Triangle Choke, a deadly Jujitsu (I believe) choke. Russo locks it in perfectly and applies pressure around the neck, right arm and shoulder of the champion. The champion tries everything he can to break the hold, but Eric Russo isn’t going to let up. He continues to apply more pressure around his neck and Jason Ortiz’s face is starting to turn blue. After having the move on for about thirty seconds, “Superstar” breaks the hold. CS: Eric Russo is toying with the champion and I’m loving it. JD: Of course you would love it. You have to have your lips surgically removed from his backside every time you Eric Russo a visit. Eric Russo gets to his feet, looking down at Jason Ortiz...everyone’s favorite Crack Head and the LOW World Heavyweight Champion. For a brief moment he pitied Ortiz in the disposition he was in. Pity moment over...time to annihilate. He picks up the paper champion and whips him into the ropes. Afterwards, he buries a knee into his stomach. Next, he locks in a kneeling Dragon Sleeper, keeping Ortiz in his clutches, applying pressure around the champion’s neck. Tom Bentley is asking Ortiz if he surrenders and Ortiz refuses. Russo realizes that if he applies more pressure around the champion’s neck, it won’t do him any good and he won’t submit. So, he breaks the hold and gets Ortiz to his feet. He whips Ortiz into the ropes and attempts a Flapjack, but Ortiz counters with a Running Spear! The fans erupt after that. He gets to his feet and picks up “Superstar” and sends him into the rope. After coming off the ropes, Ortiz executes a Spine Buster ala Arn Anderson! The fans erupt with cheers! Crack Head! Crack Head! Crack Head! Crack Head! JD: Jason Ortiz is making a comeback! Kick his ass, Crack Head! CS: The bastard got lucky. There is a reason why Eric Russo has the moniker “Superstar.” Jason Ortiz picks up Eric Russo, kicks him in the gut and does a Front Face Lock. He picks him up and sets him there, allowing the blood to rush to “Superstar’s” head. After hanging Russo upside down for about ten seconds, Ortiz falls backward with a Hanging Vertical Suplex. Russo arches his back in pain and Ortiz buries a knee into Russo’s forehead. Ortiz gets to his feet and bounces off the ropes, and drives the point of his elbow into Russo’s forehead. The World Champion goes for the cover, hooking Russo’s leg... One... Two - kick out after two and one half. Jason Ortiz gets to his feet, picking up Eric Russo. He picks him up for a Body Slam setup, hikes him up to his shoulders and then executes a beautiful Death Valley Driver! He goes for the cover again... One... Two... Three! No! Eric Russo managed to get the shoulder up. The World Champion sends the challenger outside of the ring. When Russo got to his feet, he was greeted by a two hundred and eight-pound Corkscrew Pancha Suicida! Russo had almost been flattened by the larger champion and Jason Ortiz gets Russo to his feet and sends Russo into the steel ring steps. Russo bellows out in pain and the World Champion stalks his victim. “Superstar” manages to kick Ortiz in the jewels and pulled him headfirst into the steel stairs. Russo gets to his feet and rolls into the ring to break the count. He pulls off a portion of the safety mat to expose the deadly concrete floor. A smile plays his face and we all know what he is about to do. JD: Oh, no! Don’t do it! CS: Do it! Turn his ass into a vegetable! Eric Russo gets Jason Ortiz to his feet and sets his head in between Russo’s legs, standing right over the exposed concrete floor. Russo then wraps his arms around the champion’s waist and attempts to pick him up. Nothing happen. He tries again and nothing happened the second time. Russo attempts the third time but Ortiz counters with a back body drop onto the padded floor. Ortiz manages to roll into the ring and climb the corner closest to Russo. When Russo got to his feet, he is greeted by a Moon Sault by the World Champion, pressing him against the floor. Crack Head gets to his feet and he picks up “Superstar.” Ortiz throws Russo into the ring and Ortiz slowly follows suit. Russo gets the upper hand by Dropkicking Ortiz in the side of his head. Russo gets the champion to his feet and whips him into the ropes. After trying a Clothesline attempt, Ortiz countered with a Wrap Around DDT! He goes for the cover... One... Two - and Eric Russo kicked out after two. The champion gets to his feet and picks up Russo. Somehow Russo springs to life and picks up Jason Ortiz. The momentum of the lift shifted Ortiz’s weight forward towards Russo, forcing Russo to stagger backward. Russo staggers all the way to the ropes and dropped Ortiz throat first onto the top rope! Ortiz bounced off the top rope and landed on the mat, writhing in pain, holding his throat. “Superstar” goes for the cover... One... Two... Three! No! Jason Ortiz managed to get the shoulder up! Russo is getting frustrated. Perhaps he can’t beat Ortiz for the LOW World title, but he must! He has to! If he does, he’ll make the Shadow Riders the most feared and powerful stable in Sports Entertainment today! JD: Eric Russo thinks that he can’t do it! Look at the doubt in his eyes. CS: That’s not doubt...that’s determination! Eric Russo picks up Jason Ortiz and kicks him in the gut. He sets his head in between his legs and executes El Rehabilitator! (360 Front Flip Pile Driver. In Russo’s case it’s called “Flavor of the Week.” He’ll adopt his opponent’s finisher hence the name.) An egoistical smile appears on his face and does a nonchalant cover: One... Two... Three! No! Jason Ortiz managed to get the shoulder up! The fans are going crazy! Eric Russo gets Ortiz to his feet and kicks him in the gut. He sets him up in a Fireman’s Carry, does a spin and does a Samoan Neck Breaker. “Superstar” calls this finishing move “The Falling Star.” He goes for another cover... One... Two... Three! No! The World Champion will not be denied! Jason Ortiz managed to get the shoulder up before the count of three. Eric Russo is literally beside himself. He has done everything he can think of to put the champion away but he has come up short each and every time. Russo whips Ortiz into the ropes but Ortiz counters and whips Russo into the ropes. After coming off the ropes, he executes an Overhead Belly-to-Belly throw. It wasn’t exactly a suplex, but more like a throw from the World Champion. Crack Head runs over to Russo, picks him up, kicks him in the gut and does one of the fastest El Rehabilitator!’s the LOW as ever seen! Ortiz goes for the cover... One... Two... Three! No! Eric Russo kicked out! JD: Whoa! How did Eric Russo kick out of the El Rehabilitator!? CS: Because he’s “Superstar” Eric Russo, that’s why! Jason Ortiz is now becoming frustrated. He didn’t think that Eric Russo would kick out of his new finishing maneuver. The World Champion picks up Russo again and does another El Rehabilitator! He goes for another cover... One... Two...and Eric Russo kicked out again! The fans are beside themselves, booing not at Jason Ortiz, but at Eric Russo for kicking out of the second El Rehabilitator! maneuver. The champion looks over at one of the corner and was struck with an idea. He picks up Russo and sends him into a corner. Afterwards, Ortiz sandwiches Russo with a Clothesline into the corner. Ortiz sits on top of the top turnbuckle and picks up Russo. Ortiz does a Sunset Flip and turns it into a Super El Rehabilitator! Holy shit! Holy shit! Holy shit! Holy shit! Holy shit! JD: I have to agree with the fans there. Holy shit! CS: That bastard could have broken Eric Russo’s neck! JD: Do you see me shedding a tear? No! Satisfied that he has defeated Eric Russo with the Super Rehabilitator!, he goes for the cover: One... Two... Three! No! Eric Russo miraculously raised his shoulder off the canvas. JD: I...don’t...believe it... CS: Eric Russo will not be denied! You might as well say it now, Jerry: “Superstar” Eric Russo is the new LOW World Heavyweight Champion. Jason Ortiz can’t stop Eric Russo. After Eric Russo kicked out, Jason Ortiz’s demeanor changed. It changed from a determined champion to depression. He picks up Russo and gets him to his feet. Before Russo could strike him, Ortiz held up his hands to top him. The fans stare at Ortiz quizzically. Afterwards, Ortiz begins to talk with Russo. JD: What the hell? Jason Ortiz stopped Eric Russo, and why is he talking to him? CS: I’m just as baffled as you are, Jerry. After talking to Eric Russo for a few seconds, Jason Ortiz lays down for Russo so he can take the pin. The crowd immediately boos at Ortiz and Russo goes for the cover: One... Two... Three! It was over. “Devil Without a Cause (Superstar Mix)” by Kid Rock starts to play and Rusty Monarch announces the winner. RM: The winner of the match, and NEW LOW World Heavyweight Champion - “SUPERSTAR” ERIC RUSSO! Senior Referee Tom Bentley hands the title over to Eric Russo. Even Russo himself doesn’t understand why Jason Ortiz laid down for him. CS: What the fuck is the CRACK HEAD doing? The man just laid down for Eric Russo. What was that for? JD: I have no idea Casey, I mean, the guy didn't have any heart. Ortiz just laid down for the man he hates the most in the company. The question is... WHY... WHY! CS: Well no fucking kidding. I am just glad the CRACK HEAD lost his World Title. Now Eric Russo is the champion! Outcome: Victory over Jason Ortiz Show URL: http://
