(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. Slowly the logo fades out to black. For a moment, nothing. Then the sound of Russo’s voice speaks out as he continues his recount of the past.) Russo (VO): “I was soon to find out that Tristan Belmont was not the only enemy within my ranks. After seven long years of carrying him on my back, Jason Kage decided that he wanted out on his own.” (The scene flashes to the present day where Russo sits across the table from Jerry the interviewer. Jerry begins to question Russo with a shocked expression.) Jerry: “Not Kage!” (Shaking his head, Russo responds.) Russo: “Yes Jerry, I’m afraid that Jason Kage too was not grateful for all that I had given him. Jason Kage decided to throw seven long years of friendship into the trash. I don’t know if I’ll ever be able to forgive him for that.” (Russo pauses for a moment. He then leans back in his chair and lets out a sigh. Russo continues.) Russo: “We must however press forward. Forward into the future. Forward into my future.” (Russo leans forward once again as he looks Jerry in the eye. He continues.) Russo: “This Saturday night my story comes full circle. This Saturday the past that inspires so much hate in the public for me brings me face to face with the LOW World Champion. The thing that scares them even more is that I . . . I. A man that they all detest might just become the one who represents them.” (Russo’s anger begins to grow, fueling his speech.) Russo: “This Saturday night I get my one chance to finally prove without a shadow of a doubt that I am the best there is today. I get my chance to show once and for all that my comments are not ego. I get my chance to finally become the World Heavyweight Champion. I get my chance to finally show everyone that they just like the rest are nothing but another falling star.” (Jerry finally is able to get in a word edgewise.) Jerry: “Well I wish you the best with that.” (At this comment, Russo launches into another tirade.) Russo: “I don’t need well wishes! Save your wishes for one who believes in them! Save them for Tristan Belmont!” (Jerry meekly responds.) Jerry: “Sorry . . .” (Russo responds with a grumble followed by speech.) Russo: “Forget about it.
