Untitled roleplay

captured 21 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eric Russo vs. Jason Warrior and Jason Ortiz JD: Well fans, we have been informed that Jason Kage is seriously injured in the back and he will be unable to work the match tonight, which is a god damn shame. CS: So where does this leave the main-event? Is it cancelled or something? JD: No, by orders of Yuka, the match will go on as scheduled, with or without Jason Kage, so I guess this is a tough break for the Shadow Riders. CS: This is not fair whatsoever, Jerry, why are you not complaining like you are usually doing? JD: Because I happen to believe that the Shadow Riders, whoever it be in that group, deserve all the harm that is given to them, and I am quite happy at the prospect of seeing Jason Warrior and Jason Ortiz whoop the hell out of Eric Russo here tonight… CS: You are a sick sick man my friend. This isn’t fair though, who the hell did this to Kage anyways? In the ring now, Eric Russo is alone. The cage has settled around him, and he finds himself caged in a very hostile playground. In front of him is the World Champion, Jason Ortiz and his partner for the night, Jason Warrior. Both men grin widely as they bask in the fans’ reaction to this match becoming a handicap match for the tag team titles. Russo looks on and about as the bell rings, before finally, finding himself in the middle of the ring. Warrior steps up to him, and they lock up in a standard grapple in the middle of the ring, starting this match off. Russo gains the upper hand, throwing Jason Warrior over his head with an early arm drag takedown. Eric does not let go of the arm however, as he slowly begins his assault on Jason Warrior, applying great pressure on the elbow of the returning superstar. Warrior rises to his feet, still feeling the pain produced by a now standing arm wrench. The former extreme Champion pushes one half of the Tag Team Champions into the ropes, breaking the grapple. Eric Russo comes back and barely ducks a stiff looking lariat. Warrior turns around and he feels Eric Russo’s boots kiss his face with a beautifully executed standing dropkick. Outside of the ring, Brody Dahmer and Luke Justice clap their hands, encouraging their partner and friend. CS: Keep this up Eric; you can beat both of these idiots with your eyes closed! JD: I can’t wait to see Ortiz and Warrior pounding on that ass hole, for all the pain he has caused, he deserves to die in that cage tonight. CS: Will you calm the fuck down? Shit, I haven’t seen you this aggravated since that time your doctor told you the problem wasn’t your wife, just that your boys couldn’t swim. Sheesh! JD: -silence- Back inside the ring, Russo stands up and basks in the hatred thrown at him. Fans around ringside begin to shout obscenities at one half of the Tag Team Champions, which does nothing but accentuate the smile on Russo’s beaming face. Slowly, he turns around and looks back at Jason Warrior, who is beginning to get back up to his feet, in a small daze. Russo silently creeps up behind him and awaits for him to get back up to vertical basis. As soon as Warrior is back, Russo runs towards him and plants him face first with a running bulldog. As soon as they have hit the mat, Russo turns his opponent around and attempts to pin him. The referee’s hand taps against the mat once, but before it can tap a second time, Warrior’s shoulder is up. Russo gets to his feet and immediately, begins laying down punches, rights and lefts, to Jason Warrior, who with immense difficulty is able to get back up to his feet. Russo comes running at him, but his eagerness costs him, as Warrior side steps and sends him face first into the bamboo cage behind. Dazed, Eric Russo stumbles backwards into a release German suplex, courtesy of the Warrior. JD: Yes! More! Without asking his partner, Ortiz slaps Warrior in the back and tags himself in, very eager to have apiece himself. As soon as he gets in the ring however, his own partner meets him. Both men stare down, as the fans chant for them to go towards Russo and not each other. Both parties exchange what seem to be heated words. JD: No no no, focus on Russo! Focus on Russo!!! CS: Ha, these two won’t get along. Both of them have ego’s the size of the Sun. You have the attention craving whore that is Jason Ortiz on one side and the jealous attention seeking Jason Warrior. At least the Shadow Riders work cohesively. Warrior pushes by Jason Ortiz and steps out of the ring, creating a little rise of jeers towards him. Jason Ortiz turns around to meet Eric Russo, but before he can even lay a finger on him, Russo lifts his bicep into the groin area of the World Champion. CS: HA! This is why he is one half of the greatest tag team of all times! Russo gets back up to his feet to see the referee checking up on Ortiz, who is heard complaining about his pain. Immediately, the official confronts Russo, asking him about such an attack, but the Shadow Rider brushes him off, pushing him to the side before going back to Jason Ortiz. With both men back up, The Superstar throws Ortiz into the opposite corner of his ally for the night, with an Irish whip. Climbing the turnbuckles himself, Russo begins to lay in blows onto Ortiz as Luke Justice and Brody Dahmer look on from close by with smiles on their faces. The shots go up to 6 when finally, Ortiz pushes himself out of the corner and drops Eric Russo’s groin onto his knee in the middle of the ring. Russo hops up and down, holding his groin, before he is brought down to the mat by a Rock Bottom-like manoeuvre by the World Heavyweight Champion. CS: Is that referee stupid?! Did he not see the blatant groin shot there? JD: I did not hear you complaining when Russo did it. In fact, I think you said something along the lines of “HA!” CS: -sigh- You are so immature. Ortiz has no intention of ending the match there however, as he picks up his opponent and throws Russo’s arm around his own neck. Within instants, and with a nice hefty hold of the tights, Jason Ortiz has Eric Russo in a upside down candle like position. Ortiz walks a little in the ring, before falling backwards, connecting with a picture perfect suplex. The World Champion steps up and tags in his partner, Jason Warrior, without hostility!!! JD: Yes, they are working together. CS: Calm down there, it was just a tag. Stinkin’ cracky’s probably out of breath. Outside of the ring, Adam Stryker who had come out earlier on in cheers for the opposition to the Shadow Riders. In comes Jason Warrior, but out does not go Ortiz. Instead, both men work cohesively as a team and pick up Eric Russo, sending him into the ropes. As the Superstar returns, the challengers push him up, before forcing him down with a double pancake face buster. Russo’s stomach bounces first onto the mat, before he finds himself at his side, wincing in pain. JD: Great double teaming by the challengers. CS: Can we get some fucking control in there please? Referee, do you have no job? Soon after the double teaming, Ortiz is out of the ring and Warrior is on Eric Russo, grabbing hold of his arm, and locking in an arm bar, just like had been done previously in the match. With Warrior on his knees, Russo on his side has no choice but to try and wiggle free, an attempt that fails, as Jason Warrior runs one of his elbows into the sides of Eric Russo. Let’s Go Warrior … –clap- –clap- –clap- –clap- –clap-… Let’s Go Warrior A chant breaks out in the arena as Jason Warrior has broken his hold and is picking up the Champion by the hair now. Warrior tucks Russo’s head beneath his arm, before driving the champion’s head into the mat with a DDT that shakes the ring. On the outside, Luke is whispering something into Brody’s ear. With an intense look on her eyes, she simply nods her head and hands the Realm title to Luke Justice who grins and walks around the ring, towards Adam Stryker. JD: What the hell? CS: What? It was just a DDT, don’t go claiming it was a super natural move that only Warrior could get done, you bias fuck! JD: No no, on the outside! Look at the outside! Stryker, who until now had done nothing wrong is ran into by Luke Justice who flattens him with a running Realm Title shot to the side of the head. The former Champion collapses to the floor, while Brody, begins to climb, INTO the cage! JD: What the?! CS: Ha! Stryker’s down! Ha ha ha! JD: Look!! CS: HA! Brody steps over the edge of the cage and begins her decent inside of it. The referee meets her as both of her feet hit the ring. A referee that tries to look intimidating but fails terribly when Brody simply knees him in the groin. Jason Warrior runs towards her, attempting a clothesline which she ducks. He turns around to feel Brody’s boot to the jaw with a spinning back kick! And then a voice beckons… Luke Justice: This match is no longer a Two on One you dick Guzzlers, say hello to Russo’s partner for the night, Brody Dahmer! A loud echo of jeers thunders in the arena as Justice and Dahmer grin. Already, objects are being thrown into the ring, at the Riders’ direction, without relent. Most objects do not get through the cage however, so it is nothing to affect them. JD: WHAT?! This match is not supposed to be Ortiz and Warrior versus Russo and Dahmer, it is scheduled to be Ortiz and Warrior versus the Shadow Riders! CS: Yeah, and what is Brody? I think Justice caught onto that little detail and he used it to the Riders’ advantage! Looks like the match is fair now! JD: I hate them …I loathe them. Not one to be impressed with such petty events, Ortiz finds himself in the ring, delivering a spine buster to the new wrestler in the ring. He grins as the fans let out a massive pop before slowly walking out of the ring and into his corner. Back to action, Russo is now back up to his feet, and so is his opponent, Jason Warrior. Both of them begin laying blows into the other’s face. Russo scores with rights while Warrior connects with lefts, all of the shots getting maximum impact in. The Shadow Rider uses his brawling advantage though, dropping to his knees and once again, laying a hard bicep shot to the groin. Warrior falls to his knees, just in time to feel the shot of pain brought forth by a running dropkick to the face, courtesy of mister Superstar, Eric Russo. CS: That was a nice move, wasn’t it? JD: No, not really. It was just a dropkick! –pouts- Without losing any time, Russo slides on top of Jason Warrior, but once again, the challenger to the gold kicks out after a mere one count. This does not stop him however, as he soon gets back up to his feet and picks Warrior up with him, sending him crashing into the corner via an Irish Whip. Russo runs into the corner, wanting to create more pain on the opponent, but all he gets for his efforts is a boot to the face, followed by a Sleeper Hold Drop. Russo’s head collides against the mat with grave intensity, which pleases the crowd to extreme amounts. One half of the challengers walks towards his partner and tags him in. The World Champion and the former Aftermath Champion walk towards a downed Russo when suddenly, in comes Russo’s partner, Brody Dahmer in the ring. She charges at both men, who just sidestep her. Into the ropes she goes, and back towards both men. With ease, they grab her around the neck and slam her into the canvas with a double Choke Slam! JD: Nice! What a tandem move by these two. CS: Yes, hit the girl! Really manly you crack heads. Ortiz turns around and he is flattened to the mat by a running neck breaker by Eric Russo. Jason Warrior, unaware of this, slowly walks to his own corner while the opponent, one half of the Tag Team Champions stomps on Jason Ortiz. Now that they are alone in the ring, Russo mounts Ortiz and begins raining down on him with rights and lefts. The Champion blocks as many shots as he can before rolling to his side, getting Russo off of him. The Shadow Rider runs into a shoulder block, but he is back up to his feet. As he runs again, Ortiz ducks an attempted clothesline, kicks Russo in the gut before driving him into the mat with a modified DDT. The Double Click! The fans go absolutely livid as Russo is pinned by the World Champion. Before the referee can even count to one however, Brody is in the ring, and has broken the pinning predicament. JD: They had this! Damn it! Referee, get that lunatic out of the ring before she breaks another rule! CS: Would you just shut up? Ortiz smiles as he gets back up to his feet and walks towards Brody. The smaller frame of the woman is not intimidated however, as she runs into Ortiz, who grabs her by the shoulders and simply throws her back into her corner. The fans, yet again, go absolutely ape-shit for The World Champion, who just looks at them, and signals for more and more cheers. As Russo slowly is getting back up to his feet, Brody runs passed the referee and towards Jason Ortiz. Ortiz is about to clobber him into oblivion when she slides between his legs. Stunned, the champion turns around, only to be greeted by a jumping double knee strike to the solar plexus. Ortiz stumbles backwards into the on waiting arms of the Superstar, who throws him over his head, sending him on a crash course with the mat. Release Over Head German Suplex Before the referee can ask one to get out of the ring, Russo tags in Dahmer, who already in the ring, drops an elbow onto the prone body of the World Champion. The Champ gets back to his feet and pushes an attacking Brody to the ground. The newest Shadow Rider is quick to her feet, landing a jumping thrust kick to the stomach of Jason Ortiz, before smashing down a double axe handle behind his neck. Only one problem though … he does not fall. Instead, he looks up and smiles. Brody runs towards him, he side steps her, and when she comes back, he picks her up and runs her face into the Bamboo Cage.. JD: Brody is not ready for a man like Jason Ortiz! Beat some sense into that nut job. CS: She has just been in a match you moron! What do you expect? Ortiz picks the fallen Dahmer up and throws her into the corner, where he follows and hits an shoulder to her stomach. The woman forms around the massive shoulders of the World Champion, long enough for him to pick her up, dropping her down with a modified body slam. As soon as this is done, Jason Warrior soars in the air and connects with a top rope leg drop. Does he pin you ask? Oh but he does. One… the referee’s hand slaps. Two… And again, another slap. The fans are roaring, could there be new tag team champions? Three!!! Just as the hand was about to make final contact, comes another soar, this time, with a higher impact level, by another member of the match. His name? Superstar Eric Russo, his move? A Shooting Star Press. A move that has become a well-known finisher for the Rider. Superstar Press A loud chorus of jeers explodes in the arena as Warrior rolls off a battered Dahmer, gasping for air. JD: We almost had new champions! CS: Key words here are “almost” and “had”. See, none of those things happened, so they do not matter! And here, it gets so much more interesting In comes the World Champion. The referee shrugs, fuck it, he has lost control. He runs towards Russo and the two begin to brawl in the middle of the ring. Meanwhile, something comes into the ring, thanks to Luke Justice. Yeah, the Realm title that Brody had given him previously. The belt is caught by Dahmer who clobbers a groggy Jason Warrior in the face with it. JD: NO!!! Warrior stumbles backwards into the ropes, and very dazed, returns towards Brody, who flattens him with another title shot to the face! CS: YES!!! Dahmer collapses on the fallen Warrior. The referee begins to slap the mat, which catches Ortiz’s attention. The World Heavyweight Champion pushes Russo into the corner and runs towards the count, that is already up to two. Three! No?! Yes? Just as Ortiz was about to break the count, he was tackled in the back by Russo, who flew his body and Ortiz’s inches close to the pinning predicament that has just given the Shadow Riders the victory. It took two title shots to the face and a Superstar Press, but it happened. JD: This cannot be happening, can it? CS: YES!!! HAA HA HA! THE SHADOW RIDERS HAVE RETAINED!!!! JD: NO!!! NO!!!!!!!!!!! THEY CHEATED! BY GOD, THEY CHEATED! Amidst a shower of jeers, insults, and flying objects colliding against the cage, “Devil Without a Cause (Superstar Remix)” by Kid Rock plays out from the Public Announce System, accompanying the roaring voice of Rusty Monarch. “The Winners of this Match and … STILLLLLLLLL Tag Team Champions, Eric Russo and Brody Dahmer, the SHAAAAAAAAAAAAAAADOW RIDDDDDDDEEEEEEEEEEEEEEERS” Still or new? It does not matter. The cage soon lifts as Ortiz retrieves his World Heavyweight Championship belt at the same moment that Justice slides in the ring with the tag straps. He hands them to the Champions who pose in the middle of the ring. …What a wonderful way to end a returning night, no? Who ever said it was over???
