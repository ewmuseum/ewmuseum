Revelations - Part One

captured 5 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“There are days when you think things could be better but in all reality they will only become worst. When you lost a loved one, like I did, you look hard and long wishing that you could have the days you once had. A picnic in the park by your home or just lying in the bed and staring at the girl you love more than life itself. Yet, when it is stolen from you, it’s almost as if your heart is ripped out. You look over to your side hoping that she will be laying there. Then, as you look over your heart shatters as you realize she is no longer there. That her life was taken from you before you even knew it and that you could do nothing about it. If you had told me that someone so unique would have this type of impact on my life I would have thought you were crazy. Now I realize I was crazy in love with Kristen,” says a deep, dark mysterious voice as the scene opens. The scene opens up showing a male sitting on a long black couch inside of an office which is inside of a tall tower in the middle of Los Angeles, California. The man is wearing a pair of baby blue silk basketball shorts and a baby blue sleeveless mesh shirt. His black elbows are placed upon his legs as he sits on the couch hunched over. He looks across from him where a young white female is sitting with her legs crossed. She is wearing a knee-length red skirt and a black blouse to match with it. Her long, straight, blonde hair is tied up in a ponytail that reaches down to the middle of her back. She also has on a pair of petite and cute glasses that make her look more sophisticated. The woman can be no older than thirty years old while the man sitting across from her is just a little younger than her at the age of twenty-six. The man is Ty Baron, a gang leader and a wrestler in a federation called Lights Out Wrestling, one of the top federations in the nation. Born here in the city of Los Angeles and being raised on the rough streets of Compton made Baron who he is today, a man with strong ideals and a unique philosophy that could one day get him killed. His gang he ran is one of the top gangs in the Los Angeles area yet was one of the smaller ones. A popular belief had been that the larger, the better. However Baron had been able to prove this notion untrue and had built his gang up differently from others. However his personal life had taken a major hit with the death of his longtime girlfriend, Kristen. She had been kidnapped and murdered by a rival gang in the area a little over six weeks ago and since then he has been looking to figure out which gang it is. However in the mean time, he has been going to see a psychiatrist to help him cope with this stress and pain that he feels on a daily basis now. “Ty, when you think of Kristen, what do you remember?” asks the attractive psychiatrist. “I remember everything about her. Her glowing smile, her silky hair, her radiant eyes, and her wonderful personality. Her looks were equal with her personality and just made her so much more attractive in my eyes,” he reveals to the young psychiatrist. The woman just simply nods her head while listening to what Baron has to say, and makes notes as he speaks. “Do you feel that your anger level has risen since her death?” she asks him. Baron takes a minute to think about this, reflecting upon his fellow gang member he just murdered a few days ago, before responding to her question, “I do not think so. I think I have just shown more of an edge as of late.” “You killed someone Ty just a few days ago, something that you are not known for which is a surprise in this area. Before the death of Kristen you had only killed one other man and since then you have killed four. What is going on in your head?” she asks him. “I am just having more problems within the gang then I have ever had for some reason. I am unsure of what is going on but I plan on getting to the bottom of it soon enough. Someone is plotting against with me in my group to have me removed permanently. I do not take threats on my life easily Diana,” he tells her as his deep black eyes meet her luscious blue eyes. She just simply nods her head as she writes some more notes. Her eyes then move over to a clock near her which reads 3:00 PM. “It is time for me to go Diana,” he tells her. He stands up and the two shake hands before she adds in one last thing for him to think about “Think about this Ty. What would you do if Kristen ended up not being dead? Have a good day.” Ty then exits the room and the building out onto the busy streets of Los Angeles. As he walks down the street towards his home, he thinks about what she just told him, running his mind through each possible scenario. The question piques his interest like nothing has ever done before this mind. Before Kristen had been murdered Baron had planned on proposing to her. Actually, the day she had been murdered was the day he was planning on proposing to her. He knew that he wanted to spend the rest of his life with her, no questions asked. Sure, he got a lot of grief from the boys about her being white but they knew that deep down they loved each other. Even though she was never fond of him doing this she understood why he had to. She understood the life he had and what this meant to him. She had respect in the gang while most gangs in Los Angeles could never even respect a girl. Hell, she even helped some of the guys out get girls into their lives. She was a smart girl, someone who had helped Baron wizen up over their two years of dating. As he continues to walk down the street thinking about Kristen he is unaware of the people who are slowly moving closer to him from all sides. Large black men in different thug appeal begin to converge on which he does not realize for a few minutes. Finally, he stops and looks around to see what is going on and curses at himself for not being more observant. He looks at the different black men and recognizes who they are with. They are members of a large group in town called the Dragons, signified by the dragons they wear on them, whether it be on their hats, shirts, pants, or tattoos on their bodies. Baron looks at each one and counts seven of them to be exact. He knows he is still another mile at least from his home and won’t be able to outrace them even though he is fast. “Well, well, well,” begins one of the guys in a southern accent. “Looks like our main man was correct after all seeing as how we got the big boss man here all by himself. Mr. Ty Baron, big hot shot and leader of the Wolves. If you ask me, the Wolves are a bunch pussies deep down inside. Trying to follow that no violence code, who the hell are you, Martin Fucking Luther King? That shit do not work around here. This is a city of shit just like you and the Dragons take out pieces of shit like you,” he tells Baron. Baron just stares at him trying to figure out the best way to handle this. He relaxes his body as he looks around at them. He knows if he goes to fight them that this could turn out ugly. “What, you the silent kind too?” the same guy asks him walking up to him. Baron looks at the man in his black eyes and hardens his glare, causing the man to step back. Baron takes a deep breath in and closes his eyes, almost daring them to hit him. However the men are either are smarter than that or intimidated by Baron as they end up not even moving a muscle. They wait for a few moments trying to figure out what he is going to do before trying to get him worked up. “Looks like your world is falling apart over there Ty. You got your own gang members trying to overthrow and kill your ass. You got gangs tryin’ to move in on your turf and you got a gang who has killed your white bitch. Seems like things aren’t going your way?” one of the other guys said. Baron then opens his eyes and looks at the man who just said that, “What did you say?” he asks him in a cold, hard tone. The man who said the words gulps hard showing his fear of Baron. They know he is not someone to mess with which is why they brought multiple guys. However, they are still worried by what he can do. “Don’t speak what you don’t know, boy. Because if you try to speak game you ain’t experienced in, you may find yourself floating in some creek somewhere wit the rest of your boys here, do you understand that?” he tells them in the same cold tone he has had for the last few minutes. The men look at Baron knowing that they have gotten him riled up like they wanted to. “Well then Baron, how about you prove yourself and show us you are still the man around here then,” another one of the guys says as they pull out various weapons. Baron rolls his shoulders and says, “Well then, bring it on.” Then the scene fades out.
