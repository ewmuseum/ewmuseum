History 101- Welcome to the LOW

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. Slowly the logo fades out to black. For a moment, nothing. Then the sound of Russo’s voice speaks out as he continues his recount of the past.) Russo (VO): “When we made our arrival in he LOW, we once again did it by crossing paths with an old friend.” (As Russo’s voice over stops, the screen reopens to a scene from that debut. Jesse Jamester walks up the ramp way, a crowbar in hand. He pauses every few feet and poses for the crowd. The more the fans cheer, the more Jamester gives them. As he is nearing the entrance, things quiet down... so Jamester decides to turn them up. He grabs onto the base of the LOW Tron--and begins to climb! A voice over by Russo starts up as the scene continues.) Russo (VO): “Why Jamester has the natural knack of getting himself into more trouble then he is already in, I’ll never know. However, as the Snake had taught me, one persons mistakes are noting but your opportunities for success.” (The scene continues. After several seconds of climbing and encouragements from the crowd, Jamester reaches the top.) Russo (VO): “Seriously, couldn’t he feel it coming!?” (As the scene continues, Jamester paces back and forth atop the LOW Tron. He is over twenty feet in the air. The fans go nuts, because although they don't all like him, they admire his talent and in this case his charisma. The camera pans around the arena, preparing to leave on a high note for the first edition of Saturday Night Realm. Russo’s voice picks up once again.) Russo (VO): “We were never known to let anything end on a high note when he Shadow Riders were concerned.” (The scene continues as Jamester celebrates on the Tron. The fans' roar louder and louder, voicing their appreciation for the amount of blood that Jamester has drawn from his opponents. The blood is rich on his own face too however. The battle he has just taken part in was a far cry from light on any of the competitors. Jamester continues waving, saturating himself with the fans' cheers, allowing his ego to grow. Then suddenly, a masked figure appears behind Jamester up on top of the LOW Tron! The mask he wears is not just any mask either, it’s just like Jamester's trademark hardcore mask! The fans are hushed in anticipation, waiting to see what will come of this masked man! Jamester turns around just in time to catch a thundering superkick to his groin! Jamester doubles over in pain, as a maneuver of this type would cripple even the greatest of warriors! The masked man then grabs Jamester and sets him up for a Powerbomb! He pauses, taunting the crowd before he hoists Jamester up the powerbomb position. Instead of holding on, he just drops Jamester off the edge of the screen, sending him to an unforgiving steel stage! Jamester hits the stage with a loud thud, causing the arena to explode at the extremeness of this insane bump! Russo’s voice starts up again.) Russo (VO): “Once again, I had stolen the entire show upon my debut. How anyone can argue with my track record, I’ll never know.” (The scene continues with a shot of Jamester lying motionless on the ramp. But wait, there's another masked man! The second masked man comes through the curtains. He is also wearing the same Jamester mask as the other. The only difference however, is that this masked man is wielding a sledgehammer! He walks over to Jamester's nearly unconscious body, and raises the sledgehammer high above his head! With one swift motion, the second masked man sends the sledgehammer crashing into Jamester chest! This shot sends Jamester writhing with pain! As he squirms on the entrance, the man raises the sledgehammer again, this time bashing in Jamester's ribs with it! He repeats this action over and over again, until the first masked man has climbed all the way down the LOW Tron. Together, the two of them drag Jamester back down to the ring. (Russo’s voice starts up again.) Russo (VO): “The fans were about to see exactly why we were given so much hype. I was about to set the tone that the LOW would survive on for the next year.” (The scene continues as the two masked men throw Jamester into the ring. They follow the body of their lifeless victim, as they step through the ropes into the ring as well. The two men pull Jamester up to his feet and drag him over to the far side of the ring. Once there, one of the men holds the top and second rope apart from one another. The other man proceeds to slide Jamester through the ropes while still holding him up. The masked man tangle the ropes around Jamester's neck, then leave go of him, making a noose effect as Jamester hangs there helplessly. As he struggles for freedom, the two men exit the ring, where the first man, who threw Jamester off the screen, picks up the sledgehammer. He looks to Jamester and takes one more shot at Jamester's ribs for good measure, possibly breaking the entire rib cage of Jesse as he does so! The man now winds back for a second shot but is stopped by the second masked man, who whispers something in his ear. In a change of plans, the first man throws the sledgehammer into the ring. Both masked men follow it in. Once inside, they pick the sledgehammer up and together they get a good grasp of it. They run from one side of the ring with it and slam it into the back of Jamester's head! Jamester immediately goes limp and hangs there in the ropes like dead weight! He remains motionless as the two masked men nod at each other, and look on in pride at what they've done as Jamester begins to bleed from the mouth and turn blue. Russo’s voice picks up once again as the scene fades out.) Russo (VO): “From that debut, the Shadow Rider name went on to develop an almost cult following. There was a certain mystique that surrounded the group the likes of which had never been seen before. It was at that point that Kage and I made the first mistake in the history of the Shadow Riders. Putting too much faith in the name of the group to carry new talent, the Shadow Riders decided to increase their membership numbers.” (The scene reopens to another shot from the past. Russo and Kage are sitting on a park bench in front of a camera. Behind the duo, a third man stands in the shadows, shielding his identity. Russo begins speaking on screen.) Russo: “I would like you all to meet the newest addition to the Shadow Riders. Now I know that most of you don’t yet know this man, but I assure you that you soon will. The boys in the back like to talk about paying dues. Well this man has paid more dues then Jamester has male hookers. Over the years, we have tormented this man much like we do all of you now. This man however possessed one quality that all of you do not. This man had heart. Through all of the pain that we caused him, he never once backed down. We may have knocked him down in the end but he at least put up a fight. He never ran away, he never tried to get himself suspended to avoid us. That is why he is now in the LOW, that is why he is now one of the elite, that is why he is now a Shadow Rider. So without any further holdup, we here at Shadow Riders Inc. would like to present to you the “Showstopper” John Starr” (The man steps out of the shadows revealing his youthful frame. He begins speaking.) Starr: “Well, one thing is for sure, no one could have ever expected there would be a third Shadow Rider. A third man in an already menacing group of just two, could turn LOW upside down. But let me lay everyone's mind at ease, I'm not here to hurt the LOW. I'm here to hurt one man...” (The scene fades out as Russo’s voice replaces the normal dialogue.) Russo (VO): “Let me take the chance to inform anyone who happens to hear this story that it was not my idea to include John Starr amongst our ranks. If fact, I was opposed to the arrangement from the first day that Kage brought it up. However, I am one who listens to his followers when they ask. So, with great regret I allowed this man to enter our select group. Then much like all of Kage’s other ideas, the new member of the Shadow Riders flopped. It was now my turn to take over.” (The scene reopens to a shot from the past. We are located in the deep deserts of South Texas. John Starr races towards the other two Shadow Riders. Frantically waving his hands back and forth above his head in a panicked manner. Starr finally makes his way all the way back in and is now face to face with the other two Shadow Riders.) Starr: “Guys, it’s me! How could you mistake me for an animal? Oh well, I guess after how you forgave me, I can’t hold anything against you. Even?” (Rather than dropping the gun to his side, Russo takes another shot at Starr. To his disappointment, the shotgun is out of bullets. Russo looks disappointed as he begins to speak.) Russo: “Ah, screw it.” (With that, Russo throws the shotgun down and reaches into his waistband from which he pulls a handgun. He waves it in Starr’s face as the Showstopper makes one final plea.) Starr: “Hey, come on guys, all you need is love...or was it LSD?” (Russo looks baffled and shakes his head before unloading the chamber of the gun into Starr’s head. With six loud bangs, John Starr’s faceless corpse falls to the desert sand. Russo tucks the gun back into his waistband nonchalantly and pulls a handkerchief out of his pocket. He wipes the blood off of his face and passes the handkerchief to Kage, and he also cleans his face of blood. He then throws the cloth onto what was once Starr’s face, so they won’t be forced to look at the gruesome sight. The scene fades out as Russo’s voice picks up once again.) Russo (VO): “That day I made a silent vow to myself. Never again would I let anyone threaten the group that I had built.” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
