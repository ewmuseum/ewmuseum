History 101- The Saga Begins

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. Instead of opening to scene as normal, the screen stays black. Suddenly, we hear Russo’s voice start speaking in a voice over as he begins his story.) Russo (VO): “My true education in the sport started in 1987. I turned 18 that year, but it was a different time in those days. At that age I was considered a man in my sport and I was expected to act as one. I however was not ready for what was ahead of me. There was still much I had to learn to truly succeed.” (The screen fills with wavy lines as though entering a flashback. The scene opens to a shot of a dirty school gym. There is a ring set up in the gym which a couple of hundred patrons watch intently. The reason they watch the ring is due to the fact that there is a wrestling match taking place inside of it. We slowly begin to realize that one of the two wrestlers in the ring is a much younger Eric Russo. As we notice this, Russo’s present voice picks up as continues to narrate the story of his life.) Russo (VO): “During all my extensive training thought the world, I still had not learned how to function to my full potential.” (In the wrestling match taking place on the screen, Russo bounces off the ring ropes only to quickly be taken down with a hard powerslam. Before he can even figure out what is going on, the referee drops to the mat and quickly makes the three count. As this happens, Russo’s voice picks up once again continuing with his story.) Russo (VO): “At that point in time, I would have been happy with a simple win.” (Russo rolls out of the ring and onto the cold hard wood of the gymnasium floor. We can see the look of anguish that fills his face. Russo’s voice over continues.) Russo (VO): “I could not bear the thoughts of another loss on my record. I was better then that and I was dead set on proving it.” (The scene switches to one that we assume is in the locker rooms of the gym. The lights in the dilapidated room flicker on and off casting deep sections of shadow in the corners of the room. In the locker room, Russo sits alone. His head hanging low, resting in his hands. A man approaches Russo who looks up with a sullen expression. The man then sits down beside him.) Russo (VO): “I had to learn how to carry myself and pull my own weight if I was going to go anywhere other then further down the card. The only problem was that up to that point, I still had not learned that you have to take what you want or simply not get it. I was a pushover, I had no backbone . . . especially when it came to promoters.” (From this comment, we guess that the man siting next to Russo on the bench is the promoter of the federation. He is quite portly in his cheap off the rack suit from K-mart. The promoter puffs heavily on a cigar hanging from his mouth. For the first time, a voice comes from within the scene on the screen as the promoter turns to Russo and begins speaking.) Promoter: “That was a horrible showing out there tonight Eric. I’m sorry but I really can’t see paying you for a performance like that. I don’t even know why I keep you around on the roster sometimes.” (We now hear the voice of the younger Russo on our screen as he works up the nerve to question the promoter.) Russo: “You haven’t paid me for one of my performances yet . . . not one of them. How am I supposed to support myself when you refuse to pay me? You said I would get paid this time around no matter what.” (Infuriated, the promoter pops to his feet and glares down at Russo with a sinister scowl. We can tell from his demeanor that he does not appreciate having his decisions challenged. Seeing the emotions that his words have stirred up, Russo shrinks back as the promoter begins yelling at him.) Promoter: “How dare you even question me! I don’t give a damn what I told you earlier, I just told you now that you’re not getting paid! That’s the way it is, if you don’t like it you can feel free to get out of my locker room!” (Finally fed up with the promoter, Russo reaches his breaking point. He pops to his feet, snatches up his bags, and starts towards the door. As Russo does this, he calls back to the promoter over his shoulder.) Russo: “You know what, you can take this job and shove it!” (As Russo storms out of the locker room, we notice that a figure in the shadows has been intently watching the situation as it transpired. The figure now slithers back into the darkness of the shadows once again concealing himself from sight. At this point, Russo’s voice over picks up again as the scene fades out.) Russo (VO): “It was at that point that I went on a mission of self destruction. I realize now that may not have been the best idea but at least I was enjoying myself. I had found my release through my actions, a release that I so greatly needed.” (The scene reopens to a shot outside of a small hole in the wall bar in Las Vegas, Nevada. Russo staggers out of the bar with his arm around a scantily clad woman. From the looks of her, we can easily tell that she is a prostitute. In his other hand, Russo holds tightly onto the neck of a Tequila bottle. Not far behind, the prostitute’s pimp follows the couple unbeknownst to Russo. Once again, we notice a figure lurking deep in the shadows. The voice over picks up again.) Russo (VO): “Unfortunately, this release had managed to dig myself into a hole deeper then I ever expected. One that I thought I would never escape. Little did I know that my world was about to be flipped upside down by a man who’s name I had followed ever since I had broken into the business.” (Russo and the prostitute duck into a dark alley, where a Russo who can barely stand up props himself against a wall. As this happens, the prostitute’s pimp pulls a small switchblade out of his coat pocket. Quietly he slinks into the alley doing his best not to draw Russo’s attention. Before he has a chance to notice the presence of the pimp, Russo feels the blade of the knife being pressed up against his throat. In almost a whisper, the pimp speaks into Russo’s ear.) Pimp: “Give me your money now or I’m gonna slice you open from ear to ear. Do you understand me?” (Suddenly, the mysterious figure from the shadows appears at the head of the alley. The pimp quickly turns to see who is interrupting his little conversation. As he turns, we get our first glimpse of the man known as “The Snake”. Snake quickly grabs the pimp’s arm and yanks it away from Russo freeing his neck from the knife in the process. Snake then wraps his hand around the throat of the pimp as he begins to choke the life out of him. At this point, the prostitute lets out a scream. Snake quickly takes care of this situation as well though. With his free hand, Snake reaches out and grabs the prostitute around her face efficiently muffling any sounds that would be escaping her mouth. He then shoves both of his hands forward with all of his might, slamming the heads of Russo’s two attackers against a brick wall. The pimp and his prostitute slide down the wall knocked out cold rom the blow as Snake releases his grip. Russo too slumps to the ground, drunk and practically passed out. This position however is short lived as Snake pulls Russo back to his feet once again. He looks into the glazed over eyes of the younger man before snapping him out of his drunken daze with a hard slap to the face. In a gruff tone, Snake begins to speak to Russo.) Snake: “So, do you still wanna keep going with this self pity bullshit? Or are you ready to get yourself on track and become what you should be . . . a “Superstar”? (Still slightly unsteady from the alcohol, Russo mutters his response under his breath.) Russo: “I’m ready . . .” (Snake lets go of Russo who slumps back down to the ground. Without another word, he then walks off disappearing back into the shadows from which he came leaving Russo to sober up on his own. With that, the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
