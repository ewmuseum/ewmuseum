Ya mean

captured 10 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera is fixed on an expensive looking black Discman in someone’s hand. The sound of cars’ rubber tires can be heard rolling over the hot streets, it seem to stick out from the background. The fabric of the person’s pants can also be heard rubbing against each side. The bass beat of a rap song ripples the blood in one’s veins and arteries. Also, loud, Hispanic yelling can be vaguely heard from off in the distance. The sun, the hot, hot sun just seems to set the smell of the ninety five degree weather. Faint smell of ocean and the sights and sounds of seagulls are very common here, as the camera pulls back to focus on the scenery. Graffiti, always graffiti of gang symbols and rap lyrics. The seagull shit fits in quite well with the gangland graffiti. Little children play at the playground just off to the right. Heat waves arise off of the sidewalk as a dealer gains another customer. A closed Taco Bell is a sorry sight to see here. The camera rises high above the streets and focuses on the walker with the Discman. So many streets, people and buildings, stores and outlets, old run-down cars and gang bangers can now be scene. The streets of Venice Beach. This is California. A hot day in California, rather. The sweat just seems to evaporate as it reaches the outer layer of skin. The walker stops and shakes hands with a black man, TOG (Thug O’Gangster), a well-known drug dealer and pimp. [TOG is also a rapper, but only known to the gangs around the area of Venice Beach. TOG resembles Afroman without an afro but wears more ice than a winter wonderland. He’s the epitome of a true baller.] TOG hands our walker a bag of marijuana in exchange for some currency. The deal’s chillin’. A pimp walks up to TOG and our walker. This pimp’s name is Pimpen Mac, a very, very well known pimp. [He is the head pimp. Everyone says that Mac could easily pass as a look-a-like of 50 Cent. And it’s true. He wears so much ice you’d think he’s made of platinum. His grill (teeth) are made of platinum and diamonds. He used to go by the name Cap’n Pimp, but when that Snoop Dogg show came out, he had it changed. Hoes and foes seem to like the name Pimpen Mac better. Mac also sells weed. He gets his ounces and pounds off of TOG, TOG gets his shipments from Westwood and the team of Grande & King. Some of the well knowns are consolidating these days, but it’s not uncommon to see disputes that lead to weapon or weaponless brawls and shootouts.] [Another well known of the Venice Beach drug world, whom has already been mentioned, is Clint Westwood. Westwood is everything in Venice Beach. Westwood is the supplier of drugs, period. If you’re inside Venice and you buy an illegal substance, it came from him. He brings it in and sells it out to people like Thug O’Gangster and the team of Grande & King. Westwood is half Jamaican and half Puerto Rican, but he almost looks like an Arabic Mel Gibson, just his skin is darker. Most people around here look like someone famous. Grande & King will be mentioned another time.] “Where’s Jon at, “ Mac asks our walker. Our walker is none other than Jonathan Benicio Masciandaro (a.k.a. Soma). Soma is in Venice Beach for a two week vacation, to get away from the utter maddening Democratic ran streets of Philadelphia. “I’m Jon,” says Soma. “The other Jon.” “Oh… I think he’s off buying crack or something again, ya mean.” “He’s one crazy-ass fool,” TOG adds. Soma gazes up at the sun through his shades, “It’s two. I gotta get goin’. Clear sailin’, motherfucker.” “Adios,” the other two men add. What a day Soma had picked to wear a black T-shirt. What a great fucking day. It is so hot. Soma passes by some young children playing in the water coming from a fire hydrant. He smiles to himself. One day those children are going to become dealers. It’s inevitable. Maybe one will grow up to become something greater, but the rest will be dealers and bangers. The soles of his Vans sneakers are getting soft from the heat of the concrete sidewalk. As Soma comes around the corner he walks into Jon, the one Mac had been asking about. [Jon isn’t his name. He never mentioned his name. He only goes by Jesus of Niger, or better know as Jon, the abbreviation of Jesus of Niger. Sometimes they’ll just call him Jesus. But we’ll just call him Jesus. Jesus is a homeless drug addict who can actually control his addictions. There’s not much about him to talk about.] “You know Mac is looking for you,” says Soma, “Do you owe him money or something?” “No. Maybe. Yes. I owe him fifteen for some crack. He spotted me, ya mean.” “Ya mean.” “Got any cash that I could borrow, maybe?” “You’re gonna pay a loan with a loan?” Soma shakes his head in disappointment. “Talk to Rick.” “I CAN’T TALK TO RICK, ARE YOU OUT’CHA MIND! He’ll kill me, homie! Remember, I twiddled his sister’s milkshakes? He’ll knock me back to Niger if he ever sees my simple arse again! Man, did she have a badonkadonk.” Soma laughs at Jesus’ face. “What’cha laughin’ at, fool?” “You said badonkadonk! I never heard anyone say that before. You could have just said ‘she has junk in her trunk’.” “Yeah… Ya mean, you have any money on you, ya mean?” “Ask Rick.” “He’ll cut off mah dick! Rick McDick don’t play around. He’s sick and twisted and nuts, homie.” “Then ask Scholl. He’ll lend it to you.” Jesus’ jaw drops. “Scholl? SCHOLL?! He’s worse than ALL OF THEM! I’m on bad grounds with him, ya mean. Very, very, very, very bad grounds with that guy. Man, just lend me fifteen dollars, please, man, oh pretty please?” “Go whore yourself.” “Man, fuck you. I thought you was my boy. I guess you ain’t nothin’ to me. Capeche? I never wanna see your stinkin’ ass around mah corner no more. Ya mean?” “Yeah.” [Rick McDick is just another name. He’s no one special. A drug user, a thug, that’s it. He’s known for his temper and hate for the Middle East. Other than being just another black man in Venice Beach, he’s insignificant.] [Benjamin Lionel Scholl is the man everyone is afraid of, along with his best friend, Henry Lee Kemper. Scholl and Kemper are very, very well-known around the Venice Beach gang world. Scholl and Kemper work for Westwood. They are his muscle. There isn’t a person in California that could knock out Benjamin Scholl in a fight and there isn’t a person in California that is a better shot than Henry Lee Kemper. Henry Lee is an ex-Navy Seal. He was discharged from the Navy and sentenced to fifteen years in prison for murdering a Marine in a bar. After only serving eight years he was released from prison. Long story short, now he works for Westwood. Scholl doesn’t talk about his past much. Only a few people, including Kemper, know about Ben’s past. Scholl is responsible for hundreds of murders but has never been arrested. The Country has no record of his existence. Other than Scholl, the only other person under Westwood’s eye that hasn’t seen jail time is Vivica Fox. She’s incidental right now.] Soma walks away from Jesus and down a street where he is to meet some specific. Down at the end of the street stands a normal looking man on the corner talking on his cellular phone. He is about Soma’s height but he wears a white button down Oxford shirt, a black tie, an ironed dark blue pair of dress pants and expensive looking shoes. He has brown hair, goatee, and a pair of black sunglasses. This man is known only as Jack. When Soma walks up to him, Jack signals Soma to wait a minute. Finally Jack is done with his phone conversation and asks Soma if he is ready. Soma replies with a yes. The sunlight spills in through the windshield of Jack’s 2003 BMW. The two men are drenched in the music of Guns N’ Roses’ song ‘Mr. Brownstone’. Alert and paying attention to the traffic and the road, Jack quietly sings along to the song. They pass by one building after another, hundreds of faces, dogs on leashes, children at play, business men and women, thugs and gangsters. After many minutes of on the road time, they arrive at a big white mansion with five expensive looking cars right out front on the off-white stoned, semi-circle driveway. The brand of cars is irrelevant. This is the house that Jack built. This is the place where one Dr. Thompson and Jack run their illegal operations. This place is a shady resort somewhere near Venice Beach. Away from the wanna be thugs of the streets. Away from the miniscule operations of burn-out drug dealers and pimps and the five mile wide vaginas of the prostitutes and whores. This is the home that crooked lawyers and doctors have built. By the way, Jack is also an ex-lawyer and a real estate agent. The lobby of the mansion is huge. Marble flooring, white, white, white all around. Everything is white. Then again, they are cocaine dealers. This is where Soma meets Dr. Thompson for the third time. Thompson is out on the back patio sipping champagne and lounging around the Olympic sized swimming pool. My God, this is the home of your wildest imagination. The brilliant green grass, so beautiful and elegant just simply makes you horny. There are no beautiful women in bikinis here. No, sir. It is unneeded. This is the life. This is the American Dream. The illegal American Dream. Thompson turns his head to see Soma. Soma smiles and nods his head, taking off his sunglasses, walking towards Thompson. Thompson quick jumps up and places his glass on the glass table to the left of him. “Jonathan, my boy. How are you these days,” Thompson exclaims while offering a hand shake. Shaking Thompson’s hand, “I’m fine myself, and you, Thompson,” Soma replies. “We’ve moved into new territory; guns. We sell guns now. Any kind of gun you want we can get! This new ‘extension’ makes us so much more money. We supply to Westwood’s boys now. It’s not just Westwood anymore. We now work with Westwood. We bring in the drugs and Westwood sells the drugs. We make a 40% cut. Of course Jack and I split it twenty-twenty.” Thompson smiles and glances at Jack. “Jack, show Jonathan around the place. He hasn’t seen this place yet. I will prepare dinner and we will dine. Jonathan, any specific drink you would like?” “No. No, I can’t think of one. You may choose for me. I trust your taste.” Thompson smiles at Soma, “Will do.” Jack takes Soma along for a tour. After an hour of showing Soma the place, they come upon the garden. The garden has everything you could ever want in a garden. It’s so beautiful to see a garden like that. Grows its own vegetables and it even has some of those Japanese designs laid into the property. So many minutes pass by and then sit down to a wonderful feast prepared by Dr. Thompson. Soma needs firearms. There’s a war arising back at Philadelphia. Something needs to be done They discuss the matters. To be continued.
