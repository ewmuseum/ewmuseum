Seperation

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders, which is accompanied by the usual voice-over of “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.” The scene opens to a shot in a dark alley in back of the restaurant where the last Shadow Riders promo left off. The ally is strangely quiet all except for a dripping sound coming from an unknown source. Suddenly a loud crash pierces through the night air as a large rusted metal door bursts open. As the door smacks hard against the water rotted brick wall beside it, Belmont stumbles through the frame escorted by “Superstar” Eric Russo. Russo’s face is beet red with anger as he pushes Belmont back against a fence. Russo raises his hand up and sticks his index finger into the center of Belmont’s chest as he begins yelling.) Russo: “WHAT THE HELL IS YOUR MAJOR MALFUNCTION!?” (Belmont just stares into Russo’s eyes offering no explanation for his recent actions. This only serves to infuriate Russo more as he continues trying his best to control his the growing anger that is apparent in his voice.) Russo: “Seriously Belmont, this shit has gotta stop! It’s tearing the Shadow Riders apart at the seems! More importantly, It’s got me thinking that Annihilation’s claims may hold a little more water then we originally thought!” (Glaring up at Belmont’s still stoic expression, Russo continues.) Russo: “Well . . . don’t you have anything to say for yourself?” (Nonchalantly Belmont tilts his head down and looks into Russo’s eyes with a cold stare as he responds.) Belmont: “I don’t have to explain myself to anyone.” (Russo’s anger builds to a fever pitch as he explodes at Belmont’s comment.) Russo: “YOU DON’T HAVE TO EXPLAIN YOURSELF TO ANYONE!? YOU’RE A SHADOW RIDER, YOU HAVE TO EXPLAIN YOURSELF TO US! WHERE DO YOU GET OFF SAYING YOU DON’T HAVE TO EXPLAIN YOURSELF TO US!? WHO IN THE HELL DO YOU THINK YOU ARE!? WHAT MAKES YOU SO FUCKING SPECIAL!?” (We can tell the level of anger Russo is approaching by the veins in his forehead that have begun rising under the skin. Luckily, Kage comes through the back door of the restaurant at this point to see how the two men are doing. Kage is taken aback by the look on Russo’s face and the utter lack of interest on Belmont’s. Knowing by the look on Russo’s face that he has become extremely volatile, Kage inserts himself between the two Shadow Riders and attempts some damage control.) Kage: “Ok . . . we’ve had enough problems for one night. Can we all just go back to the hotel and worry about this in the morning please?” (Belmont glances over at Kage with a slight smirk on his face as he responds.) Belmont: “That’s fine with me . . . I’m done here” (With that Belmont pushes Russo’s hand that is still poking it’s finger into his chest aside as he turns and begins walking down the alleyway towards the busy street at the front of the restaurant. Russo attempts to go after Belmont but he is held back by Kage.) Kage: “Come on . . . just let it go for now. We’ll worry about this in the morning. Just go back to the hotel and get some sleep for now. You need to be rested up for your match with Ezra at the pay-per-view.” (Russo attempts one last time to make some headway after Belmont but Kage firmly holds him back. Knowing that he’s not going to get anywhere at this point in time, he steps back and takes a seat on a small wooden crate. Kage looks down at the still furious Russo and begins to speak.) Kage: “Look, don’t worry about Luke and Belmont. Their big boys and I’m sure they can work it out between themselves. There is no need for you to even concern yourself with the situation.” (With a confused look of anger on his face Russo tilts his head to glare up at Kage as he responds.) Russo: “No need to concern myself? This whole little rivalry between the two of them is tearing the Shadow Riders apart. How am I not supposed to concern myself with that?” (Sympathetic to Russo’s concerns, Kage does his best to reassure him.) Kage: “Look, we made those two Shadow Riders for a reason. We have to put our faith in them that they won’t allow this little pissing contest that they have going to come in the way of the advancement of the group. We have to just trust them and hope for the best.” Russo: “That’s a lot easier said then done. I don’t feel like seeing a group that I built end up in shambles just because those two wanna act like Tom and Jerry.” Kage: “A group WE built.” Russo: “What?” Kage: “We built the Shadow Riders as a team, you didn’t build them by yourself.” Russo: “You know what I meant .” Kage: “Actually I’m never to sure with you anymore.” (Russo now stands up and looks Kage directly in the eye as he responds.) Russo: “You know me, I may talk about myself more then the group but the team will always come first. I haven’t forgotten what we were brought here to do and with that objective, we have no room for single agendas.” Kage: “As long as you remember.” Russo: “Don’t worry, I will never forget why Lights Out Wrestling will just like the rest become nothing but another falling star that will be left in the dust of the Shadow Riders.” (With that, the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders which is accompanied by the usual voice-over message of “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.” once again. The screen now cuts to black.)
