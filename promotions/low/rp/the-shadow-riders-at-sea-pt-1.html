The Shadow Riders at Sea pt.1

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wanting to get a change from the hectic wrestling schedule that they are accustomed too, The Shadow Riders decide to get away from it all by taking a little fishing trip just off the coast of Miami, Florida. This trip however turns out to be a lot more hectic then they ever bargained for. What trouble will the Shadow Riders get into this time . . . well you’re gonna have to read the story to find out. (The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders along with a voice over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens on a bright and sunny day. We are on one of the many docks along the South Beach of Miami, Florida. On the dock, “The Vigilante” Luke Justice and “Superstar” Eric Russo are on the deck of a large white yacht. Beside them on the dock, a worker is helping them as they load up their boat with what seems to be fishing supplies. As each item comes aboard, Russo reads it off of a checklist as he makes a small mark next to it.) Russo: “Fishing rods, dynamite . . . shotguns . . . flares . . . life raft, and bait.” (Russo turns his attention back to the dock worker as he begins to roll barrels of chum onto the boat one after the other. As the worker rolls the last barrel onto the boat, he speaks up voicing his opinion in a grim tone.) Dock Worker: “You guys shouldn’t be going fishing with this type of bait. There’s some pretty big sharks out in this area. This bait will attract them and that’s the last thing you want. They can be pretty dangerous you know.” (Russo pulls out his wallet and pulls out a wad of $100 bills which he hands to the dock worker as he responds.) Russo: “Shows what you know jackass. We are looking to land a big fish . . . duh.” (Luke walks over to the barrels of chum and pries one open so as to see what’s inside. As he look in he speaks up.) Justice: “I never got why they call this stuff chum. I mean what the hell? It’s fish guts . . . why not just call it fish guts?” (Russo shakes hands with the dock worker who turns to walk off shaking his head at the situation that the Shadow Riders are putting themselves into. Russo calls out to him in a semi-cocky tone as he walks off.) Russo: “Don’t worry . . . we’ll be sure we’re careful around those big bad sharks out there.” (At the sound of the word sharks, Luke perks up.) Luke: “What did you just say?” (Russo turns to face Luke as he tries to cover up that he mentioned sharks.) Russo: “I said that the word chum was probably a “spark” in some stupid ass fisherman’s head.” (Luke smiles seemingly reassured for the moment as he responds.) Justice: “So all we do is pour this stuff in the water?” Russo: “That’s the plan.” (Luke looks back into the barrel of chum and still smiling as he nods his head and responds.) Justice: “And it’s gonna attract some fish?” Russo: “Well I hope so, or else we just blew a hell of a lot of money for nothing.” (Luke reaches down and picks up the lid for the barrel placing it back on top so as not to spill any of the bait before it is time. As he does this, he begins singing the only sea song that he can think of at the moment in a deep almost operatic voice.) Justice: “Ooooh . . . he lives in a pineapple under the sea. Spongebob Squarepants! Absorbent and yellow and porous is he. Spongebob Squarepants!” (As Luke continues singing, Russo glances over to the long pier and calls out.) Russo: “Come on . . . you’re late! We have a schedule to keep.” (The shot turns to see “The Loose Cannon” Jason Kage running down the dock towards the boat. He is wearing a thick orange life vest. In his arms is a large brown shopping bag overflowing with what looks like chips and beer. Kage also has a nap sack slung over his shoulder. Seeing the sight of the chips and beer, Luke lets out a loud hoot as he breaks out of his song. As he reaches the side of the boat, Kage is helped on board by Russo. Once Kage is on the boat, Russo jumps off and unties the boat from the dock as he shoves it off with his foot. Feeling the now unobstructed current of the ocean, Kage grabs onto the side of the boat as his face turns a light shade of green. Russo quickly jumps back onto the yacht as it moves further and further away from the dock. Back on board, he walks over to Kage to check on him. When Russo sees the coloring of Kage’s face, he rushes over to the nap sack and pulls out a small bottle of Dramamine. He feeds Kage the recommended dosage. It seems the further the large yacht moves into the ocean, the better Kage feels. Once he is somewhat recovered, Russo begins to show him what each part of the boat is called. Russo points to the front of the boat.) Russo: “Ok, that is called the front . . .” (To the left.) Russo: “we can call this the left side of the boat . . .” (To the right.) Russo: “that’s the right . . .” (To the back.) Russo: “and most importantly, that’s the ass of the boat. Got it?” (Kage nods signaling that he gets it. The boat moves past the breaking waves giving us our first view of the yacht’s name. “The Wave Rider” is scrawled across the back of the boat much in the same fashion as the logo of the Shadow Riders.) Later on that Day . . . (The scene opens to a shot of The Wave Rider drifting on the surface of the water. In the far distance, remnants of the beach can be seen along the horizon. The day is hot causing waves of heat to rise from the surface. Kage is now wearing a fisherman’s hat in order to protect him from any more sunburn then he already has. He is busy at work dropping chum over the side of the boat by the bucketful. We can plainly see that the movement of the ocean is beginning to effect Kage once again. Luke is up at the wheel of the boat using one hand to drive while drinking a beer with the other. He is wearing a baseball cap and a pair of dark sunglasses. His nose is extremely white due to the thick coating of sun screen that he is wearing on it. Russo is sitting in the large deep sea fishing chair attached to the deck. He begins reeling in his bait. All three of the Riders look like they have been over exposed to the sun for most of the day. Also judging by the looks on their faces, it appears as though they have had absolutely no luck. Russo begins speaking rhetorically but loud enough to make sure both of the other riders can hear him.) Russo: “Damn . . . this is harder then getting a woman to hook up with Belmont. No matter how much we did to get them together, they always said he wasn’t their type.” (Russo reels in the bait which consists of a slice of pizza.) Russo: “Oh well, I’ll find something that these fish like. We got Belmont to loose his virginity when he was teamed with us . . . even though it cost a couple grand to get the girl to agree to sleep with that freak.” (Luke lets out a chuckle at Russo comment as he watches him toss the slice of pizza aside. Kage has stopped dumping the chum into the ocean as he is now bent over the side of the boat heaving his own form bait into the water. Russo turns to see Kage in this position and calls over to him.) Russo: “Good idea . . . maybe that’ll work. Keep it coming Jason, I think I see some movement out there.” (Kage pushes himself away from the side of the boat and opens the nap sack. He reaches in and pulls out the bottle of Dramamine once again. He pops the top and dumps the pills out into his hand. Kage quickly downs the pills and drops down to a sitting position on the deck as he awaits the relief that the medication provides. Russo jumps out of his seat and starts towards Kage. He is tripped up by a bright yellow cylindrical tank as he does so. Angered, Russo kicks the tanks aside sharply. Seeing this action, Justice exclaims.) Justice: “What are you doing!? Don’t kick them . . . they’re compressed air! If you so much as crack one of them we’re gonna be swimming back to shore because the entire boat will blow up!” Russo: “Well sorry . . . you shouldn’t just leave them lying around then.” (Russo continues towards Kage who has the nap sack lying next to him on the desk. Russo reaches down and picks the bag up. He stuffs his hand inside and pulls out a box of some sort of food in it. Russo now starts back towards his seat. Seeing the box in his hand, Luke calls down to him in a slight whine.) Justice: “No . . . you’re not gonna use the crackers are you?” Russo: “Yeah . . . the fish will love these. Here . . .” (Russo rips the box open and reaches into it. He pulls out a handful of the contents and throws it up at Luke. A shower of tiny orange crackers hits the big man in the face and fall to the floor. Luke reaches down and picks up one of the crackers holding it up for Kage to see. The cracker is shaped like a goldfish. Luke scoffs.) Justice: “Ha . . . real funny. He’s an assclown.” (Kage just shakes his head as he looks up with his hand on his forehead to shield his eyes from the sun. Russo baits his hook with a couple of the crackers. Once they are hooked, he looks at them as he begins speaking to the crackers as if they are real.) Russo: “Ok you guys . . . swim on down in that water and get me a fish. I know you can do it.” (Kage speaks up still sitting on the deck floor.) Kage: “You think there’s anything even out here? I think we should just call it a day and get down to some serious drinking.” Russo: “Just be patient . . . we’re on the ocean. It’s full of fish. It’s only a matter of time until we hook one.” Justice: “I just wonder how long that matter of time is gonna be. This is taking longer then it took Bubba McCoy to realize that his federation was never going anywhere with Price and the Stecchino’s around.” (Russo gets a chuckle out of this as he takes his seat back in his chair and casts his line out into the water. Once the line is out, Russo begins talking.) Russo: “No . . . it won’t be that long. I learned my lesson about having too much patience. I mean how long did we put up with Belmont when we knew things weren’t working out. We tried and tried to give the guy a personality but you can teach a dumb dog any tricks. If nothing bites soon, we’ll call it a night. As a matter of fact, if nothing happens in about . . . say 20 minutes we’ll call it a nig . . .” (Suddenly Russo’s line begins being pulled furiously out into the sea. Seeing this Kage jumps up. Russo quickly grabs hold of the rod and straps into the chair. He then slowly begins to reel the line in. Softly, Russo begins talking to himself.) Russo: “There we go . . . now we’re talking. Come to poppa baby. (Doing his best Fat Bastard impersonation.) I’m gunna eat ya!” (Russo now yanks back on the rod making sure to hook his catch. Feeling the tug, whatever is on the other end of the line begins swimming away from the boat causing Russo spool of line to whiz out. Kage runs over to stand beside Russo. Once there he exclaims.) Kage: “Come on . . . you got this!” (Seeing Kage beside him out of the corner of his eye, Russo yells at him.) Russo: “Get behind me you jackass! You’re gonna get in my way!” (Russo then calls out to Luke who is still by the wheel of the boat.) Russo: “Get the boat in reverse then turn it around. The fish is eating up my line!” (Russo now turns his attention back to Kage as he calls over his shoulder.) Russo: “Grab me a beer . . . quick!” (Kage rushes over to a small cooler and swings it open. He plunges his hand into the ice and yaks out an ice cold beer. Suddenly, Russo’s line changes directions. Seeing this he yells out to Luke who it hard at work turning the boat.) Russo: “The other way you jackass!” (Luke jerks the boat to the other side.) Russo: “Woah! Not so fast!” (The line changes direction yet again only ths time it goes straight down. This cause Russo to yell out to Luke once again.) Russo: “Stop! Put it in neutral!” Russo (To himself): “Where the hell is this fish going?” (Russo begins reeling in the line like a man possessed.) Russo (To himself): “Ok . . . I get it now. I know what you’re doing jackass.” (Russo rod jerks down into an arch.) Russo (To himself): “Nope . . . not gonna get away that easy.” (The fishing line rushes out again freeing up some slack. Seeing this, Russo quickly reels the slack in with all of his might.) Russo (To himself): “Trying to trick me?” (The fishing line is almost perfectly straight now as a look of confusion fills Russo’s face. He begins to reel the fish in little by little.) Russo (To himself): “What are you doing? Running . . . no that’s not it. Give me a hint . . . where you going?” (Russo begins reeling the fish in as his face fills with anger. He calls up to Luke.) Russo: “Luke! Get down here . . . grab a pair of thick gloves on your way!” (Russo looks back at the water.) Russo (To himself): “You wanna play games!? Let’s play!” (Russo calls out to Luke again.) Russo: “Hurry up man!” (Luke is moving down the steps as fast as he can while pulling on a pair of thick gloves over his hands. As Luke is greeted by the sight of Russo reeling the fish in, Luke decided to give Russo his advice on technique.) Justice: “Don’t bring him in so fast! He’s gonna get away!” (Russo who is covered in sweat at this point shoots a glare over towards Luke as he responds.) Russo: “Don’t tell me how to catch a fish . . . I know how to catch a fish!” (The sinker on the line breaks the surface of the water. Kage’s eyes begin desperately searching as he waits impatiently for his first look at the fish that is putting up such a fight. Excitedly, Kage begins speaking.) Kage: “He’s right there! You almost got him!” (Russo yells to Kage over his shoulder.) Russo: “Hurry . . . unhook me from this seat! Now . . . come on!” (Russo turns to Luke) Russo: “Grab the line . . . hold him there! I have an idea! Watch your hands!” (Russo quickly turns back to Kage.) Russo: “Here . . . hold this!” (Before Kage can even react, the large fishing rod is thrust into his hands. Russo quickly runs off to the upper level in search of something. Moments later he is barreling back down the steps with a shotgun in his hand. Just at that moment however the fishing line snaps. This cause Luke to stumble backwards where he trips over the compressed air tanks that are still lying on the deck. The rod whips back towards Kage’s face nailing him in the forehead opening a large gash and knocking him to the ground. Russo rushes over and quickly snaps up the rod and begins to reel it in. Finally he gets to the end to see that the line has been eaten through.) Russo (To himself): “Damnit!” (Russo turns to Luke who is still down on the deck.) Russo: “Luke . . . come on . . . get up!” (Kage who is still down as well speaks up.) Kage: “I think he got hurt . . . we gotta go back to shore.” (Luke pops up of the deck floor and responds still slightly shaken.) Justice: “I’m fine . . . let’s get that bastard!” Kage: “What!? We’re gonna go after it? Why? Just let it go you guys!” Russo: “No . . . nobody screws with the Shadow Riders and gets away with it!” (Russo points out into the ocean and calls to Luke.) Russo: “He went that way . . . full speed ahead.” (Luke shakes of his dazed feeling and quickly gets up to the wheel where he fires up the boat and heads in the direction that Russo pointed. Kage looks on in shock as Russo threads another line through his fishing rod. Russo then tops it off with another Goldfish cracker for bait. Rubbing his forehead, Kage gets up and moves towards Russo as he begins speaking.) Kage: “You’re gonna try and do it like that again? It didn’t work the first time, what make you think it’ll wor . . .” (Kage is cut off by Russo who tries to explain his plan.) Russo: “I just gonna use this to get him close again. Then once he’s close enough, I’m gonna shoot him with the shotgun, understand that?” (Kage shakes his head at Luke and Russo’s sheer determination to catch a fish as he walks over to his nap sack. He reaches in and pulls out a container of sunblock which he begins to put on his nose in a thick layer as the picture fades out to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again where we hear a voice-over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The picture then cuts to a black, blank screen.)
