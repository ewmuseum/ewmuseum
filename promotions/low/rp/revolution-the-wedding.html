Revolution: The Wedding

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Revolution: The Wedding The eyes of Steve open looking into the mirror as Bladez and Jesse stand beside him. Steve all dressed in a white tux and Jesse and Bladez in white tuxes. Jesse smiles as he puts Steve’s hair in a ponytail and Bladez fixes his bow. Steve smiles kissing the two with joy on the cheeks. His wedding day is only an hour away. “I can’t believe this! Today, the day I get married! Boys, I love you!” Steve mutters weeping as he hugs Jesse and Bladez. “Oh stop it,” laughs Bladez, “Come on, you’re a man!” The three are all happy and Steve knows this is a big day for himself. “Do I look good?” questions Steve. “Perfecto! Nothing could make you look better but your bride,” states Jesse. “That is all I need to hear man… My wife, Terri soon will be known as Terri Andrews. And tomorrow we’ll be on our honeymoon. Jesse, how does it feel to be married?” wonders Steve looking in the mirror at him again, “Is it pressuring to know you have to rely on her a lot or she has to rely on you?” “Steve. Love is the best thing between couples. Never forget that even if you’re in a situation where it feels your love has come to an end. Think of Terri as your teddy bear. You hug her, you kiss her, and you talk to her… In the end you will always have her,” Jesse proclaims as he sees Steve wiping his eyes. “That Jesse is poetry man… Poetry at it’s best,” replies the weeping Steve. “Yes Jesse… You’re a goddamn good poet. I hope you enjoy what I have to say in my vales tonight,” states Steve hugging the two one more time. “I know it’ll be from your heart Steve and that’s all that’ll count,” replies Jesse smiling at him as he walks out the door waving his hand. [The Bride] There sits Terri in a chair getting her face fixed up by the assistants. Jesse comes walking in the door right behind her and she smiles as she sees him through the mirror. Jesse comes over and waits as they finish the blush. Jesse hugs her, as he knows she is excited. Her face all red from the emotion from upcoming days, and her eyes open wide from being so excited for getting married, Terri is in love. Jesse knows how it feels and sits down in a chair beside hers and talks to her. “Jesse, how does it feel to get married?” Terri asks him as she gets her eyelashes played with. “It’s like your first kiss Terri, you never forget it. From beginning to end, back and forth, left to right, you will never forget this day as long as you live. When I was married it was the best day of my life and I regret losing her,” claims Jesse now shedding a tear over warm memories. “Oh don’t cry Jesse. Love hurts, but one day you’ll find another woman to take her place… And when you do you’ll have the same feeling as me and Steve does now. Even if that day is a long time from now, you will…” Terri states reaching over grabbing his hand and pulling it closer, “You’ll find another one, I promise you that.” “Terri, you’re an angel. Steve is one lucky man. Luckiest man alive, I’ll tell you that,” laughs Jesse kissing Terri’s hand, “And one day you’ll be coming to my wedding.” “I really do think your one of a kind Jesse… Remember that forever please… Remember it. Someone will enter your heart one day and when they do it’ll be the greatest day of your life…” Terri whispers to Jesse before kissing his cheek. [The Cake] Standing there beside the cake is none other than Corey and Omar. The two all dressed up. Omar uncomfortable in his tux for he rented one size too small and just manages to fit in it. Corey on the other hand received one size to large so he had to use a belt for his pants. “Yummy, this cake looks so good,” mutters Corey looking at it as though candy to a child. “Back off boy, this is for the wedding, not for you and only you. Anyway I need some help. I can’t move my arm. Can you get me a drink?” asks Omar to Corey whom is now eyeing the cake top to bottom. “Hey I said back off!” snarls Omar grabbing Corey’s neck and ripping his arm sleeve. “HA! Look at that your one arm short!” laughs the little Corey. Omar reaches over and rips his other sleeve off now wearing a no sleeved tux. He smiles as he takes the sides off and tosses them under a table and grabbing Corey by the neck. “I told you not to touch the cake, but no the dog had to bury the bone in a plant,” states Omar, “Making a mess again and again… You’re like a child or worst a dog! I don’t want you near this cake at all!” [The Choice] Near and hour after the groom and bride got ready they are now reading there vales. “Steve has chosen to read vales he had wrote for his bride,” states the Preacher. “Terri… You are my fire,” says Steve with passion, “You are the fire that kindles my heart, the fire that burns the logs at night when were on the couch, the fire I fight with in every fight I have had with someone.” The guests look on, some smiling, others crying, and a few silent as they listen to the vales. “The fire of our bond is hotter than the suns core, it’s a bond of eternity. Every waking moment with you is a tear of joy. Your laugh warms me like a babies face,” states Steve looking into Terri’s eyes directly, “You are my friend, my lover, my wife…” says Steve now crying. The guests wipe their eyes and blow their noses as they applaud Steve for his words in this ceremony. “Now Terri will read her vales for Steve,” states the Preacher. The guests quite down as she approaches Steve and holds his hand. “Steve, I have loved you with more than my heart. You’re an angel in disguise. Your smile a warmer sensation then being hit with that fresh breathes of air, after coming up from under the water. You’re a dove in the rain protecting me from falling of leaving you at any giving time. I love you like no other and I love your personality for without it you’d never have such loyal friends,” cries Terri as she pulls Steve’s hand to her face. “The Groom may kiss the Bride,” states the Preacher. Steve and Terri melt into each other’s arms kissing one another. There love as pure as gold. [Fades]
