Unanswered Questions

13 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
““The devil made me do it” I have never made one of them do anything. Never. They live their own tiny lives. I do not live their lives for them. And then they die, and they come here, (having transgressed against what they believe to be right), and expect us to fulfill their desire for pain and retribution. I don’t make them come here. They talk of me going around buying souls, like a fishwife come market day, never stopping to ask themselves why. I need no souls. And how can anyone own a soul. No they belong to themselves. They just hate to have to live up to it.” –The Sandman…season of mists The earth slid cool and wet between his fingers, earth soaked by the storm, it smelled rich and heavy, perfumed with a layer of long dead leaves and other things. Things which writhed against the soft flesh of his hands as he plunged them further and further into the earth, ripping and tearing at it as the storm raged overhead. Dark clouds rolled in on the fury of the wind, and an unearthly howl arose from amongst the trees, like the shreik of banshees, their song punctuated only by snapping branches and a sirens wail somewhere in the distance. Where is he? Have the angels finally found him and drug him into the fiery depths? Or has he finally found something to live for in the darkened hell and warmth of his own mind where he’s never alone…never alone. A trail of twisted, broken souls marked the path which had lead him here, shattered stone marker of the living’s grief….the living’s tribute. A black winged bird perched on the tombstone in front of him and pecked absently at the hard granite as he forced his hands into the mud one more time, then jerked one away, bright red tainting the deep brown and slime. Cautiously he tried again, this time seizing the object as it cut into him and pulling it free of the spot where it had rested for over a year, the spot where it was no longer needed. 'Yeah, you fucking won.' Lightning flashed across the sky, yet he did not hold up his prize for us to see, he simply tucked it in his coat and stood beneath the deluge that poured from the heavens, curse after curse spewing from his lips as he raged senselessly at a past he’d believed himself ready to leave behind. [Fade away] There is no rain here, and the chill of wind blown air has been replaced by the roaring heat of an open fire. He lays on the ground, his backpack under his head, his eyes open and blinking away the last moments of sleep…then settling on the thousands of twinkling stars that winked overhead…watching…waiting…. He watched as one grew brighter, then slide across the blue/black abyss of twilight until it dipped down, down, down, towards the horizon where it finally burned away… 'There is a season to everything…that star had its season, and then like all stars, those of the heavens and those on earth, it fell…it fell from its perch on the dark carpet of night, it fell from grace, a glittering jewel...tarnished….as I know myself to be…' 'Others believe I am just one night away from seizing greatness, of taking my place amongst the legends….of being remembered forever…..an echo in the arena…like so many echoes before me…they never die, they simply grow louder as time goes on.' 'Yet in my heart I know that I am no echo…I am no champion… it is not determination that drives me, but pure, unadulterated rage…I hate, I hate and at times I can’t even understand what it is I’m hating…myself, my life, my past, my future…I want to lash out at the world, I invite it to break me…as I have invited you to break me, Iron Lung….the only problem is… After watching how you prepare for your matches, I have come to realize that you are just not capable.' 'I do not know anymore if I hate simply for the sake of hating, or if there is something more there, something I block out with the booze and the endless nights wandering city after city. With the music, the thrill of pyro and the bigger rush I experience each and every time I step into the ring.... something drives me on even in the moments when I should feel happy... It never leaves me.' 'Only I don’t feel happy…I don’t remember what happy feels like, I don’t even think I’ve ever known. I can’t remember what the sunlight looks like, or how laughter sounds anymore…my ears have grown accustomed to the sound of screams, the music of them rising and falling, and the wet moisture of the tears that accompany them.' 'I watch as all that is suppose to be beautiful in life is broken, I watch laughing as the storms and waves crash upon the fragile castles in the sand and wash them away, I watch as opponent after opponent stands across the ring from me looking to fight, looking to prove themselves, looking to become the best, and all I can think about is ending them, ending that dream…..maybe…maybe it is because I have forgotten how to dream. I have desires, but the dreams that once drove them have been forgotten over time, in a haze of beer, in a cloud of fumes from gallons of gasoline.' 'It seems like it was another lifetime ago that I longed to be the hero, that I longed to hear the cheers of the fans, longed to watch them come out of their seats….now I stare into the stands, and see only their animated corpses, I see only maggots feasting on their dead flesh…I watch it drip from their bones, watch it splatter on the ground, drip into the aisle as their spirits dance around the shells they’ve finally been free from, laughing, laughing at the spectacle which takes place on the stage just for them….' 'I play to death and I amuse it, I am her jester, her mime, and in return she has given me back that what she has stolen…yet made me distrust the very site of his presence at my door…. Yet her pity, her gift, only causes me to hate more…hate until there is nothing left but the pain I can inflict upon myself… the pain no one can take away… A pain unlike anything you could inflict with any move you know Lung…. With any object you might find to cheat…with any underhanded tactic, any table…any chair….a pain unlike any you have in you to bring. Your just not strong enough, your just not mean enough….your just not dirty enough, Iron Lung.' 'I have returned to the LOW searching for something besides the hatred to motivate me, searching for something besides this eternal darkness I have given myself to…yet already I am beginning to question whether the darkness isn’t of my own making…whether I have not already attained that which I was meant to find, and whether I am simply blind to the fact that it is there staring me in the face, waiting for me to understand. Is there anyone out there who can make me understand…is there anyone out there who will show me that there is a sport left beneath the chairs and the flames and the blood?' 'Lung, you are not the one….you may try but you will fail…and I will walk on. Maybe in the trials that I will face in the weeks following this one, I will find the one….or maybe, just maybe I will discover what the spirits have tried to tell me….that I am the one, the only one….' We fade to black and from black to static.
