Advantages // Hypocrisy

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Money makes a man? Our monetary popularity system is something that starts at a young age. The rich look down at everyone while those being looked down upon try to change whom they are. Why does money make people act so superior towards others? Do they have more brains? Do they compete harder in sports? NO? That’s right all they have is money; money that was given to them by their family. They don’t know how to live life because they don’t have to struggle, they don’t have to work hard, and they don’t have to go hungry for a week to pay rent. Those that can survive that often times turn into the rich. But not old money…new money made by hard work they learned when they were young and being looked down upon by the people who now invite them to join their Golf Club. Cash money makes a man? It does. Isn’t that pathetic? Well I am not one to talk. I grew up dirt poor from a dysfunctional family. When I finally made it big I was totally different. I was angry, I was vicious, and I did not take any prisoners. I did not care for those less fortunate even though I was once one of them. I always looked at them as a Conservative Dick. “If I can do it, then they can.” That mentality is not needed in this dog eat dog world. Nobody looks out for the small people. I let it dictate my life and bring me on a long bad trip. I screwed people left and right leaving many homeless and many unemployed. Being a Corporate Raider is what I was taught to be. It wasn’t school and my business degree that taught me that but Ellis Jackson who did. I looked up to him from the first time I started wrestling and he took me under his wing outside of our secondary job in wrestling. I was good. Real good. But after awhile I didn’t feel good inside. I looked at myself in the mirror one day and I wasn’t smiling. I realized that I hadn’t smiled sincerely in ages. I looked behind me at all that assets I had, all the paintings, all the furniture and I didn’t care. But that was just the beginning a month later I realized Ellis cared more about the money then he did our friendship. I walked back up to that mirror and I said to myself. “It is time for a change.” So that’s what I did and that is whom you face in the ring now or look at it these days. I am not doing anything for money. I am not a slave to something I have enough of. It never made my soul fly; I just want to have fun. I just want to enjoy my job as a wrestler, money isn’t a part of that. It still is society though. I am not living in the woods so I understand the need for the green. It has its advantages… Voice: I cannot give out that information, sir. (Scene opens up outside the DMV. Jerry Higgins stands next to a man in his early forties with a mustache and glasses. Jerry looks indifferent as he nods at what the man says. The man stares at him looking cautiously at Jerry. Jerry begins tapping his foot and then shakes his head as his smile fades and is replaced with a serious look) Jerry Higgins: Cannot? Or will not? Man: (Begins sweating) I can get fired. Jerry Higgins: What do you want? (DMV guy shrugs and looks down at the ground) Man: I have no idea what you mean. Jerry Higgins: No I am sure you know exactly what I mean. Man: This type of information isn’t for anybody who wants it. It is for police officers only by request and I doubt very much that you are a cop. (Jerry laughs at the comment made by the man) Jerry Higgins: Ok how much do you want…(Looks at the mans name tag) Fred. Fred: Well I never. Jerry Higgins: Stop the charade, Fred. I don’t have all day. (Fred walks a few feet away and then signals Jerry to follow him. They get within an alley about twenty feet away from the DMV and Fred smiles.) Fred: I want it all up front. Jerry Higgins: First you tell me how much you want, Fred. Fred: (Pauses) Oh…oh yea, my bad. Jerry Higgins: And? Fred: I need a grand. (Jerry smiles and nods as he takes his wallet out and hands Fred the cash) Jerry Higgins: Now you are mine until you get me that information. Fred: It shouldn’t take very long, sir. This will make it take even less, haha. Jerry Higgins: Good because this is important and my employer won’t be happy if it takes a while. Fred: (Sweating again) Don’t worry, don’t worry. I am your man. (Scene fades) You have it and they want it. There in lies the advantage. You need information you use money. People will do anything for the right price and in that truth lies the reason I do keep my money. You cannot be nice to someone and suddenly get what you want. That doesn’t work with women, don’t lie to yourself, or listen to your mom. Money is what they want, it attracts obedience and in doing so I can become a master just for that time. I don’t let it go to my head though. I just use what I have to get what I need. This isn’t immoral in my opinion. I just prove that money makes the world go round no matter if the money makes you dirty. I send Jerry to get the information with a wad of cash. I know that people won't do anything unless they are compensated. Does that make me wrong? No I am just using my cash as an advantage. I am not conforming to this hypocritical society; I am using those who are already conformed to do my bidding. Oh don’t look at me like that. I am not the person you should look at like that. This world is greed. Destroy the earth for oil. Even though Electrical has been proven to be more efficient. Money, money, money got to make those Corporations money. Ha, my sarcasm isn’t for everyone I suppose but all I say is the truth. The rich look out for the rich and who is richer then the Government? Well just ask them why they decided upon building cars instead of creating the country railroad system? Ask why we go to war every time oil prices go up instead of just saying fuck those idiots building gas cars and drilling for oil and look ahead to the future with electrical cars. Oh man, I mean god hate me for wishing a better environment and cheaper costs. Damn I forgot who pays for the campaigns. Screw it I just follow in those footsteps and then fill them with dirt hoping people can stop doing it. I am not what is wrong if it wasn’t for how the society is ran I wouldn’t be bribing people for information by sending my employees. Take the money and run Steve Miller said and hey that’s what people do. Give them the money watch them get the job done and then someone else will watch them splurge the extra cash on something worthless. Ah what a great circle. What an advantage. I pay you deliver. I love how pathetic you fools are. Cash Me. Out. CADE. I intimidate most and get emulated by all. I toss out a bill and the vultures swoop. I can dictate how you act by one action and that is why I have always been considered one of the hardest men to read. The thing is you worms are always easy to read and I use that to my advantage. I play mind games but often times I can't even consider it a mind game. I am sure you can guess why. The fact that it is hard to warp the minds of those who don’t even think for themselves would be why. Those who align themselves with Mike Meyhu, I have a hard time believing think for themselves. It is an easy estimation to make seeing as they listen and act upon one mans commands. A man who holds the cards, oh my bad…the cash and promises of title shots float in your head. What now? Guess he still has some power but to be honest none of that matters to me. All that matters to me is this upcoming match with Matt Robinson. I hope he doesn’t trip over his own shoelaces on the walk down to the ring. Meyhu may give him money but he obviously doesn’t have the ability to give him heart. Matt you are already defeated. Take your ball and go home because you are nothing but a pitiful former star that is bragging about some wins, when all you've faced in some curtain jerkers. I will accept the white flag now, Matt because right now you are just wasting my time. Think about it chump. You overrate yourself. Of course you do so by listening to the conformist fools. Just because a few people backstage suddenly suck up to you it doesn’t make you amazing. I can tell you right now that they are talking to you because you were a star, not because you’re going to be a star. Listen to them all you want but all they have done is made you soft. You are fresh laundry soft now. You rely on your opponents no showing to get your victories these days. You haven’t had a real match since joining LOW, but now you will. You are only proving that you are a shot in the dark and it is fading away. Quit or get your head out of your ass, bitch because you people are boring me. Get out of my way. Do something. I am CADE. Bored to death. Yet still doing his job. Eh least I will continue to… WIN!
