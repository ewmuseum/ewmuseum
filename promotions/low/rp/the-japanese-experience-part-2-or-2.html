The Japanese Experience [Part 2 or 2]

captured 7 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Unknown Ninja LOW Roleplay #695 Date: Feb. 03, 2003 Location: Forest somewhere in Japan [[The scene opens back up with Unknown Ninja and AJ Storm walking through the forest. They’ve been traveling well into the night. Both of them were tired, but had to go on if they wanted to find the monestary. It’s really hard to see what is going on.]] (AJ Storm) “I want to be the very best, Like no one ever was. To catch them is my real test, To train them is my cause.” (Unknown Ninja) “Huh?” (AJ Storm) “I will travel across the land, Searching far and wide. Each Pokémon, to understand the power that's inside.” (Unknown Ninja) “What the hell are you singing?” (AJ Storm) [[Completely ignoring Unknown Ninja]] “Gotta catch 'em all Gotta catch 'em all Gotta catch 'em all!” (Unknown Ninja) “STORM!” (AJ Storm) “What?” (Unknown Ninja) “What the heck are you singing?” (AJ Storm) “I was singing?” (Unknown Ninja) “Yeah.” (AJ Storm) “What was I singing?” (Unknown Ninja) “Something about Pokémon.” (AJ Storm) “Oh, heh, I do that sometimes.” (Unknown Ninja) “Why?” (AJ Storm) “That show is freaking awesome! With the little monsters running around killing each other.” (Unknown Ninja) “You know that is a kids show right?” (AJ Storm) “I don’t care. It kicks ass!” [[ Suddenly a loud crash is heard. Someone or something begins to moan.]] (AJ Storm) “Sonuvabitch!” (Unknown Ninja) “You fell didn’t you?” (AJ Storm) “Uh Huh.” (Unknown Ninja) “You probably tripped over your feet lefty legs.” (AJ Storm) “I tripped over those stupid bamboo bushes. Stupid pandas.” (Unknown Ninja) “That made no sense at all.” (AJ Storm) “Or didn’t it? Man, I don’t feel too good.” (Unknown Ninja) “Maybe it was all that fish you ate.” (AJ Storm) “I didn’t eat any fish.” (Unknown Ninja) “Yeah, your right, you didn’t.” (AJ Storm) “What did you guys do to me?” (Unknown Ninja) “Uhh, nothing, heh.” (AJ Storm) [[Imitating Unknown Ninja]] “Uhh, nothing, heh. You liar, what did you do?!” (Unknown Ninja) “I didn’t do anything, they fed you fish scales.” (AJ Storm) “THEY DID WHAT?! FISH SCALES?! I HATE FISH!!” (Unknown Ninja)'Heh, feeling dilusional?' (AJ Storm) 'Only Charlie the Tuna Fish Sammich can save me now!' [Still only sounds can be heard as AJ Storm and Unknown Ninja continue trying to walk through the forest. Suddenly bright lights dramatically turn on and all these other ninjas in white are standing there holding swords. AJ Storm turns all blue.] (AJ Storm) “Man, I don’t feel so good.” [AJ Storm collapses as the scene fades out.]
