Metallic Mayhem Pt. 1

2 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~{ When we last left The Metallo Zords, our heroes had just finished off an evil monster, known as Egg Sucking Sammy, that was sent from Queen Mogambu as part of her plot to destroy the world. After thwarting this rotten attempt at global conquest, the Metallos decided to take some time off and hang out with their friends. As they relax with Brainiac Benji, Afro Otis, Thin n’ Trim Tammy, and Pleasantly Plump Patty, The Gold and Silver Rangers are forced to realize a truth. The truth that being an intergalactic defender of the peace is a full time job. There are no vacations. Now we join our heroes at the local Recreational Center, moments before the call of duty ends their fun and games. }~~~ ::We open to a picture of the Metallo Zords and their friends sitting around a round table. They are all sitting on wooden chairs facing into the table, save for the Zords, who have their chairs facing away from the table but are seated on them backwards. They lean over the backs of the chairs, facing into the table. The group of friends has a table full of sodas, as well as orders of cheese fries and onion rings. The group enjoy their time together as the hustle and bustle around them is made lower so we can hear them talk.:: Silver Ranger – So why didn’t you guys just explain to Mr. Burnacre that you were not the ones who stole the copy of the test? Thin n’ Trim Tammy – We tried to tell him that but he just wouldn’t listen! He kept demanding that we must have taken it! Gold Ranger – How much trouble did all of you get in? Brainiac Benji – The rejoinder to your inquiry would be that we as a whole did not bare the full extent of the just punishment for the mischief that was caused due to the fact that we were able to convince our dear educator that it would have been physically impossible, even unthinkable, for us to have acquired the examination prematurely because at the time that the papers were stolen, we were present at the talent show. Afro Otis – And if you don’t speak nerd, he said that we didn’t get in trouble because we got Mr. Burnacre to believe us. Silver Ranger – Well that is a load off of my mind. Gold Ranger – Indeed. It relieves me as well to know that my good friends are not in trouble. If you guys ever need us to help you out, remember that we are always there for a friend. Pleasantly Plump Patty – Awww, you guys are the best! Gold Ranger – There is no need for an expression of gratitude, our offer to you is deserved. ::The Gold Ranger nods at Pleasantly Plump Patty to assure her that he means what he is saying. Thin n’ Trim Tammy grabs the head of the Gold Ranger and stops him from nodding any more.:: Gold Ranger – Why, Thin n’ Trim Tammy, why did you steady my head? Thin n’ Trim Tammy – I can’t see my reflection in your helmet to fix my makeup when you’re bobbing it all around like that! Gold Ranger – Oh, ha ha ha, you are a riot! ::The whole table begins laughing now at the idea of the reflection in the helmet. As they laugh and carry on, a small sound is heard coming from the wrists of the Gold and Silver Rangers. Once the Metallo Zords realize what the sound is, the duo stop laughing. The rest of the table does as well, then the Zords solemnly look down at their com links. As the pair prepare to answer the call, the scene cuts to a shot of the moon. Upon closer inspection, we can see Queen Mogambu’s palace. The shot cuts to inside the palace, where Queen Mogambu peers into here crystal ball.:: Queen Mogambu – Just look at those little, sniveling Metallo Zords! How dare they stand in my way! ::The Queen’s crew of henchmen come barreling into the room. Led by Raziel, in stumble Big Bad Barry Bear, Lulu de Blue, and Tim. The four clutter around the Queen as she continues.:: Queen Mogambu – Raziel! You’re last attempt at a monster to end this threat failed! It failed miserably! For that, I should lock you in a dungeon for a thousand years! ::Big Bad Barry Bear cuts in:: Big Bad Barry Bear – Yes, a dungeon! Lulu de Blu – In my native tongue, we would call it a cachot! ::And just imagine how much time Lulu must have spent locked in a ‘cachot’ while his homeland of France was under Nazi rule in the second World War.:: Queen Mogambu – Hush! Unfortunately Raziel, I don’t have a thousand years to lock you up, so instead, I’m going to give you another chance! I want something that will rid us of these pesky Metallo Zords once and for all! Big Bad Barry Bear – Yes, once and for all! Tim – Would you shut up, we don’t need an echo! Queen Mogambu – Quiet all of you! Raziel, could you do this for me? ::Raziel sheepishly nods:: Raziel – Of course, anything for you, my Queen. Queen Mogambu – Excellent. Before you start working, I must advice you. I do not want any old monster for this job, Raziel. This is a job that needs to be finished. I have a special request for this one. Raziel – Whatever it is you want, my Queen, I will provide. ::Queen Mogambu leans over and whispers into Raziel’s ear. Raziel listens, then takes a few steps back, stunned.:: Queen Mogambu – Is this possible? Raziel – We shall see, my Queen. We shall see. Queen Mogambu – Good! I don’t want you to muck this one up either, you yutz! Tim, watch over Raziel, make sure that his work is up to snuff so to speak. Tim – I’ll make sure of it. Queen Mogambu – Well don’t just stand there, there’s work to be done! Off with you! ::Raziel and Tim scurry off into another room to get to work on this project. Meanwhile, Lulu de Blu and Big Bad Barry Bear still stand next to their Queen.:: Big Bad Barry Bear – And what would you like us to do, Queen Mogambu? Queen Mogambu – Anything to get you out of my sight. Go lick dry wall or something. ::As Big Bad Barry Bear and Lulu de Blu rush out, the scene cuts again. We are brought back to the table where the Metallo Zords and company sit. The Zords glance down at their wrists, then at each other.:: Gold Ranger – Are you ready to accept our call, Silver Ranger. Silver Ranger – Ready as a rooster, Gold Ranger. Gold Ranger – All right, let’s do it. ::The Metallo Zords look down intently at their com links and press in the buttons to receive their urgent message. As they do, the scene cuts off. What is to become of our heroes? Find out next time!::
