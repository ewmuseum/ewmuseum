Bigger than a Rock Star

captured 7 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Canadian Perfect Chris Wrestling LOW Roleplay #696 Date: 2/3/03 Location: Concert Arena (The scene opens up inside an empty concert arena. There is a band doing a sound check. The band then finishes the sound check, and walks off the stage. Canadian Perfect then appears on the other side of the stage. He walks out to the center of the stage and looks out to the empty arena, as though he is visualizing thousands of people screaming and going crazy. The camera then comes up and stands right in front of Canadian Perfect. Canadian Perfect is not looking directly into the camera, but instead looking past it) (Canadian Perfect) Many people dream that one day they will stand in the middle of a stage like this and have thousands of people screaming their name. Why? Because everyone in some way would love to be a rock star. But many people will never become a rock star. Instead they are the people in the mosh pits, screaming their lungs out for the person they desire to be... The Rock Star... Well I am no Rock Star, I am bigger than any Rock Star. Why? Because I am a SUPERSTAR. Wherever I go, I always get people coming up to me, or gawking at me, asking themselves 'Hey isn't that Canadian Perfect guy from the LOW?' My life is what many people dream of, but yet those same people who want to be just like me, boo me every time I enter an LOW ring! Why? Partly because of jealousy, the other part is because the other guys on the LOW roster sleeper themselves out to the crowd, just to get a simple cheer when their music hits. Why bother? If every wrestler just acted like they really are backstage, it would be me getting the cheers. Why? Because I am the only real person in the LOW! (Canadian Perfect then steps back and grabs the mic on stage and begins to talk into it, as if he were talking to a huge crowd) (Canadian Perfect) On Sunday Night I will show that I am a fighter. Pax thinks he will just walk into War Wounds and walk out still the champion. But now he is not so sure. Why? Because he is finding out that he cannot over power me and cannot out fight me. But on Sunday his luck will run out. If it were not for those d**n referee's I could kill him. There would be no more Pax if I were allowed to unleash upon Pax. Pax does not know whom he is really dealing with. Sunday will be just a taste. I will give it all to Pax at War Wounds and then I will become a SUPERSTAR... WITH THE LOW AFTERMATH TITLE! (Canadian Perfect holds up an imaginary title in the air) (Canadian Perfect) But before that is War Wounds. One more night that Pax has to try and survive. Why? Because Pax is signed to get into the ring with his greatest challenger for his title in Canadian Perfect. But the threat does not begin on Sunday Night as the bell sounds. No the threat begins when he gets into the ring with me. Why? Because I don't care how I get the title... If it involves injuring Pax beforehand I will do it, and that is what I may indeed do on War Wounds. The fate of Pax's title reign is dependent on Sunday Night and Pax's ability to survive the partial wrath of Canadian Perfect. Because you see this Sunday Night I will not entirely unleash upon Pax... No I will wait to fully unleash when I get rid of Marcus Miller. But Pax will have to be at his best even on Sunday Night, because if he is slightly off, I will injure him. Why? Because I can. And no one will be able to stop me. Not Pax, Marcus Miller or the LOW fans! Cause I am Canadian Perfect! (Canadian Perfect adds a sound effect of a massive crowd cheering) (Canadian Perfect) I am bigger than a rock star, and I am a bigger star than any other in the LOW today. People claim to be bigger and state this and that but really can anyone really claim that I am not the superstar of the LOW? Is there anyone better looking than I am? Is anyone more talented than I am? And does the LOW Aftermath Title look better on anyone else? The answer is no! And can anyone do this as good as me? (The arena lights then flash on and off and a smoke machine lets smoke fill the screen as Canadian Perfect belts out a few bars of Drowning Pool's 'Tear Away!' After Canadian Perfect finishes the arena goes back to normal) (Canadian Perfect) You see I am just so talented! Trigger must be thanking god that he gets to have such a star like me in the LOW! I bet when Trigger saw that he had me on the roster, he fell to his knees and went into a deep prayer, thanking god that he can better his federation by finally having Canadian Perfect in it! Most guys would not see this great piece of fortune that has fallen onto them, but Trigger isn't stupid. The last couple of weeks showed this. I know Trigger will come up to me in the next couple of days and shake my hand and thank me. For what? Because I am the biggest star that has ever been in LOW. From that point he saw things for what they were, and finally gave me a shot at the Aftermath Title. Coincidence? I think not! With a couple of victories, I knocked some sense into Trigger and made him realize that I deserved a promotion and this new promotion that starts this Sunday! (Canadian Perfect pauses and poses) (Canadian Perfect) But I am still a little p*ssed off with Mr. Trigger. It was you Trigger who made me wait a long time to get this opportunity. But I am a forgiving man. And anyway, anyone who has Canadian Perfect on their roster is a top businessman... I mean the best looking man! Please, as if his middle aged, Pax could be classed as better looking than Canadian Perfect. (Canadian Perfect shakes his head in disgust) (Canadian Perfect) But this Sunday Night will be just a taste of 'the future!' Why? Because I will destroy Marcus Miller and Pax. Because at this Sunday's pay-per-view, I will show the World Champion and all the top contenders what I am all about... But more importantly, this Sunday Night will show the world a small preview of the devastation that I will inflict upon the LOW, as I destroy the soon to be former Aftermath Champion, before moving on and taking the World Title in the future! 'War Wounds' is your worst night Pax... Too bad there will be no paying back on your part because I am the greatest technical wrestler in history, I am Wrestling and above all I am absolutely Perfect! (The arena lights then cut out leaving the arena in total darkness)
