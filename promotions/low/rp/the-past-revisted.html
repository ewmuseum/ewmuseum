The Past...Revisted

24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=X=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Rehashing the past is something we all do, dwelling on what was, imagining what could have been. Some believe it is fair to dream of times long ago with the knowledge that the slight little rift or contortion of your actions of yesteryear could today have you standing in an entirely different place in an entirely different situation. Others shake their heads in dissapointment of those traveling back in time mentally to revist the unchangeable. It is a subject to debate yet despite your outlook deep down we all wish that we could somehow restructure even the most *minute* segment of our past in some form. The truth though brings about dissapointment in us all thus we have the dreamers and the nay-sayers if you will. Which category do you fall into? Minus the dissapointing realization of the impossible would you perhaps contort your view? What is it that you perhaps would like to change the the past? Can you speak the truth? It is possible but for the most part doubtful...Thus w! e are faced with and enter that which we are stuck with for all times with no chance in escaping it...Welcome to the present...Enjoy it because you have no choice... =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=X=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= || The world is covered in darkness as the sun has found itself hidden for the next 12 hours or so, at least in the world which we deal with. The location we speak of is none other than the heart of a nation in some people's eyes, New York City, the supposed 'city that never sleeps'. Yet for some reason we find our surroundings quiet silent as well as isolated in a shocking discovery. Blinking my eyes I feel as if I've just awoken from a slumber only to find myself standing alone in a dark strange place. Running my hand through my hair I feel that my hair is more than a mess and is in need of serious attention, looking at my palm following the hair check I see my hand soaked in blood. Unaware of what is going on I'm not startled yet a puzzled look crosses my face as I wonder what is going on and where I am. Looking down at my attire I can see that my clothes are in shambles and it is not a pretty sight as my shirt is all but torn from my chest as if I was attacked by some sort! of beast. My once new $65 jeans seem as if they'd been soaked in a mud puddle and trampled over by a wild tribe of sorts. The difficult part of this all is that I can't recall what I'd been doing prior to my standing here surveying myself. || 'What's Going On Here?' || My voice sounds different as the words fall from my lip and it pains my neck to let loose any sort of speech. Grabbing for my neck in pain I feel a large scar running horizontally across that which connects my head to my body and suddenly a vision flashes through my mind too quick for me to piece it together. As the vision leaves my brain a sharp stinging headache overtakes me making me want to collapse to my knees. Yet the sharp peircing pain is only brief as thankfully exits my head as fast as it came. Closing my eyes I release a sigh only to heard a rumbling not far from me forcing me to shoot my eyes open. Now tense I clutch my fists in preparation of the worst... || 'Who's There?' || To my question my only response comes in the form of a large rat running across the junk sprawled out about the crowd causing more noise as his large body scurries across the area knocking a few things over in his path. Letting out another sigh of relief I begin to ponder the reason I'm so tense yet quickly leave the questioning of myself alone and instead begin to pick up on my surroundings. It is so strange because the place I find myself in seems strikingly familiar yet at the same time I have no clue where I am. Whats worse is I can not begin to understand my reason for being here as the place is nothing more than a junk heap. Looking around I can see that this was once the residence of someone yet either fire or some kind of disaster changed all of that. the ceiling seems as if it has caved in at different spots in the room and the entire room is charcoal black as if it just spent time as the fire source of someone's afternoon cookout. The floor is covered in either fr! agmanets of wood from the ceiling or burnt furniture and clothing and just all sorts of things. || 'What is this place?' || Walking slowly with small steps cautiously I step over the ruins of the apartment making my way deeper into the open area steadily looking around wondering just what this place once was. Spotting a burnt up dresser I begin to make my way over to it for one reason....a mirror! Kicking some of the large chunks of ceiling aside, and walking around the burnt chairs and such I finally reach my destination without much stress. I look above the dresser to see the ceiling seems like it is about to collapse completely over the dresser as it looks at many other sections of the apartment. Cautiously I look into the mirror from a safe distance yet staring into the frame of the mirror it is easy to see that the mirror in question is indeed shattered and from my point of view I'd have to be pretty close up to see a clear image in the mirror. Looking up to the nearly collapsed ceiling I weigh my options and determine through carelessness that the risk is not as high as it could be. Walkin! g up close to the dresser and the mirror through the shattered glass mirror I see many reflections of my figure yet it is not until I am directly in front of the mirror that I can clearly make myself out. In the dark apartment it is hard to clearly see so I'm forced to lean forward which is exactly what I do. Yet before I can get a glimpse of myself a lound crashing sound is heard. Assuming that the noise hails from above and that the ceiling is about to collapse upon my head I tense up and duck my head under my arm as if that would protect me from the wooden rain from above. With my eyes slammed shut I brace for impact yet after a few moments my eyes open to see that there is no wooden planks falling from the ceiling, turning around it becomes apparent that ceiling collapse did indeed take place yet at another nearby section of the apartment. With a slight grin I turn my head back towards the mirror to examine my face... || 'Yeah, its me...Pax...sorta...' || Looking at the many lashes, scars, and such that plague my once perfect face it is apparent that alot more has happened to me in the not so distant past that I am unaware of. Looking to my once silky black hair that use to hang down and send the women into a frenzy it is obvious that something has gone terribly wrong in the past few days and the large amount of blood in my hair doesn't make things any better. Reaching slowly to my head looking for the source of the blood I feel around yet can't feel anything. Towards the back of my head is pretty sore as it appears to be swollen yet no blood is leaking from there, contorting my face is confusion I come to realize...|| 'This isn't my blood!' || Unaware of what the hell is going on I lean my head up and examine the long gash on my neck, as I touch it the vision once again flows through my head this time more consistent and pieced together. I see something covered in barbed wire slamming towards me and then a large flash of light takes over everything and I flash back to me staggering blood down a long corridoor...suddenly though immense pain runs through my head as I fall from standing to kneeling on a single knee. One hand grasping my skull as the other is balled up into a fist resting on the burnt dresser for support. Breathing heavy for a moment as a female would to contend with a vicious contraction during labor I make it through the pain and open my eyes more than gladd that the pain is over. Yet quickly forgetting about the pain one words falls from my lips...|| 'Pulse...' || Pulling myself to a standing position once again I stare into the mirror to view an image of myself that is far from what it should be, Realizing where few of my wounds have originated from is pleasing yet the new blood, the new scars, their origin remains a frustrating mystery that perhaps will come together in time. Becoming ever the more frusterated as I continue to view what I can not believe to be me in the mirror's reflection I send a fist flying into the mirror's glass followed by another and another until the glass from the mirror shattered even moreso and falls from the frame leaving me to stare at nothing but the wall. Breathing heavily with my fists clenched and now bleeding with glass sharps implanted in my flesh I suddenly begin to blink a great deal followed up by a face contortion as I turn my head to look at each fist...|| 'What the hell was that?' || Looking at my fists I quickly can determine what happened yet for some reason my recollection of the event is just not there. Disturbed by what just transpired I slowly take steps back away from the mirror with my fists still clenched as I look at each bleeding hand. While walking backwards I stumble and trip over one of the many burnt heaps of junk on the ground and collapse to the floor. As I fall to the ground a vision shoots into my head and I see myself walking past another man and listen to myself as I speak...'I'm Back'...the vision quickly shifts to a photo of myself placed next to a photo of a man that I do not know. The vision distorts sending my mind on roller coaster ride with images of things I don't remember nor can I grasp them becaue of the speed at which they run through my head...suddenly the face of the man in the photo pops into my head as he stands talking on a cell phone and is heard saying my name...'PAX'...with that my mind is wiped clean and opening! my eyes I find myself laying on the ground on my back staring at the ceiling. Before I do anything almost as an instinct I speak. || 'Realm...Raizo...Death Match Tournament...' || Sliding myself up through the pain felt in my hands I adjust myself to sit up on the floor and looking around the area once again I begin to speak to myself after remembering and coming to a sudden realization. || 'They did it, they pushed me to it, to returning. So now it has been said and quickly put into action and formally I suppose, I'm back?' || A slight grin runs across my face until once again the extreme pain in my head runs through my head leaving me to believe that perhaps a blood vessel in my skull is set to explode. I clutch my head with both hands and shut my eyes tight awaiting the pain to pass through and when it finally does I am once again breathing heavily. Opening my eyes and maintaining my clutch on my skull I contiue to speak to myself as before. || 'But what is going on, what is happening to me? I have to figure this out and get myself together before Realm, I'm returning to my world, I have to be focused. Raizo can not stain my clean slate, I have a chance to turn the past into exactly what it should be and that is history. My short comings will not matter. The fact that everyone holds me to small insignificant triumphs will no longer matter if I can return and strike hard with the talent the world is aware I have. I just have to... || Releasing the clutch of my skull... || 'I just have to get it together...' || Managing to push myself up to my feet I begin to dust myself off. || '...and for starters I have to get out of this place...' || With those words a loud smashing sound is heard echoing through my ears and as I look I can see the ceiling collapse over the spot where the dresser and mirror stood. Dust flies all over the place and the burnt rubble of the ceiling and house send ashes flying my way. || *Cough* *Cough* || Covering my face I walk in the opposite direction of the dust and ashes as it flies towards me. Coughing and blinded by the ash and dust I stumble over a few things yet maintain my balance until once again in a sudden instant my head is filled with pain again sending my collapsing to the floor belly first dissapearing intom the cloud of dust and ash. As I hit the ground my mind replays to me something I apparently have seen before as I see my Realm opponent Raizo being handcuffed byt policemen.My mind replays things weird as it seems to be playing things backwards as I then see Raizo standing in a bathroom stall with a pistol pointed at another male. Finally my mind shows me an image that is not familiar to me as I see Raizo laying face first in a pool of blood with a chain wrapped around his neck. Before all of these images clear out the face of my good friend Annihilation falls into view as he stands with a grin on his face, yet only for an instant as a bright light takes! over my mind and I open my eyes to me laying on the ground coughing. Unable to see, and coughin roughly as if all the smoking I've done over the years has caught up to me I begin to think to myself about a number of things. First which falls into mind is Raizo and the final image that I saw of him laying in a pool of blood...unable to focus though my mind jumps to thinking of the view of 'The New Revolution' a man I'd call friend yet have not heard from since the closing of Pulse...attempting to recall why I am unable to as my mind becomes clogged and once again an immense pain comes over me this time causing to be bellow out in pain. As the pain leaves me breathing heavy the dust and ash begin to settle and my vision begins to become restored. While laying belly down on the ground I hear a rumbling sound followed by a peculiar nibbling sound, as the ash and dust clear futher I can see directly in front of me sits the large bodied rat from before. Looking through the settling! dust and grime I see that the rat is nibbling on the edge of some rectangular form of paper or something. || *Cough* || Letting out a final cough I reach and remove the paper from the grips of the rat to much of his dismay as he squeals and takes off in the opposite direction. Feeling the paper in my hand is is apparent that this is not a paper at all, it is instead a photograph half burnt and half chewed up. Pulling it to my face as the last of the dust and ash settle I look at the photo and then look again. My eyes can not believe what it is that I see in the photograph and in and instant from my belly I push myself up to a standin position with the picture in hand...it is a picture of me as a child...and my mother! || 'What...what the fuck is this? Where...Where the fuck am I?' || Dropping the photograph I look around the area once again and it seems as if the room is spinning if not the world. Sections of the ceiling begin to collapse around me but I can not determine which way to go or what to do at the moment as I am in a state of confusion...Where am I? What is goin on? Unable to comprehend the truth I drop to my knees and lift the photograph into my hand again and gaze at the photo of the past, a past long forgetten and perhaps for good reason. Before I can say anything else, before I can think or do anything else the pain is back in my head and at a higher level this time. In an instant I collapse to the ground and it would seem this time for good. The world goes black, the feeling of pain dissapears and I find myself in a sudden sleep as the pain forces me to lose conciousness for the time being...still with all of my question unanswered and even more brought to the table...|| =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=X=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Do you ever seem to forget the past? Are your memories ever distorted or as some would say on the 'tip of your tongue' or the brink of being remembered? Perhaps the mind much like many in the world attempts to forget about the past for some odd reason. Perhaps the past is suppose to be in some aspects of life a forgotten time which to you and others never existed. Nobody knows for sure but the fact of the matter is that the present and the past functioning together can not coexist thus you must choose to live in either the past or the present, and if you make the wrong choise your mind may indeed let you know it...ask Pax...though an answer from him is far from promised... =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=X=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Note: *minute*- Not minute as in talking of time...but minute as in small and perhaps insignificant...*
