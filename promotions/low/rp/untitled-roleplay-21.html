Selling Yourself 2 ::The scene opens to a pre-taped shot of Eric Russo walking along a large hallway. He begins speaking as he continues walking down the hall.:: Russo: “We here at Shadow Riders Inc. know what a wrestler in today’s industry needs to know. We also know what all of the advertisers out there want as well. That is why we here at Shadow Riders Inc. have decided to bring you Shadow Riders’ University. Now SRU is unlike any other training facility in the market today. We teach our students how to deal with oppressing authority figures.” ::The scene flashes as it switches to a shot where a older man wearing a business suit comes flying through a door. We can hear Russo’s student Matt’s voice from inside the room from which the man was thrown.:: Matt: “I don’t give a damn what your marketing team tells you! The Shadow Riders are not kid friendly!” ::The scene flashes back as it rejoins Russo as he continues down the hallway. He continues speaking.:: Russo: “We do not waste the time of our trainees by filling our lessons with nothing but mindless dribble for the masses of untalented dreamers. We have a special line of training material which caters to the physically capable 17-32 age range that we are trying to attract. After all, they have to be able to handle the lifestyle.” ::Just then another of Russo’s students, Rob, comes out of an office with a woman dressed in a disheveled business dress. As they pass in front of Russo, we pick up Rob’s comment to the woman.:: Rob: “I think you’ll make a fine secretary here at the University. Come with me, I think you still need some work on your dick-tation skills.” ::Russo shakes his head and smiles before continuing on with his walk and speech.:: Russo: “We don’t promote an overly friendly exterior. Our time is extremely limited and we do not appreciate having it wasted. If you are serious about your future in the industry however, feel free to contact us and reserve you place for next semester. But you better hurry, because space is limited. We even have a daycare program for future stars with children. No longer will those little accidents hold you back.” ::Russo passes what looks to be the daycare center. Just then another of Russo’s students, Chuck, bursts out of the room with a baby in each arm and a pacifier in his mouth. He casts a dirty look in Russo’s direction as he walks off down the hall. Russo continues.:: Russo: “So whatever your needs, be sure to check us out here at Shadow Riders’ University.” ::After a few more adds, the show returns.:: Show URL: http://
