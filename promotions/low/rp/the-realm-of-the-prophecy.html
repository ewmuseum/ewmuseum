The Realm of The Prophecy!

28 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~ But now thy kingdom shall not continue: the Lord hath sought him a man after his own heart, and the Lord hath commanded him to be captain over his people, because thou hast not kept that which the Lord commanded thee. - King James Bible I Samuel 13:14 ~ :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: ~ The stage is set at all hallows eve, the day of my birth. Three men going into that very ring one is of the past, the other is the present, and then there is the future. The future of LOW, the future of this business, many have stood up against me and have failed. The Christian Beast, a man who thought that his super powers and faith would help him prevail against “The Prophecy”, and then there was Tessa……..what can I say about the most famous underdog in wrestling today? In GTT4 she took me for granted just like they always do, then at Catastrophic when she had her chance at redemption, “The Prophecy” of her career was revealed as once again she fell victim to my destiny! Now at Realm 61 at all hallows eve, the day of my birth I will face The Demond and Cobi Deekins for the Realm Title. Both respected wrestlers in there own right, but going into this match they will shortly realize that they will only be sacrifices to my success! Welcome to my Realm……………………………… ~ Time: 2:47 PM Date: October 27, 2004 Location: Riverside, CA. Riverside General Hospital [The scene opens up in Riverside California. The weather outside is dark, devastating clouds with a lot of rain in the southland. A man is seen walking to the overly extravagant Hospital. What also can be seen are some random ambulances rushing to the south side of the hospital towards the Emergency Room because of a major car accident nearby. The man looks at the hospital and wonders why they ever changed it. For you see this man ever so long ago on all Hallows Eve was born at this Hospital when it was called Riverside General. Why was he here? What was his purpose? The sky outside started to get darker as lightning could now be seen in the distance. As the man reluctantly moves closer to the Hospital, he is revealed to be John “The Prophet” Adams. The rain started to get heavier by the second, to let loose all the tears from the angles up above. The lightning that is seen is now followed by loud, booming thunder to show that it was getting closer each step the young gladiator took. The scene and commotion around seemed to slow down, and I mean that quite literally as John Adams reaches the front of the Hospital.] ~ I am the light in your darkest hour! ~ Thoughts of John Adams: What’s going on? Why am I here? Could this be the very answer to what I am looking for? [The scenery all around John Adams seems to melt away. The cars in the parking lot, the ambulances bringing critical patients to the Emergency room, and the sky above is melting away to form new surroundings.] ~ I am your relief when you have pain! ~ :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: [The new scene that has formed is inside the maternity ward in General Hospital. There is millions and millions of expecting mothers about to give birth. John Adams looks on at the screaming women going through their labor pains. The air was thick with emotions of pain mixed with joy. All around the ward is decorated for Halloween with Skeletons, pumpkins, and ghost. The attention is drawn though to a room in the far right corner. John Adams feels quite compelled to go there.] ~ I am your substance when you are hungry! ~ :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: [Inside the white lonely room is a lady about to give birth. But there is no one else around to help her. Scream after scream and still no nurse or doctor in sight. The walls of the room look white and clean, until the once sturdy walls began to crack by some unknown force. The cracks in the wall start to reach all the way to the ceiling. As this is going on the woman about to give birth is having her contractions more intense by the minute. Out of the large cracks maggots, roaches, and earth worms are starting to spill out into the once white floors of the room. It takes about two minuets before a doctor enters the room. When he turns to the woman it is revealed to be John Adams father Frank Adams.] ~ I am your liquid when you are thirsty! ~ Frank Adams: Well Mrs. Adams, it looks like you’re about ready to deliver. [The woman’s screams of pain get louder as more of the maggots, roaches, and worms start to spill out in clumps. As her contractions get closer and closer blood starts to pour out of the cracks also. What is supposed to take forever is done in a matter of minutes as the baby is delivered into the world.] ~ I am your salvation when you are damned! ~ Frank Adams: Congratulations Mrs. Adams, it’s a boy! [The woman tries to show a sign of joy, but is way too exhausted to. Frank Adams holds the young John Adams in his arms.] ~ I am your hand when you need help! ~ Frank Adams: Ahhhh, how are you Johnny? You see your Mother there son? Yeah that’s right…….Do you see her? Now see her for the very last time! [Frank pulls a gun out of his doctor jacket and points it at the woman. As he pulled the trigger screams inside the maternity ward get so loud, as everyone screams in a joint melody of terror. Also when the bullet enters the woman’s head splattering her brains unto the floor of now bugs, the blood that was pouring out of the cracks from the walls gets more intense destroying part of the wall in the process.] ~ I am your life when all you feel is death! ~ Time: 2:24 AM Date: October 28, 2004 Location: Motel 6, Pittsburgh Philadelphia [John Adams wakes up in a cold sweat from the intense dream that he experienced. He thought about the dream and what it meant for him as he goes for his first title in LOW. It will be all hallows eve, the day of his birth. 28 long years of battling his inner demons, but why not when it’s the battle that gives you the strength. LOW where some of the greatest talent has been developed, and John “The Prophet” Adams was going for his first….”The Realm Title.” A title that was held by the likes of Adam Stryker, Tristan Belmont, Eric Russo, Jason Ortiz, and Cyrus Skar. The three way match will prove to be interesting as the past, meaning Cobi “The idol” Deekins and the present, meaning The Demond will be going up against LOW’s future, meaning John “The Prophet” Adams. So as he goes into his match this all hallows eve, and as he battles his demons of his past, he will embrace it as he uses that pain and sorrow as his strength to win. A strength that people have known as “The Prophecy”, and then they will all know that the past and present of LOW is gone…..welcome to the future, welcome to his Realm.] ~ I am your future when you need “The Prophecy”! ~ ~ So that’s my dedication, the dedication of a man who plans to take what’s rightfully his! Delusional some may say….no I’m just practical when I say that a new era is dawning in LOW, my era. The lesson I preach to you is this, over the past shows you have witnessed a change in LOW. A change where the past legends have died off or have just faded away and you have witnessed even some of the present superstars that still have not reached that pedestal of perfection that they all so very seek. Well the future is coming to LOW, the future is now! So be forewarned of this prediction I give to you…..because come this Sunday hell has opened it’s doors and in LOW’s darkest hour “The Prophecy” will be the only light! ~ :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: ~ Prophecy, religious phenomenon in which a message is sent by God (or by a god) to human beings through an intermediary, or prophet. The message may contain a reference to future events, but it is often simply a warning, encouragement, or piece of information. Prophecy in its fullest sense thus includes augury, divination, and oracles, which are techniques by which, it is believed, the will of the gods can be learned. Prophets have often spoken in ecstasy, a state that may be induced by various methods, including dance or music. The emphasis of the prophetic message has varied, some prophets stressing the cultic, others the moral, and still others the missionary aspect of religious life. Prophets have appeared throughout history and in virtually all societies. – Encyclopedia ~ ~ Dreams and predictions ought to serve but for winter talk by the fireside. Francis Bacon (1561 - 1626) English philosopher, statesman, and lawyer ~ ~ Fin ~ ::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::
