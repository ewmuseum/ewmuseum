Origins

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::We open to a shot of the outside of a large tent. The tent, which is striped red and yellow, is round around the sides and pointed at the top. It is an easy fifty yards wide, and somewhere in the area of two stories high. The grassy hills around the tent are filled with people, scurrying about the many stands that surround the tent. At the stands, vendors sell miscellaneous food and trinkets, ranging from hotdogs to programs. There are wooden stakes in the ground with square signs nailed on to them, signs that read as follows. “Come one, come all, come witness the greatest spectacle known to man. We have acrobats, jugglers, freaks of nature! July 1st – July 8th!” We are taken now, inside the tent, where three rings are set up and an audience sits in make shift bleachers. The crowd watches as a short man, about five foot four, stands on a stool in the middle ring. The man wears a tall black top hat with a red band around it, an Ace of Spades sticking out the top of the band. He also has on a red tuxedo. He twirls his handlebar mustache around his gloved finger and he starts yelling into the yellow mega phone that he holds in his other hand.:: Ringmaster – Step right up, my curious audience, inch yourselves as close to the barricades as you like. For today you will witness things that you never thought possible, and you will want to be as close to the action as you can get! For this is a once in a lifetime experience, and an experience that can only be believed when viewed with your own two eyes! Gawk at the medical marvel known to man only as the Floating Torso, fascinate yourselves with the high flying antics of the Amazing Flutter Brothers, enjoy the skill of the Great Horatio, and peer into the soul of an insane killer, the Madman from Amsterdam! All this, not to mention our great jugglers, jesters, and jokers of old! Ladies and Gentlemen, I give you, the Circus of the Kings! ::Fire shoots up in a circle around the ringmaster and circus music begins playing from speakers that are set up around the perimeter of the tent. The lights inside flicker off now and when they come back on the ringmaster, the stool he was on, and the fire around him have all disappeared. The crowd applauds for the cheap trick, and then they hear the voice of the ringmaster. It is now being broadcasted over the speaker system.:: Ringmaster – Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome our first act, the Great Horatio! ::The crowd claps their hands and the curtains behind the three rings open up. Out from them steps a over seven foot tall, African American man with a shaved head. He wears large gold hoops on each of his ears and drags behind him a thick, metal chain with a hook at the end of it. The hook has a prime cut piece of meat attached to it. Horatio drags the chain and meat behind him, rustling up some dirt. The shirtless giant wears a pair of rainbow striped baggy pants with a set of red suspenders holding them up. He enters the ring to the far left and the Ringmaster’s voice is heard again.:: Ringmaster – This man, the Great Horatio, comes to you live from the rainforests of South America. When Horatio was just a young boy, his entire family was killed savagely by an invading army of rebel forces, leaving Horatio the sole survivor. Without any of his family or neighbors left, Horatio fell into the care of a den of tigers. By the tigers Horatio was raised, and with the tigers Horatio to this day lives. Ladies and Gentlemen, the Great Horatio! ::Suddenly, a door to the side of the ring lifts up, letting a tiger escape onto the main floor. The tiger roars mightily in the crowd’s direction, designed to thrill every attendee. The tiger roars at all four sides of the crowd, and then begins circling the Great Horatio. The tiger creeps slowly towards Horatio, who begins waving his hands in a “bring it on motion.” He then grips the chain he’s been dragging a bit tighter and starts swinging it in a circular motion. As he does, the tiger’s eyes lock on the meat and begins swirling around, following the chain as it swings. The circles made by the chain begin to grow larger and larger, until eventually Horatio begins spinning himself, holding on to the chain with both hands, whipping it around. The tiger begins chasing the meat now, circling Horatio time and time again. The faster Horatio swings the meat, the faster the tiger circles him. The swinging grows so great that Horatio stops spinning it around himself at knee level and raises the chain above his head to swing it. When this occurs, the tiger rears back onto it’s hind legs and starts pursuing the meat. This creates the illusion that the cat is running like a human being, an illusion that inspires an applaud from the crowd. Horatio begins slowing down now then pulls the chain towards himself, grasping the meat with both hands. He tosses the meat up onto a stack of steel boxes. Around the stack, there are many smaller stacks, all varying in size. With a clap of his hand Horatio signals for the tiger to retrieve the meat. The tiger runs up the numerous stacks, lunging from one to the next, and retrieves the meat. He begins tearing it apart in his mouth as the Ringmaster’s voice comes over the speakers again.:: Ringmaster – Ladies and Gentlemen, you have yet to see the true wonder of the great Horatio! However, before we continue with him, it’s time to take a look at another unbelievable act, the Amazing Flutter Brothers! ::The lights dim and two spotlights come on, each facing opposite sides of the far right ring. Suddenly, two men swoop into the spotlights on trapezes. The men fly towards each other, then jump from their trapezes and trade off. They are both dressed identically in black tanktops and black pants, with red stripes down the side. They also both have on masks to conceal their faces. The two put on quite a trapeze act, swapping back and forth, flipping around, and taking extended dives before recatching the handles. They swing over the open ground without a net, defying gravity and death itself. After a few minutes of swinging, the two, who are unusually large for acrobats, release their trapeze sets and flip down to the ground. There, they raise each others arms and bow before the adoring crowd.:: Ringmaster – The Amazing Flutter Brothers, everyone! Now, for our next act, I bring you a wonderfully twisted nugget of curiosity. A man born without a head. A man born without arms. A man born without legs. A man born as only a torso. This medical miracle stunned doctors by responding with shakes when spoken too, showing that somehow he is living with a will of his own. I give you, the Floating Torso! ::The Ringmaster’s voice fades and our view of the circus cuts away from the three rings. Instead of catching a glimpse of the floating torso, we are made to follow the Flutter Brothers. The Flutter Brothers walk out of the main floor, back behind the curtain. The bustling sounds of the circus die off now and we can overhear the conversation being held by the Flutter Brothers. The duo make their way over to a table with two chairs at it and they have a seat.:: Flutter Brother 1 – Wow, I can’t believe the ovation we got out there. It gets bigger with every town we go to! By the time the season is out, gosh, we’ll be celebrities! Flutter Brother 2 – Don’t get ahead of yourself there, my fluttery friend, we haven’t struck it rich yet. Flutter Brother 1 – I know, but did you hear those people? It’s like they actually believe we can fly! It’s like we’re some kind of superheroes or something! Flutter Brother 2 – But superheroes REALLY can fly. We can just flip around at unusually fast speeds. Could you imagine if we had the powers of a superhero? Flutter Brother 1 – I don’t know, it might be a little rough. After all, like that movie said, with great power comes great responsibility. Flutter Brother 2 – Oh well, there’s no point in discussing it. There’s no such thing as superheroes anyway We may as well get ready for our next act, we’ll have to be heading back out there soon. Flutter Brother 1 – You’re right, let’s go. ::The Flutter Brothers now get up from their chairs and begin walking back towards the curtains. As they get there, they peer out the sides to see what part of the show they’re up to. When they see that the Floating Torso has just finished up, they know that it is now their turn to return to action. The acrobats step through the curtain and back onto the main floor, where the crowd focuses their attention back on them. The ringmaster begins to introduce them again as they make their way back into the show.:: Ringmaster – Oh, did you think you saw all the acrobatics we had to offer? My friends, what you saw before was a mere taste of what the Amazing Flutter Brothers are capable of! In fact, if you cast your eyes to . . . ::Suddenly, there is a puff of smoke that engulfs both Flutter Brothers. Two beams of light, one gold and one silver, now shoot down from the top of the tent to where the brothers are standing, and that quickly, the lights disappear again. When they do, however, they take the Flutter Brothers with them. The smoke clears to reveal and empty spot on the floor where the two trapeze artists once stood. The crowd breaks loose in pandemonium, applauding the effect. Little do they know that this is not part of the show.::
