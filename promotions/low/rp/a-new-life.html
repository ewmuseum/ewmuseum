A New Life

12 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~{ Just over a week ago, an acrobat team from the circus known as the Amazing Flutter Brothers had their lives changed forever. For it was these two entertainers who were chosen to don the role of the Metallo Zords. Selected for their skill in martial arts and gymnastics, the Flutter Brothers were beamed into the secret headquarters of Bobbabu, a mystical, omnipresent visionary responsible for saving the world. Bobbabu chose the duo to take on the responsibility of protecting the planet Earth from the evil Queen Mogambu. Mogambu and her army, who had been raiding star system after star system, are now headed towards Earth in hopes of conquering the planet and harvesting their inhabitants. Now it is up to the Metallo Zords to stop her. When we last left our heroes, they were being acquainted with their new powers. After mastering the art of Zord summoning, as well as learning how to use their high-tech gear and living weapons, the Metallo Zords are now setting out to rebuild lives for themselves. Lives bound to saving the world from certain doom. }~~~ ::We open to a shot of a large, open meadow. The green grass seems to be well mowed although the land is no inhabited. A cool breeze blows on the hot summer day, stirring up some dirt. The focus now turns to the bright blue sky, void of many clouds. After a few seconds, two beams of light, one silver and one gold, streak through the air. The two beams hit the ground next to each other at the same exact speed. When the blinding lights die down to a glow, we can see the Metallo Zords standing where they hit. Our heroes look at each other, communicating on a subconscious level. They then turn, nod their helmet clad heads, and begin walking along a beaten path. As they make their way through the hilly terrain, the duo exchange words.:: Gold Ranger – I still cannot believe that we are now actually superheroes. This will make our lives so much better. Silver Ranger – No, Bobbabu said that we are not superheroes. Superheroes are made up characters for comic books, television programs, and feature films. We are galactic guardians, protectors of justice, and defenders of the planet Earth! Gold Ranger – And we even get to wear these neat helmets! ::The Metallo Zords continue walking through the meadow as their conversation grows more meaningful.:: Silver Ranger – I sure hope that we can deal with the responsibility that has been handed down to us. Judging by what we were told at headquarters, this Queen Mogambu sounds pretty evil. Gold Ranger – Not only that, but we have to worry about her evil henchmen. Bobbalu said she has a fear inducing army along with a virtually endless supply of ninja fighter servants called Ich Van Herbes. Silver Ranger – Ich Van Herbes?! That name alone sends chills down my spine. I can only prey that we are not in over our heads. Gold Ranger – Fear not, my silver sidekick, if we could not handle such a task, the job would not be ours. Silver Ranger – What you say is true, however, I am not your sidekick. We are peers, my gold clad chum. ::As the Metallo Zords continue to make their way over the hills and through the clearing, the subject of their conversation turns to a solemn remembrance of their lives that once were.:: Gold Ranger – I only wish that our lives of entertaining people in the circus were not over. I will miss our time making children laugh. Silver Ranger – Do not worry, Gold Ranger, without the Metallo Zords children would never laugh again. Our responsibility is greater now. We are responsible for not just the children at the circus, but for all children. For it is our job now to make sure no child, woman, or man is harmed. It is our job now. We are the guardians of Earth. Gold Ranger – And guard it we shall! I do not care how difficult it may be, we will not be deterred! I will not let anyone, no matter how evil and strong they are, hurt anyone! It is not acceptable! Silver Ranger – With mindsets like that, I would love to see Queen Mogambu even try to touch this planet. Boy will she be in for a shock. ::The Gold Ranger stops walking now, and sensing the stop of his partner, the Silver Ranger comes to a halt as well.:: Gold Ranger – Speaking of evil, did you know that we already have a villain trying to prevent us from protecting the planet? Silver Ranger – No, I was unaware. Who is this villain? Gold Ranger – They call him Fathom, and although he claims to be independent of foreign influences, I have a strong feeling he is a puppet of Queen Mogambu. Silver Ranger – Why is that? Gold Ranger – This Fathom fellow, he said that our cause was futile. He warned us that we were finished, that there was no changing the evil on Earth. He said that disease, famine, war . . . these things kill people every day and there was no changing that. Silver Ranger – Little does he know . . . little does he know. Gold Ranger – While right now Earth is plagued with these disturbing illnesses, it is not a cess pool as he put it. Earth is a planet worth saving, it’s a planet worth dying for. And if it comes to that, I will lay my life down for this planet. Silver Ranger – As would I. This Fathom you speak of does sound fairly evil himself. Gold Ranger – Oh, you have yet to hear the worst part. Silver Ranger – And what is that? Gold Ranger – He said that Queen Mogambu was . . . that she was . . . Silver Ranger – What’s wrong? What did he say? Gold Ranger – . . . a savior. Silver Ranger – A SAVIOR?! Does he know nothing? Queen Mogambu will destroy the world, and he calls her a savior? Something must be done, for he is surely an evil villain! Gold Ranger – Evil indeed, and if hypothesis proves true, a servant of Queen Mogambu as well. Silver Ranger – Something must be done! Gold Ranger – Something will be done, Silver Ranger. This Saturday night, I am going to confront him. This threat must be eliminated, for the sake of all mankind! ::As the Gold Ranger finishes, the Silver Ranger stops walking. Following suit, the Gold Ranger stops as well. The silver ranger motions forward, pointing at something. The Gold Ranger looks up and sees the circus at which they used to perform.:: Silver Ranger – There it is. And now it’s over. ::The Silver Ranger hangs his head in sorrow.:: Gold Ranger – We cannot go back, Silver Ranger. It’s time to move on. Silver Ranger – It’s time to save the world. ::The Metallo Zords turn their back to the circus from which they once came. They now cross their arms over their chests in an X fashion and beams of light engulf them. Seconds later, the beams shoot into the sky and the Metallo Zords are gone without a trace.::
