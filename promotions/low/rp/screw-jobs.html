Screw Jobs

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The logo of the Shadow Riders appears on the screen accompanied by a voice-over which says... “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The picture opens to a live shot from within the living room of the Shadow Riders’ South Beach home in Miami, Florida. Sitting on a large plush black leather sofa is none other then “The Vigilante” Luke Justice. Justice is sitting with his eyes trained on the screen of the big screen television in front of him. As he continues to stare unwaveringly at the screen, the front door of the house swings open as “Superstar” Eric Russo storms into the house slamming the door shut behind him. A scowl is etched on Russo’s face indicating that he is not in the best of moods. He continues towards Justice as he begins to speak.) Eric: “Can you believe this crap!?” (Russo plops down on the couch next to Justice who is still captivated by what is on the television.) Eric: “They’re trying to screw me over again.” (Still fixated on the television screen, Justice responds showing he is not even listening to what Russo is saying.) Luke: “Yeah . . . that’s good.” Eric: “You’re not even listening are you!? Luke: “Oh . . . really? That’s to bad.” Russo: “What the hell are you so interested in anyway?” (Russo glances over to the set and finally notices that Justice is enchanted by a lesbian sex scene taking place on the screen. Seeing this, Russo’s eyes become glued to screen as well as a smile creeps across his face. As the two Shadow Riders sit transfixed by what is taking place on the screen, Tristan Belmont enters the room.) Tristan: “Hey guys . . . what are you watching?” (Hearing no response from either man, Belmont walks behind the sofa and look at the screen to see for himself. Belmont stares at the screen intently as he becomes mesmerized by the lesbians as well. Uneasy over the quietness in the room containing the other Shadow Riders, Kage walks into the room to investigate. A look of concern fills Kage’s face as he enters the room only to find Russo, Justice, and Belmont staring at the television set. Kage shakes his head as he begins to speak.) Jason: “Not again.” (With that, Kage walks behind the televison set and grabs hold of the plug. With a swift tug, Kage pulls the plug out of the socket causing the picture on the televison screen to turn off with an electrical click. The other three Riders look up at Kage with a threatening glare as they speak collectively.) Collective: “What did you do!?” (Coyly Kage responds.) Jason: “You guys looked like zombies . . . I did it for your own good.” Eric: “But they were lesbians!” (Showing that his resolve for the situation, Kage responds.) Jason: “That may be so, but there is a time and a place for that and this is neither. You guys should be concentrating on this weeks Realm where once again they are going to try and screw over the Shadow Riders.” (Knowing that Kage is not going to reconsider the lesbian sex scene, Russo does his best to focus on the new topic at hand.) Eric: “Tell me about it. It all started with War Wounds. Trigger knew I was going to win the Realm title so what did he do? He goes out and hires some new guy to steal it from me. Now, he has me booked in a title match with that thief as the special guest referee. He’s gonna try and screw me out of my title.” (Kage walks around the television set and takes a seat on a red chair next to the couch as he responds.) Jason: “Well what did you expect? Did you really think he would let a Shadow Rider have a week off after putting on a display that damn near stole the entire show . . . especially considering the fact that you have a title now.” Eric: “I still don’t understand why I have to defend my title. For the past seven months, none of the champions had to defend at anything other then pay-per-views. Not only that, but did you notice what champions have to defend their belts on Realm?” (Justice perks up and joins into the conversation.) Luke: “I know, only the Shadow Riders have to defend their belts! What’s up with that!? Pax has his #1 contender facing him but is his belt on the line . . . no. On top of that, not only do I have to defend my baby, but I have to defend it against Kage. Trigger is such a choad! Why can’t he understand that he is not going to split us up?” (Listening to the conversation going on between the other Riders gets Belmont’s blood boiling. Angrily he cuts in.) Tristan: “I don’t know what you three are complaining about, at least you three actually have matches! I’m not even fucking booked on the card! Watch, Trigger is gonna have Matt Meyhu book me on Aftermath!” (Russo looks over his shoulder at Belmont.) Eric: “You know, I wouldn’t be surprised if he did get you booked on Aftermath. It would be just like him to try and hide the talent of a Shadow Rider by getting him on a show that nobody watches or even cares about. I mean when was the last time you heard about anything ever taking place on Aftermath that was worth while. Oh well, we’ll just have to do things the hard way and show them why no matter what they do, they will all still end up as nothing but another falling star that will be left in the dust of the Shadow Riders.” (The rest of the Riders nod in agreement with Russo’s statement as the shot fades back to the logo of the Shadow Riders with the usual “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.” voice-over before the picture goes to black.)
