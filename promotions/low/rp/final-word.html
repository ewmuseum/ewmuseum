Final Word

1 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Everything was dark. The interviewer shook wildly. He tried to stay calm, but he could not. Before him, shrouded in the darkness of the lightless room, towered a man of giant proportions, both physically and in status. The young interviewed had been sent out on this assignment, and at first he was very excited, but sooner than later, excitement turned to fear, and now that he sat on the cold and uncomfortable wooden chair, fear had turned to sheer horror. In front of him stood none other than Tristan Belmont, his back turned to him. On this cold night, Belmont contemplated the streets beneath this apartment room. Rain was splashing down against the window. A cold rainy night. Silence overtook the room. The interviewer was paralyzed by fear. It was not before long that a cold sweat established itself around the interviewer. Belmont’s black eyes continued to survey the surroundings beneath him. Locks of hair shrouded his vision. He did not respond to the obstruction, instead, he simply stood as a tower. And then, the interviewer’s greatest wishes came to fruition, when Tristan began talking. Within seconds, the interviewer had reached into his briefcase, pulled out his recorder and set it on ‘Record’] I still remember that night, as if it just had happened. Such an event is nothing that you tend to forget. It was as influential as death could be to me. In many ways, on that very night, I died. I had given my life to it. Everything that could possibly be given from me was. It had led me to the highest of highs, and the lowest of lows. I had even brought the cannon of a pistol to my head … because of it. If I could die for it, if I was ready to take the plunge into the darkness for it, it was for it to be forever shinning. My life would assure its stardom, or so I thought. That night came shortly after I had attempted to end it all for his creation. But before I could even start describing the pain that I felt that night, I must give prelude to it. It all started with him … him and his creation. He had created that empire, and I had given to him what he wanted. Peace of soul. Yet after I had cleared the nightmares from his dreams, after I had cleansed the dark from his light, he had abandoned me. I still don’t understand it. And without really understanding anything, I lifted the weapon to the side of my head, trembling. I sat in fear and in hate. I used to be devoted to him. I used to love him. No longer. My love, my devotion has all gone to serve the purpose of one selfish man. He had used me to cleanse him of his demons, but had fled when I had needed him to help me cleanse mine. The only way I could cleanse my demons, was to blow them out, and splatter them across the tiled floor. His words were calm and filled with sorrow. “All I ever wanted was this one thing. All I ever wanted was closure. I need to leave, Tristan, and I must never return.” He wanted closure, and I gave him his fuckin’ closure! What else did he want? I had drove him away, I had slain the demon, stabbed a sword in his head. There was no more demon, he had lost, he had to retreat and be forgotten. In victory, I smiled and thought it was all over. In victory, he showed me I was wrong. In victory, he took away my will to live. The interviewer Power had fleeted from his body. He never really explained why, all he told me was that the stress, the fear and the anger had consumed him and made him to be a wreck. It had left him alone, to die. In his darkest hour, I had grabbed his hand and helped him out, and he had promised to mold me into what he used to be. A Warrior. I could have asked for nothing better. I would help him, he would help me, and in the end, after I had set a term to all the wars that dwelled inside the walls of his empire, after I had destroyed all who stood as a threat to his will, I would find what I was looking for, serenity. That was the plan that was what I envisioned when he came to me on a dark night and talked to me. I remember when I took my head out of my hands and looked up at him. Something inside me said “This man will help me”. That something would prove to be wrong. Everything and every one would prove to be wrong. You know the rest of the story, don’t you? The training was hard and severe. He constantly told me what a he wanted me to be. He talked about his hardships, his challenges. Every time he spoke, I saw the fear in his eyes; I saw the torment that hovered above him. I never stopped to ask myself the only question I should have asked myself. “Do I want this to be me?”. I followed him blindly through his god damned training, where he molded me into a Warrior, into the combatant of evil that he needed to purge his torment with. He molded me into one thing … nothing! Now that I look back at it, I wish I had never shaken his god damned hand. A Warrior never was meant to succeed; it was always bound to fail by his command. He was nothing more than a depressed jack ass that could no longer do anything for himself, which is why I came into play. I got lost in his game, convinced that his pain was immense, convinced that once the threat was over, and once there were no more demons, he would soar high above the others, and dominate, as only a Warrior could. I was wrong. He made me into a Warrior. He made me into a paranoid, delusional and depressed individual that could no longer think for myself that could no longer handle the demons. I had fought them off for so long, and won the battles, but in the end, thanks to his “help”, I had lost the war, I had become just like he was. Just a shell. How could I be victorious in such circumstances? How could he think of me as a savior to all when he had destroyed me? How could I be a Warrior, ready to f ace all that passed my path, when my greatest enemy sat behind me as my friend? How could I fight when I was blinded by lies and false promises? I couldn’t. How could he think of me as that savior? How could he possibly look into my eyes and know that the war would be over, that I would be the one to put an end to it. How could he send me into a battle knowing what he had made me into? How could he look at me as his student? He didn’t. He never did. I was the weapon he used to get his empire back, plain and simple. There would be nothing else to me than that, and he made sure of it when he molded me into the suicidal freak that I became. I came so close to ending my life because of him. What did I want to end was not my own life however, no, it was what he had made my life become. I wanted to start a new page. With him gone, I could start crowding the inner regions of my gut with who I was, not with the crap he fed me. When he left, he left without his creation. When he left, he left without his armies. When he left, he left me alone, with no real intent. When he left, he left me high and dry, he left me to die. When he left, he left behind the one thing that I had always wanted When he left, he left Lights Out Wrestling behind. When he left, the real Demonic Warrior emerged inside of me. The greed, the power, the hate, the anger, it all rose inside of me within seconds one simple night. I knew then what I was truly. I was not a shield anymore, I was no longer the soldier Warrior, the one that lay at the mercy of his King on all accounts,. No longer was I a simple minded zombie. I had chased him out… Yes, that’s right, that night, when I put an end to his war, I put an end to him. The only thing is, I had not done it by my own will. In fact, that night, where I drove Mike Meyhu down to the ground and gave him the victory, a victory he never deserved by the way, I felt something else inside of me, I was not only trying to help out. That night, at the Pay Per View, the real Warrior began to come inside of me. I knew what I wanted. I acted upon it. But it was too late. The demons inside of me had already started, and the weakness that I had endured when I was with him had made me fall into the hands of depression. When he left, after the episode where I tried to blow the demons out, was when the Demonic Warrior came out. That was the night where Tristan Belmont, a soldier for a weak and pathetic lord, became a tyrant. Lights Out Wrestling was mine! Lights Out Wrestling WAS mine! I had all the power in the world in the palm of my hand. Nothing could stop me now. He was gone. And then, as soon as it came, it left, when he, wherever he was hiding, decided to shut down Lights Out Wrestling. He stole every thing I had. He took every thing that I had given, everything that I had earned, and just threw it out. He had won. Everything had slipped between my fingers. From having all, I found myself alone … with nothing. And then … She came… She came in, and she took the empire that I helped create, and decided to call herself master of it. And the people that I made, all those lowly superstars that once were cheered and jeered in the halls of my empire; they all followed her like blind sheep. They all accepted her as their savior, forgetting who the true savior of the damned land was. They forgot who chased away all the darkness, they forgot me. How could they forget me? They owe everything to ME! No one else. They don’t understand exactly who I am, do they? They don’t understand that they were dying without their knowledge of it. I was the breath of life within all of their lungs; I was the one that kept them high when they should have crumbled. I was the unknown guardian to all of their fates. They should have all perished, but I made it otherwise, I saved them all. I gave life to the dead, and now, the living watches me die? No, I refuse to be discredited like this. I am Tristan Belmont. I am the man who made LOW … I am … I am. I am nothing anymore…
