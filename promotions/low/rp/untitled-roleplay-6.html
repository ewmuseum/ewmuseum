Another Falling Star :: The screen cuts from the current scene and is replaced by the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. The scene opens inside of a large conference hall jam packed with different reporters flanked by camera crews. A door at the front of the room slowly opens to reveal Eric Russo. Russo is nicely dressed in rather expensive looking suit. We also notice that Russo is supporting himself on a pair of crutches due to the recent knee injury that has indefinitely put him on the sidelines. We can tell by his demeanor that he is not in his normal frame of mind. Instead he seems to be taking on an overly professional one instead. Russo pauses for a moment to survey the room full of people awaiting his words before starting towards a podium atop a small stage at the front of the room. Russo steps onto the stage using his crutches to support his weight and makes his way behind the podium. Once there, Russo looks out upon the gathered masses who are now on their feet pulsing with anticipation. Slowly he begins allowing the tension to build. He lowers his head positioning his mouth next to the microphone and begins. :: Russo: Thank you all for coming. If you could all please be seated, then I shall begin. :: Russo stops as he waits for everyone to take their seat and get settled. He then continues. :: Russo: LOW news team, camera crew, remaining members of the Shadow Riders, and all of those people watching out there in TV land, for the first time ever, I the leader of the Shadow Riders, have decided that this meeting was needed to discuss the state of the Shadow Rider faction as it pertains to it’s future in the LOW. :: Russo quickly glances down in front of him as if reading from a pre-written script. He then goes on with his speech. :: Russo: I act on my role as leader of the Shadow Riders at a time of great consequence. As such, it is my duty and my duty alone to decide the fate of a name that I have single handedly made great. I however will not shrink back from this challenge to my legacy. No, I will push on and I will answer every danger and enemy that threatens the name that I have put on the map. :: Russo clears his throat then continues. :: Russo: In these confusing days ahead, you can all rest assured that what I do, I do for the good of the Shadow Riders as a whole.' :: Russo glances down in front of himself once again before looking back into the camera and continuing. :: Russo: Another matter that has come to my attention is that a certain renegade duo has decided to begin proclaiming themselves as the 'New' Shadow Riders. Yes . . . yes, I do realize that it is a very lame attempt to rip off the true Shadow Riders but I will still address it none the less. :: Russo goes on in an almost official sounding tone. :: Russo: I would like to take this opportunity to make the public aware that the true Shadow Rider name has absolutely no affiliation with cheap imposters that have decided to blatantly rip it off. :: Russo adjusts the crutches under his arms before continuing. :: Russo: The next thing that I would like to address is the sudden disappearance of 'Vigilante' Luke Justice. Well let me just assure all of you that Luke is doing just fine and will be making a return to the ring in the near future to continue his career under the Shadow Rider banner. :: Russo clears his throat once again before going on. :: Russo: It is now that we begin the Q and A session of this interview. :: As all of the reporters begin to clamor in hopes of getting the first question, Russo randomly points out into the crowd. His finger lands on one of the male reporters standing at the front of the group. Quickly, the reporter states his question. :: Male Reporter 1: Well I’m sure the question on everyone’s mind has to be what are your thoughts on the surprising actions of Jason Kage at the pay-per-view. How does this major shock effect the future of the Shadow Riders stable as a whole? Russo: Jason Kage leaving the Shadow Riders will not have any effects on the group whatsoever. I’m the one who built the name of the group in the first place and I will continue to do just that with or without Jason Kage. In fact, I am already in talks with a number of different 'replacements' for Kage and they are already looking like they will have no problem filling the spot. Male Reporter 2: You have recently injured your knee, has your doctor given you any kind of time frame as to when you will be able to get back in the ring? Russo: As of right now, my doctor and myself are working on a day to day basis regarding my injury. What that means for my in ring future is that it’s all up in the air at this point in time. There’s a chance that I may wake up tomorrow morning and my knee could be good to go with little more then a brace to keep it steady. Then again it could also worsen enough to keep me out of the ring until the pay-per-view. We’re just gonna have to wait and see what happens. Female Reporter 1: Mr. Russo, it is being said by many that your knee injury is nothing more then a put on so that you do not have to step into the ring with Jason Kage. Is there any truth to those claims? Russo: I’m insulted that you would even suggest that I would fake a serious injury just to get myself out of a match. Not once in my career have I ever dodged a challenge from anyone. I’m a contender through and through and I will face anyone, anywhere, anytime. I thought you would have learned that by now from my Realm title run, during which I took it to everyone they put me in the ring with. Female Reporter 2: Many people have been talking about how your ego has gotten way out of proportion in recent weeks. Do you think that this could be the cause of all of your problems? Russo: You know, I don’t know where all of these people get the idea that I have an ego problem. Every time I open my mouth I speak nothing but the truth. I do nothing other then tell people what I have accomplished. What’s wrong with that? Why should I censor myself just because I’ve done more then any two people in the LOW locker room put together. It’s not my fault that I was born better them. It’s not my fault that their skills don’t match up to mine. It’s reality and it’s something that they have to learn to deal with because I don’t ever plan on stopping. Male Reporter 2: Do you hold Kage’s actions at Shell Shock against him? Russo: Well of course there’s gonna be some hard feelings on my part with the way he turned his back on me after all I’ve done for him. It’s not like it’s something I won’t get over though. Kage is easily replaceable and I plan on doing just that very soon. :: As Russo finishes up answering the question, he waves his hand signaling that the Q and A session is now over. Once the room settles, Russo leans into the microphone and looks out to the gathered press as he begins speaking. :: Russo: That’s all the time I have tonight folks. Thank you for your time and goodnight. :: With that, the screen once again fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders before fading out to black. :: Show URL: http://
