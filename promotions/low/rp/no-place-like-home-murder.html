--NO PLACE LIKE HOME- MURDER-- [Scene: Inside of the Burke Residence. Jason Ortiz, hell bent on destruction, and on CRACK- he stumbles around his bedroom. The lights dimmed down. Blinds shut. The camera moves in closer as they see the stubble starting to form on Jason’s face. Ortiz gives out a harsh smirk.] It had continued to stare at Jason. Ortiz had glanced at it a few times, not thinking of anything but himself. He had no control of his body functions anymore. He tried to move, but the same thing happened, he didn’t have the strength to do it. Jason Ortiz cringed. He knew this was his only chance to make himself look like he had some sort of “PUSH” in the Burke Residence. As he watched closely. Watching the small little kitten’s every move; Jason began to wonder- think, hard. Jason closed his left eye; the pain was getting to him. Jason Ortiz was a little nervous right now; his arm’s shaking violently. He stopped. He would glance out the small pain glass window a few times- just to see the sun’s raise blast him in his bloodshot eyes. He opened his left eye again. Jason knew that today was going to be another great day. With the sun, as hot as it was, shinning down upon his brow, he could only think of one thing. Ratings. Larry Vaughn has been pushing Jason promptly about making the show great- but these past few shows the Ratings have staid the same. Why? What has caused this to happen? Was it Jason Ortiz? Was it the Burke family? Who knows? The only thing Jason wanted more was his “self-pride“. Looking back on the situation, he wanted this to be the biggest show of them all. Not only did he want it BIG, he wanted much more. Extravagant. Spectacular. Those were just some of the words that were flashing in the mind of Ortiz. “This Crack is starting to get to me. UGH!” snapped Jason Ortiz, bending his head down then lifting it back up- with the blood now rushing to the top of his head. Somehow Jason was trying to manage not getting into that much trouble today. He had promised himself that the wouldn’t. He had lied. It had become instantly quite inside of the small bedroom of Jason Ortiz. He moved around the bed, grabbing onto the wood bed frame with his large palms. Jason Ortiz clinched his teeth as he made his way around the small “JAIL CELL.” That’s what he had continued to call his bedroom, the JAIL CELL. Most of this day was taken up by the time. Jason had hardly moved an inch since smoking the small bag of Crack that he had found- nesting in the bottom of his gym bag. The time was ticking down. The day was flying by, and yet, there was no sign of the Burkes. Pretty Boy Jason Ortiz smiled largely. He knew that he didn’t want to talk to the Burkes- not on this very day. This was his time. This was Ortiz’s fucking time. Finally, after much consideration, and much thought in his small minded brain- Jason had figured a way to make this show great. The success of the show was something Jason didn’t want to look back on. A drug addict living in a house full of deranged Church Members. It made him sick. In the past, Jason Ortiz had tired to admit himself to a DRUG REHAB facility. Nothing happened. Hell, he couldn’t even pull himself to leave his own Beach House in the past. As a small drip of sweat rolled down his eyebrow, Jason licked his lips. Another drop did the same thing, only this time it ripped on his nose- then fell on the white carpet. Moving around the room for a few seconds, he had lost sight of the small Burke kitten. Ortiz wanted to feel it. He wanted to pet the son of a bitch- yet, it wasn’t in sight. What had happened to it? All Jason wanted to do was be friends with it. Could it love such a CRACK HEAD like him? Who knows? Only time could find out. Jason’s voice broke the silence in the room as he frantically searched under the bed for the kitten. Jason Ortiz shook his head. “Where the fuck are you,” demeaned Ortiz. “Come here you little cock sucker!” Jason shook his head for a second time. “What the fuck happened to him? One minute he was sitting on my bed- the next, he was gone.” Jason Ortiz muttered- his eyes still bloodshot from the drug’s he had smoked earlier. “Unbelievable” He turned and walked back towards his desk that was at the corner of the small bedroom. Unable to think of his next move, mostly because the CRACK was getting to him, Jason leaned against the desk. Holding on for his own life, Ortiz grabbed the top of his sweaty forehead. He pressed his fingers deep inside of his skull, feeling the pain inside of his body. What would the Burke's do if they came home and found out that Jason had did something with the kitten. Would he be sent free? Would the “NO PLACE LIKE HOME” show be over- done? This was another act that Jason would have to pull off, to save his own fucking ass. There hadn’t been much activity in the Burke home, these past few days. A few words were said- nothing really big or harsh. This past week, Ortiz had tried to admit to himself, and to the Burke’s that indeed- he did need some kind of help. Jason didn’t know what kind of help he needed, he just wanted some. Sitting in the hard wood chair again, his fingers still pressed in his head, it was hard for Pretty Boy to think. He smacked himself a few times in the face. A red spot was starting to form on his forehead. He had remembered the day’s when he hadn't have to worry about a thing. He had left most of his problems with his former girlfriend, Kelly. As the days had passed in the Burke Residence, Jason was starting to feel some kind of remorse for his dead girlfriend. He had loved the bitch. Sure she made his life a living hell- and vice versa, but so what. Every relationship does that, right? What was wrong with Jason Ortiz? He had it made. A beautiful house. A beautiful, caring girlfriend. His stash of the dank FIRE GREEN. What happened to his life that he once loved? No longer was Jason afraid of the past. He was more afraid of what the future was holding for him. What would be his next move? Was the drugs taken over his body now? Jason Ortiz knows that his life isn’t the greatest, and as the days pass in this Burke household, god knows what can happen to the Realm Champion. Jason looked down and stared at the small kitten. “HOLY FUCKING SHIT!” nodded Ortiz. There was some life inside of Jason’s large body. Jason Ortiz drew a huge smile as he continued to stare at the kitten. “God I am so fucking glad to see you...” He grimaced, thinking. “You hadn't died- you little bastard. You have saved my life, and my shot at becoming the man of the BURKE house.” Jason stood still and leaned back towards the desk again. He seemed a little uneasy about what had happened here. Without saying a word, Pretty Boy clutched the small kitten in his large arms. He squeezed with all his might. Feeling the cat’s fur rubbing against his dirty arm was a thing he had missed in his past. Jason ignored his feelings. “All right,” he muttered gratefully. “Now I won’t lose my ass over you!” Still using all his strength to hold the kitten in his arms, Jason lifted his large palm up and pressed it against the soft cat’s head. It was smooth. Glancing down at the small kitten, Jason could see that something wasn't right. Something wasn’t right at all. Releasing some pressure from his arms, Ortiz knew he was FUCKED at that very moment. Dropping the kitten on the floor, Jason backed up- in shock. The kitten was fucking dead. “Oh,” Jason mumbled very frantically, “FUCK NO! FUCK NO! OH, FUCK NO!” Those were the only words that Jason could think of at a time like this. What was he going to do? Looking down at the lifeless body of the kitten, as it laid there- dead on the floor, Jason had to think of something, quick. With his large shoe on the ground, Jason lifted it up and kicked the kitten a few times. It rolled over. Jason cried, knowing that his time here in the Burke home was over. DONE. “It can’t just fucking die on me! It just fucking cant. It won’t! Wake up you stupid bastard!” Ortiz sighed loudly. The sound of his cracked voice fluttered in the room, as it bounced against each wall of his own bedroom. Jason swallowed harshly. “UGH! UGH!” As Jason Ortiz leaned down; his knees pressing on the hard white carpet, he lifted the small- brutal kitten in his hand. He rubbed it a few times, making sure that it wasn’t dead after all, but the fact had still remained. It was fucking dead. Standing back up, with the lifeless kitten in his massive palm, he had to think of something before the Burkes came home. It was too late. “JASON!” yelled Tyler, from down stairs. “JASON ARE YOU UP THERE?” He didn’t feel like moving at a time like this. What was he going t do. Time was short, and the dead kitten was still laying there in the palm of his own hand. He was holding the evidence. Jason Ortiz was a murder! “Oh fucking god! Oh fuck no!” Jason said, grinning now. “I have to get rid of the kitten’s body- and I have to do it fast!” The sound of the young Burke child’s foot steps were becoming closer, as Jason realized that Tyler was making his way up the wood stares. Frantically searching for a way to get out of this one, Jason hurried towards the window of his bedroom. He flung it open, watching the window blow inside of the room. As the large, cold gust of wind blew inside of the room it messed up Jason’s hair, and all the white papers laying on his oak desk. Jason didn’t give a shit right now. He held the kitten in his hand. He fired back, and launched the cat out the window. It landed in the bushes. “RIGHT ON TARGET!” For the first time, Jason was safe. Tyler came stumbling in, with a dazed look on his face. Jason studied the young Burke child’s face. He was still a little shocked at what just happened. “What have you been up too Jason?” asked Tyler. “You probably didn’t have much fun when I was gone- eh?” Jason looked into the boy’s eyes. He stepped back a few second- catching his breath. What was he going to tell the Burke child? He had found the words he was looking for...“Nothing,” Jason Ortiz proclaimed. “NOT A GOD DAMN THING TYLER, WHY?” [Scene Fades]
