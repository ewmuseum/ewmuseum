History 101- Hell's Winter

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. The scene opens to a present day shot from inside the large empty apartment where Russo sits across the table from Jerry the interviewer. Jerry holds the microphone of his recorder directed at Russo as he continues with his story.) Russo: “As always, Kage and I made sure we left the NRW with a bang.” (The picture fades out with squiggly lines as me enter yet another of Russo’s flashbacks. As the scene focuses, we are joined by a voice over.) Russo (VO): “Early in the day, Kage and myself left the NRW arena for good with little expiation other then a few vague hints on what we were about to do. Over the course of our last few weeks under the employment of the NRW, we made a new connection that would take a chance on us and welcome us into his federation with open arms. This move worked out better then we could have ever expected as we soon found out that we would get to work with more then one familiar face.” (The scene opens to a shot from the past. Russo and Kage are sitting on a couch inside of the Shadow Riders’ private jet. Russo is wearing a black sleeveless Shadow Riders’ t-shirt tucked into a pair of black jeans. He has the NRW US title over one shoulder and his Superstar title over the other. The NRW world title lying on the couch between Kage and himself. Kage is wearing long black jeans and the same Horsemen t-shirt only with sleeves. N the foreground, we can also see a large potato sack which appears to be moving, sitting in front of them. Russo kicks the sack which stops the moment. He then looks into the camera in front of him as he begins speaking fro the first time in the TXW.) Russo: 'What a difference a day makes. Yesterday, Jason and myself were headlining Wrestlepalooza 2. The biggest event of the year in the NRW. Today, the NRW’s two biggest stars.' (Russo pauses to rethink his thoughts.) Russo: 'Hell strike that, make it the only stars of the NRW, have decided to show up here and tell you exactly why we came to the NRW in the first place. You know, I almost feel bad for the guy we used to work for. I mean he really had no idea did he? He actually believed that Kage and myself were in the NRW because we wanted to be.' (Russo’s voice cuts in through a voice over.) Russo (VO): “I was on that night. As usual, I managed to single handedly get us over with the fans fight from the first moment they laid their eyes on me.” (The scene continues as Kage cuts in on screen. We listen as he speaks.) Kage: 'Wanted to be in the NRW, now that’s a laugh.' Russo: 'Tell me about it. Well anyway, the reason we came to the NRW was simply to destroy it. Why you ask, because we could.' (Russo cuts in through a voice over once again.) Russo (VO): “Now some have called me heartless and uncaring for comments like these in the past. I however would like to set the record straight when I say that my comments are neither of these things. It’s just that my comments are realistic, and most people can’t understand that. So as the saying goes, people fear what they don’t understand.” (The scene on the screen continues as Kage begins speaking again.) Kage: 'Don’t forget the money.' Russo: 'Yes, and the money. Were not talking about that piece of crap that you tried to pass off as a pay check either. Oh no, someone paid us and they paid us very well to do what we did to the NRW.' Kage: 'Should we tell him who? I want to see his face when he hears it.' Russo: 'Just hold on, let’s make him wait. Let him sweat a little.” (The scene flashes as it switches back to the present day. Jerry looks on at Russo as he continues his story.) Russo: “Things however did not go exactly to plan.” (Jerry quickly perks up.) Jerry: “Wha . . . What do you mean? What happened?” (Russo smiles at the interest that the young reporter is showing for his story. He goes on.) Russo: “Less then a week after our arrival, the TXW inexplicably closed it’s doors for the final time. When this happened, fingers were immediately pointed at Kage and myself as the main suspects behind the sudden closing of the federation. For the first time, I found myself without a federation to call home.” Jerry: “What did you guys do?” (Russo shakes his head as he responds.) Russo: “That’s just it Jerry. During that time, the Shadow Riders did the worst thing they could . . . absolutely nothing. You see Jerry, anyone who wrestles under the Shadow Rider name, does so for competition and challenge. Without anything there to work for, Kage and myself lost our purposes and desire to wrestle.” (Jerry worriedly pushes forward with his line of questioning.) Jerry: “Oh no . . . did you guys ever come back from it?” (Russo lets a little smirk slide across his face as he continues.) Russo: “If it wasn’t for me, we wouldn’t have. I would not let the Shadow Rider name disappear so quietly though. Every chance I had, I worked and I pushed until I could feel that old fire coursing through my veins once again. I actively began looking for somewhere for the Shadow Riders to continue and build their legacy. It was a long search, but it was one that would pay off in the end.” (Jerry cuts in with another question.) Jerry: “But what about Kage? What happened to him?” Russo: “Well when I finally found that new home for the Shadow Riders that I had been searching for, I picked up the phone and gave Kage a call. Now admittedly, this was a large mistake on my part. Here it was, my chance to finally get back on track with my singles career and I ruin it. I ruin it for something as trivial as friendship. It was a mistake however that I would not make again.” (Jerry pipes up.) Jerry: “So where is it that you two ended up?” Russo: “We ended up in a federation that was just starting. Strangely enough, the man who ran this federation was the same one who ran TXW during our all too short stay there. He was calling this new venture . . . LOW.” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
