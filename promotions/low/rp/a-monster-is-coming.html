A Monster is Coming...

14 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A monster is coming to the LOW. A monster so evil, he’ll send chills up and down your spine. A monster so sinister he’ll make parents lock up their young children and throw away the key. But, this man wasn’t intended to be a monster. Through eight years of abuse, he was slowly molded into one. He never intended to murder his foster family, found guilty and pleaded insane. He never intended to be locked away in a psych ward. That damned psych ward. He never fit in that place. In his view, he was the sane one, the rest were completely out of their minds. SPRINGFIELD BOY CHARGED WITH THREE COUNTS OF MURDER! SPRINGFIELD BOY FOUND GUILTY ON MURDER CHARGES! SPRINGFIELD BOY PLEADS INSANITY! SPRINGFIELD BOY SENTENCED TO A LOCAL PSYCH WARD! Yeah, those were a few headlines back in 1995. I, Simon A. Kreed, brutally murdered my foster family---the Kreeds---and raped the mother and sister. Since I was a minor at the time, they couldn’t charge me as an adult. I was fifteen when those murders took place. They all deserve to die, after what they did to me. I didn’t do anything to deserve all the abuse I went through. I was a poor, defenseless boy, being at the wrong place at the wrong time. Hell, Steven found it convenient to beat me for no apparent reason. Apparently, I was some punching bag to him, until he told me the truth--- He raped my mother some years ago to produce me. All the pain that I was suffering faded away. The pain congealed into hate. The next thing I found myself doing was leveling Steven with a right hook, dislocating my jaw. Afterwards, I found myself lying on the basement floor, my body wracked with pain. The bastard threw me down the stairs, landing back-first on the concrete below. I was lucky to not have any broken bones. When I was fifteen, I had enough of the abuse! I went into the kitchen, took out a butcher knife, hacked Steven’s head in two while he sat in his chair watching TV, and finally cut his body into six pieces and piled them up on the middle of the room. One down, two to go. Next was Rachel, a tall, fat bitch who was no better than her lovely husband. She never really abused me physically, except for a few slaps here and there and I disobeyed her, no, she sexually abused me. She made me to horrible things. I buried that clever in her back, brutally raped her and snapped her neck. I threw her fat body on top of her husband. Two down, one to go. Emily is next. Then... Sigh... Then there was Emily, my foster sister. She was the only one that was nice to me. She and I had a healthy relationship. She was the sister I never had. As she gotten older, I developed sexual feelings for her. I used to watch her as she had phone sex with her boyfriends, imaging myself pleasing her in everyway that I could. She would always catch me spying on her, calling me a “fucking pervert.” Heh...yeah, those were the days. But she could never help me in my time of need. Where were you, Emily? Where were you when Steven beat me? Where were you after the sexual torture Rachel inflicted? Where were you?! You had to die along with them. But I hesitated. I couldn’t really bring myself to it, but I had to, and I’m sorry. I couldn’t rape you, but I killed you, and I’m sorry. I’m sorry that I had to take your life away from you. I know you were going to talk if you caught me. I had to cover my own ass. Finally, everyone is dead, my nightmares are over. But there was one thing I had to do. I had to turn myself in. This doesn’t make sense. I could get away with everything. But something told me to turn myself in. Those damn voices made me do it! I couldn’t deny them! Three counts of murder, I did them all! The voices made me do it! INSANE! INSANE! I’ve gone crazy! I’m insane! I’m psychotic! I am Psymon! Will I ever be free?
