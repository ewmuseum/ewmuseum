Kidnapping Kaos Pt. 2

12 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~{ The last time we saw the Metallo Zords, the metallic heroes were high above the beach as they rode a Ferris Wheel on a peer at the Jersey Shore. While they were enjoying their vacation, there were shadows of evil lurking in the background. Back home, Thin n' Trim Tammy and the gang were enjoying a day at the mall, wondering how their metal clad friends were doing. It was during this time that Pleasantly Plump Patti fell into a trance at the hands of the evil Queen Mogambu and her vile henchman the Platinum Ranger. Leaving a trail of salt water taffies on the ground, the Platinum Ranger led the weight challenged Patti through a maze of illusion as he tricked her into entering a portal which brought her to Mogambu's Moon Palace. Once in Mogambu's liar, she was cast into the dungeon, where she would be held hostage. With one of their friends in danger, it would be a safe bet that the Metallo Zords may very well be lured away from their vacation. Little do they know that if they succumb to saving Patti, they would be playing right into Queen Mogambu's hands. }~~~ ::We open to a scene of the exterior of a large white building, molded to look like an Indian masterpiece. Our view soon allows us to see the large red lettering on the front of the building that reads 'Trump Taj Mahal'. We cut to a door within the building now, with the numbers 758 above the peep hole. Our view is cut to inside of the room, showing us the Metallo Zords, each sitting on a separate bed. The Silver Ranger sits on the bed closest to us, and the Gold Ranger leans off the edge of the bed on the right. The two heroes begin to exchange comments about their day.:: Silver Ranger - Wow, I cannot believe that our vacation is already almost over. It is a shame we did not get to spend more time here, for I am enjoying myself. ::An exhausted Gold Ranger responds in his usual robotic tone.:: Gold Ranger - I am having a good time as well. I never thought a vacation would succeed in easing our minds and spirits, but Bobbabu was right. This was very therapeutic. Silver Ranger - At least we still have a few days left here at the sunny beach. It seems like every moment we have here is more fun than the last. Gold Ranger - Yes, but I do not know if there is any way for us to top today's bungee jump. Silver Ranger - Ah yes, the bungee jump. That was ever so much fun. Let us not forget about the man we gave money to on the boardwalk. That was another highlight of this trip. Gold Ranger - There is nothing like the satisfaction of helping those in need. I sure help that our donation helped him out sufficiently. He seemed grateful. Silver Ranger - Do not be silly, I am sure he was aided significantly by the Brazilian coins you gave him. Gold Ranger - I suppose that is so. I hope that . . . ::The Gold Ranger's speech is cut off by a beeping sound. The Zords look at each other as their vacation is brought to a screeching halt by the sound of their wrist comlinks going off. Reluctantly, the Silver Ranger raises his wrist to his mouth and answers his.:: Silver Ranger - Hello, Silver Ranger here. ::From the other end, the familiar voice of Bobbabu is heard.:: Bobbabu - Rangers, there has been a disaster here at home. It is imperative that you return immediately. We need you. Silver Ranger - Is there not a way this could wait until tomorrow. I am tired like you never knew, Bobbabu. Gold Ranger - Yes, I need to rest my feet. I have blisters from this missized shoe, Bobbabu. ::Bobbabu responds to them through the watch in an extremely serious tone.:: Bobbabu - This is not a time for jokes. Report to me immediately, both of you two. ::Sadly, the Metallo Zords stand up and grasp their belt buckles. With two flashes of Gold and Silver light, the Zords disappear out of the hotel room. Our scene now cuts to the inside of the Metallo Zords command center, where Bobbabu's head floats in a glass tube. After a moment, two beams of light shoot down in the room and then disappear. Left in their places are the Metallo Zords. The Zords stand in front of Bobbabu and wait to be filled in.:: Bobbabu - I am glad you came, you're spirits are honorable and true. Gold Ranger - This had better be good, you floating head you. What is the story, Bobbabu? Bobbabu - It appears as though the evil Queen Mogambu has once again planted her seeds of scum and villainy. Silver Ranger - I tell you, she is as evil as a shrew! Bobbabu - Yes, evil as a shrew indeed. It seems as though one of your friends has succumb to her wretched power and was kidnapped. Gold Ranger - No! One of our friends? Tell us, who? Bobbabu - I direct your attention to the Orb of Knowledge. Well, don't just stand there, look through. ::The Metallo Zords turn around to face the Orb behind them. In it they see Pleasantly Plump Patti eating salt water taffies off of the mall floor. She continues to scarf them down until the trail runs out, landing her in front of a large taffy machine. Unable to resist, she tries to get into the machine, only to be sucked into a portal set in place by the evil Platinum Ranger.:: Silver Ranger - I do not understand. How could what I am seeing be true, Bobbabu? Bobbabu - Look into the Orb again. Mogambu and the Platinum Ranger orchestrated this entire scheme in order to get closer to you. ::The Zords look back at the Orb to see a picture of Pleasantly Plump Patti chained and shackled to a wall in Queen Mogambu's dungeon. The Platinum Ranger stands next to her, assuring them that this is legitimate. The Zords turn away from the orb and look back at Bobbabu.:: Bobbabu - Yes, it is true. Patti has been enslaved, and if you're not careful, you could be too. Silver Ranger - We must save her, that much we must do, Bobbabu! Gold Ranger - Yes, before they cook her in a stew! Bobbabu - So be it. Be sure that you . . . ::Bobbabu stops speaking for a second and looks at the Orb of Knowledge in horror.:: Bobbabu - Turn back around! Look at the Orb, the market place is askew! ::The Metallo Zords pivot to look at the screen. There we see a picture of the local market being torn apart by an army of Queen Mogambu's foot soldiers known as the Ich Van Herbes. Seeing this, the Zords agree they must take action.:: Silver Ranger - Gold Ranger, hurry, we must stop this heinous act! Gold Ranger - Yes, let us be on our way to end such treachery! ::The Metallo Zords put their hands on their belt buckles and are carried away by two beams of light. What will they be met with when they arrive at the market place? Will the Ich Van Herbes get the better of them? Will our heroes be able to save Pleasantly Plump Patti from certain doom? Will the Platinum Ranger be included in the next installment? Does Bobbabu have a body somewhere? All this and more, next time with the Metallo Zords!::
