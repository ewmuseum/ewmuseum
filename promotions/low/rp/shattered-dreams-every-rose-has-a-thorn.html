Shattered Dreams- Every Rose has a Thorn

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. Slowly the logo fades out to black. For a moment, nothing. Then the sound of Russo’s voice speaks out as he launches into a voice over.) Russo (VO): “The world as I knew it was spinning around me. I was having trouble keeping myself grounded in reality, a trait that I had prided myself on in the past. Was I really content with all of this material wealth? Hell yes I was content with being able to buy whatever I wanted. Could I really put the LOW and my storied past with Jesse Jamester behind me? Of course I could. Think about it, do you really think I would miss the grueling and hectic life that went along with the job of being a pro wrestler? I was more then happy to sit around and get paid for doing nothing, but as they say . . . “all good things must come to an end.” and this is the beginning of where my good thing came to an end.” (The scene opens to the disco room inside of Russo’s new house. The room is filled with dozens of party goers and guests. The room pulses with the beat of the music as the guests gyrate and dance on the professionally installed dance floor. One of the guests at the front of the room turns to the rest of the crowd and calls out to them.) Guest #1: “Everybody, Eric's on t.v.! Hey everyone look, it's Eric!” Woman: “Oh honey, here's that interview you did on T.V.!” (The girl now begins to call out to the guests.) Woman: “Shut off the music! Shut it off! Shut off the music!” (She pauses for a moment as the room quiets. She then continues.) Woman: “Everyone we're going to watch Eric on the T.V.! Now sit down, sit down on the floor! Turn around...” (The guests all turn the attention to a large projection television at the front of the room. The scene on the television set is taking place inside of a news studio. A very clean and pressed news anchor begins to speak from his seat behind a polished wooden desk.) News Anchor: “American Time News Magazine turns its probing eye on Eric Russo, creator of the Shadow Riders cartoon. That little foray into the animation industry has managed to sell over ten billion dollars worth of merchandise in just a few short months.” (The scene now cuts to what appears to be a very interesting interview. Eric Russo sits in a chair across from a neatly dressed Barbra Walters. She begins her interview.) Barbra Walters: “Mr. Russo, you've become a millionaire overnight. Who are you?” Russo: “Who is Eric Russo? Eric is a complex personality, as are most of the small breed of modern day renaissance millionaires.” (Strangely, the interview cuts off as the shot returns to the news anchor sitting behind his desk. He begins to speak in a serious tone.) News Anchor: “We had planned to show you the entire Eric Russo interview. However when we returned to our studio, our news department informed us of a sensational development in the Russo story. It seems that an irate group of citizens led by the celebrity artist of his very own comic, Mr. Josh Daniels has filed a class action suit against Mr. Russo and his cartoon. Here's what Mr. Daniels had to say at a press conference.” (The scene on the television set switches to one of Josh Daniels, co-creator of the Shadow Riders’ cartoon, sitting in front of a room of reporters. As the noise in the room quiets down, Daniels leans towards the microphone and begins to speak.) Josh Daniels: “When the Shadow Riders cartoon came out, I thought it was the greatest thing ever, and I invested all the time I had in it. And this is the result.” (Daniels removes his pair of dark glasses to reveal a pair of crossed eyes. He continues.) Daniels: “The little color scheme for that characters, that your very own Mr. Russo came up with, well it seems that your eyes are constantly drawn to it and you end up cock-eyed. Now as an artist, I am constantly using my eyes and this cartoon has caused irreparable harm to my career. For an example, let me show you a clip from my attempt to transfer my career over to real film. It was here where my faulty depth perception kept me from yelling cut at the proper time.” (A quick scene from a movie of a little red sports car speeding off a cliff is shown. We can hear Daniels yell from off screen just after the car goes over the edge.) Daniels: “Cut!” (The scene then quickly switches back to the press conference where Daniels continues.) Daniels: “If I had yelled cut on time, those actors would be alive today. That's why I am spearheading the ten million dollar class action suit against Mr. Russo and his irresponsible selling of a cartoon that he didn't even allow us to test on prisoners. Thank you.” (Back in Russo’s house, one of his many guests turns to him and begins to speak.) Guest #2: “Looks like the parties over dude.” (With that the man gets out of his seat and begins to exit the room. One by one the rest of the crowd begins to follow.) Russo: “Hey wait a minute, where are you going?” (The guest from the party turns and responds in a very honest and to the point manner.) Guest #2: “You don’t have anymore money. I'm going to get in touch with that Daniels guy. He’s the rich one now.” (The man turns and follows the rest of the guests out of the room. As the guests leave, Russo chases after them, calling them back.) Russo: “Hey wait . . . I've got some change upstairs. We'll get some potato chips, we'll make it a less formal thing.” (Russo turns to his newest girlfriend who is sobbing on the floor.) Russo: “Honey, why the gloom? It's not the end of the rainbow! I'm Eric Russo, superstar. This is no big deal . . . this is a parking ticket to me, only instead of five dollars, it's ten million.” (The woman on the floor looks up at Russo with a tear in her eye.) Woman: “I don't care about you losing all your money and property, it's the fact that I’m losing all of your money and property too!” (Russo looks back to her. For the first time, he realizes what this could mean to his future. Doing his best to reassure this woman, he responds.) Russo: “Don’t worry we're not gonna lose the stuff, this is America. I’m going to receive a fair trial from an impartial jury.” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
