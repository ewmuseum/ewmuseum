Metallic Mayhem Pt. 6

8 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~{ Last time we met up with everyone’s friendly neighborhood Metallo Zords, they had seemingly met their match. When they were captured by a band of evil Ich Van Herbes and transported to Queen Mogambu’s palace, they were left at the mercy of the merciless Tim. With some cunning maneuvers, the Zords managed to free themselves from the shackles that once bound them. Tim, never the slouch, was prepared though and locked the Silver Ranger in a force field box, not allowing him to fight. With the Gold Ranger flying solo, will Tim be able to put an end to the Metallo Zords once and for all? }~~~ ::Fighting with all he is worth, the Gold Ranger pushes himself off the floor. Seeing that the Metallo still has some fight left in him, Tim moves in for the kill. He swings his arm trying to connect with another clothesline but the Gold Ranger sees it coming and ducks under it. This however does not deter Tim as he spins his body back around and catches the Gold Ranger with a glancing blow. The blow from Tim staggers the Gold Ranger but does not stop him, as the Gold Ranger fires back with a spinning crescent kick of his own.:: Gold Ranger – Yeah, how do you like those apples, Tim? ::Tim charges at the Gold Ranger and delivers a big boot to his helmet, which sends him reeling.:: Gold Ranger – Silver Ranger, get out of there, I need your help! Silver Ranger – I am trying, Gold Ranger! Maybe it would help if you hit that button over there. Gold Ranger – Which button, Silver Ranger?! Silver Ranger –The red one, on the wall! ::The screen is consumed by a picture of a button on the wall that says “Force field deactivator” under it. Tim charges at the Gold Ranger once again, trying to level him with a running shoulder block, but the Gold Ranger is able to evade the move by rolling under him. He rolls across the room and kips up to push the button in with his hand. The force field surrounding the Silver Ranger now disappears and the Metallo Zords are united. The Silver Ranger attacks Tim from behind with a drop kick to the back of the head, but the move seems to have absolutely no effect on Tim. The Silver Ranger, seeing that his attack does no damage, takes to the ground and rolls across the room to join the Gold Ranger. He gets to his feet and the two Rangers stand together.:: Tim – You cheaters, this is a two on one! That is not fair! Bwuah, I will still taste victory! Harty har har! Gold Ranger – Not if we can help it, you evil swine! Silver Ranger – We will defeat you and your tyrannous ways! ::The Silver Ranger drops to the ground as Tim runs towards the Zords. He attempts a sweep kick on Tim but is unsuccessful, as Tim leaps over the sweep attempt. In retaliation, Tim fires off a soccer kick to the head region of the Silver Ranger. The Silver Ranger’s limp body slides across the floor, smacking against the wall. From behind Tim, the Gold Ranger rushes in for an attack. Much to his dismay, Tim spins around in place to go face to face with him.:: Tim – Now it is time to feel my wrath! Harty har har! ::Tim, for some odd reason, backs away from the Gold Ranger, putting a distance of about six or seven feet between them. A puzzled Gold Ranger, looks on, unsure of what to make of the situation.:: Gold Ranger – Are you backing down, Tim? Tim – Mwuahahahahahaha! Far from it, Metallo Moron! ::Tim extends his hand in front of him and stares at it. Soon, a swirling pink energy begins circling his hand. The light spins and spins around his hands until it forms a centralized ball in the palm of his hand. With a sudden thrust of his arms, a stream shoots out from the ball and engulfs the Gold Ranger. The Gold Ranger looks around frantically, confused by the pink energy surrounding him. Tim laughs menacingly as the Gold Ranger stands helpless. The stream of pink energy connecting Tim to the Gold Ranger begins to rise up now, pulling the Gold Ranger with it. As Tim raises his arm, the Gold Ranger begins floating off the ground. The Gold Ranger hovers in a helpless state a few feet off the ground, not knowing what is going on.:: Gold Ranger – What is this kinetic string of evil energy that you shot me with? Tim – It’s a Uranian energy trick, and it’s going to kill you! ::Tim lifts his arm up even higher, bringing the Gold Ranger up near the ceiling. Then, with a series of rapid movements, Tim slams the Gold Ranger's head into the ceiling of the dungeon over and over again. The Gold Ranger is being picked apart by this Uranian energy trick. As his head slams the ceiling repeatedly, he exclaims in pain.:: Gold Ranger – Ow, ow, ow, ow, ow, ow, ow . . . ::The Gold Ranger continues voicing his pain, but that does not stop Tim from maintaining his form of attack. When he tires of slamming the Gold Ranger’s head into the ceiling, he chooses a new branch of attack. While still holding onto the Uranium energy beam, he lowers the Gold Ranger to ground level. Then with a sudden tug of the beam, he yanks the Gold Ranger towards him. The Gold Ranger helplessly flies towards Tim, only to be caught with a massive short arm clothesline. The Gold Ranger falls in a heap, but Tim is not done yet. He pulls the beam, causing the Gold Ranger to rise back up from the ground. With the last bit of life left in him, the Gold Ranger utters a warning to Tim.:: Gold Ranger – I must warn you, if you strike me down now, I will become more powerful that you could ever imagine. Tim – Ha ha ha, that won’t work. This isn’t Star Wars. ::Tim then swings the beam over his head like a lasso. He continues spinning the beam, swinging the Gold Ranger around like a rag doll. Tim whips the Gold Ranger around and around, causing him to grow very dizzy. When Tim sees that the Gold Ranger has had enough, he decides to suddenly let go of the Uranium hold he has over him. Releasing the beam so suddenly causes the Gold Ranger to fly like a dart through the air. His flight is cut short by a wall, which he collides with and proceeds to slide down. He falls into a heap with the Silver Ranger, who is still lying against the wall. Tim applauds the duo sarcastically, mocking their efforts.:: Tim – I’m so impressed with the all mighty Metallo Zords. I at least expected some slight challenge. ::The downed Metallo Zords now begin to stir. Defiantly, each of them stick a hand in the air, clenching fists as if to say they’re not done yet. As they do, a shelf above them becomes visible. The shelf, which is attached to the wall, has two belt buckles on top of it. The belt buckles of the Metallo Zords.:: Tim – What’s that? You still want more? ::The Metallo Zords realize now that their powerful belt buckles are on the shelf above them. Simultaneously, they grab their respective buckles and slam them onto their waists.:: Gold Ranger – Maybe, just maybe, they’ll work. ::When Tim sees what is transpiring, his smug grin turns into a worried look.:: Silver Ranger – I can feel the power returning to me! It is flowing through my veins! ::The Metallo Zords begin to push themselves up to their feet. They make it to a vertical base as Tim backs away from them.:: Silver Ranger – Metallos unite! ::With that, the Silver Ranger leaps up onto the shelf. From there, he climbs onto the shoulders of the Gold Ranger. The Metallo Zords combined, who now stand a good ten feet, march towards Tim.:: Tim – Wait guys, hold on, I’m sure we can talk this through. Can’t we all just get along? Gold Ranger – Ha! It is time you pay for what you did to our predecessors like Jimmy Hoffa and Elvis! Silver Ranger – Yeah, you are about to be nothing but a hunka hunka burning monster! ::The Metallo Zords continue their steady pace towards Tim. When they near him, both start swinging their fists, and the Silver Ranger begins kicking his feet as well. With four fists and two boots nailing him in his face, Tim begins to feel the damage.:: Tim – Ah, my face! My beautiful face! ::The Metallo Zords continue pounding the monkey-dog like face of Tim, until he finally drops to the ground. Tim lays face up on the ground, almost unconscious. The Silver Ranger jumps off of the Gold Rangers shoulders and now heads for the shelf he climbed on to earlier. He waits for the Gold Ranger, who climbs on to a shelf opposite it in the room. From there, the Metallo Zords flail their arms about wildly, before ending with a thumbs up to each other. The Gold and Silver Rangers dive off the shelves now with the Massive Metallo Meltdown on to Tim! As the Metallo’s gracefully soar through the air, Tim suddenly and unexplainably vanishes. Seeing this, the Metallo Zords roll through the move and avoid injury.:: Silver Ranger – What? Where has he gone? Gold Ranger – It seems as though Queen Mogambu knew he was in trouble and managed to save him. Silver Ranger – Oh well, let us get out of here. ::The Metallo Zords look at each other through their helmets then take off with a double jump kick to the door of the dungeon, breaking it down. They walk outside of the dungeon into the main room of Queen Mogambu’s moon palace. There they see her crystal ball, which features an image of the two monsters ripping through down town. Between the monsters stands the mysterious evil ranger that has been stalking the Metallo Zords for so long.:: Gold Ranger – I suppose our belt buckles are working because we are on the moon and the evil ranger is on Earth, so he is not close enough to us to neutralize our powers. Silver Ranger – It would seem so. We had better save the city before those nefarious creatures finish destroying it. ::The Metallo Zords clench onto their belt buckles, and with two sudden beams of light, they disappear out of the palace. Will they be able to save the city? Has Tim disappeared for good? How long will the Metallo Zords’ powers last? What exactly is the Uranus beam called? If it clings to them, would that make it a Uranian Clingon? All this and more, next time with the Metallo Zords.::
