Turning The Times(PPV)

12 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Turning The Times Prologue Last week on Realm we saw Juvian Ramorez take it to his limit when he stepped in the ring with Pysmon, The Extreme Champion of lights out wrestling. However the result wasn’t what Juvian had hoped for at all, he lost, but what was more insulting than taking his first loss in three months it was the manner of this loss came. Juvian tapped out, that’s right he tapped out to a move that will cause him humiliation for the rest of his life, Juvian started off in the wrestling world a good seven years ago at the tender age of eighteen years old, and has never ever tapped out in his life, however last week he did just that. And what does he get for a reward for his loss? A shot all over again at Pysmon, but this time its for the title, this will be Juvian first title shot this year and I am sure he is more than happy, but its October the thirteenth, this day is a very bad and painful one for Juvian, this is the day that his music career ended last year. For those un-aware of the musical knowledge and background of Juvian Ramorez than maybe this can help you out. End of Prologue -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- -Flash Back three Years- Marc – Today we are joined by former Savage Darkness and Random Xaos Guitarist Juvian Ramorez. Welcome Juvian. Juvian – Thank you for having me Marc. Marc – For those whom don’t know who you are ill go over a few things and just step in at anytime if these are wrong. Juvian – Ok Marc – Your first band was called Rock Steady and was formed in your graduating year, after that you had success with your own band Savage Darkness whom made a lot of records and sold a album on BMI, after that you joined Random Xaos as their vocalist. And now you have your own project going on. Juvian – Yes, that’s pretty much it. Marc – ok, we will go back to the beginnings. Juvian – Ask away marc. Marc – Rock Steady, what memories do you have of playing with them, looking back on it did you feel it was a bad mistake, or are you happy that you was in rock steady. Juvian – when I first started in rock steady it was ok, we was mostly covering songs thou and playing at school gigs, the first thing what went wrong was the front man who was called Joe Vickers wouldn’t play any type of music that I really liked. Week in week out it would be just indie music, and to be honest I am not that much of a fan. Then their was the drummer called Rob Cramner, that guy just couldn’t keep a beat to save his life and saw me switch to drums from guitar. We played a few gigs then Joe got egotistical that he was offered a trail on a local label to this point I had had enough and was working with a Hull based band called Infobia, after Vickers booked a gig at a birthday party I had had enough and left them after the gig. Marc – So you regret working with them? Juvian – No, if I never worked with them I doubt I would be in the music business, it opened my eyes and ears to a sound that I had no heard before and I liked it. So I don’t regret it, though I will never work again with them. Marc – So on an average night who would you cover? Juvian – Oasis, Radio head and the Fucking Beatles. Marc – ok, try to calm on the language please. Juvian – No problem Marc – So Infobia helped you back then? Juvian – yes I was working for a local label as a intern after I graduated and Infobia was in their recording, so I would work close to them picking up tips and even helping them on their EP called Breathe. Marc – So they influenced you? Juvian – in a way they did, however death metal wasn’t really my scene. Marc – how did you ever get to work with Rand Burkey? Juvian – Randathaniel is a very talent guy, not only from his work with Atheist and Judas Priest but in his own. After living with Kelly {shaefer} for around four months I went to a recording with him and rand was jamming in the studio, I remember him just strumming away in a world of his own and the riffs where like poetry coming out of his Gibson. I just sat next to him and began to sing out the lyrics he was working on, and they fitted in so well, we both knew from then that it was a good duo, time passed and I helped him write more and more and finally switched back to guitar. Marc – how is rand to work with? Juvian – I have nothing but respect for Randathaniel. From this day forward any work I produce Randathaniel will be in my thank you’s, to work with him I agree with Kelly, you have to have a open mind, his music is like a sprit and it flows in all directions, you just have to be along for the ride as you don’t know where it will take you. Marc – what caused the break Of Xaos? Juvian – it basically came down to where Rand was in a world apart from us all and needed a break, he’ll be back, just in about 4 years or so. I have no doubts that we have not seen or heard the last of him, and I will await to see him again. Marc – Would you work with him again? Juvian – Without a doubt, I would work with him for the rest of my musical career if I could thou only if he joined {dark} Sapphire. Marc – So Dark Sapphire, How did that come about? Juvian – To cut a story short I was looking for a band to fill my new project with, I wanted to have a band with a darker more progressive feel and I knew a guy who could help me out. That was Stewart Frew. Stewart is a guy who knows what music I like and we play off each other well, he writes and I sing, and we wouldn’t have it any other way. Marc – What’s his style of Play? Juvian – In the words of Stewart it would be, “spontaneous and un-planned with a Jani Gers influence” that’s his style of play. Marc – And yours? Juvian – well I am more focused on my vocals this time around, I mean I’ve played drums guitars and now I’m back to master the vocals. But I will be lending a hand plaything rhythm guitar on this album, my style like always is Progressive/Melodic rock. I’m a simple and sweet guy who cranks up the volume. Marc – Who else is along for the ride? Juvian – Davie Russell is on backing vocals and Keyboards. If I am to fill in on guitars I know and trust that Davie will do a good job on vocals, and his keyboard skills add more melody to the music. Also we have Petti Orlsen’s Brother Claus filling in on Bass, this isn’t confirmed yet as I am meeting with him in a few days, I’ve listened to his old band and he has the style I am looking for. We are also currently looking for a drummer but I’m sure that’s not going to be a problem finding. Marc – So after this album will you be touring? Juvian – well I hope it progresses as fast as these questions marc, but so far we have pencilled in a few dates, we will be playing three major festivals in 2002. that I can guarantee, also after the recording I want to line up a gig where we can play some new stuff. Marc – will any old Xaos material be used? Juvian – right now I am not to sure, but I would love to remake the silence inside. That song stands out for me and I think I could turn it into a real great hit. Marc – now some questions from the forum we have and some random questions. Juvian – OK, Marc – What was the first gig that you went to? Juvian – in 1994 I went to Maine Road, England to watch an Oasis Gig, back in the day Oasis was my favourite band, since then time has passed. Marc – What was the Last gig you went to? Juvian – Franz Ferdinand, at a local venue. Marc - who in your view has the best vocals and the guy you look up to and model yourself on? Juvian – I don’t model myself on anybody, however if I was to say one person who stands out from the rest, then out would be Alice Cooper. I think he is amazing, and his live shows are the best. Marc – Metallica or Iron Maiden what’s the best? Juvian – what’s the best? In vocals and melody then maiden has it, however if its for best crunching and progressive music then Metallica has it. Six months ago I hated maiden however now thanks to Stewart I like them, however ill pick Metallica as Kirk Hammet is one of my idols. Marc – Who would be in your dream band? Juvian – Right now it would be, Myself, Stewart, Jani Litmaninen, Twiggy Ramirez and Joey Jordison however you say the slipknot/murderdolls guy. Marc – If you couldn’t have your bands members? Juvian – Dave Navarro and Kelly Shaefer instead of Stewart and Myself. Marc – Last Question Juvian – Okies. Marc – Where do you see Sapphire in five years time? Juvian – I see us signed to a major label, fans around the world knowing our lyrics, Stewart with short hair and us all be very happy. I see Sapphire winning some awards and making news. That’s where I see us in five years. Marc – Juvian, thank you for your time of day, what song shall we play next? Juvian – no problem man, I like doing interviews, for a song I will go for a classic. “Turn the Page” by Metallica. {Turn the Page Starts on the Radio} --- End of Transmission – -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- --- Back to Present Day --- You know I could sit here for the rest of my life remising on what has passed me by in life and I would still not be able to come up with a good enough reason to tell myself that I am living in the past. You see I know that we all have a purpose in life; some of us go to school dreaming of becoming pro football stars and playing in the National Football League, others they care about their scholarships trying to get into Harvard and Yale. But that wasn’t the root for me. I knew that I wanted to be a professional wrestler, so that’s what I would train for, every hour of the day, in between my studies and after school, I remember I would stay up late so I could sneakily watch it on the television, if I ever told my father I was going to watch it then he would make sure I stayed in my room all night, because of him I was a late developer on the up to date wrestlers. I would go to school and talk about all my favourites but nobody else would know them because I was only able to watch local stars. It wasn’t till I got a bit older and started to wrestle myself that the shows I saw on television was rationalised. Every state I moved to I would talk to these people and note who my influences where and an uncertainty look would come across there face to say “who?”. But that’s not the reason of my stories today, that’s not why I am living in the past, no that’s got to do with music, you see after I was travelling my sorry ass up and down I needed some spare cash and turned to a art that we all love, listen and want to become a part of, that’s a subject known to us all only as Music. Now music is special because you can always make it sound the way you feel, music is a expression of how u can change your life, and it’s a expression that I once had so well. You listen to Johnny cash you can tell that he is a man that puts his heart behind the music, take his cover of “hurt” for example, which happened to be the mans last ever song but that’s not the point I am trying to make. If you listen hard to that song you hear past the lyrics and you can hear his voice, at such a old age he still has it, he still has that level of quality that your ears pick up on, that level is hard to come across in life, but I was a man who once had it, the word once is used quite a lot in my life, Infact it should be the title for my autobiography as that’s all I have ever had. I mean “once” I was famous, “Once” I was rich and more than that “Once” I was happy with my life. I wonder if I can get back what I “Once” had. I doubt it somewhat but I am here to try. I believe I can try and get what I “Once” had by winning this Sunday at Lights out Wrestling Pay per View show titled Catastrophic. The way I look at it my opponent, Pysmon…wait I faced him last week, anyhow I face him again but this time he has everything I would want. You see if he wins he wins money…he wins a good amount of money that I would “once” have. And if he loses then he still has that title around his waist, either way it’s a win/win situation for Pysmon but I would like to prove everybody wrong, I would like to walk into the fight knowing that I could make a change, knowing that I could win the match, but more than that I would like to know that I can do it, I can put my problems behind me and win again. Though the question is can I become what I “Once“ Was?
