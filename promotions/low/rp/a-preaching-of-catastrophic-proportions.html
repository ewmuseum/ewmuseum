A Preaching of Catastrophic Proportions!

16 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~ War is not its own end, except in some catastrophic slide into absolute damnation. It's peace that's wanted. Some better peace than the one you started with. Lois McMaster Bujold, 'The Vor Game', 1990 ~ ~ Tessa, Tessa, Tessa, Darling why does it have to come to this? Wasn’t our first date enough? Or was it the fact that you just couldn’t get enough and decided that you wanted more? Tessa you’re right, forgetting about Enigma, and Aaron Rain is probably the best thing you can do right now! Because when it’s all said and done the only thing that matters is me on top getting the 1…2…3! You think my lost to Blaise 'Hazard' McClancy in GTT4 is going to stop me? I am 7’3’’ 350 pounds and I still see the top of that mountain darling, it’s just going to take a little longer to get there! And Blaise 'Hazard' McClancy don’t think I have forgotten about you, you may be hiding in your Federation 4WW but I swear one way or another I will have my rematch and I will show you and the whole world the true meaning of “The Prophecy” ~ ~There can be no light without darkness…~ Time: 4:32 PM Date: 10/14/04 Location: On the highways of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. ::::: It’s been 24 hours since John Adams was handed his defeat to Blaise 'Hazard' McClancy in the 3rd round of GTT4. So many thoughts, emotions, and feelings going through him at once, but what could he do he lost to Blaise 1…2…3. But as we see him racing down the highways of Pennsylvania there is no look of remorse or regret. Just a sign of determination, determination to get past his 3rd loss in his career, determination to continue to be the best he can be. But now there is a new challenge in front of him, a challenge from a woman that he previously beaten in GTT4……Tessa Windsor. John Adams goes to a local chapel to contemplate the situation. When he gets there to the old stucco church, he notices they are having a prayer service for the tragic events with the bombing in the Middle East. The seven foot giant goes into the church to see a couple families praying in the chapel. He heads to the front left side of the chapel to sit down. ::::: ~There can be no good without evil…. ~ ---Our Father who art in heaven hallowed be thy name. Thy Kingdome come, thy will be done. On Earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread and forgive us from all evil. --- ~There can be no love without hate…. ~ ::::: A prayer to live by, a prayer where the people of this congregation will pull all of there beliefs and there faith into just a single prayer. Just like Tessa, she wants the upper hand on John Adams, so she is putting all her determination, and hard work into this match. And for what, so she can get the one thing John Adams took from her….RESPECT. ::::: ~There can be no heat without the cold…. ~ A nearby man: Hail Mary full of grace the Lord is with thee…. ::::: The Lord is with thee. The ever so popular question on everybody’s mind in this world is “Is the Lord with me?”…..with the question in mind we take a closer look at John Adams. ::::: ~There can be no faith without disbelief…. ~ John Adams with an evil grin: So Tessa, was the lord with you? Was he there when I delivered –The Prophecy- on your fragile, and ever so delicate body? Where was he Tessa? And will he be there as you, Enigma, and Aaron Rain walk the shadow of death into Catastrophic? ::::: A monstrous, thundering laugh booms all through the chapel. It sends all sorts of chills and shivers down the spines of the occupants in the chapel. John Adams gets up from his pew and starts to walk to the altar. The fear of the Priest and others can be obviously seen as well smelled. As he looks at the crucifix on the middle of the far wall, he begins to hold his own crucifix that his mother had gave him ever so long ago. ::::: ~There can be no joy without pain….. ~ John Adams: I ask you people, when it’s all said and done is God are Lord really with you? The people that you pray for, was he really with them when they died? The question of all ages that has been said since the dawn of time, is he there or just a figment of our imagination? And when tyranny and corruption blanket your lives in darkness, is he your light that illuminates that darkness? ::::: John Adams waits for a response from the people but fear is preventing them. The Priest on the other hand decides to break the unruly silence. ::::: ~There can be no voice without silence….. ~ Priest: My son, God is every where. And he watches us through the bad and good. If you have faith in that then the keys to heaven shall be reviled upon to you. John Adams: If that was true Father, then why do we still live in a world of hurt, why do we still live in a world filled with so much damnation? And if a priest was a true man of God, then why do they still get tempted with flesh let alone a child’s! Priest: But my son, to doubt him is to be no better then the Devil himself! It’s a sin! ~There can be no forgiveness without sin….. ~ John Adams: And to use his name to hide behind so you can sin is being a bigot! And why so many Churches with so many different religions, how is one supposed to know if the religion you practice is the right one? Priest: Simply by faith my son! John Adams: I’ll tell you what I have faith in, I have faith in my abilities, I have faith in that I will dominate the business that I have chosen for myself, and I have faith in “The Prophecy”! Priest: You talk of devil worship! ~There can be no order without anarchy….. ~ John Adams: No, I talk about the corruption in the world that blinds even the keenest of eyes. I talk about a ray of hope, a beacon not only in my future but for all. All you need to do is trust and believe! Priest: Get out of the house of the Lord! And may God have mercy on your soul!! John Adams: No, may God have mercy on your soul for being a man of the cloth and taking real good care of your alter boys father!! ::::: John Adams leaves for the Motel 6 down the street laughing. The confused looks on the other occupants raise more questions to the stressed priest. ::::: ~There can be no justice without crime….. ~ Time: 9:35 PM Date: 10/14/04 Location: Motel 6 down the street. ::::: That night in the $40 dollar room John Adams has a peculiar restless night. A series of dreams happened that night, some of the murder of his Mother, and some peculiar ones where he ended up sharing intimacy with Tessa herself. But what did these mean? Was he falling for her? Was the desire of the prize at hand her? John Adams woke up in a cold sweat around midnight later on. Still trembling from the warm embrace that he felt from the dream, as he pondered on the meaning he began to laugh a diabolical laugh of pure evil. He knew what he must do and how he must do it…..He knew what the prize was. ::::: ~There can be no God without the Devil….. ~ Time: 10:50 AM Date: 10/16/04 Location: LOW Press Conference in downtown Philadelphia. ::::: The building was packed with news reporters from all different stations wanting to know about what could be the best PPV LOW has ever put together. The last time John Adams was in a Press Conference like this was during the days of EPW. So it was weird to be in that environment again. But just like riding a bike, you never forget. John Adams gets up to the podium with what was a lump in his throat, due to nerves but is now the passion and determination he feels for this match at Catastrophic. ::::: ~There can be no peace without war…. ~ Reporter #1: John Adams, how do you feel about your lost in GTT4? And how do you feel about going up against your now rival, a woman you beat in the first round of competition? John Adams: How I feel is simple, my lost in GTT4 was only but a minor setback to my claim at greatness! Blaise 'Hazard' McClancy of 4WW might have beaten me for the 1…2…3! But mark my words his time will come, I still see the top of that mountain folks, and my Prophecy shall be revealed! As far as Tessa goes, the hour draws near for her demise! She experienced “The Prophecy” before, and the way it looks she can’t get enough of it! Sure I feel bad that I have to hurt a well respected Mother of two! But I’ll tell you this, when she is in the Hospital recovering from her injuries that I will inflict upon her and she’s thinking about the stipulations of our singles match afterwards, I want her to remember that I’m not a bad guy and all! Her kids will be well taken care of when she is recovering! Reporter #2: That’s sick, twisted, and not to mention cruel Adams considering the background you had! Isn’t the fact of that you are a 7’ monster bothering you at all……she’s a woman for God Sakes!!! And do you consider Enigma and Aaron Rain any sort of factor? John Adams: If you’re talking about the moral issues of it all then no, she knew damn well what she was getting herself into! There are so many different feds out there, 4WW, Prime, Core, and others! But no she took the one federation where I was in! And for what, the money, those other feds could have paid her quite nicely! But she knew, and for the other two….no there not or will they ever be a factor in this match! Enigma talks about that I taught him in EPW, well I know I taught Alias but that man killed himself because he couldn’t hack the fact that he never belonged in this business! This Monday Tessa, Aaron, and Enigma will learn why I am the chosen one, why I am the phenomenon, why I am “The Prophet” John Adams! ::::: Strong words from John Adams, but as we all now actions speak louder then words. And at the PPV it will be jammed pack with action. The hour draws near with each passing moment as John Adams prepares mentally, physically and emotionally to be three gladiators, judge, jury, and executioner. There Judgment day is coming and it will be Catastrophic. ::::: ~There can be no Prophet without John Adams! ~ ~FIN~
