Love at first sight... Again

captured 6 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
KNOCK, Knock, knock..... KNOCK, KNOCK, Knock..... KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK! RING! RING! RING! Matt Meyhu: WHAT!? From inside Matt Meyhu's house, you can see Matt walking towards the door, in a bath robe, walking slowly, rubbing his eyes. It is 9:00 AM, and Matt has just been awoken earlier than we would have liked... He slowly opens the door, yawning... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Yo Matt! Lets go man! We gonna go find ourselves some honeys! Matt Meyhu: As... Thrilling as that sounds... I think I'll go back to sleep... Maybe I'll take my car and find you later in the day... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Theres a problem wit dat yo... See, I kinda hit your car by mistake... It's pretty much wrecked for now.. Matt Meyhu: WHAT!? YOU HIT MY CAR!? THE HELL IS WRONG WITH YOU!? 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Mah bad, yo... Don't worry, once I get mah record deal, I'll pay for the damages dawg... No worries... Matt Meyhu: Oh great... Matt rolls his eyes and throws his arms in the air. Matt Meyhu: Ok then, genius, how are we supposed to... 'find ourselves some honeys'? No way in hell you're getting me into a car with you behind the wheel... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: No worries... We be takin da bus... Matt stares at Ryan for a minute, and shakes his head... Matt Meyhu: Ok, I'd rather take my chances with you driving... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Nah man... Da bus is crawlin wit honeys dawg... Just you see... You won't be able to take your eyes off of some of them hotties... Matt gives a heavy sigh, and rolls his eyes once more... Matt Meyhu: Ok, fine, let's get this over with... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Awwww yeeeeah dawg! Lets bounce yo! Ryan Taylor leads the way, eager to get the hunt for honeys started. He smiles the whole way down the street, walking at a fast pace, while Matt lazily follows him down the street, not believing he is actually going to ride the bus... They arrive at the bus stop, and take a seat on a bench, next to another man. Man: Life is like a box of chocolates... You never know what you're gonna git.... The man turns to Matt, and smiles... Matt Meyhu: Shut up... I'm not in the mood. The man sets his box of chocolates down, stands up straight, and runs down the street. Matt sits there, not moving, just staring at the man running down the street, almost glaring at him, but at the same time, wondering what the hell he is doing... The bus pulls up to the stop and Matt and Ryan get on, and walk down the isle. Ryan smiles at all the ladies they pass and knods his head at them. 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Whad I tell ya Matt? Crawlin wit honeys! Crawlin wit em! Matt Meyhu: Uhhhh.... Yeah.... Dawg.... Matt looks around at the people on the bus, and sees nothing special... Nothing they couldn't find anywhere else... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Yo Matt! Les go sit in da back of da bus wit mah peeps! Matt Meyhu: Uhh... Sure thing, Homez.... Matt follows Ryan to the back of the bus, and they take a seat. Ryan turns to a brunette in the seat next to them, and he smiles and knods at her. 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Yo! Sup honey! Les bounce to mah crib tonight and make some magic... The girl looks at Ryan, looks at him from head to toe a couple times, then laughs for second. Girl: Ha... In your dreams... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Its aight honey! Its all good! I'm free anytime! Girl: I don't doubt that... The bus stops, and the girls gets out of her seat as fast as she can, and gets off the bus. Ryan shrugs, and turns to Matt... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Damn! She wanted me bad! Matt Meyhu: Yes, I'm sure she did... Ryan's mouth hangs open, and he freezes as he turns his attention to a lady a few seats ahead of them. He stares at her chest for a moment, then turns to Matt with a smile on his face... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Damn, she's carrying quite a load if you catch my drift... You pickin up, what I'm puttin down dawg? Matt Meyhu: Yes, I get what you're saying... Why don't you go get shot down again?... Oh, I mean, Why don't you go see how much she... 'wants' you... Ryan nods, stands up, and walks over to her. He stands next to her, and clears his throat. She looks up at him, and he lunges foward, and grabs her chest. He stands back up. 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Oooops... Sorry... Damn speed bumps............... So..... You wanna... Make out? The woman looks at Ryan, and immediatly slaps him across the face, then gets up, and moves to the front of the bus. Ryan walks back to his seat next to Matt, defeated. Matt Meyhu: Well, better luck next time... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Awww yeeeeah! The next honey dat gets on dis bus, is gonna kick it with Ryme Tyme tonight... Fa sho! Matt and Ryan sit back and relax in their seats, as the bus drives around the city. It makes a stop, and Ryan sits up, looking at the door. However, to his displeasure, two old bald men get on, no ladies. He sits back again, and the bus continues to make its way around Los Angeles. It makes a second stop. An elderly couple gets on, and a teenager with a mullet get on the bus. At the last second, just before the door closes, a tall, beautiful blonde gets on the bus. Ryan's eyes grow big and his mouth hangs open. Matt also stares at her, and smiles. Ryan closes his mouth just in time to stop the drool, and stands up. However, Matt places his hand on Ryan's chest and pushes him back down into the seat, and stands up. Matt Meyhu: She's mine... Ryan shrugs, and lets Matt through. The blonde sits in a seat alone, next to the window. She stares out the window, and Matt walks up to her seat. He sits down next to her, and admires her beauty. Matt Meyhu: Eh hem... Excuse me miss... Beautiful Woman: ...Not you aga..... She turns and faces Matt, and stops what she was saying... Beautiful Woman: Matt! What are you doing here? It's so good to see you! Matt Meyhu: It's good to see you too, Tiffany... She gives him a big hug, and smiles at him. Matt smiles back, and looks at her left hand. She was still wearing the ring. Matt was too. Matt and Tiffany had been engauged... However, they called it off for numerous reasons. They hadn't spoken in, or even seen each other in months. Tiffany: So... I hear you're back into wrestling... Just couldn't stay away huh? Matt Meyhu: Yeah... I didn't know what else to do... How'd you hear that? Tiffany: Well thats good... Stick with what you're good at... I actually just talked to Megan a few days ago... Matt Meyhu: Well that's good... So... You dating right now...? 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: DAYAM! She is fiiiiiiiiiiiine! Tiffany: Ah! It's you again! Back off! 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: No! Not the peppa spray! Ryme Tyme runs back to his seat in the back of the bus. Matt Meyhu: You know him? That's my old high school friend... Tiffany: Oh... I'm sorry... Yes, I unfortunatly know him... kind of... Every day for the last few days he's been hitting on me... Matt Meyhu: That's Ryan for ya.... or... 'Ryme Tyme'... Tiffany: Ryme Tyme? Matt Meyhu: Heh... Yeah... He thinks hes black... Tiffany: I see... Matt Meyhu: Yeah... So, as I was saying... Are you... dating anyone, right now? 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Damn Matt... Shes got it goin on dawg... You gonna tap dat shit? Matt Meyhu: GO AWAY RYAN! 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Aight... Aight... I know when I'm not wanted... Ryan goes back to his seat again, mumbling... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Always hatin the brothas... Tiffany: Well, you certainly handled that well... Matt Meyhu: Well, I learned from the best... Matt and Tiffany smile at each other. Tiffany slides a bit closer to Matt. Matt Meyhu: So, like I was saying, before he interrupted me... twice... Are you seeing anyone right now? 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Damn... Das nice mah nigga... Yous gonna get some tonight... I can feel it dawg... Tiffany: Ok! That's it! 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: NOOOOOOOO! Tiffany pulls out the pepper spray and sprays Ryan in the eyes... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: AHHHHHHHHHH! MY EYES! Matt Meyhu: Serves you right... Listen, I should probably take him somewhere... Are you busy tonight...? Maybe you could stop by, see everyone, maybe we could go to dinner or something... Tiffany: Ok... I'd love to... See ya tonight... Matt Meyhu: Great! It's a date... Tiffany: It sure is... Tiffany smiles, and waves goodbye to Matt, and he smiles back. The bus comes to a stop, and Matt leads Ryan off the bus. He sits Ryan on a nearby bench, and pulls a cell phone out of his pocket. He dials a few numbers, shakes his head at Ryan, and then holds the phone up to his ear... Matt Meyhu: Hey Mike... Can you come get us... Yeah, we were... but... I bumped into Tiffany... and well, he kinda got on her nerves... pepper spray came into play... long story short, his eyes hurt, and I've got a date tonight... Yeah, I know... Shes going to stop by and say hi to everyone before we leave... Hope she doesn't mind taking her car... 'Ryme Tyme' here took the liberty of wrecking mine... huh? It's fine eh? Matt turns and glares at Ryan, who is still having trouble with his eyes... Matt Meyhu: Well ok then, anyway, just come pick us up... We're on the bench right outside of that new taco place... No way... I'm not getting you anything there... Especially if Chubbs is there... Ok... Alright, see ya then... bye... Matt folds his phone up, puts it back in his pocket, and sits down next to Ryan on the bench. Matt Meyhu: Well... I trust you learned your lesson... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Oh fa sho... Who was that sexy thang... Matt Meyhu: That... was my fiance... Or... Former... Fiance... I guess... Hopefully still my future wife... I guess we'll see how things go... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Aww... One of dose kinda relationships... Guess I screwed up big time, yo... Sorry, I just want a girl so bad... I got my needs too, dawg! How about hookin me up wit dat scary chick you fightin dis week Matt Meyhu: Ryan... Ryan... Ryan... No one wants that... and I mean.. no one... Just then... A loud car is heard pulling up, with a cloud of black smoke. Mike Meyhu is driving the car, and motions for them to get in. Matt helps Ryan into the back, and then forces his way into the passengers seat. Mike drives off towards Matt's house... Mike Meyhu: Heh heh... So, Ryan... 'Ryme Tyme'... I hear you had a little run in with Mr. Pepper Spray... Heh heh... 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Awww yeeeah... Dat peppa spray messed me up bad dawg... Dat hottie got me good Mike... Mike Meyhu: Speaking of that... Hottie... What time is she coming over tonight...? Matt Meyhu: Not sure... Just sometime tonight... And since none of us have anything to do... I figured she could come over and see everyone... Mike Meyhu: Sounds good... You hungry? Matt Meyhu: Yeah, sure... Let's go... Mike Meyhu: How about you, 'RYME TYME' 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Aww yeeeah! Les get some fried chicken yo! Mike Meyhu: Or even better... Chicken nuggest from McDonalds! They all agree on it, and Mike pulls into the McDonald's drive thru. Mike pulls up to the intercom and someone takes his order. He pulls up to the seconds window, and pays for the food. He hands the food to Matt, who hands it out to everyone. They leave the parking lot and drive to Matt's house. Mike pulls up and parks in the driveway next to Matt's undamaged Viper. They climb out of the car and Matt does a quick inspection of his car, before following Mike and Ryan into the house, where Megan and Mark are. Matt Meyhu: Anyone know what time it is? Megan Meyhu: Yeah, it's 1 o'clock... Matt Meyhu: ONE O'CLOCK!? HOW CAN YOU ALL JUST SIT THERE?! TIFFANY WILL BE HERE SOON! Megan Meyhu: Tiffany is coming over? Mark Meyhu: That's cool... Matt imitates his younger brother Matt Meyhu: That's cool... You bet it's cool! This place is a mess! Let's clean! clean! clean! Mike Meyhu: Relax Matt... You got at least 5 hours... Matt Meyhu: Relax... Right... I'll just sit here, and eat my lunch... Then calmly get ready for tonight... Calmly...... Calmly... ***ONE HOUR LATER*** Matt Meyhu has just finished cleaning up the living room of his house. Mark Meyhu: So... First date with Tiffany in... weeks... months even... Matt Meyhu: Yes... I'm aware... Mark Meyhu: You'll do fine... Matt Meyhu: Uhh.... Thanks... Mark Meyhu: Don't worry... Matt Meyhu: I'm not... Mark shrugs, and walks away... ***ONE HOUR LATER*** Matt is just finishing up cleaning the rest of the house a little bit, when his sister, Megan Meyhu, approaches him... Megan Meyhu: You all right, Matt? Matt Meyhu: Of course I'm all right... Megan Meyhu: Nervous about tonight? First date in nearly a year with your one true love... Must be pretty scary... Matt Meyhu: Ah, I've got it under control... Really, I'm fine...Megan gives her brother a hug and walks away. Matt tilts a picture on the wall just a bit, and nods, satisfied. He goes into the kitchen to get a drink. ***ONE HOUR LATER*** It's 3:00 PM. Matt is in his bedroom, just laying out some jeans and a nice shirt on his bed. He hears a knock on his door, and turns around to see 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor standing in the doorway. 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor: Sup G... I just wanted to wish ya luck an all tonight... I know I'd be nervous around a honey like dat all night... But.. yeah... She kinda scares me... So, Ima bounce fo da night... I'll hitcha up later dawg... PEACE! 'Ryme Tyme' Ryan Taylor shows Matt a peace sign, and walks away. Matt throws his hands in the air and rolls his eyes... Matt Meyhu: It's not that big of a deal... is it? It's just dinner Matt... Just dinner... Matt whipes his forehead, and leaves his room. He heads to the kitchen to get another drink... ***ONE HOUR LATER*** Matt Meyhu turns off the water in the shower. He reaches, and grabs a towel off the bar across the top and wraps it around him. He opens the shower curtain... Matt Meyhu: AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! Chubbs Magoo: Hey Matt! Matt Meyhu: Chubbs! What are you doing here!? Chubbs Magoo: Oh, I heard Tiffany was coming over tonight... I wanted to drop by and say hello... You know, be polite... By the way... No need to be nervous... You two are great together... Matt Meyhu: I am NOT nervous! Not! It's just a friendly dinner... Well... It's a date... But I've gone on pleanty of dates with her... It's all good... Chubbs Magoo: Right... Keep telling yourself that... Chubbs walks out of the bathroom, humming, and chewing on a cheeseburger... Matt shakes his head, and begins to dry his hair... ***ONE HOUR LATER*** Matt is in his bedroom, standing in front of his mirror. He grabs the shirt off of his bed, and puts it on. He leaves the room, and walks into the bathroom, where Mike is standing. Matt stands next to the sink and the mirror, and messes around with his hair a little bit. Mike Meyhu: I'm sure you've heard it all already... Matt Meyhu: Over and over again... Mike Meyhu: I just wanted to give you a pointer... Try talking wrestling with her... You know... Tell her about how you're about to beat the most hideous thing you've ever seen and win the Realm Title... Stuff like that... Thats what I do... Mike flashes a smile... Matt Meyhu: Yes, and you're also single... Don't worry, I've got this under control... Mike's smile is whiped from his face. He shrugs and walks away. Matt finishes his hair and follows Mike downstairs to the living room. They sit around on the furniture for a minute or two, and then there is a knock at the door. Matt springs to his feet, and answers the door. There, he sees Tiffany standing there, and they smile at each other. She steps inside the door, and Matt closes it behind her. Tiffany: Hi everybody... They all greet her, and Matt opens the door again... Matt Meyhu: Well now that thats done, lets get going... I got us some reservations... There will be pleanty of time for chit chat later... Tiffany: Well okay... I'll see you all later... Bye! Matt closes the door behind them, and they hop in the Lime Green Viper... Matt starts it up, and pulls out of the driveway. They drive away into the night... Together, once again...
