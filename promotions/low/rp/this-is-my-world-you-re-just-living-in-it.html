This is my world, you're just living in it.

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by a voice over message which says . . . “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to a shot from high above the bustling streets of New York City during the night time hours. The streets however are still flooded with the lights coming from the cars below. There is however no sound penetrating the air from the traffic below. Instead, the air remains strangely quiet and undisturbed. Suddenly the sound of a voice cuts through the air causing the camera to jolt in surprise.) Voice: “Glad to see you could make it.” (The camera now turns and stops on a shot of “Superstar” Eric Russo who is leaning next to a rusted metal door that leads back into the interior of the building. He is wearing a Shadow Riders t-shirt tucked into a pair of black jeans as well as a quarter length, black leather coat. Russo pushes himself off the wall and begins to walk towards the camera as he continues speaking.) Russo: “Most cameramen wouldn’t touch an assignment that involved being around a Shadow Rider and a rooftop at the same time. You must have quite a set on you.” Cameraman: “Well it’s my jo . . .” Russo: “Did I ask you to talk!?” (Russo stops directly in front of the camera as he continues.) Russo: “Are you being paid to talk!? Do you think I asked you to come up here so I could have a conversation with you!?” (The cameraman just looks on in silence.) Russo: “That’s what I thought, now do what you’re being paid to do.” (Without hesitation, the cameraman focuses in on Russo’s face. He puts his hand out to the side of the camera and begins to count Russo down from five. As the cameraman counts him down, Russo adjusts his hair using his hand to smooth the top back. He then looks directly into the camera and prepares to begin speaking. As the cameraman reaches zero, Russo begins speaking.) Russo: “I’m glad you have finally decided to speak Cade. I was beginning to worry that you were not even going to attempt to put up a fight against me. I wouldn’t have been able to blame you though. After all, you did suffer quite a loss at the Pay-Per-View. I mean it must be extremely hard to lose a match of that magnitude. A match that had that much riding on it. It must be hard to not be able to live up to the expectations placed upon you by your peers and society in general. A loss like that would leave me rather hopeless as well. Then again, I would not lose in a match of that much importance. I would not choke under that pressure like you. No, I would rise to the occasion and live up to the moniker of “Superstar” that I so proudly carry in front of my name. We can’t all be me though can we?” (The camera begins following Russo as he begins pacing from right to left. He continues speaking.) Russo: “No, there are some of us who simply cannot get the job done. I am happy about one thing though Cade. I am happy to see that you are not trying to pass the blame off on someone else for your lack of ability to measure up. I am glad to see that like so many others you do not come out and make excuse after excuse for your shortcomings. You see Cade, I like the fact that you are a straight shooter. I like the fact that you know that the reason you failed so miserably rests solely on your shoulders. Because it does Cade, the blame and fault is yours and yours alone. You are the one who lost then and you will be the one who loses at Realm when you come face to face with one of the new breed.” (At this point Russo walks over to the stone ledge of the building as the camera follows him. He takes a seat with his back to the street below and continues speaking.) Russo: “The next generation of champions have arrived with Jason Kage and myself standing at the forefront. A breed that surpasses you and your kind in every way. Now you want to talk about heaven and hell and how you believe that it no longer exists well that is fine with me. I had to put up with enough sermons already from my dealings with Lotus. I will however ask one thing of you before you lock yourself into your new found belief system. All I ask of you Cade is to wait until you face me and then make your judgement. Wait until you step into that ring with me before you tell me that hell does not exist. Because I believe that after Realm, you will have a whole new understanding of the word hell Cade.” (Russo stands once again and begins pacing in front of the brightly lit skyline behind him as he continues talking.) Russo: “You see much like you, I know that what we do out there is a business. I also know that it also less about what you do in the ring rather then what you do outside of it. Take a look at my records Cade and let them speak for themselves. Look at my history and tell me that I don’t know that. I have been one of the main players in virtually every happening of any importance here in Lights Out. I hate to sound repetitive but who closed out the first show? Who was one of the first two wrestlers picked up by Kevin Miller and asked to join his winning team? Who is the man that defeated the guy who beat you in a match for the world twice? It was me Cade. Admit it or not, I am more of a prime time player here then you could ever hope to be and I can say that without a doubt in my mind.” (Russo pauses in his tracks and turns his attention directly to the camera before continuing.) Russo: “So Cade, you can sit back and talk about how my wins are nothing but flukes until you are blue in the face. You can tell me how it’s people like you who win my matches for me even though I do not deserve the victory. The fact still remains that I know the truth. I know that you fear me Cade as well you should. That is why you call me lazy isn’t it Cade? Those remarks are nothing but shallow attempts to deter me from my task at hand. Your ploy will not work though Cade. If anything, all your worthless talk has done nothing other then to motivate me. It has done nothing other then add fuel to my fire. A fire that will leave you as little more then a pile of ash in it’s wake. You have made yourself a marked man Cade because you don’t know how prophetic your words really are. You see, you were right when you said the flukes stop here. You we’re right because after Realm this Saturday I will walk over you leaving no shadow of a doubt that I am the better man. I will expose you for what you truly are Cade. I will show the world each and every one of your faults and believe me when I say that there are many. In essence Cade, I will rip you apart layer by layer until there is nothing left. No useless babbling to hide behind . . . no vague metaphors. Nothing but a limp body that your soul had at one time occupied.” (A strand of Russo’s hair falls into his face and he brushes it aside using his hand to once again smooth it down. He then continues.) Russo: “You see Cade, you seem to have the strange notion that I am afraid of pain and injury. Well I can assure you that I am not. I have built my entire career on pain and suffering. Although it was the pain and suffering of others, I still have watched enough to gain a perspective from the flipside of the coin. As for the brass here in Lights Out wanting me out of commission, that may very well be true. It is no secret that I am not a well liked person when it comes to authority. However, no matter what has ben put in my path, I have overcome it and you will be no different. That is why I am not worried Cade. I am more then confident that you just like the rest will pose a less then a mere annoyance to me during our match. Call it ego if you like, but realize that it will become fact come this Saturday. Like it or not Cade I am going to use you as nothing more then a stepping stone for my advancement towards Mike Meyhu and the Lights Out world title and there is not a damn thing that you can do about it. So Cade prepare yourself because it is you who will be the one suffering yet another crushing loss. For I too can see the future Cade and it is a future in which I am god. A god with no equal who reigns supreme as the world heavyweight champion until he sees fit to relinquish his hold.” (Russo now turns to his back to the camera as he looks out over the city of New York. The camera quickly moves around to Russo’s side and gets a profile shot of his face. Russo continues.) Russo: “Cade this is my world, you my self deluded friend are just merely living in it. The only reason that you still live and breathe is because I allow it. The only reason that you can claim to be the first Lights Out champion is because I allowed it to be so. It is my decisions that shape this federation into what it is. Take Lotus for example. Lotus claimed that he controlled all. He said that nothing happens unless he wants it to. Well where is he now? He is back in his nethervoid where I told him I was going to send the first time we ever crossed paths. I could have sent him there the first time we met, but what fun would that have been? Instead, I decided to humiliate him. I decided to strip him of his beloved cloak. I decided to shred his belief system in front of his eyes and spit on the shards that were left. I will do the same to you Cade. I will show you that size truly does matter even though others have told you differently in the past. I will show you that the bigger dog always wins. It’s the fact of life Cade, there are those who make waves and there are those who do nothing but ride on them. Don’t feel sorry for yourself though Cade it’s not like there’s anything you can do about it. You can’t change the nature of the game, you can only play with the hand that you are dealt. Even if it is a losing one. So quit the act and stop bluffing Cade because it is nothing more then a waste of both our times. It’s time to lay it all out on the table. I’m ending it right now, let’s get this over with. As you said yourself, it’s game over. I’m calling your bluff.” (Russo turns his attention back to the lens of the camera. He looks deep into it as he continues.) Russo: “I want you to understand that I don’t need nor do I want your respect Cade. The fact that you respect me is of little or no importance to me at this juncture. All I care about now is that your time is up. There is no changing fate so why try? Accept the inevitable and release your grasp on your so-called reality. Realize that I am willing to go farther then you could ever imagine to accomplish my goals. I am willing to do whatever it takes to win. Just ask Rob Sinn or Obsidian. So I welcome you to take your best shot at me Cade. Show me what all of your hype is about. I will be more then happy to teach you the true meaning behind the name Shadow Rider. I will be more then happy to add your name to the list. It really doesn’t matter to me because the outcome will always remain the same. You see Cade, it doesn’t matter if it’s one on one, two on two, or three on three. The fact still remains that you just like the rest will come to the simple conclusion that you are nothing but another falling star.” (With that the cameraman calls out from behind the camera.) Cameraman: “That’s a wrap.” (Angered, Russo responds.) Russo: “No shit you jackass.” (Russo then smacks the camera away from his face as the screen cuts back to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again. The logo is accompanied by a voice over message which states “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The screen now fades to black.)
