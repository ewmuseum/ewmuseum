The Shadow Riders at Sea pt.2

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When we last left the Shadow Riders, they were hot on the trail of a large fish that somehow managed to get away from them. This has sparked them to move even further out into the ocean in search of the one that got away. What awaits them as they move even deeper into the sea? Will they ever find the fish that slipped through their grasps? Read on to find out all that and more in this the second instalment of “The Shadow Riders at Sea”. (The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders along with a voice over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens out on the open sea at what appears to be dusk. The Wave Rider is cutting through the small waves that ripple across the surface of the water leaving a trail of thick white foam behind it. “The Vigilante” Luke Justice and “Superstar” Eric Russo are standing on the upper level of the boat in front of the wheel peering out across the wide open area. Suddenly, Russo raises his hand and points as he exclaims.) Russo: “Over there!” (Luke looks at Russo with a confused expression.) Justice: “Why over there?” (Russo still glares out over the ocean as he responds.) Russo: “At least you can see good enough to drive the boat.” Justice : “I can see perfectly fine . . . maybe you’re seeing things Eric.” Russo: “Shhh . . . cut the motor. He’s close.” (Luke cuts off the engine as Russo jumps down to the lower deck. Once there he looks over to Kage and makes a motion to tell him to keep his mouth zipped. Russo reaches out and grabs hold of his fishing rod which is resting up against the wall. He then begins to creep over to his seat so as to cast off. Once he is at the seat, he quietly climbs up onto it and begins to sit down. Just as Russo is about to hit the seat though, he freezes in place. Not daring to make a move. Russo face fills with a look of shock as he peers down into the water. On the upper deck, Luke looks like he is in a state of shock as well. Seeing the facial expression of the other two Riders, Kage decides to move in to get a closer look for himself. As Kage moves towards the edge of the boat, he looks over the side into the deep, dark, blue water only to jump back from the sight that greets him. There in the water is the beast that they have been looking for . . . the beast that they had battle earlier. In the water we see what can only be described as a monster. A dark colored fin juts out through the surface of the water. Attached to the fin is a dark shadow that the water obscures from a clear view. However from the shadow we can make out that whatever is attached is much larger then what any of the Shadow Riders expected. The beast swims off to the side as it’s tail breaks the surface with a slap to one side. Russo drops his fishing rod to his side as it drops to the deck.) Russo: “Jesus that’s a big fish.” (Luke calls down to Russo.) Luke: “Eric . . . what would you say 12 feet?” (Shaking his head, Russo responds.) Russo: “No . . . more like 15. Not to mention about 450 lbs..” (Luke responds with a slight smirk on his face.) Justice: “I didn’t know Belmont’s mom swam this far out.” Russo: “No way brother . . . too light to be her. Maybe his sister.” Justice: “Hell maybe it’s Belmont himself.” Russo: “Doubt it . . . Belmont in water don’t make me laugh. He never showered once the entire time he was with us. What makes you think he would start now?” (Kage cuts into the conversation snapping Luke and Russo back to the task at hand.) Kage: “Hey . . . where’d it go? It disappeared . . . it’s gone.” (Luke pulls open a small door and heads down into the inside area of the boat where he begins looking around for something. Kage peeks his head into the room as he watches Luke grab one of the flares out it’s box. Luke quickly runs back out onto the deck. Once there, Luke begins attaching the flare to one of the barrels of chum. Russo who is still watching the surface of the water hears the movement behind him and spins around to see what is going on. When he sees what Luke is up to, Russo becomes curious and begins asking questions.) Russo: “What are you doing? Don’t play around with the flares . . . we may need them later.” (Luke looks up as he tries to explain what he is doing to Russo.) Justice: “Have you ever seen that movie where the three guys head out on a boat to catch a shark? I can’t remember what it was called . . . Fins or something like that. Well in the movie they attached one end of a rope to a harpoon. Then they tied the other end to a barrel so that when they shot the shark with the harpoon, the barrel would go with it so the shark couldn’t go underwater. Get it?” (Russo still looking confused responds.) Russo: “Yeah I get it but why are you playing with the flares?” (Luke sets the flare off and it lights up in a bright red flame. The flame is so bright that even in the sunlight it hurts the eyes to stare directly into it. With a smile on his face, Luke answers the question.) Justice: “It’s getting dark . . . what if we have to follow him some more before he gets tired out enough for us to catch him?” (A smile fills Russo’s face as he now understands where Luke is going with his idea. He now begins speaking.) Russo: “Ok . . . so now we have a plan. Jason . . . can you get the harpoons ready? We have to work quick, we never know when this damn thing is gonna show back up. I wanna be ready to go, it may be our last chance because once it gets dark, we may loose him for good.” (Kage runs off and begins threading a thick length of rope through the end of the harpoons. While Kage is doing this, Luke is busy at work tying the other end of the rope to one of the many barrels of chum. Just at that moment, The Wave Rider is rocked hard as something hits the side of the yacht. Russo rushes over to Kage and snatches the threaded harpoon out of his hands. With his hands now free, Kage rushes over to his nap sack and pulls out a small hand gun which he begins pointing where ever he believes the beast could be. Luke looks up and sees Kage waving the gun about wildly in the air with a look of sheer terror on his face. He quickly reaches up and grabs hold of Kage’s hand as he exclaims.) Justice: “Put that frigging thing away . . . you’re gonna end up shooting one of us.” (While all of this is happening down below, Russo has managed to climb on top of the roof covering the captain’s area. He has the harpoon aimed at the surface of the water ready to fire the next time he sees the beast that is rocking the boat of the Shadow Riders Russo (To himself): “Come on . . . just give me one shot you jackass . . . just one shot.” (Boom! The ship rocks again. The force of the shot almost sends Russo off the roof and into the water with the beast down below. From Russo’s vantage point he sees the shiny, slick back and fin of the monster down below. Steadying himself, he fires off the harpoon catching the large mammal dead in the back. The beast thrashes violently in the water smacking hard against the side of the boat nearly sending Russo into the water once again. The slack of the rope attached to the harpoon quickly unravels from it’s coil on the lower deck. So fast in fact that Luke tackles Kage to the deck floor in order to get him out of harms way just in the nick of time as the barrel with the flare on top flies by mere inches in front of his face. Back on the roof of the captain’s area, Russo is already set and ready to fire yet another harpoon into the flesh of the beast. The first barrel skips across the surface of the water and then suddenly drops below the surface extinguishing the flare in the process. Still keeping his eyes trained on the water surface, Russo begins exclaiming out loud to the monster of the sea in an almost maniacal tone.) Russo: “Come on back you bastard! You can’t hide from me now . . . that barrel is gonna pull you to the surface any minute! As soon as it does, I’m gonna hit you with another! Do you think you can handle that!? I don’t! What are you waiting for you jackass! Let me see you so I can tag you again you bastard!” (Realizing that the beast is gone for the present time, Russo makes his way down to the lower deck where he meets up with Luke and Kage. Russo walks to the side f the boat and stares out over the water that is glistening with the setting sun. Luke and Kage approach Russo from behind each on opposite sides as all three men now look out at the water. Kage speaks up.) Kage: “Hey Eric . . . just let him go for now man. We’ll go looking for him tomorrow, let’s call it a night for now.” (Russo turns to look Kage in the eyes. Kage is immediately taken aback by the sheer determination that fills Russo’s eyes and words as he responds to Kage’s suggestion.) Russo: “I’m gonna stay out here until he’s mine. There is now way he’s getting away from me now.” (Russo turns back to face the ocean as he watches for any signs of movement. With his back to them, Kage turns to Luke as he mouths words to him under his breath.) Kage: “He’s insane . . .” Nightfall (The scene opens to a shot of The Wave Rider drifting on the surface of the water. It is now night and the water is dark. Towards the back of the yacht, Jason Kage is passed out with a half empty bottle of beer in his hand. It appears as though the long day has more then tired him out as his eyes move rapidly darting back and forth un the skin of his eyelids indicating that he is deep in dream. To the front of the yacht, Luke Justice and Eric Russo are sitting with their feet hung over the side splitting a bottle of tequila between them shining a searchlight out over the water. Luke takes a long swig from the bottle as he turns back towards Russo. Both men a visibly buzzed from the alcohol that they have been consuming. Russo is the first to begin speaking as we join a conversation in progress.) Russo: “How can you say that was a close call? Head never stood a chance against yo in that match. You had him beat before you even got in the ring with him. You were the biggest threat to our power back there. You were the only one who ever posed any sort of a challenge to us. I mean hell, you took out Rhino when nobody else could.” (Luke hands the bottle to Russo as he responds.) Justice: “You guys had a monopoly . . . once you chased Jamester out of there, nobody was taking your power away. You guys had it all. Half the time we were feuding, I was just waiting for you guys to ask me to jump sides. I wanted to be a part of the group from then brother.” (Russo lets out a little sigh then takes a big gulp from the bottle. As he hands it back to Luke, Russo continues.) Russo: “See that’s what I don’t get about Belmont. He says that we drove him away. The hell we did . . . we made Tristan Belmont! You’re the one who gave him a run with the Xtreme title. Would he have ever gotten that without us . . . no. Without us giving him the path, Belmont would still be walking around with Broken Reality.” Justice: “We did kind of use him to take out Broken Reality though at least he got that part right. That’s a lot for a Canadian. Eh?” (Russo chuckles slightly as he responds.) Russo: “Yeah . . . we did. But I mean come on. The guy came to us the day of the event knowing that he was out matched and generally outclassed. We’re smart, if a member of the team we’re going against wants to jump sides, of course we’re gonna take him aboard to get the advantage. We’re not in a sport, we’re in a business and you pull out all the stops to get yourself whatever power you can.” Justice: “I know, did you hear Belmont talking about how we’re power hungry? It’s like duh . . . no shit assclown.” Russo: “I don’t get why he thinks that’s a bad thing. Look where it’s got us. I ask you to name one entity in the entire history of LOW who has done more then the Shadow Riders have. You name it, we’ve done it in some form or another. When it was just Kage and myself we transcended the tag team division. We were the hottest ticket in town no matter what some others may claim and we still are.” (Taking another sip from the bottle, Luke responds.) Justice: “You don’t have to convince me brother. I’m in this for the long haul. I know a good thing when I see it.” Russo: “Well it’s good to know that some people still know what loyalty mean . . .” (Something out in the water catches Russo’s eye and quickly pulls his attention away from the conversation. Slowly, he begins speaking to Luke without taking his eyes off the water.) Russo: “We’ll finish this conversation later . . . he’s back.” (With that, the picture fades out to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again where we hear a voice-over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The picture then cuts to a black, blank screen.)
