Hate Part 5 Archive - 8.6.2003

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Ring. Ring' Nobody answered. 'Ring. Ring' Still nobody answered. 'Ring. Ring' 'Please pick up the phone Cyrus, where are you?' Hannah muttered as held the phone to the side of her head. She felt the gun in her back and the perspiration on her forehead grew. 'Ring. Ring' 'Hello.' Cyrus voice was solemn. He knew who was on the phone. He knew what to expect. He knew it was getting to the end. It was the beginning of the end… 'Cyrus, I need your help.' Hannah was not allowed to finish her sentence. Cyrus already knew what she was going to say. He didn't know how or why he knew. But he did. He knew everything. He knew why she was ringing him, he knew that she was being held hostage. Then he remembered. He told him. But wasn't that a dream? No, it wasn't a dream, the scary thing was that was reality. 'I know you do, what do I have to do?' Cyrus did not want to mess about, he wanted to get this sorted out as soon as possible. No more waiting around for phone calls. No more pussy footing about trying to find clues. No more bad marks against his name. Soon it would be all ever. But before that, there was a lot to sort out. 'Ok, I'll be there within a few minutes. Don't worry Hannah just do as they say and you'll be fine.' With that Cyrus put the phone down and started to walk towards the door of his house. He picked up his keys and walked out of his front door, locking it behind him as he went. With a 'Bleep' his car was unlocked and he jumped in. He put the keys into the ignition and revved his little baby up. He put his foot down and sped off down his road. Whilst he was driving, he decided to make himself heard. 'So we're finally getting this sorted? It's been a long time coming. Two or three weeks now? I can't remember how long ago it was when this all started. Cyrus this has taken you too long to get to the bottom of. Just think if you'd have been home sooner this could have been over a lot quicker. All you needed was to hear one of those answering machine messages Hannah left you and you would have known, but no…you had to go out trying to find clues, when all the clues you needed were back at your house.' The voice had a point. Cyrus had wasted a lot of time going out and looking for clues. He'd wasted time that he could not afford to waste. But why had the framers waited? Why did they wait for Cyrus to find them? Did they want him to find them? Was it all a set up? Was he going to get into more trouble when he went to rescue Hannah? Only time would tell… 'Well, if you would have told me sooner about the fact that you knew so much, maybe I wouldn't have wasted so much time. This may be a little game to you my friend, but to me this is serious. When I find the punks that have been doing this I'm going to kill them, I'm going to slaughter them alive. They f***ing don't realize what they've gotten themselves into…' Cyrus was angry. He'd thought he'd lost the love of his life, twice. Now he'd found out that she was still alive. His emotions had been on a roller coaster with so many ups and downs recently. Cyrus was feeling only one emotion now though, hate. Hate towards the men and women that could have orchestrated such an evil and vile plan. However, Cyrus was going to end up getting himself into more trouble if he was not careful. 'Cyrus, slow yourself down. Going in there all guns blazing isn't going to solve anything. It'll just get you into more trouble. The Police already think you're the bad guy, don't let them think that anymore. Don't give them any more reasons to suspect you as a criminal.' The voice, for once, was right and unfortunately Cyrus knew it. Cyrus had to admit defeat, he realized that if he did go in there and cause some serious damage he'd end up being arrested and that wouldn't be good. For him or his LOW career. 'Ok I'll take it easy. But only for now, as soon as I find out who did this I'm going to get them done over. I still know a few people from the street. I can have something arranged.' Cyrus was planning his revenge, he'd felt so much pain recently that he just had to give out some himself. He had to get even with the crooks that had done this to him. He had to get revenge, not just for him, but for Hannah as well. Cyrus and the voice stopped talking as he pulled up to what looked like and abandoned warehouse. That was the address that Hannah had given him. He walked up to the huge brown, rotting, wooden doors and pushed them open. There, inside was a mirror. 'What the f** is going on?' Cyrus was confused, there was nothing inside the warehouse but a mirror. Cyrus looked at it, then noticed something. A gun was lodged in his belt. He pulled it out, he shot the mirror again and again and again. Each time he squeezed the trigger he filled the mirror with more and more steel, again and again he kept shooting until the mirror was in pieces on the floor. Then he spun round to face a man about the same size as him. He looked at him right in the eyes. He did not move or flinch. His eyes stared down the barrel of the gun, he did not tremble, he did not fear. He just was. The gun moved, beckoning him to walk towards Cyrus. He did not move, he just stared. The gun was then pushed against his head. He did not move, he just stared. Perspiration dripped down Cyrus's brow. He still did not move, for he did not care… His finger trembled as he squeezed the trigger. He threatened to shoot, he shouted and screamed. No effect. He hit him round the head with the barrel of the gun, he started to bleed from a gash on his forehead. No effect. Cyrus placed the gun to his temple and closed his eyes. He went to squeeze the trigger, it was nearly the end, yes still there was no effect… --- Cyrus awoke with a start. Something had woken him up from his very strange dream. A dream that seemed so real, but yet surreal at the same time. A dream where reality merged with insanity. A dream that revealed who had been setting him up. He now knew who it was. Then it repeated. 'Ring. Ring' The phone woke him properly this time. He got up and went to answer it.
