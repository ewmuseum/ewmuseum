False Idol!!

7 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~ Science can purify religion from error and superstition. Religion can purify science from idolatry and false absolutes. Pope John Paul II (Karol Wojtyla), James Reston, Galileo, A Life, HarperCollins, NY, 1994, p 461. Science can purify religion from error and superstition. Religion can purify science from idolatry and false absolutes. Pope John Paul II (aka: Karol Wojtyla) ~ ~ The power of my own Realm is growing in size everyday. And soon the circle will be complete and “The Prophecy” will take affect! Enigma or Alias….whatever his name may be, felt it and soon so shall Cobi Deekins. My will is spreading all through the divisions of LOW….all except for the world division!! That has yet to be untouched by me….that has yet to be unscathed by the will of the prophecy!! Cobi, you my friend are about to embark on a trip into a hell I like to call “The Prophecy” but can you handle the ride? ~ Time: 6:38PM Date: 12/7/04 Location: 9799 Laurel Ave North Huntingdon PA Center of Light Church. `Of what value is an idol, since a man has carved it? Or an image that teaches lies? For he who makes it trust in his own creation; he makes idols that cannot speak. Woe to him to says to the wood, ‘Come to life’? Or to lifeless stone, ‘Wake up?’ Can it give guidance? It is covered in gold and silver; there is no breath in it. But the lord is in his holy temple; let all the earth be silent before him…..` “That my brothers and sisters was the verse from The book of Havakkuk! “ {That was the sounds of the priest teachings as Adams stepped into the halls of the Center of Light Church. The irony was amazing for he planned on facing an Idol at the next Realm. He walked pass a donation basket for the homeless and sat down in not only deep thought but also in deep prayer. How was he going to battle against a man that was so much of a veteran compared to Adams….how does he defeat a man that used to hold the Realm title?} ~ A group of unnatural potential……… ~ Priest: Now this is the part of the service where we ask you to join hands and say the Lord’s Prayer! {As the people of the parish got up to join hands, it looked quite odd that Adams was the only one not to join. But why should he when he was in a deep thought of the things that has transpired since the last Realm. Had the return of Richard Rush, also defeated the man now known as Enigma. But the best is not yet over with yet.} ~ Filled with an evil waiting to be unleashed…….. ~ Everybody in the church: Our Father full of grace, the lord is with thee….. {Wondering the real reason of the lords prayer, reminds Adams that his work in LOW will take a change for the better since the new Owner Richard Rush has taken ownership of LOW. Rush and him have before teamed up to shake the very foundation of the wrestling world. There is no will without the Prophets way…..that has always been the way of things between the two. But this time Rushes will and Adams will falls along the same things. But he also realizes that time is of the essence….the rebirth of the new era in LOW must happen…..it will happen.} ~ All is for warned to not stand in there way………. ~ Everybody in the church: Now and forever…..Amen! {That was the sign of the end of the lords prayer, but that didn’t faze Adams much….he didn’t even turn around to look at anybody to acknowledge it. Nothing at all from Adams, for he was still in thought and prayer….a prayer of not for hope, not for good will towards men, and not for peace on earth….he was praying for the total destruction and annihilation of all his enemies.} ~ The end is near for the prophecy of the world is upon us……….. ~ John Adams thinking to himself: Dear lord up high, give me the strength….give me the strength to smite the damned that are against me…give me the strength to smite all of those who would stand against me and give praise to those who would stand for me and my cause as we go into the battle fields of LOW! {John Adams after prayer decides to head out and get ready for his big match ahead of him. There is a new path for the man called The Prophet, but where will it take him in life and what of this new found alliance with Richard Rush? Can he be trusted or is history doomed to repeat it’s self? These are questions that not even a Prophet can answer. The night sky in Pennsylvania had an inviting sense of loneliness and solitude, as Adams got on his bike he had to wonder if that was the life he was going to always live….alone. He was always known as the future of wrestling all together, but was that even enough?} ~ Ravaging through the infidels that would dare protect the weak……… ~ Time: 10:02PM Date: 12/7/04 Location: The Adams estate in Pennsylvania. {Into the house, the new home of John “The Prophet” Adams, one has to wonder why it looks a lot like the one of his child hood, and why does he have furniture, old furniture that only his father and mother would like? Strange and unusual behavior from a man who came from a home filled with hate and anger. John Adams is seen in the dining room of his multi million dollar home….remembering the pain and carnage he had to endure all those years back. Yes a house like this is very odd to get for him, but a constant reminder of all the hell that he had to go through is good for him and has given him the strength to become the phenomena and monster of LOW that you see today.} ~ Showing them a hell that has no escape……. ~ John Adams thinking to himself: Why after all these years am I still obsessed about the past that was my hell? Why am I still obsessed with all of this?? {The thought made absolute no sense to him, he then got a urge to laugh uncontrollable….the laughter filled the whole house with a booming sound that echoed all over the place. The general mood of his is that he has lost it, and that all he needed was sleep.} ~ Another will try to defeat what many have failed before……… ~ (The Phone Rings.) John Adams: Hello……who is this? Yeah, what’s up? Listen Rush, you just live to your end of the bargain….and by God you know I will live up to mine! {John Adams slammed his phone down in a small explosion of rage. He damn well knew what he had to do….he didn’t need to be reminded constantly about it. He heads over to where he has a picture of the people he chose for the group he calls S.I.N. (Strength In Numbers.) Cyrus Skar- hasn’t been booked yet, but the man in charge has promised soon, Juvian- is out even though it was a shame to see him leave the group it was well expected, Richard Rush- a man who was very familiar to Adams has control over LOW, and they have just gotten there newest edition to the group….when will he make a appearance that is though another story.} ~ A false idol trying to claim his Realm that was taken from him by another………. ~ {John Adams tired and alone decides to turn in for the night, alone only the demons of the past to comfort his every whim. The Prophet vs The false Idol…..now there was a match to watch….but before Adams closes his eyes for a nights rest the only thought that was on his mind was the house of sin, and the past it has always represented for him.} ~ Only to just be another body added to the count of The Prophet!! ~ ~ FIN ~ ~ One should never direct people towards happiness, because happiness too is an idol of the market-place. One should direct them towards mutual affection. A beast gnawing at its prey can be happy too, but only human beings can feel affection for each other, and this is the highest achievement they can aspire to. Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn You know who I idolized? Mr. Rogers. Is there a market for the next Mr. Rogers? Because I'd love to do that. I'd much rather be quiet and important like him than live large and be some useless celebrity. Clay Aiken, Elle ~
