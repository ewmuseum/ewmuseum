The Arrival

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by a voice over message which says . . . “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to a shot inside of a bustling airport’s baggage claim center where “Superstar” Eric Russo and “The Vigilante” Luke Justice are standing as they wait for their bags. As the two men stand watching the conveyer belt rotate bag after bag past them, Justice begins to speak.) Luke: “So, Kage is picking us up right?” (Russo glances over at Justice as he responds.) Russo: “Well that’s what he said. We’re supposed to be going out to some sort of dinner.” Luke: “Great . . . Kage is picking the restaurant isn’t he?” Russo: “Unfortunately I think so.” Luke: “Damnit! I hate it when he picks. Ten to one says it’s some form of buffet.” Russo: “Well you know Kage . . . if you he can get more for less, it’s has to be good.” Luke: “Cheap bastard.” (At this point the bags of Russo and Justice come around on the belt. Russo reaches out and grabs hold of his duffel bag. He then puts the strap on his shoulder as Luke does the same with his. As the two men turn and start towards the exit, Russo continues the conversation.) Russo: “Speaking of cheap, when I watched Ezra’s latest promo I realized that his gimmick is nothing but a rip-off.” (Slightly chuckling at Russo’s absurd accusation, Justice plays along.) Luke: “How do you figure that?” Russo: “Well lets look at his gimmick. First of he’s Brazilian.” (Justice lets out a laugh as he responds.) Luke: “Dude, that’s not a gimmick. Ezra is Brazilian.” Russo: “So what if he is. With how much he stresses that fact, it becomes a gimmick.” Luke: “Whatever, continue.” Russo: “Well as I was saying, Ezra is a Brazilian and he likes to kick things.” Luke: “Ok, for the sake of argument, let’s say he is.” Russo: “Well this who thing got me thinking and it made me realize that Ezra is nothing but a Pele rip-off.” (A look of confusion spreads across Justice’s face as he responds Luke: “Who in the hell is Pele?” Russo: “He’s a Brazilian soccer player.” Luke: “Ok, that comparison just plain old sucked.” (Russo stops in his tracks and turns to look at Justice.) Russo: “Well sorry, it’s kind of hard coming up with things to make fun of Ezra for. He beats me to all of them by just being himself. There’s nothing I can really say to him to make him look any worse then he already does.” Luke: “Your right, but Pele?” Russo: “Look, just forget it. Let’s get the hell of this airport and go eat.” Luke: “See, now that sounds good.” Russo: “Of course it does, it was my idea.” (Luke just shakes his head as he and Russo exit the airport into the windy night air of Chicago, Illinois. Once outside, Russo and Justice begin to scan the parking lot as they look for Kage. After a few moments of looking around, they realize that Kage is nowhere to be found. Annoyed, Russo pulls out his cell phone and flips it open. He pushes a button and raises the phone to his ear as he begins speaking to Justice.) Russo: “I can’t believe Kage. He knew we were coming in. Where the hell is he?” Luke: “He’s probably hanging out with Belmont laughing that we’re stuck out here in the cold.” Russo: “Don’t even joke around like that brother.” Luke: “Who’s joking?” (Finally Kage picks up on the other end of the line.) Kage: “Hello.” Russo: “Where the hell are you!?” Kage: “Who is this?” Russo: “Uncle frigging Sam! It’s me!” Kage: “Oh . . . Russo. Where are you and Luke?” Russo: “We’re at the airport where you’re supposed to be picking us up!” Kage: “Picking you up? When I talked to Belmont earlier he said that you guys called him and were going to meet us at the restaurant.” Russo: “What!? Is Belmont there now?” Kage: “No, but he should be getting here any minute.” Russo: “Good, keep him there. Me and Luke are gonna get a cab, we’ll b right there.” (Russo snaps his phone shut and places it back into his pocket. He then turns to Justice as he begins speaking.) Russo: “Seems that Belmont told Kage we had a ride.” Luke: “That choad. I’m gonna kill him.” Russo: “Let’s get a cab and get over to the restaurant. According to Kage, Belmont is going to be there any minute.” Luke: “Sounds like a plan me.” Russo: “It’s about time we fix this problem.” (With that, the screen cuts back to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again. The logo is accompanied by a voice over message which states “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The screen now fades to black.)
