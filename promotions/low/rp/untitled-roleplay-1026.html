History Lesson Part One John Chernega LOW Roleplay #1026 Date: 4/15/03 Location: Chicago City Hospital 'Johnny, you are needed at the hospital...you mother has been in an accident.' ::(These are the words that raced through twelve year old John's mind as he raced down the busy street. He had no idea what was going on with his mother, just that things were bad. He also had a feeling inside of himself that something terrible was about to happen. As much as he hated to admit it, he realized in his mind that these were his mother's last moments. Cars screeched to a hault as John sped through the crosswalks as fast as his little feet could carry him. He knew there was a chance that he could be injured being this wreckless, but he didn't care. The sooner he got to the hospital, the better. He reached the front doors of Chicago City Hospital and burst in through the crowds of people. Diwn a long corridor he went until he reached a reception desk. The lady sitting at this station was quite busy, but her presence was warming in John's mind.):: :::Johnny::: [out of breath] E...e...excuse me? :::Receptionist::: Yes? How may I help you today? :::Johnny::: I...I need a r..room number please. :::Receptionist::: Are you in the immediate family of the person you wish to visit? :::Johnny::: Yes, I...I'm her son :::Receptionist::: Oh, right then. Name please? :::Johnny::: Hillary Chernega...C-h-e-r... :::Receptionist::: I have it, thank you... ::(After a few seconds of punching keys, the receptionist looks up in John's eyes and smiles.):: :::Receptionist::: Ahh, there she is. I have her listed in room 306. Do you need me to walk you... ::(Immediately, Johnny bolts of through another corridor on the right.):: :::Receptionist::: In a hurry, I suppose... ::(At this point, Johnny is not quite sure as to his course, but he knows the room is around somewhere. After seceral minutes of frantic searching, Johnny finds himself into the hospital room, screaming for his mother. As he attempts to get near her, his father steps in his path. John stares up at his father's eyes for a few seconds before streams of tears begin to trickle down his face. As they hug, Johnny's father makes an attempt to comfort him):: :::Mr Chernega::: Alright...it's going to be okay... :::Johnny::: A...are you sure? :::Mr. Chernega::: Positive. Your mother is one of the strongest people I know, and she is not ready to go just yet, my boy... ::( Suddenly, Mrs. Chernega's eyes open, and she catches the last part of her husband's sentence before she speaks.):: :::Mrs. Chernega::: Bill, quit humoring the boy, we all know the truth...when it's my time, I will go, and that time is now... :::Mr. Chernega::: Now Hillary... :::Mrs. Chernega::: Bill, listen. Johnny is old enough now to realize that things are going to happen during which we will not be happy. He is also old enough to know that every rhyme has a reason. Now, if I may, Johnny, I need to speak to your father alone for a moment. ::(Johnny does not move. He simply stands there, gazing at the tubes, needles, and meters that surround his mother in her hospital bed. Suddenly, the tears begin to flow once again as he runs close to her side. Looking into his eyes, she whipes the tears away from his face with her undamaged hand.):: :::Mrs. Chernega::: Listen, I need you to be strong, and do as I say...it won't be long, I promise. ::(John's mother pats him on the head as he reluctantly backs out of the room. Closing the door behind him, he sits against the wall beside it. For what seems like an eternity, he waits. Thoughts rush to his mind about his mother and her safety, and how he and his father would make it if she were to die.):: :::Johnny::: [quietly sobbing] Be strong, be strong, be strong. be strong, be... ::(Suddenly, the door opens, and Johns father steps out into the hallway. Johnny stares into his eyes, trying to find a shread of hope. However, there is not but a cold expression on his face as he speaks sternly, and almost coldy to his son.):: :::Mr. Chernega::: She wants to see you. ::(Slowly, Johnny gets to his feet and shuffles into the doorway as his father moves aside and closes the door. Inside, his mother is now sitting up, and a smile grazes her tired face. Trying to be polite, Johnny smiles backm but knows that his mother sees through it. He slowly walks closer to her, but suddenly stops several feet in front of her, staring.):: :::Mrs. Chernega::: Come here... ::(At the sound of her voice, the floodgates open once again as Johnny rushes to his mother's side. He tries his best to hug her as she pats him on the head. For several minutes, they simply lay there, saying nothing until Hillary kisses her son on the head and speaks softly to him.):: :::Mrs. Chernega::: My boy...this is not something you need to fear. It is merely a part of life. :::Johnny::: But, I'm not ready for you to leave. :::Mrs. Chernega::: I am not ready to leave you or your father either, but these are the cards we have been dealt. Things happen sometimes...and we cannot find a morsel of good in them. However, everything always turns out for the best in the end, and that is something you must never forget. No matter what trash comes your way, you must realize that it is for your own good. :::Johnny::: I guess... ::(Hillary continues to hold Johnny close to her for several minutes. Neither of them says a word, simply soaking in each other's presence.):: :::Mrs. Chernega::: Johnny, I have something very important I want to tell you...and I want you to listen me me carefully. :::Johnny::: Of course. :::Mrs. Chernega::: Throughout your life, many people are going to try and tell you how your should run your life, and what kind of decisions you should make. You need to remember that only you are in control of your destiny, and that no one can ever take away what is rightfully your's. Live your life...follow your dreams. :::Johnny::: I will, I promise... :::Mrs. Chernega::: Oh, and son… :::Johnny::: Yes? :::Mrs. Chernega::: Take care of your father. He is not sure what to do with himself. He is more than capable of taking care of you, he is just in a state of shock at the moment. So, do him a favor, and mind him, alright? :::Johnny::: I will. :::Mrs. Chernega::: I feel my body fading, Johnny boy… :::Johnny::: NO! Mom, please… :::Mrs. Chernega::: Baby, everything will be okay, remember? :::Johnny::: I…I… ::(Before the words can leave his lips, his mother’s grip on his body loosens as her arm falls. The heart rate monitor turns from a steady beep, to a loud screech as Johnny stares at the lifeless body of his mother. At first, he does nothing. Eventually, however, his mind functions clearly again, and he calls for his father.):: :::Johnny::: DAD! DAD, PLEASE! COME QUICKLY! ::(Almost immediately, Bill Chernega comes from outside the door, and immediately realizes what has just happened. He slowly approaches the body of his wife, and places his head on her side. John stands in complete silence as he proceeds to weep uncontrollably.):: :::Mr. Chernega::: Stolen. STOLEN FROM ME! So young yet…so much life to live. Those…those fools who did this to you…may they rot. May they rot forever in an eternal fire! ::(John lets out a small gasp at the words of his father, for he had never heard him speak like this before. This new persona was a strange and evil one, and Johnny was not a fan of it. Suddenly, Bill stands on his feet, and takes one last look at his wife before turning around to face his son. Johnny was, at first, hoping to receive some emotional comfort. When there was no embrace, no hug of any kind, Johnny became afraid, very afraid, of what his father was going to do next. He dare not show this, however, he simply stood in silence…in awe of his father as he had done already today. As he stared, his father stared back at him. What was a face of sorrow and sadness soon became a face of anger and bitter rage. Bill suddenly leaned down to his son’s level and stared him in the eyes as he spoke.):: :::Mr. Chernega::: You…you did this….THIS IS YOUR FAULT! :::Johnny::: I…I… :::Mr. Chernega::: SHUT UP! This is your fault, and don’t you say differently, do you understand? DO YOU? ::(Johnny was now trembling at the knees as his father began to yell and curse in his direction. The words became so extreme that little Johnny could actually feel the hatred in his voice. Deep down, he knew these words were not meant, and that they were simply the frustration of his father spilling over in a classic case of misplaced anger. However, they soon became too much, and Johnny began to cry. As the tears spilled yet again from his face, Bill began to shake him violently at the shoulders and wiping the tears away. Not with care, but with great anxiety.):: :::Mr. Chernega::: Stop CRYING! You are too old for that now…IT’S TIME FOR YOU TO GROW UP! YOU HEAR ME? ::(John said not a word, merely shaking his head in agreement of his father’s statement. Just when John began to think he would never get out of the situation, his father stormed out of the room without saying a word. Slamming the door behind him, he left Johnny alone, who began to cry even harder than before. As he sat in the chair thinking to himself, he came to the conclusion that perhaps his father was indeed correct, and that it was time for him to do some things differently.):: :::Johnny::: Time to grow up…time to grow up…. ::(Johnny wipes the tears from his eyes and exits the room as the camera fades to black)::
