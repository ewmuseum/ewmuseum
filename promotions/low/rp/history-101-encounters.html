History 101- Encounters

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. The scene opens to a shot from the past, where Eric Russo is sitting in the front passenger seat of the Snake’s deep green 1970 Oldsmobile 442 as it speeds down highway Rt. 666 somewhere in New Mexico. He appears to be deep in thought as he stares out his open widow watching the desert scenery fly by. A voice over by Russo begins.) Russo (VO): “I had a lot of questions running through my mind that morning concerning this surprise that the Snake had sprung on me so suddenly. Who was this that he was taking me to meet? How would we react to each other? Could we really be expected to function as a unit simply because we were both trained by the same men? I decided to voice my concerns to the Snake.” (Russo breaks his stare from the view through the window and turns to the Snake. The Snake stares straight ahead at the road in front of him, seemingly hypnotized by the white lines separating the lanes. Russo begins to speak.) Russo: “Snake, don’t get me wrong when I say this but are you sure about this idea?” (The breach of silence breaks the Snake’s state of hypnosis as he turns his head to glance over at Russo for a moment as he answers.) Snake: “I am trying to make you the best all arounder that has ever been seen Eric. Before I began working with you, you already had ability. I taught you how to use that ability both inside and outside of the ring. Now all that is left is this last realm of competition. If you manage to master this, you will have successfully mastered this sport. That is what I want out of you Eric . . . that is what it has been about from the beginning.” (The Snake returns his attention back to the road in front of him as Russo responds.) Russo: “I understand that, but I just can’t imagine this actually working. I have never been good in teams. My former tag partners have always seemed to do nothing but hold me down. None of them have ever been able to keep up with me long enough to click as a team.” Snake: “This one will be different, I promise. I wouldn’t put the two of you together if I didn’t think that Eric.” (The scene begins to fade out as Russo’s voice over begins once again.) Russo (VO): “I decided that it was best to push my concerns aside for the time being. The Snake had not steered me wrong yet. I settled back into my seat as I just relaxed and enjoyed the rest of the ride to Atlantic City, New Jersey. The Snake knew that to get the higher paying jobs in the larger federations, I would have to first get my name known thought the independent scene. Lucky enough for me, the man that the Snake decided to pair me up with just so happened to own his own promotion. It was a small upstart federation with a cult following known as the UWF. The talent that I was forced to compete against there may have been a little lackluster, but at least it was a job.” (As Russo’s voice stops, the scene snaps back to the present day. Russo is snapped out of his dream like state of reminiscence by Jerry who begins speaking.) Jerry: “Um . . . could you just hold that thought for a second? I’m gonna have to switch tapes.” Russo: “Sure, no problem.” (After a few moments of silence, Russo looks to Jerry questioningly as he begins to speak.) Russo: “So, how are you enjoying the story so far?” (Jerry looks up at Russo to respond as he continues to fiddle with a new tape as he tries to put it in the camera.) Jerry: “I think it’s coming along great so far.” (Jerry pauses for a moment. He then looks back to Russo.) Jerry: “So tell me, did you uh . . . ever really hurt someone . . . uh . . . seriously.” (A smile crosses Russo’s face as he responds.) Russo: “If you mean have I ever ended anyone’s career, I’d have to plead guilty. In fact, I’m thinking about adding another name to that list right this week.” Jerry: “Really? Who?” Russo: “Oh don’t worry, I’ll get to him before I’m through with my story. It’s just not gonna be right now.” (Jerry smirks as he responds.) Jerry: “Well whenever you’re ready, just feel free too. Also, the tape is ready so you can pick up whenever you want.” (Russo perks up.) Russo: “Oh . . . shall we continue then?” (Russo raises his hand to his chin and begins tapping his pointer finger against his chin as he continues before Jerry can even respond.) Russo: “Now where was I? Oh yes . . . that’s right.” (Russo now looks to Jerry as he continues his recount of his story.) Russo: “Now when he saw me, Kage knew full well what an asset someone of my talent would be to his little promotion. For my debut match, he matched me against the world champion at the time. Now understand, the man that reigned as the world champion was someone that Kage could not get the belt away from no matter what he tried. I in a way became his last resort to regain the control of his federation. Being the man that I am, I went into the ring that night and I destroyed my opponent. I made a mockery out of him and all that he stood for. I also saved Kage’s for the first time as well. It however would most definitely not be the last.” (Jerry interrupts with a smile.) Jerry: “Seems like you and Kage did click after all.” Russo: “Surprisingly, we really did. He was the first guy I ever met that knew their place. Kage seemed to understand that even though he had the power, I was the one that allowed him to hold it. I even built a team around Kage to ensure that no harm would ever come to him. I surrounded him with some of the most dangerous men in the world. This unfortunately did not last forever. Soon, egos inside of the group began to clash and a split occurred. On one side, stood Tony Castle, Steve Hayes, Jason Kage, and myself. On the other, a renegade set of brothers by the names of Chris and Slater Storm. Once again, it was up to me to carry Kage’s entire future for him because he couldn’t do it himself.” (Jerry cuts in once again.) Jerry: “Wow, you really took care of Kage didn’t you?” Russo: “You know what Jerry? I did take care of Jason Kage. I treated that man like he was my brother. As far as I was concerned, making the unit of Jason Kage and myself succeed was my top priority.” (Russo hangs his head as he continues.) Russo: “Unfortunately even I would not be able to overcome the battle that was ahead. You see, the Storms were even more conniving then we had originally given them credit for. They realized that they were going to be no match to the team that we had assembled. Legally however they could cause some serious damage. In a strange turn of events, the Storms were able to gain control of the UWF under the pretenses that Kage was unfit to run the company due to his in ring exploits. The Storms however were not prepared for what repercussions their actions would bring . . .” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
