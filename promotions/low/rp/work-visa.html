Work Visa

captured 7 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Canadian Perfect Chris Wrestling LOW Roleplay #697 Date: 2/3/03 Location: Classroom (The scene opens up in what looks to be a classroom. Most of the desks are filled, and it is quite as the people sitting at the desks, sit in silence looking at their watches and other objects. As the camera pans around the classroom, it stops and focuses on a person, wearing a black baseball cap, with their head down. When the camera zooms right in on the person, the person lifts his head and it is revealed the person in the hat is none other than Canadian Perfect himself... Chris Wrestling! Canadian Perfect is looking extremely bored as he sits in silence, looking up at the roof. Canadian Perfect has one a single pen on his desk. A man in a gray suit then walks to the front of the class with a stack of papers under his arm. He puts the papers on the desk and looks up at the class. On the blackboard behind the man, the only writing on the table reads 'TEST TIME - 11:30am to 12:30pm'. The man then clips on a badge that reads 'TEST SUPERVISOR' and then he looks up at the class and clears his throat) (Test Supervisor) Welcome everyone. Today's exam is a simple exam to gain or re-gain your United States of America work visa. I will now hand out the test papers. Please do not open the exam paper until I say so. (The man then hands out the test papers, before heading up the front) (Supervisor) This test goes for 1 hour. There are no aids allowed in this exam. If anyone has accidentally brought in aids or blank pieces of paper, can you please hand them to me now, and there will be no penalty. You may begin writing now! (The people in the room begin the exam. The camera then goes in on Canadian Perfect, who is still looking at the roof. After about a minute, Canadian Perfect then decides to start the exam) (Exam Question) Question One! What is the USA's largest state? (A) California, (B) New York, (C) Florida or (D) Texas? (Canadian Perfect) Hmmm... (E) Mexico! (Exam Question) Question Two! Which term is used when referring to people from Canada? (A) Canadian's, (B) Maple's, (C) Northen's or (D) Mexicans? (Canadian Perfect) Hmmmmm..... (E) Superior to Americans. (Exam Question) Question Three! What is the major religion in the USA? (A) Islam, (B) Christianity, (C) Buddhism, (D) Judaism? (Canadian Perfect) (E) Perfectanity! Man I always thought multiple choice questions were always C, but I am getting a lot of E's! Oh well... (Exam Question) Question Four! Who is the current president of the USA? (A) George W Bush, (B) Bill Clinton, (C) Vince McMahon or (D) Al Gore? (Canadian Perfect) Man another E... (E) The guy off of the West Wing show! (Exam Question) Question Five! What is the nickname for the American currency? (A) Red back, (B) Paper back, (C) Green back or (D) Plastic back? (Canadian Perfect) Another trick question... When they say American they must mean Mexican, cause Mexico is part of North America... Therefore it is another E... (E) Worthless! (Canadian Perfect then flips over the paper and sees that there is 50 questions in total. Canadian Perfect groans) (Canadian Perfect) Screw this... They must be all E's! (Canadian Perfect colors in the entire test answers with E's even though he actually had to write in the 'E' box himself. Canadian Perfect then stands up and hands the paper in to the supervisor. He then goes to shake his hand) (Canadian Perfect) Now here is a little something to make sure that this paper gets me a work visa... OK? (Test Supervisor) I'm sorry sir, I am not allowed to do that! (Canadian Perfect then squeezes the man's hand and the man winces in pain) (Canadian Perfect) Got it? (Test Supervisor) Sure... Whatever you want! (Canadian Perfect then lets go and walks out of the test room, with the camera following close behind. Canadian Perfect then walks out of the building and onto the street. His mobile phone then begins to ring. Canadian Perfect reaches into his pocket and pulls out his mobile phone and answers it) (Canadian Perfect) What do you want now Bob? (Bob) Yo Chris I am in a bit of a mess. I am down at the mall and I think I may have p*ssed off the wrong group of people. I think I may need backup! (Canadian Perfect) Bob! I'll be there in a sec, I'm just around the corner. (Canadian Perfect then jumps into his car and pulls out of the parking lot and drives towards the mall. When Canadian Perfect reaches the mall, he hops out of his car and immediately sees Bob surrounded by a bunch of seven-year-olds. Canadian Perfect swaggers up to them) (Canadian Perfect) Righto, that's enough, beat it you little punks! (One of the kids turns around and begins to laugh) (Kid) Hey look everyone it's that Canadian Perfect guy from the LOW! (Canadian Perfect) Yeah, yeah, yeah. No move along now, there is nothing to see here... Well besides a massive superstar like me! (Kid) My big brother watches LOW and told me that you are going to get your @ss kicked by Pax this Sunday Night! (Canadian Perfect) Well obviously your brother is a mentally retarded ape... And I can see it runs in the family! (Kid) What are you gonna do? Beat me up? (The group of kids begin to laugh and then Bob charges and crash tackles the smart-@ss kid from behind, triggering a small brawl. Canadian Perfect begins picking up some of the kids and tossing them aside until finally he gets to Bob, who he picks up off the ground, so he is looking at him eye-to-eye) (Canadian Perfect) What the hell are you doing Bob? I have better things to do than protect you from little punk @ss kids... I've got Pax in a matter of days you know! It will be the day when the LOW finally has a champion they can be proud of in Canadian Perfect because I am the greatest technical wrestler in history, I am Wrestling and above all I am absolutely Perfect. (Kid) Hey you Canadian Wanker! (Canadian Perfect) What? (The kid then stamps on Canadian Perfect's foot. Canadian Perfect looks down at his foot, then at the kid, before pie facing the kid to the ground. Just as Canadian Perfect pie-faces the kid, the police arrive and grab Canadian Perfect) (Police) You are under arrest. You have the right to remain silent, and everything you say and do can and will be used against you in a court of law! (Canadian Perfect) Oh for Christ sakes Bob, look what you have done now! (The police slap cuffs on Canadian Perfect and Bob before taking them to the squad car, as the screen fades to black)
