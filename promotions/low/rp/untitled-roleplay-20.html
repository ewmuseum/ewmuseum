Declaring War 2 JD: What a match! CS: I thought tonight couldn’t be any better, but somehow the LOW roster managed to do it. JD: Hold that thought Casey, we have something going on backstage. We take you there now. CS: I wonder what’s happening now. ::The scene opens in the locker room of the New Shadow Riders. Strangely, Eric Russo is still in the locker room of the stable. Just then we can hear voices coming from the other room. Russo walks to the doorway and peaks his head inside. In the room, Big Daveylicious stands at the sink washing his hands. To his side, Jason Kage is busy relieving himself in a urinal next to the sink.:: JD: They don’t even realize that Russo is in the dressing room do they? CS: I’m sure they will soon enough. JD: But will it be too late by that time? CS: Quiet, I wanna see what Russo is gonna do. ::Quietly, Russo places his string of firecrackers into the thick metal pot. He then places the pot on the floor as he lights the fuse and takes off. Within a split second, the firecrackers begin popping. The noise from the firecrackers startles the two New shadow Riders. Without thinking, Kage spins from the urinal to see what is causing the noise. Unfortunately for Big Daveylicious, Kage has not yet finished. Big Daveylicious is taken by surprise when a stream of warm wet liquid hits his leg, soaking it.:: JD: Ha . . . Ha . . . Ha! Can you believe what we have just seen!? CS: Russo just caused Kage to mistake Davey for a toilet! JD: I can’t believe Russo would stoop this low! CS: Come on, it may have been low but it was also funny as shit! ::The scene fades out as Big Daveylicious gets into an argument with Jason Kage over Kage’s accidental urination on him.:: Show URL: http://
