History 101- The NRW Years

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. Slowly the logo fades out to black. For a moment, nothing. Then the sound of Russo’s voice speaks out as he continues his recount of the past.) Russo (VO): “With the Snake back at our side, the Shadow Rider group took the NRW by storm. In fact, we managed to capture all but two of the federations titles on our debut night. Thus began the true new era of the NRW.” (The scene fades in to a shot from the past as Russo’s flashback begins. We open in the nicely furnished locker room of the Shadow Riders back in their NRW days. The group of the Snake, Russo, Kage, Francine and Terry “The Rhino” Richards sit around the locker room talking freely as a camera films their every action. The Snake’s voice is the first to be herd as he speaks to the camera in front of him.) Snake: “I have sat back and watched patiently as Jason, Eric, and Terry here have prepared the NRW for my arrival. With their help, I was able to walk out of Carnage last night with not just one of your belts but four of them. And were not even close to being finished yet.” (The Snake adjusts his hair, brushing it out of his face. He then continues.) Snake: “The NRW wants outstanding moments in it’s history!? Just look at last night and you will see history. A lot of people came out here and complained and whined about their belts being taken from them. All I have to say to is that you are the idiots who put them up in the first place. I do remember Jason, Eric, and Terry all warning you time and again that they were going to take your precious titles.” (The scene continues as Russo’s voice take’s the place of the normal dialogue as he continues his story.) Russo (VO): “It’s easy to see now why so many people in the NRW hated us. We were a breed apart from the average talent pool of the NRW. They held that against us and we held it against them. We knew that we were better then our competition, and we were going to prove it. First however we were going to rub the dominance we already had in their faces.” (The Snake reaches down to the ground and grabs hold of something. He returns to his original position only now he is holding a large black bag. The Snake unties the bag and reaches in, he first pulls out the Canadian title which he presents to Terry with a smirk) Snake: “One for you.” (He next pulls out the X-Xtreme title which he hands to Kage.) Snake: “One for you.” (The Snake then reaches into the bag yet again and pulls out the US title which he hands to Russo.) Snake: “And here’s one for you.” (As the Snake returns to sifting through the bag, another voice over by Russo begins.) Russo (VO): “Such a disrespectful action. Taking the belts that everyone had worked so hard to win, taken away by a group of men that had not done one thing to earn them. Funny how much power one can wield when in control. It can be a very intoxicating feeling. One of total bliss. One that many men would kill to have, or at least we would later come to find out. As for now though our celebration continued.” (The scene continues as the Snake reaches into the bag and finally pulls out the European title which he hands to Kage. Kage takes one look at the belt and throws it at Russo. Russo looks down and sees that the belt on his lap is the Europan title. He jumps up causing the belt to fly at Terry. Terry then proceeds to throw the belt to the floor. Seeing this, the Snake cuts in. ) Snake: “Fine . . . fine, give it to her.” (The Snake looks to Francine who quickly yells out. ) Francine: “I don’t want that thing!” Snake: “OK! We’ll then we can all draw straws for it! Is that good enough!?” (The group lets out a collective OK. The entire group draws their straws only to find out that Terry lost. He reluctantly takes the European title and hangs it on his shoulder letting out a grunt followed by a mumble as he does so. The Snake continues speaking.) Snake: “As you can all see, I no longer have any belts. I am too busy to worry about the little titles, I have the world just waiting to be seized by me and once I claim that belt in the name of the Shadow Riders will truly rule over the NRW with it’s presence.” (The scene continues as Russo’s voice resumes.) Russo (VO): “I had waited for the day that I would get to wrestle alongside my mentor. With the Snake once again by my side, my confidence grew to levels I had previously thought of as unattainable. My work illustrated those facts.” (The voice over fades out as the scene continues. Russo remains silent for a moment, examining the silver horses head at the top of his cane. As he continues to study it, he begins speaking to the camera sitting in front of him.) Russo: “You know it’s nice to go back and reminisce once you’re at the top. I remember the first time the Snake handed me this cane all those years ago. He told me that this cane was the symbol of my future. Now I will admit that I did not understand this at the time but now everything is clear to me. You see, this horse’s head not only symbolizes my future but everyone in the NRW too. I mean take a look at us after just one night and think to yourself, if their this good after one night how good will they be in a week... a month... a year? Only time will tell.” (The scene fades out as a voice over by Russo begins once again.) Russo (VO): “As I said these words, visions of the pain and oppression that lied ahead danced through my thoughts. I did not have to wait long however for my chance to turn these visions into a reality. Within a matter of months, the Shadow Riders had successfully established our control over th entire federation. There was no longer anything for us to accomplish. Once that point was reached, Kage and myself decided that it was time for us to move on to bigger and better things. To do that, we had to cut free a few loose ends. Terry and the Snake understood our position, and took the news like any true Shadow Rider would. It was not long however until we found our next target.” (The scene flashes as it switches back to the present day where Russo is still sitting across a table from Jerry the interviewer. Jerry speaks up as he expresses his interest in the form of a question.) Jerry: “What was that?” (Russo smiles as he recounts the events.) Russo: “It was a little federation by the name of SCW. Now although we never had any real problem with the roster of this little backyard venture, we did have a problem with the man behind it.” Jerry: “Really? Who was it?” Russo: “Through various sources, we came to find out that our old friend from the NRW, Jesse Jamester, had found his new home. This federation did not pose much of a challenge to close down, but it is still fun to mention from time to time all the same. The next chapter in my story is much more interesting. It was a period in which the group that I had worked so long and so hard to establish almost fell off the face of the earth.” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
