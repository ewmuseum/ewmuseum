The Ortiz Life- Chapter II

24 July 2004

† In the Mind of a Champion † ....Struggle ....Passion ....Honor ....Pride ....Warrior Those were some of the words that had described Jason Ortiz these past few weeks here at Lights Out Wrestling. It was strange to think that last week, at Realm, his winning streak was finally over. How did this happen. Why was this loss so hard for the man to take. It was hard. He would even admit that. Getting stabbed in the back by the man he once looked up too- Mike Mehyu was really disappointing. To think, Ortiz wanted to be like this man. Why did he have to do it. Why was Mike that way. Now, Jason had realized what his name was all about. “Mean” Mike Mehyu. It fit his persona. It was really disappointing the night after the match, waking up and realizing that your friend had just fucked you over. That was still in his head. He thought of every possibility to why he had done this. Was it for the World Title. That was the key factor in this situation. Jason Ortiz knew that Mike Mehyu wanted his World Title. It wasn’t rocket science. Everyone did. Since the first night he got that title, he has watched his back. Always turning around. Keeping his eyes open. He has to. Mike wants to have that gold raped around his waist again. It was an addiction. An addiction to the World Title. Mike Mehyu had won the title twice, and god knows how bad he wants it again. Same with Annihilation, the man he lost to last week. He knows, sooner or later, all three of those men were stand face to face. In the center of the ring. It might be in a few weeks. Could be never. But deep down, everyone sees it. It would only be a matter of time before those three men fight for the one thing Jason Ortiz loved. The Lights Out World Title. Annihilation was now two above him now. He had two wins. This hurt him very much. Jason had explained over and over about how Annihilation had dropped the ball. But now, he was the one that dropped the fucking thing. It was out of his reach. He dropped the ball because he didn’t pay attention. Because he didn’t pay attention to the people around him. The Warrior, Jason Ortiz should have known. He should have known someone would stab him in the back. But that was all in the past now. He would get his revenge. The Warrior would get his revenge on both of them. Sure Annihilation had two wins upon this man, but sooner or later he would lose. Then he would walk away. With his tail between his legs. Never looking back at Jason Ortiz. That was all put aside right now. Jason had put this whole issue of Realm on the back burner. He would stop worrying about it for a few weeks. He didn’t want to stress over it. This week was a whole new beginning for Jason Ortiz. A new light at the end of the tunnel. Last week, Ortiz had noticed that the one man he enjoyed working with from time to time, Jason Warrior was back in action. He was a Warrior, just like Jason Ortiz had become. For weeks, Jason Ortiz had gained plenty of respect for this man. They had become mutual friends. Each week there friendship became stronger. Then something terrible happened. They started to hate each other. Ortiz parked in his parking space. Jason Warrior got pissed. Jason Warrior drank Ortiz’s orange juice. Jason Ortiz was furious. Both men were at each other’s throats. Trying to hunt each other down each week. The feud became bigger each week. Both men wanting to go at each other in the back. But then, when both of them didn’t expect it. They got their first match together. It was for the Lights Out Wrestling, X-Treme Title. You could see that both men wanted that title. It was bigger than each man had expected. Jason Ortiz, a former Realm Champion. Jason Warrior, a former X-Treme Champion. The match had come and both warriors’ were standing face to face. They looked over, seeing that X-Treme title lying at the corner of the ring. The bell rang. It was over from the start. Jason Ortiz was the superior man in the match. He won. Naturally. Taking down Jason Warrior’s leg with him. After the match he felt bad for the man. He had injured his leg. Jason Warrior would be sidelined for weeks to come. Right after winning the X-Treme title Jason Ortiz dropped it. For his World Title shot. The greatest move in the history of his wresting career. It was a smart move. Anyone would have done it. Hell, even Jason Warrior would have done it if he would have won. It was very simple. After that, Jason had gained the one thing he had been looking for these past few months at Lights Out Wrestling. The World Title. But there was another thing in the past he had been looking for. A Tag Team partner. He had searched high and low. Everywhere for someone that might be interested in making a Tag Team with him. He wanted to see what it was like. To be a Tag Champion. Now, this coming week at Realm, he finally had his partner, in the man he faced just a month ago. Jason Warrior. Both would be together. Two Warriors. Two men looking for some more gold. Jason Ortiz never expected a title shot right now. He was still getting over the shock of him winning the World Title, just a few weeks ago. This title shot wasn’t for him. Instead, this was a chance for Jason Warrior to become the man he used to be. The man that he once respected as the Aftermath Champion. This was his shot to get in the “groove” again. Make something out of his return. Just think what it would be like if these two men won the Tag Titles against The Shadow Riders. It would be HUGE! The last time The Shadow Riders faced both Jason Ortiz and Jason Warrior was when they were the Metallo Zords, a mere cover up for there weakness in themselves. They won. Jason Ortiz will admit that. Why? Because half the time Ortiz and Warrior were to busy at each others necks, they didn’t care about the tag titles. That’s why they won. This time would be different. This time they would focus on the match and not on each other. There hasn’t been two great Warriors’ like Jason Ortiz and Jason Warrior. Tristan Belmont was one, but this was different. He was long gone. Never to return. They were the new Warrior’s. The ones that would bring light into the locker room. Into this world. Never have the odds of winning the World Title been so high for these two men. Now, they have to take down the greatest Tag Team in the history of Lights Out Wrestling. The three time Tag Team Champions, The Shadow Riders. This would be a tough challenge. He wasn’t going to lie. The Ego Riders would be tough to take down. That was their new name. The Ego Riders. It had fit them well. Over the past few years these men have manipulated. Destroyed. Crushed. Made a mockery of everything in this company. They were the men that people feared. That people didn’t want to face. Over time they had fooled everything. Thinking that they were split apart, only to rejoin at the end of the Trigger Era. They fooled Jason Ortiz. Then, they came back as the Metallo Zords. It shocked Jason Ortiz. He would admit that. That was probably the greatest cover up of all times. It shows that they were clever. Smart. Also, very fucking stupid. Why did they have to do all this? For one little return. To pull the masks off and show there faces. It shows that they are scared to come face to face. Face the facts. They wanted to make a name for themselves again. Gain the spotlight they did. It’s funny that the Shadow Riders have to do that. Always be in the spotlight. Every week. Every moment of there lives. They didn’t have to do the Metallo Zords. They didn’t have to do any of it. Deep down, inside of Ortiz’s head, he knew why they did it. They were fading away. People were forgetting their names. Eric Russo and Jason Kage. People forgot about the Riders. It hurt them. They were pushed aside. Out of the spotlight. No longer would they be a great tag team. That’s why they did this. All of this. From the beginning where they came back as the Metallo Zords all the way from the unmasking of the Metallo Zords. They wanted attention. They got it. From the fans they got it. From the people in the back, they got it. They loved the feeling it gave them. A feeling of excitement. It made their ego’s grow even bigger- yet, Ortiz knew that they were already big enough. This had to come to an end. The end of the Shadow Riders. Once and for all. This whole ego thing was becoming a little to far fetched. When would they give up this whole idea of them being great? They weren’t. They were nothing more then a typical tag team. With an ego. What was the difference? What made them different from any other tag team? Was it their manipulation skills? Maybe. Was it their talking skills? Maybe. Was it their wrestling skills. No. In the past the tag team division in Lights Out Wrestling was horrible. There were not many teams out there that could hold down the grounds of The Ego Riders. Until now. Until the era of Jason Ortiz and Jason Warrior. This would be a whole new beginning for great tag teams in Lights Out Wrestling. No longer would this division be plagued by The Shadow Riders. Ortiz and Warrior were put in this match at Realm for a reason. What was the reason you ask. Simple. To win. To take down the souls of two men. Eric Russo and Jason Kage. Everyone in the back knows damn well that the World Champion can take down to egotistical assholes like Eric and Jason Kage. The man has talent. Just think, through all there years in Lights Out Wrestling, the two of them have won three Tag Team titles, and Eric Russo alone won the Realm Title. Give these people a fucking hand. All the Shadow Riders are is a bunch of trash talking wrestlers. Anyone could do it. It’s no challenge. Jason Ortiz does it sometimes, but these people did it for a living. There talking skills were defiantly better than there wrestling skills, anyone could tell you that. If you could rip there mouths from there heads, they would lose. Each week. It would be a fact. Clap for them. They deserve it to be the trash talk kings Really, they do. But it will bit them in the ass soon enough. Watch. Listen. Observe. To think, there victories were false, due to the fact that ever Tag Team they faced were nothing. A mere piss on to the Shadow Riders. Eric Russo’s Realm Title was nothing as well. Look at them. That’s all they have to show for themselves. What does Jason Ortiz have to show for himself. Realm Title. X-Treme Title, and now, the greatest title of them all. World Title. The one title The Ego Riders have never won. Why? Because they aren’t good enough for the title. That’s why they kept their egos in the Tag Division, to prove that was where they belong. It’s true. They belong there. No where else. If any of them were to come back to singles competition, and face Jason Ortiz. They would be done. No more. They know that. They dare not to step out of Tag Division. So instead of stepping out of the Tag Division, Ortiz and Warrior have stepped into it. They have their shot. They are going to take them out, and take back the gold that has been plagued on the shoulders of those ego bastards. When it’s all over.... ....Warriors’ will prevail. ....Destiny will prevail. ....Talent will prevail. ....Jason Warrior will prevail. ....Jason Ortiz will prevail. That‘s not EGO talking that‘s a true WARRIOR speaking! July 22nd, 2004- Downtown, Miami Florida The last time we left Jason Ortiz and his new girlfriend Jessica Devine, he had received his bonus check for winning the Lights Out World Title. It was a great start in this new life that he had. Everything was still running smoothly. The World Title. His life. His girlfriend. Everything. Now, since cashing the check, it seems like his girlfriend has been going on a shopping spree. If you may have remembered, his girlfriend had decided to buy a factory- for some odd reason. She claimed this would be a business for the both of them. She told Ortiz not to worry. He did anyways. To think, his money would be going down the drain for something he would never use. It was worthless. What the fuck did he need a factory before. He was Jason Ortiz. He didn’t need to work anymore. He would do his job at Lights Out Wrestling, that’s it. But, as always, she had insisted for Ortiz to leave all the faith in her. That was really hard for the man to do. Last time he left her with money she went out and bought new furniture for his bedroom, something he didn’t needed. Again, another waste of good money. “Where are we going anyways Jessica?” he muttered, as he reached over the passenger seat in his girlfriend’s car. He taped her on her shoulder. Jessica turned around. “I told you where we are going. We are going to look at our new business that we bought. Remember?” He had no idea what the hell she was talking about. The past few minutes he had been staring at all the pretty women out the window. There were tons of people walking down the sidewalks of Miami. The sun was still high, and the weather there was the typical Miami weather. Hot. Sunny. Warm. You can’t explain it. Every day it stays the same. Until the winter comes, and it starts to cool down. But now, it seems like it doesn’t get less than a hundred at the middle of the night. Hotter during the day. He had to live with it though. That’s why Jason Ortiz loved to live here in Miami. Jason knew a little bit where he was going. He knew he was downtown near most of the restaurants and the big buildings that he could see from his house. He just didn’t know where she was taking him. “I hope this isn’t going to be a waste of money you know. I could have spent it on more important things,” scuffed Ortiz; he pressed his right arm against the window of the black BMW. “Heh.” “What the hell would you spend your money on Jason?” his girlfriend asked politely. Everyone knows what he would LIKE to spend it on. Jason Ortiz mumbled in his head. “Drugs. Crack.” He gave up Crack a few months ago, so in reality, he didn’t need to worry about it anymore. The addiction had somewhat rubbed off, but there was a case still of him wanting the drug. They call that the side affects of using it over time. That’s what happens when you are a drug addict like himself. There were two things in his life far more important then some drug. The World Title. His girlfriend. That was the greatest thing of all. Finally having someone he would love and care about. But now, Ortiz was getting a little agitated with this whole “new business” idea that Jessica had. The car took a sharp left down 75th Street. Jason stared at the sign of the street, and tried to remember if there was even a street called that. “You’re lost. Admit that Jess!” says Jason Ortiz, his voice raising in a louder tone. They weren't lost. Well Jason Ortiz wasn’t but his girlfriend was. “WE ARE NOT LOST!” she screamed. The car pulled to a stop in the center of the road. He was glad that there was no other cars behind him. He knew in few more seconds of keeping this up he would get a right hand slapped a crossed his face. It has happened before. Pissing off Jessica was the last thing he wanted to do before leaving to Realm. They hadn’t gotten in a real big fight for a long time. He wanted to keep it that way. So, like a true gentlemen, he wanted to give the lady some credit. Maybe she was lost, but right now, he would say she wasn’t. To get her off Jason’s back. Jessica Devine pointed out the driver’s side window. “Right there. You see it. That’s our new factory.” He read the name of the sign on the factory. It was hard to read the letters, most of them were old and scratched off, but you still could read it. Hardly. “Frootio’s Cereal.” Jason Ortiz’s chin dropped. Why the fuck did his girlfriend buy a factory that once made CEREAL? What the hell was she thinking. They spent over two hundred thousand dollars on this factory. On this piece of shit. Please give Jason Ortiz a gun- he felt like shooting himself. “Cereal,” he repeated himself. “Cereal. You bought a factory that makes Cereal? To top it off, the place is run down and isn’t in working condition anymore. What were you thinking. What the fuck were you thinking Jess.” A tear rolled down her beautiful face. Look what he did. Made her cry. What a real gentlemen inside of Jason Ortiz. Deep down he was pissed, but seeing his girlfriend cry was making him feel like shit. Maybe it wasn’t that bad. Maybe making a cereal for the hyper children of America was a good thing. It would bring a whole new aspect on the SUGAR for children. It would. The official breakfast of hyperactive little bastards. He even had a name for it. C.R.A.C.K. Cereal for Rocking and Chilling Kids. Jason even had a slogan for it. “Because we all know that the powder gives us that edge.” Her face trembled with tears. “I did this for you Jason. I really did. I thought you would like it,” Miss Devine continued to cry, but you could see she was putting on the act to make him happy. Jason swallowed. “Jessica, I am not that pissed at you. Really. I just think you could have spent money on something better than a Cereal factory. I am gratefully. I truly am. The thing about it is. I have no idea how to run a Cereal Factory. I am Jason Ortiz. Lights Out Wrestler. Lights Out World Champion. I am not Tony the fucking Tiger. I can’t stand outside and scream at kids telling them my Cereal is great. I just can’t.” Ortiz laughed. “So....” she continued in a soft voice. “You don’t want the factory?” Damn it. He was put in a tough decision. Take the factory and make her happy. Loss more money. Get rid of the factory, and make her sad. Gain more money. FUCK. Why was he put on this earth anyways. “I guess we will keep it. If you want to run it, be my guest. I want no part of the fucking thing. I will help you out, but I am not going to dress up in a suit and stand outside and wave at people. I will stab myself in the eye before I do that.” He could see that her face was lighting up with joy. She leaned over in the BMW and gave him a big kiss on his lips. Jason Ortiz smiled. Ortiz leaned over and kissed her again. “You won’t be let down Jason.” That’s what he was afraid of.
