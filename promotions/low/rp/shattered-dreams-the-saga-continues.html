Shattered Dreams- The Saga Continues

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. The scene opens to a shot on an old, dusty, country road. Russo slowly plods along the side of the road towards an unknown destination. As he continues forward as a voice over begins.) Russo (VO): “It had been almost a week since my loss to Tristan Belmont. That match however was no longer having an effect on my mind set. No, by this time my mind was focused on a new goal. A goal that I had fought for so many times in the past. This time my mind was focused on the upcoming clash between myself and my mortal enemy, Jesse Jamester.” (Russo pauses for a moment. He then continues in the voice over.) Russo (VO): “This was the first time that I would get to go one on one with Jamester inside of an LOW ring. It was an opportunity that I had been waiting for since my arrival in the federation. Sure, adding a name like Jesse Jamester to my win record wouldn’t do all that much for me professionally, but personally it was a whole different story.” (Russo continues to elaborate.) Russo (VO): “This match between myself and Jamester was going to mean a lot more then a simple win or loss. This match was going to be the final chapter to our story. This match was going to mark the end of over a year worth of hate. This match was going to be one that I could not afford to lose. This was a match that I wouldn’t lose.” (The scene fades out as Russo’s voice continues.) Russo (VO): “So I pushed myself forward using the desire that now consumed my very being. The desire to once again reign supreme over all that I see. I was out to win, and I was ready to do whatever was needed to succeed. I soon began my preparation for the upcoming match with Jamester. To win, I was going to push myself harder and further then I ever had before. I was more then ready for the challenge.” (Russo’s voice pauses for a moment.) Russo (VO): “I however did not expect what was going to happen next . . .” (The scene reopens to a shot of Eric Russo. He is sitting, with an ice pack on top of his injured knee trying to freeze out the pain coursing through it, on the steps of a rundown farmhouse. As Russo sits massaging his injured knee, another voice over begins.) Russo (VO): “I was soon to find out that I was not in the condition that I used to be. A career worth of pain and agony had finally taken it’s toll on my body and I could defiantly feel the effects. This however was something I was not going to let stop me from getting what I want. I had learned to work through pain before and I would do it again. It is what was about to happen next that really threw me for a loop. As I looked into the bushes across the road from my makeshift home while I training, I heard a rustling. Upon closer inspection, I realized that I was being watched . . .” (The scene quickly switches to a view of the bushes from Russo’s vantage point. There in the bushes, a short, stout, man intently watches the superstar with a pair of binoculars. This seems to unnerve Russo somewhat as he reacts.) Russo: “Who the hell are you!? What do you want!?” (At this point, Russo notices that the man has a elongated, thin, metal, cylinder beside him. Given the fans current opinion of him, Russo naturally assumes that it is a gun.) Russo: “Shit . . . shit! He’s got a gun!” (Russo immediately dives to the side and ducks for cover. The man from the bushes makes his way out of the thick underbrush. As he does so, we realize that what Russo has mistaken for a gun is actually nothing more then the man’s cane which he uses to support himself as he walks. The man hobbles over towards the still cowering Russo. Once he reaches him, the man pulls out a clipboard and pen as he begins to speak.) Man: “Excuse me . . . Mr. Russo, you’re gonna have to sign for this.” (Russo looks up at the man who holds the clipboard out for him. Somewhat confused, Russo pushes himself to his feet as he responds.) Russo: “Wait . . . I have to sign that paper before you shoot me?” (The man looks at Russo with a bewildered look as he responds.) Man: “What? I’m not here to shoot you.” (Russo reaches out and grabs the clipboard.) Russo: “Why not?” (Not sure how to respond to the question, the man does his best.) Man: “I don’t really do that kind of thing. I’m a private detective and I was hired to find you. I don’t know where you got the idea that I was here to kill you. I’m just here to deliver this letter.” (The man pulls a small white envelope from his pocket. With a nod of understanding, Russo hands the signed clipboard back to the man. He then snatches his letter as the man responds.) Man: “Well I’m sure that I’ll be seeing you around, so long.” (As the man turns to walk off, Russo responds.) Russo : “Thank you.” (Russo looks down at the letter and opens it up as he begins to read it aloud.) Russo (Reading): “Dear Mr. Russo, please visit me, suite 2580, at the Liberty Plaza Tower, Philadelphia. I have something of great importance to inform you of.” (The scene fades out as a voice over by Russo begins.) Russo (VO): “I had no idea what this letter meant, but I decided to check it out nonetheless. I knew that I could not really afford this break in my training for Jamester, but my curiosity had won over. So without a clue of what was awaiting me, I traveled to the closest airport and caught the first flight out to Philadelphia. There I found out that the letter I had received had been sent to me by a Mr. Josh Daniels. What exactly this man wanted with me, I did not know. I was however about to find out.” (The scene reopens in the penthouse office of Josh Daniels. Daniels, a young man in his late twenties, sits behind his desk as though awaiting an extremely important meeting. Slowly the door to the office opens to reveal Eric Russo. Daniels springs to his feet at the sight of the Shadow Rider. In an excited manner, Daniels greets Russo.) Daniels: “Eric! So good to see you again . . . remember me?” (With a slight smirk, Russo responds.) Russo: “No, not really. I don’t remember a lot of the little people though so don’t feel bad.” (Paying no attention to Russo’s less then friendly comments, Daniels continues.) Daniels: “It’s me . . . Josh Daniels! Remember, the bar down in Texas? Man, you are one hard guy to get a hold of.” (Daniels notices that none of his comments seem to be ringing a bell with Russo.) Daniels: “I can’t believe you don’t remember me! You gave me the ok to go ahead with the Shadow Rider cartoon! Do you remember me now? Am I starting to ring a bell yet? I went ahead with the production.” (Russo’s eyes light up as he responds.) Russo: “Oh yeah, I almost forgot about that. How are things going with it?” (Russo reaches for what appears to be one of many story boards for the show as Daniels responds.) Daniels: “It’s going great in Japan!” (Russo looks at Daniels with a disconcerted look.) Russo: “Japan?” Daniels: “Yeah, that’s where we produce all of our cartoons. Japan lets us have a lot more freedom with what we can do in our cartoons. The Shadow Rider one would have never gotten the ok in the states the way we wanted to produce it. It’s a good thing too, the cartoon has really caught on over there. You’re like a national hero.” Russo: “So what you’re telling me is that I’m a megastar, but in Japan? That’s what you called me all the way to Philadelphia to tell me? You couldn’t just tell me that in the letter that you sent!? What the hell do I care about what is happing in Japan!?” (Daniels tries to get a word in.) Daniels: “No, you don’t understand. We . . .” (Russo quickly cuts him off, not allowing him to get is explanation out.) Russo: “I don’t wanna hear it! I have more important things to worry about. I have a big match coming up this Saturday. I don’t have time to waste sitting around and talking about my fame over in Japan. Seriously, when was the last time I even went to Japan!?” (Finally Daniels cannot take any more and he bursts into the conversation, determined to get his point across.) Daniels: “Eric, look we’re in business! I’m splitting everything with you, fifty-fifty. It’s just like we said when you gave me the go ahead. In fact I have your first check right here. It’s for two-hundred and fifty big ones.” (Taken aback by the statement, Russo manages to get a few words out through his shock.) Russo: “Two-hundred and fifty?” Daniels: “Oh and that’s just the beginning my good friend. There’s going to be more. Lots . . . lots more.” (Russo looks down at his check then back up at Daniels.) Russo: “Can I cash this thing?” (Daniels replies with a smile.) Daniels: “You can do whatever you want with that money. It’s yours, spend it however you want.” (Russo’s face fills with a smile. He responds.) Russo: “Don’t worry, I will.” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
