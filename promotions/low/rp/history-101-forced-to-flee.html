History 101- Forced to Flee

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. The scene opens to a shot from the past where Russo and the Snake are sitting at opposite ends of a finely crafted table inside of a plush dining room. On the table in front of them sits a couple untouched plates of food. The Snake begins plucking the grapes off of a plate of fruit and throwing them indiscriminately at the wall of the room as he begins to speak.) Snake: “Consider yourself lucky Eric, in New York a wrestler has to be able to do it all without ever second guessing himself. Not to mention it’s not as easy to talk yourself out or trouble over there.” Russo: “New York?” (Paying little attention to Russo, the Snake continues.) Snake: “Around these parts all you have to do is keep yourself out of trouble is know how to talk to these country bumpkins.” (Russo questions the Snake again hoping to get a better answer this time.) Russo: “You came here from New York?” (Knowing that Russo will keep questioning until he finds out what he wants to know, the Snake decides that it would be best to just answer his question.) Snake: “Yes, that’s where I gained all my knowledge. That’s the big time Eric and that’s what I’m trying to prepare you for.” (Showing that the conversation has sparked his attention, Russo continues with his line of questioning.) Russo: “Would you tell me about the man who trained you there? I mean you must have learned the things you know from someone.” Snake: “I learned everything on my own through experience. I wasn’t as lucky as you are now to have someone like me to guide my career.” Russo: “But you must have had some kind of mentor in the sport. Someone who taught you how to function between the ropes. That kind of stuff does not just occur naturally, you have to learn it.” (The snake slams his fist down onto the table in front of him.) Snake: “Why, why do you think I had to be trained by someone!? Everything I have ever gotten, I have gotten on my own!” (The Snake rubs his brow, massaging the skin with his fingers as he continues once again in a calm even tone.) Snake: “I don’t want to talk about this anymore Eric. It tires me to dwell on the past when there is so much ahead of us.” (Trying to make the Snake understand why he is so curious, Russo responds.) Russo: “The only reason I ask so much is that I think we might have to be moving on from this territory very soon.” (Questioningly, the Snake looks up.) Snake: “And why is that, just out of curiosity.” Russo: “I know that being the most hated wrestler is what makes me my money, but I am starting to fear for my safety each and every time I walk out to that ring.” Snake: “Believe me, I won’t let any serious harm come to you Eric. Not like it has come to me in the past.” (With that thought, the scene fades out as a voice over begins.) Russo (VO): “It was at that time that I realized that me being a wrestler meant revenge to him. Revenge against all that had been done wrong to him in the past throughout his career. Every time he taught me a lesson, it was his form of revenge on the business and those who loved it. Without me realizing it, he was training me to become something no one was prepared for. He was training me to become a machine that was fueled by hate and greed. A machine that many would try and destroy.” (The scene reopens at a later time about a hundred yards off from the house where Russo and the Snake are situated. A group of men stand around a small pickup truck. In their hands, each man holds a bottle filled with a fire bomb. Rags drenched in gasoline hang out from the neck of the bottles. From the sight of them, it appears as though the men have consumed a fair amount of alcohol. The men now pile into the truck and start towards the home with evil intentions filling their minds. Inside the house, Russo is sitting in his living room watching tapes of his opponents past matches. He watches intently looking for even the smallest weaknesses in their wrestling styles. The Snake, is nowhere to be seen at the time. Back outside of the house, the men in the pickup truck pull along the side of the house and park. They quickly file out of the truck and begin to plan their attack. One of the men begins to speak to the others.) Man #1: “Ok, does everyone have their stuff.” (The collective group in front of him nods their heads. The man continues.) Man #1: “Good. Everyone take a different window and remember to throw the bottle hard enough to break the glass in the window and get it in the house after you light it.” (As the man finishes his instructions, the group begins to break off in different directions as they circle the home. It is at this point that Russ begins to hear the rustling outside of the windows. He passes off the sound as nothing more then some sort of wild animal looking for food in the thick bushes just below the windows. His opinion of the sound is soon changed however as the window behind him shatters ad one of the fire bombs comes flying into the room where he is sitting. The bottle crashes against the wall and quickly engulfs it in flames. Before he has a chance to react, the other rooms throughout the house are beginning to burn as the other men on the outside launch their bottles through the home’s windows. Russo begins wandering from room to room feeling his way through the areas where the smoke has become to thick to see through as he searches for an exit to this horror. He looks on as all that he has amassed through his hard work melts and contorts as it is engulfed by flames. From the look of him, we can tell that heat and fumes from the raging inferno are beginning to weaken him. Back outside, the men frantically load back into their truck and take off into the night, hooting and hollering into the air, happy with the drastic retribution that they have preformed out of their hate for the wrestling superstar. Inside the house, Russo continues to deliriously look for an exit to save himself. Suddenly, a door behind him swings open as the Snake enters the room up from the basement of the home. He is taken by surprise at the sight before his eyes. Through the thick smoke, the Snake spots Russo over by one of the walls. He calls out to him.) Snake: “Eric, is that you!? What’s going on!?” (Blindly, Russo yells back into the thick smoke in front of him.) Russo: “I can’t see you! I can’t find my way out of here! They threw fire bombs through the windows when I was watching the tapes! Everything is torched!” Snake: “Just follow my voice Eric, we can still get out through the basement!” (Putting his faith in the Snake, Russo begins to make his way through the thick smoke. Before he can make it though, he body gives out on him from exhaustion due to the heat and smoke, and he collapses on the floor in a heap. Seeing this, the Snake quickly moves in and lifts Russo up and over his shoulder. With Russo now in his possession, the Snake makes his exit back through the door from which he came into the room. He manages to make it down the basement steps and out through the back door of the house. The Snake continues to move away from the house until he feels that he has gained a safe distance from the bellowing fire that has now consumed the entire house. Once there, the Snake drops Russo off of his shoulder and to the ground. Fatigued, he drops down next to his protege as he watches the light from the fire burning the house that he called a home fill the night sky with light. As he sits watching on, he turns to the still unconscious Russo as he begins to speak.) Snake: “Maybe it is time to move on to a new territory.” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
