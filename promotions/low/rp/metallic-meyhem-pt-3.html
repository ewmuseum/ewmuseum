Metallic Meyhem Pt. 3

6 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~{ The last time we saw the Metallo Zords, it appeared as though they had come across a villain so utterly shocking that they could not believe their own eyes. When the evil Queen Mogambu decided that she needed to special order her next ‘monster’, she demanded a new creation be made immediately to thwart the good intentions of the Metallo Zords. Shortly there after, the Metallo Zords were put on alert about a group of Ich Van Herbes, Queen Mogambu’s foot soldiers, wreaking havoc in the park. When the Rangers went to check it out, what they found was a completely empty park with no trace of the Ich Van Herbes. Although unusual, the Rangers were about to chalk it up as a sensory malfunction and return to their control center. Much to their surprise however, a bolt of lighting struck the ground between them, causing a puff of smoke from which a new figure would emerge. The figure, giving off a metallic glare, sent the Metallo Zords into a state of panic and shock. This new villain, created by Queen Mogambu, left the Metallo Zords in disbelief. We now join our heroes back at the command center, where they are trying to piece together what they saw with the help of Bobbalu and Bobbabu. }~~~ ::We open to a scene of the Metallo Zords standing on the main floor of the control center. Behind them is the Orb of Knowledge, which currently features a plain white smoke within it. Before them, Bobbalu scurries around franticly, trying to settle down the equipment within the room. Something seems to have sent all the computers, machines, and electronic devices into a frenzy, causing all of the equipment to begin malfunctioning. Beyond the computers is the tube that houses the giant floating head of Bobbabu, the commander and chief of the Metallo Zords. Despite all of the trouble in the room, Bobbabu seems to be relatively collected. We begin to pick up the scene as the Rangers and Bobbabu go over what has and still is occurring.:: Gold Ranger – But Bobbabu, I don’t understand. What we saw out there cannot be true. It just cannot. You said it yourself, the title of Metallo Zord is a sacred blessing passed down millennium after millennium to worthy hand servants of justice. This creature . . . this man that we saw out there was not a guardian of justice. It does not make any sense. Does it make sense to you, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – You are correct. What you saw out there was not a keeper of peace. Rather, he is a proponent of evil. Silver Ranger – But how then could he appear to be one of the guardians? Why is it that he looked so much like . . . like one of us? Bobbabu – The reason for ----- ::An upset Silver Ranger continues his thought in his computer altered robotic sounding voice.:: Silver Ranger – I thought you told us that the right of the Metallo Zord was exclusive to those who were chosen to protect the universe and all of it’s inhabitants. If that is so, how then did this puddle of scum and villainy appear to be on the side of justice? Are not our suits, helmets, and equipment specially made for Metallo Zords only? Is this not true, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – From what I can gather, the man that you saw out in the park was a creation of Queen Mogambu. You surprised the Queen when you defeated her last plan and stopped her from taking over the world, so she turned to the next thing she could. She’s going to try to destroy you two by any means necessary as long as you stand between her and intergalactic domination. Gold Ranger – But that still does not explain how an evil entity gained access to gear similar to that of ours. There is no duplication of our suits or helmets, and our gadgets are one of a kind. Silver Ranger – Two of a kind. Gold Ranger – Yes, since we both have them I guess they would be two of a kind. However, how did this evil force manage to attain such items? Furthermore, how did his phony duplications look to be so real? To what do we owe this false frame of view, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – There is something I never told you, Rangers? There is something I thought would be best kept a secret, but I see know my notion was not correct. Gold Ranger – In what is this in relation to, Bobbabu? Silver Ranger – Please do not tell us that we are through, Bobbabu. ::Bobbabu sighs and begins to tell the Metallo Zords just what the big secret is.:: Bobbabu – Rangers, you must forgive me for keeping this from you for so long. I never thought this would ever come up. Everything I told you was true. You were chosen among all Earth dwelling creatures to protect and serve the cause of justice from the intergalactic threat of Queen Mogambu. You were indeed to fill the role of the Metallo Zord legacy. What I said was true. However, there was a factor that I failed to tell you. ::In a robotic voice, the Gold Ranger questions Bobbabu.:: Gold Ranger – Is it that we are an intergalactic break through, Bobbabu? Silver Ranger – That we have another secret headquarters in Kalamazoo, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – If only it were that simple, Rangers. If only if it were that simple. ::Bobbabu bows his head down in it’s cylindrical tube before he answers.:: Bobbabu – When the concept of guardians to watch over the universe was first brought up, it was thought that to keep intergalactic peace secure, a number of people would be needed to watch over the universe. This is why the Metallo Zords were created. For millennium after millennium, the Metallo Zords have been used to keep rival planets from warring, and to keep larger star systems from invading smaller, less technically advanced ones. Such was the case with Queen Mogambu’s evil army and earth. As you know you were selected to keep Earth save from her evil reign. Gold Ranger – Yes, we know, but how does this relate to what we had to hear from you, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – Oh, excuse me, I seemed to have trailed off. Back when the Metallo Zords were first created, it was believed that the larger the group was to watch over a specific planet, the more secure the planet would be. This is why enough suits were created to sustain a force of four peace keeping Metallo Zords. ::The shocked Silver Ranger responds.:: Silver Ranger – F-F-F-Four Metallo Zords? Bobbabu – Yes, four Metallo Zords. Gold Ranger – How come there is but two of us? Will a duo do, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – It was decided shortly after the suits were made that a group of four would be two hard to maintain control of. With four people come four separate wills, and it would be nearly impossible to keep them all functioning on the same page. That is why two of the suits were immediately destroyed, leaving only two in tact. The two that remained, the Gold suit and the Silver suit, now grace the two of you. Silver Ranger – But if the suits were destroyed, how did Queen Mogambu add this new member to her crew, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – The source of power for your suits, helmets, and all of you’re equipment is not spread throughout your bodies. Rather, there is a central power unit within your suits from which all of your powers emanate. Gold Ranger – And from where does our power accrue, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – If you direct your attention down towards your waists you will notice you’re belt buckles. These are no ordinary belt buckles, I assure you of that. The buckles that adorn your waists are indeed the source of all your powers. The supply your suits with it’s heavy metallic armor, they run all of the power to your helmets, and all of your equipment is energized by them. These buckles are what make you Metallo Zords. Without them, the Metallo Zords do not exist. ::Stunned, the Metallo Zords take a second to absorb all of this.:: Silver Ranger – But that still does not explain how Queen Mogambu managed to send out her mock Metallo. If the whole costumes were destroyed, the power of the buckles would be voided, would it not? Did Queen Mogambu come to the buckles’ rescue, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – Indeed. The buckles were pulled from the fiery wreckage before they were destroyed. Mogambu’s henchmen managed to save the buckles, but I never thought they’d be able to put them to use. Without suits and helmets, there is nothing that the buckles can supply energy to. Although by the looks of things, Mogambu’s monster makers were able to reconstruct the suits and helmets. Thus, there is a new threat to the galaxy, more specifically, the planet Earth. Gold Ranger – But why now? Why did it take so long for this new metallic threat to debut, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – I can only imagine that Queen Mogambu was previously unable to make a compatible suit. Another possibility however is that the evil Queen may not have ever seen a need for this new warrior until now. Mind you, she’s faced relatively no resistance from the planets she’s invaded up until this point. Until you, no one stood in her way. She may think that she can eliminate you through use of this new entity. Silver Ranger – Why did you not tell us of this hitherto, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – Like I said, I never thought I’d have to. Mogambu’s use of this ranger is most unexpected. ::There is a pause in the conversation as the Metallo Zords are pushed to the side by the small robot known as Bobbalu. Bobbalu continues working on the machines that surround them.:: Gold Ranger – I do not understand how this person caught us off guard. Our Metallo Monitors would have usually warned us of approaching danger. What kept this villain out of view, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – When the other two suits were destroyed, that Metallo Zord technology was altered accordingly. Thus with the addition of a third ranger to the fray, your powers were sent awry. Unfortunately, you will not be able to sense the approach of this villain for quite some time. Never in fact. As long as he is around, you’re suits, helmets, equipment, and most importantly your belt buckles will not function properly. Your powers are neutralized by his presence, rendering you nearly helpless save for your natural abilities. Gold Ranger – What about the computers here, is that why they were sent askew, Bobbabu? Bobbabu – Indeed, that is why the computers here are not working correctly. ::The room goes quiet and the Metallo Zords contemplate their next step.:: Silver Ranger – Wait a moment. There were originally four suits. Two of them are being used by the two of us, so that accounts for the Gold and Silver suits. Queen Mogambu saved the other two buckles, but there is only one person using one of them for evil. What became of this forth buckle? My mind cannot construe, Bobbabu. Bobbabu – Your question is legitimate and your worry is warranted. However, you will be relieved to find out that the forth and final Metallo buckle was confiscated from Mogambu and her evil forces thousands of years ago. It is said to be hidden in the depths of darkness, where no one would ever recover it. There is no need to fret over this, as it will never prove to be an issue of concern. Gold Ranger – I sure hope that is true, Bobbabu. Silver Ranger – I sure do too, Bobbabu. Bobbabu – As do I, Rangers. As do I. ::The scene fades away and we cut to a picture of Queen Mogambu’s palace on the moon. There we see the evil Queen along with her sidekicks, Raziel, Big Bad Barry Bear, Lulu de Blu, and Tim. Queen Mogambu peers into her crystal ball, viewing the Metallo Zords in their command center. She quickly turns away from the globe and begins shouting to her servants.:: Queen Mogambu – Ah ha ha! This is working much greater than even I could have expected! We have those pesky little Metallo Zords right where we want them now! Tim – Yes, my Queen, our army could squash them at any minute! Big Bad Barry Bear – Or better yet, our new friend in the metallic armor could do the job for us and wipe those meddling Metallos out once and for all! Queen Mogambu – Yes, he could do that without a problem, as could our army. With their Metallo powers taken away from them, we could destroy them at our leisure! Tim – Yes, destroy them! Queen Mogambu – But that wouldn’t be any fun, now would it? After all the trouble those Metallo Zords have caused me, they don’t deserve a quick and sudden death! They need to be punished! Tortured! Tim – As leader of your army and second in command of your intergalactic empire, I must advice that we eliminate their threat immediately! Queen Mogambu – Poppycock! I will see to it that their deaths are as slow and agonizing as possible! Raziel, make me more monsters! Raziel – More monsters, my good Queen? Queen Mogambu – What part of that didn’t you understand?! Yes, more monsters! Raziel – Yes, my Queen. ::Raziel scurries off into the distance to work on the new monsters for the Queen.:: Queen Mogambu – With the Metallo Zords powers crippled, our monsters should have no problem putting them through hell! Muahahahahaha! ::The gang begin laughing together now as the scene once again cuts. We are taken back to the inside of the Metallo Zords command center, where the Silver and Gold Ranger still stand talking to Bobbabu.:: Bobbabu – And that is how little baby Rangers are made. Gold Ranger – Ah, I see, that explains a lot. Silver Ranger – I’ll say it does. ::A panicked Bobbabu yells out.:: Bobbabu – Rangers, look at the Orb of Knowledge! ::The Metallo Zords turn around and stare into the orb that sits behind them. In it we see a giant monster destroying buildings in the down town area of the city. The monster, which appears to be an oversized Chihuahua is kicking down numerous buildings as it marches through the streets. Soon, another monster becomes visible to it’s left, joining it in the destruction. This monster is not as clear, as half of it is still out of camera’s view. In the command center, the Metallo Zords ready themselves for battle.:: Gold Ranger – We have to stop those things! Silver Ranger – Let us get to it! Bobbabu – Rangers, I must forewarn you, as long as this metallic clad villain is around, you will not have your powers. That means that your helmets and weapons and hi-tech suits will do you no good in this battle. Silver Ranger – What about our ----- Bobbabu – Yes, your Zords will be rendered useless as well. If you chose to enter the city and attempt to stop these monsters, you will have to do it as mere humans. The choice is yours’ Rangers. Choose wisely. ::The Metallo Zords turn back around to look at the carnage being wreaked down town. As they watch the monsters destroy parts of the city through the Orb of Knowledge, the picture grows more vague. It continues to do so until it disappears completely, then the Orb fills with static.:: Bobbalu – It looks as though the Orb of Knowledge has been short circuited! Oh my, we’re losing power! ::The Metallo Zords look back into the Orb of Knowledge, and for one brief second the static disappears. For that solitary second, the entire Orb fills with an image. The image of a helmet. The helmet of Mogambu’s metallic warrior. The Orb again fills with static. Our picture now dies out as well. What will become of our heroes? Without their powers, will they be able to save the city? More importantly, will they manage to save the world? Have they met their match in this metallic clad enemy? Are there any fire hydrants big enough to service the giant Chihuahua monster? Answers to all these questions and more, next time we meet up with the Metallo Zords.::
