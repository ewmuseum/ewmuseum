Together Part 2

30 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Hello?' No answer, just silence. 'Is anybody there?' Still nobody responds, still the eerie sound of nothing. 'Fuck this.' Cyrus lashed out punching one of the metal bars of his prison cell with his fist, he brought it back quickly as the pain flowed into his knuckles. He looked at his now red hand and cursed loudly. Was this it? Was he going to be trapped inside his own mind forever? He couldn't stand living this way; it was eating him up. He couldn't do anything, but yet he still felt a part of his mind, he still felt attached. He slouched on his bed; he had to think of something, before his other personality took over, permanently. ---------- 'So let me get this right, you were only trying to help me all along? Even from the beginning?' Cyrus spoke to this unknown man, he seemed to have been in his life before, but was he an aid? Was he really there to help him? Cyrus would find out soon. He looked at the phone in his hand with shock, he'd always thought this man was trying to kill him. Now he had the hard task of trying to accept his help. 'Come round to my house in five minutes, I want to speak to you in person.' The offer had been placed, the man accepted. Cyrus had planned a little test, he knew if this worked then he'd know whether the man was telling the truth or not. He'd done the hard bit and gotten him round to his house, now it was just a matter of waiting. ---------- The doorbell rung and Cyrus arose from his seat, he went towards his door and turned the key. He opened the door and let the Dr. in. He took his jacket and hung it up for him then beckoned him to sit down. The Dr. sat down on the leather sofa whilst Cyrus sat on one of his leather chairs. 'So, let me get this right, you never wanted to kill me? Right from the moment you brought yourself into Cyrus's life you were trying to help me? Why should I believe that?' The Dr. smiled a little and readjusted the way he was sitting. He'd been thinking about what to say now for a few days now. He'd prepared himself quite well for the following speech. 'Because Cyrus, I know how we can make sure he stays trapped forever. It involves one person, one being. Somebody who isn't normal, just like you Cyrus. As long as you are aligned with her Cyrus he will be trapped inside of you forever.' With that Cyrus walked over to the Dr. and shook his hand. The perspiration dripped off the Dr.'s forehead as he was not comfortable being this close to the new Cyrus. Cyrus then placed his hands upon the Dr. and forced him up out of his seat. 'Thank you for that Dr. I don't need your assistance anymore. Now get the hell out of my house!' The Dr. was thrust out of Cyrus's front door and onto the cold, hard floor below. He picked himself up off the floor and brushed the dust off his shirt, he turned around and then realized he'd forgotten his jacket. He turned around to see his jacket flying through the air aimed right at his face. He managed to catch it and cursed at Cyrus who was stood in the doorway smiling. 'You'll pay for this Cyrus.' Muttered the Dr. under his breath as he walked over to his car. Cyrus slammed his front door shut causing the Dr. to turn around expecting more punishment. His heart skipped a beat, then he opened his car door and got in. He waited a few seconds and sighed. He hadn't expected that to happen. Cyrus, the 'new' Cyrus, had tricked him. He was far more manipulative than the real Cyrus was. The sun was bright, something gleamed high up on a roof. It caught the Dr.'s eye and he looked up. The bullet entered through his right eye socket. Blood sprayed out of the wound covering the Dr.'s clothes and his windscreen in front of him. His body slumped forward, free from life. ---------- There was somebody at the door again, Cyrus rushed downstairs and opened the door to see himself face to face with a Police Officer. He smiled a sly grin as if to say he knew why he was there, the officer took no notice and began to speak in a monotone. 'Mr. Skar, I'd like a statement off you regarding the last activities of a man called Derek Blowstein. He was shot dead right outside your house. You're not under suspicion but we would like to know everything that happened before he was shot.' Cyrus took a deep breath and then began to speak in an evil tone. He was going to tell the Police what how he'd acted before the Dr.'s death, they would presume it was him immediately. He couldn't risk telling the truth, it's not as if there were any witnesses… ---------- He sat on his bed and listened, listened to the lies coming out of his own mouth. He listened to himself breaking the law. He heard himself shut the door, he heard his evil little cackle after he'd gone back upstairs. He hated it, he lashed out slamming his hands against his bed. 'You lying bastard! How could you?' Cyrus was irate, he couldn't believe his other personality had committed so many sins in his body, it was driving him crazy. The worse thing about it was he could do nothing, he was as helpless as an ambulance driver with no limbs. He felt so worthless, then he spotted a sharp fragment of glass from when he'd smashed his drinking glass on the floor. The guard obviously hadn't cleared it all away. He'd regret not doing that… ----------
