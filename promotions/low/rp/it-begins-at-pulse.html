It Begins at Pulse

23 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fairy Man of the River Styx: Come aboard each and every one of you. It is time to go and visit a man we haven’t visited in many months. To most of you this probably felt more like an eternity. But once again you will have your chance to leave the fiery pits of Hell to gaze upon your King as he searches for more souls to drag down into the pits of hell. ~~As everyone boards the boat it begins to float down the lava river known as the River Styx. As it floats down the river the fairy man begins to speak again.~~ Fairy Man: Here we had thought our King had given up. It isn’t so though it appears. But I’m starting to wonder if his objective really is the same or if it has changed to something different. ~~Off in the distance, past all the flying demons, the fiery tornado's and the fire geysers, the castle of Hell can be seen. As the boat flows down the river we get closer and closer to the castle. The Fairy Man continues.~~ Fairy Man: I have stuck off in the night from my post and into the room of Ron Diamond himself. I have seen him lay there many-a-night without any rest at all. He lays there and talks to himself about a new love and how his old love seems to be slipping away. It is odd for the ruler of Hell to love at all, but our King seems to have a heart. It may be black as night, but none the less, it seems as though he really does have a heart. ~~The boat pulls up next to the caverns that lead up into the castle of Hell. When the boat stops, the Fairy Man steps out and begins to escort the souls off Hell from the boat.~~ Fairy Man: Be careful not to fall into the lake. You thing your pits of fire are Hell to deal with? Try drowning in lava for the rest of eternity. We must now take our mist form so that we may not be noticed. Many demons lie ahead. Stay close to me as well, it is very easy to become lost in the caverns. ~~The Fairy Man of the River Styx and the souls of Hell turn into a mist form and begin to move through the corridors of the caverns. On their way through many screams can be heard. In some of the walls are cells where souls are being tortured. Some are being tortured by demons pecking their eyes out. Others are being poked and prodded by demon spears. Some are even being tortured by demons throwing fiery balls at the soul. The Fairy Man and the souls following him quickly make their way through the caverns of the castle of Hell and into the castle itself. They make their way down an empty hall way. When they reach the end they move through the key hole of a door into a wide open room where we see a man sitting on a throne made of black bones, probably black from being burnt. The man is of average size and is wearing a red trench coat. He has a black t-shirt on along with black leather boats, black leather gloves, black leather baggy jeans and black sunglasses. His hair is long and is jet black. Little horns can be seen protruding from his forehead.~~ Fairy Man: There he is, our Lord… our Master… OUR KING! Let’s quickly dive into the mind of our King to see exactly what our master is thinking. ~~The Fairy Man and the souls following him dive into the head of Ron Diamond. The moment they dive into his mind they are flooded with thoughts from their King.~~ ~|~Thoughts~|~ ~|~Why must things become so complicated? How does a evil man like me fall in love with another woman? I fell in love once but that love seems to be falling apart, now another love has crept into my life. I know this should be the least of my concern for my debut in LOW is soon at hand. Some place new, a place that is suppose to be so much more exciting that any other wrestling federation on the face of the earth. A place where all the best talent is. I have yet to see this. I have watched the LOW fall apart once and it all had to do with the shitty ass talent they have. Now the LOW is trying to rise from the ashes with even worse talent than they had before.~|~ ~|~It is rightfully named though, Lights Out Wrestling. The lights did go out and they are having a hell of a time coming back on. The talent there now is worthless, not even worth naming but I guess they have to be names so I know who’s souls will be mine after only my first night in LOW.~|~ ~|~To think that Matt Meyhu is even trying to wrestle. He thinks just because his big brother was so great, or so he though, that he can take his brothers place and become what his brother was. In seriousness Matt Meyhu is just as pathetic as Mike Meyhu was. And this Rocky Mounts, who does he think he is? Talked himself up pretty good during Realm 52, but that’s all he is capable of, talk. As for these other names, they are not even worth mentioning. Adam Stryker thinks that he can take me just because I come from DWO. DWO was the most dominate force in PTC at one time. But that is the past, this is now.~|~ Fairy Man: I can tell that our King has much more on his mind besides the match. He is trying to hide it even from himself by thinking of only this match now. Deep down inside there is a struggle. He is torn between two women by the sounds of it and that isn't like our King. Like dive deeper into his mind to find out exactly what is truly going through his mind. ~~The Fairy Man and the souls of hell dive deeper into Ron Diamond’s mind and more thoughts flood the mind.~~ ~|~Thoughts~|~ ~|~How could I let a man like myself fall in love in the first place? For crying out loud I’m married and in love to Dominique, or use to be in love with Dominique. That seems to be falling apart all because another woman has crept into my life. How could I let this Amanda sneak into my life and make me feel something for her? I know that I still love Dominique but every day we seem to be growing further apart. As for me and Amanda, we seems to be coming closer together with every passing day. If only I would have never fallen in love in the first place. The ruler of Hell isn’t suppose to feel love or compassion. That makes me weak! But what can I really do?~|~ Fairy Man: So it is true, another woman has caught the eye of our King. This could be very bad. Human emotion is becoming to much for him and seems to be changing him into something he isn’t. We better watch this part closely through the coming weeks. But it is about time for us to go and our King seems to have something to say. Let us listen. Ron Diamond: That’s right LOW, the Diamond family has now come to be dominate in yet another federation. Come Pulse I bring home a title in my very first ever match in the LOW. The Realm title will be mine but first I must put eight other people over the top rope. Not that this is any challenge for me because the competition sucks. Let me give a fare warning to all those in this match, if you even think for a minute I’m going to trust one of you to help eliminate everyone else just so it can come down to you and me, think again. When the moment is right I will eliminate each and every single person. If you turn your back on me even once, prepare to meet the floor. Ron Diamond: I have come to bring fear, chaos and destruction. Prepare for Hell will soon rise and the Army of Hell will take the earth by storm and it will belong to me along with everyones souls. I extend and offer to each and every one of you wrestlers here, sell your soul to me. Sell it to me and I promise you a place in Hell with me. A place above another other. You will not suffer in the fiery pits of Hell if you sell your soul to me and give your complete allegiance to me. You all have a choice to make LOW. The choice is follow me and live happily for all eternity or defy me and be doomed to the fiery pits of Hell. Next week on Realm I want to hear from at least one of the super stars here in the LOW. Ron Diamond: Fear… Chaos… Destruction, I bring all these things to you and more. Hell will rise soon, the end is near. ~~The Fairy Man and the souls of Hell make their way from Ron Diamond’s mind, chamber and castle back onto the boat that sits on the River Styx. The boat begins to float down the river once again.~~ Fairy Man: Our King is different and no different all at the same time. He is different when it comes to love. Love is becoming to much for him. He is no different when it comes to gathering souls for Hell’s Army. How will LOW respond though? That is the biggest question at this time. ~~The boat pulls up at the drop off point.~~ Fairy Man: Well here we are one and all. I hope that you all enjoyed your journey for today. Now you must return to your fiery pits until the next journey begins. Farewell.
