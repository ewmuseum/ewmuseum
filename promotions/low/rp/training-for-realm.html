Training for Realm

captured 8 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Prologue What was a wrestling championship? I know I haven’t been wrestling for some time now, but what does it mean to have a wrestling championship. Does it tell you that you are the king of the federation you are in. Or does it just mean that you are the best wrestler in that divisional title. The last championship I’ve ever won in a fed was back when I was in Global Wrestling. I had won the Cruiserweight championship. Did that mean I was the best Cruiserweight in Global Wrestling at the time? No, it didn’t. It meant that I fought for it and I earned it. For the World championship, does it mean you have to be 6 feet and over 250 pounds to win it? No it doesn’t. That would be nice but it wouldn’t have to be those specific rules or specifications. Now, I was put in a match that said whoever win’s gets a shot at the Realm championship. It would be nice if I won this match but it would be kind of hard seeing as there is more than 8 guys in this match. But as I said, you have to fight for it and earn it, so that’s what I’m going to do on Realm. The Story I got off the bed I was laying on and looked into the mirror. My shaggy black hair was all bunched up in my face leaving little marks of skin barely able to see. The black eyebrow ring in my eyebrow was sitting there enjoying none of this. I blinked a few times moving the eyebrow ring back and forth. There were bags under my eye’s probably meaning because I haven’t slept so long for the past few nights. I have been thinking of LOW and if I’m going to make an impact or just quit again. Don’t worry all you LOW fans. I won’t be quitting anytime soon unless it’s getting fired but I highly doubt that is going to happen seeing as I don’t do drugs or anything. I walked out of my room onto the spiral carpet that was covering the whole floor upstairs. I guess my mom like the look of it because she picked it out without even asking me but I guess it was better than having like cement on the floor. My shoes clapped against the carpet every time I took a step. I started walking down the staircase. When I got to the bottom I saw a white letter on the table by my door. Someone must have dropped it by when I was sleeping or something. I picked up the white envelope and cut the top of it. I took the white piece of paper out and started reading it. “Hello Max Roswell, we are pleased to announce that you are in a Octagon match on the next show of Realm. I’m not sure if anyone has told yet, but I just wanted to make sure you got the message so you can start your training or whatever you do to get ready for your match. Thanks for your time. We hope you do well in this Octagon match because if you’re the lucky winner you could get a shot at the Realm title against The Prophet.” FCC I put the letter down on the table and smiled. I knew I was in the match already but I had to start training. I grabbed my car keys off the key hook and grabbed a black gym bag from under the coffee table. I walked out the door and locked it. I walked down the walkway to the driveway and saw my red convertible parked 3 feet away from the garage door. I pressed a button on the car keys and the car’s door’s unlocked and the horn honked. I grabbed the handle of the door and threw my gym bag into the seat beside me. I hopped into the seat and slammed the door shut. I buckled my seat belt and took the gear out of park. I backed out of the driveway and started driving down the street. The gym was usually about 15 minutes away from my house. 15 Minutes Later I parked my car on the street in front of a big red bricked building. It had a big sign on it that said, “Larry’s Gym.” I grabbed my gym bag and got out of the car. I pushed through the brown doors into the big building. There was a huge hallway that had doors on each side. There was also a huge brown counter that had some lady’s behind it talking to other people on headphones. They were probably booking appointments for them or something. I knew where to go in this building because I have been here a million times. I walked down the hallway and walked into the second door from the right. There was a huge open space with a whole bunch of training activities in each corner of the space. In the middle was a wrestling ring. They would let people use it because lots of people were wrestling in this city. There was no one in the wrestling ring now at the time though. I walked down the side of the wall and pushed another brown door that led into the change room. I had to change into my gym stuff before I started my training. I took my shirt off and unzipped my gym bag. I grabbed a white tank top out of the gym bag and put it on. Then I took of my black wind pants and grabbed a pair of black Nike shorts. I also put on a pair of Nike sneakers. Last but not least I grabbed a black sweatband to go around my arm. I always put that on when I was training. It was just a tradition. I put my normal clothes inside the gym bag and left the bag on the brown bench in the change room. I walked out of the room into the gym. There were now 2 guys in the ring practicing some moves. I walked over to them and jumped onto the apron. They both stopped what they were doing and looked at me. They didn’t look to happy when they looked at me so I smiled. I don’t think they got what I was trying to tell them. They both snorted and then spit in the ring. Finally one of them opened there mouth to speak. “You want to wrestle?” he groaned. “Well I was kind of thinking that it could be both of you against me. I have a match in the federation I’m in and I need lots of practice because there are more than 8 other guys in the match. It will be hard to win so that’s why I need some practice.” I said. “Ok, how about for us since we have two, only one of us can be in the ring at a time, and if you pin one of us that person is out and the other guy comes in. Kind of like an elimination match?” he groaned again. “Okay, that make’s it even better.” I said. I climbed into the ring. One of the guys was about 6 feet and 235 pounds. He had no shirt on with red tights. He had red elbow and knee pads and black boots. The other guy had a red singlet on. It had a cool black and red design on it. He looked about 5’11 and 215 pounds, probably the same as me. The one in the singlet started out with me. He rushed at me and hit a clothesline. I went down but quickly got up. The guy took no time jumping at me and giving me a standing cross body. The guy fell on me and then picked me up. He kicked me in the gut and then hit a quick DDT. Man was I rusty. I only had one match in LOW that I won. I couldn’t brag too long. The guy tagged his partner in who came after me. He picked me up but I reversed and threw him against the ropes and then hit a flying calf kick right to his skull. He fell to the ground holding his head. I climbed to the top rope and jumped off with a Shooting Star Press. Everyone always knew I was a daredevil when it came to wrestling. Even I thought I was going a little extreme but that’s what people like. I quickly picked the guy up and threw him into his partner’s corner. I was about to run at him and hit a diving splash but he turned around and tagged his partner in. The guy in the singlet ran at me and tried for a big boot, but I ducked and he went right into the rope. He held his sack as I hit a kick to the back of the skull. The guy fell down and I hit a standing leg drop. I picked him up and kicked him in the gut. I put him into the pile driver position. As much as this move hurts, I needed to practice it before my match, so I took a bounce off the mat and did a full 360 with the pile driver at the end. The guy’s neck bounced off the mat. I went for the cover. 1… 2… 3…! The guy in the singlet was rolled out of the ring by the referee. The guy on the apron entered the ring scared knowing I might have done that move to him as well. We both locked up and he punched me in the stomach. He threw me against the ropes and then big booted me on the way back. He picked me up and then grabbed me for the military press slam. I quickly slipped out of it and landed on my feet on the mat. I know this is very wrong to do in a wrestling match but it was worth a shot. I made a fist and then gave the guy a HARD low blow. He fell to the ground. Somehow the referee didn’t see it and asked me if I did anything. I had to lie and I said no. I picked the guy up and went for my move, but he reversed it into a huge Chokeslam! My back bounced off the mat hard. The guy smiled and then went for the cover. 1… 2… 3..NO! I had kicked out at the last second. It was hard because the big guy on me was hard to get out of but I did it. The guy started arguing with the ref letting me get to my feet and regain my senses. I shook my head making my black hair go all over the place. The guy turned around and I kicked him in the gut. All of you knew what was coming. The System Crash. I put the guy in the pile driver position and then took a big bounce doing a full 360 hitting the pile driver at the end. Holy crap was that guy heavy. I rolled the guy over and went for the cover. 1… 2… 3…! I couldn’t believe I won. At the beginning of the match I thought I was going to be squashed by the size of this big guy. I picked the guy up and held out my hand for good sportsmanship. He shook it and then the other guy in the singlet slid into the ring and raised my arm. Wow, I never had seen a wrestler so generous. All of us slid out of the ring. I had to sit down because I was tired after that. I sat down on a bench and wiped my face with a white towel that was on the bench. I think I was ready for my match on Realm. At least I think I am. I got off the bench and walked into the change room as the scene faded.
