Untitled roleplay

captured 23 May 2005

Taking What's Mine ::As Jamester leaves the ring and starts up the ramp, “Anger Management” by Methods of Mayhem begins blaring over the arenas pa system. Just as Jamester reaches the top of the ramp, he is met face to face by the world title contender for tonight, “Superstar” Eric Russo. Russo is wearing a sleeveless Shadow Riders’ t-shirt tucked into a pair of black jeans. He pauses for a moment at the top of the ramp in a stare off with Jamester. The tension between the two mortal enemies is short lived however as Russo continues his way down the ramp, pushing past Jamester. As Russo continues towards the ring, he begins to speak.:: Russo: Jason . . . Jason . . . Jason, you did a lot better here tonight then I would have originally thought possible. ::Russo reaches the ringside area. He is quickly backed up a little bit however as Kage charges at the ring ropes and fakes at diving over onto Russo. Knowing that Kage is not about to give him the time of day, Russo raises the microphone to his mouth and begins speaking once again.:: Russo: Woah . . . hold it there! I just wanna talk. I’m not here to cause any trouble, I just wanna talk to you for a minute . . . man to man. ::Against his better judgement, Kage backs away from the ring ropes as Russo climbs inside. Once inside the ring, Russo is quickly back to his feet with the microphone to his mouth.:: Russo: You may not be all that intelligent Jason, but let me just say. Letting me in the ring now . . . good choice. I think you’re gonna wanna hear what I have to say. ::Russo begins pacing back and forth in the ring as Kage watches on, curious to hear what his former tag team partner has to say this time. Russo begins speaking.:: Russo: Now Jason over the past few weeks, I have watched as you drag a name that you have absolutely no right to, through the mud. You have polluted the talent pool that has been associated with the group for all too long. ::Russo stops dead in his footsteps as he turns and looks directly at Kage with an evil glare in his eye.:: Russo: But that all stops here. You see Jason, I watched and I watched until finally I broke. Now we all know what that means don’t we Jason? We all know the motto that the Shadow Riders live by. We all know that you don’t get even, you get a lawyer. ::Russo resumes his pacing as he continues.:: Russo: So that’s what I did Jason. I went out and I hired the best lawyer in the state of Nevada that money could buy. But boy did I get a return on my investment. ::Russo stops casting a shit eating grin in Kage’s direction as he continues.:: Russo: You see Jason, what that lawyer was able to secure me was better then I could have expected in my wildest dreams. What that lawyer managed to get me is the exclusive right to both the Shadow Riders name and logo. ::Kage’s expression drops as he hears the unfortunate news. Russ continues.:: Russo: Now you’re probably asking yourself right now what that means for you. Well let me tell you. What that means for you is that from this point out, the free ride is over. There will be no more building your name off of one that I have single handedly made famous. What that means is that you just like the rest have become nothing but another falling star. ::Russo does not waste any time exiting the ring and leaving the dumbfounded Kage behind. Russo makes his way to the top of the ramp. Before continuing to the back however he stops. Russo slowly turns back to look as Kage in the ring. He brings the microphone to his mouth and begins to speak.:: Russo: Oh and Kage . . . that’s not ego, it’s reality. ::Russo drops the microphone and continues into the backstage area to the sound of “Anger Management” by Methods of Mayhem as the show goes to a commercial break.:: Show URL: http://
