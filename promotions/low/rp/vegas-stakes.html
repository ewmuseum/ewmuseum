Vegas Stakes

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over message which states... “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to a shot of the Las Vegas strip in the mid day sun. The shot pans from left to right as if searching for something. All of a sudden, a black 65' Chevelle comes speeding down the street. The car screeches to a halt in front of the camera as the passenger door swings open. A voice comes booming from inside the car.) Voice: “You getting in or not!? I don’t have all day to be waiting around!” (The cameraman quickly gets in the automobile closing the door behind him as we see the driver or the car for the first time. Sitting in the drivers seat in none other then the superstar of the LOW himself, Eric Russo. Russo is wearing a sleeveless Shadow Riders t-shirt tucked into a pair of faded blue jeans. A pair of black, Ray-ban Aviators sit on his face blocking the glare from the bright sun outside. Russo sits looking out the window at a homeless man sitting across the street as he begins talking.) Russo: “It’s funny, the way things change in the blink of an eye. Just the other day that bum across the street was sitting at the high rollers table. He was laughing and having a good ol’ time. Now look at him, sitting there on the sidewalk, begging everyone that passes by for change and food. You see, he failed to realize exactly how high the stakes were. He got in over his head and ended up paying the price. That reminds me a lot of Cade. You see, Cade does not realize what he is getting himself into. He does not realize that I, the Superstar Eric Russo, do not play for simple wins and losses. I deal in much higher stakes... I deal in careers. My good friend Kage as well as Cade himself say that he is not special, I however see it differently. Cade is special, he is special because he is the one that I am going to use as an example. Through Cade we will show everyone exactly why I talk such a big game. Rob Sinn and Obsidian liked to talk about how neither of the Shadow Riders were on their level. Well they we’re right, we we’re above them. I ask you, where are they now? Buried in a six foot deep hole in south Texas. You see when I play, I play for keeps. As for Cades useless babbling about how this Saturday will be likened to that of World War 2. Well, I tell him to bring it, bring the pain and anguished suffered by each and every person that died during those years of carnage. Because he is going to need all the help that he can get. It’s just to bad for him that no matter what he brings, the outcome will remain the same. It will still end with Cade lying face down in a puddle of his own blood, unable to even utter a word as his soul returns to Lotus’ own nethervoid to join the list of ended careers. As it sits in the darkness and solitude with nothing but memories of the life it once knew, one thought will repeat for eternity. A thought quite familiar, the thought that Cade just like the rest has become nothing but another falling star.” (Russo turns his attention to the road in front of him as he presses his foot down on the gas pedal. As the smell of gasoline fills the air, the car speeds off down the street. Russo does not speak as he drives the car. After a few blocks, Russo makes a sharp right turn onto a small side street. The car speeds down the street as it gets further and further from the glamor of the strip and deeper into the rundown section of sin city. Russo continues driving for a few more minutes before pulling into the parking lot of a small dilapidated bar. Russo parks the car and opens the door as he speaks to the cameraman.) Russo: “Come on, I’m supposed to meet Kage here in a little bit. Let’s get a beer while we wait.” (Russo and the camera man exit the car and walk into the small bar. As the walk to the back of the bar, a few of the patrons reach out their hands to shake Russo’s. As if he does not even notice them, Russo passes by them avoiding their touch. The two men take a seat at a small table towards the rear of the bar. A waitress who looks to be in her late forties comes over to the table. She is wearing a low cut, white t-shirt which shows off her sagging cleavage as well as a pair of super short cut-off jeans. She begins speaking.) Waitress: “Hello honey, what can I get you.” Russo: “I’ll have a tequila on the rocks and he’ll have a...” Cameraman: “Nothing thank you, I don’t drink.” Russo: “He’ll take a straight up shot of 151.” Waitress: “I’ll be right back with you order.” (As the waitress leaves the table, the cameraman begins to question Russo.) Cameraman: “What’s 151? I never heard of that before.” Russo: “Don’t worry about it, it’s good for you.” (The waitress returns to the table with the two men’s order. She sets both glasses down on the table then walks away. Russo takes a large sip of his drink as he motions for the cameraman to drink his. The cameraman raises the glass to his mouth and quickly takes the shot. The camera begins to shake as we hear the cameraman begin to cough violently. Russo lets out a little chuckle as he watches the cameraman. He allows the cameraman to compose himself before he looks into the camera and begins speaking once again.) Russo: “Did you see that waitress? I hate people like that. You know, the ones who don’t know when it’s time to move on. The kind who try and recapture their glory days by acting like something their not. It reminds me a lot of Cade. He comes back to the Lights Out talking about how he is a former LOW champion. Is that supposed to mean something to me, because it doesn’t. The letters LOW and word champion do not mean a damn thing to me. As far as I’m concerned they are nothing but another three letters in the alphabet that we’re strung together. As for being the world champion... so what? How in the hell does that effect your chances against me? I could sit here all day and name different titles that I’ve held over my career but I’m not gonna. Do you know why I’m not gonna Cade? I’m not gonna name them because they won’t change a damn thing here and now. But I’ll humor you for just a second here Cade. I’ll go along with your story about how you were the champion of Lights Out and how this is supposed to mean something. Now you have decided to come back and make an appearance here in Lights Out Wrestling once again. So how do you debut? You show up and do nothing but fail miserably. You sit stagnant and weather away into oblivion. I can’t blame you though, if I was as overrated as you, I’d stay as far away from the public as I could too. There is one thing however that you did not take into account. Just like Kage said, we don’t play well with others especially cheap knock-offs of real talent. So Cade even though I don’t like you very much, I am still gonna give you a chance. Pack up your bags, book your flight, and get as far away from the LOW as you can because if you don’t you will leave me no choice but to run you out just like everyone else who crosses my path.” (Russo lifts his drink to his mouth and finishes it with one gulp. As he waves the waitress over to order another drink, a drunk man stumbles over to the table. The man looks at Russo in a drunken stare as he begins to talk with a sight slur.) Drunk: “Yer Erick Russso... I’m yer biggesst fan...” Russo: “That’s good, now get out of my face. That stench coming off you is making me sick.” (Looking dejected, the man stumbles off as the waitress arrives at the table once again. Russo orders another drink. As the waitress goes to get the drink, Russo begins talking to the camera once again.) Russo: “I hate the drunks in these run down bars, they are always trying to tell you that their your biggest fan when you know they probably don’t know a damn thing about you except that your famous...” (The waitress returns to the table and places Russo’s drink on the table in front of him. Russo lifts up the glass and takes a sip of the tequila. He then returns the glass to it’s position on the table before continuing.) Russo: “As I was saying, they probably don’t know anything other then the fact that I wrestle for the LOW. It kind of reminds me of the almighty ape god himself, Cade. You see, Cade just like that drunk likes to pretend that he actually understands what he is saying when he is talking to you. He likes to act as if he has an intellect far beyond his reach. Cade is nothing more then a two faced bastard, he talks to everyone as if they are lower then him... as if they are not on his mind. In reality though Cade only has one favorite, Cade only has one person that he looks out for... himself. I will give the devil his due, Cade is a master player when it comes to mind games. He is an expert at lying straight through his teeth. I however am not one to take comfort in false statements. That is one of the main reasons that I have decided to align myself with the so called rebels without a cause. Although they are not a very caring and forgiving bunch, they do not put up false fronts. If they don’t like you, you can be assured that they will tell you that right to your face. You all saw that first hand when Kevin Miller sent Malice after Trigger. Kevin Miller did not pretend to be Trigger’s friend... no, instead he made his intentions known right from the beginning. Just as Kage has said in the past, we do not hide our agendas. We are here for one reason and one reason only... we are here for money. I have said it many times in the past, my services are available to whoever has enough money to pay us. I am a very talented individual and I make sure I get paid very well to be just that. As for Cade, I wish him a speedy recovery, because as soon as I’m through with him . . . he’s gonna need all the well wishes that he can get.” (A loud clamor is heard off camera. The camera swings around as we see the bartender dragging one of the many drunks to the front door. The bartender kicks the front door open and throws the drunk man out into the parking lot. A large grin slides across the bartenders face as he admires his handy work. He then yells out to the drunk who is trying to drag himself to his feet.) Bartender: “Come back when you have some money you fu(k!ng bum!” (The bartender pulls the door shut as he begins to walk back behind the bar. He notices that one of the customers is staring at him. The bartender lunges foreword and motions as if he is going for a punch to scare the staring customer. The customer falls of his stool as he jumps back in fear of being hit. The bartender lets out a hearty laugh as walks behind the bar. The camera comes back to a shot of Russo as he begins speaking once again.) Russo: “There is nothing more pathetic then a bully. Unlike a real man, a bully is rarely ever able to back up what he says. This too kind of reminds me of Cade. Cade is kind of like that fat kid at the back of the class that nobody likes. The one that either out of anger of rejection or necessity becomes the town bully. Strangely enough, this is nothing more then another attempt for them to fit in. A way for them to become a part of the crowd. Cade has re-entered the LOW with little fanfare. He has had his chances to do his best to try and promote himself but those attempts have failed miserably. You see Cade... or should I call you ape god? Oh well, it doesn’t really matter what I call you so lets just leave it at Jacka$$. You say that I think I am some kind of god. Nothing could be further from the truth, I don’t think of myself as a god... I know I’m one. As for the reason I use the moniker of superstar, it is not to make myself look better then the rest. It is because I earn the right to use that name with everything that I do. The fact that you are a “rebel with a cause” does not bother me in the least. I have taken down men a whole lot bigger and a hell of a lot more talented then you without blinking an eye. So you can talk all you want about how your fighting for a cause and how you’re such a dangerous man. Because that is not going to make a difference when you get in the ring with me and I take you down to the mat and show you exactly why they call me Superstar.” (As Russo finishes the cameraman begins to question the whereabouts of Jason Kage.) Cameraman: “I thought you were supposed to meet up with Kage here.” Russo: “Why are you so antsy, you got something more important to do? Kage will get here when he gets here. Until then, you just keep that camera on me cause I still have a few things that I wanna talk about.” Cameraman: “You got it.” (Russo finishes his drink before continuing.) Russo: “Finally I come to the PTC tag tournament. Now some of you know what I’m talking about and some of you are probably asking yourselves “What the hell is the PTC tag tournament?” Well for the uneducated masses, allow me to expound on exactly what the tournament is. The PTC tag is a tournament in which 38 teams battle it out to determine exactly who is the best at what they do. Now unfortunately for them, most of Lights Out for some reason was already eliminated. Kage and myself however are still going strong as representatives of Lights Out Wrestling. This information however is incorrect. You see I want to take this opportunity to make sure that Trigger and every other member of the LOW roster knows that we are not there to represent them. We are in this tournament to represent the team of Shadow Riders Inc.. When we win this tournament we want all of you to know that it is not a win for this company... it is a win for me and Jason Kage. I do however want to thank this company for one thing. I would like to thank it for the entries of our beloved former world champion Rich Sherwin as well as the entry of his partner Eric Morgan. This tournament is more then a chance to show everyone exactly why they call me the Superstar. This tournament gives me a chance to show everyone that we are the most dominate tag team ever to step foot in a wrestling ring. It also gives me the perfect opportunity to remind each and every one of those 38 other teams that they are all nothing but another falling star that will be left in the dust of the Shadow Riders.” (Russo stands up as Kage walks in the front door of the small bar. Russo and the cameraman walk over to meet Kage. Once they are there, Kage begins speaking.) Kage: “Good to see you man. Did you find anything out about our mysterious benefactor?” Russo: “Of course, did you actually think I would come back without any answers?” Kage: “Who was it man? I can’t wait to hear.” Russo: “Get this, it was... hold on.” (Russo turns to the cameraman.) Russo: “You got any money on you?” Cameraman: “No, why?” Russo: “It’s gonna be hard getting out of here with no money to pay the tab or get a cab. Oh well... sucks for you.” Cameraman: “You can’t just leave me here like this!” Russo: “You know what, you’re right. Give me the tape.” Cameraman: “What.. why?” Russo: “I don’t wanna loose this tape if you don’t make it back. Give it to me now or else I’m gonna take it.” (The picture begins to shake as the cameraman reaches up in fear to press the eject button. Suddenly, the screen goes to the Shadow Riders logo with the voice over that says... “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The screen now fades to black.)
