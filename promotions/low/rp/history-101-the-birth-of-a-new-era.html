History 101- The Birth of a New Era

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. The scene opens still in Russo’s previous flashback. The morning sun is just breaking over the horizon, it awakens Russo who is still sitting slumped against the wall in the alley. The pimp and his prostitute are no longer anywhere to be seen. Russo shakes his head from side to side trying to regain some form of composure. Once he has his wits about him, he pushes himself off the ground and starts towards his poor excuse of a home where he can get some solace from the previous night’s occurrences. Within the blink of an eye, Russo’s mind travels directly to that night. We come to a shot of him fast asleep in his bed. Suddenly, he begins tossing and turning in his sleep. Tossing ad turning as though he is having a nightmare. Quickly he pops out of his slumber and reaches over to the night stand beside his bed. There he finds what he is looking for, a small handgun. Russo cocks the gun and points it into the darkness of the room surrounding him as he calls out.) Russo: “Who’s there!? What are you doing in my room house!?” (A voice comes from the darkness of the room.) Voice: “You said you were ready.” (The snake steps out of the shadows in the room allowing Russo to get his first good look at the man. He is dressed in a pair of black jeans and a leather jacket. In the streetlight shining in through the window, we can see that Snake has suffered through his own fair share of torment. Even now, his hair is beginning to thin as his body becomes fuller. There’s a certain mystique that surrounds him though. One that leaves you questioning if he is human or the devil himself. In a calm even tone, Snake begins speaking.) Snake: “I’ve been watching you Eric. I know how it feels to be in your position. I know what it’s like when your life loses all meaning. Nothing is quite the same is it? The things that you once loved have become nothing more then forgotten memories of a time long ago. It seems as though there is no reason to go on, am I right?” (Russo sits in awe as the Snake paces through the room, continuing.) Snake: “What if I told you that I could give all of that back to you? If I told you that I could take away all that has plagued you in the past? If I told you that I could give you a new lease on your life, one that would last forever? I can do that for you Eric, but you are going to have to make a choice. You are going to have to choose wether or not you are willing to commit yourself to what will be required of you. That’s why I’m giving you this choice .” (Snake sits on the bed next to Russo and helps him sit up as he goes on.) Snake: “A choice that will affect your entire future.” (The scene quickly changes to one of Russo walking down the Las Vegas strip in the early hours of the morning as he contemplates which path he should take. He continues to walk along the street as the sun begins to rise and the neon lights on the casino’s begin to fade signaling the start of a new day. As the scene continues with Russo walking down the street, another voice over begins.) Russo (VO): “There was a lot running through my head that morning, and I remember all of those questions that ran through my mind that day even now as though it had just happened. That was one of the hardest decisions that I ever had to make in my entire career. It would take a lot for me to actually put my trust in someone. Could he really give me all that he promised or was he a liar just like everyone else I had met through my travels thus far? I don’t know why, but I decided to give it a chance. So with a deep gulp, I took the first step forward in my new direction.” (The picture seamlessly shifts from the daytime scene of the Las Vegas strip to the darkness of night outside of hotel room door. Russo comes into view as he approaches the door. He stretches out his hand and lightly knocks his knuckles against it. Within a few seconds, the door creaks open to reveal the Snake. He quickly welcomes Russo into his hotel room. The two men take a seat across from each other at a small circular table just inside the door of the room. Once they are there and settled, the Snake begins to speak.) Snake: “So have you made your decision?” (Russo looks at the Snake, still second guessing his decision.) Snake: “You will not survive much longer the way you are going. If I leave you hear and now, you will surely never live up to what you could be, or you can come with me now and live a life that you never dreamed imaginable. I must know though, are you willing to commit to what lies ahead if you come with me, because there is no turning back after this point. Are you ready, yes or no?” (Russo responds to the question with almost a whisper.) Russo: “Yes . . . I am.” (The Snake reaches his hand across the table in as a gesture to seal the deal. Still showing slight signs of reluctance, Russo reaches out and takes hold of the Snake’s hand, sealing his fate. A transition in the setting take place once again. This time to the backstage area of an arena a few months down the road. Russo is dressed in his new wrestling gear with the Snake now at his side. As the two men weave their way through the maze like hallways, the Snake looks to Russo as he begins speaking.) Snake: “Tonight is your night Eric. Tonight you begin your journey to the top of this business under my guidance.” (Russo’s face is etched with determination the likes of which he has never before known. The Snake continues.) Snake: “Just remember what I taught you about this business and there’s nobody who’s gonna be able to stop you.” (The scene switches back to the present day where Russo is sitting across the table from Jerry. Inquisitively, Jerry looks on at Russo as he begins to question him.) Jerry: “What did he teach you?” Russo: “That’s a secret that I will take to my grave with me. Besides, asking me that is like asking a blind man what he sees. You can never know unless you experience it yourself.” (The scene switches back to the past where Russo and the Snake are still walking along through the corridors. Another voice over begins.) Russo (VO): “I noticed things that I had never paid any attention to before. I now knew how to look for weak points in my opponents.” (As Russo and the Snake walk down the hallway, Russo notices his opponent for the night. He makes a quick mental note as he watches the man apply an ice pack to his shoulder. The voice over continues.) Russo (VO): “Everything that I had once known had changed yet stayed the same.” (The Snake leads Russo to the locker room. As he continues to walk, Russo seems as though all that is taking place around him is somehow new. His voice continues as the scene fades out.) Russo (VO): “It was almost as if I had been reborn and I was eager to test my new found life out on my opponent.” (The scene quickly re-opens however. This time inside of the dark and dreary locker room at the same arena. Inside the room, two benches sit facing each other in the center of the room. On one bench sits Russo who is eerily silent as he hangs his head awaiting his match. Across from him the Snake sits, constantly reassuring him that everything is going to go just as planned.) Snake: “Don’t worry Eric, soon you’ll be exactly where you should. You’ll be the one standing above all others as the best in the world at what you do. When you go out there and wrestle tonight, you’re gonna have me in your corner. I’ll be watching you and after tonight, so will the rest of the world.” (Sill staying in silence, Russo nods his head in acknowledgment of the words from his mentor. At this point, a voice over is heard once again.) Russo (VO): “I was about to find out just how much the Snake had taught me in our time together so far. That night as I walked to the ring, I did so with a raging fire inside of me. A feeling that I had never felt before. A feeling that I would never wrestle without again. A feeling that I was better then anyone else who faced me and a felling that I was about to make a reality.” (With that, the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
