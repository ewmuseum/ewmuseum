Love Part 2 Archive - 1.5.2003

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: Ok now I haven’t written in this style for a long time. Towards the end of this role-play there is a section between two *’s. Now sometimes people can get confused by what is happening here. I’d just like to say that, that is what Cyrus is thinking but it is not happening. He black outs whilst it happens. I hope that clears up any confusion. Cyrus was buttoning up his white shirt when the doorbell rang. He cursed loudly as he was not ready. He quickly ran down the stairs and tripped on the last step, falling flat on his face. He cursed again even louder this time. He got to his feet and unlocked the door to see Hannah stood at the door, wearing a black dress, showing off her cleavage magnificently. Cyrus’s eyes nearly fell out of their sockets when he saw her, but he managed to pull himself together to ask her to come in and sit down. He told her he’d just be a minute and rushed back upstairs. He opened the sliding door of his wardrobe and searched desperately for his black jacket. He couldn’t find it. He swore loudly again, Hannah must have heard him because she shouted up after him. “Everything ok?” She sounded concerned. “Yeah, I’m just trying to find my jacket.” Cyrus sounded anxious. He’d booked a table at an Italian restaurant for half past seven and if they weren’t there on time they’d give the table to somebody else. Cyrus heard her laugh from downstairs. “You mean this black jacket that’s on the sofa?” She shouted up to him. He realized he’d put it there before, he ran down the stairs, careful not to fall this time, and grabbed his jacket off the chair. He put it on and opened the front door. He let Hannah go out first and then locked the door behind her. “It’s ok if we go in my car isn’t it?” Asked Cyrus, uncertain. “Why? I was going to drive so you could have something to drink.” Hannah sounded rather put out. “I don’t drink any more. I’ll drive, you enjoy yourself.” Cyrus smiled at her and it won her over. She smiled back as he opened her door and she got into the silver BMW Z3. Cyrus then walked back round to his side and got in. He looked at his watch. “Shit!” He shouted at the top of his voice, causing Hannah to jump out of her seat. “What’s the matter?” Asked Hannah sounding worried but intrigued. “If we don’t get to the restaurant within five minutes then we probably won’t have a table to sit at!” Cyrus sounded very worried, Hannah looked devastated that she might not be getting taken out after all. Cyrus however, wasn’t thinking about the possibilities. He just wanted to go as fast as he could. He put his foot down and sped down the road, he turned right onto the main round and got stuck right in a traffic jam. He cursed loudly and reversed back down his road. He then sped off in the opposite direction and back past his house. He turned right at this end of the road into another not quite as big road as the main road, but still rather big. “This ways a longer way round, but I’ll be able to go faster,” replied Cyrus with a huge grin covering his face. “Make sure you’re strapped in tightly.” Cyrus laughed then really put his foot down. He raced as fast as he could, hitting 70mph in seconds. He kept the speed up and whenever it looked like he was going to run up the boot of another car he just simply over took them. Cyrus pulled up the restaurant car park and let a shaken, but all right Hannah out of the car. “Don’t you ever drive that fast again! I thought I was going to die!” Hannah said sounding quite upset, then she looked at him and smiled. “I wouldn’t want to die now that I’m only just starting to feel alive.” With that she placed a kiss upon his lips and then led the love struck giant into the restaurant. They entered the prestigious establishment together holding hands. Cyrus looked around, the restaurant was decorated with light coloured, orange wallpaper and pictures of famous people were hung all over the four walls. Cyrus aproached the stand and a waiter came to him. “Name sir?” Said the waiter in a rather snobbish voice. “Skar, Cyrus Skar.” Replied Cyrus still pumped with adrenaline after the way he’d driven to the restaurant. “Yes I see, table for two?” The waiter asked still speaking a snobbish tone of his voice. “Yes that’s right.” Replied Hannah before Cyrus could reply. She was letting her presence be known to the waiter. The waiter then beckoned them to follow him, he led them over to a littler circular mahogany table in the corner of the room. Fine silverware was laid out in front of them, with a candle in the middle of the table. They both sat down as the waiter went off to get them so menus. “Wow.” Said Hannah in shock of how up-market the place was. “Are you sure you can afford this? I mean it looks very expensive. If you can’t afford it I don’t mind paying half…” Cyrus cut her off mid sentence. “Of course I can afford it, I wouldn’t have brought you here if I couldn’t have afforded it now would I?” Cyrus smiled. “Just sit back, relax and enjoy the evening.” A few minutes later the waiter came back with two menus. He passed one to Hannah and one to Cyrus. Cyrus had a look at what was an offer and chose very quickly, Hannah on the other hand, was a little bit longer and the waiter got very impatient. Cyrus ignored it though and ordered for them both. Whilst they waited for their starters they quietly chatted. “So how long have you been wrestling now then?” Asked Hannah interested in Cyrus’s career. “I’ve been fighting ever since I was 16, I’ve been a professional wrestler for many years now. I can’t remember exactly how many.” Replied Cyrus smiling “So I take it you enjoy what you do then?” Asked Hannah. “I mean you wouldn’t keep on doing it if you didn’t enjoy it would you?” “I certainly wouldn’t. I love wrestling, it’s my life. It’s what I believe I was put on this Earth for. My purpose in life is to wrestle.” Cyrus took a sip of water from his glass. “Are you sure you don’t want anything alcoholic?” Hannah was trying to find out why Cyrus didn’t want anything alcoholic now. “I’m sure I’ve had some bad experiences with alcohol in the past. I don’t want to ruin our evening by having to relive any of them again.” Cyrus spoke strictly letting Hannah know he didn’t want that topic raising again. Then the waiter came and brought them their starters. They sat quietly and ate their food and did not talk whilst doing so. When they had finished the waiter came and took the plates away. “That was really nice. I’m hoping the main course is just as good!” Said Hannah with excitement. “I’ve eaten here before, the food is excellent, though I can’t say much for the service. Some of the waiters are right snobs.” Cyrus laughed but Hannah didn’t she wanted to find out more about Cyrus’s past. “So did you used to come here with an ex girlfriend or something?” Hannah was being very nosey now and Cyrus didn’t appreciate it, but he realized he’d have to tell her sometime so it might as well be now. “Ex fiancée.” Said Cyrus; those two words told a story in themselves. “Oh I’m sorry, what happened?” Asked Hannah before adding. “If you don’t mind me asking.” “No I don’t mind, I’m sure you would have found out sooner or later anyway. She got murdered whilst I was out with her. It was a set up by a gang, a professional mugging if you will. There’s a decoy to lure the victim’s partner away, and then they strike. I didn’t realize and well, she died later on that night in hospital.” Cyrus brushed away a tear from his eye and tried to be strong. “I’m sorry.” Replied Hannah in a caring tone of voice. “I bet it was hard for you.” “I blamed myself for it the whole time and I vowed to myself that I’d never let anything like that ever happen again.” Cyrus stopped grieving and changed the topic. “Here comes our food.” The waiter brought the plates up and put them down on the table. They began to eat in silence. Cyrus had practically killed the conversation for now. After they had finished eating, Hannah spoke again. “Cyrus, I don’t think I can eat any more right now.” She said feeling satisfied and bloated. “It’s ok, how about we go home to my place and than have some coffee?” Cyrus winked as he said this in a provocative voice. Hannah nodded as Cyrus took the bill to the stand and paid it. They walked out of the restaurant and got into Cyrus’s silver BMW Z3. He drove off, a lot slower than when he had arrived. Cyrus pulled up outside his house and slowed to a stop. He got out of the car and dashed round to the passenger side to open Hannah’s door for her. She thanked him and walked up to the front door. Cyrus locked the car then rushed past her and let her in. She took her shoes off and sat down on the sofa. Cyrus took his jacket off and hung it up on a hook in the hallway. He then took his shoes off and went in to the living room where Hannah was sat. “Would you like a coffee?” Asked Cyrus, trying to delay the inevitable for a little while. “I’d love one thanks.” Replied Hannah. “Black please.” Cyrus rushed off to the kitchen and started to brew up, he smiled as he thought about what was happening to him. He’d fallen in love again and it was working out. But yet there was a part of him that sensed something bad was bound to happen. A part of him knew that something was going to happen and crush his heart, cutting through it like a knife, releasing all ties from his body. He was happy, but worried. He walked back in with the two coffees and placed them down on the coffee table in front of him. He flicked the television on whilst they sat and drank their coffees. They chatted for a few minutes about mindless thinks that didn’t matter. Then it started. *I looked into her eyes and she looked back into mine. I could feel that she felt the same way as me. I slowly moved my head towards hers and placed my lips upon her own. I passionately kissed her again and again. Then I saw her, my fiancée as clear as anything. Telling me what I was doing was wrong. Telling me that I never loved her and that I was betraying her. No…I wasn’t…it was ok…but…was it?…I slowly backed away from the kiss causing Hannah to ask what was wrong. I lost my temper. I put my hands around her little throat and choked, she tried to breathe but my hands were cutting off her oxygen. She tried to kick and struggle but I was too strong for her. I let go and she fell of the sofa dead.* “Cyrus wake up!” Came Hannah’s plea as Cyrus slowly opened his eyes. As his vision started to focus he realized what had happened. “You’d better leave now.” He muttered. “Please, I’ll ring you later.” Hannah could see that Cyrus meant what he said. She slipped her shoes on and let herself out of the door, just about muttering ‘Goodbye’ as she went. Cyrus propped himself up on the sofa and started to think to himself. Then his realized his questions would be answered soon. “You had a blackout again didn’t you?” The voice was back, this time sounding a little bit happier. Cyrus’s date had not ended the way Cyrus had planned. “Yes, right when I least wanted one.” Cyrus was still perplexed. “Why then? I’ve not had one for so long and then to have one right at the time when it was least needed…it just doesn’t make sense.” “Oh it does Cyrus. It must be something to do with your emotions. You see, you’re very emotionally active at the moment. You’ve not felt like this for a long time. It may have been a shock to the system, the system may have reacted by producing this blackout.” The voice was bull shitting and Cyrus knew it. “No…I know exactly why…yes…it all makes sense now.” Cyrus broke off from what he was saying and smiled. “It was you wasn’t it? You managed to save up enough strength to cause that right when you wanted. You bastard, no wonder you’ve been quite for so long manifesting waiting for the right moment.” “Cyrus I had to, you’re getting too involved before you big match. I couldn’t just stand by and watch this woman manipulate you.” The voice was worried, Cyrus had realized what it had done. “Go now, if you cause any more trouble, I can see me and you falling out.” Cyrus got no reply from the voice as it returned to the depths of his soul. He sat on the sofa and placed his head in his hands. It’d be a hard day, he needed some rest. He went upstairs to go to bed.
