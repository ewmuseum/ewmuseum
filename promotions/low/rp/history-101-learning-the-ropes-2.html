History 101- Learning the Ropes 2

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. The picture once again opens to a scene of the past. Inside of what appears to be a large dance hall. A small band plays on stage at the front of the hall as many of the well dressed patrons dance a waltz on the dance floor. To the side of the room young women sit along the wall watching the people on the floor. On the other side, young men stand against the wall awaiting their chance to dance. Suddenly, a voice over by Russo begins.) Russo (VO): “Aside from what the Snake was teaching me inside of the ring, he was also teaching me how to carry myself outside of it. He was a master with words and could normally get whatever he wanted through a simple conversation. This ability fascinated me to know end. To think of getting whatever you wanted without even having to step inside the ring. These are the lessons that I enjoyed the most, these are the ones that I made sure to pay extra attention to. A move that I would become extremely thankful of later on in my career.” (Finally Russo and the Snake come into view. Both men are finely dressed in custom tailored suits. The Snake is looking over at a middle aged couple sitting over in one of the corners of the hall. The man is short and fat in his tuxedo while his wife takes on an opposite look of tall and thin in a short black dress. The Snake calls Russo’s attention to the couple. As the two men look on at the duo, the Snake begins to speak.) Snake: “You see that man over there?” (Russo nods his head as the Snake continues.) Snake: “That’s David Alfonso, he controls most of the southern territories. The territories that we want to break into. The secret to doing that however is not through him. No, the way to guarantee your spot in his territory is through that angel of a woman that he calls his wife. If you can manage to impress her, you’re as good as in. He does whatever she tells him to, she’s our target tonight.” (Questioningly Russo looks to the Snake.) Russo: “How do you know all of this?” (With a sly smirk, the Snake responds.) Snake: “I’ve been around a long time Eric. I know a lot of things.” Russo: “Are you sure about this?” (The Snake lets out a sigh as he leads Russo closer to the couple. He begins to speak setting Russo’s doubts at ease.) Snake: “Just trust me Eric. Why can’t you do that? Why do you always have to question every one of my lessons that I try and pass onto you? You’re gonna have to learn to just take my word for it. She is the way to get in with him. Once you have accomplished that, it is a free ride straight to the top. Besides, is it really too much to ask for you to become friendly with a beautiful woman?” Russo: “No, it’s not. I’m sorry for questioning you.” (Russo and the Snake continue moving in closer to the couple. Once there, Alfonso greets the Snake with a smile which is quickly answered with one in return. The beautiful wife holds out her hand as she introduces herself.) Woman: “Pleased to meet you, my name is Shannon Alfonso.” (The Snake suavely grabs hold of her hand and raises it to his mouth as he kisses the back of it.) Snake: “The pleasure is all mine.” (Shannon then extends her hand towards Russo who follows the lead of his mentor and kisses it as well. He then proceeds to extend his hand towards Alfonso as he introduces himself.) Russo: “It’s good to meet you Mr. Alfonso. My name is Eric Russo.” (Alfonso extends his hand, gripping Russo’s tightly and shaking it as the scene fades out and another voice over begins.) Russo (VO): “The Alfonso’s took a quick liking to the Snake and myself. It didn’t take us long to have the couple eating out of the palms of our hands. It was at this point that the Snake set yet another task before me. A simple task of keeping the lovely Mrs. Alfonso occupied while he ironed out the details of a contract for me with Mr. Alfonso. I was soon to find out that this task was not as simple as I had originally thought.” (The scene re-opens with a shot later on in the night. We are now outside in a vivacious garden. The Snake comes into view. He is walking along in the garden in mid-conversation with Alfonso. The Snake looks back at Russo, who walks along the opposite side of the garden with Alfonso’s wife. The music from within the dance hall fills the night air. As the Snake continues his walk, Alfonso looks to him as he begins speaking.) Alfonso: “So, you really think this kid is going to become the future of the industry? I’ve heard that a million other times from a million other managers about their clients. What makes you think that this kid is so special?” Snake: “Because he is. If you don’t believe me, then just watch one match of his and see for yourself. All we want is a chance to show you what we can do. That’s not so much to ask now is it?” Alfonso: “I suppose not.” (Meanwhile, Russo continues to walk along through the garden with Shannon at his side. They seem to have walked deep enough in the garde to lose sight of the Snake and Alfonso. The two continue their walk as Shannon turns to Russo and begins to speak.) Shannon: “Now Eric, you really surprise me. I'm old enough to be your mother.” (Shannon leans towards Russo with an enticing look. Russo, not thinking of what he is doing, eyes her beautiful figure in the moonlight. He allows her lips reach his. He takes her in his arms, gently . . . romantically, and kisses her neck. As the two break their embrace, Shannon turns and runs off without a word. Realizing what she has just done. As she does so however, she looks over her shoulder towards Russo with a small smile. As the woman distances herself from him, Russo turns to continue his walk. He quickly jumps back startled however as he comes face to face with his mentor. The Snake looks at his pupil with a fury filling his eyes. He explodes in anger at Russo.) Snake: “What the hell is wrong with you Eric!? What are you thinking doing something like that!? Do you know how easily Alfonso could have been by my side when I saw you!? You could have ruined us both!” (This time, Russo does not back down as usual. Instead he fires back with heated words of his own.) Russo: “I’m tired of taking orders from you! It’s always . . . Eric do this and Eric do that! What about what I wanna do!? If you don’t like it, why do you keep training me!?” Snake: “I’ve already told you, there is no turning back now! You’re stuck with me wether you like it or not!” (It seems as though Russo has finally had enough, he charges at the Snake with his arms outstretched. The force of Russo’s charge sends the Snake’s back into a large oak tree as he stumbles back. Russo does not let up however continuing the assault on the Snake. Strangely the Snake laughs at Russo as he moves in. He yells out at his pupil.) Snake: “That’s it Eric, that’s the reason I chose you. That anger . . . that fury, that’s what is going to make you one of the greats.” (As the words finish leaving his mouth, the Snake charges off the tree into Russo. This sends Russo backwards into another tree. The Snake continues.) Snake: “Don’t you realize yet what I have done for you? I have molded you into the perfect wrestling machine. An unfeeling monster who will stop at nothing to achieve what he wants. Do what you want though, I will no longer try and stop you.” (The Snake lets out a sinister laugh as he continues.) Snake: “I’ll leave you to make your own mistakes, and I’ll be waiting there when you come around and realize all of the unneeded trouble that you have caused yourself. I just want you to remember what your life was like before I took charge of it Eric. I want you to think about how I’ve already changed you for the better.” (The Snake smiles at Russo with a devilish grin as the scene fades out. The screen now fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
