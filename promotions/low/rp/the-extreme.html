The Extreme

31 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Valiant begat The Hypocrite. And The Hypocrite will beget The Extreme. Seymour’s guilty conscience no longer haunts him. If he survives his first Global Title match, he will give Iron Lung a shot later on. But for now, the LOW Xtreme Championship is in Seymour’s sights. A championship battle waged under rules Seymour is unfamiliar with. If Seymour triumphs, he will hold four championships simultaneously, an extremely rare feat. But to turn the trick, he’s going to need to learn to use some non-lethal weaponry. Let the Home Depot hilarity ensue! “Zounds, Aerith! Look at all the weaponry! SOMETHING here has to be able to help me defeat Iron Lung and capture his championship!” Pushing the trademark orange Home Depot shopping cart through the cavernous aisles of the Home Depot, Seymour’s eyes scan the shelves, looking for anything he can find. Aerith walks alongside him, a hand resting on his shoulder. Seymour Almasy: This is truly a gigantic Weapon Shop! I had no idea there was so much excellent weaponry here! Say, I woender if this thing is legal? The Final Fantasy scoops up a sabre saw, and pushes the button. The device turns on, as Seymour swings it around. Customers flee wildly, as the saw bites into the plastic shopping cart, leaving a nice-sized cash in the industrial strength plastic. Seymour Almasy: Hmmm…that would seem to indicate ‘probably not’. Aerith Heartilly: Yes, I think I’d be inclined to agree with you on that one, Seymour. Despite herself, she smiles, as he continues to look around. Seymour Almasy: Man…Iron Lung’s not a happy camper. Aerith Heartilly: Why would you say that? Seymour Almasy: Because, I found that thing in your drawer, you know, the battery-powered one? Aerith’s face pales slightly… Aerith Heartilly: Did it…vibrate? Seymour Almasy: No, no. But when I turned it on, sound came out of it! Aerith Heartilly: That’s the radio, Seymour. Breathing a sigh of relief, Aerith waits for Seymour to continue. Seymour Almasy: So, anyway, Iron Lung was on with this guy…I think he called himself Howard Stern. Aerith Heartilly: That’s odd. I saw him on Regis and Kelly this morning…he’s making the rounds of the talk shows. Seymour Almasy: But why? Last time I checked, you don’t prepare for battles by talking about them! You prepare for them through action! Through training! Aerith Heartilly: Well, yes…but seriously, how often do you actually train for a battle? Seymour Almasy: Well, um…there was that time in 2002 where I trained to fight Chaos… Aerith Heartilly: My point exactly. So, what did Iron Lung say? Seymour Almasy: Well, first, Howard Stern referred to me as a cat. Aerith Heartilly: A cat? Seymour Almasy: Yeah, he called me a pussy. I don’t THINK I look like a cat…but I guess he does. Aerith Heartilly: Uh, Seymour? Seymour Almasy: Yes, Aerith? Aerith Heartilly: Calling someone a pussy means you think that he or she is a coward. Seymour Almasy: C-c-c-coward? Coward? COWARD?! I SWEAR I SHALL GO DOWN TO WHEREVER THIS HOWARD STERN WORKS, AND TEACH HIM THAT I AM NO COWARD! AND I SHALL DO IT WITH THIS! Seymour reaches out for the nearest weapon at hand, and comes up with a garden hose. He swings the hose around over his head like a lasso, but it slips from his hands. The green object goes flying, and careens into a light fixture shelf. Seymour winces, and quickly pushes the cart away from the scene. Seymour Almasy: Errr…I guess that’s probably not the best weapon either, huh Aerith? Aerith Heartilly: Once again Seymour, I would be inclined to agree. Seymour Almasy: Ah well. But, do not fret, my love! There are still twenty more aisles for us to search through! Under her breath, Aerith sighs. Aerith Heartilly: That’s what I was afraid of… Seymour Almasy: What was that, Aerith? She scrambles to recover, doing a poor job of it at that. Aerith Heartilly: Uh…nothing Seymour! Nothing at all! Thankfully for Aerith, Seymour’s perpetual state of naivete prevents him from following up any further with the line of questioning. He shrugs, looking around the store, still in search of weaponry. Seymour Almasy: Out of all the weaponry here, Aerith, I find it impossible to believe that there is NOTHING that I can use against Iron Lung! Aerith Heartilly: It’s not that there’s nothing here, it’s just that most of it would cause mortal injury, and LOW would probably frown on you killing one of their top stars. Seymour Almasy: True, true… All seems bleak for the Final Fantasy. However, it is then that he spots his saving grace. The one weapon that will lead him to victory. Seymour Almasy: THERE IT IS, AERITH! I HAVE FOUND IT! Aerith Heartilly: What? Where? Who? Ditching the shopping cart, Seymour sprints at a dead run across the store, knocking aside children and old ladies a like. He baseball slides neatly under two people carrying a long board, and finally arrives at his destination. Seymour Almasy: Here we are! Come, Aerith! Aerith looks up at the sign, and shakes her head. Aerith Heartilly: No. He can’t. I mean, there’s just no possible way his ultimate weapon to defeat Iron Lung could be… Seymour Almasy: A STEEL CHAIR! Aerith nearly faints, for indeed, Seymour Almasy has a typical steel chair in his hands, and has risen it skyward as if it were Excalibur itself. Seymour Almasy: WITH THIS WEAPON, I AM INVINCIBLE! Aerith Heartilly: Seymour… Seymour Almasy: I AM INVULNERABLE! Aerith Heartilly: Seymour! Seymour Almasy: I AM GUARENTEED VICTORY! Aerith Heartilly: SEYMOUR!!! Aerith’s scream snaps Seymour out of his grandeur. Seymour Almasy: Yes? Aerith Heartilly: EVERYONE USES STEEL CHAIRS! Seymour’s happiness fades, and is instantly replaced by dejection. Seymour Almasy: Really? Aerith Heartilly: Well, yes, Seymour… Seymour Almasy: That merely means I shall have to learn how to use a chair better than anyone else! Come, Aerith! Aerith Heartilly: Where are we going NOW? Seymour Almasy: Well, first, to pay for my great new weapon, and then, TO PRACTICE ITS USE! Before long, I shall be the best chair-wielding warrior in all of Lights Out Wrestling! Then, and only then, will I be able to wrest the LOW Xtreme Title from around the waist of Iron Lung! Aerith cannot even get a sigh out, before Seymour is already halfway to the checkout line. (Fade; to be continued.)
