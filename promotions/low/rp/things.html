Things...

25 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Dax. Take a walk on the wild side and come with me to another place. Walk with me beside your fears and revel in my pity' A long dark passage way lies before our eyes, upon both sides of the passage trees grow large their tall branches stand agelessly staring towards us as though judging us telling us that we are no better than any other man or woman to have walked that path before. There is a slight difference in opinion there though I suppose, Robbie Elapidae is walking down that path and I suppose, so are we. Robbie is a changed man nowadays, his debts with Max Vain have been cleared but still Robbie has no idea who cleared them, his bewilderment however didn't mean much to him obviously as two days later (today) Robbie took himself back into the little club house and once again got himself even further into debt. Robbie panicked and knowing that this time he won't be getting any money from his anonymous benefactor he decided to actually train for his fight at Lights Out Wrestling and win the number one conterdership fight against Dax. Comparing Dax and The Blue Coral we see many things that are the same, the way they move within the ring is almost identical but their personalities, well they are totally different. Robbie isn't scared of Dax and I wouldn't think he ever will be. The Blue Coral has never bothered about his opponent and now with his need for cash he will not start worrying about it now. >> >> >> >> >> Robbie sits staring into the camera sitting upon the muddy floor as night finally draws nearer. He frowns at us through his white locks and pushing them behind his ears he grins suddenly and speaks out in his young child like voice. 'I face Dax upon the show named Aftermath. I fight for the chance at taking on Jason Warrior and being crowned the Aftermath Champion' He sighs gently and shakes his head as though the title doesn't matter. The wind is blowing in the background and it begins to make a slight whistling noise as Robbie continues to talk. 'There is great pride in being chosen to fight for that title I suppose' he says with a large hint of sarcasm, he smiles again and drags his hand through his knotted and tangled white hair. Inwardly Robbie had no intention of winning this fight for it's honor, he would be fighting and winning this match for the pay he would receive afterwards, he was winning this fight for himself 'Dax. Do you really believe that you can beat me? I who stood with Josh Starwind during the tag team competition, I who has been fighting since I was a child? I am far beyond you in both skill and charisma Dax and soon you shall find that out' Robbie's words are spoken with confidence and what has seemed to be a hidden maturity so far in his LOW career. 'I do not care who you think you are. I do not care of your achievements here or anywhere else. I am the snake and soon you shall suffer from my bite' with those words spoken Robbie rises to his feet and begins to walk away down the long winding path once more. We rise and follow him slowly from behind his footsteps even now are confident even with the danger that he could be attacked for the money he owes at any time. There is a constant tension in the air around him what it is that could be eating away at him there is no knowledge, we could ask ourselves questions about it all day and there is no doubt that we'll learn soon enough why Robbie Elapidae is so tense. Until then the question should and will remain. >> >> >> >> >> What is wrong with me is probably everyone's question now. Why do I have such confidence in my victory and why am I all but even the slightest bothered that Dax has talent and that is why he too is here at Lights Out Wrestling. The answers are simple really. I am here now to fight and I understand this. I do not bother any more with the politics behind some fights, as I understand that Lights Out doesn't have that. I was signed to battle it out within the best company in the world and I put pen to paper in a deal that has led into something new, something unexplored. I was raised a black sheep and I remained as such for the longest period I can think. I have fought every thing and every body since I was a small child and even now I fight and still I have no goal. Jimmy gave me a reason to live and with his death that reason died also, protecting Emma Logan from Dan should never have been my task for like the fool that I am I fell in love with a woman who knows not how to love. I became a man with more issues than any other man I can name now and that is not what I should be here for, I am a fighter of men not of issues and from now on its back to business. My debts are larger than I can handle but my victory against Dax and then beating Jason Warrior should help me clear them soon enough. The clubs know my name and hunt out my weakness I love to watch the ball roll round and to see the cards being laid out in front of me ready to be played, yet as most men I cannot play them well. I stand in the way of my own success and keep myself in poverty as I cannot bear to stop this one desire, I am a man who is addicted to gambling, whether it is someone offering me odds on myself winning or just choosing a number and hoping that you got it right, I am always ready to be had by the con artists. One day I shall no longer have this problem and I shall once more have control over all that I do, until then I am going to a place where they can help me, I am going into a place where I will only come out when I am booked to fight. My training shall be coordinated from within its grounds and I shall be safe... >> >> >> >> >> Robbie stops walking as he comes to a large black gate he nods and wipes a tear from his eye as he reads the sign post. 'Gambling Closure Center' Pressing the button a young woman answers, 'Hello, Gambling Closure Centre main office. Can I help?' Robbie smiles inwardly and nods his head as though hoping that the woman on the other side does not require any words as she can see his head movement. 'Erm, excuse me... are you still there... hello?' for a moment longer he is silent and then finally gathering all the confidence that remains within his body he replies. 'I'd like to sign myself in...' {End}
