Set up for a Stryke Out!

20 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~ To find yourself jilted is a blow to your pride. Do your best to forget it and if you don't succeed, at least pretend to. Moliere (1622 - 1673) ~ ~ Pride is a powerful narcotic, but it doesn't do much for the auto-immune system. Stuart Stevens, Northern Exposure, Brains, Know-How, and Native Intelligence, 1990 ~ ~ Adam Stryker, which is the name of my next opponent, which is the name of my adversary who is in my way to the level of excellence that I seek! Here is a man that could very well be the next World Champion, but yet he sits there and cries about being treated unfairly! Well Stryker, I’m going to show you why pride is a dangerous element to have when dealing with me! I’m going to show you that I am a man not to be taken lightly! You won’t be able to silence me, turn away from me, or pretend that I am just some damn Jobber on the streets! At Realm, I will show you why I am an entity to be feared! At Realm, your Pride will ultimately be your downfall, Adam….welcome to my REALM!! ~ ~ The striking resemblance in one’s heart….. ~ Date: November 19th, 2004 Time: 12:04 AM Location: PNC Park at North Shore, 115 Federal St. Pittsburgh, PA 15212 = The twilight of the half moon was the only light that was available to John Adams in PNC Park. He liked to go to places where he thought he could absorb the most about his opponent before a match. And the man that is on his mind is Adam Stryker….who does this guy think he is? He really feels that he is that important to LOW? With so much new talent coming in, Stryker is going to be lucky to even be in the running still for the world title. John walks through the giant stadium alone with wonder of what to expect from all the hype that is Adam Stryker… = ~ All of the wants and desires that we crave in our life……. ~ Adams thoughts: What causes a man to risk so much? What causes a man to put his career on the line due to unfair treatment? Here is a man that has put that level of excellence aside for his own pride and self respect because all he was was treated unfairly! What a load of crap!!! =Adams walks through the stadium where fans from Pittsburgh would cheer for there MLB team the Pirates. There was a team who had an unfair time in there season because of there skills. Adams hated baseball; he saw no point into it where a man goes up to bat just to hit a ball. Now in wrestling there he could do what he does best, and that’s make people hurt. Every man has a quirk about them, that one thing that drives them to be the very best at what they do. With Adams it’s “The Prophecy”, what he calls the very life blood of this business today but with Stryker its different cause his quirk is simply on how unfair he has been treated. = ~ Finding it hard in life to keep that endless hunger full………. ~ = Adams walks to the field down below to reflect on symbolism he saw. The stadium tenders thought nothing of it when he did, for they had to much work to worry about to be concerned with one guy. Adams reflects the many times he has been held back with a federation, reflecting on times of old when he got his chance he quieted all of the non believers. He showed them all that he was for real, and that he was clearly a dominate force in this business today. Now he has a chance to do it all over again. But this time with a whole new set of non believers, a whole new set of people who have doubt in there mind that Adams is for real and that he is this dominate force in the Professional Wrestling World. = ~ But yet we move on, with only a shed of light called Pride to be our guide….. ~ Adams thinking to him self: I’m dangerously close in seeing my dream, seeing the birth of a new order in LOW…my order! And when it happens, I will raise my hand of dominance once more and prove to the world that I am for real! All of the signs are in place, and LOW is oblivious to The Day of Reckoning before them! Even in Prime Time Central, signs every where and the poor infidels are too busy pleasing the FCC to even notice! For when it happens, I will bury them all by force that is my will!!! = A couple of security guards flash there lights towards Adams direction….when they spot him, they proceed to head his way. Adams takes no notice at the Security Guards for he had more pressing issues on his mind, Stryker, his interfed match with Dan Logan, and his plans for total domination not only in LOW, but the whole damn world of Professional Wrestling itself. = ~ Leading us all on a quest to find that one thing that matters most to man or woman alike………. ~ Guard #1: Hey, you can’t be here! You’re going to have to clear out of here!! Guard #2: Yeah, if you leave quietly…we will forget that this whole thing ever happened!!! John Adams: You think of me as a threat? A man of my size…7’3’’ 350 pounds a threat? Guard #1: We..Well you are still going to have to leave sir!! Guard #2: Ye..Yeah, so turn around and ju..just walk out that entrance!! John Adams: Ahh the pride you guards must feel when you do your job!! But I ask you, will your loved ones reward you for the job you have done tonight? Will your employer give you a raise because you two got a suspicious looking guy to leave the stadium? Guard #1: Just leave sir and we don’t have to get physical!! Guard #2: No, I’m not getting a raise for this!! = The guards start to argue about the appropriate time to talk about these revelations. As this was going on, John Adams takes another look around the stadium in a type of awe followed by disgust. Every one is oblivious to the carnage that is to be released and he likes it that way. The Prophetic Conspiracy will take the world by storm and everybody will be too much in a rush with there everyday ordeals to stop it. = ~ Some may stray from fear and disbelief…… ~ Guard #1: Hey….where are you going? John Adams: You two have answered my questions and for that I take my leave! But ask yourselves a question, instead of setting yourselves to strike out in life, take the initiative to leave your handicaps and live for the future you may or not bring! = Leaving the guards with a dumbfounded look, the man known as “The Prophet” takes his leave. Strike…..strike, funny to think about a man with the name will strike out at Realm. The End is near. = ~ But in this ever continuing circle we call life; “The Prophecy” will always lead you back!! ~ ~ Ah, what fun I will have to toy with you Stryker! That coming of a New Era is upon us and it will weed out the weak minded fools like Annihilation was! The poor deluded ass was complaining also about his big break, that he killed his only friend so he would have a companion in the afterlife! How pathetic and weak, he had not enough pride in him to accept that after so long…he won, but at what price mind you? ~ ~ O, now, for ever Farewell the tranquil mind! farewell content! Farewell the plumed troop and the big wars That make ambition virtue! O, farewell! Farewell the neighing steed and the shrill trump, The spirit-stirring drum, the ear-piercing fife, The royal banner, and all quality, Pride, pomp, and circumstance of glorious war! And, O you mortal engines, whose rude throats The immortal Jove's dread clamours counterfeit, Farewell! Othello's occupation's gone! William Shakespeare (1564 - 1616), 'Othello', Act 3 scene 3 ~ ~ Ill fares the land, to hast'ning ill a prey, Where wealth accumulates, and men decay; Princes and Lords may flourish, or may fade: A breath can make them, as a breath has made; but a bold peasantry, their country's pride, When once destroyed can never be supplied. Oliver Goldsmith (1730 - 1774) ~ ~ FIN ~
