We're Sending You back To The Future

captured 3 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Time: Unknown Place: The Las Vegas War zone When: Sometime in Early Jan. I can see the smoke bellowing from the fire fighting. I can hear the guns a blazing and then scream of people dying. I can see tanks moving through the streets trying to move past our anti tank entrenchments. I am in my little hideaway, watching as the enemy troops moved about. I was adjusting the scope on my sniper rifle as I had a certain target and had to wait for him. I had been waiting for over three days hoping that he would pop his head out so I could take him out. Colonel Justin Evers was the leader of the government forces in this area. He was a cold and ruthless and was as hated as almost anyone else in the military. I had wanted to get my chance at him and staying in my little perch had so far proved to be a totally fruitless adventure so far. I reached down, unclasped one of my side pockets and then pulled out some crackers. I opened the pack and then slid one in my mouth. I just sucked on it gently and let the remnants get soaked up and go down my throat. My future was so bleak, was so utterly crappy that I hated being here. I hated the fact that I was hiding like a rat, waiting for a piece of cheese to be placed on a rat trap. I was going to do whatever I could to make sure that Evers never saw the light of day. The problem was that it should have never come down to this. We as Americans should have the right to be able to live, as we want. However, our grand president has other things in mind. He likes to be able to pull all the strings and make things the way he likes it. “Shane?” a voice crackled on my voice communicator. I pulled my communicator from one of my pockets. I put the headphones on and then plugged it in. “Secure station alpha…” I replied. “Confirmed secured station, Shane.” The voice replied. “What is going on?” I asked. “You need to call off the stakeout, code beta beta alpha Charlie.” The voice replied. “Why?” I asked. “Not important, return to base immediately.” The voice replied. “Affirmative…” I replied. I gathered up my gear and slowly made my way down to the streets. I had my gun at the ready, just in case I ran into any unsavory characters. I was not expecting anyone on this side of the zone. The government forces have not been able to break through our defenses that we had set up on Main Street and we had defended everything else with our very last breath and we are determined to drive the government out of the Las Vegas zone. The sheer numbers have taken its toll on everyone, I mean it was starting wear us down. I walked cautiously through the back alleys, hoping that I would not run into any of the enemy. I peeked around the corner making sure that there was nobody around. “Good…” I whispered to myself as I made my way out into the dark cover of the night. Most of the street light have quit working or have been destroyed all together so it is pretty easy to hide and as for all the lights from all the old casinos and hotels. Well those were destroyed for the most during the conflict a few years ago. Most of the casinos are no longer in operation and are inhabited by stragglers and homeless people. Kids can usually be seen at night trying to act like the machines work and sometimes I see the kids actually getting metal coins from the things that have the words “Slot Machine” on them in big red lettering. I have no idea what would be done with the coins, unless they were used as some sort of currency. I imagine that could have been possible back then. “Bamm, Bamm, Bamm” I turned hearing the cannon fire and mortars exploding nearby. “Could they actually be advancing on our positions?” I asked myself as I reached down and pulled my gun to my shoulder. I slipped on my night vision goggles and turned them on. I looked around but saw nothing. I flipped up my goggles, as I did not feel I was going to be threatened anytime soon. As I turned a corner though I was quit mistaken… “Hold it, right there rebel disease!” a deep voice beckoned. “What?” I said as I slowly raised my hands concealing a knife under my hand. “Where do you think you are going? This is a restricted area, the government as outlawed all firearms except for those who have been issued by the military or police. I do not see a badge so you must be a rebel!” The man assumed. “Maybe I am, but thing is you will never know…” Moreover, as I finished saying that in one fluid motion I slipped behind the man and then slit his throat. The blood flowed from the mans neck as he rolled onto his stomach and his body flinched and then it subsided. “Ya know there are other non violent ways of doing that.” A familiar voice replied. I turned an it was Alexandria Formatose. We had grown very close and had become lovers for the last few months she had joined the resistance almost when it had first started and was widely considered the best at hand to hand combat. Even though in my mind I was by far the best that our battalion had. She patted me on the shoulder and then leaned into me and gave me a deep, sultry kiss. It was almost as good as having a beer, if I had not forgotten what those had tasted like. We searched the enemy soldier and found a few useful items. We found some rations, a small radio that when we turned it on was broadcasting propaganda. “Look Alex, we have to be aggressive and you know it cause this is not some kind of little tea party, this is a battle for survival.” I angrily snapped as I let the limp body fall to the ground. “How can you stand there and tell me the way I try to do things is wrong when we live in terror everyday we wake up?” “Because there is always another way, there is always another option. Sometimes, the hardest part is finding the “other” way.” She said as she glared at me and then walked by my running her fingers under my chin. “You know I love you but sometimes you can really make me crazy…” I said as I kissed her hand and then grabbed her pulling her towards me. “This is not the place and you know it…” She said in a whisper as she gazed deeply into my eyes. “I know, but I can still kiss you…” I said just before a large explosion rocked the building just the right of us. “Ba-Booom!!!” “What the hell?” I yelled as the wall of the Cineplex started to crumble. “We have to get out of here…” I watched as the building started to fall apart and then crumble towards us. We ran and as I looked back to see the building fall just where we was and then I gasped as a huge tank rolled around the corner. The turret turned towards us and then another explosion rang at us. “Ba-Booom!!” This one just missed us as it hit a gas station in front of us. We lay on the ground for a few moments and then I looked over at Alexandria and seen that she was not moving. I crawled over to her as the tank rumbled through the debris of the building. I flipped her over and then smacked her face, trying to get her to come to. “Alexandria….” I yelled as I tried to shake her, I looked back seeing the tank bearing down on us. “Wha….” She muttered as she tried to regain her composure. “Come on, get up we need to move…NOW.” I yelled as I pulled her to her feet and dragged her into an alley. We then ducked into a building, and then I pulled up Alexandria trying to get her to snap out of it. “Alex, come on girl!” “Huh?” she blinked her eyes slowly and then looked up. “Where am I?” “In a world of hurt if we don’t move.” I yelled as I pointed the tank that was turning towards the front of the building we were in. “Oh shit….” She gasped as we got ran out the side door and back down the alleyway. “Ba-Boooom!” I looked and saw the building we were in crumble to the ground as the force of the blast ripped through the building, kicking out smoke and dust. “What the hell is going on?” Alex yelled as she ran alongside me. “Let’s just say the government is close to getting that bounty they have on us.” I was a little scared; this was the closest the government has ever come to collecting that bounty that they had on me. My heart was actually beating pretty fast and the thought they could have actually gotten the deal done was weirdly exciting. Running from the tank, although dangerous was also making my adrenaline rush and I was really getting excited. We finally made it to the entrance of the rebel stronghold and went down the secret passage. We could hear the tank rumbling overhead and the roof shook under the intense pressure causing it to shake and the beams creaked like they were going to fall through and crush us all. I walked past the commanding officers and then to my barracks… “You ok?” Jesse asked as he could see I was exhausted. “I dunno today was pretty bad, was pretty close to getting Alexandria and I killed. It was to close for myself.” I said as I sighed and sat against the barren wall. “You worried about Alexandria huh?” Jesse asked. “Yeah, guess so.” I replied as I reached down and grabbed the photo of Noah Hanson. “ya know other than Alex there is nothing else that drives me more to do whatever I can to make this time a better time….NOTHING!” “That is good to hear babe!” I looked and there was Alexandria, she was wearing a bandage on her forehead and a smile. “You ok?” I asked, a relieved smile appeared on my face. “Yeah, I think so. A little groggy but I think for the most part I will be just fine….” She replied as she sat next to me and then placed her hand in mine. “Everything will be better…” I replied. “It has to be…” Time: The Present When: March 20, 2005 Where: Las Vegas, Nevada “So what do you think of your competition for this match Shane?” Johnny asked as he looked over all of the entrants in the match. “Chris Johnson” “Mike Wade” “The Inferno Kid” “Michael Blair” “The British Bomber” “Sektor” “El Zilcho” “Those are the hurdles I have to clear; those are the men that are standing in my way. They are standing in my way of me moving a step closer to my goal. I will not be denied, I will not rest till I get what I want.” I replied angrily. “And that is what?” Johnny asked. “I need to get the funds to be able to get my hands on Noah Hanson. I also need the self respect and dignity that I strive for each and every day. This means I need to go out there and be at my very best for a match where I am probably the biggest underdog. I mean there are guys in there that have huge reputations outside of LOW. I mean The British Bomber was famous in FAW; Volcano can be linked to World Wide Wrestling and Wrestling Midwest. Chris Johnson earned my respect when he handed me a clean pin fall loss, and then there is The Inferno Kid who I defeated in my debut match. Hell, the kid is more than just a kid he is a world class talent. I mean any of these guys even Mike Wade, Sektor, El Zilcho could very easily pull this match out and walk out with the win and a chance to face the number one contender to the Realm title. A chance to shine, a chance to rise to the very top and show everyone in LOW that you are the very best.” I said almost sounding like I was making a sales pitch for myself. “Wow, that was great. You sounded like a wrestler there for a minute.” Johnny replied. “Really?” I was astounded that I actually was starting to feel like a wrestler. I love the thought of doing this ppv thing and the fact that I get paid money to act perform for all the fans. At Subliminal Dawn the wrestling world will be shown that there is in fact a rising star in Lights Out Wrestling and his name is Shane Hahn. In addition, whether they like it or not or just afraid to admit this light will never burn out or fall to earth. I will rise to the very top of LOW; there is no denying what I will do. I will succeed not because I have to but to save the future that is my home… My future…. My life… My pain…. My suffering….
