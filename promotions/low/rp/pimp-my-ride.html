Pimp My Ride

19 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dark Haze is sitting in a room with Xzibit. Also in the room is a camera crew from MTV. The room has a natural feel, and the MTV crew is recording “Pimp My Ride: Superstars Uncut.” Dark Haze just arrived, so him, Xzibit, the West Coast Customs crew, and the MTV people are all heading outside to see what Dark Haze brought them. Dark Haze speaks, “Now, I didn’t bring the tightest car, but this one will be hot.” Everyone gets outside and looks in the lot. There are cars varying from Escalades to classic Impalas to Ferraris and Bentlys. In the midst of all the beautiful cars is a nice, but not spectacular Chevy Colorado. It has a glossy black paint, an extended cab, and looks stock. There are mixed reactions to the car, but nothing too negative, just a more surprised expression. Xzibit comments, “I see, you want the truck. We’ll make it hot.” “For sure. I have a couple things in mind.” Dark Haze replies. They go back inside, to a staged room, where they can talk about plans for it. Dark Haze and Xzibit stay toward one end of the table. Dark Haze explains a few things he wants, “I think we definitely gotta go with blue, for one. Two, I think we should slam it, with bags.” Haze is talking about air suspension. “Also, high top rims, and a mammoth system.” One of the guys from West Coast Customs throws out his idea, “That’s all good, but we gotta go extreme. This is a one time thing it’s gotta be big.” Xzibit remarks, “Haze is the Xtreme Champ, and he may be going world.” The WCC continues with his idea, “I say we stretch the cab, not too big, but give it room some room like a Hummer.” Another guy brings in a suggestion, “Well, a powerful man needs a powerful ride. Not only do we want this to turn heads; we want real enthusiasts to notice how much power this truck has. I suggest a corvette engine, bored out, a super charger, and a totally racing feel.” “Damn nigga! I’m gettin’ hooked up cuz.” Dark Haze exclaims, talking about their ideas. “They always do it big at WCC.” Xzibit says. “Hey. Remember. Blue paint, and not shitty decals or nothing.” “Alright Haze.” One of the men assures him. The men continue to talk about the truck a little bit more, and eventually, they part their separate ways. A MTV camera crew stay with Haze, but one stays at the shop to record the work. Dark Haze walks outside, and the Los Angeles atmosphere hits him. Haze walks to a pearl, blue ‘63 Impala. He opens the door and the pearl white leather complements every aspect of the car; it’s clean. Haze answers a phone call, he holds the screen to the MTV camera. It reads, “GAME.” “What up cuz?” Dark Haze says into his cell phone. You can barely pick up the word on the other end. “Yo, blood. What’s good?” The Game replies. “You chillin? I’mma swing through.” Dark Haze hangs up the phone with, G-Unit rapper, The Game. It’s ironic that the two men are from ‘rival’ gangs, arguably the most well-known. Dark Haze drives through California, and heads to The Game’s residence. Meanwhile, Haze raps to an old-school, West Coast style, instrumental beat. Hit the golden state, Pimp My Ride in Cali I’m West Coastin’, watch as my money tallys From the city of Atlanta, to west side valleys I keep it street, ride through the gutters and the alleys I’mma be chillin’ with CPT’s second nigga wit attitude If you don’t watch your step, his set will splatter you Paste yo face to the cement and say they plastered you Don’t make me bring the Crips, cuz we’re the master crew Check me out though, this is Game’s residence A Blood and a Crip, you could say gangs irrelevant We wearin’ red and blue, but the purple you smellin’ it That is the hood in us, so we don’t dwell on it At the end, we all have got one main goal To go platinum, it ain’t about world or gold We take the game, don’t let it take you soul You pockets will grow, and your stomachs get full Like I said, you better pack up, nigga we packin’ guns, that’ll leave you done Tell your ass, “suckas better run, this is my slum, better watch where you come ” I’ll leave you head slumped, body with lumps, turned into chunks Shotgun blast lay you out like punks, , fall like a slam dunk, leave ya in da street, gotta nice clean trunk. Dark Haze eventually gets to The Game’s house. He street is as hood as they come. Gang activity is evident. Blood gang members walks the streets, and graffiti filled the public walls nearby, as well as some nearby shops. Haze walks in and the inside is somewhat nice, but is far from special. Compared to the average house on MTV cribs this one looks like an ant pile. Out back on the minuscule amount of grass, that they own, is a stand up punching bag, cemented in the ground. Outside, there is also a weight set. The two gang hooligans sit down on the sofa and get comfortable. Both of them pull out bags of the most popular, widely used illicit substance in the U.S. The two of them begin the process of rolling that substance into cigars. Haze looks at The Game’s bag. “That’s some chronic.” Haze tells The Game. “Yeah; it’s purple.” At a closer look the nugs and they can be seen clearly. They are about twenty percent green and eighty percent purple, with a mixture of reddish-orange hairs. They finish rolling the cigars, and they trade to let each other try different smoke. Haze tries it and his mouth is overwhelmed by a piney, subtle flavor, yet his friend hits his bud, and he tries the fruity-skunk flavored plant which is very strong. Both men look pleased. They get situated again and fall back into the cushions. “I hear you gotta big match comin’ up.” The Game states, to strike conversation. “Yeah. I’ll be fighting Jason Kage. It’s supposed to be pretty big and anticipated and shit.” Dark Haze says in a ‘high’ tone, and his eyes loosen up and relax. “Who’s he?” Haze replies, “Jason Kage? He’s the LOW World Champion. I have to make a good impression; it’s my biggest match since my return, and shit, a lot of people are waiting to see me.” “True, you gotta remember, I don’t watch your show that much. I thought you were fighting The Demond. I’m not too into the whole wrestling thing.” The Game says. “Whatever, it’s straight.” The two continue to chill, talk, and smoke all day. Later that week.... Dark Haze pulls into the West Coast Customs shop, parks the car, and goes inside. The MTV camera crew is rolling live footage. Xzibit walks to the LOW star and leads him toward there final product. “Now you remember what we had to work with, a stock Colorado. We’ll here’s your new ride.” Xzibit says. “Oh shit!” Dark Haze yells. He goes over to the truck. “This shit is hot.” The truck is a deep blue similar to a Lexus RX. The paint has shiny silver flakes in it and glistens from miles away. As well, it has a light, white colored, airbrushed mural. It is so detailed and crazy that many words come to mind when you look at the faces, money, and party style life portrayed by it. The cab is stretched out, about four to six feet, and the truck is now a SUV conversion. The rims are twenty inch Giovanni custom chrome wheels. The frame is flat on the ground, literally touching the cement. If an expert was to look at the car, they would notice that it has been shaved clean. There are no door handles, no third brake light, no gas cap, and no tailgate handle. A WCC guy opens the expensive topper. In the bed is a fifteen gallon fuel cell and six Kove Audio Armageddon speakers. They are invisible to the eye, but that is only because they are hidden by a box. They WCC guys explain how the box is ported into the cab, so that the system will have enough air space. They shut the bed topper, and press a remote button. The driver side door pops open, and a hydraulic sound is made as the door slides up, like they would on a Lamborghini. “Damn that’s hot. 50 Cent ain’t got shit on me.” A look inside is amazing. The interior is so clean. It’s blue suede. The original small backseats were replaced by a bench row of seats. There are TVs in the headrest, two in the front seat, and one thirty inch TV out the back window. They explain how one of the TVs in the front seat, for the driver, is hooked up to a camera, so the driver can see behind him. Along with the TVs come two Playstation 2s. Also in the backseat is a microphone and a machine to record any freestyles or songs. There is also a cd/dvd burning machine, so the music can be put onto a disc. “Xzibit you gotta cut one on that.” Haze says. Then Haze touches one of the switches on the dash board, and his car’s suspension pops up about five inches. “Raw, you did it.” Haze plays with all the switches and his car maneuvers in ways that few cars could. Haze hops out, and they pop the hood. Inside is a Corvette Engine. It has a supercharger and many upgrades. It also look very clean and new. A man then walks over with a big piece. “You take this and put it in like this, now your engine is finished.” The man puts the blue painted engine cover and snaps it in, giving the truck it’s final touch. The guys then bring him back, to show him the inside some more. “Here’s all the little things you missed.” One of them says. That man pushes a button and a storage pops out of the center console. It’s a cigar holder and a torch lighter. Then he reaches into the backseat and opens up a door that was hidden, without a close look. It’s a fridge, a decent size fridge. “But you can’t have a fridge, without your personal microwave.” The man says. He then shows Haze a miniature microwave. “Everything a person, like you would need.” A WCC employee says. “Yeah. I can smoke, get the munchies, eat, and drive, all at the same time. Oh yeah, while playing PS2. Straight.” Haze replies. Xzibit concludes, “So that’s it, you’ve officially been pimped.” The MTV crew yells, “Cut,” and they stop recording. Meanwhile, Haze hops into the backseat, and Xzibit follow. Dark Haze starts to roll one of his infamous cigars. Xzibit grabs the microphone and presses the record button. Listen to the story I’ve got weapons of mass destruction I’m about to clear out all of the rap games corruption With Haze, in CA, we ride out head bustin’ Let other people know we that they amount to nothin’ I rap one time, and bag Haze fires up th ly Yeah we’re chillin’ in his new, pimped-out ride Backseat PS2, eatin’, smokin’, of course gettin’ high Decked out in all blue, cuz, you know the side California to Atlanta the streets are filled with gangstas We show love to the founders, they the ones who made us If you wear the wrong colors, bitch we may just shank ya Shout out to West Coast Customs, I gotta say thank ya Dark Haze has the cigar lit, and he passes it to Xzibit, making Xzibit stop rapping. The two continue to smoke out the new truck, and enjoy their time together.
