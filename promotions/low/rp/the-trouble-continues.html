The Trouble Continues

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders along with a voice over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to a shot of hundreds of fans standing entranced as they watch actions transpiring on a large projection screen in a darkened room. The fans remain quiet a few with ear to ear smiles on their faces. Suddenly they erupt into a roar of applause. As the lights rise, we can now see that we are at some type of wrestling convention. The fans continue cheering as the host of the show takes the stage. He pulls out a cordless microphone and raises it to his mouth as he begins to speak to the legions of fans.) Host: “Wow! What a video . . . can you guys believe that today you will actually have the chance to meet and greet those four men that were just up on the screen!?” (The fans burst into a shower of applause.) Host: “Now . . . it’s time for what you have all been waiting for! Ladies and gentlemen . . . here they are, the masters of disaster . . . the Shadow Riders!” (Off stage, a stagehand quickly signals for the host to try and buy some more time. Grasping for what to do, the host tries his best to cover.) Host: “Before we actually meet these larger then life superstars of the wrestling world though . . . lets take a look at a few more clips.” (The scene cuts to a backstage dressing room where Jason Kage, Luke Justice, and Tristan Belmont are all decked out in full Shadow Rider attire. Kage is pacing back and forth in the small room. As he does this, he begins questioning the rest of the other two members of the group who are also walking nervously around the room.) Kage: “Where the hell is he!? He’s an hour and a half late . . . a freaking hour and a half! That’s just ridiculous!?” (Luke takes a peek through the curtain to see the fans becoming more and more ravenous by the second. He steps back allowing the curtain to close as he begins to speak to Belmont and Kage.) Justice: “Great . . . their gonna tare this place apart pretty soon. You know how these wrestling fans can get. Their like wild animals.” (Joining in with his friends, Belmont speaks.) Belmont: “He’s a twit . . . Russo is a fucking twit.” Kage: “Oh yeah, the office let me know he booked another fan appearance this week without us.” Luke: “For when?” Kage: “Tomorrow night after the photo shoot.” Belmont: “What do you expect? He’s a twit . . . a little twit.” (Growing more and more furious at Russo by the second, Kage responds.) Kage: “How big can his ego get? Now he doesn’t need us at all? Russo is a selfish bastard!” Luke: “Not to mention he ate my Doritos.” (Confused, Kage responds.) Kage: “He what?” Luke: “The other night, he ate my whole bag of Doritos after I fell asleep.” (Suddenly, the back entrance into the dressing room opens with a slow metallic squeak. Eric Russo steps through the frame of the door dressed in full Shadow Rider attire just like the rest of the group carrying a duffel bag. Russo has a big smile on his face as he strolls into the room and drops his bag on the floor.) Russo: “I know . . . I know . . . I’m late. So sue me.” (The other Shadow Riders just glare at Russo with a sinister gleam in their eyes.) Russo: “What? I got here as fast as I could.” (Back on stage, we can hear the host of the show nervously continuing to stall. As the Shadow Riders continue to argue back and forth.) Justice: “I thought we weren’t supposed to make any personal appearances without the rest of the group Eric.” (Backing down from the larger man, Russo tries to cover himself.) Russo: “When did I ever say that? I didn’t say that . . . I said it might be nice if we only appeared as a group. I never said we have to appear as a group. Besides, you guys said that you don’t need me. You guys don’t even like me so why does it matter?” (Seeing straight through Russo’s lies, Belmont speaks up.) Belmont: “My ass that’s what you said.” Kage: “You’re so full of shit Eric.” (Still trying to cover himself, Russo responds.) Russo: “Calm down guys . . . it’s nothing. It’s just a small appearance at a little club. Nothing major.” Luke: “Yeah . . . nothing major. How much are you getting paid for it?” Russo: “What do you guys want from me? You said you don’t need me, the fans do . . . what do you expect?” (A stagehand quickly peeks his head in the dressing room interrupting the argument between the Shadow Riders.) Stagehand: “You guys ready? This crowd is gonna attack if you don’t get out here soon.” (Seeing an opportunity to save himself, Russo quickly pipes up.) Russo: “Yeah . . . we’re ready. Let’s get going . . . time is money.” (Reluctantly the rest of the Shadow Riders zip up knowing that they have to get out on stage. Back out on the stagehand gives the thumbs up to the host that everything is set and ready to go. The host begins to introduce the Shadow Riders to the delight of the fans.) Host: “Okay, we’re ready . . . everyone put your hands together for the first member of the group known as the Shadow Riders . . . “The Vigilante” Luke Justice!” (Luke walks out onto the stage and raises his hand in response to the cheers of the fans.) Host: “Next . . . we have none other then . . . “The Demon” Tristan Belmont!” (Belmont walks out onto the stage unresponsive to the applause of the fans. He takes his place next to Luke.) Host: “Now, one half of the greatest tag team in history and co-founder of the Shadow Riders . . . “The Loose Cannon” Jason Kage!” (The fans roar as Kage walks onto the stage. He does his best to act like a heel but finally gives in to the cheers and slaps a few hands.) Host: “And finally . . . the other half of the greatest tag team in history . . . the current LOW Realm champion . . . and co-founder of the Shadow Riders . . . “Superstar” Eric Russo!” (Russo steps through the curtain where a spotlight shines down on him and follows him on the stage. Kage leans in towards Belmont and Luke as he mutters under his breath.) Kage: “He rented a spotlight . . . what an egomaniac.” (Russo raises his arms out to his sides basking in the adoration of the fans for a few seconds before joining the rest of the Shadow Riders on stage. The fans go nuts as the Shadow Riders stand united as a team. As they do this, the host speaks up once again.) Host: “Don’t forget folks . . . the Shadow Riders will be available for autographs and pictures over by table 34 later on today!” (The fans cheer in response to the announcement as they admire the group of wrestlers in all their glory. Little do they know however the turmoil that is creeping underneath the surface. The camera focuses in on a shot of the Shadow Riders as a group one last time as the picture fades out to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again where we hear a voice-over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The picture then cuts to a black, blank screen.)
