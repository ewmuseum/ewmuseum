Shattered Dreams- The Root of all Evil

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. Slowly the logo fades out to black. For a moment, nothing. Then the sound of Russo’s voice speaks out as he launches into a voice over.) Russo (VO): “With my match against Jamester still looming on the horizon, I had a new situation on my mind. As much as I wanted to get back to my training for the match, I wanted to get my hands on my new found money as well. As it always does with me, the money won out in the end. When I left my meeting with Mr. Daniels, I headed towards the closest bank in the area. I’m sure that once I got my money, returning to my training would not be as much of a problem.” (The scene opens to an interior shot of a major metropolitan bank. Inside the bank, Russo approaches one of the workers seated behind a desk with his check in hand. He pulls up a seat opposite of the tellers and begins to speak.) Russo: “Yes, I have a check. I’d like to cash it as soon as possible.” (The bank teller looks at Russo in a professional demeanor as he falls back on his trained response.) Teller: “How much is the check for?” (With a smile, Russo responds.) Russo: “It’s for two-hundred and fifty big ones. Two-hundred and fifty smackers. Two-hundred and fifty grand.” (The teller takes the check from Russo and opens it up. He pauses for a moment as he examines the check, it is for a little more then two-hundred and fifty thousand dollars. The teller looks back to Russo as he responds.) Teller: “You want to cash this entire check right now?” Russo: “Tell you what, give me fifty thousand and just deposit the other two-hundred into an account. That should work.” (Seemingly more appeasing to Russo since finding out about the amount of money he is dealing with, the teller responds with a smile.) Teller: “Why don’t you get comfortable, Mr., ah, Russo. I’m going to need two forms of identification to move any further with this.” (Russo begins reaching into his pockets and searching for something as he responds.) Russo: “Ah yes . . . no problem. I have my driver’s licence, and my astronaut application form. It doesn’t matter if I passed that or not does it? I kind of failed that test. They said something about me not being able to work in a group because of some sort of superiority complex. Guess it just goes to show you that even rocket scientists can make stupid mistakes.” (Russo hands the teller his paperwork. Then, shaking his head at Russo’s strange comments, the bank teller responds.) Teller: “Well it looks like everything is in order. All you have to do now is endorse this check, just sign here.” Russo: “I’m gonna need a pen.” (The teller responds with a smile.) Teller: “Sure . . . no problem.” (The teller quickly snatches a pen off his desk and hands it to Russo. Russo now points down at the paper.) Russo: “Sign here?” (The teller nods his head excitedly as Russo signs the paper.) Russo: “Well I guess that’s everything.” (The teller quickly stops Russo.) Teller: “Wait! I . . . uh . . . need you to fill out this deposit slip too.” (With a smirk, Russo responds as he picks up the pen once again and begins to fill out the deposit slip.) Russo: “Sure thing . . . endorsing and filling out the deposit slip. Filling out a few forms for two-hundred and fifty thousand dollars.” (By his demeanor, we can tell that Russo is a little surprised by his new found wealth. In fact, Russo is a lot surprised. The scene fades out as a voice over by Russo picks up.) Russo (VO): “That day, I made a vow that massive amounts of money would not change my life that greatly. I decided that I could live with only a few little extravagances such as a live-in butler and housekeeper. Seriously though, who was I kidding? I’m Eric Russo, and I deserve to have everything I want handed to me. It’s just a simple fact of life that people have to learn to deal with. This was my money, and I was going to use it to get everything I always wanted. I remember my dreams of owning a big house of my own on top of a hill. I remember wishing for a living room with a decorative, plaster lion from Mexico or a large twenty four seat dining table in a dining room with original oil paintings by Michelangelo and Rembrandt. Well now I could have it all. For the first time, I no longer cared about wrestling. What did I need it for now? I could buy anything I wanted. Why would I concern myself with the lesser people like Jesse Jamester when I didn’t have too?” (The scene reopens inside of Russo’s new house. The voice over continues.) Russo (VO): “I was soon to find out however that the lesser people still wanted things from me.” (Russo sits in the study of his fancy new house reading the daily paper. His butler walks into the room carrying a small steel serving tray with a drink in it. He approaches Russo and begins to speak.) Butler: “Your drink sir, just like the one you saw in your magazine earlier. I hope that it meets your expectations.” (Russo places his paper down on his lap as he looks at the drink.) Russo: “Well it looks like you got the bamboo umbrella and everything that I wanted. Not to shabby, what else should I expect though? After all, I am rich.” (The butler then smiles as he responds.) Butler: “Very good sir, very good. Oh, I also meant to make you aware that there are some charity people at the front door to see you.” (In a very serious tone, Russo responds in a grave tone.) Russo: “No, send them away! There are a lot of people in the world today who need charity more then I. I can get along just fine on my own for the time being.” Butler: “Ah . . . but sir, these people want you to give them money. Not the other way around.” (With a look of surprise, Russo responds.) Russo: “Me . . . give them money? Oh, ok. Send them in.” (With a nod, the butler leaves the room. Moments later he returns with the head of the charity organization. The head of the organization is dressed in the clothes of a priest. He approaches Russo and introduces himself.) Priest: “Good afternoon Mr. Russo. My name is Father Juan Las Vegas Il Corona.” (Russo smiles and offers the priest a seat as he responds.) Russo: “Father, you seem to be a religious man. What can I do for you? (Seeing his opportunity, the priest continues.) Father Juan: “All I ask of you is three minutes of your time so that you can see first hand, some film of a great ugliness that has been rearing it’s evil head throughout my country.” (Russo’s face fills with a look of terror as he responds.) Russo: “Oh God, I bet it’s disgusting.” (Russo now turns to his butler as he continues.) Russo: “Jeeves?” (The butler quietly responds.) Jeeves: “Yes sir?” Russo: “Can you dim the lights?” Jeeves: “Of course sir. One must do something to earn his keep.” (As the lights in the study begin to dim, the priest continues.) Priest: “Let me just say that you will not believe what you are about to see. You will not believe that human beings could have sunk so low. That they can actually take pleasure in performing acts like this to another of God’s creatures. I seriously hope that you have a strong stomach senor, this is pretty disgusting.” (Russo responds with a head shake.) Russo: “Roll the ugliness.” (A bright light projects a picture onto a lowered movie screen as the footage begins to play. The scene opens in a cheap Mexican bar. We finally see the evil that the priest was talking about. We finally see the . . . goat humping! Russo is shocked by this display. Strangely, the man engaged in the sexual activity with the goat looks suspiciously like Jesse Jamester, but rest assured, it cannot possibly be the real Jamester . . . or can it? Either way, Russo cannot take much more of the disgusting footage. He begins to speak.) Russo: “Good Lord! I’ve heard about this . . . goat humping!” (Russo turns to Jeeves.) Russo: “Stop! Stop! Stop it! Stop it! Stop it!” (Russo turns back to the priest as the footage cuts off.) Russo: “Good Father, could there really be a god that would let this kind of thing happen? How much do you want?” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
