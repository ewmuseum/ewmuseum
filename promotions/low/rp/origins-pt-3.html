Origins pt. 3

4 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::We open to a view of a dry desert. There is a cactus that can be seen off in the distance, and some vultures circling around some sort of dead animal. Other than that, the landscape is completely void of anything and everything. Suddenly, two beams of light are seen soaring through the sky, one gold one and one silver one. The two beams curve and land on the ground, producing a thick smoke when they do. After the smoke clears up, our new heroes, the Metallo Zords, are seen standing where the light left them. The Metallo Zords, who are now clad in vests and helmets of the appropriate colors, survey their surroundings and exchange glances with each other. They begin to talk.:: Gold Ranger – My goodness, where are we? ::The Gold Ranger’s voice comes out as a robotic sounding, computer altered, monotone surprise.:: Gold Ranger – And why do I sound like this? ::The Silver Ranger begins to respond.:: Silver Ranger – It looks as though we are in a desert. And I must say, your voice does sound awfully humorous. However . . . ::The Silver Ranger trails off and stops talking when he realizes he sounds exactly the same as the Gold Ranger. The Silver Ranger turns to the Gold Ranger now and talks, bobbing his head as he does.:: Silver Ranger – I suppose we have these voice changers in our costumes to help conceal our identity. Gosh, with these helmets and the funny voices, no one will ever realize we were once . . . ::The Gold Ranger stops the Silver Ranger from finishing.:: Gold Ranger – Shh...Someone might here you! ::The Silver Ranger doesn’t reveal that the heroes used to be circus performers, nodding in agreement with the Gold Ranger. We now see the rest of the land around them, which is completely empty. However, it is not worth risking their identities, as SOMEONE might here them.:: Gold Ranger – I’d be willing to wager that we were put out in this desert to test out our new powers and see how they work. Silver Ranger – By George, my golden comrade, I think you have got it! ::The Gold Ranger glances down at his costume, spotting a watch on his left wrist.:: Gold Ranger – I wonder what these nifty watches might do. I bet they are super-charged with items like compasses and things of that nature. Silver Ranger – That would be especially fitting for a situation like this. Compasses rule. ::The Gold Ranger begins tinkering with his watch, trying to get it to work. He fiddles around with it for a minute, then he lifts it closer to his face for a better look. He shakes it around, trying to hear a tick or something, but much to his chagrin, the watch does not seem to be working. He starts toying around with it again, and finally hits a button on the underside of the watch. When he does this, the voice of Bobbalu speaks out.:: Gold Ranger – Holy windable whining wrist watches, Silver Ranger, I think my compass watch is talking to me! Silver Ranger – Golly gosh, Gold Ranger, it looks like you are right! ::The Silver Ranger moves closer to the Gold Ranger now, wanting to see the talking wrist watch first hand. As the two Metallo Zords clamor over the watch, it speaks up again.:: Bobbalu – Metallo Zords, this is not a regular wrist watch. It is a com link. Whenever you need anything, you can simply hit the button that you just pressed and I will be readily available to speak to you. I am able to zap anything you need directly too you as well. You are correct in guessing that you are in the desert to explore you’re powers. Please, do not hesitate to contact me if I can do anything for you. ::The Metallo Zords look at each other and then simultaneously speak into the com link.:: Metallo Zords – Does the com link have a compass in it? Bobbalu – No, the com link does not have a compass. However, your helmets do have belt in navigation tools. You should not need a compass. ::The Gold Ranger lowers his wrist from his face and points at the wrist of the Silver Ranger:: Gold Ranger – Wow, Silver Ranger, you have a com link too! Silver Ranger – Crikies, I do! Gold Ranger – I wonder what other helpful gadgets we have. ::As the Gold Ranger finishes this statement, a muffled hum is heard. The hum gradually becomes more obvious, and it’s tone begins to change. The hum turns into a multi-tone song, and the song is sung by what sounds like a human voice.:: Silver Ranger – From where could that heavenly song be emitted? Gold Ranger – It sounds like it is getting closer. ::The song continues, then suddenly the Gold Ranger looks down and realizes where it is coming from.:: Gold Ranger – I think I know where the song is originating, Silver Ranger. Silver Ranger – And where would that be, Gold Ranger? Gold Ranger – It sounds like it’s coming from my ribs. ::The Silver Ranger throws his head back and laughs.:: Silver Ranger – Don’t be so silly, you gullible golden goof, ribs cannot sing! ::The Gold Ranger looks to his side and sees a holster on his waste. From it he draws a wooden staff. The staff seems longer than the holster, leaving one to wonder how it fit in to begin with. The Metallo Zords look at the staff in wonderment as it begins to sing to them.:: Staff – My name is Sonya the Singing Staff, it is my job to cut you’re work in half. I am here to help you on you’re quest, and to overthrow Queen Mugambo’s evil nest. It was awfully cramped in that holder on your waste, so if you don’t mind I’d like to remain out in open space. ::The Gold Ranger and the Silver Ranger jump back in shock.:: Silver Ranger – Gold Ranger, from where did you get this staff? ::Before the Gold Ranger can answer, Sonya the Singing Staff answers for him.:: Sonya the Singing Staff – Do not be so quick to question Silver Ranger, for you too have a weapon to help you when in danger. Check in you’re front left karate pants pocket, for you have a weapon greater than any rocket. Straight to you from the factory in the heart of El Paso, I give you you’re weapon Leaping Lena the Lasso. ::The Silver Ranger looks down to his waste now and sticks his hand in his pocket. He pulls out a luminous lasso, and begins swinging it around like a cowboy.:: Leaping Lena the Lasso – Hey, watch how you’re swinging me! ::The Silver Ranger immediately stops swinging the lasso and begins shaking his head, trying to realize what’s going on.:: Silver Ranger – How did we get this word-webbing weaponry, Gold Ranger? Gold Ranger – I guess it is part of the Metallo Zords way. Silver Ranger – Well now that we’ve found them, what should we do? Should we contact Bobbalu and Bobbabu? ::The Gold Ranger raises his com link to his helmet and answers.:: Gold Ranger – I suppose that would be the best thing to do. Bobbalu, oh Bobbalu? ::After a brief moment, Bobbalu answers.:: Bobbalu – What can I do for you? Gold Ranger – What should we do now? Is our training through, Bobbalu? Bobbalu – There is one thing left you still have to get through. ::The Gold and Silver Ranger nod at each other in acceptance of the final task. As they do, the scene zaps away and we are left to wait until next time to see the fate of our heroes.::
