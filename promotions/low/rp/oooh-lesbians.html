Oooh Lesbians

captured 16 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The logo of the Shadow Riders appears on the screen accompanied by a voice-over which says... “Life may end but legends never die.”. The picture opens to a shot from within the living room of the Shadow Riders’ South Beach home in Miami, Florida. Sitting on a large plush black leather sofa is none other then “The Vigilante” Luke Justice. Justice is sitting with his eyes trained on the screen of the big screen television in front of him. As he continues to stare unwaveringly at the screen, the front door of the house swings open as “Superstar” Eric Russo storms into the house slamming the door shut behind him. A scowl is etched on Russo’s face indicating that he is not in the best of moods. He continues towards Justice as he begins to speak.) Russo: “Can you believe this crap!?” (Russo plops down on the couch next to Justice who is still captivated by what is on the television.) Russo: “They’re trying to screw us over again.” (Still fixated on the television screen, Justice responds showing he is not even listening to what Russo is saying.) Justice: “Yeah . . . that’s good.” (Russo snaps his head in Justice’s direction as he exclaims.) Russo: “You’re not even listening are you!? Justice: “Oh . . . really? That’s to bad.” Russo: “What the hell are you so interested in anyway?” (Russo glances over to the set and finally notices that Justice is enchanted by a lesbian sex scene taking place on the screen. Seeing this, Russo’s eyes become glued to screen as well as a smile creeps across his face. As the two Shadow Riders sit transfixed by what is taking place on the screen, Jason Kage enters the room, his right hand encased in a cast from the past Realm’s mysterious backstage attack.) Kage: “Hey guys . . . what are you watching?” (Hearing no response from either man, Kage walks behind the sofa and looks at the screen to see for himself. Kage stares at the screen intently as he becomes mesmerized by the lesbians as well. Uneasy over the quietness in the room containing her Shadow Rider brethren Brody Dahmer walks into the room to investigate. A look of concern fills the young woman’s face as she enters the room only to find Russo, Justice, and Kage staring at the television set. Brody shakes her head as she mutters under her breath.) Brody: “Disgusting.” (With that, Brody walks behind the televison set and grabs hold of the plug. With a swift tug, she pulls the plug out of the socket causing the picture on the televison screen to turn off with an electrical click. The other three Riders look up at Brody with a questioning look of concern on their faces as they exclaim collectively.) Collective: “What did you do!?” (Brody simply shakes her head as she responds responds.) Brody: “You guys never get anything done because of that.” Russo: “But they were lesbians!” (Showing her firmness for the situation, Brody responds.) Brody: “We have a match this week. Try and touch the plug and I’ll make sure I’m the only one able to show up.” (Knowing that Brody is not going to reconsider the lesbian sex scene, Russo does his best to focus on the new topic at hand.) Russo: “Fine, we’ll talk about Realm. We’ll talk about the Sakio System and DUI, that’s much better then two naked women grinding their firm, toned bodies against each other. Yeah, I’m so glad we’re gonna talk about that instead.” (Brody walks around the television set and takes a seat on a red chair next to the couch as she responds with a smirk.) Brody: “I know, isn’t it. Just the thought of how I’m going to hurt them . . . the blood that is going to flow . . . the pain that is going to be felt. Now that is a real turn-on. I don’t see why the titles have to be on the line though.” (Kage looks to Brody after hearing her odd comments about what turns her on.) Kage: “O . . k . . . then.” (Kage shakes his head to clear it of the thoughts coursing through it as he begins to respond to the second part of her statement.) Kage: “Really though, did you actually think they would let a Shadow Rider have a week off after putting on a display that damn near stole the entire show? That and the fact that LOW’s two golden boys failed for a second time against a Shadow Rider team. The Shadow Riders are marked now, they want the tag belts off us and they want them off us now.” (Russo quickly jumps into the conversation.) Russo: “I still say it’s not fair we have to defend the tag team titles again. None of the other champions have to defend a damn thing on a near weekly basis. Why are we the ones who always get stuck defending our titles?” (Brody now comes back into conversation.) Brody: “I know, only the Shadow Riders have to defend their belts! I should stab Trigger for that!? As if that weren’t enough, not only do we have to defend the tag team belts this week, but I have to think about whoever wins the Realm contendership match too. Trigger is just asking for me to rip off his head, stretch out his uvula, staple it to a board, and use him like a paddle ball! Why can’t he understand that all he is doing is getting me mad?' (Listening to the conversation going on between the other Riders gets Justice’s blood boiling. Angrily he cuts in.) Justice: “I don’t know what you three are complaining about, at least you three actually have matches! I’m not even fucking booked on the card! Watch, Trigger is gonna start Aftermath back up and try and stick me there!” (Russo looks over his shoulder at Justice.) Russo: “You know, I wouldn’t be surprised if he did start Aftermath back up and try to you book you on it. It would be just like him to try and hide the talent of a Shadow Rider by getting him on a show that nobody ever watched or even cared about. I mean they did drop that show and the title that went with it for a reason. Oh well, we’ll just have to do things the hard way and show them why no matter what they do, they will all still end up as nothing but another falling star that will be left in the dust of the Shadow Riders.” (The rest of the Riders nod in agreement with Russo’s statement as the shot fades back to the logo of the Shadow Riders with the usual “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.” voice-over before the picture goes to black.)
