Untitled roleplay

captured 24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Excuses, Excuses JD: Well it looks like we’re finally ready to get things underway here tonight. CS: I can’t wait to get to the action! It’s gonna be extreme! JD: Looking at the card, yes it will. CS: Wait, what’s that? ::“Anger Management” by Methods of Mayhem begins plating throughout the arena as “Superstar” Eric Russo steps into the entryway. He is wearing a sleeveless Shadow Riders’ shirt tucked into a pair of black jeans. In his hand, Russo carries a microphone. He starts down to ringside paying little attention to the hatred spewing forth from the fans. Russo reaches the ringside area and slides into the ring. He moves to center ring and turns towards the camera side of the ring as his music slowly fades out. Russo raises the microphone to his mouth and begins to speak.:: Russo: Last week in this ring, I was cheated and robbed of my chance to become the LOW World Champion. I was forced into a match with the LOW World Champion even though the office knew full well that I had not yet received medical clearance to wrestle. They knew that there was a good chance that my knee would go out on me during the match, and it did. I did not jump from the top of the cage, my knee gave out. I had the World Title in my grasps and my knee let it slip away. JD: I can’t believe he’s making excuses Casey. Why can’t he just admit that he lost? CS: Because his knee cost him the match. He would have had the World Title right now if it wasn’t for that. JD: You can’t seriously believe that! Everyone knows that knee injury of his was a fake, his knee was fine for that match last week! CS: You’re wrong Jerry. A competitor of Eric Russo’s caliber would never do something like that. Shame on you for even questioning that man’s sincerity. ::The audience boos loudly at Russo’s refusal to take responsibility for the loss. Unaffected, Russo continues.:: Russo: Last week still served a purpose however. It showed everyone that my rants are not just ego. It showed them that they are also reality. I had Tristan Belmont beat and he knows it. Tristan Belmont knows that the next time we step into that ring, he might not be as lucky as he was this time around. That fact scares him, that fact will slowly eat away at him until there is nothing left. When that happens, I will make my move and take what should be mine . . . the LOW World Title. JD: Those are pretty big words from Russo. He’s awful confident isn’t he? CS: Well why shouldn’t he be? I happen to agree with him. I know Belmont and last week will eat away at him. JD: Why!? He beat Russo. What does he have to worry about from this man now? CS: Russo always get’s the last laugh. He has ever since he’s come to LOW. I’ve learned better then to count him out. ::The crowd loudly voices their disapproval of Russo’s deceleration. Keeping his composure, Russo continues.:: Russo: This week however I have another challenge set in my path. A challenge that has a lifetime worth of history behind it. Tonight, nearly one year of hype and build up comes to an end in one epic clash for the ages. The superstar of the LOW, Eric Russo, goes one on one with his mortal enemy, the “Canadian Cowboy” Jesse Jamester. JD: What a match that’s gonna be. I can’t wait to see it. CS: I can’t wait to see the violence that takes place in it. JD: No doubt it will be one of the most vicious battles in LOW history. CS: Which one do you think is gonna draw first blood? ::The audience boos at the sound of Jamester’s name. With a smile, Russo raises the microphone back to his mouth and continues.:: Russo: Yes . . . yes . . . I know a “Canadian Cowboy” is a pretty stupid gimmick to actually have to sit and watch, but hey . . . it’s what the LOW’s got. Let’s not worry about the details though because as if the match weren’t enough, all this action takes place inside of a steel cage. A very good thing considering Jamester has made a career out of running from me. This match however is not going to be what you expect. You people are not going to be getting the normal every day Xtreme style of wrestling that you have become accustomed to here in LOW. No, tonight you will see what this sport is all about. Tonight you will see a display of wrestling. JD: Display of wrestling!? This is LOW, our fans want extreme! CS: What!? He can’t do that, damn Eric Russo! JD: I thought you liked this guy Casey. CS: Me like this “wrestler” get serious. I like action, not headlock take downs. ::The crowd boos at Russo’s comments about his upcoming match. Keeping a professional exterior, he continues.:: Russo: I know that disappoints some of you, but that’s just too bad. You’re gonna have to just get over it. I’m sick and tired of putting myself on the line for a bunch of unappreciative jackasses. Wine and complain as much as you want, but that’s the way it’s going down. JD: Eric Russo is laying down the law here tonight. CS: This guy is starting to make my skin crawl. Is he finished yet? JD: Casey, you know Russo. He could go on forever. CS: Someone should shut this guy up . . . for good. ::A smirk creeps across Russo’s face as the audience explodes with an “asshole” chant. He continues.:: Russo: In conclusion, tonight Jamester, just like the rest becomes nothing but another falling star. Oh and that’s not ego, that’s reality. JD: I have never seen a wrestler with that much confidence about himself. CS: Confidence!? That’s an ego. JD: Well we’ll find out for sure a little later on tonight. CS: I can’t believe I’m saying this, but I hope Jamester kicks his ass. ::Russo gives a cocky wink to the crowd driving them into a frenzy of hatred as he drops the microphone. He exits the ring and starts up the ramp as “Anger Management” by Methods of Mayhem echos throughout the arena. On the way up, he pauses from time to time to taunt different members of the audience who are seething with hate for the superstar. Finally he makes his way backstage as his music fades out.:: Show URL: http://
