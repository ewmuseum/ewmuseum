The Perfect Tree

captured 28 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::Last week you saw the destruction of LOW’s Extreme champion Juvian Ramorez. You saw Scottywood put Juvian through a make shift table made of hockey sticks. You may not have realized this but you all witnessed the future of Lights Out Wrestling. The man that is the frontier of the Next Generation has finally shown that he belongs in chaotic world of LOW. He has taken out one champion and now he has his sights on another. Whether is be John Adams or Cobi Deekins it doesn’t matter, what does matter is the bulls eye will be on the forehead of the man that holds the gold around his waist. You see champions act very cocky, like Juvian Ramorez. He thought that he was gonna take out Scottywood, destroy him by putting him extreme pain. Well guess what happened? It was Scottywood that destroyed Juvian. This week there are many overly cocky people in this Octagon Challenge and just like last week you will see Scottywood put them away like Juvian. This week for Scottywood the opportunity is there and finally he will reach out to grab it and get that shot at the Realm champion at the PPV. For Scottywood Christmas will be coming early and his present will be wrapped in gold.:: ::We open up with Scottywood and Frankie driving inside the Hummer in a snowy and icy New York City. The streets are packed with holiday shoppers, Scottywood and Frankie aren’t out looking for gifts though, today it is all about finding the perfect Christmas tree. Too many it is almost an art, find the right tree, decorate it with ornaments, garland, popcorn and that angel or star on the top. For Scottywood its just fun, looking at the ornaments that have so much history to them, remising about Christmas’s past. This is his favorite time of the year, he loves everything about the holiday from the movies on TV, Christmas lights at night, Christmas songs on the radio and of course Christmas Eve dinner. The Hummer finally pulls up to the small spot on the out skirts of the city that is filled with trees.:: Frankie: Why don’t you just get a fake Christmas tree? I mean there is no mess and plus I partially allergic to trees…..and grass, flowers, cats, dogs and elephants. Scottywood: Elephants…..I don’t even wanna know. Anyhow, there is no way I’m buying one of those ugly fake trees. I mean it just isn’t the same, it is so much better to have those presents under a live tree on Christmas day, the smell, the feel, the look. No fake tree can duplicate those feelings. ::Scottywood parks the Hummer and the two exits the vehicle. Frankie runs like a little child through the trees and starts jumping up and down at a tree that he had been eyeing the minute they pulled the Hummer up.:: Frankie: This one Scotty, this one! ::Scottywood walks up to Frankie who is standing next to a tree which is barely as tall as himself and looks like it is half dead and is skinnier then Frankie.:: Scottywood: You gotta be kidding me, that tree looks like shit. Now here is a real tree. ::Scottywood walks up to a tree that stands at slightly over six feet tall and is full of branches and needles.:: Frankie: I still like this one, it has got spirit. Scottywood: The only thing that tree has is a meeting with a wood chipper. Frankie: Fine, lets get your tree and go. Time to go see Santa Clause like you promised. Scottywood: You serious about this? You’re a grown man and your gonna try and sit on Santa’s lap. They’ll kick you out of the store in a minute. Frankie: But how is Santa gonna know what to bring me? Scottywood: E-mail him it. Its 2004, ya think Santa would be down with the latest technology. Frankie: That would be perfect, then I’ll be able to tell him everything I want. Last year mall security dragged me out of the North Pole only after 15 minutes of talking to Santa. Scottywood: You sat on Santa’s lap for 15 minutes…..Your gonna be spending a lot of money on a therapist when your older. Now lets get this tree on top of the Hummer….let me rephrase that. Let me get this tree on top of the Hummer. ::Scottywood drags the tree over to the man who Scotty hands the money too for the tree. With the help of one of the assistants they lift the tree up and place it on top of the hummer. Scottywood ties the tree down too the roof as Frankie hops into the Hummer and picks up his Game Boy Advance.:: Scottywood: Ya know it be sorta funny if one of these trees flew off while someone was driving. Going down the highway and suddenly you see a tree flying though the sky. I’d probably pass out from laughter if I saw that. Frankie: Well if your driving home and that happens you better not stop. I need a lot of time to type that list up for Santa. Its gonna be at least 25 pages long. Scottywood: You know that Santa isn’t gonna get you that much stuff. I mean he would have to make a whole trip just for you if that was the case. Frankie: No he wouldn’t, he has his magical bag. Haven’t you ever seen The Santa Clause with Time Allen. Scottywood: Yes, but that was a movie, not everything in movies is real. Frankie: Well we will see on Christmas morning when I have piles of presents from Santa. ::Scottywood just shakes his head as he pulls out of the parking lot and Frankie buries his head back into his Game Boy Advanced.::
