Love Part 1 Archive - 28.4.2003

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A quiet, secluded public house in the middle of the countryside. Sat in the corner of the main bar area, an outsider sips his glass of orange juice. He is content with the events of the past week or so. He knows he’s done something great. He defeated five people in one night, quite an achievement by anybody’s standards. Cyrus Skar however, is alone. He has nobody. Apart from himself. But then again, depending on who you are, yourself could be all the company you could ever need. Cyrus looks at his half-full glass of orange and thinks, he thinks about himself. He wonders if he’ll be on his own for the rest of his life, the way it’s going at the moment it certainly looks that way. Cyrus finishes his orange juice and walks up to the bar to get another drink. “You want another one?” Said the bartender in a hurried voice. “Yeah, stick it in there.” Cyrus gives the empty glass to the bartender who in return fills it with orange juice. Cyrus goes to pay the man but a sweet, young voice interrupts. “I’ll get that.” Said a young woman with long blonde hair. “Come and join me for a drink, I recognize your face. I think I’ve seen you on television or something.” Cyrus smiled, he walked over to a woman, a lot smaller than him. She was petit compared to Cyrus. He looked into her deep blue eyes as he sat down on the stool next to her. Her short red skirt revealed a lot of her endless legs. Cyrus was nearly drooling at this gorgeous woman that was sat next to him. He tried to pull himself together. “So, I take it you watch wrestling then?” Cyrus tried to break the ice with a conversation starter. “Wrestling?” She let out a slight giggle. “No, I think it’s stupid to be honest with you. Sorry if I’ve offended you. Do you watch it?” Cyrus was taken aback, how did she know him if she doesn’t like wrestling? Cyrus was now feeling quite sad and depressed, but alas he could not let that show. “I’m a wrestler, I wrestle in a company called LOW.” Cyrus spoke calmly, even though inside he was a whir of emotions waiting to explode in a instant. “Oh.” Said the woman, shocked. “My names Hannah. Nice to meet you.” Cyrus’s emotions now took a sharp turn. He didn’t know what to think. She thought wrestling was stupid, but yet she was befriending a wrestler…Cyrus was confused. He didn’t know what to do, other than the obvious. “I’m Cyrus Skar, so you didn’t see me on wrestling, where did you see me?” Cyrus was very curious now as to where Hannah had seen his face before. “I’m not sure, maybe I was mistaken. Have you been in here before?” Asked Hannah, now revealing where she had really seen Cyrus before. “Yes I have. I used to come in here a lot before I made it into the big time. This used to be my second home. I used to come in here practically every night and get pissed out of my face. Not any more though. It’d kill me.” Cyrus started opening his heart to her, yet he hardly knew her. He couldn’t stop himself, he just felt as though he could tell her anything. “I used to come in here every now and again, I probably saw you in here before. That’s why I thought I knew you.” She smiled as she spoke. “So, I take it you don’t get time to do much other stuff other than train and wrestle?” “I’m afraid not, my life is wrestling. I’m either on the road, training or wrestling, Every so often though I get a chance to relax like now.” Cyrus was feeling more and more comfortable around Hannah. He was starting to feel an emotion he had not felt for a very long time. “I take it you don’t have time for relationships much then?” She asked a direct question, being very up front with Cyrus. Cyrus didn’t mind though. He was falling for her, hook, line and sinker. “I can always make time.” Cyrus started flirting with her, he was not going to let her get away without her giving him her phone number. She was just as flirtatious with him as he was with her. “Is that an offer?” She said giggling like a little schoolgirl. Cyrus was feeling more and more confident in his ability now as he asked her out right if she would like to see him again. “Well, I’ve got to be getting off right now, but I’m free tomorrow night. Do you fancy coming out for a meal with me?” Cyrus crossed his fingers behind his back as he prayed that she would say yes. “I’d love to Cyrus. Here’s my phone number. She got a piece of paper from the bartender and got a pen out of her handbag. She wrote it down for Cyrus and passed him the little scrap of paper. He held it tightly in his hand as he said bye and walked out of the public house. “How rude of me, I’ve not said congratulations yet.” The voice spoke mockingly. “Nice to speak to you to. What do you want?” Cyrus was not happy at being disturbed by the voice as this moment in time. “I’m going to say, well done. You did what I knew you could. You beat five people Cyrus, five people in one night. One after enough. You’re the king of the world, you can do anything now.” The voice was genuinely happy for Cyrus. “I’m really proud of you Cyrus. It’s one thing talking about doing something, but it’s another thing actually doing it. Now you’ve got your Title shot this Saturday on Realm! You even got promoted from Aftermath. Cyrus everything’s starting to fall into place now.” “Everything’s starting to go right.” Said Cyrus with a sly smile creeping onto his face. The voice was worried by this comment and knew where it was going. “Cyrus, you cannot get distracted now. That slut cannot get in the way of you winning the Aftermath Title. This is the opportunity you’ve been waiting for ever since you entered LOW, you can’t let is pass you by now!” The voice was scared of Cyrus rejecting him, he knew that three in a relationship doesn’t work. “Don’t worry, I’m not going to concentrate less, hell, I’m only taking her out for one meal tomorrow. I’m not missing any training and I’m just eating with somebody else rather than on my own.” The voice realized Cyrus was happy and accepted the fact he was in love. “Ok Cyrus, I understand I’m not going to be able to change your mind here. But please promise me you will not let this woman lead you astray, ok?” The voice was not happy, it was obvious. “I promise. Like I said to her, wrestling is my life. I sleep, eat, drink, and breathe wrestling. This match means the world to me. I understand that opportunities like this won’t come every day. Iron Lung, he’s been competing well at the moment. But only against people, who are worthless, he beat somebody who doesn’t even wrestle properly at the PPV. I beat five LOW Superstars. I’m better than Iron Lung, all I’ve got to do is perform like always and I’ll be fine.” “Iron Lung will be a tough fight Cyrus, but as always I know that you can handle him. Give him respect, but not too much. Do not over respect him so much as to be worried about beating him. However, do not underestimate him as to let him pull out a surprise victory. I know you have good judgement Cyrus, I’m just reminding you of things you should always be aware of.” The voice was in a serious mood, all work, and no play. “I know, thank you for being there for me as well. It’s strange how we’ve become so close, almost ironic. You’re supposed to be destroying me inside, ripping me apart, yet you’re almost like a best friend to me. Hell, I don’t know what I’d do now if I lost you.” Cyrus nearly wept at the sheer thought of him losing his other personality. “It’s ok, this may sound soppy, but I feel the same way too. It’s nice to be able to get along with you Cyrus. I’m sure that we’ll be just fine. We’ve always got through whatever challenges have been in our way, together, and we’re not going to stop now.” The voice sounded relieved and also overcome with emotion just like Cyrus. “Freak!” Called a man who walked past and overheard Cyrus talking to himself. He then hurled a rock at Cyrus, that missed but Cyrus still felt the hatred that came from the man. “Why do people insist on being so narrow minded and hateful?” Cyrus was now pissed off, as was evident in his voice. “I don’t know Cyrus, but Iron Lung’s exactly the same.” The voice replied cunningly, using Cyrus’s aggression and turning it against his up-coming opponent. “Iron Lung says he hates ‘freaks’ like you. He said that people like you Cyrus, should be shot. In cold blood. What kind of a man wishes that upon anybody?” The voice was really getting to Cyrus now. “I’m going to murder him. He’s going to wish this was a normal match, not a Warrior Match. I’m going to make him feel so much pain he’ll go unconscious.” Cyrus’s face had started to go a shade of red as he got pumped up full of rage. “Remember how you’re feeling now Cyrus. Wait till Saturday. Let this hatred that you feel now breed inside of you, getting stronger and stronger. Use it to show no remorse against your opponent. You will annihilate him, you will win!” The voice had done its job yet again. It had manipulated Cyrus for it’s own purposes. The voice returned to the depths of Cyrus’s soul leaving him to think over what it had said. Cyrus was ready for his match. He knew that this was his time. After a long time waiting, it had finally come…
