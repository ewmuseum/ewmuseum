Not part of the Hate Series Archive - 5.6.2003

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cyrus's career was really starting to take shape now. A Realm Title shot was up for grabs in his next match against Canyon Thunders. The one man in LOW that he really did despise. The one man in LOW who he could honestly say he hated. The one man in LOW that he wanted to tear limb from limb, the one man in LOW who would feel his wrath. What made it even more appetizing for Cyrus was the fact that after the match that he wanted, he would be able to face the Realm Champion, Jason Ortiz, a man who he respected, but also a man with a target on his chest. That Realm Championship he holds dearly may be taken away from him more quickly than he would like. Nothing he can do change the future, it has already been decided. The day Cyrus Skar got booked in his match for the PPV was the day that Ortiz's reign as champion was put in danger. Jason Ortiz may be one of the best wrestlers in LOW, he may be the current Realm Champion. But to Cyrus, he's just a man and any man can be beaten. Cyrus has the desire to win when he fights, he doesn't just want to win his matches, he needs to win his matches. Cyrus is fuelled with rage most of the time, but motivation can come in all different shapes and sizes for Cyrus. He doesn't always win through beating his opponent unconscious, he has the brains to know when to use tactics to achieve his goal. He is far more intelligent than what people give him credit for. Though people now do give him credit, which is strange because in the past he was overlooked as 'Just another wrestler'. Was it because he's been promoted in LOW more than in his other federations? Not at all. Is it because he's accomplished more than he has before? Not at all. The reason? The fact that people keep their eye on LOW, people take notice when stars are being born. Though the fans may not know it, Cyrus was born a long time ago. To them he's a new superstar rising to the top, for the people that really know him, he's been held back a long time. He's a veteran in the business. It's about time that he gets the shots that he deserves. He's slaved for years in poor conditions for poor salaries. Why? Desire. The desire to one day make it as a big time superstar. The knowledge that one-day, somebody would recognize him for the talent that he has. That day came, the day he was scouted for LOW was the happiest day of his life. As everybody knows wrestling is Cyrus's life. Nothing else matters to him more in the world. Even love. Cyrus has had his fair share of relationships. But every time they've seemed to end in disaster. Why? Was it because of Cyrus's destructive nature? No…Was it because of the fact he's a wrestler? No…The reason? With some things there aren't any reasons. Who knows why Cyrus has been unlucky in love? It might just be that he's still not met his soul mate. But then again, he thought Hannah was his soul mate. The question now that needs to be answered is, will he ever see Hannah again? Will he get to place his lips upon hers and passionately kiss her ever again? Or was the last time they kissed, the last time? So many questions that need answering and so little time… Then again time is the answer to all of these questions. Time, they say is a healer. But will it heal Cyrus's wounds? In time will Cyrus be mentally healed? Will his other personality continue to bother him for the rest of his life? In time we will tell. But for now, we wait. What do we wait for? Who knows…all we do know is, what Cyrus is waiting for. One little phone call, so harmless normally. Yet this one could blow his world apart. This one could have catastrophic repercussions, this phone call could do so many things, yet until it happens we know not what they will be. All we do know is that Cyrus will be ready. But ready for what? A successful career as a professional wrestler? Or a lifetime in jail? Either one is a possibility at the moment. But Cyrus has the power to choose for himself. Cyrus can decide his fate. If he pursues this framing incident he may find out who was behind it…but then again…does he want to know who was behind it? It could have been anyone…somebody with a grudge against him. But there were lots of people with a grudge against Cyrus. The suspects were numerous… Canyon Thunders? Statuz Quo? Chris Wrestling? Iron Lung? Anybody could have done it. But then again it might not have anything to do with wrestling. It could have been something to do with Cyrus's past. Somebody he beat up when he was younger, somebody who was jealous of how well he'd done for himself. The list of people it could be went on and on. So we look to the future. We try to predict what it holds. It's amazing how everybody's predicting Cyrus to beat Canyon Thunders. The match hasn't even taken place yet but people are so sure of Cyrus winning. Why? Because of his past. People forget that we predict that future from the past. People say the past is not important, when really, the past is more important than the present. If we forget the past how do we know what to expect in the future? The answer is we don't… But maybe we shouldn't be predicting the future, predictions can always be wrong. Yet when they’re right they normally cause nothing but trouble. A person bragging about how they were right and somebody else was wrong. Then we look further than meets the eye. Bragging is just a human characteristic. As long as we're human we’re going to brag no matter what we do. Though still maybe we should just live life as it comes without trying to foresee the future, we might foresee something that we may not like…
