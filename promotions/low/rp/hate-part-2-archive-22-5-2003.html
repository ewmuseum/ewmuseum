Hate Part 2 Archive - 22.5.2003

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The old, decrepit house barely stood. The wood was all rotten, with wood worm crawling in and out of little holes. Rats ran from place to place in their abode, making the faintest of noises as they went. An owl nestled in the roof of the house and looked down upon the microcosm of death. Cyrus pushed the rotten door out of the way and entered the house. He looked down and his eyes widened. There lay a body. Cyrus now realized why death was oozing out from the house. He knew who it was, yet he did not wish to touch it to verify his thoughts. It was definitely a man, a young Caucasian male of about twenty-five years old. Cyrus carried on walking though, he tried to ignore the stench of death as he delved further into the house. 'I told you to come here, I told you…' The voice was still playing a game with Cyrus, it loved being in control of him, manipulating him and being omnipotent. 'Why though? All that is here is him…' Cyrus spoke sounded perplexed, he was unsure what he was looking for. 'What I am supposed to be looking for?' 'Search him Cyrus.' The voice shocked Cyrus but then explained. 'Look in his pockets.' Cyrus walked over to the man's jacket and put his hand in, he pulled out some documents. They were plane tickets to Spain. There were two, one in his name and one in Hannah's name. 'So they were planning on fleeing to Spain?' Cyrus still did not know the answer to any of his questions, why was that important? Why was he letting the voice control him? He had to…the voice knew something. He had to let him think that he was in control, otherwise he could risk getting put in jail. Cyrus knew he had no other options left. He didn't like it but he'd have to do whatever the voice told him to do. He was completely helpless. 'So then, doesn't that tell you anything? Or are you completely brain dead?' The voice waited for a few seconds but Cyrus had no answer for him. He was trying to put the wrong piece into the wrong puzzle. 'You still don't understand do you? Cyrus I give you far too much credit, you really can be stupid…They were in a hurry to go to Spain. They were running Cyrus. The answering machine message, then they both die. It all ties in Cyrus. They knew they were going to be killed, so they thought they could run.' Cyrus took a few moments to take all of this in. He was starting to put it altogether now, but yet he still couldn't figure out with the voice knew all of this. It couldn't possibly have worked it out just from the clues Cyrus had, it must have known something else. 'What else do you know?' Cyrus was interrogating the voice, he was getting tired of playing the game. 'Tell me now.' 'Don't be so nasty Cyrus, or I might just decide to leave you on your own. I'm sure you wouldn't want that now would you?' The voice still realized it had the ball in its court. It still had power over Cyrus, even more so now that Cyrus knew the voice knew something he needed to know. 'How about we make a little deal Cyrus. I'll give you some information if you do exactly as I say. That ok with you big guy?' 'Fine by me, now tell me what you know.' Cyrus was getting angry with the voice now but he tried not to lose his temper, he did not want the voice to feel his emotions. He pushed them away and asked calmly. 'So, tell what you know.' 'I couldn't do that Cyrus, it'd spoil the fun we're having. I'm getting to spend more time with you. I don't want to stop now. I'll give you another clue. Get his mobile out of his pocket.' Cyrus bent down and reached cautiously into the dead man's pocket. He pulled out his mobile phone and then awaited his next instructions. 'Now, see who rung him last.' The voice knew exactly what it was doing. It was enjoying having so much control over every little thing Cyrus was doing. 'Right now call the number.' Cyrus called it. 'Ring, ring' It rang once. 'Ring, ring' It rang twice. 'Ring, ring' It rang three times. 'Nobodies going to answer it damn it!' Cyrus shouted with rage. 'Hold on, give it a while. They might be busy doing something. I'm sure somebody will answer soon Cyrus.' The voice laughed, revealing it knew a lot more than what Cyrus thought it did. 'Ring, ring…Hello?' An old man answered the phone in a croaky voice. 'Hello, I'm…' Cyrus quickly looked at the plane tickets. 'David Barnes, brother of Andrew Barnes. I believe you rang my brother just before he died…' 'Ah Mr. Barnes. I was hoping I could get in contact with one of his relatives rather soon. I work as a travel agent. I rang him to tell him his tickets were ready for collection. He left something here though, I tried to ring him but his mobile was switched off…' 'Yeah, he's a bit busy at the moment. He told me to ring you to check everything was ok.' Cyrus pulled off a good impression. The travel agent believed every word Cyrus said. 'Is it ok if I come round and pick up what he left? May I also ask what was it?' 'It was an envelope addressed to Mr. Barnes. It must be something to do with the holiday or something. If you come down before five-o clock tonight you can have it. I'm closing up then.' 'Ok I'll be round in a few minutes, which travel agency is it?' Cyrus nearly lost his cover. But then spoke so convincingly the travel agent didn't even think to ask any questions. 'On Short Street.' Replied the croaky old man. 'I'll see you soon bye.' 'Bye.' Replied Cyrus solemnly, then he put the phone down. 'See Cyrus, follow me and you get to find things out. You need me Cyrus, without me you're not going to clear your name, without me, you're going to get put in jail.' The voice was having a power surge, it was being cocky and annoying towards Cyrus. 'Whatever you say…' Cyrus did not snap though, he still needed his help, even if it was only for a little while he still needed it. 'We need to get a move in if we want to get to the travel agency before it shuts. I want to find out what was in that envelope. It could be something important.' 'Cyrus, I know it will be something important.' The voice laughed. 'We'd better get going then hadn't we? Wouldn't want to be late now…' Cyrus walked out of the house leaving the dead body where it was, he got into his silver BMW Z3 and then put his foot through the floor. The car's tires screeched as smoke came up from the back, the smell of burning rubber filled the air as Cyrus drove off towards Short Street. He went straight ahead through two sets of traffic lights and kept on going. He traveled for about five minutes before turning right and pulling up right outside the little travel agency. The old man was stood outside just locking up as Cyrus got there. 'Hey, I'm David Barnes, I've come to pick that letter up for Andrew Barnes…' Cyrus did not say any more and the small, old, white haired man passed him the envelope. Cyrus did not say thanks or goodbye. He just jumped in his car and started to drive home. 'Aren't you going to open it?' Asked the voice wondering how Cyrus was containing his curiosity. 'Don't you want to know what's inside it.' 'I'll open it when I get home. I've not been away from home this long before. People might be wondering where I am.' Cyrus was genuinely worried. 'Nobody would worry about you Cyrus, I mean come on…' The voice was trying to make Cyrus take a detour. 'We're about a three hour drive away from your house now. Let's stop at a motel and we'll get home tomorrow.' 'Ok, I'll pull into this one here.' Cyrus pulled into a big fancy hotel with a huge driveway and a large car park in front of the main building. He parked his sports car and walked up some marble steps into the posh reception area. 'I think I'm going to get a good night's sleep tonight…' ---Bleep--- 'Cyrus where are you? I've been worried sick. You've not been home for a while now. This isn't like you. Please call me back as soon as you get in, thanks.' 'You have no more space for new messages…' ---Bleep--- 'Message deleted.' ---Bleep---
