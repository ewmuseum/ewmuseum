I think his name was James Dodge. “Can’t...breathe...I swallowed too much gas,” was my reply, then I passed out. My face was turning a dangerous shade of blue. “Someone get a doctor in here!” I heard a voice call out. “This man is dying!” Uriko Kreed sat on her couch, watching Realm 50 on her big-screen television. She smiled as she saw her husband defeat Emilio Dervante on his debut match. About five minutes after the match, the phone beside her rang. She looked over at it, picked it up and answered it on the second ring. “Hello?” she said. “Yes, this is Uriko Kreed, what can I do...” She listened as Chryste filled Uriko in on the news that she recently heard. Uriko’s eyes widen and brimming with tears. “Please tell me he’s all right, Chryste, please!” Uriko continues to listen to Chryste. Uriko covers her mouth as tears stream down her face. Her husband is in the intensive care unit of a hospital and his health doesn’t look too promising. “I’ll hop a flight over there and I hope I can get there as soon as I can.” Uriko hung up the phone and ran upstairs to her bedroom to pack a suitcase. About three five hours later, Uriko made it to the hospital where her husband is being held. She is sitting out in the corridor. On the other side of the corridor to her right, there are double doors that have the sign that says ICU on it with big, black letters. She looks over at the double doors almost half expecting to see Simon walk out of the room as if there was nothing wrong with him, and that there had been some sort of mistake. That never happened. No one walked out from those double doors. She looks down at the floor, her head hung low. Tears are dripping from her eyes and hitting the floor. What if there’s something wrong with him? What if he doesn’t survive and dies? He has died twice in his life; dying a third time and the chance of him coming back is slim and none. “Mrs. Kreed?” Uriko looks up at a tall, slender man, wearing a white lab coat, holding a clipboard in his hands, looking down at her. She stands up to greet him. “I’m Mrs. Kreed.” The man extends his hand. “My name is Doctor Ambers, and I have some good news and bad news about your husband.” Uriko’s eyes got as big as saucers. She completely ignores the man’s extended hand. “Bad news? What is wrong with my husband? What has happened to him?!” “Please, ma’am, keep your voice down,” Ambers said. “The bad news is that his health doesn’t look too promising at the moment. After consuming so much gasoline in the match he had, there is some internal damage, but nothing too severe. All he’ll need is some antibiotics and he’ll be good as new.” After hearing the end of that sentence, Uriko felt herself regaining some of her composure. It sounds like he won’t die after all. “What’s the good news?” she asked. “The good news is that he’ll have a good chance of coming around. He should be out of the hospital in a few days, perhaps a week at most. But I assure you, his condition shouldn’t worsen.” Uriko let out a sigh of relief. But it is rather depressing that she has to wait about a week for Simon to get out of the hospital. “May I see him?” “Yes, you may, but you need to keep it down, there are others sleeping in the room as well. I’ll take you to him.” Doctor Ambers escorts Uriko down to the intensive care unit room. She can’t wait to see her darling husband... Until she got into the room. About a month has passed after I had my first match on Realm, and I still feel terrible. All I have been doing and pissing and puking up blood almost on a regular basis. Man, this is worse than being sick, I can tell you that much. The only thing that is keeping my sanity is this beautiful view.
