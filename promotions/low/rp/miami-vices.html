Miami Vices

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen once again fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over message which states... 'The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to a shot of the South Beach strip in the mid day sun of Miami, Florida. The shot pans from left to right as if searching for something. All of a sudden, a cherry red 73' Pontiac Firebird comes speeding down the street. The car screeches to a halt in front of the camera as the passenger door swings open. A voice comes booming from inside the car.) Voice: “You getting in the car or not!? I don’t have all day to be waiting around for your stupid ass!” (The cameraman quickly gets in the car shutting the car door behind him as we see the driver of the car for the first time. Sitting over in the drivers seat of the car is none other then the current LOW Realm champion himself, “Superstar” Eric Russo. Russo is wearing a sleeveless Shadow Riders t-shirt tucked into a pair of faded blue jeans. A pair of black, Ray-ban Aviators sit on his face blocking the glare from the bright sun outside. Russo sits looking out the window at a homeless man sitting across the street by the beach as he begins talking.) Russo: “Look at this bum across the street. He’s worthless . . . living his life off of whatever he can get his grubby little hands on. You know, that kind of reminds me of my good old pal Daveylicious. I mean just look at Davey, he shares a lot of similarities with that bum over there. For one, Davey likes to sustain himself off of the work of others. Hell, he’s been trying to do it with me since he’s come to the LOW. Since his arrival in the federation, Daveylicious has done nothing but come along for a ride on my coat tails. Just look at my past with him . . . he’s obsessed with me.” (Russo now turns to look into the lens of the camera as he continues.) Russo: “You know . . . while I’m thinking about it and I have a camera here, I really have to tell you that that’s been freaking me out a little too. I mean I feel like I’m stuck in the movie Single White Female. Every time I turn around, there Davey is trying to add another part of me into his persona. First he wants my Realm title, then he tries to leach off my charisma, after that he rips off my promos . . . what’s next for him? Is he gonna start calling himself “Superstar” Eric Licious? Is he gonna join a team called the Shadowlicious Riders? Seriously I can’t wait to see what precious gem of an idea he comes up with next. What will end up in his next promo? Despite his feelings of my promos, I will admit that I rather enjoy Davey’s new ones. I mean why shouldn’t I? They are nothing more then my material being preformed by Davey. At least it’s better then that old crap he used to try and pass off as entertainment. Before he started ripping me off it used to be like . . . “Yay, what’s next Davey? Are we gonna hear some more from little Norman Bates? No, what about Uncle Bob?”. What the hell was that stuff anyway? Oh well, it’s not like anyone was watching them anyway. Seriously, why is so hard for him to accept the fact that people don’t like or even care about him? Is it really that hard for him to see that he’s not thought of as entertaining? Just look at his history over in LOW. Whenever he steps into the ring with me he ends up with an upper-card bout for the ages. Whenever he dosen’t, he’s lucky to even break mid-card while I on the other hand continue to put on match of the year candidates in the main events week after week. Big Daveylicious is nothing more then a cheap knock off of me, a true talent capable of selling himself by beating the best, not just getting high profile matches against them. To each his own though . . . it’s not like the opinion of a jobber, that just so happened to pin Davey’s shoulders to the mat, means anything to an ego of that size now does it . . .” (Russo turns his attention to the road in front of him as he presses his foot down on the gas pedal. The car takes off like a bat out of hell as the car speeds off down the street. Russo does not speak as he drives the car, paying more attention to the road instead. After a few blocks, Russo makes a sharp right turn onto a small side street violently shaking the camera as the cameraman slides hard against the door. The car speeds down the street as it gets further and further from the beautiful bikini clad women walking from the beach and deeper into the rundown section of the city. Russo continues driving for a few more minutes before pulling into the parking lot of a small dilapidated bar. Russo parks the car and opens the door as he speaks to the cameraman.) Russo: “Come on, I’m supposed to meet a friend of mine here in a little bit. Let’s get a beer while we wait.” (Russo and the camera man exit the car and walk into the small bar. As the walk to the back of the bar, a few of the patrons reach out their hands to shake Russo’s. As if he does not even notice them, Russo passes by them cockily avoiding their touch. The two men take a seat at a small table towards the rear of the bar. A waitress who looks to be in her late forties comes over to the table. She is wearing a low cut, blue t-shirt which shows off her sagging cleavage as well as a pair of super short cut-off black jeans. She begins speaking.) Waitress: “Hello honey, what can I get you.” Russo: “I’ll have a rum and coke on the rocks and he’ll have a...” Cameraman: “Nothing thank you, I don’t drink.” Russo: “He’ll take a straight up shot of 151.” Waitress: “I’ll be right back with you order.” (As the waitress leaves the table, the cameraman begins to question Russo.) Cameraman: “What’s 151? I never heard of that before.” Russo: “Don’t worry about it, it’s good for you.” (The waitress returns to the table with the two men’s order. She sets both glasses down on the table then walks away. Russo takes a large sip of his drink as he motions for the cameraman to drink his. The cameraman raises the glass to his mouth and quickly takes the shot. The camera begins to shake as we hear the cameraman begin to cough violently. Russo lets out a little chuckle as he watches the cameraman. He allows the cameraman to compose himself before he looks into the camera and begins speaking once again.) Russo: “Did you see that waitress? I hate people like that. You know, the ones who don’t know their true role in life. The ones who think that their something their not. I have a solution for that dilemma though. One that Daveylicious got to experience first hand. What is that solution you ask . . . well I’ll tell you. That solution is labeling. I did Daveylicious a great favor when I labeled him, but did I get any thanks for showing him where he belonged? No, I had my kind gesture thrown back in my face with less respect then the money that the John throws in Davey’s mom’s. I mean so what! Davey got labeled . . . big freaking deal. He deserved to be labeled. Would he have been happier if I just labeled him a jackass instead of WO. It’s not like there’s much of a difference anyway. I was just showing Davey where he belonged. Big Daveylicious does not belong in the PTC. The PTC does not have room for rip offs of true talent nor do I have time to deal with them. That is why I gave Big D his out. I gave him a chance to get while the getting was still good. He decided not to take it though . . . that was a big mistake. Now, I cannot be held responsible for my actions. Now, I will hold nothing back when I step into the ring with Daveylicious like I did last time. This time, I’m pulling out all the stops when I step into that lightning dome. You see, the lightning dome is a match like no other. An electrified steel cage can cause some serious harm to a human body. Not to mention that it is one of the only match types that I have never taken part in. That doesn’t bother me though. After all, I don’t say that I can do it all for nothing. I do have to admit that there are a few questions weighing on my mind though. It’s a question that only Daveylicious can answer for me. What is that question you ask. Well it’s quite simple, I just want to know how Davey likes it . . . Original Recipe or Extra Crispy. Why, because just like the cowardly chicken that he is . . . at Shell Shock, Daveylicious is gonna get fried.” (Russo lifts his drink to his mouth and finishes it with one gulp. As he waves the waitress over to order another drink, a drunk man stumbles over to the table. The man looks at Russo in a drunken stare as he begins to talk with a sight slur.) Drunk: “Yer Erick Russso... I’m yer biggesst fan...” Russo: “Thanks, now get out of my face. That stench coming off you is making me sick you frigging drunk.” (Looking dejected, the man stumbles off as the waitress arrives at the table once again. Russo orders another drink. As the waitress goes to get the drink, Russo begins talking to the camera once again.) Russo: “I hate the drunks in these run down bars, they are always trying to tell you that their your biggest fan when you know they probably don’t know a damn thing about you except that your famous...” (The waitress returns to the table and places Russo’s drink on the table in front of him. Russo lifts up the glass and takes a sip of the tequila. He then returns the glass to it’s position on the table before continuing.) Russo: “As I was just saying, they really don’t know the first thing about you. It’s kind of like Daveylicious when he made his debut in the LOW. You see, when Daveylicious made his less then impressive debut in the federation he knew nothing about what he was getting himself into. He thought by stealing my title he was going to make a quick and easy name for himself. It didn’t work out that way though now did it? All Davey did by stealing my title was successfully draw my attention directly to him. Now one thing that anyone who has been around in the LOW locker rooms for awhile will tell you is that the last thing you want is the attention of a Shadow Rider. We have made a name by destroying careers. Daveylicious won’t be any different no matter what he tries to say. Hell, I’ve already heard excuse after excuse from everyone else about why I wouldn’t be able to beat them. Did that change the outcome of their matches? Did their excuses ever affect my unblemished singles record? I think not. All it did was make them look like an even bigger jackass when I shoved their words right back down their throats. With as much as Davey likes to talk, that’s gonna be a pretty big job. It’s one I will enjoy every minute of though. After all, there’s nothing I like more then defeating oversized egos and bringing them right back down to earth. I can’t wait to see the look on his face when I shatter his perceptions of reality. When I bring his entire world crumbling down around him. What’s he gonna do then? What’s he gonna do when he realizes that he just like the rest is nothing but another . . . hold on, can’t finish this one off just yet. I still have a few more things that I wanna talk about.” (A loud clamor is heard off camera. The camera swings around as we see the bartender dragging one of the many drunks to the front door. The bartender kicks the front door open and throws the drunk man out into the parking lot. A large grin slides across the bartenders face as he admires his handy work. He then yells out to the drunk who is trying to drag himself to his feet.) Bartender: “Come back when you have some money you fu(k!ng bum!” (The bartender pulls the door shut as he begins to walk back behind the bar. He notices that one of the customers is staring at him. The bartender lunges foreword and motions as if he is going for a punch to scare the staring customer. The customer falls of his stool as he jumps back in fear of being hit. The bartender lets out a hearty laugh as walks behind the bar. The camera comes back to a shot of Russo as he begins speaking once again.) Russo: “There is nothing more pathetic then a bully. Unlike a real man, a bully is rarely ever able to back up what he says. Daveylicious acts in much the same way with his actions. Just look at him, he’s supposedly some kind of huge star according to himself. What has he done in LOW to back this up? Oh yeah, that’s right . . . jackshit. Big Daveylicious has done nothing but prove to me time and time again that he is all talk when it comes to preforming in that ring. I on the other hand have always been able to back up everything I said. When I came into the LOW I told everyone who would listen that I was one half of the greatest tag team on the face of the earth. Then I went out and proved it. Next I said that not only was I one half of the greatest tag team but I could also out wrestle almost anyone on the roster in singles as well. I went on to back that one up too. Are you starting to see a trend yet? Are you starting to get the idea why I’m so high on myself? As the saying goes, it ain’t cocky motherfucker if you back it up. That’s exactly what I plan on doing when I get into the ring with Davey. I plan on backing up every single threat that I have made to him. Why, because I can no matter how much Nathan Jones milk he gulps down. Once I do that, Davey will finally realize that he just like the rest is nothing but another falling star that has a teeny tiny penis.” (As Russo finishes the cameraman begins to question the whereabouts of Russo’s friend.) Cameraman: “I thought you were supposed to meet up with your friend here.” Russo: “Why are you so antsy, you got something more important to do? He will get here when he gets here. Until then, you just keep that camera on me cause I still have a few things that I wanna talk about.” Cameraman: “You got it.” (Just then, Russo stands up as Mike Roberts walks in the front door of the small bar. Russo and the cameraman walk over to meet Mike. Once they are there, Mike begins speaking.) Mike: “Hey Eric, good to see you man. You ready to show me the new house yet or do you still have stuff to take care of here?” Russo: “No, I’m ready. It’s not like this stuff is really that important anyway.” Mike: “Well then, let’s go.” Russo: “Ok, just give me one second.” (Russo turns to the cameraman.) Russo: “Hey . . .you got any cash on you?” Cameraman: “No, why?” Russo: “Well It’s gonna be kinda hard getting out of here with no money to pay the tab or get a cab. Oh well... sucks for you.” Cameraman: “Wait! You can’t just leave me here like this!” Russo: “You know what, you’re right. Give me the tape first.” Cameraman: “What.. why?” Russo: “I don’t wanna loose this tape if you don’t make it back. So, give it to me now or else I’m gonna have to take it from you. Now I know you don’t want that . . . do you?” (The picture begins to shake as the cameraman reaches up in fear to press the eject button. Suddenly, the screen goes to the Shadow Riders logo with the voice over that says... “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The screen now fades to black.)
