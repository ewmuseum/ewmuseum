History 101- Dark Days Ahead

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. The scene opens to a shot from inside the large empty apartment where Russo sits across the table from Jerry. Jerry holds the microphone of his recorder directed at Russo as he continues with his story.) Russo: “The Storms were not bargaining on us still not backing down after their devastating blow. We may have no longer had control, but we knew that we could still out fight them.” Jerry: “You just never give up do you?” (Russo replies with a slight smirk.) Russo: “Once someone forces me to take personal offence to certain actions, no . . . I don’t ever let up. Believe me, I am the last person that you want a problem with. The reason for that being that once something is started, I always make sure that I’m the one to finish it. After all, you don’t get the glory when you don’t finish the job.” (Questioningly, Jerry looks to Russo.) Jerry: “What do you mean when you say . . . “finish the job.”?” Russo: “What I mean by that, is that once I start there is no escape other then retirement. No running, no hiding . . . just a living hell lasting the rest of your days. You’ll get a better idea of what I mean a little later on.” (Russo pauses for a moment to adjust himself in his seat. He then begins speaking once again.) Russo: “As for now, what do you say to getting back to the story?” (Jerry’s eyes light up and a grin come to his face at the thought of more stories. He excitedly responds.) Jerry: “Hey . . . it works for me.” (Russo clears his throat and then resumes the tale.) Russo: “Week after week, the Storms took their frustrations out on us by booking each of us in handicapped matches with insurmountable odds. In laymen’s terms, we were getting the living $h!t kicked out of us. Soon though the Storms were going to get something .” (Jerry sees an opportunity and quickly takes advantage of it as he begins speaking.) Jerry: “I’m sorry, but do you mind just holding that thought for a second. I kind of have to switch tapes again.” (Russo nods his head as he responds.) Russo: “Go right ahead, I still have a lot to talk about.” (Jerry smirks as he hears Russo’s comment. He responds.) Jerry: “Just out of curiosity, how much more do you have?” Russo: “Lets just say that we haven’t even gotten to the middle of my story yet.” (Jerry pops the new tape into his tape recorder. He points the microphone at Russo as he responds with a smile.) Jerry: “We better keep going then, huh?” Russo: “It would probably be best.” (Russo pauses for a moment, he then kicks back in his chair as he continues to reminisce.) Russo: “The Storms without realizing it had made each member of our group more dangerous then ever imagined. They signed their own death warrants when they effectively took away the last thing that we could lose. A man who no longer has anything to lose, does not fear anything that is placed before him. For the first time, I felt that feeling. I could tell that Kage felt it too. The Storms had woken up a monster, a monster that would go on to become known as the Shadow Riders.” (Russo shifts his weight in his seat and continues.) Russo: “Much to the dismay of the Storms, I formed a group the likes of which had never been seen before. A group hell bent on nothing more then complete and utter destruction of anyone and everything around them. I included Tony Castle, Steve Hayes, and Jason Kage under my leadership that first time around wit the name that I would go on to make famous. I finally understood what the Snake meant when stressed the importance of working as a unit. For the first time, I saw first hand what could be accomplished when personal goals could be set aside long enough to focus on the needs of the group.” (Jerry breaks in.) Jerry: “Sounds like you really took your role as a leader seriously.” Russo: “Of course, it was my creation after all. It was up to me to make that group succeed, and I was more then up for the challenge.” (Jerry smiles as he responds.) Jerry: “Yeah, but you had to have lost sight of some of your personal goals. How did that make you feel?” Russo: “Actually, it didn’t bother me. Being the humanitarian that I am, I was happy enough simply taking comfort in the fact that was not only making a name for myself. No, I was making a name for the entire group. I was single handedly putting all four of us on the map. That seemed to quell my desires for personal glory. I learned that we could accomplish more by pushing the group above all else. And so the first motto of the Shadow Riders was born.” (Jerry cuts in.) Jerry: “If you don’t mind me saying, it sounds to me like the other Shadow Riders were doing nothing but riding your coat tails to bigger and better things.” Russo: “You know, you make a very convincing argument. I’ve actually said the same thing in the past myself.” (Confused, Jerry looks to Russo.) Jerry: “Then why did you keep the group going?” Russo: “Because I blindly put my trust in someone who I would later find out could not be trusted.” (Jerry fires back in his normal inquisitive nature.) Jerry: “Do you wanna talk about that?” (Russo just smirks as he answers.) Russo: “I will . . . in time. For now, let us continue.” (Russo pauses for a moment to once again get on track. He then continues.) Russo: “Once the Shadow Riders were formed, the Storms could not keep up for long. After a long battle for supremacy, we finally won out and re-gained the control of the UWF in the process. As I said before though . . . once I get started, I don’t stop until the job is finished. The Storms were about to learn exactly what the Shadow Riders were all about . . .” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
