The Choice I Was Forced To Make.

28 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Please help me dear brother, don’t let him hit me again.” The young boy whispered this as he was in tears. “You were never there for me when I needed you. It was always about you and your needs no matter if it involved us both” This teenage boy yelled as he released his anger. “Over the years you never really changed, deep down inside your still the stupid manipulative son of a bitch I knew you to be. When are you going to grow up and think of your family for once instead of always thinking about yourself?” Disappointment was heard in his voice as he spoke out. [In one swift motion I sit up in my king-sized bed with beads of sweat across my forehead. Passing my hand across my face I rub my eyes some as I notice I’m still safe inside my home. For the first time in years I actually felt my heart beating inside me. Was I having a nightmare, or was someone trying to remind me of something? I could not make up my mind, but I tried my hardest to dismiss it from memory as quickly as possible. Sitting on the edge of my bed I lower my head a bit trying to shake off the remaining slumber only to have my focus shift to a picture on my wall. Squinting my eyes a bit I try to see past the darkness to no avail so I get to my feet and walk towards it. As I stepped closer and closer I started to make out the shapes and noticed it to be a picture of Ron and me at a much younger age.] “But I have no pictures of my family…” I speak softly with confusion in my voice. [Reaching towards the picture I try to grasp it only to have it vanish. Then against my window seal I hear the pouring rain knock against the glass. Lightning strikes down every few seconds causing a little light to shine into my room. Then with one sudden strike I look to the mirror and notice these two boys standing behind me. Glass shatters before me as the mirror is destroyed and I abruptly turn around to see these boys with my own eyes. Looking at these beings before me I notice them to be spirits and slowly they inch towards while I press myself backwards against the wall.] “Look at us Donny and you will understand our purpose. We’re not here to cause you pain of any sort, we’re simply here to help you remember.” The younger boy speaks to me first as they continue to pace in my direction. “Take a look at us dear tortured soul and remember the pain of your life. Maybe it is in the past you will learn not to make the same mistakes in the future. Deep down inside of you is the memory of what we are. Somewhere inside your heart is the feeling of love all you need to do is look hard.” The older boy speaks to me as if he has known me all life. [None of this makes any sense to me; it only makes me that much more scared. Even I the bringer of darkness gets scared, but until now I have not felt fear in so many years. Look inside myself for love? The mistakes they speak of are something I have thought of hard as of late, ever since I was sent into that realm. After thinking about that I knew their reason, this was all about Ron wasn’t it? I was so sure of this to be the answer I was seeking for until I heard the crying of a baby girl. Was that… Could that be...? No…] “Elizabeth! Elizabeth!” At the sound of hearing that baby cry it was the only thing feeling my mind and thoughts. [The one person I have cared for more than any other was Elizabeth. I knew better than to think she was really crying, since I know her life was taken so many years ago. After hearing that cry I felt a feeling of weakness deep down inside of me. Behind the two kids I see my former wife Katrina holding our baby girl in her arms. A smile over comes me as I walk towards them with tears falling from my eyes, just wanting it to be real. Suddenly a bolt of lightning strikes down and all it begins to fade away while everything around me begins to fade into a blue like color. My house soon changes to what it used to be before I became what I am now.] “Now do you remember who you really are Donny? Do you remember the person you once were, before you sold your soul? You were a loving father that cared so much for his daughter. Now you’re only a shadow of the man you once were.” After a few seconds of hearing this person speak I know it is the same soul that visited me not so long ago. “A shadow of the man I once was? Do you even know why that is? I once had feelings; I once had compassion for others! Then that bitch came and took it all from me in one single night when she got my daughter killed! You were not there at the funeral when she looked at me with a smile on her face and blamed me for the death of our daughter! So do not come here and tell me I should learn to love again when I have loved and that feeling was taken from me!” My voice was filled with anger as I began yelling at him the longer I spoke. “I know how your daughter was taken from you, but how does that justify what you have become Donny? Last week I told you that the right choice had to be made; did you make the correct choice for your brother? Now you need to learn that sometimes the right choice has to be made not only for others, but for yourself as well. The emotionless bastard no longer lives Donny Diamond; you’re now a completely different person inside. Even if you are not willing to admit it to yourself just yet, it shows to those around you.” Even though I spoke to him with anger he remained calm when speaking to me. [Turning around I look him in the face, eye to eye, being slightly confused at what he said. After looking at him for a moment I realize the truth of which he speaks and nod to him slightly. I let myself take a seat back on my bed as I look to him.] “Something is bothering you as of late, ever since the day you saw your brother become what you are. Until that day you would have did the bidding of the devil without even asking, but now you stand in his face. No matter what you do from this day forward your brother will question your actions because he knows something is different about you. Perhaps that was the mistake you made, the biggest mistake of your life.” He speaks softly to me knowing that his words are getting through to me. “My brother… Ron… I felt sorry for him being left to fend for himself while I sat here just watching him. He wanted to be just like our Dad, but he didn’t know the kind of person our Dad really was. But… After seeing what I did I guess I never what our father really was either. He made the same mistakes I did and I didn’t want Ron to the only one left without a purpose. Looking back at things Ron already had a purpose… He was doing what he wanted to do without the aid of anyone and now because of me he will never be able to go back to that. What kind of monster have I become?” When speaking you can almost hear the tears in my voice as they fall from my face. “There is always an opportunity for you to undo the mistakes you have made in life. All you have to do is look for the chance to do just…” [Before he can finish his sentence the realm begins to shake. The mirror that was once shattered now returns to normal as I notice a flame dancing for a few seconds. Again the area shakes brutally before returning to normal. As I lay back on my bed preparing to go back to sleep the match I have with Ron is on my mind. Brothers forever we promised… Let’s hope he can keep that promise…]
