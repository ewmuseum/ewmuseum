Revelations - Part Two

captured 5 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Baron stands there looking around at the men surrounding him knowing that the only way out of here is to fight if he wants to live. However, these seven guys had weapons with them which weakened his chance s of doing anything really. However he was determined to do what he had to do before he went down. He knows he can take out all seven guys but that it will just be harder then fighting them without weapons. The first guy moves in with a baseball bats and goes for his abdomen area but Baron shuffles back slightly to get out of the way before throwing and connecting whit a hard right hook. The man stumbles as Baron follows it back up with a hard left punch to the man’s jaw. The man then swings again with the baseball blindly and ends up missing. As Baron goes for another punch he feels a forearm come down against his lower back which throws him off his game. As he begins to realize that this is not going to be where one man comes up like a man he decides to take over the fight. As the man goes for another shot to his back, Baron slams his elbow into the man’s nose causing him to clutch his nose which has blood shooting out of it. Baron then is able to duck a punch to his jaw from another one of the gang members by dropping to his knees. The man then finds himself on his back as Ty knocks the man’s feet from under him with an easy leg sweep. However, within moments all seven guys are on top of him barraging him with left and right hand punches that end up bruising his body and face. After minutes, two of the men drag Ty off the floor to show blood seeping out of his left eyebrow. His right eye is also somewhat closed shut from a hard punch to it. The eye is already beginning to swell up and Baron is having a hard time looking out of it. The main guy who was talking earlier to Baron laughs at him as he slaps him across the face. “Looks like you are not as tough as used to be. Getting old in your ways I guess, not as strong or fast as you once used to be. I remember hearing when I joined the Dragons hearing about this awesome fighter that was running this small gang called the Wolves. Said that the man had a spirit of a wolf and could kill anyone he laid eyes on if he wanted to. I was told to stay away with him because I was still a rookie and it burned in me to hear someone to tell me to stay away from someone else. I refused to believe that you were as good as people said you were. Then… one night that all changed,” he tells Baron before pausing for a second. “That night… I saw you face off against our top fighter, Ricky. Ricky was the shit in my eyes and could have whooped your ass. I sat there and watched as Ricky confronted you. You didn’t want any part of this shit and as I saw you try to walk off I thought you were scared but then I saw something I have never seen anyone ever do. Ricky came up behind you with a knife and in seconds you took the knife out of his hands, nailed him with some hard punches and stabbed the knife Ricky had back into his stomach. You did it with no remorse and in a smooth style unlike I had ever seen. Then, you just walked away. It was that moment right there that I knew you were someone special,” he continues to tell Baron. Baron just stands there being held up by the gang members and listens to the words the guy is saying. He remembers that night vividly as it was one of the few times he had ever been attacked on the streets. Most people had kept their distance and knew of the reputation Baron had. However, that one time the guy had been high on drugs and not aware of what he was doing. Baron realized that and let the man live but made sure to make a profound impact to make sure the guy never did it again. Baron does not make any moves as the guy slaps his face again trying to incite some type of rage in him. “Now look at you Ty, you are nothing. You are a shell of your former self. Seven guys were able to take you down when it used to be a group of ten could never do it with guns in their hands. You are getting senile old man, and your ways do not work anymore. No one fears you out here on the streets anymore. It is time to take the streets away from your ancient philosophies and infuse some new blood around here. The only way to do that is take you and your gang down. That is what we plan on doing, after we take you back to our boss. He will kill you for himself. The time of the Wolves has just officially run out,” he tells Baron before punching him again across his jaw, almost breaking it. As they start to pull Baron down the street he feels hopeless. For once in his life Baron has no will to fight and for the first time ever he feels hopeless. How had Baron fallen so far? This was the end of everything he had worked so hard on for so long. After years of fighting and training, he was about to see his gang killed off and he was going to be killed as well. As they take them to their gang headquarters he thinks of everything that has happened so far in his life. He thinks of all the hardships he has endured and the work he put into make his gang the best. Within minutes it has all been taken away from him. Within weeks he had seen his whole entire life shatter into pieces that he would never be able to glue back together. After walking through the streets they finally reach the Dragons headquarters. Once inside he is taken to a smaller room which he presumes is the boss room. The room is dimly lit and he is unable to see the face of the boss. He looks around with his good eye and laughs at how lavishly they spent their money on unneeded items such as paintings or drapes, among other unimportant items. “Is there something funny?” came a dark harsh voice from in front of him. Immediately he knows the voice comes from the boss himself who had heard him laughing to himself quietly. “Yeah, you do not know the first thing about running a gang. You put paintings that are worth thousands of dollars in here? Just shows you value material things then the things that matter the most like respect, trust, and ideals. Saddens me to see someone who wants to take down my gang acts this way. Hurts me to see who plans on taking over these streets after my gang has been dismantled and destroyed for good. Actually, it makes me sick to my stomach. So how about you just kill me now so I don’t have to witness your terrible reign as leader of my city,” he tells the boss disrespectfully. “You have some balls talking to me like that when you are in my house. Ricardo, have you gotten the reports back concerning Mr. Baron’s group?” asks the covered boss. Then from behind Baron walks Ricardo, known to him as Ricky, and hands some papers to Baron. Ricky then begins to walk away but before he does so he lands a hard left punch to Baron’s jaw. Baron then can feel the blood in his mouth and spits it out on the floor with a smile on his face. “Nice to know you could never beat me as a man Ricky. Also nice to see that my thoughts on you are correct, that you truly are a pussy,” he tells Ricky. “Nice to know that I got to fuck your girl’s pussy before she was killed,” Ricky tells him which causes the rage to build up in Baron’s gut. He tears away from the two people holding him and head butts Ricky in the head before dropping him with a hard right punch. Before Baron is able to inflict any more damage on Ricky, the two men holding him from before grab at him and pull him down to the floor. “Enough of this foolishness. Someone get Ricky out of here and make sure he knows why he got dropped like he did, because of Baron hadn’t done it, I would have,” he tells some of the other gang members in the room who immediately pick him up and drag him out of the room. “That was a nice display of athleticism you showed their Ty. You showed some flashes of yourself when you were younger and it was refreshing to see that you defended your deceased girlfriend’s honor, something that touches me in my heart. You would be a great asset to the Dragon’s yet your attitude and your brashness are far too large for this group. We actually have control around here Baron, which you have shown none of lately,” he tells Baron. “Now, concerning your gang… well it seems like you no longer have a gang. They were killed within the last hour. Every. Last. One. Of. Them. Seems like you are next to go. But, before I do so, I will show you who I am and I will tell you why I am doing this to you once and for all,” he tells him. Then, the man stands up and walks out of the darkness into the little bit of light that is actually in the room. Baron looks up at him with some shock on his face as he sees who it is. The man, who leads the Dragons, is Kristen’s father. The scene then fades to black.
