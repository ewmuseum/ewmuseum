Clowns Archive - 9.4.2003

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Roll up, roll up, see the freak show!” Came the Ringmasters voice through the megaphone, as he promoted his circus. “We have crazy beings that are bewildering, bedazzling and bemusing! They’re unbelievable! You’ll have to see them to believe them!” His voice boomed out towards the crowds of people in the town centre. “We have the man with two souls!” The Ringmaster shouts, as he knows this will get people’s attention. He slowly walks over to a cage and unveils the cloth. Inside is Cyrus Skar. Skar is asleep. The Ringmaster rattles the bars of the cage with his megaphone causing Cyrus to come to an abrupt awakening. “Here we have an example of one of the many freaks at my circus.” Cackled the Ringmaster in an evil tone of voice. “This man has two souls inside one body!” With this comment the crowd ‘OOO’ in awe of this strange man. “Now if you want to see freaks like him and many more rejects at my circus, it’s only $10 a ticket!” Cyrus is getting angry smashing the cage with his fists. He reaches out and grabs the Ringmaster and slams him against the cage, again and again until he falls limp to the floor. The crowd that had gathered all run away in fear. Cyrus awakes with a start, sweat dripping down his forehead. He turns around and looks at his clock. It’s four thirty in the morning. Cyrus pants heavily, but within a few seconds regains a normal breathing pattern. He gets up and out of his bed clad in some black boxer shorts. He walks into his bathroom and looks in the mirror. He turns the cold water tap on and cups his hand underneath it. He splashes himself in the face reviving him from his slumber. “What a strange dream that was Cyrus.” The voice spoke with a sense of irony. “I mean you, a freak?” The voice laughs. “It’s not like you to have nightmares.” “Well, everybody has one now and again don’t they?” Replied Cyrus in a calm tone of voice. “The thing is, nightmares mean nothing. You’ve got to remember that, what just happened, means zero.” “Have you been watching the television recently?” Asked the voice, changing the subject. “Yes I have, why do you ask?” Replied Cyrus sounding slightly confused at the voices unusual question. “Chris Wrestling’s been mouthing off on LOW programming.” The voice spoke cunningly now, trying to stir up Cyrus’s emotions. “Oh Cyrus, it was painful watching it, he called you a ‘jobber’. I mean what sort of a thing is that to call somebody? You don’t lie on your back and let people pin you do you?” “No…” Replied Cyrus as if it was an obvious answer. “So why is he calling you a ‘jobber’? He’s supposedly so experienced in wrestling yet he doesn’t even understand the terms used!” The voice hard started to get angry. “I mean this is some guy who’s been in LOW for a long time as he admits, yet he still hasn’t even had a World Title shot! It’s not a conspiracy theory against him it’s just that he’s so crap he doesn’t deserve a World Title shot!” “I’ve seen Chris Wrestling. He’s all right, but he rates himself too highly. Guys like him just piss me off. Maybe if he had the talent to back up what he was saying I could take him seriously. Whilst he’s running his mouth at how he’s the best and how the staff are trying to hold him back, he’s only making a fool out of himself.” Cyrus spoke from experience. “I mean does he honestly think this is the first federation I’ve been in?” “Probably!” Sniggered the voice. “ Damn, does he not watch tapes of my previous matches to see what my strengths and weaknesses are?” “He probably thinks he’s so damn good he doesn’t have to!” Laughed the voice. “I’ve watched tape after tape of him wrestling. I know his wrestling style inside out. I prepare for my matches. I make sure I know my opponent well. I respect the fact he’s been in LOW for so long, but I certainly don’t respect how lightly he’s taking the match!” Cyrus was getting more and more angry as he spoke. “I mean hell! He probably doesn’t even realize we’re in a scaffold deathmatch!” Cyrus punches the wall hard, causing a slight indentation to it. “Calm down Cyrus, save your anger for your match with him, throw him off the scaffolding, it’ll make you feel so much better.” The voice tried to calm Cyrus down. “I am calm, I can’t wait to get my hands on that punk. He has no idea what he’s getting himself into. If he’d have kept his mouth shut I might have kept his humiliation to a minimum, but now, now he’s opened his mouth and spouted a load of total and utter nonsense he needs teaching a lesson.” Cyrus has calmed down a little bit now, but still seems full of rage. “Cyrus, just remember when you fight him. He’s disrespected you, he’s underestimated you, and he’s called you. Bare those three things in mind, then kick his f***ing arse as hard as you can!” The voice says this with conviction, spurring Cyrus on. “I know, I know, he’ll get what he deserves when it comes to Aftermath, he’ll help me carry on with my clean record of no losses here in LOW. I won’t brag about people I’ve beaten, it’s just what idiots do to try and make themselves look good. Everybody loses once in a while, I’m just hoping I can keep getting stupid opponents like Chris Wrestling.” Cyrus had calmed down a lot when he finished saying this. He turned the tap on again and splashed some more water in his face to cool him down. “After Chris Wrestling, it’s on to the PPV to win the Gauntlet. Do you think you’re ready now Cyrus?” Asked the voice. “I’m ready, I’m more than ready. I’ve accustomed myself to the ways of LOW now. Now all I have to do is let my talent do the talking.” Cyrus smiles as he finishes his last sentence. “Excellent Cyrus, excellent. I’ll make a World Champion out of you in no time at all. Ciao.” The voice disappears back into the depths of Cyrus’s soul as Cyrus walks off into his bedroom to go and get changed.
