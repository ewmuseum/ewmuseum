Two out of Three is Bad (for You)

captured 5 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Starring Iron Lung Lights Out Wrestling's Saturday Night Realm show last week was less of a homecoming than expected for superstar and Xtreme Champion, Iron Lung. For he had lost to the Realm Champion Cyrus Skar, and the speculation was created that the Realm Division of the company was in fact better than the Xtreme Division. After many woes in his locker room, Lung left the arena in an almost unbelieveable pace, racing through the cooridors of hallways and backing out of his parking spot before even letting his automobile run for a few moments. It was a dramatic moment indeed, but something Lung had come across far too often. Always running from his problems, and afraid to confront them when it came to be 'crunch time'. But now, the stage was set again for another match with Cyrus Skar, this time it would be to the advantage of the Xtreme Champion. For he had set the rules for this particular engagement, it would be a two out of three falls match, and the winner would take all. Certainly, fatigue would play a larger part in this match, especially since both men already are still nursing the battle wounds from the junkyard brawl. But no matter to IL, he was used to the pain, and had grown used to letting it set inside of his skin. That was really one of his unique traits, he didn't seem to let any amount of pain or any amount of adversity thrown at him by the public bother him. He knew that whatever happened to him, in the end it would all end up the same, with him on the top of the ladder once more, looking at the chaos he had created with a grin on his face, and the crowd on their feet with his name on the tip of their acid tongues. So, would the trait continue to roll this Saturday Night? We would soon find out. But for now, we were inside the Villa Roma apartment complexes, in the heart of Robert Pendragon's hometown in Connecticut, and we were prepared to get his exclusive tale of what he thought would go down in the squared circle in a little more than twenty-four hours from now.. We were already inside the complex's farbicated steel structure, thus making the reception of our cell phone virtually impossible to detect a signal. We already had an appointment, so there was no need to check in with reception, but we would however check out the hottie with a body that was readily behind there working, twirling her hair in her finger and foxingly trying to pull us in for a closer look. We were almost putty in our hands, if it weren't for us fighting our temptations. We had a tight schedule to keep, there was always time for pleasure later. Upon pressing the button to go to the thirteen of twelve floors (it was for private access only with a keycard inserted into the elevator button system), the elevator rose up to the VIP suites. Of course, this was the most magnificant of the floors, complete with personal staff to fulfill any need and also with any accomodation you could possibly dream up in your feeble minds. This was a quick elevator, for before we could even take time to relax and enjoy the newest music coming out of the speaker system, we were stopped and the metallic doors were opened, showing us to the Persian carpetting and mood-lighted fixtures. The hallways were doubled in size, allowing enough space for three, possibly even four occupants to roam through it. If we were to tell you all the details, we would be here for another half hour, but just to let you know, it is nice... We were now walking down the extended hallway, looking for the infamous cherry oak door that the Champion resided in. Soon enough, we were there, clanking on the door with a closed fist and waiting to hear a mutter from inside. It was usually an inviting welcome from Lung, even if he didn't really like all the same questions being asked to him all the time. He was adorned in the spotlight, it always seemed to bring out his good side, even on his worst days, and that was why the crowd loved him. (While opening the door and slyly peeking his flourescent red eye out to look) All right, you guys are here. Let's get this interview over with, I need to make a grand opening at a Subway. All right, we'll just come right in then. Thanks for inviting us. (While swinging the door open) Yah yah, well take a seat and I'll see what I can do for you. Thank you very much. (While closing the door) Don't mention it... (While sitting down) So, what do you want? Just your opinion on -- (Interrupting) My opinion on my Two out of Three Falls Match on Realm with Cyrus Skar right. Exactly, would you mind telling us? I suppose I could let you in on a secret Jeff... But my name is Steve... Eh, I was close. Anyway Mark, my opinion is the following... Our subject sifts through a stack of papers with his hands, and makes a slight 'a ha' noise when he discovers the writing he has been looking for. Looking upon it with a grin on his face, he clears his throat and begins to read! The more surprising thing about this is he doesn't show much emotion while he is reading, but rather just blanky reads line by line with a stoned impression. We were used to this from him anyway, but this was a rather strange change... (In a Ben Stein type of voice) Of course, I'm very excitied about my two out of three falls match, insert interviewer's name here. Why wouldn't I be? I mean, I get to fight Cyrus Skar again, the man who thinks that Xtreme is just a fad. We both got fairly bruised in our Junkyard Brawl, but that just didn't catch my eye, especially since I wasn't prepared for it. He took advantage of me, and that's something that I can't have happen once more. So therefore, I truly am preparing for my match. I've been training nonstop insert interviewer's name here, I really have! I've even stepped up my routine to insure a victory on my part. As a final word, I have this to say to Cyrus Skar. Come the end of our match, Xtreme won't just be a fad anymore, but it will truly be an experience that you will not soon forget. For you see, you seem to forget who you are dealing with here. That's a very moving -- (Interrupting once more) I'm the most determined member on this roster, Cyrus. I've been through any type of Hardcore or Xtreme match that you can dream about, even the ones that are in your worst nightmares. And you know something? I've survived them all, as you can already tell by the way I'm still walking and breathing. So what would be so different about this time? Perhaps the fact that by the end of it, one of us might end up in a hospital for the second consecutive time, becoming more familiar with the nurses. I suggest that you think very hard about that fact before you step into the ring with me Cyrus, because I am one hundred percent positive that if you push me around enough, you will be the one that is in traction. I'm not going to be in a good mood, and I'll be just waiting for you to push the wrong button. And when that time comes, I can promise you one thing my friend, and that is that you won't be thinking that Xtreme is a fad any longer. For you see, I will set it into your veins, and let the pain seep into your skin. And when it's all over, you will have Xtreme involved in all of your body, and your reign as Realm Champion, will crumble through your hands like sand... With that being said, Iron Lung points to the door, putting his paper back down on the table and smiling once more with a sickening grin, his flourescent red eyes peering into Steve's very soul. He knew that this interview was over, as the scene began to fade into darkness with Steve getting up and heading towards the door, obeying the Xtreme Champion before he became another example of what happens when you don't listen...
