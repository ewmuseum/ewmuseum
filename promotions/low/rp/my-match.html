My Match

18 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Umm...smells great.” Dark Haze lifts his nose in the air smelling the sweet aroma of rich fudge chocolate brownies. Yet, the smell also has a slight pungent odor that goes almost unnoticeable. Haze removes the chocolate treats from the oven and places them atop the stove. After they cool he slices them into squares, each about 2.5 cubic inches. Haze grabs three and sets them onto a small plate, leaving seventeen behind. “Damn, these are gonna be raw.” He take his ration and goes towards the television where he takes a seat. Haze finishes off two of the brownies, and not too long after his eyes tighten and his pupils become slightly dilated. “Booyakasha!” Ali G speaks, as he introduces himself. Dark Haze cracks up at the sound of Ali G’s voice. “Yo, Yo, I’m here today with none other than my main man...” Ali G rambles on as he is about to interview a respected politician. Haze continues to laugh hysterically. To be continued... The next day, Dark Haze is getting ready to make his trip to Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Everything appears to be packed into his limo, at his Atlanta, Georgia residence. Right now, it seems that he is just waiting to leave. “I’ve got another hour and forty-five minutes before I have to get to Hartsfield International, so I guess it’s time to eat these.” Haze stops by his counter top and opens up a tin Christmas container. Inside the container are brownies from the day before. Haze takes a few brownies and puts them onto a plate. “You can have some too. Like two of them will have you feeling it for a good five to six hours.” Haze says, speaking to his trainer. His trainer, Ernest, replies, “Aight.” “Tight, we have seventeen to go.” Dark Haze hands Ernest the plate, and they go sit on his patio. The pool is bright, clear blue, and the sun is beaming in, despite the cool weather. The breeze is stead yet light, and the environment is calm and peaceful. Haze swallows a brownie in only a couple of bites as does his trainer. “How long till it kicks in?” Ernest asks. “Within the next couple hours you’ll notice it; it’s pretty gradual. You better finish the other two though. I know you’re not a lightweight.” “Shut the fuck up.” “Hey, I’m giving you the benefit of the doubt here.” Dark Haze chomps on his next brownie, leaving only crumbs behind. In the same time, his trainer eats about half of one. “Damn, I need some water. This shit makes my mouth dry and my saliva thick.” As he speaks he rises to go grab a water. “True that; that’s why I hate chocolate. Although it does reap it’s benefits sometimes.” Just as Ernest opens the door Haze yells, “Hey, grab me one too!” A couple minutes later, Ernest returns with the waters. “Here you go.” He tosses it in Haze’s lap. “Watch yourself son. You could have made me a very unhappy person.” “Ahh...how refreshing.” Ernest sets his water on the table. “You better quit babysitting those brownies and start doing something.” Haze gives an evil look to his trainer. Alas, they both clean their plates. “Now until this kicks in were gonna need something else.” Haze reaches in his pocket and pulls out a nice mahogany pen case. “What the hell is that for?” “It’s a bleezy,” Haze replies. He opens the case and to his surprise there is no cigar. Instead, a shiny pen sits in the special pen groove. “That’s not a blunt!” “No kidding, it’s the wrong case you stupid fuck.” Dark Haze pulls the golden pen from the case, looks at it, and places it back in. “Damn, I didn’t know you became the fancy businessman.” “I didn’t, this is for Cyrus Skar.” Haze puts the case back in his pocket and pulls another case, exactly the same, out. “Here we go.” “I thought you guys were enemies.” “No, I told you, ‘If I win my first match, I’ll probably have to face him later for the Xtreme Title.’ We may be opponents, but I don’t think it will escalate to enemies.” “Oh, I see, you’re butt-buddies.” Before Dark Haze can speak Ernest continues, “It’s okay.” “Fuck you. I’m trying to be nice. I figured he doesn’t have much family, so why not try and make his Christmas a little better than those of the past.” “So, why a pen?” “He can use it to sign the papers for a title shot against me. You know, a rematch.” Dark Haze sparks the special ingredient cigar and hits it over and over. “Pass some here.” Ernest demands. Dark Haze comically replies, “Yeah, niggas love to be nice when they want shit. Other than that they call you names, imply that you’re gay...” Haze is cut off immediately, “Shut up!” Haze passes him the blunt. “Oh shit, it’s about time to get going.” Haze scurries inside and grabs the brownies. “These are going carry on.” He then locks up and rushes out the front door, along with his trainer. Still smoking the blunt, they get in the limo and pull away. After arriving, checking in, and boarding the plane they are ready for take off. They remain seated, buckle up, and off they go. Dark Haze opens up his tin, with brownies, and starts devouring more. He also passes a couple to his trainer, who is laughing hysterically. “I’m straight. I’m so fucked up.” “Me too, I’m just going for the next level.” Haze laughs. Next thing you know, they are in Pennsylvania. Haze is in the middle of the ring, the lights are out, and tens of thousands of fans fill the arena. “Now, making his way to the ring, standing in at seven feet, three inches, weighing in at 298 pounds, the one they call Hell’s Guardian, THE DEEEMOOOOOOOND!” Dark Haze stares down the isle waiting to see The Demond. As he appears, the crowd hops to their feet and starts cheering. “What’s going on, since when do they like him?” The seven foot, three beast runs towards the ring. Hair disguising his whole body, like Sasquatch, and horns coming from his head, like the devil, make him look even more intimidating. Flames trail behind him, and he hops into the ring. He immediately charges for Haze, so Haze’s first reaction is to throw a punch. He swings with a big right hook, and the punch appears to go clearly through his opponent. The Demond wasn’t fazed. Haze, the former pro boxer, throws a quick 1-2 combo, and the same thing happens again. “Noooo! What the hell...” All of a sudden, The Demond picks Haze up for a piledriver and completes the move. From here on, he dominates the match. Eventually, The Demond hurts himself while trying to pull off an aerial move, so Dark Haze capitalizes and goes for a cover. “1, 2...” For some unknown reason the referee stops counting. “Come on, hurry up!” Dark Haze is going insane, as it appears that this match is rigged. “Count!” Dark Haze stands to his feet and clocks the ref, but again his punch doesn’t do any damage. “What’s happening?” Out of nowhere Haze gets The Kamikazi, The Demond’s finisher. The Demond covers, and the ref starts the count. “1, 2, 3.” The ref is waving his hands in the air signaling that the first match of the first round of the Xtreme Title Tournament is over. “That’s fucked up,” Haze cries. Perturbed by the outcome, Haze runs into the backstage area. Walking, he finally calms down, just before running into Cyrus Skar. “Hey, C. Skar, what’s happening?” “Not too much, Haze. Just getting ready for my match.” “True.” The Tru Blue Superstar reaches into his pocket and pulls out the Mahogany pen case and hands it over. “It’s a little something. Merry Christmas.” “Thanks.” It seems as if Skar is a little emotional, but he holds it in. “It’s eighteen karat gold, and it has your name engraved in it.” “Now, if I give you a title match, that is after I win it tonight, I’ll let you borrow this to sign for it.” “Fuck you!” Dark Haze flips out, and tries to attack Cyrus. Meanwhile, Cyrus stands there smiling. Haze throws two lefts, a right, a left hook, and a right uppercut. All of them land, yet no damage occurred. With beads of sweat dripping down Haze’s face Ernest yells, “Wake up! Wake up Haze!” Dark Haze’s head pops up and his eyes open. “Oh shit.” Relieved, he speaks. “I’m glad it wasn’t real.” “Yeah, you were mumbling, sweating, and you looked quite uncomfortable. Anyway, we’re almost here. You need to strap up for landing.” Dark Haze puts his seatbelt on and starts eating the rest of the brownies. Before long, the landing gear opens and they hit the runway. “That was intense.”
