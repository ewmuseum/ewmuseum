The Shadow Riders at Sea pt.4

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Prologue When we last left the Shadow Riders, we had just found out that Kage was receiving a cell phone call from some girl named Emily because as we all know even this far out at sea a Shadow Rider cannot escape from the hounding attention of their side conquests. We also watched as the Shadow Riders tagged the mammoth beast that they had been chasing for the past day with yet another barrel attached to a harpoon. Will this be enough for the Shadow Riders to finally bag the gigantic fish and have some dinner? Read on to find out in this the fourth addition of . . . “The Shadow Riders at Sea”. (The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders along with a voice over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens on the dawn of a new day aboard “The Wave Rider”. On the upper deck of the yacht, “The Loose Cannon” Jason Kage is standing in front of the captain’s wheel getting some sun with a pair of binoculars in his hands. Kage raises the binoculars to his eyes as he looks out into the distance to get a better look at the two barrels bobbing up and down on the surface of the water. The barrels are bouncing in place on the surface of the water a mere two hundred yards away from the boat. Below the deck, “Superstar” Eric Russo is busy at work trying to fix the engine the only way he knows how.) Russo: “Stupid engine!” (Russo’s voice is followed by a echoing clang as he yells out again.) Russo: “Oww! My foot!” (Back up on the deck Kage, finally showing that he’s getting his sea legs about him, walks over to the ladder to the lower deck and slides down by positioning his feet on the outsides of the ladder and dropping instead of using the steps. He hits the lower deck where the “Vigilante” Luke Justice is busy at work coming up with a way to capture the beast as he slides struggles his way into a wet suit. Hearing Kage’s feet hit the deck, Russo calls up from below.) Russo: “That frigging thing moving yet?” (As Kage watches Luke prepare for yet another plan, he answers Russo.) Kage: “Nope . . . he’s not moving. Maybe he’s sleeping or something.” (Justice is busy at work getting the last of his body into the wet suit and zipping it up. Luke’s eyes are full of purpose as though he is working against some unknown deadline. As he suits up, he keeps making short glances towards the open hole to the engine room where Russo can be heard cursing and loudly clanking metal together. Kage turns to Luke as he begins to speak.) Kage: “Luke man . . . please don’t go after it on your own. Russo is nuts, if you tag it without him there he may go over the edge and try and sink the boat or something. You know how he can get when things don’t go his way. He’s an egomaniac.” (Paying little attention to what Kage is saying, Luke just goes on with his preparations. He makes sure all of his gear is in order, selects a long and strong steel pole and pulls out a tiny black bag from a nap sack. Luke opens the tiny bag as Kage continues to plead with him.) Kage: “Come on man . . . put that stuff away. I know you could catch the damn thing right now, but Russo is obsessed with it. Please don’t do this . . .” (Luke reaches into the tiny black bag and pulls out a long sharp needle. He examines the needle looking for flaws as he responds to Kage.) Justice: “Look, Eric had his turn to catch that fish. Now, I’m gonna go and get my turn at it. I would like to eat before the day is out.” (Luke fastens the needle to the end of his steel pole as he continues.) Justice: “Hey, maybe you can get a shot at it next if I don’t get the job done. It’s only fare that we each give it a try. (Kage begins to try and talk Luke out of his idea when Russo pops his head out from below deck. Russo’s face is streaked with dirt and sweat from the work down below causing the blood shot redness of his eyes to stand out even more. Seeing Luke prepare for something, he pulls himself out of the engine area and onto the deck as he begins to question what is going on.) Russo: “What are you doing? Got another idea?” (Refusing to take his eyes off his gear, Luke responds.) Justice: “Yeah . . . I’m gonna catch the fish. I’m hungry and I don’t feel like waiting for you to get the job done.” (Russo obviously annoyed that Luke may beat him to his goal, responds.) Russo: “Fine . . . go ahead and try to catch him all you want. Just don’t expect to get any of the food when I catch him and fry him up.” (Russo begins to climb up the ladder heading towards the captain’s wheel as Luke responds.) Justice: “Why do you have to be the one who catches him? I just need something to eat because some genius decided to use all of our food as bait! That fish looks like a damn smorgasbord to me and I don’t feel like waiting for another one of your crackpot ideas only to have it fail again!” (Russo mutters to himself as he approaches the wheel of the yacht.) Russo: “I need to eat . . . well I need a boat that doesn’t spring a leak every time that . . .” (Russo turns the ignition key, starting the engine.) Russo: “. . . damn whirly thing goes around . . .” (We can hear the engine creak and crack loudly from down below.) Russo: “. . . all crooked and shit! It’s really a problem . . . that’s pissing me the fuck off!” (Russo slowly forces the boat forward to a chorus of scraping, cracking, and creaks towards the two floating barrels. The engine can clearly be heard loudly sputtering along as the boat moves closer and closer to the barrel. Luke climbs to the upper deck and stands beside Russo as he begins speaking.) Justice: “You know can catch that damn thing real easy . . .” Russo: “Yeah maybe, you’re not gonna do it today though. Not by yourself.” (Stating his case, Luke responds.) Justice: “And why not? I can finish that damn fish off and have him fried up in less then an hour without even batting an eye.” (Russo comes back trying to change the subject as he listens to the creaking engine.) Russo: “You hear this engine? We’re screwed . . . and that damn fish knows it too. He knew what he was doing when he screwed with it.” Justice: “Listen to yourself brother! You sound like Captain freaking Ahab when he was chasing Moby Dick! Will you cut that crap out and tell me why I shouldn’t just go get that fish myself!?” (Russo turns and looks Luke in the eye as he responds.) Russo: “Because I wanna do it as a team. He wronged the Shadow Riders and now he’s gonna have to deal with us . . . all of us.” (Russo shuts off the engine as he pulls alongside the bobbing barrels in the water. Excitedly, Luke makes his way down to the lower deck and starts towards the side of the boat where the barrel is. Russo quickly follows behind him reaching out and grabbing his shoulder as he speaks out.) Russo: “Careful . . . go slow.” (Russo then motions over to Kage as he speaks out again.) Russo: “Stay there and keep a look out just in case.” (Russo and Luke quietly move closer to the side of the boat. As they move in, Russo grabs hold of a harpoon and puts it in front of him as though in preparation for a battle. Luke grabs a pole and leans over the side of the boat as he begins to pull one of the barrels in closer. The barrel bobs easily towards the boat causing Russo to become extremely uneasy and paranoid over the effortless movement. In almost a whisper, he speaks to Luke.) Russo: “He’s playing with us . . .” (Luke moves the pole away from the barrel and begins to pull up on one of the ropes as Russo continues in a whisper.) Russo: “Go easy, we only want to get his attention. If he even so much as feels like he’s gonna take off, drop the pole and get back. He could easily take off your hands if he wants to.” (To get a better feel of the rope, Luke puts down the pole and begins using his hands to pull the rope in. Strangely, the rope does not falter in the least as it seemingly rises to the surface with no tension on the other end. Luke looks to Russo who has a look of extreme confusion on his face. Seeing this, causes Russo to move in on the action. Russo places his harpoon on the floor of the deck as he leans almost his whole body over the side of the boat and joins Luke in pulling up the rope. He begins to speak.) Russo: “Here, let me get this. I don’t like it, I don’t know what he’s . . .” (Both Luke and Russo are leaned over the side of the boat nearly touching the water surface with their torsos. This position affords them the disadvantage of not being able to see that towards the front of the boat, fully unattached from the barrels, the beast has made it’s presence known. The fin of the monster emerges through the surface of the water and begins moving. It cuts through the water in almost total silence at a rate of almost unreal speed hurtling itself towards the two occupied Shadow Riders. Luckily, Kage spots the in that is almost on top of the men now as he yells out in a state of panic.) Kage: “Get away from the water! It’s coming! It’s almost on you guys! Hurry!” (Russo and Luke hear Kage’s warning just in the nick of time. Both men drop backwards onto the deck floor away from the water saving themselves from an unfortunate confrontation with the beast. The monster passes by the side of the boat with a trail of three feet of chewed off cable trailing behind it on the surface of the water. As it goes by the boat, the monster rolls to it’s side causing the tail end of the harpoon that is still in it’s side to break the surface of the water. Then with a swat of the tail, the beast lashes against the side of the boat causing the rope that is still being held by Russo to be ripped from his grasp while taking of a nice chunk of paint off the side of the boat in the process. It then makes it’s way back out further away from the boat as it turns back to a right side up position and only the fin becomes visible again. The beast begins widely circling the boat. Russo looks down at his hands which are now bleeding from the rope burns on the palms. Realizing how close he had just come to losing his hands, Russo becomes infuriated and more unstable then ever. Russo yells out to Kage as he pushes himself back to his feet.) Russo: “Get that rope in the boat, it could end up screwing us over if we don’t!” (Russo now yells to Luke who has gotten back to his feet as well.) Russo: “Fire up the engine!” (Luke climbs up to the captain’s wheel as Kage quickly begins hauling in the rope. Luke fires up the engine to a horrific grinding sound. Hearing this, Russo yells out again.) Russo: “Easy! That damn thing will blow out if you push it too hard!” (Kage who is still pulling in the rope next to Russo decides that now would be a good time for him to make a suggestion about the situation. He looks towards Russo as he begins speaking.) Kage: “Look, maybe we should just quit while we’re ahead. Let’s just forget about the damn fish and head back in to shore. That’s what I think at lea . . .” (Kage is cut off by Russo who turns to him with a fiery look in his eyes.) Russo: “Shut up!” (Slightly taken aback, Kage still tries to reason with the tyrannical Russo.) Kage: “Come on man, the damn thing chewed through the ropes holding the barrels on . . . he wrecked the boat . . . there’s no telling what he’ll do next. Let’s just radio for help and call it a day.” (Paying no attention to Kage’s pleas, Russo calls up to Luke.) Russo: “Try using the pumps a little!” (Seeing that Russo has not intention of listening to his plans, Kage speaks up.) Kage: “Eric, I’m not joking! Call the Coast Guard!” (Russo turns to Kage now strangely detached from his words.) Russo: “I don’t have time for jokes right now, I’m trying to catch this fish.” (Hearing this, Kage becomes angered and exclaims.) Kage: “Fine . . . I’ll call them!’ (Kage turns and begins to stalk off down the steps into the cabin. Seeing this, a sinister look comes over Russo’s face. He turns and heads after Kage disappearing into the cabin behind him. Russo pauses by the door to the cabin and sees his shotgun resting against the wall. He quickly grabs up the weapon and follows after Kage. Inside of the cabin, Kage picks up the handset of the radio and begins turning the knobs and flicking the lights on the small box as he tries to find a signal. Suddenly, Russo’s voice booms out from behind Kage.) Russo: “Move . . .” (Kage turns to see Russo standing with his shot gun pointed. Knowing the instability of Russo at the present time, a look of horror fills Kage’s face.) Russo: “Sorry, can’t let you do that.” (Not knowing what Russo intends to do, Kage jumps for cover. Russo aims his shotgun and pulls the trigger . . . BOOM! He blasts a shot directly at the small radio. The box explodes in a shower of sparks and metal pieces. The pieces fly out in all directions as Russo fires another shot at the radio finishing it off for good. Russo takes a deep breath as a smile crosses his face. He then looks towards Kage and gives him a wink. Russo then turns and heads up the steps out of the cabin and back onto the deck leaving Kage quivering on the floor secure in the knowledge that Russo has finally gone over the deep end. Back on the deck, Luke excitedly points out to the ocean ahead of the boat as Russo re-emerges onto the deck.) Justice: “Look, he’s coming right to us!” (Russo trades his shotgun for the harpoon that he placed on the deck earlier. He responds to Luke.) Russo: “He’s not coming to us, he’s coming at us! Slow down the boat, he’s gonna try and ram us head on . . .” (We can hear the clank of the engine down below deck.) Russo: “Slow us down! Ease off the gas . . .” (The fin closes in on the ship as Russo holds his harpoon up like a spear. He yells out to Luke again.) Russo: “Quick! Bring us to the right . . . hard!” (Luke follows Russo’s instructions and takes the boat hard to the right. This gives Russo a clear shot at the beast as it swims by the side of the boat. Russo jams the harpoon down into the back of the beast below the water as they pass by. He exclaims out happy with the shot.) Russo: “Ha . . . let’s see you get cocky with me now!” (Kage emerges onto the deck just as the rope attached to the end of the harpoon reels out into the ocean. Following on the end of the rope, another of the barrels of chum flips into the air before landing in the water with a large splash. Kage yells out rhetorically.) Kage: “Why won’t it die!?” (Russo calls up to Luke.) Russo: “Bring the boat around! We got him on the run now!” (In front of the boat, Luke can see the barrel floating on top of the water trailing behind the fin of the monster. The beast is trying to dive into the water but is quickly being brought up again. As the boat nears the monster, Russo prepares another harpoon. He begins calling instructions up to Luke.) Russo: “Speed up . . . move to half speed! I wanna get right next to that bastard!” (We can hear the engine putter along. Hearing the strain, Luke yells to Russo.) Luke: “I can’t keep up this speed, it’s gonna . . .” (The barrel trailing behind the beast smacks against the side of The Wave Rider. Almost falling into the water from the sudden jolt, Russo exclaims.) Russo: “Yo . . . watch where you’re going!” (Luke quickly gets back control of the boat avoiding getting caught up in the rope.) Russo: ‘There you go . . . we got him now!” (Russo leans over the side of the boat just as it pulls alongside the beast. He raises the harpoon over his head and yells out.) Russo: “Here . . . A little gift from the Shadow Riders!” (Russo plunges the second of the harpoons into the beasts back. The sound of gunshots ring out through the air. As the rope attached to the harpoon reels out, we see Kage standing on the ladder holding on for dear life with one hand while firing his hand gun at the monster with the other. Russo grins at the thought of the Shadow Riders finally working as a unit to accomplish the goal. Luke then calls over to Kage.) Justice: “Stop firing at him! You’re gonna start a frenzy and end up attracting other fish from the blood and I want something left of Ma Belmont to eat!” Kage: “I can’t just wait here and not get a piece of the action!” Russo: “Stop bickering you two . . . this guy is ours!” (The two barrels float along the surface just in front of the boat. Russo runs to the front to check it out as he yells out to Luke and Kage.) Russo: “I wanna get one more on him! He can’t do anything with two, I think three will put him out of the game!” (Russo quickly moves around and ascends to the upper deck as he speaks to Luke.) Russo: “Here . . . I’ll take the controls. Go help Kage.” (Luke moves away as Russo takes the helm. He then calls out to Luke and Kage.) Russo: “Get a couple of hook poles you two, I got an idea!’ (Russo steadies the boat as he slows it down some. Kage and Luke grab the poles. Russo maneuvers the boat towards the barrels. He watches intently as he moves in closer and closer) Russo: “Get ready!” (Luke and Kage prepare the hooks hanging them over the side of the boat just as it pulls next to the barrels.) Russo: “Hook em’!” (Kage and Luke hook one of the ropes attached to a barrel holding on tightly as the fish begins to fight. Luke and Kage will not be deterred though as they fight back with all their strength.) Russo: “Reel in the ropes and tie the damn things to the back of the boat. We’ll give this son of a bitch the ride of his life!” (Kage and Luke pull for all their worth as Russo circles the beast to help out in their efforts. Seeing the sight of Kage and Luke, Russo chuckles to himself as the picture fades out to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again where we hear a voice-over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The picture then cuts to a black, blank screen.)
