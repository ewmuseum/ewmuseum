No matter the odds, I will win.

captured 11 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NOTE: The following Role Play in no way expresses the beliefs of the writer, or anyone involved in this Role Play. The manner in which the character’s act in no way dictates the writer’s personal opinions, beliefs, or preferred actions. With that said, enjoy. :: The scene opens up in the Arundel Mills Mall in Maryland, in front of the local EB Games store, where a large crowd is gathering outside. They all are awaiting the store opening, for today is March 24, the launch day for the PlayStation Portable, or PSP. One of the men in this line is the up- and coming superstar for LOW, Mike “Volcano” Blair, who is second in line to get into the store and get his PSP. In front of him is long-time friend Adam Depaolo, known to the wrestling world as Blackman. It is just minutes away from the opening of the store, and everyone is on the edge. :: Adam: Mike, man, I can’t believe it. It’s March 24 man! Today’s the day!!! PSP!!!! Mike: Yeah man, Jesus, I’ve waited so fucking long for this day…I’ve had it reserved for over a year man, it’s finally good to see that $250 is finally going to be worth it. (At this point, there is 3 minutes until the doors open. Suddenly, a large crowd of black people try to cut through the line, instead of just going around. They push and shove their way, but two unlucky black guys make the mistake of pushing Mike into Adam, causing them both to fall to the ground) Mike: hey, assholes! (The two guys turn) Yeah, I’m talking to you. I think you owe me and my friend here (nods towards Adam) an apology now. What do you think, Adam? Adam: I think they need to grovel. Get on their knees and beg for forgiveness now. They almost broke my back, shoving me up against the door like they did. Black Guy #1: Yo, cracker, let me get this straight. You want me to- Mike: (cutting the black guy off) What did you call me? Black Guy #1: I said cracker, bitch. What’s it to you? Mike: My point is, NIGGER, I’m not taking shit from you. (At this, the whole crowd quiets. The owner of EB Games is sitting behind the door, not unlocking the door for fear of the men outside his store brining the fight into his store. Instead, he sits back and watches the scene.) Black Guy #1: (hysterical) WHAT THE FUCK DID YOU JUST SAY!!!! Mike: I believe I said, Nigger. Isn’t that right, Adam? Adam: I heard Nigger. Black Guy #2: Yo, Antoine, let’s kick their asses. Nobody goes racist on me man, not even fuckin’ Bill Clinton. Antoine: Yeah, lets. (Pulls a knife out of his pocket) We’ll show them why they shouldn’t use our word. (A big fight erupts, as Antoine and his friend rush at Mike and Adam, fists raised, and Antoine with a knife gleaming out of his hand. The owner of EB Games goes to the back towards the phone, ready to call the police. The crowd circles around the four men, ready to see the fight begin. The black guy beside Antoine rushes at Adam, who calmly swings his fist right into the side of the black guy’s head, causing the guy to twist in the air like a tornado would. Meanwhile, Antoine rushes at Mike, who, once Antoine gets close enough, reaches out and grabs his arm, then puts him in a half-nelson, with the arm holding the knife still unaccounted for. Antoine swings the knife at Mike’s head, and manages to slit Mike’s cheek ever so slightly. The second Black guy gets up and rushes Adam again, who ducks when he tries to punch him, then kicks him right in the face, sending the black guy onto his back. Mike, feeling the knife cut him, lets go of Antoine, and pats his cheek, feeling the cut spurt out blood. The crowd gasps, seeing the blood drip out of Mike’s cut. Wiping his cheek off with his sleeve, Mike laughs, and tells Antoine he’ll have to do better than a scratch if he wants to prove anything. Angered, Antoine rushes at Mike again, who side-steps and trips Antoine, sending Antoine sprawling face-first into cement. Mike then quickly goes to Antoine’s sprawled out body and stomps on the hand with the knife, causing Antoine to drop the knife and clutch at his now swelling hand. Mike, laughing, looks back towards Adam, who now has just body-slammed his “opponent” into a nearby bench, knocking the guy out cold.) Adam: hurry up Mike, the cops are on their way, we need to split. Mike: Alright, alright, chill, let me just tell this ass- (stops, grimacing in pain) Adam: Mike? Yo, what’s wrong man? (Mike collapses, and Adam runs over. Looking past Mike’s collapsing body, he sees a kneeling Antoine, grinning, pulling the knife out of Mike’s back. Adam gives him a swift kick in the face, sending Antoine sprawling again, before kneeling before Mike’s bleeding body, who now is in bad need of first aid. Adam, lifting Mike, almost drags Mike’s body halfway down the mall, where the nearest first aid station is. Seeing the bloodied body, the first aid attendant radios an ambulance, and tells Adam to put Mike onto a nearby stretcher. Doing as he’s told, Adam snaps his fingers in Mike’s face, which is currently losing facial coloring, and consciousness, all at once. Mike then completely faints, and the scene fades to black.) :: The next scene opens up in hospital room 318A in North Arundel Hospital, which Mike “Volcano” Blair is currently residing in. Sitting around the bed is several of his closest friends; Adam Depaolo, who has been here since the stabbing; Danny Amazing, fellow LOW star and numerous-time tag team partner; and the Punisher, who has had an influence on Volcano since the day he first stepped into the ring. The time is around 7:00 P.M., on March the 25th. Mike is sitting in bed, running his hands over his brand new shiny PSP. :: Danny: Mike, stop fondling it. Get some rest man, you need it. Adam: Yeah, isn’t your guys’s Pay-Per-View for LOW on Sunday? Mike: Yeah, about that…Danny, I’m not sure if I can compete. I mean, I have a fucking hole in my back man, I’m obviously not 100 percent. Pun: Mike, when the hell has that stopped you…or Danny…or any of us, for that matter. Look, you can say what you want, but fact is, the old you would’ve gone out there with a bullet-wound in the head, without any second thoughts…so why the doubts now? Mike: Look guys, this is different…unlike the XuWF, or EWA, everyone in this fed is good, and- Danny: Stop it right there. Don’t fucking act like you’re equal to them. You got screwed on the card, I mean, did any of those other fuckers in your match get to be on television after just one Dark match? Hell No! Are any of them anybodies? They are all nobodies man, except you. You could go in at half strength and win that damn match. Mike: Yeah, but- Danny: But nothing! Look Mike, LOW may be a step up from the last fed we were in, but our skills haven’t changed man, it’s just a change of scenery. Wake the hell up and smell the coffee, man, this match is yours. Take it or leave it, this is your match to win, and your match to lose. Don’t go weak on me now, we haven’t got where we are now by being weak. We’ve gotten here by being the best… Mike: You know what? Danny: What? Mike: You are the cockiest mother fucker I’ve ever known, and a damned horrible sympathizer…but, lucky for me, you’re a damn good friend, and a pain in the ass that won’t go away…You know what, fuck it, get the nurse. If I’m gonna wrestle, I’m gonna need her to stitch me up now, damnit. (everyone just stares) DID YOU FUCKING HEAR ME? GET THE DAMN NURSE!!! (Pun slowly walk out of the room, but Danny and Adam just stare) What the hell are you staring at? Adam: Well…you really should try riddalin. Danny: And you should calm down, the angry guy doesn’t really suit you. Adam: Yeah, you kinda just sounded lame and gay when you shouted. Danny: Not at all how an angry character should sound. Adam: And- Mike: OK, OK, I get it, I get it. I can’t be angry, because I’m gay. (stops) Scratch that. I can’t be angry because I sound gay. Danny: Anyway man, I gotta get back to the arena…you know how it is. Mike: yeah man, alright, you go do that. Tell everyone I’ll be there, and Danny? Danny: yeah? Mike: Do me a favor, and don’t tell anyone about this…you know, how wouldit sound, and all that. Danny: So…tell everyone…got it. (leaves) Adam: Well Mike, I gotta slip outta here too…after all, the ACW’s nothing without me, eh? Mike: Yeah man, tell everyone whats up from me, sorry I couldn’t make it last show. Adam: Aight man, later. (leaves) (Mike lays back, staring off into space…suddenly, the door barges open, making Mike jump. He sees Punisher huffing and puffing, while dragging a nurse into the room. Pun looks around, looking confused) Pun: Where did everybody go? Mike: They left, they have things to do. Pun: I see. Well, I guess I should be- Mike: Heading out, too? Pun: Yeah. Sorry I couldn’t stay longer, but- Mike: You got GWA to deal with. Pun: Stop finishing my thoughts. It’s creepy. Mike: Then leave. Pun: ???. Whatever man, I’m outta- Mike: Here. Pun: Damnit! (he leaves) Mike: Alright, Nurse, stitch me up. Nurse: Yes sir. (As Mike rolls over, he thinks back to his early wrestling times, and how ferocious and fearless he was. As he feels the needle go into his skin, he thinks ahead to his match at Subliminal Dawn, where, against all odds, he will win his match. He just has to, it’s in his blood.)
