We're Back

captured 4 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over message which states... 'The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to a shot of a seemingly bad neighborhood in the heart of downtown Detroit. There is a slight haze that fills the air making the street look almost surreal. Suddenly a car screeches down the street heading straight for the camera only to stop at the last second. Seeing this car seems to strike fear into the hearts of everyone who is doing anything even remotely wrong. The street punks and thugs scramble in fear as the driver’s door of the car swings open. “The Loose Cannon” Jason Kage jumps out of the driver seat wearing a flower print button down shirt tucked into a pair of powder blue bell-bottoms. Around his neck is an oversized gold chain which hangs down to about the mid-chest area where it becomes obscured by a patch of chest hair. Topping off the outfit is a curly black afro that is extremely over exaggerated and a pair of purple sunglasses. Kage quickly takes off the sunglasses as the camera freezes on a shot of his face. With the picture on the screen, an announcer’s voice begins speaking . . .) Announcer: “Jason Kage!” (The frame unfreezes as Kage drops to one knee and whips out a gun. As he does this, he calls out to the people fleeing from the scene.) Kage: “Freeze, Jackass!” (One of the thugs comes at Kage from the side only to be taken down to the ground with a hiptoss. Another one of the many thugs throws a knife at the head of Kage. Seeing it coming, Kage dives under the flying blade into a forward roll. As he comes out of the roll, he points the gun and fires using two hands in a police like fashion. The scene now quickly cuts from Kage to a shot inside of bedroom with a 70's decor of lava lamps, red velvet bed sheets on a water bed, and a leopard print carpet. Also on the water bed is a group of girls wearing brightly colored bikinis. The camera pans around to get a shot of “Superstar” Eric Russo. Russo is wearing a purple leisure suit with a black dress shirt that is only buttoned half way up underneath the jacket. Russo also has a thick gold chain hanging from his neck that ends up being obscured by a patch of thick chest hair much like Kage. The camera shot now pans back to the girls as Russo turns and drops back first onto the bed. As he lands, the screen freezes on a shot of his face. Once again the voice over of an announcer begins to speak. . .) Announcer: “Eric Russo!” (The scene unfreezes as the bikini clad women entangle themselves with Russo. As they do this, Russo speaks.) Russo: “That’s right . . . who’s your Sugar Daddy baby?” (The announcer starts to speak again as the scene begins to fade into a new one.) Announcer: “Star in . . .” (The screen fills with the words “Natural Highs and Falling Stars” printed in thick gold bubble lettering on a black background. The title fades out as a montage of short action clips begins. Kage rolls over the hood of a car with his gun in hand before diving and tackling a faceless thug to the ground . . . Russo knocks out a pimp in a white fur coat with a hard right . . . Kage is in a high speed car chase through downtown Detroit . . . Russo makes a pass at a girl only to be slapped . . . Russo and Kage are ducked behind wooden crates in a shoot out on a waterfront dock . . . a superior officer rises from behind his desk to yell at Russo and Kage for their actions . . . Russo and Kage jump through a glass window just as the building that they were in explodes . . . Kage smiles for a picture that ends up on his ID badge . . . Russo smiles for a shot of his ID badge as well . . . In a fight on the roof of a building high above the city streets, Kage knocks his attacker down and kicks him off the edge of the roof . . . a voluptuous blond woman removes her dress for Russo from a behind shot . . . Russo and the woman fall into bed locked in an embrace. Suddenly the screen goes black. The camera now pans back to reveal that we have been watching the TV screen inside of the Shadow Riders’ Miami beach house. Russo is standing next to the TV with his and by the VCR pressing the eject button. On the couch in front of the TV is “The Loose Cannon” Jason Kage sitting with a big bag of Doritos. Kage looks up at Russo with a look of surprise on his face as he begins to speak.) Kage: “Hey . . . I was watching that!” (Russo looking down at Kage with a glare in his eye responds.) Russo: You can finish it later. First I need to know what’s on the agenda for us this week? (Looking to his partner, a smirk slides across Kage’s face as he responds putting his intentions of watching the movie aside.) Kage: Well it appears that LOW finally booked us in our return to the ring as the Riders. (Happy to hear that they have finally been booked in a match, Russo moves to the couch and takes a seat next to Kage as he responds in an excited fashion.) Russo: Well it’s about friggin’ time. (Allowing a slight chuckle to escape before his words, Kage continues to inform Russo of the situation.) Kage: Tell me about it. Wait though, you haven’t heard the best part about it yet. Guess who the match is against. (Without even a moment of thought, Russo blurts out an answer.) Russo: Knowing LOW, probably Dipsa Epsilon Niner Iota or the Crosshair boys. One of those fodder teams. (Kage grins widely as he responds to Russo’s guess.) Kage: Well . . . (Kage then pauses for a moment as a puzzled look fills his face. He then turns to Russo as he questions his response.) Kage: Wait a second, did you just put a niner in one of those names. (Russo shrugs his shoulders as he answers.) Russo: Maybe, what difference does it make? (Shaking his head at Russo’s guesses from left field, Kage now continues.) Kage: Oh none really, I was just wondering if I heard you right. Anyway, at Realm they have us set to go against the team of Jason Warrior and the LOW World Champion, Jason Ortiz! (Hearing the mention of their opponent’s names sets Russo off into a fit of laughing. Trying his best to contain himself, he turns to Kage as he does his best to speak.) Russo: Pl . . plea . . .please tell me you’re joking. We already ripped through them and as the Metallo Zords no less. Do they really expect Warrior and Ortiz to stand a chance when we’re not holding anything back? (Shaking his head at the mismatch of teams, Kage responds.) Kage: Well they must. Either that or they know Ortiz can’t draw a main event crowd from last weeks ratings and need us to pull in the viewers . . . yet again. (Hearing Kage’s explanation for the match-up seems to strike a chord with Russo as he pushes himself off of the couch in an angered fashion.) Russo: Damn, it’s just like when Meyhu and Belmont were champs. Their supposed to be the flagships of the company yet everything always falls on us. (Seeing Russo’s obvious annoyance over the situation, Kage tries to laugh off the mood with a simple response.) Kage: Well that’s what happens when you’re the top draw of a federation. (Not taking the bait to calm down, Russo continues to push on his frustration.) Russo: Yeah but if they’re gonna keep doing this to us, the least they could do is give one of us a World Title shot one week. I mean just look at our history. I’ve beaten Mike Meyhu on four separate occasions and how many World Title shots have I gotten? One, in two years of us carrying the main event scene I have only got one world title shot! Mike Meyhu has gotten at least five! Two months ago we beat Jason Ortiz yet he was the one getting the World Title shot at the pay-per-view! It’s complete bullshit! When are we going to be given what we deserve!? LOW owes us the world and everything in it! (Fully agreeing with Russo, Kage breaks into his rant.) Kage: I know how you feel. It’s just another part of being a Shadow Rid . . . (Suddenly the front door of the beach house bursts open as Luke Justice enters the room with a 24 pack of Miller Lite under each arm. Closing the door behind him by using his foot, Luke heads over towards the area in which Russo and Kage are sitting. Justice places the cases of beer on the table and quickly rips one open to grab a beer for himself before sitting down on a red leather reclining chair. Now relaxed, Justice begins to speak. Justice: Damn you’re loud Eric. I could hear you all the way down at the street. Oh and by the way, consider yourself lucky. I haven’t gotten a World Title shot yet other then in a clusterfuck. You at least had a singles match. (Sneering, Russo responds.) Russo: Don’t even start man. At least you’re entered in that King of the Death Match tournament. Where was the invitations for Kage and me? Taken by Pax and Jason Warrior that’s where. (Putting his hand up to stop Russo, Justice cuts in.) Justice: You don’t have to go preaching to the converted man. I know the Riders have been getting the raw deal since day one in LOW. I’ll tell you what though, when I become King of the Deathmatch and then take that World Title, I’ll give you a shot at it. (Hearing Justice’s promise to Russo, Kage perks up.) Kage: What about me? What about the Loose Cannon? (Justice looks to with a small snicker as he responds.) Justice: I’ll give you a shot too. As long as it’s in the family, it’s all good. (Getting away from the topic of actually having to defend his title once he wins it, Justice quickly changes the subject.) Justice: Anyway, where’s Brody? (Hearing this question, Kage shakes his head and waves his hands in front of himself as he responds.) Kage: Don’t ask me, I asked her where she was off to the other night and she nearly took my head off for it. I learned my lesson then. (Seeing a chance to get a word in, Russo quickly interjects himself.) Russo: She’s a friggin’ psycho, but it’s better to have her with us then against us. (Nodding his head in agreement, Justice echos the statement.) Justice: Amen to that brother. (Getting a little antsy just sitting around the house, Kage speaks up.) Kage: So what’s on the plan for tonight? (With a smile, Russo shoots out his idea for the night’s plan.) Russo: Well I was thinking we’d maybe head down to the club for old times sake. First round is on me. (Never ones to turn down a free drink, Kage and Justice quickly pop to their feet and begain heading for the door as they respond.) Justice: Sounds like a plan to me. Kage: I’m up for it. (Pushing himself off the couch as well, Russo speaks.) Russo: Let’s do it then. (As the three Riders open the front door and exit the house the scene begins to fade out. Before it does however we hear Russo’s voice one last time as he closes the door behind him.) Russo: Watch out ladies cause the Shadow Riders are back and in full effect. Oh and that’s not ego talking, that’s reality. (With that the picture fades out as the screen once again fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over message which states . . . 'Life may end but legends never die.”. The screen now fades to black.)
