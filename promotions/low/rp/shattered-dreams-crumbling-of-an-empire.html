Shattered Dreams- Crumbling of an Empire

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. Slowly the logo fades out to black. For a moment, nothing. Then the sound of Russo’s voice speaks out as he launches into a voice over.) Russo (VO): “So I suffered a minor set back. Big deal, I was rich now and as everyone knows the rich always win. Besides, I wasn’t gonna loose all of this money that I had amassed over one simple law suit. No fair and impartial jury would do that to someone. It’s not like people hated me as much as Jesse Jamester. Yeah right . . .” (The scene opens to a shot inside of a court house where Eric Russo’s trial is taking place. By the looks of the judge and jury, we can tell that this trial is going to be far from fair. The judge and all the members of the jury seem to have encountered the same effects as Mr. Daniels did from the Shadow Riders’ cartoon. This can’t be good for Russo. The foreman of the jury rises to his feet as he begins to deliver the jury’s verdict.) Jury Foreman: “Your honor, we the jury find for the plaintiff.” (With a smile, the judge begins to speak.) Judge: “In that case, I award to Mr. Daniels and the other 9,987,652 plaintiffs the full amount of the suit. Court is adjourned.” (As the words leaves the judge’s mouth, we can hear Russo’s young female companion call to him frantically from off screen.) Young Woman: “Eric!” (The camera pans over to Russo who is busily guzzling down a flask of whiskey. Hearing his name, he breaks himself from his drink and harshly responds.) Russo: “Honey, can't you see I'm drinking?” (The scene in the courtroom fades out as a voice over by Russo begins.) Russo (VO): “Well this was defiantly a harsh blow to my new found happiness. Just like in LOW, once you have something everyone else wants it. I could get through this though. I’ve gotten through a lot worse. After all, I did have to put up with Jason Kage for seven years. This was going to be a walk in the park compared to that. Or so I thought . . .” (The scene opens in Russo’s study where he sits behind a finely polished oak desk. He is busy at work writing checks to pay off his law suit. Not to mention that he is still drinking as well.) Russo: “Pay to the order of Mrs. Willamina Jenkins, one dollar and nine cents!” (Russo places the check in an envelope and licks it closed.) Russo: “Pay to the order of Brass Balls Bucci, one dollar and nine cents!” (Russo’s young female companion walks into the room crying, wearing the dress she wore the day she met Russo. Seeing this, Russo comments.) Russo: “Why are you crying and why are you wearing that old dress?” Young woman: “Because I just heard a song on the radio that reminded me of the way we were.” (Curious, Russo responds.) Russo: “What was it?” (The young woman replies through her sobs.) Young Woman: ““The Way We Were”. I get it. We've hit bottom.” (Russo responds in a spirited manner.) Russo: “No! Maybe you've hit bottom, but I haven't hit bottom yet! I got a ways to go. And I'm gonna bounce right back up when I do. And when that happens, I'm gonna buy you a diamond so big it's gonna make you puke!” (Still sobbing, the girl responds.) Young Woman: “I don't wanna puke! I don't want wealth! I just want you like you used to be when I met you one week ago! What happened to that man?” (We can tell by his voice as he responds that Russo is now looking for an argument.) Russo: “Me? What happened to the girl I believed in to partner with in a dart game? The girl I fell in love with at the bar? The girl that believed in me to hit a bulls eye on the last throw? Well, there's plenty of places I can go where people believe in me!” (Angry at Russo’s comments the young girl replies.) Young Woman: “Well go! The sooner you're out of my life, the sooner I can go back to being the girl in the little flowered dress that you picked up in that bar.” (Infuriated, Russo responds.) Russo: “Well I'm gonna to go then! And I don't need any of this! I don't need this stuff . . .” (Russo pushes all of the letters off the desk as he gets to his feet.) Russo: “And I don't need you. I don't need anything except this . . .” (Russo picks up a paperweight off his desk and begins moving towards the front door.) Russo: “And that's it and that's the only thing I need, is this. I don't need this or this. Just this paperweight.” (Russo now picks up a Gameboy game with an LOW game stuck inside that is laying on a table.) Russo: “And this Gameboy, the paperweight and the Gameboy game and that's all I need.” (Russo now spots a wooden back scratcher and picks it up off the couch.) Russo: “And this back scratcher. The paperweight, the Gameboy game and the back scratcher, and that's all I need.” (Russo stops once again to pick up a small book of postal stamps from a small ceramic change dish.) Russo: “And these stamps. The paperweight, and these stamps, and the back scratcher and the Gameboy.” (Russo now spots a fancy designer scarf on top of a table. He quickly picks it up.) Russo: “And this scarf. The paperweight, this Gameboy and the back scratcher and the scarf and that's all I need. And that's all I need too. I don't need one other thing, not one . . . I need this!” (Russo latches onto a Lay-Z-Boy chair. He adds the chair to his small collection of items as he drags it along behind him.) Russo: “The Gameboy, and the Lay-Z-Boy, and the back scratcher, and the stamps, for sure. Well what are you looking at? What do you think I am, some kind of a jackass or something? And this!” (Russo picks up a Playboy magazine as well as he continues to the front door.) Russo: “And that's all I need. The paperweight, the back scratcher, the Gameboy, this Playboy and the Lay-Z-Boy.” (The scene follows Russo through the front door of his house and onto the lawn. We listen in as Russo continues.) Russo: “And I don't need one other thing except my dog.” (Russo goes to pick up a small dog on the lawn but it growls and bares it’s teeth.) Russo: “Well I don't need my dog.” (The screen flashes with various scenes of Russo as he travels along, carrying his back scratcher, his Gameboy, his stamps, his scarf, his Lay-Z-Boy, his Playboy, but not his dog. A voice over begins.) Russo (VO): “Well here I am, back at square one. My new found riches left me just as quick as they came. I thought I finally had it made but as usual, everything falls through. At least I got to keep some of the things from my time as a rich man. Only question now is what was I gonna do with them . . .” (Eventually, Russo spots a woman carrying a Shadow Riders’ t-shirt. He trades everything he has for the shirt. As the scene begins to fade, we hear Russo’s voice one last time.) Russo (VO): “It’s time for me to go back to making a name for myself the old fashioned way. Well I guess I could always get my joy from smacking Jamester around like usual. The more things change, the more they stay the same.” (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
