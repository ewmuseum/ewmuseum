A Grave Mistake!

captured 2 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~ 1…2… and know we are reaching 3 right? So Demond you think that 3rd times a charm huh? You think that since you lost the people in your life that were holding you back and rejoined up with your brother that now you have the skills needed to beat me? Don’t make me life! The first time that I beat you….you were so called in your prime, the second time I beat you was because of the aide of another…..but know the epic confrontation between two of the biggest giants in our industry is about to happen…..again! I’ve been in LOW for a while and so far there has been no one that has been able to stand in my way! Max, Tessa, D.U.I., Enigma, Cobi, and even you Demond have yet to meet up with the challenge! Is it because of the emotions you feel for this Ami that holds you back? What holds you back from being the monster I know that you can be? And by the way in regards to how extreme you can get……I by far can get a lot worse….after all, I BEAT YOU DIDN’T I! ~ ~ Day One- A Call to Rebuild! ~ {The alarm inside the Three Million dollar home woke the sleeping giant with a violent jolt. The bedroom of the now double champion was in a confusing disarray of trash and disorganization. The champion had a restless night of constant thought……he just got word from Juvian that “The Christian Beast” Max Roswell was having his very last match this Realm. “Well there goes another member of S.I.N.!” he thought as he got up off of his bed and headed into the shower. The shower had a relaxing feel to it as he let his mind wonder about the upcoming match of epic proportions.} - The hour of truth comes to reality as the General watches as his troops become weak…. - ~ The Phone Rings! ~ John Adams (Answering the phone): Hello! ..... Well I haven’t heard from you in a while…. Yes…. Yeah I’ll meet with you tomorrow Chapel! {Chapel….a name that hasn’t been heard from since the last days of EPW, he was the world champion there and an undefeated one at that. But the question was, what did he want with Adams? What was his motive in calling the man that he has never beaten, did the whole motive behind it was some kind of trick for a rematch? Well all of these questions will be answered tomorrow. John Adams goes towards his bed and turns on the TV.} - So close to his dreams being completed the desperate leader is even willing to make a pact with the forces of darkness…. - TV: “Tonight on Entertainment Tonight we will look at the wrestlers of yesterday, focusing mainly on the federation EPW. We will look at the Champion, Chapel and another who has his own dojo called “The Khaotic Corner” Khaos!” {John Adams turns off the television…..”My Brother?” he thought as memories of his past with his brother start to flood through. He was a man conceived by John Adams father and some Mexican girl. He was also the man who was left everything by Adams father when he died. Yet they still looked past there differences and remained friends, competitors, rivals, and brothers. “Looks like I have another place to go after my meeting with Chapel.” He thought as he settled back to his bed. He had a lot to contemplate, Chapel, Khaos, and the future of LOW as a whole…….how do these things tie in together, wait till tomorrow to find out.} - They abide showing him two more…. - ~ Day 2- Meeting with an old nemesis! ~ {The scene was late at night near a local pub in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. The air reeked of mildew from the dampness in the air. The pub is a causal place unlike most of the bars in this town. When John Adams walks into the pub in his leather he automatically gets the impression that he is out of place. There is defiantly money here as everyone here inside is in some kind of suit. He heads towards the back past the bar and old Irish business men. In the back of the bar is four men sitting around a table with over four thousand dollars in the middle……it was a poker game, and one of the gentlemen with red hair was the ever charismatic Chapel.} - Two with the power to move mountains….. - Chapel: Well boys and girls, looks like lady luck is smiling on me again! {Chapel ends up taking the pot with the rest of the players sulking where they sit. After a while of grown men pouting like when they were young the back room clears out, leaving Chapel and John Adams alone in the room.} - So with what’s left the General rebuilds….. - Chapel: Well mate, you look good. John Adams: Why don’t you cut the crap Chapel and get to the point! Chapel: That’s what I always liked about you Adams, no chit chat just straight to the point! John Adams: Of course, that’s what gave me the win over you all of the time! Chapel: That was a fluke; lady luck was just not on my side then, but enough about the past Adams I’m here to talk about the future! LOW has been treating you good Adams, you and Rush and me and an associate of mine want in! Now if it was up to me lad it would be for the honor of facing you again to get my victory over you, but I am a gambling man and what I propose is a game of chance! John Adams: Fine, I’m game but who’s your associate? Out of the darkness: Well hello brother, it’s been too long! {A man comes out of the darkness with a silvery white hair and piercing eyes that told the story of the hell and carnage that he has felt.} - As his advisories get stronger….. - John Adams: Well Khaos, I was wondering when I was going to cross paths with you again! Khaos: Yes, you have made a lot of headlines brother with quite the record under your belt. Undefeated, Realm Champion and now one half of the tag champions, impressive it truly is! But I ask you dear brother; do you ever wonder when your luck is going to run out? John Adams: If you are implying about my match against The Demond then no! I beat him not only once but twice! So facing him for a third time is not going to make much of a difference to me! Chapel: Well it does to us lad that is the reason for this game of chance! You see when you go into this match with him your not only putting that belt and your reputation as a dominating wrestler at stake, but your also putting our reputation and EPW’s also! Even though EPW is closed down, you John “The Prophet” Adams still have it in you inside! So it’s simple we both draw one card each, highest card wins! John Adams: Wins what? Khaos: Well if we win, then since you my brother have a dominating record on both of us….. Then you owe us a rematch handicap style! Chapel: But if you win, then consider us two of your newest members of S.I.N. and a part of LOW! {So cautiously John Adams agrees to the game…..but the outcome? What about the out come of it?} - So does the General, with The Prophecy as his guide….. - ~ Day 3- Assembly of a new team! ~ {At a remote abandon warehouse in the middle of the day we see Juvian walking inside. He went here due to a phone call from John Adams earlier on. The Warehouse used to be a shipping company for medical supplies for all of West Philadelphia.} - The Prophecy is about complete with these numbers there is a lot of strength….. - Juvian Ramirez (inside of the warehouse.): John, I’m here! Voice out of the darkness: Juvian, let me be the first to introduce to you the team that will take LOW and shake it from the very foundation in which it was built from! {With that said Khaos and Chapel come out of the darkness to introduce themselves to Juvian. Then following that, comes John Adams after.} - The battle field is set and the pieces to this chess game are now in place….. - John Adams: The new S.I.N. the prophecy is now complete as soon as Donny realizes the fact that his brother is only holding him back, and for The Demond……. Chapel: Don’t worry; from the look of things I don’t like his chances! Khaos: Yes I must admit, with the four of us together things will defiantly get Khaotic! {As the rest of the team strategizes about the future of LOW, John Adams is seen with a wicked grin on his face….”Yes, with this team there will be no one to stand in our way now!” he thinks to himself.} - One man’s madness will soon be everyone’s reality…. That is the way of The Prophecy! - ~ FIN ~
