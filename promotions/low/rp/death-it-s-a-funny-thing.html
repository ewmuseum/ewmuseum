Death...it's a funny thing...

5 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I took my first footsteps, or were the footsteps? My feet made contact with something, but it certainly wasn’t the floor. I looked down and there was seemingly nothing there, but I could feel a strange sensation on my feet. The white of the room was blinding, I squinted to try and ease the pain on my eyes. Then, soothing music flowed into the room, travelling to my ears easing all of my pain. I closed my eyes for a brief moment, when I re-opened them I felt another presence in the room. Cyrus Skar. The presence spoke without speaking; its words were heard even though no sound waves were created. The shrouded hood covered most of its face, I could just make out its glowing blue balls of light that one may have called eyes. For the first time in my life I started to shake, I was starting to realize where I was. Ah yes, you’re a special case one person with two souls. A person like that is not destined to live for a long time. You however, are the youngest I’ve ever met; I understand it was because of circumstance though. I had no idea what he was talking about, but I presumed I was dead. The scariest thing was I couldn’t remember how I had died. I tried to explore my memory, then something hit me. Before I awoke here I felt pain. A mass of great pain. But that was all I could remember. I see you can’t remember how you died. You got killed Cyrus, how does not matter now, but you got killed. You’re dead; somebody else smashed your hourglass. They took away a big part of your life, I had you down for quite a few more years, I hate it when people get killed it messes up my plans so much… I couldn’t believe what I was hearing. Death was stood in front of me complaining about people getting killed. He didn’t understand how annoyed I was at being killed never mind him! This whole situation felt so surreal, I didn’t want to accept I was dead. Death laughed. I thought he laughed, in reality he didn't even move. You don't want to accept the fact your dead, this happens nearly every time. If only I had a skull every time somebody said that, I'd have…a lot of skulls. What I am doing? I'm mumbling on again, it's a terrible habit I have. So, Mr. Skar, you're ready to go and be judged now. I've done my bit in bringing you here, it's time for you to go to the gates. I don't know what came over me, I walked over to Death and swung for him. I put all my might into that one punch. I didn't want to die, I was so young, I had so much more to do in my life. I had the love of my life back. Everything had started to go right for me, then this. This shit. I didn't need this, I had nothing to lose. Why shouldn't I punch Death? He couldn't do much to me could he? I'm already dead… Cyrus you fail to realise this is my realm, we play by my rules here. Don't make me put in a bad word for you with Peter now, he and I go way back. One little word from me could have you spending an eternity in Hell and we wouldn't want that now would we? I ignored him, I carried on with the punch, I was inches from his glowing eyes when my arm snapped back. How I don't know, it must have been him. I felt my bone crunch, I saw it stick out from my skin, I recoiled in pain. Pain? Why was I feeling pain? I thought I was dead. You may not be able to die again Mr. Skar but you can still feel pain. Let that be a warning to you, believe me you're not the first to think you shouldn't be here. Accept it so we can hurry this up. I have an appointment in New York; a prostitute has just been killed by the guy who hired her. She's waiting for me, so get your ass moving. My arm fell numb, I looked at it again and it was healed. It was he, he could manipulate me any way he saw fit. I had to do what he said, the pain I felt before was almost unbearable. I got to my feet still clutching my arm and followed him through a set of white stairs. We kept travelling for what seemed like hours, but time had no meaning here. Time just wasn't. We reached the top and I saw two gold gates stood before me. Nothing seemingly holding them up. In front of them was a man. He looked like any ordinary man, but he wore a white robe. He looked at me and smiled. Death was gone. 'So Mr. Skar it seems there is a certain matter of your judging to take place now.' Peter's voice was calm, he actually spoke unlike Death. He seemed at least a little human, I had always imagined what it would be like in Heaven. Never had I imagined this. I looked at Peter who stared back with his green eyes, he looked at his palms and a book appeared. He opened the pages and I saw the title of it. 'Cyrus Skar' it was like 'This Is Your Life' I felt so feeble and insignificant. 'So it says here you lived on the street for a few years, beating people up, stealing things. Though you have done some good in your life. Saved a few people's lives here and there, that sort of thing. You've done especially well considering your illness. Though in my opinion Cyrus you've done more bad than good with your time. I'm afraid to say you're not going any further this way…' I couldn't believe it, I wouldn't believe it. I couldn't be condemned to Hell. I was a good person deep down. Surely Peter could see that, he could see I meant to do good, I didn't mean to hurt anyone. But the fact was I had hurt people, I did little good. Then it sunk in; there was no going back no. This was it, I had to accept my punishment for the life I'd led. So it's time Cyrus. Come on, Satan's waiting. He knew you'd be coming sooner or later, we don't want to keep the Devil waiting now do we? Death was back. He stood beside me, this time scythe in hand. He beckoned me to walk over to a hole next to him with his bones for fingers. I slowly walked over to the hole, then I felt him push me in, downwards I fell. Then I stopped. ---------- I woke up from my slumber, perspiration dripping down my forehead. I looked at the time, it was still technically night. Had that all been a dream? Or was it a premonition?
