The Beginning...

captured 8 December 2004

Unsoundness of mind sufficient in the judgment of a civil court to render a person unfit to maintain a contractual or other legal relationship or to warrant commitment to a mental health facility. The ray of light that poked its way through the mini blinds flashed against his eyelid. It was more like a gentle poke in the eyeball. As gentle as a poke in the eyeball could be, Myles made a failed attempt to brush the light away. His newfound pest continued its minor assault on his eyelid. He finally got enough sense of himself to roll his face away from the light, but as always happens having enough sense causes one to break out of sleep. Which was the case with Crux… it was time to rise from the burial plot, as Jack had been calling it. He sat up, rubbing the sleep away from the corners of his eyes. Trying to salvage some saliva to wet his tongue, he opened and closed his mouth like a cow chewing on a very tasty cud. Swinging his legs over the side of the bed, he placed one foot down on the chilly hardwood floor. This sent a quick shiver up his spine into his cerebral cortex, making his body quiver momentarily. “I really need some slippers,” he muttered to himself. Chuckling to himself a moment, it’s not everyday you hear yourself say slippers, he thought. Looking at the digital clock sitting on the nightstand, he made out a blurry ten and what looked like seventeen. He pulled the rest of his body from his warm tomb of blankets. The wood beneath him creaked and groaned as his slow steps brought him to the bedroom door. As he pulled the door open he could hear a faint thud, followed by a short silence, then another faint thud. Jack must be cutting more wood, his mind assured him. Getting to his destination he pushed shut the bathroom door behind himself. As he glanced in the mirror, he noticed his scraggly hair. He brushed the hair from his face. He looked closer, looking deep into his own eyes. He wasn’t sure what he saw there, the pent-up anger…the face was there… “The face…” his voice trailed off. His eyelids slammed shut as another chill ran up his spine. The fear returned…he thought it was over…he thought he was past this...his life finally had some balance. An adult male who didn’t feel the need to show his dominance… A warm bed to where he could finally have a deep sleep… Someone who actually gave a damn about him… The face would never go away… The one boogey monster that has haunted him, the one thing he could not run away from… he couldn’t hold his eyes closed any longer… He couldn’t recognize the contorted face in the mirror he let out a hellish scream and in one motion punched the mirror. The screech and shattering glass startled Jack, who was in mid-swing with his axe. He dropped the axe and bolted to the door, swinging the door open he moved quickly through the kitchen and into the bathroom. There sat his young protégé, against the bathtub in the fetal position, blood dribbling from his right hand. Jack’s head turned to see the large mirror with an intricate spider-web crack spread from the middle outward. “Are you alright, kid?” Jack said to him…Myles looked up, his cheeks wet from tears… his eyes filled with sheer terror… “Let’s go.” Jack said in the calmest possible voice. Another hospital, Myles thought… Another doctor… Another person to tell him how crazy he is… He thought it was over… but it was just the beginning…
