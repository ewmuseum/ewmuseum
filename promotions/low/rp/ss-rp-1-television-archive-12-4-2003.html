SS RP 1 Television Archive - 12.4.2003

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cyrus sat on his bed watching the flicker of the lamp above his head. He turned to face the television and picked up the remote next to him. He turned the television on to one of the sports channels. A program about LOW was on. Cyrus’s ears pricked up and he looked at the television with interest. A man was sat at a desk with a woman next to him. They were both wearing gray suits with white shirts. “So Mary who do you think will win the Gauntlet match at Shell Shock?” Asked the man. “Well Kent, I don’t really know. It’s quite an open ball game. There are a few old faces and few new faces in this match. It’s going to be unpredictable. I mean on one hand we have Mark Devlin, Seth Araya etc all the new talent just starting off in LOW, then on the other hand we have Canyon Thunders and Chris Wrestling who’ve been around a while. I mean it’s going to be very close. All the men in the match are about the same ability and all have the talent to be the Aftermath Champion.” Mary spoke as though she knew what she was talking about. “Interesting.” Replied Kent, preparing to take Mary’s argument apart. “I think there’s one man that has a slight advantage.” “Oh really?” Mary spoke in a tone almost begging to know who this ‘man’ could be. “Cyrus Skar.” Kent waited for Mary’s response. “Cyrus Skar? Are you joking?” Mary started to produce a laugh that was faker than Pamela Anderson’s chest. “I’m being deadly serious.” Came the solemn reply from Kent. “Cyrus Skar hasn’t been in LOW for very long. I’ll admit that. But Cyrus Skar has been around PTC for quite some time now. He’s showed time and time and again he can pull out impressive victories when he wants. Look at his LOW record. At this moment in time he’s 2-0-0. He’s not got one loss on his record yet.” Mary interrupts him. “Yes but his match against Chris Wrestling hasn’t aired yet. He might have lost that.” Mary was trying to regain some pride, but Kent was not finished taking her argument apart. “Even if he doesn’t beat Chris Wrestling, he’ll still have a lot of momentum going into Shell Shock. He’ll have the confidence in himself. He’s already beaten two of the nine men in the match. I mean what more does he need to prove? He didn’t just get an easy pin either, he beat them both decisively.” Kent finishes speaking now, happy with destroying Mary’s argument. “That’s just your point of view Kent…” Mary tried to change the subject. “Onto the next match on the card…” At this point Cyrus had heard enough. He switched the television off and smiled. He actually had some support from the wrestling journalists. It made a chance. Normally Cyrus wasn’t given any support. He was always the wrestler who was unknown. Then when he did win, he’d still not get any recognition. He was glad that finally some people were taking note of his ability. It’d taken long enough. “Somebody actually thinks you might win your match Cyrus.” The voice had returned. “It certainly looks that way doesn’t it.” Cyrus was feeling confident with himself and this showed when he spoke. “I mean just think, if this had of been a year ago, they’d have been calling you the unknown warrior and playing you down. Man, how times change.” The voice was speaking with a slight hint of sarcasm. “Have you got a point to make or are you just trying to annoy me?” Cyrus was starting to get angry at the voice’s small talk. “Can I not have a proper conversation with you any more Cyrus?” The voice laughed. “I just thought I’d wish you luck on your match. It’s your first big opportunity in LOW, you’re not going to let it slip through your fingers now are you?” “Of course not.” Said Cyrus, not sounding too convincing. “Now that didn’t sound very confident. That didn’t sound like a man who was going to dominate in his first PPV outing for LOW. That didn’t sound like a fierce warrior.” Replied the voice, almost mocking Cyrus. “Shut up!” Shouted an enraged Cyrus. “I’m getting sick and tired of you sticking your nose in where it’s not wanted.” “So you don’t want my help come Shell Shock?” Asked the voice. “How can you help me? I don’t need you for this one…” Muttered Cyrus. “Oh you don’t?” The voice laughed. “When you’re in the final few, you think you can’t go on any more. You’re feet feel like they’re cast in stone, you feel like there’s no oxygen left in the air around you, you fall to the floor and find it hard to get up. What happens then? You give up? It’s over?” “No…” Said Cyrus was wondering where the voice was going with this. “But it would be if you don’t embrace me Cyrus. I’m that little source of energy you’ll need to keep you going in that match. I’ll be there cheering you on, coaching you all the way. I’ll be there making sure you don’t do anything stupid. I’ll be there making sure you win. You need me Cyrus. I’m here to make you the best damn wrestler here in LOW. Can’t you see that?” The voice awaits Cyrus’s answer. Cyrus laughs. “Maybe you’re right. Maybe all the time I’ve been trying to do the right thing, when that’s not what has been needed.” Cyrus pauses for a moment. “Then again, should I go against morals and embrace my evil side?” Cyrus pauses again and thinks for a few seconds before carrying on. “Evil or good?” “So now I’m evil am I?” The voice laughs. “ Cyrus, it’s in your nature to be evil. It’s how you were brought up. Don’t try and deny it.” “Once again you’re right. I keep finding myself agreeing with you. I’ve got to let you in. Open the door, let you take control of my soul, but…but…” Cyrus tries to get his final sentence out. “What…what…what if it goes wrong?” “It won’t go wrong Cyrus.” The voice spoke in a calm reassuring tone. “I’ll make sure of it.” “I think back and wonder how did I ever cope without you by my side? How did I manage to survive myself, whilst fighting against you within? I know I’m a strong person mentally, but I’ve always had to deal with more than others.” Cyrus speaks with confusion and perplexity in his voice. “Oh Cyrus, don’t kid yourself! You’re the strongest person mentally because of me. You adapted to the situation. You learnt to be so mentally strong because of me, the threat. I was a threat to you when you were younger and didn’t understand me, the barrier you put up was very strong. It took me years to break it down. But now you realize I didn’t do that to hurt you, I did it to help you. You’re strong minded because of me Cyrus, if I wasn’t here you would have never adapted and became so strong minded.” The voice finishes and pauses allowing Cyrus to take all of this in. “Now, let’s focus. The most important thing now is Shell Shock. We need to get you training ready for your big match. It’s not going to be a walk in the park like your last few matches Cyrus. This one is like some of the older battles you’ve been in. Now it’s been a long time since you’ve been in a match that stretches you. Do you think you’re ready?” Cyrus laughs. “I was bon ready.” “That’s what I like to here.” The voice laughs as well. “For now Cyrus, I must go. I’ll be back later. Go. Start training. We’re going to win this together. I can feel it. Ciao.” With that the voice returned to the depths of Cyrus’s soul.
