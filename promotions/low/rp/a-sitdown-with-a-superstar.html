A Sitdown with a Superstar

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over message which states... “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to a shot in the lobby of a large New York hotel. You can tell that the hotel is rather fancy due to the marble floors and gold trim that outlines virtually every object from the floor to the ceiling. A pair of glass doors at the front of the hotel slide open as “Superstar” Eric Russo steps into the lobby. He is still wearing his suit from the last time we saw him. As Russo continues through the lobby, he spots the cameraman crouched by a circle of couches. Seeing this, Russo approaches him in a calm manner and begins speaking.) Russo: “For once I’m actually glad to see you.” (Nervously the cameraman responds.) Cameraman: “Really?” Russo: “Yeah, on my way here I had a chance to catch Cade’s latest promo and I’ve got quite a bit to say about it. I just have to make a quick phone call first.” (With that Russo pulls out his phone from his jacket pocket and flips it open. He presses a button and raises the phone to his ear. After a few moments, Russo begins speaking as if someone has picked up on the other end of the line.) Russo: “Yo . . . I’ll be up in a few minutes. I wanna cut a promo on Cade down here first. I’ll talk to you in a few. Later.” (Russo now hangs his phone up by snapping it shut once again. He then places it back in his pocket as he takes a seat on one of the couches across from the cameraman. Once he is settled, he begins speaking to the cameraman.) Russo: “Is that thing ready?” (The camera shakes in a yes motion.) Russo: “Good, count me down.” (The cameraman begins his count as Russo prepares himself to begin. As the count reaches zero, Russo looks deep into the camera and begins speaking.) Russo: “Cade, I see that you now like to talk about decisions. I see that you like to tell everyone how every decision that they make can have a huge impact on their lives. Well no shit Cade. What the hell do you want for that astute observation of yours? A frigging cookie! Do you think that you are some kind of genius because you have finally figured this out? Well your not Cade, your statement is one of common knowledge. What you do not see though is that you yourself have made one of those very life changing decisions when you signed the dotted line to step into a junkyard with me.” (Russo pauses for a moment as he adjusts himself in his seat. He then continues.) Russo: “You see Cade, the decision to sign up for that match will be one of the last that you make here in Lights Out. It is one that will leave you wondering for the rest of eternity why you didn’t choose a different path for your life. It will leave you wondering why you didn’t become a lawyer . . . doctor . . . policeman . . . firefighter . . . construction worker . . . office worker . . . technician . . . or so on. It will leave you wondering why you didn’t just take the easy way out this time. Because believe me when I say that you will have ample time to consider the answers to those questions after I am through with you. Why you ask, because a road through me is one that will not have a prosperous end. In fact, you could say that a road through me has a dead end.” (Russo tilts his head back and smiles to himself. He then returns his attention to the camera as he continues.) Russo: “It doesn’t matter what you say or do, because nothing will change this. I can’t believe that it hasn’t sunk in yet. Your background doesn’t matter. Neither does the months you spent in rehab after your knee injury. I don’t really give two shits about any of that because none of it matters. In fact, the only thing that matters in our match is me. I am the one that the people are coming to see. I am the draw that puts our match on the marquee. The sooner you realize that the better off you will be.” (Russo draws himself in closer to the camera as he continues.) Russo: “I don’t see why you are still denying the inevitable Cade. Why do you find it so hard to face the facts? Each time I hear you talk, your story changes. Just yesterday you said how this sport is a business about making money. Today it’s not a business about money, it’s a sport. Make up your damn mind you jackass. Which one is it. Pick one or the other and stop trying to back talk your way out of the situation. It’s not gonna work, all your backtracking is doing nothing but pissing me off more. You and your beliefs are a crock of shit Cade and this is the true fact behind the matter. It is people like you that ruin my business and a business it is. If you are trying to fool yourself into thinking it’s not, well then I already have you beat. You’re on the ropes already and I’m not stopping until you’re out. I’ve ripped through far better then you on my worst day Cade and on Saturday I will be nowhere near my worst.” (Russo sits back once again and crosses his left leg over his right. He then continues.) Russo: “Do I place myself above you? Yes Cade, I do. I place myself above you because I am above you. You are a disgrace to the people who deserve to be at the top. You are handed chances to excel on a silver platter yet all you do is cast them aside as if they were nothing more then a torn and tattered shirt. I do not see you as a threat because you are not one Cade. I see you for what you are and nothing more. I see you as a waste. A waste of talent, a waste of space, and most importantly a waste of my time.” (Russo pauses for a moment as if he is pondering something. He then continues.) Russo: “You are not worth my time Cade which leaves me to wonder why I am still talking to you. It leaves me to wonder why I am spending my time in front of this camera instead of attending to more pressing matters. I guess I could attribute it to the fact that I loath you Cade. However childish and uncivilized it may sound to you, I hate you Cade. Now I know for a fact that my good friend Jason Kage wants a piece of you, but I just may have to disappoint him this time Cade. This time he may not get what he wants because after I am through with you, there will be nothing left.” (Russo uncrosses his legs and draws in closer to the camera once again as he takes on a more angered tone. He continues.) Russo: “You see Cade, you have single handedly pushed me from my plateau. You have inspired me to climb even higher up the mountain. You have lit that fire that at one point burned so brightly inside of my soul. A fire that had dimmed to nothing more then a burning ember as I became more and more complacent in my surroundings. I thank you for that Cade. I thank you and I promise to you here and now that I will not fail as the Nazis did. Oh no Cade, I will burn you with that fire and I will make sure that nothing more then a pile of ash is left.” (As he calms down once again, Russo continues.) Russo: “Your challenge is one that I will not turn away Cade. In fact it is one that I will accept with great pleasure. Pleasure in the fact that I will have you all to myself. Pleasure that I will be the one making you cry out in pain as all the others watch on wishing that they were the ones in my position. Pleasure in the sight of watching you fall upon your knees and grovel at my feet. Begging and pleading for me to end the pain. Asking me to show you mercy as I redefine your definition of hell. Your dreams will not save you from that fate Cade. They will give you no solace in you hour of despair. For the only thing that will end your torment Cade is me. Me when I tire of you screams.” (Russo now sits back in his seat once again as he continues.) Russo: “So Cade compare me to the wars of the past. Do whatever it takes to prepare yourself for the carnage you are about to come face to face with. I want you to be as comfortable as possible before you face me Cade, because for you there will be no allied forces. There will be no mistakes. No, for you all there will be is me. Me and a world of pain and suffering. A world that you will never return from. So sleep well Cade. Rest up before our battle. You’ll need it. (Russo tilts his head back taking his eyes off the camera as he continues.) Russo: “I am not laughing at you Cade. Normally I would be, but not this time. This time, I only feel sorry for you. Sorry for the fact that I am going to do to you what you truly deserve. Sorry for the fact that when it all comes to an end your reality will be shattered. It will Cade, and once it is there will be no return to the state of mind that you once prided yourself on.” (Russo now returns his attention to the camera as he continues.) Russo: “As for me being the one who has everything to lose, I don’t know where you get that idea. Don’t get me wrong, I have no doubts in myself being able to end you, but lets just say for a second that I do lose. Tell me, how will that effect me? I am nothing more then one half of a “tag team” while you are the almighty ape god himself, Cade.” (Russo moves in closer as he continues.) Russo: “What do I have to lose? Tell me Cade, what can I lose? Am I gonna be disgraced when I lose to the first ever world champion of Lights Out? Is it gonna look bad if I get taken out by a man who was main eventing the pay-per-view a few days ago? Answer my questions Cade, you seem to have all the answers so just tell me . . . what do I have to lose? I’ll tell you exactly what I have to lose, nothing.” (Russo now relaxes himself back into his seat as he continues.) Russo: “If anything, it will give me a release. A release from all of your psycho babble bullshit that you seem to spew out time and again in a never ending stream. So if anything, I stand to gain from the loss don’t I? There is nothing that you can do to me Cade. There are only things that I can do to you. Things that I will take joy in doing. Things that you will never forget. Things that you will never recover from again.” (As Russo finishes his sentence, his cell phone begins to ring. He quickly reaches into his pocket and pulls it out. As he flips it open, he raises it to his ear. We can hear an u8nclear voice on the other end of the line. Russo waits for it to finish speaking before he responds.) Russo: “I told you I’d be right up. I’m almost finished, I just gotta wrap it up. I’ll be there as soon as I can.” (Russo now hangs his phone up by snapping it shut once again. He then places it back in his pocket. Russo now looks at the cameraman as he begins speaking.) Russo: “We gotta wrap this one up, I have something I have to take care of. So get the shot because here it goes.” (Russo looks into the camera once again as he begins to speak.) Russo: “ In closing Cade. I am about to show you new realms of pain. Pain that nobody will have seen the likes of before. I will show you what true hatred really feels like Cade. I will send your mind into a blur as you try to fathom what level of hatred allows a man to be so sadistic and cruel as I will be to you. I will leave you questioning why you just like the rest will end up being nothing but another falling star.” (As Russo finishes his sentence, his cell phone begins to ring once again. He pulls it out of his pocket and flips it open in an angered fashion. Russo then raises the phone to his ear as he yells into it.) Russo: “I’M DONE!” (Russo snaps his phone shut and slips it back into his pocket. He then leans back and lets out a sigh. Russo then shakes his head as he says . . . ) Russo: “I really need a tequila and a lap dance.” (As Russo lets out another sigh, the screen fades out to the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by a voice-over message which says . . . “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. As the message finishes, the screen cuts to black.)
