The Shadow Riders at Sea pt.3

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Prologue When we last left the Shadow Riders, we found out that there was something going on up on the deck of the ship. With Eric Russo obsessed with catching the fish that he thinks has wronged him in some way, Jason Kage having alcohol induced dreams of being Darth Vader, and Luke Justice nursing a really bad case of gas there is no telling what will happen next. One thing is for certain though, the Shadow Riders will continue to keep you on the edge of your seats in this the third installment of “The Shadow Riders at Sea”. (The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders along with a voice over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to a shot of the sea from aboard The Wave Rider. Out in the distance in the stream of light that is being cast on the water from the boat’s searchlight, floats the barrel full of chum that had been attached to the back of the beast with the harpoon. The flare that once glowed brightly has been extinguished by the submersion into the water. “Superstar” Eric Russo stands on the lower deck of the boat staring out at the bobbing barrel in the distance still with the bottle of tequila in his hand. “The Vigilante” Luke Justice emerges on the deck from below and walks over to stand at Russo’s side. Russo turns to Luke and begins to speak.) Russo: “You see him out there?” (A smile crosses Luke’s face as he responds.) Justice: “Let’s go get him quick. We’ll tag him and bag him. Then we can fry him up and have something to eat.” (Russo shake his head in disagreement of the direct approach.) Russo: “Not yet, I wanna make sure we get in slow and close this time so he doesn’t see us coming and take off. Then we can tag him and bag him and fry him up.” (Russo points out to the floating barrel.) Russo: “That barrel will keep him on the surface for a little while. I wanna try and hook him with another one or two. If we do that, there’s no way he’ll be able to dive under. Then we can take all the time in the world and toy with him a little before we take him out.” (Lowering his hand and looking back at Luke.) Russo: “There’s no way to win when it’s three on one. Anybody who has half a brain can figure that out. It’s not rocket science.” Justice: “Yeah . . . unless their from Canada. Or named Belmont for that matter.” (Russo smiles.) Russo: “Guess that’s two strikes against Tristan now isn’t it?” Justice: “Normally yes, but when you’re dealing with us the stakes are always upped. He should know that.” Russo: “Oh well, he’ll find out soon enough.” Justice: “That he will . . . that he will.” (Russo hands Luke the bottle of tequila. As he does this, Luke notices a healing wound on the forearm of Russo. Curious, Luke begins to question Russo about it.) Justice: “Nice cut there . . . how did you get it?” (Russo glances down at his arm and smirks as if remembering something fond in his head. He then looks back at Luke as he responds.) Russo: “It’s from Belmont’s turn.” Justice: “I didn’t know he actually cut you up.” (Russo laughs.) Russo: “As if . . . it’s from the EMTs. They wouldn’t let go of me when I told them I was fine. So, I pulled away. I still have no clue why the gave me an IV in my arm.” (At this point “The Loose Cannon” Jason Kage emerges on the deck of the boat from down below. Kage is still visibly tipsy starts towards the other two Riders.) Kage: “Hey guys, what are you talking abou . . .” (As Kage crosses the deck, he trips over one of the compressed air tanks and begins to stumble forward. Russo and Luke try to grab onto Kage, but they are too late as Kage takes a header over the side of the yacht and into the deep water. Kage makes a loud splash as he hits the water. Once in the water, Kage surfaces and jokingly pulls with his arms in order to get some distance between himself and the boat so that the other Riders cannot reach him forgetting the situation due to his drunkenness. Suddenly, the barrel that had been floating stagnate out in the distance begins to move. It begins to move towards Kage’s splashing and carrying on in the water like a fat cowboy towards a dinner bell. The barrel continues to move in and that’s when we see it. The surface of the waters breaks a glistening fin pokes through into the night air causing the water to ripple. The fin passes behind Kage a mere 10 feet away. The ripples of the water lift Kage up then slowly lower him back down again. This slightly startles the man who begins to sober up and remember exactly what is going on at the present time. Russo and Luke begin calling out to Kage from the boat.) Russo: “Get out of the water!” Justice: “Hurry up! Swim for your life!” Russo: “It’s right behind you!” Justice: “Swim!” (Almost frozen in fear, Kage stays motionless just staring at the other two Riders calling out to him from the deck of the boat which seems all to far away. Slowly Kage begins working up the courage to begin his swim back towards the boat. It is at this point that he hears another ripple behind him. Out of fear, Kage quickly spins in the water to see what it is. The sight that he is greeted with sends a jolt of fear down his spine. The barrel that is attached to the beast has moved in closer and is now only about 5 feet away from him. This is enough to spark Kage’s courage into overdrive. He turns and furiously begins kicking his feet and swinging his arms in an attempt to make it back to the boat. Luke and Russo continue to call out to Kage in fear for the life of their friend.) Justice: “Come on Jace! You’re almost here!” Russo: “You can make it! Swim faster!” Justice: “Just a little more brother!” Russo: “You’re gonna make it!” (Kage’s expression is one of frozen fear as he frantically paddles as fast as he can to the boat in hopes of attaining some sort of safety from the monster that lurks in the depths of the water with him. The fin in front of the barrel races towards Kage who lashes out with all his might desperately clawing for even the faintest touch of something other then water. Suddenly, the beast under the water makes contact with Kage. The force of the jolt shoots Kage upright out of the water to about waist level. Without thinking, Kage quickly reaches down under the water to check what sort of damage was done to his leg from the blow. Kage however does not feel his leg but rather some sort of skin of an animal which is sleek and smooth as it glides past him under the water. This feeling is enough to send Kage into a panic as he lunges for the boat through the water calling out to Luke and Russo.) Kage: “Help! It’s gonna eat me!” (Luke and Russo do the only thing they can as they call out to Kage try to urge him on.) Russo: “Just a little closer!” Luke: “Come on brother!” Russo: “I can almost reach you!” Luke: “Just a few more feet!” (Just then, the beast slams into Kage again causing him to jolt to the side. Then another shot catches him from the other side sending him back in a whip like fashion. Kage bobs up and down in the water. Water fills his mouth preventing him from calling out to the other Riders any more. Yet another jolt hits Kage’s body knocking what air is left in him out and driving him under the surface of the water. Only Kage’s hair is now visible on the top of the water as it sways back and forth as if he is fighting for his life against some overpowering presence in the water. Finally Kage’s hair too disappears below the surface of the water. It seems like an eternity goes by with no movement on the surface of the water until finally . . . SPLASH! The arm of Luke Justice breaks the top of the water as he reaches down into the darkness in search of his friend. We can see Luke’s arm tense up as though he has hold of something. Seeing this, Russo quickly wraps his arms around Luke’s waist as the two men pull using all the strength they have. Suddenly, the back of Jason Kage emerges out of the water as Luke keeps a firm grip on the back of his pants. Luke and Russo manage to get the other Rider onto the deck of the boat where Kage quickly perks up and backs away from the edge of the boat still filled with fear. The silence of the night air is now pierced by the sound of a whale off in the distance. Crying out into the night. Barely able to get his words out, Kage speaks . . . shaking.) Kage: “Wha . . . What . . . What was that!?” (Relieved that Kage is now safe, Luke answers.) Justice: “I think there’s a whale out there.” (Russo pushes himself to his feet and looks out over the water as he mutters to himself.) Russo: “So is our fish.” (The picture then shifts to a shot of the dark ocean where out in the distance a barrel sits bobbing up and down on the surface. Almost mocking the Shadow Riders. The shot switches back to the face of Russo as he begins to speak once again to Luke and Kage. All the while keeping his attention on the barrel out in the distance) Russo: “We should call it a night . . . let’s get some sleep now.” (Thankful to be alive, Kage pushes himself towards the cushioned couches on the deck. Still shaken, Kage closes his eyes as he begins to drift off to sleep out of sheer exhaustion from the ordeal that he has just been put through. Luke stares at Russo who continues to watch the sea for a few moments before following Kage’s example and lying down on the deck using a bag of chips as a makeshift pillow to rest his head on. Finally, Russo takes a seat in the fishing chair doing his best to get comfortable. Slowly, Russo begins to fade off into a dream as he rubs the still tender wound on his arm.) Enter Russo’s Dream (The blur of the scene slowly dissolves the image of the sleeping Russo to that of a small flickering fire. Over the fire hangs an iron bucket with a spout coming off the side. A steel gloved hand grabs hold of the bucket’s handle and tilts it sideways as it begins to pour the contents into some form of mold. A stream of liquefied gold pours off the spout and into the mold as a disembodied voice begins speaking.) Voice: “It began with the forging of the great belts.” (An image of Cade dressed in a suit of armor holding his championship belt high above his head flickers with the fire as the voice continues.) Voice: “One belt was given to Cadicus, champion of the race of wrestlers and leader of their armies.” (Another image of Jamester with a big, full, bushy beard and his Xtreme title slung over his shoulder now replaces that of Cade as the voice goes on.) Voice: “Another was given to Flamester of the dwarfs. Opportunist and master of the lame moves of his sport.” (Now an image of Rick Static and Biggy swinging their Tag Team titles above their heads as a weapon replaces Jamester. The voice continues.) Voice: “And two were given to the tribe of RIP. This team above all else desired to be the best at whatever they did. Within these belts was the power needed to govern each of their divisions with pride and fairness.” (The pictures now fade away as the voice continues to speak.) Voice: “But all of them were fooled . . .” (The picture resumes to a shot of a map of the land of Primetime. We slowly mover closer and closer in on the map to the name of a place. A place named the Office of Trigger the Wizard. The voice goes on with it’s tale.) Voice: “ . . . there was yet another belt made.” (A shot of Trigger slaving away wearing the clothes of a metal worker is seen. He is hard at work forging the belt that will be known as the . . . Realm Belt. In the background of Trigger working, we can see Rick Static with an ear to ear grin on his face as he watches the creation of his title . . . the Realm title. The voice continues.) Voice: “In the land of the Office of Trigger the Wizard, deep in the darkest depths under his desk, the being known as Rick Static watched as Trigger forged him a master belt a belt that would hold more value then all others due to it’s lack of corruption from false stories.” (A picture of the Realm Belt reflecting the light of the fires beneath the vat of gold. The name of Rick Static sprawls across the name plate of the belt in an almost fire-like script. The voice goes on.) Voice: “ . . . but into this belt, Trigger poured his cruelty, his malice and his will to have everyone who wore it kiss up to him (The belt falls into a deep abyss into a pit of flames . . .) Voice: “One champion to surpass them all . . .” (A picture of the land of the Primetime. Slowly a shadow casts it’s self around the map circling in on a town where all the Lights are Out. The image cuts to one of fans running and screaming. They are chased by black cloaked wrestlers who are trailed by a stream of thick fog. The voice begins to speak once again.) Voice: “One by one arena’s across the land fell to the power of Trigger and his champion. There were some however who resisted . . .” (We now see a shot of Tristan Belmont dressed in full gothic armor leading an army into battle.) Voice: “Sir Tristan of Belmont and his motley crew known as Broken Reality marched against the one known as Rick Static, who had made them what they were thus far. In the middle of a giant ring, they waged war and fought for the right to claim the Realm Belt as their own.” (Stills from the battle are flashed one after the other with Belmont and the rest of Broken Reality laying waste to all that stands in their way.) Voice: “Broken Reality had almost attained what they wanted.” (A shot of Rick Static from behind as he watches over the battle can be seen. His Realm Belt around his waist.) Voice: “But the power of the belt could not be stopped.” (Rick Static charges through his opponents one by one. Desperate, Pax who is dressed in a suit of black armor charges at Static. Static lashes out with a large club causing Pax to fall to the ground like a limp noodle. Seeing this, Belmont rushes over to Pax and cradles him in his arms. Static approaches the two comrades. The voice cuts in.) Voice: “It was at this moment when everyone thought that all was lost that Belmont took us the sword of his fallen friend . . .” (The scene continues as Belmont snatches up Pax’s sword and swings it with all his might. The sword slices through Rick Static’s waist just below the Realm Belt causing the belt to fall to the ground.) Voice: “Rick Static the holder of the Realm Belt had been vanquished.” (The two halves of Rick Static’s body fall to the ground and vaporize into thin air. Belmont reaches out and grabs the Realm Belt in his hands. He holds it up examining it, almost mesmerized by it.) Voice: “The belt passed to Tristan of Belmont who had this one chance to destroy Trigger the Wizard’s power of the belt forever.” (A shot Belmont leading his group of Broken Reality through a dark forest is now seen. The Realm Belt sits firmly around his waist.) Voice: “But the hearts of demons are easily corrupted. And the belt has an agenda of it’s own.” (Suddenly a knife flies into the back of Belmont who falls off his horse. With a twisted smile, Ezra jumps down off his horse and grabs the belt for his own. He then runs off into the woods as Belmont lies hurt and wounded on the forest floor.) Voice: “The belt came to the Brazilian Ezra who ran with it deep into the tunnels of the misty rainforests of his native land.” (A shot of Ezra bent down in front of a river of water is now shown as the voice goes on.) Voice: “And there it consumed him just like the rest.” (Ezra’s voice cuts through the darkness.) Ezra: “This is my belt. Mine. Nobody will ever take it from me . . . nobody.” (The voice resumes.) Voice: “The belt brought to Ezra unnatural powers and a long reign. For months, it affected his mind and drove him deeper into his insecurities. And there it waited . . .” (The shot changes to one of the dark forest once again as the voice continues.) Voice: “Rumors grew of a power throughout the land. A nameless . . . entity that wanted control of his creation back. It was then that the Realm Belt decided that it’s time had now come to leave Ezra and return to one who worshiped it’s creator.” (Unbeknownst to Ezra, the belt slipped from his grasp while he was fast asleep and into the river.) Voice: “But something happened to the belt that it did not expect . . .” (A shot is seen of someone reaching into the river that the belt had fallen into and fishing it out.) Voice: “It was picked up by one who would never worship Trigger the Wizard . . .” (The scene now transfers to one of Eric Russo. He is wearing a thick suit of rd and black armor with the Shadow Riders’ insignia engraved on the front.) Voice: “It had fallen into the hands of a Shadow Rider.” (The scene now changes to one high above the lands as soars away from the jungles of Brazil as the trees and foliage fade into a cloud of mist. The voice rings out again . . .) Voice: “For the time has come when the Shadow Riders will shape the destiny of the entire land as Eric Russo defends the honor of the Realm Belt” End Dream Sequence Middle of the Night (The scene opens to a shot of The Wave Rider sitting on the calm and quiet open ocean. The waters are completely still surrounding the boat. Above the boat the sky is filled with a beautiful serene blanket of stars which is disrupted from time to time by a shooting star as it cuts through the heavens leaving a glowing trail behind it marking it’s path across the atmosphere. The air is flat with not so much as a breeze blowing. Suddenly, the barrel floating on top of the water comes into view. Slowly the barrel begins moving towards the yacht carving a path through the water. The fin of the beast breaks through the surface of the water. The fin and barrel begin to circle the yacht slowly closing in closer and closer. On the boat, the three men are fast asleep enjoying a well needed rest from the days’s hectic activities. Suddenly, a sound travels through the air. A dull scraping sound can be heard against the side of the boat. The sound is not loud enough however to wake any of the men. The sound now echos through the night air again. This time the scrape is louder. It is a dull scratching sound that sounds much like a mouse chewing through a plastic container. There is then a slight bump at the front of the boat. This causes Russo to begin slowly awakening. Kage shifts his position and turns over onto his side as he continues with his dreams. Luke does not even budge, he is fast asleep not showing any sings of being disturbed by the noises. Suddenly, the boat shakes violently. The sound of wood splintering and popping echos through the air making the once peaceful quietness disappear in an instant. Russo springs to his feet wide awake as he yells out in terror.) Russo: “I will never bow to you Trigger the Wizard!” (Luke gets to his feet as well but he quickly loses his footing sending his straight back down to the deck. Kage holds on tightly to the edge of the boat clutching his hand gun tightly. Shaking off the haze from his dream, Russo comes to his senses and grabs his shotgun as he begins yelling out to Luke who has managed to get to his feet once again.) Russo: “Luke . . . start the engine!” (Luke quickly gets to the upper deck managing to climb the six steps in three movements. Russo fires his shotgun out into the water hoping to connect with the beast that is attacking them. Luke fires up the engine but something about it sounds off. Hearing it, Russo yells out.) Russo: “Cut it off! Cut it off!” (Russo peers over the side of the yacht but he can not see the floating barrel anywhere. Confused, Russo begins yelling out.) Russo: “I can’t see him! I don’t know where he is! He must have ripped some of the machinery out. Maybe a shaft or something.” (Russo drops his shotgun on the deck and falls to his knees. He then pulls up a small panel in the floor as he pulls a flashlight out of his pants pocket. Russo drops his head down into the cavity and begins shining the flashlight inside. We can hear Russo from inside the hole.) Russo: “Shit . . .” (Luke jumps back down to the lower deck as he begins speaking.) Justice: “I’m gonna kill that damn thing! Kage take over at the wheel . . . I’m gonna go kill it right now!” (Russo pops his head out of the hole as he replaces the plank.) Russo: “Luke . . . start the pump man.” (Russo’s face is filled with fear. Seeing this, Kage begins to panic as he questions Russo.) Kage: “Are we sinking!? Is there a hole!?” (We can hear the pumps start up as Russo responds.) Russo: “Don’t worry, we’re not gonna sink. Just watch for that damn barrel.” (With the panic not allowing anything to register, Kage continues to ask questions.) Kage: ‘Is there anything I can do!?” (Reassuringly, Russo grabs Kage by the arm and sits him down in the fishing chair. He then points out into the ocean.) Russo: “You just keep watching, that’s all I want you to do. Just keep watching for the barrel over there.” (Russo then turns to Luke.) Russo: “Luke . . . you take watch on the back.” (Russo then takes the side of the boat opposite to where Kage is sitting and begins loading up his shotgun, Kage checks on his handgun, and Luke takes up arms with the harpoon gun. Russo speaks out to the other two men.) Russo: “Now, nobody falls asleep . . . got it?” (Luke and Kage nod their heads yes as the shot moves out from the boat. The three Shadow Riders are watching intently out into the distant waters. The top of the water glistens in the moonlight as we hear the cry of the whale once again. With this scene, the picture fades out to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again where we hear a voice-over message which says “The Shadow Riders have arrived, so sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The picture then cuts to a black, blank screen.)
