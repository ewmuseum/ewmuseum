SS RP 4 A Past Archive - 18.4.2003

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He placed his hand upon the bench that was before him. So many memories, he sighed as he started to recall one of them. He walked around to the front of the now, graffiti-covered, bench. He sat down and looked out into the park. A plain field was all that was in front of him, but yet children covered it, some playing football, others playing with their dogs. Everybody was happy. Everybody, that is, except for Cyrus. -Flashback- The doorbell rung. Cyrus rushed downstairs dressed in a purple shirt and black leather pants. He was just fastening his buttons on his shirt when the bell was rung. He opened the door hastily letting in his fiancée. A beautiful young woman with long golden locks. She had a curvaceous body, but was still quite petite. She was wearing a short, black skirt, revealing her long sexy legs and a white shirt, unbuttoned at the top showing a peek of her cleavage. Cyrus had dashed off to collect his shoes, he was now sat on his sofa lacing them up. “So where are we going tonight then?” She spoke like a siren singing at sea, in a mysterious and enticing song. “We’re going to a new Italian that’s just opened up down the road. That’s ok with you isn’t it?” Cyrus sounded a bit worried incase it wasn’t ok… “That’s fine.” She smiled “You’re so good to me Cyrus. I never want to lose you.” She then walked over to him and placed her arms around him as she gently placed a kiss upon his lips. “Come on lets go.” They walked out of the door, Cyrus locking it as they went. Cyrus walked up to his silver BMW and opened the door for Jane. He let her get in, which she did so very carefully and delicately, before shutting the door. He then walked round to the driver’s side and got in. He put the keys in the ignition and started the car up. -Reality- A football hit Cyrus on his leg. Cyrus looked down at it and smiled. A little child was running towards him to come and collect his ball. Cyrus however didn’t seem like himself. He picked the ball up and kicked it in completely the opposite way to the child causing him to cry. The child eventually stopped crying and carried on running after his ball. “That was rather mean.” Came the sarcastic comment from the voice. “I’m not in the mood for little brats annoying me.” Cyrus replied with anger in his voice. He was worked up about something. “Is this because of your match?” Inquired the voice. “No.” Replied Cyrus with a definite answer. “It’s nothing to do with wrestling.” “Oh, I see…” Said the voice now sounding confused. “So what is this about Cyrus?” The voice was dying to know what Cyrus was beating himself up over. “It’s three years today.” The sun caught Cyrus’s eye and there was a glint, the light reflected off the newly produced tear that now dripped down Cyrus’s cheek. “I’m sorry Cyrus.” Replied the voice sounding apologetic. “I forgot, I should have realized…” Cyrus cut the voice off in mid-sentence. “Don’t worry about it.” Cyrus then was silent, as was the voice. -Flashback- Cyrus opened the car door and let her get out. She said thanks; Cyrus shut the door and locked the car. They strolled up to his front door and were about to go in when Jane stopped. She turned around and walked back down Cyrus’s drive. “It’s a wonderful day, it’s gorgeous weather. Let’s not go in just yet. Let’s go for a walk.” Jane wasn’t going to take no for an answer. “Ok then, if you insist.” Cyrus tried his best at being romantic, but sometimes he just didn’t see the point in going for walks and such. He thought flowers, chocolates and a nice meal was better than a walk in the park. However, he loved Jane very much and would have done anything that she asked of him. They walked off towards the park and saw some children playing football, the ball hit Cyrus on his leg and he passed it back to him. Cyrus smiled and carried on walking, holding Jane’s hand as he went. They came to a brand new bench. Cyrus let Jane sit down first, then he sat down beside her. The sun had starting setting causing the park and field to be covered in a warm glow. “This is how I pictured me life when I was a little girl. This is exactly how I planned to live Cyrus. Thank you for making my dream come true.” With that Jane placed a long passionate kiss upon Cyrus’s lips. As she broke away from Cyrus she smiled a smile made from love. “I never honestly thought my life would work out this well either. I haven’t had any outbursts while I’ve been wrestling and I don’t think I have my mental condition anymore.” Cyrus smiled as well. For the first time in his life he was truly happy. -Reality- “Three years ago…” Muttered Cyrus. “It seems like yesterday when I was sat here with her. Thinking everything was great. Why did it have to go wrong?” Cyrus is starting to get angry as more tears stream down his face. “Cyrus, get a hold of yourself.” The voice was not being sensitive, but it knew how Cyrus needed to be treated. “Crying isn’t going to solve anything. You can’t turn back time. The best thing to do is to move on, you have other things to worry about at this time in you life. Like LOW.” “LOW” Screamed Cyrus “LOW? You think LOW is more important?” “Cyrus calm down…”The voice was anxious. “You’re right.” Cyrus started to smile. “You’re so right, LOW is more important. LOW is what all my attention should be focused on. I should forget about the past and move on.” “I’m glad you came to your senses Cyrus.” Replied the voice sounding more reassured. “So am I.” With that they both stopped talking and Cyrus dried his eyes with his hands. -Flashback- “It’s getting late Cyrus, I think we should be heading back home.” Jane was sounding quite tired now as she spoke. It’d been a long day for her. “Yeah, I think you’re right.” Cyrus agreed and with that they got up off the bench. It was now night. It was pitch black outside. Cyrus and Jane started walking down the passage that led to the road where Cyrus lived. Just as they got halfway through the passage they saw a group of lads mugging an old woman. “Give me your purse bitch!” Shouted one of the teenagers. “No, get away from me.” Screamed the old lady followed by “Help!” Cyrus could not stand and let this happen. “Wait here, I’ve got to go and help her.” Said Cyrus seriously, showing he meant business. “Ok.” Jane was not happy but she agreed to it anyway. Cyrus walked up to the three lads that were around this old lady and through them all to the floor one by one. He then gave them all a chance to get away. They all said sorry and ran off. The old woman then thanked Cyrus. “Thank you very much young man. That was a very kind thing you did there.” The old woman walked off, a bit shaken up but unhurt. Then Cyrus heard screaming. It was Jane. He ran as fast as he could to get to where he left her, but alas he could not run faster enough. He got to where she should have been and saw her lying on the floor. “Jane…” Cyrus murmured shocked. Then it hit him what had happened. “JANE!” Cyrus screamed at the top of his voice. “No. No, no…” Cyrus was now shaking with fear. Blood was dripping from her head where she had been struck with a baseball bat. The mugger had stolen her purse as well. “Help, somebody help me! I need and ambulance!” Cyrus shouted at the top of his voice but nobody was around. It was late at night, most people were at home now. Cyrus realized he’d have to get her to a hospital. There was one at the end of his road. He picked her up and carried her, then he started to run. He ran past thorn bushes that drew blood as they scraped his skin. He ran and ran, his legs feeling fatigued. He got to the hospital and shouted for help. “Please somebody help me! Cyrus screamed as loud as he could. Within a few seconds a Doctor and some nurses had come to his aid and had Jane on a bed. Cyrus was by her side as they tried to bring her back to life. They did it. Cyrus was in a world of his own with worry, but then it all disappeared. She was alive again. She opened her eyes and looked straight at Cyrus. She then tried to speak. “Cyrus, I…I…I…” Then her eyes suddenly closed, her breathing stopped and the monitor bleeped. That high-pitched noise cut through Cyrus’s heart like a knife. He knew it was over. He burst into tears as they tried to revive her again. But they could not. The Doctor went over to Cyrus and tried to put his arm around him but it was no good. Cyrus threw the Doctor to the floor and stormed out of the hospital. He ran home and let himself in. He walked up to his mantelpiece, he picked off the picture of him and Jane. He lit the fire up, still crying, then he threw the picture into the fire and watched the flames caress it. He stopped crying. It was over and he had to be strong. He could not cry. Not at the moment anyway… -End- He placed his hand upon the bench that was before him. So many memories, he sighed as he started to recall one of them.
