Dark Haze is sitting on his patio. There is a light shining on him and the table he works at. Sirens are easily heard in the distance, and the sky is rather clear. Stars are visible, crickets are chirping their wings together, and the moon is full. At the table is a piece of paper, a little jar of honey, and about six to seven cigars. Dark Haze starts by breaking the end off of one cigar. He then peels the outside leaf and it comes off perfectly. Next, he spots the glue line and cracks the cigar down the middle. Haze empties the guts of the cigar into the piece of paper. Subsequently, Haze peels the glued piece of cigar paper off of the inside paper. Now, he reaches into his pocket and out comes a bag of a well-known yet still unmentionable substance. It comes in nuggets and is a bright almost neon green. It is sort of light in color due to the crystals on it, and it is covered in what appears to be reddish-orange colored hairs. Haze speaks briefly, “Yum...” He then breaks it up rather fine before putting into the inside paper of the cigar. Once he has a substantial amount in it, he does his best to fit it all in and roll it tightly. Barely getting it closed he rests it on the table and opens the honey jar. Haze gets a little honey on the tip of his finger and then spreads it on the green outside leaf. Once the leaf is covered, he takes the inside piece and sets it on the greenleaf, where he rolls it tightly around the cigar. Haze rips off the end and tosses the cigar into a little mahogany case that he had in his pocket. “How I love these greenleaf Dutch Masters ” Haze then crumples up the piece of paper containing all the unused tobacco and leaves it in a ball. After that, he continuess the same process over putting honey on some cigars and saliva on others, and he separates them by putting them into two different cases.
