Rp 1 for Aftermath Archive - 19.3.2003

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Time. ‘Time waits for no man.’ A saying that has past through time itself. Ironic to say the least. Time is a strange concept, one that can’t be properly explained. There is one thing we are sure of however, time cannot be stopped. That is why, when you get your chance you take it. You grab it by the throat and don’t let go, because if you do, you might miss it. And you don’t want to miss a golden opportunity, because you never know how much time will pass before another arises… Cyrus Skar is a complex individual, many people now realize this. His mind helps solve problems like the average man’s, but unlike the average mind, his creates problems. Recently Cyrus has been able to control his twin personalities, but for how long? How long will it be before his other personality resurfaces again without him being in control? Cyrus knows it’s only a matter of time…he can feel it… Coughing could be heard from under the blanket of rubbish. As the man stirred the rubbish started to shake, slowly it slid off his body. As the man started to regain his sense, he finished brushing the remaining trash off himself. Cyrus managed to get back up to his huge vertical base with the help of the Dumpster next to him. He violently shook his head and then opened his eyes very wide at the same time as his mouth. Then he shut his mouth and eyes. When he reopened his eyes, he looked awake. Very awake… Cyrus had spent the night in an alleyway however, this was not something new for him. All throughout his childhood he had lived on the streets. It’d never done him any harm before, why would it now? Cyrus doesn’t care about materialistic things. He has a nice mansion yes, he has an expensive car yes. But that doesn’t make him happy. Cyrus is only happy when he is wrestling. If it’s easier for Cyrus to sleep rough than go home, he’ll do it. Simply for the fact that to him, it’s no different. Cyrus started to walk out of the alleyway and into the spring sunlight. He squinted his eyes to adjust for a few seconds until finally his eyes allowed him to see properly. In front of him was a busy main road. Cyrus started to walk up the sidewalk and was greeted by a row of shops. Cyrus slowly ambled past each and every one of them until he came to the end of the road. There was a car park. He reached into his pocket and pulled out his car keys. With one bleep, his car was unlocked. He walked over to the silver BMW and got in. Rubbish littered the passenger seat. Cyrus gathered up the empty crisp packets and empty bottles and put them into a McDonalds fast food bag. He then got out of his car and walked up to a bin. Cyrus walked back up to his car when from nowhere, he was struck from behind. A thudding blow came across the back of his head causing him to drop to one knee. Cyrus wanted to defend himself, but as he tried to stand up the pain overcame him. He managed to turn around to see a man about a foot smaller than him holding a baseball bat. Cyrus then passed out. The man forced Cyrus’s body into the back of his own car. He then tied him up, making sure there was no way he could escape. He didn’t, however, gag Cyrus. He left his mouth free. The attacker got into the driver’s seat and turned the keys in the ignition. He started to drive away as Cyrus regained consciousness. Attacker: Ah, the sleeping giant awakes… Cyrus still groggy from the attack manages to mumble a few words of little sense. The attacker laughs and concentrates on his driving. Cyrus’s eyes start to focus again, he looks through the mirror to try and see the attacker’s face. However, the attacker is wearing a mask. Cyrus is now fully aware of his surroundings, he begins to speak. Cyrus: Who are you? Attacker: I couldn’t tell you that now could I? Because if you managed to get away you might just go and tell the police who I am. Then they’d want to lock me up, we wouldn’t want that no would we? Cyrus ignores the man’s mocking tone and carries on trying to interrogate him. Cyrus: Where are we going? Attacker: You’ll see soon enough… Cyrus: You’re a man of few words aren’t you? Attacker: Only to you my friend. I can’t give anything away. That would spoil it all, all the months of planning, all the fallen attempts, would all be for nothing and I’m not going to let that happen. Cyrus: Why me? Attacker: We feel you could be of use to us Cyrus. Cyrus: In what way? Attacker: We need somebody who’s on television and in the press quite a lot to be associated with. If we’re associated with a wrestler like yourself it would do wonders for us. Cyrus: So you’re a company? Attacker: In a way yes… Cyrus: Bodyguards… Attacker (Laughing): You’ve heard about us then? Cyrus: Yes I have. You attack famous people then offer to protect them. You get them to sign a contract when they are at their weakest. You try to use brainwashing techniques to get people to sing those contracts. You will be sorry, you’ve picked the wrong guy this time… Attacker (Mocking Tone): I’m so scared! Now I suggest you get some sleep it’s going to be a long day for you. Cyrus does as the attacker says and slowly shuts his eyes. He falls off into the land of slumber as the car slows down and pulls into a warehouse. Cyrus is fast asleep by this point. The attacker gets out of the car and slaps Cyrus to awaken him. A few other men, a bit bigger than the attacker, also wearing masks, come and help pull Cyrus out of the car. They throw him onto a chair and place a bright light in his face. Man 1: So Mr. Skar. I’ve waited a long time for this moment. Cyrus: Really? Man 1: Yes, now I’ll offer you the contract now, seen as though you know who we are. The man picks up a briefcase off the floor and opens it. He pulls out a contract and a pen. He places them in front of Cyrus. Man 1: Now obviously we’ll have to untie you to let you sign the contract, so we’re going to give you a sort of sedative, it’ll weaken you. The man reaches back into the briefcase and pulls out a needle. He walks over to Cyrus, who tries to struggle to get away but to no avail. The man injects the needle into Cyrus’s skin and injects the sedative into his blood stream. Cyrus struggles for a few seconds before finally falling limp. The man walks up to Cyrus and lifts his head up, his eyes are rolled back completely in his head. Man 1: Release him, he’s out of it… The attacker and the other man walk over to Cyrus and untie the ropes that are binding him to the chair. They finish untying him and let him fall to the floor. Cyrus crawls on his hands and knees to the contract. The three men laugh loudly. Voice: I wouldn’t laugh if I were you… Man 1: Who said that? Voice: Cyrus was right. You’ve picked on the wrong person. Man 2: It’s coming from Skar?!?! Man 1: No! That doesn’t sound anything like him! It can’t be.! The voice comes from inside Cyrus. Cyrus slowly arises to his feet, his eyes have returned to normal, but now he has an evil look about him. He walks over to the first man who is quivering with fear. Man 1: The sedative should have…it hasn’t…NO! He suddenly runs at Cyrus in a scared rage. Cyrus sends the man tumbling to the floor with a hard right hand as he runs in. The other two men just stand and stare, the third lies on the floor with a black eye. Cyrus walks towards the exit. Man 2: You can’t go! You haven’t signed the contract! Voice: I can do what I want. Cyrus walks out of the warehouse as the other two men run to their fallen ally and help him to his feet…
