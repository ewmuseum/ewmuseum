The Ortiz Life- Chapter I

17 July 2004

July 14th, 2004- Ortiz Residence It seemed like almost an hour had passed since returning back to his Miami residence. Most of the past few weeks, since becoming the Lights Out World Champion were spent doing autographs. Photo sessions. Radio interviews. News interviews. That was his job. He wasn’t going to be that kind of World Champion, that would never show up at any of those things. He was a true champion. Jason Ortiz was going to do whatever it took. It seemed like every day, when he woke up before an interview, he would lie in his hotel bed. His eyes would be slightly opened, but not too much. He still had to adjust to the bright light. His right eye would glance across the room, and would see that World Title lying on the chair next to the dresser. He would smile. It seemed like every day it would make him smile when he looked at it. He would then pull himself out of the bed and stare at the clock. Realizing how early it was in the morning. He would have to get used to this. As long as he was the World Champion, this was part of his new life. But he would enjoy it. Sure right now it was kind of a pain in the ass to get up so early, but he would pull himself through it. His body would want to go to bed, but once he looked at the belt again, he was wide-awake. The day was early, just like always, and he would just sit in his room, thinking of different ideas. But now, the life of living in the hotel’s were gone. He had the next week off to prepare for Lights Out Wrestling’s, Realm. He was now home, in his Miami dream house. Jason had his feet pressed against the table as he pulled himself higher up on the white couch in his living room. Jason Ortiz shrugged. “What the hell are you looking at Jessica?” he asked his girlfriend. She had just got in the front door and was rummaging though the stack of bills. Well it seemed to Ortiz that they were bills. What else could it be. “I am looking through the mail. Most of it is yours. Credit card payments. Electricity bill. Same stuff like always,” Jessica Devine flipped through them even faster. The one big benefit of being the Lights Out World Champion is the raise that you get. He kind of needed the money rather badly. Another thing was also in his head again. If you don’t know. Jessica Devine actually got a real job. She was no longer confined to the gold digger lifestyle she once lived. You may be thinking to yourself. What kind of job could this chick get? She was a blonde haired chick with big boobs who liked to spend money. Hell, it seemed like Jason’s bank account went empty even faster with her living in the house. That, and having the Nazi, Steven Scott live there. But she actually had a great job, and Jason saw potential in it. It was a wrestling job. At the affiliated wrestling organization called PRIME. PRIME. A wrestling organization that would not only benefit Jessica Devine, but it would show her the light. Sure the big titted women didn’t know anything about WRESTLING, but that’s where Jason Ortiz would come in. This would help her with some moves and such. When he was not busy with the Lights Out Wresting stuff, and everything else evolved in his daily life, he was going to help her out. Make her the greatest women wrestler of all time. Not only was this a great thing for her to learn the real aspects of a dollar, but it would show her family that had disowned her, that she was never a gold digger. Jason Ortiz knew she was. That’s why she was now with her after the Crack or Love show. This was only the start for her, and she hadn’t even debut at PRIME yet. She was waiting for there upcoming Pay Per View. During that time, Jason was open to helping her get ready. She knew some of the basic moves. Suplex. Arm bar. Nothing really special yet. It wasn’t like she was going to step in the main event and start kicking some ass. She was far from it. Jessica was going to start a the bottom of the ladder. Like her boyfriend once did at Lights Out Wrestling. If things go according to plan, she might just be a World Champion, just like Jason Ortiz. As Jason looked acrossed the living room he seen Jessica’s eyes light up. He wondered what she was looking at. “What is that?” Ortiz wondered as he pulled himself off the couch and landed on his feet next to the coffee table. Jason took a few steps towards her. She stiffened. “I really don’t believe this,” she exclaimed, opening up the envelope and unfolding the paper inside of it.” It was his mail. Why the fuck was she reading it. Wasn’t that a felony to read someone else’s mail? “GIVE IT TO ME!” “No!” screamed Jessica, pulling away from him. Jason Ortiz hated it when she did that. It almost- irritated him. “Hurry up and read the fucking thing out loud!” Jason Ortiz shouted. His voice bounced off the white walls of his Miami home. “Read! Fast!” She jumped and screamed. “You won’t believe this.” “You won’t believe this, Jason!” He didn’t know what she was taking about, so he didn’t really care right now. “What the hell are you mumbling about Jessica?” he scuffed. Jessica Devine’s face light up again. “Jason, you got over one hundred, seventy five thousand dollars for your bonus from Lights Out Wrestling. For winning the World Title.” This can’t be possible. It would have taken him almost five months to save up that much money for working at Lights Out Wrestling. He expected a bonus, but he never realized how big it was going to be. His face lighted up as well. He finally had some extra money to spend. On what though. That was the question. All this money. He had no idea what to buy with it. Ortiz shook his head a few times. “What are we going to get? New home?” he prompted next to her. She grabbed him by his shoulders and gave him a huge kiss on his cheek. Jason loved it when she did that. He smiled back at her. Looking into her eyes made him almost fall asleep. Maybe this money would change things. Maybe he would get some SEX tonight from her. The first time he would ever receive that from her. Maybe not. “Let’s buy a factory. I have a plan for it. A new business that we can run on our spare time.” Why the hell did they need a factory in Miami. Even a new business. He was to worried about everything else in his life. The last thing he wanted to worry about was more business. “What kind of factory are you talking about Jessica?” Jason asked softly. “You will see,” she continued. “You will see. Let me handle all of it. You will like it. Trust me. Something I have been planning for a while now.” He didn’t like the sound of that. What the hell did she want with a factory? What would this cost him? The man had to find out the hard way. † In the Mind of a Champion † He never realized how big the Lights Out World Title meant to him until he had it in his massive hands. He stood there, in the center of that ring, with a tear rolling down his face. What a night. What a perfect start to his new life as, Jason Ortiz. People said it couldn’t be done. People said that this drug addict. This guy looking for a break would never get his shot at a World Title. It would never be done. Ortiz took that into consideration. He realized that maybe he wasn’t World Title material. Maybe he just wasn’t. Maybe he wasn’t cut out for wrestling. Then match after match came, and he started to enjoy it. Every week it seemed he was getting better. Every week more fans’ were on his side. It started to make him feel good on the inside. But then, out of know where, after winning the X-Treme title, he had that one big choice. The choice that could either break him as a wrestler, or give him a new start. What would it be. Keep the X-Treme title, or drop it- going for the World Title. It wasn’t a hard decision for the man to make. Anyone in his position would have done it. This was what he started to live for. This is what he always dreamed of being. Sitting in bed each day with the covers slightly draped over his face, he could only imagine what it would like to be a WARRIOR. A World Champion. He had never won that such prize. He was glad if he would even get a shot at it. But now, it was all his. He didn’t have to dream anymore. The dream became reality at Pulse. When he beat Nuke, Cyrus Skar, Luke Justice, they all realized that their dreams were over. Ortiz, on the other hand, his legacy was just the beginning. This was the start for bigger and better things. The past was gone, and he wouldn’t look back on it. Cyrus Skar, Nuke and Luke Justice were all images that had faded in the past. They would never step in the ring with Ortiz again, and if they did, he would do the same thing over again. Win. Something they haven’t achieved over him. For Nuke, his loss to Jason Ortiz was his second. For Luke Justice, his loss to Jason Ortiz was his first. For Cyrus Skar, his loss to Jason Ortiz was an embarrassing, number three. Why did they even bother showing up at Pulse. They knew, coming in, that the fans were on Ortiz’s side. Why bother with it. He had said it many times before his matches. Like he could predict the future. He knew he was going to win. He had that feeling. But that was all gone now, and things were starting to look on his side. He had the World Title now. The belt that everyone wants. The person that the new wrestlers look up too. That was Jason Ortiz. He now imagined himself, at the bottom ranks of Lights Out Wrestling. Just like he was over a year and a half ago. Walking in the back. With his black bag tightly gripped in his right arm. He would walk slowly. Not knowing many people. He would occasionally stop and talk to the more experienced wrestlers of LOW. They would stare at him. Why was this rookie talking to them. He knew, that’s what they were thinking. But when things changed, and months passed, he didn’t feel like a rookie anymore. He felt like a wrestler at Lights Out Wrestling. As a rookie he looked up too all the greats. Lotus. Mike Meyhu. Tristan Belmont. Jason always wondered what it would feel like to be one of them. Just for one day. For one hour. For one minute. Don’t you wish you could hop out of your shoes and become someone you look up too? Everyone wants too. It is just natural. Jason wanted to be a World Champion- he wasn’t going to lie about it. He was never a man that would lie. So he listened to what people said. Week after week, people ridiculed Jason Ortiz for being a CRACK HEAD. A fierce name that stuck in the back of his mind. That’s what he was, wasn’t he? A typical drug addict that would come to the arena half stoned all the time, and wouldn’t give a shit if he would win or lose. All he would care about was his paycheck? It was never about coming to work, stoned. It was about feeling good. That’s what made him feel good. He believed that drugs were the solution to his problem. Were they? Fuck no. It was all Ortiz. He was the high. He was the drug inside of him. Pretty Boy, Jason Ortiz may have said that a hundred different times in the past few weeks, but it was defiantly true. Winning the World Title was a new jump-start for his life. It was like someone hooked his body up to a machine and shocked the living hell out of him. It gave him that extra JOLT. No longer would he be called the Crack Head. No longer would he consider him one. He was free of drugs now, living in the comforts of his Miami house with his beautiful girlfriend. That was all the drug he needed, a headache from his girlfriend’s voice. But now, that was all gone now. The match at Pulse. The drugs. Everything. He had to look ahead. With his Lights Out, World Title around his broad shoulders. This coming week at Realm, he had his first match with a man who considered himself a Hall of Famer in Lights Out Wrestling. It is funny that you think about that. A Hall of Famer. What is really the definition of that. Jason Ortiz had always considered it a person who gave one hundred percent every week. A person who had won the World Title, and had become a true great. People like Mike Mehyu and Lotus were true Hall of Farmers. They had done something with their career. But now, a person like Annihilation wanted to be in the same ranks as those people. Where is the logic. This man hasn’t done shit. Week after week it seems like he drops the ball. He never can hold onto it. Those thick hands of his, never really seem to grip the fucking thing. What is wrong with that picture. A man, who never gives it his all, wants his place in the Hall of Fame? That’s just fucked up. “What Annihilation needed to do is focus on me next,” Jason Ortiz continued. “This fantasy of his, making it into the Hall of Fame is a joke. The man hasn’t done shit in Lights Out Wrestling. It is offal funny that my first match I get for the World Title, I win it. The man has had a shot at the World Title several times. What does he do when he gets it? You guessed it.” Jason took a deep breath. “The man drops the fucking ball. Typical, Annihilation. Another thing that makes me laugh is the fact that the man has been here longer than I have. Trying to gain some respect and the World Title. I come along, and win it before he does. Isn’t that funny. What does it tell you about, Annihilation? It shows that he is a cowered. A man with no respect. A man with an EGO just as big as the rest of his Shadow Rider friends. A guy who has lost to Belmont almost ten times and still can‘t figure out that he is not good enough to beat a real champion. Deep down inside of my body, I am just like Belmont. Same talent. Same everything. Ask him. He will tell you. We are just a like. Both World Champions. Both beating Annihilation.” Jason Ortiz knew that Annihilation was never a Shadow Rider. He never would be one. But they all had something in common. Most of all the people Jason had beat had something in common. People like, Nuke, Luke Justice, Annihilation, Cyrus Skar, Eric Russo and Jason Kage. They all had EGO’S. Instead of winning matches and trying to be the best, what they need to do is all get together. Each and ever one of them. Sit in the center of a room. Stare each other in the eye, and just cuss at each other for hours. Their ego’s will be the ones talking, not there mouths. If that ever happened it would last for hours, knowing that they don’t leave their ego’s at the door. He had faced many people in that ring with an EGO. Just like last week at Pulse. Just like every week. He came. He saw. He won. They need to give up this acting, and start to focus on more important things. For, Annihilation it wasn’t about the Hall of Fame it was about the World Title. Jason Ortiz knew the man was coming after him. The man was a target in the sights of Annihilation. This was his shot. His shot to take down a World Champion. But that little idea is still frying in the back of everyone’s mind. Including, Annihilation’s. Would he drop the ball again? If he dropped the ball again, how many times would that be? To many. Right now, Annihilation was really the last thing he had on his mind. Sure he was up there above everyone else, because he was the man facing him at Realm, but deep down inside. Like he always knew. Like he had said week after week in the past. Jason Ortiz was going to WIN. Win. Something Annihilation had never done in a few weeks. Jason would walk in the same he does every week. Crowd cheering him on. Ortiz! Ortiz! Ortiz! Ortiz! The fans would be cheering for the New World Champion. He would step in that ring again, for the hundredth time it seemed here at Lights Out Wrestling. He had grown to enjoy walking down to the ring every week. It was a like drug rush. His large body would climb in the ring- everyone still saying his name. Ortiz! Ortiz! Ortiz! Ortiz! Jason Ortiz stiffened. “Once I get in there, I would stare at Annihilation, and basically walk up too him. I would hand him the World Title belt, so he can finally take a good look at it. I wouldn’t let him touch it for long, knowing that he might try to take it and claim it was his. Many people have tried it before. I would take it back to my corner and drape it over the turnbuckle. Look at Annihilation, with that blue rubber ball in his hands. It would be imaginary, but I would see it. The bell is sounded. Boom. The man drops it. Like every week. He would stand there. Like a tree. Not moving. Just staring. One hit. Boom. Down. Cover. Win. Just like every week. It wasn’t going to be different. Except, after this week, I would destroy another legend.” After this week, Jason would cross out Annihilation’s name on his list of people he has beaten. A few words for Annihilation. Stay home. Don’t even bother showing up at Realm. You will be facing a World Champion, and as Jason Ortiz knew it, Annihilation would follow in his footsteps of never beating a World Champion.
