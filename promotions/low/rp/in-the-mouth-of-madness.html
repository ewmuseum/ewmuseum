In the Mouth of Madness

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by a voice over message which says . . . “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to a shot from high above the bustling streets of New York City during the night time hours. The streets however are still flooded with the lights coming from the cars below. There is however no sound penetrating the air from the traffic below. Instead, the air remains strangely quiet and undisturbed. Suddenly the sound of a voice cuts through the air causing the camera to jolt in surprise.) Voice: “Glad to see you could make it.” (The camera now turns and stops on a shot of “Superstar” Eric Russo who is leaning next to a rusted metal door that leads back into the interior of the building. He is wearing a Shadow Riders t-shirt tucked into a pair of black jeans as well as a quarter length, black leather coat. Russo pushes himself off the wall and begins to walk towards the camera as he continues speaking.) Russo: “Most cameramen wouldn’t touch an assignment that involved being around a Shadow Rider and a rooftop at the same time. You must have quite a set on you.” Cameraman: “Well it’s my jo . . .” Russo: “Did I ask you to talk!?” (Russo stops directly in front of the camera as he continues.) Russo: “Are you being paid to talk!? Do you think I asked you to come up here so I could have a conversation with you!?” (The cameraman just looks on in silence.) Russo: “That’s what I thought, now do what you’re being paid to do.” (Without hesitation, the cameraman focuses in on Russo’s face. He puts his hand out to the side of the camera and begins to count Russo down from five. As the cameraman counts him down, Russo adjusts his hair using his hand to smooth the top back. He then looks directly into the camera and prepares to begin speaking. As the cameraman reaches zero, Russo begins speaking.) Russo: “Derek Vinyard, I have asked for this time to have a chance to speak directly to you without the other Shadow Riders present. The reason I’m doing this is because I think someone needs to set a few things straight in that screwed up mind of yours. Now I could easily try and do this around the other Riders, but you as well as I know that would only degenerate into nothing more then a comedic commentary about you. No, instead I have decided to take on my less often used approach of serious discussion. Now although I prefer the prior, this situation calls for a little more candor to be talked about in the degree that I would like. I really enjoyed your most recent commentary on the society at large. I mean hell, you brought up a lot of good points that I’m sure would have most wondering exactly what they are capable of. The only problem is I already know exactly what I am capable of. Am I capable of murder? Yeah, I guess you could say I am . . . at least John Starr seems to think so. Does blood make me queasy? Nope, I’ve shed enough blood in my lifetime that it comes almost as second nature to me. This Saturday I’ll shed blood once again. Does that frighten you Derek? Does it make your stomach churn with anticipation knowing that the blood I shed will be yours? Does it make you queasy knowing that there will no longer be a protective window separating me from you? Deny it all you want Derek because I know it does. You were right when you said “others kill the setbacks.”, because that’s what you are to me. You are nothing but a setback, a minor glitch in the program of life which I will correct. Just like your precious serial killers that you seem to be fascinated with all of a sudden, you don’t pose any real threat to anybody who actually puts up a fight. I mean let’s be honest shall we, that’s the real reason they attack the weak isn’t it? Take John Wayne Gacy for instance, he was nothing more then a repressed homo with a bad heart that dropped out of high school. So what!? Is that supposed to scare me or something? The guy dressed as a frigging clown to kill people . . . and you don’t want me to call this asshole stupid. What other term should I use to describe an ass raping clown? We also have Andrei Chikatilo. What a winner there . . . we’re talking about a guy who couldn’t even get it up to assault his victims. No wonder his wife treated him like a bitch. Oh and how could we forget Jeffery Dahmer? Now this guy has to take the cake. Dahmer was nothing more then an over-glorified snake swallower, and I mean that in more ways then one considering his penchant for consuming and sexually assaulting the remains of his anal adventurers. Hell, the jackass died in a pool of his own blood on a prison floor. See what happens when a so-called monster meets a real challenge. And you look up to these people . . . what does that say about you Derek?” (The camera begins following Russo as he begins pacing from right to left. He continues speaking.) Russo: “Now I also hear you talking about this perfect girl that you knew . . . stupid mistake. Did you actually think I would hold back on you with that comment!? It seems as though you don’t know me half as well as I thought you did. If you did, you would have known not to bring up Whitney. You see Derek, I will exploit every weakness and flaw that I find in you no matter how underhanded or dirty it may be and if she is one of them . . . then so be it. I will talk about her and infuriate you to a point that you never thought possible. I mean “potential friends” . . . that has to hurt. Knowing that your love will forever go unfulfilled. Knowing that your caring for her means nothing. That’s almost enough to make you a serial killer by your standards now isn’t it? Then again maybe it’s enough to push that little suicidal tendency of yours just enough to give you the courage to actually follow through for once. That would be my choice, at least you’d go out with some dignity. Why you ask . . . because unlike your heros, it would mean that you faced up to the real cause of your problems. It would mean that you figured out that it is not society that made you the way you are. It would mean that you knew that your only real problem is you.” (At this point Russo walks over to the stone ledge of the building as the camera follows him. He takes a seat with his back to the street below and continues speaking.) Russo: “The truth hurts doesn’t it Derek? It’s hard when there’s nobody to cast the blame on other then yourself. However painful the truth may be though Derek, it is still the truth. Just look at your past, you’re the only one holding yourself back. In fact, I’ve arranged for a little footage to illustrate my point even further. It took some digging to find this, but here it is nonetheless.” (The screen cuts to a grainy picture of Derek Vinyard standing in the middle of a wrestling ring which has the letters NRW printed on the center of the mat. Vinyard raises the microphone to his mouth as he begins to speak.) Vinyard: “By watching your matches you are out of my league. Your....better wrestler then me. However, I will not let you just slip by, but I won’t do my best. Your gonna win anyway what’s the point of fighting, well putting my all into it! Why waste my time on a match I’ll lose anyway? Eric, I’m sure as hell not gonna call you Superstar or Mr. Russo, I’m not afraid of you. No, shut up, zip it. Shut the #### up! I just don’t wanna waste my time when I’m gonna lose anyway. What’s the point! I don’t feel up to wrestling right now anyway. Well, congratulations on your win!” (The picture now cuts back to Russo who stands once again and begins pacing in front of the brightly lit skyline behind him as he continues talking.) Russo: “Who was to blame for that loss Derek? The least you could have done was try and put up a fight . . . and you wanna blame society. You know what Derek, go ahead blame society. Blame religion. Hell, blame me. Truth is that it really doesn’t matter who you blame. Because the fact still remains that no matter who you blame, you just like the rest will become nothing more then another falling star.” (With that the cameraman calls out from behind the camera.) Cameraman: “That’s a wrap.” (Angered, Russo responds.) Russo: “No shit you jackass.” (Russo then smacks the camera away from his face as the screen cuts back to the logo of the Shadow Riders once again. The logo is accompanied by a voice over message which states “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The screen now fades to black.)
