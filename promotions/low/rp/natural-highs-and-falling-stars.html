Natural Highs and Falling Stars

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over message which states... 'The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to a shot of a seemingly bad neighborhood in the heart of downtown Detroit. There is a slight haze that fills the air making the street look almost surreal. Suddenly a car screeches down the street heading straight for the camera only to stop at the last second. Seeing this car seems to strike fear into the hearts of everyone who is doing anything even remotely wrong. The street punks and thugs scramble in fear as the driver’s door of the car swings open. “The Loose Cannon” Jason Kage jumps out of the driver seat wearing a flower print button down shirt tucked into a pair of powder blue bell-bottoms. Around his neck is an oversized gold chain which hangs down to about the mid-chest area where it becomes obscured by a patch of chest hair. Topping off the outfit is a curly black afro that is extremely over exaggerated and a pair of purple sunglasses. Kage quickly takes off the sunglasses as the camera freezes on a shot of his face. With the picture on the screen, an announcer’s voice begins speaking . . .) Announcer: “Jason Kage!” (The frame unfreezes as Kage drops to one knee and whips out a gun. As he does this, he calls out to the people fleeing from the scene.) Kage: “Freeze, Jackass!” (One of the thugs comes at Kage from the side only to be taken down to the ground with a hiptoss. Another one of the many thugs throws a knife at the head of Kage. Seeing it coming, Kage dives under the flying blade into a forward roll. As he comes out of the roll, he points the gun and fires using two hands in a police like fashion. The scene now quickly cuts from Kage to a shot inside of bedroom with a 70's decor of lava lamps, red velvet bed sheets on a water bed, and a leopard print carpet. Also on the water bed is a group of girls wearing brightly colored bikinis. The camera pans around to get a shot of “Superstar” Eric Russo. Russo is wearing a purple leisure suit with a black dress shirt that is only buttoned half way up underneath the jacket. Russo also has a thick gold chain hanging from his neck that ends up being obscured by a patch of thick chest hair much like Kage. The camera shot now pans back to the girls as Russo turns and drops back first onto the bed. As he lands, the screen freezes on a shot of his face. Once again the voice over of an announcer begins to speak. . .) Announcer: “Eric Russo!” (The scene unfreezes as the bikini clad women entangle themselves with Russo. As they do this, Russo speaks.) Russo: “That’s right . . . who’s your Sugar Daddy baby?” (The announcer starts to speak again as the scene begins to fade into a new one.) Announcer: “Star in . . .” (The screen fills with the words “Natural Highs and Falling Stars” printed in thick gold bubble lettering on a black background. The title fades out as a montage of short action clips begins. Kage rolls over the hood of a car with his gun in hand before diving and tackling a faceless thug to the ground . . . Russo knocks out a pimp in a white fur coat with a hard right . . . Kage is in a high speed car chase through downtown Detroit . . . Russo makes a pass at a girl only to be slapped . . . Russo and Kage are ducked behind wooden crates in a shoot out on a waterfront dock . . . a superior officer rises from behind his desk to yell at Russo and Kage for their actions . . . Russo and Kage jump through a glass window just as the building that they were in explodes . . . Kage smiles for a picture that ends up on his ID badge . . . Russo smiles for a shot of his ID badge as well . . . In a fight on the roof of a building high above the city streets, Kage knocks his attacker down and kicks him off the edge of the roof . . . a voluptuous blond woman removes her dress for Russo from a behind shot . . . Russo and the woman fall into bed locked in an embrace. Suddenly the screen goes black. The camera now pans back to reveal that we have been watching the TV screen inside of the Shadow Riders’ Miami beach house. Russo is standing next to the TV with his and by the VCR pressing the eject button. On the couch in front of the TV is “The Vigilante” Luke Justice sitting with a big bag of Doritos. Luke looks up at Russo with a look of surprise on his face as he begins to speak.) Justice: “Hey . . . I was watching that!” (Russo looking down at Luke with a glare in his eye responds.) Russo: “I don’t ever want you to put that movie on in here again.” Justice: “Why? What’s the problem with you and Kage all of a sudden?” (Walking over to the couch and plopping down beside Luke, Russo begins to speak.) Russo: “I’m not the one with the problem, he is. He’s blaming me for his loss on Realm.” Justice: “Well you did kind of just leave him hanging.” (Allowing his motions to show his displeasure with Luke’s comment, Russo responds in an angered tone.) Russo: “The hell I did! I put faith in Kage and he let me down, not the other way around. It was him who lost that match for the team not me. I should have known better then to let him have my spotlight. I should have learned that he couldn’t carry things as well as I could. I mean just look at the opening to that movie. I gave him the spotlight there and he had to overact with all those unneeded jackass rolls to simply fire a gun. What a glory hog. ” (Doing his best to dance around Russo’s temper while still expressing his view, Luke begins to speak.) Justice: “I’m not judging or anything, I’m just saying that you left the ring to get your belt instead of taking care of Shadow Rider business first. When I came into this group, you told me to always put the group ahead of myself. Push the group above all else.” Russo: “I am pushing the group above all else. I’m the Realm champion . . . arguably the most contested belt in the entire LOW. Just look at the former champions, Rick Static . . . one of the most respected men to ever compete in the LOW, not to mention the man who made you what you are today. Tristan Belmont . . . a fellow Shadow Rider and one of the most feared men in the entire wrestling world today. Ezra . . . a man who beat nearly every opponent that he ever faced by knocking them out cold. Now there’s me. How would it look if a Shadow Rider, someone who constantly talks about how they are the be all end all of this sport were to fail in continuing that reputation?” (Russo pauses as if he is awaiting a response from Luke but as soon as Luke begins to speak, Russo cuts him off as he continues.) Russo: “It wouldn’t look too good. So you see, I am doing this for the Shadow Riders. I’m doing this for you, Belmont, and even that ungrateful bastard Kage. I’m putting my reputation on the line for the betterment of you three considering I’m the only one of us who has a belt.” (Taking some offence to Russo’s comments, Luke glances at him and then responds.) Justice: “I don’t see a belt anywhere.” (Russo calms down slightly, sensing that Luke is just trying to get under his skin as he responds.) Russo: “Ha Ha . . . real funny. Don’t even get me started on Big Daveylicious. I mean who the hell does this guy think he is? Oh wow . . . he was in PCW! Big freaking deal, is that supposed to mean something to me? So what if he was in PCW, I was in NRW. Does that make me sound special? Does that better my chances here and now? I don’t think so. PCW does not mean a damn thing to me and I don’t see why it should.” Justice: “Well PCW is a pretty prestigious federation.” Russo: “The hell it is, PCW houses nothing but ego boosted prima donnas and washed up has-beens that can’t cut the mustard anywhere else. Over privileged jackasses who haven’t had to actually work for anything since they were hired. If that’s what you call prestigious Luke, then I really feel bad for the future of this industry.” (Just as Russo finishes his sentence, the front door of the beach house opens to reveal “The Loose Cannon” Jason Kage. Kage seems quite out of breath as he enters the house closing the door behind him. Luke pipes up on the couch.) Justice: “Hey man . . . where have you been?” (Still out of breath, Kage responds.) Kage: “Jus . . . just out jogging.” (Finally catching his breath, Kage continues.) Kage: “So, what are you up to?” Justice: “Nothing much, just talking with Eric here.” (Kage sneers at the mention of Russo’s name as he stalks through the living room of the house.) Kage: “About him no doubt.” (Kage continues past the couch and down the hallway to his room without even acknowledging Russo. Unaffected, Russo begins to speak as he turns his attention back to Luke.) Russo: “What an asshole. I can’t wait to be in the same ring as him.” (Confused, Luke responds.) Justice: “What’s that supposed to mean?” Russo: “Let’s just say as far a beatings go, I might not stop at Daveylicious.” (Seemingly relaxed Luke leans back as if he comprehends Russo’s statement.) Justice: “Oh . . . I get it. You two are gonna pull a joke like me and Belmont did at the Pay-per-view aren’t you?” (Russo’s serious does not break however as he answers Luke’s question.) Russo: “No, what happens after I beat Daveylicious on Realm isn’t gonna be a joke. What happens on Realm is gonna be quite serious. In fact, you could say that what happens on Realm might just shake the team known as the Shadow Riders to their very core.” (With his suspicions rising, Luke states his opinion.) Justice: “I don’t like where you’re going with this brother. You’re being all cryptic and stuff. You have something planned don’t you?” (Letting out a little chuckle, Russo responds in a foreboding voice.) Russo: “I know someone else who isn’t gonna like where I’m going with this either. Someone who is gonna end up just like the rest . . . nothing but another falling star.” (The screen once again fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied by the usual voice-over message which states... 'The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The picture no fades to black.)
