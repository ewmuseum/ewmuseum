Ranger Runaround Pt. 4

29 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~{ The last time we saw the Metallo Zords, our heroes had been vacationing at the beach. Due to the suggestion from Bobbabu that they take some time off, the Zords thought a relaxing day at the shore may help them relax. While they took up surfing however, there was an evil plot in the works on the part of Queen Mogambu. Mogambu and her evil henchmen, namely the Platinum Ranger, were once again working on ways to destroy the Metallic clad heroes. After learning that the Metallo Zords had an irrational fear of broomsticks, the Platinum Ranger did some further investigation, only to uncover to reason for this fear. When the Metallo Zords were still children, they worked as janitors for the circus that their parents were acrobats for. One night, after sweeping, the tired tandem left their brooms in the middle of the circus, and when their parents did their trapeze act without a net something went terribly wrong. The accident took the lives of the mother and father of the Metallo Zords, who were impaled on the broomsticks. The Zords, who blamed the accident on themselves, are deathly afraid of broomsticks to this very day. With this knowledge, the Platinum Ranger surely has a plan to destroy the Metallo Zords. Will he succeed? Only time will tell. }~~~ ::We open to a shot of Queen Mogambu’s Moon Palace. We now see the inside of the Palace, where the evil Queen stands before her troops. She lectures the likes of Lulu de Blue, Big Bad Barry Bear, Raziel, and Tim. Conspicuous by his absence is the Platinum Ranger. However, due to his treacherous nature, we can be sure that this vile villain is not far from the scene.:: Queen Mogambu – For the last time, you fools, I will not tolerate this insubordination from my army! If I say I want you to bring me my scepter, then I want you to bring me my scepter! ::As Queen Mogambu yells at her henchmen, a platinum beam shoots down from the ceiling and fills an area of the floor next to Tim. When the light clears, the Platinum Ranger is seen standing in its place. In full costume, the Platinum Ranger reports his findings to the Queen.:: Platinum Ranger – You will be pleased to learn, my Queen, that I have discovered the enigma behind the death of the Metallo Zords’ parents. They Metallo Zords, the great intergalactic defenders, were at one time . . . sweepers. Yes, mere sweepers at a circus. They would swizeep the floors between shows. ::The Queen is shocked.:: Queen Mogambu – Sweepers?! At a circus?! Platinum Ranger – Yes, sweepers. Their best friends were their brooms. That was of course until . . . the accident. Queen Mogambu – Oooh, yes, the accident! Tell me about the accident! And please, do not spare the details. I want to know the whole, grizzly truth. ::Queen Mogambu, Raziel, Big Bad Barry Bear, Lulu de Blu, and Tim all begin listening intently as the Platinum Ranger begins speaking.:: Platinum Ranger – The Metallo Zords were not always the defenders of peace and jizustice that we know today. They were born to circus folk, acrobats. Their parents, the Flying Flutterers, were the most graceful acrobats that the circus had ever seen. Growing up in the circus, the young brothers were employed there to sweep up the floors when such wizork was needed. This was perfect for the youngsters, as one day they hoped that they to could be a part of the show. However, something went very, very wrong. It was a dark and stormy nizight, and the boys had been sweeping all day long. Too tired to return their equipment to the broom closet, they left their cleaning gear in the middle of the circus floor. Planning to return the brooms to their closet later, the future Metallo Zords did not realize that they would fall asleep before they had the chance to pack the cleaning equipment away. The young boys did not even make it to their sleeping quarters that night, rather they both feel asleep on the bleachers. When their parents awoke the next morning, they went to the main ring of the circus before it opened to prizactice their routine for the next day. This was before the other circus workers were awake, and therefore the show was not set up with all the proper things, such as safety nizets. A little while into their routine, the father of the Zords was to catch the mother’s arm as she leapt from one trapeze to another. He barely got a hold of her however, and she became plummeting downward. In an attempt to grab hold of her, he reached out, letting himself fall as well. As the acrobatic couple fell to their untimely demise, their screams of horror woke their two young children. The children, who are now the thorns in our sides, awoke to see the terror-sticken faces of their pizarents as they fell not just onto the floor, but onto the brooms that they had left out the night before. The children cried in fear as their parents were impaled upon the broomsticks, dying almost instantly. From that day forward, it was said that the duo would never be themselves again. They later went on to follow in their parents footsteps and become an acrobat team known as the Amazing Flutter Brothers. It was during this time that they disappeared to become the Metallo Zizords. It is believed that the Metallo Zords never forgave themselves for leaving the broomsticks out that night, and that their guilt still eats them alive to this very day. That is why even the sight of a broomstick could drive them to a breakdown, and possibly render them helpless against our reign of evil. ::The Platinum Ranger lets out a loud, evil laugh. He is joined by Queen Mogambu and the rest of the troops as they rejoice in this disastrous revelation.:: Queen Mogambu, Platinum Ranger, and company – Mwuahahahahahahahahahahahahahaha! ::The group continues laughing rather loudly as the scene fades away. We now cut to a scene of the Metallo Zords, who are now walking down the boardwalk. The pair lacks their usual costumes, as they wear Hawaiian shirts of the appropriate gold and silver colors, black board shorts, and their bulky helmets. They walk down the boardwalk seemingly fitting in, despite their outlandishly shiny helmets. Both members of the team carry with them world famous boardwalk hotdogs as they stride down the wooden boards.:: Gold Ranger – Who ever would have thought surfing could be that therapeutic? Silver Ranger – I know what you mean. I did not think anything could take our minds off Queen Mogambu but that sure seemed to do the trick. Gold Ranger – This vacation may not have been such a bad idea after all. I am sure that all is fine at home without us there. Silver Ranger – Yes. I wonder what our friends are up to right now? ::The Metallo Zords continue walking down the boards, chit chatting. They push buttons on the side of the helmets to open slight holes in the mouth areas for them to eat their hot dogs. As they enjoy their walk, the scene fades out. We now cut to a shot back home of the local recreational center, where the Metallo Zords friends sit around a table talking. Afro Otis, Brainiac Benji, Thin n’ Trim Tammy, and Pleasantly Plump Patti all sit around talking about their heroic Metallic friends.:: Thin n’ Trim Tammy – I wonder how the Zords are holding up. I hope they’re unwinding a little bit, those guys are always so tense. Maybe they are getting massages or something like that. Afro Otis – Like hell they be gettins massages. Dos’ boys wear so much ofs that Metal armor they woddent be able to feel a damn things. Thin n’ Trim Tammy – I guess you’re right, Otis. I just hope they’re enjoying themselves. Brainiac Benji – I am inclined to desire the same wish, Tammy. I do want the Metallo Zords to better themselves through some R&R. Pleasantly Plump Patti – I hope they bring my back Salt Water Taffies. I love that stuff. ::The pals continue talking as the scene fades out once again. We are now brought back to Queen Mogambu’s Moon Palace, where the evil Queen stands barking orders at her most prized warrior. The Platinum Ranger stands at attention and listens to the Queen’s demands.:: Queen Mogambu – Listen, Platty my boy, we have got to do something. With those Metallo twerps on vacation, that doesn’t leave us much of a window of opportunity. It would be too obvious for us to send a monster to the city. Bobbabu probably already has planned for that. We need to do something better. Something more personal. ::The Platinum Ranger nods in agreement.:: Platinum Ranger – Yes, I agree with you fully, my Quizzle. Queen Mogambu – Excellent. That is why I am sending you to bring the fight to their backyard. Make it real for them. I want you to kidnap one of their precious little buddies down on Earth. That’ll teach those Metallic morons to mess with Queen Mogambu. Platinum Ranger – Which one of them would you like me to retrieve? Queen Mogambu – That is entirely up to you. But please, don’t bring me that one with the afro, he seems very annoying. Other than that, you have full control over this project. Platinum Ranger – I will not fail you. We will show those Metallo Zords who rule this solar syzzle! ::The Platinum Ranger raises his hand in the air, filled with pride. Queen Mogambu smiles at the plot as the scene fades out. What is in store for our heroes? More accurately, what is in store for their friends? Who will be abducted? Will Brainiac Benji become bait? Will Thin n’ Trim Tammy be taken? Will it be Pleasantly Plump Patti who is picked? All this and more, next time with the Metallo Zords.::
