Hell's Fury #2

10 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Hell’s Fury match should be quite interesting for my first match here in the LOW, pitting myself against Emilio Dervante, a man formally of Vintage Wrestling. This Dervante kid is interesting to say the least. But he’s no Psymon. He’s not even close to being me. This match will go in my favor because I have no problems killing people who stand in my way, and a Hell’s Fury match can let me do just that, throwing this match to my advantage. You start inside a boiler room drenched in gasoline. The premise of this match is to find a box or book of matches---whichever they’ll use---light the room on fire and escape, no questions asked. But that would pose a problem: what if neither man escapes the boiler room? What if the boiler room goes sky high, taking Emilio and myself with it? That’s not a problem, since I don’t have much to live for. If you knew the pain and anguish that I lived through for eight years, you would know why. Emilio Dervante will know my fury, and hell doesn’t have shit on me! My fury can top Hell any day. Where am I? How long have I been here? I awaken from a restless sleep. I survey the perimeter around me, nothing but bitch black dark, except for a window to the left of me, spilling faint moonlight into the room I‘m in. My arms are over my head, my feet are several inches off the ground with my ankles tied together. There’s something in my mouth, perhaps one of Rachel’s ball-and-gag torture devises. I feel a drafty chill on my body, and it‘s racked with pain, even my rectum is sore. I guess Rachel had her way with me again with one of her strap-ons. After processing everything into my brain, three things came to mind: a) I’m in the basement; b) my arms are shackled to the ceiling, as my body dangles several inches from the concrete floor; and c) I’m stark-ass naked. That explains why I’m feeling chilly drafts on my skin. Here we go with the torture again. What is it this time? Rachel to sexually molest and rape me with her sex toys or Steven using me as a human punching bag? Either way you look at it, they both come to the same conclusion--- They both suck ass. The silence inside the room breaks when I heard myself making muffled cries. Tears running down my cheeks, wanting this nightmare to end. Can’t someone fuckin kill me and get it over with already? Even though I’m only thirteen years of age, a scrawny thirteen-year-old at that, I pretty much have nothing to live for in this house. The Kreeds are monsters! All they do is belittle me and treat me like I’m some animal. What did I ever do to deserve this? I did nothing wrong. I wish Emily were here. She would protect me from those monsters. I wish she never moved in with her boyfriend after her high school graduation Sometimes I wish I were never born--- The basement door opens, filling the room with light. The next thing I hear are footsteps. Someone’s coming down to torture me! Who is it? Is it Steve? Is it Rachel? Is it both? Aw, fuck, I’m screwed if it’s both! But I only hear one set of footsteps, so this is a good sign. What am I thinking? This isn’t a good sign! I’m gonna get my ass kicked by whoever’s coming down. “Hiya, ya fuckin whelp!” I hear Steve say. Oh, fuck---FUCK! Now, I’m in for it. First Rachel, now him. That bastard’s gonna break some of my bones and I can’t do anything about it. I hear myself making muffled whimpers. Finally, the final steps of the basement stairs have passed, now there’s heavy footsteps on the concrete floor. Steve chuckles at me as he sees me hanging from the ceiling with my wrists shackled together with cuffs and chains. He walks closer to me, an evil smile plays on his lips, anticipating the onslaught he’ll inflicted on his foster son. “There’s nowhere for you to run, whelp,” I heard him say. “I got you right where I want you. Shit, you’re easy to catch once I get my hands on you and have my way with you, so this ain’t much different than the other times I kicked your ass.” Steve lunches forward, driving a fierce punch right into my solar plexus. A loud, muffle OOF! escapes my body, followed by muffled screams of pain and anguish. But this doesn’t stop Steve, oh, no, this is just the beginning. Hard blows land in my chest, kidneys, stomach, back, kidneys, jaw and the kidneys again. All those body shots---along with the excruciating uppercut to the jaw---I wish all of them would just kill me. All I can do is take the body shots, one by one, screaming through each and ever one of them. I slowly drift into unconsciousness. My scrawny body cannot withstand Steve’s malicious onslaught. Finally, I went under. Being in a state of unconsciousness is peaceful, nothing can harm me or scare me or get me. I can finally be in peace, away from reality. I was rudely awaken by Steve’s hard right hook right into my left cheek. The ball-and-gag dislodges out of my mouth, along with a few teeth. I hear the ball bounce on the concrete floor, along with the rattling of my teeth. Steve stands there, lightly huffing, staring at his damage he inflicted on his foster son, me. “You had enough, whelp? I’ll be obliged to serve you more.” Without thinking, I spit some blood out of my mouth and looked up at Steve. “If I wasn’t shackled to the ceiling, I would---” Steve wraps a large hand around my throat, squeezing. A grip so strong, it could break my neck. If he wanted, he could rip my throat out, no questions asked. I struggle to breathe, but I can’t. “You would what?” the six-foot-six, nearing three hundred-pound man replied. “Try to fight me? Try to beat me up? Try to kick my ass? You tried that once remember? And look where that got you, a trip down the basement stairs, breaking half your ribcage on the way down. I can kill you anytime I want to, and in the disposition you’re in, I could kill you right now without any problems.” “Then...then why don’t you don’t kill me and get it over with?” I challenged. I struggled to say it, but I did. “That’s what you want, isn’t it? To kill me? Do us both a favor and finish me off!” Steve drew his arm back, his fist clenched, to deal out his final---perhaps life-ending---blow. The punch is measured so it can cave in my face, killing me instantly. His arm shot forward. All I can do is wait, wait so my life can be destroyed by the man who created me. He can finally kill his creation. A creation resulting the raping of my mother so she can spawn me. Hello, pearly gates, your next arrival is coming. But that ceased to be. Steve’s fist stopped within inches of my face. I can feel the hair of his knuckles tickling my nose. Instead, he patted my cheek. “Not today, whelp,” he said, chuckling. “You’re not ready to die. One of these days, boy, I’m gonna make a man outta you. And when that day comes, then I’ll kill you. But until then, you’re gonna hang here and suffer.” He deals one final blow to the stomach, forcing me to vomit blood. He turns around, mounts the steps and heads upstairs. I hear him close the door behind him. My body, bruised, destroyed after Steve’s hellacious onslaught. Once again, I drift into unconsciousness, hearing Steve‘s final words ringing in my head. I’m gonna make a man outta you... And when that day comes, then I’ll kill you... But until then, you’re gonna hang here and suffer...
