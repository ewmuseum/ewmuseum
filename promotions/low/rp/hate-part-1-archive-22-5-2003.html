Hate Part 1 Archive - 22.5.2003

16 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The car door opened and he stepped out, he slowly walked towards his door unlocked it and went inside. He was confused, annoyed, angry and worried all at the same time. There was no way that he could possibly have been thinking straight. He walked up to his answering machine and saw the little right light flashing from the corner of his eye. He pressed the play button down and the message played. ---Bleep--- 'You have one new message. Message received at five past five on the 15th of May.' 'Hey Cyrus's it's me, Hannah, I really need to talk to you, I'll try and ring you again some other time…' 'End of messages.' ---Bleep--- 'Message deleted. That was your last message. All messages deleted.' Cyrus sat down and wondered what could possibly have been going on. Hannah had been killed, he was the main suspect. How could this all have happened? How could he have found himself caught up in the middle of something like this? Then the phone rang. Cyrus however did not move, he'd left the answering machine on, he didn't wish to speak to anybody now. ---Bleep--- 'Hey Cyrus, you might want to pick this up and speak to me if you're in. I have something to tell you about Hannah Selby…' The voice then went silent. The man on the other end knew Cyrus was at home, he waited for him to pick up the receiver. 'What do you want?' Asked Cyrus in a pissed off tone. 'It's not what I want Cyrus it's what you want. You want answers, I can give them to you, do not worry about who I am. That you do not need to know. I can help you find out the truth Cyrus. This line isn't safe. I'll ring you back at the phone box at the end of your road in five minutes. I'll let it ring ten times before I put it down, I wouldn't miss it if I were you. I won't call again.' 'Wait!' Cyrus pleaded, but the line went dead. Cyrus rushed out of his front door letting the phone fall to the floor. He started running towards the end of his road, his quadriceps started to get fatigued, and he started to slow down. He then saw the phone box. He stopped running and changed to a brisk walk, then he heard it. Once it rang. He tried to speed up but he was too tired. Twice it rang. He could see it but it was still so far away. Three times it rang. He carried on but still the phone box seemed out of reach. Four times it rang. He was getting closer. Five times it rang. But would he reach it? Six times it rang. Was he just too slow? Seven times it rang. Or did he have enough energy left to pull out a short burst of speed? Eight times it rang. Cyrus was very close now, but was he too late. Nine times it rang… '…' 'Well done Cyrus, you had me worried for a minute there, I thought you might not make it.' The man laughed. 'Cyrus I have a lot to tell you, all of it being true. That is what I offer, the truth. Now this could effect you mentally, you may not be able to take in what I have to say. You may not like what I have to say, do you understand that Cyrus?' 'I understand.' Cyrus replied in a tone that showed he was all business. 'Good, now it's a shame I'm going to have to tell you most of this through the phone. I would have liked to have done it in person. But that's not possible right now. You see there are a few people after me at this moment in time, they know I know too much. They are the people you should be scared of Cyrus.' The man's voice trailed off… 'Hello are you there?' Cyrus was worried, there was no reply at the other end of the phone line. 'Hello? Sh!t!!!' Cyrus didn't know what to do, he was confused as to what had happened on the phone. Then he heard it, a gun shot. On the other end of the phone. His informant was dead. Cyrus put the phone down quickly and then walked away from the phone box feeling dejected. His only lead had been snatched away from him. Cyrus was now alone, he had nobody to talk to, nobody to consult. How was he going to solve the framing without a lead? 'Cyrus….you need my help…' The voice talked as though it knew exactly what was needed in this situation. 'Cyrus, I have all the answers. Don't ask why or how. I just do.' 'This better not be some sick joke.' Cyrus didn't sound to hopeful. 'This could just be a trick, me and you haven't been seeing eye to eye now recently have we?' 'No maybe we haven't. But I wouldn't like to see you get framed for a murder you didn't commit now would I?' The voice laughed. 'Don't answer that one. Anyway onto business. That man on the phone, that was Hannah's ex boyfriend. You know the one who she said she loved and ran away with? The one that she DUMPED you for.' 'I get the picture, but still, if anybody I would have thought he was the lead suspect. He was the only person I would have thought would have wanted her dead for whatever reason. I can't think of anybody else that wanted her dead.' 'I know who killed her Cyrus. But I can't tell you, you'll have to find out for yourself.' The voice laughed again. 'It would spoil all the fun if I told you the answer right now wouldn't it? No more playing Mr. Detective for you, no more attention from people. You'd be just a normal, lonely man if I let you free now. Well I wouldn't want that now would I?' 'Fine I'll play your little game, but you'll be sorry if anything happens to me, remember if I die, you die.' Cyrus had no choice but to go along with the voice for now. He had no other leads and no other possible suspects. Nobody he knew had a motive. It was just he and his other personality now. Was it all just a big trick to annoy Cyrus? Only time will tell… ---Bleep--- 'Hello Cyrus. Anybody there? Pick up if you're in… I'll ring again later.' 'You have one new message.' ---Bleep--- ---Bleep--- 'Cyrus where the hell are you? I've been ringing for two hours straight now. This isn't like you. I'll ring back again soon.' 'You have four new messages.' ---Bleep--- 'Cyrus I'm really worried now. Where the hell have you got to? As soon as you get in ring me, please.' 'You have fifteen new messages.' ---Bleep---
