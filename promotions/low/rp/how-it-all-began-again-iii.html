How It All Began...Again III

captured 5 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
.::Review::. Jason Warrior has spent his entire life fighting fears of the past and fighting fears of the unknown. His world that once surrounded him has been completely destroyed and the only thing he has left to hold onto is his sanity. Two days ago marked the anniversary date in which Jason and his brother were separated through a tragic event and every year Jason must face the fact that he could have saved his brother...But didn't. His dreams and thoughts are plagued by this fact; eating away at every fiber of his being. And worst of all, Jason knows his brother is alive; alive and watching his every step. Entering himself into Lights Out Wrestling is Jason's escape from the outside world. Its as if he depends on his legacy for survival; for without wrestling, Jason considers himself worthless. He has come to the realization that he has been called to the sqaured circle yet again and is now ready to indulge on a new journey. .::Present Time::. :: Room B316. The current month and last month's rents have been paid in full; its time to go. Everything has been packed and U-hauled away. Inside the complex we see Jason and Alla checking every room to make sure nothing is left behind. Neither of the two look excited at all. Jason stops Alla and speaks to her. :: 'Are you sure your okay with this? If you want to go with me, I wont object.' :: A tear trickles down Alla's face. She is happy, yet sad simutaneously. :: 'Jason, this is what you have to do and you can't afford to have any distractions right now. I still love you and I always will. I think this is for the best, at least until you get your feet on the ground. Besides, my parents wont mind me living with them for a while. I haven't had that much time with them in a few years, so I think this will prove to be the best for both of us.' :: After Jason returned home last night from his encounter with the street thugs, he and Alla and a serious conversation. Alla will be staying with her parents in Kentucky while Jason persue's L.O.W. '...at least until he gets his feet on the ground.' The decision is hard on both of them and they know they probably wont be seeing each other for a few months...Maybe more. :: 'My plane leaves in a half hour Jay, I better get going now.' :: Jason hesitantly responds :: 'Yeah...I, I suppose. Listen, I know this is going to be hard, but in time everything will smooth itself out. I just need to have my head in the right place right now and having you ring-side would not only be an unsafe place for you, but it would also be an unfair place as well.' 'I know....I know.' :: The two take time to say their final farewells. After a painful ten minutes, she is out the door and gone. :: Two Hours Later :: Jason has just left his complex for the final time. As he finds himself in the busy traffic, he thinks of his father and grandfather and how he wasn't able to make it to their grave sights for Memorial Day. And then he thinks of the old man rotting in a decaying church; the man who not only sheltered him, but also taught him the facts of life and what it meant to be warrior. Jason decides to head back to the church one more time before he leaves town. As he is driving, he begins to think back in time...:: † In the mind of a Warrior † :: Its the inside of an arena locker room. Wrestler's everywhere prep up for their evening matches. Jason Warrior is seen lacing up his boots. Suddenly a women bursts in the room to the wrestler's amazement. :: 'I'm looking for a Jason Warrior.' :: Puzzled, Jason answers the call. :: 'I'm Jason Warrior. Who are you and what's this all about?' 'I think you might want to come with me. There's a breaking news story taking place in Tampa, Florida at this very moment.' :: Jason's heart races and anxiety begins to set in. Pastor Norman Winter's comes to mind immediately. What's going on? :: 'Listen mam, what in the hell is going on?!' :: The women leads Jason into a private room where a television monitor sits on a table playing the breaking news from Tampa. :: CNN Broadcaster: 'For those of you who are now just joining us, we have some upsetting news coming from Tampa, Florida. Apparently at 5:30 PM this evening a crazed gunmen burst into St. Peter's church in downtown Tampa and began open-firing on everyone present in the church at the time. The reason's for his actions are unknown and more than likely will never be known. Only moments ago we were told that this gunmen had shot himself and is now dead on the scene. Lets take you now to the Tampa area where our fellow on-scene news reporter Gorden Scott is standing by. Gorden...' CNN/Gorden Scott: 'Yes, I am now standing in front of the church in which this tragic event took place only an hour ago. From what I've been told, the man walked in with a semi-automatic machine gun during an evening Church service and just open-fired on every man, women and child present. I've been told the head of the church, Pastor Norman Winters, leaped in front of the gunmen in order to protect a young child. Sadly, this young one was shot in the head and died instantly. The gunmen shot Pastor Winter's but was unsuccessful in killing him. Pastor Winter's is now in the Tampa Hospital. We've been told by police that there were no survivors, except the Pastor, creating a death toll of 147 people. We'll have more on this as developements continue to unravel. Back to you.' :: Jason, in utter confusion and awe, smashes the monitor with his fist. The women with him jumps like a scared cat and quickly tries to call him down...:: 'Jason, I know it wouldn't do any good to ask if your alright but, is there anything I can do for you?' :: Jason remains speachless looking at his bloodied hand shredded by the monitor's glass. Nothing comes from his mouth. :: 'Oh my goodness, your hand. I'm going to run and get some gauzes and anit-infectent, you stay put.' :: The women helps Jason bandage his hand. She stays in the room to comfort him while other wrestlers soon join in. The women looks into Jason's eyes and speaks with a whisper...:: 'Oh...My name is Alla.' .::Present Time::. :: Jason arrives. He enters the church looking for Norman but there is no sign of him. He even calls out his name aloud but still no response. Jason looks in the pews and in the confession booths but to no avail. He then remembers where he saw Norman a couple nights ago. Jason heads to the alter and there he finds him... ...Laying there.... ...Lifeless... ¿Dead? .::Four Hours Later::. :: The scene opens to the inside of a hospital waiting room which is filled with people consumed with anxiety and worry; fear of the unknown. We see a mother and father being told that there son was killed in a drunk-driving accident; news no parent should ever have to bare. Another doctor walks in and takes hold of an old women's hand. He tells her that her husband she has been married to for 60 years has just passed away. The old women bursts into tears and finds comfort in the doctor's arms. About 15 minutes later, another family receives a visit from a doctor. He tells them that their father will live but is going to be paralyzed from the waist down. All of the news can be heard by Jason Warrior who is sitting in the far corner; away from everyone. He hears everything and soon begins to worry about his life-long mentor Norman Winter's who he had brought in three hours ago. No word yet...Not one damn word. Seeing the tears flow and hearing the cries of suffering overtake Jason's mind. His world is completely falling apart all around him. The inside of him boils and his adrenaline maximizes. He is almost ready to explode. But just when it all seems like to much to handle, the doctor finally appears. :: 'Mr....Warrior? Uuummm, well sir, we've got some good news and we've got some bad news.' :: Jason knows that line all to well; there is no good news...Only bad. :: 'Go on doc.' 'Well...Norman suffered a severe heart-attack tonight and luckily we were able keep him alive. The bad news is he only has a few days to live, maybe not even that. We looked up his records, and over the years his body and health have been dealt a lot of 'bad cards' if you know what I mean. Especially that gun shot wound he sustained a few years ago.' :: Jason tries his hardest to push out that fatal memory. :: 'Can I see him?' 'Well...Yes, but lets try to keep it short Mr., uh, Jason. He's in the I.C.U. right now and he just came to. He also suffered some brain damage so he may have a hard time remembering much of anything. Follow me.' :: Jason and the doctor enter the Intensive Care Unit of the hospital where only the severely-illed cases are placed. Looking around and seeing all of the depression and pain sends shivers up Jason's spine while causing his stomach to turn. The two finally reach Norman's room where he is seen lying on his back, connected to what seems like a million tubes scattered about all over the floor. The doctor leaves Jason with Norman and shuts the door. Quietness. Stillness. Death is near. Jason approaches Norman's bed and pulls up a chair. A lamp sitting on a desk nearby is the only source of light; its beams illuminate a Bible...Norman's Bible. :: 'Norman?.........Can you hear me? It's me, Jason.' :: Silence :: 'You know you gave me quite a scare heh. sighs I know you can hear me, and I don't expect you to say much, or anything at all. Just hear me out..' :: A tear flows down Warrior's face :: 'I just want you to remind you of how much you've truly meant to me. You, in a sense, are my true father. You taught me everything that I know today. Everything about life, God, what it means to be a man, what it means to be responsible; the list could go on forever. You've been the role-model of my life and you still are and always will be. You, Norman, are more of a Warrior than my entire family combined. God put you on this earth for a reason Norman...And you should know by now that that reason was to care for me and my family, because without you I don't know where I'd be today. ' :: Jason takes a moment, and stairs at Norman whose eyes are shut. He knows death is right around the corner. The thought brings more tears. Suddenly Norman moves his arm and places his hand over Jason's. He coughs a dreadful cough and speaks with a frail, rusty and decaying voice..:: 'J-J-Jason deep sigh...My boy. I'm sorry.. 'Sorry??...For what Norman?' 'S-Sorry that I failed you. Everything, I coughtaught you I threw away because I was mad at God. cough***coughI shouldn't have been Jason. I-I....' 'Take it easy Norman. It's okay; take your time.' :: Norman takes hold of his old, beat-up Bible and holds onto it tightly; its the only thing now between him and death. :: 'Jason...Listen to me this one last time. You've got yourself a gift...A very special gift from God. Don't throw that away when you think your world has turned against you. This thing with your brother Drake, let it go....let it cough go. Please. I wish I could see you this Friday in your match...In the Lights Out Wrestling. Heh, ya know something Jason...?' 'What's that Norm.?' 'Every time I saw your Grandfather or cough father in the ring, even you, something inside me wished that I could be doing that exact same thing. Flying like eagles and winning like warrior's. This past week cough I think God and I have made up....I feel at peace knowing that your going to make me proud. I don't want you to worry about me anymore Jason; Heaven is waiting for me...I'm confident in that now.' :: Norman begins to cough dreadfully causing the nurse to rush in the room. He's coughing up blood so the nurse encourages Jason to say his good-bye's. The time has come sooner than expected. Jason still can't believe this is happening; its to surreal for him to grasp right now. :: 'Norman...When I leave this room, you know as well as I, that we'll never see each other again. So when you get into Heaven, I want you to remember this...I wont let you down; I promise you this. I will take those very words to my grave.' 'cough***coughJason..Warrior, I love ya kid. Now I want you to leave and not let this be on your mind. You live a good life...You show this world everything you've got to offer. Trust in God my son, for He is the one who will get you through hell on earth. Go...be a warrior......be a warrior.' :: With that said Jason kisses Norman on his bald forehead. Leaving the room poses as one of Jason's biggest challenges as hard as that may seem to be. With every step comes another tear...And with every tear, comes a promise to give everything he's got to give to the world. :: :: † Lights Out...Pastor Norman Winters † ::
