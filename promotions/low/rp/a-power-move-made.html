A Power Move Made

3 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** There are power pieces, pawns, and plays that make a difference, but just remember, at the end of the game, the pawn and the king go in the same box. As do the white pieces the same box with the black… ** :: We open in an oddly familiar scene. Run down homes and torn up streets. Poor families watching the camera crew as they make their way through a neighborhood that they visited only a week before. This week we visit again in the inner city neighborhood of the winner of last weeks over the top battle royal, Tony Morrison. The stares and gawks from the neighborhood’s residence are different this week however. Last week it was because there was a camera crew there. This week the site may be more of a common place thing as the residence are getting used to it, however what they are NOT used to is almost 8 feet of demonic presence walking down their street. In front of the crew walks The Demond, large grin on his cocky face, and almost a strut in his step. He walks around looking at all the homes, and if it weren’t for the facade he was currently putting on with that smile, he’d probably have a very disgusted look on his face. These homes are very unlike his place of residence. Run down and poorly built homes line the streets instead of nicely designed and comforting looking town homes. The pavement is cracked and broken, pot holes lining it and lines barely visible anymore, as apposed to where he lived with it’s newly poured black tar streets. The fences are chicken wire and old rotting wood, as apposed to the high security fences in Demond’s neighborhood. However, undaunted, The Demond makes his way down the street looking around curiously. Suddenly a child spots the huge man from his home, he cries out that Demond was right outside and suddenly many children from all over the neighborhood come running to see the LOW Superstar, forgetting if even for a moment that he serves as a major source of torment for their hero’s in the ring, in the face of a real life wrestler in their neighborhood. Demond is suddenly surrounded by the inner city children, some asking questions, while other for autographs. Demond just smirks and halts his movement standing in the center of the street. He opens his arms wide to make some room, the children back away from them as his massive hands push some aside and he squats down, being as tall as most of their parents even though he’s now at half his height. He attempts to quiet some of them, and when they die down he speaks openly to them all. :: The Demond: “ Hello hello hello, thank you for such a warm welcome, I expected to be booed. “ :: A small Hispanic child moves forward and speaks out. :: Child: “ Well we know all that stuff you do on TV is just a big act so no worries man. “ :: Demond seemed amused. :: The Demond: “ Is that so? “ Another Child: “ Yeah, no one is REALLY that evil to people just to get ahead, unless of course they’re from this neighborhood. “ :: Several of the children laugh, as does The Demond, though not as innocently. :: The Demond: “ Well I wouldn’t be so sure if I were you, there are a lot of people in this world that are very evil indeed. “ :: A child with a familiar face makes his way to the front of the group. He has cornrows in his hair and his name is Tyrone. :: Tyrone: “ Yeah well, you don’t scare me, I don’t care how big you are, you can’t do anything to me, not as long as Tony’s around. “ :: The Demond looked down at the small child, icy glare burning into him. :: The Demond: “ Ah yes, Tony Morrison, do any of you know where he is? He, in fact, is the reason I am here today. “ Tyrone: “ What? Letting him know you give up already? Just wait until the night of the fight, I’m sure he’ll be around for you to ask for mercy then. “ The Demond: “ Well well, you’ve got a little attitude on you don’t you son? “ Tyrone: “ Only when I’m talking to someone like you. “ :: The Demond stood now, towering over the children. His sheer size alone is enough to make some of them back up a few steps. :: The Demond: “ Well let me share a little secret with you Tyrone. I was watching the TV last week and you know what I saw? I saw Mr. Morrison out here speaking to you all about when you grow up and what you want to be. What a classic scene that was. Is the child named Tanya here? The girl that wanted to be a ballerina? “ :: The little Hispanic girl came to the front of the group, frizz still in her hair. :: Tanya: “ Y…yes….I here. “ :: The Demond looked down at her and scowled for a moment. :: The Demond: “ Ah…. I see the rule about TV making you look worse than you actually are DOESN’T apply here. Dear I have a bit of reality for you. No matter what “ Tony “ says, and no matter what you do, there’s one rule of the world that holds true no matter what. And do you know what that is little Tanya? “ Tanya: “ N….no. “ The Demond: “ Ballerina’s have to be attractive and have culture. They don’t let some ugly little brat street rat be a ballerina. You don’t have what it takes, try all you want, but you’ll fail because you’re an outcast. “ :: Tears welled in the child’s eyes and she buried her head in her hands running off down the street. The rest of the children became upset as well, some angry, some unbelieving. :: The Demond: “ And how about Themetria? Is she here? “ :: She didn’t move. This huge man even uttering her name was enough to scare her still. :: The Demond: “ Well, is she here? No matter, when you see her, make her aware that POOR people and people with filth and degration in their past NEVER become doctors. She’ll be lucky to hold the cup when I spit at the Dentists office when she grows up. “ :: Again, the child burros her head and runs off crying. A sadistic smile crept across Demond’s face once more. :: The Demond: “ And then we have Mr. Attitude Tyrone. Little Tyrone that thinks he’ll solve the worlds issues with Fire. Well Tyrone, I’d like to see you put out the fire in my soul. I don’t think there’s enough water, no matter how many little pansy girls are crying, in the WORLD to extinguish that blaze. And besides, firemen are looked at as heroes in today’s society, and poor little black boys from the hood don’t count. But don’t worry, you’ll have plenty of fire’s to put out while you’re working at Burger King, those fries get out of control every now and then you know? Just remember, no onions on my Whopper little man. “ :: Outraged Tyrone runs from the group and attempts to kick Demond in the shin. Demond side steps him and turns just as Tyrone begins punching and kicking at Demond’s leg. Demond just smirks and looks off to the side like he doesn’t even want to see what he’s about to do. He then does something more despicable than he’s ever done. Demond reared back and kicked the small boy extremely hard, knocking him to the ground and rolling through the group of children like a bowling ball. Several children fall down, and then all scramble off and run home. Tyrone lies in the dead grass of a near by front yard, gasping for air as Demond begins to laugh. Parents on their porches begin heading inside with their children as they get to their respective homes, shaking their heads but still neglecting to do anything about the situation. :: The Demond: “ You see, little Tyrone, this is the issue. Tony Morrison and I have a match this week at Realm, and since he was to frightened to show his face in his own hometown, I’ll just have to get my point across in other ways. Last week at Realm he faced several other new comers in an over the top battle royal, which he won, however as I said, it was with a bunch of new comers. He now steps into the ring with a force the likes of which he’s never seen. “ :: Demond reaches down and picks up the small black boy, still injured and gasping for air. He drags him across the street to a car sitting in front of a house. :: The Demond: “ You see Tony, I’m a lot bigger than most folks, and a lot meaner to, I have very few values, and I dislike the majority of the people that live and breath in this world. I especially hate POOR people who REFUSE to make something with their lives. GET A JOB YOU FUCKING BUMS! “ :: Demond sends his forearm and elbow through the driver’s side window of the car, glass showering down on top of the young boy at his feet. He reaches in and takes hold of the wheel of the car. :: The Demond: “ And I don’t think there’s anything I hate more than someone who uses the system like you people do. We all know you can work. Disability is NOT for someone who weighs 200 pounds more than they should, just because you’re a FAT ASS doesn’t mean you can collect MY tax dollars. “ :: He pulls extremely hard and literally rips most of the wheel housing, the wheel itself, and the top half of the steering column right out of the car. He holds the pieces in his hand outside the car now. :: The Demond: “ I’ve heard all the sob stories and I’ve heard all the “equal opportunity” BULLSHIT, this country gives everyone a fair chance, but it’s not the working mans fault if you have 20 convictions on your record and you’re wanted in three states. I wouldn’t hire you either, NOR would I give you ANY chance in MY town! “ :: He lobs the steel column and steering wheel towards a home, and watches as it smashes through the front window and into the home. Demond then moves to the back of the car. :: The Demond: “ Tony Morrison, you want to make some changes around here?! FINE, then get a US Naval plane to drop a bomb on this HELL HOLE and rebuild something useful out of it, like a gas station, which should give at LEAST four criminals around here a job. You have no chance in the ring with me at Realm Morrison. You’re to goody good. You’re not angry enough. You have a lack of the goods to deal with someone like me. So I decided to give you a little inspiration, a little push in the right direction. Perhaps I need to make myself a bit clearer. “ :: Demond leans down and takes hold of the rear bumper of the car and emits an enormous scream, he lifts the back of the car off the ground slowly and then stands completely erect bringing the car with him. He kneels again quickly, switching hand positions and then emits another scream, lifting the car higher. He then pushes with all his might and the car flips over, end to end, landing on its roof only feet away from Tyrone’s prone body. Glass shatters and metals bends, breaks, and creeks. The car is literally destroyed and the owner to frightened to come out and deal with it. Demond makes his way back to Tyrone and lifts him off the ground by the waist of his pants. He looks into the camera again. :: The Demond: “ Tony Morrison, I warn you now, and I warn you well, if I were you I wouldn’t even show my face at Realm this week, not that you do anyway you coward, hiding behind a mask. Bad things happen to people I do not like, just ask Swift Payne and Maximum Carnage. Or you could ask your little friend Tyrone here. He can tell you, once he wakes up anyway. “ :: Demond literally tosses the child up into a tree and smirks when he becomes stuck on a branch. Demond walks to the camera once more and looks deep into it, black eyes searing into the souls of all who watch. :: The Demond: “ Tony Morrison a dark wave rises on your horizon, and you damn sure better learn the name. The souls of a thousand tormented victims beg you to steer clear of its path, but you’ve got no choice. Chaos, Destruction, and Evil have only one ally, and his name is, The Demond. “ :: Demond turned away from the camera and walked away. He went to get back in his truck, but then caught eye of a fire hydrant. He reared way back and delivered a might blow to the side of it, busting off the adapter and watching as gallons of water shot out and straight up from the hydrant. The monster got back into his truck and made a single call. :: The Demond: ( Into his phone ) “ Hey babe, yeah….oh I think I got my point across. Yeah it went well. I’m getting out of here now, I’ll be home later. Man I tell you, if the gods were to give the world an enema , it would be right in the middle of this neighborhood. Bye. “ :: He hung it up, and pulled away slowly, leaving behind him a scene of utter destruction and chaos. The neighborhood looked like a tornado literally ran through it, and few survived its torment. The black Ford Demond drives pulls away slowly out of site, as the residents come out to survey the damage, though a question arises. Can one really survey the damage done to one’s own confidence and soul? :: ** A power move has been made, and anger is sure to follow. The question is, what will become of the Black Knight and the Good Knight, once they are both placed back into the box? The game…is soon to be completed, we shall soon find out. **
