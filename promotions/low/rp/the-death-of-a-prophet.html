The Death of a Prophet

captured 8 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Laid out cold, 1…2…3… The Prophet’s hand is raised in glory. The shame he will feel when I get another crack at the title. A screw-job? It could be called that, I think of it as fate though. Fate - The supposed force, principle, or power that predetermines events. The alarm clock in my room began ringing the night after Realm 65. I rolled over and reached slowly out to turn the alarm off. I crept out of bed still sore from the night before and having a slight pain in my head after the chair shot. “Rough night” I said to myself as I walked into the bathroom to wash my face. I flipped on the television waiting for them to announce the card for Realm 66. And Last but not least 8 men will enter and 1 will leave as the number one contender to the Realm title. The man who competed last night with The Prophet and nearly ran away with victory, Enigma. I was stunned. I thought it was be week’s maybe months later that I would have my next chance at The Prophet. I disreguarded who my opponents were in the match. No man can come in the way of me and The Prophet. Last night was the second time he had escaped me, as it has been said before many times...the third time is a charm. The hit to the face ran through my mind constantly the rest of the day, I was unsure what to think about it. I should have secured victory. I was still dwelling on what had happened the night before when I should have been preparing for the next night. EPW… The closing of the EPW brought tears to my eyes; I thought I was a lost cause without them. The Prophet slammed me through a table, he woke me up. I honestly must say I have him to thank for where I am at today. The thing is though he is a mortal enemy to me now, the man has what I want and he will be defeated. I walked under the radar at EPW, I was unnoticed. I regret the times I spent there contemplating the death of myself because no one would see who I was. I wanted to be the show, but I wasn’t. The Prophet brings lies about the way I received my amnesia, ill show him how I received it by getting rid of it the same way… I walked into the kitchen and pulled out a razor blade and walked back to the corner where it had happened once before. I sat there thinking about whether or not to do it, I had to I had to run Alias out of my life. I had to have a physical reminder that I was not two men, but that I was Enigma and him alone. I began to carve into my arm; I held my breath to keep from screaming. I clinched the razor blade tightly and began making the letter A in my arm. The pain was unbearable I began to scream as I finished the A, I continued tears running down my face in anguish. L, O, N, E. I needed a physical reminder that finally again I was Alone. No Alias, No Shane Carbon. Enigma and Jakob Bach. My main problem was gone; the feeling to express emotions was there again. Death was not a factor for me right now, the blood loss was nothing compared to the feeling of glory and victory through what I had just done. Although gruesome and horrid I had accomplished what I wanted. Now was the time to get back what I wanted, a shot at The Prophet, a shot at the gold once again. I will not fly under the radar in LOW. I will show the world who I am and they will never forget the name of Enigma. So brothers of war suit up because come Realm 66, there will be a war like no other. The man who emerges victorious will go for all of the riches. The man they call Enigma will rise again. I’ve held my ground with The Prophet once, and once more I will do it again this time emerging victorious. “The Death of a Prophet” Dear father, I have come to tell you about the death of a prophet. A man who stood tall and stood even taller in his believes. This man proclaimed the rising of a new era, a change made round the world. You see this man died a most gruesome and OBSCURE death. The Holy Prophet was a large, built man, he was better known as a priest. He told me his prophecies though. Can you tell me was this man the false prophet? He prophesized the death of me. Will this man bear the mark of the beast? Dear Lord can you please help me? This man is enraged with an anger bearing down upon me. He will annihilate anyone at all costs to get to me. I must defeat him, please lord give me the strength to overcome this monster. PLEASE LORD! The death of me has not come, the death of me will not come. “You may have overcome my will once Prophet, but your false prophecies will be overcome for I have the lord on my side this time.” I said [fade out]
