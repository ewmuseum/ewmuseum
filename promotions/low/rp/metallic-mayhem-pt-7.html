Metallic Mayhem Pt. 7

9 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~{ When we last left the Metallo Zords, our heroes had just escaped the evil grasp of Tim. After tricking the evil commander of Queen Mogombu’s army to free them of their shackles, they engaged him in an epic struggle for their freedom. After a hard fought victory, the Metallo Zords seemed to have Tim beat and their freedom won. However, as they went for the final blow on Tim, the monster mysteriously vanished, leaving the Metallos without the satisfaction they deserve. With their newly re-acquired belt buckles and freedom, the Metallos caught a glimpse of the carnage in the city on Queen Mogombu’s crystal ball and decided to put an end to the chaos. But first, they’d stop in at their command center to check in with Bobbabu and Bobbalu. }~~~ ::We open to a shot of the inside of the Metallo Zords’ command center. There, the tube which houses Bobbabu’s floating holographic head is brightly lit. Also, Bobbalu, the small robot in charge of the technical aspects in the center, seems to be calmed down from the last time we saw him. At a closer glance, we can see that all the computers and machinery in the center our up and running in perfect working condition. After a moment or two, beams of light shoot into the center, one gold and one silver. When the lights disappear the Metallo Zords stand in their spots, respectively. Firmly holding on to their all powerful belt buckles, the Zords question the condition of the center.:: Gold Ranger - What is going on? I thought the center would not function properly as long as that evil ranger was still around. Is this assumption untrue, Bobbabu? Silver Ranger - Yes, with the Silver Ranger, how is it that the center does not have problems a few, Bobbabu? ::Brainiac Benji, the close friend of the Metallo Zords, pops his head up from behind a computer screen. When the Metallo Zords see him, their questions are answered.:: Brainiac Benji - There, all done. ::Brainiac Benji pushes himself up to his feet and walks over to the Metallo Zords. He sticks his hand out and shakes the hands of both Zords.:: Gold Ranger - It is good to see you again, old friend. Silver Ranger - Yes, and how kind of you to fix our command center. I knew those brains of yours would come in handy some day. ::The Metallo Zords and Brainiac Benji laugh at the off beat joke.:: Gold Ranger - So, how did you fix all of these computers? Brainiac Benji - It was quite simple actually. All I had to do was disconnect and reconfigure the central output device linking the computer system to the intergalactic source of power known as S.W.O.R.D. Once this task was completed, I rewired the hard drive of the right-most computer to connect it to the left-most screen, causing a confusion of the system. With the system confused, I overrode the neutralizing power of that evil ranger, causing our systems to reboot. Once they were rebooted and formatted, I simply tweaked a few knobs and before you knew it we were back up and running. Gold Ranger - Ah, I see. Simple indeed. ::The Orb of Knowledge behind the Metallo Zords flickers on now and shows the two monsters ransacking the city. The monsters, which we can now see are a giant Chihuahua and a massive playing card, kick buildings down as the Metallo Zords look on.:: Silver Ranger - Oh my goodness, we must do something! Gold Ranger - Let’s go, Silver Ranger! Power up! ::With that, the Zords grip their belt buckles and are carried away by two beams of light. They leave the Brainiac Benji, Bobbabu, and Bobbalu in the command center as they go off to save the city. The Metallos appear in the cockpits of their individual Zords. From there, they are instantly transported to the city where the evil giant Chihuahua and it partner in crime a giant playing card stand side by side almost as if waiting for the Metallos’ arrival. The oversized dog begins to speak incessantly as the card remains strangely silent.:: Giant Chihuahua – Ah Metallo Zords. I was beginning to wonder if you would ever show up. Please, allow me to introduce myself. I am the evil Chihuahua Ren from the planet Pluto and this is my partner in crime. He really doesn’t have a name but due to the markings on the front of him, we refer to him as Ace. ::Preferring just to get on with the battle, The Metallo Zords pay little attention to the Chihuahua’s words and get into a battle formation. The Gold Gargantuan is standing behind the Silver Sentinel who poses in front of it, a few paces ahead. Both Zords are facing their foes ready for a fight. In a sarcastic tone the giant Chihuahua know as Ren speaks out to the Metallos’ Zords.:: Ren – Oh! Judging by your fancy shmancy moves, you’re not up for talk! A little overzealous aren’t we?! Well then, if you're done with the show, let us begin the battle! ::Ren looks to the giant playing card with the markings of an Ace next to it and speaks.:: Ren – Let’s get em’! :: Ren and the Ace charge at the Metallos’ Zords. Seeing their opponents coming right for them, the Metallos’ go on the attack as well.:: Silver Sentinel – Let us do it!! ::The Silver Sentinel flips over a ducking Ren who then stands up and takes a swing at him. Seeing it coming, the Silver Sentinel rolls clear and fires back with a reverse thrust kick at the oversized Chihuahua, driving it away. The Gold Gargantuan twists through the air and lands in front of the Ace. It pushes the playing card away and the flips it to the ground. Both villains make it back to their feet and lunge wildly at the Metallos’ Zords only top have their advance stopped as the Zords simply sidestep them. The evil doers now turn and renew their attack. The Silver Sentinel back-flips between them, easily avoiding their grasping claws. It then punches Ren and zips away, leaving the creature with nothing but a handful of air. It’s foot then finds the large dog’s back.:: Silver Sentinel – Gonna have to be quicker than that! ::The Gold Gargantuan blocks a kick from the giant Ace and lashes out at in retaliation without success. This allows the Ace to kick the Gold Gargantuan back into a large office building. The playing card then grabs hold of the Gold Gargantuan and flings it aside. The Silver Sentinel kicks twice at the evil duo who block the shots and in turn knock the Sentinel back into another building. The giant Ace then shoves the Silver Sentinel away past Ren, who attempts to claw it. Somehow however, the Sentinel flips over and lands awkwardly near the Gold Gargantuan. Both Zords stand side by side ready to continue.:: Silver Sentinel – Hah! Let us neuter these two! ::The giant fist of the Gold Gargantuan clenches as he agrees.:: Gold Gargantuan – Right! ::The Gold Gargantuan flips backwards several times and goes into battle stance facing the two foes. As it stands ready to attack, the Gold Gargantuan summons up two balls of swirling golden energy.:: Gold Gargantuan – Here you go, boys! I brought a couple of presents for you! ::The Gold Gargantuan slings these projectiles into the fray. They explode on impact, taking down both villains.:: Gold Gargantuan – Got a little kick to them, don't they? ::The Gold Gargantuan lets out a robotic chuckle as the Silver Sentinel charges the two weekend enemies and hits a massive spin kick on the giant Ace causing it to spin around on the spot. The Silver Sentinel spins too as he delivers a couple of extra kicks. Somehow the giant Ace manages to duck the first, but it is struck by a second and sent flying. The Silver Sentinel poses triumphantly as the giant Ace crashes into Ren and explodes in a shower of golden sparks and smoke. The explosion sends Ren reeling. The giant Chihuahua stumbles back to regroup.:: Ren – Okay! Now I'm mad! ::The Silver Sentinel and Gold Gargantuan regroup in a fighting stance.:: Silver Sentinel – Hah! Why don't you give it up, Ren? You are not going to beat the Metallo Zords! ::Confidently Ren speaks out.:: Ren – Give up? Why? Me and the Ace here are gonna - - - ::Ren turns to realize that the Ace has already been destroyed and that it is now a one on two situation. Panicked Ren glances from side to side frantically.:: Ren – Uh...ah...it was here a minute ago! Silver Sentinel – Looks like your big bad Ace got shuffled! ::The Silver Sentinel lets out a robotic laugh as the now back peddling Ren responds.:: Ren – Eh..yeah. Well, ah... ::The Gold Gargantuan now joins in.:: Gold Gargantuan – Well, I guess you're just gonna have to take us on yourself! ::In a severely frantic state now, Ren responds.:: Ren – Yeah...well! See ya! ::Ren turns to run and smacks into a large building. The giant Chihuahua topples backwards and hit the ground with an explosion of golden sparks and smoke. This brings about another robotic chuckle from the Metallos’ Zords.:: Silver Sentinel – Ah...just like a dog to run off with its tail between its legs. ::The Metallos’ Zords share another victorious laugh as the scene cuts to a view of the mysterious evil Ranger watching the celebration of the Metallos through Queen Mogombu’s crystal ball. As he continues to watch, Queen Mogombu approaches from behind and begins to speak.:: Queen Mogombu – Fear not my evil creation, you will soon get your chance to defeat those metallic morons. First however we must find their weakness. ::The evil Ranger turns to face his ruler as she continues.:: Queen Mogombu – I want you to follow them this week. I want to know exactly what it is that they do at this so called LOW Realm. ::With a silent nod, the mysterious evil Ranger turns and walks off as our picture dies out. The evil Ranger coming to this week’s Realm? Will Queen Mogombu find a weakness in the Metallo Zords to exploit? Will this be weighing on the Metallo’s mid during their match? Will the mysterious evil Ranger ever be given a name other then mysterious evil Ranger? Will any of these questions ever actually be answered in the next installment? Find out all the answers to these questions and more, next time we meet up with the Metallo Zords.::
