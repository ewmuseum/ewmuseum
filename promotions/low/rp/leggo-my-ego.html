Leggo my Ego

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
( The screen fills with the logo of The Shadow Riders with a voice over that states . . . “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The scene opens to shot of “Superstar” Eric Russo and “The Vigilante” Luke Justice in the back of a well stocked limo. Russo is wearing a long black leather trenchcoat over a pair of black dress pants and a Shadow Riders’ t-shirt. He is also wearing his sunglasses as well as carrying his black cane with the golden horse’s head at the top. Russo stares out the window looking at the morning skyline of Philadelphia. We can clearly see that he is annoyed with something. Justice begins speaking.) Luke: “What’s wrong with you?” (Russo turns to Justice with an angered look on his face as he responds.) Russo: “It’s Ezra! He thought that he was pretty funny pulling that little stunt of his on Realm. Well I for one didn’t find it funny in the least. What the hell was he thinking!?” Luke: “You got me, at least nothing got on my X-treme title.” (Russo acts as if he does not even hear Luke’s comment as he continues.) Russo: “I mean come on, he put a frigging cow in my locker room! That’s not funny! Do you have any idea how long it’s gonna take me to get that smell out of my shirt!? I can’t believe he actually did that!” Luke: “Calm down . . . it was just a shirt brother.” (Still paying no attention to Luke, Russo continues venting his anger for Ezra.) Russo: “I mean attacking me would be one thing but what he did was just wrong! Who in the hell does he think he is!? Who is he to insult a Shadow Rider in that fashion!? Does he think he’s something special!? Does he think that the fact that he has a belt around his waist means something because it doesn’t! He had no right to break into my locker room! I should press charges! Yeah . . . that’s it, I should have him arrested.” (Russo pauses for a moment as he considers the possibility of having Ezra arrested. As Russo continues to ponder that option, Justice speaks up again seeing an opportunity to finally get a few words through to Russo.) Luke: “You gotta calm down brother.” (Acting as if he doesn’t even hear Luke’s words, Russo continues along with his rant.) Russo: “No you know what, that would be too good for him . . . I have a better idea. He wants to ruin my possessions . . . fine, I’ll take one of his. I’ll take that little title belt around his waist that he holds so dearly. I’ll take that belt just like the Shadow Riders took Tristan Belmont from Broken Reality. . .” (Justice snarls at the mere mention of Belmont’s name before finally cutting into Russo’s rant and making some headway with getting his comments heard.) Luke: “Look brother, I don’t know what your so bent out of shape about. I mean so what he put a cow in your dressing room and ruined a couple shirts. It’s not like you don’t have the money to just go out and buy new ones. Besides that, I’m the one who has to live with the fact that I just jobbed to a rookie like Judgement.” (Finally taking the time to listen to Justice’s comment, Russo becomes even more angered as he responds.) Russo: “Oh and I’m the glory hog. This is about me, not you. This camera is here for me not you. So would you please just stop interrupting me!?” (Shaking his head and showing some self control, Luke responds to Russo’s self centered comments.) Luke: “There goes that ego of yours again.” Russo: “What ego? I don’t have an ego.” Luke: “You don’t have an ego!? Yeah, that’s real believable. I’d actually believe that Jamester didn’t blow anyone to get his spot in the world title match faster then I’d believe that.” Russo: “I don’t know what the hell you are talking about. I always put others before myself. Why do you think Kage has had such a fruitful career here in LOW? I sacrificed my time that I could have spent being a singles star putting him over in our tag team! Does that sound self centered? I think not. Hell, I should be named humanitarian of the year.” Luke: “Whatever brother . . . I think you ego might even rival Mike Meyhu’s.” Russo: “This coming from someone who calls themself the “Paragon of Physical Perfection”. Luke: “Well just look at me . . . I am the paragon of physical perfection.” Russo: “Yeah and I’m the Dali Lama.” Luke: “Dude you have an ego, just deal with it.” Russo: “Look Luke, I don’t have time to argue with you about this right now. I know I don’t have an ego so lets just leave it at that and get back to what’s important, that being my match with Ezra at the pay-per-view.” (Luke sits back shaking his head and lets out a sigh as Russo continues voicing his feelings about Ezra.) Russo: “As I was saying, next time Ezra wants to mess with me test the odds he should remember that his arms are too short to box with God.” (Hearing this perks Justice up again as he tries to prove his point off Russo’s comments.) Luke: “See what I mean!? Now you’re God!?” Russo: “It’s just a figure of speech.” Luke: “Whatever . . . could you please just wrap this up and get to the point?” Russo: “Oh, you want my point? Well my point is come War Wounds, Ezra just like the rest will realize that he just like the rest is nothing but another falling star.” (With that the picture fades out to the logo of The Shadow Riders once again. The logo is accompanied by a voice over that states . . . “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.”. The screen now fades to black.)
