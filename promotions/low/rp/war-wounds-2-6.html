He gets some medicine and then takes a seat by the kitchen table.:::: [--Michelle--]- What is wrong with you babe?:: [--Alyasis--]- Nothing out of the ordinary really. I guess that I am getting the cold because of going out and playing hockey earlier. I have a runny nose, coughing, and all of the sudden my head has gotten hot as well. That will keep me from focusing on the match.:: [--Michelle--]- Yeah you better go and run to the doctors office here in town. I am going to call and make sure that you can come in. I am sure that they won't mind, even if they are about to close.:: ::::Judgmeent gets out of his boxers and gets into some winter clothes. Michelle calls the doctors office and the clerk says that the doctor will be waiting on Alyasis to arrive there. She hangs up, and tells Judgement to head down there. He gets the keys for the car, and drives to the doctor.:::: [--Doctor--]- I heard that you weren't feeling that well.:: [--Alyasis--]- Yeah I have the flu I believe. (Judgment puts it in his mouth and under his tongue) Thanks you!:: ::::After two minutes the thermometer beeps, and Judgement takes it out of his mouth, and looks at it.:::: [--Alyasis--]- I knew it damnit!:: [--Doctor--]- 102.7 that is pretty bad. What you should be worried about is that fever of yours. I will give you some strong fever medicine, and then some other medicine for the runny nose, and coughing. (Gets the medicine out of the cabinet) That should do it! If there are any more problems please feel free to call me at my home number. Do you have it?:: [--Alyasis--]- Yeah I have it saved on my cell phone at home. Thanks for the medicine doctor, and I hope that I can get my sleep and get some food before I got to sleep. I haven't ate in a while.:: [--Doctor--]- Ohh this is bad. I forgot to tell you that you can't eat nothing with that medicine, only lots of fluids. I am sorry but that is the way it has to be. Becaus if you do eat with that medicine, then most likely you will wake up and through up all over the place. So no eating, just go to sleep. Sorry...:: [--Alyasis--]- Ahh this is great! I haven't had nothing to eat since basically ten o'clock this morning, and that didn't even fill me up. And now I have to wait until tomorrow morning to eat again. This is bullshit!:: ::::Judgment gets the medicine and grabs his keys and jacket on the way out. He slams the door and then gets in the car. He gets home, and then gets in the door, and Michelle is there waiting for him, with a hot plate of food.:::: [--Alyasis--]- Oh come on! I thought that it couldn't get any worse. My nose is stuffed up as hell, and still I can smell that delicious food. Could you please put that in the kitchen babe?:: [--Michelle--]- Sure....:: ::::Michelle goes in the kitchen and puts the plate on the counter, and then comes back in the living room where she finds Judgement watching television.:::: [--Michelle--]- Aren't you hungry babe? I haven't seen you since real early this morning? That stomach of yours has got to be hurting you, and I know it.:: [--Alyasis--]- Could you please shut up about the food? The doctor said that I can't have nothing to eat, and I have to tough it out, until I fall asleep. He gave me some strong medicine, but I will be happy if you get me a class of orange juice so I can take this big ass horse pill.:: [--Michelle--]- Okay....:: ::::Michelle goes in the kitchen, fills the glass with Orange Juice, and then gives it to Judgement. He opens th bottle where the medicine is for the fever, and then the other bottle for the regular flu medicine. He then sits back and turns on the television and watches it with Michelle for a few minutes. Then Michelle decides to bring up some of the people involved in the match at War Wounds.:::: [--Michelle--]- So do you think that this flu is going to have any effect on you come War Wounds?:: [--Alyasis--]- Well it could badly, but I won't allow it to get to me. But then again usually I heal quick when it comes to the flu, which is a good thing. But if by some reason it is still there when the match comes, then I will just tough it out and win the match. Like I said to Rodney and Jeff there is nothing that can hold me back from getting what I deserve!:: [--Michelle--]- It is good to see that you know what you are doing. Anyways which of these guys have you fought in the rumble? You have been here seven weeks at the most, you had to of fought on of these guys.:: [--Alyasis--]- Come to think about it, I have been in a match with a few of these stars. This is in no way going to be a fluke. Let's see now if I can remember right I have faced Canyon Thunders, and Iron Lung. That is about it really.:: [--Michelle--]- Well two is better than none. It is good to have faced you're opponents before, because then you have a slight advantage, even though it gives them an advantage as well. But that also puts on a better match, than when you were in the ring before. Because you know how you wrestle.:: [--Alyasis--]- You have a good point right there. I really think out of the two that I have faced Iron Lung will definatly be the harder of the two to beat. He is the one that beat me in our match, and Canyon Thunders lost when we faced. So I am really not worried about him right now. I am however more worried about some of the other contestands in this match, because I don't know much about them, and haven't had time to prepare for them. And they haven't had time to prepare for me in anyway.:: [--Michelle--]- You never know babe. For the last seven weeks you have been showing off your skills and the power you have, and you have been getting lots of attention. Many people have heard of you, and some people that have been here a while have had time to prepare for you, because they knew at one time they would have to face you in that ring.:: [--Alyasis--]- Yeah but you have to also remember that usually I am a step ahead of some people. I have gotten tape of most of the wrestlers in action, and even before this match was signed I studied some matches. Some of Cade's, Zurick's, and even Sik. Zurick is also a new rising star in LOW, and I figured that I want to check him out, you know to see if he has what it takes to face me. So I say that this one between me and him will be very interesting to see.:: [--Michelle--]- But what about Cade? He has been in LOW longer than you, and he is an awesome wrestler.:: [--Alyasis--]- Oh no doubt about that all. I have to give him credit, where it's due. Being the first World Champion here in Lights Out Wrestling. The guy definatly knows what it means to be on top, not only that, but how to survive in the ring. This is the man that I have been wanting to meet more than anyone. When me and Cade face in the ring, it will be nothing but respect on my side. He set the paths of many other wrestlers here in LOW.:: [--Michelle--]- He is one of a kind. But do you think that you can actually beat him? It might come to you having to pin him, or vice versa.:: [--Alyasis--]- Am I ready for this challenge that has been brought to me? The answer most definatly has to be yes. I don't back down from anyone, and many have already seen that here in LOW. There isn't a single wrestler that can intiminate me, and Cade isn't about to be the first. Sure he has lots of experience, but you have to remember that he has never faced someone like me! I have already beaten Luke Justice, and Mike Meyhu both champions of different divisions. So I just see it as another challenge for me to go through. Whatever someone sets in my path, I will come at it full throttle speed, and not stop to slow down.:: [--Michelle--]- That's about righ. I gues that you can say it that way. But let me change subjects here for a second. What about that Main Event they are having? Possibly a match for the ages in LOW or anywhere for that matter!:: [--Alyasis--]- Oh you bet! After my match I am definatly watching this match, whether it is in my lockerroom, or if I have to buy a ticket to get a seat. This is a match for the ages. I wouldn't miss it for the World. You have three very talented atheletes going at it, and its for the World Title. Mike Meyhu vs. Lotus vs. Jesse Jamester, what else could you possibly want in one match. I don't think the ring will be able to hold all three of those men. They all have the drive to be the best, and all of them either have been or will be an awesome World Champion! It is just a matter of who wants it the most, that is how it goes in this business.:: [--Michelle--]- What do you think of the World Champion Mike Meyhu? Is he going to keep the title?:: [--Alyasis--]- As much as I like the guy I have to say that he will loss this one! He is World Championship material, but with Lotus back and Jamester in the race, there is no way he can come out the winner. I like the guy and all, but I just think that this isn't going to be his night. He has lived his reign, and now its time for someone else to take over!:: [--Michelle--]- Alright now that you have said that Mike Meyhu is going to lose the World Title, who is it exactly that you feel he will lose the title to? In your opinino of course.:: [--Alysais--]- I have been in this business for almost three years now. And I know what a perfect World Champion is and what he represents. Both Lotus and Jesse Jamester have their up sides, and their down sides. But I honestly feel like Lotus is going to take this one! He has just returned, and he is a predator out there in that ring. Each match before has just been a warm up for what is about to come at War Wounds. Come PPV that yard will belong to Lotus, and he will win the World Title!:: [--Michelle--]- I have to disagree with you there. I as well say that Mike Meyhu will lose the belt, but it won't be to Lotus. It will be to Jesse Jamester. The guy deserves this title and he would make the ultimate World Champion of LOW. There is no other wrestler in the ring as great as him.:: [--Alyasis--]- So I am not a great wrestler? I could beat Jesse Jamester anyday of th week! You name the place and time an dI will be there. You know it and so do I!:: [--Michelle--]- Okay sorry, maybe not the greatest, but definatly up there with them. Is that better?:: [--Alyasis--]- No not really, but it will have to do I guess. I am tired of talking about wrestling. I am starting to feel really tired here, and I need to get some sleep, so I can get some training in tomorrow morning. So goodnight!:: [--Michelle--]- Okay I will be up there in a few minutes, whenever I get done cleaning up the kitchen and putting you're plate in the refrigarator.:: [--Alyasis--]- Okay......:: ::::Feeling very sick Judgement goes up the steps slowly.
