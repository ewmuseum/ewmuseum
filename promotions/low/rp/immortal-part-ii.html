Immortal part II

captured 5 April 2004

(OOC- Sorry I had some personal problems and couldn’t finish/post this earlier, if you don’t want to accept it I totally understand –Joseph Magnus) Immortality Part II Prologue: :: The hearts of men are easily broken and corrupted, it only takes so much for a single being to break and lose all sanity with the rest of the world he knows and loves. Recently Joseph Magnus has gone through a few chains of events that have lead to self destruction. He’s in a world of torture and cannot escape to the reality he once knew and treasured. It all started when he became heavy into his religion, it then escalated to him believing he was the son of GOD and would bring peace to the world. He was later taken to a mental institution for vandalizing a Crucifix inside a church, claiming he was trying to drink the blood of GOD from it. Now in this day and age, is it possible that the son of GOD does exist? And that if anyone was to come out and say that they would be put in a mental institution? It’s sad that in this era people can’t say what they believe in without being labeled “insane” or “racist” or “wrong”. Who knows, maybe Joseph Magnus truly is the son of GOD, a resurrection of Jesus, but even if he tried to prove his point he would be labeled “insane” or “mental” and he wouldn’t go anywhere accept to a mental institution his whole life. Where do you draw the line between believing and not believing? In the end, in order to protect yourself you have to keep everything you believe in inside so you cannot be labeled a danger to society. Throughout history people have been put to death for going against other peoples beliefs, like Jesus Christ for example. He claimed to be the son of GOD, and people refused to believe him so they crucified him and killed him in the most brutal way possible. Today since people can’t get away with that in society, there are other forms of punishment. Like correctional facilities, mental institutions, and even death row. Therefore you have a choice between keeping everything to yourself, or speaking in what you believe in and taking the risk of ruining the rest of your life. :: Saints: “I shouldn’t be here, I’m a saint, a messiah, everything that you wish to be and can’t… You’re all jealous that must be what it is. For I don’t see why you can’t believe me, I claim to be the son of GOD, a messiah, and prophet. Yet you condemn me to a world of torment that I cannot escape. Where is the fairness in that? What can a man do to get his point across without being shunned upon…?” Joseph Magnus says to himself as the darkened room slowly becomes lighter and lighter. Shades of light and color begin to appear as the sun gleams through the barred off window. “Will he ever be the same again doctor?” The voice of a young lady is heard as the camera fades out showing Joseph Magnus behind a sheet of glass; the lady is seen watching over him with a doctor near bye “I really can’t tell you grew up with him as a child being his sister and all, did you ever notice anything strange about him or his behavior? Violent outbursts, imaginary friends, or seclusion from others?” “No… Not that I can recall, he was the kid who was well liked. Had all the friends and all the girls wanted to date, he was like the town hero. Everyone wanted to be around him…” She answered while a tear slowly ran down her cheek as the doctor grabbed a tissue from the Kleenex box and gradually handed it to her “I just can’t believe this is happening to him, since when did he believe he was the son of GOD?” “Well as you know, police were called in; someone reported a disturbance in the local church. While police tried to apprehend him he apparently stabbed a cross trying to “drink the blood of GOD” as he claimed… He’s been here ever since.” “YES FATHER… I HEAR YOU!” Joseph Magnus screams to the sky while looking up to the heavens for an answer “Father I don’t know, they don’t believe me, they just don’t believe me what can I say?” He says as the doctor turns to his sister “If you don’t mind me asking, is your father diseased? Or is he trying to converse with the almighty again?” “No our father is alive and well, thing is he kicked out Joseph years ago and they haven’t seen each other since…” “Really? How come this wasn’t said to me earlier?” “Well I didn’t think something like that was a big deal, kids get thrown out of there houses a lot growing up it’s just something that happens” “I understand that, but maybe this is what triggered him to go temporarily insane…” “Well he was kicked out years ago, so I really don’t know what to say” “Thing is, I believe somewhere inside of him the Joseph you once knew is fighting to come back, it’s just a matter of time before that happens… If it happens at all” The scene opens with Joseph Magnus rocking back and forth fighting to come back to the world of reality
