Am I Man Enough?

14 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Silence. That’s what I like, silence. Getting away from Uriko is just what the doctor ordered. I lay in my bed watching television in my hotel room. TV definitely sucks the big one. Well, it’s three in the morning and there’s nothing good on. I do more channel surfing, all of it garbage until I stopped on a documentation. It was a documentation about a boy’s life turned upside-down in Springfield, Illinois. Jesus Christ, this can’t be happening... Could it? Everything I’m seeing is the real deal. All the photos that they are showing on the program are real. I did those murders. Seeing Steven’s mutilated body piled into six pieces. Rachel’s raped, bloody remains, even Emily’s topless, hacked-up body, they were all real! Why are they doing this to me? I know what I did and I put it behind--- “...me. Are you listening to me, you fuckin’ whelp?” I heard Steven yell from the living room. That’s all I hear from him: his constant yelling and berating and the deafening blows he inflicts on my body. I continued to ignore the man and concentrated on the task at hand: making a hand sandwich. I continue to slice ham as thin as possible. Slicing the thinnest of ham was a delicacy of mine when I was younger, also an impossible task for me. Sometimes cutting it too thin to the point where you don’t have to really poke it to punch a hole in it is too thin. I’m the kind of kid who likes a thin, sturdy piece of ham. I carefully cut a piece of ham, study it, nod with approval and placed it on one of my mayo-covered slices of bread. One down, one to go--- “What the fuck is wrong with you? When I’m talking to you, you either listen or answer me!” I jumped when I heard Steven scream in my ear. I didn’t hear come get up from his “throne” and enter the kitchen because I was too busy making myself a goddamn ham sandwich. I stopped what I was doing, not even making eye contact with the man. “Go away,” I said quietly. It wasn’t quiet enough for anyone to hear in the room, no, it was within Steven’s earshot of hearing me. His face contorted into furious anger. For a man who’s six feet, six inches tall and weighs about three hundred pounds, and for a man who is over six inches taller and outweighs by about one hundred and fifty pounds making a face like that, shit is going to hit the fan. “What did you say to me?” he said. I can feel him getting angrier by the second. And when he gets angry, shit is definitely going to hit the fucking fan. “How dare you tell me to go away? This is my fucking house and I go wherever I damn well please! Do you understand me, whelp?” I ignored him. I figured if he was going to hit me, now is his golden opportunity, but he didn’t, which somewhat surprised me. All he did was go on his little tirade. “Answer me, you piece of sh---” Steven felt a jolt of pain going inside his protruding gut. He looks down to discover the knife handle I was using to cut some ham was sticking out of his gut. Blood starts to form around the deep wound and drops of blood splattered to the tiled kitchen floor. He staggered backward, gasping for air, grabbing at everything he could to keep his balance. He grabbed at a cabinet door, but to no avail. The door ripped was easily ripped off from its hinges and went crashing to the floor. Steven’s back heel tripped on the edge of the kitchen counter and fell on his back, smacking the back of his head on the floor. He let out a yelp after this, which made it sound oh, so good. After watching him in agony for a few moments, I smiled. The shoe’s on the other foot, isn’t it, Steven? I slowly advanced towards my fallen victim, who is staring at me with eyes the size of saucers. My smile continued to grow larger and larger. “Am I man enough for you now?” I said, continuing to advance toward Steven, who is unable to do anything. No wonder Steven liked this power. He enjoyed beating on helpless victims like me. He’s been doing that for almost eight years! “Well, whelp, am I man enough for you now, huh?!” That only infuriated Steven and tried to grab at me, but I kicked his arm away. I walked over to Steven’s side and kicked him as hard as I could. He screamed out loud after that kick and that exhilarated me. “Am... BOOT! “I... BOOT! “Man... BOOT! “Enough for you?!” The last kick connected right in his face, knocking him out. I started down at him and grimaced in pain. All that kicking hurt the right side of my body. It was almost a year about when Steven picked me up over his head and threw down the basement, ten feet to my doom, and broke the right half of my ribcage. Satisfied with my work, I smiled, heavily panting. “Am I man enough for you now? That’s what your was, wasn’t it, to make me a man? Well, you can congratulate yourself because you did just that. After all this time, you berated me and abused me and look where it got you! Well, I’m not gonna have anymore of it. I’m sick of your bullshit and I’m sick of you treating me like shit! What did I ever do to you, huh? What the fuck did I ever do to you?!” I felt every being of my sanity slipping away as I yelled at the man who abused me for almost eight years. I hope to Christ that the knife wound I inflicted on him would kill him. “I remember when you told me you were gonna kill me. Not this time, Steve. When the time comes---and if you survive this---you’ll be the one that’s gonna die, not me! Christ, I’m sick of this bullshit. I’m outta here. I tried to get out of the kitchen and I felt Steven’s left hand grab a hold of my ankle and hanging on for dear life, squeezing as hard as he could. “Oh, no, you don‘t,” he struggled to say. “You’re not going anywhere. Wait until I get my hands on you...you’ll be sorry.” I turned around and stomped on his wrist. A sickening CRACK! filled the room, along with Steven’s screams of anguish. He drew his arm back, holding his wrist, which is bent unnaturally to the side, with the bone sticking out. “Oh, yes, I will. I’m getting the fuck out of here. Don’t try to find me. Because if you do, I’ll kill you.” I ran out of the kitchen this time, leaving Steven on the kitchen floor, writhing in pain. I ran down the hallway and into my room. I need to pack as much clothes as I can muster and take as much money as I can. Money...that’s something I’m definitely going to need. After stuffing enough clothes to last me a few weeks into my backpack, I ran into Steven and Rachel’s bedroom. They have a miniature safe tucked away underneath their bed. I pulled the safe out and discovered that it has a combination lock on the safe. This isn’t good. I don’t know the combination to the safe. I surveyed the room, looking for something that will break the lock off the safe. No dice, there’s nothing that can break the lock off. Then it finally hit me--- Steven keeps a Beretta in one of his dresser drawers...that’ll work! I ran over to Steven’s dresser and start searching each drawer. Nothing in the top drawer, nothing in the middle drawer---here it is! I found in the second to the last drawer! I pulled it out and examined it. A silver Beretta M92FS nine-millimeter handgun. It was mesmerizing and holding it in my hand was almost orgasmic! I walked over to the safe, aimed and fired. Okay, so I hit the safe dead center. My aim is bad, get off me. I aimed again, pulled the trigger--- BAM! I shot the lock clean off the safe. I opened the safe and discovered that there was about five hundred dollars in the safe. Eh, it’s better than a kick in the ass. I pilfered the money, stuff the handgun in the front of my jeans (I just might need it if Steven does decide to come looking for me) and exited the room. I looked back to see if Steven was still in the kitchen. Sure enough he was and he’s trying the get to his feet. He managed to remove the knife out of his gut. He looks at me, beads of sweat has covered his face. He has evil intentions on his mind. “I’m gonna kill you, you son of a bitch!” I aimed the gun at him and open fired. I managed to shoot him right in sternum. He stopped dead in his tracks, dropped to his knees and fell forward. I studied him for a moment and he didn’t move. Did I do it? Did I actually kill him? I’m not going to stick around to fine out. Without thinking, I dropped the gun on the floor. It went off, hitting one of the walls. “I’m fucking out of here.” I ran out of the kitchen and exited out of the house. Not once did I look back to see if Steven was going to come out of the house and chase after me. Not once. I proved to Steven that I was man enough for him and now the question lays dormant in everyone’s mind: am I man enough for Shadow? Correction, it should be the other way around: is Shadow man enough to face me? This coming Realm, Shadow, in forty-eight hours, we will see who walks into the ring and exits the ring the man. You saw what I did in my past, Shadow. Can you handle it? Can you handle me? Not very many people could. Everything you know, everything you’ve strived for, you’ve dreamed for, will come to an end.
