Danny Amazing, You Don't Know Me...

captured 11 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera slowly fades into the dark, shadowy halls of what appears to be the backstage area of an arena of some sort. The sound of hastened footsteps can be heard beating down the floor of the hallway as you run down the hallway in fear of what is to come. A loud, harsh voice can be heard yelling in fury.] Voice: Hey!!! Get ya punk ass back here!!! You ain’t gettin’ away, b*tch!!! [The camera rounds the corner at the end of the hallway as you trip, fall, and slide a few feet across the hallway floor. Your heavy breaths can be heard as you try your best to catch your breath and get back up to your feet. You try and try your best, but to no avail because you feel as though you may have landed on your right knee and injured it on the way down The dark, shadowy pursuer slowly rounds the corner wearing all black with what appears to be a black baseball bat in his gloved hand. The figure begins chuckling to himself and just shakes his head at you, his now wounded prey.] Shadowy figure: Finally! The time has come, b*tch! You’ve been holdin’ me back for way too long! Ya hear me?? Way too f*ckin’ long! [The figure leans forward and continues to move closer and closer to you as you try with all your might to block out the pain of your injured knee and try to get away. Shadowy figure: This cat has been sittin’ back waitin, just waitin’ to see the light of day, but you’ve hidden me away, but no more!! [You try to pick yourself up to your feet, but the shadowy figure lunges forward and seizes you by your right ankle and just gazes down at you.] Shadowy figure: Hmmph! You’re not goin’ anywhere, lil man! [You try to struggle free from his grasp and finally spit in his face in defiance. The shadowy figure wipes the spit from off his face and instantly flares up in rage as he grabs your right ankle once again.] Shadowy figure: Oh, so you think you funny, huh?? Is that it?? [The shadowy figure begins kicking you over and over in the back of your injured right knee sending a surge of pain shooting up and down your leg. The shadowy figure continues pounding away as if he is a madman that has lost all control over himself.] Shadowy figure: You gonna disrespect me, after all the sh*t this cat’s had to put up with you, huh??? No one in their right f*ckin’ mind eva disrespects me and lives to tell that sh*t, b*tch!!! [The shadowy figure lifts your battered, beaten, and weary body up to its feet and leans you against the wall. You try to cover up and defend yourself as he begins wailing away at you with hard, stiff shots to the kidneys, ribs, chest, and midsection with thunderous blow after thunderous blow from his baseball bat.] Shadowy figure: Ya hear me?? No one!!! Ya got that, b*tch?? [The shadowy figure seizes by your throat and gazes deep into your eyes.] Shadowy figure: Look at me when you bein’ holla’d at!! [The shadowy figure takes one final swing at your chest and just watches you drop down to the floor in a heap as you begin coughing up your own blood. The shadowy figure watches this in amusement as he begins circling you and slapping you in the back of your head.] Shadowy figure: Look at you. Look at you!! Coughin’ up your own blood like a lil pussy! Man up! Man up, b*tch! Oughta be ashamed of your lil weak ass! You’ve been holdin’ the both of us back ever since you set foot in L-O-Dubb!! But that’s soon gonna change! [The shadowy figure quickly rolls you over, grabs your leg, and holds it down in place on the floor with his boot as you writhe in pain after the onslaught. He looks down at you in fury and takes quick swing with the black baseball bat and nails you square in the kneecap. You instantly clutch your knee and begin rolling around on the floor in agony. As you clutch your knee in pain you begin waving your free hand in the air hoping that your attacker will have some compassion and even an ounce of mercy for you, but he just looks down at you in amusement and begins laughing to himself as he points the black baseball bat at your head.] Shadowy figure: Ya know somethin’?? Time’s up for you! No more playin’ games! Game ova! This cat finally finished with your weak, sorry ass for good! [The shadowy figure raises the baseball bat high above his head and roars with maniacal laughter.] Shadowy figure: Say goodnight, the conqueror’s takin’ this operation ova from now on!!! [The shadowy figure takes a quick swing at your head. All of a sudden the camera switches to a bedroom where Tony Morrison can be seen quickly waking up in a cold sweat. He looks around the room, half in shock and half in relief that it was only a dream that he was experiencing. He turns around and sits at the edge of his bed. He lowers his head and clutches at the mask on his face as if he is now reflecting on the nightmare he just had. ] Tony Morrison: In due time…in due time… [The camera slowly fades to black.] Later that day… [The camera slowly fades into a rundown gym in Harlem where Tony Morrison can be seen sitting on the edge of the bench after finishing a set of bench press reps.. He is wearing a white tanktop t-shirt that is stretched snugly over his chest, a silver necklace with a cross on the end, the usual black mask on his face, and black baggy sweats with a pair of black Nike gym shoes on his feet. He stares staring blankly into the distance as he grabs his towel and wipes away the sweat from his face. The thoughts of all the events that have taken place recently in his life and his career begin to race in and out of his head at the speed of comets in the sky as he takes a quick break from lifting. Things haven’t been going as well as Tony would like lately with his loss at this past LOW Realm at the hands of The Demond. Despite his failure, he yet again has a chance at glory as he steps into the ring “The Family” member, Danny Amazing. Tony grabs a remote control from off the floor beside him and turns on a small t.v that is sitting across from him on a table. To his dismay, an LOW commercial highlighting the latest rise of “The Family” begins to play. Images of Dark Haze, Deguicci, Danny Amazing, and Volcano flash across the screen and Tony Morrison instantly turns the t.v off in disgust and bows his head in thought.] “LOW Subliminal Dawn…Dark Haze…”The Family”…Danny Amazing…so much to be prepared for, so lil time. Many cats gonna be steppin’ up to the plate lookin’ for their chance to make their presence felt in L-O Dubb, but when its all said and done there only gonna be one man’s name etched in the minds of everyone at Subliminal Dawn…” [The camera slowly fades out and then fades back in as Tony Morrison can be seen pounding on the heavy bag with his wrapped fists.] “…Dark Haze? Dark Haze??” [As the thought of Dark Haze echoes through his mind over and over and over again he begins pounding on the heavy bag with more and more successive force.] “…Dark Haze??? F*ck a Dark Haze! F*ck “The Family”! My name gonna be the only damn thing people gonna be talkin’ ‘bout when its all said and done! Tony’s been quiet long enough! Tony’s been watchin’ and waitin’ for the perfect time and perfect opportunity to make his move! Tony may not be steppin’ foot into the ring with Haze, but when the show’s ova there will be only one of us two still walkin’ and talkin’ that talk in L-O-Dubb! Tony got plans! Tony got big plans for Haze! Tony gonna end Haze’s career in L-O-Dubb or see the end of Tony Morrison in the process! And that’s a guarantee! “ This cat gonna make his presence felt and make an impact on “The Family”. [Tony Morrison takes a break from pounding on the heavy bag and wipes the sweat from his face.] “When Haze finally taken care of the only business left to handle is this Danny Amazin’..” [Tony Morrison begins to laugh to himself softly at the thought of his upcomin’ match.] “What’s funny ‘bout this whole match is the fact that this cat don’t even know what he up against at Subliminal Dawn. He don’t even have a clue. All he knows is that he goin’ toe-to-toe with a Tony Morrison, a name he saw on a f*ckin’ piece of paper or posta somewhere. He don’t know jack sh*t ‘bout the trials and tribulations this cat had to go through to get here, to get to this point in his career. All he knows, is that he steppin’ in the ring with a cat named Tony Morrison…” [Tony just shakes his head and laughs outloud.] “…just anotha wrestla’. He don’t know ‘bout the man that watched his pops walk out the door on his family way back when he was a lil kid comin’ up in NYC! Nah. This Danny Amazin’ don’t know ‘bout the man that once got stabbed in his f*ckin’ back by the one man in this game that he felt that he could trust with his own life, the man that he had been through thick and thin with since the beginnin’, his own f*ckin’ brotha!” [The pent up anger and rage from within Tony begins to creep out little by little as events from his past begin to flow through his mind. Tony Morrison once again turns towards the heavy bag and begins wailing away at it in an attempt to focus and channel his rage.] “He don’t know ‘bout the man that had to fight for his survival, fight for his respect, and fight to put food his damn table each and every night! Danny Amazin’ don’t know ‘bout the man that had to go through punk ass afta punk ass to make a name for himself on his block and gain the respect he still holds today! This b*tch don’t even know ‘bout the long list of chumps that have stepped up to the plate against him only to get silenced and see their careers come to a sudden close!” [Tony Morrison winds up and takes a wild swing at the heavy bag and nails it square up with one final thunderous blow. Tony Morrison is literally boiling over with emotion as if there’s something, a feeling, or certain degree of aggression within himself that he’s held suppressed for a long time. Tony Morrison takes a deep breath and tries to regain his composure. Despite his very best efforts he continues to tremble with anticipation and frustration.] “Danny Amzin’ don’t know jack ‘bout the man he steppin’ in the ring with! He don’t jack ‘bout the man behind this f*ckin’ mask! All he knows is that he facin’ a Tony Morrison, a name! Well, this cat hopes he ready! He hopes Danny Amazin’ is damn ready come our match at Subliminal Dawn cuz he ain’t steppin’ in the ring with name on a posta! He ain’t tanglin’ with a bodyguard in a mask! He ain’t gonna be facin’ just anotha wrestla that happens to be named Tony Morrison! He gonna be up against a man on a mission, a man that knows no bounds in this game, a man with a history in this game, and a man that can and will silence him and end his f*ckin’ career in that ring as he knows it with no questions asked! ‘Nuff said’! [Tony Morrison leans against the heavy bag and his cellphone begins to ring. Tony Morrison walks over to his gym bag, snatches out his cellphone, and answers.] Tony Morrison: Yo! [A soft, sweet, female’s voice can be heard on the other end.] Female’s Voice: So, you finished workin’ out at the gym, boo? [Tony Morrison just shakes his head and rolls his eyes.] Tony Morrison: Finished? Finished?? You know damn well what’s in store at Subliminal Dawn, Jaz! This ain’t just any night here. This a special occasion that’s comin’ up. Bein’ prepared is key. You know the damn drill with me! Female’s Voice: [sighs] Yeah, yeah, yeah. I know. I know how seriously you take these things and all. I was just wondering if you could spare a little bit of time from your busy schedule to uh…I don’t know, maybe spend a lil quality time with me. [Tony just growls at the statement.] Tony Morrison: This is important business, aight?? They’ll be time for some play lata! Right now its all about comin’ through in a big way at Subliminal Dawn. If all goes well the night could be huge. Female’s Voice: Huge…that’s what your partner in crime’s been telling me since day one. That’s what your all knowing advisor has been spitting in my ears for just as long. Now, I understand how big this all could be to you and them, but what about us? [Tony raises an eyebrow in confusion.] Tony Morrison: Well, what about us? Female’s Voice: You know how long its been since I’ve seen you? It seems like an eternity. I can’t stand this time apart any longer! Phone conversations and seeing you on t.v, just aren’t cutting it for me anymore! I wanna be with you! [Tony Morrison just shakes his head.] Tony Morrison: Look, just suck it up for a lil while longa, aight?? That’s all you gotta do! And besides you’ll get to see me at Subliminal Dawn so quit your damn whinin’… Female’s Voice: I don’t wanna watch you on t.v. I wanna be with you, right there. Tony Morrison: Ya know, if you had let me get the words out you would’ve known that we headin’ out there together for L-O Dubb’s Subliminal Dawn. Female’s Voice: [surprised] What?? You mean it?? You’re bringin’ me along?? [Tony laughs to himself.] Tony Morrison: Why wouldn’t you be brought along to celebrate the occasion with yo mans? When everything goes down and this cat walkin’ out the ring with his hands raised high in the air you betta be out there bringin’ that support and all. Female’s Voice: Oh, don’t worry, baby. I’ll be there. [Tony Morrison nods his head in approval.] Tony Morrison: Good! Now you betta make sure you packed and ready to leave! Holla back! Peace! [Tony Morrison hangs up the cellphone and tosses it back in the bag. He then lowers his head.] Tony Morrison: [to himself] In due time…in due time, Vachon. You will be proud. [The camera slowly fades to black as Tony Morrison grabs his bag and makes his way to the locker room to get changed.]
