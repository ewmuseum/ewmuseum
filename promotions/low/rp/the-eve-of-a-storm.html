The Eve of a Storm!

12 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~ (Christianity) the act of delivering from sin or saving from evil- Definition of Redemption from the Dictionary. ~ ~ Well this sure is an interesting turn of events, is it not? No not for me, for you see the transpiring events are all happening to my ultimate desire. For I have a vision of the future of LOW! A dawning of a new era is about to begin, and be fore warned because no one will be safe from its wrath!! Some have already departed LOW in fear to what might happen….. Tessa, so sorry to see you go my dear. We had a lot of history you and me in such a short time. And to the others that have left our humbled abode known as LOW I say this…..Smart! Yes very smart to leave before the coming of the storm. Hell even LOW’s hero Annihilation left the land of the living to escape the hell that is about to happen. If it sounds like I’m quite passionate about this, if it sounds like I’m even excited about this….it’s because I am! The circle is almost complete and LOW will never be the same again! ~ ~ Redemption of wicked acts will no longer go unpunished….. ~ Time: 12:02 AM Date: November, 9th 2004 Location: Airport terminal in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania - The scene is one of complete anarchy as a swarm of people are leaving and coming into Pittsburgh, and for some this country. The scene goes off to one of the corners at Gate 20 where a man is waiting for the incoming 12:30 flight from London England. As the man is waiting he sees a couple sitting across from him from afar, it’s a family of 4 waiting for there flight. The father of the family is getting bugged by a passing religious fanatic passing out fliers of how to save your soul. And to the opposite side of the family security is watching a couple of Iranian passengers also waiting for there flight. Sitting there and waiting for the flight to come in, ones own thoughts happen to drift as the man remembers why he is there so late at night waiting for the flight to come in……….- ~ A woman’s love for her daughter will be the fuel for a new beginning….. ~ ~ Flash Back. ~ Time: 9:49PM Date: November, 4th 2004 Location: Gross Memorial Coliseum, Hays Kansas. - The scene opens up with a man standing by himself in an empty stadium. Why you might ask? Well by the looks of things, the answer is coming around that corner. A tall slender woman with exotic silver hair comes around the corner to meet with him. - ~ Messenger of chaos she will become…… ~ Woman: John Adams I presume? John Adams: Then you would presume right, Luna Storm. Luna Storm: You never did tell me how you know my name? John Adams: I was an avid watcher of MW and all of there talent, that is why I have come to meet with you. Luna Storm: Well there is plenty of talent here, why would you have come to see me? John Adams: Simple my dear, I feel your pain and sorrow! Luna, I know you lost a daughter, I know that you’re once love has her know! What I offer you today is a chance at redemption! A chance of taking back your life and regaining the one thing you most desire…..your daughter! - Luna’s face was shocked to see that one man knew so much of her life. - ~ The Prophetic word she will enforce….. ~ Luna Storm: But... I… John Adams: Join me, help me complete the circle and all of your fears, and all of your doubts will be gone. And Luna all you have to do is take my hand and accept me in to your life! - John Adams has his hand extended to hers in hopes that she will take it. - ~ Born into a new life in a Realm of darkness……… ~ Luna Storm: I…don’t…. John Adams: Take it Luna; I am the key to your salvation! - All of a sudden there is a moment of silence that seems to last for oblivion. And then in an instance with a little hesitation in there she takes his hand……which is quickly switched with a LOW contract and a plane ticket. - ~ And out of the ashes of the forgotten…… ~ John Adams: I will wait for you at the airport in a few days! Luna if you look inside yourself you will see that I am right! ~ The flashback scene fades. ~ Time: 12:45 AM Date: November, 9th 2004 Location: Airport terminal in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania - Yes that’s what went on a couple of days ago, but what for? What was the purpose of recruiting a women wrestler? He has his motives, and his reasons. But what is more on his mind is the match between him and The Demond with the Realm title on the line. A junkyard match, what a joke I mean who did they think they were kidding? - ~ A circle will be formed out of Prophecy…… ~ ::::: Now arriving from gate 20 the 12:30 flight from London England ::::: - The voice of the announcer over the intercom broke “The Prophet’s “train of thought as people start to get off the arriving plane. Was she on the plane? Did she accept his offer? All of those answers were soon laid to rest as the 5’11’’ 120 pound silver haired beauty stepped out of the plane to greet him. - ~ To ravage the land of the wicked…… ~ Luna as she walked up to the Prophet: Lad, you better be right! John Adams: Darling Luna, your life as you know it will only change for the better! - The last scene to see is the parking lot of the airport as John Adams and Luna Storm get on the motorcycle and drive off, to the arena, to LOW. - ~ And to complete the circle of The Prophecy! ~ ~ Well there you have it, only a small piece to the puzzle in this grand design I have for LOW. The battle field has been set and at Realm 62 I will show that no one is safe from me, for I will be there judge, jury, and executioner! Fear me, despise me, and hide in fear for my will shall be heard, my force shall be felt, and my domination shall be seen! ~ ~ 'Humanity has advanced, when it has advanced, not because it has been sober, responsible, and cautious, but because it has been playful, rebellious, and immature.' 'If little else, the brain is an educational toy.' 'Disbelief in magic can force a poor soul into believing in government and business.' –Tom Robbins ~ ~ Born into the hellish night, but raised by the Prophetic light. – John “The Prophet” Adams. ~ ~ FIN ~
