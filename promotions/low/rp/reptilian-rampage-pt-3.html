Reptilian Rampage pt. 3

26 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**When we last left our heros, the Silver Ranger had fallen into the clutches of the evil Queen Mogombu. This unfortunate turn of events has left the Gold Ranger alone to continue to wage the age old war against evil. What will happen? Will the Gold Ranger fail the first true test put before the Metallo Zords? Will the Silver Ranger ever escape the captivating bonds that have held him away from helping his team mate? Find out all this and more in this, the exciting conclusion of the Reptilian Rampage.** ::The scene re-opens to the control center. Just as Gold Ranger tells his friends to stay put, he waits to be teleported to face off against Tim and his evil cohorts. In an instant, the Gold Ranger disappears out of the Control Center. The scene cuts to the woodland area in which Tim and his henchmen are about to square off with Sammie. Gold Ranger is zapped down not far from these villainous swine. He spots his weapon, a long talking staff, not far from where he is standing. He runs over and picks it up:: Gold Ranger - Gold Ranger is here and he has harnessed the power of Singing Sonya the Staff! Yeah! Silver Ranger must have dropped this here when he was captured. ::Tim turns to Egg Suckin’ Sammie and begins barking orders.:: Tim - You! Attack! Egg Suckin’ Sammie - No!! Not until I get some supercalo protein eggs! Tim - The Metallo Zords have all the supercalo protein eggs! Attack, and you can have them! Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Really? Lulu de Blu - Yeah! With sprinkles on 'em! Delish! ::Tim, Lulu de Blu, and Big Bad Barry Bear throw themselves out of the way as Egg Suckin’ Sammie charges past. His monstrous fingers claw convulsively at the air as he reaches the edge of the ravine. Egg Suckin’ Sammie stamps at the ground like an enraged bull, showing two feet with large pointy appendages. Now working himself into a frenzy, the snarling monster beats at his chest and then knocks some small boulders into the ravine. These rocks have little effect upon the Gold Ranger, who is standing down there.:: Gold Ranger - Sonya Sonya - Whoa! Gold Ranger - Let's send this guy back to where he came from! Egg Suckin’ Sammie - I want supercalo protein eggs! ::The Gold Ranger leaps up out of the ravine. Egg Suckin’ Sammie charges at him, easily knocking him down. The Gold Ranger falls to the corner of the ravine, dropping Singing Sonya as he does. Sammie charges at the Gold Ranger to inflict more damage but Sonya the Staff positions herself so that he trips over her. Sammie begins to tumble but doesn’t leave his feet. He looks briefly at the staff but then turns his attention back to the Gold Ranger. He is caught off guard by the Gold Ranger but is able to dodge his lariat attempt. Sammie then rakes him across the chest with his clawed fingers. The Gold Ranger rolls back and stands up, the front of his suit now scorched. His Staff Sonya rejoins him, and he goes into battle pose. Tim, Lulu de Blu, and Big Bad Barry Bear come running up to join Egg Suckin’ Sammie.:: Lulu de Blu - You'd better leave, or I'll be forced to raise my voice in anger! Go get 'em, Egg Suckin’ Sammie! Be gone! Skedaddle! Big Bad Barry Bear - Yeah! What he said! ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie raises his hands in triumph, but makes no further move to attack. The Gold Ranger waits to see what will happen next.:: Lulu de Blu - Huh? Tim - Egg Suckin’ Sammie! Attack them! Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Yes! I'll mash them into stardust! ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie goes over to a pile of large rocks. Three cutaway shots show him throwing them, and before long the Gold Ranger finds himself on the defensive against a steady bombardment.:: Gold Ranger - Hey! That’s not nice! ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie laughs.:: Big Bad Barry Bear - Hey Egg Suckin’ Sammie, you really know how to rock! ::Tim charges up his sword and fires blasts at the Ranger, knocking him back into the ravine. Big Bad Barry Bear, Lulu de Blu, Tim, and Egg Suckin’ Sammie step up to the edge to gloat.:: Lulu de Blu - Hey! Tim, you were splendiferous! ::Big Bad Barry Bear laughs happily; Lulu de Blu nods. Egg Suckin’ Sammie says nothing.:: Tim - Yeah! ::Back in the woods, The Silver Ranger has started to work his way out of the net. Ich Van Herbe #2 has been joined by a fellow Patroller, and both are standing guard below. Fortunately for The Silver Ranger, they are mostly engrossed in an examination of his utility band and com link.:: Gold Ranger's voice - ::via com link:: The Silver Ranger, come in, man; can you hear me?? ::The Silver Ranger stares down at the two Ich Van Herbes(both of which have their backs turned), and starts to plot his next move.:: ::Back in the mountains, Lulu de Blu, Big Bad Barry Bear, and Tim are still laughing to themselves. But Egg Suckin’ Sammie has had enough; he turns and runs away.:: Tim - Huh? ::Tim starts to pursue Egg Suckin’ Sammie, along with Lulu de Blu and Big Bad Barry Bear.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - I'll never get my supercalo protein eggs! They're gone! Leave me alone! Lulu de Blu - Egg Suckin’ Sammie! W-where ya going, Egg Suckin’ Sammie? Get a box or something! Yeah, get a box! ::The distraught Egg Suckin’ Sammie soon out-distances them and returns to his broken eggs in misery. He kneels down, holding the shells.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - My supercalo protein eggs! They ate my supercalo protein eggs! ::Tears trickle down his scaly face. Queen Mogambu appears to be on Earth now, as she approaches Egg Suckin’ Sammie.:: Queen Mogambu - So Sammie, why the long face? ::Once again, Egg Suckin’ Sammie gently sets down the shells before standing. Queen Mogambu laughs.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - What's so -funny-? Queen Mogambu - Do as I command, and you shall have your eggs! Do you promise to obey? ::The exhausted Egg Suckin’ Sammie sighs heavily.:: Queen Mogambu - Watch this! ::The orb on Queen Mogambu's Wand emits a green spark. Egg Suckin’ Sammie's broken eggs glow brightly and suddenly become whole again.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Oh boy! My eggs!! ::Queen Mogambu laughs and casts a second spell, returning the eggs to their broken state.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Oh! No!! ::The cruel sorceress laughs again.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - My eggs! Queen Mogambu - You want eggs? Destroy the Metallo Zords! Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Anything you say! Queen Mogambu - Now grow, Egg Suckin’ Sammie, GROW!! ::Queen Mogambu throws her Wand. It falls out of the sky in its usual manner and enlarges Egg Suckin’ Sammie in the regular way. The 'spring-blades' extend from his wrists, and a pair of horns grow out of his head. One of his larger eyes opens up. He starts to chuckle gleefully; a sound with little sanity left in it. Queen Mogambu watches from the ground, well satisfied with her own cunning.:: Queen Mogambu - Get 'em, Egg Suckin’ Sammie! Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Yeess, my Evilness! ::Back down in the ravine---:: Gold Ranger: I need Gold Gargantuan Power now! :: The Gold Gargantuan, a humongous golden robotic machine, raises from behind the ravine. There is a brief shot of Gold Ranger in the cockpit, followed by one of the travelling Gargantuan.:: Gold Ranger - Engage The Gold Gargantuan Battle Mode! Voice - The Gold Gargantuan Sequence has been initiated! ::The Gold Gargantuan contorts to a thicker, more intimidating looking shape.:: Voice - The Gold Gargantuan activated! ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie kicks at the ground.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Eggs! I want eggs! Gold Ranger - ::in cockpit:: I don't know what he's talking about, but I'd better stop this thing before he does some real damage! ::The Gold Gargantuan blocks Egg Suckin’ Sammie's first kick, but is struck by his second. Egg Suckin’ Sammie rushes forward to grab hold of The Gold Gargantuan, but is easily pushed away.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Eggs! Queen Mogambu says you have eggs! I want them now! ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie darts past The Gold Gargantuan, slashing it as he goes.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Give them! ::The Gold Gargantuan whirls around to face Egg Suckin’ Sammie. The Gold Ranger lays down the law.:: Gold Ranger - ::in cockpit:: Back off! I do not have any eggs! Queen Mogambu lied to you! If you do not stop fighting, I am going to have to make you stop! Got it? Egg Suckin’ Sammie - I'm gonna get you! ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie stabs his right wrist blades into The Gold Gargantuan, pushing it back a few steps. The Gold Gargantuan steps deftly aside, and Egg Suckin’ Sammie's intended follow-up goes wild. He turns around to continue the attack, quivering with rage. The Gold Gargantuan blocks his kick, but fails to avoid another wrist-blade raking.:: Gold Ranger - ::in cockpit:: All right; time to finish this execute this egg evangelist! ::The Gold Gargantuan restrains Egg Suckin’ Sammie, but can't hold him down for long. After pulling free, Egg Suckin’ Sammie slashes The Gold Gargantuan twice across the chest in a criss-cross manner. The combined force of these blows is enough to spin The Gold Gargantuan around.:: Gold Ranger - ::in cockpit:: Whoa!! ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie laughs crazily.:: Gold Ranger - ::in cockpit:: This lizard is not going down easily! I have got to give it all I've got! Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Give me the eggs! Now! Give me! ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie slams both blade-enhanced hands into The Gold Gargantuan's chest.:: Queen Mogambu - Yeahhh!!! ::The Gold Gargantuan topples over onto its side.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Give me! Eggs! Gold Ranger - ::in cockpit:: Oh man! Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Now I'm going to finish you off, Gold Ranger, once and for all! Gold Ranger - This reptile is a lot tougher than he looks! Egg Suckin’ Sammie - EGGS!!! Gold Ranger - ::in cockpit:: I could sure use The Silver Ranger's help! Where is he? ::Apparently convinced that the Metallo Zords no longer pose a threat, Queen Mogambu turns her attention to a nearby dam.:: Queen Mogambu - Hah; the dam! ::She looks up to Egg Suckin’ Sammie:: Queen Mogambu - Okay..now stomp it to the ground! Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Yeahh-heh-heh-heh-heh! ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie focuses in on the dam.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - We'll soak them! ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie laughs and walks off towards the dam.:: Gold Ranger - ::in cockpit:: He's heading for the dam! Egg Suckin’ Sammie - We are victorious! Heh! Gold Ranger - ::in cockpit:: That's what you think! ::The Gold Gargantuan stands back up and follows after Egg Suckin’ Sammie.:: ::Back in the woods, the two Ich Van Herbes are still engrossed in their study of The Silver Ranger's com link and utility band. Consequently, they fail to notice the owner of these items clambering down the net. The Silver Ranger checks on his lax guardians one last time, and seeing their backs still turned, swings into action. His fists slam into each Ich Van Herbe's back, knocking both of them down. His utility band and com link go flying. One of the Ich Van Herbes has ceased to move, but its companion is still capable of putting up a fight. This Ich Van Herbe spin-kicks at The Silver Ranger, who dodges and retaliates with a kick of his own, of the sweet chin variety. Another Ich Van Herbe charges into the fray. The Silver Ranger quickly blocks its initial attacks and then ducks under its swing. He too swings at its head, and the Ich Van Herbe ducks in an identical manner. The Silver Ranger ducks a kick, repeatedly punches the Ich Van Herbe's chest, and then kicks him in the stomach. The Ich Van Herbe doubles over in pain, and while he is doing so, the Silver Ranger hooks him in a DDT setup position. He lifts and drops the Ich Van Herbe with a big implant DDT. The Ich Van Herbes vanish, defeated at last. The Silver Ranger recovers his utility band and com link.:: Gold Ranger's voice - ::via com link:: The Silver Ranger! The Silver Ranger; do you read me? The Silver Ranger - ::to com link:: I am right here, buddy. Where are you? Gold Ranger's voice - ::via com link:: I am at the Widow’s Willow Dam! I need your help! Hurry! The Silver Ranger - ::to com link:: I am on my way! ::After a short moment, the Silver Ranger arrives at the battleground.:: Silver Ranger - All right! Let’s get dangerous! ::The Silver Sentinel, a gigantic silver robotic creature emerges from the ravine to guard the dam against the rampaging Egg Suckin’ Sammie. With a roar, it lashes him with his tail, and then steps aside to avoid a blow. The Silver Sentinel deflects a kick, claws at Egg Suckin’ Sammie, and then launches another swinging tail attack. Egg Suckin’ Sammie rolls under the tail and knocks the Silver Sentinel back next to the The Gold Gargantuan. The Silver Sentinel is right back up and going after Egg Suckin’ Sammie again. Egg Suckin’ Sammie jumps over the Sentinel’s tail, then blocks an attack from the Battle Mode Silver Sentinel and pushes it aside. He slashes the Gold Gargantuan across the chest, and both Metallo Zords look to be in trouble now. The Gold Gargantuan strikes Egg Suckin’ Sammie. He spins, rolls back, and quickly stands again. Electricity flows through his horns. It blasts into both the Gold Gargantuan and the Silver Sentinel.:: Gold Ranger - ::in Gold Gargantuan:: Switch Modes! ::The Gold Gargantuan starts to re-assemble itself into what it was at the beginning of the battle.:: Gold Ranger - ::in cockpit:: We need Toady McToad now! ::Toady McToad arrives.:: Gold and Silver Rangers - ::in cockpit:: The Massive Metallo Mashing Machine! Power Up! ::The Silver Sentinel flies up in the air and connects to the top of the Gold Gargantuan. The two machines intertwine, forming half of a giant fighting machine. They rise off of the ground and land on top of the horizontal Toady McToad. Together, they form The Massive Metallo Mashing Machine.:: Gold Ranger - ::in cockpit:: Let's finish it! ::Egg Suckin’ Sammie sneers and starts to charge.:: Gold Ranger - ::in cockpit:: All right, Silver Ranger! Silver Ranger - ::in cockpit:: Prepare for devastation! ::The Massive Metallo Mashing Machine walks over to Egg Suckin’ Sammie, punching him in the chest. Egg Suckin’ Sammie holds his chest in pain and his head is grabbed by the Massive Metallo Mashing Machine, 4M for short. The 4M grabs Sammie by the head and sets him up in a powerbomb position. The 4M lifts the giant monster and hauls him high above the ground. The Massive Metallo Mashing Machine slam him down and takes a step back. As Egg Suckin’ Sammie falls to the ground, he can be heard shouting.:: Egg Suckin’ Sammie - Ohh...nooo!!!!! Please... EGGS! ::And with a final strangled cry, Egg Suckin’ Sammie explodes.:: Gold Ranger - ::in cockpit:: Guess we took care of you, Frogface! ::Toady McToad roars in triumph. The Metallo Zords celebrate inside the The Massive Metallo Mashing Machine cockpit.:: Gold Ranger - All right! Silver Ranger - Yeah! Leaping Lena the Lasso - All right! Singing Sonya the Staff - We did it! Queen Mogambu - You'll PAY for THIS!! ::Cuts to a shot of the fair again. The Metallo Zords enter the scene. The Gold Ranger contacts Bobbalu. Silver Ranger stands near by to listen in, while Brainiac Benji appears in the background watching the Talent Show.:: The Gold Ranger - ::to com link:: Good news, Bobbalu. We destroyed Queen Mogambu's plans -again-! The Silver Ranger - ::to com link:: Yeah, it was Metallonominal! ::This is accompanied by laughter.:: Bobbalu - ::communicating:: Congratulations, Metallo Zords, on a job well done! Burt's voice - Hey! ::Burt takes them by surprise as he emerges from behind a concession stand. The Gold Ranger quickly covers his com link.:: Burt - Where have you guys been? You're up next! ::The two of them hurry to join the Talent Show.:: Afro Otis - ::to The Silver Ranger & The Gold Ranger:: Rock the house, fellas! ::Burt is now up on stage, apparently acting as a host. Stagehands are setting up a couple of wooden blocks for The Silver Ranger and The Gold Ranger's performance.:: Burt - ::at microphone:: Okay, next up we got The Silver Ranger and The Gold Ranger. Let's give 'em a big hand, huh? ::The Silver Ranger and The Gold Ranger head for the stage accompanied by applause and cheering. The Gold Ranger is now wearing his black belt also. They leap up onto the stage, bow to the expectant crowd, and slowly sweep their arms up to their chest in a focusing sequence. They turn their heads rapidly from side to side, punch the air from side to side, and then go into a couple of different defensive stances. They front-kick, spin-kick, and then turn to face each other with a flurry of rapid hand movements. The Gold Ranger spin-kicks and low-kicks. The Silver Ranger ducks and jumps accordingly. He blocks The Gold Ranger and throws two spinning kicks, first high and then low. The Gold Ranger ducks, jumps, and then blocks a punch. Both of them pose to the front and then shatter the boards. The Gold Ranger and The Silver Ranger bow once more to a burst of warm applause. Their friends are shown clapping. Afro Otis jokingly punches at the air. As they walk off down the central aisle, a man in the audience gives The Silver Ranger a pat on the back.:: Burt's voice - ::through microphone:: And now...Vill & Eee! ::Vill & Eee are now up on the stage playing their instruments and singing boisterously, accompanied by a drummer. Vill's long hair is worn down underneath his odd gray hat.:: Vill - ::singing:: My name is -Vill-! Eee - ::singing:: My name's Eee! Together - ::singing:: Together we are villainy! Vill - ::singing:: We're tough! Eee - ::singing:: We're meann! Together - ::singing:: And that's the truuuuthh! Vill - ::singing:: 'cos we're baadd!! Eee - ::singing:: We're baadd!! Vill - ::singing:: All you geeks out ready to scam!! Eee - ::singing:: Yeahh, ready to scam!!! Vill - ::singing:: 'cos we're cool! How cool? Eee - ::singing:: So coooooooooooo-owwwwwwwwwhhh... ::A girl in the audience widens her eyes in amazement as Vill & Eee dance around manically on the stage. Several audience members shake their heads, some looking mildly shocked. Vill & Eee finish their number in a crouched position, out of breath. Vill's sunglasses and hat have fallen off during his performance, and his hair now hangs loose around his sweaty face. Vill smiles widely and has a bit of trouble standing back up. Eee bows twice. Unfortunately for them, this sort of music is evidently not to the liking of the audience; their reaction is a mixture of hostility and amazed disbelief. The room is utterly silent, except for the sound of the Zords and their friends chortling away at the back.:: Pleasantly Plump Patty – So, aren’t you guys proud of saving the world again? The Metallo Zords ::collectively:: - All in a day’s work. The Silver Ranger – Say, do you guys want to go get some ice cream or something like that? Thin n' Trin Tammy - Sounds good to me. I could really go for a banana split. The Gold Ranger - I'll race you to the ice cream parlor. Last one there is a rotten egg! ::The Silver Ranger, the Gold Ranger, and their friends laugh at the joke and they all share friendly smiles. The scene slowly fades out as the group races away.::
