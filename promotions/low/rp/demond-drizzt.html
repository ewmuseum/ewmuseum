Demond = Drizzt ???

14 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** What a difference… a week makes. ** :: We open late in the day this day. Normally when the LOW promo channel, who conveniently spies on each and EVERY one of your favorite LOW superstars, fires to life and focuses on the LOW Realm Champion, The Demond, we see the clam serenity of the interior of Demond and Amy Lee’s home. However this day, we see a new surrounding. The interior of a building yes, but the calm and serenity…no. The interior of this building is dank, dark, wet, and hot. We walk down a long corridor, an exposed pipe dripping steaming hot water onto the floor below. The sound of a rat running across the floor could be heard, and the camera suddenly seemed to be moving faster…obviously the cameraman didn’t like being in this hallway anymore than anyone would like to be in this hallway. A heavy iron door swings open at the end of the hall and we open into a huge lit training room. A ring is set up in the center of the room, which will be the center of our attention for the moment, but for now, lets scan the room. Hung from the rafters are banners from various different Pay Per View events and TV Shows. “ AOW Revenge “ “ AOW Obituary Entry “ “ WEWF Kill Em All “ they’re all different and brightly colored. To the right is a small office built into the wall, with a big window large enough to see the entire complex from. Inside that window, we can see Amy Lee watching something on a huge screened TV. We turn our attention now to the center of the room once again and to the ring. The Demond stands tall in the center, flanked in the front and the rear by the impressive so far Jester X and Al. Al is behind Demond, and the Realm Champion seems to be watching him from the corner of his eye. Jester charges in and Demond easily deflects a straight punch to the side, and twists Jester’s arm, turning him downwards and away from the champion. Demond saw the deception for what it was though and turned quickly, putting a boot to Al’s jaw as he ran in. Demond smirked and suddenly fell, as Jester landed a picture perfect drop kick to the LOW Realm Champions back. Demond hit the mat and lay there a long moment, not moving, but staring straight up at the ceiling. Jester was in a knelt position and Al was still putting his jaw back in place after that boot, and they both remained very still, watching the huge man lying there. Demond finally took in a deep breath and pounded both fists on the mat in anger. He let out a deep sigh that sounded more like a curse and sat straight up off the mat. He looked over at Jester and nodded his head at a job well done by him, but then turned it into a disapproving shake of his head at his own failure. Jester and Al were both breathing heavy and seemingly fighting for every breath. But above it all Demond seemed calmer than he had in days. :: Jester: “ D man, no need to be angry, that’s only the third time I’ve hit you in four hours. “ Al: “ Yeah and all four times have been in THIS hour! “ The Demond: “ Still, if I want to be perfect come Catastrophic, I need to not be hit at all during these training sessions. You guys are my friends and only trying to help, what am I going to do when the men in the ring with me are angry and ready to take my head off and I make a mistake like that? “ Jester: “ You wont, don’t worry. We’ve got Maximum Carnage scouted so well that you probably know them and their style better than they do. “ Al: “ And you’ve always got us down there on Sunday letting you know where to watch your back and when. We can’t get in there, or else you’ll be DQ’ed, but we’ll damn sure make sure it’s good and fair. “ The Demond: “ Yeah I know, but still I have to be perfect or else I’m not going to be the champion anymore, and I can’t lose this strap if I intend on being World Champion any time soon. “ :: Amy stepped out from the office and walked toward the ring, smiling, and one hand on her hip. :: Amy: “ Now, now, that sounds like doubt D. And we all know you have none of that in your heart now do you? Your brain, and your stubbornness keeps telling you to continue the training and the absurd running and exercising; but look into your heart D, look into your soul and tell me you aren’t ready after being here for three days with these guys and doing that same drill for hours on end. Hell, you didn’t even get hit once until today, and even then you’ve only been struck three times. Even if those guys do get lucky and get a shot in, the sharks will swarm, but you’re the biggest guppy in the pond and I don’t think even a three on one assault is going to stop you. Like I said, look into that dark heart and that disturbed soul and tell me you aren’t ready. “ :: Demond stood there a moment ( he had gotten to his feet and was leaning against the ropes while his soul mate spoke to him ) and just looked at her. He then rebounded up a bit and stood there, looking at the mat. He smirked a bit and looked down to the Realm Championship belt sitting near by. Amy smiled as well. :: Amy: “ Come on now, I went and got us some Subway, it’s waiting for you guys in the office. “ :: Al and Jester hauled themselves from the ring, looking much like zombies crawling from the crypt. Demond rolled out and grabbed hold of the belt, taking it into the office and setting it down on a desk. He sat in a big comfy looking leather recliner they had in the office and tore into his lunch. Al and Jester came in a moment later and did the same. Amy took the time out in the training to go over some of the stuff she had learned about Maximum Carnage from the videos she’d been watching all day. :: Amy: “ So here, I’ve been watching these all day you’re going to learn something from them. If you look at this one here… “ :: The scene faded a bit as Demond, Jester, and Al all watched the videos and Amy began to explain her opinion on each wrestler. :: :: We fade back in on Demond sitting in the recliner again. Two wrappers sitting in front of him and an empty container that once had PowerAde in it. He’s asleep, and no, he’s not snoring like the dog from “ Little Nicky “, though the sound coming from him as he snores is almost similar to Adam Sandler’s snoring. Amy, Jester, and Al are all out in the gym talking as Demond sleeps. :: Amy: “ I’m so happy he’s asleep finally. “ Al: “ I know he wants to win this match on Sunday, but this is crazy. This is the first time he’s slept in two days. It’s not healthy. “ Jester: “ Since when has D ever been healthy when it comes to his titles and his Pay Per View appearances? Amy just be ready now, when he wins on Sunday he’s going to party all night, then come home Monday morning and sleep until Tuesday night if not until Thursday morning. “ Amy: “ I know, I know. But what can I do, he wont go to bed unless he just blacks out, and you guys know when he wakes up you better be ready to get back into that ring with him and train, he wont have it any other way. “ Al: “ You ain’t kidding, but what the hell are we going to do? We aren’t a LOW champion, we don’t have his stamina, and we can’t keep up all day. “ Amy: “ Well you’re going to have to come up with something. “ :: Just then a cell phone ringing was heard. Amy reached into her purse and came out with her cell phone. :: Amy: “ Hmm, isn’t mine. You guys? “ :: Al looked at his phone sitting across the table from him. :: Al: “ No, mine’s set on vibrate. “ Jester: “ I don’t have a cell phone… “ Amy: “ Oh than it must be… “ :: The three looked at one another with the sudden horrifying notion and all three similarly jerked their heads around to look at the office. A cell phone sat on the desk in the office, vibrating a bit across the wooden surface as it did. It was Demond’s. Al was the first to act, jumping up and running for the office, but Amy and Jester were soon behind him, running full blast to get there before Demond awoke. Al fell and Jester stopped to help him up. :: Al: “ GO ON WITHOUT ME! I’LL BE FINE! GET THAT PHONE! “ :: With a moment of determined ire, Jester looked at his brave friend and continued the run. Amy was in the lead and Jester quick behind her, but, taking a corner, Jester hit the ring steps and fell over them, hitting the floor. It was all up to Amy now. She sprinted into the office and dove for the phone… . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Only to get a handful of leather gloved Demond paw. He was awake and looked down at Amy as he picked up his phone. :: The Demond: “ I got it babe, it’s cool. You alright? “ :: Defeated Amy looked down at the ground and sighed. Al and Jester moaned in their own pain out in the gym as Demond lifted the phone to his ear after flipping it open. :: The Demond: “ Yes? “ … The Demond: “ Hey man what’s up?! I haven’t talked to you in so long! “ … The Demond: “ Yeah, yeah, Realm Champion, it’s pretty sweet. Catastrophic’s coming up though. “ … The Demond: “ Yeah definitely, have you thought about it? “ … The Demond: “ Yeah that’s always a bitch, but we were hoping… “ … The Demond: “ Yeah. “ … The Demond: “ Alright…well yeah I can hook that up, no worries. “ … The Demond: “ Yes they already said it was cool. They’re just waiting to hear from you or me about it. “ … The Demond: “ Really?! Yeah alright man that’s awesome, I appreciate it so much. Make sure you come by the house on Saturday and pick up your passes…maybe we’ll have a BBQ or something too. “ … The Demond: “ Yeah, that’s a plan then. Awesome, again thanks, and tell the guys I said hey and thanks to ok? “ … The Demond: “ You two man, and I’ll tell her. Later days. “ :: Demond hung up the phone, and smirked wide, looking down at Amy. He reached down and picked her up off the ground, swinging her around him a bit and hugging her close to his body, smiling wide. :: Amy: “ Whoa, Whoa! Who was that Mr. Serious?! “ :: The sarcasm was lost on Demond, he was too happy. :: The Demond: “ That was David! He confirmed, they’re coming on Sunday! This is going to be sooo cool thanks for fixing it up for me babe! “ :: Amy smirked, he was like a little boy…well ok, a huge, monstrous, beast of a little boy, but like a little boy none-the-less. She eased herself down and ran a hand across his face. :: Amy: “ I was glad to do it for you, have fun with it. Now why don’t you give these guys in here a break and take a girl out for some dinner would ya? “ :: Demond thought back to the huge lunch he’d eaten and was still digesting, but just shrugged. When you’re almost 7 ½ feet tall and over 250 lbs. it takes a lot of fuel to hoist around your body all day, especially in training like this. Demond gave her a pat on the ass that a million men wanted to pat and kissed her on top of her head. :: The Demond: “ Let me get a shower and get dressed and we’ll go. “ :: He took off to the showers and she went into the gym. :: Amy: “ You guys are lucky he answered that, now he’s so damn happy he’s forgotten about training for a while. Take off while he’s in the shower, and go have fun tonight, you know he’ll be back at it tomorrow. “ :: They thanked her and took off. Amy smirked as she heard the door closed and headed off towards the locker room where Demond was showering. If he was happy before he went in, he’d be ecstatic when he got out if she had anything to do with it. We fade to black as Amy heads into the door, unbuttoning her shirt as she goes. :: - We warp now to a final look at the LOW Realm Champion before battle – :: The LOW promo channel flares to life and we see a still shot of Demond. It’s obviously been a few days since the last scene, because the normally trip and neat sideburns and goatee Demond has is scruffy and shabby. He stands alone in the ring of his training facility. A single red light is on in the building and it’s right on top of the ring. Demond’s body glows in a crimson hue as he stands there, breathing heavy. Sweat pours from his face and head, its obvious Amy was right and he went right back into it come the next day. Today though, Demond decided to train alone. He prepped all day and now, as night settled and Demond was preparing to go home and rest for the next day’s trip, he was reflecting on the weeks past and the weeks ahead. But most of all, he reflected on what was to go down on Sunday night. He though of all the neigh sayers of his youth and he thought of all the negative affects his life had seen. He thought of why he was what he was, and thought of what his recent efforts had done. He smiled at the thought of what he’d accomplished so far, but the smile soon faded. What could be going through his mind now? We now notice that the sounds of the song “ One “ by Metallica playing lightly in the background. Demond seems to be entranced now, listening to the music is almost the last thing he’s doing. The thoughts swarm his head and he looks up into the red light above him, trying to escape the tortured existence he lived in, constantly training, and being sought after as an enemy. As much as Demond would like to believe that’s what he wants, and that’s what keeps him going, no one wants to be hated…though Demond is a close example of the first person ever that would accept that fate. No one wants to be the outcast, no one wants to be exiled from the world and to live alone in their thoughts, but that’s what Demond forced himself to do. He had once read a book with a character in it named Drizzt. Drizzt was a person exiled from his family and all that he knew and was forced to live out in the wild as a loner. He had to fight for survival and became something he labeled “ The Hunter “. Demond now felt like Drizzt. He made Amy, Jester, and Al all leave the day before, and had spent the last 24 hours alone in his thoughts. He thought about how lonely it would be in that ring against three other men and felt as if he himself needed to become “ The Hunter “. Suddenly, as the guitar rifts changed in the song, so did Demond’s expression. It was one of total self-realization. Demond smirked a bit, looking up still and taking in a deep breath. He didn’t need to become “ The Hunter “ at all… he already was something much greater and much more powerful… he was… “ The Demond “ and that far surpasses anything he could become. He thought once more about the energy needed to succeed in the match to come, he thought once more about his opponents, and about his internal rage. In one fail move, Demond acted upon this intensity, this anger, this rage, and all this emotion. He raised his arms high above him and let out a primal scream, a sound of total and utter confidence and self-assurance, and brought his arms crashing down as hard as he could onto the turnbuckle he was leaning on. As the last sounds of the song played, the last moments of the rings life were lived out. The shot Demond just delivered to the ring post brought it crashing into a heap of metal below him on the floor, and in turn, brought the entirety of the ring to the floor. The three other posts couldn’t support the weight and the ring crashed to the ground in a heavy collapsing sound. Dust flew and the ring ropes fell limp around the Realm Champion. The destruction of the ring stopped the music in it’s played and as the echo of the destructive sound died down, silence held the room. Demond stood there, arm still flexed hard and breathing deep, still staring straight down at the mat, and now partially the floor of the gym. As the camera began to fade to black, it zoomed in a bit, and showed the eyes of The Demond. Different now as they always are at a moment like this, Demond’s eyes were flat, black, and somehow…determined. They narrowed and shook a little, as did his entire head. The under lid of his left eye twitched a little, and he closed his eyes. He stood there a moment longer as the camera continued to fade. As blackness took the screen, Demond once more felt like Drizzt from the book he had read, for he went back into an utter calm, his rage and anger, confusion doubt, happiness and sadness all leaving him now and his personality going into somewhat of a total tranquility. He was ready, and he knew it… he just hoped his enemies were as well, because their end… was near. Truly a Dark Wave was Rising on the Horizon on Maximum Carnage… and his name was… The Demond. Demond stood tall and full once more, and turned. Lifting the Realm Title onto his shoulder and turning towards the door. He walked slowly as the scene faded completely. He opened the main door of the building, and as white light flooded the room, he disappeared into it… headed for the ring of his destiny. The door shut… as did the scene. Fade… to black. :: ** No words are needed… for the one’s who know. **
