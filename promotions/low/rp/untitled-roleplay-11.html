Eric Russo vs. 2 Legit :: 'Skillz' by Gangstarr bursts out of the PA system. The fans get up, and surprisingly, they boo the hell out of 2 Legit, a team that is supposed to be one of the fan favourites. Both men step out to a huge chorus of insults and jeers from the crowd. :: 2 Legit, AJ and Mike Anderson, stepping out here to receive boos, which is quite surprising knowing that there opponents are the Shadow Riders this week. They are booing them because two of them could not beat one man! That is why they are booing them. That man was not just anyone that was Tristan Belmont! Regardless, who wants to see two men go at it with the same guy, who now has a friend of his own, the Realm champion! This match is already a sure win for the Riders! ::The brothers slide under the third rope and walk to the middle of the ring. They look at themselves with an odd look, not knowing why people are booing them so much. AJ flips a fan off; pissed off at the crummy reaction they are getting from the fans. Their music slowly dies down and now silence takes over the arena. The fans turn to entryway and begin to cheer loudly. In the End by Linkin Park blasts out of the public announce system. The fans’ cheers drown the music out. Tristan Belmont followed shortly by the Realm champion, Eric Russo step out from behind the curtains. They stand high above all on the top of the ramp, looking down at the ring. Belmont smirks at Eric Russo who laughs. Belmont runs towards the ring and slides under the third rope. Both brothers begin stomping away at him, but that is not enough to keep him down. Belmont pushes himself back up to vertical basis and knocks AJ down with a stiff right to the jaw. Mike goes for a left, but his punch goes out of course when Belmont ducks the shot and kicks him in the gut, causing him to double over. Belmont follows through with an implant DDT, where he pulls Mike up like he would suplex him, but drops him half way in a DDT. :: Damn! Belmont is not losing any time, his partner hasn’t even entered the ring yet! Heh! Well, speaking of the devil, here he comes now! ::Eric Russo steps through the second and third rope, slowly unbuckling the title around his waist, and throwing it to the mat. He walks over to AJ Anderson and kicks him in the gut. Eric continues with a hard elbow shot to the back of the head. AJ backs a few steps, but clobbers Eric now, knocking him down to the mat with a clothesline. He immediately jumps on Russo and begins pounding away at him. The Realm champion tries to block the shots, but to no avail as AJ gets a couple of good shots in. Belmont turns to this and walks over to AJ. He tries pulling him off, but AJ hits him in the gut with a hard elbow, which causes Belmont to double over and back a few steps. Belmont holds onto his stomach when he looks at AJ with a look of sheer hate. He walks towards him. AJ jumps off Eric Russo and meets Belmont head on with two shots to the face, followed by a hard push, which sends Belmont into AJ’s brothers’ arms, who looks a backwards bear hug on Belmont. :: It seems 2 Legit have just gained the upper hand now! Belmont is stuck in a bear hug and Eric Russo is getting pounded on by AJ. The two brothers have more of a chance then you would have expected Casey. You’ll see … that’s all I can say! ::Mike wrenches on Belmont, who shows a face of pain. Suddenly, from a grimace turns a smile, as Belmont breaks the hold, turns around and knees Mike in the gut. Mike doubles over in pain as Belmont throws him in between his legs. Belmont throws him on top of his shoulders and turns around, so he is facing AJ. In a quick moment, AJ jumps off Eric and dropkicks Mike’s back, causing Belmont to lose balance and fall on his back, with Mike serving as extra weight. Mike falls on Belmont and immediately positions himself for a pin fall. 1 . . Belmont throws Mike off of him and jumps back to his feet. Mike barely gets up when a thunderous clothesline courtesy of Belmont beheads him. Mike falls down on the canvas as Belmont makes his way towards AJ. AJ is now stuck, as Eric Russo gets up, and he does not look happy. Russo strikes AJ in the back. AJ turns around only to be punched by Belmont. AJ stumbles into a suplex by Russo. Belmont pulls him back up as both team members (Mike and Russo) step out of the ring, after being ordered by the referee. They both stand on the apron. Belmont throws AJ into the barbed wire ropes. AJ’s back begins bleeding as he runs into a high impact spine buster from Belmont. Belmont follows up with a pinfall. 1 . . Kick out by AJ. Belmont gets back to his feet and watches AJ slowly get back to his. AJ stumbles back up to vertical basis, and falls in Belmont’s arms, who throws him all the way to Mike’s corner with a belly to belly suplex. Belmont looks at Mike and taunts him to get in the ring. Mike tags himself in and hops into the ring. He runs towards Belmont who tries a clothesline, Mike ducts the shot and follows through with a running neck breaker on Belmont. Mike does not let Belmont any time to recover as he immediately jumps on the mat and locks Belmont in a headlock. :: A headlock? A freakin’ headlock? Common’! Anything to cause damage Casey! That is the right way to think. If that can slow Belmont down, then why not apply it on him? This is a freaking Barbed Wire match, not a headlock match! :: Mike wrenches on Belmont who begins to get back up to his feet. Mike wrenches even more as both men are now in vertical basis. Belmont elbows Mike twice in the gut and tries to throw him in the ropes. Mike however does not let go. Belmont continues to elbow Mike in the gut, until Mike breaks the hold, blocks a right from Belmont and dropkicks him all the way to his respective corner. Russo tags himself in, which brings anger to Belmont, who angrily goes outside. Russo steps in the ring and rubs his hands together. Mike and him tangle in a neck to elbow grapple. They both try to get the advantage, but Mike gets it when he kicks Russo in the gut and double axe handles him in the back. Russo does not fall though, getting back up striking Mike with a surprise rabbit punch. Mike stumbles back and walks right into a release northern suplex! Russo gets back up and walks over to Mike Anderson. He picks him up and throws him into Belmont’s corner. He comes running towards him, and is met with a high boot to the face, which causes him to jump back 5 feet, right on his ass. Belmont however leans over the ropes and grabs Mike by the neck. Mike breaks the hold by pushing the arm off and dropkicks Belmont off the apron, neck first onto the security railing. Belmont falls down on the security mats, holding his jaw. Mike turns his attention to Russo now, who is getting back up. Mike runs at him and knee lifts him. Russo stumbles back into the arms of AJ, who just entered the ring, who levels him with a backward spinning heel kick to the back of the head. Russo is propelled forward into Mike’s arms who lifts him up by the armpits and drives him down hard body first with a one-man flapjack. AJ and Mike pick Russo up now and throw him into the ropes, shredding a part of his back. They meet him with a double clothesline that sends Russo down hard. The referee pushes AJ out of the ring, who just laughs the whole way out. Mike begins to walk his way towards the turnbuckles. He steps out of the ring and climbs the buckles to the third one. He stands there, trying to keep his balance as Russo lies on his back in the middle of the ring. He jumps in the air and drives his two knees into Russo’s stomach. Russo’s body suffers a huge spasm as he rolls around the ring, holding on to his stomach, screaming in pain. Mike rolls his way to AJ and tags his brother in. AJ walks in cockily and struts his way over to Russo. Suddenly out of nowhere, his head almost f\lies off as Belmont hits the hardest punch ever seen in LOW history right to his face. Blood gashes out of his nose as he falls unconscious! AJ stumbles and falls back on his back, eyes closed … he is knocked out! :: OH MY GOD! AJ is knocked out completely. He is out of this one! Damn! Where the hell did that punch come from? Look at Belmont he is smiling! ::Belmont drags Russo to his corner, before stepping out of the ring himself. Belmont now tags himself in the ring and goes right after Mike, who tagged in at the same time as Belmont. Mike clotheslines Belmont down, who gets back up and is knocked down again by Mike, who kicks Belmont in the nuts now. Belmont doubles over in pain as Mike drives him hard with a fisherman suplex. Leg hooked, the referee begins the count. 1 . . 2 . . Kick out! Mike picks Belmont up by the hair and throws him in the ropes. Belmont is able to slide under the ropes, avoiding the jagged barbed wire. Belmont immediately drops to one knee and pulls out a chair from under the ring. He slides in the ring and meets Mike head on, kicking him in the gut, and following through by smashing the chair against Mike’s back. Mike falls down as Belmont continues hitting him in the back twice, before throwing the chair down on the mat. He walks over and tags Russo in. Russo gets in the ring and both men begin stomping away at Mike. :: No one can stop those Riders! As long as they are united, no one will ever be able to stop them! ::Russo tells Belmont to halt the kicks as he picks him up and tells Belmont to hold him up. Belmont says something in Belmont’s ear who nods his head and smiles. Russo walks over to turnbuckles and climbs them to the top. He perches himself, while Belmont throws Mike in between his legs. Belmont pulls Mike up to reversed vertical status and walks a few steps. Russo jumps in the air, and applies more weight to Belmont dropping a piledriver, which turns out to be …:: SPIKE PILEDRIVER! DAMN IT! ONE IS KNOCKED OUT, AND THE OTHER ONE PROBABLY HAS A BROKEN NECK NOW! I TOLD YOU … I TOLD YOU! ::Russo laughs as Belmont slowly walks out of the ring. Russo walks himself towards Mike Anderson and gets down to both his knees, right in front of Mike. He slowly puts one finger on Mike’s sternum. The referee shrugs at the cocky pin, but none the less, he begins to count. 1 . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . Out of nowhere, Russo falls down hard on his back as AJ kicks him right by the side of the head, with a violent soccer kick. AJ stumbles back and forth completely dazed, and finally, he crashes down head first into Russo’s groin, falling back down to the mat. Russo holds his groin, kicking on the mat in pain. Belmont looks on and smirks and laughs a little bit as he slowly enters the ring. He slowly makes his way towards AJ as the referee tries to step in his way. Belmont just gives him an evil stare, which sends the referee in the corner, letting Belmont do whatever he will wish. Belmont picks AJ up and grabs him by the back of the head with his right hand as he rams his left time and time again into his stomach. The impact is so big that AJ practically jumps a meter in the air with all shots. Belmont slowly drags him towards the ropes. Belmont throws him onto the second rope, cutting his neck a little bit. Blood starts trickling from the neck area, as Belmont, caring not for his own health, pulls on the third rope, and traps AJ’s head between the third and second rope, between barbed wire, that slowly etches its way into AJ’s neck who begins to panic. Belmont smiles at the carnage he is doing, while he wipes the blood off of his hands. AJ tries to get himself out, but the blades get further into his neck, so he stops moving, and begs for Belmont or the referee to help him out. The referee goes to help, but Belmont pushes him back. Russo now begins to get up, as Mike starts to struggle back to his feet too. Eric Russo and Belmont now corner the man. Belmont grabs him by the side of the head and kicks him in the gut. He throws him in between his legs and hoists him up high on his shoulders for a powerbomb. Russo grabs on to his head and as Belmont drives him to the mat, Russo jumps up in the air and dropkicks him down to the mat, in mid-air! :: What a move! Never before seen! Eric Russo and Tristan Belmont are innovators! Too bad they are on the wrong side of the track; we could use them against the Meyhu’s and Lotus’! These Shadow Riders’ are no joke! They claim they are the greatest team ever, and I can say that I do believe that claim right now. They are sick and twisted, and I love it! Look, Belmont is laughing at AJ whom is desperately trying hto get some air without having blades reaching further into his neck! And you like that? Yeah! It just proves that they have not let success make them soft! They are still the sickest team out there! ::Russo has just stepped out of the ring now, and is standing on the ring apron. Belmont picks Mike up and throws him into the ropes. The throws is sop big that even if the metal shreds his back, and he is out of energy, he is still propelled back towards Belmont. Belmont applies a 3D into Spinebuster with the Russo doing the cutter part of the 3D on the apron, causing the Mike's neck to hit the top rope and whiplash off into a spinebuster by the Belmont, who is still in the ring. :: SOUTH TEXAS DEATHRIDE! You know what that mean? What? IT’S OVER!!! Dear god! I do not think you have all realised this, but Russo applied the cutter, driving Mike’s neck even more into the barbed wire, and putting all his weight on it too. Mike’s throat might be completely chopped to pieces as we speak! I don’t think he feels it after that big ass spinebuster! ::Russo stays out of the ring, basking in the opponent’s boos! :: They don’t like you jackass! They like Belmont! Not so much, after all, he does not give a shit about them, he is a Rider! ::Belmont now kicks Mike in the ribs. Meanwhile, AJ has been able to pull himself free. Just as Belmont kneels down to pin Mike, AJ comes running at him and punches him three of four times in the face. The fifth strike however is blocked, as Belmont knees him hard in the gut and throws him between his legs. Belmont throws him up in the air in a crucifix position. He pushes AJ back into the aria, and catches him coming down with a sit down neck breaker! The whiplash is HUGE! Fans: HOLY SHIT! HOLDY SHIT! :: Recurring Nightmare!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ::Belmont gets up and throws AJ on top of his brother Mike. He then pins both of them and tells the referee to make the count. :: It’s just systematic from now! Get the EMT’s out here now! HA HA HA!!! ::EMT’s are seen rushing to the scene. 1 . . . . 2 . . . . . 3!!! In the End by Linkin Park blasts out of the PA system as Belmont gets back up to his feet and raises his hands in victory. Russo slides back in the ring and grabs onto Belmont’s hand. He raises it too, as both Riders stand tall over the broken brothers, whom are now being loaded on stretchers, and having neck braces put on them. Belmont looks down at them as Russo starts trash talking them, his back turned to Belmont. Belmont walks over to one part of the ring and grabs the Realm title, which he throws on the ground. Russo turns around and is kicked in the gut by Belmont! :: WHAT THE? ::Belmont throws him in between his legs and does the same thing he did to AJ …:: RECURRING NIGHTMARE! OH MY GOD! YES YES !!! YOU DAMN RIGHT YES! BUT WHY? ::The fans go absolutely nuts as Russo holds onto the back of his neck, rolling around in pain as Belmont slowly gets back to his feet. Belmont picks up the Realm title, which he looks at for a moment before throwing on top of Russo’s helpless body, which EMT’s are now rushing too. Belmont pushes the two EMT’s and picks Russo back up. He throws him in between his legs again and hoists him up on his shoulders. He walks over to the ropes and powerbombs him out of the ring, to the concrete! :: DEAR GOD!!! ::The fans break out in a holy shit chant as Linkin Park’s In the End blasts out again, while Belmont looks down at the broken Rider … his former partner. Belmont walks over to the ring announcer and is tossed a microphone. The fans quickly settle down as Belmont brings the microphone to his mouth. Tristan Belmont: Eric… take this as my resignation from you and the Shadow Riders … :: YES YES YES!!! BELMONT IS BACK!!! THE RIDERS JUST SUFFERED A CRUCIAL BLOW! ::Belmont rolls out of the ring and slowly makes his way up to the very entrance of the aisle. He looks at walk three men being loaded on stretchers and laughs, as he disappears to the back! :: Outcome: Victory over 2 Legit Show URL: http://
