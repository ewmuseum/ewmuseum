A New Infatuation

24 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What a week it had been for one Brody Dahmer. The start of her second week as a free woman had given her great news, granting her a shot at a title she had taken a liking too. In that same week, she defeated the champion, Adam Stryker, to gain rightful possession of the Realm Title and call it her own. No longer would people refer to her as ‘the girl who stole the belt’, for she had taken it… in advance. Thousands had witnessed her cover Adam Stryker for the victory. How that had happened was not important, all that was important was that Brody Dahmer had already made a name for herself, with only one match. Then, something possibly greater than winning a title happened to Brody on that very same night. She was invited to the fourth member of what many believe to be the greatest stable of all times, the Shadow Riders. It seemed that Russo, Justice and Kage had taken a liking to the rookie when they had witnessed her swing a metal pipe into a man’s throat. In one night, she became, quite possibly, the biggest force to be reckoned within LOW. This week, she had yet another opportunity, an opportunity at advancing forth in a tournament that could lead her either to the World Title or the eXtreme title, a chance that she could not pass upon. Secretly, she wished she would get the ladder of both, because after all, with the gruesome rules that followed the belt, she thought of the pain she could distribute as more of a prize than any World Title could be. All in due time she thought to herself, but before she could pound the Champion’s face in, she needed to get rid of a nuisance, a nuisance that came in the shape of her first opponent in the King of the Death Match Tournament, a nuisance whose name was Matt Meyhu. “Meyhu,” she began, “he like his cousin?” There she was, sitting in a broken down recliner, her feet tucked in under her bottom. Her left hand went in and out of her hair as the right held up a phone receiver to her ear. A small buzzing noise followed, probably the answer of whoever was on the other end of the line. On her stomach was her newest prize, the Realm Title, a belt she had won in her very first match… “I don’t know, he must be hittin’ those crack pipes pretty hard givin’ that worthless shit shots after shots,” she continued, “don’t matter though, he’ll be an example I guess.” Everything was dark around her. A faint light was produced by a broken down lamp next to her. She had a black ‘Shadow Riders’ tank top on her upper body, and a pair of ripped and torn, faded blue jeans. Half calf high heel boots were on her feet. As she straightened out in the recliner, placing her feet on the dirty ground beneath, she looked at her championship belt, and a grin became visible. “One by one they will fall Eric, it does not matter.” Eric? Could she be talking to Eric Russo? It was very possible, and it would not be a surprise. They had become allies, and allies often spoke. The buzz had traveled in the internet communities that Eric Russo, Jason Kage and Luke Justice saw in her the same figure as a little sister, and just like any older brother, they did not wish to see her get harmed. “Ha, yeah, I knew you were shitting me,” she said while laughing slightly, “Matt Meyhu can’t even get passed that Stryker pussy, he does not stand a god damned chance.” Dahmer looked around her, slightly disgusted at what she was seeing. The man known as ‘Eric’ continued to speak on the other side of the line, while she pressed the receiver closer to her ear to her better. The lamp beside her began to flicker on and off before, letting life be gone, plunging Brody in darkness. “MOTHER FUCKER!” she screamed out as she pushed the lamp off of the table it once sat on. The lamp crashed onto the floor, forcing the light bulb to explode into many smaller particles. It was truly ‘Lights Out’. On the other side of the line, the man retorted, somewhat shocked at her calling him a mother fucker out of the blue. “No no, not you man… fucking lamp let out on me…” Another reply came. Brody sunk her body deeper into the smelly and old recliner, taking comfort in it’s soft touch. Her eyes closed as he lips curled up in a little smile. Maybe she was not living a life of luxury, yet, but at least she was free of truth telling drugs and intensive questioning courtesy of the doctors at the mental hospital she had been admitted too. No, tonight she could sleep with a clear –not so clear since she had actually committed the homicide- conscience. “Yeah,” she mumbled as her cheek rubbed itself against the soft brown recliner cushion, “listen, I have to let you go ok? I’ll see you tomorrow.” Click The phone was shut off. Brody threw it against the far opposite-side wall of this dump she called a home. The plastic receiver collided against the hard wood and crashed to the floor. Brody opened up her eyes and looked out of the window. She could hear noises coming from beneath, which was really not surprising, considering where she currently was. Where you ask? In one of the harshest punk neighborhoods of Los Angeles, California. The sounds of cries, screams, gunshots and roars of anger were not uncommon in this part of the city. For too long she had missed those sounds, those sounds that once lulled her into deep sleep. The streets were something that could never be taken away from her, as it was on those same streets that she learned everything she knew to this day. It was on those streets that the ‘real’ Brody Dahmer was born. Pushing herself back up, Brody slowly walked out of the apartment, taking no look back at it. This would be the last time she ever stayed in this shit hole. She was a Shadow Rider now, and whether or not she wanted to live off the streets, Shadow Riders had class. She could fuck up anyone she wanted and go back to a mansion-like house, so were the words Luke Justice had told her during the week. Outside, she saw the source of the noise. A young man, dressed in a suit was on the floor, on the receiving end of brutal kicks to the ribs by a band of punks. They must have been about, what… half a dozen? Street punks that carried knives and guns, looking only for that one guy that will look at them with a crooked eye to pull the knife out. Brody grinned, remembering the many times she had been in such a circle, and she had pounced on whoever the victim was. She walked closer to the group when finally, one man caught side of her backside and grinned with intent. He nudged at the man next to him and motioned at Brody with his head. The second man smiled as well, and slowly, they broke out of the circle and walked behind her. Their friends paid no attention, they knew were the two were going, and they knew they would get sloppy seconds, thirds and fourths when it was there time. The man finally caught up to Brody and wrapped his arms around her waist, bringing his head closer to hers. “You didn’t think we’d let you go wit’ that tight lil’ ass like that, did ya?!” The man had no fucking idea who he was messing with. As he laughed, Brody turned around, with a spinning back hand that’s truck the man in the face. He stumbled backwards, tackling the back of his hand against his bleeding mouth. “You gonna pay slut…” But before he could say another word, Brody had pulled out a knife from her boot and had thrown it with all her might towards the man. The tip of the blade first pierced through the man’s pants, and then into the skin of his crotch. He let out a horrendous scream of pain as he fell to his knees, teary eyes. The five remaining men all rushed towards Brody and all halted when she looked up, and they all caught sight of who exactly she was… Brody Dahmer was known on those streets, and any one with any street knowledge knew that you did not fuck with her. It was not worth whatever she would make you endure. This idiot thought he would rape her just like that, and for that, he would never rape anyone again, matter fact, he’d never see another sun rise… “Oh shit… It’s Dahmer…” yelped out one as he jumped back, ready to make a run for his life. Brody walked her way to the man who had attacked her a few moments ago. Still on his knees, he began realizing just who he had tried to take advantage of, and regret filled in his mind. Regret and Fear. Lots and lots of fear. Brody lifted her knee under his jaw, following it up with an elbow to the side of the head. His body fell limp… Bending over, her hands wrapped around the handle of her knife, one that she screwed out of the unconsciousness man’s body. As all of this, none of his friends did a single thing. Brody looked up to them, and just her gaze was enough to send them all running for the hills. Ah how it felt back to be on the streets, she thought to herself. Slowly, the new member of the Shadow Riders made her way to man’s face. She bent her knees and came in half sitting position, holding the knife in a stabbing position. Without any sort of hesitation, and with a wicked smile on her blood red painted lips, Brody put the knife in the corner of the man’s mouth and sliced into the check, three inches long. She did the same for the other side, before looking at the bleeding mess that was her attacker, as an artist would look at a piece of art. To her, this was art. “Now, you will always remember who you tried to fuck…” The knife still tightly clasped in her right hand, she caressed the tip on the man’s Adam apple, in circular motions, as if she were trying to cut it out. Slowly, trickles of blood came from where the blade scratched, and the unconscious man began to move frantically. Brody grinned, as the whole neck area became nothing but a pool of blood when she pushed it into the middle of the man’s throat, applying more pressure by using both of her hands to push her weight down. As she stood up, a warm feeling within her produced a smile of satisfaction. A feeling that had been robbed from her for countless amount of months. That feeling was that of being home, of being where you truly belonged. To some that was in a rich manor to others it was feeling comfortable within a group of people, but to Brody, it was being on the streets, those same streets that had turned her from the useless Theresa Driver to the legend to be, Brody Dahmer. Her walk would eventually lead her into downtown Los Angeles, on the sunset strip. The sun had begun to fall, giving way for the upcoming moon that would shine down on this sleepless city. The sky was of an orange color, produced by the setting sun in the far off distance. The urban scene had gotten more active, as many people, all shapes and colors had flocked to the strip, looking for something to do. Brody passed each and every one of them by, as she felt the humid air envelop itself around her body. Tonight was truly a night to celebrate, one to spend on the town. She thought to herself, it’s too bad that her fellow Shadow Riders were not with her, this could have been even more of a celebration, but without giving it more concern, she kept on walking towards a location, she herself did not know. She would know when to stop. She would sense something when it would be needed for her to get to a halt. But for now, she walked, enjoying the sights of a city she called her own. This was not the first time she had walked down Sunset Strip, and this was probably not the last time, but every time she did, she enjoyed it. It was a mixture of the aromas of the people walking; the noises, the beauty and ugliness of those that surrounded her, all of that made her feel great. All of those things put a fuel in a fire that burned deep inside of her. The desire of harming other people was not something that she held very strong onto; it was simply a nature that she could not rid herself off. Granted, she took great pleasure in watching what happened when knives entered such and such parts of a human body, but she did not do it only for her pleasure, she did it to fill a void that only pain could. She had tried self-mutilation as an alternative many times, but it was obvious that only the pain of others gave her the rush she needed. That was in fact why she had gotten in wrestling to begin with. The pain-rush had been filled for this night, now, she would simply wander on the streets until the early hours of morning, probably find herself a fix of drugs; the drugs she had missed so much in her incarceration. Her nose tickled and begged for the ecstasy that only the white powder could give her. Where would she find it? Common, she thought to herself, this is Los Angeles. So she walked until finally, there was no longer a purpose to follow her. She would do what she had to do, and do it for herself. No one could cage her anymore. This was her time. Everything was going according to plan. Sooner than later, Brody Dahmer would reign supreme… Much Sooner than Later…
