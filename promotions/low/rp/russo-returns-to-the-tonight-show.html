Russo Returns to the Tonight Show

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The logo is accompanied with a voice-over message that states 'The Shadow Riders have arrived, so just sit back and enjoy the ride.' The scene opens to a shot of the Tonight Show. The Tonight Show’s music begins to play as Jay Leno walks out on to the set. He goes through his opening monologue and tells a few jokes. Jay then walks to the center of the set and begins to talk about who is going to be on the show tonight. ) Jay: “Ladies and gentlemen, we have a great show for you tonight. First off, we have none other then Adam Sandler here to talk about his new movie Anger Management.” ( The crowd goes nuts. ) Jay: “We also have the lovely Roselyn Sanchez with us to talk about her hot new movie during this spring sweep, Chasing Papi.” ( Once again the crowd goes nuts. ) Jay: “As if this weren’t enough, we also have “Superstar” Eric Russo of the LOW wrestling promotion here tonight.” ( The crowd goes crazy in anticipation of hearing what Eric Russo has to say. ) Jay: “But first, we have to take this commercial break.” ( The show comes back on and Jay introduces Adam Sandler. Sandler talks about working on the production of Anger Management with Jack Nicholson and cracks a few jokes. Jay then asks him for his predictions on LOW’s Shell Shock. ) Sandler: “Well, Jay, to tell you the truth, I have been following the LOW very closely since it has opened. You may or may not know that I’m a huge fan of professional wrestling. As for my thoughts, even though I think the LOW is the $h!t, I’m gonna have to say that it hasn’t been the same with “Superstar” Eric Russo walking around with the Realm title. I can’t wait to see him get his a$$ kicked. I know that’s why I wanna watch the show.” Jay: “Why is that you want to see him get beat?” Sandler: “I just can’t stand the ego on that guy. I mean it’s huge. I don’t know how he even manages to fit through the ring ropes carrying around that thing. Even though I’m no big fan of Big D, I just want Russo to get what’s coming to him. It just that Russo’s one of those guys that you just love to hate.” Jay: “Well thank you for that insight Mr. Sandler. Now we have to take another commercial break.” ( As the show returns from commercial break, Jay introduces Roselyn Sanchez. Just as Adam Sandler did, she talks about filming her movie Chasing Papi and her role in the movie. Now Jay asks her for her opinion on the coming LOW pay-per-view card. ) Meg: “That’s an easy question Jay, I watch the LOW closer then ever now.” Jay: “Can I ask you why?” Meg: “Well isn’t it obvious? On one hand, you have “Superstar” Eric Russo a guy that is as handsome as ten movie stars and on the other you have a great comedy acts like Big Daveylicious and Eric Dane. Now seriously, isn’t that reason enough? How can you expect me not to sit at home watching that? I mean as long as Eric Russo is wrestling, I will never stop watching. I can’t wait to see him win his match with that muscle bound bonehead Big Daveylicious.” Jay: “Well I guess we know now. Sorry folks but its time for another commercial break. We’ll be right back with one third of the LOW’s most dominate group, the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo himself.” ( As the show comes back from commercial, a few clips of Russo’s wrestling matches are shown on the screen on the set. Then the camera cuts to Jay Leno. ) Jay: “Well were back. Our next guest is one of the superstars of the LOW wrestling federation. This man is one of the most talented wrestlers on the roster, not to mention a good friend of mine. Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome “Superstar” Eric Russo!” ( Kid Rock’s “I’m a Dog” begins to play as “Superstar” Eric Russo walks out onto the set. He is wearing a pair of dress pants with Italian leather shoes. He is also wearing a Rolex watch on his right wrist and a very nice suit jacket with a Shadow Riders’ t-shirt under it and to top it all off, he is wearing his sunglasses and carrying his black cane with the golden horse’s head at the top. Russo continues walking onto the set and takes his seat after shaking Jay’s hand and giving him a little smile. ) Jay: “Welcome to the show Eric. It’s good to have you back once again in a form other then taking over my show.” ( Russo interrupts. ) Russo: “That’s Mr. Russo to you.” Jay: “Sorry, welcome to the show Mr. Russo. We are extremely happy to have you on.” Russo: “I know you’re extremely happy to have me on, I mean it’s not every night that a true superstar comes on your little second rate show. You really need this boost in ratings. Letterman is killing you.” Jay: “Well, thank you all the same. It seems you’ve been pretty busy lately.” Russo: “As a matter of fact, I have. You see, I have been doing a lot of shoots for TV shows and quite a few shoots for movies. Not to mention getting ready to take down Big D. This is actually one of my first chances to get back into the eye of the general public.” Jay: “Well, you’ve decided to be thrown back in pretty deep haven’t you? Why don’t you tell us about this.” Russo: “That’s right Jay, I have decided to sign my name to a contract to face Big Daveylicious in a rematch from our last encounter. I’m hoping this time I can stop all his bitching and moaning that he likes to do.” ( The crowd boos feverishly in response. ) Jay: “That’s all well and good but I think the fans would like to see you lose this match. They don’t seem to like you very much.” Russo: “Well that’s too bad now isn’t it? You see Jim... I mean Jay, The fans opinion doesn’t matter. Besides, they don’t really hate me, they hate my success. They hate the fact that they went to college for four years only to come to tune into LOW every week and see someone who make three times as much as them for only twenty minutes of work. You could probably understand this a little better if you were somewhere close to being as famous as me, but then again nobody is even close to being as famous as me.” ( The fans go nuts with boos. ) Jay: “Well, those are some strong words. We just want to ask you to do one more thing before you go.” Russo: “What would that be?” Jay: “I am going to read off some names and I want you to tell me the first word that comes to your head.” Russo: “OK, shoot.” Jay: “First one, Big Daveylicious” Russo: “Pinned” Jay: “Tristan Belmont” Russo: “Pinned” Jay: “Sean Dahmer” Russo: “Pinned” Jay: “Mike Meyhu” Russo: “Pinned” Jay: “Cade” Russo: “Pinned” Jay: “Only two more. Lotus” Russo: “Beat.” Jay: “Finally, Jesse Jamester” Russo: “Hey, I have a win over him too. What do you know.” Jay: “Ok then, thank you for your time Mr. Russo and I’d like to ask if you have any final thoughts before you go.” Russo: “Just one and that is to remind everyone here and everyone watching at home that they just like the rest are nothing but another falling star.” Jay: Well, once again I would like to thank you for your time Mr. Russo.” Russo: “You’re damn right you want to thank me again.” Jay: “Ok, well that brings an end to the show for tonight. Don’t forget to check out Anger Management and Chasing Papi at a theater near you and don’t miss the LOW’s upcoming pay-per-view Shell Shock this Sunday. You can also check your local listing for their weekly show LOW Realm. Thanks again to all of our guests and have a good night.” ( The Tonight Show goes off the air but instead of the usual music all that is heard is “The Shadow Riders have arrived so just sit back and enjoy the ride.” The screen then goes the Shadow Riders’ logo followed by black as the show ends. )
