The Final Mile: Part II (The Final Step)

captured 7 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Final Mile: Part I is a necessary read to flow smoothly into this continuation. ----- The scene returns to the scorching southern desert with Jayson Mecca looking up at the sun through his hood. The sun blinds you for a moment before his arm comes up and blocks in from the view. The scene shifts down to his feet where Kevin Thompson resides. Most of his bandages have been ripped and his wounds are filled with sand and dried blood. His lips look parched and on the whole he looks like shit. His face remains bruised from possibly being shoved through a car window and his goatee is scraggly and pathetic. Jay stands tall and he resumes his walk from earlier while pulling Kevin with him. “Soon, everything will be all over.” Kevin glares at Jay. “What are you talking about? “ “Soon we will both be at peace.” “Jay, what the hell are you talking about?” Jay’s silence speaks volumes as Kevin becomes dead weight and stops moving. Jay's shoulders slump as he lets out a sigh, turns around and kicks his former best friend in the gut. He reaches down and picks Kevin up showing no remorse and starts to carry him on his shoulders and begins to walk as if Kevin did not weigh close to 300 pounds and lets out a strong statement. “Sometimes I wish we had never met you know.” Kevin, barring what seems like no energy, replies. “I get that impression.” “If only you and Tim never became roommates. Things would have been different. If only you had never came to Lights Out and needed someone to do your bitch work, we would have been invisible to each other.” “Listen to yourself Jayson; we all made choices. Some were great choices, others were not…” Jay is quick to reply. “Choices you say?” Kevin attempts to calm Jay again. “Look man, you didn't have to team with me, you didn't have to hang with Tim and I and go on road trips. You do remember the road trips? “ “I recall driving a lot because you Ashley and Tim had too much to drink. I recall us visiting midgets and clowns and buying pegs.” “Fun times?” Jay shakes his head. “I do not recall many fun times.” Kevin begins to name some off. “The pool party?” Jay shrugs “ Fun for you maybe.” The scene becomes shadowed slightly and the view changes to about a mile ahead of Jay who still without seeming tired, carries Kevin, to an old wooden oil rig. It looks deserted, a relic from the 1800's. In the distance you see what remains of the tower, a small building that might have been an office and a small water tower, rusted with age. The silence returns between the two as Jay walks that mile and comes to step upon a baseboard. He lifts Kevin up and tosses him into the small building, sending a shovel skittering along the wood and dry splinters into the air as the corner collapses under The Kevin’s weight. Jay takes the time to prop Kevin up and then smacks the back of his head against the remains of the building wall. Kevin, who seems to be getting annoyed by this day begins to get sarcastic as if he is slightly worried. “Damnit, not so hard.” Jay glares at Kevin. “Have you figured this out yet Kevin?” “I really can't figure you out at all.” “I don't intend for us both to return from here.” “Oh for fucks sake Jay, just shut the fuck up. You’ve gone insane and now you’re just talking a bunch of shit. Well I’ve heard people like you before…and those people could never back up their words with actions. “The world would be much better off without you. Perhaps if you can accomplish it, the world would be much better off without me.” Jay walks back onto the sand and looks into the sun. He looks up for a moment and think of something that could breakdown Jay. A few years ago…that one girl… “Bah, I'd bet money Tina would miss you. Jay collapses to his knees in a heap as if Kevin had pounded him in the back with a 2x4. His hands reach up and pull down the hood. Small tufts of hair wisp in the dry air. Jay turns his face towards Kevin, and at the same time Kevin catches his glimpse and reaction of Jay in shock. Jay speaks quietly. “Tina would not miss...THIS. She was fed up with me years ago. She has no feelings for me now.” “Sure she would, I saw the way that you two keep in touch since last May. I remember how good it was between you two prior to me entering LOW.” “You know nothing of how she was with me.” “I know more than you think I do. I'd stake fifty grand if you showed up on her doorstep, she'd embrace you and invite you inside.” Jay grabs his hair and shakes his head. “ Lies...more lies. Jay's words are a little choked, almost wanting, needing to believe what Kevin is saying about his ex-fiancé from over a year ago. He hunches over and pounds the sand and yells. “ You know nothing about love!” “I had Ashley. She was all I wanted in life, until she tore my heart in half and I have no more feeling to love.” Jay picks up his head and is lost for words. Kevin continues. “Yes Jay, despite me being the so-called biggest asshole in the world, I knew somebody loved me.” Jay speaks in a gasping tone. “Must be a nice feeling.” “You had it once also Jay; longer than it was between Ashley and I. What ever happened to Tina anyway? “I would rather not talk about it.” Kevin begins to get aggressive with his words. “Of course not you stupid shit. You made another one of your choices. You chose to leave her behind and travel overseas. She called me a couple times in Chicago to see how you were and she tried to get in contact with you. She'd have traveled the world with you if you'd have let her.” Jay seems to be getting pissed off even worse and also gets heated with his words. “Would you shut the fuck up?” This time, Kevin does not talk. Jay shifts his legs in place and sits Indian-style on the ground, saying nothing. You can tell the Tina thing is really bothering him. But he mutters under his breath. “Tina doesn't matter anymore.” “Why did you leave anyway?” “Because I thought working with someone who was actually making it big could make my future better. You could get money, the ladies, anything you wanted. It would have been easy for me. Hell, it was…but now I realize that I killed one part of my life, to live off of someone else’s. Kevin feels what Jay is actually telling him and he tries to continue the calming fixation that Jay is in now. “I couldn't grow in my career if it weren‘t for you almost the whole time when I was out of that ring. I needed to find a way to have someone clear my mind of the hell I went through there the first couple of months and someone that could act as my trainer and make sure I didn’t do anything stupid. Soon Jay, that small thing turned into a friendship between us, and it was some good times. Why would you ruin it now?” “Because for once, I wanted to not feel like the “assistant” or the “second stringer.” This is my time to prove to myself that I can go out with something that I feel I need to do. Kevin denies the words from Jay. “More bullshit. You know you don‘t want to do this and it is yourself that is holding you back from already going out with it.. If you want to do what you want to do, then why are you declining the fact that you Jay would rather not do this to us, than actually do it?” “So it’s me versus me. Well for at least once Kevin, that means I’ll win.” Jay snickers in Kevin’s direction and speaks a few words. “I like to think I have a little skill when it comes to going one on one with you Kevin. So far, I‘ve turned you into the biggest piece of shit right now. I mean, you can barely walk.” “I believe it’s the fact that I was jumped from behind, stuffed in a trunk, tossed helplessly lied up through a window and beat drastically without being able to defend myself. Congratulations on fighting like a man you fucking coward.” Jay is quiet for a moment, shifting his weight to lie down at the base of the building. He smirks at the shit that Kevin is shooting at him, but seems not to care. Kevin continues. “Maybe if I wasn’t tied up like a hog and you faced me like a man, I’ll show you how much of a piece of shit you really are.” Jay smiles and proceeds to Kevin. Jay pulls the knife out of his pocket and points it at Kevin where he slices quickly, pulling apart the rope. Kevin stares at Jay as he didn’t expect him to do that. Jay turns around, tosses the knife to the side and sits back down where he speaks. “I have no reason to prove to you who I am by leaving you like that. I’ll let you do what you want. Kind of sucks that you can’t walk though. Kevin releases a sigh and also just sits, the both of them quite thirsty. “I miss the old you, Jay.” “I wish I could say the same.” A little more uncomfortable silence until Kevin breaks it after a few minutes. “So, uh, what happens next?” “I promised myself Kevin Thompson would never leave this desert. I try to keep my promises…” Kevin, unfelt by the previous comment of Jay, continues to ask the questions. “So when do we do this Jay?” “When the time is right.” “You know we'll both die without water.” “So be it…” Jay pauses for a moment, but seems to be distracted by Kevin and his plans. “Once again Kev, you annoy the fuck out of me. I’m going to look around.” “Heh, have fun…too bad I can’t.” Jay gets up and starts to inspect the remains of the oil shaft as Kevin shifts his weight to keep an eye on Jay’s every move. Suddenly, the sky turns gray and the sound of water is heard in the distance and all of a sudden, rain begins to fall from the sky onto the face of Kevin, removing the dust from the dried dirt on his face. He rubs the water through his hair and goatee as what looks like Jay spinning around, quite pleased with the rain. Cupping his hands together, Jay drinks feverishly before too running his hands through his hair. Facing away from Kevin, Jay remains excited and kneels down, basking in the damp wetness of the freak rainstorm as he yells to Kevin. “Sweet Jesus Kevin, it’s raining. This is incredible and I can’t believe that I’ve been doing this to you.” As Jay rolls around on the ground like a child, a glimpse of sunlight reveals Kevin standing strong behind Jay with a shovel and the rain beading off of his face. He raises his head to the sky to take in the moment as Jay looks up into the darkened clouds above with a smile on his face. A smile that turns quickly to saliva flying through the air… TWANG ...as Kevin clubs Jay in the back of the head with the shovel. Jay falls forward limp while his eyes glaze over and roll up into their sockets and the rain pelts down on his back. Kevin bends over and sticks the shovel into the sand, and begins to dig. ----- Some Time Passes It's sunny now as you come upon Kevin sitting near the damaged shack, his whole body bloodied. By his feet lays the shovel and in the short distance lies a mound where in blood on a flat rock reads, “RIP Jay…My Best Friend.” Focusing on Kevin with a cigarette in his mouth, a tear rolls down his cheek. Kevin stands up and begins to walk in pain towards the sunlight of a hot evening. Ducking his head from the blinding light with Jay‘s hoodie covering his neck, he speaks a few words before limping out of sight. “I’ll never be the same again…I’m left on my own again.” The End...Forever
