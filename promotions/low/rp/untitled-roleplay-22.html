Selling Yourself 3 ::The show cuts to a commercial. After a few meaningless ads are shown, we are shown an add featuring Eric Russo and George Foreman. The scene opens inside of a nicely furnished kitchen. Russo and George Foreman are standing behind the counter. Foreman begins speaking.:: Foreman: “George Foreman here to bring you yet another “Foreman Grill”. You see my grills are selling so well and that makes me happy. So happy in fact, that I decided to invite my good friend Eric Russo here to help me promote our newest model.” ::Russo holds up one of the new grills as he begins speaking.:: Russo: “Just look at the sleek new design of this model.” ::Russo now opens the lid of the grill as he continues.:: Russo: “And just look at all the room... you could fit over six burgers on this baby at once.” ::Russo now begins shaking the grill up and down.:: Russo: “As if that’s not enough, it’s lightweight too. Just look how lightweight it is.” ::Russo holds the grill up high in the air showing how easily he can lift it. George Foreman cuts back in.:: Foreman: “Those are all good things Eric, but the best thin about this grill is it’s patented grooved surface. This surface cooks your meat to perfection while draining away all of that fat that clogs you arteries. So next time your at the store, look for the new lightweight George Foreman Grill and “Knock Out the Fat”!” ::Russo now swings the grill at Foreman catching him with a hard shot to the back of the head. Foreman collapses in a heap behind the counter. Russo steps over his body as he begins talking.:: Russo: “Or you could just “Knock Someone Out. Either way, buy the grill. After all, the superstar owns one so it must be great. Oh and that’s not ego, that’s reality.” ::The add ends as Realm returns.:: Show URL: http://
