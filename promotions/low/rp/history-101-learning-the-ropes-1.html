History 101- Learning the Ropes 1

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. The scene then opens once again to a shot from the past, Russo and his mentor are sitting backstage at yet another event. We can tell by the quality of the locker room that the Snake’s lessons have been a success. The room is brightly lit and the floors clean, a major transition from what Russo was accustomed to up until this point. The air is filled with noise from crowd cheering as a match takes place in the ring. This noise however does not seem to have any effect on Russo’s concentration and mental preparation as he sits quietly keeping mainly to himself. As he stays sitting in silence, a voice over begins.) Russo (VO): “The Snake had taught me a lot over the few months that we had been together. Already I was starting to see the fruits of my labor paying off. I had moved on to bigger and better things then I had ever dreamed possible. How far could the Snake take me? More importantly, what would it cost me? I had been taught by the Snake that nothing comes without a price. Little did I know how high the price would actually be.” (As the voice over stops, the door of the locker room opens revealing the Snake. He walks into the room and starts towards Russo. Once beside him, the Snake looks down on his pupil as he begins speaking.) Snake: “After tonight, nobody will ever think of you in the same light again Eric. After tonight, you’ll be a champion for the first time.” (Russo looks up at his mentor finally breaking his silence as he responds.) Russo: “I’m not sure about what you told me to do. It just doesn’t sound right, I don’t know how I’m gonna be able to look myself in the mirror after something like this.” Snake: “Don’t you trust me Eric? Haven’t I gotten you everything I promised I would?” (Russo pushes himself to his feet and begins to pace the length of the locker room. It is almost as though he is waging a mental war with his conscience over the age old question of greed vs. happiness. As he does this, he speaks up once again.) Russo: “I just don’t know why I have to do something like this. Isn’t just winning the match enough?” (The Snake steps in front of Russo stopping him dead in his path. As Russo looks up, the two men meet eye to eye. With a strange calmness to his voice, the Snake answers Russo’s question.) Snake: “No, it’s not.” (The scene transitions to the time just before his match. As Russo watches his opponent walk out into the entryway, he listens to the cheers of the fans. Once again, his conscience cause him to begin questioning what he is about to do to this poor unsuspecting opponent. He turns to te Snake as he begins voicing his concerns.) Russo: “I’m not gonna put this guy on the shelf tonight. It’s not right, he’s just trying to make his way up in this business like me.” (The Snake glares at Russo with an evil look in his eyes.) Snake: “If you don’t do it, I will Eric. We’ve come too far for me to just let you throw it all away over a simple bout of conscience. Believe me, I know how easy it is to feel sorry about doing these kind of things at first. You’ll get used to it soon enough though. All you have to do is learn to shut all of those people who are going to try and tell you that you’re wrong out. The only reason they say that is because they’re jealous of your success. They will only wish that they had the backbone to do the things that you will Eric.” (At this point, one of the stage hands at the events approaches the duo. Meekly she interrupts the conversation taking place.) Stage Hand: “You’re up Mr. Russo. Are you ready?” (Before Russo even has a chance to answer, the Snake speaks up for him.) Snake: “He’s more then ready little girl.” (The Snake now turns back to Russo with a devilish smirk as he gives his last words of encouragement to his pupil.) Snake: “Break a leg Eric . . . literally.” (Shaking his head, Russo turns back to the stage hand as he answers her question himself.) Russo: “I’ll be over in a minute. Start up my music.” (The young stage hand nods her head at Russo order and walks off back towards the entryway. Russo turns to follow, but is stopped by the Snake’s hand on his shoulder. Russo turns to see why the Snake is holding him back. As he does so, the Snake begins speaking.) Snake: “Don’t let me down tonight Eric. We both have a lot riding on this match. Let me see that my teachings are going to good use. Take him out Eric, don’t mess up what I have given to you.” (The Snake slithers his hand into his pocket and withdraws a set of handcuffs. He then holds them up, dangling them in front of Russo’s face almost as though he is daring him to grab hold of them. He speaks.) Snake: “These should help you out with the task I’ve given you. Do yourself a favor and use them.” (Reluctantly Russo reaches out and grabs hold of the handcuffs. He quickly bends down and slides them into his boot making sure nobody sees this action. As he does this, the Snake speaks up once again.) Snake: “That’s good, now you’re getting the hang of it.” (Shaking his head, Russo looks to the Snake. He speaks.) Russo: “I can’t believe you’re making me do this, but I’m gonna to put my trust in you none the less.” Snake: “There’s no reason that you shouldn’t Eric. Have I ever given you bad advice? Have I ever led you down the wrong path? I told you when we started this that you were going to have to commit yourself to what I had in store for you. Did I not?” Without even having to think of the answer, Russo nods his head in agreement. The Snake continues.) Snake: “I only have the best intentions for you Eric. You’re gonna need to learn to do these kind of things when you have to. That is one of the things that will place you above all the others. It will help in the long run and that is why I am doing this. A man who will stop at nothing cannot be beat. There is no action that I will ask of you that does not hold some . . .” (Russo breaks in.) Russo: “ . . . Value.” (A smile crosses the Snakes face as he responds.) Snake: “Yes . . . value. Do you understand why I am asking this of you now?” (Russo nods his head as he responds.) Russo: “I think I’m beginning to.” Roberts: “Good. I knew I saw something special in you Eric. Well enough talk, lets get out there and get you a reputation.” (With that, the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
