Shattered Dreams- Out to Lunch

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . 'Please stay tuned for an important announcement from 'Superstar' Eric Russo, leader of the Shadow Riders'. Slowly the logo fades out to black. For a moment, nothing. Then the sound of Russo’s voice speaks out as he launches into a voice over.) Russo (VO): “Just like everyone wanted a piece of me in wrestling because of my ability, everyone else now wanted me because of my money. I guess I now know what the saying . . . “out of the frying pan and into the fryer.” really means. What was even worse is that because of all this I had totally neglected my training for my upcoming match. Why should I care though? I have more then enough money now. My upcoming match with Jamester was gonna be nothing but a way to kill time. Right now, I only cared about one thing. How to get even more money.” (The scene opens to a shot in the backyard of Russo’s new house. Russo is standing under a large canopy with a glass of champagne in his hand. Surrounding him are a bunch of well dresses men giving him tips on investments for his money. One of the advisors begins to speak.) Financial Advisor #1: “Mr. Russo, if your initial investment in this project is in the neighborhood of half a million dollars, and your apartments are up by the end of March. You should be able to have x amount of dollars coming in by the end of the year.” (Russo smiles as he responds acting as though he knows what he’s talking about.) Russo: “Oh, I see . . . x amount. That’s pretty good isn’t it? Yeah, I never even have to think about wrestling for money again.” (Another one of the advisors cut in.) Financial Advisor #2: “As if that weren’t enough, you can then depreciate the entire building to get back the full amount!” Russo: “Depreciate! Well, very good. I like that idea.” (The advisor continues.) Financial Advisor #2: “Not only that, but we also found a way to get around all of this fair housing crap that the government has been force feeding us!” Russo: “Ah, good. Getting around the crap! That’s good!” (Russo smiles at the advisor as the first one continues.) Financial Advisor #1: “Yes, by keeping the rents high in the area, we’re going to appeal to a certain class of people . . . a select class of people if you will.” Russo: “Ah . . . select class. Very, very good.” Financial Advisor #2: “Yeah, we’ll keep the garbage out of the neighborhood.” (Russo responds with a smirk.) Russo: “Ah good! We won’t have to pay extra taxes for a sanitation department.” Financial Advisor #1: “Na, na. We’re gonna keep out the bums.” Russo: “Of course! The bums will follow the garbage! (The second advisor turns to the first as he begins to speak.) Financial Advisor #2: “Do you think I could talk to him for a minute . . . alone?” (The other advisor nods his head before turning and walking off while his friend tries to explain what is happening to Russo.) Financial Advisor #2: “Look what we’re trying to say to you is that we are going to keep out the people without money. You know, the undesirables.” (With a look of surprise on his face, Russo responds.) Russo: “The what!?” Financial Advisor #2: “The undesirables! We’ll keep them out.” (Russo’s face fills with anger as he responds.) Russo: “Yo buddy, you’re talking to a former undesirable!” (With that comment, Russo launches into a full out physical assault on the poor defenseless advisor. He also manages to take down the second advisor as well as he comes to help out his friend. The hulking bodyguard of the two advisors presents a bit of a problem however. As Russo delivers a swift kick to the man’s groin a loud clank is heard. Russo drops to the ground in pain holding his foot as the scene fades out. Russo’s voice starts up again.) Russo (VO): “Would I ever be able to escape what I once was? I finally had all the money I could ever want yet I still could not break myself away from my old days. Not to mention that now on top of everything else, my foot hurt. In order to cheer myself up a little and forget about my looming match with Jamester, I decided to go out for a nice dinner with a young woman who’s name escapes me at the moment.” (The scene reopens in a fancy restaurant. Russo sits at a table with a beautiful young woman. We can tell by his expression that while he is physically there, his mind is a million miles away. The young woman also notices this and does her best to bring Russo’s attention back to her. She begins speaking.) Young Woman: “Don’t be so hard on yourself Eric. How could you know that those business men would have connections with someone like Brass Balls Bucci?” (Before Russo has a chance to respond, the waiter approaches the table with a tray full of food. Quickly he places the plates on the table as he serves the dinner. He then turns towards Russo as he begins to speak.) Waiter: “Would monsieur care for another bottle of Chateau Latour?” (Russo looks up with a gleam in his eyes as he responds.) Russo: “You know what, yeah. I want you to do me a favor this time though. I feel like splurging tonight so I don’t want to see another one of these bottles from 1943. I’m a rich man, I can afford the good things in life. Bring me some fresh wine! I want the freshest wine you have . . . something from this year! I don’t wanna see anymore of this old shit on my table.” Waiter: “Oui monsieur.” (As the waiter begins to walk away, Russo turns to his female companion as he continues.) Russo: “He doesn't realize that he's dealing with sophisticated people here . . .” (Russo pauses for a moment as he looks at one of the plates on the table. Frantically he begins speaking to his date.) Russo: “Ok, now just stay calm. Stay calm. Don’t look down . . .” (Out of natural reaction, the girl begins to look down.) Russo: “Don’t look down! Look up! Just keep your eyes up and keep them that way, ok!?” (Not sure of what is going on, the girl complies with Russo’s wishes. Enraged, Russo waves another waiter over to the table. Once he arrives, Russo begins to lay into him.) Russo: “Waiter there are snails on her plate. Now get them out of here before she sees them!” (Out of the corner of his eye, Russo notices his dates gaze slipping back to the plate in front of her. Russo yells out.) Russo: “Look away, just look away, keep your eyes that way!” (Russo turns back to the waiter as he continues.) Russo: “You would think that in a fancy restaurant at these prices you could keep the snails off the food! Hell, there are so many snails on that plate there that you can't even see the food! Now take those away and bring us those melted cheese sandwich appetizers you talked me out of before!” (Reluctantly, the waiter goes along with the request.) Waiter: “Oui monsieur.” (The waiter removes the plate from the table and walks off. As he does so, Russo turns to his date and begins to speak once again.) Russo: “Can you believe this place? First, they don’t have the bamboo umbrellas for that old wine they gave us and now snails on the food! Two boobs! That's what he takes us for! Well that’s fine, guess who’s not getting a tip tonight! (With that the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
