History 101- Running Rampant

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Russo (VO): “We once again had the control of the federation. Along with that control, came a hold over the Storms and their contracts. They had their fun with us, well now it was our turn. I remember our first payback on those two just like it was yesterday.” (The scene begins to scramble as it fades out from the current one to a shot from the past instead. We are inside of one of the many UWF shows. In the ring, Jason Kage stands side by side with Eric Russo, each one holding a microphone. Russo raises the microphone to his mouth in order to speak. Just before he does however, his own voice over cuts him off.) Russo (VO): “ The Storm were about to be introduced to the hell that is the Shadow Riders.” (The voice over stops as the Russo on screen begins speaking.) Russo: “Now since we’ve been back in power, we’ve been noticing that the Storm brothers love to do game shows. Well Slater and Chris, it’s sweeps week and you know what that means don’t you? It means that this week, the game show hosts get to be the contestants while the contestants become the hosts. So, I would like to welcome everyone to a special episode of “Storm of the Century”. Let’s meet our first contestant, Slater Storm.” (As Slater Storm makes his way out from the backstage area, a voice over by Russo begins.) Russo (VO): “The second I laid eyes on Slater Storm that night, I could tell that he was not enjoying our antics in the ring. That really didn’t matter to me though as I was not concerned with his enjoyment. The only enjoyment that I was concerned with was my own.” (The voice over stops and the scene continues. Slater Storm reaches the ringside area and slides into the ring. He quickly pops to his feet in almost a fighting stance as he maneuver around the ring. Keeping his eyes on both Eric Russo and Jason Kage at all times. Russo begins speaking.) Russo: “OK Slater, clam down. All we’re doing is playing a simple little game here. I think you know how this game works, so just spin the wheel.” (Reluctantly, Slater spins the wheel. After a few moments, it stops and it lands on “win a car”. Russo smiles as he moves in closer to Slater Storm.) Russo: “Wow! Well, it looks like you just got yourself a new car there good buddy.” (Russo looks towards Kage.) Russo: “Hey Kage, go bring it out.” (Kage rushes off to the backstage area as Slater stands in the ring, looking surprised that nothing has gone wrong yet. Kage now pulls a beautiful car out from the backstage area and begins driving it down to ringside. Once there, he gets out. Kage then gets in the ring and hands the keys over to Slater. Still slightly of how to react, Slater runs out of the ring and gets in the car. Russo yells out after him.) Russo: “Slater by the way, the cars papers are in the glove compartment. Before you go, make sure that everything is in order.” (A voice over by Russo quickly picks up.) Russo (VO): “Did you really think we would let him off that easy?” (With that, the Shadow Riders exit the ring and start towards the car. Russo begins speaking again.) Russo: “Slater, can you read the papers to us?” (As he reaches his side, Russo holds his microphone to Slater Storm as he awaits a response.) Slater: “Um . . . I can’t read.” (Without missing a beat, Russo turns o Kage.) Russo: “Kage, will you be so kind as to read the papers for him.” (Kage nods his head as Slater Storm hands him the papers. Kage looks at the papers for a moment, studying them. He then glances up from the sheets with a grim look on his face. Kage leans towards Russo as speaks into his microphone.) Kage: “Eric, it seems we have a problem. It says this car doesn’t belong to Slater here. It’s stolen.” Russo: “Stolen!? Oh no, you know what that means don’t’ you?.” (Russo and Kage both pull out bats from the backseat of the car. They then pull Slater out of the car. The Shadow Riders begin to beat on him with the bats. After a few moments, Russo climbs on the roof of the car as Kage stands on the hood. Together they pull Slater up onto the car. Once up, Kage sets him up for a powerbomb while Russo helps him. Together they powerbomb Slater through the car windshield. The Shadow Riders get off the car and walk up to the top of the entryway. A voice over breaks in.) Russo (VO): “We had dealt with one of the brothers already. We were not ready to stop there however. Chris Storm was about to learn the same lesson that his brother did, you don’t fu(k with the Shadow Riders.” (Back in the scene, Russo begins speaking.) Russo: “Well you all just got to see one of the famed Storms in person. Why not make it two. That’s right folks. It’s time for our second contestant, Slater’s younger brother, Chris Storm.” (Chris warily walks out to meet the Shadow Riders who are still holding the bats.) Russo: “Welcome to the show Chris.” (Suddenly, a siren goes off startling the younger of the two Storms. Happily, Russo continues in his best game show host impression.) Russo: “Lucky you Chris, you get the bonus round. OK, you have one of two choices. You can either take the prize that your brother already got or you can have what’s behind the mystery curtain. What’s your choice?” (Russo holds the microphone to Chris as he awaits his answer to the tough question. Finally he responds with a deep gulp of trepidation.) Chris: “I guess I’ll take the curtain.” (Russo pipes up as he holds the microphone to his mouth and looks out to the crowd.) Russo: “Well I guess Chris made the curtain call.” (Before he even knows what is happening, Chris Storm is being beaten with the bats by Kage and Russo. After they finish beating him, they drag him over to the curtain. Once there, they pull it open and we now see that an outhouse is behind it. The Shadow Riders throw Chris in the outhouse and proceed to shove it over. The outhouse rolls all the way down the ramp and smacks into the car that Slater Storm is still knocked out in. Russo raises the microphone to his mouth as he begins to speak once again..) Russo: “Next time it would do you good to remember the three rules of life. You don’t spit in the wind, you don’t tug on Superman’s cape, and you don’t ever fu(k with the Shadow Riders.” (As Russo finishes he drops the microphone in the ring. Slowly the scene begins to fade out. As it does however, a voice over picks up.) Russo (VO): “We had begun a war that would see the closing of the UWF. This however did not deter our new found clique. We had found our purpose in life and that purpose was destruction. Before we had a chance to move on to another federation, Hayes and Castle broke away from the group leaving Kage and myself as the only ones left to continue the fight. We bounced around from place to place destroying everything that we came into contact with. Then one day, we found our next true target in the form of a little federation known as the LOW. There, the Shadow Riders would build a legacy that is guaranteed to never fade away.” (With that the screen once again fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. Under the logo, a message is written across the bottom of the screen which reads . . . “The previous message has been brought to you by the leader of the Shadow Riders, “Superstar” Eric Russo. The screen then fades to black.)
