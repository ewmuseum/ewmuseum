Untitled roleplay 821

captured 7 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Enigma sits on the end of his bed watching the TV as the L.O.W. Press Conference is underway. He listens closely to the words of 'The Prophet' John Adams. John Adams speaks words of Alias, and how Alias had killed himself. I look down at my watch, it reads 6:59 P.M. Enigma rises from the end of the bed and grabs a coat and a tie and begins to walk towards the door, exiting the apartment and locking the door behind. > I began to try and process what had happened earlier in the day, the dark emotionless voice speaking the words 'Alias is dead, Enigma is next.' The words had a frightening sense to them. The tone of the voice sounded as though it was the voice of Satan himself. > 'Hello?' I said as I answered my cell phone. 'Hey Jakob.' Jenna's sweet voice called. 'Oh, Hey how are you holding up?' 'Everything is fine lately, I have moved on from the death of my father and the death of Alias, partially because of you, thanks for helping me through the hard times Jakob, you are a sweet guy.' > I felt myself blush as she said these words to me. What Am I thinking? This is like a fatal attraction. This is Alias' ex-girlfriend, I can't do this. I quickly though to ask her out to dinner, out of kindness. But She beat me to it. > 'Hey Jakob, Would you like to meet me at Ramero's for a nice dinner tonight? Just..me and you.' 'Yeah, sure.' 'Okay, Meet me there around 7:45' > 'Just..me and you.' 'Just..me and you.' What is this supposed to mean? Yes, She is very attractive..but I can't..it's not right..but maybe it is..I looked at my cell phone and went down the phone book as I drove down the narrow road. I found the name Erin and dialed in the phone number. It rang 3 times before she answered > 'Hello?' 'Hey Erin, It is Jakob. I am going to have to cancel tonight ok? After Catastrophic I will take you out.' 'Aww, Alright sweetheart, Is everything ok?' 'Yes, I just have to meet a friend.' 'A girl?' 'No, never. I must go though Ill call you later bye.' > I hung up the phone, knowing that I should not have lied to her. Erin is far too important to be lied to. But Jenna...I rubbed my face..the stress levels in my body were rising, Erin, Catastrophic, and Jenna. > '7:26' I said to myself as I looked down at my watch. I had made my way to Ramero's by 7:35 and I sat in my car trying to process everything going through my head. First, Erin, a girl that I had been seeing unofficially for nearly 1 year now, far before I ever met Jenna. Catastrophic, the match is going to be sheer mayhem, my worries are of The Prophet..his intentions are cruel, could it be him that made the annonymous phone call? Could it be another member of Adams' team. Then, Jenna, she causes the most stress, I can't start anything with her, I cannot get personal..I have Erin, it is Alias' love. I had thought too deeply obviously zoning out from my sourroundings. Someone knocked on the window and startled me..It was Jenna 'Are you coming Jakob?' 'Yes, I am' Enigma stepped out of the car and began walking beside Jenna 'So How are you Jake?' 'Quite alright, Under a lot of stress. Weird things have been happening to me lately.' 'Like what? You've been here for me now its time for me to be here for you.' 'I'd rather not discuss it.' 'Ok fine, lets go enjoy dinner then' She said as she smiled at me. Enigma and Jenna sat at dinner for around one hour and both of them enjoyed it very much. The night ended at Enigma's house with Jenna kissing Enigma. He kicked her out. > Now I really can't take this. The tension is rising, the anger is building, it must be released, I'm coming for you Prophet,Deekins, and Mehyu. > Enigma's cell phone rang, he answered 'Dear Enigma, you fucked with a girl..named Jenna tonight, Enigma, I see you. I saw the kiss. The sheer destruction of your career will occur, Enigma..you might even be killed in the ring. Enigma watch your back.' Enigma turned around and a huge figure was standing there in the doorway. The figure ran towards Enigma and nailed him in the head with a lead pipe. Enigma laid out cold. 'Enigma, have fun getting beaten by John Adams and his gang.' Nearly two hours later, Enigma rose from the floor and looked at his cell phone that had been vibrating. ONE NEW MESSAGE ------------------------------- Alright Enigma it is over. I have gotten what I wanted..I got the taste of your blood. You may not have died but I am sure that with the stress building inside of you..I can see it on your face..that stress will cause your death..just like that of the pathetic Alias. > I stood there looking at the cell phone. Still cringing in pain from the shot to the head. It's not over, I want to know who you are... > 'If you overtake your fears..a man cannot conquer you..death cannot conquer you..Catastrophy will have been caused if you overcome the fears of man, the fears of death, and the fears of life itself. I will cause Catastrophy to all of you in the ring...' [fade out]
