A Prophetic Dream!

captured 11 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*In case you never noticed, the path you never chose has chosen you. Jason Mraz* *Lost in a endless battle, Tired and hungry being led to slaughter all of the unknowing cattle, What drives me to this bloody massacre of hate and total destruction, Do you even have to ask when you can clearly see all of the corruption, Effortlessly preaching day and night like a man possessed, About a chosen one that will reshape the world as we know it is what I confess, The time for redemption is at hand for judgment day is upon us, Ashes to ashes dust to dust. ~ John “The Prophet” Adams* ~ The stage has been set; know is the time where LOW can witness there own destruction for a chosen one has come forward. One who has the ability to change the course of LOW all together and destroy the one thing SIN hates worse…………Tradition! Yes that’s right, tradition is the real threat to SIN and what it stands for, all of the Haze’s, Ronny’s, D.U.I.’s, Flying Vergomos and the rest follow that tradition that made LOW great back in the days. SIN will take out this tradition like the disease it is and the people who stand strong for it. Haze…….my oh my how cute, you have a little group of your own, did it take all of you long to figure out the name? And my dear friend The Demond, what makes you think that this is a battle between you and Haze? What makes you think that CJ let alone me, are just fillers to your big match against Haze? Should I remind you that I have beaten you not once but twice, in fact if it wasn’t for Haze in the first place I would still be Realm Champion! But that’s all in the past now, and what’s left for us is the future……..my future…….SIN’s future! ~ Time: 12:00 midnight Date: 3/25/05 Location: A restless night of sleep {The sky is dark and gloomy with a hint of death and decay in the air, the clouds are forming in a circular pattern centering over one area of the earth below it. The air around was cold and had an aura of pain and sorrow with it. The vegetation around the landscape was brown and dying from the life it used to have. A single man can be seen walking this land of death and despair, a man that has the determination and will to stand beyond the rest. The man has no visible guide to lead him to his final destination, only his Prophetic Destiny, the only thing that has lead him since birth. As he moves forward the ground opens up to reveal the lost souls that have tried to travel that same path but have failed.} ~ Where am I? ~ {After a couple of hours of traveling he notices a following behind him, warriors willing to follow him to the ends of the earth. Advisories try to stop them…….ones who fly in the air, ones who look like from a frat party, one who has a diamond in his eye surrounded by pirates, and a family of warriors that try everything to stop the man and his followers from getting to the destination that they seek. But nothing will stop them as they move on through the desalinate wasteland of sorrow.} ~ What is this? ~ {The scene around him changes dramatically to inside an altar, the lights are dim and a weird chanting can be heard in the background. As the traveler moves forward towards the altar he notices the chanting is getting louder by the minute. He then notices a stone tablet lying out in the center of the altar.} ~ Is this a dream? ~ *It Reads: The tradition that once was shall be destroyed by the sins that they have created and in the desolation of waste a new being will emerge with the promise to reshape the land in it’s own image………this is the Prophecy……this is the will of the chosen one.* {Right by the stone tablet was a book, almost like a bible and it stated that a chosen one would emerge to smitten the nonbelievers in a Prophets quest to reshape the world. A chosen one huh…..well this proved interesting as he picked up the book. As he did the chanting turned into loud moans and squeals as he saw tormented spirits flying around him. And near the northern side of the wall it opened up to reveal a skeleton with flesh and maggots hanging off of it. Staring at it for a while you realize that you cannot tell if it’s a man or woman, but with a sudden outcry and loud chanting the flesh starts to reshape onto the bone structure……………..} ~ Oh my God! ~ -Phone Rings. – {John Adams awakes from his deep sleep; the sweat is continuously dripping from his forehead. He goes to answer the phone with some heavy breathing.} John Adams: Hello? Chapel: Yeah man, this is Chapel…..are you all right John? John Adams: Yeah I’m fine….what do you want? Chapel: I want to know what the game plan for the PPV is. John Adams: Listen right know isn’t a good time….you and Khaos just be on your guard against DUI and The Flying Vergomos! I’ll call you two later on after I get my head straight! Chapel: Ahhhh ok will do boss! {As John Adams hung up the phone he notices on the table a book……”Hey that wasn’t there?” he thinks to himself as he moves closer to the book. He then notices that it is the same book as the one in his dream…….} ~ The Chosen One has arrived! ~ ~ FIN ~ * Nothing feebler than a man does the earth raise up, of all the things which breathe and move on the earth, for he believes that he will never suffer evil in the future, as long as the gods give him success and he flourishes in his strength; but when the blessed gods bring sorrows too to pass, even these he bears, against his will, with steadfast spirit, for the thoughts of earthly men are like the day which the father of gods and men brings upon them. Homer (800 BC - 700 BC), The Odyssey*
