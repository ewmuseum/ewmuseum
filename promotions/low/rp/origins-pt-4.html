Origins pt. 4

5 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::We open in back inside of the elaborate control room of the Metallo Zords headquarters. All at once the room fills with light as the gold and silver transport beams of the Metallos return them to their secret hideaway once again. Curious as to what final task they have left to complete, the Metallos begin to question Bobbabu.:: Silver Ranger - What ever was Bobbalu talking about? What could we possibly have left? Gold Ranger - I do not understand either. I thought we were through Bobbabu. ::Reassuringly, Bobbabu tries to put the Metallos minds at ease.:: Bobbabu - No my precious Metallos. There is still one more feature of your powers that you must learn to master. It is a power that has saved the lineage of the Metallo Zords many times in the past. So do you think you can handle it you two? ::With a nod of their helmet incased heads, the Metallos respond.:: The Metallo Zords - We will handle any task that comes into view Bobbabu. ::With a smile on his oversized face, Bobbabu continues.:: Bobbabu - That is good to hear my Metallos for your next challenge may be one of your toughest. You must now learn how to conjure your massive robotic weapons the Metallo Zords! ::Confused the Metallos look to Bobbabu.:: Gold Ranger - But I thought we were the Metallo Zords. Silver Ranger - Yes, that is also what I construe Bobbabu. ::Slightly angered Bobbabu responds.:: Bobbabu - What can I say, we don’t have enough money in our budget to copyright yet another name. Seriously, how much money do you really expect a disembodied head to have? It’s not like I have pockets to keep it in or anything. What do you want me to do? Keep it in my nose goo? ::Dejected the Metallos shake their heads. Seeing their now lowered attitude, Bobbabu attempts to make up for his harshness to the two intergalactic warriors.:: Bobbabu - There is no need to fret over that predicament though because each of your Metallo Zords will only be present if you are in them so it shouldn’t get too confusing because you both won’t be around at the same time. Either way, we can just bull through. ::Their normal pleasant demeanor returning to them now, the Metallos begin to express their interest in their Zords.:: Silver Ranger - May we see our Zords now? Gold Ranger - Yes, I would also like to see what they can do. ::Bobbabu looks down to his Metallos.:: Bobbabu - I’m afraid that I can not bring your Zords to you. One of the powers of the Metallos is that only the ranger that represents the Zord may summon it. Therefore only when you have mastered that art of hand summoning you Zord will you be able to test it out. Now then, if you could please follow Bobbalu, he will take you to the screening room where you will learn the hand motions required to summon your Zords. When it comes to summoning Zords, the video is the only thing that can give you a clue. ::Excited to learn how to summon their Zords, the Metallos gleefully follow Bobbalu with a noticeable spring to their step. The trashcan like robot leads the two Metallos into a huge room with a gigantic sliding wall and four seats. Each seat is a different color, there is a seat that appears to be platinum, another seat that looks to be made of diamond, and finally a gold and a silver seat. Almost as if instinctively knowing where to go, each of the Metallos takes a seat in the chair that corresponds to their color. As they sit down though, they begin to question the need for the three other seats.:: Gold Ranger - Tell me, what is the need for these other two seats? Silver Ranger - I would like to know as well. Why are there four seats when we are only two Bobbalu? ::A voice begins to emit from the tiny robot through an even tinier speaker.:: Bobbalu - Those seats are from the other two. For the Metallos to reach full strength, there must be four members. One to watch over each point on the compass. Unfortunately, Bobbabu has yet to find another two humans capable of filling those slots. Do not worry though he will not rest until he does. Now watch the screen and learn the art of hand summoning kung fu. ::The lights in the room begin to dim as the sliding wall begins to open. Slowly the wall opens to reveal a small TV with nothing more then pieces of tinfoil for it’s antennas. The Metallos cast a look in the direction of Bobbalu. Bobbalu addressees them.:: Bobbalu - We spent all of the money on the fancy sliding wall. Don’t worry though this TV will still allow a good enough view. ::The Metallos now turn their attention back to the screen of the small TV as a video begins to play on the tiny monitor. The two Metallos watch intently as various images of a man preforming a complex motion with his arms repeatedly flashes on the screen. As the man continues his demonstration, the Metallos practice along with him. Finally, the Silver Ranger gets the motions down. As he preforms the motions in the correct fashion, Bobbabu’s voice echos throughout the room.:: Bobbabu - The Silver Sentinel has been summoned. Silver Ranger prepare to be transported to your Zord in three . . . two . . . ::The Silver Ranger is engulfed in his silver transport beam and quickly removed from the room. Wanting to keep up with his partner, the Gold Ranger frantically attempts the motion with his arms once again. Luckily this time around he gets it right. Bobbabu’s voice echos through the room once again.:: Bobbabu - The Gold Gargantuan has been summoned. Gold Ranger prepare to be transported to your Zord in three . . . two . . . ::The Gold Ranger is engulfed in his golden transport beam and quickly removed from the room and is taken to his Zord. Now fully prepared to face the forces of evil, the Metallo Zords await their next challenge as the shot flashes out. Will they be able to save the Earth from the advances of the evil Queen Mogombu? Find out next time.::
