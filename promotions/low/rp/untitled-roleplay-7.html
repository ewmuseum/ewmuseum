Untitled roleplay

captured 23 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Selling Yourself 6 ::As the opening chords of “King of my World” by Saliva begin to play, the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. The lyrics to the song begin.:: I'll never seen any side of heaven I'll walk for miles through a blazin' hell It doesn't matter what you think I'm supposed to be 'Cause I myself know all too well ::The scene continues with various clips of Eric Russo in different matches . . . Russo picks Meyhu up into a Reverse Fireman’s Carry. He spins it into a sit-down neck breaker while the crowd goes insane, and Russo covers for the win! one - - two - - three! The bell tolls as Russo stands up, the referee raising his hand in victory. Russo pulls it away with anger as the crowd boos, and Russo stomps on Meyhu once more before leaving the ring. Now a clip as Russo lifts Dahmer up, and then throws him on top of his shoulder in a reverse fireman’s carry position! And then, in an instant, a blinded Eric Russo connects with his finisher; The Falling Star! Still dazed, Russo falls over Dahmer. 1 …… 2 ……3! The song continues.:: I'll open your eyes and make you see I'm the king of my world King of my world I'll break down the walls around you down ::Various scenes from Jason Kage’s matches are now shown . . . Jamester slides out of the ring and grabs a gasoline tank that was under the ring. Jamester slides back in the ring and pores the gasoline on and around the table that lies in the ring. Jamester then throws Kage on it and lights the table and circle around the table on fire. Jamester then heads to the turnbuckle. In a swift motion, Jamester jumps hitting the Frozen Fear and putting Kage through the table. The next scene shown is one of Pax and Belmont getting into the ring just as Kage starts to climb up a ladder. They walk over to the structure as Kage reaches the halfway point, he looks down at both Pax and Belmont and begins trying to plead his way out of not suffering such a horrible fate. Despite his cries though . . . Belmont pushes the ladder ahead of him. Kage falls from the ladder… crashing straight through a table pyramid!! The song continues.:: I'm the king of my world King of my world I'll break down the walls around you ::Another scene where Pax and Russo start to battle in the ring, struggling to get the better of one another. Finally Pax is able to sneak a knee to Russo’s gut and a kick to his groin. He then proceeds to take the stunned Russo by the head and throws him up and over the top rope… down to the outside of the ring. Pax sets the up a ladder and begins to climb it . . . he is about to get the belts when Tristan Belmont comes flying through the air, right into the ladder! It wobbles, and the ladder falls... just as Pax grabs the belts! He comes hurling to the ground at an immense speed, and hits right outside of the ring. Suddenly Eric Russo appears climbing up the apron. He gets into the ring and raises his right hand holding the Tag Team Championship belts!!! The song continues.:: My mind alone is filled with rage for you I am the pastor to your sheep I am the master of my destiny I am the king of all I see ::Clips of Russo at various times are shown . . . As Big Daveylicious looks up at a smiling Russo on the LOW-Tron with a confused look, he is taken down from behind by a shot with the Realm title to the back of the head by Eric Russo! Russo grabs Daveylicious by the hair and pulls him up to his feet. He then gives one well placed boot to the gut before setting Davey up. Russo positions Daveylicious’ head between his legs and wraps his arms around his waist. He then hoists him up and drops him with the Daveylicious Bomb! Another clip of Justice spinning Ezra around and sets him up for a powerbomb. Luke then lifts Ezra into the air and just holds him in around into a powerbomb position. In front of the two Russo turns his back to Ezra and reaches up hooking his arm around Ezra’s neck. Russo now calls out to Justice, giving him the signal to drop the bomb. As Justice thrusts down with all his might, Russo drops in a sitdown neckbreaker motion. Ezra lies motionless on the canvas as Russo gets to his feet and stands over his opponents fallen form with his hands raised high into the air.:: I'll open your eyes and make you see I'm the king of my world King of my world I'll break down the walls around you down ::Now clips of Kage at various times are shown . . . Kage anxiously stands up and welcomes Jennifer Aniston back behind his desk. She begins to flirt with him, and just as he starts to put his guard down she knees him in the crotch causing him to double over. As he’s hunched over holding himself, Aniston grabs the back of his head and slams it into the desk. Kage falls backwards back into his chair, whimpering in pain. The next scene features Kage pulling Madonna away from Russo. In the process, Kage cops a feel on Madonna. Feeling Kage behind her, Madonna lashes out with a mule kick which connects with Kage’s groin. Kage drops to the floor in pain. The song continues.:: I'm the king of my world King of my world I'll break down the walls around you ::The scene shows Russo climbs up one side of a ladder slowly while Meyhu starts to get his footing on the other side. They each take a step at a time . . . moving up, trying to get to the top first. Fortunately, Meyhu manages to catch up with Russo and the two meet at the top! They start to brawl momentarily. Finally Meyhu gets the upper hand and as Russo falls back, he reaches for the belts. Grabbing on in a lucky attempt and TAKES IT OFF!!! A downed Russo proudly holds up his half of the tag team titles!! The song continues.:: Here I come again Come again 3,2,1 Here I come Here I come again 3,2,1 Here I come again Here I come 3,2,1 Here I come HERE I COME! ::As the beat of the song picks up, various action shots are shown . . . Judgement turns around and grabs Kage by his neck. He lifts him into the air with both hands on his neck and keeps him there. Kage grabs onto Judgement's arms and tries to tear them off to the side, but the strength of the big man is way too much. Judgment continues to choke Kage as he struggles and struggles! Judgement makes his move towards the barricade where he holds Kage higher then before. He lets out a loud roar as he throws Kage right through the barricade! The next scene shows Jesse Jamester come running towards Russo, wanting to connect with a big splash in the corner, but just as he jumps in the air, Russo waltzes out of the corner, which gives Jesse a mouth full of turnbuckle. Jamester, backs up, stunned, and falls into Russo's arms, and in what soon looks top be a perfect belly to back suplex! Jesse's neck and back bends in a way that none should, before he finally crashes down, and stays there, in pain. Russo runs towards the turnbuckles, and slowly climbs then. Now, he sits perched on top of the third turnbuckle, using the cage behind him as rest and a sure fire way that he will not slip off. Jesse begins to move, and just when he gets to full vertical basis, Eric Russo jumps up in the air, flies right to Jesse Jamester, and connects with a missile dropkick from the third rope, right to the face of Jesse Jamester. A gash of blood explodes upon contact as Jesse is propelled back into the cage, only to fall back, hurting like crazy! The song continues.:: I'm the king of my world The king of my world I'll break down the walls around you down ::The scene shows Kage and Annihilation as both men make it back to their feet at virtually the same time and begin exchanging blows back and forth. First Annihilation . . . then Kage . . . Annihilation . . . Kage. Annihilation finally scores a hard right that sends Kage down to the mat. With Kage down, Annihilation jumps into the air and lands with a heavy leg drop across Kage’s throat. The song continues.:: I'm the king of my world King of my world I'll break down the walls around you ::The scene switches to a shot of Kage down and out cold on the mat as Annihilation wearily makes his way to a cage door. Annihilation sticks his head between the middle and top rope as he reaches out and pushes the door open. He manages to ges his head out of the door then . . . WHAM! The door is slammed shut hard on the head of Annihilation knocking him out cold as well. As the camera spins to see the cause of this, Eric Russo stands with a grin on his face. The song continues.:: I'm the king of my world King of my world I'll break down the walls around you down ::As the song wraps up, the screen fills with the logo of the Shadow Riders. As the picture begins to fade out, we can hear Russo’s voice.:: Oh and that’s not ego, that’s reality Show URL: http://
