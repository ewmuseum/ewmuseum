LOW — Roleplay board

Confidence, personified.

11 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Desperation is a horrible thing. It makes men of reason and of purpose and of rationale do things they normally wouldn’t do, or would think of as a silly notion. When back into a corner and desperate, a man of calmness and of coolness, might just act as a cornered dog that has no other option than to bite…it’s whether or not that man can contain the beast and use the rage and explosion of desperation in a positive way or not that makes or breaks him. ** :: We open in the familiar surroundings of Amy Lee and Demond’s home. It’s been rebuilt since the last time we were here, and the furniture has been repaired and reupholstered. The Lights Out Wrestling Realm Championship Title hangs in its stand above the hall to the master bedroom proudly, it’s recently been shinned with Never Dull and is literally throwing off it’s own light. The plate on the main part of the belt proudly bears the title “ The Demond “ and looks almost brand new…which it should. The case has also been recently cleaned and the glass sparkles in the brand new spotlight that’s been placed near by on the ceiling. Above the entire spectacle of the title belt hangs a picture. It’s in a cherry oak frame and has a small placard on the bottom reading “ Cobi Deekins 2004 “. The picture is a masterpiece of carnage itself. It’s a shot, taken at precisely the exact moment that Demond was flying through the air, with Cobi clutched tight, falling into the ring from the top of the cage, moments before impact. Camera flashes can be seen all over in the background and the look on Demond’s face is calm and focused, while the look on Cobi’s is one of pure terror. The camera pans around the room a bit, and catches a new face. The man that assisted Demond last Realm sits calmly on a couch, a PlayStation 2 controller in hand. He’s playing “ Def Jam: Fight for New York “ and is quite into it. He’s moving his body with the controller and jerking this way and that as he plays. His character, Busta Rhymes, finishes the match with his special move and the man settles, leaning back into the couch and smiling, chuckling a little. From behind him we hear a door open and Demond, and the beautiful Amy Lee walk from their room. Amy looks exhausted and wears some green and white, old, pajama pants and a wife beater tank top. Demond makes his way out right behind her and is wearing some black karate pants with no shirt and his hair pulled straight back into a ponytail, soaking wet as it is. He also looks spent, but smiling, and Amy moves towards the kitchen, as he takes a seat next to his friend on the couch. :: Amy: “ You want anything? “ The Demond: “ Yeah just get me some juice, I’ll be alright with that. “ :: Demond looks to his friend, and his friend looks back. At the same time they both look up at the Realm title, then back at one another, and then suddenly they both burst into laughter. They finally calm after a few minutes and Demond makes the first bit of words we’ve heard since the frantic laughing began. :: The Demond: “ Oh man…that’s great… What fun last week huh? “ Unknown man: “ No jokes! That was great, and the blood bath was a nice touch, gotta admit it. “ The Demond: “ Well I didn’t expect you to whip out your special move on Deadly Myst either, the cartwheel so soon? Don’t you want to leave ANYTHING to the imagination? “ UM: “ Since when do I NOT leave EVERYTHING to the imagination. Did you hear those announcers on the replay? They have no idea who, what, or when I am, and they have no idea where I came from; I’d say I left plenty to the imagination. “ The Demond: “ Who, what, or….when?…..Dude… “ UM: “ You know what I meant! “ The Demond: “ Well regardless it was a great night and now Maximum Carnage has been put to rest. Maybe now I can move onto something important…like the World Title. “ UM: “ Well with half of Counter Culture by your side now, things are looking up for The Demond. “ The Demond: “ Well I have to thank you Jes…. “ :: Right as we’re about to find out what this mystery mans name is…. AMY comes running from the kitchen with Demond’s juice almost spilling it everywhere and seems over excited. :: Amy: “ D! You gotta turn it on the promo channel. They just released the card for Catastrophic! “ The Demond: “ Ah…about time. Maybe they finally got me in a good match. Like the one I proposed against Justice and Annihilation. “ Amy: “ It’s not that, I don’t think you’re going to be to happy about this actually. “ :: Demond looks confused, but takes the AVD cords from the TV and turns it on the promo channel. Just as we come back to the channel, the spot for the match Demond is involved in comes flashing across the screen. :: Clip from the LOW promo channel: :: A flash comes across the screen and lightly, EVER so lightly in the black background the image of Demond taking Cobi Deekins to the mat from the top of the cage is shown. The clip stops right as their bodies begin crashing through the ring. Then a very clear, very sudden shot of the referee looking into the hole is shown, and it shows the ref counting the 1…2…3! He rings the bell as the shot fades away again. A shot of the fans looking around in wonder is shown then, and Rusty Monarch can be heard making the call :: RM: “ Ladies and Gentleman, the winner of the match….. and NNNNEEEEEEWWWWW LOW REALM CHAMPION!!!! THE DEMOND!!!! “ :: The fans saddened and heartbroken looks can be seen as the sound of “ Fear “ by Disturbed hits. The scene fades to black again. The words, toned in red hues and dripping onto the screen like blood, : “ One of the most dominate forces to hit LOW since it began… “ flash onto the screen. A shot then of Demond standing tall, blood dripping from his face, but holding the Realm Championship high for all to see is shown. He lets out a yell, and through the magic of the recording studio they turn the yell into a deep and throaty roar. That fades to black. The clip of Demond making his way towards the back is then shown, and we see Maximum Carnage hit the stage and take Demond and Al out in the truck. We then see that fade to a shot from backstage of them transferring the unconscious Demond and Al into the trunk of a car, but not closing it, then taking off out the service entrance. That fades to black. Then words again come to the screen: “ Angered by one of the most forceful and aggressive stables in the LOW today “. They fade and then we see a shot from Demond’s clip the week before. He tosses a couch across the room and lets out yet another roar. Suddenly, that fades and we hear violin and harp music hit in a terrifying rift of music. A shot from Demond’s promo last week is then shown. :: The Demond: “ Payne is no challenge and I will destroy him this week! NO QUESTIONS ASKED! “ :: That fades and we see the words once more: “ They paid dearly for what they had done. “ And we see a shot from the massacre of Viper and Deadly Myst by Demond and the Unknown Man. That fades into a shot of them covered in blood and Payne backing out of the locker room. Words once more: “ And they were taught a valuable lesson… “. A shot then of Demond putting the final mark on the match with Swift Payne last week is shown, and he places his foot on top of him for the 1…2…3! Which is slowed down for dramatics. Then the words: “ …or so the champion thought! “. Quickly a shot of Catastrophic’s card being read by an LOW official is shown. :: LOW Official: “ And in our next match we’ll see a grudge match, which will be a handi-capped 3 on 1 match, putting the LOW Realm Champion, The Demond, against the entirety of Maximum Carnage, Viper, Deadly Myst, and Swift Payne, and it will be for the Realm Championship. The first man to pin or make Demond submit will win the Realm Champion, Demond must survive to retain. “ :: And it’s slows and fades. A single shot of Payne smiling is shown, along with the versus screen from last week showing Demond and Payne facing one another and looking pissed. A cut in of Viper and Deadly Myst is then cropped on top of that and a fire erupts in the background as the Catastrophic logo appears as apposed to the Realm logo that was there. Then finally, the words: “ Three men, facing the one-man army…who will survive, and who will be the Champion…find out this Sunday, at LOW CATASTROPHIC! “ are shown, and the screen erupts into flames once more. The screen goes black, and the TV is shut off completely. :: We’re now taken back to the home of Demond and Amy Lee. :: Demond sits and stares at the screen as the spot ends. He stares blankly, not angry, not upset, not happy or sad or confused. He just stares. But every intelligent person knows there’s more than one way to tell when a person is mad. With flat black eyes, it’s hard to read The Demond’s face for emotion…so we just have to look at his hands. They shake and crack as the knuckles pop and snap. They are being clenched tighter and tighter until finally, the remote for the TV explodes into pieces of plastic and rubber, and it goes flying in everywhere. Amy and the unknown man dive for cover. Demond stands and still stares at the TV, blood dripping from his now cut, but still tightly clinched hand. He turns slowly to look at the Realm title once more, then looks down at Amy and his friend, who are looking at him with worry in their faces. He then suddenly calms down greatly and sits back down on the couch. :: The Demond: “ Amy… “ Amy: “ Y..yeah D? “ The Demond: “ Could you please go get me the iodine and a ace wrap for my hand? “ Amy: “ Sure thing… “ :: She walks off to do so, and Demond looks to his friend. :: The Demond: “ What the FUCK is that X? “ The man now know as X: “ I don’t know bro…sounds like their trying to fuck you out of the title. “ The Demond: “ Obviously. Who’s dick is Payne sucking around that office to get a shot like that? “ X: “ No clue man, but you better get a game plan in order, you’ve only got 6 days till show time. “ The Demond: “ NO shit! What the hell kind of shit is this? 3 on 1 handi-capped, what did I do to deserve that BULLSHIT?! AND WHERE THE FUCK HAS AL BEEN?! “ :: Amy makes her way into the room with a bowl, a bottle, and an Ace wrap. She takes a small TV tray and puts Demond’s hand over it, uncapping the bottle. :: Amy: “ He called last night, he said he’d be by today after work. “ The Demond: “ About damn time, he wasn’t even there last week…not that I needed him, though this week might be a little different! “ X: “ Look D, maybe this is a good thing? “ :: Amy takes the bottle and pours the Iodine over Demond hand where he cut it and it still bleeds. The cut is about 4 inches long and pretty deep. It fizzes and bubbles in the cut and the blood thins out as the Iodine takes effect. Demond however, doesn’t even flinch. :: The Demond: “ How X, how could this possible be good for me? “ X: “ Well think about it. What have they been doing since you got in the LOW? “ The Demond: “ They’ve been pissing me off. “ X: “ No, not that. I mean with the matches. They’ve been testing you. You got through the battle royal pretty good, and your next match you blew through with Payne. Then they gave you a shot at the title, which you won. So why do you think they’d be giving you a match like this at the PPV? “ The Demond: “ Enlighten me X. Why? “ X: “ Because they are just testing you again. It’s obvious LOW needs a new World Champion already, and I think they’re looking right at The Demond to fill the position. But they have to make sure you’re ready. You’ve proven you can handle a big time match when you beat Deekins for the title in the cage. They’ve proven you can make it on your own by putting you in that battle royal, and you almost won, and now, now they want you to prove you’re ready to go to the next level, and beat all three of these clowns. Next up, I think, is the Xtreme Championship…or even a jump right to a match against Russo or whoever is World Champion at the time. It makes perfect sense if you think about it. “ :: Amy cleans out the wound and takes a piece of the remote that’s about 3 ½ inches long out of Demond’s hand…who still doesn’t flinch. :: The Demond: “ You know what X? You might just be right… it does make sense. I mean, obviously Russo cant be champ to long, he’s a clown and has like, no skill at all, and I obviously need something bigger than the Realm title…I’m wasted talent with all these losers in the Realm Division… you might just be on to something X. What do you think Amy? “ Amy: “ I think you need to stop getting so damn mad and hurting your damn self. But other than that, I think X might be right. It’s like a job interview…in a very odd….very over the top sort of way. They are giving you enough rope to hang yourself with, and if you’re successful against Payne and his two goons, then maybe you ARE ready to be the World Champion. Whatever you want I know you can achieve…just please be careful. “ :: Demond smirked down at her…the couple really did look like beauty and the beast. The huge, dark, demonic looking Demond, and the small, petite, beautiful Amy Lee. The sudden pounding on the door however, suddenly jerked his attention up. Al…was here. :: The Demond: “ Come on in Al, the door’s unlocked. “ :: Al burst into the door, as usual, fuming angry about something. And showing it this time, he yelled out the door. :: Al: “ And next time, park in the MIDDLE of the parking spot and I wont hit you’re old busted ass piece of SHIT! “ :: Someone outside yelled right back. :: Al: “ I DON’T CARE IF IT’S NEW! IT’S A PIECE, NOW QUIT YOUR BITCHIN! “ :: Al slammed the door and walked in, almost being blinded by the Realm Title. :: Al: “ GOD DAMN! New spot light huh?! “ The Demond: “ Oh yes…gotta make it look good. “ Al: “ Old bitty ass neighbor of yours… “ The Demond: “ You hit Mrs. Butters’ new Jag?! “ Al: “ SHE WAS ALL THE WAY IN TWO SPACES! It was that old, wrinkly ass bitches fault! “ :: Demond, X, and Amy all looked at one another, shook their heads, and dropped them to the floor in disbelief. :: Al: “ What? … ANYWAY, so what’s up, they let out the card for Catastrophic yet? “ :: Demond and X just looked at one another and shook their heads again. :: Amy: “ Yeah Al, they just did, D has to fight against all three members of Maximum Carnage by himself, and it’s FOR the title. “ Al: “ Damn….that’s some bullshit D. “ The Demond: “ Yeah Al, I know. But what can I do? Really? Besides, I think X and I just figured this whole thing out anyway. “ Al: “ What do you mean. “ :: For the next few minutes X and Demond explained the reasoning they just talked about while Al, surprisingly, just sat and listened. After they were done Al just sat and figured it all out in his head. Finally he got up and walked over to the title belt hanging over the doorway and looked up at it. Demond and X joined him and they all looked at it for a long moment. :: Al: “ Well, I guess it’s pretty obvious what we have to do now. “ X: “ Is it now? “ Al: “ Pretty much. “ The Demond: “ I have a feeling I’m in store for a long week. “ :: In response Al and X just smirked and kept looking up at the title. The scene began to fade to black, and as it did the three and Amy headed for the door, after Demond reached up and took hold of the title. Fade to black. :: LATER THAT DAY :: Just as last week the LOW promo channel flares to life and Demond stands there, flanked by X and Al, with Amy standing in front of him, wrapped by his huge arms. LOW reporter Samantha Lee stood there, also like last week, and waited for her signal. :: SL: “ Good afternoon LOW fans and welcome once again to the LOW promo channel. I’m standing here once again with LOW Realm Champion The Demond, his beautiful girlfriend Amy Lee, and his two friends Al and …. Well we still don’t know this gentleman’s name Demond; want to clue us in on who he is? “ The Demond: “ He can speak, why don’t you ask him? “ SL: “ Very well, want to introduce yourself to the world sir? “ :: She goes to hold the mic in front of him, but X just takes it out of her hands and holds it sideways and close to his mouth. :: X: “ ME? You want to know who I am? Heh heh… I’ll tell you who I am. I’m the Boogey Man, I’m the shadow that moves around in your room at night. I’m the monster under your bed. I’m the Grim Reaper, and Satan all at once. “ SL: “ Yes, but what do we call you? “ X: “ Just call me Jester X, that’s what I’m known by, but you’ll all soon know me by another name all together. “ SL: “ And what’s that? “ Jester: “ DEATH! “ :: Jester went into a frantic laughter and fell off to the side out of the camera’s vision. He could be heard laughing in the background though. :: SL: “ Yes well… Demond, last week at Realm 60, you and Jester X took out two thirds of Maximum Carnage when you attacked them, then blood bathed them backstage. Then, after that, you took out their leader, Swift Payne, in a Hardcore match, pinning him by disrespectfully putting your foot on top of his broken body. It seemed as if at the end of Realm last week you had put the final nail in the coffin that was Maximum Carnage, however we all heard the announcement just yesterday that you will be facing all THREE members of MC at Catastrophic in a handi-capped match for the Realm Championship. What’s your opinion on this match? “ The Demond: “ Well it’s pretty obvious I’m either being completely screwed and the LOW brass is trying to take this title from me, or I’m being tested. “ SL: “ Tested? “ The Demond: “ Yeah, you know, like being scouted to see if I can handle something. “ SL: “ Who do you think is testing you, and for what? “ The Demond: “ Come on Samantha, I’m sure you’re smarter than that. The LOW brass is scouting me. They want to see if I can handle the big time, because it’s BLATANTLY obvious that Russo can’t handle being the Champion. “ SL: “ What do you mean? He’s been a fine Champion so far. “ The Demond: “ Are you serious? Look at that clown, he doesn’t have the look, the talent, or the popularity he needs to be the Champion. “ SL: “ And you think you’re a better candidate? “ The Demond: “ Obviously. Come on, I haven’t been beaten since I got here, except for that bullshit battle royal my first night here, and even then, it took every man in that ring to beat me, and in the end it took me being ganged up by them all to take me down. Other than that, I’m unstoppable. Who else in this federation can take me down? I’ve openly challenged at least four people since I’ve been here, and they all stay cowered away in their homes and scared to accept the challenge, so now I’m doomed to stay down here with these little punks in the Realm division until I show the LOW I deserve to be in the big time. So that’s what Catastrophic is all about, me showing the LOW hierarchy that I need to be in higher profile matches. “ Amy: “ At Catastrophic, I’ll be walking down with Demond as well for the first time, just to show the kind of ratings he can bring to the show. With me by his side, the enormous amount of ratings D normally brings in will triple with my pretty face walking down their next to him. There’s even rumor that a few friends of D and Mine will be performing at Catastrophic. “ SL: “ Really? Like who? “ Amy: “ I dunno, you’ll just have to wait and see, but it may just be the biggest thing to happen to an LOW PPV since the first one. “ SL: “ Well Demond, I have one more question. Since this will be a Handi-capped match, will Jester X and Al be at ringside with you? “ The Demond: “ That’s their call, but even if they are, they’ll just be down their as enforcers, making sure Maximum Carnage doesn’t try anything stupid. As far as I know, it’s not a hardcore match or anything, so Jester and Al will be down there for mainly moral support and to make sure everything’s nice and fair ya know? Kind of like extra refs. “ SL: “ Anything else before we go Demond? “ The Demond: “ Yeah as a matter of fact, I wanted to specifically address Maximum Carnage before we were done here. “ SL: “ Well then the mic is yours Champ. “ :: Samantha hands Demond the mic and steps out of the shot. Amy breaks away from Demond, and she and Al try to contain Jester in the background as the camera zooms in on Demond and Demond alone. :: The Demond: “ Now listen you three. It’s been a little over a month since I joined the LOW, and I’ve had enough of your bullshit. I’ve dealt with all three of you in your own way, and now I’ve got to actually face all three of you at once, and that’s fine with me. But this is it, no more. After Catastrophic, Maximum Carnage is to fade away to me and disappear. I figure that you three need a final sign that I’m better than your combined efforts, so I’ll give it to you. I’ll destroy all three of you at one time, and then, THEN you’ll need to go find something else to do, because if you FUCK with me again, you’ll regret it more than anything you’ll ever regret for the rest of your miserable little existences. Viper, you know exactly, first hand, what I can do to a man. I don’t need to warn you about what will happen to you if you mess with me in that ring, you’ll be dealt with in turn, just like the other two. You’ll suffer another Kamikazi and be helped into the back, that way you can receive some medical attention. You know what will happen Viper, so this is your posted warning. Deadly Myst. You saw what happens when Jester gets moody, and by the feeling of your tag partner being draped across your own lifeless body, you should know not to mess with me. But I expect nothing but your best in that ring. Show me what you have and maybe I’ll let you walk free, since you were the dumb ass that left the trunk open I suppose I owe you at least that much. And finally, FINALLY, Swift Payne. How many more nails must I put in your coffin? How many more beatings must I give you until you get it through your head that I far surpass your abilities and that you can NOT beat me? How many more times must I completely obliterate you in that ring for you to learn the truth? I mean hell, you were put in a position where you could hit me with any weapon, and anything in your arsenal and what happened? I stood on your chest at the end of that match in complete dominance over you. It isn’t going to get better for you Payne, only worse. It’s only going to get harder, no matter how many men you have by your side, I’ll always be better than you and I’ll always have better men at my right hand than you. I’m better than you, that’s why I have this, and you do not. “ :: Demond smacked his giant hand over the plate of the Realm Championship. :: The Demond: “ As we wind on down the road of life, you wont get better than me, I’ll only continue on towards the freeways exit as you continue on until you finally get in that ten car pill-up of life and go away, like we all want you to. Understand this, and understand it GOOD Maximum Carnage… at Catastrophic, it’s not a game plan of team tactics you’re going to want to formulate…it’s a game plan of survival for each and every one of you. And if you cant handle surviving, what makes you think you can handle living at all? “ :: Demond lowered the belt from his shoulder, a sign of utter lack of caring for the thing and what it stood for right now, showing he was saying this as a man, and not as just another face in the industry. He wanted MC to know what he said came from inside, and not from the notes of a writer, he wanted them to know his seriousness. :: The Demond: “ Maximum Carnage, a Dark Wave Rises on your Horizon…and his name is… THE DEMOND. Have a nice day today, for tomorrow brings the start of a new day, and who says…you…will…LIKE…IT?! “ :: The screen slowly fades to black, Demond’s cold black eyes burning into the souls of all who watched the scene. The eyes told the truth, Demond truly believed that he need not worry about surviving Sunday, that was for his opponents to worry about. We fade totally to black, the final sound…the sound of laughter. :: ** The blood of mine enemies is sweeter than even my own mother’s milk. **
