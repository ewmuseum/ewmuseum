"LAST CHANCE!!!!!"

17 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello its your very last chance to join the EUWF we will be closing to new members soon. We have put together one of the best rosters I have ever seen. We have got: Hardcore Harris Tower Magus T.A. Hawkins Origional Gangstas Jeff Bidwell Damann Prophet Many many more..... Here is the title history!!! EUWF WORLD TITLE! Kidman- Won it on Opening Day the Battle Royal Sniper-Won it at GO For the Gold PPV! Vs Savage-Hightower in a Steel cage triangle electric baseball bat match.
Kidman-Rewon it VS Sniper at Monday night Massacre. **** NOTE KIDMAN NEVER LOST A MATCH IN EUWF, He left for awhile it went vacant he had a record of 9-0***
EUWF IC TITLE: (Which is changed to Diamond title now) Mr.Excitement Alex Extreme-Beat Spartan in a Ladder match at Go For the Gold. Johnny Banger: Beat Extreme on Monday night Massacre. Spartan: Beat Banger at Armegeddon PPV! **** NOTE Extreme Retired from EUWF at 6-1**** EUWF US Title: (Which is now EUWF Internet title) Auston: Won the Battle ROyal on Monday Night Massacre Mooncharmer: Beat Auston at Armegeddon PPV! *** Note Auston was 5-2 and claimed never to lose the title, He had a fake title the whole time., Mooncharmer was 5-0*****
EUWF WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPS: Americas Least Wanted: Won the Tag team Battle Royal on Go For the Gold! Dukes of Hell (Now known as Just Dukes): Beat ALW at Armegeddon. **** NOTE Dukes were 14-0 marking them as the Best team I HAVE EVER seen in my life) THIS IS JUST TO SHOW THE EUWF TITLE HISTORY EVER!!! Thanks and I cant wait to add to this! COMING SOON THE 1st EVER EUWF HALL OF FAME!
