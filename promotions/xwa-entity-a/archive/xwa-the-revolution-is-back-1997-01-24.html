[XWA]The Revolution is back....

24 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, I'm Robbie Greer,handler of wrestlers such as Psychosis,Padded Room,Robbie Greer and The Kings of the Kage(All very good wrestlers).Last year in April,I started up a league known as the WAIWA.9 months it was open,but when no one roleplayed anymore,it was declared dead...... ...............It's about to change The WAIWA may be dead,but the XWA is it's re-encarnation!!!!! The XWA(Xtreme Wrestling Alliance) is going to be the new fed from me,the original president of the 9 month old fed,the WAIWA(Wrestling Advantage International Wrestling Alliance).This federation will be much more different than the WAIWA was.It will have.......... - Flash Cards - House Shows - 3 Title belts(World,IC and TV) - One tag belt - 2 weekly cards - PPV every six weeks - and most important...........Xtreme ACTION!!!!! If you have the expierience in E-wrestling to join(I'd like some EXP wrestlers),E-mail me at [detail withheld] to get more details on the XWA.If you are a rookie,and think you can RP enough to be a good choice,than mail me as well for details.... We here at the XWA use not booked,not simmed but a booked/simmed system.I use TNM6.2+ to sim the fights and moves,but every RP counts on the part of your wrestler.Say your wrestler originally has a push of 70.If you send in some good RP's,you have a better chance of winning that the other guy,cause you'll get a push boost.That means your push may change from 70 to 75-80 with some good solid RP's. House happen everyday,mostly being jobber fights,but some good ones as well.They are less detailed and provide action all week round.... -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- House show example.... Butt Soldier defeated Seargent Monkey at 0:12:09 when Butt Soldier piledrove Monkey through a table for a countout victory. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- Flash Cards also come out every day.They have wrestlers Flashes on them so we can have challenges in between cards. TV Cards happen twice a week.They also have flashes on them,and they have full matches,not just the summary.Like a match being announced by live announcers like on RAW or ECW or shows like that. XWA is a hardcore league,though some fights and refs are strict to the rules.Many matches may result in bloodbaths,while others may result in clean pins.I will be fighting in this league as well,and I will be TOTALLY FAIR AND UNBIASED in my desicions. You have a limit of four wrestlers per handler,as I don't waht one guy with 2 wrestlers,and another with 27.You do not need to have 4 wrestlers.You can have less,but four is the highest amount you can have. I hope to have some good feuds in the fed,as I am not going to start any of the feuds.You have to start fueds with RP's and run-ins and Hardcore matches. Remember,if you want more information on the XWA,E-mail me at [detail withheld] you,and I hope to see you in the battle fray known as the XWA. Robbie Greer( [detail withheld] )
