Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Xtreme Wrestling Alliance announced itself on 24 January 1997 as the re-launch of a nine-month-old promotion, the WAIWA, with its founder introducing the new federation under the same wrestler persona he says he used there, Robbie Greer, alongside Psychosis, Padded Room, and the tag team the Kings of the Kage.

The WAIWA, the post says, had opened the previous April and was “declared dead” nine months later “when no one roleplayed anymore” — its founder's own account of why he was starting over under a new name.

The new federation set three singles belts (World, Intercontinental, and Television) and one tag title, two weekly cards and a pay-per-view every six weeks, on a booked/simmed system in which a wrestler's push rose or fell with the quality of the roleplay submitted for him, daily “Flash Cards” for quick challenges, and twice-weekly TV cards written up with full commentary “like on RAW or ECW.”

The post offers a sample house-show result as a specimen of its daily style: “Butt Soldier defeated Seargent Monkey at 0:12:09 when Butt Soldier piledrove Monkey through a table for a countout victory.”

Whether the Xtreme Wrestling Alliance held to the four-wrestler-per-handler limit it set, ran an actual card, or crowned any of its four championships is not attested; no further document survives.
