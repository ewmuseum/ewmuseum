Nagoya Organization of Puroresu, News and Notes, Call to Applicants

October 18, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
News for October 17th, 2001 Nagoya Organization of Puroresu (NOP) President Kobe Koresawa announced at a press conference today that NOP has signed its first three members, two gaijins, 'Brattitude' Michelle Bailey, the North American renowned junior heavyweight, one of the top wrestlers in the world and her partner, 'Sweet
Dreams' Shane Destiny. Both trained by the great' Supreme Being' Jeremy Rhodes,
Destiny and Bailey bring a truly paradox-laced tag team to NOP.
The final of the three, Angel Suicida is in his early 20s and already a seasoned ring veteran, working with the lucha style in his native Mexico,
Suicida should be a mainstay of the NOP Junior-Heavyweight division, which will captivate the fans with its excitement.
NOP has announced its first tour will be called the Crystal Gamma Tour, the first show will be held in Hokkaido, Japan, the Monbetsu City Sports Center.
Date is to be announced. Anyone near the area may call their local ticket agency and find themselves tickets, at only 5 dollars (American Currency) a piece, it promises to be well worth the money.
NOP is open to more signees, looking for one more in the junior division and 2 in the heavyweight division. At this point in time, Asian and Hispanic are preferred. NOP Head Trainer/Booker, Keiji Kurasaki, has stated that he is extremely excited about the upcoming talent he will be working with. Destiny,
Bailey and Suicida have all begun to hit the gym.
Finally, a Television agreement has been reached with local station, Nagoya
Action TV, to air televised NOP shows at Midnight. More details to come.
News for October 18th, 2001 [AP Nagoya, Japan] The Nagoya Organization of Puroresu(from here on called NOP) has many tidbits today. They have announced their Crystal Gamma Tour shows, but no dates yet. Many arenas around Japan, mostly gyms and small venues will be the sights of a much anticipated Tour. Dates for this tour will be announced as soon as NOP has a larger roster, so be on the look-out for that.
Exciting, yet very, very cocky duo known as Tech-Force has been formed in NOP consisting of Sho Shoji, and "Rising Star" Fuyuki Hamada Jr. The two men speak a lot about their talent, and are now looking to back it up. Also signed is enigmatic Goshikku Mitsukai, a lucharesu hybrid, new-age, very talented young man. Expect him to make a splash in the already exciting NOP Junior Division.
Rounding out the new signees is a heavyweight, "Blinding Storm" Tetsumi Nanami, a strong-style puroresu wrestler who seems to have a lot of talent, with even more untapped. As the first native heavyweight signed, expect him to be a mainstay in the division.
