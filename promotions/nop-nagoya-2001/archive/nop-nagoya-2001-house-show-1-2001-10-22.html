Inagural House Show, 10-21-01

October 22, 2001, 12:00:00 AM

Nagoya Organization of Puroresu HOUSE SHOW #1 -------------------------------------------------------- PROMOTION: Nagoya Organization of Puroresu EVENT: Opening Show, Crystal Gamma Tour, 10/21/01 VENUE: Monbetsu City Sports Center LOCATION: Monbetsu, Japan The Nagoya Organization of Puroresu held their first ever show, a house show at the Monbetsu City Sports Center in Monbetsu, Japan. A crowd of 125 showed up, crowding in to see the new promotion, which hopes to make a mark on the Lucharesu scene.
At 5 dollars of American Currency, many felt they would get their money's worth, the action brought to them could be either the continuation or death of this newly founded company.
NOP President Kobe Koresawa came out to greet the crowd and took a place inside the crowd as the show began.
[NOP] OPENING MATCH: Shogun Mask (5'11, 184, Lucharesu)/Osaka Hiraguchi (6'2", 235, Technician) v. Sho Shoji (5'10", 187, Lucharesu)/"Rising Star" Fuyuki Hamada Jr. (5'10", 170, Lucharesu/Stiff Brawling )--Tech Force Mask and Hiraguchi came to the ring and shook hands with the fans at ringside, standing in their corner and politely bowing when the fans clapped....Tech-Force came out to a Techno Remix of 'Girls of Summer' by
LFO, they rudely ignored the fans and simentanously jumped over the top rope into the ring. Introductions were made and Mask and Hiraguchi both bowed while Tech-Force proceeded to make fun of them...Hamada and Mask started the match out.
Mask took the early advantage for his team by doing armdrag variations of all kinds, sending Hamada across the ring every time, finally after a back drop Hamada slid outside and Shoji jumped down from the apron, the two talked strategy as Mask did a head fake, Tech-Force slightly jumped but then pointed to their heads as a sign of their intelligence, right when Mask finished the head fake he dropped and Hiraguchi flew over the top rope and to the outside with a Space Flying Tiger Drop, sprawling the three men on the floor to a loud amount of clapping from the crowd.
[TIME: 3:20] After every man re-grouping Hamada went back into the ring, he tagged in
Shoji who ran in and was dropkicked in the knee for his pleasure by Mask.
Mask tagged in Hiraguchi and they worked Shoji over with various double team maneuvers. [TIME: 5:10] Hiraguchi was tagged in by Mask and took advantage of Shoji, throwing him into the ropes and giving him a drop-toe hold, moving up with a La Magistral for two and a half. Shoji quickly got up, trying to call a timeout but failing of course.
[TIME: 6:20] Hiraguchi threw Shoji into the ropes and Shoji came back with a sunset flip take over, the two proceeded to have a string of _TEN_ near falls, cumilating with both men rising to their feet to applause from the crowd.
[TIME: 8:45] Shoji now took advantage by poking Hiraguchi in the eyes and throwing him into his corner, Hamada grabbed the rope attached to their turnbuckle and began choking Hiraguchi much to the dismay of the crowd as Shoji distracted the ref, saying he had something in his eye.
[TIME: 9:30] After atleast 30 seconds of choke action, Hiraguchi stumbled disorientated towards Shoji who grabbed him and executed a falling brainbuster, Sho tagged in Hamada and both men proceeded to attack every point of Hiraguchi's body with blinding attacks.
[TIME: 10:10] The referee tried to get Hamada out of the ring but before he could, he lifted Hiraguchi up in a surfboard position and Shoji bounced off the ropes, nailing a tumbling senton backsplash on Osaka.
[TIME: 12:20] Clutching at his ribs Hiraguchi was whipped into the turnbuckle by Sho,
Shoji tried following up with a cross-body avalanche but Osaka moved out of the way and dove, tagging his partner in.
[TIME: 12:55] Mask came in like a house of fire, dropkicking Shoji directly in the face and taking him down, Hamada tried to come in but was dealt a dropkick also, sending him down, Tech Force regrouped and Mask grabbed them both in a front facelock, running up the ropes and nailing a _DOUBLE_ Tornado DDT!
[TIME: 14:10] Shoji rolled outside and now Hamada and Mask were left, Shogun went to the top rope and tried for a 450 splash but Hamada rolled out of the way, Mask got up in a daze and was thrown over by a head and arm suplex by Hamada.
Shogun got up again and fell victim to a single arm ddt by Hamada. [TIME: 16:35] Hamada tagged in Shoji and they threw Mask into the ropes, both ducked down for a back body drop but Shogun flipped over both and dropped with a seated dropkick to the back legs of Shoji and Hamada.
[TIME: 18:00] Mask bounced off the ropes and executed a flipping double elbowdrop. Shoji and Hamada came up and Shogun clotheslined them both over and to the floor.
Mask quickly tagged in Osaka and he came in, jumping over the top rope and onto both men with a plancha.
[TIME: 19:10] Osaka rolled Shoji back in the ring and picked him up, throwing him into the ropes, Shoji dropped down and hit Hiraguchi right in the mouth with an uppercut, Osaka fell backwards into the ropes and got a kick to the back of the head by Hamada. Hiraguchi fell forward and Sho bounced off the ropes, landing a back splash to the chest of Osaka. Shoji motioned Hamada into the ring and they hung Hiraguchi in a tree of woe on the turnbuckle. Shoji and
Hamada proceeded to kick Osaka in the head multiple times over and over then circled the ring, running and nailing Hiraguchi with a double dropkick to the face and mouth of Osaka. Shoji went for the pin as Hamada fought Mask off and got the three count.
END RESULT: Sho Shoji ( O - Double Tree of Woe Dropkick - X ) Osaka Hiraguchi TIME: 22:34 [Intermission] [NOP] SECOND MATCH: Chon Go (6'6", 235lbs, Muay Tai) v. "Blinding Storm" Tetsumi Nanami (6'4",
289lbs, Japanese Strong Style)
The second match of the night, seemed to have the fans on the edge of their seats. Chon Go, a man who is known for his Muay Tai kickboxing, drew boos from the fans as he made his way to the ring. Decked out in a hooded robe, he looked prepared for the night, but he wasn't prepared enough.
Out next was one of NOP's more heralded talents in "Blinding Storm" Tetsumi
Nanami. This seasoned star from Osaka, Japan, had the fans on their feet cheering for him. His long black robe covered him as he walked to the ring, a serious look on his face.
This match was to be seen as a stiff fight between the two, as quite a few times it looked like it would break out into a fist fight. Chon Go sported his Muay Tai style, while Nanami, gave the fans a look at his Japanese
Strong Style. The two men stared at each other, as the announcer gave their names to the crowd. Then the bell sounded.
Almost immidiatly, Chon Go raced toward Nanami trying to jab him with a rushing right punch, but connected with air. Nanami, having sidestepped this move grabbed hold of Go from behind, and hit a vicious looking Side Belly to
Belly Suplex, that put the fans into a frenzy.
[TIME: 1:04] Go slammed hard into the mat, confused as to how he had missed his punch. Nanami moved forward, but not fast enough as Go hit him with an elbow shot to the gut, and a knee to the chest. Chon Go then proceeded to attempt a running knee strike, but Nanami simply lariated him to the ground.
Picking him up, Nanami hit a Low Yakuza Kick that seemed to slow Chon Go down. Go fought to his feet, and nailed a stiff sidenick to the kidney area of Nanami. The crowd was then treated to a series of nearfalls, with Nanami showing more of his strong style hitting a Release German Suplex as well as a Northern Lights Suplex.
[TIME: 3:45] A missed straight right by Go, led to a near fall after a Tiger Suplex from
Nanami that the fans went crazy about. Chon Go then tried to hook on a
Juji-Gatame armbar, which Nanami powered out of, allowing a Dragon Suplex attempt, which Go blocked. Nanami in turn, hit a stiff looking Kawada Kick to the side of Go, then picked Go up for a brainbuster.
A quick pin by Nanami led to a very slow kick out by the Muay Tai expert,
Chon Go. [TIME: 5:01] The end saw Chon Go hit a rushing knee strike to the lip of Nanami, making it pour of blood. Nanami, obviously not pleased, ran at Go, and nailed a forearm smash to the side of his head, making him dizzy then shortly after hooking him into a Tiger Driver which Chon Go somehow kicked out of.
To make his point, Nanami pulled the now hopeless Go up to his feet spun him around, and hit his version of a Dangerous Back Drop Driver which he calls the 'Blinding Storm Driver' for the pinfall and the victory.
[TIME: 6:34] Nanami was very impressive in his NOP debut, giving the fans something to look forward too. President Kobe Koresawa. who was sitting ringside for this match, stood up clapping, supporting his newly signed talent. Chon Go got up after the pinfall, and looks angry as he kicked the turnbuckle off and left, as the crowd booed him relentlessly.
END RESULT: "Blinding Storm" Tetsumi Nanami ( O - Blinding Storm Driver - X ) Chon Go TIME: 6:34 [Intermission] [NOP] MATCH THREE: El Tigre (5'9", 180, Lucha Libra), El Gato Loco (5'9", 180, Lucha Libra),
Gordo Vaca (5'9", 240, Lazy-Lucha Libra) v. Angel Suicida (6'0", 208,
Lucharesu)/Britney Spears' Boyfriend (5'10", 210, Spot-Blowing Comedic Lucha
Libra)/Goshikku Mitsukai (5'9, 178, Lucharesu)
NMM came out together, all sprinting a little bit and sliding under the ropes into the ring . Suicida came down to the ring first, slapping the hands of the fans on the way to the ring, sliding underneath the ropes and shaking hands with his counterparts, then going to his corner. Next out was
Britney Spears' Boyfriend who's unorthodox appearance made the crowd laugh a bit as he tried to look serious, he went into the ring and into his corner, beside Suicida. Last entering was Goshikku Mitsukai, who walked coldly to the ring, sliding underneath the ropes and removing his trenchcoat and throwing it to ringside....
All men were introduced and Britney Spears' Boyfriend(BSB) started out for his team while El Tigre started for his.
Circling each other, BSB and Tigre locked up, Tigre took advantage and threw
BSB into the ropes, Spears' Boyfriend tried for an Asai Moonsault but slipped on the middle rope, falling down to the ring to a sizeable amount of laughs from the crowd. He got up and looked around, then turned right into a Tigre superkick. BSB fell down like a sack of potatoes and Tigre followed up quickly with a falling legdrop.
[TIME: 1:10] Tigre quickly ran over and tagged in Gato Loco who ran in, both men threw
BSB into the ropes and did a double shoulderblock, Loco tagged Gordo Vaca and he went to the top, jumping off with a big splash on BSB. Vaca rolled outside, breathing heavily from exerting just a little bit of energy and
Gato Loco went to work, kicking BSB in the head.
[TIME: 2:25] Loco tried one too many times for a kick to the head and BSB caught his foot, moving up and hitting Loco in the groin. Loco hunched over and BSB raked him in the eyes, then walked over, tagging in Suicida. Angel ran into the ring and jumped onto Loco's shoulders, spinning around and throwing him over with a rana, sending Loco out of the ring. Tigre ran in and Suicida back dropped him on arrival. Suicida ran up the turnbuckle and came off with a skytwisting moonsault onto Tigre, getting a nice reaction from the crowd. Angel went for the pin, getting a two and a half count.
[TIME: 4:45] Both men back up and Tigre snuck behind Suicida, who was trying a superkick and threw him up and down with a Blue Thunder Powerbomb, almost getting a three count. Tigre brought him to his feet and threw Suicida into the ropes, Angel bounces up and off with a handspring back elbow. Tigre rolled out and Vaca came in the ring, the man known as Fat Cow manhandled Suicida, bench pressing Suicida over the top rope and to the outside.
[TIME: 6:50] Mitsukai entered the ring and dropkicked Vaca in the legs, knocking him down. Gordo got up, breathing heavily and hobbled to his corner, tagging in
El Tigre. Tigre came in and Mitsukai drove him to the mat with a leaping
DDT. He followed that up quickly with a leaping elbow to the chest of
Tigre, going for the pin and almost getting a three count but Tigre kicked out at two and a half.
[TIME: 8:10] Mitsukai went back over, BSB wanted to tag in but Mitsukai seemed to ignore him and tag in Suicida. Angel jumped over the top rope and ran in, dropkicking Tigre right in the face, sending him back on the outside. Gato
Loco came back in and Angel hit him with a backspin ddt, quickly rising up and ascending the top rope, jumping off with a moonsault right onto Gato
Loco for the two and a half count.
[TIME: 9:50] Quickly, El Tigre ran back in as did Gordo Vaca, Mitsukai came in also and
BSB tried jumping over the ropes, but tripping and falling on his face.
Mitsukai kicked Gordo Vaca in the gut and bodyslammed him as Angel kicked
Tigre also in the midsection, locking him in a standing headscissors and lifting him up for a crucifix powerbomb. Gato Loco ran right into Mitsukai, who lifted him up in a Death Valley Driver position. BSB ascended to the top rope and in succession, BSB hit the 'No Strings Attached' Frog Splash
Elbow, Mitsukai dropped Gato Loco in the Rusted Root DVD into a piledriver and Angel dropped Tigre with the Cloud 9, sit-out crucifix powerbomb. The referee counted all three men at the same time.
[TIME: 11:30] After the match, BSB got up, celebrating and Mitsukai walked over, pushing him and screaming about his messed up spots. Suicida came over, trying to pull the two men apart when from the crowd jumped Christina Aguilera's
Boyfriend and Ricky Martin Mask, from behind they kicked Suicida and
Mitsukai with a double dropkick. Angel and Mitsukai fell to the ground and the group collectively known as the Pop Stars did a triple pelvic thrust to the heads of Mitsukai and Angel.
END RESULT: Britney Spears' Boyfriend/Angel Suicida/Goshikku Mitsukai ( O -
Triple Finishing Move - X ) New Mexicana Millenium
TIME: 11:30 [Intermission] [NOP] MAIN EVENT: The Beautiful Destruction: "Brattitude" Michelle Bailey (6'2", 204lbs, U.S. Junior Style)/"Sweet Dreams" Shane Destiny (6'2", 233lbs, Technician/Submission) v. Kourui Saionji (5'9 1/2", 204lbs, Lucharesu)/Murmur: The Fallen (6'2", 238 lbs, Ultra Stiff MMA/Technician) The first team to appear from backstage happened to be one of NOP's most talked about duos. The team dubbed The Beautiful Destruction, consisting of
Michelle Bailey and Shane Destiny, as often been called the future of NOP.
They showed why tonight. They walked out aruguing, foreshadowing the upcoming match. NOP fans showered Bailey with cheers, and groaned at the sight of Destiny.
The U.S. Junior Style Bailey was decked out in a light blue halter top that laced up on the sides and had the word "angel" across the front in white along with several clouds, a loose-fitting black miniskirt, black kneepads, and black knee-high lace-up boots that have three-inch platforms. Very classy indeed.
Her partner, the Submission artist/Technical specialist in Destiny saw him in black canvas pants that had visible white stitching, black wrestling boots, black electrical tape adorned his wrists, and a black bandana rested under his hair. Both members of the team looked prepared, receiving a mixed reaction from the crowd.
Their opponents were met with an onslaught of boos. Murmur walked out first, followed by Kourui Saionji. Both men appeared to enjoy the fans hating them as they moved toward the ring. These two were clearly the underdogs in this match to the fans, but they didn't perform like them.
Murmur wore black leather pants with his skull adorned elbow pads and his faced was nicely painted with skull looking features. This stiff style worker seemed to be a driving force in NOP. His partner, a lucha style wrestler, came out with a pair of long shiny metallic red tights with a matte black rose design on the rear with white boots and black kick pads. He also wore red and white fingerless gloves.
Bailey was set to start versus Murmur, but Destiny had other plans, pushing
Bailey out of the way, and naming himself as the starter in the match.
Bailey pouted before leaving the ring, and standing on the apron. Murmur shook his head, as he wanted a chance at the semi-male. Both men braced themselves as the bell rang.
Murmur and Destiny circled eachother, both waiting for the other to make a move, both being booed, till finally Destiny moved close enough for Murmur to grab his arm and lock it into a standing-chickenwing style submission.
Destiny countered by freeing his arm, and dropped Murmur to the mat with a vertical suplex.
Murmur thuded off the mat much to the delight of the fans, as Destiny waltzed over and pulled him up by the hair. A sidehead lock by Destiny led to a push to the ropes by Murmur who in turn flailed a lariat at Destiny which he in turn ducks, bouncing off the ropes, hitting a clothesline of his own.
[TIME: 2:23] He pulled Murmur up, and hit a quick snapsuplex, which turned into two more, as he held on to the move. Impressive work by the man dubbed "Sweet Dreams".
Murmur fought out of an early slingshot suplex attempt by Destiny, stunned him with a stiff kick to the side, and dropping him with a DDT which appeared to plant Destiny's head fairly hard into the mat.
A couple of near falls later, Destiny was looking to be in bad shape after suffering a stif kick combination by Murmur, in which two of the kicks collided with the side of his head. Bailey could be heard screaming to be tagged in, Destiny refusing to do so.
Murmur took advantage of a stunned Destiny and was able to climb up to the top and hit a top rope flying shoulder block, which in turn knocked Destiny to the mat. A pin to no avail followed as Destiny made it to his feet. A double arm DDT was countered by Destiny, as he somehow hit a desperation
Northern Lights Suplex which put Murmur to the ground for the first time in awhile.
[TIME: 5:03] Destiny needed a tag, but decided to ignore Bailey's pleas. A tag did occur however, when Murmur whipped Destiny into the ropes and collided hands with
Shane's as Shane held onto the ropes. She giggled with glee as she entered the ring but was soon gagging as she was dropped to the mat with a massive
Murmur leg lariat.
Her troubles continued as Destiny, obviously annoyed at the blind tag, started to argue with a prone Bailey as a fresh Kourui Saionji was tagged in. Saionji could only watch as Destiny threw Bailey to the mat with a Tiger
Suplex, one of his signature moves.
Saionji took advantage without a pin, picking the now damaged Bailey to her feet, and hitting a vicious looking Yakuza Kick to her side, then a beautiful looking brainbuster that made Bailey's head crumple to the mat.
Bailey kicked out of a lazy pin by Saionji. [TIME: 8:38] Bailey emerged to her feet, and nailed a spinning heel kick that stunned
Saionji, and then followed up with a stiff superkick to the jaw. She quickly covered as the fans went nuts, but Saionji was able to kickout after a slow two count.
The match looked to be over, as Bailey climbed to the top for what appeared to be her Moonsault press, but Saionji crotched her, which affected her greatly. He climbed then threw her off, and covered for a quick kickout.
Bailey seemed determined to go aerial as she dropped Saionji with a bulldog and climbed to the top rope, nailed a pretty body press on the now standing
Saionji which led to a close fall.
[TIME: 10:00] Saionji then tagged to Murmur who look anxious to be in the ring with Bailey. He shouldn't of been as he was slow moving to her side, and she, being quick, was able to drive him to the mat with her Swinging Clothesline, looking to end the match.
At the count of two and three-fourths, Saionji ran in and broke up the count, as the crowd booed relentlessly. Destiny appeared also distrought at his partner for not getting the job done, and yelled for a tag. He got one.
Destiny ducked a clothesline attempt from Murmur and locked on the Destiny
Strangle which appeared to be working on the now tired Murmur. But, Destiny gave up with the move after Murmur refused to tap out, and couldn't reach the ropes. He picked him up again by the hair again and gripped Murmur by the chest and dropped him over with a Backdrop Suplex.
[TIME: 12:01] Murmur attempted to get to his feet and did while Destiny argued again with
Michelle Bailey. He surprised Destiny with a rollup which accounted for a two count, which Destiny reversed into a small package which ended in a two count.
The ending saw Murmur attempt his 'Burnt Rose' Cradle Piledriver, but was in turn reversed by Destiny into a Griffin Suplex style grip which appeared to signal the end, but Murmur powered out and took the advantage with a stiff kick. Destiny woke up from a daze and caught Murmur with a German Suplex, which he bridged. Saionji tried to break it up but wasn't in time. However the outcome was different then most believed.
[TIME: 14:45] Bailey jumped up and down as she thought victory belonged to her, but NOP senior referee, Keijo Moyahshi, kept pointing at Destiny then at his shoulders .
Both mens shoulders had been on the mat, so he signaled a draw. Destiny got up and into the referees face. Bailey held him back, but he got out of control, and dropped her with a Cutthroat Suplex, much to the dislike of the fans.
Destiny left the ring pissed off with the outcome as Bailey tried to stand up. Murmur and Saionji shook hands and smiled. Their effort was acknowladged by the fans with some clapping which in turn led to boos as Murmur kicked a fallen Bailey. NOP fans see Murmur as a disgrace to the federation, if one could call him that.
After the wrestlers left, the fans gave them a standing ovation for their effort. END RESULT: "Sweet Dreams" Shane Destiny ( Double Pin Draw ) Murmur: The
Fallen TIME: 14:45 The crowd gave this match quite the ovation, even though some weren't happy with a draw, as they wanted Murmur and Saionji too lose, but in the end, both teams ended up settling for a hard fought draw.
Kobe Koresawa came out at the end of the show, to announce the next NOP
House Show's lineup on the 23rd at Yakayama Prefectural Gym. He bowed to them as he appreciated their precence.
He also annoucned that the first NOP television taping will take place on
October 27th at the Tokyo Differ Ariake. He encouraged all to attend. The fans left the building happy with NOP's first offering. They seemed to be impressed.
