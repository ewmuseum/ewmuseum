Nagoya Organization of Puroresu, House Show, #2, 10-23-01

October 24, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nagoya Organization of Puroresu HOUSE SHOW #2 -------------------------------------------------------- PROMOTION: Nagoya Organization of Puroresu EVENT: Second Show, Crystal Gamma Tour, 10/23/01 VENUE: Yakayama Prefectural Gym LOCATION: Yakayama, Japan The gym was packed, as the show seemed to draw a larger crowd than their inaugural show. Kobe Koresawa was said to be pleased at how the Crystal
Gamma Tour was going, and how the NOP was shaping up. He also announced that the new signees to NOP would be booked for matches following the Nagoya
Action TV taping, NOP's first.
The gong sounded, thus leading us into our first match. [NOP] OPENING MATCH: El Tigre (5'9", 180, Lucha Libra), El Gato Loco (5'9", 180, Lucha Libra),
Gordo Vaca (5'9", 240, Lazy-Lucha Libra) v. Britney Spears' Boyfriend
(5'10", 210, Spot-Blowing Comedic Lucha Libra)/Ricky Martin Mask (???,???,???)/Christina Aguilera's Boyfriend (???,???,???) Out first came the Team NOP Representitives, New Mexicana Millenium. The crowd seemed to give them a mixed reaction. It wasn't because some people liked them, it's because they pretty much felt sorry for them. The wrestlers didn't understand as they spoke random spanish expletives at people, as the fans couldn't help but laugh at these Mexico Natives. If these guys were willing to work, they might make it somewhere. But they don't seem to care about anything but a pay check.
The lights in the gym went out and a flood light was laid down on the ramp, as three shadowy figures approached the ramp. They stood there , the started running toward the ring. It was the Pop Stars and they had come out next.
The fans were unsure whether to treat this group as serious or not, but they laughed anyways. They climbed into the ring and they started to pelvic thrust everything in sight. This group of guys is really a sight to see.
The match saw Ricky Martin Mask take on El Tigre to start. El Tigre went on the offensive first dodging a dropkick attempt by RMM. He then landed a dropkick to the back of RMM's head, and started to parade around the ring.
His buddies in the corner told him to turn around, but it was too late. RMM nailed a standing Tornado DDT right off the bat, planting El Tigre into the mat for a two count.
TIME: 1:34 RMM let El Tigre get to his feet for some reason, he whipped Tigre to the ropes and attempted a second rope moonsault (we love you Hayabusa) which failed as he fell flat on his face. As he withered in pain, Britney Spears'
Boyfriend went to the top rope and jumped off nailing a beautiful cross body press which took El Tigre to the ground. He quickly slid out as the ref warned him.
While the ref was talking to BSB, Ricky Martin Mask poked El Tigre in the eyes, then started to dance around him in circles. The crowd was not pleased. He then proceeded in locking his arm over his head and another leg crossed over the other, as he threw El Tigre back on his head with a Russian
Leg Sweep. Quick cover for a two.
RMM then tagged out to BSB who ran in and ran up the ropes hitting a rope assisted Tornado DDT which was done with ease. He pulled him up and went for another, but his wig got in his face, and he crashed right into the turnbuckle, giving El Tigre time to recover. He then tagged out to El Gato
Loco.
TIME: 5:32 EGL immidiatly went after BSB who still had the wig over his head and nailed a Uraken that sent BSB to the mat, motionless. EGL covered to only a one count. He got mad and started going after the ref. The other NMM members complained as the ref was distracted. This allowed all three Pop Stars members to get in a triple team. They all took turns hitting bulldogs as they all hit one each, then the two illegal members scurried out as BSB was in position to strike.
EGL got up staggering, and this allowed BSB to nail a double springboard armdrag takedown. He posed for the crowd which brought heat, then did a standing moonsault pin. Gordo Vaca ran in and broke the count up at two.
TIME: 7:00 Christina Aguilera's Boyfriend demanded a tag, but BSB just stood there adjusting his wig, laying another pin down with a Surfer pose that got the crowd heated again. This was a one count to say the least. EGL got up, and was met with a Russian Leg sweep into a La majistral cradle which led to another two count.
TIME: 10:03 Gordo Vaca demanded a tag and got one from a dazy El Gato Loco. He ran right into a Rolling Elbow from BSB that looked rather stiff...Gordo got up looking pissed. BSB was waiting for him...thus began the Ali Shuffle which the fans booed endlessly. He began to dance around Vaca moving in and out, nailing a left jab, backing off, then repeating.
He then began to swing his right arm around like in a windmill motion, before suddenly doing a pelvic thrust and nailing Vaca with a headbutt. The wig was out of place, so he just fell over. Not knowing where he was he stood up, Gordo Vaca tried to grab him, he ducked under his arms, then nailed a quick Rocker Dropper for the pinfall.
END RESULT: Gordo Vaca ( O - BSB Ali Shuffle - X ) TIME: 11:23 Final Thoughts: Christina Aguilera's Boyfriend didn't even get into the match so we still don't know his style. Ricky Martin Mask looked impressive in his debut and Britney Spears' Boyfriend seems to be improving, a few blown spots, but not as many. These guys can sure piss off the fans though.
NMM was said to have been pissed at Gordo Vaca after the match. El Tigre could be seen pushing him which led to El Gato Loco breaking the two up.
Could this be the beginning to a fight? [INTERMISSION] [NOP] MATCH TWO: Angel Suicida(6'0", 208, Lucharesu)/Goshikku Mitsukai(5'9", 178, Lucharesu) v. "Rising Star" Fuyuki Hamada Jr.(5'9", 170, Lucharesu/Stiff Brawling hybrid)/Sho Shoji, 5'10", 187, Lucharesu) -- Tech Force Suicida and Mitsukai entered first, coming off their trios match win they seemed in high spirits, slapping the hands of the fans slightly before entering the ring.
Next out was Tech-Force, 'Summer Girls(Techno Remix)' by LFO played and they came out, very cockily dancing and ignoring the fans, they slid underneath the ropes, dancing some more and waiting for the introductions.
Both teams were introduced, the crowd seemed aligned with Mitsukai and
Suicida, booing Tech-Force, besides a few young girls in the audience.
Shoji and Suicida started out for their respective teams and went through a feeling out process. Sho tried sweeping Angel's legs out from under him but was inevitably kicked in the face for his pleasures, a sequence of one trying to get the advantage over the other then came until finally Angel took advantage with a dropkick to the chest of Shoji, sending him down to the mat.
TIME: 1:45 Shoji rose to his feet and threw Suicida up and over with a back body drop,
Angel landed like a cat on his feet on the outside, springing up quickly and catching the just turning around Shoji with a flying bodypress, knocking him down for a two count. The two then proceeded to have a slew of near falls, resulting in 7 2 and a half counts and one near three count for Suicida.
They both rose to their feet, getting a cordial set of clapping from the fans before tying up again.
TIME: 4:20 Shoji took advantage, throwing Suicida over with an armdrag takedown, Angel rose to his feet but was grabbed by Hamada in the corner, being choked by the tag team rope. Shoji distracted the referee as Hamada did the dirty work. Looking miffed about the entire thing, Mitsuaki dropped from his corner and went over, grabbing Hamada by the foot and pulling him down to the floor below.
TIME: 5:45 Suicida came stumbling out of the turnbuckle and Shoji tried throwing him up and over with a vertical suplex but Angel wriggled free, landing behind Sho and pushing him over the top rope and to the outside. Mitsukai and Hamada, previously brawling a bit stopped in their tracks as Shoji rose to his feet,
Suicida took advantage and bounced off the opposite ropes, coming back and jumping over the top rope with a Space Flying Tiger Drop onto all three men, causing a loud ovation from the crowd. After a while, Shoji and Suicida reentered the ring.
TIME: 8:45 Suicida threw Shoji into the ropes and Hamada tagged his partner on the back when he bounced off, the blind tag was not seen by Suicida who ducked down,
Shoji lined up and soccer kicked him right in the face, sending him down and
Hamada ascended to the top rope, jumping off with a high velocity frog splash and going for a pin, getting a two and a half count. Hamada picked
Angel up and threw him into his corner, taunting Mitsukai and telling him to come in, Mitsukai obliged and tagged himself in, stepping through the ropes to a nice reaction from the crowd.
TIME: 11:25 Mitsukai and Hamada then had a nice strike-fest, Mitsukai laying into Hamada with elbows and knees while Hamada came back with chops and kicks. Neither man backed down until finally Mitsukai nailed a roaring elbow, sending
Hamada to the ground in a heap. Mitsukai then took over, dropping a knee across the face of Hamada and implying many mat skills to wear down the body of the serious side of Tech-Force.
TIME: 14:20 Mitsukai rose Hamada to his feet and executed his deadly Rusted Root finisher of a Death Valley Driver into a piledriver, hitting it perfectly
Hamada looked down for the count. The referee went down and nearly got to three before Shoji leaped in and knocked Mitsukai off, saving his partner from inevitable defeat.
TIME: 16:45 Mitsukai chased Shoji off and was surprised from behind by Hamada with a schoolboy roll-up, getting a near three count. Mitsukai looked angry and bashed Hamada right in the face with an elbowshot then threw him outside,
Shoji tried coming from behind but Mitsukai caught him and nailed him with a
Michinoku Driver #2. Mitsukai proceeded to tag Suicida in and lifted Shoji on his shoulders. Suicida jumped up top and executed a flipping hurriacanrana off of Mitsukai's shoulders, falling down and pinning Shoji for the three count to a nice reaction from the crowd.
END RESULT: Angel Suicida ( O - Super Assisted Dragon Rana - X ) Sho Shoji TIME: 18:35 Final Thoughts: Suicida showed his high risk attack philosophy, getting the pin on Shoji. Suicida and Mitsukai seem to work well as a team, their paradox in styles, Mitsukai's seemingly stiff-striking, high impact offense combined with Suicida's flying ability should prove to be a formidable force in NOP.
Tech Force on the other hand seem to be hit or miss, they work well at sometimes, then have miscommunications at others, both of these young men have talent and with time, they should become a great team.
[INTERMISSION] [NOP] THIRD MATCH: The Beautiful Destruction: "Brattitude" Michelle Bailey (6'2", 204lbs, U.S. Junior Style)/"Sweet Dreams" Shane Destiny (6'2", 233lbs, Technician/Submission) v. Kourui Saionji (5'9 1/2", 204lbs, Lucharesu)/Murmur: The Fallen (6'2", 238 lbs, Ultra Stiff MMA/Technician) Straight from the back headed Murmur who was booed out of the gym by these fans. Murmur wore black leather pants with his skull adorned elbow pads and his faced was nicely painted with skull looking features, the same as last show. His partner, came out to cheers. Kourui Saionji acknowladged them by bowing, but Murmur looked back and glared at him, as Saionji followed. He too was wearing the same outfit from last show, a pair of long shiny metallic red tights with a matte black rose design on the rear with white boots and black kick pads. He also wore red and white fingerless gloves.
Out next came The Beautiful Destruction. Already they were arguing Destiny slamming his fist into his palm, almost pulling his hair out. Bailey just skipped out, mumbling something under her breath.
The U.S. Junior Style Bailey was decked out in a light blue halter top that laced up on the sides and had the word "angel" across the front in white along with several clouds, a loose-fitting black miniskirt, black kneepads, and black knee-high lace-up boots that have three-inch platforms. Very classy indeed.
Her partner, the Submission artist/Technical specialist in Destiny saw him in black canvas pants that had visible white stitching, black wrestling boots, black electrical tape adorned his wrists, and a black bandana rested under his hair. Same as the 21st. Bailey slapped Destiny, and he finally gave in.
Bailey and Murmur started the match out, tying up, this struggle for strength quickly descended into a strike fest as Murmur kicked Bailey in the shin over and over, doubling the confused persona over.
Murmur bounced off the ropes and came up with a boot to the head, knocking
Bailey down. Murmur picked Michelle up and quickly threw her off the ropes, she came back and was thrown down with a powerslam, getting a two and a half
count. Bailey and Murmur both rose to their feet, Bailey a bit stunned as Murmur tried for a takedown, Bailey sidestepped and locked him in a front facelock, dropping back with a guillitone choke.
TIME: 2:23 Murmur broke out as Bailey was no match for his power. Murmur swung with a right hand but missed and Bailey countered with a whip to the ropes. Murmur bounced off the ropes, taking Bailey down with a kick to the head and going for a pin, getting a near fall of two and a half again. Bringing Michelle to her feet again, Murmur was surprised by a punch to the gut, doubling him over. Bailey grabbed him in a standing headscissors, lifting and dropping with a piledriver, snapping Murmur's neck against the mat.
In a daze, Murmur rose up and Bailey bounced off the ropes, taking him down with a satellite headscissors, then going for a pin, getting a two and a half count. Bringing Murmur up to his feet, Michelle stepped back, going for a superkick but Murmur ducked underneath, grabbing her and throwing her over with a capture suplex, dumping her right on her head.
Bailey crumpled down and began crawling, towards his corner and Shane Destiny. Murmur just smirked a bit, a sick grin on his face as the fans booed, before she could jump towards Destiny Murmur kicked her directly in the face, sending her back against the ropes.
TIME: 5:33 Picking her up to her feet again, Murmur threw her off the ropes, bouncing off the other side as she came back, Murmur jumped for a flying clothesline that was ducked underneath by Bailey, sending Murmur across the mat hard.
Both men were down as the fans began cheering on Bailey, even if they didn't like Destiny, they liked Bailey, they wanted her to succeed. Something about her was just 'it'.
TIME: 6:45 Murmur lariated a prone Bailey, as he/she began crawling again, as did
Murmur, diving and tagging in Destiny who ran in, grabbing Murmur by the foot and dragging him to the middle of the ring before laying into him with kick after kick to the midsection. Murmur overpowered these stiff kicks from
"Sweet Dreams" and made the tag to Saionji. They wanted him to kick
Destiny's ass. But as Saionji ran toward Destiny, he was kicked in the gut and sent for a Griffin Suplex, which seemed to dig Saionji into a hole.
A cover by Destiny was broken up. It would of been over had it not been. Saionj slowly got to his feet and started to gain the uppder hand by nailing a series of knife-edge chops, followed by Yakuza kick to the side of Destiny which softened him up for the finishing move of the combination, a Liger
Bomb that only offered a two count.
TIME: 8:34 Saionj pulled him up and attempted a tombstone, but Destiny was able to make his way out of hit and hit a quick lariat. Shane Destiny picked up the prone
Saionj and took him over with a Dragon Suplex, that almost got Saionj thrown across the entire ring. Impressive.
But this didn't stop the young Puroresu junior. He fought back. He hit a nasty looking stalling brainbuster for a close three fall, then hit a Cradle
Suplex Driver that stunned Destiny. But not for long as Destiny tagged out to Bailey who in turn ran in and landed a Superkick to the jaw of Saionj who in turn fell to mat. Murmur was looking for a tag, but got none.
TIME: 10:45 The end saw Murmur tagged in which in turn led to the tagging in of Destiny, who got nailed with a Fallen Melody, and went out like a light. But Murmur couldn't pull off the win as Bailey stiff kicked him in the head while the pin was applied. Murmur, pissed off about not picking up the win after his finishing move, he tagged in Saionj who fell to a stiff looking Tiger Suplex which led to Destiny picking him up, and nailed the Griffin Suplex for the win.
END RESULT: Shane Destiny ( O - Griffin Suplex - X ) Murmur: The Fallen TIME: 12:50 Final Thoughts: Outstanding match by both of these teams. They seem to be the cream of the crop here in NOP.
[INTERMISSION] [NOP] MAIN EVENT: "Kekkou Mata" Mahito Yakuma(5'10", 209, Dardevil/Lucharesu)/Rynosuke Uraki(6'2", 255, Martial Arts) v. "Blinding Storm" Tetsumi Nanami(6'4", 289,
Japanese Strong Style)/Ryouseika (6'0, 241, Technical/Brawling)
Entering first was Yakuma and Uraki, getting a nice heel reaction from the crowd, Yakuma's odd styles of always pointing to his crotch and cussing in
Japanese seemed to not hit kindly to the fans in attendance, Uraki seemed to walk with confidence, not looking around or at Yakuma, they entered the ring and awaited their opponents.
Nanami and Ryouseika came to the ring, walking slowly and entered without much fan fare. Then the introductions came, the only person not to bow was
Yakuma, who just did a half crotch chop and flipped the crowd off. The match started off and Yakuma and Nanami drew their respective teams initial beginning.
Nanami quickly over powered Yakuma, throwing him on his head with a head and arm suplex and an exploder. Yakuma got up, in a daze and ran blindly at
Nanami, who made him pay by throwing him hard, chest first into the turnbuckle. Yakuma stumbled backwards and Nanami tried for a release German
Suplex but Yakuma landed on his feet, right when Nanami turned around he was met with a kick to the groin by Yakuma, sending him down to his knees.
Yakuma taunted him a bit then bounced off the ropes, nailing a front dropkick directly to the face of Nanami, crumpling him over to the mat.
TIME: 1:30 Yakuma ran to the top rope and Uraki tagged himself in, Yakuma jumped off and nailed a picture perfect legdrop from the top rope, getting an actual round of applause. He stood up, flipping off the crowd and going outside as
Uraki stalked in. Uraki then took control, kicking Nanami relentlessly in the face, then in the ribs, then in the legs over and over and over. Nanami rose up and came back with a few elbow shots, dazing Uraki a bit, Uraki bounced off the ropes from the impact and came back with a huge lariatho, sending Nanami to the mat.
TIME: 3:45 Picking Nanami up, Uraki threw him into the ropes but Nanami reversed, Uraki came back and was met with a very stiff big boot to the face, sending him down in a heap. Nanami walked over and tagged in Ryouseika who walked in, touching at the face of Uraki before rising him to his feet and slapping him repeatedly. Uraki looked miffed as he came out of the corner, trying for another laratho that was ducked underneath by Ryouseika who grabbed him and threw him over with a German Suplex for a near three count.
TIME: 5:45 Uraki stood up in a daze and went to his corner, Yakuma tagged himself in and came in, drop-kicking Ryouseika in the face, only dazing the tubby man who bounced off the ropes and came back with a hard elbow shot to the face of Yakuma. Yakuma didn't take this too kindly as he bounced off the other side, nailing a spinning heel kick to the face of Ryouseika, sending him to one knee. Yakuma got up again and kicked Ryouseika in the face not once, not twice, but three times, one to the left side, one to the right, and one directly in the center. Ryouseika fell over and Yakuma went for a pin, getting a two and three-fourths as the crowd became excited.
TIME: 7:50 Yakuma picked Ryouseika up and threw him into the ropes, Ryouseika came back and hit an avalanche-style chin crusher on Yakuma, sending him down to the mat. Ryouseika picked him right back up and hit an elevated facebuster, busting the youngster open from the lip. Picking him up again, Ryouseika sent him into the ropes, as soon as Yakuma came back he lifted him up and down with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker to a nice reaction from the crowd.
Picking him up again, Ryouseika lifted and nailed a High Angle Powerbomb, crumbling the youngster up like an accordion and going for the pin. Knowing the match was probably over, Uraki entered and knocked Ryouseika off of his partner with a kick to the head.
TIME: 11:45 Uraki drug his partner over and tagged himself in, walking in and kicking
Ryouseika over and over in the ribs, sending him back and into his corner,
Nanami tagged himself in, stalking towards Uraki.
TIME: 12:30 Standing face to face the two large men then went into a striking frenzy,
Nanami hitting elbows and knee lifts and Uraki coming back with stiff kicks to the legs and rib area. Nanami fell backwards and came back with another roaring elbow, a move he hit earlier but Uraki ducked underneath and nailed a Double Inside Crescent Kick, sending Nanami down to the mat. Uraki went for a pin, getting a two and a half count before Nanami kicked out.
TIME: 14:45 Both men were on their feet, Nanami looking tired and broken down and Uraki tried finishing him off with a roundhouse kick but Nanami ducked underneath and threw him over with a release german suplex, right on his neck, crumpling Uraki down. Both men collapsed and the referee checked on both competitors.
TIME: 16:35 Nanami was the first to his feet and lifted Uraki up, locking on a double chickenwing, he lifted up for a tiger suplex but Uraki put his weight down, and reversed with a back drop. Both men crawled up and towards their corners, diving and tagging in their partners.
TIME: 18:20 Ryouseika and Yakuma came in and Yakuma came right in with a Yakuza Kick, knocking Ryouseika to the mat. Yakuma jumped up and off the ropes and landed with an Asai Moonsault. A two and a half count followed and both men rose to their feet, Ryouseika threw Yakuma into the ropes and ducked his head, Yakuma jumped over and tried with a sunset flip, they both proceeded to have a set of near falls, exciting the crowd.
TIME: 21:23 Both men rose up again and Yakuma missed a roundhouse kick, Ryouseika grabbed Yakuma from behind and lifted, nailing a Blue Thunder Powerbomb right into a pin. Ryouseika then proceeded his unorthodox style of rubbing the face of his opponent as he had the. Right before the three count, Uraki entered and nailed a heavy thrust-kick to the face of Ryouseika, busting his forehead open and sending him rolling back. Nanami came inside the ring and clotheslined Uraki over to the outside, both men began brawling as Yakuma came to. Seeing his opportunity he ran up the ropes and nailed a moonsault, getting the three count.
TIME: 25:00 END RESULT: "Kekkou Mata" Mahito Yakuma ( O - Moonsault Pin - X ) Ryouseika TIME: 25:00 Final Thoughts: All four men looked good in the ring, especially Uraki and
Nanami, who seem to already have begun the foundation of the heavyweight division. Yakuma held his weight with the heavies and came out with the win, showing his vast array of styles and his uncanny ability. Although
Ryouseika is unorthodox with his wrestling, he almost had the win, but fell just a little short. Great competition thrown out by all four men.
Kobe Koresawa, NOP President, came out to thank the fans afterwards, and told them to come in advance for ticket sales for NOP's next house show, being tomorrow, in the Miyazaki City Gymnasium, Japan. He bowed and left, as the crowd gave a loud ovation for the wrestlers and everyone involved.
