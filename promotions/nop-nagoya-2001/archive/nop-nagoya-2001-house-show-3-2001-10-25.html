House Show, #3, 10/24/01

October 25, 2001, 12:00:00 AM

Nagoya Organization of Puroresu HOUSE SHOW #3 -------------------------------------------------------- PROMOTION:  Nagoya Organization of Puroresu EVENT:  Third Show, Crystal Gamma Tour, 10/24/01 VENUE:  Miyazaki City Gymnasium LOCATION: Miyazaki, Japan Instead of NOP President Kobe Koresawa coming to the ring, NOP Head Booker
Keiji Kurasaki came out to ringside to a small ovation as he was to scout talent for the upcoming shows after the television taping. It is said that many newcomers will be on their way, as a bigger roster is building within the NOP.
The gong sounded, thus leading us into our first match. [NOP] OPENING MATCH: Kourui Saionji (5'9 1/2", 204, Lucharesu)/"Brattitude" Michelle Bail ey(6'2", 204, U.S. Standard Junior) v. Murmur: The Fallen (6'2", 238,Ultra
Stiff MMA/Technician)/"Sweet Dreams" Shane Destiny (6'2", 233,
Technical/Submission) Saionji and Bailey entered first to a nice reaction from the crowd, lacking their usual tag partners they seemed a little uneasy with each other but
Bailey cheerfully skipped to the ring while Kourui followed behind, looking focused.  They both entered the ring at the same time, waiting in their respective corner.
Next out was Murmur and Destiny, who seemed at each other's throat from the get-go, Destiny walked quickly towards the ring, wanting to get as far away from Murmur as he could while the one known as the Fallen followed very slowly behind.  Finally reaching the ring, Murmur slid under the bottom rope, locking eyes with Destiny and looking generally pissed off.  Destiny stood in one corner and Murmur the other, staring across at each other.
The introductions were made and naturally, Murmur and Destiny were booed and
Saionji and Bailey were cheered.  A quiet discussion in Kourui and
Michelle's corner resulted in Bailey starting, Saionji respectfully bowed to
Bailey, who giggled and did the same, starting off for her team.  The other side was not near as friendly as Murmur and Destiny just stared at each other, finally, Murmur stepped out, never taking his glare from Shane and we had the start of the Beautiful Destruction against one another.
Bailey offered her hand(her wrist looking limp, of course, almost in a way of Destiny should kiss it) to her partner, but now opposition and Shane slapped it up, into her face.
This seemed to set Michelle off as she quickly dropped Destiny to the mat with a drop-kick to the knee, taking Shane to the mat.  Destiny fell down flat and Michelle bounced off the ropes, flipping forward with a back splash on Shane for a two and a half count.  Destiny got up, staring a hole through
Bailey who just giggled a bit.
TIME:  1:20 Bailey and Destiny locked up and Shane showed his superior power, pushing
Michelle into the corner.  The referee tried for a clean break and it seemed as if Destiny would give it to Michelle before coming in with a hard shoulderblock to the stomach of Bailey.  Shane tried for another but
Michelle flipped over and locked on a sunset flip pin.  A two and a half count followed and they continued to reverse the other's pinning combination for atleast 45 seconds before finally rising up to their feet, inciting a loud reaction from the crowd.
TIME:  4:23 Michelle backed up a bit and tagged in Saionji and Destiny did the same, tagging in Murmur.  Although these two don't have the long standing relationship of Bailey and Destiny, they have been teaming together. 
Murmur, although a heavyweight is smaller than most of the heavies in NOP and showed it here against Saionji.  They started out in a strike war, which
Murmur inevitably won, showing his superior MMA style, using stiff kicks to bring Saionji to his knees.
TIME:  6:35 Murmur bounced off the ropes and delivered a stiff thrust kick to the face of Saionji, busting him open and crumpling him to the mat.  Murmur bounced off the ropes, leaping high in the air for a legdrop but Saionji moved out of the way, sending Murmur to bust ass onto the mat.  Saionji quickly got up, hitting a succession of moves including a back senton splash, a quick elbowdrop, and a snap legdrop, after the set of moves he went for the pin, getting a near three count.
TIME:  8:30 Murmur rose to his feet, locking Saionji from behind and nailing a pumphandle fisherman buster.  Quickly rising up, Murmur went to the top and jumped off with a huge Jumbo Tsuruta-esque knee from the top rope, nearly crushing Saionji's chest with the impact.  Quickly scurrying over, Murmur got a near three count but Saionji kicked out to a large face pop from the crowd.
TIME:  10:05 Picking Saionji up, Murmur threw him into the ropes, ducking underneath a lariat on the rebound, Saionji proceeded to nail a backspin ddt, planting
Murmur into the mat.  Both men were lying on their backs for a while as the crowd stomped, waiting for one man to get to their corner, Saionji began moving first, crawling towards his corner, Murmur also moved, looking up at
Destiny with a glare, not even looking like he was interested in tagging him in.  Saionji dove and tagged Bailey in who ran in, nailing a Superkick on
Murmur, sending him tumbling in the ring, Destiny reached through the rope and tagged himself in, enciting a heel pop from the crowd.
TIME:  13:55 Bailey and Destiny once again met in the ring, Destiny took control at first, dropping Bailey with many suplexes, most noteably an Exploder and a strait-jacket suplex.  Bailey came back with quick strikes and high impact flying maneuvers, a moonsault, and a missed Brattitude Adjustment.
TIME:  16:32 Bailey threw Destiny into his corner and Shane rose to his feet, Murmur grabbed him from behind by his bandana, throwing him to the mat, Destiny rose and turned around, screaming at Murmur, he turned around _RIGHT_ into a
Britney Spear by Bailey which led to the finishing three count.
After the match, Destiny got up, clutching at his ribs and threw the grinning Murmur into the ring, kicking him, Bailey tried pulling Destiny off and got a Griffin Suplex for his troubles, Saionji ran in and was also thrown down with a Griffin Suplex and was planted beside Murmur and Bailey,
Destiny walked out by himself to a sizeable heel pop as the referees checked on the other three.
'Brattitude' Michelle Bailey ( O - Britney Spear - X ) 'Sweet Dreams' Shane
Destiny FINAL TIME:  20:21 Final Thoughts:  Everyone looked impressive in the match, especially Bailey who pulled out the win.  The tension between Destiny and both Murmur and
Bailey could be cut through with a knife.  Is it just growing pains between
Destiny and Bailey or is real trouble on the horizon?  We shall see.
[INTERMISSION] [NOP] MATCH TWO: "Kekkou Mata" Mahito Yakuma (5'10", 209, Daredevil/Lucharesu)/Sho Shoji
(5'10",187, Lucharesu)/"Rising Star" Fuyuki Hamada Jr. (5'9", 170,
Lucharesu/Stiff Brawling) v.Britney Spears' Boyfriend (5'10", 210, Comedic
Luchadore)/Christina Aguilera's Boyfriend (???, ???, ???)/Ricky Martin Mask
(???, ???, ???)
First out was Tech-Force and Yakuma, dancing a bit, Yakuma not as much but mostly making lewd points to his crotch as Tech-Force danced around to the
Techno Remix of 'Summer Girls' by LFO.  The fans booed all three of them rabidly as they got into the ring, taunting the audience.
Next out was the Pop Stars, who look, well, goofy to say the least, they were also booed as they slid into the ring, in their corner, awaiting the action.
Both teams were introduced, both getting a less than nice reaction from the crowd and the people to start were chosen.  Yakuma started out for his team as Ricky Martin Mask did the same for his team.
RMM dropkicked Yakuma in the knees quickly and bounced off the ropes, trying for an Asai Moonsault but botching it badly, falling on his head, to which the crowd laughed a bit.  Yakuma grabbed RMM and threw him into the ropes, nailing a kick to the stomach of RMM, Yakuma grabbed him in a standing headscissors, lifting him up and driving him down with a piledriver, flipping over for a pin, getting a two and a half count.
TIME:  1:45 Yakuma lifted RMM to his feet and threw him into the ropes again, Martin
Mask jumped to the top rope without falling(!) he turned around but slipped, crotching himself on the top rope, Yakuma bounced off the opposite side, running and knocking him off the top with a spinning heel kick, sending both men tumbling over the top rope and to the outside.  Christina Aguilera's
Boyfriend(CAB) and Shoji entered the ring.
TIME:  4:10 CAB and Shoji proceeded to trade armdrag takedowns, Japanese armdrag takedowns, _AND_ Mexican armdrag takedowns to the delight of the crowd. 
Both men got up, staring the other down before beginning once again, CAB threw Shoji over with a hurricanrana, Shoji popped right back up with a dropkick to the face, sending CAB into the ropes, Shoji bounced off the opposite side and tried cross body-blocking CAB over the top rope but
Aguilera's Boyfriend side-stepped, sending Shoji through the ropes.  Quickly, CAB jumped up to the top rope, almost slipping but dove onto him with a flipping body splash.
TIME:  7:45 Coming into the ring to replace the two going to the outside where Hamada and BSB, they tied up and Hamada grabbed him in a side headlock, BSB quickly threw him into the ropes, and Hamada ducked over a BSB drop-down, BSB quickly got to his feet, stopping Hamada in his tracks by doing a series of cartwheels around the ring, finishing it off with a back flip to a pop from the crowd, the move accomplished nothing, but the fans liked it, and that's all that matters, don't you think?
TIME:  9:34 Both men stood toe-to-toe, Hamada grabbed BSB and threw him over with a hiptoss, which BSB reversed by landing on his feet, he stepped on the foot of Hamada, bouncing off the ropes, and coming up with a dropkick to the side of the head, sending Hamada to the mat with a thud.  Quickly, BSB tried for a skytwisting moonsault but Hamada moved, making BSB crash to the mat.
TIME:  11:05 Hamada picked BSB up and threw him towards the ropes, Shoji of Tech-Force pulled down the top rope on the outside, sending BSB over the top.  Yakuma and Shoji jumped down, trying to beat BSB down before CAB and RMM came over, evening up the odds.  The five men brawled on the outside a bit, in a group before Hamada bounced off the ropes, cameras flashed as he jumped over with a Space Flying Tiger Drop on all five of them.
TIME:  13:25 A long stall session ended up occurying as the obligatory train wreck on the outside was finally cleared, in the end, Yakuma and BSB where the ones in the ring.  BSB poked Yakuma in the eyes, doubling him over and clutching at his eyes.  Yakuma came back with a blind low blow, doubling BSB over and both men were clutching at their respective body parts.  Hamada and Shoji ran in, going towards BSB but on the other side CAB and RMM came in for their team.
TIME:  16:45 Hamada and Shoji paired off with CAB and RMM, respectively, brawling to the outside.  Yakuma locked BSB up and picked him up in a Death Valley Driver position, bringing him up and dropping him with a brainbuster, completing his finisher the Divide Driver.  After a few seconds, Yakuma draped his arm over, getting the three count.
After the match, Yakuma rose to his feet in victory,  kicking at BSB while the other four competitors brawled on the outside, BSB blocked one of
Yakuma's incoming kicks and rose to his feet, giving him a hiptoss, surpisingly to the crowd's delight.
END RESULT: "Kekkou Mata" Mahito Yakuma ( O - Divide Driver - X ) Britney
Spears' Boyfriend
FINAL TIME:  19:30 Final Thoughts:  Although they looked very sloppy in the ring, the Pop Stars have charisma oozing off of them, the fans are enthralled with their matches.  Yakuma seems to be coming into his own, pretty much outclassing and outshining everyone from a workrate standpoint in this match, he seems very talented, although very cocky.
[INTERMISSION] [NOP] MATCH THREE: "Blinding Storm" Tetsumi Nanami (6'4", 289, Japanese Strong Style) [vs] Chon Go (6'6", 235, Muay Thai Kickboxing) Coming out first was Chon Go, stalking to the ring to a sizeable round of boos.  He walked to the ring, climbed up the apron and wiped his feet on the outside, stepping through the ropes and to his corner, in fighting stance.
Next was Nanami, who walked also slowly, climbing up the apron and into the ring, staring at Chon Go as he entered his corner.
Both men were introduced but never took their eyes off the other, not even paying attention to the crowd and with that, the bell rang, and it was on.
To say this match was stiff would be an understatement, these guys were out to hurt each other, plain and simple.  Chon Go proceeded to lay into Nanami with strikes for atleast two minutes, nearly beating him unconscious, blood already ran from the face of Nanami as he lied in the corner, trying to guard himself from the onslaught.  The referee finally pulled Go back and
Nanami pulled himself up.
TIME:  2:45 Nanami pulled himself up and seemed enraged by the blood pouring from his face, grabbing Go quickly and throwing him over with a release german suplex, going for the pin and getting a near three count.  He quickly brought Go to his feet, unleashing with knee strikes to the face, busting
Chon's nose up, breaking it and sending blood down his chin and chest.
TIME:  4:20 Nanami picked him up quickly, grabbing him on his shoulders and dropping him with a Death Valley Driver, splattering blood on the mat.  The fans cringed at the brutalness of this contest as Nanami wouldn't stop the assault, picking Go up and throwing him towards the ropes, Chon stopped in his tracks, falling to one knee as Nanami tried coming back over, Go quickly came up with a forearm uppercut, making the blood pour out more from the cut of Nanami.
TIME:  6:45 Go went back on the offensive, catching Nanami with a roundhouse kick to the face, crumpling Tetsumi to the mat.  Go tried hitting some more knee strikes but Nanami blocked, pushing him back and into the ropes, and spinning him around on the way back, grabbing him from behind and dropping him hard with his Blinding Storm Driver, Dangerous Backdrop Driver for the pin and the win.
After the match, Nanami rose up to a nice reaction from the crowd, he looked around as they applauded him then stopped as a deafenying silence fell over.
  Turning around he saw Chon Go right in front of him, blood dripping down his face before letting out a primal scream and tackling him to the ground, pounding on him with mounted punches before the referees and officials finally took him off.
END RESULT: "Blinding Storm" Tetsumi Nanami ( O - Blinding Storm Driver - X ) Chon Go FINAL TIME:  9:15 Final Thoughts:  These men took it to each other from the get go, both men battered and beaten after the final bell.  This feud has not ended, in no way, shape or form, although the loser again, Chon Go may have gotten the moral victory, laying into Nanami after the match.
[INTERMISSION] [NOP] MAIN EVENT: Angel Suicida (6'0", 208lbs, Lucharesu )/Goshikku Mitsukai (5'9", 178lbs,
Lucharesu ) v. Rynosuke Uraki (6'2", 255lbs, Martial Arts)/Ryouseika (6'0",
241lbs, Technical/Brawling
The match was thought to be unfair, with Keiji Kurasaki booking what seemed to be a lopsided match. The Junior heavyweights were placed on one side of the ring, while the heavyweights were place on the other.
Also, given the relationship between Suicida and Mistukai, this booking was pondered, but the product turned out better than expected. Thus far, these two Juniors have worked well together as have the other two, so this Main
Event actually looked like a great booking job on Kurasaki's part.
Out first came the main hailing from Japan and Mexico, one of the NOP's top
Lucharesu talents, Angel Suicida. He came out decked in a bulky, baggy, sleeveless white robe. The back had a black cross with a halo over it in black. The crowd gave this sucidal product a loud ovation, and he bowed as he got into the ring, much to the delight of the fans.
The cheers suddenly turned to boos, as another Lucharesu prospect Goshikku
Mitsukai made his way to the ring. The fans seem to be unsure of what his motives are, but they do know he is willing to take a risk...but his attitude isn't getting him anywhere. He wore a nicely designed trenchcoat, along with black sunglasses covering his piercing eyes up.
Next out came their opponents, walking stride for stride. Ryoseika has lost some close falls as of late, but he appears to be getting into the swing of things. He obviously wore his mask which covered his face along with his black robe. He bowed to each fan that yelled his name.
His partner, a very promising heavyweight followed him. Ryunosuke Uraki, the
Martial Artist in NOP has made quite an impact on the Crystal Gamma Tour.
NOP is expecting big things out of this big guy. He wore his traditional red
Silk kimono with the Japanese Flag on the back, along with the name "URAKI".
The fans love both of these guys already, and they let them know with a nice ovation. The cheers soon turned to anticipation, as Uraki and Suicida were going preparing to begin in their corners as the bell rang. Suicida immidiatly cartwheeled in Uraki's direction and popped his jaw with a springboard elbow. Very impressive/intelligent start for the Junior.
Uraki was stunned by this quick and agressive move and soon found himself falling victim to a Reverse Russian Leg sweep in which Suicida made sure the back of Uraki's head slammed against the mat. Angel then made his way to the top.
TIME: 00:31 The crowd gasped as he went for a corkscrew moonsault early, but Uraki rolled out of the way, and pulled up the prone Suicida to take the advantage. He quickly hit a stalling vertical suplex, and held on, using his brute strength to hit a snap suplex on Suicida.
Uraki got the fans excited as he climbed to the top rope, and he, being the heavyweight, actually pulled off the top rope move, landing down on Angel with a beautiful Shooting Star Press that Suicida kicked out of.
TIME: 1:34 Uraki pulled him up and nailed a spinning back fist, ran off the ropes, attempted a lariat that was ducked by Suicida, Uraki came back and Suicida dropped him with a mexican leg sweep followed by a elevated surfboard into a dragon sleeper, a beautiful ground game/move that the fans applauded.
After fighting for a while, Uraki somehow reached the ropes, out of gas. Both men were now on their feet, and Suicida quickly went back to the ground, this time with a victory roll, trying to get the quick three, but only a two. Uraki looked winded, but he fought back, hitting a Rolling Elbow that drove Suicida back, then hitting a stiff looking Yakuza kick to the
_face_ of Suicida, that bloodied his lip.
TIME: 3:00 The fans loved the move, but didn't like the blood. Uraki, getting tired, tagged out to Ryoseika. Suicida jumped to his feet but ran right into a arm drag, got back up, right into an arm drag from the big man, then, got up, expecting a arm drag, but he ran straight into a nasty looking belly to belly suplex that made Suicida bounce off the mat.
Ryoseika stood Suicida up, and started quick kicking his knees, then he starting striking his throat in a nicely done combination.
He tried to finish Suicida off with a powerbomb, but Suicida got loose, nailed a stiff looking European uppercut that put the big man down, and ran up the ropes and landed a Guillotine leg drop that seemed to be the end, but at two Ryoseika kicked out.
TIME: 5:03 Suicida tired, tagged in his partner, Goshikku Mitsukai. Mitsukai took over where Angel left off pulling up the dazed Ryoseika and nailed a spinning heel kick right into a elevated camel clutch in which Ryoseika powered out of almost immidiatly.
Mitsukai attempted a Tiger Driver, but it failed and Ryoseika wouldn't go for being lifted up and got out and hit Mitsukai with kick to the head followed by a nice looking fist drop that made Mitsukai cringe in pain at the chest. He picked up Mitsukai, pretended to whip him off the rope, but instead threw him right back into a powerful lariat. A cover for a two count ensued.
TIME: 7:03 Ryoseika missed another lariat attempt and Mitsukai was able to land a jumping knee lift to the face of Ryoseika that seemed to mess up his mask.
Ryoseika looked pissed off as he suddenly grabed at Mitsukai, and leveled him with three consecutive palm strikes. The rarely angered Ryoseika tagged out to Uraki in disgust.
Uraki came in and took over completely as he almost had a three count after he nailed Mitsukai with a nice uppercut punch bringing him to his knees, then Uraki connected with a stiff kick to his head. Mitsukai looked done, but he countered with a punch to the gut, slowing the big man up, then he threw Uraki over with a German Suplex, letting out the last of his energy before tagging out to Suicida.
TIME: 10:25 Suicida got in and quickly went for a pin with a La Majistral, but Uraki kicked out after a two count. Suicida then planted him with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker that looked to take out all the energy from the now gassed
Uraki. The cover got a two. Puzzled, Suicida looked to finish the match as he signaled for the Cloud 9 Powerbomb.
He had Uraki set up, but he couldn't get him up. This led to a stiff knee to the bloodied face on Suicida, and then he went to the top rope and crushed
Angel's skull with  The Mikado's Hammer, but it wasn't enough. Suicida tagged out to Mitsukai.
TIME: 11:15 Mistukai rushed Uraki, who attempted his Uraki Kyaku finisher, but he missed as Mitsukai ducked and locked on the Nocturnal Submission which looked to end it, but Ryoseika smelled it out and broke it up as Uraki's hand was about to fall for the third time. The ref started to argue with Ryo, and as this happened Mitsukai had Uraki set up on his shoulders.
TIME: 13:00 Angel then went to the top as the the crowd sensed the finisher they had used the night before, the Super Assisted Dragon Rana, but this wasn't the case. Uraki woke up from his trance, and as Angel wrapped his legs around
Uraki, he was sent flying to the mat with a Pseudo-Powerbomb that looked to be rather stiff. He then surprised Mituskai with a victory roll from the shoulders as Ryoseika pointed for the ref to count. And that he did for a three count.
END RESULT: Ryunosuke Uraki ( O - Victory Roll - X ) Goshikku Mitsukai
FINAL TIME: 14:01 Final Thoughts: Great match for both teams. These four showed what NOP is all about. After this match, Kobe Koresawa came out and reminded the fans to attend the first NOP Nagoya Action TV taping that will take place on October 27th at the Tokyo Differ Ariake. Advance tickets would go on sale tomorrow and would stay on sale until the start of the first bout. The gong rang, and the show was over.
