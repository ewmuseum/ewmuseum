Standing in front of this backdrop is a rather large, tall Japanese man. By the looks of his stomach, it seems like he's been drinking a little too much Saki, but the rest of his body defies this, sturdy and muscular in a power-lifter way. He bows solemnly before addressing us.] ???: Honor is a thing that is sought much in our society. From the highest of politicians to the lowliest of the working class, honor and joy go hand in hand. It is a rare thing, then, that honor is given out freely. Many a man and woman holds onto whatever honor they have like a vice, hoping it shall not slip through their fingers to another. But I? I freely give my honor, and my very life, to Naogoya Organization of Puroresu. The "Blinding Storm" that swept through the arenas the first time NOP rose to notoriety, has returned. I, Tetsumi Nanami, have returned. [Nanami lightly taps his chest.] TN: I will not fail this organization, nor the fans whom love it. I will rise higher than ever before. Filthy gaijin. As anyone who knows the name Genji is aware of, I work a very stiff submission style of wrestling. And during this match, he swung at me with his big, flabby arm. I blocked his right with my left arm, and then hooked his right arm with both my arms. I kicked out my feet, and swung around, screaming in midair as I brought him down with my patented Genji arm breaker. And what happened? Snap. The man broke his arm, and begins to whine like a baby. What, I am to blame for that? I am to blame because this man cannot take the pain? I am to blame because this man comes from a country of laughable wrestling and doesn’t know what to expect when he wrestles in my home country? I am to blame when a man is so ignorant he doesn’t know to RESPECT and FEAR the name Genji? I don’t understand that at all. But you see my side of the story. You understand me. Unless you’re a filthy American, that is. [He chuckles to himself. GG: Ah, where was I?
