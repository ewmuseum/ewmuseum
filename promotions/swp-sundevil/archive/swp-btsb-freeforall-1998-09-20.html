"Back to Skool Brawl: Free For All"

Rich: (sarcastic) Wahoo! Sam: Rich, you of ALL people should be thrilled. When the EWO merged with the SWP, you lost your job. This job saved you. Rich: Yeah, so what. I know I'm better than those other guys, I should be the head commentator! Sam: Rich, cram it, be greatful for what you have. At least you're not Homeless. Rich: Yeah, I could just be a Homeless bum like... Sam: Don't say it! Rich: I won't Sam: Any-who folks, Lets talk PPVs. Back to School Brawl is only 30 minutes away! Rich: They shoulda called it....[Rich stares out to space] a "new beginning." Sam: hey Rich, thats pretty good..... Rich: You think so? Maybe I should name PPVs from now on. Sam: Don't count on it. First, lets talk a little 'bout our main event. The IeWC rep VS the EMWO Rep VS the EWO rep VS the CROW rep, and all for the pride of the SWP, the World Championship Title. Rich: Who so takeths that belt, is king of all England..... Sam:(looks oddly at Rich) yeah.....anyways...Robert Lee, the man representing the EWO is who we'll hear from first. The funny thing is, in this segment, Lee seems to represent..... Rich: Who...who...come on, spit it out! Sam: "Gorgeous" [name withheld]! Rich: *GASP* Sam: Why Robert Lee would want to mock Cryus Greene, I don't know, take a look. -=rolls clip=- [SCENE: Oh yes, the old folk's home. The main lobby looks very melancholy. The old green couches seated next to the panoramic window, neither brighten the room nor make it feel more at home. The "elderly" people are seated on the couches, starring out into a world they were once part of. The front desk of the home has a young woman seated at it, fiddling with her blond hair and chewing gum blankly. Robert Lee is seen in the back round walking towards the desk.] [Robert is wearing the basic white coveralls required by nursing home personal. He has his obviously dyed blond hair is slicked back and tied in a ponytail. He also has a pair of black sunglasses covering his eyes. A man who must be his manager approaches him] Manager: Lee! What is wrong with you? Please follow the dress code, or get out! Robert Lee: Hey Jack, you are 45 and working in a nursing home! God, that was a waste of your life. Manager: I am going to pretend I didn't hear that. Robert Lee: Pretend if you want, I said it and there is nothing you can do about it! [Lee walks over to the green couches and beings dusting a table. An elderly woman asks him a question] Old Woman: Excuse me young man, my pudding is a little cold. Could I get another? Robert Lee: No you can't. Do I look like a bloody vending machine? Take what you get and if it is bad, die and get better pudding in heaven you old bag! Old Woman: I just wanted a different pudding. You have a whole cart of them over there [She points to a silver cart full of pudding] Robert Lee: Do you have hooks in your ass? Get up and get yourself one if you want it that bad. God, you whine allot.... Old Woman: But-- Robert Lee: Say one more thing lady you are going to be wearing that pudding, all right? Just shut up and eat it. Don't make me take you back to the rat room! [Robert gets up and walks down the hall towards the maintenance room. An old man stops him on the way] Old Man: Excuse me sir, my wa----wa----wa-- "Cyrus" Lee: C'mon spit it out buddy. Old Man: M---m---my walker broke. D---do you have a--a---another? Robert Lee: [Sarcastically] Oh yes I do as a matter of fact. I always keep a couple of walkers in my [CENSOR] pants. Let me just get one out for you. [He reaches into his pocket and pulls out.....His middle finger.... Sigh....] [The old man's grandchild comes running out to the hallway] GrandKid: Hey! Why did you give my grand dad the finger? Robert Greene: Kid, your granddad is old, obnoxious and an all-around [CENSOR] head. You question my job one more time I am going to throw you out the goddamn window out into the f'n street. Do I make myself clear? [The kid cowers back into the corner. Lee walks back to the matenancee room where a camera crew is waiting for him.] CameraMan: Lee, what took you so long? We need your comments for the upcoming match! Robert Lee: Those old people are so needy. Anways, I want to rap a little bit about My opponents in the 4-man match for the world title....but....I won't because I don't feel like wasting my breath. CameraMan: Lee, you have to man! Robert Lee: Alright, lets start if with Christian York... I don't know much about York except that he was the former EMWO champion. Does that scare me?......Not really. I may be from the indy leagues back in...Well...That's not important. The important thing is that I am an indy diamond from the rough. York, you are probably used to the spotlight but I will tell you, It is my turn to shine. I spent over 12 years in a league called the APwC. You probably never heard of it, and that doesn't surprise me. APwC may be a little fed but it has balls if you know what I mean. I was the champion for 11 of those 12 years. Sure, that doesn't say much but I think that speaks for my work ethic. Paul Dare is a man I hate with a passion. He comes to us from CROW. Another place I spit on. I was once in the CROW, but I learned my lesson. Two things come from CROW--- [The door swings open and there is Lee's manager!] Manager: Lee, what the hell is all this? Why is there camera equipment in my back room? Robert Lee: Well, I am a pro wrestler-- Manager: Oh wow, a pro-wrestler! So you are a drug user in layman terms? Robert Lee: You better watch your mouth sonny. I will not hesitate to knock that rug off your head and knock your teeth out. Manager: I have no choice but to terminate your employment here at St. Mary's Rest Home. Robert Lee: Hey ace, you can't fire me! Manager: Why the hell not? Robert Lee: Because I quit. Have a nice life; I am out of here. [Lee begins to walk out of the rest home with the manager yelling at him] Manager: You won't amount to anything! A wrestler? What is wrong with you? Why don't you get some real goals? This job was the best thing you had going for you! I am telling you Lee, you are going to come crawling back! You hear me! [The screen fades to black and white words pop up...] WHO LAUGH'S LAST? [End transmission] -=clip fades out=- Sam: So Rich, whaddya think? Rich: about what? Sam: About the main event....do you have a prediction for us. Rich: I predict that....Rich Jericho will be the winner! Sam: You can't do that! Rich Jericho isn't even in it!!! Rich: Fine...I predict...Sam's big, fat mom to win!! Sam: Thats bett....WHAT!! Rich: Our next clip is of the man who is f'n crazy, Paul Dare -=rolls clip=- ( Scene starts inside a huge empty dimly lit arena. All around is seen thousands and thousands of empty orange seats, every seat but one. In the middle of the floor, under a bright spot light, is a wrestling ring. Near the wrestling ring, sitting in the front row is the CROW World Heavyweight Champion "Flamboyant" Paul Dare. Dressed in a fine Armani suit he just sits there and looks at the wrestling ring before him. Rising to his feet, Dare walks toward the ring and then rolls inside. Almost looking at every inch of the ring. He then looks to the thousands and thousands of empty seats inside the huge empty dimly lit arena. Dare then begins to smile. ) Paul Dare- ( his back toward the camera ) There is no better feeling than wrestling in a full house of screaming wrestling fans. ( turning toward the camera ) Who cares if most of them curse me or boo me? In there hearts they know that I am the one they came to see because I give them their money's worth. ( Dare begins to unbutton his Armani jacket. ) As a matter of fact, I am the one who puts butts in the seats. Where ever I go. That is where the money goes. And that's a fact. As for my upcoming title match against those pretenders. The pretenders who feel they are World Heavyweight Champions. ( Dare cracks yet another smile. ) Listen up. I am going to show all three of those chumps. Who the real champion in wrestling is. That would be yours truly. Often imitated but never duplicated. I am going to show those three freakboys ( York, Lee and Williams ) that when it comes to flat out wrestling. I am the smartiest and most technical wrestler in wrestling today. When it comes to finishing a match. If I slap on the Market Crash. No matter who it is. It is lights out for anyone that faces the Flamboyant One. That is not just talk because I am proven. Just ask Jack Vars or Mad Goat. Two of the best all around wrestlers in the game today. It was Dare who took their belts. It was Dare who put them on their backs for the 1- 2- 3. It was Dare who's hand was raised in victory afterward. In the four way SWP World Heavyweight Championship title match, the results will be no different. It will be every man for himself. Which is fine with me because I am no ordinary man. I am the best at what I do. And that is pinning freakboys for the big win. Love me or hate me, deal with it. Because when I win. There will be a new era of wrestling. A era of domination in the SWP for me and a few select individuals. Which individuals you might ask? That's a secret. If I told you know now. I would have to kill you afterward. So just pray you are on the list. If you are not, something wicked this way comes. ( Dare begins to loosen his tie. His facial expression begins to become more intense. ) Because I am not extreme. I am f**king crazy. ( Scene ends ) -=end of clip=- [Rich and Sam are fighting] Rich: So! Ya don't like my sisters cooking huh!!! Sam: I like it better than looking at your nasty grandma's face!!! Rich: Oh Yeah.. Sam: yeah!!! -=rolls clip=- (The camera opens to a scene of a very palatial mansion located in the hills of Raleigh, NC. The sunlight shines off the branches of the trees and rich greenery is detected everywhere. The cameraman slowly moves to the back of the estate, where a huge Olympic-sized swimming pool has been placed in the ground. One can't help but notice an attractive female in her early 20's sunning in one of the retractable chairs, wearing only a deep blue bikini. All of a sudden, a man makes his way from the back porch to the pool area, dressed in a baby blue pair of Hilfiger swimming shorts. The female wakes herself from a state of near-sleep to watch as as the gentleman, also in his 20's, walks toward the camera. He carries with him two champagne glasses and a bottle of White Zinfandel. He puts the glasses down, kisses the young lady, and then makes his way to the diving board of the pool. Seemingly preoccupied, he finally notices the SWP cameraman has arrived and he begins to address the camera. For those of you who don't know.....you'll soon find out. This is the Charismatic One.....Christian York.) Christian: (to cameraman) You see this? All of you at home? Can you see this? (points to the ladder of the diving board) You know, I've never been big on symbolism, but this right here, without question, has to be the very symbol of my career. Throughout the various federations that I've been in, the ladder has always symbolized my rise to the top. My ascent to the peaks.... Denise: Honey? Aren't you getting a little ahead of yourself? You haven't introduced yourself. Half of these people don't even know who you are! Christian: (laughs hysterically) Don't know who I AM?!?! They don't know? Hell, all of the transfers, both fans AND wrestlers, from the EMWO know who I am. My name, for the past two and a half months, has been on the lips of every wrestling fan around the country. I am the new era of wrestling. But, if you need a name, I suppose I should tell you.....you may call me the Charismatic One, YOUR "main eventer," forget the showstopper, I'm the damn show ITSELF,......"Mr. Charisma" Christian York!!! This lovely young lady with me is none other than my wife, Denise. (Denise just raises her glass at the camera.) Christian: Are you happy now, girl? Anyways.....back to the subject at hand.....this ladder has been the symbol of the Charismatic One's rise to the top ever since he entered the world of professional wrestling. Ever since I entered the sport, I've had to climb rung by rung to inch my way to the top. And finally, I reached the summit. I reached the top. Undefeated in the E-Mail Wrestling Organization. World Heavyweight Champion. Then.....foreclosure. The bottom drops out, if you will. The Charismatic One is forced to pack his bags and move on to the next spot.....meaning that once again, I have to climb the ladder. Only this time....HAHA....the ladder is a little bit smaller. Due to a little wheeling and dealing and due to my spot as the only EMWO World Heavyweight Champion, I've earned a spot in the main event of the first SWP PPV to crown the Heavyweight Champion. I mean....in all factuality, where else could they put a man of my talents, my charm.....my CHARISMA.....BUT in the main event, right?! (laughs once again) Just look at this! (spins around) I am the ratings-getter....I am the main eventer.....I am the SHOW ITSELF! Right, babe? Denise: Absolutely, Chris. But don't get ahead of yourself. You've got three men to go through to get that title belt. (A look of serious determination returns to Christian's face.) Christian: Oh, I know. You see, there's ALWAYS another ladder to climb. There's always another mountain to scale. You see this ladder here? (points again to the diving pool ladder) Three rungs. What a coincidence! The ladder that I have to climb Sunday has three rungs as well! Let's see.....where should I start? (Christian climbs up the first rung of the ladder.) Christian: Robert Lee. No offense, but I've never heard of you in my life! No idea what to expect here, but the Charismatic One is prepared for anything. I was a Boy Scout. I know the motto! You, Mr. Lee, are just the first step that I have to climb to get to my rightful prize. Hell, come this PPV, they may just call me "Stonewall" York, because that's what you'll feel like once you get slapped with the Class Act! You'll feel like you hit a stone wall! HAHAHA! Next.... (Christian climbs up to the second rung.) Christian: Paul Dare. Oh, I KNOW who you are, Paulie! You see, fans, this is the guy that claims that I OWE him a debt of gratitude. You say that I owe my EMWO title to you, Dare? That I'm insolent because I didn't thank you for MY title?? How about this? How about I show you my gratitude this Sunday night? How about that, Dare? You want my gratitude, Dare? Well, you're damn sure going to get it....right upside your skull! I and I alone won the EMWO World Heavyweight Championship, and I will prove to you that I can and will win the SWP World Heavyweight Championship! If you think I'm bluffing.....why don't you just try to stop the show?! Why? Because you can't, Dare!! (Christian makes his way up to the third rung.) Christian: Finally....Lawrence Williams. I must admit that I'm a little bit confused here. The last time that I saw you, Lawrence, you were limping out of The Grand Finale with your career tucked between your legs. You were beaten, man! You were out of here! The Homeless Joe sent you packin', brother, but somehow now you're back, aren't you? It doesn't matter, my friend, it really doesn't matter. Don't get me wrong, now! I owe a lot to Lawrence Williams. It was his EMWO that gave me the break of a lifetime. But it was ME that ran with the ball when it was handed to me. ME. You gave me the shot, Lawrence, and for that, I'm grateful. But I made YOU the money, now didn't I? I'm the one that kept your show afloat with the ratings. I'm the one that brought all the little Charismatics to the ring so that they could plunk out their last nickel just to see ME. I made you rich, Lawrence, and unfortunately, on Sunday night, I have to go for broke. There can be no other way, Lawrence. I respect you and I admire you, but you're not taking my shot now, Lawrence. I've got the ball and I ain't giving it back. Not to you, or Dare, or Civil War boy, or anybody! The show will once again take the stage this Sunday night, and what you already know I'm capable of will shine through again. Why? (Christian dives off the board, arching into a perfect swan dive and splashing into the water. He quickly rises to the surface and jumps out of the pool.) Christian: Because the show simply must go on!!! HAHAHAHAHA! Lee.......Dare......Williams......York. The four-way dance of death. You're not going to want to miss this one! (The camera fades to black as Christian joins Denise at the side of the pool and the two raise a toast.) -=end of clip=-
