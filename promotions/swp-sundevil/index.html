A Seattle-set fed, formed by a merger with a prior promotion (the EWO), that ran a three-hour Back to Skool Brawl card in September 1998 built around a four-way match to crown its own SWP World Heavyweight Champion from wrestlers drawn from several rival feds. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

SWP's own surviving broadcast opens under an ASCII banner reading “SEATTLE WRESTLING PROMOTION,” called by Richard Siegel and Sam Poteet, former commentators for the eWo, which the same broadcast says has merged into SWP by the time of this card, 20 September 1998.

That card, Back to Skool Brawl: Free For All, built toward a four-way match for the SWP World Championship — “for the pride of the SWP,” as its own commentators put it — pulling one challenger apiece from IeWC, EMWO, the eWo, and CROW. The surviving segment carries pre-match words from three of the four: Robert Lee, working as a nursing-home orderly by day, threatening to end the career of an EMWO alumnus in the ring; Christian York, poolside at his own mansion with his wife Denise, framing his own career as a ladder he keeps having to climb again from the bottom; and CROW's own World Heavyweight Champion, “Flamboyant” Paul Dare, promising to show all three of his opponents “who the real champion in wrestling is.”

How the four-way itself came out, and how, or whether, the promotion continued past its own last surviving document, dated 10 October 1998, isn't recorded.
