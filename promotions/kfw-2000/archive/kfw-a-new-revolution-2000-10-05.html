KFW: A New Revolution

5 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kempsville Fallout Wrestling The NEW Revolution in Professional Wrestling ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ [INTRODUCTION] Welcome to the KFW, a new e-federation in search of a quality aspect in the world of Role-Playing. First three things I'd like to let you know is, first, the KFW is mostly a real wrestler fed, but dooccasionally allow fake wrestlers.
I like to try to keep things as close as possible to the real federations but with our own twist. For example, I'll use Bobby Heenan and Micheal Cole one week for announcers of our show, and the next week Jim Ross and Jerry Lawler.
This keeps things new and creative. Secondly, the way our shows are run is once a week on Sundays. The shows will always vary as well. A good example would be the first week in a month I'll do a regularly watched wrestling show(i.e. - Raw Is War, Nitro, Smackdown, Thunder, etc.). The week following will be a show not seen on TV. I'll create a name for this show, and as the months pass, new shows will show up in the second week. The third week will be a show named after a real life Pay-Per-View, such as Souled Out or
Backlash. It will more than likely be a regular PPV, and not a big PPV, such as SummerSlam or Royal Rumble. The last week in a month will also be named after a real life PPV, but will more than likely be one of the bigger
Pay-Per-Views. And the last thing I'd like to point out is a point a keep in mind... controlled flexibility. I want everyone here to have fun while
Role-Playing. I don't want RPing to become a burden, and that's why I don't believe in a minimum amount of lines or a certain quantity of RPs. I don't like to see someone who RPs twice a day, with both RPs being very long, yet have no creativity, such as a House Show setting where the character(s) come out and yack about their opponent. I like to see creative settings, and lots of interaction. I, of course, allow my RPers to create their own shows where they can further angles they are working on, but I would like encourage creative
RPing. I look for how well a character can be played out and what they do in certain situations. I'm not going to release you from the fed if you can't RP one week, or if you only do one RP a week. I may if after two or three shows you show no sign of effort, but usually I'll just take you off the list until you're actively able to RP again.
To list a few quick notabilities about the KFW, the KFW is an e-mail federation. I like this opposed to board feds where basically anyone can gain access to it and spam on it as they please. I only allow two RPs per day, to keep in mind the fact that I look for quality over quantity. I grade RPs by a few things: how well the person stays in character, creative settings, imagery(how well things are described), interaction(speech between your characters and other people in the setting), and lastly, and foremostly, angle development. Angles are something that show your effort in RPing and ability to further KFW storylines. Angles will definitely gain you a big push. I want everyone to feel that I am a President that isn't looking to just help out his fed, but watch some of the RPers gain accomplishments. This is why I feel flexibility is a good thing in order to keep a fed alive. Now I know some of you may be begginning RPers, and that's what the KFW plans to do... turn beginning RPers into great RPers. If you are a beginning RPer I'll work with you on angles, and help you grow into a well contendable RPer.
In closing, if you're interested in joining the KFW, e-mail me, Simond Gold at [detail withheld] . Just come ready with a good attitude. Don't whine or complain. I don't want you to feel as if you don't play as big a role in the
KFW, and if you feel you aren't you can e-mail personally with any problems you have. Just go with your strides and your good RPing will pay off. I stay unbiased in my grading, and always look for enthusiastic, upbeat players. With that said, I hope to see you soon, and I hope that you choose to join.
iiPresident Simond Goldii [-RULES-] GENERAL RULES 1.) At no time should anyone spam the list with complaints or requests to quit. If you have any problems, show some class by e-mailing me personally. But anything encouraging good RPing is always welcomed.
2.) You are only allowed 5 characters. Sometimes people will question this, but I always think this goes along with giving the RPers a good amount of flexibility, and especially with angles. I also allow one tag team if you choose so. The characters you choose are all up to you.
3.) Bad attitudes will not be tolerated. I want people to feel freedom in the KFW, but I don't want the freedom to cause people to use it in a bad way. Go with the flow, and, as I've said, your rewards will come with time and quality RPing.
RPing RULES 1.) Only 2 RPlays per day. Remember quality over quantity. 2.) No cursing, racial or ethnic slurs, or extreme sexual references. I hate to sound prudent, but some of our members may take offense to certain phrases.
3.) Do not use other wrestlers in your RPlays that are *ON THE ROSTER*. However, you can use wrestlers that aren't currently on the roster in your RPlays. 4.) Don't use any OoC or other unneccesary space in your RPs unless noting something that we may need to know. I don't care for your opinion of your RP, how many lines it is or anything that is complete unneeded.
5.) Since some people may not have fast computers, refrain from using pictures in your RPs. My computer is somewhat slow, and sometimes don't have but a couple of minutes to read RPlays.

To work up to World title contention, you should be able to show a good amount of work and quality RPing.
I usually think of pushing someone up to the World Title after they've worked a good feud or angle with someone in the mid-card. But no doubt, you will have to show your best quality RPing and be consistent to reach this milestone.
INTERCONTINENTAL TITLE The very basis for reaching up to the World Title. I've always liked the IC title division because it is filled with your future World champions. When in contention of this belt, you're proving whether or not you can be World title material. This is the belt for most of the intermediate RPers, and takes a good bit of work to get up to.
CRUISERWEIGHT TITLE This title is mainly a beginner's title. This division is made for wrestlers 220 pounds and under. I like to work with many of the Cruiserweight title contenders in helping them with the basis in RPing. From here RPers can work up to mid-card status, and hopefully work in an angle in getting there.
TAG TEAM TITLES The tag titles are for all levels of RPers. In fact, if you read the introduction all the way through, you'll notice that I give the option of everyone that joins to choose a tag team. Although this is only optional, I would like to see this be a very hot division. And therefore I'm giving everyone the option of being a part of it. In this division I will basically award the belts to the top quality RPer. But I will give a lot of emphasis on the beginner RPers in this division.
One thing else to note, there's two ways these belts can be won. First, one person that handles a tag team can win it. Or, two people that handle two singles wrestlers can win them together.

[detail withheld] . I really look forward to working things out and running the KFW. I look to make this a fun, respectable environment, and hopefully if you decide to join you will bring that attitude as well. See you soon.
