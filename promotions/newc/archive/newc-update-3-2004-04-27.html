Outro =========================================================== 1. Introduction Guys, sorry about the lateness. Birthday week took up a lot of my free time, as well as some unscheduled events that I spoke with some of you about privately on IRC and AIM. Nevertheless, great show IMHO. We had a lot more participation this time as far as on-card stuff was concerned. Also, I'd like to thank [name withheld] for writing a kick ass last second match from work. Expect to receive your one-night stand hooker sometime this week. :-) Not much I can say here, other than I hope to get back on track with things now that my birthday is behind us all. Last time I got very little constructive feedback. I realize it's a most horrid and difficult thing to do, but please give me feedback in order for me to try and make Danger Zone as entertaining a card to read as possible. =========================================================== 2. News & Notes - NEWC's second show had a total of 1027 fans. It's a little over 300 less fans than the first show that took place in CT 2 weeks ago, but fans seemed more excited about finding out when the NEWC would be returning to Bean Town. - In PPV News, the date was already announced as July 4th, which will be on Independence Day. The NEWC front office felt that no other place would be more fitting than to have the first PPV take place in Philadelphia, PA. The venue will be the Wachovia Center, which has a capacity of about 20,000 fans. We're not expecting to sell out, but we are expecting a great crowd of at least 5,000 people or so. - Sabbath surprised everyone by showing up at Danger Zone and taking part in the Main Event Battle Royal. Even more surprising to some was the fact that he debuted with winning the rumble and becoming the first ever NEWC Television Champion. Already, there's a buzz in the locker room as many await their shot at the champ. - NEWC's front office men, Dave Powers and Christopher Collins decided to stay in the backstage area at the last show. They thought it'd be good to let just the wrestlers handle the show and seemed very proud of them. - What's next between the already bloody fued of Bryan Vincent and Zack Sharp? Sharp was assaulted during and after their match on Danger Zone, to the point where he wasn't expected to be at the rumble later in the night. Sharp came out with a steel chair, eliminating Bradley Vision first, then Bryan Vincent shortly after. The two continued to fight all the way into the locker room bathroom area, where they had to be restrained from one another by security and Boston PD Officers. - Steve Greedy didn't do himself too many favors at the rumble, gaining a lot of heat when he came out to cost Misery a chance at becoming the NEWC TV Champion. He also helped get rid of Ethan Everitt by pulling down the top rope from the outside. He might've accidentally made himself a wanted man by two wild monsters of the NEWC. - NEWC GM Christopher Collins has announced that each main event of the next 4 shows will be Three Way Dances to qualify four men to the PPV Main Event that will crown our first ever NEWC Heavyweight Champion. That match will be a 4 Corner's Match, elimination style. - Also announced for the PPV, by Commissioner Dave Powers, is the TV Title match. It has been made official that Misery and Phoenix will both get their second chance at being crowned NEWC TV Champion in a Triple Threat Match, also featuring TV Champion Sabbath, or whoever the champ may be by then. Next Card Lineup Date: 05/07/04 Venue: Verizon Wireless Arena Location: Manchester, NH NEIGE THIRTEEN looked to be done as far as not only the NEWC goes, but wrestling in general. He took his beating on the previous Danger Zone like a man. Still trying to make it to his lucky number 13, he wrestles the naive rookie, HERNAN IGLESIAS. Neige is just trying to survive his 13 match contract and see if wrestling is made for him, while Hernan is just trying to get his name out there in the wrestling world. In a double debut match, we'll be seeing THE STRANGLER, a very mysterious man, as he'll be taking on the pride of Belarus, ALEXEY ISAKOVA! The size different between the two is about 60 pounds, but Alexey said that he's up to the task of taking out the bigger opponent. The third match of the night will feature another debutee, as "LOWLIFE" CHRIS PRESTON will be taking on "SICK & TWISTED" ZACK SHARP! Sharp has got his own problems in the NEWC, as he's involved in a heated rivalry with "The Jackal" Bryan Vincent, who is expected to be at the show. Preston is trying to groove his way to victory in his NEWC debut. NEWC's first ever TELEVISION CHAMPION will make his first title defense against "THE RICH ONE" STEVE GREEDY. Greedy kept himself busy at the rumble, costing the match for Ethan Everitt and Misery. SABBATH says that Greedy's luck won't be helping him, as he plans to make a successful first title defense. And the MAIN EVENT for the evening, is a three way dance to decide the first man to take part in the NEWC Heavyweight Title Match at the PPV. ALEXANDER ANDERSON has been unstoppable so far, picking up huge victories against "Drifter" JD Carter and Ethan Everitt so far. "PRIMO" CHRIS CAPRI has quickly set his vision from the TV Title to the Heavyweight Title. He expects nothing less than a win to qualify himself for the Heavyweight Title match at the PPV. MISERY completes the Three Way Dance. He's been on a hot streak, winning both of his matches, and falling short of winning the NEWC TV Title, only because of outside interference from Steve Greedy. That's the card lineup.  Strats and RPs are due on Tuesday, May 4 at Midnight Eastern the latest.  Send everything to [detail withheld]. =========================================================== 6.
