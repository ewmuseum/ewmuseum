Introduction        One show down, many more to go.  I'm pretty happy with the fact that just about everyone did a RP or segment for the show.  Even anyone who didn't had a good reason for it, so I couldn't be mad at them anyway.  I created a poll on the Yahoo Groups site, so everyone could could vote on what they thought of the first card.        Also, I know that it wasn't the most perfect card in the world. It was the first show, so constructive feedback would very much be appreciated.  Not just telling me if the show was good or it sucked, but let me know what worked well, what you thought should've been different, things like that.  That way, for future shows, we could improve on those things to make a show of higher quality for you guys.        I'm not just doing this for myself, I'm doing it to keep you all interested and having fun with the fed.  Let's move on to the Update. =========================================================== 2. News & Notes - NEWC's first ever Danger Zone brought in 1,355 live viewers. A very solid fan-base appears to have been founded in the Fairfield/Bridgeport area of Connecticut. - GM Chris Collins stirred up a lot of excitement in the back when he announced that there will be a battle royal taking place on the next Danger Zone to decide the NEWC Television Title. He has changed some minor things, after the show adding that it will be open to ALL members of the roster. He said that wrestlers have until April 15th to sign up for it. - NEWC's GM also mentioned something that grabbed the direct attention of a couple of the NEWC Stars right away. Announcing that he plans to decide the NEWC Heavyweight Title on June 6, which he later released to the press that June 6 is the date of NEWC's first ever SuperCard, which has yet to be named. - "The Jackal" and the "Sick & Twisted" one showed to us that they aren't really too fond of one another. This has led Commissioner Dave Powers to sign the two to a match this week. This should help them get some steam off their chests. - We saw what Ethan Everitt has to offer to the NEWC. If he really follows directions from no one but Commissioner Powers, then the whole roster might have something to worry about. Powers was very happy with Everitt's short work of Ogama and even Neige. He promises that more of that is to come. Next Card Lineup Date: 04/21/04 Location: Cabot Center               Boston, MA After a great reaction from wrestling fans in the Bridgeport/Fairfield area of Connecticut, NEWC hopes to do the same again as they have announced their second card is set to take place on Wednesday, April 21 in Boston, Massachusetts. Following that match will be another rebounding man, who plans to do something about his beating that took place twice in the same match. NEIGE THIRTEEN hopes to pull out a magical win against MISERY, a man who looks to have a very promising future here in the NEWC. Marcus Viktus was furious about NEFARIOUS not defeating Misery on Danger Zone. This time he expects otherwise from him as he takes on TAKISUMA OGAMA and BRADLEY VISION in a Triple Threat Match. Ogama took quite a beating from Ethan Everitt, but we were told that no bones were broken and that he only suffered a mild concussion and will be able to compete. "THE RICH ONE" STEVE GREEDY showed us why he should never take place in an opener, by defeating Bradley Vision. The two put on a great performance, but Greedy proved to may have been too much for Vision. This week he'll see how much more he can handle as he takes on a pissed off "DRIFTER" JD CARTER. He might be in for something he does not want to get himself into. Pissed off that "SICK & TWISTED" ZACK SHARP rudely interrupted his promo-time on Danger Zone, "THE JACKAL" BRYAN VINCENT was definitely not happy about that as we saw from what ended up happening. This time nothing unexpected should take place as they battle it out, one on one in a GRUDGE MATCH. Vincent wants to put Sharp in his place for ruining his debut on NEWC's inaugural show. After defeating the "Drifter" on the last show, ALEXANDER ANDERSON seemed very happy with himself. This time he shouldn't enter the ring all too cocky though, as he'll be taking on Commissioner Dave Powers' own personal weapon, ETHAN EVERITT. Everitt was very impressive in his NEWC debut and is trying to keep on the same path of destruction. The MAIN EVENT of the evening should be a real good one. Announced as a regular rumble, then recently changed into a BATTLE ROYAL where the last two men standing will only be able to eliminate the other via PINFALL. Every man on the roster is welcome to enter this event. The last day to sign up for this is April 15 as it was announced by GM Christopher Collins. The winner of this will walk away as NEWC's first title holder, winning the NEWC TELEVISION TITLE. That's the card lineup.  Strats and RPs are due on Friday, April 16 at Midnight Eastern the latest.  Send everything to [detail withheld]. =========================================================== 6.
