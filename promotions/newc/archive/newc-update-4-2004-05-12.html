Outro =========================================================== 1. Introduction Another late card. Sorry guys. I don't like excuses, so I'm not going to give any. I'll just try to do better next time. I'll need at least one voluntary match writer this time around. I don't usually ask this of the handlers because I already had a staff from the beginning. Some changes have been made and I'll need at least one emergency writer. Send me your thoughts on the show, rather it be bashing us or acknowledging the good work. I need critiques if the shows are supposed to improve. If you like it the way it is, then just e-mail me and say "I like it the way it is." That's all for now. To the Update we go. =========================================================== 2. News & Notes - NEWC saw it's largest crowd yet, having 1,452 fans at the latest Danger Zone! - GM Christopher Collins surprised everyone by announcing the creation of the NEWC Tri-State Title, a belt that was made to represent the NJ, NY, and CT area. NEWC's headquarters are in New Haven, CT which makes sense that a belt would represent the region that they come from. - Who was the man who attacked Neige Thirteen so viciously? Sources tell us that the NEWC has signed this man, known throughout the Southern indy circuits as TRASHBAG JACKSON. This man has a violent streak that's unmatched from any we've seen thus far in NEWC ... and he's one HELL of a talent to sign. Look for him to make an immediate impact! - "The Jackal" Bryan Vicent and "Sick & Twisted" Zack Sharp are taking their feud to the next level. Having officially faced each other once, but having fought one another at least four times already, Commissioner Dave Powers said he's had enough of that. He announced last night that they will face off at the Pay-Per-View in a Last Man Standing Match. He hopes that after that, one of them will at least want nothing to do with the other. - "The Walking Contradiction" Sabbath retains the title in a successful defense against "The Rich One" Steve Greedy, but he was not happy at the fact that he had help from Misery. Misery added fuel to the fire that is already burning for that Three Way Dance at the Pay-Per-View. The two almost went at it, had it not been for security rushing the scene. - Speaking of PPV's, NEWC has said that the PPV will be named before the month's end. They're hoping to have a name for it by the next week or so. - Alexander Anderson outlasted Misery and "Primo" Chris Capri in a Three Way Dance, becoming the first man to qualify for the NEWC Heavyweight Title Match. Afterwards, he was attacked by JD Carter, who seemed to be a pissed off man all show. On the next show, Carter will try to become the second man to qualify, taking on Ethan Everitt and The Strangler! - Bradley Vision was released from the NEWC shortly after Danger Zone. Commissioner Dave Powers signed the release papers, wishing Vision good luck, but saying that the NEWC hasn't a use for him right now and hopes that someday they could sign him back and make him a major piece of the NEWC puzzle. Next Card Lineup Date: 05/22/04 Venue: Dunkin Donuts Center Location: Providence, RI After its largest crowd yet, the NEWC makes their way down to Providence, Rhode Island on Saturday May 22nd! Opening the night, we will have a match in the NEWC Tri-State Title Tournament! Being signed over week ago, ALEX HAWKINS will be making his inr-ing debut, taking on "THE JACKAL" BRYAN VINCENT! Vincent and Sharp have been going at it up and down the NEWC appearances thus far. Maybe making this match the opening bout will keep Sharp from showing up early and taking part in this match. "THE BUDDHIST MONK" DILLI RAM IYAYI somehow got a win in a match that should've been a no-contest! The man that attacked him and Neige Thirteen has been identified to everyone as TRASHBAG JACKSON! This week he'll get to try and get his revenge on Jackson, or not. Whatever the case is, they'll meet up in the ring. In the second NEWC Tri-State Title Tournament match of the night, we will have THE STRANGLER. His opponent is a man who almost beat Alexander Anderson on Danger Zone to move on to the NEWC Heavyweight Title Match at the PPV. "PRIMO" CHRIS CAPRI will be taking on the slobby fat man, trying to move on the to semi-finals of the Tri-State Championship Tournament. Making his first title defense a total success, SABBATH will try and continue his streak here in the NEWC by defending his NEWC TELEVISION TITLE up against "SICK & TWISTED" ZACK SHARP! Sharp picked up a big win against Chris Preston, even with outside help from Bryan Vincent. Sabbath hopes that this will be a match without any interference, much unlike his first title defense, which was against Steve Greedy! And in the main event, Alexander Anderson was the first man to qualify for the NEWC Heavyweight Title Match! Afterwards, he was attacked by "LAST OF A DYING BREED" JD CARTER. This time around, it's Carter's turn to qualify for the match. He'll be taking on NEIGE THIRTEEN and Dave Powers' boy, ETHAN EVERITT! All men want to move on, but only one will get that chance to do so. That's the card lineup.  Strats and RPs are due on Wednesday, May 19 at Midnight Eastern the latest.  Send everything to [detail withheld]. =========================================================== 6.
