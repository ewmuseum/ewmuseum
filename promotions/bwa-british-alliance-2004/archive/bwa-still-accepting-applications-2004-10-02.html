Who's the Boss - In Character and Out. 2. TV 3. PPV 4. Titles 5. BWA On Screen Personalities 6. Application 1) Who's the boss? The British Wrestling Alliance formed under the guidance of a man with a vision; to bring British wrestling to the people of the world, and show that
Britain had the talent and the knowhow to create the perfect wrestling experience.
There's only one problem... no one knows _who_ this man is. There has been rampant speculation, with everyone from Sabbath to Tommy Lionheart being mentioned as the head of the newly formed organization. What is known is that the man behind the BWA is a wrestling legend (it was in the press release, baby!) who is looking to take the BWA to the very pinnacle of the wrestling world. Other than that, nothing else is known.
OUT OF CHARACTER Who's the boss? That'd be me. Wayne. Handler for many years of Tommy Lionheart and current sensation Jamie Underwood. Former co-fedhead of PAW a couple of years ago.
I've been involved in E-W since late 1997. If you don't know who I am, you probably never will. :)
2) Television Shows The British Wrestling Alliance has inked a twelve month contract with The
Wrestling Channel (channel 432 on Sky Digital) to air their fortnightly
(bi-weekly) Saturday Night show LIVE from 8PM til 10PM. The BWA will travel up and down the country to many events ranging from the occasional football/soccer stadium to Ice Hockey and other sized locations to showcase their live television show.
SATURDAY NIGHTS (Twice a month) BWA SATURDAY NIGHT The show will consist, usually, of 5-6 matches. 3) Pay Per Views As for Pay Per Views, they too will be shown exclusively Live on The Wrestling
Channel. These will take place six times a year, meaning every other month, on a given Sunday night. More details on this at a later date.
4) Titles To start with there will be Three Championships in the BWA. They are: BWA National Championship - The Big One. BWA Commonwealth Championship - The Almost Big One. BWA Junior Heavy Championship - The Big One for the Smaller guys. (Under
215lbs) ** In time BWA may create a Tag Team Championship and Women's Championship if and when they see fit 5) BWA Personalities PLAY BY PLAY COMMENTATOR - Jerry Stone - An old veteran of wrestling play-by-play, Jerry Stone is reknowned as Britain's most accomplished wrestling commentator. He's getting up there in years, but he remains exciting, lovable, and most of all credible in his calls. His "catchphrase" is "Oh Good Lordy".
COLOR COMMENTATOR - Tony Green - Ex-bouncer Tony Green loves a good brawl. He's been breaking them up for over twenty years now. He prides himself on picking the "right side" of a fight, which usually means he'll praise the biggest man in the match. He's not "heel" or "face", he just loves commenting on fighting, period. He doesn't have any special catchphrases, though he is known for calling moves his own special names, as he doesn't know a wristlock from a wristwatch.
BACKSTAGE INTERVIEWER/REPORTER - "Inquisitive" Jake Allaway - Jake is young, he's energetic and above all else, he loves the sport. He loves being up close to the wrestlers and on occasion, his inexperience catches up with him. He'll ask heaps of questions. He wants to know everything. Sometimes his over-the-top ways can get on people's nerves. Rookie.
HEAD REFEREE - Michael Romford - Romford, or "Big Mike" as he's known in the business has been plying his trade as a referee for almost three decades. He's taken bumps but come back from them. He knows all the rules, he's strict, to the point and won't allow any B.S.
