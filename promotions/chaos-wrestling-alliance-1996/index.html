Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

CHAOS!!! Wrestling Alliance traces to at least March 1996 by its own broadcast's own account, and survived a shutdown that summer to relaunch that October as, in its own words, the same organization picking back up rather than a new one: “Same President, same announcers, same office.”

Its relaunch card, the Highway from Hell Tour's first episode, aired live from the CoreStates Spectrum in Philadelphia on 6 October 1996: six matches, opening the first round of a World Tag Team Championship tournament between the Powers of Rain and the HammerHeads, and between Anarchy and the Japanese Dragons.

That December its own real-world parent production company pitched a New Year's Eve interfederation supercard, Wrestle Wonderland, inviting other e-wrestlers and whole federations to enter on an open application, floating that the whole thing “MAY turn out to be a tournament... a 'World Cup of Wrestling'” and billing it as the promotion's own second pay-per-view.

Nothing after that recruitment pitch survives; no result of the New Year's Eve card, and no closure notice, is in the record.
