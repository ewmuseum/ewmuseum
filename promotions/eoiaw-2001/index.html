Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

End Of It All Wrestling announced itself on 13 April 2001, run entirely by e-mail, its first card originally slated simply for “May” and soon fixed to the 19th. It launched with four singles championships and, by deliberate choice, no tag division at all.

By 25 April its singles championship count had settled at three, its recruitment posts still insisting on no tag titles — “tag teams are crap” — and dangling an incentive for anyone who joined before the 19 May date: a place in an opening World Title Tournament.

Recruitment posts ran almost daily from mid-April into May, each carrying the same application form, several noting only a couple of tournament places left as the field filled. A post on 6 May set a hard deadline for new applications: Friday, 11 May.

That Friday, its founder posted the federation's end outright, in his own words: “This is the first e-fed I have decided to make and I thought it would be easy. It wasn't.” He announced he was abandoning EOIAW “for now,” that the first card “won't be occuring,” and apologized “for any inconvenience I have caused anyone.”

The announcement drew a run of replies from other e-fed operators on the newsgroup, several commiserating over how hard it is to build a roster from nothing; one asked, of EOIAW's span of not quite a month from founding to closure, “is that, like, a record or something?”

No card, roster, or result was ever produced. End Of It All Wrestling closed exactly as its own final post promised: before its first show ever ran.
