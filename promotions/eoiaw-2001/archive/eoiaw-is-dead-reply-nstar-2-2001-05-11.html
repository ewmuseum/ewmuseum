[detail withheld] wrote in message <
[detail withheld] >... >>Well not to blow my own horn here or anything, but I've been trying to >>advertise the living hell out of WFW (World Fanatic Wrestling) and had very >>little sucess from the start. > >Dude, my cousin and I ran XWW (which is now, more or less AVW) for about 7 >months. We had 5 handlers, no one wrote for us. We pretty much coulda said >"fuck it, ain't worth the time to mess with this shit"...and closed XWW. But, >we didn't. So, don't cry cause you don't get people to join your fed right off >the bat. You gotta win people over, and invest time into a fed if you really >want it to take off. Who said I was crying? Look, all I stated that it's very tough to gain people at the start and very hard to win them over. That much I knew from the start. I also know that
WFW would be considered shit by some people who saw it... that's just the way it goes. I can't open some fed and expect it to be WWF quality and for the most part thats what alot of people look for.
My job as a fed head is to keep pressing, work with what I have for wrestlers and build up to that level where I have to turn away guys from joining instead of begging them. As you said, bust my ass and keep working.
I will note as well that even with three wrestlers I made up guys for them to face and started writing cards.
If you had noticed the posts before, I had stated in "join me" postings that a card was wrote to show what a person can expect when joining, an RP packet was sent out, an FAQ and about sections on the web site were created.. all in an effort to let peopole see what WFW was about. It was made clear people could e-mail me and ask any questions that they had so they could find out more. A few did but really no one joined. Again, not that I am upset or crying about it, but I knew from the start that it was going to take more than a line of "join my fed" to get people to come.
But my point was that I didn't and haven't given up on running a red and making it the best I can possible make it becuase what I did was instead of folding my tent was merge my fed and the three guys I had with another fed that I was already familiar with but was struggling itself to be ontime and updated. I think that's called making the best of a bad pair of situations.
And I know that I still have to win people over, that PTW still has to prove itself and that will come with time. But unlike other feds I just didn't give up when things looked weak and things were slow.
It was just a matter of shameless self promotion.. that's all. N-Star -A sucker for self promotion.... http://ptw.i85.net
