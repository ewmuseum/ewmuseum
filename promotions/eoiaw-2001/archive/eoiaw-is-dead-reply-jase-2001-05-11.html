>Well not to blow my own horn here or anything, but I've been trying to >advertise the living hell out of WFW (World Fanatic Wrestling) and had very >little sucess from the start. Dude, my cousin and I ran XWW (which is now, more or less AVW) for about 7 months. We had 5 handlers, no one wrote for us. We pretty much coulda said
"fuck it, ain't worth the time to mess with this shit"...and closed XWW. But, we didn't. So, don't cry cause you don't get people to join your fed right off the bat. You gotta win people over, and invest time into a fed if you really want it to take off.
I didn't just say "Here's my fed...join" and people came running. I started with 5...busted my ass, and now I get _TOO_ many apps. It's a much better situation to be in...but, you really have to bust your ass in the beginning.
Too many guys think they'll just open a fed and people will run to it. Doesn't work that way.
Ah well...enough of my rammbling. Point is, getting members is a monumental task. Doesn't come easy.
