One more sad e-wrestling day

Mar 12, 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, it appears that the UEA, after a run of over 9 months is about to shut down. UCW is closing(Shawn M. please let me know if I'm wrong), LAW will prolly remain open as a stand-alone fed(I think, Spencer let me know if im wrong), and HOWF is already dead. Anyway, any current and former UEA members are invited to take part in the closing UEA ceremony, which will feature both 1997 and all-time awards. This voting is even open to Jimmy Clark, if he so wishes to vote, as he did provide some memorable, if not unforgettable moments. The UEA was prolly one of if not the best group of TNM feds anywhere. Along with all the normal categories, Im gonna add some humorous ones, as well as some HoF categories and the already mentioned UEA all-time votes. The ballot will be made later. Any former SCWF and ILOC members may vote, and of course HOWF, LAW and UCW members past and present can vote. Also, anyone wanting to get the awards it will be posted on the web page when it's completed and on RSPWF and e-mailed. If you want the email copy, just give me you e-mail address at [detail withheld]
