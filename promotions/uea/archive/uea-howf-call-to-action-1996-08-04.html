UEA-HOWF Call to Action

4 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens with Thomas "The Beer" Stein and Jerry Jenkins sitting at a control desk, monitors all around showing shots of various UEA members,
Thomas looks decidedly uncomfortable] Jerry Jenkins: Hi everyone and welcome to the HOWF's first call to action. Thomas Stein: What are we doing here anyway? JJ: As I'm sure HOWF President Jagermeister told you, we're making our first pitch for wrestlers to join the High Octane Wrestling Federation.
TS: That guy doesn't tell me anything, he just told me to show up tonight or I'd lose my job. JJ: I'd say he knows you very well Thomas. TS: Call me Aaron. JJ: We are not going through that again. Well I'm sure some of you remeber us from the recent Tuesday night LAW card, if not, just give [name withheld] an
e-mail at [detail withheld] , and he'd be happy to send you a copy of the card. TS: Was that the one were the Admiral, you know, where he went into the locker room (starts laughing), and Peterbilt (breaks off into howls of laughter).
JJ: Yes Thomas, that's the one. It's also the one where Admiral Clark lost to James Havoc.
TS: Well I see no need to send anyone that part of the card. JJ: Regardless of what you think, everyone is entitled to see all the action. TS: That wasn't action, that was a travesty of Justice. JJ: Well folks, the deal here is that the HOWF is starting up, we've got our motor revved up, and the High Octane action is ready to start. Would you care to give everyone some details on the HOWF Thomas?
TS: No, you do it. JJ: Somehow I'm not surprised. OK guys, the HOWF will be running three cards a week, Loudmouth Donnie will be running a Monday card, [name withheld]
will be running a Thursday card, and [name withheld] will be running a Saturday card. There will of course be a PPV every month, and in addition to that, the HOWF will be joining the UEA shortly after the first UEA PPV. In case you are unaware, the UEA is an alliance of 4 wrestling organizations, the
UCW, the LAW, the ILOC, and soon the HOWF, as well as possibly the SCWF. So Thomas, why don't you read what's on that monitor in front of you. TS: Why should I? JJ: If you don't you won't get paid. TS: The first 16 members to enter the HOWF will be entered in a Double elimination tournament to crown the first ever HOWF World Champion. The tournament will take place over the first three weeks of our existance, and will culminate in the championship match, which will take place on our inaugural PPV card. In addition to that there will be 2 other singles belts, and one tag title up for grabs.
JJ: Thank you Thomas, you do an admirable job when your balls are in a vice. So here's the deal, if you're interested, send [name withheld] an e-mail at
[detail withheld]
, and ask him to send you the rules for the HOWF, and a wrestler creation sheet. He will promptly do so, and you could be the next member of the HOWF. Thanks for listening, for Thomas "The Beer" Stein, I'm
Jerry Jenkins, saying so long, and hope to see you soon.
