OK, I have to step in here, because hell, the UEA rules ;). 1. Da J-Man (just friggin hilarious) 2. Robski (No one puts as much time into building a character as this guy) 3. Ed Salazar (When he's not ripping into people for their poor flashing skills, he writes up some classic rants) 4. Hardcore (He has the badass character down pat, plus he's the HOWF Henchman) 5. Payton Mack (Gotta love that kool-aid grinnin' phat talkin son of a gun) 6. Outcast Rob Cole (Demented and proud recipient of a 12 on 1 beatdown) 7. Striker Shirak (Just a little ego boost for yours truly. Currently an inactive wrestler, but I like to think he created a fair bit of heat.) 8. Professor Mike Janus (Just a really mean guy) 9. Retribution (Just a sick son of a bitch.) 10. Death Row (No one but Al could make a mute so damn interesting, I just wish he still had the time to participate) Honorable mentions: Diamond Brett Robbins, Masked Plague (gotta love the metal pooh), Fast Fighter, Red Raven, The Mosher, War Machine, Knightrous, and anyone else I've forgotten :).
