UEA's Top Ten! (a fan's own list)

21 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I guess I should give my own top 10. THe only order here is in the way the names popped into my head.
1. J-Man- like previously mentioned, he's hilarious, and to be the first inductee into the UEA Hall of Fame, he's gotta be a pretty damn good flasher. 2. Retribution- Any body who can lead an alliance of 20+ people must be awesome. 3. Ed Salazar- A word of advice- if you can't flash, don't mess with this guy. He'll tear you limb from limb and leave the reader of his flashes ROTF. 4. Striker/Tundra- The newest big boy in LAW. Sure one of them is currently near death if not dead, but, he drew tremendous heat, especially when he went insane.
5. Payton/Acey Mack- I know more about the Mack family from a few of his flashes than I know about some people in the UEA. 6. Robski/Aravinda- I'm not too familiar with Robski, since I'm not in the UCW, but I know that Aravinda is done extremely well (except for the fact that he's in the Underground, which makes NO sense to me whatsoever.)
7. Diamond Brett Robbins- There's nothing really to the guy, but he's been in LAW a long time and is still capable of drawing good heat and getting title shots. 8. War Machine- I'm always a sucker for the badass character gimmick. 9. Outcast Rob Cole- Any man who's objective is to single handedly take down a 20 man alliance by himself and yet, be quite successful at it definitely deserves props.
10. Red Raven- His UEA News show is something I look forward to all the time. Gotta love them rumors. Honorable mention: Zodiac, Doug Bacon, Masked Plague, Mosher, Professor
Janus/Big Jim/Matt Vortex, and a whole lot of other people who didn't currently come to mind since its 1:30 AM.
