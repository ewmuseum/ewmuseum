So long UEA, it's been one helluva ride. Coming sometime after the Awards show, the official UEA Hall of Fame page. This page looks like crap with the AOL browser. What was the UEA? UEA stood for the United E-Wrestling Alliance. The UEA was made up of three feds, Live Action Wrestling(LAW), Ultimate Championship Wrestling(UCW), and the High Octane Wrestling Federation(HOWF). Each fed had its own titles, its own cards, and governed itself. So, what did the UEA do for all these feds? Well, alot of things. 1, it provided unified titles(a singles, a cruiser, and a tag team) that anyone from any UEA fed could have won. Also, many handlers had a wrestler in each fed. The UEA also promoted inter-fed wrestling, especially for Battle Royals, Survivor Series, and for takeover angles.
