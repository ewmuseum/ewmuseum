Halloween Horror: A Cage of Horrors Preview

27 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
 HANDLER NOTE: I know most people don't care about other feds. But I am trying to get feedback on how to improve my flashes.
just excuse the typos. Post a reply on how to improve, thanks Jay (The house is dark except for a flickering light. The cameraman moves closer to the window to see what the flickering light is. The curtains part, the camera jumps back a bit, a little startled. The camera moves in. He sees a figure moving about with the candle. The figure places the candle on a table. The candle is next to a pumpkin. The figure is starting to carve the pumpkin.
Cameraman: Who the hell is that? (Suddenly the candle flickers out. The door swings open. We hear a voice) Voice: Trick or Treat HOWF Cameraman and I hope you choose right! Cameraman: <a little shakily> Treat? Voice: Then come on in, young man. Don't be afraid. Come look at the horrors of Halloween!
(The cameraman walks in. The only light that is on is from the camera. the cameraman slowly walks through)
Voice: Stop, look at this. (The camera peers over and sees a pot with a human head in it. The cameraman jumps and starts to head toward the way he came in. he is stopped by something in his way. A huge Frankenstien!)
Cameraman: HOYL FUCKING SHIT! GET ME OUT OF HERE! (The cameraman runs and stops short. He stepped in something soft and gooey! He looks down and sees a human liver! He then starts running foward toward the exit. He runs right into a wall. The camera light goes out, but the camera is still running. The room turns into a graveyard. The room is lit up all of a sudden by a strobe light.)
Cameraman: OH MY GOD, SOMEONE HELP ME! THEY'RE GONNA KILL ME! (All of a sudden, hands starting coming through the "ground" from the graves. the cameraman faints) Voice: You know, I think we scared him a little too much! Maybe we better turn on the lights!
(The lights come on to reveal a Haunted House. There are some people standing there. We have never seen most of these people before. But Frankenstein comes into view. It is Gryphon, Red Raven's manager in LAW and UCW. The voice from before comes over the speakers)
Voice: Is he gonna be ok? Maybe I better come down from the sound booth! (There is a slight silence. Then Gryphon speaks) Gryphon: Yeah, you'd better Red! It seems he pissed his pants also! (The group laughs. There is a pause, and suddenly Red Raven appears with a microphone in his hand)
Red Raven: Someone get some water from the 'human head' in the pot. Splash it on his face to wake him up!
(The man leaves the group. A few seconds later, he returns with a glass of water. He splashes it in the face of the cameraman. The cameraman wakes up and looks around the room)
Cameraman: Gryphon? Red Raven? What's going on here? Gryphon: You were in our annual Haunted House! Were you scared? <Gryphon looks down at the cameramans soild pants and laughs> I guess so!
(the cameraman looks to see what Gryphon was looking at!) Cameraman: <blushing> Ohh, hehehe, I guess I was a little bit! Red Raven: I know you came to interview me about the Cage of Horrors Match, but we decided to have fun. Are you ready for the interview?
Cameraman: Maybe we should go into another room? RR: Ok, sure. <To Gryphon> You go ahead without me, we'll be back for the unveiling! (Gryphon nods and leads the actors back to their sets. RR and the cameraman go into a separate room!)
RR: Ok, shoot, what's on your mind? C: Well, the first day of the Big Melee' has passed. You put in a tremendous showing. You were in the final three with the two other HOWF participants. You held the Figure-Four leglock on Nuclear Fallout for over 5 minutes. Not even
Ric Flair has done that much damage with that move. Right after that, you got tossed by the winner Payton Mack.
RR: Let's clear that up right now, I didn't get tossed. I was over anxious. I had him reeling and I ran right toward him. He had a natural instinct and back body-dropped me. Unfortunately, we were too close to the ropes. That is why he was the National Champ. I lack the experience, but that was a good learning one. Next time, I won't make the same mistake!
C: Do you think you had the chance to win if you didn't make that mistake? RR: yes I do. In all respect to Mr. Mack, I think I would be facing Big Jim after I beat Zero in Day 2. I took out Nuclear Fallout. He didn't have a leg to stand on, literally. (Red Raven chuckles to himself) However, that is how the world turns. I congratulate Mr. Mack and wish him all the luck in the world. I would also like to correct Professor Mike Janus. Mr. Janus, you stated that Mike Higgins and Fast Fighter were part of the Brigade of Freedom.
You either missed the fact or didn't know that I am also part of the BOF! C: Let's move on to Halloween Horror! (Red Raven's face went from seriousness to a smile) RR: Well, I think basing my momentum from the Big Melee', I have one of the best chances of winning the Cage of Horrors. That would be my first title shot in HOWF IF I win. I am looking forward to locking up with my opponents. BTW,
Knightrous, it was a fine match. If I happen to win the National Title, Knightrous, you are GUARENTEED the first shot! (We hear some screams in the background.) C: What's gonna happen in Day 2, you got Zero! RR: Me and him are gonna rumble. He bashed my face in the pavement. He beat me in a match, but I am gonna win the war!
(Suddenly the lights go black) C: Oh shit, not again! RR: Don't worry! (Gryphon's voice comes over through the whole building) GRYPHON: I'D LIKE TO INTRODUCE THE MAN WHO MADE THIS ALL POSSIBLE, THE ONE, THE ONLY, RRRREEEEEEDDDDDDD RRRRAAAAAVVVVVVEEEEEEENNNNNNNNN! (Red Raven and the cameraman go out of the room into the lobby. There is a huge crowd cheering)
RR: Thank you all for coming. This benefits a good cause. It is to help injured and disable former wrestlers such as Striker Shirak and Magnum T.A. We have one thing left to do and we all know what that is!
(The crowd starts popping loudly) RR: Let's unveil the Jack-o-Lantern! (The room dimmes and a drum roll sounds. The curtain is lifted. There is a a jack-o-lantern with a steel cage over it. Carved on it is the faces of the five particpants in the Cage of Horrors Match. Above them is a picture of the
National Title)
