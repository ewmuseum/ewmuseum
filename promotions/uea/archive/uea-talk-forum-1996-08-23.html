The UEA Talk Forum, You Have to See It!!

23 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Go to the UEA Talk Forum. It is the place to talk about e-wrestling. Although it is based on the alliance of four federations (LAW,UCW,HOWF,SCWF) together, you can go there and respond to the comments made by the UEA Wrestlers and Executives. You can keep up to date on the happenings of the greatest Federation Alliance in the e-wrestling world. You can talk about other activities in other e-federations if you want. It is a OPEN FORUM. The script it is run on is GREAT. Some might reconize it from what SCOOPS using on their webpage.
This UEA Talk Forum, will soon become the Forum where e-wrestlers talk about their wrestlers and find out about the UEA. It will be mostly
UEA Talk, but as I said before.. be free to participate. Trust me, you will enjoy it.
So, what are you waiting for? Oh yeah, you need the URL... well through the LAW Homepage you can link to the UEA Talk Forum.
LAW Homepage = http://www.geocities.com/TimesSquare/4889 Also, stay tuned for the AMAZING UEA Tournament that will be held this
Saturday. An eight man singles tournament and 8 team tag tournament will crown the UEA Champion and UEA Tag Champion.
See you on the FORUM!. [name withheld]
