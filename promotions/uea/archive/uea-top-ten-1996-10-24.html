UEA's Top Ten! (a writer's own picks)

24 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
at the behest of one guy, i am gonna vote for my faves. i do Ed Salazar in
LAW and am notorious for slamming crappy flashes -- gotta love Ed though.
my votes. 1) Hardcore -- THE badass of UEA. one of the only guys who seems to really carry off being a badass effortlessly and without coming off as "ugh, i'm gonna kick your ass"-style flashes. really knows how to bring off a character.
2) J-Man -- Fucking hilarious roleplays every time and he always has a creative way to say he can beat your ass. Definitely knows how to put a wrestler in his place too.
3) James Havok/Rob Cole/Gravel -- played by the same guy and all good characters, even if they're slight spins on each other. Mark, who plays them, is really great at portraying a range of emotions and rage in his roleplays. when his characters are upset or pissed, you know it.
4) Tundra -- really good roleplays and complex storylines makes this character and the owner's previous guy, Striker Shirak, really enjoyable to watch. 5) me.... just kidding. War Machine, who is really fun to watch. 6) Doug Bacon -- this character bored me at first but he's gotten heaps better and one can't help but check out what the champ has to say. 7) Brett Robbins -- no particular reason. 8) Zodiac -- hey, he has midgets. need i say more? =-) 9) Robski/Aravinda/Dave Bluenoze -- Ed feuded a bit with him in the XWC before i started booking there. a good roleplayer, though i thought he was better in the XWC.
10) Angel of Death -- if only he'd flash more ;) AoD is kinda one of those dark characters, cool and confident and you always know what he thinks. HONORABLE MENTION: "Showstopper" Simon Lebec -- who isn't in the UEA but gave props to one of my other guys, Kuwasi Shakur. I've been in many feds and, in my book, there's only one dirtiest player in the e-wrestling game and it's Lebec.
sleazy attacks, cheapshots and some good roleplays. Lebec and Shakur had ugly wars in the BCWF and XWC so much so that Lebec got himself a butler to watch him and Ed came in as Shakur's "security consultant" in the BCWF. too bad Robert, who has Lebec isn't in the UEA. =-)
frank
