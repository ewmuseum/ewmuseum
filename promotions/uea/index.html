Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The United E-Wrestling Alliance joined five federations under one banner in the summer of 1996 — Live Action Wrestling, Ultimate Championship Wrestling, the High Octane Wrestling Federation, the Internet League of Champions and a Supreme Cyber Wrestling Federation of its own — sharing a talk forum, a joint ranking board and joint super-shows across the member feds' own rosters. The Internet League of Champions had already closed by the time the alliance ran its first supercard.

That first supercard, on 24 August 1996, crowned the alliance's own first champions: an eight-team tag tournament went to War Machine and Zodiac, wrestling as Revelations, who had lost to the Beasts only three days earlier and been attacked by their own teammate, Striker Shirak, in between; and an eight-man singles tournament crowned Agaguk the first UEA Champion over Retribution.

The alliance's own community stayed active through the fall: a Halloween Horror preview that October staged an entire haunted-house skit around Red Raven and his manager Gryphon ahead of a Cage of Horrors match, raising money along the way for injured and retired wrestlers including Striker Shirak and Magnum T.A. The following February, a joint UEA/UCW card gave Red Raven himself a retirement tribute — a final match, a look back at his own best match by his own pick, and a farewell speech delivered in front of a sold-out New Mexico crowd.

After a run of more than nine months, the alliance's own fedheads announced its close: UCW shutting down, LAW carrying on alone outside the alliance, and HOWF already gone. Former members across all five federations were invited into a closing ceremony built around a two-part ballot — a slate of 1997 awards and an all-time, career-spanning set of Hall of Fame categories — open even to members the alliance's own posters remembered less fondly, “if he so wishes to vote.”

How it ended isn't stated beyond the ballot itself. A final call for votes survives into the following spring, but no announcement of the results, or any later document, is held.
