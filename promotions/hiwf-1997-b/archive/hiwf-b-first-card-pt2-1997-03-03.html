HIWF First Card: Inaugural Battle Royal (Part 2 of 2)

3 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (When last we saw our intrepid wrestlers, they were standing in the ring staring at each other, waiting to hear a bell go... ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ *DINGDINGDING* (At this point, all fifteen men tear into each other like starved animals who have just been let loose in a meat store. General chaos fills the ring for a minute or so, then suddenly a blast of static drowns out any noise in the arena.) *PSSSHSHHHHHHSHHHHT!* [Davis] (over the loudspeaker) Testing 1-2-3... (All fifteen wrestlers stop and look up, as does most of the audience.) [Silverman] (in the background) You dolt, that's not the television feed. That's the loudspeaker. [Davis] (still over the loudspeaker) Oh, sorry. Carry on, gentlemen. (And they do.) =) [Davis] *psssshhhhhhht* ... think I got it right now. Yep, it's working. [Silverman] About time too... kind of hard to do a play by play if you mess up the wiring. [Davis] I was trying to clean the coffee up. [Silverman] That's enough of that Mike... there's a match going on in the ring! [Davis] I know that... at the moment, it's a huge crowd of flying fists and elbows, and I can't even see those Delgado twerps in there. [Silverman] You think I should tell them you called them that? [Davis] Not really. [Silverman] I thought not. Things seem to be breaking up into groups now... Blaze, The Dark Knight, and Ricky Johnson have all decided to gang up on the mammoth Highlander, while Killer Instinct and Obscuridad and going at it tooth and nail in the center of the ring. [Davis] (excitedly) I found the Delgados! They're both pounding on the Stormtrooper of Death, and are taking occasional shots at Hellraiser and the Megalomaniac, who are nearby. [Silverman] Yes they are and... LOOK! Highlander just grabbed the Dark Knight by the throat and pressed him over his head! [Davis] Wow, that's a big boy. He looks like he's about to throw the Knight out and... [Silverman] ... he gets his leg clipped out from beneath him by Jonney Gold, who had been in the corner this entire time! The Knight is down, but not out, and Jonney Gold is desperately trying to remove himself from under the Highlander. [Davis] And it looks like DOA is about to help him, as he's picking the Highlander up by the hair and trying single-handedly to heave him over the top rope! [Silverman] That doesn't work, as HE gets blindsided as well by Khan and the Strangler, who had been exchanging headbutts in another corner. In the meantime, Franky Delgado is whipped into the ropes, and met with a HUGE back-body drop by the Stormtrooper! It looks like he's up and over the top rope... [Davis] Nope, he grabbed it, and is standing on the outside apron now... hey, what's his brother doing with him? [Silverman] A SLINGSHOT by the Delgados, as Ricky catapulted his brother back into the ring! Franky flew right onto the top of a pile of people, knocking them ALL down into a big heap in the middle of the ring! [Davis] His brother obviously doesn't understand how battle royals work. He should have pushed him out. [Silverman] Where's your heart, man? They're brothers! [Davis] So? [Silverman] *sighing* Back to the action, the newcomer Highlander seems to be thoroughly enjoying himself in there, just randomly bashing heads. [Davis] He's obviously trained in the Scottish Martial Art of "Scru-Yu", which involves headbutting people a lot until they fall down, then kicking them. [Silverman] VERY funny. You must think that... OOHH!!! The Delgados have both just double-clotheslined the Stormtrooper of Death out of the ring, over the top rope! We have our first elimination! [Davis] Oh, he's not happy about that. But it's the first time out, there's still plenty of time for him to make up for lost face here. [Silverman] That may be the smartest thing I've heard you say all night. Right now, however, the Delgados are facing off against another twosome here, Blaze and Hellraiser, as all four are exchanging blows. [Davis] Not for long! Killer Instinct just nailed both Delgados from behind with a double clothesline, DOA just pegged Blaze in the head with a savatte kick, and the Megalomaniac CLOBBERED Hellraiser with a double axe-handle. [Silverman] However, it seems like the Dark Knight's getting the short end of the straw in the far corner! Obscuridad's choking him out against the top turnbuckle with... it looks like a piece of cable! Where'd he get THAT? [Davis] I don't know, but it seems to be having the desired effect against the Dark Knight, who may be going "Good Night" if he's choked any longer. [Silverman] BEHIND THEM, IT'S... [Davis] (at the same time, shouting towards the ring) LOOK OUT!!! (Highlander comes stampeding across the ring, barrelling over Ricky Johnson, Jonney Gold, and the Megalomaniac, and SLAMS into the Knight and Obscuridad, sending all THREE over the top rope and onto a huge heap on the floor outside. He then stands up and brushes himself off, laughs, and strides to the back, leaving the other two lying there.) [Davis] I... don't... BELIEVE he DID that! [Silverman] Neither can I, but he did, and now there are 11 men left in the ring! [Davis] Ten! Killer Instinct has just picked up the prone body of the Megalomaniac, and HEAVED him over the top! This guy wasn't kidding when he said he meant business! [Silverman] One of these ten men will be receiving a bye into the second round of the HIWF World Championship tournament! Any predictions, Mike? [Davis] I'm going to have to put my money on Killer Instinct. This man isn't messing around at ALL. [Silverman] Not a bad choice, seeing as how at this moment he's working over Richie "Baby Taz" Delgado with some stiff punches to the head! Franky is trying to get over there, but is double-teamed by Blaze and Hellraiser, who quickly take him down to the mat with a team-shoulderblock. [Davis] Good strategy by those two... keep the brothers apart, and there won't be as mch double-teaming. [Silverman] They DID double-team, Mike. [Davis] Details. [Silverman] Well, Khan doesn't seem too concerned with that, as he just walked up to BOTH of them and started laying into them with punches and kicks... [Davis] ... and is met with one of for himself, courtesy of DOA! That one got him right in the chin, and lifted him about a foot off the mat before gravity kicked in. [Silverman] Meanwhile, Ricky Johnson and Jonney Gold are toe-to-toe, and here comes the Strangler! [Davis] Good strategy by the Siberian Strangler, just watching from the sides until he could sneak up on folks. And with one strong shoulder tackle, Gold and Johnson are both KO'd! [Silverman] That's two more gone, folks, but I don't think they care... they're still going at it outside the ring! [Davis] Someone get security down there... back to the match, though. Khan has just groggily pulled himself to one knee, and is rubbing his jaw. That kick by DOA nearly took his head off. [Silverman] It almost did, Mike. And here comes Blaze, grabbing Khan by the hair... [Davis] And there's Khan, grabbing Blaze by the tights and slingshotting him out of the ring! Great leverage move by Khan! [Silverman] Unfortunately for Khan, Blaze went between the second and third ropes, and isn't eliminated yet. [Davis] Khan's on his feet now, but he doesn't see Blaze crawling back into the ring behind him! He must think he eliminated him! [Silverman] A quick nod of the head by Blaze to Hellraiser, and Hellraiser's running off the opposite ropes and launching himself into a dropkick at Khan's head! [Davis] And Blaze is launching HIMSELF into a shoulder tackle at the back of Khan's knees! Khan just did a 360 as they both hit him! [Silverman] You were saying about double-teaming? [Davis] (ignoring him) And Killer Instinct's getting rid of the trash, picking Khan up and dumping him over the top rope like yesterday's garbage! [Silverman] Another out, and Blaze and Hellraiser don't look thrilled about that one. They feel it was THEIR work, and they should get the credit for it. [Davis] Too late now, crybabies. [Silverman] The Delgados have in the meantime mamaged to regroup, and are both working over the Strangler. They've just about got him over the top.... [Davis] ... and there he goes! But he's got a deathgrip on Richie's hair, he's not going out without a fight! [Silverman] And here comes Killer Instinct again! He must smell a kill in the air. He's off the opposite ropes, and is heading straight for them! [Davis] Blaze just whipped DOA into the OTHER ropes! They don't see each other! (collective OOH goes up from the crowd as DOA and Killer Instinct slam into each other) [Silverman] (wincing) That had to hurt. [Davis] No, but Blaze and Hellraiser saw what the Instinct was going after! They're pouncing on the Delgados, who are still tied up with the Strangler! [Silverman] Hellraiser just helped Richie out, sending him sprawling on top of the Strangler on the floor! And two more go down! [Davis] We're down to five! Blaze and Hellraiser are pounding on Franky Delgado, and DOA and Killer Instinct are getting up from their slight meeting in the middle of the ring! [Silverman] DOA's the first one up, and he charges at Blaze, clotheslining him over the top in a spectacular flip! Four people left! [Davis] And Killer Instinct has finally gotten fully to his feet! He's leaning against the far turnbuckle, trying to catch a breath, and letting the others beat the pulp out of each other! Good move, Killer! [Silverman] I'll have to agree with you there, Mike... he's resting a bit, and that'll make a person even MORE dangerous in this type of match. [Davis] And now Hellraiser has thrown Franky off of him, sending him sprawling on the mat by the corner. He seems to be taunting him... [Silverman] And DOA grabs him from behind into a Samoan Drop! NEVER turn your back on this guy! [Davis] I don't plan to. He's twice my size. [Silverman] I don't mean... oh, nevermind. [Davis] (jumps up and shout something, but his headphones fall off in the scramble.) [Silverman] (hands the headphones back to Davis) Yes, that WAS a great clothesline by Delgado, who just POUNCED on Killer Instinct out of nowhere. I guess they DIDN'T forget about him. Now sit down, and settle down. These headphones are expensive. [Davis] Sorry. [Silverman] Just don't let it happen again. We're down to four wrestlers, folks... Franky Delgado, Killer Instinct, Hellraiser, and DOA. Delgado and Killer Instinct are POUNDING on each other, and ellraiser and DOA are doind likewise on the other side of the ring. [Davis] There's a whip by Killer Instinct, sending Delgado right at the others! [Silverman] REVERSED by Delgado! Hellraiser hits the deck at the last minute, and Killer Instinct and DOA BOTH go toppling over the top rope! We're down to our final two! [Davis] Damn! There goes my pick! [Silverman] Either Hellraiser or Franky "Puerto Rican Ice" Delgado will be getting a bye into the second round of the tournament! They're circling each other now, trying to measure each other up... [Davis] (standing, more slowly this time, and shouting) GET ON WITH IT! [Silverman] Sit DOWN. [Davis] But... [Silverman] NOW. [Davis] (sheepishly) OK. [Silverman] And Delgado and Hellraiser lock up! [Davis] Hellraiser's at a distinct disadvantage here. Delgado's got almost a half a foot on him, and a couple of dozen pounds. [Silverman] Yes, but that doesn't stop Hellraiser from doing moves like that dropkick there! Delgado's on his back, stunned! [Davis] GET 'IM!!! [Silverman] *sigh* You just don't learn, do you? [Davis] Hellraiser's picking Delgado up, and whipping him into the ropes... spinning kick! Delgado's down again! [Silverman] Well, Delgado's been on the wrong end of quite a few people tonight, he's obviously worn out. [Davis] Probably, but he's up again. Points for courage, Delgado, none for brains. [Silverman] Be quiet. Hellraiser's backing Delgado into the corner... vicious chops! That's going to sting for a few days. [Davis] And a whip by Hellraiser... [Silverman] ... reversed... [Davis] Reversed again! Delgado's into the turnbuckle... AND BACK OUT WITH A CLOTHESLINE! Hellraiser is laid out! [Silverman] Oh boy, these two are REALLY going at it. [Davis] Thank you, Doctor Obvious. Just call the match, and leave the tough conclusions to the folks in the crowd. [Silverman] *grumble* [Davis] I thought so. Delgado's picking Hellraiser up now... another whip into the buckle, but Hellraiser hopped to the top rope! That's a dangerous place to be in a battle royal! [Silverman] Delgado's charging in... Hellraiser with a backflip over him, onto his feet! And a DROPKICK to Delgado's back that smashes him chest-first into the turnbuckle! [Davis] Delgado stagggers backwards out... RUDE AWAKENING by Hellraiser! This could be it, folks! [Silverman] Hellraiser to the top again... there's the moonsault! Delgado has GOT to be out after that one! [Davis] Hellraiser's picking Delgado up, and trying to heave him out, but he's too heavy. Hellraiser's just not big enough! [Silverman] Looks like he's going to try a different approach to it.. off the opposite ropes... [Davis] And met with a kick to the stomach by Delgado! Hellraiser's doubled over, and Delgado looks like he's setting him up for a power bomb! [Silverman] Oh, this could REALLY be it. [Davis] You just said that about the OTHER guy. [Silverman] He's got Hellraiser up for a power bomb... Hellraiser throws all his weight back, into a headscissors! He's flipped Delgado out! We have our winner!!! [Davis] And HELLRAISER wins the Battle Royal, earning the bye into the second round of the tournament! A great match, by ALL our wrestlers! [Silverman] That's going to wrap it up from here, folks! Until next time, I'm Jason Silverman... [Davis] ... and I'm Mike "Technical Difficulties" Davis... [Silverman] ... and we'll see you next week with the first round matches of the tournament! (fade to black) -- --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Never, EVER taunt Happy Fun Ball.
