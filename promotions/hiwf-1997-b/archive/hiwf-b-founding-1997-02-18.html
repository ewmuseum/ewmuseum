HIWF (Feb-Mar 1997) Card Archive

High Impact Wrestling Federation: founding advertisement

18 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------ T H E H I G H I M P A C T W R E S T L I N G F E D E R A T I O N (or the HIWF for those people who like acronyms) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Hey there. Well, you're looking at this, so I guess it sort of means I got your attention. I'm not going to go about bragging about how this is the best league ever, or how it kicks other leagues' @$$es, or anything else like that because, well... the league doesn't exist yet. That's why I'm posting this. =) What I WANT to do is create a league that can contend with the best of them in terms of action, roleplay, and all-around enjoyment. With a bit of help, I think that's entirely possible. OK, the intro's out of the way. Now comes the neat important stuff. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ T H E I M P O R T A N T S T U F F (See? Told you.) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ For the time being, I will be accepting 16 wrestlers into the league. One wrestler per handler, no tag teams as of yet. I'd like to get the singles department underway before I start throwing tag-teams into the mix. There are a few limits on things I'd allow in, so please pay close attention. 1. NO REAL WRESTLERS. 2. NO COPIES OF REAL WRESTLERS. I don't want 25,000 Ric Flair wannabes running around my league. Be original, be creative. Make up your OWN personality. 3. BE SERIOUS. One league I was in once, there was a guy called "The Caveman", who was 7'8", 400+ pounds, and looked and acted like Fred Flintstone. I do NOT want this. No. Uh-uh. No way. The character sheet I'd ask you to fill out is at the end of this post, so you'll know what to send me. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ R U L E S O F T H E L E A G U E ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ This is a BOOKED league. The better your roleplay, the better the chance your character will win. Here's a chart to show relative roleplay values. worst --> 0 never send anything 1 "Grog smash!" . . 5 Occasional flash, responds to others . . best --> 9 Active in sending flashes, strategies, and responses to others. I do NOT want "War and Peace" showing up in my e-mail account, however. Try to limit flashes to a page or two, with a minimum of perhaps a paragraph. Shows will be weekly (probably Sundays), with smaller events happening in-between, as well as flashes being sent out pretty much daily (or as I receive them). A major show will happen every month or so, and will be announced on the Sunday shows. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ C H A R A C T E R S T U F F ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Here's what I'd need from you, in order to get an idea of how your character will work. PLAYER NAME: Who's behind the wrestler? PLAYER E-MAIL: So I can send you stuff. CHARACTER NAME: What's he/she called? HAILS FROM: Where's he come from? ENTRANCE MUSIC: Music that plays when he/she comes in. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION: Height, weight, appearance, clothing, etc. ALIGNMENT: Face? Heel? Equal opportunity destroyer? STYLE: Is he a high-flying luchador, or a lumbering mountain of a bruiser? Technical? Mat worker? Spray-on-kung-fu maniac? BASIC MOVES: Give me 10 moves your character normally uses. ** NOTE ** Do not send me a 150 pound high-flyer with moves like "gorilla press" and "Torture rack", because I WILL let your character try them, and it won't be my fault when he snaps in half trying to pick the other guy up. =) FINISHER: The big gun. Your put-down move. Give me a name for it, as well as a description. MANAGER: Do you have one? if so, describe him/her. OTHER STUFF: Tell me a bit about your character... why they're wrestling, how they act, etc. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Well, that's about it. Please send characters, questions, etc. to: [detail withheld] (that's me!) Hope to hear from you soon! [name withheld] [detail withheld] -- --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Never, EVER taunt Happy Fun Ball.
