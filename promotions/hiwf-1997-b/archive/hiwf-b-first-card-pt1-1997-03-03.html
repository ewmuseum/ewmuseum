HIWF First Card: Inaugural Battle Royal (Part 1 of 2)

3 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------ T H E H I G H I M P A C T W R E S T L I N G F E D E R A T I O N I N A U G U R A L B A T T L E R O Y A L ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (spiffier title screen coming soon) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (Cameras fade in on a crowded arena, the one ring in the center highlighted by multicolored spotlights. Two men are seated behind an announcer's table nearby, and seem to be joking cordially with one another, oblivious to the fact that the camera is zooming in on them.) [Silverman] (to the other man) ... so he says to the guy, "Hey! That's not a duck! That's my wife!" Get it? [Davis] (sipping his coffee) I swear, Jason, your jokes get worse every day. One of these days I'm... hey, what's that? [Silverman] What's what? [Davis] (pointing towards the control panel in front of them) The little red flashing light there. [Silverman] Red flashing... HOLY SMOKES!!! WE'RE ON THE AIR! [Davis] (sputtering and spilling his coffee in what can only be described as an uncomfortable location) What?!?! (Both men throw their headsets on and try to look dignified.) [Silverman] Good evening, everyone, and welcome to the first EVER card of the High Impact Wrestling Federation! My name is Jason Silverman and this is my partner... (Motions to Mike Davis, who is squirming uncomfortably in his now very coffee-filled seat) [Silverman] ... Mike "Coffeeboy" Davis. *chuckle* [Davis] Oh, shut your trap, Silverman. You would have spilled it yourself if _I_ had surprised you with the little fact that we were broadcasting. It was your job to stay on top of that anyway. [Silverman] Whatever. Tonight we have a very important event in determining the immediate future of the HIWF- a 15-man battle royal, with the winner receiving a bye to the second round of the World Championship Tournament. [Davis] That's right... the original plan was for the top three to advance, but an oversight by the brain surgeons in the front office nearly excluded two fine grapplers from tonight's card. Boy that Rossel's a real... [Silverman] (cutting him off) ... nice guy who signs your paycheck. [Davis] (thinking about this for a moment) Oh yeah. That's right. [Silverman] Well, we're counting down now to the entrance of our competitors... we've received a last-minute mesage from a certain Damon Oscar Avonoiaa... [Davis] (interrupting) ... also known as DOA! [Silverman] Thank you, Mike. Here's what he- actually, his manager- had to say. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ (Scene..a state of the art training room in New Orleans, LA, headquarters and home offices of Stone Cold Enterprises....Vanessa Stone watches a ring as DOA obliterates three men in a light workout) [Stone] Battle Royal, huh? HIWF, why don't you do the smart thing now and just HAND D.O.A. the belt? D.O.A. was honed for this kind of battle in Samoa, in fights with his 11 brothers over a drumstick at the family meal. You're looking at over 300 lbs of force and destruction...with no distractions and nothing on his mind except maiming the 12 other competitors involved in all of this. We don't care who it is..it could be that Johnson milquetoast....it could be that Russian dweeb with the thick accent and the thicker *ss, it could even be that reject from the Holiday Inn, Jonney Gold. The only Jonney we're concerned about is the one we're gonna flush everyone else down as we advance to the HIWF Championship. Everyone betteer wear their winter parks, cause it's gonna be a Stone Cold evening. Later. (Stone turns her attention to the ring as D.O.A flattens a guy with a savatte kick as the camera fades) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ [Silverman] Wow... SHE looks like she knows what she wants in this league. [Davis] She can't. She hasn't even SEEN me yet. [Silverman] *sigh* And you're humble too, Mike. [Davis] Why, thank you. [Silverman] Well, it's time to get this show on the road... our ring announcer, Joey "Tonsils" MacGruber is about to start with the introductions. Let's get down to ringside! (Scene cuts to a small balding man in a tuxedo by ringside, but not before we hear Mike Davis mutter...) [Davis] Damn, that was some HOT coffee... ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ [Tonsils] Good evening, everyone, and WELCOME to the High Impact Wrestling Federation's very first card! Please refrain from throwing items into the ring at all times, except in the off chance that a wrestler deliberately asks for it. (Crowd breaks into wild applause at this announcement.) [Tonsils] However, I am pretty sure that you all didn't come out here to see a short guy in a monkey suit talk to you... here are the participants for tonight's 15-man battle royal! ("Tonight, Tonight" by the Smashing Pumpkins starts to play over the loudspeakers, as a tall man garbed mostly in black makes his way to the ring.) [Tonsils] Introducing first, from London, England... the DARK KNIGHT! (The music fades into a faster beat, as a well-built man in camoflage face-paint and fatigues with a black bandana strides out.) [Tonsils] Next, from New York City... KILLER INSTINCT! (The theme to "Jesus Christ, Superstar" now begins to play, and a gold-clad wrestler struts out.) [Tonsils] From Golden Coast, California... JONNEY GOLD! ("The Distance" by Cake now vlares over the speakers, and a man in red pants with black hair jogs out.) [Tonsils] Hailing from Hartford, Connecticut... RICKY JOHNSON! (The beat quickens into Tito Nieves' "I Like it Like That", and a man clad in tights adorned by the Puerto Rican flag comes out.) [Tonsils] From patillas, Puerto Rico... FRANKY "PUERTO RICAN ICE" DELGADO! (Offspring's "Bad Habit" drowns out the previous song, and a man dressed similarly to the prior one makes his way down the aisle.) [Tonsils] And his brother, also from Patillas, Puerto Rico... RICHIE "BABY TAZ" DELGADO! (The chords of Metallica's "Creeping Death" now sound throughout the arena, and another black-clad man comes to the ring.) [Tonsils] Hailing from Chicago, Illinois... THE STORMTROOPER OF DEATH! (These notes blur into the equally heavy "Be Quick or be Dead" by iron maiden, and a smaller man in tights adorned with flames jogs out.) [Tonsils] From Brooklyn, New York... HELLRAISER! (Another Iron Maiden song, "Judas, Be my Guide", plays now, and a second smaller man in flaming tights comes out.) [Tonsils] And from The Bronx, New York... BLAZE! (Marilyn Manson's "Sweet Dreams" grinds out, and a giant of a man in black jeans and a muscle shirt lumbers out of the dressing room followed closely by a smaller man with a kendo stick.) [Tonsils] From parts unknown, accompanied by his manager, Barnabas... OBSCURIDAD! (In a sudden change of pace, Tchaikovsky's "The Sick Doll" plays out as a burly Russian in a Soviet military garb-looking outfit strides out with an attractive blonde woman.) [Tonsils] From Nugeen... Nagenn... from Russia, accompanied by his manager, "Lucky Lucy"... THE SIBERIAN STRANGLER! (Guns and Roses' "Live and Let Die" plays over the loudspeakers as a large Samoan man with DOA on his tights comes to the ring with a young woman in a red dress.) [Tonsils] Accompanied by his manager, Miss Vanessa Stone, and hailing from the island of Samoa... DAMON OSCAR AVONOIAA, also known as DOA! (Black Sabbath's "Iron Man" now blares out as a tall muscular man in black trunks comes out.) [Tonsils] (doing a doubletake at the announcer's sheet) From... "the darkest corner of your mind"... KHAN! ("Iron Man" fades into "Megalomania" as a man in a trenchcoat with long brownhair and face paint comes to the ring.) [Tonsils] One of our newest competitors, from parts unknown... THE MEGALOMANIAC! (Bagpipe music now begins to play, and a burly man with a kilt and bagpipes comes to ringside.) [Tonsils] And our final competitor, from Cape Breton, Canada... THE HIGHLANDER! (At this point, the announcer wisely ducks out of the ring, leaving the fifteen competitors to stare each other down until the sound of the bell.) [Tonsils] Gentlemen, welcome to the High Impact Wrestling Federation... now let's get down to business! *DINGDINGDING* ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ -- --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Never, EVER taunt Happy Fun Ball.
