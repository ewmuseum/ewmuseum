A three-week interfederation touring event in the summer of 1999 that gathered sixteen women wrestlers from several federations for a single-elimination tournament. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A Woman's Scorn '99's surviving record is two announcements: a 15 July 1999 pitch for what it called “a nearly three week tour, showcasing some of the best in female e-wrestling,” open to any handler's wrestler regardless of promotion, and a roster-introduction broadcast four days later.

That broadcast, staged as a press conference, introduced its sixteen touring wrestlers one by one to reporters: among them “The Extreme Goddess” Kieran Erotikka, a veteran of the UWF, WISE and ICW; Envy, sister and manager of “Arch Angel” Gabrielle Cross; “Tornado” Tara Smith; and Erin McCoys, billed as the UWF's own reigning women's champion alongside her stablemates in the BOD Squad.

The tour's own organizer read out a first card for 23 July 1999 at the Good Times Unlimited Building in Hartford, Connecticut — a sixteen-woman battle royal headlining seven further singles and tag bouts — before putting the wrestlers on a bus, calling the tour, in his own words, “one small step for them, and one huge leap, for women's wrestling,” and building toward a single-elimination tournament at a final stop on 11 August to crown its own champion.

How the tour itself played out isn't in the surviving record; its last surviving document is that same 19 July 1999 broadcast, previewing a first card that isn't itself part of what survives.
