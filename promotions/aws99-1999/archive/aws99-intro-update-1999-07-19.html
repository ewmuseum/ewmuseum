A Woman's Scorn '99 — Roster Introduction

19 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hell Hath No Fury Like... ...A WOMAN'S SCORN!! Parker Promotions Presents... *A WOMAN'S SCORN '99! TOUR!!!"* [SCENE: A standard press room. Our cameras are at the back of the rectangular room, behind the number of reporters that have gathered here to witness the
departure of the first "A Woman's Scorn" Tour. At the front of the room is a stage and on top of that, and tall podium with a mic set up on it. Currently, there is no one on stage. The reporters are busying themselves, when suddenly
"Girl's Just Wanna Have Fun" by Cyndi Lauper plays loudly over the PA, and out steps...Ryan Parker?!? The organizer of the tour steps out to a polite round of applause, and signals for the music to be cut off. He steps to the podium and taps the mic, testing to make sure it all works right. He stares with a cocky smile for a moment, then address the crowd of reporters.]
(Organizer of the AWS99 Tour)Ryan Parker: "Hi. I'd like to thank you all for coming today and being on hand to witness what we hope is the first of many successful independent women's wrestling tours! We have lined up many top female grapplers lined up, and that's why I've called you all here today...to introduce the sixteen fighting beauties who make up the inaugural "A Woman's
Scorn '99!" Tour! Without further ado, allow me to presents...The Girls of the
AWS99!!!" [At this point a hardy applause comes from the reporters and witnesses as the lovely ladies all step onto the stage. Most of them are dressed for traveling, wearing very casual clothes such as jeans and T-shirts. The good portion of them are drop dead gorgeous. They each shake Parker's hand, as they form a line across the stage.]
Ryan Parker: "First up is 'The Extreme Goddess' Kieran Erotikka. Take a step forward please, Kieran."
[From the line steps a pretty girl with dirty blonde hair and deep green eyes. She is wearing grunge-esque attire, and through small tears in her shirt, some funky looking scars can be seen. Over all, Erotikka is still very attractive.]
Ryan Parker: "25 year old Kieran has been in this sport for a good time now, and has seen it all. She's competed in such leagues as the UWF, WISE, ICW and many others. Kieran has managed some greats including Bob Todd and Liam Rogan.
Well boys, it's time for you to get into the background, this is Kieran's time to shine. She's as hardcore as it gets, and a truly welcomed addition to the
AWS99 Tour! Thank you Kieran. Next up we've got an...interesting young lady...Envy!"
[Another woman steps out, and this one looks slightly different...to say the least! She has on a tight leather tube top with matching leather pants, and high black heels, that seem to go up forever. She has long brownish hair, and a faraway, yet mischievous look in her eyes. She has a twisted smile on her face, and as she steps forward...she gets VERY close to Ryan Parker.]
Ryan Parker: "Oh...ummm...well 21 year old Envy is a youngster of sorts and hasn't seen a good deal of action... well...at least not in a wrestling ring.
She has managed her brother "Arch Angel" Gabrielle Cross to success in a short
ICW stint and in the WWO. Though she has little experience, she can certainly hold her own in the ring. My advice to the other girls on the tour...watch out for this lady's bag of tricks! Thank you Envy..."
[Parker gives the cue for her to go back in line, but she stares at him like a lion stalking her prey...then steps back into a line...]
Ryan Parker: "Next we have "Tornado" Tara Smith! Step on out please Ms.Smith..." [An alarmingly beautiful girl with waist length red hair, and seductive green eyes. You can tell just by looking at this girl, she keeps herself in GREAT shape. She is wearing simple jeans and a blue T-shirt, and a smile that seems to make Ryan blush a bit.]
Ryan Parker: "21 year old Tara Smith is a graduate of Arizona State, and has been in the wrestling game since earlier this year. She has kept busy with a big time schedule, competing in VCW, WISE, IWWA & the UWF against some of the top females in the world. With a background in martial arts, and a more than qualified teacher in "Diamondback" Chris Myers, you might just be able to say that Tara is the most athletically gifted girl on the tour. She'll take her wrestling ability and be a wonderful representative of the AWS99. Thank you very, very much Tara. Next we have Chanel, step forward please Chanel..."
[The reporters all gasp as the 6 foot tall, 230 pound Chanel steps forward, a menacing scowl on her face. She is wearing a black leather weightlifting outfit, and her long black hair is left down on her face. Chanel is VERY muscular. Her large fists are balled...and ready to strike! Parker looks on in amazement, but begins his speech when Chanel gives him a hard look.]
Ryan Parker: "28 year old Chanel is one of the largest and most intimidating women on the tour! She weighs in at a massive 230 pounds of pure muscle, and has proven on many occasions that she is more than most MEN can handle! Her size alone makes her a top contender for AWS99 Crown, and I imagine is enough to give the rest of these woman some serious cold feet! She has competed for the FWF and the WWO, and had a good deal of success as the bodyguard of WWO star, Brian Bradley. Thank you Chanel. Our next girl I'd like to introduce is
Vanessa Bellucini..."
[Chanel slowly steps back into line, bumping into Vanessa along the way. It seems everyone tenses up in the brief stare down, but both women realize this is not the time or place for physical involvement. Vanessa is dressed very differently than most of the women, wearing a men's three piece suit with matching raybans and despite the large sign seen above the stage saying 'No
Smoking', a lit cigarette dangles from her mouth. Her is dyed jet black, and she is sporting decent amounts of black eyeliner and black lipstick.]
Ryan Parker: "22 year old Vanessa Bellucini has been a wrestling fan all her life, and a top notch amateur boxer nearly as long. She was brought into the wrestling business by her childhood friend and fellow wrestler Rick Sly, and has had a taste for it ever since. She is not afraid to speak her mind on any manner, and for that reason she doesn't play well with others. She has worked for ASW, FWF and the WWO, where she has both managed and fought some of the toughest competitors out there. Watch out ladies of the AWS99, she's single!
Thank you Vanessa." [Vanessa steps back into the line.] Ryan Parker: "The next lady on the tour is the lovely Destiny." [Another girl steps forward, and she is built awesomely. She has long brown hair and a very confident manner about her. She is wearing a cut off T-shirt that says "BOD Squad" on it.]
Ryan Parker: "28 year old Destiny is former Miss Fitness USA and though, tough ring competitor. As a friend of fellow AWSer Erin McCoys, she entered this business managing her husband, WWO star Daniel Lewis. Since that time she has been competing for herself more and more and recently signed with the UWF and is currently a member of the BOD Squad there. Yes ladies, she's just as dangerous as she looks! Thank you Destiny. Our next competitor is a young lady by the name of Satin Sheets..."
[One of the sexiest ladies yet steps forward, wearing...well, not too much. SS's hair is long and brown, flowing all the way down to her back. Her figure is just damn close to PERFECT, and she could certainly appear in any adult magazines that I subscri...I MEAN...on the market today. She steps up next to
Parker and gives him an eye that makes him stumble on his words a bit.]
Ryan Parker: "uhhh...ahem...21 year old Satin Sheets is a former cheerleader, and can certainly use her body to get what she wants. Came in to the wrestling world after meeting UWF wrestler Kyle Backwood at a bar one night. Sheets is currently embroiled in a bit of a feud with Lady In Red in the UWF, and in addition to her in ring talents, Satin is also a promising singer and dancer.
Ms.Evans would you please step forward.." [Satin steps back into line, and out steps a pretty...yet somewhat frumpy looking girl. Piper Evans is a strange one at first glance, as he hair is cut in different lengths and dyed different shades of blue. Jewelry covers the young girl, as she is loaded with earrings, necklaces and rings of all shapes and sizes. She is wearing nothing fancy, old torn jeans and a faded T-shirt with a leather jacket that has obviously seen better days than these. She has a distant look in her eyes, and shows nothing like the pride that shines through the other girls. Still, and almost whimsical smile rest on her lips.]
Ryan Parker: "23 year old Piper is one helluva athlete who originally came to us from England. Don't let her laid back appearance fool you ladies, Ms.Evans is a solid threat once she climbs into the ring and will not take it easy at all. She has mastered many styles of martial arts including kickboxing, basic tai kwan doe and self defense as well as spent much time in wrestling school.
Piper has just recently invaded the world of pro-wrestling. Thank you Piper." [Piper steps back into the line with a nod.] Ryan Parker: "Our next girl is 22 year old Mystique..." [Mystique steps forward and sure enough is another looker. Sort of reminiscent to Tyra Banks, a great face and a near perfect body. She has a cocky smile on her face as she steps to the front of the stage.]
Ryan Parker: "Mystique is an elite member of the ICW's ladies division, and in her first few months in the business shot up in the rankings, and was quickly considered a top contender for the women's championship. Then suddenly, she disappeared. Luckily enough, Mystique has resurfaced and opted to hop on the
AWS bus in search of excellence in women's wrestling. She came into the sport as the baby sister of Westside Walavan, but quickly rose to prominence on her own. She is surely a force in women's wrestling. Thank you Mystique. Please step forward Mistress Sonia..."
[Another women steps forward, giving a look to Parker that seems to startle him and cause him to take a step back. She is a well built Asian woman who just by looking at her, you can see power. The scariest thing about her is the long, leather whip she has in her hand. She snaps it toward the reporters, and they squeal in fear. Eventually, she takes her position, and nods at Parker to begin the speech, almost as if she were the one running the show.]
Ryan Parker: "Mistress Sonia is another member of the ICW's women's division and is surely an intimidating force. It's been said that in addition to mastering several forms of the martial arts, Sonia is also a master of all devices used to inflict pain...and pleasure to some. Watch out folks, this kitten has a whip! Um...thank you Sonia. Next up is Jenny "The Cheerleader"
Anderson!" [Sonia gets back in line and Ms.Anderson steps forward. She is wearing her ring outfit, a typical cheerleader ensemble, and looking very, very cute. Her semi-long blond hair is pulled up into a pony tail, and she has a big, infectious smile on her face, causing most of the room to smile with her. Her cuteness is enough to really embitter some of the ladies behind her.]
Ryan Parker: "At 18 years old, Jenny is the youngest competitor on the tour, but don't let that fool you! She's worked hard and succeeded in many tough female divisions.
She has competed in the IFWF, IWWA and AWCW, and has won several nice looking titles. She has come to the AWS99 to prove that she is a top contender in the world of women's wrestling! Thank you Jenny!"
[Jenny skips back into place, drawing dirty looks from several of the women behind her.] Ryan Parker: "Next on the tour, "Queen" Victoria Rockafella!" [Another woman steps forward, and you can tell right away that she is a snot! With her jet black hair tied back into a french bun, and her nose raised high in the air, Victoria steps to the front of the stage.]
Ryan Parker: "22 year old Victoria is a newcomer to the world of wrestling, but some people have gone far enough to call her the 'future of women's wrestling'. She wants it all, the fame, the money, the glory. She believes the AWS99 is the way she'll get it. This woman wants to prove that she is the only true Queen of women's wrestling. Thank you Victoria. We're nearing the end folks. Next up is "Lady B" Brenda Blood!"
[Mrs.Blood take a step or two straight ahead. Blood has long brown hair and deep, dark brown eyes. She is dressed in head to toe in black leather. Around her neck is a set of rather peculiar pearls, they don't seem to go with the rest of the outfit.]
Ryan Parker: "23 year old Brenda Blood is a new comer to the 'show' of professional wrestling, and hopes to use the AWS as a way to vault herself into some prize contract negotiations. Hint, hint companies. She is the wife of
ICW competitor Bryan Blood and she lives by the age old adage of 'whatever it takes'. Her husband has her more than ready to take names, and this gal is more than anxious to begin. Thank you Mrs.Blood. Ms.Marshall, if you will."
[Parker motions towards the ladies. One of the remaining women steps forward. She is petite but built very nicely, and looks like a stereotypical California beach beauty. She is wearing a comfortable pair of jeans, and a black "BOD
Squad" T-shirt. Her blond hair is tied in a full pony tail, and held there by a black scrunchie. With a knowing smile, Tara steps to the front of the stage.]
Ryan Parker: "Ms.Marshall is true treasure for us here at AWS99. She broke into this business long ago as a manager and had a boat load of success, but recently like many managers, she got the craving to have the glory be hers, and not one of her charges. Tara is the second of three BOD Squaders, and is very loyal to her "Beauty On Demand"sisters. She currently competes for both ICW and the UWF, and is a former gymnast. Thank you Tara. Our next find is Bloody
Mary!"
[This girl has long, wavy blond hair, and a look on her face that just SCREAMS toughness. She is wearing a long western style trenchcoat as she moves to the front. It seems the reporters are taking extra pictures of Bloody Mary.]
Ryan Parker: "34 year old Bloody Mary is the eldest member of the tour, and one of the very best. She was the final NWW champion when that league closed as well as a top 3 contender in WISE in the final days. In a recent internet poll, Mary was ranked the number 13 woman in the world of pro-wrestling. There is an old adage in wrestling that says, 'to be the man, you've got to beat the man'. Let me revise that. On the AWS99, to be the woman, you gotta beat THIS woman...as Bloody Mary certainly goes into this thing as one of the favorites.
Thank you Mary." [Mary steps back into line.] Ryan Parker: "The final lady on the tour, "Erotic" Erin McCoys!" [A very beautiful and charming lady steps forward, and the reporters are now clicking away snapping pictures of young McCoys. She is noticable smaller than most of the other girls, and it kind of makes you wonder if she can compete in the same class as them. Her blond her down, hanging on to a perfect neck.
She, like Destiny & Tara Marshall is wearing a "BOD Squad" T-Shirt.] Ryan Parker: "24 year old Erin McCoys, is simply a sensation. Starting out as a manager of such regional talents like Andru Edwards, Erin grew up around wrestling. Her grandpappy is reputable instructor Roger McCoys, so it was only natural Erin get involved. After many physical disputes, Erin decided to get serious and train to go all the way, as a full time wrestler. She is living out a dream in the UWF where she is women's champion, and reigns aside her friends and stablemates, the BOD Squad, Tara Marshall and Destiny. All 3 ladys add excitement and fire to the AWS99, and we thank them for coming aboard.
Thank you, Erin." [Erin steps back into line, and the reporters give a good round of applause to the ladies that have been introduced.]
Ryan Parker: "Now that you know the ladies on the tour, there's only one thing to do before we kick things off. I know these ladies have been waiting anxiously to find out their first opponents, and I will now read the line up for the July 23,1999 show...
================================================<> JULY 23,1999... from the GOOD TIMES UNLIMITED BUILDING in Hartford, CT... Hell Hath No Fury Like The... "A Woman's Scorn 1999!" Tour MAIN EVENT--"It's time to set the standard!" 16 Women Over the Top Battle Royal!(Includes ALL ladies on the tour!) Destiny vs. "Tornado" Tara Smith! "Erotic" Erin McCoys vs. Mystique! Jenny "The Cheerleader" Anderson vs. Tara "Sunburst" Marshall! Bloody Mary vs. "Lady B" Brenda Blood! Envy & "The Extreme Goddess" Kieran Erotikka vs. Mistress Sonia & Chanel! Vanessa Bellucini vs. Satin Sheets! "Queen" Victoria Rockafella vs. Piper Evans! =========================================<> Ryan Parker: "Wow! I don't know about you, but I'm wondering how we booked a card where EVERY match seems like a main event! With all that business handled, we're ready to start this tour. As you saw when you came in, there is a bus outside waiting for us, and there's just a few more things I'd like to say. The AWS99 is an attempt give women's wrestling some more publicity, and to show the all the promoters out there that these gals are more than T & A.
They are all very different, and very talented. No, they are not interchangeable with any local stripper/waitress. They are wrestlers. As these 16 woman board that bus outside, well, to paraphrase again...it'll be one small step for them, and one huge leap, for women's wrestling. Thank you all, and we'll be seeing you July 23rd!"
[The reporters clap and snap pictures as Parker leads the line of 16 warriors onto the bus, to officially kick off the tour...]
=================================<> Ryan Parker Handler Of... Loki[DFK], Brian Bradley[WWO]. And currently running the A Woman's Scorn '99 Tour!
