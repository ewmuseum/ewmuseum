A Woman's Scorn '99 — Tour Announcement

15 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hell hath no fury like... A Woman's Scorn! Welcome to the official shill for "A Woman's Scorn!" tour! Right about now your saying, "Shut up and tell us what the tour is". Okay, okay! No need to be mean about it. "A Woman's Scorn '99!"(AWS99) is a nearly three week tour, showcasing some of the best in female e-wrestling. I know you've got questions, so we'll get right to them...
*Why would you want to do this?* Simple. I like women's wrestling. I like women's e-wrestling. Any little bit of exposure for it is great. Plus, it'll give handlers of women e-wrestlers a chance to work together, even if they are not in the same league.
*How does the tour work?* Well, that's simple. This tour will be three weeks long. You can find the dates for the tour below. Anyways, the first 2 weeks of the tour consist of detailed summary cards. Then the last week is a week off before the fully written out super-show, The Final Stop, which will feature a single elimination tournament to crown the AWS99 Champion. The seedings in that tournament will be decided by the win/loss records of the previous two weeks of the tour.
*How are matches scheduled?* Full line up will be done at least two cards in advance. If you want to speacillly challenge someone, you can do that, just e-mail me(at
[detail withheld] ). *How do you win matches?* Through nepotism and cronyism of course! Oh wait...what I meant to say was through roleplaying and stratting! I realize that due to there being a card every other day, that it will be nearly impossible to RP for every show.
Before the tour begins, a Pre-Tour update will go out, and you can just give a general RP about the other women, your charecter or anything you want. You are encouraged, though not required to RP once for each week of the tour. If you don't want to, you don't have to at all...just expect to lose alot :). Once a week would be wonderful though. Strats will really help you too, but they'll be hard to pull off with the time restraints. Strat when you can for the first two weeks, no big deal if you don't. I do however highly suggest you strat for the super-show. I'll be personally picking the winners, and I am a fair judge, just...trust me... :)
