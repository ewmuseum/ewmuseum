It has been around since
March of 1997. It has gone away for a few weeks on 2 occassions, but it has never died. Now, this fed isn't for you if your gonna RP 30 K and send in one or 2 rps a week. Sure, we like good rps, but not to the point of ridiculousness like there is in most of today's so called TOP federations. The fed doesn't take up much of your time, though. A few rps a week all with somewhere between 40 and 100 lines is fine. We don't care much about how original you are, because the whole object of it is not to be original, its to have fun. Sometimes we'll have completely new angles, or sometimes they will look like what happened on Monday night. It really doesn't matter because usually everything we do changes from what happened in real wrestling. We started this current angle out as a rip off of the current WWF corporate angle, things are rapidly changing, and it looks less and less like that everyday. However, we do suffer from a lack of wrestlers. We are looking for an ideal number of about 30 wrestlers. After we get 30, we will still allow some in, but they will most likely only be friends of current handlers, and people who we think will be dedicated. Each handler is allowed to have 3 wrestlers. You can either create your own, or use a real wrestler. There are some real wrestlers already on the roster that aren't currently handled [The Rock, and
Mankind.] You can take them as they are, or have them quit and start with a new record the following week. If you quit and your wrestlers are in the middle of a big angle. We hold onto them, and add them to a list of NPCs. NPCs are wrestlers that are jobbers unless a handler drafts them. You can draft 2 NPCs. NPCS have an NPC label next to them on cards. Just e-mail at
[name withheld] with whatever wrestler it is. However, you take them as they are with whatever their record currently is. You are responsible to rp for them until you don't have anymore use for them. Then, you just e-mail me saying you drop them, and they go back into the NPC pool. When and if you decide to quit the fed, if you are in the middle of a big angle your created wrestlers will stay behind until they can be worked out of the angle. Then, they will be dropped. Your real wrestlers stay behind permanently. Current
NPCS: The Rock, Mankind, The Patriot, and PG-13: J.C. Ice, and Wolfie D. We have 2 cards a week, Sunday slam which is a minor show. With only 3 or 4 matches every week, usually with one interview as well. It is completely summaries. The other card is Hardcore Thursday which is the main card. It has as many as 12 matches depending on how many wrestlers we have at the moment. The Main Event is fully written out while other matches are Play by
Play. The World Title is defended on here once and a while, the US Title, and IC Title are defended twice a month here. Which means that there is rarely a Hardcore Thursday without a Title defense. We also have pay-per-views. Currently we are at a loss for wrestlers as I stated before, so Pay-per-views are held bi-monthly. When we get 25 wrestlers the PPVs will shift to monthly. IN place of a PPV we have a special edition of Hardcore Thursday. Every title is put on the line, and another special match is put in as well. These cards are completely written out, but the Hardcore Thursday Special is a bit less quality then the Pay-Per-Views. Finally, the titles World Title-Few have been able to claim that they have held this. It is the most prestigous title in the fed. Only the great where it. Most of its holders are still wrestling with the fed today. Which proves that if you wait, you will usually wind up with it around your waist. CURRENTLY HELD BY: Freddy Allocca IC Title- The secondary title, it will be crowned in a football season-like tournament, when we receive 20 wrestlers. This title is usually used as a fall- back for World Champions who have recently lost their belts. US Title-A stepping stone to the world title, most notable for its constant changing hands. It changes more often then the TElevision title did, and that was defended almost every card. CURRENTLY HELD BY: Tommy Richards Tag Team Titles- The World title for tag teams. To be decided at the first big HArdcore Thursday which will go down on February 18th. Thats about everything......The first 9 people to join, you'll immediately be entered into the IC Title Tourney. One last thing, the fed is a very mature, we do however have morals so swearing is to a minimum, and you can talk about sex, but do not actually have your wrestler rp in the middle of intercourse. I've seen it done, and it was just plain sick. Also remember there is a line that stands there even in a fed, don't go over the edge, and make racial slurs.
