Professional F'K'N Wrestling

6 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene fades into what vaguely resembles the office of a somewhat important figure. The back wall of the office is a window looking down at the surrounding highway, and even further onto the surrounding countryside. It's about now that notice something incredibly odd about the room...IT'S PURPLE!
What kind of businessman would paint his office purple!?] Voice - "That's right, SURF'S UP BEEYATCH!" [And with those words, fans of PFW would instantly know that this could be none other than the office of Tommy Richards. If your not a PFW fan...YOU SHOULD
BE! But in the mean time just pretend like you are, and know that this is
Tommy Richard's office. The man walks into the office. He doesn't look like much of a businessman. He's wearing surf shorts, and a PFW: Welcome 2 Hardcore
t-shirt.] Tommy - "Ugh. I can't believe all this paperwork. I had NO idea what I was getting into when I bought this flamin' federation."
[Tommy walks over to his desk, and sits down. Then, we hear a beeping noise. And a staticy voice comes from a small box on the desk.] Voice - "Mr. Richards, Ms. Sinne is here to see you." Tommy - "Here we go again...send her in Betty." [A few seconds later, a young woman with blond hair walks in. She's wearing a mint green blazer, and the same color skirt. We assume this to be Ms. Sinne.]
Ms. Sinne - "Tommy, tommy, tommy." Tommy - "What do you want NOW, Ash?" Ms. Sinne - "I just thought you would like to know about the PFW's talent pool." Tommy - "Well...I'm a busy man. Get straight to the point." Ms. Sinne - "Other than the faces currently on television, it's virtually non-existent. There seems to be little to no interest in the current product from the upcoming stars around the world."
Tommy - "Alright. Your telling me this why? That's your department. Why don't you handle it like a good little commisioner, and leave me to my paper work."
Ms. Sinne - "I figured you would want to try and start some form of program to get..." Tommy - "No. I want YOU to start some form of program to get more wrestlers. Advertise. We've got plenty of money in the budget from the success of Shadowed Light. Go buy some talent scouts. Scoop up some people. There are bound to be a few guys interested."
Ms. Sinne - "But Tommy..." Tommy - "Hush! Just make sure you prevent the PFW properly to all the wrestlers. Let them understand that, we don't care what they've done in the rest of the e-wrestling world. Their coming to the PFW now. That's
Professional [BLEEP]en Wrestling, and yes we are JUST as angry as the name implies. Don't forget to go over the requirements of a contract. You have to be willing to fall from at least 15 feet in the air, at least once per year. You must have experience in the use of blount weapons, and of course...you MUST NOT be disturbed by the sight of your own blood.
And if they ask for benefits...show them a tape of the Hardcore Hotties dancing at the show. That's PLENTY of benefits."
Ms. Sinne - "I've been meaning to..." Tommy - "Can this wait for some other time? I really need to get some things organized." Ms. Sinne - "Fine, Tommy." [Fade to black.]
