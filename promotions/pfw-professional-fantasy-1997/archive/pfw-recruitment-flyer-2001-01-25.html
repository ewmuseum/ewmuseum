Looking For A Few Good Men

25 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1. What's PFW? 1.1. In-Character PFW began in February/March of 1997 as the brainchild of Mr. Brian Sullivan. Brian had a vision to make PFW the greatest federation on the planet, and he had a list of legends to back him. Brian Stone, Myke ICON, Post, Squala, and the list goes on. The fed made shockwaves through the nation, and broke onto TV with 3 televised cards a week. Sunday Night
Slam, Tuesday Tremors, and Hardcore Thursday. As the PFW climbed up the ladder of success slowly but surely, more people began to help Brian Sullivan out. And as the work was spread out this left room for improvement. And the PFW reached it's epitome...the PFW reached,
Wylde Winter in the beginning of 1998. It's most successful Pay-Per-View at the time. Unfortunately, due to some problems to PFW went on hiatus. Of course, it wouldn't last very long. The PFW came off of hiatus, and immediately held a HUMONGOUS tournament for the World Title. And it seemed since Wylde Winter that the PFW had a brand new attitude. The heat was on, and PFW
Revival took place, surpassing Wylde Winter, and cementing PFW as the # 1 Federation in america.
Then, Brian Sullivan sold the rights of PFW, and the company changed hands quite a few times leaving a lot of wrestlers unhappy with the organization, and some of the biggest influences in the PFW leaving. It all changed for the better though. Myke
ICON became PFW President and Owner. He took the federation from it's struggling state, and put it back where it belonged....# 1.
Myke ICON brought back the wrestlers who left, and quite surprisingly put an awful lot of political power into the hands of Greg Allocca. A man who Myke ICON openly expressed his dislike for. But it proved to be a good decision because with
Greg Allocca, as one of the men at the front of the organization...they would reach the top once again. Blood, White &
Blue. The single greatest PFW Event ever recorded on tape. Edward Glide battled Josh Cagle in the Chamber Of Horrors. OZ fought Post in the Hell In The Cell. And countless other great matches took place on that card. And we proceeded onward to Summer Sizzler 2. Which although nothing like Blood, White & Blue proved to be a great card in itself. Of course, due to some serious backstage problems...and the apparent work ethic(or lack thereof) in the PFW locker rooms...we went on another long hiatus.
And there it was, PFW, 1999. January 1st, reopening. New Year's Massacre. This time Greg Allocca was at the head of the organization. The PFW began on a road that would display it's excellence. Although nothing would surpass the pure perfection of Blood,
White & Blue the PFW found itself more popular than it had ever been. With legends like Post, Myke ICON, Edward Glide, and Brian Stone...as well as new blood like The Almighty Prey, Markus King, and High Flyer the PFW stayed # 1.
But...personal problems, and a bad business decision caused the organization to go bankrupt just before it's big follow up for the biggest thing in wrestling since Hulk Hogan, the sequel to Blood, White & Blue. And it seemed that the organization would never recover.
But then, on January 24th, 2001...something stirred in the long empty PFW Towers. Sykotik Enterprises sold the rights of PFW to...Thomas Richardson. Known to the wrestling world as none other than "The ICON™" Tommy
Richards. And now the bleach blond surfer from New York City has decided to bring it back from the grave... he's decided to give new life to the PFW! With a new attitude, Professional Fantasy Wrestling has been changed to Professional F'K'N Wrestling. And The New Age of Rage is about to begin!
1.2. Out Of Character PFW's an e-fed started by <span class="redacted">[name withheld]</span> and a few of his friends. It ran from March of 1997 through June of 1999 with a few hiatuses in between, and lots of management changes. Of course, it was left in the hands of Greg Allocca when it closed down. Who gave the fed to that moron anyway?
But now, Greg, is back...and he is trying to give PFW ONE LAST SHOT to bring itself back to greatness. Can he do it? Or will it flop out like so many attempts before him.
2. Rules 1.1. Roleplays Roleplays, are without a doubt important. Nowhere near as important as angles, because angles can very easily override a match decision with roleplays. But, without roleplays it gets ridiculously hard to further any of your angles. And for those matches that don't have too much heat going into them...it's pretty much the only way for the bookers to determine a winner.
1.2. Angles Angles, are the most important part of any e-wrestling federation. You can be the best damned roleplayer on the face of the planet. If you can't work with the rest of the federation, and put together some sort of angle...RPs won't do ya any good. Because without some form of angle...I seriously doubt you'll be finding a gold strap around your waist. Not to mention that when it's just a bunch of looneys running for belts, the fed isn't all that much fun!
1.3. In-Rings, Sneak Attacks, Promos, etc. This is all the stuff you want your wrestler to do on the card. Whether it's a quick little rp from backstage, a nice little promo spot for your wrestler or his match, a sneak attack, or a HUGE in-ring rp to do some angle furthering. This is the best way for your wrestler to get noticed.
1.4. Strats This is what your wrestler wants to do in the match. If you want to do these, there will be a form once the webpage is up. Their not required, but they make matchwriting a hell of a lot easier. They also make sure we portray your wrestler the way you want him/her to be.
1.5. Helping Out Alright, I know I'm the fed-head, but the trust me when I say this...The Fed will work a lot better if everyone puts some work in. Rather than have me doing all the work myself, and more than likely shutting down miserably in a month. Everyone should just help out. Now, the minimum requirement is that you write one match a month[more than likely going to be a PPV match]. But that's not the only thing you can do. If you know HTML tell me, and you can help me around with the webpage. If your good with graphics, help me do logos. If you've got that Poser program, You are god! LOL! And if you've got a lot of free time one week, offer to help out a bit more with another match...or any other ideas you can come up with. The more help I get, the more likely this fed is to succeed. And if you don't offer any help...you better not be complaining when the cards are late!
3. Titles 3.1. PFW World Title The big one. This belt has to be defended once a month. No loopholes. At least once a month, but if you've got the balls...you can defend it whenever you like.
The man who holds this title is the federation's top dawg. No ifs, ands, or buts about it! You've gotta be the best to hold this strap, and if your not...well, I hope you like working the undercard.
3.2. PFW North American Title Ok, not quite ready to play with the big dawgs yet? It's alright. The North American title is a great stepping stone that many of the legends have taken on their way to winning the world title. Hell, sometimes it's even been said that the North American champ is actually better than the World champ. This is where to make your mark.
3.3. PFW World Tag Team Titles This is just like the world title, but for the Tag Teams! 3.4. PFW Dogg House Title This is the one title you don't wanna get. You have to LOSE to the person who holds it in order to get it! It symbolizes you as the King of
The Workers, The Crowned Prince Of Pin Me, Pay Me. Of course, the workers have seemingly adopted the title as some sort of odd reward...and they actually ATTEMPT to win...er...lose...er...NEVERMIND!
3.5. Additional Straps If the roster grows big enough...the PFW gets to around the 35 wrestler limit...the Television Title will be added. If we get at least 6 wrestlers 225 lbs or under...The
Skyweight[Cruiserweight, Light Heavyweight] Title will be introduced. 4. Cards 4.1. Hardcore Thursday The PFW's legendary show known for it's extreme content returns to it's original day and time! This is the big show. Where the titles are going to be defended, and where you know you'll see the big dawgs in action!
4.2. Bootleg TV The PFW's secondary TV show. Sunday Nights. Think you saw all you needed to see on Hardcore? Well, the guys who didn't get to strut their stuff on Thursday have something to say about that! Bootleg may not have as many of the big dogs, but it's gonna have more pure wrestling! And who knows, you just might get a big surprise one night?
