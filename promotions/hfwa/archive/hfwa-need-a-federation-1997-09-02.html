I need a federation (HFWA bycatch), 2 September 1997

2 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I need a federation, with heavy email roleplaying action. It needs to have an experienced president, and it must be an established federation. I would prefer a webpage, and I need some competition. Email me if you can help. Here is a sample of one of my roleplays: (The scene opens with an overhead view of Jam Packed Seattle's Key arena. Signs with Scoops, HFWA, Schultz in 2000 flood the arena. We see Mene Gene Okerland standing by preparing to do an interview with Crusher [name withheld]. There is a capacity HFWA croud around as Gene steps into view. Gene announces that the Crusher is going to be on today, and the croud gets up screaming. Chants of Crusher are made through the arena. Finally, when [name withheld] steps into view with his denim shorts, "CRUSHER" T-Shirt and Purple metallic Sunglasses similar to bret Harts on, the croud goes nuts giving him a standing ovation) Mene Gene: Well, Mr. Martin... The Croud certainly is behind you today... Martin: Just one second Gene. (Martin walks up to a handicapped fan and gives him his sunglasses) Mene Gene: That was very generous of you Chris bu... Martin: Gene... I will answer all your questions in just one minute. I just have one thing to say. I first, would like to thank all my fans and all these wonderful Washingtonians who support the Crusher in his long journey to becoming the HFWA Unified Champion. (Croud roars as he says that) Martin: I would also like to say that, in the 4 way title match tomorrow, I will become the HFWA unified champion, and I will become the best thing to ever happen to the HFWA. Not only, will I be the current 2 time HFWA Cruiserweight champion, but I will be the HFWA Unified Champion. I will be the only one in the HFWA to hold 2 belts at once! (The Croud rises and the stadium goes crazy. The seats of the stadium are vibrating. Mene Gene is overwhelmed by the responce the croud. A smile comes across Martins face.) Gene: Can I talk now... Martin: Okay... go ahead. Gene: I hate to rain on your parade, but in your contract it says that you are on the line for heavyweight and cruiserweight championchips. You can't hold the cruiser and unified title at the same time... You can either compete cruiserweight or heavyweight. (The croud shouts and boo's at this announcement. Plastic soda bottles, paper, popcorn and other things are thrown at Mene Gene for this announcement. A look of disgust comes over Chris Martins face.) Martin: What the hell is this supposed to mean? I want to speak to my god damn attorney. Read my god damn contract. No where in it, does it say that I have to give up a title if I win it. It's mine until I lose it. This is a bunch of Bull {bleep}. Gene: If I can ask you... wha Martin: Shut up Gene! Listen to me, Mr. Schultz. Mr. HFWA, or whatever the hell you like to be called. I am going to win that damn Unified Title. Then, I will not only defend my Cruiserweight title, and my unified title. They are mine and mine only. Watch out... I am coming for some gold (Croud screams with a chant of "Crusher... Crusher... Crusher" going as he leaves to the backstage area.) Gene: Wait... I still had more questions... (You could barley hear gene over the mega phone because of the croud.) (Scene cuts to the back stage. Martin is outside President Schultz office banging on the door) Martin: Open up you piece of {bleep}! (Martin turns around and pushes the camera man away and the scene fades)
