Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Hardcore Fantasy Wrestling Association opened 28 February 1997, hosted on Angelfire and pitching itself on one stated rule: “I need good Role-players.” By July its own recruitment posts claimed around fifty wrestlers, still looking for more.

It ran jointly with a self-described “sister e-fed,” WMEW: its one surviving broadcast is co-hosted by an announcer for each side, and a WMEW-billed wrestler runs in mid-match during one bout, while a mystery lights-out finish elsewhere on the same card is blamed on the son of a WMEW World Tag Team co-champion.

That card, 17 July 1997, ran nine matches entirely with real-wrestler personas — Lex Luger, Dean Malenko, Taz, Sandman, the Eliminators, Kevin Nash, Chris Jericho, Chris Benoit, Shane Douglas, Ric Flair, Rick Rude, Sabu and the Great Muta among them — staged as a dream card that spilled out through the stands, the concourse, and the parking lot.

Two titles changed hands on it: Mr. Nice Guy beat the Great Muta to become a new US Champion, and a full Intercontinental Championship tournament, which Muta fought through to the final, ended with Docta Devistate as champion. Its main event, a barbed-wire cage match, crowned no new titleholder: Destroyer, addressed only as “the champion” without the belt itself ever being named in the surviving text, retained by pinfall over a wrestler called Smile.

It closed 3 November 1997. Its own president reopened it five weeks later, on 7 December, recruiting fresh roleplayers and staff under the same operator and promising it would be “better than ever.”

Its last held post, 21 December 1997, marks nine months since it first went online. No roster document, rules sheet, or second show survives; whether the reopened federation ran any further cards isn't in the surviving record.
