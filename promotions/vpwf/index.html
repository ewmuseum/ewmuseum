Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

On 9 October 1997 its own operator introduced the Virtual Professional Wrestling Federation with a promise of cash for winning: “you can win MONEY $$$$$$.” It ran on a pay-to-play structure — fifty cents a match, or a five-dollar monthly or twenty-dollar yearly membership — open only to original wrestlers, “must roleplay to win.”

A further recruitment post three days later renamed it the Virtual Pro Wrestling Federation and admitted its operator “had been president before, but it didn't work the way I wanted it to the first time.” It set two weekly shows, Saturday Slam Jams and Tuesday Night Terrors, planned a tournament to crown its first World and Tag Team Champions, and sanctioned six titles in all: World, World Tag Team, US, TV, Cruiserweight and North American Tag.

It kept advertising through 7 December 1997, still without a finished home page and still without a launch date for either weekly show or its planned pay-per-view.

No card, roster, or result of the promotion was ever posted; it is never observed to have run a single match.
