Make $$$$ for e-wrestling in the VPWF

10 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi. My name is Larry. I am starting a brand new federation which is going to require a lot of hard work from not only myself, but all of the wrestlers. This isn't an ordinary federation. This is called the VPWF, or the Virtual Professional Wrestling Federation. What id different about this federation is that you can win MONEY $$$$$$ if your e wrestler proves to be one of the best. Of course you must be willing to roleplay to be accepted. Here are the rules. 1) You must come up with an original wrestler!! No REAL WRESTLERS!! 2) To have your e-wrestler compete, it will cost $.50 cents per match. Or you can buy a montly membership for $5.00 and can wrestle as many matches as you want. And a yearly membership will cost $20.00. The amount you make will depend on how many matches we have per card. You will probobly be making close to double what you put in for the match. 3) You must roleplay to win!! This is important if you want to win and earn money for yourself. So if you are interested in joining, e mail me at [detail withheld] and I will give you further details!!!! Thanks a lot!
