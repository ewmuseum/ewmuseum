Murder 15: Business Is Picking Up

25 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Murder 15; a new fighting federation with a twist... only those with criminal background allowed. Why? To demand that these criminals show what they're made of. Murdered a police officer? Pulled off a big bank heist? Made big money by dealing cocaine? Big f'n deal.... try surviving Murder 15's challenge.   Win 15 straight fights, and you gain freedom. Plus immunity from all further crimes you committ. Lose 4 straight or a total of 8... and you are history. Simple as that.     You've read the in-character advertisment for the fed... here's the "out-of-character" one. Take all that stuff above, and plus this...   We currently have 12 fighters. And we're looking for at least 13 more to have a total of 25 by the time we officially open, in December 2001. We've already opened, but this is just to attract members. As said above, your character must have some criminal background. Be creative. We don't have any arsonists or bank robbers yet... so... yeah.   We are also looking for staff members. We already have a Poser artist, but another one would be nice. What we also really need are match-writers, bookers, a Flash designer, columnists, a webmaster and... a VP.   Murder 15 is also gonna open up a school-fed, called "East Coast Street-Fighting". We need staff-members for that too. We'll open up applications for fighters later on in the year.   So, if you're enticed... interested... or just plain curious, head over to the site at http://murder15.ewuncensored.com and take a look at it. The site may not look flashy or awesome to you, but is the design all that matters? For a fed that is officially opening in December, I think we've done a pretty darn good job so far. So... I'll end this little rant here... :-)     Thanks for reading, and Ciao; ~Kamlesh/[name withheld]; Co-Owner Of Murder 15 ~E-mail us at [detail withheld] for more info
