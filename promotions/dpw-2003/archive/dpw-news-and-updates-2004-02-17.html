Dublin Pub Wrestling — News

News & Updates

17 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DPW staff on the hot seat - Wrestlers have been stating there opinions ever since the royal rumble card came out. DPW has gone mad crazy. Mitch Armour says Bill Fury should have won the rumble and that Mitch should of been one of the final 4 left in. Slicer says Bill Fury should have won. Jason Kelly says he was never eliminated. Then things still didn't settle down when the preview for the next card came out. Slicer says he does not want to wrestle Bill Fury. DDP says that if he doesn't wrestle Triple X next card that he is leaving. Malibu Mike Donovan got involved by doing a segment making fun of Bill Fury. Bill Fury is calling it immature. Now Irish Devil has had it with all the nonsense. Irish Devil has now appointed Stan The Man Riley as the head chief in the complaint department. All complaints will go straight to him and he will personally deal with them. Slicer gets rid of Paul Bearer - Slicer claimed that it was his manager that has been bringing him down in the DPW. Slicer feels that he will be more successful without his manager. Mitch Armour's bullseye gets bigger - He is enemies with the PACK. Not only that but everyone wants to fight him for the title. Now Batman comes out from no where and blinds Mitch with a blue powdery substance. Batman may have a different reason for attacking Mitch. We are not sure what it is yet. Batman is probably the most mysterious wrestler in DPW history. Bill Fury lasts the longest - "The Big Bad" Bill Fury drew #2 in the royal rumble and was one of the last guys to be eliminated. Bill Fury dominated alot of the rumble and came close to winning the rumble. Bill Fury told Rufus Yoder that he is a dead man. Rufus eliminated Bill from the royal rumble. The Amish Champ - Rufus Yoder has climbed his way to the top of the ladder and has become the new intercontinental champion. A question is still up in the air as to if Rufus will join a stable or be a loner. Malibu over the edge? - Mike Donovan has been known as one of the most hated wrestlers of all times. A classic Donovan maneuver happened when Donovan read a letter from Bill Fury on national tv and making fun of Bill Fury and calling the big man out. Some say Donovan pushes things too far. However in DPW anything goes and Donovan knows how to play with the rulebook better than anyone in the business. Is DDP confused? - DDP wants Mike Donovan to be his friend so badly. It seems as if DDP always looked up to Mike Donovan. Donovan seems to not really care as he threw DDP out of the rumble. Will DDP stop trying to be Donovan's friend or is he obsessed with good ol' Malibu? Jason Kelly is Just Perfect - Dont call him the gigallo anymore. He is now refered to as just perfect. He may have a point. He has a perfect looking girlfriend. He is the tag team champion. And hey, did he actually ever get eliminated from that royal rumble? The cameras never caught it. The ref's never saw him get eliminated. So what happened? He seems just perfect. Rumors - A big stable that was well known may be reforming very soon. - Will Batman side with a stable? He was once a PACK member. - Slicer has been spotted talking to the PRODIGY. Could there be a future there for him? - One of the Mongs may be signing with DPW soon. - There has been talk about Stan Riley coming out of retirement to wrestle Mitch Armour.
