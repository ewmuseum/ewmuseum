DPW NEWS TOP STORY EXTREMEIST AND BULLDOZER RE-UNITE AND TAKES A SHOT AT THE PACK - EXTREMEIST AND BULLDOZER PRACTICALLY STOLED THE HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP FROM BADASS JOHN. THIS HAS MADE BADASS JOHN FURIOUS AND BADASS JOHN HAS DESTROYED ALL SORTS OF EQUIPMENT IN THE LOCKER ROOMS AND CAUSING LOTS OF COMMOTION. BADASS JOHN IS ONE PISSED OFF SON OF A BITCH AND HE CANT EVEN WAIT FOR THE NEXT CARD TO GET HIS HANDS ON THE EXTREMEIST AND THE BULLDOZER. BADASS JOHN AND KEN MASTERS WILL BE DEFENDING THERE TAG TEAM TITLES AGAINST THE 2 MEN. JOSE USED AS A WEAPON? - COULD IT BE POSSIBLE THAT THE DONKEY THAT MADMAN BRINGS TO RINGSIDE IS ALSO HIS SECRET WEAPON? WELL LAST CARD MAY HAVE PROVED THAT TO BE CORRECT. JOSE KICKED MITCH ARMOUR IN THE NOSE CAUSING A BROKEN NOSE AND MADMAN RETAINING HIS INTERCONTINENTAL CHAMPIONSHIP. AND THEY SAY NOBODY LIKES TO HANG OUT WITH A JACKASS? WELL MADMAN WILL TELL YA A DIFFERENT STORY. AND STILL U.S. CHAMPION - POLISH DEVIL HAS REMAINED U.S. CHAMPION FOR QUITE SOME TIME NOW SETTING NEW RECORDS HERE IN DPW. HE HAS DEFEATED SOME OF THE GREAT TALENTS IN THIS LEAGUE AND NOW THE QUESTION IS....WHO'S NEXT? HE HAS HAD A BIG FUED WITH HARDCORE HACKER AS OF LATE AND MAY BE IN CAHOOTS WITH SILLY BITCH. CAMERA MEN HAVE SPOTTED THEM 2 HANGING OUT BACKSTAGE BUT IS THIS ALL JUST A PLOT BY SILLY BITCH? ALOT OF QUESTIONS DO NEED ANSWERING AND THIS LUMBERJACK MATCH COMING UP MAY GET ALL THE ANSWERS. SABER INJURED? - APPARENTLY DURING A HARDCORE TITLE MATCH AGAINST MITCH ARMOUR, SABER SUFFERED A TORN BICEP. SABER HAS BEEN RESTING AT HOME AND HAS TAKENED SOME TIME OFF. SABER SHOULD BE BACK INTO THE RING SOON. A FORMER CCW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION HAS SIGNED WITH THE DPW! - SHOCKING NEWS BACKSTAGE AS A FORMER CCW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION WILL ONCE AGAIN LACE UP THE BOOTS AND NOW WRESTLE FOR THE DPW. THIS WRESTLING LEGEND WILL BE MAKING A GUEST APPEARANCE ON THE DPW CARD CALLED RECKLESS DRIVING. IS THE SUPERSTAR HEARTBROKEN? - WELL APPARENTLY THE SUPERSTAR MAY HAVE A SOFT SPOT. A LOVE SONG THAT HE HEARS REALLY BOTHERS HIM. MAYBE A LONG LOST GIRLFRIEND PERHAPS? A HOODED PERSON CAME DOWN DURING HIS LAST MATCH TO THAT SAME LOVE SONG AND HANDED CASTOR KANE A HEART SHAPED BOX. WHEN HE OPENED IT A FIREBALL SHOT RIGHT IN THE SUPERSTAR'S FACE. WHO WAS THAT HOODED PERSON? AND MOST IMPORTANTLY WHAT IS THE CONDITION OF CASTOR KANE? WILL HE BE IN ANY SHAPE TO WRESTLE AGAIN? HARDCORE HACKER ASKS IRISH DEVIL FOR A SHOT AT THE HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP - HARDCORE HACKER HAD A MEETING WITH THE IRISH DEVIL AND HE IS LOOKING TO FACE THE HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION. HARDCORE HACKER IS ALSO ASKING FOR MORE MATCHES EVEN IF IT HAS TO BE HIM FIGHTING 2 TIMES ON ONE CARD. HE IS VERY CONFIDENT IN HIS ABILITY AND FEELS THAT HE IS THE NUMBER 1 MAN. IRISH DEVIL IS CLOSE TO SIGNING HACKER TO A HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH. IRISH DEVIL SAYS THAT HE WANTS TO TALK TO SILLY HO ABOUT THE FINAL AGREEMENTS TO THE SIGNING. I WONDER WHAT THAT'S ALL ABOUT? PSYCHO CO-PRESIDENT? - INDEED THIS IS TRUE. PSYCHO WILL BE CO-PRESIDENT IN THE DPW WHICH MEANS MORE CARDS WILL BE OUT. THIS WILL BE A BIG STEP FOR DPW AND ALSO MORE TALENT. PSYCHO WAS RUNNING THE NJWA BUT NOW HAS DECIDED TO JOIN WITH IRISH DEVIL. WILL ANY NJWA STARS JOIN DPW? PROBABLY NOT SINCE IRISH DEVIL WAS THE MOST HATED WRESTLER IN THAT ORGANIZATION AND THEY WOULDN'T WANT TO WORK FOR HIM. SHAWN "THE STONER" KITTS WAS HANGING OUT WITH WHO? - THIS MAY BE JUST A RUMOR BUT IT'S BEEN GOING AROUND THAT SHAWN KITTS WAS HANGING OUT IN A LOCAL BAR WITH MALIBU MIKE DONOVAN AND PSYCHO. NOW EVERYONE MAY SAY, NO WAY SINCE PSYCHO IS FIGHTING DONOVAN NEXT CARD AND HAS FOUGHT SHAWN KITTS AND NEVER GOT ALONG WITH EITHER MAN. BUT WHAT IF THIS IS TRUE? DONOVAN LIKES ALCOHOL. THEY WERE IN A BAR. KITTS MIGHT HAVE BEEN PASSING A BONG AROUND AND PSYCHO HAS BEEN IN REHAB A FEW TIMES. COINCIDENCE OR JUST A SILLY RUMOR? THAD THORTON PURCHASES A WRESTLER? - RUMORS ARE FLYING AROUND THAT THAD THORTON HAS BOUGHT A WRESTLER TO BE IN THE PACK. IF THORTON PURCHASED THE WRESTLER THEN THE WRESTLER MUST BE AS TOUGH AS NAILS. THIS IS STILL A RUMOR. WE WILL KEEP YA POSTED. DOES TOY GIVE GOOD HEAD? - WHEN IRISH DEVIL WAS ASKED THIS QUESTION, HE RESPONDED: IRISH DEVIL: I DID NOT HAVE SEXUAL RELATIONS WITH THAT WOMAN. - SO THE QUESTION STILL REMAINS A MYSTERY. HAS MITCH LOST HIS SCREWS? - lately mitch armour has snaped and is now on a hot streak thrashing everything in his way,will this finally be the end of the terrible drout of armours record in DPW? psycho has something on his mind. im not malibu mike donovan, thank god. im psycho and your not article for the dpw paper.... Some of my problems have been spilled out over the various segments that have been aired by DPW television. I know that everyone loves Ol' Uncle Malibu... so I'm going to make my request short and, typical of my style, sweet. I need more alcohol. I could also use some money to buy more alcohol. Even better would be a ride to the packy so I won't be forced to drive there. Hey guys... that would be great. Oh yeah, I'm not picky... I'll drink whatever you decide to donate to the Malibu Alcohol Fund. Thank you very much.
