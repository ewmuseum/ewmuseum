DPW News & Updates The Controversy - The Anti-Hero Ice Kold captured the heavyweight championship by defeating Mitch Armour in the final round. However it was done with one punch at the end that knocked out Mitch. The ringside commentators disagreed at what they seen. Miss Toy said she saw a foreign object in Ice Kold's hands. Stan Riley said he saw Mitch get punched with no object in Ice Kold's hand. This war is far from over. A Fued from another fed enters DPW - Cable and Krypton have crossed paths in other feds. Now they bring there fued over to DPW. Both men cost each other a chance at the heavyweight championship by interfering in each others matches. Will these 2 guys get there differences settled? The Amish man returns! - Rufus Yoder is back in DPW and here to kick some arse. Rufus has entered the invitational battle royal. Rufus is a DPW hall of famer. Godfather takes offense! - The Godfather recently took offense to a smart ass remark made by Maker. Godfather may be always smiling with the ho's but ya dont want to piss off this big man. Big Daddy wants a shot - Big Daddy Magic claims that he deserves a shot at the heavyweight championship. Ice Kold says that he is nothing but some punk kid who came off the streets. Will Big Daddy prove him wrong? Rumors - Irish Devil was seen talking to the former Kings Of Wrestling owner. If Irish Devil gets him to sign up as a staff member, it may get the ratings up higher. The KOW was a very successful fed. The KOW had some DPW members competing there. Irish Devil, Polish Devil, Miss Toy, Stan Riley, and Brian Reese were in the KOW. - Rufus Yoder may be in search of a tag team partner for whenever the tag team titles go up for grabs.
