Hangover Heatwave

13 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hangover Heatwave Bulldozer VS Corey "Outlaw" Davis Irish Devil: Here we go with our opening match. Um wait a minute. Someone just came out through the crowd and jumped into the ring! Miss Toy: This guy just clotheslined Corey Davis over the top ropes. He kicks Bulldozer in the stomach and powerbombs him over the top ropes! Security is in the ring now. Irish Devil: This guy goes outside the ring and grabs a microphone. Guy: I came down here to chew bubble gum and to kick ass! And I'm all out of bubble gum! For those of you that dont know me, my name is "Insane" Eden Sykes and I want to fight! Miss Toy: Security is taking this guy out of here. Is he even a member of DPW? Irish Devil: He will be soon enough. I have to try to get this guy a DPW contract! Draw Lil Blue/with Sha-Sha VS Mr Love/with Venus ( Ding Ding Ding ) Irish Devil: Lil Blue with a kick to the stomach followed up by a piledriver. Lil Blue bounces off the ropes and drops a big legdrop. He hooks the leg 1....................2.....kickout! Miss Toy: Lil Blue has Mr. Love in a suplex position....Oh.....Venus Just grabbed the leg of Lil Blue and Lil Blue falls. Irish Devil: Here comes Sha Sha and she is chasing Venus around the ring. Venus jumps in the ring and Sha Sha jumps in after her. Sha Sha tackles Venus and unloads with lefts and rights. ( Ding Ding Ding ) Miss Toy: The referee just threw this match out. Irish Devil: Lil Blue and Mr. Love are still fighting. We are gonna have to get security to break this up. Double disqualifacation Draw Texas Death Match JT Styles VS Nikki "Mad Man" Nitro "Scheme" Jean Blunderland:Jean and Tony back in the house to bring you some more commentary. Tony Scamony: Yup, and we've got a good one here tonight. A Texas Death match between JT Styles, and Nikki Nitro. There isn't going to be an classical wrestling moves here. This is a no holds barred battle to the bitter end. As you can see, both men are ready and waiting to start. This is going to be great. The bell sounds. JT Styles is a man who is trying to step out of his older brother's shadow. and Nick Nitro has something to prove after blowing his World title shot last week. With both men having something to prove, this should be a good fight. JT Styles going to the attack, nailing a nice clothesline on Nitro, flooring him. JT Styles immediatly takes this to the outside. Remember, anything goes, and falls count anywhere. Alright! Chairs are always fun! JT Styles is lying in wait outside the ring with a steel chair. Nitro reaches over the ropes! CRACK! Listen to that echo throughout the arena. That could be a quick victory here. One Two NO! Nitro kicks out with authority. He's still very much in this match. JT Styles is complaining of a slow count. Well, now he gives up, and goes to the outside. Tables are just as awesome as chairs, but less versatile. Right. JT Styles is setting up the table, but Nitro comes from behind and nails a double low blow. That sort of reminds me of something called "Toll the Bell." But... nevermind. Nitro's getting up on the table with Styles! BOOM! A brutal Double arm DDT through the table. And now Nitro has control of this match. If this type of match has control. Nitro is beating Styles towards the entranceway. These two men are headed backstage. Now's when the fun begins! These two are in the backstage area, and Nitro is throwing Styles back and forth into the narrow hallway, bouncing him off the walls, so to speak. Styles lays limp at Nitro's feet, and Nitro puts one foot over his chest. One.. Two... NO! JT Styles got his shoulder up. That was a rather arrogant cover. Well, if I was wrestling some nobody's little brother, I'd be cocky too. That's your opinion. Call the match. Nitro is looking for something hidden in an equipment box here. Look! That's Styles' barbed wire 2 x 4. Such an overdone hardcore weapon! It's just some barb wire. What we need are barb wire ladders, and flaming chairs, something interesting. Nitro swings the 2 x 4, but JT Styles hits him with a blatent low blow. Jt Styles picks up the 2 x 4, and clocks Nitro in the face, and again. JT Styles goes behind Nitro, and begins to choke Nitro with the 2 x 4. This is perfectly legal. Nitro seems to be dimming here. Arm check. Drops once... Drops twice.... Drops three times! And a rather basic wrestling tatic- the choke out- ends this hardcore Texas Death Match. I can't believe the nobody's little brother won that match. Pathetic, really. Winner: JT Styles by chokeout. Rufus Yoder "The Amish Giant" VS Big Daddy Magic Tony Scamony: Tony and Jean back again to bring you a nicely devolping fued here in the DPW. The Christian Crusader, Rufus Yoder, the Amish Giant, versus the Spawn of Satan, Big Daddy Magic. "Scheme" Jean Blunderland: Big Daddy Magic has openly spoken out against Rufus Yoder's beliefs, and has gone as far as to burn bibles on both Yoder and Triple H in the past two weeks. This should be a good match. "Symphony of the Night" by Cradle of Filth plays as Big Daddy Magic comes out with a bible, lighter fluid, and some matches for his "after-match" ceremony. Didn't this guy have different theme music last week? I guess. Who pays attention to that anyway? "Amazing Grace" by SonicFlood plays and Rufus Yoder comes out. Rufus Yoder charges the ring. He gets in a spears Big Daddy Magic to the mat, and now is pounding away at his face. This is a vicsious attack by Rufus, I don't think this is what Jesus would have done, you think Tony? No, but I remember Edward Norton doing something like this in "Fight Club." Whatever. The referee is pulling Rufus Yoder off of Big Daddy Magic, and it looks like Rufus might have broken BDM's nose. He is bleeding profusely. BDM is rearing up, and he looks oblivious to his nose, and he is seeing red! BDM charges Rufus Yoder, and clotheslines Rufus Yoder and the referee over the top rope! This is going to be worse than the Texas Death match now that the ref's out of commission. BDM is now got a kendo stick, and whap, whap, whap! BDM is just wailing away at Rufus Yoder! I bet you Rufus didn't expect this type of match. Where's his god to protect him now? We'll see if Rufus' faith leads to help "from above." For now, just focus on the match, and leave religion out of this. BDM just SHOVED Rufus face-first into the ring post. The referee is stirring, and OH! BDM just stomped him one time for good measure. Big Daddy Magic seems to be truly hateful of Rufus Yoder. Big Daddy Magic is setting up two open chairs closly together. NO! NO NO NO! MY GOD! BIG DADDY MAGIC JUST HIT HIS PATENTED "SOUL CRUSHER," SLAMMING RUFUS BETWEEN THE TWO CHAIRS. This is getting violent. OH! COME ON! Big Daddy Magic just nailed the referee in the back with a chair! He has no intention on winning this match, he just wants to hurt Rufus Yoder. Can you blame him? Shut up! Big Daddy Magic is now throwing a table into the ring. It looks like Rufus Yoder has also been busted open, his face has begun to bleed. Big Daddy Magic rolls in the referee, and now rolls in Rufus Yoder. Big Daddy Magic is setting up the table. An irish whip, and BAM! A Frankenstiener through the table! Big Daddy Magic is clearing the ring of the table, and now he is unlacing his boot. HEY! An old Ice Kold trick! BDM has a chain hidden in his boot. Big Daddy Magic is measuring Rufus Yoder, but WAIT! THE HAND OF GOD! A spinebuster powerslam. Ding ding ding! What the hell's going on? The referee never even counted. Oh, look at this, the zebras always gotta ruin our fun. Another referee has come down, and is conversing with the first referee and our ring announcer. Let's wait for the official word. Mike Oxlong: Ladies and gentlemen, the referee has informed me, that due to a disqualification, your winner, "The Amish Giant," Rufus Yoder! Tag Team Championship Krypton & Cable VS Buffy Summers & Christian Sheetz ( Ding Ding Ding ) Irish Devil: And Buffy wastes no time nailing Krypton with a hurricurana. Miss Toy: She grabs Cable and nails Cable with a tornado DDT. Irish Devil: Buffy quickly goes up to the top ropes and nails Krypton with a moonsault. Miss Toy: She quickly goes up to the top ropes again and nails Cable with a frog splash. Irish Devil: Christian Sheetz is in the ring. He tagged himself in by smacking Buffy on the ass as she was climbing the ropes. Miss Toy: Christian goes for a figure 4 on Krypton...no...small package by Krypton 1...................2...............3!!!! Irish Devil: Buffy looks pissed. She clotheslines Christian! Now she scoops up Christian and climbs the ropes with him. Miss Toy: A tombstone piledriver off the top! She is leaving the ring in a pissed off way. Irish Devil: I hate to see what her ex boyfriends look like. Winners Krypton & Cable by pinfall still tag team champions Intercontinental Championship Godfather VS HHH ( Ding Ding Ding ) Irish Devil: Godfather pushes Triple H in the corner and nails him with a chop and a right hand. Irish whip into the turnbuckle goes Triple H. Miss Toy: Here comes the ho train! Wham! Triple H is hurt. Irish Devil: Godfather with the pimp drop! A cover 1................2................3!! An easy victory for the Godfather. Winner Godfather by pinfall still intercontinental champion Main Event Heavyweight Championship "The Anti-Hero" Ice Kold/Tarja Turunen VS "The Maniacal Mercenary" Mitch Armour ( Ding Ding Ding ) Irish Devil: Godfather is the special guest referee for this match. Why? Who knows? This match is a hell in a cell match. Mitch asked for it. Mitch with a kick to the midsection. Twirling powerbomb by Mitch. Mitch goes for the sharpshooter but Ice Kold counters with the Kone of Kold. Mitch grabs the ropes forcing the break of the hold. Miss Toy: Ice Kold with an f5 right into a rock bottom! Irish Devil: Ice Kold goes up to the top ropes. A shooting star legdrop off the top! He hooks the leg 1......................2..............kickout! Miss Toy: Ice Kold locks in the anti-lock and Mitch grabs the ropes. Irish Devil: Ice Kold goes for a DDT but Mitch picks up Ice Kold and drops him down on his back. Miss Toy: Mitch is now trying to shake off the cobwebs. Mitch with a spear on Ice Kold. Mitch is signaling for the hellbound! Irish Devil: He picks up Ice Kold but Ice Kold flips over Mitch and Ice Kold nails the Kold kradle driver. He hooks the leg 1........................2....................3!!! Ice Kold retains the title. The cell is raised back up to the roof. Miss Toy: Oh God! Here comes Old Man Jim into the ring with his DPW toy belts that he bought in toys r us. He has a microphone. Old Man Jim: I am willing to put my DPW heavyweight and intercontinental belts up against any of you guys! Irish Devil: The fans are laughing at Old Man Jim because he wears toy championship belts to the ring. Old Man Jim: If you's win I'll leave DPW but if I win you's have to rejoin BAWF so I can win more matches in my own fed. And you's have to go with every storyline that I give you's! Miss Toy: The cell has just been lowered again! Old Man Jim just realized he is trapped! Irish Devil: Ice Kold locks in the anti-lock. Old Man Jim is crying like a baby and he just tapped out! Miss Toy: The Godfather nails Old Man Jim with the pimp drop. A cover 1................2................3!!! Godfather pins Old Man Jim! Irish Devil: Mitch Armour nails Old man Jim with the hellbound! Mitch puts his foot on Old Man Jim's chest 1...............2...............3!!! Mitch pins Old Man Jim. Now the 3 of them throw Old Man Jim out of the ring. They start smashing his head into the cage. Old Man Jim is bleeding alot. He is crying and on the ground in pain! Ice Kold throws Old Man Jim's toy belts on top of him as they leave the cell and leave Old Man Jim a bloody mess on the arena floor. Winner "Anti-Hero" Ice Kold by pinfall still heavyweight champion
