Reckless Driving, Part 1

2 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BARTENDER: WELCOME EVERYONE TO RECKLESS DRIVING! TONIGHT WE WILL SEE 3 TITLE
MATCHES AND MUCH MORE. FOLKS, I'M BEING TOLD THAT SOMETHING IS GOING ON IN THE
BACK. ( CAMERA MAN ZOOMS IN TO WHERE POLISH DEVIL IS BEATEN UP HOLLYWOOD HUSTLER WITH
A PIPE. SECURITY IS JUST GETTING THERE NOW STOPPING THE POLISH DEVIL. )
SHAWN "THE STONER" KITTS VS HUMAN HURRICANE IRISH DEVIL: THIS SHOULD BE AN INTERESTING MATCHUP, A STONED HIPPY AGAINST A
MAN'S WHOSE NAME IS A WEATHER CONDITION! COMING DOWN THE AISLE IS SHAWN "THE
STONER" KITT, HE HAILS FROM NEWARK DELAWARE AND HE'S 6'2 AND WEIGHS 257 POUNDS!
POLISH DEVIL: AND HERE COMES HIS CHALLENGER IN HIS FIRST MATCH HERE IN DUBLIN
PUB HUMAN HURRICANE. HE HAILS FROM CALGARY ALBERTA CANADA AND IS 6'6 AND
WEIGHS 265 POUNDS. LETS GET THIS MATCH GOING! ( DING DING DING ) IRISH DEVIL: THEY LOCK UP! KITTS THROWS HURRICANE TO THE GROUND, HE GETS UP AND THEY LOCK UP AGAIN! KITTS THROWS HIM TO THE MAT AGAIN! POLISH DEVIL: WHAT A FUCKING RETARD, HURRICANE UP AND HE'S RUNNING AT KITTS, OH YEA THAT'S A SMART MOVE! DROP TOE HOLD BY KITTS! IRISH DEVIL: KITTS MAY BE STONED BUT SO FAR HE'S TAKING CONTROL OF THIS MATCH. KITTS HAS HURRICANE UP AND AN IRISH WHIP INTO THE ROPES, NO WAIT REVERSED AND FLYING ELBOW BY HURRICANE! POLISH DEVIL: FINALLY I'M TIRED OF WATCHING HIS ASS GET THROWN TO THE FLOOR. HURRICANE NOW STOMPING ON KITTS AS HE'S TRYING TO GET UP. HURRICANE SETTING KITTS UP FOR A NECKBREAKER AND EXECUTES IT BEAUTIFULLY. IRISH DEVIL: THE COVER....1...2...KICKOUT! KITTS IS UP NOW BUT LOOKS DAZED AND HURRICANE DELIVERS A KICK TO THE GUT, AND A CHIN BUSTER! POLISH DEVIL: HURRICANE GOING UP TO THE TOP, LOOKING FOR AN ELBOW OFF THE TOP,
BUT NO SKITTS MOVED! KITTS UP AND HE'S PULLING HURRICANE UP BY THE HAIR, AND A
SUPLEX BY KITTS! IRISH DEVIL: CAMEL CLUTCH BY KITTS, I HAVEN'T SEEN THIS MOVE IN AWHILE, KITTS APPLYING ALOT OF PRESSURE NOW, BUT HURRICANE WONT GIVE IN! POLISH DEVIL: BUT HE'S TOO CLOSE TO THE ROPES AND HURRICANE JUST BROKE THE
HOLD! HURRICANE IS HANGING OFF THE BOTTOM ROPE AND KITTS JUST BOUNCED OFF THE
ROPES, BASEBALL SLIDE TO THE OUTSIDE AND AN UPPERCUT BY KITTS!
IRISH DEVIL: KITTS GOING TO THE TOP, KNEE TO THE SIDE OF THE HEAD! THE HOOK OF
THE LEG AND A COVER.....1...2...SHOULDER UP! DAMN THAT WAS A QUICK COUNT!
POLISH DEVIL: KITTS PICKING UP HURRICANE LOOKING FOR A SUPLEX NO REVERSED AND A
DIAMOND CUTTER BY HURRICANE! GREAT COUNTER THIS SHOULD BE OVER....THE
COVER...1...2....KICKOUT!! HOW DID HE KICK OUT OF THAT! IRISH DEVIL: HURRICANE ON THE SECOND ROPE AND AN ELBOW TO THE STERNUM OF KITTS! HURRICANE HAS KITTS UP AND AN ABDOMINAL STRETCH APPLIED! THE REF LOOKING TO SEE
IF KITTS GIVES IN, BUT NO KITTS IS STILL IN IT! HURRICANE APPLYING MORE
PRESSURE NOW. POLISH DEVIL: AND GRABBING ONTO THE ROPES LIKE THAT HELPS TOO! TELL THIS MR
MAGOO OF A REF TO GET HIS HEAD OUT OF HIS ASS AND LOOK WHAT HURRICANE IS DOING!
IRISH DEVIL: THE REF JUST SAW IT NOW, AND HE BREAKS THE HOLD! THE REF WARNING
HURRICANE NOW, AND HE JUST PUSHES HIM ASIDE AND PICKS UP KITTS! ELBOW TO THE
STOMACH BY KITTS AND HE BOUNCES OF THE ROPES AND DELIVERS A FLYING CLOTHESLINE!
POLISH DEVIL: THE COVER......1....2...KICKOUT! KITTS IS STAGGERING TO HIS FEET
AND HE JUST GRABBED THE BONG, HES GONNA HIT HURRICANE WITH IT, BUT NO THE REF
CAUGHT IT AND HES TRYING TO GET IT FROM KITTS! WAIT! HURRICANE WITH A KICK TO
THE CUT AND A PEDIGREE!!! ITS OVER! IRISH DEVIL: THE PIN....1....2......NO KICKOUT!! HURRICANE DOESN'T KNOW WHAT TO DO NOW! HE'S DEFINITELY FRUSTRATED AND I DONT BLAME HIM! POLISH DEVIL: MAN I HAVE TO TAKE A HUGE PISS RIGHT NOW, THIS FUCKING MATCH
BETTER END SOON! HURRICANE PICKING UP KITTS NOW, WAIT LOOK AT THIS, HURRICANE
GOING FOR THE CROSSED FACE CHICKEN WING, BUT NO KITTS RAN INTO THE ROPES AND A
RUNNING BULLDOG!! IRISH DEVIL: KITTS TO THE TOP, LEG DROP ACROSS THE NECK! HURRICANE STAGGERING TO HIS FEET, IRISH WHIP BY KITTS.....DOUBLE ARM DDT!! THE COVER....1.....2..KICKOUT!! POLISH DEVIL: KITTS NOW LOOKING FOR THE BONG HIT, AND NAILS IT!! THE COVER...1...2...3!! KITTS WINS AND GIVES HURRICANE HIS FIRST LOSE IN HIS FIRST MATCH HERE IN DUBLIN PUB! IRISH DEVIL: THAT SURE ISN'T GOING TO HELP THE CONFIDENCE LEVEL! WHAT AN EXCITING MATCH AND NOW POLISH DEVIL IS GOING TO GO TAKE HIS PISS!! WINNER BY PINFALL= SHAWN "THE STONER" KITTS GERMAN DEVIL VS "HEARTBREAK KID" SHAWN MICHAELS IRISH DEVIL: THIS NEXT MATCHUP IS SURE TO BE A SHOWSTOPPA! HA HA HA! I FEEL
LIKE A HBK, AND SPEAKING OF WHICH HERE COME HBK MAKING HIS WAY DOWN THE AISLE.
HE'S 6'2 AND WEIGHS 210. HE HAILS FROM SAN ANTONIO TEXAS. POLISH DEVIL: NOW MAKING HIS WAY IS GERMAN DEVIL, HEY IS HE RELATED TO US? IRISH DEVIL: I AIN'T SURE, HOPEFULLY NOT! POLISH DEVIL: WELL OK! HE HAILS FROM NEW YORK CITY NEW YORK. HE WEIGHS 256 AND IS 6'8. ( DING DING DING ) IRISH DEVIL: THIS SHOULD BE A GOOD MATCH! A FORMER HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION AGAINST
A MAN LOOKING TO MAKE A NAME FOR HIMSELF, BUT WITH THE LAST NAME DEVIL THAT
SHOULDN'T BE HARD! POLISH DEVIL: THEY LOCK UP. ARM BAR APPLIED BY HBK. THAT'S LEADING TO SOMETHING
AND A CLOTHESLINE BY HBK! LEG DROP BY HBK, THE COVER....1.....KICKOUT!
IRISH DEVIL: GERMAN DEVIL UP NOW, IRISH WHIP BY HBK LOOKING FOR A BOOT TO THE
FACE, BUT NO! DROP KICK BY DEVIL! DEVIL BOUNCING OFF THE ROPES AND DELIVERS AN
ELBOW TO THE CHEST! THE COVER.....1....2...KICKOUT! POLISH DEVIL: GERMAN DEVIL NOW PICKING UP HBK LOOKING FOR A DDT AND NAILS IT! DEVIL ISN'T GOING FOR THE COVER, WHAT AN IDIOT! IRISH DEVIL: SHIT, HE'S GOTTA BE FAMILY THEN! HE'S GOING TO THE TOP, MOONSAULT
BY DEVIL BUT NO HBK MOVES! BOTH MEN ARE OUT! WAIT NO, HBK IS GETTING UP!
POLISH DEVIL: GERMAN DEVIL IS UP AS WELL HE'S RUNNING AT HBK, LOOKING FOR A
CLOTHESLINE, BUT NO HE DUCKS LOOKING FOR SWEET CHIN MUSIC BUT NO DEVIL SLIDES
UNDER IT AND A CHIN BUSTER! IRISH DEVIL: DEVIL PICKING UP HBK, LOOKING FOR A PEDIGREE, BUT NO BACK BODY
DROP BY HBK! HBK HAS DEVIL UP AND A DOUBLE ARM DDT!! THIS IS IT! THE
COVER...1...2...KICKOUT! POLISH DEVIL: HBK CAN'T SEEM TO PULL THE TRIGGER TO END THIS MATCH! HBK PICKING
UP DEVIL BUT NO DEVIL WITH PUNCHES TO THE GUT. DEVIL NOW GETTING MOMENTUM AND
BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES, BUT NO DROP TOE HOLD BY HBK. IRISH DEVIL: DEVIL RUNNING AT HBK AGAIN, HIP TOSS, DEVIL UP AND ANOTHER HIP
TOSS! IF THIS GUY WAS SMART HE'D STOP THAT! DEVIL STAGGERING TO HIS FEET, HBK
RUNNING AT HIM, BUT NO HE DUCKS, HBK BOUNCING OFF THE ROPES, A KICK TO THE
STOMACH BY DEVIL, HE'S GOT HIM SET UP FOR THE NAZI KILLER! POLISH DEVIL: HE NAILS IT! GERMAN DEVIL JUST NAILED THE NAZI KILLER...THE
COVER....1....2....3!!!! GERMAN DEVIL HAS DEFEATED THE FORMER CHAMP! WHAT A
MATCH! WINNER BY PINFALL= GERMAN DEVIL TRILLIONAIRE THAD THORTON VS MADMANN DEAN HAUGHT IRISH DEVIL: HERE COMES THE NEWCOMBER MADMANN DEAN HAUGHT. HE STANDS AT 6 FEET
3 INCHES AND WEIGHS 256 POUNDS. HE HAILS FROM SACRAMENTO CALIFORNIA.
DRE: DEAN IS IN THE RING AND HERE COMES HIS OPPONENT TRILLIONAIRE THAD THORTON. HE IS BEING ACCOMPANIED TO THE RING BY HIS MANAGER SUNNY. HE STANDS AT 6 FEET 5
INCHES AND WEIGHS 265 POUNDS. HE HAILS FROM PORT ST. JOE FLORIDA.
( DING DING DING ) IRISH DEVIL: DEAN CHARGES AT THORTON BUT THORTON CATCHES HIM WITH A DROP TOE
HOLD. THORTON WITH A FRONT FACE LOCK. THORTON BACKS DEAN UP TO THE CORNER AND
DELIVERS A KNIFE EDGE CHOP. AND ANOTHER. IRISH WHIP.....REVERSAL BY DEAN...AND
CATCHES THORTON WITH A BIG BACK BODY DROP. DRE: SCOOP SLAM BY DEAN. DEAN SETS HIM UP...BACK SUPLEX. DEAN IS PUTTING THE BOOTS TO THORTON. IRISH DEVIL: THORTON SLIDES TO THE OUTSIDE FOR A BREATHER. DEAN GOES RIGHT
OUTSIDE AFTER HIM. THORTON PUTS SUNNY IN FRONT OF HIM, USING HER AS A SHIELD.
BRILLIANT MOVE BY THORTON. DRE: THORTON SLIDES BACK INTO THE RING. DEAN SLIDES IN AS WELL BUT GETS CAUGHT BY A SERIOUS OF KICKS BY THORTON. IRISH DEVIL: THORTON NOW SETS UP DEAN....SUPLEX. WELL EXECUTED. A COVER 1.....2..KICKOUT. DRE: THORTON PICKS UP DEAN. DOUBLE UNDERARM SUPLEX. THORTON NOW UP TO THE
SECOND TURNBUCKLE. HE LEAPS AND DRIVES A FIST TO THE FACE OF DEAN HAUGHT. HOOKS
THE LEG 1......2...KICKOUT. IRISH DEVIL: THORTON PICKS UP DEAN. BACKBREAKER BY THORTON. HOOKS THE LEG 1.....2..KICKOUT. DRE: THORTON PICKS UP DEAN. IRISH WHIP....DEAN DUCKS THE CLOTHESLINE AND HITS ONE OF HIS OWN! DOWN GOES THORTON. IRISH DEVIL: DEAN LOOKS FIRED UP. THORTON IS BACK UP. DEAN UNLOADS WITH LEFTS
AND RIGHTS. DEAN WHIPS THORTON INTO THE TURNBUCKLE AND CATCHES HIM WITH A BELLY
TO BELLY SUPLEX. DRE: DEAN IS GOING FOR THE TAZ MISSION. HE'S GOT IT SLAPPED ON BUT THORTON RAMS HIM INTO THE TURNBUCKLE AND BREAKS THE HOLD. IRISH DEVIL; DEAN IS GOING FOR IT AGAIN. HE'S GOT IT ON THORTON AGAIN BUT HE'S
GOT TO GET THORTON ON THE GROUND. THORTON STILL STANDING AND FIGHTING IT.
THORTON WITH A BACK KICK. OH! DRE: A LOW BLOW BY THORTON. HE KICKED HIM RIGHT IN BETWEEN THE LEGS. THORTON

1.......2.......3!! THORTON WINS. WINNER = TRILLIONAIRE THAD THORTON BY PINFALL "CRAZY DEVIL" MITCH ARMOUR VS MONG IRISH DEVIL: HERE WE GO WITH OUR NEXT MATCH. "CRAZY DEVIL" MITCH ARMOUR IS
