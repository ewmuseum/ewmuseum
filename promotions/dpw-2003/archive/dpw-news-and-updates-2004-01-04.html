News & Updates

4 January 2004

DPW News & Updates Donovan victorious over the champ - Malibu Mike Donovan defeated Mitch Armour by a countout. Although he may not have got the title, it still leaves him noticable for a future rematch. So it is written - Rufus believes that its written in the bible that he will win the heavyweight championship. Is he right? Has Jesus Christ made Rufus Yoder the chosen one? The scaffold match - A dangerous match. Last time Thorton was in one he made the opponent fall 30 feet landing on to the ring. his opponent was never the same after that and had to end his career. Thorton wants to end another career. Will Spellbound cast his last spell here in the DPW. Mitch goes nuts - Mitch Armour is pissed off at president Irish Devil. He hates the way he runs his business. He stated to Irish Devil that he can run a fed alot better than Irish Devil. Mitch Armour said the same thing to him years back and went out to prove it. He failed and the fed died after his first card. Mitch claims he is older and more mature now. Irish Devil has stated that he is still a crybaby and will never be able to understand how to run a good fed and could never kick his ass. Former AOLCW wrestler joins DPW - Darin Thomas once competed in AOLCW. Now he joins the ranks of DPW and gets his first match in short term notice against Polish Devil. Is Darin ready to take a challenge in such short notice. Darin says he remembers Polish Devil from AOLCW and looks forward to his match against him. Title shot demand - Slicer is putting the challenge out to Mitch Armour. He wants a title shot. Mitch has yet to respond to Slicer's comments. Can Slicer prove to Mitch that he is a top contender? New Tag Team Champions - "The Greatone" Kevin Spindler and Ken Sharper were the underdogs in the tag team tournament and walked away tag team champions. Who will be there first title defense against? The gigallo is here - "Gigallo" Jason Kelly has signed with DPW. He is a cocky arrogant young man who thinks he is a gift from God. His first match is against Malibu Mike Donovan who is pretty much the same way. 2 big ego's will be battling it out. Rumors - Irish Devil may be negotiating with someone from outside the DPW to come in and be a part of the front office executives. There are no clues as to who he is but its almost a signed deal. You can bet if Irish Devil is hiring him, that cant be good for Mitch Armour. - Mitch Armour was heard screaming at Irish Devil about his loss to Malibu Mike Donovan. - DDP has talked with Irish Devil. Maybe contract negotiations? Note: If anyone ever wants to write there own article, e-mail it to me and I will copy and paste it into the next newsletter.
