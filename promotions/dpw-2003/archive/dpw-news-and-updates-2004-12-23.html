DPW News & Updates BAWF Strikes - 2 members from a little league wrestling league called BAWF interfered in the last card. They attacked Ice kold in the locker rooms and then went into the battle royal illegally and tossed out Mitch Armour and beat up Irish Devil and tossing him out of the ring. However it was Mitch Armour that chased them out of the DPW arena. DPW now has a resatraining order on the 2 unwanted misfits. The 2 overrated BAWF superstars did it as a publicity stunt to promote there bushkill fed. They recruited DPW wrestler Jeff Hardy to BAWF. Jeff Hardy didn't do too well in DPW. Jeff is hoping to make a big impact in BAWF where the competition is not as tough. Godfather wins Intercontinental Championship - The ho train picks up a new edition and that is the intercontinental championship. Godfather went through 13 other members in the battle royal to get that title. Can the ho train be stopped? Mitch in love? - Mitch Armour looks to be in love with new superstar Eris Fury. Eris is a beautiful lady who can make a grown man cry. Alot of jaws dropped in the locker rooms when Eris was walking around naked. Mr Love plays a hoax - A verbal agreement by BAWF owner and Mr Love went sour when Mr Love publicly humiliated the BAWF owner. Mr Love verbally agreed to sign a contract with DPW. The BAWF owner wanted to do the contract signing on national tv. When Mr Love came out he ripped up the contract and said he is staying with DPW. It was an embarrassing moment for BAWF. Cable and Krypton put differences aside - 2 guys that fueded for a long time in different leagues have decided to put aside there differences and try to win the DPW tag team championship belts. Both men are great competitors and want the gold. Can they do it as a team? A sin? - Rufus Yoder accidentally touched Eris Fury's tits in the battle royal. Rufus felt like he did something against the bible that he follows. Now he must wrestle Eris in a match. Rufus claims that the DPW is evil for signing this match. He feels uncomfortable with the whole situation. Will Eris take advantage of a emotional Rufus? Rumors - A DPW superstar is sick and tired of Rufus's religous beliefs and wants to put an end to it all. - Ice Kold is seeing a shrink in order to help him to not use illegal objects when wrestling in matches. Quote of the week "The Anti~Hero" Ice Kold:From now on, the "Manacial Mercenary" is dead.
