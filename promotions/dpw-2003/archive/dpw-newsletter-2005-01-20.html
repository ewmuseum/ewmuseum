DPW News & Updates Message from Irish Devil Hi everyone. Welcome to another edition of the newsletter. Just want to go over some things. First off, to all newcomers who dont know, if you recieve an e-mail from [detail withheld] you may send it to [detail withheld]. He is not a member of the DPW and he gets our mailing list from someone on the DPW mailing list. He is just a guy from a fed called BAWF who has a grudge against me because some of his members that he used to have in his fed jumped ship to DPW. If you somehow get on a mailing list that combines BAWF and DPW mailing lists, block the mail from the person who e-mails it to you. To block a members mail, go to get members profile. Type there screen name and click on block mail. Just dont reply back to there mail because then someone from DPW may get confused and block your screen name for replying back on there mailing list. Use only the mailing list that I send for roleplaying. Roleplays will not count unless they are sent on the mailing list that I send out. If anyone ever wants to put an article into the newsletter they can e-mail me whatever they want in the newsletter and I will put it in. It can be anything. You want to advertize your fed or advertize even anything outside of DPW, e-mail it to me whatever your advertizing and I'll put it on the newsletter. You want to write game shows, contests, polls, trivia, make fun of wrestlers, or anything, e-mail me and i will put it on the newsletter. Just make sure you write on the letter for the subject title "put on newsletter". On Thin Ice - Anti-Hero Ice Kold just managed to keep his heavyweight championship belt against Eris Fury in a double countout. However more wrestlers want to take that title from Ice Kold. Ice Kold has been dominating thus far in DPW. Intercontinental Championship surrendered - Godfather decides to give up the intercontinental championship to Rufus Yoder in order to get a shot at the heavyweight championship. Godfather says if he loses, he will quit DPW. Some members are hoping that he will lose. Womens Championship - The women of DPW will now have a title in there ranks for them only to compete for. Although the womens roster in DPW is small, DPW staff members are hoping with the new title that more women will join DPW. Bat Daddy? - Big Daddy Magic came to the ring looking to hit Mitch Armour with a baseball bat. When Mitch got out of the ring, Big Daddy Magic hit Krypton with the baseball bat. It is unsure as to why Big Daddy Magic did that to Krypton but everyone knows Big Daddy Magic isn't playing with a full deck. Mitch gone wild - Mitch Armour was backstage pshing people around and letting out his agression. Mitch wants that DPW heavyweight championship so bad tht he is starting to snap on people. Bikini Censorship - A bikini match goes sour when Tarja refused to show herself off with a bikini on. She said she didn't want to stoop to Eris's level. Then Buffy came out called Eris a slut. The 2 girls fought until it was brokened up by security. Quote of the week Buffy Summers: Now, as we lost spoke about Lil' Blue asked JT Styles to be his Tag Team Partner. ::In a mocking Voice:: Aw, how cute! Not! Rumors - A new wrestler may make an impact here in DPW. - Someone close to Buffy Summers just signed with DPW. - JT Styles wants to turn over a new leaf and get the fans to start respecting him. - Big Daddy Magic might be sleeping with Rufus's wife when Rufus isn't around. - Surprise members may enter the royal rumble next week. Irish Circle Irish Devil: Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to yet another edition of the Irish Circle. My guest today is the beautiful and sexy....Buffy Summers! Welcome Buffy. Please have a seat. Buffy Summers: Thank you Irish Devil. Irish Devil: Now Buffy, you have been turning alot of heads lately. You surprised everyone by interrupting the bikini contest. You seem to not like Eris Fury. Is it because she might have bigger tits than you? Buffy Summers: ::Laughs:: The way I feel about Eris Fury, is a way that I can not expalin. I mean, she does a lot of things I can't stand but, that dosn't mean I don't have respect for her. Irish Devil: What about Godfather? Do you respect the way he got rid of the intercontinental championship in order to get a quick title shot at the heavyweight championship? Buffy Summers: ::Shakes her head:: No way. The Godfather is an ungrateful bastard. He should be happy he had that Intercontinental Championship because, I can almost guarantee you that he won't win the World Heavyweight Championship. The reason why, he's not worthy enough. Irish Devil: You referred to Lil Blue as the dumbest wrestler around. Did you really mean that or were you just in a bad mood at the time? Buffy Summers: I really meant that. He hasn't done one thing that proved he's smart. Well, actually, I take that back. Teaming up with J.T. Styles was the one and only smart thing he has done thus far. Irish Devil: You mentioned that Mitch Armour is hideous looking. Does this mean you wont ever go on a date with Mitch? Buffy Summers: No, not at all. It just seems like whenever anyone ever talks about him, they make him sound like "The Hottie" of some soap opera and he's not even close. Irish Devil: You seem to not like Rufus Yoder for some reason. Why is that? Buffy Summers: Because, he's all about god and I can't stand people like that. Don't get me wrong, I'm not anti-god or anything it's just, I'm not a bible hugger. Plain and simple. Irish Devil: What about Cable. You say he is annoying. What is annoying about him? Buffy Summers: Oh, my god where to start?! ::Laughs:: First off, he dosen't deserve to be champion of any kind. I don't like Krypton either but, at least he has class. Cable is just a classless jerk who's biggest thrill in life is probably picking his nose. He and Lil' Blue would be perfect tag team partners, both stupid and annoying. Like two peas in a pod. Irish Devil: You are so sexy. Can I give you a kiss baby? Buffy Summers: You may on the cheek but, if you even come close to my mouth I'll make sure you'll never talk again. Irish Devil: ( Irish Devil kisses her cheek and then grabs Buffy's ass at the same time. ) Buffy Summers: You ass! ::Slaps him:: Your lucky I'm wearing a dress or I'd do worse! Irish Devil: ( Irish Devil starts trying to pull up Buffy's dress. ) Oh I like a girl who plays rough! ::Buffy kicks him in the head with her heel and walks off:: Irish Devil: Ouch! I guess that's all for this week folks. Next week I will interview someone who doesn't wear heels! Ouch!
