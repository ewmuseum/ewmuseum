Dublin Pub Wrestling Rules and application Our website is: http://dpw.s5.com/ Intro As of December 7th 2003 I have opened the doors back up in the DPW. Alot of good classic matches have been in this fantasy wrestling league. Some of the top entertaining wrestlers have been in this league over the past years. I shut down about 2 years ago because of working 2 jobs took most of my time. Now I am working 1 job so I will try my best to make it as great as it once was. I wrestled in feds under Irish Devil for years and it seems as though it gets harder and harder to find a great fed. So I decided to show everyone why I have such a good reputation of running one of the best entertaining wrestling leagues on the internet! Hall of Famers Trillionaire Thad Thorton Stan "The Man" Riley Polish Devil "Godly" Ken Davison Psycho Malibu Mike Donovan Badass John Miss Toy These superstars helped make this fed what it was back in those days. I'm hoping that some new talent will be edged in the hall of fame within time. Rules I'm not much for rules. I like a fed that has hardly any rules. If anyone has ever seen me roleplay you will know what I mean. However, God created 10 commandments. I guess everything needs rules so here it goes. - No TOS. Its immature. This is fantasy wrestling. Its only words being said. If you get offended by someone making fun of you even if its really bad, remember its just words. If you cant take it then this fed isn't for you. This fed is not for the weak hearted. Its not like they can reach out through the computer and start beating you up. So please dont be a crybaby. If you complain to me about someone's roleplay, I will remove you from the mailing list. There will be no crybabies in this fed. - Roleplays can be as long or as short as you want them to be. I judge by how entertaining your roleplay is. I am not a fan of an extremely long roleplay because usually it gets boring. Unless if you can do it in an entertaining way. I am looking for entertainment. Keep that in mind because that is what will win matches. - You can use other peoples characters in roleplays. I am probably the only fed left that still allows this rule. I am old school. I loved those feds back in the 90's where you can go and beat on someone in a roleplay and your opponent can roleplay back and do things to your character as well. Hey, it happens in the WWE, why cant it happen in fantasy wrestling? I know most people these days never been in a fed like that so they dont like changes. Well if your looking for a change than this fed is for you. - This is an e-mail fed. Roleplays get e-mailed to everyone in the fed by using the reply to all. - Both real and fake wrestlers are accepted in this fed. - One wrestler per screen name only. - If you want to plan an attack on somebody on the card, e-mail me exactly what you have in mind. I will keep a note on it and try to work it into the card. If you want to have interview time on the card, e-mail me your interview and I will copy and paste it on the card. E-mail me any ideas and I will try to work something out. I feel its important to work with everybody especially if they are participating with roleplaying. - Your wins, losses, and draws will be recorded and a stats report will come out once a week. - Cards will come out once a week unless if I develop a big helpful staff. I also plan on having a newsletter to come out once a week. Other ideas are up in the air as of right now. - Once every 2 months will be a huge pay per view special.
