DPW News & Updates Irish Devil talk show Irish Devil: Welcome to the Irish Devil talk show. Ladies and gentlemen please welcome my guest at this time, a member of the Enforcers...Pvt Holliday!!! Now Pvt Holliday, you seem to have a dislike for the Mongs. What is it that bothers you about them? Pvt Holliday: Well its their attitude. Irish Devil: I see. It looks like you formed a strong stable called the Enforcers. Are there gonna be any more members joining any time soon? Pvt Holliday: Well I don't know. We are always looking for new members to join. Irish Devil: Who else is on the list of people that the Enforcers dont like besides the Mongs? Pvt Holliday: We don't like that pussy, Trilpe X. We also don't like the heavyweight champion, "The German War Machine" Mathias Mathews Irish Devil: Ok. Thanks for the interview military dude. Pvt Holliday: your welcome...that's it?....i thought there would be more Irish Devil: Your time is up pansy. Now get off my show before I stick my Irish foot up your military ass!!! Pvt Holliday: bring it on, you fucking idiot ::::::::: Irish Devil smashes his bottle of thunderbird over Pvt. Holliday's head. Pvt. Holliday gets up and says: Is that all you got?" and kicks Irish Devil in the stomach and delievers a USA-Bomb. Irish Devil is out cold. Pvt. Holliday stands up and says: "Don't fuck with me or you will wish you hadn't:::::::::: Prodigy split - A turn of events occured when Mitch Armour was defending his heavyweight championship title against his Prodigy friend Mathias Mathews. During the match another Prodigy member Jason Kelly came out and helped Mathias cheat to win the heavyweight championship title from Mitch. They backstabbed Mitch Armour and abandoned the Prodigy. Now Jason and Mathias call themselves Prodigy Sons Inc. Jason and Mathias have made some new enemies like the rest of the Prodigy members. Mr. President shocks everyone - In an upset victory Bill Clinton wins the intercontinental championship with the help of his new found friends called the Enforcers. Bill is now known as the chief og command in the Enforcers. The Enforcers look like a tough new stable in the DPW. Its war!!! - Sgt Mike Tatum and Pvt. Holliday have declared war on the Mongs. There last match with the Mongs there was explosions, gun shots, stabbings, and other sick stuff. This war is far from over. Mitch brings back hardcore - Mitch Armour was once known as a great hardcore wrestler. Then as years went on Mitch became a great all around wrestler. Now the old Mitch looks to be back. Mitch has been running around swinging a barbwired bat at people ever since he was backstabbed by Jason and Mathias. The rookie defeats the legend - In a shocking event, Triple X defeated Malibu Mike Donovan. Mike's lawyer claims that Triple X cheated and is trying to get the ruling reversed. Triple X has been trying to form a new stable called the New B. However the last 2 people he asked to join turned there back on Triple X. "The Greatone" joins the Pack - Kevin Spindler has become the newest member of the Pack. Kevin is the first former member of the Prodigy to join the Pack.
