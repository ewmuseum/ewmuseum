DPW News & Updates Message from Irish Devil Hi everyone. Welcome to another edition of the newsletter. Just want to go over some things. First off, to all newcomers who dont know, if you recieve an e-mail from [detail withheld] you may send it to [detail withheld]. He is not a member of the DPW and he gets our mailing list from someone on the DPW mailing list. He is just a guy who has a grudge against me because some of his members that he used to have in his fed jumped ship to DPW. If anyone ever wants to put an article into the newsletter they can e-mail me whatever they want in the newsletter and I will put it in. It can be anything. You want to advertize your fed or advertize even anything outside of DPW, e-mail it to me whatever your advertizing and I'll put it on the newsletter. You want to write game shows, contests, polls, trivia, make fun of wrestlers, or anything, e-mail me and i will put it on the newsletter. Unstoppable - It seems that no one can defeat Anti-Hero Ice Kold for that heavyweight championship. However the list of new talent just keeps walking into DPW. Many more people want a chance to become the champion. Godfather upset - Apparently the Godfather wants more than just the intercontinental championship. He wants the heavyweight championship as well. However it is rare that the DPW executives will book a match to unify the titles. Godfather has stated that he refuses to wrestle until he gets a shot at the heavyweight championship and has threatened to go to BAWF. Irish Devil has stated that if he doesn't appear on next card then he will automatically forfit the intercontinental championship to Big Daddy Magic. When Irish Devil was told by reporters that Godfather may go to BAWF, Irish Devil laughed and said good luck. Wrong move - A big mistake by Christian Sheetz costed him and Buffy to lose a match against the tag team champions. Buffy was beating up both men when Christian decided to tag himself into the match by smacking Buffy's ass. Then he lost the match. Buffy was pissed and beat up Christian. Rufus and Magic..far from over - Its good verses evil. They just cant stand each other and nothing seemed to have gotten settled last card. Rufus won the match but Big Daddy Magic once again burnt a bible on Rufus. Rufus still wants to get even for when Big Daddy Magic kidnapped his wife. Who's hotter? - Eris Fury has challenged Tarja turunen to a bikini match. Apparently Eris wants to prove that she looks sexier than Tarja. I'm not sure what this has to do with wrestling but it should be fun to watch. Insane - "Insane" Eden Sykes appeared last card uninvited and beat up Corey "Outlaw" Davis and Bulldozer. Eden said he wanted to show up just to fight. Irish Devil has signed him into DPW now and will be fighting next card. Quote Of The Week Rufus Yoder: Yea, I say to thee Gene, God did make Roaches but he gave me the power to stomp them out, and killeth them, and God is giving me power now to destroy Fat Daddy and let the world see where the Power truly Lies AMEN! Rumors - Godfather may be out a few cards or may want a release out of his contract if his demands arent met. - Eris Fury may have a stalker.
