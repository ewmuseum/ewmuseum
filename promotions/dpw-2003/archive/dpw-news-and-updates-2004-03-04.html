Trillionaire Thad Thorton asks for one more chance - Thad has been on a losing streak. However he has asked Irish Devil for a shot at the heavyweight championship. Irish Devil yelled at him for his lousy performances these past few months. Can Thad redeem himself by becoming the champion? Just the perfect singles wrestler? - "Just Perfect" Jason Kelly lost the tag team titles and blames his tag partner Kevin Spindler for losing the belts. Jason is giving Kevin another chance to redeem himself in another tag team match. However, if they lose this match, it looks like Jason is gonna just remain a singles wrestler. Wolfpack3000 makes an impact - Last card wolfpack3000 proved that they have a great stable. The Mongs won the tag team championships and DDP defeated the undefeated Malibu Mike Donovan which shocked everybody and proved that DDP is a top contender. DPW staff even put DDP up for an intercontinntal championship match for the spring bash PPV. The strange thing is that DDP said he doesn't want the title shot. No word yet as to if the DPW staff is gonna remove DDP from the title shot. The Long awaited rematch? - "The Maniacle Mercenary" Mitch Armour and Malibu Mike Donovan have been fueding for the longest time. There last match ended with Mike recieving a countout victory. Last card Mitch interfered in Mike's match causing Mike to lose his undefeated wrestling streak. DPW staff has decided that these 2 men will have to have a rematch. The big rematch will take place in a couple of weeks. Bill Fury demands competition - "The Big Bad" Bill Fury has demanded competition in the DPW. He is disappointed so far with his matches and asks for better. Who wants to fight this monster? Prodigy new member - There will be a new member joining the prodigy. The prodigy has yet to announce who the new member is but they seem to be excited about him joining the group. Where does Slicer stand? - Sometimes it appears as if Slicer is a member of the prodigy. But actually he is not a member. The prodigy has not allowed him in the group. Slicer has been trying to impress the prodigy in hopes of becoming a prodigy member. Malibu drunk again? - Malibu Mike Donovan had his lawyer entering arenas during Mike's tv time scheduled interviews and the lawyer actually spoke for Mike. It seems very odd but sources have spotted Mike drunk on the days that the lawyer spoke for Mike. Mike had a bad drinking problem years back. We thought that the problem was over. Is Mike still an alcoholic? Triple X beat down - It looked like another episode of Rodney King getting beat up. However it was Triple X getting beat up by the Wolfpack3000. Then they threw TripleX in the Prodigy locker room and told the Prodigy to finish the job. Why is Triple X hated so much? Triple X has a chance to fight DDP next card. DDP organized the attack on Triple X. The Apocalypse has arrived - "Apocalypse" Joey Armstrong has signed with DPW. This man is known not to care about rules. He has been banned from wrestling leagues. However in DPW, his behavior is more than welcomed. His fowl mouth and violent ways will make a huge impact here in DPW. Dublin Bowl - The next card will be called Dublin Bowl. It will have randomly selected tag teams competing against each other. The winners will go into a batle royal. Whoever wins the battle royal will be known as the man who won the Dublin Bowl. The big question will be who each wrestler will be teamed up with. It could be a friend. It could be an enemy. It could be someone you dont know too well. Whoever it is, they have to depend on that partner to help them advance into the battle royal. It should be a fan favorite to watch. Stables - Lets clear everything up. Here are the stables in the DPW and the members that are in each stable. Sources have stated that he is not happy at the way things are going for him and that he wants to be more recognized. We will keep you posted. - "The Greatone" Kevin Spindler has been seen talking to a member of the Pack. Is Keven tired of Jason bitching to him about losing the tag titles? Is Kevin looking to jump ship or was he just talking trash to the enemy? - Rumor has it that Rufus had an arguement with another Prodigy member. Trouble in the Prodigy group? - Rumor has it that all 3 stables are interested in having "Apocalypse" Joey Armstrong join there stable. Can this guy be friends with anybody?
