Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WRESTLING is FidoNet's own dedicated professional-wrestling discussion echo, self-titled on its own directory listing “Fido Pro Wrestling (FPW)”: “an echo for discussion of professional wrestling. All federations/organizations covered. This is THE professional wrestling echo for Fidonet. Open access to all.” Real names were required to post from March 1996 on — the same flag WRESTLE_RPG carried, and one this echo added rather than started with. Its own listed volume ran to four hundred messages a week when the earliest surviving directory holds it, was revised down to three hundred that same March, and was restated monthly at thirteen hundred by 1999 — against WRESTLE_RPG’s own twenty a week, the real sport drawing a far bigger room on the same network than the fantasy game about it ever did.

Its own record is longevity without an origin or a close. WRESTLING is present in the earliest EchoList capture this project holds, from December 1995, already running rather than newly listed, and present still in the latest, from December 2000. No FidoNews backbone-change column names it, in either direction, across the two years of the newsletter's own run searched for this accession — it was already carried before that window opens and still carried after it closes, with no institutional record of when it began or when, if ever, it formally ended.

Two more wrestling echoes rode the same network under one moderator by late 1997. WRESTLING_CHAT, “a free Speech Wrestling discussion conference for wrestling participants and fans,” gatewayed to its moderator's own homepage, was advertised to sysops by January 1997; PRO_WRESTLING, “News from the WCW & WWF,” followed that October, billed alongside its sibling in one classified ad as the pair to carry for “all the latest wrestling news both in the WCW and the WWF” — a narrower, real-wrestling- news charter distinct from WRESTLING's own open, all-federations one.

What the directory itself records, edition by edition, is an echo that barely changed and then quietly changed hands. Its own four-sentence description is identical in December 1995 and in December 2000, reflowed twice by the directory’s own format changes and never rewritten. Twenty-one dated revisions are stamped on the entry across those five years, and only four of them alter anything a reader would see: the volume figure twice, the real-names flag once, and the moderator list once. Two people ran it for its first three years. The revision stamped 10 March 1999 drops the first of them without comment and leaves the second listed alone; five months later the echo’s own Origin field moves to that surviving moderator’s node, and the year after that it moves back, with nothing in the directory explaining either. The entry still says “Status: Active” in the last edition anyone banked.

Both WRESTLING and WRESTLING_CHAT outlived the backbone era as their host network thinned to almost nothing. Their gatewayed Usenet shadows, fidonet.wrestling and fidonet.wrestling_chat, show them still alive into the 2010s by one rescued archive's own count, but reduced by the mid-2000s to a syndicated wrestling column reposted a handful of times and, increasingly, spam — a fate this project's own search confirmed but does not serve here, since spam carries no content of the echo it once was.

No message WRESTLING itself carried, from any year of its own long run, has been located. One contemporary tagline elsewhere on the network jokes about filing a fan for the echo's own Hall of Fame — the only other trace of its culture found outside the bare fact of its own listing.

Storylines

No wrestling was staged here to recount Written by AI, 2026

WRESTLING was a discussion echo, not a promotion: its own charter is “discussion of professional wrestling. All federations/organizations covered,” and every document that survives of it is the directory paperwork describing that charter. No card was ever run in it, no wrestler was ever named in it in any surviving line, and not one message of its own five-year run has been recovered from any archive. There is no arc to recount, and inventing one would be the only way to have one.
