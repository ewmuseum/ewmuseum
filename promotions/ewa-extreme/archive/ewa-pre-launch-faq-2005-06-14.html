How do I submit a wrestler? +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 1. Overview (Out of Character) This EWA is back!!! Yes, I know there have been various EWA's all over the place. Since it was an EWA where I got my start, I plan on making this place an easy and fun experience for all that have will be involved. This FAQ is going to be very straight forward because I want there to be a fairly open environment for building the foundation of this federation. +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 2. Who is in charge of the EWA? (In-Character) The EWA is owned by Trineo Wrestling Promotions, and the Owner/CEO of that company is Johnny Sledge. When everyday operations on EWA had shut down back in 2000, Sledge stepped in and bought the rights to the name and the tape library from partners Art Blake and Wayne Rothschild. Somewhere in the last two or three years, Sledge has taken all of his wrestling properties (the dead EWA name, a wrestling school, and an extensive tape library from promotions all over the world) and created Trineo Wrestling Promotions. +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 3. Where is the home base of the EWA? EWA is going to be based out of the Trineo Wrestling Promotions Offices in Boston, Massachusetts. The EWA is going to run its weekly house shows out of the Wonderland Ballroom, in Revere, MA. The Wonderland Ballroom is one level on the second floor of a building whose day to day operations is Greyhound Racing. The only way to the Ballroom is through the front entrance and then up an escalator. There is a small lobby area where tickets are taken before getting into the main area where the ring and seats are set up. seats surround the ring on three sides, as there is a small divider that prevents seats from being put on that side (This is where the announcer "table" is set up). On the opposite side of that wall is where the entrance from the locker room is. There is no big video screen or anything like that, just a red curtain and blaring speakers to pipe out the ring entrance music. on either side of the entrance are concession stands. A small one to the right that raps around to ticket taking area. The other concession stand is longer and the small amount of wallspace on that side left has a souvenir stand where EWA memorabilia can be purchased. On the back wall is where the restrooms are, along with a back entrance/exit to the Ballroom which is not used by the fans. The ceilings are about 14 feet high, so there cannot be too much crazy high flying done in the Ballroom +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 4. Personalities * Owner/Booker - Johnny Sledge * Sledge has wrestled all over the world and is one of the most popular wrestles on the planet. As owner/booker expect his finger prints all over the promotion, but don't expect him to be out in the public all that much. If he is needed in the open, he will show his face, other than that don't expect Sledge all that much. * Announcer - [name withheld] * Long time EWA play-by-play man, and is now the lone announcer. He is extremely knowledgeable about the sport and makes it obvious through his commentary. He will be the host of Extreme Measures and (for now) will be the only announcer for any Live Specials. * Ring Announcer - Jayson Lyons * Former commentator will now be the EWA's ring announcer. He started with EWA before they closed in 2000, and has since been doing work in ring announcing and other wrestling related tasks with various promotions up and down the east coast. * Interviewer - Brandon Edwards * Brandon was a rare person who came into the wrestling business knowing he could not be a star. He does not have the body to survive the rigors of pro-wrestling, but his love for the sport brought him to Sledge's wrestling school. His great mind for the sport has made him one of Sledge's most trusted lieutenants. +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 5. What are the actual show formats? The EWA has inked a deal with the local WB affiliate in the Boston area. WB 56 has agreed to show a one hour program every Friday night at midnight. The deal will also include a live to tape special to air on Sundays at 5pm every six to eight weeks. Starting off, the EWA will be running one house show a week, on Saturdays * Extreme Measures * This is the weekly program that will be airing on Friday's at Midnight. This is where all the promos will appear, and usually there will be one or two highlighted matches from the past house shows. * House Shows * EWA House Shows happen every Saturday night with a 7pm bell time. All the EWA superstars will be on the show. * EWA Presents * These will be the live to tape specials. The culmination of all the big angles will happen here. Expect big things at these events. - OOC NOTE - Here are the basics. Extreme Measures is the combo flash card along with a highlighted full commentary match or two. I am treating these like the old school ECW Syndicated Shows. The House Shows will be summary style shows, but will have enough detail to progress stories. The live specials will be full commentary style, and those I will probably need help on. +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 6. What titles are in the EWA? For now there will only be 2 Titles in the EWA. They are the EWA Championship and the TV Championship. A Tag Team Title might be added in the future if the need arises. +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 7. How do I win? There are three parts to this, and are not the usual three parts you see in most federations. The first part is your character. I am looking for something original, as well as cool. If I see 10 Undertaker ripoffs I will be sad. Second is roleplays. Based on the premise that we have one hour to get all the promos in, and maybe a highlighted match or two, They should not be too long. I am going to put a min and max on them just to keep them all in the same range. All roleplays, unless you get permission to do otherwise, must be between 3 and 7k. Last is a good angle. I want everybody to have something going on, so the better the storyline the better your character will do. Notice I don't have any strats. I do not require them, because sometimes they are not as helpful as a good application. If you want to send one in, you can, but it will not influence if you win or lose. Also, in regards to things that will not help you win/lose, you can do pre or post match promos, but they MUST be summary style (as that is how the house shows are). And like the strat, even if you send it in does not mean it will get used. +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+ 8. How do I submit a wrestler?
