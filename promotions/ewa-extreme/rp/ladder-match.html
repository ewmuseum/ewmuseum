Ladder Match

7 April 2008

The One and Only · The Model · Andy · Phoenix · Red Steel · War Master · Dark Storm · King · “The Real Deal” Jimmy Steele

The one and only stand up from the desk and takes a piece of paper to his door. He puts the piece of paper on the outside the door and it reads. LADDER MATCH AT ROYAL DEMOLITIONS The first six people to sign this will get a world title shot at the PPV ROYAL DEMOLITIONS. (NO TAG TEAMS ALLOWED SO ONLY ONE PERSON FROM EACH TEAM) 1......World champ 2...... 3...... 4...... 5...... 6...... The one and only looks and smile at it and then closes the door. Andy and the model are by the CEO's office when they see the message on the door. Andy:" yes my chance at the world title!!" Model:" wow wow I am a 3 time world so i should get the title shot!" Andy: " ok lets have thumb wrestling match to see who get the title shot" They have their thumb wrestling match and some how model wins and the andy looks shocked. Model starts to sign the form but signs all 5 spaces. the one and only comes and sees what he has done and slaps the model. One and only:" you idiot, i knew some one would do that, thats why i made any other one!" The one and only pins another sheet. One and only:" right this time one space for one name!!!!!!!!!!!!" The one and only walks in to his office as the model signs his name on the new sheet. Andy: "you know what you are dumb!" Model:"but i thought if i wrote on the space it would just me vs the world and when i win the title at caged i wouldn't need to go through the match!" OXR walk off. scene ends Red Steel and Phoenix are walking down the hallway towards Phoenix's office. Phoenix 'hey Steel you here about the real deal asking for a hardcore match as well" Red Steel looks at phoenix. Red steel "you think that little jerk bothers me i mean look at him hes a joke I've seen midgets scarier than him i mean come on you think he has a chance to beat me" Phoenix stops and looks past Red Steel as if he didn't here him say anything. Red Steel "hey man you listening" Phoenix "yea man of course i am and no i don't think he can beat you" Red Steel so why did you stop and look past me as if you were avoiding my question" Phoenix "oh that i was looking at the notice on the door of the CEO'S office" Red steel turns and looks at the notice. Red Steel "well i would like to but I'm after another hardcore tittle rain" Phoenix looks at Red Steel as if he is crazy. Phoenix "well if you don't want a world tittle shot i will take it" Phoenix sighs his name on the notice on the CEO'S door. Red Steel "well I've gotta go prepare for my matches see you around.Phoenix enters his office and Red Steel walks off. *War Master walks by the door and sees it and stops and smiles.* War Master: Sweet here is my chance to regain my stature as on of the best. *War Master goes into is pocket looking for a pen but does not find one, and he asks a employee for a pen and he gives him one. War Master: thank you. *War Master signs the paper and hands the pen back, and War Master walks off.* War Master: I really need to be more prepaird. *The camera fades to black.* Dark storm is walking around back stage he sees an employee. Dark Storm "hey you" the employee turns and points at himself. Dark Storm "yea can you help me find this place" Dark storm hands the employee a piece of paper. Employee "oh this yea go down here and turn left after the ceo's office" Dark storm points down the hallway. Dark Storm "down there and left" Employee "yes" the employee give Dark Storm his paper back. Dark Storm "thanks man' Dark storm walks down the hallway looking at room doors to see which one is the CEO's office when he finds it he notice piece of paper on saying LADDER MATCH AT ROYAL DEMOLITIONS The first six people to sign this will get a world title shot at the PPV ROYAL DEMOLITIONS. (NO TAG TEAMS ALLOWED SO ONLY ONE PERSON FROM EACH TEAM) Dark storm "Hey what do you know i could win gold in my first match" Dark Storm sighs the paper and walks off and turns left down the hallway. *just as dark storm turns the corner he is superkicked by king* *king stands over him* King:Welcome to EWA kid *king walks off down the hallway to commander ds locker room* as king leaves dark storm on the floor he sees the clipboard on the door King:you know i think its time i get into the title picture *king signs the clipboard and walks away* the real deal is walking down the corridor doing a little dance when he sees the clipboard and he goes over. "Ok this is my chance to bring the gold back to a real english not the fakes like the others in this company who acts american." The real deal signs the form and then walks off.
