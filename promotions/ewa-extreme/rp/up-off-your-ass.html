Up off your Ass

23 May 2008

Phoenix · Red Steel · The One and Only

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Phoenix and Red Steel burst into the CEO'S office. the one and only turns.Phoenix walks up to his desk and slams his fists on his desk. Phoenix "hey what the hell is going on here you are letting the chimps jun the zoo you need to get some control over those jack asses out there and stop sitting on you fat ass sipping on the drink so much" Red Steel sits down and leans forward. Red Steel "you see if you don't step up and do yo job what happened to justice will look like a rain drop in the ocean so if you want some wrestlers for the ppv i would get your act together" Phoenix and Red Steel wait for a reply.
