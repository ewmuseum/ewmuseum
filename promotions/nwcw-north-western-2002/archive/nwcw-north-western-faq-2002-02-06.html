Center Stage." I. About the league. 1.What is the NWCW? The NWCW is the regional development federation for IWF/WOW. It's purpose is similar to that of "real" reigonal federations like the OVW or HWA, in that it will give new prospects a place to be seen, as well as serving to showcase the talents of wrestlers that have yet to find their niche in other federations. It will be used as a place for wrestlers to work on problem areas, or to get noticed and await a roster position. If you are not doing too well in I/W, they might send you down for a few weeks to work on the problem area(s) and give you time to come back, most likely rejuvenated and with fresh ideas. 2.Who Runs the NWCW? NWCW is ran by a few handlers, mainly [name withheld]. Also helping out are [name withheld], [name withheld] along with other IWF/WOW alumni and roster. Any help is greatly appreciated as we will be a small time federation. 3.What titles are there in NWCW? Hmm.. Good question. NWCW will have it's only titles seperate from IWF/WOW. NWCW will carry three titles. The main title of course is the Heavyweight Title. The NW title (Northwest) is the secondary title, the IC of sorts-- and NorthWest Tag Titles end the list of titles to be won in the NWCW. II. Rules and Regulations. 1.How are matches determined? Matches will be determined by roleplaying, character development and angle. There will be light strategy as it's more of a place to develop your character and get noticed than to win matches. It's nice to carry the Heavyweight title, it's even better to get notice and get the call up to IWF/WOW. 2.What are the rules? The centerpiece of this federations is self-improvement. This is a place of fun, so try not to curse, fornicate with animals in character roleplays... play nice. All other basic rules that are "standard" in your conventional federations also apply. We ask that you do not use other characters in your RP if you do not have permission, etc. See the FAQ on the I/W web page if you're not sure about something, and when all else fails, let common sense and good judgment be your guide. 3.How do I get matches? If you have an idea you'd like to see played out in a match, come to us. We're pretty open and will basically try most ideas as we are trying to help you. 4. How long are matches? Ok, here's the tricky part about this fed. Most of the matches will be summarized, except for supercards, PPVs, and whatnot. In-rings are still encouraged, as are backstage interviews-- but this isn't a place for 65k classics, it's a place to get noticed and to have fun. (Have we mentioned that?) 5. Roleplaying? We will send out a packet-- plus all the in-rings and backstage segments you'd like to do is fine by us. 6. Applying Send in any application you have to me at [detail withheld] If you don't have an application-- Give us the basics, character name-- height, weight, gimmick-- entrance music... You can fill out the real app when you hit I/W. :) Best of luck to you all! Hope you join and have a great experience with us.
