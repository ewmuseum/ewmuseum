Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The EWA staged its own first pay-per-view, Extreme Assault, on 1 August 1998, introduced on air by its own President, Joe, alongside Vice President PJ and colour commentator “Handsome” Dan Knight.

The card opened with a hardcore City Match fought through the streets and a mall parking lot, won by Antonio “El Desperado” with a swandive headbutt off a mall roof, and an Extreme Tables Match in which Acid Overkill powerbombed “Wolverine” Johnny Rotten through a stack of tables. A three-way dance staged inside a hospital ended when Lock Lowry powerbombed a corpse taken from an examination room onto Carnage for the pin, then set the room on fire on his way out.

A five-team Tag Team Battle Royal crowned the Extremists as new champions after a rival team, Rampage, turned on their own temporary allies at the finish; a nine-man Cruiserweight Battle Royal, fought partly with Johnny Rotten handcuffed to the ropes by his own choice, ended with Rotten outlasting the field to become Cruiserweight Champion. In a Scaffold Hell Match for the Extreme Championship, Mystic Wolf beat Barb Wire by sending him through a pane of glass from twenty feet up; Barb Wire's postmatch handshake with Mystic Wolf was interrupted when Raven, previously unannounced, jumped the rail as his new guest manager and struck Mystic Wolf down.

The main event put the World Heavyweight Championship on the line between Muf$ta and Super Loco Pelirojo under a stipulation requiring each man to land the other's own finisher before a pin would count; Muf$ta climbed a ladder brought in by his manager and brother, Hakeem, to hit a splash billed at 810 degrees for the win, then turned to boast to his own guest valet, Salma Hayek, before she knocked him down with her purse.

No document survives before or after this broadcast; nothing establishes when the EWA was founded, whether Extreme Assault was ever followed by a second pay-per-view, or how any of the five championships it crowned that night changed hands again.
