Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

DSW, Deep South Wrestling, opened its books on 4 April 2001 under a poster using the handle XHWMac, who spent its first month pitching the concept (on-card factions built around chosen staff, not friendship stables) and rebuilding its own website before handing the promotion to a new president that May. Its own July handbook dates the promotion's creation to that May instead, an account this record states without resolving against its own earlier served documents.

A second change of hands followed in July 2001, to an incoming president who inherited an Atlanta independent built around a weekly "Stone Mountain Aggression" broadcast and a weekly "Tenacity" promo packet, running toward bi-monthly supercards. Its first, Collateral Damage, crowned the promotion's first Southern Heritage Cup, its first Tag Team Champions and its first Heavyweight Champion inside a single card on 18 June 2001.

The federation's own name and initials double a Huntsville, Alabama e-mail fed attested a year earlier by a single surviving rulebook; nothing in this record ties the two beyond the coincidence, and this Atlanta run is treated as a distinct promotion.

How the run ended is not in the surviving record. Its last served document, dated 23 August 2001, is an ordinary weekly promo packet; no closing notice survives.
