Tenacity (08/09/01)

Dressed in wind breakers and a "Got Wood?" t-shirt. Over his left shoulder is ½ of the Tag team title belts. His head is down, both hands clutching the strap of the belt.] Wooden: Very rarely are uphill battles won. History has showed us that, D-day, Battle of Fredricksburg. The losses take are 2 to 3-1. But sometimes you have no options left, and the only thing left is to fling yourself forward and take the offensives. Last week we were desperate. We lost two man we couldn't afford to lose: Ricky Smith, and my Former tag team partner, and best friend Terry Madison. Churchill, Blair and Harley. You thought you had it all wrapped up from here on out. Taking out 2 of the 3 people that were gunning for you. Last night it was basically a 3 on 1, but the tide turned again. And it turned just in time. I don't know what his motives are, what he is getting out of this. Hell I don't even know if he is really my new tag team partner. All that I know, and everyone else knows, he put your ass on the mat for a 1..2..3. That is right Churchill, we beat your ass, and it is the biggest turning point in DSW to date. [Staring intently at the camera, Cee shifts the title belt, and runs hisright hand through his hair.] Wooden: Now I have never been in the Heavy Weight Title match before in my short career. But I have held the DSW Southeast title, and currently hold the DSW tag team title belts. I am no stranger when it comes to title matches, No stranger when it comes to complete brawls. See this time Churchill, you won't be able to bust break my arm and get a win. Tossing me from the top rope through a table will not put me out. Do you really think that you can last in the ring with me? When it comes to heart? I bust my ass every night, and finally it is paying off for me. I survived the streets of New York. Taking out guys twice as hard as you, three times as strong. Sometimes sending them packing in body bags, other times keeping their teeth as souvenirs. [ Cee grins, and laughing to himself.] Wooden: Cee when it rains it pours, and well ever heard of Murphy's Law? Everything that can go wrong will go wrong? What is the worst thing that can happen for me? Nothing, I have nothing to lose, but you have everything to lose. Just think about how much respect Harley will have for you after he had to see you get your ass kicked, and toss in the towel for his suppose to be leader? And when you lose the DSW Heavyweight title? Everything is on the line right now for you. Can you handle it? Someone once use a line, a couple of federations back. And I find it a appropriate time to quote him. [Cee pauses, looks right into the camera, with fire in his eyes.] Wooden: "I'll make ya famous." +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ <<<<<"THE INVESTOR" DRAKE STEVENS>>>>> The following is a paid promotion of... __ The voiceover clip fades out and repeats. __ The following is a paid promotion... __ It stops and repeats again. __ The following is... __ Again. __ The following... __ It just stops. We fade up. The view shows an opulent home. In the background, we can hear a man's voice. He never comes into the camera's view. __ Man: So long ago I don't remember when...I competed in my first promotion. I was fresh onto the wrestling scene, a mere rookie. I was new, but far from green...unless we're talking money...but that is a whole different topic of discussion __ The man clears his throat. Even the slightest and most common of sounds, this man makes a point to make it heard. __ Man: Then I entered my second territory. That crew wrestled out of Ohio, a state which I disgust...its so bland, but I digress...there I was looked upon as a blue-chipper, a rising star, all those terms they label fresh talent...I even ran into one of YOUR own, Deep South. Woody knows what I'm talking about, don't you PARTNER? __ He must be walking; his footsteps can be heard. __ Man: What ever happened to HERITAGE? We could have been such a GREAT team, couldn't we Woody? I've wondered since then, why haven't you called? If you would have called, I'd been GLAD to come on down here to DSW and help you win the Tag Titles. EVERYBODY knows I'd be a better partner than Rob Traxler... but at least Rob's a step up from that chump Terry Madison you won the belts with. __ His footsteps cease. The camera view now shows a huge iron gate, which leads to this home. __ Man: Everybody who is anybody knows who I am. I need no introduction. I don' t even need to show my face for recocnition. I'm the man who was undefeated in the UWF. I'm the man who single-handedly eliminated thirteen men in one match en route to winning. THIRTEEN men ladies and gentlemen. There was $50,000 dollars on the line as well. Did I neglect to say that I created the match, the Investor's Special, and that the money up for grabs belonged to me to begin with? Details, so worthless. Don't you agree? __ The scene slowly fades to black. __ __ Fade up, real quick. The camera shows "The Investor" Drake Stevens,standing in front of his home. Beside him is a small, quirky man. Peering through his glasses, the man follows as Drake is talking and walking. __ Drake Stevens: Isn't this such a beautiful house? Wait, don't answer yet. Let me show you it all. __ Drake approaches the living room of the home. The room is filled with the finest modern day furniture as well different antiquities. __ Drake Stevens: This is my living room. I consider it one of the finest, if not THE finest room in the house. My collection of antiques, the exquisite furniture, DON'T TOUCH THAT! __ Drake scolds the man for putting his fingers on a vase. The man jumps back in shock; Drake keeps walking and talking. He leads them into the kitchen. __ Drake Stevens: Look at the cabinets. Hand-crafted. I personally had the gold handles added since wood doesn't look so good on its own. __ Drake winks at the camera and keeps walking. He approaches a flight of spiraling stairs. They ascend them as Drake keeps the conversation flowing. __ Drake Stevens: No home is complete without a staircase such as this. It is like a painting without its signature. __ Drake enters a bedroom. It is very well set-up. __ Drake Stevens: I know what your thinking. Your thinking this is an excellent room, worthy enough for "The Investor" to sleep in at night? Well, your wrong. Not even THIS room is worthy of my resting hours of the night. __ Drake exits the room. He walks down the hall to a huge double door. He turns the handles on both doors and pushes them open. __ Drake Stevens: This is the master bedroom. __ The camera starts with the left side of the room. A huge oak chest...a magnicent Chinese rug...a huge vase with fresh flowers...a King size bed with a black lingeried female laying across the beds width. __ Drake Stevens: I think you've seen enough. I'll be back. __ Drake smiles at the female; she rubs a leg up and down the other. Drake closes off the doors, as the man he is showing his home peeks in the room until the door completely shuts. Drake walks out onto the balcony of the floor. Drake stands against the rails, and looks to the man. He breaths deeply, and crosses his arms. __ Drake Stevens: You see this property? Over the course of the last nine years, I have worked and earned my way into this. I made one man very rich with my ideas. I was his chief consultant, at the age of twenty-two, fresh out of Stanford. With the money he paid me, I took every last hundred of it and invested it. I made myself even rich than he ever dreamed of being. Sometime in my sixties or seventies, I figure I'll have to retire. Its the fashionable thing to do. One day I will pass all of this down to someone who has been loyal to me for years. __ The young man standing beside Drake speaks up for the first time. __ Young Man: Sir, I'll be loyal to you. I really want this job. Drake Stevens: Now whats your name again? Young Man: Its Edward sir. Drake Stevens: Well Edward, let me tell you this. You are on my payroll now. However, YOU will NEVER inherit all of this from ME! You could work for me until the day I am incapable of carrying on day to day business and I STILL would not consider YOU as worthy of all of THIS! __ Edward's enthusiam dies down a bit. Drake grabs the man's chin and lifts it upward. __ Drake Stevens: Do you want to know why? Edward: Wh..why sir? Drake Stevens: You have no backbone. Drake Stevens: He's a Champion in Deep South Wrestling. Albeit he may have awarded himself the DSW Cruiserweight crown but nonetheless he holds it. Hell, that was initiative on the poor chaps part. However, that is next weeks business. Book my flight to Atlanta for the 5th. I must be there for the 7th. Its an important date. __ Drake turns around, his back to Edward. Edward stands there for a moment, wondering whether to book the flight now or later. The scene fades to black. __ +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ <<<<<SEAN GUNN>>>>> "I shoulda known..." [A gruff voice pierces the darkness. It is followed immediately by the sound of heavy breathing. The orator is either exhausted or..] "I shoulda known not ta trust a low-down, dirty, snake in the grass like Carl Blair. Yeah, I shoulda known. And I will have my vengeance." [...or pissed. The words from the man we now assume is Sean Gunn come deliberatly hard and with great intent. They are more seethed than spoken.] "I made a mistake. Yes. Sean Gunn is fallible. I made a deal with the devil and I got burned." [The scene materializes effortlessly into a shot of Sean Gunn's scowling face. He is surrounded by nothing save for the aura of death. He stands, on a chilly southern night along an empty street. The picture is illuminated by the orangish glow of a lone streetlamp in the distance.The light silhouettes Sean's face. Giving him a pale, ghost-like appearance.] [He takes an aggressive step forward. His eyes burn with rage.] "And he's comin' after you, Carl Blair." [For the first time during this promo Sean affords himself a quick grin.] "I shoulda known you wouldn't be true to your word, Blair. I shoulda known that you Thrilogy scum-suckas were devoid of honor. I shoulda seen that double cross from a mile away." [A grimace. Its as if it hurts him to think about how easily he was taken for a fool.] "I shoulda saw that you have no loyalties other than to yourself. I shoulda realized who my real friends were. Cee Wooden was a good friend of mine, and I burned that bridge for a shot at a lousy piece of tin. I shoulda saw all that and so much more. But I was blinded by the bullsh*t artist that is Carl Blair." [The grimace turns into a sinister grin.] "Fool me once, Carl, shame on me. There won't be a second time so the cliche ends there." "Yes, Carl, you pulled the wool over my eyes. Boast about it to your cronies in the back all you like, and gloat on national television to your little hearts content.. then think about this.." "What have you really gained?" "I mean, really? I've still got you in the ring. Your precious little belt is still on the line, and it's still me, Sean Gunn that you've got to beat to earn the right to wear around your waiste another day." [He begins to stalk forward now. Slowly at first, but increasingly swifter.] "The truth is, Thrilogy needed me. Thrilogy needed Sean Gunn to beat Kerry Powell and Cee Wooden. Damien Churchill, Carl Blair, and Harley Phiresteiner despite all his hype couldn't collectively beat TWO men without the help of yours truly." [Silence. The streetlight above him flickers, although the venemous tone in his voice doesn't.] "It's ironic, Carl. The scenario we find ourselves in here, today. You're looking down the barrel at me with your gold in tact, and Thrilogy's dominance unquestioned. And yet, here I am. A thorn in your side. With what I wanted all along in my grasp. A chance to prove that Carl Blair is all hype. A chance to prove that Carl Blair needs Thrilogy." He needs to know that they'll be there for him, or that a pair of brass knucks will be there, he needs something. Carl Blair needs a safety-net to catch him when he falls. Well, this week on Stone Mountain Aggression the net is gonna removed, and it's just gonne be you and me on the tight rope, Carl." [Sean stops suddenly. The heavy breathing has increases.] "You know Blair, there's a saying where I come from. Perhaps you've heard of it; it goes... 'the buck stops here.'.." "It means the bull, the tricks, the cheap shots -- they all stop here. It means that all the crap you've spewed since arriving in DSW will be rendered meaningless when I expose you for the pretender you really are. You're phony tough, and foolishly brave, Blair. But courage isn't enough. Not this time. This time you fall off the tight rope, Carl. This time, you fall hard." "And no one will be there to catch you." "Not Churchill." "Not Phiresteiner" "Not even your mamma can save you from me this time, Carl." [Sean takes his hands out of his pockets and slowly sits on on the curb.] [With both elbows atop the knees of his semi-spread legs and his hands pointed downwards, Sean Gunn looks deep into the soul of everyone watching.] [That last comment comes out more seethed than said. A dramatic pause is held before he talks again.] "I see through you. I see through the hype and the false bravado. I see the real Carl Blair. And what I see is sad. Weak. Pathetic. A scared little boy in a man's body. A little boy who for the first time in his life finds himself in a situation he cannot control. And it terrifies him. You can lie to yourself and the nation all you want, Carl, but I know the truth. You're scared to death. You're scared because you too know the truth. You know what I know. You know that I'm better than you. You know that your reign as champion is coming to an end, and you know that I'm the man who's going to end it." "I will be the new Southeast Champion, no if and's or but's about it." "Period." [Sean lowers his head and for the first time the expression on his face relents] "It's funny, Carl. That little tough guy facade you like to flaunt. Because like I've already established, it's easily seen through. When people look into your eyes they don't see a badass. They don't see the second most wanted man in all of Deep South Wrestling. They see a joke. A henchman running interference for Damien Churchill. They see you, like I see you Carl, as an errand boy." [Again he stares at the ground in silence for a moment.] "No more games, Carl." "No more tricks" [He hesitates] "Just you and me mano y mano. And the world is finally gonna see who the better man is. I'm that man, Carl." "I've given too much, came to far to let this opportunity slip through my fingers yet again. I've turned my back on friends, fans, and loved ones to get to this point. I betrayed Cee Wooded. I betrayed everything that Sean Gunn stood for. And I did it for glory. I did it for immortality. I did it for _respect_. I know you can understand that, Carl. This game is dog eat dog. It's not about friends and family. It's about gold. It's about being great. And great is the legacy Sean Gunn is gonna leave on DSW." [He begins to shake his head] "No more." "No more will I play second fiddle to undeserving pukes." "No more shaking hands, kissing babies, and all that other mumbo-jumbo. It's about me now. It's about Sean Gunn and how he will be remembered twenty -- thirty years from now." "Coulda shoulda woulda... F*ck that! Now I'm telling you what I am gonna do. I'm gonna walk down that aisle, look Carl Blair in the eye, spit in his face, and proceed to pound the ever living hell out of him. I'm gonna reach down inside myself, take everything he's got and keep right on going. I'm gonna beat Carl Blair. And remember, you heard it here first." [Cackle.] "This Sunday, two men enter a ring in search of immortality. Only one can achieve it. The other will be left in the dust." [Pause. Smirk.] "Eat my dust, Carl." [End.] +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ <<<<<"ELUSIVE" RYAN DELANY>>>>> [The camera fades in. The candle lights dance with ease, erasing and creating all the same. The shadows move, the light does burn and the voice does speak.] " The anvil rings a metal tone, the swords for war are forged. Evil seized the soul of man, upon the hatred gorged. " [Ryan Delaney does sit there, with his legs crossed. His hair pulled back, his face set in stone and his voice making its self-known.] " The clash of steel and smell of blood addicted those who fought. The land lay barren, scorched and bare, on battlefields bodies rot. " [The dresser stands tall, making itself useful as a leaning post. The carpet is bare and barren. The room is dark and damp, just like the outside world. The candles still dance and the shadow still move.] " The sun sets cold, the bells toll long and the wind moans and sighs. In the gloaming nothing stirs and lightning rips the sky. " [With a strong glare at the camera so near, Delaney does look, hard and fierce. His lip curls, his teeth bare in this distant, death stare.] " In the end the deeds are done, the lesson goes unlearned. Still the people follow blindly, stand in line to die in turn. " [Fade Out.] +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ <<<<<DIETRICH JONES>>>>> When Legends Die: Villains Are Made [The scene opens with a black screen, and nothing more.] [Heavy-breathing -panting--penetrates the obligatory darkness. Someone is there, seething. Perhaps, foaming at the mouth would be a more accurate cliché.] "I've waited a long time for this." [The words come sharp and hard. The voice itself is distinctly baritone.] "I've paid my dues. Bled buckets, sweated buckets, and taken as much from others. Oh yes, I've paid my dues! And now, it's time for me to step out of the shadows and take what is mine." [A candle is lit; the light pierces the darkness with its timely presence. It provides just enough illumination to silhouette a face. A familiar face. A face so familiar that the name is on the tip of your tongue. A face so long forgotten that the tip of your tongue is as far as it gets.] "I'm here to make a man pay for his arrogance. For stealing my thunder, hogging my spotlight." [Another candle is lit.] "I'm here Harley, you over-bearing whore, for my respect." [And another.] "For years. Months on end passed as I watched the legend of Harley Phiresteiner grow and grow and grow.. and grow." [The candles glow bright enough for us to stare deep into a pair of hollow bluish-green eyes. They're hollow in terms of the soul that lies beyond them. In a physical, more concrete sense, they burn bright with carefully controlled rage.] "..all the while waiting for your promises of grandeur to come to fruition. They never did. But I trudged through the excrement of indy-league hell like a good little soldier, anyways. I spilled blood and laid waste to pretenders all across the Ohio Valley. All because I believed the fairytales. I believed in a ghost." [Gasp. Followed by a short, deliberate pause.] "I believed the hype that did, and still does define Harley Phiresteiner." [That cuts deep.] "But that was then, and this is now. I'm not that same starry-eyed kid that frequented bingo halls and took care of the dirty work of a washed-up, deranged, [scoff] 'legend.'" "Not by a long shot." "Ya see, gentlemen, you might be in a good situation here in DSW. You've all but monopolized the title scene. Hell, you boys might just be the Thrilogy you claim to be. But I'm not your average, run-of-the-mill mark. I'm not the man you remember. I've evolved from a brash, trigger happy thug - into a cold, calculating monster the likes of which professional wrestling hasn't seen since EIW." [The nameless face. That pale, ghostlike face is encompassed by a grin. A sinister grin.] "The likes of which hasn't been seen since the name Harley Phiresteiner actually meant something. Since HP actually personified.." [Cackle] "Yeah, you guessed it.. homicide." [That laugh. That face. Maniacal is the only word that seems to fit.] "But all things come to an end. I no longer believe in ghost-stories." [His head tilts to the left, and for the first time those bottomless eyes waver. They don't peer into the camera lens anymore.] "I no longer believe in Harley Phiresteiner." [Snap. Those mysterious eyes are back, burning a hole through the camera, and seemingly looking directly into your soul.] "No. Harley's dead to me. Now I believe in a man who has battered, bruised, beaten, and broken some of the toughest men in this business in the last couple years. I believe in a higher power, something with a more sturdy foundation." [Dramatic pause.] "I believe in myself." [A small step forward brings the long, lithe, muscular frame of one of this business' most infamous henchmen into view. Now we know who he is. We recognize him. We know him. We fear him.. and so does Harley Phiresteiner.] "I believe in Dietrich Jones." "And whether you do or not, Harley, is of no consequence to me. Because you taught me a lot of things, chief. You took me under your wing. And yes, when I knew you, Harley Phiresteiner was the best wrestler in the world.." "..bar none." [His big hands fisted on his hips, he stands like a statue of a Roman Gladiator. Tall. Strong. Proud.] "But everything changes. As time marches on and the next couple-a-months roll by, you will learn a harsh truth." [Smirk] "Time passes us all by. And the student always surpasses the teacher. There will be a day when Dietrich Jones is better than Harley Phiresteiner. I believe that day is already upon us." [He stalks forward.] "It's the law of life, Harley." "The window in this business is a small one. It's why we must all grab what we can, as fast as we can, and know when to get out. Unfortunately, some of us hold on just a little too long. Inch by inch I draw closer, chief. The walls are closing in on your career, and you're runin' out of room. The hunter has now become the hunted." [A long pause] "Ya know, HP, someone once told me that the brightest flames burn quickest. And I never put much thought in it. But now, as I look at you and what you've become.. I believe that's true. Yes, it's true." "So as that lump builds in your throat, and you feel your heart pounding, your pulse racing, and that rush of adrenaline starts to flow through your veins.. know that those footsteps are mine. Know that I'm comin' to get you, Harley. I'm comin' to extinguish your flame." [Smirk] "I'm comin'" [Dietrich leans over and blows out all three candles -- and the picture goes black.] [Cut] +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ <<<<<"THE PREDATOR" ROB TRAXLER>>>>> [A stormy sky fills the scene with its dark clouds and intermittent bursts of lightning and rolling thunder.] This is a time for evil. [The camera pans around to a brick wall and slowly comes down to reveal...] Darkness has covered the Deep South Wrestling promotion with its grotesque and horrific shadows. [Rob Traxler??? Since when did he ever talk like this? Ah yes...you just don't remember him ever being this serious when talking about the state of a promotion in which he was participating.] The brief vacation supplied to us by VMG ownership was a chance for us to visit with our families...maybe hit the gym for a light workout or two...but what were the REAL motives behind it? Was VMG trying to give Damien Churchill time to recover from the vicious roundhouse kick that I connected to his jaw at the last Aggression? Was VMG trying to give themselves time to groom Carl Blair into the champion they so desperately want him to be? Was VMG trying to give Harley Phiresteiner the chance to remain silent just a little longer so that when he does finally show up...we'll have no way of seeing it coming? [A slight smirk comes across the veteran's chiseled face. He looks up at the stormy sky for a moment.] Just what WERE their motives in this hiatus? I am very confused by all of this. Do they not realize that the two men they need to build this promotion around are the two men who are most affected by breaks in the action? Yes, I understand that Wooden has had his fair share of beatdowns recently, but the guy has always fought through them...do they not see that the fans continue to cheer for him because he has more heart than perhaps anyone in the business? [His eyes come back to the shot.] It is a dark time for DSW...but there remains a glimpse of hope. This Sunday, I am in an unusual position...one I am not readily accustomed to...I must stand in the corner of a man fighting for a Heavyweight Title that would have been mine under slightly different circumstances....I must stand there and hold a white towel in my hand as the symbol of his hopes and dreams. And if I throw that towel into the ring, Cee Wooden loses. [Solemness fills the air.] I know that many people, including Cee, wonder why I came to his aid at the last Aggression...why I took it upon myself to help a man who has gotten everything I wanted from DSW. You want to know why? I'll tell you....it wouldn't have mattered if it was Wooden or Sean Gunn, I would have run down...because MY priority lies with Thrillogy. I am seeking to dismantle them...humiliate them...hunt them...KILL them. [Anger is clearly filling Traxler's face, but he calms himself back down and continues.] However...now that I have aided Wooden, I stand committed to him. I refuse to treat Cee the way so many others have treated me in the past. Others helped me in need and then turned around with the knife in their hand like Brutus did to Caesar. I am not like that. Wooden needs someone to help him fight this battle since Terry Madison is no longer with us, and I happen to be one of the few capable of helping him. He has my support for as long as his agenda doesn't interfere with mine. We have an understanding, and that's enough to keep me by his side through all of this. [Traxler gets up from his leaning position against the wall and starts walking, the camera remaining in front of him as he moves.] Let me step away from the Thrillogy confrontation for a moment and quickly address someone else in DSW. You know, when I was UWF Commissioner, I witnessed one of the most coniving, cheating, no-good, lieing assholes I have ever seen in this sport......Drake Stevens. Drake, let's just lay this out...I don't like you. I don't respect you. I don't care for your presence in DSW. [Traxler stops, and the anger re-enters his eyes.] If you ever decide to step into my territory, you will be hunted. If you cross the line into my domain, you will be preyed upon. If you come into my realm, you...will...be...KILLED! I will expose your weaknesses and dismantle your confidence. So let this be a fair warning to you to take your time and gain some experience before you make any effort to come this direction. [Traxler begins to move forward again.] As we approach this intriguing matchup Sunday night, I urge you all to consider the path VMG has taken DSW on thus far...the time has come for us as honorable wrestlers to rise up and make it known that we will not take it anymore. Let's not wait for them to throw the first punch...rather, let us go on the prowl and capture them by surprise. I'll see you on Sunday.
