NEWS AND RUMORS =====-----=====-----=====-----=====-----=====-----=====-----===== --> Visit the "dsw" channel on the StarChat server for a jolly good time! Discuss angles, give feedback and suggestions, or just goof off with the most interesting personalities in all of e-wrestling! --> Collateral Damage went down with only a couple cosmetic glitches. The card has been getting rave reviews from most everyone that has seen it. If you wanna see what the fuss is about go to: http://www.jess-designs.com/DSW/events/CD.txt --> In one of the most shocking events in DSW to date, Harley Phiresteiner debuted and made sure Damien Churchill walked away with the DSW Heavyweight Championship belt. --> Troubleshooters Inc. walked away last night as the new and first ever DSW Tag Team Champions. For a brief moment they looked like they were regaining control in their storied history with the Thrillogy until the Phiresteiner debut and the Churchill victory. Now they're outnumbered 4-2, but are holding 2 of the four belts in the promotion and won what most consider the most important battle in the ongoing feud. --> Also walking away with newly won gold last night was "Your True Life Fantasy" Carl Blair after he defeated the former Southeast Champion, Cee Wooden, to kick the show off. --> Remember: Stone Mountain Aggression this week is a promo only card. There will be no matches and promo's should be done with just your character sitting in front of a black backdrop. HOWEVER, we will also run vignette's if you would also like to send one to further an angle. Since the card is completely promo oriented, the Promo Packet will not be sent out. Instead, everything will be placed on the SMA card. --> We are trying to make the tag division stronger but to do so we must "reward" handlers for their work in the division. Therefore, any handler that colaborates with another handler in the tag divison will be awarded "push points". These points will used to help determine how much of a push you receive in solo competition. There are three things you can be awarded push points for, such as: colaboration in the tag team division, strats, and in-rings/on-cards. Below are the push point value's: Working the tag divison -- 5 push points In-rings / On-cards -- 3 push points Strats -- 2 push points --> "Code Red" Ryan Sharpe has joined Deep South Wrestling. --> Reminder: Visit the "dsw" channel on the starchat server if you have mIRC. =====-----=====-----=====-----=====-----=====-----=====-----===== 2.
