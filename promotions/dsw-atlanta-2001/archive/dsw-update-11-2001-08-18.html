NEWS AND RUMORS \`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\ GOLD RUSH! THRILLOGY COUP AND NEW CHAMPIONS Thrillogy was a delivered a series of blows, culminating to "War Machine" Damien Churchill being exiled from the group he's lead since May after the Heavyweight Title Towel Match on Stone Mountain Aggression. The group that was once though to be united fell apart. First, after a botched interference from Damien Churchill saw Sean Gunn defeat Carl Blair inside the Steel Cage to capture the DSW Southeast Title. After months of accumulating animosity on and off the camera, Damien Churchill suffered another loss, this time to Cee Wooden. Phiresteiner tossed the towel in for reason he made clear after a vicious post attack on Damien Churchill. Blair and Phiresteiner are now rebuilding the Thrillogy empire that was seemingly ready to crumble. WOODEN, GUNN DEFY THE ODDS With his toughest challenege standing before him, Cee Wooden survived the Towel Match to capture the DSW Heavyweight Title. Wooden will now face toughc hallenges, with everyone from Carl Blair, Harley Phiresteiner, to his own team mate Rob Traxler eyeing the champ. As for Gunn, the Texan stood victorious over Carl Blair inside the Steel Cage. Fending off a Damien Churchill interference, Sean Gunn finally snagged himself the Southeast Title, but now must go on to defend it at The Battle of Atlanta against The Flying Fin! THE HAUNTING OF DIETRICH JONES The terrifying presence of Dietrich Jones was felt at SMA. Jones, who's vendetta against "Homicyde" Harley Phiresteiner has become evident over the past few weeks, went completely bezerk on DSW Secruity Chief Tobar Dykes. The hospitalized Dykes has requested a leave from DSW. VMG suits are threating to ban Jones from TV if he doesn't tone down his act. ------------------------------------------------------------- \`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\ 2.
