As much as I wish Perry Vaughn would take out Churchill .. he's a member of Shootfire-Pro! He's not even on the DSW Roster, he has no business out here! Alexander: I never thought I'd be saying this but you're absolutely right. [Vaughn steps into the ring with the pipe but is taken down by members of DSW Security as members of the front office make an appearance, including Steve Mac.] [The lights black out.] Jared: What's going on??? The lights just went totally out here in the arena! [Some mellow classical music begins to play softly over the PA system. A low and raspy female-sounding voice comes in over the music.] "MacArthur once said that old soldiers never die...they just fade away." [Loud cannon shots play over the speakers as the fans clamor amongst themselves. The monitors all over the arena, including the ones at the announcers' table and in the locker rooms backstage, turn on to the image of a bloodied and beaten Rob Traxler, hanging by his arms in an empty steel-walled room. The fans erupt into boos and screams as the voice continues.] "Traxler is certainly an old soldier in wrestling, but unlike the fate suffered by so many others, he will not likely fade away in the eyes of the fans if he has anything to do with it. This is why it is our mission to make sure that he either chooses for himself to fade away, or we will render him useless to the fans through mental and physical abuse." [The camera zooms in on Traxler's face, whose eyes are glazed over in his dazed state.] "The conversion has begun. The end is near. Traxler shall pay for the sins of humanity....with his life, if necessary. There is no way to prevent this from occuring, for it has already started to transpire. Your only hope is that when we are through with him, that he won't try to hold each and every one of you responsible for what is happening to him." [Another chorus of boos from the crowd, as Jared and Alexander sit silent, mortified at what they see on the screens in front of them.] "A long habit of not thinking a thing wrong, gives it a superficial appearance of being right. These illusions commend themselves to us because they save us pain and allow us to enjoy pleasure instead. We must therefore accept it without complaint when they sometimes collide with a bit of reality against which they are dashed to pieces. This is your new reality that is crashing the illusion that Traxler is a great man in this sport. You are being warned now...our mission shall be accomplished soon...and he will be released when our guidelines are met. But for now, you can watch in terror as your hero is beaten down like the mortal he really is." [Many of the fans scream in horror as the camera zooms out, revealing 6 masked men wielding kendo sticks. They bring them back, and as they start to swing, with the fans screaming in terror, the camera feed cuts, ending the SMA.]
