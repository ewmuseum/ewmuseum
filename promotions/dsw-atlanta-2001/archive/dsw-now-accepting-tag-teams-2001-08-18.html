Deep South Wrestling (DSW) is currently accepting any and all Tag Teams for some crazy ol' wrestling. Check egos at the door. ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ DEEP SOUTH WRESTLING HANDBOOK VERSION 1.0 CREATED JULY 12, 2001 ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ I. OVERVIEW II. RULES III. SCHEDULE 1. Weekly Schedule 2. Supercard Schedule IV. ROLEPLAYING/STRATTING 1. Roleplaying 2. Stratting V. PERSONALITIES I. OVERVIEW Deep South Wrestling (DSW) was created in May 2001 by [name withheld]. It is an Atlanta-based Independent Wrestling Promotion. From May until July 2001, [name withheld] was the head of DSW. In July of 2001, [name withheld] stepped, and [name withheld] stepped up to become the new President of the Promotion. The in-character story goes that [name withheld] sold the company to Vision Media Group, out of Dayton, Ohio. They named DSW Play-by-Play man Walter Jared as interim President. Pat's booking experiences goes back to early 1999, but is most notable for his time spent as Allstar Wrestling of the Motor City/Allstar Global Wrestling President (AWMC/AGW) from January 2000 till AGW's closing in October 2000. DSW is a weekly e-mail league. There is one flash/roleplay packet a week, called "Tenacity", and one show a week, called "Stone Mountain Agression". Supercards occur bi-monthly and are treated with the utmost importance. II. RULES Taken from the AGW Hand Book: 1. The occassional curse word is ok, but try and censor it out using a *beep* or *censor* or whatever. 2. The Happy Clause - I can make some of the people happy all of the time, but I cannot make everyone happy all of the time. Keep that in mind at all times, please. 3. The Spunky Clause - From here on out, we're shooting for realism. If I don't think something you write would get aired on national TV, then don't send it to me. I allow more leniency with this then anything else. 4. The Egomaniacal Tyrant Clause - The basic premise of this rule is "No Bragging Allowed". Anyone who develops a serious chip on his or her shoulder will go through what's known as a "humbling process". I don't care how many titles you have won outside of DSW, or how many people you have defeated outside of the federation -- if you're coming into DSW, don't expect an insta-push. 5. Character Assassination - The basic premise of this rule is that a person is not allowed to attack another character in a way that the other character has potential to be 'ruined', without getting the permission of that character's handler first. This includes any and all attacks that would leave a character injured, overly humiliated, or permanently scarred. 6. The Diggs Clause - DSW administration reserves the right to refuse flashes of inappropriate material (i.e. racism, personal shots, various retardedness) as well as the right to refuse to count the same inappropriate material towards a match. If it's refused, that's the end of it.Anyone who tries to talk me into running an inappropriate roleplay will be canned REAL quick-like. This is just a game. It's not life. If you lost, you lost because I thought you should've. If you wonder if you were out-rp'ed, you were. If you think you need some more heat, you do. If you take it out on me, don't expect to get any reaction, except reference to this rule. If you're seriously ticked, then you're seriously fired. If you've ever been a fedhead, you know that it can be a hard job, and the less aggravation I have to suffer, the better. 7. The Brain Clause - Just because you write a strat that covers all the grandest moves, every counter imaginable, provisions for interference and the rest, don't have a fit if it isn't used or the match goes contrary to your plans for world domination. The whole concept of pro wrestling is that the wrestlers vouluntarily submit themselves to being hit with the moves that are tried against them. We all know that any wrestler could realistically get out of any hold, or avoid any move if he really wanted to. But he CHOOSES not to, because that's the way the show is done. Just because you have a counter or protection doesn't automatically mean it'll be used. Sometimes an angle is better furthered (while accomplishing mutual goals) by a different outcome. If you can't sell something that turns out contrary to your master plan and decide to take it out in roleplays, you'll have plenty of practice applying to other feds, guaranteed. 8. The Strat-Or-Can Policy - If you're booked for a PPV, and you don't Strat/RP without a pretty damn good excuse, your gone. Chances are, if you haven't shown interest in your match, then no one else has, so we won't bother doing a Play-by-Play for it. We will not tolerate laziness for PPV's. We treat each PPV like a Wrestlemania, so please, strat for your match. Failure to do so without a VERY good excuse results in immediate dismissal from DSW. We are VERY strict with this policy. (See - AWMC - Sin City Showdown II: Proving Grounds) 9. Use of other handlers' characters in your RPs without permission is prohibited. III. SCHEDULE 1. For those of you who are ghetto (heh ;)), I basically just want feed back, angle ideas, etc. Wednesday - PROMO DEADLINE - 11:59 PM PACIFIC/Deep South Dirt when I have the time for it. Thursday - Tenacity [Promo Packet] Friday - STRATS/ROLEPLAYS/INRING DEADLINE - 11:59 PM PACIFIC Saturday - Work On Card Day 2. Supercard Schedule Starting as of August 2001: AUGUST - THE BATTLE OF ATLANTA OCTOBER - MISSISSIPPI MASSACRE DECEMBER - TEXAS SHOWDOWN FEBRUARY - BRING THE PAIN APRIL - ALL FIRED UP IV. ROLEPLAYING/STRATTING 1. Roleplaying Active Roleplaying is one Roleplay a week. If ya go mute for two strat matches, your gone. The Max RP Limit is 2, plus whatever on-card stuff ya got. 2. Stratting Strats can be is in-depth or so small as you'd like. They do, however, weigh into your match (very little though). Make sure you read the Brain Clause for clarification on those monster strats, though. This is the simple, small template that is basically the bare bones if ya do one. A. Overview B. Entrance C. Offesinve Strategy D. Defensive Strategy E. 5-10 Moves F. Possible Finisher G. Other (Spots, Interference, etc.) Shouldn't take too long to fill out...like I said, you really don't even need all that. If you spend more time strategizing then roleplaying, you're working too hard ;). V. PERSONALITIES Walter Jared - Golden-Age Wrestling Color Commentator, biased to faces. Brad Alexander - Twentysomething Color Commentator, biased towards heels. Ronnie Traitt - Backstage Interviewer. The Rumor Man of DSW. Elliot Kuhn - Ring Announcer of DSW.
