Critique [Dietrich Jones: DSW]

13 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just a little feedback if ya'll would be so kind. Be brutally honesy, I can take the critisizm.. I just wanna get better.When Legends Die: Villains Are Made [The scene opens with a black screen, and nothing more.] [Heavy-breathing &#8211;panting&#8212;penetrates the obligatory darkness. Someone is there, seething. Perhaps, foaming at the mouth would be a more accurate cliché.] &#8220;I&#8217;ve waited a long time for this.&#8221; [The words come sharp and hard. The voice itself is distinctly baritone.] &#8220;I&#8217;ve paid my dues. Bled buckets, sweated buckets, and taken as much from others. Oh yes, I&#8217;ve paid my dues! And now, it&#8217;s time for me to step out of the shadows and take what is mine.&#8221; [A candle is lit; the light pierces the darkness with its timely presence. It provides just enough illumination to silhouette a face. A familiar face. A face so familiar that the name is on the tip of your tongue. A face so long forgotten that the tip of your tongue is as far as it gets.] &#8220;I&#8217;m here to make a man pay for his arrogance. For stealing my thunder, hogging my spotlight.&#8221; [Another candle is lit.] &#8220;I&#8217;m here Harley, you over-bearing whore, for my respect.&#8221; [And another.] &#8220;For years. Months on end passed as I watched the legend of Harley Phiresteiner grow and grow and grow.. and grow.&#8221; [The candles glow bright enough for us to stare deep into a pair of hollow bluish-green eyes. They&#8217;re hollow in terms of the soul that lies beyond them. In a physical, more concrete sense, they burn bright with carefully controlled rage.] &#8220;..all the while waiting for your promises of grandeur to come to fruition. They never did. But I trudged through the excrement of indy-league hell like a good little soldier, anyways. I spilled blood and laid waste to pretenders all across the Ohio Valley. All because I believed the fairytales. I believed in a ghost.&#8221; [Gasp. Followed by a short, deliberate pause.] &#8220;I believed the hype that did, and still does define Harley Phiresteiner.&#8221; [That cuts deep.] &#8220;But that was then, and this is now. I&#8217;m not that same starry-eyed kid that frequented bingo halls and took care of the dirty work of a washed-up, deranged, [scoff] &#8216;legend.&#8217;&#8221; &#8220;Not by a long shot.&#8221; &#8220;Ya see, gentlemen, you might be in a good situation here in DSW. You&#8217;ve all but monopolized the title scene. Hell, you boys might just be the Thrilogy you claim to be. But I&#8217;m not your average, run-of-the-mill mark. I&#8217;m not the man you remember. I&#8217;ve evolved from a brash, trigger happy thug &#8211; into a cold, calculating monster the likes of which professional wrestling hasn&#8217;t seen since EIW.&#8221; [The nameless face. That pale, ghostlike face is encompassed by a grin. A sinister grin.] &#8220;The likes of which hasn&#8217;t been seen since the name Harley Phiresteiner actually meant something. Since HP actually personified..&#8221; [Cackle] &#8220;Yeah, you guessed it.. homicide.&#8221; [That laugh. That face. Maniacal is the only word that seems to fit.] &#8220;But all things come to an end. I no longer believe in ghost-stories.&#8221; [His head tilts to the left, and for the first time those bottomless eyes waver. They don&#8217;t peer into the camera lens anymore.] &#8220;I no longer believe in Harley Phiresteiner.&#8221; [Snap. Those mysterious eyes are back, burning a hole through the camera, and seemingly looking directly into your soul.] &#8220;No. Harley&#8217;s dead to me. Now I believe in a man who has battered, bruised, beaten, and broken some of the toughest men in this business in the last couple years. I believe in a higher power, something with a more sturdy foundation.&#8221; [Dramatic pause.] &#8220;I believe in myself.&#8221; [A small step forward brings the long, lithe, muscular frame of one of this business&#8217; most infamous henchmen into view. Now we know who he is. We recognize him. We know him. We fear him.. and so does Harley Phiresteiner.] &#8220;I believe in Dietrich Jones.&#8221; &#8220;And whether you do or not, Harley, is of no consequence to me. Because you taught me a lot of things, chief. You took me under your wing. And yes, when I knew you, Harley Phiresteiner was the best wrestler in the world..&#8221; &#8220;..bar none.&#8221; [His big hands fisted on his hips, he stands like a statue of a Roman Gladiator. Tall. Strong. Proud.] &#8220;But everything changes. As time marches on and the next couple-a-months roll by, you will learn a harsh truth.&#8221; [Smirk] &#8220;Time passes us all by. And the student always surpasses the teacher. There will be a day when Dietrich Jones is better than Harley Phiresteiner. I believe that day is already upon us.&#8221; [He stalks forward.] &#8220;It&#8217;s the law of life, Harley.&#8221; &#8220;The window in this business is a small one. It&#8217;s why we must all grab what we can, as fast as we can, and know when to get out. Unfortunately, some of us hold on just a little too long. Inch by inch I draw closer, chief. The walls are closing in on your career, and you&#8217;re runin&#8217; out of room. The hunter has now become the hunted.&#8221; [A long pause] &#8220;Ya know, HP, someone once told me that the brightest flames burn quickest. And I never put much thought in it. But now, as I look at you and what you&#8217;ve become.. I believe that&#8217;s true. Yes, it&#8217;s true.&#8221; &#8220;So as that lump builds in your throat, and you feel your heart pounding, your pulse racing, and that rush of adrenaline starts to flow through your veins.. know that those footsteps are mine. Know that I&#8217;m comin&#8217; to get you, Harley. I&#8217;m comin&#8217; to extinguish your flame.&#8221; [Smirk] &#8220;I&#8217;m comin&#8217; [Dietrich leans over and blows out all three candles -- and the picture goes black.] [Cut]
