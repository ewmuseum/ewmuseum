Federation Concept

5 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The concept in which Deep South Wrestling will try to sink it's teeth into is one that I don't recall seeing. Is it because there is a lack of interest? Or is it because no actual wrestling promotion has ever tried it? I don't know the answer, honestly. But if I were you .. I'd check it out and judge for myself. Aight, now that all that bravado is out of the way let me get to the meat of this post. The DSW concept is simple: factions. The DSW "locker rooms" will be powered by factions. Factions which will be powered by "on-card characters". Chosen staff members (translation from kayfabe: handlers with enough time to do a promo or vignette every week but not enough to actively roleplay) will be the "Godfather's" of these factions, but in all reality the factions will be pushed only on the strength of the roleplays. Of course, not everyone will be forced into these factions. There are some characters better left as loners. Although, those types may be rare in the DSW. We smile upon handler-interactivty and feel on-air factions are a good way to get the ball rolling in most cases. Also, we don't want to lead anyone into believing that being a part of this faction will make you allies with your faction-mates. As opposed to other factions you may have been involved with, these factions will not be run as "four horsemen" type stables (except in a rare instance). These are more along the lines of "necessity" stables. We want to make it seem necessary to be in one faction or another on the cards, not to make them seem like a gathering of friends (again, except in rare instances). So now that you know the concept, join the fed damnit! Click the link below... http://www.jess-designs.com/DSW/dsw_index.shtml
