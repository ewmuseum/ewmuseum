NEWS AND RUMORS \`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\ TRAXLER, WOODEN ALIGN -- THRILLOGY READY TO IMPLODE? Thrillogy's hope for continued domination of DSW came to sudden halt on Stone Mountain Agression after "The Predator" Rob Traxler scored an upset pin on DSW Heavyweight Champion "War Machine" Damien Churchill. Traxler, who came in after Terry Madison was put out of commission by Thrillogy earlier in the evening, delivered a walloping Rounhouse kick to the Champ from the top rope, kicking a DSW Tag Team Title in his face. Traxler covered and scored the victory. After the macth, the Champion was livid, and a brief shouting match occured between the group. Will the newfound alliance of Cee Wooden and Rob Traxler be able to stop the "Killing machine" that is Thrillogy? Or will Thrillogy stand tall once more? BLAIR SCORES CHEAP WIN, WILL CLASH WITH GUNN ONCE MORE Carl Blair resorted to some cheap tricks to upend Sean Gunn this week on Stone Mountain Agression. Bound to make Blair earn his title, Interim President Walt Jared signed a match for Carl Blair to defend his title once again against Gunn at Stone Mountain Agression this week. FLYING FIN DECLARES HIMSELF CHAMP The Flying Fin is DSW's best Cruiserweight. Reason being...he is the only Cruiserweight. But that fact hasn't stopped the Fin, as he declared himself the DSW Cruiserweight Champion. DSW officials have recognized him, so the decision stands. MADISON SIDELINED, SMITH RELEASED FOLLOWING ATTACKS Terry madison has been put out indefinatley after a vicious attack by Thrillogy on Stone Mountain Agression. Harley Phiresteiner cracked a cinder block over the big mans knee, putting him out of commission. "The Little Brit" Ricky Smith made a kamikaze attempt to seek retribution on his Collateral Damage foe, but fell victim to a roundhouse kick from the big man, then a Powerbomb through the announcers table from the ring apron. Smith, who was rather distraught, asked for his release form the promotion afterwards, and was granted it. NEW STARS MAKE PRESENCE FELT Shadow King, who was discovered to have put Phoenix out of DSW by shattering his arm, as well as "Elusive" Ryan Delany were both on Stone Mountain Aggression. VMG is pexpecting big things out of these new faces. ------------------------------------------------------------- \`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\ 2. STONE MOUNTAIN AGGRESSION \`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\`\ DSW will return to action LIVE from the DSW ARENA in ATLANTA, GEORGIA on TUESDAY, AUGUST 7TH! -DARK MATCHES- Cruiserweights will tangle as "ELUSIVE" RYAN DELANY will debut by tackling the recently resigned SHADOW KING! Will THE FLYING FIN be eyeing his possible opposition to his newly...uhm...acquired gold? Speaking of the Cruiserweight Champion, THE FLYING FIN will be colliding with TIMMY "THE GILA MONSTER" COLE for a shot at the Southeast Title! Is Fin on the verge of becoming a possible double champion? Or will "The Gila Monster" score a shot at the Southeast Champion? -LIVE MATCHES- After his cheating effort last week, DSW Interim President Walter Jared has signed a STEEL CAGE MATCH for the DSW SOUTHEAST TITLE pitting challenger SEAN GUNN against champion "YTLF" CARL BLAIR! Will Gunn walk away from the confines of the Steel Cage victorious? or will Blair shut him down ocne more? The battle lines have finally been drawn, and it has been signed. DSW Heavyweight Champion "WAR MACHINE" DAMIEN CHURCHILL will defend his title against CEE WOODEN in a TOWEL MATCH! In Cee's corner will be "THE PREDATOR" ROB TRAXLER. In Churchill's, "HOMICIDE PERSONIFIED" HARLEY PHIRESTEINER! This is what the war has boiled down too. No pinfalls, no countouts, no disqualification, no submissions! The only way to score victory is for your opponents towel to be tossed in the ring!
