Promo Packet - 05/23/01

23 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ P R O M O P A C K E T v.3 +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ I. "The Little Brit" Ricky Smith II. Razorblade III. "Your True Life Fantasy" Carl Blair IV. "The Predator" Rob Traxler +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ "THE LITTLE BRIT" RICKY SMITH +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ (The scene opens with a shot of Ricky Smith in a rough London bar, some 5 years ago. He looks somewhat different from the young wrestler that we now know him as. His hair is still long and scruffy, though it is his natural light blonde colour. His face is adorned with about a weeks worth of stubble, and he wears thin wire frame glasses. The youngster, who is wearing a pair of blue denim jeans and a scruffy leather jacket, sits at the corner of the bar, staring into a half-pint glass of bitter. The scene is reminiscent of just about every rough bar you see in the movies-biker gangs split into various parts of the establishment, women offering their services, and a disgruntled bartender polishing glasses with spit whilst scanning the area for trouble. As Smith finishes the dregs of the beer from the bottom of his glass, he calls over to the snarling landlord, and speaks in his squeaky, yet well-pronounced accent…) -RS- “Excuse me mate, can I have another half a bitter please?” (The bartender takes the empty glass and begins to fill it back up at an unmarked pump. The beer is cloudy and dirty looking, but the beefy looking man ignores this. He slams the glass down next to Ricky, sending about a quarter of it flying over the rim. Ricky throws a pound coin on the bar and begins to drink up. Suddenly, two leather clad men walk over to where Ricky is seated. They are both large and bearded, and begin laughing at Smith…) -Biker 1- “Half a pint? What the f**k are you? A woman? -Biker 2- “Sounds about right to me-look at the little b*stard” -Biker 1- “Yeah; ‘alf a pint for ‘alf a man.” (The two men laugh as Ricky mumbles incoherently under his breath. The first biker picks up on this, and the smile on his face soon drops…) -Biker 1- “What did you say, ya little puff?” (Ricky shakes his head and quickly downs the rest of his pint. He passes his empty glass to the bartender and begins to speak to him…) -RS- “See you tomorrow Roy-there’s a bad smell in here tonight.” -Bartender- “Alright Rick, see ya.” (Smith jumps down from his tall bar stool, and walks past the first biker, flashing him a dirty look. The biker responds by pulling Ricky and grabbing him around the neck, sending Ricky’s glasses flying. The second biker pulls a flick knife from his jacket pocket, and puts it to Smith’s throat. Within what seems to be a second, the bartender has a sawn off shotgun pointing right up against the biker’s face…) -Bartender- “If you’re gonna do anything, you do it outside-NOT in my f*cking pub.” (The first biker gives the landlord the finger, and drags the young Smith outside. The second, knife-wielding biker soon follows, as do a crowd of other leather-clad louts. Upon leaving the grotty bar, the crowd are stood in a dark, dank, rainy alleyway. The bikers form a circle around Smith and the two men that have set about him. The first biker throws Ricky to the ground. Smith climbs to his feet, shakes his head, and tries to walk away. However, one of the people standing in the burly crowd throws him back into the circle. Smith tries to leave again, but is met by the same. The second biker pulls Ricky up, and holds both of his arms behind his back, instructing the other one to punch him. As the overweight biker swings his right fist towards Ricky’s face, the youngster bows his head down, causing the fist to land square on the other bikers nose. In a swift movement, Smith brings his elbow up, and connects with the throat of the now bloody man. He falls to the floor choking, gripping his throat. The other man runs at Ricky, who ducks out of the way. Running over to one of the people in the crowd, Ricky grabs a large bottle of whiskey from the biker’s hand. Ricky turns around, to see a dazed and confused biker, who has lost his bearings having just missed Smith. The man who Ricky took the bottle from trips Smith up, and the youngster falls to the wet tarmac. Hearing a crack in his arm, Ricky looks up to see the second biker once again running at him. Despite riling in pain, Ricky smashes the bottle on the floor, sending the liqueur and pieces of glass flying everywhere. The bleary eyed Smith lifts the jagged bottle remains into the air. The short glass spikes puncture the biker’s stomach as if it were a wedge of butter. Blood flies, with some landing on Ricky’s face. Ricky squints and climbs to his feet. He spins around, practically blind. As the blood mingles with the raindrops on Ricky’s face, the youngsters dripping hair flails about like a wet mop. Dazed and confused, Smith tries to scan the area. He can just about make out the biker clutching his own throat, and the other blood soaked biker screaming on the floor. Still disorientated, he hears a loud bang. Ricky, in almost slow motion, feels the skin puncture on his right shoulder. Blood flies from the wound, and knocks Smith back for six. As the burly crowd jeers, the blood drenched Smith makes an attempt to run away. A small gap opens for him at one side of the circle, and Ricky takes the advantage. Running, Ricky trips several times, before escaping through yet another London back street.) (Then, darkness.) ……….:BBBBUUUUZZZZZ:……… ………….:BUUUUUUUUUUZZZZZZZZZZZZ:………. …………..:BUUUUUUUZZZZZZZ:……….. (Suddenly, we see Ricky jumping from a bed, shocked and startled. He is staying in a motel somewhere in America. It now becomes evident that we are back up to the present day, as Ricky looks like his usual self. A sign just outside his window continues to make a buzzing sound, and makes Smith’s room flash a bright pink colour. Smith climbs to his feet, and wipes sweat from his brow. He turns on a bedside lamp, and closes the blinds at the window. Though the odd pink flash still escapes through the blind, the lamp now dimly illuminates the room. Ricky gets back in his bed, and sits up with his back to the headboard. Smith looks at his right hand shoulder, staring at a circular scar. He then looks at his left forearm, stroking a long, deep scar. Again wiping sweat from his brow, Ricky reaches down to beneath his bed, and pulls out a black zip satchel. After opening it up, Ricky pulls out a laptop computer. He unplugs the lamp, and refills the empty socket with the chord of his computer. Smith sits back up, turning on the power supply, the start up screens reflecting off his face. As the black screen and white text passes across his unshaven features, Smith stares into space. Occasionally running his left hand over the scar on his right shoulder, he continues to stare for the few minutes that it takes for his computer to load up. Once it does load, Smith comes back around, and the shot changes to one of his computer screen. Smith double clicks a desktop icon, simply labelled as ‘diary’. This brings up a word processing document, some 2000 pages long. Smith scrolls down to the bottom of the page, and begins to type. It reads…) Monday 21st May 2001 Woke up in middle of night again. I continue to dream about my past experiences. This time it was a bar fight that I was involved in around five years ago, in London. I can’t change the past, but I can try and forget about it. Sometimes I think about my family and my home. It’s usually the little things that I miss. But still, I can never go back now. Deep South Wresting could be a good move for me. I don’t ever want too much of a high a profile again, but a good reputation would be nice. In my eyes, wrestling is fun, not work. And if I enjoy it, so should the fans. I’ve been entered into the tournament to crown the first ever DSW world champion. It’s rather daunting, but it’s an honour just to be included. I understand that my first opponent will be one Darrius Campbell. Unfortunately, I don’t know too much about him, but I am told that he is a more than capable wrestler. I hope that we can put on a good show. As always, I’ll offer my hand to shake before the match. I’m sure he will be courteous enough to shake it. But then, my naivety has got me into trouble in the past. As usual, I’ll take every day as it comes. There’s no other way. The injuries are starting to take their toll on me, but I’ll just carry on until I can carry on no more. (The shot switches back to Smith, who makes a couple more clicks, before packing his computer back away. He slides it back under his bed, and settles back down. As Smith attempts to get back to sleep, the scene fades to darkness.) +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ RAZORBLADE +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ [Scene opens to a fairly large estate and slowly closes in the on the front porch. Razorblade, wearing only a bathrobe, opens the door, squints at the morning sun, and picks up the newspaper laying slightly to the left of the door mat. He unfolds the paper, flips through it a bit, and towards the back he focuses in and lets out a laugh.] "(reading from the paper) 'Pro Wrestler Calls It Quits!'; The Associated Press reports that Deep South Wrestling superstar, Derick Craven, calls it quits due to an injury to his neck that the wrestler said had possibly been aggrevated when attacked, only a few days prior to his possibly temporary retirement, by another DSW Wrestler who was wearing a coal miner's glove." [Razorblade smiles a bit, then looks up at the camera.] "You went home. Craven, you couldn't take it any longer and when your neck got a little sore, you went AWOL. How nice. One less thorn in my side here in the DSW. Now that I'm done with you, maybe I should set my sights on some gold? Perhaps. It will come in due time, however.... you cannot keep the greatest in the business down for very long. (Razorblade looks down at the paper again.) Wha... oh! This week in DSW action, I open the broadcast. Let's see... my opponent is..... Terry Madison? Who the hell is that guy? Never really ever heard of him.... I know! (Looks back up at the camera.) MAYBE I can injure him as well and send him to the same retirement home that I sent Craven! Who knows? Nonetheless, I really should be gettin' to the gym, gotta look your best when you're pounding some two-bit nobody into an early...." [All of a sudden Razorblade freezes up. He looks like a deer caught in the glare of headlights, the camera quickly does an about-face and focuses on the other side of the street, where an attractive brunette bends down in a very skimpy nighty to pick up her copy of the same paper. Razorblade starts to say something off camera, but he stutters badly and it is not understandable. The brunette folds the paper under one arm, flips her hair to one side and then gingerly walks back inside as the camera spins back around to look at Razorblade.] ".....*sigh*...... this Sunday! It's time. This Sunday....." [Camera fades to black as Razorblade stumbles back into the house and closes the door.] +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ "YOUR TRUE LIFE FANTASY" CARL BLAIR +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ "Just a whiney bitch..." [The camera turns to fuzz, then pitch black. Slowly coming into focus it reveals "Your True Life Fantasy" Carl Blair lounging out in front of a tv. Blair rests on a lay-z-boy leather recliner, he looks rather comfortable, with a pair of gym shorts, a t-shirt with the arms cut off, and a remote in his hand. His evil grin flashes into the camera as he smiles.] BLAIR: "Is that all I am to you? Just a whiney bitch?" [In his right hand a remote to the television in front of him is held. He presses play, and the camera switches from Blair, to the tv. Blair looks intrigued.] BLAIR: "Lets see, it should be right about here..." [CLIP: Madison has his fist raised in the air, celebrating with the fans when all of a sudden.] (_______________SMACK_______________) ALEXANDER: "DAMN! Blair just SLAMMED that chair into the back of Madison's head!!" [He presses the pause button, freezing it with the chair on Madison's head.] BLAIR: "Now if you look closely, you started to turn around but just didn't make it..." [He laughs turning the tv off.] BLAIR: "Terry, I saw opportunity knocking, sorry I didn't let you turn around, I just couldn't wait to splatter you brains in all different directions. What difference does it make anyway? Whether I hit you in the front or the back, it all has the same effect right...BUT if you so desperately want your face mangled, I'll be sure next time to knock a few teeth out when I dent the chair face first, that way you know for sure who done it. If you remember that is... "As for being a tag along, we're a TAG TEAM, team as in partners, meaning your there for your teammate, that might be alittle much to grasp for someone with such a high IQ level, like yourself." [Carl pauses, still flashing that million dollar smile of his.] BLAIR: "I've got bigger and better things than you Madison. Like the Heavyweight championship, which I'm soon to have in my posession. Ticalion Stallion, looks as though you'll be one of the first to be eliminated, ofcourse you could save yourself sometime by going ahead and packing your bags and getting the hell outta dodge. Madison it looks as though me an you are set to face not once, but twice. That is if you get past Razorblade, which looks to be a difficult task...All pun a side, the Troubleshooters Inc? You and Wooden, two of my favorites...boy o' boy I can't wait. Me and Churchill are gonna have some fun with this one... "But until then, just remember one thing. For some peoples fantasies to come to life, they must sleep and dream, but for all of you, you just have to open your eyes..." [Camera fades to black.] +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ "THE PREDATOR" ROB TRAXLER +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ -[- It's 4:30 p.m. on this rainy Georgia Tuesday afternoon. The playground behind the Cross of Life Montessori School in Roswell, Georgia is now empty, the few kids from the late stay program all picked up by nannies or parents. Rob Traxler sits on one of the benches, looking around the 13 acres of wooded beauty that surrounds the prestigious early entry, advanced learning school. The rain is falling down softly, but it does not bother him at all as he inhales deeply, taking in the full scent of fresh rain falling on Georgia pine trees. His peaceful solitude is broken temporarily by the sound of a car pulling up to the school. -]- <+> Kirk Rose gets out of his fully restored 1971 Dodge Charger, a gift from Traxler a few years ago as a sign of their friendship. He holds an umbrella above his head, obviously cautious about his hairstyle on a day such as this. <+> -[- I don't need to turn my head..I already know who it is...the sound of the engine is too distinct. It must be Kirk Rose coming to check up on me...he's no doubt heard everything by now. -]- <+> Ok, I need to proceed toward Rob with caution...I've seen this man go through trauma before, but I've never seen him turn it into such power inside the ring like he has of late. I know Rob is simply using wrestling as his venting point right now, but if it could spill over into real life violence, when will it begin? Now? No...we're friends...he wouldn't harm someone whom he has claimed a deep amount of respect for, would he? Wait though...he went after Wooden in the ring Sunday night with ferocity and almost anger. But then again, Wooden provoked him before the match slightly...ok, that settles it, just don't say anything stupid that could set Rob off, and you'll be fine... <+> -[- He's probably read all the DSW dirt sheets...studied the guys already... but at least he'll focus on my DSW jobs ahead as I compete in the Heavyweight Title tournament. Surely he won't be like most stupid reporters, including his usual self, and start off with some dumb crap about my personal life by making some generic comment of pity... -]- "So...been a rough week, huh Rob?" <+> DAMNIT! You imbecile! Ah hell, now I'm gonna be on the receiving end of a beating! "Been a rough week, huh Rob?"...you fuckin' moron! <+> -[- Moron...how did I know I was going to be wrong...chalk another one up to pure imbecility for Kirk. -]- "You always were the observant one, Kirk." <+> Thank God...he's pretty calm right now it appears...okay, let's continue things on a different foot this time. <+> "Rob, I'm sure you know how sorry I am about the situation with Janice and the kids. But I am also sure you don't really want to talk about it, so how about we just talk Deep South Wrestling? That seems to be your main focus at the moment." -[- Kirk's known me for 7 years, and he still doesn't know when the cameras aren't rolling...talk about being desperate for a job again. He hears I'm back in the ring, and the first thing he wants to do is become my interviewer again. Hrmpf, ok, fine...time to make him just about wet his pants... -]- "We'll get to me in a moment. Kirk, how would you like to become my official promoter in DSW? Mac's been hounding me that I need a front office presence, and since I can't do it myself, I could use you as my liaison with the suits. What do you say?" <+> Promoter??? He wants me to be a goddamn promoter? I have been basically his exclusive interviewer and reporter for the last 6 years, and instead of using me for what I'm good at, he wants me to be his damned promoter? What in the hell is going on here??? Wait though, maybe it pays well. <+> "Uhm...I say that I'd like to know the compensation package? I mean, I'm more suited to do interviews than to try to make arrangements with the "big boys" ya know?" "Whatever Mac pays you will be enough...you know I always treat you right...I see you are still enjoying the Charger, hmm?" <+> Damn...he got me there. Sometimes I wish he was just a dumb jock. <+> "You're right Rob, I'm sorry I was so greedy. So what's up? I hear you're in the title tournament starting this week? And you're facing Delaney?" -[- I knew it...gotta love how he does his homework. -]- "Correct...this gives me a wonderful chance to get back at Marty for not doing his part to help us win the tag team titles like I would have preferred. The fans deserve to see him get his ass handed to him for letting them down the way he did Sunday night...for letting ME down, Kirk. I chose him because I knew he was capable of doing great things, but he just wasn't there for me on Sunday." "You say he's capable of doing great things...do you still feel that way? In other words, do you think he presents a good opponent for you this week?" <+> Awesome question, Kirk...you really opened this issue up for a good discussion. <+> "Kirk...how many times have I EVER picked my own opponent and picked a no- name lack of talent...I always go after the challenges, Kirk, you know that." <+> DAMNIT AGAIN! I am such an idiot...maybe he's right...maybe I am not cut out for interviewing anymore if I can't carry on a decent discussion about his matches. <+> "Yeah, I do...sorry. Uhm...So what are your thoughts going into this match, then? I know you're not scared, but aren't you at least a little anxious? I mean, this is the first singles title tournament you've been a part of in quite some time..." -[- Anxious about a title I haven't had the privilege of holding in 3 years? Anxious about a tournament where the only person to blame is yourself, just like in my real life? Anxious...ah, stop it, Rob...you're tormenting yourself again...just answer the question the only way you know how... -]- "I don't view this as a tournament, Kirk. I am taking this Heavyweight title one match at a time. Sunday, it's "The Hustler" that stands in my sight. This man knows extreme fighting like few others, and I know he will bring it all to the table. But he will be leaving with nothing but crumbs, wondering what he could have changed in order to truly be ready for my attack. I have proven these last few weeks that I can come out of nowhere and shift the momentum into my favor, and then I seal the deal. Marty may want to think that he's the sure underdog in this match, but if he looks at it from that perspective, he is only guaranteeing himself a slice of mediocrity out of the DSW pie. Right now, I am enjoying a large portion of it all, and I'm not about to hand my plate off to anyone else just because they feel sorry for themselves. It could be anyone this Sunday...the fact that it is Marty just gives me more motivation since he didn't do his job for the team." <+> I swear...this guy never cracks...he has always been so firm on his ground when discussing his sport. There's just gotta be someway to get inside that head of his...I wish I could examine his thoughts... <+> -[- Whew...way to go, Rob. He ate that one up...look at the smile on his face. Man, I haven't smiled like that in a while...I wonder what it must be like to be the one asking all the questions instead of answering them...I bet it's easier to do than trying to come up with sophisticated answers... -]- <+> Crap! I'm out of questions...I knew I should have prepared more for this. Thank God there are no cameras around, or I'd really be screwed right now...ok, time to move this conversation to food...I am getting hungry as hell. <+> "That makes perfect sense to me, Rob...you truly are focused on this match. Now, whaddya say I take you out to dinner...after you clean up some...you are absolutely soaked from this rain." -[- Kirk always changes the subject to food when he's out of questions...oh well, so be it. -]- "I may be soaked from the soft rain that pours down from the heavens today, but Marty Delaney is going to be soaked in his own blood, sweat, and tears on Sunday night because I may hunt for the thrill, but I always get the kill. Let's go eat." <+> Thank goodness this discussion is over...food!!!! <+> -[- Damn...glad to be done with that conversation...time to refuel. -]- "Uhm, Rob...how'd you get here anyways? I don't see your Cherokee anywhere." -[- Oh shit...not again. -]- "I'm not quite sure, Kirk...I just opened my eyes, and I was here..." <+> Oh shit...not again. <+> "Rrrrrrright." -[- The two men get up from the bench and head for Rose's car. Kirk cranks up the heat to help dry and warm Rob as they head to the hotel before dinner. Off in the distance atop a nearby hill, a silhouetted female stands against the dark sky with a pair of binoculars at her side, cursing at the wind. -]- "Damn you, Kirk Rose...I thought I had gotten rid of you, too. Time for plan B to take effect..."
