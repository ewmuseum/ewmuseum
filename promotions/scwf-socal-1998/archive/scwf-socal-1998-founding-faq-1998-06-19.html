Southern California Wrestling Federation is a regional fed based out of Southern California. You may be asking yourself why is SCWF different from all the normal feds. Well SCWF is a regional fed, or umbrella league, of the Universal Wrestling Alliance. We will have our own flash shows and our own cards, but once a month the alliance will culminate in a card that will showcase all of the regions. Interaction between the regions is allowed and encouraged on any card held. The SCWF is slightly different from the other regions as we wrestle all matches by No DQ rules. 2. Who runs SCWF? Rules and Regulations 1. How are the Matches determined? Matches are determined after consulting with the SCWF and UWA Executives. All FINAL decisions about winners are decided by Brandon, Gordon, and Casey. If you want to WIN, here is the breakdown of how a winner is decided. 75% Roleplays 15% Gameplans 10% Heat/Angles 2. What are the roleplay rules? You are allowed *THREE* roleplays a week. These can either be during the week in the flash shows, called "California Ramblings" or at the weekly card, which is called an 'in-ring'. The flashes in the flash shows will count towards the match, and the in-rings will count towards heat/angle/push. These roleplays *MUST* be at least 30 lines in length. Those less than 30 lines will *NOT* be counted, nor posted. 3. How do I get matches? There are a couple of ways - I sign you to a match - You challenge someone in a roleplay - You send in the 'formal' request 4. Are sneak attacks allowed? Yes, in moderation. I have the right to refuse a sneak attack, but I do not have the right to create one without your permission. Sneak attacks will be counted ONLY if it has to do with an angle, or a decision to START a new angle. <>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>! III. Events and Shows. 1. What shows are there? **Showcase Sunday** *5 LIVE matches* 2. Are there PPV's? Yes, there are PPV's held every other month. Since we are members of the UWA, the PPV's are held by them, but we, as a fed, take part in them. When PPV's are NOT held, on the odd months, there will be a SUPERCARD held at the end of each month. At this card, ALL of the titles will be decided from each region as well as the UWA titles. <>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>! IV. Titles 1. How will the titles be decided? The titles will be decided in the beginning in different ways. The Heavyweight Title will be decided with a tournament, and the other titles will be decided in matches of MY choosing. 2. How often will the titles be defended? *SCWF California Heavyweight Title* -Every month and at ALL PPVs/Supercards *SCWF California Television Title* -Every other week and at ALL PPVs/Supercards 3. How can I get a title shot? - If the champion challenges you and I see fit. - If you're one of the top three active contenders for the belt. - If I see fit you deserve one. <>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>! V. Match Info. 1. How often can my guy wrestle? There is no limit. Sometimes he'll wrestle twice on the same card, sometimes once a week, and sometimes once a month. It all depends on your angle, your RPs, and how many wrestlers are contracted. 2. What is the Rankings System? +5 - Win by Pinfall or Submission +2 - Win by Disqualification or Countout 0 - Draw -2 - Loss by Disqualification or Countout -5 - Loss by Pinfall or Submission 3. Can I add stipulations to my matches? Yes, you most certainly can, as long as it is REASONABLE. I might be a little picky if it's TOO extreme, since I am planning on saving those matches for PPVs. And I don't want any of the 'Exploding Turnbuckle, Electrified Barbed Wire, First Man to Die Loses' Matches, because I'll laugh you out of the office :) 4. What are the regular rules to an average match? One Fall - 15 Minute Time Limit, No DQ Win by Pinfall, Submission, Disqualification, or Countout Titles Switch Hands - Pinfall or Submission <>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!<>!
