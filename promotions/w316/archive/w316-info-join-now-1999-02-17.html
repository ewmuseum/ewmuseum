Introduction We're BAAACCKK! Ya may remember us as being as we put it a year ago "The most
ELECTRIFYING fed on the net!". You may be new, or be one of the old guys, either way, thanks for your interest in joining. Below is all the info you need on us. Feel free to send this around to your friends!
Requirments Wrestling 316 roleplayers are required at least 45 lines a roleplay, and must roleplay at least 3 times a week. If you can't meet those requirements, please do not submit an application.
World Wide Web W316.com is viewed aproximately 50,000 times a day and over 1,500,000 times a month. You have to understand that when you're a roleplayer in W316, you have
1,500,000 watching your attitude. W316.com is the home base for Wrestling 316.
It may or not be up when you recieve this application. For all the latest news and updates, visit
www.w316.com . The Rules Unfortuneately, you have to follow some rules. These rules are expected to be followed 100%. If a rule is broken, the member will recieve a warning. If the member breaks another rule, he recieves a violation. 1 violation is a 1 card suspension. If a wrestler gets 3 violations, they are fired from Wrestling
316, reguardless of their championship and talent status.
1. AOL members can not TOS other Wrestling 316 AOL members. Any problems should be reported to the staff, and will be taken care of immediately. 2. Wrestling 316 has the right to fire anybody at anytime. A wrestler is expected to be mature if fired and not try to reak hell. Should a wrestler try to cause problems, they will be blocked from W316.com. 3. If you decide to quit, you must give 48 hours notice. You will be removed from any e-mail lists. Other Wrestling 316 members are expected to use updated rosters. If you continue to recieve mail, let us know and we will deal with the problem.
4. You cannot have control over any other characters except your own. You can talk about a wrestler, however, you can not make him do any physical or talking actions. Any roleplays useing characters besides your own will be disqualified.
5. You cannot copy and paste sections of roleplays. This includes useing roleplays from other feds. 6. All wrestlers are required to three 45 line roleplays a week unless otherwise arranged. Events/Judgement/Results Wrestling 316 holds one main card a week and two House Shows. Pay Per Views are once a month. Roleplays are judged by the effort, not neccisarily the length. For example, if someone roleplayed three 45 line roleplays, and their opponent roleplayed one 200 line roleplay, the 200 line will win for the effort. Results for Incoming: Friday Night are written out in their entirety:
move by move, word by word. House Show results are breif recaps of the matches. Non Player Characters Non Player Characters, or NPCs, are not controlled by anyone. NPCs wrestle
House Shows. Each NPC has his own personality and skill level. NPCs are used on new members to develop better skills. You can read about NPCs in the Bios section of this webpage. Wrestlers without much talent or in training will wrestle NPCs.
World Tours Sometimes a wrestler may go on tour. Some tour destinations include Europe,
South America, Japan, Africa and others. When a wrestler is on tour, he will be fighting NPC's. If a wrestler is sent on a tour and does well, it could mean a title shot when he returns to Friday TV. If a wrestler does poorly on a tour, it could mean a career of house shows. When you're on a tour, you must roleplay in order to get yourself a push in Wrestling 316.
Your Gimmic No one can ever make it in e-wrestling without a good gimmic. Wrestling 316 permits fake wrestlers only, so you need to create your own character. You can make him a take off of a real wrestler, such as Stone Cold John Smith, or create something totaly new, like The Immortal. You need a strong gimmic.
Some top choices are gothic, bad ass, pretty boy, and anything else you can possibly think of.
How to Join You can join Wrestling 316 anytime, however, it is possible you will be placed on a waiting list. We may also reject your application. If we reject your application, feel free to try again. Remember, practice makes perfect.
The Application Because of the overwhelming demand to jojn Wrestling 316, you may be put on a waiting list. However, you may get right in. Once you submit the application you should recieve more information and our response in 48 hours. If you don't hear from us, chances are you weren't accepted. If you weren't accepted, feel free to try again. Remember, if you do not fill out this application in its entirety, YOU WILL AUTOMATICALLY BE REJECTED! FAKE WRESTLERS ONLY!
