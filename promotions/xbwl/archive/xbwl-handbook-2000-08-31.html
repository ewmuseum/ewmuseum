XBWL Handbook

XBWL Handbook Version 1 1. Introduction: The XBWL is the premiere e-wrestling federation on the Internet. The XBWL is one of the best e-feds that you'll ever find. XBWL is a Create-A-Wrestler federation and we only accept original wrestlers, no copies or imitations from
Televised Wrestling Leagues. In December of 1996, three men had finally had enough of the close-the-door-in-a-week e-feds that were popping up all over the internet and decided that it was time to found something that would last forever. So on January 1st 1997 the fed that was then called USWL held the
Pay-Per-View that started a tradition in E-Wrestling. Soon there after the name was changed to XBWL and the tradition was carried on. That was over 4
Years ago. Since then the XBWL has risen above and beyond all speculations.
Through good times and bad times, the XBWL has always survived. Everytime it was thought to be dead, it rose again.. better than before.
2. Roleplaying: The XBWL's roleplaying is done through an outside server called EGroups. EGroups is a mailing list that you send a mail to and it automatically then sends it to the entire EGroups list. The reason for this is simple. On AOL, that keeps anyone from every being about to TOS your account and on the
Internet it gives all users equal opportunity to send the roleplays to everyone without having to keep up with multiple email addresses. The only address you have to remember is
[detail withheld] . Once you are a member of this mailing list, you are a member of the XBWL. XBWL has no limits on the amount of roleplays you can do each week although the minimum is TWO. We have a very unique way of deciding the winners of our matches and it is what keeps the XBWL strong. If you do two roleplays and your opponent does two roleplays then we compare them head to head to see how well they were done. If you do two roleplays and your opponent does four roleplays then we judge your two against his BEST two and go from there. Roleplaying more than the minimum will not hurt you, it will just give you an opportunity to perfect your roleplaying style and compile your best roleplays for the judging.
All roleplays are judged by the XBWL Committee. The XBWL Committee consists of the XBWL President, Vice-President and anyone else deemed necessary to keep the league running smoothly. There will always be an Odd number of people on the
XBWL Committee that vote for the roleplays and this prevents ties.
All roleplays must be done IN CHARACTER. Any remarks that severely go Out of
Character or target Out of Character events will subject your Roleplay to being
Invalid.
3. XBWL Events, PPV's, and Weekly Shows: At this time the XBWL only has one weekly show, Tuesday Night X-Treme. The
XBWL has one PPV/Supercard every month and it is where the major titles are decided. The World Title is only defended on PPV and occassionally on the
Weekly Show, depending on the angles/heat surrounding the belt. XBWL has 12
PPV's per year and they are as follows:
New Years Brawl - A large Battle Royal consisting of all XBWL members main events this show, and the winner recieves a shot at the XBWL World Heavyweight
Title at the following PPV. This PPV is usually held on January 1st.
Nuclear Winter - This is the Febuary PPV, the last blowout of the winter consisting of the winner of New Year's Brawl highlighting this PPV against the
World Champion.
WrestleWar - Usually held in Early March, either a re-match from Nuclear Winter takes place or the person with the most heat will go for the XBWL World
Heavyweight Title. The Hardcore Title is defended here in an Exploding Cage
Match. This is the biggest PPV of the XBWL.
Out of Control - This PPV usuall begins to set up one of the brutal months in the XBWL called Suicide Month. In Suicide Month, all titles are defended randomly and against random opponents. Out of Control is a tradition, and a name. This PPV is held in late April.
XBWL Suicide - This PPV ends Suicide Month and the long road through blood and sweat that the athletes have endured finally pays off as the last remaining members of the Suicide Tournament fight in a Chinese Death Match for the XBWL
World Heavyweight Title. This match is the most brutal and bloodiest matches in the history of the XBWL.
Monarchy of Violence - Still reeling from Suicide Month, several athletes will still yearn for that sadistic style leading in June as they nurse injuries and pull themselves back together, they get ready for the next PPV in June which is highlighted by a Monarchy of Violence World Title Match.
Summer Sizzler - This is the annual Summer PPV for the XBWL. Held in July, the tradition for this is to have a special guest referee for the main event, usually a previous retired World Champion, sometimes it's the man that the two men involved in the World Title match have the most problems with.
Caged Combat - This PPV, held in August is built up through the month with several random teams fighting each other to the climax, Caged Combat which features a double-stacked, double cage match where Four Men Enter and one man walks out with the XBWL World Heavyweight Title.
River of Blood - The September PPV that usually contains many brutal matches with the World Heavyweight Title decided in a Barbed Wire Cage.
Halloween Haunting - This PPV held on Halloween is an annual PPV set deep in the tradition of the XBWL and USWL. The Main Event is usually a gimmick match of some sort.
Chaotic November - This PPV is held usually in Mid-November and starts working towards building up New Year's Brawl. This year will be a first for this style of PPV and it will be intersting to see how it pans out.
Eternally Damaged - This PPV held in early December is the last PPV of the year and is usually one of our biggest PPV's.
4. Conduct of Members: We expect our members to conduct themselves in a manner that does not infringe on the rights of others. Racial Comments or Slurs are handled and dealt with swiftly. We don't mind firing you from the XBWL if you get out of control and we will do it in a heartbeat, although we do give you a warning. If you are talking through IM or ICQ, do not talk to the handler of a character as though he is that character. All discussions through IM's by members are considered private matters, until they begin to infringe on the rights of others. If you use profanity or any other language through AIM then you are subject to the punishments of AOL or your local internet provider depending on your violation of thier Terms of Service.
5. Punishment System: The Punishment System in the XBWL for those who; violate rules, verbally attack someone out of character, Spam the Mailing List, Do any other action that the XBWL deems worthy of Punishment is subject to the following THREE
STRIKE warning system.
1st Strike - Warning, Put on Notice. 2nd Strike - Membership to the XBWL is Moderated, meaning you can't post a roleplay or message to egroups without Presidential Approval. 3rd Strike - Final Strike - You are banned from the XBWL Mailing List for the length of One Month, when you return you will be on probation for one additional month, meaning any further screw-ups will result in permanent suspension.
