XBWL Update

30 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When I decided to re-open the XBWL I figured that it would take me around a months time to get the XBWL off the ground and up and running so I layed out a prospective date of the end of November being the opening day with the first card not being scheduled till December. Well, It's been 48 hours since I announced the return and rebirth of the #1, Most spectacular E-Fed on the face of E-Wrestling. The response has been phenomenal to say the LEAST.. I've signed 12 Superstars in 2 days.. With the original plan only calling for 20 at a Max, I may reopen the doors of the XBWL even quicker than I planned! If you were thinking of joining the XBWL, there's roughly EIGHT spots left before the
XBWL becomes full and we begin to go over applications with a fine tooth comb as we did in our days of glory! Visit the website and get your application in today, Don't be one of the 100's that WISH they could be in the XBWL, be a part of the ELITE and BEST E-Fed before we fill up again!!!
Thanks, XBWLPresident www.angelfire.com/wrestling3/xbwl Yes, It's true. The XBWL will be reopening. The webpage can be found here: http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling3/xbwl For those of you who don't remember, we were around for FIVE years before we closed. I had a personal tragedy in my life that forced me to close the doors around a year and a half ago and then I got sidetracked. Bottom line is, I'm back.. And I'm putting 100% into the XBWL this time. Wanna hear something better? I've got teh President of the EVCW as my vice president AND, I have personally signed some of the best talent on the Net. My Fed was respected for
YEARS as the best federation out there. I will only accept 20 Participants, so get your application in.
XBWL is a totally fantasy based wrestling league, meaning you can have only the fake wrestlers created. No rip offs! Only the Originals. Go to the WebSite and fill out the application. You won't regret it!
This federation will feature a unification of the XBWL/EVCW/WRX Federations. Look for talent from all three highly recognized federations to be present!
