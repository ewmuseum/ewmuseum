XBWL Handbook

1 March 2000

http://come.to/xbwl Go there for Application introduction Welcome to the X-Tremely Brutal Wrestling League! This is a league unlike any other. We are committed to making this the most pleasurable e-wrestling experience that you have ever had. We have what we consider to be an unbeatable combination of dedicated staff and members. The XBWL has been in existance since January 1996 when we opened our doors as the USWL. We changed our name after about six months when our league went through a transitional period.
There are many members still in the league that were here when we started on day one! Considering that we have kept those members and stayed open through many difficult times, we figure we must be doing something right! We know we won't be the right leaguefor everyone, but if you are the type of person that enjoys e-wrestling for what it really is, a hobby that was meant to be "FUN"
then the XBWL may be the place for you. All we ask is that you take a few minutes to read all the way through this handbook before you make a decision on whether to join or not. We hope you like what read and we hope to see you in the XBWL!
league explaination The XBWL is an original league, we go the extra mile to try and make our league different from the other ones out there. The XBWL is "primarily" a BOOKED league. We do use a simulator for three things, those three things are for occasional card commentary, to simulate our special battle royal card and to simulate an "OCCASIONAL" match. Generally we will pick the winner of the match by who out roleplayed their opponent, however if both people roleplayed equally then we will use the simulator to decide the winner. (A further explanation about how we use the simulator for our special battle royal card that we hold is in the Wednesday card section.) If you want to win your matches you HAVE to roleplay! A simulator will NOT help you win a match in this league except for the battle royal card. The XBWL is also unique in several other ways like we will use a specialized RANKINGS SYSTEM so that rookies aren't held down simply because they are new to the league. With our rankings system you will know exactly what you need to do to get a title shot and thus, more effectively be able to adjust your strategy to position yourself where you want to be in the league. Your future is in your own hands, there is no one in this league that will hold you down. If you roleplay and win your matches the gold belts are there for the taking!
are we 'hardcore'? In the past, the xbwl has featured some matches that have been called 'hardcore' by ourselves and others. However, as time has gone by, we're finding that some of these matches take away from the realistic enivorment we like to work with. At the same time, we realize that many of our current members, and possibly future ones, do enjoy particpating in hardcore matches, and would be upset if we totally removed them from this league. In an effort to make a comprimise, we've made the following decisions: hardcore wrestlers/matches will still happen in the XBWL (within reason), in matches surronding the Hardcore
Title, and when angles and fueds need or deserve it. However, in other parts of the card, we're going encourage more 'traditional' matches. And we may disallow gimmick matches strictly for the fact of doing a weird gimmick.
To summarize, people will still bleed, go through tables, and other wrestling standards will continue to exist in the XBWL, as will normal hardcore matches exisit in relevant matches. At the same time, we reserve the right ot refust to allow hardcore matches in situations where we don't think it's needed or deserved, or to reject a gimmick match if we find it to be too unrealistic rankings system (effective 01/08/2000) *UPDATED* overview
In many leagues rookies are at a disadvantage because the veteran members have a jump on in them in the amount of matches they have had and are further up in the rankings. That sort of a jump makes it hard for rookies to catch up and get the big matches they want. Our system eliminates that problem for the rookies without punishing the veterans! The XBWL bases its rankings on every wrestlers
"last 10 matches" only. This is the best system because it bases everything on what has happened recently. This rankings system affects both the veterans and the rookies equally. The veterans past is not taken into consideration so as not to harm the rookies chances of being ranked fairly high quickly if they come in and do well right off the bat by winning a lot of their matches. It also does protect the veterans because rookies have to pay thier dues and win the lower ranked titles before they can challenge for the higher ranked titles. Furthermore, it's possible to turn around your XBWL career if you struggle, as only the last 10 matches are counted, so old loses can be replaced by new wins (and vice versa, if you don't keep up your performance.)
awards We trust in the committee's ability to judge who won matches fairly, thus having interviews or vignettes on cards do not help your rankings.
Each wrestler or tag team earning a pinfall or submission win will be awarded three (3) points.
Each wrestler or tag team earning a disqualification or countout win will recieve two (2) points.
Each wrestler or tag team earning a draw will receive one (1) point, while losses receive no points.
Separate rankings will be kept for singles and tag team matches. No tag team match can count towards singles rankings, and no singles match can count towards tag team rankings.
points expiration The points awarded to a wrestler after a match shall expire after ten (10) matches in the same bracket (singles or tag team) have been fought or after four (4) months, whichever comes first. Points are not held, saved, or spent, they are mearly a way to gauge how your wrestler is doing in the league.
rankings Contenders shall be ranked in ascending order, with number one having the greatest amount of points. Champions, while they will have the same information available (wins, losses, draws, points), will be kept separate from all contenders. All rankings shall be available by digest or via the XBWL web site.
challenging for titles when rankings are relevant Although there will not be separate rankings for each singles title, it will be the responsibility of the wrestler/handler to reasonably consider what title(s)
they are eligible for and challenge accordingly. The XBWL Committee reserves the right to deny any title challenge with due reason.
when rankings are not relevant There will be occasions, such as challenges from a champion, or in the natural procession of an angle/feud, when a lower ranked wrestler will receive a title shot. Wrestlers/Handlers in the XBWL should recognize this through reading cards and roleplays and should not voice complaints. The XBWL is about titles, but it is not ALL about titles. If a wrestler involves himself in a storyline then he can be rewarded with title matches.
general xbwl rules *UPDATED* Below are a few rules and codes of order that we currently use in the XBWL. Note that despite the author's (and the committee's) best intentions, we may have neglected to mention something that's currently enforced. This document will be updated with changes as they happen, but if you're ever unsure of something, the best thing to do is e-mail the XBWL committee.
Please note that we strive for originality in the XBWL. No "real" wrestler, nor any wrestler mimicing a "real life" character under a different name, will be permitted entry for competition in the XBWL.
roleplaying *UPDATED* *UPDATED* Currently, each XBWL member is required to do one roleplay a week or face penalties under the "strikes" system. There's no roleplay length requirement, although extremely short roleplays may not be counted. Members are encouraged to e-mail the XBWL committee ahead of time if they're going to be prevented from doing a roleplay that week.
Roleplays must be submitted no later than 12 noon CST the day before a card is sent, or it will not count.
There is currently no limit on swearing in roleplays, but when done excessively it can show a lack of originality and count against you in judging match results. Try to use moderation when using swear words in roleplays.
Unacceptable content in roleplays (overly sexual, racist, etc.) will be dealt with harshly, and can result in punitive action up to a wrestler's ejection from the XBWL.
Wrestlers should take note to keep their roleplays "in character." That is, do not use roleplays to make personal verbal attacks on other handlers. It is unprofessional and may be punished using the "strikes" system.
Any direct rip-off of a "real" wrestling catch phrase, angle, or gimmick seen in a roleplay, although not necessarily deserving of disciplinary action, can and will result in negative judging of roleplays and should be avoided at all costs.
Wrestlers should take care not to break "kayfabe" in their roleplays, as well. This includes refraining from calling people "jobbers," referring to "face" and
"heel" alignment, and other such terminology that is typically kept to the backstage in professional wrestling. Referring to grammar and spelling errors in roleplays fall under this rule as well. Failure to do so can result in anything from roleplays containing such breaks being judged negatively to punishment as per the "strikes" system.
No wrestler is, for any reason, to use another wrestler in a roleplay with out prior consent from the other wrestler's handler and prior knowledge of the XBWL
Committee. Any wrestler caught doing so will be subject to the "strikes"
system. Any wrestler making an attack on another wrestler in a roleplay without prior consent will be subject to a one (1) month suspension from competition and a stripping of all points earned or outright ejection from the
XBWL, depending on the severity of the case.
*NEW* Any wrestler portraying another wrestler without expressly noting that it is an in-character parody or satire within the set-up or scenery of a roleplay may have their roleplay judged negatively and may be subject to the "strikes"
system. roleplays and winning matches *NEW* The XBWL understands that there are circumstances under which handlers cannot put out one roleplay a day, or may not have enough material to work with to put out an equal amount of roleplays against an opponant. The XBWL has implimented a system which we feel is fair and rewards quality of roleplaying over quantity of roleplaying.
A wrestler must roleplay to win. If a wrestler does not put a roleplay out the week of a match, he will lose his match. Also, he will be subject to penalties as per Sec.V.A.1.
If two wrestlers or tag teams roleplay equally in terms of quantity, then all roleplays will be judged by the committee and each member will pick a winner for each match. The votes will be tallied and the wrestler with the most votes will end up winning the match.
If one wrestler out-roleplays his opponant as far as quantity, then the roleplays of the wrestler roleplaying least will be judged against the same number of what are considered the best roleplays the wrestler roleplaying most wrote. For example, Wrestler A roleplays 3 times for a match and Wrestler B roleplays 2 times. The 2 best roleplays Wrestler A wrote will be put up against Wrestler B's 2 roleplays, and the committee will judge a winner.
If neither wrestler roleplays for a match, either a winner will be determined randomly or the match will be scrapped.
strats Currently the XBWL does not use a strat (short for strategy) system for matches. If there is a particular spot or backstage happening you would like your wrestler to perform, let the XBWL Committee know at least one (1) day in advance of the match in question.
attacks Attacks are written by the committee and card writers. Wrestlers are not to write out in play-by-play (PBP) format an attack on another wrestler. Handlers who wish to have their wrestler attack another wrestler are asked to send in who they want to attack, when, and any special details about how the attack should happen, to the XBWL committee before the roleplay deadline for that card. Attacks are on a "first come, first serve" basis and may not be used if it doesn't fit with previously submitted attacks.
No wrestler is to attack another wrestler in a roleplay without prior consent of the attacked wrestler and prior knowledge of the XBWL Committee. Any wrestler caught doing this will be subject to a one (1) month suspension or outright ejection from the XBWL, depending on the severity of the case.

discipline *NEW* "strikes" system Overview: The XBWL shall deal with minor problems with handlers using a system of strikes. This is to insure education before ejection. We realize sometimes that people make mistakes, and this seems to be the best system to allow for those mistakes.
the Strikes Strike 1: For the first breaking of any rule in the XBWL Handbook in a given month, a handler will be given, via e-mail, a written warning from an XBWL
Committee member explaining the exact infraction and the rule broken, referencing this handbook.
NOTE: If, after warning, this rule is broken again within the span of three (3) days, Strike 2 will be skipped. Strike 2: For the second breaking of any rule in the XBWL Handbook in a given month, a handler will be informed, via e-mail, of the exact infraction and the rule broken, and his posting status within the XBWL will be "moderated." That is, an XBWL Committee member must approve all e-mail sent to the XBWL mailing list. Also, the member will be put on probation, and his actions concerning the XBWL will be closely monitored until the end of the month.
Strike 3: For the third breaking of any rule in the XBWL Handbook in a given month, a handler will be informed, via e-mail, of the exact infraction and the rule broken, and he will be suspended from competition for a period of one (1)
month. He will be prohibited from posting to the XBWL mailing list, although he will still receive XBWL mailings. Likewise the character handled will, through injury or other means, be removed from competition for the suspension period. All points that the wrestler has acquired in the rankings will be erased, and any title held at the time of suspension will be immediately stripped.
the Outs Each time a handler reaches Strike 3 in a given month, he will also be given an
"out." Any handler who gets two (2) outs in one year's time will be removed from the XBWL. The wrestler will, in character, be either injured such that they can no longer compete in the XBWL or fired from the XBWL. All points will be removed and any titles will be stripped.
Any handler removed from the XBWL in this way must wait a period of six (6) months before re-applying to the XBWL. expiration of strikes and outs On the first day of each month, any strikes against a handler are removed and they start with a "clean slate." Handlers should remember that this is not a free ticket to break the same rule consistently every month, and any handler seen abusing this privilege will be dealt with outside of the "strikes" system.
On the first day of each year, one (1) out will be removed from a handler's record. This removal does not end a outs-related suspension or shorten its term. No handler may have less than zero (0) outs.
Other Discipline Minor discipline problems of discourteous behavior towards other wrestlers not explicitly dictated in this handbook will be dealt with outside of the
"strikes" system. No discipline dealt forth in this manner will exceed the resetting of a wrestler's win/loss record and removal of all points from a wrestler's record. Any case dealt with this way will be considered for entry into the General Rules section (Sec. V.E.) under "Other Behavior."
Major Discipline problems, including hate-filled roleplays, IM abuse, wrestler abuse, excessive spamming of the mailing list or the committee list, will be dealt with outside of the "strikes" system. Any problem dealt with this way will result in a minimum of a one (1) month suspension and may range up to permanent removal from XBWL competition.

xbwl events The XBWL has three events every weeksort match of some . We have three events because we believe that if we keep people involved in a that it will not only help keep people interest in our league but it will also give them even more to roleplay about"try" to . We generally also make sure everyone gets at least one match a week if they roleplay.
tuesday - terror Tuesday's card is a basic card with between 5 and 8 matches. This card is not fully written out, instead we give you a basic summary of that happened the match hitting the high points. The main event will usually be written in play-by-play (PBP) format, and other matches may be PBP, if card writers or handlers are interested. Terror is typically used to recap the previous week's events and help to set up matches for Saturday Slobberknocker.
saturday - slobberknocker Saturday's card is another general card containing 5 to 8 matches written in full PBP form. This is the league's flagship show, and is comparable to "Raw"
or "Nitro." The show will contain interviews, attacks, and multiple title matches. Slobberknocker is also the show that sets up the pay-per-view matches.
xbwl ppv's The XBWL hosts 12 Pay-Per-View events a year, one each month. Much like the
WWF had their "Classic 5," the XBWL has their "Big 4" pay-per-view events:
January 1 - New Year's Brawl from Times Square in NYC, featuring a battle royale. March - WrestleWar September - Summer Sizzler October 31 - Halloween Hauntings Our other 8 offerings do not have set names, and may change from year to year. They are all important shows, representing the best the XBWL has to offer. All are written in full PBP format. These are the shows where titles are decided, feuds are finished, and stables are created. Each weekly show builds to the
Pay-per-view events. Most or all of the roster will usually compete on
pay-per-view shows. xbwl titles In the XBWL we try to run our league like the professional wrestling leagues do. When a wrestler is trying to reach the ultimate goal of winning the World
Championship he first has to make stops along the way. In the XBWL it will be no different! We have four singles titles, the World, International, Hardcore and the North American titles. Every wrestler has to pay their dues in every league, we like this system used by the "real" wrestling promotions and we will use it also to help reduce the number of people that join our league just to try to add another singles title to their e-wrestling resume'. We want our members to be here long term because we really believe we are one of the most
FUN leagues around.
world championship This is the most prestigious title in the XBWL. It was incepted in 1997, in the beginnings of the XBWL, with Monster Mac as it's first champion. It has signified since then the best wrestler in the XBWL roster.
hardcore championship This title currently is the secondary title in the XBWL, ranking directly under the XBWL World Heavyweight Title. It was introduced on November 28, 1997, with
Scorpion as it's first champion. It signifies the man most willing to spill his blood to win a match, and the man most able to spill others blood to win matches.
international championship This is the second highest singles title, ranking right under the World title. This is the XBWL's tertiary title, ranking under the Hardcore championship. It was introduced with the XBWL World Heavyweight Championship, with "Mean" Mark Mease as it's first champion. Since then it has been held by some of the greatest wrestlers of the XBWL, and has been used as a stepping stone to the XBWL World Heavyweight Championship.
north american championship The XBWL North American Championship is the lowest title on the totem pole. It was introduced on September 29, 1998, when Chang Takata defeated Incinerator to claim the belt. It is considered a "rookie" title, because this is the first title that any wrestler shoots for before making it to the big time.
unified tag team championships This is the pinnacle of the tag team wrestling world. It is comprised of four different tag team championships from 6 different federations, the XBWL World and International Tag Team Titles, the WWFW Tag Team titles, and the UWL Tag
Team Titles, all now on display at the XBWL Arena, and is awarded to the best tag team in the XBWL. The new belts were awarded to Destruction, the winners of a 14 team tournament to determine the champions after the belts were vacated.
rules *UPDATED* All titles must be defended at least once a month. Failure to do so will result in the stripping of a title. NO EXCEPTIONS.
No new wrestler may attain any title without first winning the XBWL North
American Title. No wrestler may challenge for the XBWL World Heavyweight Title without first holding the XBWL Hardcore Championship, or holding the XBWL International Title and maintaining a number one (1) contenders ranking for a minimum of one (1)
month. No tag team may challenge for the XBWL Unified Tag Team Titles without first competing in a minimum of three (3) tag team matches in the span of one (1)
month.
