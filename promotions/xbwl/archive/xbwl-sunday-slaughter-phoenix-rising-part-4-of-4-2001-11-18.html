XBWL Sunday Slaughter: Phoenix Rising (Part 4 of 4)

18 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera's come back on and they show a set of double doors. Suddenly they open and Timmy Princeton walks in. He has a few traces of blood on his tights.
As he does a bloody man suddenly crawls in.. It can be recoginized as Slezy D. Princeton looks back for a second.) Timmy Princeton: I told you! Your FINISHED! (Princeton then runs and smashes his boot into Slezy D's face, sending him flying back into the door. Slezy D collapses on the floor and Timmy Princeton continues to walk. He is walking and suddenly he see's a camera showing a replay of what just happened in the ring and his eyes go wide and he rushes towards the ringside area.)
WD: Well, It looks like Timmy Princeton is going to find out what happened to
Arezzo's ankle. Maybe we'll get an update.
MW: Yes! I'm really upset with this Jon Talon. What kind of man does he think he is? WD: A blackheart MW: WHAT? WD: He's a blackheart. MW: Figures.. Why the hell do the fans cheer him and why does he hurt people like that?? WD: Ask him yourself, I'm not going to! MW: Hmm.. Moving on. It's time for the MAIN EVENT!! WD: Nice change of subject. [Camera cuts to backstage just prior to the Four Corners, Flaming Table, North
American title match. SuperCat, El Camero and El Dorito are all gathered.
Actually, Camero is sitting on the couch watching 'Cheech & Chong Up In Smoke',
El Dorito is eating.... Doritos....and SuperCat is reading a newspaper. When they see the camera, they all jump up and huddle, as if they've been planning all along)
SuperCat - Ay caramba! My match is coming up, with cuatro hombres, mesas del fuego!! How do you say in Americana... YOWZAS!!!
El Camero - No need to worry, mi amigo. Me and Dorito, we escort you. We help you win if needed. Tu tiene mucha fuerte!!
SuperCat - I have much strength? Wait, I think to properly state that in
Spanish you would have to say...
(El Dorito elbows SuperCat in the ribs) SuperCat - UMPH!! El Dorito - Both of 'ya, shaddap a' you faces!! You paisans, you dagos, you... Oops! Hombres, we must pull the win out of this match for SuperCat, it is YOUR turn to shine. Make Mexico proud, mi nino!
SuperCat - Si, senor! I shall! And when I do... Only one more thing will be said... (All three men turn to the camera with a blank stare) SuperCat - MEXICO ES MUY BIEN!!!! (The camera cuts back to Doc and Wilkins) WD: Well, Supercat's ready. MW: Good. I hope the rest are ready too. I'm not sure if I'm a big fan of these guys. They did pull off a win on Princeton earlier.
WD: Yeah, well.. That wasn't really thier fault. MW: HAHA WD: Moving on.. It's time for the MAIN EVENT! It will be a Four Corners,
Elimination, Flaming Tables Match. With the winner recieving the XBWL North
American Title!!!
(As he says that the XBWL-Tron bursts into action. It shows a picture of the
XBWL North American Title and you hear a voice-over.)
The XBWL North American Title. First introduced into the XBWL on August 9th,
1998. The first champion back then was Chang Takata.
(Chang Takata is shown winning the belt and holding it above his head. Then several superstars are shown, most of them are now XBWL Veterans, but they are shown young and new to the scene.)
Many XBWL superstars made thier careers in the XBWL North American Title scene. Most of them moved on while some never made it any further. (Many different past champions are shown in a rapid slide-show. Noticable faces are Jack Diamond, Brandon Thornton, and Matt Austin.)
This prestigious title will now go to one of Four men. (Taylor ThreaT, Deadly Domination, SuperCat and Tseng are shown.) Will it make or break them?? (The picture shatters and falls away to show "XBWL North American Title Match" and then fades away.) MW: Nice promo there. WD: Alot of history, We'll just have to see if they can live up to it. MW: Yeah. WD: Ladies and gentlemen, making his way to the ring escorted by El Camero and
El Dorito, two members of The Mexican Mackdaddies, weighing in at 192 pounds of
Mexican steel and sex appeal, standing at a height of 5'11", here is,
SUPERCAT!! (The lights dim while red and yellow strobes begin pulsating on the entrance ramp. "I'm Afraid Of Americans" by Trent Reznor and David Bowie begins to pump through the PA system and the crowd boos at the cheesy trio. SuperCat struts out in a pair of gold sparkly underwear with long black tights underneath, a dark orange spandex tanktop, his yellow and black cat-like mask, and a cape that reads "LAS CARAS DEL TERROR", the tag team name between he and El Camero.
He waves at the crowd even as they flip him off and curse at him, and he acts as if they love him. He rolls into the ring and blows kisses to everyone, still loving every one of them. Following his charade, he begins warming up and removes his cape.)
WD: Well.. The Mexican's are here. MW: Everyone is rejoicing now! ("Broadway" by Goo Goo Dolls plays into the arena. Deadly Domination steps out from the back, wearing black leather. He is followed by Donna the Dominatrix.
He walks down the aisle and slides into the ring. He looks at Camero and
Dorito before finally staring at SuperCat.)
MW: Hmm.. This guy is strange WD: To say the least! ("Wake Up" by Rage Against the Matchine plays and Tseng comes out. The fans immediatly begin to cheer Tseng as he motivates them and runs to the ring. He slides in and stares at his opponents. He backs up as Deadly Domination gets near him and looks at him with a smile. Deadly Domination backs up and flashes a smile of his own. SuperCat just watches and blows them each a kiss)
WD: Tseng looks like a powerhouse. MW: The fans seem to like him too. WD: Well, It's time for the man that everyone has high hopes for. The one who's the odds on favorite to win tonights match.
("Days of the Phoenix" by AFI plays and Taylor ThreaT steps out into the XBWL arena. The fans immediatly begin to boo him, knowing his previous accomplishments in other federations. He simply ignores them. Never losing the focus of his opponent. He walks, slowly and climbes to the ring. He stares at each of them before stepping through the ropes and moving to his corner. SuperCat points at him and laughs and he focuses on SuperCat.)
WD: Well, Dorito and Camero just lit a table outside and the match hasn't even started. They must have some facination with fire.
MW: They appear to be worshipping it! WD: The bell rings now and Taylor ThreaT immediatly goes after SuperCat. Seeing this, Domination and Tseng lock up. MW: Taylor ThreaT just knocked SuperCat to the outside. WD: SuperCat now rolls on the outside, pulling himself to a standing position as ThreaT drops to the floor. SuperCat quickly slams him with a low blow and rakes his eyes!! SuperCat screams like a girl as ThreaT swings wildly and hits him. SuperCat now moves. What the hell?
MW: Dorito and Camero are standing there. WD: OH GOD! Dorito and Camero just dropped Taylor ThreaT through that table with a 3/4 Neckbreaker! Dear God! Taylor ThreaT is eliminated!!
MW: Well, it took three men to do it, but Taylor ThreaT has been eliminated from this match..
WD: SuperCat now rolls back into the ring where Domination is in a headlock from Tseng. Tseng fires Domination to the ropes and catches him with a belly to belly, Domination hits the mat hard and doesn't move. Tseng turns only to be booted in the gut by SuperCat and now SuperCat with an elbow to the back of the head. Tseng is down.
MW: SuperCat is a strange character, but he can get it done if he has to. WD: SuperCat now locking in a sleeper. He's holding it tight, but Domination is back up. Domination breaks the hold! SuperCat is up but Domination quickly slams him with a DDT. Domination now drops an elbow on Tseng and rolls out of the ring. Domination setting up a table with the help of Donna the Dominatrix.
MW: Why do they keep setting up more tables. There's like 8 tables out here already set up with lighter fluid and matches on them already.
WD: Maybe they don't like where they are. MW: They can MOVE them!! HELLO! WD: Don't ask me. Donna now covers it in lighter fluid as Tseng is pulled out of the ring by Domination. Domination sets up Tseng for a Powerbomb. NO!
Tseng blocks it and headbutts Domination. Domination staggers and Tseng with a massive Mafia kick, sending him sprawling. Donna now rakes Tseng's eyes!
Wait! SuperCat is on the top rope!! MW: What is he going to do? This table is an inferno! WD: SuperCat now, cross body from the top! Wait! Tseng just pulled Donna in front of him and rolled away! OH GOD!! Supercat just put Donna the Dominatrix through a table! Deadly Domination won't be happy with that!!
MW: Man, These people are causing some havok and breaking some furniture!! WD: Deadly Domination stands up as Tseng and SuperCat roll into the ring.. SuperCat and Tseng lock-up on the inside as Deadly Domination tends to his valet on the outside. Inside the ring Tseng sends SuperCat to the ropes and delivers an arm drag. SuperCat back to his feet and they lock up again.
MW: Deadly Domination is pulling her over to a chair and he's not taking his eyes off the action in the ring!
WD: SuperCat now with a hammerlock. Reversal by Tseng. Elbow to the face by
SuperCat. Tseng is reeling and SuperCat with a northern lights suplex. He doesn't hold it and he's going to the top.
MW: Risky move here in a match like this! WD: OH! Deadly Domination just caused him to lose his balance. SuperCat comes crashing hard on his groin and Deadly Domination with a dragon stiener into the center of the ring. Tseng with a body splash on SuperCat and they're both now stomping on SuperCat!
MW: I think SuperCat may have pissed off the wrong couple of superstars. WD: SuperCat trying to get out of the ring as they both Continue to assault him. Tseng now pulls SuperCat to his feet and holds him while Deadly
Domination punches him. Tseng now drops SuperCat to his knees and Deadly
Domination drops a leg across the back of his skull!
MW: What a load of pain!! WD: Tseng now slaps in the STF and SuperCat grabs the ropes! MW: NO USE! This match isn't using that rule! WD: SuperCat screams as Tseng refuses to let go of the STF. SuperCat tapping the mat wildly, but no use. There's no submission in this match!
MW: He's in alot of pain and Tseng won't let go! WD: Wait! Camero just got in the ring! MW: El Perro Fuerte!!!!!! WD: El Camero just snapped Deadly Domination in half with El Perror Fuerte! Tseng still thinks Domination is standing there. And Camero jerks him off of
SuperCat! SuperCat laying motionless as Camero sends Tseng to the ropes and catches him with a Hurankarana!
MW: I guess they're gonna cheat to get this belt. They're not dumb people! WD: El Camero now tosses Deadly Domination to the outside!! Dorito lights another table on fire and El Camero lifts SuperCat in the air. El Camero hip tosses SuperCat over the top rope and he hits Deadly Domination! Deadly
Domination falls through the Flaming Table!! MW: Deadly Domination has been eliminated!!! WD: El Dorito now slides a table into the ring and rolls SuperCat in. Tseng is just now standing up.. El Camero with El Perro Fuerte on Tseng!! El Dorito lights the table as El Dorito sets Tseng up. SuperCat now getting to his feet.
He see's what's going on and he pushes Tseng! El Dorito lets Tseng go and he crashes through the table! OH GOD! SuperCat has cheated his way to the North
American Title!!!
MW: I think I REALLY like these guys! Tseng eliminated and SuperCat is the new North American Champion!!!
WD: The first man to win a title in the new XBWL! MW: Amazing! WD: We've got a commercial and then we've gotta wrap it up!! (Commercials) XBWL Sunday Slaughter: Phoenix Rising. Available everywhere next week on
Video Cassette as well as a special DVD feature with more blood and gore than the actual show!! Featuring an XBWL World Title History!
Mello Yello - Man, I need a Surge. McDonalds - We Love to See You Smile, so we're giving Oral Sex with every Happy
Meal!! (End Commercial) WD: Welcome back fans! It's been an exciting night. More action than imaginable with God only knowing what next week will bring!
MW: More Blood! More Gore! WD: For Michael Wilkins, this is the Wrestling Doctor.. Signing off. (The camera fades to show Brandon Thornton walking out of the XBWL arena, his roller-bag in tow. As he steps out the door of the arena we see Jack Diamond step from the side of the arena with a bat. He slams it into Thornton's ankle and Thornton hits the ground holding his ankle)
Jack Diamond: Bitch. (Jack Diamond drops the bat and walks into the arena as the show fades to black)
