This is a league unlike any other. We are committed to making this the most pleasurable
e-wrestling experience that you have ever had. We have what we consider to be an unbeatable combination of dedicated staff and members. The XBWL has been in existance since January 1996 when we opened our doors as the USWL. We changed our name after about six months when our league went through a transitional period. There are many members still in the league that were here when we started on day one! Considering that we have kept those members and stayed open through many difficult times, we figure we must be doing something right! We know we won't be the right leaguefor everyone, but if you are the type of person that enjoys e-wrestling for what it really is, a hobby that was meant to be "FUN" then the XBWL may be the place for you. All we ask is that you take a few minutes to read all the way through this handbook before you make a decision on whether to join or not. We hope you like what read and we hope to see you in the XBWL!
II. league explanation The XBWL is an original league, we go the extra mile to try and make our league different from the other ones out there. The XBWL is
"primarily" a BOOKED league. We do use a simulator for three things, those three things are for occasional card commentary, to simulate our special battle royal card and to simulate an "OCCASIONAL" match. Generally we will pick the winner of the match by who out roleplayed their opponent, however if both people roleplayed equally then we will use the simulator to decide the winner. (A further explanation about how we use the simulator for our special battle royal card that we hold is in the Wednesday card section.) If you want to win your matches you HAVE to roleplay! A simulator will
NOT help
you win a match in this league except for the battle royal card. The
XBWL is
also unique in several other ways like we will use a specialized
RANKINGS SYSTEM so that rookies aren't held down simply because they are new to the league. With our rankings system you will know exactly what you need to do to get a title shot and thus, more effectively be able to adjust your strategy to position yourself where you want to be in the league. Your future is in your own hands, there is no one in this league that will hold you down. If you roleplay and win your matches the gold belts are there for the taking!
A. rankings system In many leagues rookies are at a disadvantage because the veteran members have a jump on in them in the amount of matches they have had and are further up in the rankings. That sort of a jump makes it hard for rookies to catch up and get the big matches they want. Our system eliminates that problem for the rookies without punishing the veterans! The XBWL bases its rankings on every wrestlers "last 10 matches" only. This is the best system because it bases everything on what has happened recently. This rankings system affects both the veterans and the rookies equally. The veterans past is not taken into consideration so as not to harm the rookies chances of being ranked fairly high quickly if they come in and do well right off the bat by winning a lot of their matches. It also does protect the veterans because rookies have to pay thier dues and win the lower ranked titles before they can challenge for the higher ranked titles. Furthermore, it's possible to turn around your XBWL career if you struggle, as only the last
10 matches are counted, so old loses can be replaced by new wins (and vice versa, if you don't keep up your performance.)
B. are we 'hardcore'? In the past, the xbwl has featured some matches that have been called
'hardcore' by ourselves and others. However, as time has gone by, we're finding that some of these matches take away from the realistic enivorment we like to work with.
At the same time, we realize that many of our current members, and possibly future ones, do enjoy particpating in hardcore matches, and would be upset if we totally removed them from this league.
In an effort to make a comprimise, we've made the following decisions: hardcore wrestlers/matches will still happen in the XBWL (within reason), in matches surronding the Hardcore Title, and when angles and fueds need or deserve it.
However, in other parts of the card, we're going encourage more 'traditional' matches. And we may disallow gimmick matches strictly for the fact of doing a weird gimmick.
To summarize, people will still bleed, go through tables, and other wrestling standards will continue to exist in the XBWL, as will normal hardcore matches exisit in relevant matches. At the same time, we reserve the right ot refust to allow hardcore matches in situations where we don't think it's needed or deserved, or to reject a gimmick match if we find it to be too unrealistic.
III. xbwl events The XBWL has three events every week. We have three events because we believe that if we keep people involved in a match of some sort that it will not only help keep people interest in our league but it will also give them even more to roleplay about. We generally also "try" to make sure everyone gets at least one match a week if they roleplay.
A. tuesday Tuesdays card is a basic card with between 5 and 8 matches.This card is not fully written out, instead we give you a basic summary of that happened the match hitting the high points. The main event will usually be written, and other matches may be written, if card writers or handlers are intersted.
B. thursday Thursday's card is a "specialty card" meaning it's our Battle Royal card. We will have this card every week and the winner will recieve a title shot for the title they are eligible for at that time. There may be 1 or 2 occasional singles matches on this card but it will generally focus on the battle royal.
XBWL Title Points can be earned by your performance in the Battle
Royale. The points correspond to your finish. (Since there are no double eliminations in TNM currently, there are no ties.)
- 1st Place - 15 points - 2nd Place - 10 points - 3rd Place - 7 points - 4th Place - 5 points - 5th Place - 3 points - 6 through 10 - 1 point. Those points can be accumlated by a wrestler (but not traded or otherwise exchanged between wrestlers.) They can be exchanged for Title Shots...
You can redeem 20 points for a shot at the highest title YOU ARE ELGIABLE FOR, except the World Title. You can redeem 40 points for a shot at the world title, if you are elgiable.
C. saturday Saturday's card is another general card containing 5 to 8 matches in summary form focusing on the highlights of the match. As with the Monday card, the main event will usually be written, and other matches may be written depending on if handlers and card writers are intersted.
IV. xbwl ppv's The XBWL will have 4 MAJOR pay-per-view's a year. The other 8 big events will be smaller type pay-per-view's similiar to the old "Saturday
Night Main
Event" that the WWF used to hold, or the old "Clash of Champions" held by the WCW. On the big ppv's every wrestler will have a match, on the smaller ppv's there will only be around 7 to 9 matches total. Pay-per-views will
ALWAYS have full commentary and on the weeks that thos ppv's come out the Saturday card will not happen so we can concentrate on making the ppv as good as possible. V. xbwl titles In the XBWL we try to run our league like the professional wrestling leagues do. When a wrestler is trying to reach the ultimate goal of winning the
World Championship he first has to make stops along the way. In the
WWF
before a wrestler becomes the World Champion he has to hold the
European and Intercontinental titles. In the WCW the wrestler generally has to hold the T.V. title, U.S. title and then the World Title. In the XBWL it will be no different! We have four singles titles, the World, International,
Hardcore
and the North American titles. The North American title is the lowest and you MUST hold that title BEFORE you can hold any other title. You must hold the International or Hardcore Title title before you hold the World title.
Every wrestler has to pay their dues in every league, we like this system used by the "real" wrestling promotions and we will use it also to help reduce the number of people that join our league just to try to add another singles title to their e-wrestling resume'. We want our members to be here long term because we really believe we are one of the most FUN leagues around.
A. world championship This is the most prestigious title in the XBWL. B. international championship This is the second highest singles title, ranking right under the
World
title. C. hardcore championship This title currnetly has the same ranking as of the International
Title, but
may have it's standing reduced in the future, as a lesser title than the
International Title. It is higher the the North American Championship. D. north american championship This is the lowest ranked singles title, it's the title all new members shoot for when they enter the league before advancing to the next title.
E. world tag team championships This is the only tag-team title the XBWL has right now and probably will have anywhere in the future.
