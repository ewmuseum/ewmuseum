XBWL Recruiting New Members

6 March 2000

Hello, lurkers, regulars, and newcomers to RSPWF. This is your resident "guy who posts sometimes but no one really cares about" [name withheld], and the time has come, oh yes, the time has come for me to promote my fledgling little promotion. Well, it's not mine really, because it's committee run, but I'm on the executive committee, and one of my jobs is to promote the league on RSPWF, so here I go. I represent the X-tremely Brutal Wrestling
League (XBWL) and before you shoot me down just for the name, read a few things about our league.
-0- We use E-groups ( www.egroups.com ) to manage our mailing list. This allows you to choose how you like to get your flashes: Each one as they are written, Once-a-day 'flash packet', or just going to the site and using it like a RP board.
-0- We have been running for over three years now, and have survived some pretty big crises. We're not going anywhere. Along with that, we made the name back with names like this were cool, and we're still cool! Hoo-ha!
-0- There are chances at every level, as long as you are willing to work for it. -0- Probably the most important, NO ONE PERSON CONTROLS WHO WINS OR LOSES. All cards are decided by committe vote based on roleplays per week. Also, you don't have to roleplay a lot to win. The general rule is that if you roleplay 2 times and your opponant roleplays 3, he won't automatically win.
Rather, his best 2 RP's will be put up against your 2 for vote. Well, that's about it about my league. If you'd like more information, or a copy of our handbook, feel free to e-mail me or to visit our website. Thank you and good day!
