Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera cuts to somewhere in the back of the Arena. A light-bulb swings slowly from side to side, giving off a dim glow inside the small closet. A large physical creature steps into partial view, still hidden by the shadows, yet too familiar to be truly safe from prying eyes. His hulking figure is barely contained by the tiny room, yet the figure moves freely as if out in the open. Fans at ring-side brighten up as the Big Screen clicks on, and his voice booms out over the PA and they soon let loose their hatred.)
Voice: You wanted brutal? Yet, you've never really seen it...you wanted extreme but you never really felt it..YOU WANTED ME...and now you refuse to accept...
(Broken steps closer to the camera. His disfigured facial features hiding beneath a tattered gruesome mask, yet magnified a hundred times upon XBWL
Television. A wave of boos fills the Arena. He continues slowly...)
Broken: Unwanted, perhaps...fearsome, most definitely...but most of all permanent, that's what I am. The XBWL in the past showed itself to be long-living, and with that knowledge in mind I have tagged along for the long run. To spread the...GOOD WORD...to share my story with a new crowd and to struggle in this new element.
I could tell you a lot of stories...about a lot of different times, but none could compare to the stories yet to come, the times yet unhad. I have sat patiently, even quietly and watched those who would oppose me play games. I have watched and barely put my hand into the pool, always observing, always considering...waiting for the time when WAITING...would no longer be an option.
Unneeded...that's what the fans have shown me that I am. I'm a guest staying far past his welcome...after everything I've done, after everything I've gave
THEM...they push me aside. They're done with me, and I'm done trying to please, trying to earn respect where none was offered. They cheered for Bobby, they chanted his name, and yet the hypocrites have the gaul to insult me...to judge me after everything I've been through. They can't even see that everything that happened to Bobby was nothing compared to the pain that I went through as I struggled to escape, as I lay in limbo waiting for the time my day would come.
So it came to me quite reasonably, that the only way to show them the truth was to let them see it from my own eyes...to make them that much more like me...to connect. They hate, I feed...they call for my defeat and I grow STRONGER at the mere denial of their lust...
No...the XBWL doesn't need me to survive, but I knew that from the beginning. The real question is whether or not they can survive what I'm going to do to them. (The light-bulb sizzles for a moment before going out all together leaving only an after-glow behind. Covered by the darkness Broken speaks a final time.)
Broken: I can live with the truth...I can live knowing that the fans who once cheered me now look upon me with hate, but all the hate in the world isn't going to change the fact that when I come out tonight my opponents aren't going to know what hit them as I wrap their faces around a steel chair and bring them one step closer to perfection. The people in the stands may hate me, but their hatred only brings them that much closer to becoming ultimately..like me. To become like me, one must accept that hate is the only emotion that can ever result in a worthwhile outcome, that hate is the source of strength. That to be whole, one must first be broken...
That the one clear inevitability in life is that.. IN THE END... (pause) ...WE ARE ALL... (pause) ...BROKEN... (Fade to black.)
