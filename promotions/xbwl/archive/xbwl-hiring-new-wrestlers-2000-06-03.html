Currently the X-Tremely Brutal Wrestling Federation (XBWL) is hiring new wrestlers. We only accept the elite wrestlers on the NET and in our Four 1/2
Years of Existence we have had some of the greatest wrestlers ever to grace our rings. Come become part of what is called the E-Wrestling Revolution!
Participate in a Fed ran completely without the Owner-ship of Kids. Come join with adults and late teens who really want to bring the Roleplaying to
E-Wrestling! Come where the wrestlers are Original and are completely born and bred Roleplayers. Come set the standard in E-Wrestling in the 4 year steady E-Wrestling Federation XBWL. Your Roleplays will COUNT and they will mean something every time you write them. All roleplays are judged on the Quality and never the Quantity. How hard is it to put out 200 Lines that don't mean anything? How many times have you lost because your roleplays didn't meet the
Line Quota? Come to the XBWL where we have a 5 Person Committee who judges the
Roleplays and votes on the winners of each wrestling card! Join the true revolution of E-Wrestling.
