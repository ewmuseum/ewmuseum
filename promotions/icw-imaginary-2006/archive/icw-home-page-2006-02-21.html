Imaginary Championship Wrestling A New Generation Of Fantasy Wrestling Welcome to Imaginary Championship Wrestling, the brightest fantasy wrestling star on the horizon. Guide your wrestler from his rookie debut to his retirement match, through years of feuds, titles, stables and more! This wrestling is imaginary, but the prizes are real! You could win a real wrestling championship belt, custom action figures of your wrestlers, or even cash! Will your wrestler become an ICW legend and enter the Hall of Fame? Join and see, for years of fun! See you in the ring! Read about the crazy things we will do to make you love ICW! The scroll box is easy to operate, just place your arrow on the arrow. You can read about the exciting happenings and promote your wrestler, on the ICW Message Board. Be sure to read our handbook, which can be viewed in full by clicking the link. The handbook is designed to teach you all the different aspects of the ICW which combine to make it so enjoyable. Read it thoroughly before you begin playing. GET IN ON THIS NEVER TO BE REAPEATED SERIES OF EVENTS AGAIN! Sign up to join Imaginary Championship Wrestling by April 2006 and be part of a series of tournaments that will shape ICW for years to come. As with any upstart wrestling organization, ICW will open up with virtually no champions. Your won-loss record building up to these events will determine your seeding in the tournaments to decide the first Promotion, Tag-Team, Six-Man, Region, United States, World, World Tag-Team and World Six-Man Champions. These tournaments will lead off with a tournament to decide the very first Promotion Champion. The runners-up will be awarded State Championships according to their promotion's area. These tournament matches will play off automatically. However, there will be a $2 fee per wrestler into each tournament. Again, these tournaments may never take place again to these extremities. You have the chance to become the first of many champions to mark the beginning of a game that will be played worldwide for years to come. You have a chance to do what nobody else will ever be able to do. Become the first. But wait! There's more! Your wrestlers can get into these tournaments for half the fee! That's right! $1 per wrestler! How? Sign your friends up for Imaginary Championship Wrestling and your wrestlers will be allowed to enter these tournaments for $1 each! If your friends get in by April 2006, they too will be allowed to enter the Promotion, Tag-Team, Six-Man, Region and United States Championship Tournaments for $1 per wrestler! Send your application and fees in, today! Let the fun begin!
