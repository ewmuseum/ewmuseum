A New Generation of Fantasy Wrestling Check Your Mailboxes, ICW Could Be There Now! ICW announces its upcoming pay-per-view, ICW Revolution. It will read out as a 4 day event hosted in 4 cities. Taking place will be a tournament to crown the new Women's Champion, a Light Heavyweight tournament, a Tag-Team Tournament in which the winner(s) will receive an engraved trophy, a 50/50 Singles Tournament where the winner will receive 50% of the money brought in for the tournament, probably around 20 matches, a $100,000 Battle Royal where the winner will also receive 5 free matches and a host of interviews. There will also be a unique contest where teams of 4 will compete against each other in Survivor Series matches. The survivors will face off in tag-team or 6-man matches in the 2nd round. The final round will be settled in a 2 out of 3 falls singles tournament to decide a final winner. The winner will receive 30% of the money brought in for this particular tournament, along with $100,000 and 10 free matches. There is a $2 fee for the Tag-Team Tournament. There is a $1 fee for the Battle Royal. There is a $4 fee for the 50/50 Tournament. The final tournament has a $5 entry fee. If you want to be in all four contests, the discounted fee is just $10. The Women's Championship Tournament and the Light Heavyweight Tournament have no fees and are open only to those wrestlers that are listed in those classes. Auction Blocks have also been announced and the results will be shown at the pay-per-view. The deadline is set for June 1st but is subject to change if an extension is needed.
