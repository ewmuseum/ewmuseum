Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Imaginary Championship Wrestling, ICW on its own pages, billed itself as “A New Generation Of Fantasy Wrestling”: a game in which a player guided a wrestler from his rookie debut to his retirement match through years of feuds, titles and stables, with the chance to win a real wrestling championship belt, custom action figures or cash, and to enter the Hall of Fame. Its page of February 2006 pointed to a members-only area, a handbook and a community board, asked players to sign a wrestler up by e-mail, and said the game would make its debut toward the end of February 2006.

Players who signed up by April 2006 were promised a series of tournaments to name the first Promotion, Tag-Team, Six-Man, Region, United States, World, World Tag-Team and World Six-Man champions, seeded by won-loss record, at $2 a wrestler for each tournament or $1 for a player who had signed friends up. By August 2008 the home page announced the pay-per-view ICW Revolution, a four-day event in four cities with tournaments for a new Women’s Champion, a Light Heavyweight title and tag teams, a 50/50 singles tournament that paid its winner half the money taken in, a $100,000 battle royal, and a contest of four-person Survivor Series teams that ended in a two-out-of-three-falls final. Fees ran from $1 to $5, or $10 for all four contests, with a deadline of June 1st that was subject to change. The page called ICW the work of former fantasy wrestling managers, for both the wrestling fan and the role-playing fan.
