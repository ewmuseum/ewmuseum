Powerhouse Wrestling Rules 1) Wrestlers cannot be real wrestlers. It makes things more interesting when wrestlers are made up. No real wrestlers are excepted because of personalities would be to predictable. Wrestlers can be a mix between real wrestlers since that that wrestler's personality wouldn't be known and be able to be copied. Example: Bret Michaels (a mix between Shawn Michaels and Bret Hart), some of Hart's personality would show through and some of Michaels' (that would be strange, huh?) Each person can have a limit of 2 wrestlers, this may change. 2) Even though PHW uses TNM, you will have to send in flashes (interviews) at least once a week. If for some reason you cannot send in flashes, e-mail any official from PHW. Role-playing is very important, if you miss too many flashes your wrestler will become a jobber for a while. The more you miss the worse off you'll be (jobbing, stripped of title(s), suspension, fired). 3) No excessive swearing in flashes or when talking to other wrestlers in Powerhouse chatrooms. Think of it like this, if you can say it on TV (not HBO or something) it'll go here. Flashes are edited before posted, so just make it easy on the staff. 4) Currently there are 3 titles, World Powerhouse Title, Semi-Powerhouse Title, Tagteam Powerhouses Titles. When you have a title it must be defended at least once a month and you will be automatically placed in a match. 5) There will be 2 cards a week, Tuesday Night Crush and The War Zone on Saturday (cards may not always be on time, there will be message sent if not). After you read the card on the web site, you can go to Powerhouse Chat via PowWow. From there interviews, challenges, threats, or anything else can be done in real time. PPV events will be held once a month, a minor PPV then a major PPV. Like WWF has In Your House. 6) To interfer in a match (or just having your wrestler(s) come to ringside) e-mail the Pres. 7) Have fun! It's a game and don't take anything personally. If you have any questions about the rules e-mail the Pres. or page the Pres on powwow.
