Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Powerhouse Wrestling HQ survives as a working rules-and-application skeleton rather than a running record: its own home page announces "POWERHOUSE IS RE-OPENING!" over a scrolling banner reading "FEEL THE POWER OF THE POWERHOUSE WRESTLING LEAGUE, FANTASY WRESTLING AT IT'S BEST," and its own rules page describes a promotion built to run on a booking engine it calls "TNM," weekly written promos ("flashes"), a PowWow chatroom for live interaction, three titles (World Powerhouse, Semi-Powerhouse, Tagteam Powerhouses), and two weekly cards, Tuesday Night Crush and Saturday's The War Zone, with a minor-then-major pay-per-view once a month. Its own copyright line credits "Powersurge Productions," dated 1997.

No card, champion, or named wrestler survives anywhere in the held record — the rules and a blank wrestler-creation application are the whole of what this holding preserves. Its own president is referred to throughout only by role ("the Pres."), never by name.
