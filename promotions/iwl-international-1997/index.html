Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The International Wrestling League opened on 6 March 1996 at Agricultural Hall in Allentown, Pennsylvania, with an eight-man World Title tournament. Ring announcer Sensational Sherri Martel welcomed the crowd to “the first ever International Wrestling League card” before Brian Pillman crashed the ceremony to urinate in the ring and declare the promotion under new ownership by “the infamous NAGAMACHI corporation,” fronted by himself as “the loose cannon” with Jim Cornette as its spokesman.

Its Second Annual Sabby Awards, 15 December 1997, sent broadcaster Gene Okerlund on a chaotic bus ride toward Radio City Music Hall — sharing a seat with Mike Tenay and, briefly, a startled Tupac Shakur — before knocking on the actual Sabby family's own front door by mistake.

Its record kept extending well past that point. A recruitment post four years later, 17 September 1999, still advertised open slots against “OUR top guns” and claimed close to four years of continuous running from a new address. Separate recruitment posts from its own president in August and September 1998 name Sean Waltman as its reigning World Champion by that point, a title change beyond anything reproduced here. No closure notice survives.
