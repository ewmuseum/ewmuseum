IWL NEWSLETTER # 1

30 March 1996 (reposting cards of 6-7 March 1996)

IWL NEWSLETTER # 1 INTERNATIONAL WRESTLING LEAGUE "Jump in on the Action" INTERVIEWS RODDY PIPER "Wwwwwwwell, It seems like ol' R. P.'s come face to face with an ol' buddy a mine, Ricky Flair. They call ya the Nature Boy, huh? Pal, I can guarantee that there ain't a thing livin' in the natural world like ya, and don't ya take it the wrong way, ol' friend. Them vultures and bloodsucking leeches ain't got nuthin' on you. Heh. You're runnin' around like a dog in heat, ya know, 'wooooooo', 'wooooooo', kinda like yer matin' call, there. What are ya know, 70? Pretty soon you're gonna be attractin' flies, not women. Heh. Well, if women's what ya call 'em. And ya know, I'd hates to be the wife sittin' at home all alone while little Ricky goes out to play at night. What does she have to say 'bout all those mule-faced missys you got on yer arm after the matches are through, huh? I got me a family, kids I gotta feed, and I stay true ta them. I make an honesty days' pay and I spend it in an honesty way, Flair. Hey, I'm no rookie, an' I ain't pretendin' to be. But Ricky...I ACT MY AGE. You run around like a giddy little school kid...pullin' the pigtails of the girl in front a ya to see if she likes ya. Struttin' around like ya got ta use the toilet. Gettin' some in the back a yer limo, just like on prom night. Ya like ta talk about space mountain...ya know, all the times you've ridden it. Well, when I goes ta Disneyland it says yer supposed ta stay *off* the ride if ya got a pacemaker and are this high. (holds his hand out by his hip) Heh. You're flirtin' with death, old man, and she's a helluvalot more receptive than the women you go after. Yer about ta go on the ride a yer life, pal, and ol' Hot Rod's operatin' the roller coaster." ELIMINATORS (Kronus laughing hysterically in the background) Saturn: "WE ARE THE ELIMINATORS! And no one eliminates the competition better than us! We're being tossed aside because Public Enemy is here! No one forgets about the Eliminators! We don't care who gets in our way and who tries to hold us back! It don't matter if it's PE or the Gangstas or the officials of the IWL! They *will* be totally ELIMINATED!" IWL WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT Agricultural Hall Allentown, PA 3/6/96 ********Lights go on at Ag Hall and the crowd erupts! Sensational Sherri is in the middle of the ring with a microphone.******* SHERRI - "Welcome one and all to the first ever International Wrestling League card! *crowd cheers* I am Sensational Sherri Martel, and I hope you are ready for a huge night of action, cause that is what you are going to get!!! *crowd roars* We are here to make IWL the BEST wrestling federation out there and I think we will accomplish the task. So sit back and enjoy the..." ******Light go out in the building********* SHERRI - "What is this? Am I back on Nitro or something?? Get the lights back on!!!" *******Lights come back on and Brian Pillman is standing next to Sherri in the center of the ring******** SHERRI - "Oh no... Not YOU! Jeez....." PILLMAN <smiling> - "Hello everyone in teeeeveeee land. Hello from Eric Bischoff's, Tod Gordon's and especially the IWL bookerman's worst nightmare...You're probably all saying 'WHAT THE F**K IS HE DOING HERE?' Well, I'm here to christen this new fed, and what better way could I think of other then to p*ss in the middle of the ring!" SHERRI - "Oh my God, NO!!" ********* Pillman procedes to do just that as a little black dot covers his unmentionables********** SHERRI - "Now I know why they call you 'Pill'man." PILLMAN - "Shut up, Sherri.Another reason I'm here is money, cash, big green. Yeah, some wise Japanese men have given a nice size pile of it." <smiles even bigger> <spits into the crowd> "That's right...the infamous NAGAMACHI corporation has abandoned that defunct league they were running, and are going to steamroll through this one. <smiles again> And who better to do the job of leading the corporation then Brian Pillman, the loose cannon." ******the crowd begins to stir as the Nagamachi corporation's managerial spokesman, Jim Cornette, makes his way to the ring.****** CORNETTE <smirking> - "Brian, I don't mean to burst your bubble, but while the nagamachi corporation is going to dominate the IWL, I haven't been informed of your membership. You should stop running your mouth off and claiming to be involved in the world's most powerful organization." PILLMAN <SCREAMING LOUDLY> - "THE REASON YOU HAVEN'T BEEN INFORMED MR. CORNETTE, IS BECAUSE YOU'VE BEEN FIRED. <speaking normally> And I will right now,introduce you to my fist as a token of Nagamachi's appreciation." ***Lex Luger runs to the ring and grabs Pillman and pulls him off Cornette** CORNETTE - "You see Pillman, don't mess with me, and don't claim to be something your not. Lex, kick his a**." ******Luger turns to face Pillman, and suddenly turns around and closthlines Cornette, as GARY HART makes his way to the ring**** HART - "You see Jim Cornette, it goes like this...some very rich men paid a lot of money for you to do a job...that job was domination...which means running roughshod over the entire federation. Second place just won't do. You failed Cornette. You've been replaced. The Great Muta has been replaced by the man to my right, Mr. Pillman. So Mr. Cornette, I suggest you get out of the ring, get out of the building, and get out of this federation. And Lex, you get to the back and prepare to be crowned champion tonight." *****Gary Hart and Luger return back to the locker room. Sherri follows them. Pillman stays out and gets into an argument with a fan. Pillman spits on him. Suddenly, Hunter Hearst Helmsly comes down to ringside. Hunter grabs the microphone, looks at the ring in disgust, and walks up to Pillman ********* HUNTER - "This is not proper behavior, and I refuse to wrestle in the rings of IWL if you continue. Pillman, you are utterly disgusting! How can you be allowed to compete in the IWL with such vile habits and erratic behavior? I for one will not wrestle in the IWL as long as a man of this low moral stature is competing here!" ******Hunter turns to head back to the locker room, and Pillman punches him in the back of the head. Pillman proceeds to slap, punch, and spit on Hunter. Helmsly finally explodes in a rage and kicks Pillman in the stomach. Hunter punches and kicks at Pillman until the IWL officials come and break up the fight. They take Hunter back to the locker room. Hunter can be heard yelling, "You're history, Pillman!!!" After a few minutes, Pillman heads back to the locker room, laughing all the way...***** IWL WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT Lex Luger vs. Shawn Michaels In the opening match, Lex Luger attacked Shawn before the bell, as everyone expected. Shawn had trouble mounting an offence during the whole match. Lex mixed it up using closelines, ariplane spins, and knee lifts to wear Michaels down. Lex then brought a chair into the ring and attempted to hit Shawn. Shawn moved and started his comeback. Shawn connected on a flying forearm and a moonsault. He couldn't put Lex away though. The Heartbreak Kid made an error in judgement and Lex caught him in a huge press slam. Lex then got the easy pin on Shawn. Luger wins! Chris Benoit vs. Sid These two went at it like wild dogs! Sid used power moves such as the Double Ax Handle, while Benoit kept to the wearing down moves like arm bars and leg holds. The momentum changed in this match many times. Sid and Benoit then got into a test of strength. Sid easily won, but Benoit managed to push Sid away...right into the referee. As the referee lay motionless, Sid grabbed a chair from outside the ring. Sid smacked Benoit on the back with it. As the referee was getting up, Sid power bombed the lifeless Benoit. 1 - 2 - 3. Sid wins. Cactus Jack vs. Alex Wright This one looked to be a laugher from the outset. Cactus used every illegal tactic in the book. He raked Alex's face, he sctratched him, he rubbed his face into the ropes. Then something sparked Alex. He made a HUGE comeback, hitting Cactus with drop kicks and closelines that had Cactus reeling. Alex looked to be on the verge of victory, then Cactus tossed him out of the ring. Alex's arm was injured badly. Cactus then hit Alex with the ringside steps! Alex eventually got into the ring. He tried to fight back, but he had little luck. The referee was going to call the match, but Alex said no. Referee still wanted to call the match. Alex and ref get into a shouting match, Alex punches referee out, Cactus wins by disqualification. Roddy Piper vs. Goldust After Goldust's grand enterance, Goldust hid behind his manager Elizabeth while Piper and Sunny came to the ring. Goldust seemed scared of the Rowdy One. As the match began, Goldust kept trying to feel Piper up. Roddy did not take this kindly and began leveling Goldust with punches and kicks. Piper got Goldust up in an atomic drop and sent the Golden One flying over the top rope! The crowd goes wild. Sunny is jumping for joy! Piper got sloppy then. Goldust came back with closelines and 2 pile drivers which rocked Piper. Crowd was really into this one as they booed Goldust. Goldust continued to totally dominate the match. He even gave Piper a chicken wing! Sunny and Elizabeth GLARED are each other during the WHOLE match. Goldust gave Piper a bulldog and it looked to be over. Suddenly, Sunny jumped onto the ring apron and called Goldust over. She started to feel Goldust's chest. As Goldust was in a trance, Piper came up from behind, grabbed a hunk of Goldust's tights, and rolled him up for the 3 count. Crowd goes nuts! Lex Luger vs. Sid Sid and Lex started the match out by pounding away on each other. They spilled out of the ring and Lex whipped Sid into the ringpost. Sid was hurt badly. While ref warned Lex, Gary Hart kicked at Sid. Throughout the match, Lex wore down Sid. Lex uses backbreakers and chops to get his point across that he is the dominate force in IWL. Sid couldn't get anything to work. Finally, Lex ran at Sid with a closeline. Sid got his boot up and nailed Lex flush in the face! Luger goes down hard and Sid gets a very surprising and shocking pin! Cactus Jack vs. Roddy Piper Both wrestlers surprised the crowd by doing many amateur wrestling moves to start out this match. After they had done many takedowns, Cactus and Piper looked at each other. Piper slapped Cactus. Cactus kicked Piper in the groin and gave him the Double Arm DDT! Piper kicked out at 2 and a half! While Cactus argued with the referee, Piper came up from behind, rolled Cactus up, but only got a 2 count. Piper started to mount a comeback. He had Cactus reeling with punches, slaps, and kicks. Piper whipped Cactus into the corner. Piper missed the splash in the corner. Cactus gave another Double Arm DDT to the stunned Piper and got the pin. Cactus Jack vs. Sid In another IWL surprise, before the match, it was announced that Demi Moore would be presenting the IWL WORLD Championship Belt to the winner. Demi came out to a large ovation and cat calls. Woman gave Cactus Jack some last minute instructions, and the match started. Cactus grabbed Sid and tossed him out of the ring. Sid almost lands on Demi Moore, who flees from her ringside seat. Cactus takes it to the extreme by tossing Sid into the crowd! People scatter as Cactus hits Sid with chairs. Cactus then broke a bottle of beer over Sid's head! Sid was busted open! Both men eventually made it back into the ring. Cactus bit Sid's open cut, causing it to bleed more. After Cactus missed a closeline, Sid kicked him and pile drove him. Sid got nasty too as he punched Cactus in the groin. As he stomped on Cactus's head, he laughed and taunted Woman. Cactus rose up behind Sid and gave him a huge back suplex. Cactus tried a top rope splash, but missed horribly. As Sid hit the ropes, Woman tripped him. Cactus then landed many elbows on Sid. Cactus tried for the pin, but couldn't hold the big man down. Cactus got a chair from ringside. He ran at Sid with the chair. Sid got his boot up, nailed the chair, which railed Cactus in the head. Sid covered Cactus Jack for the pin! Sid Wins! Demi Moore enters the ring, takes the microphone and says, "The winner of this match, and NEW IWL WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION.... Sid!!!" Some people cheer, some boo. A bloody Sid looks at the belt and laughs loudly! Then a whistle blows.... The crowd groans as a man comes down the aisle blowing a whistle. It is Referee Bill Alphonzo!!!! Alphonzo climbs into the ring, grabs the microphone from Demi and says, "Do You Know Who I Am? DO YOU? I am Referee Bill Alphonzo! I am in the big leagues now, baby. What happened tonight was an injustice!!! A mistake! A big mess up by the referee!! Sid, you are NOT the IWL Champion! See that cut on your head? Yah, ya big dummie, the match should have been STOPPED because of the cut! I am demanding that this WHOLE tournament be started over.. NOW!!" Demi grabs the microphone and says, "Buddy, he won it fair and square!" Bill snatches the microphone back and says, "Listen you Hollywood floozy! Do you know who you are talking to? NO! I am Bill Alphonzo!!! You think that you are SOOOO special cause you can wear a tight dress, wear a WonderBra, and look sexy? Well, Slut, I know how you really get your acting jobs. Now shut the hell up, you bimbo! Sid, gimmie that belt now! It is NOT yours!!" Sid grabs Bill Alphonzo, picks him up, and tosses him out of the ring. Sid shakes Demi's hand and they walk back to the locker room together. Sid has on hand around Demi and one hand holding the IWL WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP BELT!! Congrats, Sid. IWL THURSDAY RAMPAGE!!!!! IWL Tag Team Tournament IWL Arena Macungie, Pa 3/7/96 *********Lights go on in the IWL Arena. The crowd cheers. Cheers quickly turn to boos after the crowd see Brian Pillman standing in the ring. He has the microphone********* PILLMAN <sporting black eye from fight with HHH> <laughing>- "People don't seem to realize something. Nobody can control me. NOBODY CAN F***ING CONTROL ME. <calmed down> Mr. Waylon (IWL President), you can't control me! Hunter Hearst Helmsley, you damn sure can't control me. Yeah you throw a stiff right hand, but you can't hurt me. <laughing again> What's the deal Mr. Waylon, are you like Sullivan and Bischoff? Do you want me to respect you? Is that why you send people to hurt me?" ******Esteemed IWL president Mr. Waylon walks to the ring***** MR WAYLON - "Pillman, I have warned you for the last time, THAT'S IT." PILLMAN <frowning> - "Oh what, are you going to fire me in front of all the MARKS? Is that what you are gonna do? I'll tell you what...I'll score you some ratings points mr. president. I'll teach HHH about respect, and teach you some respect at the same time! How about a strap match...me and HHH next week...another respect match...how about it????" <laughs> MR WAYLON - "If that's what you want." PILLMAN - "C'mon bookerman, do you think that i'm stupid? You love the idea of someone beating some sense into me with a strap don't you...Well, bring it on...Like I said, NOBODY CONTROLS BRIAN PILLMAN!" <sees Gary Hart walking towards ring> PILLMAN - "Except this gentleman right here." <calms down> HART - "Brian, I'm gonna let you go through with this match because it will do you some good. Now that I have the president of the IWL in the ring, I have one more proposal. Last night, Lex Luger proved to the entire world that HE is the most powerful force in wrestling. Sid was lucky, he was beat within an inch of his life by my man...and then he left with Demi Moore. well, I have a proposal for Sid and his handlers. The wealthy men I work for have written this check, endorsed by the Nagamachi Corporation, for $1 million dollars. I am going to issue a Million Dollar Challenge to Sid right now. If you can beat Luger in a cage match next week, you get one million dollars Sid. If you can't, Lex is the new champion. That's all I have to say." MR WAYLON - <sounding very excited> "Well, I guess it's Indecent Proposal 2! Next week! Oh Pillman, because of your actions last night, I have signed a very interesting match for you next Wednesday. Hope you like it. <Pillman snears> FANS GET READY FOR THE TAG TOURNAMENT!" Tag Team Tournament Gangstas vs. Rob Van Dam/Tony Williams Gangstas started out the match by using roughhouse tactics. Many punches, kicks, and eye racks were used. Williams tried to fight back against New Jack, but had no luck. Williams finally tagged Rob Vam Dam, who stunned New Jack and the crowd with a flurry of high risk and amazing moves. Van Dam missed a cross body block and New Jack tagged Mustafa. Mustafa started to beat Vam Dam senseless! Mustafa connected on a big powerslam, and tried for a pin. Vam Dam kicked out and mustered up enough strength to tag his partner. Williams went nuts. Tony confused the big man with body slams, take downs, and finally hit a big closeline in which he pinned Mustafa. Van Dam/Williams advance. Ric Flair/Ricky Steamboat vs Raven/Tommy Dreamer Brawling overcame skill in this match. Raven and Dreamer right off the bat hit Flair and Steamboat with every illegal move in the book. Tommy poked his thumb in Steamboat's eye more than 4 times during the match. Flair was off of his game tonight. Raven punched at him with no problem at all. Raven and Tommy looked like a well oiled team while Flair and Steamboat looked like a rusty old car. Tommy DDT's Steamboat for the win. Eliminators vs. Public Enemy As the Eliminators made their way to the ring, "Natural Born Killaz" began playing in the arena, and the Gangstas came out and attacked the Eliminators with baseball bats. Eliminators were caught off guard and were beaten up very badly. Blood was everywhere in the aisle. Public Enemy simply dragged Saturn of the Eliminators into the ring, gave him the drive-by and pinned him. Public Enemy wins. I smell a feud coming.... Owen Hart/British Bulldog vs. Shawn Michaels/Mr. Perfect Shawn stayed was not involved a lot in the start of this match, probably due to the fact he wrestled last night. Perfect started out in stunning fashion. He hit Owen and Davey with everything except the kitchen sink! Perfect even stole a page out of Hulk's book and gave Owen a leg drop. Owen fought back and put the Sharpshooter on Perfect. Perfect broke the hold. Bulldog came in and gave Perfect a thunderous suplex. Somehow, Perfect tagged in Shawn. Bulldog tossed Shawn out of the ring. Owen then proceeded to smack Shawn over the head with a steel chair! Owen tagged back in. Shawn then bagan to fight back, hitting Owen with a drop kick and a moonsault! He whipped Owen into the ropes, made an error in judgement, and Owen connected with a flying forearm. Shawn was knocked into next week. Owen pins Michaels. Rob Van Dam/Tony Williams vs. Raven/Tommy Dreamer This was the Tommy Dreamer show. Tommy took Van Dam outside the ring right. He choked out Rob, slammed him into the railing, tossed him into the post, and hit him with a cherry pie in the face!! Vam Dam, however, did fight back. He choked out Tommy with the microphone cord. Finally both got back into the ring. Williams tagged in and hit Tommy with a neckbreaker! Tommy fought back hitting Williams with closelines and suplexes and finally a DDT to get the pin. Raven sat back and watched.....he never got into the match! Public Enemy vs. Owen Hart/British Bulldog Johnny Grunge started this one out fast, drop kicking Davey Boy Smith and landing an elbow. Grunge stayed in the match too long though, and Owen and Davey took advantage. Bulldog gave Grunge a power slam, but Johnny kicked out. Owen tossed Johnny outside the ring and smashed him with a Sega Saturn one of the fans gave him. Johnny was gushing blood. Later in the match, Johnny tagged Rocco who was a little more than ticked off. He gave Owen Hart a frankensteiner which tossed him from the ring. Johnny threw Owen into the ring after hitting him with a tennis racket. Owen was dazed and Rocco hit him with a spin kick and pinned him. Raven/Tommy Dreamer vs. Public Enemy As expected, this match started on the floor. Raven tossed Rocco into the first row. Raven then hit Rocco with a full beer can causing it to explode. Meanwhile, Johnny and Tommy fought on the other side of the ring. Tommy gave Grunge a groin punch and then a knee lift. Johnny fought back giving Tommy a shoulder breaker on a chair! Tommy broke his shoulder on that move. Raven and Rocco took it back into the ring. Rocco gave Raven a slingshot moonsault press and almost got the pin. Raven was quick to retaliate with a DDT. He was too worn out to cover Rocco though. Johnny made it back to the ring and he and Rocco beat on Raven for a good five minutes. Tommy, with one good arm, made it back and somehow DDT'ed Rocco for the pin. BUT, Rocco kicked out at two and a half! Johnny tossed Raven outside the ring. While Rocco recovered, Johnny gave Dreamer a back suplex and a power slam. He held out Tommy's bad arm, and Rocco Rock gave Tommy's arm the drive by!!! Tommy was screaming in pain as Rocco pinned him. Public Enemy wins the IWL WORLD TAG TEAM TITLES!!! As Raven and Tommy went back to the locker room, Public Enemy danced with the title belts as the IWL fans filled the ring. Congrats, PE!!!! NEXT WEEK's MATCHES!!
