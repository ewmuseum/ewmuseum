IWL NEWSLETTER #3 International Wrestling League "The Best and can Back it Up!" STATE OF THE IWL ADDRESS by MR WAYLON "Hello IWL staff, wrestlers, managers, and fans. I am here to inform you all of some late-breaking news concerning "Night to Fight". It seems as though Bill Alphonzo kept true to his promise and went to the State Police, Fire Department, Council of Philadelphia, and even the mayor to complain about the Armageddom Match. He told them about the extreme chaos and violence of this match. It appears as though he talked them into it because I got a call today from a representative of the city of Philadelphia. He told me that the Armegeddon Match was not going to happen and that if it was, STIFF fines would be levied and IWL would be shut down. It looks like the Armegeddon Match will NOT be happening because of Referee Bill Alphonzo. I do have lawyers and others talking to City Council about this matter. I am still listing Armegeddon on the card (with Luger added to it), but realize it might not happen. I will keep you posted. ALSO, Bill Alphonzo complained about the violence that might occur at the Gangstas/Eliminators match. Because of his complaining, a special stipulation has been put on this match. Gangstas vs. Eliminators will be a "Perfect 'lil Angel" Match. In this match, the slightest bending of the rules, such as a punch, will cause a DQ and that team will not get shot at IWL Tag Team Champions. As you can all see, Bill Alphonzo has screwed up "Night to Fight". I am working my tail off trying to get Armegeddon back. The Gangstas/Eliminators thing is out of my hands and the "Perfect 'lil Angel" Match will happen. Thanks for your time."
