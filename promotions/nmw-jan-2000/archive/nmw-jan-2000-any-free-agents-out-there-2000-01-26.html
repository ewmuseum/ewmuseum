Any Free Agents Out There? NMW May Be The Place For You!!!

26 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello. I am the president of a brand new e-fed called the NMW, which stands for New Millennium Wrestling. I currently have about 10 members and needless to say, I need quite a few more. Anyway, before I go any further, here is the roster and info.
SevernRulz - President JoeSantaCruz - Vice President Scsantrock1 - Eliminator ViperDanny - Slim Shady w/ Miss Kitty Liz z Hi - The Rock MikeGroz - Triple H Peppy296 - Undertow w/Debra TWEET01073 - Road Dogg w/Stephanie McMahon TheGreyKing316 - Mankind BeNz NwC 187 - Mike Benz Undertaker689 - Matt Hardy Rules: - First of all, never EVER TOS any member of this fed. If you do and I find out, you will be the one fired and you will TOSed as soon as a suitable violation is found.
- Obviously, the final authority in all matters is me. Any dispute must be brought to me before proper judgement can be made. - For crying out loud, don't complain when you lose a match. You all know it's gonna happen. For all intensive purposes, you probably lost because the other guy was just that much better than you. Therefore, don't complain unless you really feel you have a legitimate complaint.
- Never use another wrestler in your RP for any reason unless you have permission from BOTH myself and the wrestler himself. - I already told most of you this, but in case I didn't or in case you forgot, all roleplays must be at least 15 lines in length with NO EXCEPTIONS. Lets face it guys. If you can't put out a halfway decent 15 line roleplay, you don't need to join any e-feds.
-Don't spam. If you're gonna roleplay, do it in a timely manner. None of us want about 10 roleplays from one person right before the roleplay deadline hits. Any spamming will result in an automatic loss and possible suspension if it is out of hand.
- You are allowed to cuss in roleplays but don't use racial slurs. That will not be tolerated and it will lead to immediate termination depending on the seriousness. - If you do feel the need to quit this fed, two, maybe three, things will happen. First, you have to contact me and let me know. Do not send a mail to the entire fed. Second, I will ridicule you for even joining in the first place. Lastly, I will send you on your way to another e-fed that may be better suited for you. The second of those three things may not happen, but the first and third will definitely happen.
- And finally comes the most important rule of all. Have fun. It's all about having fun. Titles: NMW World Title: This belt belongs to the person who has proven himself to be the elite wrestler in the fed.
NMW United States Title: This belt belongs to the guy who I feel is poised and capable of moving to the next level, which is contending for the world title.
NMW Hardcore Title: This belt belongs to the person who is above and beyond the others in term of hardcore wrestling.
More titles will be added once we get further along. Right now, we just don't have the roster to have 5 titles. One of my main goals is to make sure that each and every one of you captures a title at some point in your tenure here in the NMW.
Deciding of Matches: I will decide each and every match based on what we saw in each of your roleplays. You all are allowed to send a maximum of 3 roleplays per match and you will be given a grade out of 10 for each roleplay. Whomever has the higher grade at the roleplay deadline will win that match. If there is any confusion on this, be sure to contact me because I came up with this system. Roleplay grading will be based on quality and quantity. Quality will make up the majority of the grade, but quantity will factor in.
Cards/PPVs: We will have two cards per week one on Tuesday and the other on Friday. The offical names have yet to be determined. As for PPVs, we will have one per month starting in February . Basically, what I want to do in the first 6-8 cards is get a solid grasp as to who will be the top gun in the fed and who needs some motivation.
Conclusion: Well, that's about it for now. We may need to make some addendums to these policies, but for now, these will do. If you would like to join, please don't hesitate to e-mail or send me an Instant Message. And if you know somebody who would like to join, they are more than welcome. All I want is for this fed to be as great and maybe even greater than I know it can be. By looking at the roster, you can probably derive that real and fake wrestlers are allowed and you have the option of no more than one valet/manager. Anyway, that's all for now. Thank you for reading and please don't hesitate to e-mail me or send me an Instant Message if you would like to join.
