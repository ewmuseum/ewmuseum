NMW: In Dire Need Of Great Roleplayers!!!

21 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello. I am the president of a very small fed (14 members) called the NMW, which stands for New Millennium Wrestling. Well, the reason I am posting this here is because I need members very badly. SInce I have a small roster, the loss of even one member is devastating to the fed. That's why I need established roleplayers in my fed in order to make this fed great. Right now, all I have are one or two established guys and a bunch of 1 week fed rejects.
Now, I strive to be successful in absolutely everything I do. I ran 1 fed about a year ago and it stayed open for about 3 months. That is great considering that I opened it with 6 total members. I ended with 34. I just became busy and had to close down. My VP took it over and now I hear that it has over 120 members and is only 1 year and change old. Anyway, back to this fed. I opened it with 8 members and now I have 14. I was up to 18 at one point, but a little incident happened and I had to fire 4 guys. Right now, there are only about 7 guys who roleplay consistently and roleplay well along with that. Needless to say, I am in need of great roleplayers. I want to succeed in this fed and I am determined to do so. I know all of you, by reading the posts, are established and and have won numerous titles in highly competitive feds. I am telling you now that competiton in this fed is anything but competitive. That doesn't mean that things can't change. What I will be doing in just a few short days is filter out the slackers and keep the workers.
That coupled with some new members, we can get this fed going and it can be given it's chance to succeed. Some who have left have said that it doesn't have a snowball's chance in hell of succeeding, but I am set on proving them wrong. With your help, I can do that. So, I am asking you to join. No, strike that. I am BEGGING you to join. We need great roleplayers and in time, this fed will become elite and it will be a success. If you've read this message all the way through me incoherent baggling, you must have some interest. You might as well join. Anyway, I leave the choice up to you.
E-Mail me to join. Thank you very much.
