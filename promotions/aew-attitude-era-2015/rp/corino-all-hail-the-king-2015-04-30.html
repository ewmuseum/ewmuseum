AEW (Attitude Era, 2015) — Roleplay

AEW #01: "ALL HAIL THE KING!" (Steve Corino)

30 April 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Roleplay #: One [01 ] - Record: 00 - 00 - 00 - Next Match: [vs ] ' Everyone AEW Achievements: None [ [05/03/15] Wrestle War! - ] Steve Corino Vs. Everyone [ The logo for Wrestle War fades up and the scene opens inside a 1985 Mercury Cougar that quite frankly looks like a peice of shit, with obvious signs that it had been in a accident or two. Jack Victory ofcourse is behind the wheel wearing his usual New Jersey Devils jersey and eating doritos from a bag on the passengers seat, Steve Corino sits in the backseat wearing jeans and a black t-shirt that says "The King Of Old School" in the middle of the shirt in orange letters, as Steve Corino sits there he looks somewhat anxious]
' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "How is it possible that we haven't found this place yet?" ' Old Schools Biggest Fan ' Jack Victory: "I don't know Steve..it all looks the same to me." [ Steve Corino starts to look around and notices something, he just shakes his head and begins to speak.]
' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "Why does it seem like we're going around in circles?" ' Old Schools Biggest Fan ' Jack Victory: "Uhh..because we are, damn it looked so easy to find on the map." ' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "Well..did you happen to bring the map with you?" ' Old Schools Biggest Fan ' Jack Victory: "Ummm no..I meant to though." [ Steve Corino just rolls his eyes and begins to speak.] ' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "Well..that's just great, Jack." [ The scene then goes to black with the words '1 Hour Later' in the middle of your screen and the scene goes back to Steve Corino and Jack Victory still driving and no closer to finding TD Gardens, a arena you'd think would be easier to find but as always with Jack Victory, even smallest task can be quite difficult.]
' Old Schools Biggest Fan ' Jack Victory: "Yesss..I think I found it! High Spot?" [ Jack Victory suddenly pulls into a big parking lot, stops the car and points to the building but when Steve Corino finally looks at the building he just smirks.]
' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "You idiot..that's a TD BANK." ' Old Schools Biggest Fan ' Jack Victory: "What's the difference..it says TD." ' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "JACK, i'm not in the mood for this..now find TD Garden!!" ' Old Schools Biggest Fan ' Jack Victory: "You got it, Steve." [ Jack Victory sits there waiting for a high spot but it never happens so he just turns around and goes back to driving. The scene once again goes to black but only for a few seconds and this time when the scene opens we see Steve Corino and Jack Victory walking down the hallway inside TD Garden with Jack Victory ofcourse carrying all the bags and really struggling.]
' Old Schools Biggest Fan ' Jack Victory: "WOW Steve, these are really heavy!" ' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "Are you kidding, my son could of carried those bags when he was ten." [ Jack Victory stops and simply drops the bags on the floor, unable to carry them any further.]
' Old Schools Biggest Fan ' Jack Victory: "Phew..that was exhausting!" [ Steve Corino just shakes his head at Jack Victory and his sloppy behaviour.] ' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "Must I do everything myself?" [ Steve Corino just picks up the bags and continues walking down the hallway with Jack Victory walking behind him, once he reaches the end of the hall he finds his locker room and it's right next to the janitors closet, something that he really notices.]
' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "Well that's just great..they put the KING OF OLD SCHOOL next to the janitors closet, what the hell is going on around here?!?"
' Old Schools Biggest Fan ' Jack Victory: "I don't know about that, I think it's kinda cool." ' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "Yeah, you would..its about what I would expect with Paul Heyman." [ Steve Corino opens the door to his locker room and puts the bags down when he enters the room, its average sized with a couch in the middle of the room. Jack Victory takes a walk around the room looking quite impressed with it but that isn't at all surprising.]
' Old Schools Biggest Fan ' Jack Victory: "Can I borrow some money..sooo hungry!" [ Steve Corino rolls his eyes and reaches into his pocket, handing Jack Victory a $10 dollar bill and with that Jack leaves the room with the look of a kid in a candy store. With Steve Corino now left alone and looking focused on the task at hand, he begins to speak.]
' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "Now, let me get this straight..AFTER everything Iv'e done over the years..THIS is where I find myself? In a locker room this disgusting, it makes the one I had in ECW look like a luxury hotel room and ontop of that, they put me next to a FUCKING janitors closet, are you kidding me?!? Is that anyway to treat The King Of Old School? a MAN that sweat and bled for the sake of this business night after night. At Wrestle War I will bring back the one thing that people thought was dead..OLD SCHOOL and I will bring it back on the night I have a chance to be the AEW World Heavyweight Champion, a title that's mine for the taking. From the moment I enter this match I have ONE thing in mind and that's giving the whole roster a OLD SCHOOL LESSION that it clearly needs and SHOW the world one more time why I am the king of all kings in this business, a man that needs no introduction. It doesn't matter when I enter this match or who I face because..I know in the end the RESULT will always be the same..and thats me standing in the middle of the ring with my hand raised and the belt over my shoulder."
' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "Now, that brings me to a man that I use to know quite well, someone that NOW goes by the name of Kevin Owens and it wasn't all that long ago that Kevin was my protege and tag team partner UNTIL he made the first mistake of his career and turned his back on me, after everything I DID for him but just look where he finds himself today..under the wing of Paul Heyman of all people and I really don't find that very surprising because Paul Heyman has ALWAYS been the guy that likes to fill your head with all sorts of fake promises that will NEVER come true and the people he manages are usually weak minded individuals that will LISTEN to basically anything he tells them and foolishly believe it..I guess Kevin falls under that category, now that explains why he sees himself as "The Leader Of New School"..a claim he TRULY and seriously believes, that's the sad thing. I will admit one thing about Paul Heyman..he is a great salesmen! He could easily sell you a car that was the biggest peice of shit you EVER seen. Kevin Owens..you can change your name to whatever you like but it doesn't change the fact that I WILL always be better then you and that's ONE thing I will make you realize because when you get right down to it..New School means nothing to..OLD SCHOOL and if we're ever in the ring at the same time during this match I will SUPERKICK your teeth down your throat."
' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "It doesn't matter who gets in my way during this match, weither its CM Punk, Mr Anderson, Samoa Joe or Bozo the FUCKING clown, I really don't care and the truth of the matter is they won't be in my way for LONG. To win this match I will use every move that I have..it could be the Old School Expolsion, the Old School Plex or the Old School Kick and then get thrown over the top rope, once again it really doesn't matter to me. When I was thinking about this match I couldn't help but think about ALL the battles iv'e been in over the years and when it was all said and DONE I stood as the winner and that's EXACTLY what's gonna happen at Wrestle War infront of everyone and with me as World Heavyweight Champion I will BRING back the days of Old School, being the FIRST Old School Champion in years and who better to do it then the "King Of Old School" himself. Now..I just have one final thing to say..."
[ Steve Corino looks at the camera with a cocky grin on his face, just then Jack Victory comes back into the room with his hand in a bag of chips and Steve Corino looking at him with a puzzled look on his face.]
' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "That's what you bought with my ten dollars?" ' Old Schools Biggest Fan ' Jack Victory: "Well no..I traded the 10 bucks for this bag of chips! It was a good deal." [ Steve Corino just shakes his head back and forth, unable to believe what he just heard.] ' The King Of Old School ' Steve Corino: "Alright, let me get this straight..you traded my 10 dollars for a bag of chips that's about two dollars? Simply stunning, Jack."
' Old Schools Biggest Fan ' Jack Victory: "High spot?" [ "The King Of Old School" Steve Corino reluctantly high spots with Jack Victory as the scene slowly goes to black, followed by the graphic for Wrestle War being displayed in the middle of your screen.]
