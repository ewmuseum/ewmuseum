{THE GREATEST MAN THAT EVER LIVED.} (Austin Aries)

27 May 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[[NOT JUST THE BEST BUT THE GREATEST MAN IN HISTORY]] There you have it with ANOTHER Wrestle War in the books and three new champion's crowned in ReDragon, Candice Michelle and Roman Reigns respectively, easily the biggest event of the year so far and the main event was exactly what everyone expected it to be..anyone's match to win and when it was all said and done Roman Reigns was the one that left the TD Garden as the new AEW World Heavyweight Champion and there isn't anyone on the roster that can say he didn't earn the title. During the course of Wrestle War it looked like it was gonna be a showdown between The Sons Of Sandow and The Wyatt Family but then something happened that nobody expected..Luke Bryan turned his back on the Wyatt Family and joined the Sons Of Sandow, a act that shocked the whole world. Last week on Monday Night Concussion was the fallout from Wrestle War and a 16 man "Road To Superclash" tournament was announced and next week Austin Aries will face Kevin Owens in the first round but before that, Austin Aries must face the newest member of Sons Of Sandow..Luke Bryan.
title: title of roleplay?; stars: austin aries; date: 05/00/2015 The logo for Attitude Era Wrestling fades up and the scene opens outside of the Richmond Colliseum in Richmond Virginia, a fitting location considering it's the American holiday Memorial Day and several events are planned in Virginia for the holiday. As we get one final look at the outside of the Richmond Colliseum, the scene comes into the arena and right away we see a young woman walking down the hallway in a business suit and carrying a bag in her right hand, we can hear the click of her heels as she walks down the hallway. She stops and opens the door to her left and then we see Austin Aries sitting on the couch, looking at her and pointing to his watch.
"THE GREATEST MAN THAT EVER LIVED" AUSTIN ARIES; "Can you explain to me what took so long?" "ONE SEXY SECRETARY" MISS TESSMACHER; "Seriously? you have no idea how far I had to go for this food." Austin Aries starts to laugh after he hears her say that. "THE GREATEST MAN THAT EVER LIVED" AUSTIN ARIES; "Do I need to remind you what a HONOR it is to be managing the greatest man that ever lived?"
"ONE SEXY SECRETARY" MISS TESSMACHER; "No sir, everybody knows that!" "THE GREATEST MAN THAT EVER LIVED" AUSTIN ARIES; "And yet here I stand without the AEW title over my shoulder.." Miss Tessmacher just simply shakes her head then hands Austin Aries a bag with his meal inside and right away he opens it up, noticing that something isn't right.
"THE GREATEST MAN THAT EVER LIVED" AUSTIN ARIES; "What the hell is wrong with these people..I explained my order in the simplest way." "ONE SEXY SECRETARY" MISS TESSMACHER; "That's terrible! I will get to the bottom of this at once." Miss Tessmacher pulls out her cellphone but just then theres a soft knock at the door so she puts it away and opens the door.
"ONE SEXY SECRETARY" MISS TESSMACHER; "How can I help you?" "AEW'S OWN INTERVIEWER" RENEE YOUNG; "I would like to speak with Austin Aries about what happened at Wrestle War." Miss Tessmacher adjusts her glasses and looks through the papers on her clipboard then looks back at Renee Young.
"ONE SEXY SECRETARY" MISS TESSMACHER; "Hmmm..well..did you make an appointment?" "AEW'S OWN INTERVIEWER" RENEE YOUNG; "I didn't know I had to." "ONE SEXY SECRETARY" MISS TESSMACHER; "EVERYBODY has to." Suddenly Austin Aries enters the conversation, having heard enough. "THE GREATEST MAN THAT EVER LIVED" AUSTIN ARIES; "It's okay..I think we can fit her into our busy schdule." "ONE SEXY SECRETARY" MISS TESSMACHER; "As you wish, boss..I have other things to take care of." She grabs the bag and leaves the locker room, leaving Austin Aries alone with Renee Young..so many questions that need to be asked.
"AEW'S OWN INTERVIEWER" RENEE YOUNG; "Austin, I thought you came pretty close at Wrestle War.." "THE GREATEST MAN THAT EVER LIVED" AUSTIN ARIES; "Pretty close? fifty eight other men can say they came 'pretty close' but pretty close doesn't put the title over my shoulder, its just a wasted opportunity to win what I only deserved to win...the AEW Championship and yeah, I give Roman Reigns all the credit in the world for everything he did at Wrestle War but as far as I'm concerned..the wrong man won that night and instead of winning that belt in front of everyone I was eliminated from the match by Goldust of all people..GOLDUST! that's almost as embarrassing as being eliminated by Hornswoggle. Wrestle War was suppose to be MY moment to take the next step in this company and capture the title that should've been mine but the way I see it..I will have another chance at that title and that's BEGIN'S next week with Kevin Owens."
"AEW'S OWN INTERVIEWER" RENEE YOUNG; "Were you surprised at Wrestle War when Luke Bryan turned on the Wyatt Family and joined Sons Of Sandow?"
Austin Aries smirks and looks around then answers the question. "THE GREATEST MAN THAT EVER LIVED" AUSTIN ARIES; "I WAS..but do you know what really surprised me? the FACT that I didn't leave as World Heavyweight Champion, not to mention the fact that I didn't even make it into the top 3..those are two surprising things. Renee, you must be under the impression that I CARE what goes on between the Wyatt Family and the Son's Of Sandow, personally..I hope they both destroy eachother, then I can just sit back and watch the show. What I care about is this match on Monday Night Concussion against Luke Bryan and showing him that it doesn't matter what group you join..weither it be the Wyatt Family or The Sons Of Sandow, you are still gonna be the same person and in Luke Bryan's case..a NOBODY. Luke Bryan may walk around AEW thinking that he's a different man these days but all that does is prove how crazy he really is and completely out of touch with reality."
"AEW'S OWN INTERVIEWER" RENEE YOUNG; "When it comes to this match on Monday Night Concussion, how ready do you feel?" "THE GREATEST MAN THAT EVER LIVED" AUSTIN ARIES; "I am MORE then ready for Luke Bryan, the real question is..how does Luke Bryan feel facing Austin Aries, the greatest man that ever lived?
