Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Powerhouse Online Wrestling staged its first pay-per-view, Catastrophe, out of Madison Square Garden on 28 October 1998. Its own president, Matt Bryant, opened the show in a three-piece suit, promising the crowd “the dawning of a new era in wrestling” and telling them, before a bell had even rung, “you'll be witnessing history!”

The card built toward a triple threat match at a future event, Never Say Die, to crown the promotion's first World Champion, with Catastrophe's own undercard used to fill two of that match's three spots. The main event, a six-man battle royal for the third spot, came down to Parker and Paseno tumbling over the top rope together, tangled around each other, with neither man's elimination clearly first — and the broadcast simply ran out of time before saying who had won: “We've run outta time,” one announcer told the other, as the feed cut to a rerun of Drew Carey mid-episode.

No closing statement survives. Its own last attested activity under the Powerhouse name runs to 17 November 1998; whether Never Say Die itself was ever held, and who if anyone became its first World Champion, isn't known from what remains.
