WFC — Roleplay

Dean Roy

7 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The shot opens at black for a few moments before cutting to the following in golden letters.]
</center>INHUMANE<center> [The scene cuts to Dean Kauffman Roy at Mad Dog’s Wrestling School in downtown
Detroit, Michigan. He is standing in a makeshift octagon: large planks of wood put together, and cut into an octagon, with fencing around the outside. The padding, cloths rapped around the top, covered in duct tape. A man, looking to be in his early twenties, enters the octagon with Roy. They circle each other for what seems like an eternity before Roy makes the first attempt. He grabs the man around the waist, circles around and locks him in a full nelson. The maneuver doesn’t last long, as Dean quickly plants the man’s face into the scarcely padded plywood. He applies a crossface chickenwing to the man, and interlocks his hands together over his adversaries shoulder. The man screams violently and soon taps out with his free hand. This however does not stop Roy, who powers to his feet and rams the man’s chest into the fencing.]
</center>TORTUROUS<center> [The shot returns to Dean still in the octagon with the man. He is straddling the man’s chest, pounding his face with flurries of fists. He quickly gets to his feet and drops a knee across the man’s face. He jumps to his feet and repeats the kneedrop. He stands at the man’s feet and underhooks his knees. He falls backward and slingshots the man into the guard, he stomach wrenching against the top of the fencing. He stomps away at the man’s gut, causing the man to regurgitate blood. He opens the gate of the octagon and throws the man out. He slowly walks to the locker rooms.]
</center>UNRELENTING<center> [Another man steps up to the gate of the octagon, on the back of his sleeveless sweatshirt, it says “R. Lewis.” As soon as the gate closes, Dean is all over him. He hits a head and arm suplex on Lewis and pulls him back up by the head.
He picks Lewis back up and lifts him up and drops him in an inverted DDT. He flips Lewis onto his stomach and pulls his leg back. He wraps his other arm around Lewis’ head and locks his hand together, bending his back greatly. He does a deadlift from this position and drops Lewis on his stomach. He stands at the side of the octagon until his adversary returns to his feet. Lewis charges at him, but Dean does a standing dropkick, hitting him squarely in the jaw.
Dean steps back and hits a Thesz press from behind, sending Lewis into the cage.] </center>DANGEROUS<center> [Lewis is still down on the ground, Dean standing above him, stradling his chest. Lewis tries to land a low blow on Roy by throwing a punch at the groin.
Dean, however, catches the hand and twists Lewis’ arm before spinning around and dropping to the ground down above the head of Lewis. He rolls Lewis onto his
back and applies a Fujiwara armbar. Lewis tries to get in some offense by rolling back and headscissoring Roy. Dean powers up and drops Lewis into a powerbomb, but picks him back up without releasing. This time he hoists Lewis up on his shoulder and locks on a Canadian backbreaker. He does a forward sommersault, driving the head of Lewis into the almost bare boards. He once again opens the gate and rolls Lewis out.]
Roy: Lawrence... are you ready? </center>D.K.R.<center> </center>SILENT BUT DEADLY<center> Sid Snow [Holder of the retired SWF Cruiserweight title] & Dean Roy [Silent but deadly] Snow Ranked #16 in RSPWF Top 100 Brawlers Snow: "I'm not suffering from insanity... I'm enjoying every damned minute!" Roy: "......."
