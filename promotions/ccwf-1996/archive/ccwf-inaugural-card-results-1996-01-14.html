You are going to pay for even thinking of getting into the ring with my team . They are so Psycho that your career might end on this card. ANNOUNCER : Introducing their opponents,weighing in at a combined wieght of 380 lbs, ladies and gentlemen I give you MAC-A-RENA!! ["Macarena" by Los de Mar sounds over the PA and a small averaged sized couple hip-hop their way to the ring. Once inside both Mac and Rena dance for the crowd on the turnbuckles just before...] Both members of Mayhem Inc. blind sided the smaller duo as they danced. Using thier huge hieght and wieght advantage they quickly threw Mac-a-Rena about the ring with reckless abandon. It looked really bad for the smaller team asa Masked Mayhem press slamed mac over the top rope to the flooor, and then helped Miss Mayhem apply a double-boot-to-the-face to Rena. Following a devistating Double-choke-slam the ref forced Mayhem to go to their corner. "Legend" looked impressed with the destruction his team was reeking on their smaller opponents. Miss Mayhem feeling the match was about over tried for a leg-drop but missed as Rena kipped up and rolled out of the way to tag Mac. mac came in a house of fire but was soon extinguished when as he tried for a cross-body-block Miss Mayhem caught him and drove him into the mat with a powerslam.Tag to Mayhem.Mayhem thinking like his partner,that this match was over pulled Mac up and went for a powerbomb, but as Mac was pulled skyward he used the monetum of the throw to roll himself over Mayhem' s shoulders and snag a sunset flip on the 7 foot giant. Not even a one count as Mayhem showing so agility of his own rolled out of the pin and slammed Mac in the face with a big right. Tag Miss Mayhem and a big double- clothesline later Mac was on the mat at Miss Mayhem's mercy. This time Miss Mayhem was careful as she pulled Mac up and appilied a devistating Brain- buster-suplex to him. Pin attempt Miss Mayhem. Twice the ref's hand hit the mat but just before it came down the third time Rena slammed down on the back of Miss Mayhem's head with a 360-leg-drop-off-the-top, breaking the pin. Mayhem rushed in and clobbered Rena with a clothesline that flipped her in a 360 to the mat, and while the ref tried to restore order Mac and Miss Mayhem lay unmoving on the mat. As Rena regained her feet, Mayhem again pushed past the ref and attacked her,but she ducked away and hit a seris of flying-forearms that staggered the big man. As both Mac and Miss Mayhem reagined their feet the bell rang signaling a time limit draw. Mayhem took it rather badly and pummeled Mac and Rena mercilessly for several minutes before help arrived in the form of the Golden Brothers, Dan and Bret. Mayhem saw them slide into the ring and attempted a double-clothesline. The Golden Brothers ducked and sent Mayhem packing after a double-superkick. Conclusion : Pin fall win by Sam over Khopesh at 23:09 Triangle Match : 1 fall; 30 minute time limit Jacque Williams <vs> Johnny Rotten <vs> KO Kid Kevin Allan ANNOUNCER : Introducing first wieghing in at 280 lbs, ladies and gentlemen I give you JACQUE WILLIAMS!! [Jacque enters to the "Sound of Music" and walks past the impressed fans with adisgusted look on his face,swearing in frenchat several of them] ANNOUNCER : The next commbatant weighing in at 235 lbs, accompianed to the ring by his manager Malcom Maclearn, ladies and gentlemen I give you JOHNNY ROTTEN!! [Both men saunter to the ring,while Johnny sings "Anarchy in the UK"] ANNOUNCER : And the third and final combatant, weighing in at 200 lbs even, accompianed to the ring by his bodyguard Sinister Sam, ladies and gentlemen I give you KO KID KEVIN ALLAN!! [both men walk to the ring, Kevin taunting the fans as he also taunts both Jacque and Johnny who are waiting in the ring] A draw of lots decided Johnny and Jacque would start, which they did with reckless abandon. Both men brawled uncontrolablly for several momments before Jacque gained firm control. Using drop toe holds and powerslams along with various brawling techniques he rocked Johnny into corners and around the ring. Johnny tried to come back several times with failed attempts at press-slams and a leg-drop. Neither man payed any attention to Kevin as he waited on the apron for a tag from anyone. Sensing victory Jacque slammed Johnny in the back of the head with a getto-blaster which set him up for Jacque's patented SLEEPERHOLD. Johnny struggled to reach the ropes which he did, close to Kevin's corner. The ref forced a break which Jacque questioned while stepping back with his arms out in a so what expression. Kevin Reached out and forced a tag. Jacque argued with the ref as he was forced back to his corner, while Kevin quickly annhilated the worn out Johnny witha tiger- bomb for the pin. Conclusion : Pin fall victory by Kevin over Johnny at 11:02 Singles Match : 1 fall; 30 minute time limit JC Freeze (0/0/0) <vs> Canadian Dragon Bret Golden (0/0/0) w/AC Frost W/ Dynamite DAn Golden ANNOUNCER : Our Last contest, coming to the ring first, weighing in at 215 lbs and accompianed by his manager AC Frost, ladies and gentlemen I give you JC FREEZE!! [both men walk to the ring as 'ricky the Dragon Steamboat's' WWF music playes over the PA] This match was a shocker. Jsut as Bret enters the ring Freeze jumps him using a lighting fast thrust-kick under Bret's chin to stagger the larger man which allowed him to follow it with the ICICLE DROP PILDRIVER to secure the quickest win in the card. Bret's brother argued with the ref about serveral things ranging from a fast count to Freeze's attack on his brother, but the offical stood firm and awarded the bout to JC FREEZE via pinfall.
