CCWF Press Conference (03/04/96)

4 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--**Speacial Report**-- Last Tuesday as they were leaving the Arena after the last CCWF Card Dixie Hart and her manager James Mason were attacked by who witnesses have said appeared to be the Heavanly Hawaiians and their manager the Statesman. James and Dixie fought back but the three on two advantage seemed to wiegh against they until Mac-a-Rena and the Golden Brothers appeared on the sence as they exited the building. The two teams came to Dixie and James' resucue by chasing the three off with just their presence. Dixe and James both suffered several lacerations and briuses but not real serious damage was apparent when they were being examined by doctors at OutPatients. Both were stitched up and escorted to their vehical by the Goldens and Mac-a-Rena. ====================================================================== --**OTHER NEWS**-- The Shock : JC Freeze and Powersurge along with thier manager AC frost have decided to leave CCWF, citing personnal reason and comitments as the reason. WE at CCWF are sorry to see them go but must let them leave. Good Luck to you where ever you go, and if you can comeback again to compete soon. ====================================================================== Sky Walker: Moo Kame, now we're even, and we can put this all behond us. You've beaten me, I've beaten you, let's get on with our careers. I can now devote my attention to two things: defending my tv title, and the new Canadien Champion, Marcus Magus. Khopesh, I wasn't there because of you at all. I have no problem with you. I was there to see that Marcus abided by the rules, but Ravonak's meddling ironically gave gave Magus the opportunity he was looking for. I'm the match ended as it did, but it was Magus I was after. One last thing- Slasher, I haven't forgotten you, although it looks like everyone has. Why are you hiding? ====================================================================== (Artemis stands with both CCWF Tag Team Title belts hanging over her shoulders, while Athena stands behind her and to the left in her usual arms-crossed, dark-sunglass-wearing, expressionless pose). Artemis: The Revolution is at hand! Clear Spring has abandoned her oppressor-partner, and the Daughters of the KAOS have walked over all of the competition, both male and female. Slobs like the Transit Authority, buffoons like Mac-a-Rena, and bimbos like the Heavenly Hawaiians are all compelled to aspire to the heights only we have reached. Do not make the mistake of believing that we are satisfied with our gains so far. A new PIL awaits, and revenge for the way we drove the first partner away must give them even greater incentive than ever before. It may not be so easy this time, but the saem fate awaits this PIL as befell the last. And Athena still awaits her Canadien Women's Title Shot. The male- dominated powerstructure behind the CCWF may have been able to prevent our getting a fair chance at that belt, but as the events of the tag tourney proved, even the CCWF Executive Committee can't keep good women down. The movement has begun, and those who are not with us are against us. And those who have been against will attest to the desirability of NOT being against us. ====================================================================== Dr. Insane: Ladies and Gentleman of the CCWF I have come to introduce you to the next level of wrestling superstars. From every wrestler's worst nightmare I bring to you Night Train. Night Train: I HAVE COME TO THE CCWF WITH THE INTENTIONS OF MAKING MONEY AND BEATING A LOT OF PEOPLE UP. I DON'T CARE WHO YOU ARE OR WHAT YOUR MOMMA DOES FOR A LIVING - ALL OF YOU HAVE A TICKET FOR A RIDE ON THE NIGHT TRAIN AND IT IS ONE RIDE YOU WISH YOU WILL HAVE NEVER TAKEN. Dr. Insane: And now I being to you from every wrestler's fantasy the Ladies of the Night. Lady Dynamite: We hear that this is where the big girls play in our sport. Daughters of the KAOS and the Heavenly Hawaiians- have you looked at yourselves in the mirror lately????? You are the ugliest women I have ever seen in my life and I am sure you can't even wrestle. Lady of Darkness: It's like this. We are here to do whatever it takes to reach the top of our sport and if any of you stand in our way, we'll just have to remove your chicken necks out of our way. Lady Dynamite: Don't hate us because we're beautiful. Dr. Insane: There you have it folks. Ain't I great???? ====================================================================== (Back in the locker room of the Heavenly Hawaiians, the Statesman is hugging and celebrating the victorious Kimiko and Kiana who have sweat glistening down their tights and holding on to the Commercial TAg Team championship Belts) Statesman: Girls you were as usual beautiful out there!!! you Did some serious punishment on those other teams!!! WAY TO GO!!! (He hugs and kisses both girls and opens a bottle of Dom Perignon Champagne) Kiana: OOOOOOOH!! I really THAWED OUT FREEZIEEE DIDN'T I!!! HAHAHAHA!! I WANTED SO MUCH TO WATCH HIM BLEED TO DEATH!!!! BUT IN WRESTLING THE PIN IS THE THING!!! HAHAHAHAHA!! (She turns to the camera) WELL , WE PROVED IT AGAIN!!! BEAUTY AND STRENGTH ARE SYNONYMOUS WITH CHAMPIONS IN WRESTLING!!! WE GOT THE BELTS AND WE ARE GOING TO HAVE THEM FOR A LONG,,,,LONG....TIME!!! Kimiko: THATS RIGHT KIANA!!!! AND LEST WE FORGET...THE CHEATERS WHO STOLE THE WORLD TAG BELTS FROM US JUST A FEW WEEKS AGO!!! WE HAVEN'T FORGOTTEN YOU SLIMY GEEKS!!!! WE DESERVE A TITLE SHOT AND WE WILL HAVE BOTH THE COMMERCIAL TAG AND THE WORLD TAG BELTS!!!! ATHENA....YOUR MINE !!!! YOUR GOING TO RECEIVE THE BEATING OF A LIFETIME!!!! I WILL PERSONALLY PUT YOU OUT OF WRESTLING AND SHOW YOU THAT BEAUTY IS A WONDERFUL THING IN A WOMAN AS LONG AS YOU KNOW HOW TO USE THIS!!!!(She shows her fist!!!) Kiana: Artemis, (She whispers), remember....the only thing that stops me from hurting you in the ring....(SHE YELLS)....IS THAT IM NOT THERE NOW TO PUNISH YOU....!!!!!! HAHAHAHAHA!!!! (Both girls pose in their wrestling tights and show their belts to the camera!!) Kim and Kiana: WE DID THIS OUR WAY AND IF ANYONE WANTS TO TRY AND TAKE THE BELTS...BETTER HAVE YOUR INSURANCE PAID UP!!! BECAUSE YOUR IN FOR A LONG HOSPITAL STAY!!!! HAHAHAHAHAHA!! Statesman: Come on girls, Hawaii awaits....lets get out of this miserable Canadian weather...Kimiko, I think Jenny McCarthy wants to try and wrestle you!!! Kimiko: ALL RIGHT!! PRACTICE MAKES PERFECT!!! (she sings) "A BEATING WE WILL DO....A BEATING WE WILL DO ....JENNNY AND KAOS WANT TO BLEED......A BEATING WE WILL DO!!!!! HAHAHAHAHAHA! SEE YOU ALL IN THE RING!!!!! (Both girls laugh and celebrate as the camera goes black) ====================================================================== The Blade Larry Brown says: Tonight you are going to see the greatest exhibition of wrestling talent ever when I make my ring debut. After I polish off my first victim, I'm headed straight for some of you other so called wrestlers. I make money for beating people up and if the titles come, so be it. The bottom line is that I intend to make a name for myself in this great sport of wrestling and you minor leaguers will be stepping stones on my road to glory. And as you stay awake tonight and your heart is racked with fear wondering what you'll do after I end your career, remember that there are plenty of openings for garbage men in your local city. ====================================================================== Today is friday march 1st and I am here with EARTHQUAKE and we are going to talk about my name is chris west.
