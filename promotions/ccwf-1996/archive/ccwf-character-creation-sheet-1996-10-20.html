CCWF - Canadian Championship Wrestling Federation A home developed stat using wrestling fantasy e-mail league. The matches will all take place in Canadain cities or sites. -- Disclaimer : No money is being made by the runners or players in this league. It is purely for fun.-- Rules : - each player can have up to three wrestler's in this league - you can make alliances and challenge anyone you wish to matches, - you can compete in any division - you characters can hold a singles and tag title at the same time - TV Champions will defend every third card against any challenger they chose from their division - Canadian Champions defend once every five cards against someone from thier title's top contender listing - attacks are allowed at anytime, but as presidents we can at anytime limit the number used on a single card as we see fit. - attack request can only be made after the card schedual has been made public. - challenges may be made at any time before/during/after a card - Note : the prezs will have thier own characters in the league as well, but we will noy be allowing our selves to launch attacks on others. - HAVE FUN. Cards : - Card scheduals will come out two days in advance of the card's posting date( the day the results will be made public) - There will hopefully be a card out every Sunday - Each card will have at least three tag matches and six singles (three matches from each division) - For each card there will be brief prematch interviews(placed before the entrance of each wrestler),and longer pre card interviews (sent out before the day of the card)Doing interviews are optional Divisions/Titles : Men's Division : Division head : Rene "Jaguar" Babineau email : [detail withheld] Titles : Men's Canadian Champion Men's Tv Champion Women's Division : Division Head : Jeremy "Cobra" Mosher email : [detail withheld] Titles : Women's Canadian Champion Women's Tv Champion Tag Team Division : (Features mixed and single sex teams) Divison Head : Jay "Khopesh" Collings email : [detail withheld] Titles : Canadian Tag Team Champions TV Tag Team Champions Wrestlers : Each Player may have 3 wrestlers in the league 2 males, and 1 female or, 2 females, and 1 male - anyone can compete in tag team compotitions, even combos of wrestlers not controled by the same player. Everyone is considered to be entered in the singles divisions unless they state they are tag team only. Character Sheet : (copy/send/forward a completed version if you want to join) Player's name : E-mail Address : Tag Team Name : (if any) Wrestler's name : sex: ht: wt: alignment : heel/face/neutral manager :(if any) description of character & ring entrance : style of wrestling : (aerial,brawling,technical,cheating,etc..) gimick : Split 20 points between : (min of 4 in each) - strength (muscle power of character) - agility (speed and dexterity of character) - endurance (stamina and pain resistance of character) Split 10 points between : - intelligence ( ring smarts of character) - savvy (cheating smarts of character) 5 favorite moves : ( leave out basic things, like punches and kicks, we're looking for trademark holds/moves here ie : flying forearms, fallaway slams,etc...) Your Finisher & finisher's descrition : FOR Tag TEAM ONLY : 5 favorite double teams:(these can be basic or complex) Tag Team finisher & description : THAT'S IT FOR INFO, HOPE TO SEE A CHARACTER FROM YOU!
