With this new company is, a "Championship Productions" television station, a "Championship Productions" training facility, located in Orlando, Florida, and fifteen CWO arenas being built in the USA! The projected dates for the finish of those have not yet been given out. ~~~ The Championship Wrestling Organization is getting bigger and bigger almost every day.
