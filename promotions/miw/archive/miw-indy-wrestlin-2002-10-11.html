Indy wrestlin'

11 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://marylandwrestling.cjb.net Maryland Independent Wrestling (MIW) Let's see, here's what we offer.... * a regionally based indy fed * we support both an RP and OOC board * we only accept created wrestlers * a fed where your overall participation -- from your role plays, strats, history with the fed and overall attitude when dealing with the owner -- is taken into consideration as to how far you go in MIW.
* a weekly written card, called "Code Red." if this were reality, it would be about an hour show, broadcasted on a Maryland area independent network "MHz Networks" and syndicated to other parts of the world. we're looking for about 5 matches with other segments.
* room to improve. we're new, but the group that's formed so far (the owner, commissioner and 3 wrestlers) really want this thing to work. jump on the bandwagon early and when MIW is considered one of the indy feds to watch, you can say you had a part in it.
* loyalty. the more you put into this organization, the more you'll be rewarded. here's what we need.... * wrestlers! from heavyweights, cruiserweights (230 and below), women, tag teams. we are really looking to get this fed going strong. * match writers. however often as you can give is fine. all we really expect is to be willing to do about 1 match a week. shoot, even if you can only promise one match a month we'd like to talk to you.
check us out at http://marylandwrestling.cjb.net our first Code Red is posted.... ~jon~
