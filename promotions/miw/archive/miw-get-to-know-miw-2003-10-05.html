[MIW] Because you know you want to....

Oct 5, 2003, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Maryland Independent Wrestling is at it once again trying to get some talent on the roster in order to make a claim at a little bit of the e-wrestling pie. If you think you would be interested, check out our website at http://www.miwwrestling.com
From the page "Get to know MIW" GET TO KNOW MIW The main purpose of this page is to give you a quick overview of what
MIW is and has to offer....
First & Foremost: The Rules Original characters only. The only exception to this is if you TRULY are the said wrestler and CAN PROVE IT.
Profanity and explicit situations will only hurt you here. Even a little bit of it. Remember, there ARE children and grandparents watching.
Role Play. If you aren't going to role play, why bother joining? But if you don't role play, your character becomes a jobber.
If you want to quit, or take some time off, just let us know. Remember that MIW is a small federation. We don't have an elaborate stage, pyrotechnics, laser-lights, jumbo-trons etc that you would see in, say, the WWE.....
It should go without saying, but, we're saying it: Do not use any other characters (other than the reporters) in your role plays without permission of the handler!!!
MIW reserves the right to edit and/or reject any role plays. Hopefully we never have to, but we still reserve that right.
Okay, that's fine so far..... MIW operates on an "every other week" schedule, but there is always something going on. That is to say, on one week, MIW holds Verbal
Assault, our role play/interview packet/show. The next week, is Code
Red, the MIW wrestling show. In addition, there is Aftershock, a post-Code Red RP show. And then the next week is Verbal Assault and so on and so on.
So, how do I role play? All role plays should be sent to [detail withheld] (put "MIW
ROLE PLAY" in the subject line!!!!). All role plays are put together and sent out as part of MIW Verbal Assault, or Aftershock (depending on when they are sent to us). Deadlines for role plays are 11pm
Eastern on the Thursday night they are being sent out. What kind of role plays can I do? You can do any style you would see in your typical independent wrestling promotion. The only restriction is that you keep all In-Ring role plays for Code Red only. Remember that during the taping of
Verbal Assault and Aftershock, there will not be anyone in the arena!
What are Verbal Assault and Aftershock anyways? Verbal Assault is the main Role play email show for MIW. We ask that you do not send more than 2 or 3 role plays for Verbal Assault.
Aftershock is the secondary role play show designed to give handlers an opportunity to react to the previous Code Red.
Code Red? Isn't that a flavor of Mt Dew? And wasn't that the Billy &
Chuck finisher? Uhm, Code Red is our wrestling show....... What about In Rings, Strats, etc for Code Red? We ask that everything you want to send in for Code Red is sent to [detail withheld] by 11pm the Tuesday before Code Red (Code
Red comes out on Thursday nights/Friday mornings).
Can I be a handler and a match writer? Yes. You just won't be assigned to a match involving your character or your direct rivals or allies.
I have more questions!!!!! Send them to [detail withheld] and you should receive an answer.
