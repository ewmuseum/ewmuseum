[MIW] Not an e-fed, anymore.....

Nov 13, 2003, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://www.miwwrestling.com So what exactly is MIW, you ask? If you know what "e-wrestling" is, well, MIW isn't that. At least, not anymore. At one time it was, but it never really worked out. So, now, here I am still with that whole promoter bug biting me.
So, I figured, I still got a hold the miwwrestling.com domain name but I don't have enough money to actually run a fed (Dude, that would be AWESOME if I could). So, instead, I'm running a "fictional"
indy promotion and basing it out of Maryland (my home state) while using indy wrestlers from the East Coast.
Now, for those of you who know about Maryland and pro wrestling, there are a lot more restrictions in the state of Maryland than there are in most other places. I will be doing my best to keep MIW within those limits...at least, the limits that I am aware of. The main limits are: No fighting/wrestling outside the barricaded area; if
there's blood, the match stops; and no male-vs-female matches/violence. MIW will be based out of The Show Place Arena, a real life arena in Upper Marlboro, Maryland. Show Place seats about 4,000 people and its intimate atmosphere is perfect for an indy wrestling promotion
(WCW had held shows here before).
Now, on occassion, MIW may want to go a little more "extreme" and, under Maryland state rules, we can't. But, hey, here's the thing. We are based right outside of Washington, DC! That's right, every now and then MIW will INVADE THE CAPITAL! Holding a few shows from the
DC Armory (also a smaller venue perfect for indy wrestling where WCW has booked shows before!) we can get away with A LOT more in DC.
Now, I have to be straight forward. Even when we go a little more
"extreme" than normal, we will not be a crude, perverse promotion. The characters portrayed here will be portrayed with respect because in reality, they are real human beings. So, we won't be booking any bra and thong, mud wrestling, or any other degrading or "This match is gonna kill someone" type matches.
With that all said.....hope you enjoy us! -Jon, MIW Promoter And with a Message Forum, and eventual Yahoo Group, there will be plenty opportunities for interactivity as well....
