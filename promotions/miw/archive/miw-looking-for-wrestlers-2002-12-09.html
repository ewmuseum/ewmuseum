Looking for more wrestlers: Singles, Teams, Women

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Maryland Independent Wrestling ------------------------------ First, a little history: Maryland Independent Wrestling opened its doors a few months ago on rocky ground. We struggled, but things are looking bright now. The biggest move we have made so far is joining the new interfed Internet Wrestling Coalition. By bringing in a
Creative Team from the IWC, a lot of our wrestlers ((*cough* role players *cough*)) are being trained at how to be better workers... The production level of our weekly show (Code Red) has gone up drastically... And, overall, MIW is becoming a great place to be involved with.
But, we're still very small. So, that's where this message comes in... I'm here to put out an invitation to anyone who's looking for a fun place to join... a place that is growing... a place where your work will make you a superstar not only in MIW, but in the ever growing IWC as well.
While I will not turn down any singles wrestlers, I am especially looking for tag teams and female wrestlers.
Our premier ((er, well, only)) tag team, "The Money Brothers" believe that no other tag teams will join the MIW ranks because of their superiority. The Money Bros. are very arrogant, a little obnoxious, and improving their skills greatly. While they think they deserve to be handed the MIW Tag Team titles... I believe they need to earn them in the ring. If you want to give them, well, a run for their money...
c'mon over. ...And... Crimson Fyre has the ability to become one of the best female stars. Unfortunately, finding some quality competition is proving to be very difficult. So if you're a female wrestler also suffering from the lack of competition, c'mon over to MIW.
Also, all singles wrestlers out there that want to step into the ring with the likes of "Hunka Love" Johnny Dynamite, Kyle Maxwell, Johnny
"Da Phreak" Killa and MIW Chesapeake Champion Worth Millions, bring it on.
Finally....by joining MIW you'll be eligible for two upcoming tournaments. First, MIW is putting together a tournament to crown the first Maryland State Champion of wrestling. If you want to get your hands on MIW's top prize and help turn it into a well respected regional title, we're open to ya. And, also, the IWC is in the development stage of a interfed tournament to see who's the best of the current crop of IWC wrestlers. While I can't give a lot of information out right now...with the promotions of Maryland
Independent Wrestling, Steel City Wrestling and Northern Ireland's
Armagh World Association Of Wrestling....I'm sure that this is going to be a huge incident.
So, come on over: http://marylandwrestling.cjb.net PS: We're also looking for a few more guys to help out with the match writing loads. Hey, the more people we have, the less likely any of us will suffer from match writing burn out.
