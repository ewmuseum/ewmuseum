Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Maryland Independent Wrestling's surviving record opens as an ordinary e-wrestling federation, recruiting by post from 9 July 2003 on an every-other-week rotation: Verbal Assault, a roleplay and interview show; Code Red, its wrestling show; and Aftershock, a supplementary roleplay show reacting to the Code Red before it.

By 13 November 2003 it had recast itself entirely. Its own announcement states plainly that the e-wrestling format “never really worked out,” and that its promoter, still holding the domain name and unable to afford a real promotion, was instead running MIW as a fictional independent promotion, based out of Maryland and built from East Coast independent wrestlers' own ring names. It set itself in a real venue, the Show Place Arena in Upper Marlboro, citing Maryland's own combat-sports restrictions — no fighting outside the ring, no continuing a bloodied match, no mixed-gender bouts — and staged the promotion as occasionally “invading” the DC Armory across the state line to work around them.

Its last surviving document, posted 21 November 2003, lays out four title tournaments about to begin: a Maryland State Championship, a Chesapeake Championship, a Women's Championship, and a Tag Team Championship, the last still short one team and awaiting its final entrant.

No match under either format survives, and no result from any of the four tournaments does either.
