Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The German American Wrestling Federation posted its own recruitment, “GAWA: Become a President!!!!!!!!!,” across eleven near- identical copies between 11 and 17 December 1997, seeking a President and a Vice-President to have the promotion ready “at the beginning of the new Year.” Its own homepage address had previously carried a separate promotion, the GFWWF; its own recruitment is explicit that GAWA is a new venture, not that fed under another name.

Its own constitution split the front office three ways: an Executive Chairman, filled by its own founder, who would sign wrestlers and publish a weekly rankings magazine; a President who would write three cards a week and rule on every match's outcome; and a Vice-President who would run three weekly interview and roleplay shows and recommend winners of his own. Applicants were asked for their name, age, hometown, e-wrestling experience, and, for the presidency, a sample card.

No card, roster or result of any kind survives. Its own application deadline was 2 January 1998, and nothing later is documented.
