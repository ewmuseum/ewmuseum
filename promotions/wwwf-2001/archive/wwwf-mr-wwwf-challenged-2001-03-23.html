>I think you guys >just hang around the bushes and wait for the perfect victim to flame. You brought it on yourself man. You came in here, and said all us "new fedheads and handlers" suck. Well, it may be true in my case...but, there are alot of good new fellas out there. I learned a long time ago, you don't come on here and say how great you are, and how others suck. I tried it, just to kinda get a lil' attention. While I still goof off around here, I don't flame anyone. I also, don't come out and tell everyone how just t-totally fucking great I am, and how I'm new school...and all the old schoolers suck. If new school sucks so much Mr. WWWF, then please...you dodged this question 5 times now. WHY is it so bad? Why is e-wrestling on a decline? WHY is WWWF a savior to us all? And _WHAT_ feds have you read and watched that brought you to that conclusion..other than it's just a "radical thing to say?" So far, I've heard nothing from you, to tell me why my fed, my handler, myself, and countless others are so "horrible" but we only have "flash concepts" that get handlers...but no "actual" skill. >Well I >don't dodge points being made to me. Horseshit. You still haven't answered the aforementioned questions. And to be honest, you probably won't. Cause you've probably not read any of this "new" stuff. I did the same thing..I dogged EMWC, before I read em. Now that I've read em, they're pretty goddamn good. So there. Read some stuff, and find out who sucks, and who doesn't...before you come at us all and say "all new feds, that weren't around in 1952, suck." Note: Exaggeration there. >I guess I really began the WWWF when I >got my first PC in early/mid 96. Now I'm very sorry if 5 years is not enough >for you guys but I would consider the WWWF to be a grandfather fed I think it was your first post, that said...it had been gone for 3-3.5 years. Maybe it was your site. So, technically, eventhiough it may have been started 5 years ago, it's only been "around" for 1 and a half years. Well hell man, let me roll out the red carpet. >at this >stage considering how fast e-feds are born and die on the net these days So, more feds are born and die nowadays. Well, did it ever occur to you, that the game is probably more popular now, than it ever was? I mean, that's like saying there's more deaths in America now, yearly, than in 1801. Why fuck man, ofcourse more feds close. Cause more are opened. However, more feds stay open too. Your fed was around one and a half years it's first run...well, holy crap. I'm sure everyone here can name countless feds that have that long of a streak going. Point blank, there are more feds closing nowadays, but there are just as many staying open. There has to be, to house all the talent that is out there today. >I >know that there were feds before this time however I feel the real e-fed >rush began in 96/97. How convenient. Accomodate yourself why don't you? Hell, if there wasn't WWWF, EMWC wouldn't be around. UEW wouldn't be. UWF wouldn't be. Errrrr...wait. They're all older than 5 years. Well, hell. Guess we all need to bow to [name withheld] for sowing his wild e-fed oates long before you. >I meant that the WWWF is an >"old fed" it's been hanging around for 5 years Well. It's official, at the one and a half year anniversary of AVW, I'm gonna close down shop. Pop back up in 2006 and tell everyone on RSPWF how bad they suck, and how my fed has been around for 5 years. When in actuallity, it's only been around for a year and a half, and that was ump-teen years ago. >and I'd consider that a good >amount of time Yeah. It would be, if it was _OPEN_ and _FUNCTIONING_ all those years. >I cannot understand how the simple advertisement of an >e-fed can send all you guys into mating season.... Dude. Had you come on here and said, WWWF is back!!! Come join now!!! All woulda been cool. Instead, you totally dissed every new fed out there. You said we were "talentless 14 year olds, with flash-concept skills (which I still don't know WTF is), and we kill off all the old school feds..eventhough we have no talent". That, isn't a "advertisement". >> dammit, i'm 17 Gee. 17 now...so you were what? 11-12 when WWWF was around, it's first glorius run? Why hell, I wish I coulda been in that fed so bad. Atleast that explains the name however. (WWWF) Got any old cards to post by chance, from that glorius 5 years ago...so that I can take pointers, and sit in awe and envy? Jase * A serious post from me? Scary. [detail withheld] http://www.efeduniverse.com/avw/
