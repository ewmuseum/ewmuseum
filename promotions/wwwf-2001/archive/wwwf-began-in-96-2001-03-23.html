I Really Began the WWWF in Early/Mid 96

23 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Firstly, apologies for whatever state you guys read my messages when they are posted. The server I am using (Ireland On Line) is very poor at the moment and I don't really know what state it's sending my messages in. Amazing how all of you dodged my points in the last post, I think you guys just hang around the bushes and wait for the perfect victim to flame. Well I don't dodge points being made to me, I guess I really began the WWWF when I got my first PC in early/mid 96. Now I'm very sorry if 5 years is not enough for you guys but I would consider the WWWF to be a grandfather fed at this stage considering how fast e-feds are born and die on the net these days. I know that there were feds before this time however I feel the real e-fed rush began in 96/97. And Hollywood, how do you get "the beginnings" of e-wrestling from the "grandfather" of e-wrestling? Was your grandfather around at the dawn of time? Maybe in Internet Jackal code they mean the same thing but for me they are completely different. I meant that the WWWF is an "old fed" it's been hanging around for 5 years and I'd consider that a good amount of time. I cannot understand how the simple advertisement of an e-fed can send all you guys into mating season....
