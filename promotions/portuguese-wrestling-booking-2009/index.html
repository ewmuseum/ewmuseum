A one-author booking whose founding post gives a wealthy Russian immigrant's fictional backstory for starting a Portuguese wrestling federation, naming a ten-wrestler roster and two already-held national championships before any show is scheduled. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The founding post, dated 10 December 2009, gives the promotion no name of its own, only an origin: Dmitrii Zaurev, a wealthy Russian immigrant living in Portugal, missed wrestling in the country and decided to start his own federation with, in the post's own words, “a good roster.” That roster arrived ten wrestlers deep, and two national titles already had holders before a single show existed — a World Champion of Portugal, João Félix, and Tag Team Champions of Portugal, Dráculo and Rigo. Its shows, the post adds, would run at an invented Lisbon venue, the Pavilhão Mundi, “while the company doesn't have much money.”

A follow-up two days later filled the roster in further, giving each wrestler a home city and a face or heel alignment spread across the country — Ricardo Sousa a face out of Porto, Francisco Pavão a heel out of Leiria, Ruídor a face out of Santarém, and so on down the full ten names, evenly split between the two alignments.

No show, match or title change is on record. The last reply the thread carries asks the author for more developed wrestler biographies, and nothing further survives to say whether any were written.
