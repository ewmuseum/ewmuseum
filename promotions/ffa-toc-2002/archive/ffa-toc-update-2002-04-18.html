Female Fighting Alliance Inaugural Tournament of Champions and Heidi Leick Hardcore Cup ============================================ Update and News! ====4/19/2002==== Website: http://ffatoc.cjb.net FFA ToC Ok Folks, here's your update and news for April 19th! We've now got 15 Singles Applicants and 4 Tag Teams so far, The Tag Teams are all in the Tag Cup/ Money Match, and if they want in the Hardcore Cup, they are welcome to be in that too. We've got a few women that *only* want to be in the Super 8 as well, just remember ladies, a note of warning, if you fail to make the grade ( and we've got some pretty awesome handlers here ) you're off the show then... You'll still be welcome to work the Hardcore Cup if you dont make the Super 8 grade, and rest assured, your women would be OK, as I'd never have your characters do anything out of thier character, or injure them permanently. Below are the list of characters accepted so far... all are invited, pass the word, and as soon as we get 20 singles handlers, I'd say The Singles can start flashing for Super 8 Contention, with April 21 or 28 looking to as the start date for the 1st flash packet. The Tag Teams can start flashing as soon as we get 4-6 teams, or you can wait till I give the word. There will then be 2 more flash packets, all 3 flashes will be judged & graded along with your strats, Strats & Flashes do count equally in my book. The Judges for this event are Myself, Stevie from BRA, Mike from UWF, Dan from NIWF, Bob from LEW, and Mark from Wrestlebowl. No one will judge thier own feds wrestlers flashes. And these judges will be consulted about certain matches and positions that are close in my opinion, not all. ** This card will take place in ONE DAY.... but there will be Parts released on different days, allowing your character to FLASH BACKSTAGE, and re-strat as she Wins/Loses. I think this is fair, and unique, as it allows for me/us to re-judge what you do. If anyone does not like this idea, please Email me and we can discuss it before *** We COULD use some more famous women E-W characters for Guest Refs in the Super 8! GDWA Handlers take note- yours, and other retired famous women E-W are welcome to apply. There are 3 as of right now, One is "Big Gun" Janet James,  and I mistakenly misplaced the other 2 names that were offered, folks, please re-send. **** I'd LOVE to have some kind of a Tribute to GDWA on this show, as it is the 1st fed I'd ever actually had any kind of a challenge in with Heidi. Anyone who has ideas, EM me with them, also, if anyone is still in contact w Lawrence, ask him if he has any ideas. ***** Fedheads.. want to showcase a match, title or otherwise on this show? You're more than welcome, with your announce team, as long as YOU write it ;o) Just make sure the ladies aren't in any other matches ;o) ******** Handlers, if your ALREADY IN and would like to add a TAG TEAM, feel free to do so, as the Tag Teams can particpate in The Heidi Leick Hardcore Cup as well, this is fair in my opinion. *********** The ENGLISH/BRITISH/UK Contingent is well represented here, as 3 women from the Isles are signed up! *********** BRA is has a few vacancies for anyone looking for a Womans fed that runs regularly is invited to apply. The link is at the bottom of this page. BRA will also be showcasing a Womans World Title Match at this event!. ==================================================== With that said, I hope I didn't forget anything...and here's your list of competitors so far, links to all feds represented that I have webistes for, and a handler contact list! Talk away, yes ANGLES will be ACCEPTED! ;o) The Application is on the website, ( see top of page ), so go there, post away and have fun, and I'll have another update to you on Thursday.
