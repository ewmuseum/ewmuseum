Female Fighting Alliance Inaugural Tournament of Champions and Heidi Leick Hardcore Cup =============== Update and News! ====4/07/2002==== Website: http://ffatoc.cjb.net FFA ToC Ok Folks, here's your update and news for April 7th! We've got 13 Singles Applicants and 3 Tag Teams so far, The Tag Teams are all in the Tag Cup/ Money Match, and if they want in the Hardcore Cup, they are welcome to be in that too. We've got a few women that *only* want to be in the Super 8 as well, just remember ladies, a note of warning, if you fail to make the grade ( and we've got some pretty awesome handlers here ) you're off the show then... You'll still be welcome to work the Hardcore Cup if you dont make the Super 8 grade, and rest assured, your women would be OK, as I'd never have your characters do anything out of thier character, or injure them permanently. Below are the list of characters accepted so far... all are invited, pass the word, and as soon as we get 20 singles handlers, I'd say The Singles can start flashing for Super 8 Contention, with April 21 or 28 looking to as the start date for the 1st flash packet. The Tag Teams can start flashing as soon as we get 4-6 teams, or you can wait till I give the word. There will then be 2 more flash packets, all 3 flashes will be judged & graded along with your strats, Strats & Flashes do count equally in my book. *** We COULD use some famous women E-W characters for Guest Refs in the Super 8! GDWA Handlers take note- yours, and other retired famous women E-W are welcome to apply. **** I'd LOVE to have some kind of a Tribute to GDWA on this show, as it is the 1st fed I'd ever actually had any kind of a challenge in with Heidi. Anyone who has ideas, EM me with them, also, if anyone is still in contact w Lawrence, ask him if he has any ideas. ***** Fedheads.. want to showcase a match, title or otherwise on this show? You're more than welcome, with your announce team, as long as YOU write it ;o) Just make sure the ladies aren't in any other matches ;o) ******** Handlers, if your ALREADY IN and would like to add a TAG TEAM, feel free to do so, as the Tag Teams can particpate in The Heidi Leick Hardcore Cup as well, this is fair in my opinion. *********** The ENGLISH/BRITISH/UK Contingent is well represented here, as 3 women from the Isles are signed up! *********** We had a real funny EM from the owner of CWF, who's women I invited. I'll post it at the bottom, I had a flame war with him and his handlers on RSPWF, and kicked thier asses and shut them up. I read it, have a great laugh. With that said, I hope I didn't forget anything...and here's your list of competitors so far, links to all feds represented that I have webistes for, and a handler contact list! Talk away, yes ANGLES will be ACCEPTED! ;o) The Application is on the website, ( see top of page ), so go there, post away and have fun, and I'll have another update to you on Thursday.
