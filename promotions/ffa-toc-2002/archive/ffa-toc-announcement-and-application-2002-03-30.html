Announcing The 1st Annual FEMALE FIGHTING ALLIANCE Tournament Of Champions 1.Who is this for? 2.What is this? 3.Who is it run by? 4.What's the deal? 5.How do I join? 1.Who is this for? This is for WOMEN E-WRESTLERS Only, and is the equivalnet of Japan's and ECWA's ' Super 8 ' but for Womens E-Wrestling, with a Harcdcore Cup, and Tag Championship to go along with it. 2.What is this? It's a 1 night tourney, A Super-Card or Uber-Card, equivalent of Wrestlebowl, taking place in the USA, @ NYC's Madison Square Garden the 1st year, and then in Tokyo @ Jingu Stadium the next, and switching from NYC to Tokyo every year. The date this will be taking place is June 1st. 8 Women, who are either invited/selected, or are champions of thier respective federations, will take place in 1 loss elimination matches, and compete until 1 woman is left and awarded with the FFA Cup and Belt, and a $100,000 prize. and the contestants will be decided by apps submitted, flashes and standings as champions of respected, and respective federations. Also on the card, the losers of the 1st 4 matches, and the next 2, will be placed in a HARDCORE BATTLE ROYALE for the Heidi Leick Hardcore Cup, It's anything and everything goes, Pinfalls count anywhere, and the reward is a $100,000 prize as well as the " Heidi Leick Hardcore BYTCH Cup". 20-30 Women will be in this match, and the contestants will be decided by apps submitted, flashes and federations champions. 6 spots WILL be left open for the losers of the Womens "Super-8" And there will be a 4 Corner WOMANS TAG TEAM MATCH, where 6 Womens Tag Teams will be competing, also an anything goes match, but if a member of a team is pinned, that whole team is eliminated. Both members of both tag teams will be in the ring fighting at the same time, or on the outside, pinfalls only count IN THE RING however and can only be made by LEGAL ( tagged ) Tag Teams. 3.Who is it run by? Fantasy Land- It's Run by the former owner of WHiP ( Women's Hardcore international Promitions ) EFW ( Extreme Fantasy Wrestling ) and EFWF ( Extreme Female Wrestling Federation ) Frankie Starz, along with Jerry Styles and womens wrestling legend Heidi Leick. IRL- It's run by me, [name withheld], pro wrestler, and accomplished E-Wrestling handler and fed runner, and YES I have the time to run a Yearly Supercard, but not the time to run a federation. for those concerned. I used to run EFW, EFWF and WHiP, and the Top 50/100/200 of Womens E Wrestling Poll, . 4.What's the deal? The Deal is, this is the "Super-8/Wrestlebowl" ( yes [name withheld] you can officially "mark-out" now ) equivalent of Womens E-Wrestling, No bias will be held towards any handler that's had heat with me personally in the past, as I will comission a independant panel of 3 judges to decide and judge flashes and strats. In the event of a TIEBREAKER I will decide by evaluation of the judges decision and my own expertise. In my experience, this is the 1st Womens-Only E-W Event being held like this, on RSP-WF or otherwise, and I'm looking to make this an annual event and also a recognized one. 5.How do I join? You ask ME for an Application be it a Singles, or Tag Team, to compete in the Super-8, Extreme-Hardcore Battle Royale, or Tag Team match, Fed-Heads , if your submitting a worker, or tag team, please make sure they agree, and EM addies will be required for all those APPLYING, as well as FED HEADS. YES- We will be accepting INDEPENDANT Female Workers as well. INCLUSION to The Super 8 will be decided by the FIRST FLASH/ROLEPLAY packet, with a FEW spots being reserved for ACKNOWLEDGED FEDERATIONS CHAMPIONS . REQUIREMENTS- 1. Your fed must be a strat/flash fed, not a Sim determined fed. 2. There will be 1 ( ONE ) Flash per Flash show, and it will be judged on QUALITY, not QUANTITY. 3.
