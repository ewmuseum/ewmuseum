A single unsanctioned crossover exhibition, billed as pulling wrestlers from across several other fwrestling.com federations for one advertised card in Orlando, Florida, whose promo threads trickled in through May 2005 before its own operator called the show off that November. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Backyard Wrestling billed itself, in full, as “Steve Savoy and the WFW present: Unsanctioned, Uncensored, and Ill-Advised!!!” — a single one-off exhibition card, Orlando 00, announced 22 April 2005 as a “5-Hour Tape Delay From Le Chateau Du PHENIZZLE in Orlando, Florida.” The between-match host, billed only as the Phenom, was joined by former FWF color commentator Jake Shades on commentary and former IWF star and reality-show “Survivor wash-out” Felicia Hart on interviews.

Rather than build its own roster, the card drew wrestlers straight out of other promotions' own current storylines for one unsanctioned night: a six-man-plus-mystery-man showdown for the invented Chris Horowitz Memorial Softcore Championship Trophy, JA the Anglo Luchador against Anarky, a six-man tag under a stipulation forcing every competitor to consume unnamed illicit substances beforehand, a Reverse Bra and Panties match staged in “Granny's House O' Pain,” and a reunion tag pairing Michael Manson and Alex Wylde, the Vampire Hunters, against Jonathan Marx and Pitt.

Promo threads trickled in for only a handful of the five matches through May 2005, and by 9 May the operator himself wondered aloud whether the roleplay period had really ever begun. A June update promised the show was two segments from finished; it never surfaced.

On 11 November 2005 the operator called the show off for the record: “the BYW was cancelled as there were like 3 RPs for the entire card.” A single follow-up post, seventeen months later, asked whether anyone wanted to try again; it went unanswered. No result of any match, on this or any later attempt, survives in the record.
