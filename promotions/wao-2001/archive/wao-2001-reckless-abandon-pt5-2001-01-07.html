Reckless Abandon (part 5)

January 14, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CD: ...told me about this. He gave me a call one evening while I was busy counting the masses of extra money I'd made from my one month <Chris makes quotations with his fingers> "Premier" performance elsewhere. He wondered how interested I'd be in joining forces with him.
[Chris smiles.] CD: I told him I'm very interested, and that's the truth, baby! [Pop from the crowd. Houston, Emerson and Keenan smile, looking pleased.] CD: You see, Mr. Main Event knows a good thing when he sees it. I could come out here and talk all day about how I'm the Greatest of all Time. About my past accomplishments, about who I've beaten and about what I've won. But I'm not about to. Different time, different place and all that, you know?
[Chris pauses for a moment before continuing.] CD: But what I am out here to say is exactly the same as I told Eric Emerson. A chain is only as strong as it's weakest link. [Chris flashes his trademark smirk again.] CD: And in this chain, there are two week links. [Houston and Keenan look at Douglas, puzzled.] CD: Oh c'mon guys. Do I really need to spell it out? We don't need deadweight hanging around us.
[Douglas throws the mic to Emerson, who pops Houston in the back of the head with it. Keenan starts turn to look at Emerson, but gets shoved by Emerson towards Douglas. Douglas kicks Keenan in the midsection as he's pushed towards him, then hits him with a vicious DDT. Douglas and Emerson start to lay the boots in on Houston and Keenan to huge heel pops from the crowd.]
GN: OH MY! WW: Talk about a double-cross! This rules! [Douglas calls for another mic, and is thrown one from ringside. Emerson continues to lay the boots in on Houston and Keenan as Douglas flashes his trademark smirk.]
CD: We don't need weak links in this chain. Houston... Keenan... I'm not about to come out here week in and week out and make up for the mistakes that you two would no doubt make. Myself and Double-E, the Elite Sensation, are not about to carry you two and allow you to ride our coattails to superstardom.
[Chris laughs.] CD: You see, it goes like this. The true greats prove themselves with their actions as well as their words. And hell, if a picture paints a thousand words, just take a look at this one... The two of us out-smarting you, and the two of you beaten down.
[Chris flashes his trademark smirk once again.] CD: That says more than I ever could, and that's *really* saying something. [Chris throws down the mic and shakes hands with Eric Emerson.] GN: OH MY LORD LADIES AND GENTLEMEN! I'M ABSOLUTELY SPEECHLESS! WE'LL SEE YEAH NEXT WEEK! [Fade to black.]
