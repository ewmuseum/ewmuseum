VAN STRIFE: The WAO needs a champion. A man that can hold the Icon Heavyweight Title with pride. A man that has toured the world over, and fought with men now known as "legends" in this sport. A man that has the respect of many, though he's liked by few. A man who can actually back the words he utters.
Ladies and gentlemen... the WAO needs a champion like me. [The crowd boos as Van Strife reaches into his pocket, pulling out a small packet of papers from them. He raises them into the air, a cocky smirk upon his face.]
VAN STRIFE: That's why I brought "All The Rage" Justin Cage to the WAO in the first place. I saw an organization that needed a wrestler of my skill, and an organization that was amongst change... a change that would make it a contender. I knew that I couldn't just come out of retirement without a bang.
What better way to come back than to sign a contract under everyone's nose? When noone was expecting anything to happen, when people saw me as a manager that just helped out a young kid looking to score in the big time. That's when
I decided to make my mark... and to sign my name to a contract. Mister Cage here couldn't cut it... so instead of sticking with an embarassment, I decided that I'd erase my mistakes.
Which now brings me to the future of the WAO. [Van Strife looks into the camera.] VAN STRIFE: Get a good look at me. You'll be seeing this face more often than you'll be seeing men like Jeff Keenan getting their asses kicked on a weekly basis. You'll be seeing this face winning... and this face smiling with the WAO
Icon Heavyweight Title strapped firmly around his waist. It's my time once again WAO... I'll be _damned_ if I let anyone step in my way. However, if anyone would like to try, I'd absolutely love to show you why I'm going to come back into this business the way I left it...
The greatest damn wrestler ever to step between these ropes. Count on it. [With that, Van Strife throws the microphone down, and "One Step Closer" by Linkin Park begins to play over the PA System. Van Strife celebrates inside the ring for a few moments before stepping out. As he walks down the aisleway, he is passed by a group of paramedics who swarm into the ring to help Justin Cage.
