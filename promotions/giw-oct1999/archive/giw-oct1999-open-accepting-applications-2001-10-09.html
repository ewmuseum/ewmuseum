Open, Accepting Applications

October 9, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://www.angelfire.com/md3/giwwrestling Global Independent Wrestling (GIW), founded in 1999, has undergone a major facelift and is ready to once again jump into the action. If you think you have what it takes, read the guidelines and fill out an application.
GUIDELINES Introduction This page has been developed simply to lay out everything you need to know as a competitor in Global Independent Wrestling (GIW). First, and foremost, GIW is a fantasy professional wrestling game. Please remember, this is only a game. Do not take things too seriously, and do not neglect school, work, or family life for the sake of GIW. Now, on to the meat and potatoes of this page.
Rules * Created/fake wrestlers and stables only. Yes, you can use wrestlers you've created in other fantasy feds, but there will be no real-life wrestlers/managers here. * No profanity or vulgar language in your roleplays. This is a "family show" and thus let's treat it like that. This includes ethnic/racial and religious attacks. If your character is Buddhist and Buddhism is a big part of his/her gimmick, that's fine. But any anti-Islam, anti-Christian, etc rhetoric will not be tolerated.
* Roleplay, roleplay, roleplay. This is the way you get exposure. This is the way you get matches. Best yet, even, this is how you win matches. "Quality beats quantity."
* All roleplays go past the Owner's eyes before being released to the public. If anything appears to be offensive or vulgar, it will be edited and/or deleted. * There is a section below on what the GIW views as good role plays. * Do not use any other GIW wrestler or staff member (other than the interview crew) in your role plays without prior permission. * The decisions of GIW are final! Cards All cards are released on Mondays to give you a chance to work on your role plays. Shows: Verbal Assault A weekly program released to the media on Thursday nights hosted by our commentators will broadcast interviews and comments from GIW wrestlers, crew and ownership. We all know that "talking smack" is a big part of sports entertainment, and GIW does not neglect this aspect of our business. The deadline for Verbal Assault material is the Wednesday before the program.
Shows: GIW Showcase Every Saturday, GIW staff put on their best smiles, wrestlers suit up, and the spot light is on the squared circle. It's what the fans truly want: ACTION.
Generally consisting of about 5 matches, GIW Showcase holds grudge matches, title defenses and all sorts of other types of pro-wrestling matches.
Shows: Supercards ---coming soon--- Run Ins Whether you're trying to help a friend, or hurt an enemy...a run in could be a good way to get exposure for your wrestler. Simply, a run in consists of a wrestler who is not scheduled for a certain match to interfere (usually via running down the isle to the ring) whether blatantly or in a more "sneaky"
manner. Send an email to [detail withheld] by the Friday before the GIW Showcase you wish to do a run in telling us which match and who you are attacking.
Mic Time If your character finds that a once a week commentary program just is not enough to get out everything he/she needs to say, or if you're looking for that extra moment in the spotlight, "mic time" just might be the place for you. "Mic
Time" consists of a non-wrestling appearance you'd like to make on the action program, "GIW Showcase." The deadline for Mic-Times are the Friday before
Showcase. Just send an email to
[detail withheld] telling us what you're doing and what you're saying. How does GIW run and decide matches? First of all, Role playing is where its at in GIW. The better role player wins. We use the Zeus Pro E-Fed Management simulator for the play-by-play with occassional additions/modifications when needed. However the winner and the way the wrestler wins is based on the better role player. Now, here's a run down of what's most important in role playing.
#1 Verbal Assault: This is a requirement. If you don't submit a suitable role play for Verbal Assault, you can pretty much forget winning. #2 Mic Time: If you have a feeling you got beat in Verbal Assault, or if you think you are pretty much tied with your opponent, perhaps some "extra credit" with an on-card speech could help you out some? DO NOT ASK who is doing better, GIW will NOT answer. What makes a good role play in GIW? The rules behind role playing in Global Independent Wrestling at first may appear rather strict and oppressive, but we promise you, they are not. The style we've developed is an attempt to make sure quality work is put forth all the time. Instead of having a message board where anyone can post anything
(including spam, and ads), everything goes through the Owner's email address.
They are then put together on our weekly "Verbal Assault" commentary program. So, again, what exactly makes a good role play in GIW? Well, quality is a main part. With one weekly commentary program, this limits the one-line, quick replies to assaults that tend to clutter online message boards. Instead, this forces handlers to sit down and think out exactly what they would like to say.
First, you should start out with an opening description. Telling people where your character is -- NOTE: For "Verbal Assault" DO NOT stroll down to the ring thinking the arena is filled to capacity - because, well, it isn't. Only on card "mic times" can be located inside the ring. Also, use the interview crew to your advantage. Although a physical assault on the interview crew is not acceptable, you can verbal assault and berate them all you want. But, remember who your true opponents are. The other wrestlers. Not Mark and Alexandra! Also, another possibility would be in the studio with the commentary crew.
The content of your role play should be informative, explaining why you're cutting an interview. If you have a match scheduled, how do you plan on scoring a victory? And finally, is there anything else you'd like to say?
WOW, you got this far? If so, just your dedication to read all of this shows you have what it takes to put up with the GIW Owner. Why don't you head over and fill out an application?
