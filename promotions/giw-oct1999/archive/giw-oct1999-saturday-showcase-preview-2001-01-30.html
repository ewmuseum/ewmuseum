Saturday Showcase Preview

Global Independent Wrestling (GIW) can be found at http://members.nbci.com/giwwrestling --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Saturday Showcase Preview February 3, 2001 ====================================== Heavyweight Championship First Round Match Shaun Kassidy vs. Killa Kassidy comes from the original days of GIW, so most fans know him and have come to love his sheer lack of self preservation, all in the name of entertainment. Killa is the Hardcore champion. They both originally lost first round matches, but their opponents have such left GIW. Jon Denny decided these two deserved a second chance.
Heavyweight Championship First Round Match Triple X vs. Soldier This will be the debut match for both of these men, however that's where their similaries end. Triple X grew up with all the luxuries, living in a mansion, riding in nice cars. On the other side is Soldier. Where did he grow up? What has he done for a living? We haven't figured that out yet. We just know he can put a beating on just about anyone....if he can only keep his focus.
Television Championship Match Champion BigPoppa LouDog vs. The Baton Rouge Brawler Bobby Johnson Technically Johnson defeated Shawn Waltman, although with a little help from
Shaun Kassidy. Kassidy claims he didn't do it because he likes Johnson, nor because he dislikes Waltman. He did what he did because he did it. So, really how good is Bobby Johnson? LouDog has grown to be a favorite here in GIW.
Perhaps the TV title is just one step in his career here? Standard Match Shawn Waltman vs. Dizzy This match was set up by the owner because, well, he felt like booking it. Waltman and Dizzy are both ring veterans, and have faced each other many times before. Who's going to come out on top this time?
Intergender Challenge Flaxen vs. "Testosterone Filled" Adam Scroggy with "Soon To Be Deflowered" Brenda Rosales Flaxen issued a challenge to Adam, a girl versus a guy, in the middle of the
GIW ring. Okay, so male-vs.-female battles aren't exactly new in the world of professional wrestling....but they always seems to be complete fiascos. Will this match, as Flaxen predicts, shake things up? Or, will it be a total disaster for Global Independent Wrestling?
This Saturday, the Third of February, see all these exciting matches as well as whatever else the GIW Department of Entertainment can come up with.
PS: There's still room to get into the Heavyweight tournament...and soon another tournament for the Cruiserweight title will begin, think you deserve a shot at the Womens Champion, AngelGurl? And, well, our Tag Team roster is empty.....
