Think you got what it takes to win the GIW C-Weight Championship?

October 12, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GIW - Global Independent Wrestling - GIW http://www.angelfire.com/md3/giwwrestling the first Cruiserweight athlete for Global Independent Wrestling, HardKore
(hey, I didn't name him, okay?) has issued a challenge to anyone who wants to fight for the GIW Cruiserweight Championship. Let's just let him put it in his own words.
---- ["Awake" by Godsmack plays as a man dressed in a black T-Shirt that reads
"HardKore" in flamming letters. He also wears black, torn jeans & a backwards balck Yankees hat. He grabs a mic & jumps into the ring.]
HK: Hello people! I am here for two reasons! I will win the Cruiserweight title
& to establish a Hardcore division & title! So woner, next Cruiserweight you sign, I challenge to a Ladder match for the Cruiserweight title! And the next person after that who has the testicullar fortitude to step in the ring with me, I challenge to a match. But, if I win that, you will pick an opponent & if
I win that, we have a hardcore title & I become it's first champ! So, owner guy, what do you say?
["Awake" plays again as he drops the mic as the crowd cheer their heads off. ] ----- Right now, there is no such thing as the "GIW Hardcore Championship". However, if enough people want it.....ya never know what could happen.
~jon~ http://www.angelfire.com/md3/giwwrestling
