AW (Anarchy, 2008) — News

News (entry of 3 December 2008, captured 2 January 2009)

2 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
December 3rd 2008 Ladies and Gentleman News is scarce but one thing is for sure. Alexandre Caine is back ready to go. Not much has been revealed as in what has happened to him but he promises all will be revealed on the upcoming show of Friday Night Action. What we do know is that One Day Alexandre Caine shows up in the AW offices with an attitude over what happened to the fed in his absence. Pretty sure something along the lines of "Cant depend on anyone for anything" mumbo jumbo.
Anyway, there has been some hirings and firings and some reorganization going on. Evan Ross, JCS, and Maxx Kalifornia are all gone. The ones we know are difinatly going to be at Friday Night Action which has an air date of December 12th are James Caine, Star Caine, Suriel N. Crowlie, Vinny Vegas, TDS, and Mesterio Raine. Those on the fence but should be coming back are Alec Fleming, RDawg, Morrigan, and Jeff JAckson. And of course with Alex in control, you know there is going to be more people in the works. SO stay tuned as Anarchy Wrestling is back and ready to well, tear the house down.
