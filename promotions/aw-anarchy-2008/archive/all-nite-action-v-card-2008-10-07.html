AW All Nite Action V ---------------> 07.10.2K8 -Live from: Joe Louis Arena , Detroit, MI -DEADLINE: 12:00pm {US General Mountain} - Sunday - October 5th ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
=Curtain Call= ==Anarchy Rules Match== Jesus Christ Superstar v. RDogg (Two winless losers in an opening match... one of these two men is guarenteed to come out a winner...maybe.)
1 Promotion Limit =Match 2= ==Non-title Normal Rules Match== The Diablo Slayer v. Assassin (TDS is on a roll, and Assassin made his spectacular return at Cold Fall 2008. Now he will meet the man with one loss, in a non-title bout. The only thing on the line is pride and glory.)
2 Promotion Limit =Match 3= ==KO Only Match== Jeff Jackson v. Vinny Vegas (Jeff Jackson and Adrian Cornell took each other the distance at Cold Fall. At HalloWicked 2 they're going to do it again. But first they have to decide who gets to choose the stipulation. Vinny Vegas and Maxx Kalifornia became Chaos Theory champions together at Cold Fall.)
3 Promotion Limit =Match 4= ==KO Only Match== Adrian Cornell v. Maxx Kalifornia (Jeff Jackson and Adrian Cornell took each other the distance at Cold Fall. At HalloWicked 2 they're going to do it again. But first they have to decide who gets to choose the stipulation. Vinny Vegas and Maxx Kalifornia became Chaos Theory champions together at Cold Fall.)
3 Promotion Limit =Heavyweight championship Celebration= Jason Lovell (Jason Lovell has arranged to throw himself a celebration, will he bring in a new Syndicate, or will someone show him how it feels to have his party crashed?)
1 Promotion Limit =Main Event Grudge Match= ==Anarchy Garbage Match== James Caine v. Alec Fleming (Alec Fleming attacked and eliminated James Caine as though he was sent by 'someone' specifically to do it. Caine is out for blood, and Fleming doesn't care. This is an old-fashioned grudge match.)
