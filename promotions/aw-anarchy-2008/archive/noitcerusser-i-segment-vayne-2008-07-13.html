Noitcerusser I: backstage with Vayne

13 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Noitcerusser I - Main - Voting - Card - Rez Noitcerusser I Hosted by: Foxx Newtorks & Sony Live from: Jobing.com Arena in Glendale, AZ Sunday, July 13th, 2008 - 8:00 GMT 'Down and Out' by Tantric :Piper: Folks I have to remind you we have an amazing card tonight, in fact the remaining card promises to be so unforgettable Alexander Caine, has gone so far as to invite several stars from around the globe to attend.
:Coors: Yea, I had to pay for my ticket... Lucky B****es. :Piper: ...Did you check yoour mailbox? :Coors: .....mailbox? :Piper: Ye-...nevermind. One such face many of our returning fans might recognize is- [The camera changes from the announce table to the moneybox to show former EWA Knockout star, Vayne, enjoying the show when AW interviewer Marcus Jackson enters the picture.]
:Marcus Jackson: Enjoying the show Vayne? :Vayne: So far it could be better, but knowing Alex... I have no doubts about the awesomeness to come.
:Marcus: So what are the chances of seeing Vayne stepping between our ropes again? :Vayne: Only time can answer that question and I don't personally know him Marky... Why don't you go find him?
:Marcus: That's a great idea, Why didn't I think of that?! Guys, back to you, I have to go find Time.....Hey... Any ideas on where this guy might be?
[The camera cuts back to a stunned Piper and Coors at the announce table.] :Coors: Seriously? :Piper: Who's to say it's a guy Marcus? [The camera cuts back to Marcus who looks to be thinking really hard on something.] :Marcus: Holy crap on a thunderstick, you may be right Peter. That just means I have to look ten times harder... Guys, back to you.
[Marcus leaves the scene and the shot goes back to the announce table where Piper and Coors sit even more shocked than before.]
:Coors: ...... :Piper: ...... :Coors: I thought I was retarded... :Piper: You may be right there Jim. :Coors: Why can't I just drunk?
