Slamm X! (the weekly top-ten, 28 October 2007 to 12 August 2008)

12 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Anarchy Wrestling =========================================================================== ~~Week of: 08.12.2K8 =========================================================================== #1: The Diablo Slayer Dominated Vinny Vegas, only to be cheated out of the North American tournament. Found redemption in Alex Caine's 24 hour rule he placed on the Iron Man championship.
#2: Kansas Dark Defied the critics and odds by beating insane veteran James Caine cleanly in one on one action.
#3: Vinny Vegas Managed to slick by The Diablo Slayer this week after pulling some VERY NICE spots. #4: Suriel Night Crowlie Turned on his partner, and paid the price by losing his Iron Man title to the monster that is TDS.
#5: Jeff Jackson Finally ended his losing streak against Assassin, but added another L to his record after he tried to get involved in the main event.
#6: James Caine Lost a heartbreaker to Kansas Dark and following the match snapped on him and decimated the poor rookie.
#7: Breeze Showed just how evil he can be this week by assaulting and forcing the Mighty Voovoo to tap out.
#8: Assassin Had a phoenominal match with the Hardcore Hero, but fell just short of victory, after Jackson used his head and countered nicely into a finisher.
#9: Hardcore Jono Was stabbed in the back by the man who questioned his level of trust, and ultimately left bloodied and beaten at the end of Action.
#10: Matt Ward Showed up in the main event to plant Alex Caine's face into the stage, and then got into a fight with The Diablo Slayer.

08: Kain Harbinger Well I figure now might be a good time to let the world know...{Probably not but who cares?} Kain Harbinger was pronounced dead on arrival at St. John Detroit Riverview Hospital. He was said to have died from a severe cervical fracture.
09: Star Caine Put on a fair showing at the Hardcore open despite wrestling with broken ribs. 10: James Caine Had an inspiring motivational promotion, but was just a weak link in the chain at HalloWicked.
