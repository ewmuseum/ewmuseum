Pic Name Position Small Bio Alexander Caine Owner Alexander first took the reigns by starting with two weeks as the number one Monday night wrestling program in XWA, Desecration. Alexander went on to a full on makeover, after buying out the XWA and immediately renaming it the EWA. Only the road to Destiny and greatness await him now. (See the history page for more)
Jon Tees Co-Owner An old friend of James Caine, Came to Alexander with a deck of cards and asked if he wanted a hand. Alexander smiled and gladly accepted the idea as something good.
TJ Diamond General Manager [Bio coming soon.] Jim Coors Color Commentator A Long long time ago in a crappy town known as Flint, Michigan Jim worked as a country music station morning radio personality. He always loved the wrestling scene however, and finally got his big break by when a group of his listeners pushed him to fill out an application. Somehow he miraculusly got the job because James Caine thought he had a good taste in beer. He worked in AWA until he was cut by Corey Hanes in 2004. Since then Coors floated aimlessly around the indy scene eventually moving back to his country radio position. That was until longtime friend James Caine phoned him up with an opening on Desecration, in Aug. of '07. He is generally described as a "beer drinking moron," or "beer swelling boob" As quoted by Peter Piper.
Peter Piper Color Commentator Met James Caine in 1999 at a wrestling convention in Japan. He happened to be on tour with a sister federation of MCW, and the two hit it off. James and Peter have been close friends since, and he now works Color Commentary alongside Jim Coors for AW.
"The Brilliant One" Bertram Perry Color Commentator Bertram has been calling matches for five years, and has developed a keen sense of everything ring related. He is very sarcastic, and is never afraid to state the obivous. Where Caine found him is a mystery, but Piper doesn't like him, and Coors is to drunk to care.
Laura Layne Ring Announcer The biggest, and most well kept secret about Laura Layne is that she is the younger step sister of James Caine, and has followed him through his entire career. She got her big break when James was in WWWWF and they found themselves short a ring announcer. Since then he has always made sure she had a job doing what only she could do best. Yell and scream names. Laura has however also been known to present a more melodious tone, often opening shows, and PPV's by singing the National Anthem, or other various patriotic themes.
Marcus Jackson Interviewer Marcus? Marcus? Marcus who? Head of the Talent Department, First Alternate within the Administration... This guy sits pretty, but why? Nobody actually knows. We suspect a mafia tie.
Mike Semen Head Referee Mike Semen became a referee when he turned 19 many years ago. He first became acquainted with the Caine family through Ace Kingqueen of IPWL in 2004-2005. He had been working alongside Ace for a few years before that and refuses to say anything else. Damn old man...
Terry Fritzpatrick Referee Only Terry could get into the kind of trouble Terry has gotten into. I don't believe a match he's supervised has actually been finished. We could be wrong on that though
Billy Dee Menza Referee Terry's stepbrother... Yea not even we know how that one worked. (Actually we're too polite to say.)
