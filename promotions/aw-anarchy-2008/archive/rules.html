Anarchist Rules

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Anarchist Rules 1. Have fun. 2. It's a general fed rule but - No attacking or using another person's character in your RPs without permission from that person.
3. Match stipulations and cards are subject to change at anytime, up to THREE days prior to the event.
4. Matches will not start to be written until we have at least one rp by one of the participants.Matches will be given 4 days in advance of a show so we know who can write what and what is left up to the owner.
5. You may have a MAXIMUM of THREE (3) seperate Characters divided any way you wish. (This does NOT include interviewers)
6. Have some fun people. It's not about wins or losses it's about a good show! 7. At Anarchy Wrestling we try to keep a relaxed environment, and we always try to push you to further yourself. Anarchy Wrestling encourages you to strive for excellence and challenge not only others, but yourself.
8. At Anarchy Wrestling we WE DO NOT POST IN-RING PROMOS. If you are wanting to talk trash on camera and in a ring you must make a notation that you are appearing at a local indy show, special AW event (discussed with a staff member), or a local house show in your promo. (preferably near the begining.) Those who violate this rule will have that promo thrown out and yes is it will count against you. SO DON'T DO IT!
9. And just because it's the only important rule that must ALWAYS BE REMEMBERED AND FOLLOWED: HAVE FUN!
