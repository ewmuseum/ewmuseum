POSTED BY: Marcus Jackson 17/09/2K8 || Jackson Report #4: WHAT AN ACTION! Jackson Report #4: WHAT AN ACTION! HOLY SHITBALLS AND BLOOPERS! I'm still not sure if hat was the best Action, or the worst action I've ever seen. I do know one thing, is that it lived up to the namesake of Anarchy Wrestling and was chock full...of a lack of rules. The proverbial rulebook was lit on fire, and thrown out the window as we watched AW newcomer Dazz, beat the ever living hell out of another AW newcomer, Jesus Christ Superstar.
As if that wasn't a great jaw-dropping opener, the fans of A-dub watched in awe as No Limit star, Breeze pulled what could quite possibly be the upset of the year, and defeated AW's own heavyweight champion Morrigan. Was Morrigan just too frustrated and focused on his Cold Fall opponent Jason Lovell? We may never know.
Follwing the massive upset, John SMith, Alex Caine's wild card North American contender, was socked in the mouth by temporary CEO Evan Ross, Taking him out of his match completely. Jeff Jackson squeaked away with the win over heavy favorite Adrian Cornell, but how will he fare when Smith is allowed to enter the fray?
The main event of the night saw TDS, defeat Vinny Vegas, one more time. TDS has been a thorn in Vegas' side for sometime, and there's no doubt, at least in my mind, that the bad blood between these two has far from run it's course. At Cold Fall the two will not meet in the ring, but that doesn't mean they may not run into each other backstage.
Speaking of Cold Fall the matches have been signed and set, save for the main event world war matchup which could still see more entrants. As of this moment, I will definately be using my company ticket to be there, and I highly advise all those not able to make it there live, order it on Pay-per-view for the phoenominal price of only $14.99. Marcus Jackson...Out.

POSTED BY: Marcus Jackson 13/08/2K8 || Anarchy Jackson...Report #2. All Nite Action 2... What the hell can I say? It was intense, it was action packed, it was raw, it was wild...hell just go watch it for yourself, the replay is playing now on AW / Foxx NetworX.
So what all went down? You wanna know? You wanna hear it from the Jackson? Alright well I'll tell ya. It started with XWU owner Jason Lovell calling out Morrigan. After that, I watched hell freeze over and James Caine get pinned cleanly by rookie Kansas Dark, in Dark's first match. Following the match Caine snapped and put Kansas through the announce table with a Psycho Crusher v2. Man that was brutal...
After that we saw the North American championship tournament start, and we now know that Jeff Jackson, and Vinny Vegas have moved on to round two, where they will be met with Rachael Daniels, Adrian Cornell, Hardcore Jono, and none other... than the coldest sumbitch in wrestling... Evan 'mother fucking' Michaels. How Alex Caine signed him, I'll never know, but going off what Caine said, I don't expect him to show up either. Then again, I have been wrong before, I could be wrong again. It's a long shot, but it could happen.
I'll go ahead right now and place a bet on who's making it to the final triple threat of the tournament. Cornell, Jackson, and Michaels, (shouold he show up). You heard it here first, those're my predictions. Partly based on their skill, and partly based on the fact that it would be a match of the ages.
Getting back to this past Action, we saw Breeze decimate an injured Voovoo, who apparently has been having issues with AW booking. Alexander Caine's only comment was this;
"If Voovoo has a problem with something in AW, my door is always open...until I go home... but that's another story. He knows how to cntact me, and I have yet to hear of these complaints directly from him. As I understand he feels he shuld've won the title at Noitcerusser, but the match was booked as a draw because those two blew the lid off the Jobing.com arena. I wanted to see them do it again. He knew this then, he knows this now."
Apparently that's true from what I could gather from other sources, although none of which were the Mighty One, so it only begs the question... After the beating he took this week... will he be back?
In the main event we were supposed to see Eradication against Jono and Night. That changed however when Ruin failed a physical. Prior to the match at some point Jono and Night, AGAIN eliminated their opponent and took Raze out of the equation. Tees showed up and was about to hand over the titles without much of a fight when none other than the Hardcore Hero himself showed up, seemingly to save the day all on his own. Alex Caine had a backup plan for him though....as everything here went awry.
Now if I followed my facts right Jeff Jackson saw what happened to Raze, and wanted to make Jono and Night pay for it, He asked Alex to book him in his spot... Alex did. Then this guy, Matt Ward walks into Alex's office and asks for a match. Alex says sure, and books him with Jackson. It's now Jackson and Ward against Jono and Night.
That brings us back to where things got chaotic. Matt Ward planted Alex's face into the stage with what he now calls 'The Bulldog's bite' and Suriel Crowlie dropped Hardcore Jono with his own, faster version of the bite, that he calls 'The Nightcap'. Ward, Night, and Tees all then began to beatdown Jackson and Jono. In other words: Tees is at it again.
That's right, the evil big daddy Tees, is clearly plotting and scheming as well as ever, as he flipped the script on Alex Caine. This element of surprise helped garner Ward, and Night the Chaos Theory championships to end the main event. But that didn't stop the Action...
As The Diablo Slayer, still sore about his earlier loss to a cheating Vegas no doubt, burst down the ramp and took out all three of the men and pinned Night, capitalizing on the 24 hour rule placed by Caine last week on the Iron Man title, and giving us a new Iron Man Champion. After pinning Night, TDS and Ward exchanged a few blows before TDS put the London bulldog down with a Diablo's Nightmare, grabbed his title and headed home to close out the show.
Now if that doesn't say Action, well I don't know what the hell does. What I do know, is that I'll definately be tuning in to the next All Nite Action to see my prediction come true, and find out all the answers to all those questions that everyone has. Until next time.
