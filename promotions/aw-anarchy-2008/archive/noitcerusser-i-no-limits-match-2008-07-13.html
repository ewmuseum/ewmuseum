AW (Anarchy, 2008) — Card

Noitcerusser I, match one: Breeze v. Mighty Voovoo (Sadistic Glass Match)

13 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Noitcerusser I - Main - Voting - Card - Rez Noitcerusser I Hosted by: Foxx Newtorks & Sony Live from: Jobing.com Arena in Glendale, AZ Sunday, July 13th, 2008 - 8:00 GMT 'Down and Out' by Tantric [Laura Layne is seen getting into the ring, high above her is the cage made of solid glass, surrounded around her is many, many glass tubes. She looks up, seeming a bit nervous standing under the glass cage. But she begins to announce the upcoming match.]
:Laura Layne: The following contest is scheduled for one fall, and is a sadistic glass match, and it is for the No Limits Championship!
[The fans cheer loudly, but it quickly stops as “The World is Not Enough” by Garbage hit’s the PA system. The fans begin to boo as Breeze emerges onto the stage, for once he is wearing shoes along with some wrestling shorts and t-shirt. He walks down the isle way, ignoring all of the fans booing him. Breeze slides into the ring, and does some fancy spin kicks, not really impressing the crowd that much.]
:Coors: These fans are letting their voices being heard. :Piper: I’m sure Breeze is not enjoying the right to free speech at this time. [Suddenly Breeze’s music is stopped, and “Through the Fire and Flames” by Dragon Force is played, and the fans stand up from their seats, Breeze is yelling at them to sit their asses back down. Mighty Voovoo emerges onto the stage, and the arena gets much louder, the camera cuts to Breeze who is looking more angry each second. Mighty Voovoo is seen playing to the crowd, doing many generic super hero poses. He begins walking down to the ring, slapping many fans hands, as Mighty Voovoo gets to the ring, Breeze attempts a baseball slide to catch Mighty Voovoo, but he sidesteps it, his music stops playing as Mighty Voovoo drags Breeze out of the ring.]
*DING*DING*DING* :Coors: Oh! Mighty Voovoo seen that coming a mile away, and now it looks like Breeze may wish he didn’t do that.
:Piper: Can you really blame Breeze for trying that? Mighty Voovoo has been under Breeze’s skin for many weeks!
[Mighty Voovoo quickly gets on the offensive, giving Breeze chop after chop, he finally tears off Breeze’s shirt, and throws it out into the crowd, where it is torn into many pieces. Mighty Voovoo continues the chops, Breeze’s chest begins to turn red, but he quickly escapes into the ring, as he pokes Mighty Voovoo’s eye with his thumb. Mighty Voovoo looks at Breeze squarely in the eyes, he takes his entrance attire off, and he rolls into the ring, the bell sounds, and the match is officially on it’s way! The glass cage begins to lower, and Breeze charges at Mighty Voovoo, but Breeze is hit with a standing dropkick, which hits him directly on the chin, Breeze quickly gets up, as does Mighty Voovoo, he quickly grabs Breeze, and connects with an arm drag, he doesn’t let go, and locks in an arm lock. Breeze refuses to have it locked in, and punches Mighty Voovoo right on this chin, Mighty Voovoo lets go and turns around a second to compose himself, Breeze quickly gets up, and does a low dropkick to the left leg of Mighty Voovoo, he falls down against the middle rope, his face is very close to the glass cage. Breeze runs towards the opposite side of the ring, bounces off the ropes, charges to Mighty Voovoo, he attempts to jump onto Mighty Voovoo’s neck, but he moves out of the way, and Breeze practically throws himself against the glass cage. The fans let out an “OHH” as the glass cage vibrates.]
:Coors: Neither man are being able to get any sort of advantage thus far, who knew this match was going to be so close!
:Piper: I think this match may come down to one of these men making some sort of critical mistake, but until then, lets just enjoy the contest.
[Breeze tries to free himself from the ropes, but Breeze gets more tangled up in them. Mighty Voovoo notices the situation Breeze is in, he climbs up onto the top turnbuckle, Mighty Voovoo plays to the crowd a bit, he then launches himself off the top turnbuckle, and hits Breeze with a missile dropkick. The impact frees Breeze from the ropes, but now both men are down on the mat, Mighty Voovoo uses the ropes to pull himself up to his feet, as for Breeze, he seems a bit motionless. Mighty Voovoo is finally to a vertical base, he walks to Breeze, and pulls him to his feet, but out of no where Breeze punches Mighty Voovoo in the gut, grabs him, and connects with a devastating DDT. Breeze finally takes advantage of the match type, and grabs one of the glass tubes, he holds it tightly in his hands, being very patient for Mighty Voovoo to get up, slowly but surely Mighty Voovoo gets to his feet, Breeze tries to hit a home run to Mighty Voovoo’s head, but he ducks, Breeze runs right past Mighty Voovoo, he stops, turns around, and Mighty Voovoo does a hug Mighty Kick to the glass tube, making it go into the face of Breeze! The fans gasp as the glass tube shatters into the face of Breeze. The screams of Breeze echo through the arena as he lays on his back on the mat, Mighty Voovoo goes for the pin. The referee begins to count, but can’t get past 1 since Breeze is moving up and down off the mat from all of the pain.]
:Coors: My God! You can see the glass sticking out from his face! :Piper: I think we’re going to have to change the name of this title, No Limits doesn’t do this any justice Jim.
[Mighty Voovoo gets up from Breeze, grabs Breeze’s legs and begins to turn him over onto his stomach, obviously trying to get into a Boston Crab. Breeze fights and fights to try and stay on his back, after a few moments Breeze gets enough strength to pull himself up and grab Mighty Voovoo by the shoulders, he rolls him up and gets into a small package pin on Mighty Voovoo! The referee counts....1...2... Kick out! Mighty Voovoo was able to escape from the pin attempt. Both men get up to their feet, and Breeze goes for a spinning low kick to Mighty Voovoo’s legs, but Mighty Voovoo jumps over the kick. As Breeze stands all the way up, Mighty Voovoo whips him to the corner, Mighty Voovoo charges at Breeze, and thrusts his shoulder into the gut of Breeze. Mighty Voovoo climbs up onto the 2nd turnbuckle, and starts to lay in some punches to the glass covered face of Breeze. The fans begin to count all of the punches]
1! 2! 3! 4! 5! [Suddenly Breeze begins to get a bit desperate, he takes Mighty Voovoo and sets him into a power bomb position, he carries Mighty Voovoo near a pile of glass tubes, all of the fans begin to stand on their feet, knowing what’s about to happen, and finally Breeze power bombs Mighty Voovoo on the stack of glass tubes! The glass shattering onto Mighty Voovoo’s back and head echoes around the arena, and the fans begin a “HOLY S***!” chant. Instead of going for the pin, Breeze decides to take the glass out from his face, Breeze’s face begins to bleed from each glass shard removed.]
:Coors: What is he doing? Take the glass out after you pin him! :Piper: This right here is what separates rookies from pros, anyone else would go for the pin right after offense like that!
[Finally with all of the glass shards out of Breeze’s face, Breeze shocks the crowd once again, by still not going for the pin, Mighty Voovoo hasn’t moved since the devastating power bomb, but Breeze is going to where there are more glass tubes, he takes one of the tubes up, and waits for Mighty Voovoo to get on his feet again. After a few moments, Mighty Voovoo begins to get to his feet, Breeze once again charges at Mighty Voovoo with the glass tube in hand, he swings for the fences, and smashes the glass tube across the top of Mighty Voovoo’s head. It is smashes into hundreds of pieces, most of which stick out on Mighty Voovoo’s head. Mighty Voovoo falls down, and slumps against the bottom turnbuckle. Breeze looks rather proud of his handy work, he grabs yet another glass tube, then another… And another! He has three glass tubes, and he lays them across Mighty Voovoo’s stomach and crotch area. Breeze now climbs the turnbuckles that Mighty Voovoo is under, he looks as if he may do something sadistic, he gets on the top turnbuckle, and suddenly jumps off, and lands onto the glass tubes which are on Mighty Voovoo’s crotch! The tubes shatter, and Mighty Voovoo lets out a horrific scream. Breeze starts laughing in Mighty Voovoo’s face, mocking him as he screams.]
:Coors: Breeze is simply wanting to get some revenge on Mighty Voovoo tonight! :Piper: Breeze seems to be an emotional man, he has to focus and just win the No Limits title.
[Breeze goes to the opposite corner, waiting for Mighty Voovoo to get up, Mighty Voovoo slowly gets up, while holding his damaged self, Breeze runs at Mighty Voovoo, he gets to him and goes for a super kick, but Mighty Voovoo catches the kick, he holds onto Breeze’s foot, and pushes him down, turns Breeze around and locks in his devastating ankle lock. The fans cheer loudly as Breeze screams, once again feeling the pain of Mighty Voovoo’s ankle lock. Breeze begins to crawl to the ropes, but the area of the ring they’re in is covered with glass shards, as Breeze crawls to the ropes, he starts to cut his hand on all of the broken glass, but Mighty Voovoo pulls him to the middle of the ring, and then gets on his back and wraps his legs around Breeze’s leg, making it more hopeless for him to escape. Suddenly Mighty Voovoo lets go of Breeze’s leg, and starts to un-tie his shoe. Breeze begs him to stop, but Mighty Voovoo takes off one shoe, and then the same to the other. Mighty Voovoo frees Breeze and gets up, Breeze slowly gets to his feet, and looks for his shoes. He sees that Mighty Voovoo has them, he demands them back, Mighty Voovoo acts like he gives them to him, but suddenly throws both of them out of the glass cage. Breeze’s face turns completely red from anger, he runs at Mighty Voovoo, and then starts to jumps around from all of the glass that’s in the ring, suddenly Mighty Voovoo charges at Breeze, and hits him with a close line, Breeze falls down, and squirms around as he lands on many pieces of glass.]
:Coors: Damn! So much damage from a simple close line, any simple move from now on is going to be devastating thanks to all of the glass shards.
:Piper: And Breeze can’t really go anywhere, his shoes have been stripped from his feet! [Mighty Voovoo begins to get sadistic himself, he grabs the head of Breeze, and starts to rub it against the mat. Breeze screams loudly as the back of his head is scraped with glass. Breeze finally frees himself as he grabs Mighty Voovoo’s hands, and pushes them down on the glass covered mat. Breeze gets a painful look on his face as he stands up, but his determination allows him to deliver kick after kick to the back of Mighty Voovoo’s leg. Finally Breeze has Mighty Voovoo down enough, Breeze places Mighty Voovoo’s head between his legs, picks him up, and drives his head down into the glass shards with a piledriver. Breeze finally wants to finish the match, and goes for the cover!.... 1...2...Kick out again!Breeze does not waste any time, he goes to the corner and seems as if he is going to go for his finisher move, Breezylicious. Mighty Voovoo slowly gets up, and notices that Breeze is about to go for his finisher, suddenly Breeze gets distracted by the crowd, and tells them all to shut up. While Breeze is shouting at the crowd, Mighty Voovoo grabs some glass, and places it into his mouth. The fans start to quiet down as they see Mighty Voovoo place glass into his mouth. Breeze finally sees that Mighty Voovoo is up, he goes for the Breezylicious, and connects, but Breeze quickly begins to scream, he quickly removes his foot from Mighty Voovoo’s mouth, and notices more cuts on his foot. Mighty Voovoo reveals to Breeze that he had glass in his mouth, as he spits it out onto the mat. Breeze looks at Mighty Voovoo with a disgusted look on his face, and calls him a freak. Mighty Voovoo starts to chuckle at Breeze, which sends him fuming.]
:Coors: Oh boy, look at Mighty Voovoo, I think you mad him more mad! :Piper: Of course he’s mad, I would be pissed off too! [Breeze finally flips off Mighty Voovoo, and walks over to the glass cage, he starts yelling at Laura Layne for them to raise the cage. Saying that he doesn’t feel like wrestling the “freak” anymore. Soon Breeze realizes that he shouldn’t have turned his back on Mighty Voovoo, as he sneaks up on him, grabs the back of his head, and starts slamming Breeze’s head against the glass cage over and over. Once again, the crowd counts along with how many times Mighty Voovoo slams Breeze’s head against the cage.]
1! 2! 3! 4! 5! 6! 7! 8! 9! [For the last one, Mighty Voovoo backs up Breeze’s head way back, and practically throws Breeze against the glass cage, which leaves a large dent with blood on the cage. Breeze falls onto his back, seeming to be completely out of it. Mighty Voovoo doesn’t waste any time, he’s not going to pin, instead he’s going straight for a large pile of glass tubes, he grabs at least 5 of them, he lays them across Breeze. Mighty Voovoo begins climbing the turnbuckles, he gets to the top turnbuckle and is about to leap off, but then starts looking up higher, he then places his feet and hands against the two sides of the cage, and starts climbing the glass cage! After a few moments, Mighty Voovoo is on top of the cage, sitting on the edge of it. He lets out a loud scream, jumps off the top, and lands on Breeze and the glass tubes with a huge leg drop! The glass tubes shatter all around Mighty Voovoo and Breeze, both men squirm all around the ring, and then begin to lay on the mat motionless as the entire arena begin a huge “HOLY S***!” chant.]
:Coors: My God! They’re dead! I swear it! They’re dead! :Piper: For once, I think I am speechless… I don’t know what to say! :Coors: Thank god. :Piper: Nevermind... You're an idiot. :Coors: Damn it! [Breeze and Voovoo are both rising to their feet, each man bleeding beyond reason from multiple cuts and scars. Breeze swings on Voovoo and connects. The two begin a back and forth punch festwhen sudden;y Voovoo gets the upperhand with a stiff shot. Voovoo goes on the offensive hammering Breeze into a corner, and slamming him with a devestating forearm. Voovoo sets breeze on the turnbuckleand wraps him up. Voovoo screams to the fans and puts a hand in the air, before hoisting Breeze up. Voovoo holds Breeze in a stahl suplex for a moment then falls backwards into nothing but glass. Neither man is moving outside of miniscule rising and falling of their chests. Fritzpatrick looks down at the two men, and drops to the ground and slams his hand to the mat....1.....2....3!]
*DING*DING*DING* :Piper: What the hell? :Coors: Who won? :Piper: I don't know! :Laura Layne: Ladies and Gentlemen, as a result of a double pinfall... this match has ended in a Draw. :Piper: A DRAW!? :Coors: Has that happened before? :Piper: I'm pretty sure that's a first Janet. :Coors: My name is Jim. :Piper: I know, but you sound like a woman soo I was confused. :Laura Layne: As a result of the draw, the No Limits championship will remain vacant.
