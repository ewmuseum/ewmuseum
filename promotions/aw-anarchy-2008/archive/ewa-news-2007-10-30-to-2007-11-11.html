11/11/2007 ||New EWA Site Launched! -A New Extreme- After a lot of hard work the new site has been uploaded. There have been problems dealing with this layout. For some people it has displayed just fine, but for others it has had a couple of glitches. If you have any problems viewing the site perfectly please leave a comment on the OOC board so Kaige can keep trying to work with it. It may just be because of resolution differences between people's computers. If not we will continue to work with it. Also expect many if not all of the sub pages to be re designed to match the new front page. If you have good ideas that you would like to incorporate in to the design message Kaige on AOL Messenger. 01/11/2007 ||Kaige picks a partner!! "Remember Remember the 5th of Nevember" Truer Words have never been spoken as FOR ONE NIGHT ONLY! The monsterous Tag-Team of Kaige and James Caine that fronted Caine's OWF Faction, 'Scarred' will be reunited in Tag-Team Action this week on knockout to take on the Team of Vic Powers, and Johnny Davis. EWA's TWO Number one Contenders to Kaige's World Heavyweight Championship.
So this just begs the question, Can EWA's two number one contender's work cohesively against a team as devestating as Scarred? Tune in this week to find out!
In other news... well I don't think there really is any oh Yea, Welcome Mike Mayhem who is coming out of retirement, as well as TCW, and EWE star Magna!
30/10/2007 || It's A KNOCKOUT!!! Hallowicked has come...and gone... The roster has evovled yet again... It's finally becoming stable around these parts, only time will tell when they will shake up again.
The Slam!0 awards are running at full speed, as well as the championship divisions and we can't forget the weekly awards that are updated at least on the main page...
Congratulations to Kaige, Morrigan, and Devestation Inc. on their recent title victories. But how long will they last is now the question... Don't worry I'm moving as fast as I can to make them short reigns :) .
