All Nite Action IX (25 March 2010)

25 March 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Anarchist Jonathon James Alexander Caine: Epilogue Running Time: approx. 3 min. 'Suicide is Painless' by The Mash plays as we see Alexander Caine cleaning out his office desk. We then view a montage of Alexander Caine clips ranging from him fighting in the ring against the likes of Jon Tees, James Caine, Ben Morrigan, and others. The montage fades to Alex walking down the hall backstage carrying a box of his most prized AW possessions, as several AW staff and roster members are seen melancholy and teary-eyed. Alexander hugs some, shakes some hands, and as he comes to the final door he stops and the clip fades to a montage of more Alexander and Anarchy Wrestling clips ranging from more in-ring action, to press conferences, to being on the Gi-Screen, and even backstage signing contracts. The clip fades back to Alex standing before the exit, he takes a breath and opens the door stepping into the birght afternoon sun. As the door closes the song fades and the screen fades to the AW logo.
Anarchy Wrestling & the Foxx Ntworks present: All Nite Action- Ep. IX Running Time: approx. 2 hours 10 minutes(130 minutes | 10 minute OVERRUN) All Nite Action IX Introduction Running Time: approx. 12 min. The screen then opened up to a BRAND NEW INTRODUCTION VIDEO showing a montage of clips featuring all the current AW stars and a couple past stars ranging from Dave Wilson to Vic Powers, Spaz, the Raine brothers, James Caine, Vinny Vegas, TDS, Morrigan, Scott Owens, Sean Chainz, and more. The clips air over the song 'Livin on Borrowed Time' by Puddle of Mudd. The song is even uncensored. AS the song nears it's close we see the Sprint Center in Kansas City forefront a night backdrop. The camera zooms into the building past the fans crowding into their seats and slows when it reaches the main stage, the ring area. It circles the ring as Pyrotechnics flare, and lands on the stage zooming in to the expensive pyrotechnics there and the song fades, where our audio track is taken over by the three commentators, Star, Peter, and Jim.
The three introduced us all to Anarchy Wrestling's Ninth All Nite Action, and moved our attention to the ring where the Justice League was in the ring.
Vic Powers, Jason North, @$$@$$in, Derek Daniels, Dave Wilson, Trent Montana, Legion, Eric sinn, Ryan McWilliams, and a large cloaked figure are in the ring. Scott Owens and Ben Morrigan are seated in steel folding chairs outside the ring. Vic Powers says somethings about why he is supporting the League. Claiming the chickens with their heads cut routines around AW were finished the day he signed up. A whole lot of blah blah blah penis stroking as one announcer put it. Jason North cut in about how some of the League hasn't been pulling it's weight, when Scott Owens cut him short and directly addressed Jason. He hollered about how some really weren't pulling their weight. How a stable should make up for that weight loss by shifting, but the League didn't do that. Instead it was an AW man who came to his aid at Idus Martias. A man who stood against everything Vic and Jason just finished "Stroking off". With that he flipped Vic and Jason the bird and as most of the league attempted to move towards him, Vic simply shouted to "Let Them go. Because they're not the only ones leaving the League." As Owens and Morrigan hopped the guardrail and headed backstage through the crowd, Jason snapped his fingers and Sean Chainz appeared from beneath the cloak and began hammering shots into both Eric sinn and Ryan Mcwilliams. The rest of the league then joined in the beatdown, except for Trent Montana who sniveled his way into a corner. Vic Powers called a halt to the pummeling and eyed Montana.
CURTAIN CALL - Spotlight Match: @$$@$$in v. Trent Montana Running Time: approx. 7 min. "You're a joke, a disgrace. You don't deserve to have that last name." He shouted at the young star. "And The first match of the night...is you against @$$@$$in. It starts NOW...and if you can't win...then you'll suffer the same fate as those two men." With a yell to ring the damn bell, @$$@$$in charged at Montana and crushed him in the corner. @$$@$$in then decimated the weak Montana. After aabout five or six minutes of pure m@$$acre. Powers spoke into the microphone and declared this was now an 'Anarchy Rules' Match, and the Justice League took turns beating on poor Trent. The commentators made no real effect as they practically paid no attention. After about another five or six minutes of the beating, Jason then called a stop to it and said he had a surprise. Ryan Payne then headed out from behind the entrance on crutches. Ryan Payne gimped his way to the ring, commentary team saying something about a fridge, and beat the ever living S*** out of Montana with his crutches. @$$@$$in covered Montana, and Vic Powers declared North the referee. North counted and Wilson threw Montana out of the ring by Sinn, and McWilliams. Vic Powers then hollered to them, that they were...FIRED!
MATCH II: Tiberius White v. Sean Chainz Running Time: approx. 5 min. Vic Powers then introduced Tiberius White, who entered the stage and said he'd get in the ring to fight Sean Chainz, but not the whole JL. Powers said that's fair, and everyone but Chainz left the ring area. North and Wilson appeared to wish Chainz luck as they left, and Tiberius entered the ring. The bell rang and the two men squared off and it looked like a modern day david and goliath. Although the height difference wasn't so great. The two men tied up with Chainz trying a bunch of power moves to get the upper hand, but Tiberius' quick thinking allowed him to counter each one. Eventually the bigger man makes one small mistake. Chainz throws Tiberius over his head with a back body drop, and taunts the crowd. When he turns around Tiberius flies over the top rope, springboarding into him with a cross body block. Tiberius then taunts the crowd but the physically imposing Chainz is up in no time. When Tiberius turns around he is met with a kick to the gut and lifted for a power bomb, but White counters it into a Queen Anne roll up. Fritzpatrick makes the count, and calls for the bell. Chainz is furious and the Justice Leage surrounds the ring. Chainz backs Tiberius into a corner but TW holds up a finger and then in a puff of smoke....vanishes. Leaving the League to scratch their heads as we went to a commercial break, and Star made comments about the smoke.
~We had a 3 minute Commercial Break for Mountain Dew's AMP Energy drink involving an AW superstar. And some other less interesting commercials.~
MATCH III - Gauntlet Match: Mesterio Raine v. Alvin Soareyes | Adrian Cornell | Votre Ame Running Time: approx. 13 min. We return from the commercial break and are thrutst into seeing Mesterio Raine pummeling Alvin Soareyes before he's even made it to the ring. We're not sure who came out first, but listening to Piper tell it Soareyes was just making his entrance when Mesterio Attacked. Star Caine on the other hand supports Mesterio saying "he is a fine AW superstar, and he's showing why he's one of the greatest." Mesterio got Alvin to the ring and nailed him with a vicious signature, and covered him. Adrian Cornell was watching and smart however, and as the first bell rang Cornell slid into the ring and locked in a rear naked choke. Mesterio Managed to get back to his feet after being brought down by Adrian, and slamming elbows into Adrian. Adrian could hold the move no longer and let go only to be met with an elbow to the face and a Fate Sealer. Mesterio Covered and as the third bell rang the arena blacked out, and the Gi-Screen played a thirty second movie clip of a horrified 'Christmas Carol' starring Rob Zombie as Mesterio Raine. As the Clip ended the lights brightened slowly and there was a man sitting on the turnbuckle. He was wearing an all-white mask beneath a snow cap, and long sleeves, so I really have no idea who this guy is, but When Mestero turned around, and was calling for a mic, he was quickly silenced by the mysterious stranger who hit him with a soaring missile dropkick, from nearly halfway across the ring. The Stranger then went from Mysterious to dangerous as he superkicked Mesterio back across to the opposite corner the stranger just flew out of. Mesterio was knocked right out beneath the bottom rope, as the Stranger was on a roll. The mystery man kept his momentum going by executing a springboard 450 splash modified into a body block. Mesterio by this point seemed only to want to leave. Star made a comment after Peter insulted Raine, about "how he (Raine) had no knowledge of this guy. Mesterio wasn't prepared to face...whoever the hell that is." The stranger grabbed a steel chair and slid it into the ring in front of the turnbuckle then rolled Mesterio onto the chair. Mesterio was dazed and confused and lifted himself to his hands and knees, when the Stranger changed from dangerous...to calamitous. The stranger jumped straight into the air off the top rope and brought both his feet down, stomping hard onto the back of Mesterio's head and neck, sending 'The Final Fate's' face, into the chair. The stranger rolled him over and covered him, the referee shrugged, and counted the pinfall. By the time the bell had finished ringing the Stranger was gone in a flicker of lights. The Referee called for EMT's and helped Mesterio out of the ring. As we cut backstage to Randy Gregory who was smiling at the 'fate' that had befallen Mesterio Raine.
MAIN EVENT I Idus Martias Grudge Match: Rocky G v. Eric Dafoe Running Time: approx. 4 min. Randy Gregory was backstage and Coors asked him why he was smiling. Randy said because Mesterio deserved that for being so C***y, arrogant, and always treating him like dirt. But that wasn't why he was backstage, and someone should ask that question so he can stop smiling. Star Caine did, between joint hits, and Randy Gregory answered as the camera panned out to reveal m@$$ive chaos just outside the parking lot behind him. Erik Dafoe and Ace Ward were being wheeled into an Ambulance in front of a nasty four car pile-up. He said that both men were involved in the crash. One person has already been pronounced dead, but it was neither AW star. "Both are in Critical condition as of this moment." I actually had to wonder myself if this was a rouse, considering the realism here. Randy even said there were talks among police officers overheard stating that Drunk driving was not the cause, and that the cause of the crash is unknown at this time.
~Here we had another 3 min. commercial break with nothing interesting other than loud selling of products until an advertisement for the AW schedule.~
MATCH V Untamed Championship Match: TDS© v. Jesse Williams Running Time: approx. 3 min. When we came back after the report of the automobile accident outside, Jesse Williams was testing the ropes, John Yuma and Peter Piper were shooting the S***, Star Caine was hitting a gas mask, and Jim Coors was face down in a puddle of drool and beer. Suddenly TDS entrance music kicked on and He came out to a roaring crowd. TDS didn't even acknowledge them as he just walked into the ring, and was charged by Williams. TDS threw him backwards onto his head and as Williams stood TDS crushed him with A Diablo's Nightmare. TDS picked him up and hammered him with a Slayer's Salvation and referee Joey Moe counted the three. TDS just grabbed a towel from a stage hand and threw it over his head. He did at least slap a couple hands on the way out, however one hand actually grabbed him and he made eye contact with the woman. He seemed to smile briefly before continuing backstage. No word yet as to how this little incident turned out. I'll keep you posted. While TDS made his way to the back Yuma entered the ring and looked at the wrecked Williams. He was holding a microphone and a piece of paper. He set the paper on Williams chest, and said only one thing. "When you come to sun, you can take that long slow walk the F*** outta here." I laughed so hard it hurt. I do apologize here I went a little out of order, because Yuma firing Williams happened between TDS slapping hands, and meeting some hot chick.
MATCH VI North American Contender Chance: Devlin Raine© v. Shawn Dreamer Running Time: approx. 23 min. After TDS was gone the commentary team laughed at Williams, made mention of how ruthless TDS really is. Devlin Raine then entered the ring, hoisted the NA title to a good crowd pop, and waited for Shawn Dreamer to enter. Dreamer's music hit and Shawn received a bigger pop that Devlin. Shawn made his way to the ring. The two circled and tied up. The match had a lot of great back and forth action, with neither man really gaining any kind of momentum. After about twenty minutes of intense back and forth fighting, Dreamer hit a HUGE cross body block on Devlin Raine, and followed up with his 'Sweet Dreams' and a cover to officially end his losing streak, and be the first man to defeat Devlin Raine in his AW career. Not only that, but he also earned a North American title shot at Confidence Game in April. Overall, an amazing night for the Hardcore Prince, and a BAD NIGHT for both of the Raine brothers.
MATCH VII: Scott Owens w/ Ben Morrigan © v. Louis Anthony w/ Dollface Amy Running Time: approx. 16 min. Following the match we skipped backstage to see Louis Anthony walking alone to the ring. Scott Owens then made his entrance accompanied by Ben Morrigan. Louis Anthony came out to the entrance ramp with a microphone and announced that Amy couldn't be here tonight on account of a flu bug she caught. But that wasn't going to stop him from kicking the "Ever-living S*** outta both Owens and Morrigan". Star, Piper, and Coors laughed at the remark and doubted his claims. They were sure to point out his confidence, arrogance, and aggression. Louis then booked it to the ring and commenced to beating on Owens. Louis held a good head of steam until a capital counter by Owens turned the tables. Owens took over slamming, and smashing on Anthony for a minute before Anthony wound up in the corner. Owens charged him and Anthony moved. Louis readied for a 'Paper Shredder' but Morrigan grabbed his foot. Louis turned away from Ben in time to catch Owens charging him and delivered a nice drop toe-hold. Anthony then rolled his way out of the ring, and started moving towards Morrigan. Morrigan didn't back up but held his ground, even urging Anthony to come towards him more. A little ways from Morrigan, Anthony stopped and cracked a smile, when Dollface Amy appeared from the crowd and dropped Morrigan with a hurricanranna. As Morrigan got up and started to move towards Amy, @$$@$$in slid from beneath the ring and tackled Morrigan. Louis Anthony turned his focus back to Owens who was getting up in the ring while Amy, @$$@$$in, and Morrigan tangled on the outside. Anthony hit a Paper Shredder and covered Owens for the win after about five minutes. Sean Chainz then ran to the ring and started beating on Owens post match. Security rushed the ring to stop @$$@$$in, Amy, Anthony, and Chainz from decimating Owens and Morrigan as we cut to a commercial break.
~We had our last 3 min commercial break here with no AW ads placed. Bummer...should've had one.~
MAIN EVENT II Idus Martias Grudge Match: Dave Williams v. Spaz (w/ Special Guest Referee: Mesterio Raine}
Running Time: approx. 35 min. When we returned from the break the ring was cleared out and the cage was being lowered to the ring. Piper, Coors, and Star made comments about AW's style of Cage match, how it is more ruthless and violent than any other. It's not about escape. It's about survival, or raw and pure animal instinct. Once the cage was lowered Spaz made his way to the ring, then came Wilson. After Wilson and spaz were inside Mesterio Raine made his way to the ring where he locked and chained the cage door from the outside as per his insructions from John Yuma. Spaz checked the door to make sure it wasn't going anywhere then turned to look at the AW Heavyweight champion. The look he gave scared Coors clear out of his chair. Star coughed up a lung, (not literally), and Piper just laughed. Spaz charged the champ, but the champ was smart enough to dodge. But he wasn't fast enough to dodge Spaz who had caught himself and moved to make a different strike. Spaz took the offensive and decimated the Heavyweight champion, making Wilson's victory at Idus Martias look like a fluke. Spaz beat Wilson off every side of the cage, bloodied him, battered him, and broke him into tiny little pieces within TEN minutes. After another TWENTY minutes, Wilson was nearly dead in the corner and Spaz shouted to the heavens before charging and leaping halfway across the ring to crush Wilson with what he calls an 'Iconic splash' As Wilson staggered from the corner Spaz cranked him around and dropped him with the 'Spazim' and covered. Mesterio counted the three slower than the night before christmas, and unlocked the cage. Star, and Coors celebrated while Piper was confounded, bewlidered, and all around confused. Spaz walked, nay strutted, to the door and as Mesterio opened it, Spaz stopped and held the ropes for him. Mesterio looked to the crowd and the crowd cheered. Raine smiled and entered the ring area. He removed his referee shirt to reveal a brand new AW shirt that read "Hell'sAnarchy's Fire". The crowd erupted in the biggest pop of the night so far as Mesterio looked sadistically at the fallen Wilson. Mesterio then stopped and turned toward Spaz and nodded. He then kicked Spaz right in the gut and delivered a 'Fate Sealer'. Mesterio got up and tore off the AF shirt to reveal a kick-@$$ Justice League shirt with and image of 'The Band'. As the cage raised, and Mesterio shouted to the crowd the scene faded to the AW logo.
