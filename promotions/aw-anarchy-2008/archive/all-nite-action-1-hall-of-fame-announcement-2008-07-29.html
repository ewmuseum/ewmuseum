All Nite Action 1: the Hall of Fame announcement

29 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
===================================================================================================================
~~~We return from commercial to see that Alexander is still standing at the podium about to make the announcement of who the first competitor to be inducted into the AW Hall of Fame is.
:Peter: Well we're waiting on Alex to reveal who the first man in the AW Hall of Fame is. we just found out Jonathon Tees was indeed the second man chosen to be allowed in, but who will be the first? Any ideas guys?
:Jim: zz.zz....zzzzhuh? what? Hall of Fame? Kaige... :Bertram: I'll tell you right now who it's gonna be. Madison Divera. She's held the most titles and one of which she wasn't even supposed to hold.
:Peter: And you're brilliant right? :Bertram: Yes. :Jim: zzz...zzz...zz..z...zzzzz.z.zzz.. :Alex: ...before I announce the name of the first person selected I must say that there were a lot of great competitors to choose from and I had to search through the archives more than a couple times...And While I was searching I found the name of someone I had almost completely overlooked who also deserved a spot in our Honorable Halls of Fame... And it is with great pleasure that I say I decided to add a third name to our first class, in honorary fashion...That name..is Madison Divera!
[YEAHHHH! WHOO! YEEEAAA!!! ALLRIGGGHHHHTT!!!] :Bertram: Told you so. ~~~Peter just stares in amazement, as Jim drools on his script. Peter takes Perry's script and begins flipping through it.
:Peter: How did you know that? :Bertram: I already told you, she was Iron man champion for two weeks, and was one half of the tag team champions. She could've been heavyweight champion if she was fat enough, which hey...I'm glad she wasn't.
:Peter: ......Alright who's the other one then? :Bertram: How the hell should I know? :Peter: ...I hate you. :Alex: And the first person...that was selected for entry into our Hall of Fame....none other than...
~~~"Eat the Rich" by Aerosmith plays and Vinny Vegas enters the stage to a mixed reaction of mostly boos.
:Alex: Cut that music...What the hell do you think you're doing? :Vegas: collecting my award, what's it look like. Go ahead, I'll let yu finish saying my name now. :Alex: Oh...you think, your name is the name I'm about to read? :Vegas: I know it is. :Peter: This kid is an idiot. :Bertram: I think he's brilliant. :Peter: ...You also think you're smart. :Bertram: Didn't we just prove I was? :Alex: Well guess what...You're wrong. The name of the number one entrant into aW's Hall of Fame...is none other....than the Clockwork soldier.....The Anarchistic Assassin himself.....
:Peter: IN YOUR FACE MORON! [YEAHHHH! WHOO! YEEEAAA!!! ALLRIGGGHHHHTT!!!] [YEAHHHH! WHOO! YEEEAAA!!! ALLRIGGGHHHHTT!!!] ~~~Vegas' face drops as Drowning Pool's "Step up" shakes the arena. The crowd erupts as Morrigan enters the stage area.
:Alex: BEN...MORRIGAN! :Peter: Not so smart are we?! :Bertram: What a waste of a perfectly good hall of fame spot. [YEAHHHH! WHOO! YEEEAAA!!! ALLRIGGGHHHHTT!!!] [YEAHHHH! WHOO! YEEEAAA!!! ALLRIGGGHHHHTT!!!] [YEAHHHH! WHOO! YEEEAAA!!! ALLRIGGGHHHHTT!!!] ~~~Alex has to scream loudly into the microphone as Morrigan dances over to Alex, and shakes his hand.
:Morrigan: Thank you....thank you. ~~~The music fades away. :Morrigan: Thank you. I have to say... it wasn't easy earning this title. Just like my North American title, that I mistakenly lost, when I lost my way. But I've found it again... and Tonight...in that very ring... I'm gonna take the next step...up...and beat the hell out of this man here, who likes to gamble with his life. I'm gonna beat him, thrash him, and I'm gonna pin him... and take what is rightfully mine. The Anarchy Wrestling Heavyweight Championship!!!
:Peter: I've got my money on him. :Bertram: I'll bet you do...fool. :Peter: What did you say? :Bertram: Nothing...fool. [YEAHHHH! WHOO! YEEEAAA!!! ALLRIGGGHHHHTT!!!] :Vegas: We'll see about that one...We'll see about that. ~~~Vegas drops his mic and heads backstage as Morrigan shakes, Alex's hand and leaves. :Peter: I thought I heard you call me a fool. :Bertram: No..no..wasn't me. Maybe it was a fan back here. :Alex: Moving on to other business... I regret to inform everyone that both Breeze and the Mighty Voovoo are receiving medical attention at the moment, but they will be back next week just in time, to start the GLOBAL...NO LIMIT TITLE TOURNAMENT. Now Tees and myself haven't covered all of the specifics just yet, but the final tw competitors will square off in a match decided by you...our fans, for the No Limit championship, as well as the loser's services as a manager for one month... With that being said. I must now regretaly inform everyone that Morgan Call and Judith orestes are missing in action and have been released from their contracts. This means our North American championship is now vacant, but don't worry, the staff is doing all we can to rememdy that as soon as possible in the most amazing way. Now that I have nothing left to say... Do enjoy the rest of the show...Thank you...and FUCK OFF!
~~~Alex leaves to "devil without a cause' by kid rock as the screen cuts to commercial.
