AW (Anarchy, 2008) — Card

Blunt Trauma, match one: James Caine v. Adrian

20 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
< "Getting away with Murder" [Getting away with Murder plays as the screen changes green, red, and white. Various images from the EWA, and XWA are shown before the new AW logo explodes through them and the screen fades to black.]
=========================== [James Caine and Adrian are in the ring, as Caine tests the ropes.] Peter Piper: Alright so who is Caine ACTUALLY FIGHHTING? Jim Coors: After all this week's changes, I don't even know. It was supposed to be his older brother Star, but then it changed to Adrian...and now....well it's still Adrian, but still...
Peter Piper: Right Jim, Don't hurt yourself. {*DING*DING*DING*} [Caine takes Adrian to the mat with a clothesline from nowhere, and is back on his feet quickly, stalking Adrian who gets up but is put quickly back to the mat with a DDT.]
Jim Coors: Man, Caine is coming out the gate... Peter Piper: Of... Jim Coors: Of what? Peter Piper: the gate...? Jim Coors: What Gate? [Caine drops an elbow to Adrian's Sternum, and quickly stands to hit a second. He connects and stands for a third, but Adrian rolls just out of the way. Caine holds his elbow for a moment, before he is back on his feet. Adrian attempts to tackle Caine but Caine reverses Adrian's momentum and side suplexes Adrian back to the mat. Caine rolls Adrian over and locks in a reverse front necklock, as referee Terry Fritzpatrick checks the hold.]
Jim Coors: A nice submission by Caine here. Peter Piper: It's a simple choke...You moron. [Adrian struggles, and somehow manages to make it to put his foot on the rope and Fritzpatrick starts the count...1........2.........3...........4.....Caine releases the hold, and begins yelling at Terry, while Adrian gasps for breath. Caine is upset and Terry isn't helping the situation, when Caine turns and kicks Adrian square in the face, blood splatters the ring, as Caine grabs Adrian, and whips him to the ropes. Caine ducks and backbody drops Adrian over the top rope, but Adrian lands on the apron.]
Jim Coors: Wow...he's agile. Peter Piper: Do you even know what that means? Jim Coors: Yes...it means........................... Peter Piper: Don't hurt yourself. [Adrian taps Caine on the shoulder who steps forward as he turns then springs towards Adrian kicking him in the chest and backwards off the apron his back smashing into the steel guard rail.]
Peter Piper: Oooh Ouch.. [Adrian is slow to stand and as he gets up Caine steps on to the top rope of the turnbuckle and leaps towards Adrian connecting with a flying heelkick. Caine is a little slow to return to his feet as well as Adrian who is bleeding everywhere. Caine finally reaches his feet, and drags Adrian back to the ring. Caine rolls adrian in and signals that it's time to finish him off.]
Peter Piper: You know what that means Jim... Jim Coors: He didn't shave? Peter Piper: ............You ate paint chips as a child didn't you? [Caine sets Adrian up in the center of the ring and prepares him for a Psycho Crusher. Adrian is in an electric Chair position, dazed and confused.]
Peter Piper: Just drop him James this is over...he's done, you- "Soul Survivor" [Soul Survivor by Young Jeezy plays and the lights flash.The crowd erupts into a hellish frenzy screaming like the world is over. Caine looks to the entrance way with a look similar to something of fear, unsure of what to do.]
Jim Coors: Vic Powers? He didn't re-sign... Peter Piper: No shit Shirley. Jim Coors: Who're you callin- WHOA!.. [1...2....3! Adrian used the momentary distraction by the music to roll Caine up. The target weak point of his finisher.]
{*DING*DING*DING*} Laura Layne: Here is your winner... by Way of pinfall....ADRIAN! Peter Piper: He didn't get but three offensive moves.... Jim Coors: *Belch*
