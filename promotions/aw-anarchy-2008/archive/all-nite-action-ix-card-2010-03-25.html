All Nite Action IX: the card

25 March 2010

AW All Nite Action IX ---------------> 03.25.2K10 -Live from: ===The Sprint Center in Kansas City, MO=== -DEADLINE: 12:00pm {AW Standard Time} - Thursday - March 24th ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
=Justice League Celebration= Vic Powers, Jason North, Derek Daniels, Dave Wilson, Assassin, Trent Montana, Legion, Eric Sinn, Ryan McWilliams, & Scott Owens
(The Justice League, and Vic Powers are hosting a grand celebration to kick of the Ninth edition of All Nite Action, and all the fans, staff, stage hands and locker room are invited. What are they celebrating? The success they finally have acheived at Idus Martias.)
=Curtain Call = ==To Be Announced== ??? v ??? (Apparently the new head honcho, Vic Powers has a special match in store for somebody, and he's not releasing who that is until Action itself actually begins.)
=Match 2= ==Exhibition Match== Sean 'the Truth' Chainz v. Tiberius White (Aw newcomer Tiberius White makes his Action debut against a man who had a resounding AW debut at Idus Martias. CRUSHING another AW debutant, Jesse Williams. Will he continue his streak and move one step closer to becoming the next Diablo Slayer?)
2 Promotion Limit =Match 3= ==AW's FIRST EVER GAUNTLET MATCH== 1: Mesterio Raine v. Alvin Soareyes 2: Mesterio Raine v. Adrian Cornell 3: Mesterio Raine v. Vinny Vegas | Billy M. Wells (Mesterio Raine volunteered on the Barbed Wire, making the boast that if he didn't walk out of Idus Martias as AW Heavyweight champion, he would take on 3 AW Jobbers in a handicap match. Vic Powers decided that wasn't good enough for such a large name in AW and made this match a GAUNTLET MATCH, as well as added Adrian Cornell's name to the list of opponents. Breaking news came in this morning that following Idus Martias, Vinny Vegas was involved in a horrible car wreck, so he may or may not be able to attend. If he cannot then his place will be taken by actual Jobber Billy M. Wells)
3 Promotion Limit for Mesterio 1 Promotion Limit for Mesterio's Opponents =Main Event I= ==Idus Martias Grudge Match== Rock and Roll God Ace v. Erik Dafoe (Rocky G cost Erik Dafoe the heavyweight title, by being, what some fans are calling him, a coward. Rocky G waited for the fourth finals participant to be decided before he even showed up to the finals ring. And then blindsided whoever he felt he had the best chance against. No one really knows why, but following the show, Erik Dafoe went on a manhunt for the resident rocker. Erik couldn't find him, and demanded Powers make this match.)
2 Promotion Limit =Match 5= ==Untamed Championship Match== The Diablo Slayer(c) v. Jesse Williams (Following a dramatic finish to his involvment in the Rabbithole match at Idus Martias, TDS still has to defend his title at every Action, as per the belt's stipulation. Jesse Williams, who got his ass handed to him at Idus Martias is somehow getting that shot. What was Powers thinking when he signed this match? This reporters mind believes there is some type of deeper plot behind this match.)
2 Promotion Limit =Match 6= ==Contender Shot== Devlin Raine(c) v. Shawn Dreamer (Following Devlin's clear title defense at Idus Martias, and Shawn's heartbreaking and tragic loss, John Yuma has booked Shawn in an opportunity to face the North American Champion for his title at the next Action....All he has to do is defeat the currently undefeated Devlin Raine, that's easy enough right?)
2 Promotion Limit =Match 7= ==One on One w/partner== Scott Owens(c) w/Morrigan(c) v. Louis Anthony w/Dollface Amy (Following a blockbuster battle that included thumbtacks, ladders, barbed wire, blood, and the shocking return of Ben Morrigan. The 'Million Dollar Man with the Billion Dollar Plan' will square off with Scott Owens in one on one action. Their respective partners accompanying them at ringside. What impact will their partners have on the match, and how will this end?)
2 Promotion Limit for Scott & Louis 1 Promotion Limit for Ben & Amy =Main Event II= ==AW Cage Match== Dave Wilson(c) v. Spaz Special Guest Referee: Mesterio Raine (Spaz was the only man to left to beat in the finals, and Wilson had help from Vic Powers to do it. This time around, General Manager John Yuma has taken Vic out of the picture and put MEsterio Raine into it. How will Wilson handle the seemingly overwhelming odds? Will he show his true championship status and 'rise to the occasion'? Will Mesterio and Spaz use a dirty underhanded tactic to give Wilson a taste of his own medicine? Will either of them score a clean victory? Yuma has attempted to push this outcome by disallowing escape as a means of victory. The door will be locked permanently, Mesterio will be outside the cage, DQ's are not allowed, interference is not allowed, and escape will not result in a victory. The only way to win this match, is by pinfall, submission, or knockout. Interference apparently WILL result in a disqualification, and if Wilson has any interference he will forfeit the title. While if Spaz has anyone interfere he will forfeit his number one contender status. What will happen? Who knows! This...IS....ANARCHY!!!!)
