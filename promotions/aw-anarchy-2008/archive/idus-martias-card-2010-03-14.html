Idus Martias: the card

AW's Idus Martias ---> 14.03.2K10 <--- -Live from: ===The Sprint Center-Kansas City, MO === -DEADLINE: 12:00am {US Central} - Friday - March 12th ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
::Dark Matches:: AW Idus Martias: Live Intro w/ Peter, Jim, & Star (The traditional AW opening with a little special extra for the pay per view.) =Curtain Call= ==North American Championship Match== Devlin Raine(c) v. Mark Cannon (These two men have gone at each other since AW returned with Noitcerusser II. Now they will finally fight one on one for the most prestigious championship in Anarchy Wrestling.)
3 Promotion Limit =Match 2= ==Exhibition Match== Spaz v. Suriel N. Crowlie (Alex sent a call to some old friends and members of AW.One of those members...the great SNC. He then gave an open challenge, and Spaz was the only guy to accept. So at Idus Martias, as a warm-up for his Rabbit-hole match. Spaz will fight the possible future hall of famer, super scientist, evil genius, and a laundry list of other things...Suriel Crowlie.)
2 Promotion Limit =Match 3= ==Chaos Theory Championship T.L.W. Match== Parental Advisory v. Ryan Payne & Scott Owens (Owens and Payne cost Parental Advisory the championships once, last week the four signed this match into making. An AW first, Thumbtack, Ladders, and WIRE match. With all the ingredients this match has to offer, this match looks to live up to the title of Chaos Theory. Anything and everything can happen, and it more than likely will.)
2 Promotion Limit PER TEAM MEMBER =Match 4= ==Exhibition Match== Jesse Williams v. Sean Chainz (Newcomer Jesse Williams will debut at Idus Martias. His opponent will also be making his AW Debut. Two AW newcomers warming the audience up right before the big MAIN EVENT! How well will they do?)
1 Promotion Limit =Main Event Match I= ==The Rabbithole Heavyweight Championship Match== Cage #1:Mesterio Raine v. Trent Montana Cage #2:Shawn Dreamer v. Dave Wilson Cage #3:Seth G. Morgan v. Rock N Roll God Ace Cage #4:Ali Khadafi v. Erik Dafoe Cage #5:Eddie Stitchard v. The Diablo Slayer Cage #6:Spaz v. Vinny Vegas (In honor of James Caine this match was created to be a sadistic, brutal, and COMPLETELY UNSANE MATCH. With SIX cages stacked and one man from each cage advancing to the ring to fight for the right to be called AW Heavyweight champion who knows what will happen!)
2 Promotion Limit for each Cage Match 1 Promotion Limit for the Finals * ** ***
