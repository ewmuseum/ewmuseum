Anarchist Rules (captured 27 September 2008)

27 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Anarchist Rules [Some Rules Under Staff Re-Evaluation] 1. Have fun. 2. It's a general fed rule but - No attacking or using another person's character in your RPs without permission from that person.
3. Match stipulations and cards are subject to change at anytime, up to TWO business days prior to the event.
4. You may have a MAXIMUM of THREE (3) seperate Characters divided any way you wish. (This does NOT include interviewers)
5. [Under Re-Evaluation] 6. [Under Re-Evaluation] 7. If you stir up a lot of shit OOC, like calling people names or just forcing people to drop matches or something like that (which we have experienced before), you will be subject for termination.
8. The owner's descion is final, if you fight with him... he will e-kick your fucking e-ass.
9. You may not always be on the card, I am going to try and keep this as simple as I can for the myself and as real for everyone as I possibly can by limiting the amount of matches on a show. So on All Nite Action there will be a limit of 7 matches a card with 2 being Pre-Show matches. And on the PPVs there will be a limit of 6 with 1 pre-show match with a total of 7.
10. Have some fucken fun people. It's not about wins or losses it's about a good show! 11. Obey the Owner, and DO NOT Piss and Moan about losses. If you complain about a loss it will be explained to you, and if you insist upon bitching, you do so at your own risk.
12. At Anarchy Wrestling we try to keep a relaxed environment, and we always try to push you to further yourself. Anarchy Wrestling encourages you to strive for excellence and challenge not only others, but yourself.
13. And just because it's the only important rule that must ALWAYS BE REMEMBERED AND FOLLOWED: HAVE FUN!
