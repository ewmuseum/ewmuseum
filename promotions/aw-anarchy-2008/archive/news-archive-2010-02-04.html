POSTED BY: Marcus 'Your Favorite' Jackson 04.02.2K10 ||Action Jackson Report It is official as of this moment, that rumor backstage has been confirmed that John Yuma and Alex Caine have come to an agreement regarding the Heavyweight title. The newest star to be signed to Anarchy Wrestling will be considered the number one Contender for the Heavyweight championship. His opponent will not be one contender however but an unannounced number. This number and the strategy for choosing those will be revealed at the upcoming Action.
Also heard backstage were rumors of fusing in stars to AW. These stars have been allegedly sourced from an outside recruiter looking to take "The Outlaw's" GM position.
Ben Morrigan has been unreachable since the announcement of James Caine's death. We wish him well, and the Caine family all the best.
Willhelm "The Diablo Slayer" Barstow and Mesterio Raine were both cleared to leave the hospital as of Tuesday Morning. Both men sustained multiple injuries in their hellacious bout at Noitcerusser Two, leaving SERIOUS questions as to their physical health in their upcoming match at All Nite Action Seven.
Jeff Jackson has not even officially signed a contract but has been asked by Stitchard himself to compete. Being an old firned and former Employee Alex Caine has allowed the match to be signed. There is no word as to whether or not Jeff will show, or leave Eddie to Parental Advisory.
No one knows who the hell Wicked is or where he came from, and in the same way No one really know wheres Mark Cannon came from at the ppv in the scramble match. Now these two are going to be fighting for the Number one spot to challenge the clear champion, Devlin Raine. Devlin himself will serve as special outside enforcer in this match. How will that go over?
Neither Erik Dafoe or Jon Tees have been available for comment since the announcement of their match at Action.
The Letters W-B-E-M-J I-N-C-A have started appearing throughout AW programming and the AW website. what the shit does it mean?
Shawn Dreamer is indeed in talks with A-dub. He has not officially penned his name to a contract, but sources confirm both John Yuma and Alex Caine are negotiating with the Florida Indy star.
