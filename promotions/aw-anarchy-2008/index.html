A fantasy federation that opened as the Extreme Wrestling Alliance on 13 October 2007, became Anarchy Wrestling in 2008 with Blunt Trauma, Noitcerusser I and a bi-weekly All Nite Action, and came back in January 2010 with Noitcerusser II, keeping the EWA's belts through the rename. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Anarchy Wrestling opened as the Extreme Wrestling Alliance on 13 October 2007, under the company name Death of Mankind 2K8, with the money of its owner, Alexander Caine, who by its own history had bought out the Xtreme Wrestling Alliance and renamed it through the courts. Its first season ran Knockout I to V and the pay-per-views HalloWicked 2K7 and RIOT! 2K7 to December 2007, with Kaige its Heavyweight champion and Morrigan its North American champion. The fed's own history says it collapsed in January 2008, when its owner disappeared on a vacation that went wrong.

It returned as Anarchy Wrestling in April 2008 with the special Blunt Trauma, and from July with the pay-per-view Noitcerusser I and a bi-weekly All Nite Action on the Foxx Networks. The belts came with it: its history calls Morrigan the first Heavyweight champion since Kaige, and its champions page lists the EWA's records and its own side by side. The season ran through the Hall of Fame ceremony of 30 August 2008, where the owner was kidnapped and Evan Michaels became temporary owner, and Cold Fall on 21 September, where Jason Lovell took the Heavyweight title and the Untamed Championship replaced the No Limit and Iron Man belts. A news entry of 3 December 2008 announces the owner's return and a Friday Night Action for 12 December.

The history, written in March 2010, says the company closed in the autumn of 2008 and came back in October 2009 with its owner, his co-owner Jon Tees and its old stars; it does not mention the December 2008 return. The surviving pages then show a Noitcerusser II pay-per-view in January 2010, the death of James Caine in the story, the Idus Martias pay-per-view of 14 March 2010 with its six-cage Rabbithole match, and All Nite Action IX, where Vic Powers, who had bought Alexander Caine's stock, ran the show and the owner left. Its calendar lists shows to 9 May 2010, and no page it published later survives; its page on the eWrestling Encyclopedia says this run closed on 1 August 2010 and that James Caine bought the company in January 2011 and reopened it in Port Huron, Michigan. The roleplays of a player who writes James Caine, from the EWA's first weeks to the prison promos of January 2010, are served beside its pages.
