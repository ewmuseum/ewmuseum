A February 1996 real-roster simulation of the real-world Extreme Championship Wrestling, its only surviving broadcast an eight-man battle royal that crowned Hack Myers the promotion's first Television Champion. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This real-roster simulation of Extreme Championship Wrestling opened on 18 February 1996 with an eight-man battle royal for a vacant Television Championship, announcer Joey Styles calling the action for a field of eight: 2 Cold Scorpio, Taz, Dick Dudley, Chris Jericho, Rey Mysterio Jr., Hack Myers, JT Smith, and Rob Van Dam. Before the bell even rang it hyped two further cards for the same week — a tag team battle royal that Tuesday, a World Heavyweight Championship battle royal that Friday.

Chris Jericho eliminated Rey Mysterio Jr. from behind early, and Hack Myers outlasted the field, eliminating JT Smith, then Jericho, and finally Taz and Dick Dudley together to become, in commissioner Tod Gordon's own words at the belt presentation, “the first ever Fantasy ECW Television champ.”

The presentation itself was interrupted: 2 Cold Scorpio stormed out to demand a title shot, arguing he had been “cheated” out of the match by Dudley's interference. Gordon ruled it on the spot — Scorpio and a partner of his choosing would face Myers and a partner the following week, and a win over Myers would earn Scorpio a title match the week after that.

Neither that tag match, the promised title shot, nor either of the two teased battle royals is in the surviving record.

This federation’s fiction portrays the ECW.
