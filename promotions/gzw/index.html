A GeoCities-hosted fed running from October 2000 to September 2003 under the banner “GroundZero Wrestling 2K1,” its titles carried forward from a named predecessor, EWO, into an eight-title, forty-wrestler roster documented across three sequential message-board-linked addresses. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GZW ran under the banner "GroundZero Wrestling 2K1" from its own debut episode, dated 27 October 2000, into September 2003, its parent credited on-air as "GroundZero Enterprises," across three sequential GeoCities addresses and a trio of ezboard message boards. Its own title-history pages credit a named predecessor, EWO ("Extreme Wrestling Order"), whose own Heavyweight Championship carried over directly into GZW's.

The GZW World Heavyweight Championship's own served history runs an unbroken twelve-champion chain opening with "The First" Paul Spartan and closing with Kage — a separate roster note elsewhere in the corpus instead calls Paul Spartan's own reign secondary, naming Above Average Josh Reetz the promotion's actual first-ever champion, a claim the fed's own Dead Zone broadcast independently corroborates — a contradiction the surviving record does not resolve. Zion, a billed 7'2" giant, won the title from Reetz at Autumn of Armageddon, the fullest dated PPV the corpus preserves: nine matches, closing on a mystery lights-out angle and a count refereed by GZW's own President, Mike McKane. Zion's own reign ended at Dead Zone, GZW's own Christmas Eve PPV and the corpus's own second-fullest structured show, where Crucyfix won the title in the corpus's own first Crucyfixion Match, strapping Zion to a burning cross after interference from the heel stable Black Sunshine.

Four further title chains are now built end-to-end across the corpus's own three structured shows — Friday Night Fury #1 (GZW's own flagship debut), Autumn of Armageddon, and Dead Zone: the Hardcore Championship runs Sadistic Sam to Jake Smith to Blackbird; the Deathmatch Championship runs Jake Smith to Griz; the Tag Team Championship runs Crucyfix & The Fro to Gargoyle & Hydro; and the United States Championship runs Big Earl to Thane Blackout. The fed's own separately served Champions snapshot page corroborates the Tag Team and United States lines directly, while contradicting the World and Hardcore lines outright — naming Zion and The Hammer, not Crucyfix and Blackbird, as reigning champions. Both contradictions are preserved on each title's own record rather than resolved.

Eight titles ran across GZW's life — Heavyweight, Intercontinental, United States, Regional, Light Heavyweight, Television, Tag Team, and a Women's Championship held by a single wrestler across the whole of its own history, "The Only Women's Champion In GZW's History," before its division closed "due to lack of interest." A heel stable, Black Sunshine, recurs across the fed's dated shows as its own house rivalry engine, unmasking a new recruit, The Lion, live at Dead Zone.

Storylines

Black Sunshine's rise Written by AI, 2026

Black Sunshine recurs across all three of the corpus's own structured shows as GZW's own house rivalry engine, and its own numbers grow show to show. At Autumn of Armageddon, Gargoyle and Hydro retain the Tag Team Championship via DQ after Anarkyst runs in on them, and Dark Arachnid interferes to help Blackbird beat The Lion — the same night the announcers name Blackbird as having turned on The Lion to join the stable outright. By Dead Zone, the stable ends a man's career (Renegade, in a No DQ match against Eturnalwolf), throws Madd Dogg into an Inferno Match's own flames to retain the Tag Team Championship again, and unmasks its own "cloaked man" live on camera as The Lion — his old rival Blackbird's own victim, now recruited into the same stable that cost him the match. President Mike McKane ejects Black Sunshine from the arena mid-show for its own interference in Blackbird's Hardcore Championship win over Jake Smith, only for the stable to run back in at the very end of the night to help Crucyfix dethrone Zion for the World Heavyweight Championship.

The Fro, Reetz, and Rave Written by AI, 2026

The corpus's own commentary places the feud's own origin at Massive Anarchy, GZW's first pay-per-view, where a chair shot from Commissioner Rave cost The Fro his World Title shot and let Above Average Josh Reetz walk away GZW's own actual first World Champion. At Autumn of Armageddon, Rave beats The Fro himself in the undercard, cheating a second time with a low blow and a small package, before serving as special referee for Zion's own World Title win over Reetz later the same night. The rivalry pays off at Dead Zone: Reetz and The Fro fight to a Hell in a Cell for the World Title's own #1 contendership with Rave again the special referee, and Rave's own attempt to raise the cell mid-match backfires when Reetz falls 75 feet into the announce table, handing The Fro the win Rave spent two shows trying to deny him.

Zion and Crucyfix Written by AI, 2026

Zion wins the World Heavyweight Championship from Above Average Josh Reetz at Autumn of Armageddon on a count from President Mike McKane after a mystery lights-out angle. Dead Zone's own commentary places what came next: Zion defended the title successfully at least once more before Crucyfix, an already-injured rival Zion himself put out with a knee injury, returned for one night to settle the score. Their Crucyfixion Match — the corpus's own first — ends with Black Sunshine's interference, a run-in from The Fro, and Crucyfix strapping Zion to a burning cross to become GZW's new World Heavyweight Champion.
