Autumn of Armageddon

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Fireworks, pyro's and a laser-light display go off then the camera pans around a sold-out arena full of sign-waving fans. Then the camera cuts to Jack O'Connell and Lance Haven at the announcers table* Jack O'Connell- Hello everyone and welcome to Autumn of Armageddon! I'm Jack O'Connell with Lance Haven and tonight has become a jam-packed night full of title fights and grudge matches. Lance tell everyone what our card is for tonight's pay-per-view. Lance Haven- Alright then here's what we have for tonight. Furykainne vs Griz (Deathmatch Title) The Lion vs Blackbird Madd Dogg vs Big Earl (US Title) Eturnalwolf vs Anarkyst (Hardcore Rules) The Hammer / The Blade Runner vs Black Sunshine's Gargoyle / Hydro (Tag Titles) Johnny B Badd vs Renegade vs Dark Arachnid vs DevilTress (Deathmatch #1 Contendership) Jake Smith vs Sadistic Sam (Hardcore Title) Commissioner Rave vs The Fro Zion vs Above Average Josh Reetz (World Title) Jack O'Connell- We have an excellent night tonight, now lets get to our first matchup. DEATHMATCH CHAMPION GZW DEATHMATCH CHAMPIONSHIP -Furykainne starts on the offensive with a few punches and kicks. Then Furykainne takes down Griz with a bodyslam and then stomps away at the freshly crowned champion. Then after a few more stomps Furykainne then calls for his finisher. But Griz prevents Furykainne a chance of executing his finisher. Then Griz takes over with punches of his own. Then the fight goes outside were Griz get's thrown into the ring-pole and Furykainne grabs a chair. Griz ducks a fatal chair shot and then grabs the chair from Furykainne and then hits the challenger over the head with it. Furykainne gets up almost unfazed and throws a right hand at Griz, but Griz reverses the punch and executes The Close Griz. The ref checks Furykainne and calls for the bell. Announcer- Winner via TKO and still GZW Deathmatch Champion, Griz! Jack O'Connell- A nice defense for Griz, I hope he turns out to be a true champion. VS Lance Haven- These two superstars have had a grudge ever since their debut in GZW were Blackbird turned on The Lion and joined Black Sunshine. This match should be interesting, Blackbird won the #1 contendership for the Hardcore Title next week on Termination and The Lion is due up for a title shot too, so this match could be good. -The Lion erupts out of his corner with a spear to Blackbird. An enraged Lion starts stomping and kicking Blackbird like a government mule. Then Lion takes down Blackbird with a suplex and then hits a twisting-senton splash to Blackbird. A pumped up Lion then starts jacking-up the crowd, giving Blackbird time to recover. Then Dark Arachnid of Black Sunshine runs to ringside and distracts The Lion. As Lion is distracted with Dark Arachnid, Blackbird grabs The Lion and hits an Evenflow DDT to The Lion and then goes for the pin. The ref counts 1...2...3... Announcer- Winner via pinfall, Blackbird! -Then suddenly Johnny B Badd comes out of the crowd and jumps Blackbird and kicks and stomps Blackbird until Dark Arachnid scares him outta the ring. Johnny B Badd then helps The Lion out of the ring quickly and runs to the locker room. Jack O'Connell- Speaking of grudges, Blackbird and B Badd have had a grudge lately too. Lance Haven- This next match outta put speed against strength. VS GZW UNITED STATES CHAMPION GZW UNITED STATES CHAMPIONSHIP -Madd Dogg quickly gets out of his corner and hits a clothesline on Big Earl. The US champ quickly gets up and clobbers Madd Dogg with a powerfull clothesline of his own. Big Earl then starts to stomp away at the light-heavyweight rookie not allowing him to get up. Eventually Madd Dogg did get up and irish whiped Big Earl into the corner and speared him on his way out. Madd Dogg then went for a dragon-attack from the top rope but Big Earl dodged Madd Dogg's attack and Madd Dogg fell smack on the canvas. Then Big Earl picked up the challenger and hit the Last Stop and went for the pin and the ref counted 1...2...3... Announcer- Winner by pinfall and still GZW US Champion, Big Earl! Jack O'Connell- Well I guess Madd Dogg still isnt up to the big-boys league yet, he's still a rookie, he shouldn't let frustration get to him yet. Our next matchup also should be a barn-burner in a hardcore rules matchup. VS HARDCORE RULES -Anarkyst comes out fighting as the bell rings and hits a hook kick to Eturnalwolf. Anarkyst then climbs the ropes early and hits a flying lariat to Eturnalwolf. Then the Black Sunshine music hit and the other 4 members of Black Sunshine run to the ring and proceed to attack Anarkyst. Jack O'Connell- This isnt fair! All 5 members are attacking 1 man! Lance Haven- But this will prove something else, Jack. Anarkyst said himself he would only fight with Crucyfix, he has no other friends in the back and nobody will help him. He might have had this coming. -Eturnalwolf picks up Anarkyst and delivers the Beyond The Shadows to Anarkyst while the other Black Sunshine members watch in humor as Anarkyst gets beaten up. Then Eturnalwolf goes for the pin and the ref counts 1...2...3... Announcer- Winner by pinfall, Eturnalwolf! Jack O'Connell- You were right Lance, scary I never thought I would say that, Anarkyst has no friends in the back. He might have brought this Black Sunshine attack upon himself. Speaking of Black Sunshine, Gargoyle and Hydro defend their titles NEXT. GZW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS GZW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP -Hammer and Blade Runner dominate the first minute of the match by double-teaming Black Sunshine and keeping Hydro away from his corner and taging in Gargoyle. Then suddenly Anarkyst's music hits and Anarkyst runs to the ring and gives Hydro an Anarchy-Bomb and then gives an Anarchy-Bomb to Gargoyle. The ref quickly calls for the bell. Announcer- Winners via DQ and still GZW Tag Team Champions, Gargoyle & Hydro, Black Sunshine! -Hammer and Blade Runner then approach Anarkyst asking him why he did that. Anarkyst says nothing and turns around and looks like he's going to leave but suddenly turns around and clotheslines Hammer and gives an Anarchy bomb to Blade Runner, then leaves like nothing happened. Lance Haven- *Laughing uncontrolably* WHA HA HA! W-w-well I guess Anarkyst got payback on Black Sunshine, but just seeing Anarkyst take out Hammer and Blade Runner, that just cracks me up... WHA HA HA HA! Jack O'Connell- While Lance gains back his sanity, I'll tell you that the Deathmatch #1 Contendership is next. DARK ARACHNID DEVILTRESS -The match starts out with Renegade locking up with Dark Arachnid and Johnny B Badd throws DevilTress out of the ring. Then Johnny B Badd and Renegade double-teamed Dark Arachnid. Meanwhile outside the ring DevilTress was searching for something underneath the ring. Then DevilTress pulls out a small pocket knife and enters the ring. Deviltress then puts blood-drawing cuts on the arms of the other 3 wrestlers and the ref calls for the bell. Jack O'Connell- Err... eh... what? Why did the bell ring? Announcer- Winner by first blood, DevilTress. Lance Haven- Oh come on! Even if first blood is a part of a deathmatch that's pure and simply cheep. *DevilTress exits to a sea of boos* DEATHMATCH #1 CONTENDERSHIP Jack O'Connell- Well our next match will involve blood, but its not a way to win. The hardcore title match is next. GZW HARDCORE CHAMPION GZW HARDCORE CHAMPIONSHIP -The champion sharts out with a crossover clothesline to Jake Smith. Jake fights back with a few left and rights and a clothesline of this own. The two then start slugging it out against each other and Jake Smith sends Sadistic Sam to the outside of the ring. Then Sadistic Sam grabs a chair and re-enters the ring and almost takes Jake's head off with the chair. Sam then hits a few elbow drops and then backs up and looks like he's going to go for a spear. Jake gets up and Sam charges at him, but Jake apparently see's the spear coming and kicks Sam in the gut and quickly hits the Twist of Doom. Lance Haven- We could have a new Hardcore Champ! -Jake goes for the cover and the ref counts 1.. . 2 . . . 3... Announcer- Winner and NEW GZW Hardcore Champion, Jake Smith! Jack O'Connell- Wow! What a win for Jake Smith! Jake becomes the second Hardcore Champion and the first GZW Wrestler to hold more then one title in his GZW career. Now the former Deathmatch champ is now the Hardcore champ. Lance Haven- Now lets go to our first match of our double-main event. I've been waiting to see this match, the beginning of this feud started back at Massive Anarchy when Rave screwed The Fro out of his GZW World Title shot. VS Jack O'Connell- You know, Rave better not get too hurt in this match, he's the self-appointed special referee for the Zion vs Reetz - World Title Match which is after this match. -The Fro runs out of his corner like a madman and spears Rave. The Fro then starts stomping a mudhole the size of Idaho on Rave. Then after a couple of mighty punches The Fro connects a superplex from the top-rope. Then The Fro calls the fans for The Fro Bomb. The Fro picks up Rave and hits The Fro Bomb and the crowd goes completly wild. But then Above Average Josh Reetz runs to ringside and distracts The Fro before The Fro can go for the pin. As Reetz and The Fro exchange comments at each other, Rave sneeks up behind The Fro and hits a low-blow and quickly puts The Fro in a small package and the ref counts 1...2...3... Announcer- Winner by pinfall, Commissioner Rave! Lance Haven- Well The Fro got what he had coming to him. Jack O'Connell- I'd like to hear ya explain that Lance, The Fro just got screwed out of that match. If Reetz didn't go to ringside The Fro would have gotten the pin. Lance Haven- The Fro deserved to lose, and you know what! The World Title match is next! VS GZW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION GZW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP special referee: Commissioner Rave Jack O'Connell- If this match goes anything like the cage match we saw between these two a while ago, well tonight might just very well be a war. But I dont trust Commissioner Rave, he's a major wild card in this matchup. -The match starts out with Zion and Reetz exchanging punches and moves. Both wrestlers appear at the top of their game tonight. Reetz then hits a flying shoulder block and then slaps on a lion tamer to Zion. Zion escapes the hold and then clotheslines Reetz, and then Zion puts a submission hold of his own onto the champ. Lance Haven- Wow, both wrestlers really do appear to be ready for this match. -Zion and Reetz then have a test of strength and Zion competly overpowers Josh Reetz. After Zion sends Reetz to the canvas with a powerbomb Zion begins to stomp away at Reetz. But the stomping is cut short by Commissioner Rave who orders Zion to stop it. Then Reetz quickly puts Zion in a small package and Rave gets down for the count and coutns 1-2-Kickout! Jack O'Connell- Oh come on! That was an extremely fast count! Damnit Rave's going to stack the deck against Zion like he did to The Fro back at Massive Anarchy. -Zion stares at Rave for a second and Reetz hits a low blow to Zion. Reetz then hits a clothesline to Zion and then climbs the ropes and signals for the 4-Star-Frog-Splash, and perfectly executes it. Reetz goes for the pin and Rave coutns 1-2-kickout, another fast count. Zion then starts fighting back and then hits a chokeslam to Reetz. Zion then climbs the ropes and hits a flying clothesline to Reetz as he gets up. Then Zion looks at the crowd and calls for the Destruction. Lance Haven- Oh boy, looks like Zion's going to finish this match. -Zion then grabs Reetz and sets him up for the Destruction. Zion holds Reetz up in the air for a second and then executes the Destruction. Zion then goes for the pin and referee Rave counts 1........2......... then Rave stops his count and runs to the outside and grabs a chair and then runs back to the inside and clobbers Zion with the chair. Reetz gets back up and then Zion gets back up a little dazed and Rave goes for another swing with the chair. -But before Rave hits the second chair shot the lights go black in the arena and there is a really quick flash of red light and when the lights return to normal Josh Reetz, Zion, and Commissioner Rave are all out cold in the ring. Jack O'Connell- Who the? What the? They're all out! *Jack looks to his right* OH MY GOD! LANCE IS OUT TOO! WHAT THE F*CK HAPPENED! -Zion starts moving in the ring and starts to crawl over to the still out-cold Reetz. Zion then places one arm over Reetz but there is no ref to make the count. Then suddenly President Mike McKane runs to the ring in a referee outfit and then gets into the ring and counts 1...2...3... Jack O'Connell- President McKane just made the count for Zion!!! Zion wins! Zion wins! Announcer- Winner and NEW GZW World Heavyweight Champion... ZION! -President McKane hands Zion the belt and raises his hand in victory. Jack O'Connell- Wow what a night! New champions crowned and Reetz finally got some payback! GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE AND STAY TUNED FOR TERMINATION THIS TUESDAY NIGHT! *Camera goes black* (GZW IS IN NO WAY RIGGED OR BIAS, THANK YOU FOR WATCHING)
