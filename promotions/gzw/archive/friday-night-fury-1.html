Friday Night Fury #1

*A minute long display of fireworks and pyro's hit and then the camera pans around a completly full Ground Zero Wrestling Arena then the camera cuts to Lance Haven and Jack O'Connell at the announcers table* Lance Haven- Welcome everybody to Friday Night Fury. I'm Lance Haven and along side me is Jack O'Connell and boy ladies and gentlemen do we have a show for you tonight. Jack O'Connell- Boy do we ever! 3 of the 5 GZW Championships will be crowned tonight and 2 opening round matches of the GZW World Title Tournament will be held tonight. Here's our card for tonight. EturnalWolf vs Sadistic Sam vs Above Average Josh Reetz (Hardcore Title Mach) Jake Smith vs Gargoyle (DeathMatch Title) Renegade vs Big Earl - World Title Tournament Matchup, Round 1 DevilTress vs Hydro - World Title Tournament Matchup, Round 1 Madd Dogg & Geno vs Crucyfix and The Fro (Tag Team Titles) Lance Haven- Wow, all of these matches look like blockbusters tonight. Now lets get to our first match. HARDCORE TITLE *Freak on a Leash by Korn hits and Eturnalwolf slowly walks to the ring* Lance Haven- Eturnalwolf is fresh of a tag team win with Renegade, he's got momentum going into this matchup. *Hold My Own by Biohazard hits and Sadistic Sam comes to the ring with a mixed reaction from the fans* Jack O'Connell- It sounds like the fans cant decide either to like Sam or hate him. Sadistic Sam has lost the 2 matches he's been in but I'd put my money on him cause he might just be due up for a win. *Bad Blood by Papa Roach hits and Above Average Josh Reetz walks to ring with a great ovation from the crowd* Jack O'Connell- It sounds like the fans are behind Reetz in this matchup. Well, the bell has rung to lets get to the action! -Sadistic Sam runs towards Reetz but Reetz telegraphed Sam and body pressed him and then dropped Sam like a bag of potatoes. Then Reetz slapped a sleeper on EturnalWolf. After Wolf escaped the sleeper and Sam regained his feet Sadistic Sam put a sleeper hold onto EturnalWolf. Eturnalwolf then went for a kick to Sam but Sam caught Wolf's foot. Sam thinking he had Wolf started to look around sadisticly only to get an insiguri by Wolf.  But then Above Average Josh Reetz hammered EturnalWolf with a right hand and then Sam and Reetz started to stomp away on Eturnalwolf.  Then while Wolf was down Sadistic Sam challenged Josh Reetz to a test of strength and Sadistic Sam overpowered Reetz and sent the Above Average one to the ground. Lance Haven- Looks like Sadistic Sam is dominating the matchup. -Then Eturnalwolf got up and put a sharpshooter onto Josh Reetz but Reetz refused to give out. Then Sadistic Sam grabbed Eturnalwolf and threw him out of the ring then Sam and Wolf started exchanging hands outside of the ring. Then Josh Reetz regained his feet, ran across the ring and did a flip over the ropes taking out both men on the outside! Lance Haven- Wow! Sweet lord Reetz just put his body on the line to go for a title! -Then all 3 wrestlers returned to the ring and started beating the daylights out of each other. Sadistic Sam them haymakered Reetz out of the ring. While Reetz was out of the ring Sadistic Sam slapped on another sleeper hold on EturnalWolf, EturnalWolf took most of the pain during the match and was forced to submit. Announcer- Winner by submission and NEW GZW Hardcore Champion, Sadistic Sam! Jack O'Connell- I told you Sadistic Sam was due for a win and this win was a big one! Lance Haven- That was an excellent example of hardcore in that matchup. And Sadistic Sam I think proved that he is not only sadistic... but hardcore. *Camera cuts to The Fro's locker room were The Fro is lacing up his boots preparing for tonights main event. Then Crucyfix walks into the locker room and the two stare holes into each other. Then Crucyfix speaks* Crucyfix- Fro you and I have been down this path before. But dont fail me like you did in the past, ok? I dont care if we're The Devils Senate, all I want is that tag team gold you got that? Madd Dogg and Geno are new but they're skilled non the less so we have to give it what we got. The Fro- Crucyfix, I'm alot better then I was in EHW and CHWF. I want that gold too, but I'm more concerned about the World Title Tournament. I'll keep my end of the deal so you keep yours got it. Crucyfix- Yeah. *The Fro and Crucyfix shake hands and Crucyfix leaves. Then the camera cuts back to Lance Haven and Jack O'Connell* Lance Haven- Well I guess the respect AND rivalry between those two still burns hard. But if they can stay as a unit they'll win later tonight. But now lets move on to another title fight. DEATHMATCH TITLE *Cactus Jack's music hits and Jake Smith comes to the ring with a crowd full of boo's* Jack O'Connell- The fans certainly dont like Jake Smith. But I wonder what they'll think of his opponent! *Enter Sandman by Metallica hits and Gargoyle walks to the ring with an even larger response of boo's* Lance Haven- That answeres that question. Jack O'Connell- Now GZW has its own version of the deathmatch. The only way a person can win is by either knockout or drawing first blood. So I would think this match would greatly favor Gargoyle because of his size and pure brawn. -Jake starts off and insiguri's Gargoyle. Then Gargoyle challenges Jake to a test of strength and Jake won it! Then while Gargoyle was down Jake ran off and got a chair. He then ran into the ring and completly LEVELED Gargoyle with the chair. Lance Haven- Hey! Hey Jack loot at Gargoyle's mask!!! Blood's coming from it! I think Jake Smith just beet Gargoyle!!! -The ref goes and looks at Gargoyle's mask then asks for the bell. Announcer- Winner by first blood in 43 seconds and NEW GZW Deathmatch champion Jake Smith! Jack O'Connell- Wow what an upset. But lets quickly move on to our next matchup, Lance give them the breakdown. Lance Haven- Ok, the World Title tournament is an 8 man tournament that will span from tonight up to our first pay-per-view Massive Anarchy on November 5th. 4 of the 8 wrestlers name's have been released. As of right now we know that Renegade, Big Earl, DevilTress, and Hydro are going to participate in the tournament. I believe that at Tuesday Night Armageddon we will find out the remaining 4 competitors, and if not Tuesday we WILL know all 8 wrestlers by next week's Fury. And now lets go to our first match of the tournament, Renegade vs Big Earl. *Here I Go Again by Whitesnake hits and Renegade comes to the ring with a fan-favorite ovation* Lance Haven- Renegade looks focused *Turn the Page by Metallica hits and the crowds cheers instantly turn to boo's as Big Earl makes his way to the ring* -Big Earl and Renegade began be exchanging right hands. Then Big Earl delivered a torture rack-pancake to Renegade. Big Earl then dominated the matchup for about a minute with headbutts and low-blows. Then after a mighty right hand by Big Earl, Renegade was busted open. Lance Haven- Things aren't looking good for Renegade. -Then Renegade must have gotten frustrated by not dominating the match got a surge of confidence and speared Big Earl.  But the spear seemingly did nothing to Big Earl and Big Earl returned to being in control of the match.  Big Earl delivered a mighty german suplex to Renegade and followed up with an elbow drop. Then Renegade started to come back and connected with alot of haymakers and then took down Big Earl with a scissor kick.  After Big Earl was sent to the canvas Renegade picked up Big Earl and signalled for the Falling Off The Barstool. Jack O'Connell- If Renegade hits the Falling Off The Barstool this match could be over! -Renegade then grappled Big Earl and perfectly executed the Falling Off The Barstool. Renegade then went for the pin and 1...2...3... Announcer- Winner and moving to second round... RENEGADE! Lance Haven- Wow what an amazing comback by Renegade seemingly coming out of the darkness to crush Big Earl. Our next match is also a tournament matchup. *Kane's music hits and the female wonder DevilTress slowly walks to the ring with a mostly silent crowd* Lance Haven- Apparently the fans are either awed at the site of DevilTress, or are going to go take a crap cause they think this match is going to be ALLLLLLL Hydro. *Grease Paint and Monkey Brains by White Zombie hits and Hydro comes to the ring with a slightly mixed reaction* Jack O'Connell- Apparently the crowd doesn't know weither to like this guy or not. -DevilTress came storming out of the corner with a body splash onto Hydro. The shocked Hydro then got up only to be taken back down by one of DevilTress's feet. Then DevilTress picked up Hydro and did some sort of a dance while Hydro was dizzy then clotheslined Hydro. Then DevilTress became more serious and signalled for her finisher! Then DevilTress grabbed Hydro and delievered a 3/4 Turning Neckbreaker to Hydro. Jack O'Connell- DevilTress calls that move the Bedazzled Bomb! Can she get the pin though? Wait... whats she doing? -DevilTress picks up Hydro again and delivers a second Bedazzled Bomb and the crowd starts coming to their feet cheering. Then DevilTress goes for the pin and 1...2...3... Announcer- Winner and moving onto second round... DEVILTRESS Lance Haven- Wow another upset tonight! DevilTress defeats Hydro and moves on to the second round. And now we've come to our main event... The tag team titles will be decided NEXT! But first a commerical break. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Voice- "What man will win the gold of GZW? It will take blood, sweat, detirmination... and the ability to live with in anarchy! GZW Presents "Massive Anarchy"! Live Sunday, November 5th on Pay Per View ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Lance Haven- Now here comes our main event!!! TAG TEAM TITLES *Awake by Godsmake hits and Madd Dogg and Geno make their way to the ring with a tremendoes ovation from the crowd. Jack O'Connell- These 2 are fresh off a tag team win and these high-fliers are no-questions-asked fan favorites. *Voodoo by Godsmack hits and the crowds ovation exceeds that of the previous ovation as Crucyfix slowly makes his way to the ring* Lance Haven- Crucyfix is a wrestling legend and you can tell by the fans. *Wait and Bleed by Slipknot hits and the entire crowd explodes as The Fro makes his way to the ring* Lance Haven- And here comes The Fro! No need to question if The Fro is possibly the biggest fan favorite in GZW right now, and I think his nickname "The Peoples Answer" really does fit him. -Madd Dogg starts out with a back breaker to Crucyfix. Crucyfix then tags in The Fro who goes after Madd Dogg but gets a knee to the gut. Madd Dogg then takes The Fro and delivers an amazing bulldog driving The Fro's face into the mat. Then The Fro regains his feet and takes down Madd Dogg with an insiguri. Madd Dogg got back up as quick as The Fro did but Madd Dogg didn't prepare for The Fro's powerfull spear. The Fro then made his way back to the corner and tagged in Crucyfix. Crucyfix instantly kept on going were The Fro left off by giving Madd Dogg multiple elbow drops. Madd Dogg then got up and tagged in Geno and Geno took on Crucyfix in a test of strength. While the test of strength was progressing in the ring, outside of the ring The Fro and Madd Dogg were fighting like hell-bent soldiers. The Fro took down Madd Dogg then grabbed his trademark 2x4 and leveled Madd Dogg with it, but the match wasn't ruled a DQ because the ref was busy with Crucyfix and Geno who were still in the test of strength. Jack O'Connell- Wow this is an amazing tag team match. -Geno then won the test of strength against Crucyfix. Geno then hits a reverse DDT on Crucyfix. Then Geno tags in Madd Dogg and Crucyfix barely manages to tag in The Fro. Lance Haven- Tags on both sides and 2 fresh men come into the match and look at them go! -The Fro and Madd Dogg exchange powerful punches but Madd Dogg couldn't shake off all of the punches that The Fro delivered and Madd Dogg finally went crashing to the mat. The Fro then picked up Madd Dogg and delivered his finisher The Fro Bomb! Lance Haven- There it is! The Fro Bomb in the center of the ring! -The Fro then centered Madd Dogg in the ring and then signalled for his trademark move The People's Answer! The Fro took off his elbowpad and tossed it aside, ran from rope to rope and did The People's Answer and the crowd went wild and The Fro covered Madd Dogg and the ref counted 1...2...3...! Jack O'Connell- ITS OVER! ITS OVER! THE FRO AND CRUCYFIX WIN THE TITLES! Announcer- Winners by pin and NEW GZW World Tag Team Champions... Crucyfix and The Fro! Lance Haven- This was an excellent display or wrestling by all 4 wrestlers, but the rookies couldnt beet the team of The Fro and Crucyfix and now The Fro and Crucyfix have claimed the GZW Tag Team Titles!!! Jack O'Connell- Thats all the time we have for this debut of Friday Night Fury and what a way to end it! I'm Jack O'Connell and for Lance Haven and GZW, GOOD NIGHT! *Camera goes black with the last image seen is The Fro and Crucyfix celebrating with the Tag Team Gold and a crowd that has gone wild* (GZW IS IN NO WAY BIAS OR RIGGED, THANK YOU FOR WATCHING)
