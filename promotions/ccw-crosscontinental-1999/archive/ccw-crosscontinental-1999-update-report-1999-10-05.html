CROSS CONTINENTAL WRESTLING INVITATIONAL UPDATE REPORT CURRENT NEWS: Current signings include several regional and world champions from the world of professional wrestling, as well as up and coming superstars. The federation is looking to also sign some light weight talent to fill out it’s roster as well…. And rumor has it the first Supercard will determine the CCW
Championship Title holder.
 The first CCW Championship Title holder was decided on October 3rd of the year 1999. His name is Da’ J-Man. In a battle royal, the same night, Aaron
“The Bruiser” Stone was crowned the first Television Champion.
 Travis Wade has accepted sponsorship from several companies and will be working closely with other independent orginizations with a hope for some talent trade agreements. This could bring CCW wrestlers to other venues, as well as more talent into the CCW.
 Many CCW stars have so far refused to make comments in regards to their goals in the CCW so far. There seems to be very little fire and excitement in regards to the television title Battle Royal, and President Travis Wade has opened negotiations with other orginizations for a talent trade agreement.
 Tuulenux has replaced his brother, Dr. Hate, in his contractual duties here in the CCW. However, President Travis Wade had decided to replace Hates slot position in the Championship Title Tournament with well known fan favorite, Da’ J-Man instead of allowing Tuulenux to compete. Tuulenux made a great showing for himself in the Battle Royal, however, and could be a future contender.
 NEED MORE TALENT!!! There are still slots open for this federation. Please, if you know of people who want to join or would like to join, have them sign up ASAP.
 Visit http// ewzone.virtualave.net and vote for CCW as one of the top Federations! EWZone is well known E-Wrestling Page with reviews and connections, and is a great way to get your wrestlers noticed on a broader scale. It is also a great way for federations to gain new membership and interest.
 The Second Supercard has been announced for December!!! No matches have been signed, but President Travis Wade is letting people know that this will be a December to Dismember???
 ROLEPLAYS are a big determination in Pushes. If you don’t roleplay, you will not win. You will be made fun of by the announcers for your Muteness, and at long last, you will become jobbers. If you do not roleplay within a full month, you will be gone from the organization.
 AWMC Loudmouth, The Prince, has called out members of the CCW to come to
Detroit after the brief tirade from fan favorite color announcer, David
Wilson. Wade is expected to make a showing for himself at one of the future
AWMC cards, though he has stated publicly that he fully respects and appreciates the hard work of the AWMC. He has no intention of pulling off an invasion…. But people in the back offices of both promotions are starting to talk.

OOCNOTE UPDATE This is an independent organization with very low funding. Large entrances will not be done… the most any wrestler can hope for is maybe a few strobes, and a dimmer on the light switch. Please keep this in mind when you are reading the cards and wonder why I may have altered your entrances.
Another note, there will be no breaking Kayfabe. No “smart” remarks are to be made. No reference to “Jobbers”, “Jobbing”, “Marks”, “Smarks”, “Title
Pushes”, or contractual discussions are to be made during Roleplays. Chances are, the most any of our wrestlers will be paid will be about five hundred a night, the least being about fifty dollars a night. There are also several curse words that will NEVER be allowed in roleplays. They are as followed…
FUCK, COCK, PUSSY, CUNT, ASS, SHIT. Please, keep it clean guys. I don’t want to go through all of your Roleplays, editing it. You’ll note that “Bitch” is allowed… regular television allows it… I will also allow Jack-ass, simply because television also allows that. Otherwise, no potty-mouths!
ALL ROLEPLAYS will be done on the card, in the format of either an interview by Brian Morgan, David Wilson, or a few comments you make with a video camera. Again, we don’t have the budget for special effects, and I’m not running things that seem expensive to run.
