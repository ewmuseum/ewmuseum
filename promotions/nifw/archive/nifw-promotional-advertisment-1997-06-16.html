NIFW Promotional Advertisment (New Federation)

16 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens onto an empty wrestling ring in an empty arena. You hear the theme for Terminator II begin to play in the background. As the camera zooms out from the view of the wrestling ring, you start to hear the voiceover, and see some short video clips of wrestling events...] Voiceover: The squared-circle... [Switch to 5 quick video clips in a row of men getting slammed, clothes-lined, etc..] Voiceover: The place where stars are born... [A clip of a wrestler perfectly executing a flying cross-body block from the top rope. As he lands on his opponet, the referee counts 1-2-3, and the crowd pops!] Voiceover: The place where agony is defined... [Another short clip of one wrestler in a cloverleaf-submission that is being executed by another wrestler. The crowd boos as the man that is in the cloverleaf is screaming in pain, with sweat pouring off his face...] Voiceover: And the place, where champions are made! [A clip of a wrestler down on his knees holding a championship belt high in the air. The crowd cheers loudly as you can see tears of joy running down the man's face.] Voiceover: But this fall, there will be a NEW place where this all happens... [As the theme from Terminator II dies out, you see a screen full of t.v.'s that are all showing different wrestling clips. All of a sudden, the screen explodes as "Kickstart My Heart" by Motley Crue plays, as the federation logo appears on the screen...] Voiceover: New...Internet...Fantasy...Wrestling.... [The music stops, as all of the clips rewind at a super-fast speed, and the all you see is a black screen with the words "New Internt Fantasy Wrestling" in silver.] Voiceover: The NEW force in fantasy wrestling!
