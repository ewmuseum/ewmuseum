PWG Press Conference Details

23 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-A press conference was held earlier today concerning various matters pertaining to Pro Wrestling Glory. There was a strong turn out for PWG at this event, which can only mean great things for all of the members of the Pro Wrestling Glory Family. President Corey Collins (pictured, at the podium) led the ceremonies and dropped a huge news piece on the waiting reporters. It is now official - PWG Legends Are Born will take place July 13, 2004! Whether more matches are added to the card or it remains as it is now remains to be seen, but a date has been set and Pro Wrestling Glory is ready to get underway! Collins also made mention of matchless newcomer Emo Kid Joe, stating that he will be placed in the match featuring "Blockbuster" CJ Rowell and "The Thai Land Murderer" Wreck, making it a three way dance. This, however, was not all. Collins announced that this match would be the first of many Scramble Relay matches. The guidelines for this match are as follows: only two men involved in the match will wrestle at a time. These two men will compete for a time of five minutes, and after that five minutes is up, the other wrestler would come into the match, while one of the original two steps out. This will create a highly competitive atmosphere, as wrestlers will only have a limited time to put away their opponent before time is up. The match will be wrestled one fall to a finish, with no eliminations. -Collins went on to introduce PWG's play-by-play man Ron Martin (pictured, sitting right) and color commentator Vincent Coltrane (pictured, sitting left). Both men shared their thoughts on PWG, as well as the trip they will be making to Japan for the Super Japan Pro Wrestling match between "Skyhigh" Bobby Skylark and "The American Champion" Chip Masters. The match takes place on June 26th, and is just one match of many on what will surely be an outstanding event. -Collins concluded the press conference by stating that in the near future, details would arise about the Pro Wrestling Glory Heavyweight Title, such as when the first champion will be crowned and in what type of event it will take place, as well as unveiling the belt to the public for the first time. -For more on Pro Wrestling Glory and all of its happenings, check the website often! -- Corey Collins .... now with TWO gold stars! http://www.geocities.com/pwglory
