Pro Wrestling Glory - Looking For Members

21 July 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pro Wrestling Glory just came off of their first show, Legends Are Born, last Tuesday. The video release hit last night, and we've got another big show coming up on August 23 called Smashed Into Pieces. In September we begin the hunt for the PWG Heavyweight Title at Road To Glory. I would love to see some new faces heat things up in Pro Wrestling Glory. We have a lot of great, active members who are running some pretty good programs right now, but I'd love to see even more, especially in the tag team division. Pro Wrestling Glory was born on May 8th, 2004. Its home base is around Chicago, Illinois. You will find that it is clearly inspired by Ring of Honor in many ways. Basically what PWG is, is an e-fed attempting to be as realistic as possible. Instead of opening up and playing to tens of thousands of fans, we're starting at the bottom and will have to work our way up. We will present angles and storylines in real life situations. No roleplay boards or anything like that. The only place things happen is on our shows, no matter how few or how many there are. We are a professional wrestling organization - not sports entertainment. We present real and lifelike angles, not soap opera storylines. We will not house ridiculous "evil owner" storylines. The owner and officers of this league will not come into play at all in an on-screen role. The only place the owner matters is creating cards, putting up results, and maintaining the website. Pro Wrestling Glory is a league for the wrestlers and by the wrestlers, and we believe that all of the focus should be on them and nobody else. There is not a whole lot to say about PWG. Join it, compete, and most of all, have fun. Pro Wrestling Glory will run shows once a month in Illinois. Eventually we hope to run more shows, as well as travel outside of Illinois to bring PWG to a much wider fanbase. If you'd like to join, or just check it out, go to: http://www.geocities.com/pwglory Thanks! -- [name withheld] http://www.geocities.com/pwglory
