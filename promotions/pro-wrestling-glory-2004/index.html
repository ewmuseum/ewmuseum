Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Pro Wrestling Glory was born, in its own stated words, on 8 May 2004 out of Chicago, Illinois, pitched as “clearly inspired by Ring of Honor” and built around realism rather than spectacle: “We are a professional wrestling organization — not sports entertainment... We will not house ridiculous 'evil owner' storylines.” Its own founding statement promised the front office would stay off camera entirely, existing only to book cards, post results and run the website, with every bit of focus reserved for the wrestlers.

That promise didn't survive contact with its own build-up: a press conference on 23 June 2004 staged the promotion's own president presiding at a podium, confirming a date for the promotion's first show and unveiling a new match type, the Scramble Relay, wrestled two at a time with a fresh man cycling in every five minutes. The same event introduced Pro Wrestling Glory's own broadcast team, play-by-play man Ron Martin and color commentator Vincent Coltrane, and previewed a Pro Wrestling Glory Heavyweight Title still to come.

That first show, Legends Are Born, ran 13 July 2004 out of Skate West in Crest Hill, Illinois, opening on the promotion's first-ever tag match and closing on a four-way Scramble Relay main event won by Adrian Lauderdale over a wrestler billed only as Dune. Its own house rule, “The Pillars of Glory,” bound wrestlers to shake hands before a match began, honored even by a heel opening the card with a reluctant handshake before turning it into a takedown.

A video release of that show followed within the week, alongside word of a second show, Smashed Into Pieces, booked for 23 August, and a title chase, Road To Glory, set to begin that September and crown the promotion's first Heavyweight Champion. A recruitment post the same week called for more talent, particularly for a tag team division the promotion was still building out.

The last document in the surviving record, a recruitment post dated 8 January 2005, still describes an active roster and a planned tag division underway. Whether the Heavyweight Title was ever crowned, and how the promotion ended, isn't in the surviving record.
