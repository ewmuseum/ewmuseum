Grizzly:"I hereby lay down an open contract to any men, to fight myself and Darklon, the original Destruction Crew, the New one consists of Darklon and SUICIDE, at Saturday's card."
Sexy Boy Scott Franklin:"Oh my goodness gracious, Steve "Helen's big brother" Keller, wants a piece of the Sexy boy, so be it. Tonight Keller, you will be going down, there's no way in fucking hell that I'm going to let you beat me. I'm going to pound the fucking shit out of your sorry little ass. Lights Out baby!!"
SUICIDE:"JH, or whoever the champ is as of Saturday, I hereby challenge you to a fight , for the title. I was the first man to wear that title, and I will be the next man to wear it. I went through hell for that belt, and I'll go through hell to get it back. And as for Chicken Hawk, next Wednsday you and me in the strap match, bring it on motherfucker!!!"
