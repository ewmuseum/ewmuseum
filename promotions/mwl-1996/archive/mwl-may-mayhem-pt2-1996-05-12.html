Mayhem Wrestling League — Card

[MWL] May Mayhem

12 May 1996 &mdash; Kingdome, Seattle, WA
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens) McDonald: And we're back!! And onwards to the tournament!! Ring Announcer: First, from Manchaca, Texas...he weighs 575 lbs...he is Suicide!! Ring Announcer: And his opponent, from Parts Unknown..he weighs 280 lbs..he is Dangerous Kid Cruise!!
Dangerous Kid Cruise executes a football tackle on the floor. Dangerous Kid Cruise reenters the ring. Suicide follows him back in. Dangerous Kid Cruise throws Suicide out of the ring. James Beard counts: one, two, three, four, five, Suicide reenters the ring. Dangerous Kid Cruise goes for a reverse neckbreaker, but Suicide blocks it. Suicide catches him in a belly-to-belly suplex. James Beard counts: One, two, shoulder up. Suicide executes a backbreaker. Suicide executes the Death Drop and goes for the pin. James Beard counts: One, two, three. The winner is Suicide. Time of match: 0:09:29 Ring Announcer: And the winner of the match, is Suicide!!!!!
McDonald: And Suicide advances in the tournament!! Wrobleski: That was a good match, but I'm still the champ! Ring Announcer: This is another first round matchup in the TV Tournament!! Ring Announcer: First, from Harlem, New York...weighing 285 lbs..he is "Paralyzer" Patrick Elliot!! Ring Announcer: And his opponent, representing the WeWA...he is from Parts Unknown, and he weighs 344 lbs...he is Hack Myers!!
Paralyzer Patrick Elliot executes a back heel kick on the floor. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot reenters the ring. Hack Myers follows him back in. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot takes him down with an enzuigiri. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot takes him down with a belly-to-back suplex. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot goes for a German suplex, but Hack Myers blocks it. Hack Myers executes a lariat. Hack Myers whips Paralyzer Patrick Elliot into the ropes. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot and Hack Myers get hit with a double clothesline. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot nails him with a forearm to the back. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot executes a thrust kick to the head. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot executes a back heel kick. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot whips Hack Myers into the turnbuckle, but Hack Myers reverses it. Hack Myers runs shoulder-first into the corner, but Paralyzer Patrick Elliot moves out of the way.
Paralyzer Patrick Elliot nails him with a belly-to-back suplex. Hack Myers takes him down with a single-leg takedown. Hack Myers nails him with a kick to the midsection. Hack Myers executes a flying clothesline. Hack Myers goes for a DDT, but Paralyzer Patrick Elliot counters it with a backdrop. In turn, Hack Myers counters it with a sunset flip. James Beard counts: One, two, kickout. Hack Myers nails him with a jawbreaker. Hack Myers goes for a kneedrop, but Paralyzer Patrick Elliot counters it with a roll away. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot goes for an avalanche, but Hack Myers counters it with a kick to the head.
Hack Myers whips Paralyzer Patrick Elliot into the ropes. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot misses with a clothesline. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot executes a sleeperhold. Hack Myers reaches the ropes after being trapped for 4 seconds. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot takes him down with a German suplex. James Beard counts: One, shoulder up. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot takes him down with a sleeperhold. Hack Myers reaches the ropes after being trapped for 3 seconds. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot goes for the The Crippler, but he can't do it. Hack Myers nails him with a jawbreaker. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot goes for a German suplex, but Hack Myers counters it with an elbowsmash.
Hack Myers takes him down with an elbowsmash. Hack Myers whips Paralyzer Patrick Elliot into the ropes. Hack Myers misses with a shoulderblock. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot hits Hack Myers with a clothesline. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot whips Hack Myers into the turnbuckle. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot takes him down with an avalanche. Paralyzer Patrick Elliot goes for a belly-to-back suplex, but Hack Myers counters it with an elbowsmash.
Hack Myers nails him with a roundhouse right. Hack Myers executes a back suplex. Hack Myers executes a flying kneedrop. Hack Myers nails him with a DDT. Hack Myers executes the Death Drop and goes for the pin. James Beard counts: One, two, three. The winner is Hack Myers. Time of match: 0:14:00 Ring Announcer: The winner of the match, is Hack Myers!!!
McDonald: And Myers goes on in the tournament.. Wrobleski: <Snoring> Ring Announcer: And another first round matchup for the TV Title Shot...
Ring Announcer: First, from Parts Unknown, weighing 244 lbs..he is Christian North!! Ring Announcer: And his opponent, weighing 234 lbs...he is Cancer!! Christian North goes for a snap mare, but Cancer blocks it. Cancer goes for a double underhook piledriver, but Christian North counters it with a backdrop.
Christian North nails him with a double chickenwing submission. Cancer submits immediately. The winner is Christian North. Time of match: 0:09:07 Ring Announcer: And the winner of the match, is Christian North!!
McDonald: North advances.. Ring Announcer: This first round contest is scheduled for one fall.. Ring Announcer: First, from Los Angeles, California...weighing 252 lbs...he is The Spartan!!
Ring Announcer: And his opponent, from London, England...weighing 290 lbs...he is Lord Baltimore!!
The Spartan takes him down with a German suplex. James Beard counts: One, kickout. Lord Baltimore executes a rolling kick. Lord Baltimore nails him with a kneebreaker. Lord Baltimore takes him down with a European uppercut. Lord Baltimore goes for a double underhook suplex, but The Spartan counters it with a backdrop.
In turn, Lord Baltimore counters it with a sunset flip. James Beard counts: One, two, shoulder up. Lord Baltimore goes for a double underhook suplex, but The Spartan counters it with a backdrop.
The Spartan runs into the ropes. Lord Baltimore hits The Spartan with an elbow. Lord Baltimore takes him down with a Frankensteiner. James Beard counts: One, two, shoulder up. Lord Baltimore goes for a leg lariat, but The Spartan counters it with a duck-down move. The Spartan attemps to place Lord Baltimore on the turnbuckle, but Lord Baltimore blocks it.
The Spartan runs into the ropes. Lord Baltimore executes a double-axhandle chop. Lord Baltimore whips The Spartan into the ropes. The Colonel pulls down the top rope. James Beard disqualifies Lord Baltimore. The winner is The Spartan. Time of match: 0:11:09 Ring Announcer: The winner of the match, as a result of a DQ, is The Spartan!!
McDonald: And this tournament is full of DQ's!! Wrobleski: This is just the way they like it!! The Spartan must be feelin great now!! <Laughs> Ring Announcer: This is a first round contest, and is scheduled for one fall!!
Ring Announcer: First, from Orlando, Florida...weighing 250 lbs..he is Tony Star! Ring Announcer: And his opponent, from the Military Experiment Base...he weighs 450 lbs...he is the RAF Bomber!!
The winner is RAF Bomber. Time of match: 0:02:26 Ring Announcer: The winner of the match, is the RAF Bomber!
McDonald: The Bomber advances.. Ring Announcer: This is another first round contest scheduled for one fall.. Ring Announcer: First, representing the WeWa...he is from Miami, Florida...he weighs 255 lbs..he is "Beefstew" Lou Macourni!!
Ring Announcer: And his opponent, from London, England...weighing 317 lbs...he is King Curtis!!
The winner is Beefstew Lou Marconi. Time of match: 0:08:08 Ring Announcer: The winner is "Beefstew" Lou Marconi...
McDonald: And the outsider wins.... Wrobleski: Who's this guy?? His name is ridiclous!! Ring Announcer: This contest is another first round matchup.. Ring Announcer: First, from Detroit, Michigan..he weighs 270 lbs..he is The Backstreet Giant!!
Ring Announcer: And his opponent, from Ottawa, Ontario, Canada...he weighs 386 lbs...he is Mister Man!!
Backstreet Giant runs shoulder-first into the corner. Backstreet Giant nails him with a jawbreaker. Backstreet Giant runs into the ropes. Mister Man misses with a clothesline. Mister Man hits Backstreet Giant with an elbow. Mister Man goes for a cobra clutch, but Backstreet Giant counters it with an armdrag takedown.
Backstreet Giant goes for a jumping DDT, but Mister Man counters it with a backdrop. Mister Man nails him with a full nelson. Backstreet Giant reaches the ropes after being trapped for 17 seconds. Mister Man executes an avalanche. Mister Man takes him down with an avalanche. Mister Man whips Backstreet Giant into the turnbuckle. Mister Man runs shoulder-first into the corner, but Backstreet Giant moves out of the way. Backstreet Giant goes for a headbutt, but Mister Man blocks it. Mister Man takes him down with a chop. Mister Man executes a savate kick. Mister Man executes a dropkick to the knee. Mister Man nails him with a German suplex. Nick Patrick counts: One, two, kickout. Mister Man runs into the ropes. Mister Man misses with a kick. Mister Man takes him down with an enzuilariato. Mister Man goes for a kick to the thigh, but Backstreet Giant blocks it. Backstreet Giant executes a swinging neckbreaker. Backstreet Giant whips Mister Man into the ropes. Nasty Nelson trips Mister Man. Nick Patrick warns Nasty Nelson. Backstreet Giant goes for an armbar submission, but Mister Man blocks it. Mister Man takes him down with an abdominal stretch. There is no referee there to ask Backstreet Giant. Backstreet Giant breaks the hold after 4 seconds. Backstreet Giant runs into the ropes. Backstreet Giant misses with a clothesline. Backstreet Giant goes for a chokehold, but Mister Man blocks it. Nick Patrick is back on the job. Mister Man executes a cobra clutch. Backstreet Giant submits immediately. The winner is Mister Man. Time of match: 0:14:14 Ring Announcer: The winner of the match, is Mister Man!!
McDonald: And the Mister Man wins.. Wrobleski: This guy is just another chump... Ring Announcer: First, from New York, New York...he weighs 250 lbs..he is The Hitman! Ring Announcer: And his opponent, from Parts Unknown...he is the Crippler!! The Hitman attacks The Crippler before the bell. The Hitman nails him with a Soviet suplex. The Hitman takes him down with a flying bodypress. Frank Morrell counts: One, two, kickout. The Hitman whips The Crippler into the ropes. The Crippler hits The Hitman with a backdrop. The Crippler executes a rana. Frank Morrell counts: One, two, kickout. The Hitman nails him with a Frankensteiner. The Hitman pulls the tights. Frank Morrell counts: One, two, he sees it. Frank Morrell disqualifies The Hitman. The winner is The Crippler. Time of match: 0:00:36 Ring Announcer: The winner of the match, is the Crippler!!
McDonald: The Crippler pulls off the upset!! Wrobleski: That WAS a upset... Ring Announcer: First, from parts unknown...he is "The Death Dealer" Kerry Hailing!! Ring Announcer: And his opponent, from parts unknown...he weighs 255 lbs..he is Blaze!! The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze and Kerry Hailing get hit with a double clothesline. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze goes for a diving shoulderblock, but Kerry Hailing counters it with a duck-down move.
Kerry Hailing runs into the ropes. Kerry Hailing executes a kick to the midsection. Kerry Hailing goes for a German suplex, but The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze counters it with an elbowsmash.
The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze takes him down with a powerslam. Frank Morrell counts: One, two, kickout. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze goes for a kneedrop, but Kerry Hailing counters it with a roll away.
The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze goes for a legsweep faceslam, but Kerry Hailing counters it with a faceslam.
Kerry Hailing goes for a stomp, but The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze counters it with a roll away.
The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze runs into the ropes. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze hits Kerry Hailing with a clothesline. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze nails him with a springboard splash. Frank Morrell counts: One, two, shoulder up. Kerry Hailing whips The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze into the turnbuckle, but The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze reverses it.
The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze runs shoulder-first into the corner. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze executes a kick to the midsection. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze goes for a Gorilla Press, but Kerry Hailing counters it with a rolling reverse cradle.
Frank Morrell counts: One, two, shoulder up. Kerry Hailing runs into the ropes. Kerry Hailing hits The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze with an elbow. Kerry Hailing climbs to the top turnbuckle, but The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze throws him to the mat.
The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze nails him with a kneedrop. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze goes for a kick to the midsection, but Kerry Hailing blocks it.
Kerry Hailing takes him down with an armdrag takedown. Kerry Hailing goes for a headlock takedown, but The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze counters it with a back suplex.
Kerry Hailing whips The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze into the ropes. Kerry Hailing goes for a headlock takedown, but The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze counters it with a back suplex.
The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze nails him with a single-leg takedown. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze executes a rolling kick. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze executes a kick to the midsection. Kerry Hailing executes a double underhook suplex. Kerry Hailing takes him down with a headlock takedown. Kerry Hailing nails him with a piledriver. Kerry Hailing goes for a piledriver, but The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze counters it with a backdrop.
The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze whips Kerry Hailing into the ropes. Kerry Hailing hits The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze with a backdrop. Kerry Hailing runs into the ropes. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze goes for a powerslam, but Kerry Hailing counters it with a lariat.
In turn, The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze counters it with a duck-down move. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze executes a legsweep faceslam. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze runs into the ropes. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze misses with a kick. Kerry Hailing hits The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze with a clothesline. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze falls out of the ring. Kerry Hailing goes through the ropes. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze reenters the ring. Kerry Hailing follows him back in. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze whips Kerry Hailing into the ropes. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze misses with a kick. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze misses with a clothesline. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze hits Kerry Hailing with a kick. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze nails him with a forearm to the back. The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze goes for a kneebreaker, but Kerry Hailing blocks it. Kerry Hailing catches him in a Northern Lights suplex. Frank Morrell counts: One, two, three. The winner is Kerry Hailing. Time of match: 0:14:29 Ring Announcer: The winner of the match, is Kerry Hailing!!
McDonald: And we'll be right back..
