[MWL] Week in Review

4 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MWL Week in Review- _________________________________________________________________________ ____ World Champion - John Henry TV Champion - Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski North American Champion - vacant Tag Team Champions - vacant _________________________________________________________________________ ____ The MWL had a very successful opening. On Saturday March 30, 1996 the MWL debuted with its first card, Saturday Night Slamfest. On this card we saw a Royal Rumble. The last three people remaining in the Rumble fought in a mini tournamet to declare the World and TV champions. We saw Suicide defeat Cancer to become the first MWL World Champion. Then we saw Cancer defeat Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski to become the first ever MWL TV Champion. The night was full of action. All competitors were pushed to the limits, and all did their greatest.
On Wednesday April 3, 1996 Wednesday Night in the Cage debuted. This was the first real card for the MWL. Newcomers were still entering. In one suprising request, "Human Pyrotechic" Blaze and his partner NightHawk wanted to fight each other. Why, who knows? We saw the debut of The Legend's Corner. The president of the MWL conducted his first interview with the World Champion Suicide. There we saw John Herny try to stare down Suicide, then he tryed to shove Suicide, but he was the one who was shoved out of the ring. Raekwon made himself known in the MWL with his many attacks on a lot of people. During the Iron Man match between Tower and Heatstroke, Raekwon made one thing clear, he doesn't like Tower. Mr.
Mark R. Rose is becoming a force to be reckon with. One of his men, Rick Styles, caused The Spartan his match against Tony Star. Take a look: The Spartan rakes Tony Star's face across the cage.
Tony Star is busted wide open. The Spartan nails him with a diving elbow smash. The Spartan takes him down with a flying elbowdrop. Tim Brown counts: One, two, thr... kickout. The Spartan takes him down with a cobra clutch. The Spartan is heading for the door. The Spartan is opening the door. "Top Dog" Rick Styles slams the door in The Spartan's face. Tony Star goes for the pin. Tim Brown counts: One, two, three. The winner is Tony Star. Time of match: 0:11:52. There was a very impressive match-up inside a cage between Bad News Bob and Grizzly. After Bob won the match, Storm made his enterance: Storm jumps off the top of the cage, slamming into Bad News Bob.
Storms executes the Crashlanding on Bad News Bob on the floor. Storm walks over to Grizzly. Storm and Grizzly are laughing. Storm shoves Grizzly into the cage. Storm slams Grizzly's head into the cage. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, eleven, twelve. Storm walks off. We saw an incredible No Count-Out No DQ Ladder Match between TV Champion Cancer and Challenger Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski. There were a few stipulations added to stop outside interference. Tower, for Jeff, and Heatstroke, for Cancer, were strapped together on the outside.
The strap didn't make any difference. Heatstroke ended up knocking Tower out with a sleeper, then removing the strap and handcuffing Tower to the guardrail.
Heatstroke's interference hindered Cancer, more than it helped him. Take a look: Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski gives the cut throat sign.
Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski gives the sign for the Rocket Launcher. Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski attemps to place Cancer on the turnbuckle, but Cancer blocks it. Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski executes a spinning backbreaker. Heatstroke enters the ring and hits Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski with a chair. Before Heatstroke connects, Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski moves out of the way. Heatstroke hits Cancer. Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski throws Heatstroke over the top rope. Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski tries to climb the ladder. Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski is on his way up. Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski is half-way up. Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski is almost on top of the ladder. Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski is in grabbing distance. Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski has retrieved the belt. The winner is Jeff "Rocket" Wrobleski. Time of match: 0:23:34. In the final match of the night, we saw World Champion Suicide, taking on Challenger John Henry, inside a Steel Cage. The match was full of action, in the end we saw John Henry take home the gold. During the match, we saw King Curtis get attacked by Grizzly. Grizzly proceded to give KIing Curtis several piledrivers, then he placed Curtis in the Mauler for two minutes. I can see a major fued brewing here.
