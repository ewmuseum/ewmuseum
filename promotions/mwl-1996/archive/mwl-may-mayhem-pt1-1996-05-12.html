Mayhem Wrestling League — Card

[MWL] May Mayhem

12 May 1996 &mdash; Kingdome, Seattle, WA
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens in a sold out Kingdome in Seattle, Washington) McDonald: Hello, everyone!! And welcome to May Mayhem!!! (Crowd Cheers) McDonald: I'm Vince McDonald along with my special guest for the evening, the MWL Television Champion, Jeff "The Rocket" Wrobleski!
Wrobleski: Hey, Vince.. McDonald: And The Rocket waiting to see who he will face at the end of the TV tournament.. Wrobleski: It wont matter...because I'm walkin out TV champ... McDonald: And lets get to the opening round...but first...here's the tournament bracket! Moo Kame vs Sabin Rene Figaro Tom Wong vs Tommy Rich Sexy Boy Scott Franklin vs Darklon Grizzly vs Vulture Steve Keller vs Mark Destructo Suicide vs Dangerous Kid Cruise Paralyzer Patrick Elliot vs Hack Myers Christian North vs Cancer The Spartan vs Lord Baltimore Tony Star vs RAF Bomber King Curtis vs Beefstew Lou Marconi Mister Man vs Backstreet Giant The Hitman vs The Crippler Kerry Hailing vs The Human Pyrotechnic Blaze Suwer Rat vs Heatstroke Nighthawk vs Nuclear Fallout Note: These matches are set at joined in progress. If there is no wrestling, the match was unusuably quick..
McDonald: And lets get to the ring!! Ring Announcer: The following contest is a first round matchup in the TV tournament and is scheduled for one fall!!
Ring Announcer: First, coming down the aisle, from Hong Kong...weighing 248 lbs.. he is "The Awesome Lion" Moo Kame!!!! Ring Announcer: And his opponent, from The Valley of the Shadow of Death..he weighs 330 lbs...he is Sabin Rene Figaro!!!
The winner is Sabin Rene Figaro. Time of match: 0:06:16 Ring Announcer: The winner of the match, is Sabin Rene Figaro!!
McDonald: And Figaro advances.. Wrobleski: Yay...(sarcastically) Ring Announcer: This following contest is a first round contest in the TV tournament! and is scheduled for one fall! Ring Announcer: First, from Hong Kong...weighing 223 lbs...he is Tom Wong!! Ring Announcer: And his opponent, from Atlanta, Georgia..weighing 280 lbs..he is Tommy Rich!!
McDonald: Rich in the tournament...from WeWa... Wrobleski: What's he think he's gonna do? Tommy Rich runs into the ropes. Tommy Rich hits Tom Wong with an elbow. Tommy Rich takes him down with a forearm to the back. Tommy Rich executes a jumping neck snap. Tom Wong nails him with a flying clothesline. Tommy Rich executes a kneelift. Tommy Rich whips Tom Wong into the ropes. Tom Wong goes for a jumping DDT, but Tommy Rich counters it with a backdrop. Tommy Rich whips Tom Wong into the turnbuckle. Tommy Rich charges into the corner. Tommy Rich executes a chop. Tommy Rich nails him with a chop. Tommy Rich executes a sleeperhold. Tom Wong submits after 1 seconds. The winner is Tommy Rich. Time of match: 0:05:48 Ring Announcer: The winner of the match, is Tommy Rich!!
McDonald: And now Rich advances.... Ring Announcer: This following contest is scheduled for one fall and is a first round TV tournament matchup!!
Ring Announcer: First, from Manchaca, Texas...weighing 289 lbs..he is "Sexy Boy" Scott Franklin!! Ring Announcer: And his opponent, from Manchaca, Texas..weighing 329 lbs...he is Darklon!! McDonald: Darklon..making his debut...lets see what he's got.. Sexy Boy Scott Franklin executes a German suplex. Randy Anderson counts: One, two, Christy distracts Randy Anderson. Randy Anderson disqualifies Darklon. The winner is Sexy Boy Scott Franklin. Time of match: 0:10:45 Ring Announcer: The winner of the match, as a result of a DQ, is "Sexy Boy"
Scott Franklin!! McDonald: And Franklin advances.. Wrobleski: Whoopee....it doesnt matter....because the Rocket is hangin on to this belt!! Ring Announcer: The following contest, is a first round matchup in the TV tournament! And is scheduled for one fall! Ring Announcer: First, from Manchaca, Texas...he weighs 329 lbs..he is Grizzly!! Ring Announcer: And his opponent, from Parts Unknown, he weighs 265 lbs..he is Vulture!! Grizzly goes for a Samoan Drop, but Vulture counters it with an elbowsmash. Vulture runs into the ropes. Christy pulls down the top rope. James Beard disqualifies Grizzly. The winner is Vulture. Time of match: 0:04:52 Ring Announcer: The winner of the match, as a result of a DQ, is Vulture!!
McDonald: And Vulture advances to the second round!! Wrobleski: Who is this chump? Its gonna be fun beating him!! HAHA Ring Announcer: This contest, is another first round contest for the TV tournament, and is scheduled for one fall!!
Ring Announcer: First, from Chicago, Illinois....he weighs 369 lbs..he is Mark Destructo!! Ring Announcer: And his opponent, from Hollywood, California..he weighs 240 lbs.. he is Steve Keller!! Mark Destructo whips Steve Keller into the ropes, but Steve Keller reverses it. Steve Keller goes for a spinning mule kick, but Mark Destructo counters it with a duck-down move.
Mark Destructo executes a spinning DDT. Steve Keller takes him down with a spinebuster slam. James Beard counts: One, two, three. The winner is Steve Keller. Time of match: 0:12:19 Ring Announcer: The winner of the match, is Steve Keller!!
McDonald: And Keller advances!! We'll be back after these messages!!
