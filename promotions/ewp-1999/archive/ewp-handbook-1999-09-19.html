ENGLISH WRESTLING PROMOTION -HANDBOOK- 1] Introduction 2] Rules and Regulations 3] Weekly Cards 4] Pay Per Views 5] Titles 6] Staff 7] Application 8] Final Thoughts =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 1] INTRODUCTION Well, many of you will know me as Tommy ''Lord'' Lionheart but others will know me as [name withheld]!!
Now, I have been in many feds and handled some too and I am very experienced in the e-wrestling business. Now, coming from England I thought to myself about re-starting another fed but only this time make it really close to home and that's when I thought about an ENGLISH based fed! This fed will be very special as I'm from England and will be very different to other feds which are based in
New York and L.A etc....
I hope you check this out and hopefully it will be very unique and cool... =-=-=-=-=-=-=- 2] RULES AND REGULATIONS Now, like in every single federation in e-wrestling, there are rules and regulations and the English Wrestling Promotion [EWP] is no different...
Now, in the EWP, it is all about enjoying yourself like every other federation which means that there should be NO Real heat between roleplayers etc....
In Roleplays, you will be allowed to have a MAXIMUM of TWO Roleplays for each
Roleplay packet. There will be Two RP packets a week which means a MAXIMUM of
FOUR roleplays a week...
You MUST roleplay atleast ONCE every fortnight to stay in the EWP! If you don't, I'm affraid you'll be kicked out because I am looking for dedicated members..
In roleplays, you must not use any FOUL language....Words like ass, damn, hell etc... Are fine but when you want to do words such as ''Fuck'' you must type
[BEEP] in it's place....Thanks
Of course Roleplaying is the main way to win matches but you can also do
Interviews, In-Rings and Strats....You all know what I mean so need I say more....
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 3] WEEKLY CARDS Now in the EWP, there will be [For Now] ONE MAJOR SHOW A WEEK!! It will come
LIVE every Saturday night from different locations and arena's in England...
EWP - SATURDAY SUPREME - This card will be HUGE!! Angles, feuds, title matches the lot all happen on this card. It will consist of about 6-8 matches in a Two hour show. In-Rings, Sneak Attacks and Strats are ALL needed for this big card to make it a success..
Once we get started, I will add a House Show card for the week but not just yet.... ROLEPLAY PACKET will be called.....''Words of Wisdom'' =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 4] PAY PER VIEWS In the EWP, there will be a big supercard, better known as a Pay Per View every other month!! The list of months are:-
December February April June August October The names of PPV's haven't been decided yet! Also in the months that have not got a PPV, occassionly the EWP will go on tour across Europe and the World...
==--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- 5] TITLES In the EWP, there will be FOUR titles [To start off with] These titles will be.... EWP - World Heavyweight Championship - The big one! Obviously!! Only the cream of the crop and best of the best in the EWP, get to hold this and even get a shot at it!
EWP - United Kingdom Championship - This title is the second biggest in the
EWP. The big names only get a chance of winning this too...Will be defended
EVERY card!!
EWP - World Tag Team Championship - The main title for all the EWP Tag Teams. The best duo will wear this title and will defend it with honour. EWP - World Juniorweight Championship - This is for all you guys under 235lbs!
