Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The English Wrestling Promotion introduced itself on 19 September 1999 with a handbook staged as a nation's answer to the American-run scene it meant to displace: “Forget all those American based fed, this is English and us brits shall never EVER be slaves,” run over clips of Wembley Arena, the MEN Arena and the NEC. It planned one weekly card, Saturday Supreme, broadcast live from a different English arena each week, with a house show and European tour dates to follow once it had the roster to support them.

Four championships were announced from the start: a World Heavyweight Championship as the top prize, a United Kingdom Championship billed to defend every card, a World Tag Team Championship, and a World Juniorweight Championship for wrestlers under 235 pounds. “Lord” Tommy Lionheart was billed as the promotion's own on-screen president, with Jay Johnson and Geoff Williams announced as its commentary team, Ronnie Taylor as ring announcer and Darren Jenner as main interviewer.

Its one further surviving document is an advertisement for Halloween Brawl '99, billed “Out of this World” and set for Sunday, 24 October 1999, from the NEC Arena in Birmingham on Sky Box Office — an eight-man tournament to crown the promotion's first World Heavyweight Champion, headlined by “Top Notch” Tim Beck and the promised debut of “The Wife-Beater” Bubba Corkpepper against an unnamed opponent billed as a household name.

No result of that tournament, or of any Saturday Supreme card, survives. The record stops with the Halloween Brawl advertisement.
