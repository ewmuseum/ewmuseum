The Empress Cup is now officially underway! Just this morning, the 24th entrant was approved. Anyway, let's get down to business. I sent you guys a planned format should we get up to 24 participants. That's what we'll be using. In a nutshell, the first round will be a Royal Rumble type match. I originally though to use a Battle Royal format, but realized that it would be more difficult to put the characters over that way, since 24 would all be in the ring at once. The second round will be four tag matches. I'm personally looking forward to this one, as it should produce some interesting combinations. With only 8 left in the tournament, the third round will be four singles matches. Only there's a catch. Each match will have a different gimmick. The particulars of those matches will be announced when the time comes. The fourth round (semi-finals) will entail a single, four-way cage match. The first two women to escape cage by going over the top will win. The door will be welded shut once the four participants enter the ring. The finals will entail a two out of three falls match. However, again, gimmicks will be involved for each fall. Like the third round, those will be announced as the time arises. It was originally intended that the entire tournament take place at Chicago's Aragon Ballroom, site of past ECW events. This is true, but only for the first three rounds. I thought I'd make things a little more realistic by having the semi-finals and finals take place at the UIC Pavilion, a venue with quite a bit more room. To make it easy for myself and everyone else, please ensure that your flashes' lines are no longer than 75-77 characters. These seperator lines are a great example of how long that is. Also, it's highly recommended that you use either Notepad or TextPad to write your flashes, as other word processing programs like Word may add unwanted additional characters. Remember to keep strats short for these first three rounds, as they will be written in summary form. A brief gameplan and two or three spots at most would be perfect. For those of you who put down on your apps that you'd write, I'll take you up on that offer for the second round. Since there's only one match in the first round, I'll take care of that. There is no official date that Beginnings is scheduled to come out on. Why? Because life has a way of interjecting itself at critical moments. So in short, it'll come out when it's ready to. :) But I'm eager to get started writing, so it shouldn't be _too_ long a wait. That's it for now.
