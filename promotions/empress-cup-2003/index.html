Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Empress Cup was announced 25 November 2003 as an all-women's invitational tournament staged at Chicago's Aragon Ballroom, opening with a planned field of thirty-two and settling, once entries were in, at twenty-four. Its own format ran five rounds: an opening battle-royal-style match narrowing the field from twenty-four to sixteen, four tag-team matches cutting to eight, four singles matches — each fought under its own surprise gimmick — cutting to four, a four-way cage match in which the first two women to escape by going over the top advanced, and a final fought two out of three falls, a different stipulation for each fall.

Its own broadcast team named Rusty Waters and Rebecca Howard at the announce table, Doc Draven as head referee, TJ Malone as ring announcer, and a security unit billed as The Authorities. An opening-night bulletin of 17 January 2004 confirms the finalized twenty-four-woman field and its own Round 1 bracket, and moves the semifinal and final from the Aragon Ballroom to Chicago's larger UIC Pavilion “to make things a little more realistic” — but records no completed match: “None at this time.”

How the tournament's own rounds played out, and whether an Empress of Wrestling was ever crowned, isn't in the surviving record: no later document survives here.
