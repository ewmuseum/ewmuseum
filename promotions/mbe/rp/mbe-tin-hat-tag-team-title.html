Tin Hat: MBE World Tag Team Championship Match

4-10 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yori Yakamo jr — Jul 4, 2007 at 10:09 PM The Thrillbillies (c) v. The Sheffield Wednesday Lot Stipulation: Ladder Match Promos go here. Promo deadline is Monday July 16th at 11:59 PM TH — Jul 5, 2007 at 10:59 PM The scene is in front of a generic MBE backdrop on a soundstage in the legendary MBE Arena... NOW WITH CONCRETE LOCATION! In the front of it are the Thrillbillies, Hoss Garrison and Jake McCody, standing by in their ring gear and t-shirts; Hoss in a Confederate flag shirt, Jake in a Shinedown shirt, both sleevless. Their MBE Tag Team Championships are slung across their shoulders. Hoss has the mic first. Hoss: Jus' when I thought that we was outta the woods an' into somethin' NEW wit' this MBE restart, jus' when I thought we was in the clear... JUS' WHEN I THOUGHT we was done wit' them tea-drinkin', crooked-tooth soccer faggits, WE GET SUCKED RIGHT BACK IN! It ain't right, I tells ya! We done kicked the crap outta them fairies and their nippy buddy more times than Strom Thurmond done got elected to Senate, an' they still get to keep comin' back at us! Whut, do they got explicit pictures of Yori or somethin'? Jake: Hoss, I think everyone has explicit pictures of our chairman somewhere in their person. Hoss: Well EXCUUUUUUSE ME then! I have no idear how they keep gettin' shots at our titles in that case, an' you know whut? I'm getting SICK AND DANG TIRED of havin' to put these babies up against them mealy-mouthed sons of b*tches! So you know what we gon' do? First, we gon' come down to that ring. Then, in front of the most loyal an' dedicated fans in all a' 'rasslin', we gon' kick the ever lovin' bull-dookie of them snot nosed Limey punks. An' then, after all's said an' done, we gon' grab them belts from up high off a twenty foot ladder, an' then, we gon' take a dip in the neighbor's new hot tub. Jake: Waitasecond... I thought that was just a metaphor fer' havin' a show name. Hoss: Really? Are you sure? Jake: I dunno, man. I thought that's what it was. Although it'd sure be nice to soak my bones in some hot water after a match like that... Jake closes his eyes momentarily before realizing he has to cut his part of the promo. He grabs the mic from Hoss. Jake: Anyhoo, my slightly off-color friend here is right in some respects. You two hooligans musta got more lives than a litter of kittens for you to keep coming back and making runs at our titles. Sometimes I think it's cos you guys are the only other real team here after Fuse and Ace went MIA, but that's another story for another day, but really, can't no other guys on the roster team up and make a run at us? It works in any other company. Still, y'all are challenging us, and there ain't nothing in the world that's gonna change that. Hoss is still burnt up 'bout it, but I made my peace with it. Y'all know, the hardest thing in the world to do is beat up on someone on a constant basis. Somewhere along the line, they bound to get the best of you, especially if you let your guard down. That's something me and Hoss have been trying not to let happen to us, but let's face it, there's only so many times a team can break down film on another team whose number they gots. So Nate, Mac, lemme make this real clear. Y'all should go to whoever gave you this shot, and y'all should thank 'em. Y'all should get down on your hands and knees and kiss their feet, because they gave you the best dang opportunity to take our titles that you ever got. They gave you the opportunity... to come inches closer yet still so dang far away from being where we're at right now. What, y'all didn't think I was conceding this thing, did y'all? Hell no, I may not be as brash as my pardner over here, and I do know how to show respect when it's deserved. However, I also know that we're a great team, and great teams don't let their guards down that easily. Nope, we're the only team to hold these titles ever since they was instituted, and we ain't giving 'em up that easy. You guys can try, but y'all will just end up coming up short against the best tag team that no one else seems to notice. Hoss: WE'RE THE TAG TEAM OF THE YEAR! Dang you pencil neck bloggin' geeks! Jake: Yep, Hoss gets burned up 'bout too. I don't. I don't get burned up about anything, actually. I just go out and I do what I do best... ...and that's throwing myself through the air to make sure me and Hoss put down folks like you two, time after time after time after time and again. Hoss: YEEEEEEE-HAAAAAWWW! Fade to the MBE logo. Yori Yakamo jr — Jul 10, 2007 at 11:50 AM (fade in, Biff Bentley outside the Lot's locker room with Nate and Mac. Juen is suspiciously absent) BIFF: Biff Bentley reporting for Message Board Entertainment. I'm here with The Sheffield Wednesday Lot, who at "The New... MAC: Come on now, ya bloke, everyone knows who you are, and everybody knows who the Lot is. BIFF: Mac, some of us try to keep things fairly professional. MAC: Is that so, Biff? Are you saying it isn't professional to politely challenge for a world tag title shot over tea. BIFF: That sounds very polite. What might be less polite would be costing the Billies their first match as a team and beating them down afterwards. MAC: That's just plain slanderous, Biff. We were just showing off the new Wednesday kits for the fans to see, and poor old Hoss just got tangled up in it. BIFF: But what about the beatdown? MAC: So indignant Biff, you'd almost think you were a real reporter from the Saturday Evening Post or something. That's what you yanks read, right? Nate'll tell ya, we can't have some blokes running around scuffing up the sacred kits of the Wednesday, come on now. I don't go into the Billies locker room and take an ol' whizz on their mama's daisy dukes. I have a bit of respect. BIFF: So about this ladder match... MAC: I'll tell ya Nate, always down to business ol' Biffy is. Always trying to get the big scoop. Well we know our way around a ladder, how else do you think we broke into Bryan Robson's bedroom and spraypainted "Blades suck" all over his walls....allegedly. BIFF: Well, I suppose... MAC: And the Blades do suck, let me tell you. But we'll have no problem climbing that ladder and pinching those old ladies. We could even do it in ski masks and Sheffield United uniforms...if we ever would lower ourselves to doing that. BIFF: Never. MAC: Never is right Biffy. I'll even ignore that little spritely tone in your voice. BIFF: But what will you do if you lose and can't challenge for the belts for 365 days? MAC: Don't get your knickers all in a bunch there, Biffy. The joke is on the Billies anyway. Next year is a leap year. Though I suppose they don't teach that in those rural public schools. So we get a free day out of the whole bargain. Not that it'll matter a bit. And just to show you what kind-hearted blokes we are, we'll let 'em have a rematch after we win. And they'll only have to wait 364 days. BIFF: You are nothing if not a gentleman, Mac. MAC: Yeah, well Nate's sister can attest to that. (Nate flips up the visor on his motorcycle helmet and glares at Mac. Ah come on Bub, I was only gnoshing ya a bit. BIFF: Nate seems awfully quiet today, even by his standards. MAC: Aw, he's just worried about Juen, the little bugger. BIFF: Where is Juen, anyway. MAC: Well, he was hoping the winter games'd come to his homeland. So he went back home to practice his luge skills. BIFF: They went to Russia. MAC: I know, I haven't had the heart to telegram the little git anf tell him. BIFF: You know, they do have phones in Korea. MAC: Yeah, but all those roaming charges, much less pence for the old lager, Biff. You have to have your priorities straight. BIFF: So Juen is in Korea. MAC: Yeah, no way he'll be able to make it to the arena to help us...by providing moral support, you know. BIFF: Really? MAC: Yeah, it's a shame. I was hoping the little bugger would be there to see us finally win those shiny tag titles. But he'll be bringing home the gold in other venues. He really is quite the luger. BIFF: And with that, this is Biff Bentley, signing off. Yori Yakamo jr — Jul 10, 2007 at 11:50 AM Double post. Shocking, I know.
