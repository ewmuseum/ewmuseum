The Baneful Soul Polaris - Rumble Promo

10 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PLEASE GIVE ME FEED BACK!!! E-Mail me with it... thanks... If you guys are prez out there tell me how much quality i got... THANKS.. I am looking to improve alot!!! [The scene fades into an empty locker room. The lights are dim and "Rust in
Peace... Polaris" by Megeadeth is playing very softly in the background. You see "The Baneful Soul" Polaris sitting down on a bench. He is wearing black pants with a Polaris T-shirt on. The camera zooms in on his face. His eyes are dark and is reflecting the little light in the room. Then he looks within the camera giving you a shiver. Then he speaks.]
["The Baneful Soul" Polaris] Like a new moon rising I have risen again. This time in FWA. This time ... forever. This new federation is mine for the taking.
It is mine to conquer and to collect the belts. No more getting off track in this wrestling world. The call from hell has been heard and it shall be done.
The truth from below is right, and nothing else matters. This is my calling. This is my life. I am now here to get my gold and to collect anything I wish to hold ... money, belts, woman, anything I want is mine. No one can stand in my way. I will first prove my power in the Rumble on Saturday Nights Main Event. I only have to beat the entire roster of the FWA to win. [Smiles] This will not be hard. You _ALL_ must be thinking that I am being cocky. Why would a man of such power and such magnitude such as "The Baneful Soul" Polaris be scared of a few weaklings? No one in the FWA scares me. No one in the FWA holds to the power to conquer me or win the rumble other than I. IT is my destiny that I shall win the rumble. I can feel it within my baneful soul. This is not a slanderous lie or a false claim to glory. This is the truth and it is my _TRUE_ destiny now and forever to be a FWA champion. To be here and take over the FWA is what I have came here for. Not to be the "punching bag" or the "jobber." I cannot lose. I can only win and conquer those weak fools. Not beating myself with mistakes that people like Moonsault would make. [Grins and Laughs] Yes the mere soul know as Moonsault is nothing better than a priest in hell. Yes, it is true Moonsault the people of this federation are already beginning to see you as not a wrestler, but a mere nobody for them to beat every week again and again. Till the day you leave you will be hunted so people will gain in their win columns. The rumble is still at hand. The time of demonstration is to begin. First with the eradication of the men you seek me out. Those are the
_REAL_ losers in this. You come after me and I shall make sure that you are yelling for a way out of the ring and out of the pain. Like the master beating his step child there will be no mercy for no one. Not any of the people which I have known for quite some time. No mercy will ever be shown by me. Prepare for the fight of a life time. No fucking around this time!
["The Baneful Soul" Polaris sits down on the bench. He looks into the camera again. Then the music changes to "Fade to Black" by Metallica.]
["The Baneful Soul" Polaris] The light fades to darkness when the call from hell is unleashed. I know it must seem that all of you people in HWA are looking at me saying, "That guy is a fucking Gothic Piece of trash!" But, think what you want to say. But a wise man once said, "Real power is taking a human mind and making it think what you want it to think." Like war is peace this has its own meaning. And I intend to prove this... to prove that I am not a "loser"
in a gothic costume. I am for real. I am not a little nothing that Chuck Williams or Bullet can make submit. I will not tap out or yell, "I quit!" I am not joining to give up in a match this big. It is stupid the believe that I am easy to defeat. Your stupidity is your down fall. Like Cornwallis at Yorktown you cease to look at all the possibilities. He was conquered because he thought the English Navy could give him more men and support. But, then the French set up a blockade. What a dumb mistake. He could not understand that things are
_NOT_ always what they seem. Maybe if he would have retreated to a safer place other than on a peninsula he could have escaped unhurt. It is a pity that people die for the mistakes of one man. But, in the rumble you won't die. You will just lose your chance at the FWA National Title. That is what I shall win.
That is what I will hold in my hands at the end of Saturday Nights Main Event. [He looks within the camera and you can't help but to believe he is true.] No mere mortal shall beat me. I will walk out the National Champion. No one can stand in my way of that. Not even the former IWF World Champion Chuck Williams.
[Polaris reaches over and takes a drink of an ominous dark liquid... almost a red. Then he looks back into the camera. He then opens a locker to reveal a picture of "Mr. Wonderful" Chuck Williams. It has Chuck Williams with the IWF
World Title. He takes the picture off and rips it up. Then he speaks, again.]
["The Baneful Soul" Polaris] Chuck Williams the man who held the IWF World
Title for 6 months. A great wrestler in many people's books. Yet, somehow he hasn't earned my respect. I have never been in the same ring as this "simply wonderful" pool boy. I have see him wrestle with the Reaper and other IWF
Greats. Still many people are scared of this trash. [Grins] Do you even think I am scared of a boy like Chuck Williams? A boy scout. Ohh thats right... He was a good man in IWF, but know he is a "bad guy." The boy scout has turned into a man that the people on "Cops" deal with. Yes, Chuck Williams in the rumble I will be hunting you like a dog. I will be after you like a cop after a donut. I will follow you till the end of the fight. I will be the one that forces you to tap out. I will be the one who slaps a hold on you, then you will scream in pain and in terror that I might break a bone or tear a muscle or other part of your body. [Laughs] Then perhaps my true calling will be done. To hold the second best title in the FWA. That is just a stepping stone. You see, Chuck
Williams you will play a major in my climb to the top. Maybe one day you could be on the side of the battle field that you were meant to be on... my side.
Only time will tell. After I come after you at Saturday Nights Main Event you might be on a mission to beat me. I welcome all challenges. I await you in my second home, the ring. But, something gets me Chuck Williams. No matter where you go the president of that federation picks you as the top seed in a tournament for their world belt or you are the one of the 2 men who fight for that belt. It seems people are giving you _TOO_ much respect for a man who didn't earn it. I don't believe you are all bad and good just because Ms. Kelly and Chuckster gave you the world title for 6 months. Now, I know you too well.
Your going to come out and call me a liar. Only stupid people pose slander as their attack. If you wish to try to use slander I advise you don't do it. It only makes people more pissed and even more pain will come to you. You know as well as I do, you didn't "win the IWF World Title because you earned it!" That is about as much bullcrap as teenagers use in arguments. Your gonna say, "I beat Reaper and.....!" on and till the bats come back to their cave 30 times
And to end it all you even called me, "Truly one of the Greats!" [Laughs so hard he almost falls over.] Never will you beat me Chuck Williams!
[Polaris stands up. He crosses his arms.] ["The Baneful Soul" Polaris] Now... On some other people in the Rumble. Geno
Sydde, you said, "If knowledge is power, then a God am I." Your speech incites a great load of ignorance and stupidity. You say you will win because that is the way you are. [Laughs] I really needed that one. But, Geno Sydde you have a lot to learn about wrestling. Your first lesson is you cannot win. Or at most you will one of the last 15 in the rumble. Unlike Chuck Williams, I see that you really are a nobody like Moonsault. Hmmm... there are so many people in this match. Mike Hall. Ohh Yes, a man who I have known for a few years. Well, he thinks he is good. [Chuckles] I have seen you in the ring. I know, that your even cockier than I. You will fall to me and you will cry out and leave the ring knowing, "Polaris is the greatest wrestler alive!" Everyone who I force to leave will see me as a greater wrestling power than you gave me credit. Now, I demand respect. Respect me or go down like Chuck Williams or Moonsault. Now...
we have "The Smooth Nigga" Drag-on Youmans. To be blunt, What the fuck kind of name is that? And I see you are bringing back a stable that _WAS_ powerful in the IWF some time ago. You will never make it as big nor and strong as the first Inner Circle. Try to do it and watch it fall to the likes of Moonsault or some other weak fool. I am not hunting you like I, Chuck Williams, Youmans.
But, if you do meet eye to eye with me then I will begin a great attack on you. Like the Nazis over Poland you will fall to the greater force and power. ************************** The quote is from the book 1984.
