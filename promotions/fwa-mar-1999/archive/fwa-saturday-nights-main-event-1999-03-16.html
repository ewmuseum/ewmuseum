fWa Presents: Saturday Nights Main Event Sunday March, 1999 Dark Matches Briefly Summarized ___________________________________ Lenny Carson vs Jimmy Stevons This Match was pretty much one-sided Lenny Carson took control of the match early and never lost his control. The Match was 5 minutes into it and Jimmy
Stevons came back with a few aimless punches and Threw Carson into the ropes, he went for a clothesline but came up short as Carson ducked the clothesline and kicked Stevons in the gut and applied a neck-breaker, bringing Stevons to the ground. He then rolled Stevons up and got the win.
Winner: Lenny Carson ___________________________________ ___________________________________ Da Show Stoppa vs MoonZault This Match was easily described as a see-saw match. The tide turned every other minute Moonzault superkicked Da Show Stoppa out of the ring and Da Show Stoppa went flying to the outside. Holding his head in agonizing pain Da Show Stoppa laid on the ground. MoonZault took the advantage and as Da Show Stoppa was begining to regain conciousness and taking to his feet excecuted a CorkScrew plancha sending Da Show Stoppa down with MoonZault on top of him. Moonzault then picked up Da Show Stoppa and grabed him and held him tight under his arm-pit and excecuted a DDT on the ground floor. Da Show Stoppa was then nocked out as MoonZault rolled him into the ring he regained his charisma and beat down on Moonzault as he rolled into the ring through the bottom rope and finishing with a leg-drop on the man 100 pounds smaller he got the win over
MoonZault. And Da Show Stoppa then helped MoonZault up and tag-team partners walked out together.
Winner: Da Show Stoppa ___________________________________ ___________________________________ LeX Diamonds vs X-Factor This was one of the quickest matches in the history of proffesional wrestling. X-Factor ran in and in a aggressive rage clotheslined LeX down and then picked up his fallen opponent and threw him into the ropes excecuting a spike powerbomb and getting the 1.2.3.
Winner: X-Factor ___________________________________ ___________________________________ Action Jackson vs Bob Capri This match was one of the best Dark-Matches of the night. Both men gave all they had in this slobber-nocker. Action Jackson opened up with a swinging ddt taking Bob Capri off his feet, with Capri down Action Jackson applied a boston crab setting off pain in Bob Capri's lower body. Capri struggled for the ropes but could not get there. Jackson feeling that he wanted Capri to suffer more let go of the Boston Crab and picked Capri up and let loose a standing side-kick yet again taking Capri off his feet and to the ground. Jackson then did a little taunting and laughed in the face of Bob Capri. Capri tripping
Jackson took to the offensive, he swiftly lifts up Action Jackson and applies a
German Suplex sending Jackson down to the ring mat in agonizing pain. Bob Capri then spit on Action Jackson & Applied a figure four leg-lock sending pain through Jackson's Legs and Spine. Jackson reaching for the ropes can not make it, he goes for the reversal...and...and..No he can not turn it. This goes on for another half-minute and Jackson finaly tapped.
