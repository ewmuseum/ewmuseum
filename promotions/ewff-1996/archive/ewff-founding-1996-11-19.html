Extreme Wrestling Fantasy Fed — Founding

"Extreme Wrestling Fantasy Fed EWFF"

19 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am starting a brand new ALL BOOKED wrestling fed. This means that there are no untrustworthy simulators and that pushes will be determined by the QUALITY OF ROLEPLAYING!!!!!
I am looking for quality role players. Since every fed has about 10
Undertaker clones, I am limiting this fed to only one. No more "Grim
Reapers" or "Deathmasters" or "Executioners" please. Anyone can invent a wrestler that is impossible to beat, but only a good role player can take a mediocre wrestler and make him a star.
There will be Four titles: World Heavyweight Title North American Champion TV Champ (Must defend belt at EVERY card) Tag Team Champions For now I will limit the EWFF to 25 singles wrestlers and 8 Tag Teams. You may submit up to 3 wrestlers total. Not all are guaranteed to make the cut because this fed is top quality. Originality will decide whether your wrestler is accepted or not.
Rest assured this isn't one of those feds that folds in no time. I have years of E-Wrestling experience and have consulted many experienced fed heads before finally deciding to start my own.

No 600 pounders doing shooting star presses.) 2 weaknesses 2 strengths Repeat above process if you submit a tag team. ---------------------------------- There will be 3 cards every 2 weeks. After the fed is established I will ask for new writers.
Respond ASAP ZMark
