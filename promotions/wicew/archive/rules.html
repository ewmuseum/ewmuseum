Rules and Regulations 1. Only original wrestlers. No "Hollywood" Hogan. No "Stone Cold". No Warrior. Use your imagination. 2. Roleplaying is a must. You must roleplay at least 3 times a week. Roleplay for your matches because roleplaying is the key to winning mathces. 3. Please keep the swears to a minimum and try to use symbols such as @$$ or S#!t. And definately no Fs or B*tch or P#$$y or any other words. Real wrestlers rarely say this words and they will not be tolerated here. Anyone who does will be subject to being jobbed, suspended or fired, it is up to my discretion. 4. NO RACIAL SLURS. Anyone who breaks this rule is fired. 5. All match results are final. No whining or complaining. If you have a gripe send me an email. Remember this is for FUN. 6. Email me with all match requests and sneak attacks. However, not all sneak attacks will be used. Email any suggestions for possible angles, too. 7. Roleplays will be judge on quality. So the better your roleplays, the better chance you will win your match. A tip, try not to right essays when you roleplay. They tend to be boring and pointless. Try to be creative, to the point, and interesting. If you don't roleplay before a match, chances are you will lose the match. 8. No impersonating other wrestlers in your roleplays. 9. You can use staff members in your roleplays. However, do not physically harm them unless otherwise approved by the Prez. If you do physically harm any member of the staff in a roleplay, it will hurt your career in the WICeW. 10. If I think of any other rules, they will be added. Now let's get ready to roleplay!!!!!
