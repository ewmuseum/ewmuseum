WICeW Saturday Showdown, 12 December 1998

12 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Results of December 12, 1998 Live from the Scope Colosseum in Norfolk, VA At the beginning of the show, President Joseph came out and stripped the Hickerson Brothers of the WICeW North American Tag Team Titles. He stated the titles would be filled at the PPV. He also warned Jupiter against interfering in any more matches, and when he found out who was behind the list, severe punishment could be in order. Finally, he stated that the semi-finals of the U.S. Tournament would be held at the Thursday House Show, at a local arena in New York. Benjamins $quad defeated the Dangers of the Ocean with the Decapitator in a time of 15:17. Chris Champion defeated Stan Smith with the Champion Crossface in a time of 8:21. Suicide Kid defeated Gary Copley with the Suicide Kick in a time of 8:41. Johnny Hardcore defeated Hurricane with the All Canadian Driver in a time of 25:44. Berserk defeated Cobra with the Blood Rush in a time of 9:13. Red Dogg successfully defended the WICeW World Television Title by defeating Junior Skaggs with the Million Dollar Dream in a time of 12:29. Berserk defeated Cobra with the Blood Rush in a time of 9:13. Blizzard and Thunder-Bolt wrestled to a double count-out. Original Gangstaz successfully defended the WICeW World Tag Team Titles by disqualification when Venus nailed Masta Gangsta with her loaded purse. After the match, Jacqueline came to ringside. Jacqueline and Venus had an altercation, but each tag team was able to restrain their managers. 2nd Round Matches for the U.S. Tournament Sabre defeated Blitzkrieg with a Flying Cross Body in a time of 21:42. Dr. Doom defeated Crush with a small package in a time of 20:55. "The Machine" Matt Gavin defeated Silkk the Shocker by countout, as Silkk was a no show at the match. Duke "The Predator" Puzo defeated Jupiter with the Predator's Bite in a time of 28:06. Puzo had a great game plan against the much bigger Jupiter. Puzo controlled most of the match. However, about the 23 minute mark, Jupiter began a comeback. All of a sudden, the Shadow came down to ringside, and was watching the match. Jupiter finally noticed the Shadow, and he became distracted. Jupiter and the Shadow were exchanging words. Puzo took advantage by grabbing Jupiter and nailing him with the Predator's Bite, and getting the victory.
