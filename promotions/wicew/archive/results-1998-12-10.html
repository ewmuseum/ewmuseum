WICeW Results, 10 December 1998

10 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Results of December 10, 1998 Gary Copley defeated Golem with the Figure Four Leg Lock in a time of 9:48. Rage defeated Detour with the Ultimate Rage in a time of 15:40. After the match, Berserk came out of the crowd, and attacked Rage. After tossing Rage into the steel step and the ring post, Berserk nailed Rage with the Blood Rush, and left him on the arena floor. Chaos defeated Stan Smith with the Death Valley Driver in a time of 13:28. Jupiter defeated Dom Valliere with the Black Hole in a time of 8:21. Gweedo the Wicked Klown defeated "Lucky" Luke Hickerson with the Big Top in a time of 18:46. Junior Skaggs defeated Dark Hart with the Frog Splash in a time of 10:10. Thunder-Bolt defeated Albert Eaton with the Thunder-Bolt Driver in a time of 9:58. "The Thriller" Brian Jackson defeated El Diablo with the Moon Walker in a time of 14:22. Justice defeated Albert Stanley with the Blindfold in a time of 7:33. Original Gangstaz defeated Nuclear Disaster when they executed the Harlem Hangover on Nuke in a time of 13:19. Total Carnage defeated Dangers of the Ocean when the executed the Guillotine on Tidal Wave in a time of 17:13. Dr. Doom defeated Volcano with the Doomsday Device in a time of 14:31. Black Mafia defeated Darkstar & Red Dogg as a result of a disqualification. During the match, while Red Dogg and Nemesis were fighting in the ring, Darkstar pulled a baseball bat out from under the ring. He clocked Pat Nickerson with it, casuing the DQ. Then he began to attack Nemesis and DS Hustla with it. Darkstar and Red Dogg continued the beat down of Black Mafia until security came to get things under control. Gangsta New Jack defeated Exacutioner by pinfall in a time of 12:53. The Shadow defeated Kyle Barrows as a result of a disqualification. This match was a back and forth match between these two wrestlers. Near the end, the Shadow was in control, and looked like he was about get the victory. However, Barrow began fighting back when Sabre came to the ring, and began to attack the Shadow. Pat Nickerson called for the bell, as the Shadow, Sabre, and a pi$$ed off Barrows began brawling in the ring. Security came to the ring to regain order.
