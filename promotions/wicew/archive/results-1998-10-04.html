WICeW Pay-Per View -- October GoldFest '98 Results October 4, 1998 - Live from the United Center in Chicago, IL. The lights have dimmed and the spotlights are flashing around the United Center. Pyrotechnics and explosions are goint off at the entrence way. The WICeW theme music is blaring over the P.A. System as the sold out crowd is going nuts. Larry Anthony - Ladies and Gentlemen welcome to the first Pay-per-view in WICeW history, October GoldFest '98. Tonight we will have five titles on the line, two battle royals, and a Stable War Games between the Hell's Angels and Dominion of War. This should be an exciting evening tonight. Alongside me for our broadcast will be the Absent Minded Professor, "Rusty" Mike Sheehan. And also joining us, a former World Champion, Fred Francis. Hickerson Brothers (Luke & Denny) w/Misha Tucker Vs Killers (Scorpion & Thunder-Bolt) This was an action packed match. Both teams wanted this belt, you could sence it. He then tagged in Denny, who then went to work on Scorpion. He hit Scorpion with a Double Ax Hammer to the back. Denny then went for the Dirty Lock, but Scorpion's quickness enabled him to escape and make the tag to T-Bolt. Denny greeted T-Bolt with a vicious lariat. Denny grabbed T-Bolt and whipped him into his corner where Luke pounded T-Bolt while Denny distracted the ref. Denny then tagged in Luke. They double teamed T-Bolt within the legal time frame.
