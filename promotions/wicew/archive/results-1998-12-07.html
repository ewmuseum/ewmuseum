WICeW Monday Madness, 7 December 1998

7 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Results of December 7, 1998 Live from the Georgia Dome in Atlanta, GA At the begining of the show, Blood Killer came out and challenged Suicide Kid to match. Later on during the show, "The Machine" Matt Gavin came out an announced that he had joined the M.S.S. Gavin also said that he was sick of the SoB, especially $oulja & Dominique. He challenged $oulja and Dominique to a mixed tag team match between him and Amanda for later in the show. Berserk and TRU fought to a no contest as Thunder-Bolt gained control of the cage, and raised it to the ceiling. The fight escaped the ring and went to the back as a broken VCR, and splintered baseball bats remained scattered in the ring. Suicide Kid defeated Blood Killer with the Suicide Kick in a time of 11:49. Red Dogg defeated Nemesis by disqualification in a time of 15:17, when Nemesis decked referee Pat Nickerson. Blizzard defeated DaLinkwent by pinfall in a time of 23:48 to become the new WICeW Internet Champion. This match went back and forth the whole time. It was fought very evenly. However, as DLwent went for Capital Punishmen, the ref, Pat Nickerson, was inadvertently knocked out. During the time while Nickerson was out, Jupiter came to the ring with his briefcase. He climbed in the ring, and grabbed DLwent by the neck. Jupiter nailed him with the Black Hole, not once, but twice. Jupiter then climbed out of the ring and revived the ref as Blizzard made the pin. Jupiter then pulled the list out of his briefcase, and crossed out another name. 1st Round Matches for the U.S. Tournament Sabre defeated Gangsta New Jack by pinfall in a time of 18:21. Duke "The Predator" Puzo defeated Masta Gangsta by pinfall in a time of 28:16. Dr. Doom defeated Down South Hustla by disqualification in a time of 10:40. Berserk came down to ringside during this match, and instead of attacking Down South Hustla, he nailed Dr. Doom with a forearm to the back of the head. He then began to stomp Dr. Doom, causing Pat Nickerson to give Dr. Doom the win via disqualification. An irate Down South Hustla went after Berserk. However, he and Dr. Doom left the ring together, smiling as they got the better of Down South Hustla on this occasion. Blitzkrieg defeated Jon Northcross by pinfall in a time of 19:55. $oulja & Dominique defeated "The Machine" Matt Gavin & Amanda in a time of 22:10 when Dominque pinned Amanda with a hurricanrana.
