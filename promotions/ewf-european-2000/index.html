Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A comeback notice opens the year: the promotion, closed since some point in the prior year after what it calls a successful run, announces it is returning for 2000, bigger and better than before, and points readers to its own site.

The relaunch did not fully take hold until the spring. A second posting in April frames the promotion as reopening once new talent can be found, describing itself as fully booked, with one show and one pay-per-view a month, and promising newcomers a fun rather than an over-promising experience.

Recruitment continued in fits and starts across the rest of the year -- May, July, August, and again in October -- each notice repeating the same pitch and the same site address, and one August posting adding a call for a vice-president to help with storylines and card-writing.

No card, roster, or result survives anywhere in the held record, and nothing says whether the promotion ever staged a single show under either its January announcement or its spring relaunch.

Its initials were already carried by more than a dozen earlier and later federations by the time this one used them, one of them under the very same full name; nothing beyond the four letters and the words behind them ties this promotion to any of them.
