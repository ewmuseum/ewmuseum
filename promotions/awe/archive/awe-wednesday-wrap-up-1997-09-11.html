AWE Wednesday Wrap-Up, 11 September 1997

11 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<<The AWE logo is centered on the screen. It rotates and spins away, becoming a small mark in the upper corner, with the following video segment appearing underneath: >> Chris: Douglas has the advantage... he whips Chainz to the ropes - reversal - Douglas coming off, a huge backdrop out of the ring, and Chris Douglas lands squarely on Dean Ackerman! Oh no! Mike: Well... at least he had someone to help him break his fall. Chris: Coach Brown and Dr. Andrews help both men up, and they roll Douglas back in the ring to attend to Dean Ackerman. Mike: I'm not sure what to make of this whole Academics thing yet.... Chris: Chainz right back on the attack, bodyslam lays out Douglas, and Chainz is going to the top! Mike: Wait, it looks like the Academics want some payback, and here goes Coach Brown! Chris: He's shaking the ropes, and Chainz falls onto the turnbuckle, and down to the mat. The referee was checking on Dean Ackerman, he never saw any of it! <<During a short section of steady rock music, coming back to the center of the screen, the AWE logo spins through the screen, leaving the previous scene still playing on the screen. The letters fly back, one at a time, to the middle of the screen. With the logo centered in large letters filling most of the screen, a small banner slides on the screen from the right, with Wednesday Wrap-Up appearing under the AWE logo.>> <<We open to a newsdesk-type studio. The lights are dimmed, but the brighten quickly: >> Greetings all you AWE fans, I'm Chris Winchester, to my left is Sally Stryder, and welcome to the second week of our Wednesday Wrap-Up show. You just saw what took place during the rematch between international superstar Chris Douglas and the worldwide rock legend, Skull N. Chainz. Douglas was the scheduled opponent last week, after the now-famous unmasking of the Black Skull, revealing his true identity. But after Douglas defeated Chainz cleanly, the rocker asked for a rematch and was given one on AWEsome Monday Night. <<footage of the match>> The two went at it all over again, with some dazzling counters and fantastic matwork. However, when the Academics came to the ring to observe, this happened. Without realizing they were there, Chainz sent Douglas over the top rope right onto Dean Ackerman. And to pay him back, a fellow Academic, Coach Brown, disrupted Chainz's momentum by knocking him off the top turnbuckle. Sally: That is correct, Chris. Chainz went on to rally back and win the match <<clip>> with his famous Skull Crusher manuever, scoring the win. However, both men are disturbed due to the presence of the Academics during their match. Chris Douglas is standing by with commentary: <<Cut to video: >> (Chris Douglas is sitting in the locker room area after AWEsome Monday Night with his head in his hands. He suddenly looks up at the camera.) Chris Douglas: Academics, you guys are the biggest fools I've ever known. You messed up my perfect record here, and while other people that barely interview get the wins, a cheap interference by you cost me mine. I challenge either of you.... no, wait all of you to a gauntlet match. I take you on one after the other. You might think you were trying to be my friend, but standing there on the outside sticking your beaks in put me off my game. You idiots wouldn't know a technical move if it jumped up and smacked you in the face. So, I'll take a match with any of you, any time, any place. In fact, get your little tag team out there, and I'll take any partner they throw my way. Dallas, Bob, anyone. I'll take any partners to get you moronic pieces of trash in the ring. All I need is a couple of people in my corner to stand there and look pretty and make the match legal while I beat the Academics within an inch of their lives. You academics could have jumped on my bandwagon, but you cost me, and you pay. I expect a formal apology from you guys, or you will not know what hit you. Skull N. Chainz, I gave you a rematch, now you give me a rematch. A man that prides himself on sucking up to the fans clearly does not know what wrestling is all about. God only knows how you pulled off that upset, but never again. At the next card, I challenge you to a match, and you'd better dig your grave, because I'm gonna put you in it. Sign the match, or face the consequences. (Camera fades.) <<Back to the studio: >> Sally: Obviously, Chris Douglas is not taking this lightly. However, neither is his opponent, Skull N. Chainz. Earlier today, our own Joel Trubin was able to get a few words with Skull N. Chainz: <<Cut to video: >> (Skull N. Chainz is in the sound booth of a recording studio, relaxed, wearing jeans and a 'Whips and Chains" T-shirt. He has his trademark black hat in hand, and he fingers it as he faces Joel Trubin.) Joel: I understand you are quite upset with the way things went down on AWEsome Monday Night. The fact that you were able to overcome the interference by Coach Brown of the Academics is a credit to your resilience, as well as your ability in the squared circle. Many people out there are interested to hear what you have to say on the matter: Skull: Well, Joel, I have no idea what they were doing out there. I'm not a mind reader, I'm a wrestler and an ex-musician. But the moment Coach Brown put his hands on me, he crossed the line from observer to participant. Now the fans came out to see me wrestle Chris Douglas, not the Academics. But if these men want a match, I'd be more than happy to oblige them. Joel: So, are you challenging the Academics as a whole? Skull: I know I would like to get Coach Brown in the ring, but I suspect it's a package deal - if I go after one of them, I'll end up with the other two interfering. So I'd much rather have them all in front of me, right where I can see them. I guess what I'm asking is for the AWE to grant me some sort of tag team match with them. Of course, I'll have to find a partner to do this, but that can be worked out later. Joel: There's no doubt the executive committee will consider your request. In the meantime, what are your thoughts on Chris Douglas. Skull: Hmm. In a way, I feel vindicated. I told the people I was able to beat Douglas, and I did it. I knew that I could, but I'll say it again. Chris Douglas, you are a fine competitor, and a close match in wrestling ability. I know our paths will cross again, and I actually look forward to it. We've each won a match against each other, and you really tested my skills, as I'm sure I did to you. So anytime you want a match with Skull N. Chainz, you got it Douglas. Joel: Thank you again for your time. <<Back to the studio: >> Chris: And we thank Joel Trubin for getting yet another great interview with Skull N. Chainz. Also last night, the announcement was made concerning the first Pay Per View event for the American Wrestling Enterprise: AWE presents Rocktober, on October 27th, live from the United Center in Chicago. And we're told the World Heavyweight championship, as well as the Tag Team titles will be decided at this event, right Sarah? Sarah: That is correct, Chris. I've been speaking to the front office the last few days, and I've also been told the comissioner of the AWE, whoever is chosen for that position, will make a announcement concerning the circumstances of the title matches at Rocktober. This announcement will be next week on AWEsome Monday Night. Chris: We will certainly be looking forward to that. On that card, we will see a match that was supposed to happen last Monday, 'Mr. Fantastic' Robert Richmond vs. Bob, the taxi driver. This match had to be postponed when a careless mistake happened. <<footage>> As we saw earlier this week, Richmond stopped to argue with some fans, while Bob was driving out to the ring. Richmond has been examined, and he is cleared to wrestle. Sarah: Good to know. Also on the card will be a debut of the rough rider from New Mexico, Jeremiah Jones. Here's what he had to tell us: <<Cut to video: >> Jeremiah Jones here. I don't know what more they expect me to say. I told them already. I'm coming to the AWE. That's all there is to know. <<Back to the studio: >> Chris: We were told he was a man of few words, but I never realized how few. (chuckles) Sarah: Also, we will see the debut of another man, who hails from New York City. He is known as "Butcher" Steven Jensen: <<Cut to video: >> The camera moves through the darkness past hanging shapes you can't quite make out, towards an area that's dimly lit. Soon, you can tell that the shapes are huge slabs of raw meat - You're in a meat locker. Voice: Hey, you! The camera turns and you see a towering man standing with a bloody apron on, holding a rather menacing-looking meat cleaver. Man: Heh, heh. So, you're here. Took ya long enough, sucker. The man raises his cleaver and takes a step forward. The camera's viewpoint wavers a little bit, and moves back slightly. The man chuckles. Man: Git over here, sucker. I'm not finished with ya. Yo! You AWE fans! My name is Stephen Jensen... But you can call me da Butcher. And there's nothin' I like more than fresh meat! I'm tired 'o da weaklings I been kickin' tha crap out of on da circut, so I figgered I'd come here and see if dem chickenshit pro-fesh-in-all wrestlers could stay on der feet for more than a few seconds in da ring with me! Heh, heh. Who's first? I wanna break me some bones. The Butcher's eyes shift to fall upon the camera. He smiles sadistically and begins to walk towards the screen as the viewpoint shakes, turns around, and begins bobbing up and down to the sound of running footsteps. The screen goes black. <<Back to video: >> Chris: Now let me assure you our cameraman is safe and sound, with no harm done to him. I don't know if we'll be able to say the same when the Butcher hits the ring next Monday Night. Sarah: Chris, while we were running that last segment, I received this communique from the AWE front office. It seems they have decided to hold a match this Monday Night, with the Academics to take on Skull N. Chainz ... AND Chris Douglas! As well as a third partner of their choosing, anyone not already wrestling on Monday Night's card. Chris: That is an amazing development! I can't wait to see the reactions of everyone involved, as well as who Chainz and Douglas choose to be the third man in their corner. Sarah: At this time, we also have the cards for the shows this week.: Thursday, September 11th Convention Hall - Ashbury Park, NJ 7:00 Bell Time LiveWire vs. Los Diablos Mad Hatter vs. Maniac Warrior Colin Green vs. 'Mr. Fantastic' Robert Richmond Slasher vs. Skull N. Chainz Brooklyn Boys vs. 2 Smooth 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie vs. El Phantasmo 'Northern Sensation' Dave Gubbins vs. Masked Maniac B Hound Dogs vs. Academics Chris Douglas vs. Apocalypse Now Jonny Fontaine vs. Kailef Vincenzo Galliano vs. Masked Maniac A Saturday, September 13th Civic Center - Poughkeepsie, NY Skull N. Chainz/??? vs. Academics El Diablo 2 vs. "Northern Sensation" Dave Gubbins "Mr. Fantastic" Robert Richmond vs. Jonny Fontaine LiveWire vs. Hound Dogs Mad Hatter vs. Maniac Warrior Masked Maniac A vs. 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie Masked Maniac B vs. Kailef 2 Smooth vs. Brooklyn Boys Apocalypse Now vs. Bob Vincenzo Galliano vs. El Diablo 1 Chris Douglas vs. Slasher Chris: Those will be some great shows, there's no doubt. That's about all the time we have this week, but before we go, let's leave you with this hilarious footage of LiveWire's match last Monday, where they were scheduled to face the Masked Maniacs - these maniacs were masked, but the masks came off to reveal a very different tag team. For Sarah Stryder and myself, Chris Winchester, have a great night everyone, and we'll see you this Saturday morning for the Weekend Wake Up! Good night, all! <<Cut to match footage, LiveWire vs. "Masked Maniacs", already in progress: >> Chris: Do you remember the Masked Maniacs being this... well... small?
