AWE Wednesday Wrap-Up, 4 September 1997

4 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<<The AWE logo is centered on the screen. It rotates and spins away, becoming a small mark in the upper corner, with the following video segment appearing underneath: >> <<The crowd cheers immensely as Black Skull walks out of the entrance without music. An AWE crew member comes over to stand beside him, carrying a bag. Black Skull looks to the crowd in several directions, and makes the motion, indicating the removal of the mask. Finally, he starts to unlace the mask.>> Chris: Here we go, this is it! Who is the Black Skull? <<The mask is removed slowly, revealing...>> Chirs: It's Skull N. Chainz! From the 80's rock band, Whips and Chains! How many million records did those guys sell around the world? Mike: You mean this guy that everyone's been so ga-ga about is nothing more than a heavy metal screamer? What a ripoff! <<The biggest hit from Whips and Chains, "Raging Love" comes to life over the house speakers.>> Chris: He wasn't kidding, we was recognized all right! This crowd is on its feet, and from the package, Chainz takes his trademark black hat from the bag, and he's doing his strut for his fans. I can't imagine this place getting any louder! <<During a short sectoin of steady rock music, coming back to the center of the screen, the AWE logo spins through the screen, leaving the previous scene still playing on the screen. The letters fly back, one at a time, to the middle of the screen. With the logo centered in large letters filling most of the screen, a small banner slides on the screen from the right, with Wednesday Wrap-Up appearing under the AWE logo.>> Hello everyone! I'm Tim Cameron along with Sarah Stryder, and welcome to the AWE's Wednesday Night Wrap-Up. More than just the wrestling community, the whole world was shocked by the unmasking of international superstar Black Skull last Monday before the main event match. As you've already seen, Skull N. Chainz, formerly of the rock band "Whips and Chains," has spent these years following the band's breakup becoming the international wrestling sensation Black Mask. Tonight, we will have an exclusive interview with the elusive rocker. Sarah: Also tonight, we saw some impressive debuts, including the monster of a man known as Vincenzo Galliano. Besides winning his match, <<footage>> he turned down the offer of newcomer tag team Brooklyn Boys in definitive fashion. As you see here, he let his actions speak for him, laying out the already-defeated team. We attempted to get word from his about his match, however <<footage>> we were denied admission to his dressing room after the conclusion of the show, and were unable to hear his comments. We will again attempt to get some words from the former World Champion Galliano - keep watching the AWE network of shows, as we will air this as soon as we recieve it. Tim: We also saw an unusual newcomer to the wrestling ring in the man known simply as Bob. This New York City cab driver calls the Bronx his home, and was quite noticable at the AWE tryouts held recently. <<footage>> A last minute stand-in, Bob showed he can handle himself by defeating the veteran 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie. He would later be challenged by 'Mr. Fantastic' Robert Richmond <<footage>>, and this match will be held next week on AWEsome Monday Night. Here are some comments from both competitors: <<Cut to video: >> BOB -- Hey, you know I think I like doin' this wrestlin' here in the American Wrastlin' Enterprise. They tell me I can do this as long as I want, so I'll stick it out and see how it goes. But I hear this bag o' wind Richmond talkin' a buncha garbage about takin' me out. Well if you wanna piece a me, I got no problem wid dat. We can meet up in the ring next week, and I'll run all over you just like you was some punk skippin' out on a fare! <<Fade out, Fade in: >> I am Fantastic. You are not. That, my bumbling friend, is the bottom line. For even though I was troubled in my last match, the truth is I am imply that much more fantastic than any other AWE would-be wrestler. Many will try, but all will fail, so accept my challenge and I will show you, and all the people in the stands, that you are just not as fantastic as I. <<Return to the announcing desk: >> Sarah: This should be an exciting match-up, as two up-and-coming AWE combatants prove themselves. Tim: I agree. Last Monday also saw the strong debut of 'Maverick' Dave Dallas, already establishing himself as a name with his first match anywhere becoming a win. He also garnered much fan support, as the crowd was truly on it's feet for this 18-year old young man out of Austin, Texas. Here are some pre-recorded comments from 'Maverick': <<Cut to video: >> (Camera opens on a standard locker room. A young man, barely more than a teenager, is smiling wide. He looks confident and yet hopeful. Standing 5'11" with a fair degree of muscle, he is happy, eager as he speaks:) Hello everyone in AWE! I am the "Maverick," and I've spent my whole life wanting to be a professional wrestler, to step in the squared circle. All through high school, I trained hard, and learned everything I could. Because I wanted to get in the ring, and show everyone what real wrestling is all about! I'm hoping everyone enjoys what I do in the ring, because I'm here to put on some great action to wow the fans of AWE. To Mr. Fantastic, you were the first person I wrestled professionally, and I know if I did it once, I can do it again. To everyone else I will face, I know I may not look like much, and I know I'm young, but don't underestimate me, because I won't rest until I've given everything I can in the ring. Dave Dallas is a lot of things, and there is a lot he's not - but one thing is certain, nobody, but nobody, has more heart than me! Yeee-hah! <<Back to the booth: >> Tim: Wow, this young man may just go all the way to the top, both here in the AWE and in his career conquests. We certainly wish him the best. Sarah: I agree. Well, our top story is undoubtedly the astounding unveilment of Skull N. Chainz underneath the mask on AWEsome Monday Night. Unfortunately, his return to the spotlight was marred by his loss in the first AWE match of Chris Douglas, an outstanding technical wrestler who had a great match with the rocker. Before we take you to the interview with Skull N. Chainz, we have this statement from Chris Douglas: <<Cut to video: >> (Camera opens with Chris Douglas in the AWE locker room area. He looks into the camera and starts to speak.) Chris Douglas: I'm glad to see they respect me around here. Well, enough to put me on the main event so that I can showcase my talent. But Skull N. Chains? Come on, that over rated dirt bag had the privilege of facing me? It's an outrage! I got to show all you moronic fans, and all you wannabe wrestlers how a real man wrestles. I'm not going to sugar-coat any of it, because the fact of the matter is, Skull N. Chains got beat up by the awesome wrestling ability of Chris Douglas. You know the drill... some upstart hopeful steps in the ring with me, wants a piece of me, and tries to further his career, and then falls flat on his face. Literally. Skull, you did well for someone who's career isn't even particularly in wrestling. A rock musician, and not a particularly good one at that. You can take your loud blaring music somewhere else, because it isn't welcome where I work, and I work here in the AWE. Take a look, AWE wrestlers and fans, at the total wrestling machine, Chris Douglas. The man who will take wrestling into the future, and secure a place for this federation in amongst the top federations of all time. I've proven my ability time and time again, and I'll prove it here every time I step into the ring. Sure, you people can stand up and punch each other, but you can't out wrestle Chris Douglas, and by the looks of things, very few people here will be able to beat me. The first thing I did when I got here was to take a look at the competition. We have an ex-rock n' roller, who is probably by now contemplating retirement. We have a cab driver of all things. Bob the cab driver. I ridicule that man. A young upstart named the Maverick. Maverick, if you're looking for a wrestling lesson, then you've come to the right place, pal. Maverick, you can have all the heart and courage in the world, but you don't have the talent to compete with me. A fan hugging people pleaser is what you are, and trust me, I know, they don't make it in the ring. There are plenty of others here who will one by one fall to Chris Douglas, the greatest wrestler the world has ever witnessed. Skull N. Chains was merely the first to be taken apart piece by piece, and I have plenty more punishment to dish out. I'll see any of you AWE wrestlers in the ring. Just remember one thing, AWE... I'm Chris Douglas. Nothing more needs to be said. (Camera fades.) <<Back to the booth: >> Sarah: Strong words from Douglas, who will face both Bob and Dallas in cards this coming weekend. But now, here is an interview from the man of the hour, the superstar Skull N. Chainz: <<Cut to video: >> <<Backstage, an average size man, wearing black wrestling tights under a black trenchcoat, is sitting in a plastic chair. In his hand is a black mask, decorated by the skull-and-crossbones of the face area. He appears very relaxed as Joel Trubin, seated on the opposite chair, interviews him:>> Joel: Skull N. Chainz, your career in the music industry is known to all. Whether they liked your style of music, your on-stage antics, or they did not, you were one of the most recognizable figures in the music of the 1980's. The breakup of "Whips and Chains" was expected by those on the inside of the music industry, although it seemed to cause quite a surprise to your fans. While this subject could fill a whole interview, this is not the matter at hand... Skull: Of course, Joel, this isn't VH-1, this is the AWE. You want to ask about wrestling. Joel: Precisely. Many wonder what caused you to put on the wrestling tights in the first place. Why the sudden change in career? Skull: I had always kept in shape, even when we toured Japan, Europe, and our one tour in Russia. And as I said before, I've always been a closet wrestling fan. Everywhere I went, my roadies would sneak tapes into my dressing room of the latest wrestling shows, and if there wasn't anything new to see, they would bring me old ones! Joel: So you decided this was something you wanted to pursue? Skull: Yes. As a musician, I had helped create three Top Ten albums, with 8 singles reaching number one on the charts. What could I possibly do that I hadn't already done? Make more music? I suppose, but I had done that. I wanted to participate in something that was completely new to me. Joel: Such as professional wrestling. Skull: Exactly, Joel. I had no worries financially, as I was very careful about the money made from the Chains albums. Let's just say I invested wisely. So I could take time off and see if I would be able to get in the ring, not as a musician trying out a new P.R. stunt, but as a legitimate wrestler, which is why I put on the mask - so the fans could take me seriously. Joel: One last question, if you don't mind. Chris Douglas. What are your thoughts on your match against him? Skull: I don't mind at all. Douglas is an outstanding competitor, though it seems as though he's trying to convince himself as much as anyone else. But in the ring, he certainly backed up his words. I'm no stranger to loss - I lost quite a few matches when I first started, it's good for character. However, I would like another shot at him, and if the AWE officials will sign it, I'd like to take on Chris Douglas one more time. Joel: Thank you again. Skull N. Chainz, exclusively here in the AWE. Tim? <<Back to the booth: >> Tim: And that match has been signed for AWEsome Monday Night next week - a return match between Chris Douglas and the rock sensation Skull N. Chainz. Sarah: Before we go, let's run down the cards for this weekend: Friday, September 5th 7:30 p.m. Harrisburg, PA Vincenzo Galliano vs. Bob El Phantasmo vs. Jonny Fontaine Slasher vs. Skull N. Chainz LiveWire vs. Masked Maniacs 'Mr. Fantastic' Robert Richmond vs. Kailef Colin Green vs. 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie Brooklyn Boys vs. Academics 'Maverick' Dave Dallas vs. Chris Douglas Sunday, September 7th 2:00 p.m. Pittsburgh, PA El Phantasmo vs. Colin Green Academics vs. Brooklyn Boys Kailef vs. Maniac Warrior Jonny Fontaine vs. 'Maverick' Dave Dallas Slasher vs. Vincenzo Galliano Masked Maniacs vs. LiveWire Skull N. Chainz vs. 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie Chris Douglas vs. Bob Tim: That's all the time we have. Join us next week, where we'll have more exclusive interviews, as well as footage from the next AWEsome Monday Night. In the meantime, don't forget to check out AWE Saturday Morning, with more footage and interviews. For Sarah Stryder, and myself Tim Cameron, this is AWE signing off! <<The lights on the studio fade as the AWE logo appears, and we fade to black.>>
