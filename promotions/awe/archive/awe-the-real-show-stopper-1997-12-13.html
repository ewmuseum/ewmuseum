"The Real Show Stopper Stops the Show", 13 December 1997

13 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From AWE's AWEsome Monday Night. APW World Champion Chris Douglas decides that it's time the show stops here, as both feds gear up for the champion vs. champion match on Wednesday 31st December at War of the Worlds... *** Mike: He was robbed! Oscar the Grouch should never have barred the Academics from ringside! It was all part of Douglas's gameplan, and the striped-shirted moron ruined it! Chris: That not withstanding, the match for tonight is Galliano and Creed, for the Heavyweight title. <<The Godfather enters, leading the way for his man Vincenzo Galliano, while the theme from the movie "The Godfather" echoes across the air. Stopping to cuss out the occasional fan, the duo finally gets to the ring, and Galliano steps through the ropes, giving the finger to the people in the stands in general.>> Mike: Gee, and he wonders why they boo. Big dummy. Chris: What? I was under the impression you were impressed by this man. Mike: Ah, I used to be, but he's lost a few steps. He's just not as vicious as he used to be. Chris: That may be, but he's still the World Champion - though that may change within minutes. <<"Drag the Waters" rips through the arena, and Creed cracks his knuckles on his way to the ring. His eyes lock with Galliano, though the distance separates them, the meaning clear. Angel accompanies her man, slapping the hand of a fan or two on the way.>> Chris: Both these men are fierce competitors, and this promises to be a great match! Mike: I'm telling you, Creed does *not* deserve another shot at the title! Chris: I know you may think so, but these fans would solidly disagree! Mike: What, the sheep? Who cares? I mean, really, if you're going to... <<The pageantry of "Pomp and Circumstance" causes numerous boos and jeers. Chris Douglas arrives, with the remaining three Academics in tow. Douglas walks over to the ring announcer's table and grabs a mic.>> Chris: What the....? Mike: Yes! Come on, Chris, tell them what's on your mind! Douglas: Hold it. Hold it right there. Since I'm the show stopper, I demand that the show stops right here!! <<Crowd erupts in boos as Galliano and Creed stop and stare at Douglas as he climbs into the ring.>> Chris Douglas: EVERYONE knows that it should be me in there wrestling for that title belt, but I'll get to that in a moment. You saw it several weeks ago as I was screwed out of the world title tournament. You saw it when Faulkenberg gave me no explanation as to why I had to take the fall for a lesser opponent. And then tonight, against Bulldog Bruce MacDonald, I had to take the fall again so to speak. A man that everyone, including himself, knows that I could beat anywhere else. So why not here? I'll tell you why not. It's because I'm such a huge megastar that the execs can't handle the fact that I'm the real champion. <<Douglas walks over to Creed, who glares at him in defiance.>> Douglas: Creed, would you like me to remind you what I did to your career somewhere else? I didn't think so. And I don't think you need me to remind you that you suck either. You were begging to be my tag team partner a while ago. I took you to the big time, but you couldn't handle it. You don't deserve to be in this match. It should be me. You had an easy paved way to the final, and you couldn't even beat this man. (Points to Galliano) <<Douglas walks over to Galliano, who is cracking his knuckles.>> Douglas: Vincenzo Galliano. Vinman, may I call you that? Well I'm going to anyway. How did you get to the final match? By beating anyone credible? Nope. I was forced to make someone else look good just so that you could beat them. Did you know that? Well you do now. <<Douglas walks to the center of the ring as Creed leans against the ropes impatiently, Galliano gesturing, clearly upset at the interruption.>> Douglas: Everyone knows I'm the real main eventer around here. There is nobody that can compete with me. I've been doing what I've been told up until now, aside from the Faulkenberg figure four incident. Well no more. I was the first wrestler to sign a contract here. I was the man who filled the seats. First three main events, I was there baby. The main event is where I should be now, and I demand that I am. I demand a World title shot. I deserve it. I've beaten both of you two before and I can beat you anytime again. I made this federation, and as of right now, I'm going to be the one to bring it down. <<Creed yawns.>> Douglas: I'm the real champion, and you damn well know it. Enough of the whining from Creed and Galliano... Oh wait, neither of them seem to show up to do interviews. Hmm, there's something pretty odd going on there. And they're put across as the two wrestlers in the main event? I hate to make false accusations, so I won't. I'll make a truthful and valid accusation. These two shouldn't be here, they should be cleaning out my locker room where they belong. The accusation that they're being put across as something they're not is a pretty strong accusation I know, but it's the truth. <<Creed looks at Galliano, points at Douglas, and shrugs. Galliano shrugs back.>> Douglas: And everyone knows that too. Go ahead and ask the fans who should have the belt around their waist, and even though they supposedly despise me, if they're totally honest, they'll tell you that I'm the real champion. I'm the only one man enough to take the fall, but never again in this rinky dink small time organization. From now on, you executives had better pay attention. You'd better do something for me. I'll give you a hint... <<Douglas walks over to Creed and pushes him hard, getting a scowl in response. Douglas then walks over to Galliano and shoves him hard too. Creed and Galliano start to advance as Douglas starts to yell.>> Douglas: This is for the so-called executives! Just like I've given these two morons, you can give ME a big push Chris: Vincenzo and Creed have had enough of this! They're going to shut Douglas up the hard way! Mike: What is this?! What happened to freedom of speech? Huh? Chris: Here come the Academics! They are putting the boots to Creed, and Galliano has Coach Brown and Dr. Andrews all over him! Mike: That's more like it! Take these boys back to school...! Chris: Galliano taking a shot in the head from those gold tag team belts! Mike: Keep them after class...! Chris: Creed being held by Ackerman, as Douglas wraps a chain around his fist, pounding the face of Creed! Mike: And give them all detention! AWE Homepage: http://members.aol.com/creedp/AWE/HQ.html
