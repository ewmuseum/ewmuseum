AWE "UPI Press Release", 8 November 1997

8 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WRESTLING FEDERATION PUT IN SLEEPERHOLD (UPI) This past weekend, the fledging pro-wrestling organization known as American Wrestling Enterprise, or AWE, suffered a setback when the FCC suspended their television coverage for 14 days, due to an improperly filed permit for their planned Pay Per View in the United Center in Chicago. Sources say this was a minor matter that could have been easily overlooked, but it is admittedly enforced routinely. The Executive Vice President of the AWE, Willam Faulkenberg, Esq. held a press conference to clear up the matter. He explained the reasons behind the suspension, and stated that the Pav Per View event, Rocktober, would be held as soon as the suspension is over, on November 9th. He also added that despite the fact the event would now be held in November, there would in fact be no change in the name of the broadcast. It was further announced that normal programming for the group will resume after Rocktober. A top executive at AWE, who asked to remain nameless, claims to have been terminated from the company for his reporting the infraction to the FCC. He says a certain 'other organization' approached him, requesting he review the files to determine everything was in order and report anything that was not. He has since filed suit against his former employers, who released him for 'conflict of interest.' -- Creed -- AWE Homepage: http://members.aol.com/creedp/AWE/HQ.html
