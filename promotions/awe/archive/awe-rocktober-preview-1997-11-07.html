AWE Rocktober Preview Special, 7 November 1997

7 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is the transmission, now that the FCC matter has been cleared up. Also, a new Internet wrestling news source has arrived. Check out this site: http://www.avincent.demon.co.uk/poops/news.html Rocktober arrives this Sunday, November 9th. Expect to see the beginnings of the next wave of wrestlers to enter AWE. If you want to put a flash into the Pay Per View, please send it in soon. The same applies for any strategy you may have. If it's already been received, then it has already been implemented into the show. Feel free to E-mail any questions you may have. Again, the line-up for Rocktober: Tarot vs. 'Giant' Joey Williams 2 Smooth vs. Tenesian Tribesmen Kailef vs. Wraith Jeremiah Jones vs. 'Eliminator' Eric Scott 'Northern Sensation' Dave Gubbins/Akira/? vs. Diablo 1 & 2/El Phantasmo Crossbones vs. Ezekiel Rage 'Maverick' Dave Dallas vs. 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie Hawaiian Crippler vs. 'Butcher' Steven Jensen *Cage Match* Chris Douglas vs. Skull N. Chainz *World Tag Team Championship* Academics vs. LiveWire *World Heavyweight Championship* Creed vs. Vincenzo Galliano We'll see you there! -- Creed AWE Homepage: http://members.aol.com/creedp/AWE/HQ.html Rocktober Preview Special: * Bob vs. 'Butcher' Steven Jensen (After Jensen was severely booed and more than one fan was removed for throwing things at him, Bob arrived in his cab, much to the delight of the crowd. In short order, Jensen had folded him in half with knees, elbows, and kicks. At one point, Bob pulled a desperation clothesline to knock the Butcher out of the ring, and Bob went out after him. Jensen tries a kick that would have taken Bob's head off, but Bob dropped down out of the way, and the big man shattered one of the windows of the taxi! Bob got him back in the ring, but couldn't lift the big man up for a body slam and paid the price. Jensen used several moves to wear him down, finally using the Meat Rack to finish him off and get the victory. After the match, he threw Bob out onto the hood of his cab, and the 'El Matador' Miguel Menendez rushed out to help him back to the dressing rooms.) * Ted Travail vs. Colin Green (Colin Green came out waving to the crowd - oddly enough, so did Travail, who chose to wrestle in his suit! Colin Green tried several lock ups, but Travail continually backed off, implying he wasn't ready. Finally, when Green looked around impatiently, Travail blindsided him with kicks to the knees. He used a DDT to stun Colin, and it looked like he reached into his pocket when he got up. Travail put slipped something around the neck of Green and put him in a sleeper hold, with his arms covering the object. Green quickly passed out, and after the referee checked the arm three times, he awarded Travail the win.) * 'Northern Sensation' Dave Gubbins/Akira vs. Diablo 1 & 2 (This match was nothing but action, as these four men would all be part of the six-man match in Rocktober. The Diablos showed great action, but they were clearly outclassed by the Japanese style of Akira and the odd adapted style of Gubbins. Modified arm drags, top rope planchas, Asai moonsaults, missile dropkicks, suicide dives, and the now-famous HurricanPlancha of Dave Gubbins marked this match as the fastest-paced out of the card. But the Blackpool Illuminations of Gubbins scored the win for his team. Unfortunately, El Phantasmo arrived at ringside to defend his teammates with a chair, bashing both Gubbins and Akira more than once, until security intervened.) * 'Giant' Joey Williams vs. Maniac Warrior (The crowd booed the Warrior, though not as much as the Giant. With Freckles Brown in tow, the cheating tactics of both men were obvious to the crowd, and during the brief, two-move comeback mounted by the Warrior, he was momentarily cheered! But after a whip into the turnbuckles, a Giant superkick spelled the end for the Maniac. Tarot rushed out to ringside, although Freckles tried to interfere. Security again arrived to prevent any further infighting.) * Kailef vs. 'Mr. Fantastic' Robert Richmond (Before the match, Richmond bragged about how lousy Kailef's wrestling style was, and how it simply did not hold up to the purely fantastic style of Richmond. In response, Kailef gave him a high side kick to start the match. While Richmond held his face, Kailef proceed to work over the legs and torso of his opponent, until the Jackhammer took down Mr. Fantastic.) * Jeremiah Jones/Crossbones vs. Ezekiel Rage/'Eliminator' Eric Scott (This match started off with a brawl, until Jones dumped Rage over the top, and a double clothesline knocked Eric Scott right out of the ring, causing the crowd to go wild. When the match resumed, Rage started against Crossbones, with nothing more than a basic brawl. Some actual wrestling moves were used after a bit, as Rage used a neckbreaker, backbreaker, and a couple of illegal moves to gain the advantage. But Crossbones came back with a flying clothesline and a Northern Lights suplex. He tagged in Jeremiah Jones, who went for the Hog Tie, but couldn't quite get it locked on Rage. Ezekiel Rage tagged in the Eliminator, and the two men in the ring brawled wildly, until the action spilled out of the ring. Rage rushed around the outside to blindside Crossbones, and those two begin to fight as well. Finally, the ref ruled it a double countout, and security had to come out once more to break it up.) * Creed vs. 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie (Rassie made his usual entrance, pretending he was shooing away all his female fans, who weren't that interested in him in the first place. Creed stormed to the ring, very upset. He went right after Rassie, pummeling and thrashing him. After several warnings by the referee, Creed slipped into a more conventional wrestling style, using a suplex, a piledriver, and a huge clothesline. Creed then called for the End of the Line, but the video wall lit up, and yet another video appeared on the wall, with more threats and mockings of Creed. Visibly distracted, Creed didn't notice Rassie sneaking up behind him, and after a quick roll-up, 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie scored a huge upset! Creed was furious, yelling at everyone until Angel calmed him down.) * LiveWire/'Maverick' Dave Dallas/Skull N. Chainz vs. Academics (Huge, massive, enormous, colossal crowd noise for LiveWire and company! But the Academics were booed, flipped off, etc. A couple fans threw trash at them, but they too were promptly removed. Maverick started off against Ackerman. The Dean and Dave Dallas wrestled, with a lot of reversals and counters. Then Dean Ackerman tagged in Chris Douglas, and they double teamed Dallas. However, Maverick slyly broke away and tagged in the 'Generator' Bruce Williams. He slammed Douglas twice, handling him with ease, but Dr. Andrews snuck in for a clipping shoulderblock to the back of Williams's knee, allowing Douglas to tag in Coach Brown. Brown and Douglas use kicks and a double suplex to take down Williams. Brown, followed by Dr. Andrews, each spent several minutes working over the Generator, until a double clothesline set him free, and he made the desperation tag to Shaun 'Sparks' Popper. He cleaned house after all four members of the Academics came in the ring with dropkicks, hiptosses, and a hurricarana to toss Dean Ackerman out of the ring. But the ref didn't see Chris Douglas sneak a chair in the ring and waffle Popper in the back. Chainz comes in and starts to brawl with Douglas, but the referee forced him out. Douglas wrestled Sparks, though he was not the legal man, until Popper reversed Douglas's Asai moonsault into a powerslam, finally making the tag to Chainz. Almost immediately, the other members of the Academics rushed the ring, as did Maverick and the Generator, and the ref threw the match out as a no contest. However, 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie came out of the back to jump Maverick, and Creed came out after him! Then Vincenzo Galliano, the WidowMaker, Kailef, Jensen, Mr. Fantastic, Akira, Gubbins, Diablos, El Phantasmo and soon the whole area was covered in fights and brawls, and the show ended on that, just in time to see Ted Travail at the entrance, surveying the area.)
