MegaCorp to Complete Latest Hostile Takeover Virgin Island (AP) - The infamous Multi-national conglomerate. MegaCorp, added to their wrestling division today by purchasing American Wrestling Enterprises (AWE). The group will be merged with their current promotion, All Pro Wrestling (APW), with a Pay Per View event on New Year's Eve. The champions of the two federations will meet in a unification bout, with the victorious league taking over the loser. The show will air on the MegaCorp Sports Network (MCSN) at 9PM Nationwide (tape delay to the West coast and Central areas), running until the stroke of midnight.
