I am not an imposter. I'm not a charlatan or a pretender. I'm just someone that also goes by the name of CreeD. The Creed that you refer to in your message isn't the "real" Creed. That's like some guy named Bob calling himself the "real" Bob and accusing everyone else named Bob of being an imposter. The Creed that runs AWE and myself, have in fact cleared up this misunderstanding. Also, I never said that the WWA that I'm involved with(a completely different fed than the one you run) is "mine". I am not involved in any way in running it. I just roleplay there and thought I could lure some more wrestlers into the fold. Before I end, I just want to say that the WWA is a brand new fed that is accepting new wrestlers now.
