AWE House Show Results, 12 September 1997

12 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thursday, September 11th Convention Hall - Ashbury Park, NJ 7:00 Bell Time Report by [name withheld]. --- LiveWire vs. Los Diablos Los Diablos are apparently a new tag team in the AWE, Lucha Libre style, complete with matching outfits and masks. They tried to use this to their advantage, making switches behind the referee's back. But they were no match for LiveWire, who showed some great moves, including a running springboard plancha (a la TAKA Michinoku) from Sparks. Another great spot was when the Generator launched Sparks from the top rope into Los Diablos, with Popper nailing the new team with a double clothesline. Los Diablos has potential, but tonight was a rough debut for the Mexican team. Winner: LiveWire, with the Electric Chair. --- Mad Hatter vs. Maniac Warrior The Maniac Warrior came out with his usual reckless abandon, but the crowd (like me) is tired of his same old schtick, and booed him loudly. The Mad Hatter then came out, with a Jamiroquai hat, doing the same moving-floor kind of movements. He used this same kind of slide to evade several rushes by the Warrior, who usually crashed into the turnbuckles. Finally, Hatter ducked a clothesline and used a faceslam to stun the Warrior, hit his finishing move, and get the win. Winner: The Mad Hatter, with the Hat Trick. He danced his way to the back afterwards. --- Colin Green vs. 'Mr. Fantastic' Robert Richmond Before the match, Richmond grabbed the house mike and completely insulted Green, belittling everything from his choice of clothes (a karate uniform, or gi) to his ruffled hair. Finally Green grabbed the mike, smacked Richmond, and went for a pin. But of course, it takes more than a microphone to pin a wrestler. Green didn't seem to have the killer instinct to stay on Richmond, and a missed move led to a Richmond comeback. Richmond got the submission but wouldn't let go until Green admitted Richmond was more fantastic than he was. Winner: Robert Richmond, with the Fantastic Finish. --- Slasher vs. Skull N. Chainz The crowd erupted in cheers and hand-clapping for Chainz, and he was giving it right back. However, he spent too much time enjoying the fans' attention, as he didn't even notice Slasher sneak up into the right. Despite the blindsiding, Chainz came back with some creative moves, such turning a bodyslam attempt by Slasher into a sunset flip. There was little offense Chainz didn't counter, and it didn't take long for the rocker to hit the Crusher and get the win, partying with the crowd afterwards. Winner: Skull N. Chainz, after the Skull Crusher. --- Brooklyn Boys vs. 2 Smooth 2 Smooth came out first, high-fiving the crowd and even Skull N. Chainz on his way out. The Brooklyn Boys rushed the ring, but fell back after Funk and Groove kept them at bay. The Boys grabbed the house mike and tried to intimidate 2 Smooth. It didn't work. Instead, 2 Smooth lauched an all-out assault, with some great tandem manuevers. The fans were on their feet when Funk used Groove's hands as a step to launch up to the top turnbuckle and kept going for a huge plancha. Winner: 2 Smooth, following the Party Crasher. --- 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie vs. El Phantasmo El Phantasmo put on some great high-flying moves in his Luchador style, and Rassie tore himself away from trying to impress the female fans long enough to show some decent proficiency. Phantasmo attempted a hurricarana outside the ring, but Rassie fell back, letting the Lucha Libre star crash into the guardrail. I feel if Rassie spent more time concentrating on wrestling, he could go far. Winner: 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie used his Flash in the Pan to score a victory. --- 'Northern Sensation' Dave Gubbins vs. Masked Maniac B The AWE oddity known as Dave Gubbins was surprisingly popular, as his odd English mannerisms made the crowd laugh. When he waved his miner's hat, fans waved hats back at him. The Masked Maniac received a lukewarm reception at best, and his wrestling seemed to match. Trying to rely on big power moves, Maniac B fell victim to some clever moves by the smaller man. Winner: 'Northern Sensation' Dave Gubbins, using the Blackpool Illuminations to win. --- Hound Dogs vs. Academics This match was a complete waste, and for a while the 'boring' chants were heard. The Academics embarassed the Hound Dogs, and Dean Ackermen even took time to take out Uncle Pete outside the ring. Coach Brown and Dr. Andrews completely squashed these two Dogs. Winner: Academics (Brown & Andrews) with the Crash Course. --- Chris Douglas vs. Apocalypse Now AWE Security escorted Douglas to the ring to prevent an altercation with the Academics, and it was apparently a good idea, though Douglas didn't seem to happy about it. He certainly took his frustrations out on Apocalypse, who suffered move after move out of Douglas's arsenal. Leglocks, moonsaults, and submission holds galore, until Chris Douglas seemed to get bored, and finished off his opponent and left promptly. Winner: Chris Douglas with a huge top rope DDT. --- Jonny Fontaine vs. Kailef The match was also a disappointment. While Kailef showed some great moves, he only needed three of them to put Fontaine away. Kailef evaded the first few moves Fontaine attempted, then used a side kick to double over Fontaine, followed by chopping kick to the back of his head. He finished with that strange move of his, almost a judo throw with an airplane spin thrown in for good measure. Winner: Kailef after the Jackhammer. --- Vincenzo Galliano vs. Masked Maniac A Galliano was so hated, they ended up cheering Maniac A - strange, considering the fans didn't seem to care much for his partner earlier in the night. This Maniac didn't fare any better than the other one, though. Galliano absolutely crushed the masked man, with devastating clotheslines, a great Russian legsweep, and a devastating piledriver. He even hit a top rope plancha - quite unusual for a man his size. His jumping powerbomb finisher looked like it might have ended the Maniac's career, and the Godfather proudly congradulated Vincenzo afterwards. Winner: Vincenzo Galliano with the Concrete Boots.
