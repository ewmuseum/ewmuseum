AWE Weekend Wake Up, 20 December 1997

20 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*********************************************************************** T H E A W E P R E S E N T S Saturday, December 20th, 1997 A W E W E E K E N D W A K E U P *********************************************************************** (A quiet morning, in a quiet suburban neighborhood. The sun is almost over the horizon, birds are chirping pleasantly, flowers are in bloom, and somewhere off in the distance, a rooster crows. A young boy, not quite in his teens, is pedalling a bicycle through the neighborhood delivering the morning newspaper, smiling happily the whole time. As see his face up close, his jaw drops and his eyes widen, just before a huge Thai kick from 'Butcher' Steven Jensen knocks him off his bike. The newspaper in his hand flies over to smack against the front door of a nearby house. A man opens said door, yawning over his cup of coffee. His face is suddenly fearful, and he gasps as Smasher knocks him down with a huge clothesline.) (Bob's cab pulls up by the downed paperboy, and the Mad Hatter, Bob, Kailef, and Dave Gubbins all get out. From the other direction, a Cadillac stops in the street, with the members of the Academics stepping out to rush the other men. A limousine pulls alongside the Cadillac, and from the sunroof Chris Douglas climbs out to execute a moonsault. A minivan turns and screechs to a halt sideways, with LiveWire, Akira, and 'Maverick' Dave Dallas joining the fray in the middle of the street.) (The Brooklyn Boys and 'Giant' Joey Williams are seen running between the cars, as is 'Mr. Fantastic' Robert Richmond, who is yanked back by a lasso thrown from the horse of Jeremiah Jones. 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie is dancing on the roof of the minivan, but he is knocked off by the landing gear of a helicopter, from which Skull N. Chainz steps out, his guitar slung over his back. Ezekiel Rage walks out from between the cars, pulling his hood back, and 'Eliminator' Eric Scott is running to the brawl over the tops of the vehicles. The WidowMaker gets out of a hearse, Xern Nymtha climbing out of the back as Creed kicks the door shut while riding by on his Harley. Vincenzo Galliano watches the whole event while standing atop a large mailbox, the World Title slung over his shoulder. As the rumble continues in the street, the camera pans over to a bedroom window. The curtain is pulled aside, and a shocked homeowner watching the scene pulls his blanket over his head and goes back to bed.) (As the rumble continues in the street, the camera pans over to a bedroom window. The curtain is pulled aside, and a shocked homeowner watching the scene pulls his blanket over his head and goes back to bed.) (The horizon is shown, an the AWE logo rises from behind it, with rays of sunshine illuminating it. The words 'Weekend Wake Up' rise up below AWE, forming this show's logo. The sun's rays become brighter and brighter, until the screen is filled with a flash of white light. When it fades out, an arena is shown, the Weekend arena.) Tim Cameron: Gooooood Morning everyone, and welcome to the weekend! I'm your host, Tim Cameron, and this morning in addition to footage and results from the AWE weekend card, this week coming at you from Queens, New York, we have some exclusive interviews, and the upcoming line up for AWEsome Monday Night. So, pull up a chair, grab a coffee, turn the TV up, and let's see if we can't wake up the neighbors! Our opening match pits the aptly named 'Flashy' Rick Rassie taking on Ted Travail. Let's get to the action, folks! <<Cut to ringside>> * Ted Travail vs. 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie Ted Travail came to the ring to some fairly loud cheers from the fans. As 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie came to the ring to boos, he gyrated his hips and made various other gestures to the crowd. While Rassie took his time getting into the ring and started strutting around, Ted Travail got the upper hand with a somewhat questionable blow and locked in an armbar. Rassie backed him into the corner, sends him into the ropes and hit him with a running dropkick to gain the advantage. The match continued in this fashion, with Travail attempting armbars and other such submission style rest holds, and Ricki Rassie trying to take to the air. Rassie hit with a vertical suplex, and once again took to the air as he went to the top rope for a flying legdrop, only to find nobody home as Travail rolled out of the way, and had enough time to recover and lock in a cross-face chicken wing. Rassie was able to get to the ropes, but was worn down enough for Travail to hit with what appeared to be a clean blow, but was possibly low, and then a brainbuster to the delight of the fans for the one, two, three. * Winner: Ted Travail via pinfall <<Cut to the WWU TV Studio>> Tim Cameron: Some might say a jaded win for Ted Travail, who knows, but a win nonetheless. Tag team action up next folks, as the Destroyers take on the Masked Maniacs. <<Cut to ringside>> * Destroyers vs. Masked Maniacs The Destroyers, Tremor and Overkill came down to the ring, getting a lot of heat from the crowd. The steroid enhanced Maniacs came running down the aisle and quickly got into the ring, only to be met by a pretty bad beating at the hands of Tremor and Overkill. Tremor threw Masked Maniac A over to the outside of the ring, making Maniac B the legal man. Masked Maniac B did manage to hit a clothesline on Tremor, which phased him, but as he started to beat his chest, Tremor hit him with a bulldog. A few tags later on the part of the Destroyers brought about "The Executor" which consisted of Overkill powerbombing Maniac B, then Tremor coming off the top-rope with a leg-drop for the easy pinfall after a match pretty much dominated by the Destroyers. * Winners: Destroyers via pinfall <<Cut to the WWU TV Studio>> Tim Cameron: Wow. A mighty impressive win for the Destroyers right here on Weekend Wake Up! I believe Chris Douglas is in the ring, although he isn't scheduled for a match up today. Let's see what he has to say. <<Cut to ringside>> ("Pomp and Circumstance" plays as we see Chris Douglas and the rest of the Academics getting into the ring. Chris Douglas grabs the mic.) Chris Douglas: How in the hell can we have a show without me wrestling? Well it wouldn't be a credible show anyway. Dean Ackerman is going to be giving Creed the beating he deserves in just a few minutes, and later on today the Academics will be taking apart Kailef and Bulldog Bruce MacDonald. But what about now? I KNOW the fans want to see me beat someone up, so let's give them what they want and get the ratings. Okay, so drag someone out here. Anyone, it really doesn't matter. But just out of interest, am I the only AWE wrestler ranked in the RSPWF Top 200? (Camera view of the aisle, but nobody comes towards the ring.) Chris Douglas: I suppose that means nobody has the guts to face me. How about Bob the Cab Driver? I know he's been here, or how about Skull N. Chainz again? Or have I beaten him enough? (Camera view of the aisle, as the 6'7" 270 pound Ezekiel Rage slowly makes his way to the ring.) Chris Douglas: Oh, another one with the old loner schtick gimmick. Seen it before. Let's see if I can't beat him in record time. (The Academics stay at ringside as Ezekiel Rage gets into the ring to take on Chris Douglas.) * Chris Douglas vs. Ezekiel Rage The match started off with Ezekiel Rage laying into Chris Douglas with rights and lefts, and started to brawl with him. However, Chris Douglas was quick to take the match to the canvas with a standing side headlock into a headlock takedown. Douglas continually locked in rest hold after rest hold to keep Rage from getting off of the canvas, but after a low blow and an eye gouge, Rage took control and sent Douglas into the turnbuckle. Rage ran in, but Douglas moved and hit with a DDT. Douglas hit with a snap suplex and then a piledriver. Douglas dragged Rage into the center of the ring, and locked on the patented Figure-Four Leglock for the submission victory. * Winner: Chris Douglas via submission <<Cut to the WWU TV Studio>> Tim Cameron: An unscheduled match, but certainly one to keep the fans entertained. Up next is the big 370 pounder Apocalypse taking on Jeremiah Jones, who is returning to action, but doctors report that he's not quite at full capacity yet. But you can bet one thing - it'll be a great one! <<Cut to ringside>> * Jeremiah Jones vs. Apocalypse The big man from Devastation Inc had early control of this match, although Jerimiah Jones was able to get in a few good shots as the fans went wild every time he did. Jones was not one hundred percent, and it showed in this match. Both guys are about the same height, but Apocalype has a pretty big weight advantage which he used well in this one. The man known as the "Enforcer" of Devastation Inc was able to use a lot of impact power moves early on, inluding a bodyslam, a powerslam and a chokeslam. Jeremiah Jones showed his toughness by managing to hold on for quite a while, and got in a running lariat and a bulldog, but that was pretty much it. The awesome Apocalypse executed a shoulder breaker, and from there on it only took "The Avenger", which is a gut-wrench powerbomb to get the pinfall victory. * Winner: Apocalypse via pinfall <<Cut to the WWU TV Studio>> Tim Cameron: Well folks, Jones put up a good fight, but the might of Apocalypse overcame him. If I were a betting man, I'd put the house on the ol' fashioned brawler from Las Cruces, Jeremiah Jones wanting a rematch when he's at full capacity. The action is really heating up here on Weekend Wake Up as we go down to ringside for a tag team match which is in action as we speak! <<Cut to ringside>> * 'Giant' Joey Williams/Freckles Brown vs. 2 Smooth We join the match as 'Giant Joey Williams is in the ring with Funk of 2 Smooth. A lot of fast paced action with the fans really into the match. Funk and Groove made numerous tags in and out, but 'Giant' Joey Williams took control of the action after a rocker dropper on Funk, and made the tag to Freckles Brown. Brown took his time with all of his moves, and was out-paced by Funk. Every time Funk used his speed to get out of a hold, Freckles Brown would start cursing and kicking the bottom turnbuckle, much to the amusement of the fans. The action soon spilled to the outside, where all four men got involved, and a DDT onto a chair by Brown on Funk gave the team of Williams and Brown control of the match. Brown managed to throw Funk back into the ring and follow in before the ref counted them out. Brown tagged in Williams, who hit Funk with the Joey-Kick and hooked the leg for the pin. * Winners: 'Giant' Joey Williams/Freckles Brown via pinfall <<Cut to the WWU TV Studio>> Tim Cameron: What else can you say besides great, solid tag team action. We have quite a match up scheduled next. Dean Ackerman of the Academics is set to take on Creed. And you've got to wonder how far away the rest of the Academics are going to be. <<Cut to ringside>> * Dean Ackerman vs. Creed "Drag the Waters" by Pantera played over the loud speakers, as the fans erupted with a huge pop as Creed came out from the locker room, pausing only to run his thumb along his throat. As soon as "Pomp and Circumstance" cut in, the fans started to boo loudly. Dean Ackerman slowly made his way to the ring, and as the ref attempted to hold Creed back, Ackerman got into the ring. Dean Ackerman turned to the crowd as the heckled him, as he shook his head in disapproval. The match was slow to start, with neither man wanting to make a mistake, but once the action set in, the fans really got into it. With both men evenly matched as far as size and weight is concerned, it turned into a typical technical versus brawler match. Dean Ackerman went to work with an armbar, but Creed was able to hit a headbutt to escape. Creed hit with a series of forearm smashes, and sent Ackerman into the ropes for what looked like what was going to be the End of the Line, but Ackerman held onto the ropes. Creed ran towards him with a clothesline, but Ackerman ducked and Creed went over the top rope to the outside. Ackerman followed him out, but was a mistake, since Creed usually seems more at home brawling outside the ring. As Ackerman went to throw Creed into the steel steps, Creed reversed it, and Ackerman went crashing into the steel. The action continued on the inside and outside of the ring with both men getting in many great moves. At around the twenty minute mark, Dean Ackerman locked in a half boston crab for what would have finished off most opponents, but Creed not being one to give up managed to make it to the ropes. As Ackerman argued with the referee, Creed started to recover, and as Ackerman picked him up, Creed hit him with a DDT. At this point Chris Douglas started to make his way to the ring. Creed with an irish whip sent Ackerman into the ropes, and nailed him with a spinebuster slam, but the count was stopped as Chris Douglas put Ackerman's leg across the bottom rope. As the ref checked on Ackerman, Creed went over to Douglas who was on the ring apron, but Douglas reached into his boots and pulled out a chain, nailing Creed. The ref had managed to turn around in time, and saw it, calling for the bell. * Winner: Creed via DQ <<Cut to the WWU TV Studio>> Tim Cameron: Good lord! Outside interference on the part of the Academics, who would have thought it! Talking of the Academics, the team of Brown and Andrews are up next, taking on Kailef and 'Bulldog' Bruce MacDonald. <<Cut to ringside>> * Kailef/'Bulldog' Bruce MacDonald vs. Academics As "Pomp and Circumstance" played, and the fans started to boo and jeer. Coach Brown scowled at the crowd and arrogantly flexed his muscles, while Doctor Andrews scratched his chin thoughtfully and sneered at the crowd. After they got into the ring, Kailef and 'Bulldog' Bruce MacDonald came out to possibly the loudest cheers ever at a Weekend Wake Up show. MacDonald and Kailef got jumped by the Academics as they got into the ring, and the Academics were able to use their superior teamwork to cut the ring in half and keep Kailef in the ring, and work over him. However, as Brown was scowling at the crowd, Kailef hit him with a Double Tornado Kick. MacDonald tried to come in for a double team, but Kailef was determined that the rules were to be followed, so kept MacDonald out of the ring. Kailef hit Brown with numerous Thai-style knees and a jumping side kick. Kailef made a legal tag to 'Bulldog' Bruce MacDonald who came in with various suplexes and a couple of roll ups for near falls. However, as MacDonald went for a vertical suplex on Brown, Andrews came in and took the knee of MacDonald out, and Brown fell on top of MacDonald. The Academics double teamed extensively on MacDonald, but Kailef refused to come in without a legal tag. After a while, the Academics went for their finisher, the Crash Course, with Dr. Andrews executing a German suplex, and Coach Brown hitting a top rope legdrop on MacDonald for the pinfall as Kailef didn't come in for the save. After the Academics left, Kailef and MacDonald started to shove each other and argue. * Winners: Academics via pinfall <<Cut to the WWU TV Studio>> Tim Cameron: It looks like the play by the rules ideology of Kailef didn't pay off this time, but a great match nonetheless. It seems that there might be some dispute between Kailef and Bulldog judging by what happened in that one. <<Cut to ringside>> * Spider Green vs. Mad Hatter With both wrestlers fan favorites, the fans really didn't know who to cheer for, so cheered for both. This was a fast paced match, with Spider Green showing his adaptability and combined High Flying Luchador style moves with brawling tactics. Mad Hatter put on a good show also, with many counters and a lot of surprises. The fans were intrigued by this one, which consisted of Mad Hatter taking control until Spider hit him with a somewhat low blow and a hurricarana. Spider went to the top rope and finished the match off with the Spinning Spider Sting, which is a Moonsault with an extra rotation and got the pinfall, although left the ring to a pretty mixed reception from the crowd. * Winner: Spider Green via pinfall <<Cut to the WWU TV Studio>> Tim Cameron: Hmm, effective, but not quite what we've come to expect from Spider Green. Quite a match up next, as veteran rocker Skull N. Chainz takes on the massive 'Butcher' Steven Jensen. <<Cut to ringside>> * Skull N. Chainz vs. 'Butcher' Steven Jensen Lots of cheers for the well known former rock star from Hollywood, CA. Skull struts around the ring as the Butcher comes towards the ring to a lot of boos. A lock up put Jensen in control with several big knee lifts. See-saw action for the first few minutes, and WidowMaker came out to the ring entrance as the match continued. Jenson then really went to work, and executed an overhead slam, sending Skull N. Chainz right to the outside of the ring. Jensen followed him out and threw Skull knee-first into the steel ringsteps, further damaging his already injured knee, before getting a chair, taking it back into the ring and sitting for the countout victory. As the ref raises the hand of Jensen, WidowMaker leaves ringside, but not before Jensen spots him and gives chase, both men disappearing through the entryway. * Winner: 'Butcher' Steven Jensen via countout <<Cut to the WWU TV Studio>> Tim Cameron: Well folks, we're just about out of time here on Weekend Wake Up, but don't forget to tune in next week for some more great action, and don't miss AWEsome Monday Night, Monday at 8pm. So long folks!! <<The Weekend Wake Up graphic is shown again, then fades to black.>> AWE Homepage: http://members.aol.com/creedp/AWE/HQ.html
