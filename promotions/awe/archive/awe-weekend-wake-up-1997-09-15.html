AWE Weekend Wake Up, 15 September 1997

15 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A quiet morning, in a quiet suburban neighborhood. The sun is almost over the horizon, birds are chirping pleasantly, flowers are in bloom, and somewhere off in the distance, a rooster crows. A young boy, not quite in his teens, is pedaling a bicycle through the neighborhood delivering the morning newspaper, smiling happily the whole time. As see his face up close, his jaw drops and his eyes widen, just before a side kick from Kailef knocks him off his bike. The newspaper in his hand flies over to smack against the front door of a nearby house. A man opens said door, yawning over his cup of coffee. His face is suddenly fearful, and he gasps as Vincenzo Galliano knocks his down with a huge clothesline.) (Bob's cab pulls up by the downed paperboy, and the Mad Hatter, Bob, and Dave Gubbins all get out. From the other direction, a Cadillac stops in the street, with the members of the Academics stepping out to rush the other men. A limosine pulls alongside the Cadillac, and from the sunroof Chris Douglas climbs out to execute a moonsault. A minivan turns and screechs to a halt sideways, with LiveWire and 'Maverick' Dave Dallas joining the fray in the middle of the street.) (The Brooklyn Boys are seen running between the cars, as is 'Mr. Fantastic' Robert Richmond, who is yanked back by a lasso thrown from the horse of Jeremiah Jones. 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie is dancing on the roof of the minvan, but he is knocked off by the landing gear of a helicopter, from which Skull N. Chainz steps out, his guitar slung over his back. As the rumble continues in the street, the camera pans over to a bedroom window. The curtain is pulled aside, and a shocked homeowner watching the scene pulls his blanket over his head and goes back to bed.) (The horizon is shown, and the AWE logo rises from behind it, with rays of sunshine illuminating it. The words 'Weekend Wake Up' rise up below AWE, forming this show's logo. Cut to a television control room:) Gooooood Morning everyone, and welcome to the weekend! I'm your host, Tim Cameron, and this morning on our second edition of AWE Weekend Wake Up, we'll have some footage and results from the AWE weekend card in Poughkeepsie, New York, as well as some exclusive interviews. We'll also have the line-up for AWEsome Monday Night. So grab your coffee, make those eggs over easy, set that newspaper aside, and let's start the day with a smile! First off, recently announced on AWE Wednesday Wrap-Up, was the upcoming six-man tag team match between all three member of the Academics against Skull N. Chainz, Chris Douglas, and a partner of their choosing. <<footage>> This came about after Douglas was accidently dropped in Dean Ackerman's lap during the Chainz/Douglas rematch last Monday Night. Coach Brown took matters into his own hands by pushing Chainz off the top turnbuckle. The rocker went on to win the match, but both men were quite upset, and had a lot to say on Wednesday night. The Academics have responded, and here are their comments: <<Cut to video: >> (A gymnasium, complete with bleachers and basketball hoops. Coach Brown is conducting a class, with over 20 students doing sit-ups. Dean Ackerman and Dr. Andrews are watching but turn to face the camera.) Ackerman: Well, well, well. I see we have to make travel plans. The Tag Team Championship will be decided at the Rocktober Pay Per View in Chicago, IL? Then that's where we will be, because the Academics will be the first team to hold AWE team gold. In the meantime, we have to deal with Chris Douglas and that rock and roll flake, Skull N. Chainz. Andrews: Mr. Chainz, you do realize that years of being exposed to loud music, especially with the deep bass and intense high notes of your band, can be detrimental to your hearing, don't you? So turn your television up and listen carefully. If you can't be bothered to pay attention, then you get what is coming to you. Brown (wanders over): Are we talking about that whining pretty boy Chainz? Let me tell you something, girly-man - cut that dirty long hair, take off that stupid fedora, drop and give me 100 pushps! Maybe if you get back into shape, and concetrate on your own physical fitness, you'll stop whining and take punishment that comes to you! Andrews: And as far as Chris Douglas is concerned - Chris, we're all a bit disappointed in you. Go back and look at the tape again. Quite by accident, the Dean broke your fall and kept you from hitting that concrete floor. And the only action we took was against Chainz for his incompetence. So no, we did not cost you your match. Ackerman: I'll be more than happy to tell you why we were out there, why we were out at your match once before. We were impressed. I had heard about you before the AWE was started, and when you arrived you lived up to your reputation. We were out to invite you into the most exclusive group in professional wrestling today - the Academics. Now we're all professionals. We know misunderstanding happen. And as far as the match this Monday, don't worry about it - don't hold back on our behalf. Show us every ounce of talent you have, every lock, every hold, every moonsault you have in your arsenal. And let us know if you're interested in joining with us. Become one of the elite. Coach, yelling to his class: Students, sound off! The whole class: ACADEMICS, SIR! <<Back to the control center: >> Some strong words from the Academics, and they may just make good on their words by winning the Tag Team championship at AWE's Rocktober, but one of the teams they will have to contend with at some point is without a doubt LiveWire. This odd couple is still undefeated, and they have no intention of stopping their winning ways. Here's what they had to say: <<Cut to video: >> Shaun "Sparks" Popper: We are the hottest thing around today, people! LiveWire is hotter than a volcano, hotter than a toaster, hotter than a microwaved Pop Tart... hey, I could go for one of those... I think I have one around here somewhere... "The Generator" Bruce Willams: Hush, Shaun, we'll eat later. Right now I want to tell all these people watching, and all other AWE tag teams, how we have dominated every team we've taken on, and I know we are definitely going to be considered for the AWE Tag Team Championship. Sparks: Belts? Are you talking about those belts again? Maaaan, I told you! I want suspenders! (Generator smacks him in the back of the head) Ow. Generator: Pay attention! Now there's one team we haven't beat, and that's the Academics. You three snot-nose punks talk to much - but you *can* wrestle. So whenever you're ready for a real match, come take us on. We're offering you snobs a chance to take an the best tag team in the AWE! Sparks: Yeah, you snobs! <<Back to Tim: >> That would be a great match, we'll see what happens with that. On Wednesday Night, we heard from a man who terrified our cameraman. Before we show you this footage, let me assure you no harm came to our cameraman. With that, here is the 'Butcher' Steven Jensen. <<Cut to video: >> The camera moves through the darkness past hanging shapes you can't quite make out, towards an area that's dimly lit. Soon, you can tell that the shapes are huge slabs of raw meat - You're in a meat locker. Voice: Hey, you! The camera turns and you see a towering man standing with a bloody apron on, holding a rather menacing-looking meat cleaver. Man: Heh, heh. So, you're here. Took ya long enough, sucker. The man raises his cleaver and takes a step forward. The camera's viewpoint wavers a little bit, and moves back slightly. The man chuckles. Man: Git over here, sucker. I'm not finished with ya. Yo! You AWE fans! My name is Stephen Jensen... But you can call me da Butcher. And there's nothin' I like more than fresh meat! I'm tired 'o da weaklings I been kickin' tha crap out of on da circut, so I figgered I'd come here and see if dem chicken<bleep> pro-fesh-in-all wrestlers could stay on der feet for more than a few seconds in da ring with me! Heh, heh. Who's first? I wanna break me some bones. The Butcher's eyes shift to fall upon the camera. He smiles sadistically and begins to walk towards the screen as the viewpoint shakes, turns around, and begins bobbing up and down to the sound of running footsteps. The screen goes black. <<Back to Tim in the both: >> Wow. That looked scary. Well, let's show you something a bit more tame. Robert Richmond challenged Bob two weeks ago, and we'll see that match on AWEsome Monday Night. 'Mr. Fantastic' had this to say about the match: <<Cut to video: >> I am Fantastic. You are not. You are not even man enough to step in the ring with me. Instead, you feel it necessary to try to run me over with you cab than face me. You're afraid of what a real man, a fantastic man, can do. So be forewarned, I will beat you with whatever it takes. Do you understand? I hope so. <<Back to Tim: >> All right, ladies and gentlemen, the time for talking is done. Let's take you know to where the action is. This week, it's the Civic Center in Poughkeepsie, New York. Joel Trubin is standing by, Joel what's the word? <<Joel is next to the ring in an arena where most of the people seem to be on their way out: >> Thank you, Tim - here's the run-down of the card here in Poughkeepsie: > El Diablo 2 vs. "Northern Sensation" Dave Gubbins Dave Gubbins showed why he is a sensation in the high-flying match against the new masked Luchador. A great way to start the show, with the fans really into this awesome match! Flying moves, springboard moves, and a great reversal by Gubbins - a top rope plancha by Diablo became a huge powerslam by Gubbins. The Blackpool Illuminations from Gubbins finished the match and got the win for the "Northern Sensation." > Chris Douglas vs. Slasher The fans booed both these men, with Douglas definitely getting the worst of it. Slasher's usual antics of lurking around the ring were no help, as Douglas went right after him. Slasher suffered a wide range of wrestling moves out of Douglas's arsenal, and never really got started in this match. Chris Douglas got the win with a top rope moonsault. > "Mr. Fantastic" Robert Richmond vs. Jonny Fontaine Following a lengthy speech by Richmond over the house mike about how fantastic he was, Richmond rushed Fontaine, who had taken to leaning on the turnbuckle waiting for Richmond to finish. As usual, Fontaine seemed lost in the ring, and Richmond was all over him. The Fantastic Finish cut this match short, mercifully, and Richmond went home with another victory. > LiveWire vs. Hound Dogs The new team of the Hound Dogs tried varied tactics to overcome the teamwork of LiveWire, including the interference of Uncle Pete on the outside. This particular tactic ended when Sparks leaped over the top rope to bulldog Pete on the semi-padded floor at ringside. Mason and Dayton double teamed Generator, but to no avail as Williams took on both men. The Electric Chair was used on both men, and Sparks pinned Dayton while Generator pinned Mason, to the audience's delight. > Mad Hatter vs. Maniac Warrior After the Warrior's usual crazed run to the ring, the Mad Hatter was cheered as he came to the ring dressed as an astronaut in full gear. Fortunately, he stripped down to a flight suit to wrestle. This time, Hatter used an arsenal of high-flying moves to wear down the wild Warrior. Then the top-rope flying headbutt, the Hat Trick spelled the end for the Warrior, as he suffered yet another defeat at the hands of the Mad Hatter. >Masked Maniac A vs. 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie The Maniacs tried to wrestle in singles competition to see if they would fare better. They didn't. Rassie danced up one side of Maniac A and down the other, using some sharp quickness to outmanuever the masked man. Rassie finally finshed him off with the Flash in the Pan and got the win. > Masked Maniac B vs. Kailef His partner didn't seem to fare any better. While gaining a slight advantage by attacking Kailef during his ceremony of bowing, this seemed to anger Kailef greatly. The man from the Orient used many fascinating holds and dazzling kicks to pummel the other masked man until the Jackhammer put him away, and Kailef won the match. Maniac A rushed out to ringside to attack Kailef, but never got in the ring as a tornado kick knocked him back off the ring apron, to the crowd's approval. > 2 Smooth vs. Brooklyn Boys Groove and Funk partied with the crowd until they got to the ring, where they got serious with the Brooklyn Boys. 2 Smooth has really improved, and they showed the Boys just how much. With their finished, the Party Crasher, 2 Smooth put the Boys away in short order. > Apocalypse Now vs. Bob Driving to the ring in his cab, Bob was widely applauded and cheered. He was a slow starter against Apocalypse Now, but soon caught up and used his larger side to squash the smaller man. Using a front slam, Bob took down Apocalypse and got the pin. > Vincenzo Galliano vs. El Diablo 1 After a five minute wait for Galliano, someone came from the back, saying Galliano would not be able to compete, and this match was scratched. The crowd was not happy. > Skull N. Chainz and a Mystery Partner against the Academics: The crowd went wild as the mystery partner was 'Maverick' Dave Dallas! Chainz called out Ackerman and Brown, who were happy to oblidge. However, Dr. Andrews interfered repeatedly, even causing the referee to miss a pin on Ackerman after a combined Skull Crusher and Texas Tornado, which surely would have seen a victory for the crowd favorites. When the Coach and the Dean finally came back with the Crash Course, Dallas was pinned. The crowd was not happy, booing loudly. One fan through a soda, and security dragged him out and had him arrested. This was another great AWE show, and we're looking forward to our next stop, when we travel to New Haven on Thursday, and Hartford on Saturday. Back to you, Tim. <<In the control room: >> Thanks, Joel. Here's the itinerary for the AWE during the next week: Thursday, September 18th 7:00 Bell Time Colliseum, New Haven, Conneticut "Northern Sensation" Dave Gubbins vs. Masked Maniac B Brooklyn Boys vs. Tenesian Tribesman Slasher vs. (Newcomer) Apocalypse Now vs. Jeremiah Jones 'Maverick' Dave Dallas/Bob vs. Hound Dogs Jonny Fontaine vs. 'Flashy' Ricki Rassie Kailef vs. Todd Masked Maniac A vs. Sgt. Bill Diablo 1 & 2 vs. Smith & Wesson 'Butcher' Steven Jensen vs. Colin Green Chris Douglas vs. Mad Hatter Saturday, September 20th 12:00 Bell Time Civic Center, Hartford, Conneticut Masked Maniacs vs. Brooklyn Boys Jeremiah Jones vs. Todd El Phantasmo vs. Diablo 1 Sgt. Bill vs. 'Mr. Fantastic' Robert Richmond Jonny Fontaine/Apocalypse Now vs. Smith & Wesson 'Northern Sensation' Dave Gubbins vs. Diablo 2 Colin Green vs. (Newcomer) Tenesian Tribesmen vs. Hound Dogs 'Butcher' Steven Jensen vs. Bob 'Maverick' Dave Dallas vs. Vincenzo Galliano All right fans, that's about all we have time for tonight. However, before we go, we've got a preview for AWEsome Monday Night: <<Cut to video, fast clips mixed with an announcer's voice over: >> A CHALLENGE WILL BE ACCEPTED - Richmond: I've come here to the AWE because they needed someone fantastic. I see you have a two-bit hack Bob wrestling here. What does he know about excellence? I don't see anything special about this fat slob. So Bob, I challenge you to face me, right here on Monday Night! BODIES WILL FLY - <<Footage of El Phantasmo, 'Northern Sensation' Dave Gubbins, and Diablo 1 & 2>> OLD FACES WILL BE IN ACTION - <<Quick clips of Kailef and Colin Green, LiveWire and Hound Dogs, Vincenzo and Mad Hatter, Brooklyn Boys and Masked Maniacs>> NEW FACES WILL BE SEEN - Man: My name is Stephen Jensen... But you can call me da Butcher. JJ: Jeremiah Jones here. People say I'm a man of few words. Yup. I let my actions speak for me. THE MATCH WILL BE ON - <<From AWEsome Monday Night...>> Mike: Wait, it looks like the Academics want some payback, and here goes Coach Brown! Chris: He's shaking the ropes, and Chainz falls onto the turnbuckle, and down to the mat. The referee was checking on Dean Ackerman, he never saw any of it! <<From interviews...>> Skull N. Chainz: I'm not a mind reader, I'm a wrestler and an ex-musician. But the moment Coach Brown put his hands on me, he crossed the line from observer to participant. Chris Douglas: All I need is a couple of people in my corner to stand there and look pretty and make the match legal while I beat the Academics within an inch of their lives. Ackerman: We have to deal with Chris Douglas and that rock and roll flake, Skull N. Chainz. AND THE AWE WILL *NEVER* BE THE SAME: Tomorrow - on AWEsome Monday Night... <<Fade to Black>>
