[Part 1 of 4 — posted 16 November 2001]

Well, in the midst of MotR 6, yours truly has been working on a tournament of which will go down when MotR is all said and done. Not much to say here, as everything's in the FAQ, but we are accepting applications for it while
MotR is running, but we won't be reviewing them until after thus supercard is complete sometime in the early portions of December. So, yeah, look over the FAQ below. If there's any questions of which it doesn't fulfill, please email me at
[detail withheld] or contact me on AIM. My handle is [detail withheld]. My name's [name withheld]. You may know me, you may not. I run FWG, and I handle Ernest "Brick" Wall and "Hell's Only Angel"
Harley Dunn in such feds as NEW, AVW, RAW, M15, etc. Have I got a proposal for you. After originally being a part of [name withheld]'s Fall Classic tournament that never materialized, I thought to myself "Hmmmmm.....Out of everyone I know, who exactly has the most free time to spare to run something like this?"
Of course I answered; "Me". This tournament is a good ol' fashioned reason not to do homework, just like everything in e-wrestling ought to be. I've spoken to various people about being "judges" [I'll go over this later in the FAQ], including Joe
Birmingham, who referred to this as the "People's Tournament". Quite fitting of a label, I must say. The matches will be decided upon by a panel of judges to all be named at a later date. Everything would be just and fair to all handlers in the tourney. It's not going to be huge, only 16 people is the ideal number, but we will be accepting ALL applications, and will review each accordingley. The panel will then select the 16 best out of what is sent to us, and place them in random seeds in the tournament brackets. I will run the day to day part of this tourney, and also be one of the people on the commitee. The judges do nothing more than judge roleplays, if that's only what they choose to do.
I intend to make this thing better than good; to make it _great_, to be precise. I'm looking forward to apps of all shapes and sizes, and they can all be submitted to me at
[detail withheld] . Either fill out the app at the bottom of the FAQ, or send one in that you may have on your hard drive. I will need a sample RP from you, in either case.
By the way, I've created a dandy little fictional story to why this tournament is being run, which gives it much more of an edge fantasy-wise.
2. No really, what the hell is this thing? [Fantasy Explanation] After being charged with the assault of a police officer, FWG Commissioner Salverda meant with the shareholder of FWG Productions Inc., and they decided the best way for Salverda to repay the board would be to head up a major truckload of work
Enter the Grand Slam Invitational Cup. There's no better way than to drive someone insane with a ton of work, and running a fed _and_ a tourney at the same time is just the way the board thought they would do it. The ticket sales and PPV revenue from the tourney would go to FWG Productions, Inc., and would go to pay Salverda's salary, so the board won't have to pay him, which gives them a pretty damn good reason not to fire him.
But there's one prob with this whole thing........Salverda is a _major_ weasel, and it looks to me like he just weaseled his way out of this one, as he has gone and pawned the work off to another FWG illuminarie.....Cowboy
Underhill, the FWG color man. The Cowboy's got quite a cult following in FWG for his.....well, his "personality". The question in this one is will the
Cowboy be able to run a tournament, but not run it _into_ the ground? Only time will tell. 3. The Judge Panel This part applies mostly to judges, although it does review the points system for the tourney.
The role of a judge is only to do one thing, and that is judge roleplays. Nothing more, nothing less. There will be roughly about 3 flash packets over the course of a couple of months, and all a judge has to do is read 'em, and grade 'em.
No more work than that. The judges that I would like to see on the panel are a few fellas that were suggested to me by Joe Birmingham, who has already agreed to be a judge on the panel. They're all fedheads, and great guys, and they are all now aboard for this thing:
Mike Beeby [UWF] [name withheld] [KCW] Joe Birmingham [NEW] [name withheld] [FWG] Now here is the set-up for the points system: Roleplays will be marked in four categories: Quality and Creativity [out of 5]; Spelling, Grammer, and proper formatting [out of 5]; Length (either not too short so it doesn't make a point, or too long so that it just rambles on) [out of 3]; and relevance to the event/and/or wrestler's match in the tournament [out of 2]; for a total of 15 points overall.
Things are then percentaged out. Let's say......we've got he Masked Mexican against Joe Jobber. The Masked Mexican scores from all four judges [scores on his roleplay for the match, that is] are add up and divided by 60 [4 x
15 - no. of judges times highest total possible] to reach a percentaged score. The same goes for Joe Jobber. Then the simple part: the rassler with the highest percentage wins. Nothing more than that; nothing less.
We just need judges so everything's fair to the people handling, and a wide variety of expert e-wrestling opinion is given to their RP, so winners and match results are done fairly.
4. Tournament Size and Bracket Information I'm aiming for a 16 man tournament, plus one special challenge match. The challenge match competitors have already been selected (they will be announced later), and so have some reserve seeds for the tournament (whom also will be announced later).
That leaves about 13 or 14 as the number of apps that will be accepted. You can fill out the application on the bottom of the FAQ, or send one in from off of your hard drive. The addy they go to is
[detail withheld] . Note that we are _NOT_ accepting applications until after MotR. The following process will be used by the judges when accepting the apps.
Each judge chooses their 13. The number one guy on their list receives 13 points. Number two gets 12. Three gets 11, and so, and so on. All four judges submit their lists, and the highest 13 wrestlers are in. In the even of a tie on the 13th and final wrestler, there will be a revote done by the judges on the wrestlers that are involved in that tie. That's it. That's how it's done.
5. Schedule Well, I've got a schedule to the Grand Slam Cup, so I'll ring of the dates for when I've got things set up. The schedule has been formatted to work after MotR, so all is well.
All dates are tentative: December 21st - Applications close December 22nd through January 2nd - Selections List made for 16 competitors January 3rd - 16 competitors announced January 10th - Roleplays for Packet #1 due January 11th - Packet #1 released January 18th - Roleplays for Packet #2 due January 19th - Packet #2 released January 25th - Night #1 released January 27th - Roleplays for Post-Card Packet (#3) due January 28th - Post Card Packet released February 1st - Roleplays for Packet #4 due February 9th - Final Night [#2] released So as you can see there will be four packets and two nights of rasslin'. The first night will consist of the eight first round matchups, and the second night of the last 7 matchups in the tournament, and the special challenge match. Note again that all dates are tentative, and are more of a guide than an exact timing.
6. Rules? Yup, we got a few. Well, I lied. There isn't all that many. Judges just need to judge fairly, and handlers need to write roleplays _and_ strats, and submit them on time for them to be acknowledged. Notice the underscores on the word "and", as this tourney will require strats, making things that much easier for the match writers [and everybody, really].
And have fun. It's a rule, and if you refuse to have fun and prefer to create trouble, it wont last for you. But I doubt I'll have problems with that, as everyone that would be involved in the event would be of the most civil types. :)
7. So where do I sign up? There is an app listed at the bottom of the FAQ, or you can take an app from off of your hard drive and send it in to me at
[detail withheld] . The apps will be collected more than likely for a two to three week period [maybe longer, maybe shorter], and then sent to the panel, and the judges panel will decide who is in the tournament, and who doesn't make the cut.
They will do so by filling out a form by reviewing the apps, and selecting the 16 best of the bunch. Number 1 would be their favourite of the pick, thus meaning 16 is their bottom of the list. All 5 of the lists would be collected, then 16 points would be given to a number one on the list, 15 to number 2, 14 to number 3, and so on. The sixteen wrestlers with the highest point totals are in the tournament.
It's very dandy, isnt't it? :) 8. And what about a web site? I'm unsure of whether or not I will develop one. If I do, it will be gorgeous, but I'm unsure of the need for one for a one time deal like this.
If I do go ahead and do that, I'll run this site, and let everyone know of happenings off of it ASAP.
I just may, though. It would be nice to do it, but as I said, the need may not be there. I'll let everyone know later about this one.
