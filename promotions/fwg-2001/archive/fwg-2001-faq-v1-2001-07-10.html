What's the goal of the FWG? The goal of the FWG is to bring you quality, entertaining e-fed storylines, which allow you to participate and recieve entertaining and realistic results. We have a schedule that has defined goals to meet our requirements to expand to a much larger audience and increase membership here in the FWG through innovative advertising and presenting realistic possibilities and entertainment for members and even non-members of the FWG.
Expansion to include more talent will significantly boost the quality of the league, although it stands as an extremely entertaining and valuable source for e-wrestling, this boost of quality from expansion can only make things better for everyboy in the league, from staff to talent. The FWG has a bright future in which everyone can participate equally.
2. The Chairman The Chairman and CEO of Fantasy Wrestling Group, Inc. is Jeremy Dale, or me. I run everything in the league, from writing the results, to promos, advertising, booking, etc. My offical status in the FWG would mainly be as head writer, head booker in a small team of bookers, and just about a major or complete hand in everything else.

What are the titles here? 3.1 FWG World Heavyweight Title This is the main belt in FWG, bar none. Only the best in the FWG can take it, and only the greatest can hold it! It is made of black leather with a gold plate, lined with diamond studs and plates on the straps. The belt is usually defended on a ration of once per week,
3.2 FWG Continental Heavyweight Title The FWG Continental champion needs to be a very hard worker! It also is defended about once a week, give or take a title shot. It is made of black leather with a silver plate, lined with diamond studs and plates on the straps.
3.3 FWG World Tag Team Title The FWG Tag Team title is only for the best of the best in the tag team division. It is defended at least once every two weeks, and is black leather with platiinum plating, lined with studs and plates on the straps.
3.4 FWG Deathmatch Title The FWG Deathmatch chmapion needs to be a lunatic in order to survive. The title is defended 24/7, which means that anyone can win the title at any time in any circumstance. The title is made of black leather, with a metallic plate emblem, and studs and plates outlining the strap.
3.5 FWG World Cruiserweight Title The FWG Cruiserweight championship has to defended about once a week. The maximum possible weight to compete in this division is 240 lbs. The is no minimum. The title is made of black leather and has a bronze plate, with studs and plates outlining the straps.
4. What are the cards in the FWG? 4.1 Weekly Televised Cards 4.1.1 Tuesday Rampage Tuesday Rampage is the flagship show of the FWG. It usually consists of about 3 to 4 interviews per card, and about 8 or 9 matches. It is hosted by the commentating team of Wayne Robbins and Cowboy Underhill. The show takes place every Tuesday night as a two hour segment.
4.1.2 High Impact Friday's High Impact Friday's is the secondary show of the FWG. It consists of about 2 to 3 interviews per card, and about 7 or 8 matches. It is hosted by the commentating team of Wayne Robbins and Cowboy Underhill. This show takes place every Friday night as a two hour segment.
4.2 Weekly House Show Cards 4.2.1 Wenesday Night Elektro Shok Wednesday Night Elektro Shok is the weekly house show card of the
FWG. It consists of about 2 interviews and 6 or 7 matches. This is used mainly is a ground for unused and new talent to work on their skills and get some attention in the public eye. There is no commentating to this, as it is not broadcast on TV. There will be no play-by-play style results for this card, just basic results. This applies to this card only.
4.3 PPV's 4.3.1 Bi-weeky PPV's PPV's in the FWG are done every other Saturday night. The consist of about 10 matches and the odd interview, and are the premier area for action to take place in the FWG. The PPV schedule is shown below for the summer:
July 7, 2001: Sinn Saturday July 21, 2001: Highway to Hell August 4, 2001: License to Kill August 18, 2001: WrestleBowl (FWG's premier card) September 1, 2001: Hardcore Heaven (tentative) The PPV's for the fall will be posted up in late summer. 4.4 Non-PPV Supercards 4.4.1 TandemBowl (TBA) TandemBowl is a just your basic Tag Team Tournament. It will usually consist of either 8 or 16 teams, depending on the league status.
Non-usual tag teams are allowed to enter, meaning two wrestlers that usually don't team up together or don't do tag teams can enter into the tournament.
The tournament is of a round robin fashion. The date for it is yet to be announced. 4.4.2 WrestleWAR (July 16, 2001) WrestleWAR is excatly what is says it is: a war! This is a battle royale featuring all of the competitors of the FWG, and no more no less. The first WrestleWAR takes place on this Monday, July 16, 2001. It is of over-the-top fashion. All competitors involved are automatically entered.
Yhis is a NON-televised card, so it is like a Supercard house show. 4.4.3 King of the Deathmatch (TBA) The King of the Deathmatch tournament is a tournament featuring only the toughest of competitors in the FWG. Each match contains different and dangerous rules, the most dangerous humanly possible. The date for the nect tournament is yet to be announced.
5. Who are the staff members? 5.1 Front Office 5.1.1 Jimi Bronson Jimi Bronson is the Commissioner of the FWG. He is not a former wrestler himself, but he has worked as promoter of many indy circuits until he finally got his job with the big one here in the FWG. He is a fair man who knows right from wrong and will go to any point to defend his, or the FWG's, honour.
5.1.2 James Spencer James Spencer is another staffer who has no previous experience as an actual pro wrestler. He is the Vice-President of the FWG, and handles such matters as discipline, and can make a good fill on for
Commish when Jimi Bronson is out of town or away on business. 5.2 Announcers and Interviewers 5.2.1 Nathan Joseph He is the head ring announcer in the FWG. He is a very smart man and has a keen sense when to know to stay out of the action. He is a prime example of the hard work that the FWG has among it's members and staff.
5.2.2 Amanda Norton Although she is a very attractive blonde, she is by no way a dumb blonde. She is the backup ring announcer to Joseph, and she announces house shows in the FWG. Just try telling her a blonde joke and see what happens!
5.2.3 J.F. Scott He is a part time interviewer employed by the FWG. He does a fare share of the backstage and off location interviews for the FWG. He is not a very strong man, and ends up getting beat up by the rougher of oour employees one way or another.
5.3 Referees 5.3.1 Michael Lemmon Michael Lemmon is the head official and ref is the FWG. He refs a fair amount of the main event matches, and he calls everything right down the middle. It's hard to get one over on Mr. Lemmon.
5.3.2 Walter Machevsky Walter Machevsky is a former rule-breaking pro wrestler, but he has grown old and has plied his treacherous ways to refereeing. He will turn a blind eye to many things, but he may just get you with a fast count or DQ you on a seconds notice without you even realizing whats been done.
5.3.3 Paul Henderson Henderson is a ref very similar to Michael Lemmon, except the fact that he is very much younger in age than the head ref. He is the assistant to the head official, and he calls all of his matches straight down the middle, although he can be very naive about some things such as cheating and rulebending.
5.3.4 Rico Giovanni Rico Giovanni is a retired lucha libre referee, although he is not mexican, he is obviously Italian. He is a tough little guy and he does his bets to keep everything even between both sides, even if that includes letting each wrestler cheat just a little.
5.4 Ringside Crew 5.4.1 Brock Spade Brock Spade is the head security guard for the FWG. Any wrestler fan or staffer get out of line here in the FWG, Brock is ready and willing to eject you: and forcifully.
5.4.2 Rick Plant Rick Plant is the head of the ringside crew, which sets the rings and matches up and keeps things clean after the matches are over. Rick is a hard worker who never lets his employers down.
5.5 Commentators 5.5.1Wayne 'Nightrain' Robbins Robbins is the head play-by-play analyst here in the FWG. He takes no biased, and is one of the easiest men to work with in the business.
He is a hefty man, as he is a former pro wrestler himself, holding numerous championships. He's one of the top commentators in the business, and sees everything straight down the line.
5.5.2 Cowboy Underhill Cowboy Underhill can be summed up in two words: perverted cheater. He's not a former wrestler, but he's know all the rules and how to break them. He also knows all the women of the FWG, but they aren't interested in a lowlife like him. Hates the faces, loves the heels, this guy is a shit disturber. His very favourite quote is "Win if you can, lose if you must, but always cheat".
6. What do I do here in the FWG? 6.1 Interview/Run Ins Most of your roleplaying will be done through interviews which you send to me when you find that your wrestler is scheduled for one.
You can talk about any topic off the topic of your head here. It's your time. You can also send in run-ins, as in you have your wrestler run into another match to attack another wrestler of your choice. All matches are simmed.
6.2 Character Angles and Gimmicks You have control over your character. If you have an idea for an angle or spot with your wrestler, let me know, because I'd love to hear it. If I have an idea for a gimmick or angle for your wrestler, I'll let you know and we'll work on the thing together. Team work on writing and booking is what counts here in the FWG.
