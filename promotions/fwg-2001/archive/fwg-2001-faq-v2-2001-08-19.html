Application 1. What is FWG? FWG (Fantasy Wrestling Group) is what we all know to be an efed, which operates as for pure entertainment value (shouldn't all efeds be that way?).
So, to keep this part short, it's entertainment, the same television, music, film, and weird-and-wacky religious rituals all are. It's for FUN.
1.1 Fictional Explanation The FWG was founded by former Commissioner Jimi Bronson, who was the face man for a number of mystery backers, who all have no part in the day-to-day operations of the fed. Bronson started FWG up and ran it for awhile, until former NIWF Commissioner Salverda walked into his fed, and would defeat
Bronson in a Commissioner's matchup for the rights to run the fed. Salverda runs it under his and ORDER's iron fist to this day.
1.2 Non-Fictional Explanation FWG has been running for about 3 months now, as I started it up out of the fact that I thought I'd be able to murder myself with a HUGE amount of work and stress in e-wrestling. As far as I'm concerned, it seems to have worked.
2. CEO of FWG That would be me. The name is either George or Jeremy, whichever one floats yer boat, matey. I am the head writer, the only booker, the webmaster, and the messiah of FWG. If you need to get a hold of me, cowboy, try one of the following:

3. How matches are decided? We are a purely hand written fed, and results are based *MOSTLY* on RP quality. The only other factors that can determine the result of a specific match or event (or whatnot), are angles (as in, if it fights the angle for a certain feller to win, he gets the thumbs up), and I give some leeway to those who are willing to write for me, and help me out in anyway possible.
Just like every other fed in existence, I don't tolerate invalid complaints or whining. If you need to make a complaint about the fed, email
[detail withheld] , and a large, burly man will show up at your house, stuff you in a wool sack, and beat you over the head with varying degrees of golf clubs.
4. The best ways to succeed 'round here It's pretty easy beans to succeed in the FWG, as long as you can comply and do your best with THESE objectives listed below. So read on, faithful reader.
4.1 Roleplaying We have an RP board at the site, and you'll need to RP at LEAST ONCE PER
MATCH. You can RP as much as you like to no limit (the same applies to length), but if you RP less then the minimum (or don't even RP at all), I'll show up at your place and shoot your porch lights out.
By the way, rules and regulations for posting are listed in the rules section later on in the FAQ.
4.2 Strats Strats will NOT affect the outcome of the particular match of which you are strategizing for, they're just to help the writers get a better feel for your character.
Strats should include the following: Entrance (if not provided, a default on will be used) Wrestler's Strategic Outline for the match, baby Suggested Spots/Ideas for the match What wrestler would do in case of Win/Loss Any interferences that you would care to make at a different point in the evening 4.3 Angles You need an angle. It's as simple as that. How long can you last with out one? A long time, but your win-loss record will look similar to that of the
LA Clippers. An inventive, ingenius, in......well, ingood angle is quite a major part of determining your success in FWG.
5. Weekly Cards of the FWG 5.1 Tuesday Rampage Tuesday Rampage is out every Tuesday night by 12am EST. It features the higher ups in the FWG, and will contain more title bouts and succh things.
I'd give you an estimate of the size of the card, but that would be bastard-like of me, now wouldn't it? Here's an example of a Tuesday Rampage card. Use the link: http://www.efeduniverse.com/fwg/rampage.html 5.2 High Impact Friday High Impact Friday is a somewhat smaller card that is out every Friday night by 12 am EST. More undercarded talent, and newer talent get more air time on this card, and there are less title shots, although the main event still features some of the greater talent in the FWG.
Take a look: http://www.efeduniverse.com/fwg/highimpact.html 6. PPV's PPV's are announced from cycle-to-cycle, same with their dates. They will feature the wrestlers with the best angles, and the most entertaining/interesting/time consuming storylines. Some past PPV's have included Sinn Saturday, Highway to Hell, License to Kill. They are generally down in one month cycles.

Staff 7.1 Commentators 7.1.1 Wayne 'Nightrain' Robbins Robbins is the typical unbiased commentator. He works hard at his play-by-play commentary, and he also has to work hard in just working with his partner, Cowboy Underhill, who enjoys driving Nightrain up the wall. He will admit this he loves new talent, which explains the help he gave to the
NIWF some time ago, in breaking them into the FWG. But he is the overall neutral character commentator in wrestling today.
7.1.2 Cowboy Underhill The Cowboy is not only a slack jawed son-of-a-gun, he can also be a pervert. He's the type of fellow who roots for the women, and cheers on the rulebreakers. A classic villain, in every definition of the word.
7.2 Interviewers 7.2.1 JF Scott Somewhat of a nerd boy, who can always manage to say the wrong thing at the wrong time, JF Scott is the main interviewer in the FWG. He also acts as a replacement commentator on the FWG shows, if something happens to one of the other regular commentators.
7.2.2 Amanda Norton Amanda Norton is one tough blonde! Although very sexy, she has a wrestling history herself, and can take out almost any man. She announces for FWG High
Impact Friday, and some of the non PPV supercards.
7.3 Referees 7.3.1 Michael Lemmon Michael Lemmon is the head official in the FWG. He is very stringent with the rules, and gives a good, moderate count. He always stands up for what is right in the ring, sometimes too often.
7.3.2 Walter Machevsky Machevsky is very lax with the rules. He counts to quick, and most of the time he'll just turn a blind eye to some serious cheating, as he used to be a rule bending wrestler himself. He has retired, though, and now is a ref for the FWG.
7.3.3 Paul Henderson Paul Henderson was the model ref, up until Commissioner Salverda and ORDER corrupted him. SIck of salary cut after salary cut (aren't we all?), he is now out for vengeance (sounds like a real fun boy, doesn't he). Will cause as much crap as possible, IF he doesn't like you.
8. Championship Titles 8.1 FWG World Triple Crown Championship This is the one and only, the belt to be wearing here in the FWG. Only the cream of the crop, of the best of the best, of whatever other accolades you can think of can hold this strap. It's name derives from the fact that it is a unification title of the old FWG World Heavyweight Championship, the NIWF
Heavyweight title, and the FWG Continental Championship. Only few have worn this strap. Defended mainly at Pay Per Views.
8.2 FWG Deathmatch Championship For hardcore extremists, and little old ladies with bad attitudes only. To hold this belt would define you as one of the most violent folks in the FWG, and you would need to be in order to keep the damn thing. This belt is defended pretty much on any card.
8.3 FWG Television Title This title is for the up-and-comers in the FWG, thus giving new people to
FWG a chance to shine in their own rights. The type of title to swap hands quickly, though, and is defended regularly on the weekly cards.
8.4 FWG World Tag Team Championship The duel straps are for the best duel wrestlers in the FWG. You'll need to put together a pretty handy-dandy team in order to capture this title. The title is defended regularly.

How matches are deteremined Matches are determined mainly by the length and QUALITY of RPs that people submit, and if you do or do not submit a strat (AKA strategy) for your matches. Simple as that, and winners and outcomes are determined from there by me, and by me only. I want to juts state one thing about winning and losing, is that your wrestler will lose, and at some point everyone loses a big match, so don't be upset if it happens. It will happen. This league is fair to all participents, as long as everyone participates. The bottom line is that the more you participate, the more you will win. Simple as that.
3. Roleplaying Roleplaying, better known as RPing, is the foundation to the FWG. I will go over RPing through a few different sections in a moment. Before I do that, though, I just want to say is that the more you RP, the more you will be succesful in the FWG, simple as that.
3.1 Using the RP board The RP board on our site is only to be used for RPs, and RPs only. Any other message will be either deleted or ignored from the board. The other board on our site, the Message Board, is used for all purpose and generic messages.
RPs can be of any length at all, small or large, and can use any topic at all, as long as it DOES NOT violate the RP rules listed a little farther down below. Otherwise, have fun RPing!
3.2 Writing RPs Pretty much see above for writing RPs, and below for the Rules and Regs of the RP board. 3.3 RP posting Rules and Regulations Any breach of the rules and/or regs of the RP board can result in a stiff punishment handed out and decided upon at the time of the offending RP. And, on another note, people do NOT need to tell me what RPs are offensive and what RPs are not. I make the decision there, whether to punish or do whatnot to the offending poster. It is between me, that poster, and the victim of the RP (if there is one). It is NOT anyone else's business, otherwise.
Here are the rules for posting RPs: - you cannot use another person's character without direct permission from that person. I will be giving out a list of people to contact, who they handle, and various contact methods at the beginning of next week. Breach of this rule can result in severe punishment
- you cannot book matches on the RP. That means you can't say that there will be a match on a certain date. Only I can do that, as I am head booker of FWG. - you cannot make any racial, religious, or basically any offensive and harassing remarks to other on the boards. This will result in dismissal from the FWG, as we don't stand for prejudism in any shape or form
- you cannot post rules and/or regulations on the board. Only I can do that, as I run the FWG. This will result in severe punishment, and in me telling you to "Bite me crank, matey"
- acts of violence cannot become too violent. Any acts involving such things that would permanently or severly injure another wrestler are prohibited. Examples of the such are shootings, stabbings, killings, paralyzing, and others along this line. It wouldn't make much sense to shot someone, then wrestle them the next day, would it?
- foul language is fine, as long it is kept low. If you need more clarification on this, I can provide you with rules on what to and what not to say if you email me at
[detail withheld] - you cannot write an RP for another person; althought I think that's obvious - non RP posts are relegated to the Message Board ONLY - final rule is to have fun and be creative with your RP. The more unique it is, the better it is. Capeche? 3.4 RP due dates RPs due dates are to be announced every week in the newsletters. If you do not have a match for that week, you are still very welcome to RP, but the basis is that you need to RP at least once per match. So if you have two matches that week, you'll have to RP once for each match (at least). There is no limit on the amount of RPs you can post.
4. Strats Strats are good tools to control how your wrestler acts in a match. Below is guidelines to writing a quick strat. Remember, a strat only takes about 30 seconds to write.
4.1 Writing Strats Here is a list of things to include in a strat. The strat should only take about 30 seconds to write
- wrestlers entrance (if this isn't included, a defualt one will be used) - wrestlers strategy for the match - suggested spots or ideas for things to happen in the match - what your wrestler would do if he or she won or lost - other intereferences or run ins you want to make during that card 4.2 Strat due dates Due dates are announced periodically, same as RPs. One final note on strats, they will NOT be followed to a T by writers of your match, but will be used as a guideline to how you want your character to act in said match.
5. Message Board The same rules apply to the message board as the RP board. The Message board is used to post such things as generic messages, challenges that you don't want to make in an RP, questions, comments, and occasionally opinions, and any thing that you can think of.
6. Making Challenges I prefer it if you make challenges on the RP board or the Message board, as people can view it and respond to it, rather than sending challenges to me.
7. Interviews If you are scheduled for an interview (interview schedules are released the preceeding week), you need to send one in to me. An interview can cover any topic in the world your wrestler wants to talk about, but I liketo keep them in line to what is currently developing in the fed. So basically it's best to stay on topic here. Think of the interviews as being similar to those that you see on RAW on the WWF every week. Write according to that, as they only need to be a paragraph or two long. There is no punishment for failing to submit an interview, other than the fact that you won't get an interview for quite awhile.
8. Intereference/Run-Ins If you want to run into a match and interefere, either email it to me or include it in your strat, and I'll let you know whether you'll be able to do it or not.
9. Newsletter Updates and topics can be found at the site, but are mainly in the newsletter, which is distrubuted after every card, and sometimes more. The newsletters include: news, hot topics, rankings, stables, results, upcoming cards and more.

3.1 Using the RP board The RP board on our site is only to be used for RPs, and RPs only. Any other message will be either deleted or ignored from the board. The other board on our site, the Message Board, is used for all purpose and generic messages.
RPs can be of any length at all, small or large, and can use any topic at all, as long as it DOES NOT violate the RP rules listed a little farther down below. Otherwise, have fun RPing!
3.2 Writing RPs Pretty much see above for writing RPs, and below for the Rules and Regs of the RP board. 3.3 RP posting Rules and Regulations Any breach of the rules and/or regs of the RP board can result in a stiff punishment handed out and decided upon at the time of the offending RP. And, on another note, people do NOT need to tell me what RPs are offensive and what RPs are not. I make the decision there, whether to punish or do whatnot to the offending poster. It is between me, that poster, and the victim of the RP (if there is one). It is NOT anyone else's business, otherwise.
Here are the rules for posting RPs: - you cannot use another person's character without direct permission from that person. I will be giving out a list of people to contact, who they handle, and various contact methods at the beginning of next week. Breach of this rule can result in severe punishment
- you cannot book matches on the RP. That means you can't say that there will be a match on a certain date. Only I can do that, as I am head booker of FWG. - you cannot make any racial, religious, or basically any offensive and harassing remarks to other on the boards. This will result in dismissal from the FWG, as we don't stand for prejudism in any shape or form
- you cannot post rules and/or regulations on the board. Only I can do that, as I run the FWG. This will result in severe punishment, and in me telling you to "Bite me crank, matey"
- acts of violence cannot become too violent. Any acts involving such things that would permanently or severly injure another wrestler are prohibited. Examples of the such are shootings, stabbings, killings, paralyzing, and others along this line. It wouldn't make much sense to shot someone, then wrestle them the next day, would it?
- foul language is fine, as long it is kept low. If you need more clarification on this, I can provide you with rules on what to and what not to say if you email me at
[detail withheld] - you cannot write an RP for another person; althought I think that's obvious - non RP posts are relegated to the Message Board ONLY - final rule is to have fun and be creative with your RP. The more unique it is, the better it is. Capeche? 3.4 RP due dates RPs due dates are to be announced every week in the newsletters. If you do not have a match for that week, you are still very welcome to RP, but the basis is that you need to RP at least once per match. So if you have two matches that week, you'll have to RP once for each match (at least). There is no limit on the amount of RPs you can post.
4. Strats Strats are good tools to control how your wrestler acts in a match. Below is guidelines to writing a quick strat. Remember, a strat only takes about 30 seconds to write.
4.1 Writing Strats Here is a list of things to include in a strat. The strat should only take about 30 seconds to write
- wrestlers entrance (if this isn't included, a defualt one will be used) - wrestlers strategy for the match - suggested spots or ideas for things to happen in the match - what your wrestler would do if he or she won or lost - other intereferences or run ins you want to make during that card 4.2 Strat due dates Due dates are announced periodically, same as RPs. One final note on strats, they will NOT be followed to a T by writers of your match, but will be used as a guideline to how you want your character to act in said match.
5. Message Board The same rules apply to the message board as the RP board. The Message board is used to post such things as generic messages, challenges that you don't want to make in an RP, questions, comments, and occasionally opinions, and any thing that you can think of.
6. Making Challenges I prefer it if you make challenges on the RP board or the Message board, as people can view it and respond to it, rather than sending challenges to me.
7. Interviews If you are scheduled for an interview (interview schedules are released the preceeding week), you need to send one in to me. An interview can cover any topic in the world your wrestler wants to talk about, but I liketo keep them in line to what is currently developing in the fed. So basically it's best to stay on topic here. Think of the interviews as being similar to those that you see on RAW on the WWF every week. Write according to that, as they only need to be a paragraph or two long. There is no punishment for failing to submit an interview, other than the fact that you won't get an interview for quite awhile.
8. Intereference/Run-Ins If you want to run into a match and interefere, either email it to me or include it in your strat, and I'll let you know whether you'll be able to do it or not.
9. Newsletter Updates and topics can be found at the site, but are mainly in the newsletter, which is distrubuted after every card, and sometimes more. The newsletters include: news, hot topics, rankings, stables, results, upcoming cards and more.
