Hey all, Well, here it is. I'm just waiting for a couple of fedheads to get me their teams for the teams matches.......and that'll be announced when they do *ahem*. Other than that, here's the Grand Slam tournament card. I'll have the due date for the first roleplay packet out in a couple.......despite what it says in the FAQ, this is being split up over three nights, with a packet due before each night (not like MotR, which releases all of the packets and _then_ the nights)...no, we're going to intertwine the two. And this also means that you only have to roleplay for the night you're on (unless you're motivated to do more). So if you're only on night three in the team matchesm you only need to flash for the third packet. If you're in the tournament, you need to flash for every night you wrestle on (one for each packet). Got all that? So, yeah, dates to be announced in a bit. You can catch me on AIM at '[detail withheld]'.
