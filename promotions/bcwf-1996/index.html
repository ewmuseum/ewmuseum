Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The BCWF dated itself to the start of March 1996 in its own words, and by early April was recruiting openly, looking for “consistant roleplayers who like to get right in the middle of the action.”

Demand outran the roster fast: within a week its singles division had closed to new applicants entirely, and only a wrestler who could “really knock me off my feet in the originality category” would still get a look, though tag team applications stayed open. Around the same time a homepage went up carrying a wrestler creation form, wrestler bios, a full copy of its last card, a news magazine called The Irish Whip, and its own rankings.

Community fed directories attest it still running as late as December 1996, and one reader's own recollection that October already called it “the old BCWF.” No closure notice of its own survives, and nothing beyond these three posts is in the record.
