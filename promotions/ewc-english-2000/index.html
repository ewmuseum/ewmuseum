Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The English Wrestling Championship opened on 10 July 1999 and by its own account ran continuously for a year without an inactive stretch, staging two weekly shows, Monday Night Slaughter and Thursday Evening Inferno, called by its own commentators Dougie Wright and Demented Dave.

Outcomes were judged by roleplay on a password-protected board, and the federation's own English Heavyweight, English Trilogy, English Tag Team and English Junior Heavyweight titles carried equal billing across both shows, with a Hardcore title announced as still to come.

Its surviving cards fall at the end of its first year: a second annual Royal Jeopardy pay-per-view built around a defending English Heavyweight champion, and a one-off December special, Santa's Saturday Storm, that closed out one title reign and opened another under a new manager.

How its run continued past December 2000 isn't in the surviving record.
