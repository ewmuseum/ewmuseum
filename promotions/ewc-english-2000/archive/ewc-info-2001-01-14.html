English Wrestling Championship http://www.english-wrestling.com EWC: There's an art to ewrestling! Contents 1. Opening Notes from the Owner 2. What sort of fed is it 3. Who runs EWC 4. Shows 5. Results 6. Roleplaying 7. Titles 8. Web Site 9. Closing Note Opening Note Let me introduce myself, my name is [name withheld] and I have been ewrestling since 1997. I have been and still am a successful wrestler all over the internet and my experience is second to none. EWC was created on the 10th of July 1999 and the first show was eight days latter. Though the wrestlers may have changed but the atmospher and dedication from myself has not. I had been wrestling for what a good time before I created EWC and wrestled in many different feds. I took the best things from all the feds I have been in created my own. I always wanted my efed to stand out from other's so I made sure that I followed my 3 rules to running an efed: be fair, be determind and beaware. This is why EWC has lasted so long and has never been unactive. So with this I invite you to read the rest of this information and apply. What sort of fed is it EWC is a booked fed that uses Roleplays to determin it's winners and loosers. We use a password protected Roleplay board and the results are posted on the web site. Who runs EWC The English Wrestling Championship has a staff of two. A dedicated owner and vice president. The owner writes the shows and handles most of the web site. The Vice President helps grade roleplays and book cards and is currently re-designing the web site. Shows EWC holds two weekly shows and a monthly pay per view. The first is Monday Night Slaughter, it is usually the largest show. The second is Thursday Evening Inferno. No show is 'better' than the other. The World Title has been defened on bot of them. Results The results are wrote using commentators; who are Dougie Wright and Demented Dave. We have backstage incidents and interviews that are supplied by the wrestlers. We have a great mix of storylines and wrestling. Roleplaying We use a password protected board so that the handlers can take greater care in the material they write.In the English Wrestling Championship matches are determined by roleplays,making them very important in the career of your wrestler, making the difference between jobbing and becoming a champion. Roleplays are graded on the quality not quantity. But you would usually find there is more quality in a 300-word roleplay than a 5-line roleplay. I judge roleplays on the following: ·        Creativity - you can only have a certain amount of in ring interviews before it gets a little boring. Try and find new locations make it different every time. ·        Effort- how much effort has been put in with the writing of the roleplay, this can be adding colours to the roleplay or the length. ·        Originality - has this roleplay been done before? Have I already seen it on Raw or Nitro, I hope not?  Don't copy catch phrases or gimmicks. ·        Interesting - is the roleplay boring, saying the same thing over and over and over. Or does it have that special ingredient that makes you want to carry on reading and look forward to the next roleplay. ·        Grammar/Spelling - as you can tell most of the time in the shows that I write, I sometimes make mistakes, mainly because I have to write so much in such a short time, making it hard to keep re-reading the card. But with roleplays you should always proof read it. Silly mistakes can make you look like an 11 year old not a seasoned professional Titles Here in EWC we have the following titles English Heavyweight Championship - This is the big one. Only the best will fight for this and only the great will win it. The gold belt is one of the highes accolades in the buisness. English Trilogy Championship - History has show us that winners of this title will go on to bigger and better things with the main gold. English Tag Team Championships - Unlike most efeds we take tag team seriously and love them to bits. Any two wrestlers can wear them weather or not they are a tag team. English Junior Heavyweight Championship - This is where the little guys go who are also taken seriously in EWC. This title is always active and has produced some thrillers of a match in the past. The limit is 240lbs Coming Soon English Hardcore Championship - Soon England will get Hardcore Web Site We have a good web site, there are better and many worste. We have a domain name as well. http://www.english-wrestling.com or http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling/Interfed or http://www.angelfire.com/ks/fantasywrestling Closing Note Well you've read all of this, now check out the web site and sign up. We promise that you will have fun and compete with some very good roleplayers. Please feel three to email me any questions Chris
