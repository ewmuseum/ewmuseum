Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Legacy of Champions kept a forum on the hub from at least November 2014, where its owner drew its roster as posers. In February 2015 he moved the art to a comic-book style with a new render model, and for the next six months members and artists posted renders of Rune Winters, Turk, Ninja K, Sven Deadly, Monte Burns and others, some of them arguing it looked like an action figure.

In March 2016 he wrote that a search of another site showed the fed's name 'from 2009' and that its old shows would move to a new site. In May 2018 he posted, for all those who knew him, the news that Sean Williams, the writer of Seymour Almasy, had died that April.
