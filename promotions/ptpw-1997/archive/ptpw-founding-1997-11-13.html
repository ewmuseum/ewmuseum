X-X-CauTioN-X-X "Some Say It`s the best of all time" "The New Generation and The Next Force In Pro Wrestling" "Where the ICON`s Play" Prime Time Professional Wrestling " The True Fighting ground for True Wrestlers" ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ------------------------ (Enter At Your Own Risk) It has finally gotten here! And it`s landed with a huge impact. PTPW Is The premire federation and it`s at your reach! This is where ICONs start and where the ICONs Play. This is where the action is! But enough bragging, I want to tell you a little about myself and my expeiriences in the front office, in the ring and just about everywhere else. I started My career in a little federation called Total Championship Wrestling about a year ago. It was pretty cool for the time but it got pretty boring pretty fast. The president was very biased and blamed all of everything on his Simulator. And thats when I decided Simulators SUCK. That`s kinda off subject. Then I went to NwO. (Not nWo) and there it was pretty fun. The prez was a nice guy and pretty smart in the way he ran his buisness. He inspired me a big deal and I decided to start a federation of my own. I named it The World Wide Wrestling Federation. I know, It`s pretty unoriginal but the federation was over Okay. I had average RPers with great cards. Not great but Better then average and I`d say they were good for my first try. The fed went on for about a week but then Christmas came. I told everyone not To RP until January 4th. When I came back, they either forgot about the fed or just weren`t intrested. I`ve ran about two other federations since then and each one gets better and better but they keep lacking something. I`ve found out what it is and now it`s time to unleash the greatest federation that will ever grace the internet! For about four months I`ve been working here and there in my free time jotting things down for that one day when I`d be able to run my own federation. Now all of those ideas are ready to go into action! I`ve thought out ways, strategies and great plans on how to run this.Things like How to keep it running and how to keep it from egtting stale and boring. How to keep the action and RPs up. How to Write Cards when there isn`t much time left. How to find the time and make the time to write my action packed cards. It`s not like all I do is stay on the computer the whole day.I go to school, Play on the basketball team and have a social life but I know I can Whip up a Good Card In a short amount of time! I like to not have any Dark Matches, Jobber matches or any other crap like that. I want All Out action! But onto some other things. I`d like to keep the limit of wrestlers to 40 . With 40 I can get every one in on the excitement. So that means join now while you can! Before all the spots are taken! Well, enough about the introduction. Lets get on with it! (Stuff) PTPW matches will be decided on a couple of things. 75% Role-plays, 20% Angles and 5% Strats. Ok I know that you all get bored with Fantasy Feds, well I dont want that to happen with PTPW. I want PTPW to be a place you can really call your favorite Fed around. I have put a lot of things in PTPW To make this a great fed, and to keep you interested. For one you can send me Stragities/Sneak Attacks. Those are things like Who you want to fued with, Who you want to focuse on beating, what kind of stragety will you use in your matches, Sneak attacks and things like that. So as you can See I want to make PTPW The best Fed Around, and I want you all to love this fed, so We can all have fun..If you like the fed, and the role plays are doing well, then I will be a happy camper, and have no problem writting the Cards for you. Ok now lets get on with the show. (PTPW Weekly Shows) Okay, there will be three shows each and every week. One card will be a big Nicely Written excitement packed Card that will be nothing but pure action from opening bell `til the last B ag Of popcorn is sold. (Saturday Night Prime Time) Ok This will be are biggest Card of the week. It will be the Monday Night Raw or Nitro of PTPW. It will have About 5-7 full written out matches,With no dark matches. When I say 5-7 I might and might not mean it. It all depends on how many wrestlers we have. I can promise that we will have 5 At the very very least. The Main Event will always be good, and will have tons of live action! This Show will be Hosted by "Cool" Ray Roland, Gary Smith, & Jim Williams... (Wednesday Night War!) This show will be held every week, and it will have Interviews, An update Center, and tons of all the wall uncensored stuff, there wil be no live matches because this is a show just for interviews and updates, but belive me there will be tons of action, who knows who will get into it on this show! It will be hosted by "The Mystery Man" and will have All of the House Show results from the past week. (Friday Night Extreme Zone) This Show may not happen yet, but if it does it will be A Great Card. It will have off the Wall Kind of Matches and Matches only SWWF Can think of. It will have about 5 Matches, and the Main Event, always being Off the Wall, and Extreme...This show will be hosted by "Bubba" Bob Silvers, & Dante Jones. (Canvas Clash) This will be our "Clash of the Champions" it will be held every three months, and will have the same PPV quality type matches, Big Titles are defended, and there are no dark Matches. All Live Matches, It will be hosted by "Cool" Ray Rolan, J.T. Taylor, and Jim Williams. (PTPW Singles Titles) Ok These will be the Official Titles of PTPW. Titles can be handed down to the #1 Contender, and Titles can be stripped. Well here they are... (World Heavyweight Title) his is the #1 Ranked Title, it will be defended up toonce a month, and if you hold this your the best wrestler in PTPW. (International Title) This is the #2 Ranked Title, and will be defended up to Twice, maybe once, a Month. If you hold this you are the Second Best in PTPW. (United States Title) This is the #3 Ranked Title here in the PTPW. Screw Europe this is America! This will be defended Twice A Month. ( Television Title) This Title is the #4 Ranked Title, If you hold this Title You will be the true worker of the PTPW! (The Toughman Title) This title is the 5th ranked title and is only for the Toughesrt man alive. Enter the Toughman Tournament if you dare! You won`t be the Toughest man in the PTPW, You`ll be the toughest man in the World! (PTPW Tag Team Titles) (World Tag Team Titles) his Title is the #1 Ranked Tag Team Title, If you hold this Title it means you are one of the Best Tag Teams in the World. Its an honor to have this title. This Title is defended Twice a month. (United States Tag Team Titles) This Title is defended Once a week, and is the #2 Ranked Tag Team Title. This represents The U.S.! So if you have this you may not be the best world wide but you are in your home country! And If you`re not from America then you can Be the best well.... In America! (PTPW Pay Per Views) PPV's are all Live Matches and the Biggest Events and matches take place! January- New Years Crash (Held in the middla of January! After Words is a Big New Years Eve Party in the Arena..) Febuary- Soul Of The Survivor (A Survivor Series Type PPV!) March- March Madness (This Will be a Special PPV, always held somewere in Other Country's, Like Japan, New England, ETC.) April- Canvas Chaos (All Stipulation Matches, all titles on the line!) May- Spring Bash (Springs Biggest Event!) June- King of Extreme (This is A Tournament to Crown the King of Extreme) July- Summer Ring Wars (This will be Held at a beach, and has plenty of summer action) August- Hardcore Heatwave (The Wrestle Mania of PTPW) September- Ring Riot (A PTPW Style Royal Rumble) October- Judgment Night (PTPW's Type of Halloween Havoc) November- Extreme Fest (Main Event is a Giant Steel Cage Match) December- Winter WarZone (The Biggest Event of the Winter! Always a good card, and always a big Main Event!) (Other Things about PTPW) (House Shows) Dont Let these fool you, just because there not fully written out and just brief summeries doesnt mean a thing. These are great cause you get a lot of matches, and they also bost up the win-loss %. (News Letter) This may be one of the best things about PTPW, in this news letter you will find articals, Rankings, Win-Loss Records, Ratings, and a whole Lot More. These will be out once a week and they are fun to read! And you may also put your articles into the news letter. Just E-Mail [detail withheld] with the article! Articles can count as RPs also! (Injury List)Ok You can suffer Injury's in PTPW. Like if your oppenant just Dominates you in Role Playing you will most likely Be Injured, or you may be Injured to develop an angle, You can also be injured if you are going out of town, and you will recover when you get back. The longest someone can get injured for in PTPW is 2 Months, but there are ways of Recovering Fast. (Contracts) Ok I will send you out Contracts for a certian amount of Money when you fill out the application. This will explain your Contract Terms, and what you can do and how long you will stay, ETC. (PTPW Application) Well have you decided, well if you want to join just fill out this app. You can have up to 3 Wrestlers, Please make a Singles, and Tag Team if possible, because we dont want to run short of Tag Team's here in PTPW like most other feds, Ok here is the app. I also might be bringing in a Six Man Title depending on how many wrestlers we get.
