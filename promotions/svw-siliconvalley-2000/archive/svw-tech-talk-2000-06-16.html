Tech Talk- SVW Recap Show June 16th, 2000 http://www.efeduniverse.com/svw *The opinions expressed on this show do not necessarily reflect the opinion of the SVW front office* ------------------------------------------------------------- [Highlight stills from Saturday Night Superstars roll as "Pump" by Bionic
Drive plays the opening
theme. The words "Tech Talk" scroll across the screen rapidly, then back the other way just as fast. The SVW Logo appears and quickly tears away to reveal Richard
Lancaster and Johnny Houston sitting behind a news-type desk with a large screen behind them positioned in the middle.] Richard Lancaster: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to the first edition "Tech
Talk" where we will
bring you the latest developments in Silicon Valley Wrestling, an in-depth look at last week's card and a preview of what you will see tomorrow night exclusively on KICU
36. And as always, my broadcast partner, the 2-time MAWF Cruiserweight Champion, Johnny Houston, joins me. Johnny Houston: WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO-EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE. Thank ya Dick. What
I'm bringing to the
show tonight is something that hardly any fans see and that's what the SVW wrestlers do during the week prior to wrestling on Saturday Night. We've got exclusive interviews with the superstars you want to see and you'll see it nowhere else but here. And that's a god damn fact.
RL: How eloquently put Johnny. Well last Saturday Night at the Event Center on the campus of San
Jose State University, it was a night that I'll remember the rest of my career as the first SVW card took place. It was short on quantity, but extremely high on quality. We didn't even get 10 minutes into the show before we heard from a man that will certainly make an impact in the coming weeks.
JH: And that man is Todd. He came to the ring on Saturday and was all business. He's cocky, he's arrogant, and he's basically a junior version of me.
RL: Let's take a look at what Todd had to say. [The screen behind Lancaster and Houston flashes to life] Todd: As you all know, I will later tonight, for your entertainment! Will be wrestling Jason
Blake! The big superstar! And I know alot of you know him, and you probably don't know me too well, Let me introduce myself! I am Todd, and this to the right of me is
Tory, my best friend in
the whole world and my manager! And I'm here to let you all know that it will be my pleasure to kick Jason Blake's ass! Cause the way I see it, He totally sucks and cannot compare to me! I am in far better in shape than he is, a more rounded fighter and has more ring experience than he does! And way smarter than he any day! So I am very confident that I will easily defeat him!
RL: Boy, He is laying it on thick. JH: What do you mean? [The crowd is somewhat annoyed.] Todd: Now, I know some of you might think I am what you would say "cocky" BUT I beg to differ, For I am confident about what I can do in the ring and what some others can't do. So for you later tonight, I am dedicated my win to each and every one of you! My fans!
Like the saying, this bud is for you, I have for you, this win is for you! I'm doing my part to entertain you all!
[Camera switches back to Lancaster] RL: Todd certainly had big words to start off the night, but the question was, could he back them up?
JH: Personally, I thought he would, but Jason Blake had other plans. RL: Let's take you highlight of Todd versus Jason "The Bandit" Blake. [Highlights roll] RL: Both men to their feet now, Blake to the ropes...oh wait just a minute. Tory tripped up Blake and now Todd with a knee to the back sends The Bandit to the outside. Here comes Amy Sparks over to help out her man.
JH: More like her meal ticket. RL: Todd runs against the ropes...what's he gonna do...SUICIDE DIVE over the top rope and he takes out both Jason Blake and the lovely Amy Sparks. Todd did bang his leg against the metal guardrail and he's limping a bit. He rolls Blake back in the ring and sends him for the ride...
Spinning Heel kick connects right to the face Jason Blake and a cover...One...Two...Kickout.
Another close call in this match. JH: Now we are seeing the athleticism of Todd. RL: Todd to the ropes again...Hurricanrana...no SITTING POWERBOMB. Blake planted himself and blocked the move. Blake wastes no time going to the tope rope...SHOOTING
STAR PRESS connects.
One....Two....THR...NO...Todd gets the shoulder up. Blake gets Todd to his feet and sets him up...Double Underhook suplex...no Todd with a backdrop, but Blake hangs on for a sunset flip...
One...Two...Todd with a kick to the head and a Rolling Prawn pin. One...Two...Blake escapes. Todd with a standing switch, but an elbow to the head by Blake and the
Dragon Sleeper is applied.
A good wear-down move here. [Another highlight] RL: Todd is again showing his frustration by pounding the mat 3 times. That won't do him any good. He gets Blake to his feet and goes the ropes...leap frog by Blake, back comes Todd...
TILT-A-WHIRL POWERBOMB by Jason Blake. No cover, that could be a mistake. Blake picks up Todd... bulldog...no wait, he springboards off the second rope...ACID DROP. It could be over here. One...
Tory gets in the ring with a chair...Two...Blake gets up and dropkicks the chair right back into
Tory's face, he saw it coming all the way. [Another highlight] RL: The Bandit goes to the top AGAIN. Can he possible press his luck some more...BLINDSIDER. He connected with that twisting somersault Senton he calls the Blindsider.
One...Tory is up on the apron...Two...oh wait, Amy Sparks just pulled Tory's legs out from under him and he hit the apron.
..THREE!!!!!! Jason "The Bandit" Blake is victorious in one helluva match. JH: I'll say, but they still can't perform like me. [Camera back to Lancaster and Houston] RL: That certainly was the match of the night with Jason Blake taking home the victory. JH: He better not rip off my moves or again or I'll teach him a thing or two. RL: I'd love to see that, your back goes anytime you even see a wrestling ring. [Pauses] Moving on, Todd and Jason Blake weren't the only ones on the card were they Johnny?
JH: No, they weren't. The opening match featured "The Vagrant" Vic Venice against "Terrific"
Tommy Jamison [Highlights roll] RL: Venice is climbing to the top now and Jamison is very slowly getting back to his feet. Jamison has his back to Venice....Venice jumps....double axehandle!! Venice grabs Jamison's legs and he's going for a sharpshooter.....Jamison is fighting it....Venice almost has him over....
YES! Venice has the Sharpshooter locked on Jamison.... JH: Venice is slowing down the pace of the match with that move...and he has it locked on in the middle of the ring....but Jamison is slowly getting to the ropes.
RL: Every second he is in that move is take a minute off this match....the sharpshooter is a deceptively devastating maneuver.
JH: Jamison is inches away from the ropes....he's got them! One last surge of energy brought
Jamison close enough to the ropes to grab onto them...but he is obviously hurting now. RL: Venice was really applying the pressure with that move....and he had it locked on for well over a minute. Jamison is as good as done....nobody can come back from that kind of punishment.
Venice is on his feet...waiting for Jamison to get up. Jamison is taking his time...and rightfully so.
JH: He must be in excruciating pain right now.... RL: Jamison on his feet....he's in a daze! He looks to be in immense pain....FALLAWAY SLAM!!
Jamison walked right into it and Venice once again goes for the pinfall.... 1................... 2................... JAMISON KICKS OUT! What is keeping this kid alive? JH: He should just let it go....there's no way he can win! RL: Venice looks pissed...and hungry! He picks up Jamison and sets him up in a suplex position...this could be it! He lifts Jamison up into a suplex....slingshots him off the top rope....JACKHAMMER!!!
JH: He calls that the Freeloader!!! RL: Venice covers.... 1....................... 2.......................... 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Vic Venice wins [Camera back to Lancaster and Houston] RL: Vic Venice got all that he bargained for and more from Tommy Jamison
JH: I don't know what the hell got into Jamison. I've seen him get beat by crash dummies in the
NCWF and all of a sudden he puts on a great match with Vic Venice. I guess all those trips to the gym finally paid off.
RL: Speaking of Venice, he had another interesting adventure backstage. The
SVW cameras caught
this.
[Suddenly, the camera switches to the back where Jason "The Bandit" Blake and Amy Sparks have just left their dressing room. As they turn the corner, "The Vagrant" Vic
Venice appears and
opens up the room, he takes something out of Blake's bag and disappears.]
RL: You can't help but wonder what Vic Venice took out of that bag of Jason
Blake. JH: Well our own Alyssa Lockhart filed this exclusive interview with Jason
Blake. (At that moment, Alyssa Lockhart walks in. She notices the expression on
Blake's face and she immediately takes a step back.)
Alyssa Lockhart - Are you alright Jason? You look like you are ready to kill someone. Jason Blake - Yeah I'm fine. I'm assuming you'd like an interview? Alyssa Lockhart - If it's no trouble. Jason Blake - Well I have a few minutes to spare. But please make it as brief as you can. Alyssa Lockhart - No problem. So how do you feel after your impressive debut tonight? Jason Blake - Well it was nice to get the win, but Todd has a lot of talent. He just let his cockiness get a little too out of hand. If he can focus, he could be a tough challenge to anyone. He pushed me to the limit, which is something I wasn't really expecting.
Alyssa Lockhart - Next week you are scheduled to face "Model Citizen" Ian
Wright. What are your thoughts on him?
Jason Blake - In all honesty, he doesn't really scare me all that much. He may think he is the epitome of perfection, but I will expose all his imperfections on Saturday. He will fall to the same fate as Todd did, and
I will show everyone that he isn't as perfect as he thinks he is. Of course I'll be watching out, and I won't underestimate him. But I feel that I possess the talent and experience necessary to pull out a victory against Ian Wright.
Alyssa Lockhart - One last thing. We got a little footage of something that went on after you left your locker room earlier. I think you'll be quite interested in seeing it.
Jason Blake - Are you serious? Let me see it now please. Alyssa Lockhart - All right. Follow me. (Jason and Amy follow Alyssa to the TV truck outside the arena. Alyssa puts a tape in the VCR and Jason watches in shock. It shows Jason and Amy leaving the room, and Vic Venice then is seen entering the room. He rummages through Jason's bag, takes a small object, and sneaks out.
Jason's face turns red and his eyes light up as Alyssa turns off the tape.) Alyssa Lockhart - Well I don't know what that thing was that he stole, but
I have a feeling that is what is bothering you.
Jason Blake - (Yelling) THAT BASTARD VENICE IS GOING TO GET THE BEATING OF A LIFETIME!! I PAID FIVE THOUSAND DOLLARS FOR THAT!! Amy Sparks - Calm down Jason. What could you have possibly paid five thousand for that was so small? I hope you didn't get some new fancy watch or something.
Jason Blake - I'll explain it later. We gotta get going. But come next
Saturday, Vic Venice had better return what he took, or else I'm going to make his life miserable. Thanks Alyssa, you saved me a lot of aggravation just now.
Alyssa Lockhart - Not a problem. good luck next week. (Jason and Amy leave the TV truck, and they rush to Jason's car. They get in, and Jason speeds off. The screen fades to black.)
RL: Whatever Venice took, it meant a lot to Jason Blake. JH: I've never been an advocate for crime, but if that thing was so special to him, why did he leave it in his bag? I mean, if I paid 5000, well let's say 50,000 since 5 grand doesn't fit my style, I'd keep it locked up tighter than Fort Knox, not in a gym bag.
RL: Venice had no right going into his room and searching through his things. That's downright rude.
JH: Finder keepers, losers weepers. RL: I feel like weeping now. [Pauses] We will definitely follow this story tomorrow with great interest. Now let's talk about some of the newcomers you will see tomorrow night. The man I'm most intrigued with is the "Model Citizen" Ian Wright.
JH: I don't think "intrigued" is the word I'd use. This guy is more focused on getting the dirt underneath his fingernails out than his opponent. Just take a look at his press conference earlier in the week.
[Our scene opens inside of the smoky chambers of the S.V.W. press conference room. A multitude of tables littered with coffee cups, soda bottles and ash trays are set up in rows. A variety of reports from the wrestling circle--internet reporters, radio broadcasters and newspaper columnists alike-are on hand, sitting attentively, all focused on the stage at the front of the room.]
[A long table on the front stage seems to be the center of attention. Sitting at this table are S.V.W. president Michael Ellis and vice president Don Hutchinson, as well as S.V.W. correspondents Alyssa Lockhart and Jay Joseph. Sitting in the middle is a fifth man, around 240 pounds, his build showing from beneath his t-shirt which reads "W.W.I.D.?"]
[Correspondent Jay Joseph rises from his seat and sets up at the podium. Heputs on his spectacles, takes out index cards from his shirt pocket, and taps the microphone twice to make sure it is working.]
Jay Joseph ~~ Ladies and gentlemen, members of the press, on behalf of the
S.V.W., I would like
to thank you all for making it here today for the introduction of our latest signing. So without further ado, I present the "Model Citizen" Ian Wright.
[The gentleman with the "W.W.I.D?" t-shirt rises to his feet, hands raised in the air as he soaks in a relatively modest applause. He turns toward President Ellis in order to shake his hand, momentarily turning his back to the crowd of reporters, revealing the back of his t-shirt to read "What Would Ian Do?"]
[After greeting the executive committee, the "Model Citizen," showing nothing but smile, takes the spot at the podium.]
Ian Wright ~~ Thank you ladies and gentlemen. It is a privilege and an honor for all of you to be here today. I too am exited to have (SNIFF) this opportunity to (SNIFF,
SNIFF) introduce
myself and...
[As he speaks, Ian Wright is becoming visibly distracted. Still maintaining his pleasant demeanor and smile, he breaks from his introduction to address a young, female reporter from Pro
Wrestling Illustrated sitting at the second table who is taking notes, a cigarette burning in the ash tray next to her.]
Ian Wright ~~ Excuse me maam, but can you please put that cigarette out? Reporter ~~ I beg your pardon. Ian Wright ~~ That cigarette next to you. Can you put it out please? Smoking is really bad for you, maam. It causes emphaszima, lung cancer, heart problems, and birth
defects-- not to mention that it stains your teeth and smells up your close. It has been said that kissing a smoker is like kissing an ash tray-- not that I would know because I would never date a smoker-but that's what I hear.
[The reporter, visibly disturbed by the Model Citizen's advise, puts down her pen and shoots
Ian an all-so-deadly stare.] Reporter ~~ First of all, I don't have to worry about birth defects because
I am not pregnant--
Ian Wright ~~ Well that's good to hear, because I don't see a ring on your finger and premarital sex is definitely not--
Reporter (cutting him off) ~~ So what you're saying is that you want me to put my cigarette out?
Ian Wright ~~ Yes ma'am, if you wouldn't mind. And that goes for the rest of you in here. On behalf of all the non-smokers in the room who should not have to subject themselves to second-hand smoke, please put your cigarettes out.
Reporter ~~ I am sorry, but I have a big problem with that. I am a journalist. My job is founded on First Amendment rights-- the freedom of speech, the freedom of the press, and most importantly, the freedom of expression. I have been reporting for the S.V.W. for a very long time and there has never been a rule against smoking in the S.V.W. news room, so I ain't putting this cigarette out for no one.
[Ian rolls his eyes and lets out a sigh. Although he attempts to maintain his pleasant demeanor, a hint of frustration seems to be setting in.]
Ian Wright ~~ I believe the correct grammar you were looking for is "I am not putting this cigarette out for anyone," maam. "Ain't" is not a word, and even if was, using it in conjunction with "no one" is a double negative. Essentially, what you just told me is that there is no one for which you will not put the cigarette out.
Reporter ~~ You know what? I give up. I don't need to take this. You cannot possibly be good enough of a wrestler to justify this much abuse.
[The reporter rises to her feet and storms out of the press room, all other smokers following suit. This brings a smile to Ian's face, as he takes a deep breath of relief.]
Ian Wright ~~ Ahh, see that-- fresh air already! So where were we? (pointing to another journalist at the first table) Yes sir, you have a question? Go ahead and fire away.
Second Reporter ~~ Yes, this is Carl Wilkes with the World Wrestling Weekly, can you give us some information into your background-- perhaps something about your experience, your--
Ian Wright (interrupting) ~~ Excuse me sir, I don't mean to interrupt, but is that coffee that you are drinking? Let me divulge to you a well-known statistic regarding the intake of caffeine...
[That does it. The remaining reporters rise to their feet in defiance, all mumbling to themselves as they file out of the conference room in frustration, cutting short Ian's introduction.]
Ian Wright ~~ Wait, where are you all going!?! We're not done here!!! I heard that-- you really shouldn't curse like that...
[The "Model Citizen" Ian Wright storms out after the reporters to continue his lecture on cursing as our scene FADES TO BLACK.]
JH: See what I mean Dick? He's nuts. RL: I only hope that the "Model Citizen" is prepared for Jason Blake tomorrow night. In addition to Ian Wright's debut, we will see Samuel "Mule" Lowenstein, Viper and
Cecil.
JH: Geez, what is it with these one word names? I think I'll change mine to just Johnny. RL: Might not be a bad idea. Quickly, in the time we have left Johnny. How do you see tomorrow night's matches?
JH: The real sleeper here is Top Lop against Cecil. I both of these men during the week and it looks like it could get ugly and violent. Just the way I like it.
RL: Opening up the card will be Screwface against Tommy Jamison. JH: Screwface? Is he even on the roster? I haven't seen him yet, so he's more like Screwed Up.
RL: Next we have Todd versus Samuel "Mule" Lowenstein and Vic Venice against
Viper. JH: Todd and Mule could be another sleeper match considering the words those two have exchanged during the week And if Jason Blake has anything to say about it, Vic Venice won't have his hand raised against Viper.
RL: Finally, Ian Wright against Jason Blake. JH: If Ian Wright is more concerned with Blake than the speck of dust on the ring apron, this will be a great match, possibly match of the night. I think Blake's on a roll and might have enough to pull it out.
RL: Well that's about all the time we have tonight. For Johnny Houston, I'm
Richard Lancaster,
we'll see you tomorrow night at the San Jose Convention Center for Saturday
Night Superstars.
Good night everyone. [Credits roll] http://www.efeduniverse.com/svw
