SVW Tech Talk July 7th, 2000 http://www.efeduniverse.com/svw [Highlight stills from Saturday Night Superstars roll as "Pump" by Bionic
Drive plays the
opening theme. The words "Tech Talk" scroll across the screen rapidly, then back the other way just as fast. The SVW Logo appears and quickly tears away to reveal Richard
Lancaster and Johnny
Houston sitting behind a desk with the SVW logo on the front of it. A large, black screen is positioned behind them in the middle.]
Richard Lancaster: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to "Tech Talk," the weekly
SVW recap show. I'm
Richard Lancaster and what a night at the Santa Clara County Fairgrounds...
Johnny Houston: WHOAH, WHOAH, WHOAH Dick...aren't you forgetting something? RL: Oh my...yes. Tonight, Tech Talk is brought to you by... JH: NO, not that...you forgot to introduce me! RL: All right fine...joining me, as always is a man who needs no introduction, the 2-time MAWF
Cruiserweight Champion...Johnny Houston.
JH: WHOOOOOOOOOO-EEEEEEEEE, it's great to be here once again. RL: I wish the feeling was mutual, but nonetheless the show must go on. JH: And it sure can't go on without me. RL: That's debatable...[pauses] As I was saying before I was rudely interrupted, it was an historic show last Saturday Night at the Santa Clara County Fairgrounds...
JH: It was historic for you, but that place stunk to high heaven. RL: Despite the way Johnny Houston felt, the crowd was electric and before we even started, Vic
Venice was up to his usual antics.
[Prior to the opening of Saturday Night Superstars, "The Vagrant" Vic Venice is walking around the backstage area. He appears to be looking for someone. He finds the door that says "Jason
Blake". Vic knocks on it and awaits an answer. After a few moments, Amy
Sparks opens the door]
Amy: Oh, it's you. Jason is in the shower. But while I have you alone.... Venice: Won't Jason be mad if you leave him for me? Amy: You pig!!! [Sparks slaps Venice across the face]...I wanted to know what it is that you have on Jason.
[Venice composes himself] Venice: Lots of feisty broads around here. Anyway.... [Amy is pulled away as Jason Blake is now at the door] Blake: Venice, what do you want? I'm not giving you my locker room again, that one last week was awful.
Venice: Oh, keep your locker room, I have something else I need from you. [Vic leans in and whispers into Blake's ear. Blake turns as white as a ghost.] Venice: Good luck tonight [Venice slaps Blake on the shoulder and leaves. Blake rubs his hand over his chin. The Superstars intro begins] [Fade back] RL: Vic Venice continues to amaze me at what he has the guts to do. JH: No doubt he has the balls of steel, but we didn't know what made Blake turn ghost white.
RL: Just after we officially started the show, Todd had something of his own going on. [Backstage in the parking garage, Todd is greeted by Catch 22. The two masked men walk up to
Todd, checking out the garage to make sure no one is around.]
Todd: Bout time you two. Here's your test for the night. Get this done and we'll talk about a job.
[The two men shake their heads as Todd hands them a folded piece of paper.] [Fade back] RL: A short, but meaningful meeting because it didn't take long for Todd and
Catch 22 to make
some noise.
[Catch 22 floods Jason locker room and nail him from behind. Catching him off guard, they stomp away on Jason, while Amy screams and stands off to the side.]
RL: They are putting the boots to him, whoever they are. The one guy in
Black with a body slam
on Jason Blake, as the small white dressed guy smacks Jason across the chest with a chair.
JH: "Not just once, four times, Blows like that can break a few ribs Dick. And with those few added stomps, that just adds to the damage." White guy: Hey, how about a piledriver on the bench?" [The black guy looks at the nice hard bench. Amy cries out no!] RL: They wouldn't? JH: These guys are really going all out! Looks at them pick up the beaten and bruised Jason
Blake.
RL: The.........can we please get a name on these two? Please, saying the black guy is retarded
JH: Blackie has Jason set in the pile driver, and man, this is going to be great!
[A sickening thud echoes in the locker room as "Blackie" drops Jason Blake on his head and Jason slums to the floor. Amy slaps "Blackie" on the back.]
JH: I don't think that was wise for Amy, She is tough, but? RL: Now the white guy.....ohhhh, PLEASE! SOME NAMES? JH: Pearl is now in the face of Amy Sparks! "Pearl": Hey (beep)! YOU shouldn't have DONE that! DON'T you KNOW who WE are? "Blackie": WE'RE!" "Pearl": CATCH 22, and don't YOU forget THAT! Blackie: and WHEN your OLD man GETS up! MAKE sure Pearl: YOU Blackie: tell Pearl: HIM! [Blackie pulls out a bottle of Jim Beam and pops the top. Taking a sip, he looks over Amy.]
[Fade back] RL: Certainly a brutal and uncalled for attack. JH: As far as I'm concerned, Blake had it coming to him because Todd warned him and he did nothing.
RL: Come on, he was attacked 2 on 1. And those two men were monsters. Todd was conspicuous by his absence, but not for long as he took on Cecil in the opening match.
[Fade to match] RL: Todd is right back up! Round house kick, Cecil ducks, Cecil with a punch to Todd's knee!
JH: Take down by Cecil. RL: Cecil tries to roll Todd in a Boston crap, WAIT! It's those guys! (Catch 22 has walked out from the back and stand at ringside.) JH: Cecil sees them... Todd escapes from the hold, But Cecil right on top of him, Irish whip into the ropes, Leapfrog by Todd, OH!
RL: What a shame, Todd's foot catches on the bottom rope, tripping him. JH: I hate when that happens! RL: Cecil with a fishermen carry with bridge... 1.... 2..... 3..... JH: I can't believe that! Todd was robbed! RL: Cecil gets a win with some help from the ropes! [Todd throws a fit. As Cecil gets his arms raised, Catch 22 floods the ring swing some chairs.
Tory starts to walk towards the back. Pearl and Blackie nail Cecil from behind. The ref tries to break it up, But Todd DDT's him for his trouble. Pearl lays the two chairs on the mat as
Blackie power bombs Cecil on the chairs, Pearl then jumps and rolls Cecil over, locking in the ankle lock submission.]
RL: OH COME ON! WE SAW THIS LAST WEEK WITH TOP LOP! SOME ONE STOP THIS! JH: All is fair in love and war Dick. RL: This is just unsportsman like. Todd lost, That doesn't mean he has to kick the guy in the head as his ankle is being broken!
JH: Shhh, Todd has a mic! [Todd stops kicking Cecil in the head, As Cecil screams for mercy, Todd picks up a mic.] Todd: YOU SON OF A BITCH! I HAD YOUR ASS BEAT! I HOPE YOU LIKE YOUR LITTLE WIN, IT WILL BE THE LAST ONE YOU GET FROM ME! [Todd kicks Cecil one last before Pearl breaks the hold.] Todd: NOW! I'M SURE YOU ALL SAW MY TWO NEW PARTNERS HERE BEAT UP AND GET REVENGE ON THAT PRIMA DONNA JASON BLAKE! LAST WEEK JASON, I STOOD RIGHT HERE IN THIS RING AND TOLD YOU I WOULD GET REVENGE! BUT YOU CHOOSED TO IGNORE ME! AND NOW YOU PAID THE PRICE!] RL: Man is he pissed. [The crowd boo's as Todd takes a pause, the "You're a Loser" followed by three claps chants is started, And that grates Todd's skin.]
Todd: OH, SO YOU ALL THINK I'M A LOSER? OK! I'LL SHOW YOU ALL WHO IS A LOSER! THERE REMAINS ONE PERSON ON THE LIST THAT LAST WEEK I SAID WOULD PAY THE PRICE! SO MULE! I KNOW YOU CAN HEAR ME BACK THERE! BEFORE THIS NITE IS OVER! AS GOD IS MY WITNESS! I WILL WEAR YOUR BLOOD AND CARRY YOUR ASS TO THE RING FOR YOUR MATCH! SO IF I WAS YOU, YOU BETTER WATCH YOUR BACK! CAUSE, REVENGE SHALL BE MINE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Break it begins to play as the crowd is in an uproar] [Fade back] RL: Todd with another brutal attack. Can this guy be stopped? JH: He doesn't have anything a good dose of Prozac can't cure. Maybe Mustang Bradley can loan him some. RL: Who in god's name is Mustang Bradley? JH: He's.ah never mind. RL: Todd's vicious attack was a prelude to what Ian Wright had in store for
Samuel Lowenstein.
[ENTER Ian Wright. The self-proclaimed "Model Citizen" enters the locker, whistling suspiciously as Lowenstein approaches a metal locker with his name on it.]
[Lowenstein grabs the steel masterlock and begins to turn the combo. He gives it a tug, but to no avail. Again, but nothing. The stubborn wrestler tugs at it the padlock with both hands, but nothing doing.]
[Suddenly, Lowenstein becomes confused, nervous as he realizes that his hands are stuck to the padlock and he cannot remove them.]
[The Model Citizen nonchalantly picks up a steel chair as he begins to chuckle.] IW: Sammy, you know what a mule is?-- it's nothing but a dumb horse. And do you know what happens to horses when they pass away? [Ian pulls out a tube of crazy glue] They are turned into glue.
[Lowenstein turns pale as he realizes that he has been set up. He sees Ian slowly wind up with the chair, but is defenseless as the steel meets his back with deadly force.]
[Ian repeatedly delivers chair shot after chair shot into Lowenstein, finally leaving a bloody, dented chair at the Mule's side.]
[Fade back] RL: Wright was certainly looking for revenge and he got it. JH: Mule had better protect himself better. Perhaps the SVW needs some security or something.
RL: Wright didn't have to worry about Mule coming out as he took on the battered Jason Blake.
It doesn't take much to conclude who won this match. [Fade to match] RL: Blake's in full control now. Wait a minute, why is Vic Venice out here? JH: I don't know, but remember what we saw before the show started. I wonder if Vic is coming out to see what Blake has to say.
RL: Oh no, and Edgar is with him and he has the metal detector!! [Edgar goes over by the announcer's table while Venice gets up on the apron and yells something at Blake and Ian Wright]
RL: What does this guy want? JH: Probably looking for loose change. RL: C'mon, Johnny, we both know that metal detector is not used for finding loose change. [Venice continues to exchange words with both wrestlers as Edgar gets into the ring. In the mean time, Blake shakes his head at Venice as if to answer a question Vic had for him]
RL: What was that all about? JH: I don't know, but the action seems to have stopped as a result. [Blake waves his hands at Vic as to tell him to get lost and turns around. Edgar winds up with the metal detector and whacks Blake with it and gets out of the ring] RL: He hit Blake with the metal detector!!! He hit Blake with the metal detector!! JH: That's twice now! RL: This has gone to far, someone has to stop Vic and his croonie from interfering in matches they have no business being involved in.
[Venice gets off the apron as Edgar gets out. The ref and Wright turn around and see that Blake is out cold. Wright looks around and smiles. He covers]
RL: Oh no, Wright is going for the cover!! 1.......... 2........... 2 ½....................... 3....................!!!!!!!!!! Son of a......Venice and Edgar cost Jason Blake this match. CD: The winner of this match...."Model Citizen" Ian WRIGHTTTTTTTTTTTT RL: Ian Wright moves on the finals of the Heavyweight Tournament, but he can't be proud of how he did it.
JH: Any way you can get to the finals, is a good way. Everything can't be all kosher you know.
RL: Speaking of kosher, we are told that something is going on in the back again involving Samuel Lowenstein. [Again backstage, in the hallway before Samuel "Mule" Lowenstein's match.] RL: And here we are, in the back stage area and we see The Mule walking out for his match.
JH: You have to wonder what he is thinking, after what Todd has said and all. RL: I don't know,.......HEY! [Catch 22 rounds the corner just as the Mule was walking out to the floor. Blackie with a chair and Pearl with a chain whip. Blackie cracks the Mule with the chair over his head, The Mule falls to one knee, but Pearl whips him in the back with the chain. Blackie with another chair shot, sending the Mule to the cold concrete.] RL: IT"S CATCH 22 AGAIN! JH: Boy, They're good for their word. RL: They need to take that damn chair away from Blackie, how many chair shots can the mule take?
[Pearl wraps the chain around his hand, Blackie drops the chair and picks up the Mule. Pearl unloads with a shot to the forehead, breaking The Mule open, causing him to bleed.]
Blackie: HEY! Mule! Pearl: TODD Blackie: says Pearl: HI! Blackie: Bitch! [The crowd is in a up roar, and then it gets deafening as Todd walks out from the back.] RL: OH! Come on! If those two weren't enough! Todd: That's it! ONE MORE TIME! HIT HIM! [Pearl unloads with another headshot with his fist wrapped in the chain.] [Just then SVW security arrive on scene and break up the attack. VP Don
Hutchison orders Todd
and Catch 22 be escorted out of the building.]
[Fade back] RL: What a crazy sequence of events there as we have Ian Wright advance to the finals and then
Samuel Lowenstein gets attacked AGAIN, this time by Catch 22. Unreal.
JH: Ellis finally hired some security, but he must have gone to Winchell's at Midnight because those security guys were anything but fit.
RL: The final match of evening was Vic Venice against Samuel "Mule" Lowenstein and the end of this match featured another strange ending. [Fade to match] RL: Yes he is. Venice picks him up and gets him in a suplex position, he lifts him up for The
Freeloader, BUT LOWENSTEIN GETS LOOSE!! Lowenstein slides out and lands behind him.....STIFF

Venice with everything
he's got!!! Lowenstein slowly getting to his feet...wait a minute...here comes "The Bandit"
Jason Blake to ringside!!! JH: What's that goody two shoes doing here? RL: Lowenstein sees him...and he shakes his hand!!! What is with Lowenstein and shaking hands tonight!?! Well Blake seems amused...Venice is getting up behind
Lowenstein....Blake tells Lowenstein!!! Lowenstein turns around as Venice runs at him.....TILT-A-WHIRL INTO A FACE SLAM!!! THAT'S HIS MOVE!!! THE LOWEST COMMON DENOMINATOR!!! LOWENSTEIN COVERS!!!! 1.............................................. 2................................................ 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! JH: NOOO!!!! RL: LOWENSTEIN IS GOING ON TO THE FINALS!!!! What an upset victory for
Lowenstein!!! He's going
to fight Ian Wright for the SVW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP!!!!
[Fade back] RL: Overcoming 2 earlier attacks and a fresh opponent in Venice. Lowenstein was still able to come out a winner.
JH: Yeah, with help from Blake. It's about time he did something. RL: As we turn our focus to this week, the match everyone is talking about,
Ian Wright against
Samuel Lowenstein for the SVW Heavyweight Title.
JH: This one is practically a no-brainer. Ian Wright knows what it takes to win and he has the skill and the smarts. Lowenstein got laid out last week and I wouldn't be surprised to see
Wright strike again.

JH: He's running on luck and it only gets you so far. Just like that cheap cologne you are wearing.

"The Bad Act" Dallas Blake.
JH: Anyone with "The Big" as a nickname has to got to be the favorite in this one Dick. RL Next is "Legal Eagle" Fred Farrell against Zandig. JH: Farrell is seedy, he's distasteful, he's rude, crude and crass, that's why he'll win this one going away. Zandig....isn't that a band?
RL: I believe that's Danzig. JH: Same difference. RL: "Varsity" Vince Riley makes his debut against the returning Top Lop. JH: I'm real impressed with Riley Dick, he's young, athletic and he'll take

RL: This is definitely a can't-miss show folks. Tomorrow night you can watch the first SVW

High School Sports
