Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NWA TNA, written NWA-TNA on its later pages, was a website e-fed. Its front office of July 2004 listed Join, Staff and Rules, a Roleplay Board and an Out of Character Board, an Xplosion card and results, a Pay Per View card and results, and a locker room of male roster, diva roster, couples, stables and champions. The update of 5 July 2004 added a four-divas gab card and named a “Director of Authority” and a president, and its RP’er of the Week was Ferocious P.

By February 2005 the menu added NWA-TNA Knockouts, an Injured page, a Tag Team/Stables page and Thursday Night Domination beside Sunday Night Xplosion and the Sunday Night PPVs. Its news of 7 February 2005 said the Xplosion results and card were up and the injured and champions pages updated, signed “Comissioner Shawn Michaels and Prez Sunny”. It listed Sean Orton, The Rock, Kane and Southside Shane as joining and Goldberg as leaving, and a top five of Jason Harris, Matt Hardy, Alex Chance, Rose Mendoza and Haylie McAdams.

Its welcome of 29 July 2008 announced a new layout: the Xplosion card up, the Mid Summer Bash results up, the Hall of Fame page updated and a new Tag Teams page. Its menu now listed superstars, tag teams, couples, stables, managers, champions, an Impact title history, a Hall of Fame and its induction, a TNA Spotlight, Impact, Xplosion and pay-per-view cards, results and roleplay boards, a back-up board, an In Memory Of page and the 2007 End Of The Year Awards. Weekly top tens ranked who had roleplayed: the Xplosion list of the week of May 18 began with TJ Lightning, the Impact list of the week of May 23 with The Maverick Elite and then TJ Lightning, and the pay-per-view list of the week of April 27 with Madison and then Candice Michelle.
