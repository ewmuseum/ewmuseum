A Change in FWF's Format

Jan 02, 2000, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings all and welcome to a New Century of Frontier Wrestling Federation action! With a century just ending and a new millennium coming next year, what better time for growth and changes?
With that in mind, after taking a vote of the FWF Roster (and that of RSPWF as a whole as well), the Executive Board of the FWF and myself have made the decision to change FWF's current "Real" format to that of a "Mixed/Real"
format. This is a decision which opens whole new worlds of possibilities for both FWF and E-Wrestling as a whole.
To the Singles Wrestlers of RSPWF and the leagues within: Do you think you have what it takes to take the FWF World Heavyweight Title from our current champion, "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash? Do you believe you have the roleplaying skills to stand toe to toe with some of the other great wrestlers of the
FWF, such as The Undertaker, Raven, Sting, Chris Jericho, or Justin Credible? To the Tag Teams of RSPWF and the leagues within: Do you believe you have it within you to take the FWF World Tag Team Titles from the current champions?
If so, then you should know what stands before you. The Dark @lliance of Crush and Primetime Brian Lee... Two men who have held the World Tag Team Titles in Every league they have wrestled in! Two men who have already beaten some of the best RSPWF and it's leagues have to offer already on more than one occasion! Do you think you have what it takes?
Then step forth, and take up the gauntlet! Fill out the application below, and send it to [detail withheld] along with a sample roleplay. If you are accepted, you will recieve every opportunity to prove yourself against the best!

Its nothing but silent, the lighters in the crowd are being turned on, and the people are watching in vain to see whats going to happen. No movement is detected within the ring.
Suddenly, the speakers throughuut the arena go apeshit, and the insane guitar tabs of "Come Out and Play", by the Offspring rings out through the vast space within the arena. The entranceway is lighted up by yellow and red lights, but they dont move, they simply light up the aisle-way. The curtains part, and the ever volumptious Beulah shows her head. The then removes the curtain, and reveals HER ring attire, which consists of a sheer, see through white top, and yet another see through garment, "short shorts".
Through the see through daisey dukes, you can see her white panties, and the male partition of the crowd goes wild.
Finally, the words of the song are heard, and Raven makes his appearance. His head is down, staring directly at the floor. He pauses for a second, standing outside of the curtains. Then, finally, he looks at the ring, and ultimately at his opponent standing within. He begins his walk down the aisle, wearing his trademarked leather jacket, biker style. He is wearing a black "SandMan" T-Shirt, from the comic series. The blues and purples travel rampant through the shirt, until the jacket finally covers the remainder of the pictures. He has a red, black, and green flannel shirt tied around his waist, yet another trademark of Raven. And then finally, his wrestling boots are seen. The left foot has a small INCLINE in the sole, due to an accident hes had as a child. His left leg is SLIGHTLY shorter than his right, so he must wear these boots to wrestle properly in. Otherwise, risk severe permenant injury.
As he walks, ever so slowly up the metal staircase right by the ring, he pauses at the top stair, letting Beulah sit on the rope, and hold them open for him. He ducks his head, moves it under the rope, then pulls first his right leg in, then his left. Beulah enters diurectly behind him. He stands in the middle of the ring, in the "Crucifixion" pose, and listens to the crowds boos and drunken slurs towards him. He removes his leather jacket, and gives it to Beulah, who he now instructs to take it to the time keeper outside of the ring. She does that, and while she does this, he walks over to his corner, and basically plants his ass right on the mat, while he uses both arms to hold onto the second rope while he sits.. Finally, after all ring preparations are done, the bell rings, and he pulls himself up into a standing position, and walks into the center of the ring to meet his already waiting opponent. )
