Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Primetime Wrestling Academy said what it was in its own words: “This is an ewrestling school and not an efed. Once you get good enough you can go to PCW, where there are titles, multiple cards and PPVs. Welcome to school.” Open since late 2000, it sat its students in named classes under teachers, graded every submitted roleplay against a published rubric — size, quality, ambience, storyline, originality, grammar, readability — and ran a simulated card most weeks, its results decided on the Zeus simulator from stats its own students submitted.

Its own record disagrees with itself about whether it ever crowned a champion: a February 2003 rules page says plainly “no titles,” while that same month's own “Sunday Night Rave!” card stages a singles match for Tempest's Coming Storm Championship, and a June 2003 news post runs a tournament for what it calls “the soon to be renamed PWA Championship.” No result for either survives.

A June 2003 Main Roster of seventeen students carried an injured list of two and a single tag team, L.O.G.; by December 2003, after a change of hands and a new headmistress, sixteen students sat one class under a teacher billed Rent-A-Hero and two more sat a second. The academy fed graduates onward to five federations by its own account — PCW, OSW, PWC, RUSH and PURE — and its own graduates lists from December 2001 and April 2002 mark out the ones who went furthest: future champions of PCW, OSW and NTICW among them.

Sean Simon Williams' character The H.I.T. taught a class here in 2001, during his own first run at PCW, a debt one of his students repaid in the ring years later by calling him, simply, a proud teacher.

The academy relaunched on 26 Nov 2003, calling itself “the most successful training center in the history of online wrestling,” and its own surviving record ends less than two weeks later, on 8 December 2003. How it closed for good is not in that record; the federation its own FAQ named as the family's flagship, PCW, went dark the following year.

Affiliations

Primetime Championship Wrestling (PCW, Primetime, 2000–2004)

Fed by its academy PWA (Primetime, 2000–2003)
PCW's own FAQ, verbatim: “It is a wrestling academy, which has been open since late 2000... We take the best workers from the PWA into PCW.” It is by far the most-cited outside promotion across PCW's own wrestler bios — nearly every long-tenured PCW wrestler passed through it first. OSW's own record independently confirms it as a shared academy feeding both OSW and PCW.

Old School Wrestling (OSW, Easton, 2002)

Fed by its academy PWA (Primetime, 2000–2003)
PWA appears constantly across OSW's own record, most often as a shared developmental academy feeding talent into both OSW and PCW — multiple bios describe wrestlers training in PWA before “graduating” into OSW (e.g. Tom Walczak: “about a month after joining PWA he was one of the first to graduate... waiting to showcase his talent in the new OSW”). Amy “Aimz” Campbell and PCW's own Rent-A-Hero are shown reminiscing over a shared PWA-era photo, tying the academy directly into the OSW/PCW crossover romance thread as well.

Puroresu Ultimate Ring Entertainment (PURE)

Academy — feeds into PWA (Primetime, 2000–2003)
The academy's own FAQ names PURE among the federations it fed graduates to (“whether it be the Primetime Wrestling Academy or New True Internet Wrestling, we also run these organizations”; PURE is named alongside PCW, OSW, PWC and RUSH elsewhere in the same document); independently, The H.I.T. (a PWA-trained wrestler, see the character's own tenures) wrestled at both.

New True Internet Championship Wrestling (NTICW)

Academy — feeds into PWA (Primetime, 2000–2003)
The academy's own December 2001 graduates list annotates Semore Smoke “*** Later became NTICW Champ,” and NTICW's own record holds him as a champion. A single annotation rather than an FAQ-level claim, so graded a step below the FAQ-named federations.

PWC

Academy — feeds into PWA (Primetime, 2000–2003)
The academy's own FAQ names PWC among the federations it fed graduates to, alongside PCW, OSW, RUSH and PURE. PWC is not held.

RUSH

Academy — feeds into PWA (Primetime, 2000–2003)
The academy's own FAQ names RUSH among the federations it fed graduates to, alongside PCW, OSW, PWC and PURE. Independently receipted from RUSH's own side: RUSH's Adrenaline 18 commercial break asks its own audience to "grade whenever you've got a spare moment" at the Primetime Wrestling Academy, and twelve of RUSH's own "Other Feds" bio fields name PWA beside school-shaped claims ("PWA Graduate", "PWA #1 Student", "PWA Top 5", "PWA Brandi Class Champion", "PWA Tiger class champion"). RUSH is not held.
