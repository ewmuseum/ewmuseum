Joing the Primetime Wresting Academy You've been accepted.

Yes, that's it. Here in the PWA we believe that everyone has the right to learn, so if you want to join simply post a promo on the PWA Roleplay Board and it will be graded. If it's your first promo then once it's graded you will be added to a class.

The PWA Roleplay Board is HERE.

If you otherwise have any questions then feel free to send them HERE [detail withheld] or ask on the PWA Out of Character Board HERE.

Remember that once your promo has been graded and it has been recorded onto the site, it is moved to the Graded Roleplays Board HERE.
