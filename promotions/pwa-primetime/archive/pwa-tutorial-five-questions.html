Five Questions You Should Ask Yourself When Creating a New Character by The Phantom Booker
2/17/03

I am not a believer in long, preachy tutorials.

Below are five basic questions you should ask yourself when you create a new ewrestler.

Can you describe your character's gimmick in a nutshell?
You should be able to give a one to two sentence statement, giving an overview of what your character is all about.

Yes, your character may have many different layers, but when describing him to someone, such as another player or fedhead, its helpful to give them the quick picture.

What has happened in your character's (real/created)past that have helped build him to where he is today?
Characters need a backstory, whether it be real or created, to help flesh out why they are the way they are at the present time.

What are your character's goals?
What drives this character and ultimately, where would you like to see this character go, or what goals would you like to see this character reach? This is important to determine the direction of the character from the present on forward. You should look in the short term, six months from now and a year from now.

What role would this character fill in a federation?
Whether you are joining a new federation, or an established one, you need to have an idea of what role within the fed do you see this character filling, whether it be "Monster Heel" or "Hardcore Specialist" or "Pathetic Babyface."

What sets this character apart from others?
What makes your character unique?

Originality and diversity is key.


The more detailed your answers to these questions, the closer you are to building a well-defined character's past, present and future.
