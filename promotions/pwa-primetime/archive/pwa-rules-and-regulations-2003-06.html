PWA Rules and Regulations
2/16/03

1.a) Grading is going to be extremely tough and you are going to be graded by at least five of the best roleplayers to ever hit the internet wrestling circuit. Remeber we are here to make you the best efeder possible. Don't wuss out.

2.b) You are not to argue a grade that you have recieved. The graders are all experienced RPer's, and have been for some time. You are here, because you are not. They know their stuff, you don't. Don't argue unless you think you have a REALLY valid point!

2. Yes,. there is one card a week, no PPV, no titles and the results are simmed. This is an ewrestling school and not an efed. Once you get good enough you can do to PCW or PCW2, where there are titles, multiple cards and PPVs. Welcome to school.

3. Cards will be posted on Friday or Saturday afternoon. You will have one week to roleplay for your match. You do not roleplay, I tell the simulator to job you. End of story. You need to roleplay at least 3 times per card or you will be jobbed. If for some reason you can not rp three times during the week let me know and it better be a damn good excuse.

4. To be succesful in PWA you are going to have to follow the grading system to a T. You want an A, you do everything the grading system tells you to do and then some. I will not hesitate to give out F's. If I think your rp's suck, you will see an F next to your name in the grade book and you will get an email saying your rp's suck and how to improve them (well, maybe not that harsh!!!)

5. You may be beginners, but I am not going to tolerate mediocrity. I want roleplays to be at least 50 lines each. Now that is not hard, should take you a half an hour to do it. I can do a 250 line rp in at least an hour. You should be able to do a 50 liner in half. Failure to make a roleplay 50 lines will result in a fail for the rp. Yes I will count each and every line too.

6. You will only contact The Phantom Booker by email and OOC board. Do not ask for his AIM, ICQ or MSN. The answer will be no everytime. Until you graduate. If you wish to talk to your teacher on MSN, ICQ or AOL, talk to him and he'll tell you his responce.

7. With all that said. I also want you guys to have fun. I know that seems like a lot and I am going to grade tough, but you are also here to have fun. That is what ewrestling is about. Pro wrestling is fun, but you have to go through the hell of wrestling school first.
