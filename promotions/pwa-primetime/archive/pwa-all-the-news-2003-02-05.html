All the News!
2/05/03

Results are up in the Results Forum!

The new card is below and the new deadline will be Saturday, 2/8/03, 10pm EST. (You can always assume the deadline is Saturday at 10 unless I specifically make it later in a post.) Everyone needs to understand that you must do an RP every week, even if you're not booked! If you don't, you'll be fired, unless you have specifically spoken to me and I've given you permission not to post.

The roster has been updated as have the quotes and I added in-card grades to the roster page as well!

Eligible to do in cards this week are: Calvin "RX" Riley, Shawn Stewart, and Jaxon Pierce

Also, if anyone knows of a better free simulator than Zeus, let me know! Zeus's idea of a ladder match was two paragraphs of perfectly standard stuff, at the end of which a ladder magically appeared... and no one climbed it.
