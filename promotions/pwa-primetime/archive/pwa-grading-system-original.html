Efed Ewrestling Acadamy Grading System

The grading system will be used here to help advise you on what you need to improve about your roleplays. Roleplays are graded in sixe categories as defined below. Each category is worth 10 possible points.

Size: You should make sure the size of your RP isn't too small, and don't use different sizes to make it look bigger!!!! 20 possible points given.

Ambiance: What this is? It is the description of your RP's. Anything that's non talking, is Ambiance. To get a good mark here, be very descriptive. Too get a poor mark, don't be descriptive, it's that easy! 20 possible points given.

Originality: Mostly used to prevent you guys from using Stone Cold Steve Austin trash talking, or the Rock's signature "SHUT YOUR MOUTH"! If you use original phrases, you'll be fine. Also, an in ring promo is fine, but if you do it all the time, your mark in this category will go down, down, down! 20 possible points given.

Storyline/ Character Devolopment: Don't just RP for the sake of RPing! If you RP about a match reply, why don't you go to a cafe or something? If you are in a storyline, this mark is used to make sure you follow that storyline. I'm not to hard on marking with this one though, so don't worry!

Technical: If you spell well, use proper grammar and provide clean spacing, you'll get a 10 everytime. If you mispell words, use improper grammar and run your spacing together, your score will suffer. If you do, I'll give you a hint now, run your RP's through MS Word and spell check it! Note, if your RP isn't good on size, you can't get a good tech nicalmark either! *Note: I am a stickler for this, nothing makes me more angry than horrible grammar and spelling and I will severely dock you for this.*ible points are given.

Every RP is out of 100, and that number is turned into a letter grade. Here is how you get the following letter grades:

90-100=A

87-89=B+

83-86=B

80-82=B-

77-79=C+

73-76=C

70-72=C-

67-69=D+

63-66=D

60-62=D-

59 and below=F

It's common to get a few F's at first. If you listen to the advice your given and keep working, you'll be getting B's before you know it.

All RP's above this post will be automatically graded. All RP's below this won't be, unless you request they be, (A few of you should actually do this, some of them are pretty good!) Feel free to ask any questions if they arise!
