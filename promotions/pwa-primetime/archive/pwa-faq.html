Frequently Asked Questions

Below is a list of frequently asked questions concerning Primetime Championship Wrestling. We advise you also check our wrestling terms and HTML Help links for additional information. These questions are in no particular order.

Q: I applied to PCW, but received an acceptance letter to another fed. Does this mean I was not accepted to PCW?

A: Yes and no. We have working relationships with other feds. Good work in one of these other feds could certainly lead to a roster spot in PCW. If we refer you to another fed, whether it be the Primetime Wrestling Academy or New True Internet Wrestling, we also run these organizations.

Q: Do in card interviews count towards my RP total?

A: No, but they will help you if a match is close or for pushing an angle.

Q: Do you write or sim your matches?

A: We hand write PCW cards, but simulate PWA cards.

Q: How many wrestlers may I contol at once?

A: A maximum of two, and they should be a tag team. Each member of the team may have a singles career, but the duo should be a tag team first and foremost. Should the team split up, one member of the team should be dropped from the roster.

Q: May I use other wrestlers or their property in my roleplays?

A: No, unless you have permission from the wrestler or the PCW President.

Q: May I use PWA Front office personalities in my roleplays?

A: No, unless you have permission. You MAY use announcers, referees or PCW reporters in your roleplays. Injuring PCW personnel in roleplays may (and will) have serious reprisals.

Q: I want to join the PWA Staff. What do I need to do?

A: Generally, we only promote staff from within the ranks of our loyal player base. If you have not been involved in PTC long, it is unlikely you will be considered for a staff position.

Q: I work in management for another efed. How did you guys get so high in all the top 100 and 1000 efed polls? Can you help me to do the same?

A: We have achieve our high rankings through hard work, popularity and some script work. We will be happy to advise any fed on how to start up and rise in the rankings, but we will not share our technical expertise unless your fed has some resouces/services to trade back in return.

Q: I have been accepted to PWA, but don't have a login name and password to your RP board. Where can I get this?

A: Go to the roleplay board and register.

Q: I lost my login name/password for the RP or OOC board. Can you send me a new one?

A: Drop us a line at [detail withheld] and we'll forward the message to our web monkey.

Q: All your message boards are from the same host. Do I have to reregister on every board?

A: No.

Q: I am in another fed. May I post an announcement on PWA's boards promoting the other fed or looking for members?

A: You may not. If you are are a participant in PCW, you will be fired for such an offense, and yes, we have ways of knowing who you are, even if you post under another name. If you come from another federation and spam our boards, we will retaliate in kind.

Q: I lost my match. Who can I complain to?

A: You can complain to the mirror or talk to the hand. Take your pick.

Q: I run a federation and want to affiliate my fed with PWA. How do I go about doing this?

A: Drop us an email at [detail withheld] and we will be happy to discuss the details.

Q: I used to be in PCW, now I want to return. Am I automatically accepted?

A: No, you are not. You must send in another application and we will drop you an email.
