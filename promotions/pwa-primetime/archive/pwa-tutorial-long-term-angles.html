Long Term Angles
by Tempest

Angles that last for months or even years can provide a simmering backdrop and then an exciting climax to events in your fed. Bad blood or longstanding friendships often serve as the basic truth about a character from which all other things spring. It should be noted that all angles are not necessarily feuds, but can add a great deal to a fed. The love story between Ivan Stanislav and Tempest is now more than one year old and still remains active and interesting, and the feud between Tempest and Alan Kriegman also shows no signs of abating even though they aren't fighting over anything at the moment. Such long term angles work best by going through hot and cold periods which allow the characters to clash when circumstances change and then to carry out other angles away from each other while the heat from their angle simmers.

A few things that are necessary for successful long-term angles, either positive (alliance, friendship, love story) or negative (feud, power struggle, homicidal obsession) are:

1) Handlers who get along and work well together. If you've got a best pal who you've never been at odds with, consider a long-term alliance or feud. The best feuds I've seen are not between people who don't like each other OOC, but between people who get along famously.

2) Stability. Know what's going on with your angle partner OOC. If they're about to ship out with the Navy or go to school in Quantum Physics, you'll have problems doing anything long-term with them. On the other hand a long-term angle can be handy when absences arise. If you need a month off you can always be injured by your partner and then have an automatic feud when you return.

3) Fundamental differences in the characters. Even in a positive angle, there must be conflict. That's what makes it a story worth following. Ivan and Tempest will always be different. She will never understand hard line Communism and he will never understand why she goes dancing whenever there's trouble. Alan Kriegman will always be fundamentally evil and Tempest will always be fundamentally good. Those differences can be exploited over and over again within different sets of circumstances.

4) Dedication. Sometimes you will have to make sacrifices in an immediate angle, say a title related one, for the sake of your long-term angle. You have to be willing to see rewards in terms of storytelling and also in terms of a more distant future.

5) Flexibility. You must be willing to have your character's life changed by the actions of another. The depth at which your characters effect each is what gives a long-term angle its strength. You can't be totally attached to a single outcome or attitude for your character. A hard and unfeeling character must go ahead and feel to make this work or a powerful and controlling character must lose some of that control.

With these things in mind, you too can create an angle that will keep people talking for months, maybe even years and will remain one of your most fun experiences.
