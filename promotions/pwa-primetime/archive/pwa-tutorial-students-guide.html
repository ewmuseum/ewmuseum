Students Guide to PWA for new students
2/17/03

Seeing you are new here, you should be told that there are a few basic tips that will help you through PWA. Remember, this is a school, not a fed. You are here to role-play, work on your characters and brush off the clichés and problems that you may have suffered in other feds. The end result is you will be a more refined role-player and have a better attitude. These are not rules, but guidelines that will ensure your transition through PWA is as smooth as can be.

1 - EVERYONE here has been doing efedding for a while. Do NOT question a grader, headmaster or anyone else's judgment. Rebellious behavior will see you fired, banned or jobbed to PWA's resident punishment, the rabid wallabies. You don't want ANY of those, so treat everyone with respect - that goes for your fellow schoolmates as well.

2 - Your RP will be graded when a member of WrestlingTattler has time and patience. Asking people to read, grade or hinting at those will slow down your grading process and may result in punishment. Once your RP has been graded, follow the advice passed on to you. Do not IM anyone who seems responsible, including other graders, headmasters, or fed heads, seeking additional advice. These people have a responsibility to their feds, not you personally and you are hurting your chances at future advancement by bugging them. You don't stand much of a chance getting into a Fed if you're on the fed heads Block List. Also, warning them is extremely bad. Lying about warning them is even worse. Please, don't make an ass of yourself because you are replaceable. And don't rat the graders out for not doing yours even though it was first. It's in poor taste and I already know.

3 - Visit the site - http://academy.wrestleonline.org - for information on your classes, grades and how the graders mark your RPs. Grading structure is the uniform standard that all graders must follow. Look to see what we want to get a better understanding and thus higher grades.

4 - Promotion occurs when the higher powers think you are ready. Don't expect to get into a WT fed right away. One thing we're looking for here is consistency and commitment. Don't think you're the greatest thing we've ever seen and think that the PWA is some little stepping stone. It's a school, yes, but unlike all those detentions that no one really cares about after you're out, it can have a large impact, positive or negative, on your WT future.

5 - If your RP is going to be you either: Talking trash about your opponent, mentioning your old fed's achievements, acting invincible in combat, doing nothing but getting laid or a "he's coming" video style segment, expect a low mark now. PWA has no TV deal, you are not famous and you'll receive no hype. You're not big, so kill your ego before it kills you. There are a million e-wrestlers who are rich, can knock out men in one hit and women find irresistible. We don't want any of these dime-a-dozen show-offs. Also, calling your opponent's "Jobbers" or saying you will take over or rule PWA will see you being looked upon harshly. Also, maybe a personal bias of mine, don't tell me your character is immortal or an ancient vampire or demon. You're just not. Maybe you play one on TV, but that's not the same thing. Think about subtle horror films that never really sit there and tell you all this, they just let you learn it over time.

7 - If you receive a failing grade, that does NOT mean you are fired. It means that in one of our feds, your RP would probably cause you to lose the match, or be considered well under our standards. The bottom line is that you should follow the grader's advice and do better next time. Quitters are looked upon as lazy people who couldn't take the pressure. People who fail but slowly climb up in their grades are seen as those who were willing to make the effort and will probably be rewarded.

8 - Often, a gimmick will help you to RP. A gimmick is NOT theme music, a finisher name or a nickname. It's your character's day-to-day life. How he talks, what he does, a secondary job, his hobbies, how he behaves in certain situations. A gimmick is not "He destroys people who get in his way" or "He's a badass". That's indy fed copycat stuff which will not be tolerated here. Also, make it original. Anything the WWE have done recently will be frowned upon. Acting like Jericho (Cocky, trash talking heel), Austin (alcoholic badass with no respect for authority) or any other blatant rip-offs will get several yawns in your direction. AVOID.

9 - Look at all the WT feds. Looking at OSW or PCW from afar is what a lot of PWAers do but looking around the other feds will help you with ideas for gimmicks, RPs and how the place works. While your stay in PWA will usually only be a month or two, try to make an impact. Become "the guy famous for..." if you can. Get noticed for the right reasons and your future here will be rewarding.

10 - And lastly, have fun. We can tell if you're really into what you're doing and the excitement is often contagious. Don't do the minimum amount of work, hoping to get the maximum gain from it.

So to sum up - leave your ego and former feds at the door, they make no difference here, where you are an unknown guy having his first big-time shot. Show respect to people and don't act like you're above anyone. Follow advice and don't argue with your grades. Asking or hinting at grading or promotion will get you in trouble. Keep trying, you're adjusting to how the big boys and girls play. Work hard and you'll be one of them. Look around for help but don't rip anything off - originality is the key here. And finally, get into a gimmick you enjoy doing! Good luck with your future WT careers.
