In-Card School Rules
2/17/03

Rules

Segments in the realm of Wrestle Online are basically smaller versions of the RPs that you will be writing. The difference lies in the fact that an in-card will generally be involving other people, and further angles on your federation's weekly shows. Here in PWA, we will use the same basic guidelines that the big feds have set out for their in-cards. They are as follows, with one addition:

1. Only PWA students who receive an RP grade of 80 or higher will be eligible to post an in-card to the forum the next week. The chance to post there is not cumulative, nor deferrable. Eligible students will be listed on the main news in an announcement with the card. Each eligible student may post only one in card per week, due to limited graders, although it may be in segments with the breaks marked.

2. Every in-card that you write must be OKed by everyone involved. This means that you will NOT use another handler's character without express consent of that handler. Upon giving you that permission, he is trusting creative control of his character to you. To abuse this right, and have your character participate in any action that is outside the bounds of the agreement made between you and the other character's handler, is to give the utmost disrespect to that handler. While each fed handles things their own way, for the purpose of learning in this establishment, your segment will receive a grade of ZERO if we find out that you committed this act.

3. Every in-card will be sent to the head of your federation as an attachment. The type of file is specified by the respective federations, but for the purposes of this tutorial you will simply post it to the In-Card forum. It will be graded by Magnum Wolf, The End, Tempest or any graders who are later added. Segments must receive a 25 or higher grade to be included in the weekly Rave results.

4. Creative control ultimately remains in the hands of the person editing the card. If he/she feels that what you have written is poorly written, has things that won't work in the angle, or has inappropriate content in it, he/she is able to edit the segment in any way they wish. The person may also decide that the segment is un-salvageable, and merely scrap it altogether. In this context, that means Tempest will work with any in-card meeting the minimum grade, but may re-work it. If this happens, take a good look at the editing, since that in itself is an indication of potential improvements.
