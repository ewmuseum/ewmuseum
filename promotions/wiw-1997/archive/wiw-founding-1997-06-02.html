WIW — Founding

WIW Great Action...We Need Great Roleplayers

2 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
World Internet Wrestling PLEASE JOIN. GIVE IT A CHANCE Welcome everyone . As you know, you have been chosen as one of the top roleplayers in the roleplay world. And many people have told me that I am capable of running a great E-fed. I have ran a few but they have fallen apart for various reasons. The main reason is that people wont stay and bare with me as I try to get things running. If you are like this then do us both a favor and delete this message and pretend that you never looked at this. If you arent like this and you are loyal keep reading. ROLEPLAY/FLASH The only way this league will stand is if you roleplay. You must roleplay at least 2 to 3 times per week. And no one paragraph roleplays. I want at least 15 line roleplays CARDS There will be one card per week. It will be a completely comentated program. It is on Wednesday's. It will be Wednesday Night Warfare. Now because of summer restrcitions where im gone on the weekends Wednesday is the only time id be able to do a full card . Now once we gert started I am gonna ask everyone in the fed if you want a second card which would only be a house show which would not be play by play. There will also be one ppv event per month. TITLES Well, this is the best part of the feds. The TITLES. Here they are WIW World Heavyweight Championship Most pretigious title in WIW. This title MUST be defended at least once every three weeks. Like I said this is for the best WIW International Championship 2nd most prestigous title. It is a stepping stone to the World Title. It will be defended every two weights week WIW Superpro Tittle This title is very prestigous. This title will be defended every week. This title is for the men that are willing to put it all on the line for a title WIW World Television Title This is basiclly a working man's title. It will be defended almost every card and it will prove to other people that you are worthy of a World Title WIW World Tag Team Title This is the only tag team title as of now. It will be defended every week RANKINGS/MATCHES Well everyone needs to know about how matches go. Well its decided upon a umber of things . First how much and good you roleplay. Next it matters where you are ranked. And finally it just comes down to whos hot and whos not. Now the ranking system is decided upon three things. First , where i think you belong. Second, have you wrestled lately, have you won or loss, has it been a big win. The thing that determines a lot plus chances in winning matches are points. You will get 2 points for a pinfall or submission. You will get one point by winning by countout or disqualification. And if you lose by pin or submission you lose two points and by dq or countout , you lose one point. Now you might ask what if a match comes to draw. Well draw matches award no points or deduct no points from anyone. Except if you are a champion. Some people try to hold onto titles by putting a time linit stipulation or whatever in it. Well to make sure champs when fair and not dominait by holding onto a title by coutout this is what ill do. If a champion comes to a time limit or draw with another person. The other person recieves or deducts nothing. But the champion loses a point. This is just so that noone can become dominate. Beause everyone knows its no fun when its impossible to when a title. RULES Dont you hate these? I know i do but the are nessacary. Ok you must roleplay at least twice per week. No terrible cussing in it. Try to restrain yourself from usinf g it but if you really gete that urge i guess its ok. Dont complain to me if you lose. Just roleplay and try again. Also when Im onlin , more than likely im busy with the fed or something else so if youre on AOL dont im me , email me and in turn i will do the same. Also , I will have al lwreslters and email adreses on the web page when it is up. I do that so u can emai lthem and not bug them either. Most people like that. And most important have fun. Thats what its all about APPLICATION Well here is the application. You are aloud one tag team AND one singles. FAKE WRESTLERS ONLY. Singles Wrestler's name: Height: Weight: From: Face/Heel/Nuetral: Manager: Entrance Music: Finishing move: Setup move to finishing move: gimmick: (what does he wear & are there any special effect while coming to the ring) Tag Teams Team Name: Individual name: Individual name: Height: Height: Weight: Weight: From: From: Manager: Face/Heel/Nuetral: Finisher: Setup to finisher: Entrance Music: Gimic: Well that about does it. Help mak WIW the best fed going. Get in the apps
