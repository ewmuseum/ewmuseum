Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A recruitment drive introduced a federation calling itself the Hardcore Wrestling Alliance, hosted on its own earthlink.net homepage. Its own posts give two founding dates eight days apart — the earliest surviving post claims the fed "just opened" on 2 March 2000, while a form letter repeated across the following week instead dates the launch to 18 March 2000; no further document resolves the discrepancy.

The federation billed itself for custom wrestlers only, running one weekly card and a monthly pay-per-view, with a roleplay requirement and a points system its own recruitment copy calls unique among e-feds. No roster, card, or further update survives beyond the recruitment run itself.

The federation shares its acronym and its full name with several other federations recovered from the same newsgroup and year alone, none sharing a host, an operator, or a roster with it; no document links it to any of them.
