Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Action Extreme Wrestling billed itself, on its own front page, as "the newest and best fed" and named its engine outright — Zeus, a wrestling simulator its own operator was still learning, after "the last fed I had got screwed up because of people not knowing what to do." Its roster mixed a handful of its own creations (Slayer, Jack Hammer, Bossman, Doomsday, Splash, The Perfectionist, Whoopass WildCrow, Tortured Man, Rattlesnake) with real WWF names booked straight off television — Stone Cold Steve Austin, Undertaker, Kane, Bill Goldberg, X-Pac, Buff Bagwell, and "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash all wrestled under their own names here, split across two stables, the EXtreme Icons and the nWo.

Only one full show survives: Thursday Thrashin' of 30 April 1998, five matches deep and closing on a twelve-man Royal Rumble that Jack Hammer won in just over an hour and forty-eight minutes. A Barbwire Match, a tag bout that put the AEW World Tag Team Titles on Undertaker and Kane, and a title snapshot naming a champion for six of the fed's seven belts — the AEW Top Stable Title sat vacant, contested for in both of the fed's own announced-but-unresulted next cards. Nothing survives to say how the show's own champions actually won their belts, or how long AEW ran before its guestbook went quiet for good. The results script's own commentary team billed a rotating cast of real broadcast personalities under their own or near-verbatim names — Vince MacMahon, Sting, Howard Finkel, Nick Patrick — alongside the fed's own on-air "Prez" persona, Prez Ryan.

The fed's own guestbook, a roleplay board that kept taking promos two years after the last result was ever posted, preserves matches and rivalries the results archive alone would have lost: a Hardcore Title feud between Bossman and The Demolition Man, an Internet Title callout war between Bossman and "Big Sexy" Kevin Nash, and one guestbook-only challenger, The Finisher, who called out The Perfectionist for the European Title four times over without ever getting an answer. Four more guestbook signers — The ReVereN, U.K Agent, Scorpious, and The Finisher — never made the roster page at all.
