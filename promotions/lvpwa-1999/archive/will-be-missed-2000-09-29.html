LVPWA Will Be Missed (Valtharius vs. Nick Demola)

29 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is an excerpt of the last match in a best 2 out of 3 between Valtharius and Nick Demola.... The ending couldn't have gone better... =) LVPWA WILL BE MISSED! =-=-=-=-=-=-= SM: KICKOUT! And what a kickout! Demola had to have been launched a good two to three feet straight into the air. [Demola falls to the mat, landing face first as Valtharius tries to get back up to his feet. He finally does as the chain begins to unwrap a bit and he gets completely free. Valtharius pulls Demola up to his feet and hurls him over the top rope and hangs him there by his neck. Demola kicking his feet wildly as he is hung by the collar around his neck. Demola then reaches up and unclasps the collar and falls hard to the arena floor as he gasps for air. Valtharius going out of the ring now as well as Demola makes his way around and grabs the vendor he knocked over the rail earlier and shoves him into the ring. The vendor's food spills out all over the ring and Valtharius sees this and immediately turns his attention to it and gets down on the mat and begins to eat..] SM: What in the world is he doing.. KL: Having a light snack.. [Valtharius stuffing his face as Demola slides back into the ring and lifts one of Valtharius' tree trunks of legs up and puts on a very weak looking ankle lock submission. The ref goes over and asks Valtharius is he wants to give it up..] VtM:"YEP! ME VALTHARIUS GIVE UP! NOW LEAVE ZEBRA!!! VALTHARIUS WANT TO EAT NOW!!!" [DING,DING,DING!!] SM: You have got to be kidding me... RICK MILTON - Here is your winner as a result of a Submission, Nick DEMOLA!! [Demola on his feet like he just won the world title. Valtharius still eating with a big smile on his face as Karachel looks in the ring in disbelief. Demola makes his way out and up the aisle, gloating with every step he takes. The camera moves back onto the ring as Valtharius simply keeps eating and we fade to a promo..] [Camera fade to black] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Another stimulating thought from the sick and rotting mind of.....
