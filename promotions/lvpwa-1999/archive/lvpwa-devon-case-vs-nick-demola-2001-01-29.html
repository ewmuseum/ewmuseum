Challenge Grudge Match: Devon Case vs. Nick Demola

posted 29 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the reopening of the LVPWA, I just wanted to put up a reminder of how good this fed was and will be again. Devon Case vs. Nick Demola. Over a half hour of a wrestling style that the two wrestlers define. In turn, I think Mike will agree with me, this match helped define these two wrestlers. It's a hybrid of "old school Texas" and "new style Extreme". It also shows that it's not always bad when a handler writes his own match. At least when the handler is mature enough to put over the other man. This match took place just as Case was rising up to the superstardom he has now. Demola was fresh off of jacking Brody Thunder earlier on in the night (boy I'd love to see the blowoff for that feud). So check it out. And write to [detail withheld] to get in on the second coming of LVPWA.
