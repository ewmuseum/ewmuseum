[Fades out..] [The camera picks up to the back area of the Pontiac Silverdome. The camera picks up on the various Detroit Lions paraphernalia, adorning the walls, showing the blue and silver. Slowly, a car begins to pull up, seemingly _to_ the locker room area.
The roar of the engine is heard, and as it pulls in, you stare at the awesome sight of such a vehicle. Yes, it’s overly masculine, overly powerful, overly showy, and flashy, but would “he” have it any other way?
Black, black as the night, black as the souls of the greedy, washed up legends of this sport, the truck seems to be consumed with black. The tint, the overall truck color, the brush guard, the bed liner.
Slowly, the passenger door opens of the 2001 Dodge Ram, modified with a 8” lift. He steps down, out of the massive truck. The sound is released, ZZ
Tops’ “La Grange” is blaring out of the truck. The man is hidden behind the door, and the tint, but slowly he gather his items, and closes the door. He walks away from the truck, carrying a small bag, nothing major, just a regular bag. His cold blue eyes are fixated on the locker room, his face is full of focuses, nil of emotion. He seems to ignore the throng of people in the backstage area, he seems to be more then intense, his face is cold, his eyes are colder.
He wears a pair of simple faded blue jeans, frequently scene week in, and week out. They seem to never be covered in enough blood, never to get too dirty. He wears them with pride, much like a soldier wears a battle scare, or a metal of honor. For each blood stain, each drop of another’s blood, or of his own signifies another war he has survived. His black shirt, tightly fitting his developed chest seems to be a badge, a badge of hate, of anger.
The words come off the shirt, and dart at you, with their pristine white color… “Bitch Maker” The man slowly walks into the locker room area, opening the door, and slowly flipping his hair to one side. His golden hair, slowly getting longer, and longer. He brushes it off his face, and as he glares back at the truck, at the people looking, staring at him, his statuesque chin peers out. Facial scars are beginning to form, taking away the once model like charm he once had. They are now necessities of this business, of the wars he has fought, of the wars he must _still_ fight. Tonight, he may fight the hardest war he may ever enter into, yet, he seems at peace with his odds, with himself. The door opens, and he disappears into the locker room.]

Gentlemen…Chris Courtade has arrived, and he is ready for war.
