H.Closing ============================================================= A.Introduction 1.What is the LVPWA? LVPWA stands for Las Vegas Pro-Wrestling Association, opened on September 28, 1999. The LVPWA isn't a regional fed despite it's name. It's based out of Las Vegas,
Nevada and many shows will take place in the city of Las Vegas and state of
Nevada but will also go out to places all over the West coast. For the norm but it will also go out all over the US and Canada. It will even occasionally venture into other countries all over the world from Europe to Japan.
The LVPWA will house many different types of matches depending on the occasion. For the norm it will be the usual, standard match with the regular rules. D's and Countouts are in effect but it will be based on the referees judgment. If
Wrestler A pulls out a weapon, or brings a chair into the ring and blatantly hits Wrestler B with it he'll most likely be disqualified. If the match spills out to the floor for the most part chairs and other objects can be used, the ref may warn about it but unless it gets out of hand he won't call for a DQ. If another wrestler not involved in the match that will also cause a DQ.
As for countouts, For the most he'll only count if one of or both are down on the floor not showing much movement. If the two are battling on the floor the ref will usual warn them to get in the ring but won't count them out. If after several warnings and if the brawl begins to move down the aisle or into he crowd he'll count them out.
But the most important thing of all, the LVPWA is here for fun. To create characters and run angles with other handlers. If you're hung up on winning and gonna throw a fit if you lose than don't bother applying. I know I'd rather watch interesting angles and storylines than boring, heatless matches.
2.Who runs things?<Fictional> The LVPWA was founded by Sebastian T. Myerson in September of 1999. He's in his early to mid Twenties and has been around wrestling most of his life. He usually dresses simple, in a blue blazer or sportscoat, and a pair of denim jeans or black dress pants. He has short, dark blonde hair. He's around 6'2
1/2" and weighs around 230.
Sebastian booked and wrestled for an Independent Promotion in California where he learned the ins and outs of the business before he and the Promoter had creative differences leading to Sebastian's departure. Afterwards he went to
Nevada and got his promoters license. He went to Las Vegas and decided to open up his promotion in the City of Sin.
3.Who runs things?<Real> Me, Sebastian Myerson. President, Head Booker, Match Writer, and a damn handsome man. I'm not looking for a vice president but I could use all the help I can get when it comes to writing matches. It's not required of you to write matches for the LVPWA but I definitely will appreciate it if you can, even if it's every other card, or even just for PPVs or other special cards.
B.What are the Titles? 1.LVPWA World Championship The granddaddy of 'em all. The LVPWA World Championship. Whoever holds it is the top dog in the game, but will also be the one wearing the bullseye on his chest. Everyone will be trying to be the one who dethrones you. The belt must, and I mean MUST be defended once every month. Greatly encouraged to defend it more often, but it has to be defended at least once a month or it will be stripped away.
2.LVPWA North American Championship The number 2 belt in the LVPWA. If you hold this you're the top contender to the World Title but it may be awhile before you get the shot at the top belt as you will be busy defending this title. The NA title must also be defended at least once every 30 days or it will be declared vacant. Like the World title it is encouraged to defend it more often.
3.Las Vegas Championship This is pretty much the Television title of the LVPWA. It must be defended every week on Heavy Metal Monday and at the PPVs. This is probably the hardest belt to hold onto but depending on how well you do as the Las Vegas Champion you can rise to superstardom.
4.LVPWA Featherweight Championship For wrestler 235 and under. This belt is also under the 30 day defense rule and can be a major stepping stone.
5.LVPWA World Tag Team Championship This is the top tag team title. Tag Team wrestling has had a lot of ups and downs over the years but I've always liked tag divisions and if harnessed right great angles and storylines can come out of it. 30 day rules applies here.
6.LVPWA Women's Championship<possibly> I've seen a lot of feds with women's divisions where only a few of the handlers participated, but women's wrestling seems to be coming back strong lately so if
I can get enough good women for this will be the top gold for the women's division, 30 day rule applies here as well.
C.What are the Cards? 1.LVPWA Presents - Heavy Metal Monday The main show of the LVPWA. Hosted by LVPWA President Sebastian Myerson, and
Kevin Lockland. This is where angles are born, feuds are ignited and heat is built up for the PPVs. The matches are in full written form. Each show will have between 6 to 10 matches.
2.Who, Then, Now? This is the weekly flash show. Hosted by Kevin Lockland, and Janet Moxen. The show will also have dark matches for debuts and low-carders and an occasional full match for mid-card wrestlers. You'll be on here a few times before you get to Heavy Metal Monday.
3.PPV's/Supercards The LVPWA will have 5 PPVs a year. The way it looks right now it will start with "LVPWA:The Birth of a Wrestling Revolution" at the moment I'm shooting for mid-late October, Countdown to Judgment Day in December, Sin City Showdown I in
February, Canadian Conquest in May, and Sex, Drugs & Rock 'N Roll in August.
Names for future PPVs are always welcome. 4.Update Nothing major here. Just the basic information. Rankings, champions, injuries, upcoming cards. Everything to keep you updated on the things going on.
5.Schedule Friday - Flashes and strats are due. Saturday or Sunday - Who, Then, Now? Comes out. Monday - Heavy Metal Monday comes out. D.LVPWA Staff Members 1.Commentators a.Sebastian Myerson - The man who runs the show also likes to call the matches. He doesn't like to sit back in an office watching on a monitor he likes to sit right up on the front lines and give his take on things. He tries to stay impartial but it doesn't always happen..especially with his
co-commentator running his mouth. b.Kevin Lockland - The basic smart mouth, egotistical jackass that didn't have the size nor the talent to make it in the ring so he sits in the booth and runs his mouth. Myerson found him in the California Promotion trying to wrestle and brought him to the LVPWA as his head heel commentator. He likes to insult the fans and wrestlers<except for the few he likes> as well as annoy Myerson.
He's around 6'0"" and weighs around 205 pounds. He has blond hair and blue eyes. He's a boyishly cute kind of guy, but he usually ruins that with his big mouth.
c.Janet Moxen - Janet is a very attractive young woman and isn't afraid to show it off. She likes to cheer for the good looking wrestlers but isn't completely oblivious to the wrestling world. She has long auburn hair and a curvaceous body.
2.Interviewers a.Janet Moxen - As well as co-hosting Who, Then, Now? she does backstage and in-ring interviews on Heavy Metal Monday. She likes the job 'cause it gives her an excuse to go into the mens locker rooms.
b.Josh Davis - Nothing special. Just a guy to hold the mic and ask wrestlers questions. Not a real big buy so he tries to stay in-line with the wrestlers.
He does some interviews but mostly covers things as they go down during the shows, 3.Referees a.Dan Bernie - The LVPWA head ref. He's been around the wrestling world for a long time and knows the ins and outs in the ring. Another guy Myerson took with him when he left the cali based promotion. Dan is in his mid-thirties and is in decent shape, he's around 5'10" and weighs around 190. He's not going to be beating up the wrestlers but he's not afraid to stand his ground.
b.Roy Ash - Hired for his size at around 6'1" and 215. He has learned the rope as a ref under Bernie and has become quite good at it. He's the type that will let it all hang out during the match, but if you go over the line, or put your hands on him he's not afraid to call for the bell or just put you on the mat.
c.Jim Adams - A veteran ref. He's in his early 40's. He's not used that often, and has a lot of old school values, so don't be surprised if he calls for the DQ more often than others.
4.Ring Announcers a.Rick Milton - The ring announcer for Heavy Metal Monday and PPVs. He knows what he's doing and does it well. He's in his mid thirties, and is a relatively good looking guy with short dark brown hair, 5'10", 175.
5.The Bosses a.Sebastian Myerson - EMWC President and CEO. b.Tom Murphy - LVPWA VP. Handles the marketing,and recruitment of the
LVPWA. He'll also take over other duties if Myerson is busy.
E.What you must do. 1.Roleplays The thing to do, if ya don't Roleplay you don't win, simple enough. If you do not Roleplay, you will be warned, jobbed and then cut from the roster.
-VULAGARITY:I can let the occasional one slip. "Ass","asshole", and "bitch" can be tolerated on occasion but anything bigger than that must be bleeped. -No Attacking other wrestlers/managers/announcers or anyone else without getting permission. -Don't break character. This may be wrestling but it's Fantasy wrestling which means no mentioning any real wrestlers. Also don't mention other Fantasy federations unless it pertains to something, like if you're facing someone in the LVPWA that you faced in another fed then that's fine.
-AMOUNT - One Roleplay a week is required if you have a match, you can do more but it's not required. If you don't have a match I recommend you keep to one RP on those weeks. In-Rings and locker room interviews can also be done. Let me know ahead of time if you are going to do this as I don't want to have to many.
2.Strats Strats are encouraged as they help the writers of the match get a good feel of the character and what you want him to do in the match. They don't hold as much weight in booking as flashes and angles but they can be good in close matches.
3.Angles This is what makes things go round. An interesting storyline helps build up much needed heat in feuds and makes people want to read the matches. Get involved with other handlers, come up with ideas and go with them. That's what makes things fun.
F.How things are booked/decided The LVPWA is a Booked fed outcomes of matches aren't based on roleplays alone, roleplays/strats/angles/heat all come into play. Your character isn't going to go undefeated, you're going to have to take loses, pay your dues so to speak.
If you're not willing to take a lose to further an angle than you shouldn't be here. Newcomers may find themselves doing the job to the bigger names early in there stay in the LVPWA, but that doesn't mean you can't go onto beat him later down the road. Everyone has to pay there dues, it doesn't matter how big a name you were somewhere else, when you first come to the LVPWA you have to take your bumps like everyone else. It's patience and hardwork that will bring you to the top.
