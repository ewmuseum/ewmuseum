MOTSC Stand's For Master Of The Squared Circle. This will be "eWrestling's" huge sixty-four man tournament, to see who really is the "top dog." To Tell you now, this Tournament will be run as if it were a NET federation. Everything will be done in NET style. Simply to help me, and you out. MOTSC will consist of five round's, plus the final match. There are four brackets, North, South, East, and West. Each Bracket will have different dates, and places for each round. Wrestler's will be seeded on my own perception. It's up to me, It won't have anything do to with how good you are etc. The only thing you have control of is what bracket your placed in. Basically first come, first serve.
b.) Who Run's MOTSC? Heh, [name withheld]. That's Me. I'm very experienced in this whole thing, I know what I'm doing. That's basically all I will say.
Just trust me on this one, you won't be upset with the outcome. c.) A Bit More Info. Well, once you do the app, you'll get more information on this whole thing. This is just a start.
CARDS/ROUNDS a.) Explanation Of Round One Round One Will Have 32 Matches In It. All 64 Wrestlers will be in action on 4 different nights. Round one will be written in house show report. In my opinion round one is most important, cause you only have a one Flash Limit. You can only flash one time to win your match. No In Ring's. No Strat's. Round One will be held in San Diego Ca, and Portland, OR.
b.) Explanation Of Round Two Round Two will have 16 Matches, 32 Wrestlers. This will be written in show format, but still small matches. This One Will Have a Two Flash Limit. No In
Rings, No Strategies. Round Two Will Be Held In Buffalo, NY; and Denver, Co. c.) Explanation Of Round Three Round Three will have 8 Matches, 16 wrestlers. This card will be fully written. It will be in good detail. 3 Flash Limit. No In Rings. But I will allow strat's. This round will be held in Boston, Mass, and Phoenix, AZ.
d.) Explanation Of Round Four Will Have 4 Matches. Eight Wrestlers. Will be written in full. I will take 3 Flashes, and Strats. Again, No In Rings. Their just not needed. This will be held In Orlando, FL, and Salt Lake City, UT.
e.) Explanation Of Semi Finals The Semi Finals consist of 2 matches, 4 wrestlers. This Will be written in full. 3 Flash Limit, and It's Held in Nashville, TN. No In Rings, Strats are okay.
f.) Explanation Of Finals The Finals Will be Held In Honolulu Hawaii. 1 Match, 2 Wrestlers. Written very well, 3 Flash Limit. Strat and In Ring's Okay. I know you want to know what the winner gets, but I'm not sure yet.
