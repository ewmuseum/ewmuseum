[PCWF] Special Thursday Match

The fans in attendance are holding several signs directed at tonight match. Some include: "Ka$h me tonight Stacy!", and "Legacy is a Legend!". The camera zooms in on the announcers table near ringside.] Tim Cross: Welcome everyone to tonight special match up between "Dashing" Derek Lankford and "Legacy" Johnny Skiles. To give you some insight into this match, my broadcast colleague Jerry "The Feen" Leelan! Feen: Everyone know that this match is taking place because of what happened last week on Black Sunday. John Crandell brutally attacked "Rudedog" Alex Reed in the Chicago arena parking lot. Reed was injured so bad that PCWF President Derek Owens had to remove Alex Reed from the World Title Tournament. TC: That's right, and someone had to face "Badboy" Randy Acorn on this upcoming Black Sunday, so this special bout between Johnny Skiles and Derek Lankford was scheduled. The winner will take Alex Reed's place this Sunday. JL: As you all saw on the various PCWF updates this week, "Rudedog" Alex Reed will be alright come the First Night PPV. As for John Crandell, he's suspended till First Night as well. Both will meet in a "Spin the Wheel, make a Deal" match. TC: The wheel will be spun by PCWF President Owens on the December 21st edition of Black Sunday, ten days before the Pay per View. But tonight's special presentation isn't about that. It's about two men, one will get a shot at becoming the PCWF World Champion, the other will have to wait for another chance. JL: Before we went on the air, "Dashing" Derek Lankford and his manager Stacy Ka$h came out and gave a special interview to these Kentucky Fried students. TC: Our cameras where on hand to tape it, so let's roll that clip for the fans at home! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (Afraid by Motley Crue cranks up on the PA. "Dashing" Derek Lankford walks down to the ring wearing khaki pants and a black polo shirt. He's a decently built man, but not buff, with short brown hair. Stacy Ka$h is a gorgeous 20something blonde with legs that won't quit. She walks to the ring with him wearing a black sequined mini dress. After they get in the ring, he takes the mic) Lankford: So... this is where the real stars of wrestling are. I would've thought the arena would be bigger or something. For all of you morons out there who don't know any better... you're looking at a legend in the making. You're looking at "Dashing" Derek Lankford.... AAPW Light-Heavyweight Champion... IFWF International Tag Team Champion... the "Real" NCW World Internet Champion... shall I continue? (the crowd boos as some of the fans start to litter the ring with trash) Lankford: Now... I'm here for a reason. You see... my entire career all I've heard about was these so-called stars like Trey Porter... or The Heartbreak Kid... or what is he calling himself these days? Ka$h: "The Idolizer" Trace Michaels... (most of the crowd pops one way or the other at his mention) Lankford: Whatever... he's just what I like to call another bargain basement Shawn Michaels ripoff. Anyway... all I hear is how great that this guy is, so I figure... who better to measure myself against than him. Well... I got a phone call from a good behind the scenes buddy of mine... The Badboy and he told me that the PCWF was going to merge with TAEWF... would I be interested in joining the federation? I found out about what's his name again? Ka$h: The Idolizer... Lankford: Yeah... I found out about him coming into this merger, and I thought... hot damn, he's my opportunity to meet a legend... an opportunity to see what he's made of... an opportunity to see if what they say is true. You know what Stace? Ka$h: What's that? Lankford: It seems that Mr. Rotating Name isn't the only piece of talent that I've heard about coming here. It seems that there's another guy who is supposed to be here... someone who I can at least say I have a little respect for because he kept his name the same the last 3 years, and that's Zack Malone. (some of the crowd pops at the mention of the superstar) Lankford: The only NFW World Heavyweight Champion... the man who took Brian Knight to the limit... the legendary Body Shop man himself. Well... I was at the mall doing my Christmas shopping and you'll never guess what I found... (Stacy opens her hand back and hands to action figures to Lankford) Lankford: Looky here... an old Trey Porter and a Zack Malone FoPEW action figure! Of course, the packaging was dusty because they haven't wrestled in that fed for a year, but hey... they were in the bargain bin and I couldn't resist. You know what I think of these two? (Lankford throws the action figures on the ground and starts stomping on them) Lankford: That's exactly what I think of those two legends!! You see... right now the PCWF is buzzing... why? Because The Dashing One has arrived and where he arrives... people take notice. PCWF... I could give a rat's ass about what talent you acquire with this merger, because right here... right now, I'm declaring the PCWF "Dashing" Derek Lankford's turf!! Not Zack Malone's.... not what's his name again? Ka$h: The... Lankford: nevermind... those two has beens are ancient history. You're looking at your future PCWF and that future is "Dashing" Derek Lankford. (Afraid by Motley Crue cranks up as Lankford & Ka$h leave the ring. The scene cuts back to Tim and Jerry at the broadcasting table at ringside.) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= JL: Well before he thinks about going after Trace Michaels and possibly Zack Malone, he's got to take care of "Legacy" Johnny Sklies tonight. And Skiles better watch out for Lankford and possibly some other people. TC: I take it your refering to Wacko Feen? Skiles and Wacko have been at a stand off since the battle Royal for the PCWF lightweight title two weeks ago. And that feud continued into last week on Black Sunday as well. JL: He's got to worry about getting that no vacated spot in the World title tournament left by the injured Alex Reed. TC: Let's get to the ring for this special match! Take it away David Pelter! Pelter: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to this special presentation match by the PCWF. The winner of this match will replace "Rudedog" Alex Reed's place in the world title tournament. The winner will be facing "Badboy" Randy Acorn this week on Black Sunday. Ladies and Gentlemen.. A R E Y O U R E A D Y?????? [Huge pop from the crowd.] Pelter: This match is scheduled for one fall and is a 60 minute lime limit. First.. Hailing from New York City, New York.. And weighing in 227 pounds.. Here is "T H E L E G A C Y" J O H N N Y S K I L E S!!! ["Foxy Lady" by Jimi Hendrix begins to play as Johnny Skiles makes his way to the ring. He's wearing long black trunks to the ring with a red heart on the back and "Legacy" written there.] TC: He's made quite a name for himself in the old MLW Feen. And now tonight he'll be laying what could be the foundation of a great PCWF career. JL: What he did in the past means nothing right now, all that matters is where you are right now. Right now he's got a match against an opponent who's be a champion in ever federation he's stepped into! TC: You just said that it doesn't matter what you did elsewhere!? JL: Never mind what I said Cross. I know what I meant. Pelter: And his opponent... Accompanied to the ring by his manager Stacy Ka$h.. He weighs in at 237 pounds.. Here is "D A S H I N G" D E R E K L A N K F O R D!!! [Afraid by Motley Crue begins to play over the PA system as Derek Lankford comes from the back. Lankford is a decently build man (think a younger Randy Savage) with short brown hair. He comes to the ring wearing one of his multicolored rhinestone robes (think Valentine or Flair), which Stacy Ka$h removes in the ring to reveal simple one color trunks. Stacy is a gorgeous blonde who dresses in sexy, and expensive dresses.] ___ ___ __ __ ___ | _ || _|\ \ /\ / /| __|-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= | _|| |_ \ v v / | __| SPECIAL BONUS WORLD TOURNAMENT MATCH |_| |___| \_/\_/ |_| "LEGACY" JOHNNY SKILES VS. "DASHING" DEREK LANKFORD =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= TC: There's the bell and this one is underway! They lockup in a Collar and Elbow.. Skiles with a duck under and into a FIREMAN CARRY TAKEDOWN! JL: Nice move by the Legacy. TC: Skiles tries to hold him down.. JL: I wonder what the ratings will be for this show? TC: ..but Lankford to his feet and shoots Sklies off to the ropes.. back off and CLOTHESLINES LANKFORD'S DOWN! JL: Look at Lankford POP back up to his feet! That didn't phase him much! TC: I don't know about that.. Skiles sends Lankford to the ropes.. back off and HIP TOSS! Nicely executed by Skiles. JL: The winner of this gets a few days off then has to face "Badboy" Randy Acorn on Sunday. You got to give Acorn the advantage coming into Sunday Night.? TC: Of course.. Skiles has Lankford's arm locked in an Armbar down on the mat, as he didn't let him up after the Hip Toss. JL: Yeah, but NOW Lankford's powering his way to his feet. TC: Skiles FLOATS over his back, Breaking him back down! Armbar Still On! JL: What kinda move was that? I just hope it gets into a brawl.. TC: What's with you and brawl type matches anyhow? Skiles now turns it into a Wristlock. Really working over that right arm of Lankford. JL: Stacy's not looking pleased either.. I hope there isn't a run in her panti-hose.. Lemme go look up close.. TC: You stay here! Skiles now spins the arm around his own leg.. Applying a lot of pressure to the arm here. JL: Kinda like a spinning toe hold but on the arm.. Weird! TC: But effective! He's spinning it around over and over.. Lankford in pain.. JL: You wouldn't know by looking at him though.. the man's tough! TC: That's true.. Spins around the arm again, and this time Lankford reaches over with his other arm and EYE GOUGES SKILES! JL: Not exactly a technical move, but it gets the job done! TC: Derek Lankford now using the same arm for a.. HOOKING LOW BLOW! And now he's yelling at the booing fans. JL: C'mon Lankford.. stay on him! TC: He's shaking the arm out.. The referee warning Lankford about the questionable tactics.. JL: Lankford ignores him and grabs Johnny Sklies as he gets up.. and THROWS HIM THROUGH THE ROPES TO THE FLOOR! HAHA! TC: Lankford distracting the referee.. I don't like the looks of this.. Stacy has her high heel shoe off! AND NAILS SKILES IN THE HEAD WITH IT! JL: Girl got bite! I like em like that! TC: That was terrible! Lankford now going out after Skiles.. He's setting for a suplex.. JL: here's no room there..? TC: No, it doesn't look like it.. Has him up.. MY WORD!! HE SUPLEXED SKILES ONTO THE STEEL RING STEPS!! JL: HOLY *@^#! That was Badass Cool! TC: He now rolls Skiles back in.. and climbs back in himself. Still favoring that right arm, but sends Skiles to the Corner.. NO! REVERSED!! Lankford hits the corner, and stumbles out.. INTO A DROP TOE HOLD! Skiles now Grapevines the Leg.. into a KNEE BAR here! JL: A knee bar? Is that where midgets go to get a beer? TC: FEEN! SHEESH! JL: What? TC: I must apologize for Jerry Leelan here.. Meanwhile, Skiles has stood up and spun around the leg with his own leg.. and down with a FIGURE FOUR LEGLOCK! JL: DAMN! Skiles almost has Derek Lankford's own finishing on! TC: Stacy screaming for Lankford to get out of it.. but he can't seem to do anything about it. JL: Go in and hit him with your shoe again! TC: <Sigh..> Actually Stacy IS up to the apron! Skiles lets go of the Figure 4... and CHASES her Off The Apron! JL: HEY! He can't do that! TC: He just DID! She jumped off and fell to the floor as her heel broke from the impact! JL: Ok.. sue him! Those have to be $150 shoes! TC: Please, Feen.. Lankford has limped to his feet.. And CLOTHESLINED SKILES FROM BEHIND! JL: HAHA! TC: Skiles FLIPS OVER THE TOP ROPE! But he managed to SWING Himself back in by the ropes on the way over! JL: Right to the back of the head! I love that move! TC: Lankford shakes his leg and then sends Skiles to the far ropes.. BACKDROP OVER THE TOP! NO! Skiles STOPS and executes an ARMBAR TAKEDOWN!! And then hooks an Arm Bar on that right Arm again! JL: Lankford quickly to the ropes to force the break.. But his arm and leg both appear hurt.. TC: Skiles with a SINGLE LEG Takedown.. NO! A HEADBUTT BY LANKFORD! JL: YES! The brawling begins! TC: And ANOTHER HEADBUTT DROPS HIM! JL: YES! TAKE HIM OUT! TC: Lankford with a LEGDROP! And now hits the ropes.. ELBOW DROP BUT SKILES MOVED!! That looked like it hurt Lankford badly. JL: Hey what's HE doing here? TC: It appears that Wacko and his manager Dr. Samuels have just walked to the ringside area. Man does Wacko look scary in that vest. JL: I thought Halloween ended 2 months ago? TC: Skiles over to the ropes and his yelling something at Wacko! I can't make out what he's saying, can you Feen? JL: Something about Monkey Gods sucking something.. Heh! Not sure I'm allowed to repeat it on the air! TC: All this going on, Derek Lankford is sneaking up behind Johnny Skiles.. SMALL PACKAGE ON SKILES!!! 1..... 2........ 3!!!!!!! HE got him! JL: And he's quickly out of there too! Lankford doesn't want to stay in there any longer, even to get his hand raised in victory! Pelter: The winner of this match... "DASHING" DEREK LANKFORD!!! TC: Dr. Samuels just un-tied Wacko's vest! JL: I'm outta here, this guy is crazy! [Jerry "The Feen" Leelan takes off his headset and quickly leaves the broadcasting table.] TC: Ok sure.. leave me alone! Wacko and Johnny Skiles are fighting in the ring with PCWF officials trying to break them up! This is total madness folks. We're out of time, thanks for joining us for this special card, we'll see you all at Black Sunday in 3 days! Good night everyone! [The credits begin to roll as you see PCWF officials trying to get Wacko and Johnny Skiles separated from each other in the ring.]
