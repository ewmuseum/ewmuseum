The French Fury Jean De Mort, back in the game

1 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<The camera's view is from a helicopter, circling over a French vineyard, in which media from all over the world have assembled. The helicopter lands in the designated area. A limousine approaches and idles as a makeshift podium is quickly assembled, and a short wiry gentleman assumes his position> Gentleman: Please, at this time I would beg your full attention. I realize why all of you have assembled here at this time. I am prepared to answer your questions and confirm or dispute any rumours floating around. Reporter: Sir, with all due respect, you know why we're ehar, there's one question that needs answering. You know it and we know it. Gentleman: Oui, please bear with me. I understand. And I do understand your eagreness. The wrestling community has been buzzing in recent weeks, regarding the return of one of France's proudest exports to the ring. I am honoured to make the annoucnement, that the legendary wrestler, who competed successfully in federations such as the WAR and ICW federations, is making his return to wrestling. Ladies and gentlemen, the most gifted natural athlete ever to grace the sport of professional wrestling, THE FRENCH FURY JEAN DE MORT! <Jean climbs out of the limousine,clad in a white business suit, and pushes his way through the droves of assembled media, to assume his position at the podium> Reporter: Jean, you are saying that you are returning to wrestling on a full time basis after a nearly two-year absence? Jean: That is correct. Since the LAW promotion disbanded, I have had a great deal of time to ponder my options. Every promotion out there wanted my services. I sat back at the family chateau here and thought about my options. I had conquered the ring and the business world. I took this time to train some wrestlers at my school, but never did I drop one bit out of ringshape. Instead I honed the craft of wrestling and never lost my thirst for the ring. Then I saw the direction wrestling is taking. When style overcomes substance. I always carried myself with class in and out of the ring. When the current torch-bearer became Playboy Ronnie D, I knew in m heart returning was the right thing to do. Reporter: You still haven't specified which promotion you have signed on with, and for how much!? Jean: That choice was a difficult one. I have few friends in the wrestling business. Most will stab you in the back at a momentt's notice. I make few acceptions in that regard. In the WAR federation, the gentlemen I ran with as a Horseman, are those execptions. Some are still around and I consulted them on my move. Of those Bad Boy Mark Bagwell was in an executive position in the ICW at the time. He know currently runs the PCWF. I can trust and respect Mark Bagwell professionally and personaly. As for the sum of my contract. That will remain undisclosed. Unlike most Americans, Mark and I conducted ourselves professionally, and I have no desire to exploit that relationship. Suffice it to say that Mark paid for services he knew would be develivered on. Reporter: Jean, have you been notified of any contractual obligations arising immediately. Jean: Yes. Although Mark and I are friends,we both have an understanding that I have no desire to be catered to, and he has no desire to cater to me. Which is why he has placed me in the World Heavyweight Title Tournament. My first opponent is someone named "Shane Axis". If your wrestling ability is as forgettable as your name Monsieur Axis, you will be short work. Fear not, for though you will recieve a defeat at my hands, you will recieve the wrestling lesson of your life. You are first on my list. Then the rest of tournament.Wrestling is in a disgusting state right now. There are no icons in wrestling, just egos. I've earned mine and I'm about to shwo the wrestling world how I did it. VIVA DE MORT! <De Mort boards a private plane that has just landed, as the media hounds him. They are informed by De Mort's staff that he is leaving for the tournament, and to vacate his property immediately>
