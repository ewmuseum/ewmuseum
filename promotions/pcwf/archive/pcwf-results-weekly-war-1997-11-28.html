[PCWF] Results from Weekly War 11/28/97

1 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm not going to post the entire card, since it never comes out properly here on RSPFW, but you can read it at the PCWF webpage: http://pcwf.home.ml.org (or) http://home.ican.net/~mstraz/pcwf/ On to the results: --[ Debut Match ]-- * Le Rebel made a great debut putting away "Slim" Jim Savage with "Le Superkick". Basic jobber match here. --[ Debut Match ]-- * Reckless Youth beat Marty Jobnetty in almost record time. Another jobber match, but the first match of the PCWF Juniors division! --[ Tag Team Action ]-- * US Express shocked everyone when they beat Sex & Violence to advance. The highly regarded and controversial team of S&V got upset by the US Express, who now advance to the semi-finals of the tag team tournament against the winner of the next match. --[ Tag Team Action ]-- * British Knights defeated Penthouse Players to also advance and they'll be facing the US Express on the next Weekly War, with the winner going to the finals to face the winners of Aerial Assault vs. Fame & Fortune. --[ Battle Royal for the PCWF Light-Heavyweight Title ]-- * El Kondor Magnifico won the match and became the new PCWF Lightweight champion. This match had it's share of HUGE controversy.. First, RSPWF's favorite son "Playboy" Ronnie D was officially eliminated by "Main Event" Mick Callahan with about 6 more men left in the ring. However.. due to the fact that "Rudedog" Alex Reed and John Crandell where brawling (using everything but the kitchen sink) down the aisle after eliminating each other, all 4 ringside referee's had to leave their posts to break up the altercation before a fan or anyone else got hurt. Well since no referee saw Ronnie D get thrown out and hit the floor, he simply went back in the ring! Ronnie D then threw out Mick Callahan with the 4 ref's all back now! The last 3 men where: "Dangerous" Danny Dynamite, El Kondor Magnifico and "Playboy" Ronnie D. El Kondor managed to threw out Ronnie D and this time the ref's saw it. On his way back around the ring to go to the locker room, Ronnie D pulled the top rope down while Danny Dynamite was coming towards it (El Kondor threw him into the ropes in an attempt for a cross body) and eliminated Dynamite!! For all the details and the full card.. visit the PCWF website!! [name withheld]
