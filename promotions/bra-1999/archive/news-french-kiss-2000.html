Battling Ring Angels: French Kiss 2000 — Hostile Territory?

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
French Kiss 2000 - Hostile Territory? French Kiss 2000 - Hostile Territory? By Evan Cates You bet your bottom dollar that it will be. This is Evan Cates reporting for the first time, in these final moments leading up to the Battling Ring Angels
March Pay-Per-View. Sure, the coveted Celestial Title is on the line, a new Television Champion will be crowned AND new Tag Team Champions will be decided all in one night, which makes for a spectacular showing. However, this PPV is much unlike any of the others. As most already know, this PPV is being sponsored by the Sisterhood of Seduction and their French Marquise,
Yvette DeSade Malreaux. Guess what? That also means that any BRA wrestler who has the unfortunate job of having to face a member of the Sisterhood is going to get booed from the building. Leave it to the French to stoop so low. This is America, where we cheer our heroes no matter whether they come from Canada, Ireland, Japan, or even Louisiana.
So, I might as well go ahead and do a run down of things that have taken place within the last couple of days. But before I do that, I want to go ahead and get my predictions for the different matches off my chest. This PPV should be one of the greatest shows in BRA history, and even though I can't be there to cheer my favorites on, I can certainly give them their credit in these pages. Let's move on.
Mia Lexington VS Tina Dream While Mia is coming off a loss and Tina is coming off a win, this is still a tough one. Tina definitely has the experience, but Mia just isn't stopping.
In the last month since she's appeared in BRA, the fans have already taken a liking to her. Take out the fact that she's a little self centered, and you have the makings of a fine BRA athlete. On Tina's side, you have ring savvy.
This girl knows exactly where she is at all times in that ring, and knows what to do. She can watch for the rookie mistakes that Lexington still hasn't learned from, and act on those. Of course, she'll probably have her repertoire of low blows in her favor, but that still doesn't make her any less of a great wrestler according to the general consensus. Of course this all may be thrown out the window, because my sources report that Mia
Lexington was seen getting out of the car at the Paris photo shoot with - get this - Sherry Ann and Veronica Millions. Could this mean that the Body Shop has their sights set on a new member? Just think what this could do for all the parties involved. The Body Shop is already the greatest group in women's wrestling history, and the personal favorite of this reporter.
The Winner: Unknown Debbie Loretto VS Medusa Rage Okay, so maybe Medusa handed Debbie her can once already. Still, that's not going to stop her. Debbie has returned to her normal training regimen and she took quite a victory this past week. She's also found some kind of friendship with Shannon McCourt and Sabrina Perezi. Still though, Medusa is one of the most powerful, destructive forces in women's wrestling, so you have to take that into account. And when you throw in hardcore rules, it's all a matter of who's nastier. Well, there's also a matter of who's smarter, but it don't account for much...
Winner: Medusa Rage "The Goddess" Nina Larue VS "Miss America" Sabrina Perezi (Television Title Match) Two women with funky monikers. Nina has put quite a number of victories under her corset in recent weeks, but Sabrina Perezi is one of the best newcomers to hit Battling Ring Angels in quite some time. I would have to go ahead and choose Sabrina as the winner here, but there's that fact that the fans are going to HATE Perezi in Paris. You see, Nina's one of the dreaded
Sisterhood, and that puts everything in her favor. Sabrina Perezi feeds off the reactions from the fans, and she's not going to have any positive cheers here. Plus, you never know where members of the Sisterhood are going to be, just waiting to hand the Television Title to the Goddess. However, Sabrina's pumped and she's prepared, and that's why I have to go with her in this matchup.
Winner: "Miss America" Sabrina Perezi "Sweet Misty VS Peggy Christian (Fall From Grace Match) When's the last time that you saw Peggy Christian win a match? Yeah, I don't remember either, but this old gal's got spirit. She just keeps coming back for more. I'm sure that Misty will be happy to give it to her too. Peggy's recently returning from Japan, where she's been wrestling for the last few weeks, and I'm eager to see what she's learned over there.
The Winner: Unknown The Double Ladder Match (Tag Team Title Match) Okay, who's all in this thing? You've got Lil' Alexandra, Laura Parker, Kelly Mase, Kayla Vandergriff, Alexandria Parker, Tiffany Epitaphine, Sherry Ann, Shannon McCourt, Missy McKinley, Jenny Anderson, and Kimie Kurita. This one's going to be a stinker to call and even more of one to referee. Expect the two Body Shoppers to get together, as well as the Parker Sisters. Mase and
Kurita will probably also band together, and then you have Missy McKinley, Shannon McCourt, and Kayla Vandergriff. Expect this thing to look like a war. In fact, it probably will be a war. Some of these people aren't going to care who gets the other belt, as long as they're able to get one themselves. That's where people like Sherry Ann, who will be watching for their friends are going to be at a disadvantage. I'm just going to have to make a guess at this one, instead of a prediction.
Winners: Shannon McCourt and Tiffany Epitaphine Veronica Millions VS Zantara Underworld I can't lie, I'm a little biased. Veronica is probably my favorite BRA wrestler of all time, in fact, I know that she is. Still, Zantara is going to be MAD, especially at the fact that Millions has been able to one-up her this whole time leading up to the PPV. She's got a steel plate in her head, so maybe Ronnie will bring a really big magnet. All kidding aside, this should be the match that brings this feud to a close. My vote's going for the good girl.
Winner: Veronica Millions "Miss America" Sabrina Perezi VS The Marquise Yvette DeSade Malreaux (Caged Mercy Match) This will probably be the most brutal thing witnessed all night. It's going to be Sabrina's SECOND match of the night. Yvette came to Paris to win. I don't see either of these women begging the other for mercy to end the match.
Malreaux would die before she admitted defeat in her own hometown, and that's where the deciding factor is going to come into play. Expect Nina Larue to have attempted to hurt Sabrina earlier in their match, so that Yvette would have even more of an upper hand.
Winner: The Marquise Yvette DeSade Malreaux Evonne Carmikel VS Lisa Dream (c) (Celestial Title Match) Okay, so maybe this will be the most brutal match of the entire year. There's waaay too much history involved to go into, but Evonne is here to extract vengeance on her former friend - a friend whom Evonne believes is trying to take her company and her Syndicate. Maybe Evonne still isn't right and needs to go back to that looney bin? In fact, no one's heard from her in a few days. Lisa Dream is the BRA Celestial Champion, by the way. She's going to do everything in her power to hold onto that belt. Personally, I don't like either of them, so this decision is totally unbiased. I gotta say though, with the Syndicate behind her and this being a no DQ match, my bets are on
Lisa Dream. Winner: Lisa Dream And that wraps up my predictions for the PPV: French Kiss, coming to you live from Paris, France March 6th. I suppose I'll close with a little thing that I may do every week or so, since this column is one big opinion written by me.
