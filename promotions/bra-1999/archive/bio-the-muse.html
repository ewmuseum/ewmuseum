Roster: The Muse The Muse THE MUSE History: Muse is a walking confliction. Ruthless and cruel in business, suprising compassionate amongst people she cares for. Every once in a while, that kindness spills over into her business. Make no mistake though. She is in control and crossing her, can end careers or even worse, make them.
First arriving on the BRA scene as the Archangel's assist, Muse quickly worked her way through the ranks to become the Archangels most trusted advisor. As a graduate of Harvard Business school, she interned with a up and coming modelling business run by Delilah Strain. The young intern impress her new boss and was given a position as VP of marketing.
Muse learned quickly under her new boss and became the ruthless, uncaring manager that Delilah was looking for. Muse drove many competitors out of business, often with underhanded tactics.
Delilah's dolls quickly become one of the top young modelling agencies in North America. The girls were all treated and trained like soldiers, they followed orders and did not question anything they were asked to do. Muse treated it like a business, no time for friendships.
Thats how she caught the eye of the Archangel. A friend of Delilah's and starting up a Wrestling Program for women. The Archangel wanted someone who knew how to handle talent. Someone who wouldnt take no for an answer, even from 180 pound wrestlers.
Delilah was not impressed that her friend was luring away her top management prospect. Delilah spouted to Muse, words like Duty and loyalty. But Delilah had trained her protege to well and Muse quit, leaving for the strange and money rich world of Professional Wrestling.
As the Archangels right hand. Muse had direct control over match making, hirings and marketting. For the first year and a half, BRA dominated the monday night wars. Money was made hand over fist.
Then in January of 2001, the Archangel turned the reins over to her young apprentice. With the sudden power gap, troubles brewed, finally climaxing in a strike which nearly buried the Federation.
Determined not to let her first chance at Ownership fall away. Muse signed major stars to major contracts. Betting everything on the popularity of her stars.
After a few struggling months, BRA started to regain its former glory. The money was starting to roll in. But Muse's inexperience caught up wit her and the big contracts handed out to her stars were eating up the profits.
With Battling Ring Angels near bankruptcy, she received a call from an old friend. Delilah, with pockets as deep and black as her heart smelled blood in the water and offered to pick up a 51% share of the Federation.
Muse hated to do it. But Business is business and she needed the help. Reluctantly she agreed and signed over majority control to Delilah. But Muse didn't relinquish total control of her entity. She still has the power to hire and fire. A power she plans to use to make the life of Delilah Strain, very difficult.
