Looking around the BRA arena was a site for owners to behold. Another packed house for the night's action awaited wrestlers and staff alike as the camera panned the crowd and a plethora of home made signs and banners declaring devotion for fan favorites. Some included "I go insane for Tiffany Lane!" "Big Barb = Big Heart" "Pia Ling, the next Featherweight Champion" and "Lady Victoria is BRA's best kept Secret!" Kenny: A short card tonight, but an exciting one regardless! Cari: You call this first match exciting! The terrible teens and a couple of rookies! Kenny: Say what you want about Young and Restless… Cari: They stink as wrestlers! Kenny: …they are still very popular with the fans! Says here they have an 89% approval rating! That's way higher than accomplished stars such as Akira Hagawa and Lesley Magnus! Cari: Pity that doesn't stop then getting killed every time they enter the ring! As the first few bars of Young and Restless by Prism echo across the sound system the crowd noise rises as three young women stride out from the wrestler's entrance. The first two stop at the top of the ramp and smile and wave at the crowd with youthful exuberance. Both wear white jackets with the words "Boise Semi-pro Wrestling Club" emblazoned across the back in lavender colored letters. The third woman, older and larger than the other two stands a short ways back with her hands on her hips. She wears an identical jacket over a t-shirt and jeans. All three make their way to the ring slapping hands with the fans all the way. As the two younger women enter the ring they remove their jackets and hand them to the third woman revealing identical lavender one piece spandex suits, nude colored dancer style tights and white lace up wrestling boots. Smiling, they high five each other and await their opponents. Kenny: Like I said, the crowd just eats them up! Cari: So do their opponents! Kenny: I have just been handed a note! It appears there has had to be a last minute replacement. "Queen Rana" Miriam Mkumba will replace the leopard Jeanie Miller in this match! Cari: Mkumba is back from being injured. She was impressive in beating Jenn Christian; these two teens should prove no obstacle! The lights flash on and off as Pia Ling and Miriam Mkumba break the curtains and stride towards the ring. Pia wears a simple black one piece suit with white boots while Miriam wears her traditional two piece tiger print spandex short and top set and is barefoot. They work either side of the crowd before making their way into the ring. Kenny: A strange mix, Pia and Miriam, it will be interesting to see how they work together. Cari: It will be an easy match tonight for Oni Malvolio. She definitely needs a break from Lesley Magnus! DING DING As the bell goes both Pia and Chelsea step from the ring and leave the other two to start. Heather then finds herself quickly snapped over her opponent's hip and tossed hard to the mat as Miriam stays with her, laying across her chest and keeping a solid lock on the hold. The teen twists and writhes, trying to break free, preventing Miriam from snaring a leg. The African queen realizes this and works her way to her feet, still keeping the headlock on the teen. The with a flash of speed Miriam drops to one knee and snap mares Heather over once, twice and then a third time! Kenny: Wow! Young Heather doesn't know what hit her! It was Hurricane Miriam! Cari: A quick tag to bring Pia Ling in. Let's see what she can do now, though I doubt very much! Pia tags and leaps through the ropes, pulling Heather up by an arm and whipping her off the far ropes. As Heather rebounds off Pia greets her with a dropkick to the chest. Heather rolls to the ropes and pulls herself up as Pia follows, smacking a hard chop to her chest that sounds across the arena. Again Pia grabs an arm and whips her opponent across the ring, this time bending over for a backdrop. To Heather's credit she dives over Pia and rolls the Oriental girl into a sunset flip, pinning her shoulders to the mat. Cari: Sunset flip! Can Heather hold it! ONE! TWO!! Kenny: Kickout by Pia! Chelsea climbs to the second rope and raises her fists, jumping off and slamming them into Pia's back with a force that drops the tiny wrestler to her knees. Heather releases the headlock and climbs out, receiving a warning from Malvolio in the process. Kenny: Pia in trouble now! She is about the smallest wrestler in the Fed these days! Cari: And not much of a wrestler. If the terrible teens handle this right they should walk off with this match now. They have to keep Mkumba out of the ring! Chelsea pulls Pia up by the arm and proceeds to twist on a standing armbar. A look of anguish crosses the small girls face as she gets dragged to the middle of the ring and then whipped into the ropes where Chelsea waits for her with clothesline to the chest. Pia drops to the mat wincing and tries to get back up but Chelsea grabs her by the head and with a sharp breath snap suplexes her back to the mat. Pia rolls away, reaching for her back. Dani and Heather cheer from the corner as their friend controls the match. Pia still grimaces as she is pulled again to her feet and Chelsea bends down and starts to scoop her up for a body slam, but with great presence of mind Pia reverses it and rolls Chelsea into a small package, but before Oni can start a count the teen kicks out. Kenny: Nice reversal from Pia! Cari: Too small to hold it though, even for the terrible teens! Pia is quickest to her feet and manages to reel off a spinning heel kick to Chelsea's stomach before she could set herself. Pia quickly pulls Chelsea by the arm across to her corner before Chelsea could resist and makes a tag with her partner. Kenny: Mkumba's in! Pia has made the tag! Cari: Nitey nite Chelsea! Miriam leaps into the ring, sending Chelsea sprawling with a forearm to the chest. Queen Rana followed up with the speed of lightning!
