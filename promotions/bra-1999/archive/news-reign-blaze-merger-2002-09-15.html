JAPANESE CORPORATION COMES INTO AGREEMENT WITH TWO PROMINENT FEMALE WRESTLING FEDERATIONS. BLAZE AND BATTLING RING ANGELS TO DETERMINE COMBINED CHAMPION UNDER REIGN UMBRELLA. TOKYO, JAPAN, - September 15, 2002 - BLAZE Owner Akira Kahn and Battling Ring Angels Owner Delilah Strain have been meeting for the past two weeks with REIGN CEO Yasuo Masami in effort to bring the women?s wrestling industry together in a show of not only business unity, but in sports unity. REIGN has purchased 45% of each company from each respective owner, allowing both Kahn and Strain to continue running their individual operations. REIGN will collect 45% of each company?s profits from here on out. In addition, REIGN has created a new championship that will combine BLAZE?s Triple Crown Women's Universal Championship and BRA?s Women's World Championship into a unifying single championship that will signify the most prestigious spot in all of women?s wrestling. This championship will be known as the Grand Phoenix Crown Women's Championship. Each company will be allowed to hold their own individual company champions. Kahn and Strain will be allowed to determine top contenders from their respective roster, however Masami would have deciding vote in case of a tie in the decision making process. Yasuo Masami has been a longtime fan of women?s wrestling and has been looking for the opportunity to get involved. ?Starting a new wrestling promotion has never really been an option, as the top talent in the world is already within these two promotions. Any other promotion starting up would be so far beneath what these two promotions can offer. The potential of a combined effort between these two promotions would greatly benefit the sport. The competition would be tremendous. I have been courting both of these groups for a couple of years now, and finally we?ve been able to come to a mutual agreement.? Delilah Strain, who has been the owner of BRA for about a year was also exciting about the possibility of things to come. ?This merger, though that is not quite the right word for it, is going to only increase the demand for our product. BLAZE has some tremendous talent. Talent with heart and courage. BRA has always had talent with heart and courage, and when you put these two companies in competition for a top prize, you?ll have a contest of universal proportions. What I really appreciate is the fact that Akira and myself will be able to maintain control of our individual companies while still be in competition with each other. We could possibly see some BRA talent wrestling in Japan, as well as some of BLAZE?s talent coming to compete in BRA. The possibilities involved in this merger is not lost on BLAZE owner, Akira Kahn. ?Since the beginning I've always believed that BLAZE is a promotion that stresses the motto Progression Beyond All Standards. The goal was to always be unconventional in everything we do. BLAZE has developed tremendously over the years to reach the highest standard. I don't know if BLAZE can be considered the best women's promotion in the world, but there is not doubt we have the best quality of talent in the business. BRA has never been that far behind in regard to talent. To finally see which promotion truly has the better athletes will be a competition of the greatest magnitude. It will be like the World Series or Superbowl of wrestling. I have no doubt that the interest in what we do will be increased by this combination of BLAZE and BRA.? On tap for this joint adventure would be to merge the BLAZE Triple Crown Women's Universal Championship and the BRA Women's World Championship. ?We actually had to toss a Yen piece to see who would host such an event,? said Masami. ?Delilah Strain won the coin toss, so in December at BRA?s "Endgame", the BLAZE Triple Crown Women's Universal Champion, currently held by Madison Stone will take on the BRA Women's World Champion, currently held by Cassie Hopkins. From that point on, the defense of this title will be alternated between the two promotions. With all three parties looking forward to this venture, and the limitless possibilities, it will be interesting to see how this drama plays out. ?I believe with all my heart this will only further the popularity of our craft to the world,? replies Kahn when asked. ?I guarantee you that something unexpected will occur.?
