SissyLanders Sissy Landers Name: Princess “Sissy” Landers Height: 5’ 4” Weight: 132 pounds Date of Birth: March 24, 1980 Hometown: Baton Rouge, La Physical Appearance: Sissy is a Caucasian blond with hazel eyes. Hair a little frizzy and wavy. Her physical build is that of a natural athlete. Strong muscles all over her body. Not much fat, except on her cheeks to give her a cute look. Six pack abs, tight biceps, tight leg muscles and excellent fitness. All formed by her time in the gym.
Ring Attire: Sissy comes to the ring in a white Adidas ringer tee, with blue trim and blue Adidas emblem over a black sports bra. Her shorts are a pair of tight black “boxer shorts” made of a stretchy material and doubled once at the waist band to make them look tighter around her perfect thighs. The back of the shorts is decorated with white stitched-on letters bearing her personal motto “Kick It Or Kiss It.” Her boots are black with white trim and she completes her look with black knee and elbow pads.
Background: From a well to do family in Baton Rouge, La., no one ever thought Princess Landers was going to be a Pro wrestler.  She was the only child of her parents and they named her Princess mostly because they thought she was going to grow up to be really special. She was a pretty basic high school girl, competing in Soccer and Gymnastics, as well as Cheerleading. She was a popular girl, never alone on a weekend night. One of the more popular girls in school, both in high school and college. But she grew up in front of the TV watching MTV and other such stuff that Gen-X enjoys. She adopted the short attention span her generation is known for. Very little kept her attention for very long. In college she changed majors 6 times. She played lots of sports and tried lots of extra-curricular activities. She also broke up with boyfriends often as they couldn’t hold her attention.
After college she had no idea what was next. Nothing interested her all that much. A wrestling promoter saw her working out and signed her to wrestle. She was a natural. But more importantly, she seemed to like it. She actually paid attention. Every other thing she did left people believing she had un-diagnosed Attention Deficit Disorder. But in the ring she was all business. Between bells she’s a focused and intent wrestling machine. Before and after the bell she’s an absent minded and bored girl.
Finisher: The Sissy Splash - A Frog Splash. But she gets so much elevation she’s able to pump twice in the air before landing on the other wrestler.
