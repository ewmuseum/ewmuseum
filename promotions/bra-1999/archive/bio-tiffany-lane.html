TiffanyLane "The Blonde Bombshell" Tiffany Lane Name: "Blonde Bombshell" Tiffany Lane Height: 5'8 Weight: 120 lbs Date of Birth: Feb. 14, 1978 Hometown: Malibu, CA Finisher: Bombshell Bomb - A Senton Splash Physical Appearance: Tiffany is just what her name implies, a blonde bombshell. She has long, blonde hair and stunning, emerald eyes with a flawless face. Her body is her most sought after commodity. She has a petite, sleek, curvaceous figure with an ample amount of cleavage, giving a whole new meaning to the term "busty":-)
Background: Born into a family of wealth and luxury, Tiffany Lane was afforded everything that her heart desired. This blonde beauty was spoiled by her parents and began to see herself as naturally superior to others. She excelled at such competitve games as volleyball and ice skating, enjoying the chance to prove her superiority over another athlete. During her high school years, Tiffany and her best friend since childhood, Nina Larue, created a clique called The Babe Squad, consisting of only the most beautiful and wealthy, young women. Although Tiffany was a notorious snob, she remained popular, especially with the male students. Tiffany served as captain of the cheerleading squad as well as homecoming queen, class president, and prom queen, using her popularity and prestige to punish those she didn't deem fit (i.e. the geeks and freaks).
After graduating high school, Tiffany didn't know what she wanted to do with her life. She wasn't particularly the...uh...brightest student so college definitely wasn't an option. She was doing a little modeling but was looking for something with a little more excitement. After answering an ad in the newspaper, she embarked on a career as a catfighter. Tiffany loved the job. She not only got to compete against other women, that she deemed unworthy, but she was also able to humiliate and dominate them. Tiffany was extremely popular because of her great beauty and savage nature. It was also during this time that she became friendly rivals with Holly Hotbody, another popular catfighter, both women competing for the title of Queen of Catfighting. Growing bored with the low amounts of pay, Tiffany decided to try her hand at another career that would allow her to compete against others: professional wrestling.
After graduating from wrestling school, Tiffany made her pro wrestling debut in the FWO with Nina serving as her manager. Tiffany competed against both men and women, rising in the federation's ranks. After engaging in a feud with the tag team, The Surfer Circuit, Tiffany persuaded Nina to join the league and form a tag team. They re-christened themselves The Babe Squad and dominated the tag division, including their feud with The Surfer Circuit, with their mix of seduction and cheating. The Babe Squad soon won the FWO World Tag Titles and held them until the federation closed down. Tiffany, with Nina again serving as manager, then joined Gyeah Wrestling, where she competed in their women's division.
She then joined Nina in Battling Ring Angels where both women joined The Sisterhood of Seduction stable. During her first months in BRA, Tiffany "won" the title of "Miss BRA" in a dubious fashion. She reigned as "Miss BRA" for almost a year before starting her own line of lingerie with Alexandria Parker, called Tiffany's Bedroom. Tiffany also did a few lingerie specials for Playboy. It was shortly after this that she won the BRA Celestial Title in a four way elimination match. She dropped the title to Lindsay Locke but won it back a few weeks later. Tiffany held the championship until she was stripped of the title by Gabrielle, the Arch Angel's right-hand man. It was during this time that Gabrielle and Johnny Angel both feuded over control of BRA, Lindsay and her Children of the Revolution siding with Gabrielle and Tiffany and the Sisterhood of Seduction siding with Johnny Angel. Gabrielle christened Lindsay champion while Johnny Angel named Tiffany the champion. The two champions were heading towards a battle to unify the titles when the promotion's wrestlers went on strike. Tiffany currently works a second career as a model.
