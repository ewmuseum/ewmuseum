DanaSheriff Dana Sheriff Name: Dana Sheriff Height: 5’2 Weight: 116lbs DOB: Aug 11/1975 Hometown: Nottingham, England Dana is the child of an English father and a Turkish mother. She has slight Middle East coloring and dark hair cascading half way down her back.
Her ring attire consists usually of a black spandex one-piece suit with black, billowy chiffon sleeves, traditional English black fishnet tights and low cut black wrestling boots.
“The lights go dim briefly before they raise fully as the beat of Hall and Oates “Maneater” crashes around the arena and Dana Sheriff saunters out wearing a long red robe open in the front over her ring attire. A contemptuous smirk plays lightly over her lips as she barely acknowledges the crowd, walking slowly down the ramp. A mixed reaction greets her appearance but she seems not to take notice of either as she walks directly to the steps and climbs through the ropes and heads to her corner after circling the inside of the ring once. She continues to ignore the largely jeering crowd as she slides out of her robe and tosses it outside the ring.”
Dana is unique in the fact she claims to be a descendant of an actual Sheriff of Nottingham that appeared in the time in England’s past when Robin Hood was written about. Consequently she has airs of nobility and no time for those who play at being something they are not. Her attitude is one of arrogance for both fans and opponent’s alike.
Dana was a pro in England for a little over a year in small promotions before coming to BRA.
Dana’s strength lies in her experience. Though a pro for barely a year she still holds an edge in experience over a lot of the other wrestlers. Her weaknesses are her size, she is small and not at all powerful, and her attitude. Her contempt of fans and opponents usually means she garners no support.
Dana is the daughter of an English politician father and a Turkish exotic dancer mother. She claims to be a descendant of a sheriff of Nottingham of old and acts it. She grew up in England but was always on the fringe of the better class because of her mother’s background, which was highly frowned upon. Consequently she grew to resent those she felt below her, despite herself being shunned. She took up wrestling as a means to make those she feels beneath her get the treatment she feels they deserve and vent a lifetime of frustration. BRA statistician and recruiter Angelo Enumerelli recruited her at the same time as Tanzi Charlton and Miriam Mkumba.
Dana is a technician in the ring, with no qualms about a few cheap shots against those deserving, which is just about everyone.
Wrestling moves in clued headlocks, scissors, head and body, full nelsons, grapevines, step over toe hold, snap mares, hip tosses, drop kicks, body slams, bulldogs, forearm smashes, eye gouge and eyes along the ropes.
Dana Sheriff, 5’2, 116, Nottingham, England, small, half English, half Turkish, arrogant, technician, heel.
