LonnieYamboa Lonnie Yamboa Name:Lonnie Yambao Height: 5’5 Weight: 115lbs DOB: Jan 3/1974 Hometown: Manilla, Philippines Physical Appearance. Lonnie is well streamlined but rugged with black hair to her shoulders. Her generally thin structure belies the toughness she has attained from her past.
Ring Attire. She wears a multi colored one-piece swimsuit with black tights and white trainers. Entrance. As the lights brighten the sounds of Bruce Springsteen’s “Born in the USA” boom loud over the sound system as Lonnie Yambao struts out defiantly dragging and American flag behind her. She stops at the top of the ramp and sneers at the crowd with her hands on her hips and one hip cocked casually to the side. She then thumbs her nose to the crowd before pulling the flag up in front of her. With one quick motion she rips the flag in half and drops it on the ground before wiping her feet on it. She then heads to the ring, grinning as the crowd reacts strongly against her.
Why is she unique? Lonnie is pure Philippine, born and raised; only coming to America to make a success as a wrestler.
Attitude. Lonnie shows nothing but contempt for all things American, and loves to show it. Her choice of entrance music is meant to mock and she at all times shows nothing but arrogance to the crowd.
Experience. Pro wrestling is relatively new to Lonnie but she has over ten years experience catfighting for a film company.
Strengths and Weaknesses Lonnie is a fighter. She is a scrapper who never backs down from a fight. She is hardened from making a living as a fighter and will rarely submit. Her main weakness lies in lack of pro experience as a wrestler. Her knowledge of holds is not too strong and her tactics will lead to many DQ’s.
Summary of experience. Lonnie grew up outside an American air force base in the Philippines. Her family was poorly treated by the servicemen, which is where she learned to despise all things American. At age 15 she began working for a catfight film company in Manilla. In the next ten years she fought for the company, reveling in the combat and learning the basics of wrestling and boxing as well as out and out brutal catfighting. She was noticed by an agent and tried out for BRA. The selection committee noticed her lack of wrestling skills but loved her brutal, tough style and accepted her on the spot.
Moves. Lonnie has no finisher but likes to inflict pain, especially on pure homegrown American girls.
Headlock, hip toss, snap mare, kicks, punches, nail rakes of the eyes and backs, stomps and boots, eye drag along the ropes, head smash to the corners, chokes on the ropes, dropkicks, bodyslams, elbow and knee smashes, forearm smashes.
