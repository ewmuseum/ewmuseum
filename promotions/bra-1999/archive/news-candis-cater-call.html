Battling Ring Angels: Candi's Cater-Call

Candi's CATER-CALL Candi's Cater-Call By Candi Cane Welcome once again, true believers, to another edition of the Great One telling you how it is. We got lots and lots to talk about. I don't even know where to begin. This Pay Per View is certainly one to remember. The good girls played bad and the bad girls played good! So, with no particular rhyme or reason, let's talk about Viper and Amanda Strike.
Viper, you just plain suck! Yeah, you heard me! Well, then again, maybe you can't seeing as you are probably going to be in ICU for the rest of your life.
Amanda cleaned your pathetic clock. For those of you who did not get to see the Pay Per View (Cheap Bastards! Get it next time!), Amanda beat her with kendo sticks, chairs, and chains. The blood from that cheap tramp, Viper, ran like beer at a frat party. Of course, the greatest thrill came when Amanda, one of the greatest wrestlers in this federation, came off the top ropes pile driving Viper's head into the mat. Ah, the snap made me squeal with delight as
I sat in the VIP booth. Man, that was something to see! And hear! Amanda did feel bad over what happened, though. Kelly the Assassin' Mase, came to help out when it looked like Viper was really hurt. I am pretty sure that Mase had paramedic training and was just trying to give Viper some help. Regardless, though, I think we've seen the last of Viper. I'll make sure to tell you readers when I get around to crying.
Skye Soaring Hawk was robbed! Robbed I tell you! That skank, Americana, coming out of retirement, somehow pulled avictory out of her fat ass. Have you people seen Americana lately? Yuck! Looking at her reminds me of Bill Cosby's Pudding ads. All that jiggly stuff. Look out, Bill, you might be out of work!
Tiffany the Wrath' did it! She grabbed the belt and became champion of the midcard title. I would really like to send out a big congratulations, but every single person who she fought against was a big loser. I swear, as I sat there watching that match, I kept thinking to myself that I was watching a Marx
Brother movie with all the blunders and goof-ups that those retards fouled up.
I mean, was it really a surprise to anyone that Tiffany won? No. Duh! I would have bet on her at my local bookie, but the odds weren't good enough.
Anywhere. And this is Las Vegas! I am sure that Tiffany will keep the title because she is one tough bitch. In case you haven't guessed. I like her.
Let's give three cheers for the Sisterhood. They tossed those lame Shoppers around like rag dolls (or, should I say, hag dolls?') The Shop just plain sucks. I mean, I tried to come up with something clever to slam them but they simply aren't worth the time to come up with anything. Why the Hell is it called The
Body Shop,' anyway? My guess is that these girls spend more time repairing their bodies after matches that they might as well open up a shop to tend to them. However, I must say that Lisa Dream is pretty cool. No, I'm serious!
Really! Honestly! There is something pretty cool about someone who uses Nookie' as their entrance music. However, Lisa, you are wasting your life away hanging out with a bunch of washed up goofs. The Body Shop is all talk. And that is saying something from me!
I think it is great that Tiffany Lane is the first Ms. BRA.' Yvette, Amanda, and
Electra were so nice to get together and sponsor such a contest. When I saw her name on the ballot, I voted in a heartbeat! Congratulations, Tiffany!
Kisses! Now, we come to the best part of the Pay Per View. Yeah, we all know that Bianca
Dupree won the title, somehow. Hoorah! Whatever. The real story is that someone smacked the poor Marquise Malreaux in the back of the head as she was about to take the title in her grasp. Some miscreant, for some unknown reason, came up behind Marquise Malreaux and hit her in the back of the head with a camera. A camera! How low-down and dirty can you get? That was an expensive piece of equipment and as a journalist, I was offended!
Well, this reporter has gotten to the bottom of it, sports fans. It's hotter than that stuff your uncle Frankie got off the truck! That's right, in just one week I have already justified my annual salary. I had the opportunity' to talk with Sweet Misty, and folks, she is anything but. I had always thought that there was at least one person in the Body Shop which stood for something decent. Ha! I was wrong! That disgusting little boy-toy makes me sick to my stomach which is a pretty major accomplishment seeing as I could drink the frat boys under the table back at Marquette keggers. Anyway, it seems that
Misty, in a recent interview with the company's own Grinnan Barret, admitted no, make that reveled in the fact that she not only wants to get into the ring the Marquise, but she also is the one who whacked her in the back of the head at the Pay Per View.
Can you think of anything more disgusting? Well, Americana Pudding Ass does come to mind. Why would someone who claims to be so nice and so innocent at everything go behind someone and hit them with a camera? I think that you fans should take notice of that. Yvette may not be liked by you people but does anyone deserve to be hit in the head with an expensive piece of machinery? What you people fail to understand is that Yvette is classy all the French are. Do you people really want to cheer for someone who plays dirty? Plus, is Misty out of her mind?
Challenging the Marquise? You have to be crazy. Yvette is emotionally charged.' Would you go up to Schwarzenegger and tell him that YOU were the one that wrote the script for Batman and Robin?' No way! He'd kill ya! Now, I am sorry, Misty, for reveling your dirty little secret but this is the news, honey.
Finally, Misty, I know that you are now a dirty player, as is the rest of the Shop, but don't bother sending anyone after me! What would it prove anyway? Oooh! The great Body Shop can beat up untrained reporters. Then again with the tactics they have taken lately, I better be packing heat.
That is all the news fit to print!
