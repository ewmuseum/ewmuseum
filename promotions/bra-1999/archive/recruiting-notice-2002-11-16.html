Below is the FAQ and app. But, if you need more info, just check out the website at: http://www.shepherdsflock.net/bra/index.php . Thanks in advance. 1. What is BRA? 2. Who runs it? (IC) 3. The application 11. Contacting the fedhead 1. What is BRA? BRA stands for Battling Ring Angels. Essentially, this is a women's promotion headquartered in Las Vegas, Nevada. The promotion has a television show, Power Trip, aired on the Lifetime Network. Most of the shows take place at The Battling Ring Angels arena in NV but the promotion has also been known to tour. BRA has been up and running for over two years now and is one of the few women's promotions on the net. It's website address is http://shepherdsflock.net/bra/index.php . 2. Who runs it? (IC) The promotion is run by Delilah Strain, business woman and owner of Delilah's Dolls. 3. Who runs it? (OOC) [name withheld]. He is a "veteran" of e-wrestling and has been in the 'sport' since the fall of 1998. He's handled in a number of wrestling federations and is the only member to remain in BRA from the very beginning. He helped the former, BRA president put cards together but this will be his first time as complete fedhead. 4. Who are the personalities? a. Delilah Strain: Owner/CEO: It was last year when mega-businesswoman Delilah Strain made the offer to the Archangel involving the purchase of BRA. She claimed that she would be able to put the money up to pull BRA out of the problems it had been having. However, in the aftermath of the strike, in an attempt to rebuild, BRA had nowhere else to turn when the well ran dry. Eventually, Muse contacted Delilah, who, after quite a bit of discussion, purchased half of Battling Ring Angels. She began her career in wrestling by bringing one of the most hated women of all time back into the ring - Cari "The Brat" Tramell. She also debuted her own tag team, the rookie team of Cindra McCain and Skye Johanassen - better known as the Heavenly Bodies. Now, she seeks to take BRA to the next level - a level never before achieved. Somewhere, something has to give... ...and the success of this company is the VERY most important thing in Delilah's life. b. Kenny Harbor: Announcer: Perhaps too smart for his own good, Kenny is rather knowledgeable about the most minute statistics and details in the field of wrestling. Never seen without his black laptop, Kenny uses various sources and charts to give insight to anything presented by the Battling Ring Angels. He has a definite love of fair play, traditional wrestling, and the kind of guy that girls one could take home to his mother. Cute and naive in the ways of the world, Kenny likes to see the ‘good girl’ win, mostly because the bad girls intimidate him and are the kind of girls that broke his heart in high school. He has trouble seeing without his glasses, even though it does a great deal to improve his looks. c. Evonne Carmikel: Announcer: A former BRA and IFWF wrestler, Evonne is a mean-spirited shrew that shows open disdain for the faces and adoration for the heels. Attractive and strong, Evonne is highly opinionated and not afraid to "tell it like it is", as she sees it. Also, she is known for giving poor Kenny a hard time, "Geek Boy" being one of her favorite terms of endearment for him. d. Jenna Jamestown: Interviewer: Nobody is more pure and sweet and innocent and cheery and etc., than Jenna. She comes off as everyone’s sweetheart and ‘girl next door.’ Likely a former cheerleader and likely a former president of the pep club, Jenna does her job with professional accuracy and always a smile. However, somehow she manages to get paired up interviewing the nasty girls who yell, scream, and push her around. She is available to be used in rps. e. Garry Grimmoire: Interviewer: Loud, lewd, and overweight, this arrogant interviewer is the complete opposite of Jenna. Gary is balding and usually wears unkempt slacks, a wrinkled shirt, and BRA blazer, all decorated with various food stains. He favors the heels and has no bones about giving the faces a hard time, although he cowers if they decide to fight back. He is also available to be used in rps. f. Candi Bratton: Head referee: After announcing her retirement on January 7, 2002 due to injury, Candi Bratton was approached by BRA Owner Delilah Strain and offered a position as Head Referee. g. "The Voice" Fiona Miranda: Ring announcer: Recently hired to be the ring announcer, Fiona Miranda is happy. 5. What are the titles? a. BRA World Title: The World Championship, originally called the Celestial Title, was created to acknowledge the best wrestler in Battling Ring Angels. There is no weight limit to hold the World Championship. b. BRA Eastern Continental Championship: The Eastern Continental Championship, orginally called the Fallen Angel Title, also known as World TV Title, was created to acknowledge the second best wrestler in Battling Ring Angels. There is no weight limit to hold the World Tag Team Championship. c. BRA Middleweight Championship: The Women's Middleweight Championship, originally called the Featherweight Title, was created to acknowledge the best wrestler under 130 pounds in Battling Ring Angels. Wrestlers must weigh under 130 pounds to be eligible to compete for this championship. d. BRA World Tag Team Championship: The World Tag Team Championship, originally called the BRATT (Battling Ring Angels Tag Titles), was created to acknowledge the best in the tag team scene in Battling Ring Angels. There is no weight limit to hold the World Tag Team Championship. 6. What are the shows? a. BRA PowerTrip: is the BRA television show that is shown every other Monday, with the exception of the weekend of a 'special'. TV cards are 'play-by-play'. b. Girl Talk: is the show where BRA's "vignettes" (i.e. role plays) are shown. It is produced before each PowerTrip. 7. What are the Special shows? Specials are featured about 6 times per year. They are play by play and are usually much longer than TV cards. They are the equivalent to a PPV. The schedule for PPVs are as follows: Crime Spree - Third Monday in February. Theme for this event is a completely hardcore card. Matches are all extreme gimmick matches. Naughty or Nice - Third Monday in April. This event has a tournament theme with a bit of luck thrown in. Names are thrown into a barrel and drawn one at a time at random. The first two names compete. The loser is gone. The winner will then flip a coin and if it lands on heads, they go back to the locker room, advancing to the second round, and two more names are drawn for the next match. If it lands on tails, then that person will have to wrestle against the next name drawn. This process continues throughout the tournament. Winner will get a shot at the World Title at the next special. To Be Announced - Third Monday in June. To Be Announced - Third Monday in August To Be Announced - Third Monday in October Endgame - Third Monday in December. The best of the best. Potential matches of the year type of card. The main event will always feature a title for title match as the World Champion will defend against the Eastern Continental Champion. 8. What's required of handlers? a. Role-playing: **For the first time in it's nearly two years of existence, BRA is moving from an open rp system to a closed one. ** It is expected that every wrestler scheduled for a match will role play for Girl Talk, although you can also rp if you aren't scheduled, as well. There is a maximum of one rp per Girl Talk show. Also, it is recommended that handlers role play for PowerTrip, as well. PowerTrip rps will be set at the arena (i.e. backstage interview, in-ring, locker room altercation, etc.) While Girl Talk promos are more like vignettes, prerecorded at various locations. Promos will be highly considered for win/losses. If you don't role play and your opponent does, you should probably consider that you'll be on the losing end that night. You may submit your role plays on the web site, however, they will not be posted until either the PowerTrip or the Girl Talk card. Preferably, they should be sent to Stevie at [detail withheld] . Any questions regarding any of this can be sent to that address as well. Also, failure to role-play regularly means you aren't likely to be involved in the top storylines, and could lead to you being dropped from the roster if you don't role-play for a long time. b. Strats: For Power Trip, always use the KISS (Keep It Short n' Simple) method for strats. Don't give me 20 different things you want your wrestler to do, because I can guarantee you they won't fit in. And keep it to a general match approach, with maybe a few suggested spots, and a suggested finish. Bear in mind, though, that not every part of the strat may be used, depending on how storylines and angles play out. Strats can be longer for the Specials. c. Angles: If you have an idea for an angle, talk to me about it. Angles can be submitted for cards as well, but bear in mind that it may not always be possible to work those angles out. That's why I ask you go through me if you have angle ideas, so they can be properly worked into the shows, and to also make sure there isn't conflict with other angles. And if there is conflict, please be understanding about things. It isn't always possible for things to go your way, and it's never personal if I don't use an angle. Handlers are encouraged to work together on angles and storylines, because, after all, it's more fun that way and you have a better chance of making sure your angles are included. 9. Certain things you need to know Call these rules, reminders, guidelines, ultimatums...whatever you want. This is the section which outlines some things that you, the handlers, need to remember as you go about doing things in this promotion. I really don't want to include these, but given the way some people act, they need to be included to serve as notice as to what will not be acceptable in BRA from any handler. a. NO SHOOTING. This rule is nonnegotiable. I don't care whether you "shoot" about something said in a chat room, "shoot" on another promotion, "shoot" on a handler, "shoot" on me...any and all "shoots" are unacceptable, PERIOD. If any handler does shoot, regardless of the reason, these things will happen on the first offense: 1) The role-play in question will not be used. 2) If your wrestler has a match on the card, he jobs, end of story. 3) You will be given a warning and your wrestler will be suspended from the next two cards. This means no matches, no role-plays, no angles, nothing. Second offense occurs...you are gone and permanently banned from BRA. So don't get into "shooting" because it will not be allowed here. c. Complaint Department: I have no problem if you have concerns about the promotion to discuss with me, but constant complaints about late cards, losses or angles being excluded when I explain to you that I couldn't fit them in, aren't going to be acceptable. Those who do nothing but complain are likely to find themselves heading out the door. Again, there are some valid concerns that may come up, but I don't want people who do nothing but complain being a part of BRA. d. Leaves of Absence: If you anticipate you will be gone from BRA for a lengthy period of time, just let me know and I'll work things out with you. Those who work out leaves of absence will be assured of getting their roster spots back when they return. If you simply disappear and I get no notice from you, chances are you'll be dropped from the roster. So always notify me if you'll be gone for a long time. Likewise, I'll happily work things out with you if you will be gone because of vacation or other commitments, as long as you notify me in advance. e. Storylines involving the commissioner/president: I'd prefer these be kept to a minimum. I don't want the personalities in charge becoming focal points of storylines. It may be necessary at times, and I understand that, but I don't want the personalities becoming the stars of the show. It's your wrestlers that deserve that honor. And please don't make constant mentions of said personalities in your role-plays, either. Focus on your storylines, not who is in charge of the promotion (unless, of course, it's necessary for any storylines planned or discussed with me). f. Match writers: As the promotion grows, chances are I'll be looking for more people to help with writing matches. That goes back to how contributions to the promotion will make a difference as to how things progress here. So, for example, if we want more matches on PowerTrip, then I'll be looking for people to write some of those matches. Although we do have a staff of writers currently available, any and all help would be much appreciated. g. HAVE FUN! That's the most important thing to remember. It's what this is supposed to be all about, right? 10. Application: Pro Singles Creation Sheet Fill out the following form and send to [detail withheld] .
