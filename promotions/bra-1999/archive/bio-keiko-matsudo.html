KeikoMatsudo Keiko Matsudo Name: Keiko  Matsudo Date of birth: 11/24/72 Hometown: Osaka, Japan Height: 6’3” Weight: 187 lbs. Physical Description:  The biggest Japanese woman you’ve ever seen. She is huge and powerful. Mostly muscle, she looks like a statue. She has long black hair and brown eyes. Her look is one of quite contemplation. Note: she is missing the top 2/3 of the pinky on her left hand. It has been replaced by a prosthesis made of some sort of metal.
Ring Attire: She wears black leather pants adorned with the image of a silver Dragon on the legs, and a black short sleeved collared shirt. The short sleeved shirt allows for viewing of her extensive tattoos that start around her wrists and head all the way up her  very powerful arms. The intricate tattoos of fires and demons are the marks given to all members of The Yakuza, the Japanese Mafia. She finishes her outfit with black biker boots
Keiko Matsudo is a member of The Yakuza, the Japanese Mafia. Her father and grandfather were both members. They encouraged young Keiko to get herself involved in the “family business”, and so she did. At first, she was skeptic. But later she discovered she enjoyed the work. Though she was a woman, she was very good at the “physical” aspects of the job. She enjoyed hurting people, and was one of the only female “enforcers” around. She received the tattoos on her arms, back and chest which signify her membership in The Yakuza. She is also missing the top 2/3 of her left pinky. Yakuza members are often required to cut off their own fingers when they fail their higher ups. This portion of her finger has been replaced with a metal prosthesis.
How Keiko became a wrestler, and how she became indebted to Akira Hagawa, are closely related. There has always been a close relationship between wrestling and gangsters in Japan. One women’s promotion that found itself under the control of Keiko’s employers was in the midst of a re-negotiation with its female stars. Keiko was sent to insure that the women did what was expected of them. Keiko was unable to remain “behind the scenes” and soon found herself in the ring with the leader of the opposition. The women overpowered her and Keiko was at the mercy of 5 women. But Akira Hagawa and her mentor Sasha came to Keiko’s rescue, driving off the good girls. As of that day Keiko was loyal to Akira. She has joined Akira in tag matches and has fought singles matches, mostly against women Akira dislikes. As was the case in her first career, in her wrestling career she is still the “enforcer.” Now she is the enforcer of The Hit Squad Japan, and the dutiful bodyguard of Akira Hagawa.
Finishers: Towerhacker bomb (Argentine Back Breaker Rack Powerbomb: The attacker lifts the victim up so that they are laying across the attacker's shoulders and hooks the victim's neck and leg. The attacker releases the victim's head and swings their body out over the attacker's head and sits down, dropping the victim down to the mat on their back.)
Yakuza Kick (a superkick to the head)
