[Again, Candi Bratton pulls Sissy off her opponent and begins to warn her about her actions. Again, Sissy pushes her away. Only this time, Candi shoves back, sending Landers crashing onto her backside. The crowd roars with laughter.] Evonne: Disqualify the ref! Disqualify the ref! Kenny: That didn't make much sense, even for you, Evonne. But with as much animosity as exists between these two, I don't see Candi calling for a DQ. [As Sissy gets up and starts to advance on Bratton, she ends up on the receiving end of a spear from the world champion. Huge pop.] Kenny: And Lindsay Locke back on the offensive. [As the two topple to the mat, Lindsay lands atop Sissy, straddling her at the waist, firing punch after punch into her face.] Kenny: An uncharacteristically physical approach from Lindsay Locke. Evonne: She wants to skin Sissy alive. You don't really achieve that effect with hurricaranas, now do you? [Lindsay climbs to her feet, pulling Sissy up with her. Evonne: I'd pay real money if someone would shut them up. [Gripping Sissy's wrist, Lindsay whips her across the ring, into the opposite ropes, catching her on the rebound...] Kenny: Spinebuster! [Both women bounce on impact, Lindsay practically bouncing all the way back to her feet. Flopping like a fish out of water, Lindsay rolls off her opponent and toward the ropes.] Kenny: Fast thinking from Sissy Landers... how often did you ever think you'd hear yourself saying that? [Rising a touch unsteadily, Sissy advances on Lindsay Locke, pulling her to her hands and knees by the hair and guiding her to the ropes. Holding her neck above the middle rope, Sissy leans down with all her weight pressing Locke's neck into the ropes. Lindsay's eyes bulge, her feet kick frantically as Candi begins to administer the five count.] Evonne: Things not looking too good for your future in-law there. Kenny: Um, er... [On the count of four, Sissy backs away. As Lindsay wheezes, draped across the middle rope, Sissy breaks into a sprint toward the ropes behind her. Rebounding off them, she charges back at Locke. Leaving her feet, she sails through the air, crashing with her legs straddling either side of Lindsay's head, straddling her at the shoulders. Gripping the top rope, she slams her body weight down repeatedly onto Lindsay's back.] Kenny: Candi Bratton again looking to break these two up. Evonne: Party pooper. [Again, on the count of four, Sissy extricates herself from her rival, Locke sliding off the middle rope and collapsing in a heap at Sissy's feet.] Evonne: You want to start polishing Sissy's new belt, Kenneth? Kenny: This match isn't over yet. [Reaching down, Sissy entwines her fingers in Lindsay's hair, pulling the gasping blonde to her feet. She whips Lindsay into the ropes, catching her on the rebound with a flying forearm shot. Lindsay drops like a ton of bricks.] Kenny: And Sissy going for the cover. ONE... TWO... THR... Kenny: Kickout by Lindsay Locke! Evonne: Delaying the inevitable. [Slapping the mat in frustration, Sissy hops back to her feet, pulling Lindsay up with her. Backing her into the nearest corner, Sissy climbs onto the middle buckle, facing Lindsay, and begins firing punches into her face.] Kenny: Both of these women have strong scientific backgrounds, but I can't say I'm surprised that this has been more a brawl than anything else. Evonne: Considering the feelings between these two, if you expected anything else, you were only deluding yourselves. [In spite of Sissy's betrayal of them, the crowd cannot resist instinctively counting along.] FOUR... FIVE... SIX... SEVEN... EIGHT... [Before they can reach nine, Lindsay's arms wrap around Sissy's waist, and pulling her feet off the ropes, Locke takes three steps out of the corner, the crowd roaring as Sissy frantically shakes her head.] Kenny: Lindsay looks like she's hand enough! [After holding Sissy aloft for a few seconds, Lindsay drop to one knee, slamming Sissy's pelvis down into her outstretched thigh. Sissy springs upright, bouncing on her feet, eyes closed, mouth rounded into a silent oh. Lindsay, though, waist no time, thrusting herself off the mat and leveling Sissy with a short arm clothesline.] Kenny: And Lindsay with a cover! ONE... TWO... THRE... Evonne: No! Sissy's still in this thing! [Lindsay is quickly back to her feet. Pulling Sissy up, she backs Landers into the ropes...] Kenny: Irish whip coming up... Evonne: But Sissy reverses... Kenny: Only to be reversed by Lindsay again! [Placing her hand on Sissy's back, Lindsay pushes her up and over the top rope, sending Landers flying to the concrete below. The crowd pops.] Kenny: And for the first time tonight, this match goes to the outside! [Lindsay backs up to give herself a running start, and as Sissy gets to her feet, Lindsay takes off, leaping over the top rope and crashing onto Sissy Landers, sending the Princess's back slamming into the guardrail.] Kenny: Incoming! Evonne: You gotta wonder, though, with Sissy now Akira's wing, who does this match favor now that it's on the outside? Kenny: Excellent point, Evonne. [Lindsay drags Sissy over to the ringpost, slamming her face hard into the unyielding steel. Sissy screams as the crowd erupts.] Kenny: Looks like Lindsay's trying to do some amateur cosmetic surgery. [Lindsay pulls Sissy's head back, only to repeat the procedure. As she pulls Sissy's head back, a slight stream of blood can be seen beginning to pour from the bridge of her nose.] Kenny: And Sissy's been busted open. Evonne: Barbaric. What kind of role model for our youth is this Locke woman supposed to be? [As Lindsay goes to slam Sissy's face into the post a third time, Landers lifts her leg, blocking the attempt. She then fires an elbow into Locke's wide open midsection, causing Lindsay to lose her grip. Sissy reaches back, grabbing the back of Lindsay's head and slamming her face down into the ringsteps.] Evonne: Look whose coming back, Kenny? [Lindsay staggers away, her hands over her face. Sissy jumps up on to the ringsteps, rushing onto the apron before leaping from the side of the ring, grabbing Locke's head as she flies by, slamming her down with an improvised bulldog. Lindsay lies motionless, face down and spreadeagled as Sissy smiles triumphantly.] Kenny: A savage bulldog from the challenger. I hate to say it, but Lindsay Locke is in a world of trouble. [Standing, Sissy approaches the time keeper's table, grabbing a folding chair and carrying it towards Lindsay, who is shakily starting to rise.] Kenny: Look out, Lindsay! [As Sissy starts to slam the chair down toward Locke's head, Lindsay fires a boot up into Sissy six pack abs, causing the Princess to drop the chair and double over.] Evonne: Curses! [Lindsay reaches forward, tucking Sissy's head under her arm before dropping back, planting the Louisiana girl with a crisp DDT, the top of Sissy's head slamming into the steel chair. Now it's Sissy who lies face down and motionless. Lindsay, meanwhile, sits beside Sissy, leaning against the side of the ring, clearly dazed.] Kenny: Both women certainly beginning to look the worse for wear. [Standing and wobbly legs, Lindsay reaches over to pull Sissy up, and slowly rolls her under the bottom rope and back into the ring.] Evonne: Typical dumb blonde. She can just as easily get the win on the outside.
