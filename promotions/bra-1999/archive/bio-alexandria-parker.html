Being the only child in a wealthy family, you can imagine that Ms.Parker was incredibly spoiled.
No matter what she wanted, she got it, and she was raised with the attitude that she was better than everyone else. This attitude stuck, and is the same way he feels today.
After her training she's had tons of success in many small men's feds. Their she sharpened her strenths and polished her weaknessess. She's alo an active member of BRA's most elite stable 'The Sisterhood of Seduction'.
Her combination of high flying moves, technical wrestling and martial arts skills make hera very dangerous combatant. Alexandria Parker uses her quickness and agility to its utmost, dodging her opponents blows before dropping them to the canvas with a series of martial arts kicks and elbows. She possesses a good technical knowledge of wrestling and her high risk and aerial maneuvers are second to none. While she does lack upper body strength, she tires less easily than most, and she is a very tenacious fighter and will always take the fight to her opponent. She sometimes relies to methods of winning and a quick poke in the eye to send her opponent reeling in confusion . Her sheer viciousness and refusal to quit has one her many prizes in her career.
