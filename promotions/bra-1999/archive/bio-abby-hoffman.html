AbbyHoffman Name: Abby Hoffman Height: 5’7 Weight: 115lbs. of groovy fun Place of Birth: Humbolt County CA Birth- date: 7/15/80(like a total Leo) Background: Abby was born on a commune belonging to Nearly Normal Jesse’s Fantastical, Magical, and Didactical Circus of Wonderment. Her mother was a fortuneteller, and her father was a snake charmer. Abby sort of gravitated to the acrobats though (pun intentional). At the tender age of 10 she was already an integral member of the trapeze/aerial bubble-sculpting act.  On her 17th birthday she was in the middle of a tour just outside of Seattle. In the audience was a strapping young lad, who  caught Abby’s eye. After the show, she tracked him down, and after an evening of birthday festivities Abby took her new friend and a few blankets to express her attraction to him in a physical way, as was the fashion of the time.  The next morning Abbey found that she had overslept, and missed the caravan. Totally bummed her new friend consoled her. He told Abby she could hang with him. The young man’s name was Phoenix Haight, one half of the Great Northwest Alliance of
Wrestling’s (GNAW) tag team champs, Haight/Ashbury.  At first Abby acted as their valet, but soon found herself in the ring, surprising everyone who thought she was just a sweet spaced out hippie by taking the normal valet catfight to the hardcore level. Abby has put more failed fitness models through tables than the ECW and the Internet combined. Although GNAW never could get a woman’s division together, they were able to pit Abby against some of the top indie women out there, and she even stepped into the ring with some of the smaller men.
No one really knows why Abby loves to wrestle, and why she loves to wrestle hardcore. She is a grappler in the style of Mick Foley (albeit much more mobile), and it is truly amazing how much abuse she can take, and still get up and fight. She is a normally sweet, caring person who is totally against violence. Part of it is the "good vibes" she gets from the crowds. Part of it is definitely getting the feeling she is righting many wrongs- proving that women can are just as physically able as men and being a role modal, as well as putting "meanies" in their place.  And it is quite possible she gets a buzz from the pain.
Ring Attire: The Tie-dye crop top and a pair of tie-dyed bikini briefs. For hardcore matches she wears rings on all ten fingers and toes.  Always wrestles barefoot (for comfort, you pervs!)
Abby Hoffman Finisher: The Bad Trip- Top Rope Moonsault through a table
