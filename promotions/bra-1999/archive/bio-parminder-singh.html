ParminderSingh Parminder Singh Name: Parminder Singh Height: 5’6” Weight: 128 pounds Hometown: Cawnpore, India DOB: Oct 28/1972 Parminder enjoys taunting the crowd and her opponents. She likes to use an over the knee backdrop as a setup for her finisher. She will taunt the crowd and her opponent before rolling her off to the mat and finishing with the camel clutch where she will continue to taunt her opponent. She will always release the hold when the bell sounds but when the referee gets up and turns his back she will resume the hold for as long as she can get away with.
Favorite Moves:  Arm drag, top wrist lock, hammerlock, body slam, over the knee back breaker, boot scrape, cobra clutch, double edged knife chop, clothesline, elbow smash, forearm smash. Irish whip, mule kick, leg sweep, monkey flip
Finisher:  Camel clutch Background: Parminder was trained in India in the classical style similar to Tiger Jeet Singh. She came to North America and turned pro six years ago bouncing around the smaller Feds and never gaining a lot of success. She is an experienced pro but her lack of desire to advance herself and her laziness usually led to her usually resorting to cheating to try and win matches. With this attitude she was branded a heel and never garnered a big following, nor cared enough to try and change it. She is a capable wrestler when she chooses to be but will never have the ability to make it big.
Entrance:   “Classic music of India pipes in to the arena heralding the arrival of Parminder Singh. She splits the curtains and stops at the head of the ramp surveying the crowd with her hands on her hips with a look of contempt on her face in response to the negative reaction from the crowd. She walks to the ring exchanging insults with the nearest members of the crowd dressed in a single black one-piece suit and black wrestling boots with a silk cover-up over top. She snarls as she climbs through the ropes and takes her position in her corner.”
