Glinda's Predictions Glinda's Predictions! By Glinda Gossie Glinda's Predictions! Forget the Psychic Hotline! This is Glinda Gossipe' with the real 411 on the exciting ladies of the Battling Ring Angels! Whew! What a pay per view! We saw the crowning of our first Television champion as well as the debut of IFWF superstar Evonne Carmikel! And that's just a small amount of what was seen at this awesome event! For any of you poor souls that missed the ppv, it is still available on replays throughout this week. I strongly advise you to order it and see this great card! On the heels of the ppv, we have a great card coming up this Monday! And I, the ever gorgeous Glinda Gossipe', am here with my expert analysis!
Tiffany Lane vs Brynn Kennedy: Brynn comes off of an impressive win, over her aunt
Princess Peg at the PPV, while Tiffany, the self-proclaimed "Miss
BRA", comes off of an impressive beat down at the hands of The Body Shop! Brynn has the momentum but you can't count Tiffany out. That girl is full of dirty tricks and even cheaper tactics. I'm sure that she'll do everything in her power to win this one. Plus, where ever you find Tiffany, you are sure to see her tag partner "The Goddess" Nina Larue. I see Tiffany winning this one with help from Nina and the other members of The Sisterhood of
Seduction! Jenny "The Cheerleader" Anderson vs Meagan Delanoire: Jenny was beaten and humiliated by Alexandria Parker at the PPV so you can expect her to try to avenge herself against Meagan. Plus, Jenny isn't afraid to fight dirty. But, then again, neither is Meagan! I'm expecting to see Meagan, who has more experience fghting dirty, win this match in a close batttle.
Lil' Alexandra vs Tina Dream: This is Alexandra's debut while Tina comes off of an impressive showing in the 8 woman elimination battle. I think we can expect to see Tina take this match.
Kelly "The Assassin" Mase vs Tiffany Epitaphine: Hmm. This is a toughie. Both of these women are strong competitors with great skills. But I think Mase is going to defeat Epitaphine. Kelly Mase, after nearly killing
Viper at the PPV, has shown herself to be a ruthless wrestler with no morals.
I think that she's going to use everything at her disposal to beat Tiffany and maybe try to end her career!
Marquise Yvette Desade vs Princess Peggy Christian: I think that Yvette, after losing out on the Celestial title, will be looking for a way to prove herself. And what better way to revenge herself against Sherry Ann than to destroy The
Body Shop's newest member? Peggy may be a talented grappler but she will be no match for Yvette's savagery and wickedness!
Rebecca Case vs Miranda Ellis: This will be Miranda's debut as we saw Rebecca in action previously. I think that Rebecca will surprise her fellow rookie with a victory.
Triple Threat: Alexandria Parker vs Americana vs Samantha Page: Each of these women has held gold, in other feds, and each of these women will be vying for a shot at the television champ. My money's on Alexandria Parker. She's the wild card in this match. Both Samantha and Americana are fan faves who play to the fans while Alexandria is known for her cunning and guile. She will do anything to reach the top and that attitude will help her greatly in this bout!
Kayla Vandergriff vs Lori Wilson: Two tough grapplers but I see Lori winning this one. She has been on quite a streak, as of late, and I see her pulling a surprise victory over the Ab- Dominator.
I guess you also want to know who I think the teams should be to face The Babe
Squad? Why, I never thought you would ask! Glinda's 3 choices (in no particular order):
The Gothic Princesses (Kayla Vandergriff and Lisa Dream) The Super Heroines (Mistress Catwoman and "Lady Lightning" Lori Wilson) Smoldering Rage (Meagan Delanoire and Brynn Kennedy) Well, gotta go. Until next time, this has been Glinda Gossipe' with Glinda's Predictions! Kisses and Hugs, Glinda
