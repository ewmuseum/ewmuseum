GloriaEscudero Gloria Escudero Name: Gloria Escudero Height: 5’1 Weight: 103 lbs Hometown: Quezon City, Philippines Physical appearance Gloria is small and slight with jet-black hair just past her shoulders. Ring attire She wears a blue one-piece swimsuit, white tights and white trainers. Entrance An unknown but catchy salsa beat fills the air as the house lights go up full heralding the arrival of tiny Philippine grappler Gloria Escudero. She walks through the curtains and casts a quick glance either side at the crowd as she walks down the ramp to the ring. Gloria rolls under the ropes and to her corner where she starts light stretching before the match.
Why is she unique? She is the second wrestler from the Philippines to appear in the BRA, the other being her tag team partner.
Attitude Her attitude is pretty neutral compared to her tag partner, though she is self centered and really does not care for anything American.
Experience Gloria worked the same catfight company in Manila as Lonnie Yambao. She came to America at the same time but joined BRA slightly later after several years as a catfighter/wrestler.
Strengths and weaknesses Gloria is another who fools people by her small size and frail appearance. She is a brutal and very aggressive fighter who backs down from nobody. She does have a lot of experience in female-to-female combat and has learned an extensive array of wrestling moves, though she still favors a brawling approach to her matches.
Summary of experience Gloria was born to a large family in a poor neighborhood in Quezon City in 1975. She grew up without a lot of things most people consider essentials. Her fighting spirit was recognized one day fighting with a much larger woman (and beating the tar out of her!) for food. She went to work for the same catfight film company as Lonnie Yambao and eked out a living fighting on film. She is a very scrappy fighter and joined BRA at the urging of her compatriot.
Moves Headlock, forearm smashes, punches, kicks, eye rakes, claws, snap mares, dropkicks, karate style chops, arm bars, camel clutch, Boston crab, hammerlock, tombstone piledriver, kneelifts, hip toss.
Gloria Escudero, Nov 14\1975, 5’1, 103 lbs, small and wiry, Philippine, laissez faire, catfighter/brawler, heel.
