SheaLondon Shea London Name: Shea London Height: 5'4" Weight: 120 Date of Birth: 01/14/78 Actual Hometown: Manchester, England Hometown as announced when wrestling: Manchester, England Physical Appearance: Short and athletic–not particularly muscular, but clearly fit.  She has long
dirty blonde hair and round brown eyes. Ring Attire: A red one-piece, with the Union Jack depicted on the front. Red boots. Finisher: The Sensational Leg Drop - a leg drop performed from the top rope across her opponent's neck.
Background: Shea first came onto the wrestling scene in late 1997, joining Cherry Bomb Pro Wrestling, an organization based out of Japan.  The fed closed up shop before Shea could compete, however.  Months later, in early 1998, Shea hit the big time, landing deals with both the IEWA and WSIW.  During her WSIW career, she scored several impressive wins in the singles division–including one over eventual champion Kasume Nanase.  Her IEWA reign saw her earn a shot at the Skyweight title, though that match resulted in Sonja and Sarah Victory severely injuring her ankle.  That injury, coupled with the collapse of WSIW, lead Shea into temporary retirement.  After extensive rehabilitation, she signed with the Battling Ring Angels.  In her first tenure with the fed, she became a contender in both the singles and tag team ranks (along with Tara Sawyer), coming within inches of winning the BRATT titles on no less than three occasions.  However, escalating tensions between herself and Tara, plus a growing obsession with Jennifer Christian (in spite of their never having a formal match against each other) lead her again down the path of inactivity.  Now, rededicated to her profession, she has sought one final chance to prove herself.
