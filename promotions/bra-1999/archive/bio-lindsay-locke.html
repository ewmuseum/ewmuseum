LindsayLocke Lindsay Locke [No Photo Available] Name: Lindsay Locke DOB: 05/23/78 Hometown: Los Angeles, California Height: 5' 6" Weight: 132 lbs. Wrestling Attire: Blue Spandex shorts, with silver lightning bolts on them, and the word "Lightning" on the back in silver letters a silver sports bra style top, and blue boots with silver lightning bolts on them. Blue knee pads with silver bolts on them.
Finisher: The Lightning Strike: Lindsay lays her opponent on her back next to the corner. Lindsay then climbs the turnbuckle and hold both her hands above her head in a "V". When she feels the fans are properly excited she performs a front flip off the top rope and drives her elbow into the chest of her opponent. Think of it as an elbow drop with a twist.
History: Lindsay grew up in a middle class family in Los Angeles. She was an athletic youngster, participating in volleyball and soccer in High School. Being a smart and talented girl, she attended Princeton. But when her father lost his job due to corporate downsizing she was forced to leave college and fend for herself. She worked in a number of other jobs, such as waitress, topless dancer (which she found degrading), and secretary. It was at this job that she ran afoul of one of her co-workers and wound up in a fight with the woman. Lindsay won the fight and decided that maybe she could make a living as a fighter. Her first encounters in the sport ended in her getting her butt extremely kicked. When she was just about to give it up she was seen by a well known Mexican trainer, who taught her the high flying techniques of Lucha Libre. With these new skills, and never being a quitter, she set out to try her hand at wrestling again. She has been successfull on the independant circuit and is ready for "the big leagues".
Lindsay is a fitness nut and keeps her body in excellent shape. She also keeps her mind in good shape by reading up on the science and sport of wrestling. She trains constantly and never quits.
She is a two time BRA Celestial Champion and head of The Locke Family, with her cousins CJ and Michelle (known as Mixie).
