------------------------------------------------------- Akira Hagawa ------------------------------------------------------- A black screen. The slow pounding of Tycho Drums begins to well up slowly in the background. Building. Building. Growing. The sounds of warriors. The call to arms of the Samurai. It starts to pound. A figure appears on the screen. The figure of a woman. But more than she is a woman, she is a force of nature. She is a machine. She walks perfectly. To say she walks is almost an understatement. She moves. At once fluid and liquid. The dark figure, only a silhouette, it approaches the screen. With each step, the drums grow louder. For the first time, a voice speaks to us. A man’s voice. Deep. Cool. Authoritative. "This Fall...a storm is coming." She just keeps walking as the drums pound on. Louder. Fiercer. A warrior’s chant echoes behind the walking woman. Upright. Strong. Her hair jangles only slightly as she moves towards. A cat. A creature. A force of nature. She is perfection in motion. "This Fall...the wind will once again blow into BRA." She shakes her head. Her hair dances to the tune of the Warrior’s shrieking call. The drums pound. Her steps are quick and light. Almost in tune with the calling drums. She seems to be stalking you. Relentless in her approach and unwavering in her resolve. "She knows your weakness." The drumming grows. "She knows your fears." She just keeps coming. She is power. She is grace.
