MixieLocke Mixie Locke Name of my wrestler – Mixie Locke Basic Strategy –Mixie prides herself on being “The hardest working girl in Wrestling.” She’s a road warrior, traveling the Indy circuit and going from paycheck to paycheck. She wants to have a home in BRA, and thinks the best way to find one is to win. Sooo she won’t give up or back down. Basically, she’ll fight a good match and do what she can to win. Without cheating, of course.
There is a working class element to her that translates into her wrestling. She isn’t flashy with her entrance or in her dress. She is just a meat and potatoes wrestler. She doesn’t try to win the fans over by playing to them in the ring. She wants to impress them with her wrestling and get them to support her by being a tough competitor and a never give up performer. She doesn’t talk big, she just fights.
How Mixie makes her entrance: “Intergalactic” from The Beastie Boys starts to play throughout the arena. Mixie Locke appears at the ramp. The crowd gives a good cheer for this popular newcomer. Mixie has a very intense and focused look on her face as she walks briskly towards the ring. She slaps hands with the fans at the rail, making sure to get as many of them as possible on the way. The toned and tanned athletic young woman wears a pair of black spandex shorts with the words “All Mixed Up” on the back in red letters. She wears black wrestling boots with the letters “ML” on the top in red letters and red knee pads. She wears a white T shirt that says “Mixie Locke: The Hardest Working Girl In Wrestling” on the front in blue letters. Beneath the words is a South Park style drawing of Mixie. On the back it says “Mixie Locke: Not Just Another Jobber World Tour 2000” and beneath that a variety of cities where she has wrestled and dates next to each one. Beneath the T she wears a black spandex sports bra style top. She has two ear rings in her left ear, and three in her right. Her belly button is pierced as well, as shown by the slightly too short T shirt. When she gets to ringside, she takes a good look at the ring and the arena, taking it all in. She enjoys the crowd’s support for a moments before she climbs to the ring apron and to the ropes.
She enters the ring and sticks her tongue out at her opponent, showing off the stud in her tongue. She pauses for the referee to check her for any foreign objects, and stretches in the corner waiting for the start of the match.
Detailed idea of what Mixie will do in this match: Mixie’s style is a lot like Lindsay. They are very close in size and ability. They are both very athletic and in great shape. They use similar tactics. This is an idea of what Mixie will look to do in the match.
Mixie has a 4 part plan for the match. She will start with her speed, making her opponent pursue her. This will tire the less fit her opponent. Next, Mixie will come out with some quick strikes…still keeping her distance. This will make her opponent wonder where Mixie is coming from and what she has planned. Once she has her opponent worn down and agitated, she will go after Her opponent’s  legs…taking her down and controlling her movement. Last, Mixie will go the ropes and deliver her trademark aerial moves on the prone opponent.
The three words that best describe a Mixie Locke match are: Athletic; exciting; high energy. Mixie will use her superior speed and physical conditioning to outlast her opponent.  Mixie’s #1 weapon is her body and her ability to throw it at opponents. She moves quickly at first, trying not to get in protracted brawls with opponents. She is well conditioned and can maintain this pace. When she senses her opponent is tiring of the fast pace, she starts to move in. She wants to ground her opponent. Once she’s accomplished this, she goes to the ropes and delivers her aerial assault. Mixie cannot allow her opponent to ground her…so she must be fast paced from the opening bell. Employing quick strikes early…like dropkicks and clotheslines. Once she has a handle on her opponent’s movement, she’ll open up on her. Basically 1) frustrate her, 2) confuse her, 3) ground her and lastly,4) bombs away! Mixie is not a cheater or a rule breaker. She listens to everything the ref says and never, ever, will use a shortcut to win.
Moves: Regular/technical: Knife edge chop, kick, leg drop, headlock, front face lock, spinning toe hold, elbow smash, corner splash, running lariat, drop kick, standing surfboard, abdominal stretch, spinning heel kick, Lou Thesz press (followed by punches to the head when opponent is down).
Power: Belly to belly/belly to back/rolling German/Northern Lights suplexes, DDT, spinning neck breaker, cradle piledriver, jawbreaker, Rikishi driver, DVD, X-factor. Finisher: All Mixed Up (a brainbuster suplex).
Aerial: Frog splash, corkscrew dive, missile dropkick, tornado DDT, hurricanrana, suicide plancha, shooting star press, guillotine leg drop, diving headbutt, high cross body, somersault dive, flying elbow, moonsault, flying forearm, firebird Splash (450 degree splash), tumbleweed. Senton.
Humiliation: Mixie never cheats or humiliates an opponent. She never says anything. She is focused and intent. A true competitor.
