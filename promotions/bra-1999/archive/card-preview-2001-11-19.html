Battling Ring Angels: Card Preview, 2001-11-19

19 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens inside the Battling Ring Angels control room. Jenna stands against a row of monitors.) Jenna: 'Hello everyone! Wow! What a crazy week we've had here at Battling Ring Angels. With new ownership comes a few new problems as Delilah Strain's people begin to implement some new policies. We even had to delay this broadcast, so that everything was run in accordance with our new bosses rules.' (A shot of the remaining wrestlers in the Featherweight Tournament appear.) Jenna: "Well, its down to 4 matches, to decide this championship and folks, what a number of contenders we have left. Lets look at the line up!" Jennifer Christian vs CJ Locke! Wow! Can Jenn get out of her slump? If she's not at the top of her game, CJ will blaze right through her. Abby Hoffman vs Shea London! The rookie vs the veteran. This one's a close call folks. Abby has been on a tear lately. But the new an improved Shea London might be too much for the youngster. Tiffany Lane vs Katie McClaine. I like Katie, she's got a bright future in this sport. But facing someone like Tiffany, in a quarter final? I think the Bombshell will win this one. Sissy Landers vs Malibu. Tough call. The vicious and seductive Malibu is a dangerous opponent. Anyone that can take out Lindsey Locke for 2 months needs to be watched carefully and Sissy's attention span lasts about as long as a plate of Peggy's cookies at a fat farm. Still, Sissy is unfazed by Malibu's viciousness and if she hits the Sissy Splash, she'll be going through. (Insert shot of Mixie and Maxine) Jenna: "Talk about a strange pairing. Is this BRA's newest tag team? Two women who started off hating each other and now, best buds? Stranger things have happened. And strange things will happen as Mixie gets a chance to get some revenge for her cousin. Maxine and Mixie vs The Heavenly Bodies Jenna: "Delilah's hand chosen tag team has targetted young CJ Locke over the past few weeks. Now lets see what happenens to this 'Bad' girls when they fast 180 pounds of walking graveyard destruction and a Locke with trouble on her mind." (Insert a shot of Alex Parker and Casey Gaines.) Jenna: "In a one on one contest. Alexandria Parker takes on fan favourite Casey Gaines. With the Sisterhood reunited, Alex becomes even more dangerous, but Gaines pulls out the wins when the odds are stacked against her. This one is too close to call. (A shot of Candi Bratton and Shannon Fitz appear) Jenna: "And finally, we, along with Akira Hagawa get to see who she will face at the Naughty or Nice PPV in December. This is going to be one heck of a fight. The last time these two met, Candi whipped Fitz into the canvas. I'm sure the Fixer will be out for blood and you have to know that East/West will be lurking in the wings. Akira would love nothing more than to have a member of her crew challenge her for the title at the PPV. It would guarantee her another victory." (A shot of the Celestial Title and Tag Titles appears.) Jenna: "But folks, we've got important news for you. Its unofficial at the moment, but our sources tell us that Akira and East/West will have to put their title up for grabs before the PPV. With no matches for them on this card, that means in two weeks, we could have new champions going into the PPV!" (The image returns to the control room.) Jenna "Well, that's all the time we have tonight. We'll see you ring side Monday night!" (fade to black) *******OOC********* Sorry about the late posting of this card. Writers will be notified. Quick, short strats will be accepted up until saturday night. Here are the matches again Featherweights Jenn vs CJ Abby vs Shea Katie vs Tiffany Sissy vs Malibu Alex Parker vs Casey Gaines M&M vs Heavenly Bodies Candi vs Shannan for the #1 contender spot.
