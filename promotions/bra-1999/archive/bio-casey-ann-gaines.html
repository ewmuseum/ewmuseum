CaseyAnnGaines Casey Ann Gaines Name: Casey-Ann Gaines Height: 5 ft. 7 in. Weight: 135 pounds Hometown: Bath, Maine Date of Birth: 4/8/75 Finisher: The Net Gaines – A pumphandle slam. Description: Tough and a little beaten up. Blond hair in ‘corn rows’ – a bunch of braids. Strong physical condition and great muscle tone. All business, she doesn’t play around or try to win the crowd over.
Background: There’s not much to say about Casey. She was a local tough girl in Bath, Maine. She worked hard to get herself a life outside of her small town. She worked as a maid in a hotel for seasonal residents and fought in pit fights and club fights at night. Somewhere along the line, at the age of 19, she met a man and he got her pregnant. When she had the baby – a girl named Vanessa – the man took off leaving her to care for the girl. It was a few years later that Casey made enemies with some rich girls. The girls framed her for the theft of some money and Casey was sentenced to five years in prison. She was released and started wrestling as a way to pay for her daughter’s living expenses.
Casey is a tough and hard girl. Her time in prison made her a lot tougher than most girls her age. She has been beaten, broken and stabbed. But she always finds a way to come back. She is not afraid of hardcore and extreme matches.
Casey is devoted to her daughter Vanessa. She will do anything to make sure the young girl’s life is not hard like hers was. The only reason she fights is to make enough money to care for Vanessa. She is motivated by the thought that a loss means less money to care for Vanessa. Fame and fortune doesn’t matter to her.
