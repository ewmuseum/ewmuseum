East / West Members: Kimiko “The Eastern Star” Yamashida and Kimberly West Team Alignment: Heel Wins: Losses: Status: Active Description: Kimiko is a five foot seven, 138 pound Japanese woman. Born April 16, 1980 in Kyoto, Japan. She is a natural athlete and the daughter of a well known Japanese wrestler. Kimberly is five foot nine and 140 pounds of sculpted femininity. She was born on January 23, 1980. She hails from Cherry Hill, New Jersey and has light brown hair and a more sleazy look than her more refined partner. She is a former member of the Rutgers University Dance Team and is very flexible.
Combined Weight: 278 lbs Entrance music: "Another One Bites the Dust" by Wyclef Jean History: As a team, Kimi and Kim complement each other very well. Kim is the risk taker and high flyer while Kimi is more of a technical wrestler. They like to tag in and out quickly and stay fresh, while giving their opponents a lot to think about. They have been working together for a long time, in Japan and in the United States, and understand exactly how to succeed as a unit. They seem to have a mental bond. One can tell when the other is tired or in need of a tag. They can usually figure out quickly which one matches up against which member of the other team and use that to their advantage.
They are free spirited and very rarely follow what they consider to be other people’s rules. Society’s norms and social standards don’t mean much to them. They say and do what they want. Most people think they’re cocky and brash…and they are. They’re also arrogant, childish, offensive and lacking in almost any social grace. But they are fantastic in the ring.
They stick up for each other in and out of the ring. They are adrenaline junkies who live fast and take risks. They like to get right in the thick of things and go after anyone in their way. When no one’s in their way, they find someone they can put there. They don’t look for trouble…they find it. Their slogan is “We’re in the business of hurting people, and business is good!” That pretty much sums them up.
