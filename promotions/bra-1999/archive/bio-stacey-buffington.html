StaceyBuffington Stacey Buffington Name: ‘The Perfect Ten’ Stacey Buffington Height: 5 ft. 7 in Weight: 135 pounds DOB: April 11, 1981 Hometown: Reading, Pennsylvania Description: Stacey is a strong and athletic young woman. She has reddish brown hair and hazel eyes. She is known as ‘The Perfect Ten’ because she has a fantastic body, formed through countless hours in the gym. In fact, when she was an Indy wrestler she was voted ‘Best Body’ on the Indy circuit. She’s not a body builder, nor does she have huge muscles. Rather, she has an athletic build with six pack abdominal muscles, solid biceps and perfectly formed quads and calves.
Her personal style runs toward what you might call ‘New Jersey hip.’ Her hair is a little big and has an 80’s look to it. Her clothes are usually what you might find on a girl in a New Jersey shopping mall. She speaks with a Central Pennsylvania accent and doesn’t show the greatest amount of refinement. She’s a diamond in the rough.
She can act cocky and a little self-assured. This is a product of her physical conditioning and her success on the Indy circuit.
Finisher: The Ten Plex – A Belly To Belly Suplex Background: Stacey Buffington grew up in the steel town of Reading, Pennsylvania. She wasn’t born into a wealthy family. Her father was a mill worker and her mother worked in the local grocery store. This family situation was not much different than that of any other young girl in the area and she was popular growing up. Stacey enjoyed a normal childhood and teenage years. But when she turned 15 her mother died in a car accident. Having no brothers or sisters she didn’t have much family. The only one she had was her father. They didn’t have much in common. What they found in common was wrestling. They watched it on TV and bonded over it. Since he was a former PA state champion, Stacey’s father decided to teach his daughter a thing or two about amateur wrestling. Stacey excelled at it and by the time she was a senior she was the only female member of the High School team. Right before graduation she completed her father’s dream for her – she won the state championship in her weight class.
After graduation Stacey decided to stick with wrestling. Her constant devotion to physical fitness and training got her a try out with a New Jersey based federation that promoted in her area. Her good looks, solid body and amateur proficiency made her an instant hit. After only 4 months in the federation she won its TV Title. After a year she won its Regional Title. She was popular with male fans that liked her looks, and with female fans who appreciated her attitude. ‘The Perfect Ten’ was riding high. It seemed like the ‘big time’ was just around the corner.
The big time finally came calling. She was asked by Akira Hagawa to audition for her new Wrestling School. Stacey went into it with her usual attitude, sure that she’d impress everyone with her skill and talent. What she didn’t know was that Akira had other plans. Akira beat and humiliated Stacey before turning her over to East-West for more beating and humiliation. She was left broken and beaten. It was the lowest day of her life. The only thing that saved her from her career being ended was Casey Gaines coming to her rescue.
Stacey sought to rebuild the career she almost lost that day and to get some revenge on Akira. With the help of Casey she was signed to Bra.
