AkiraHagawa "The Killing Wind" Akira Hagawa Name: The Killing Wind Akira Hagawa Height: 5’6” Weight: 135 Date of Birth: 11/14/73 Actual Hometown:  Tokyo, Japan Hometown as announced when wrestling:  Tokyo, Japan Finisher: “Dark Dream” (Dragon Sleeper) Physical Appearance: Strong and athletically built Japanese woman with long black hair and brown eyes. She is not huge or overpowering, but she has a presence in her physicality. She is a creepy presence.
Ring Attire: an outfit consisting of loose, shiny black vinyl pants, adorned with red “twisters”  and tied with a red belt. She wears red wrestling boots. She usually wears a Tee shirt advertising her membership in The Hit Squad Japan. It is a black shirt with Japanese writing on the front, and “Hit Squad Japan” on the back
The Killing Wind Akira Hagawa is the daughter of a wealthy Tokyo businessman and his wife, the daughter of a wealthy Japanese industrial family. She was raised in the lap of luxury and was never deprived of anything. Her father’s Taxi company was the most prosperous in Tokyo and he was able to provide her with whatever her heart desired. Mr. Hagawa had hoped for sons, but his wife was only capable to give birth to one child…Akira. So he spoiled her. But beneath it all, he secretly hated the fact that he would never have a male heir. To compensate, he trained Akira in Judo and Karate. He taught her to fight, as he would have a son. He taught her to defend herself, and to attack when she had to. He pushed her interests in athletics and in business, preparing her to someday take over his business. He also took her to Japanese Pro-Wrestling matches as much as possible. She loved it. She immediately took an interest in it and her father provided her with the training she would need to excel. He retained the top Japanese pros to teach her.
But much to his dismay, it seemed that Akira’s interests were in the so-called “Garbage” wrestling matches. She loved the sheer brutality of the bloody and violent hardcore matches. She had always had a sadistic side, which was encouraged by her father’s desire to make her a “win at all cost” businesswoman, but even he was shocked at her blood lust. Akira was hooked, and she joined up with a female wrestling federation. She immediately took to the hardcore matches. She was the only rookie entrant in the federation’s “Queen of the Death Match” tournament. No one gave her much or a chance, but she made it to the championship round. It was there that she faced the American star Sasha Crowley, known as “The Dragon Lady.” Sasha was a top American in the Federation, and was known as an insane competitor. It was rumored that she had pushed her own sister to commit suicide in revenge for some unknown slight. Sasha defeated Akira in one of the most brutal matches in federation history. But the business-like attitude and lack of emotion that Akira showed impressed Sasha.
Sasha was a teacher of sorts. She had made her apprentice (a horribly burnt loner named Janus) into a hardcore star. She decided to do the same for Akira. She gave Akira the ring-name “The Killing Wind”, in reference to her swift and decisive submission moves. But unlike Janus, Akira didn’t have rage to motivate her. She wasn’t angry at the world for her scars or burnt face. Her stare was cold and her face never showed any emotion. To her, bloodletting was business. She hated to lose. And she relished the opportunity to bleed an opponent. But she never lost sight of the fact that it was all a paycheck. Her father had taught her never to let her emotions get in the way of business, and she never did.
Sasha had a stable called “The Hit Squad.” The purpose of this stable was to enforce the will of any wrestler who paid them enough. They were muscle for hire. Janus and Sasha’s bodyguard and former tag partner Brenda Busley made up the muscle of the team.  Akira provided the business sense as well as muscle. The Hit Squad flourished in Japan. But cracks began to occur. It culminated in a vicious match between Sasha and Janus. Akira stuck with Sasha and ran The Hit Squad while Sasha was in a self imposed exile. She added new members and was regarded as the leader of the Hit Squad Japan.
The legend of “The Killing Wind” grew as leader of The Hit Squad Japan. Wrestlers were known to offer protestations, shed tears, fake injuries, and, in the case of one young rookie, wet their pants with fear at the realization that she was their next opponent. Even top veterans and stars balked at the possibility of facing her. It was a known fact that she made her opponents submit. That she would stop at nothing to win. She was one of the most feared women in the sport. She once won a title because her opponent refused to show up and fight her.
The Killing Wind has blown into the United States with the intention of spreading her reputation and finding new victims, er, opponents.
