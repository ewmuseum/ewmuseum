Malibu Malibu Name: Malibu Height: 5'8" Weight: 126lbs Date of Birth: July 3, 1976 Actual Hometown: Carlisle, PA Hometown as announced when wrestling: Long Beach, CA Physical Appearance: Malibu has a lean, athletic physique while retaining a busty image. Her bleached-blonde hair is slightly curled, just past shoulder length. Brown eyes give the first hint of a pleasant, wide mouthed smile. Her skin is decently tanned and her makeup usually consists of a medium red-shade lipstick combined with a light blue, almost silver eyeshadow.
It's true that she has lost around 10 pounds since her last tenure in BRA. Ring Attire: Malibu wears a red, one-piece, lifeguard style swimsuit . The back is also cut in a "brazil" style. In addition, she also wears nude colored tights/hose to give her legs that extra "push". The swimsuit reveals her ample cleavage as well as the fact that the back has to be "adjusted" regularly. She also wears red wrestling boots with white laces.
Malibu is the daughter of corporate tycoon Corrigan St. John.  Growing up, Tamie St. John (Malibu) never had to hold a job as long as she maintained her 4.0 GPA. Upon graduation, Tamie went to college and took a lifeguard position to take up some of the extra time. She went to college with "Wingman" Shane Tatum, who eventually went on to wrestle in independent circuits. Tamie attended a few of the events that Shane wrestled in, and found a little interest in becoming a wrestler, herself. She began training at the small gym that Shane trained at, and has had a couple of matches in the last year. Matches that provide very little pay, and get very little recognition. Unpleased, Tamie had been in contact with Battling Ring Angels, and was granted a try out with them. Eager to get things underway, Tamie looked forward to her future in BRA, as Malibu, and dreams of one day becoming the BRA Champion and wearing the belt around her waist.
However, everything fell apart all at once. Tamie received a call one afternoon from her displeased mother. After an argument, Myra St. John decided that Tamie would be cut off from the family funds. Only a matter of days later, Tamie would virtually explode in the BRA offices – and found herself cleaning out her locker with tears streaming down her face. This, of course, was not televised or made into big news; but some of the other wrestlers were disappointed to see her go. Some of them laughed as Tamie retrieved her belongings from the locker.
Four months went by, and since BRA owned the copyrights to the character Malibu, Tamie had been working the independent circuits as “Aimee St. John”. Needless to say, this left much to be desired, as she had no luck in getting “over” with fans at independent shows.  After four months since her firing, Malibu has come back to the BRA Offices in an attempt to plea for her job.  Apparently, the Archangel is inclined to enjoy a little (okay, maybe a lot) brown-nosing, and Malibu is back in business.
However, duing the BRA Pay Per View in August, 2001, Malibu turned her back on her tag team partner, Lindsay Locke, and showed her true colors as she and the tag team of East/West pummeled Lindsay. Malibu ended the match by securing Lindsay's arm in a steel chair and then jumping on it, breaking her enemy's arm.
Finisher: "Ozone" - Malibu locks the opponent's head underneath her arm as if she were going to perform a suplex. Then, as she has the opponent halfway lifted, she drives the opponent's head into the mat, making the move a thunderous DDT.
