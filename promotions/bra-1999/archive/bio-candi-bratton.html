CandiBratton "Tattle Tail" Candi Bratton Name: "Tattle Tail" Candi Bratton Date of Birth – August 26, 1964 Hometown – Bovill, ID Height – 5 ft, 8 in Weight - 188 pounds [No Photo Available] Physical description – One word describes Candi Bratton.  Big.  While the fans usually chant, “Candi’s Fat!”, in truth, she’s just a big girl.  She is probably overweight, but her stocky disposition tends to hide that.  There’s no way around the fact that Candi Bratton could stand to lose a few pounds or 20.  She’s plain jane, with her long black hair, and her cute face, is a little chubby.
Finishing move – “The Confessional” – Cross Face Chicken Wing Submission Candi grew up in the logging community of Bovill, Idaho.  She’s the daughter of a lumberjack and she learned the trade at a young age.  It built her body into the stocky person she is today.  However, she married  young and never went into the business.  She had four children, then her husband ran off with her best friend, leaving her to take care of the children all by herself.
She took odd jobs to try and support her children, however she could never make enough money to get ahead of the game.  In fact, each month she found she was going deeper in debt.
As she struggled for financial freedom, she met old time, and now retired wrestler, Jack “The Ox” Carter who took her under his wing and began to train the distraught mother.  He taught her all aspects of the game, including how to create heat.  He made her watch his old matches on tape and she totally enjoyed the heel character that he played.  She trained in the ring and he taught her many things, molding her into an old-school wrestler.
He took her to nearby local small time cards and she was able to perfect the craft inside the ring against various opponents.  Secretly, Jack was video taping her matches and compiling a documentary of her work.  When he felt she was ready, he secretly mailed the tape to the promoters of the IEWA and the GDWA.  She was called a few weeks later and offered contracts to both federations.  She accepted both contracts and began to compete at an international level.
As the money began pouring in, Candi was able to pull herself out of debt and set aside money for her retirement, as she knew that at her age, she would not be wrestling long.  Candi drew monster heat in both federations and remained unpinned in the IEWA and it was almost a year before she was pinned by the GDWA World Champion in that federation.  In fact, in 1998 (her first year wrestling), Candi was voted #23 in the RSPWF’s Top 150 Women Wrestlers.
After the IEWA closed, Candi wrestled exclusively for the GDWA, until she began having conflicts with the management and promoters.  She caused them so much grief that she was eventually fired. Candi took her earnings and retired quietly in Bovill.  However, she always felt that urge to return to active competition.
As she watched wrestling from Bovill, she came across BRA on television and was immediately hooked.  She got familiar with all the characters and finally send a tape to the promoters of her matches in the GDWA and the IEWA.  They immediately mailed her back a contract, which she signed and made her way back to the big time.
