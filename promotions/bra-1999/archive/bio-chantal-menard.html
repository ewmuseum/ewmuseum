ChantalMenard Chantal Menard Name: Chantal Menard Height: 5'6 Weight: 123 lbs DOB: Jan 30/1981 Hometown: Montreal, Canada Chantal is average height and slender of build with a medium complexion. She has straight brown hair that comes down to approximately half way down her back.
Chantal usually wears a spandex short and sports bra outfit of varying colours, matching knee and elbow pads and ankle wrestling shoes, sometimes opting for full lenth tights in place of shorts.
"Across the loudspeakers the sounds of She Bop by Cindi Lauper cranks out and the slender figure of Chantal Menard strides out smiling. The crowd breaks into applause as the popular French-Canadian slaps hands with the crowd on her way to the ring. She nimbly leaps the top rope after climbing the steps and walks to her corner.
She is unique in being a former figure skater and French-Canadian. Her attitude is usually cheerful, always approaching the ring with a smile and always taking time for the crowd.
She turned pro a little less than a year ago and wrestled sporadically in an independant promotion in Quebec before being accepted in BRA.
Her strengths include her agility and athleticism. She is fairly quick and comfortable in front of a crowd. On the flip side she is fragile for a wrestler and does not absorb punishment well. Her lack of toughness leaves her prey for those with few morals and her ring skills are limited.
Chantal was born and raised in Quebec. She was always very flexible and spent a lot of time in gymnastics and figure skating. Always a fan of wrestling she nevertheless concentrated on competitive skating to the point she knew she had reached her full potential. It was then she tried out for a small wrestling promotion where she made a few appearances more as a novelty than as a fully fledged wrestler. When she heard about the strike she saw her chance to finally be taken seriously.
Her wrestling style is that of a technician. She is agile but not a high flyer or brawler.
