Recruiting Notice (7.4.00)

4 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With recent comings and goings, BRA (Battling Ring Angels) is looking to add a few names to their roster. We are currently looking for all types of female wrestlers, both faces and heels. The fed is a message board fed but is highly competitive and stresses quality over quantity. More importantly, it is a fed where you can have loads of fun. BRA stresses working with one another and crafting fun and exciting angles. IMHO, it is the best women's fed on the net. So, come check us out at:
www.geocities.com/MadisonAvenue/Newsstand/5080/index.html If you like what you see, feel free to fill out an app and join the team. With the slew of closings and hiatuses in women's e-feds, it's nice to see a women's fed that will be celebrating their one year anniversary in two months.
Dante' handler of: "Alluring" Arielle Starr (UWF, WWO) The Babe Squad (BRA) "Beauty Star" Angel Saito (BLAZE) Princess Yamaguchi (GDWA) Leanna Love (ESWP) Lolita Love (MBC) Serenity Blue (IFWF) "Cupid" Corey Hart (AGW, NCWA) Holly Hotbody (NCWA)
