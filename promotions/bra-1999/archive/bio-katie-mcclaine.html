KatieMcClaine "The Cutie" Katie McClaine [No Photo Available] Born August 14, 1982 Hometown: Dallas, Texas Physical Description: Katie is 5'4" tall and weighs 123 pounds. Her weight is not muscle. She's a solid but "comfortable" build with a very feminine figure. Not the type you'd imagine as a Wrestler. Her arms and legs are not without definition, as she does hit the gym, but she's not a "strong" looking young woman. She can turn heads with her looks. Neck length auburn hair and blue eyes are placed against tanned skin and a smooth, almost "girl next door" look. She has a smile that can light up a small town, with cute dimples and a "go getter" attitude. A classic "face."
Style: All-around "catch when catch can." A basic style of some grappling/technical and some high spots or aerial moves. Katie isn't big and can't force anyone down or power them. She's a speed wrestler and a technical wrestler all the way. She knows many holds, taught to her by her family, but often has trouble getting to a point where she can use them. Right now she uses a basic "rookie" style.
History: Katie comes from a Wrestling family. Her father, mother and three brothers have all been champions in the squared circle. The McClaine Family is legendary in Texas. Katie is the youngest in the family and the least athletic or talented. But her heart is strong and her dreams are big enough to sustain her lack of pure skill.
Katie received training from the members of her family before she started her in-ring career. Her early career has not been so hot. The losses far outweigh the wins. But none of the beatings have detered her from her dream. She believes she will be a great Wrestler someday and keeps getting back up every time she gets knocked down.
A total fan favorite, she has no trouble winning people over with her attitude. The good looks help some as well. She always takes time to sign autgraphs and make friends with her fans. Also, she admits she's still learning. She takes on hard matches with the belief that they're a stepping stone to something better. Katie has never backed down and never shirked a match...no matter who was on the other side of the ring.
The kid is all spunk and heart. With each match her skills improve and her career is on the rise.
Finisher: The McClaine Claw (The Iron Claw)
