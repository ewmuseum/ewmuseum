PeggyChristian Peggy Christian Wrestler's Name: Peggy Christian Height: 5’6 Weight: 168 Date of Birth: Dec 1, 1962 Actual Hometown: Edmonton, Alta, Canada Hometown as announced when wrestling: Edmonton, Alta, Canada Physical Appearance: Average height, but well over her ideal weight. She is stocky, but tending more to soft tissue than either muscle or big bones. Fitness is a constant struggle with her.
Ring Attire: Peggy dresses in an older style of one piece suits, tights and lace up wrestling boots.
The Big Splash - Peggy bounces off two sets of ropes before splashing down on her prone opponent.
Background: Peggy grew up a fan of wrestling, largely due to having an aunt who was a successful wrestler. Peggy often dreamed of wrestling, but she showed no aptitude and never really thought of pursuing it. She married early, producing two daughters and generally living the life of a housewife and mother. Her husband traveled extensively with his job, leaving Peggy alone with the kids a lot. Finally, at age 36 she grew discouraged and looked for a change. It was at that time that BRA began its start. Peggy acted on impulse and took the plunge, applying for a tryout. Initially she was rejected due to her lack of experience, but just before opening up she was called back with 3 others to apply for an opening that suddenly opened up. Her first ring appearances were disasterous, losing easily.
But freak circumstances and a withdrawl put her in a position to wrestle once more for the spot. Somehow, she won the match and took her place in BRA. Her inexperience was quickly evident and Peggy paid heavily for it in the ring, but always gave 100%, thus earning a strong following. In her career she briefly shared the tag team title with Eden Starr, but the reign lasted one match. Since then she has struggled, working the indy circuit in small towns when BRA went on strike, and only just, through the efforts of her manager, managed a return to BRA.
