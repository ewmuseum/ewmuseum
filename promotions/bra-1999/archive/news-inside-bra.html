Inside BRA Inside BRA By Grinnan Barett BRA upfront: Lots of fan interest has been shown in the Tiffany "the Wrath" Epitaphine vs. Kelly "The Assassin" Mase. Dr. McKinley of the Dentonvale Clinic (Another name for Funny Farm) Has shown the most interest. Saying these two would be ant psychiatrist life's work.
Fans are also interested in the "Three Way Match" between Americana, Samantha Page and Alexandria Parker, to decide who will be the first to challenge Tiffany Epitaphine for the BRA TV title.
Our Cups runeth over: A review of last weeks big PPV matches. Americana vs. Skye Soaring Hawk - Timberrrrr Americana defeated Skye Soaring Hawk using her BIGGEST asset. Skye was unable to hold
Americana over her head. Skye normally bench press three hundred pounds, making this reporter wonder what Americana's weight really is.
Brynn Kennedy vs. Princess Peg This little Peggy went Waa Waa Waa all the way home A teary eyed Princess Peg submitted to her niece Brynn Kennedy. It was a Canadian Family Feud., they were so polite nobody knew they were feuding. Jenny Anderson vs. Alexandria Parker - Blind landing the Blond Jenny's pepper spray failed this time as a blinded Alexandria managed to make Jenny cheer "Give me an S, give me a U, give me a B, give me an aspirin."
Queen of BRA - Definitely not a Maiden Form "First Lady "Tiffany Lane was crowned Queen of BRA after a vote by BRA fans. Laura Parker claims there was massive voter fraud and demanded a recount when she learned she came in second for the eighteenth straight beauty pageant.
Amanda Strike vs. Leslie Magnus - Disunion of Snakes In the bloodiest match in BRA's young history, Leslie "Viper" Magnus was taken to the hospital after a brutal beating by Kelly "the
Assassin" Mase, who somehow tunneled into a caged ring. 4 on 4 elimination match - Attention K-Mart Body Shoppers the blue light special is low blows, choking and Eye gouges
Like most things, you get what you pay for and the Shop's cheap tactics fell apart on them as the Sisterhood won.
10 woman war for the BRA TV championship. (TV belt suspended from a ceiling/ladder match) - Catch a falling star
Tiffany "the Wrath" Epitaphine won the covetted TV title as she managed to get hold of the belt just before Catwoman knocked the ladder out from under her. Tiffany held on to the belt, even though she fell 15 feet.
Sherry Ann vs. Yvette Malreaux for the Celestial Title. - Heavenly goals, Devilish tricks In an interference marred match, Bianca Dupree stole the title from the rightful contenders. BRA Straps and Scraps: Body Shop Biz: Sherry Ann continues to ascribe to the "more the merrier" belief, and had 117,987 new members of the Shop admitted this week. The fact that the last three, Huey, Loui and Dewey were male and ducks, raised no eyebrows at the Shop.
Sister 'hood: In a move that this reporter must condemn, the Sisterhood of Seduction included reporter Candi Kane in one of their parties. It was an obvious attempt to bias the press in favor of the Sisterhood. This reporter condemns the unprofessionalism displayed by that whore Candi "Five Bucks" Kane.
By the way Marquise, my address is [detail withheld] Syndi 'list: There's currently only one member of this stable and she's up to no-good as usual. BRA WRESTLER CROWNED MISS TEEN USA: Jenny "The Cheerleader" Anderson was crowned Miss Teen USA this week. Some members of the public have been crying "FIX". Laura Parker commented
"Of course it was rigged. I didn't win, did I?" When it was pointed out that she was too old to be Miss TEEN USA, she hit this reporter in a very sensitive spot.
BRA finally shows some class: The fans of BRA have shown that they are capable of making intelligent choices, when they chose Tiffany Lane as the Queen of BRA. Snide remarks made by some of the meaner wrestlers did not alter the fact that the fans made the right choice in Tiffany. A woman with class and beauty and a real asset to BRA.
Ta ta 'til next time. Grinnan Barett
