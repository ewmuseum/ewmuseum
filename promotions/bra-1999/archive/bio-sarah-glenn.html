SarahGlenn Sarah Glenn Name: Sarah Glenn Height: 5'6 Weight: 121 pounds DOB: Aug 30/1977 Hometown: Las Vegas, Nevada Bio: Average height and build, leaning towards slender. Wiry build. Blonde hair midway down her back with blue eyes.
Sarah likes to wear revealing attire. Normally she wears a thong backed one-piece suit, shiny tights to accentuate her shapely legs and white lace up wrestling boots with black kneepads.
"As the thumping beat of Donna Summers' I Feel Love cascades around the arena the lights shine on the figure of Sarah Glenn as she stops at the wrestler's entrance and winks seductively at the crowd. She does a little hip grind, showing off her figure before starting towards the ring. The lights reflect off of her outfit as she maintains a coy smile all the way to the ring, circling once around in a provocative fashion before climbing the steps and into the ring"
Sarah is not very unique in appearance. She fits with the glamour image that appeals to some in the organization. Her attitude is one of seductive charmer. A favourite of the male 18-35 demographic. She likes to emphasize her physical "attributes" to gain a following. Being the niece of a minor official in BRA she spent a lot of time around the Federation, occasionally acting as a sparring partner for several wrestlers. It is her only experience with wrestling.
Her strengths lie in her ability to get the crowd behind her due to her antics as well as an inside knowledge of some of the other girl's weaknesses which she has obtained both sparring and through "inside" sources. Her weaknesses lie in lack of aggression and actual experience. She is knowledgable of moves and strategies but has no real knack for wrestling, a fact she is aware of. She hopes to use wrestling as a springboard for another career, which leaves her desire to succeed in wrestling lacking.
Sarah grew up around Las Vegas with her family. Her uncle has been a minor official with BRA since inception and Sarah spent a lot of time with him learning the ins and outs of the business, eventually moving her way into being a sparring partner for several of the wrestlers past and present. Though not really too keen she nonetheless made the move to wrestle at her uncle's urging when the strike hit BRA.
Sarah is a technician with a limited ability at high flying. Finisher: She likes using a chicken wing suplex before hooking the leg for an easy pin.
