KarenDeWitt Karen DeWitt Name: Karen DeWitt Height: 5’4 Weight: 121 lbs. Hometown: Seattle, WA Physical appearance Karen is short and wiry with straggly brown hair past her shoulders. Ring attire She wears a full body black spandex unitard with black wrestling boots. Entrance The lights dim for a moment then return to full brightness as AC/DC’s anthem “Highway to Hell” blares loud across the sound system. A defiant looking Karen DeWitt steps from the back and stops at the top of the ramp with her hands on her hips and a sneer on her face. Hanging from one hand is a large cloth bag that she proceeds to drag to the ring and leave on the floor as she rolls under the ropes and gets to her feet in her corner.”
Attitude Karen’s attitude reeks of contempt, uncaring as to what the fans may or may not think of her.
Experience Karen is primarily a bar fighter, both for money and just for the thrill of it. She has participated for years in organized women’s bar fighting. Generally her “matches” have been no holds barred affairs with one or both of the participants ending up unconscious.
Strengths and weaknesses Karen is extremely aggressive. She shows no fear or mercy and will never give up. On the down side she is small and lacking in experience in fights that involve rules.
Summary of experience Born and raised in the low rent area of Seattle, Karen was always an aggressive girl. Her parents abused her from an early age and by age seven had already been through four different fathers, all abusive. She left home and lived on the streets since she was 15 years old, scraping by on wages in a variety of menial jobs. She got into a fight with a larger woman over a man and found that she reveled in it. Ever since she fought whenever she had the chance and eventually ended up in the basement bar fight circuit in the city. A BRA official (who shall remain nameless) attended one of those fights and was so impressed with Karen convinced her to come to BRA not only as a wrestler, but also as a potential hardcore contender.
Moves Head butts, elbow smashes, punches, kicks, hip tosses, snap mares, body slams, dropkicks, piledriver, sidewalk slam, chops, headlock, hammerlock, hair pull, eye rake.
Karen DeWitt, Dec. 16/73, 5’4, 121lbs., small and wiry, Caucasian, contempt, brawler, heel
