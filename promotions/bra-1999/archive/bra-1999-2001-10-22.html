(With those words, the crowd again erupts in both cheers and laughter, the Blonde Bombshell and her sisters fuming inside the ring. Tiffany, in fact, begins to stomp and pout.) Shea: "They say ya can judge a person by the company she keeps, and lookin' at the three of ya–one stuck up, talent-less would-be diva who owes `er career successes to an `andy supply o' mirrors, and an ungrateful li'l brat who's ALREADY an `as-been–I suddenly realize just `ow truthful that is." (Another huge crowd pop, as Alex screams insults down the ramp. For her part, Jenn stalks toward the ropes, glaring and shouting a few choice comments for the Brit's benefit.) Shea: "But, Tiff, love, YOU just take tha cake. I can't deny ya got talent, but if ya were even one-tenth of as good as ya think ya are, my God, you'd be dangerous! As it is, you're just a gifted, spoiled rotten tramp who can't be bothered ta put forward tha effort ta make yerself into the best. Ya want everything `anded ta ya. I'm sure that's what you're used to. You've probably never `ad to work a day in yer life until ya tried ta go straight. And it was too difficult fer ya, so ya packed up yer marbles and decided ta go `ome, didn't ya?" (Shea starts walking toward the ring, mic still in hand.) Shea: "I never `ad much given to me meself. I've `ad ta scrap fer just about everything I've gotten in me life. Just like everyone else out `ere tonight. And I got a newsflash fer ya, duchess. Every single person in those stands, and watchin' us on telly, `ave more class in their pinkies than you've got in that entire, gussied up plastic body o' yers." Shea reaches ringside, rolling under the bottom rope, before quickly springing to her feet, keeping her guard up with the numbers heavily against her.) Shea: "And ya also seem ta `ave some memory issues goin' on, Tiffany. I seem ta recall a couple of weeks ago now, before Casey did tha world a favor and knocked ya out o' the Celestial tourney, that ya got taken ta yer very limits by someone else. And no, she didn't beat ya, but she DID take ya closer to defeat than ya'd been in a very long time. Refresh me memory, folks, but just who might that `ave been?" (Shea holds the microphone out toward the audience, which picks up several shouts of Shea's name. Her face suddenly beaming, she again brings the microphone to her lips.) Shea: "Oh, yeah, it WAS me, wasn't it? But Tif, just cause ya beat me a couple o' weeks ago, that don't mean that you can beat me tonight." (The fans in the arena are absolutely roaring now, as Shea never takes her eyes from Tiffany's.) Shea: "I got nothin' ta do tonight, and apparently, neither do you. Why don't we just see `ow `ard ya really are?" Jean: "Yeah, kick her ass! I want to see some action!" (The crowd roars as Tiffany exchanges worried looks with Jenn and Alex. They confer for a bit before Tiffany speaks.) Tiffany: "Whoa, now! Wait a minute! I'm all for taking you on, Shea, and beating you like the dog that you are but not tonight. After all, I'm not exactly dressed for the occasion and there's no way that I'm ripping my new Chanel gown!" (The crowd boos as Tiffany smiles smugly, Jenn and Alex nodding their agreement. Shea just rolls her eyes.) Tiffany: "But, if you're so eager for a wrestling lesson, how about we set something up for the next card? But not just any old regular match. Let's make this a match in the Featherweight tourney. That way, I can get the pleasure of not just kicking your ass but also of ensuring that you never win that belt! (smirks) Are you game?" (The crowd cheers Shea on.) Shea: "Ya got a deal, Tiffany. I'm goin' ta enjoy teaching ya a lesson in humility!" Next card, Shea London vs. Tiffany Lane in the featherweight title tourney! If their last match is any indication, Tiffany is going to have her hands full!" Jean: "Hmph. Tiffany Lane vs. Shea London! This is gonna be good!"
