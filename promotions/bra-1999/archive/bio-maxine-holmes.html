Maxine Holmes — Battling Ring Angels bio card

Maxine Holmes "Texas Tough" Maxine Holmes [No Photo Available] “Texas Tough” Maxine Holmes Hometown: Corpus Christi, Texas Born: March 25, 1965 Physical description: Brown hair and brown eyes, with weather beaten face and rough features. Maxine is not a beauty by any stretch, but she isn’t unattractive. She is tall, fit and lanky. A body built for hard working and butt kicking. She is five feet, eleven inches and 182 pounds of lean muscle, crafted by years of ranch work and exercise. You won’t find much fat on her and not many weak spots. Maxine is a tall, lanky tower of muscle from head to the ground.
Personality: Maxine is a rough, old school Texan. Reserved and quiet, she lets her Wrestling do the talking. She is deliberate, rugged and pulls no punches. She speaks with a slow Texas drawl and rarely gets excited about anything. Anyone looking for anything like overt warmness in Maxine will spend a long time looking. But she is loyal to her friends and a generally good person to be around if she likes you. Maxine likes to eat steak, chew tobacco, drink beer, work on her ranch and kick butt in the ring.
Style: Bar room brawler all the way. Old school and rough, Maxine is not a spot machine. She is a power Wrestler of the old style. A puncher, kicker, brawler and stretcher. She wears down opponents with a mix of power moves and tough tactics. She is very strong and methodical in the ring, usually working over a body part until it becomes a weakness. Then she pounces, grinding away until the other Wrestler is in just too much pain to continue. She isn’t above cheating and is no stranger to hardcore. She’s an eye gouging, low blow using, foreign object carrying, biting old school brawler. Maxine is a heel and fans don’t like her. Just for that, she doesn’t like them. So it’s a hate-hate relationship.
Finisher: The Rio Grande Lariat – A running lariat that has the force and power to take an opponent out of her boots.
From there: The Texas Screwdriver – Maxine sets her opponent up for a powerbomb, but as she brings her down, she twists her in the air and comes forward…catching the woman’s head between her legs and the move ends much like a tombstone piledriver. It looks really bad and is not easy to do. But her strength and agility makes her able to maneuver her victim in the air.
Entrance: “Hey Hey What Can I Do” as performed by The Black Crowes and Jimmy Paige announces the arrival of Maxine Holmes to the arena. The fans stand and turn to the curtain area as the woman known as “The Bad Woman from Corpus Christi, Texas” steps out. Looking cold and mean in a black leather vest over a black University of Texas Longhorns shirt, and black elbow pads. Her legs are covered by black leather chaps, each leg bearing half of a white map of Texas (such that if she brought her legs together her chaps would show a white map of Texas), worn over a pair of light blue Wrangler jeans and black knee pads. Her feet are clad in old school black “Cowboy style” Wrestling boots. She has a black Cowboy hat on her head and a bull rope draped over her neck, each side dangling over her vest. In her left hand she carries a branding iron. Maxine raises her right hand gives the crowd a Texas Longhorns salute (a closed fist, with the index finger and pinky extended out at an angle, bent to simulate the horns of a bull).
The Texan starts her slow walk to the ring, stopping along the way to exchange words with some fans, or just back a few down. Her solid physique, mixed with her bad attitude and branding iron, make her an imposing figure. One hundred and eighty two pounds of walking, talking graveyard destruction. She works her entrance, generating as much heat with the audience as she can before she reaches the ring.
Maxine scales the steps and enters the ring under the second rope. As she stands back up, she gives another Longhorns salute, this time adding a yell to it. As she finishes her last heat building moment, she walks to her corner and removes her hat, bull rope and places them in her corner next to the iron. Maxine removes her chaps and vest and puts them with her other items, before turning back to face the center of the ring and gets prepared for the match ahead.
History: Born in Texas, the daughter of ranchers, Maxine grew up on Longhorns football, cattle and rasslin. She worked the ranch alongside her daddy and grand daddy, developing a strong body and weather beaten mind. Maxine never avoided hard work, nor did she avoid trouble. In high school, she was known as the toughest girl around and even the boys didn’t mess with her. She attended the University of Texas and was a track star. She still holds school records in shot put and hammer throw.
After college she decided to try her hand at Wrestling. She was always a brawler and loved a fight. She worked locally in Texas, but her style wasn’t making it in the U.S. so she traveled to Japan where she became a sensation. Her brawling and bad attitude caught on. She was a big, mean American monster cast in the role of Goliath against the smaller Japanese girls. Japanese fans lined up to boo this big Gaijin who did all she could to embarrass and humiliate the local girls. Few of the Japanese wrestlers could match up to her physically and she ran roughshod over much of the competition. Anyone who managed to beat her became a hero in her native land. While in Japan, Maxine racked up the trophies and titles. She won a number of tournaments, titles and other accolades. Japanese fans gave her the nickname “Mrs. Blackjack” and she carried it with honor. She was just as at home in garbage matches as in regular matches and never hid from a fight.
Maxine has never wanted to change her image. She is a big, bad heel and proud of it. Japanese fans appreciated that persona and it is part of what made her a legend.
After the match: Maxine flips her defeated victim onto her front and hog-ties the lady like she would a calf on the ranch. Then, with the branding iron in her left hand, she gives her Longhorn salute and places the iron on the flank of the victim, just below the leg loop in her bottoms of outfit. The iron is inked, and does no permanent damage to the lady’s body. But being branded leaves a permanent mark on the victim’s pride. A final demonstration of victory by Maxine.
When the branding is done, she pulls a can of Skoal from her vest pocket, takes a big dip and places it beneath her bottom lip. She looks down and spits the excess on the seat of her victim’s bottoms and walks away…a final sign of disrespect for her vanquished opponent.
