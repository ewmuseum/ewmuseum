The Heavenly Bodies Members: Skye Johanassen and Cindra McCain Team Alignment: Heel Wins: Losses: Status: Active Description: (Skye): Skye is the smaller of the two women, but that certainly doesn't hold her back, as she's a little spitfire. She stands 5'6" in height, and was born 3/22/79. She hails from Charleston, SC. Her muscles are compact and she stays in good shape. She has long, light brown hair and brown eyes.
(Cindra): Cindra is the larger, and stronger of the duo. She has long, slightly curled, dark brown hair and brown eyes as well. She is 5'9" tall and was born 7/3/78. She's from Kansas City, Missouri. She has an impressive physique (from a looking-at standpoint, anyway), for an average build woman; and has thighs that were made for crushing.
Ring Attire: Both women wear suits styled in the same design. Cindra's is a dark blue, while Skye's is a deep purple. They are one piece suits, with the word "Heavenly" embroidered on the left side, just below the shoulder strap. Both members of the team also wear black lace up wrestling boots as well.
Combined Weight: 263 lbs (Skye 128, Cindra 135) Entrance music: "Day By Day" by Taproot History: It is unclear where Delilah Strain found the Heavenly Bodies, but one thing is certain - she has brought them into BRA as her own personal tag team. No one can be sure of where they originally came from, or even if they have any wrestling experience prior to joining BRA.
