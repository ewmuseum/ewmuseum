CJLocke Wrestler's name - Candice James “C J” Locke Date of Birth – June 4, 1981 Hometown – Pebble Beach, California Height – 5 ft, 4 in Weight - 120 pounds Physical description – Hey, she’s a Locke. That means she’s cute, toned and tanned. She has beautiful blond hair and blue eyes. CJ has looks similar to those of her cousin Lindsay. She has the same girl next door look that made her cousin Lindsay famous in BRA. Her body is always in perfect shape and she trains constantly.
Wrestler's gimmick –  “I’m a Locke!” Not an Anderson : ) CJ is the third member of what is fast becoming women’s e-wrestling most talented family. She is the cousin of former BRA Celestial Champion Lindsay  Locke and Michelle “Mixie” Locke, an up and coming new star in BRA. She is the youngest Locke and still has many of the same good girl characteristics Lindsay had at the beginning of her career.
In-ring style – High flying luchador. Finishing move – The CJ Scramble. A DDT from the top rope (Tornado DDT). CJ’s Swing. An Acid Drop CJ Locke [No Photo Available] History Wrestling has always had its families. The Anderson’s, The Von Erich’s, The Armstrong’s. Sooo, please welcome the third member of the first family of women’s wrestling…CJ Locke.
CJ’s family was the most well off of all the other Locke family members. Unlike her cousins Lindsay and Mixie, CJ did not have to wrestle to pay her bills. She wrestles because she saw her cousin Lindsay do it. CJ had always looked up to her cousin Lindsay. She was several years younger and idolized Lindsay. She did whatever Lindsay did. She always looked somewhat like her. But she would dress like her, act like her, try to speak and move like her. She got into sports because Lindsay was an athlete. She watched Lindsay become a star in BRA and secretly hoped to be like her. She watched all of Lindsay’s matches and bored her friends with stories of her famous cousin. Lindsay always had time for her and was very supportive of her younger cousin.
But when she was finally old enough to try and follow in her cousin’s footsteps, Lindsay had changed. She was no longer interested in helping others. She was only interested in herself and her own career. Luckily, CJ’s other cousin Mixie was a wrestler in Mexico. She agreed to train CJ and help her become a wrestler. The two grew close and though CJ never particularly liked Mixie’s personal style, she became the best of friends with her other cousin.  CJ fought alongside her cousin in Mexico and all around the world.
Even though she’s the smallest and youngest of the Locke’s, she is still just as scrappy as her more famous cousins.
CJ has reunited with Lindsay and Mixie and she’s ready to become a star…even if it kills her cousins.
