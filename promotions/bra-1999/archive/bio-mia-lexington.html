Roster: Mia Lexington Mia Lexington Mia Lexington DOB: 03/01/77 Hometown: Boston, MA Leaning: Neutral Fighting Style: While Mia has no certain fighting style, so to speak, she is more like a kickboxer, her long legs giving her a reach on her opponent. She loves to use front kicks to the belly, ribs and chest to keep her opponent away.
Height: 5' 10" Weight: 136lbs (Now 146lbs) Hair: Brown Eyes: Blue Wrestling Attire: In the ring, Mia wears a baby blue one-piece that grips her body tightly, complete with a cutout at the belly, to display her midsection. She wears black wrestling boots also. Her baby blue outfit looks something like this.
Finisher: "The Shrinking Violet": Mia holds the opponent's head in a reverse headlock, as if she were going to perform a DDT. Instead she leaps back, kicking her legs outwards, and drives the opponent's head and face into the mat - almost like a reverse bulldog.
History: Mia Lexington is the typical "rich-girl" persona. However, she remains smart and will eventually become a consumate businesswoman. She's the ultimate show-woman, always enjoying being in the spotlight. Her parents are the owners of a large company, and while she has no need for a job, she has somewhat trained in the arts of combat. She has always been athletic, and is excited about the chance of putting these abilities to use.
Other: Mia rarely skips the chance to show off: which could, at times, be a weakness. Since her injury, Mia hasn't kept a training regimen like she had while employed by Battling Ring Angels. She's gained somewhere around 10 pounds in her absence.
After suffering a spinal injury more than a year ago, before the strike, Mia returns to B.R.A. under the guidance of Delilah Strain. She's been named the Executive Director of Delila's Dolls, making all business decisions directly under Delilah Strain, herself. She also serves as the Commissioner of Training BRA.
