JennChristian Jennifer Christian Name: "Twilight Zone" Jenn Christian Height: 5’2 Weight: 107 lbs Date of Birth: March 5 1982 Actual Hometown: Cold Lake, Alberta Hometown as announced when wrestling: Cold Lake, Alberta Physical Appearance: Jenn is a slim blue-eyed blonde looking more like a teen model than a wrestler.
Ring Attire: Jenn usually wears a black spandex sports bra style top, black spandex shorts over black tights and collegiate style wrestling shoes. Sometimes she will wear a print one piece in place of the two-piece.
Background: Jenn is the daughter of BRA original and former tag champion Peggy Christian. Jenn joined her high school wrestling team in her senior year but never stood out. She only made the cut after another girl was forced to drop out. Though interested in BRA, Jenn was largely sheltered from it by her mother who jealously guarded her own career, a move that started the seed of resentment. It was further enhanced when Peggy announced she was divorcing Jenn's father, a man she loved a great deal. Shortly after Jenn sent a letter to another BRA wrestler, Marquise Yvette DeSade Malreaux. The Marquise was taken by the young teen and invited her to the PPV in France she was hosting. Almost immediately Jenn was taken on as the protégé of the Marquise and under her tutelage and guidance grew as a person and a wrestler. Jenn became La Petite Marquise and trained diligently under Malreaux's watchful eye. Yvette also cultured the resentment in the teen, using it to develop Jenn in her own style. Though life with the Marquise was often hard the change in Jenn was dramatic. Yvette recognized and developed the natural skill in Jenn that had been lying dormant and success was fast as her career developed. Once Yvette retired from wrestling Jenn remained loyal to the Sisterhood, but developed a new persona as she developed from a sheltered teen to a young woman.
Finisher: The Outer Limits - Jenn incapacitates her opponent and lays her out on her back near a corner. She then runs from the opposite corner and springs over her downed foe to a handstand on the top turnbuckle before pushing off backward and splashing across her opponent's chest for the easy pin.
