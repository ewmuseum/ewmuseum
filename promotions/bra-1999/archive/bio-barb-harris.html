BarbHarris Barb Harris Name: Barb Harris Height: 5'7 Weight: 194 lbs DOB: May 3/1976 Hometown: Minneapolis, Minn Finisher: Big Splash Barb is of average height but is very overweight. She is big boned and solid but packs very little muscle. She has fair hair and fair complexion and would look quite attractive if she could shed around fifty pounds.
Barb wears a a variety of one piece suits, usually dark coloured to try and downplay her size. Mostly she goes bare legged but sometimes wears clear lycra tights. White lace up wrestling boots finish her ring attire.
"As the sounds of the Bee Gee's classic Stayin' Alive fills the air the spotlights play over the wrestler's entrance and the imposing bulk of Barb Harris walks out. Her face betrays a mixture of excitement and nervousness as she faces the crowd and begins her walk to the ring. A black satin jacket covers her from the waist up and her blonde hair flows freely over her shoulders. Her every movement seems like an effort as she climbs the steps to the ring and splits the ropes before climbing in to her corner."
Barb's imposing size and complete lack of experience make her unique in itself. Her attitude is one of someone getting a chance to live out a fantasy. She is cheerful and excited at been given this chance.
Barb has no experience in wrestling whatsoever, but like her all time favorite and idol Peggy Christian is a lifelong fan and avid supporter of the sport.
Barb's strength is measured up in one word. Size. She is very large and hard to move and hopes to use this advantage to gain a lot of victories by pin. Her weaknesses are many though. No wrestling experience is big. As well she is not really in good shape, endurance is low and she has little tolerance for pain. She is painfully slow in her movements and her knowledge of counter moves is practically non existent.
Barb was born and raised in the north of the U.S.A. the only daughter along with two brothers. Her whole family basically grew up in front of the TV watching wrestling. She wrestled her brothers at home who were also big boned but never anything seriously. At school she was picked on because of her size and was beaten up frequently because of it, but somehow she retained a cheerful disposition. She followed BRA since its inception and grew to idolize the underdogs, especially Peggy Christian whom she could identify with. She was working as a cashier when the call came out for anyone and everyone to help rescue BRA and inspired, took the plunge. To a lot of peoples surprise, she was accepted despite a poor showing at the tryouts where the 105 pound daughter of her idol showed her glaring deficiencies with some ease. On the upside though, the humbling she took has given her some purpose, and hopes to exact revenge against the teen in public.
Barb is working toward being a technician, hoping to develop past the need to use just her size. Right now her repetoire consists of few wrestling moves.
