Crowd shot. You remember it. Rows upon rows of banner waving, excited fans both trying to show their enthusiasm and get themselves on TV. To this end many choose to show their creative side on banners. A few of the more notable ones include "Bring back the Babe Squad", "Teen Queen's Rule the Ring" and "Samantha Staffer is the best thing to happen to wrestling since Jean Schultz!". Kenny: It's time for the match I have been waiting for! Cari: I see your crush on granny Schultz continues unabated! You know I think there is a FED in Canada that could use a talentless pair like you and her! You can join that other has been, Amanda Starr! Kenny: I wondered where she vanished! The only way Schultz will win a match is if Peggy Christian comes back! Kenny: Actually, it was Staffer I was excited about. Cari: Keep that part to yourself, Romeo! Suddenly the crowd noise dies as the unmistakable sounds of "Another One Bites the Dust" by Queen echoes around the stadium. Then through the curtains struts the tall and striking figure of Samantha Staffer as she stops and looks around seductively to the crowd. She is dressed in a tight pink spandex one-piece suit that clings to all the right places and very high black leather boots. She then works her way along the aisle slapping hands and exchanging high five's with as many people in the crowd as she can before stepping smiling through the ropes into the ring. Kenny: A VERY popular return to the ring! The crowd adores Samantha! Cari: I am pretty impressed. You made yourself pretty clear through all that drool! Kenny: I never drool! Cari: What's that running down your chin then? The lights go black for a second before snapping back on again. At the top of the ramp looking menacing stands a legend of the mat game. Jean "the butcher" Schultz glares to the audience with a well practiced look as she walks contemptuously down the ramp towards the ring. The crowd reacts mostly negatively as the thirty-year veteran heads for the ring and steps through the ropes. As she moves to the center of the ring she stands still for a second, the crowd jeering, as they know what comes next. Then, like she has done countless times before, she throws up an arm in a Nazi salute before snarling at her opponent and moving to her corner. Cari: Again, I am impressed! The gritty veteran lays on her back for just a moment too long as Samantha gets up and jumps up, bringing her shapely thigh crashing down across the old woman's throat! Schultz gags as she tries to roll away to the ropes but is stopped as Samantha grabs her legs and drags Schultz loudly to the middle of the ring on her belly! Holding the legs up high, Samantha looks around at the crowd smiling which drives the noise level up. Schultz slaps at the mat, yelling obscenities, which makes the crowd even more vocal! Then Samantha folds the legs across each other, bent at the knee before falling across them with all her weight, which sends a loud shriek of pain from the cagey veteran. Kenny: Samantha Staffer looking she has not lost a step in her layoff! Jean Schultz in trouble yet again! Cari: Samantha is a talented wrestler, but Schultz is hardly a test for her.
