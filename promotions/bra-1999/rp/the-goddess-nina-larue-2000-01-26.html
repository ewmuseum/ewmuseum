Battling Ring Angels — Roleplay

"The Goddess" Nina Larue (1.26.00)

26 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi! I posted a role play, a few weeks ago, and got a pretty good response. So, I thought that I would do it again:-) This one is for one of my other wrestlers, "The Goddess" Nina Larue. She wrestles in the
Battling Ring Angels promotion and is one half of the Babe Squad tag team. Some background on the rp: recently BRA held a PPV in Vail where
NIna was assigned the task of "babysitting" the new BRA rookies by
ArchAngel, BRA's booker and power that be. One of the rookies is underage and was caught drunk, which upset ArchAngel who then accused
Nina of failing her task. Feel free to critique, discuss, butcher, flame, etc. etc.
==================================== (The scene opens at the elegant, Beverly Hills mansion of "The Goddess" Nina Larue, an estate both majestic and exquisite. More specifically, we find ourselves at the home's terrace where "The Goddess" Nina Larue sits at a circular, white table. Placed before her is a silver platter filled with her usual morning treats: a half bagel, grapefruit, fat-free yogurt, and a bottle of Evian water. But, from the immaculate look of the foods, it is quite obvious that they haven't been touched. With a sigh and a weary look upon her beautiful face, Nina slowly rises to her feet and makes her way towards the edge of the terrace. The busty brunette hugs herself as she feels a slight morning chill, her sensual, violet eyes watching the sun rise.
Nina wears a flimsy, pink negligee that caresses her curvaceous figure like a second skin. Her long, black hair falls down her back, the ends lightly curled. Why was her morning filled with dread, she asked herself. She and Tiffany had walked away victorious, last night. It hadn't been the type of victory that she had wanted but it had been a victory nonetheless. So, why wasn't she happy? She turned away from the sun's rays as her thoughts roamed to past events. She knew the reason that her mood was spoiled and it had everything to do with BRA's power that be, the ArchAngel!
Nina remembered a few days ago, a meeting with Gabrielle, the ArchAngel's messenger. The ArchAngel had sent word that, since Nina had failed her, "The Goddess" could expect a measure of retaliation. What could the ArchAngel had meant? What type of revenge had she planned for
Nina? Miss Larue had no idea and the suspense was killing her! Nina leaned against the terrace and pouted. It just wasn't fair! Next week,
Nina was going to be in one of the biggest matches of her career, a chance to earn BRA's television title, and she could hardly concentrate!
Her mind kept focusing on the ArchAngel and her ominous threats. She knew that ArchAngel held a great deal of power in the Battling Ring
Angels and would waste no opportunity using that power against Nina or any others that she felt had crossed her. What was Nina going to do? How was she going to deal with this?
Nina pushed the questions from her mind as she made her way back to the table. She couldn't let this get to her! She had been in tough spots before and gotten out of them. She would do the same with this one, as well! Besides, she always had the Sisterhood to turn to, her almost surrogate family. She knew that they would come up with some way to help her. Nina looked down at the morning paper and noticed an article about
Princess Peggy Christian and her upcoming divorce from her husband.
Nina grimaced as she skimmed the article. Why didn't this news help cheer her up? Peggy Christian had been one of Nina's hated enemies and worst rivals. In the past, she would have relished in the news that Peg was experiencing unhappiness, in her otherwise perfect life. But now, all Nina felt, strangely enough, was pity. Not pity for Peg. It would be a long time before Nina would ever pity that ancient hag! But pity for
Peg's children. How would they cope with the break-up of their family?
Would they be forced to chose between their parents? Or, worse yet, would they be forced to live with one parent, forever losing contact with the other!?!
Briefly, Nina remembered her own childhood and losing her own mother at six years old, a victim of cancer. At the time, Nina hadn't been able to understand her loss. How could a six year old cope with losing the one of the most important people in their lives? Her father had tried to provide the love that Nina craved but it hadn't been enough. She had wanted a mother. She had wanted someone to talk to about her first boyfriend. Someone to help usher her into womanhood. And someone that would offer her nothing but unconditional love and support. And the women that her father had bought home hadn't been viable replacements!
What were their names? Bambi? Babette? Lana? Nina couldn't remember. All she knew was that they got younger and younger as Nina grew older. In fact, his newest girlfriend, Cricket, was younger than Nina! What kind of name was Cricket!?! Nina shook her head at the thought of the young, blonde bimbo. She had resented these women for trying to take her mother's place. None of them had been good enough and none of them had been worthy! Still, she resented her father even more for attempting to replace her mother's memory with such inferior models! She had known that it was his own personal way of coping but Nina still didn't think that it was fair!
She shook her head violently. No! She wasn't going to allow herself to think about this! Not now! Besides, she was grown now, her own woman.
That stuff didn't affect her any more. Even as she thought the words, she knew that the wounds were still as fresh as when she was a child.
Nina looked at the phone and bit her lower lip. She would hate for any child to go through what she went through! She reached for her nearby, cordless phone and dialed a familiar number.)
Nina: Martin? Hello. This is Miss Larue. I wanted to talk to you about using some of the money in my account. Yes. I want to make a private donation to a Miss Peggy Christian.
(The scene fades to black.)
