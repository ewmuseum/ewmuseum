Battling Ring Angels — Roleplay

"The Goddess" Nina Larue (9.9.00)

9 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens at the luxurious, Beverly Hills mansion of "The Goddess" Nina
Larue, on a cool, summer afternoon. More specifically, we find ourselves in her impeccably designed study. Filled with ancient tomes and Victorian furniture, the large room is the perfect showcase of class, style, and old money.
Suddenly, the buxom vixen enters the room, clad in a slinky, pink, slip dress, showcasing her sleek, curvy figure and long, smooth legs. She completes the look with matching, pink pumps and her long, black hair falls straight down her back, framing her lovely face in a sexy, layered fashion. Adorning her silky locks are two strategically placed hair clips. Walking across her plush carpeting with grace and ease, it is easy to see why she is one of the world's most in-demand supermodels. Taking a seat at her large desk, Nina lets out a small sigh. "The Goddess" had been enjoying her recent vacation, with her fiance', Troy, and she had hated ending their time together, a time filled with romance and the opportunity for the young lovers to reaffirm their feelings for one another. Yet, the real world beckoned and neither could resist it's call.
Troy, a major movie star, had film projects and other Hollywood business that called his attention while Nina returned to her dual careers as model and professional wrestler. Letting out a small sigh, Nina brushed her hair behind her ears. It seemed that, at the moment, her wrestling career was going to take precedence.
As enforcer of The Sisterhood of Seduction stable, it was Nina's role to act as official leader until the return of The Marquise Yvette Desade Malreaux. No more was that apparent than now, at a time when the Sisterhood was going through one of it's roughest periods. With recent comings and goings, The
Sisterhood was no longer the strongest group in BRA and it worried some of its infamous members. As a consequence, those same members turned to Nina, in the hopes that she could provide some answers. Worst of all was Nina's best friend,
Tiffany Lane. Tiffany had always been a tad neurotic but, since winning The
Celestial championship, The Blonde Bombshell was even more paranoid than usual, seeing everyone as a potential threat to her title. How could Nina provide everyone with what they wanted to hear when she didn't know all of the answers herself? The beautiful brunette wasn't sure but she had faith that she could make everything right again. After all, if Yvette named her as enforcer, it was obviously because she felt that Nina could do the job. And, if Yvette placed such faith in Nina, it had to be for a good reason.
"I can do this." Nina whispered. "By Hecate, I promise that I will return the
Sisterhood to our rightful place as the greatest stable in BRA, in the name of
Yvette Desade Malreaux! This meeting will serve as the perfect chance to vanquish all fears and uncertainties." She promised, referring to the upcoming
Sisterhood soiree. "I will prove exactly why Yvette entrusted me with such a prized position."
"The Goddess" grinned to herself. Speaking of BRA, there were many issues that
The Sisterhood would have to deal with, including some of the promotions more unsavory wrestlers. But there was time to deal with that at the meeting. At the moment, Nina was going to use her last free moments to enjoy herself. Reaching across her desk, she grabbed a bridal magazine, her mind flooded with thoughts of her upcoming wedding. In just a few short weeks, she was going to become
Mrs. Troy Daniel and she wanted everything to be perfect. Flipping through the magazine, she noticed a small envelope tucked within its pages.
"What..." she wondered aloud, her sensual, violet eyes narrowing curiously. "Where did this come from?" She opened the envelope and scanned its contents, a sinister grin forming across her lips. The letter was from John Nanakami and he was asking Nina for some form of a truce. So, the old man was now groveling, begging Nina to leave himself and his protege, Sakura Ito, alone. How had Nina missed this buried treasure? With a smirk, she folded the letter and brought it to her crimson-painted lips, kissing it softly. So, Nanakami wanted a truce, eh? Not on his life! After the callous way he had dared to speak to her, "The Goddess"
was going to make sure that both Nanakami and his protege were destroyed! But how could Nina use this bit of information to make them both pay? Slowly an idea formed in her mind. She grabbed a pen and piece of paper, quickly writing a response to his letter.
Dearest Nanakami-san, I believe that you are correct. Maybe it is time that we put the past behind ourselves. I regret that our previous meeting did not answer many of the questions in both of our minds. I would also like to sincerely apologize for my behavior. It was never my objective to upset you or cause you further grief. I can completely understand your feelings and stance and I promise to leave you and Sakura alone. You will not have to worry about me causing either of you any added problems. Again, I apologize and wish you both the best.
Nina With a look of satisfaction, Nina sealed the letter in an envelope and prepared it for mailing. Now, maybe it was time to pay Sakura a little visit......
