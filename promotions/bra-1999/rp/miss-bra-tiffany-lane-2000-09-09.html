Battling Ring Angels — Roleplay

"Miss BRA" Tiffany Lane (9.9.00)

9 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tiffany Lane let out a contented sigh as she surveyed the surroundings of her
Malibu, beach house, her stunning, emerald eyes landing on her tastefully designed mantle. Perched atop her mantle was the Battling Ring Angels Celestial title, the light's rays reflecting off of the precious leather and gold.
Crossing her long, shapely legs, the Blonde Bombshell leaned back in her plush sofa. Since entering BRA, obtaining The Celestial Title had been her one main goal. Now, after months of putting her body on the line, she possessed her heart's desire. She was the Celestial Champion, the undisputed queen of BRA.
Even as she basked in her glory, a small voice reminded her of those that would love to toss the queen from her throne, jealous hags that would covet everything that Tiffany worked so hard to possess! A frown graced her flawless face at the thought of Lisa Dream and Missy Jones, women who would stop at nothing to take Tiffany's gold! Tiffany also couldn't ignore the legion of new faces entering BRA. Any of these hungry rookies would welcome the chance to snatch Tiffany's prized possession, like hungry buzzards going after prey.
Although Tiffany had spent the last few weeks away from the states, working fashion shows and photo sessions in Paris and Milan, she kept an eye on BRA and it's wrestlers. After all, as the reigning queen, it was her duty to keep up with the competition! Regardless, none of those wretches were good enough to breathe the same air as Tiffany, much less hold The Celestial Title. From Viper to Angel Dust, none of the BRA wrestlers could compare to The Bombshell's beauty, class, and sex appeal. Other than Tiffany, what other woman was more deserving of the Celestial gold?
Rising to her feet, Tiffany made her way through her living room, passing one of her many mirrors. She stopped for a second to admire her reflection. Clad in a tiny, white, Versace, tube top and even smaller, matching, micro-mini skirt,
Tiffany showcased her sleek, curvaceous form to great effect. The busty blonde finished the risque look with white, platform heels and her long locks graced the small of her back in a cascade of luxurious curls. Pleased with her perfect reflection, the statuesque beauty made her way to a nearby phone, immediately dialing the number of her best friend and tag partner, "The Goddess" Nina
Larue. Tiffany frowned as Nina's maid answered on the second ring.
"Lupe', this is Tiffany. Where's Nina?" Tiffany asked the older woman. "So, she's still vacationing with Troy, huh? Hmmmm. I see. When do you expect her back? Tomorrow? Excellent. Please tell her to contact me immediately."
Tiffany hung up on the older woman and dialed the number of Alexandria Parker, a good friend and stable mate as a member of the infamous Sisterhood of
Seduction. "Alexandria! How have you been?" Tiffany asked with a smile, pleased to talk with her friend. "That's great to hear. I just returned from Milan yesterday and I have some exciting news to tell you. The Tiffany's Bedroom, lingerie line is selling much better than we expected in the foreign market. I think those commercials were an excellent idea. Alexandria, you are a genius." Tiffany's jovial mood changed as she noticed the urgency creeping into Alexandria's voice. "BRA? To be honest with you, I haven't been keeping up with the promotion too much. I've caught a few shows here and there and am aware that we have new management. Why?"
Tiffany's eyes darkened as she listened to Alexandria's words. "I see. So, what have the others done about this? Hmm. Interesting. Well, Nina returns tomorrow and she will definitely have something to say about that!
After all, with Yvette's temporary absence, Nina is the leader of The Sisterhood." Tiffany stated. "I want you to alert Laura and tell her that we are having an important meeting. It's high time that The Sisterhood returned to their place as BRA's dominant stable. Don't you worry about a thing. Everything will be okay. Nina will see to it."
After a few more words, The Blonde Bombshell ceased her conversation, running a hand through her locks. What was happening? How could the Sisterhood slip so easily from their spot as BRA's top stable? With the losses of Jennifer and
Yvette, they were now a shell of their former selves. More importantly, how would this all affect Tiffany and her role as Celestial champion? After all, it was no secret that she relied heavily on the support of the other Sisterhood members to maintain her gold. What would this do to her? Tiffany sighed as she shook her head. How could she have gone from total contentment to blind panic in just a few short minutes!?! Regardless, Nina would come up with a solution,
Tiffany promised herself. She could always count on Nina.......
