"[name withheld]" < [detail withheld] > wrote: >[name withheld] < [detail withheld] > wrote in article ><5grjm8$bfa$ 1...@gryphon.phoenix.net >... > >> you can get by with $5 or $10 a month. So if all you wanted to do was >> play in a wrestling game, which is cheaper? $5 a month in a PBM or $20 a >> month that you must pay your ISP to play e-mail wrestling? > >Your point is very valid, that it's cheaper to play by IWA than by e-mail, >assuming all you ever use your Internet account for is e-wrestling... but >I'm left with the question "Who the heck would buy an ISP *solely* to join >an e-fed? My point is, if it's just e-mail wrestling vs. PBM wrestling, PBM is cheaper. As for who would buy an ISP solely to join an e-fed, well, there's lots of people who only play e-mail wrestling because that's all they have time for. They may say they are on it for other reasons, but look at what they've bookmarked and where they spend all their time. Many are basically on the Net just to play e-mail wrestling now if you'd look at what they spend their time on. >I think a vast, vast majority come across e-wrestling because they're on >the net, not get on the net because they want to e-wrestle. In fact, I'd >doubt most people have even heard of e-wrestling until they're on the 'net. True, but once they are on it that's all they use the Net for! Believe it or not, that's true for a lot of people. And if that is the case, then PBM is the way to go. Like I said, on a strictly e-mail vs. PBM showdown, PBM is cheaper if all you're interested in is wrestling.
