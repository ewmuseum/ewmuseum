An AOL-hosted 1997 real-wrestler-portrayal simulator fed, a second and wholly distinct user of the letters SWF in the same corpus, results-driven with WWF/WCW-name portrayals crowning Steve Austin its first champion. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This federation called itself, like more than one other promotion that same year, simply SWF. Its founding post, on 8 March 1997, ran on America Online and told readers it was opening beyond AOL members for the first time: “We might seem new to you because this is the first time I've let non-aol users to enter the league.”

A results post eight days later recorded a nineteen-match bracket run entirely with real-world wrestlers — Owen Hart, Shawn Michaels, Ric Flair, Ahmed Johnson, Lex Luger, Sabu, Dean Malenko, Kevin Nash, the British Bulldog, Hollywood Hogan, Sting, the Ultimate Warrior, Diamond Dallas Page, Rey Mysterio Jr., the Undertaker, the Giant, the Sultan and Eddie Guerrero among them — ending with Steve Austin, its final victor, declared the promotion's first champion.

No further document of this SWF survives beyond those two postings. What its second show looked like, or how long it ran, is not recorded.
