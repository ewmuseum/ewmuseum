Since <B>Real American Wrestling</B> has been around for nearly 4 years now, and we haven't had much success in getting "over" with the RSPWF board folks other than folks checking out the <A
HREF=" http://members.aol.com/RAWwebsite/RAW.html ">The RAW Website!!</A> and commenting that we were doing a good job with it even though it was a very simple site. So, I wanted to show everyone how our weekly shows are and maybe catch a couple of "big" names into the league. At any rate, I just wanted to let everyone know a little bit about us....first of all, we are an "Open"
Rp'ing league (via eGroups), we have been around since <B>September of 1996</B> and 80% of the roster has been with the league for at least 2 1/2 years! That tells you something there! We have one weekly show (Midweek Massacre) and a bigger bi-weekly show (Weekend Warriors) along with our PPV shows which occur every two months. Other than that, if you have any questions, comments, or requests for lap dances...please e-mail me....[detail withheld]!!
Well, the following posts will be our most recent Weekend Warriors show that we just had crowning the NEW RAW World Champion for the millenium! Hope you enjoy our most recent show....
