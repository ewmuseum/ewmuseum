Thanks to everyone who has applied and congratulations to those of you who have actually been accepted!
Just wanted to post that we are now at a <U>full singles roster</U> and do appreciate the overwhelming reaction to the last few posts. But, we <U>still have one more tag team spot left</U>. Again, you can send in an application for review, but there are no singles roster spots left at this time.
For those of you who aren't familiar with <B>Real American Wrestling</B>, we are a fed that has been around for nearly <B>4 years</B> now and we have a talented and loyal group of members. 80% of them have been in the fed for at least 2 years. At any rate, we only have that one tag team slot if you are interested. If so, please e-mail me for an application. Thanks again!
