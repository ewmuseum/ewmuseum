FASW Saturday Main Event, 10 May 1997, Part 1

14 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene cuts to a black screen, and sets there for a moment like this, until finally a few images of light and life begin to flicker onto the screen. And eventually the whole scene fades to a shot of a crowded press room. Where 100's of people, mostly reporters, of every race, creed, and color from around the globe sit in fold out chairs that cover most of the room's floor. All of which facing a large stage that sets at the front of the room. Where on which sets a long table. Running the length of the stage with a large, wooden podium in the middle. To which microphones, with logos from many different wrestling show and magazine from around the world are attached. And seated at the table are many different men in distinguished suits. Most of which appear to be execs from different companies. Two of these men are recognizable. One, Classic Championship Wrestling President David Lombardi, and another is Federation of All-Star Wrestlers President Mark Straz. They sit on opposite sides of the podium, both right next to it. The room buzzes with the sound of many people talking but they all quiet a little as a man in a gray suit stands and takes the podium, shuffling through some papers and putting on a pair of small reading glasses] Man: Um...excuse me. If I might have your attention please! [The room begins to quiet faster] Man: Thank you! I'd like to thank everyone for coming out tonight. It's a pleasure to have you, and so on behalf of CCW and FASW...thanks! Now, onto business. You all know why you are here. It's to hear the official word on whether or not Ricky Revolution's contract lies solely with Classic Championship Wrestling. Well, I'll let Ricky tell you the extents of the agreement, but I am here to tell you all that it does not. As you all know, Ricky wrestled for a few months in both CCW and IWF/WOW, as well as competing in the World Invitational Championships Inter fed Tournament. But was forced out of both the IWF/WOW and WIC due to injuries, thus keeping his CCW career going strong. Well, Ricky announced that he would remain exclusive to CCW for the duration of his career, but for some reason, this is not the truth. As, Ricky Revolution has recently signed with the Federation of All-Star Wrestlers. Now, the reason for this is unknown, but we hope to find out answers about this here tonight. Now, without further adue, I'd like to introduce to you, the current CCW International Champion, with his manager "SEXY" Sally McClane..."The Bullet" Ricky Revolution!!! ["It's time for a Revolution" followed by a gunshot is heard, as then "Little Things" by Bush begins to play, as out a doorway in the back of the room, comes two people hand in hand] [The one on the left, a good looking, young man, with long brown hair, and a good tan, appears to hold the CCW International Title in his hand. He wears, a pair black and white DC Boxer shoes, that protrude from beneath his baggy, kacky Jenko jeans, while on his white t-shirt is a logo for "Grind King" skate company, and on his face are a pair of small aerodynamic shades as well as a big grin] [The other person is a VERY attractive woman, with long blonde hair and a beautiful smile. She wears some black high heel boots, with a short black skirt, with suspenders and a tight white t-shirt that has a red "13," X'ed out on it] [These two are CCW International Champion, "The Bullet" Ricky Revolution and his manager, "SEXY" Sally McClane. They walk onto the stage and stop in front of the podium as the music dies out] ["The Bullet" Ricky Revolution lays his CCW International Title on the podium] RR: Now, they told me to come out here and answer all of your questions, but before I do, I've got a few things I want to get off of my chest. First one being, my injuries...NO, they will not be a factor. Don't anyone ask! I'm healed up. I think I proved that at CCW Explosion, when I won this. [holds up the International Title] I'm at 100%, aw hell, I'm a 102%, by god. I'm ready to get my career going again. Now CCW's given my a lot of opportunities. I mean, it is there that I realized, on February 15th, the day after Valentine's Day, that I no longer needed the idiotic fans to win matches, and more so that I NEVER did. And I mean, after all, it is there, that I realized that I no longer needed my so-called mentor, and former AEWA Champion, "Superman" Mike Stewart, to win matches, and mores o, never did, and after all it is there, that on one night. On MY BIRTHDAY, on March 30th of 1997! My 25th B-Day, I not only became CCW International Champion by defeating my former friend Bradley Warden in a no holds barred contest, but I also shocked the world by showing everyone, including a fellah by the name of "GQ" Geoffrey Quartz, that Sally McClane was mine and had been all along. And mores o than that, me and Sally proved we're the best thing going today, by unveiling myself a member of the "NEW PHOENIX SQUAD!!!" That legendary stable founded by Fara Phoenix, Curt Crane, and Brian Anthony! So, I must say that without CCW, I would be no where in wrestling right now. But I also must say that I must move on. RR: And...NO! I'm not leaving CCW. I'm just extending my career past it, by joining the Federation of All-Star Wrestlers! And YES, I can hold up!! Now, if Sally has nothing to say, I'll take some questions. Sal? SM: No Rick, that's quite alright, besides, I don't think that these idiots deserve to hear me talk anyway! RR: You're probably right baby. Now, on with the questions, and could we please make it snappy. I'm a busy man. [all the reporters jump and scramble and begin to yell out there questions. Revolution looks around for a moment then points at a blonde woman sitting up front. She is, Sue Lewis from Pro Wrestling Illustrated] RR: You right there, the blonde, from PWI!! [Sue Lewis of PWI Magazine stands with notepad and pen in hand as everyone else sits] SL: Um..thanks! I'd just like to know, if "The Shocker" Brian Anthony's involvement in FASW complicates things? We all know that he's the leader of the "NEW PHOENIX SQUAD" since Curt Crane's retirement, and that you and him aren't on the best of terms at the moment...to say the least! Do you feel that his presence here cold complicate your goals? Or is he the reason you came to this federation, to settle some things that needed to be settled outside of CCW? RR: First off. Anything I need to settle, can be settled in CCW, FASW, YMCA, or wherever else you want to be! I don't need to pick a certain federation to settle things. And NO, he's not the reason I came here. I was looking to sign with a new fed and after taking a look around the best 2 I could find were FASW and SJPW, and since I'm not up to flying back and forth to Japan every week, I chose FASW. And also, his presence won't complicate anything for me. Basically, our problems in CCW, come from the fact that he's getting soft. So if he takes his soft, punchy @$$ out of my way, then he won't have to experience the Revolution. SL: Well, is there still a Phoenix Squad and if so will it be formed in FASW as well? RR: I don't know man. Ask the fearless leader. As for me, I'm still in the Squad, don't know about Brian or Les Canadians, but I'm still a member. Maybe I'm the Final Phoenix, I don't know, but if so then that's the way it'll be, cause I don't plan to go anywhere. Now, next question! [once again they all scramble as Revolution eventually points to a young man in the back. He is Shawn Thorpe, from the RSPWF Network] RR: You, the guy from RSPWF! [Shawn Thorpe from the RSPWF Network stands as everyone else sits] ST: Yes, Rick, I have to know. Are you hear to settle a score with Chris Deugau? RR: Who? ST: [looks confused] Chris Deugau! Winner of Best Technician at the 1996 RSPWF Awards, ranked 13th at the RSPWF 200. You know! RR: Deugau? Deugau? OH YEAH...that guy that I beat during the WIC Inter fed Tournament! I remember him. And what score? I don't have a score to settle with him, I think that if anything, he should have a score to settle with me. Cause I mean I DID BEAT HIM! Remember? ST: Yes I do. RR: So why should I have the score? But I will agree that, me and Deugie did have a great match, and we never had a rematch. Because, well, I beat him when I had only been in wrestling for a couple months and when I wanted a rematch on the last card of the Tourney they were scared to give it to me, cause they didn't want to ruin the best Technician in the world's image. It's as simple as that. ST: But what about Sally's shirt? RR: What about it? ST: The 13, marked out? RR: She doesn't like Friday the 13th movies, so what? ST: You mean it doesn't stand for #13, the number Chris Deugau was voted for in the RSPWF 200? RR: Deugau was voted for in the RSPWF 200? Man, I need to pay more attention, now can we quit talking about him. Next question! [the man sits down as they all begin to scramble once again. Until Revolution points at a good looking brunette, who represents The BagPipe Report] RR: You, from TBR! BR: Thanks Ricky! And I was wondering if the following rumors are true. Are you planning on bringing in a tag team partner to FASW, or are you going to tag with someone that's already here? And is it true you're planning a world tour? RR: As for the tag team thing. I don't know where that one got started. I'm a singles wrestler, plain and simple. And for the world tour, I've got to defend my title and wrestling in 2 feds. I don't think I'm ready for a World Tour just yet. Next question! [once again they scramble as Revolution eventually points to Rob Taylor of The Wrestler magazine] RR: You, hurry up! RT: Um Rick, with all the people gunning for you how can you hold up? RR: What kinda question is that? RT: I mean, you have feuds with people in CCW, and potential ones setting up in FASW. Basically, you've pissed off everyone in CCW at sometime, even your own stable mates, and you've got some people that could be mad at you in FASW. With all of them gunning for you, how can you hold up? RR: I just will okay? I'm not worried about who's gunning for me or not. I'm worried about, how fast I can take care of them. I am a busy man you know? Now if the questions don't get better, I'm getting out of here! Next! [they all scramble and he points to an older gentlemen, in a brown suit, this man is Rick Scott from The Wrestling Insider] RR: Rick, from the Insider, go for it. RS: [stands] Okay Ricky, I have to know, when are you and Sally getting married? RR: [looks around disgustedly] What the hell kind of question is that? What's next from you journalistic geniuses, "Boxers or Briefs" and before you ask, it is boxers. Now I'm not going to stand here and be asked idiotic questions by feeble idiotic people. Now if you'll excuse us. THAT, was the last question. [Revolution picks up his CCW International Title and turns to walk off the stage, taking Sally's hand as he does and then walks by, CCW Prez. David Lombardi who extends his hand in friendship as Ricky ignores him.
