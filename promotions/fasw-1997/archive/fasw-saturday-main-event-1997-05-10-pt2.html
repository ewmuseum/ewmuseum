FASW Saturday Main Event, 10 May 1997, Part 2

14 May 1997

MW: What a great opening match, as experience is victorious over the new commer. Chris Deugau advance to the next round to face the winner of our next match, featuring "Kid Attitude" Scott Flashe and "The Sportsman" Marcus Wilkins. JL: Deugau looked quite impressive against Rick Hunter just now, so whoever he faces ion two weeks time will in for a real challenge. MW: Both these guys coming up are great wrestlers, either one of them has a legitimate shot at the Television Title, Deugau is the one who should be watching this match very closely. JL: He's the best technical wrestler around, he knows how to do his homework. MW: He didn't seem to do it against Ricky Revolution when they faced each other, same thing can happen here in two weeks! Let's go to the match! RA: This next match is scheduled for one fall, and is the second match in the FASW Television title tournament... Introducing first... Coming down the aisle weighing in at 256 pounds... Hailing from parts unknown.. Here is "T H E S P O R T S M A N" M A R C U S W I L K I N S !!!! MW: Wilkins looks ready for this match, he told me earlier tonight that not only being in the FASW is an honor, but he main goal is that Television belt. JL: He's got a long way to go before he gets there, first he has to deal with Scott Flashe tonight, here and now. MW: Your not giving Wilkins much credit are you? JL: I've never seen him before? Why should I? MW: You haven't seen Scott Flashe before either before, yet you think he's going to whipe the floor with him. JL: Experienced guess! MW: Let's get back to the introductions.. RA: And his opponent... Hailing from Denver, Colorado... Weighing in at 250 pounds... Here is... "K I D A T T I T U D E" S C O T T F L A S H E !!!!! [Simply the Best by Tina Turner begins to play over the PA system as most of the females cheer, and the males boo him as he makes his way down the aisle. Scotty Flashe comes to the ring wearing his biker jacket and aviator sunglasses. He struts down the aisle, telling off the male fans, making sure they know that HE is the only REAL man in the entire arena. He flirts with the ladies, and, if one is attractive enough, he may even kiss one. After all, the pleasure is all theirs.] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- "Kid Attitude" Scott Flashe vs. "The Sportsman" Marcus Wilkins -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Well, Marcus Wilkins took it to Flashe early in this match when he nailed him with a clothesline upon entering the ring and then a big boot to boot. Wilkins dropped a leg on his neck and covered for a two count. Wilkins lifted Flashe up in a hotshot and covered again for a two count. Wilkins then whipped Flashe into the corner and nailed him with a running clothesline and then scooped him up in a slam. Wilkins dropped the elbow and got a two count. Wilkins sent Flashe to the outside, but Flashe reversed a whip into the ringpost. Flashe then dropped him with a running knee lift. Flashe rolled Wilkins into the ring and nailed him with a powerslam for a two count. He controlled the next several minutes before going to the second rope for a clothesline. Wilkins caught him and spikeslammed him. Wilkins controlled the next few minutes with power moves and almost got a pinfall with a somoan drop. Wilkins then set Flashe up for the powerbomb, but Flashe backdropped out of it. Scott Flashe took his momentum and hit Wilkins with a sidewalk slam and then a flying shoulderblock for the 1-2-3. RA: The winner of the match... "KID ATTITUDE" SCOTT FLASHE!!! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= MW: Damn your two for two here tonight Feen, I got talk to you about the NBA playoff's after the show! JL: Sorry there Mikey, but I don't give my picks to anyone. Last time I did, the guy made over ten grand and me nada because I didn't play. Not this time! MW: So your admitting you gamble eh? That can cost you your job here in the FASW! JL: Ummm.. weeeelll... Talk to me in the parking lot after ok? MW: No thank you, I don't gamble. JL: But you just asked me.. MW: I asked you that so I can if YOU gamble.. wonder who they'll replace you with next week? JL: That's low man.. even for you Whalden. MW: Folks up next is our first tag team match of the FASW, and what a match it's going to be! Multi-fed champions, the Awesome Alliance will be going up against two of the brightest young stars in the game today, the "Z-Man" Brian Zordich, and a wrestler who's done exceptional well in the singles ranks, "Wild Thing" Shane Micheals! JL: Bah! Sure they are both great singles stars, but can they work together as a team? The Awesome Alliance have been together for a long time, and have won several tag team belts. You got to give the edge to the AA. MW: AA? Ok we all know that they go there, but that's no reason to talk about their personal life on the air. JL: I thought I'm the one who's supposed to come up with the wise crack remarks? MW: Let's go to the ring! RA: This next match is a tag team scheduled for one fall.. And is the first match in the FASW tag team tournament!! Introducing first.. Hailing from parts unknown.. Weighing in at a total combined weight of 523 pounds... Here is MR. FABULOUS and MR. MARVELOUS... They are T H E A W E S O M E A L L I A N C E !!!! [No music is played as they walk down the aisle to the ring, they completely ignore everyone as they step into the ring and wait for there opponents.] MW: Is it me or where they more emotional when we heard from them last week on FASW Report? JL: I got no idea why they are acting like that, my using some mind games on the Danger Zone? MW: Could be, let's hope they can focus on their match or they'll be out of here faster than a Mark McGuire home run! JL: Show me the Money! MW: That's the wrong McGuire there Feen. JL: Ya I know, but I always wanted to say that on the air! MW: Let's go back to the ring for the rest of the introduction.. You know Feen, you really amaze me sometimes! RA: And their opponents... First hailing from right here in Minneapolis, Minnesota!!! Weighing in at 234 pounds... Here is "WILD THING" SHANE MICHAELS!!! And his tag team partner.. Hailing from Tallahasse, Florida.. And weighing in at 245 pounds... Here is the "Z-MAN" BRIAN ZORDICH.. They are T H E D A N G E R Z O N E !!!!! [The fans of the St-Paul Civic center explode with cheers as Shane Michaels walks threw the curtain followed by his partner Brian Zordich as "Danger Zone" by Kenny Loggins blasts over the PA system. Shane is a muscular caucasian with long blond hair. He looks a lot like Chris Jericho from WCW. The Z-Man is a muscular caucasian with short black hair.] MW: What an ovation for the hometown hero Shane Michaels! JL: The fans can cheer all they want now, but once that bell rings the only thing that can save the Danger Zone is Emergency team back stage. At least Michaels is close to his family tonight. He won't have to dish out money to fly them over to see him and his partner in the hospital beds! MW: Your too much Feen.. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Awesome Alliance vs. Danger Zone -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Both teams where at a stare down before the bell went. Shane Micheals and Mr.Marvelous started things out. Mr.Marvelous quickly used his strength to his advantage and got Shane Micheals in a bearhug. Mr.Marvelous let go and nailed him with a tilt a whirl sidewalk slam for a two count before Zordich made the save. This caused Mr.Fabulous to get into the ring and he nailed Zordich with some lefts and rights. Mr.Marvelous whipped Micheals into Zordich into the corner and then nailed him with a belly to belly suplex and Mr.Fabulous followed it up with a big splash. This would have garnered the three count had Zordich not recovered in time to make the save. Zordich and Mr.Marvelous were ushered back to their respective corners as Mr.Fabulous punished Micheals for a few more minutes. Mr.Fabulous made the tag out to Mr.Marvelous and Mr.Marvelous came in and nailed Micheals with a Somoan Drop for the two count. Mr.Marvelous then whipped Micheals hard into the buckle and went for the avalanche, but Micheals fell out of the corner too quickly. Micheals made the tag out to Zordich and he came in and nailed Mr.Marvelous with a big boot and gave one to Mr.Fabulous as well when he came in. Micheals slammed Wrath and then hit Mr.Fabulous with a clothesline that got him caught in the ropes. Micheals recovered and they nailed Wrath with a double team clothesline, but the ref was too busy trying to get Mr.Fabulous out of the ropes to make the count. By the time he finally did, Mr.Marvelous has recovered and schoolboyed Zordich for a quick two count. Mr.Fabulous nailed Zordich with a big legdrop and then rolled out of the ring. Mr.Marvelous covered Zordich for a two count and Micheals nailed Mr.Fabulous with a double axehandle from the apron and then ran him into the guardrail. Mr.Marvelous tried to get Zordich in a torture rack, but Zordich got out of the move and then slammed Mr.Marvelous into a corner and back bodydropped him. By this time, Micheals was on the top ringpost, Zordich grabbed him and tossed him onto Mr.Marvelous for the three count. RA: The winners of the match... THE DANGER ZONE!!!! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= MW: What a huge win for Michaels and Zordich! And listen to the fans as they continue to cheer and chat Danger Zone! This is what the FASW is all about Feen! JL: They got a lucky break Mike, the Awesome Alliance didn't seen to be into this match at all. MW: Now you see why the home filed advantage is so important, no matter what sport your into! Folks we got to take a small break in the action, but when we come back, we'll go to Bucky Malone who will bring you a special report on the FASW's first Pay Per View spectacular..
