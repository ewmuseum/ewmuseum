About The League 1. What is the FASW? The FASW stands for "Federation of All-Star Wrestlers", and is one of many e-wrestling federations around. The FASW is a simple yet challenging promotion, structured to bring fun and excitement back in e-wrestling. 2. Who runs the FASW? Ficticious Operators - Derek Owens: FASW President and member of the Executive Commitee. Handles all the paper work, fines and other duties behind the FASW scenes. Tim Cross: The voice of the FASW. Handles all the play by play coverage on Main Event's and FASW pay-per-views. Jerry "The Feen" Leelan: Smart-ass color commentator on FASW Main Event's. David Pelter: FASW Ring Announcer par excellence. Will announce the winners and losers of all FASW matches. Ed Wailen: Backstage interviewer. Tends to favor the face wrestlers. He's also the head of the FASW Hotline. Sandra Wyld: Ex-adult movie star, now a backstage interviewer. She tends to lean towards the good looking wrestlers, either face or heel. 1. What titles are there in the FASW? FASW World Heavyweight Title (This is THE title of the federation. The best overall and it must be defended at least once every third card.) FASW World Television Title (This is the second-best title in the FASW and must be defended _EVERY_ week.) 2. The FASW does not currently have a tag team division so no tag team titles available at this moment. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= II. Rules and Regulations 1. How are the Matches determined? The outcomes of all matches are decided by a booking system based on heat. How does one get heat? Roleplay. The better you roleplay, the bigger the heat, the bigger the push. Everyone will lose once in a while to further an angle so if your worried about having a perfect record, don't bother joining. 2. What are the roleplay rules? The MAXIMUM amount of roleplays that can be done per-week are two (2). One "In-Ring" to be shown on the weekly card, and a second one (a flash type) to be aired on a weekly interview show. The reason for only being able to do 2 RP's per week is to force handlers to make the best possible roleplays and to prevent any spam. 3. How do I get matches? There are a couple of ways. - I sign you to a match - You challenge someone in a roleplay and send a "formal" request to me - You send in the "formal" request 4. Are sneak attacks allowed? Yes, in moderation. We have the right to refuse a sneak attack if we see it has no value. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= III. Events and Shows. 1. What shows are there? FASW Saturday Night Main Event (Main Show) Basically we have combined the weekly show with the standard house show. The cards will writen like normal cards. Most of it will be short reports, but we'll try and give as much detail as possible. Each card will have 4-6 name matches and the occasional jobber match. Most "in-ring" interviews will be shown here. Every once in a while, the scheduled FASW Main Event will be house show reported, to give a small break from doing whole cards. FASW Countdown to Main Event (Hype up show) - Interviews (flashes) from wrestlers - Preview of Next show - Recap of Previous show - Supercard Watch - Possible bonus match(es) 2. Are there Pay Per View's? Yes there are Pay-Per-Views. In order to get on a pay-per-view you'll have to be good. What we mean is that your wrestler must be drawing "heat" and fans want to see him on the card. Not everyone on the roster gets on a pay-per-view. There are 4 "Pay-Per-View's" per year. 3. Where are the shows held? - FASW holds it's cards across Canada and the United States in various arenas. From the big arenas to the smaller convention halls. We might go on tour out of North-America at times as well. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= IV. Titles 1. How will the titles be decided? They can be decided in a number of ways. There will be tournaments to decided the first holders of the Titles. 2. How often will the titles be defended? FASW World Heavyweight Belt - At least every 3rd show and all pay-per-views. FASW World Television Belt - _EVERY_ Main Event and all pay-per-views. 3. How can I get a title shot? - If the champion challenges you and we see fit. - If you're one of the Top 5 active contenders for the HW belt, or the Top 10 for the TV belt. - If we see fit you deserve one. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= V. Match Info. 1. How often can my guy wrestle? There is no limit, but you'll most likely see your guy in the ring every second card or so. 2. Where are the matches held? In a regular match, they're held in an 15'x15' square ring (WCW standard size) that has a black mat with the FASW logo in red and white in the middle of the ring and a black "skirt" around the ring with "Main Event" written on it in silver. The top and bottom ropes are in dark red with the middle one in black. 3. Can I add stipulations to my matches? Yes, within reason. However both parties (wrestlers) have to agree to them). 4. What are the regular rules to an average match? Standard rules are always in effect, which means a 20 minute time limit, DQ's and Countouts are applied. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= VI.
