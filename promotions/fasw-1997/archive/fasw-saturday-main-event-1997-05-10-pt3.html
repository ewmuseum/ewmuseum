For the first two rounds, you must, and I mean you MUST put your differences aside to get to the Battle Royal part. Then you can beat up on your tag team partner. When the FASW World Heavyweight Title is on the line, you make your worst enemy your best friend for two matches. And then... you can kick his butt and everyone elses to get that gold! MW: We'll be bringing more details next week on Main Event for Super Bowl I, but now we got some action here on FASW MAIN EVENT! JL: This next match is going to be REAL interesting! MW: That's right as Japanese sensation Akria Hasimoto will be going up against the snob from Connecticut Jean-Paul Richard! Well due to contractual obligations, Akira was obliged to fly down here tonight to face his opponent as scheduled. JL: I got a feeling this will be his last match in the FASW. Can you imagine him winning the FASW TV belt or worst yet the FASW World title, only to trash it in Japan? MW: The ironic part of it all, is that Akira beat Shane Michaels for that belt a while ago in the TAEWF, so you know he's gonna be watching this match very closely. JL: Didn't the AC have something to do with that belt? Hahahaha!! Couldn't they just make another belt? I remember a wrestler there BURNING a title, they didn't exactly do much just replaced it. Why go threw all this because he trashed a belt? MW: Well we really shouldn't dwell on what's going on there. It's not our, or the FASW's concern to the problems of other feds. Akira was obligated to show up here tonight, and he did. And with that, let's go to the introductions! RA: This next match is scheduled for one fall... Introducing first.. Coming down the aisle at a weight of 234 pounds... He is multi fed champion.. Here is A K I R A H A S H I M O T O !!!! [Almost the entire crowd stands up and boo Akira as he makes his way to the ring. People are even throwing their drinks and paper at him as he walks emotionless to the ring. Several "USA" chants are heard from the audience.] MW: An interesting ovation for Akira. I don't think he ever got an introduction like that from the fans! JL: Well he deserves it! He turned his back on all these morons and this country, no kidding they are all over him. I wouldn't be surprised if he never comes back to the Untied States again after this match. MW: For once, I got to agree with you on this one! Let's go back to the ring. RA: And his opponent... Hailing from Greenwich, Connecticut... Weighing in at 279 pounds... Here is J E A N - P A U L R I C H A R D !!!! [As "O Fortuna" preformed by the Philharmonia Orchestra plays over the PA system, the fans actually cheer Richards as he makes his way to the ring. He has very long dirty blonde hair. He wears long black tights with a red mesh in the inner part of the tights which ends near the knees. He also wears black boots and a black vest to the ring.] MW: What a surprising ovation for Jean-Paul Richard! Not even once during his career did he ever get cheered, much less like this tonight! JL: Richard is one of my favorites. He's got class, money, fame, and finally the fan respect him! MW: I don't think the fans respect him Feen, I think the fans just hate Akira so much that they rather cheer Richard. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Akira Hashimoto vs. Jean-Paul Richard (J) -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Akira Hashimoto started the match off very quickly with a few chops and European uppercuts. Akira then hit Richard with a running/jumping dropkick after throwing Richard into the ropes. Akira then hit Richard with side suplex side suplex and went for the pin but only got a 2 count. Akira whiped Richard into the corner and followed that up with a cartwheel/ summersault/elbow smash combo and that made Richard fall down like a dead tree trunk. Akira then quickly leaped to the top turnbuckle and hit Richard with Shooting Star Press and went for the cover. However once again Richard somehow managed to kick out at the 2 count. Akira seemed to become extremely frustrated, especially with the fans continuing to chant "USA", "USA". Akira picked up Richard who looked to be out of it, and sent him into the ropes again and attempted to hit a backbody drop on Richard, but Akira put his head down to soon, and on his return, Jean-Paul Richard hooked Akira's arms and hit "The Pedigree" on Akira and got the 1-2-3 pin on Akira Hashimoto. RA: The winner of this match.... JEAN-PAUL RICHARD!!!! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= JL: Huh! I guess anything can happen in wrestling! MW: What a huge upset we have here tonight folks! Jean-Paul Richard has not only beat one of the most respected wrestler stars in the world, but he pinned him 1-2-3 right in the middle of the ring! All I can is WOW! JL: What a way for Akira to start his FASW career. MW: If thing continue to go like this for Akira, this might have been the only time we ever see Akira Hashimoto in a FASW ring! JL: Who cares anyway, let him go back to his country and stay there! MW: That's not really nice to say.. Anyhow our next match here tonight on Main Event should be a great one indeed! As "Ambusher" Mike Trent takes on Blair Allen in FASW Television title tournament action! JL: Got to go with Blair Allen on this one. He seems to be focused on winning the Television title ever since he signed his FASW contract. JL: Hey your right, that is the best known stable in wrestling today.. the best known stable to get the crap kicked out them! Hahaha! MW: <sighs> Let's go to the ring.. Allen no showed and Chris Masters as the opportunity of a life time for a shot at the FASW World Television title! JL: This is not fair! This match should be re-scheduled for next week or something! MW: Listen Feen, he knew he had a match here weeks ago, it's like any other job.. You don't show up to work, you find someone else to replace you! They started brawling on the outside. Trent rammed Masters head into the guardrail several times. He went to irish whip Masters backfirst into the ring apron, but Masters reversed it and Mike Trent went backfirst into the apron and Masters nailed him with a powerslam on the outside. Masters then rolled him into the ring and nailed him with a side Russian legsweep. Mike Trent got right back up though. Masters poked Mike Trent in the eyes and then nailed him with a vertical suplex. Mike Trent got right back up though. Frustrated, Masters sent Mike Trent into the ropes only to taste mat when Mike Trent stopped midstride and grabbed Masters in a Diamond Cutter. Trent then started to bite Masters who rolled to the outside. Mike Trent was giving him no breaks though as Mike Trent went for a somersault plancha, but Masters moved and Mike Trent hit the floor. Mike Trent got up to have Masters clothesline him in the back of the head and roll him into the ring. Masters nailed him with a headbutt from the second rope and then got a two count. Masters hit Mike Trent with a fallaway slam, Mike Trent kept rolling until he was outside the ring. Masters followed and choked him out with a rope he found under the mats. Masters, who was momentarily detracted by the referee, was then flipped over Mike Trent's back. Trent then went to find some kind of weapon, and managed to find a fire extinguisher. He brought in into the ring and sprayed the whatever it is in those things all over Masters and then rolled him into the ring. Mike Trent went to the top and jumped off with a frankensteiner on Masters, but Masters kicked out at 2. Mike Trent nailed several high impact moves in the next minute, each one should have put away Masters but each time the Lionheart kicked out at 2. The end came when Mike Trent went to clothesline Masters over the top, but Masters backdropped him over the top. Masters then nailed him with gutwrench suplex on the outside. Masters got ahold of the fire extinguisher and returned the favor to him! Masters rolled Mike Trent into the ring, but could not get a pin with a brainbuster. Masters then nailed Trent with the "Lion's Strike" (diamond cutter) to get the 1-2-3. RA: The winner of this match...
