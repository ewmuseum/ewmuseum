Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

TSC (theSquaredCircle) survives, in its own right, through one document: a viewer's own write-up, posted 28 December 1999, of the promotion's Superstar Saturday card two nights earlier, pointing readers on to the fed's own site for the full results.

The write-up centers a World Title defense — champion Shawn Parker against Neo Zorbey, an animosity the correspondent traces back to Zorbey opening the feud with a chair shot to Parker's back — alongside a women's match between Akira Kahn and Peggy Branigan (“you won't believe the ending”), a singles bout between Randy Acorn and Jack Dawson, and appearances by Dylan Rapture, Brian Coleman and “tormented” Tommy Rifles.

Beyond this one document, TSC is known only through brief mentions in other federations' own records. No founding statement, roster document, or further recap survives; how, or whether, the world title program it describes was ever settled is not recorded.
