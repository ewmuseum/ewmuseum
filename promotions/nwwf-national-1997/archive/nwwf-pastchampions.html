NWWF PAST CHAMPIONS

Since the NWWF first opened it's doors for business in the early summer of 1997, many great roleplayers have stepped through. Here is a list of the men who were able to win a championship in the NWWF.

"The Dominator" Don Cameron -- 11-29-97 8. Deuce Demon -- 1-4-98 9. Alien-1 -- 1-31-98 10. Demi God -- 2-18-98 11. VACANT -- 3-12-98 12. "The Ultimate Destroyer" Katis -- 4-13-98 13. VACANT -- 4-26-98 14. PhantomLord -- 5-5-98
