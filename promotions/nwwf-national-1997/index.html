A GeoCities-hosted federation that survived a change of leadership in mid-1998 to keep running two weekly shows and eight championships into the summer of 1999. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The National World Wrestling Federation dates its own founding to 21 July 1997 on its own council page, and recruited by September of that year as a fed “going since early summer,” run mainly by e-mail. Its own president signed a recruitment post that month naming himself proudly: “This is the best fed you will join today.” It crowned its first World Champion the week it opened — “Hotshot” Mike Stewart, on 7 July 1997 — and ran an Intercontinental, a United States, and a Television/Internet Title alongside it, the United States belt retired outright that September after only two reigns.

Fourteen World Title changes are recorded across its own history, from Stewart's first reign through “The Ultimate Destroyer” Katis and a wrestler using the name PhantomLord, the last recorded champion, crowned 5 May 1998. Leadership changed hands repeatedly in that same stretch — a new president that June 1997, another that April 1998, a commissioner installed for ten days that same April — while an owner and CEO retained standing authority to overturn any of their decisions.

The fed carried on past its own leadership turnover: its GeoCities site, unchanged in its rules and rosters from October 1999 through captures as late as 2001, still ran a Hardcore Title and a Tag Team division and pointed wrestlers turned away toward its own “farm” league, the Degenerate World Wrestling Federation. Its newest surviving content is a 7 July 1999 news update. No closure statement survives; nothing later is held in the record.

Shared continuity with NWWF (1997)
The newer federation's own FAQ credits this federation as a major influence that, after its first closure, gave it many of its own top stars; the newer federation's own tribute page separately names its own highlight results and an eleven-name shared roster, several of them (this federation's own "Dominator" Don Cameron chief among them) already recorded as continuing careers on both federations' own held records.
