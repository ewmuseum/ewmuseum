Prime Time in North America, 21 July 1998

21 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
 (From the rafters the cameras pan down on The Palace of Auburn Hills in Auburn Hills, Michigan. Fireworks begin to explode, sparks ignite from the ringposts, and mammoth spotlights sweep across the legions of fans. Enormous banners bearing the "CWC: Prime Time in North America" logo hang in all four corners of this massive arena. The camera zooms in on many fans' signs, such as "Come back, Neptune!," "Come back, Ares!," and "Stay the F@## away, Liam Walker!") (The camera then shows CWC announcers Matt Randall and "Sweet Daddy" Al Diamond. Randall is your usual neutral announcer, though he's blonde, the correct weight, and has no facial disorders. Al Diamond is a huge individual, as well as the quintessential hippie.) RANDALL: "WELCOME EVERYONE! WELCOME TO AUBURN HILLS, MICHIGAN!!!!!!" (The camera sweeps through the berserk crowd) RANDALL: "WELCOME TO THE BEAUTIFUL PALACE OF AUBURN HILLS!!!!" (The cheers only grow more deafening.) RANDALL: "WELCOME EVERYONE TO THE CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING COALITION! WELCOME… TO PRIME TIME IN NORTH AMERICA!!!!" DIAMOND: "Good lord, we have NEVER had a crowd like this!" RANDALL: "Al, we've had great special events in the past, namely the Public Exercise and, of course, First Night. But no card in CWC history compares to the one we'll be seeing here tonight!" DIAMOND: "Matt, for once I have to agree with you. And why? Because this is our most unpredictable event EVER!" RANDALL: "Exactly, Al. We tried to find out what the people were predicting earlier today by asking fans in the crowd, but we just couldn't get a consensus. St. Claire versus Garces. The Icons versus the Wrecking Crew. The Four-Way North American Title Match. And of course our Main Event. You're not going to see any squashes tonight, fans. These bouts are going to be as close as they come!" (Techno-type music begins to play over the P.A.) DIAMOND: "What is this? Did we finally get those Nitro Girls I was asking for?" RANDALL: "Well, Al, we couldn't get them. Seems all the old `In Living Color Fly Girls' are working for WCW. But we did find our own dancing troop!" (Racing down the aisle comes Toki Dobi, the midget in the Barney-like green dragon suit. Following him are nineteen other midgets also in dragon costumes. They are all identical except for their colors, which span from white to pink to blue to yellow… basically every color you can imagine.) DIAMOND: "THIS is our dancing troop?" RANDALL: "The crowd seems to be enjoying it." (Led by Toki Dobi, the dragons begin to hop about in a very Nitro-girlish type of way.) DIAMOND: "Oh, well. The Nitro Girls aren't too fun to watch, either. I mean, it's so frustrating to know that a girl who looks like Kimberly is married to such a nasty old man like Diamond Dallas! Come on, girls, isn't `Sweet Daddy' old and nasty enough for you?" (Suddenly the lights go out. The crowd instantly erupts into a deafening pop. The dragons immediately scatter.) RANDALL: "And it seems that things are about to get underway here in Auburn Hills!" ("Victory" by Puff Daddy begins to play over the P.A. Somehow, the crowd seems to get even louder than before!) DIAMOND: "Man, these fans sure love Jeremy `The Juice' Goodlett. When they hear that song, they know their hero-" RANDALL: (interrupting) "Right theme music, wrong wrestler, Al! Or should I say wrestlers… I think we're about to see the Hardcore Icons!" (The fans begin to wave their pro-Hardcore Icons signs and chanting "EYE-CONS...EYE-CONS". The beat picks up and Steel and Earl "The Hammer" Mandigault appear at the top of the entrance ramp. Hammer begins doing a lil' shimmy dance, which the crowd seems to eat up as it continues its chants. Steel pumps his fist in the air as he lets out a thunderous war cry, again exciting the crowd.) DIAMOND: "I have to hand it to the Icons; they know how to work over a crowd. Of course, they've had about ninety years to practice their technique." RANDALL: "Of course, some of the crowd is still chanting `Wreeeeecking Crew,' which has been going on ever since the building was opened! Both these teams have their legions of fans, that's for sure." (Hammer, smiling the entire time, attempts to silence the crowd. But they begin to chant his name, so he bows...yet again they pop loudly. Steel finally silences the crowd, and while still standing at the top of the entrance ramp, he pulls out a microphone and extends it to the crowd for one last round of cheers. Then he silences them as he speaks...) STEEL: "Auburn Hills...if you're ugly, SHUT UP!!" (Much of he crowd explodes into laughter and another tremendous face pop.) STEEL: "Now, being near my hometown of Coldwater, I know that the Palace is 100% pro-Hardcore, right?" (Many people in the crowd cheer "DAMN RIGHT!" Others, however, continue to grow louder and recruit others with their Wrecking Crew chant.) STEEL: Now that's Michigan for ya'. Only the toughest survive! Now, down to some business! L.A. Wrecking Crew... (The crowd gives a mixed reaction of boos, slightly over-shadowing the cheers.) STEEL: "I feel the same way. Tonight, the Wrecking Crew gets everything they ever asked for. You wanted to take us on...here's your chance! You wanted the tag title belts...here's your chance! You wanted to die young...WE'LL DELIVER! "Cause tonight...tonight we're playin' hardball, the way a Michigan man learned to play on the streets! The journey started early on in the year...now here we are in the dead heat of summer and the fire of hate and anger is burnin' me up inside! In a lil' bit we'll be layin' the smack to ya, HARDCORE STYLE! We made it outta the hospital, and we're lookin' for a WAR! Ol' goat, teach tha' kids..." (Much of the crowd continues to chant "EYE-CONS!" "EYE-CONS!" as Steel hands the microphone to Hammer. As soon as he touches the mic, his fans give him another standing ovation. He gives a slight bow before he speaks...) RANDALL: "Undoubtedly Hammer's fans are saluting him for having the courage to step back into the ring even after that horrible injury." DIAMOND: "Please. Hammer should be committed for entering this match. The only thing he should be commended for how he cleaned his own bedpan in the old folks' hospital." HAMMER: "Looks who's back!!! (Much of the crowd pops tremendously again) CWC...L.A. Wrecking Crew...EVERYONE who doubted us...after tonight you'll have hell t'pay 'cause this midnight train is on a one-way track and we're headin' straight to the top! In our path we're leavin' a trail of broken bones and shattered dreams...Wreckin' Crew...welcome to hardcore HELL!!! DIAMOND: "Well, we're obviously witnessing the brain damage of Earl Mandiault." RANDALL: "I think you mean Mandigault, Al. There's a `g' in there." DIAMOND: "Not to him, there's not. From listening to him talk, I don't think he's aware that letter exists!" (Hammer throws down the mic and the crowd rises to their feet, many cheering and chanting for the Icons while others express their support for the Wrecking Crew. The Icons then walk back into the locker room area.) RANDALL: "Wow, as if this crowd wasn't crazy enough, the Icons seem to have raised it to a fever pitch! The atmosphere in this arena is just incredible, fans!" DIAMOND: "Well, I couldn't care less about that tag match, now that Superstars, Inc. was cheated out of it. But Superstars, Inc.'s not leaving here empty-handed, Matt, courtesy of `The Playboy' Jude St. Claire and `The Blockbuster' Johnny Detson!" RANDALL: "Fans, let's introduce our guest commentator for this match!" ("Siren" by Tori Amos begins to play over the P.A. A thirty-ish-looking, fairly slight-of-frame woman walks down the entrance ramp, wearing a brown suede jacket, black slacks and boots, and a yellow collar shirt. Her blonde hair is down, partially covered by a Detroit Tigers baseball cap. She sits down between Matt and Al.) RANDALL: "Welcome back, Elise Marie Amico." AMICO: "Wow, my first special event. Mom would be proud… that is, she'd be proud if she wasn't totally embarrassed that her daughter was involved in professional wrestling." DIAMOND: "Don't worry about it. My father, "Sweet Granddaddy" Al Diamond Sr., wasn't too into wrestling, either. But after I hit him with a steel chair, he stopped criticizing me entirely." AMICO: "Gee, Al, you should be a family counselor." DIAMOND: "Nah, I'm not into families, monogamy, or any of that garbage. All of that runs against my promiscuous hippie lifestyle." RANDALL: "Promiscuous? Please. Even Goldust in his early days got more women than you!" DIAMOND: "Shut up, Matt! You've never even been with a woman, unless you count your Jose Garces doll!" RANDALL: "Well, I'd love to continue this debate with you, Al, but apparently we have Steve Norwood up in the rafters with an interview. Steve, are you there?" (The camera cuts to a shot of the Baby Face Brawler; he's up in the cheap seats among the crowd signing autographs for a group of three teenage girls, all of whom have a dreamy look in their eyes.) (Steve Norwood approaches wearing a basic suit, kneeling down next to Babyface's seat. Babyface is wearing jeans and a T-shirt that reads "It's ALL About Respect".) DIAMOND: "Who gave him air-time?" RANDALL: "The Baby Face Brawler had a good showing at the eight-man rookie match last week, Al, and since then his popularity has been on the rise. Just ask those three girls." DIAMOND: "Oh, I have a question for those girls, all right, but believe me, it has NOTHING to do with the Baby Face Brawler!" NORWOOD: "So, we meet again, Mr. Face. What are you doing up here in the nosebleed section?" BABYFACE: "These are the best seats in the house; this is where the fans are really into it. The people up front get on TV with the signs and stuff but up here you can really feel the love." (As he says that a cup goes whizzing by.) NORWOOD: "The first match is a 4-way dance for the North American Title. Frank Claiborne is one of the participants and a lot of insiders anticipate interference from the Strangler, a man you don't seem to take a liking to. Is there any chance that you will continue your campaign to be put on his hit-list by preventing him from interfering? BABYFACE: "You better believe it, Mr. Norwood. I'm going to move a little bit closer to ringside for that match and if the Strangler does show himself, let's just say it'll be Nap Time!" NORWOOD: "How's your quest going to gain some `rookie respect?' Have you seen any CWC officials? What about Bryan Sweet?" BABYFACE: "No, but I expect to be in Chicago a lot over the next few weeks until I get some answers that I'm satisfied with. As for Bryan Sweet, he says as far as he can tell I'm nothing more than a Bryan Sweet clone. Keep dreaming Bryan; you could never be as good as me, heck you're not even half the man your momma is. That's why I don't understand why CWC is pushing you as one of the top two rookies. Unless they got confused while going over the bios as well and thought they were talking about me and they mistakenly said you name. Time will show who is the better man, but I'm not a patient individual." NORWOOD: "What about the other rookie that's being pushed as being one of the top two, Chris Pain?" BABYFACE: "I have no problems with Chris Pain; he hasn't been on TV talking about me has he? He may be one of the top two rookies in the CWC but I know one thing for sure: I'm the other one." NORWOOD: "Well, you heard it here from the Baby Face Brawler. Back to you, Matt." (The camera switches back to Matt, Elise, and Al.) RANDALL: "Thank you, Steve. The Brawler certainly raised an interesting question there, guys: will the Strangler interfere in our upcoming North American title match?" DIAMOND: "He's crazy if he comes to the ring. The other three will probably mistake him for Claiborne and attack him; they look so darned similar." RANDALL: "Well, we heard a few minutes before the show began, fans, that Claiborne was spotted fighting with somebody backstage. Could it have been the Strangler?" DIAMOND: "I'm not sure those two guys would fight. They'd probably have some sort of `fastest-draw showdown' or whatever it is that cowboys do." RANDALL: "Let's go to the ring and get our first match, the Four-Way North American Title Match, underway!" (CWC President Jean Watts comes out as "Bulls on Parade" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play over the P.A.. As always, she wears her dull-looking business suit, which is not at all flattering on her six foot, 150 pound frame. She enters the ring and grabs the microphone.) DIAMOND: "What? No Buffer Daddy this week? This is an outrage!" RANDALL: "Don't you have any respect for your boss, Al?" DIAMOND: "No way, Matt. If you show respect for your boss in the wrestling world, you end up having to wear a stupid shirt and tie like Mankind!" WATTS: "AUBURN HILLS… ARE YOU READY TO GET PRIME TIME IN NORTH AMERICA UNDERWAY?" (The crowd responds with the biggest pop of the night.) WATTS: "Then let's get started! This first match is for the NORTH AMERICAN HEAYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP!!!" (Huge pop) WATTS: "The rules are simple. Four men will participate, though only two will be in the ring at one time. To leave the ring, you must tag out to one of your opponents on the outside. This is an elimination match. The last man standing will be the first North American Champion!" DIAMOND: "That's certainly true! He will be our first champion from North America, and it's about time! I'm sick of these foreigners like Leviathan and Garces who are from Singapore and Spain and Youngstown, Ohio and all those other weird places that no real people are from!" RANDALL: "Al, Youngstown, Ohio is part of the United States." DIAMOND: "Not if I get my petition signed to have it forcibly removed from the Union! I call it `involuntary secession.'" AMICO: "Besides, Lord Keeblor isn't from North America… maybe. He's listed as being from `Parts Unknown.'" DIAMOND: "`Parts Unknown' meaning a big tree with a lot of cookie-lovin' elves running around inside it." AMICO: (rolling her eyes) "That joke is SO old!" DIAMOND: "So are the Hardcore Icons but they're still around." RANDALL: (sighing) "Let's go to Jean Watts with the introductions." ("Just Another Victim" by Helmet and House of Pain begins to play over the P.A. to a great face pop - this crowd is obviously really fired up.) WATTS: "Introducing first, from Parts Unknown, at 6'2" and 255 pounds, here is LOOOOOOOOORD KEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEBLOORRRRRRRR!!!!!!" (Lord Keeblor is a well-built individual. He wears a very decorative mask, as well as embroidered robe, underneath which he wears karate-type pants.) DIAMOND: "Boy, his theme music sure is appropriate. He is `just another victim' - a victim of his own mediocre wrestling, that is. And he'll be wishing he wore a `helmet' when Nekrataal sends him into a `house of pain' with that powerbomb-" AMICO: (interrupting) "Al, these jokes are truly awful. What is your point?" DIAMOND: "My point is this: how'd this guy get into this match? Who has he ever beaten?" RANDALL: "Keeblor has indeed often been viewed as a bit of an underdog in this match, but I've never seen him look as focused as he does now. Should he win this title, all his past misfortune will be forgotten." DIAMOND: "Yeah, especially if he's the one to pin Nekrataal! You know, Jude St. Claire's putting up a pretty hefty sum of money for the man who does so! You know, maybe I should get in the ring and see if I can win that nice little prize." AMICO: "Go for it. He'd probably powerbomb you through the mat!" DIAMOND: "For St. Claire's moolah, it would probably be worth it."("Ironman" by Black Sabbath begins to play of the P.A.) DIAMOND: "WOO HOO! Ozzy's gonna wrestle?!?!" RANDALL: "I don't know. I don't recognize this music as being for one of the four competitors for the North American Title." (The music continues to play. Just after the words "I aM iRoNmAn" are sung Paul Kingstead walks out from behind the curtain. Instead of wearing his usual attire he's dressed in navy blue Curt Hennig-style tights, black boots, and a black knee brace on his right leg. On the back of the blue tights is a picture of a flaming halo. The crowd gives a huge pop as they recognize him.) DIAMOND: "Kingstead?!! NOOOOO!!!" WATTS: "And his opponent… from Youngstown, Ohio, at 6' and 234 pounds, here is PAAAAAAAAAAAUUUUUUULLLL KINGGGGGGSTEAAAAAAAAAADDDDDDDDDDD!!!!!!!!!!" RANDALL: "A new look for Paul Kingstead here." AMICO: "I don't know about this; those Hennig-style tights are just begging for wedgies." RANDALL: "Judging by that knee brace, he's still suffering the effects of that attack by Jude St. Claire and Romulus last Sunday." DIAMOND: "That's wrong in so many ways…" RANDALL: "I'm glad you're finally seeing things our way, Al. Jude used every dirty trick in the book last week against Paul to-" DIAMOND: (interrupting) "No, not that. Jude's the man! I mean Paul Kingstead pulling that joke on us! Playing Black Sabbath to make us think we'd get to see Ozzy wrestle only to reveal it was a submission wrestler! It's like when you see a beautiful girl across from you at a bar but when you go over to talk to her you catch a glimpse of her Adam's apple!" RANDALL: "You're a sick man, Al Diamond." DIAMOND: "Thanks, Matt. I try." (Kingstead and Keeblor shake hands somewhat hesitantly in the ring. Both have intense, focused looks on their faces.) ("The Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson begins to play over the P.A. to a HUGE heel pop!) DIAMOND: "Oh, boy." RANDALL: "Not a single person in this arena is sitting as we all wait for arguably the most hated individual in the CWC to make his entrance!" (The song plays for a while until the chorus begins, at which point Nekrataal steps through the ring curtain to an absolutely unbelievable heel pop. As always, he wears black pants with "The Sacrificer" down the leg, as well as black boots with red, bloody marks on them. His pale skin is covered with tattoos, his hair is long and black, and his frame is powerfully muscular and well-toned. His lips curl in a sadistic grin as he slowly approaches the ring.) WATTS: "And their opponent… from Grand Rapids, Michigan, at 6'7" and 290 pounds, here is NEKKKKKKKKKKRRRRRRAAAAAAATAAAAAAAAAAAAAALLLLLLLLLLLLLL!!!!!!" (Some of the crowd cheers, realizing Nekrataal is from their home state; others boo, disgusted for the same reason.) DIAMOND: "This is great! Who needs Ozzy? I'm sure Nekky would be more than willing to bite the head of any animal I gave him! In fact, let's test that… where's that chihuahua that hangs around Jose Garces?" (Nekrataal stands in the opposite corner from Keeblor and Kingstead, grinning casually as he leans against the turnbuckle.) DIAMOND: "Nekrataal looks super-confident. Of course, anyone who's willing to powerbomb the world champion and then fight him in a steel cage has got to be pretty sure of himself." AMICO: "What are you talking about? The reason he powerbombed Leviathan was because he has no self-confidence and he didn't think he could take Levi in a fair match!" DIAMOND: "Shut up, Elise! You weren't even around when that happened! You know, sometimes I wish Monique would follow up on her threats and come out here and kick your butt!" ("Back in Black" by AC/DC begins to play over the P.A. to a big heel pop.) WATTS: "And the final participant… from Batesville, Texas, at 6'6" and 276 pounds, here is FRAANNNNK CLAAAAAAIIIIIIIIBORRRRRRRRRRRNNNNNNNNE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!" (The powerfully-built Texan cowboy heads down the aisle to a chorus of boos. For the first time we see him wearing a bandanna over his face.) AMICO: "What's with the bandanna over Claiborne's face?" DIAMOND: "He's probably trying to shield his nose from Kingstead's stench. Lately he's been hanging out with Jose Garces, and you KNOW what kind of smell those frequent Taco Bell visits leave behind!" RANDALL: "This match is ready to get underway as soon as two men step outside on the ring apron." (Claiborne stands defiantly in the ring, demanding to be one of the first two. He points to Paul Kingstead, who happily accepts the invitation. Lord Keeblor and Nekrataal step outside. Jean Watts exits the ring as referee Tom Raper enters it.) (The bell rings.) DIAMOND: "Look, I was right! Claiborne DOES want Kingstead for stinking up the ring!" (Claiborne rushes at Kingstead for a clothesline, but Paul ducks. The big Texan turns around to take several quick jabs from Kingstead.) RANDALL: "Kingstead staggering the big man… he throws him against the ropes… head scissor takedown!" (Claiborne quickly rises to his feet… but Kingstead catches him with the bear hug.) RANDALL: "Kingstead holding Claiborne now… OVER THE TOP RELEASE BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX!!!" (The crowd cheers as Kingstead goes for the cover.) ONE… (Kickout) DIAMOND: "C'mon, Frank! I REFUSE to accept the idea of Paul Kingstead holding an actual title!" RANDALL: "Kingstead leaps on Claiborne's back, applying a sleeperhold…but Claiborne charges backward, crushing Kingstead against the turnbuckle!" (Paul slumps in the corner as Claiborne raises his hands in triumph.) (Suddenly, the crowd absolutely ERUPTS!) AMICO: "Uh, oh! Here comes the Strangler! That other cowboy is already on the way!" (Indeed, another huge cowboy races down the aisle.) RANDALL: "Wait a second… those tattoos… that's not the Strangler! THAT'S ACTUALLY CLAIBORNE!!!" DIAMOND: "Then who's this guy in the ring?" (The "Claiborne" in the ring removes the bandanna hiding his face, revealing himself to be the Strangler!) RANDALL: "THE STRANGLER HAD DISGUISED HIMSELF AS CLAIBORNE!!!" AMICO: "I heard Claiborne was fighting with someone backstage… it must have the Strangler! And apparently the Strangler won and headed off to the ring in Claiborne's place!" DIAMOND: "Well, you might be able to knock Claiborne out, too, if you carried a loaded noose around with you all the time!" (Claiborne slides into the ring and is immediately stomped by the Strangler. Unfazed, he rises to his feet, and the two begin to exchange huge punches!) RANDALL: "And here comes the Baby Face Brawler, climbing through the crowd to get to the arena floor! He said he'd be watching out for the Strangler, remember?" (While the Strangler's massive fists seem to beginning to have an effect on Claiborne, the Baby Face Brawler slides into the ring.) DIAMOND: "Baby Face is making that `L' with his hand… what does that mean, again?" (From behind, the Baby Face Brawler executes his Lullaby superkick toward the Strangler, grazing the back of his head!) DIAMOND: "Oh, yeah. The Lullaby. Sounds like one of Kingstead's moves; after all, HE'S the one prone to putting people to sleep!" (The Strangler spills to the outside, followed immediately by Claiborne. The two continue their fight. Tom Raper blocks the Baby Face Brawler's path, telling him to return to the stands. The Brawler waves to the fans and does so, seeing that Claiborne has the situation under control.) RANDALL: "Now Kingstead standing in the middle of the ring, wondering who his next opponent will be. And in comes Lord Keeblor… he certainly seems VERY anxious to test his skills here!" (Keeblor rushes in. Paul, pretending to be watching Nekrataal, has his back turned.) RANDALL: "Kingstead with his back turned… WAIT! SUPERKICK BY KINGSTEAD!" AMICO: "But Keeblor saw it coming… he ducks… SUPERKICK OF HIS OWN!" RANDALL: "That one connected! Keeblor follows it with a splash… a cover!" ONE… TWO… (kickout) RANDALL: "Keeblor lifting Kingstead… he pushes him into the corner, connecting with a few vicious chops!… but now Kingstead pushing Keeblor to the mat!" AMICO: "It looks like Kingstead might have the slight power advantage, though I suspect Keeblor is quicker." DIAMOND: "The sugar in those cookies must give him extra energy." RANDALL: "Kingstead charges with a running lariat… but Keeblor dropkicks him in the knee, dropping him! Now Keeblor grabbing him from behind… GERMAN SUPLEX!" (Keeblor tosses him back, but Paul flips over entirely, landing on his feet behind Keeblor!) RANDALL: "What a display of agility there by Kingstead… now he has the rear waist lock… GERMAN SUPLEX BY KINGSTEAD!" (But now Keeblor is the one who flips over, reversing the hold!) DIAMOND: "Third time's a charm!" (Nekrataal sneaks into the ring.) RANDALL: "Keeblor going for the German Suplex again… but here comes Nekrataal and… OH MY GOD!!! NEKRATAAL JUST GRABBED BOTH KINGSTEAD AND KEEBLOR FROM BEHIND, AND LIFTED BOTH OF THEM IN A BACK SUPLEX!!!" (The entire crowd pops with disbelief! Nekrataal gets back to his feet, smiling sadistically.) RANDALL: "WHAT A DISPLAY OF POWER! I have NEVER seen one man execute a belly-to-back suplex on TWO men at the SAME TIME!" DIAMOND: "What are you talking about? I used to do that all the time!" AMICO: "Even the referee looks a bit intimidated. He's not reprimanding Nekrataal at all - in fact, he's letting him stay in there, even though he hasn't been tagged." (In the meantime, Frank Claiborne and the Strangler continue to brawl on the entrance ramp. Both have lifted the other and dropped him along the steel guard-rail several times.) DIAMOND: "The Strangler had better be careful… we saw what Big Frank did to Mr. Shhh! last week up there!" RANDALL: "Oh, no! Nekrataal with an awesome military press on Kingstead… and he's walking towards the ring post!" DIAMOND: "Finally, someone's going to take out that boring old geezer!" RANDALL: "Oh my god!" AMICO: "Oh, no!" RANDALL: "Nekrataal tosses Kingstead out of the ring… AND HE LANDS RIGHT ON THOSE STEEL RING STEPS!!! OH MY GOD! DID YOU HEAR THAT SICKENING IMPACT?!?! OH, HOW HEINOUS!!!" (The crowd gasps in horror as Kingstead is dropped from gorilla press slam position all the way to the steps on the outside!) AMICO: (covering her mouth) "Oh, no, this is going to be worse than the Boiler Room Rumble!" DIAMOND: (grinning) "Hey, carnage is to be expected when Nekrataal is around!" (Nekrataal turns around slowly, his mouth curling in a malicious sneer. Kingstead clutches at his back, moaning in horrible pain.) (Lord Keeblor rises to his feet groggily, not seeing the monster stalking towards him. The crowd cries out in warning, but to no avail.) RANDALL: "Nekrataal slaps his hand around Keeblor's throat…CHOKESLAM!!!" (The crowd again is brought to their feet as Lord Keeblor nearly bounces off the canvas.) RANDALL: "Nekrataal putting one foot on the chest of Keeblor… is this a cover? Yes, Tom Raper in position!" ONE… TWO… (kickout with authority!) RANDALL: "Look at the fight still left in Lord Keeblor as he struggles to his feet… he seems angry and insulted that Nekrataal thought he could pin him so nonchalantly!" DIAMOND: "Yeah, Keeblor looks pretty ticked. Maybe Nekrataal sacrificed his St. Bernard." AMICO: "Lord Keeblor has a St. Bernard?" DIAMOND: "Probably. Aren't those the dogs that bring you alcohol?" (On the outside, The Strangler slams a steel chair into Claiborne's head. He then walks down to the ring, pulling a gym bag out from underneath.) AMICO: "The Stangler must have put that bag underneath the ring hours ago! He certainly came prepared!" RANDALL: "What does the Strangler have in that bag?" DIAMOND: "I don't know, but if it's a snake or a pair of hedge-clippers, the CWC's probably going to get sued!" (The Strangler pulls a loaded noose out from the bag!) DIAMOND: "A noose? (he shouts) Strangler, forget about Claiborne! Hog-tie Kingstead instead! He's right out there in front of you!" RANDALL: "Strangler seems to be ignoring you… he's marching after Claiborne. But look in the ring… Lord Keeblor bounching against the ropes… clothesline on Nekrataal. And he bounces back with another! And another! The big man is staggered, but he doesn't fall!" (Keeblor tries again, but Nekrataal sees him coming!) RANDALL: "Nekrataal goes for the big boot high kick… but Keeblor stops, grabbing his foot! He swings his own leg over Nekrataal's, bringing the big man to the ground… knee bar submission hold!" (Tom Raper asks if Nekrataal wants to give up, but the monster uses his tremendous power to slowly pull himself towards the ropes. Raper forces Keeblor to break the hold.)
