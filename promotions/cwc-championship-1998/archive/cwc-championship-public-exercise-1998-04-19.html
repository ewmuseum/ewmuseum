[The scene fades in, and we see Wrigley Field in Chicago, Illinois. The >field is looking in beautiful condition....except for the huge wrestling ring erected smack in the middle of it. The ring reads "Championship Wrestling Coalition" on the sides, and there is a large CWC logo in the center of the ring. Part of the center field wall has been removed for the occasion, and an elaborate set of curtains is hung over them. The entryway in is lined with fans sitting in chairs on the grass of Wrigley Field. Then, the camera pans down to the announcer's table, where we see Matt Randall, and "Sweet Daddy" Al Diamond. Randall is your regular neutral announcer--he's decked out in a grey suit with CWC cufflinks, and has a perpetually nervous look on his face. His broadcast partner, Al Diamond, is a huge, tall man with a tie-dye shirt and long, nappy black hair. He has a bratwurst in one hand, and is chewing leisurely. His shirt has a few mustard stains on it. The graphic says "CWC Public Exercise--APRIL 19, 1998."] RANDALL: WELCOME EVERYONE TO THE CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING COALITION'S FIRST-EVER PUBLIC EXERCISE! [Crowd pops.] RANDALL: I'm Matt Randall, and with me as always, former WSW and WAR champion, "Sweet Daddy" Al Diamond! Al... [Diamond, still chewing, grunts and motions for Matt to continue talking.] RANDALL: Well, I guess Al is a little pre-occupied right now, but what a show we've got for you tonight...we've got eight of CWC's finest talent putting on an exhibition for all the good people of Chicago and all the millions watching at home. But first, we've got a special announcement from our esteemed president, Mrs. Jean Watts! [Diamond swallows the rest of his brat. "More Than a Woman" by the Bee Gees plays over the PA, and the CWC president walks out. She is a tall woman, around six foot in height, and her long brown hair is being tossed by the notorious Chicago winds. She is in a severe business suit and a set of heels, so she appears even taller. She comes out to a mild pop from the crowd, steps through the ropes, and grabs a mic.] WATTS: Hey, everybody! Are you ready? [Crowd pops, not knowing about the potential copyright infringement.] WATTS: Are you ready to WRESTLE? [Crowd ponders this a second, then responds with a big pop.] WATTS: Well, you'll all get a chance to see the finest talent we have to offer. But first, a few business items. All the wrestlers participating tonight know that this exercise will have no bearing on their win-loss record, but it is my belief that their competitive nature will result in their best efforts--and a GREAT show for you, the fans. [Crowd shows interest in this...and, after a brief debate, decides to pop a third time.] WATTS: But, secondly, we'd like to inform you all that there will be absolutely NO women in CWC used as sexual objects. So, no dancing girls or scantily clad ring announcers. I will be CWC's ring announcer, until I think of someone better. If you have any comments, you may direct them to the left-field wall, which, I'm sure, will be fascinated to hear your opinion. Now, on with the show! [Cut back to the announcers.] RANDALL: Strong words by the president of the company, Mrs. Jean Watts. DIAMOND: Everybody LIKES scantily clad girls dancing around, though. Well, except purists. It's "sports entertainment", though--hot chicks entertain. ["Squeal" by No Doubt plays on the PA.] RANDALL: And we're underway here, with the CWC's first ever live event! WATTS: Our opening match has a twenty-minute time limit, and is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, from Youngstown, Ohio, here is PAUL KINGSTEAD! [mild pop from the crowd. An average-looking man with blond hair sets out from behind the curtain. He is wearing red wrestling pants, black boots, and a sleeveless trench coat with his name on the back. He walks calmly to the ring, and removes his coat.] DIAMOND: Hey! I think that guy delivered the pizza to my hotel last night! RANDALL: I hardly think so...fans, we're looking at Paul Kingstead, one of the most feared submission wrestlers in the game today! He's a consummate professional, and he will be making his CWC debut against a man with a bit of an ego problem, "Blockbuster" Johnny Detson. DIAMOND: Hey, if you're good, you can HAVE an ego problem. ["Everything About You" by Ugly Kid Joe come on the PA, to a big heel pop from the crowd. A medium-sized man in a stunning gold robe comes out, wearing sunglasses and carrying an academy award.] DIAMOND: There's the man. WATTS: And his opponent, standing six-foot three inches in height, weighing in at 248 pounds here is "Blockbuster" Johnny Detson!! [Detson steps into the ring, and removes his robe. After flexing a few times for the crowd, Detson gives his shades and his award statue to the timekeeper at ringside, and we are underway. The bell rings.] RANDALL: We've got a good contrast of styles here--in fact, in most of our matches today. The athleticism of Detson versus the submission style of Kingstead. Looks like a barn-burner, to be sure. [Detson whips Kingstead into the ropes, and leapfrogs him on the rebound.] DIAMOND: Great athletic ability there by Detson...he must have been 4 feet above the ground. [Kingstead comes back, and gets planted on his face with a drop-toe hold. Detson immediately sneaks around to the backside of Kingstead, and hooks in a sleeper.] RANDALL: A great series of moves there by Detson. He's trying to wear out Kingstead right from the very start with submission holds. DIAMOND: I don't know if that's such a good tactic against a guy with Kingstead's endurance...Detson might be better off going for more high-flying impact maneuvers. RANDALL: Nonetheless, this move seems to be taking it's toll on Kingstead, whose head is dropping slightly. DIAMOND: No way. I've told you once, I'll tell you again. A sleeper is just a set-up for the other guy to... [Kingstead hits a series of elbow-smashes to the stomach.] DIAMOND: ...elbow you in the stomach. RANDALL: Kingstead breaks into a dead run, and comes flying back at Detson...but then pulls up. [Detson puts his head down, attempting a backdrop. Kingstead grabs his head, and connects with a BIG piledriver.] RANDALL: And overconfidence by Detson there, as he put his head down in anticipation of Kingstead, but was DROPPED by a piledriver. Kingstead is dropping a series of elbows right onto the back of the neck of Detson...concentrating on the area that was damaged by the piledriver. Now, Kingstead goes for a side headlock...and he is squeezing the life out of Detson as we speak! DIAMOND: No way, Matt. A side headlock is just a set up for the other guy to give you a suplex. [Detson picks Kingstead halfway up for a suplex.] DIAMOND: See? [But Kingstead is able to plant his feet and go back to the ground. He grabs more firmly onto Detson's head, and runs forward, hitting a BIG bulldog!] RANDALL: What was that? DIAMOND: Never mind. [Kingstead goes to the ropes again...and drops a fist right on the face of Detson! A cover!] [ONE... TWO... Kickout by Detson.] DIAMOND: No way do you put out the Blockbuster with that wuss move. RANDALL: Detson staggers to his feet...but Kingstead grabs him from behind, and THROWS him backwards with a release German suplex...he pulls Detson to his feet again, and goes for the same moves, but gets elbowed in the head for his troubles! JD goes for a forearm smash...but Kingstead GRABS him about the waist, and delivers a belly-to-belly suplex! So far this match has been all Paul Kingstead. DIAMOND: Unfortunately. [Kingstead goes off the ropes, and attempts a head-scissor takedown...but Detson, shows good presence of mind, pushes him off, and Kingstead falls to the mat, cracking his head. Detson begins stomping his foot, and the crowd stomps with him.] DIAMOND: There it is! We're about to see the Johnny Kick! [Detson goes for the superkick...but Kingstead grabs his leg, kicks out his other leg, and turns the move into a half Boston crab.] RANDALL: What ability by Kingstead! A great counter to the Johnny Kick...but Detson able to get to the ropes, and our referee Tom Raper forces the break. Kingstead comes back to work on Detson...but gets PLASTERED with a spinning heel kick! Kingstead goes down like a ton of bricks, and Detson begins stomping away at Kingstead's left knee! DIAMOND: A little payback for big JD. [Detson puts Kingstead in Sharpshooter position...then drops a leg onto his left knee. Detson picks up Kingstead on his shoulders, and goes over to the turnbuckle. Detson ties up Kingstead's leg in the ropes.] RANDALL: A blatant act of cheating by Detson...and he is jawing with Tom Raper, who is trying to free Kingstead. Detson raises a fist, and the ref backs off. DIAMOND: I'm really starting to like this Detson guy. [Detson backs up...and hits a running bull charge, SLAMMING all his weight into the exposed knee of Kingstead. He does it AGAIN, and the crowd groans at the expression on Kingstead's face. DIAMOND: He's playing possum. [As Kingstead walks over, Detson thumbs him in the eye and goes for a belly-to-belly suplex. But Kingstead reverses the move, and hits his own suplex.] RANDALL: Kingstead in genuine control here...Detson bails out to the outside. But Kingstead RUNS after him, and throws him back in the ring by the hair. DIAMOND: He's ruining a national treasure there. RANDALL: Detson with a lariat attempt...but Kingstead slips behind him, and lock in a cross-face chickenwing! Detson is struggling, but Kingstead picks him up and slams him to the mat with a chickenwing suplex! DIAMOND: Shades of the late, great David Lister. RANDALL: Who? DIAMOND: Forget it. RANDALL: And Tom Raper is breaking the hold! Kingstead is irate, but the referee points out that Detson hit the rope on the way down to the mat, and Kingstead obliges, albeit somewhat grudgingly. [Kingstead suddenly gets an angry look on his face, and he FLOORS Detson with a running axhandle to the face!] DIAMOND: Ouch. RANDALL: Kingstead is a house afire here...he picks up Detson...and hits a legsweep faceslam! Then, he picks up Detson overhead, and DROP him with a military press-slam. Kingstead with the cover... [ONE... TWO... THR...kickout.] RANDALL: OH MY! ALMOST A THREE-COUNT BY KINGSTEAD! Kingstead goes up to the top-rope...what's he up to? DIAMOND: He's pulled out all the stops here...Kingstead isn't known for aerial ability... RANDALL: Paul Kingstead with a flying axhandle...but he is MET on the way down with a HUGE superkick! DIAMOND: The Johnny Kick! Johnny Detson with the Johnny Kick! Kingstead is barely conscious after that! RANDALL: Detson scoops up his prone opponent, and hits an inverted piledriver! He calls that the Showstopper! What a comeback here by Johnny Detson! He signals for the Closing Credits! This capacity crowd is on their feet, booing madly! He sets up Kingstead for the hanging neckbreaker--and DELIVERS! Debris is flying into the ring, and Detson slides over for the cover... [ONE... TWO... THREE! Bell rings.] RANDALL: Let's get the official word. WATTS: Your winner, "Blockbuster" JOHNNY DETSON!! [Crowd boos. Detson takes an elaborate bow, wipes away a fake tear, and fake-sobs, "You like me...you REALLY like me!" He raises both his hands in victory, and walks back up the entryway, triumphant.] RANDALL: What a match...terrific display by both men. What a way to kick off our show! RANDALL: Wow, it's hard to believe that that is the same guy we saw at training camp. Storm looks like he's aged about ten years in the last month.
