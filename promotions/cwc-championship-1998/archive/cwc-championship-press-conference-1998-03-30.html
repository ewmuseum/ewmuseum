CWC Press Conference and Joining Info, 30 March 1998

30 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello! The Championship Wrestling Coalition is still on the lookout for hot new talent to join its ranks. Recenlty, the CWC held a press conference to promote itself, and a transcript has been included within this message. If you are a top-notch roleplayer and would like to join an up-and-coming top-notch federation, please check out our webpage at http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Loge/9360/index.html Thank you and hope to hear from you soon! [name withheld] CWC Rep ------------------------------------- The scene opens in a large television studio in New York, New York full of reporters. The sidebar reads News 32 Sports. One reporter turns to his cameraman, starts to slick his hair back, but notices that the red light is on, and begins to speak, squinting a bit myopically at the Teleprompter.] REPORTER: "Hi, I'm Ernest Bello, and we're back at News 32. Truly, professional wrestling has become the pre-eminent form of sports entertainment today. Everyone marvels at the skill, tenacity, and athleticism the performers bring to the table, along with a healthy dose of humor. Sounds to me like a perfect recipe for fun...in addition to the 3 major federations currently warring with each other in America, these being..." [All of a sudden, a seven-foot giant in a tie-dye shirt runs onto camera, his long black hair waving. He puts a large hand on the reporter's mouth and ushers him gently, but firmly off camera. Then, a much smaller man, somewhat portly, comes onto camera. He has sandy blond hair and blue eyes, but he is unshaven and swaying unsteadily. He grabs the microphone from the captive reporter.] BLOND MAN: "Ok, that's enough of this blather. Damn it, I didn't want to have to do this, but I haven't had work in months...my kids need to eat, and..." [The big guy comes back on camera.] BIG MAN: "Relax, man. Relax...well, we're here in New York City, and..." BLOND MAN: "I'm Matt Randall, and this is Al "Sweet Daddy" Diamond, former WSW champ. I'm Matt Randall and this is Al "Sweet Daddy" Diamond. I'm Matt Ra..." DIAMOND: "As you can see, Matt's a little distressed. We've been out of work for about two months now, and although I can always wrestle on the independent circuit, Matt's a commentator by nature, and he needs to pay the bills. So, we hopped a Greyhound to New York to see if we could land a gig with this new fed, the Championship Wrestling Coalition." RANDALL: "WELCOME EVERYONE TO WEW HALFWEEK!" DIAMOND: "Shhh...Matt, the press conference is about to start. Be calm." *peers at one of the News 32 aides* "Hey, there..." [Al sidles up to the young aide, putting one long arm around her. The camera follows him and Al, annoyed, points it back up towards the podium. At that moment, a nervous-looking man, about five foot nine, scampers up to the podium, and clears his throat. His brown eyes flash nervously, as he seems a bit shell-shocked from the size of the crowd.] LITTLE MAN: "Er...ahem. Is this thing on?" *taps the mic* [A burst of MASSIVE feedback erupts from the speakers, causing the audience to wince collectively. The little man coughs again, uncomfortably, and begins to speak.] LITTLE MAN: "Hello...cough...everyone, and welcome to the first press conference for the Championship Wrestling Coalition...I am Vice-President and Head of Security Andrew Holden, and I'd like to explain, er, that is, to expound upon...where are my notes?" [Holden shuffles through his pants pockets and pulls out a stack of index cards. He puts them on the podium, and begins reading off of them mechanically.] HOLDEN: "We are here today to witness the opening of the premier wrestling federation in the world, the Championship Wrestling Coalition. The CWC will be the watermark by which al other federation are judged. Although our administration is currently solid, we are actively recruiting both wrestlers, managers, trainers, and commentators. There will be time at the end for questions. But, now, let me introduce the president of the CWC, Miss Jean Watts!" [Light applause fills the room as a six-foot brunette in a business suit steps out from behind the curtain, followed by four expressionless men in suits. Watts strides up to the podium and begins to speak.] WATTS: "Thank you for the kind welcome. I'm sure you're all just FULL of questions about me, and the CWC, and these four men behind me. Well, the four men behind me are CWC's board of directors...the mans with the plans, you might say. The CWC, as Andy over there was so kind to pointout, WILL be the greatest E-federation ever, bar none. Just read the first word in the name of the league--'Championship'. Everything about this league is championship-caliber so far, from the administration, to the...well, at this point, it's just the administration. But we are confident we will be able to attract champion wrestler from the entire world. As for yet, we only have three wrestlers...The Backbreaker, the "Naturally Gifted" Peter Hand, and Zero "El Niño" Gonzalez." [She looks a bit disillusioned at that.] "BUT, that number will substantially increase, I'm sure. As for me, I am no stranger to the world of professional wrestling...after a short-lived stint in GLOW, I have been touring Japan for the last seven years. When this opportunity came up, I grabbed it...and, frankly, that's all you need to know about me. I'm here to plug the federation, not myself. We'll begin taking questions now." [A mousy-looking reporter in a bad tie speaks up.] REPORTER: "How will match winners be determined in CWC?" WATTS: "By their skills and athletic ability, of course." HOLDEN: "And then, by the quality and quantity of their role-plays." WATTS: "As you can see, Andy here is the official kayfabe-breaker as well as VP." [A tall, thin reporter raises his hand.] REPORTER: "How will booking be determined?" WATTS: "By the six of us...we'll meet in secret committee to sift through roleplays, and determine the winners." REPORTER: "I see. And how will these `role-plays' be sent to the participants?" HOLDEN: "We will send a weekly flash packet to each of the handlers." REPORTER: "How many events will CWC hold?" WATTS: "Well, our first event will be May 3rd, the first of our regular cards to be held on Sunday nights. Then, CWC First Night is tentatively scheduled for early June. After that, cards will be held every Sunday night, with Pay-Per-Views occurring every two months or so." HOLDEN: "Whenever we need the money." [A small, rather rotund man speaks up. Meanwhile Al has disengaged from the News 32 aide, and he and Matt are moving toward the front of the crowd.] REPORTER: "How many title belts will you be offering?" WATTS: "As of right now, plans are for three belts--the World Title belt, a secondary belt to be named later, and a tag team title." REPORTER: "OK. Can you be fired from CWC?" WATTS: "Of course...if you violate any of the rules listed in the CWC rulebook. OR, if you forget to roleplay. No slackers allowed in MY fed, and that's for damn sure." [Matt and Al are almost at the front, as a female reporter stands up.] REPORTER: "Jane Eyres, CBC News. What will you do to prevent the inmates from running the asylum, that is to say, large stables of wrestlers essentially replacing administration." WATTS: "We'll cross that bridge when we come to it. Right now, "large stables of wrestlers" sounds like a pretty good idea to me. And, furthermore..." [Matt and Al finally make it to the front of the crowd, and wave frantically at the stage. Watts looks down at them, a VERY amused look on her face.] WATTS: "Yes?" RANDALL: *a little breathless* "Hey, hey, hey...you guys need announcers, right?" WATTS: "Yes, we do...in fact, screening tests are underway as we speak..." RANDALL: "WE are your announcers...we're hard-working, dedicated, with tons of experience. Please, let us work for you!" [Al coughs and looks slightly embarrassed.] WATTS: "Well, I don't know..." [The crowd of reporters is getting behind the strange pair of men, nodding in affirmation.] WATTS: "OK, OK, maybe...what do you have for me? I need a sample match." [Matt pulls out a bedraggled tape from WEW Perfect Strangers and hands it to Watts. Al climbs up on stage too. Watts hands the tape to Holden, who puts it in a slot on the wall. A large video screen comes down, and the tape plays.] BEGIN TAPE RANDALL: Hello, everyone, and welcome to the next hour of "Prime Time: Perfect Strangers". I'm Matt Randall, and with me as always, the poor man's Barry Lawless, former WSW and WAR champion, "Sweet Daddy" Al Diamond. DIAMOND: Always a pleasure, Matt. RANDALL: You're right, Al...it is always a pleasure to at one of WEW's GREAT PPV events...in fact, today, we're at the United Center, home of the 5-time world champion... DIAMOND: Bret Hart lives here? RANDALL: Pardon me, I meant the five time NBA champion Chicago Bulls. We've seen so much tonight, but there is so much more to come, with the Perfect Partners tag tournament, the grudge match between Mr. Shhh! and Night's Harbringer... DIAMOND: It's a career match, Matt. But it's not as if Harbringer has anything at stake. RANDALL: Yeah, right. Harbringer is an up and coming superstar, and perhaps the cornerstone of WEW's "new generation". DIAMOND: Ummm... RANDALL: And these next matches are the biggest matches in the wrestling world... DIAMOND: Matt... RANDALL: Slobberknockers, everyone. Well, business is certainly about to pick up...let's go to the ring. DIAMOND: *sighs* [Cut to the ring, where Laura Johnstone is standing with the mic.] LJ: This next match is a tag-team match, scheduled for one fall. This match is the FINAL match of the Perfect Partners tournament!! [crowd pops] LJ: Introducing first, at a combined weight of... [Suddenly, Darien Bane and KKK come STORMING down the aisle, and not a word passes between them. They run into the ring, and sit it opposite corners of the ring, drawing a mixed reaction from the crowd.] RANDALL: Hardly a cohesive tag team there. DIAMOND: Yeah, but the most talented one in the tournament...if these guys can be even a little friendlier towards each other, they might walk out of here with the belts. LJ: And, THEIR OPPONENTS... ["Rock This Town" by the Stray Cats plays to a big pop from the crowd, and the team of BoneCrusher and Rockin' Ron Joseph hit the ring full speed, and Johnstone bails.] DIAMOND: Way to ruin your own introduction, guys. RANDALL: RRJ and Bonecrusher not even waiting for the bell! The two men toss KKK to the outside and begin pounding on Darien Bane! RRJ hits a dropkick to the knee of Bane! And another! Then Bonecrusher follows with a HUGE football tackle! That's seven feet of impact right there! Oh lord! And Bonecrusher going for the pin! DIAMOND: Bane's not going out like that. RANDALL: But referee Reed Lewis is refusing to count, demanding that one of the two men leave the ring! Bonecrusher gestures for Ron to go to the outside, and reaches down for Bane. [RRJ pulls Bonecrusher back, and out of the ring by the hair.] DIAMOND: I think that might count as a tag...Rhonda seems to be at that time of month all of a sudden. [Joseph hits a running bodypress, and gets up and CLOCKS KKK, who just got up on the apron with a hard lariat, knocking him back down.] RANDALL: Ron has never been the most stable competitor, but he is taking particular umbrage at being paired with young Bonecrusher, it would seem. [Joseph goes for a springboard moonsault onto the staggering Bane, but Bane catches him in mid-air, and drops him with a DEVASTATING spinebuster. Bane plays to the crowd, who boo fanatically.] DIAMOND: Darien is such a popular guy. RANDALL: Bane with a side suplex now...OH!! Did you see RRJ's head strike the canvas? What impact! DIAMOND: He's picking Rhonda up! AGAIN with the side suplex! And another! Oh Ron is out! Bane with the pin attempt... [KKK comes RUNNING into the ring....he picks Bane off of Ron Joseph, THROWS him to the outside, and begins to wail on RRJ!] RANDALL: KKK is a house afire! Lefts and rights and lefts and rights...he is UNLOADING on Ron Joseph, who definitely needs a tag. DIAMOND: Yeah, and then we can release him back into the wild. RANDALL: What? DIAMOND: Never mind. [KKK lifts Ron Joseph up...and PLANTS him with a double-underhook suplex.] RANDALL: Strong moves being displayed here by Keith Keo...another fine young talent here in WEW. DIAMOND: Yeah, you've got to plug the "fine, young talent"...because so much of it is GONE following the battle royal. [KKK picks Ron up, and swings him to the ropes, where Darien Bane catches him in a sleeper.] DIAMOND: Wow, a double-team! RANDALL: Not really. I think Bane just saw an opportunity to hit somebody, and went for it. And Ron's FACE IS TURNING BLUE! BANE REALLY HAS THAT SLEEPER LOCKED IN! REED LEWIS IS TRYING TO PREVENT THIS BLATANT DOUBLE-TEAMING!! BUT HERE COMES BONECRUSHER!! DIAMOND: Amazing, that full-throttle scream of yours. How DO you do it? [Bonecrusher steps easily over the top rope, and just tosses KKK into the turnbuckles, where he hits and slumps over like rag doll. Bonecrusher strides over to where Bane and RRJ are tangling and grabs Bane by the neck...CHOKESLAMMING HIM EASILY INTO THE RING!] RANDALL: Bonecrusher going to work here on Darien Bane...what power shown by the big man. He is stomping right on the chest of Darien Bane! [Ron Joseph gets in Bonecrusher's face SCREAMING! Ron is pointing a finger in the face of the young man, informing his that he was not the legal man...Ron pulls back, and slaps Bonecrusher's face.] DIAMOND: Shades of the Truth Commission there...I wonder if Bonecrusher can break a pre-cut football helmet with his bare hand? RANDALL: Ron Joseph has really, really been temperamental as of late...his constant legal actions against WEW, the fact that he couldn't take the world title from Shooter, and who can forget the whole "I hate you" movement? Ron has become a real serious loose cannon, and.... DIAMOND: Gee, Tony, do you think we could pay a little attention to the MATCH? RANDALL: Sorry about that. But in Chicago, the place where World Wide E-fed Wrestling, that predecessor to WEW began, it's so hard not to talk about a representative of tradition like Ron Joseph, and I... DIAMOND: Well, try...there's a great match going on... [Bane, who has been in a figure-four leglock, reaches up and hits RRJ with a low blow, breaking the hold.] DIAMOND: See? Good, solid technical wrestling by Darien Bane. [Bane scoops up Ron Joseph...POWERSLAM! A cover...Reed Lewis makes the count...] [ONE...TWO...kickout by RRJ.] RANDALL: What resilience by RRJ...I can't believe he kicked out! DIAMOND: Bane is really taking it to the former champ right here....you know, you see a youngster like Darien Bane...six-foot-eight, three hundred and some pounds, and you wonder why the man doesn't at least get some sort of title shot. [Meanwhile, Bane is DELIVERING shots to the gut of Ron Joseph, and he Irish whips him into the corner, and follows with a sloppy-looking Stinger Splash.] RANDALL: Bane with a power move there, but showing surprising agility. A great athlete right there! Bane climbs the rope and is hitting Ron Joseph with shots to the head! But KKK tags HIMSELF in, pulls Bane off of RRJ, and goes to work himself! [The two partners are engaged in an intense staredown, and the are gesturing frantically at one another. But, while they are talking, Ron sneaks up behind the two men, hits a double low blow, and tags in Bonecrusher!] RANDALL: Oh, no...the BIG man steps over the top rope...he grabs the throats of both KKK and Bane at lifts them both into the air, dropping them with a massive DOUBLE chokeslam!! Oh LORD!! DIAMOND: Impressive...too bad BC's a one-move wonder. [Bonecrusher rolls KKK out of the ring, and goes to work on Darien Bane...he lifts him up in gorilla press position, and drops him with a HUGE press-slam!] DIAMOND: OK, a two-move wonder... [Bonecrusher rips Bane to his feet, and hooks in an abdominal stretch...Bane is in visible pain, and he struggles to escape Bonecrusher's vise-like grip.] DIAMOND: OK, a three-move wonder... [Bonecrusher lifts Bane, still in the submission hold, and THROWS him half the way across the ring with a HUGE ab-stretch suplex.] DIAMOND: Aw, forget it...he does have moves. RANDALL: Bonecrusher drops an ENORMOUS elbow right on the face of Darien Bane. What elevation by the big man! DIAMOND: Oh, no!! [Bonecrusher is signaling for his finisher, that HUGE tiger driver...the crowd is going bananas, and Bonecrusher scoops up the prone Darien Bane onto his shoulders, carrying him about the ring like a rag doll.] RANDALL: This could be lights out for Darien Bane... DIAMOND: WAIT!! Darien Bane hit KKK in the head with his foot accidentally as Bonecrusher was carrying him, and KKK is rushing into the ring! RANDALL: Bonecrusher HITS the MASSIVE tiger driver, and covers! But Bane is no longer the legal man in the ring! KKK is going berserk on the big man...he FLOORS him with huge lariat! And a shoulderblock! OH MY GOD!! KKK put down the enormous Bonecrusher with a shoulderblock! DIAMOND: All the laws of physics are in opposition to that, you know. RANDALL: What would you know about physics? DIAMOND: I think it was Newton who said..."Big guy hit little guy...little guy fall down and go boom." [KKK lifts Bonecrusher up into a bodyslam, and PLANTS him to the canvas. He floats over for the cover!] {ONE... TWO... TH...kickout by Bonecrusher.] DIAMOND: Narrow escape there by Bonecrusher. KKK continues to pound the big ogre with chops and kicks...he sets Bonecrusher up on top...SUPER-FRANKENSTEINER BY KKK! RANDALL: Oh, what a move...Bonecrusher is not moving...but KKK climbs up top again...and CONNECTS with a top-rope fistdrop! Bonecrusher appears nearly unconscious, and KKK goes for the cover again! [Ron Joseph comes in to make the save.] RANDALL: RRJ helping out his partner once again...and what's this!?!? Ron in the face of Bonecrusher again, telling him to get up and fight like a man! Bonecrusher is obviously confused by this reprimand from his partner, and hangs his head... [Darien Bane rushes in from the outside and hits a flying bodypress on all four men in the ring (RRJ, Bonecrusher, KKK and the ref). All four wrestlers go to the outside where a HUGE brawl ensues.] RANDALL: WHAT A MELEE! OH DEAR LORD! KKK AND BANE ARE EXCHANGING BLOWS, AS ARE RRJ AND BONECRUSHER!! NO LOVE LOST AT ALL BETWEEN THESE FOUR MEN!! KKK SWINGS... AND KNOCKS RON JOSEPH OVER THE GUARDRAIL INTO THE CROWD! BANE PULLS BONECRUSHER INTO THE RING... [Bane suplexes the big man back into the ring, and goes for the cover...but Reed Lewis informs him that he is NOT the legal man. Bane is visibly upset...he hits a legdrop and goes over to the corner, where KKK is waiting for the tag.] DIAMOND: Don't do it, Bane...he's the legal man, there's no need to tag him. RANDALL: But Bane extends his hand anyway...and then CLUBS KKK with a huge lariat. KKK manages to hold on to the ring ropes, and pulls himself into the ring where he is screaming at Bane, asking him if he wants to win the match or not! DIAMOND: But Bane respond by simply staring at KKK unblinking... [Bonecrusher creeps up from behind, and rolls up Bane into a small package.] RANDALL: Great presence of mind by Bonecrusher... DIAMOND: Except that KKK is the legal man...and now he's started to wail on the big oaf again. [Bonecrusher is staggering about, and KKK bounces off of the ropes and hits a HUGE kneelift...Bonecrusher staggers but doesn't fall.] RANDALL: KKK unable to take the big man off of his feet...he bounces off, and tries another clothesline...no effect! And another! Bonecrusher is not responding at all to the shots KKK is delivering. [Ron Joseph gets back up on the apron.] DIAMOND: Wow...I never realized KKK was such a weakling. RANDALL: He's not...it's just that Bonecrusher is a physical marvel. KKK grabs Bonecrusher's head...and LEVELS him with a huge DDT! A camel clutch hold applies by KKK, and Bonecrusher is in serious pain. DIAMOND: KKK really working on the back of Bonecrusher...amazing the amount of punishment that all four men have received in this matchup. [Reed Lewis raises Bonecrusher's arm to see if he is out or not. He raises it once...it drops. He raises it again...it drops. He raises it a third time...] RANDALL: OH MY!! RON JOSEPH HAS COME IN TO MAKE THE SAVE AGAIN...HE HOLDS UP THE COUNT BY DISTRACTING REED LEWIS...Bonecrusher is slowly staggering to his feet, with KKK on top of him holding on for dear life! Bonecrusher backs KKK into the corner...but RRJ is between the two wrestlers and the turnbuckle, and he suffers a severe blow. DIAMOND: 600-plus pounds driving Ron into the turnbuckle, and he is out... [Meanwhile, Bonecrusher is going to work on KKK...he hits a bodyslam, and then a suplex, and goes for the cover...] RANDALL: This could be it... [ONE... TWO... Kickout by KKK.] RANDALL: The crowd is on its feet!! Bonecrusher whips KKK to the ropes, where he CRASHES into Darien Bane! Bane gives KKK a baleful look, but steps over the top to meet Bonecrusher... [Bonecrusher gives Bane a big boot to the face, flooring him.] DIAMOND: Come on, Darien! Get UP!! RANDALL: Bonecrusher planted that HUGE foot right into the mouth of Bane, and he is barely moving... [Crowd pops big.] RANDALL: ...and Bonecrusher gives the sign...he's going to deliver another Tiger Driver! DIAMOND: He's got Bane up...but LOOK! RRJ has woken up! RANDALL: He doesn't look too happy...he smacks Bonecrusher in the back of the head, and Bane slips out of the hold. Bonecrusher is visibly angry now, and he takes a swing at Ron Joseph...WHO COUNTERS IT WITH THE ROCKIN' CHOP BUSTER! OH NO!! RON TOOK OUT HIS OWN PARTNER!! NOBODY KNOWS WHAT TO THINK! DARIEN BANE GRABS RRJ FROM BEHIND, AND SIGNALS FOR KKK TO COME IN AND HIT HIM! DIAMOND: Finally, some teamwork... [KKK comes in, and goes for a superkick...but RRJ ducks, and Bane gets leveled!] DIAMOND: Or not. RANDALL: Darien Bane is out!! KKK has a stunned look on his face, but RRJ grabs him from behind, and NAILS HIM WITH THE ROCKIN' CHOP BUSTER!! KKK is unconscious! RON JOSEPH IS A ONE-MAN WRECKING CREW!! DIAMOND: But he's not pinning him, he's after Bane now... RANDALL: The former champ drags Darien Bane to his feet, and hits a low blow! The fans cheer wildly...and Ron delivers the THIRD ROCKIN CHOP BUSTER OF THE NIGHT!! HE COVERS!! [Reed Lewis slides into position... ONE... TWO... THREE!!!] LJ: Here is your winner, RO... [Ron Joseph slaps them mic out of her hand, and stares at the carnage he had left in the ring. Reed Lewis tries to raise Joseph's hand, but get spit on for his troubles!] RANDALL: What's happened to Ron? Has he gone nuts? This is unbelievable! What a temper by the former WEW champion! [Ron stalks back to the locker room, ignoring everyone and everything, and muttering "I hate you." to himself.] DIAMOND: What in the-- RANDALL: I know, I can't believe it either...Ron Joseph has gone berserk...laying waste to three other men, who are just now waking up.... DIAMOND: Wow...I've never seen Ron act this nasty...I kind of like it. RANDALL: Regardless, the team of RRJ and Bonecrusher advances to the tag team final match...we'll be right back, after we clear the carnage from the ring... END TAPE [The tape stops. Watts blinks, impressed, and the audience bursts into applause. Al bows, somewhat awkwardly, and Matt grabs Watts' hand expectantly.] WATTS: "OK, OK...you've got the job...Congratulations guys, you're a part of CWC." DIAMOND & RANDALL: "Yes!" [The two embrace, while the audience continues to clap.] WATTS: "I hate to cut this little display short, but because of the length of that video, we've got to cut this short. I'd like to end the press conference by saying that we may have announcers now, but CWC needs more wrestlers...if you're out there, give us a call, a fax, a smoke signal, whatever. Like Uncle Sam, we want YOU! That's it for now... I'll see you at CWC First Night! And remember, the challenger is here... are you game? [The entourage leaves the stage, and the camera crews pack it up and head home.]
