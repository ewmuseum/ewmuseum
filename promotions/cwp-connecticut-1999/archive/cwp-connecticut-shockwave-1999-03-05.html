CWP — Shockwave

5 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to a broadcasters desk in New Haven Coliseum in New
Haven, Connecticut. There are two men sitting at the desk, which sports a black and red CWP logo on the front. In the background, we see a large banner that reads
"Shockware Center".) JB: Welcome fans to the first ever televised broadcast of Connecticut
Wrestling Promotions. Tonight we'll hear from some of the most feared men in our sport, the athletes of the CWP. I'm Jeremy
Bane, son of CWP Commishioner Chris Bane...
MH: And I'm "Mean" Mike Henderson. Welcome to Shockwave!!! (The scene cuts to a shot of two men battling it out in a cage. One man is climbing the cage, but the other man throws a chair at him, dropping him to the mat. The camera cuts to a different shot, of two men in a ring surrounded by tables. One of the man, a masked cruiserweight, puts his opponent onto the top turnbuckle backwards. The masked man climbs the buckle, and takes his adversary down to the floor, through a table with a huricanrana.
The camera cuts to another shot, this time of a man with his opponent set up for a front faced jawbreaker. The man runs toward the ropes, and dives over the top, snapping the mans neck over the top rope. The screen cuts again, this time to a view of the same man who was on the receiving end of the front faced jawbreaker being loaded into an ambulance on a gurney. They back doors of the ambulance close, and the ambulance pulls away as the world "CWP Shockwave" appear on the screen, with "Copyright,
Connecticut Wrestling Promotions, 1999"
appearing near the bottom. The screen fades to black, and then cuts back to the Shockwave Center.)
JB: Welcome back to the Shockwave Center. First up, we've got a previously recorded interview with none other than Jim
"The Clan" Cambridge. ================== Jim "The Clan" Cambridge ================== (Scene opens up with Jim "The Clan" Cambridge in a nursery, holding a new born infant gently, rocking the child to sleep. He looks up and whispers quieltly, not wanting to to disturb the baby.)
Jim: Its a boy. I look into his face and see a brave new beginning, the child has all it takes to be succesfull, loving parents, plenty of food, and all the other neccisities to become a winner in this world.
A look into his face and I see a brave new begining, ready to meet brave new challenges. However, this baby did not always have these same bright hopes and prospects. (Jim pauses to control his emotions)
You see, this child had cancer and he almost did not make it... almost. But the child fought hard, and his body defeated the cancer. (Jim trails off with a sound of sadness in his voice. He looks at the child and shakes his head in disgust. He kisses his forehead and then gently places him in the crib)
Jim: I look at this child and I see the CWP. Just like the child, a brave new beggining. The similarities dont end there though...
The CWP also has a cancer that seeks to destroy and corrupt everything. My competition is this cancer. Iron Spike, Ghost, Razor, you among others are the disease that infect this fine institution. However, the cycle goes full circle, and just as the child defeated the cancer, I will defeat you...
(Scene fades with Jim giving a stern serious look) =================== JB: Touching words from the man who calls himself "The Clan". MH: (wiping his eyes) That was beautiful. JB: Are you crying Mike? MH: Uh, no *sniff*, I just *sniff* got something in my *sniff* eye. JB: Sure you did. Ok, next up we'll hear from a man we're all familiar with, the man who hails from Chicago, Illinois, Raid!!
====== Raid ====== (A dark cloud casts over Hartford, Conneticut as a man walks through the fog. He is carrying a walking stick, and has a venomous look about his face. He walks into a T.V. studio and demands a camera man. A short guy of about 25 years turns on the tape and begins rolling. Raid stares directly into the camera.)
Raid: I have been wrestling for 6 years now. 6 YEARS!!! And now I have come to the fork in the road. I have battled many men over my career, and I have had many different roles, be it a Good
Guy, and sometimes the most satanic thing you've ever seen. I have focused most of my career on one thing: ruling every fed I am in. Lately, I have been thinking that that isn't right: I have decided to make my goal here in the CWP to be the most hardcore wrestler to step foot into the ring.
(Raid lights one of his signature cigars, puffs it slowly, and begins to speak again.) Raid: There are not many familiar faces here. The one that sticks out the most is Razor. Razor, we have had quite a past, and I realize you may have a lot of hatred for me. But I say let's get on with our lives. No hard feelings this way. I wish you the best in the CWP, but I doubt you'll reach the status of me. You see, before, I was among the elite in all of wrestling. Now, I plan on becoming THE elite superstar in all of wrestling, not just one in a crowd. So if anyone would like to step into the ring with a legend, then you know my number. I'm out.
(Raid puts out his cigar, and the camera follows him outside, only to lose him in the fog of the night.)
=========== MH: What a mysterious man. It seems he has a bit of a grudge against the man they call Razor.
JB: Indeed it does. I could see Raid going places here. He's a fine athlete, and he sure knows how to get the crowd behind him.
MH: Whatever. Next up we'll hear from Ghost. Now this guy's creepy. I almost wet my pants just looking at one of his t-shirts in the gift shop. ====== Ghost ===== (The scene opens to a graveyard. It is nighttime, and the moon illuminates the scene with an eerie white light. The camera pans across the graveyard, and we realize that one of the mausoleum's is open. The camera slowly moves closer, but stops as there is movement from inside the tomb. Slowly a figure dressed in dark garments appears from the grave, and makes his way toward the camera. The figure moves toward a gravestone in the shape of a crucifix, and props himself up onto it. He removes the hood from his head, revealing a young man, with black hair and totally white eyes. He motions for the camera to come closer, and then begins to speak in a soft, raspy voice.)
Ghost: Thank you for coming to meet me my friend. As you know, the CWP requested me to join them, seemingly needing a true athlete who can defeat anyone pitted against him. I did not join for any titles. I didn't join to show the world what a great wrestler I am. The CWP is full of lost souls. Wrestlers who were once great, but now only succeed in angering him. Yes, him. The man who sent me to retrieve the souls of the former great wrestlers, and bring them to their final resting place to serve him. Razor, you will be the first. I will haunt you, and you will be defeated. You will meet my maker. Him. You will meet him.
(Ghost pulls the hood back over his head, hiding his face in shadows. He jumps down from the gravestone, and proceeds towards the mausoleum. As he reaches the door, he turns around, looking into the camera, his eyes seemingly glowing despite their previous white appearence. He tilts his head up and begins laughing maniacally, and turns and continues into the tomb, his laughs echoing from the walls. The screen slowly fades to black.)
================ MH: Talk about wierd. This guy should seek professional help. JB: I've got to agree with you on this one Mike. Ghost certainly is a, well, unique individual.
MH: He's a freak!! JB: I wouldn't do anything that would upset him Mike. Who knows, you may be the first to meet HIM.
MH: I meant freak in a good way. JB: Next we're going to hear from a tag team who call themselves the
Oriental Alliance. MH: They don't even speak english. They don't call themselves anything that we can understand.
JB: Ok, let me rephrase this. Let's go to Matt James who's live in the locker room with the Oriental Alliance and their manager, Mr. Naguta.
================ The Oriental Alliance ================ (The scene opens to a locker room in the New Haven Coliseum. CWP reporter Matt James is standing with his back to the lockers as three men make their way into view. The first man is small, with distinct Chinese features such as his slanted eyes.
He wears glasses, which rest on the tip of his nose. His pinstripe suit is offset by his polka-dotted tie, and he smiles as Matt
James extends his hand in a friendly gesture. The man's hands remain at his side, and James' hand slowly returns to his side as the man laughs. The two other men accompanying the small chinese man are notably larger, built like tanks, and closely resemble each other. They both wear black tights and no shirts, revealing their glistening chests and bulging arms. It reads
"Oriental Alliance" down each side of their pants. Then we come to their hair. They both share identical haircuts. Long black hair hangs down past their shoulders, accompanied by short black spikes resting atop their heads. Matt James again extends his hand to the men. They frown, and turn toward the camera as James lowers his hand, grabbing the microphone from his pocket.)
Matt James: (to the smallest man) Mr. Naguta I presume. Mr. Naguta: Oh yes. I am Mr. Naguta, the most feared man in professional wrestling. Matt James: Excuse me? Did you say most feared? You aren't even a wrestler, are you? Mr. Naguta: No, I need not wrestle in order for people to fear me. I am the manager of the most dangerous tag team in the history of this sport.
Matt James: I won't deny that, by I also won't agree with you. Now, shall we start the interview?
Mr. Naguta: As you wish. Matt James: First off, you claim that these two men form the "most dangerous tag team in the history of this sport". What makes them so dangerous? Who have they beaten to prove that they are the best?
Mr. Naguta: For years these men travelled throughout the Orient, facing any and all challengers who believe they had what it takes to be the best. But my men, they have never lost a match. Now, as for dangerous, let me show you a short demonstration we have prepared.
(With that, he signals to Fuji, the slightly shorter member of the tag team. Fuji pulls his leg back, and extends it with a tremendous amount of force, putting his foot through the locker. He pulls his foot out, and returns to his original position, grinning slightly.)
Matt James: That was amazing!! Mr. Naguta: That is not all. (He signals to Kumoseki, who approaches the bench to the side of the locker room. He stares at the thick wooden bench for a minute, and then brings his head smashing down, through the bench.)
Matt James: How did he do that? That was absolutely amazing! I've never seen anything like that in my years as a professional wrestling reporter!
Mr. Naguta: You see, this is why my team is the most dangerous in the sport. And that is not all. They will not turn down a challenge, whether it be for a singles match or, their specialty, a tag team death match.
Matt James: What do you mean by death match? Mr. Naguta: It is exactly as I said. Any team who steps in the ring with the Oriental Alliance surely will not survive. Now, we must go, and prepare.
(The three men walk off the screen, leaving Matt James surrounded by a destroyed locker and a smashed bench.)
Matt James: Connecticut Wrestling Promotions, watch out, because the
Oriental Alliance will be a team to be reckoned with.
Jeremey, back to you. (The scene closes as Matt James walks out of view, and screen suddenly cuts back to the Shockwave Center.)
================= JB: Fans, we're out of time! I'm Jeremy Bane... MH: And I'm "Mean" Mike Henderson. Tune in next time for another edition of... BOTH: Connecticut Wrestling Promotions' Shockwave!!! (The camera slowly backs up as Jeremy and Mike talk to each other. The screen slowly fades out.)
