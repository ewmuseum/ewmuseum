CWP — Tournament Announcement

11 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to an empty arena. Darkness fills the building, and it is totally silent. We can see the outline of a ring, and there is a slight movement in the ring. Suddenly, a light bursts from above, shining down onto ring announcer Marty Mann. Mann adjusts his tie, and begins to speak clearly and confidently.)
Marty Mann: Ladies and gentlemen, it is my pleasure to introduce to you the man who gave me this job, the man with the most power in the world of wrestling today, the CWP president, "Superman" Shawn Rosenthal!!!
("Kids of the K-Hole" by NOFX begins to blast out of the p.a., and the arena fills with the sounds of pure punk music. The spotlight, along with a blue fog light, shine towards the entranceway. Shawn Rosenthal steps out from the entrance, and the empty arena erupts in cheers.
Shawn Rosenthal makes his way slowly to the ring, limping slightly, favoring his right knee. He slides into the ring, and takes the microphone from Marty Mann. He stares out across the empty seats, as cheers boom through the p.a. He begins waving his hands for the "crowd"
to quiet down, and clears his throat. He steps over toward the ropes closest to the announcer's table, and leans against them as he begins to speak.)
"Superman" Shawn Rosenthal: (to the crowd) Thank you! Thank you! Please, quiet down! (The cheers suddenly come to an abrupt end, and the blue fog light turns off, leaving him illuminated only by the spotlight.)
"Superman" Shawn Rosenthal: Fans, wrestlers, media, I'm here to make the biggest announcement in wrestling history. The recently formed CWP has decided to hold a tornament for the top players in the world of wrestling. Some wrestlers will be contacted, but an application is available at the CWP website at
http://www.concentric.net/~Eckx666/index.html . The winner of this tornament will be the new CWP New England champion, and will go onto the first CWP/OWWA Pay-Per-View to compete for the unified CWP/OWWA World
Heavyweight Title with the number one contender from the OWWA. All others may or may not be contacted to negotiate CWP contracts. Please consider this tornament, as it will determine the big players in the world of wrestling. Thank you very much, and good night.
(The screen closes as the spotlight suddenly disappears, and "Kids of the K-Hole" begins to play again as the screen slowly fades out.)
