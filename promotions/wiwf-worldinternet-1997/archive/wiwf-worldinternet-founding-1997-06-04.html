WIWF (World Internet Wrestling Federation) — Rules

Want a fed that is not Extreme Hardcore are short term? READ ON

4 June 1997

Hi I am the WIWF President. My fed World Internet Wrestling Federation
Below is a Package of info for you guys.
1.0 Roleplaying
1.1 Basic Rules of the WIWF
1.2 Cards and PPVS
1.3 Titles
1.4 Wrestler info
1.5 Homepage
1.6 IRC

1.0 Roleplaying
I use TNM Plus for the WIWF but Roleplaying is the most important thing you need to do. I do not use mass maling because I don't have no control over the fed that way. You must Roleplay 2 times or more a week to do well in the WIWF. And Roleplaying is a good way to get fueds going. And I forgot to add before if you join all flashes are sent to me.

1.1 Basic Rules
The Rules are simple, you can't have more than 2 wrestlers in the league unless is say so. If you don't roleplay at all your wrestler will be jobbed after two weeks are up you will be kicked out. If I find out you are using a diffrent e-mail to have more wrestlers in the fed I will kick you out.

1.2 Cards and PPVS
The WIWF holds Three cards a week they are Monday Night Bash, Wednesday Night Superstars and Saturday Nite Top Turnbuckle. We will have a PPV once a month. All cards *expect* PPVS will have a limit of 8 matches.

1.3 Titles
The WIWF Has a Total of 6 titles 4 Singels and 2 tag the list in the following:
1. UNIVERSIAL CHAMPION (Top WIWF Title)
2. United States Title (Doesn't mean that only US people go fight for it)
3. Superstars Title (Only defended on Superstars card thats what the belt is made for)
4. Gimmick Title (May sound stupid but you can only win the title with a gimmick match that I have on the sim)
TagTeam Titles are listed below:
1. World Tagteam Titles
2. Million Dollar TagTeam Titles

1.4 Wrestler Info
As you know you are alowed 2 wrestlers *NO REAL ONES* If you submitt an app with a real wrestler I will not let him in. For the time you are alowed to have two wrestlers, They can be tag and singeles but no real wrestlers.

1.5 Homepage
The Homepage is located at:
http://www.geocities.com/Area51/Zone/6497/
its nothing really but a homepage, it will be updated almost all the time.

1.6 IRC
WIWF Will have an IRC chat room real soon. If you want to get and IRC client go here:
http://www.mirc.co.uk

Thanks for reading this. And I hope you could join my league thanks!
WIWF President.
