The Construction

c. 24 Jan 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A “works in progress” roster-reveal page, pairing character art (not reproduced, per Curation §6) with a one-line billing for each name. Four of the six — Corky Spectre, White Tiger, The Communist, and Bowchickawahwah — already hold registered UiWF tenures from the GENESIS card; Jack Union and Kenny Mercury were previously known in this collection's UiWF holdings only as art with no accompanying script text; this page is the first surviving UiWF document to give either of them a billed role in words. “Soviette” was a name new to the collection when this page was first accessioned, billed here as the Communist's own manager; she is now her own registry entry, Soviette — also The Communist's own manager across a two-year UWF run, confirmed the same person under the same pairing.
Works in progress. The UiWF can't afford photographers or digital design artists. But we make do. Corky Spectre Owner. Bastard Offspring of Hall of Fame Inductee Lew Spectre. Jack Union Announcer. Overly Excitable British Ninny. White Tiger Announcer. Bitter, Racist, Drunk, Angry. The Communist Wrestler. Big, Angry, Russian, Smashes Things. Soviette Manager. Keeps the Communist From Smashing Too Many Things. Kenny Mercury Wrestler. Legendary Wrestler, Legendary Ego, Will Wrestle For Food. Chief Bowchickawah. Wrestler. Lakota Love God. The UiWF. The "i" Means it's Different.
