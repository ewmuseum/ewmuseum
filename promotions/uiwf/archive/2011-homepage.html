What this is The actual homepage of uiwf-online.com as the Wayback Machine first captured it — the deployment proof for the relaunch pitch built on the promoter's second laptop (see the earlier local draft). Its own browser-tab title, preserved here as a quote rather than reproduced as this page's title: “[The UiWF] All New Decade, Mostly Reused Dick Jokes” — identical to the laptop draft's own title, minus its “(2)” suffix. The page's splash image, an affiliate link-exchange banner, and a small footer logo accompanied the original and are not reproduced here, per the Museum's own image-curation policy (Curation §6). The splash image's own link target, shenanigans/category/news, does not resolve to anything else in the recovered corpus — a dead or never-archived link, left exactly as found.
