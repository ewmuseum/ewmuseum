Character notes

c. 1 Feb 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Planning document A private planning document — unrealized character and storyline sketches for the 2010 relaunch, never confirmed against any surviving card or served roleplay. The largest entry sketches a UiWF storyline for Shane Stone and Eric Cameron of the Phenomenons — not reflected in either character's own registered tenure record, and not added on the strength of a planning note alone. Two further character concepts here (a used-car-dealer manager, a luchador tag team) never surface in any other UiWF document recovered to date. Served whole under the same content notice as any other document; a scene of comic-abduction plotting stays exactly as written.
Local used car dealer that advertises with the UiWF and begins appearing to promote his dealership and because of a childhood obsession. Can easily manipulate Corky, but can't fight for himself. So who fights for him? Tag Team - At one point a major luchador team in Mexico, they were banned from the entire country after ... what? Drug use? Involvement with the drug cartels? One of the brothers has been obsessively taking steroids and every technique he can find to increase his muscle size to stupid proportions. Think Bane from Batman. The other one has become so fried on hallucinegic drugs that he permanently has voices in his head and other psychological issues. Maybe they have a "manager" that doesn't really exist, except in the head of the second brother. Think Zapato Brothers, with more Deadpool influence on the second brother. Thinking from New Mexico ... cowboy hats/luchador mask blend. "Mexican Radio" - Authority Zero. Names? The Phenomenons - Shane Stone is miserable working a 9-5 and being bullied by his wife. Eric's bored trying to write their autobiography. Talks Shane into coming back to wrestling, going with the UiWF because his wife can't see them on TV. She finally gets wise and Shane leaves her in a hurry. (Originally for BBS as a bored ex-supervillain working in insurance/banking. Now married with kids and starts working with the UiWF on the side trying to hide it from his wife.) ---- Eric Cameron stages desperate breakout for Shane, believing him to be kidnapped or under hypnotism. Gathers Phenomenal Pets and anyone else he can find. Over the top breakout attempt while Shane stares depressingly out the window. When Shane's finally broken "free," slow motion run to one another followed by a giant man-hug ... "Dude, that's kinda gay." Probably explain all of this after the Phenoms make first appearance on TV? Make first appearance, then ask them what the hell they're doing bothering with the UiWF when they could go anywhere. They trail off wistfully staring off Scrubs-like. "What the hell are you doing?!" "Explaining everything through a flash back and and an inner monologue, clearly." Then explain with the Phenomenal Breakout. Pietasters/Reel Big Fish theme .... I don't remember everyone that was involved with the Phenoms, and I need them all back. Eric needs to gather them all together before breaking out Shane. (Wife opens closet, camel hides behind raincoats ...) First RP, all of the backstory, Shane going through life depressed, Eric writing depressed, maybe a depressing phone call between them that starts to get back to how it was, only for his wife to yell at him so Shane has to go. Right afterwards, Eric decides that all of this has to be the work of an evil villain (blame Tsunami Oni, Baron Black Scourge, whoever) to finally kill the Phenomenons, and decides to fight back! "That's right ... we're BREAKING HIM OUT!" Dun dun dun! To be continued ... Second RP, Eric gathers the Phenomenal Army back together. Their scrawny bodyguard, animals, etc. Third RP ... the ridiculously over-the-top breakout. Angles: Declare war on the UiWF. Everyone. For no reason. Also, go after the World title ... only accolade the Phenoms have never had. World champ + partner vs Phenoms, all titles on the line?
