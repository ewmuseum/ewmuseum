The Beginning

c. 18 Jan 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reuses an already-served document The text below is identical, word for word, to the opening scene of the already-served GENESIS founding script — the Rabbi Mort Schwartzman's visit to Corky Spectre, up through Corky landing on the promotion's own name. The 2010 relaunch site repurposed that same passage, unaltered, as its own origin-story page; it stops exactly where GENESIS turns to the actual debut show, which this page does not include. Served here as its own artifact because it is one — a distinct webpage, on a distinct site, doing a distinct job — not as a duplicate of the card archive entry.
"If I were a rich man... da da dee dee da da dee dee da dee dum dum... " The Rabbi Mort Schwartzman sings to himself as he walks through a working class but picturesque Chicago neighborhood. Once known as the Rabbi of Pain, he has long since given up his referee stripes for the somber suit of a local Jewish community leader. But the Rabbi hasn't forgotten his past as an official for the Universal Wrestling Federation. In fact, he's headed right now to the home of a former colleague of his, young Corky Spectre. It's been some time since the Rabbi has seen the boy due to his responsibilities at the synagogue. But that doesn't mean that the scrawny and pimply lad isn't still near and dear to his heart. Coming to a quaint townhouse, the Rabbi climbs the steps and rings the doorbell. As he waits, he checks the sack he carries with him, making sure the soup and rolls inside are all right. "I wonder when the last time Corky had a home-cooked meal was. Oy, poor bubby." No one has come to the door, so the Rabbi rings the bell again. He waits longer, but still nothing. A third try gets the same result. "Hmmm... I thought for sure he would be here... " Acting on a whim, the Rabbi tries the knob and finds it unlocked. He pokes his head in and can just make out a faint clicking sound. "Corky?" There's no reply but the Rabbi swears he heard what sounded like heavy breathing. Concerned, the Rabbi enters the townhouse. The living area is empty except for mismatched furniture, multiple video systems, and a big TV. A look in the kitchen reveals that Corky has been subsisting off of pizza and Chinese takeout. The Rabbi sighs sadly and puts his bag of home-cooked goodness on the counter. He walks back out to the hall and notices the clicking sound gets louder by the stairs. Cautiously, the Rabbi climbs up until he reaches a door that's ajar. The clicking sound definitely seems to be coming from inside that room and the heavy breathing sound can be heard as well. As the Rabbi gets closer, he can make out words. "Yeah...yeah... yeah... " The Rabbi pushes the door open and his jaw drops in horror at what he sees. "Jumping Jehovah!" Inside the messy bedroom, Corky Spectre sits at a computer only wearing a set of Tsunami Oni underoos. His bespectacled eyes stare at the monitor with the intensity of a religious zealot. Corky's attention is so focused, he doesn't even notice his visitor. The Rabbi quietly walks over to see what has his young friend so mesmerized. Porn. Lots of porn. Porn of every kind and orifice. Spanking, watersports, gangbang, girl on girl, and the Rabbi is pretty sure he saw a llama pass by on one of the many windows Corky is furtively opening. "Corky! What are you doing?" The youngster doesn't even blink, let alone look at the Rabbi. "So much... there's so much... all for me... " In his heart of hearts, the Rabbi knows he must protect his young friend from the sinful temptations of carnal pleasure. So he reaches over and turns off the power to the monitor. As the screen turns black, Corky's expression turns from worship to horror. "Noooooo! My pretties!" Corky's head whips around and he stares at the Rabbi with bestial rage. "You! You took my pretties from me!" The Rabbi is afraid... not of Corky, but of the fact this whole thing is teetering close to being an LOTR knock-off, one already done by South Park. But then his attention is grabbed by Corky jumping up onto his chair like he was a pale ape with a whole lot of hair replaced by acne. The sight of Corky's yellow stained undies with a bulge poking out underneath them makes the Rabbi praise Rod that he hadn't eaten yet. Corky snarls and lunges at the Rabbi. "Rrrrraaaarrrrr!" SMAK! A backhand catches Corky across the face! SMAK! SMAK! SMAK! Corky gets a first hand lesson from where the name 'Rabbi of Pain' comes from! SMAK! "Ow! Owie owie ow ow! Please stop hitting me!" The Rabbi stops his bitchslappng and Corky settles down in his chair, gently rubbing his pinkened cheek. The Rabbi gently pats the top of Corky's greasy head. "Sorry, boychik. But you were completely meshuganeh there! You shouldn't be so obsessed with those....bad girls on the computer!" Corky looks up at the older man sadly. "But... they're the only friends I got... " "Don't be a yutz! I'm your friend, Corky!" "Yeah, but you're always busy with your Jew stuff." "I think I'd prefer it called 'work with the synagogue,' if you don't mind." "Oh. OK." "And you have to know other people, Corky." "Not since UWF closed. That was the only time when people talked to me. Some even treated me with... with... what's it called when someone doesn't give you swirlies or beat you up for your lunch money?" "Respect?" "Yeah, respect." "Corky, we all had a tough time in school when growing up." "Growing up? That's what happened to me last week when a couple Girl Scouts dropped by to sell cookies!" The Rabbi sighed and shook his head. "Corky, then what you need to do is find yourself another community. Take up a hobby. Do some volunteer work, anything other than sit around here all day. And how can you afford to do that anyway? Not like being an ex-ref pays that much" "My dad." "Lew Spectre?" "Shhh... he really doesn't like that brought up very often. He sends me a check very month or so. More than I can spend, even on premium sites." "Like a trust fund?" "More like hush money, I think. Too bad I can't buy friends like I had in UWF... " Suddenly, Corky's eyes grow wide and he hops out of his chair. "That's it! What a great idea! I can start my own promotion! I'll have friends again! Yippee!" Corky throws his arms around the Rabbi, who figures he'll have to burn his suit to get rid of the stink. Letting the Rabbi go, Corky looks over at his beloved computer. "And... and... we can put it all on here! Streaming video! On the internet! It will be an electronic federation! An.... e-fed!" The Rabbi steps out of the way as Corky dances around his bedroom with the grace of an arthritic orangutang. "Rabbi! This will be great! I bet there's plenty of small time wrestlers that could use the exposure!" "Speaking of exposure, bubby... how about getting cleaned up and putting some pants on?" Corky looks down at his attire, stained from hours of self-lovin' and a reluctance to tear himself from his pretties to use the bathroom. "Hmmm.... maybe you're right. But then we can get started!" The Rabbi blinks at Corky's choice of words. "We?" "Yeah! you'll help be right? You know all about wrestling, even more than me! And you're my best friend! So you'll help, right?" When Corky looks at the Rabbi with those big, pathetic eyes, the holy man has no way to say no. "Well, I can guess I can spare a little bit of time. And my Jewish community center might be able to be rented out once in awhile..." "Woo hoo! We can call it... the Slaughterhouse!" "Er, I don't know if my fellow elders would like our community center called that." "What if it was like the Kosher Slaughterhouse or something?" "Ummm.... I'll see." "OK, Rabbi! I'll go get cleaned up and we'll get started!" This will be the ultimate! Yeah! The Ultimate internet Wrestling Federation! Oh boy oh boy oh boy! I can't wait! Be right back!" And with that, Corky Spectre runs off to the bathroom as fast as his scrawny legs can carry him, leaving the Rabbi alone in his room. "UiWF... must admit, it does have a ring to it. And on the internet too. Crazy schmuck might have something there... " The Rabbi glances over to Corky's' computer. His eyes get a sly look to them. He listens closely until he hears the sound of the shower running. Then the Rabbi quickly turns the monitor back on. His bearded face lights up just like the X-rated images on the screen. "Oy gevalt! Look at the matzo balls on that one!"
