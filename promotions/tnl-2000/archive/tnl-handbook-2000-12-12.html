What is TNL?            The Next Level is a organzation based out of Salt Lake City, Utah.     There are much protest, and much praise for what goes on in the     home of the TNL, the E-Center. Fans back into this structure to watch     some of the finest wrestling action in the world.
    TNL is promoted by Integrity Incorporation, owned also by TNL Co-     President [name withheld]. Unlike TNL, Integrity Inc. is based in the
    "Big Apple," New York City. They might be far apart, but the TNL and
    Integrity Inc. have a close relationship. TNL is by all means a     corporation, holding shows across the United States and in years to     come Europe as well as Japan. TNL's fanbase is in the western states     of the Continental US. There's also a giant following in Japan as well     as Europe.
    All talent in TNL will make there fare share of money. They will be     fighting in many off the wall, death defying matches. Obviously, the     base of TNL is good ol' fashioned wrestling. But the TNL will not count     extreme men out of the federation to make way for guys who know     how to sell a move. If you find this statisfactory, please continue to     read what kind of system TNL runs.
    2b. Rules & Regulations 1.     This is something that isn't stressed much in federations anymore:     Have Fun. This is why e-wrestling is here. 2.     In order to take part of this "fun" you gotta particpate. One flash a     week is all TNL will ask from you. When you start to particpate, the     TNL head office will start sending you angle ideas with other handlers     that you can collaborate with. If you stay inactive in TNL for a two     weeks to a month, you will be removed from the roster/mailing list no     questions asked.
3.    DO NOT BREAK KAYFABE.  That means no mentioning of real life     characters such as; Stone Cold Steve Austin, Kevin Nash, The Rock etc.     As well as no mentioning of real life promotions such as; WCW, WWF,     AJPW, ECW etc. If you insist on breaking this rule, well you'll be fired.     We don't want people "laying the smack down," and saying there the     "excellence of execution" around here. 4.    TNL will ask for at least 3k [30 lines] per flash. A ideal length is 3-5k.     However, if you feel the need to expand that go ahead. As long as the     flash is beneficial to our eyesight, there's no beef. TNL will _not_ stand     for 10k flashes that make me want to doze off at the keyboard. Write     what you think will be entertaining, interesting and worth reading for     all to enjoy! 
5.    No OOC [Out of Character Comments] in flashes, please. This means no     throwing in those OOC comments of what the handler of the character     has done in the past. There's no room for shooting in the TNL.
6.    The maximum allowed roleplays to be sent in per week is two. If you     send more than two, we will just not use the extras. This will result     in a warning of spamming, a major issue the TNL does not want to deal     with, and won't.
7.    No attacking wrestlers in your flashes, please. Unless you and the     handler of _that_ character have agreed to let this happen, feel free to     do so. TNL will not tolerate this and breaking this rule will result in     explusion from the organzation.
8.    No Bitching About Titles.  I know it sounds funny, but I have had guys     bitch to me about holding what they think is the lower belt in the fed.       As far as I'm concerned, if you complain about holding a title, you will     be stripped and taken from the TNL list. That's, that.
    2c. Card Information     Sunday Night Revolution is the MAIN CARD of TNL and it will house all     the big matches/progressions in angles. It will usually take place     in TNL's home arena the E-Center, but will often occur around the
    United States as well as Japan. The card will be FULLY WRITTEN unless     noted otherwise. Sunday Night Revolution is the only card for the week and     it occurs on Sunday Night.
    2d. Title Information 1.    TNL World Championship Title     The top of the mountain, the pinnacle of TNL. The man who holds this     belt is the workhorse of the fed. He will have a big say in what goes     down here, and will carry much of the feds weight on his shoulders.
    He will have a big say in what goes down. You gotta be the best of     the best to have this around your waist. The Top Brass of TNL is 35     pounds of pure platnium.
2.    TNL Revolution Championship Title     The single most difficult belt to hold onto in TNL. A belt which     defintely prepares a man to step into the shoes of TNL World Title.  
    The Revolution Champion will be one of the best men of the federation     and will also hold great power over things in TNL.If you want     respect, this is your belt. If you want to step up to another level?  
    This is your belt. 40 pounds of _pure_ gold. 3.    TNL Television Champion     This belt is the belt which will propell you onto greater things. Is     that to say it is a lesser belt?  No way.  Some of the greatest men in     the federation will hold this. The holder of this belt will have much     say in what happens in TNL and will represent TNL as the worldwide
    Television Champion. 4.     TNL World Extreme Champion     For you hardheaded, bones of steel, insane psychopaths out there, this     is your belt. As the title says: World Extreme Champ. That means you     will be the most Extreme man in the world. You will take on the best     of all nations when TNL travels around the world. You hold sway over     big time events as well as the admiration of TNL and the world. 25     pounds of pure blood and heart.
