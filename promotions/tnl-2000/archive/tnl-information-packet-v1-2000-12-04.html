What is the ASWA?              The All-Star Wrestling Alliance is a federation based out of  Salt Lake City, Utah.  And as you can guess, there are many protests as well as praise for the violence which occurs in the doors of the ASWA's home arena, the
E-Center located in West Valley City, Utah.         The ASWA is a federation promoted by Integrity Incorperation, owned also by [name withheld].  Unlike the ASWA, Integrity Inc. is based in New York, New
York.  Although so seemingly seperated from eachother, the ASWA and Integrity
Inc. are a tightly ran ship and are connected at the core.  The ASWA is by all means a big corperation, holding shows accross the United States as well as holding shows outside of the Country.  The ASWA's fan base is made up of mostly western states, with a giant following in Japan as well as many European nations.  
      All talent making up the All-Star Wrestling Association will be paid big dollars. As they will be fighting in many off the wall, death defying matches.
 Of course, there is good ol' fashion wrestling invovled, but the ASWA will not count out the extreme men of the federation to make way for guys who know how to sell a move.  
If you find all of this satisfactory, please continue on and read more about the ASWA as well as Integrity Inc.
      2.b. Rules & Regulations 1. The ASWA is an e-mail federation, by sending an application to [detail withheld] , you are agreeing to be placed on a BCC'd mailing list.  Thus, waiving your rights to TOS anyone on the mailing list as well as the master of the list.
2. If booked on any card, you must Role Play.  If you fail to do so, you will be warned once, and if you fail to do so again after warning, I will take you off the mailing list without informing you.  We don't need people who don't RP, we need people who are willing to have fun.
3. DO NOT BREAK KAYFABE.  That means no mentioning of real life characters such as; Stone Cold Steve Austin, Kevin Nash, The Rock etc.  As well as no mentioning of real life promotions such as; WCW, WWF, AJPW, ECW etc.  No using pictures of real wrestlers, even if you use him as a basis for your character.
 If you insist on breaking this rule, you will recieve one warning, and if you continue to ignor it, you shall be removed from the list.
4. I particularly do not care how many lines you can roleplay.  Nor will I hold you to it.  I want you to write what your comfortable with.  Write what you think will be entertaining, interesting and worth reading.  
5. The maximum allowed roleplays to be sent in per day is two.  If you send more than two, we will hold all extra RP's until the following day.  This is to prevent spamming as well as other complicated problems.
6. No using another wrestler in your RP's unless given permission by the handler of the character.  That also stands for attack in Role Plays. 7. No Out of Character Comments in Role Plays.  This means no throwing in those OOC comments at the end of your RP's such as how many lines you had, what quality you gave it etc.  The only things I will allow are wrestler's stats.
 Such as record, description, stable name etc.   8. No Bitching About Titles.  I know it sounds funny, but I have had guys bitch to me about holding what they think is the lower belt in the fed.  As far as I'm concerned, if you complain about holding a title, you will be stripped and taken from the ASWA list.  A title is a title.  
9. Above all, have fun.  Contribute to the federation instead of making it a problem.  If you want to run an angle, contact the office.  If you want to run a fued, contact the guy who you want to fued with and work something out with them.  You have to participate in order to have fun.  
      2.c. Card Information                                                                                        ASWA Presents... All-Star Revolution!       All-Star Revolution is the MAIN CARD of the ASWA and it will house all the big events as well as small.  It will usualy take place in the ASWA home arena, but will often occur around the United States as well as Japan.  Titles, grudged, fueds...all will be settled here.  The card will be FULLY WRITTEN unless noted otherwise.  All-Star Revolution is the only card for the week and it occurs on Sunday Night.  If the roster becomes larger, then there will be a second card which will also take place on Sunday.
