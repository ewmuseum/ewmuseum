GSWP Flash: Mitch Gatlin

18 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MAXIMIZE FOR BEST VIEWING [-The scene opens up in Atlanta, Ga. It seems to be a very dark night. There is no moon in the sky and the clouds are covering the stars. There is a bit of snow falling from the sky.-] [-The camera focuses in on a couple of guys standing around a fire barrel. All of them are wearing ragged clothes that appears to come from someone's trash. They are all huddled together ever so tightly with there hands over the fire barrel.-] [-The Camera pans to the right a slight bit and further down the street so that you can see the corner. There are a few really nice cars sitting next to the corner. You can see about three black men dressed in expensive clothes and warm coats. Walking from car to car taking money and giving the people in the car something in return. It is too far off to see what they are giving them.-] [-The camera starts to pan to the right a bit faster than earlier. You see a wall with different pictures that has been drawn in spray paint on it. As the camera continues to pan to the right you begin to see a chain linked fence. You can notice behind the fence there is a basket ball court. The lights in the basket ball court light up each goal. You can barely see the bleachers.-] [-The camera slowly zooms in on the bleachers. You start to notice that there is a signal man sitting on these bleachers. He is sitting on the third row of bleachers. With his legs propped up on the set of rows in front of him and he is laid back on the set behind him.-] [-The camera begins to get a close up on this man sitting by him self on the bleachers. There is no one else around him. You have to stare hard to see him in the shadows but you are able to tell that the man is wearing a white hooded sweatshirt, some snow camouflage pants and a pair of black work boots. The man has his head hanging downward as of he was looking at the ground.-] Man: "Let me tell you about my self. I have been around the wrestling ring ever sense I was the ripe age of 14. When my father died. I got sent to live with his Fathers best friend Boltz. Boltz trained me to the fullest. He made me go to all of his Matches and help set up the rings and so fourth. I used to sit back and watch all the wrestlers do there thing. I was around all the greats Joe Boxcar, Ian Walsh, and his ideal and trainer Boltz. Watching was all I did at first. That is where I learned most of my moves." "Boltz really started cracking down on me when I turned seventeen. Boltz had me in the gym everyday with him. He always told me "I am going to be the best _damn_ wrestler he has ever trained." I went from the ring to the gym with him. Boltz was teaching me everything he _knew_." "Three yeas went by and I got sighed into my first promotion. It was the Extreme Wrestling Council. Boltz told Me "I should not sign with them it is not a quality organization, they are just brutal." I just did what I always do. I joined up." "My first match was against a man by the name of The Rebel. The Match was a No D. Q., Pin fall anywhere. As was all the matches in E.W.C. I came into this match more as a technical wrestler. Just like Boltz had taught him. The Rebel had his way with me in the begging? Hitting me with chairs and Garbage cans. The only thing is that I did not hate the pain like everyone else. Something made me start to like it. Eventually I forgot about all the technical moves he Boltz taught me and did what I do best just fight. As soon as I did that I won the match." "I started to progress in the E.W.C. I turned into a very angry and violent man. Something came over the me. It was like I had gotten possessed. I went through everything E.W.C through at me. Eventually I got a title shot for the E.W.C Southern States Title." "I Had won my first title. I was onto of the world. After a while I got tired of the Southern States title so finally I just drooped the title. I drooped it off the Golden Gate Bride. I said "I has bigger things on his mind." "I started making my way up the ladder. I was really learning a lot about how to be an Extreme wrestler. Hell I went through scalpel matches, Barb-wrire cage matches, ect." "Soon after I dropped the S.S. title I won the E.W.C World title. I held the title for about three months until the president got shot in an argument with a loan shark. I will for ever be the E.W.C World Champion." "I was jobless and went back to my Trainer Boltz to find a job. Boltz got me a deal in The M. L. W. The people in M.L.W did not know what think of me. With my Extreme back ground I was breaking all the rules and a few necks. After my first match Joe Boxcar gave him the nick name that has stuck with me forever. He said damn boy "You _Sure_ do have some _Fire_ in you." Box car started calling me Sure Fire and it has just stuck with me ever since then." "I was dominating the action around M.L.W. Until I could have sworn I met my match. I got put in one of his biggest matches of my entire career. I had to face my trainer Boltz." "I worked out everyday. As soon as the match started I went toe to toe with Boltz. I knew every reversal for every hold Boltz had. This is where everyone new I would make it in the wrestling world. After forty-five minutes of non stop action I Fire Plexed Boltz through the announcers table and got the pin. After the match I picked Boltz up gave him a hug and we both walked to the locker room together. Shortly after I got fired from the M.L.W for not having enough authority." "I just shrugged it off and went to the N. B. W. New Blood Wrestling. I fought there and beat Tommy Lionheart, and Alex Cote for the World title. The N.B.W days were numbered and I seen that coming. I then drooped the title and went on my way. I went through a lot of promotions wrestling some pretty big names. Until I got to the P. C. W. Where I got charged with two counts of Aggravated Assault and got sent up state to Woodland hills correctional felicity for two years." [-The man raises his head up from starring at the ground. The shadow from the hood of this man's sweat shirt prevents you from seeing his face.-] The Man: "Prison life is not an easy life. The people in prison will kill you for anything, or atleast try. Think about this. Your first night in prison and waking up with blood running down your face and dripping all over your chest. Then you raise up in a panic and see A guy sitting in the corner rocking him self back and forth just mumbling to him self. The guy is holding a shank with your blood dripping off of it. If you are not crazy before you go in you are crazy when you come out but in my case I was crazy before I went into prison. One only has to think of what it has done to me now. I had to fight to eat, Fight to sleep, Hell I even had to fight to sleep. I have been fighting to survive for so long it is as if it is natural to me now." [-The man pulls his hood down from over his head and it reveals a dark complicated face. This man has a long black hair that is up in a pony tail and He has a deep scar that start's from his lower right cheek and stretches across the right side of his face just missing his eye.-] Man: "My name is Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin. Some of you may have herd of me and some of you may not have herd of me. It really does not matter to me because the ones of you out there who does not know who I am will get to know me real soon and the ones who do know I am will get the honor to know me even better." "I am in the Garden State Wrestling Promotion for a few reasons. The first reason is to make a name for my self. When people think of Mitch Gatlin, I want them to think of fear. The thought of getting into the ring with a guy like me will damn near make you want to hang your wrestling boots up and call it quits. I am the _future_ of wrestling as we know it. I am the man that will signal handedly change the aspects of the game." "I am a the top rookie to step into the G.S.W.P. All though I am no rookie but to most of these guy's here I am. What you all do not understand is that I am the best. I am a new breed of wrestler. I am the one who is going to be headlining the Pay Per Views. I am the one who is going to be holding the G.S.W.P Strap. I am the _future_!" "The second reason I am here is because I am sick and damn tired of seeing all of these old out of shape men who should have retired years ago. They are trying to wrestle like they did in there prime. They are not in there prime anymore and I am here to show them that. They want to stay in the "Spot Light". A solid example is Steve Houston. Steve is an old man. He has accomplished just about everything one man can accomplish in the wrestling world and he is still trying to be in the spotlight. James Pillow is yet another example. These two men _was_ great. There time has passed." [-Mitch reaches into his pocket and pulls out a pack of Marlboro Lights and black zippo. He lights him a cigarette and continues to talk.-] Mitch "SF" Gatlin: "Now let me focus on Steve Houston a bit more considering he has to step into the ring with "The New Breed Of Wrestler". Steve I am going to be your worse nightmare in this match between are little teams. I have seen what you can do now and I have seen what you were able to do four years ago Steve and you my friend have lost a step. You just cannot cut it with the someone like me. Steve unlike you I am in my prime." "Steve I am going to eat you for Breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Just the thought of an old timer like you trying to compare your self to a guy like me just makes me want to laugh. I am going to show you and the G.S.W.P. That you are old and washed up. You just cannot make it in today's wrestling society. I am a new breed of wrestlers. I am the talent that is going to over come the G.S.W.P and I am the talent that will soon end your career." "Houston you have been trying to stay in the "Spot Light" for as long as possible. That is why you are still here. You tried the retirement thing. You got out on your sandy beaches with all your money and pretty ladies and just could not stand it. It was the worst thing you had ever done. Yet it was your dream. It scarred you that your dream was so simple and boring. It was a nice place to visit but you just could not live there. You are like me you thrive on getting noticed. You thrive on being the top dog. Well, Steve I am telling you not asking you. You better go ahead and pass that "Spotlight" on before I have to _take it_!" "Steve when me and you step into that ring. You will know that you are getting _slower_, _weaker_, and turning into a sad old man. Steve you better grow eyes in the back of your head because when you are not looking and least expect it I will be there and it will not be pretty. Remember this one thing Steve, if you play with fire. Your old ass is going to get _Burnd_!" [-Mitch takes a draw off his cigarette.-] Mitch "SF" Gatlin: "I have seen the card announcement for "The Resurrection" and I noticed that conveniently I am in a Team Survival match with Chris Page, Scott Viper, and Jake Oneil." "I cannot say I know to much about Chris Page, but I do know that he will be able to hold his own in this match. I have herd nothing but good things about Chris but Chris if you do somehow mess this match up for me then you have a big problem on your hands pal. I will hunt you down and beat you personally. I do not trust anyone and you shouldn't either." "As for Scott Viper and Jack Oneil. If you two get in my way in this match I will treat you the same way I am going to treat Steve and let me tell you it is not pretty. So stay out of my way and do not cost me the match and we will have no problems." [-Mitch takes another draw off his cigarette.-] Mitch "SF" Gatlin: "Now onto Shawn Sylkk. Shawn my boy long time no see. I believe the first time we met was about three years ago back when I was just a rookie. I remember it all to well. It was in the A.P.W.A for the world title and you got one over on me. Well, Shawn even the sun shines on a dogs ass." "Sylkk you are not going to know what hit you. I am going to be all over you like a fly on horse shit. Shawn I have wrestled you before and I know what you got. Yes, Shawn you may think you know what I got up my sleeve, but I am not the same person that I used to be. This is a whole new guy. I have my back against the wall and nothing to lose so you know for damn sure I am pulling out all the stops. You can count on it." "Shawn I am not a very forgiving man. You see I have changed. I am not that same little snot nosed punk I used to be. I have had some growing up to do. Now Shawn we will see who the better man is and I will not make the same mistakes I made the last time we got into the ring with each other. So "ShowTime" Shawn Sylkk your eviction notice is in and the _Show_Is_Over_ come January 31st." "I do not know anything about Oz and Dave Pietka but I do know one thing. You better be prepared when you come to that ring the Thirty-first because I am coming head hunting for you two fella's and you better get to studying because I am going to do my homework on you two fellas." "The G.S.W.P has not seen a guy like me in along time. I am going to be the back bone of this organization. I intend to have that strap around my waist and I will go to any length to make it that way. So everyone in G.S.W.P can either "_Like me_, _Love Me_, Or _Hate Me_! I do not give a damn but you will learn to _Respect Me_!"
