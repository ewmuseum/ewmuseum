Introduction ============== A Rookie by time a Veteran by standards..The GSWP is a play by email fantasy wrestling fed. The sole intent of the GSWP is for the fun and enjoyment of all members who choose to get involved. Started by [name withheld] and backed by some of the best roleplayers around the GSWP was most recently voted in the top 15 of all efeds on the internet. Take a look and find out why... ================ 2. All About GSWP ================ --[GSWP Origin]-- Formed on Sunday March 21, 1999 by master-mind Marcus Robinson. Robinson used to be the head booker for the now defunct East Coast Wrestling Alliance back in '96. Taking the knowledge of booking and front office experience Marcus started his own fed The Garden State Wrestling Promotion(GSWP). The GSWP is based out of Lakewood, NJ where they have their training center as well as Main Office Complex. The GSWP's weekly card, Sunday Night Impact, comes to you live every week from the GSWP Madhouse. But there is often travel across NJ and PA. --[GSWP Broadcasts]-- Each week the GSWP brings you two great shows. Every Saturday the GSWP brings to you GSWP Talk is Cheap. This is where the stars of GSWP say what they want uncut and uncensored. This is where it's proved somethimes talk can indeed be cheap. Then every Sunday night at 5PM on local New Jersey stations, the GSWP proudly brings to you GSWA Sunday Night Impact. Live every week from the GSWP Madhouse in Asbury Park, NJ. Jam packed with two full hours of the best GSWP has to offer. Featuring 7-8 exciting matches as well as plenty of surprises week in and week out. --[GSWP Championships]-- HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP - This is the top dawg. If you hold this belt it proves you are the best and most elite in the GSWP. GARDEN STATE CHAMPIONSHIP - Second best? not at alll. This belt shows you have what it takes to be be the next heavyweight champion. TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP - This belt is also known as the endurance belt. The champion goes out week after week and defend the belt. Defended weekly at Impact and ,like all other titles, at every Pay-Per-View. HARDCORE CHAMPIONSHIP- He's Hardcore!! He's Hardcore!! He's Hardcore!! The man that carrys this title indeed has the right to callhimself one of the toughest in the sport. TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP - Read Heavyweight Championship. --[GSWP Pay Per Views]-- Every February, April, June, August, October, December the GSWP goes national on Pay Per View. Every title is on the line. And major surprises happen. Angles are usually ended and new ones started on these shows. ================= 3. Meet The Faces ================= --[Marcus Robinson]-- This is the man that makes this all possible. Hes the main man, the head honcho. He calls all the shots. Every decision must go through him. He is in charge of firing people as well as hiring new talent. --[Commissioner Christian Davis]-- This man is second in charge. He can fine wrestlers, assign matches, do all that good stuff. He is known to be a d*ckhead throughout wrestling. So if I were you, dont cross the Commissioner! --[Anthony Sigtini]-- Hes the head commentator. Hes the man that calls the play by play. Loved by the fans of GSWP. And is a great role model for young children. A great all around guy. --[Frankie Lobeck]-- The complete _OPPOSITE_!!! A pompous jerk. Has no respect at all for anyone except himself. He hates Anthony Sigtini and all of GSWP with a passion. Doesn't care, will do anything for people to hate him. A loner. --[Rick Fuller]-- He's the man that brings you the backstage happenings each week at Sunday Night Impact and every Pay Per View. Gets pre-match interviews from every wrestler. --[Mike Capone]-- The voice of the GSWP. GSWP's version of Michael Buffer. Announces the wrestlers as they come to the ring. Gets the crowd going crazy before each show. ==================== 4.
