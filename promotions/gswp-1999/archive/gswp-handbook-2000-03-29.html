About The League <><><><><><><><><><><><><> 1. What is GSWP? Garden State Wrestling Promotion is an e-mail-based fantasy wrestling federation, which is booked and utilizes a closed-flash (RPs sent in flash packets) system. The GSWP was formed in early 1999 by [name withheld] (known for
"Five Stars" [name withheld]). The GSWP is based out of the Jersey Shore. Mainly
Asbury Park.
2. Who runs the GSWP? REAL STAFF - The GSWP is ran by one person. And that person is [name withheld]. He is known for his wrestling character "Five Stars" [name withheld].
FICTIONAL STAFF - Rick Fuller - If any man knows wrestling it is this man. He knows the business inside out. He is the head commentator for GSWP Sunday Night Impact. Tends to play favorites to the good guys.
Johnny Martoni - If there is any bigger pompous a*shole in this business, this is the man. He thinks he is a fake mobster and he tries to keep off the subject of wrestling. Tends to play favorites to the heels.
Anthony Jacobs - He is the man who interviews the wrestlers back stage before and/or after matches.
[name withheld] - He is the real-life president and he is the on-screen president. Dont cross him because he will make sure your stay in GSWP isnt a good one. Mike Capone - GSWPs world famous ring announcer 3.What titles are there in AWMC? a. GSWP Heavyweight Championship The top title of the GSWP. It signifies that you are the top wrestler in the federation with the belt. Top contenders for the belt will be decided by booking as well as other factors. However, a good feud will easily replace the top contender.
b. Garden State Championship This is the equivalent to a United States or IC Title, and it is the second-best title in the fed. Top contenders for the belt will be decided by booking as well as other factors. However, a good feud will easily replace the top contender.
c. Television Championship This is also labeled as the "Endurance" belt. Because it is up for grabs every week on GSWP Sunday Night Impact. If you hold this belt you might as well just be the most physically fit competitor in GSWP.
<><><><><><><><><><><> II. Rules and Regulations <><><><><><><><><><><> 1. How are the matches determined? -Roleplaying -Heat/Angles (You do not have to be heel to get heat) -Strats -Effort (If effort is put forth, you'll eventually get a push) 2. What are the roleplay rules? Two flashes and one in-ring per week is the limit on roleplaying, though one great roleplay can sometimes beat out two average ones. Numerous RPs sent in for one match will be frowned upon. Several RPs, whether weak or strong in quality, inconvenience bookers and break the rules. Focus instead on producing the best roleplays you can within the limit. Descriptive, consistent
(with the character), creative roleplaying ("RPs", "flashes") helps bring your character wins.
3. How do I get matches? a. You get assigned a match b. You challenge someone in a RP c. Send in a 'formal' request for the match *It is the bookers decision if the match will be signed or not* 4. Are sneak attacks allowed? Attacks and interference are allowed in moderation and administration has the right to refuse a sneak attack. Try to keep it somewhat realistic in the context of wrestling. All members are expected to be professionals and
'sell' attacks and incorporate them into flashes, strats, etc. However, if you do have problems with the way your character was portrayed in an attack (used in a flash without your permission; portrayed in a way inconsistent with his character, etc.), e-mail the President and AWMC administration can decide if it's worth acting upon. Attacks and interference will never be used to affect the outcome of a match, unless (among other reasons) the party being attacked is already booked to lose, one of the parties in the match has something developed, or (extremely rarely) if an angle might benefit from the attack/interference. Don't ask to cost someone a match; if a member has worked on a match, they'll get consideration before attack/interference requests. You can ask for your attack to be considered if the person is booked to lose.
However, be aware it might not get used. 5. How many wrestlers may I handle? You are allowed one wrestler or one tag team. <><><><><><><><><><><> III. Events and Shows <><><><><><><><><><><> 1. What shows are there? GSWP Sunday Night Impact Usually: Sundays Written: Play by play Matches: Live matches Announcers: Rick Fuller and Johnny Martoni GSWP Talk is Cheap Usually: Wednesdays and Saturdays Written: this is the RP show Matches: None Announcers: None 2. Are there PPV's? Yes there are. GSWP holds a Pay Per View LIVE every two months for the world to see what just GSWP is all about. The usual months are February,
April, June, August, October, December.
3. Where are the shows held? Sunday Night Impact every week is held in the GSWP Madhouse in Asbury
Park, NJ. It is a 2100 seat facility. Sometimes we will travel around the
east-coast. <><><><><><><><><><> IV. Titles <><><><><><><><><><> 1. How will the titles be decided? The Heavyweight Championship will be decided in a 16 man tournament at the first Pay Per View in April. The Garden State title will be decided in a fatal fourway elimination match at the first returning Impact. And the TV
Title will be decided in a ten man battle royal.
2. How often will the titles be defended? Heavyweight - Once a month .. and at every PPV. Garden State - Once every other week Televison - Every week 3. How can I get a title shot? -If the champion challenges you and I think you deserve a shot. -If youre one of the top three active contenders for the belt. <><><><><><><><><><><> V. Match Info <><><><><><><><><><><> 1. How often can my guy wrestle? Once a week pretty much since we only have one show. 2. Can I add stipulations to my matches? Yes, within reason. No too extreme matches to start the feud off. <><><><><><><><><><><> VI.
