GSWP Press Conference

11 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To be put on the mailing list e-mail [detail withheld] [Suddenly you are taken away from your regularly scheduled program. The screen turns pitch black. Suddenly the words "Special News Bulletin" flashes across the screen in canary yellow letters. The darkness fades out and we are taken inside of a news studio. Sitting at a desk is a middle aged man. His black hair is brushed to the side as we move in closer...the man is staring in to the camera. He shuffles through the papers on the desk before speaking to his audience.] News Broadcaster: Long time wrestling organization the Garden State Wrestling promotion will reopen it's doors. It closed in 2000 due to financial problems. But word is that long time friends of Eric Cooper, Jay Ralston and Danny Wilson stepped in and bought portions of the company. [Short pause.] NB: We'll keep you posted on any other information we have, but right now, let's head over to the GSWP Conference Hall where we understand that the new reigning commisioner of the GSWP will speak publically of the matter. Be sure to tune in to the Ten O'clock news broadcast for more information. [We exit the broadcast studio and are taken inside the GSWP Conference Hall. The fairly large room is filled to the brim. All wondering what the announcement could be about...or even who they are going to hear from. They're not quite sure what, but they know it's going to be big. All commotion comes to a hault as a short man in a black suit makes his way in to the smoked filled room, up the stairs, and to the podium where he stands in front of the awed crowd of reporters who all ask themselves the same question. "Is this the commisioner?"] Man: Ladies and gentleman it is my pleasure to introduce to you...the commisioner of the GSWP... [A big sigh of relief falls over the crowd.] Man: Here he is...former GSWP Champion himself... [The suspense rises.] Man: King Snake! [Flash bulbs flicker and pop as the stunned crowd of reporters can barely believe their ears. King Snake walks out to a nice ovation. The majority of the reporters are old timers who respect the once great superstars accomplishments. He walks out in shiny black cowboy boots, denim black jeans, a black duster, and his famous black stetson hat. He reaches the podium with a grin on his face. He takes a moment to take in the applause...probablly becuase he hasn't recieved one in a long time. He motions for the reporters to quiet down so he can begin. With the GSWP reopening, there's a lot of work ahead...he's a busy man anyway.] KS: It's been kicked around...it's been rumored about...an' now it's becomin' a part o' reality. You probablly all heard that the Gee Es Dubbya Pee is returnin'. Money bags Ralston an' his compadre Wilson dug deep down in to their pocket books an' bailed Coop out o' a bind. [Short pause as Snake takes a gander over the sea of reporters.] KS: So yer all prolly wonderin' what this old timer has ta say here today. Gee Es Dubbya Pee was a happenin' place back in it's hay day. An' what better way ta bring it back from the dead than ta hold a happenin' card, so ta speak. An' that is why on January 31st, we will be back in business as we proudly bring you Survival o' the Fittest. An' all o' the survivors that make it ta the Battle Royal are in fer a treat. Sure, a hell o' a beatin'. But when the smoke clears...an' the fightin's done...the winner will be the new Gee Es Dubbya Pee World Heavyweight Champion. A title, that has been held by Fang, Johnny Glamour, Duke Putrid, an' yers truly. [Flashbulbs flicker and pop once again as a grin comes to the Texan's face.] KS: Now, in the past the Gee Es Dubbya Pee has always had a healthy roster. An ain't nothin' changed. An' that is why with further adue, I bring ta all o' you...the lineup for Survival o' the Fittest 2001, "the Ressurection"... [A graphic comes up on the big screen which reads... Survival of the Fittest 2001 "The Ressurection" [Main Event--Survivor's Battle Royal for World Heavyweight Title] [Superbout] TBA v.s. TBA [Survivor Match 4] James Pillow\Scottie Extreme\Brian Floyd\Brutus Bombastic Logan Armstrong\Damon Knight\The Dark Side [Survivor Match 3] Moo Kame\Whog Shrog\Eric Emerson\Zakk Van Malmsteen Rune\The Reaper\Sean Sixberry\Shawn Jamison [Survivor Match 2] Steve Houston\Shawn Sylkk\Oz\Dave Pietka Chris Page\Mitch Gatlin\Scott Viper\Jake Oneil [Survivor Match 1] Tommy Lionheart\Jarrett Scott\Jack Vars\Wardancer Terrance Goldworthy\Eric Mendell\Vinnie "the Jersey Boy"\K. Morlock [We pan back down to King Snake as the graphic remains up on the screen behind him.] KS: No matter who it is, whoever makes it through this test is going ta be more than deservin' o' the title...World Champion. While they might not be the champion I was...they'll be respectable. [Suddenly a reporter blurts out...] Reporter: Didn't you stab Johnny Glamour in the back to win the title? KS: Woah, did I ask for questions? No, not today. But to answer the question. Bein' a champion is doing whatever it takes ta win. An' that's what I did. Bottom line. An' I hope any one o' these men would do the same. That's all I've got fer ya today. But you haven't seen the last o' me. I'll be back. An' be sure to be present for the Gee Es Dubbya Pee's return show on the 31st. Wrasslin' history will be made...an' that a fact. [The last quote lingres in the air as Snake makes his way down the stairs and out of sight. Ignoring the reporters as he makes his way out. We fade out as you are taken back to your regularly scheduled program.]
