"Ice Storm" vs. James Sandison (7/11/99)

11 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
___ ____ / _ \ / ___| | (_| |___________________________\__ \ \__, | |____/ |___/ TELEVISION TITLE ON THE LINE! | | Chris Winters vs James Sandison_ __| \ \ /\ / / | '_ \ \ V V /________________________| |_) ) \_/\_/ | __/ |_| [A spotlight drops onto the curtain as the opening notes of "Dead President" by Jay-Z hit the Public Address. The shadow of a well cut figure is seen in the beginning of the aisleway. The fans begin to pop as the Jumbotron shows a large photo of "Ice Storm" Chris Winters standing with his arms at his sides in the middle of a blizzard. The Jumbotron then cuts to a shot of Winters delivering his Freezer Burn DDT to numerous opponents.] MICHAEL CAPONE: Introducing first, weighing in at 243 pounds and standing 6 feet 3 inches tall, he is the retired PPWO, FSW and RHW Heavyweight Champion, fighting out of Boston, Massechusetts, he is your current GSWP Television Champion ... [Winters steps out through the curtain and raises his arms to the sky to a thunderous ovation from the crowd. The Television Title dangles from his left hand. He runs his fingers through his short blonde hair and gazes into the ring with his ice blue eyes.] MICHAEL CAPONE: ... "ICE STORM" CHRIS WINTERS!!! [Chris gives a meager smile as he makes his way slowly to the ring. He wears a pair of long white tights with iceicles hanging down from the waist, a pair of sky blue kneepads over the knees. His chest is firm and cut, and his abs are as defined as ever. Chris wears a white elbowpad on his left arm from a previous injury. He checks his wrist tape and steps up into the ring, ready for battle.] FL: Hey, Ant...ever wonder why these guys entrances never vary? AS: *hack* The name is Anthony; and, no. FL: Chrisright, Annit. Whatever you say. Chris Winters is going up against James Sandison for Winters' TV title. AS: Yeah. Winters seems ready....don'tcha think? FL: HEH...we'll see. NG: "About to come down the aisle ... hailing from Newcastle, England, standing at six-feet, one-inch and weighing in at two-hundred and fourteen pounds ... here is ... JAMES SANDISON!!!" [There is an excited crowd pop throughout the arena as "Simply the Best" starts to play over the P.A. address system. The crowd are cheering in anticipation, then suddenly there is a burst of fireworks, and the spotlight focuses onto the entrance way. Moments later, the highly talented James Sandison appears from behind the entrance ramp. Sandison is dressed in a pair of long, black wrestling tights, and a pair of black wrestling boots. The tights read "God's Gift" down the sides in bold, white lettering. He is also wearing a black T-Shirt which reads, "The Headliner" on the front, and has a large, blue cross on the back. Sandison's long, blonde hair is hanging loosely by his side. His blue eyes stare menacingly at the ring. There is a large heel reaction for Sandison as he now starts to make his way down towards the ring. The young star ignores the fans as he walks quickly down towards the squared circle. He then hops onto the ring apron, and he springs over the top rope and into the ring. Fireworks shoot up as Sandison prepares for his match.] AS: Sandison surely looks ready tonight. He wants Winters' TV title. FL: Of course he does, Ant. I mean...who in the right mind *wouldn't* want the of gold...the best of the best...the prime commodity...the one...the only...!!! AS: It's not the top gold, Frank. Hey--spinning heel kick to the head from Sandison to start off the match! Wow! FL: Sandison pulls a move out of his butt to start the match off; and it's gonna' be a good one for the GSWP TELEVISION TITLE! Yes, *the*-- AS: *ahem* Looks like Sandison is on top of the much larger Winters...his arm wraped around his neck. [Sandison sends Winters into the ropes, and leaps over him as he runs by. Sandison turns around only to be clotheslined fiercely to the ground--after he flips twice in the air! Sandison holds his neck in pain, but Chris Winters shows no remorse as he stomps away on the back of James Sandison. He picks up Sandison, and sends him into the ropes. Sandison fires back at him, and Winters catches him off of the ropes--and slams him to the mat with a belly-to-belly suplex.] FL: Owwwie.... AS: I know that one hurt--I mean... FL: We need the obvious, Antini. We *really* do. AS: Thanks. Oh--here's an obvious opinion. Winters should've covered Sandison. FL: Nah--he's too busy now. Winters pushes Sandison into a far turnbuckle, and begins boxing him in! The fists are going so fast...Christ, I can't see anything! AS: Your glasses are right there-- FL: Shaddup. [Sandison falls from the corner in a slump as the GSWP official pulls away Winters. Chris grins and immediately picks up Sandison again. He whips Sandison into the ropes one more time, and sticks his arm out for a huge lariat. The smaller Sandison (but still large in his own right) dropkicks Winters right in the gut, and grabs on to his arm, putting him in an arm bar onto the mat.] FL: Oooooh, talk about pulling moves outta nowhere. I didn't see that one coming--and by the look on Winters' face...he didn't either. AS: Wait! Winters has the ropes with his...available arm. Sandison *must* let go of the hold. FL: C'mon, you have 3 seconds, Sandison! Every one of 'em counts. AS: Sandison...ABIDING BY THE RULES, lets go of the hold. FL: James waits for Chris to rise, and once he does, he runs off the ropes, and tries to dropkick Winters in the mouth as he leans up against the ropes! AS: Ouch, not so fast, Sandison...Winters moves!! [As Winters moves, Sandison's legs go right through the ropes, and he lands outside of the ring. Winters slides out...and immediately whips Sandison into the barricade. Sandison crumbles to the ground, and Winters picks him up, and signals for a piledriver! He tries to pick up Sandison--but Sandison doesn't budge! Sandison lifts up Winters--for a back body drop! Winters lands right on his back, and screams out of utter pain!] FL: Ow. I know that hurt like hell. AS: I'll bet. Sandison runs right over to the fallen Winters, and takes his leg--are you ready for an ankle lock submission hold!? FL: Not really--and Winters isn't either. He's screaming again. Hey, do you think if he tapped out...here, outside of the ring, it'd count? AS: No. [Sandison, seeing that the count is almost to 10, lets go of Winters' leg. However, the damage was done, and Sandison waits inside of the ring for Winters to crawl back in. As Winters tries to climb into the ring, a hand from underneath trips him! Winters falls to the ground and then gets a baseball slide right from above from Sandison! Sandison rolls him back in.] FL: Whoa! A hand from underneath the ring! AS: What do you think-- FL: *Who* do you think is the question. A human was the only thing I've known to have a hand. AS: Thanks for the intelligent input. FL: It was a pleasure, Ant. [Sandison looks to the fallen Winters, and picks him up. He whips Winters into the corner, and hops on him. He begins punching! The fans are going nuts!] AS: They love Sandison tonight! FL: What do they think of Winters? AS: You tell me. [The fans are going crazy for Sandison...until Winters grabs his legs and slams him down to the mat--a backbreaker. PIN! 1!!! 2!!!!! 3????? NO! So close. Both men are very fatigued at this point...to the extent that they simply lay on the mat. Winters is the first to stand up, and Sandison follows behind him. Winters boots him right in the gut, and as he kneels over, Winters grabs his arms and drives him down into the mat with a facedriver.] AS: Yow...that's gotta' be it!! FL: Winters isn't pinning, he's merely looking down upon the fallen Sandison! [Winters begins laying the boots on Sandison, until Sandison rolls outside of the ring. Winters grins, evilly, and rolls off of the ropes...and dives over the ropes, landing right on James Sandison! Both men sit for a minute or so, until Chris Winters stands, and DDTs Sandison right into the outside area. Winters throws Sandison into the fan's faces as they boo loudly.. and Winters frog splashes right onto Sandison. He crumbles to the ground.] FL: These guys are outside again--I'm getting worried.. AS: Hahahaha. FL: That's not funny! [After a brawl between the two men, Sandison stands up and rolls back in the ring with Chris Winters. He yells, and the fans give a mixed reaction--but nonetheless signalling for Sandison to jump off of the top rope with a 450 splash.] FL: THERE'S NO WAY OUT OF THAT! D'OE! AS: YES! IT'S OVER! FL: HEY!! Winters moves!! AS: WINTERS MOVES!! FL: Winters is up, he's measuring out Sandison as he rises.... AS: FREEEEZER BURN!! 1!! 2!!!!! 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Ding, ding, ding.] AS: Wow, what a grueling victory. FL: And it came outta nowhere--I've been lost for quite a whi--hey! SANDISON! BEHIND YOU! [Behind Sandison, a cocky TJ Masters puts his hand on his shoulder and turns Sandison around...nails him with a superkick! TJ picks up Sandison and hits his own BronXBomb on him! TJ grabs a mic...] TJ Masters: I told you I'd get ya Sandison. I may not be the same thug I was, but its still in me. When you cross me, you better watch your back, cause you never know when I'm gonna strike. How ya like me now Sandison?! How you like me now? Next time you wanna attack someone, think twice before you go after TJ Masters! [Masters stomps on the back of his head and spits on him] TJ Masters: This won't be the only time, this is far from over Sandison, but.... You just found out why I'm..... _SIMPLY_PERFECT_!! [Masters throws his mic down and walks back into the back] AS: TJ is sending out quite a message-- [From the crowd, Mike Douglas is seen. Chris Winters is making his way back to the locker room, but before he gets there, he is clocked not one time...NOT TWO TIMES...but three times by a steel chair, sending him back to the ringside area. Douglas takes the steel chair, and nails him with a "Magnificently Done!!"] FL: Wow. AS: STOP THIS CARNAGE!!! FL: We can't... AS: Is it over?! FL: Well, Douglas is getting something from under the ring....a shirt?! AS: It says "I Got Beat By Mike Douglas!" FL: Hahaha, we know that's not true, half of Winters' wins have been from Mike Douglas. [Douglas shows the shirt to the crowd, and lays it on Winters' chest. Douglas rolls into the ring and grabs a mic..] DOUGLAS: YOU TAKE THAT MESSAGE AND YOU BRING IT TO MORLEY!!! [Crowd Boos] DOUGLAS: You see what happened here? Chris Winters thought he was Good, Chris Winters thought he was Great, But........Chris Winters will never...... MAGNIFICENT!!!!! [Douglas rolls back out and begins stomping on Winters...until!!] AS: LOOK! LOOK! SYLKK! MORLEY! THEY'RE OUT...HERE TO HELP!! [A scared Douglas runs back into the crowd as Sylkk and Morley help out an injured friend.] AS - DOUGLAS HAS STRUCK!!! He has attacked Winters!!! FL - This isnt good one bit! AS - Folks we have to get to the ring... <---> [name withheld]!
