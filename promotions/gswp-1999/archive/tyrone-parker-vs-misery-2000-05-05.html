Tyrone Parker vs. Misery

5 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWF, Hey, Here is a match I wrote for the GSWP. It's Tyrone Parker VS. Misery in one of the two main events. Let me know what you think.
[name withheld] ################################ Handler of: ---> "Gang Related" Vinny Green [GSWP] ################################ Founder of: ---> "The E-Wrestling Mid-Year Awards" Š ################################ ========================================== - GSWP SUNDAY NIGHT IMPACT - ========================================== ------- MAIN EVENT #1 -------- "International Excellence" Tyrone Parker VS. Misery ========================================== RICK FULLER - Ladies and Gentlemen, It's time for Main event number 1. JOHNNY MARTONI - It's gonna Parker going one on one with Misery. This feud started at the PPV when Setzer Van Strife and Tyrone Parker were doing a number on Duke Putrid after he had just won the world title.
RICK FULLER - Yeah, Then out of nowhere come Misery to break up the beating and help out Duke Putrid.
JOHNNY MARTONI - Parker is not happy about that and now Misery is gonna find that out. Misery has been quiet all week, I bet he is just scared of Mr.Parker.
RICK FULLER - Yo know Johnny, It doesn't really matter what Misery says out of the ring, It's what he does in the ring that makes him who he is. He can be quiet but I believe once that bell rings, There ain't gonna be nothing quiet about Misery.
JOHNNY MARTONI - Mike Capone, If you will.... MIKE CAPONE - The following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 30 minute time limit..
[The lights go out as a spotlight comes in to effect on the curtain. The light bounces off the curtain time after time as the first couple beats to the Intro sound of DMX's "It's Dark and Hell is
Hot." The crowd begins clapping and stomping their feet in sync. The music starts off softly with each stomp getting louder and louder, adding suspense.
Finally the suspense is broken as the man known as Misery steps out from the curtain.] MIKE CAPONE - Introducing first, Standing 6 feet , 6 inches and weighing in at
285 pounds, He hails from Chicago, Illinois,
Here is.... ...... MISERY!!! [The crowd pops as Misery stops and remains focuses at the ring and his opponent. Suddenly, he begins walking to the ring surrounded by security guards. Misery wears his black boots, with his black cloak covering his wrestling attire.
the big hood covers his bald head, giving him a very cold complexion.] [Misery takes his time to the ring, psyching his opponent out. His opponent stands in the ring just watching trying not to get intimidated but finds it rather hard with the entrance of the man known as Misery, and the erie music that echoes throughout the arena.
[The security guards leave, once Misery makes it to the steps. He slowly makes his way up the steps and on to the apron where he enters through the middle ropes. He stands in the corner opposite of his opponent where he just stares as he takes off the cloak to reveal his wrestling attire.]
MIKE CAPONE - And his opponent... ["Better Than You" by Metallica starts up through the crowded GSWP arena. The crowd buzzes with boos. The theme is of "International Excellence" Tyrone
Parker. The lights glow blue, and a huge picture of Tyrone Parker's face stands in the middle of the ring....
MIKE CAPONE - Standing 6 feet , 1 inch and weighing in at 217 pounds, He hails from Aspen, Colorado, Here is.......
........."INTERNATION EXCELLENCE" TYRONE PARKER!!! Now the booing comes full tilt, as Tyrone Parker walks out from the back. Parker is wearing a Wife-Beater shirt, over black spandex like pants. The word
International, and Excellence are down each sides in yellow. His boots are of
Army origin. They have the lettering TP on the tongue in a gold, cursive font.
The booing is almost compulsive now, as Parker slowly slides on the ring apron. A sly smile spreads his face, almost begging the fans to come on in and fight. He takes his time to slide in the ring. He rips off his shirt, and grins. The music fades, and Parker awaits the bell.] RICK FULLER - All we need is the bell... [DING!] [DING!] [DING!] JOHNNY MARTONI - And there it is!! [Both men come to the center of the ring in a face to face stare down. The crowd stirs as they wait to see who makes the first move. Tyrone Parker then spits right into the eys of Misery drawing a heel reaction from the crowd.
Misery gets a looks upon his face of pure anger. Misery stands there for a second as Parker just talks trash and laughs in his face. Misery then strikes
Tyrone Parker right across the face staggering him to the corner. He climbs on top of Parker in the corner and begins to hammer away with hard right hands as the crowd begins to count along with him.]
CROWD: ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! EIGHT! NINE! [And Misery winds up for the third shot which NAILS Tyrone Parker right between the eyes. Misery jumps down and Parker quickly jumps out of the ring to take a breather.]
JOHNNY MARTONI - Parker doing the right thing now, Taking a trip to the outside to gain his composure.
RICK FULLER - Yeah, But Misery looks pissed in the ring, What was Parker thinking? He spit right in the eye of Misery.
[Parker hops back up onto the ring apron and Misery is there to greet him. Misery trys to grab Parker, But Parker drives his shoulder right into the gut of Misery which hunches Misery over. Parker filps back into the ring with a sunset flip of Misery but he can't quite get Misery over for the full roll up.
Misery grabs Parker by the throat and lifts him to his feet and throws him into the ropes, Parker rebounds and ducks a clothesline from Misery, Parker grabs
Misery from behind and nails and inverted DDT with a cover.]
REF: ONE!!! TWO!! RICK FULLER - TWO COUNT!! JOHNNY MARTONI - I saw three counts. RICK FULLER - You always see three counts. [Parker picks Misery back up to his feet and whips him into the ropes, Misery rebounds and Parker jumps into the air for a leap frog, Misery catches Parker of his way down ad nails a HARD SPINEBUSTER!! He hooks the leg.]
REF: ONE!!! TWO!!! JOHNNY MARTONI - An easy kickout for Parker! [Parker gets back to his feet but Misery is already there waiting for him. Parker turns around and catches a hard lariet fro Misery which takes Parker down to the mat. He pops back up dazed and Misery grabs Parker and sets him up for a hangman's neckbreaker, He hooks him and then Tyrone Parker uses his leg to back kick Misery right between the legs. Misery falls to the mat in pain but
Parker picks him right back up and NAILS him with a hard brainbuster. Parker covers.]
REF: ONE!!! TWO!!!! RICK FULLER - KICKOUT!! Only a two!! [Parker picks Misery back up and throws him into the corner, He follows him over and irish whips him over into the other corner. Parker runs at Misery and leaps in the air for a big splash , But MISERY MOVED!! Parker hits the turnbuckle hard and is stunned, Misery quickly grabs Parker and whips him into the ropes, Parker is sent into the ropes but Parker grabs the ropes as he hits then and holds onto them. Misery runs at Parker.]
RICK FULLER - MISERY LEAPS!! LOW BRIDGE BY PARKER!! PARKER JUST PULLED THE TOP ROPE DOWN AND MISERY HIT THE CONCRETE HARD!! JOHNNY MARTONI - Parker is the smartest man alive. [Tyrone Parker once again gives off that cocky smile and he and the referee head to the outside of the ring where Misery lays. Parker picks up Misery and whips him HARD into the steel ring steps.]
** CRASH ** [The stairs are sent flying into the air from the impact and Misery screams in pain on the ground. Parker doesn't waste any time and he walks over and drags
Misery to his feet, He picks him up in position over his shoulder for and powerslam and he swings him around.]
RICK FULLER - MISERY'S LEG JUST SMACKED THE REFEREE IN THE FACE!! AND THE REFEREE IS OUT OF IT!!! JOHNNY MARTONI - DAMNIT, DQ MISERY!! RICK FULLER - Johnny, We both know it was an accident but Parker doesn't seem to care. [Parker notices the referee knocked out on the ground and he throws Misery off of his shoulder and drops him throat first across the steel guard rail. Parker walks over to the ringside announcers table and he throws ring announcer Mike
Capone out of his chair and brings the chair over by Misery.]
RICK FULLER - Parker's got a steel chair!! This isn't right!! Somebody stop him!! JOHNNY MARTONI - TOO LATE!! ** CRASH ** JOHNNY MARTONI - STEEL CHAIR TO THE HEAD OF MISERY!! ** CRASH ** JOHNNY MARTONI - AND THERE'S A CHAIR SHOT TO THE LEG!!! ** CRASH ** JOHNNY MARTONI - HOW ABOUT ONE TO MISERY'S ARM, CAUSE THAT'S EXACTLY WHAT HE GOT!! RICK FULLER - PARKER IS DESTROYING MISERY!!! [Tyrone Parker throws the chair down and rolls Misery back into the ring, He grabs the referee and slides him back into the ring. Parker climbs in the ring and covers Misery. The referee comes to his senses.]
REF: ONE!!! TWO!!! RICK FULLER - SHOULDER UP!! [Parker gets an angry look over his face and he picks Misery back up to his feet. He sets him up and plants Misery with a Piledriver. Parker heads to the top rope and perches himself up there. He leaps.]
RICK FULLER - HUGE FROG SPLASH!! THE COVER.. REF: ONE!!! TWO!!! JOHNNY MARTONI - ONLY A TWO COUNT!!! RICK FULLER - Misery had got to be hurting, He took a chair shot to the head, leg and arm. He's got nothing left.
[Parker once again gets an angry look on his face and he picks Misery back up. He whips Misery into the ropes.] RICK FULLER - Hiptoss?....NO!! MISERY TURNED IT INTO A HARD DDT!!! HE HOOKS PARKER'S LEG!! REF: ONE!!! TWO!!! JOHNNY MARTONI - PARKER KICKS OUT!! [Both men get back to their feet at the same time and Misery swings at Tyrone
Parker, But he misses and he is spun around with his back facing Parker, Parker picks him up for a belly to back suplex, He gets him up....NO!! Misery turns it into a bulldog. Misery heads to the top rope and Parker gets to his feet holding his face. Parker is up and Misery is perched on top.]
RICK FULLER - TOP ROPE CLOTHESLINE FROM MISERY!!! INTO A COVER!! REF: ONE!!! TWO!!!!! JOHNNY MARTONI - SHOULDER UP!! PARKER KICKS OUT AGAIN!!! [Misery picks Parker back up to his feet and puts him in position for a powerbomb, BUT!! Parker takes Misery legs out from underneath him and hooks hi in a boston crab.]
JOHNNY MARTONI - Misery is done, Stick a fork in him. RICK FULLER - Parker does have it locked in and Misery's leg has to be hurting. [Just as that is said Misery uses his legs to spring Tyrone Parker off of him and send him face first into the turnbuckle. Parker stumbles backwards and
Misery rolls him up.]
REF: ONE!!!! TWO!!!! RICK FULLER - PARKER POWERS OUT OF IT!! BUT THAT WAS CLOSE!! [Both men get back up and Parker drop toe holds Misery face first into the middle turnbuckle. Misery gets up staggered and Parker grabs Misery and uses the ropes to NAIL a tornado DDT with a cover.]
REF: ONE!!! TWO!!! JOHNNY MARTONI - Only a two count!! RICK FULLER - We are seeing a great match here in our first main event. [Parker drags Misery back up to his feet and whips him into the corner, Misery smacks it hard and rebounds to the center of the ring dazed, Parker taunts
Misery while he stands there dazed.]
RICK FULLER - PARKER TAUNTING MISERY!!! Parker picks Misery up over his shoulder BUT!! Misery falls off behind Parker. Misery spins Parker around and.....HEARTPUNCH!!! MISERY CALLS THAT "THE FORGOTTEN SOUL"! THE COUNT!!
REF: ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!! RICK FULLER - NO!! NO!! NO!! PARKER'S FOT WAS ON THE BOTTOM ROPE THE WHOLE TIME!!! MISERY IS PISSED!! [Misery picks Parker back up to his feet and places him on the tope rope, he follows him up. As he does so, Someone begins to stalk down the aisle.]
RICK FULLER - THAT'S VAN STRIFE!! SETZER VAN STRIFE!! WHAT THE HELL IS HE DOING OUT HERE??!!! JOHNNY MARTONI - Maybe he wants to watch the match. [Misery sees Parker and begins yelling at him from the top rope.] RICK FULLER - PARKER WITH A LOW BLOW!! MISERY FALLS OFF THE TOP ROPE!! PARKER ON TOP!!! LEGDROP FROM THE TOP ROPE!! THE COVER!! REF: ONE!!! TWO!!! RICK FULLER - NO!! NO!! SHOULDER UP!!! [Parker pulls Misery up and he looks over to ringside where Setzer Van Strife is now standing. He points to him with a cocky look and then drops Misery with a powerbomb. He picks Misery back up and places him on the top rope. He follows him up.]
RICK FULLER - This could be the Superstar Driver 2000!! That German suplex from the top rope which he rolls over into a camel cutch! Parker has him hooked!!
JOHNNY MARTONI - GERMAN SUPLEX!!! MISERY SHIFTS HIS WEIGHT IN MID AIR AND HE LANDS ON TOP OF PARKER!!! THE COVER!!! REF: ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!! RICK FULLER - NO!! NO!! NO!! FOOT ON THE ROPE!! [Misery gets up once again pissed off and gets an angry look on his face. Tyrone Parker crawls over to the corner of the ring and grabs the referee by his shirt and he begins explaining to him that his leg is hurt.]
RICK FULLER - PARKER COULD BE HURT!! WAIT!! VAN STRIFE IS IN THE RING BEHIND MISERY!!! THE REFEREE IS PRE OCCUIED!! [Van Strife pulls a pair of Brass Knuckles out from his pants, He place them on his hand.] RICK FULLER - NO!! NO!! ** SMACK ** JOHNNY MARTONI - YES!! YES!! BRASS KNUCKLES TO THE HEAD OF MISERY!! RICK FULLER - VAN STRIFE IS OUT OF THE RING AND LOOK AT PARKER!! HIS LEG IS FINE IT WAS A SET UP!! [Tyrone Parker walks over to Misery who is out cold. He picks him up and places him on the top rope, He follows him up.]
RICK FULLER - SUPERSTAR DRIVER!!! TOP ROPE TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER!! THE COVER!!! REF: ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!! [DING!] [DING!] DING!] MIKE CAPONE - Ladies and Gentelmen here is you winner at 23:46...... ........."INTERNATIONAL EXCELLENCE" TYRONE PARKER!!!
