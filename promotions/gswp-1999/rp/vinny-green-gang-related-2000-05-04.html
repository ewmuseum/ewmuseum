Vinny Green, 4 May 2000

4 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWF, Hey, I haven't posted on here in a long time and I haven't posted an RP on here in a really long time, So here you go. This is my character "Gang
Related" Vinny Green who currently competes in GSWP. Let me know what you think.
[name withheld] ################################ Handler of: ---> "Gang Related" Vinny Green [GSWP] ################################ Founder of: ---> "The E-Wrestling Mid-Year Awards" Š ################################ [Fade in.] [The scene opens up inside a large apartment located in Brooklyn, New York. The most visible things in the apartment are a large leather recliner and a big screen TV, Although the apartment is decorated with much, much more. The camera pans around the apartment and comes to a pine door, We do not know where this door leads to.
The camera gets closer to the door and it can pick up something coming from inside this room. The voice of Vinny Green is heard talking to someone on the phone.]
VINNY GREEN: Hey buddy, How ya' doin? Yeah, It's Big Vin'. [Pause as Vinny listens to the receiver.] VINNY GREEN: Yes, Vinny Green. Do you know any other Big Vin'? Anyway listen, I need a favor. I have this guy who had been giving me a little trouble and I need some place to put him, You know for a little while.
[Pause.] VINNY GREEN: You want the story? Look man, I'll tell you later. Just tell me, Can I stick him at your place?
[Pause.] VINNY GREEN: Alright cool. And by the way, Say hello to your _NEW_ GSWP Television Champion.
[Another pause while Vinny listens to the receiver.] VINNY GREEN: What the hell do ya' mean...WHO? ME. I'm 7 feet tall, 360 pounds and I just added 10 more pounds of television title to that. [Pause.] VINNY GREEN: NO!! We can't sell the belt. I mean sure we'd get a lot for it but this belt shows everyone that
I am the _MAN_. And plus, The ladies love is and it goes kinda good with my outfit. Anyway, I've gotta go do a little working out at the gym. I mean, I have to keep this muscular physique of mine. Thanks man, You'll get the "delivery" later.
[The phone is heard being hung up. Then moments later, "Gang Related" Vinny Green swings the door open and walks out. He see's the camera and gets a shocked look on his face. Vinny is wearing a pair of blue jeans with a black sleeveless shirt.
His skin tone is a light white skin and his head is shaved completly bald to a shine. The 7 footer speaks.]
VINNY GREEN: How long have you been here? CAMERA MAN(Panic): Uh, I just got here like 2 seconds ago. VINNY GREEN: Yeah, Well your lucky. You know, Just because GSWP made me give them my spare key so they can get the so-called, "Inside Edge" on pro wrestling,
Doesn't mean that you can barge in here any time. You know, I have ladies over here all the time and it would go _WAY_ beyond that _MATURE_ rating you put on your programs if you were to catch me, "In the act".
So from now on, Give me a little ring whenever you wanna hear the sensation that is Vinny Green speak.
[Pause.] VINNY GREEN: But since your here, I guess I'll have to let everyone know whats on my mind and what is _NOW_ on my shoulder.
[Vinny walks over to a large dresser located in the room and opens up one of the draws, He reaches in and pulls out,
The GSWP Television title. The title shines bright as it has been newly restored. Vinny puts it over his shoulder and walks over to the large leather recliner. He sits down and props his feet up on a small cofee table. He continues.]
VINNY GREEN: Ah yes, The GSWP Television title. Granted, It's no world title but it is _DEFINATLY_ a milestone in the wrestling career of Vinny Green. A career which has not been going on for long, but already it contains the word "Champion" in it.
I outlasted 9 other guys in what was, In my opinion, A laugher. It wasn't even a challenge. I had the size and the smarts and that's basically all it takes to succeed in this buisnees. The proof is over my shoulder right now as we speak.
[The camera zooms in on the title belt as Vinny Green pats it approvingly.] VINNY GREEN: Even a masked luchadore which later revealed himself to be Terrance Goldsworthy could not stop, Hurricane
Vinny from tearing up New Jersey. By the way, Has anyone seen Goldsworthy? I mean sure, You all saw me hit him with me car and throw him in my trunk last week on Impact but that was just an old game we always played back in the old days. You know,
First person to hit the other with their car and throw him in the trunk wins? Haven't you guys ever made up games when you were kids? Well, Terrance and I did the same. [Vinny gives the camera man a look of disbelief.] VINNY GREEN: WHAT!? YOU DON"T BELIEVE ME!? Listen buddy, If you think that I "kidnapped" Terrance Goldsworthy or something sick like that then you are sadly mistaken. I was just reminicing about old times when we rolled together up until Terrance couldn't hang with me anymore.
You know, He went "Soft". I've explained the whole story already. Listen, I'm not that bad of a guy. I can be nice. You wanna hear a story where Vinny Green is nice just listen to this.
[Pause.] VINNY GREEN: One day, I was walking down the street in my hometown of Brooklyn, New York. It was an average day for me. A few sex invites, A couple of offers, Like I said an average day.
So all of a sudden, I see this lady walking in front of me. Her age could range from anywhere from 50 up to about 95 but that's not the point. The point is, This lady drops her purse.
Right in front of me, And then she keeps walking. I quickly picked up the purse and ran after this lady. She wasn't too much of a runner because of the little age thing and I caught up to her rather quickly.
I took that purse and I handed it right to the lady. The look on her wrinkled face was priceless, A wide smile and a thank you and she was on her way. Boy that made me feel go. Of course, Little did she know..........
...I TOOK ALL HER DAMN MONEY OUT BEFORE I GAVE IT TO HER!! [Vinny breaks out in a histerical laugh.] VINNY GREEN(Laughing): HA!HA! Man, I love that story. She just kept walking along, No clue at _ALL_. I bet when she reached down in that purse to get her glasses, Well.....
.........I TOOK THEM TOO!!!! [Vinny laughs histerically for a few more moments and then continues.] VINNY GREEN: But let's get back to buisness now. Just about an hour ago, I got a little fax from Eric Cooper with my opponent and the first opponent to challenge me for the TV title. Out of all the names of the GSWP roster,
Why Rick Stevens, Eric? Has he proved anything to me or you? No. Has he even beat _ANYONE_ since coming to GSWP? No. So I really don't see why he has been placed in this match.
I mean, Throw me someone in which I can actually gain something by beating them. I'm not gonna gain anything by beating Rick
Stevens. I certainly won't lose anything, But I'm always looking for a gain. [Pause.] VINNY GREEN: I mean anything Eric Cooper, An established star, A young talent. Rick Stevens does not fit either criteria. Oh well, I'll just beat the hell out of him, Pick up the win and move onto bigger and better things.
Hey, Who knows? It may even be fun. Throwing around Rick Stevens for a little while. Trying out some new moves on him to use in the upcoming weeks. Actually, This may not be that bad.
So Rick, Don't even put on your wrestling attire come Sunday Night. Because this will _NOT_ be a wrestling match, It will be a one man show. And being that I am the _ONLY_ man who will be in the ring you can just assume that the show will be run by _ME_.
[Vinny stands up and the GSWP TV title and holds it in front of the camera.] VINNY GREEN: You see this? [Points to the belt.] VINNY GREEN: All you guys sitting at home right now watching this, Take a look. I may be mean, I may be a somewhat cold individual. But damnit, I _AM_ the NEW GSWP TV CHAMPION!!! And
I am taking on _ALL_ the competition. That's right, You want this belt? Step up and stand toe to toe with Vinny Green and T
RY TO TAKE IT FROM ME!!! Some people say things like, "Nobody can hold the TV title for more than a few weeks." And, "They can't defend the belt every week and not get worn out." Well, Let me tell you something, I am the man that will prove those statements wrong and I am the man who _IS_.....
........THE FUTURE OF PRO WRESTLING!!! [Vinny looks at the camera with an angry look.] VINNY GREEN: I'm big, I'm mean, and DAMNIT!! I'm Vinny
Green. And I have just laid down my prophecy to the world. And the world better listen to me.
[Vinny begins to walk back towards that room he came out from at the beginnning of this segment. He stops and looks back.]
VINNY GREEN: Oh yeah and by the way, If you see Terrance
Goldsworthy. Tell him.......??? [Pause.] VINNY GREEN: FORGET IT!! YOU WON'T SEE HIM!!! [Vinny begins to laugh histerically and he walks back into that room and slams the door.]
