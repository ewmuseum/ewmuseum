GSWP Roleplay Archive

Dustin Carwile, 14 May 2000

14 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We cut to a shot of "Delicious" Dustin Carwile, whose sitting at his home in the better parts of Memphis, Tennessee. He watches the television set, as "Flatliner" Duke Putrid emerges on the screen...] >On the man's shoulder rests the GSWP Heavyweight Championship "Polish it up, Duke... nice and shiney. I want it to look better than ever when I take it from ya' in just a matter of weeks." >GSWP World Champ >'Flatlander' Duke Putrid "GSWP World Champion, eh?" [Flashes a grin of conceit...] "You're on borrowed time, Duke... borrowed time." >To be proud, a man must accomplish something that he doesn't expect, >something that no one expects from him. "Duke, you'll _never_ beat me, so don't even think about it, boy." >This is why I need your help, RSP-WF. "I assure you... you'll need all the help you can get." >I've been trying to lose weight for years; it gets me basically nowhere. [Carwile looks dumbfounded, as he watches and listens in...] >To this day I stand 6'5", and weigh 356 pounds, but I'm not that big inside. >Inside, I'm... > > > > A Cruiseweight." "A cruiserweight!? Duke Putrid!?" [Carwile laughs histerically...] >Dp: "You see, inside my head, I've always been a luchadore, I've been >wanting >to do huracanranas in my heart ever since I was convicted of assault. "I thought this was gonna be about _beating_ me... but instead... ...it's about _being_ me!" [Continues to laugh...] >But all of this, ALL of this, would be different, if only I could perform >one twisting moonsault into a flying headscissors takedown. I could be a >complete man if I could only do an Asai flip into a rolling reverse neck >snap. "Duke, not only will you never _beat_ me, but you'll damn sure never _be_ me!" [Now, mocking Duke...] "I'm too fat... I wanna be more like 'The Delicious One'! Help me RSPWF! HELP!" [Still laughing...] >But first, I need to lose one hundred seven pounds." "BWA, HA, HA, HA!!!" [Almost crying now...] >Help the 'Flatlander' take to the sky. "TAKE... TAKE... TO THE SKY!?" [Carwile laughs, as he throws a cernal of popcorn in his mouth. He continues to laugh... until he begins choking on the cernal...] >How, you ask? Just simply put my name down on your ballot for the >RSP-WF Cruiserweight Poll.. help old Duke get on there. Help me realize my >dream. > > RSP-WF, please..." [Carwile hacks the popcorn up, finally...] "Duke, you ain't no cruiserweight. And you'll never be. No, Duke... you'll _never_ be like me. And when you finally decide to stop hiding behind Cooper's dress... that title you're holdin' will finally be _mine!_" [Carwile turns his head to the camera...] "RSPWF... don't vote for a coward of a Champion like Duke Putrid. Don't vote for a wanna-be, spineless jellyfish like him. If you wanna vote for a _real_ cruiserweight, with some _real_ talent... ...you'll vote for "Delicious" Dustin Carwile." [Carwile winks to the camera and puts a thumbs-up...] "Vote Carwile in 2000." [Camera fades out...] Dustin Carwile - Former handler of "Dangerous" Dusty Love - Currently handles "Delicious" Dustin Carwile GSWP [2-0-0] "Ya know what my new hobby is these days? Rapin' deaf girls then breakin their fingers." - [name withheld]
