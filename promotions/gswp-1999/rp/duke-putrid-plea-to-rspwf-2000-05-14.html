Duke Putrid, 14 May 2000

14 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The following is an open message to RSP-WF, paid for by 'Flatlander' Duke Putrid: [The cap is slowly unscrewed from the camera, bringing in a blinding quantity of sunlight. There is a solitary man standing by a small swingset made of peeling paint and cracked wood. The man looks around him and sees a few standing structures, but mainly piles of once smoldering wreckage that has been washed over by years of rain.
On the man's shoulder rests the GSWP Heavyweight Championship; but he pays no attention to it. His quiet head is bowed in thought, presenting only a thin layer of salt and pepper stuble. The man is wearing some old jeans and a flannel over shirt, and has on a pair of snakeskin boots.
The mammoth black man looks up into the camera, revealing his identity... he is a 35 year old alcoholic wrestler/ex-convict who is struggling to get by.. this man is from Devil's Tower, Wyoming... GSWP World Champ
'Flatlander' Duke Putrid. Duke wipes his face a bit and looks into the camera with pleading eyes before speaking in a rich, Western barritone.]
Dp: "All my life, I've never really been proud of anything. I quit drinking, sure, but that took years... I survived the Wyoming state correctional facility for charges of assault... I've won the GSWP World Title.
But these things were all expected of me, in a way. To be proud, a man must accomplish something that he doesn't expect, something that no one expects from him. To be proud, a man must succeed.
This is why I need your help, RSP-WF. I've been trying to lose weight for years; it gets me basically nowhere. To this day I stand 6'5", and weigh 356 pounds, but I'm not that big inside. Inside, I'm... A Cruiseweight." [Duke smiles for a second at this dreamy thought before he continues.] Dp: "You see, inside my head, I've always been a luchadore, I've been wanting to do huracanranas in my heart ever since I was convicted of assault. Some would say I've led a hard life...
My father left before I was born... My mother left me in the care of my uncle Ray at age eleven, when she headed out to Oregon for a job...
My uncle left me his ranch, where I got successful... It was burnt down by a small group of cowardly racists who did all but sign their names... The minute I touch them without proof, I go to jail for three years... But all of this, ALL of this, would be different, if only I could perform one twisting moonsault into a flying headscissors takedown. I could be a complete man if I could only do an Asai flip into a rolling reverse neck snap.
Only if. But first, I need to lose one hundred seven pounds." [Duke lifts his arms and surveys his body, shaking his head at the unlikeliness of it.] Dp: "I CAN DO IT, though. All I need is the right motivation. Come on, RSP-WF, help an old man out with his lifelong dream. Help the 'Flatlander' take to the sky. All you have to do is give me the motivation, and I can take it from there. How, you ask? Just simply put my name down on your ballot for the
RSP-WF Cruiserweight Poll.. help old Duke get on there. Help me realize my dream.
RSP-WF, please..." [Duke cracks a hopeful grin as he stares into the lens.] Dp: "Make my dream come true?" [Fade out..] The preceeding plea was a 'Flatlander' Duke Putrid production.
