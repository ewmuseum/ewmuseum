Ava Nichole

20 July 2012

Ava Nichole (Miranda Lukas) is a Former General Manager of Wednesday Night Catfight, Former General Manager of Storm, and Current Vixen Administrator. Starting her career as a valet for Jupiter, Ava proved valets could get down when interfering in Jupiter's match causing him to pick up wins. After success with Jupiter, the duo split with Jupiter falling to the bottom and Ava rising to greater things. Friday Night Storm: Soon after breaking things off with Jupiter, Ava recieved word that then GM Madman would be quitting his job as GM. Without thinking Ava filed for the job and within days, Ava had the job. Running a tight ship, Ava wasn't shy to step to the roster addressing their mistakes. With excellent shows each week, Ava found herself with Hall of Famer Ice . The two were very flirtatious with one another. Pregnancy: While dating Ice, Ava found herself pregnant with the duos baby. Keeping it to herself, Ava realized things needed to be addresed before things got out of hand, in process almost getting Ice fired. Taking a minor break from the show, Soon Ava left Storm to fulfill other things. Wednesday Night Catfight/Feud with Lashay Monay and CWF: Soon after leaving Storm, News arosed that Ava and The Obw Board would be bringing an All-Vixen brand titling it Catfight. Pissed, Ava's sister Lashay Monay revealed that Ava Nichole had an abortion and the baby was really by Jerry Lightning . While running the show perfectly, Ava had things to do and with her absence Lashay called the shots. Firing Obw Vixens Cookie and Bubbles , Lashay introduced her own brand CWF and the CWF Girls to take over the show. When word reached Ava she returned with vengence. Un-firing The Party Girls , Ava challenged Lashay and The CWF Girls to a series of matches with Ava's team on the winning side. After success with the show, it was announced Wednesday Night Catfight was on it's last days before finally ending June 27, 2012. She is currently the Administrator of Obw Vixens.
