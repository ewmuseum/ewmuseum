Info Real Name | Ryan Kineph Fenix III DOB | August 23,1991(Age 23) Originally From: | Calgary,Alberta,Canada Now Residing in: | Chicago,IL Ring Name(s) | Ty Sebastian | King Alpha | Drag Alpha | Ryan Tesla Family | Ryan Fenix Jr.(deceased)Father | Selena Fenix(deceased)Mother | Steven Fenix(Brother) | Neville Fenix(brother) | Carly Fenix(deceased)Sister Professions | Musician (2006-2010) | Stuntman(2010-2011) | Professional Wrestler(2011-Present) Overview Ryan Kineph Fenix III is a professional wrestler signed to OBW who now fights under the name Ryan Tesla.He is also the son of the biggest wrestling booker in Canada,RJ Fenix. Childhood Born August 23,1991,Tesla(AKA Fenix) was born to Selena Fenix(Maiden name:Selena Bryant) and RJ Fenix(Ryan Fenix Jr.).Ryan was really bright as a child.His full potential was not reached in school because of violent tendencies he had.This was due to the untimely death of his father when he was 8.The constant abuse from his step-father Adam Richards,lead him to bullying and teasing his fellow students.His step-father constantly scarred him,bruises on his wrist that have never healed and were covered by wearing long-sleeve shirts.At the age of 12,Fenix thought enough was enough,and got his stepfather taken away by Child Protective Services.For the rest of his childhood,he was happy.The bruises were gone and his grades in school shot up. Career Paths Ryans path of careers started when he was 15.It was then he would lead The R.I.P.s as lead singer/guitarist .He made many amateur albums in his time with the band.In 2010,the band broke up.Fenix pursued a career being a stuntman that same year.He performed a total of 57 stunts.Each one was done to absolute perfection with absolutely no incidents.Through that,he still wanted to follow his childhood passion and become a professional wrestler.He signed with Calgary Online Wrestling (His fathers company)in 2011 by the name of Ty Sebastian.He stayed there for 3 years until the company was shut down on July 31,2014.It didn't take long for him to get back into the wrestling game.On August 3,2014,he was signed to OBW. OBW Career In such a short time,Fenix has made a real impact in OBW.Beating 5x OBW champion,Super Krmi,in his debut match and starting stable called The Velocity (RPM,Martin Drawback,Trabcon,and Essa Randoss).Both Friday Night Storm General Manger,Chad Bowens,and former wrestler and commentator,John Evergreen,believe that this man is bound for greatness.
