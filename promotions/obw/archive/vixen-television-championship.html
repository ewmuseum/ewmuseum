It is secondary contested for in OBW's women's division (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TNA_Knockout). It was debuted on February 28, 2012 at OBW's Big Shot pay-per-view (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pay-per-view) (PPV). Like most professional wrestling championships, the title is won via the result of a scripted match. Overall, there have been 6 reigns shared between 5 wrestlers. The first champion was Kitten . The current champion is Leda , who is in her first reign.
