OBW Vixen Tag Team Championships

4 September 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Vixen Tag Team Championships are belts for Vixen Tag Teams. Holders: Alexa Robbinson & Nikki Nitro: July 2, 2011-July 15, 2011 [13 DAYS]; (Friday Night Storm) defeated Sabrina & The Cheerleader. Allison Kim & Nikki Nitro(2): July 15, 2011-September 9, 2011 [57 DAYS]; (Friday Night Storm) defeated Alexa Robbinson & Jayla Brooks. Lieutenant Fyre & Tiffany: September 9, 2011-November 29,2011 [81 DAYS]; (Friday Night Storm) defeated The Cheerleader & Sabrina, Allison Kim & Nikki Nitro in a 3 Way Tag. Ashanti & Paisley: November 29,2011-December 30,2011[31 DAYS]; (Friday Night Storm) defeated Lt. Fyre & Tiffany. Rosa Chiahuahua & Big Bertha : December 30-February 1,2012 [33 DAYS] ; (Xtreme X-Mas 2011) defeated Ashanti & Paisley. Ashanti (2) & Paisley (2):February 1,2012-March 30, 2012 [59 DAYS]; (Wednesday Night Catfight) defeated Rosa Chihuahua & Big Bertha. Cassie James & Ashley James: March 30,2012-April 4, 2012 [5 DAYS]; (Heart of Wrestling 2012) defeated Ashanti & Paisley. Ashanti (3) & Paisley (3): April 4, 2012-April 30,2012 [26 DAYS]; (Wednesday Night Catfight) defeated Cassie James & Ashley James. Layla Lace & Kayla Lace: April 30, 2012-August 30 2012; [122 DAYS] (Rebirth 2012) defeated Ashanti & Paisley and Cassie James & Ashley James in a 3 Way Tag Match. Snickers & Jade Mystique : August 30, 2012-present; (Violent Voting) rewarded after Twins were fired.
