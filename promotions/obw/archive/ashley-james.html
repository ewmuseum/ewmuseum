Ashley James

19 July 2012

Ashley James (Ashleigh Miller) was born on 18th November 1993 in Austin, Texas. She has an older sister who is also fellow OBW Vixen, Cassie James. She is currently an OBW Vixen signed to both brands Execution and Storm. Ashley's Career: Ashley first debuted on the 24th episode of Monday Night Execution, where she first appeared backstage arguing with OBW Vixens Essence Flawless and Brooke. Ashley then had her first match against Essence Flawless and had won by pinfall. Later, she had been in Pay-Per-Views such as Last Man Standing, Xtreme Xmas and Survival, but was not succesful. In early January 2012, it was heard that Vixen's Administrator Ava Nichole was opening an all-vixen brand "Wednesday Night Catfight". Ashley and her sister then formed a duo called "Sugar&Spice". The James Sisters were both given such opportunities to grab the Vixens Tag Team Championship. They have been in a feud with former three-time Vixens Tag Team Champions, Paisley and Ashanti. They were booked in a match in Big Shot against their two opponents but were brutally attacked in the ring leaving them title-less but the sisters didn't give up on getting their gold. Succesfully, they were in a match against Ashanti and Paisley in Heart Of Wrestling and left victorious. Five days later, they lost their titles to the same opponents on Catfight, making a short title reign. Then came their new opponents, The Lace Twins. The three tag teams have been feuding for weeks and put in a match in Rebirth, ending up with newcomers The Lace Twins. Ashley and Cassie were dissapointed but still kept moving forward. Sugar&Spice had been in several matches against different vixens after their loss, leaving either victoriously or laid out in the ring. Then, Vixen Administrator Ava had put all vixens in seperate brands as Ashley and Cassie were drafted to Execution. Though Ashley had some wins and loses, she kept on trying her best to be a champion. Before wrestling Ashley had always been a fan of professional wrestling since the age of eight. She had developed an interest in cheerleading and kickboxing as well. She had taken classes in gymnastics and loved playing other sports like volleyball, baseball and netball. But one thing for sure, she had always kept her strong passion for wrestling.
