Ring Name : Manny Fresh Billed height : 6 ft 5 in (1.96 m) Billed weight : 235 lb (106 kg) Born : | April 5 | New York City, NY Resides : Los Angeles, CA Billed from : New York City (OBW) Manny Fresh (born April 5) is an American professional wrestler and actor. He is signed to OBW as a member of its Storm brand. In Obw, Fresh has became a very well known and respected worker. Fresh has competed in so far 9 OBW PPVs and has been in 4 world title matches since his debut. Fresh has only been in one faction, he created. which he named "Genesis", who included OBW's Chad Bowens and former OBW wrestler Roshandala Babcock. Bowens and Babcock have won the tag team titles while in Genesis. Fresh is currently trying to capture the world championship. Early Life Manny Fresh was born on the 5th of April, in New York, New York. Fresh grew up learning Martial arts, he spent a couple of years studying and practicing the arts until he earned the black belt. Before pursing his dream of becoming a wrestler, Fresh was acting in short film roles, while doing male modeling on the side. Fresh worked for many modeling agencies and had many movie roles, he was known for being in Subways "Eat Fresh" commercials. Professional wrestling career (OBW 2011) On Sep 7, 2011. Fresh was training to become a OBW wrestler, he suddenly got a call from then Storm general manager, Ava Nichole. The call led to Manny signing up to be on the Storm roster. Coming Soon.
