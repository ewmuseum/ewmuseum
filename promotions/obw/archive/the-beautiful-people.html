The Beautiful People Members | Leda | Angelina Name(s) | Angelina-Leda Entertainment | The Beautiful People Formore Member(s) | The Body Guard (2009) Debut | March 5, 2008 Promotions | DSRW | OBW The Beautiful People is an alliance of professional wrestlers that is best known for its appearances in Online Brutal Wrestling (OBW) , currently consisting of Leda and Angelina, formerly counting The Body Guard as a member. The stable is known for their narcissistic gimmick. Their characters (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_professional_wrestling_terms#Gimmick) are portrayed as arrogant blonde barbie dolls, whose main goal as a team is to "cleanse" the Online Brutal Wrestling roster "one ugly person at a time", based on their belief that their physical appearance is superior to all others. Concept The Beautiful People were vain (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanity) and narcissistic prima donnas (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prima_donna). After a brief initial period without these traits, they became villainous characters who emphasized an interest in looks and created the motto "cleansing the world, one ugly person at a time." Their fixation with physical beauty is emphasized when they use cosmetics (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmetics) and hair spray (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hair_spray) as weapons. They also favored placing paper bags over the heads of opponents they regarded as ugly, as a way of humiliating them. History Angelina-Leda Entertainment (2008) Angelina and Leda made their DSRW debuts as fan favorites (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_(professional_wrestling)). At a pay-per-view in 2008,Leda and Angelina were placed into a tag team named Angelina-Leda Entertainment.).  On an edition of DSRW, a roster member resisted their attempt at a makeover, offending Leda and Angelina, causing them to react violently. Angelina and Leda retaliated against the resistance by using the makeover to hurt, rather than help the roster member. During the attack they utilized their cosmetics to humiliate him by spraying him in the eyes with hairspray and smearing makeup all over his face, thus becoming villains (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heel_(professional_wrestling)) The Beautiful People (2009, 2012-) Adopting the personas of superficial prima donnas (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prima_donna), who loathe and insult anyone they consider to be physically unattractive (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_attractiveness), they dropped the Angelina-Leda Entertainment moniker and became The Beautiful People. They where a team until DSRW became defunct. The Beautiful People returned in 2012 to OBW. Incarnations There have been different incarnations of The Beautiful People.
