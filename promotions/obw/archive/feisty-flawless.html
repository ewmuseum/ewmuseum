Feisty Flawless

Feisty Flawless (Elizabeth Mendez) is an Obw Vixen, Former Vixen International Champion , and 2011 Hottest Vixen Award winner. Feisty first came to Obw as a Party Animal Vixen. Clearly one of Obw's sexiest ever, Feisty didn't have to do much before men drooled over her. Although a pretty face, Feisty began a rivalry with fellow vixen Kitten . While feuding with Kitten, Feisty join Team Vixen . Career: Soon after joining Team Vixen, Feisty became more noticed for her in-ring skills. The Team became one of Obw's Top ever stable. While still a group, Team Vixen found themselves up against Killer K . Feisty dteremined to see this through till the end, the two teams did battle with both sides getting wins. After much success, Team Vixen went their seperate ways. After Team Vixen, Feisty continued to perform in-ring with moderate success. Feisty soon found herself dating then boyfriend Chad Bowens . With moderate success as a couple, Chad began becoming verbally abusive towards her and restraining her from her own goals. Blinded by love, Feisty finally ended things with Bowens. Afterwards, Feisty alongside other Vixens were moved to Wednesday Night Catfight . On Catfight, Feisty became a mid-carder with less activity in-ring wise. Although determined not to fall behind, Feisty won her first title, Vixen International Championship on April 4, 2012. Retirement/Comeback: On April 21, 2012 Feisty retired (17 days after wiing the championship) vacating her title in the process. On the 17th episode of Catfight, Feisty made her on screen return to attend Jade Mystique and DJ Ando 's wedding. Feisty has since began competing again.
