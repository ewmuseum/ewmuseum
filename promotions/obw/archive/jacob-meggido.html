Jacob Meggido

20 July 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jacob Meggido is current General Manager of Friday Night Storm. Life History Jacob Meggido was born on January 25th, 1980. It is unconfirmed where his hometown is. He started in the wrestling industry at the age of 18, were he was a young, business suited Manager. He managed a lot of indie wrestling prospect in the local area (names such as K-Rok, Boston Behemoth, and Lord Zoltan. None of which took off.) He then found Mike Solomon at a young age. Managing Solomon Meggido became the manager for Mike Solomon as soon as he started to wrestler. He is the key reason why Solomon made it into OBW. However, Meggido refused to work with Solomon until he changed his gimmick. He felt Solomon was too bland, and he needed something more feirce. He gave the man a mask and helped him dawn the monicker of Armageddon. Managing Armageddon First playing behind the scenes, Meggido got Armageddon a spot as Jack Fender's bodyguard. Though shortlived, it helped Armageddon get his name out there. Meggido then began to play a much bigger role, leading his client to become Intercontinental champion. Though, this too was short lived. Armageddon was injured and Meggido was left without a client. Becoming Storm General Manager After he lost his client, Meggido became General Manager of Storm. He brought ratings up to the 1.2 million view mark, a feat Storm had been having trouble doing in the time before he stepped in. His first order of business was to try and ruin the man that injured his star, Sean Shade. He failed to do so however, and eventually the storyline fizzled out. He hired an assistant, Miss Isabelle, to help him run the show. She played more of a support character however, as not much was done with her. His most recent work is a feud with Jack 'Jackal' Fender. In said feud, it is apparent that Mr. Meggido is trying to push Jackal out of his job. And jackal is trying to do the same.
