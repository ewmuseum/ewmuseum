OBW Vixen World Championship

1 June 2013

The OBW Vixen's World Championship is a professional wrestling (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling) women's (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling#Women.27s_wrestling) world championship (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Championship_(professional_wrestling)) owned by the Online Brutal Wrestling (OBW) promotion (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_wrestling_promotion). It is main contested for in OBW's women's division (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TNA_Knockout). It was debuted on February 1, 2012 on the debut of Wednesday Night Catfight. Like most professional wrestling championships, the title is won via the result of a scripted match. Overall, there have been 10 reigns. The first champion was Cream Stylez . The current champion is Ayden Starr-Malice , who is in her second reign.
