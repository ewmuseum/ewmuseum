When devising my character, I,like everyone else, hoped to create an interesting character who would entertain. My wrestler, Bob Thurmond, wrestles in two feds. (NAEC and WHW) I watched and I read before I began roleplaying and I learned something quickly. The face is dead. All the wrestlers were heels. In addition, I found that most of the wrestlers aligned themselves in a stable. Now, I have always enjoyed good heels. I thought the original Cactus Jack was/is the most entertaining wrestlers of all time. But, (and I am afraid I am sounding like Bret Hart) wrestling is built on the classic struggle of good v. evil. Fans must have someone to root for. It seems as all we have in wrestling today is evil v. evil. Fans must root for the "worst" of two evils. I guess the purpose of this tirade is a question: Are all e-feds this way? Are there any good faces out there? Any advice for the lone face in his fed?(BT is a family man who needs to wrestle to support his family) Thanks, Kevin Faces aren't a dying breed, there are just separate factions of being a face these days... For instance The Fallen ANgel is a face, he's a face in every sense of the word...Always poltie, always plays by the rules... Christain Cannon is a face, but he's got more of an edge to him, an attitude, he insults, he's arrogant...but he's there to appeal the fans... Well, I have two definite faces out there, both with a twist, though none in the feds you're in. Old Brown Heineken would be the ultimate clean guy. He'd take an illegal object off his opponent to then throw it away. He would _not_ use it on his opponent! The same goes for other 'illegal' activities. However, you don't want to see him in a no holds barred match - he can be more vicious than a wolverine if the rules allow. Mumonkan essentially is an oriental face. Clean, highflying action is his trademark. His twist is that he doesn't speak a word of English. Not having a translator with him, this makes for some fun scenes during cards and interesting, unusual roleplays. Besides, WCW/WWF make us believe that all foreigners are evil, especially when from the 'mysterious East'. It's broken quite often in e-feds, but still worth it. >Any advice for the lone face in his fed?(BT is a family man who needs to >wrestle to support his family) Are all others in the fed total heels? Maybe there are some faces you haven't recognized yet. Or some tweeners that have a hard time against the heels themselves. Get them together, find someone to watch your back while you watch his. Get a (face) manager. Maybe not all of this, but some things should work.
