Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

CWW opened as a conditional pitch on rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 4 January 1996, its founder laying out the whole format before a single member had signed: matches run on TNM 6.0, covering “fair matches, Ladder matches, Battle royals, I Quiet Matches, Towl matches, Cage matches and Lumber jack matches,” one pay-per-view a month, two cards a week, four singles belts and a tag title — World, Intercontinental, US, and CWW Tag Team — and a roster limit of two singles wrestlers and one tag team per member. The launch itself was conditional: “If enough responses The CWW Will start.”

The bar cleared fast. By 6 January the same founder reported “Ive already got 17 people,” and a live web page followed within days, announced on 10 January.

The clearest evidence the promotion actually ran comes from outside its own record: a rival federation's own inter-federation tag-team tournament roster, posted five weeks later on 14 February 1996, lists one entered team holding a current or former CWW Tag Team Championship among title credits drawn from eight other feds — the only surviving trace of an actual title reign following from the January launch.

How or when it closed isn't in the surviving record under its founder's own name.
