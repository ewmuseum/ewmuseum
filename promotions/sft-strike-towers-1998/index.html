Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

SFT, Strike Fantasy Towers, ran a website e-fed whose home page listed rules, staff, staff expectations, a join or application page and an FAQ; a roster with a tag team roster, a stables roster, ringside personnel and official rankings; and five boards: a promo area, a backstage area, an intro promo area, a test area and a pay-per-view promo area. Its shows were Tuesday Night Titans, Friday Night Inferno and Sunday Night War Games, with a pay-per-view and a results archive. Forms let a visitor request a run-in, a match or an interview, and from August 2003 a banner. Its extras were Search SFT, Wrestler Homepages, Strike Chat, Fantasy Warfare, SFT News Central, a hex color chart, history, awards, a title history, a roleplay guide, SFT Favorite Superstars, a hall of fame and a wall of shame.

On 11 July 2003 its champions panel read Radiant as World Champion, Brandi James as Intercontinental Champion, Damien Sparks as United States Champion, Schizophrenia as Television Champion, Lucretia Black as Lightweight Champion and Rein Webb as Hardcore Champion, with the Tag Team titles vacant and a TNT Champion billed Hardcore. By 6 August 2003 the panel read Aj Nin Red Rum as World Heavyweight, Sean Lane as Intercontinental, Cyril Acheston as United States, Schizophrenia as Television, Knife as Lightweight, Travis Hardy as Hardcore, Preach and Destroy as Tag Team and Hahn Mann as TNT. A row of affiliate links ran along the foot of the page, EWA, WFW, ACDC and, until August, SCW, with an invitation to others to link SFT and become an affiliate.
