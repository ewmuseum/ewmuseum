A self-described European Wrestling Federation staging its first 'Death Warrant' pay-per-view in Riga, Latvia in late November 1998, with a developed multi-title slate. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This European Wrestling Federation staged its first pay-per-view, Death Warrant '98, live from Riga, Latvia on 27 November 1998, opening with a video package set to a Marilyn Manson remix and a full entrance montage cut to Metallica's “Battery.”

The announced card carried an already-developed title slate: The Mailman defending the British Title against a former champion, the Homicidal Threat; Future Shock defending the European Tag Team titles in a three-way against Khaos Inc. and Totally Amazing; and, in the main event, Paralyzer defending the European Heavyweight Championship against DeCre in a ladder match. The undercard added a chain match, an eight-man falls-count-anywhere elimination brawl between the stables Sex & Violence and New Breed and the Highlanders, a grudge match, and a four-corners bout the promotion billed as “the Battle for the Future” among four newcomers.

The surviving text carries the opening match, “The Arabian Knight” Amar Jabul against “Magnificent” Mike Smith, well into its second half — Smith blinding Jabul with a handful of powder, then denting a steel chair over his spine for a two-count — before breaking off mid-match with “TO BE CONTINUED.”

No further part of Death Warrant '98 survives; not even the outcome of its own opening match is known, let alone any of the titles it advertised.
