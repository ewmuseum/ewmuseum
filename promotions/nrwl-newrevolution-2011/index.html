Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The New Revolution Wrestling League, NRWL, kept a blog at nrwlwrestling.blogspot.com that posted its event results and its Action scenes, scripted backstage segments and promos, under the labels Action, News, Results and Spotlight. Its blog archive counts 96 posts for 2011 from February, 30 for 2012, 44 for 2013 and 49 for 2014 to May. A post of May 2014 reports that the Action and Results articles of the first half of 2011, NRWL Season Four, had been missing since a change of site and were restored, with two opinion columns, Sweat and Tears and Flawless Facts. Its sidebar lists a roster of 37 names and links NRWL TV, the NRWL Network and an NRWL Wiki.

The page holds the fed's run of 1 to 15 May 2014. At the event of 1 May Johnny Young and Jacob Washington took the NRWL Tag Team Championship from Bruno Kowalski and Marty Anderson, Scotty Paris kept the NRWL Championship against Max Destruction, and a Loser Leaves Musclegate match sent Lovable Liam out of Musclegate. The Development League results of the same day were posted beside it. At the event of 15 May Young and Washington retained against the Muscle Bombers and Franco Mancini pinned Scotty Paris with the Pick Pocket to become the new NRWL Champion. The Action scenes between the events ran its feuds: Paul Priest and Jade against Jax Rivers and Mandy, the Holy Duo against Ash, the Four Elements against Mark Blood, Tornado and Warlock, Mr. Lifeguard against Paul Priest, and Travis 22, who announced that he was still undefeated.
