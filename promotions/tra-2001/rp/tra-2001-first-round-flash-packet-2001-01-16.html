Ketiak [In the begining, there was darkness.  Yes indeed, for all we see is a pitch-black screen.  As boredom, confusion or fear wells up from the beings of those who are anticipating the image metamorphosis of the still-blank screen, a voice booms from within the darkness - the voice of One Who Calls From the

V/O:  Blooming Rafflesia... [The screen at last becomes alive as a photo of a giant Rafflesia gradually dithers on the screen.  It is a majestic flower in its monstrousity - red petals with white blotches embedded in it, a good foot and a half long in circumference, yet repugnant in smell.] 
V/O:  climax of fulfilled life glow... [A fade-in, fade-out sequence occurs, a manifestation of the transition of this photographic slide show.  We then see a photo of the same flower, albeit from a slightly further distance.  It is blooming proud and defiant, as if the treacherous surrounding rainforests are but a dream.  Yet, we are cruelly reminded that it blooms for only once in a lifetime, and what we are seeing is but its zenith.]
V/O:  The distant sunset... [The sequence repeats itself, and we now view a photo of the same Rafflesia flower, albeit we notice the darker background - a signal of the day transcending into night, the sign of the sunset.  The beauty of the twilight beckons, the habitat of Destiny herself is made a welcome abode.] 
V/O:  brings dimness to sad petals... [After another bout of repetitiveness, we come across a similiarly coloured background.  However, we see the Rafflesia in the latter stages of its pathetic existence.  In its degradation, we realize how hopeless the life-cycle, about how cruel Mistress Destiny is.  Even in the atmosphere we can sense the malevolent air of hoplessness and resignation in the face of inevitability.]
V/O:  as it withers eclisped black... [The Rafflesia dies as this tanka - a Japanese form of poetry - concludes.  With nary cathartism nor significance.  Alas, in its invinsibility of beauty, it is ultimately reminded of its frailty.  The futility of going against

4. "Vagabond" Dominic Ash
[With Valentine's Day approaching, we lay our scene outside a very beautiful, colorful, and sweet smelling florist.  It seems that the pretty petals of the flowers are the only bright and cheering things around, what with it be a dull, blustery winter's morning. The surroundings is very quiet, that is until a man comes into the picture...
Not any ol' man, but Dominic Ash, better known as the 'Vagabond'.  As usual, dressed in his lifeless, colorless dull drags consisting of torn dirty jeans, and fleece overcoat.  With his hands tucked well into his jacket pocket, he wanders over towards the camera and the florist and speaks.]
"V" DA:  Halloween has come, and gone... Thanksgiving, thankfully has ended... Christmas and its festive spirit have finally faded away... And now it's time for another meaningless, overhyped, 'special occasion' on the calendar...
That's right, it's that time again for the loved to show their love and appreciation to their partners... For the oldies to rekindle their bond with their lovers ... and for spotty little teenagers to pluck the courage to ask out their bitchy girl classmates to go out on a date with them.  Yep, it's almost time for...
[Ash pulls a disgusted face, it pains him to say the words] Valentine's Day. [With the same grimacing, angered expression on his face, he paces over towards the entrance which is full of different types of flowers and plants.]
"V" DA:  Look at all this crap... [Bending down, he picks up a bunch of delightful violet colored tulips] "V" DA:  What a waste of money! [In a show of total disrespect and stupidity, Ash one-by-one begins to dismantle each petal from the flower, leaving a trail on the floor.  Once he's through having his fun, he drops the whole bunch down and treads on them.]
"V" DA:  Instead of using their money wisely, by offering to help the less fortunate and needed, the general public who walks these streets instead decide to dish out large amounts of cash, cash that the likes of myself and many others around the world are in desperate need of, on buying bunches upon bunches of these things. 
Flowers don't put food on the table!  Flowers don't pay the rent!  Flowers are good for nothing apart from making a mess.  I mean, what's the big deal with them anyway?  People buy them one day, the next they dry up and die, how amusing!
Little things like that really get on my nerves... It's about time people stopped looking down on people like myself, and started treating them with more respect.  I'm human too, I have feelings and emotions.  I have a heart, I'm not a robot or a freak that people judge me being.  Respect is all I'm after, and at the upcoming 'Road Ahead' tournament, I intend to make people respect me for who I am, and why I am.  If they decide not to... [sadistic grin] they'll soon find out the consequences.
[As Ash walks away from the mess he has made, courtesy of the flowers, the shop assistant walks out bewildered by what she is seeing.  The camera follows Ash, who, slightly limping walks away, still talking, however.]
