Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The IWF was the one division of the IW network already open for business when that network announced itself in December 2000, a federation whose own writer was already signing as its president-elect for the new year. Its first address was on Netomia; by February 2001 its cards were posted from the network’s own GeoCities site, beside the minor-league IWL that fed it talent.

Its president called it a five-month-old, board-based federation that February, which puts its opening around September 2000. Its Broken Hearts card of that month was all title and stipulation matches — a Highway-to-Hell four-way for the Hardcore Title, a wrapped steel-chain cage for the Internet Title, a last-man-standing match between the Supreme Survivor and the Intercontinental champion, a no-DQ Texas Tornado for the tag titles — and closed with Damian Storm, Eight Ball and Drake Lawson in a triple-threat iron-man match for the Mastermind and World titles, the president himself the special referee.

Two days after the card its president offered the presidency of what he called a popular and very competitive federation to anyone reliable enough to take it. A day later he had decided to stay, and asked instead for a commissioner to steer its angles, run the Hardcore Title’s storyline and help write its weekly show, Treachery — with a sample of the results style he wanted, Blake Stone against Pyro and Owtsidr.

Its results went up on its own site rather than the newsgroup, and nothing later of the IWF survives.
