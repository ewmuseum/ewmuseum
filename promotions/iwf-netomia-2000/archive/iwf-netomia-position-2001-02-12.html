Well, my "proposal" post garnered about 8 replies, most of them asking me if I wanted a merger/takeover/buyout.   Well, I've decided to stay on as President of the IWF. BUT, I desperately need a Commissioner.   Basically, you would have a say in the way certain angles go and even have full-control over the storyline of our Hardcore Title. You would also be required to help out with 3 matches for our weekly show treachery; each match being between 30 to 5o lines on NotePad with word-wrap enabled.   You would also be scouting new talent for the minor-league and also scouting probable candidates for results-writers.   In fact, I have two Commissioner spots available. One for my fed(IWF) and one for the IWL. If you wish to be a Commish for the IWL, you would probably have to write 2 more matches.   Being a graphics-wizard will also be helpful.   If you are interested, please e-mail me at  [detail withheld] and provide a sample of your results in this format :   This next contest featured Blake Stone against Pyro & Owtsidr in a 1-On-2 Match. Blake came out first with a mic and told the crowd that he realised the things he had done in the past were wrong, but the past is the past, and the future is now. He went on saying that id didn't matter if he had to face Pyro and Owtsidr, or all 3 HisteriKals; he was gonna prove that he is one tough S.O.B. and that he was gonna get the respect he deserved tonight; regardless if he won or not. "One Step Closer" by Linkin Park blared over the speakers and out came Owtsidr, who dashed into the ring and started pummelling Stone with huge right-hands. "Kill The King" played over the speakers and out came Pyro, but The Firebug didn't seem in a hurry; he was actually eating a slice of pizza!! In the ring, Owtsidr just countered a suplex attempt from BS into a front-suplex and saw Pyro!! Owtsidr then yelled at Pyro to get his ass in the ring, but Pyro motioned that he hadn't finished his pizza. This gave Blake the chance to sneak up from behind and hit a first-rate spinning samoan-drop!!! Blake quickly got to his feet and put the boots to Owtsidr, before he pulled The Supreme Survivor up and gave him a gut-wrench front-suplex, before making the cover : one......... two.......... OWTSIDR EASILY KICKS OUT!!     Thanks & Ciao!! ~[name withheld]~ ` Handler of The Maniac(June 1996 to June 2000), K316(June 1996 to December 2000), Nazenoth(June 1998 to October 2000) `All very successful characters
