CWA (Jul 1996) — Founding / House Rules

"CWA eWrestling at it's best!!"

5 July 1996

Want to Join an eWrestling league?? well try out CWA North America's newest eWrestling Alliance. Here are the rules for the CWA . 1. Character Applications - Fill out the application - Character must be original. No Harts, Heartbreakers, Gold People, Nature Boys, etc. - If you are in MKWF make different wrestlers and Managers for CWA. We are going to be having interpromotional cards, every once in a while. 2. Titles - CWA All-American Tiltle - CWA Great Canadian title ( Will Not Go In Effect If Lack Of Canadian Wrestlers ) - CWA T.V. Tag Tiltle - CWA " World wide web Title" 3. Matches - All matches are 2 out of 3 falls, unless it is a gimmick match of some type. 4. Number of people - As of right now, I will only allow a maximum of 40 wrestlers in the CWA. That leaves room for
10 tag teams and 20 singles. - Tag Team members will stick to tag team matches unless one member is injured. 5. Events - A weekly Thursday night show called Thursday Night Thunder- TNT - Each card will have 8 matches. - The first card will be a 3 day event where every title will end up having a holder. - There will be a Supercard either every month or every other month. It will have 10 matches on
it. - Every 3 months there will be an interpromotional card with MKWF - If you want the magazine currently under construction, CWA Spotlight. Email me at [detail withheld].
