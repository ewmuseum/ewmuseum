NeWA Championship Calendar, February 2000

captured 4 Mar 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NeWA Championship Calendar — February, 2000 Roleplays for NeWA Title matches are due at 11:59 EST the night before the match OR the deadline for that card. All flashes must be posted on the NeWA board to count. NeWA World Heavyweight Title — Current champion: 'The Lord of the Ring' Byron Tanis 19th, IWA, Venom: "Either you know or you don't, fag." 20th, ESPN [Hawaii], Adam Fierce: Many people have said "Finally!" and this time, it really shows, Adam Fierce receives his NeWA World Heavyweight title shot and what stage is better than ESPN Hawaii? 27th, OWC, Outkast: The new forearm in the alliance, OWC, gets a visit from the NeWA World Heavyweight champ, will it be Fierce or would it be Tanis dropping by in Ohio state? We'll see him none other than Outkast. NeWA North American Title — Current champion: Agent Mandell 13th, PNW, Bo Justice: Agent Mandell wins via DQ. 18th, NCW, Kai Lewis: For once, this match is going to be on the undercard, in the weirdest card ever, Kai would have to face 3 man in ONE night. 29th, HCWF, Blade: HCWF's Alaska State Champion enters the mix of NeWA guys to receive title shots, and will face Mandell in the last day of the month. NeWA World Tag Team Titles — Current champion: The Texas Outlaws 24th, PNW, The Hired Guns: A chance of a lifetime... but will the duo be on target? 29th, HCWF, The Angels of Destruction: HCWF's Tag-Team champions go on and face the NeWA World Tag-Team champions. NeWA World Television Title — Current champion: Harbeneth Rookens 13th, OWC, Herbeneth Rookens: Rookens d. Lennox to capture the Television Title. 18th, NCW, Justin Lynn: The Dynasty newcomer gets the chance to rain the OWC's parade. 23rd, SWF, Shooter Maloy: SWF's excellent new rookie gets his shot at fame when he'll face his own stable mate once more, Michael Lennox. 28th, IWA, Boog-Man: IWA's Northern champion and former World Champion gets to reclaim his long lost fame, at the expense of the green TV champion. NeWA World Cruiserweight Title — Current champion: El Scorco 12th, SWF, El Scorcho: El Scorcho, ugh, d. Drew Jacobs to claim the Cruiserweight Title. 19th, HCWF, Brian Hale: HCWF's Cruiserweight champion goes shooting for gold. 27th, PNW, King Neptune: PNW's top cruiser gets a return shot. Tradition is on trial in Portland.
