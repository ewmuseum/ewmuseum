Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The National eWrestling Alliance presented itself as a coordinating hub rather than a fed with its own roster, founded in 1997 by an operator its own welcome page names but does not identify beyond that page, and copyrighted continuously from 1997 through 1999 on every captured page of its site. Its one dated 1997 event on record is Halloween Hell, run 31 October in New York, New York. Member territories carried its banner into the field — among them a Great Britain promotion and a Mid Atlantic territory — while the hub itself held six titles of its own: World Heavyweight, World Tag Team, North American, World Television, World Hardcore, and World Cruiserweight, each defended by wrestlers drawn from across its membership.

By 1999 the NeWA ran a full pay-per-view calendar under its own name — Lord of the Ring, Wrestling Classic, an International Peace Festival staged in Tokyo, Constitutional Combat, Battle of the Belts, and a season-ending Battlecade billed “When Worlds Collide” — and published a monthly ranking compiled, its own site says, from five respected handlers, alongside a title-challenge calendar naming the member and rival promotion a challenger came from. That May it advertised openings to relaunch four lapsed member groups outright: New Zealand, Singapore, Germany, and a Middle East chapter.

Retired title lists on its own site record former champions going back further than any show the hub's captured pages otherwise document, including a World Lightweight Championship and a World Woman's Heavyweight Championship it no longer defended by 1999. No closing statement survives; the hub's own last captured pages all date to 14 May 2001, with nothing later held anywhere in its own record.

World Class Championship eWrestling (WCCW)

Member (circa 1998 – circa 2001)
WCCW’s own held record already self-describes as ‘A World Class Championship eWrestling territory of the National eWrestling Alliance hub,’ hosted at the same GeoCities address the hub’s own pages use directly. The hub’s own ‘Roll Call of Champions’ (27 Nov 1999) names the reigning NeWA World Heavyweight Champion as ‘Dark Arthur,’ exactly matching WCCW’s own title history for that same date — confirming WCCW hosted the hub’s own flagship title on its behalf.

British Championship Wrestling (BCW, 1999)

Member (1999 or earlier – 1999 or later)
BCW’s own held record already self-describes as ‘a member territory of the NeWA (National eWrestling Alliance) hub,’ billing itself on its own pages ‘N.e.W.A Great Britain’ — matching the hub’s own Membership Listing entry ‘NeWA Great Britain.’

NeWA Mid Atlantic Wrestling

Member (1999 or earlier – 1999 or later)
NeWA Mid Atlantic’s own held record already self-describes as ‘a Mid-Atlantic member territory of the NeWA (National eWrestling Alliance) hub,’ with its own Application page pointing directly at the hub’s own shared GeoCities address — matching the hub’s own Membership Listing entry ‘NeWA Mid Atlantic.’
