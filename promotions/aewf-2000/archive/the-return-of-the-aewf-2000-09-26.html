The return of the AEWF

26 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, it has been a long time in coming, but it is back. The Australian
Email Wrestling Federation is about to return, and is looking for people interested in joinng. The requirements for joining are listed below. The
AEWF started in June 1996, and went for three strong years, before an unfortunate demise in May 1999. But now, the AEWF is back, ready to return to the glory days. An interesting fact about the AEWF, is that a wrestler from the AEWF was the first ever winner of the Scoops Wrestling
Hall Of Fame tournament, back at the beginning of 1997.
Once again, I will be running the federation, but am always willing to listen to ideas, and offers of help.
Now, for the requirements to join. 1. Real wrestlers only. 2. Anywhere from one to four wrestlers are allowed. You can have up to four singles wrestlers, two singles wrestlers and a tag-team, or two tag-teams...the choice is yours. 3. A maximum of TWO managers per stable (group of wrestlers). 4. Those people who are active in the federation, will be rewarded...those people who are not active, will not be kicked out, but also will not have as high a profile as those who are active.
5. By active, I mean those people who send in interviews, ideas for feuds/angles etc. 6. Above all else, enjoy yourself... Anyone interested in joining, email me at [detail withheld] and I will get back to you as soon as possible.
