Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Professional Independent Combat Organization was “officially formed on August 6, 2003, in a very brief press conference” in Tokyo, staged as the work of Hiroshi Yamasaki, the older brother of Project BATTLE*DREAM's own owner, Kayoko Yamasaki, and the owner of a restaurant chain. Its own rules capped the roster at two non-Japanese wrestlers, rotated as spots opened.

Its broadcast team named Shinya Tsukioka for blow-by-blow and Patrick Rhodes, an English-born wrestler fluent in Japanese, for color, alongside a three-referee crew and a matchmaking committee with final say over every pairing. Two championships ran the roster, the PICO Singles Title and the PICO Tag Team Titles, contested across a twice-monthly TV show, PICO TV, a reaction show, PICO Battle Station, and occasional commercial video releases.

Its surviving broadcast, PICO TV — Episode One (28 November 2003), ran two matches under a “Hyper Death Scramble '03” banner: Toshiji Yamanaki beat Odin Trollslayer three baskets to two in a Basketball Death Match fought with weapons scattered across the court, and the Gremlin beat One Ton Wakai in an Ice Cream Parlor Death Match staged inside Takuya Nobu's own shop, pinning him through a table after a barbed-wire clothesline.

No later broadcast survives here. How PICO's own run continued isn't in the surviving record.
