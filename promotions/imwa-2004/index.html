Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The International Masters Wrestling Association introduced itself in a founding update dated 24 October 2004, its own text describing a promotion built on roleplay, written match strategies, and story-lines all steered, by its own account, “to my discretion.”

It planned two weekly shows out of a home venue it called the IMWA Masterdome in Ottawa, Ontario — a minor Wednesday card, Anarchy, mostly told in summary, and a bigger Saturday Night, Zero Hour, carrying five or six full matches plus dark matches — alongside quarterly pay-per-views and an annual 32-man single-elimination tournament, the U-Cup, whose final eight would settle the trophy in a battle royal.

Four championships anchored the promised roster — the International Masters Heavyweight, National Masters, Junior Grand Prix (for wrestlers under 230 pounds), and International Masters Tag Team titles — every one of them still vacant at the time of this posting, alongside an on-air president, Ruppert Kennedy, and a commentary team of Dean Grass, Seth Stevens, Harry Reynolds, and Donald Blackstone.

Only a handful of wrestlers had signed by then: five singles competitors — Super Lazy, Stronghold, Cyclone, The Street Pharmacist, and Ghetto Master — and two tag teams, Ogre and Simpleton, and Pegasus and Juxtapose.

No card, result, or further trace survives; whether the promotion ever crowned a champion isn't in its surviving record.
