My name is [name withheld] and I am the one who is masterminding this whole thing. IMWA is an e-wrestling federation. IMWA stands for International
Masters Wrestling Association. IMWA is one of countless internet-based wrestling federations. In IMWA, you run a character or a tag team and follow the character's career from start to finish however long you desire to run that character in this federation.
In IMWA, roleplaying, strategy, and story-line are all important. Why? Well, in e-wrestling, these factors all combine to equal success. Roleplaying in short, is where you become the character that you run and speak for him. As time goes on, a web page will be made for IMWA and this will be talked about in more detail.
All roleplays are sent to me at: [detail withheld] Strategy . . . these are not as important as roleplays, but they are important nonetheless. A strategy is a summary of how you want your match to go. As you write one for your character, your opponent writes one for his. The strategies are then meshed together to give an idea of how the match is going to go.
Strategy format for IMWA is as follows: Strategy for (Name of wrestler/team) Entrance: Besides the entrance write-up, we want to know if there is anything special for this match, in particular, that you want done. Comments, notes, etc . . .
During the match: Write a summary of what you want the character to do and how you want him to wrestle.
After the match: If there is anything special you would like your character to say or do, then write it out in this section. If the time calls for it, it will be used.
Key Points: List and number as many key points as you would like in this match. These will be used to my discretion. These can also be used to build angles.
All strategies are to be sent to me at: [detail withheld] Story-lines/Angles: These can be sent to me at any time. I choose where a character goes depending on what is produced by the handler. However, you can change how your character's story-line goes by sending suggestions to me. Depending on time and what the angle would do, I will approve it and it will happen. But remember, it's to my discretion.
All angle ideas are to be sent to me at: [detail withheld] ============================================================================ II.Titles: International Masters Heavyweight Title: Most important title in IMWA. This title is the ultimate goal as titles are concerned.
National Masters Title: As IMWA is international, a title is needed for the national division. The National Masters is the minor title in the universal singles division.
Junior Grand Prix Title: For wrestlers under 230, this is their title. Having this title means that you are the king of the IMWA Juniorweight Wrestlers.
International Masters Tag Team Titles: Most important rank in the tag team division. The team that holds these must be tough and have stamina for these titles are defended often.
U-Cup: Each year, IMWA will hold a Universal Tournament where the winner receives the U-Cup. Universal Cup . . . 32 men fight in a single elimination tournament. The final 8 will face off in a battle royal where the winner will become the U-Cup champion.
**Titles may be changed, dropped, added in the future. ============================================================================ III.Shows: The schedule goes a little like this: Sunday: Nothing/PPV (Pay Per Views are every three months) Monday: Nothing Tuesday: Roleplays Sent Out Wednesday: Wednesday "Anarchy"/Update Thursday: Nothing Friday: Roleplays Sent Out (Pre-Show) Saturday: Saturday Night "Zero Hour"/Update Wednesday Anarchy: The minor show. Matches, except for the main event, or if important enough, a double main event, are written in summary form. This show is hosted by Dean Grass and Harry Reynolds.
Strategies and roleplays for Wednesday Warfare are due 7:00 PM EDT Tuesdays. Pre-Show: A show hosted by Dean Grass and Donald Blackstone. Roleplays are aired here. A run-down and preview will also be attractions of this show.
Roleplays will only be accepted for the Pre-Show until Noon EDT on Fridays. Any others may be counted less and be put on Saturday Night. Saturday Night: This is the big show, so to speak. This show will have 5-6 written matches as well as a few dark matches. A report will be included in the beginning of each Saturday Night. Night's before a PPV, format may be changed to one like Wednesday Warfare's. This show is hosted by Dean Grass and
Seth Stevens.
Strategies for this event are due the Wednesday before the event. There will be plenty of time to complete strategies. Any strategies sent after the 9:00
PM EDT Wednesday deadline will not be used.
Roleplays for this event are due Friday at Noon EDT. ============================================================================ IV.People: This section lists the fictional people to be familiar with in IMWA. President: Ruppert Kennedy This is the man behind the scenes. Others will be introduced, but he will stay neutral as the ruling party of IMWA.
Commentators: Dean Grass - Dean is the most knowledgeable commentator in the IMWA. He is on every show. Dean is in his late 30's, is married, and has two kids.
Seth Stevens - Seth is a wise-ass. He is in his ealry twenties and quite often refers to Dean as "Old man," and some obscene names, as well.
Harry Reynolds - Harry is less known to the public and works more behind the scenes with the booking committee. He is in his late 20's and down to earth.
Donald Blackstone - He is a senior commentator and analyst. He is in his mid-50's and has studied wrestling for over 30 years. We are proud to have such a mind of the business. **He was also a manager in small federations after a brief stint as a wrestler in the late 60's.
Ring Announcer - Deviant Soul Deviant is a massive man who is in his early 20's and a party animal. He has long blonde hair and stands a tall 6'9". He gets along with the fans and many other people. Basically, he's fun-loving.
Reporter - Diana Freedman Diana is a gorgeous woman who likes to bear all, like a typical wrestling woman. Ah-ah, boys . . . she's taken by Deviant Soul.
Kodiak - Head of security. He likes Diana Freedman, but she doesn't like him. Deviant Soul and Kodiak are not friends because Kodiak has a tendency to bother Diana.
