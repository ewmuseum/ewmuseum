War Child picks him up off the floor and throws him head-first into the lockers. Child lifts Franklin up and slams him onto the bench. He grabs a chair and lifts it above his head. BF: This match hasn't even started yet, and these two are already going at it. EC: It looks like Brandon Franklin got jumped by War Child. It's the first

Child's lower back and plants him on the floor with a faceslam.]
BF: Good Lord! His head just bounced off the concrete! Franklin putting the boots to War Child now!
EC: He's stomping on his head, his chest, everywhere! LW: They haven't broken anything yet! [A table sits up against the wall. Franklin whips War Child into the wall near it.] LW: All right! [Franklin locks a front waistlock on War Child. Child headbutts him, staggering "Pure Chaos," but the hold remains. Child gives him another.
He rears back for another, but Franklin rakes his eyes. Brandon reaffirms his grip and lifts Child up and over his head in an overhead belly-to-belly suplex, through the table and to the concrete.]
LW: SWEET JESUS! BF: War Child is semi-conscious on the cold floor! War Child is a full 30 pounds heavier than Franklin! That took a lot of strength!
EC: Someone stop this! Get these two into the ring before they murder one another! LW: And what do you think they're going to do in the ring? [Security personnel flood into the hallway. They begin to force the two towards the ring. Franklin grabs The Extreme War Child by the hair and starts to walk him towards the ring. When they hit the arena, Franklin whips Child into the guardrail. War Child bounces back with a lariat across the chest of Franklin, sending him to the floor.]
BF: Stiff lariat by War Child! Franklin's to his feet! War Child drops him with another one!
EC: War Child now, bringing Franklin the rest of the way to the ring,
Franklin head-first into the ring post!
LW: His head hit with a thud! Now THIS is great wrestling! [War Child rolls Franklin into the ring and follows after him.] [DING DING DING] BF: Finally the bell sounds! The match begins, but the fight is continuing! EC: War Child gives Franklin a couple of forearms to the back. You can almost hear the squish of internal organs moving around inside him!
LW: Eww! [War Child pushes Franklin into the corner. Child hooks Brandon's arms over the top ropes, rears back and chops him hard across the chest.]
Crowd: WOO! LW: Shut up! Why-oh-why must the crowd shout "Woo" everytime a chop is given?! Like Flair invented the damn thing!
[War Child places Franklin on the top rope and follows up. War Child sets him up for a suplex, but Franklin pushes him to the mat. War Child lands face down.]
BF: Brandon Franklin just shoved The War Child to the mat! EC: He's turning around on the middle rope now... [Franklin moonsaults backwards, squashing War Child's ribs and face against the mat.] BF: Franklin showing his agility by crushing The War Child against the mat with a moonsault!
EC: Look at the blood starting to pour from War Child's head! That moonsault must have rubbed him the wrong way on t he mat, judging from that gash!
LW: War Child's doing the right thing by rolling out of the ring. He needs to get that blood out of his face. He needs to take a rest. He needs to...SWEET JESUS!
[Franklin hits the opposite ropes and projects himself between the third and second ropes onto War Child.]
BF: Franklin just leveled the War Child with that suicide dive! This man can do it all! [Franklin whips War Child into the time keeper's table and walks after him. War Child slams up against it, sending it to the floor. Franklin drops a couple of elbows into the sternum of the War Child and rolls back into the ring to break the count. The ref tells him to keep it in the ring and argues with him. In the process, the referee nearly slips on a
still-drying pool of the War Child's blood.] LW: Eww! We never bled like that in my day. EC: Your day was boring by today's standards. LW: I paved the way for your future, punk! Show me some respect! EC: Larry, you wrestled in bars and armories. You're hardly a Bret Hart or a Ric Flair. LW: I'm better than both those chumps. And these guys in the ring, too! [War Child reaches over and grabs the ring bell. The referee pulls out a handkerchief and tries to dry up the blood. Meanwhile, Franklin comes back after War Child. When he bends down to lift him up, War Child clocks him with the ring bell. With the ref still not looking, War Child digs the corner into Franklin's head, eventually drawing blood.]
BF: This is brutal! EC: This is exciting! LW: This is kinda gross! [War Child rolls Franklin back into the ring. He lifts him onto his shoulders and drops him head-first with a DVD. Child rolls onto him for the cover.]
BF: One! Two! Oh, shoulder up and two and a half. EC: First pin attempt in this match. Had it been falls count anywhere, it'd probably already be over!
[War Child picks him up and whips him into the ropes.] EC: Franklin bouncing off the ropes now...HUGE spinebuster by War Child! LW: As wrestling commentators, we're continually searching for new synonyms for the word "big." However, we tend to leave out "big." For the rest of this match, I'll use the word "big" in all of my sentences.

Hajime locked in on "Pure Chaos!"
LW: A big Kata-jahoosle-thingie. EC: Kata Hajime. LW: Big word. [The referee checks with Franklin, Franklin refusing to give up. Eventually, the referee grabs Franklin's arm to raise it...it falls.] BF: That's one! The referee raising his arm again...that's two! One more time...that's thr...no! It stays up! Brandon Franklin fighting to his feet! Amazing resilience! He's to his feet!
EC: An elbow to the stomach of War Child. LW: Big elbow. EC: Another! Franklin's free! He runs into the ropes! [Franklin bounds back, and is caught immediately with a sleeperhold. War
Child looks around, then throws his legs out and executes the sleeping neckbreaker. He goes for the cover.]
LW: Big move! EC:One! Two! Kick out! [War Child lifts Franklin up off the canvas by the hair. Franklin fires a few shots to the stomach of War Child on the way up, then leaps and gives a dropkick to Child's chin. War Child's head lies against the first turnbuckle.]
LW: Franklin now with a BIG foot choke against War Child's neck! That's really big, Bob. BF: It sure is, Larry! The air seems to be leaving War Child's lungs in a hurry, judging from the gasping and coughing.
EC: The referee is laying down the count! Referee Josh Babcock: One! Two! Three! Four! Five! All right Franklin! You want a DQ? BF: Franklin backing off...only to charge right back in! He's now kicking away at the chest area!
EC: It seems like he wants to lessen the breathing ability of the bigger man. He's dragging him to the center of the ring now...
LW: BIG cross armbar by Franklin! BF: He's trying to hyper-extend the elbow of the Extreme War Child. I guess if the arm doesn't work, neither do the power moves.

Child while cranking back on that arm. I'd have given up by now!

BF: Both men up! War Child runs in for a lariat...ducked by Franklin...Franklin with a lariat of his own! EC: War Child hits the mat but gets right back up!
