Monday Night Mayhem (March 27, 2000) (Part 1 of 6)

28 March 2000

[An instrumental of "Epic" by Faith No More begins to play as we see a video clip of Nicklaus Kain working over an opponent. Then another clip of a moonsault by Damon Knight onto a helpless opponent. Then we see a computer image of "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams walk into the screen, carrying a chair, and look around. He laughs and then swings the chair and seems to hit the camera. Just as he hits the camera the whole picture dissolves into the title screen...]

Indiana. Fireworks begin to explode as the fans are cheering wildly.
There are banners and signs everywhere. One of the signs say, "!". Another says, "!". Yet another says, "!"] [The fans are working up to a simmer as the screen switches to show our announcing team. On the left sits Eric Chaney. He is wearing a blue polo shirt and has the traditional headset on his head. In the middle is the main announcer, Bob Frankhauser. He is wearing a blue shirt with an NeWF logo on it and the traditional headset. The third man, on the right, is former wrestling great Larry "The Crusher" Watters. He is rugged looking and has a scowl on his face constantly. He is wearing a suit and tie and has jet-black hair. The music dies down as Bob begins to speak.]
Bob Frankhauser: Welcome to Monday Night Mayhem! We are finally on the air live from Indianapolis, Indiana! My name is Bob Frankhauser and with me is former wrestling great, Larry Watters...
Larry Watters: That's Larry "THE CRUSHER" Watters moron. These losers at home need to know my full name.
Bob Frankhauser: Uh....Well...alright. And on the other side of me here is new comer Eric Chaney...
Eric Chaney: Hello everyone! Wow this is just exciting to be apart of! BF: It sure is Eric. Fans do we have a show for you tonight! [The screen switches and shows a graphic of Kurt Castle standing with his arms crossed on one side of the screen. On the other side is Baldwin looking ready to rumble. Behind the pictures is a background of lightning flashing over a dark cloud screen. The announcers can be heard over the graphic.]
BF: We kick off our television parade with a super match between Kurt
Castle and "Black Jack" Baldwin!
LW: "The Devastator" is going to be one of the wild cards here in NeWF. Not many remember him from two years ago, but I can tell you he is a prime killing machine!
[Screen switches back to our announcers.] EC: You can't count out Baldwin. He was LITERALLY a Wild Card at one time. That tag team rocked! He is solo now and seems like he is primed for the big time!
LW: You are so sad. EC: What? BF: Just ignore him Eric. Also tonight fans... [The screen switches and shows a graphic of Brandon Franklin standing with his arms crossed on one side of the screen. On the other side is War Child looking mean and nasty. Behind the pictures is a background of lightning flashing over a dark cloud screen. The announcers can be heard over the graphic.]
BF: Two men known for their hardcore style get matched up tonight and the main question here is which one can actually be the most feared Hardcore man in NeWF?
LW: That's an easy one [Screen switches back to our announcers.] EC: How so? LW: Well one of them is actually named War Child. I mean come on! EC: Well Franklin has a nickname of "Pure Chaos". Names mean nothing, it is what you do inside the ring that counts!
BF: Or around the ring as these guys are famous for their hardcore exploits. This one promises to be an awesome match.
EC: Yes it will! BF: Fans we have time set aside for what is being called a "special message from NeWF Owner, Chris Hopper". So without holding back any more, let's go to the ring with our announcer, Steven Stewert.
[The screen cuts to the ring where Steven Stewert is standing in the center holding the microphone and a index card.]
SS: Ladies and Gentlemen, the owner of the NeWF....Mr. Chris Hopper! [A loud voice booms over the PA system.] VOICE: MAKE WAY FOR THE KING OF COOL !!! [The lights go out suddenly as the beginning strums of "TNT" by AC/DC start to blare over the loudspeakers. The crowd erupts with a huge face pop as the screen lights up with images of "Too Cool" Chris Hopper. The music plays for a bit and then burst into the chorus as the big screen shows footage of Hopper's past wrestling glories. Hopper then enters the arena and the attention is off the big screen. The music continues through the chorus as Hopper struts down to the ring. Hopper is wearing an armani suit that is black with a red tie. He is carrying a card in his right hand and briefcase in his right hand. He reaches the ring as the chorus ends and another instrumental has begun. Hopper enters the ring and walks to the center of the ring as the music dies down. He sets the briefcase down and takes the mic from Stewert, who leaves the ring. He raises the mic to his mouth.]
Hopper: Welcome to NeWF everyone! [Crowd pop.] Hopper: This is probably the first and only time I will get a chance t speak to NeWF fans directly so I want to make the most of it. This league is about more than a couple of weekly wrestling shows.
[Hopper opens up the card in his hand and read a bit.] Hopper: This is about our fans, a great group of workers, and a major chance to show the world how a wrestling league should be ran!
[Major crowd pop.] Hopper: I felt that if we wanted a big send off, then why not come to the capital of my home state, Indianapolis!
[Power crowd pop.] Hopper: Tonight we bring you an exciting night of sports entertainment action. I know you will all have a great time. Now I want to speak about the future of NeWF for a moment.
[Hopper pauses a moment.] Hopper: As excited as I am to be the owner of this federation, I do not have time to oversee all of the day-to-day operations of the federation.
Thus I went and found the most competent man for the job. This man will assume the position as President of NeWF and will report only to me. Meet
the new "Law" of NeWF...K E V I N K A S H ! ! ! [A softer sounding rendition of "Epic" begins to play as Kash steps out onto the entranceway. He is a man that is barely above six feet tall and he is also wearing a black armani suit. He walks down to the ring and enters through the ropes. Hopper gets a smile on his face as he continues.]
Hopper: This man is my pick to lead this federation on to the honor and accolades it rightly deserves. Kevin, it's great to have you aboard.
Please share with us what you have in store for the NeWF. [Hopper hands Kash the microphone and steps back from him so he can have center stage.] Kash: Thanks boss. I must say I was a bit leery when I was asked to become President of NeWF because my main livelihood came from announcing and interviewing the talent in front of the cameras. Mr. Hopper here talked me into taking the job and I really think it is the best decision I ever made. There are hundreds of ideas in the works and I just wanted to share a few with you to give you a taste of what is to come here in NeWF.
[Kash pauses as he pulls a piece of paper out of his suit jacket and opens it to read from it.]
Kash: The NeWF has a bevy of fine wrestlers that are all championship material. Therefore our titles will almost all be handed out over the next three weeks and it starts tonight!
[Crowd pop.] Kash: Tonight's scheduled main event between Nicklaus Kain and "Tiger" Jay
Moore is now for a Television title contender position. And the winner will wrestle for the Television Title on April tenth!
[Major crowd pop.] Kash: Next week we will be deciding the second of three Television contenders as well as awarding the first NeWF Light Heavyweight Champion his title belt! That match will be a six-way dance! Then on the tenth, we have a battle royal for the final Television contender spot and the winner wrestles the other two qualifiers in the main event on THE SAME NIGHT!
[The crowd erupts in cheers as the announcers can be heard over the picture.] LW: What a crock. he is stacking the deck against the rumble winner already! This guy is gonna kill this league before it gets going!
BF: Pipe down Larry. [Kash folds up the piece of paper and keeps it in is hand as he continues.] Kash: As you can see we have tons planned for you fans to cheer about over the coming weeks, so just sit back and prepare for the ride of your lives!
Now on with the show! ["Epic" by Faith No More begins to blare out over the PA system as Hopper and Kash exit the ring. The crowd is cheering their heads off as we switch to see our announcers sitting at ringside.]
BF: Kevin Kash is the new President of NeWF and he has some great things in store indeed! EC: It is an exciting time to be in NeWF for sure! BF: It sure is and we have our main event fully announced... [The screen switches and shows a graphic of "Tiger" Jay Moore standing with his arms crossed on one side of the screen. On the other side is Nicklaus
Kain looking mean and nasty. Behind the pictures is a background of lightning flashing over a dark cloud screen. At the bottom of the screen is a phrase written in red letters that says "Television Title Qualifying
Match" The announcers can be heard over the graphic.]
BF: ...as it will be Nicklaus Kain battling Jay Moore for a spot in the
Television title Triple Threat Match in two weeks!
LW: Moore is an idiot that I remember from a little while back last year. He can't match Kain in any way! [Screen switches back to our announcers.] EC: Why do you say that? Moore is a street fighter who has made it to the top levels of his profession by being a tough-as-nails brawler with superb technical talent. He can compete with Nicklaus Kain or President Kash would not have made that match what it is.
BF: Good point Eric. Kain and Moore are both great athletes and telling who will win just might be the toughest thing we could do right now.
LW: Kain is a former television Champion who should never have had the belt taken from him. He was damn good in my opinion.
EC: Well many feel Moore never got the shot he deserved in ACCW and this may be his first real chance to step up to the plate!
BF: Fans those matches are all great, but we also have a triangle Light
Heavyweight affair tonight!
[The screen switches and shows a graphic of Tyrone Parker standing with his arms crossed looking cocky on one side of the screen. On the other side is
Damon Knight looking nasty. In the middle of them is Derek Diamond standing with his hands on his hips and looking ready to rumble. Behind the pictures is a background of lightning flashing over a dark cloud screen. The announcers can be heard over the graphic.]
BF: Three of our top Light Heavyweights in the same ring where the first pinfall or submission decides the match! This one could be the show stealer!
LW: Oh Please! These tiny losers are not worth the time. Why do you think everyone wants to bulk up? It is because weighing less than 235 pounds is basically a first class ticket to nowhere!
[Screen switches back to our announcers.] EC: I disagree. LW: Oh course you do. EC: Well it is proven that great matches that showcase wrestling skill are just as over with the fans as guys who talk incessantly.
LW: What?!?! EC: Oh sorry, big word. I mean guys who talk alot. LW: I ought to take you in that ring and show you what I will do to you if you ever do that to me again!
[Bob gets the peace as he continues on.] BF: And if you thought that was enough, we have one final match for your tonight in addition to all of this!
[The screen switches and shows a graphic of Brian Collins standing with his arms crossed on one side of the screen. On the other side is Adam Rodgers is standing and holding up the "#1" sign. Behind the pictures is a background of lightning flashing over a dark cloud screen. The announcers can be heard over the graphic.]
BF: Adam Rodgers goes up against Brian Collins. Collins calls himself the
Main Event, but "The Natural" just might be his ultimate match!
LW: Collins has no equal for what he does inside that ring. He is a beast, a man's man. he is going to take that Nancy Boy Rodgers and turn him into a pretzel!
[Screen switches back to our announcers.] EC: Rodgers may be one of the most technically superior big men our league has. He is already beloved by the fans when he went out and signed autographs and greeted fans as they waited for the doors to open. This guy is a crowd pleasure extraordinaire.
BF: He sure is and the fans love him for it. I think this match will be another one that is too close to call!
LW: Both of you will be bowing down to me when Collins wipes the floor with him! BF: Now I bet that doesn't happen! EC: Yeah, even if you do turn out to be right! (chuckles) LW: Why did I take this job? I could have stayed doing small promotions and actually worked with guys worth working with!
EC: Well don't let the door hit you....well you get the picture old timer. BF: Fans, we will be back right after these commercials with our first match in NeWF history! Stick around for more Monday Night Mayhem!
[Cut to commercials. As they run, the following NeWF commercial runs.] [The screen is black as a deep, booming voice comes over the screen. As he talks the first sentence, the words come up on the screen.]
VOICE OVER: Do you want to be a Champion? [The screen switches to shows the NeWF Television title revolving around. Then we see the NeWF Light Heavyweight Title revolving slowly as the light glimmers off of it.]
VOICE OVER: Have you ever tried to be like your favorite NeWF Champion, but always fell short when you wanted to celebrate a title? Well now you don't have to.
[The screen switches to show a kid walking in front of the camera with a replica of the future NeWF National Heavyweight Title around his waist.
The child has his arms in the air and he is acting like he just beat Nicklaus Kain.] VOICE OVER: Now you can own this replica of the belt that men have fought for. This belt has had some great Champions, and now you can put it around your waist!
[The screen shows one of the replica belts with a black screen behind it as it rotates and seems to glimmer in the light.]
VOICE OVER: For only $49.95, plus shipping and handling, you can truly become an NeWF Champion. This belt has a synthetic rubber belt with a gold plated face on it. This belt was made to be as real as possible, and it is.
[The screen switches to show a kid placing a nameplate in it's lower center and you can hear him say, "It says my name! Cool!"]
VOICE OVER: That's right, you can even have a special nameplate engraved for it so the belt is truly yours forever.
[The screen now shows parents watching their son and smiling.] VOICE OVER: And parents, this belt is one hundred percent safe as it has no sharp edges and everything is made from safe chemicals and plastics.
How can you go wrong. [The screen again shows the belt alone with a black background, as it rotates around and just glimmers in the light.]
VOICE OVER: So call 1-800-NeWF-GFT and order yours today. Rush delivery is available and satisfaction is guaranteed. Become a Champion today!
[Fade to black as the rest of the commercials begin to play.]
