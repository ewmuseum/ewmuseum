Flash Frenzy (3/22/00) (Part 2 of 3)

23 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1- "Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin 2- "Superstar" Sean Cage 3- "Ace of Hearts" Ace Hart 4- "Excessive Force" Eric Vanguard 5- The Big Chill ============================================================== "Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin ============================================================== [The Scene opens in an empty parking lot. A building, all brick, and faded is off towards the back of the scene, as lights shine down upon the red doors, with tinted black windows, that lead inside the building. As the lights gleam of the wet, cold, and dark pavement a glare comes off the camera lens. An engine is heard in the background, probably from an automobile.]
[The Camera pans over to where the noise is coming from, and two Headlights gleam into the lens, almost blinding the viewers. As the headlights come closer, it just gets brighter and brighter. Then the Automobile comes to a halt, and the engine turns off, after a two second delay, the lights do as well. This is a red Hummer, with a black top.]
[A man steps out of the Hummer after the door opens. He is directly under a street light now, and as the light shines down on him, we see that he has brown hair, and great muscular physique, and he's sporting some black jeans and a black leather jacket. The man locks the doors as we hear the jingling of his keys. The man then proceeds to walk towards the entrance of the building as he lights up a cigarette.]
[Two benches, one to the left, and one to the right sit in front of the door. The man sits on the bench off to the right, while we are at a view directly in front of him, on the bench to the left. The man is none other than "Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin. Brandon takes a puff of his cigarette, as he begins to talk.]
Brandon Franklin: "I got my line-up this morning, and that's why I called you out here. It seems they put me up against a guy worth a bucket of s[censored]t, if even that. His name: 'The Extreme Warrior' War Child."
[Brandon takes another drag of his cigarette.] Brandon Franklin: "Yes, I am talking about the man who paid the front office to cut a damn interview for him to get him over as the 'bad-ass' of the NeWF. Let me tell ya, it didn't amuse me, one bit. I sat back on my black couch, watching my big-screen Television, when I saw a press conference which turned into a f[censored]n' zoo by the time it was over."
[Brandon blows a ring of smoke out of his mouth.] Brandon Franklin: "Supposively this 'Extreme Warrior' has the President, Kevin Kash in check. I don't buy it. Let me open up by saying this, going out and trying to cut an interview was bad, but paying off the president to make it better... even worse! What I'm going to do here, Child, is I'm gonna' insult the living piss out of you, after I let you know what the hell went wrong in your interview. Then, after that in about a week, instead of insulting, I'm going to literally beat the piss out of you, to teach you that I'm not one of those 'so-called superstars' you were referring to."
[Brandon flips his Cigarette to the ground after another drag, he steps on it, as it sizzles in the small water puddle.]
Brandon Franklin: "Because, when you were looking down that roster, you must have skipped my name, or missed my profile, because I sure as hell ain't one of the guys you were referring to. One thing that annoyed me the most was the fact that you were bad-mouthing the competition in this organization, and then you went on to say that we are the 'best wrestlers'. Don't believe me, check out your interview, jack-ass."
[A Brief pause...] Brandon Franklin: "Not only did you waste time for your elders who would've rather been watching an episode of 'Golden Girls' but you also wasted my time. Time is a key to life, and I don't like to waste it. So, when I have to hear you cut an interview so bad that my four year old nephew has to ask if you're *REALLY* a Wrestler, it annoys me. Then, I have to wait a week to stick my foot in your mouth, hopefully shutting it for good."
[Brandon smirks.] Brandon Franklin: "I'd also like to remind you that a 'war' is not going on, well, maybe a small one in that other country... but a 'war' in the
NeWF? The only war in the NeWF is between you - and - yourself! In your mind... and in your heart."
[Brandon rolls his eyes.] Brandon Franklin: "So, that's why I'm going to do you a favor, Mister...Child." [A serious look comes over Brandon's face.] Brandon Franklin: "I'm going to put you out of your misery before you get so caught-up in a world of your own, and immediately play yourself for a fool. Bring all of your little Ninja buddies, and throw all of the 'Judo
Throws' you can get out of your body, and see if that's enough to take me down, okay, Warrior? You're pathetic War Child."
[Brandon begins to spell the word out.] Brandon Franklin: "P-A-T-H-E-T-I-C! PA-THET-IC! PATHETIC! I've got the whole four lines of your interview memorized now, although you tried to make it a lengthy one, but when you opened your stupid mouth, all that came out was the same s[censored]t you said, exactly two minutes before that!
Do me a favor, get someone to write your interviews, have them put it on a flash-card, and hold it for you, so you can read. But, with your fourth grade geniusy, I don't know if you *CAN* read over a sixth grade level.
So, better yet, shut your damn mouth before you give me five more reasons to break your neck!"
[Brandon stands up, and walks into the building. The Camera fades.] ============================================================== "Superstar" Sean Cage ============================================================== (Sean Cage is standing at center court wearing basketball shorts and a NeWF basketball jersey, at the RCA Dome in Indianapolis Indiana)
(The dome is completly empty) Sean: Welcome to the Indianapolis RCA Dome. In about a month the best in the world of college basketball will be here competing to become the national champion. Unfortunatly my alma mater didn't make the turnament this year.
(Sean begins to walk towords one end of the court taking a dribble ever two or so steps) Sean: So if im a wrestler why am i here you might ask. Well wanted to get into a turnament atmosphere. See it's March Madness in the sports world and the NeWF has thier on little version of March Madness. Which i am gladly a part off.
(Sean stops at the free throw line) Sean: Only the NeWF prize isn't as illustrustrious as the NCAA National Championship. That prize comes a little later. I have a match with The Big Chill in this little tv title turnament. A man who I seen compete back in the ACCW. He impresses me and i look forword to matching skills aganst him.
Then the winner of that match moves onto the TV title triangle match with two other super athletes.
Sean: But first i must go into the ring with a man who i know absoulutly nothing about. That scares me. Pure Choas i don't know a think about you and im sure you really don't know anything about me so i guess the playing field is even. You are my first match here in the NeWF and it will be an interesting test for me.
(Sean then attempts to shoot a free throw, only misses) Sean: Yuk! I just made myself look bad on national television. Hey im not perfect. (Sean then dunks the ball) Sean: I guess i will have to work on my high percentage shots. (The scene fades out as Sean begins shooting around) ============================================================== "Ace of Hearts" Ace Hart ============================================================== [The camera fades into a shot of the Las Vegas skyline. The sun is setting over the desert as the neon lights begin to glow welcoming the night to the city that never sleeps. Off in the distance an unfamiliar sight can be seen. It appears to be a man walking from the desert. The wind is whipping at the tail of his coat flipping it violently. He continues forward as the desert heat radiates from the ground seeming to create shimmer in the air around him. With each step he draws nearer, more detail can be revealed through the shimmer in the air.]
[Footsteps can now be heard as the figure closes towards the camera. The echos of them filling the silent void. The silence continues as the figure stops facing the camera. The echoes fade slowly as the camera pans to the figure. The wind blows the tail of his jacket viciously as the camera pans up and we can see the figure before us is the man they call the "Ace of
Hearts". The sand from the desert wind has blown up and deposited a small layer upon the side of Ace's face. The sand is covering the five o'clock shadow that graces the right side of his face. He brings a hand up and wipes some of the sand away from his cheek. His piercing deep blue, determined eyes glare into the camera's lens.]
|Ace of Hearts|: There comes a time in a man s life when he looks deep within his soul and analyzes his future and career alike. This time in my life stands before me here today.
[He smirks to the camera as he licks his lips to get the much-needed moisture to them.] |Ace of Hearts|: I stand here before the fans and wrestlers alike of the NeWF. I stand here in Las Vegas at the crossroads in my illustrious career. [Ace motions to the desert road before him as he brings his arms up and displays the three roads for all to see. He is standing directly in the fork, in the middle of the road. Three roads branch off of this fork. One, the one to the right, is a small dirt road. It seems to look hardly traveled. The second is a small gravel road. This one lies to the far left and appears to be well traveled with ruts cut into and through the natural roadwork. The third, it is the one in the center. It is the concrete monster that travels throughout the land.]
|Ace of Hearts|: Having torched the path s of the independent federations recently. I have full-filled the goals I had set forth for myself. In doing so, I have been advised to look for a greater challenge. To look for an organization that is capable of handling a man of my talent. I have been advised that the challenges that I seek may just lie within this very organization.
[Ace smirks as his piercing blue eyes gaze into the camera.] |Ace of Hearts|: The NeWF, a talent filled roster of men that are respected throughout the wrestling industry. Men of the likes of "Mr UEW" Tyrone
Parker, "Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin, and "Superstar" Sean Cage. These men, some of which, the greatest professionals to ever grace the squared circle. They are legends in their time.
[Ace begins to walk forward a few steps as he never looses his eye contact with the camera.]
|Ace of Hearts|: Until now... [Ace raises his arms and crosses them across his thick chest. He glares confidently into the camera.]
|Ace of Hearts|: For upon my signing, a look of concern has crossed the faces of the many men who consider themselves legends. It would seem as if their lives are about to change. [pauses] Do they fear the newcomer?
[He looks to the camera with a confident gaze.] |Ace of Hearts|: Probably not...but soon they will! [Ace brushes his hair back and wipes the side of his face. He starts walking down the paved road and stops as he passes the cameraman. He looks to the camera as it gets a tight shot of him.]
|Ace of Hearts|: For I have chosen the path for which I shall travel. This path leads me directly through the ranks of the NeWF. Quite a daring feat to say the least.
[Ace shoots that familiar to some, and soon to be familiar to you, infamous smirk towards the camera as he gazes intently into the lens before he continues.]
|Ace of Hearts|: NeWF, that golden strap that you cherish so. It is the ultimate prize, and yet. The biggest bullseye and target around no matter who is wearing it. Being the champion you are the man of the moment, the man to beat. Granted, I am just a "rookie" to this organization as I have yet to earn my right to challenge anyone here. However, in the weeks to come...you need to keep your eyes upon me. For it won't be long until I will slide up the ranks of the NeWF. And before you know it, I'll be challenging for that championship strap.
[Ace laughs confidently into the camera as he hopes the wrestlers and fans of the NeWF are listening intently.]
|Ace of Hearts|: Will you boys of the NeWF be ready for my challenge? [Smiles] |Ace of Hearts|: Cause I will be ready for yours... [Ace stops walking down the concrete road. He turns towards to face the camera with a sheer look of determination.]
|Ace of Hearts|: I shall take my journey through the WWO one-day at a time. One step at a time if need be. So as to end my quest upon my ultimate goal. This goal being the NeWF World Championship. Choosing to live this vision, this quest as it is, shall be a large undertaking that I feel that I am very determined to finish.
[Dust from the wind begins to pick up and start blowing heavily. Ace has to raise his voice to be heard from the loud sounds of the wind howling in the background. Off in the distance, beyond Ace's right shoulder. Storm clouds begin to roll into view. The unmistakable crack of lightning can be seen over his right shoulder as the storms grows nearer.]
|Ace of Hearts|: You see boys, my journey here in the NeWF begins on the eighth of April. But I intend on having you all feel my presence before my arrival.
[Smirks] |Ace of Hearts|: Now, you may look to your television screen and think to yourself that this "rookie" is brash and over-confident! But you see I have tasted success and failure. Success is an ever so sweet taste and
Failure.... Well failure is very bitter and heart wrenching. And yet I have persevered! [Ace points to the camera. He knows the wrestlers of the NeWF are now fearing the man they see before them. As they should...]
|Ace of Hearts|: Boys, I have the ability! I have the determination! And I damn sure have the attitude...
[The storm clouds now linger overhead as the wind blows stronger. Forcing Ace to yell to be heard over the aggressive winds.] |Ace of Hearts|: ...to run to the top of the NeWF!!! [Ace smiles as he looks to the sky.] |Ace of Hearts|: So be on the lookout boys, cause I'm coming! [Ace laughs as he slowly starts walking towards the city lights. The camera follows him a ways until the screen goes black and is replaced with a NeWF logo. The logo burns a bright neon red as the screen suddenly lights up as a lightening bolt slams down from the heavens and strikes the NeWF logo setting it ablaze. The logo quickly goes up in flames as the camera fades to black.]
============================================================== "Excessive Force" Eric Vanguard ============================================================== [The camera fades into a dimly lit office somewhere in the confines of a State Correctional Facility in Southern California. It is a modern office with shelves stocked with thick law manuals and a painting of a muscular prison guard surrounded by four men in the state's orange jumpsuits. A long onyx desk stretches the length of your screen, it's top strewn with opened files, crumpled papers, and broken pencils. Behind the desk is a large leather bound chair that stirs a little as the camera moves a bit closer.]
[A large wall of video monitors provides a backdrop for the chair as a strong glove clad hand reaches out to scoop a remote control from the desktop. In a matter of seconds the room is illuminated as the video wall springs to life an an image appears of NeWF Superstar "Showtime" Matt
Stone. The boyish good looks of the Hollywood Actor turned wrestler are soon tattooed as a hand reaches into the monitor and brands his face with a label reading "Vanguard's Most Wanted"]
[Suddenly the chair turns slowly to reveal an unshaven brut of a man, wearing a leather jacket that seems to blend in with the large chair. He looks adjusts his dark black shades as he looks around the desk for a file folder of some sort. As you glance at the man, and then at the picture alongside the desk you recognize him as the security guard in the painting.
Finally he finds the folder and opens it up, speaking in a voice filled with the anger of 100,000 men.]
Man: "Name....Showtime Matt Stone. Height....6 foot 6 inches. Weight....275 pounds. His crime....being in the wrong place at the wrong time." [The guard, Excessive Force Eric Vanguard pushes the folder shut and slowly rises, making his way around the desk to sit on it's edge.]
Eric Vanguard: "For the past couple of years I have floundered in what many people would call the minor leagues of professional wrestling. I have been laughed at, I have been humiliated, and quite frankly I have been driven just a little bit over the edge. For the past couple of years I have stood
NOT in the spotlight, basking in the cheers or jeers of millions and millions of fans worldwide, but instead in the shadows of some of wrestling greatest egomaniacs. Take Bradley Waubash III for example..."
[The image on the screen switches to that off a blonde hair, blue eyed, adonis of a man. He stands in the middle of a ring with a World Title held high above his head, as Eric Vanguard stands in the background looking on.]
Eric Vanguard: "Bradley Waubash III is a superb athlete that very few could ever come close to competing with. I on the other hand was better, he knew that - and for three years he held me down. He thrived on standing in a spotlight that time after time I tried to reclaim, but never could. He drove me over the edge. It was because of Bradley Waubash III that I ran a fellow professional wrestling, Bill "The Ironman" Macragge, over with a
1996 Lincoln Continental. It was because of Bradley Waubash III that I drove a man by the name of Tantrum to the brink on insanity, insisting that he killed an innocent man in cold blood. It is because of Bradley Waubash
III that you Matt Stone will suffer." [The image on the screen once again flickers and it shows a duo of men that could easily be mistaken for twins. They stand side by side with short blonde military crewcuts, and physiques that anyone would be envious of.]
Eric Vanguard: "The men you see behind you are not twins, but instead one is a creation of yours truly, Eric Vanguard. He was a pawn - a tool in my quest to drive Tantrum beyond his limits, a genetic freak that was bigger, quicker, and what I felt was smarter than his look alike. Unfortunately he was smarter than I realized, and soon began to believe the innocent please of Tantrum. He listened to the cries, and become weak of heart and bullheaded, thus leaving me in a search for a new direction. It is because of him that my precious Cellblock was taken away from me. It is because of him that YOU Matt Stone will suffer."
[The screen once again switches to show a monster of a man draped in an old pagan goat skin. His bright red eyes illuminate the dark background of the screen as a minister kneels before him.]
Eric Vanguard: "FREEK is the one man that I could never beat. I stood alongside FREEK and helped him to dismantle the FEW's esteemed President
[name withheld]. I have him a day in the sun that he would never return to me. Instead he cast me away into the dark confines of a casket. Instead he forced me deeper into the shadows, into obscurity. It is because of people like him that my heart is filled with jealousy, with distain, with rage. It is because of him that YOU Matt Stone will suffer!"
[Eric Vanguard raises a hand and flips off the monitors via remote control as the words "X-Tremely Dangerous" appear on the monitor, the date April
1st, 2000 emblazoned below it.] Eric Vanguard: "I do not use the actions of these men as excuses for my shortcomings. Instead I use their exploitation of my mistakes to make me stronger. It is because of their dishonorable deeds and actions that my heart is filled with the fury that will end your career before it even takes off Matt. When we step into the ring on April 1st in Martinsville, IN
I will not be held responsible for we are both innocent parties. These are the men that are guilty of crimes, these are the individuals that built the machine that stands before, there are the people that spell your defeat.
Because Matt Stone I will NOT sink into the shadows again, and I REFUSE not to have my name shouted from the mountains as the most dominant force in
NeWF. So I will come to Martinsville with all my hate, all my rage, and all my anger...and I will take back MY spotlight. Next Saturday I will begin my personal quest for glory by means necessary, even if I have to use....EXCESSIVE FORCE!"
[Vanguard lifts his trademark nightstick off of the desktop, beating it against his palm as the image fades to black.]
============================================================== The Big Chill ============================================================== (Scene opens in an empty arena. The camera pans around the arena and finally zooms in on the ring. The Big Chill stands in the ring, leaning on the ring ropes, looking out.)
TBC: So the first sacrificial lamb has been chosen. Step right up, Humpty Dumpty, you're the next contestant on "End you Career!", the fun new game show brought to you by the T, the B and the C, the Big Chill. At least I'll be getting some sort of work out from you, big boy. But I've actually seen your disgusting flabby body so I won't be getting my hopes up to high for a good match. I might just break more of a sweat jogging to ringside than actually beating you like the redheaded step-child you remind me of! You see this.....
(TBC extends his arms out and turns from side to side.) TBC: This is my house. In my house, my rules apply. I don't come here for the sport, I don't come here for the fun of it. I come here for one reason and one reason only: to prove my dominance time and time again. That's it.
And by you stepping into the ring with me, you are questioning my ability. Cuz only a fool wrestles a man that he knows can beat him. You actually believe you have a chance or you would have signed the contract. But by signing to meet me, you have insulted me. And it's not wise to get me angry. You wouldn't like me when I'm angry.
(TBC smiles smugly.) TBC: It all seems so innocent. Humpty Dumpty, fat ass and all. Humpty Dumpty, going to take a big fall. But that would be too easy to believe. I'm not going to fall for it. Oh no. I take a look at you, listen to your inane babbling and I wonder: "could a man, and I use that in the loosest possible manner, actually be this pathetic?" While your physique makes me say yes, I look a little deeper and think differently. I see a man who enjoyed scaring a teenager. I see a man who enjoys being an egg. I see a man who is trying ever so hard to make people think he is harmless. I see a deranged man and those are the most dangerous. So here's what I think I'm going to do, Dumpty. I think I'm going to treat you like you are the greatest wrestler alive. I'm going to go into this match thinking I need to kill you to beat you. And I'm going to aim to do it.
(The smile on TBC's face disappears slowly.) TBC: But it's not because I have an issue to settle with you, Dumpty. No, in reality, you're not important enough for me to truly worry about. The reason I'm going to destroy your disgusting fat body is because I am tired of having to jumping through hoops. I guess certain people weren't paying attention when to me last time. It seems as though they just considered it another chump talking out of the wrong end. Well, its all well and good, because Dumpty's fat, bleeding carcass is not going to be on my conscience.
No, I'm not the one who decided to put the T, the B and the C in there with the T, the O, and the N. I guess I'm going to have teach another poor slob the lesson of a lifetime.
(TBC pauses.) TBC: This shouldn't be just a lesson for the him. No. This should be a lesson for ... (TBC pauses and chuckles.) TBC:...(sarcastically) "Superstar" Sean Cage. That's about as funny as calling yourself the "main event". Chump, I've read your bio and I've seen the tapes. Let me clue you in on something: this ain't the NBA where every little touch gets a whistle blown. This ain't some rinky dink promotion where you get $25 per match and my 80 year old Aunt Mary is the hardcore champ. This is the big leagues and I'm the MVP! Think you got a little size on me? Think its an advantage for you? Watch how I cut old Bubba down to size. Then you'll understand what kind of fight you're in for. Pay attention. As a matter of fact, everyone pay attention. Even with the number of mental midgets, and trust me, there are plenty around here in the
NeWF, 2 things should become crystal clear. One, never get on TBC's bad side and two, never step to the T, the B and the C, cuz you'll end up wanting to D, I, E!
(Scene fades to black.)
