Sunday Night Mayhem [June 4, 2000] (Part 2 of 7)

7 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We return from commercials to our announcers live at ringside.] EC: Welcome back to Sunday Night Mayhem and already we have seen some major happenings. LW: So much for a clean slate! EC: That is true Larry. President Hopper came out to proclaim to the world the clean slate he promised for all the NeWF superstars, yet was interrupted by someone that could only be called a blast from the past!
LW: Vic Valiant! And boy was he pissed! EC: He sure was! Citing past problems with the current owner, Valiant said he signed a contract with Colt Anderson just hoping Hopper would return in some way.
LW: And Hopper was too scared to do it himself, so he decided to use his position to get rid of someone who was a threat!
EC: Well that may be how you saw it "Crusher", but I saw Hopper wanting his top draw to take out the trash, so to speak.
LW: Speaking of trash, we get to watch Don Gately in action! EC: Hey! That just wasn't even right. LW: Like I care. This guy has been a silent member of NeWF and somehow he ends up in the tournament? I mean what is Hopper thinking except to give
Chill an easy walk through for this round.
EC: I don't see it that way at all. Hopper said this was a blind draw and the fact Gately got in shows it WAS a blind draw. So Chill probably is not getting a "free pass" as you seem to think. Gately could pull off an upset here.
LW: Maybe if Chill got hit by a truck or something! EC: You are just pathetic sometimes. LW: I got to be me. And if being me is deemed pathetic by you, then I must feel sorry for what you think of yourself!
[Eric just shakes his head and tries to move on as Larry has a cocky smile on his face.] EC: Well, let's head to the ring now and our ring announcer Steven Stewert! [The screen cuts to the ring where Steven Stewert is standing in the center holding the microphone and a index card.]
SS: Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is a one fall twenty minutes time limit match. It is a First Round match in the National Heavyweight Title
Tournament!
[Crowd pop.] SS: Introducing first, he is one of the first men to gain recognition as a contender in NeWF and tonight he looks to take his first step towards fulfilling that potential. Ladies and gentlemen, I give you...
T H E B I G C H I L L ! ! ! ("Calm like a Bomb" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play as the The
Big Chill walks out from the backstage entrance and stands still. His blonde hair is cut short into a Caesar cut and sports a neatly trimmed blonde goatee. TBC is wearing a tank top which reads "The T, the B and the
C" on the front, black wrestling trunks, black wrestling boots, a "RoT"
doo-rag and black Oakley sunglasses. TBC raises one arm as pyrotechnics explode around him. The crowd breaks into cheers. He begins to walk to the ring. As TBC walks by a camera, he says "Gotta start all over again? Dem's the breaks!" TBC enters the ring. TBC mounts the second turnbuckle and raises his arms in the air. The crowd cheers more as TBC looks over the crowd. He hops off and goes to the ring announcer and demands the mic.)
TBC: You know, this is getting to be a bad habit here. Every time, The Big
Chill gets rolling they throw him back into the pack. In past days, this would have upset me. This would have caused me to go off on a tangent and scream and get all hot under the collar. Luckily, the T, the B and the C doesn't get riled up as easily. So, those in charge want to set up another
King of the Hill and see who gets to the top? That's fine and dandy with me. It's not like its the first time I had to fight from the bottom up and it won't be the last I'm sure. What's a man to do?
(TBC pauses.) TBC: Let em tell you what I'm going to do. I'm going to be punching tickets and not waiting long enough to ask for directions. People are going to get in my way, people are going to get knocked down. You want a piece of me, I hope you're fully insured.
(TBC pauses momentarily and takes a few steps toward Gately.) TBC: Gately, let me fill you in something. TBC has matured. Yes, he has. In the past, I would have told you that I am the real deal, the legend slayer, the man who has dropped more people than Muhammad Ali. I would have told you that the T, the B and the C means "This Boy Cooks!" All ego driven ranting. But there is something I do have to tell you. I wouldn't feel right if I didn't. This match ain't going to be easy. Ask any of those chumps that thought it was going to be a cake walk. Go ask them. They're all at Memorial Hospital, licking their wounds, wondering how so much aggression could be found in one man. You had better have prepared for the fight of your life. You should have sat back, watched some video of me and said to yourself "man, o man, this guy doesn't play around. I better get a little more serious." I've killed more careers than cancer and if you ain't ready, yours is done as well. Why am I so confident? I'll let you in on the worst kept secret in professional wrestling: Don't step to the T, the B and the C, cuz if you do, you'll D, I, E!
(TBC takes another step to Gately.) TBC: So, Gately, I hope you are ready. There is a reason they call me
T-nice, and while I hate to admit, I'm taking out you suckers and you don't know how I did it! Let's see who brings it first.
[TBC flips the mic over his shoulder and takes off his entrance gear. Steven Stewert bends over and picks it up to continue as the announcers are overheard over the screen.]
EC: The Big Chill seems ready for this one! LW: And Gately is nothing but a smear on the road to glory for him! I love it when a plan comes together. I wonder if Chill has scouted his second round opponents yet?
EC: I doubt he is thinking that far ahead. LW: Yeah right, I would be. EC: Well here comes Gately! [Crowd pop.] SS: And his opponent! He is also one of the founding members of NeWF and a fan favorite wherever he wrestles. Here is
D O N G A T E L Y ! ! ! [Before Stewert is even done announcing his name, Gately comes running out of the entrance way towards the ring. Chill has his back to the aisle as he yells at ringside fans. Chill seems to notice something out of the corner of his eye.]
LW: Gately takes off running and his music never got the chance to cue up! This boy is fired up! Chill is ready to meet the sprinting man! [DING!! DING!! DING!!] EC: Ouch!! LW: TBC nails Gately with a huge running lariat! Gately is stunned, trying to get his wits about him as TBC lifts him up by the hair and sends him to the ropes again.
EC: Don ducks under another lariat and both men come running off the ropes once again, TBC catches Gately again with a back body drop, sending Don high in to the air. TBC immediately goes back at him, lifting him up into a double-underhook suplex. TBC jumps on him again with some fists to the head as referee Curt Shingle breaks it up!
LW: Give it to him Chill! Ha, The Big Chill is all over him! EC: TBC becoming more aggressive in the early stages of this bout. TBC setting Gately into position again, and nails him with a jumping piledriver, bouncing Gately's skull off the mat. Gately still remaining aggressive picks Gately back up and leans him in to the corner where he proceeds to drill Gately in the midsection with some vicious jabs. I don't know what's gotten in to TBC tonight, but he's certainly came to wrestle tonight in this first round match up.
LW: He knows this may be his big chance at the title! Look him go! Wailing
Gately with stiff shots to the ribs. Referee Curt Shingle breaks it up as
Gately drops to his knees in obvious pain. TBC lifts him right back up, and sends him to the opposite rope with an Irish whip.
EC:WOW!! TBC delivers a back body drop sending Don Gately out of the ring and on to the cold floor! Gately's body just bounced off the concrete! He could be seriously hurt! The Big Chill turns back showing no remorse and plays the crowd to a chorus of boos. Gately's just starting to move around as TBC climbs outside of the ring to deliver more punishment!
LW: TBC with two huge forearms to the back of Don Gately. Gately's then tossed back in to the ring and TBC follows, TBC sets up Gately again, MY
GOD!! What a sit down powerbomb by The Big Chill! He goes for the cover...
1 . . . 2 . . . LW: Kickout by Don Gately!! EC: Did you see Don's head bounce off the mat with that vicious powerbomb? He hasn't even gotten in any offense yet! TBC is on his game tonight folks! Gately's starting to get up as TBC kicks him stiffly in the ribs rolling him over in pain. TBC takes a breather for a moment as Gately is just starting to get up.
LW: Man, I sure hope the main event is like this! EC: I wouldn't expect any less, an outsider trying to fight his way in to
NeWF? I bet Adam Rogers is gonna go all out!
LW: I wouldn't count of V3 either buddy, this guy's got a real mean streak in him! But the fans are sure looking forward to it!
EC: Don Gately is on his feet as TBC takes notice and charges him with a big clothesline, Gately ducks under it and catches TBC with a drop toe hold, TBC's through bouncing off the second rope as he kicks around in pain. This gets a rise from the crowd as Gately moves in to control. He brings TBC to his feet and nails him in the chest with a heavy drop kick sending TBC through the ropes to the outside of the ring. TBC is on his feet on the outside as Gately leaps to the top rope, and connects with a suicide dive!! A big shift in control as Gately connects with a huge maneuver there!
LW: TBC is just playing with him, The Big Chill is truly the one in control, he's just playing around with him.
EC: It certainly doesn't look that way to me or the fans for they went nuts with that suicide dive from the top. Gately slams TBC's head in to the steel ring post sending TBC staggering. Don Gately tosses The Big Chill back into the ring and he slides in after him, connecting with some jabs to the face. He throws a stiff kick to TBC's midsection and connects with a soviet suplex to the delight of the fans. Gately gets TBC in position with a snapmare takedown and then heads towards the turnbuckle, he stands on top, then goes for a splash, but TBC rolls out of the way!
LW: See! What'd I tell you! See how Gately just flopped off the mat! TBC had this planned all the way out! TBC rises to his feet and drops a big leg on to Gately laying on the mat. TBC lifts Gately up and whips him to the ropes, he gets set for a back body drop but telegraphs it and Gately hops over him in to a sunset flip!
1 . . . 2 . . . LW: Kickout by TBC!! Both men are up immediately and they tie up, jockeying for position, Gately hits an arm drag, both men up quickly,
Gately hits another arm drag. Both men up quickly again, Gately ducks under a right cross and moves into a waistlock from behind, quickly TBC sends a swift reverse kick to the groin of Don Gately, sending him down and getting a big chorus of boos from the crowd.
EC: TBC regains the advantage referee Curt Shingle is reprimanding TBC, threatening to end the match if it happens again.
LW: What did he do wrong? Shingle has to call this down the middle! EC: It sure looks like he is to me as TBC drops a big elbow on to the chest of Gately and goes for the cover,
1 . . . 2 . . . EC: Gately gets the shoulder up! Gately has put up with his share of punishment so far here, and I think he may be done real soon! The Big
Chill, throws Gately to the corner, charges at him for a big splash, Gately dodges out of the way! And he's on the attack throwing a series of punches into TBC in the corner ! TBC is reeling as Don Gately is throwing some huge shots to the body and head of The Big Chill, and the fans are loving it!
Gately pulls him out of the corner, and hits him with a big suspended suplex. Now he's putting TBC in the figure four as the crowd is still going wild! The Big Chill is obviously in a huge amount of pain here!
LW: He's gonna reach the ropes! TBC is way to strong to have this done to him! EC: It looks like Gately may have him finished! LW: Oh what do you know anyway? TBC is just one reach away from the ropes! He's showing some resiliency here! EC: Don Gately breaks hold before TBC reaches the ropes! Don Gately lifts
TBC up now, and brings him over to the turnbuckle. He sits TBC on the top has he plays the crowd.
LW: He looks like he's going for a superlex! There's no way he could get
TBC over! EC: If Gately could hit a suplex off the top, this match would be over! Gately is standing on the top rope now as the crowd gets louder with anticipation. Gately has TBC in position, he goes for the lift..NO! TBC blocks it and throws and elbow to the face of Don Gately!
LW: See! I told you! There's no way he could throw TBC off the top! The
Big Chill has Gately in a waistlock now, he turns him around..
EC: OH MY GOD!! Deep Freeze from the top rope! A huge belly to belly suplex from the top rope! Gately's not moving on the mat! TBC moves in for the cover..
1 . . . 2 . . . 3!! EC: The Big Chill is your winner!! What a belly to belly suplex! From the top rope even!! LW: I told ya so all along, I knew TBC would have no problem. EC: Well, I wouldn't say he had no problem but he certainly showed us something tonight. And folks, there's more where that came from, tonight we have a double main event, in which we will see Vicious Vic Valiant up against Adam Rogers! Roger's spot in the tourney will be up against the contract Colt Anderson gave to V3!! This is gonna be an exciting night in the Ne...What the Hell?!?!?
["Dragula" by Rob Zombie plays over the sound system and the crowd explodes into a frenzied mix of boos and cheers. The camera cuts to the curtain from the broadcast position. War Child appears from the behind the curtain, carrying a gym bag. War Child is wearing an NeWF "War Child distributor of pain" T-shirt, black cut-off Jean shorts, black sunglasses and his customary black, leather trench coat. War Child walks methodically to the ring, hops up onto the ring apron and steps over the top rope. He gets a mic from the announcer, walks to the center of the ring and drops the bag down at his feat. His music fades.]
"The Extreme Warrior" War Child: I have been placed into the NeWF National title tournament. But one wonders how much value the National title now has. The company has gone from Chris Hopper, to Colt Anderson, back to
Chris Hopper again. This company is full of corruption, scandal and backstabbers. The Extreme lowest of low life dwell with the NeWF's talent roster, all the way to the offices of the front office. It is a sess pool of locker room politics and ego. I am here to put a little dignity back into this company. I am here to clense the NeWF of all I deem as corrupt. I am here to drive the scum back to the gutters and make the National Title have value again. To make it mean something. So far the tournament has been haulted. We'd get half way through it, then someone pulls the plug. IT's as if someone is deliberately trying to stop the growth of what could be a great company. But due to this garbage going around the NeWF and all of its titles have ZERO credibility. I am here to change that, by winning the
National title tournament. But at the same time I don't believe the NeWF's fans need to wait that long. So I am interjecting something with dignity, respect and meaning to it right NOW.....
[War Child picks up the bag, opens it and pulls out a shiny, golden title belt.] "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: You see this! The last time I was in
Japan, I won the Japanese Hardcore title at the Karuken Hall. As far as I'm concerned this belt has meaning. Unlike the pieces of leather they call titles around here. This is a title what I and all wrestling fans can be proud of. The NeWF titles are meaningless, worthless pieces of s[censored]t. I spit on the titles and any future holders of the belts.
With this said. I announce that I am on a mission. I could care less about the NeWF national title. It means as much to me, as the wrestlers backstage. But my mission is to do my best to put some meaning and worth into titles that are not worth the BRASS they are made of. As of right now this hardcore title is the single most prestigious title in the NeWF. This is the one belt that is worth it's weight in gold in the NeWF. I suggest if you want a title with value, you go for this one. Otherwise you are fighting for nothing. Actually if you were to go buy a belt from Sears or
JC Penny... you would find another belt worth more than the stinkin' titles they have around here.
[War Child places the hardcore title around his waist] "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: Now to my mission... My mission starts tonight with Jack Mannon. I have nothing personal against Jack.... But he is the biggest hypocrite in the NeWF. He comes out and acts all wholesome, trying to reclaim past glory. Well, Mr. Mannon, you should've let all of your past glory remain in your head. You should have never entered the
NeWF. You should've stayed at home, watching your Karate Kid movie series on DVD and you would've been safe from your ultimate destruction. Now you've been placed right on the path of destruction. And just like Brandon
Franklin, who won't re-sign a contract with the NeWF out of fear of me, I will send you back to the retirement you came out of to reclaim your glory.
Your feeble martial arts won't save you. If you insist on using them, hell
I grew up in the Orient. I learned the very crap you use. You want to use it, I can go toe to toe with an obvious weakling like you.
[War Child removes his sunglasses and begins laughing] "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: Ha ha ha, which brings me to Brandon
Franklin. I hear you are tucking your tail between your legs and heading out of the NeWF. Why? Brandon, because you are a COWARD! You knew I'd catch up with you so you opted to do what I told you to do when our war first started. You are running away. I'm glad to see you're a smart man. Get as far away as possible, because the war machine may not see you, but it will not forget you. If our paths cross again. You will finally face your destruction!
[War Child places his sunglasses back on, still laughing, he points to the camera] "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: Jack Mannon, you are the first step in my new mission to bring meaning to the National Title. You have been targeted by the most Extreme individual in wrestling today. I hope you have said your prayers to whatever God you worship. I hope you have asked forgiveness for all of your sins. Awwwwe f[censored]k it, it doesn't matter what you do because I'm taken you straight to hell and back. After that I will be one step closer to putting meaning back into the NeWF and it's titles. And you will never, ever be the same again. Ash to ash, dust to dust... Fade to black.
[War Child exits the ring as the announcer chime in over the screen.] EC: War Child seems to have decided to name HIMSELF as Champion in NeWF! LW: Well he is one of the best we have, but he better watch who he calls out. EC: Fans we have to take a commercial break, but when we return we will have more National Tournament Action! Stick around here for more Sunday
Night Mayhem!
[Cut to commercials. As they run, the following NeWF commercial runs.] (The camera fades in on a Back Jeep pulling up to a gym. It screeches to a halt in the parking space labeled "Adam Rogers". It shuts down and the door opens. Out walks... Predator? He is carrying a black Gym bag and a bottle of water. He starts walking towards the building when he notices the camera.)
Predator: Oh, you. Cameraman: Hello, Mr. Predator. Do you have any comment on the rumors going on in the back?
Predator: What rumors? (Predator turns towards the camera, intrigued) Cameraman: Their has been some talk that you'll be an easy win in the tournament. (Camera starts to shake as the cameraman is obviously terrified.) Predator: Now, who has been saying that? Is it that Roy Rogers guy? Cameraman: W-well... if you mean Mr. Adam Rogers, yes. Predator: Whatever. (Predator takes a sip of water and dumps the rest on his head. He wipes off his face and flings back his dreadlocks, smiling, amused.)
Predator: You see, the reason Roy is making up all these rumors is because he's truly terrified of me. Who could blame him?
Cameraman: R-right. Umm... so, you're not at all... shaken? (Predator stares directly at the camera and the cameraman knows he's made a mistake.) Predator: No. See, I may be a "marked man" in this tournament, but they don't call me Predator for nothing! You don't hunt the hunter!
(Predator enters the gym and the cameraman tries to fallow, but the door is slammed on him. He stumbles back, and the camera hits the ground, going to snow.]
