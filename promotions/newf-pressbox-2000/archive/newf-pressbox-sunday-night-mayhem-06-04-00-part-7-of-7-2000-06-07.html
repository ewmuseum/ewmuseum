Sunday Night Mayhem [June 4, 2000] (Part 7 of 7)

7 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We return from commercials to our announcers live at ringside.] EC: Fans we are ready for tonight's second Main Event Match of the evening! This one has TONS of meaning!
LW: It sure does! EC: Let's take a look at what set this match up. Vic Valiant had came out and interrupted President Hopper and informed Hopper of his valid contract that says he got a National Title Shot. Hopper said he would decide when that shot would happen, then we pick it up with this footage...
[The screen goes black and the following footage airs. In the lower right-hand corner, we see a graphic written in small font that says
"earlier tonight"] [Hopper tosses the contract back at Vic and hits him in the chest and turns to leave the ring. Valiant rushes over and grabs Hopper's shoulders, whirling him around.]
Valiant: Hopper, you dirty son of a bitch! I will be in that damn tournament if I have to fill every room in the local hospital to do it.
You'll have to call off the tournament because there won't be any competitors left! You'll regret it, Hopper, if it's the last thing I ever do!
[Hopper had began to turn around again, but stopped in his tracks as Valiant had said his last phrase. Hopper turned around slowly and raised the mic again.] President Hopper: Interesting. Very interesting idea Vic. Valiant: What the hell are you talking about? President Hopper: Well honestly everything you said meant nothing to me until that last phrase, "if it is the last thing I ever do". See that is the only way I will even give you a chance here.
[Valiant is incensed with anger as he seems to not be following.] President Hopper: I will put a spot in the tourney up in a match. You win the match, you take the place of the wrestler you face tonight. If you lose...
[Crowd seems to be tirelessly waiting...] President Hopper: THEN THE CONTRACT IS VOID AND YOU ARE GONE FOREVER!!! {The crowd erupts in an insanely loud face pop.] President Hopper: Do you have the balls to accept the wager? Valiant: Two types of people would take a wager like that without really knowing all the stakes: a fool, like these "brave" people who work for you.
[The crowd boos at the insult of the roster.] Valiant: Or someone like me, who just doesn't give a damn. So yes, Chris,
I accept your challenge. Who is my opponent?
[Hopper pauses and smiles.] President Hopper: Just a guy that goes by the name... "T H E N A T U R A L" A D A M R O G E R S ! ! ! [The crowd erupts in the loudest face pop of the night as Valiant looks upset at the choice, knowing Rogers is the top good guy in the league.]
Valiant: What?! Rogers? President Hopper: Yes! Beat him and it is simple, you're in. Lose, and
YOU'RE GONE! "Double V" Vs. "The Natural!" It is all in your court now
Vic! Good luck!
[The footage freezes and then the screen fades back to the announcers, who are sitting at ringside.]
EC: And that leads us to where we are right now! Adam Rogers against Vic
Valiant. This one has everything! Rogers could find himself out of the
National Title Tournament. However, Valiant could find himself UNEMPLOYED if he loses!
LW: This is Hopper's attempt to make sure that Valiant is stopped before he become a force. Unfortunately, I think it is too late for Hopper.
Valiant is already too strong for Hopper to stop. EC: Well Valiant is cocky and seems ready to go here, Hopper does seem to help his cause by using Rogers. Adam has been money in big matches.
LW: Just like my ex-wife's bank account...all money eventually runs out. Rogers may have agreed to write a check that his body can't cash! EC: We'll see, Let's go to ring announcer Steven Stewert and get this thing started! [The screen cuts to the ring where Steven Stewert is standing in the center holding the microphone and a index card.]
SS: Ladies and Gentlemen! This is the MAIN EVENT OF THE EVENING! The second of tonight's two main event matches is a match that pits a spot in the National Title Tourney against the very contract of employment. This match is a one-fall, NO TIME LIMIT match!
[Major Crowd pop.] SS: Introducing first! The man who dared to challenge the authority of our returning President and Owner. He is the man who wants to change things right now by becoming the man in NeWF. Here is...
"T R I P L E V" V I C I O U S V I C V A L I A N T ! ! ! [An ambulance siren blares over the PA. The boos start as "Kashmir" by Led
Zeppelin mixes in with the sirens.]
("V3" Vicious Vic Valiant steps out in the entranceway, Katia Syan by his side. Dark glasses cover his eyes as he looks stone-faced at the jeering crowd. He walks slowing towards the ring, never breaking his focus. Fans attempt to reach out and touch him, but he simply ignores them.)
(When he arrives at the ring, he hands Katia his glasses, takes her hand, and leads her to the corner. He deposits the chain in the corner and steps between the ropes. He raises his right arm slowly and holds it there while fireworks in the shape of three "V"s" interlocking. The fireworks die down, and immediately following...)
[Simultaneously, the sound of the ambulance crashing coincides with the lights going out.] [....] (The lights come back up to find Vic in his corner, leaning up against the turnbuckles.) EC: Valiant seems confident and ready, but can he beat the NeWF's "Money Man"? LW: Beat him" The question should be whether or not Rogers will SURVIVE this match! EC: Now I have no doubt that Rogers can not only survive, but beat this loud mouthed freak!
LW: Well I know what Valiant is capable of and that loser Rogers has no shot. You were still in diapers when Valiant was just getting started in this business.
EC: Well here comes the NeWF's "Defender"! [Crowd pop.] SS: And his opponent! The most popular wrestler in NeWF history and the number one contender for the National Title! I give you...
"T H E N A T U R A L" A D A M R O G E R S ! ! ! ["Smoke On The Water" by Deep Purple begins to blare out across the arena. Adam Rogers comes through the curtain to a monster crowd pop. He walks to the ring as he slaps the hands of ringside fans. the fans are eating this guy up! Adam reaches the ring and stares down Valiant. He slides in and
Valiant immediately rushes over and nails him with an elbow and starts kicking him down.]
EC: THIS ONE GETS OFF TO QUICK START!!! EC: Valiant elbowed him down and is kicking him! Referee Herman Gehl is over and trying to pull Vic off! Rogers falls back outside the ring and just sits up. He seems shocked, but just nods his head.
LW: What a moron. EC: I don't think he expected an early attack. LW: Like I said, a moron. You ALWAYS expect an attack. Please! EC: Well Rogers has slid back inside and seems ready for any other sneaky moves here as they circle one another.
LW: Vic Valiant....what a guy! He almost reminds me of me when I wrestled. EC: Well now THAT is something to be proud of. Somebody tell Vic to change before he ends up like Larry over here.
LW: HEY! EC: They lock up and Valiant just shoves Rogers down to the mat hard! LW: He is a bit taller and more muscular than Rogers, so he should have won that battle. Rogers needs to stop fighting battles he can't win and just forfeit.
EC: Please! Rogers is just sizing him up. The crowd is beginning to get into this match, just listen to them!
[Crowd: Rogers! Rogers!] LW: They lock up again and Valiant immediately whirls around and schoolboys Rogers over for a quick pin attempt!
1 . . . 2 . . . th... EC: No! Valiant went for a quick kill there, but nothing doing! LW: Valiant steps back as Rogers gets up and look at the cockiness. I love this in a young wrestler!
EC: Valiant is just holding his tongue out in taunting mode as Rogers stares him down. Valiant is back again leaning on the corner, just waiting.
LW: Rogers should just run away! EC: Rogers fakes a lunge at a lock up and ducks Valiant's arms. He goes for a waist-lock, but Valiant counters with an elbow thrust back to the head! Rogers is down to a knee as Valiant continues his taunting!
[The camera shows Valiant mouthing at Rogers saying "That all you got old man?"] LW: Rogers is in trouble if Valiant gets inside that head of his. If that happens, wrap it up and send it home now.
EC: Rogers stands and just stares at Valiant, who seems to be laughing at the popular star!
LW: Why not, first match in NeWF and he is gonna beat the fans' favorite wrestler! What could be better?
EC: They lock up again! Valiant with an armbar! Shoulder thrust by
Valiant! And another! He is just hammering that shoulder area now!
LW: Rogers is no spring chicken, that's for sure. So anytime you can wear down those joints, you go for it.
EC: Valiant goes for a third shoulder thrust, but decides to try for
Somoan DDT! Rogers with a block and he pushes Valiant out of the ring!
[Crowd Roars for the turn-around.] LW: I wish those idiots would shut up. EC: Hey they actually pay your salary if you think about it. LW: They are sheep. Period. Sheep that go where we tell them to go. EC: Well Valiant smiles as he seems ready to get back inside the ring. Valiant smiles as he stands face to face with Rogers. LW: Scary thing is that both men look totally fresh! Neither can seem to psych-out the other! EC: Valiant just hauled off and slapped the taste out of Rogers' mouth! What a show of disrespect! [Crowd boos loudly at the slap.] LW: I love it! EC: Valiant goes again, but Rogers blocks it and lands a right jab to the cheek! Valiant staggers backwards somewhat due to that shot!
LW: Rogers with a right forearm shiver to the back of Valiant and the big man goes down to his knees!
[Crowd goes nuts for Rogers. A loud "Rogers" chant can be heard starting up again.] EC: LOW BLOW BY VALIANT!!! Valiant took a major short cut and now he drops a fist to the side of Rogers' head! Rogers is trying to stand, but
Valiant kicks him in the rib area!
LW: Look at the pain Rogers is in. Valiant is a smart man. EC: How so? LW: Well he is attacking the ribs and hurting his breathing. You take away some of the breath support, a man's body deteriorates quicker and leads to easier wins.
EC: I see. Well Valiant kicks him between the shoulder blades and then steps backwards against the ropes!
LW: YEAH! Kick that Dog baby! EC: Valiant sent Rogers over a foot off the ring with that kick to the ribs! Rogers may have a broken rib, he is holding the side of his torso oftly tight.
LW: Valiant garbs Rogers by the hair and gets him partially stood up and then lays the hammer down! Great work by "Triple V"!
EC: Rogers is down again and that was quite a violent blow to the head by
Valiant to Rogers! Roger is backed into the corner hard by Valiant!
Knife-edged chop by Valiant! [Crowd: WHHOOOOOOO!] LW: He took his feet off the ground! This guy has power! EC: Valiant grabs his head and performs a snap mare take down. He tries to get a standing headlock, but Rogers reverses it into a hammerlock!
[Crowd cheers the reversal.] LW: C'mon! He knows better than to go all technical with a third generation wrestler! EC: Rogers raises Valiant up and whips him into the ropes. The collide and Rogers hits the mat hard! Valiant over as Rogers bounces up right into a DDT! Rogers grabs the leg and tries a quick cover!
1 . . . 2 . . . thre... LW: WHAT?!?! This match should be over! EC: The referee only counted two! LW: What a screw-job! EC: Valiant jerks Rogers up off the mat and literally tosses him into the corner! Now he follows in with a knee to the stomach area!
LW: Smart move. He follows up all those kicks with a well placed knee. You can't ask for more than that! EC: Valiant with a chop! [Crowd: WHOOOOO!] LW: And another Chop! [Crowd: WHOOOOO!] EC: There is a third Chop! [Crowd: WHOOOOO!] EC: He is taking Rogers' mid-section and pulverizing it! LW: He is lifting him off the ground every time he hit him in the chest! Not a good sign if Rogers intends to wrestle this one out! EC: Valiant grabs the right shoulder of Rogers and goes for a whip across, but Rogers reverse it! Valiant hit hard in the corner and rebounds right into Rogers' arms!
[Crowd goes nuts for the reversal.] LW: Oh crap! EC: BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX BY ROGERS! What a turn around this has been! Valiant is beginning to get to his feet, but Rogers looks like a re-energized man! LW: This is why you take guys like this out early. They always get their crowd pushes. EC: Rogers grabs a handful of hair and raises Valiant up and he delivers a right cross to Valiant's jaw! The fans are loving this! Here is an Irish Whip!
LW: Valiant goes down from a collision! EC: Rogers keeps going and leaps over Valiant, and Valiant ducks his head for a move, but a bit too early....FRONT FACESLAM BY ROGERS! Here is a quick cover!
1 . . . 2 . . . thr... LW: HE KICKED OUT! That referee was counting way too fast for my taste! EC: Both men are up and Valiant blocks a punch attempt and drills Adam right between the eyes in that corner! Rogers flies OVER THE TOP ROPE!!!
LW: Man did he land awkwardly too! He may have twisted a knee! EC: Rogers tries to get to his feet He climbs up to the apron, but there is Valiant to meet him! Valiant grabs him by the head, but Rogers drops down and VALIANT HAS HIS THROAT DROPPED ON THAT TOP ROPE!
LW: Hot shot by Rogers! Valiant is down inside the ring! I never thought
Rogers would blatantly cheat like that!
EC: Like what? LW: Use the ropes against the throat like that! EC: Gosh you are so biased! Rogers slides in and grabs Valiant and puts him in suplex position. He tries to lift, but you can tell those ribs are killing him!
LW: I told you that would work! Reversal by Vic into his own vertical suplex! Great move!
EC: But no cover? LW: He doesn't need to yet. He needs to wear him down more. He gets over there and stands with his legs straddling Rogers' back. He jumps and lands right on the back edge of those ribs!
EC: OUCH! Now you have to know that lifting might be impossible for
Rogers now! LW: Yes indeed! EC: Valiant puts his hands on Rogers' back and jumps, ramming his knee down into that right side of ribs!
LW: Systematic baby! This is so textbook! EC: Rogers rolls away as Valiant stands and Adam fell out of the ring! LW: Here comes the man after him! Vic is on a mission! EC: Vic gets on the outside and lands a boot to the mid-section. Now Vic grabs Rogers and whips him right into the steel steps! Valiant is just stalking him now!
LW: This is the way you wrestle to win these days. It is not so much technical skill as it is brute force WITH that skill! This guy has it all!
EC: Valiant slides into the ring and breaks off Referee Herman Gehl's count and now he steps out of the ring again and grabs Rogers' hair!
LW: What a ring master he is. I mean to have the presence of mind to stop that count so that the win is clean.
EC: Valiant with a kidney punch! And another! Rogers drops to a knee! LW: Not only that, but tonight he will piss blood! EC: That is so crass. LW: Well it's true! EC: Valiant rolls him back inside the ring and then slide in after him. Here is a kick to the ribs! Vic has been a machine! Keying in on those ribs he seemingly hurt earlier in the match!
LW: That is what champions do. They find the weaknesses and then exploit them! That is what I used to do in my glory days!
EC: Valiant literally steps onto the back of Rogers and holds onto the ropes for leverage! He is yelling at ringside fans as he tries to force all the air out of Adam's body!
[Crowd goes nuts with heel heat on that side of the arena. One man can actually be seen flipping Vic off.]
LW: These sheep know nothing of what real talent is! This man is the real deal! EC: Valiant with a stomp on the head as he jumps off the back! Now a kick to the shoulder again! Valiant goes down to a knee and grabs Rogers' head by the hair and yanks up hard!
[You can see Valiant talking trash to Rogers before slamming his head directly into the canvas.]
LW: GOOD LORD! Vic nearly broke his nose with that shot! EC: Rogers fires a punch to the mid-section, but it has little effect as
Valiant hammers his head again. Rogers hits the mat again and Valiant seems unstoppable!
LW: He is! He really is! EC: Kick to the head! Another kick to the head! Rogers is trying to get up and he will not stop trying!
[Crowd begins a HUGE Rogers chant.] LW: Shut up already! EC: The fans are beginning to give Rogers a crowd push here! This could be bad news for Vic Valiant!
LW: Valiant start pulling him up again! EC: Punch by Rogers! And Another! These gut shots are finally doing something! Rogers to his feet! He grabs Valiant and whips him hard into the corner! He rushes into..
LW: (interrupting) A big boot by Vic! What a counter! EC: Rogers is staggered again and Valiant rushes and nails a running lariat that nearly takes Adam Rogers' head off! Here is a cover by
Valiant! 1 . . . 2 . . . three?!?!?! LW: YES!!! EC: NO! KICKOUT BY ADAM ROGERS! Gosh is he resilient! What an athlete! LW: What a loser! That referee should be tarred and feathered! EC: Valiant grabs Rogers and tosses him over the top rope! Valiant grabs the top rope and propels himself over in a high cross body! Both men are down!
LW: What talent! EC: I must admit I didn't know a man that large could fly so well, but he can folks! Wow! Rogers isn't moving and Valiant is just walking over to our time keeper's table and taking a steel chair!
LW: Now the fun begins! EC: Valiant over and waiting for Rogers to turn around. SMACK! He drilled Rogers with that chair right on the forehead! Rogers is down!
LW: He's busted open! Adam Rogers is bleeding like a faucet! EC: Rogers stands again as the blood pours out of his forehead and takes another chair shot. This one staggers him, but doesn't knock him down.
Referee Herman Gehl comes down to try and break this up and he grabs Rogers from behind. LW: GOOS LORD! Valiant went to drill Rogers again and Adam moved! Referee Herman Gehl is down and out folks and this one just got interesting! EC: Valiant lifts the chair, but Rogers is there to block it. Both men have a hold of that chair as Rogers kicks him in the gut once! Twice!
Three times and finally Vic lets go of the chair! [Crowd screams "Holy S(bleep)t!"] LW: Rogers wails on Valiant! Get out of there Vic! EC: Rogers with a second chair shot to Vic's back and shoulders! He drops the chair and walks over to him. DDT ON THE STEEL CHAIR BY ROGERS!
LW: He is trying to kill him! EC: Rogers picks him up and rolls him inside the ring! He lifts him up and nails him with a stomach breaker! Great technical move by Rogers!
LW: Come on Vic! EC: Adam stand shim up and nails a reverse Russian legsweep! Rogers is taking it home now!
LW: But no referee to call it! EC: Rogers grabs Vic and nails him with a Fisherman DDT! We all know what this means!!! LW: CRAP! EC: Rogers sets him up for that spinning Tiger Driver. He lifts...THEBATURAL DISASTER! THE NATURAL DISASTER! THIS ONE IS OVER
FOLKS! Rogers with a cover!
[Crowd chants: 1] LW: No referee! [Crowd chants: 2....3...4....5.....6....7] EC: Rogers is up and he slides out of the ring to go get referee Herman
Gehl. Gehl is out cold. Now what?
LW: Only good things can happen now! EC: Well Valiant is still out cold! [The crowd erupts as the camera switches just before the announcers see what is going on. Chris Hopper is running down to the ring wearing a referee's T-Shirt.]
EC: HOPPER IS HERE! HOPPER IS HERE!! And he is wearing a referee's shirt! LW: And the screw job is complete! EC: Rogers sees Chris and smiles as he slides in and covers Valiant! 1 . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . THREE!!!! LW: HE KICKED OUT! VALIANT KICKED OUT! EC: It took so long to actually get a possible cover that he had a chance to recover for a kickout! Valiant is really showing his resiliency here!
LW: Look at Hopper's face! He can't believe that wasn't the end! EC: Rogers picks Valiant up and nails him with a snap suplex. Rogers picks him up and whips him into the ropes. POWERSLAM BY ROGERS! Here is a cover!
1 . . . 2 . . . thr... LW: Kickout! I tell you Hopper is counting way too fast. He is trying to screw Valiant out of this match, just like he screwed Valiant out of the
MCW! EC: Hopper is calling it as fair as Gehl did. LW: That is not exactly an improvement from where I sit! EC: Well Hopper is calling a good match right now as Rogers performs a butterfly suplex! What a move!
LW: If his finisher can't do him in, this match is over! EC: Rogers goes for a whip, but its reversed by Adam! Rogers hits hard! Valiant charges, but gets boot to the face for his trouble! Vic is staggered! LW: Come on here! This sucks! EC: Rogers jumps to the second rope and leaps at valiant, but Vic catches him! Uh-oh! LW: YES!!! EC: He hold shim for a second and then just drops him...CRITICAL IMPACT! CRITICAL IMPACT! Oh my God! He turned that into his Northern Lights Bomb variation! WOW!
LW: Here is a cover and Hopper must count it! I love it! ONE!! . . . . . . . . . . . . TWO!!!! . . . . . . . . . . . . . THREE !!!!!!!!! EC: Valiant wins the match! LW: YES! Rogers is out of title contention! EC: Hopper is upset! He storms out of the ring and over to the timekeeper's table as Valiant grabs Rogers and performs another CRITICAL
IMPACT! GOODNESS, HE IS TRYING TO KILL HIM!!! LW: Yes, yes, yes! EC: Valiant has taken Rogers' spot in the tournament and now he is trying to kill the man! He lifts him up for a powerbomb! THROUGH THE RINGSIDE
TIMEKEEPER'S TABLE AND PRESIDENT HOPPER!!! Rogers is bloody and hurt bad and he nailed Hopper as Hopper was telling something to that official!
LW: A bonus! EC: Look at the smile on Valiant's face LW: A thing of beauty indeed. EC: Valiant exits the ring and walks over to where the crumpled pile of bodies are. He grabs...HOPPER?!?!
LW: Oh yeah baby! EC: Valiant rolls Hopper into the ring and picks him up...CRITICAL IMPACT! GOOD GOD! HOPPER IS LEFT LYING ON THE MAT! LW: Look at Valiant! He is leaning down towards an unconscious Hopper and just screaming at him that he belongs here! YEAH BIG FELLA!
EC: Fans we have run out of time, be sure to be back here next week for more Sunday Night Mayhem! Good Night everybody!
