NEWF (GeoCities, 2000) Card Archive

Monday Night Mayhem (March 27, 2000) (Part 6 of 6)

28 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We return from commercials to our announcers live at ringside.] BF: IT'S MAIN EVENT TIME ON MAYHEM!!! This match is not just our best match for the night, but it is also very important! Earlier new NeWF
President Kevin Kash declared this match to be a top contender's match for the Television title.
EC: One of three such matches over the next three weeks. BF: Right. This week, one next week, and a battle royal two weeks from tonight for the final one. Then two weeks from tonight, the main event will be the three contenders slugging it out for the TV title!
LW: Oh Glory Be! EC: What is your problem now Larry? LW: Well I think it is all a foregone conclusion that Nicklaus Kain will be the first NeWF Television Champion. He is the most experienced and best qualified to wear the strap!
BF: Well there are others that are qualified. Like Adam Rogers, Sean
Cage, and even Brandon Franklin and Kurt castle. All of whom we have seen in some capacity tonight.
LW: None of them have a chance. Jay Moore is a freak and Kain will have a cakewalk into the finals. And you can bet he will win once he gets to that point because the man is a finisher. He comes to win and not just compete.
EC: Well I disagree. Jay Moore has the background to do well in every match he is involved in, and I think he might be the man who stakes the first claim at a chance for Television gold.
BF: Well no matter what, it is time to go to the ring and get our Main event started! [The screen cuts to the ring where Steven Stewert is standing in the center holding the microphone and a index card.]
SS: Ladies and Gentlemen, the next match is our MAIN EVENT OF THE EVENING! This match is a one fall, no time limit match for a contender spot in a
Television title match two weeks from tonight!
[Crowd pop.] SS: Introducing first, hailing from The Necropolis. He is one of the early contenders for title stardom in NeWF. Here is
N I C K L A U S K A I N ! ! ! ["Moonlight Sonata" plays softly from a piano over the PA.] (The arena goes pitch black.) ["Moonlight Sonata" stops...demonic laughter, mixed with human screaming invades the arena. "Moonlight Sonata" starts back, this time on an organ.]
(The darkness is broken with a silent wall of flame that fires up from the entryway. The cloaked figure of Nicklaus Kain steps slowly from behind and through it. Head down, Kain walks at a steady, slow pace down to the ring.
The fans boo him, but he gives them not a second thought. Kain arrives at ringside, he climbs the ring steps and unties the belt. He lifts his arms to the side and the cloak falls to the floor, quickly retrieved by a ringside attendant. Kain steps through the ropes and leans against the turnbuckle.)
BF: What a specimen! This guy is in top form and in much better shape than anyone saw him last!
EC: Rumor has it we went to the gym and got himself in shape for this league because he felt he wanted a fresh start in NeWF. I think he has the tools to do it, but Jay Moore isn't an easy first match
LW: Sure it is. Especially when there is something like this title shot attached. Jay Moore is a "never will be". He always comes this close to titles and then something always happens. Last time the leagues actually
CLOSED when it was his turn for a title shot! Bad Karma my friends, bad karma. BF: You are basing your prediction on karma? LW: Knowing his history? Yes I am. EC: Well here he comes now! [Crowd pop.] SS: And his opponent! He comes from the mean streets of New York City! He has surpassed all obstacles in his way up until now and has a chance to finally break through and take his first title. Ladies and Gentlemen, I give you
"T I G E R" J A Y M O O R E ! ! ! [A thunderous tiger's roar shakes the arena as "Closing In" by The Living End plays over the PA system. The lights immediately dim and a faint orange glow emanates from the entrance. Out steps "Tiger" Jay Moore, clad in long orange tights baring the Tiger pattern on it, black wrestling boots and his favorite pair of black Oakley sunglasses. The music kicks into gear as the orange glow becomes brighter and Tiger Jay makes his way down the aisle with a smirk, but concentrating on the ring the entire time. He rolls into the ring and spins around with an arm raised, signaling to each section of the arena, before backing into his corner, removing his Oakleys and performing some last minute warm-ups in preparation for the match ahead]
BF: Moore looks more than ready for this challenge. LW: Karma people, I'm telling you he is a loser. EC: Karma-Smarma, I think he can do it and Nicklaus Kain better not go into this match as arrogant as you are right now, That is for sure.
BF: True. Kain gets too cocky and confident, he just might find his shoulders down for the big three count.
LW: Kain never gets over-confident. He is the perfect wrestler for this time period and he will lead NeWF forward!
[DING! DING!! DING!!!] BF: Kain walks towards the center now and stands face to face with Moore. These two are both six feet, four inches tall, so it really becomes a matter of strength advantage and endurance.
EC: Kain shoves Moore back! Moore with a big right hand that staggers Kain back. Now they are trading punches! Kain with a right hand! Moore with a big overhand right! Kain with a left jab and then he ducks the big swing of Moore! Thumb right in the eye!
LW: The great equalizer! Stops them dead in their tracks! BF: Moore is holding his eye as referee Herman Gehl warns a laughing Nicklaus Kain about the illegal move. The men are circling each other, both hesitant to lock up.
EC: Well Kain is easily afraid to lock up after getting punched around like a red-headed stepchild a few moments ago.
LW: Kain fears nothing! I tell you he fears nothing at all! BF: They lock up and Moore goes directly into a side waistlock. He lifts Kain into a side slam! He drove that elbow right into the sternum area! That had to hurt. EC: Kain is beginning to stand as Moore walks over and grabs Kain, pulling him up. He whips him across into the corner! Man did Kain hit hard!
LW: Hey ref! Get that big Yankee loser off him in the corner already! BF: Moore is laying some major punches to the midsection and chest! Moore takes Kain by the arm and steps out for an Irish Whip. He's going for another Ride!
LW: SWEET JESUS!!! EC: Moore with a short arm clothesline that flipped Kain nearly all the way around! Kain is in major trouble here now!
LW: Well it is easy to get the advantage when you cheat like Moore did in the corner. Don't act like you didn't see it.
EC: See what? LW: That's what I'm talking about. Call it down the middle you Ass-kissing moron! BF: Moore grabs Kain and once again whips him hard into the corner! HE FLIPPED OVER THE TURNBUCKLES!!! Kain is standing on the ring apron and looks very dazed! CLOTHESLINE BY MOORE SENDS HIM CRASHING TO THE FLOOR!
EC: Kain is in a bad way now! LW: Not if this idiot follows him out there. He would be in Kain's domain if he gets out of the ring.
BF: Moore is stepping out of the ring and he lands a double Ax-handle right into the area between the shoulder blades! Kain is down again and the fans are loving it!
EC: Moore picks Kain up and looks like he is going for the ring steps. Here comes the whip...REVERSAL BY KAIN! Moore hits the steps and flips over them awkwardly! I think he may have hurt a knee there!
LW: I told you that Kain rules the outer domain! BF: Kain grabs the top section of the stairs that got knocked off and he drills Moore in the left shoulder! He hits him again in the shoulder! Now a head shot!
EC: GOOD LORD!!! LW: Kain is really showing how nasty he can be now! He is climbing the corner! BF: Moore is having problems even moving now as he tries to stand. Kain is at the top and he leaps! FLYING LEGDROP BY NICKLAUS KAIN!!!
EC: Kain hit him hard and Moore is really hurting as Kain landed mainly on that left shoulder area! Kain is systematically tearing Moore apart!
LW: As it should be baby! BF: Kain rolls Moore into the ring and covers him. Referee Herman Gehl with the count! 1 . . . 2 . . . thr... EC: NO! Moore got his right shoulder up! LW: He might have, but the strain of lifting up on one and pushing down on the hurt one probably made him damage himself more!
BF: I agree. If Kain is smart, he will soon go for a submission hold that will capitalize on this shoulder injury.
EC: Kain pulls Moore up and whips him into the corner across the way! He follows him in...Boot to the face by Moore! What a counter!
LW: But he can't really follow it up can he? BF: RUNNING LARIAT BY MOORE! He hit him with that left arm and he immediately grabs that shoulder again after leveling Kain!
EC: Kain may have done all the damage he needed to do. It might just be a matter of how long Moore can keep himself away from submission holds!
LW: And that is not going to be very long at all. Kain is a master at a variation of the STF that will really put a hurt on that shoulder.
BF: Kain is up, but Moore is right there. Moore misses a right swing and Kain immediately punches Moore in that shoulder! Moore screams in pain as Kain grabs that left arm and straightens it our. EC: Kain jerks down hard and Moore falls to the mat in pain as that injured shoulder is just being destroyed!
BF: Kain pulls up on that arm and gets Moore to his feet. Standing sidekick right to Moore's face! That ends him falling back into the corner! Kain over quickly and he begins punching and choking Moore in the corner!
EC: Finally referee Herman Gehl is over and literally pulls Kain off of Moore in that corner! Kain takes exception to this and gets in Gehl's face! Not a smart move if you ask me.
LW: No one asked! I mean this referee can't decide a match and if he does, it's a shoddy move on his part. Just stay out of it pig!
BF: Kain is still arguing with Gehl as Moore slides in and rolls him up for a quick cover!
1 . . . 2 . . . t... EC: NO! Kain nearly got defeated after taking his attention off the match and onto the referee!
LW: That referee is biased! He distracted Kain long enough for the cripple to get the roll up. That is wrong!
BF: Well if he takes his eyes off Moore, then he deserved it. I would call that karma....wouldn't you Eric?
EC: I sure would! LW: I hate both of you already! BF: Kain is up and beginning to stomp the living daylights out of Moore. I guess he is trying to get some measure of revenge for that two count. EC: Must be. Kain lifts Moore to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Jay slides down under the ring and just holds that shoulder as he tries to catch his breath! That is ring savvy my friends!
LW: He is a sitting duck, you idiot! BF: Moore stands outside and is bent over catching his breath as Kain runs at him and leaps over the ropes! KAIN HIT THE GAURDRAIL!!! MOORE MOVED!!!
What a counter! EC: Moore lifts Kain up with that right hand and he manages to land a couple of right hand punches to Kain's head! Kain is down again!
LW: This is so terrible. The referee is not even counting and hasn't counted since Moore went outside. Blatant favoritism!
BF: Moore with another shot to the head! Well his legs still work! EC: I'll say! Moore is going after Kain with his legs! Kicking his upper body as he has him pinned to the floor!
LW: See! When Kain had Moore pinned down, Gehl had his ass over there pulling him off, but now he is just letting this happen?!?!
BF: Well in defense of the referee, they are outside the ring where he technically has no dominion.
LW: SO! EC: So nimrod, think about it. LW: You little Bi... BF: ANYWAY! Moore rolls Kain back inside the ring and pulls him up. he puts him in suplex position. NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX BY MOORE!!!
EC: What a move, especially with that bum arm he has right now! You can tell it took a toll as he has reached for that shoulder again!
LW: Moore grabs an arm of Kain's and spins around for an arm lock of sorts! BF: Spinning arm hold is the name Larry. LW: I used to do moves like that in my sleep. I think I know their names. EC: Whatever. Kain grabs the ropes and forces the break and Referee Herman Gehl calls for it immediately! Great job by the referee. BF: Indeed. Moore on the attack again as he lifts Kain up, but Kain with a hand thrust to the throat!
EC: Moore grabs his throat and turns away from Kain. I don't think the referee saw that one!
LW: KAIN WITH A RUNNING BULLDOG! This man is awesome! BF: Kain nailed that and is trying to catch his breath as Moore struggles back to his feet.
EC: Kain grabs Moore and puts him in position for a POWERBOMB! OH MY GOODNESS!!! He isn't done! He raises him up again! LW: YES! YES!! YES!!! BF: A second powerbomb by Nicklaus Kain! Both of those drove that injured shoulder right down into the canvas! Moore has to be seriously hurt right about now!
EC: Kain over and he covers Moore! Here is the count! 1 . . . 2 . . . 3?!?!?! LW: HE DID IT! KAIN WINS IT! WHA?!?!?! BF: Moore got his foot on the ropes! The match isn't over yet! LW: Damn it! That referee sucks! EC: He is doing a fantastic job if you ask me! Your guy just can't finish him off. LW: Moore is just being a lucky son of a gun tonight. He should be out by now! BF: Kain pulls Moore up and starts to whip him across the ring, but reversed by Moore! Re-reversed by Kain! MOORE IS SENT RIGHT INTO REFEREE
HERMAN GEHL!!! They pancaked that corner and both men are down! EC: Kain slides out of the ring and grabs a chair! This doesn't look good for Moore! LW: What is he doing out here? [The screen switches to show "The Devastator" Kurt Castle standing in the entrance way just watching the match and nodding his head as he watches.
The announcers talk over the view.] BF: Kurt Castle is out and watching this match! EC: He must be scouting his future opponents! LW: Or he plans on interjecting himself into this match like an utter fool. BF: Castle is the kind of wrestler who loves to scout opponents, so I doubt he intends to interfere.
[Screen switches back to our ring view just as Kain whacks Moore with a chair on the head.]
EC: GOOD LORD! Kain just drilled Moore in the head with that chair! The referee is out cold and isn't stirring at all! Look at Kain just standing and waiting for Moore to stand up.
LW: What a strategist! BF: Moore is up! Kain raises the chair high. This is gonna hurt big time! Kick to the gut! TIGER'S CAGE! TIGER'S CAGE!! TIGER'S CAGE!!!
Moore nailed his big move onto the steel chair! Kain is out cold! EC: Moore with a cover, but there is no referee to count the fall! [The crowd chants the three count and keeps going.] LW: Told you guys.....karma. [The crowd reaches ten and stops.] BF: The crowd reached a ten count and Kain STILL hasn't moved after that move. Referee Herman Gehl is out cold. Moore is up and over to try and help Gehl come around.
EC: Gehl is moving! LW: So is Kain! BF: Moore move in towards Kain. They lock up and Moore whips him across the ring! The rebound...DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE!
EC: Both men are down! Referee Herman Gehl is still shaking out the cobwebs but begins his count!
[Gehl: ONE!] LW: Get up damn it! [Gehl: TWO!] BF: Neither men moving at all, Kain is still ringing from that Tiger's Cage and Moore hasn't done well since that big legdrop several minutes back. [Gehl: THREE!] EC: Herman Gehl standing between them making this count. This match has been brutal for the wrestlers AND the referee.
[Gehl: FOUR!] LW: Who cares about that, just let them get up already. If this match ends in a double ten count, I'm gonna be pissed!
[Gehl: FIVE!] BF: Both men are stirring! Moore reaches for a set of ropes! Kain rolls over and faces the set of ropes he is closest to!
[Gehl: SIX!] EC: Moore is almost up to a knee. he has one hand on the middle rope and one on the bottom rope. He is really struggling, but pulling yourself up
HAS to be more difficult with a bad shoulder. [Gehl: SEVEN!] BF: Kain is up to a knee! [Gehl: EIGHT!] EC: So is Moore! [Gehl: NINE!] BF: Moore and Kain both beat the count! That was close! The crowd was chanting along with them, I haven't seen a crowd this wired in ages!
EC: You got that right. Kain and Moore trade punches again and these have a staggering effect with both men as drained as they are.
LW: Kain looks to be in better shape right now though. BF: You might be right. he does look a bit more fresh out there. Kain ducks a punch and grabs that left arm! ARMBREAKER! ouch!
EC: Kain with a leg tackle! Now he is working on Moore's knees! LW: What a great wrestler he is! BF: Kain grabs Moore and clamps on a submission hold! It looks like a variation of the Ring of Saturn! Moore's shoulder is just not going to take much more!
EC: I don't think it can take this! Moore is screaming that he doesn't want to submit and Kain is really applying the pressure!
LW: This is it baby! I know what horses to pick. You guys should come to the track with me sometime!
BF: Referee Herman Gehl continues to ask Moore if he gives in and Kain continues to apply the pressure!
EC: Moore looks like he might pass out! His head is really looking woozy out there! LW: Yes! This is very good! Moore's head was always kind of light, if you know what I mean. (chuckles)
BF: Referee is tapping his cheeks to get a response. Nothing yet! [On the third try, Moore's eyes open wide and look enraged.] EC: Holy Crap! Moore looks pissed! LW: Call the match now ref! C'mon! BF: Moore begins kicking his legs! and tries to worm out of the hold, or at least near the ropes. He made it! He got his foot on the bottom rope!
EC: Referee is forcing Kain to break the hold and Nicklaus is not happy at all with the situation! Moore struggles to stand up!
LW: Yeah! Take it to him Nick! BF: Kain begins kicking at Moore's back as he tries to stand, but it is having no effect at all!
[Crowd chants "Tiger!" "Tiger!"] EC: The crowd is solidly behind Moore as he turns around and grabs Kain with his right hand around the throat! CHOKESLAM!!! MOORE DROPPS HIM HARD
TO THE MAT! LW: This is a travesty! Moore submitted just a moment ago and now he may win. What a crock!
BF: Moore signals for the Triple Tiger and the fans are going nuts! he grabs Kain and whips him into the ropes. Kain slides between Moore's legs and kicks him right in the groin!
EC: OUCH! That will ruin his weekend! LW: Kain around to a bent over Moore! TWIGHLIGHT MADDESS!!! BF: KAIN HIT HIS FINISHER!!! He hit the powerbomb lift into the face buster! Kain with a cover!
1 . . . 2 . . . . . . 3!!! EC: Nicklaus Kain is going to the Television title Finals! What a big win in tonight's main event!
LW: I told you! I am the man! BF: Fans we are all out of time here on Monday Night Mayhem Be sure to come back next week when we are live from Jeffersonville, Indiana. So long everybody!
