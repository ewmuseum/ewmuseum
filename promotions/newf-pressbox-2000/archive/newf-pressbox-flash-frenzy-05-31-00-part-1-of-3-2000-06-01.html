Flash Frenzy (5/31/00) (Part 1 of 3)

1 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1- "Crazy" Lou Natic 2- Predator 3- The Rawls Brothers 4- "Box Office" Tripp Shade 5- "Sex Pistol" Jaime Roberts 6- "Showtime" Matt Stone =================================================================== "Crazy" Lou Natic =================================================================== [The scene opens just outside NeWF Headquarters in downtown Indianapolis. The camera views the large building, before coming into focus on the main entrance. The sidewalk area infront of it is empty, as we see a loose newspaper drift by with a gush of a wind. Out of nowhere, an out of breath
"Crazy" Lou Natic comes speeding after it. He is wearing black semi-baggy sweat pants, an orange t-shirt, and a black bicycle helmet with star stickers on it and the buckle strapped around his chin. His black slightly-curled hair is a mess. In an act of desperation, Lou dives onto the ground and outstretches his hand to get a hold of this loose newspaper.]
Lou Natic [out of breath]: "Now... now... I know why... they say... news travels...fast!" [Lou takes a few moments to catch his breath, he then seats himself indian style in the middle of the sidewalk, as he at long last begins to look through this loose newspaper. He unfolds it and begins looking over it. The side of the paper facing the camera reads in bold lettering: "Hopper Wins
Back NeWF". Lou then puts the paper down as a look of frustration comes over his face.]
Lou Natic: "Who am I kidding.. I was never much good at reading. [to himself] Lou... YOU CAN DO THIS!"
[Lou nodds approvingly.] Lou Natic: "Yeah! I can, can't I! I CAN do this!" [Lou puts the paper back up, this time he takes notice of the headline.] Lou Natic: "Ho-Ho-Hopper... Hopper! Wins! Back! N-e-W-F!" [Lou smiles.] Lou Natic: "Hopper Wins Back New F! Alright Hopper! Wait a second... that doesn't say New F... it says N-e-W-F! That's where I work. Man, I haven't been to work in a while, it's about time I head back and start making some money."
[Lou turns around to see NeWF Headquarters.] Lou Natic: "How convenient!" [Lou stands up and tosses the paper away. He walks into the building and approaches the front desk where a secretary is seated behind the desk and computer.]
Lou Natic: "Excuse me ma'am. Yeah, I work here. I want to start making some money as soon as possible."
Secretary: "Well, what's your position?" [Lou stares blankly at the secretary.] Secretary: "In other words, what do you do?" [Lou stares blankly at the secretary. She begins to work at the computer. ] Secretary: "Can I have your name?" Lou Natic: "Lou... Natic." Secretary: "Oh! Mr. Natic, I have a message for you." Lou Natic: "What is it?" [The secretary hands Lou a paper. Lou is obviously not too excited at facing the challenge of reading again. He turns away from the secretary and begins walking aimlessly as he tries to read. The camera gets a quick shot of the paper which reads:
--------------------------- Welcome to the NeWF Lou, Your first NeWF Match will take place June 1, 2000. It will be... ***TRIPLE THREAT MATCH-1st PINFALL WINS*** "Crazy" Lou Natic -VS- Johnny Holiday -VS- "Hella Razor" Cyrus Bennett --------------------------- Lou tries hard to read it himself.] Lou Natic: "Welcome... Lou... NeWF Match... June 1, 2000. Natic VS. Hol..Holiday VS. Ben-nett!" [Lou puts the paper down aghast.] Lou Natic: "WHAT!! They expect me to wrestle! I'm not a wrestler!! [to himself] Calm down Lou.. you probably read wrong."
[Lou checks again.] Lou Natic: "WHEW! You're right!! I skipped a line. Let me see, it says...Tryple... Threat... Match. Tryple Threat Match!"
[Lou looks up.] Lou Natic: "I DON'T LIKE THE SOUND OF THAT!!!" [Lou continues to read.] Lou Natic: "1st... Pitfall... Wins..." [Lou's jaw drops and his eyes widen.] Lou Natic: "1st PITFALL WINS!?!? WWWHAAAT!! Who in their right mind would fall into a pit to win a stinkin' match!?! Whatever happened to good old fashioned wrestling! But what am I thinking anyway, I'm not a wrestler! I don't have to do this! I can't!"
[Lou pauses for a moment of reflection. He then talks to himself again.] Lou Natic: "Relax Lou, you CAN do this!" [Lou argues.] Lou Natic: "But I'm not a wrestler--" [Lou cuts himself off.] Lou Natic: "You can do this! ... I CAN!" [Lou's Brooklyn accent kicks in.] Lou Natic: "You're-a 'Crazy' Lou Natic! .... I'm-a 'CRAZY' LOU NATIC!!" [Lou's got the adrenaline pumping and his voice returns to normal as he continues to talk to himself.]
Lou Natic: "So I want you to look deep into that camera, and tell Holiday and Bennett they don't know what they're getting themselves into!"
[Lou looks deep into the camera and screams.] Lou Natic: "H-H-HOLIDAY... BENNETT, I HOPE YOU'RE WATCHING!! YOU DON'T KNOW
WHAT YOU'RE GETTING YOURSELVES INTO!! YOU KNOW WHO'S GONNA BE THE FIRST ONE
TO FALL INTO THAT PIT JUNE 1ST... NOT YOU HOLIDAY... NOT YOU BENNETT..."
[Lou looks around before pointing to himself with his two thumbs.] Lou Natic: "IT'S GONNA BE ME!! I'M GONNA KICK BOTH YOUR BUTTS... AND THEN
I'M GONNA KICK THAT PIT'S BUTT!! D'YOU KNOW WHO I AM!? I'M-A 'CRAZY' LOU
NATIC!!!" [Lou begins to calm down.] Lou Natic: "You don't think this would make those guys angry at me... would it?" [Lou pauses and appears to be becoming nervous.] Lou Natic: "C-C-Cuz I wouldn't want that... I'm sure they're nice guys, don't you think? ... don't you?"
[The camera fades to black.] =================================================================== Predator =================================================================== ["Machinehead" hits the speakers as Predator walks out to a mixed reaction. He seems unusually happy as he walks to the ring and slides through the ropes. HIs music fades down as he grabs a mike. He is wearing a new outfit.
He is wearing a NeWF black T-Shirt with the logo pn the front, and " The
Place Where Boyz Become Men, And Men Become Champions" on the back.]
Predator: I'm free at last, free at last, halleluja, I'm free at last. Or,
I should say, the NeWF is free at last! Hey, I got no problem with rugs,
Prostitution, or Rackateering, but crappily running this fed was the last straw. Ever since Anderson took over, I've gotten the shaft. Now, with
Hopper Back in Control, I'm gonna make some things happen. First, I'm throwing my hat into any ring at any time. I'm open for challenge whenever someone pleases. That brings me to my match against "The Ace of Hearts".
Well, if you're intrested in cards, I'd say you're about a few cards short of a full house to fight me! So, let me tell it to you "strait". I'm more like the ace of spades, I'm a death card to whoever draws me. So, come house arrest, I'm gonna break hearts, and flush them away. Because Predator is here....AND YOU'RE IN MY SIGHTLINE!
[Fade to black.] =================================================================== The Rawls Brothers =================================================================== ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Dear NeWF, i want to right you guys at NeWF to congradjulate Chris Hopper on the grayt job he has done in getting power back with the NeWF. i don't get the channel 8 here in Tickfaw but I did get to see some NeWF acshun on a video tape. you guys is the best.
inside my letter of congradjulashuns is a news clipping about too of your laytest editions to NeWF jody and TC Rawls it gave your organizashun sum good publisity in our small town and helped to get you sum more exposhure. keep up the good work guys.
jody & TC's numbur won fan, cleet Rawls ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Local Boys Make Good by Ola Mae Rawls-Wyatt After fighting with alligators and bears in the pits of our own town of
Tickfaw, Louisiana - Jody Rawls is headed for the bright lights and the television camera's of professional wrestling. Jody recently signed with a wrestling promotion after killing a bear with his own two hands, garnering the largest pay windown in the history of Louisiana Bear Wrestling.
Following the battle all Jody had to say was that "For some people killin' comes naturally." Jody penned his name to a contract with The National E-Wrestling Promotion not long after the battle, under the stipulation that his brother
Ty Cobb Rawls get a contract as well. After a small bit of deliberation by both parties, NeWF President Chris Hopper agreed to the terms and commented that he intends to "take The Rawls Brothers to the moon and beyond" - not too bad for a couple of boys that have never left the swamps of Louisiana.
Upon hearing word that Ty Cobb Rawls, better known as TC, was going on the road with his brother Tickfaw Mayor Rudy Rawls announced that there would be a huge parade to see them off. Anyone looking to wish Jody the best of luck, and celebrating the departure of TC can join us down at the
Dew Meadow Docks at 7PM this Friday Night.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ =================================================================== "Box Office" Tripp Shade =================================================================== [Scene opens in the spacious estate(read: cramped apartment) of NeWF Superstar "Box Office" Tripp Shade. Along the walls, we see several titles won from Shade's past. In the middle of all this gold are two prized possessions: His IWF World Title, and his Academy® Award. Okay....well it's not really >HIS< Oscar, per se....it was Jack Palance's....Shade swiped it one time when an obviously drunk Palance was doing one-armed push-ups at an industry party. Sitting on a dusty old couch is Tripp Shade himself, wearing a "The Leading Men" T-Shirt. He appears to be struggling mightily over a script. Surely it must be Shakespeare! Or perhaps something by
Scorsese!]
TS: What...Whatchu talkin'....about...Willis? [Ah....or, of course, "Diff'rent Strokes: The Movie"] TS: Damn..I just CAN'T get this lip pout down! Damn the...eh? Camera? How'd you get in here?
[WRESTLING ROLEPLAY CLICHE #87: TALKING CAMERA PERSON FORTHCOMING!] Talking Camera Person: Um...you buzzed me up, remember? I buzzed and you said "Eh? Who's this?" and I said "Um...I'm from NeWF" and you said "OH!!!
Come on up, it's been too long, I'm very very lonely!" TS: . . . . . . . TCP: Um....well you did. TS: Hey, do me a favor? TCP: Yes....sir? TS: Die. [At this point, we'll put to rest the Talking Camera Person...and now to the meat and potatoes of the RP]
[Cut to a diner. Tripp's ordering from a menu] TS: Yeah, I'll have the meat...um...and the potatoes, please. [Ugh...I feel so cheap and dirty. Let's get back to the scene, already in progress] TS: .....says, "Bangor? I hardly knew her!" HAHAHA! Boy, that Queen Elizabeth has some mouth on her....ahhh....So....where was I? Ohhhhhhhhhhhh yeah. Noof is back up and running, and who's back at the helms, none other than Chris Hopper. I would pretend to be delighted, and school girl giddy.....but I'm not. Me and Hopper have quite the history. If memory serves me right, Chris Hopper killed my parents.
[Shade ponders for a moment] TS: Oh..no...wait...different guy. >BUT<, in the past, Chris Hopper >DID< hold down the pre-eminant(college word) Hollywood Attention Getter slash
Wrestling Legend, "Box Office" Tripp Shade! Just because I'm not some blown-up, stiff, barely-able-to-move, muscle-head, Hopper put the ol' glass ceiling over my head, and kept me in the, ugh, Light Heavyweight Division.
You might as well have tattooed "Dark Match" on my forehead. Well, Chris, let me tell you this. I will no longer be held down. And why, you may ask?
Well, simple, in my time off from the ring, >I< have gained a pound. Yep, so now you can't simply pigeon hole me into the "Curtain Jerker" Division.
I'm here for big gold, to add to my already full mantle(note: Shade doesn't actually HAVE a mantle).
[TRIPP SHADE, MASTER OF THE SEGUE(pronounced "sayg-way" for those of you without College schooling.....or from South Carolina)]
TS: Speaking of big gold, it seems that >I< am entered in this big get-together shindig tournament type of thing to crown a Noof National
Champion. And who do I draw first round? "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams.
Wow..I haven't heard a name so...so...unoriginal since...."The Main Event" Brian Collins. Psh....you and Palumbo, pal. [Again, I have nothing to really say here...just thought I'd break up the monotony(college word)]
TS: Ya know, Joe, you remind me a lot of Donna from 90210. Besides being from Beverly Hills, besides being a spoiled little rich girl, and besides being a virgin until being well into your adult-hood...you my friend, look like a horse. But I'm not here for childish name calling, Ne'er Been Laid,
I'm here to, as we say in the industry, *does quotation fingers* "Build heat". So, tell me Joe, have you ever been in a ring with someone that was and is loved and adored by millions of fans world wide? Have you ever been in a ring with a founding member of '80s super group "The Brat Pack"? Have you ever been in a ring with someone that has slept many a-time with musical diva, Paula Abdul? No? Hmm...I haven't been in the ring with Emilio
Estevez, either. But that's besides the point. You see, the point is, It doesn't matter where you're from. Or how great you think you are. Because
Tripp Shade is simply better. Bad Attitude? Heh, I'd have a bad attitude too, if I looked like Tori Spelling.
[Shade looks off camera at a clock.] TS: Ah, as much as I'd love to continue to toot my own proverbial horn, >I< actually have things to do. Auditions, press junkets...uh..laundry, that kinda stuff. Joe, the best advice I can give to you, to paraphrase the great Jed Clampett, is to simply tell you, Joe, you don't wanna face me, so pack up your car, and head back to Beverly.
[Shade pauses for dramatic effect.] TS: Hills, that is. [Fade out.] =================================================================== "Sex Pistol" Jaime Roberts =================================================================== [The scene - a press conference, called by 'Sex Pistol' Jaime Roberts, newest signing of the NeWF. The cocky youngster is on stage, accompanied by his valet and girlfriend Sarah Maynard, and has told reporters that he has a HUGE announcement to make.]
Pistol: Alright, let's start things off. Now, many of you will recognise both me, and the lovely young lady standing next to me, but for those who don't, I'm Jaime Roberts, and this is Sarah Maynard. I would normally take this opportunity to tell you of the titles I've won, and the awards I've had, since I debuted in POW last October, but to be honest, we haven't got enough time. Because, I have an announcement to make. Normally, when I come into a fed, I announce that I'm starting the Sex Stable. For those who don't know, the Sex Stable is the most dominant force in professional wrestling, EVER. Oh... you do all know. Ain't fame great? Anyway, as I was saying, normally I'll announce the formation of the SS, and a few weeks later, I'll recruit a new member.
Pistol: Not this time. Because, gentlemen, today, I am announcing the formation of Sex Stable: Heartbreak, and right now, I will introduce the second and third members.
[The Pistol smiles.] Pistol: Gentlemen, I give to you, following in the footsteps of such wrestling legends as Chris 'Doomsday' Schneider, Larry 'Nature Boy'
Spikowski, and the 'Hollywood Heart-throb' Dave Diamond, the Sex Stable's first ever tag team...
[The door onto the stage opens, and the photographers strain to try and see who the newest members are]
Pistol: ... The Chippendales!!! [There is an unaccountable lack of any reaction whatsoever, as the Chippendales make their way on stage.] Pistol: I said, the Chippendales!!! [There are one or two claps, probably from people who feel sorry for the poor guys.] Pistol: Alright, let's take some questions. [he shrugs] Reporter: Mr Roberts... Pistol: Yeah? Reporter: Some people would say that the Chippendales are, how shall we put it? Not the greatest tag team in the NeWF.
[While Sex Pistol is talking, Monty is posing in the background. Sarah is watching Pistol most of the time, but her eyes are drawn to Monty occasionally.]
Pistol: Wait right there, bud. Do you remember Chris Hopper coming back in? Reporter: Of course I do, I was at that press conference as well - and that was a REAL surprise.
Pistol: Well, when Hopper came in, he said that we were starting with a clean slate. So, everybody has the same record. And, that means, the
Chippendales are the best team in the NeWF.
Reporter: Joint best. Pistol: Whatever. Reporter: But if the Chippendales are so great, why aren't they in the Tag title match? Pistol: Why would they want to be in a four way match? Reporter: Well, they could win the belts, couldn't they? Pistol: Now, buddy, I realise that good looks is a subject you know absolutely NOTHING about…
[There are a few laughs from some of the other reporters.] Pistol: …But, when you have a face like that of these two men, you don't risk it by letting six idiots try and beat you up. We don't care who wins those Tag titles, the 'Dales will beat them two on two.
Reporter: Could I take this opportunity to ask the Chippendales a few questions? [The Chippendale eyes light up, and Monty steps forward, but as he is about to speak, Pistol addresses the reporter himself.]
Pistol: Excuse me? You think that you can march into a Sex Pistol press conference and completely ignore him? You want to spend Sex Pistol interview time talking to somebody else?
Reporter: But I just wanted to ask a couple of questions… Pistol: And you think that you can fob off the greatest tag team in the
NeWF with just a couple of questions? How can you call yourself a reporter?
Reporter: I’m sorry, but… Pistol: Well, you want to talk to them, sure you can talk to them! Reporter: Thanks. So… Pistol: Not today, idiot! The Chippendales will have their OWN press conference, on Friday! And I’ll see you all there, because I’m not stopping here to be insulted by idiots like you!
[Pistol storms off stage. The Chippendales follow, while Sarah tries to salvage some respect for the Sex Stable from this mess.]
Sarah: Well, that was the first ever press conference for Sex Stable: Heartbreak. Thank you all for attending. I’m sure I’ll see you all on
Friday.
