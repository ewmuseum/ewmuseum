SS: Well yes there is actually, I would like to explain why I am here and what I am going to do to take home gold in this fed….Well I arrived here in the NeWF really not knowing why I was here. But then I took some time thought about it and then realized. I am here through destiny, I was brought here to the NeWF to wrestle, maybe not to win, but to put up a Damm good effort, and make my opponent work harder than he ever has in his life to get me pinned to that mat. You see I am here to reach my full potential, and to make everyone else around me work to there full potential, cause if
I ever do get pined, I will not be upset if I know hat I have put in one hundred percent, then put in another hundred, and then if I get pinned congratulations to the man that does it. But if I lose cheaply through interfernce or me just not trying, there will be hell to pay. Because I have not came here and put in so much blood and effort to be cheated out of a match, by some little smart @$$ with a chair. But you beat me fair and square I will congratulate, and hold you in high respect cause you know as well as I do that I aint no easy beat. And to my adoring fans all I have to say is that I will put on a good show, no matter what I will, cause if it wasn't for you, I wouldn't be sitting here now if it wasn't for you.
Reporter no 1: Well that was enlightening speech, but truly do u think of any of them listen:
SS: Well I don't know, Its up to them. But if they step into the ring, underestimating me I think they be in for a rude shock. I know some of the people here know I can wrestle. The Football Hooligans, they know. As does
"Tiger" Jay Moore, and Maybe TBC saw, but no-one else. And they Under estimate someone its there fault when they lose no one elses. I am sorry guys I think I might have to leave you there.
Reporter no 3: Thank you for your time and patients. [With this Snow Storm stands up, turns around showing that the no 1 is on the back of his shirt, and opens the door walks through it, and then closes it behind him.]
