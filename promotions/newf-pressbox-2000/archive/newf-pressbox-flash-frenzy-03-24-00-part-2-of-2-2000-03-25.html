Flash Frenzy (3/24/00) (Part 2 of 2)

25 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1- "The Natural" Adam Rodgers 2- "Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin 3- "The Extreme Warrior" War Child ============================================================== "The Natural" Adam Rodgers ============================================================== [People. Everywhere. That's all we see as the scene opens. In some sort of large lobby-like area, numbers upon numbers of people, young and old, male and female, black and white, all crowd into the area, probably causing a fire hazard of some sort. Amdist all the commotion, we can see a few lines, or something that resembles lines at any rate. Luckily, the man holding the camera has automatic precedence to walk through the crowd, and does just that, taking us to the source of the excitement.]
[A long table -- probably more like five or six tables lined up together-- seems to mark the point for the end of the line. A large black cloth banner covers the tables, with the letters 'NeWF' on the front in a familiar symbol. On top of the cloth, we see various merchandise, like t-shirts, caps, posters, and so forth. And finally, sitting behind the table, we see a few recognizable faces, such as Sean Cage, Jay Moore, but the camera is focusing in particularly on one individual... 'The Natural'
Adam Rogers.] [A young fan stands in front of Adam, holding out a white NeWF t-shirt hoping for Adam's autograph. Adam takes the shirt and his black Sharpie
(tm), and signs his name on the top left shoulder. Smiling, he hands the shirt back to the youngster, who is probably in the neighborhood of 12 years of age.]
Rogers: "There you go, show it off to all your friends." Kid: "Thanks, Adam. I'm a big fan of yours, I keep up with you in Glo[BEEEEEEP]." [No, the kid didn't curse. But do you think NeWF management will allow free promotion of another organization on it's airwaves?]
Rogers: "Thanks, buddy. Let's just hope I can have the same success in the
NeWF as I am havin there."
[The kid smiles and shakes hands with Adam before moving on to get time with the other NeWF stars in attendance. Adam glances at his watch and surveys the room... still crowded as ever. A tap on the shoulder turns his attention elsewhere, to a man standing behind him. The man seems to be in his mid-30's, dressed nicely in a suit and tie.]
Man: "Alright, Adam, you can take a break." [Adam seems to breathe a sigh of relief, ready to take a break from the commotion. The man, who we only figure to be a promoter of sorts, makes the annoucement to the crowd that Adam will be leaving for a while, and gets met with a not-so-kind reaction. Seeing a chance to do a little peace-making, Adam stands and raises his hand.]
Rogers: "Look, I'm sorry about that, but I have a few other commitments. I will be back, though, and I'll try to catch each one of you before the day's over."
[That didn't do just a whole lot of good, as a few audible groans are heard in the crowd. But, having a schedule to keep and a life of his own, Adam still proceeds away from the table. The camera follows him as he comes to a small makeshift rest area, with a circular off-white table and four folding chairs surrounding it. Adam pulls a canned drink from a nearby cooler, and takes a seat at the table.]
Rogers: "Phew! Talk about wearing you out... don't get me wrong, I love the fans of this great sport, but man, can they ever be demanding. Still,
I'm just glad I get the chance to interact with them and give them a few minutes with me, if that's what they want."
[Adam pops the lid to the can, and takes a couple of big gulps. Wiping his mouth with his shirt sleeve, he continues.]
Rogers: "Like most everyone, I watched the first edition of 'Flash Frenzy' a couple of nights ago. And who else would lead off the show, but my
'worthy' opponent for Monday night, Brian Collins."
[A grin comes across Adam's tanned face. Apparently, he got a real kick out of some of Collins' comments.]
Rogers: "One thing's for sure, Brian... you talk an awfully good fight. Especially for someone with as little professional in-ring experience as you seem to have. You see, like you, I was a successful amateur wrestler.
But I can tell you from spending a lifetime around this business, and four years as a competitor in it, the two are entirely different worlds...
_entirely_ different. You may know that to some extent, but I can guarantee you don't know it well enough. But you're lucky, Brian...
because come Monday night, you'll get a first-hand introduction to the real world, and get you out of this fantasy you seem to be dwelling in."
[Adam lifts the can again and takes another gulp.] Rogers: "That was really cute, getting someone to transpose a Stanford jersey on me, you're a real riot. You can compare me to Stanford and you to North Carolina all you want, but this isn't March Madness, and the only basketball player around here is Sean Cage. I'm not about hoops, pal, I'm about _wrestling_... and if you think I'm going to play the Stanford to your UNC, you're living in a fantasy world. If you truly think that you can 'kick my ass' on any given day, then you're living a lie. What you need is a good dose of reality, and I'll be more than happy to fill that prescription come Monday night."
[Adam kicks his feet up into an adjacent chair, allowing a look of confidence to cover his face.]
Rogers: "You've got it all planned out, don't you? You're going to 'toy with me', then 'liven the pace much to my dismay', then finish me off with whatever you like to term your MO. Well, Collins, what are you going to do when things don't go your way? What are you going to do when you get in that ring and find out I'm not the 'loser' you think I am? What are you going to do when your attempts to 'toy' with me get you planted through the mat? Collins, you better be coming in with a _much_ better game plan than that, or you'll be in for a very unsuccessful debut in the NeWF."
Rogers: "Soon, Brian, the time for talking will be over, and it'll be left only to who is the better wrestler in that ring. For all the talking you seem to enjoy doing, for all the empty words you like to say... you'll have to back those up, and you'll have to do it against the most technically sound wrestler in the world. You see, unlike you and many others, I don't
_have_ to take shortcuts, to break the rules, to get ahead. I _respect_ the rules, I _respect_ this sport, and I still win. Can you say the same about yourself? Can you honestly look in the mirror and tell yourself that you can survive on your ability alone? I can't answer that, but you can;
and if you're honest with yourself, you may not like the answer." [The same man we saw before sticks his head into the makeshift room.] Man: "Adam, the people are raising a pretty big stink. They want you back out there, you up for it?"
[Adam pauses, seemingly in thought. He takes a few more drinks from the can of soda, then sets it on the table, smiling as he stands up.]
Rogers: "Hey, that's what I'm here for. If they want to see me, I'm happy to oblidge." Man: "Thank goodness, they were about to jump me. I'll tell 'em you're coming back." [As the promoter walks out of the picture, Adam turns back to the camera.] Rogers: "See, _I_ am the one the people want. Why? Because they know who the best is. And come Monday night, Brian Collins... so will you."
[Adam turns from the camera and walks back out to the masses. As he disappears from our view, we hear a burst of cheers, and the scene fades out.]
============================================================== "Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin ============================================================== [The Scene opens in an empty arena, well sort of. There are workers setting up the Wrestling Ring, and fixing some of the bleachers, as man sits on the second balcony with his hands outstretched on the back of the seats. As the camera zooms in, we realize that it is "Pure Chaos" Brandon
Franklin.]
[Brandon is wearing a black leather jacket, and a gold necklace around his neck. We can't see his legs, because of the barricade in front of him, which is there so no one falls off the second balcony.]
Brandon Franklin: "Welcome, to Indianapolis, Indiana. This is the place where Brandon Franklin will make his debut in the NeWF. This is where
Brandon Franklin will get his first win as a Wrestler of the NeWF."
[Brandon looks to his left, and then down diagnolly at the ring, while staring to the ring, Brandon continues from where he left off.]
Brandon Franklin: "I watched War Child's last interivew - and I thought it was the most horrible thing I've ever seen. I almost feel sorry for The
Child - knowing that he's under a great deal of pressure going into his first match."
[Brandon looks back to the camera.] Brandon Franklin: "Anyone going into their first match with a man of my
Physique, and of my Athletic ability should be at the peak of insanity thinking of how they're going to get out of it."
[Brandon snickers...] Brandon Franklin: "And I don't blame 'em." [Brandon takes a pause.] Brandon Franklin: "Quite frankly, I couldn't sit through five more minutes of that C-N-N crap that War Child was putting out on Television. Talking about Wars, and Deaths, and Chaos. The Word 'Chaos' means absolutely nothing to Brandon Franklin. I thought it'd be a catchy name, so I tacked it onto the beginning of my real name."
[Brandon chuckles a bit and starts speaking sarcastically.] Brandon Franklin: "Yep, that's what I did. I didn't think about the name,
I found it in a Dictionary somewhere and decided to use it. I barely know what it means..."
[Brandon stares into the camera, as his brown eyes give off a glare that could almost kill.]
Brandon Franklin: "Yeah right War Child - You wish! None of what you said made any sense to me, or half the viewing audience out there, and quite frankly, I'd be happy if you'd just shut your mouth for more than five minutes and let me have a little time to breath, and absorb what you're trying to imply. I'm not all about gimmicks, if it wasn't for agents and promoters, I'd be wrestling under the simple name of Brandon Franklin. You shouldn't be worried about my name though, you should be worried about the talent you're stepping into the ring with. The ability you're going
'head-to-head' with." [Brandon sighs and shrugs his shoulders.] Brandon Franklin: "But, what do I know? I'm just an average guy with a gimmick to try and impress people."
[Brandon folds his hands and sets them his lap, tweedling his thumbs around, while staring into the camera, with a half-smile over his face.
Almost an evil smile.] Brandon Franklin: "There are a couple of things you don't know, War Child, so turn your head this way, and allow me to teach you!"
[Brandon grabs the top of the barricade with his hands, and leans in closer to the camera.]
Brandon Franklin: "First off - I don't Wrestle in Japan. To tell you the truth, I'm not stupid enough to fight in those little 'exploding matches'
but I have went Barbwire about five times. It's like Wrestling in my backyard. I was in Japan for a Pay Per View, not because I work all my matches there. Don't assume too much about me, War Child - because what you assume is wrong. I'm not Wrestling for a Japenese Hardcore Title, and
I don't play with my 'Pecker'. It's nice to know you're thinking along the lines of an immature fifth grader who thinks the word 'Pecker' is funny though, really, it is. The last guy who proclaimed that he was 'extreme'
was a guy by the name of 'Mr. Extreme' Andy Slauenwhite. Yeah, you know - the one who has never beaten me One on One but has been pinned by me seven times. You don't scare me War Child. Not one bit! I've put my carrer - and body for that matter on the line many times - why is this any different?"
[Brandon acts as though if he has just gotten the answer to a tough question.] Brandon Franklin: "OH! I know - it's because I'm fighting a guy who
'knows no gimmicks' even though he thinks he's the meaning of the word
'war'. I'm fighting 'The Extreme Warrior' War Child. I better do what he says, stick my tail in between my legs and run for high hills, otherwise he will catch me, and beat me worse than any one man has ever beaten me?"
[Brandon laughs.] Brandon Franklin: "Scary - real scary!" [Brandon chuckles some more, and returns to a serious tone of voice again.] Brandon Franklin: "Predict your future, predict your matches - but just don't underestimate the man you have already underestimated - ME! Don't look past what is ahead of you, because if you walk too far from it, it might just catch up to bite you in the ass. And *DON'T* threaten me with being Extreme - because I *AM* by far the most Hardcore Son of a Bitch in the NeWF! You wanna' talk Extreme - War Child? You wanna' try and take
Brandon Franklin to the limit. Go ahead! But, I'm far and beyond the limit, I'm over your head, and you're in too deep. When I get my hands on you, that'll be the *LAST TIME* anyone ever strangles you by the neck, because I'm *NOT* letting go!"
[Brandon shakes his head.] Brandon Franklin: "When you look up, and all you can see is lights - then when you realize you're in a Hospital bed being worked on, remember what you said to me. Remember - you threatened me first, and you made the empty promises. You're the man who signed and signatured his own will to death.
You are the man who tested Brandon Franklin - and pushed Brandon Franklin over the limit. Now, you can just wait and anticipate you're meeting with
Brandon Franklin. It's going to be a rugged trip for you, boy. Brandon
Franklin is going to take you on the ride of your life. The ride you will never forget. So, step up and spin the wheel... Brandon Franklin's wheel..."
[Brandon pauses.] Brandon Franklin: "OF DEATH!" [The Camera fades as Brandon stares deeply into the camera.] ============================================================== "The Extreme Warrior" War Child ============================================================== [Scene opens in an empty Davis arena. The arena is virtually black, the only light is from a spotlight on the ring that is already set up for
Monday Night Mayhem. The cameraman begins his descent through isle of seats towards the ring. Suddenly footsteps can be heard, then a loud bang and the spotlight goes out. The arena is now pitch black, nothing can be seen or heard. Suddenly a man laughing is heard. The laughter is not of humor, but cold and threatening. The laughter echos throughout the arena, suddenly the laughter is stopped and a voice is heard speaking.]
Voice: He says I'm not worth a bucket of s[censored]t. He comes out, puffs on his cigarette, opens his mouth. Spewing out accusations of payoffs and conspiracy, thinking the whole word including president Kevin Kash, is against him. His back is against the wall, and the only thing he can do is spew out vulgarities and unfounded accusations. "Pure Chaos" Brandon
Franklin is truly the most pathetic piece of garbage I've ever seen. One might ask who am I? Well I'll tell you....
[Suddenly another big bang is heard and the spotlight comes back on. The light breaks the darkness only to reveal something even darker. The Extreme
Warrior, War Child stands in the center of the ring, wearing his long black leather trench coat, black sunglasses, a pair of cutoff blue jean shorts and a War Child "distributor of pain" T-shirt. War Child's long black hair drapes over his shoulders as he stands, head down in the center of the ring.]
"The Extreme Warrior" War Child: I am the garbage man... I'm here to take out trash like Pure Chaos, crush them and then bury them in a land fill.
Franklin, you come out here wining and crying like the little b*tch like you are....<<<in a wining voice>>> "you paid off front office to get yourself over as a badass," Boo F[censored]kin' Hoo. You think by insulting me it makes you some kind of big man. Let me tell you something, Franklin, if you think you're getting under my skin with your petty insults and threats, you've got another thing coming. You think you can intimidate a man who is feared by all, including yourself? Punk, b*tches like you make me vomit. There was no payoff, just you trying to make excuses. Just you with your back against the wall, not knowing how to escape the inevitable.
Stop trying to put yourself over as a badass and come to terms, you're just a coward hiding from the simpe truth. And that simple truth is, Franklin on your best day, you still couldn't lace my boots. You think you know what
Chaos is about. You don't and I'm going to show you in this very ring come
Monday Night.
[The camera moves closer to War Child. The cameraman is at the ring apron, filming upward at War Child. War Child removes his sunglasses and speaks again.]
"The Extreme Warrior" War Child: And apparently he's as stupid and illiterate as much as he is a coward. He speaks of me trashing other wrestlers than saying they're the best, well jackass. Apparently someone forgot to read the entire interview. I said in answering a question about why I was hear, my answer was, "I had other offers from other organizations, and to tell you the truth, eventhough these wrestlers aren't worth what I scrape off my boot, they are still the best wrestlers on top of the heep of sh[censored]t that is today's wrestler".. So I guess you're too stupid to grasp the concept of what I said. And you know what it doesn't surprise me. A total coward like you, who doesn't know he's bound for the hospital on Monday night, thinks he can put himself over by insulting my interviews. He also tried to put himself over by going out and buying a Hummer, ripping off me, and drives it into his interview.
Franklin, I think you should take a good hard look at yourself, because no one likes a copy cat. You're problem is, not only do you fear me, but you want to be me. The problem is you're too weak, too pitiful and too much of a stinkin' yellow coward to even come close to the Extreme caliber I am.
And that's the difference between me and you.. I'm an innovator... You...well you are a cheap immitator. So take your cheap, carbon copy ass, get in your little red Hummer, and drive home to watch the Golden Girls with your little four-year old nephew. Sit there on your ass on your black couch and take wrestling lessons from your Nephew, perhaps you'd learn something. You don't have to wait to stick your foot in my mouth, I'm easy to find. I'll be here at the Davis arena, waiting for you. Come early if you like and we'll see if you can lift your leg to put that fought in my mouth. But how are you going to put your foot in my mouth after I break both of your legs? OR I could tie you up with this...
[War Child removes his trench coat and let's it drop to the mat. On his left arm, a long strand of barbed wire is wrapped around it. War Child unravels the wire from his arm and stretches it out. Blood drips from ever barb, and War Child's arm is dripping wih it too.]
"The Extreme Warrior" War Child: Oh yes, do you really know what you've got yourself into my friend. You can ignore the War that is about to start in the NeWF, like you are doing now and get swallowed up by it anyway. Or you can choose to fight the war and loose everything, the match and quite possibly your career. Not sure what you're talking about when you question the so called War that is in my mind and in my heart. For my mind and heart have never been more clear. My objective... clear as my mind, my heart pounds for the Extreme and my soul craves fire. I am not in misery as you same I am in, and little do you know it, you are the one caught up in my world. For my world is what I am standing in. This is not just a ring, it's my canvas for my own special brand of art. The art of war, this is more than a mere a ring, it's MY battlefield. I have no partners, no "ninjas", just me the Extreme Warrior....
[War Child begins to laugh again. He turns and twists the barbed wire in his fists] "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: ha ha ha, you know it just occurred to me. Brandon Franklin likes to think I am the one living in my own little world. When the fact of the matter is Brandon, it's YOU who is living in your own little, dellusional world. You speak of conspiracy, you even have me with a group of Ninjas because Judo is in my fighting background... BOY, this ain't an episode of the X-Files and you're definitely not Fox Mulder..
maybe you're like Scully, a little b*tch in a world too big for her. Well
Brandon, I'm here to help you out, I'm here to remove you from this big world and send you back to your black couch and your big screen TV a crippled, drooling mess to watch Golden Girls with your putrid nephew. That way you won't have to deal with reality. With that said, maybe YOU are the one who should hire a writer for your interviews because you PATHETIC attempt at insulting and intimidating me failed. And now it is you that'll have to live with all of your failures, including the failure to heed my warning for you to RUN.
[War Child, drops the barbed wire, picks up his coat and puts it on, he turns around looks into the camera with a smile of hatred.]
"The Extreme Warrior" War Child: One last thing before I go, a message to
Brandon Franklin. I am your reality. I am the man you most admire and you most fear. It's time to wake up from your dream world and enter mine. Mine is a world of fire, the fire of war... It will consume your future, and there's no escape. Your very existance is in danger. Your livelihood will be extinct. The Path of Destruction starts with you Brandon... Welcome to my world. Welcome to your end.... Ash to ash, dust to dust... Fade to black.
