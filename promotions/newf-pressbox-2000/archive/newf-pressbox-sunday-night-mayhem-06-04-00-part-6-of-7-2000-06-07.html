Sunday Night Mayhem [June 4, 2000] (Part 6 of 7)

7 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We return from commercials to our announcers live at ringside.] EC: Welcome back to Sunday Night Mayhem! It is time for the first of our two main events as Tripp Shade takes on Joe Williams.
LW: Williams and Shade are both strange birds because one cant truly figure them out. I mean Shade can be a do-gooder and yet be brash and cocky. Yet Williams can be the baddest man on the planet or he can be a generous soul. These guys need to ditch the stuffs who come to these shows and work for one thing only....THEMSELVES.
EC: It is all about you isn't it Larry LW: Of course. EC: Well Both men are strange that way, but Shade seems to be more on a hot streak as of late. Williams is actually doing allot of treading water right now. He seems to lack direction.
LW: Hell, he has been that way forever! EC: I seriously doubt that. Let's head to the ring and get this match going! [The screen cuts to the ring where Steven Stewert is standing in the center holding the microphone and a index card.]
SS: Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is the first of our double Main
Events this evening. It is a one fall twenty minute time limit match. It is a First Round match in the National Heavyweight Title Tournament!
[Crowd pop.] SS: Introducing first, hailing from Hollywood, California. He is the self proclaimed star of action movies world wide. He is...
"B O X O F F I C E" T R I P P S H A D E ! ! ! ["You a Fan" bellows a voice, followed by Grandmaster Flash's, "The Message". The crowd gives a decent sized pop, as the self-proclaimed
Hollywood Heartthrob slash Wrestling Hero, "Box Office" Tripp Shade, appears from behind the curtain, wearing long blue wrestling trunks, with a nifty design (vague baby, vague) and a "You a Fan?" T-shirt.. Several pyros go off, made to look like flashbulbs. A long red carpet has been laid out along the aisle, leading to the ring. Shade pauses at the end of the carpet, and gives a smug smile to all his adoring fans(and the
"paparazzi"). Shade slowly, and methodically makes his way down to ringside, pausing so often to pose for a picture, as if actually walking down some Hollywood Premiere. Cut to a fan carrying a sign reading: "Tripp
Trooper". Cut back to the ring. Shade grabs the microphone from the ring announcer.]
TS: Y'know, I had planned to come out here and say how great it was to be back here.... [Crowd gives another decent pop.] TS: Eh? And by "here", of course, I meant the main event..... [Crowd dies down a bit] TS: But Chris Hopper decides to >ONCE AGAIN< hold a brother down. I >SHOULD< be in the main event, but once AGAIN, I get shunned. And not only that, Hopper is slapping me in my face by having the far less talented, and far more uneducated "Vic Valiant" take my place...bah, Double V, 3V, V8, it doesn't matter....you still aren't Tripp Shade.
[The crowd pops loud.] TS: Ya know, before they bring out ol' "Bad Attitude" Tori Spelling, I would like to introduce someone very near, and very dear, to my heart.
While not nearly as big a star as I am, he still is a shining star in this industry! And who better to accompany me to ringside for my match against a spoiled rich Beverly Hills brat, then someone who >PLAYED< a spoiled, rich,
Beverly Hills brat.
[Crowd sorta buzzes in anticipation....could it be..Ian Ziering?!] TS: Ladies and gentlemen, please give a warm..uh...your city welcome, to.......JIMMY JONES!!
[Tripp's music cues up again, as the crowd sorta becomes...hushed.....half out of disappointment, and half out of "who the hell is this guy?"
uh..ment.] TS: C'mon, people, put 'em together! [Jimmy Jones(who portrayed Rich Snobby Beverly Hills Kid 473 on the show
"Beverly Hills 90210) makes his way down to ringside, wearing a tight pink golf shirt, and khakis. He has short cropped blonde hair. Basically...he's a dork]
TS: AH.....Jimmy, you serve as an inspiration and a reminder to all of us everywhere! And that reminder is....Spoiled Rich Snobs from Beverly Hills aren't as good as "Box Office" Tripp Shade!
[The crowd pops.] TS: Hey, Jim, do me a favor? JJ: Uh...s-sure. TS: DIE! [And with that, Tripp hits his *deep breath* Front Russian Leg Sweep Full
Nelson Face Smash, called "The Matinee" on poor Jimmy. The crowd pops.]
TS: Ahhhh, that's ONE Snotty, Snobby, Spoiled, Silver Spoon Sucking 90210-dweller outta the way.....Tori, let's make this a two-fer! [Shade tosses down his mic, and takes off his shirt, as he awaits the arrival of Williams. Security rolls poor Jimmy out of the ring.]
EC: This guy can be funny, yet he can be such a jerk sometimes. LW: I feel the same way. Sometimes he can be a tough cocky competitor, and others he is a fan begging loser.
EC: I guess we see it the same way, yet different. LW: You are so stupid. EC: Here comes Williams and that means Shade better get serious really quick! LW: I do not think he will have a problem there. [Crowd pop.] SS: And his opponent! He is a man that used to be Ice Cold, but this
Beverly Hills Native is now known for his Bad Attitude. Ladies and
Gentlemen, I give you... "B A D A T T I T U D E" J O E W I L L I A M S ! ! ! [Joe Williams comes walking out and no pyro, nor is there any music playing. He looks around confused as the fans are all dumbfounded as well.
In the ring, we see Tripp Shade laughing. Williams walks down to the ring and climbs in, waiting for the start of this match.]
EC: Shade must have arranged for the De-Hollywooding of Williams' entrance. LW: I must say that was quite funny! (chuckles) EC: Well these two are ready, so let's ring that bell! [DING! DING! DING!] Larry Watters: "And here we go with our fourth First Round Match in The
NeWF National Title Tournament, and the first half of our HUGE Double Main
Event tonight. It is "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams taking on The
Self-Proclaimed Hollywood Superstar "Box Office" Tripp Shade." Eric Chaney: "What do you mean 'self proclaimed?' Didn't you see "The Pizza
Man Always Rings Twice" - it was one of his more famous roles, having an opportunity to be in a classic fight scene with Garrett Morris. But his past doesn't matter, as Tripp has been picked as one of the favorites to take The NeWF National Title as he and Joe Williams circle each other in the ring."
Larry Watters: "Neither man seems to anxious to make the first move, because with the technical expertise of both men - their first move could easily counter into their first mistake. But we have a collar and elbow tie up, as both men are jockeying for position. It is Williams with the advantage as he has Shade back against the ropes - but Referee Josh Babcock is right there calling for the break 1...2...3...4...We have a clean break!"
[Not fully trusting Williams, Tripp throws up his fists defensively, until he is sure that Joey doesn't have anything up his sleeve - and immediately charges in for a second collar and elbow tie-up.]
Eric Chaney: "A second lock up from Tripp Shade as he quickly pulls Bad
Attitude down into a side headlock - as Williams is claiming a hair pull. I didn't see any hair pull."
Larry Watters: "Well you must have a different camera angle, because I saw it clear as day as now it is Williams powering Tripp Shade into the ropes
-" Eric Chaney: "POWERING? Now THAT is a hairpull, and Referee Babcock is oblivious to it as Williams forces Tripp off the ropes and across the ring.
Bad Attitude charging in with a clothesline, but it is ducked by Shade he rebounds off the ropes and FLYING FOREARM!!!!"
[The fans take to their feet in cheers as Joe Williams hits the canvas hard from the impact of the forearm, and Shade quickly grabs both legs of Bad
Attitude going for a grapevine.]
Eric Chaney: "Tripp is wasting NO TIME! He has the legs of Williams, and is trying to lock in his Texas Cloverleaf submission that he likes to call
Critical Acclaim! But Bad Attitude is reaching for the ropes and has them!"
[A flustered Joe Williams rolls to the outside of the ring after the hold is broken and quickly calls for his manager Steve Jackson and the beautiful
Toni Winter in an attempt to regain his strategy.]
Eric Chaney: "Tripp Shade now lounging across the top rope as he soaks in the cheers of these people in Reno, in an ovation that just may rival that of Adam Rogers - who will take part in our SECOND Main Event tonight, when he puts his spot in The National Title Tournament up against the contract of "Triple V" Vicious Vic Valiant."
Larry Watters: "Well Williams does appear to be off a step, but thanks to the coaching of Jackson, he looks to have his head back in this game as he rolls under the bottom rope and is ready to go again."
Eric Chaney: "Tripp gets down from his perch on the top rope, and prepares for another lock-up NO! Thumb to the eye by Joe Williams, who backs Shade into the ropes for an Irish Whip! But it is Tripp Shade with the reversal as Bad Attitude hits the ropes, and Tripp goes for a backdrop - but
Williams meets him with a knee. He telegraphed that one!" Larry Watters: "Williams now seizing the advantage as he locks in a front facelock, and hoists Tripp over quickly with a snap suplex. - and there is the float over as Joe goes for the pin. But Tripp manages to kick out before Referee Josh Babcock can even register a one count!"
[With a grunt of disapproval, Williams pulls Shade back up to his feet and scoops him up in a bodyslam position - dropping "Box Office" throat first across the top rope, garnering a warning from Referee Josh Babcock.]
Larry Watters: "Williams shoving the official out of the way and backing
Tripp into a corner of the ring as he plants that forearm against the throat of Tripp Shade, choking him as Babcock starts another count...1...2...3...4....Irish whip out of the corner from Williams as
Shade hits the opposite set of turnbuckles sternum first."
Eric Chaney: "Box Office staggers backwards out of the corner, and has the back of his head drilled into the canvas with a backdrop driver courtesy of
The Beverly Hills Brat, Joe Williams - who is back to his feet and gloating at finally being able to put Tripp Shade down."
Larry Watters: "Well the impact of that move could cause some swelling in the head of Tripp Shade, and that is the LAST thing that HE needs. He has to be one of the most conceited, and self centered superstars to come down the pike in a long time. But speaking of swelling heads, will you take a look at Toni Winter, who is up on the apron, showing her support for Joe
Williams with a little kiss on the cheek."
Eric Chaney: "Tripp Shade back up to his feet rather quickly as he sneaks up behind Joe Williams and goes for a roll-up, but Williams sidesteps The
Hollywood Also-Ran and he collides heads with Toni Winters! Winters off the apron and Tripp is hoisted into a GERMAN SUPLEX! Referee Josh Babcock down with the count..."
ONE! TWO! Eric Chaney: "KICKOUT BY SHADE! Williams now shouting at the official about a slow count as he puts a few boots to Shade, who looks to be favoring his neck. It looks like Bad Attitude smells the blood too as he places the head of Tripp Shade between his legs and goes for the double underhook - this could be a pedigree!"
[There is some struggling, but Shade manages to loosen his arms and throws
Williams to the canvas with a double leg takedown. Without wasting a second, Shade goes for a leg grapevine again as Joe Williams struggles to reach for the ropes.]
Larry Watters: "ANOTHER CRITICAL ACCLAIM! But look at the fight in Williams, he is NOT going to go down that easily and Shade realizes it as he now turns it into a slingshot as Joe Williams hits the top turnbuckle jaw first! Shade back to his feet as he goes for a waistlock and a German
Suplex of his own!"
Eric Chaney: "NO! Joe Williams kicks his foot back and catches Shade in the groin! Now it is Bad Attitude off the ropes, and into the air for a rocker dropper, but Shade catches him and holds him up for a WOOOOOOOOOAH!!!!"
[The fans leap to their feet once again as Shade holds the body of Joe
Williams aloft for a powerbomb, and then charges forward - driving the head and shoulders of Bad Attitude into the top buckle with a powerbomb.]
Larry Watters : WHAT A MOVE! Williams looks stunned, and Shade seems to be getting back some of his momentum as he now goes for a whip across the ring into the opposite buckles - and follows in closely with a PICTURE PERFECT
DROPKICK!" Eric Chaney: "He just snapped the bottom of his feet into the jaw of Joe
Williams, as Williams is into the buckles again and Box Office goes for a springboard off the second rope with a twist and a BULLDOG!!! Shade goes for the cover..."
ONE! TWO! THREE! Eric Chaney: "TRIPP WON! TRIPP WON! TRIP-" Larry Watters: "NO! Referee Josh Babcock saying it was only a 2 and 7/8ths as Shade seems a little shocked that he didn't get the pin either. But keeping his head in the match he is pulling Williams back to his feet again, but Joe is just lifeless on the canvas...OUCH! Joe Williams with a low blow that leaves me eating my words..."
Eric Chaney: "...and Tripp Shade eating his testicles as we have a small package!..." ONE! TWO! Eric Chaney: "Shade with a convincing kickout as he is back up to his feet before Joe Williams and quickly forcing him back into the corner for a knife edge chop!"
Larry Watters: "WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!" Eric Chaney: "Thanks for adding to the commentary. What's your name again? Mark?" Larry Watters: (confused) "It's Larry?!?" [Joe Williams clutches his chest in pain as the outline of Tripp Shade's hand can be seen across his well tanned and chiseled physique - and Box
Office lifts him up onto the second rope.]
Eric Chaney: "Shade now taking things up the strands as he makes his way into the second rope as well - but Williams catches him with an elbow to the jaw. Tripp looks like he may be on a trip to La-La Land as he is not exactly solid with his footing on the second rope, and Williams has a double handful of hair."
Larry Watters: "It looks like Joe Williams is preparing to drive his knee into the chest of Tripp Shade from that second rope! If he hits this one it will be as good as over. But not only for this match, but quite possibly the career of Tripp Shade! He could break Box Office's stern-"
[Before Larry Watters can finish his sentence a groggy Tripp Shade points towards the crowd screaming out...]
Tripp Shade: "Colt Anderson? What are you doing here?!?" [As Joe Williams, Referee Josh Babcock, and the over 3,000 in attendance follow the pointing finger of Tripp Shade he drives his fist into the groin of Bad Attitude and wraps his arm around Williams, who is still on the top.
In one fluid motion he hoists the body of Williams into the air and drops back onto the canvas with a stun-gun like maneuver that drives the face of
Bad Attitude into the canvas.]
Eric Chaney: "Joe Williams with a mouthful of canvas as Tripp Shade rolls out onto the ring apron, and starts towards the top. But what is that...that....BITCH doing on the ring apron?"
Larry Watters: "She obviously saw that low blow perpetrated by Tripp Shade - who leaps from the top with a frog splash into an elbow! It's The Box Office Smash! Shade with a cover, but there is no referee to see it as he is distracted by the beautiful Winter and I think Tripp just realized this as he makes his way over and shoves the official out of the way."
Eric Chaney: "Look at Shade licking his lips! It looks to me like he is going to plant one right on Toni Winters! DO IT! DO IT!!!"
[With the fans showing their support for him, Tripp looks around the building as his trademark smug smile crosses his face. Suddenly he raises his fingers and pinches the nose of the beautiful Toni Winter, and slaps his fingers away with his other hand in a Three Stooge-esque move!]
Larry Watters: "WHAT THE! That's no way to treat a woman! And Joe Williams obviously agrees as he spins Tripp around and drives a boot into his stomach! Williams obviously feels the momentum shifting back in his favor as he locks on a front facelock and gives the motion for THE ATTITUDE
ADJUSTMENT!!!!" Eric Chaney: "One quick, snap and this one could be over - and here it is!" [As Williams spins around for the diamond cutter to complete the combo, he is shoved off by Tripp Shade and connects face first with the official, sending Babcock out of the ring and to the floor of The Lawlor Events
Center.]
Larry Watters: "The ref is out, and Williams staggers back into a full nelson! Shade goes to hook the leg for a reverse Russian leg sweep, but is spun around by a chair wielding Steve Jackson."
Eric Chaney: "THAT got the attention of Tripp as he points a stern finger of warning at Jackson - but gets caught in a full nelson of his own from
Joe Williams."
Steve Jackson: (To Tripp Shade) "Eat STEEL!!!" [With a look of anger on his face, Joe Jackson hauls off and swings at
Tripp Shade...] + PLOINK + [...with the weakest chair shot ever witnessed by mankind.] Tripp Shade: (To Jackson) "What the hell was THAT! You trying to kill me with laughter?!?!?"
Larry Watters: "Will you listen to Tripp taunting Steve Jackson - and Joe
Williams doesn't seem to be pleased with it at all as he screams at his manager."
Joe Williams: "What was THAT! YOU HOLD HIM!" [Joe Williams spins Tripp Shade around and has Steve Jackson hold him as
Williams scoops up the chair and winds up for his shot.]
Larry Watters: "This is gonna hurt!" + CRACK + [As the fans take to their feet, Tripp Shade lifts both of his feet and drives the chair back into the face of Joe Williams - knocking him senseless as he kicks back into the groin of Steve Jackson.]
Eric Chaney: "Jackson is prone on the canvas, and Williams comes with a wide right hand - but Shade ducks and locks in his full nelson, and hooks the leg! This could be FADE TO BLACK!....."
Larry Watters: ...and it IS! He just drove the face of Joe Williams down into the groin of his manager with that front Russian leg sweep and now points down at the legs of Joe Williams. It looks like it is time for THE
CRITICAL ACCLAIM!!!! He has the legs of Williams, but here comes Toni
Winter to save the day!"
[As Winter charges across the ring, Tripp reaches out and grabs her by the hair with two hands and plants a very Un-Hollywood Like kiss on her lips, throwing her to the canvas.]
Tripp Shade: "THERE! You got what you want. You happy? You just kissed the
BIGGEST Movie Star in Hollywood!"
Eric Chaney: "Winter is DOWN! Steve Jackson is DOWN! But here comes Joe
Williams in a last ditch charge from behind!!!"
Larry Watters: "SPINEBUSTER BY TRIPP SHADE! He just lifted Williams like he was a lightweight and holds onto the legs, struggling to turn him over with that Texas Cloverleaf! Williams is fighting it - but Shade looks to be getting the upper hand!"
Eric Chaney: "Shade with a kick to the behind of Joe Williams for good measure and he has him over. Will you listen to Shade, he is providing his own commentary..."
Tripp Shade: "I take out the two henchman, and finally I have the bad guy on the ropes ready to give..."
+ TAP TAP TAP+ Tripp Shade: (Looking back over his shoulder at the blatantly tapping Joe
Williams) "Now we add the drama to it, as the referee slowly rolls into the ring and sees Joe Williams giving up."
[DING! DING! DING!] [The head of Tripp Shade spins around in shock as Referee Josh Babcock is back in the ring calling for the bell as Joe Williams is still tapping out with all his might.]
Eric Chaney: "Talk about calling your shots!!!!" Tripp Shade: (To himself) "Talk about drama!!!!" (Ring Announcer): "Your winner of this contest "Box Office" Tripp Shaaaaaaaade!!!!!" [The sound of "Numb" by U2 echoes from the rafters as Referee Josh Babcock calls for the break, and a seemingly exhausted Tripp Shade drops to his
knees - his arms raised in victory.] Eric Chaney: Folks we will be right back with the biggest match of the night! Stick around for more Sunday Night Mayhem!
[Cut to commercial.  The first commercial is the following.] [The screen fills with flashing pictures of NeWF superstars such as Johnny
Holiday, Joe Williams, Brian Collins, "Ace of Hearts", and the rest.  As each one flashes up we hear the familiar deep voice over the pictures.]
VOICE OVER:  Wanna know what is going on with the NeWF? [We see pictures of some of the superstars out of the ring.  Sean Cage getting inside a limousine.  Then we see Kurt Castle working out. Followed by Tyrone Parker entering a restaurant.  The final photo is Adam Rodgers washing his car.]
VOICE OVER:  Ever wanted to keep up with your favorite NeWF athelete? [The pictures come faster and faster for about 3 seconds until they just explode into a black screen.]
VOICE OVER:  Well now you can! [The words come over the screen in white letters, they say "NeWF Hotline"] VOICE OVER:  Just call this number and get the latest scoop on your favorite superstars of the NeWF.
[The screen fades to total blackness again.  The numbers come over the screen in white letters.  It says 1-888-NeWF-INF.]
VOICE OVER:  Just call the following number, One-Eight-Eight-Eight, N-e-W-F, I-N-F.  You must be 18 to call.  Children should get their parents' permission before calling.
[Fade to black.]
