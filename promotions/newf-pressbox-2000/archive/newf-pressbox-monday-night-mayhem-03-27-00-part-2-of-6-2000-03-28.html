Monday Night Mayhem (March 27, 2000) (Part 2 of 6)

28 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We return from commercials to our announcers live at ringside.] BF: WE ARE BACK! It is time to get on with the action as our first match in NeWF history is about to take place!
LW: We got "The Devastator" versus a wannabe Cowboy! EC: Baldwin is not a "wannabe Cowboy"! LW: He will want to be a cowboy rather than a wrestler when Castle gets done beating his ass in the ring. That is for sure!
BF: Well Baldwin is one tough customer, so I doubt it will be nearly as easy as you say it will, Larry. In fact my money is on Baldwin if all things are equal.
EC: Glad we agree Bob! BF: Alright it is time to take things to the ring with Steven Stewert! Take it away! [The screen cuts to the ring where Steven Stewert is standing in the center holding the microphone and a index card.]
SS: Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is a one fall, twenty-minute time limit match. It is the first match in NeWF history!
[Crowd pop.] SS: Introducing first standing six foot, seven inches tall and weighing an astounding three-hundred-five pounds! Hailing from Seattle, Washington, here is
"T H E D E V A S T A T O R" K U R T C A S T L E ! ! ! [The lights dim a bit as "Bulls On Parade" by Rage Against The Machine begins to play. Just as the drums kick in there is a horrendous explosion of red fireworks on the ramp. As the fans begin to cheer and the smoke fades away, Kurt Castle emerges onto the scene. You notice the droplets of water fall off of his short black hair. His upper body very apparent. As
Castle holds his arms up high, the camera pans in on Castle, and you can see the word "Devastator" printed on the legs of his shiny black pants. As
Castle slowly makes his way to the ring, he points at a few fans and nods his head, acknowledging them. Castle proceeds to the ring and climbs up on the apron. He looks around at the crowd for a moment and ducks under the top rope into the ring. Castle leans back on a set of ropes repeatedly as he stretches out a bit.]
BF: Castle sure does look ready for this battle! EC: He is quite a large man, that is for sure. I didn't know how big he really was! He is intimidating!
LW: See ladies? I told you he was a "real deal". Now maybe you will trust me when I say something.
BF: Well let's not go that far. EC: Here comes his opponent! [Crowd pop.] SS: And his opponent! From Las Vegas, Nevada; he stands six feet, ten inches tall and weighing in at three-hundred-five pounds! Ladies and gentlemen, I give you
"B L A C K J A C K" B A L D W I N ! ! ! [The lights dim, smoke pours from either side of the entryway, and laser lights play around the arena as "Everything About You" by Ugly Kid Joe begins to play over the sound system. Then, as the lead guitar cuts in,
"Black Jack" Baldwin emerges from the obscured entryway with "The Godfather" Vito Scapelli at his side. Baldwin is wearing a black windbreaker over his wrestling gear with "Go Wild" scrawled across the back in purple lettering, and a pair of Ray Bans. He high five's fans, and chats light heartedly as he walks slowly up the aisle...Upon reaching ringside, he takes off the jacket, and hands the sunglasses to a pretty woman in the crowd, after flirting with her briefly.]
BF: My Goodness! He is actually bigger than Castle! LW: That's a big Bitch! EC: I guarantee you he is not what you say he is. He is taller, and better than Castle and this is why this match will probably see the ring move around a bit. These guys both carry alot of weight and that is far from an exaggeration.
BF: You are right about that! LW: (in awe) Damn! EC: Surprised that I know something there Larry? LW: (still in awe) DAMN! EC: That's what I thought. BF: Well Referee Chris Elliott has the men ready to go and here comes the bell! [DING! DING!! DING!!!] Watters: There is the bell! Chaney: (Pounding his hand on the desktop) "Will you listen to the fans clapping their hands and stomping their feet in anticipation as "Black
Jack" and "The Devastator" stand nose to nose in the middle of the ring, trash talking one another."
Frankhauser: "OOOOH! Well I don't know what Baldwin had to say, but Castle just shoved him back and few steps and we have a lock-up!!! Both men now jockeying for position as it looks like Baldwin is the more powerful of the two men - no wait a second, a burst of strength from Castle as he moves
"Black Jack" back towards the ropes" Chaney: "Referee Chris Elliott right there with a count as Castle has Baldwin in the ropes...one...two...three...will we get a clean break? NO! Baldwin rolling Castle into the ropes now, and another reversal on the part of "The Devastator" as he has "Black Jack" in the corner and begins to lay into him with repeated boots to the stomach."
Watters: "My my my, it looks as if "The Devastator" is looking to make a name for himself right out of the gate as he is beating "Black Jack"
Baldwin right down to the canvas as these fans here in The RCA Convention Center continue to root him on." Frankhauser: "I don't know if these fans are into the actions of Kurt Castle as much as they are into the action of the very first match-up in NeWF History. This is a huge opportunity for both participants to make a name for themselves, and leave a great first impression on the crowd as
Castle takes Baldwin back to his feet and whips him into the far ropes." Chaney: "Well as Baldwin rebounds off the ropes, here comes Castle with a charging clothesline that is quickly ducked by Baldwin as both men are sent off the ropes again and WOAH! FLYING FOREARM from "Black Jack" sending Kurt
Castle to the floor of the Convention Center." Watters: "The fight now moving to the outside as Baldwin steps out through the ropes and pulls himself up to the second rope, just waiting for Castle to get back up."
Frankhauser: "Castle using the guard rail to pull himself back up to a vertical base, as he seems a little stunned by that forearm shot from
"Black Jack" - but here comes Baldwin with a double axe-handle off the second rope!" Chaney: "You have to admit that a move of that nature is not something you would normally see out of a three hundred pounder like Baldwin, but they know what is at stake here. It is a chance for glory and fame as Referee
Chris Elliott begins a count on both men." Frankhauser: "Elliott a little caught up in the action as well, which in turn gives these guys a little extra leeway that they obviously weren't expecting."
Watters: "Baldwin pulls Castle back up to his feet and whips him towards the guard rail, but "The Devastator" manages to reverse it and instead it is "Black Jack" UP AND OVER THE RAIL!"
Chaney: "These fans getting a first hand experience of what the action of The NeWF is all about as Baldwin gets to his feet and finds himself coming face to face with a few short European Uppercuts courtesy of The
Devastator." Frankhauser: "Well Baldwin looking to take control as he blocks that uppercut from Castle and takes his arm, dropping him armpit first across that steel railing. "Black Jack" now waiting for Castle to get back up as he climbs atop the guard rail and jumps - "
Chaney: "YES! Right into a powerslam from "The Devastator" as he head bounces off the arena of The RCA Convention Center! Castle now clearly as the advantage as he rolls into the ring looking for the approval of this capacity crowd, which he doesn't exactly seem to be getting."
Watters: "And Castle is taking exception to this response from the crowd here in Indianapolis as well as he climbs to the second rope and shows these fans EXACTLY what they should be cheering for."
Frankhauser: "Well Larry, it sounds to me like you like Kurt Castle here tonight?!?" Watters: "Well just look at him, he is the far superior athlete. He has the body, the look, and most importantly he has - "
Chaney: "Baldwin right behind him!" Watters: "No! The mindset to take him places here in NeWF, but look at "Black Jack" just waiting to nail him from behind as Castle turns around and is scooped up by Baldwin in a gorilla press slam!"
Frankhauser: "These fans are very appreciative of the strength of Baldwin as he carries the three hundred pound Castle around as if he were a small child and OH!!! Baldwin just deposited Kurt back out on the arena floor, as he drops him out over the top rope."
Chaney: "A powerhouse AND an aerialist! Baldwin seems to have it all as he ignores the orders of Referee Chris Elliott and goes back to the floor, looking to take it to the air again!"
Watters: "The man they call "Black Jack" is now just stalking Castle, as he waits for The Devastator to get back up to his feet and levels him back down to the floor with a diving clothesline off the ring apron."
Chaney: "It looks like The Devastator is getting devastated! As Baldwin rolls back into the ring to the cheers of this crowd. But he could be making the same mistake that Castle made just a few seconds ago?"
Frankhauser: "The Devastator groggily rolling back into the ring after that power display from Baldwin. And now Baldwin also is aware of Castle as he kicks him in the head, telling him to get to his feet."
Chaney: "Baldwin just toying with The Devastator as he has things totally under control. Now calling for a test of strength as Castle gets back up to his feet."
Frankhauser: "Castle looks up at the arm of Baldwin, as obliges his request for the test of strength as he raises the opposite arm. And here we go -"
Watters: "BOOM! Kick to the stomach by Castle as he quickly wraps his arm around the head of the doubled over Baldwin and sends him face first to the canvas with a devastating DDT! (Sarcastically) He has this match firmly in control."
Chaney: "He did, Baldwin just let his guard down for a second as Castle rolls him over for a cover...ONE...TWO...Kickout by Baldwin as Castle pulls him back to his feet."
Frankhauser: "Kurt Castle now driving his knee into the stomach of "Black Jack" Baldwin, backing him into the corner as he wraps a hand around the throat of the Las Vegas gambler. But Referee Chris Elliott -"
Watters: (Interrupting) "Cabin Boy!" Frankhauser: "Huh?" Watters: "Referee Cabin Boy is right there with a count on Kurt Castle, looking for him to break the choke a The Devastator takes "Black Jack" out of the corner with a snapmare."
Frankhauser: (Still confused) "His name is Chris Elliott?" Watters: (Laughing) "You call him what YOU want, and I will call him what *I* want to. Okay?" Chaney: "Kurt Castle now off the ropes as he drives the heel of his feet into the back of Baldwin's head, giving him a serious case of whiplash, and now pulls Baldwin back up to his feet."
"The Devastator" Kurt Castle: (To the fans) "It's OVER!!!" Frankhauser: "Castle showing a little confidence now as he lifts up the body of "Black Jack" Baldwin and sits him on the top rope, facing to the outside of the ring?!?"
Watters: "Kurt Castle goes to the outside of the ring now, and drives his elbow into the head of "Black Jack" Baldwin for extra measure. And it may have been enough to daze the gambler as The Devastator scoops him up into the air in a military press!!!"
Frankhauser: "We saw this move a little earlier on the part of Baldwin, when he dropped Castle unceremoniously onto the floor - and now The
Devastator is looking to return the OH MY GOD!!!" Chaney: "OH MY GOD!!!" Watters: "WHAT A MOVE! Castle just dropped Baldwin from the military press backwards and into a Samoan Drop off of the ring apron! He has to have broken Baldwin in two!!!"
Frankhauser: "Will you listen to these fans! They are going wild as The Devastator rolls back into the ring and soaks in the cheers of these people who obviously likes that train wreck that Kurt Castle likes to call The
Needle Drop!" Chaney: "Referee Chris Elliott now with a count as Kurt Castle joins him and these fans in counting Kurt Castle out -"
ONE! . TWO! . THREE! . FOUR! Frankhauser: "Well Castle may want the count-out, but don't count Baldwin out yet! He is getting back up!!!!
FIVE! . SIX! Chaney: "Referee Chris Elliott, Kurt Castle, and these 2600 fans here in Indianapolis looks to be in shock as "Black Jack" Baldwin is pulling himself back up to his feet!"
Watters: "COUNT FASTER!!! SEVENEIGHTNINETEN!!!" SEVEN! . EIGHT! Frankhauser: "HE DID IT! "Black Jack" Baldwin has just gotten back into the ring after a move that would break most normal men in two! But he seems to be favoring his neck and back as Castle angrily pulls him back up to his feet."
Watters: "Kurt Castle obviously feeling that he had this match won right there! But it as good as over as Castle whips Baldwin into the rop - NO
Reversal!" Chaney: "Baldwin with a reversal as Castle comes sailing off the ropes and is scooped up into the air for THE ROULETTE!!!"
Frankhauser: "Baldwin lifts the body of Castle up into the air for his tilt-a-whirl piledriver that he calls The Roulette, but Castle quickly up and over the back of "Black Jack" - reversing it into his own variation of the tombstone!"
Watters: "BAM! The neck of Baldwin just bent sideways as Castle dropped him down to the canvas with Total Devastation! That HAS to be it! There is no way Baldwin can get up after that one-two combo of The Needle Drop and
Total Devastation!" Frankhauser: "Well Castle seems to feel the same way as he presses both shoulders of "Black Jack" Baldwin down to the canvas, and lets out a little laugh of his own. Referee Chris Elliott - "
Watters: "Cabin Boy!" Frankhauser: "Enough with The Cabin Boy jokes already, as he make the count!" ONE! . . . . TWO! . . . . . THREE!!!! Watters: He did it! I told you he would dominate this match! Chaney: I can't believe this young man beat Baldwin! He is really something! Watters: Damn right he's something...a future NeWF Champion is what he is! Frankhauser: This was quite a match to kick off our federation's first show! Wow! Two major heavyweights duking it out with the younger Kurt
Castle ending up on top for the three! Chaney: Great way to start indeed, and there is more where that comes from after the break, right?
Frankhauser: Yes there is, but the break is not immediate! Right now we are going back to the ring where Steve Stewert is about to have a special guest! Steve, take it away!
[The screen cuts to the ring where Steven Stewert is standing in the center holding the microphone and a index card. He smiles as he begins speaking.]
Steven Stewert: Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome from Boston Massachusetts, the most Extreme man in professional wrestling. "The Extreme Warrior" War Child!!! ["Dragula" by Rob Zombie plays over the sound system, and War Child steps through the curtains. War Child is wearing a long black leather trench coat, black sunglasses, black jean shorts cut off at the knee, and a War
Child "Distributor of Pain" T-shirt. War Child hops up on the ring apron, steps through the ropes and walks towards the center of the ring. The Music cuts off.]
Steven Stewert: Welcome War Child couple of big things happening tonight, and one of them is a match between you and "Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin.
Your thoughts on tonight's contest? "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: My thoughts on tonight's contest? First of all dumbass this is no contest, not by a long shot. What we have here tonight with me and Brandon Franklin is equivalent to the old days in Rome
Italy, when they used to put Christians in the Coliseum to do battle with the lions... [War Child removes his Sunglasses and looks dead into the camera.] "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: It's going to be a slaughter tonight. I'm the Lion and he is the Christian. I will chew you up and spit your battered carcass out into the center of the ring. Cameraman, get a close up of my eyes... That's right... Brandon Franklin, look into the eyes of your the-eliminator-danny-williams, of your ultimate end. These are the eyes that will light the fire that burns you to a crisp. Do you see mercy in these eyes Brandon, look deep, look really deep, you won't find none. The only thing you'll find in these eyes is fire. The fires of War, and the fires of hell that'll burn your soul from your body. Tonight Mr. Franklin, you meet destiny in a head on collision that you cannot possibly survive. I've said it all before, you really want to know the true meaning of the word Chaos, all you have to do is look in my direction. I am at the extreme top of chaos. I've seen wrestlers crumble in that ring with just a mere look from me. I've had wrestlers beg me for mercy, the harder they begged, the more I took pleasure in causing them extreme pain.
Steven Stewert: Those are pretty strong words, you sound like you have a personal grudge against "Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin..
[War Child begins to laugh, not a belly laugh, but a small, smirking, evil laugh] "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: I have nothing personal against Brandon Franklin. It's really just business, if I had something personal against him, he wouldn't just be going to the hospital tonight, he'd be heading for the morgue. But he's only going to the hospital tonight and that I guarantee. Brandon, I'm a man of my word and I see you chose to ignore at least some of my words. Remember, back when the NeWF camera, in my War
Room, where I train. I gave you advice in that interview. I told you to run, and run fast away from the NeWF.... I see you chose not to take my advice nor did you heed my warnings, I'd call that a fatal error in judgment. Now you have to suffer the consequences of your decision.
Steven Stewert: Will you be scouting your next opponent tonight, "The Natural" Adam Rodgers, as he takes on "The Main Event" Brian Collins? [War Child loses his sadistic grin and goes straight-faced] "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: That question is even more stupid than you actually appear. I will study the tape of his match, as my concentration is on the destruction of Pure Chaos, Brandon Franklin tonight. I will say this, Adam Rodgers better look at tonight's match, because the pure chaos that happens in this ring tonight, is in Adam Rodgers' future. Look at it
Rodgers, study it closely because Brandon Franklin's demise tonight is your future in one week at the Davis Arena. Count on it.
Steven Stewert: Final thoughts War Child? "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: Brandon Franklin, are you ready? Are you ready for the torture you're in for, the total destruction of your mind, body and soul? I hope you've said your prayers. I hope you asked whatever
God you worship for forgiveness, because that's the only thing that'll save you tonight. The path of destruction starts tonight. The first pavement will be laid right over Brandon Franklin's stinkin', rotting, burned carcass. I just hope the NeWF can survive the fire that'll be started tonight.... Ash to ash, dust to dust...Fade to black....
["Dragula" by Rob Zombie plays again, and War Child leaves the ringside area, through the curtains back to the locker room and the screen then switches to again show our ringside announcers.]
BF: It sounds like War Child is VERY focused tonight guys. LW: Just like a champion! This guy has an agenda and that is what a real man needs to have in his career, an agenda.
EC: It looks like that agenda includes a certain Adam Rodgers as well as Brandon Franklin. LW: Yes it does, and why not. Two losers are always better than one! (chuckles) BF: Fans we have to take a break. We will be right back with more Monday Night Mayhem! Stay Tuned! [The screen switches to show the mouse clicking on the rumors page link.] VOICE OVER: Go to the rumor section to find out some information that even the Board of Directors may not want the public to see. It is all here right now, and you have to do is point and click.
[The screen shows the mouse going back to the home page and the logo from the screen literally jumps from the computer screen to fill the entire screen.]
VOICE OVER: Yes NeWF has gone cyber with their new web site. So get you hook up here! [The voice trails off and we see the logo stay on the screen as the following URL come just under the logo]
