Sunday Night Mayhem [June 4, 2000] (Part 3 of 7)

7 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We return from commercials to our announcers live at ringside.] EC: Folks this is an exciting time in NeWF as you can see. We are in the middle of a National Title Tournament and Lord knows it has been, fun...but now this match is business for both men.
LW: That's right. These men are both contenders in EVERYBODY'S book and they drew each other in the first round. Just not fair when this happens and idiots like Don Gately and Jack Mannon get in as well.
EC: Well I am not going to expound on that point, but this match does have
Brian Collins against Kurt Castle. Both are considered top men in this league, so it is strange that they face off in the first round. Just one more way to prove this tournament really WAS a blind draw.
LW: Well I still say that Hopper is trying to screw Collins here because we all know how high he is on Castle. Collins better get ready for his Job tonight.
EC: Now Larry, come on. Hopper is much more fair than that. LW: I am just not sure of that anymore. EC: Well why don't we watch the match and find out who can be the better man tonight and move on in the tournament.
LW: Alright, whatever. EC: Let's go to Steven Stewert in the ring! [The screen cuts to the ring where Steven Stewert is standing in the center holding the microphone and a index card.]
SS: Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is a one fall twenty minute time limit match. It is a First Round match in the National Heavyweight Title
Tournament!
[Crowd pop.] SS: Introducing first, he is one of the largest men in our sport and he causes devastation wherever he goes. Ladies and Gentlemen, I give you
"T H E D E V A S T A T O R" K U R T C A S T L E ! ! ! [Castle walks out with no music and no explosions. The crowd pops for him, but he seems focused. He enters the ring and just raises his arms to let the crowd know he is here and into the match. The crowd erupts as he acknowledges them.]
EC: What focus from Castle. LW: What the fact he doesn't have pyro? EC: Yeah. He has canceled everything to stay focused on this match. I think that is admirable.
LW: I think it is stupid. You always have a major entrance to try and intimidate your opponents. It is the law of the jungle.
EC: What jungle is this? LW: Evansville? EC: You really sicken me sometimes. LW: Thanks! [Crowd pop.] SS: And his opponent...formerly known as "The Main Event", but now simply goes by his given name. Here is...
B R I A N C O L L I N S ! ! ! [The lights dim, as Black Sabbath's "Iron Man" begins to play throughout the arena. As the fans hear the music, a chorus of boos, mixed with a few mild cheers goes throughout the arena, as well. Then, Brian Collins makes his way from behind the curtains. He is not dressed in his usual attire,
Brian is wearing a pair of black pants, and a pair of Grey and black New
Balance tennis shoes. Brian is acting a little bit more cocky than usual.
He makes his way down to the ring yelling at the fans. He stops in front of the ring, and looks out into the crowd. He makes his way up the ring steps and enters the ring. The music dies down as Brian begins to get ready for his match.]
EC: Coolins looks like a new man since the dropping of his nickname, I think he has a good chance here.
LW: I agree. As long as he kept the edge he had. It was that mean streak thatmade him worthwhile forme.
[DING! DING! DING!] Eric Chaney: "Here we go with a match that has been a few weeks in the making as Kurt Castle has not been too forgiving with the way Collins was soooo accepting of the whole Colt Anderson fiasco."
Larry Watters: "Well what ever happened to letting bygones be bygones? I mean if Chris Hopper can overlook what Collins did - due to his natural talent - then I am sure that Kurt Castle will be able to do the same. Don't you think?"
Eric Chaney: "You tell ME, as this one is underway with Castle powering
Collins back into the corner and BAM! Drills him with a stiff forearm that is certain to leave a mark."
Larry Watters: "Hell, I felt that one, as now Castle takes the staggering
Collins and WOAH! Hoists him high over his head in a military press, as if he were dealing with a small child. What a feat of strength!"
[As the fans cheer wildly for The Devastator, he takes a few steps before depositing Brian Collins rudely, groin-first on the top rope.]
Larry Watters: "I tell you, since Brian Collins dropped that "Main Event" moniker, he has been in search of a new nickname...May I suggest "The
Soprano" or maybe even "High-C" - because that is what he is going to be signing tonight!"
Eric Chaney: "Speaking of dropping The Main Event, it looks like Castle just dropped The Main Event to the floor here of The Lawlor Event Center.
Collins slowly getting back to his feet as Castle points a stern finger of warning in his direction."
[A shocked Brian Collins waves his hands at the ring, and turns towards the aisle as he makes his way to the back. Seeing this, Referee Josh Babcock quickly goes to work on counting him out.]
ONE! TWO! Eric Chaney: "What the? Where does Collins think he is going?!? Not only is he walking away from Kurt Castle - but he is also walking away from HIS opportunity to win The NeWF National Championship."
Larry Watters: "Relax, it's a time honored tradition called "stalling" for time." Eric Chaney: "Time honored tradition? Who was EVER known for doing anything like that?!?" Larry Watters: "Moi!" Eric Chaney: "Oh, that makes it tradition alright as the count continues on
Collins." THREE! FOUR! [A frustrated Kurt Castle tries to get out of the ring, but he is held back by Referee Josh Babcock who attempts to keep this contest under control.]
FIVE! SIX! Eric Chaney: "How can you call this stalling? Brian Collins is taking the high road out of here, all because he can't hang with a man that was so aptly named "The Devastator" Kurt Castle?"
Larry Watters: "Just watch..." SEVEN! EIGHT! [Upon hearing the officials count reach eight, Collins takes off down the aisle towards the ring once again.]
NINE! Larry Watters: "Told you! Collins is back in the ring under the bottom rope..." Eric Chaney: "...and back out again! This is ridiculous, and Castle is NOT going to stand for it as he shoves Referee Josh Babcock out of the way and gives chase to the former "Main Event" Brian Collins."
Larry Watters: "Well Collins is a spry little fellow as he slides back into the ring, and quickly gets back to his feet - running the ropes aaand...BAM! He just caught Kurt Castle right in the kisser with that baseball slide."
Eric Chaney: "I will admit that Collins was one step ahead of Castle there as the former "Main Event" is off the ropes once again and dives THROUGH the ropes like a torpedo, forcing the lower back of Kurt Castle against that retaining barrier."
[Before Castle even has a chance to react to the pain, Brian Collins pulls him back up to a vertical base and lays into him with repeated open hand chops that echo throughout The Event Center.]
Larry Watters: "Castle let his temper get the best of him, and as soon as he flew off the handle, Collins seized opportunity by the sack and is exploiting it to it's fullest extent. Now it is Collins taking it back into the ring as he rolls Castle in and makes his way up to the top rope. What does he have up his sleeve?"
Eric Chaney: "He's waiting for Castle to get back up to his feet, and here he comes with a double axe-handle NO! Castle just caught him in a waistlock and RELEASE OVERHEAD BELLY TO BELLY!!!"
Larry Watters: "What were you saying? Collins just rolled with the suplex and is back up to his feet, and Castle has NO clue what is waiting behind him..."
* SMACK * Larry Watters: "...a SUPERKICK from Collins that sends Castle to LaLa Land. Collins now down for the cover as Referee Josh Babcock is there for the count..." ONE! TW- Eric Chaney: "Kickout by Castle, as he has a world of fight still left in him. Collins does seem a bit shocked, because he hit nothing by jaw with that superkick - and by his expression thought he had the match-up won."
[A dismayed Collins shoves Referee Josh Babcock for giving him a slow count - but not one to backdown, Babcock shocks Collins when he shoves him back.] Larry Watters: "WHAT! Who does Babcock think he is, putting his hands on the wrestlers? Somebody needs to learn what their job is! But Collins will not be detered as he drives the bottom of his foot into the side of Kurt
Castle's head, knocking him for a loop again."
Eric Chaney: "Brian Collins is REALLY on his game tonight. He has planned for this match well as he pulls the larger Kurt Castle back up to his feet, and whips him into the ropes. He drops his head for a back body drop and
NECKBREAKER! Collins telegraphed that one, and Castle took advantage of it as he finally has a chance to catch his breath."
Larry Watters: "Well that move may have been a little more devastating when you consider the size of Castle - BUT he does have the weigh advantage, and that is an extra 45 pounds that Collins does NOT have to carry around. That could very well play a factor in the match-up, as it is now Castle pulling
Collins up and applying a front chancery."
Eric Chaney: "The Devastator once again displaying his tremendous power as he hoists Collins up into a suplex position and holds him....and holds him...and holds him....what uncanny balance on the part of Kurt Castle who finally drops him back with a textbook suplex!"
[As Kurt Castle rolls to his knees and goes for a cover on Brian Collins the fans turn their attention to the aisle as the seven foot "Superstar"
Sean Cage makes his way out through the curtain and onto the entrance ramp.] Larry Watters: "We have company and a cover as Referee Josh Babcock drops to make the count..."
ONE! Larry Watters: "EASY kickout by Brian Collins as the basketball oaf that we all know and love as Sean Cage watches on. What business does he have out here anyway? I mean it is obvious that Castle has decided to move on past his little "better man" rivalry and focus on some gold here in NeWF."
Eric Chaney: "...and that is exactly WHY Sean Cage is out here - to scout out the competition like a REAL professional. He wants that NeWF National
Title in the worst way, and he wants to know the men he may face down the line from every angle."
Larry Watters: "Well Brian Collins happens to be at a rather uncomfortable angle right now as Kurt Castle continues to work over the back with a single leg Boston Crab - and Referee Babcock is right there listening for the submission."
Eric Chaney: "Suprising to see a move like this out of Kurt Castle as he is much more power oriented - but from time to time you have to pull something out of your sleeve to surprise the competition if you want to remain on top in this sport."
[The camera zooms in on the face of Brian Collins as he shakes his head back and forth, not wanting to give up - until finally Castle gives up on the hold and Collins rolls to the floor again.]
Larry Watters: "Brian Collins again breaking the pace in this one as he rolls to the floor, obviously favoring his back."
Eric Chaney: "Which is actually a good move on the part of The Devastator as one of his favorite moves, The Needle Drop focuses exclusively on the lower back of his opponent. But look at Collins waiting out the count again..."
[At ringside Brian Collins snatches a beer out of an older fans hand and takes a mouthful of the beverage as the official continues with a count.]
SIX! SEVEN! Larry Watters: "A refreshment break for Brian Collins as he now returns to the ring, climbing up on the ring apron - where Kurt Castle comes to meet him. Castle has a handful of hair, and he is pointing to the steel ringpost!"
Eric Chaney: "Castle is going to run Collins head right into the steel, but
NO! Brian puts on the breaks...and spits that mouthful of beer in the face of Kurt Castle blinding him!"
Larry Watters: "Now Castle forcing his way back into the ring as he shoves
Referee Josh Babcock out of the way, and burries a boot in the stomach of
Kurt Castle. He's giving the sign! Could this be The Frankhauser
Killer...er THE MAIN EVENT!" [As the fans groan in disapproval, Brian Collins easily lifts Castle up and drives his head into the canvas with his piledriver.]
Eric Chaney: "DAMMIT! He hit it, and now he goes for the cover! This one is over!" ONE! TWO! THRE- Eric Chaney: "NOOOOO!!!! Castle rolled his shoulder at the last second, and you can TELL that Brian Collins was NOT expecting that. This is HIS move!
That is his trademark, and Kurt Castle just kicked out of it!" Larry Watters: "I can't believe it myself! Castle had him - I think that
Babcock was just a little slow on that count. But Collins is NOT going to let the move derail him. He is sliding to the outside of the ring and is ousting the ring announcer from his seat."
Eric Chaney: "This isn't hardcore rules! He can't bring a chair into the ring with him on a whim!"
Larry Watters: "Are YOU going to stop him?!?" [Frustrated with the kickout, Brian Collins slides the chair into the center of the ring and quickly pulls Castle back up to his feet. With a look of sheer determination he lifts Castle up again...]
Larry Watters: "He is going to piledrive Castle onto the cha- what the hell is Babcock doing? He just pulled that chair out from under Castle's head, and Collins does not look pleased."
Eric Chaney: "You can see the steam just rising off of Collins as he is screaming at Referee Josh Babcock, who was only enforcing the rules. You can NOT bring a chair into a regular match!"
[The argument between Babcock and Collins soon turns to a shoving match as
Kurt Castle tries to shake out the cobwebs. Suddenly the crowd turns hostile as a man in black jeans and a NeWF Staff T-Shirt climbs over the rail and slides into the ring.]
Eric Chaney: "It's SOLO! Solo is in the ring!" Larry Watters: "Kurt Castle has no idea what is behind him, as he is watching this physical shouting match between Brian Collins and Referee
Josh Babcock. But he is going to find out as Solo takes Castle by the shoulder and spins him around for a right han-NO! It's blocked!"
Eric Chaney: "Castle just put a stop to the surprise attack of Solo, and knocks him silly with a European Uppercut. Solo is dazed, as Castle lifts him into the air...Could this be The Needle Drop?!?"
[With one fluid motion, Kurt Castle drops Solo down into a fireman's carry position - but Solo manages to slide off his shoulders and spins Castle around.]
Eric Chaney: "NOOOO! Solo lifts the body of Castle up into the air and drives his back into the canvas with a Spine Bomb Slam! But he doesn't look to be done as he quickly climbs to the top rope, and drives all 275 pounds into the chest of Castle with a somersault senton. That was a crushing one two combo on The Devastator."
Larry Watters: "Devastated is more like it, as Solo bails out of the ring and Brian Collins finally surrenders his argument with Referee Josh
Babcock." Eric Chaney: "You don't think Collins KNEW about this do you?" Larry Watters: "I don't think that it really matters as the result is the same either way. Brian Collins drags the unconscious body of Kurt Castle back to his feet and lifts him up....for....a....BRAINBUSTER! That is IT!"
Eric Chaney: "Referee Josh Babcock drops to make the count...." ONE! Larry Watters: "What's up with the count?!?" Brian Collins: (To Babcock) "COUNT!" TWO! Eric Chaney: "This is KILLING Babcock to make the count! As Brian Collins grabs the hand of Josh Babcock and forces it down to the canvas for the count!"
THREE! [DING! DING! DING!] [Seeing the result of the match a satisfied Sean Cage nods his head and turns towards the locker room as an excited Solo makes his way towards the back as well.]
Steven Stewert: "Your winner of this contest, moving on in The NeWF National Title Tournament - Brian Colliiiiiiiiiiiins!!!!" [A relieved Brian Collins pulls himself to his feet, laughing in the face of Referee Josh Babcock as a determined Kurt Castle suddenly rolls out to the floor.]
Larry Watters: "Brian Collins is moving on in the tournament, while Kurt
Castle moves to The TV Title Battle Royal - but where in the name of God is he off to now?!?"
Eric Chaney: "Kurt Castle is going after Solo, who is still gloating to the fans up on the stage. GET HIM! GET HIM!"
[With the fans cheering him on, Kurt Castle reaches out and grabs the shoulder of Solo - going for a right hand. Just as he is about to connect another figure appears behind him, blocking the punch.]
Eric Chaney: "It's Eric Vanguard! The new head of security is out here to break up the ruckus as Solo drives the palm of his hand into the nose of
Kurt Castle - blinding him with pain."
[Before Solo can strike a second time he is swarmed by security guards and quickly escorted towards the locker room as Vanguard tosses a bloody faced
Kurt Castle to two of his guards.]
Eric Vanguard: (To Kurt Castle) "YOU have to learn to keep that little temper of yours in check!"
[A scowling Kurt Castle spits a mouthful of blood in the face of the head of security as Vanguard reaches into his Jean pocket and pulls out an extendable nightstick - pointing it into the chest of Kurt Castle before walking away. The screen switches back to the announcers at ringside.]
EC: What an ending! LW: Vanguard looks like he could still go a match or two...You see how
Cage reacted to him?
EC: This was interesting indeed! Fans we have to take a quick break, we will be back with more Mayhem!
[Cut to commercials. As they run, the following NeWF commercial runs.] [The screen is black as a deep, booming voice comes over the screen. As he talks the first sentence, the words come up on the screen.]
VOICE OVER: Do you want to be a Champion? [The screen switches to shows the NeWF Television title revolving around. Then we see the NeWF Light Heavyweight Title revolving slowly as the light glimmers off of it.]
VOICE OVER: Have you ever tried to be like your favorite NeWF Champion, but always fell short when you wanted to celebrate a title? Well now you don't have to.
[The screen switches to show a kid walking in front of the camera with a replica of the future NeWF National Heavyweight Title around his waist.
The child has his arms in the air and he is acting like he just beat Nicklaus Kain.] VOICE OVER: Now you can own this replica of the belt that men have fought for. This belt has had some great Champions, and now you can put it around your waist!
[The screen shows one of the replica belts with a black screen behind it as it rotates and seems to glimmer in the light.]
VOICE OVER: For only $49.95, plus shipping and handling, you can truly become an NeWF Champion. This belt has a synthetic rubber belt with a gold plated face on it. This belt was made to be as real as possible, and it is.
[The screen switches to show a kid placing a nameplate in it's lower center and you can hear him say, "It says my name! Cool!"]
VOICE OVER: That's right, you can even have a special nameplate engraved for it so the belt is truly yours forever.
[The screen now shows parents watching their son and smiling.] VOICE OVER: And parents, this belt is one hundred percent safe as it has no sharp edges and everything is made from safe chemicals and plastics.
How can you go wrong. [The screen again shows the belt alone with a black background, as it rotates around and just glimmers in the light.]
VOICE OVER: So call 1-800-NeWF-GFT and order yours today. Rush delivery is available and satisfaction is guaranteed. Become a Champion today!
[Fade to black as the rest of the commercials begin to play.]
