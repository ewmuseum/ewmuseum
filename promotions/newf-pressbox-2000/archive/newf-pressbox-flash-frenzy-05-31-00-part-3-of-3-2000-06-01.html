Flash Frenzy (5/31/00) (Part 3 of 3)

1 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1- "The Devastator" Kurt Castle 2- "The Fallen Angel" Taryn St.Archer 3- Nicklaus Kain 4- "Superstar" Sean Cage 5- Knightfall =================================================================== "The Devastator" Kurt Castle =================================================================== (The scene opens in a large, dimly lit bar. The camera pans around a bit a small groups of people are shown. They all appear to be mingling amonst themselves, not harming anyone. As the camera pans around a little more, it focuses in on a groups that is much larger than the rest. They all seem to be huttled around one person. As the camera gets a close up view, you see
"The Devastator" Kurt Castle showing a few moves to a young man. The man appears to be about 20. Castle slowing puts the man in a headlock and gently flis him over into a hip toss. Castle gets to his feet and spots the camera. He helps the other man up and gives him a slap on the back as he turns to a table and grabs a t-shirt off of it and puts is on. He then turns to the camera and nods his head a little bit as he begins to talk)
KC: I should of known that you guys would have followed me here. After all, the NeWF changes hands again and the National Title tournament is soon to begin, so you wanted a few comments from your's truly; Kurt Castle.
(Castle makes his way through the crowd of people and makes his way out of the bar, slapping a few hands on the way out. Caslte begins talking again as he is walking, meanwhile the camera is a step or two behind, trying to catch up with him.)
KC: All things that go aorund come around don't they Colt? The resheem of cowardess and underhanded deeds that gained you control of this company just so happened to be the same deeds that not only lost you this company, but your freedeom too.
(Castle lets out a half laugh) KC: You see Colt, from day one you used people, you tried to manipulate people, and you tried to buy people off just like you've been doing your whole life, but it didn't work with me. That's the main reason you didn't like me wasn't it. It was because I was one of the few men that had the balls to stand up to you. and we didn't mind raising hell in the process.
You had better consider yourself a lucky man that you're locked away where
I can't get hold of you......But, there are a few men that you left behind, and unfortunately, the just don't get the same option of hiding behind steel bars like you do. And one of those men who stand out in my mind from the others just so happens to be the first man to jump ship and join you back in April....."THE MAIN EVENT" BRIAN COLLINS.
(Castle stops walking and turns around to face the camera. The camera zooms in for a full screen view of Castle's face. Castle continues to speak)
KC: Collins, you're destiny was set when you attacked "Too Cool" on the first ever Sin City Showdown, who by the way, is YOUR boss again. You see
Collins, I've been gunning for people like you since the first day I walked into this organization. You're one of the ones with no respect for anyone, one of the ones who think everyone owes them something, and one of the ones actually believe that they are superior to everyone else. Well I got news for you Collins, you're not.
(Castle look turns very serious) KC: June 4th, Reno Nevada, you have no choice but top get in the ring and face me one on one for a chance at being the first ever NeWF National
Champion. But to me Brian, it's more than a tournament, it's more than titles, and it's more than proving how good I am. It's going be about extracting revenge for everyone that got screwed at that hads of Colt
Anderson. It's about "The Devastator" systamatticly destroying the very evil that you stand for.
(Castle to around and heads back towards the bar that he had exited moments ago) KC: The fact of the matter is, your done......Plain and simple. You already burnt the proverbial bridge that you need to cross back over in order to obtain greatness here. The only thing is is that I don't feel sorry for you one bit. You're going to get what you deserve. So on the forth, it IS personal, it IS going to be a war, and it's going to be the beging of MY time and MY era. And there's nothing that you, or anyone else can do about it, so deal with it.
(Castle then opens the door to the bar and re-enters as the scene goes to complete darkness.)
=================================================================== "The Fallen Angel" Taryn St.Archer =================================================================== (Scene opens in a run down church. Broken glass from several of the stained glass windows lay scattered all over the floor and several of the pews are strewn all over the place turned over. The sun slowly sinks into the sky casting red and yellow all along the inside of the church. Where the pulpit once stood, a young man wearing a trench coat, black pants, black shirt and sunglasses is sitting and deep in thought.)
Man: (looking up at the camera) Oh, is that you Manny? Cameraman: Ahhhh... no, I’m not Manny. I’m from the NeWF. Man: (puzzled) Where’s Manny? I thought he was with you? Cameraman: Nope, no Manny at NeWF. Man: (shrugs his shoulders) Well, ok. I was just hoping Manny was with you. I miss him. Cameraman: Umm... is there another reason you wanted me here other than seeing if there was a Manny?
Man: (looking up at the camera) Oh yes, there is. For those of you who don’t know who I am, my name is “The Fallen Angel” Taryn St.Archer. And over there against the wall is David. Say hello David. Good. (looking back at the camera) David’s kinda shy, so forgive him if he doesn’t talk that much. Once he gets to know you, he’ll open up.
(The cameraman looks around and only sees nothing.) Taryn: Now, the for the reason I came to NOOF. Cameraman: Ahhh... it ain’t NOOF, it’s the NeWF. Taryn: And I’m giddy, so it’s NOOF to me. Where was I? Oh yes, the reason why I came to the (he shoots a shot at the cameraman) N-O-O-F is to make sure everything runs smoothly for those who just want to compete.(shaking his head) But right off the bat there is a threat to that happening.
(standing up) And that threat comes from none other than “Mr. Titlist” Adam
Calloway. Cameraman: Oh come on. Adam’s a great guy. What could possibly be wrong with him? (Taryn stares intently at the cameraman and slowly walks towards him until he’s right up against him.)
Taryn: Are you saying that I don’t know what I’m doing by suggesting that this little golf sh-- is just that, a piece of sh--?
Cameraman: (backing up) All I’m saying is that I like Adam, he’s never really done anything to me to make me not like him.
Taryn: (looking past the cameraman) What David? You mean that this is yet another place where I can’t do anything to a person who works here who isn’t a wrestler? Damn. (pointing at where he believes David to be) All I have to say is that Manny deserved it that one time.
(The cameraman turns his head to look where Taryn’s pointing, but he sees absolutely nothing)
Taryn: (backing off of the cameraman) You should thank David because if not for him, you’d be hurt. Well, are you going to thank him or not? Because if you don’t, David’s going to get mad, and when David gets mad, people get hurt.
Cameraman: (not sure what to do, just like real cameramen) Ahhh... umm... (not totally sure where to look) Thanks... David. Taryn: (sitting against one of the overturned pews) Now, was that so hard? Noooo, it wasn’t. Now, back to the topic of Adam Calloway. (a stern look comes over his face) Adam, you are just the first of many who’s going to know the effects of the Falling Angel. (Taryn gets up from his seat and starts walking towards the front of the church) Adam, for your sake, I hope you realize the mistakes you’ve been making. For the Lord knows I don’t want to have to sevearly hurt you.
(Just as he says this, a car slowly drives past the church) Taryn: You might want to get out of here in the next 30 seconds. The gangbangers are out, and you’d be perfect as a moving target. (looking at the door) David, lets go.
(Scene fades to black as the cameraman’s footsteps shuffle out the door.) =================================================================== Nicklaus Kain =================================================================== (Scene opens to the middle of a desert, somewhere in the East. The bright sun beats down on the sand and anything foolish enough to be among it, heating it and causing an oven-like effect. The only things that can be seen are cacti and buzzards, save a large rock formation and a dark figure.
The figure moves towards the rocks like a predator stalking it's prey. Once it reaches the rocks, it lies across one of them and throws back its hood. Nicklaus Kain sits on the rock, staring down at the sand. The camera moves in close and he speaks.)
NK: The time has come. It has come that all the nay-sayers, all the detractors, all the critics have been waiting for. It's like a predator, hunting for prey, searching. Hungry and tired, they cry out in the night, like a terrored child to his parents, awakened from a nightmare. It's a despicable happening, but one that is imminent. It's a terrible thing, and you know it would happen on a Friday, but then, what doesn't? I can't begin to contemplate what the aftermath will be like, but no doubt they will line the aisles, all knowing deep down inside they shouldn't but they can't stop themselves. It's no secret, but it's not well known. It all comes down to whether or not people will try. But they cannot try, for they seem to be weak. They think themselves weak and are convinced of it, and then something such as this comes along and puts it all into perspective. Ace of Hearts and Sean Cooley. Adam Calloway, The Big Chill,
Tripp Shade. No one can predict it, but they feel it. They KNOW it, but they don't know. It's like Lou Natic, it's almost as if Joe Williams and the War Child. El Puerco and Kurt Castle?
(Kain smiles.) NK: Not quite. Johnny Holiday and Rick Wallace. The Ides of March have past, but the heat of the core of the centuries burns on. No longer will the wheels of oppression turn. Cabana Boyz and The Rawls Brothers, ha ha!
No one will ever even know what will become of it. No one will see it coming because it will happen in a flash so bright that the eyes of the on-lookers will catch flame, it will seem. And even if all the sand in the desert of the Earth were to turn to glass by some enormous heat source, even then would the tides be turned and the skies brought to the ground, the clouds mixing with the soil. Vegetation and life, flora and fauna, water and dirt, all rounded together to bare witness to the glory of the might of the predator of light. Let go the will to suffer and continue the fight against dark light, for it is a war of attrition, and the aura effect brings strong the numbers of the past. Can you smell it? Can you feel it?
Does it speak to you in tongues you understand? If so, may whatever God you worship help you, it won't be long now.
(Kain puts his hood back on and lies there, motionless.) [Fade to Black] =================================================================== "Superstar" Sean Cage =================================================================== (Sean Cage, decked in suit and tie, wearing a pair of sunglasses. is coming out of the hotel at the Press Conference in Los Vegas and is getting into his limo with Miss Keri, who is wearing a Red dress and is also decked in the expensive glasses.)
(Before he can do so he is stopped by some press members) Press: Sean can we have a few minutes of your time. Sean: YEah sure. Press #1: What are your thoughts about what has just went down today? Sean: Its about time. Hopper started this thing and was screwed out of it. Now we get a fresh start. 0-0. Press #2: Thursday you have a match with El Puereco aganst Matt Stone and
Nichalaus Kain. Care to comment?
Sean: Of course, my tag team partner, don't know much about him, except his manager reminds me alot of Colt Anderson. Puereco would be alot better if he would lose him.
(cage then pauses as Keri wispers something in his ear) Sean: OK thats fine. My opponents well i think its well documented about my dislike towords Nick Kain and Matt Stone, don't know much about him either.
Should prove to be an interesting contest. Press #3: Any predictions for the match? Sean: ITS SHOWTIME. (Sean and Keri climb into the limo and shut the door, the scene fads as the limo is driving off)
=================================================================== Knightfall =================================================================== [New York City, 6:00 PM] [Pure air… we can breathe pure air, for what could be the first time, in
New York city. The sun, descending upon the horizon… leaving the sky filled in the decadent red, that emerges from Sundown. The moon is nearly visible to us...almost claiming it’s domain… symbolical, perhaps. Well, the day must give way to the night… and that is what we are witnesses to, here today. The camera having already a bird’s eye view… begins descending upon the earth below...passing by the skyscrapers, buildings, and high structures that define the urban jungle that is New York. We scroll the skylines for a few moments...our destination? Unknown to us as of right now. But nonetheless, curiosity drives us… and we, follow.]
[Soon enough, we reach what appears to be a local airport… and the camera, finally reaching a low level, as it goes on trough the airport doors. We can see the people awaiting arrivals, or maybe even awaiting their own departure. But we, somehow, someway, know, that we are searching long and high for a single person… maybe a slow sinking feeling that it’s obvious… or maybe just a wild guess. From the distance then, a man is seen walking towards the camera… well-built… young looking… and familiar… very… very familiar. His eyes hidden behind a pair of black shades, while the man himself is dressed in a pair of black leather pants, and a deep dark red, silk shirt. Whilst he runs his hands trough his short blonde hair, we realize now, that this man is none other than former AWF intercontinental, and world champion, “The Originator” Randy Kreed. The cocky, self-centered, ever interested bastard seems to be carrying his travel bags along with him… as well as his all-for business trademark look… He tunes into his watch.]
[Randy]” Go[Bleep]mn it… barely caught the damn flight to New York… now I gotta find this… knughtfell? Knaghtfall? Knitefull? Can’t even remember his freakin’ name… all I remember is damn Hopper tellin’ me a limousine’s waiting outside for me… it better be. After all, now I got to manage some grappler who I haven’t heard squat from… Damn work obligations.”
[Randy walks outside the airport… casually catching a black limousine which seems to be waiting for someone within his sights… he walks towards the apparent driver… who holds a white sign with black lettering, reading
“Randy Kreed”… “The Originatior” approaches the driver, and soon enough converses with the awaiting worker…]
[Randy]” Yeah, I’m Kreed. You from NeWF, right?” [Driver]” Yes, sir.” [Randy]” You know the address where I can find this kid I’m supposed to manage in Hopper’s federation?”
[Driver]” Kid? Sir, the man stands at a blistering 6’8, weighting over two hundred sixty pounds… he is no small kid…”
[Randy]” 6’8?!?! The kid’s bigger than me? Aw hell, I knew there would be a flip-side to this damn job… I tell you, if my friggin’ salary wasn’t as good as it is, Hopper would lament the very day he met me!”
[Driver]” Erm… yes, sir. Are you ready to go?” [Randy]” What? Yes, yes, I’m ready. It’s just the polluted air in New York… clogs up my head…”
[Driver][Whispering]” Or maybe it’s that over sized ego of yours…” [Randy]” What was that?” [Driver]” Nothing sir, please, step into the limousine…” [The driver opens a door for “The Originator” as Kreed steps in, and puts his bags into the limo. The driver heads to his correspondent seat, however, his thoughts are caught out loud…]
[Driver]” Self centered, egotistical Son of a bitch… never liked him in the
AWF… now I got to work with the ba[bleep]rd…”
[The driver steps into his seat, as Randy pulls out a bottle of champagne from a icebox within the very limo… he pours a bit in a cup for himself… the driver starts the engine and begins driving across the streets of
Manhattan…Kreed speaks to the driver…]
[Randy]” So, have you seen this Knughtfell fella’?” [Driver]” It’s Knightfall, and no. Nor would I want to. What I’ve heard he does to people… scary.”
[Randy]” Oh, come on, he can’t be that anti-social, after I use the irresistible Kreed charm, he’ll fall down like a lap-dog and do my bidding…”
[Driver]” Um, sir, your bidding?” [Randy]” Yeah, see, I figure that if Randy Kreed can manage a World Champion, Randy Kreed can get tons of money as a manager, as well as a good reputation, and a bit more fame than I already have.”
[Driver]” So you expect this man to do everything you say?” [Randy]” Of course.” [Driver]” What if he’s… I dunno… hard to control?” [Randy]” Are you implying that I could very well fail at controlling him?”
[Driver]” No I—"
[Randy]” That I could perhaps, be un-able to handle him?” [Driver]” I didn’t—“ [Randy]” Who the hell are you to give your opinion, wheel-boy? You little un-grateful, insignificant, piece of backwater, hick trash…”
[The driver, obviously angered by the remarks of Kreed, accelerates the limo, leaving Randy’s champagne to fall all over him… finally, after speeding a few blocks, the driver stops… we look outside the window, and realize that rain pours down from the now, nocturne sky… And as we pan back into the limo, we can see that “The Originator” Randy Kreed is nothing less than furious…]
[Randy]” What are you trying to do, punk? Get me killed? Get us both killed? For crying out loud, I’d understand if you’d want to take your own life, but leave “The Originator” out of it… I’m far to valuable to die in a car accident with an imbecile such as yourself… and look you condescending asshole, you poured that champagne all over me!!!”
[Driver]”[smiling, almost… grinning…] We’re here Mr. Kreed…” [Randy][Looking out the window]” Here? What, you mean in that dark alley out there?” [Driver]” That’s where I was told he was found… now… good luck Mr. Orginiator…” [Randy]” Friggin’… fraggin’… sonnuva…” [Kreed steps out of the limo, and begins walking towards the alley… and as soon as he sets foot out the vehicle, wet now by the rain, he begins cursing low-toned under his breath… meanwhile, we can see the driver grinning from his window…]
[The camera over-paces Randy… getting to the end of the alley first, than
“The Originator”. And with that accomplishment… we see a man… fitting the description given earlier by the driver… six feet eight and a half inches, two hundred seventy pounds or so… this monster of a man embraces himself in silence, in the shadows. Trench coat billowing from the falling rain, from the cold night breeze… his dread locked, colored black hair, lifted by the wind, whilst his eyes remain hidden behind a pair of shades… he stands underneath the rain… well… they say the rain provides cleansing for the soul… or perhaps, his is a soul, that can never be cleansed… as we wonder in awe… Randy Kreed reaches our position, and sees the man… his first impression, that of fright… but soon… finding that blasted confidence of his, Kreed finds the courage to approach the man, and begin, or attempt to begin a coversation…]
[Randy]” So… you’re Knightfall?” [‘Knightfall’ looks down towards “The Originator”, and while his emotion-less expression remains… he speaks… grim-toned…]
[Knightfall]” Yes.” [Randy]” Nice to meet you…I’m Randy Kreed, former Intercontinental, and
World AWF champion… better known to the world as “The Originator”. I take it you’ve heard of me…”
[Knightfall]” No.” [Randy]” Well… erm… nice to meet you, I’m sure the pleasure’s all yours…”
[Knightfall]” You do not amuse me, little man.”
[Randy]” Heh. [Gulp]. Well, as much fun as it is standing in the rain kid,
I think we’ll catch a cold out here, so, let’s leave—“
[Knightfall]” Who are you?” [Randy]” I’m your new manager – “ [Knightfall]” I asked for no such thing.” [Randy]” But NeWF president Chris Hopper has assigned me as your manager… he thought I might be an asset in your career.”
[Knightfall]” I need of no one’s help to get what I want, much less of a person as small, and pathetic as yourself, now get out of my sight…”
[Knightfall turns his view to the sky once again… while Kreed drowns the in the profound words of the dark figure… Randy angered by the remarks of
Knightfall, gets in the face of the big man, exchanging a few words of his own…]
[Randy]” Now listen here, punk! Nobody talks to “The Originator” like that! You will do as I say, and you will like it!!” [Knightfall, devoid from all expression, doesn’t even anger himself a tad... instead, in a moment of quick action… he grabs Randy by the throat, and lifts him up into the air with one hand… as “The Originator” ‘hangs’,
Knightfall speaks…]
[Knightfall]” Give me one reason… one… simple… reason, why I shouldn’t end whatever future you may have ANYWHERE… one mere, simple reason.”
[Randy][Choking from the pressure applied by Knightfall]” *Gasssp*, *Gasssp*, I—I—“ [Knightfall]” I thought so… I should systematically take you apart… but your suffering is not as important to me as the suffering of every other
NeWF superstar.”
[Knightfall lets go of Kreed, as Randy falls to the floor in a quick heap…
Knightfall walks away, turning his back on “The Originator”. As he does so, he speaks before departing…]
[Knightfall]” Treasure every moment you have from now on, and consider yourself lucky… because you live to tell other of what transpired here.
Nevertheless, if you interfere in my business once again, your luck runs out, like will the luck of every other man in the NeWF roster. Knightfall is coming… and by the time he reaches the NeWF, the beginning of the end will meet it’s birth.”
[Randy]” Gaaaasp….Gaaaaasp….[Grabbing his throat]… W-Wait!!!!” [As lightning and thunder crash upon the skies above… Knightfall disappears into the distance, as Randy begins walking back to the limo… slowly, still grabbing his throat, he steps into the vehicle, as the driver looks to the backseat, whilst he starts the engine…]
[Driver]” How did it go?” [Randy]” You wouldn’t believe…” [Driver]” What the hell happened?” [Randy]” Knightfall happened… he grabbed me by the throat, and lifted me up high…” [Driver][Smiling, and mocking Kreed]” I thought “The Originator” was going to have Knightfall as a lap-dog to do his biding…”
[Randy]” Don’t you even start, punk… but by some reason… I grew to like the guy… and call me crazy… but I believe having a man as violent as he on my side… is simply perfect.”
[Driver]” From what you’ve told me, I doubt he’s on your side… We going back to the airport?”
[Randy]” No… I want to stay in New York… I want to convince this fellow… because if I get a hold of him… he’ll be the most unstoppable force in the
NeWF yet!”
[The limo then drives off into the distance… as the camera begins ascending towards the heavens... in doing so… we notices a man watches patiently from the edge of a rooftop… crouching… while his trench coat flaps wildly against the wind… we realize it is Knightfall watching as the limousine departs… and as we attempt to look trough his shades… into his eyes… the camera shifts to static, as the image burns out…]
