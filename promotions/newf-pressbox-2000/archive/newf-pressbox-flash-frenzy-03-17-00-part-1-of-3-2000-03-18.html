Flash Frenzy [3/17/00] (Part 1 of 3)

18 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1- "The Natural" Adam Rogers 2- "Showtime" Matt Stone 3- "The Devastator" Kurt Castle 4- "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams 5- Humpty Dumpty ============================================================== "The Natural" Adam Rogers ============================================================== [The camera opens with a majestic scene of a beach at sunset. The off-white sand rolls up and down as far as the camera eye can see. The white caps from the ocean's waves pound onto the shoreline in a pure, unbroken rhythm. And the bright orange sun is beginning to creep behind the horizon, and the vast ocean in front.]
[The beach itself is inhabited by a few people... vacationers, mostly, based on the appearance of things. Most of them have already gone to dinner or night clubs, probably. A few birds can be heard squawking, and soon afterward we see them fly past the camera. As they disappear to our right, we see a figure, and a muscular one at that, walking into our scene.]
[The camera keeps its distance for the time, but is close enough to catch many of this man's features. He has golden blond hair, cut short and moving gently in the breeze. His body is very well defined, though not overly bulky or thick. He wears only a white t-shirt, with some unreadable writing on the front, and a pair of blue shorts, coming down just above his knees. He begins to near the camera, and we now can see his crystalline-blue eyes, which are focusing on something away from the camera itself. Finally, he comes close enough to the camera for us to take a good look at him, and we see a handsome man, probably in his mid-to-late-20's.
Stopping only a few yards in front of the camera, he bends over and picks up a green towel, proceeding to toss it over his shoulder. As he does, we finally hear him speak.]
Man: "Welcome to my hometown. Beautiful scenery, isn't it?" [He offers a kind smile, showing his white and perfectly aligned teeth. Gotta wonder if those are caps - 600 bucks a tooth.] Man: "First, allow me to introduce myself to the NeWF viewing audience, and to all my fellow competitors who probably don't know who I am...yet. I am
Adam Rogers, from right here in Naples, Florida. If you know wrestling,
I'm sure you know the Rogers name, and yes, that is my family. You probably know that my father and grandfather were both long-reigning World
Champions... and now they have passed the torch of the family legacy to me."
[Adam stops rather suddenly, and fixes his gaze to the left of the camera, seemingly focusing on nothing in particular. It doesn't take long to snap out of that trance and continue.]
Rogers: "You see, from the time I was a young boy... 6 years old to be exact... my brother and I have been trained for this very sport. Brett has since suffered a very serious knee injury, leaving yours truly [points to himself with his index finger] to carry the load."
[Without warning, Adam moves past the camera toward a step of wooden steps leading to a boardwalk. Sure enough, he begins walking up the steps, forcing our cameraman to rush to ctach up with him.]
Rogers: "For a few years, I experienced limited success [Adam pauses] Tell you what, why don't I skip all the career history, that's all boring stuff anyway. Why don't I fill you in on something much more important... what I stand for. You see, the longer this business has lived, the more it's warriors have thrown away values, morals, ethics... all just to get ahead.
But there are still a select few of us who show this great sport the honor, the dignity, and the respect it deserves. And I am one of those few."
[Reaching the top of the steps, Adam proceeds to turn and walk down the boardwalk toward a parking lot, where a few cars are scattered around (not
literally - you know what I mean).] Rogers: "As I have been looking at the talent list distributed by NeWF management, I've seen a lot of attitudes that I don't much care for. Looks like this is going to be another place where it's every-man-for-himself, a place where the majority forgets the rulebook, and more importantly, the people who ultimately pay their salaries...the fans. Nothing, and I mean
_nothing_ makes me sicker than to see some no-talented sleezeball attack people from behind, yell at the fans, and cheat their way to the top.
That's _not_ what this sport is all about, it was never meant to be about that, and I'll be damned if I'm going to let crap like that go unnoticed."
[The intensity in his voice, and on his face, is beginning to rise. By now we've walked into the parking lot, and Adam has moved to stand beside a shiny red Lexus - must be his ride - sweet!]
Rogers: "The fact is, I've been successful in this game _without_ taking short cuts and cheating the fans out of their admission, and you can bet the farm that same success will carry right on over to the NeWF. So guys like that country bumpkin Allen Taylor... the guy named after some crappy
80's movie, The Big Chill... 'The Main Course' Brian Collins...the Canadian fat boy Snow Storm... and anyone else who wants to take this sport's great legacy and destroy it... there's a new sheriff in town, and his name is
'The Natural' Adam Rogers."
[Adam gives the camera a last glare before opening the door to the Lexus and climbing in. With a SLAM, the door shuts, and with a roar the engine comes to life. The cameraman begins backing his equipment up as the scene fades out.]
============================================================== "Showtime" Matt Stone ============================================================== (A man is sitting in a director's chair with the word "Showtime" on the back of the chair. He has short brown hair, not crew cut, but not long enough to hang down any. He is light-skinned and looks to be pretty muscular. He seems to be somewhere in the range of 6'6 and about 275 pounds. He, apparently, is "Showtime" Matt Stone. Matt is in a studio, not necessarily a typical studio, it looks kind of dilapidated and the first thoughts are it is a low-budget independent film studio. Showtime sits up in the chair and begins talking.)
Showtime: I'm "Showtime" Matt Stone, here in NeWF to show you REAL entertainment. They don't just call me Showtime for nothing, you know.
I've had several starring movie roles in my time, and not a one has ever failed. I am >THE< greatest wrestler slash movie star slash entertainer this world has ever known. Not to brag or anything. I wouldn't want you to think I was bragging.
(Showtime has a thoughtful, yet dumbfound look on his face.) Showtime: Oh well, not like you people that see this matter anyway. I don't expect many people will see this, oh, what, new fed + no exposure= no ratings! Well, I'm here to change all that! I'm going to take this fed straight to the top, in what surely will be some sort of record time for federations making it to the top. Many people say, "He'll change moods on you at any time" and "He suffers from multiple personalities and he can't be trusted" and other, not so nice things. I'm here to say right here and now that my psychologist and I have achieved great things in our sessions, and I think I'm past all that stuff. I'm "Showtime" Matt Stone, here for your viewing pleasure!
(With this, Matt stands up out of his director's chair and tries to rip off his shirt, ala Hulk Hogan, but seems to be having trouble with it.)
Showtime: Arrrrrrrrgh! (Matt rolls on the floor grimacing in pain. It seems he caught a cramp in his arm while trying to force his muscles to rip off the shirt.)
Showtime: It's OK! Everything's OK! I'm gonna be OOOOOOKKKKKK! (Matt gets up and just walks away from the camera, obviously mad at the way in which he was able to screw up his first ever appearance on NeWF TV.)
============================================================== "The Devastator" Kurt Castle ============================================================== (The scene opens in an empty, dark room. There is no apparent sign of anyone in the room. As the room begins to lighten you can faintly make out a figure sitting in a chair. As the camera begins to pan in a little further a voice is heard)
Voice: For the last year and a half, this was it........Darkness (The figure stands up and you get a good shot of him. He's decked out in a long, black leather trench coat and a pair of black jeans. He bares no shirt. The upper body is very defined in noticable. As the camera focuses in a liitle more on the upper part of this man, you see his face, which is very clean cut. The man again begins to speak)
Voice: A year and a half of sitting on the sidelines. A year and a half of not being able to do what I love the most in the world. But that is no more. The time has come for me to reemerge onto the scenes of the professional wrestling world.
(The room becomes fully lit, and you can now see many plaques and awards on the walls of this room.)
Voice: For those few who don't remember me, or don't know me, I'm Kurt
Castle. But most people know me as "The Devastator".
(The camera zooms in a plaque which holds a picture of a bloody, long haired Kurt Castle with his hands held high in the air)
Kurt Castle: I have been to hell and back in my career. I've done it all. From riding 12 hours a day on a bus to get from house show to house show, to flying the leer jets to nationally televised events. My career has already established my career over the last ten years. But something happened to me in 1998 that lead to my "darkness" if you will. In the height of my career, where I thought I was indestructable, and where I thought I was unbeatable, I was just the opposite. In just one swing of a bat my career was nearly ended. I broke my knee in 3 different places, and the doctors said I may never walk again.
(Castle laughs a little bit and then begins to talk again) Kurt Castle: They also said that I would definately never wreslte again. Well that's why I have come to the NeWF. Number one, to prove the doctors and everyone who said that I could never do it again wrong. And the second, and most importnat, is to prove to myself that I still have it.
(The camera pans back in on the bloody picture of Castle) Kurt Castle: My appearances have changed over time, but my beliefs and mentallities will always be the same as they always have.
(Castle goes over to a case that stands in the corner of the room and opens the door and lifts two belts out of them. He turns back to the camera and again begins to speak.)
Kurt Castle: The PFWL retired it's world heavyweight championship and it's Pro championship on ME. They recognized and respected the character that I am am and the beliefs and ways that I practice. I have proven to thosands of fans over the years, and hundreds of athletes that I NEVER say die. I am not a quitter and I never will be. When I go out to the ring, not matter what kind of match it is, I treat it like it's my last.
(Castle sets the belts back in the case and closes the door.) Kurt Castle: Some people like me...... Others don't. It really doesn't matter to me which side you stand on. If you're with me, than cosider yourself lucky. If you're against me, then you'll have hell to pay. Just ask some of the greats that I have had fall to thte mercy of The
Devastator. Names like Brad Morris, Dan Maddux, Monstrosity, Mentor, Vega
Dread, Curt X, and the lsit goes on. Don't get me wrong , I'm not here in
NeWF to rub my past into everyones faces. I'm sure there are athletes here with the credencials equal to or if not higher than mine, but none of that matters. The past is just that......History.
(Castle beginss to pace back and forth slowly) Kurt Castle: You might be asking yourself right now; What is this all about? Why are you telling us this. And if so, that's a good question. The one thing that I've always believed is being straight up with someone. This rambling of mine is to let everyone in the NeWF to know that I'm for real.
I am the real deal. I do mean business. So this is my call out to anyone here who wants to step into the ring with me. Come on down. You may get lucky.
(Castle holds both arms straight out to the side and does a 360 of the room, looking at the walls)
Kurt Castle: Or you may become the next plaque on my wall..... COME GET SOME! (Castle shoves his hand in the lens of the camera and walks out of the room. The camera does a final pan of The Devestator's "trophy" room before fading into darkness.)
============================================================== "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams ============================================================== [The viewers are faced with a plain black screen whilesome soft dignified piano music plays in the background as if from an old Victrola. A voice comes on, clears it's throat and continues with a few words.]
Voice: <ACHEM> Welcome to the celebration of one of the sport's youngest and greatest heroes....Joe Williams.
[The black screen gives way to a plethora of clips of Williams in the NeCW. He's seen flipping people over into a Scorpion Deathlock, earning submissions. He's seen hugging his manager, Steve Jackson and wrestling fairly in the ring. The piano music continues to play as the young wrestler is seen holding up various title belts, being carried on the shoulders of his stablemates in Next Generation, mixed with other clips of various big matches. The last scene is him standing with Jackson looking into the crowd and smiling before the screen goes back to black. The same, boring voice comes on once again]
Voice: So there we have it....the brief history of a true American hero..we are all richer for knowing a man of his integrity exists in this sometimes slimy business....
[The voice pauses for a second before breaking into a shriek] Voice: YEAAAAAH RIIIIIIGHT!!!!!!! [The piano music jumps into "My Name Is" by Eminem as the clips start coming at a much quicker rate. However, these clips differ from the last.
They show Williams as a boorish degenerate. He feels up women managers, blasts chairs over people's heads, hits low blows, and rakes eyes. He's seen walking to the ring with Next Generation in loud Hawaiian shirts, trashing Jack Micheal's wedding. He brutally beats the league president.
The series of clips end with one of a cocky Williams blowing a kiss to an enraged, booing crowd]
[The scene pans out....watching the series of clips in a studio is none other than "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams. The trademark smirk engulfs his lips. He's wearing baggy khaki's, and a designer print shirt. His eyes are covered by sunglasses and his short blond hair is combed back. He stops watching the TV and looks straight into the camera...still with that arrogant smirk]
Williams: Miss me? You hick rednecks probably thought you were rid of
"The Man", but I'm back...and badder than ever!
[Williams strokes his chin before he continues] Williams: If any of you have been out of the loop...allow me to fill you in! I'm "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams...and I enjoy pretty women, fast cars, and <gestures to his clothing> the hippest threads. Probably like many of you watching this right now. The difference between me and you? I can afford them! That's right...I've not only got the skills and the looks...but also the cash!!!! Jealous yet? You will be. <smirks>
[Joe continues to walk through the studio. He comes to a much bigger TV screen, showing the evil acts of Williams. He turns around in an arrogant fashion and smiles]
Williams: In the next couple weeks..you're going to get to know the real
Joe Williams. And I'll tell you something..you're not going to like me.
I'm the guy who you've always been jealous of. I go to the parties, I get the chicks...hell....I get to get paid to get on TV and kick the living crap out of my opponents. Not like I need the money...as I mentioned before...I'm filthy rich. It's a dream life for all you fools out there...but THE MAN is right here living it up. You've got a right to be jealous.
[Williams ponders another thought before speaking again] Williams: I bet your fat ass wants to put down the Twinkies, climb out of your Lay-Z-Boy and come here to kick my "arrogant butt." Well, I've got news for ya....there's no way! Because I like to brag...I've got a lot to brag about...but I don't lie. I'm just as good as I say I am....probably better. I've got the belts and the credentials to prove it. What do you have? A couple of inbred kids and a mortgage, probably. Good luck, pal.
But if you can't beat me up, who can? You'll find your faceless losers to follow around and cheer for..hoping they'll put me in my place.
WEEEEELLLL....that ain't going to happen either. [Williams spits on the ground] Williams: That's what I think about the NeWF roster. We've got an old loser who's going to hang it up, a third generation kid who sucks as much as his dad and grandpa did, a guy with MPD, and a fat-boy the office doesn't even want to wrestle. Yeah..that's going to be a laugh. I can wrestle circles around ANY DAMN ONE OF 'EM and you can take that to the bank...since you don't have any money there.
[Williams smirks once again] Williams: But you're all thinking <high mocking voice> "Joe..you're so young..you can't compete with all the old goats" In that case, you're an idiot. I'm the guy who puts the goats out to pasture...I'm the future and i'm the best...and that's the truth! Plus, in addition to being young and better conditioned than most of these fools, I also bend the rules a bit more. I'm not gonna lie. Rules are made to be bent and broken..and there's nobody that can do it better than "Bad Attitude." I've been on the bad side of so many promoters i can't count! And I'm only 22! I've been fired, I've been blacklisted, but I've never been shut up! And I never will be! Because money speaks...and my dad's got most of it. So as you enjoy that cheap swill beer you're drinking and prepare to go to bed with your dog-faced wife...remember that Joe Williams will be driving a sports car..going to parties you don't have a hope in hell of attending. And one last thing....
[Joe points to the screen showing the clips] Williams: You think I was bad there.... [Joe turns, smirks and crosses his arms] Williams: You haven't seen ANYTHING YET!!!! ["Cowboy" by Kid Rock plays as the screen fades to black] ============================================================== Humpty Dumpty ============================================================== Title: Welcome to the flabby, jello-quivering and smiley-face adorned world of Humpty Dumpty!
*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* [The scene opens to a close-up of Marshmallow Man masked head. His eyes which is unconcealed by eye-holes are loving & tender, not in the homoerotic buggery kinda way, but as a friendly 'hug ya & kick your ass'
statement. The camera zooms out to the biggest ball of flab that you would never E-E-E-EVER... experiance in your short-short life on earth. The heaving breathing figure of enormity glances into the camera before speaking.]
Humpty Dumpty: Welcome to my world... where the grass is green, the skies are blue, the birds are chirping (gaily too, I might add) & where residents with a halo on their heads walk on air. (He laughs a happy laugh, & breaks into spontaneous singing)...
# I love you, you love me We are happy family...# (Great tune but sucky voice by the way) Humpty Dumpty: My name is Humpty Dumpty, and if you would like to live in my world. (His tune changes for the sadder) Well, it does not exist, it is just a figment of my screwed-up imagination. (He looks into the camera with doleful puppy eyes) You see friends, my life was not exactly a bed of roses, my childhood wasn't like something from Barney's World, all because of this... (He points downward to his sea of flab) sigh... but no matter, through out the years I had learned the craft of survival. I went through trial after trial after friggin' trial, until I saw the light at the end of the tunnel.
[He flashes a warm smile.] Humpty Dumpty: NeWF is that radiant light. Their scouts gave me a tryout, and for once in my life, I'd found out I was good at something. I had found my divine calling in wrestling. Never again do my hafta lag behind with the other fitter, & in the eyes of the masses, better folks. I now can turn my bulk, which was once a curse, into a blessing... to bully the tiny folks into submission. I will belly-to-belly suplex my way to the top. I will throw my weight around & squash whoever is in my path. I will be the new NeWF National CHAMPION!!!
[He almost raises his hands in the air in mock triumph, but seeing his burst of emotionalism, he sheepishly puts his hands down & say in a humble tone...}
Humpty Dumpty: Oops, sorry. Got a tad carried away. I'm not too used to the feeling of victory, how does it feel like? Hmmm.... Anyway, I know that I will succeed, & though I am a practicioner of Ahimsa (Gandhi's teaching of non-violence), I would confine it to my outside-the-ring activities. Make no mistake, you may think I'm soft... But when I club you down, bash your brains in & bounce on your chest into submission with my buns, you will think differently.
[There is a shrill cry of help in the background, a little boy's voice. Humpty Dumpty immediately turns to the source of the scream. The camera then zooms in to a crying little boy & a youth of probably 16 years. The youth is laughing and licking the ice-cream that he'd stolen from the poor little kid. Humpty Dumpty's eyes flare with anger for a moment, before confronting the bully.]
HD: Hey you! Bully: Hey, mind your own busi... (turns around & eyed the 6'5, 467 pounds monster for the 1st time before saying something motivated by fear)...
errrrr... HD: Give back the ice-cream ... c'mon give it back... B: (Still hesitantly shuffling his feet, probably contemplating running away rather than going through this humiliating ordeal of brotherly love)...er...
HD: BEFORE I KICK YOUR ASS!!!!!!! B: (quavering in shock) Ok, ok... please don't hurt me... (His turn to cry...) [After all the sequences in which explaination is not needed, Humpty Dumpty
& the little kid goes hand-in-hand into the really red sunset. There's probably a lesson here, but nah... I guess let sleeping dogs lie. Screen fades to black.]
