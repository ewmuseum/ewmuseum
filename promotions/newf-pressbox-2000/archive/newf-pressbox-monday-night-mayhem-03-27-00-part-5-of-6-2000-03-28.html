Monday Night Mayhem (March 27, 2000) (Part 5 of 6)

28 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We return from commercials to our announcers live at ringside.] BF: Welcome back as we have everything fixed at the booth after that terrible spot in the last match!
LW: I loved it! I ruled the world baby! EC: Now we have to listen to THAT the rest of our careers! LW: No you don't. You can always leave! BF: I don't think so, we all saw what happens when you are left here alone. Fans we are set for a great one now as one of our more popular wrestlers, Adam Rogers, will take on Brian Collins, who just happens to not be that popular in anyone's mind
LW: Except mine! EC: Now THERE is a shock! LW: Collins is the man! He is cocky, arrogant, and can back it up in the ring. He may not be a total dark-sider, but he is just the kind of guy you love to hate.
BF: Well he will have his hands full with NeWF favorite Adam Rogers as it is time to head to the ring!
[The screen cuts to the ring where Steven Stewert is standing in the center holding the microphone and a index card.]
SS: Ladies and Gentlemen, our next match is a one fall, twenty-minute time limit match! [Crowd pop.] SS: Introducing first, a man who claims that he is the defacto main event wrestler in NeWF. Ladies and Gentlemen I give you
"T H E M A I N E V E N T" B R I A N C O L L I N S ! ! ! ["Iron Man" by Black Sabbath begins to play over the PA system. The lights dim, as Brian Collins steps out from the back. Brian is wearing black pants with BC wrote in bold, white letters down the legging. He is also wearing black wrestling boots with white trim. Brian stops after a few steps, and some fireworks go off. Brian waits a few seconds, then makes his way down the aisle. Brian stops and bad mouths a couple fans who are booing him.
Brian stops just in front of the ring, and bathes in the boos. "Iron Man" is played even louder as Brian steps inside the ring. A couple more fireworks go off and Brian raises his arms, as the boos pour in. Brian begins warming up as the music dies down.]
LW: Rogers is a dead man! I am already sick at how he panders to the fans. BF: Well Collins seems capable of giving anyone a great match, but I really think Rogers is being groomed for top positioning in this company.
LW: Well then the higher ups are stupid! EC: Collins might be good, but Rogers is the one wrestler every fan here loves wihtout a doubt. His signing autographs earlier today really got him in the fans' favor.
BF: It sure did, and now they get to see the man perform! LW: Even though he will lose! This is a great day when we can dash the hopes of fans everywhere on night one! I love it!
EC: Here comes the man! [Crowd pop.] SS: [The opening heavy guitar riff of Deep Purple's "Smoke On The Water" hits hard over the house speakers, sending the crowd into a ecstatic cheer as they know who is about to proceed into the arena. Suddenly, the lights dim, leaving a lone spotlight shining at the edge of the ramp that leads from backstage down to the ring. The high-hats kick in during the opening of the classic rock song, and as they do, smoke shoots up from underneath the stage portion of the entrance ramp.]
[The video wall comes to life, first showing the word "PRIDE", followed by a brief clip of Adam Rogers fighting back against an opponent. Next, we see the word "RESPECT", followed by a brief clip of Adam handing someone a championship belt. At last, the words "THE NATURAL" appear, remaining for a few moments, then giving way to a slow-motion close-up clip of Adam turning his head from one side to the other. Finally, after keeping the fans waiting for a few moments, Adam Rogers emerges from the smoke, standing in the center of the spotlight, arms raised triumphantly in the air. Tonight, he is decked out in his traditional violet trunks with the letters "AR" in a cursive script on the right front, Shane Douglas-style black boots with the same writing in white on the outside of each, and a multi-colored ring jacket with "THE NATURAL" airbrushed on the back. The crowd begins to cheer quite loudly for one of the most popular athletes in
NeWF. As he proceeds down the aisle, the lighting effect now changes into a wave of multi-colored laser lights flashing all over the arena. Adam walks down the aisle at a somewhat slow pace, taking the time to slap hands with as many fans as possible. The video wall continues to show various clips of Adam in action and interacting with the fans, with the same words randomly flashing into the mix. Adam finally climbs into the ring, as the house lights come back on. He climbs the ropes in the nearest corner, placing one foot on the middle rope and one on the top, raising his hands in the air again, which draws another huge face pop. He hops down off the ropes and removes his ring jacket, tossing it to the ring crew member below him.]
BF: He certainly is popular! Listen to this sold out crowd! LW: What a bunch of corn-fed morons! EC: They know greatness when they see it. They booed you when we were introduced. LW: Shut up! EC: Well they did! BF: Both men are in their corners ready to go at it! What an epic battle awaits us here...
LW: <snorts> Oh please! "The Main Event" is going to walk all over the so-called "Natural." EC: Yeah, right! As you can hear, the crowd loves Rogers! He's got the skills and experience to be the best wrestling has ever seen...
LW: In my day, I would've... [Watters is interrupted as the in-ring action progresses. Not waiting for the bell, Collins attacks Rogers with a few punches from behind. Rogers fires back a few before the ref breaks it up]
EC: What a cowardly attack!!! LW: That's what we real men call killer instinct boy! And that's why
Collins is going to win. He's had to fight his way to get where he is, while Rogers is running on his family's reputation alone!
BF: Now that just isn't true, Crusher! Rogers is definitely one of the best wrestlers walking today no matter if you like him or not! And there's the bell!
[The bell rings thrice as both men walk to the center of the ring. The crowd loudly chants Rogers's name as he goes to face his opponent. Collins mutters something towards his opponent]
EC: Somebody's talkin' trash!!! LW: Tell him like it is, Brian! BF: It looks like something's about to erupt! [Collins finishes up his thought and goes for a punch, but Rogers blocks and delivers a haymaker of his own that sends Collins to the mat]
EC: WHAT A PUNCH, WHAT A PUNCH!!!! BF: Collins doesn't seem to like being embarrassed like that...he's getting up with an angry look in his eye!
[Collins gets up and charges at Rogers, but "The Natural" takes him down with an armdrag. The crowd goes crazy]
BF: Listen to the crowd..they love Rogers!!! EC: Who doesn't..this guy is good! LW: Come on Brian...think and take it slow... [Collins slaps the mat and gets up once again. He clears his head and goes to the center of the ring to meet Rogers who is inciting the crowd]
BF: Collins raises his hands...he's proposing a test of strength! Rogers seems hesitant to lock up..
LW: Of course he's hesitant...look at Collins! EC: He's wondering if he can trust him, knucklehead! [Rogers looks to the crowd..and finally locks hands with "The Main Event." However, Collins responds with a boot aimed quite low as Rogers slumps to the mat] LW: Oh yeah!! EC: That's a low blow! Do something ref! BF: The official is chastising Collins, but Collins just moves him out of the way as he resumes punching the fallen Rogers!
[The crowd boos heavily as Collins goes to work on the fan-favorite. The official finally breaks things up as Collins taunts the crowd.]
BF: Wait a second...Rogers is getting to his feet! EC: GO!!! LW: TURN AROUND, BRIAN!!!! [Collins eerily turns around and finds himself in trouble with Rogers nailing him with a clothesline]
EC: THE CROWD IS GOING NUTS!!! BF: He darn near cut his head off with that clothesline, and Collins learned a valuable lesson about counting the Natural out..you NEVER do it!
[Collins is picked up by Rogers who delivers a scoop slam before going for the pin] BF: Early cover by Rogers... 1 2 NO!!! Collins kicks out! LW: Of course he did...it's going to take more than a bodyslam to put "The
Main Event" down!
BF: The cover was a little premature, but what does Rogers have planned now. [Once again Collins is brought to his feet, but this time he is whipped over to the corner. He hits the turnbuckles with an audible THUD]
BF: Brian Collins hits with an amazing impact! EC: Something tells me that isn't all.. [Gaining a running start Rogers follows him in and delivers another vicious clothesline] BF: He has to be seeing stars after that move! [Collins rolls out of the ring, drawing boos from the crowd] EC: That wuss!!! LW: Smart move I say! Always go for the breather when you have a chance. [After a while, Collins rolls back into the ring. Rogers lets him get to his feet] LW: Look at that! Any real wrestler would've taken the advantage! EC: It's called honor, Crusher..maybe you should look it up some time. LW: All I'm saying is..in the ring, honor will get you squashed. [The men meet up in the ring again and tie up. Rogers forces Collins into a corner, and the ref breaks it up. Rogers backs off in accordance with the official's wishes]
LW: There it is again! Rules are made to be broken! No killer instinct at all! EC: We'll see... BF: They've tied up again! [This time, Collins pushes Rogers into the corner. The ref once again goes to break it up, but Collins nails a cheap slap on his opponent. The crowd's loud boos echo through the arena]
LW: There's the guts! That's why this kid is going to the top, and why
"The Natural's" going to be a bottom feeder forever! What a disgrace to his family!
EC: Don't say that! You can hear how the crowd loves Adam and he's a great guy in the ring and out!
LW: Yeah...he pretends to be..but trust me, all fan-favorites are scum! [Collins once again moves the official out of his way, and nails Adam with an eye gouge. Once again, loud boos prevail. Collins follows it up with a knee to the gut and sets Rogers up for something]
BF: Collins locks Rogers into position...and nails a vertical suplex! EC: Yeah, too bad he only got it off by cheating. LW: Do what you can, kid! [Collins walks over to the prone Rogers and lays a couple of stomps into him before picking him up again and whipping him towards the ropes]
BF: Rogers to the ropes... [Collins picks him and powerslams him to the mat...HARD!] LW: That's it! There goes the hero... BF: Collins for the cover... 1 2 3---NO!!! Two count! Adam is up and so is the crowd! EC: AMAZING!!! [Collins is frustrated and throws Rogers into the ropes again, and ducks down for a backdrop, but Rogers stops and plants him to the ground with a DDT!]
LW: NO! BF: He telegraphed that one and paid the price for it! [Rogers acknowledges his fans and drops an elbow. He then grabs Collins around the head to slap on a headlock submission]
BF: This is experience and Roger's family here...he's slowing the match down to get the pace he wants.
LW: Looks to me like he's afraid! EC: What match are you watching?!? [Rogers keeps the hold on until Collins squirms to the ropes. As soon as he does..Rogers breaks the hold.]
BF: Rogers again..a very honorable man...he lets go right away... LW: W-U-S-S! [Rogers gets up and tosses Collins into the ropes. When Collins rockets back toward him, Adam drops down as Collins goes over him]
BF: Drop-down by Rogers... [Collins rebounds once again and Rogers jump on him with a Lou Thesz press, but is caught by The Main Event]
LW: <laughs> Uh-oh! [Collins slams Rogers down HARD with a spinebuster as the crowd reacts furiously] BF: And now Brian Collins has the advantage! LW: It's time to put that runt out of contention! BF: It looks like he's going to the ropes! EC: C'mon Adam! You can do it! [The crowd chants again for Rogers as Collins climbs the turnbuckles. One fan shouts something and Collins yells back. The two exchange insults while Collins is on the top rope]
BF: I don't know if this is such a good move.. LW: That stupid fan deserves it! Collins deserves his spot in the NeWF and it's about time the fans respected that!
[Adam gets to his feet to the delight of the crowd, but Collins still doesn't notice. He runs into the ropes causing Brian to lose his balance and fall onto the turnbuckle]
EC: THAT'S GOTTA HURT!!!!! BF: You've got that right! And here comes Rogers to take advantage. [Rogers scales the turnbuckle and grabs a hold of his opponent] BF: He's got something on his mind! SUPERPLEX!!!! EC: What an impact!!! LW: I've seen better.... [Rogers goes over to cover Collins, but The Main Event's foot is on the rope] EC: Lucky for him...that spat with the fan nearly cost him the match! BF: It sure did, but Collins will learn that in time! And now Rogers has got a chance to end this.
[Rogers throws Collins into the ropes and elevates him HIGH with a backdrop and Collins takes the time after to roll out of the ring again]
EC: AGAIN?!?! LW: Hey, if Rogers wants him he can go out and get him! EC: That's not the way "The Natural" works! He likes to settle things in the ring! LW: I didn't say he wasn't an idiot! [Collins taunts Rogers from the outside, but Rogers stands his ground. Finally, in frustration Collins crawls back into the ring.] EC: What is he doing now?!? [As Rogers walks over to him, Collins drops to his knees and begs] LW: Huh?!? [When Rogers backs off, Collins springs up and rakes the eyes] LW: HA!!!! [With that done, Collins grabs Rogers by the head and tosses him over the top rope. Rogers hits the arena floor hard and Collins rolls out after him]
LW: That'll teach that punk, Rogers to take the high road! BF: Well, Adam is in trouble. The fans haven't given up on him, but he sure looks hurt! But you can never count him out!
EC: Too true! [Collins grabs Adam and starts throwing him into everything around ringside..the barricade..the stairs..and finally the pole]
BF: The Natural is taking quite a beating here..oh no..what's he going to do now?!? [Collins grabs a ringside chair and folds it up...] LW: That's it, Brian! Make these punks respect you! EC: This isn't fair... [Collins ignores the referee's warning and prepares to swing the chair, but at the last second Rogers snatches it and prepares to deliver a blow himself!]
LW: Heh! What do you think of your hero, now? [Adam looks into the crowd, stops, drops the chair and rolls into the ring] LW: HUH?!?!? What just happened? EC: You just saw a true hero...no wonder you didn't recognize it. BF: Collins looks surprised as he rolls back into the ring...he wasn't expecting that... [Rogers waits for Collins and then goes to tie up, but he is met with a low blow. Then, Collins nails him in the face with a superkick]
LW: SUPERKICK!!!! BF: What's he got planned now? [Collins picks him up and prepares to go for his finisher...a piledriver called "The Main Event"]
LW: Are you ready?!? Here comes the Main Event, boys! EC: C'mon Adam....come on. [Collins tries hard to get him over..but he can't] BF: Rogers is blocking the maneuver and the crowd's going insane! [Finally, Rogers summons all his strength and punches out of the hold. He then grabs Collins in suplex position...hooks the leg and DRIVES Brian's head into the mat]
EC: Fisherman DDT!!! You know what's coming! BF: Indeed I do...he's hooking the arms] [Rogers hooks the arms in double underhook position and executes the "Natural Disaster", a spinning Tiger driver] BF: There it is! The Natural Disaster...the crowd is on it's feet! LW: No...no...no EC: OH YES!! No one gets up from that! BF: he's got the leg hooked...... 1 2 3!!! EC: He got him! That's it! The Natural has lived up to his expectations so far in the NeWF and the crowd couldn't agree more!
BF: What an ending! LW: That was such a screwjob! Someone file a protest! BF: Fans we have to take another break, but we will be back without MAIN
EVENT right after we get a look at another new superstar in NeWF, "Ace of
Hearts", and these commercials. Stick around!
[Fade to black.] [The screen fades from a black background to a locker room deep within the confines of the Frank Irwin Center in Austin Texas. The Ace of Hearts sits before a mirror with his elbows upon his knees as his head and shoulders are slumped in exhaustion. A towel is draped over his head as sweat drips from his body and slowly falls to the floor.]
# I've been sittin' here # # Tryin' to find myself # # I get behind myself # # I need to rewind myself # [From over his shoulder a figure appears to be making his way towards Ace. This man, wearing wrestling tights, seems to be twice Ace's size and is limping noticeably. He approaches closer and in passing, pats Ace upon the back in a show of respect. Ace looks up from the floor as he glances to the man he just defeated in the ring.]
# Lookin' for the payback # # Listen for the playback # # They say that every man bleeds just like me... # [Ace flips the man a nod back as he brings the towel up and wipes the sweat from his face. The towel comes back and falls upon the GCWO's Global
Heavyweight Championship belt. The camera zooms in upon the towel as it has a slight trace of blood from a cut above his right brow. Panning back from the towel the scene fades out.]
# And now I feel like number one # # Yet I'm last in life # # I watch my younger son # # And it helps to pass the time # # I take too many pills to help to ease the pain # # I made a couple of dollar bills still I feel the same # [The scene immediately fades into the rear exit of the arena as the Ace of
Hearts makes his way through the screaming crowd to a waiting limousine.
The crowd erupts at the sight of him as they start chanting his name. Ace throws up a wave to the crowd as he quickly jumps into the back of the limousine.]
# Everybody knows my name # # They say it way out loud # # A lot of folks [bleep] me # # It's hard to hang out in crowds# [The limousine carrying the "Ace of Hearts" can be seen pulling away from the Frank Irwin Center. The fans immediately rush the limousine and begin to rock it back and forth as it slowly proceeds forward. The camera stays with the chaotic scene a moment longer before fading out.]
# I guess that's the price you pay # # To be some big shot like I am # # Outskirt stands and one night stands # # Still I can't find love # [The scene fades in to another arena as the "Ace of Hearts" can be seen running down the aisle towards the ring. He slides under the ropes and begins wailing upon two wrestlers standing center ring.]
# And when your walls come tumbling down # # I will always be around # # And when your walls come tumbling down # # I will always be around # [The scene shows one of the men laying unconscious as the other is being elevated by Ace and put into the C.R.A.P.S. The second man drops to the mat with a vicious bounce as Ace can be seen standing over them in a very taunting way.]
# People don't know about the things I say and do # # They don't understand about the [Bleep] that I've been through # # It's been so long since I've been home # # I've been gone, I've been gone for way too long # [The screen shows a shot of the "Ace of Hearts" sitting in his private Lear jet looking out the right side portal as they make their way towards the next arena.]
# Maybe I forgot all things I miss # # Oh somehow I know there's more to life than this # # I said it too many times # # And I still stand firm # [The screen fades from the Lear jet to another arena where the "Ace of
Hearts" is standing center ring with the GCWO's Global Heavyweight
Championship belt draped over his shoulder. He can be seen, but not heard, as he is speaking on the mic. He seems very upset and animated as he pulls the belt from his shoulder and lays out the challenge to the locker-room.]
# You get what you put in # # And people get what they deserve # [The camera zooms to the ramp-way as a figure rushes the ring. The figure slides under the rope and is met by a furious charge by Ace. Both men are going at it with a fist of flurry as the camera fades out.] # Still I ain't seen mine # # No I ain't seen mine # # I've been giving just ain't been gettin' # # I've been walking down that line # [The scene fades into the desert just outside of Las Vegas. The "Ace of
Hearts" can be seen walking towards a deserted crossroads. Three roads, dirt, gravel, and concrete. Each signifying his past, present, and future.
He stops at the crossroads and turns towards the concrete road. He proceeds slowly down this road as the screen fades out.]
# So I think I'll keep on walking # # With my head held high # # I'll keep moving on and only God knows why # [A new shot fades in as the "Ace of Hearts" can be seen signing a contract at the NeWF headquarters in Indianapolis, Indiana. He crosses all the "T's"
and dots the "I's" as he smiles and turns to face the camera. Various flashes go off simultaneously as the media is having a field day. Ace stands and makes his way to the mic as the camera slowly fades to black leaving just the audio.]
# Only God.....Only God # # Only God knows why # # Only God....knows....why, why, why only God knows why # [From the back of the black screen a logo appears. It is that of the NeWF in bold red letters upon the black background. The logo slowly begins to pulse as flames emit from its top. As the last notes fade, the pulse stops.
The logo then explodes into small pieces as a voice over can be heard.] Voice over: Coming Soon to an arena near you! [The screen goes black as the screen begins to show commercials.]
