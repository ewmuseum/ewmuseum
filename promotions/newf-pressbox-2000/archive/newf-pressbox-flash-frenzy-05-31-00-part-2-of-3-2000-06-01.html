Flash Frenzy (5/31/00) (Part 2 of 3)

1 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1- Brian Collins 2- "Ace of Hearts" 3- The Cabana Boys 4- Johnny Holiday 5- "The D-Man" Thomas DiArgo =================================================================== Brian Collins =================================================================== [We open up to a picture of the former President of the NeWF, Colt Anderson. Colt is wearing a flashy, brown suit and blue tie underneath. He has on his infamous "I'm better than you" smile. Right before everyone at home pukes, we slowly fade to black. And we hear the beginning notes of
"Iron Man" by Black Sabbath. As the song crescendos, we see a new picture of Colt Anderson emerging from the darkness. When the spinning image comes into to focus we see a Colt Anderson with buck teeth, a afro, and a mustache. We fade to black again, and suddenly we see that the darkness is actually a curtain. Out of nowhere comes Brian Collins. He is wearing a white button up shirt, a plaid tie, and khaki pants. Brian straightens his tie, then begins to speak.]
Brian Collins: Colt Anderson, quite honestly the biggest pile of crap I have ever seen. I mean, you people actually believe that I liked this guy.
Come on. Everyone knows I was in it for the gold. Well, Colt got caught, just like I said he would. And now we have the sissy back at the helms.
Quite frankly, I don't give a damn what you do, Chris. As long as I get to whip someone's butt every time I step in that squared circle. And seeing that I'm paired up against Kurt Castle, the first round should be a walk in the park for someone of such a high caliber, as myself.
[Brian begins pacing back and forth.] BC: Now, Chris, Chris, Chris. I know you like kissing the fan's ass, but this has gone far enough. If the fans want to see me win the National
Title, then you should have at least given the fans something to watch. I mean, Kurt Castle?!?!? Who is that?
[Brian takes a brief pause.] BC: My thoughts exactly. Now to move on to bigger and better things. Kurt
Castle, when you step in that squared circle with me... I hope you plan on leaving with a broken arm, or leg. Because when I get through with you, even all MY money couldn't pay for your damages. Since I am guaranteed victory in the first round I can concentrate more on the later rounds. And the way things are shaping up it appears that either Jack Mannon or War
Child will have to deal with me in the second round. So, you two need to make up your mind who is going to win, so the winner can save his energy for me. And the way I see it, the rest of the tournament is a done deal. So you can slap the belt on my waist and call me "champ" already.
[Brian begins rubbing his chin, deep in thought.] BC: Now, getting back to the power change. I came out here and spoon fed you idiots the idea I actually liked Colt Anderson. And you took to it like a fish to a worm. Hell, I was the one that told Colt he should go for the most money he could, and he did it. That goes to show you what kind of crap he really is. But, I forgot to warn him, should he be caught, Chris would be back in charge. So, I lost one punk only to be welcomed by another. But,
Chris forgot what he said. As much as he may despise me. He forgot that this tournament is randomly drawn. So, Chris will pay for his mistake by helping announce me the very first... NeWF National Champion!
[Brian throws his arms in the air in victory, as we fade to black.] =================================================================== "Ace of Hearts" =================================================================== To: Predator (First mention of Grave) From: Ace of Hearts Title: Hunt or be Hunted... ----------------------------------------- Canadian Rocky Mountains Alberta, Canada May 29, 2000 6:00 PM ----------------------------------------- [The scene opens in a large clearing set deep within the Canadian Rocky
Mountains. The camera slowly pans around the spacious clearing. Taking in the spectacular images of the western borders of the Canadian Wilderness outside of Alberta Canada. A deeply green meadow, sparsely littered with coniferous trees, stands before us. It's spring-like green appearance sharply contrasts with the permanent snow covered, steep slopes of the rugged mountains just off in the distance.
"Slam Cam" Roberts, our camera man, takes a step back as he attempts to take in a shot of the dense forest to his left. His footfall crushes a small pine cone cradled in a bed of dying pine needles thus startling a small Grey hare. The crunch of the pine cone and the fleeing of the hare, draws the mans attention to the ground. He chuckles as he watches the hare scamper off into the dense underbrush.
Sensing something odd in the hares behavior, he follows it into the dense forest out of curiosity. Not paying attention to his surroundings, he strays away from the trail for a few minutes. Roberts brings the camera up and begins to film the hare as it quickly hops past a small outcropping of rocks and streaks under some low lying brush to hide. Roberts cautiously approaches the area, and zooms the cameras lens under the brush to the trembling hare. The small hare's pink little nose flickers as his body trembles involuntarily in fear.]
"Slam Cam" Roberts: "It's alright little fella. I won't hurt you. [Roberts zooms the camera's lens in tighter on the hare. The lens picks up on the sheer terror lying deep within the dark brown eye's of the little hare. The look of terror sends a shiver of fear straight down Roberts back.
He suddenly snaps out of his little wilderness getaway and comes to realize that he has wandered off the beaten trail. Sensing movement off to his right, Roberts turns slowly towards the motion. He hears the low, throaty growl of the hundred and sixty pound Cougar before seeing it standing upon the outcropping of rocks. The Cougar from nose to tip is at least eight feet long and sleek in appearance.]
"Slam Cam" Roberts: "Oh, hell..." [Roberts freezes in fear as the Cougar drops from the rock outcropping and slowly stalks towards him. The Cougars head stretches out as he appears ready to pounce. His short black ears twitch as he steps over a fallen tree approaching yet closer. One by one the Cougars legs clear the fallen tree limbs. The Cougar lets out a furious roar sending a warning of things yet to come. The long rounded black tipped tail flitters over the tree limb as the Cougar snarls showing its teeth. The sleek beast squats down and swiftly leaps from the ground. It's powerful legs launch the Cougar high into the air and heading towards Roberts.]
< - - B - - A - - N - - G - - > [The Cougar's body, in midair, is catapulted backwards as a bullet smacks the Cougar between the eyes. The sickening thud of the slug connecting with the head of the Cougar, slightly muffles the sound of the rifles report.
Blood and bone fragments spray the ground as the twitching body of the
Cougar lands dead at the feet of the stunned reporter. Roberts takes a step backwards away from the Cougar. Still in shock he turns back and is startled by an approaching figure. Roberts takes a few more steps backwards as the ominous figure closes. His size and the rifle slung over his shoulder make him very menacing looking.
The man is dressed in a pair of hiking boots, black denim jeans, and a warm red and black flannel jacket. As the man approaches, his dynamic features clearly stand out. Thus distinguishing him from many other mere men and making it definitively easy to identify him as the One and Only, Ace of
Hearts.]
Ace of Hearts: "How's it going Robert's?" [Recognizing Ace, Roberts steps closer as he breaths a sigh of relief. He takes the extended hand of Ace and shakes it very graciously.]
Roberts: "Thank God you were around! I kind of wandered from the path a while back when this Cougar attacked."
[Ace chuckles a bit at Roberts, then he squats down and examines the dead animal. Ace looks up to the camera and smiles. He brings his brown leather gloved hand to his face and removes the dark pair of sunglasses that hide his eyes. Placing the sunglasses into a interior pocket, Ace squints up to the camera man.]
Ace of Hearts: "You know son, you need to pay a little more attention out here." [Ace reaches down to the deceased animal and opens its mouth. He shows
Roberts the size of the Cougars extremely white teeth. Then glances back up to the camera with a small smirk.]
Ace of Hearts: "Robert's this ain't like the big city son! The animals out this way ain't gonna get indited for tax evasion and such. [shaking his head] No, the animals out here will have you for lunch son. So lets just stay to the trail next time, okay?"
[Ace stands and re-adjusts the rifle slung over his shoulder as he glances down at his customized Rolex.]
Ace of Hearts: Besides...just what the hell are you doing out here anyway? [Roberts quickly scoops up his camera and brings it to his shoulder.] Roberts: "I'm on assignment Ace. I was sent out here by Johnny Jaxson to get an in-depth interview or some "Wrestling" behind the scenes kind of footage."
[Ace reaches up and scratches the back of his neck as he shakes his head negatively. Chuckling slightly he continues.]
Ace of Hearts: "So how's your footage going so far?" [The camera bobbles a bit as Roberts struggles to stabilize it. He focuses the camera on Ace and begins rolling footage.]
Roberts: "I think I've had my fill of the Canadian Wilderness!" [Both men chuckle as the camera settles on a picturesque shot of the profile of the Ace of Hearts standing before the rugged mountain backdrop.]
Ace of Hearts: "So let me get this straight. Jaxson sent you out here to interview me but didn't come out here himself?"
[The camera shakes up and down in an affirmative manner.] Roberts: He said he had some business to tend to up in Chicago. Something about someone gaining Revenge on someone or something. He just told me to interview you and we could edit him in later."
[Roberts shrugs as he disappears back behind the camera.] Ace of Hearts: "Well, I guess I had better give you what you came for. Before I then show you the way back home." [Peeking from behind the camera again Roberts smiles.] Roberts: "Please do..." [Ace laughs as he knows that Roberts is well out of his element here. Shaking his head Ace looks to the camera and brings the leather gloved hand up and scratches his chin. With a cocky smirk he turns and looks to the fallen animal and then back to the camera.]
Ace of Hearts: "It's kind of ironic how the symbolism of this moment can draw me to think of my next opponent.
[He pauses as he looks to the fallen animal.] Ace of Hearts: "You see...these woods here [motions around him] and the world of professional wrestling, actually have quite alot in common."
[Ace cocks his head to the side a bit as he drops to a knee. He lifts the head of the Cougar, or what's left of it, and shows the destruction the bullet left behind. Dropping the Cougars head, he turns and wipes the blood off his gloves on the blades of grass around him.]
Ace of Hearts: "Both the wilderness and the ring are based upon one simple and yet, very true concept. This being, only the strong survive! In the wilderness, as they do in the wrestling ring, the weak fall to the way-side. Only here in the wilderness they are killed if not devoured.
Where in wrestling they are devoured if not retired!" [Ace smirks towards the camera's lens as his piercing blue eyes glint off the evening sunlight filtering through the trees.]
Ace of Hearts: "Do you see the simplicity in the laws of nature? I sure as hell do...You either *hunt* or you become the *hunted*!"
[Ace smiles as he stands back to his full height.] Ace of Hearts: "Now, my next opponent. Predator, [laughs] He may just understand this simple fact of life. But just understanding the facts ain't gonna cut it! You see this man, called Predator, [shakes his head] he lacks the knowledge and skill required to hunt a man of my caliper. He may victimize, plunder, and destroy many mere men. But as you can tell..."
[Ace stands and brings his gloved hands out to his sides. He laughs loudly enough for the eruption to echo through the area before continuing.]
Ace of Hearts: "I'm not much like many mere men! I am far superior to perhaps any man that this organization has to offer! You see Predator, when you deal with a man of my caliper you find yourself way out of your element. Your used to being the hunter boy, but as soon as you signed that damned contract you my friend became the hunted! You see boy, your not dealing with just a mere mortal here!"
[snickers] Ace of Hearts: "What you have gotten yourself into is something far beyond your worst nightmares, Predator. Cause signing that contract means that you will have to face a man this coming Thursday. A man unlike any other man you have ever faced! For as you stand in that ring and prepare to face me.
You remember this, our match will be something that your going to have to be dealing with deep inside your soul for many years to come. For as you attempt to deal with the Marauder of Mat, you remember one simple fact.
[Ace smirks towards the camera as he flashes that million dollar smile.] Ace of Hearts: "You signed that contract to face me voluntarily. Nobody forced you to sign your name on that dotted line! Therefore that tells me that, you wished to make a name for yourself, by facing me!
[chuckles] Ace of Hearts: "So if, you want to step up and take a taste of what the big time has to offer! That's fine by me! But you remember one thing pal..."
[Ace sneers into the cameras lens as he removes the bloody glove from his hand.] Ace of Hearts: "When its all said and done, the blood won't be upon my hands. For I, as the hunter, will vanquish and exterminate the predator set before me! I shall drive you to your *GRAVE*!
[Ace chuckles as he cocks his head to the side.] Ace of Hearts: "But then again...that's another story altogether now isn't it?" [Ace chuckles as he steps back from the camera and drops to a knee next to the Cougar. He looks to Roberts and motions for the camera to be cut as the scene slowly fades from the wilderness dense underbrush.]

(*fade to black*) =================================================================== Johnny Holiday =================================================================== (The scene opens inside a lavish Hollywood home. The camera takes us down a long hallway that is painted white with oak trim. On the wallls we notice that there are several pictures of one man standing with many different actors, and wrestlers. As we reach the end of the hall we can see that man sitting in a black leather chair, that man is Johnny Holiday. Johnny has his long black hair pulled back in a pony tail and he is clean shaven. He is wearing blue jeans with snake skin boots and a solid black silk shirt that is buttoned 3-4 the way up. The camera moves in a little closer as
Johnny leans forward.)
Holiday: So the NeWF has decided to sign the deal with Johnny Holiday? Well, look for the ratings to go through the roof, and watch as the main-stream media starts to stand up and take notice of this promotion!
Hell, since the news broke that I signed a contract, my phone has been ringing off the hook! Everyone is asking what I am going to do here in the
NeWF, who am I going to team with and will I rebuild the "House Of
Holiday".Well, I have been watching the NeWF for a little bit and I have seen a few people here that I have called friend and a few that I have called foe. I know that people like Ace Of Hearts, Adam Calloway and Sean
Cooley are all asking where it is that Johnny Holiday is going to stand.
Well guys, Johnny Holiday is going to stand in the same place he has since day one, right in the middle of the heat!
(Johnny leans back in his chair.) Holiday: I see where the NeWF has formed a tournament to decide who is going to be the new champion, and Johnny Holiday is not in it. Bring every crazy little stunt you have and be sure you try every one of 'em! And "Hella Razor", I have not got to see you in action as of yet either, so I really don't know too much about you. Cyrus, ya sound big and nasty, and I am praying to the good lord above that you are because I am going to love bringing you and "Crazy"
down a notch or two and using the both of ya to show everyone else in the
NeWF that Johnny Holiday has arrived!

=================================================================== "The D-Man" Thomas DiArgo =================================================================== [The camera opens looking down towards the ground on what appears to be a nice summer day. You slowly pan upwards to see a quiver of arrows leaning against a bench with a bow laying ontop. The camera slowly turns to the right to show, in the distance, a target. From behind the target a man slowly emerges and begins to walk towards the camera. Dressed in a pair of black jeans and a white t-shirt that reads Bermuda Triad walks the D-Man,
Thomas DiArgo. He walks past the camera crew and grabs the bow off of the bench and the quiver and slowly turns facing the target. He grabs a few arrows and jams then into the ground as he raises the bow. Placing one arrow in the bow he draws back and within an instant the arrow races towards the target, striking it dead center.]
TD: You know, ever since I made my debut here in the NEWF people have been talking. Wondering just how tough the D-Man is....wondering just how bad this guy is....wondering if I was going to target them as the next person whom I was going to beat down. Well wonder no more....here in a matter of days my first victim will find out who I am. Tripp Shade, the "Box
Office".....now I dont know you nor do I give a damn who you are....all I have to say is that you are the first in a long line of punks that will find out why I am the greatest technical wrestler to ever step foot in a wrestling ring.
[DiArgo reaches down and grabs another arrow and fires it down towards the target. This time striking it just above the first. He turns towards the camera once again.]
TD: People have been asking me how I feel about what is going on here in the NEWF. How I feel about Anderson being taken away after he brought me here. Well lets just clear a few things up right now.....noone brought me here. Anderson called me one night beggind and pleading for me to come and work for him. Going on and on about how he needed the best to help him.
Well I laughed but decided to show up anyways and look what happened. I took some of your "great" stars and threw them around like the women they are. So do I care about what happened to Anderson....HELL no...it couldnt have happened to a nicer guy if you ask em.
[The D-Man reaches down again and slowly draws back the bow. After a few moments he releases the string and the arrow flies down to the target, tearing one of the arrows in half.]
TD: I also found out that I am not in the tournament that is about to begin. Am I mad about that....a little. You see, I am here for one reason and one reason alone.....to take everything and anything that I want. So go ahead and have your tournament because all you are doing is prolonging the inevitable....the D-Man, Thomas DiArgo as your champion. So I dont care who wins because sooner or later they will have to face me and when they do...well lets just say that they shall be taking a one way trip to the hospital. So Shade, you better bring all of your little fans with you because you are going to need them. Prepare for the beating of a lifetime boy....and lets just hope you make it to the tournament.
[The camera fades as Thomas DiArgo takes the last arrow out of the ground and places it into the bow. He slowly draws the string back then releases the arrow along its path. The final arrow strikes the target...sending wood and feathers flying through the air.]
