NEWF (GeoCities, 2000) Card Archive

Flash Frenzy (3/22/00) (Part 1 of 3)

23 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1- "The Main Event" Brian Collins 2- "Showtime" Matt Stone 3- "The Extreme Warrior" War Child 4- "The Devastator" Kurt Castle 5- "The Natural" Adam Rogers ============================================================== "The Main Event" Brian Collins ============================================================== [We open up to what appears to be an empty arena. We see the familiar face of Brian Collins, standing in the middle of a wrestling ring, positioned in the center of the arena. We pan the scores of empty seats which will soon be filled full of screaming NeWF fans in only a few short days. We zoom closer on Brian. He is wearing a North Carolina t-shirt, with a pair of
North Carolina warm-up pants. Brian is holding some sort of a photo in his right hand. He places his hands behind his back, and turns to face the camera.]
BC: Adam Rogers... what a fool! I take pity on you Adam. You see, it is not every day that a no-talent loser like yourself gets to compete with one of the "big dogs" like myself. And we all know the old saying... "If you can't run with the big dog's stay on the porch." While on the topic of losers, I have an interesting scenario for you Adam.
[Brian whips his hand out to reveal a picture of Adam Rogers wearing a
Stanford Cardinal jersey.]
BC: Ah, yes the Stanford Cardinal. Like you they were full of courage, unfortunately they had no idea what awaited them. Much like yourself. So, come March 27th I will be North Carolina and you can be the unlucky
Stanford Cardinal. It will all be textbook. I will toy with you at first, but then I will liven the pace of the match, much to your dismay. Then, I will finish you off with "The Main Event".
[Brian tosses the picture to the floor, and begins to speak once more.] BC: Now, who am I kidding. I take no pity whatsoever for you Adam Rogers. You are one of the low-lives that Brian Collins intends to wipe out of
NeWF. How dare you have the audacity to call me The Main Course. I, myself fall into the category of delicacies. I mean look at this sexy body.
[Brian motions his hands over his body to show his abs of steel.] BC: You should already be aware of my athletically tuned body so I will stop bragging. However, one like me should love to brag. But, I like to let my actions speak for themselves. Adam Rogers, you call yourself "The
Natural" well, when it comes to pro wrestling there is only one natural, and his name is Brian Collins. So, you can go on with your little charade, but when it comes right down to it, you and I both know I can whip your ass on any given day. Whether it be televised or not. Don't believe me do you?
Just wait until the Tuesday morning when you wake up in a hospital bed. You will have different feelings about "The Main Event" then. I guarantee it.
[Brian begins pacing the ring.] BC: You know, being a top competitor in NeWF is a tough job. As a matter of fact, I have a very pressing issue. Maybe you idiots idolizing me at home will be of some assistance. I know I can will all the gold in NeWF, but I need help deciding which title I should win first? I mean, there is the glory of being National champion, but Television title means you fans will get to see me every single week. Well, how about just for fun.. I win both of them? Yeah, that's it. I can see the headlines now. "Brian Collins wins two titles in one night."
[Brian stops at the turnbuckle. He climbs up, and sits on the top turnbuckle.] BC: What gets me though is the thought of all these other losers competing against me! "The Main Event"
[Brian shudders at this thought, chuckles slightly, then glances at his watch as he continues.]
BC: Well, according to my calculations right now it is about 5 days until
Operation Main Event goes into place.
[Brian laughs again, then his tone becomes very stern.] BC: Make no mistake about it, Adam Rogers. Brian Collins may be a comedian outside of the ring, but when you get in that ring with me, you had better hope I don't show up for the match.
[Brian checks his watch once again, then hurriedly speaks.] BC: I will close with this, Adam Rogers... you may consider yourself lucky to have been beaten by the greatest wrestler to walk God's green Earth. For the record, this has been "The Main Event" Brian Collins.
[Brian walks out of the ring, and up the aisle as we fade to black.] ============================================================== "Showtime" Matt Stone ============================================================== (SCENE: What appears to be some sort of an office. In the back of the room, a small man is seated behind a large, wooden desk. On top of the desk is a name plate that reads "Dr. Bill Jeffries".)
Dr. Jeffries: Hi, I'm Dr. Bill Jeffries, and you may have never heard of me, but I thought I needed to do this, just to let you, NeWF, know just the condition my patient Matt Stone is currently in. You may know him as
"Showtime". I am his psychologist.
(Dr. Jeffries pulls out a folder and takes out some papers from the folder.) Dr. Jeffries: Matt had a very rough childhood, his parents treated him real rough, he was physically abused over and over again as a child. He was able to get out of the bad situation and make a name for himself in the independent film business. Matt therefore tries to relive these characters he's played in films in his life. In lamen's terms, he suffers from multiple personalities. He doesn't change personalities very often, but he may be "Showtime" to all of you for a month or two, then all of a sudden he's somebody completely different. He'll still remember all of his past wrestling pursuits, he just won't remember being his past personality.
(Dr. Jeffries takes a sip of water.) Dr. Jeffries: On another note, much of his past has been covered up VERY well. I personally don't know who has been hiding his past, but whoever it is really knows what they're doing. Matt won't tell me anything about where he grew up or what the exact conditions were like when he was young.
I think he is still too fragile for me to force him to tell me these things. This is all I know and all I can tell you without violating his doctor/patient rights. That is all.
(Fade to black.) ============================================================== "The Extreme Warrior" War Child ============================================================== [Scene opens in what appears to be a basement of an old building. It is dark, moist and so silent you can hear dripping water from a leaky pipe nearby. Steam pours out of duct work and pipes on the ceiling and walls.
Behind the pipes and duct word is a basic red brick foundation, faded white and colors of gray. The room is hotter than hell. On the walls not covered with pipe are old pictures, and posters of wrestling shows. The cameraman approaches a doorway off to the far right, as he approaches the heat in the room increases with each step. He enters the room, on the right and old oil tank and furnace, on the left is a hot water tank, pipes drip with condensation. The camera sees another doorway straight ahead. Above the doorway is Japanese writing, written in blood red. Quite noticeably, one
Japanese symbol is bigger, it is in the center of the rest of the other symbols. The cameraman enters the doorway, at this point you can hear the sound of clanging metal. The cameraman breathes heavy, as the air grows hotter and thicker. The clanging metal stops, it is pitch black and the camera can barely make out shadows of images, looks like a primitive weight room. Camera pans left, and notices about three racks of free weights and dumb bells... The camera pans right and we can see a full weight bench and exercise set up. The camera also notices something else, a dark figure shrouded in the shadows, sitting in the corner by another furnace. The air inside this room is thicker than the others. The figure does not move at first, but begins to speak. The voice is deep, and scratchy]
Dark Figure: Out of the darkness a warrior did call, true pain and suffering he brought to them all. Away went the wrestlers of the NeWF to hide under their beds, for fear of this devil might take their heads.
Welcome, you have entered, what I'd like to call the War room, this is wear
I train for my matches. Each match is a different training regimen. I stick to. This room has helped me stay virtually undefeated in my career. Oh yeah, I have lost matches but those are few and far between. I know why you are here. I know I have my debut match coming up on the 27th. Let's see, who is it against. That's right, "Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin. Do you really think you know what the word Chaos means Brandon? Or did you cook up the name because you thought it would be intimidating? You see Brandon, chaos isn't just a word with a definition in the dictionary, it's a way of life. Brandon, what you see here is what you get, I live my life exactly what my name implies, do you? Or are you all gimmick? Can you live up to your name? I seriously doubt it. You want chaos, you want pain? Well, my friend, Pain and chaos has a face, allow me to show it to you....
[The sound of a match is heard striking and the room is lit in the flare up of the match. The glow of the match lights up the man's face. The scarred face of the "Extreme Warrior" War Child fills the camera, as he lights a single candle lying next to him on the floor. The camera catches a glimpse of War Child's full body, the same symbol we saw above the door is tattooed on War Child's right calve muscle. War Child seems to be wearing Black cutoff jean shorts, with no shirt, and what looks to be black leather boots, not even the candle can help the camera make out the all of the clothing on War Child. The camera moves back to War Child's face as he speaks.. ]
"The Extreme Warrior" War Child: Take a good long look at what's in your future "Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin. This is the face of pain, the face of pure extreme chaos. Take a good look at this face and remember it well, for it maybe the last face you see. I am the true meaning of chaos, for I am the very meaning of War. Brutal is the path of destruction I will lay down.
Painful is the fire that'll consume you, as you stand in the very center of the path of destruction, and the finally, as final as death, is being planted head first into the consecrated ground of the path of destruction...
[War Child gets up from the furnace and brings the candle with him to the weight bench, something odd about the bench that wasn't noticed before. The entire bench is covered in barbed wire. War Child sits on the bench, takes a deep breath and lays down all 280lbs of his body onto the barbed wire.
The barbed wire immediately pierces into War Child's skin, without a wince from him. He takes hold of the bar, which appears to have about four hundred pounds on it, and he presses it ten times with relative ease. War
Child slams the bar back down on the bench, and the weights settle in with a harsh metal to metal sound. He gets up, produces a water bottle from underneath the bench and he takes a healthy gulp, water dripping from the corners of his mouth onto his massive, scarred chest, and blood runs down his arms and back. His long black hair, matted with sweat, drapes over his shoulders. He looks straight at the camera, ice in them cold blue eyes as he stares into the lens.]
"The Extreme Warrior" War Child: So judging by the interview of Brandon
Franklin, he wrestles in Japan. The Ariake Coliseum in Tokyo to be exact. I was fifteen when I arrived in Tokyo as a stowaway aboard a freighter from my native Boston. I know the Tokyo streets well. I fought on those very streets to survive, for barely enough Yen to buy a bowl of rice. I also have fought in that very arena as a contracted wrestler at the age of 17 with Japan Pro Wrestling and Martial Arts. I was known as WAR, and was branded with this symbol
[War Child shows his calve to the camera] "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: That is the Japanese symbol of war, Mr. Franklin, I have more history there than you could ever have. History that includes 3 barbed wire championships, One king of death match championship and several C-4, exploding ring barbed wire death matches. You think by wrestling a guy named "Pecker" for the Japanese hardcore title makes you truly hardcore, then you are truly a pathetic soul playing with kids...
"Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin stop playing with your "Pecker" and come back to the states, because March 27th is your day with destiny. On this day you will find out what TRUE chaos is, what TRUE pain and suffering is. And,
Brandon, without a shadow of a doubt you will find out what the TRUE meaning of the word EXTREME is. I do not know what possessed you to sign my open contract. I don't know what you were thinking, maybe you weren't thinking at all....
[War Child suddenly stops speaking as his gaze is fixed on the camera. He is facing the camera but it appears he is staring right through the camera and the cameraman at the wall behind him. War Child seems like he is in a trance like state. War Child begins laughing, not the laugh of something funny, but the laugh of a sadistic madman.]
"The Extreme Warrior" War Child: Ha ha ha, Brandon, my doomed friend, don't worry about the other wrestlers of this organization. Don't worry about your dream of holding a top title in this organization... AND definitely don't worry about the owner of our organization. Come March 27th in
Indianapolis all your worries come to an end, because the path of destruction begins at your front doorstep. I will start paving on the 27th.
Starting with your headstone, as I plant you stinkin' carcass in the ground, you will wonder why in the world you signed to face me. A creature of war, the perfect weapon in the ring. All your worries about being the top-billed talent on the marquise will disappear. All your dreams shattered, like the bones of your body. Brandon what you don't realize is, you have endangered your career, and your livelihood. Hell, your very existence is endangered now. I have one bit of advice...
[War Child stands, and throws the bottle of water against the wall. Droplets of water scatter on the wall as the bottle falls to the floor] "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: RUN, don't walk, RUN AWAY. It's your only hope of survival. Brandon, I have nothing personal against you, couldn't care less about you. But boy, you wanted chaos, you got it. You've stepped in front of the War Machine, the headlights have got you, you're frozen like a sad deer. You won't amount to a piece of roadkill after this match.
So, Brandon, pray to whatever God you pray to, because divine intervention is all that is going save you. The fires of war will consume you, the War
Machine will bury your burning carcass... All wrestlers of the NeWF heed my warning. The Path of Destruction starts with "Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin in Indianapolis, who's next? Don't know, don't care... the open contract awaits my next victim. First it's time to teach Brandon Franklin what Chaos really is... Ash to Ash, dust to dust.... Fade to black...
[Camera fades on War Child, who is smiling. ] ============================================================== "The Devastator" Kurt Castle ============================================================== (The scene begins inside of an interview room. Everyone has cleared out and it is left empty. You can hear footsteps coming from the distance. The sound get more intense with every step that is heard. The door opens and
"The Devastator" Kurt Castle walks through the doors. He is decked out in a pair of blue jeans and a 3/4 length leather jacket. Castle walks around the room for a moment not noticing the cameras. He looks on the desks and reads through a couple papers. Castle proceeds to go through a couple drawers as if he were looking for someone. As Castle turns around there's a camera stuck directly in his face. Castle gives the cameraman a dirty look and then begins to speek)
Kurt Castle:(sarcasticcly) Well it was awful nice of you guys to let me know you were here. Yuo guys just don't quit do you. Can't never leave a man alone can 'ya.
(Castle walks over to a chair and sits down) Kurt Castle: Well since you guys are here, make yourselfs useful. (The camera pans in a little more on Castle as he begins to speak again) Kurt Castle: Well, it seems to me that action is finally going to pick up around here. The first ever show takes place in a little under a week. How fitting to have the Devastator kicking off the whole thing. It was a simple choice of course, you needed someone charismatic, yet not "preppy".
Muscular, yet not overgrown. And someone know has the looks to kill. But now onto my opponent "Black Jack" Baldwin. A man who has started from nothing and risen to success.
(Castle gets back to his feet and begins to walk around the room as he speaks) Castle: Baldwin, I know a little more about you than you think I do. I've been doing my "homework" on you. I've studied the tapes, and I've read the reports. The numbers on you are pretty impressive. It seems as though that you always seem to find a way to get the job done. You remind me a lot of myself. You have nearly the same physical attributes, the expierience is there, but I just don't quite get your mentallity.
(Castle takes off his jacket and lays it over a table) Castle: It seems as though you're one of the smart ass type of guys. Always looking to pull something over on someone for your own beifit. The fans seem to like it but I don't. Baldwin, you gotta' realize something here. I'm not playing around. I'm here to do one thing, and that's recreate the legacy that I've lived the last 9 years. It looks like you may be the right place to start. Monday night you and I will lock up for nothing more than pride. So come on down and get "Devastated"
(The scene fades out into a wide shot as Castle, grabs his jacket and walks out. The scene fades to black)
============================================================== "The Natural" Adam Rogers ============================================================== [The scene opens in a small hotel room. A single bed is positioned in the near center of the room, set between two nightstands, both of which have small lamps on their tops. One of the tables - the right one - also has a telephone and a remote control for the TV set, which sits on the dresser set across from the bed. Certainly not the highest class of set-ups, but not the worst either. The room is clean, neat, and tidy, just not exactly the Rodeo Drive of hotel rooms.]
[The sounds of a door opening is heard, complete with the click and whir of an electronic key sensor. After some rustling around, we see the occupant walk in, decked out in normal looking street clothes, the kind that would blend in with any middle class group of people. The man tosses the card key onto the dresser, the plastic key sliding to a stop beside an empty ash tray. He holds in his hand a stack of papers, and sits down on the bed to read them. The man is 'The Natural' Adam Rogers, in case you haven't figured it out by now.]
[As he reads the papers, Adam's face changes expression a bit, not enough to give away his thoughts, but enough to be noticed. Finally, he tosses the papers aside, and stretches out his muscular frame. A sudden knock on the door is followed by the sound of a woman's voice, with a touch of an
Hispanic accent.]
Woman: "Housekeeping." Rogers: "Come on in." [Walking into the scene is an older lady, mid-40's probably, in a hotel housekeeper's uniform. She gives a small smile to Adam, who returns the favor before motioning around the room.]
Rogers: "I think someone got here before you." [The woman surveys the room, nodding.] Woman: "I think you're right. Say, I recognize you. My son watches you on
TV with that wrestling thing."
[Adam smiles wryly.] Rogers: "Well, it's nice to be noticed." Woman: "Would you give an autograph for my son?" Rogers: "That would be my pleasure." [The woman pulls out a paper towel from her cart, prompting Adam to laugh. He takes the cleaning product and signs it, handing it back to the lady when he is done.] Woman: "Thank you so much, he will be so happy. Have a nice day!" Rogers: "You, too, ma'am." [The housekeeper turns and walks out of the scene, leaving only Adam and a small smile.] Rogers: "That is the best part of this business. You know, in this day and age where it's commonplace to cheat to get ahead and disrespect the rules of the game, it's nice to know that people still appreciate those who stick to the codes of ethics that have been around for years."
[Adam reaches into the air, stretching out his muscular frame once more.] Rogers: "It's a long drive from Naples to Indianapolis, good thing I've still got a few days until my NeWF debut. I was just looking over the schedules the offices just sent out, and see that I get thrown right into the thick of things. Hey, I wouldn't have it any other way. And it seems as though I get to start off my NeWF campaign with the likes of..."
[Adam shuffles through the papers, finally finding the information he was looking for.] Rogers: "Ah, yes, 'The Main Event' Brian Collins, as he calls himself. 'The
Main Event'... now _that's_ arrogance if I've ever heard it. This early period of an organization is always hard on the wrestlers. We're going through this initial feeling-out stage, don't know much about any of our opponents, so we just get to wing it a lot until things settle down.
Brian, I'm not going to pretend to know anything much about you, and talk about how I'm going to expose this weakness or that flaw or what have you.
No, next Monday night, it's all going to come down to natural ability... and I think everyone knows who will have the edge there." [Pushing the papers aside, Adam stands up and walks over to the window, where the shades are still pulled shut. He slowly opens them, allowing the sunlight to come creeping into the room, then turns to face the camera again.]
Rogers: "You see, Collins, what I _can_ control is what _I_ do. What I
_can_ do is build upon the success I have had in numerous other places, from the EEWF to Global Pro Wrestling. What I _will_ do is go into that arena next Monday night and prove to you, to all the wrestlers in the back, and to all the fans that I _am_ the best wrestler in the NeWF. I don't take shortcuts to get there like others do, I don't walk around and flaunt my talents like others do... I just go out and give it 110% every time I compete, and walk away a winner. Now next Monday, I've got something to prove... you see, I'm not here to wrestle a few matches and make a few dollars while waiting on my next gig. I'm here to _continue_ to establish myself as one of the best in the business, period. I didn't graduate from
Keening's school like you and a few others did, Collins. I started this business way before that, while I followed my father from town to town, high school gym to armory center, and learned the ropes _that_ way, the
_real_ way. And now, I bring my knowledge, my experience, and my ability to the NeWF, to _prove_ myself as the best in the game."
[Walking away from the window, Adam continues talking.] Rogers: "The road to greatness, to immortality... it all starts Monday night, Collins. You've got to prove that you're worthy of calling yourself
'The Main Event'. I've got to prove that I am who I say I am. It all starts with you, Brian Collins. I've worked my way up from the bottom many times before, and I'm prepared to do it now... and the first step on the ladder is you, my friend. We'll see who the _real_ 'main event' is Monday night, Collins. We'll see who the _real_ champion is. And I'll tell you this... I don't plan on walking away from Indianapolis as the loser."
[With those words, Adam grabs the key card from the table and walks out the door, as we fade to black.]
