Sunday Night Mayhem [June 4, 2000] (Part 1 of 7)

7 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The screen is black. Suddenly we see a flash of light and the video footage from the press conference last week in Las Vegas begins to play with a small graphic in the bottom right hand corner that says "5/27/00"]
[In the front of the room is a large desk with an insignia on it. The insignia reads "Las Vegas Hilton". There is a banner behind the podium and desk that reads "NeWF" and has the league's logo on it. There is a man standing behind the podium and that is FBI Special Agent James, Chris
Hopper is sitting next to the podium on the left side.]
[Agent James seems to move a paper around a bit and then finally continues.] Agent James: At approximately eight-thirty last night, our own FBI squad acted on a sting operation we had been planning for weeks. We invaded the building, then arrested and arraigned Mr. Colt Anderson late last night.
Mr. Anderson was under federal suspicion for many infractions. Among these infractions were...
[The screen switches to show a black and white still photo of just a few weeks back. Colt Anderson smiling as he stands in the center of the ring.
We can hear the words of Agent James over the picture.] Agent James: ...racketeering... [The picture of Anderson flips over and shows it in reverse negative.] Agent James: ...income tax invasion... [The picture flips back to what it was at first.] Agent James: ...money laundering... [The picture of Anderson flips over and shows it in reverse negative again.] Agent James: ...drug trafficking... [The picture flips back to what it was at first.] Agent James: ...and prostitution. [The screen flips back to the footage of the press conference.[ Agent James: If convicted, he will lose them all to the highest bidder at an estate auction that will go to pay all the expected fines he will be assessed besides his jail time. That is all except one.
[James puts his hand into his suit jacket and pulls out a folder of papers. He then smiles a smug smile at Chris Hopper and continues.] Agent James: The National E-Wrestling Federation will not be held under
IRS diress any longer. As a gesture of gratitude for his help in this investigation, we have been authorized to sell the NeWF back to the original owner for the amount of...
[James gestures for Hopper to stand up and come to the podium.] Agent James: One Dollar. [The press corps gasp as Hopper takes a crisp one dollar bill and hands it to Agent James. The flash bulbs are making the room light up as if on strobe lights as James and Hopper both sign the top sheet of paper inside the folder. The screen goes to static for a split second and shows the following footage from just a moment later at the press conference.]
Hopper: So I am first announcing the righting of some of the wrongs. [Hopper flashes a smile as he continues.] Hopper: First off, I am giving back the jobs of a great many of the employees who were fired by Anderson.
[Another blast of static and then the footage continues.] Hopper: I will be striking that event from the record books and re-setting all won/loss records back to zero. The wrestlers and employees of the NeWF should not have to suffer due to Anderson for any reason, and that includes professionally. Thus I am making it a level playing field from the get-go.
The men can earn their spots like every other business venture. I hope our fan following will stay high as we try and overcome this bump in our road.
[Hopper then lifts the NeWF National Title from under the podium and sets it on the top of the podium. It gleams in the lights of the large room.]
Hopper: This National Title will be given away in a tournament. To show the level playing field, I will be drawing these names at random and it is that order that they will be placed in this sixteen man field. Many men will be left out, but that is the breaks of a level playing field. This tournament begins next week when we have our next televised show in Reno,
Nevada on June fourth.
[As Hopper smiles and turns his head slightly the screen fades to black and the following words flash up in bright red...]

Indiana. Fireworks begin to explode as the fans are cheering wildly.
There are banners and signs everywhere. One of the signs say, "NeWF is
Cool Again!". Another says, "Anderson needs soap on a rope!". Yet another says, "The Mighty Pig Rules!"]
[The fans are working up to a simmer as the screen switches to show our announcing team. On the left sits Eric Chaney. He is wearing a blue polo shirt and has the traditional headset on his head. The other man, on the right, is former wrestling great Larry "The Crusher" Watters. He is rugged looking and has a scowl on his face constantly. He is wearing a suit and tie and has jet black hair. The music dies down as Eric begins to speak.]
Eric Chaney: Ladies and Gentlemen we are live here in Reno, Nevada for what is sure to be the greatest night in NeWF History!
Larry Watters: Shut up you little girlie twit. This is the worst thing that could have happens for you and me.
Eric Chaney: Fans don't mind him, he's just cranky. I'm sure you see the signs and who are we to argue with them...NeWF is back under the reign of the creator! The man who started it all, Chris Hopper!
Larry Watters: And aren't we all just thrilled to pieces! Eric Chaney: I am Eric Chaney and with me tonight is former wrestling great, Larry "The Crusher" Watters. Tonight we are expecting quite a large amount of excitement due to the fact tha...
LW: The fact that you will not shut the Hell up! I mean Good Lord! These people came to see men like myself, the true wrestling champions put on this show. Not to hear a snot-nosed punk like you talk their G[bleep]amn ears off!
EC: Larry, I do not think you can say that on television. LW: Does it look like I care? EC: Well folks, since Larry here is obviously a little out of sorts and upset at something, I can tell you that we are expecting to see the Owner of the NeWF in this early segment. Before he comes out, we do want to say that tonight's show has FOUR National Title Tournament matches in it. We also will crown the first set of Champions in the NeWF tonight when the tag team Titles are awarded. This is a banner night for the NeWF and we are just getting started!
LW: Are you done yet? EC: Yes, we need to go to the ring and to ring announcer, Steven Stewert. [The screen cuts to the ring where Steven Stewert is standing in the center holding the microphone and a index card.]
SS: Ladies and Gentlemen here in Reno, Nevada. It is my distinct pleasure to present to you the Owner and President of the NeWF...
C H R I S H O P P E R ! ! ! [A loud voice booms over the PA system.] VOICE: MAKE WAY FOR THE KING OF COOL !!! [The lights go out suddenly as the beginning strums of "TNT" by AC/DC start to blare over the loudspeakers. The crowd erupts with a huge face pop as the screen lights up with images of "Too Cool" Chris Hopper. The music plays for a bit and then burst into the chorus.]
# CHORUS: # # 'Cause I'm # #T.# [Quick clip of the final second of Hopper landing the Icebreaker on Jinsei
Shakanuzi. Followed by a Black letter "T" filling the screen for a second.]
#N.# [Quick clip of the final second of Hopper landing the Icebreaker on Robert
Hearsch. Followed by a Black letter "N" filling the screen for a second.]
#T.# [Quick clip of the final second of Hopper landing the Icebreaker on the
SECW's Tommy Gilstrap. Followed by a Black letter "T" filling the screen for a second.]
#I'm dynamite # [Clip of Hopper's eight foot tall name molding exploding at the entrance way during a TV show. Fireworks are blazing all around.]
#T.# [Hopper nailing a DDT on Gorilla in NeCW. Followed by a Black letter "T" filling the screen for a second.] #N.# [Quick clip of the final second of Hopper landing the Icebreaker on Scimitar in SECW. Followed by a Black letter "N" filling the screen for a second.] #T.# [Hopper swinging the aluminum bat right into Extreme's leg at November
Nightmare '98. Followed by a Black letter "T" filling the screen for a second.]
# and I'll win the fight # [Camera swings away from the screen at this point.] [Hopper then enters the arena and the attention is off the big screen. The music continues through the chorus as Hopper struts down to the ring.
Hopper is wearing a black armani suit, with a white shirt and gold tie. He has his black hair pulled back in a tail and his smiles shines as bright as the lights that his sunglasses are shielding. He begins to walk down to the ring as the fans go nuts for him. He reaches the ring and climbs inside and takes the microphone from Steven Stewert. The fans begin a
"NeWF!" chant. Hopper smiles as he listens to the roar and finally raises the mic to his mouth.]
President Hopper: HELLO REEEEEEENNNOOOOOOOOOOO, NEVADAAAAAAAAA! [The fans give a monster face pop.] President Hopper: It has been a long time, yet it only feels like a couple of weeks! I can't believe how much I have missed being around these guys in the NeWF. I am glad to be back!
[The fans start a massive "NeWF!" chant.] President Hopper: Speaking of guys who are away from us now, I want to send a special prayer up for Matt Stone. On Thursday Night he was attacked during the main event of one of our "House Arrest" Shows and was seriously injured. It appears he will be paralyzed from the waist down, but Doctors say he does have a chance to walk again, even if that chance is very small.
So Matt, We here at NeWF will miss you around the locker room and there is a spot here waiting for you in ANY capacity you want when you get back up and around.
[Fans give a round of applause for both Matt Stone and Hopper's proclamation about how the league will deal with the injury.]
President Hopper: Now we move to the masked man who caused this terrible injury to occur. I am not sure who you are, nor am I even sure why you felt the need to show up and hurt someone; but I know that if I EVER see you again I will have your ass thrown out of here so fast it would make your head spin...
[Hopper pauses as the crowds begin to chant for him.] President Hopper: But not before I let every single person in the locker room get a free shot that wanted one!
[Monster crowd pop.] President Hopper: Now it is also my duty to announce that former Title contender Nicklaus Kain is no longer with this company.
[The announcers can be overheard as Hopper pauses a moment.] LW: What!?!?!?! He was the backbone of this league! EC: You mean WAS the backbone of this league. I am sure there is a reason. LW: There better be. [Hopper continues.] President Hopper: After losing his match on Thursday Night, he decided to destroy league property and also physically threaten an official. I took him into an office and had a few words to warn him of the league's policies and Nick decided he didn't want to follow by those principles. In fact he went as far as walking up to the first security guard he saw and punching him out. I had him removed from the arena and the payroll as he obviously surrendered his position here by his actions. So, Nicklaus Kain is gone, period.
[Fans begin cheering loudly as many of them utterly hated Nick Kain.] President Hopper: Now I have returned and basically gave the NeWF a "do-over" if you will. This league is far too deep with talent and strong in fan base to be wasted on a dirty scoundrel like Colt Anderson. I have decreed that all slates are wiped clean and everyone is back at ground zero. This is the best way I found to give every NeWF star a chance to shone and define his role himself, rather than having it done for him as the past regime did. In NeWF, we are about fairness and just...
[Hopper gets cut off as an ambulance siren starts over the PA, and is soon mixed in with Led Zeppelin's "Kashmir."]
EC: What is this?! LW: I don't know, but it shut Hopper up! WOOHOO! [A man steps out into the aisleway from behind the curtain, followed shortly by a woman. He stands about 6'5" and looks to be anywhere between
260 to 265 pounds. A white t-shirt that's two or three sizes too small stretches tight across his expansive chest, revealing his cut physique. The bottom hem of the shirt ends just past the top of the denim jeans that hang over his black boots. The man's long, black hair hangs down around his shoulders, and he carries a microphone. The woman next to him wears a skin tight black dress, with her black hair hanging down near her waist. The fans look on, not knowing what to think or what to say. The couple makes their way down to ringside, stopping at the stairs. He helps her into the apron, them pulls himself up, stepping through the ropes. He holds them for her and she follows.]
EC: Hey! Where have I seen this guy before? LW: It's...it's Vic Valiant! He was in the MCW when you were back in grade school, Eric! Oooh la la! And his wife Katia Syan!
EC: MCW just began a little more than a year ago! LW: What's your point? President Hopper: Vic? Vic, It is nice to see you, but I don't think you are supp... Valiant: Shut the hell up, Chris. Do you really think I came here for pleasantries? No, no, no, I came here to settle an old score. You can talk all about your "clean slates" and your "do-overs," but the slate will
NEVER be clean between us!
[The crowd begins to boo when they realize Vic is opposed to Hopper.] Valiant: Got yourself some brainwashed sheep? Figures. Hopper: Old score? Vic, I'm not sure I know what you mean? Valiant: Oh, that's right, I almost forgot, you're a dumbass. [The crowd boos again.] Valiant: I'm talking about the MCW. I'm talking about how you screwed over guys like The Prime Time Players, Mr. Fantastic, The Dave, Taryn, Bennett, hell, pretty much the whole damned Inner Circle. You screwed Kain, boy did you ever screw Kain, you screwed me, most people eventually get tired,
Chris. As for where I've been, after I left that godawful MCW, like the
Inner Circle and Kain should have done with me, I went back to Canada to the MLW. Then I tried to find you in the ACCW, but instead I find some stooge. But Nick called me yesterday and I just couldn't pass this opportunity up. Seems backstage politics being what they are and all, Kain was just ass-out again.
[Hopper stands there and just seems very angry as he raises the mic.] President Hopper: Kain attacked an official of the NeWF, I laid down the law on how he was to act when he signed that contract and he then went out and violated that contract. He fired himself, period.
Valiant: Oh no! He hit a security guard? Let's string him up in the middle of the ring! If chaos breaks out over something so simple as an official with his lights knocked out, it'll be World War 3 when I get done doing what I'm going to do to you.
[The crowd boos loudly.] President Hopper: Vic, I am not here as a fighter, but trust me that is something you DO NOT want to do...and don't think I would not be happy to whip your ass for these people here tonight either, because I would!
[The crowd cheers very loudly!] President Hopper: But trouble isn't what I'm looking for on my return. See I am here as an owner...an employer if you will. I am not about beating my employees. In fact, you ARE NOT one of my employees, so the fact you are even in this ring is enough for me to have you arrested!
Valiant: Is it? Well, that would be true, HOWEVER... [Vic reaches into his back pocket and pulls out some folded papers. He holds them up in the air. Hopper looks confused as Valiant unfolds it and explains.]
Valiant: Before he was ousted from his position as head of NeWF, not only another thing I have to "talk" to you about, but also something that's going under investigation by the Internal Affairs of the FBI, my close, personal friend Colt Anderson offered me a contract, which, knowing his relationship with you, I excepted immediately.
[The crowd boos the mention of Colt Anderson.] Valiant: And so, I have as much right to be here as you do, and I plan to stay. After all, this contract guarantees me a couple of things.
President Hopper: Colt Anderson is a criminal. Whatever he promised you isn't his. Whatever bonuses he may have promised you were from his illegal funds.
Valiant: No, no, you can check the dates if you want. He was the official owner at the time of the signing. And as for bonuses, unless you're a drug dealer yourself, these funds are legit. Yes sir, I'm getting paid with
NeWF money, your money. And as for what I was promised, this guarantees me mic time, which I'm taking advantage of now, and....ah yes, a chance at the
National Title. I couldn't help but notice this tournament going on. I'll think I'll redeem my contract stipulation now. Here it's all legal, look!
[Valiant throws the contract at Hopper. It hits Hopper in the chest and
Hopper catches it. He opens it again and reads for a second. Finally he cracks a small smile as he replies.]
President Hopper: First of all, you do have a contract and there is nothing I can say abbot you standing here in this ring and talking.
[The crowd sighs with disappointment.] President Hopper: However, I do have something to say about this other stuff in there. [The crowd lets loose with a loud roar.] President Hopper: You see no matter WHAT the contract says, all air time and all title shots are up to one man's discretion. One man sets those wheels in motion. One man, and that one man is ME!!!
[The crowd roars again.] President Hopper: Air time, well that is probably going to be an easy one that can happen as everyone seems to get a solid amount of that in one way or another. However the title shot is not possible this time around Vicky!
Sorry. [Hopper tosses the contract back at Vic and hits him in the chest and turns to leave the ring. Valiant rushes over and grabs Hopper's shoulders, whirling him around.]
Valiant: Hopper, you dirty son of a bitch! I will be in that damn tournament if I have to fill every room in the local hospital to do it.
You'll have to call off the tournament because there won't be any competitors left! You'll regret it, Hopper, if it's the last thing I ever do!
[Hopper had began to turn around again, but stopped in his tracks as Valiant had said his last phrase. Hopper turned around slowly and raised the mic again.] President Hopper: Interesting. Very interesting idea Vic. Valiant: What the hell are you talking about? President Hopper: Well honestly everything you said meant nothing to me until that last phrase, "if it is the last thing I ever do". See that is the only way I will even give you a chance here.
[Valiant is incensed with anger as he seems to not be following.] President Hopper: I will put a spot in the tourney up in a match. You win the match, you take the place of the wrestler you face tonight. If you lose...
[Crowd seems to be tirelessly waiting...] President Hopper: THEN THE CONTRACT IS VOID AND YOU ARE GONE FOREVER!!! {The crowd erupts in an insanely loud face pop.] President Hopper: Do you have the balls to accept the wager? Valiant: Two types of people would take a wager like that without really knowing all the stakes: a fool, like these "brave" people who work for you.
[The crowd boos at the insult of the roster.] Valiant: Or someone like me, who just doesn't give a damn. So yes, Chris,
I accept your challenge. Who is my opponent?
[Hopper pauses and smiles.] President Hopper: Just a guy that goes by the name... "T H E N A T U R A L" A D A M R O G E R S ! ! ! [The crowd erupts in the loudest face pop of the night as Valiant looks upset at the choice, knowing Rogers is the top good guy in the league.]
Valiant: What?! Rogers? President Hopper: Yes! Beat him and it is simple, you're in. Lose, and
YOU'RE GONE! "Double V" Vs. "The Natural!" It is all in your court now
Vic! Good luck!
[Hopper leaves as his theme music starts up again. When he nearly reaches the aisle, Vic speaks, causing Hopper to turn back around and watch him.]
Valiant: Double V, Chris? Not quite. Double V smiled. Double V laughed. Double V cared. But after MCW and ACCW, where my career was nearly ripped to shreds, Double V is gone. Where I smiled, now a destroy. Where I laughed, now I cripple. Where I cared, now I hurt people, Chris. What's left here is an angry, bitter, vicious animal. And that's what you can call me. Call me "V3" Vicious Vic Valiant. Call an ambulance in advance.
[Vic throws the microphone into the aisle. He's show staring down Hopper as the announcers begin to speak over the picture.]
EC: Hopper lays down the law! Valiant wants to stay in NeWF, he has to go through one man...Adam Rogers!
LW: Hopper is a coward for hiding behind Rogers, and we all know Rogers is
Hopper's "boy". Valiant is screwed! This is terrible!
EC: Fans we will be right back! What a start to MAYHEM! Stay tuned everybody! [Fade to black, then to commercials.]
