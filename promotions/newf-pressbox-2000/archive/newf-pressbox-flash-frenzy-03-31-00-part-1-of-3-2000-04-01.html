Flash Frenzy (3/31/00) (Part 1 of 3)

1 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1- El Puerco 2- The Execution Squad 3- Predator 4- The Cabana Boys 5- "The Natural" Adam Rogers ============================================================== El Puerco ============================================================== [Fade in. Dateline: Late Sunday night, April 2nd. The scene opens in the
Double G Diner, a small but clean 24-hour dining establishment in
Jeffersonville, Indiana. There isn't a soul stirring within the diner, with the exception of Genie Guerrero, the 40-ish owner of the diner. She's seen wiping the counter off with an old rag. The chimes near the front entrance ring as the door swings open. Genie looks up and becomes startled as she sees a masked man standing in front of her. But this isn't any ol'
masked man. This man, who's also wearing a long, dirty trenchcoat, is wearing a bright green and yellow mask. It's none other than NeWF's own,
El Puerco. Unfortunately, Genie doesn't know this. She backs off as she addresses El Puerco in a frightened tone of voice.]
GG: "Who are you? What do you want?" [A smile is seen peaking out from the mouth opening on El Puerco's mask as he sits down at the counter.]
EP: "Calm down, senorita. I'm not here to rob you. I just want some grub." [Genie smiles as she wipes the sweat from her forehead.] GG: "Whew! I didn't know what to think when I saw you came walking in. The mask threw me off." [El Puerco points to his mask.] EP: "Oh, this. It's part of what I do. I'm a professional wrestler. The name's El Puerco. I just came into town to do a NeWF show at the Davis
Arena tomorrow night."
GG: "Oh wow! I should've known! My son's going to that show with his girlfriend! He LOVES wrestling! He was so happy when he heard that there was a show coming to our area. We rarely get that type of stuff here."
[El Puerco nods.] EP: "It's going to be a hot show. Our boss, Kevin Kash, signed some pretty interesting matches. I'm in one of the big matches."
GG: "Really? What's the match?" EP: "I'm facing five other guys in a Six Way Dance. The first guy to score a pinfall or submission not only gets the win. He becomes the first ever
NeWF Light Heavyweight Champion, too."
GG: "Wow! I bet you're nervous!" EP: "Not really. I've been in this business for awhile now. I rarely get nervous anymore. Besides, I don't wanna sound cocky, but I'm probably the most talented athlete in the match. I don't think I have anything to worry about."
[He points to himself.] EP: "There's no way any of those guys could be as lucha libre-a-licious as
'The Mighty Pig'."
[Genie smiles.] GG: "Well, you sure are confident! Best of luck in the match! So, what would you like to eat tonight?"
[Genie hands a menu to El Puerco. He opens it and scans the contents.] EP: "Hmmm. How about a turkey club? That looks good." GG: "Alrighty. That'll be five fifty. Will that be for here or to go?" [El Puerco pays Genie.] EP: "To go, please. I need to head back to my hotel room." [Genie nods.] GG: "Okay. Let me head back into the kitchen to fix your club sandwich. I'll be back with your food in two shakes of a lamb's tail." [El Puerco nods at Genie, smiling.] EP: "Si, si. Take your time." GG: "I'll be right back." [Genie turns around and enters the kitchen, disappearing from camera view. As soon as she exits, El Puerco turns around to face the camera, while still sitting on the stool. He points to his surroundings.]
EP: "Another day, another town. That's what life of 'The Mighty Pig' has become since he signed with the NeWF a week and a half ago. But you won't hear him complain. Hell no."
[He nods.] EP: "He missed the competition. He missed having matches that actually
MEANT something. He missed having GOALS. He APPRECIATES the opportunities that he has gotten since he became a part of the NeWF, especially the opportunity that he's getting this Monday night."
[He holds up five fingers and then one more finger as he continues to address the camera.] EP: "Six men are going to enter that ring tomorrow night. All six men are going to have one thing on their mind and that's becoming the first NeWF
Light Heavyweight Champion. But only one man is gonna walk away from the match with that belt around his waist, and you're looking at him."
[El Puerco rolls his eyes.] EP: "Damon Knight has practically made himself out to be the star of the
Light Heavyweight division. He's called himself the FUTURE the man who's going to lead the NeWF to the next level. 'The Might Pig' is pretty sure that Senor Knight thinks that HE will be walking out of the Davis Arena as the NeWF Light Heavyweight Champion."
[He shakes his head.] EP: "'The Mighty Pig' has some news for you, gringo. He's not like Tyrone
Parker and Derek Diamond. He's not intimidated by your rhyming wordplay.
'The Mighty Pig' isn't afraid of you, period. He knows DAMN WELL that he could beat you with one arm tied behind his back!"
[He points to himself.] EP: "'The Mighty Pig' thinks you're nothing more than some wannabe who bites off more than he can chew. Before your matches, you're blabbing endlessly about how you're the best, unbeatable, blah blah blah, but then, when the match finally happens, you get your punk ass handed to you on a silver platter."
[El Puerco snickers.] EP: "'The Mighty Pig' thinks you are nothing more than a steaming pile of donkey turds. You are NOT what you say you are. You think you're the main attraction? Twice the satisfaction? LOTS OF ACTION?! You're more like the light heavyweight joke of the nation, a loser of a man that 'The Mighty
Pig' could beat on any given occasion and the only way you can get any action is through MASTURBATION."
[He points to himself again.] EP: "When you step into the ring with 'The Mighty Pig', you're going to see who the BEST really is. The BEST light heavyweight superstar in the NeWF is six feet tall, two hundred and twenty-three pounds, and chock-filled with lucha libre-a-licious goodness! The BEST light heavyweight in this organization is 'The Mighty Pig'. Learn to live with that, hombre, 'cause it won't be changing any time soon."
[El Puerco shakes his head.] EP: "Damon Knight isn't the only guy on the mind of 'The Mighty Pig' right now. He's been thinking about another one of the guys he'll be facing tomorrow night - Derek Diamond."
[He cocks his head to the side.] EP: "In a way, Derek Diamond seems a little brighter than Damon Knight. He gave 'The Mighty Pig' props for having skills and being an athlete. But he made a BIG mistake when said that 'The Mighty Pig' didn't have dignity, honor, or guts. He made the BIGGEST MISTAKE of his career when he said that 'The Mighty Pig' didn't deserve to be the representative of the NeWF's
Light Heavyweight division."
[He folds his arms across his chest as he leans back on the counter, still talking.] EP: "'The Mighty Pig' says that you are DEAD WRONG with your assumptions. You said that nobody could beat a confident man. 'The Mighty Pig' hopes that that isn't your final answer because it's INCORRECT."
[El Puerco throws his arms in the air, shaking his head.] EP: "'The Mighty Pig' doesn't care if you have a heart of a champion a heart of gold or if you like that crappy Blondie song, 'Heart of Glass'.
All of that nonsense isn't going to matter once that bell rings. POW! BAM! SLAM! That'll be the sound of 'The Mighty Pig' KILLING your confidence. Prepare to hear that tune tomorrow night, hombre, because THAT is how it's all gonna go down."
[He unfolds his arms, shaking his head and grinning.] EP: "And don't think 'The Mighty Pig' has forgotten about you, Joe Williams. He has a special message for you, too." [He points at the camera.] EP: "While you were out going to amusement parks and cavorting with your skank girlfriend and that old fart manager of yours, 'The Mighty Pig' was training vigorously preparing his every move and plotting your downfall."
[He sighs.] EP: "You've said that 'big match' and 'The Mighty Pig' don't go well together." [El Puerco nods.] EP: "'The Mighty Pig' thinks he could play this game, too. How about Joe
Williams and talent? Or what about Joe Williams and common sense? Or, better yet, how about Joe Williams and NeWF Light Heavyweight Champion?
None of those things go well together, either, man. As a matter of fact,
'The Mighty Pig' thinks that the only word that goes well with Joe Williams is LOSER."
[He shrugs] EP: "The bottom line is, it doesn't matter if you're Tyrone Parker, Derek
Diamond, Damon Knight, Luscious Jasmine, or some crackhead freak named
Hardcorenstein. After this Monday night is said and done, you're ALL going to have one thing in common. You're all going to have the HONOR of saying that you fell at the hands of 'The Mighty Pig'."
[El Puerco beckons and the camera zooms in for a close-up. He points to his masked face.] EP: "Look very closely, hombres, because THIS is the face of the first, last, and ONLY NeWF Light Heavyweight Champion. Respect 'THE PIG'."
[The camera zooms away back to its original wideshot position. Meanwhile,
Genie emerges from the kitchen with a paper bag.]
GG: "Okay. Here's your turkey club." [El Puerco turns around and she hands the bag over to him. El Puerco smiles politely.] EP: "Gracias." [El Puerco stands up.] GG: "Good luck in your match tomorrow. I'm sure my son will be in the crowd, cheering you on."
EP: "Have a good night, senorita. Thank you for your hospitality." [El Puerco slams his hand on the counter.] "La buena noche y el dios bendicen!" [A translation appears on the bottom of the screen - "Good night and God bless!" El Puerco turns around and exits the diner. Genie looks down at the counter to find a twenty-dollar bill. Genie flashes a huge smile as she pockets the money.]
GG: "What a guy!" [Fade to festive green...] ============================================================== The Execution Squad ============================================================== (The screen flickers to black, and a billboard is posted) WE ARE EXPERIENCING TECHNICAL DIFFICULTIES. PLEASE STAND BY. (The billboard flickers and is erased from the screen in a flash, replaced by a long shot of a prison corridor. The dim lighting, provided by a solitary bulb, creates a chilling effect. The cell doors along either side of the corridor are open. Apparently this prison has been abandoned for some time. Water leaks in through the crumbling brick, creating a morbid cadence for the scene that is unfolding. Rats scurry about freely between the cells as the camera begins a slow walk up the corridor. A male baritone voice interrupts the echoing footsteps of the lone cameraman.)
VOICE: The final hour is fast approaching. (The camera pans left and right as we advance along the rows of cells, revealing the rotting condition of the building and its contents. Bunks have been overturned, many cell toilets have been broken. Bars have rusted and fallen away from the doors.)
VOICE: There are no more appeals. (The camera comes to the end of the corridor and stops with a jolt. The near total absence of light prevents the viewer from seeing what is in front of the camera.)
VOICE: Your time is up. (A rat screeches across the floor and startles the camera. The camera turns to try and follow the rat, but it has scurried into a hole in the prison wall. As the camera returns to its original position an overhead light explodes to life, casting an eerie shadow on an old electric chair.
The immense piece of furniture fills the screen, casting an ominous silhouette. The worn wooden frame and imposing leather restraints are a testament to all of the men who have lost their lives to its unflinching grasp.)
VOICE: Very soon, every man in the NeWF will know the feeling of taking his final walk down "The Row."
(The light overhead flickers, and then the electric chair hums to life. The generator pops and crackles in the background, sounding more like a sleeping monster come back to life than a worn out pile of electrical wiring and switches. Sparks begin to fly and smoke billows from the dilapidated wooden chair. The smoke fills the screen. Out of the smoke spirals the NeWF logo.)
VOICE: NeWF, prepare yourselves. The Execution Squad is ready to carry out your sentence!
(The light above the chair flickers once more and then expires, leaving the scene in total blackness. Out of the blackness smoke rises from the bottom of the screen and begins forming into the following words.)
THE EXECUTION SQUAD (The smoke letters hold their shape for just a moment before being dissipated by the sound of a puff of air, like someone blowing out a candle. The scene fades out to black.)
============================================================== Predator ============================================================== [Scene Begins in a Police Station. Predator is being handcufed for his actions at the Tower. Several homeless men are in casts giving statements to the police. Predator is thrown into a cell with three large bikers. They all corner him and laugh. Predator than kicks one in the groin and slugs one in the face. The other throws a punch but Predator guides it into the wall. The man screams in pain as he is whipped onto the bars. Stumbling back, Predator whips a second man into the first. Taking the third, He lifts the man with an incredable amount of strength and throws him into the other 2. The bars finnally give way to the weight and the cops draw their guns on Predator. He raises his hands above his head and stares into the camera, smiling.]
Predator: Come X-Tremely Dangerous..... NO CAGE WILL HOLD ME [Fade out at Predator being taken away] ============================================================== The Cabana Boys ============================================================== (*We come across The Cabana Boys at a "Big Kahuna Burger" just south of the
Esplanade in sunny Los Angeles. Casually scarfing down Pineapple Chicken
Kabobs and slurping red Icy's, the Boys seem oblivious to the camera crew surrounding them. Finally, a sound tec taps Shannon on the shoulder....*)
Garrett: Can't talk...eating.... Tech: (*whispers*) Garrett: Oh, right. That whole "flash" thing. I'm on it. (*wipes his mouth and begins to speak*) Greetings NeWF. I'm Shannon Garrett, and this
Stallion to my left is Jay "My Mom is a lesbian" Spencer. Collectively, we stole your little brothers Huffy...but as a team, call us The Cabana Boys.
Spencer: (*swallows a gulp of Icee*) Yeah, we've actually been hanging around in the back of the NeWF locker room for a few weeks. Not in the showers though...straight men hanging around in the showers is kinda...y'know..."Moe". Not that there's anything WRONG with that. I have
MANY gay friends. Well, maybe not MANY.....
Garrett: Jay, man. You're blowing it. Spencer: Sorry. What was I saying? Oh, yeah. We're gonna win the NeWF tag team titles. Why, you ask? (*brief pause, perplexed look*) Actually, I don't have an answer.....
Garrett: Can I field this one? Spencer: Have a ball, duder..... Garrett: We're gonna win the tag team titles because...well...because we're really good. Really REALLY good. I mean, there's some decent teams...and some decent wrestlers I guess...but we're like better. Like MUCH better.
And SOME of these teams just rub me the wrong way, man. Can't lie to ya. Some of these teams are just dicks. (*to the camera guy*) Can I call them
"dicks"? Camera Guy: (*shrugs shoulders*) You'll find out tomorrow I guess. Garrett: Right, good plan. Anyhow, leading this brigade of Tool boxes is
Germanys least favorite sons, The Iron Reich. Now here's two guys that just smell queer...
Spencer: Not that there's anything wrong with that. Garrett: Exactly. These guys just go goose stepping around, yelling "Seig
Heil" and scaring all the girls away. Thats NOT cool, brahs. For all your
"the world is gonna end" stuff, these waves just keep gettin' bigger, and life just keeps gettin' sunnier. I think a good way for us to get some exposure....
Spencer: Not that there's anything WRONG with that. Garrett: Dude, eat your chicken. A good way for us to get exposure would be this: Me and Jay here are challenging The Iron Reich next week, any type of match. And boys, bring a raincoat, because the waves are gonna be HUGE!
(*Fade to black.*) ============================================================== "The Natural" Adam Rogers ============================================================== [The camera comes to life, giving us a panoramic shot of a downright beautiful scene. The setting is a rustic one, overlooking a lake, the water as smooth as glass on the windless evening. Around the lake as far as we can see are rows on rows of various kinds of trees - pine, oak, you name it. Judging by the dull orange glow of light, the sun is setting in this early evening.]
[The camera changes angles without warning, pulling back into what we'd figure to be a cabin of sorts, giving us a view of a porch jutting out and overlooking the beautiful scene we opened with. A few pieces of furniture are positioned on the porch, and in a chair facing out toward the lake, we see a familiar face, belonging to none other than 'The Natural' Adam
Rogers.] [Adam appears to be simply relaxing and enjoying the peace and quiet, a far cry for the commotion that's been going on lately in his job. He takes a sip from a cup he holds in his right hand, and looks out over the endless scenery in front of him. Taking in a deep breath, he begins to talk, still not having turned directly to the camera.]
AR: "Man, did I need this. Getting back to nature, the peace and quiet...after all the crap I heard earlier this week from some of my
'fellow competitors', this is a _more_ than welcome break from it all." [It should be noted that the words 'fellow competitors' were said with a hint of sarcasm.] AR: "But I can't just forget about it forever, or else I'll end up on the bottom of the food chain. The only problem this week is deciding where to begin. Why don't we start with the piece of garbage I beat last week,
Brian Collins."
['The Natural' shifts his weight in the chair, and sets the ceramic mug down on a table beside him.]
AR: "You know, Collins, it's people like you who really ruin this great sport. Your arrogance, your refusal to accept reality... guys like you are all over the place, a dime a dozen, and doing very little to enhance the image of our profession. So Collins, you _really_ expect everyone to believe you coudl have beaten me any time you wanted to? Now, how many people do you think will buy into that load of crap? I can answer that for you... one person, and that person would be you. The fact is, you cheated as much as you could, you broke every rule possible, and I _still_ beat you cleaner than a baby's rear end. Now, you can moan and cry 'foul' all you want; but the cold, hard truth is that you lost to the better man, pure and simple. Don't believe me? Go watch the tape. Want some more? You know where to find me."
[Adam pauses, giving the camera a short glare, a glare more or less directed at Brian Collins. The expression on the face of 'The Natural'
then changes to a more serious but stoic look.] AR: "Now, as for my opponent this Monday... War Child thought my entrance was long and boring? He ought to check out his words earlier this week.
It amazed me how many times he could recycle a sentence. Now _that_ is the pure definition of long and boring."
[A small smirk comes over Adam's face. That's not something we see all that often, and not something he really likes to let happen, but in this case it was pretty hard to contain. Just as quickly as it appears, Adam wipes it off his face, leaning forward in the chair toward the camera.]
AR: "Let me ask you a question, 'Extreme Warrior'..." [The words 'Extreme Warrior' had about the same tone of sarcasm as we heard earlier.] AR: "Am I supposed to be _intimidated_ by the way you punch lockers and throw chairs? Is that supposed to scare me? If it is, you better go watch another 'Halloween' movie or something, because it ain't working. If this is supposed to make me want to 'beg for mercy', you need to come up with a better strategy. Your size, your tough guy look, that may frighten some...
but not me. I've been in the ring with men far bigger than you, much badder than you, and a lot uglier..."
[Adam pauses, looking upward in thought.] AR: "OK, I don't think I've ever seen anyone _uglier_ than you. And I
_know_ I've never heard anyone dumber than you, especially if you really believe the crap you said about my family."
[Most take quick offense when their family gets brought into things. However, Adam doesn't seem to be enraged by War Child's comments.] AR: "First off, if you think I'm getting by in NeWF based on my family name, you've been given some bad information. Let's get this clear right here and now... it makes no difference _what_ anyone in my family has done.
I've still got to get the job done myself, on my own. I get no perks, no benefits, from my family's long-time connection with the sport, because I flat-out refuse them. Every bit of success I've had and will have will be off my _own merit_, not someone else's. And tough guy, I plan on giving your big ass a first-hand introduction to that on Monday night. The Rogers torch isn't burning out, it's burning highher and hotter than ever, and it's going to burn _you_ soon enough."
[Adam seems to be getting a little fired up now, as the intensity in his voice and eyes begins to rise. He lifts a hand and runs it over the top of his short blond hair, now leaning back in the chair once again.]
AR: "But the thing that sickens me the most is you, _you_ of all people accusing _me_ of taking short cuts. Your entire style is centered around shortcuts... steel chairs, barbed wire, baseball bats... what do you call those, tools of the trade? You use more hardware than Bob Vila, and you don't think _that_ constitutes taking short cuts? Man, you must have been busted upside the head one too many times. You're damn right I have values, morals, ethics. You know why? Because I don't _need_ to dump them to get ahead. I don't _have_ to take short cuts, because I don't _need_ them. If I can't win on my ability alone, then I don't win, pure and simple. You want to accuse me of riding my family name, call me a panzy, go right ahead... but after Monday night, you'll know what 'The Natural'
Adam Rogers is all about." [Slowly, Adam rises to his feet, his muscular frame showing through the blue and white golf shirt he wears. We hear a gentle chuckle underneath his breath, as he walks to the wooden railing marking the end of the porch.]
AR: "Your best weapon has already failed you, War Child. Your kind operates on putting fear and doubt into your opponents, and I _sure_ as hell don't fear you. Go ahead and talk about putting me into early retirement, breaking every bone in my body, making me beg for mercy...
because it's just _talk_. In the ring is where it counts, tough guy... right there between the ropes. _That's_ where it counts, and _that's_ where I excel. And when I beat you just like Collins, you'll know that I don't ride my family name. You'll know I paid my dues a long time ago.
You'll know that I'm not a fluke, but that I am the flat-out _best_ wrestler in the game today. And while you lay flat on your back, embarrassed at losing again, you'll wish you'd never heard the name Adam
Rogers."
[As he completes the sentence, Adam gives the camera another hard glare, giving us a short chance to see the intensity and drive in his crystalline blue eyes. Shortly afterward, Adam walks away from the camera's view, leaving us only with a shot of the 'natural' scenery, as the camera fades to black.]
