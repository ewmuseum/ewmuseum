Sunday Night Mayhem [June 4, 2000] (Part 4 of 7)

7 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We return from commercials to see Dyan Cicerelli is standing outside of
Sean Cage's locker room)
Dyan: Sean? [She knocks on the door and Keri opens it.] Keri: What do you want? Dyan: I was wondering if I could get a word with Sean. Keri: Yeah hold on. [Keri steps back into the locker room and a few seconds later Sean appears.] Sean: Yeah I'm glad you're here. Dyan: Sean we wou... Sean: You would like to know why I went out and watched Castle and Collins? Dyan: Yeah do you ha... Sean: No I don't have anything against either man maybe Collins but I respect Castle. Dyan: Were you sco..... Sean: Yeah I was scouting a little bit. I will more than likely meet one of these two men in the finals of the tournament.
Dyan: You mean you are loo..... Sean: No I'm not looking past my 1st round match. But the reason I was scouting tonight is this. See me and Castle was supposed to have a move v move match. But with the controversy it seems to have been cancelled. So
Castle how about you me. Let's show the fans how this thing is done.
[Sean then shuts the door and goes back into the locker room/] Dyan: I can't believe he wouldn't let me ask any questions. Guys back to you. [Scene away from that picture to show our announcers live at ringside.] EC: We are back and it seems that Sean Cage is beginning to figure out where he stands in the Tournament draw!
LW: That moron is thinking WAY too far ahead! I mean he actually said something about the FINALS?!?! puh-lease!
EC: Well Cage has the talent to go far, and he is still considered one of the most popular performers in the NeWF right now. That may actually make him a possible Champion soon.
LW: Well he may be the most popular guy in the draw, especially when
"Triple V" takes care of Rogers and sends him out of the tourney tonight!
EC: Would you please just stop taking about Valiant for a little while? LW: Whatever. EC: Now our next match is another Tournament match....now what?!?! [Suddenly, "America the Beautiful" by Katharine Lee Bates begins to play over the P.A as red, white, and blue fireworks explode at the entrance way.
Walking down the aisle comes "Crazy" Lou Natic wearing bright royal blue sweat pants and a white T-shirt with a red dickey bow, while carrying a USA flag over his shoulder. He also has on lens-less plastic glasses reading
"USA" with the "S" sitting on the bridge of his nose, and his eyes behind the "U" and the "A". His messy black, slightly curled, hair comes down to just above his shoulders in the back, and covers his eyebrows in the front.
Lou's full-grown white beard could use a trim. He high-fives fans on his way to ringside before finally entering the ring and calling for the house mic., as the music dies down.]
Lou Natic: "I'd ask now that you all stand for the singing of the 'Star
Shambled Wrestler'!"
[The fans rise, as "The Star Spangled Banner" plays throughout the arena and Lou waves his USA flag high in the air. He then sings along to the finish.]
Lou Natic: "AND THE HOOOOOOOOME... OF TRIPP... SHAAAAAAADE." [Crowd cheer as the song ends.] Lou Natic [to himself]: "Man, that Tripp Shade guy is cool, got his name in a song and everything. How come nobody sings my name in any of their songs?"
[Lou shrugs.] Lou Natic: "Ladies and gentlemen, as you all know, today is... the 4th of
June! The day we celebrate our nation's birthday! Now, being the proud
American I am, I saw it fitting for me to come out here today and celebrate with my fellow Americans!"
[Crowd cheer.] Lou Natic: "In honor of our nation, I have prepared a poem that I call...'Lou Natic's Poem on Our Nation's Birthday.' And I'd like to read it here to you all today!"
[Crowd cheer as Lou takes a piece of paper from his pocket and unfolds it. He then clears his throat and begins the grand struggle of reading.] Lou Natic: "I pledge all e-e-egiance... to the flag... of the U n' I Ted of
America." [The camera gets a shot of the paper in Lou's hand, revealing a copy of the
"Pledge of Allegiance".]
Lou Natic: "And to the republicans... for which I can't stand... one instant... under God... invisible, with puberty and just ice for all."
[Crowd cheer. Lou takes a bow, and stays bent over.] Lou Natic[to himself]: "They liked it, they really liked it! YOU ARE THE
MAN!.. Really!? Maybe I should become a poet?... That or run for congress.
Hmm.." [Lou stands up and faces the crowd.] Lou Natic: "Well folks.. I've gotta go!!" [Crowd 'boo'.] Lou Natic: "No, I mean really... I GOTTA GO!!" [Lou starts doing the pee-pee dance. He then runs out of the ring and disappears into the back to answer nature's call.]
LW: That Guy is just nuts. EC: I think I have to agree with you there. What is he thinking? Making fun of the American pledge of Allegiance? Lou Natic is a freaking LOON!!!
LW: You are just now figuring that one out? EC: Well I had hoped he had gotten better during the past few weeks, but it looks like my hopes were just killed.
LW: Happens to the best of us kid, it really does. EC: Well I was just beginning to discuss the tourney match we have coming up between Jack Mannon and War Child.
LW: War Child was out earlier and put forth his strong words for not just
Mannon, but the entire league! They better be ready too because the man can back up what he says!
EC: He is talented and now we get to see if Jack Mannon can actually face off against this extreme monster! Let's head to the ring and Steven
Stewert! [The screen cuts to the ring where Steven Stewert is standing in the center holding the microphone and a index card.]
SS: Ladies and Gentlemen, this match is a one-fall twenty minutes time limit match. It is a First Round match in the National Heavyweight Title
Tournament!
[Crowd pop.] SS: Introducing first, already standing inside the ring. He stands a robust six-foot-four-inches tall and weighing in at three-hundred-twenty-four pounds, I give you...
J A C K M A N N O N ! ! ! [Mannon raises his arm and acknowledges a crowd that mildly pops for him.] LW: What a loser! Hopper must be out of his friggin mind! EC: Why? LW: I mean to put Jack Mannon in this match> This has to be the biggest mistake since putting Don Gately in a match. This tournament is WAY too predictable.
EC: I disagree with you there. LW: Gee, I am just SOOOOOO shocked there! [Crowd pop as Stewert gets ready to continue..] SS: And his opponent! He is the current Japanese Hardcore Champion! Here is... "T H E E X T R E M E W A R R I O R" W A R C H I L D ! ! ! ["Dragula" by Rob Zombie plays over the sound system and the crowd explodes into a frenzied mix of boos and cheers. The camera cuts to the curtain from the broadcast position. War Child appears from the behind the curtain, dressed in his usual Ring attire. He has that Japanese Title around his waist and he points to it like it is the end-all, be all of the league. He reaches the ring and takes the belt off. He stands there waiting for the start of the match.]
EC: I still can't believe he is wearing that belt, he is just disrespectful. LW: (chuckles) Not as disrespectful as Vic Valiant! That guy is a freaking God among men.
EC: Isn't that going a little far? [DING DING DING] LW: Wow, "Triple V" Vicious Vic Valiant. EC: You going to concentrate on this match? LW: Oh, you mean Jack Mannon Vs. The War Child? EC: Yes. LW: "Triple V" Vicious Vic Valiant. Wow. (Eric sighs.) EC: These two behemoths meet in the middle of the ring. Mannon has the
45-pound weight advantage, but War Child has the three-inch height advantage. This should be an interesting match.
LW: Mannon is extremely bulky for a martial artist. How does he manage a whole lot of those kicks with that much weight?
EC: That's a good question. Most successful martial artists are smaller. Bruce Lee, Jean Claude Van Damme, Jet Li, Jackie Chan, none of them are even remotely this large.
LW: There's no history of him on record, so who knows what kind of success he's had? [War Child puffs out his chest and looks down at Mannon. Mannon gets his hands up in a defensive position. War Child punches, but Mannon parries to the side and connects with a short backfist to War Child's cheek. War
Child punches again and gets the same result. Incensed, War Child dashes
Mannon, who cannot sidestep in time and gets tackled to the mat.]
EC: Mannon, starting out with some nice counter moves, was just too muscular to get out of the way of the smaller War Child.
LW: War Child's beating the life out of him! EC: But look! Mannon's going into the Guard! [Mannon wraps his legs around War Child's waist and draws his legs up so his knees protect his ribs. He then places his left hand on the back of
Child's neck and places his right hand around Child's left bicep, halting all blows.]
EC: Mannon has the guard cinched in, War Child's not going anywhere! LW: So...what, he just sits there? EC: Well, ideally, he'll roll it over and the two will switch positions, with Mannon striking War Child or locking him in some sort of hold.
LW: Doesn't look like that's going to happen now! [War Child wraps his arms around Mannon and arches his back, roaring as he lifts Mannon up, then throws his legs out and sandwiches Mannon between himself and the mat, knocking him free.]
LW: War Child is grasping his back now! EC: I don't blame him, but Mannon has to have pain shooting through his entire body. That's 280 lbs. crunching into him.
LW: The ten count is up already in this match. Referee Chris Elliot: ONE!.....TWO!.....THREE!.......FOUR!......FIVE! EC: War Child up to his feet! LW: At five? That's not very dramatic. [War Child lifts Mannon up by the hair and whips him into the ropes. Child throws a clothesline that Mannon ducks, but then turns and follows him, throwing a enzuilariato that slingshots Mannon's neck off the top rope, sending him to the mat.]
EC: War Child just NAILED Mannon with an enzuilariato, i.e. a lariat to the back of the head for people like Larry, or people in California.
LW: Hey, I knew what that was. But the people in California probably didn't. Especially El Cajon.
EC: Yeah, El Cajon sucks. [War Child hits the ropes and hits Mannon with an elbow drop, following with a cover.] Referee Elliot: ONE!....TWO!.... EC: Mannon kicked out! Not going to get him that easily! LW: How about this easily? [War Child has by this point picked Mannon up and dropped him back to the mat with a DDT.] EC: Fans, don't forget, double main event! On one side, you have "Box
Office" Tripp Shade Vs. "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams in their first round
National Title Tournament!
[War Child whips Mannon into the ropes and nails him with a spinebuster, alongside a pin.] Referee Elliot: ONE! EC: Tripp Shade... Referee Elliot: TWO! EC: The Box Office "smasher" who's made such movies as... [Mannon kicks out.] EC: "Dead Men Don't Eat Breakfast" and... LW: Um, Eric, you're not concentrating on the match. EC: Hey, it works for the "Big Boys." So, anyway, then you have Joe Williams, who has a really bad attitude. Why? LW: Maybe he didn't get enough love as a child. [War Child approaches Mannon, looking around at the crowd. When he bends down to pick Mannon up, Mannon rolls him up.]
LW: Roll up my Mannon! EC: No, no, Larry, you're doing it wrong. You don't "call the action." Psh. What you should be doing is hyping the main event. Geez, how long have you been doing this?
LW: *dejectedly* Sorry Eric. EC: And on a small side note, War Child kicked out of the roll up. [War Child charges the downed Mannon with an elbow smash, but Mannon rolls away and then back over onto War Child's back for a rear chinlock.]
EC: And on the other side of that double main event, "The Natural" Adam
Rogers, with his tournament spot on the line, defends NeWF and Chris Hopper against "V3" Vicious Vic Valiant, who's putting up his contract signed, sealed, and delivered by Colt Anderson.
[War Child attempts to crawl towards the ropes, but finds it difficult with the 325 lb. Mannon on his back.]
LW: Who do you see winning in the main events, Eric? EC: Well, I'm going to have to go with Tripp Shade and Adam Rogers. Tripp
Shade because he's just so well rounded, there's almost nothing he can't do. And Adam Rogers because...well, come on, it's Adam Rogers! He's the dominant force here in the NeWF!
LW: Well, I'm going to say Joe Williams and, as much as I hate to, Adam
Rogers. Joe Williams because his attitude is just so, so bad, and Rogers because...well, I know what he's capable of, and that's a LOT!
[Mannon cranks back on War Child's neck. War Child gets to his knees, then lunges forward, landing himself in the ropes.]
EC: OK, now we can go back to calling the match. Elliot is asking Mannon to break the hold and Mannon is gladly obliging.
LW: Wussy. [Mannon hits the opposite ropes and then drops himself across the back of
War Child, crushing his neck against the middle rope.]
LW: Someone check that man's larynx! [Mannon picks up War Child with no regard towards the poor man's larynx and belly to belly suplexes him to the mat, rolling into a pin.]
Elliot: ONE! TWO! THR-kick out! EC: Just barely a kick out from the Child of War. LW: I'm thirsty. EC: Go get something to drink, I'll cover you. (Larry takes off the headset and heads for the concession stand. Eric watches him until he's out of sight. Barely able to contain his laughter,
Eric knocks the monitors off the table, then pushes it over. He kicks his chair over and lies down on the ground, as if he's been knocked there.)
[Mannon props War Child up in the corner and begins beating him. He throws a roundhouse to the ribs, a rising elbow to the chin, and a spinning hook kick that sends War Child over the top and to the floor.]
(Larry comes back sipping on a large Coke. He sees Eric and drops the drink, dashing over.)
LW: Oh no! I leave for one minute and everything goes to Hell! Why?! Why did this have to happen?! WHY-HY-HY-HYYYYYYY?!
(Eric lets out a giggle, but conceals his face.) [War Child slowly gets to his feet amidst the ten count from the referee. He grabs Eric's chair and tosses it into the ring. He follows in with it soon after. The referee goes over to remove it, with Mannon behind him, facing War Child. Child dashes at him, but Mannon back body drops him...right on top of the referee.]
LW: Oooh, he's not getting up from that just like Eric isn't getting up from whatever put him there.
[Mannon checks on the referee while War Child retrieves the chair. He swings it at Mannon, who catches it and boots War Child in the gut, taking the chair. He sets it up and clubs War Child with forearms until he sits down in it. Mannon gets behind the chair and spins around, connecting with a kick to the side of War Child's head that knocks him from the chair and nearly from consciousness.]
LW: SWEET JESUS! Monster kick from Mannon! But there's no referee to count a pin! Better get this table back up here.
(Larry sets the table back up and puts the monitors back up on it. Eric starts to sit up, rubbing his face, mostly to conceal a smile. He starts to rise.)
LW: Eric! You're alive! EC: (through his hands) Yeah...I'm...all...right...now...HA HA HA HA HA HA! LW: What? EC: Gotcha! (Eric points at Larry and laughs.) EC: I got ya! LW: It was....a joke?! YOU BASTARD! (Larry pushes him, causing him to trip over some wires and hit his head on the guardrail.) LW: Jerk. [As Mannon attempts to wake the referee, War Child shakes off the stars he sees floating around his head and picks up the chair. He taps Mannon on the shoulder and rears back, and when Mannon turns, with all the force in his body he clashes the chair into Mannon's skull, putting him to the mat, motionless. Child throws the chair away and drags the nearly revived referee over.]
LW: War Child has the cover! The referee is waking up! Referee Elliot: ONE! . . . . . TWO! . . . . . THREE!!!!!!!!! LW: Long, slow count, but this one is OVER! EC: (awakening) It's...over? LW: Quiet you! [DING DING DING] EC: War Child has advanced! LW: I knew that he would take out this loser! I told you so! EC: Fans War Child has advanced to face Brian Collins in the Second Round! That match happens in two weeks and man is this tournament heating up! LW: It sure is, even with idiots like you trying to say that losers have a chance in it. (chuckles)
EC: Fans our next match is a Fatal Four way for the Tag Team Titles, so you will wanna stay tuned for that! We will be right back with more Sunday
Night Mayhem! Stick around!
[As commercials begin to play, this one runs first.] [The screen is black and we hear a very low booming voice come over the black screen.] VOICE OVER: There are millions of ways to find out more about the NeWF and it's superstars. But here is the newest and best way...
[The screen stays black as we hear an internet dialup connecting and then the screen goes to static for a second. Finally when the static leaves, we see the main page of the NeWF Web site.]
VOICE OVER: The internet. NeWF is now on the internet with all sorts of information about your favorite wrestling superstars. There is the current weekly rankings and the ever-popular Title Histories page.
[The screen shows a mouse pointer being moved over to the side menu and clicking on the link to show the won-loss records.]
VOICE OVER: We even have the overall records of every active NeWF superstar! See how your favorite wrestler is doing inside the ring.
[The mouse pointer goes over to the side menu and clicks on the roster link.] VOICE OVER: You can even see the vital statistics on all of your favorite
NeWF superstars. Where did they come from and why did they begin wrestling? It is all inside the roster section waiting to be explored.
[The screen switches to show the mouse clicking on the rumors page link.] VOICE OVER: Go to the rumor section to find out some information that even the Board of Directors may not want the public to see. It is all here right now, and you have to do is point and click.
[The screen shows the mouse going back to the home page and the logo from the screen literally jumps from the computer screen to fill the entire screen.]
VOICE OVER: Yes NeWF has gone cyber with their new web site. So get you hook up here! [The voice trails off and we see the logo stay on the screen as the following URL come just under the logo]
http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Pressbox/3678/ [Fade to black.]
