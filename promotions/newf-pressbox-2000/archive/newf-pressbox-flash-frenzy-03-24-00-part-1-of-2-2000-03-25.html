Flash Frenzy (3/24/00) (Part 1 of 2)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1- "Tiger" Jay Moore 2- El Puerco 3- "Excessive Force" Erioc Vanguard 4- Snow Storm 5- "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams ============================================================== "Tiger" Jay Moore ============================================================== [Scene opens in a gym. Nothing special about this particular gym; the was are plain, you got all your average gym equipment there. A few chairs in the side of the room that is where Jay Moore is seated. You could probably say that is one of the most stereotypical and boring gym’s we’re ever gonna come across in an NeWF flash. You don’t like the uninteresting surroundings? Get over it. What, did you want him skipping through a flower field or something?]
Jay: One week into the NeWF and I’m in the title hunt already. Pretty sweet deal, if ya ask me- I didn’t ask for no title chance, but I sure as hell ain’t gonna turn one down. Nicklaus Kain, you have been assigned the task of taking me on; and as intimidating a figure as you seem to be, unfortunately for you, you will be the first to go down at the hands of the
Tiger.
[For those who don’t know, Jay is a 6’4”, 271 lb. African-American. He is dressed in his usual gym attire of a gray singlet-top and light-grayish colored cargo shorts. Leaning forward with his elbows on his knees, Jay wipes the sweat off his short, combed down bangs and his forehead with the small white towel, the continuing his speech.]
Jay: Quite a spooky little promo you put together there, Nick. I’ve got to ask you though, did those seven words have any significant meaning to you, or were they just randomly chosen from your “Word for the Day” Calendar?
[Jay pauses, smirking.] Jay: But that is of no relevance. What is of great relevance is that is that you and me; “Tiger” Jay Moore and Nicklaus Kain; the good and the bad- the Tiger and the strange evil dude who obviously didn’t get enough hugs from his parents as a child- we will be going head to head, Nicklaus Kain, but to me this is about more than just a title shot. This is about proving to the higher-ups that I have what it takes to be the top competitor in this federation. This is about proving to all the fans out there that I have what it takes to become the number one drawcard. This is about proving to all the wrestlers, including you Kain, that I am going to be the man to beat around here in the coming months. And, most importantly, Kain- this is about proving to myself that I have what it takes to achieve whatever I set my sights on. And since I like taking one thing at a time, I can tell you now that this week I have set my sights on nothing but beating you around the ring and then pinning you down for the 1-2-3, the moving on to face the winner of Sean Cage and TBC. It’s as simple as that.
[With another quick of his forehead with the towel, Jay flings his bag over his shoulder and makes his way to the opposite side of the gym, speaking while he walks.]
Jay: To tell you the truth, Kain, I really don’t know what to expect from you. The way you live, the way you act- it all means nothing to me. Once we step into that wrestling ring, we enter a whole new zone. It doesn’t matter what kind of freaks you live with; it doesn’t matter how you look, or how you dress. It doesn’t matter how many kittens you’ve munched on in your life. All that matters is that I feel I have got a lot to prove to a lot of people, and you seem to pre-occupied with feeding your pet cannibal and yelling pointless threats in my direction about which hospital bed I will be sleeping on when the night is over. All this means nothing; and I’m sire that right now all that I say means nothing to you, but believe this: when you’re back hits the ground and you feel that you can’t move your arms, as you stare up through dreary eyes at the arena lights and hear that all to familiar sound of the referee’s hand slapping the canvas, everything that I tell you now will have a great deal of meaning to it- I’ll definitely be making sure of that.
[Somewhere through that last speech, Jay stopped at the other side of the gym next to the showers, placed his bag down and took a seat against the wall.]
Jay: So, Kain- no empty and pointless threats from me. No idiotic statements of how your ass is going down. All I have to say to you has already been said. I really do look forward to our clash, Kain. Just don’t stupidly underestimate me, because if you do....
[Jay rises up from the chair and puts his hand on the door to the showers.] Jay: .... then rest assured.... [The door opens and we can hear the faint sound of water hitting the ground in the background.]
Jay: .... you will be.... [As he enters the door, he turns around and faces the camera, nodding his head with each of his last words.]
Jay: RIPPED.... TO.... SHREDS!!!!! [Jay’s head disappears behind the door and as the scene fades out we hear the sound of it slamming.]
============================================================== El Puerco ============================================================== [Fade in. Dateline: Sunday afternoon, March 26th. The scene opens in a bus terminal somewhere in Indianapolis, Indiana. People can be seen walking to and from the various gates, boarding and arriving from the many buses outside. Even more people can be seen seated on benches in the middle of the terminal. Some are waiting to board departing buses while others are waiting for their loved ones to arrive on incoming lines. We hear a muffled voice-over come over the terminal PA system...]
V/O: "The 3:45pm from Los Angeles, California... now arriving... Gate 12..." [The doors to Gate 12 swing open. Almost immediately, a huge crowd of passengers comes walking into the terminal. One particular passenger strays from the crowd and the camera follows him. He walks off to the side, dropping his bags to the floor. He slumps onto one of the benches and sighs. It's none other than Mexican wrestling sensation, El Puerco.
Dressed in his infamous green and yellow mask, a long brown trenchcoat, and a pair of worn and torn Nike sneakers, he addresses the camera.]
EP: "It's been a long time, amigos. Almost a year. It's been almost one whole year since I had my last match here in the States. After the TWL closed its doors, I couldn't find any work here. I was told that I was too ethnic... or too small... and that I would never find any popularity here."
[Puerco nods...] EP: "So, I left this country with my tail between my legs, I guess. The wrestling industry here didn't want me. I couldn't stay here and not work.
I didn't have any other skills to my name except for my wrestling skills. So, I went back to Mexico and I wrestled for anyone who'd pay me." [He laughs nervously...] EP: "I wrestled in big arenas every now and then but, for the most part, my work was relegated to shows in night clubs and warehouses. I didn't make a ton of money... but I made enough money to live and support my family. But it still didn't feel right. It didn't feel as MAGICAL as it did when I was here in the States."
[Puerco shakes his head...] EP: "What I was doing was a far cry from my glory days as the TWL Cruiserweight Champion. Most of the time, there weren't even any championships involved. The promoters would just throw me in the ring with some guy and say, 'Wrestle!'. Most of the matches had no meaning. It started to bother me after awhile."
[He nods...] EP: "I started to wonder why I even bothered anymore. There was no recognition or respect anymore. I was being paid just to go through the motions for no good reason. I had to deal with money hungry promoters, too. 'The Mighty Pig' became mightily disenchanted with this business. In fact, I was about to call it quits... that is... until I received a phone call from the powers that be here in the NeWF. As it turns out, SOMEONE out there was still interested in seeing the most lucha libre-a-licious superstar in the history of this sport strut his stuff inside the ring."
[Puerco opens his trenchcoat, revealing a NeWF T-shirt. He laughs...] EP: "They gave me an offer that I couldn't refuse. They said that could be in their new Light Heavyweight division. I couldn't wait to be a part of the action. I met with some of their officials the next day and signed with the company. So, here I am... in Indianapolis... to see some NeWF action... and then, on April 1st, at 'X-Tremely Dangerous', yours truly makes his official debut."
[He shrugs...] EP: "I'm scheduled to face some dude called 'Bad Attitude'. I have no idea who this guy is nor do I care. I'm just happy to be a part of an actual organization again. IIt's restored my faith in the business. I'm
PROUD to be a wrestler once again... after almost turning my back on the business."
[Puerco points at the camera...] EP: "Joe Williams better prepare for the fight of his life. He may have a
'Bad Attitude'... but is he as lucha libre-a-licious as I? I don't think so. This unfortunate young man is going to be stepping into the ring with the fastest... the smartest... and the most talented hombre to ever lace up a pair of boots."
[He looks into the camera, pointing to himself...] EP: "Joe Williams... just WHAT are you gonna do when 'The Mighty Pig' takes you to school? Hmm? Will you be able to handle the heat? Or will you fall at the hands of my mad skills? I'm betting on number two. 'The
Mighty Pig' WILL emerge from that match victoriously... and that's my final answer."
[Puerco snickers for a second. Then, he goes back to the serious tone of voice he was exhibiting earlier...]
EP: "Then, I have a date with destiny on April 3rd. That's where I'm making my 'Monday Night Mayhem' debut. The powers that be here in the NeWF have once again showed me some respect by placing me in a Six Way Dance where the winner becomes the first ever NeWF Light Heavyweight Champion.
All I can say is, 'Bring it on!'. This match doesn't intimidate me one bit." [He rubs his hands together, like he's plotting something...] EP: "I don't care if there's TEN men in that ring with me. You're looking at one hungry competitor. Do you know how long it's been since I got a chance like this? Do you know how much I miss being called a 'champion'?
I'm going to be going into that match at full throttle... like a tornado! No one will be safe!" [Puerco points to himself again...] EP: "I'll have determination and adrenaline coming out of my ears. God help the poor hombres who get in my way. Their asses will be going down faster than Monica Lewinsky at one of El Presidente's private parties."
[He stands up, still addressing the camera...] EP: "You're looking at a rejuvenated man... a man with a goal... a man who never says die. You're looking at your first, last, and only Light
Heavyweight Champion. It's only a matter of time." [Suddenly, we hear a voice coming from off-camera...] Voice: "Excuse me, sir..." [The camera zooms away to get a wide shot as Puerco turns to see who's talking to him. We see that it's a little blonde haired girl. She offers a pen and piece of paper to Puerco...]
Little Girl: "You're El Puerco. I used to love you in the TWL! Can I please have your autograph, sir?"
[We see a smile peaking out from Puerco's mask...] EP: "Sure you can, little lady. What's your name?" Girl: "Gina." [Puerco takes the pen and paper and signs it for the girl. He hands the pen and paper back to the girl, smiling...]
EP: "There you go, Gina." Girl: "Thank you, sir." [Puerco turns to walk away. Before he can go, the little girl stops him...] Girl: "When are you going to wrestle again?" [Puerco looks down at the little girl. We can see another smile peaking out from underneath the mask...]
EP: "Soon, very soon." Girl: "Great. We missed you." [Puerco picks up his backs and walks off-camera. The little girl raises her arm in the air and shouting, as she looks on...]
"Respect 'The Pig'! VIVA EL PUERCO!" [Fade to festive green...] ============================================================== "Excessive Force" Erioc Vanguard ============================================================== [The camera fades into an empty and desolate hallway somewhere in an old abandoned prison in Southern California. The room is dark with the exception of a single light bulb that dangles from the ceiling of the hallway, casting an eerie light over the corridor. From the distant end of the hall a shadow appears, growing larger and larger as the individual behind it moves closer and closer.]
Voice: “These are the halls where I taught many an individual what it was like to me a man, and suffer for your wrong doings…”
[The shadow in the hallway soon disappears as a large muscular man draped in leather comes around the corner, a thick black nightstick pounding against the palm of his gloved hand. His eyes are hidden behind bold black shades, and his face appears as if he has been through years of torture. He walks towards the camera methodically, watching his footprints appear in the piles of dust that cover the floor.]
Voice: “It was because of these punishments and these acts of authority that I earned my name – Excessive Force. People said that I had a tendency to be a little too aggressive, a little to overzealous when I would bring my nightstick crashing down against the skull of a man who claimed he was framed…Who are they to say that I was.”
[He pauses for a moment as he turns and looks out the steel barred windows of the hallway over the littered prison yard, overgrown with weeds and shrubs. A smirk appears on the corner of his mouth as he runs his hand across his unkempt chin.]
Eric Vanguard: “It was a lesson in responsibility, a lesson on how to admit your wrong doings. I didn’t walk around randomly assaulting people, like some would lead you to believe; instead I focused on those that weren’t man enough or even woman enough to take credit for their crimes.
(Sarcastically) I’m innocent! I’m innocent I tell you…” [Vanguard motions as if he were bringing a nightstick down across the back of somebody…] Eric Vanguard: “* CRACK * Innocent my ass! The United States Judicial
System does NO wrong! They let the innocent run free and put the guilty behind bars with people like me. The judge and jury give you what you deserve, and then * I * carry out the execution. Gordon “Icepick” McNeely took credit for slaying those two whores – those two hookers – because they flashed their stuff around town like it was going out of style. He was a man that was willing to take responsibility, and so we developed a bond. He scratched my back and I scratched his – that’s the way it works in prison.”
[The former prison guard turns and continues down the hallway, dragging his nightstick against the steel bars of the cells making an ear breaking clanking sound.]
Eric Vanguard: “I lit those fires! I’m burned that Cathedral down to a pile of ashes! Those were the words spoken quite proudly by Mr. Aaron “Torch”
Parsons when I first met him. He stood up showed those scars, those battle wounds if you will, as if they meant the world to him – and I think they did. (Smirking) Torch was a born leader, helping keep other prisoners in line when I would dish out my brand of justice. I can safely say that he will make an outstanding citizen when he is realized in 10 to 12.”
[Suddenly the look on the face of Vanguard turns bitter as he grips the nightstick forcefully and brings it crashing down on an old dilapidated bench that lies in the hallway.]
Eric Vanguard: “I didn’t kill him in cold blood, I swear! It was a total accident! I should never even have been brought to trail! Those are words that turn my stomach, and those were the words of Kendall “Tantrum”
Masterson. Every single time I would get in his face and ask him to save himself a beating by confessing he would mutter those words. What did he receive? A kick to the ribs, a face full of my saliva, or a broken nose – it didn’t matter to me, and it obviously didn’t matter to him either, because to this day he still spews forth those lies! He could have it easy, but he chooses not to.”
[He finally stops and leans back against the bars of one of the cells…] Eric Vanguard: “Do you see what I am getting at here? When you spend your lives working behind these walls – these bars – you meet all kinds of personalities. Some you like, some you don’t like, some you despise, but you learn to deal with each and every one of them. That is what brings me to you Mr. Stone. I heard the words of your “psychiatrist” who went on record claiming you were schizophrenic and a man of a thousand personalities. Am I supposed to be intimidated? Just last night I was asked by Craig “Viper” Czankner how to deal with so many personalities, and the answer was simple…You may have a million and one different people living in that head of yours, but you only have one body. Will it be a sadistic horror movie villain coming at me? (Holding up his nightstick) This will neutralize him! Will it be a funny wacky clown? THIS will neutralize him!
Will it be an arrogant movie star with silver lined dreams? It doesn’t matter, because this nightstick will neutralize him.”
[Vanguard pauses for a moment letting out an almost sadistic little laugh as he strokes the nightstick in a rather suggestive fashion.]
Eric Vanguard: “Matt – Mr. Stone – On April 1st in Martinsville, Indiana I will take a great deal of pride in showing NeWF and the entire world what I do to this miscreants of society. Next Saturday Night it will be my pleasure to step into the ring with you and knock a few of those misguided personalities straight. Will I be a little too aggressive? Some would say that. But who are they to judge? I am the only one who decides what is over the edge – and soon Matt Stone you will learn what pushing the limits is all about. It’s only a matter of time before you see first hand WHY they call me “EXCESSIVE FORCE!!!”
[As the final words roll off of his tongue, Vanguard turns around with the nightstick and takes a swing at the camera smashing the lens into a thousand pieces.]
[Fade To Black] ============================================================== Snow Storm ============================================================== [The camera shows a small crowd surrounding something in front of a shopping mall. This circle of fans is outside the food Court. Queues waiting for there lunch outside McDonalds, Pizza hut etc. The camera pushes through the circle to show Snow Storm signing autographs, on his new merchandise. He is sitting by a table signing shirts, caps, magazines, and posters. He is going at quite a frantic rate because there is a huge line gathering for him. He shakes his hand, and seems as if he is suffering from writers cramp. So he Stops stands up.]
SS: How about I let you ask me a few questions before my hand falls off. [The gathering give a small chuckle] SS: And then after that I will get back to signing those autographs. [the gathering give a small round of applause] SS: Well who is first how bout you little man? [pointing at a small child, holding up his hand] Child: Can I have one of your hats please? SS: Well that's a good question little boy, of course you can. Hey Mick, grab the kid a hat will ya.
[Talking to a man holding a folder by the right of the desk. The man bends down picks up a hat and throws it to Snow Storm who catches the hat and hands it to the little kid.]
SS: There you are. This time how about a serious question. [A young man with an assertive voice steps forward] Young man: Now you have heard a little from each wrestler of the NeWF what do you have to say?
SS: Well Not much at all. I have taken in what they all have said, and absorbed it all. I But I was wondering about a couple of people who did not come and tell us all what they're all about. You see I like to have a first impression on all the wrestlers here. And for there sake I hope its not them catching a beating and then looking sorry for them selves. But everyone had there word, and I respect them all. But saying its one thing doing it is another thing. Cause if you cant Walk the Walk, don't Talk the
Talk. That actually reminds me of a certain someone in the NeWF. I have a little story about them, but there is a time and a place for that kind of thing, and now is not the time. Next Question please.
[A young lady who looks to be in here mid twenties. Wearing nothing more than short denim skirt, and a green bikini top. Puts here hand up.]
Lady: Well what do you think of your opponent in your next match? SS: Well lady first things first you are not wearing much at all, so take a shirt. [The man with the folder bends down and picks up a shirt before Snow Storm has to say anything, and hands it to Snow Storm. Snow Storm Holds the shirt up showing the phrase on the back. The Shirt reads "Whats Doin with Snow
Storm". The ladies face lights up like a Christmas tree, and she snatches the shirt from Snow Storm and straight away puts it on.]
SS: But my match. Well I am not worried at all, actually I am super confident in that I will wipe the floor with him. I am actually going to keep count how many times I pick him up from the floor, because I have not seen this guy wrestle, and I have never seen this guy training, Actually I do not even know his name. But that does not matter to me because I have trained harder than ever before and I know that he will have to physically kill me, for me to be pinned to that mat with a 1……2…….3. As I said in the
Press conference for anyone to beat me they will have to put me through hell and back. And I respect this guy for joining the NeWF. He must have some talent otherwise Kash, or Hopper would not have signed him, but some talent is not good enough beat Snow Storm. I think you get the picture no use riding the point.
[An older man in his forties wearing a Snow Storm Cap asks] Older man; What do you think about the TV Title. Do you think you have any chance of being competitive for it. Can you actually be competitive with the Likes of "Tiger Jay Moore' and " Super Star Sean Cage".
SS: Well I am glad you actually asked me that question because I would like to send out a message to the four men competing in the two title shot matches. Here it goes: Feel lucky that you are fighting because there are up to another three wrestlers I feel could have been in your spot right now. It wouldn't have surprised me that Mr Kash or Mr Hopper Pulled them out of a that because you are no way better than me or any other wrestler here in the NeWF. So make good on your chance because it may be your last one for a very Long time. Because when I get my shot at you, you will be getting off the mat a losing man. No matter how hard you try or how many times I get up. I will try harder, and I will get up more times. Because at the moment that TV title is the most prestigious title at the moment.
And the word prestigious in Snow Storm language that means ME Snow Storm. So make good of your chance, and win because it's the last chance you have a chance at winning that Title in a very very long time. And to answer your question. Yes I can be very competitive with the likes of "Tiger Jay
Moore", and so on. Of course these guys have an extreme talent for wrestling, and I respect them greatly. And in no way do I underestimate there abilities. But I do not underestimate my abilities either and I think
I am just as good as these guys, and maybe even better. And If I get a chance at them I will put in a hundred and fifty percent, and pin them to the mat 1…….2……3 And walk away a winner.
Young Lady again: Well What do you think you have to do to get a shot at them? SS: Well I think I just have to wrestle to the best of my abilities and show Kash and Hopper what I am made of, and show them I am a winner. And that if I hold the TV title the federation as a whole will look, feel and perform much better. Because I feel as if I hold the title everyone will have to lift there act to come any where near me, and that means better matches, which in the long run bigger ticket sales and bigger profit margins. And that makes everyone happy.
Older man: If you held the belt would this eclipse the feeling you had when you held gold with Snowed Under back in the ACCW.
SS: Well holding the Tag Team belts back in the ACCW. IS probably the best feeling I have had in the wrestling world to date. But I owed that feeling to Down Under. And if I hold this TV belt it would be because I worked hard, and because I trained hard, and I performed not anyone else but me.
And if I Lose I have only got myself to blame. But lets not jump the gun I have not even wrestled here yet. We cant go around making assumptions about me even getting a shot at that TV Title yet. I still have to prove myself, and to do that I have to win. And Win I will do, and Win I will do soon.
[Snow Storm shakes his hand] SS: AHH the hands feeling better now, lets get back to signing those merchandise. Now one nice Straight single line now.
[As the crowd push and shove to get to the front of the line the camera fades to black.] ============================================================== "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams ============================================================== [The sound of water pounding against the shower floor is heard as David
Lowry, an NeWF interviewer enters the dressing room. He calls out..]
Lowry: Williams?!? Where are you?!? [Lowry hears the shower and the look on his face is one of anger and disappointment] Lowry: Dang kid thinks he runs the whole show just 'cause his daddy has a couple of bucks. When a guy makes an appointment with an interviewer he should be ready to go.
[Lowry turns to head out in disgust when the faucet is suddenly shut off] Williams: <from the shower> Lowry...your fat, crippled ass better not be going anywhere... [Lowry quickly turns around at the insult and shouts back] Lowry: What did you say?!? [The shower door slams open] Williams: I think you heard... [Williams steps out...his naked body being blotted out by the censors] Lowry: JOE!!!! [Williams shrugs] Williams: What...are you going to waste my time, Lowry? Lowry: Put some clothes on, will you kid?!? [Williams grabs a hand towel and dabs his wet face. He smirks and responds..] Williams: Why...feeling a little envious? Lowry: You know I don't, but this is broadcast on TV... Williams: <impatiently> Point being.... Lowry: POINT BEING...we have rules here in the NeWF as well as rules set by the FCC... Williams: And I care? <laughs> Rules don't apply to me...never have..never will. Rules are set by little dorks who never want to have fun...like yourself. And I'm not about to waste my time trying to follow them. But if it makes you feel better.
[Williams turns his head to shout offscreen] Williams: TONI!!!! [After a few seconds, Williams' tall beautiful blonde valet responds to his beckon. Wearing a nice white dress...she brings a new towel to her man.
Williams smiles at her, grabs the towel and wraps it around his waist before he turns back to Lowry]
Williams: OK? Lowry: <a little perturbed> Whatever. Do you want an interview or not? Williams: If that's what you call your subpar work...sure. Let me get a few things off my mind. Like how I feel about all the pieces of crap that pollute this organization! Or maybe about how me...the young...best looking...most talented wrestler in the WORLD is stuck performing for poor redneck fans and being interview by has-beens that are NOTHING to me!
Lowry: <rising to anger> Listen up, punk.... Williams: Punk?!? I guess that fits. I don't care about you..or your boss, or the wrestlers or the fans. I care about THE MAN...and how he's going to go out there and show a couple of losers how it's done. Meanwhile, all the rednecks will be getting sore throats yelling at me..while they're all really wanting to be like me. They want to be the guy who gets the girl or rides the fast cars. They want to be the guy that can spend one year of their salary in a day. But they aren't...so they boo me.
Whatever...they can have their piece of crap 'heroes'..but I'm the guy who they really want to be.
Lowry: Sounds to me like you're a little bit cocky... Williams: <interrupting> DAMN STRAIGHT!!! Of course I'm cocky...I'm conceited..I'm arrogant...but I'm also THAT good. Yes...you can say I'm arrogant, but I'm the best. And I wil kick ANYONE's butt to prove it.
[Steve Jackson, dressed in an Armani suit, bursts into the room with a piece of paper in his hand]
Jackson: <waving the paper> Well, champ...I got your matches. You're wrestling El Puerco on X-Tremely Dangerous...
Williams: El Who? They're putting me against some rinky-dink flyweight? Lowry: Now El Puerco is a dangerous oppon... Williams: Who asked you, you tub of lard?!? If you don't think I can handle a cruiser...you're wrong! I'm a two-time light heavyweight champion. I'll take this El Nobody and knock him back where he came from!
Lowry: I've had about enough... Williams: You've had enough? Here I am wasting my time with a nobody like you and you say that YOU'VE had enough?!? Give me a <bleep> 'in break!
You should be grateful you're getting this close to me! If I wanted to...I'd snap my fingers and I'd make sure you NEVER came close to a career again....
Lowry: Listen up...you may be young...you say you're good...but you haven't proved anything to me...
Williams: But I will...and as you saw earlier I got nothing to hide...it's only a matter of time before this federation gets it's new franchise...and that is the young, handsome, dashing MAN that is "Bad Attitude". Do me a favor, Lowry?
Lowry: <surprised> What?!? Williams: GET THE HELL OUT!!! [The young champion walks away from the announcer as Lowry shakes his head in shock]
