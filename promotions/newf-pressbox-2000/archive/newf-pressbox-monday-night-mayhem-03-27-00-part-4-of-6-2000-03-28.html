Monday Night Mayhem (March 27, 2000) (Part 4 of 6)

28 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We return from commercials to our announcers live at ringside.] BF: It's time for another show stealer here on mayhem! Welcome back fans! It is time for our Light Heavyweight Triple Threat Match! LW: This one is a toss up for me. Damon Knight is as good as they come, while Tyrone Parker is possibly the best Light wrestler in UEW and now he is here to show us what he can do. He is just the kind of cocky that can take the Light division by storm!
EC: You forgot one Larry. LW: Who? EC: It is a triple threat, remember? Derek Diamond is also in this match and he has loads of talent and a ton of crowd support. He just might
up-end your little ideas of grandeur. BF: Good point Eric, this match is very even and even more full of action that our last couple of matches mainly because there are three men in this match and not just two.
EC: And anytime you add anything extra, you always get a major boom to anything you try to do!
BF: Well let's see what we get when we head to the ring! [The screen cuts to the ring where Steven Stewert is standing in the center holding the microphone and a index card.]
SS: Ladies and Gentlemen the next match is a one fall, no time limit,
Light Heavyweight Triple Threat Match!
[Crowd pop.] SS: Introducing first, he is already considered a top contender for the
Light Heavyweight Title. He calls himself "Mr. UEW" because he feels he has dominated any aspects of that league and says he will do it here as well! Ladies and Gentlemen I give you
"M R. U E W" T Y R O N E P A R K E R S ! ! ! [The deep, powerful chords of Iced Earth's "Stand Alone" begin to blast into the crowded NeWF arena. The fans rise in anticipation, waiting to see who will be walking out, when all of a sudden, the lights go black. The music stops. The fans start to scream out, when a white strobe light starts to flash around the arena. Then the logo on the jumbotron comes out in gold letters.]
M R. U E W [Both words mix together, until it comes out, "MR. UEW". A loud explosion follows, and then, "Stand Alone" cranks back up. Out from the back comes
Tyrone Parker. The fans boo at this. Debris flies towards Parker, who laughs. Parker walks to the ring, waving to the fans. Tyrone Parker is wearing khaki pants, and a pair of Nike shoes. The gets near the ring apron, and then slides under the bottom rope. He does a stretch in the corner, awaiting the bell.]
BF: Tyrone Parker is making his first splash in the NeWF Wave Pool! He looks pretty ready to take control!
EC: He is a solid wrestler, but I do not see him winning here. I still say Derek Diamond is the man for this match.
LW: Diamond sucks. Period. Parker is my pick here because of his attitude and the fact he comes form a top notch league and is ready to take control! You can't buy talent like that!
BF: He is well put together and seems like he is ready for anything. The crowd sure hates him already, which I suppose so good for him since he hates the people so much anyway
LW: Damn right. EC: Well here comes my pick now! [Crowd pop.] SS: And his first opponent! He might be the best kept secret in the NeWF
Light Heavyweight Division. Here is
D E R E K D I A M O N D ! ! ! [The lights dim and a silver spot shines down on the top of the ramp. "Down" by 311 begins to play over the arena speakers. Slowly the music rises to a crescendo and a pulse flashes across the jumbotron before exploding into the word 'Diamond' in gothic script. The beat of the song pulses through the word before and explosion raises the arena lights.
Standing at the top of the aisle is Derek Diamond. Dressed in a pair of black wrestling trunks with thin (1/2 inch) blue waves around the bottom.
Across the backside in matching blue is 'Diamond' (the final 'o' has been replaced by an image of the Hope Diamond) in script, blue square kneepads with a black outline and he wears a pair of black boots with matching blue rectangles that connect to his kneepads. Both wrists are taped with white athletic tape. He raises his fists to a solid crowd response and confidently strides to ringside. He climbs onto the ring apron and into the ring, atop the second turnbuckle before pumping his fists for the crowd.]
EC: Tell me this guy doesn't look like he can take this match! Derek is ready and has all the tools to be successful.
LW: Parker is better than him AND this other guy! EC: Well he looks ready to me, now we just gotta get the other guy out here. BF: From your mouth to God's ear....here comes the final participant! [Crowd pop.] SS: And finally, standing six feet and one inch tall, and weighing in at a lean two-hundred, twenty-nine pounds, and hailing from Washington D.C. He claims to be the finest physical specimen ever to grace God's green Earth.
Ladies and gentlemen, I give you "D E V A S T A T I N G" D A M O N K N I G H T ! ! ! [The arena all of a sudden goes dark. Lights go out and everyone becomes confused.] *a faint buzzing noise that gradually gets louder* [There are some small objects falling from the arena ceiling (thick confetti) Strobe lights being going all over and around the arena, as well.]
*bees buzzing* [There is a lot of confetti and as the bee buzzing noise gets real loud, most of the people think that they are being attacked by bees]
*man screaming in torture* [Mr. Sandman starts to play over the PA as the buzzing fades out and rocks the arena as the curtains are lifted to the side and Damon Knight enters, illuminated for brief seconds at a time by the strobe light.]
# RZA (singing by Blue Raspberry) #This is... [Damon Knight please don't make me scream] Serious, the craziest... d-da, [Damon Knight please don't make me scream] day-da Danger, dangerous... style#
[Lights slowly come on as Damon Knight makes his first move acknowledging the crowds huge heel pop for this young superstar.]
[After the text is shown over the jumbotron, smokes begins to rise from under the steel grating ramp. Shown on the Jumbotron is Damon Knight nailing the Total Devastation on "Delicious" Dave Davidson to win the TWL
World Television Title.]
[The strobe lights continue to flicker, slowing down, then speeding up, the blue and white lights continue to swirl around the arena, slowing down, then speeding up, the smoke continues to rise when out of nowhere, over the music and reaction from the crowd the following is printed on the
jumbotron:] _____ _ _ _ _ _ _ |_ _| |__ ___ | | | | ___ __ _ __| | (_)_ __ ___ _ __ | | | '_ \ / _ \ | |_| |/ _ \/ _` |/ _` | | | '_ \ / _ \ '__| _ _ _| | | | | | __/ | _ | __/ (_| | (_| | | | | | | __/ | (_|_|_)_| |_| |_|\___| |_| |_|\___|\__,_|\__,_|_|_|_| |_|\___|_| [Damon Knight please don't make me scream] I don't know what's going on if you can take us there... [Following that, the following appears on the jumbotron: Damon Knight winning a three way scaffold match to be named the number one contender to the TWL Intercontinental Title.]
[Damon Knight is African American. Damon Knight has braided hair down to his shoulder's which is worn in a corn-roll style. He has deep dark brown eyes. His left ear is pierced which he wears a black pearl in. On Damon
Knight's left arm he has a tattoo of a burning cross, with barb wire going up and down it. Damon Knight has a tattoo of a black panther over his upper left chest muscle, were his heart is located. Damon Knight wears black and red wrestling boots, with a shine guard over it, and he wears knee pads as well. Knight wears black and red wrestling shorts to the ring, which are decorated in a fashion that matches his boots to perfection. Damon Knight walks to the ring with the spotlight on him.]
[Damon hops on the ring apron, then sling shoots himself into the ring. Damon sets on the top turnbuckle to await the ring bell, this draws a HUGE heel pop for Damon Knight, as he throws his arms up. Damon Knight then prepares for his match.]
[Damon Knight please don't make me scream] [Damon Knight please don't make me scream] ["Mister Sandman " fades away with only the sounds of a cheering crowd dominating the audio.]
BF: What a LONG entrance! LW: Hey it's not as long as that boxer prince guy. EC: Hamed? Not quite, but it is long and it has to intimidate and anger his opponents to have to sit and watch that. I know it would upset me.
LW: Well don't get your panties in a bunch kiddo. I am sure this match is about to get under way! (chuckles)
BF: Here comes the bell! [DING! DING!! DING!!!] EC: There's the bell! This match is underway! LW: Wow, how observant. BF: There three guys have the weight of the world on their shoulders. As the first Light Heavyweight match in the NeWF, these guys know they have to produce!
EC: I'm eager to see the high flying arsenals of these guys! LW: I'm eager to see a foot in Derek Diamond's nether region. Give it to him, Knight! BF: Let's not forget Tyrone Parker, although I'm sure that Diamond can more than fend for himself!
EC: Better pay attention guys, cause Knight just nailed Parker! BF: Nailed him indeed, Parker looks like he just got shot! And look at
Knight! He's bragging like he just won the match!
LW: Well come on, you put someone on the mat like that and you may as well ring the bell. It's all over!
EC: For Knight maybe. Here comes Diamond! [Derek Diamond sneaks up behind the celebrating Knight and taps him on the shoulder. Knight turns and charges...]
BF: Armdrag by Diamond! He's up, another armdrag! Diamond's near the ropes! EC: Knight's charging again! LW: SWEET JESUS! [Derek Diamond backdrops Damon Knight up and over the top rope, sending him against the guardrail. Knight curls up into a fetal position and grasps the side of his head.]
LW: He's dead! He's dead! I get his boots. BF: You're an outstanding human being, Larry. But seriously, this match could be over for Knight as soon as it's begun.
EC: But back in the ring, Parker is up and slugging it out with Diamond! [Parker and Diamond trade right hands. Parker. Diamond. Parker. Diamond. Parker...Diamond blocks. Diamond. Diamond. Derek Diamond whips him into the ropes and follows up with a powerslam!]
BF: Devastating powerslam and there's the cover! One! Two! Thr...shoulder up! Just barely though! LW: (nearly sobbing) Where's Kni-hi-hight?! [Knight is just starting to stagger to his feet, but still face-down in his own blood.] EC: Knight was busted wide by that shot against the guardrail. Now he's pulled himself up by the bars!
LW: Bleed on that loser in the white shirt! No, the OTHER loser in the white shirt! [In the ring, Diamond has slammed Parker to the mat. He hits the ropes and goes to follow up with an elbow drop, but Parker moves.]
BF: Ouch, that'll knock your elbow out of it's socket! EC: Both men are to their feet now...European uppercut by Parker! Diamond did a 180! LW: Look! Knight's back up! And not only that! He's on the top rope! GET HIM! BF: Diamond is standing right in front of Parker, but he's right near
Knight on the top rope. Knight with a cross body DDT attempt...
LW: SWEET JESUS! [Knight leaps with a cross body DDT attempt, but Diamond quickly hits the mat and Parker throws an upward-dropkick that nails Knight in the gut.
Knight rolls into the corner and balls back up, grasping his head and stomach.] LW: Dead! Dead! Dead! Gone! Gone from the world! Doesn't he have a stereo in his locker? I want that, too.
EC: Ever thought about becoming a humanitarian? LW: No. BF: Diamond is up off the mat now, grabbing Parker's feet with a double leg takedown, spun around into a reverse chinlock!
LW: WOW! EC: What the Hell?!?!? [The Lights in the arena go black as the camera focuses upon the video screen. The sound of golf balls being hit blare throughout the arena. "New
Skin" by Incubus then starts to play loudly rattling the speakers throughout the arena. The sound is deafening as video then appears on the screen. Flashes of golf courses around the world start appearing and people being hit with nine irons appear on the screen. One by one people fall when they're struck with a nine iron. Golf courses start appearing on the screen once again then the screen turns black. Golf balls start flying all around the screen, then appear as if they're flying right at the people in the audience. One golf ball flies up to the screen then stops. The word
Titleist is written on the ball. The screen goes black once again as the music dies out. The sound of golf balls being hit blare through the arena once again. Then in red and blue dripping letters the screen says "THE NINE
IS COMING" as the video screen is cut and the lights come back on. The wrestlers inside the ring never even stopped going at each other.]
BF: What in the world does that mean? "The Nine is Coming", I don't get it? EC: Me neither, but they never stopped inside the ring and these men are still going at it!
LW: A reverse chinlock! While you losers were watching the jumbotron, I was watching the friggin match! This is the most dangerous move in pro wrestling, save the bell clap!
EC: The bell clap? LW: Eric, when I was training to be a wrestler, my mentor, the Indian
Chief, Chief Bob... EC: Chief Bob? LW: Shut the hell up. Yes, Chief Bob. Chief Bob told me, "Kid, take a piledriver on the floor. Take a DDT on the stairs. Hell, take a Hotshot on the guardrail, but be careful when you take a bell clap! When your equilibrium is shot and you're stumbling around the ring, God only knows where you'll fall." I pray I never have to see one of these young kids take a bell clap, especially with their new-fangled "hardcore" style. One of these days, a table, a chair, a bad equilibrium, disaster!
EC: ... BF: So, looks like that reverse chinlock is finished and all 3 guys are up. LW: How conveniently entertaining. EC: Quite. [Parker approaches Knight and pushes him. When Knight comes back at him,
Parker pokes him in the eye. "Mr. UEW" executes a standing headscissors and motions to Diamond. Diamond comes over and stands behind Knight.
Parker lifts Knight up for a powerbomb, but Knight pokes a finger right into the eye of his attacker. When he's dropped, he executes a back kick on Diamond, then hits an Atomic Facebuster on the stunned Parker.]
BF: A cover! One! Two! Diamond pulls him off! LW: Barely. They should hand Knight the match for the close call. EC: I'm glad you're not running this place. LW: Damn right. You'd be on the street before you could say, "Jiggaty biggaty hooby doo." EC: Why would I say that? LW: Cause I'm the damn boss! [Diamond goes for a short-arm clothesline on Knight, but Damon ducks under and throws his arm across Derek's chest, hooking next to his neck. Knight raises Diamond up, pivots, and plants him on the mat.]
LW: Spinning Rock Bottom! EC: Spinning Soviet Suplex! BF: Spinning Sambo Suplex! (The three announcers argue amongst themselves.) [Meanwhile, Diamond is tosses through the ropes, but stops himself on the apron, leaning on the top strand and breathing hard, right in front of the announce table. Knight laughs at him and turns just in time to see an enraged Parker charging him full speed. Knight drops to the mat and launches him into Diamond with a monkey flip. Diamond flies off the apron and lands in the laps of Bob Frankhauser and Eric Chaney, sending them both over backwards in a huge wreckage of bodies. The ref leaves the ring and desperately attempts to wake Diamond and the announcers.]
LW: SWEET JESUS! Robert? Eric? Guys? ...I'm the only commentator left...I'm the only voice on the show...I'M THE RULER OF THE WORLD!
[Simultaneous to Larry's epiphany, Parker landed on his hands when he bounced off Diamond and the ropes, and sprung back with a handspring elbow, staggering Knight. Parker hurries to him and snaps him down with a snapping brainbuster!]
LW: Myself being the color man, I normally wouldn't say this, but Damon
Knight has had a weak neck ever since two consecutive brainbusters in a TWL scaffold match. And boy is he feeling it...hey, what am I saying? Bring the beer! Bring the hoes! Wrestling? Who needs it?!
(Larry leaves the table.) [Parker ascends the turnbuckles while Knight writhes on the mat. He flies off with a flying legdrop that finds it's mark across the neck of Damon
Knight. Parker covers. That would be a one! That would be a two! That would be a three! In fact, that'd probably be a six, but it's not, and ya know why? Cause the ref is still on the floor! Ha!.]
(Larry is escorted back by a slew of NeWF officials. He sits back down at the table and puts his headset on. He waits until the officials are gone, then takes a sip from a brown bag-covered bottle.)
LW: Ha HA! Freakin' screws. Ya can't hold back Larry Watters! I'm the ruler of the world!
["Mr. UEW" drags Knight to his feet and locks on a rear waistlock.] LW: Uh oh! Superstar Driver 2000 coming up! What a coincidence! I know a guy who uses the suffix -Driver 2000. Popular name I guess! This match will be over if Parker hits this. That'd be a shame, cause all kidding aside...these guys suck! Ha ha ha! You thought I was gonna go soft didn't ya?
(He takes a sip from the bottle.) [Parker goes to German suplex Knight, but Damon flips backwards onto his feet and sends Parker into the top buckle with a front dropkick between the shoulder blades. Knight rolls Parker up with a School Boy, just as the ref returns to the ring.]
LW: One! Two! NO! It's not over, we can't go on to bigger guys, punching and kicking and choking and...well, I guess this really isn't so bad after all! Knight's taking a breather in the corner, I don't know if that's such a good idea, cause Parker's up too!
[Parker and Knight meet in the middle and tie up. Knight whips Parker into the ropes. When Parker gets to the strands, Derek Diamond leaps up and pulls down the top rope, sending Parker to the floor with him.]
LW: Diamond's up! Diamond's up! Wait...I HATE Diamond! Diamond sucks! Wait...if Diamond's up... EC: (slowly and with malice) Hello, Larry. LW: (slowly and with malice) Hello, Eric. BF: We're back! Did you miss us? LW: Nope, just been here with my beer. EC: Beer? They don't sell beer here! LW: What? Of course they do! I mean... (Eric grabs his bag-covered bottle and turns it up, revealing a bottle of water, plus spilling it on the floor.)
LW: My Evian! [While Diamond and Parker slug it out, Knight leaps onto the apron, onto the first rope, second rope, third rope and then back with a top rope Asai
Moonsault.]
EC: Triple Jump Asai Moonsault? LW: Who knows? I don't watch ECW that much? EC: Bob? BF: Not a lot of access to Puroresu. EC: Oh...well...THE HUMANITY! BF: Hey pal, as long as it's not us. LW: Helluva spill, guys. BF: Quite. [The three men make it back into the ring fairly quickly, Knight up first, bringing Diamond up with him. Knight bends Diamond over and grabs him around the waist. Knight lifts him up so he is over Knight's shoulder.
Knight puts one arm on the chest of Diamond and flips him over so he lands face first on the mat.]
LW: SWEET!...wait, that wasn't THAT bad. BF: But it couldn't feel good, either. A powerbomb into a Tiger Bite. The cover! One! Two!
EC: Three! LW: No! Not three! Shoulder up! Dolt. EC: I'll deck ya! LW: Deck these! BF: Put those away! [Knight stands up and turns around...right into a Parker superkick! Knight lands on the mat and sits up instinctively. Parker leaps to the second rope and blasts Knight in the face with a springboard dropkick.]
BF: Beautifully executed dropkick! A cover and this is elementary! One! Two! Thr...Diamond! EC: One, two, Diamond? LW: Looks like it just became Algebra! [Diamond ejects Parker from the ring and yells down at him. Knight makes his way to his feet and asks the ref to check on "Mr. UEW." The ref obliges, and Diamond turns around for a kick to the "little buddies."
Damon grabs his head and executes the Total Devastation.] BF: There's that 3/4 Bulldog Neckbreaker! LW: Diamond Cutter? BF: Yeah. EC: But he can't pin him, Parker's up on the outside! [Knight hits the opposite ropes and flips over the top with a plancha on
Parker. Knight immediately leaps to his feet and hops to the top rope. He signals to the crowd and lands a Shooting Star Press on Diamond in the middle of the ring.]
ONE! TWO! [Huge pop!] LW: Huh? EC: ... BF: ... THREE! [DING DING DING!] BF: Amazing match! EC: Wonderful match! LW: SWEET JESUS! BF: What?! [During the count, the Mexican sensation, El Puerco made his way to the ring, dressed in his bright green and yellow mask, his gold cape, his long red tights, and waving a Mexican flag. Puerco runs over to Parker and helps him up...then introduces his face to the concrete when he hits the
Tequila Sunrise, his version of the Rocker Dropper. He then turns and makes his way through the crowd to the applause of the partisan crowd.
They turn to boos when they see Damon Knight with his arms raised. Knight gets right next to the camera, gyrates his hips, then blows a kiss. Then turns his attention to the camera, saying...
"You can change the channel now. The only talent in this place is now leaving the building."]
BF: A cocky, yet deserving man. And what a first Light Heavyweight match this has been! Impressive!
EC: Bob, Larry, I'm getting word that something's going on in the back. We've got a camera back there, let's go! [Scene shows the inside of the locker room, with the tag team 'Nuff Said and a few other people. There's a knock on the door. "Kamikaze" Keith answers.]
KK: Who's there? (A rich, German accent comes from the other side.) Voice: Delivery of German chocolates! KK: I didn't order any chocolate. Voice: Is free! KK: Get lost! "Suicidal" Steve: Come on, Keith, free chocolate! (A man from the other side of the room speaks up.) Man: You guys want these two tables here? SS: Yeah. Man: Side by side? In front of these lockers? KK: That's the place. (They open the door slowly...and get assaulted by Omar Cruz and The German
Skull, The Iron Reich! The Skull blows black mist into the face of Steve, then German suplexes him, his head bouncing off the floor. Cruz slams
Keith into an open locker, then repeatedly slams his head with the door.
The Skull tosses Steve onto one of the tables and motions to Cruz. Cruz lifts up Keith and executes the "Cruzifixion" (T-Bone Suplex) through the table, mostly head-first. Skull climbs up on top of the lockers and motions with both thumbs down. He extends his arms to the side and falls forwards like a tree...and connects with a Flying Deadbutt onto the sternum of Steve, sending him through the table and knocking the Skull dizzy. Cruz picks him up over his shoulder and carries him out of the room.)
