1- "Devastating" Damon Knight 2- Nicklaus Kain ============================================================== "Devastating" Damon Knight ============================================================== [Fade In ...] [The picture on your screen opens to a rather wide view of an illuistrious house. This is a beautiful home. It is quite noticable that you stand the top of a driveway. This leads down to the home, however before reaching this part of the house, you admire the landscapping job.
This is something that leaves you with a "I wish I could have it like this", look on your face. Walking downt he drieway, over the horizon of a gate, and setting sun, you can see a swimming pool. Not to much is noticible about this, besides that it is rather wide, and an indiego colored light illuminates off of the pool. Still walking down you notice a four color garage, however a brand new black Hummer sets in the drive way, off to the left. Quite evident of th vechile being brand new, is due to the license plate. Moving on down towards the house, you see a decorated door.
Setting at the door are two dogs. As you reach the door, fear has been placed to your heart ...]
[... With a pounding heart and subconcious that is telling you to turn around, you notice that the dogs scurry away from the camera. This is quite the relief for you, as a fear of big dogs, with even bigger teeth, has set a certain fear to your heart. Now, at the door, a door bell is rung. After a few brief moments a man emerges. The double doors swing open, and when you walk in, you are greeted with a large smile, and a man in butlers'
outfit saying "We have been expecting you." After offering you anything and everything that you could possibly want, he makes the simple request that you follow him. This usually obstacle has become a difficult tsk as you left in absolute "awe" because of the things that you see. This house is starting to seem familiar, one of those Robin Leech shows.]
[Walking though the house and getting deeper and deeper you see several things, however the best thing that you see is your rolemodel, the cornerstone of NeWF, "Devastating" Damon Knight! At this point and time you have reached the outside deck, that you saw walking down the driveway. This is much nicer then you could ever imagine, however, your mind isn't quite strong enough to hold the capicity of witnessing greatness in Damon Knight, and beauty in Damon Knight and this lavish, luxirious home. Knight is showing his beautiful golden-brown skin, and his seemingly perfecy physique. You approach Dee Dee Kay with digression, however, he does not seem to pay you any mind as you notice that glorious over confident, cocky, and cocksure smirk on his face before making all of your fantasites reality, and making all of the ladies a little wet by the viewing pleasure known as: Damon Knight, he decides to speak, eyes closed, and with him laid back relaxing.]
DK : Derek Diamond, Tyrone Parker, and yours truely, "Devastating" Damon
Knight. LightHeavyweight Triangle Match. (Smirks) Y'all know that I should not be in the ring with some damn flyweights. Y'all know that Dee Dee Kay should not be put in the ring with the lights of people like Tyrone Parker, or (In shock) Derek Diamond.
[Damon Knight makes an undescrible noise after saying his opponents names.] DK : All I have to say is that it'll be nothing less than ... (Knight pauses and scratches his chin) devastating.
[Knight chuckles.] DK : Recently a lil' bird told me that Hopper and Kash have titled me: "King of the Indies." Well for your information, I was tha damn _God_ of tha Indies, makin' a name for myself. But that was so long ago, I'm startin' to forget it ever happened. But hey, there's no use for cryin'
'bout it now ... I'll just hafta show what I'm made of yet again. [The eyelid's of Damon Knight fly open. He turns his head, facing the camera and looks into it with a look that should be called nothing less then a "Kodak moment,' for all of you ladies out there.]
DK : An (motioning to himself) tha serial thrilla will defiantly _show_ y'all somethin'. (Grin) And from tha looks of that lack luster card, I am tha g[censored]mn show. So, off tha bat, i'll show Hopper and Kash somethin' too. First impressions are everything. Your first move should always be your best move, and mine is gon' shock tha wrestlin' world.
[Knight rearranges his body this time. He sets up and turns to the camera. The sun glares off of his oiled down body.] DK : So Diamond and Parker, i'm lookin' ta make an impact .. and it looks like your it. (Smirks) Now, I advise tha both of y'all ta' set back and take notes, 'cause y'all are gon' be gettin' totally devastated, not once, but twice.
[Damon Knight shakes his head.] DK : Can y'all comprehend ... (Pause) NO! To put it simply: Do y'all know who you are facin'? ...
... You are facin' tha future ... ... Not some old veteran who still thinks he's got what it takes ... ... Not some punk-kid with visions of glamor and gold ... ... And _surely_ not some out-of-retirement self-made hack that lives off the premise that he's just _that much better_ than everyone else, yet has to strive by sneak-attacks, and under-minded games.
[Another smirk comes over Damon Knight's face.] DK : I'm the man that's gonna lead this federation to tha next level. I've already paid all of my dues. I've already been through the _hell_ of the indies. I've taken my losses, and I've taken even more injuries. It's now time for _me_ to shine, like I have in tha past. Tha road to NeWF supremency... It's gonna start with the two never-will-bes... and continue to the
LightHeavyweight Title, and You can bank on that.
[Knight stands up and stretches. He begins to walk and talk at the same time.] DK : Tyrone Parker ... I've heard tha name. (Shakes his in disapproval) I'm not impressed. You have done nothing but whine, complain, gripe, and
_bitch_ your entire career. For this, I shall let you know how I really
feel about you by making you my very own personal _bitch!_ [Knight's last comments 'cause the egomaniac to chuckle and continue on.] DK : Parker, I honestly couldn't care less about you, or your well being. (Grin) That will be made evident even further on the twenty-seventh of March. [Knight has stopped walking and he has reached his obvisious destatination of a hot tub. A few ladies set in the tub giggling, however only a few remain in the tub as the others rush to asking questions to do Damon
Knight's bidding. He steps in, and sets down.] DK : Derek Diamond. Never heard of ya, (shrugs) don't care too. See, i'm simply tha best. Derek Diamond, you need ta' give inta' tha hype. Believe everything you have heard about me, and go down at tha mah hands.
[Damon Knight looks around tha pool and sees all of the beautiful ladies before speaking.] DK : I have people to do, and things to see ... And as you can see, some are around me. [Damon Knight motions to the females around him.] DK : So on a final note, i'll see y'all in tha ring, and when all is said and done, when your countin' tha lights, when I make that huge stwp up on tha NeWF tutem pole, you'll know that you have been _totally devastated._
[In a long swimming-like stroke, Damon Knight goes over to two of the ladies and sets back inbetween their legs.]
DK : Yo, i'm ... i'm Lots of action ...Twice the satisfaction ... and If i'm not the main event ... I am DEFINITELY the MAIN ATTRACTION!! [With that smirk comming back on the face of Damon Knight that we saw earlier when we first saw Knight, the camera fades to black.]
============================================================== Nicklaus Kain ============================================================== (Scene opens to a dark clearing. The bare trees sway slowly, scratching lightly against one another. The scratching sounds, the whistle of the wind, and the light patter of rain on the dirt are the only sounds that can
be heard. As the loneliness and emptiness of the area seeps in, the camera finally starts to move. Every now and then, thunder cracks against your ears and lightening lights up the sky. The camera moves steadily, eventually coming to an iron gate. An arm comes from behind the camera and checks the gate, finding it locked tightly. The camera looks in different directions, finally looking back at the gate.)
Camera Man: Oh well, Kash can't fire me if I couldn't get in... (The gate opens slowly and noisily, with a loud squeaking sound.) Camera Man: Son of a...! Hey, who's there? (No answer. The camera man pauses, and then tentatively continues on through the gate. As the lightening strikes again, a massive stone structure is seen in the distance. The grayish-black stones are dilapidated and overgrown with moss and kudzu. The hideous mass is almost castle-like, with towers surrounding and connecting to the main building.
The enormous doors straight ahead, the camera presses onward. As the lightening strikes again, the words inscribed on the stone sign can be made out. They read: "NORTHERN FOREST CITY SANITARIUM" The doors are boarded shut, and a huge, rusted padlock locks the chains which hold the door closed. Regardless, screams permeate the air from inside the horrible building. The camera man unsuccessfully tries to enter the building. A gruff voice speaks from within the walls.)
Voice: Who da hell are you? Camera Man: Um...I'm an NeWF camera man...here to meet Nicklaus Kain. Voice: Here to talk to Kain? CM: Y-yes. Voice: He's in the North tower. Head over there. CM: How will I know which is the North tower? (No answer. The camera man sighs and then sets out around the building. The grass is incredibly high, and the mud is thick. The camera eventually reaches one of the towers, which fortunately is the North tower. The camera man uses the huge iron knocker. The door opens slowly, and the camera man steps into darkness. A voice speaks around the screams from the
"patients".)
Voice: You've come to speak to me? CM: You're Nicklaus Kain, right? (A torch is lit, and before you stands Nicklaus Kain, clad in a long, black coat, a fancy shirt, dress trousers and dress boots. His black hair is tied back, and his blazing, red eyes stare into the lens. He begins to walk off and motions for the camera to follow. He talks as he walks.)
Kain: Yes, I am. And you're the NeWF camera man who should have been here an hour ago, right?
CM: Um...uh...well... Kain: Shut up. You're here to film and to listen. Now, welcome to the
Northern Forest City Sanitarium. Hanging around a graveyard or a morgue all the time gets rather lonesome, so I come out here for some company.
I'll introduce you to some of my...acquaintances. These are the most wretched and loathed in North America. "Patients" as they're called, get shipped here from all corners of this continent. It's sort of a...secret area. No one comes out here, and those who do assume that it is deserted.
It's called a sanitarium. But that's not what it is. These aren't "patients," they're prisoners. When you throw out the garbage, it goes to a landfill. Well, consider the Northern Forest City Sanitarium the landfill, the "patients" the garbage, and towns everywhere the people.
It's perfect. It's free, no bodies for investigators to find, and who's going to believe hundreds of criminally insane abominations are imprisoned here?
CM: Well Mr. Kain...if no one knows it's here, how do you know about it? (Kain turns and looks into the camera, a smile drawing up one side of his mouth. He turns and opens a broken down wooden door, waving the camera in.
Inside the room is a small wooden table and a small wooden chair. nothing else. Sitting in the chair is a small man, maybe 5'3", weighing roughly
110 lbs. His head it shaven bald and he wears only a pair of blue jeans that are too small. There's also dried blood everywhere. Kain nods to him. The small man nods and smiles. When he does, you see that he has no teeth, only several scalpel blades shoved into the empty sockets.)
CM: My God! (Kain laughs.) Kain: Not quite. God can't penetrate these walls. This is Herman. He was a scientist who worked for some corporation. There was a chemical explosion. He around when the explosion took place, but when he reentered the lab, the fumes got to his brain. They found him a few days later, gnawing on his co-worker's bodies.
CM: (gasping) He's a cannibal?! Kain: Quite. CM: And what about the...the teeth? Kain: You mean the lack thereof? After the human flesh stopped coming in, something clicked and he ripped all his teeth out.
CM: Th-the... Kain: The blades? He got those here. He gets served raw animals, and when there's an unfortunate death here, they don't exactly get buried.
CM: How'd he get them? Aren't they dangerous? Kain: Look, the people who run this place don't give a damn what happens to the prisoners here. As a matter of fact, no one's even here a great deal of the time. They check in every now and then. He requested some so they gave them to him. Herman, how are you doing?
Herman: Hey Mista Kain. That a new meal ya got there? Kain: No, no, not quite. He's here to hear me talk about my match. Herman: Who you ah, you wrestlin' Mista Kain? Kain: You wouldn't know him. Herman: I know...just tryin' ta make conversation... (Kain turns towards the door.) Kain: I'll try and find you something to eat, Herman. (Kain closes the door behind the camera man.) CM: That's...that's horrible. This place...it's horrible! Kain: It doesn't matter. You aren't trapped here, and so why should it matter to you? But enough. You didn't come here to see this place. You came here to hear me talk about "Tiger" Jay Moore. But what am I going to say about him? To him? What are you expecting? "I'm going to steal your soul!" "I'm going to engulf you in darkness!" Something along those lines? I could give you a whole "evil" speech. I could throw my hair back and roll my eyes back in my head. But I won't give you that. All I can say to you, Tiger, is an offer. Don't show up Monday, and I won't leave you in a hospital bed.
CM: That's....it? Kain: What more do you want? Now get out of here. And next time, bring me
David Lowry. CM: You...you got it. (The camera turns and walks away, getting through the door and eventually out of the gate without incident. A scream blasts from the building, sending the camera man running through the trees.)
[Fade to Black]
