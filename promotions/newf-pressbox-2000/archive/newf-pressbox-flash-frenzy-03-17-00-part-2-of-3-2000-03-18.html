Flash Frenzy [3/17/00] (Part 2 of 3)

18 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1- Derek Diamond 2- The Big Chill 3- "Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin 4- The Football Hooligans 5- "The Extreme Warrior" War Child ============================================================== Derek Diamond ============================================================== [The camera opens in the parking lot of the Pace University basketball arena. A sputtering Toyota truck creeps into the picture. It pulls into a parking spot beneath a flickering street lamp. The engine cuts out and the car idles violently to a stop. The door swings open and a broken down pair of Chucks and a gray sweatpant clad leg slide out. The lamp flickers and finally Derek Diamond climbs from the cab. Diamond, clad in a pair of gray sweatpants, a white t-shirt and a pair of navy blue chucks. A backwards
Baltimore Orioles cap covers his closely cropped hair and he’s sporting a five o’clock shadow, reaches into the bed and pulls out a red duffel bag.
He nods for the camera to approach.] [Derek Diamond] Welcome to the NeWF? That’s exactly what I wanted to hear, ‘course I didn’t expect the invite to be accompanied by a camera crew.
[He flashes his trademark crooked grin, his blue eyes sparkle in the flickering light] [Derek Diamond] You want my thoughts on the other members of the NeWF roster? 'Course I have opinions on ‘em. But there’s a time and a place for everything and callin’ out guys I’ve never met isn’t exactly my style.
I’d rather my talents and performance did the talkin’ for me. [Diamond nods giving the opportunity some thought] [Derek Diamond] But. I will mention a couple of the guys that have made me sit up and take notice, for good or for ill. “Mr. UEW” is one of the guys that’ve made me notice ‘em. ‘Course he’s the most abrasive, obnoxious and arrogant wrestler I’ve ever come across. And believe me, runnin’ up and down the coast, I’ve run into a lot of arrogance. Tyrone Parker, your accomplishments mean little to me. ‘Cause you didn’t do it in my presence.
The only things I’ve heard about you are from a third party or they’re your self-promoting drivel. I’ve got two simple words for you, show me.
You claim to be the be all and end all of light heavyweight wrestling. Prove it. I may be a rookie in comparison to the majority of the NeWF roster but I’m neither easily impressed nor easily fooled. Unless you can prove to me that you’re everything you claim to be you’ll remain all talk and no substance.
[Diamond adjusts the bag on his shoulder, pulling it up higher onto his lat and locks his truck door. He grabs a small cardboard box from the truck bed and cracks his neck. He nods and grins and composes himself before speaking.]
[Derek Diamond] The light heavyweight ranks aren’t the only place that’s replete with less than admirable talents. The heavyweight division ain’t free of the taint of cheaters and dishonorable athletes. But one guy, who doesn’t quite fit that bill, is “The Natural.” Pal, you may be a third, forth, hell even a tenth generation wrestler but you’re no natural. That honor is reserved for Roy Hobbs, so I’d suggest finding yourself a new moniker. [grins] The wrestling world may be new to me, but I’m a fast learner and I plan on becoming the inaugural light heavyweight champion.
It’s all about desire, and perseverance. After runnin’ up and down the
East Coast I’ve proven to myself and my critics that I have the skills, talent and most importantly the heart to become a champion. It’s just a matter of time. The NeWF is the new stompin’ grounds for Derek Diamond and
I plan on makin’ the most of the experience. See ya in next week.
[Diamond walks off screen towards the arena. He begins to whistle 311’s
“Down.” Abrupt fade to black.]
============================================================== The Big Chill ============================================================== (The scene opens with a man on a hillside. He is sitting under a tree with a ponderous look. Nearby, one can hear a babbling brook. In the tree, there are chirping birds. A feather gets pushed in the breeze and goes by the man. In the distance, group of monks can be seen walking down a mountainside. The man looks into the camera.)
Man: This ain't your father's interview! (A bolt of lightning strikes. With Rage Against the Machine's "Calm like a
Bomb" as the background music, the scene shifts to a video montage of The
Big Chill working out. The clip shifts to the Big Chill wrestling the
Japanese team known as Daiymo.)
(CLIP: Chill drags Dragon to his feet, and presses Dragon over his head. He holds Dragon there for a few seconds, then slams him to the mat. Dragon sits up, holding his back, and Chill nails him with a stiff kick to the back.)
(CLIP: The Big Chill, being spotted by his workout partner, known only as
'Monster', is using the Smith Machine (tm), doing some squats.)
(CLIP: The Big Chill, with a big smile on his face, is spitting blood during an interview.)
(CLIP: Chill drags Sammy the Kid to his feet again, and whips him to the ropes. Sammy The Kid ducks the clothesline, and as Chill turns around,
Sammy the Kid nails him with a vicious superkick! Chill staggers back, but doesn't go down! Chill moves toward Sammy the Kid, who superkicks him again! Chill staggers back, dazed, but again tries to rush Sammy the Kid, who nails him with a third superkick! This time, Chill crashes to the mat, and Sammy the Kid grapevines the leg, and the ref makes the three count.)
(CLIP: The Big Chill on a bench doing an incline chest press with a pair of dumbbells.) (CLIP: Tiger Claw and Matsuoko walk over to Chill. They help Chill to his feet, but he shoves them both off, and slides out of the ring. The
Syndicate looks down at him, but he just smirks at them, then heads up the runway.) (The scene fades into Tommy Chill's gym in New York City. The Big Chill is
6'2", 265 lbs, muscular wrestler, is seated on a workout bench. He is wearing a "TBC" tank top and black sweat pants. He is sweating profusely.)
TBC: Every once in a while, great events change the world. Imagine being there when some of these events had taken place. Imagine seeing the D-Day invasion. Imagine being there when the dropped the bomb on Japan. Imagine being there when the dropped the napalm on Vietnam. Imagine the destruction, the disaster. Well, you're all in luck. Today you are witness to that type of event! You are witness to the Big Chill entering the NeWF!
(The Big Chill removes his tank top. On his left pectoral, a small skulls and crossbones tattoo can be seen. Under it can be read "Badd Intentions".)
TBC: Now before the rest of the minions here start chirping about hearing it all before, let me give you a little background. Just so you don't think
I'm some new kid on the block. I've wrestled in the States and I've wrestled around the world. Everywhere I've been, I've done two things: I wore a little gold and I caused a little havoc! I've wrestled some of the toughest people in the world. A lot of em ain't here no more, and I'm still kicking. You've just seen some clips of the unfortunate chumps getting my way and what happened. But you know what?
(The Big Chill wipes his face with his shirt.) TBC: It don't mean a goddamn thing here! It doesn't mean a damn thing that as rookie, me and my best friend started the dirtiest group of bastiches ever. It doesn't mean a thing that when I packed my bags and went to Japan, they tried to ban me at every turn! And that when I came back form dropping punks on their heads, I still was the baddest dog in the pound! None of it matters right at this moment! All the counts is all the trouble I'm about to cause and all the tails I'm about to kick!
(The Big Chill picks up a bottled water and takes a sip.) TBC: It seems that their are some people that be recognize talent when they see it or I wouldn't have been signed to this contract! Just to be fair, I gotta tell you that the people that run this place don't really know what they are getting themselves into! Everyone had better get some video tapes, some first hand accounts, something. Cuz just like a natural disaster, a strange occurrence or an act of God, people just can't believe what they hear about TBC, until they see him in action!
(TBC wipes his mouth with his hand.) TBC: But you know what? This time everything is going to be a little different. Everywhere I have gone, I have sat back, kicked the tails that were put in front of me and waited for my opportunity. Waited. And waited.
Well, this time, I ain't going to be playing nice. On my report card, it's going to say doesn't play well with others. It's going to be a little different here in the NeWF. Alot of people are going to be surprised, alot of people are going to be shocked. (chuckles) Oh are people going to be shocked.
(TBC stands.) TBC: So, NeWF, what I want you to do is take some time out. Call up your mother, your Aunt Sallie, anyone that means a damned thing to you. Call them and tell them not to watch any of the stuff that's going to happen.
They're not going to be able to stomach the carnage that is about to begin. The Big Chill is here, so the pain begins, and it begins in mass quantity! Remember, this is the T, the B and the C, and you don't want to step to me cuz once I'm through with you, you're going to want to D, I, E!
(Scene fades to black.) ============================================================== "Pure Chaos" Brandon Franklin ============================================================== [The Scene opens up in Tokyo, Japan outside of the Ariake Coloseum. There is a man, with his back turned towards the camera looking up at the massive arena. He turns around, and we recognize his face, he is "Pure Chaos"
Brandon Franklin.] [Brandon is sporting some blue "Adidas" Windbreakers, and a White Tee Shirt underneath of his open, leather jacket. Brandon has on some silver
"Oakley" Sunglasses as well. Brandon has a look of surprise on his face as he begins to talk.]
Brandon Franklin: "HOLY S[Censored]t! Do you see that?" [Brandon turns back around, looking up to what he was referring to, the
Coloseum.] Brandon Franklin: "I'll be damned! And, I'm Wrestling there, TONIGHT! Although... you people wouldn't be able to see it until the Twenty Second of March anyway..."
[Brandon turns back around, to face the camera.] Brandon Franklin: "I s'pose you're pretty confused wondering why I've had a match there, and why I'm in Tokyo. In case some of you UNintelligent little freaks don't know, I'm fighting for the Hardcore Title, in Tokyo, against a man named Pecker."
[Brandon smirks.] Brandon Franklin: "Ain't that some s[censored]t!" [Brandon's smirk fades away a little as he takes his sunglasses off, and sticks them in his pocket.]
Brandon Franklin: "And since, 'Pure Chaos' was *FORCED* to cut an interview, that's what I'm going to do. I would *LOVE* to sit here and tell you all how bad-ass I really am, but that would serve no purpose. I'm simply going to say what I have to say, and get on with my..."
[Brandon's voice turns sarcastic as he lets out a sigh.] Brandon Franklin: "Exciting life..." [Brandon's voice is back to normal now, as he continues...] Brandon Franklin: "You know, there's just some things I don't understand. For instance, why am I in an organization where the top name is Humpty
Dumpty. Now, I'm sure the guy has a lot of talent, but Jesus H. Christ,
Humpty Dumpty? But, what do you expect from... Chris Hopper?"
[Brandon smiles.] Brandon Franklin: "Yeah, Chris. Get back to me on that one... now, as I was saying. I have *NEVER* held the Top title of an organization, and I'm not going to hang up the boots until I do. So, what I'm going to do in the
NeWF is rip through you no-namers like a hot knife through butter, claim my prize, and tell you that I told you so, and head out the door. I'm only here for one reason, the title! And I'll be Pissed off if I have to work in two Organizations at the same time, for more than two months. So, you know somethin' boys? Sleep with one eye open from now on, because the most unpredictable son-of-a-bitch has arrived. 'Pure Chaos' Brandon Franklin will make sure the NeWF is just that... PURE CHAOS!"
[The Camera fades.] ============================================================== The Football Hooligans ============================================================== [Scene opens on a dirty street in downtown New York. Litter swirls around in the wind as cars and trucks drive past a small bar. The camera pans along and enters the bar, a few unsavoury types are sitting at small tables drinking their beers. A big man with a scruffy beard sits pearched atop a bar stool, eating the complimentary peanuts and downing shots of whisky.
The camera moves past him and onto a table in the corner. Three figures, two men and a woman, sit huddled around their beers drinking and talking in hushed tones. The voices begin to be heard...]
Woman: ...so we strt soon then, are you guys ready? Big Man: Luv, we were born ready, eh boy? Smaller Man: Yeah, have you got all the contracts sorted, Tracy? [The woman, who we assume is Tracy reaches into her purse and pulls out a bunch of papers.]
Tracy: I've been through all this and everything seems O.K. The one thing that you have to be aware of is that we don't get an automatic shot at the tag team championships.
Big Man: That's no big deal, once we beat all the other teams in this fed they'll have to bow down to our superiority, right Dazza?
[The smaller man, who we now know to be Dazza, places his beer on the table and picks up his copy of the NeWF contract.]
Dazza: Do you reckon they'll accept an 'X' from Smithy, him being illiterate and all? [The bigger man, Smithy, turns to his long time friend and tag team partner and glares.] Smithy: Your sense of humour is gonna get you in trouble one day boy. Dazza: Any time tubby - you know I can take you. Tracy: Boys, less o' the bickering. We have to get our minds set on this new league - you've proven your metal in the three other main theatres of tag team wrestling. All that's left is the United States.
Dazza: Don't you worry your pretty little 'ed luv, we will prove we are the best when we step through those ropes. Our destiny, and by that I mean the tag team gold, awaits.
[The camera moves, backwards, away from the table and we can see that Dazza and Smithy are signing their respective contracts. The camera moves past the bearded man who is learing at Tracy as he stuffs more peanuts into his mouth, this time accompanied by a bottle of beer. Fade to grey.]
============================================================== "The Extreme Warrior" War Child ============================================================== [Scene opens in the NeWF corporate office parking lot where tons of media personalities and press have gathered for a press conference. A row of chairs has been set up infront of a huge stage which is curved into a quarter moon shape. On top of the stage is two long tables, curved in the same fashion, on either side of a podium. Behind the tables is a huge black curtain with the NeWF logo emblazoned on it. The press is seated in the seats in front. The press is surrounded by steel guardrails. On the side of the stage wrestling fans have gathered, wondering what this could be about.
They also have been sectioned off by steel guardrails, forming an isle between the press and the fans who have gathered. NeWF front office staff start coming in from behind the curtain and take their seats at the appropriate name tag. The NeWF President Kevin Kash, flanked by two aides, steps up to the podium and turns on the mic. The mic snaps to life with a screech of feedback which sends the press and fans alike into a groan. The president is about to speak]
NeWF President Kevin Kash: Ladies and gentlemen of the press, our esteemed fans. I've called this press conference today. To announce yet another signing to the NeWF. But this is no ordinary signing. This man is known all over the world. This man....
[Suddenly one of the president's aides whispers in his ear in the middle of a sentence. Which the president finishes as he is being told something by the aide]
NeWF President Kevin Kash: This man.... is not here right now? WHAT! What do you mean he's not here? He was told the NeWF corporate office parking lot at 12 noon. Where is h.....
[The president is cut off in the middle of a rant by a sound in the distance. The NeWF camera crew assigned to the press conference pans their camera around to the street. Down the road a bit a black vehicle is approaching at a high rate of speed, kicking up dust and debris. As the vehicle gets closer it's easily made out as a shiny jet-black military-style Hum Vee. The engine roars as the truck speeds down the street and into the parking lot. The camera moves in closer to see if he can get a look at the driver, but all they can make out is a shadow. A big shadow of a big man. Suddenly the driver slams on the brakes and screeches to a halt right at the press pit.]
NeWF President Kevin Kash: Someone arrest that maniac, he almost wiped out the press. He's a....
[The police, tending to crowd control begin moving toward the Hummer. The aides whispers into his ear again. This time a look of fear comes across his face. The aide has just told him, that was the newest NeWF acquisition.]
NeWF President Kevin Kash: Officers, no...it's alright. Misunderstanding really.... [The camera moves from the president's face to the Hummer as the driver side door of the truck swings open. Then two big black leather boots come out of the vehicle and onto the pavement. Then the driver steps out. The camera pans up to get a look at his face. The man is at least 6 feet 7 inches tall, in excess of 270 to 280 pounds. The camera pans up and up. The man is wearing a long black leather trench coat, blue jeans and a black
T-shirt bearing his face and some lettering on it. The camera then moves to his face. A face that would be considered handsome, but it's got scars, scars from battle. Covering the face of the man is a goatee. The mans hair is jet black and long, surrounding his face and black sunglasses]
NeWF President Kevin Kash: Ladies and gentlemen, without further ADO. Here he is, the Beantown madman himself. Standing in at 6 feet 7 inches and weighing 280lbs... The Extreme Warrior, War Child!!!
["Dragula" by Rob Zombie plays over the PA system as War Child walks through the isle in between the press and fans. The fans mouth are a gape.
They know his man but they are in shock to see him, as he has been out of the picture for a long time, it was assumed he was dead. The crowd's oohs and ahs mix in with the still running engine of the Hum Vee. War Child reaches the stage, doesn't bother to use the access stair cases on either side, he just puts one big boot on the edge of the stage and pushes himself onto it. He steps beside the president, dwarfing him in that instant. The president is noticeably sweating. The music slowly fades.]
NeWF President Kevin Kash: Welcome War Child, glad you can make i.... "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: Dispose of the pleasantries. I am here to sign the contract and get on with the day. I've been out of action for quite sometime and this is boring to me. This is an interruption to my training schedule you corporate pinheads could've mailed the contract to me and I would've signed it and mailed it back. But instead this is made as a huge media publicity stunt. So bring out the contract before I change my mind and take the biggest moneymaking opportunity this federation has away to a competing organization...
NeWF President Kevin Kash: <<<gulping and loosening his tie>>> OK, give me the briefcase...
[One of the aides reaches under the podium and produces a metal briefcase and opens it. In it is a black leather folder and a gold pen. The president removes the folder and pen and lays them on the flat, slanted surface of the podium. War Child picks up the folder, opens and begins reading. A smile forms across the big man's face. But this is not a smile of happiness, this is a pure deadly smile, a sadistic smile of a fiendish clown in a satanic circus]
"The Extreme Warrior" War Child: Everything seems to be in order, right down to the open contract. A contract open for anyone to sign in this pitiful excuse of an organization. I've looked up and down the roster of the NeWF and see nothing that could even be considered competition that could survive the Path of destruction I'm going to lay down right through the arenas of the NeWF. From this point on, no one is safe. A War has been declared against all the so-called superstars of the NeWF. A war that'll see a lot of bodies fall in a bloody heap. If you think you're safe. If you think you can hide, if you think you're strong enough to withstand the path of destruction. That's when you're at your most vulnerable, weakest point and that's when I'll strike. Like a deadly cobra, you never know when it's gonna happen. You'll never know when that strike will happen. But when it does, you'll fall like all others have to the War Machine.
NeWF President Kevin Kash: So, War Child, if you'd just sign the contract we can get on with the conference and have the press ask a couple of questions...
"The Extreme Warrior" War Child: Do you really think that because you're the president, you can order me around. That you can tell me what to do? I make my own rules. I live by my way of life and a f#$kin' corporate @$$hole like you does not have a chance in hell to get me to do your bidding. I'm not one of those pantywaist wrestlers of yours with my nose so far up your
@$$, they've got a full sack in their mouths if you know what I mean. I sign this because I want to, because all my concessions were met, and because even though you gotta bunch of nobodies in this organization, they are the best wrestlers, no matter how low these pieces of crap are. They're still pretty high up on the pile of dung that are today's wrestlers. Not important enough to acknowledge by name, but more important than what I scrape off my boots after walking in the Boston commons....
[War Child takes the pen and signs the contract, hands over the folder with force to the president and snaps the gold pen in half, ink squirting into his hands, looking like black blood as it trails the lines in his palms and dripping on to the stage. War Child wipes his hands on one of the aides'
shirt. The aide has a look of annoyance, which turns into a look of fear as he looks at War Child's eyes just above the rim of his sunglasses. Cold, blue eyes stare at the aide with all the fire and anger of the insane. The president ignores the broken pen which now lays on the stage. War Child steps on it, and crunches it into the stage floor. ]
NeWF President Kevin Kash: Umm, we will now take questions from the press in attendance... Yes, you sir
Man: Rob Oteri from WOW magazine, War Child, now that you have signed on the dotted line for the NeWF. What's your objective? What are your plans?
[War Child removes his glasses to reveal, cold, icy eyes... no emotion but pure rage in them. He places the glasses in the inside pocket of his trench coat. ]
"The Extreme Warrior" War Child: Quite simple really... I only desire what any normal wrestler desires. And that's gold. I plan to win every available singles title there is in the NeWF. And I don't give a rat's @$$ how many careers I end, how many bodies I put in the hospital. I don't care who gets buried in the path of destruction. Because when the smoke clears, after the war is over. I will be standing in the middle of burning flesh, broken bones, stretched muscles and torn ligaments. Like a black phoenix I will stand in their ashes, waiting to see if there's anyone left to take down.
As far as my plans, well that's really quite simple. As simple as the mind in that pretty-boy head of yours. I plan to destroy every wrestler in this federation till they can put on no more shows and the organization closes because there'd be only me left standing. I can't wrestle myself every night, although that'd be the best damn match ever seen in the NeWF. It'd get boring. But my first priority....
[War Child takes a piece of paper out of his back pocket] "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: Is to climb the latter in the NeWF and take all the gold home to Boston. And Judging by this list of so-called wrestlers. I'll have all this accomplished by the end of 2000, hell the end of the month. These wrestlers aren't worth the paper they're written on.
[War Child rips the paper to shreds] NeWF President Kevin Kash: Next question please...ah you miss Woman: Dawn Hennessy, PWI magazine, War Child, to those of the NeWF fans not familiar with your background or skills, please enlighten
[War Child starts laughing] "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: That's as stupid as the look on your face. But I will indulge your idiotic question. Some of my background includes, 5 world titles, all long reigns, one television title, 5 hardcore and extreme titles and 3 Japanese king of barbed wire death match championships. I have been trained in Judo. Have fought street-style and have been a Greco-Roman wrestling champion. You might say I'm a War Machine. The perfect weapon in the ring. If I can't get you with wrestling, I'll use a Judo throw on you...If I can't get you that way, I'll simply punch you in the mouth.. And if that doesn't work, I'll wrap you up in barbed wire and....
[Suddenly War Child puts his fist through the podium and stomps it to splinters] "The Extreme Warrior" War Child: I'LL BASH YOUR F$%KIN' SKULL IN WITH
ANYTHING I CAN GET MY HANDS ON!!! PUT YOU THROUGH A TABLE AND PLANT YOUR
STINKIN' CARCASS INTO THE PATH OF DESTRUCTION WITH THE WAR MACHINE.. THERE
YOU WILL BE, AND THERE WILL YOU STAY FOREVER!!!! One final message before I go. With the signing of this contract, a fire has been started, the fire of war... It's going to spread and consume this whole organization. Nothing is safe, and no one will survive. To all those who think you've got it. Step up, sign the open challenge and I'll see you in HELL!!! Boys, the road just got rough in the NeWF, we're traveling at speeds of unknown proportions straight to the mouth of hell. This machine has no brakes and I'm at the wheel and only I will survive... Ash to ash, Dust to Dust... Fade to black.....
[War Child kicks the remains of the podium off the stage, walks to his Hum
Vee and takes off with a squeal of his tires. Camera fades on the president's face which has a worried, what have I done, look]
