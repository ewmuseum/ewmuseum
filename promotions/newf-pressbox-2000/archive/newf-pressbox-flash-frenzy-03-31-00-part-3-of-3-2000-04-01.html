This is the Gym where "Pure
Chaos" Brandon Franklin is seen, quite often. This Gym is at an unknown location. In the middle of a boxing ring, are two men. One of the two men is Brandon Franklin, while the other is a newcomer to Wrestling, getting tips from Brandon. The other guy is about the same age as Brandon, wearing blue shorts, and a white Tee Shirt.]
[Brandon sends the man to the ropes, and as he comes off the ropes, Brandon hits him with a stiff clothesline. Brandon picks him up, and plants him with a bodyslam. Brandon follows that up with a Leg Drop, and then goes for the a "pin". The count is made by a man standing on the outside.]
[Brandon stands up, sweat dripping from his Red Tee Shirt, as he pulls his black shorts up to his waist a little more. A short stalky man, in about his fourties is on the outside, clapping for the two men, and their
Wrestling. The man who Brandon pinned gets out of the ring, and the older man puts his arm around him, giving him some tips, as the two walk out of picture.]
[Brandon sits on a brown wooden bench, picking up a white towel, and wiping his forehead and face off with it. The Camera fades, as the Cameraman begins to move into the position.]
[We open back up in front of Brandon who digs into his black, leather duffel bag, pulling out some water, and stuff the towel back into the bag.
Brandon looks up to the camera, twisting the lid off of the water bottle.] Brandon Franklin: "I know what you're here for..." [Brandon twists the lid off, and the lid falls to the ground, and Brandon hurries to pick it up before it rolls away. He looks back up to the camera.]
Brandon Franklin: "You want some interview time, with the man known as
'Pure Chaos' Brandon Franklin - ME!"
[Brandon takes a sip of his water, letting some drip on his face.] Brandon Franklin: "Well, I really don't have much to say this time around. I was thinking that Sean Cage would say a couple of lines to piss me off - and get me going, but he didn't. Quite frankly, the sorry son of a bitch looked over me, and to The Big Chill."
[Brandon takes another sip of his water, then rests it on his knee.] Brandon Franklin: "That was a big mistake, but I'll let it slide. Because, after all, Sean Cage really doesn't worry me. The thing that worries me..." [Brandon takes a brief pause, to catch his breath.] Brandon Franklin: "Is what I might do to Sean when he steps in between those ropes, and faces me 'One on One'. Maybe I should wake Sean up a little, start off with some power moves, to let him know that I'm not a guy to be looked over. Or, maybe I could run circles around him, making him dizy, then plant him with a move that gives him a headache for the rest of his life. I could end his career, by snapping his right leg in half, so he won't be able to do two of the things he loves - Basketball and Wrestling.
Hell, I could rip his ears off, shove 'em up his ass so he can hear literally hear me kicking the s[censored]t out of him!"
[Brandon pauses, taking another sip of his water.] Brandon Franklin: "So many ways to hurt you Sean Cage, I don't know which one to turn to. So many ways to win, that the outcome is even unpredictable. I mean - I know I'm winning, but the way I'm going to win is what's unpredictable. You see, Sean Cage, you may know absolutely nothing about me, but I'll teach you. On Saturday, I'm going to teach you good and well what 'Pure Chaos' is all about, and what I do for a living.
That's kicking ass, talking smack, and kicking ass again, laying my finisher on ya, walking out of the match the winner."
[Brandon guzzles his water down, to the last drop. He drops the empty bottle on the ground, and starts zipping his duffel bag up, over lapping the straps on each other.]
Brandon Franklin: "Your time is running down... slowly. Get a good night's rest, and say your prayers - because not even God can save you for what's in store for ya tomorrow night!"
[Brandon picks his duffel bag up, straps it over his shoulder, and walks out of the "Gold's Gym". The Camera fades.]
============================================================== The Christian Brothers ============================================================== [David Lowry is standing on the steps of a large nameless church in an unknown city. It is a beautiful, sunny afternoon and a few bystanders walk past, some stopping to watch what is happening. Lowry looks around curiously, straightens his tie, and asks an unseen camera operator "How do
I look?" before he realizes the camera is rolling.]
LOWRY: Good afternoon, ladies and gentlemen, I'm here on the steps of Our
Lady of Mercy Cathedral, set to interview the newest competitors in NeWF, the Christian Brothers. I was told by the head office to come out here and get an exclusive interview with the new superstars, but we just arrived and there's no one here to meet us. We're going to head inside if we can find them.
[Lowry waves the camera on and they head up the stairs and through a huge, oak door. It is dimly lit inside, and soft organ music and be heard coming from the far end of the church. A short, plump nun walks up to Lowry, to offer assistance.]
LOWRY: Hi, we're from the NeWF Wrestling Promotion. We looking for John
Michael Baptist and Mark Gideon, we're supposed to interview them for our program.
[The nun points to the altar, smiling, and Lowry thanks her and heads down the aisle toward the spectacular and beautifully ornate altar, before which kneel two large men.]
LOWRY: Well, that certainly was a nice polite woman, wasn't it? Nuns are always so friendly and -
[Lowry is stopped and interrupted by a tall blonde woman. She is wearing a nun's headpiece and cloak, but the cape is flung back to reveal a black leather number underneath consisting of a halter top, long gloves, a pencil skirt and thigh high, stiletto-heeled boots. Her arms are folded across her ample chest, and she glares at the slack-jawed Lowry.]
WOMAN: [Coldly] Can I help you? LOWRY: *AHEM* [Straightens himself up.] Um, yes, I guess you must be
Sister Mary Mercy? [She nods.] Right, well I'm from the NeWF and I was sent her to get an exclusive with the Christian Brothers. I believe that's them over there. Would it be all right if I could get a few words?
[Lowry gestures toward the two men kneeling back-on to the camera at the altar. He takes a step toward them but Sister Mary puts her gloved hand on his chest, firmly and aggressively, to stop him.]
SISTER MARY: They are not to be disturbed. LOWRY: Right. So are they like talking to the big guy or something? SISTER MARY: [She is not amused.] Show some respect in the house of the
Lord, or you will be the first in NeWF to feel the Wrath of God and the
Christian Brothers.
LOWRY: *snicker* They're a couple of priests! What are they going to do, win matches by boring people with readings from the Bible? They may be big guys, and I hear they've even had some success in other federations, but
I'm not afraid of them. I say my prayers, I take my vitamins.
SISTER MARY: I've seen you wrestle. All the prayers and vitamins in the world couldn't have saved your career. You're lucky you were forced to retire when you did, I don't think anyone could have stood watching you in the ring any longer. But I'm lowering myself just talking to you. I think you had better leave now.
LOWRY: Look, I'm sorry. But I came all the way here to get an interview, at least if I can have a few words...
SISTER MARY: [Pointing.] Leave. Now. LOWRY: Hey, I just want to - OWWW!!! [Sister Mary grabs his arm and twists it behind his back in an armlock. She pushes him toward the door, the shot bouncing and jostling as the cameraman tries to get out of the way.]
LOWRY: Lady, watch it... Argh... I've got some major medical conditions back there... careful with the...
[With one final shove Sister Mary sends Lowry out the door and onto the front steps. She glares at Lowry for a moment, then crosses herself and emits a slight girlish giggle that seems totally out of her character. She slams the large oak door, and the shot fades out on the old, weathered wood.]

============================================================== Nicklaus Kain ============================================================== (Scene opens to the Media Office in NeWF Headquarters. The desks are spread out a bit, but it still looks cramped. Employees are typing up various things on their computers. Others are making copies, send faxes, drinking coffee, and various other office practices. They're all in suits and ties or dresses. Very formal, organized environment.]
[NeWF lead reporter David Lowry heads towards the door, coffee in one hand, stack of papers in the other. His eyes are fixed on the top paper, and somehow he doesn't run into anything. He's dressed in a dark blue suit, with a tie that reads "NeWF" in big pink letters. A young aide hurries up to him.)
Aide: Mr. Lowry! Mr. Lowry! There's a man in your office who wants to talk to you! David Lowry: Tell him I'll be with him in about five minutes... Aide: I told him you had an errand to run, but he demanded to talk to you now! Lowry: Who the...is that why there's a camera guy following you around? Aide: Yes sir, the man had brought him in from downstairs. Lowry: Who is he? Aide: He wouldn't tell me, plus is was all dark in there and...Mr. Lowry? Lowry: ...I think I know who it is... (Lowry dumps the rest of his coffee and hands the papers to the aide. He then adjusts his tie and heads towards the office. Once he arrives, he opens the door and steps into total darkness. The only sounds are of two men breathing, one of them Lowry.)
Lowry: Nicklaus? (Pause.) Lowry: Nicklaus Kain? (Pause.) Lowry: Nicklaus, I know it's you. Who else would barge into my office, shove the help around, cut the lights...and turn up the freakin' A/C!
(The chattering of teeth also sounds in the room. There are a few thuds, crashes, and curses, but eventually a relaxed sigh and the sound of air seeping out of a chair as it's occupied. There's another uneasy pause.)
Lowry: So...did you come to darken and freeze my office, then sit there and enjoy it? Kain: Not...quite. Lowry: Oh good, I thought I'd be talking to myself as long as you decided to sit around. Kain: Did I interrupt something? Lowry: Well actually, yes. You see, I was... Kain: Good. I figured you could take some time out of your busy day, talking to "talent" like Brian Collins or Jay Moore. At least now you'll have the opportunity to question someone who isn't, A) a complete moron; or
B) a waste of television time. Now, you get to talk to not only the top ranked contender in the NeWF, but also the only man dead-locked for a
Television Title shot.
Lowry: Where'd this sudden flood of arrogance come from? (Pause.) Kain: David...ask me something of worth. Lowry: Hey, you came to me, Nicklaus. All right, what's this with Sean
Cage? A dispute over something that happened in Mid-Western Championship
Wrestling? Why not let it die?
Kain: (chuckling) You know, pride is one of the Seven Deadly Sins. I guess
I developed a bad case of it. Kash was right, these people don't know a damn thing about MCW. There's no use in talking about the whole history of my career there. However, when Sean Cage...the "Superstar"...opted to come out and brag about his past conquests, rather than "letting it die," it lit me up. They didn't even make me leave it. I left with it in tow to the hotel. I figured they'd go on without it or fall apart, which they soon did. However, about a week later, some pimply-faced punk shows up and told me they needed the belt back. When inquired why, he said they were crowning a new champion.
Lowry: And you were angry? Kain: Anger, there's another. Yes, of course I was. I didn't find out until Monday that Sean Cage was the thief who stole the gold from me.
Well, now I get the opportunity for revenge. I'm going to take it and run with it. Lowry: Nicklaus, in the MCW, you never had a problem with Sean Cage... Kain: Who did I have a problem with? Besides a "feud" with Jim Lewis and a brief encounter with Tommy Wyldside, my MCW career was uneventful. I was overshadowed in the MCW. It took me a long time to figure out why, but I finally did.
Lowry: And why was that, Nicklaus? Kain: I was a Light Heavyweight. El Puerco, Damon Knight, Derek Diamond, all of you can most likely wrestle. All of you may be decent on a microphone. But you'll never be much of anything compared to the heavyweights. I realized that after I left MCW, so I built up.
Lowry: Now that's debatable. Enough about the MCW, what are you planning for the NeWF? Kain: I've got a few things in the works, but nothing that I'm willing to tell you. Right now, I'll concentrate on The Big Chill, Sean Cage, and whoever gets lucky enough to win that battle royale. Sean Cage, I've said what I'm going to say to you. Actions do not come without consequences. I know for a fact you'll be looking at least partially by The Big Chill to
Nicklaus Kain. Whether that means metaphorically or physically has yet to be seen...
(Pause.) Lowry: Nicklaus? Kain: Shut up. The Big Chill...TBC...the ACCW Heavyweight Champion. A very strong, very tough wrestler. Well, TBC, I have never held a
Heavyweight Title. I'm probably not as strong as you are, and probably not as tough as you are. But I swear to whatever god you believe in that if you defeat Sean Cage and enter into the three-way match for the Television
Title, you'll regret it. I will MAKE you regret it! And as for the other
26 men in the battle royale, the same goes for you.
Lowry: That sounds fine and dandy Nicklaus, but could you truly hold up in the ring? (Pause.) Lowry: Nicklaus? (Pause.) Lowry: Hey, the A/C's off! The... (The lights come back on. Kain isn't in view, but an envelop sits on
Lowry's desk. He opens it and pulls out a figure "8" made of paper. He turns it over in his hands and shakes his head slowly, tossing it in the garbage can. He stands and leaves the room.]
[Kain walks into view from behind Lowry's chair. He picks up the "8" out of the can and looks at it himself, a smirk crossing his face. He drops it back into the can and leaves. The can sets ablaze and eventually sends the sprinklers off.)
