Flash Frenzy (3/29/00) (Part 2 of 3)

1- "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams 2- "Superstar" Sean Cage 3- Predator 4- "Excessive Force" Eric Vanguard 5- El Puerco ============================================================== "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams ============================================================== [The scene....an amusement park fulls of rides, games and lots of people. As the hustle and bustle goes on, two people are visible. One is Steve
Jackson, manager and teacher of "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams. The one-time wrestler is wearing a nice suit and sunglasses, looking out of place with the relaxed crowd. The other figure is that of Toni Winter, Williams'
girlfriend and valet, whose gorgeous figure is clad out in a green sweater and khaki shorts with her hair pulled back in a ponytail. The camera goes in closer to listen to the two's conversation.]
Winter: Where's Joe? He promised... Jackson: He'll get here when he feels like it...the problem with that kid has always been discipline...I should talk to him about it.
[The two look at eachother for a second, and then break out laughing. After a while they stop]
Jackson: <wipes brow> That's a good one...how long do you think it would take Joe to fire me?
[Toni points to something near the entrance] Winter: Is that him? [The camera goes in to see an expensive sports car pull into the parking lot. The car screeches to a stop and the door quickly snaps open. Out of the vehicle steps none other than "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams, dressed in a designer red shirt and blue jean shorts. On his face is a pair of Oakley sunglasses. He walks up, gives the attendent his ticket, pushes him out of the way and then walks up to his accomplices.]
Winter: JOE!!! [Toni runs up and gives Williams a nice, big hug] Williams: Whatever... [Joe gently moves her aside] Williams: Why am I here? This place is half full of carny freaks and the rest are people whose career ambition is to become carny freaks!!
Jackson: I thought it would be a good chance to unwind before your big match with El Puerco...
Williams: Big match and El Puerco?!?!? Those two things don't go well together...the day I can't handle a nobody like him is the day (points to someone walking by) that jerk gets a decent haircut! Plus, you know I don't unwind with the common people.
[Joe smirks at Toni] Williams: And you DEFINATELY know how I like to have fun... [Toni smiles as Williams turns back to Jackson] Williams: Someone better get Hopper on the phone and tell him what he's wasting my time on...that lightweight jerk and...who else?
Jackson: Jack Mannon. Williams: Yeah, the "hero" coming out of retirement. Well, he should've stayed there because when THE MAN is done with him, he's going to be out of the business anyway. Because I'm younger...I'm tougher...and I'm cooler!
That's right, I'm the future of wrestling and this god-forsaken league and
I'm going to take my place by force if neccessary. That's right, Mannon.
I'm looking past you, I've got my eyes on the gold that belongs around my waist. And all those pieces of garbage that got into this TV title race better watch their back because that's where I'm going to be attacking from!
[Joe looks like he's about to keep going when a short man dressed in a loud yellow shirt with a camera draped around his neck interrupts him]
Man: Excuse me... [Joe turns around a wild look in his eyes] Williams: Who the?!?!? Man: You're "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams, right? [Williams looks at Jackson] Williams: Do you know this jerkass?!? [Jackson shrugs in response] Williams: Toni?!? Winter: Never seen him before... [Williams turns back to face the man] Williams: Ok...you have five seconds to explain why you're invading THE
MAN's personal space...
Man: I'm just a fan...I've been watching wrestling for a while..and you're the greatest... Williams: Damn straight, but again...why are you talking to me? Man: I'm like your biggest fan... [Williams turns to Jackson and Winter with a big, face smile on his face] Williams: <softly> Isn't this quaint?!? Me and my biggest fan...together at last. Well, maybe we can spendthe day together..we can ride on the rollar coasters and maybe later we can..I don't know..BAKE SOME COOKIES!!!!
WHY DO YOU THINK I CARE ABOUT YOU?!?! <Williams calms down a little> Williams: Let me ask you a question, how old are you? Man: 46... Williams: You see..that's way too old to be a fan of mine..in fact... Toni: That's 322 in dog years... [Williams turns around and stares at Toni with a look that's meant to say
"What ARE you talking about"?]
Toni: What? It is... [Joe keeps looking strangely at her] Winter: OK...I'll be quiet... Williams: Now as I was saying...46 is downright geriatric to me! There's only two cool old guys in this world..and that's Stevie J and my dad. But I have another question..
[Williams grabs the sleeve of the man and looks at it] William: Do you shop at the hottest shops in town?!? Can you afford to buy the most expensive shirts money can buy?!? In fact..you buy your shirts at
K-Mart don't you?!? Off the clearance racks?
[The man nods] Williams: You're hair is not only falling out, it looks stupid anyway! Did you get a hobo to cut it for you?!? I bet you'd like to drive a fancy car, wouldn't you?
[Again..the blushing man nods] Williams: But what do you drive? Man: Well...uh... Williams: Spit it out! Man: A used Geo... [Williams laughs] Williams: You actually believe a loser like you can be my number one fan? My fans are all out on the town getting blasted, having fun, and blowing money. You're hear with your six ugly children probably..trying to be cool by associating with me! Get out of my sight!
[Williams turns around and grabs Toni's cotton candy smashing on the man's head before pushing him out of the view of the camera]
William: <shaking his head> Jerkass... Jackson: Uhh... William: You were right, Steve..this has turned out to be a great day after all. [Williams drapes his arms around Toni and Steve as the three set out to enjoy the day] ============================================================== "Superstar" Sean Cage ============================================================== [The scene opens with a woman standing at a podium. The podium has the

Mr. Cage will be out shortly to answer a you questions but first i want to remind you that Mr. Cage has called this conference to address a few issues going on out of the ring. Im sure he will indeed answer questions inside the ring as well.
[Kevin Kash comes out followed by Chris Hopper and two other NeWF executive members, one of them whispers something to the woman.]
Woman: Ladies and Gentlemen, accomponied by Miss Keri, the superstar Sean
Cage. [The woman goes and sits next to Chris while Keri sits down at the seat next to the podium while Sean steps up to the podium)
Sean: Thanks for coming. Recently the rumors have been flying about the reasons why i was hired by the NeWF. Some of the wrestlers backstage have been saying that the only reason was because of my association with Chris
Hopper. True outside the NeWF me and Chris are great friends. But while competing inside the NeWF Mr. Hopper and I are nothing but business.
(Sean takes a sim of water as Chris Hopper begins to speak) Chris: Yes me and Sean are good friends outside the ring but i assure that they only reason I approached him to wrestle here in the NeWF was because he is a young talented wrestler who has extreme potential. He will be subject to the same rules and regulations that the other wrestlers will be held too. And with Kevin Kash at the reign he will be the one to enforce rules.
Kevin: Which is why as commishner of the NeWF I am fining Mr. Cage $10,000 for missing a mandatory flash show last week. It was made very clear that all wrestlers were to compete in the show. And Sean's failure to participate isn't going to go unpunished.
Sean: (doesn't know what to say) uh..... Keri: Sean will pay the fine and adhere to all the rules of the NeWF and its executive commitee....including paying this fine.
Sean: oh yeah i guess i have to be treated fairly in the NeWF. I guess i will take a few questions.
Reporter #1: Talk abut your opponent this weekend. Sean: Well Chaos is a man who i don't know anything about. I seen him compete monday but that was about it. It is going to be an interesting test this weekend when i step into the ring with him. And without looking past him i am going to use him as a tune up for The Big Chill.
Reporter #2: tell us about the Big Chill. Sean: Well I have to concentrate on Franklin but I seen the Chill back in the ACCW. He is a great athlete, but I do believe that I can beat him.
(Keri whispers something in his ear) Sean: Well i am informed by my keri here that we have a charity event that we must go to. Me and Dean Cain will attempt to defend our title as 2 on 2 celebrity champions at the TSC Celbrity basketball challenge. All prosedes will go to the Keep kids off the streets fondation.
(Keri and Sean get up and leave) Woman: I want to thank you all for coming and hopefully this conference as been informative to you. Thanks for coming.
[Fade to black.] ============================================================== Predator ============================================================== [Scene opens inside a clocktower. A fire burns in the corner with several homeless people surrounding it. It is obvious the clocktower hasn't been in use for years. Rats scurry across the ground and spider's webs are more plentiful than people. Crows nest all over the tower and Predator stands in the center with a ciggarette in his hand, puffing on it intensly. A small baby Crow lies in his hand, screeching until he covers it's mouth.]
Predator: As much as i hate associating with you.... people, I decided to finally speak to address a few... rumors. It's been said by a few people that I'm... evil. Well, As you can see by how quiet this little bird is in my hand, I can be as gental as please. But also know that I can be as cruel as I want. many people tell me they think I'm all talk. your probobly saying, "No one ever says that". All I can tell you is the USED to. But to prove to anyone who is still doubtful of my seriousness, I'm going to CLEAN this tower.
[Predator twists the baby crows neck until it's head pops off with a horrifying sound. The Crows are driven ino a frenzy as the look for a window. Predator grabs one of the screaming homeless people and throws them through the clocks face to the ground below. One homeless man grabs a pipe and attempts to hit Predator. Wuickly dodging, them man soon joins his buddy outside fade out as Predator jumps out th same way screamng: Beat that Mr. Clean!]
============================================================== "Excessive Force" Eric Vanguard ============================================================== [The camera fades in to a shot of a large sign hanging over a store window reading "XXX Adult Entertainment." Next to the gaudy mixture of neon pink and powder blue is an image of a fully nude woman repeatedly bending over, her smile inviting the many passers by. As the camera lowers a little more you see a large plate glass window, encased in steel bars with a sign reading "Adult Books Inside." Suddenly the door to the store swings open and a familiar face comes walking out with a stack of magazines wrapped in plain brown paper - it is "Excessive Force" Eric Vanguard. He opens up to the top magazine that shows a woman sitting in an oversized champagne glass, her most private of area's concealed by the bubbles of her drink. A smirk crosses the face of the prison guard as he lowers his trademark shades to take in her beauty.]
Eric Vanguard: "Will you take a look at this whore?!? I can hardly believe that a woman with such attractive face, and a beautiful body would lower herself to pose for such a trashy magazine! It turns my stomach to think that this could be somebody's girlfriend, sister, or even mother for that sake. THIS is what's wrong with America!"
[Slowly the former prison guard turns the corner and begins to walk down the street, as vagrants, bums, and perverts walk past him ducking into various shops and showrooms.]
Eric Vanguard: "The American Culture is a society rich in pornography, sex, and filth! You can't turn to even a cable channel without the fear of seeing somebody like Dennis Frantz' bare ass. Hell, people can get a charge out of ANYTHING! Back in The Cellblock, I actually watched a prisoner masturbate to a documentary of breast enlargement. It was probably one of the most disturbing visuals to pass through my mind. When you walk the halls of The California State Penitentiary you meet murders, people that have slain countless people without a second thought, you meet white collar criminals, that have embezzled thousands upon thousands of dollars from major corporations, but nothing makes my stomach turn worse than a sex offender. It is because of these perverts and the sexual market that caters to them that our society is on a steady downward spiral."
[Vanguard stops for a minute in front of a "Peep Show 10 Cents" Sign, and flips to another magazine that shows a woman that isn't exactly a woman, due to an extra appendage dangling between her legs.]
Eric Vanguard: "How can people be proud to live in a society that puts millions of dollars into a sex filled escapade like "Eyes Wide Shut?!? Hell an Oscar was recently awarded to a movie that dealt with infidelity and pedophilia. Is there anything that won't send a warm tingle through the loins of America's youth? Is there anything else out there that can aid people in releasing their aggressions? I know I found mine!
[Vanguard takes another magazine out of the stack and flips to a wrestling magazine, the cover adorned by NeWF Fan Favorite "The Natural" Adam
Rogers.] Eric Vanguard: "I get my charge out of hurting the people that take pride in a society like this. I get my proverbial "rocks" off by taking the leg of an All-American Boy such as Jack Mannon, and twisting it so far behind his head that he looks like a pretzel. I wake up in the middle of the night in a sweat filled bed at the thought of crippling someone like a "Black
Jack" Baldwin - a man who proudly hails from "Sin City." And last night I sat in my easy chair and almost had an orgasm at the thought of breaking someone like "The Natural" Adam Rogers in two. So who am I to judge society? Who am I to point a finger at these people and call them perverts?
I am the LAW! I am Eric Vanguard! And I will write these wrongs even if I have to use…EXCESSIVE FORCE!!!"
[Vanguard takes one of the magazines and throws it out into the middle of the sidewalk. A small balding man in his middle to late forties bends over to pick it up as Vanguard raises his nightstick to bring it crashing down on his skull.]
[Fade To Black] ============================================================== El Puerco ============================================================== [Fade in. Dateline: Friday night, March 31st. The scene opens in the lobby of a Super 8 in Martinsville, Indiana. A young (and attractive!)
blonde woman, in her early twenties, is seen working behind the front desk. All of a sudden, the lobby door swings open. The woman's eyes light up as she looks over to see who it is…]
Woman: "Oh my God! It's YOU! You're...you're... EL PUERCO!" [From the entrance of the lobby, El Puerco walks on-camera, approaching the front desk. Aside from his trademark yellow and green mask, he's also wearing an old pair of Nike sneakers and a worn and torn brown trenchcoat that covers most of his body. He's carrying a small suitcase and there's a black knapsack slung over his shoulder. He grins at the young woman, nodding as he puts his suitcase and the knapsack down…]
El Puerco: "The one and only! Two hundred and twenty-three pounds of lucha libre-a-licious electricity in the flesh!"
[The woman smiles excitedly. She extends her hand out.] Woman: "Wow! I can't believe it's really YOU! My boyfriend and I are HUGE wrestling fans! We think you are so awesome! We used to LOVE you in the
TWL! We were so surprised when we saw you on that NeWF show! We haven't seen you in so long! We thought you retired!"
[El Puerco shakes her hand, still grinning.] El Puerco: "Nah. That's not gonna happen for a LONG time. It's still nice to see that I wasn't forgotten while I was gone."
Woman: "So, what brings you to Martinsville?" El Puerco: "I have a match tomorrow night. It's my big NeWF debut on 'X-Tremely Dangerous'." Woman: "Well, good luck. It'll be nice to see you in action again. Anyway, how can I help you?" El Puerco: "I'd like a room for two nights, preferably non-smoking." Woman: "Will you be paying with cash or credit?" [El Puerco reaches into his pocket to retrieve his Discover card. He holds it up and the young woman nods as she reaches over the counter to retrieve in from him. Then, she walks over to her computer to type in some information.]
Woman: "Okay. Tonight's a pretty packed night, sir, but Room 307 is free. I just have to run up to the third floor and see if it's cleaned. We're short-staffed tonight so please bear with me, sir. I apologize for the inconvenience."
[El Puerco shrugs, laughing.] El Puerco: "I'll take the room. It's no big deal. Take your time. I'm not in a hurry." [The woman nods, smiling.] Woman: "Great, sir. Your total comes to eighty dollars right on the dot." [She hands the credit card back to Puerco.] "Let me head on up to see if your room is ready." [El Puerco nods politely as the woman walks out from behind the counter to wait for the elevator. She presses the button. After a few seconds of waiting, the elevator doors open and she enters the elevator. As the elevator doors close, El Puerco turns to face the camera. The grin leaves his face as he begins to speak in a slightly more serious tone.]
El Puerco: "In this country, there are over forty million adults who are illiterate. They can't read. They can't write. Some of them can't even spell their own name. 'The Mighty Pig' thinks that's pretty sad."
[He shakes his head, looking directly into the camera.] "However, it's not as sad as the recent behavior of YOU, Joe Williams. As illiterate as those people are, they still have more common sense and more intelligence than you could ever DREAM of possessing."
[El Puerco cocks his head to the side. The tone in his voice becomes louder and angrier.] "You have the audacity to call 'The Mighty Pig' El Nobody? You think 'The
Mighty Pig' is some rinky-dink flyweight? You think you can just disrespect the most lucha libre-a-licious superstar in all of professional wrestling and get away with it? By any chance, did you ride the short bus to school when you were a kid? It sure sounds like it."
[He points to the camera.] "Listen, you damn gringo. For the next few minutes, 'The Mighty Pig' thinks it'd be a good idea for you to sit down, shut up, and listen very closely. You might learn something."
[El Puerco laughs.] "Judging from that third grade mentality of yours, I think this lesson is long overdue." [The tone in his voice becomes totally serious again as he points to himself.] "'The Mighty Pig' has held cruiserweight and light heavyweight championships all over the world. He has wrestled the best athletes this country has to offer… defeating them swiftly and with finesse in each and every match that he's been in! 'The Mighty Pig' can back up his claims of being the best with ACTUAL PROOF!"
[El Puerco throws his arms in the air excitedly.] "What proof do you have? Why should 'The Mighty Pig' believe that you are a serious threat to his success in the NeWF? Why should he admire you?
Hmm? Come on. Tell me. What proof do you have?" [He folds his arms across his chest as he continues speaking.] "NOTHING! You can't prove a damn thing, hombre. YOU are all talk and no action. You couldn't support your extravagant claims of being 'the best'
and 'the most talented' if your life depended on it." [El Puerco shakes his head.] "These are the FACTS, man, so pay close attention. 'The Mighty Pig' really doesn't care if you're handsome or dashing. He's doesn't give a rat's ass what women you date or what car you drive. He doesn't want to be anything like you."
[He pauses for a moment to collect his thoughts. After a few seconds of thought, he resumes speaking.]
"Why doesn't he want to be like you? Well, there are MANY reasons for that! First of all, he knows that the only kind of senorita you can get comes with her own puncture repair kit. And he knows damn well that you drive a '77 Dodge and that's not exactly what he'd call a 'fast car'. But, most importantly, he knows that, when it comes to getting the job done in the ring, you SUCK harder than Monica Lewinsky on President's Day."
[El Puerco points at the camera, yelling angrily.] "YOU wish YOU were 'THE MIGHTY PIG'! YOU wish you could achieve what 'The
Mighty Pig' has achieved over the years! YOU wish you had his LEGIONS of
DEVOTED FANS cheering you on whenever you wrestled! Face it, hombre. You
DREAM of being 'THE MIGHTY PIG'!"
[He snickers.] "Do ME a favor, kid." [The camera zooms in for a close-up of El Puerco.] "Muérdame, usted mono cerebro-muerto!" [A translation briefly appears on the bottom of the screen - "Bite me, you brain-dead monkey!". The camera zooms away as the elevator doors open.
The young woman from before comes walking out of the elevator. She approaches El Puerco, who turns around.]
Woman: "Okay, sir. Your room is ready. I'm so sorry about the wait." [El Puerco picks up his suitcase and his knapsack.] El Puerco: "Don't worry about it. Here's a little token of my appreciation." [He reaches into his coat pocket and pulls out a fifty dollar bill. He hands it to the young woman. Her eyes light up when she sees what it is.]
Woman: "THANK YOU SO MUCH, SIR!" [El Puerco walks over and enters the elevator. As the elevator doors close, he raises his fist in the air, looking over at the young woman.]
El Puerco: "Viva El Puerco!" [The elevator doors close. The young woman walks back behind the front desk. She shakes her head in disbelief as she looks back over at the elevator.]
Woman: "What a guy!" [Fade to festive green.]
