EULW Looking for Tag Teams and Singles Wrestlers

6 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello. I run an e-fed called EULW. It's an e-mail based fed that has been around since May of 1997, and we've got a few more good years left in us. The only problem is, most of our older talent has or is in the process of retiring from the game. What I'm looking for are creative people, who handle their character's well. This fed only requires one rp per week, and will deliver a well written card, each and every week. The regular site is being re-tooled, but I've started putting up a temporary site, complete with a Player's Guide and Application to join. Most sections are unfinished, but like I said....It's a temporary site. If you are interested, I urge you to visit.... http://geocities.com/EULW/official.com and look over the player's guide and app. Thanks!
