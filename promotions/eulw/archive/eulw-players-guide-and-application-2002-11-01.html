EULW Player's Guide and Application

About EULW 2. How to join 3. Waiting list 4. Cards 5. Flash Policy 6. Role Playing FAQ 7. Angles 8. Creating your character 9. Titles 10. Application 1. What is EULW? EULW stands for Extreme Ultra League Wrestling. It is an e-fed (E-mail or Electronic wrestling federation), created as a way for wrestling fans to live out their ambitions through characters on the internet. We encourage our members to participate with nearly every aspect of this league. Through an extensive process of trial and error which has persisted since the fed's inception in 1997, we've found that the key to longevity is through active member involvement. This means each and every constituent on our roster is expected to help out when ever possible. If you can write a match here or there or assist in any way, it would be greatly appreciated, thus insuring not only your position as a valued member, but also helps to sustain the security of the EULW's future. If you have any questions on how you could help out in EULW, please feel free to e-mail the "Fed-head" [name withheld] for further information. 2. How do I join? EULW is dedicated to providing a fun environment for it's members. Accepting too many applicants could hinder our ability to attend to the needs of those already in EULW, and that would be unacceptable. Does this mean we don't want your applications? No, of course not. Even if we are not currently accepting new members, we always like to keep an eye out for potential talent. Please feel free to submit your application at will. Of course keep this within reason. No one likes to have their mail box filled up with twenty apps from the same guy. With your completed application, you must also submit a sample role play to show that you do in fact know what you are doing. Please see the FLASH policy for further info on this subject. One final note. We try and review every application that comes across our desks. Please keep in mind that just because we don't reply within a few weeks, doesn't mean you will not be accepted. We accept new members when new members are needed. You may contact us every two weeks to check the status of your application to show that your are still interested, but any more often than that would be considered an annoyance. 3. Is there a waiting list? Some times. Of course, this is by no means a first come first serve style of list. We hold onto every application we get, and constantly review them when ever a spot opens on our roster. The character that best fits the fed at that particular time will get in. Of course if you are on the list, and you annoy any member of our staff, your odds of getting in will reduce significantly. Remember, the more thought you put into your character, the better chance you have of getting in. Strong role playing combined with a good character cannot be ignored for long. A good knowledge of what is going on in EULW at the time will greatly increase your chances of getting in aswell. Read our cards, learn our storylines, get to know our characters. This is all good advice, and I hope you consider it before submitting your applications. 4. What are the cards? This has varied from time to time. Our new line up includes one wrestling show, one flash show, and a weekly report. Our wrestling show is held on Saturday nights. It's called "One Extreme Saturday Night", and will usually feature seven or eight matches per week. For "OESN" wrestlers are allowed to submit short backstage pre-match promos which do count towards the outcome of your match. A few people per week will also be chosen to cut in-ring promos. This is usually reserved for a wrestler involved in a major angle to promote that particular storyline. There will also be a weekly report. It's purpose is to announce the matches for the weekly wrestling show. It will also give a run down of who's coming or going, title rankings, and just about every other bit of information we can cram into it. The report will be released on Mondays. Our flash show is called "Extreme Spotlight". This show will be set up like the old "WWF Spotlight Wrestling" show, or the last years of "Superstars of Wrestling". There will be reviews of the previous week's show, and previews of the show to come. Of course the main purpose for this show will be the flashes. This is where wrestlers go to get their points across. This show will take place on Thursdays, although the deadline for flashes is Wednesday. 5. Flash Policy FAQ 5.1 What is a flash? Simply put, a flash is a promo. It is a device used to get your wrestler over with the staff and the other members. In EULW flashes are very important, not only for winning your matches, but for painting a picture of who your character is, and what it is they want. 5.2 Is a good flash all it takes to win my match? Yes and no. Flashes are intended to get your character over, and when your character is over he will tend to win more of his matches. When it comes down to it, story lines help you win matches, but flashes decide who the story lines will favor. So in the long run, flashes win matches, but they won't win every match. 5.3 How often can i submit a flash? Usually twice a week. Once for the flash show, and a short pre-match promo for the wrestling show. Some times you will get the chance to do an in-ring aswell. Although it is only required that you flash once per week, our staff really does take notice to those who participate more often. 5.4 How long should a flash be? It doesn't matter. Just as long as it's not boring. A flash can be as long as one of Triple H's in-rings, or as short as one of those video packages the WWF/WWE does to hype up new wrestlers before they debut. 5.5 What are strats? Strategies. Writers in EULW don't know your character aswell as you do. Because of this, it helps to send in a strat to help the writer portray your wrestler more accurately. A strat can be as detailed or as vague as you like. Just as long as you jot down any spots you may want to see in your match. 5.6 Can a good strat win a match? It can. If you come up with a spot that the writers really like, you just may pick up the win. Of course long term success is decided by flashes, so i would advice you submit both a flash and a strat for each match. 6. Helpful FAQ for role playing. 6.1 What is kayfabe? Kayfabe is an old term used by industry insiders, which means "In character". In the old days, breaking kayfabe was the worst thing you could do in the wrestling world. Although kayfabe is not taken very seriously in "real" wrestling these days, it is an essential part of many respectable e-feds. As far as EULW is concerned, the WWE or any other real life wrestling promotions do not exist. Any mention of a real life wrestler or promotion will be edited out of your flash, so don't bother mentioning them to begin with. 6.2 Can I use profanity? You can say any thing that wouldn't get bleeped on commercial television. Other than that, please bleep out those four letter words that would get the real guys fined by the FCC. 6.3 Can I use profanity on pay-per-view? You got me there. Yeah, i suppose you could use profanity on pay-per-view. Keep in mind though, that I am not a huge fan of gratuitous profanity, and if I feel your use of this language or behavior is not in the best interest of the story line, It may not get used. 6.4 Can I use other people's characters in my role plays? Not without the permission of the person who's character you are using. If you are in a tag team with another member, try getting together with that person and writing a joint role play. If you intentionally try and make some one elses character look bad in your role play, your rp will not be added to the flash show, and you will probably lose your match. 7. Angles Who comes up with the angles in EULW? Everyone in EULW is expected to contribute in this department. A lot of the story lines will come from the EULW staff, but members are more than welcome to contribute to the process. If you have an idea for an angle you'd like for your character, please contact a member of the staff, and they will be more than happy to work with you on making it happen. Can I challenge any one I want at any time? Nope. If you want to challenge some one, contact a member of the staff and they will try and make it happen. If the person you want to challenge is already in a program with some one else, you are just plum out of luck. Oh, and please don't ask to challenge for a title. The bookers will tell you when you are up for a title shot. If a champion chooses to pick a fight with you, then that's a different story. Should a champion decide to cost you a match via run in, or some thing along those lines, then you may ask one of our bookers for the match. Other than that, you just have to wait your turn. Can I request a gimmick match? You can ask, but most of the time the answer will be no. In the old days of EULW, gimmick matches were all to common. Now days, it's more about actual wrestling. Gimmick matches will be reserved for when a feud is so intense, that it can only be settled in a more extreme type of match. Tips on creating a character. Before you sit down to draw up your application, take a moment to look over our current roster. Read some of the flashes and cards. Give yourself a good idea of what the fed has, and what the fed could use. Then, once you figure out what EULW is missing, design a character that fills that void. I don't want to see obvious rip offs of real life wrestlers. No Stone Colds, no Rocks, no Undertakers. If i get any applications for any rip off characters, i will delete them right then and there. Also, no more clowns! The clown is the single worst gimmick of all time. I would rather let a guy who dresses up like a robot into the EULW than a freaking clown! Be careful with the names of your character's too. If your character even shares a name with an indy wrestler, and i catch it, I will not grant you entry to EULW. Theme music can also play a role in not getting into EULW. I know this sounds a tad eccentric, but please refrain from choosing "over used" theme music. A lot of people pick what ever song is popular at the time, and some times I end up with two or three people wanting to use the same theme music. Your theme music should become synonymous with your character, and this can not happen if you share it with some one else. Tag team partners and stable mates excluded. EULW Titles Through out the course of the past half decade, the EULW has sanctioned many titles. However, at the present time we only sanction two titles. The Tag Team championship, and the EULW Heavyweight championship. A third title may be added at a later date, but for now I would rather focus on those two. Defunct Titles include....
