EULW Handbook (29 December 1999)

29 December 1999

Section 4- Role Playing Section 5- EULW Contracts Section 6- Making a Character Section 7- The effect of Heat in EULW Section 8- EULW Championships Section 9- Cards & PPVs Section 10- EULW Application Introduction- Founded in May of 1997 as Ultra League Wrestling, this e-fed has left a some what unique mark in the world of e-mail wrestling. The fed later re-named Extreme Ultra League Wrestling [EULW] in December of 98, to promote its newly aquired "Hardcore" attitude, then went on to lead the way as one of the most original AOL based feds of the year. Despite the success we indured in
98, the year 1999 would bring great misfortune, time and time again. Although angle wise, it was still on top of its game, the federation was still plagued by managment problems, which eventualy lead to its bitter down fall. Or so we thought. EULW has now opened its doors, and the monster breaths yet again. With the arrival of the new mallenium, Extreme Ultra League Wrestling is ready to rise again, and take on all comers, in a battle to win back the agulations we once held. The Year 2000, much like 98, belongs to the EULW.
Office- Behind the angles and the bone crushing matches, you'll find a deticated group of bookers, writers, and Executives who make it all possible.
Just like the Real life wrestling promotions, EULW has a fully functioning Office, to keep the fed on the cutting edge of the ewrestling world. The Office is headed by myself, [name withheld], also known as Eddie De'Munk. I come up with most of the angle concepts, and write a large number of the matches, however, i couldnt possible run the e-fed of the future alone. That is why i am always inlisting responsible writers and other staff to help me in my quest for e-fed greatness.
Rules & Regulations- In EULW we expect all members to behave in a responsible & profesional manner. We do not tolerate SPAM mail, TOSing, or any other activity which could make EULW less enjoyable for other members. RPs are to be sent by the correct deadline, and must obey the requirements which are listed in the
Role Playing section below. Also, there will be no name calling or low blows, towards other members while out of character. Failure to comply will result in suspension, jobbing, or termination of your EULW contract. Any violations in your contract could also lead to termination. See the contract section of this application for further details.
Role Playing- EULW is an AOL based fed, there fore it utilizes an open RP system, as opposed to the closed flash net style. This allows the members to view their opponents rps, before their rebuttle is made. Some problems have been known to accure in this system, such as spam mail, TOSing, and rp overkill, how ever EULW has banned all of these things to insure our players an enjoyable, yet non time consuming gaming experiance. Our RP limit is 3 rps per weekly card, and 5 for ppv matches. In the event of a tag team match, the best
3 or 5 between members will be counted. The next and probably the most common problem with the open rp system is spam mail. Any one who sends spam mail, meaning mail which is not an RP, or a letter which is authorized by EULW staff, is subject to removal from the list. You may get off with a warning the 1st time, depending on the mail, and your role in the league, however, no one is above the law in EULW. Now this brings me to TOSing. Just dont do it.
E-wrestling is a game, its not some thing to lose your account over. The open rp system is based alot on trust, so please dont abuse this process.
Another important part of EULW's rp system is KAYFABE, wrestling's code of silence. This means, at no time can you admit that this is only an e-fed in an rp. You cant mention any real wrestlers, promotions, or personalities, because in this world, those things dont exist. Never use the word RP in a role play, and most of all, never come out of character. A large part of winning a match, is being able to stay in character in your role plays. Dont say things that your character wouldnt say, and be sure proof read your role plays for these kind of mistakes atleast once. Following all of the above will make your EULW career a much more successful one.
Your EULW Contract- Each member starts off with a 1 month contract, which assures you a chance to leave at any time after a four week period with out the risk of being jobbed or black listed. Should you leave before the contract is up, you may be expected to JOB any matches in which you were already scheduled to compete. Should you leave on good terms, you will not be black listed from
EULW, and will be welcome to return at a later date. After 1 month, each character's contract will be up for review, and a more lengthier deal will be provided. The longer you stay with EULW, the more oppertunities you will be given. How ever, depending on your value to this league, you may be able to sign better deal than most after the expiration of your 1st contract.
The one month rookie contract includes: Atleast one on card appearance every two weeks, depending on your rping. It also starts you off with a intermediate level angle, which can be improved to midcard with a show of effort, creativity, and ability. As an EULW Rookie, you will not have all the same oppertunities as a headliner, how ever, you will be given every chance to improve your status durring your one month trial period. What you do in your
1st month, could determine how well you do for the rest of your EULW career. Durring your 1 month trial, you may be cut at any time, how ever, after your new contract is signed, management cannot fire you with out reasonable cause, meaning a breach of contract. A breach of contract could include spam mail, failing to role play for matches, and failure to comply with stipulations depicted in your existing contract. Title oppertunities will be distributed according to your wrestlers status within the fed. Midcard titles obtained within your 1st month will help improve your chances of recieving a bigger contract. So please keep in mind, no title is too small to boost your career.
Just keep rping at your top level, and you will do fine. Making a Character- In EULW we like to have a wide selection of character types and personalities. What we dont like to see are "Rip Offs" of existing wrestlers, so please dont call your character any thing like "Ice Cold" Steve
Texas, or "Heart Break Kid" Shane Mcmicheals. That will get you no where in EULW. A good character is one of the many keys you will need to unlock the door of success in Extreme Ultra League Wrestling. So what you will want to do is look around and see what we already have. Then you will brain storm about what you think we could use. If there are a ton of Dark Characters, odds are, we dont need another. If there are no tag teams, then maybe it would be a good idea to have some one in that division. Once you have your character, you'll need to come up with a matching personality for him. I dont want any dark characters speaking like Steve Austin, or vise versa. That kind of role playing is for the kind of feds you'll find in any public chat room. Your character must show depth and creativity, if he is to do well in this league. If your character does not do well at 1st, it is not always a good idea to scrap the character. Some times a gimmick change is all that is required. For example,
"Rat Boy" isnt doing too well in his current gimmick as a side show freak turned wrestler. He's got the rps, but they just arent working with the current creative direction of the league. So Rat Boy decides to snap and claim that he is being forced by the company to parade himself around as a novalty wrestler.
This establishes him as some what of a "Shooter". An over the edge type character, whom cant be trusted by the promoters. All of a sudden, Rat Boy has a little heat. He may or may not want to change his name, but the important thing is that he drew some interest into a character which otherwise had none.
This is just one of the ways to turn a failing character, into a successful one. Another way is to try another division. If you arent doing well as a singles wrestler, then try the tag team division for a while. Several EULW world champions paid their dues for months, even years in the tag team ranks.
They didnt quite draw enough heat in their early days, so they went into the tag team division for a while, where they had alot of success. Then the character becomes familiar to the promoter and other members, and is ready for the main events. So when you are making your character, put a good deal of thought into him, and be prepared for the long, hard road which is ahead of you.
The Effect of Heat in EULW- With out a doubt, the most important thing in EULW, is heat. Heat is what turns a good rper, into a main eventer. With out heat, you can be the best role player in the fed, and still never get noticed, so please be open to any suggestions or angles that the EULW office has for you.
They may not seem like the best thing for your character at the time, how ever it will atleast draw some attention to your character, which inturn will build heat. One question we get alot at EULW is, can some one win a match with heat alone? Well the answer to that is probably not. Role Plays still determine the victory, but Heat determines the oppertunities. Heat can prove to be the tye breaker in most cases, however no one will win a match that they flat out lost in rps, due to heat. The EULW staff has always attempted to judge matches fairly, and we will continue this tradition. But heat will always play an important role in the EULW Revolution.
EULW Championships- Titles play a major role in EULW. Any one who holds one of our championship belts is reguarded as being one of the top stars, despite the value of the title. In short, a TV champion still has more stroke than the #1 contender for the World title, simply because he actualy has a belt.
World Heavy Weight Title- Only the best of the best will even get a crack at this title. Some EULW superstars have been in the fed for years and still never earned a title shot. This title is reserved for the biggest and most important angles, therefore heat does play a large role in where the title goes.
United States Title- 2nd only to the world title, the United States Champion has alot of stroke in this promotion. He is often considered the uncrowned #1 contender for the world championship, and any one who holds this belt long enough, is usualy granted his fair shot. As US champion, one is expected to defend his title on atleast one weekly card a month, aswell as each pay-per-view, unless decided otherwise by the Office.
Inter-Regional Title- The Newest of the EULW titles, this belt is to be defended quite often, and is also a huge stepping stone to the world championship. This belt will give a star main event exposure on several weekly cards.
World Television Title- Although this belt has a smaller pay-per-view spot, it is a huge deal on weekly TV. Whom ever has the title is garanteed more TV matches than any one else in the league.
World Tag Team Titles- Just as important as the World Heavy Weight title, the tag team titles are main event status belts. Unlike most feds, EULW tag team wrestlers actualy do move up in title contention, after paying their dues in this rough and tough division.
Cards & PPVs- EULW has atleast one card a week. This is usualy "One Extreme Friday Night", although from time to time a 2nd show called "Static" is aired on Tuesdays. "OEFN" will feature angle building matches, segments, and interviews, plus big time main event and midcard bouts. "Static" will usualy feature a TV or Inter-Regional title Main Event, with a few intermediate or rookie matches aswell. As for pay-per-view, EULW will air one every other month. The ppvs feature only the top angles and superstars, and getting on one is a very big deal.
