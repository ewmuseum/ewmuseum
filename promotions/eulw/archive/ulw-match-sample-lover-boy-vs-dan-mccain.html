ULW Match Sample: Lover Boy vs. Dan McCain

29 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MAIN EVENT DAN McCAIN VS LOVER BOY {Best 2 out of 3 falls No DQ, world title match} (Announcer) This match is scheduled for the best 2 out of 3 falls, and it is for the world heavy weight championship. Introducing 1st, the challanger from miami florida, the former ULW extreme champion, and most charismatic superstar in ULW history, He weighs 238 pounds, ladies and gentlmen, here is LOVER BOY!!! (crowed erupts) <<<The fans go wild as Lover Boy makes his way to the ring for the 2nd time tonight. This time he is wearing black tights with silver hearts on it. He also has a blach and sliver vest much like his red one. Lover Boy is sporting a flashy new ward robe for this match. And he is driving the fans crazy as he dances to the ring. He finaly makes it to the ring, and then poses in the middle of the squared circle as fire works shoot from behind him. He then strips himself of his vest and tear away pants, and hands them to some one out side the ring. Lover Boy looks ready for the biggest match of his career.>>> (Announcer) And his opponent, The man who is known for his extreme and graphic nature. He is the winner of the 1997 War Rumble and former Intercontinental champion. Weighing 245 pounds, here is the ULW heavy weight champion of the world, Dan "The Extremist" McCain!!!! (crowed boos) <<<McCains music begins to play as he walks down the ramp way with his belt. He has an angry look on his face, as he slaps the hands of the fans away from him. He doesnt seem to want the fans to touch him, and he even goes as far as to spit on one of them. He then climbs into the ring and shows his belt off to the crowed. The fans continue to boo as McCain gives them the finger.>>> (Wells) This is the main event, and it is for all the marbles. Dan McCain and Lover Boy have had some epic battles in the pas, but this one should be the war to end all wars. We remember seeing these two men in a steel cage match back on Monday Mayhem a couple of months ago. We also saw them in the 4 way match at The War Rumble. And we know these two like to play dirty. The ref has explained the rules, and McCain spits in Lover Boy's face. Lover Boy smiles at him, and wait a minute!!! Lover Boy just slugged Dan McCain with a right hand. There is no Love Lost between these two men. (Sharp) Lover Boy is really taking it to McCain early in this match. If he keeps this up the 1st fall could go to him. (Rock) Yeah, but if he makes a mistake, McCain could capitalize and retain his title. And im sure that could happen. But i feel this is gonna be a great match no matter which way it goes. (Sharp) Thats true. This is the one that every ones been waiting for. This is truely the main event. And i know as a fact that it will be a block buster. (Wells) Lover Boy nails McCain with a belly to belly suplex. He then drop kicks him out of the ring. Lover Boy leaps up on the top rope and comes down with a corkscrew plongea. He then grabs McCain by the head and slams him into the ring post. This one is getting extreme already. And im suprised Lover Boy still has any fight left in him after his match with Avalanche. (Sharp) Well his match with Avalanche was nothing short of a classic, but this one could possibly top it. Dan McCain and Lover Boy have been feuding for months, and this may be their final battle. (Wells) They have been bitter enemies for a very long time. Both men have stuck it out in ULW for a long time, and now they are main eventers. I cant think of two men who deserve that title any more than Lover Boy or Dan McCain. Lover Boy kicks McCain in the gut, and then scoops him up for a body slam. He drops him down on the cement, and then drags him back to his feet. Lover Boy now attempts to nail McCain with a neck breaker, but McCain counters it and delivers a neck breaker to Lover Boy. McCain grabs a chair and smashes it over Lover Boy's head. He then puts it on the ground and pile drives the challanger onto it. McCain now slings Lover Boy against the gaurd rail. He then charges at him, but Lover Boy ducks and dumps McCain into the crowed. Lover Boy then leaps up to the top rope and dives out into the crowed, landing on top of Dan McCain. The world champion falls and hits his head on the floor, and the fans are moving out of the way in a real hurry. Lover Boy grabs a chair and swings it at Dan McCain, but McCain kicks it back in his face. He then catches Lover Boy with a thumb to the eyes, followed by a belly to belly suplex onto the chair. McCain smashes Lover Boys head into the gaurd rail. He then drags Lover Boy further into the crowed. He grabs a beer trey from a vender and hits Lover Boy with it, and then bulldogs him onto a chair. Now he grabs a ladder from behind the ramp way, and clubs Lover Boy with it. Lover Boy tries to come back, but McCain shoves a plastic cup down his throat. He then places Lover Boy's head between the joints on the ladder. He tries to snap his neck, but Lover Boy moves out of the way. McCain smashes into the ladder and falls to the floor. Lover Boy then places McCain's head on the chair. He climbs the ladder and leaps off with an elbow drop!!! WOW!!! What a move!!! Lover Boy just dropped and elbow off the top of the ladder!!! (Sharp) This match has gotten brutal as hell, and we havent even seen the 1st fall yet. I dont know if these guys will ever be able to wrestle again after this match. But the fans are getting one hell of a show. (Rock) Yes they are. I dont think ive ever seen a match like this in my life. These guys want to win at all cost. And im sure the man who does eventualy come out on top will be a great world champion. And right now we can see who the fans are rooting for. Lover Boy is by far the fan favorite. (Reed) He and Reign are the most popular Athelets in ULW, and im pretty sure that Reign has a title shot coming up in the near future. And what a match that would be. But i dont see how it could get any better than the one we are seeing right now. This has got to be the match of the century. (Sharp) It has defenatly topped the Lover Boy vs Crypt Keeper world title match at our last ppv. And this one still have a long way to go. But i cant help thinking that the man who wins the 1st fall will win this match. These two men have taking to much out of each other already, so the two falls could very well be scored back to back in a matter of minutes. (Rock) Thats a very good point. And as we watch these two tear each others brains out, it looks more and more like this match will be won in 2 back to back falls, and not spread out over three falls. But it is just too early to tell. (Wells) McCain is busted wide open, and the blood continues to poor out of his head. Lover Boy dumps him over the gaurd rail, and the two men fight all the way down the ramp way. Lover Boy attempts a drop kick, but McCain moves and Lover Boy plants him self on the steel ramp. McCain now drags Lover Boy back to the ring. He grabs the time keepers table and talses it into the ring aswell. Now he goes over to the mexican broadcast table, and he throws it into the ring!!! This could be a set up for the power bomb onto two tables. No one has ever gotten up from it, and that move can only be done in no DQ style matches. McCain gets in the ring and slams Lover Boy's head into the table. He then picks one table up and drops it onto his face. Lover Boy looks to be in intense pain. I dont know how much more of this kinda punishment Lover Boy can take. Dan McCain now stacks the tables up, but Lover Boy comes back with a drop kick, and knocks McCain into the tables. Lover Boy now pulls McCain up on top of the tables and ddts him!!! Dan McCain's blood oozes all over the table, as Lover Boy attempts to pin him on it. 1-2- but McCain manages to get his shoulder up. Lover Boy now picks McCain up and suplexes him off of the tables. Putting the tables in the ring has really back fired on McCain. And right now Lover Boy is the one using the tables to his advantage. (Rock) Lover Boy may be a former extreme champion, but he may still be the most extreme man in wrestling. But i guess you could say the same thing about Dan McCain. He has never held the extreme title, but he is a former IC champion, and current world champion. So that explains why this match is gonna be a gore fest. (Reed) Gonna be??? This has been a gore fest since the start of the match. We are seeing one of the greatest title matches in history. Not just ULW history, but ever. No other promotion has seen this kind of action. And i mean no body. (Wells) I would agree with that, this is a great match. The word great doesnt even do it justice. Right now Lover Boy is doing a number on Dan McCain. Lover Boy lays McCain on the table. He then goes to the top rope and dives down with an elbow!!! McCain is in deep trouble at the moment. Lover Boy now reaches under the ring and pulls out a rubber hose!!! What is that doing under there??? He crawls back in the ring with the hose and wraps it around Lover Boy's throat. He then pushes him off the table and out of the ring!!! McCain is being hung over the top rope!!! Dan McCain pulls him self back to his feet and nails Lover Boy with an elbow to the ribbs. He then pulls him out of the ring. The two men are going at it on the floor again, and it looks like McCain has regained control of this match. McCain reaches under the ring and finds a pipe wrench. He clubs Lover Boy with it and throws him back into the ring. McCain now sets Lover Boy up on the tables, and power bombs him through both of them. He then hooks the leg and gets the 1-2-3!!! Dan McCain has won the 1st fall. I cant believe it!!! (Announcer) Dan McCain has won the 1st fall!!! (crowed boos) (Wells) Dan McCain now has a huge advantage over Lover Boy. I dont know if Lover Boy can come back from the beating he has just indored. But never sell him short, he is a survivor. Just one month ago his career was almost ended when he suffered a devistating lost against the Crypt Keeper at The War Rumble ppv. But now he is back in the challanger's role, and he has put up one hell of a fight so far. Dan McCain is one fall ahead of Lover Boy, and now we begin our 2nd round. McCain scoops Lover Boy up off the broken peices of the table, and spikes him down with a ddt onto a board. He then picks up one of the table legs and beats the challanger un mercifuly. Now McCain grabs a chair. He places the chair over Lover Boy's face. He now goes up to the top rope. He leaps down and smashes the chair into the young fan favorites skull. The blood is oozing from Lover Boy's head. Dan McCain is now screaming for a micriphone. What the hell is this all about??? The announcer hands him the mic, and now McCain begins to speak. (Dan McCain) What the hell is this??? I thought i was gonna get a challange for my world title!!! All you little fagots in the crowed can stop chanting "paper champion" or im gonna come out there and kick each and every one of your little punk asses. I am the heavy weight champion of the world. I am the man who won the 1997 War Rumble!!! I defeated 49 other men for that belt, and you people want to call me a paper champion??? Well you can all kiss my ass, because when i am done with this peice of trash Loser Boy, i am gonna take my world championship belt, and im gonna burn it!!! Thats right, i am going to burn the ULW world heavy weight title, because thays how i feel about ULW, Todd Wells, and all of you. You are just a bunch of cry baby fagots who need your asses kicked. <<<McCain smashes the mic over Lover Boys head, and then begins to pound away on him.>>> (Wells) Oh my god!!! All i can say is i hope Lover Boy reaches deep in side and comes out with some thing that can put Dan McCain away. But right now Dan McCain is destroying Lover Boy. He now has Lover Boy set up on the ropes. McCain then sets the chair up in the ring and goes up to the top rope with Lover Boy. He then bulldogs Lover Boy from the top rope onto the chair!!! What a move!!! Dan McCain is one damn extreme wrestler. Love him or hate him, he is indeed the Extremist. But Lover Boy has wrestled two matches today. He lost the extreme title tonight against Avalanche thanks to Disaster, and he has also lost the 1st fall of this match. (Sharp) Thats right, and now he is having to fight an up hill battle against the current world heavy weight champion. But the statment that Dan McCain just made could spark a fire inside Lover Boy. We all no that Lover Boy is the patriot of ULW. He is a strong believer in this league and would die before letting some one like McCain burn our world title belt. So i am defenatly pulling for Lover Boy. (Rebel) I could care less about Lover Boy, but that world title belt has alot of history behind it, and i really dont want to see Dan McCain burn it. Im sure he would be fired from ULW after pulling some thing like that. Hell, he is rumored to be leaving ULW for ERUW any way, so maybe he is serious about burning that belt. (Rock) Well i agree with you for once. And this is the 1st match you have called Mr.Rebel since your suspention ended. So maybe you have changed your ways. (Rebel) I wouldnt count on that. And by the way Bobby, what the hell is that you have been snorting all through the pay per view??? Are you doing some thing ileagle over there??? (Rock) No, of course not!!! Its some thing my Doctor gave me. Yeah, my doctor. You know, for head aches and stuff like that? Yeah, thats it. Head aches. Its Head ache powder. Yeah. (Wells) Lets not even get into that right now. We are seeing a performance of a life time by these two men. They are with out a doubt the two biggest stars in ULW today, and they are going at it one on one in a best 2 out of 3 falls No DQ match. This one has been sold out for weeks, and it is now come to the main event. And we have already seen fall number one go to Dan McCain. He just may retain his world title. Dan McCain now goes to the top rope. He leaps off and nails Lover Boy with a double axe handle. Lover Boy then rolls out of the ring, but the world champion follows him. McCain then rams Lover Boy's head into the steel gaurd rail. He then does it again. Lover Boy attempts to fight back, but McCain stays on the offense. He then slings Lover Boy into the steel steps. Lover Boy is a bloody mess right now, and he has a long way to go if he wants to beat McCain for the title. Dan McCain picks up the top part of the steps and he clobbers Lover Boy with them. Hen then goes into the crowed and grabs a ladder from the back of the ramp way. He then walks back through the crowed and sets up the ladder in the fourth row. He begins to climb it, but Lover Boy quickly scales up the ladder, and begins to exchange blows with McCain. Lover Boy then grabs McCain and hip talses him over the gaurd rail and onto the time keepers table!!! What a throw on Lover Boy's part. Lover Boy now stands on top of the ladder. Oh no, he isnt...he couldnt...yes!!! He leaps off the ladder and flys 4 rows over the crowed and nails Dan McCain with a flying body tackle!!! Lover Boy and McCain are now both on the ground. I dont know how either of them could walk away from that, but some one has to win this match. And both of these guys want to do just that. Dan McCain is the 1st one up. He grabs the ULW world heavy weight belt and smashes it over Lover Boy's face. His blood is now all over the belt. Thats pretty discusting. McCain now goes up to the apron and leaps down onto Lover Boy with a leg drop. He then slams Lover Boy back into the ring. McCain sets Lover Boy up on the top rope for a superplex, but lover boy nails him in the face with a strong right hand and then reverses the hold into his finishing move "fall for love"!!!! He nailed it!!! Dan McCain is out cold!!! Lover Boy now covers McCain. 1-2-3!!! YES, Lover Boy has won the 2nd fall!!! (Announcer) Lover Boy has won the 2nd fall!!! (crowed cheers) (Reed) Just when we though Lover Boy was finished, he pulled his finishing move and scored a pin fall over the world heavy weight champion. This guy is amazing. He has more heart than any wrestler i have ever seen in my life. And its not easy to get a compliment out of me. (Sharp) Lover Boy may have just won the 1st fall, be he is in real bad shape. I just hope he did enough damage to McCain with that Fall for Love move of his. Because Lover Boy may not have it in him to do it again. (Wells) Thats a good point, but round 3 has just begun, and Lover Boy wastes no time. He goes right for the cover on the unconsious McCain, the ref counts. 1-2-3...no wait, McCain kicked out!!! I cant believe this match. It has gone back and forth so many times and both men keep coming back. Lover Boy is bleeding from his leg aswell as his face. He is wearing diferant tights from his earlier match, but you can see the blood seeping through them. He looks like he has been in a car accident. As for McCain. His face looks like bloody ham burger meat. These two men are defenatly giving it their all, but i think some one might wanna quit before this thing gets too dangerous. Lover Boy pulls McCain to his feet and attempts to suplex him, but McCain blocks it. He then nails Lover Boy with a spine Buster!!! (Sharp) This match has to be the most extreme match in ULW history. We have seen barbed wire cage matches, and four way tag team wars, but never any thing like this. The rules may not be that strange. But these two men have done every thing you can do in that squared circle. This is a best 2 out of 3 falls no DQ match, and a damn good one at that. Any one who says other wise is a damn fool. (Wells) You're right on that one. Dan McCain has dominated this match for the most part, but Lover Boy has done some of the most amazing moves i have ever seen. He has also managed to even the score to one fall each. And that is an important factor in this match. Because any one can win it from this point on. All they have to do is come off with a quick move and this match is theirs. Both men are fighting for their lives. This is personal. These two men have faced each other many times before. They have both come off with wins over each other. And back in June Lover Boy almost ended Dan McCains career. But McCain returned the favor a month later on the Slaughter. These two men were also a huge factor in the 4 way match at the War Rumble. Lover Boy won that match and ended up facing the Crypt Keeper in a losing effort for the world title in that same night. This is Lover Boy's 2nd world title shot, and it just may be his last if he doesnt get the win here. But right now Dan McCain is in control and it doesnt look good for Lover Boy. Dan McCain now goes back out side the ring. He grabs the ladder and pulls it into the ring. He then climbs to the top of the ladder and rides it down into Lover Boy. McCain then places the ladder on Lover Boy's throat while he climbs up it. He then leaps off again and smashes Lover Boy's face with an elbow drop. McCain then goes for another elbow, but Lover Boy moves and McCain plants him self into the ring!!! Lover Boy then quickly climbs to the top of the ladder, but McCain rams the ladder and knocks it and Lover Boy over the top rope and out of the ring. Lover Boy flys into the crowed and hits his head on a fans chair. McCain then pulls another table back into the ring. Now he goes out into the crowed and drags Lover Boy to the ring. He places Lover Boy on the top rope, and then power bombs him off the top rope and into the table!!! OH MY GOD!!! This match has to be over. Some one call for the bell!!! McCain now stands over Lover Boy's battered body laughing. He then yells to the ring attendant for his belt. Oh no, he wouldnt. Yes he would. The ring attendant wont give him the belt, so McCain nails him with a power bomb. He takes the belt and reaches under the ring. Whats under there??? Oh no, its gasoline and wash rag. He also has a lighter. He has been planning to do this from the begining!!! What a poor excuse for a champion. McCain also grabs a sticker. What does it say...oh god it says ERUW. McCain puts the sticker on the belt. I guess that means the rumors are true. Dan McCain is going to burn the ULW world title belt and leave to go to ERUW. I cant believe this is happening. The crowed is chanting "Sell Out, Sell Out.". But McCain doesnt seem to care. He now dumps the gas on the belt, and then lights the rag on fire. He then waves the flaming rag in the air. Some body stop him. Wait a minute!!! Lover Boy is on his feet. Dan McCain doesnt see him, but Lover Boy super kicks McCain in the back of the head!!! And the flaming rag falls in McCain's face!!! McCain's face has been badly burned!!! And now Lover Boy grabs Dan McCain by his legs and puts him in the texas Clover leif!!! We have never seen Lover Boy use this hold, but he has it hooked on McCain in the middle of the ring. Lover Boy wants to make McCain give up. That must be it. McCain is bleeding from his burnt face, and you can see the pain in his expressions. Lover Boy still has the hold on him and yes...McCain taps out!!! OH MY GOD, WE HAVE A NEW CHAMPION!!!! LOVER BOY HAS WON THE TITLE! (Announcer) The winner of this match, and new WORLD HEAVY WEIGHT CHAMPION.....LOVER BOY!!!! (crowed explodes) (24:52) (Wells) Lover Boy is the new champion!!! He has defeated Dan McCain with the texas Clover leif. And i guess that means Dan McCain will be on his way to ERUW. Lover Boy is celebrating in the ring with his new belt. He rips the ERUW sticker right off of it, and shows it to the crowed. The fans are going crazy. I guess it goes to show that big budgeted leagues like ERUW may be able to buy major superstars. But Heart is some thing they can never buy. And Lover Boy has more heart than any man i know. McCain sold out, and in the end, it cost him. I wish him good luck in ERUW, but right now we have a new heavy weight champion of the world. So for Johnny Rebel, Jeff Reed, Curtis Sharp, and Bobby Rock, this is Todd Wells saying good night ever body. And what a ppv this has been. This has been a special ULW pay-per-view production.
