17 October 1999

[Fade in.] [Suddenly, the lights in the arena dim, and Japanese katekana symbols for both "honor" and "duty" highlight the arena and the ring. An old Japanese folksong wails over the public-address system, a female voice singing to flutes and drums. The fans pop in anticipation]
Steve Higgins: What the... [After a long moment of suspense, the curtains part, and a long, slender, oriental silver dragon, it's body curled to a sphere, appears in the ring. A figure steps into the aisle who could be described as delicate if it was not for her grotesque yet beautiful appearance. The clearly Asian woman is of maybe five-and-a-half feet tall, her long, auburn hair falling over her alabaster-like skin and accentuating her soft curves. Her body is that of an athlete, maybe even a dancer, with delicate yet firm muscling and no body fat. She is wearing a jade green and gold kimono, an elaborate design ornamented with gems and pearls, showing a majestic tiger in a field of golden orchids and lotus blossoms. A sheated wakizashi is on her back. Yet her right arm is in a thick black glove, hanging in a somewhat unnatural angle away from her body, and the last three fingers of her left hand are long, carbon-reinforced, and painted in the tradition of the ancient Chinese mandarin. Her body is heavily tattooed, and the woman's tender skin still seems to scream from what must have been a gruesome process of tatooing. The ink mysteriously reflects the arena light, and her tattooes, ranging from simple katekana symbols to haiku to elaborate designs, shimmer in many colors. The fans erupt as some recognize this woman]
SH: That... that's Xieng Chi Yoshida! We've haven't seen her in, oh, almost two years... what on Earth's name could her business be here tonight?

Japan in recent history. A man whose ideals have met contempt as well as idolization, yet no indifference. A man who is admired as a samurai as well as branded a traitor. This man is Shiryo no Yoshida]

His hair is long and black, his face is painted white, his eyes barely showing any form of emotion. A sheated katana is on his back, and around his waist is the PJW Grand Prix Senshuken belt, the letters "R-I-P"
spraypainted over it. The fans are popping huge now, the fans divided in cheering and booing. A plastic drink container is aimed at Yoshida, but with barely a movement of a sidestep, he avoids it. Xieng Chi leads Yoshida down the aisle, whereupon he gracefully enters the ring and grabs the house microphone]

'wrestlers' in the back - and I will not use the word samurai or even athletes, as I would be insulting those who really deserve that recognition.
I have come here tonight because my country, my heritage, my codex, everything I once stood for has transformed into one big brothel."
[_LOUD_ boos from the crowd. Whereas before, the entrance Yoshida had received was loud and mixed, the boos are now the definite majority. Yoshida again pauses before trying to address the crowd]

Wanizames, who have been prostituting their services, body parts, and perhaps various parts of their genitals to whoever would pay more.
