About The League <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 1. What Is The gWc? The Global Wrestling Corporation is an e-mail-based fantasy wrestling federation, which is booked and uses a Roleplays. gWc was founded in August
2000 by Goat Fassie a veteran in the wrestling world. Although, he is well regarded for being one of the best veterans people have seen. gWc started when
Fassie found that actually wrestling wasn't as fun as being the CEO/Owner of a fed. He opened up a bank loan and started the gWc. gWc is based throughout the world.
2.What titles are there in gWc? a. gWc Heavyweight Championship Title The top title of the gWc. It signifies that you are the top wrestler in the federation with the belt. Top contenders for the belt will be decided by booking as well as other factors. However, a good feud will easily replace the top contender.
b. gWc United States Championship Title This is the second-best title in the fed. Top contenders for the belt will be decided by booking as well as other factors. However, a good feud will easily replace the top contender.
c. gWc North American Champion This belt is equivalent to a European or Television Title, and it is the third-best title in the fed. Top contenders for the belt will be decided by booking as well as other factors. However, a good feud will easily replace the top contender.
d. gWc Hardcore Champion This belt is for the extreme wrestlers. It will be decided in some of the most extreme matches. Top contenders for the belt will be decided by booking as well as other factors. However, a good feud will easily replace the top contender.
The titles will signify who's who of the gWc. These _aren't_ the main goals of the fed. If you've joined just to have your name placed beside them, go ahead and leave now.

Rules and Regulations <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> 1.How are the Matches determined? -Roleplaying -Heat/Angles (You do not have to be Heel to get heat) -Strats -Effort (If you put forth effort, you'll eventually get a push.) 2.What are the roleplay rules? Four flashes and Two in-rings per week is the limit on roleplaying, though one great roleplay can sometimes beat out two average ones. Numerous RPs sent in for one match will be frowned upon. Several RPs, whether weak or strong in quality, inconvenience bookers and break the rules. Focus instead on producing the best roleplays you can within the limit. Descriptive, consistent (with the character), creative roleplaying ("RPs", "flashes") helps bring your character wins.
a. The Happy Clause - I can make some of the people happy all of the time, but I cannot make everyone happy all of the time. Keep that in mind at all times, please. b. The Spunky Clause - From here on out, we're shooting for realism. If I don't think something you write would get aired on national TV, then don't send it to me.
c. The Egomaniacal Tyrant Clause - The basic premise of this rule is "No Bragging Allowed". Anyone who develops a serious chip on their shoulder will go through what's known as a "humbling process". I don't care how many titles you have won outside of gWc, or how many people you have defeated outside of the federation -- if you're coming into gWc, don't expect an instant-push.
d. Character Assassination - The basic premise of this rule is that a person is not allowed to attack another character in a way that the other character has potential to be 'ruined', without getting the permission of that character's handler first. This includes any and all attacks that would leave a character injured, overly humiliated, or permanently scarred.
[add to that…] f. The Diggs Clause - gWc administration reserves the right to refuse flashes of inappropriate material (i.e. racism, personal shots, various retardedness, attacking of another gWc member) as well as the right to refuse to count the same inappropriate material towards a match. If it's refused, that's the end of it. Anyone who tries to talk me into running an inappropriate roleplay will be canned REAL quick-like.
This is just a game. It's not life. If you lost, you lost because I thought you should've. If you wonder if you were out-rp'ed, you were. If you think you need some more heat, you do. If you take it out on me, don't expect to get any reaction, except reference to this rule. If you're seriously ticked, then you're seriously fired. If you've ever been a fedhead, you know that it can be a hard job, and the less aggravation I have to suffer, the better.
g. Use of other handlers' characters in your RPs without permission is not allowed. 3.How do I get matches? a. You get signed randomly to a match b. You send in the 'formal' request for the match It is the bookers' discretion on whether that match is signed or not. 4.Are sneak attacks allowed? Attacks and interference are allowed in moderation and administration has the right to refuse a sneak attack. Try to keep it somewhat realistic in the context of wrestling. All members are expected to be professionals and
'sell' attacks and incorporate them into flashes, strats, etc. However, if you do have problems with the way your character was portrayed in an attack (used in a flash without your permission; portrayed in a way inconsistent with his character, etc.), e-mail the Presidents and gWc administration can decide if it's worth acting upon. Attacks and interference will never be used to affect the outcome of a match, unless
(among other reasons) the party being attacked is already booked to lose, one of the parties in the match has something developed, or (extremely rarely)
if an angle might benefit from the attack/interference. Don't ask to cost someone a match; if a member has worked on a match, they'll get consideration before attack/interference requests. You can ask for your attack to be considered if the person is booked to lose. However, be aware it might not get used.
5. How is new talent brought into gWc? New recruits are issued 60-day contracts with the federation. During that period, a new signee is evaluated for her/his compatibility with the fed and the new signee can get a feel for the organization. During the
60-day contract period, new signees are obligated to stay with gWc and will complete their duties as assigned. New signees can work with handlers, develop angles and make themselves known in the fed. At the end of the
60-day contract, the gWc or recruit has the option to terminate the contract or make an "extended" contractual agreement, which is basically says the handler can stay as long as s/he wants and either party can drop from the gWc at any time for any reason.
6.How many wrestlers may I handle? Members are permitted one wrestler or one tag team. Tag team members may wrestle singles bouts but are ineligible to earn singles rankings.
