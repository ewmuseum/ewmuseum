UKWA — News

Official Press Release (Re-Launch)

27 January 2005

Press Release Source: United Kingdom Wrestling Alliance http://ukwa.shadowsoftheempire.co.uk The UKWA is currently closed, and under control of BrandCorp. This is due to the defeat at the hands of the Millennium Wrestling Alliance in an inter-promotional event, Independence, on 4th July 2004. But there are things afoot which may kick the UKWA back into action... This week the Unofficial UKWA Supporters Club (UUKWASC) launched an on-line petition to get as many wrestlers as possible to sign up on pre-emptive contracts. The idea being that Barrett Brand, who did nothing but fire the UKWA talent at his disposal, may be more inclined to release the UKWA from BrandCorp if the company had a steady base of talent to re-open with. To add some gravitas to this petition, the UUKWASC today confirmed that e-Wrestling top 200's #1 star, Tommy Danger, has been in contact with them. They could not confirm the rumour that he has signed the petition, but they did little to deny it. Danger himself was seen in the Southampton area this week, and had this to say... "My wife is my number one priority. Her health has been slowly improving since her car accident but she is stubborn. New surroundings and some old friends are what I feel will help her return to form sooner than later. If I can ply my trade here in the UK while she is here, then I am all for it. But until deals are made and we are free to sign contracts, there will be no comment." At the same time there is rumour that former UKWA President, "The Major" Richard Kendall, has achieved a position of power in the MWA that he may actually be able to re-start the company himself. UUKWASC spokewoman, Lucy Jones, said this week "While we at the UUKWASC greet this news with great enthusiasm, there is always a chance that things won't turn out as planned. We would like to think that, with those potential events and our petition working alongside each other, BrandCorp is more likely to concede defeat on this issue." The UKWA was one of the finest wrestling promotions in Europe, and to see it go out in the way it did was very hard for all of us. It's been over six months, and the fan base has not gone anywhere, they've been sat there patiently waiting for Brand to do something with the amazing array of talent he took on. Now it looks like a whole new roster of talent will be signed, all hoping to see the UKWA re-open in a matter of weeks... We here at UKWA-HQ cannot officially endorse the petition, as it would be going against BrandCorp policies. But we can tell you were to go if you should decide to sign this petition, and pledge your wrestling future to the United Kingdom Wrestling Alliance. http://ukwa.shadowsoftheempire.co.uk
