RAW Carolina — Card Archive

Weekend House Show action

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RAW Carolina kicked off a big weekend of action Saturday night with a live event at the Patriot Center, located on the campus of George
Mason University in Fairfax, Virginia!! Results:
The opening bout featured two talented newcomers slugging it out as
RYAN DELANEY continued to impress with a big win over
“COLDNOK” BROCK NEWTON, Chasing the Dragon at the eight minute mark for the pinfall. The self-proclaimed ‘professional hitman’ MR. BLONDE made his Carolinas in-ring debut in Fairfax, and was very impressive in an utter annihilation of the overmatched STONEY COOPER. The 7 foot, 300 pound Blonde, wrestling in an appropriate Richard Nixon mask, given the locale, finished off Cooper with an awesome powerbomb from the top rope he calls the Blonde Bombshell.
A second debut on the night saw second generation star “WHITE TIGER” MARK GRAY take on “SCHOLARLY” SCOTT MCBRIDE in a nice little technical match-up, won by the young Floridian with his Tiger Strike, a double springboard senton bomb at the five minute mark.
BRUISER FLINN used his new finisher The Spin Cycle to take care of
MARCUS CROSS, hitting the youngster with his reverse facelock stunner at the eight minute mark for the pin.
The future of RAW Carolina has never looked brighter, as ANOTHER pair of relative newcomers squared off in the next bout, which saw Danish superstar (and really, who else has one of THOSE?!?) WHITE WOLF hit
“THE GOLDEN DRAGON” CHANG TAKATA with Unfettered Might! at the ten minute mark of a great match to take the victory.
SIDIOUS THE CLOWN battled JUSTIN HARPER to a wild double disqualification in the next match. Harper was looking to pay Sid back from ruining his Grand Strand Slam title shot, and Sid. . .well,
Sid’s just crazy, and the referee was forced to toss the match out at the eight minute mark with both men battling outside the ring.
CHRISTIAN DEMONSTONE was successful in defense of his Piedmont Title in a good match with THE OMEGA KID, a rematch of their Piedmont
Tourney semifinal. Demonstone retained after dropping OK with a nasty
Release Canuck ‘Plex ’02 just past twelve minutes for the pin.
The main event was a defense of the RAW Tag-Team Titles, as VAN WILD &
SCOTT STARRING defeated the former Carolina Tag-Team Champions, TERRY
JACOBS & “THE PREDATOR” KEVIN SIMMONS in a great match.
Starring got the pin for his team with a spectacular Rookie Mistake shooting star press on Simmons at the thirteen minute mark.
--- Weekend action inside the Beltway continued for the superstars of RAW
Carolina Sunday afternoon with a big matinee at the DC Armory!!
Results: High-flying second generation star “WHITE TIGER” MARK GRAY scored an impressive win over young Texan STONEY COOPER, getting the pin at the six minute mark with a Tiger Strike double springboard senton.
Fellow newcomer MR. BLONDE kept pace in the next match, stopping
ISAIAH WALKER with a Blonde Bombshell powerbomb from the top rope in just under two(!!) minutes.
THE EQUALIZER picked up a singles victory over “THE PREDATOR” KEVIN SIMMONS, hitting the former tag champ with a Faceplant after Simmons’ blood rival Morris Bass had hit the ring during a ref bump to deliver a Widowmaker DDT on a steel chair.
THE BLOOD BROTHERS (Dirk Robertson & Marc Tower) were successful in the first defense of their newly-won Carolina Tag Titles, a tough contest with MARCUS CROSS & ACHILLES FREEMAN. A Chicken Wing Crash on
Freeman was all she wrote for the challengers, as Robertson made the pin.
After an impressive pair of showings against Sidious the Clown, THE
MISTRESS got her first title shot in the Carolinas, and walked out with a Disqualification victory over Coastal Television Champion
“THE CURSE” ESTEBAN CORTEZ, after he smashed a mirror over her head at the six minute mark.
TERRY JACOBS picked up a win as RIC BEAUTY continued his descent into madness in the next contest, hitting Beauty with a Charlotte Cyclone as he argued quietly amongst himself at the eight minute mark after dominating much of the previous action.
VAN WILD & SCOTT STARRING made it two for two on the weekend with another successful defense of the RAW Tag-Team Titles, this time over the monstrous duo of PURGATORY (Synn & Dementia), getting the pinfall with a Wild Ride on Synn at the nine minute mark.
The main event saw “FAST” EDDIE CUTLASS in defense of the Carolina Heavyweight Title against the powerhouse “OUTLAW” MORRIS BASS. Bass tossed the champ from pillar to post in the early going, but Cutlass hung in there with his usual tenacity, and got a quick pinfall with a small package just shy of eleven minutes to retain.
