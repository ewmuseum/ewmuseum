From the Archives: Queen City Showdown 1999

19 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We're just a few short weeks from one of the Carolinas' grandest wrestling traditions -- The Queen City Showdown!! Last year's event was a classic (details can be found at --
http://groups.yahoo.com/group/RAWCarolina/message/20 ). Let's take a stroll down memory lane to 1999, as well, and see what went down. . . . . QUEEN CITY SHOWDOWN Independence Arena Charlotte, NC December 19, 1999 The opening bout of QCS99 was scheduled for the Carolina Tag-Team
Titles, as COAST TO COAST ("Awesome" Alec Price & "Unbelievable" Jeff
Bennett) defended the straps against the formidable Wild Bunch duo of
TULLY ROEBUCK & "THE OUTLAW" FRANK JAMES. The popular champs emerged victorious in their battle with a pair of once and future Carolina
Heavyweight Champions, pinning James after a beautiful Coastal
Collision double-team finisher. Young "POWERHOUSE" PETE FISHER was very impressive in the blow-off match in his months-long feud with surly cowboy SHADRACH PUCKETT.
Fisher got the win with his impressive Power Plant slam from the second rope on the Wild Bunch veteran.
The ARABIAN KNIGHTS (Ahmed Mustafa & Ali Hussein) cemented their status as top tag-team contenders with a controversial victory over the popular pair of "THUNDERBOLT" JACK SAMPSON & GOLIATH. After blinding Sampson with their Saudi Dust, the Knights dropped him with a Sandstorm double suplex for the pin.
A new era dawned in the Carolinas, as cocksure Chicago native "STELLAR" STEVE KELLER made his Carolina debut, scoring a big victory over suicidal freak STEVIE RAY MURDOCH. Murdoch tried to put
Keller through the timekeeper's table but failed, and soon found himself on the wrong end of a Simply Stellar corkscrew suplex for the pin.
"YOUNG GUN" TODD STARR successfully defended his Carolina Television
Championship against young technical phenom JACOB LEGACY. Jericho
Weaver's protégé would rebound to win the title on New Year's Day, but Starr was the better man on this day, winning the match with a
Belly to Belly Suplex.
The savage ex-marines CODE RED (Sgt. Dan Lorenzo & The Leatherneck) knocked off the RIVERBOAT GAMBLERS (Bobby Rice & Scott Dallas) in a battle of former tag champions. As Rice tended to an injured Lady
Luck on the outside, Code Red drilled his partner with a spike powerbomb for the pin.
Longtime rivals – for no particular reason other than they couldn't stand the sight of each other – collided in the confines of a steel cage in the next match, as "TERRIBLE" TOMMY BEDFORD cheated and clawed his way to a win over Canadian superstar TOMMY VICTORY. Using a pair of wirecutters, Bedford removed a section of the cage, and grated the sharp edge across Victory's face, bloodying him before stopping him cold with a cradle piledriver.
After seeing his friend Jason Stormcloud injured at the hands of
JOSHUA STRONG, ACE MATTHEWS vowed revenge – and got it in Charlotte with a big victory courtesy of an Aces Wild flying elbow, earning a monster pop in the process.
The main event saw popular Carolina Champion "MAGNIFICENT" MIKE
MANGUM successful in his first major title defense, against Jericho
Weaver's hulking monster SOLOMON PARADISE. Paradise appeared to have the match won after a Paradise Lost powerbomb, but Mangum managed to get a foot on the bottom rope, and the match continued. Mangum eventually scored the win with a beautiful Mangum Force hurricanrana from the top rope.
