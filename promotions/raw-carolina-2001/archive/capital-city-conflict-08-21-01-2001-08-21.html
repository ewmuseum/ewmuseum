Capital City Conflict 08.21.01

21 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a brief hiatus to escape the late summer doldrums, RAW Carolina returned to the road with a spectacular show in Raleigh Tuesday night, Capital City Conflict!! Results from venerable Dorton Arena at the North Carolina State Fairgrounds:
"ERADICATOR" DIRK ROBERTSON vs. SIDIOUS THE CLOWN The opening bout pitted two of the top contenders to El Cid's Piedmont Title in one on one competition, as the increasingly sadistic Dirk Robertson took on the increasingly bizarre Sidious the
Clown. Before the match began, Robertson came out demanding that it be a no disqualification, falls count anywhere bout – stipulations to which the newly sadistic Sidious readily agreed. Interestingly enough, Sidious was accompanied to the ring by his new `best buddy', the charismatic Flash, who was more than enough to swing the crowd's support Sid's way.
For two men careening out of control down the path towards utter villainy, the stipulations could not be more perfect. The opening of the bout saw Robertson toss Sidious from pillar to post, nearly breaking the demented clown in two on several occasions, including one amazing spot that saw Robertson dust off his old finisher from the CFPW, The Super Eradicator Bomb, which saw Dirk powerbomb
Sidious – from the top turnbuckle – through the timekeeper's table at ringside (!!!). Robertson went for the pin here, only to see Flash break it up.
The match eventually moved back into the ring, and Sidious' cartwheeling offense began to subdue the stronger Robertson, including a cartwheel into a standing moonsault that scored a two count. At the six minute mark, longtime Sidious rival -- and recent victim of a vicious sneak attack -- El Cid made his way to ringside, steel chair in hand. Flash sensed his intentions, but caught the chair right between the eyes, laying him out.
The Piedmont Champion leapt to the apron with the chair, and took a wild swing at Sidious – who ducked, leaving Robertson to take the full brunt of the chair shot. Sidious moved quickly, dropkicking Cid off the apron, and then hitting another standing moonsault on the dazed Robertson, and this time it was enough for the pin.
As Cid made his way back to the locker room, irate at himself for handing the match to his rival, Robertson came up steaming mad, immediately calling for the house mike and challenging Cid to a
Piedmont Title match at next week's Summerstars.
Winner: Sidious the Clown (Standing Moonsault) Time: 6:31 (** ½) "FAST" EDDIE CUTLASS vs. ROADKILL RICK A pair of house show encounters has led to a burdgeoning rivalry between fan favorite Eddie Cutlass and the masked Texan Roadkill
Rick. First a time limit draw that left both men – and the fans – wanting more, and then a six man match Sunday afternoon that saw these two on the same side of the ring that ended when RKR sprayed
Cutlass with his disgusting black mist.
Both men had asked for – and received – a twenty minute time limit, to safeguard against another time limit draw. The match began hot and heavy, as the two veteran competitors have quickly developed the sort of familiarity found in longtime opponents.
After nearly fifteen minutes of hard-hitting action, Cutlass hit RKR with a fallaway slam, and then looked to hook the masked man into The
Guarantee. As Cutlass readied Roadkill for his reverse Russian Leg
Sweep, RKR caught him flush with an elbow to the jaw, sending him staggering into referee Jackie Boone, Jr. Boone stumbled backwards into the corner, and that was all the time RKR needed to spray
Cutlass with the black mist. A blinded Cutlass walked right into a
DDT, and Boone counted to three.
Mark Adams then hit the ring to a big pop from the crowd, arguing with Boone about the use of the mist, but to no avail. The decision stood, and Roadkill Rick had scored a controversial victory.
Winner: Roadkill Rick (Mist assisted DDT) Time: 15:08 (*** ½) SAMPSON BROTHERS vs. WALKER BROTHERS If Ike and Jack Sampson can't get along with each other, how could they possibly hope to defeat the Wild Bunch's brother combo, Isaac and Gideon Walker? That was the question on everyone's mind, as RAW
Carolina President "Showboat" Eddie Cannon had ordered the Sampsons to team together in an attempt to force the brothers to work out their differences.
Despite their lengthy respective careers, Ike and Jack had never once teamed together. As the match began, that much was obvious, as rather than impressive double-teams, the Sampsons dominated in that timeless fraternal tradition of trying to one-up the other. Jack hit
Isaac with a clothesline; Ike flipped Gideon completely around with a running clothesline of his own. Ike suplexed Gideon out of his boots; Jack caught Isaac with a superplex.
After five minutes of this uniquely back and forth action, the tension broke. Jack smiled at his baby brother; Ike stopped scowling, in what was as close to a smile as he was wont to give. As the crowd roared, Jack hooked Gideon into his Thunderclap dragon suplex. Isaac rushed in to make the save, only to be met with a big boot and Deep Freeze double arm DDT from Ike. Referee Slick Wilson shrugged, and counted both Walkers' shoulders to the mat for the three. And although they still left the ring separately, fans were hopefully that the long chill between the Sampsons was beginning to thaw.
Winners: Sampson Brothers (Thunderclap AND Deep Freeze) Time: 5:29 (* ½) As Thunderbolt Jack made his way back to the locker room, soaking in the cheers of the crowd, the Mini-Tron flickered to life, where everyone's favorite trio of Television Champion Direwolf, his kid
Barry, and nanny Ned are standing in the catering area:
DW: Ned, he's my boy, I'll instruct him as I see fit, and in MY time line. Ned: I'm sorry Direwolf, but you cannot stop nature. Barry: Yeah hairy bwah...I's fettina unleash mah nature on dat young leddy ovah dere. (Points at one of Casanova Beethoven's Hos standing by the coffee maker, then walks up to her.) Scuse me, but yo ass be like a mirra! I sees mahself all in dat!!!
Direwolf: BARRY! Ned: Stop yelling at him, let him learn. The Ho stares at Barry, ALMOST interested, then leaves, shaking her head. Direwolf: I'm warning you Ned...leave this be! The hairy TV champ heads to the locker room, to prepare for his match. "Showboat" Eddie Cannon hit the ring as the Mini-Tron faded out, obviously pleased with the results of the previous contest. Grabbing
Bobby Lee Wilcox' mike, he announced that due to a previous contractual agreement with Tokyo Puroresu Promotions, Jessie Muldoon had embarked on a tour of Japan for the next few weeks. As a concession to Muldoon's RAW contract, TPP had agreed to a talent exchange and sent over a man that is no stranger to the Carolinas,
Tak Funaki, and Funaki would be Stone's opponent for the evening.
So, rather than our scheduled match, we got: STONE vs. TAK FUNAKI Chalk it up to several things, if you want – Funaki's jet lag or
Stone's impressive offensive arsenal, it doesn't matter. Stone dominated the Japanese exchange wrestler from bell to bell.
After a brief flurry of suplexes from Funaki to start things off,
Stone took control of the match for good. Funaki set his head too early for a backdrop – and Stone nearly piledrove him through the mat. Following that up with a nasty brainbuster, Stone then dropped to the mat with a serried of crushing headbutts.
Less than three minutes after it started, it was over. Stone pulled
Funaki up, and sent him right back down to the mat with a Rolling
Stone running Rikishi driver for the three count.
Winner: Stone (Rolling Stone) Time: 2:54 (*) EL CID © vs. DIREWOLF, Piedmont Heavyweight Title In his second match against another RAW-C champion this week, Piedmont champion El Cid defended against the Television champion
Direwolf. But where his RAW-C TV match with Thomas Adler was title versus title, this time only the Piedmont strap was on the line.
As the match began, it was obvious that Direwolf had more pressing matters at hand – namely, Ned's increasing influence on Barry in the locker room. Direwolf charged right at the opening bell, looking for a running headbutt, only to see El Cid sidestep and drop him with a drop toe hold, sending Direwolf's head crashing into the turnbuckle.
Direwolf pounded the mat in frustration, complaining "Stand still and quit delaying the inevitable! I have to kick your hairless ass so I can raise my little guy's hairy one!"
Not impressed in the least, Cid hooked Direwolf in a collar and elbow in the center of the ring, only to see his larger opponent muscle him into the corner and deliver some brutal knife edge chops, all the while declaring "I'll beat you like a rented mule! Now yield!"
But Cid wriggled free and delivered a pair of chops on his own. He bounded out to the opposite corner, with Direwolf charging hard after him – only to crash headlong into the turnbuckles. Cid moved to another corner – same result. Before long, Direwolf had slammed full- speed into all four corners, and collapsed on the mat in pain.
Looking to seize the opportunity, Cid climbed to the top rope, looking for one of his textbook high-flying maneuvers. As he hit the top rope, his right foot slipped and he crashed hard to the mat, severely twisting his knee in the process.
As Cid writhed in pain, it was obvious that something had gone wrong. An enterprising RAW-C producer turned on the mini-Tron, where young Barry was at it again with an attractive production assistant.
Barry: (Tugging on her skirt to get her attention...she smiles at him.) Lady: Hi there. You want to say something to me cutie? Barry: Yeah young leddy... Ah wishes you was a do', so I could bang you all night! Lady: (Turns red, either embarrassed or insulted.) Who told you to say that! Barry: Noone don tole me to say dat foo! I's yo' heir appar' ant! No one tole me a tang! Lady: Well...they should tell you a little something about decorum. Barry: Talk normal beesh! Or betta yet, shup and blo... Ned: (Comes running into the picture as he was listening close by.) BARRY! Be nice. (The lady gathers herself and smiles at Ned--he is gorgeous after all.) Ned: I'm sorry about that...he's trying to discover the joys of women. (Looks at Barry with a scold.)
Lady: (Flirting.) Ooh, that's soooo sweet. (pauses.) Are you a single dad? Barry:[Clears voice to get there attention then taps his thigh.] Yo' just tink dis mah my leg huh? [Ned and woman looked aghasted.] As the Mini-Tron faded out, the fans' attention returned to the ring – and things didn't look any better for El Cid. He continued to roll on the mat, clutching at his right knee. Direwolf – to his credit – didn't go for the cover. Rather, he motioned for medical personnel to come out and help the fallen Piedmont Champion. As EMTs poured into the ring, the match was declared a no contest, and the fans gave
Cid a standing ovation as he rode out of the arena on a stretcher.
Decision: No Contest (Knee Injury to Cid) Time: 3:22 (*) MARK ADAMS & FLASH © vs. "X-PRESS" DAVID JERICHO & JAMAL BYERS, Carolina Tag-Team Titles After failing in his attempt with Marc Tower to capture the tag titles at Dog Day Afternoon, David Jericho called on old friend (and recent Windy City RAW stalwart) Jamal Byers to team with him in this rematch of sorts. But before the match got underway. . .
[The lights go out and a loud thumping sound is heard over the PA system. Suddenly, and in cue with Freddy Mercury's voice, five letters appear on the screen.]

Flash: "Raleigh, North Carolina..." [Pause for the cheap pop.] Flash: "Never fear, Flash is here!" [The King of the Impossible raises the microphone in the air as the crowd cheers. We catch a brief glimpse of David Jericho and Jamal

Adams and Alice follow closely behind him.]
Flash: "And as usual, Little Davey Jericho has succeeded in making a complete ass of himself. It would seem as if the Man With The Lady-

Flash: "Calm down, spanky. I know that you're in a big rush to get back to your hotel room so Davey can be the 'tag team championship hammer' and all, but you're just gonna have to wait a little bit longer because I think that there's someone else who has something to say."
["Showboat" Eddie Cannon steps into the aisle, again with mike in hand.] Cannon: Well, well, well. The champions ask for the stips. The challengers accept the stips. Just one thing left to do – Let's see what the fans want. Well, Raleigh?!? Hardcore Rules?!?
[The roar of the crowd leaves no doubt as to their preference. And away they went. . . .] As the roars faded, Adams and Flash charged the ring, quickly clearing it of their challengers. It became early on that the hardcore brawling style employed by the champions over the last few weeks was not a passing fancy; they're clearly moving in a new direction. But the fans didn't care – they were behind Adams and
Flash all the way, especially after Adams dropped Byers through yet
ANOTHER timekeeper's table with a Big Easy suplex. Adams went for the pin on the floor, but by the time referee Slick Wilson made it out of the ring, Jericho came crashing down with a tope onto Adams to break it up.
Flash followed him out to the floor, and the action spilled out with it. The action see-sawed back and forth, with the normally technically-minded Jericho and Byers more than keeping up in the brawling department. A cool spot saw the safety railing get knocked over when Flash went crashing into it. As Flash lay motionless on the railing, Jericho launched with a nice senton bomb over the top rope and onto Flash, leaving both men dazed on the floor. Adams rushed out of the ring to help, only to walk right into Byer's Drive-
By (a spinebuster followed by a Boston Crab) on the wreckage of the timekeeper's table.
After over ten minutes of great action, Byers planted Flash with a
DDT – only to turn right into a clothesline from Adams, which sent both men crashing to the floor. As the brawl raged out on the floor,
Jericho cleared the cobwebs just long enough to plant Flash with a
Fisherman's Suplex. Flash got a shoulder up at the count of two – but Wilson didn't see it, and counted to three, giving David Jericho another controversial title victory.
To say that the former champions were pissed would be an understatement. Winners (and new champions): David Jericho & Jamal Byers (Fisherman's Suplex) Time: 11:24 (***) As the crowd waited with breathless anticipation for the main event, the mini-Tron flickered to life, and the crowd was treated to another shot of the smiling new tag champions, David Jericho and Jamal
Byers. As they congratulated each other, who should appear in the hallway but old friends Jackie(!!) and Jericho's uncle, wrestler turned Brooklyn wrestling promoter The Rebel Warrior.
RW: "David, how you been? Your head feeling alright? Haha. Don't get too angry. Jackie didn't cause you to lose your Piedmont Title, you did it, you weren't watching the match.
But, anyways, Cannon and I just signed some contracts. Who's new? Look at your uncle and there ya go. So how `bout it next week? Me and You...one on one?" Before Jericho could accept the challenge, "Showboat" Eddie Cannon appeared on-screen. CANNON: "Not so fast, boys. I know you can't wait to get after each other, and you'll get your chance. Don't worry. But first things first. Jericho, you've already got a match for next week in
Savannah – at Summerstars, you and your boy Byers are gonna defend these straps against Adams and Flash.
Mandatory rematch. Besides – the fans want it." The accompanying pop from the crowd told everyone that indeed they did. As that scene faded, another one popped up on the Mini-Tron:
TV champion Direwolf had found Barry and Ned, standing amongst a gaggle of wrestling `groupies', flirting it up.
Barry: [Grabs a tush.] Scuse me, is dis seat taken young leddy? Woman: (laughing.) How cute is this little guy? Ned: Ooh, he's something special. [All the women are competing for Ned's attention which makes Barry quite peeved.] Barry: [Pokes girl with his little mahogany stick (not that one perverts) and says] Nice shoes. Wanna fug?
[Everyone gasps and Direwolf comes charging.] Direwolf: THAT'S enough! Both of you! (Grabs Barry by the back of his pants and carries him off with Ned walking slowly behind.
The 'groupies' are too frightened to protest.) To say that the crowd enjoyed this – well, that'd be about right. "LAWBRINGER" THOMAS ADLER, "THE ANSWER" JACK ANDERSON, & "GOLDENBOY" CHRISTOPHER SCARLET vs. VINCENT C. AUGUSTUS, CASANOVA BEETHOVEN, & BULL "GATOR" JOHNSON The main event promised to be a wild, wild, WILD affair, as six of the top stars in RAW Carolina met in the same ring to `work out their differences'. Before the match began, "Showboat" Eddie Cannon made his way out to ringside one last time, and grabbed the house mike:
"Let's cut the crap out of the way right now. Beethoven. Augustus. I know you're both here, I've already seen you in the locker room. And I also know you're both going to come out here and tell the world there's no way you're going to team with the other one.
"But I'm telling you now there is a way. MY WAY. You're going to wrestle this match tonight – as TEAMMATES – whether you like it or not. And if you don't – well, you can seek gainful employment elsewhere.
"And if you think RAW will take – or Windy City RAW, or RAW Atlantic, when I'm through with you – think again. Come on out, boys."
With that decree, Cannon let the remnants of the Empire Club know just WHO was in charge of RAW Carolina – and the fans got their scheduled main event.
Given their obvious disdain for even being in Raleigh this evening,
Beethoven and Augustus were more than happy to let the 450 pound
Cajun monster Bull Johson wrestle most of the match – and he was happy to oblige. The big Gator started the contest with Christopher
Scarlet, and quickly dominated the younger, smaller fan favorite with his punishing offense.
With Johnson in the ring, Anderson was very nearly frothing at the mouth to get in there for another piece of his blood rival. Johnson was well aware of this, too, constantly taunting Anderson into the ring, and then choking Scarlet within an inch of his life as referee `Cottonmouth' Joe Lynch escorted the Crimson Spitfire back out.
After five or so minutes of this, ADLER had had enough; as Lynch escorted Anderson out of the ring, Adler charged in with a big kick to Johnson's head, buying Scarlet some time. Scarlet rolled to the corner and tagged in Adler, who immediately went to work on Johnson.
Suddenly, Beethoven and VCA were eager to get involved, hoping to soften up the champion for the title shots they felt sure they would receive down the road. Beethoven tagged himself in, but in his eagerness to get in the ring, soon found himself at the mercy of the
Lawbringer.
One would expect Adler's trio to show more teamwork than their opponents, but no one would have expected this. After dropping
Beethoven with a gutwrench suplex, Adler tagged in Scarlet. Anderson hopped in the ring with Adler, and they delivered a double irish whip. As Beethoven returned, A&A caught him, pressing him way up in the air – while Scarlet, who had climbed to the top rope, leapt and caught Beethoven mid-air, driving him into the mat headfirst with an
awe-inspiring tornado DDT(!!). That deserves a rewrite: After dropping Beethoven with a gutwrench suplex, Adler tagged in Scarlet. Anderson hopped in the ring with
Adler, and they delivered a double irish whip. As Beethoven returned, A&A caught him, pressing him way up in the air – while
Scarlet, who had climbed to the top rope, leapt and caught Beethoven mid-air, driving him into the mat headfirst with an awe-inspiring tornado DDT(!!).
That was sure to score the three count, but Bull broke up the cover, only to be immediately rushed by Anderson, to one of the biggest crowd pops of the night. Anderson and Johnson traded punches – not a good idea for our boy Jack. Beaten down into his own corner, Jack appeared to be dead meat as Johnson charged – only to be hotshotted into the corner by `The Answer'. Anderson tagged Scarlet back in – wasn't he the legal man anyway?!? – and watched as Adler climbed to the second rope, long enough to catapult Scarlet high into the air, coming down with an awesome 450 Splash.
Johnson got a foot on the bottom rope to save the match that time, and soon caught Scarlet with a headbutt to take control over the relative newcomer. VCA finally tagged in, and worked over his nemesis Scott Studd's younger cousin with a feral intensity, bouncing him all over the ring with his textbook crisp suplexes.
Johnson continued to taunt Anderson, even from his corner. Finally,
Anderson had had enough; with a full-ring running start, he speared
Johnson, right through the ropes to the floor. Somehow their bodies got twisted in mid-air, with Anderson taking the brunt of the fall when the 450 pounder fell on top of him. But it didn't deter the ferocious Anderson, as he got right back to his feet, and he and
Johnson brawled back towards the locker room.
In the ring, Augustus continued to work over Scarlet, and finally relented to tag in Beethoven. He held Scarlet for a double-axe handle blow from Beethoven – and if you think Scarlet took the hit, you haven't been watching wrestling very long. The ho lover clubbed
VCA right in the jaw, sending the Empire Man sprawling. Scarlet rolled into a neutral corner and watched as Augustus got up hopping mad, and proceeded to send Beethoven over the top rope with a clothesline. And as VCA turned around, Scarlet was waiting with a
Scarletkick that was on the money. Wyngarde moved into the ring to help his fallen charge, but Adler clotheslined HIM over the top rope, and Scarlet scored the HUGE upset three count on the former Carolina champion.
Winners: Thomas Adler & Jack Anderson & Christopher Scarlet (Scarletkick) Time: 11:34 (*** ¾) Beethoven skulked back to the locker room, a big smile on his face given the misfortunes of his `partner'. As Adler and Scarlet celebrated in the ring, Wyngarde collected the woozy VCA and helped him towards the locker room.
But no one noticed all that. Nearly every eye in the arena was already on – or soon would be – the familiar figure at the top of the arena.
"You look mighty good in my gold, Cowboy." The crowd followed Adler's eyes to the top row of the Dorton Arena. So did the Mini-Tron, to find a most unexpected sight: "Stellar" Steve Keller. Absent from RAW Carolina since March, the multiple time Carolina
Heavyweight Champion – whose last title reign ended without him being pinned for the title back in January at Boot Camp Brawl – looked as smug as ever, proudly displaying his recently won RAW Atlantic
Bicoastal Heavyweight Title. "That's right – I said MY gold. After all, as all these people know –
Matthews never pinned me for that title. In fact, some would say – and I'm one of `em –- that for all intents and purposes, I'm STILL the champion."
Boos from the fans indicated that they did know that he wasn't pinned, but they didn't care. And no, no one in Dorton Arena felt he was still was the champion. Except for those three guys in the front row who are nearly orgasmic with joy at the return of their hero.
But they don't count. "So here's what I'm going to do, Cowboy. Next week in Savannah, Georgia, at Summerstars – a show that _I_ put on the map, a show that
_I've_ main evented more times than anyone in history!!! I'll give you the chance to beat me for MY title. Whaddya say, Cowboy?!?"
Adler looked, of all things, bemused. His slight grin – and even slighter nod – seemed to accept Keller's challenge. And nearly 7,000 fans began to work on the fastest route from Raleigh to Savannah.
