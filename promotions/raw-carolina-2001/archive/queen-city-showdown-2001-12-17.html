RAW Carolina — Card Archive

Queen City Showdown

17 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VOICE-OVER: Two men. . . . [Shot of Marc Tower. Shot of Jack Anderson.] VO: One Goal. [Still frame of the Carolina Heavyweight Title.] VO: One man in the midst of a bloody war. . . . [Tower getting beaten down inside a steel cage by Dirk Robertson and Dave Pitrelli.] VO: The other in the midst of a swirling controversy. . . . [A shot of Jack Anderson slowly dissolves into a shot of The Guy Dressed in All Black.] VO: Tonight. . . .their destinies cross. At a tradition as old as Carolina wrestling itself. . . . [Series of shots: Ace Matthews brawling with Steve Keller last year. . . .Doug Simpkins and Jack Sampson going at it. . . ."Superstar" Beau Ramsey and "Handsome" Harvey Cage battle over an armbar. . . .]
VO: THE QUEEN CITY SHOWDOWN. [LIGHTS!! PYRO!! ACTION!! Let's take a quick rundown of the signs before we begin: STONE'S GONNA ROLL THROUGH HANGMAN!!!! I'M TGDAB!!!!!!!!!! FELICITY!!!!!!!!!!!! BLOOD BROTHERS THICKER THAN MONEY!!!!! [Enough of that. Pan on down to ringside for your hosts on the evening, the voice of RAW Carolina Tim Dallas and his colleague "Deadly" Dean Dillinger.]
TD: It's time, sports fans – For the Queen City Showdown!! With me as always here on the big NASN broadcast, former Carolinas Television
Champion, my man Dean-O, "Deadly" Dean Dillinger!! DDD: The pleasure – as always – is all yours, Dallas!! TD: But that's enough talk -- Right now it's time for our first match-up – The Blood Brothers take on The Network's tag-team bruisers, Purgatory!!
DDD: Those guys rock – former champions all over the globe!! TD: After Dirk Robertson turned on Marc Tower last week on RAW-C TV, it appeared that this match was very much in doubt!! But "Showboat"
Eddie Cannon stepped in, and ordered Robertson to appear with Rayge as contractually obligated – and if he doesn't, he's FIRED!!!!
DDD: Cannon's gonna rue the day he pissed on The Network!!!! "ERADICATOR" DIRK ROBERTSON & JAYSON RAYGE vs. PURGATORY Robertson did indeed appear for the opening match, albeit obviously against his will. As the fans booed him mercilessly, he watched from the apron as his young partner started off fast and furious against
Synn, outwrestling him during a nice chain sequence before lifting him into an attempted Infinite Rayge Driver, only to see Dementia lumber in for the save – a move which Robertson made no move to counter.
Dementia tagged himself in, and the seven foot monster crushed Rayge with a jackknife powerbomb, and followed up with an awesome Gorilla
Press all the way to the floor. Synn moved in to work over Rayge, and Robertson made no move to stop it. In fact, he engaged in a shoving match with hotheaded new rookie referee Hank Jackson, allowing Synn to do even more damage, which he gladly followed through with a delayed brainbuster on the concrete.
Synn made his way to the apron after tossing Rayge back in, and a spike piledriver followed Purgatory's quick tag. Rayge was completely out of it at this point, and Synn went for his finisher,
The Confession, a dangerous driver. But somehow Rayge found the energy to flip out of it, surprising everyone – especially Robertson, who watched in shock as the rookie dove into their corner and tagged him in.
Robertson stepped into the ring slowly, cautiously, eyeing Synn – and then turned to his own corner and sucker-punched Rayge, dragging his protégé into the ring by his hair. Robertson dropped Rayge with a stiff DDT as Jackson called for the bell, tossing this one out the window. As Robertson cheered on his new mates in The Network, Synn hoisted Rayge into the air with Dementia behind him. As Synn brought him down, Dementia caught Rayge's legs and both Purgatory men dropped to one knee, slamming the rookie with a double backbreaker from hell –
The Desecration. Finally, a score of officials cleared the ring, and The Network had struck first blood on the evening.
Decision: No Contest Time: 6:24 (*) TD: That was disgusting!! DDD: It just showed what an idiot Rayge is!! No matter what Cannon said – he had to know he couldn't trust The Eradicator anymore!!
Blood Brothers – Heh!!! TD: An impressive showing by The Network – and up next, it's their big brute Dave Pitrelli!!
DDD: I'm gonna tell him you said that!! TD: Pitrelli's been impressive of late since heading into the singles ranks – but he's got a tough task ahead of him tonight, in the form of red hot rookie Paul Sheridan!!
DDD: And his red hot manager Shona Maynard!! PAUL SHERIDAN vs. "DREADMIND" DAVE PITRELLI One man in this match came ready to rumble – and it wasn't Paul Sheridan. It seemed from the outset that he had other things on his mind, and distracted is not what you want to be when you're in the ring with Dave Pitrelli.
Pitrelli ran right through Sheridan, bending, breaking, snapping the youngster in half with a variety of stiff suplexes and powerbombs, using his 100 pound weight advantage to full effect. Although Shona was at ringside, rallying her troop, it was to no avail, as whatever offense he mustered was completely ineffectual.
After five minutes of this brutalization, Pitrelli climbed to the top rope and ended it with the devastating top rope spear he calls the
The Hatred Within, and rolled through into a Northern Lights style bridge for the pin. An impressive win for Pitrelli, who would play a much bigger role later in the night. . . .
Winner: Dave Pitrelli (Hatred Within) Time: 5:21 (*) DDD: Damn, that was impressive!! TD: Dave Pitrelli showing why many have pegged him as a future champion here in RAW Carolina – what's that?!? Let's go to the locker room to our own Vernon St. Jean, who has a couple of guests with him!! Vern?!?
[Cut to an arena hallway, where Intern Vern has gathered together the participants in tonight's Piedmont Title match, "Fast" Eddie Cutlass and his longtime rival, Roadkill Rick.]
VSJ: Gentlemen, your rivalry has known no bounds recently – but Rick, your style in the last few weeks – Well, you've changed?!? Could we be seeing a softening of Roadkill Rick?!? RKR: Softening?!? I – [Suddenly, Dave Pitrelli bursts into the scene, returning from the ring after his victory over Paul Sheridan. Grabbing RKR by the mask, he runs him headlong into a nearby trashcan. Moving quickly, he picks RKR up off the floor, and then delivers a sort of modified powerbomb – right into the cement wall, where Roadkill's head smacks with a sick thud.]
TD: What in the hell is that?!? DDD: I'll tell you what it is, Dallas – Roadkill Rick cost Pitrelli the Carolina Title last week – and now he's gonna pay for it!!
[Then, just as suddenly, Eddie Cutlass jumps back into the picture, attacking Pitrelli (Cutlass saving RKR?!? Couldn't be!!). But
Pitrelli soon clubs him to the ground, and drops him with the exact same modified powerbomb, leaving champion and challenger side by side on the floor as he walks away, laughing maniacally.]
TD: This doesn't look good, Dean-O. DDD: Especially given Cutlass' recent history with concussions!! TD: I'm sure Vern will stay on top of that story in the back – but right now, it's time for our first title match – Hangman defends his
Television Title against the force of nature known simply as Stone!! DDD: Stone's a choker – a never-will-be!! The big one?!? He'll never even win the small one!!
TD: We'll see about that!! HANGMAN © vs. STONE, Carolina Television Title Stone more than proved himself the top contender to the Television Title by outlasting the entire RAW Carolina roster in a battle royal some weeks back in RAW-C TV; his title shot, however, was marred by outside interference, and the rematch with the Hangman was set.
This time, the leader of Judgment Day claimed that he wanted no interference – and surprisingly, that just the way it went down, as
Stone looked to erase the stigma of `best RAW-C superstar never to hold singles gold. Hangman started quickly, doubling Stone over with a cheap shot before the bell rang before lifting him by his throat and powering him into a corner. Hank Jackson moved in to break it up, and Hangman used the opportunity to knee Stone right in the, well, stones, before booting him right over the top rope.
Clearly taken aback by the bigger man's power, Stone took a trip around the ring to regroup – and came back in ready and able.
Although giving up nearly a foot in height, Stone has always been more than double tough, and took the fight right to his fellow
Coloradan. At the fifteen minute mark, Stone went for the kill – and missed a guillotine leg drop from the top rope. Hangman rolled out of the way and then to his feet, grabbing Stone by his neck one last time and dropping him on the top rope, looking to end it with the Noose. But
Stone would have none of it, and leapt off the top in a sort of leapy, grabby, tornado DDT, bouncing Hangman's head hard off the mat – for the 1-2-3.
Winner (and new champion): Stone (Tornado DDT) Time: 15:34 (*** ½) TD: What a moment here in Charlotte – Stone finally captures RAW Carolina gold!! DDD: Please – if he'da stayed in the Empire Club, he'd have more belts than he knew what to do with!!
TD: Ahem – at any rate, our next match should be a dandy, with the winner sure to be at the front of the line for a Tag Title shot when the champions return from their never-ending world tour!!
DDD: Winter in Russia?!? That's just plain mean!! Hell, if I won this match, I'd buy a plane ticket myself, fly over there, and DEMAND my title shot!!!!
COLLATERAL DAMAGE vs. HELLSPAWN These two teams have wrestled each other extensively over the last few weeks, each time to a rather inconclusive finish. So this match was set up with no time limit, and official Slick Wilson was assigned, seeing as though he's been known to let a few things go from time to time, all in the name of getting a decisive winner.
Damien Scratch's veteran Judgment Day duo started out fast, taking it to the youngsters, wrestling their highest profile match in the
Carolinas to date, and perhaps a bit nervous. The first big double- team move came just minutes in, as Hades sent Mike Diamond staggering with a stiff savate kick, right into the arms of a waiting Abyss, who crushed Big Mike with a full nelson suplex. Then, grabbing Diamond by the feet, he slingshotted him back up – right into a stiff back elbow to the bridge of the nose from Hades, for a two and seven- eighths count.
The Charlotte crowd settled in, expecting a long, brutal match. And they got one. Hellspawn kept Diamond in the ring the better part of ten minutes to open the match, brutalizing him with their unique blend of power, speed, and martial arts mastery. But after ducking under an attempted thrust kick, Diamond forward rolled into his corner, tagging in the master of the powerbomb, Mike Steele.
Down went Hades in a wheelbarrow powerbomb. He got up – right into a butterfly suplex-type powerbomb. Abyss lumbered in, and Steele turned around – right into a stiff float kick from the near 500 pounder, and Hellspawn began working him over much the same way as they had his partner.
At the fifteen minute mark, Abyss fired Steele into the corner – hard. As he hit the turnbuckle, Hades kicked him in the back of the head – hard. Abyss charged, and drilled him right in the midsection with a vicious knee – you guessed it, hard. Abyss reached over and tagged in his partner before hoisting Steele up – Spike powerbomb.
Hard. But Diamond made the save, and the match continued. The match stretched on, past the twenty-five minute mark, with Hellspawn firmly in control. But one big mistake changed everything – with Hades pinning Steele's arms behind him, Abyss went for a thrust kick – and Steele dropped to the mat as Hades caught the kick right in the throat. Steele then rose up with a mule kick, staggering
Hades even further – right into a perfectly timed bulldog from the top rope from Diamond. One. And two. And three. Collateral Damage is on their way to a title shot.
Winners: Collateral Damage (Top Rope Bulldog) Time: 27:23 (*** ½) TD: What a big win for the youngsters – truly the future of RAW's tag-team division!!! DDD: What a cheap win!! They won't be any kind of match for The Network – wait'll they get in there with Purgatory!! TD: That may be, Dean-O – wait a second, fans, I'm told Intern Vern is in the locker room with RAW Carolina's chief medical officer,
Bones McGuirk!! Vernon?!? [Cut to the locker room, where we see Vernon St. Jean is indeed standing beside the curmudgeonly Bones McGuirk.]
VSJ: Bones, can you give us an update on the condition of Piedmont Champion Eddie Cutlass?!? BM: Sure, son. For the second time in as many weeks, Eddie Cutlass has suffered a concussion. Given his history over the last year or so of head injuries, I can't clear him to wrestle tonight – the title match is off.
[Well, you had to know Cutlass was going to bust in here, didn't you?!? Here he is.] EC: Not again!! These fans paid too much money to see me wrestle, and I'm gonna give `em a show!! Where's a waiver?!? I'll sign whatever I need to to get out there!!
BM: I'll give you the waiver, Eddie, but you don't have an opponent – Roadkill Rick was taken to the hospital earlier – there's NO WAY he's wrestling tonight!! You don't have an opponent!!
EC: Oh, yes I do!! Dave Pitrelli – get your ass down to the ring in just a few minutes!! I'll be there – and that's a Guarantee!!!!
[Back to ringside.] DDD: Pitrelli?!? Not smart, Cutlass. Not smart. TD: Pitrelli did attack him earlier in the show – that should be a dandy!! But up first, the long boiling feud between Direwolf and
Vincent Constantine Augustus comes to a head – in a Falls Count Anywhere in Charlotte Match!! DDD: As much as I hate to admit it, something like this has to favor the Man-Beast!! Vinnie C's a technical genius, but we'll have to see how he does in a brawl!!!
DIREWOLF vs. VINCENT CONSTANTINE AUGUSTUS, Falls Count Anywhere (Special thanks to RAW-C TV Producer [name withheld] for this'n.) After losing his comeback match at Riverbanks Rumble by a countout thanks to outside interference from VCA's bass guitar playing buddy,
Axe Abraham, it was only natural that the rematch at Queen City Showdown between DIREWOLF and VINCENT CONSTANTINE AUGUSTUS have a "falls count anywhere" stipulation to prevent a repeat performance at the Independence Arena.
And what a good call that was on the part of RAW-Carolina owner Eddie Cannon as this match barely stayed in the ring long enough for ring announcer Bobby Lee Wilcox to finish his introductions. From the opening bell, both Direwolf and VCA were out for blood, this time with the intention of ending their feud once and for all and coming out the winner.
With neither man actually trying to wrestle the other, Referee Slick Wilson was more or less officiating over a street fight that saw Direwolf maintain the upper hand for almost all of its fourteen minute duration. After three near falls by RAW-C's favorite candidate for "Father of the Year" (is that line getting old yet or what?), VCA appeared to have had enough. Not only did the former Carolina
Heavyweight Champion open up the proverbial can of whoop ass from God knows where, he somehow opened up a small can of pepperspray that he then used to blind both Direwolf and (unintentionally) Slick Wilson as well.
The referee no longer a consideration, VCA signalled for someone from the audience and, much to no one's surprise, was joined moments later by Axe Abraham, whose bass guitar was unslung and ready for battle.
Neither VCA nor Abraham, however, was ready for D.W.'s own outside interference as little Barry Owens III and his faithful nanny, Ned, came rushing to the rescue and made a very memorable assist as Barry took out Abraham with a (GASP!!) big mouthful of groin!!
That's right, you heard me. Barry bit the bass man's balls. And, as Little Barry was busy with the bass man's balls, Ned nailed VCA in the back of the head with Barry's mahogany stick, dropping the former Carolina Champion to the floor as the stick broke in two.
Several moments passed as Barry and Ned dragged Axe Abraham away by his...family jewels...and both VCA and Direwolf struggled back to their feet. Unfortunately, VCA made it to a vertical base first and was able to nail the groggy Direwolf in the face with a piece of
Barry's mahogany, scoring the win as a still fuzzy Slick Wilson counted the one-two-three. Winner: Vincent Constantine Augustus (Mahogany!!!) Time: 14:22 (***) TD: That no good – DDD: Hey, all's fair in love and war and falls count anywhere matches, Timmy!!! TD: Augustus with a big win there, looking ready to climb back to the top of the ladder here in RAW Carolina – you know Marc Tower had to be watching closely!!
DDD: Vinnie'll kill him!!!! If he gets past Blackjack!! TD: Would you stop it with that?!? Jack Anderson has said repeatedly he's not the masked man – and that has to be good enough for you!!!
DDD: It's not. TD: Oh, brother. But first – Roadkill Rick is out, Dave Pitrelli has muscled his way in. At stake – The Piedmont Championship.
"FAST" EDDIE CUTLASS © vs. "DREADMIND" DAVE PITRELLI, Piedmont Heavyweight Title The fans didn't need any reason to boo Dave Pitrelli before tonight – number one, he's a nasty sunuvabitch. Number two, he was Mark Adams'
tag partner. Number three, he made some very distasteful comments upon Adams' death. And now he attacked their guy Eddie Cutlass?!?
Let the boos commence. Cutlass came to the ring, title slung over one shoulder, head wrapped from his earlier injury, looking every bit a fife and drum away from a Revolutionary War recruiting poster. But he was ready to fight – and so was Pitrelli.
Pitrelli went right after Cutlass from the opening bell, clubbing him to the mat with heavy forearms to the head, loosening the hasty tape job enough to rip it off and try and choke the life out of Cutlass, as Slick Wilson threatened the DQ. Pitrelli relented – just long enough for Cutlass to delight the crowd with a stiff low blow, doubling Pitrelli over in pain.
Cutlass then took to the air, keeping the much larger Dreadmind off balance with a variety of flying stuff, including a nifty leg lariat from the middle turnbuckle for two. He then got greedy, and tried another one – only to get powerbombed out of mid-air for a two count that was THIS close.
Although the shot looked painful, many were concerned at just how slow Cutlass got to his feet. Pitrelli pounced, and lifted Cutlass up for another powerbomb – this time drilling the back of his head right into the top turnbuckle(!!). Cutlass went down motionless, and
Pitrelli turned to jawjack at the Charlotte crowd. When he turned back around, Cutlass still hadn't moved. Pitrelli dropped to make the cover, and although Cutlass was certainly close enough to get a foot across the bottom rope, he didn't. Three seconds later, we had a new Piedmont Champion, and a nervous crowd watched as medical workers poured into the ring to check on Eddie Cutlass.
Winner (and new champion): Dave Pitrelli (Powerbomb into the turnbuckle) Time: 6:12 (**) TD: A very scary scene here in Charlotte, as Eddie Cutlass is being carried out of here on a stretcher.
DDD: Very scary is right – just underscores how dangerous this sport is – especially when you ignore your doctors.
TD: Eddie Cutlass certainly on his way to the hospital here with yet another head injury, and a pall has fallen over this arena – but it's time for the main event.
DDD: I think Blackjack's gonna take it!! TD: Stop it!! Jack has said over and ov – Never mind. MARC TOWER © vs. JACK ANDERSON, Carolina Heavyweight Title You think the fans were pumped for this one?!? Hoo, boy. In a break from tradition, the champion was introduced first in order to allow
Tower a moment alone with the fans to celebrate his title win publicly for the first time. A real nice moment, and the crowd reacted with thunderous applause for a hard-working guy made good.
As Jack Anderson was announced, the applause quickly died down to confusion, when who should step through the curtain but The Guy
Dressed All in Black. (DDD: "I told you!! I told you!!") Tower was a bit confused, as well, and TGDAB took full advantage, blindsiding him before the bell and putting the boots to him.
TGDAB, Blackjack, or Jack Anderson – it didn't matter. The fans were now solidly behind the champion, and he rewarded their support by quickly turning the tide with a strong Steel City Slam, getting a two count from referee `Cottonmouth' Joe Lynch.
At the five minute mark, a very confused Felicity Vass wandered out to ringside, her leggy beauty somewhat dampered by her moist eyes – obviously with tears. What has happened to her man?!? (DDD: "Come sit on MY lap, baby. I'll comfort you.") Her appearance seemed to energize the challenger, who quickly dropped the champ with a hurricanrana and then followed up with a nice guillotine legdrop from the top leaving him THIS close to winning the title.
Then, at the nine minute mark, the unthinkable happened – Jack Anderson(!!) made his way to ringside(!!). A pair of broken handcuffs on each wrist, the Crimson Spitfire was rubbing his head, evidently in pain. Felicity was surprised. Tower was surprised.
And you better believe TGDAB was surprised. Anderson leapt into the ring and quickly took down TGDAB with a clothesline. Lynch seemed as unsure of what to do as the rest of us, and let it go. Anderson grabbed TGDAB by the mask and threw him over the top rope – and then dropped an unsuspecting Tower with a bulldog!!
As the crowd roared in confusion, Anderson plopped Tower on the top rope, looking to set him up for the Fireball Special – but Tower reversed into a powerbomb(!!). Another two count. This time, Tower went to the top rope, looking to end it with his top rope shoulderblock – But here came Dirk Robertson (Overbooked, you say?!?)
with a chair, waffling him. This time, Lynch had had enough and called for the bell. Robertson followed Tower into the ring, and went to work with the chair – until Jack Anderson made the save, dropkicking Robertson out of the ring.
But that's not all, folks. As Anderson helped Tower to his feet, Robertson's Network associate Dave Pitrelli hit the scene with his own steel chair, decking Tower. Anderson went after him, but was tackled from behind by TGDAB. As Pitrelli delivered several shots to
Tower's back, Anderson managed to wrestle TGDAB down to the mat – and went for the mask. But before he could get it off, Pitrelli blasted him with the chair and TGDAB bolted towards the locker room.
Decision: No Contest Time: 9:34 (* ½) TD: What a wild scene to finish Queen City Showdown – And Marc Tower just cleared Pitrelli out with a running knee to the back!! Here comes Jayson Rayge with some backup – Jack Anderson and the Blood
Brothers standing tall!! DDD: But, Dallas – Anderson's right there!! WHO is Blackjack?!? TD: That's a good question, Dean-O!! Good night, everybody!! --- © RAW Carolina, MMI. http://www.yahoogroups.com/group/RAWCarolina
