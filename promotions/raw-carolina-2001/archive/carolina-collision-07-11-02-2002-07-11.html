Carolina Collision 07.11.02

11 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We open with a shot of legendary Carolinas promoter Jackie Boone. . . dissolve to a shot of an older Boone, standing in the ring with his
Carolina champion, "Showboat" Eddie Cannon. . .roll over to another shot, this time of an older Cannon, in his current capacity as RAW
Carolina honcho. . .
[Shot of legendary tag-team The Washington Brothers, Omar and Orlando smiling, joking with their fans. . .Another shot, this time the brutal tandem of Code Red, drilling Tommy Victory with an assisted powerbomb. . .A third shot, this time of current tag monsters
Purgatory, holding the Carolina Tag-Team Titles high. . . ["Superstar" Beau Ramsey battling "Handsome" Harvey Cage in the early
80's over the Carolina Title. . .The late lamented Ace Matthews taking on "Stellar" Steve Keller for the same title. . .and finally, "Fast" Eddie Cutlass delivers a Guarantee on "Sensational"
Scott Studd in their battle for the Carolina strap. . . [An old-fashioned Carolina Championship Wrestling logo, spinning slowly, spins into a RAW Carolina logo. . .
====================================================================== ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! C A R O L I N A C O L L I S I O N ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ! ====================================================================== [And we're LIVE on NASN at the Ace Matthews Center in Greenville,
South Carolina!! "Shining" Mike Diamond and "Dirty" Dick Rogers are stationed at ringside, ready to call the night’s action.]
Sign sampling: O WHITE WOLF, WHERE ART THOU? I WANT THUNDER TIX!! BEAUTY & THE BEAST: THE REAL TAG CHAMPS!! And of course: KILL THE CLOWN!!!!!! MD: Hello, sports fans, and welcome to another edition of Carolina
Collision!! I’m your host, “Shining” Mike Diamond, and with me as always, my broadcast partner –
“Dirty” Dick Rogers!! DR: That’s right, Mikey, and we’re less than a week away from Carolina Thunder – and what a show that promises to be!!
MD: Yessir, eleven great matches are on tap for the folks at the legendary Independence Arena in Charlotte –
DR: And don’t forget those watching US around the country on NASN!! MD: How could I?!? You’ll be there, I’ll be there – Tuesday, July 16th!! But first, we’ve got a great show lined up for you folks tonight, headlined by a mixed tag match as Van
Wild teams with Val to take on his blood rival Ric Beauty and the conniving Vicki!!
DR: Yeah, she’s as conniving as she is beautiful!! MD: We’re also going to see The Bard in action, as well as his opponent at Thunder, the enigmatic Valec Corso!!
DR: And don’t forget MY guy – Roadkill Rick is in action tonight!! MD: But we’re going to kick things off with an awesome tag match that will be a preview of TWO Thunder matches – Mark Gray teams with The Omega Kid to take on the awesome Mr. Blonde and Ryan
Delaney!!
DR: Should be a good one – take it away, Bobby Lee!! --------------------------------------------------------------------- “WHITE TIGER” MARK GRAY & THE OMEGA KID vs. RYAN DELANEY & MR. BLONDE --------------------------------------------------------------------- The fans were solidly behind young high-flyers Gray and The Kid in this one, eager for their heroes to get in one last victory over their respective rivals before Thunder next Tuesday night. As for their opponents, well, let’s just say that neither man was too pleased to be in tag action here – Mr. Blonde going so far as to don a sad comedy mask, indicative of his displeasure with this booking choice.
Gray had hardly stepped through the ropes before he was Pearl Harbored by Blonde, who hit the second generation star with a heavy double axe-handler that sent him back out to the floor, and Blonde followed him out with evil intent. This pretty much set the tone for the entire match, as The Omega Kid displayed a viciousness we haven’t seen from him before – employing not ONE single wrestling move, looking not to win but to maim Ryan Delaney.
Delaney had a hard time getting into the match; it didn’t look like Blonde understood the rules of a tag match – well, either that or Blonde just wanted to kick ALL the ass himself. Either way,
Omega kept rolling past Blonde to sucker-punch Delaney, igniting another four way brawl. After ten minutes of this wild, uncontrollable brawling, the bell rang, and the match was declared a
VERY unsatisfying time limit draw.
As the bell rang, Blonde decided he wasn’t through – and sucker-punched his own teammate Delaney, and then hit him with a
Blonde Bombshell powerbomb before deciding to call it an evening.
--- MD: What a wild scene here to open Collision!! DR: I’ll tell ya, Mikey, if that’s any indication of what Thunder’s gonna be like – I can’t wait!! MD: Hang on, Dick – I’m getting word that there’s something going on in the back – What do you have, Vern?!?
[Cut to the locker room area, where young Isaiah Walker is introduced to the audience the hard way – headfirst into the camera. The camera spins, and falls to the floor, and we get a sideways, low camera angle of the young Texan getting slammed headfirst into a row of lockers, and then get powerbombed on the locker room floor – by Purgatory??]
[Commercials.] MD: We’re back, fans – DR: And what the hell was THAT?!? MD: Well, Isaiah Walker was scheduled to take on Roadkill Rick here tonight, but it doesn’t look like he’s gonna be in any shape to make this match.
DR: Yeah, I know what the hell THAT was – but why did Purgatory attack the kid?!? Were they softening him up for R-K-R?!?
MD: I seriously doubt it – Roadkill Rick’s had problems with the former SWF contigent here in RAW-C of late – That can’t be it!!
DR: Then what?!? MD: I don’t know!! Rick’s up in the ring, and I don’t think Walker’s gonna make it – Kevin
Smith’s raising RKR’s hand!! DR: Man, we all got cheated here – I wanted to see my man in action!! MD: Well, you’ll be at ringside for Thunder – DR: And he’s gonna thrash Bruiser Flinn!! MD: We’ll see!! [Commercials.] MD: All right, folks, it’s time for us to get another look at Valec Corso, a man who feels that he has yet to be challenged here in the Carolinas!! DR: I tell you what, Mikey, I don’t care for all this painting crap, but in the ring, I like his style!!
MD: Let’s get up to the ring!! ----------------------------- STONEY COOPER vs. VALEC CORSO ----------------------------- For the second week in a row, the enigmatic Valec Corso was out for blood. Eager to prove that he’s better than this
‘inferior’ competition, Corso had the match with the obviously outclassed Cooper won several times, each time forgoing the pinfall to dish out more punishing suplexes.
When Corso finally did go for some pinfalls, he ended each at the count of two by pulling Cooper up by his hair, infuriating the
Greenville crowd more and more each time. Finally, with Slick Wilson threatening a disqualification, Corso climbed to the top rope and delivered a Highway to Hell somersault legdrop that scored the merciful three count.
As the bell rang, Corso decided he hadn’t had enough, and slapped on a Dante’s Inferno Tazzmission. Wilson urged him to break the hold as Cooper flailed wildly – until The Bard arrived, armed with a steel chair intended for his Carolina Thunder opponent, sending him scurrying to the showers.
--- MD: I never thought I’d hear myself say this – but thank God for The Bard!! DR: A painter, a writer – Bah!! Gimme some FIGHTING!! MD: Well, I will, but first I give you Vernon St. Jean in the locker room. Vern?!? [Cut to Vernon St. Jean, ace reporter on the scene, standing in an empty locker room alongside young rookie Tom Roman. Roman is a shade over six feet tall, around 240 – a good looking kid. Well, next to Intern Vern, anyway.]
VERN- I’m here with newcomer Tom Roman, fresh from the Suplex Academy. Tom, welcome to RAW Carolina. ROMAN- It’s a pleasure to be here, Vern. I just hope that I can make all the guys back at the Academy proud.
VERN- I’m sure good old Greased Lightning and his crew already are, Tom. Have you had a chance to catch up with any of your old classmates yet?
ROMAN- Yes, I worked out this morning with Othello over at Gold’s, got caught up. Scotty’s a bit harder to catch up with. But I guess being a RAW tag-team champion takes up a good bit of time.
VERN- No doubt. So, Tom – [And wouldn’t you know it, we’re interrupted by The Henchmen. Dressed in street clothes, these two dimbulbs are of course still wearing their matching black masks. Thug holds a manila envelope in one hand.]
THUG: Vernon, long time no see, tough guy. And look at you, Mister
Tom Roman, Mister Big Time Superstar, getting his own interview.
VSJ: We don’t need any trouble here, fellas. BRUTE: Yeah, maybe not, but I bet you’d like to get your hands on this here envelope. VSJ: Oh, yeah?!? BRUTE: Oh, yeah. We’ve just been. . .hanging out in the Team Chaos locker room, and somebody left this behind. THUG: You know, Vernie, I never liked you. You never had the time of day for us. Who knows, maybe if we’d ever get an interview, maybe WE’D be the RAW Tag-Team Champions, insteada that schmo
Starring. VSJ: Look, guys, I just fill my assignments. If Showboat wanted you interviewed, then – [Intern Vern is cut short when Brute slaps him across the face with the envelope.] BRUTE: Shuddup, Vern. [Suddenly, the seemingly forgotten Roman jumps into action, nearly beheading Brute with a clothesline. Thug charges the rookie, who scoops him up into a fireman’s carry, and then takes him down
HARD with the samoan drop he calls The Fall of Rome – and there’s one for Brute, as well. Having laid waste to The
Henchmen, Roman picks up the envelope, opening it as he does so. He pulls out a sheet of paper, and quickly reads over it, eyes widening as he does so.]
ROMAN: Wow. [He hands the sheet to St. Jean, and walks off. Vern, a bit shaken by the whole incident, reads over the sheet, his eyes also widening.]
VSJ: It’s The Chaos Rules. We’ve all been wondering just what they are – and I’ve got the BIG SCOOP!! From what I can tell, it’s divided into five minute periods. The match starts as a regular old match. . .
(pausing as he reads). . .Then, five minutes in – [Vern’s reading light suddenly gets a bit darker, as a 7’4” shadow falls across his face: Dementia. Flanked by Synn, I’d say they were here for the envelope.] SYNN: Now. [Vern gulps slowly before folding the sheet back into thirds, and then sliding it into the envelope. He places it in Synn’s outstretched hand, and then backs away sheepishly. Dementia reaches down towards the camera, and pulls a plugBZZZZZZZZZZZZZ. . . .]
[Commercials.] MD: Well, we’re back – hopefully we can get with Vern later on and see just what The Chaos Rules are!!
DR: I doubt it!! If you want to find Vern, you better check the airport!! He’s gonna be running for his life!! He hasn’t seen that much action since his senior prom!!
MD: Vern went to prom?!? DR: You’re right – He’s NEVER seen this much action!! ----------------------------- THE BARD vs. ACHILLES FREEMAN ----------------------------- The next match featured the. . .unique. . .Bard in action, as the veteran took on the self-styled Strongest Cruiser in the World, one
Achilles Freeman. From the get-go, this match was down and dirty on the mat, where both men prefer it. It took a few minutes, but the
Greenville crowd soon warmed to the action, and began cheering every hold and counterhold.
The Bard has nearly thirty pounds on Freeman, and soon began to exert control over the match, breaking out a nice brainbuster for a two count. Freeman then spent some time working his way out of an anklelock, but the damage was done as he could hardly stand. The veteran Bard then put him away with his patented Pen Is Mightier Than the Sword, a tombstone piledriver into a boston crab for the quick submission.
--- MD: Well, our first look at The Bard here on Collision, and I have to say – I’m impressed!!
DR: Yeah, he’s a lot better in the ring than he is on the stage!! Did you see his last performance?!? Bardeo?!? Gimme a break!!
MD: Commercial break?!? Sure!! We gotta pay the bills, but we’ll be right back!! [Commercials.] [We come back from commercial ‘hot’, as they say, in the locker room where Roadkill Rick is brutally attacking referee Kevin
Smith for no apparent reason. This vicious assault appears completely
unprovoked -- ] MD: Well, Dick, you wanted to see your man in action – there he is!! This is despicable!!
DR: Eh, that Smith always had a big mouth – he probably deserves this!! MD: And now RKR is locking on the Texas Speedbump!! We need some help back there – finally here comes RAW-C’s new head of security Tommy Victory, and the Canadian Superstar has a host of
Greenville’s Finest with him!!
DR: Shouldn’t have canceled his match, I guess. MD: Well, while they straighten things out in the back, it’s time for our main event!! Van Wild and Ric Beauty have been at each other’s throats literally since the day Beauty arrived here back in March!! The great match at the Classic, the Last Man Standing match a few weeks later –
DR: And then that pretty boy Wild went and stole The Beast’s woman!! MD: Well, technically, I think she was Beauty’s woman – I always thought Val was The Beast’s woman. . .At any rate, in just five days they’ll meet one more time to settle their score once and for all –
DR: In the STEEL CAGE!! MD: Thank you, Dick, but first: an interesting tag match. Beauty teams up with his valet Val to take on Wild and Vicki!! It’s our main event, and it’s next!!
------------------------------------- VAN WILD & VICKI vs. RIC BEAUTY & VAL ------------------------------------- As you can imagine (and probably HOPED), the men wrestled most of this match, since Vicki and Val aren’t exactly what you’d call trained grapplers. Beauty tried to get in Wild’s head early on by tagging in Val – but that didn’t faze Wild, who scooped her up into an airplane spin(!!), taking a few revolutions before dropping her on the mat and tagging in Vicki. Wild then Irish whipped
Val right into Vicki, who slapped the taste right out of her former. .
.well, WHATEVER their relationship was. Labels are so limiting. Anyway, that slap echoed throughout the Matthews Center, and the ladies soon turned to brawling, rolling around the mat to general approval from the crowd. Diamond and Rogers gave up trying to call this one as Wild and Beauty charged into the ring, not wanting to wait for a tag OR Tuesday night, and began pounding away on each other.
Val rolled out to the floor and came back with a steel chair, and took a wild swing at Vicki – but Wild was there to stop it, jerking the chair right out of Val’s hands – and held it in front of his face, just where Beauty would like to see it as he superkicked the chair, laying Wild out cold for the 1-2-3. Beauty proceeded to take the chair and deliver several more shots to Wild as Val held a screaming Vicki in a bearhug in the corner, forcing her to watch as the show went off the air.
