RAW Carolina TV -- Debut Episode!!!

16 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens at 2:00 a.m. on Wednesday, June 13, 2001. The North
American Sports Network (NASN) cuts back from commercials to a black screen and a voiceover announcement.]
VO: "Undersea Adventures" will no longer be brought to you at this time. Please stand by as NASN presents wrestling action from
RAW-Carolina on... "RAW-C TV." [The image on the screen changes to a television soundstage where a brown haired man in his late thirties stands in the center of an empty wrestling ring surrounded by bleacher-style seating. The man, in blue jeans, black boots, a white t-shirt, and a dark gray sport jacket, looks around the studio and turns into the camera with a microphone in hand.]
MAN: Good morning, wrestling fans, and welcome to "RAW-C TV" on the
North American Sports Network. I'm Tim Dallas and I'll be your host as we showcase some of the great wrestling action you're seeing LIVE! all over the Carolinas. Today's broadcast features five exciting matches that took place last night in this very ring, including two first round matches in the RAW-Carolina Television Title Tournament. So, without any further adieu, let's go back in time to six hours ago for
"RAW-C TV!!" [Fade back to the ring as RAW-C ring announcer Bobby Lee Wilcox, in black jeans, sneakers, and a RAW-C polo shirt, steps through the ropes with a microphone in hand.]
================================================== KID DYNAMITE -VS- "GREASED LIGHTNING" BOBBY PARKER ================================================== BLW: Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is scheduled for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Introducing first...from Boulder, Colorado, and weighing in at 216 pounds...Kid Dynamite!!
[The crowd pops as Dynamite makes his way to the ring.] BLW: His opponent is from Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and weighs in at 227 pounds..."Greased Lightning" Bobby Parker!!
[There is a mix of cheers and boos as the veteran Parker makes his way to the ring and the camera cuts to the Announce Desk where Bobby Lee
Wilcox is taking a seat with Tim Dallas.]
TD: Good evening, wrestling fans, and welcome to "RAW-C TV." I'm Tim
Dallas and my cohost tonight is ring announcer Bobby Lee Wilcox.
BLW: Hi, Tim. I think it's fantastic that RAW-Carolina is finally getting the recognition it deserves and I'm looking forward to being here with you and the fans every week!!
TD: So am I, Bobby Lee. Now let's go back to the ring as the referee calls for the bell and veteran high-flyer Bobby Parker locks up with
Kid Dynamite, one of the most recent graduates of The Suplex Academy!!
[Kid Dynamite executes a bodyslam on Bobby Parker. Kid Dynamite goes for a clothesline but Parker counters it with a hiptoss and goes for the pin. The referee counts: One, two, shoulder up.]
TD: And there's an inverted DDT by "Greased Lightning" Bobby
Parker...but Kid Dynamite blocks it and takes Parker down with a spinning leg lariat!!
[Kid Dynamite hits Bobby Parker with a flying dropkick. Kid Dynamite whips Bobby Parker into the ropes but Parker reverses it. Bobby Parker hits Kid Dynamite with an elbow and takes him down with a spin kick.]
BLW: Cover by Parker...but the referee is out of position and Dynamite gets back to his feet!! We're seeing some great moves from Kid
Dynamite, Tim. His time at The Suplex Academy really paid off!! TD: Yes it did, Bobby Lee...and I'm sure the time he spent working with veterans like Bobby Parker helped too!!
BLW: That's right. Parker is a former tag team champion with Mark
Golden...who we'll be seeing in action a little later tonight...and spends a lot of his time training our developmental talent at The
Academy!!
[Kid Dynamite whips Bobby Parker into the ropes but Parker nails him with an Asai moonsault.
The referee counts: One, shoulder up. Bobby Parker goes for a kick to the midsection but Kid Dynamite blocks it and hits Parker with a
Northern Lights suplex. The referee counts: One, two, shoulder up. Kid
Dynamite goes for a front facelock but Bobby Parker blocks it.]
TD: Nice series of exchanges by Dynamite and Parker...and there's a big roundhouse punch by "Greased Lightning!!"
BLW: The referee warning Bobby Parker about those punches...and Kid
Dynamite makes a nice counter with an inverted atomic drop...but Bobby
Parker blocks it and throws him out of the ring!!
[Bobby Parker runs to the ropes and goes for a slingshot somersault plancha over the top rope but Kid Dynamite ducks out of the way. Kid
Dynamite rolls Bobby Parker back into the ring and follows him in.]
TD: "Greased Lightning" Bobby Parker looking a little disoriented...and Kid Dynamite is going to the top rope!!
[Kid Dynamite hits his "TNT" top rope superkick on Bobby Parker and goes for the pin. The referee counts: One, two, three.]
BLW: Kid Dynamite wins his first match on "RAW-C TV"...and we'll be right back with Round One of the RAW-Carolina Television Title
Tournament!!
[Cut to commercials and back to the ring.] ================================================== TV#1: "ICEMAN" MARK ADAMS -VS- THE UNKNOWN SOLDIER ================================================== BLW: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is a first round match in the RAW-Carolina Television Title Tournament and is scheduled for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Introducing first...from Parts
Unknown and weighing in at 319 pounds...The Unknown Soldier!!
[The crowd boos as the monstrous Unknown Soldier makes his way to the ring.] BLW: His opponent is from Greenville, South Carolina, by way of New
Orleans, Louisiana. Weighing in at 240 pounds...he is "Iceman" Mark
Adams!!
[The crowd pops as AC/DC's "House of Jazz" plays over the P.A. System and Adams makes his way to the ring. Adams steps through the ropes and
The Unknown Soldier attacks him before the bell.]
TD: And we're back as this match gets underway before the bell with a
Death Valley Driver on "The Iceman" by The Unknown Soldier!!
BLW: What a powerful man, Tim...but look at Adams. He got right back to his feet...and he's daring The Soldier to give him more!!
[The Unknown Soldier nails Mark Adams with a power bomb. The referee counts: One, two, kickout. The Unknown Soldier goes for another Death
Valley Driver but Mark Adams counters it with a backdrop and takes The
Soldier down with a vertical suplex.]
TD: Adams a 10-year veteran of the sport and a multiple time champion. His addition to the RAW-C roster, along with men like Flash, Eddie
Cutlass, Sidious the Clown, and former Piedmont Champion Kirk Maclean, makes the playing field here in the Carolinas a very interesting one!!
[Mark Adams runs into the ropes. Mark Adams goes for a cartwheel kick but The Unknown Soldier side-steps and he only hits air. The Unknown
Soldier goes for a DDT but Mark Adams blocks it. Mark Adams goes for a hiptoss but The Unknown Soldier counters it with a facerake.]
BLW: Clothesline on Adams by The Soldier...and there's a devastating piledriver!! [The Unknown Soldier goes for a pin and the referee counts: One, kickout. The Unknown Soldier goes for a clothesline but Mark Adams counters with a crucifix. The referee counts: One, two, kickout. Mark
Adams nails The Unknown Soldier with a cradle suplex and a few scattered fans start cheering.]
TD: Adams with an Irish whip into the ropes...and there's a jumping
DDT on the Unknown Soldier!!
[Mark Adams executes a back heel kick on The Unknown Soldier. Mark
Adams uses a power bomb on The Unknown Soldier. The referee counts:
One, two, shoulder up.] BLW: Adams with a chop to the chest of The Unknown Soldier...but it doesn't phase him...and The Soldier takes Adams down with a kick to the midsection!!
[The Unknown Soldier nails Mark Adams with a power bomb. The referee counts: One, kickout. The Unknown Soldier hits Mark Adams with a knee to the back and goes for a kick to the midsection but Adams blocks it.
Mark Adams goes for a lariat but The Unknown Soldier hits him with an elbow.] TD: Adams looks a bit disoriented...and The Soldier takes advantage by throwing him out to the floor!!
[The Unknown Soldier goes through the ropes and throws Mark Adams back into the ring. The Unknown Soldier punches Mark Adams. A portion of the crowd is booing The Unknown Soldier as he goes for a piledriver but Mark Adams blocks it.]
BLW: Adams with an Irish whip into the ropes...but The Soldier catches him with a huge clothesline!!
TD: The Unknown Soldier has Mark Adams on the ropes...and he's going for another Death Valley Driver...but Adams counters!!
[Mark Adams executes "The Big Easy" (a Bridging Northern Lights
Hammerlock Suplex) on The Unknown Soldier and goes for the pin. The referee counts: One, two, three.]
BLW: Adams wins!! Just like that Adams wins the match!! TD: And let's go up to the ring as Vernon St. Jean gets a few words from "The Iceman!!" [Cut to the ring.] VSJ: Mark Adams, you've just advanced in the Television Title
Tournament. Any thoughts? MA: Aside from Jimmy Stone STILL not having the balls to get the job done and take me out like he says? Nah!!
[The crowd pops.] VSJ: Well next week you'll face either Tully Roebuck or Alec Price in
Round Two of this tournament as four other men compete in Round One action. Your opinions on Roebuck and Price as your possible opponents?
MA: Alec Price is a talented rookie, Vern, but I'll be honest. I think
Sears Roebuck will be the one staring down "The Iceman" from across the ring just because he's got a little more ring savvy than his opponent.
VSJ: And? MA: And "Iceman" Mark Adams will be freezing Mr. Roebuck's credit limit and moving on to that brand new TV Title!!
[Adams leaves the ring to a fairly good-sized pop.] VSJ: "Iceman" Mark Adams, ladies and gentlemen!! TD: And we'll be back with Roebuck and Price after these messages!! [Cut to commercials and back to the ring.] ================================================== TV#2: "AWESOME" ALEC PRICE -VS- TULLY ROEBUCK ================================================== BLW: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is a first round match in the RAW-Carolina Television Title Tournament and is scheduled for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Introducing first...from San Diego,
California, and weighing in at 243 pounds..."Awesome" Alec Price!!
[The crowd pops as Price enters the ring.] BLW: His opponent is from Greasy Corners, New Mexico, and weighs in at
284 pounds. He is Tully Roebuck!!
[The boos from the crowd drown out Roebuck's music as he makes his way to the ring.] TD: And, as Roebuck and Price lock up, there's the bell!! [Alec Price gets an abdominal stretch on Tully Roebuck. Tully Roebuck tries to escape the hold and breaks free after 19 seconds. Alec Price takes Tully Roebuck down with a bulldog and goes for the pin. The referee counts: One, kickout. Alec Price whips Tully Roebuck into the ropes and goes for a hiptoss but Roebuck counters it with a facerake.]
BLW: And now we're seeing a very familiar side of Tully Roebuck as he punches Alec Price and drives him face-first into the turnbuckle!!
[The crowd boos as Tully Roebuck kicks Alec Price and takes him down with a flying clothesline. Tully Roebuck goes for a hiptoss but Alec
Price reverses it. Tully Roebuck runs into the ropes and executes a flying clothesline on Alec Price.]
TD: Roebuck pulls Price back to his feet...and there's a jawbreaker by
Price on the man from Greasy Corners, New Mexico!!
[A portion of the crowd is cheering Alec Price. Tully Roebuck goes for a vertical suplex but Alec Price blocks it. Alec Price goes for a piledriver but Tully Roebuck blocks it and whips him into the ropes.]
BLW: Double clothesline by Roebuck and Price...and both men go down hard!! TD: I've seen stiff clotheslines before, Bobby Lee, but that one looked brutal!! [Alec Price uses a slingshot on Tully Roebuck and takes him down with a bulldog. Alec Price goes for a hiptoss but Tully Roebuck blocks it.
Tully Roebuck nails Alec Price with a chop. Tully Roebuck enjoys the boos as he executes a DDT on Alec Price and goes for the pin.]
BLW: Cover by Roebuck...but only a one-count as Price kicks out!! [Alec Price goes for a camel clutch but Tully Roebuck blocks it. Tully
Roebuck goes for a DDT but Alec Price blocks it. Alec Price executes a hiptoss on Tully Roebuck and whips him into the ropes. Alec Price nails Tully Roebuck with a flying headscissors.]
TD: Roebuck seeing stars as Alec Price whips him into the ropes...but he somehow gets it all together and catches Price with a flying bulldog!!
BLW: Price is down, Tim...and he went down hard. Why isn't Roebuck trying to wrap this up? TD: Because guys like Tully Roebuck like to hurt their opponents first, Bobby Lee. [Tully Roebuck pulls Alec Price to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Tully Roebuck seemingly enjoys the boos as he nails Alec Price with a kick to the midsection.]
BLW: Roebuck with a piledriver on Price...and there's a cover. [The referee counts: One, two, and Tully Roebuck pulls Alec Price up by the hair.] TD: Oh, come on!! [Tully Roebuck hits Alec Price with a short-arm clothesline and calls for the "Rio Grande Backbreaker." The crowd boos as Roebuck locks in the "Rio Grande Backbreaker" and Alec Price immediately taps out.]
BLW: It's over!! Tully Roebuck will face Mark Adams next week in Round
Two action as... TD: Wait a second, folks!! It looks like Roebuck is calling Vernon St. Jean into the ring!! [St. Jean climbs into the ring, a bit intimidated by the blustery, heavily sweating Roebuck.]
TR: Whoo-Whee!! The Wild Bunch rides again!! Next week, Iceman...by the time I'm done with ya, you're gonna wish you had done melted,
'cause I'm gonna break ya in half with the Rio Grande Backbreaker and feed both halves of ya to my front porch dogs!!
[...] TR: And I ain't gonna do it for some worthless tin strap. I'm gonna do it...'cause I can!! [Cut to commercials.] ================================================== WILD STYLE -VS- MARK GOLDEN AND JOSEPH NIGHTHORSE ================================================== [Cut back to the ring from commercials.] BLW: This contest is a tag team match scheduled for one fall. Introducing first...at a total combined weight of 530 pounds, the team of Mark Golden...and Joseph Nighthorse!!
[The crowd reacts with a mix of cheers and boos as Golden and
Nighthorse make their way to the ring.]
BLW: Their opponents hail from Tacoma, Washington, and weigh in at a combined 365 pounds. They are Shannon Hamilton...and Drew Brody...Wild
Style!!
[The crowd explodes in a chorus of boos as Hamilton and Brody make their way to the ring, stopping halfway down the aisle to take a sign that reads "Sidious We LUV U!!" from a young girl and tear it in half.]
BLW: That was uncalled for!! TD: Of course it was, Bobby Lee. That's why they did it!! [Shannon Hamilton locks up with Mark Golden. Shannon Hamilton whips
Mark Golden into the ropes but Golden reverses it. Shannon Hamilton hits Mark Golden with a kick and catches him in an armbar submission but Golden reaches the ropes after for 5 seconds.]
TD: Hamilton with a springboard moonsault...and there's the cover!! [The referee counts: One, two, thr...shoulder up.] BLW: Nice escape by Mark Golden. This match should be interesting,
Tim, because Wild Style is giving up 165 pounds out there tonight...and they're in the ring with a much more experienced team!!
TD: But Wild Style has the advantage, Bobby Lee, because they've worked together in the past. I think this is a first-time pairing for
Golden and Nighthorse!!
[Drew Brody enters the ring to make it two-on-one. Shannon Hamilton and Drew Brody whip Mark Golden into the ropes and hit him with a double dropkick. Drew Brody leaves the ring as Shannon Hamilton attempts to place Mark Golden on the turnbuckle. Mark Golden blocks it and nails Shannon Hamilton with a DDT.]
BLW: Oh!! [The crowd is cheering Mark Golden as he goes for the pin. The referee counts: One, two, kickout. Mark Golden hits a waistlock suplex on
Shannon Hamilton. The referee counts: One, two, thr...kickout. Mark
Golden runs into the ropes and hits Shannon Hamilton with a clothesline.]
TD: And there's a tag to Joseph Nighthorse!! [Drew Brody enters the ring but gets cut off as Joseph Nighthorse and
Mark Golden hit Shannon Hamilton with a double clothesline. Mark
Golden leaves the ring. Joseph Nighthorse goes for a kneelock submission but Shannon Hamilton blocks it. Joseph Nighthorse goes for a forearm smash but Shannon Hamilton locks Nighthorse in an armbar submission.]
BLW: Great reversal...but Nighthorse makes it to the ropes and the referee breaks the hold!!
[Shannon Hamilton takes Joseph Nighthorse down with a swinging neckbreaker. Shannon Hamilton tags out to Drew Brody.]
TD: Brody with a bodyslam on Nighthorse...but Nighthorse reverses it!! BLW: And there's a tag to Mark Golden!! [Mark Golden runs into the ropes and hits Drew Brody with a clothesline. Mark Golden executes a waistlock suplex on Drew Brody and goes for the cover. The referee counts: One, two, in the ropes.]
TD: Golden with a fireman's carry...but Brody blocks it...and there's a blind tag to Shannon Hamilton!!
[Mark Golden whips Drew Brody into the ropes as Shannon Hamilton comes from behind and takes him down with a spinning DDT. Shannon Hamilton hits Mark Golden with a Frankensteiner and goes for the cover. The referee counts: One, shoulder up. Shannon Hamilton whips Mark Golden into the ropes but Golden reverses it and takes Hamilton down with a
short-arm clothesline.] BLW: Mark Golden working the crowd...and the fans here are starting to get behind this team of Golden and Nighthorse!!
[Mark Golden nails Shannon Hamilton with a vertical suplex. Mark
Golden goes for a dropkick but Shannon Hamilton side-steps and he only hits air. Shannon Hamilton goes for a springboard moonsault but Mark
Golden rolls out of the way.]
TD: Irish whip by Hamilton...and there's an elbow to the solar plexus that takes Golden down!!
[Shannon Hamilton nails Mark Golden with a kick to the midsection. Shannon Hamilton attempts to place Mark Golden on the turnbuckle but
Golden blocks it. Shannon Hamilton tags out to Drew Brody and sets up
Mark Golden for a Fireman's Carry.]
BLW: Here it comes!! [Shannon Hamilton and Drew Brody hit Mark Golden with "Nothing As It
Seems" and Brody goes for the pin. The referee counts: One, two,
Joseph Nighthorse tries to make a save but doesn't make it, three.]
TD: And the team of Golden and Nighthorse go down to Wild Style in just under eight minutes!! Don't go away, we'll have more "RAW-C TV"
after this!! [Cut to commercials and back to Tim Dallas in the empty ring.] TD: I hope you're enjoying "RAW-C TV" so far. Right now we've come to the segment of the show where Vernon St. Jean will sit down for a one-on-one interview with a RAW-C superstar for an in-depth interview.
His guest this week...veteran powerhouse "Thunderbolt" Jack Sampson!! ================================================== IN-DEPTH INTERVIEW: "THUNDERBOLT" JACK SAMPSON ================================================== [Cut to a quiet booth in the back of Percy N' Willie's, a popular steak establishment in Florence, South Carolina, where legendary
RAW-Carolina superstar "Thunderbolt" Jack Sampson is enjoying a nice
16 ounce ribeye while his dinner companion, intrepid roving reporter
Vernon St. Jean, is helping himself to the less ambitious 10 ouncer.]
VSJ: A lot of new blood here in the Carolinas over the last few months. Thoughts? JS: A talented bunch. Maclean, Cutlass, El Cid...even that guy Augustus. I may not agree with the way Wyngarde handed him the title, but he's shown me something in the ring. He's got some talent.
VSJ: Speaking of the Carolina Heavyweight Champion, you had a match a few weeks back with Augustus, in which you suffered a loss by dubious means.
JS: Yeah, but when you've been around as long as I have, there's no excuse. What's that old saying? Fool me once, shame on you. Fool me twice, shame on me.
VSJ: You have been around awhile, Jack. I remember watching you wrestle 'Dirty' Doug Simpkins back when I was a kid...
JS: Watch it. VSJ: Oh, yeah. Sorry. Anyway...how much longer you think you'll be going out there? The aches and pains of all these years have to be catching up with you...if they haven't already.
JS: Well, I ain't gonna lie to you, Vern. It takes me a little longer to get good and stretched out before a match. It takes me a little longer to get these old bones out of bed in the morning. But as long as ol' Bones can keep stitching me up...I'll keep going out there and giving all my fans one hundred percent.
VSJ: Good enough, Jack. Now, if I can...if we can go back to your match with VCA...and I'm talking specifically about the end. Your brother Ike showed up. Now, the two of you have each had spectacular careers, but on your own terms. You've never relied on each other.
Hell, as far as I know, you've never even wrestled in the same promotion at the same time before!!
JS: That's all true. Up until now, we have always chosen different paths. Only recently have those paths...converged.
VSJ: Now I know that Ike was only here for a week before he had to return to Japan for some prior commitments. Those should be wrapped up soon from what I understand...so can we expect to see Ike back in the
Carolinas on a full-time basis?
JS: I wouldn't know. I haven't spoken with him. VSJ: Well, I'm just gonna cut right to the chase and ask the question fans all over the Carolinas want to know. Are we finally gonna see the
Sampson brothers together in the ring?
[Sampson stares at the camera for a long while. Then, rather than answer, he stands up and walks out of the restaurant. The camera swings on St. Jean, who shrugs his shoulders as the image cuts to commercials and back to live action.]
================================================== THE EMPIRE CLUB -VS- DURHAM, DOBBS, AND MURPHY ================================================== TD: We're back with tonight's main event, folks...a warm-up for Saturday's six-man match as Carolina Heavyweight Champion Vincent
Constantine Augustus, Piedmont Champion David Jericho, and the man they call Stone take on perennial favorites Luke Dobbs, Hounddog
Murphy, and Champ Durham. Let's go to Bobby Lee Wilcox in the ring for introductions!!
BLW: Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is a six man tag team match scheduled for one fall. Introducing first...at a total combined weight of 716 pounds..."All-American" Champ Durham, "Cannonball" Luke Dobbs, and Hounddog Murphy!!
[The crowd pops as Durham, Dobbs, and Murphy make their way down the aisle to the ring.] BLW: Their opponents are accompanied to the ring by "Damn Desperate" Dick Rogers and weigh in at a combined 764 pounds. They are RAW-Carolina Piedmont Champion David Jericho, Stone, and the Carolina
Heavyweight Champion...Vincent Constantine Augustus!!
[The crowd explodes in a chorus of boos as Dick Rogers leads Jericho,
Stone, and VCA to the ring. As VCA steps through the ropes, Dick
Rogers grabs the microphone from Bobby Lee Wilcox.]
DR: That's "Dirty" Dick Rogers from now on, boy...and don't you forget it!! [Dick Rogers throws down the mic as the referee calls for the bell and
VCA nails takes Champ Durham down with a football tackle. VCA goes for a fireman's carry but Champ Durham blocks it. Champ Durham punches
VCA. Champ Durham whips VCA into the ropes and takes him down with a side suplex.]
TD: Durham with a cover...but only a one count as Stone enters the ring and breaks the pin!!
BLW: What a change we've seen in Stone lately, Tim. I'm actually shocked!! TD: Don't be. I've seen it coming for a long time, especially after his less-than-impressive run in the UWF!!
[Champ Durham goes for a backbreaker but VCA blocks it. VCA goes for a piledriver but Champ Durham counters it with a backdrop. In turn, VCA counters it with a sunset flip and the referee counts: One, two, kickout.]
BLW: VCA with a tag out to Stone...and now Stone and the champion are double-teaming Champ Durham!! [Stone and VCA whip Champ Durham into the ropes and go for a double kick to the midsection but Durham counters it with a double clothesline. The crowd pops as VCA leaves the ring and Champ Durham whips Stone into the turnbuckle.]
TD: Stone hits the buckle hard...and there's the tag to Luke Dobbs!! [Luke Dobbs and Champ Durham whip Stone into the ropes and hits him with a double bodyslam. Champ Durham leaves the ring as Luke Dobbs hits Stone with a hiptoss and throws him out of the ring. The referee counts: One, two, three, Stone reenters the ring as Dick Rogers looks on with disgust from the floor.]
BLW: Dobbs with a waistlock suplex on Stone. TD: But only a two-count before the man from Boulder, Colorado, kicks out!! [Luke Dobbs takes Stone down with an elbowsmash. Champ Durham enters the ring to make it two-on-one. Luke Dobbs and Champ Durham whip Stone into the ropes and hit him with a double fist to the midsection. Champ
Durham leaves the ring as Luke Dobbs uses a waistlock suplex on
Stone.] BLW: Dobbs with a cover...and again only a two-count!! [Luke Dobbs pulls Stone to his feet and gives him a Frankensteiner...but Stone doesn't budge.] BLW: Well that proves one thing, Tim. TD: What's that? BLW: Jimmy Stone will never work in advertising. TD: Huh? BLW: The man can't SELL anything, Tim!! Get it? He can't SELL anything!! TD: *GROAN* [Stone punches Luke Dobbs. Stone whips Luke Dobbs into the ropes and takes him down with a jumping DDT. Stone goes for the pin but Hounddog
Murphy enters the ring and breaks the count. Luke Dobbs tags out to
Champ Durham. David Jericho enters the ring but gets cut off as Champ
Durham executes an enzuigiri on Stone.]
BLW: Jericho hits the ring a second time but gets cut off by Hounddog
Murphy. TD: And it looks like Durham, Murphy, and Dobbs are dominating The
Empire Club in that ring tonight!!
[Hounddog Murphy throws David Jericho over the top rope as Champ
Durham and Luke Dobbs hit Stone with a double dropkick. VCA enters the ring and takes Durham and Dobbs down with a double clothesline as
Hounddog Murphy get pulled under the bottom rope by David Jericho.]
BLW: This match is out of control!! I can't believe what I'm... [Suddenly Dick Rogers grabs a steel chair and dives under the bottom rope into the ring.] TD: What's he doing? [Dick Rogers cracks Luke Dobbs over the head with the steel chair and then turns to lay out Champ Durham the same way. The referee calls for the bell and the crowd doesn't know how to react as Rogers turns to a smiling VCA with the chair in hand.]
TD: This is absolutely disgusting. Vincent Constantine Augustus is smiling as Dick Rogers...OH MY GOD!! Rogers just nailed the Carolina
Heavyweight Champion with the steel chair!!
BLW: What's going on? Rogers has lost his mind, Tim!! He's swinging that chair at everyone around him. Stone closes in...and Stone goes down!! David Jericho and Hounddog Murphy come to the aid of their teammates...and they go down!!
TD: And the fans are on their feet!! I've never heard an ovation this loud before in my life!!
[Dick Rogers calls for a microphone.] DR: Augustus, after all I've done for you...all the kneelin' and scrapin' to get the low man's spot in The Empire Club...you and your
"boys" here couldn't even handle these three jabronies in the ring with ya!! Well ya know what, Mr. Carolina Heavyweight Champ...I QUIT!!
[The crowd pops.] DR: Hell, I more than quit!! I RETIRE!! [Dick Rogers leaves the ring and storms over to the Announce Table where Tim Dallas is just starting to stand up. He looks down at Bobby
Lee Wilcox and roughly shoves him out of his chair onto the floor, picking up a headset and taking the empty seat as Dallas looks on in amazement.]
DR: And yer on MY show now, Augustus, so you play by MY rules...'cause
I'm "Dirty" Dick Rogers...and yer not!!
[The crowd pops even louder as security puts themselves between The
Empire Club and their former lackey. Meanwhile Tim Dallas has recovered enough to speak.]
TD: That's all we have time for tonight, folks. For Bobby Lee
Wilcox...and "Dirty" Dick Rogers...
[The scene cuts ahead to Tim Dallas in the empty ring.] TD: I'm Tim Dallas saying...we'll see YOU next week...on "RAW-C TV!!" [Fade to black.] Š RAW-Carolina Television Productions 2001
