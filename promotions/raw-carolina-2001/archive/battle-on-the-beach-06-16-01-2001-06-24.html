Battle on the Beach 06.16.01

24 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RAW Carolina headed to the beach Saturday afternoon, for Battle On the
Beach, a big outdoor show literally held on the sands of Atlantic
Beach!! Results:
“CANNONBALL” LUKE DOBBS & HOUNDDOG MURPHY vs. WILD STYLE After recently competiting in the ongoing tournament to crown a RAW
Middleweight Champion, Luke Dobbs returned home to a huge ovation, brimming with confidence as he teamed with old pal Hounddog Murphy to take on the hot young duo Wild Style (Drew Brody & Shannon Hamilton).
One of these days, Hounddog and the Cannonball are going to win a match as a team – but that day’ll have to wait. Wild
Style was very impressive, showing off their trademark high-flying offense, eager to climb back up the ladder after last week’s loss in the number one contenders match.
“Suicide Bomb” Shannon Hamilton finished the match off, with his Given to Fly corkscrew top rope legdrop, that left Dobbs breathless and easy prey for the three count. As soon as the match was over, Brody grabbed the house mike from Bobby Lee Wilcox, demanding a shot next week against the winner of the tag title match later in the afternoon.
Winners: Wild Style (Given to Fly) Time: 4:22 (**) As Dobbs was helped from the ring, the Mini-Tron flickered to life for the first time on the afternoon, and the fans were given a glimpse of
David Jericho alone in the locker room, polishing his new Piedmont
Title, evidently very pleased with himself. The fans were none too pleased with this little vignette.
DIREWOLF vs. “THE BOSTON MANGLER” ADONIS MORGAN The next match was a battle of two powerhouses, as the strange newcomer Direwolf took on the young Boston strongman Adonis Morgan.
As always, Direwolf was accompanied to the ring by his foul-mouthed young son Barry, who on more than one occasion hurled obscenities at
Morgan, causing him to lose his focus on the match.
And when you’re in the ring with a savvy veteran like Direwolf, you better have all your focus, or else it’s a short night. It was a short night for Morgan. Despite giving up nearly thirty pounds to Morgan, Direwolf matched him power hold for power hold, as the two battled in a solid brawl.
At the seven minute mark, Direwolf turned the tide with a nice running piledriver, that nearly scored a three count. It did, however, leave
Morgan down long enough for Direwolf to pick him up and put him back down with his Alpha Male chokeslam, which did score a three count.
Winner: Direwolf (Alpha Male chokeslam) Time: 6:22 (* ½) “FAST” EDDIE CUTLASS vs. ALEX PATTERSON As the sun continued to beat down on the ring on this hot, muggy afternoon, two of RAW Carolina’s rising stars collided when
Eddie Cutlass met the ‘no longer known as Mr. RAW’ Alex Patterson. Patterson has suffered a string of frustrating losses recently, and looked to take it out on Cutlass, now firmly entrenched as one of the
Carolina fans’ favorites. The match was an excellent back and forth affair, as both men are excellent ring technicians, possessing of a wealth of skills.
Patterson dominated the early going, using his strength advantage to his. . .er, advantage, by hitting Cutlass with a nice array of suplexes. Fast Eddie battled back, however, using a few arm drags, a few heel kicks, and a nice German Suplex to more than hold his own.
At the twelve minute mark, Patterson planted Cutlass on the mat with a nice Hangman’s DDT, and victory looked imminent. Patterson motioned to the crowd, signaling that it was time for the RAW Bomb.
He blew a kiss to his wife Suzanne at ringside, and hoisted Cutlass up above his head – only to see Cutlass slip out the back, slid down his back into a sunset flip – and score a quick three count.
Patterson was livid – a quick stomp to Cutlass’ face, he delivered a RAW Bomb anyway. And another. And finally a third, before Sidious the Clown arrived with a steel chair to chase him away.
Winner: Eddie Cutlass (sunset flip) Time: 12:22 (*** ½) The mini-Tron again flickered to life, again in David Jericho’s locker room, where the Piedmont Champion is stretching before the main event. A knock on the door leads to a courier, who handed Jericho a manila envelope. Jericho opened it, reading softly to himself.
“New era. . .next few weeks. . .be ready. . .be warned.” Jericho laughed, balled up the message and tossed it in the trash, and then continued stretching.
DIRK “THE ERADICATOR” ROBERTSON vs. BULL “GATOR” JOHNSON Although it’s never been officially stated as such, the match between Dirk Robertson and Bull Johnson has been pegged as a top contenders match, with the winner sure to receive a shot at David
Jericho’s Piedmont Title down the road.
As the match began, the perennial thorn in Gator’s side, Sidious the Clown, made his way to ringside. But rather than antagonize
Johnson as he is wont to do, Sidious marched right up to
Johnson’s manager, the cycloptic Amazon Lilliane Devereaux, and slipped her a fifty dollar bill. . .and a condom. Before a horrifiew
Devereaux could react, Sidious winked and ran off into the crowd, much to the crowd’s delight.
The match was a nice blend of power and speed – due to the size differential, Robertson was unable to employ some of his power moves on the 450 pound monster Johnson, and relied instead on some speedy hit and run type maneuvers.
The strategy was effective, and Johnson soon was staggered – Robertson’s quickness combined with the steamy weather left him a bit short of breath, and prone for the Chicken Wing Crash, a double arm tornado DDT.
Robertson then attempted his Last Sunset (a top rope sunset flip powerbomb), but Johnson was too big, and brought his girth crashing down across Robertson’s chest. Johnson took the advantage, and brutalized Robertson with a variety of powerbombs – including a nasty wheelbarrow powerbomb that saw Robertson get his foot across the bottom rope to save a three count.
At the ten minute mark, Johnson chokeslammed Robertson over the top rope down to the sand below – and that’s when Alex
Patterson attacked. Patterson, who has had his share of issues with
Dirk Robertson over the last few weeks, quickly grabbed Robertson
– and hit him with a RAWBomb to the sand below. As Lilliane
Devereaux discussed the new tax refund with referee Jackie Boone, Jr.,
Patterson delivered another, and Johnson climbed out of the ring to join him.
The crowd erupted soon thereafter as “Fast” Eddie Cutlass arrived at ringside – battered, bruised, but ready to even the score. A wild four way brawl ensued, and Boone finally noticed long enough to call for the double disqualification. The scene got even wilder, as newcomer The Judge arrived, eager to deal with this brawl with his own peculiar brand of ‘justice’.
The brawl got just a bit wilder, with the final arrival of the evening
– a masked man, who immediately went after Patterson and
Johnson. As a swarm of RAW-C officials finally pulled them apart,
Robertson stared long and hard at the masked man – he seemed to know him, but didn’t seem too happy to see him.
Decision: Double Disqualification Time: 10:42 (** ½) But wait, there’s more. As Johnson and Devereaux made their way back to the locker room, Sidious somehow appeared behind them in the aisle. As the crowd roared, he delivered a nice running dropkick to the back of Johnson’s head, sending him sprawling headlong into a mouthful of sand. Then, just as quickly, he slapped Devereaux on the butt – and as she turned around, he leapt onto her tree-trunk like body, and planted the “Most Death-Defying Kiss of All-Time” on her lips. As she staggered about in shock,
Sidious was indeed, quicker than a hiccup as he disappeared into the crowd. “LAWBRINGER” THOMAS ADLER vs. JESSIE MULDOON vs. THE JUDGE The two new(est)comers to RAW Carolina got a stern test of their mettle at Battle On the Beach, as international superstar Jessie
Muldoon and The Judge take on perhaps the greatest competitor RAW-C has seen in some years, “Lawbringer” Thomas Adler – fresh off his grueling victory in last night’s inter-promotional
Gold Slam Cup at Madison Square Garden.
Despite his fatigue – you try wrestling four matches Friday night in New York, and then a triple threat match in North Carolina
Saturday afternoon at any age, much less Adler’s – the
Lawbringer was eager to deliver some punishment to his opponents. Their three way war of words has been well-documented elsewhere – let’s just say there’s bad blood a-brewin’. A missed blind charge sent Muldoon up and over the top rope to the sand in the early going, leaving Adler in the ring with The Judge, as the fans eagerly anticipated this battle of keen legal minds –
Clarence Darrow, eat your heart out. Adler and The Judge traded punches in the center of the ring, which quickly turned to
Adler’s advantage, given his near 100 pound weight differential. Adler then whipped Judge into the corner, and hit him with a low running dropkick to the midsection.
He then hiptossed Judge up and over the top rope, sending him crashing down into Muldoon. Muldoon didn’t take kindly to this, and he and The Judge began brawling, much to the crowd’s – and
Adler’s – delight. They barely beat the ten count back into the ring, but their brawl continued, and Adler seemed more than content to let it do so.
Soon, Muldoon had planted The Judge with a nice piledriver, and Adler pounced. He hit Muldoon with a clothesline, and carried Muldoon to the second rope with him into a nice overhead fallaway slam, that sent
Muldoon crashing into the Judge. Adler turned around, and leapt with a heavy legdrop that caught both men for a two count.
Adler dropped a big elbow on Muldoon, and then clotheslined The Judge over the top rope out of the ring. He then tossed Muldoon down to the sand into The Judge, and once again the two men hit the ground hard and came up fighting. Adler watched with amusement before climbing to the top rope, and coming off with a double Guillotine clothesline.
Moving quickly, he rolled Muldoon back into the ring, and got a two and two-thirds count. As The Judge got back onto the apron, Adler whipped Muldoon into him, sending him crashing back down to the sand. As Muldoon staggered back towards him, Adler grabbed him in a waistlock, and planted him on the mat with a German Suplex, which got the three count.
Winner: Thomas Adler (German Suplex) Time: 7:21 (** ½) As Adler celebrated in the ring, the crowd’s cheers quickly turned to heavy, heavy boos, as one of the most hated men in the history of professional wrestling returned to the Carolinas:
“Dirty” Doug Simpkins stood in the aisleway, mockingly applauding Adler. He then made his way to ringside, calling for Bobby
Lee Wilcox’ mike. The two savvy veterans eyed each other warily.
DS: “Well, well. Look at the Gold Slam Champion. All proud of himself. Thing is, I ain’t too impressed. And I got a fella with me ain’t impressed, either. He wants a piece of you in the ring next week, if you got the hair on your sack. Whaddya say. .
.tough guy? Next week, Grand Strand Slam. . . .Tommy Adler one on one with the crown jewel in the Old School Crown – ‘The
Blacksmith’ John Smythe?!?” Adler didn’t answer, but his curt nod seemed to affirm Simpkins’ on the fly matchmaking, and it was set: Adler v. Smythe next week in Myrtle Beach. SIDIOUS THE CLOWN © vs. EZ KILLER & TOMMY TRENTON, Carolina Tag-Team Titles After several appearances already in the evening, it was finally time for Sidious the Clown’s official appearance: a tag-team title defense against the hottest team in the Carolinas, EZ Killer and Tommy
Trenton.
Sidious arrived for his match without his sock puppet – er, partner – Kewlic, wearing a damn near obscene lime green bikini
– and you know the bottom half was a thong. Kewlic arrived separately, being carried to the ring on a mini-throne by four gorgeous ladies in bikinis that made Sidious more than a bit jealous.
Whether it was for their treatment of Kewlic or their nice swimwear, who can say?!? The ladies reached up and removed Kewlic from his throne, placing him neatly on the apron, while Sidious leapt to the top turnbuckle, ripping off his bikini top and firing it into the crowd, drawing shrieks of horror from the female fans in the front row.
The tag champ(s) started off a house of fire, as Sidious quickly cleared the ring with a series of on-the-money dropkicks. Sidious then tagged in Kewlic, and tossed him at Killer, who sidestepped easily – right into a superkick from Sidious. Sidious then quickly tagged himself back in.
Try as he might to convince everyone otherwise, Sidious was still in a handicap match situation, against two top young stars in RAW Carolina, and they soon took control with their ever-improving double-team repertoire.
Sidious managed some offense, including a brief Walls of Sidio on
Trenton, but each time he was quickly subdued by the challengers. In the match’s sixth minute, a quick series of tags led to an elaborate three-way crisscross, which Sidious somehow avoided, leaving
Killer and Trenton to collide in the center of the ring, head to head.
Trenton staggered, and Sidious took him down with a flying headscissors. He raced to his corner and tagged in Kewlic, returning to dropkick Killer over the top rope to the beach below. Sidious then leapt to the top turnbuckle – one ball of motion – and tossed Kewlic at Trenton. Trenton caught it and tossed it aside
– only to be hit with a dragon-rana from Sidious that sent his head crashing hard into the mat. Sidious rolled over for the pin, and
Slick Wilson counted to three.
As Sidious celebrated with his fellow bikini-clad fans, Trenton shot
Killer a look that could melt cardboard, evidently upset at the miscommunication that had in all probability cost them the tag titles.
But he quickly shook it off, and the two young fan favorites left the ring together. Winner: Sidious the Clown (Dragon Rana) Time: 7:21 (**) JACK ANDERSON vs. EL CID As both Jack Anderson and El Cid stood in the ring, receiving their instructions from Jackie Boone, Jr., RAW-C President
“Showboat” Eddie Cannon made his first appearance of the evening, to a fifty-fifty mix of cheers and boos.
“Thought I’d come out and add a bit of juice to this match-up. Both of you guys have been impressive, and I’d like to reward you both with title shots next week, but I can’t decide which. So let’s say the winner gets a shot at VCA next week in Myrtle Beach – and the loser gets Jericho!”
Cannon threw a wink Felicity’s way as he exited the ring, which perplexed Anderson – long enough for Cid to get the jump on him as the bell rang to start the match. Cid hit the Crimson Spitfire with a knee to the small of the back, sending him out of the ring to the sand. The Spanish Luchador quickly followed him out with the
Pueblo Shot, a suicida moonsault to the floor that floored Anderson.
Back in the ring, Cid took control with his high-flying, high-risk innovative offense, bouncing all over the ring, battering Anderson.
Felicity was worried out on the sand: Would Anderson’s recent crisis of confidence continue?!? She tried to rally the fans behind her man, but to no avail. Cid’s offense was too flashy, too exciting –- the fans were behind him, as well.
Finally, as a last ditch effort, she removed her normal business suit jacket and micro-skirt, revealing a teeny-tiny bikini more befitting the Atlantic Beach clime. THAT got the crowd behind her guy. As it usually does, the crowd support buoyed Anderson, and he powered out of
Cid’s Viper Strike (dragon sleeper), and landed a series of running forearms, and then a nice belly to back suplex.
With the crowd now firmly behind Anderson, thanks to Felicity’s legs that seem to go on and on and on and on. . .oh, sorry, where was
I?!? Oh, yeah. The match see-sawed back and forth, an excellent contest. At the fourteen minute mark, Cid had Anderson on the top rope for his
Wild Ride top rope DDT – but somehow Anderson reversed it into his own Fireball Special hurricanrana. As they hit the mat, Anderson landed on top, and Jackie Boone, Jr. made the three count.
Winner: Jack Anderson (Fireball Special) Time: 15:11 (****) “HURRICANE” KIRK MACLEAN & “ICEMAN” MARK ADAMS & FLASH vs. VINCENT C. AUGUSTUS & “X-PRESS” DAVID JERICHO & STONE This match was a direct result of last week’s two singles title matches – Kirk Maclean and his two new friends Mark Adams and
Flash teaming together to take on the Empire Club of Vincent Augustus and Stone and their partner, new Piedmont Champion David Jericho.
The match started fast and furious, and stayed that way. Maclean dominated the early portions of the match with Jericho, keeping the new champ off-balance with a high-octane attack that scored several two counts. A pin attempt following a running, swinging neckbreaker had to be broken up by Stone, who then brawled with Maclean out to the floor.
Adams took the opportunity to insert himself as the legal man, and referee ‘Cottonmouth’ Joe Lynch decided early on that he would have little control of this one, and should just go with it.
Adams charged in quickly, and began working over Jericho, who turned the tide with a well-timed low blow and tagged in the Carolina
Heavyweight Champion, Vincent Constantine Augustus. VCA went to work on Adams, dropping him several times with a variety of suplexes, showing off the vast Wyngarde catalogue of which he is now the sole proprietor. But nothing could keep Adams down for the long three, and after a surprising standing switch, he hit Augustus with a Dragon Suplex of his own, and tagged in Flash.
At this point in the match, Jericho became disinterested in the match, disgusted by the fact that his teammates wouldn’t tag him back in. He hopped off the apron, grabbed a folding beach chair from a ringside fan, and sat down at ringside, watching the rest of the match. Stone saw what was happening, and stopped his wild brawl with
Maclean to question his teammate. Jericho didn’t like that one bit, and the two alleged partners were nose to nose.
Flash took this opportunity to follow them over, and sneaking up from behind, grabbed each man by the back of his head and knock their heads together. A furious three way brawl erupted, with Stone and Jericho soon getting the upper hand on Flash. Adams headed to ringside to help his partner – and Jericho waffled him with the beach chair.
He then cracked Flash with it – and then turned it on his partner Stone. Jericho bashed the three men repeatedly with the chair, leaving Maclean alone in the ring with Augustus. As Augustus looked on at the ringside scene in bewilderment, Maclean snuck up behind him with a schoolboy that very nearly scored a three count. Maclean followed up with a quick Reverse Gory Special Suplex. Then, without missing a beat, he leapt to the top rope and back off and hit VCA with the Eye of the Storm, his breathtaking off the tope rope somersault Senton with a twist. Lynch slapped the mat once – twice – three times. Kirk Maclean had pinned the Carolina Heavyweight Champion.
Winners: Kirk Maclean / Mark Adams / Flash (Eye of the Storm) Time: 11:22 (***) ---- © RAW Carolina, MMI. http://www.rawcarolina.cjb.net
