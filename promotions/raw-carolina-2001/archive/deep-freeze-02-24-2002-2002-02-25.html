RAW Carolina — Card Archive

Deep Freeze 02.24.2002

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You want turmoil?!? Take a look at the RAW Empire these days. But it's business as usual for RAW Carolina – and that meant it's time for Deep Freeze!! Held this year at Trask Coliseum, the home of the
UNC-Wilmington Seahawks!! Results: SCOTT STARRING & OTHELLO WASHINGTON vs. THE HENCHMEN (Thank our newest British writing sensation Jon for this one.) The opening bout was scheduled as a tag team match between two promising Suplex Academy students in Scott Starring and Othello
Washington, and Jackie Boone Jr's former hired goons, The Henchmen. Eddie Cannon had made the match at the request of Starring and Washington, after they were the victims of an unprovoked Henchmen assault last week at Winter Storm Warning. The Henchmen's future employment in RAW-C was on the line, but after the "convenient"
mystery attack on Washington, which left him unable to compete, Cannon instead made the match a singles contest between Starring and Thug of the Henchmen. . .at least it was announced as being Thug, but with his identically-dressed partner at ringside, who could really tell?
The match didn't start well for Starring, as a mixture of pre-match nerves and too much respect for his opponent cost the youngster the early initiative. Egged on by Brute at ringside, Thug worked over the lightweight Starring with stomps and punches before getting a two- count from a hard Side Suplex. The beating continued until Starring countered a Powerslam attempt with a Flying Back Elbow, and quickly followed up with a pair of nice dropkicks, to the approval of the crowd. His momentum was quickly cut short however by a trip from
Brute, which went unnoticed by the referee. This set the tone for the rest of the match, with Brute intervening on his partner's behalf whenever Starring briefly gained the upper hand. The masked Henchmen took this strategy a step further at around the five minute mark, after an impressive offensive flurry from the highflying Starring left Thug dazed and vulnerable. With the referee focussed on Starring as he climbed the ropes to attempt a moonsault, Brute dragged the stunned Thug out of the ring and took his place! The fans voiced their disapproval as Starring missed the moonsault, Brute rolling out of the way and swiftly attacking the downed rookie.
Brute wore down Starring relentlessly, and the end appeared to be near, until Starring somehow managed to summon enough energy to roll out of the way of a second-rope legdrop. Cheered on by the fans,
Starring followed up with an impressive Springboard Flying-Bodypress which came thisclose to causing the upset, before Thug placed Brute's foot on the ropes to break the pin. This was enough for the exhausted
Starring, who went outside after Thug. But while the referee was distracted by the goings-on at ringside, back in the ring Brute delved into his tights to retrieve an object of some sort, which he slid into the front of his mask. As Starring returned to the ring, he walked straight into a loaded headbutt, and this was enough to secure the tainted victory for "Thug".
Winner: Thug (Masked Mayhem) Time: 8:22 (**) As the fans booed this miscarriage of justice, the two Henchmen mercilessly assaulted Starring, taunting him as they administered yet more punishment. They were about to deliver their patented Double
Slingshot Brainbuster, when from the back appeared Van Wild, who quickly cleared the ring of the Henchmen.
With Thug and Brute shouting abuse towards the ring as they left, Van Wild grabbed a microphone. "Hey, you two! Yeah, Bully Brothers, I'm talking to you! You're two big tough-guys when it's two on one, right? So how about you show us how tough you really are, in a fair fight? Whaddya say, you think you can take on Scott Starring and the Wild Thing? C'mon boys, let's go!"
The Henchmen looked about to storm the ring, before their cowardly nature got the better of them, and they departed while hurling a final torrent of verbal abuse.
"All right, boys, you've got a match – now they've we've got a JOB. We'll see you on RAW-C TV!!!" VAN WILD vs. LARRY GIONET Van Wild remained in the ring for this one, as veteran New England brawler Larry Gionet made his way to the ring for his RAW-C supercard debut. This looked to be an evenly-matched contest in regards to both size and speed, as well as experience – and Scott Starring decided to stick around at ringside to watch his new-found ally in action.
Gionet seemed intent on keeping the high-flying Wild grounded in the early going, but Wild wanted no part of that, constantly slipping out of hold after hold, getting back to his feet, frustrating Gionet.
Of course, a pissed off Gionet is a dangerous Gionet – and after leveling Wild with a hard right to the throat(!!), the veteran went to work.
A series of stiff suplexes left Gionet inches away from victory several times – but Wild managed to kick out each time at two and thisclose. Finally, Gionet had had enough and went for his Hangman
DDT – but Wild had him well-scouted, and shrugged off the attempt with a double-leg takedown. With Starring leading their cheers, the fans rallied behind the Surfer Dude, and he followed up with a nice running bulldog – and then took Gionet on a Wild Ride for the 1-2-3.
Winner: Van Wild (Wild Ride) Time: 7:21 (** ½) Before the next match, Intrepid RAW Carolina interviewer Vernon St. Jean caught up to the "Bladerunner" on the Mini-Tron, and got this from him before his battle with Damien Barnett.
VSJ: Xavier! So what do you have planned about tonight's match against Damien Barnett? XW: Well Vern, I don't know. I said that I am not going to take this match lightly. That was my mistake at Winter Storm Warning. I am NOT going to make that mistake AGAIN!!! You can rest assured on that!
[The Bladerunner makes his way around Vernon St. Jean and towards the ring.] VSJ: Well, there goes the Bladerunner, Xavier Windwalker – If he wins tonight, he could be in line for a shot at one of the titles here in Raw-Carolina.
"BLADERUNNER" XAVIER WINDWALKER vs. DAMIEN This is the third show in a row that these two tough competitors have squared off; having split the first two, both men have demanded a rubber match for their series, to determine just who is the better man.
From the start, it appeared that Windwalker was much more prepared for this one – surely the tumult in RAW (much of it directly affecting his family) has taken it's toll on Damien. But whatever the case may be, Windwalker dominated this one from the jump.
Damien attempted much of the offense he has picked up in his travels through Mexico and Japan, much of it lightning-quick and high- impact. But the 300 pound Windwalker shrugged most of it off, supremely focused. The end of the match saw Damien come off the top with a senton attempt – only to be caught in mid-air and drilled by
Windwalker with a backbreaker. One Bladecutter double-underhook facebuster later, and it was all over.
Winner: Xavier Windwalker (Bladecutter) Time: 4:46 (**) CHRISTIAN DEMONSTONE © vs. "X-PRESS" DAVID JERICHO, Coastal Television Title Christian Demonstone emerged from a wild series of matches the last few weeks on RAW-C TV as the brand-new Coastal Television Champion.
The wily Canuck has certainly emerged as a bonafide star here in the Carolinas. A two-time Piedmont Champion, David Jericho is certainly a RAW-C star, but has struggled of late, and looked to get off the schneide by capturing championship gold.
The match was a wild, evenly matched affair, and at the ten minute mark, neither man had been able to sustain a prolonged advantage.
With both men approaching 280 pounds, they matched power move for power move to the joy of the crowd, of course more so when Jericho was on the offense.
Demonstone tried to shake things up at the thirteen minute mark, and headed to the top rope for a rare high-flying move. He came off with a clothesline attempt, but Jericho ducked under and the big Canadian went crashing into the mat. Jericho followed up with a German Suplex for a two count, and a Northern Lights variety for another.
Jericho motioned to the crowd; he was going for the X-Press Way. But Demonstone had him well-scouted, and countered – he turned around and dropped Jericho with his Canuck Driver, earning (and I mean EARNING)
the three count seconds short of the time limit. Winner: Christian Demonstone (Canuck Driver) Time: 14:48 (***) As Demonstone celebrated his victory in the ring, he was blindsided out of nowhere by Jack Doe out of the crowd with a steel chair(!!).
Doe delivered several shots with that chair to the downed Demonstone, apparently still suffering under the delusion that the Coastal
Television Title is rightfully his. As a host of RAW-C officials came out to break it up, a few cheers came out of the crowd for Doe, as Demonstone is not exactly a favorite of the crowd, either. Doe heard them, and broke off his attack to acknowledge them, a big grin on his face.
"ACOLYTE" CHRIS KING vs. "DREADMIND" DAVE PITRELLI (Ladies and gentlemen, I give you John Tutt in his RAW-C matchwriting debut) Pitrelli towered over King and physically dominated him all throughout the match. However, things began to sour when King rallied and put together a salvo of quick moves to catch Pitrelli off guard.
With Pitrelli reeling, King launched an assault, but Pitrelli escaped and managed to escape. King kept up the attack and went for a top rope hurricanrana, but Pitrelli caught King in midair and drove him down with a massive powerbomb.
Rather disgusted with King, Pitrelli found his partner in crime, the infamous chain around the fist. The referee stepped in to try and stop Pitrelli, but got tossed aside for his trouble. Pitrelli waited for King to stagger to his feet before swinging at him. Pitrelli's fist came within inches of knocking King into next month, but he stopped short on his own accord.
Pitrelli delivered a stiff boot to the head of King and began yelling at the voice inside his head. After a minute of this yelling, screaming, and ranting, Pitrelli seemed to come to some sort of agreement with his inner self. This time, when King managed to find his feet, he was greeted with a chained fist to the head. Pitrelli pounded on him for a few minutes, busting him open, before officials pulled him off.
Winner: Chris King (Disqualification) Time: 5:21 (* ½) JOHNNY ALPHA vs. "ERADICATOR" DIRK ROBERTSON It's been an odd last few weeks for Ma Alpha's baby boy; less than a month ago, he was still wrestling under a mask as the mysterious
Macabre. But now, after an intervention from his brother Xander, he's back to his given name, and the future looks bright.
Likewise, it's been a strange month for Dirk Robertson, following the implosion of The Network. Only in the last week has he seemed to pull his career out of the tailspin he's been in, and he looked to continue that success with a victory over a former RAW Atlantic champion and climb back into title contention.
But that was easier said than done, as the youngest Alpha male came to fight. Flying all over the ring, Alpha had the Buffalo native on the ropes early, notching a twoandthisclose with a flying forearm from the second rope. Alpha felt the match was in hand, and went for the Danse Macabre – but Robertson ducked under his spin kick, and nearly beheaded him with a clothesline.
From there, Robertson went to work, dropping Alpha with all variety of his innovative, original offense, even stealing a page from former friend Marc Tower's book and using a Steel City Slam to get a two count. It was an impressive showing for Robertson, and he finished it off with a Total Eradication. Alpha stayed down for the three count, and Robertson had a hard-fought victory sure to impress the
Carolina Championship Committee. Winner: Dirk Robertson Time: 7:22 (**) Postmatch, Alpha stood in the ring a long while, leaning on the ropes deep in thought. Finally, he called for Bobby Lee Wilcox' mike:
JA: That was a fun match. I enjoyed it. I lost to the better man, but I enjoyed it. I was hoping that maybe I'd be able to resurrect my career in RAW Carolina under a different name when I was Macabre, but
I was wrong and missed out on a lot of valuable time with my family. I've decided that right now I want to spend time with my family. Fans, I love you. Maybe one day I'll be back, teaming alongside Xander where I belong, but until then, take care.
"FAST" EDDIE CUTLASS © vs. "MENACING" MIKE STEELE, Piedmont Heavyweight Title Well, Mike Steele is certainly singing a different tune these days – and many believe it's entirely due to new female acquaintance Tracy
Stanwix. Whatever the cause, Steele has completely changed his approach to his entire wrestling career – and his (former) fans are not happy about it. Eddie Cutlass has publicly stated his disappointment – and suddenly this match got a lot more personal.
From the get-go, it was obvious that the new Menace to Society was a much more aggressive Mike Steele, and he held nothing back in his attack on Cutlass. Smoother, crisper, and generally more effective in the ring, Steele's attack certainly took Cutlass by surprise, and he took several two counts in the early going.
Cutlass was in rare form, as well, returning Steele's attack with his trademark fast-paced stuff. But Stanwix soon proved she was more than mere eye candy, leaping to the apron to distract Hank Jackson as her man used a steel flexbar to work over Cutlass' ribs. Steele then dropped Cutlass with a Pittsburgh Plunge suplex for a two count.
Cutlass then ducked under a flying back elbow smash, and watched as Steele went crashing into the ropes. Cutlass went for the Guarantee – but Steele countered with a full- nelson facebuster, and Cutlass came up with a bloodied nose – right into an Overdrive Sleeper, Steele's inverted facelock sleeper.
Trapped with no place to go, Cutlass somehow worked himself towards the ropes, reaching out – draping a leg across the bottom rope forcing Hank Jackson to call for the break. But Steele refused to do so, instead leaning into the hold even tighter, sneering at Jackson as he counted to five. Cutlass was out, but Steele still refused to let go, leading Jackson to call for the bell, disqualifying the
Menace to Society. Steele relented as the bell rang – but only to reach out to Stanwix for his flexbar, which he proceeded to drill into Cutlass with a series of savage body shots. A host of RAW Carolina officials, led by new Director of Security Tommy Victory, poured out of the back to save Cutlass, finally prying Steele off. Steele made his way to the locker room, arms held high, pelted by trash from his one-time fans – the transformation complete.
Winner: Eddie Cutlass (Disqualification) Time: 8:24 (** ½) JACK ANDERSON vs. JACK DOE vs. PAUL SHERIDAN ([name withheld], braving a gassy baby to produce this classic for you fans) In what will surely be remembered as one of the best Triple Threat matches of the year, cruiserweight superstar Jack Anderson squared off against his two least favorite people in the Carolinas -- his former tag team partner, Paul Sheridan, and the man who hopes to make a name for himself at Anderson's expense, the somewhat-less-than- thrilling Jack Doe.
From the opening bell, Sheridan and Doe seemed to be cooperating in an attempt to wear Anderson down, tagging each other in frequently and leaving Jack in the ring against one or the other of them without either one ever having enough to put the popular cruiserweight down.
Finally, as Sheridan was about to score a Shona Maynard-assisted victory over Anderson, Doe's true nature showed itself and he entered the ring to try and steal ANOTHER victory for himself (you might remember he pulled that stunt once on RAW-C TV) by tossing Sheri over the top rope and going for the cover...only to have Jackie kick out at two and ninty-nine one-hundredths and throw him out of the ring as well.
Sensing the opportunity to end the match once and for all, Anderson left the ring, rolled Sheridan back in under the bottom rope, and climbed to the top turnbuckle, calling for his "Fireball Special" to a huge ovation from the fans. Just as Anderson made contact, however,
Doe managed to roll back into the ring and knock Referee Julia Barrymore into the line of fire, causing Anderson to hit Sheri AND the ref and giving Doe another opportunity to steal the win, this time by tossing Anderson out of the ring before waking the ref and covering Sheridan for the one-two-three.
Winner: Jack Doe (Heinous Shenanigans) Time: 9:24 (*** ½) TERRY JACOBS & JUSTIN HARPER © vs. PURGATORY, Carolina Tag-Team Titles The veteran Jacobs and the rookie Harper have been a breath of fresh air to the tag-team title scene, and have best most impressive since winning the belts from Murdoch & Maclean at Super Sunday. The challengers Purgatory have been in the news lately, as well, apparently bringing in a third man for their group, allegedly a portent of doom for all.
But first, they got a shot at the Carolina Tag Titles. Previous shots for the evil duo have shown that they truly don't care about the gold, only inflicting pain – and this match was no exception.
Purgatory has been together as a team much longer than the champs, but even in their short time together, Harper and Jacobs have been quite the efficient machine. They dominated the early portion of the match with quick tags and simple double-teams, wearing down the giant
Dementia. The tide turned once Synn was able to get into the match, the tide turned a bit, and the challengers managed to trap the rookie Harper in the ring for a long stretch, working him over brutally but unable to get a three count.
Finally, Harper was able to flip out of a double suplex attempt, and rolled into his own corner to tag in Jacobs to a huge pop. Jacobs charged into the ring and caught Synn with the deadliest lariat in the game – the Charlotte Cyclone. Jacobs rolled on top of Synn, and
Slick Wilson counted to three. Dementia charged into the ring, grabbing Jacobs by the hair as the bell rung, tossing him over the top rope to the floor below. Jacobs hit hard, and Harper walked right into a chokeslam from Dementia.
Synn rolled out to the floor, and came back in with a steel chair – and the sadistic duo proceeded to drop the rookie with a double chokeslam onto the chair – and then another. Jacobs came back to the ring with his own chair, running Purgatory to the hills – but the damage was done. The champs had retained their titles in this battle, but this war is certainly far from over.
Winners: Jacobs & Harper (Charlotte Cyclone) Time: 11:23 (**) MARC TOWER vs. CASANOVA BEETHOVEN, Number One Contender Match The last two Carolina Heavyweight Champions squared in the next match, to determine the number one contender to the current champion,
Scott Studd. There's nothing either man would like more to get back to the top of the mountain, and a win here would give them a shot next week to do exactly that.
These two waged some classic battles for the title back in the fall, with Tower emerging the ultimate victor in their feud. So it was no surprise that this match featured nothing even resembling a feeling- out process – both men just went at it full bore with everything they had.
Beethoven wrestled a surprisingly clean match, relying only on his size and reach advantage to work over Tower. But while giving up over 40 pounds to Beethoven, Tower can match strength with any man in this promotion – the ho lovin' superstar included. Showing an arsenal perhaps second only to former friend Dirk Robertson's in innovation, Tower worked over the purple-haired warrior, scoring two counts with a Pittsburgh Plant, and then a Steel City Slam.
At the fifteen minute mark, Tower had Beethoven up on the top, looking for a superplex – but Beethoven blocked it, shoving Tower hard to the mat. Beethoven climbed up to stand on the top turnbuckle, waiting for Tower to stand up – and when he did,
Beethoven launched with a double axe-handle – right into a superkick from Tower. Tower then went for a German Suplex, but Beethoven spun
Tower around, and planted him with a Symphony. Three seconds later, Scott Studd had his next top contender. Winner: Casanova Beethoven (Symphony) Time: 15:22 (*** ¼) "SENSATIONAL" SCOTT STUDD vs. STONE, Carolina Heavyweight Title RAW Carolina fans will of course remember the triple threat Carolina title match from the summer – VCA is challenged by Scott Studd and
Stone, only to see Stone turn on Studd and join the Empire Club. That match led to Studd's 90 day `retirement', and was the impetus for his grudge with "Showboat" Eddie Cannon. And for those of you who would question Cannon's twisted sense of humor – look no further than THIS match, as the grudge between he and Studd apparently still runs deep.
Studd got this one started quickly, shooting the much shorter Stone off the ropes for a spinebuster, getting a two count thirty seconds into the match. But Stone fired right back with a spinebuster of his own, and the Wilmington fans knew they were in for a good one.
And they were, as well over ten minutes went by, each man battering the other with high-impact moves. Stone got Studd up and over with a
Rolling Stone at eleven minutes, but the champ was too close to the ropes, dropping his big leg across the bottom one. Two minutes later, Studd dropped Stone across the top rope throat-first with a
Studd Gun – but Stone got a leg across the rope, as well. Nearing fifteen minutes now, Stone went to the top, missing a flying headbutt. Studd pounced on him immediately – locking on the Studdly
Stretch. Studd leaned back, looking to break Stone in half – but the Elemental Force of Nature wouldn't give in. Finally, after nearly three minutes in the stretch, `Cottonmouth' Joe Lynch called for the bell – awarding the match to Studd.
Studd made his way up the aisle, saluting the fans – where he was blindsided by a chair-wielding Casanova Beethoven, looking to soften him up for next week's title match. Beethoven got in several shots before Stone made his way down the aisle to make the save, helping
Studd to his feet, shaking his hand to end the show. Winner: Scott Studd (Studdly Stretch) Time: 17:11 (*** ¼) --- © RAW Carolina, MMII. http://www.yahoogroups.com/group/RAWCarolina
