Finalized Card for Super Sunday

25 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RAW Carolina holds its' annual Super Bowl Sunday tradition this Sunday at venerable Reynolds Coliseum in Raleigh, North Carolina, on the campus of NC State University.
This event, long a favorite among wrestling and football fans in the Triangle area, features a unique format: Bell time is 4:30. Fans in attendance are treated to six exciting matches before the giant projection screen TVs are rolled out and the big game is shown, with free beer and snacks for all those in attendance.
When the game ends, the fans stick around for five more exciting matches. Always a crowd pleaser, this year's Super Sunday could be extra special, as it is the first under the RAW banner.
Matches for the first session were finalized Thursday morning: - In the opener, the returning JOSEPH NIGHTHORSE takes on the self- styled Appalachian Assassin HOUNDDOG MURPHY. - Exciting tag-team action to follow, as the powerful GLADIATORS take on the young masked NIGHTMARES. - "ALL-AMERICAN" CHAMP DURHAM makes his Super Sunday debut as he takes on one-half of Code Red, spiteful cruiserweight SGT. DAN LORENZO. - ROBBIE DIAMOND defends his Piedmont Title against the 400 pound wrecking ball, "TRAINWRECK" CALEB JONES. - In a Loser Leaves the Carolinas match, former partners collide for the last time as "THE BOSTON MANGLER" ADONIS MORGAN squares off against JOSHUA STRONG. - And in the main event of the first half, "LAWBRINGER" THOMAS ADLER continues his crusade to rid the Carolinas of the Wild Bunch when he faces TULLY ROEBUCK. Then, after the fans settle back down and watch what is sure to be an exciting Ravens--Giants tilt, they get the second session:
- A bonafide wrestling legend debuts in the Carolinas as veteran Canadian superstar COREY "THE COOKER" COOKSON bows against the malcontent California stud MARK GOLDEN.
- "AWESOME" ALEC PRICE attempts to avenge his injured partner as he clashes with the other half of Code Red, the 7 foot masked LEATHERNECK. - Tag action follows, as the Wild Bunch sends brothers ISAAC & GIDEON WALKER to the ring to face "EL GAUPO" RICKY GOMEZ & "MAD DOG" BILLY MANCINI. - In what should be a masterpiece of technical wrestling, 'ho-lovin' superstar CASANOVA BEETHOVEN takes on the former Piedmont champion, suplex master JACOB LEGACY.
- The main event should be another classic for the Carolina Heavyweight Title: champion ACE MATTHEWS defends against RAW-C's hired gun, "TERRIBLE" TOMMY BEDFORD, fighting on Super Sunday for no one but himself.
All this plus much, much more!! Last week at Throwdown in Tiger Town, "Dirty" Doug Simpkins, Morten Wyngarde, and Jack Hammer showed up!! Who knows who could appear on Super Sunday!!
