RAW Carolina — Card Archive

House Show 07-20-01

21 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RAW Carolina headed for the site of the annual Throwdown in Tiger
Town, Clemson, South Carolina, for a big live event Friday night at
Littlejohn Coliseum. Results:
SIDIOUS THE CLOWN opened the show with a high-flying victory over the dastardly JUDGE. The lumbering Judge seems out of sorts of late, his attention seemingly focused elsewhere, and fell to a Pie in the Eye crucifix pin.
One half of the tag title match at Carolina Thunder squared off in the next contest, as "HARDCORE SENSATION" MARK ADAMS scored a pin over
Wild Style's SHANNON HAMILTON. Hamilton missed a Given To Fly corkscrew legdrop, allowing Adams to plant him with the Big Easy suplex for the pin.
The challengers made it two for two in the next match, as Adams' partner FLASH pinned the other half of the champions DREW BRODY in a wild match-up. Flash scored the pin with his Miracle suplex -- momentum clearly belongs to the challengers as we head to Charlotte.
Impressive newcomer CHRISTOPHER SCARLET stayed unbeaten during his brief tenure in RAW Carolina, stopping JOSEPH NIGHTHORSE cold with a devastating Scarlet Kick, the finest superkick in all the Carolinas.
Scarlet's fellow newcomer, ROADKILL RICK, had yet to make his in-ring debut in RAW-C, but the fans had already decided against him, thanks to his recent promos. But they had to admit he looked impressive in his debut Friday night against young STEVE FURY, winning handily thanks to his powerbomb into a Boston crab he calls the Texas
Speedbump. Two of the three combatants in the triangle match in Charlotte for the
Television title met in the next contest, as "THE ERADICATOR" DIRK
ROBERTSON defended his title against DIREWOLF. Still desperate for child care, Direwolf had no choice but to bring his son Barry to ringside with him -- a distraction that cost him the match, as
Robertson took advantage with a small package for the victory. "X-PRESS" DAVID JERICHO successfully defended his title against "FAST" EDDIE CUTLASS -- not with another draw, but with an actual win. El
Cid -- the third combatant in the Piedmont triangle match at Thunder
-- wandered to ringside about eleven minutes into an excellent match, and tripped up Cutlass, causing him to stumble into a DDT from Jericho for the pin. Two of RAW Carolina's biggest powerhouses met in the next contest, as
BULL "GATOR" JOHNSON defeated JESSIE MULDOON. Johnson pinned Muldoon with an awesome Gator Breaker DDT to the knee -- and not five seconds after the match ended, Jack Anderson attacked, and a wild brawl ensued, broken up by a score of RAW-C officials.
JACK ANDERSON returned to the ring for the next match, as he was scheduled to take on STONE. Fans expecting Bull Johnson to follow were not disappointed -- The Gator returned for another piece of
Anderson, only to hit Stone accidentally with a clothesline, allowing
Anderson to score the pin. Another brawl ensued, this one ending with both men being escorted out of the building.
The main event saw "LAWBRINGER" THOMAS ADLER successfully defend his
Carolina Heavyweight Title against fellow powerhouse MARC TOWER in a great contest. Adler won a wild brawl with his patented Guillotine top rope clothesline.
