RAW Carolina — Card Archive

Mountain Mayhem 01.13.2002

13 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Always one of the most anticipated cards of the Carolina year, it’s time for Mountain Mayhem!! As always, the show emanated from the Asheville Civic Center, nestled in the heart of the Great
Smoky Mountains. Results: JAYSON RAYGE vs. JUSTIN HARPER RAW Carolina continues to be a breeding ground for the ‘Next Big Things’, and both Rayge and Harper have to be considered candidates for that distinction at the moment. These two rookies have been headed in opposite directions of late; Harper has been impressive in his first month in the Carolinas, while Rayge has been on a slide since the implosion of the Blood Brothers.
But even given that, this was a helluva match. For two guys shy of 250 pounds, both Rayge and Harper are powerful, powerful wrestlers, a fact they advertised in this contest. Stiff and quick from the get-go, they traded suplexes, slams, and throws for the first five minutes or so, and that’s when Harper’s superior conditioning took over.
Few men in RAW-C are in better cardiovascular condition than Harper, who even worked in a few judo throws on Rayge – a none-too-subtle jab at David Jericho. Harper seemed in complete control – until Rayge dropped him with a Tornado DDT out of nowhere. But Harper kicked out, and managed to send Rayge over the top rope to the floor – and followed him with a senton bomb suicida con hijo, completely wiping Rayge out.
After both men were nearly counted out, the action moved back into the ring and Harper locked Rayge into a reverse legbreaker, pulling hard on Rayge’s leg in an attempt to hyperextend his knee. Rayge wouldn’t give in, however, as Harper really turned on the pressure. Finally, official Hank Jackson called for the bell, awarding the match to Harper.
Winner: Justin Harper (Legbreaker) Time: 11:21 (***) TERRY JACOBS vs. CHRISTIAN DEMONSTONE These two newcomers have met once before, at a house show not long after their debuts in RAW Carolina. Demonstone ended that one by taking a steel chair to Jacobs’ head, and the cagey vet’s been itching for a rematch ever since.
Normally Jacobs keeps his matches on the scientific side – but this afternoon, he was itching for a fight. And the 280 pound
Demonstone was equipped – and willing – to give him one. From the opening bell, they brawled all over the ring, down to the floor, and back into the ring – the kicking and punching punctuated occasionally by a nice DDT or brainbuster or something similarly stiff.
The seven minute mark found both men on the floor again, trading punches, when Demonstone ducked an attempted Charlotte Cyclone from
Jacobs, sending the Carolina native crashing bicep-first into the ringpost. Slick Wilson’s count reached six as Demonstone made his way up to the apron and back into the ring. At eight, Jacobs got to the apron – only to be sent crashing down to the floor by a
Demonstone dragonscrew legwhip. Wilson counted to ten, and Demonstone escaped with a countout victory.
Winner: Christian Demonstone (Countout) Time: 7:19 (** ½) Before the next match, the Mini-Tron flashed to life, showing “X-Press” David Jericho shooting pool in some bar with his old friend Jamal Byers. DJ: Ya know, I've been going through some video tapes and have been occupied by looking at the past. Looking at my career here in
RAW-Carolina, it looks as if I need to move up, shall we say. So...I guess I'm not going to be making it for my Piedmont Title match up tonight.
So Dreadminded, I guess you won. But I got the belt. That belt, is here with "The X-Press".
*David holds up the belt.* Jamal, I guess you’re going to beat me for this one. Too bad I didn't get to defend it. Damn, why did I have to put it on the stakes of a pool game?
*Jamal hits the 8-ball in and holds his hand out for the belt.* Well...to bad that title is in WCRAW. See ya Deadminded! MIKE STEELE vs. “ERADICATOR” DIRK ROBERTSON Since the injuries suffered by Mike Diamond, his Collateral Damage partner Mike Steele has been making a go of it as a singles wrestler
– a quite successful go of it, in fact. He has seemingly waged a one man war against the Network, looking to rid the Carolinas of the scourge that has taken out his partner, as well as allies Eddie
Cutlass and Roadkill Rick. Dirk Robertson has emerged as Dennis Cody’s top guy in The Network, so it’s only natural Steele looked to take his frustrations out on The Eradicator. But Robertson only added to the frustration in the early going, keeping the match on the mat with a submission strategy, including a strange looking leg bar that looked to put incredible pressure on Steele’s knee.
Steele escaped that hold by getting to the ropes, and when Robertson set his head early for a backdrop, Steele folded him in half with a snatch powerbomb. Steele’s training with legends Mike Wolfe and
Steven Alstein in Chicago this week appears to have paid immediate dividends. He even attempted to apply the Wolfe Bite sleeper, but he hasn’t QUITE mastered it yet, as Robertson was able to get to the ropes.
But Robertson is quite the accomplished star here in RAW Carolina, and the former Television Champion took advantage of a small mistake by
Steele, blocking a vertical suplex into a snap suplex of his own, and followed up with a nice DDT. Robertson gloated over the crowd, and stepped over into a Scorpion Deathlock – but Steele reached up, grabbed him by the hair and rolled into a small package – for the 1-2-3.
Winner: Mike Steele (Small Package) Time: 8:49 (*** ¼) After the smallest package this side of Tony Quint, The Network was pissed, and they stormed the ring in force. Pitrelli and Purgatory joined Robertson in the ring, a four on one beatdown on Steele.
Pitrelli reached into his jeans’ pocket and produced a length of chain, intent on finishing the job he started last week. But before he could, Jayson Rayge and Stone rushed the ring, bearing steel chairs, saving Steele from any further damage.
“STELLAR” STEVE KELLER © vs. MACABRE, RAW Atlantic Bicoastal Title This one’s been building for a long time. To recap: Keller became RAW Atlantic’s one and only Bicoastal Champion, and
Macabre wanted a shot at that belt. And he got several, only to be thwarted by the nefarious antics of Keller and his 450 pound flunky
“Trainwreck” Caleb Jones. When RAW Atlantic folded, Macabre headed to the Carolinas – and Keller followed him ‘home’ to settle the score once and for all. Before this one began, Bobby Lee Wilcox had a special announcement: To watch his back in this one, Macabre had made a phone call: None other than Caleb Jones’ biggest rival during his stint in the
Carolinas – Tommy Victory. The crowd popped big-time for the return of this popular Canadian superstar (who incidentally faced
Keller for the Carolina Title at last year’s Mountain Mayhem), and Keller and Jones were visibly shocked.
Macabre started out hot, clearing Keller out of the ring with a series of quick martial arts kicks. Keller went to confer with Jones – only to have Victory sneak up behind them to knock their heads together. Keller wobbled towards the safety railing – and
Macabre launched himself over the ropes with a plancha, sending the champion crashing into the steel.
Back in the ring, Keller took advantage with a decidedly low blow, and got a two count with a german suplex. Keller dominated the next few minutes with some crisp technical-type stuff, turning a side headlock into a nice tornado bulldog, and scoring several more near falls.
At six minutes, Jones lumbered to the apron, grabbing Hank Jackson by the collar. As Jackson struggled to free himself, Keller rolled out to the floor to grab his belt. Back in the ring with it, he took a run at Macabre with it – only to have Victory snatch it away from him. Keller turned, enraged – and Victory planted him with a Victory Driver(!!). Standing Keller up, Victory buckled the Coastal
Title around his face, just in time for Macabre to level him with a Danse Macabre spinkick. Victory rolled out of the ring with the belt, and Jackson made the count – a new champion.
Winner (and new champion): Macabre (Danse Macabre) Time: 7:34 (** ½) Before the next contest, the Deities hit the ring for a little mic time: [Aries and Mars hold the ropes open for Apollo. Once in the ring, he lifts the mic.] Apollo: Well, well, well. The Deities are back in the saddle and making their very first RAW-Carolina debut here in Ashville, North
Carolina! (Hometown POP!) Apollo: I've heard nice things about Ashville, and judging by some of the smiles I've seen, they are mostly true. Well, true for those of you sitting here tonight.
(Apollo flashes one of his devilish smiles, women swoon pop) Apollo: But I took a drive around the surrounding area of Ashville, and do you know what I found? Nothing but one-toothed, knot-kneed, inbreed hillbillies with a dream of moving to a big city that this little watering hole!
(WHAT POP!! Aries and Mars look at Apollo in disbelief.) APollo: Well, there is one positive to be here though. You can consider Aries and Mars tenant relocators. I can tell by most of the confused faces you want to know why. Well, its simple. Once they finish with Law and Order, they will fit right in. Though they might be cause for some jealousy because they might have more than two
WHOLE teeth in their head. [Apollo tosses the mic out and climbs out of the ring to a chorus of massive boos.] DEITIES vs. LAW & ORDER (Special thanks once again to RAW Overlord [name withheld].) This was the first match for the Deities since their shocking return at the last RAW Aggression, punking out Frank Anthony of the Kings of
Hardcore to cost the Kings the tag battle royal. It was eagerly awaited by many fans, who marked out for the former tag champions despite the presence of the perennial nuisance Apollo, their manager, at ringside.
Well, it was eagerly awaited - but it sure wasn't long. The Deities proved they've lost none of their old skills, and a slimmed down Mars even managed to hit some pretty impressive aerial maneuvers, getting a two count on Law after a flying cross body, and two and a half on John
Order after a sunset flip from the top rope. Aries wasn't taking to the air, but was just as powerful as ever, reversing an attempted
Order superplex into a frontal superplex of his own, and dropping Law moments later with a devastating powerbomb.
It wasn't all going the way of the guys named after Greek gods - a bulldog by Order got two, and Law nearly pulled off the victory with a release German suplex, but after they got the win with the Complete
Destruction, few would argue that they'll face much tougher tests. Winners: Deities (Complete Destruction) Time: 3:54 (*) Before the scheduled Piedmont Title match, Casanova Beethoven came down to the ring, grabbing the mike to let everyone know that if
Jericho wasn’t in the building, he’d be glad to step in and take care of Dave Pitrelli. Pitrelli came down, and the fight was on. No sooner were two punches thrown than that familiar music cranked up – David Jericho was here, after all – stolen Piedmont Title in tow. The brawl at ringside became a three-way affair, spilling into the crowd. Finally, a host of officials came down and pulled Beethoven out of it, and the scheduled match could begin.
“DREADMIND” DAVE PITRELLI © vs. “X-PRESS” DAVID JERICHO, Piedmont Heavyweight Title Official Slick Wilson had been one of the throng pulling Beethoven out of this mess, so he knew firsthand how intense this was going to be, and decided to let it go wild – chokeholds, eye gouges, low blows – anything goes.
So when Jericho decided to go for broke, rolling outside of the ring for a ladder, no one was surprised when Wilson let that go, too.
Pitrelli followed him out to the floor, and the two traded blows over the ladder – until Jericho dropped the big man with a sneaky headbutt, and followed up with an elbow off the apron. Jericho then folded Pitrelli into the ladder – and dove off the apron with a moonsault(!!).
Once the action moved back into the ring, Pitrelli took control, press slamming Jericho first OFF OF the ladder, and then ONTO the ladder.
Pitrelli got a few two counts, but nothing to keep the challenger down. At the seven minute mark, Pitrelli folded the ladder over a standing Jericho, and speared him through the ropes in an awesome spot.
After eight minutes of nonstop action in this impromptu ladder match, Jericho came off the top rope with a Drive By, Jamal Byers’ finisher from his days in RAW-C. Then, in a nice show of strength, he dropped a wobbly Pitrelli across the top rope, superplex style – and locked in the X-Press Way. Pitrelli began flailing, looking to get out somehow – just as Jericho fell backwards, taking
Pitrelli with him to the mat – still in the X-Press Way(!!). Then – as you might have guessed by now – Beethoven charged down the aisle armed with a pair of brass knuckles – and drilled Jericho right between the eyes with them. Wilson stopped his count at two as Jericho slumped over, and Pitrelli rolled on top of him for the tainted three.
Winner: Dave Pitrelli (Thanks, Casanova Beethoven) Time: 9:03 (***) JACK ANDERSON & PAUL SHERIDAN vs. PURGATORY Anderson and Sheridan have become quite the pair of late, and have the potential to become one of the top tag-teams in the Carolinas. But they faced a stern test in Asheville, in the form of The
Network’s bloodthirsty duo of Synn and Dementia. Anderson started the match off quick, taking Synn down with a hurricanrana at the opening bell for a two count. Synn quickly tagged in the 7’4” monster Dementia, who fared no better as
Anderson dropped him with an amazing armdrag before heading to the top rope for a missile dropkick and another two count. Anderson then tagged in the rookie Sheridan – and things took a turn for the worst.
Sheridan charged in blindly, right into a chokeslam from Dementia. Synn tagged himself in, and took Sheridan out of his boots with a Northern Lights Suplex for a two count. Grabbing Sheridan by the hair, he tossed him over the top rope. Anderson attempted to cross the ring to help his young partner, but Synn speared him, taking him down. Dementia dropped down to go after Sheridan, who scrambled up to his knees, and then UNDER THE RING to get away(!!).
Dementia got back into the ring, and Purgatory proceeded to double-team Anderson, flattening him with a double clothesline. The crowd buzzed – WHERE was Sheridan?!? And then it happened:
Sheridan emerged from under the ring – wearing a black mask. He rolled back into the ring, and the action came to a standstill, as Anderson just stared at his young partner. Sheridan nodded his head slowly in acknowledgement – Paul Sheridan was The Guy Dressed
All in Black. Purgatory attacked – Dementia grabbed Anderson around the throat, lifting him off the mat as Synn climbed to the second turnbuckle, and they delivered a nice double chokeslam. Anderson stayed down for the three count, and the crowd was stunned.
Winners: Purgatory (Spike Chokeslam) Time: 3:23 (*) As Purgatory left the ring, Sheridan stuck around, standing over Anderson, staring. Finally, he called Bobby Lee Wilcox’ into the ring, snatching his mike away:
PS: What's that you're all asking? Why, Paul? Why? I'll tell you why. I ALWAYS was a follower. Even the names my mother gave me. . .Peter and Paul, names of two of the most famous followers of all time. Two of the apostles. . .you know something, all of you?
And when all was said and done, who got all the glory? That's right, their leader. Just like ALL leaders. Just like when I was in high school, all the leaders, the preppies, the jocks, they ALL got their cheerleader girlfriends, and their awards, and all that crap. . .and me, what did I get? What did Peter Paul Sheridan get? What did the guy who ran round for all those preppies, who cheered on the jocks, get?
I'll tell you. Jack sh*t. And then I came to Carolina. You know, when I was working in the Suplex Academy, I thought things would be different here. I thought things were gonna go my way. I thought I'd get the glory. Hell, I even had a more experienced guy in the back taking me under his wing.
And then I realized. . .nothing was different. I ended up tag teaming occasionally with Jack, as Jack Anderson and Paul Sheridan. Why were the names that way round?
Because you had to be the leader, didn't you, Jack? You were the golden boy of RAW Carolina... and I was stuck as a follower again.
So, I took matters into my own hands. And I decided, I'll MAKE myself a leader. And then I thought, let's make myself into THE leader.
Let's make MYSELF the golden boy. So I took those old clothes of yours, Jacky - and I became the best leader of them all - you. And you know what, Jack? I was BETTER at being you than you were. *I* wrestled for the Carolina title. *I* took on the big guys. And when
Felicity wanted to play dress-up. . . *I* satisfied her needs. That's right, Jack. To get back to the metaphor of the Apostles. . .
I was your very own Judas. Life's a bitch, isn't it? [He smirks, and walks away.] STONE © vs. CASANOVA BEETHOVEN, Carolina Television Title After the wild brawl earlier in the evening before the Piedmont Title match, it would seem that Stone would have an advantage over an already winded Casanova Beethoven. But that wasn’t the case at all; rather, it seemed that Beethoven was fired up by the brawl, and would like nothing better than to continue it, only trading his former dance partners in for Stone.
And that suited the Television Champion just fine. Nobody in the Carolinas can brawl like Stone – lest anyone forget his RAW Carolina debut, attacking Beethoven in his own locker room at the Carolina Classic. These two former linchpins of the Empire Club went at it tooth and nail in the early going, until the taller Beethoven used his leverage advantage to drop Stone with a leaping DDT.
Beethoven continued to work Stone over with a fervor normally reserved for one of his prize hos on a crack binge (Sorry, couldn’t help that one). But Stone ducked under a Beethoven clothesline attempt, and dropped him with a reverse neckbreaker. Stone bounced up, and came off flying with a legdrop from the second rope for a two count.
And then – as if he WOULDN’T – David Jericho came flying down the aisle, head bandaged from the earlier brass knuckle attack, and into the ring, jumping all over Beethoven. Stone watched with some amusement as the two hated rivals rolled around the ring trading punches – and ‘Cottonmouth’ Joe Lynch tossed the match out as a throng of officials finally cleared the ring.
Decision: No Contest Time: 8:32 (***) MARC TOWER © vs. “SENSATIONAL” SCOTT STUDD, Carolina Heavyweight Title The main event was a big one, as two of the strongest men in the Carolinas faced off for the biggest prize in the promotion. Studd received several shots at this title during his previous run in
RAW-C; some say he underachieved, HE says he was screwed. But whatever the case, he was out for redemption in Asheville.
Tower started the match quickly with his innovative power offense, catching the challenger with a Steel City Slam early on for a two and thisclose count. Studd rolled out of the ring to confer with his corner man Jordan Valentine, and it must have worked. Back in the ring, he took control with a kneelift and began working over Tower with his more traditional power moves, including a punishing two minute stretch in a bearhug.
At the eight minute mark, “Showboat” Eddie Cannon made his way out to the ring, infuriating Studd. His contentions were correct
– everyone had conspired against him. But Tower wasn’t too pleased to see Cannon, either. It seems he wanted to win this one himself.
At thirteen minutes, Studd clamped on the Studdly Stretch in the center of the ring, and things looked bad for the champ. But he wouldn’t give in, and somehow managed to crawl – with
Studd’s full weight on his back(!!) – to the ropes, and ‘Cottonmouth’ Joe Lynch called for the break. Studd wouldn’t let go, and Lynch got in his face. As he did, Dirk
Robertson leapt out of the crowd with a steel chair – and blasted Marc Tower right in the face with it(!!).
Studd pulled Tower up off the mat, and dropped him across the top rope with a Studd Gun. He went for the cover – and Lynch called for the bell. He had seen Robertson’s actions, and disqualified
Studd for them. Robertson stood in the aisle, seemingly very pleased with himself. Studd stood in the ring, glaring at Robertson – the look of a man who had been screwed one more time.
Winner: Marc Tower (Disqualification) Time: 16:24 (***) --- © RAW Carolina, MMII. http://www.yahoogroups.com/group/RAWCarolina
