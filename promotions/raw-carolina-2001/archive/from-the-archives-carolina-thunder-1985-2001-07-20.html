From the Archives: Carolina Thunder 1985

20 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Continuing our stroll down memory lane, let’s take a look at another historic card from the annals of Carolinas wrestling, the 1985 edition of Carolina Thunder.
CAROLINA THUNDER Charlotte Coliseum Charlotte, NC July 29, 1985 The opening tag match saw the much-hated duo of “SHOWBOAT” EDDIE CANNON & VINCENT RIDGEMONT score a victory over youngster RYAN
MARSHALL and his veteran mentor, JOHNNY STORMCLOUD. Not nearly as successful in the sport as his nephew Jason would later prove to be,
Stormcloud fell prey to a Cannon stungun for the pin.
Popular powerhouse MARK GOLDEN survived a tough war with “SWEET” LOU FALCONE. Falcone appeared to have won the match, but the official restarted it when it was determined he had used a roll of coins to lay Golden out. Falcone argued his case in vain, and Golden rolled him up from behind for the pin – with a poetic handful of tights.
Second generation superstar BEAU RAMSEY, JR. scored the biggest win of his brief career to date in a battle with one of the dirtiest veterans in the game, CHIEF JOSEPH. Ramsey scored the pin with a small package.
The savage monster CALIBAN completely overmatched young PATRICK WOLFE. The match promised to be a mismatch from the start, and ended up being even more so. Wolfe got exactly zero offense in before being pinned by a big powerslam.
One of the most exciting teams to hit the Carolinas in years, THE
WASHINGTON BROTHERS (Omar & Orlando), won a great contest against THE
CENTURIONS (#1 and #2). The match was later voted by readers of
Wrestling Digest as the third best match of the year – amazing considering most fans had never seen it, voting only on word of mouth.
Two savvy vets met in the next contest, with ERIC “THE HAMMER” FRASIER scoring a victory over “DASTARDLY” DICK ROGERS. Frasier ended a great brawl with one of the few scientific moves of the encounter, a Hammer-plex.
Texas wildman TERRY BRODY wasted no time getting the fans to boo him out of the building, whacking CURT READ with his branding iron before the opening bell had even rung. Brody continued his wild assault throughout the match, eventually winning with a convenient lead pipe to the head.
THE LETTERMAN’S CLUB (Casey Duncan & JJ Baxter) successfully defended their Carolina Tag-Team Titles against the challenge of young fan favorites MITCH GIBSON & JAKE NATHAN. The cocky veterans used the shoe of Coach Bobby Blackman on the forehead of Nathan for the pin.
The main event saw “ROYAL” LANCE KING, in the midst of his only – albeit brief – run as Carolina Heavyweight
Champion, successfully defend his title with a victory over tough veteran “OUTLAW” FRANK JAMES.
