RAW Carolina weekend house shows

12 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Since I haven’t seen any reports popping up on the internet, I thought I’d post the results of the live RAW Carolina event held
Saturday night in Statesville, North Carolina:
The opening bout saw the impressive return of HELLSPAWN (Hades &
Abyss) continue, as they knocked off Suplex Academy rookies PAUL
SHERIDAN & HARBINGER. Harbinger fell to a Pandemonium double-team for the pin.
The ever popular Father of the Year DIREWOLF was up next, polishing off “SCHOLARLY” SCOTT McBRIDE with an Alpha Male chokeslam in less than four minutes.
Hellspawn’s Judgment Day mate SINDER BLOKK also looked very impressive, absolutely destroying the Appalachian Assassin HOUNDDOG
MURPHY with a running powerslam at the three minute mark.
Young JAYSON RAYGE continued to turn heads, this time with an upset victory over SIDIOUS THE CLOWN. Rayge got the pin with his innovative
Infinite Rayge Driver, a reverse Death Valley Driver.
Reigning (although no longer recognized) Windy City RAW Tag-Team
Champions PURGATORY (Synn & Dementia) made their RAW-C debut in
Statesville Saturday, topping high-flying youngsters KID DYNAMITE & EL
DORADO. Dorado missed a 450 Splash, leaving him prey for a piledriver from Synn for the pin.
Handsome young STEVE FURY has been on quite a roll as of late, even cracking the Top Ten. Saturday night, that roll continued with a win over “BILLIONAIRE” JOE FORBES. Fury got the pin after dropping Forbes with Furious Anger, his innovative pumphandle tombstone piledriver.
Two men who have focused on tag-team wrestling of late squared off in singles action, as “ERADICATOR” DIRK ROBERTSON knocked off
Collateral Damage’s “SHINING” MIKE DIAMOND. After fifteen minutes of excellent action, Robertson got the pin with a
Total Eradication.
Two struggling superstars met in the next contest, which saw “THE WEAPON” FRANK REYNARD score a victory over international superstar JESSIE MULDOON. Muldoon fell to a Rush Hour sidekick – bulldog combo for the pin after a lackluster match.
The main event was a wild six man affair, an excellent match that saw
“FAST” EDDIE CUTLASS team with MARC TOWER and JACK
ANDERSON to knock off Carolina champ CASANOVA BEETHOVEN, VINCENT
CONSTANTINE AUGUSTUS, and the masked Texan ROADKILL RICK. RKR and
Beethoven continued their hostilities in this match, and intentional or not, Roadkill sprayed Beethoven in the face with PURPLE mist, allowing Anderson to get the pin with a Fireball Special.
---- I haven’t seen these results anywhere, either, so here’s a rundown of the annual RAW Carolina Veteran’s Day show in
Asheville at the Civic Center from Sunday afternoon: The opening bout was a tag-team affair that saw recent signees PURGATORY (Synn & Dementia) defeat the Suplex Academy graduates PHARAOH HOBBES & BRIMSTONE in a tune-up for their big Thanksgiving
Thunder showdown with the Blood Brothers.
Young PAUL SHERIDAN scored what is easily the biggest win of his blossoming career when he defeating veteran JESSIE MULDOON. Sheridan scored the pin with a not exactly beautiful but still very effective moonsault, catching Muldoon in the jaw with his knee in the way down.
Tag action continued in the next match, as HELLSPAWN (Abyss & Hades) knocked off young EL DORADO & KID DYNAMITE in a good little match-up. Dynamite fell prey to a Pandemonium that got the pin. The most dominant RAW Carolina Television Champion ever, MARC TOWER, continued his winning ways with a successful title defense over
“BILLIONAIRE” JOE FORBES, dropping him with a Tower of Power for the win. The fans may not be happy, but they have to be impressed with the recently re-dubbed HANGMAN, who continued his return to his roots with a dominant victory over former shootfighter “THE WEAPON”
FRANK REYNARD. Hangman scored the pin with a Hangman’s Noose neckbreaker. COLLATERAL DAMAGE (“Shining” Mike Diamond & “Unbreakable” Mike Steele) scored a big win over the top tag contenders, the Blood Brother duo of “ERADICATOR” DIRK
ROBERTSON & young JAYSON RAYGE. Steele pinned Rayge after an awesome
Dead End Drop elbow drop – backbreaker combo.
“FAST” EDDIE CUTLASS successfully defended his Piedmont Title with a disqualification victory over VINCENT CONSTANTINE AUGUSTUS. Cutlass seemed ready to drop VCA with a Guarantee only to see VCA’s bass player Axe Abraham run in with an electric guitar to the back to earn the DQ.
The main event saw CASANOVA BEETHOVEN once again successful in defense of his Carolina Heavyweight Title, this time against the beastly
DIREWOLF. A ref bump brought out VCA, who dusted Direwolf with the
Billion Dollar Knuckles, allowing Beethoven to deliver a Symphony for the pin.
