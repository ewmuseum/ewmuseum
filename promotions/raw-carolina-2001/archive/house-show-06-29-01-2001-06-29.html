RAW Carolina — Card Archive

House Show 06.29.01

29 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RAW Carolina’s first stop on its’ weekend swing was Elon College, North Carolina, on the campus of, ironically enough, Elon
College, for a live Friday night event. Results:
The opening bout featured eight of the fastest minutes you’ll ever see, as EL CID defeated veteran “GREASED LIGHTNING”
BOBBY PARKER in a fabulous high-flying match-up. Cid won the match with a gorgeous Tears of Spain, his top rope corkscrew moonsault plancha into a rolling crucifix – a move that happens a lot quicker than it takes to describe it.
Two powerhouses collided in the next match, a nice contrast to the first. The hairy man-beast and struggling parent DIREWOLF defeated the masked youngster UNKNOWN SOLDIER. Direwolf went for the Liplock early on, which provided ineffective given his opponent’s mask, but managed to put him away with the Alpha Male Chokeslam.
Powerhouse MARC TOWER made his RAW Carolina singles debut in the next contest, easily defeating the outmatched youngster STEVE FURY. Tower quickly established himself as a rough and tumble brawler, and put away Fury with the Tower of Power, a move that starts as a suplex and ends as a spinning powerbomb.
The Only Male That Counts, JESSIE MULDOON, was equally impressive in a victory over skyweight KID DYNAMITE. The young high-flyer was game, and even had Muldoon reeling in the early going, but a Love Muscle clothesline put an end to that early on. Muldoon brutalized the youngster, and seemed to enjoy it, finishing off with a stiff, stiff powerbomb.
The next match was a rematch of sorts from one of the big tag matches at Grand Strand Slam, as the popular newcomer FLASH defeated rookie
TOMMY TRENTON. Although it wouldn’t have taken a miracle for
Trenton to defeat Flash, as the youngster is quite talented, that’s exactly what it took for Flash to pin him, as he used the reverse suplex he calls The Miracle to score the victory.
New champions WILD STYLE (Drew Brody & Shannon Hamilton) defended their Carolina Tag Titles for the first time in Elon, defeating veteran brawlers THE WALKER BROTHERS (Isaac & Gideon, Jr.). The young champs used their devastating Nothing Is As It Seems fireman’s carry—necbkreaker combo for the pin.
The other half of that same GS—Slam tag match squared off next, as “ICEMAN” MARK ADAMS upended young powerhouse EZ KILLER.
Adams seems to completely worn off all the ring rust from his recent year long retirement, and looked very impressive against Killer. He finished off the big man with The Big Easy, his bridging Northern
Lights Hammerlock suplex.
“ERADICATOR” DIRK ROBERTSON is on a bit of a roll lately, as he has steamrolled into the finals of the tournament for the RAW
Carolina TV Title. He continued his winning ways in Elon by knocking off the surly leader of the Wild Bunch, TULLY ROEBUCK in a wild, all-over-the-building brawl. Robertson won the match by DDT’ing
Roebuck on a trash can.
The evening’s second title match saw the Piedmont Title on the line, as “X-PRESS” DAVID JERICHO defended his title against ALEX PATTERSON. The fans didn’t care much for either man, but that didn’t stop them from putting on an excellent contest, the best match of the night. Jericho successfully retained his title when Patterson tapped out while locked in the X-Press Way.
The main event was a hot, hot six man tag that had every person in the building on their feet the entire team. The Empire Club –
VINCENT CONSTANTINE AUGUSTUS, CASANOVA BEETHOVEN, & STONE – took on three of their most heated rivals, JACK ANDERSON,
“HURRICANE” KIRK MACLEAN, & “LAWBRINGER” THOMAS ADLER. The match was a wild affair, lasting nearly twenty minutes and spilling into every corner of the arena, with the fans eating up every minute. But after all that, it ended in the ring, where Anderson scored some measure of revenge as he pinned Beethoven following a Fireball Special.
