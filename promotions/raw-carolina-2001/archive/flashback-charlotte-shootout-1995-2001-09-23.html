Flashback: Charlotte Shootout 1995

23 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As we move forward towards this year’s edition, let’s set the Wayback Machine for 1995, as Carolina Championship Wrestling presented The Charlotte Shootout!!
CHARLOTTE SHOOTOUT Independence Arena Charlotte, NC September 27, 1995 The opening match of Shootout 95 was a six man tag, as Wild Bunch representatives “OUTLAW” FRANK JAMES & THE RAWBONES (Tully
Roebuck & Gideon Walker, Jr.) teamed up to defeat the fan favorite trio of MARK GOLDEN, TJ PICKENS, and “MANIAC” MANNY RAMOS.
James got a submission win after Pickens blacked out in the Outlaw
Claw. Tough biker HOGG NELSON looked impressive in an absolute demolition of old-school high-flyer “GREASED LIGHTNING” BOBBY PARKER.
Parker tried a cross bodyblock from the top rope, but Nelson caught him, and tossed him with a fallaway slam for the pin.
Canadian superstar TOMMY VICTORY scored his first major win in CCW, knocking off a former Television champion, “DEADLY” DEAN
DILLINGER. The crowds were really starting to warm up to Victory at this stage in his career, and he sent ‘em home happy with a
Victory Driver for the pin. Young Television champion IKE SAMPSON successfully defended his title against veteran “SOLDIER OF FORTUNE” TROY ROBINSON.
Robinson was in the employ of someone here, though the crowd didn’t yet know who – later revealed to be Jericho Weaver, but Sampson was too tough for the Alaskan native, finishing him with a
Deep Freeze double underhook DDT.
A wild tag-team match was up next, as veterans “BOUNTY HUNTER” SCOTT O’BRIEN & SKULL KRUSHER knocked off an immensely popular pair of future stars, ACE MATTHEWS & JASON
STORMCLOUD. Two years later, and the result would have been very different, but as it was, O’Brien took this chapter in his longtime war with Stormcloud when the 300 pound Krusher pinned
Matthews after a scissors kick. Legendary Mexican superstar GOLDEN PALADIN made his Carolina debut at
Shootout ’95, making short work of brash youngster TOMMY
BEDFORD. The glimpses of Bedford’s future potential continued to shine through, but Paladin was too much for him, and Bedford tapped out to the Mexican Deathlock.
Southern independent icon “SHOWBOAT” EDDIE CANNON continued his final run towards championship contention, knocking off the 390 pound SPIRITUS MUNDI, somehow finding the strength to drop him over the top rope for a hotshot and the pin.
In the midst of their record-breaking tag-team title run, “THUNDERBOLT” JACK SAMPSON & HIRO NOGAMI were successful once again in a defense, this time knocking off the team from the
‘Savage Land’, FLESH & BLOOD. Sampson and Nogami trademark Stereo Dragon Suplexes pinned both opponents in the center of the ring for the victory.
The main event saw the last Carolina Heavyweight Title defense for
“THE BEAR” KENJI HASHIMOTO, as he successfully defended against the self-proclaimed “Sexiest Man on Earth”,
CHRISTOPHER FANTASY. Hashimoto won the match with a Bearhug Suplex, his belly to belly from the second rope, but it was that move that led to the end of his career. Two slipped discs and a third disc that ruptured led to him vacating the title, and later forced him into early retirement.
