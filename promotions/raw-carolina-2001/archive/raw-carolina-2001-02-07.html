RAW Carolina

7 February 2001

I know it's customary to do this sort of thing before you open, but honestly, who knows which questions are going to be asked frequently before you have your first post?!? So here it is, nearly three months into our run, the official RAW Carolina FAQ. . .
My gracious thanks to the various RAW fedheads who have gone before me, as I use my collage technique known as “crib and paste” to put together the official, wholly original (Heh.) RAW Carolina FAQ. Now, without further ado. . .
The Official RAW Carolina FAQ Version 1.1 ______________________________ ~I~ General Information ______________________________ ~II~ Participation ______________________________ ~III~ Cards ______________________________ ~IV~ Who's Who? ______________________________ ~V~ Championships ______________________________ RAW Carolina Established in the year of our Lord MM, ano domini. President: [name withheld] www.rawcarolina.cjb.net www.yahoogroups.com/group/RAWCarolina ______________________________ ~I~ General Information ______________________________ Welcome to RAW Carolina. What we can offer you: - One of the finest legacies in e-wrestling, RAW (and before that, WAR!). - The finest staff around – Me!! - A solid regional fed, built around storytelling and character development, which always take precedence over titles and winning matches. - Cards written entirely in report form, to take the load off of writers (read: Me.) and give everyone ample opportunity to devote their full attention to the story they are telling with their wrestlers and their angles.
- Fictional wrestlers only, and no full-time wrestlers from other RAW promotions (guest shots, however, are not only allowed, they’re encouraged!!) - Personal rapport with everyone involved, thanks to the nifty raw-ooc yahoogroup. - Strictness yet fairness. - A FAQ pretty much lifted whole-hearted from the RAW & RAW UK pages. Background: RAW Carolina is one of the growing number of “baby RAWs”. What that means is this: We are a part of the RAW “universe”, only we compete at a much smaller, regional level. RAW Carolina is a great place for newcomers to the game to cut their teeth, and a great place for veteran handlers to revisit mothballed characters or fine-tune a new character before breaking out for the big leagues. It’s a symbiotic relationship, as the new handlers can look to the veterans for guidance and instruction, and the “newbies” allow the veterans to develop new ideas at a slower pace.
Whenever RAW has roster openings, it will look to “promote from within” and grab a promising character from RAW-C. Much like real-life regional feds, I expect the turnover rate to be high, with wrestlers coming and going with regularity, often to bigger and better things.
And, of course, like Goldilocks, anyone who feels that the pacing and structure of RAW Carolina is juuuuuuuust right is free to stay as long as they wish, and become a “Southern independent legend”, or something.
Other things: (This part is lifted verbatim from the RAW FAQ, with good reason: It is the divining principle for any and all RAW feds.)
When it comes to rules, RAW uses this philosophy: THINK. Use common sense. Before you send out a possibly offensive roleplay, re-read it and try and see if anybody will be bothered by it. Homophobia, racism, or misogyny will be treated with the utmost discipline.
Anyone attempting to use such material in roleplays will first be given a warning, and if any more occurrences happen after that, will be fired, with no exceptions.
Never use another person's characters in a roleplay, in-ring, etc. without the handler's permission! Example, don't have Wrestler X's valet appear in your RP if Wrestler X's handler hasn't approved it.
There will be punishments handed down for this. You can not attack any named RAW Carolina (commentators, interviewers, etc) without permission. Chances are that we'll always give you the OK but we need to know about it first!
Once again, just use COMMON SENSE. I will add this to that: It is very important to re-read your RP. I realize that we are not all on the same education level, or even on the same competency level in the English language, but misspelled words and bad grammar are pet peeves of mine. I’m not saying it will bury your character, but I can’t help but be influenced. GOOD GRAMMAR=GOOD
WRITING, and GOOD WRITING=GOOD CHARACTER. And Good characters tend to get the push. ______________________________ ~II~ Participation ______________________________ RAW Carolina, as I’ve said, plans to focus heavily on storyline development, even at the expense of won-loss records and titles.
We want stories and we want heat. They co-exist. Plain and simple. We want wrestlers who can deliver that heat. In order to do that, you must roleplay.
RP-ing helps your chances of winning enormously. In fact, if you don't RP, you don't win. To sum up, you MUST RP. But why
wouldn't you? It's all part of the fun, and if you don't want to be part of the fun, what the hell are you doing here? One RP every “cycle” should be more than sufficient. We have a “big event” every 7-14 days or so that ends each “cycle”, with random house shows mixed in to flesh things out.
If you really want to win a match, strats (for the card) are important. In-rings are not necessary (although appreciated) but strats are. A good strat will mention some highspots you’d like to see, a reminder of the angles you want to get across in the match, both short-term and long, and possible endings to the match.
In order of importance in the fed, the needs go like this: RPs, angle participation, strats, and general effort. All of these are important if you want to do well in RAW Carolina.
______________________________ ~III~ Cards ______________________________ As I said before, we will have one “big event” every 7-14 days, with house shows sprinkled in between. The big events will have names to increase the hype, while the house shows will not.
Certain big events will be bigger than others, like the Queen City Showdown, March Madness, and the crown jewel of the RAW Carolina wrestling year, the Carolina Classic, held every year in April in
Charlotte. RAW Carolina is a small, indy-type fed, and in order to keep that feel, we have no television deal, so we have no weekly TV show. The happenings from one big event will flow logically into the next, so it will have the episodic feel of weekly TV, only without commentators and match play-by-play. The in-rings and backstage angles found on other e- wrestling cards are still very much welcomed. The “history” of RAW
Carolina goes back over 20 years, with more chapters being added all the time. Anything in the old days that could be tied to your character is an added bonus.
Most handlers I know in other feds will read their matches in their entirety, skim through the big matches on the show, and then just skip through to the winners of the other matches. So, if the whole card can be kept to the length of an average PBP match, then the handler can certainly read the entire card. At least that’s my theory.
But why ramble on about it when others have said it better?!? James Dean, President of RAW, what do you have to say?!? “RAW Carolina - A fed written entirely in report form shouldn't work, but a) it's nice and quick to read and b) the reports are incredibly well written.”
(From a RSPWF post on his 5 favorite feds) The honorable [name withheld], a fedhead in this game for the last five years, do you agree?!?
“I'm prediciting Raw Carolina, if it carries on being written in such a brilliant report format, to go on and be the success of the year, and perhaps even transform e-wrestling towards shorter, more readable cards.”
(From the RSPWF post announcing his retirement from fed-heading) E-Wrestling University, one of the gathering places for the e-w community, who are you picking in your “Keep An Eye On This Fed” feature?!?
“RAW Carolina. [name withheld] is one of the elite match writers out there. He now runs RAW Carolina, and has been for some time, operating it entirely in Report format. The catch is, the reports he writers are so damn good, you feel like you've been there! It's the best new thing in e-wrestling today folks, and it doesn't even have many players!”
Maybe I’m on to something. . . ______________________________ ~IV~ Who's Who? ______________________________ Fictional faces - RAW Carolina President: “Showboat” Eddie Cannon. Cannon is a legendary figure on the Southern independent scene, having wrestled everywhere and won titles wherever he’s been. Upon his retirement a few years back, he took some of the savings garnered over his 30 year career and invested in Carolina Championship Wrestling, a promotion where he had once been the centerpiece star, buying it from longtime promoter Jackie Boone. CCW reached new heights with Cannon behind the big desk, and he was the driving force in the November 2000 merger with
Miles Silverberg’s RAW, which saw CCW become RAW Carolina. Still lean and trim, Cannon still looks as though he could take most of his employees. Cannon is a straight shooter; not exactly a heel, and not exactly a face. His relationships with the wrestlers are on a strict individual basis. There are some faces he loves, and some he can’t stand – same way with the heels. Despite all that, Cannon has been known to be involved in some shady dealings, most of which were rumored to work towards his own financial betterment.
RAW Carolina Ring Announcer: Bobby Lee Wilcox. Wilcox is your basic dumb hick. Perhaps the last man in the world who still believes professional wrestling to be “real”, Wilcox loves his job and will do anything to keep it. Is known to do a mean Bob Newhart impression.
RAW Interviewer: Any and all interviews conducted by official RAW-C personnel will be done by Vernon St. Jean. Vernon got his start in the business as a production assistant for RAW, where he was plucked from obscurity by Stevie Ray Murdoch and given the handle “Intern Vern”.
Vernon used those fifteen minutes of fame wisely, and before they were up, he had parlayed them into this gig. He’s still a bit unsure of his abilities, and absolutely hates to be referred to as “Intern Vern”.
That’s it so far. We could still use some referees, a timekeeper, a locker room doctor, and any number of other characters to flesh out our little world. Feel free to create one in a flash, and they may become an “official” part of RAW Carolina.
______________________________ ~V~ Championships ______________________________ Carolina Heavyweight Title: The big strap, carried by the biggest star in the promotion. A title with over twenty years of direct lineage that has been held by many men who have gone on to be some of the top stars in the world. Every “big event” will feature a match with the champ.
Piedmont Heavyweight Title: Formerly known as the Carolina TV Title, the name was changed when CCW lost their television contract. This belt is generally held by young up-and-comers, looking to make their mark but not quite ready for the main event.
If I had to wager a guess, I’d say that if you’re still reading at this point, you’re interested in joining. If so, great. RAW Carolina is always in a hiring mood, and if hiring a great character makes our roster a bit too big, we’ll just have to trim a bit of the fat and get rid of a character or two who just aren’t cutting it.
