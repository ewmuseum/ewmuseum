RAW Carolina — Card Archive

Weekend House Show Action

26 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RAW Carolina’s weekend action kicked off Saturday night at Littlejohn Coliseum on the campus of Clemson University!! Results: Hot rookie PAUL SHERIDAN once again scored the biggest victory of his young career, this time toppling former shootfighting champion
“THE WEAPON” FRANK REYNARD. Sheridan got the win with a textbook moonsault. COLLATERAL DAMAGE (“Shining” Mike Diamond & “Unbreakable” Mike Steele) scored a disqualification victory over HELLSPAWN (Abyss & Hades) in a big Riverbanks Rumble preview, after Damien Scratch’s cane broke over Diamond’s head.
Former Television Champions collided with MARC TOWER coming out on top in his match with the masked ROADKILL RICK. Tower got the win with his Tower of Terror finisher.
“FAST” EDDIE CUTLASS successfully defended his Piedmont Title with a pinfall victory over handsome young STEVE FURY, dropping the Suplex Academy graduate with a Guarantee reverse Russian Legsweep.
JACK ANDERSON continued the shutout for Judgment Day with a disqualification victory over the monstrous SINDER BLOKK. Anderson took control at the eight minute mark, only to be cracked in the skull with Blokk’s massive cinder block hammer.
VINCENT CONSTANTINE AUGUSTUS teamed with the bloodthirsty “DREADMIND” DAVE PITRELLI to score a big victory over the popular duo of DIREWOLF & STONE. VCA got a tap-out after locking
Direwolf in his Empire Stretch, following a chairshot from Pitrelli.
The main event saw CASANOVA BEETHOVEN defend his Carolina Heavyweight
Title in a surprisingly competitive match with young JAYSON RAYGE.
Rayge was tough, but in the end he fell to a Symphony for the pin. -------------------------------- RAW Carolina’s weekend swing finished up down the road from Clemson in bucolic Anderson, South Carolina, with a big Sunday matinee. Results: Judgment Day’s huge monster SINDER BLOKK opened the show with a big win over veteran star “CALIFORNIA STUD” MARK GOLDEN, stopping him with a nice running powerslam.
Masked Texan ROADKILL RICK used his Texas Speedbump powerbomb – boston crab combo to secure a victory over feisty youngster PAUL
SHERIDAN, who was also subject to some unwanted interference from jailbait Shona Maynard, who inadvertently tripped up Sheridan in going after RKR, sending him stumbling right into the Speedbump.
Judgment Day’s spokesman HANGMAN defended his new Carolina Television Title in impressive fashion with a win over youngster STEVE
FURY. A top rope two handed chokeslam known simply as The Noose finished off the rookie.
Judgment Day finished its’ perfect night as HELLSPAWN (Abyss & Hades) took home a victory over Windy City Warriors PURGATORY (Synn &
Dementia), pinning the seven footer Dementia with a patented
Pandemonium double-team. “DREADMIND” DAVE PITRELLI used his awesome Hatred Within spear from the top rope to topple a tough opponent in “THE
WEAPON” FRANK REYNARD, continuing his impressive singles run in the wake of the death of his tag-team partner Mark Adams.
VINCENT CONSTANTINE AUGUSTUS picked up a big win in a battle of top contenders against the Pittsburgh Powerhouse MARC TOWER. VCA got the duke in a surprisingly clean match with his Big Profit modified brainbuster.
CASANOVA BEETHOVEN made it two for two on the weekend against The
Blood Brothers, defeating “ERADICATOR” DIRK ROBERTSON in a successful defense of the Carolina Heavyweight Title. Can he make it three for three against Marc Tower Tuesday night?!? Come to Columbia to find out.
