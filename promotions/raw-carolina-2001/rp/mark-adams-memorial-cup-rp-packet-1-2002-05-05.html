Storm's wife, Taylor, makes hotel reservations on her laptop computer.)
Taylor: "Okay, boys, you are all set. Kid: "Kewl. And I think we are as ready as we can get for our first round match against Jordan Valentine and Adam Destiny."

-------------------------- VAN WILD & SCOTT STARRING -------------------------- [Thursday 2nd May, 2002. Fade into the parking lot of the RAW Carolina Headquarters. Squinting against the late morning sun despite his sunglasses, Van Wild locates an empty spot and pulls in.
He glances into the mirror and runs his hand through his long sandy- blonde hair, noting the several days growth of stubble on his face.
Time to shave again soon, he thinks with a grin. Probably. Climbing out of the car a little gingerly, he heads towards the entrance of the building, a limp evident in his stride. Halfway there, he stops and looks back at his car, pausing for a moment in thought before returning to the vehicle. Reaching into the back seat, he pulls out a crutch and turns back towards the building.

VAN WILD: "You've got more?" CASEY: "Oh yes, I've got tattoos in MUCH more interesting places than that..." [She smiles mischievously and takes the pen back from him. Pushing the sleeve of his t-shirt up, she writes her telephone number on his arm.]

VAN WILD: "Heh, thanks Doc, don't know _how_ I forgot about that. See ya." MCGUIRK: "Bye, son."
