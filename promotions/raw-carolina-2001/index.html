A Carolinas-region "baby RAW" feeder federation, founded around November 2000 and running a full slate of monthly and house-show cards through the whole of 2001 under its own regional titles. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

RAW Carolina describes itself, in its own February 2001 FAQ, as one of a growing number of "baby RAWs" — a regional feeder federation within a wider shared wrestling universe, built to give newcomers a place to learn the craft and veterans a place to develop new characters before its own roster fed talent upward to the bigger promotion it served.

Across a full year of monthly supercards and house shows — Boot Camp Brawl, the Pee Dee Melee, Super Sunday, Ballpark Bedlam, Carolina Thunder, Franklin Street Fright Night, Thanksgiving Thunder and more — it built out a full regional title picture around its Piedmont Heavyweight, Television and Tag Team championships, twice reaching back into its own claimed history for "From the Archives" reprints of earlier, undated Carolina wrestling lore.

In 2002 it kept the same report-form cards, from New Year's Brawl in Knoxville to Grand Strand Slam in June, and staged the Mark Adams Memorial Cup tag tournament. When RAW itself closed that spring, its FAQ 2.0 described the regional RAWs regrouping as ten NWA-style territories, its own reaching from the Carolinas into Georgia, Virginia, Maryland, West Virginia and Delaware, with its events broadcast on NASN.

Its last big night on the record is Ghosts of RAW, the Halloween 2002 interfed pay-per-view it co-promoted with RAW-Ohio Valley and RAW-Vegas, where Christian Demonstone took the Carolina Heavyweight Championship from Ric Beauty and Fast Eddie Cutlass wrestled his final RAW match. A September 2002 recruiting notice is the latest of its own documents; how it ended is not in the surviving record.

Vegas Wrestling Federation (VWF, Vegas, 2001–2005)

Crossover booking with RAW Carolina (2000) (2002)
RAW Carolina and RAW-Vegas co-promoted Ghosts of RAW on Halloween 2002 with RAW-Ohio Valley, and the RAW Tag Team Champions defended on both promotions' cards.

RAW-Florida

Sister promotion of RAW Carolina (2000) (2002)
Both call themselves baby RAWs of the RAW universe; RAW-Florida's FAQ is built from RAW Carolina's and credits its head.
