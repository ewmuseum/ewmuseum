IPW-XFWL — Card

Impact Xtreme, October 18, 2003 (Part 2)

Oct 25, 2003, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Oh Yeah” plays as Aaron Briggs and Bobby White, the team known as White
Shadow, enter the ring. The “Undying Remix” begins playing as The Russians walk out onto the stage. Pawel pulls on the chain around Igor’s neck, signaling for him to walk faster.
JL: The Russians heading out here now for their biggest challenge yet here in IPW-XFWL. AR: Oh I don’t know, Techno Team 2008 was pretty challenging. JL: Two weeks ago, The Russians attacked White Shadow, beating them up just for the sake of getting some attention.
AR: Yeah well while destroying no name losers can be fun, if the Russians want to be tag champions, they need to take out the real competition.
JL: Indeed, which is why they challenged Aaron Briggs and Bobby White to a match right here tonight.
AR: I just hope Briggs and White said goodbye to all of their friends, because they’re not walking out of this one alive.
JL: We’ve seen the Russians for about a month now, just destroying people. They are ruthless. AR: And aggressive. JL: The Future and The Two Dudes have to be looking at The Russians as possible challengers for the tag belts.
AR: Well yeah. JL: The Russians could easily be the biggest threat in the tag division today. AR: You know what, they’re scary guys, but if they go up against The Future,
Lance and Paul will win. It’s that simple.
JL: You sound awfully confident. AR: I am awfully confident. But why shouldn’t I be? The Future have been every team that’s challenged for the belts. They’re the best.
JL: They do have quite a victory record as champions over the past five months. AR: And it’s not ending anytime soon. They’ll beat The Two Dudes. They’ll beat White Shadow. They’ll beat Gangsta Heaven. And if they have to, they’ll beat The Russians.
The bell sounds as Pawel and Aaron Briggs start things off. The two men circle each other some. Aaron looks over his shoulder at Igor standing in the corner.
Pawel and Aaron move in to an elbow and collar lock up. Both men struggle to gain the advantage, but neither can take control. They break the lock up and begin circling each other again. Pawel and Aaron come together again with an elbow and collar lock up. Aaron takes the advantage, pushing Pawel back some.
The tide quickly turns as Pawel gains control and pushes Aaron backwards. Once again the two men break the lock up. Aaron paces back and forth, trying to come up with a plan. Pawel stands staring at Aaron.
JL: Both men clearly frustrated that they can’t gain the early advantage. AR: I get frustrated when those Direct TV commercials come on. How Lawrence
Fishburn went from the Cowboy on PeeWee’s Playhouse, to Morpheus in the
Matrix, to hocking Direct TV I’ll never know.
Pawel and Aaron walk to the center of the ring to lock up again. This time,
Aaron lands a swift kick to Pawel’s midsection. Aaron takes Pawel and Irish whips him into the ropes. As Pawel runs, Aaron bends over, clearly planning to back drop Pawel. But the Russian is able to stop himself right in front of the doubled over Briggs. Pawel drops down flat to the mat and nails Aaron with a stiff thrust to the throat! Briggs stumbles backwards into the corner. Pawel stands up and runs over to Aaron, jumping up and taking Briggs over with a monkey flip. With Briggs down, Pawel springs up to the top rope. He stands waiting for Aaron to get up.
JL: What is Pawel going to do here? AR: I don’t know, but I wouldn’t want to be Biggs right now. JL: It’s Briggs. Aaron Briggs. AR: Like I care. Aaron stands up. He turns to see Pawel at the top rope. Pawel dives down and lands on Aaron’s shoulders for an attempted Hurricanrana, but Briggs catches him instead! Pawel tries to take Briggs over, but finds himself unable to.
Briggs takes Pawel into a powerbomb position. He charges towards the corner and drops Pawel down across the turnbuckle on the back of his head! Pawel lays motionless on the mat. Aaron walks across the ring and tags in Bobby White.
JL: Bobby White in now. White Shadow has taken control of this match. AR: Well yeah, Briggs just tried decapitating Pawel! That can’t be legal. White walks across the ring where Pawel is starting to stir. Bobby pulls Pawel up and whips him into the ropes. Pawel runs back, only to be taken down with a drop toe hold by White. Pawel looks up and slowly begins crawling towards Igor.
White grabs Pawel by the leg and drags him back towards White Shadow’s corner. Bobby picks Pawel up and takes him into an abdominal stretch. Pawel’s face wrinkles in agony. White tightens the hold some more, putting further pressure on Pawel. Bobby tags in Briggs. Aaron climbs up to the middle rope. He dives down with a straight elbow drop onto Pawel’s exposed ribs! Pawel drops down to the mat, holding his ribs in pain. Briggs pulls Pawel up and takes him over with a Northern Lights Suplex, holding onto the bridge for the 1-2-NO!
Pawel kicks out. JL: Briggs with a Northern Lights Suplex. AR: I hate to say it, but he did that perfectly. JL: Pawel is in a lot of trouble here. AR: See, Briggs really hit that move in a perfect way. JL: Alright, we get it! AR: You do mister? Perfect! Aaron stands up and stomps down a few times on Pawel. He walks over and climbs up to the top rope. Pawel stirs some on the mat. Briggs motions with his hands for the crowd to cheer him on. As they begin cheering, Aaron takes off, attempting a headbutt from the top rope. But Pawel is able to roll out of the way, leaving Briggs to hit nothing but mat! Both men lay on the mat as the referee begins a double ten count.
JL: Both Aaron and Pawel down now and they both need to make the tag. AR: Pawel especially. Igor hasn’t been in yet and he’s ready to rip somebody’s head off. Pawel slowly crawls across the ring towards Igor. Meanwhile, Briggs rises to his feet. He goes to tag in White, but turns to see Pawel close to making the tag in to Igor. Aaron charges full speed across the ring, jumping over Pawel and hitting Igor with a forearm shot to the face! With Igor momentarily dazed,
Briggs grabs Pawel by the leg and pulls him across the ring. Aaron tags in
Bobby. White places Pawel in a Boston Crab! Pawel begins screaming out in pain.
At the same time, Briggs climbs up to the top rope. Pawel’s screams become louder. Igor’s eyes widen. He steps into the ring. The referee is right there to tell Igor to get out, but Igor simply shoves the referee aside. Igor runs over and grabs Bobby with both hands by the throat. He lifts White off of Pawel and launches him over the top rope, sending Bobby crashing down hard back first to the floor!
JL: Good God! Bobby may be finished! AR: For his sake, I certainly hope he is. Briggs, at the top rope, dives down towards Igor with a cross body. Igor catches Briggs. The Russian lifts Briggs up into a guerrilla press. He walks over to the ropes and tosses Aaron down onto the already unconscious Bobby
White! The referee calls for the bell. Igor turns towards the referee and lifts him up by the throat. He throws the referee across the ring. The referee lays motionless.
JL: Igor is just destroying everybody here! AR: Uh, maybe we should get out of here. RING ANNOUNCER – The winners of the match, by disqualification, White Shadow! JL: White Shadow will get the win but Aaron and Bobby aren’t moving! AR: Funny how they look more like the losers then the winners. Not funny ha ha, but funny ironic. Though funny ironic makes me go ha ha.
Igor lifts Pawel up onto his shoulders and steps out of the ring, carrying
Pawel up the ramp.
JL: We need to get some help out here for White Shadow! AR: And the referee. JL: And the referee! AR: I already said that. JL: We’ll be right back. COMMERCIAL BREAK... “Still Fly” plays as D Money stands in the ring. A lie detector is set up in the center of the ring. A man dressed in a gray flannel suit stands next to the lie detector.
JL: Welcome back fans. D Money out here now. I’m not sure why he has a lie detector though.
AR: Oh it’s a lie detector. I thought it was some sort of machine to electrocute Killmore with.
JL: Well it’s not. AR: Too bad. Killmore on fire would so equal ratings. JL: D Money swears that he did not sign the contract to fight Killmore at
Funhouse. AR: And he didn’t. I saw him not do it. JL: If D Money can prove that he didn’t sign it, the match will be cancelled. AR: You know, Killmore has a lot of nerve. Why does he want to fight D Money so badly anyway?
JL: It’s about respect. For months D Money has refused to fight Killmore, saying that the rookie doesn’t deserve to fight him.
AR: He doesn’t. JL: Killmore needs to fight D Money. He simply has to. It’s not even about winning. Just getting in there and proving that he belongs.
AR: But he doesn’t. D Money stands smiling near the ropes. He lifts the microphone up to his mouth. D MONEY – I have to hand it to you Killmore, you went all out with this. You hired somebody to perfectly duplicate my signature. Probably paid good money you don’t have for that. The thing is kid, you may be playing my games, but you’re going to lose. In the end, people like you always lose. You’re not as smart as I am. You’re not even close. You thought you covered every base.
But you didn’t think far enough. What I have here is a lie detector, along with my own personal lawyer. He’s going to hook me up to this thing and I will finally prove that I did not sign that contract. Irwin, let’s do this.
D Money puts the microphone down on the table. He sits in the chair next to the table. Irwin, the lawyer, places the necessary connections on D Money for the test to work.
JL: D Money taking a lie detector test here to prove that he didn’t sign the contract. But how can we trust these results?
AR: Are you suggesting D Money would lie? JL: Well it is his lawyer, his lie detector, and his desire to get out of the match against Killmore.
AR: But he’s not a liar. I mean, not always. D MONEY – Alright, Irwin, is it ready? IRWIN – Yes sir. D MONEY – Irwin. IRWIN – Okay. Do you work for IPW-XFWL? D MONEY – Yes. A green light flashes on the lie detector, showing that D Money’s answer is true. IRWIN – Are you rich? D MONEY – Beyond belief. The green light flashes. IRWIN – Is your first name Darren? D MONEY – What? IRWIN – Your first name, is it Darren? D MONEY – Alright, look, let’s move to the big question. IRWIN – Okay. Did you sign the contract to face Killmore at Funhouse? D MONEY – No. A red light flashes on the lie detector, indicating that D Money is lying. D MONEY – Wait a second. I didn’t sign the contract! The red light flashes again. D MONEY – I never signed the contract! The red light flashes again. D MONEY – This stupid machine is broken! The red light flashes again. D MONEY – That’s it! I’m tired of this! The green light flashes. D Money rips off the connections to the lie detector. D MONEY – Killmore, I don’t know how you did this, but I don’t care. You want me in a match at Funhouse, well congratulations, you’ve officially pissed me off enough. In eight days kid I will kick your sorry ass, and when you’re down on the mat, looking up out of your swollen, blood covered eyes, it will be my grinning face standing over your carcass. You want to prove yourself kid? You’ve got your chance. But don’t be too disappointed when you crash and burn. And believe me, you will.
D Money throws the mic down and exits the ring. He walks up the ramp and towards the back. JL: D Money and Killmore is a go for Funhouse. AR: Though if I were Killmore, and believe me I’m so glad I’m not, I’d leave the country right now. D Money is gonna murder him.
Scout stands backstage with Hades McFan. HADES MCFAN – Scout, you called me over here? SCOUT – That’s right. HADES MCFAN – Well...why? SCOUT – I have an important announcement to make concerning the Russians versus White Shadow match from earlier tonight. The referee ruled a disqualification win for Aaron and Bobby, but as the director of decisions,
I’ve decided to change that ruling to a no contest. As I see it, while Igor may have entered the ring illegally and attacked the referee, he only did it because White Shadow was double teaming Pawel. So the match was a no contest.
Furthermore... HADES MCFAN – There’s more? SCOUT – Would you let me talk? HADES MCFAN – Sorry. SCOUT – Furthermore, since the match ended in a no contest, I have decided to sign a rematch between The Russians and White Shadow to take place in eight days at Funhouse. And this time, we will have a decisive winner. I’ll make sure of that.
Scout smiles into the camera. JL: White Shadow taking on The Russians at Funhouse! AR: Oh man, The Russians are gonna finish White Shadow off for good! “Don’t You Forget About Me” plays as The Two Dudes and Donny the Donkey appear at the top of the entranceway to a big ovation. Big Daddy Cool Dudley and Molly Ringwald’s Boyfriend exchange a clumsy high five. Donny continues to chew his cud. The three jog down to the ring.
JL: Two big matches coming up here as Molly Ringwald’s Boyfriend takes on
Lance, and Big Daddy Cool Dudley takes on Paul. Remember, if The Future wins both matches, the Future and Two Dudes match at Funhouse will be non-title.
AR: As it should be. The Two Dudes deserve a tag title shot as much as Kobe
Bryant deserves to be acquitted.
JL: If The Two Dudes can win either of these matches, the title will be on the line at Funhouse. Either way, we’ll find out next!
COMMERCIAL BREAK... Lance removes his gold Tag Team Championship belt from his waist and hands it to Paul. Paul takes the bandana off his head and shines the two title belts.
The Two Dudes watch The Future’s display of arrogance, MRB warms up with a few jumping jacks.
JL: Welcome back fans as Molly Ringwald’s Boyfriend gets ready to take on
Lance here. AR: This is gonna be more fun to watch then a Culture Club concert. And those were fun to watch. Boy George and the drummer would break up like twice a show right on stage.
Paul and BDCD step to the arena floor and stand in their opposite corners,
Donny remains on the floor and continues to chew his cud. The bell sounds and the match is underway. Lance and MRB circle one another, MRB rhythmically claps his hands and gets the audience to join him. They engage in a collar and elbow tie up, MRB musters up all his strength and pushes Lance down to the mat. Lance rapidly slides outside the ring and receives a few words of encouragement from
Paul in the form of a free style.
JL: Paul giving Lance some advice. AR: And it rhymes! Lance rolls back into the ring, he ties up a second time with MRB. Lance slips out of the tie up and gets a go-behind on MRB. Lance attempts to lift MRB up for an early German suplex, but MRB counters by hooking his legs around
Lance’s waist and turning his momentum forward into a victory roll. Lance escapes the roll up before The Ref gets down to start a pin count, both wrestlers get back to their feet. After being side-stepped, Lance bounces off the ropes with MRB keeping his head down in preparation for a backdrop. Lance dives over MRB and pulls him into a sunset flip, MRB kicks out before The Ref can even get to a 1 count. Back up again, MRB takes a charging Lance down with an armdrag. Lance gets up and charges again, MRB pivots and takes Lance down with a hip toss. Instead of getting caught a third time, Lance goes to the outside by Paul again.
JL: Lance looking for more advice from his partner. AR: You know, this is how The Future work best. Together. It’s why they’re the best tag team ever.
Paul gives Lance a few smacks in the side of his temple to clear his head and get him motivated, Lance looks fired up and ready to fight. On the other side,
BDCD snacks on some cheetos from his cheeto factory, he offers a handful to
Donny, Donny continues to chew his cud. As the Ref’s count reaches 6, Lance climbs the steel steps and gets back into the ring. MRB adjusts his tye dyed headband and is ready to go again. Feigning another tie up, Lance single legs
MRB and takes him down to his back. Once he is turned over onto his stomach,
MRB is locked in a single leg Boston Crab. Lance leans into the hold, but then stands up while still holding MRB’s left leg. MRB takes four large steps forward, positioning MRB a great distance from the ropes, and sits back into the Crab. When asked by the Ref if he wants to submit, MRB shakes his head no.
JL: Molly Ringwald’s Boyfriend refuses to submit! AR: Funny, I so would have pegged him as the submissive type. JL: Okay, really didn’t need to know that. AR: Hey, is it my fault Molly Ringwald likes to dominate? Lance stands up and pulls MRB even further. With the Ref down to verify any potential submission from MRB, Paul reaches in from the floor and punches
MRB’s leg. Immediately BDCD races towards Paul to stop the cheating, Paul sees BDCD coming and runs away. Lance releases the single leg Boston Crab and goes to see what is happening to his partner. Paul is scurrying away from BDCD to the other side of the ring, but stops in his tracks as he stands face-to-face with Donny. BDCD comes up from behind, so Paul has nowhere to go.
Paul dives underneath the ring. Lance is standing against the ropes looking to the floor, seeking to find out where Paul went. MRB is back on his feet again but with a limp, he grabs Lance by the waist and rolls him up for a surprise pin. The Ref counts 1-2-kick out by Lance.
JL: Lance kicks out! I thought it was over for sure there! AR: Well you’re a moron. JL: Paul running from the Donkey. What is that about? AR: Hey, nobody wants to go near Big Daddy Cool Dudley’s stinky ass. Trust me. Both MRB and Lance are back on their feet. Lance tries to pick up MRB and hit an Olympic slam, but MRB escapes Lance’s grip. MRB pivots so he’s back to back with Lance and hooks his arms to take him down with a backslide. 1-2-No, another kick out from Lance. Before Lance has a chance to orientate himself,
MRB leaps inelegantly onto Lance’s back, he applies his dreaded sleeper hold.
Lance staggers around the ring a few times, finally sandwiching MRB into the turnbuckle to break the sleeper. With MRB in the corner, Lance hits a series of hard knee strikes. He takes a moment to look outside the ring again for Paul, but it appears he is still underneath the ring. Lance pulls MRB out of the corner and performs a powerful Juvi driver, and then gracefully turns into a camel clutch.
JL: Molly Ringwald’s Boyfriend is in a world of hurt here! AR: I wish I were in a world of candy. Candy is good. BDCD slams his hand on the mat to encourage MRB, the crowd claps along. Lance doesn’t appear to have MRB’s arms completely locked around his knees, which is allowing MRB to press his hands on the canvas as leverage to stand up.
Slowly but surely, MRB rises up with Lance still sitting on MRB’s back with the camel clutch. MRB breaks up the clutch with a snapmare and follows up with a dropkick to the back of Lance’s head. As he runs the ropes, MRB does a handspring and hits Lance with a back elbow. Holding his neck after colliding with Lance, MRB hurries to the top turnbuckle and connects with a flying headbutt. MRB covers Lance and hooks a leg, he gets 1-2-No! MRB lifts Lance onto his shoulders and twirls him around with an airplane spin. Cheers from the crowd unexpected turn to vicious boos, MRB drops Lance and looks around in dizzied confusion. BDCD points towards the entranceway, and walking towards the ring is Kevin J Ridge.
JL: What is Kevin J. Ridge doing out here?! AR: Don’t ask questions, just bask in the greatness that is The Ridge! Kevin stands in The Future’s corner with an innocent look in his eyes. MRB and BDCD complain to the Ref and try having Kevin removed from ringside, but to no avail. Distracted by Kevin’s presence, MRB gets lifted up by Lance for an
F5. Lance swings his opponent around, but MRB counters with a DDT. MRB holds
Lance so they’re standing back to back with Lance’s neck on MRB’s shoulder, Lance is lifted up and snapped down into a seated position to complete MRB’s What A Maneuver! With Lance knocked goofy, MRB goes to the corner and climbs to the top. MRB dives off the top turnbuckle and performs a phoenix splash into a leg drop, his Look At That! MRB does a lateral press, the
Ref goes down and counts 1-2-Kevin reaches in and puts Lance’s foot on the rope to break the count!
JL: What was that! AR: What? Lance got his foot on the rope. The man has great ring awareness. JL: Oh please! Kevin put Lance’s foot on the rope and you know it! AR: Hey, I don’t know a damn thing! MRB bolts to the side of the ring and kicks Kevin away. Kevin backs up and puts his hands in the air, acting as though he’s done nothing wrong. BDCD goes on the ring apron and yells for the Ref to kick Kevin out of the arena, instead the Ref goes over to BDCD and tells him to get back down to the floor. With the official’s back turned, Kevin goes on the opposite ring apron and pulls MRB down by the throat across the top rope! BDCD sees this and gets down, he runs towards Kevin. BDCD is able to take two steps before falling flat on his face because a leg tripped him from underneath the ring. Paul finally emerges and flashes a proud grin to Kevin, Kevin reciprocates. Inside the ring, Lance drags
MRB to his feet, shoots him into the ropes, MRB ducks Lance’s elbow and bounces off the opposite ropes, they charge towards each other and are both taken down with simultaneous cross bodies! Paul is busy jaw jacking with fans at ringside and doesn’t notice BDCD climbing on the apron in front of him, when Paul turns around BDCD springboards off the middle rope and soars back with an Asai moonsault. The Ref goes over to check on the carnage. This gives
Kevin the opportunity to go in the ring, lift MRB onto his shoulders, and hit his Ridged Rupture! Kevin slides out of the ring, BDCD chases after his former
Two Duds partner. Kevin turns the corner and halts, when BDCD comes around the corner he gets laid out with an incredible roaring elbow. Now the Ref sees
Lance and MRB both down in the ring, so he goes over to see what happened to
BDCD. JL: This thing has just spilled into complete chaos! AR: As oppose to incomplete chaos? Kevin runs to Paul and helps him up, Lance and MRB are both gaining their senses and getting back to their feet. Kevin and Paul both get in the ring as the Ref has his back turned, Kevin hooks MRB and delivers a fisherman buster.
Lance and Paul pick up an annihilated MRB, The Future perform their powerbomb and neckbreaker combo Future Shock finisher! Kevin and Paul quickly roll out of the ring, the Ref hears the collision and finally turns around. Lance is covering MRB, the Ref goes and counts 1-2-3!
RING ANNOUNCER – The winner of the match, Lance! JL: Dammit! AR: Yes! Ha! Lance did it! The Future are one step closer to not defending the tag belts at Funhouse!
JL: Thanks to Kevin J. Ridge! AR: You’re right Joe, Kevin’s moral support really helped Lance in that match. JL: Moral support my big toe! AR: What? Wait...what? On the outside, Paul charges BDCD and clobbers him in the head with a double axe handle, he gets on top of BDCD and delivers a melee of right hands. Lance stands in the ring with his arms raised and a proud smile on his face, Kevin
Ridge is the only person in the arena applauding his victory. MRB struggles to his feet, sees Lance’s celebration, and ends it with a dropkick to the back of his head. The sudden force sends Lance tumbling through the ropes and down to the floor by the entranceway. Kevin scurries over to Lance and helps him stand. MRB sprints towards the ropes, dives over, and collides with both Lance and Kevin. MRB pulls Lance to his feet and starts hitting him with open-hand shots to the chest, Lance delivers chops of his own. They tie up as they try to pummel each other, and eventually brawl their way up the aisle and back through the curtain. Paul turns and sees them leave, he picks up a weak BDCD and rolls him into the ring. Paul gets into the ring and hits a running forearm smash.
The bell sounds and the second bout is underway. JL: Paul and Big Daddy Cool Dudley has begun. BDCD must win, otherwise The Two
Dudes will not have a shot at the tag title at Funhouse!
AR: Big Daddy Cool Dudley’s not gonna win! You know why? JL: I’m going to regret asking this, but why? AR: Because he a-sucks! Paul motions with his hands encouraging BDCD to rise. BDCD slowly does so, Paul hooks his neck and delivers a diamond cutter. Not giving his opponent a moment to rest, Paul grabs BDCD by the waist, and connects with a devastating German suplex. Paul holds the bridge, he gets a count of 1-2-No! Paul gives the ref a dirty look, Kevin slaps his hand on the mat three times. Paul pulls BDCD to the corner and places him in a seated position on the top turnbuckle. Paul climbs to the second turnbuckle, he hooks BDCD’s head with his arm and his tights with the other hand, preparing for a superplex. He tries to take BDCD over, but they go nowhere because BDCD has his feet hooked around the ropes. Another superplex attempt, and another block. Paul is frustrated and resorts to one of his favorite tactics, he hits BDCD with a low blow. BDCD buckles in pain, and now this time Paul performs a big superplex. He floats over into a cover, the ref counts 1-2-kick out!
JL: Big Daddy Cool Dudley kicked out! He kicked out! AR: Alright, don’t go messing your pants over it. Paul’s not done with Big
Dummy Dudley yet anyway.
Paul points his finger and shouts at the Ref, very angry for not getting a three count. Kevin quietly rolls into the ring behind the Ref’s back. Paul continues his verbal assault on the Ref, stepping forward to cause him to back up. The Ref backs up into Kevin, so Kevin knocks him out with an enzuguiri kick! Paul and Ridge share a devious pair of smiles, Paul goes out to the timekeeper’s table and takes one of The Future’s Tag Team Championship belts. Kevin picks up BDCD, he holds his arms behind his back so Paul can nail him with the gold belt. Paul swings, BDCD wiggles free, and Kevin is accidentally blasted in the forehead with the title belt!
JL: Good God! Paul missed! He hit Kevin with the belt by accident! AR: No! That’s...I...It...No! Paul stands in shock and tries to apologize to Kevin, who holds his head in a daze. BDCD turns Paul around, kicks him in the breadbasket, hooks his arms around his back into a pedigree position, lifts him up and drops him hard with a Tiger bomb. Folding his legs BDCD gets a pin attempt, but the Ref is still coming to his senses on the other side of the ring and can’t make the count.
BDCD lets go of Paul and tries to help the Ref up. Kevin stands with the championship belt in his hand, eagerly waiting for BDCD to turn around. The crowd’s boos for Kevin unexpectedly turn to cheers, because Hazzard is running down the aisle! Hazzard slides into the ring, pulls the belt out of
Kevin’s hand, and knocks him down with a roundhouse kick!
JL: It’s Hazzard! AR: Dammit, can’t we lock the locker room door during matches to keep these people away? Let’s have some order here people! This isn’t Thunderdome!
JL: Hazzard just floored Kevin! AR: I don’t believe that reckless maniac! Kevin’s seriously injured, just look at the bandage on his forehead. He took something like a hundred stitches, sewed in by a Japanese woman doing it for the first time! How dare Hazzard try to re-injure Kevin’s head! Hazzard is a menace!
BDCD turns around to see Hazzard kick Kevin, Kevin rolls out of the ring. Hazzard springboards off the ropes and crushes Kevin with a corkscrew plancha. BDCD jumps with excitement, then turns his attention to Paul. An Irish whip sends Paul hurdling into the corner, BDCD charges but Paul moves out of the way. BDCD puts on the breaks though and stops before colliding with the turnbuckles, he turns and takes Paul down with a bulldog. Turning him onto his stomach, BDCD applies his Reverse Figure Four leglock! The Ref painfully crawls to Paul, asking if he wants to submit. Paul knows if he taps out the tag titles will be on the line at Funhouse, so he endures the agony. BDCD applies more and more pressure as Paul screams in pain, but he can’t get a submission out of him. BDCD releases the hold, he points to the air and climbs the top turnbuckle. Hazzard cheers on from the outside, Donny continues to chew his cud. Paul sluggishly gets back to his feet, with a noticeable limp after being placed in the Reverse Figure Four. BDCD dives off the top rope and executes a dazzling Dragonrana! Paul’s legs are hooked for BDCD to have a pin attempt, the Ref counts 1-2-3!
RING ANNOUNCER – The winner of the match, Big Daddy Cool Dudley! JL: The Two Dudes did it! AR: Sure, with Hazzard’s help. That’s so not right. Somebody call Scout! JL: It’s official, The Two Dudes will challenge The Future for the tag title at Funhouse! AR: But they won’t win! They can’t win! The Future will walk out of
Funhouse as the tag champions. Mark my ever loving words!
JL: What are you ripping off Max now? AR: Why not, he rips off everybody else. MRB comes jogging back out and slides into the ring with Hazzard, the three of them raise each others’ hands in the air to a wild ovation. Paul grabs both of the tag team title belts, he and Kevin stagger backstage to avoid anymore humiliation. As “Don’t You Forget About Me” plays, MRB plays his air keytar, BDCD plays his air drums, Hazzard holds up his E-Championship title belt, Donny continues to chew his cud.
