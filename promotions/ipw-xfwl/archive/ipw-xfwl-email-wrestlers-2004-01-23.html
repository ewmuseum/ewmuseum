Looking for E-Mail Wrestlers

23 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've been considering mixing my "board fed" with e-mail e-wrestlers...I posted about it a while ago, got a few responses... Anyway, I've decided I'm going to give it a try...A few reasons for this: 1) As I mentioned earlier, the way my fed is set right now, it's mostly based around storylines and feuds, and most of that is done outside of roleplaying anyway...That's handlers deciding to feud, or me setting two people up to do it...So nobody should have a problem being involved in shows (as long as they roleplay)...To be honest, even in my fed as it is, we have some people who only roleplay once or twice a week, specifically stuff to further their storylines...And those major roleplays are the ones that make it to our TV show, the all roleplay (so basically a flash packet) series titled "Guerrilla TV"...Anybody who joins the fed by e-mail will have the chance to roleplay something to be included on these shows (so they're getting as much time as anybody else)... 2) Because we're so heavily storyline/feud based, winners and losers of matches are always based around what's best for the storyline...Therefore, whether a person has roleplayed one time or five times, a really long roleplay or a couple of short ones, the outcome of the matches should already be decided and agreed upon... 3) Maybe I'm too ambitious for my own good, but I think my fed could do this and make it work...We'll accept anybody into the fed, and give them the best push possible...To rip off 1010 WINS, well to rip it off and alter it a bit, you give us one roleplay for a show, we'll give you the world... Anyway, I'm going to open it up like this...Anybody out here in e-wrestling land who is an e-mail based wrestler and wants to give this a try, you can e-mail me an appliciation at [detail withheld]... From there, I'll sign you up to the yahoo mailing list...That's where you'll receive "Guerrilla TV" (the flash pack) and our major shows (usually 2 a month)...I'll also obviously send out updates with dates for roleplays and stuff to be sent in by...I've never been a big fan of "strats", but anybody who wants to send them in may do so and they will be used in writing the match...And while you don't have to join our message board to roleplay, you can join to deal with any out of character stuff, including comments on shows and anything else...We have a feedback board just for that (as most e-mail feds seem to)... This is an experiment...If it works out, I'm hoping everybody will get exactly what they should out of an e-fed...a good time...And if it doesn't work out, at least we tried...I can't make any promises, but I can tell you I'm a dedicated fed leader who will give his all and then some to put out the best product possible...All I need is the talent to do it with... Ciao... Joe...
