Impact Xtreme, October 18, 2003 (Part 3)

Oct 25, 2003, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
411 walks backstage, carrying a chair. JL: 411’s heading out here! Good God, we need to take a break! But stay tuned, this isn’t over yet!
COMMERCIAL BREAK... “Move (Get Out the Way)” hits as 411 walks out once more, again with a chair in his hand. He snatches the mic away from the ring announcer.
JL: 411 back out here, as he promised he would be. He wants Everest here tonight and that’s all there is to it!
AR: The Big F’N Deal is making a huge F’N mistake. He better hope Everest doesn’t come out here.
411 - Okay, Everest. You’ve had enough time to make up your damn mind! Get your punk ass out here!
411 crouches down. “After The Eulogy” hits as Everest walks out. The two men stare at each other for a minute, before Everest storms down to the ring.
JL: Good God! Business is... AR: I swear if you finish that line the way I think you are, I’ll have no choice but to hurt you.
JL: Well it’s true, Everest is here and these two aren’t waiting for
Funhouse! AR: At this rate, I’m willing to bet 411 won’t be able to walk in eight days let alone fight.
Everest hits the ring and begins to step over the top rope to enter. As soon as he does, he’s met with a wicked chair shot from 411! Only momentarily stunned, Everest just glares down at 411 with a sly grin on his face. The crowd looks stunned. The Big F’N Deal rushes at Everest and again nails him with an even harder chair shot! Everest’s knees wobble, and he has a stunned look on his face, but he still doesn’t go down!
JL: Everest isn’t human! AR: Chair shots like that would kill most people. But not Everest. They don’t even take him down!
Everest leans up against the ropes. The crowd’s cheering gets gradually higher and higher. This time, 411 takes a running start and levels Everest one more time with a chair shot! This time, Everest goes over the top rope, and to the floor! The crowd goes absolutely nuts, chanting 4-1-1!
JL: Everest went down! But he landed on his feet! What will it take to stop this walking mountain?
AR: I don’t know, but 411 doesn’t have it! Everest leans forward against the ring apron. 411 throws the chair away and goes to the outside. He begins punching away on Everest, talking trash all the while. But as 411 continues to punch away, Everest changes the momentum by kneeing The Big F’N Deal in the gut, stopping the flurry. He then military presses 411 high in the air! The crowd lets out a collective “OH!” as
Everest has 411 at least 10 feet in the air! Everest leans back, and launches
411 high into the steel ring post, shoulder-first! The crowd looks stunned as
Everest retreats up the ramp, staring at 411 the whole way and making the money sign, while 411 holds his shoulder and glares at Everest icily!
JL: 411’s not moving! Oh my god, we...we need help out here! 411’s hasn’t moved! AR: I wonder how much Joey is paying Everest. Whatever it is, he just earned it. 411 lays motionless on the floor as a group of referees and road agents run down to the ringside area and the cameras fade out.
