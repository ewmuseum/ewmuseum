Impact Xtreme, October 18, 2003 (Part 1)

Oct 25, 2003, 12:00:00 AM

http://xfwl.proboards9.com/index.cgi?board=EFEDTV&action=display&num=1066918025 --------------------------------------------------------------- “Move (Get Out the Way)” hits as 411, chair in hand, walks out to a great ovation. JL: Welcome to Impact Xtreme! Joe Loe along with Avery Rogers and we’re just eight days away from Funhouse on October 26th!
AR: War Games baby! JL: No, Funhouse. War Games was a few weeks ago. AR: Oh that’s right. Funhouse baby! JL: That doesn’t have quite the same ring. AR: It really doesn’t. JL: What a way to start this show, as 411 heads to the ring and he looks like a man possessed.
AR: He should be a man on a bus out of town. JL: 411 will take on the eight foot tall, four hundred pound Everest at
Funhouse. AR: It’s not gonna be a fight. It’s gonna be a massacre! JL: It’s going to be the hardest challenge of 411’s career. AR: No, it’s gonna be a massacre. Keep up. JL: Everest has been paid by Joey Gots to take 411 out. AR: For those of you keeping track, that means Everest doesn’t care about winning at Funhouse. He cares about hurting.
JL: Everest wants to end 411’s career. AR: Actually Joey wants to end 411’s career. Everest just wants money. JL: I wonder how much Joey has paid Everest to do this. AR: I don’t know, but whatever it is, it’s worth it. 411 is a menace. JL: I’ll tell you one thing, 411 doesn’t look happy right now. Last time we saw him, he was chokeslammed through the mat by Everest.
AR: That would put you in a bad mood. Plus 411 has that overall angry black guy thing going for him.
411 throws the chair into the ring. He climbs up and steps through the ropes. 411 takes the microphone from the ring announcer. JL: It looks like 411 has something to say. AR: What else is new? 411 - Everest, I know you’re in the building tonight. Bring your eight-foot-tall ass out here, and let’s do the damn thing. Now!
JL: 411’s not waiting for Funhouse! He wants Everest right now! AR: He’s gone insane! It must be the drugs! JL: You mean the painkillers for his back after Everest chokeslammed him through the mat? AR: Uh, yeah...that’s what I mean. Sure. The crowd cheers as 411 crouches down, chair in hand, waiting for Everest. “Who We Be” blasts over the loud speakers. Caramel, along with Hurrikat and
Frenzy, comes walking out onto the stage. The crowd erupts into jeers as the three members of Pride walks down to the ring.
JL: Well 411 asked for Everest, but instead he’s getting Caramel. AR: 411 should consider himself lucky. If Everest came out here, he’d kill 411! JL: We’re set to see Caramel take on 411 here tonight. Max picked 411 to fight Caramel. Last week, Caramel had Yoshihiro Toyama fight Max.
AR: And what a great spectacle that was! Yoshihiro Toyama is my new favorite
Japanese wrestler.
JL: He only beat Max because of Caramel’s interference. AR: He’s also my new favorite thing named Yoshi, replacing that green animal thing from the Mario Brothers games.
JL: Speaking of big matches at Funhuse... AR: Actually, I was speaking about Mario Brothers. JL: Max will go one on one with Caramel in what’s sure to be an emotional match. AR: It’s a match both men need to win. It’s that simple. Losing is not an option. Caramel enters the ring and asks 411 for the mic. Somewhat annoyed, 411 hands
Caramel the mic.
CARAMEL – Look fool, I think we need to talk. Now I know that Max done made the match for tonight, you and me right here the ring. And I’m sure plenty of people done tuned in just to see that. Who can blame them, after all, Cap’in
Nasty is involved. But let’s think about the bigger picture here. Do we need to fight? Do we have a reason to fight? Other then some white boy tellin us to fight, why is we doin this? See, we need to stop this brother on brother violence. So what do you say pimp, let’s step on out this ring and forget about this whole match.
Caramel smiles at 411 and holds his fist out in the air. 411 smiles back at
Caramel. He kicks Caramel hard in the gut, lifts him up, and spikes him to the mat with a spinechoppa!
JL: Spinechoppa! 411 nailed Caramel with the spinechoppa! AR: See, that’s the type of violence Caramel’s talking about. Hurrikat and Frenzy pull Caramel out of the ring. They hold him up outside of the ring. 411 stands looking down on the three. A referee enters the ring and calls for the bell.
JL: It looks like this match is ready to go, next! COMMERCIAL BREAK... Caramel paces back and forth outside of the ring as the referee yells for him to come back in.
JL: Welcome back fans. Caramel not very eager to get into the ring here. AR: Wow, you spin everything in such a negative way. JL: What do you mean? AR: Caramel’s out there calculating a plan to take down 411. You make it sound like he’s afraid of 411.
JL: He is afraid of 411! AR: Well who isn’t a little afraid of 411? The man crushes roaches with his bare hands. I’ve seen it. I think it’s some sort of ghetto pass time.
Caramel stands up on the ring apron. He slowly places one leg into the ring. 411 comes running over, leaving Caramel to pull his leg back out and jump down to the arena floor. The referee begins counting Caramel out. Lacking the patients to wait ten seconds, 411 exits the ring himself to go after Caramel. A chase begins around the ring as Caramel runs from 411. As the two pass by the other Pride members, Caramel grabs Frenzy and hides behind him. 411 stands laughing as Caramel trembles behind Frenzy’s back. He shoves Frenzy into 411.
Caramel slides back into the ring. 411 stares intensely at Frenzy. He kicks
Frenzy in the gut and tosses him head first into the steel ring steps. 411 slides into the ring. Caramel runs over and begins stomping on 411 as he enters.
JL: Caramel taking advantage of the situation now. AR: That’s how you get ahead. Caramel pulls 411 up. He Irish whips 411 into the ropes. Caramel goes for a clothesline but 411 ducks under. The Big F’N Deal bounces off of the ropes again and comes back towards Caramel with a flying forearm smash! Caramel quickly gets back to his feet, where he is met by a series of hard right fists to the face courtesy of 411. Cap’in Nasty stumbles backwards into the corner.
411 kicks Caramel repeatedly in the mid-section. The Big F’N Deal whips Caramel across the ring. Caramel charges across the ring, somehow stopping himself mere inches before he would have hit the turnbuckle chest first.
Caramel breathes a sigh of relief. He turns around as 411 runs across the ring and jumps up into a Hurricanrana, taking Caramel over to the mat! He holds on, pulling Caramel’s legs up and pinning his shoulder’s down as the referee counts the 1-2-NO!
JL: Caramel kicked out! I thought it was over for sure there! AR: Well, once again, you were wrong. Caramel crawls across the ring. Hurrikat jumps up to the ring apron, leading the referee to turn towards her and demand she get down. Meanwhile, Frenzy slides a steel chair into the ring for Caramel. Cap’in Nasty picks the chair up. He swings the chair to hit 411, but the Big F’N Deal moves out of the way, leaving the chair to hit the top rope and bounce back into Caramel’s cranium! Caramel drops the chair and stumbles backwards, clearly dazed by the self-inflicted chair shot. 411 grabs Caramel, lifts him up, and slams him to the mat with a spinechoppa! The referee turns around to see 411 covering
Caramel. He runs over and counts the 1-2-3!
RING ANNOUNCER – The winner of the match, 411! JL: 411 gets the win! AR: Sure, but he had to use a steel chair. JL: Caramel used the steel chair! AR: Go ahead Joe, just keep spreading your lies. 411 grabs the microphone. He picks up the chair and studies it for a minute, then looks to the entranceway as he speaks.
411 - So, you’re going to play that game Everest? That’s fine. I’ll be back out here later, and you better come out and face me. Don’t make me find you, ‘cause then you will have hell to pay.
“Move (Get Out The Way)” plays as 411 walks out with the chair in his hand. JL: 411 will get his hands on Everest before the night is over! AR: I hope Everest does come out here and rips 411’s head off. JL: Still to come tonight, the Russians will take on White Shadow! But up next it’s Firestrike going one on one with J-Sleazy! Stay tuned!
COMMERCIAL BREAK... Caramel walks backstage. Max stands in the middle of the hallway. Caramel stops, standing face to face with his nemesis.
MAX – I must say Karl, that was a great performance out there. I especially loved the part where you hit yourself with the chair. That was quite a plan, knocking yourself out. Good job.
CARAMEL – Still talkin trash, huh Max? Still actin like this is some sort of game. This ain’t a game to me. In eight days, it may just be another match for you, but for me, it’s the biggest match of my career. The biggest opportunity of my life. And I will do whatever it takes to walk out the winner.
That ain’t a promise. That’s a fact. Caramel begins to walk away. Max stands in his way. The two stare at each other. MAX – You think this match isn’t important to me? You’ve been running around here telling people that I’m a fake. You’ve ruined my career in
Japan with your little stunt out there. You’ve made it your goal to destroy me. And now you need me to lay down for three seconds to make you. That’s why this is important to me Karl. I don’t want to make you. You beat me, you become huge in this business. You lose to me, you stay exactly where you are.
That’s why I’m going to win. To keep you down. To keep you where you belong. Max walks away, purposely bumping shoulders with Caramel as he leaves. JL: Caramel and Max, one on one, in eight days! They both need to win. But only one can walk away the victor.
Avery Rogers and Amanda Clark stand in the ring. Avery has a microphone. JL: Avery Rogers with Amanda Clark now. I can’t believe Amanda is still walking around with that women’s title belt she stole from Peaches. Can’t we get security or somebody to take it back from her?
AVERY ROGERS – Ladies and losers, show some respect for your IPW-XFWL
Women’s Champion, Amanda Clark!
The crowd boos as Amanda holds the title belt proudly in the air. JL: Oh here we go. AVERY ROGERS – Now Amanda, I understand you have some bad news for the fans. Avery holds the mic towards Amanda. AMANDA CLARK – Yes, well as you all know, next Sunday at Funhouse I was scheduled to defend my Women’s Title against Peaches. But last week, as
Peaches was exiting the ring, she slipped and hit her head on my belt, leaving her with a concussion.
JL: How delusional is Amanda?! It’s not her title, and Peaches didn’t slip,
Amanda attacked her from behind with the belt!
AVERY ROGERS – Yeah, these mats are slippery. JL: Give me a break. AMANDA CLARK – I know it disappoints all of my fans, but there simply won’t be a women’s title match at Funhouse.
“Strength Of A Woman” begins playing as Peaches comes walking out onto the stage. Peaches walks down to the ring. She enters and stands across from
Amanda. Avery Rogers, standing between the two women, holds his hand out for
Peaches to keep back from Ms. Clark.
PEACHES – You’re right Amanda, I do have a concussion. But if you think that’s going to stop me from kicking your ass at Funhouse and taking my belt back, you can forget it!
AMANDA CLARK – You think I’m going to fight you if you have a concussion? You must be crazy. I’m not wrestling an injured woman. I don’t want you holding me responsible if you get hurt further.
AVERY ROGERS – Hey, I’ve got a question. Peaches, how did you get that concussion anyway? Was it from hitting your head on the title belt, or did like twelve guys jump you in some bar?
AMANDA CLARK – I think Peaches is used to getting jumped by guys in the bar. That is, after all, how she met the father of her child. Amanda and Avery begin laughing. Peaches grabs Amanda by the shirt collar, pulling her so that the two stand face to face. Avery sneaks the microphone in between the two women’s mouths.
AMANDA CLARK – Peaches, if you want to fight me at Funhouse, you better bring a doctor’s note giving you clearance. Otherwise the match is off.
Peaches shoves Amanda down to the mat. She looks over and notices the women’s title belt laying on the canvas. Peaches walks over and picks the belt up, retrieving what belongs to her. She raises the belt in the air for the crowd to see. They cheer her own, but quickly those cheers disappear as Avery Rogers grabs the belt from Peaches. Avery shakes his head in disapproval as he helps
Amanda up and hands her the belt. Peaches sneaks up behind Avery and lands a low blow! Avery drops to his knees in pain. Peaches exits the ring.
JL: Yes! Peaches just became my new favorite wrestler! Peaches stands at the top of the stage, looking back at Amanda Clark. She signals to Amanda that the belt is hers, which it is.
Rex stands backstage with Hades McFan. HADES MCFAN – Rex, in eight days you take on the Monster Roadkill in a No
Limits Match. Any thoughts?
Rex turns to Hades and laughs. REX – Thoughts? Do I have any thoughts? I have a lot of thoughts Hades. Two months worth of thoughts. That’s all I did while I was in that coma, think.
And now that I’m awake, I will get revenge on Roadkill. Because you see,
I’m all about two things...
Rex is about to flex. He turns to Hades and motions with his eyes to say the phrase. HADES MCFAN – Pimping and pumping. Rex flexes his muscles. REX – But I’m also about kicking some major monster ass! Now if you dare please... A technician runs over to Rex. TECHNICIAN – Rex! Rex! REX – What’s wrong? TECHNICIAN – Somebody was attacked in the parking lot! REX – And you came to get me? Why? TECHNICIAN – That’s what the letter next to the body said. Rex thinks for a moment. He takes off running in the direction the Technician came in. Firestrike stands in the ring. “In Da Club (Instrumental)” plays as J-Sleazy walks out onto the stage. JL: Firestrike taking on J-Sleazy here. AR: Duh. JL: Firestrike made his debut last week, losing to Frenzy thanks, in no small part, to J-Sleazy.
AR: Sleazy was just bringing this Firestrike guy back to reality. He thinks he’s so great, well guess what, he lost his first match.
JL: That may have been J-Sleazy’s reason, but now he’ll surely pay for it. AR: Actually I think he’ll get a win for it. Firestrike has no chance here. Let’s be honest, shall we. JL: Honestly, I think J-Sleazy better watch his step in the ring. AR: Or he might slip and give himself a concussion like Peaches did. She’s so careless sometimes.
JL: Don’t forget, both Firestrike and J-Sleazy, along with Frenzy, will challenge Mark Prohibition for the Junior Heavyweight Title at Funhouse.
AR: How could I forget? It is, after all, such a highly advertised match. The bell sounds as Firestrike and J-Sleazy lock up in the center of the ring. Sleazy Irish whips Firestrike into the ropes, catching him on the way back with an armdrag takedown. Firestrike pops right back up and charges towards J-Sleazy with a clothesline. Sleazy ducks under, jumps up onto Firestrike’s shoulders, twists around, and takes Firestrike over with a headscissors takedown! J-Sleazy runs over to one of the corners and climbs to the top rope, raising his arms in victory as the fans jeer him.
JL: J-Sleazy is so full of himself. AR: You’d be full of yourself too if you were as great as he is. As Sleazy plays up the crowd’s reaction, Firestrike gets to his feet. He runs over and sneaks up underneath J-Sleazy, lifting him off of the ropes, walking to the middle of the ring, and hitting a powerbomb. With Sleazy down,
Firestrike runs into the ropes. He quickly comes back with a sharp elbow drop to the chest. J-Sleazy writhes on the mat. Firestrike picks his opponent up and whips him into the corner. He charges full speed across the ring, looking to slam his shoulder into Sleazy’s gut. Instead, J-Sleazy pulls himself up, landing down on Firestrike’s back. He rolls Firestrike over into a pinfall attempt for the 1-2-NO!
JL: Firestrike kicks out! It was almost over there, and just think, in eight days it could be over just as easily.
AR: Meaning J-Sleazy would win the Junior Heavyweight Title. JL: Yes, the match will be one fall to a finish. AR: Hey, I’m starting to like it already. Sleazy stands up, while Firestrike remains on all fours on the mat. J-Sleazy runs to hit a basement dropkick, but Firestrike pops up just in time to miss the move. Sleazy’s back hits the mat hard, leaving him dazed for a moment.
Firestrike grabs Sleazy’s legs and turns him over into a Boston Crab! J-Sleazy begins squirming around in pain, wanting desperately to get out of the move. He reaches for the ropes, but they are too far away to help him now.
Firestrike tightens the move some, leaving Sleazy in complete agony! Just as it looks like Sleazy is ready to give up, Frenzy runs down to the ring and jumps up on the ring apron.
JL: What is Frenzy doing here?! AR: Would you believe scouting the competition? JL: No, I wouldn’t! AR: Alright then, I won’t say that. Firestrike releases the Boston Crab he has on Sleazy. The rookie Firestrike runs over to Frenzy and knocks him clear off of the ring apron. Frenzy falls hard to the arena floor! Firestrike looks down at Frenzy. He begins smiling.
Firestrike runs into the ropes, coming back and hitting a twisting plancha over the top rope and down onto Frenzy on the floor!
JL: Good God! Firestrike just took Frenzy out! AR: But he also took himself out in the process. JL: He might have. AR: No, he did. Firestrike and Frenzy both lay on the arena floor. Inside of the ring, J-Sleazy rises to his feet. He holds his back in some pain, still feeling the effects of the prolonged Boston Crab. Meanwhile, Firestrike stands up. He slides back into the ring. Firestrike runs over to J-Sleazy, but is met with a boot to the gut.
J-Sleazy runs towards the ropes and springs up to the middle rope, then to the top rope, and then moonsaults down towards Firestrike. But Firestrike manages to catch Sleazy! He takes J-Sleazy and spikes him to the mat with a tombstone piledriver!
JL: J-Sleazy’s finished! AR: But Firestrike isn’t. Firestrike drags J-Sleazy’s limp body to the corner. With Sleazy out cold,
Firestrike climbs to the top rope. He comes down with a shooting star press!
After hitting the move, Firestrike covers Sleazy and scores the 1-2-3! RING ANNOUNCER - The winner of the match, Firestrike! JL: Firestrike gets the win thanks to the Hearts Of Fire shooting star press! AR: Hearts Of Fire? Wasn’t that like a bad Bruce Willis movie? Firestrike stands in the center of the ring, celebrating his win. Frenzy slides into the ring and attacks Firestrike from behind. He begins stomping on
Firestrike. IPW-XFWL Junior Heavyweight Champion Mark Prohibition walks out onto the stage.
JL: It’s Mark Prohibition! AR: We can see that. JL: Here just in time to make the save for Firestrike! AR: You think so, huh? Mark doesn’t run down to the ring, instead standing at the top of the stage, watching as Frenzy continues his beat down. Mark shakes his head in disapproval.
JL: What is Mark doing? AR: Nothing. JL: That’s what I mean! AR: Oh, so it was a rhetorical question? JL: Why isn’t Mark helping Firestrike? AR: Because in eight days, Firestrike could take Mark’s title. Why should he help him? JL: Well...I don’t know. But I don’t like this. AR: I like it a lot. Which bothers me, cause I don’t want to like it. So I guess I don’t like that I like it. You know?
JL: We need to take a break. Stay tuned fans, there’s more to come here tonight. COMMERCIAL BREAK... Impact Xtreme - One Week Ago... Norvilas lifts Tyrant up and tosses him back into the ring. Tyrant pulls himself up some. Blood pours down out of his forehead, staining the canvas.
John walks over and grabs the chair again. He slams the chair hard into Tyrant’s back. John pulls Tyrant up one more time. Tyrant stumbles around some, clearly having a hard time standing. John delivers a chair shot so vicious it would make Mick Foley quiver! Tyrant drops down motionless to the mat! Though the champion is clearly out cold and a bloody mess, John Norvilas isn’t done yet. He takes the chair and opens it up. John takes the chair and closes the bottom half of Tyrant’s right leg in between the steel! With
Tyrant unconscious and unable to stop this assault, John takes his time walking over to the corner. Once there he climbs up to the top rope.
JL: Oh my god, John’s going to break Tyrant’s knee! AR: No! Joe, go do something about this! JL: Why don’t you? AR: Are you kidding? John’s a maniac and could hurt me! JL: He could hurt me too! AR: Yeah, but I’m willing to live with that. As John is about to dive off of the top rope and fracture Tyrant’s leg, Living Dead Girl comes running down to the ring. She quickly slides into the ring and goes over to check on her fallen boyfriend! Living Dead Girl covers
Tyrant. John Norvilas watches as the desperate girlfriend checks on her man. He climbs down off of the top rope and drops to the arena floor. John begins walking to the back, as if he had suddenly woken up from a violent black out.
Living Dead Girl holds Tyrant’s head against her chest, soaking her shirt in his blood. JL: What a vicious assault that was last week by John Norvilas. AR: The man is insane. And you want him to be world champion? JL: Well John wanted Tyrant to take him seriously. AR: So he should have sent him a Hallmark card that said hey, take me seriously. I’m sure they make them. He didn’t need to nearly kill Tyrant.
JL: The World Champion isn’t here tonight, but we have it on good authority that he will be at Funhouse in eight days to take on John Norvilas in Rage In
The Cage.
AR: I just hope John’s ready for that. Because now he’s awoken a sleeping giant. Tyrant’s going in there not as the world champion, but as a man who wants to end his opponent’s career.
JL: What a show it’s going to be on Sunday, October 26th. Not only do we have
Tyrant taking on John, but what about Max and Caramel? What about D Money and
Killmore?
AR: That’s if D Money can’t prove the contract is fake, which it is. And he still has an hour or so to do it here tonight so.
JL: Don’t forget Roadkill taking on Rex in a No Limits Match. AR: Another one where a career will be ended. My bet is Rex isn’t walking out of there in one piece. That’s right, Roadkill will cut him in two. Literally.
We know Roadkill has the hardware to do it. JL: The Junior Heavyweight Title is on the line as Mark Prohibition defends against Firestrike, Frenzy, and J-Sleazy. Plus the Women’s Title will be defended as Peaches takes on Amanda Clark.
AR: If Peaches has a doctor’s note clearing her for the match. JL: The E-Championship will be on the line as Hazzard takes on Bouncer and
Kevin J. Ridge. And The Future will take on the Two Dudes, possibly for the tag title, if the Two Dudes can win one of the two singles matches scheduled for later tonight.
AR: No, there’s no chance of that happening. JL: 411 takes on Joey’s hired gun Everest! AR: That’s if they both make it out of tonight’s show alive. JL: And Spindle will take on Lions in a match where if Lions loses, he will have to remove the mask!
AR: I can’t wait to see who’s under there. I bet he’s ugly. Ugly people wear masks. JL: I’ve known some attractive people who wear masks too. AR: Yeah but those parties you go to don’t count. JL: All of those matches plus more. Funhouse is a show you simply cannot miss! AR: And if all else fails, I’ll be there. That’s worth the price of admission in itself. Rex walks out into the parking lot. He finds a group of people standing in a circle. Rex walks through the circle, finding a young looking man beaten to a bloody pulp on the floor. Rex looks up at the car parked next to the beaten man’s body. He notices the driver’s side window is smashed. Rex walks around to see the front window. His eyes widen as he sees the words “Rex Is
Next” spelled out on the window in blood. Rex scowls as he walks back towards the building.
JL: Good God. Roadkill has sent a clear message to Rex. AR: That’s assuming it was Roadkill behind this attack. JL: Who else could it be? AR: I don’t know. I’m just saying. JL: Coming up next, The Russians take on White Shadow. Stay tuned! COMMERCIAL BREAK...
