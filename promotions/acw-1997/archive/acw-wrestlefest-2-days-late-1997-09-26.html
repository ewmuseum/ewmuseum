(ACW)Wrestlefest 2 days late!

26 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live from the Summit in Houston, this is ACW's Wed, er, Thurs, er, Friday Wrestlefest. Here are the results: 1. Stephen Douglas defeated the Legend with the piledriver at 10:25. After the match, Douglas called out for the SST's mouthpiece, Mike Bono, and hit him with the piledriver. 2. The Destroyer & The Skipper defeated LA RAZA(of the SST) at 15:44 when The Skipper "shipwrecked" LA RAZA member Eddy Menendez. 3. Los Tiburones(of the SST) became the ACW World Tag Team Champions by defeating the Insane Asylum at 18:00 when Hammerhead hit his "Ides of March" maneuver on the Mexican Maniac. 4. In a very vicious barbed wire match, RBI defeated Nazeem by pinfall at 14:09. After the match, Nazeem went into cardiac arrest. He was rushed to Humana Hospital where he was pronounced dead on arrival. At the arena, I told the stunned crowd what had happened & even RBI couldn't hold back crying. However, the matches would continue..... 5. Sniper powerbombed Vandal at 12:31 for the pinfall. 6. Stinky the Weazel, in his first match back, powerbombed Thunderlips(of the Extremists, of the SST) for the win at 11:07. After the match, Moonhawk & Maximum Adrenaline came out & carried Stinky back to the dressing room on their shoulders. 7. MAIN EVENT-DREAM PARTNER TAG MATCH: Maximum Adrenaline(with dream partner Moonhawk) defeated Mufsta(with dream partner the Seneca Street Stalker) at 14:07 via submission. Max Adrenaline had Mufsta in the Whiplash Smile(STF). The crowd's cheers were almost deafening. MA removed his mask to reveal...former ACW TV champion and now former SST wrestler Mark Austin!!!!!! After the match, the crowd still stunned by Austin's change of heart, Austin picked up the mic and declared, "The Icon is dead!" And the crowd went wild after that. Austin walked over to Moonhawk & shook his hand. They both left the ring together. For more ACW action & to be a part of the excitement, go to: http://www.angelfire.com/ks/americas1/index.html [name withheld] ACW Commish
