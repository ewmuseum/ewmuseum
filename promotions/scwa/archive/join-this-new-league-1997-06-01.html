E-Wrestlers, The Supreme Cyber Wrestling Association is starting soon and I hope to get a good response so that I amy get this league going as soon as possible. I hope to make this league one of the better e-wrestlings leagues out, but it can only happen with your help. The SCWA has a mailing list flashing system, and is totally booked. So your flashes determine how good you'll do in the league. Other things that determine your push will also be participation in the league. The wrestlers you create must be original and not just be a cheap rip off of some real wrestler, you are aloud 1 wrestler per e-mail address. The belts availble to win are as follows, brackets contains who it is availble to: SCWA World Title (anyone) SCWA North American Title (North America & area) SCWA European Title (Europe & area, includes Japan) SCWA Cruiserweight Title (weight 240 and less) SCWA World Tag Team Titles (any tag team) A homepage will be up soon. ---- Application ---- When complete send to [detail withheld] Your Real Name: E-Mail Address: Wrestler's Name: Weight: Height: Hometown: Personality: Physical Description: Entrance Music/Effects: Ring Attire: Wrestling Style: (choose from below, mix and match if needed) Brawling Technican Martial Arts Fighter Submission Power Lucha Libre High Flyer Daredevil Finisher Name: Description of Move: Extra Notes:
