[SCWA] Wrestlers Wanted

24 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWF Readers, I run a league called Supreme Cyber Wrestling Association, it is a booked federation with an open flash system (I've probally lost half of you already). We are accepting wrestlers, who can roleplay, or can create great heat. We are also looking for a vice-president, for writing a card a week. You can go to the SCWA homepage to fill out an application or fill out the one below. We aren't the best, far from it, but I really hope to become a great league with your help. SCWA Homepage: http://www.geocities.com/TimesSquare/Alley/1320/ SCWA Application - 1 Wrestler per E-mail Address - Original Wrestlers Only - Mail to [detail withheld] Your Name: Your E-mail Address: Wrestler's Name: Weight: Height: Hometown: Personality: Physical Description: Ring Attire: Entrance Music/Effects: Wrestling Style: (mix and match, with a maximum of 3 different styles, i.e. Brawling/Submission/Technical) Brawling Technican Martial Arts Fighter Submission Power Lucha Libra High Flyer Daredevil Finisher Name: Description of Finisher: Extra Notes on Wrestler: (can include some moves he does) If Your Wrestler has a Manager Fill this Out Manager's Name: Physical Description of Manager: Personality of Manager: Male or Female: Interfere? If so, with what?
