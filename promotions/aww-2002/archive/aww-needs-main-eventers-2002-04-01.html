The AWW Needs Main Eventers!

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First off, let me apologise for clogging up the group with yet another advert for a not very good e-fed, however, I'm starting to get desperate and don't know where else to turn. Due to unforseen circumstances, and a few forseen ones as well, my e-fed has a quite frankly VERY weak roster at the moment. The same fueds are dragging on far longer than I'd like them to because, quite frankly, I don't have the talent pool I need to keep things fresh. Therefore, I'm crying out for a few new members. The AWW is way less serious then most other e-feds in many ways, but doesn't go to far into the ridiculous I'd hope. Please, anyone who is a free agent, or is loking for a new fed to join up to, hop along to http://www.theaww.com and have a look around to see if we appeal to you. Rules, an extensive FAQ, all the joining forms, and an archive of past shows are all available on the site to help make your mind up about us. If you do decide to apply, mention this newsgroup on your joining form to recieve priority in being assesed for membership. Thanks for all your time. Jim http://www.theaww.com -- Some days you just can't get rid of a bomb.
