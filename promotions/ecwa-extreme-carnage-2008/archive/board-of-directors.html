The role of the Board of Directors is to ensure the everyday operations of Extreme Carnage
Wrestling Alliance, to assist in maintaining the talent roster, and to oversee the Championships of this federation. Of course, each member of this board has specific duties that they are responsible for, different areas that they have been charged with maintaining on a regular basis.
ECWA Acting Governor of the Board Karnage As Acting Governor in the absence of [name withheld], Karnage oversees majority interest of the ECWA. His responsibilities are wide and varied, and deal mostly with operations within the ECWA. Karnage holds a "Veto" vote on the Board of Directors. Karnage is a very "hands-on" interactive and approachable Governor, which compliments the ECWA, and other staff whom share the same general vision for the future of the ECWA.
ECWA Pristine Chairman Rick Storm As Pristine Chairman, Rick Storm controls interest of the ECWA Pristine and Extreme
Diva Championships, and as such, all inquiries as to the ECWA Pristine Championship,
ECWA Extreme Diva Championship, including contenderships, should be made through him. Rick is also instumental in assisting with Revolution and Pay Per View booking duties. Rick shall monitor Pristine, looking for discrepancies to our governing rules and regulations, and upon finding them, shall alert the
ECWA Board of Practices and Principles. Rick will be responsible for all disciplinary action against Pristine talents. As well, any and all inquiries, complaints, or questions in relation to violations or administration of those governing principles, as per Pristine, shall be presented to Rick Storm. His contact information is available via the ECWA Forums.
ECWA Defiance Chairman Trace Michaels As Defiance Chairman, Trace Michaels controls interest of the ECWA Defiance Championship, and as such, all inquiries as to the ECWA Defiance Championship, including contenderships, should be made through him. Trace is also instumental in assisting with Revolution and Pay Per View booking duties. Trace shall monitor Defiance, looking for discrepancies to our governing rules and regulations, and upon finding them, shall alert the ECWA Board of Practices and Principles. Trace will be responsible for all disciplinary action against Defiance talents.
As well, any and all inquiries, complaints, or questions in relation to violations or administration of those governing principles, as per Defiance, shall be presented to Trace Michaels. His contact information is available via the
ECWA Forums.
