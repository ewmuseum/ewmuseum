MLW Preview Show

20 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<Scene opens to a black screen... then the image of a Canadian flag zooms from the blackness till it blankets the screen. The letters, "MLW", burst forth embossed on the national emblem as "Blow At High Doe" by the Tragically Hip plays in the background. The music and the image fade into a "newsroom". Two men are seated behind a desk while behind them, a large monitor with "MLW" lettered in blue is shown. The man on the left appears to be in his late 20's with long semi-curly brown hair, he's wearing a plain white sleeveless t-shirt and is well built with muscle definition. The man beside him, on the right, looks also to be in his late 20's with blonde 'buzzed' hair and wearing a grey sweatshirt with "MLW" printed across it in red, and also appears to be well built. The man on the right speaks...> Man: Welcome Canadian wrestling fans to what is the most talked about topic across our Country... the re-opening of Maple Leaf Wrestling! That's right, MLW is once again opening up operation! We'll tell you all about it here tonight plus other interesting items of discussion. But that can wait... I'm Matt Edwards and this is my co-host, the first ever UEW Ultimate Champion, Eric Smart! Eric Smart: Good evening fans. Matt Edwards: While we give you this pre-opening show, you can look for us weekly on Tuesday Night Impact! where we will give you 'live' coverage of all the wrestling action! ES: Very good, I'm surprised you got all that out in one breath! <Eric smirks> ME: You are too funny for words... if your words were 'actually' funny. MOVING along... The first TNI! show will be brought to you live from the Trudeau Arena, in Ottawa, Ontario! All people in the Ottawa/Hull area are invited to join us at our premiere card. ES: And for those who can't make it... we'll be broadcasting live on TSN and BBS along with numerous independent stations. It should be a show to see, if nothing else, for my commentary. ME: A little conceited are we? ES: ME? No, I just know that I'll be the one who'll tell it like it is and not beat around the bush. ME: I see... Fans, up next we have a taped interview that our own, Shawn Douglas, conducted last week with MLW president and owner, "The Titan" Peter Bell. Let's roll the footage... <Matt says something to Eric and Eric laughs a bit, just before the screen jumps to the inside of a house. Seated are two men... one in a plush chair, the other on a wooden stool. The one on the chair looks to be in his early 30's and VERY well built, he has short dark brown hair and is wearing a black muscle shirt with jeans. On the screen the words, "Peter Bell: MLW President" appear underneath his image. The man on the stool looks to be in his late 20's and has long, curly, red hair and is wearing dresspants, and a blue blazer with a white turtleneck sweater beneath the blazer, he also appears to be a little out of shape. Underneath his image the words "Shawn Douglas" are shown before it fades out...> Shawn Duoglas: With me is the esteemed owner of MLW, "The Titan" Peter Bell. As always, Peter, it's a pleasure! Peter Bell: Thank you. SD: Now to the matter at hand. MLW closed down last December in a somewhat abrubt fashion... now we all now this, but what I'm getting at is WHY did it close? Could you explain to our viewers and the ones who followed Maple Leaf Wrestling why this was? PB: I'd be glad to. First off, we had numerous support when we first launched and the sky looked like it had absolutely no limit what-so-ever... but later we started having wrestlers "no-showing" scheduled events, and numerous other problems... that I'd rather not get into right now. SD: Of course we do realize that some personal reasons played an effect in this... PB: <cutting off Shawn> Like I said, I'd rather not get into that right now. What IS important, is that Maple Leaf Wrestling is coming back. I understand this interview will air sometime next week? SD: That is correct... PB: Well, by the time the viewers see this, yes, MLW will be officialy back in business, so to speak. SD: Now what about the re-opening, will we expect the same MLW as before? Will the viewers still flock to TNI! and our shows? Simply put, will MLW in any way shape or form, be up to standards as the first incarnation? PB: Will it be the same as before... Yes and no. Yes we will still try to put out quality... but it will be different, as I expect alot of new talent shall emerge within the ranks, thus adding a 'newer' element. As to our fans tuning in week in and week out to our shows, that's hard to say. I hope to have or recapture that fan base, most definitely! I'd even go as far to say that I'd want to INCREASE the fanbase. SD: I believe that answers my question quite well. Now what about titles? What about the PPV's?... stuff that the marks... err, 'fans' would be interested in.... PB: <Peter gives Shawn a cold look for his comment about 'marks'... but then goes back to speaking, Shawn sweating nervously> Titles... we will still feature the titles we had before, for instance, The Northern Heavy-Weight. Our scheduled Pay Per Views run the gambit of 6 per year, all a little different than the rest. That's about all I can say at the moment... SD: Any chance of any future titles being forme other than the previously mentioned Northern one and the Provincial and Northern Tag? PB: Well, as you know, we still have the Iron Man belt... but that won't be decided anytime soon. As for any new belts... none are in the works at this moment. It's a touch and go situation. If I feel the demand for another... simply put, I will put out another. SD: I guess that answers about all of my questions... except for one! How and when are the titles going to be decided?... PB: That information will be released upon the time that is appropriate. SD: Thank you, Mr. Bell... and thank you for allowing this interview... PB: You're most welcome. <Camera goes to black and then re-opens with Eric Smart and Matt Edwards, seated at the control desk.> Eric Smart: Interesting, "The Titan" isn't releasing any information about how the belts are to be decided?... Matt Edwards: Apparantly not. Though there was some other points made in the interview... new talent emerging, trying to increase the fan base... ES: Well, like I mentioned earlier... we will be broadcasting TNI! live on TSN and the BBS stations simultaneously... so that's got to increase viewership a fair chunk. ME: I think it will have a positive affect as well, no doubt about it. Heh! ES: What? ME: Among new talent emerging... that would also have to include you. After all, this is your first stint behind the mic. Why leave wrestling for commentary?... ES: First off, I HAVEN'T ruled out competing in the squared circle. Let's just say this is a break from the beating one can take in the ring. I've served my dues more than enough... I took this job 'cuz it gives me a chance to weigh my options. After UEW and the scandal... the return, the WOW organization... I needed a break. Some may say my career has been 'inconsistent'... let them. They don't know deep down what I've been through and probably never will. Besides, Peter contacted me about working here, he said he's was re-opening MLW and I thought... what the heck! It's a steady paycheck and keeps me and my family from starving. ME: Well, I must say you're a sight better than that jack-ass, Pierre Charest. I had to put up with his attitude for FAR too long. ES: From what I hear, he maybe entering competition in Europe. But that's an altogether separate discussion than the one we are discussing tonight. ME: I agree. Up next after this commercial break, the rundown of some of the wrestlers that have indeed signed with MLW... <Camera cuts to various commercials such as the Montreal NIKE goalie in the cab, Jet Moto for the Sony Playstation, Burger King, and a host of others totalling 3 minutes time. Screen cuts back to Eric and Matt at 'the desk'.> Matt Edwards: Welcome back, fans. Some of the wrestlers you can expect to see in the MLW are, "No Worries" Rob Magnum, Lazor Lemon, "Yukon" Jack Roberts, Rick Wright, "The Real Deal" Steve Studd, the Killing Machines, Dan Watterson, Eric Smart: Now let's back up a little bit, to the beginning... to "No Worries" Rob Magnum. ME: Magnum is a fierce competitor who will now doubt make a strong mark in this organization. He has the size... as he stands at an even seven feet and weighs in at 357 pounds! ES: But his size and power isn't his only attributes... he's got a killer instict and determination! I wouldn't be surprised to see him do quite well, here in Maple Leaf Wrestling. ME: You forgot to mention one thing though. ES: What's that?... ME: His elbow... ES: Yes, I can see that that MAY have an affect on his performance... but not likely. He is fully recovered from it, or that's what he told me anyways. I look to see a great future for him despite his previous injury. I mean he's a street fightin' machine... utilizing brawling and power moves. ES: Yeah, well where's that leave the technical aspect of his abilities? ME: Sure he's not the greatest technical wrestler out there, but sometimes there's not much "technical" aspect to the matches. It all depends. Me personally, as a "technical" wrestler, I find technicians a dying breed... their ain't too many of us left out there. But anyways... ME: Now what about Lazor Lemon? What's the deal with this man? No offense, but he seems to me to be a blatant rip-off!... ES: WOAH! Hold up there! I disgress, I personally know this man, and he is not the one who copied that well known gimmick. People can criticize him all they want, but the fact is, him and you-know-who traveled together in the indie circuit and you-know-who JUST happened to copy the gimmick off Lazor! ME: I find that hard to believe... ES: You'd have a hard time believing ANY truth. But don't take my word for it, take his. I'll also point out that he's a tough competitor as well... he's captured the ACEW Metropolitan title twice and he let's his skills speak for themselves. Plainly put, Lazor is NO pushover and he is NO rip-off... you'll see for yourself once he's in action. I'd also like to point out that while, you-know-who isn't actually Latino... Lazor is 100% Latino! ME: I'll agree with you there. Well, no matter... I am full aware of his skills, and despite what I believe about him, he is still a fierce wrestler... ES: Now who do we critic next? Let me see... "Yukon" Jack Roberts? ME: One of the "Originals" returning here to Maple Leaf Wrestling! A true fan favourite if I ever did see one! His charity work, his love for the kids and fans, this guy is a class act. ES: I must admit, I was too busy in UEW and WOW at the time, to really follow much of the original MLW... but doing 'charity' work has nothing to do with one's skills! ME: Well, for you and our new fans, I'll explain him a little better. Like his name implies, he's from the Yukon. Being a former lumber jack, this guy is as strong as they come! He stands at 6 foot 6 and he weighs in at 294 pounds!!! Trust me, once you see him in action, you'll never forget him! Yes, he has been described as a teddy bear in the body of a grizzly... but when it comes to straight out and out wrestling, he's all business. Trust me, he can be technical... kind of, but he's mainly a brawler... and with his strength! Wow! ES: Not the type of guy you want to get on your bad side... ME: Very much not! Though I do think he is an awesome force, he is inexperienced for the most part... MLW and AAPW being the only federations he's competed in. Though one thing about him that has my respect... he hates interference, prefering that wrestlers get their wins, cleanly and without "gang style" attacks. ES: One of the few who actually does then... fan favourite in the truest sense of the word. I congratulate him for sticking up to his ideals. ME: We all should. One last comment about Roberts... his finishing move was one of the deadliest MLW had ever seen! Nobody and I mean NObody has gotten up from his "Yukon Slam"... A running reverse power slam!!! ES: With the weight and power behind it... yeah, I can believe it. ME: Speaking about tough wrestlers... Our next wrestler is also one of the "Originals"... Rick Wright. He's also another home grown talent, coming from Edmonton, Alberta. ES: Weighing in at 270 pounds and having a height of 6 foot 4, he went undefeated in the first incarnation of Maple Leaf Wrestling. <Eric reads off a sheet.> ME: Heh heh... decided to use the papers, eh? ES: Yeah, yeah, laugh it up! I was too busy elsewhere making my comeback to pay any attention to what went on up here in Canada. SUE ME!!! ME: I understand where you're coming from... relax. Back to Wright! He's a pretty good technical brawler... and has a pretty devastating powerbomb for a match ender! It will be good to see him in action again... despite his attitude... ES: Despite his attitude? ME: Cold, cocky, son-of-a-gun! But I won't take anything away from his talent. I look to see good things from him as well. ES: Our next wrestler featured is "The Real Deal" Steve Studd. Now Studd as I know it, is in the NPWA... pretty good for a rookie. So it'll be interesting to see how he fares here in Maple Leaf Wrestling. ME: From Austin, Texas and standing at 6 foot 5 inches, he weighs 249 pounds. He's another technical-brawler but has quite an array of real solid technical skills that he uses. ES: Well, he's only 21 years old... so right there the rookie aspect comes into BIG play! It'll be interesting to see how he actually stands up to the more experienced wrestlers inhabiting MLW. Not that he isn't talented... it's just he's going to have to really work like mad to prove himself. My opinion on him anyways. ME: And a perfectly sound one. He's had a fairly well start in the NPWA, and that's a class act federation! ES: <Eric shoots Matt a look> AHEM! I don't think we should be plugging other federations... not that I don't mind the aforementioned organization... I just think "The Titan" might have a different outlook on the matter... ME: True enough. So the big question for Steve Studd is... will he BE, "The REAL Deal" here in MLW? ES: Up next we have, Overkill and Demolisher, The Killing Machines! ME: Former NICW World Tag Team Champions looking for a new home to terrorize. Seriously, these guys are all that and more! They definitely do live up to their names... Killing Machines. ES: They weigh in together at a total of 545 pounds of pure hatred and adrenaline! I caught these guys once at a NICW house show, man, I tell ya' Matt, these guys are brutal, sadistic, and just plain devastating! ME: And their finisher, "The Killing Blow"... was one of the most feared moves IN the NICW. These guys are definitely going to make a resounding impact in the tag scene here in Maple Leaf Wrestling! ES: Can you say, THUD?! <Matt laughs in the background>, Next, we have Dan Watterson from the IWF/WOW! ME: Watterson's an all around wrestler hailing from Philadelphia, Pennsylvannia. Weighing in at 255 pounds and a height of 6 foot 2, he brings with him a vast repertoire of moves... very interesting. ES: Well, it'll be interesting alright! It'll be interesting to see how he adapts to wrestling up here in Canada. He's had a fair amount of success in other organizations... but is he up to the 'regional' style of wrestling? ME: That does remain to be seen... However, we do have a new tag team making their professional debut here! ES: Yes, Mark Knight and Sean Judge... Judgment Night as they are collectively known by. Now, I had a chance to catch their tryouts and, Matt, these guys maybe rookies but they are one hell of a tag unit! Sean and Mark's wrestling abilities tend to clash... but when you pair these boys up... they can kick! ME: I understand that Mark is quite the technician... ES: Yeah, the kid's got more knowledge of moves, holds, and counter moves than any rookie I've ever seen. Simply put, watch out for this kid a few years down the road... when he gains more experience. Then he'll be showing all the rest of the professional wrestlers out there how to actually 'wrestle'. Sean, on the other hand, is a plain out and out brawler... with a little technical knowledge... not quite as adept as Mark per se, but his street fighting abilities are quite formidable. I think Jugment Night will be the best new tag team of '97, and quite possibly, given a few years time, one of the greatest. Of course this is just my theory... wether or not they can make a mark here in MLW remains yet to be seen. ME: Some very positive words from you, Eric, concerning this duo. ES: Believe you me, Matt, I tell it like it is... and these boys show a LOT of promise... ME: Well, I'm sure the fans of Maple Leaf Wrestling will be keeping a watchful eye on, Judgment Night. After these messages we will be back and we'll give you a run down of next week's Impact! <Camera fades out and cuts to a commercial break. TSN's summer line-up of Blue Jays and Expos baseball games are shown, among other ads... camera re-opens to Eric Smart and Matt Edwards...> Matt Edwards: Welcome back, and up right now, is the scheduled card for next week's Tuesday Night Impact! Eric Smart: Featured matches are... the debut of the Killing Machines, as they take on the Coastal Connection, Jimmy Vey and Luc Charest. ME: Luc Charest, I understand it, is the younger brother of, Pierre Charest. It should be interesting to see how he compares... in skill AND attitude, to his older brother. ES: Also on the line up, "No Worries" Rob Magnum debuts as he takes on Bobby Sherlock! Lazor Lemon debuts and he faces "The Polarizer" Mark Crenshaw! ME: Other scheduled matches include "Yukon" Jack Roberts debuting against "Fabulous" Antonio Ramiro, "The Real Deal" Steve Studd debuting as he goes up against Jacob "Fighting Irish" O'Reilly... ES: You will also witness Judgment Night's debut, as they take on The Gordy Brothers, Jim and Todd. ME: And in our Main Event... Rick Wright squares off against Dan Watterson, in what is sure to be an exciting match up! Catch all the action, next Tuesday on... Tuesday Night Impact! Check your TV Guide for local listings... For Eric Smart, I'm Matt Edwards... saying so long and good night! <Camera pans out from the desk and shows part of the studio... the credits roll by as "Blow At High Doe" by the Hip takes the MLW Preview Show off the air...> MLW PRESIDENT AND HEAD BOOKER: "The Titan" Peter Bell -- [name withheld]
