Handlers Wanted! (Rules, second posting)

17 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MLW RULES AND INFO [VERSION 1.1] ================================ Hey there! For those of you who don't know me, my name's [name withheld]. I'm an aspiring author of fiction and 22 years old with over 2 years experience on the net, e-wrestling. Some of you may remember the name MLW, as it was a fed that I ran last Oct. to Dec. of '96. Well, MLW is BACK! This is the new and improved MLW, so to speak, and I've learned some very valuable lessons about running a fed. How to start it, how to make it work, etc., etc. I think what prompted me most to re-establishing MLW is the lack of GOOD "quality" feds around (Though there is quite a few good feds out there... some of which I am a part of, most notably, IWF/WOW, IEWF, and the NPWA). Another factor that decided my re-opening, is the sad and recent demise of a great fed, the NICW, which I was in since its inception of Dec. 95. And so I hope in some small way, to carry on the legacy of NICW, with MLW. My friends... Razi and Jawaad, if you read this, you are sadly missed from the "booker" business... e-wrestling lost two of the brightest fed-heads, but perhaps one day a return will be made... here's hoping! :) Below you will find various questions and my answers to each of them, pertaining to Maple Leaf Wrestling... and so, without further ado! [NOTE: After reading the rules/info section and you decide to join... fill out the form and send it to, < [detail withheld] > ] "Well! If you are so knowledgable, how come the MLW isn't around today?!" ************************************************************************* Quite simply, I ran out of time. I had other things on the go and was quite busy, this lead me to the only choice available (other than reducing the quality of the fed, which I didn't want to do, since quality is a high priority to me.) and that was to shut the fed down rather than see it lag on and die a slow death. "So, what makes you so sure your fed will stay around now?!" ************************************************************ Call it a hunch... call it whatever you like, but I'm determined to make it work! I have time one my hands nowadays, and being a big fan of e-wrestling, I want to give it another go! 'Cept this time I want MLW to stay! I've had a few people comment that it was a good fed and if it stayed around till today, who knows where it could be now! Well, I'm not going to dwell on the past... Instead I'm looking towards the future and making the best fed that I can. The new motto of MLW... "We're back and THIS time we're here to STAY!" And one that I believe will ring very true. I don't plan on this fed disappearing after day one or a month down the road... trust me, I've already gone through the burn-out thing last October (MLW's second month, and overcame it to go into the third month). But enough about the past... suffice to say, this fed is stickin' around for a long, long time! "What does MLW stand for anyways? And is MLW booked or simmed?" *************************************************************** MLW stands for Maple Leaf Wrestling. It is a regional federation solely based in Canada with all cards and PPV's being held there, except for one PPV which takes place in one country other than Canada (which I'll explain later). As far as what type of league, each person has their own personal preferance, mine happens to be booked as I believe this is the best way that a fed head is able to deliver angles and excitement to a league and its handlers. "So how do you determine matches???" ************************************ We determine matches via. roleplays (flashes, in ring interviews) and participation. The better the roleplay the better chance of getting a win. NOTE: Sometimes to advance an angle we might not always adhere to this, but this in VERY special circumstances and won't happen hardly at all. Also, strats or angle suggestions can also help give your wrestler that extra bit of 'push'. (BIG NOTE: This fed is for serious players only, it is NWA style, and serious, not that we won't have our comedic moments. It's just I don't want to see the fed turn in to some outlandish or cartoonish league. MLW is my take on what wrestling "SHOULD BE!" and that is the glory days of the NWA, mixed with a 90's style of flare (No pun intended!:). "Do you 'favour' Canadian wrestlers?" ************************************* No, even though I'm Canadian... I won't be THAT biased! :) (NOTE: I do prefer Canadian wrestlers entering the league as it is Canadian after all, but I won't tell you you HAVE to, it is soley up to you. I'll take wrestlers from all over the globe... Japan, Mexico, Europe, etc. as a wise man once said... "Diversity is a GOOD thing!") (NOTE: ONLY ORIGINAL wrestlers are permitted to join!!!) "So if I roleplay alot, say, 15-20 flashes a day! I WIN?!" ********************************************************** Sadly no. Quantity means zilch. Quality means everything. I'd rather see one good roleplay a week than 15-20 "I will crush you cuz I said so" flashes. Take your time, write up a good flash. It'll enhance your character and let him shine and it'll also help him advance in the rankings and be recognized by other players. And besides, if you ain't into flashing/interviewing, this fed is definitely not for you! (NOTE: A sample flash will be required from your wrestler. It can be any fed, just as long as it pertains to your said wrestler. This is primarily to weed out lame rp'ers, more on this later.) "So how many times can I flash per week then?" ********************************************** The set limit is 3. We will usually air flashes/interviews during cards and in packets. Packets will be put out once or twice a week. The minimum requirement is one role play a week, but we strongly encourage at least 2 a week. This gives players' wrestlers a chance to further angles/develop heat/etc. If we find you aren't rp'ing, you will summarily kicked out. Nothing worse than dead weight in a fed. BUT, if you explain to me that you are on leave for some reason or another or going away somewhere, and let us know... then your butts are safe! :) "What about gimmick wrestlers? Can I make a 10 foot GIANT that weighs..." ************************************************************************* Any person who even thinks of doing this is out of their minds! But just so you know... MLW is patterned after the old NWA organization, where the wrestlers had little or no gimmick. Basically, it's primarily the person's personality etc., that is showcased along with the wrestling. This means ABSOLUTELY, WITHOUT A DOUBT, NO FRIGGIN UNDERFAKER CLONES OR OTHER SUCH GIMMICKRY! MLW is above all that. This also includes "Stunning Stone Cold" Austin rip-offs as well! So don't even bother joining if that's your intention. As for limits and heights... yes, there is limits. I ask that the height and weight be kept at reasonable and realistic rate, as this a fed of a more realistic nature. Height, the minimum is well, nowhere below 5'5" (This ain't midget wrasslin'!) and no more than 6' 8" or 9". I may allow one BIG man in though... Much like Andre the Giant during the 70's and 80's. Fact is, there isn't that many monstrously huge "real" wrestlers around! And so, if you really have an excellent idea for a BIG man... send in an app and I'll be the judge of it, but the limit is 6' 11" to 7' 0". I've seen far too many feds with TOO many wrestlers with pituitary gland problems! Height, no more than 500 pounds please... if your wrestler is obese, it'll show, and no less than 180. "Now what about T.V. shows? Pay Per Views?" ******************************************* Maple Leaf Wrestling will start off with one weekly card show, called "Tuesday Night Impact!". This will feature up to eight matches per card, all play by play, including a main event. Scheduled announcers are Matt Edwards (face), and Eric Smart (neutral/heel tendencies but a call them as he sees them type guy). "MLW Wrestle Zone" A control center type show recapping the week's events and containing news/rumours/interviews and previews of the upcoming Impact! card. This also will air weekly, and is hosted by Shawn Douglas (face). PPV's. This is where I believe wrestling has strongly lost it's edge... 12 a year, each from the big 2 is just WAY too much. And so, I'll harken back to the early 90's mixed with a little present 90's and only do six a year. Below, rating from the beginning of the year to the end, are the list of MLW PPV's and their descriptions. "The Yukon Eric Memorial Cup" (Feb.): The Yukon Eric Memorial Cup (here after reffered to as the YEMC) has the unique distinction of where one's wrestler is pushed to the ultimate limits of his endurance. The YMEC is a 16 man tourney, with all matches being showcased this night. The winner recieves the third singles belt available in MLW, "The Iron Man" Championship. This belt has the rather unusual characteristic that it can't be put up for grabs, for the victorious champion recieves the belt and is crowned the Iron Man champ and is named so for one year up until the next YEMC, when the champ must relinquish the title so it may be put up for grabs again. NOTE: Only the top 16 athletes will be allowed to compete in this tourney. But if the Iron Man champ isn't in the top 16 standings the next year, he may still compete... for he has proven himself capable and worthy of a spot in the tourney. This tourney is also named after one of the all time great Canadian wrestlers, Yukon Eric. This man's strength and endurance were well renknowned... he even had his ear removed in a match against Killer Kowalski, and yet he still fought on! And if my father (a 50's/60's wrestling fan) is correct, he went on to win the match without the ear!!! Tag belts will be defended in a match separate from the tourney. NOTE: The singles champions at the time, are automatically entered in the tourney. Wether they wish to put their titles on the line is solely up to them. This is the only PPV where it's not mandatory for singles champions to defend their said belt. Though if they don't, those titles will have to be defended either prior to or after the YEMC, in the month of Feb., on a broadcast of Tuesday Night Impact! "Battle of the Titans" (April): This is the PPV that sets the stage for things to come in the grandaddy of all MLW PPV's, The Great Canadian Bash. Basically it's an "every 2 minutes" Royal Rumble type deal, but with a twist. The last two remaining competitors will square off later in the night in a No-Holds-Barred event. All managers, etc. are banned from ringside during this final match of the PPV. The winner of the NHB match goes on to face the champion (whomever it may be at the time) at the Bash. Other matches include... The last 10 men to be ousted in the Rumble other than the NHB participators compete in a Battle Royale, the winner recieving a shot at the Provincial Championhsip on the next Tuesday Night Impact! Also, ALL titles on the line! "The Great Canadian Bash" (July 1st): This is the "Wrestlemania" of the MLW, held on Canada's birthday. All titles on the line plus lots more. "In Your Park" (late August): An 'outdoors' PPV held live each year, from Molson Park in Barrie, Ontario. Nothing 'special' about it by WWF standards (ie. not a gimmick related event) other than the outdoor setting. All titles on the line. "RingWAR!" (Oct.): MLW pushes the boundaries of violence in this "extreme" games festival. The only real time when MLW gets a chance to 'juice'. :) All titles on the line. "Northern Exposure" (Dec.): For this PPV, this is the only time the rest of the world gets to see MLW 'live', so to speak. PPV's could take place in such places as America, England, Japan, Mexico, Germany, etc. You get the idea. All titles on the line. "Okay, I'm starting to get the picture now... but what about belts?!" ********************************************************************* There will be 3 singles belts and one tag team belt to start off with. In singles we have... The Northern Heavyweight belt: To be defended once per month and at all PPV's amongst one of the top 5 competitors. Though the wrestler may wish to defend and is certainly allowed to defend as many times as he chooses. NOTE: The Northern Heavyweight Champion may allow lesser ranked opponents (ie. not in the top five) a match. this can be non title or title on the line but it won't count as a "true" title defence, the true defences are against one of the top five ranked opponents. The Provincial belt: This is the secondary title of the MLW and it must be defended at all PPV's and once a month against one of the top 10 ranked opponents. Same thing applies to the Provincial Championship as the Northern Heavyweight, the champ is allowed to defend it more than once a month, but that his disgression. The Iron Man belt: This is the third title of MLW and cannot change hands during a regular match nor at a PPV. This belt is won via the Yukon Eric Memorial Cup and said champion is allowed to wear the belt until the next YEMC, where he shall return it to Maple Leaf Wrestling to be put up for grabs at the next tourney. The Northern Tag Team belt: The only tag team belt available in the MLW. Must be defended once per month and at all PPV's amongst one of the top 5 tag unit ranked teams. Said champs can also defend more than once per month if they so choose. Now, more belts may be added as the league progresses and grows but since we are a regional promotion... we feel this is good enough of a start. (NOTE: The only two belts I would consider adding are... a Provincial Tag title, but this is VERY unlikely. And another singles belt, similar in profile to that of the T.V. or European title in WCW and WWF, respectively.) "What about pounding the crap out of a guy in the locker room? Can I?" ********************************************************************** If you are referring to a sneak attack. Yes, you may, but only if we feel it will benefit... ie. advance an angle, start a feud, etc. But don't abuse this! And if you do, just remember... what goes around, comes around. So if your wrestler continues to attack or jump opponents on a frequent basis, you'll likely find he himself the victim sooner or later. "What about strategies???" ************************** Not necessary, but we will use them if you wish to send them. Strats do help, don't get me wrong... it's just not all people like them and so this will be optional for now... "What about the rankings and win/loss records?" *********************************************** Each fed has their own particular way of doing this, but I find most feds go on a point basis, actually listing the wrestler's wins/losses/draws. To me anyways, I find this matters little. If you look at the WWF, WCW, or ECW, you don't see them come out with lists of wins and losses... and so, it'll be decided on how much heat your wrestler is recieving. Heat = Interviews, flashes, and angles. If your wrestler is getting good heat and you're roleplaying on a consistent basis... he'll rise in the rankings, enabling him for titleshots etc. And so with that, there are 2 divisions in the rankings... Main Event Wrestlers - Includes the Northern Heavyweight Champion, top contendors for the title, and basically the elite. These are the wrestlers that have gotten where they are by hard work and excellent heat. Mid-Card Wrestlers - The wrestlers who aren't quite Main Eventers but make up the bulk of any federation. The Provincial Champion is included here, though he is at the top of the Mid-Card status,... and may very well find himself listed with the Main Event'rs. (NOTE: This ranking system I find, is quite fair... if you continue to build heat for your wrestler, he'll rise in the ranks... it's as simple as that. Just because one is listed on the Mid-Card status, doesn't mean he won't rise to Main Event position... the same holds true to Main Event'rs, one day a wrestler could be 'hot' and the next he could cool down and become a Mid-Carder. It all depends on participation and activeness on your behalf. Also, ranking numbers will be applied to Main Event and Mid-Card wrestlers starting from 1. to whatever (1, being the #1 contendor for the Northern Heavyweight Championship until the list goes down) with a break in the numbers denoting the beginning of the Mid-Carders. The same rule applies to tag teams.) "WHERE DO I SIGN UP? LET ME IN ON THIS GREAT FED!!!" **************************************************** Well, we won't refer to ourselves nor MLW as great. Not yet anyways... not until people heep all kinds of praise on us... only then will we call ourselves great!:) But we do want our handlers to have fun and enjoy themselves. So suggest angles you'd like to see with your wrestler(s), do in ring or behind the scenes interviews... most importantly, BE CREATIVE and yet, be realistic, please. Doing all these things adds to your character and gives "push" power to your wrestler. Meaning, the more you become active in MLW (flashes/interviews/angle ideas/or just show plain interest in MLW) the greater chance of your wrestler rising to the top of Maple Leaf Wrestling, the premier Canadian federation around today. Oh, and a creation sheet is listed below... please fill out accordingly. For those wishing tag teams, fill out an individual for each of them and fill out the tag form that goes with it. (Sidenote: Those with tag teams don't have to send in a sample roleplay in the singles division... just the tag.) And Just so you know... I'm putting a limit on how many wrestlers may be entered. This is a regional fed, albeit on a grander scale since it covers all of Canada, but I still want to keep it fairly small... so therefor you will have a choice of either one singles competitor or a tag-team. Plus, this will add greater depth to gameplay as people won't be running around with their own stables and will instead have to form a stable with other handler's wrestlers. This makes it that much more enjoyable and realistic. And to those of you who took the time to read this... I thank you. I hope you have a better feel for what Maple Leaf Wrestling is all about, and I hope you will take the time to join, for without the fans (handlers) this sport would be nothing. "MLW. THE WAY WRESTLING SHOULD BE!" - The old motto of Maple Leaf Wrestling. "MLW. WE'RE BACK AND THIS TIME WE'RE HERE TO STAY!" - The NEW motto of MLW.
