Clothesline Commentary, Issue #1: Peter Bell

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CLOTHESLINE COMMENTARY By [name withheld] ISSUE #1 12/21/97 Interviewee: PETER BELL When I first decided to begin the "Clothesline Commentary", I thought I would be doing a rip-off of [name withheld]'s "E-Wrestling Ringpost". I came to think that if he can do it, so can I. It's the same idea, but a different person will be doing. All do respect to Mike, I'd like to be the interview and see what makes these guys tick, so here it is, The Clothesline Commentary. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ My first interviewee is a guy who is known as the handler of a psychopath and an all-around swell guy. A lot of nasty things were said about him, but we all know it to be fallicious. I didn't know him that much at the beginning, but he turned out to be just what everyone said he is. A great guy. I made it my mission to try and know this man better. From what I have seen in his work, Peter Bell is one hell of a handler. Will he be successful with his near-copy of Marylin Manson(with all respect to Peter), better known as DEREK MCMASTERS! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ [[name withheld]:] Why don't we start at the beginning. Who IS Peter Bell, really? [Peter Bell:] Well, that's a trick question. :) I'm really quite an ordinary guy. Works, pays bills, ordinary day to day stuff. I also like to write. [[name withheld]:] In your E-wrestling career, who have you handled? [Peter Bell:] Numerous ones really... [[name withheld]:] Name some that stand out. [Peter Bell:] Going all the way back to over two years ago... the ones that stand out are Sex Machine Slade, "The Titan" Peter Bell, and my latest one Derek McMasters. Those are my most well known so to speak. [[name withheld]:] What federations have they been in? Did they hold any titles? [Peter Bell:] Lesse... I've only been in a handful of feds since getting into e-wrestling. NICW (best fed I've ever been in btw), kudos to you Jawaad... and IWF/WOW, and UEW. Plus a few that closed down I've never been oneto really go after titles, but...Slade held the NICW TV title for about 4 months... plus Slade/Bell held the IWF/WOW tag team belts. "The Titan" has been my most successful... if you count all the smaller feds he's been in. [[name withheld]:] Interesting. Any outstanding matches or feuds? [Peter Bell:] Oh yeah! [[name withheld]:] I like the sound of that! [Peter Bell:] Greatest feud I've ever been in was Slade and Shawn Riser... now that was about the best feud! Lots of bitterness and anger there... just between wrestlers though...[name withheld] is a great guy IRL. But my greatest feud is coming up... and that involves McMasters. [[name withheld]:] Let's not discuss that! Could be a spoiler! [Peter Bell:] Gotcha ya'... my lips are sealed! :) [[name withheld]:] What goals do you have in the IWF/WoW? [Peter Bell:] Hmmm... [[name withheld]:] Besides having McMasters do a Marylin Manson impersonation? [Peter Bell:] LOL! Well, just to have fun, really. That's been the main thing for me about e-wrestling. It's all about fun and working with other handlers for angles, feuds, etc. [[name withheld]:] From what I heard, you got some negativeness from a "Sabu424", who was said to be [name withheld]. Recently, in a "Ringpost Rebuttal", his brother, David, came to his brother's defense. What do you think about that? [Peter Bell:] I believe Sabu424 is definitely [name withheld]... the similarities are all there, bad speeling, etc. :) I don't want to harp on the past but...Dave is covering up, without a doubt. I hold little respect for both them, Dave and Christian. They're a black mark on e-wrestling and personally, Christian went too far with his actions. They can rot as far as I'm concerned. Neither are professionals... [[name withheld]:] Earlier, you stated thanks to [name withheld]. What are your feelings towards him? [Peter Bell:] Professionaly and personally? [[name withheld]:] Both, if you like. [Peter Bell:] Okay...Jawaad IMHO is perhaps the best fed head to ever grace e-wrestling. Sure, he started off rough around the edges at first, but in 3 months the NICW was the best fed as far as I'm concerned. I never asked for belts, I've never had my wrestlers push for them... and he treated me with the utmost respect as a handler. I think every handler in NICW would say that. I mean, he never gave me belts just cuz I was his friend... [[name withheld]:] I possibly would say the same, but I never had the privilage to meet the man. [Peter Bell:] Sometime, chat with him, he appears here from time to time. [[name withheld]:] What do you want to be remembered as, Peter. What would be your defining moment? [Peter Bell:] Personally, Jawaad is perhaps the best friend I've ever met over the net... the two of us share similar interests, hobbies, etc. He's like the second brother I never had. As for what I want to be remembered by? My defining moment so to speak? That's a hard one...I'd have to say that I'd like to be remembered as a good handler who never bitched or complained about a loss, or bragged about a win... I'd like, when all is said and done, to be recognized as a fair fed head... as I restart Jawaad's old fed, the NICW. [[name withheld]:] A rebirth, huh? A tribute to E-wrestling's Living Legend? [Peter Bell:] You could say so... I hold the utmost respect for Jawaad and NICW when it was in his tenor and when he presided over it. I ran a short lived fed about a year ago MLW but I made mistakes there... this time, I want to do it right, as I had tremendous fun running it. Now, however, I want to do it right, and like you said, make it a tribute to Jawaad. He's the man, the legend of fed heads IMHO. [[name withheld]:] Will old wrestlers be called back, or will it be open to everyone? [Peter Bell:] I plan to open it to everyone... it's only fair. I mean, I'm not stuck on ego trips or anything... if someone wants to join that can rp well, I won't refuse them. I'd like new blood... it's what makes e-wrestling thrive. [[name withheld]:] Closing up, Pete. Any final notes or closing words? Now's the perfect time for Shameless Plugs! [Peter Bell:] Final notes... yeah, the NICW is back! If you would like to find out more about it, e-mail me... [detail withheld] Thanks... [[name withheld]:] No problem. You'll be getting something from me soon. [Peter Bell:] :) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ Peter seems to be a man on a mission. He wants to pay tribute to [name withheld], who many call a gift to E-wrestling, by re-creating the NICW. I don't know much about the original NICW, but I was told that it was a great federation to be in. I know that I will be one of the first to sign up for it, and hope others will to. Good Luck, Peter. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------ Up next on the Clothesline Commentary: We will have [name withheld], President of the NOW and handler of former GFWA World Champion Alex Reaver, on the Commentary.
