MLW Wrestle Zone, Episode 1

21 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Right after "TSN's Sportdesk" at 12:00 AM, eastern standard time, the screen opens to a Candian flag, with the Maple Leaf Wrestling logo becoming super embossed on top of it. Cheezy, outdated, rock music plays in the background as the screen fades into a simple looking 'newsroom' layout. A large metallic desk takes up the viewer's primary view... a sharply dressed man in a blue suit, with long curly red hair sits behind it. He appears to be in his late 20's and somewhat out of shape. Behind him is a large, low-quality monitor...] Man: Good evening... uhh... night?... err... can we do this over again? [he says with a perplexed look on his face.] Background Voice: [Barely audible, and most likely, one of the show's directors...] That's a negative, Shawn! Get your damn act together!!! Man: [loosening his collar and adjusting his tie, while a few beads of sweat trickle down his forehead] Err... heh heh... kinda got confused there... heh heh... see, we're on on Pacific time... er, ah... as well... as well as... Background Voice: [same as before] Dammit! You're wasting air time! Background Voice 2: [another barely audible, but deeper and separate voice, than the first...] Heh heh... does he even know what that is?! [A lot of chuckling can be heard in the background as the man's face goes red and then turns to somewhat angry looking.] Man: OKAY! OKAY! SHUT UP ALREADY!!! FRIG ME! MAKE ONE MISTAKE... Background Voice 2: [holding back laughter.] Heheeheeheehehe... the only mistake was Mr. Bell hiring you back again! heheheehe [The background chuckling turns into 'riotous' background laughter amongst the camera man, directers... as the camera's view continues to weevil, wovil around...] Man: AHEM! [he coughs into his fist] As I was saying... WELCOME, Maple Leaf Wrestling fans to the very first installment of "The Wrestle Zone!" Every week you'll be able to catch me... your host, Shawn Douglas, as I bring you all the up-to-date news, latest rumours, and interviews from your favourite wrestlers, and not so quite favourites! *whew* That was a mouthful! Err... getting back to topic... MLW officially re-starts this coming Tuesday at the Trudeau Arena, in Ottawa! For those of you die-hard MLW fans in the Ottwa/Hull region, this promises to be a good solid opener for Maple Leaf Wrestling! Lots of tickets are still available this inaugral... deja-vu event! As I understand it... all the surrounding colleges and universities are selling tickets to this event, as well as numerous indie stores. Heck, we even have front row seats still available! [Shawn smiles.] Shawn Douglas: As was detailed in the MLW Preview Show, matches will feature Rob Magnum in action, along with the Killing Machines, and the main event of the evening... Dan Watterson vs. Rick Wright! And as I understand it... Watterson has some comments for Wright... let's hear from Dan "the man" at his home in Philadelphia... [Camera focuses in on the giant, cheap-monitor behind Douglas.] **************************************************************************** ** (Scene fades in. We see Dan Watterson at his home ready to leave to go to the airport. He is flying to Toronto to the first card of the comeback of MLW. He sees the camera, and speaks.) Dan Watterson: MLW is back and is hotter than ever. I'm ready for anything that comes to me!! I see that I will be facing Rick Wright next Tuesday. Well Wright, we've met once, in the EWF World title battle royal, where I eliminated you. Don't think this will be an easy task, my friend. No, you shouldn't think that. When it all comes down to it, my repertoire of moves will easily make you confused, and I will come out as the victor!! Dan: I was watching the MLW preview show. Very interesting. It said I had a fair amount of success in other organizations. Fair, yes. I won two titles in TIW, and one in the short-lived EWF. Over in the IWF/WOW, i've been there since the start and I have been titleless there. And you'd expect me to have left by now! I hope here in MLW, I will have great success, not just fair success. Dan: Now, the ones that have signed so far, except for Wright, I have never heard of. The one that really intrigues me is "The Real Deal" Steve Studd. As I have said many times before, I hate nicknames like that. I've dropped my "Man" nickname here, because I needed a new start. Studd, nobody is the "Real Deal" anyplace. I also hate real big egos. I'm sure he has one. So whenever he pops up, I want to challenge this guy. I hope he realizes that he ISN'T the "Real Deal". (Scene fades out) **************************************************************************** ** [Camera focuses back in on Shawn Douglas, seated at the Wrestle Zone desk.] Shawn Douglas: I apologize to Mr. Watterson for calling him... "the Man"... heh heh. As for his match with Wright, Dan is certainly walking into this one prepared and expecting anything from the tough Canadian, Rick Wright. Well, Mr. Watterson if all goes right, you just may find yourself onto bigger and better things in Maple Leaf Wrestling... but as to his comments directed towards Steve Studd? Well, the challenge has been made... but will "The Real Deal" step up to plate? We'll soon see. Up next... some words from the "big man" himself... Rob Magnum! **************************************************************************** ** ("No worries" Rob Magnum is sitting down on the front porch to his house. Me has his long brownish black hair pulled back and is wearing a black sleeveless t-shirt reading "Bad As I Wanna Be" on the front in white. He is wearing regular loose fitting jeans and a pair of black boots underneath. He is wearing a bandana but it is on backwards and he has a pair of shades on. He gets up from his sitting position and leans against the railing next to him.) "No worries" Rob Magnum> MLW...................yeah I MIGHT like the sound of that. You guys really struck it rich in being able to land me I'll tell you that. I've fought in every big federation there is. Smart, you say you've wrestled in the UEW. Well guess what? So did I. Faired pretty well too if I say so myself. HELL I'm gonna cut with the modesty. I DOMINATED THE UEW. Now the MLW came calling me at my house back in Detroit awhile back and asked me what I thought about coming here. So I looked into it. I came to the conclusion that I mine as well come up here and give it a try. I mean I don't really like Canada because honestly.........HOW CAN YOU HANDLE THIS WEATHER? Does it ever get warm here? Anyway back to the subject at hand. MLW, thank the lord because he has sent you a savior and his name is Rob Magnum. Now I watched that Preview show and they said I've got POTENTIAL. Let me tell you something, potential is something that a little kid from Barbados has when he tries to enter wrestling. I don't have potential because I've PASSED potential. I've kicked ass and taken names all over the world. Potential my ass. Then they said I've got a bum elbow. Let me tell you something. I said my elbow is healed for good and it is so don't even mention it again or I'll come down to that show and take care of things myself. Smart seemed to live up to his name though and said many good things about me. All of which are true. I'm gonna put it simply. I'm the big man around here. Seven feet 357 pounds of POWER. They said something about somebody..........Hell, I can't even remember his name, but they said he had the most devastating move in the MLW. Look this way and you'll see the most devastating finisher. I've got my "Southern Comfort" and we're not talking about the drink. It's the same thing as the "Razor's Edge" just I do it ALOT better and at seven feet that adds another dimension to that move and next Tuesday I'm gonna show the world first hand just what it can do when I put it on my first victim here in the MLW. I'm talking about none other than Bobby Sherlock. Sherlock you are in for a real treat next Tuesday because you're the first person I'm gonna knock off the ladder. You see right now everybody is on that ladder, but when the dust settles I'll be the only one left and guess where I'll be standing? That's right on TOP of it. Pres, what's up buddy? You seemed a little lost for words there on that video. Why can't you just do the right thing and get this title thing started NOW. I'm not one for authority so if you think you can push me around like all these other puppets here you've got something else coming your way. Get these damn title matches started because I look good now but give me some gold and I look EVEN BETTER. As impossible as that may seem. My main reason here is to bring myself home a strap. I've held titles EVERYWHERE and the MLW will be no different. So put my name on one of those titles and send it in the mail to me. You know where to find me. Even if you don't I'm not a hard man to find. (He starts to walk away before he suddenly turns around) Oh!!!!!! By the way why the hell am I not the one main eventing the card next week. I'm the big name around here. MLW, you can't derail the "No Worries Express".........even though its next stop is right through you. **************************************************************************** ** [Camera cuts back to Shawn Douglas...] Shawn Douglas: My God! This man... well... WELL HE SCARES ME DANG-IT! I mean, look at him... he's as huge as huge can be! As to the weather Mr. Magnum... heh heh... get used to it! And another thing... what is it with his cocky than thou attitude? I mean, he claims he's surpassed "potential"... but that means zilch until he can step into the ring with the best! Of course, there's not that many wrestlers to choose from just yet... but we are in contract negotiations with numerous wrestlers. One word about the championship belts... since conducting that interview with President Bell last week, I have been informed that he will make a statement concerning the titles within the next two weeks... possibly even on next week's Impact! Hopefully we'll know soon how they are to be decided... there's a lot of trigger happy people out gunning for them... like Magnum. However, we will see wether Rob has the "jam" or the stuff, as he squares off against Bobby Sherlock on the upcoming card. Next however... some comments from this rather new team to professional wrestling... Judgment Night... **************************************************************************** ** <scene shows David Knight and Sean Judge working out inside a gym in Brooklyn, N.Y. David is inside the ring taking apart his sparring partner with submission moves, while Sean is busy swinging a metal folding chair into a huge weight bag. David is putting on a technical wrestling show inside the ring, and finally locks in a standing double chickenwing which forces the sparring partner to scream in agony that he submits. David immediately lets go and helps the sparring partner get back to his feet. He pats him on the back and shakes his hand gingerly before walking out of the ring. Some of the other wrestlers give him high-fives and pats on the back as he walks over to where Sean is pounding away unmercifully at the weight bag. When David gets there, he and Sean give each other a high-five in between Sean's chairshots to the bag. Sean then continues to pound on the bag as the thud echoes throughout the gym.> DK: Sean, we make our debut next week in MLW. *thud* SJ: It's about time! Just tell me when and where. DK: Next Tuesday on Tuesday Night Impact. We're going to go to Trudeau Arena up in Ottawa. SJ: That's Canada, right? DK: Yup, the whole fed is situated up in Canada. SJ: I don't care, just as long as I get paid to punk people for a living. *thud* DK: The name on the marquee says wrestling. SJ: I say potato....Whose teeth do get to kick first? DK: Next Tuesday we face The Gordy Brothers. *thud* SJ: I heard Terry is tough. Who is the other guy? DK: I don't think he's related to our opponents. We face Jim and Todd Gordy. *thud* SJ: Don't matter. I'd kick all three of their respective sets of teeth down their throats. DK: I know you would. <smiles and laughs a bit> I haven't heard too much about them, though. SJ: So what, there's not going to be much competition up there? DK: There's going to be! The team we have to watch out for the most are the Killing Machines. I used to watch Overkill and Demolisher in NICW. They were really dominating tag team champions for a long time. They're really going to push us to our limits. SJ: And we're going to push their necks to their limits, until they snap off. *thud* They might be really good, but we're still going to beat them to a bloody pulp when we meet them in the ring up in Canada. *THUD* DK: The key to beating them is to outwrestle them. We have to be on the top of our games when we eventually face off against Overkill and Demolisher. You know I want those tag team titles as bad as you do. *thud* SJ: We aren't gonna rest until we bring those titles home. DK: Wouldn't that be incredible if we won the top tag team titles in the first federation we ever wrestled in? How many teams can say that? SJ: Doesn't matter. We'll be able to say that even if I have to kick the commissioner's teeth in. We're going to come away with the tag team titles because we're going to be the best team they've ever seen. DK: We are going to be the best team they've ever seen and we're going to win the titles fair and square in the ring. We have to get back to training. Our debut is in less than a week. We have to be ready! *thud* SJ: I think I've had more than enough practice with the chair. Time to move on. <Sean throws the chair over to the side and rolls out a rack of crutches> *crack* DK: Starting next Tuesday, there's going to be justice for all in MLW. <David runs over to the ring and arranges to be next in line while Sean continues to break crutches over the weight bag as the camera fades out.> **************************************************************************** ** [Back to Shawn at the Wrestle Zone desk...] Shawn Douglas: Can you say, these guys are pumped?! Heh heh... but in all seriousness folks, Sean Judge and David Knight maybe rookies, but they seem to click somehow, and appear to be quite formidable. They also seem to have an incredible amount of confidence... yet, discipline, extraordinary for youthful wrestlers such as them. By the way it sounds, they are most definitely gunning for the Northern tag titles, along with the Killing Machines! Take my advice Sean and Mark, don't rush into things to headlong... Overkill and Demolisher are one of the most brutal teams going, and it'll take more than practice... it'll take experience! What about Sean, eh? Heh heh... he likes to practice with his chairs and crutches! What a kid... and a character... heh heh... oh boy, I'm running out of lines to say... Background Voice 1: Just get on with it. SD: Okay, okay... Anyways, Judgment Night will be one of the two tag teams we will see in action this coming Tuesday! We'll just see how ready these two boys from Brooklyn are, for the pro-circuit. Fans! We gotta take a commercial break... but we'll be right back, after these important... or not so important, messages! [Camera cuts to various commercials such as an ad for "The Fifth Element", and one for Kokonee, along with an independent ad about a wrestling school starting up in Ottawa. After 3 minutes the screen turns back to Shawn Douglas, drinking a glass of water...] Shawn Douglas: AAAHHHH! That hit the spot! Err... oops! Heh heh... guess we're on the air, eh? Background Voice 2: My lord, what a loser... [Some more chuckling can be heard going on 'behind the scenes'. Shawn ignores it and continues...] SD: Alright, fans we are back with some late breaking news! Maple Leaf Wrestling has just signed FOUR new wrestlers... yes, that's right... the roster keeps expanding up here in good ol' Canada! Signed are, Tommy Lane... "Studley" Brian Simpson and Boom Daddy... and Bobby "The Blade" Ransom! Now I haven't heard too much about Simpson and Boom Daddy, but I have heard a bit about Tommy Lane and "The Blade". And let me tell you... there's a big buzz around the offices up here concerning them. That's all I have to say... but Ransom did leave these pre-recorded comments for our viewing... pleasure, I guess?... Oh well, roll the tape! **************************************************************************** ** (The Scene: A New York City penthouse, far above the bustling streets. Bobby "The Blade" Ransom is leaning against a railing looking out over the nighttime cityscape. His black leather jacket makes him almost invisible in the night sky, yet the steel tips on his black cowboy boots glisten like the stars above. He reaches down and unsheathes a BUCK knife from its case strapped onto his blue jeans and starts plucking his fingernails with the blade absentmidedly. He turns and looks into the camera, smiling a shark-tooth grin...) Bobby Ransom: Greetings to all of the ladies in MLW. I have kinda been outta action for a little while, but The Blade is now back in shape and is as sharp as ever. Pun intended. Let me give you all some advice and suggestions to make life a little easier on all of you. (He crosses over to a corner bench and the view changes to a backdrop of the Verazanno Narrows Bridge with its blinking airline warning lights.) Bobby Ransom: First off, calling me anything other than The Blade is gonna cost you a lot... namely the price of intensive care. Ask Max Rivers... (He points to a still photograph hanging on the inside of the door. It shows one Max "The Blade" Rivers being carried from an arena, bloodied and broken.) Next, while I haven't decided whether or not to go singles or tag-team yet, ALL of you so-called champions, or as I like to say CHUMPions, better shine those belts up all nice and pretty... because I'll soon have them. If you hand them over, you won't get hurt... much. Ya see, I don't like cheap shots, nor do I like behind the back attacks. I am all man and as a man I like to FACE my victims...er.. opponnents. (He throws the knife into the apartment and it sticks in a closet door already marred by countless such tosses.) Bobby Ransom: They say it's not so scary if you know what's coming... they never met me... (Fade...) **************************************************************************** ** [Camera cuts back to Douglas at the Wrestle Zone desk, once again...] Shawn Douglas: Well, quite the words... and quite the warning to the rest of the MLW wrestlers! You know, I find it odd that he hasn't made up his mind yet about singles or tag-team competition.... but I'm sure whatever division he enters... he'll be making himself well known. [Screen changes as the camera shifts to another... and Shawn turns his head to face the new rolling camera. Behind him is a small T.V. sized monitor that has the words, "The HOT List!" displayed on it.] SD: Now MLW fans, it's time for yours truly to bring you this week's "HOT List!" Tune in every week to this program and around this time this is where I'll be bringing you all the juiciest, tastiest rumours you want to know! First up, two wrestlers, one a former "Original", the other a newcomer are slated to appear sometime in June. Why the delay? My guess is that these guys are holding out for maybe just a little bit more money... not that we have a whole lot to offer ya's. [Shawn chuckles to himself, looking like a dork... naturally!] SD: In another delectible morsel... it also appears that Maple Leaf Wrestling may perhaps be entering a shared talent type deal with another federation... which one? I wish I knew... but when I find out... I'll post it on my hotline, 1-900-MILK-ME-FOR-ALL-IT'S-WORTH! Heh heh heh... A major wrestler from the former incarnation MLW, maybe returning... guesses to as who this might be abound in rampant names, none of them really providing any clues. Cuz all of the former MLW stars were listed! HA! Background Voice 2: We gotta cut this guy off... he actually thinks himself funny. SD: But seriously folks, one rumour that has been flying around here is that all the titles WILL be decided before "The Great Canadian Bash" PPV, which incidentally, airs the first of July! What the matches are that will determine the new second era champs remains however, a mystery. In another tidbit, look to see Dan Watterson and Steve Studd get entangled in a fued. There is also reports, that are as yet unconfirmed, but say that an unlikely wrestler will recieve a BIG push in the future... ie, 2 to 3 months from now. However, this report is so vague, it's better not believing it! Will we perhaps see a fued between the upstart rookies, Judgment Night and the former NICW tag team champs, The Killing Machines? Perhaps... this stuff is sketchy at the moment, as I don't have a lot of info related to the tag scene... mind you, there's not a lot of tag teams in MLW yet. HOWEVER, this may work to some advantage to the teams currently in MLW... less competition, and that means a higher chance of claiming the gold! But who knows... perhaps we'll see an influx of tag talent here once the singles roster becomes filled out. Stuff to ponder for the future... whatever that is. One last rumour... inside sources... Background Voice 2: Namely you... SD: Oh, be quiet! As I was saying... inside sources tell me that Lazor Lemon may miss his debut due to drug trafficking convictions back in Miami. Apparantly, charges have been laid and no bail has been set... thus, Lazor would miss his debut and perhaps his whole career up here in Canada. This is unconfirmed but this is what has been leaked to me. I've said enough... Fans, be here next week, after Tuesday Night Impact! As I will give you a rundown of that night's action and a look ahead to the coming week. I'm Shawn Douglas... saying so long... and uhh... er... ahh... DANG! I forgot my line... Background Voice 1: Cut! SD: Wait! I remembered... [Screen switches to the Canadian emblem as the credits roll by in blue...] -- [name withheld] President of Maple Leaf Wrestling and ... Thee #1 fan of "The Hitman". (Bout time he told the truth about America! :) LONG LIVE THE FOUNDATION!!!:) e-mail: [detail withheld]
