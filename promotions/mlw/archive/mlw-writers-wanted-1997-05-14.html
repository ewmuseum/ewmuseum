MLW Writers WANTED!...

14 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For those of you who don't know me, allow me to introduce myself... my name's Peter Bell, I'm 22 years old and a BIG fan of wrestling in general and have been e-wrestling for over two years now, and a new poster to RSPWF. As the title implies, MLW, or Maple Leaf Wrestling, is looking for writers. Some of you may remember I ran MLW last year, well, I had to shut the fed down due to personal reasons and time constraints but now I find myself with spare time and wanting to get back into running a league. But what I need is writers... good writers who are willing to write play by play matches. MLW was a booked fed that tried to bring back some of the ol' NWA flavour. It was regional, with its cards taking place strictly in Canada with the exception of one PPV. The wrestlers that competed here had little or no gimmick, as was my intention, I wanted a league that was realistic. And so, these things hold true to the MLW of today, for I'll change none of my that. MLW was always about quality and putting out the best card that I could, and that'll hold true today. But I can't do it alone. Last time I ran the league, I found mself writing the majority of the cards, with the exception of one writer who wrote a couple matches a week, but I'm looking and advertising this time around (since I finally have access to newsgroups and RSPWF) for help. Now I wrote this short bio/history/info on MLW to give you a brief idea and rundown of what exactly Maple Leaf Wrestling is about... and so, if by what you've read are interested in writing, or seek more info, don't hesitate to drop me a line... I want to bring back MLW, but this time... TO STAY! :) -- Thee #1 fan of "The Hitman". (Bout time he told the truth about America! :) LONG LIVE THE FOUNDATION!!!:)
