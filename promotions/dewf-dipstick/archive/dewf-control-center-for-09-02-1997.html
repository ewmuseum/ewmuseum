Control Center for 09/02/1997!

Sep 3, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fed head : [detail withheld] (Open on Tony Schiavone sitting in the DEWF Control Room) Schiavone: Hello, and welcome to the DEWF Control Center, I'm Tony Schiavone. Fans, we are a few days removed from one of the wildest nights in DEWF history. But first, I want to bring you up to date on the condition of Joey Styles. Joey was the victim of a heinous attack by the 450 pound mastadon of the DEWF, Vader. (roll highlight footage) If it hadn't been for the timely intervention of "The Man From The Dark Side", The Undertaker, Vader might well have crippled Joey for life. As it is, he will be out for a few weeks at least. He sends his thank you's for all the get well wishes and wants you all to know he appreciates your thoughtfulness. Now, on to this coming week's Saturday Night Extreme. As you all remember, Justin "Hawk" Bradshaw saw fit to stick his nose in the business of "The Lost Soul" Sting, attacking him with his trusty bullrope AND stealing Sting's bat like the thief he is. This coming Saturday night, Bradshaw will put up the bat against a man who has vowed to retrieve the bat for the Stinger, Scotty Riggs, in a bullrope match. Also, The Crows and Raven will face The Neighborhood Of Evil, composed of Hayabusa and Halloween, and Joe Malenko in what promises to be one of the wildest six mans in recent memory. The Giant will try to regain the DEWF Intercontinental Title from "Outsider" Scott Hall. We will hear from "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz about "The Ivory Tower" Justin Cross and HIS challenge to Taz to meet him in "The Cage Of Fear". A LIVID DEWF Commissioner Gary Hart has *ordered* under penalty of termination that The Razor Boys WILL defend the DEWF World Tag Team Titles against BOTH Blondes 3:16 and The Road Warriors in a three way dance to be held INSIDE the steel cage! Japan has heard the challenge of Michael Monroe and as a result "The Shadow" will defend his DEWF World Light Heavyweight Title against one of the true unsung legends of out sport, Gran Hamada. Finally, DEWF World Heavyweight Champion Kevin Nash *will defend* the DEWF World Heavyweight Championship against a *mysterty opponent*. As a final touch, we have been promised that the 2Wo will both debut their newest member AND respond to the unexpected return of 911. Until this Saturday night, I'm Tony Schiavone! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----
