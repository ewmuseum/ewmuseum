DEWF Wednesday Night Weirdness Part 2!!! (fwd)

Feb 13, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fred "The Brake Man" Jakeman: Well, here is an interview with new DEWF superstar Wildthing. I feel that I must warn the viewers that this is different from anything else you've seen from this federation. Enjoy at your own risk. ** The video starts ** *scene opens up on the Baltimore Harbor.....camera zooms in on a man walk along the waterfront wearing a black denim jacket with "Wildthing" across it* *wildthing begins to speak after smoking a drag of his cigarette* Wildthing: I've just accepted a new challenge....to compete in the DEWF.....I hear of the champion, "The Thinker"...I seen his last interview *Wildthing does a shudder* what HAVE I gotten myself into here....I'll tell you....the best thing I could ever do...I wrestle for two things....not for the belt, not for the glory....but for the fans, whom I'll always be loyal...and my pride...here I know I can compete with the best...here I know I stand alone...here I begin to make my mark...I challenge ANYONE to a match, ANYTIME...win or lose....you'll have the fight of your life...DEWF be ready, be warned and beware...the Wildthing has landed *Wildthing walks off smoking his cigarette as the screen fades to black* ** The video ends. ** Trillionaire Coot: WHAT WAS THAT? That guy goes against everything DEWF stands for, he's NORMAL!!!!! Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly ** counting his hotel remote controls. **: I'm beginning to wonder if the entire middle management department of this federation can control their dipstickishness. This guy belongs in the EWA or something, but the DEWF? Why? Fred "The Brake Man" Jakeman: He said it was for the fans and his pride. Of course, the fans aren't going to get him and how can anyone have pride and wrestle for us? I think Hubert Pubert is ready to introduce him now. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Wildthing v. "The Referee" Curtis Faust ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ** "Gone Away" by Offspring plays ** Hubert Pubert: Introducing first in our main event, weighing in at 220 pounds, from Baltimore, here is WILDTHING!!!! ** Wildthing comes out to the unexpected cheers of the crowd of over 31. He is wearing a black denim jacket with his name on the back in purple and pants and boots to match. ** Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: Why are they cheering this knob? He's about as dipstickish as John Tesh. I don't get it. Fred "The Brake Man" Jakeman: This is an adult theater in Nebraska, for Poffo's sake!! What do you expect? This crowd is dumb!!! Oh, well, I think Curtis Faust is ready to come out now. ** The 1-2-3 Kid's WWF theme plays ** Hubert Pubert: And his opponent, weighing in at 234 pounds, from San Francisco by way of Edinburgh, Scotland, and representing the Six People, here is "THE REFEREE" CURTIS FAUST!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ** Faust comes out in a DEWF referee's uniform without the McDonald's hat. ** Trillionaire Coot: As much as I resent this guy for butting into my personal domain of suspensions and fines, I have to root for him here. He's closer to the dipstick ideal. Well, he's pretty normal himself, but he's not that bad. Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: Yeah, the People are fairly normal. I think they could be spies from that other federation to destroy our dipstickishness. After all, Webber is here and when you take his bowling ball away, he is normal. It's a conspiracy, I tell you. Fred "The Brake Man" Jakeman: Uh, they're the ones leading the ratings. They shouldn't have to spy on us. Oh, well, it looks like the match is about to begin. ** Ding - dong - ding - dong ** Fred "The Brake Man" Jakeman: Well, they lock up here. Wildthing with a monkey flip and takedown. The referee counts. One . . . Two . . . NO!!!!!! Faust gets up!!! He's going for an armdrag here. He gets Wildthing down here. The ref counts again, apparently scared smart by Faust's presence. One . . . Two . . . Thr -- NO!!!!!!!! The Wildthing is up and headed to the top rope. Yawn, I can't believe we scheduled this snoozer for a main event. A nice Elmo Todd match would be a change of pace here. Speaking of him, turn the TV on, there has to be something on. ** Dennis turns the monitor to the All-Gardening Network and starts watching it. Fred and Coot begin to get interested in it as well. ** Fred "The Brake Man" Jakeman: YES!!! What a way to plant that rosebush. Ms. Melba Muldoon sure has a green thumb!!!!!! THIS IS THE PLANTING OF THE CENTURY!!!!!!!!! Trillionaire Coot: You know, I think I'm going to start Dipstick Championship E-Gardening, the DEGF. I think there would be many famous gardeners who would participate, not excluding the divine Ms. Muldoon. I've never seen rose planting in such a light. This is exquisite. ** The AGN goes to commercials and Dennis flips the channel to Gab-O-Vision, where an really annoying marijuana activist is debating with one of those preachers you see every day on the really high-numbered TV stations. ** Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: YES!!! He nailed that preacher man good! You go, Chaz!!! Fred "The Brake Man" Jakeman: Yeah, Chaz is an amazing debater. That preacher man can't even find the verse of the bible that says people who smoke pot are fed to dogs. Trillionaire Coot: He's not supposed to. Don't you know talk shows are fake? Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: How can you fake a talk show? Trillionaire Coot: It's all scripted, you stupid mark. You don't think they'd let a guest pull a shoot on the air, do you? Heck, no. ** The talk show goes to commercial and an old episode of "Catch a Fish, Do a Shot" with Bubba Leon Lockhart is on the next station. ** Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: I can't believe that loser ever got a television show. He must have been way over on charisma because he can't fish worth beans. Fred "The Brake Man" Jakeman: Yeah, but the Bass Blasters circuit gave him an immense push a few years back that shocked everyone. He snagged a 20-pounder at Toledo Bend. Trillionaire Coot: That just proves one thing. Wrestling is the only competition on the planet that is actually based on skill. WHAT? There's something going on in the ring? OK, we'll actually watch it. ** While they were watching TV, Faust and Wildthing traded holds and technical moves for about 15 minutes. ** Fred "The Brake Man" Jakeman: It looks like Wildthing has Faust in the Dream Theater running sleeper. Faust is out!!! The real ref lifts his hand once . . . twice . . . and . . . KICKS FAUST IN THE GROIN!!! Well, I believe it's safe to say that Faust is out of it or he would definitely have felt that. IT'S OVER!!! ** Ding - dong - ding - dong ** Hubert Pubert: Your winner, at 21:30, WILDTHING!!!!!! Trillionaire Coot: Oh, great, that other federation's going to nail us to the wall for this one. Let's just cut the camera and be done with it. ** fade to black **
