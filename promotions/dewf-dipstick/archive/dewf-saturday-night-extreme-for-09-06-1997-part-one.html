Saturday Night Extreme for 09/06/1997 (part one)

Sep 12, 1997, 12:00:00 AM

Cage of f*cking Fear Cross? You should know better than that, I HAVE NO FEAR!! So you want me in the ring on your terms this time? That's fine by me Cross! You name the stipulations, and the Path of Rage will roll over you!! [TS] And now onto Hawk and Animal. Guys, tonight you try to get back something you had around your waists at one time before, the DEWF World Tag Titles. But first you have to get through both Blondes 3:16 and The Razor Boys. What are your thoughts? [H] WEEEEEELL!! Tony, you have to realize, we've already met The Razor Girls a long while back, and we rolled over them then, so it only makes sense we'll beat the snot out of 'em again! And as for the Blondes? Well boys, blondes may have more fun, but NOT WHEN YOU'RE FACING US!! [A] Tonight, tonight, we'll KICK YOUR ASSES TONIGHT!! Blondes, Razor Girls, you got something we want. And you can call us old farts all you want, we're gonna kick your asses and take those f*cking belts back!! [TS] And we finally get to the two biggest matches. Tonight we'll see the DEWF Intercontinental Champion, Scott Hall defend his title against the former champ, The Giant! Any thoughts? [SH] Tony mang, tonight Da Bad Guy makes his first defense chico. Giant, you big dumb goof, you can't beat Da Bad Guy mang. Tonight I will keep my title, and you can't do anything about it mang!! [TS] Ok, and tonight, Kevin, someone has a surprise in store for you. You are scheduled to meet a mystery opponent for your world title. [KN] Tony....Tony....Tony, do the suits around here really think they've got me scared? I've rolled over Shane Douglas, "The Franchise", I've rolled over Raven, and it continues tonight, plain and simple! I don't care who they throw at me!! [TS] Ok then, I guess that's about it! [KN] Oh, one last thing Tony. (Nash reaches out like he's gone grab Schiavone by the throat. The cops jump to attention, watching him carefully. Nash then smiles and reaches down and shakes Schivone's hand and pats him on the head before "Iron Man" plays and they walk out. Schiavone no sooner starts to open his mouth than "Red" by King Crimson plays over the arena PA system and Jerry "The King" Lawler leads his men to the interview area. Diamond Dallas Page is , uncharacteristically, looking *steamed*. Justin Hawk Bradshaw is carrying Sting's baseball bat in one hand, resting it on his shoulder , and carries an unopened can of beer in the other. The Razor Boys are glancing warily over their shoulders.) TS : King, you ... (Jerry Lawler grabs the mic from his hands.) JL : Shut yer yap! We barely put up with pointless blather from Joey Styles, and we certainly aren't going to tolerate it from you. DDP has something to say. DDP : Jimmy, Jimmy , Jimmy. Well, just because you dug through my tarsh and found one of my old castaways, you seem to think this makes some kind of point? Lowe, if you think that Kimmie Doll is the be all and end all of Hot Love, boy have you lived a sheltered life! She was but one of many Diamond Dolls, my friend, and she sure as hell wasn't the last ... or the best! I took her from nowhere to the fast lane, and now she's developed a taste for slumming. So be it. You just better hope your penecillin bill is paid up, Lowe! Paul Diamond : Gary Hart, if anything goes to show that there is a conspiracy against the Razor Boys, you proved it last week! After months of swerving me and Al, we finally got what we deserved ... a title shot. And sure enough, we walk home with the gold. So what do you do for our first title defence? Put us in a Three Way Dance! We're the champs, we deserve more respect than that! Well, if taking out the Over The Hill Gang and Jimmy Lowe's Carnival of Sexual Inadequacy is what it takes to get it done, then that is just what the Razor Boys will do.! It's Straight Razor time for all four of you losers! Tell 'em, Al! (Al Perez solemnly nods.) Justin Hawk Bradshaw : Scotty Riggs? Scotty Freakin' Riggs? Come one, Stinger, you could have come up with a better collection agency than that. I understand that you're not man enough to face me yourself, especially after the tarring I gave you last time, but it looks like I'm going to have to kneecap American UPS to be rid of you once and for all. If you want to step up to the plate again, Bozo the Goth, feel free! Only the rules have changed, and we ain't playing baseball any longer .. this time we're playing Fizzball! TS : Errr ... ummm ... fizzball? (Bradshaw looks at Schiavone like he's a complete idiot ... not too difficult, admittedly) JHB : Don't tell me you never played fizzball as a boy, Tony. Easiest game in the world. (Bradshaw shakes the can of beer violently and tosses it in the air. As it comes down, he swings at it and belts it with the baseball bat, showering Shiavone with beer foam and the audience with aluminum shrapnel.) JHB : Game over, Tony. DDP : GOOD GAWD! (The Royal Assassins return to the dressing room, except for Bradshaw who awaits the arrival of Scotty Riggs) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- *BULLROPE MATCH FOR STING'S BAT* Scotty Riggs vs. Justin "Hawk" Bradshaw Bradshaw tried mightily, but couldn't hold in laughter as Riggs stepped through the ropes. Bad move. Bad move because it got him dropkicked right in the mouth by Riggs. In fact, Riggs displayed an heretofore unknown bloodthirsty streak as he spent the next *14* solid minutes frankly beating the living hell out of Bradshaw. Even then, after Bradshaw busted Riggs' forehead wide open on the safety railing, he still couldn't gain any momentum as Riggs promptly Frankensteinered him onto the ring steps! It went that way for yet another 10 full minutes as Riggs was beginning to look more like Abdullah The Butcher than a former American Male. But in the end, it was Bradshaw's experience in Bullrope Matches that counted. His hair totally soaked in blood, eyes swollen nearly shut and damned near unconscious from the loss *of* blood, Bradshaw instinctively brought up the cowbell right in Riggs' face when Riggs tried for a Superfly Splash. Riggs wasn't in much better shape than Bradshaw at that point, and the cowbell in the mush knocked him silly. Bradshaw staggered to his feet and got all four turnbuckles. Slowly, but he got them. (match time:24:48) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Tony Schiavone : Folks, I ... oh, what is it now? ("Exorcism" by Killing Joke blasts over the arena PA system as Kevin Sullivan strides to the interview area, followed by the Neighborhood of Evil) KS : It's your worst nightmare, Mr. Schiavone, come to life. TS : Boy, you've got that right ... KS : Darkside, you have no idea how dark the shadows are! The Neighborhood of Evil rules the shadows , no matter what you or certain others might think. Raven, it's about time your pinfeathers got plucked. You were singled out because you claim to know the true pain of the soul, but you have no idea ! You spout your cheap Gen X aphorisms, Mr. Levy, while all the time you are just a spoiled little rich kid playing a man's game in search of cheap thrills. You sent your whey faced friends from the Fields of the Nephilim Appreciation Society to eliminate us, but even they could not stop the power of pure evil. Well, I have someone who wants to take all of you out in one fell swoop. Joe Malenko : Raven, you say I stabbed you in the back. Well, that much is true, and I'd gladly do it again. You take pride in being a former champion. Well I was a champion in All Japan, a place where you have never even dared to compete. I specialize in wrestling, not dressing stylishly, not doing cool hair flips, no making spooky speeches ... wrestling! Have you ever heard of it? Raven, when we meet again, I'm going to take you apart ... piece by piece! KS : One more thing. The last time we faced the Darkside, the Whore of Babylon sought to defile my physical person. I could say that I was going to take this out on her personally ... but I find other methods are more effective. She might be able to handle damage done to herself ... but what about someone she cares about? Her familial blood spilled? Think about it, dear, remember when you are weeping your eyes out that not only did you bring it on yourself, you brought it on your family as well. You can run, but you can't hide. (Kevin Sullivan laughs maniacally as "Boogie Man" by White Zombie begins blaring over the PA - and here we go!) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Crows and Raven (w/Succubus) vs. The NOE and Joe Malenko (w/Kevin Sullivan) Crow 2 and Hayabusa started out and the match was very nearly over a mere three moves in when Crow 2 reversed a Hayabusa whip into the ropes, booted the masked man in the midsection and scored a nasty DDT for a two count. One thing that escaped no one's notice was that Sullivan left right after the match started... Crow 2 missed a handspring elbow, and Hayabusa took advantage with a quick flying clothesline out of the corner. Hayabusa nailed yet one more flying clothesline and then tagged out to Halloween. Crow 2 simultaneously tagged out to Raven, who was met by an armdrag from Halloween, who promptly tagged out to Joe Malenko. Malenko took Raven over and down with a *nice* hiptoss and got a one count. Malenko ran into the ropes and ducked a Raven clothesline, only to get hit with a textbook single leg takedown. Raven hauled Malenko to his feet and whipped him into the turnbuckle. Crow 2 entered the ring and nailed Malenko with a savate kick, and Raven followed up with a small package which got a two count. Malenko ran into the ropes and nailed Raven with a clothesline, followed with a hiptoss, a fistdrop, then went for another hiptoss only to get it countered by a backslide by Raven, which got a two count. Raven followed up with a German suplex, which got another two. Raven hauled Malenko to his feet and chopped him (Big "WHOOOOOO" from the Mutants), tagging out to Crow 2. Raven nailed Malenko with a backbreaker, and Crow 2 dropped down on Malenko with a senton. Halloween broke up the pinfall attempt, then he and Malenko double-whipped Crow 2 into the ropes. Crow 2 ducked and leveled *them* with a springboard twisting double clothesline on the rebound! (*POP*) Halloween rolled out as Crow 2 rushed Malenko - getting backdropped over the top rope to the floor for his troubles. Malenko quickly followed, and dropkicked Crow 2 right against the ring steps. Malenko snap suplexed Crow 2 on the floor, then whipped him into the guardrail. Malenko re-entered the ring, and tried to suplex Crow 2 back in. No go. Crow 2 suplexed *Malenko* from the inside *out* to the floor, and then acted as a springboard for a spectacular Sasuke Special from Crow 1 *onto* Malenko (**POP**)! Needless to say, Malenko spatula'd himself back into the ring and made the tag to Halloween. Crow 2 hopped the top rope and ran right into a chairshot to the face from Halloween. Halloween layed the chair across Crow 2, drew his finger across his throat, and went to the top. Halloween's mistake came when he paused at the top to taunt the fans by playing to Sullivan. Crow 2 *sat up* and grabbed the chair - then knocked Halloween clean off the top and into the first row with what has come to be known as The Chairshot From Hell! Crow 2 was still groggy though, and Halloween had plenty of time to crawl back over the rail and collapse into the ring. Referee Mark Curtis started the ten count as both men were on the canvas, and trying to make their respective tags. Crow 2 *and* Halloween made simultaneous tags, Halloween to Hayabusa and Crow 2 to Crow 1. Crow 1 hopped the top rope, ducked a spin kick from Hayabusa and dropped the masked man with a stream of red mist in the eyes! Halloween rushed back in, and got dropped with a leg lariat! *Malenko* tried his luck, only to be met by Raven with a DDT! The crowd was going *insane* as both Crows and Raven hauled the NOE and Malenko to their feet, and cinched them up in headlocks! *TRIPLE DDT*!!! Crow 1 promptly sprang to the top rope and brought the F'N house down with a Shooting Star Press on Hayabusa for the one-TWO-THREE!!! Anyways, 911, I'm challenging you, come down here right now, because we've got our own giant to take you out! {as 911 aproaches, "the Giant" Paul Lauria comes out and blindsides him, then the rest of the 2Wo comes in for the beatdown. After the subdue 911} [Hulk Johnson] Hy gahs, lets gt da boof! {the 2Wo goes after the announcers booth, chases everyone away, trashes it a little, as both the Giant and Hulk grab hedsets} [Paul Lauria] I suppose you're all wondering why I joined the 2Wo... well, look what not joining got me? [Paul Lauria] All due to the 2Wo!!! So, all I've got to say is, we're taking over!!! [Hulk Johnson] Tu-dubya-oh fuh laf, brudda!!! --------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF Intercontinental Title Match* The Giant (w/"Sweet" Jimmy Lowe) *challenger* vs. "Outsider" Scott Hall (w/Paul Ellering) *champion* It looked like this might indeed be a short match as The Giant did everything short of dribbling Hall in the opening minute. The Giant was also not pleased that the referee once again was none other than Nick Patrick. But then Hall ducked a Giant boot to the head off the ropes and dropped the big man with a thundering bulldog. Hall followed up by throwing Giant out of the ring and drumming his head into the steel ringsteps once, twice, three times, which opened Giant's forehead right up. Hall then got back in the ring and executed what had to be the move of the night when he *piledriver*ed Giant as he was coming back in! the the night when he *piledriver*ed Giant as he was coming back in! the crowd went *berserk* cheering on Hall. Even Ellering looked stunned that Hall was able to pull the move off. Lowe was flabbergasted, even more so when from out of the back came DDP and the two began fighting all the way to the back, Page flipping Kim the finger on the way by. The Giant never really recovered from the piledriver, and it only got worse when from opposite sides of the arena came Kevin Nash and Tommy Dreamer. Dreamer was the first to get a shot in, kicking Giant in the ... uh ... and blasting him right between the eyes with a chair, all the while getting cheered on by an amused Nash! Giant couldn't even get a break when seventeen long minutes in, he finally got the Choke Slam on Hall, hooked the tight for good measure - and had the count broken up by an elbowdrop from Nash. As Patrick sent Nash out, Hall called Dreamer into the ring, where the two double DDTed Giant and Dreamer rolled out of the ring, heading out the back via the crowd. Giant staggered to his feet - and went right back down again via a flying bulldog from Nash! One - two - THREE!!! (match time:17:53) But the fun wasn't over (is it ever in this fed?). Gary Hart stalked down to ringside and *yanked* the belt out of Hall's hands while he was still in the ring. Hart: I TOLD all you muthas that I was gettin' sick and TIRED of all of this bullsh*t goin' on! Scott Hall, I'm gonna make an example out of your ass! Since you didn't *win* the belt like a champion, and you apparently can't *keep* it like a champion, I am *STRIPPING* - (Hart was cut off from delivering the obvious rest of his statement ("of the DEWF Intercontinental Title for conduct unbecoming a champion") by an irate Hall booting him in his ample midsection and delivering a *thundering* Outsider Edge to Hart! Damage Control rushed out to the ring as Hall spat on Hart and left) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- *3-WAY DANCE FOR THE DEWF WORLD TAG TEAM TITLES* Blondes 3:16 *challengers* vs. The Road Warriors *challengers* vs. The Razor Boys *champions* You kind of got a feel for how this one was gonna go early on as a mere one one minute forty nine seconds in, Hawk busted Pillman open on the guardrail. The LOD was *pissed off* (not that they're ever in a really good mood anyway) about what had happened to their stablemate Scott Hall, so that set the stage. Pillman frantically made the tag to partner Austin, who nailed Hawk with a right cross to the mush that did exactly one thing - made Hawk madder than he already was. You know the old line from Blazing Saddles "Don't shoot Mongo - you'll only make him mad"? That was what happened here. Hawk promptly heaved Austin over the top, where *he* hit his blad head against the rail, busting *him* open. But the first elimination came a mere six minutes in when The Razor Boys were double teaming Animal. Diamond held Animal in place, while Perez bounded off the ropes - whereupon Animal promptly ducked, and Perez nailed Diamond right in the mouth with a vicious spin kick! Diamond leapt to his feet, swearing a blue streak at Perez - and promptly got Stunnered by Austin (who wasn't even the legal man, Perez was). Perez turned around got nailed with a flying clothesline from Hawk! Say goodnight, Mister Perez! (elimination time:06:16) Austin stepped through the ropes, new found respect for the Warriors evident on his blood covered face. Hawk nodded in acknowledgement - and he and Austin began the single greatest exchange of punches since Ali-Foreman, which neither man came out on the good side of. The last punch was simultaneous by both men, and they both went down! As the crowd was going *nuts*, both men struggled to make the tag. Austin made the tag first, and Pillman stopped Hawk just short of the tag. He dropped Hawk with a backbreaker, went up top - and immediately regretted it when Hawk got a boot up and Pillman went face first right into it! Pillman crawled over, nearly unconscious and made the tag - while Hawk across the way tagged in Animal!!! Animal roared and *KEELHAULED* Austin with a clothesline. Pillman tried *his* luck, but got Gorilla Pressed over the top rope and to the *floor* for his troubles. Scratch Mr. Pillman. Animal wasted exactly no time. He tagged Hawk in, raised up Austin on his shoulders - WHAM! DOOMSDAY DEVICE!!! One - TWO - *THREE*!!!
