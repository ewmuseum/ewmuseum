Control Center ***SPECIAL REPORT***

Sep 3, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fed head : [detail withheld] (Open on a harried looking Tony Schiavone in the DEWF Control Center) Schiavone: Fans, something incredible has happened. Many fans have been inquiring into the whereabouts of former DEWF Intercontinental Champion 573 since his defeat in Tokyo at the hands of The Giant. We have *just received* word from that the former champion, who has been in seclusion since the time of that defeat, will return when he feels the time is right. BUT we have been asked to meet him in his manager "Sex Symbol" Travis Cook's Los Angeles penthouse apartment for an interview! We hope to get to the bottom of what's been going on and we hope to have a report for you on next week's show. For the DEWF, I'm Tony Schiavone. (fade to black)
