Slick Dick: We haven't heard too much of him of late, that's for sure. Brad Storch: Some of his friends, like Maverick, Angel, Irish Setter and King of Spades, have been getting more of the attention, so Badd has been taking a back seat for a little while. He turns around now, and whips Jones over his shoulder with a snap mare. Slick Dick: WHOA! A wrestling move! In MCW? Kickout by Litterbox Jones. That was pretty darned close to being a victory for Badd, though. Jones just kicked out of that in time. Badd is complaining to the referee about a slow count. Litterbox has Badd on the mat now, and he's stomping away at the mid-section of the Badd man. Slick Dick: This Jones guy packs a mean wallop. He'll do well in MCW. Brad Storch: That's for sure. He's really putting a clubbering on Badd right now. Slick Dick: HO! NO DUSTY RHODES IMPRESSIONS! I'M WARNING YOU! YOU SAY 'BELLY-WELLY' JUST ONCE, AND I'LL KILL YA, STORCH! Slick Dick: He might have some brain damage right there, if he doesn't have it from eating cat crap already. And Jones rolls out of the ring, partly from the impact of that slam, and partly to get a break from this beating. Badd going out after him now, and throwing him back in again. Slick Dick: The cat man looks WAY out of his depth here against the wily veteran. Brad Storch: Indeed he does, and Badd runs in and drops a leg right across the head of Jones. He covers, and the ref counts it... ONE!!! TWO!!!! NO!!!!! Close again, but no cigar. Twice already, Badd has come within a second of winning this match. He drags Jones up by his hair again, and whips him into the ropes - WHOA!!!! Slick Dick: Litterbox just ran full speed into the referee!!! The official is out cold! Brad Storch: LITTERBOX MISSED, AND BADD GRABS THE POOPER SCOOPER FROM HIM! HE NAILED HIM WITH IT!!! Slick Dick: **to fan behind him** Phew... clean yourself up, man, that stuff stinks! Brad Storch: BILLY SETS HIM UP.... BADD BOMB!!!! HE GOT IT!!! Slick Dick: And the official is shaking the cobwebs off... he sees the cover.... Brad Storch: His hand hits the mat.... ONCE!!!!! TWICE!!!!!! THREE TIMES!!!!!! ** DING DING ** Brad Storch: He GOT HIM! Billy U. Badd just pinned Litterbox Jones! This is a big comeback win for Badd! Slick Dick: Litterbox is a moron, though.
