Saturday Night Extreme for 09/06/1997!!! Part Two

Sep 15, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fed head : [detail withheld] (back from commercial break) Tony Schiavone : Folks, we have Craig DiGeorge back in the locker room with Michael Monroe. (Cut to the Locker room. Craig DiGeorge stands with a visibly distraught Michael Monroe, looking very much at the end of his rope. In the background, Succubus leans against the lockers, her face grim, shaking her head. The Crows, Raven, Justin Cross, Kevin Nash and various other wrestlers are muttering amongst themselves, looking uncomfortable.) CD : Michael Monroe, I know this is a rough time for you, but ... Micheal Monroe (his voice shaking) : You truly have a gift for understatement, Mr. DiGeorge. I have been through trauma in my time in the DEWF. I have been put in the hospital by Chris Canditio. I have seen ... Black Sarah taken out on a stretcher after an attack by Justin Hawk Bradshaw. But this ... this is ... Sullivan, you have no idea what you have done. To strike at one who has never done anything to you ... one so ... this is blood, Sullivan. Blood has stained your hands ... it can't be washed away ... soon you will find the blood you are drowning in is your own, Sullivan. I will not let this go unavenged, if I have to tear out you heart and feed it to you I will avenge her. There won't be enough of you left over for the Jackals to feed on when I am finished with you. This is blood, Sullivan ... CD : Michael, this doesn't bode well for your upcoming title defence against Gran Hamada - Monroe (grabbing DiGeorge's necktie and screaming) : DO YOU THINK I CARE ABOUT HAMADA? DO YOU THINK I CARE ABOUT F***ING HAMADA, DIGEORGE? DO YOU KNOW WHAT THAT WOMAN MEANS TO ME? (It takes the combined effort of Justin Cross, Raven, Tommy Dreamer, Eddie Guererro, Kevin Nash and 600lb Gorilla to peel Monroe off of Craig DiGeorge.) CD : *cough cough* Back to you, I guess , Tony. (Monroe slams a locker door shut and tears off out of the locker room.) Monroe (screaming) : SULLIVAN!!! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Schiavone: Fans, we understand that Gran Hamada has graciously agreed to postpone his title match with Monroe, given what's going on. We do have a standy match featuring - ("Iron Man" plays over the PA. Tony looks at the camera and mouths the words "What the fuck now?" Out from the back are Kevin Nash, Scott Hall, and the Road Warriors. Kevin and the Warriors carry their titles, Scott looks pissed) [TS] And what do I owe this honor (rolls his eyes) to? [SH] Tony mang, shut da hell up!! Da Bad Guy is pissed off! Hart, you think you can just take titles at will chico? Well I don't think so mang, and you know I WILL be in the battle for that belt, and I WILL win it back. [H] WEEEEEEEELL!!! It looks like we made good on our promise!! Razor Girls, you call us the Over the Hill Gang, well then, how does it feel to get beat, physically AND mentally, by the Over the Hill Gang!? You messed up big time when you didn't just be good little boys and thank us for the titles we gave you. Now you don't have your titles anymore!! OHHHHHH WHAT A RUSSSSSSHHHHHHH!!!! [A] RAZOR GIRLS!! YOU LOST IT!! You boys had it, and now you lost it!! You screwed up, and we pounded you and took the titles!! The titles that are RIGHTFULLY ours!! [H] And to Blondes 3:16, we must admit, despite the cheap heat, you boys can wrestle, and we have a new found respect for you, but you had to go and challenge us and spoil it! So we know you boys are tough, but you showed you're also just STUPID! You want to get extreme with the Triple Threat Match, fine, let's take it a step farther, a street fight, NO DQ, NO COUNTOUT!!! [A] And to Gary Hart, who is OBVIOUSLY trying to screw the LOD off, we all have a rousing..... [All] F*CK YOU!!!!!! [KN] You know, I'm sitting back there, and the rumor around the locker room is that they still haven't even FOUND anyone that wants to go after this title! But anyways, I'm sitting back there, and what do I hear but a scream and a loud crash, so I go running and find that it is Black Sarah. (Shakes his head, speaks very quietly) Kevin, ask the Giant what happens when you piss the LOD off like that. You see the LOD are the bad guys, we are the sh*t, but one thing we will never, NEVER do is to attack a woman. We f*cked up the Giant and friends because they did it, and you have to go and do it again. Well, all I can say is that if Monroe and his buddies don't f*ck you up enough, be sure that the Legion of Doom will be there to finish the job! And THAT's the bottom line, because the DE F'N WF WORLD CHAMP said so! ("Iron Man" plays and some of the crowd pops for the way they ended the interview, the majority boos though because it is still the LOD. They walk back to the dressing rooms looking quite serious as they do) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Schiavone: As I was saying, we do have a standby match in which The Undertaker will take on Hiroyoshi Tenzan! Let's go to that match! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Undertaker vs. Hiroyoshi Tenzan Tenzan was yet another competitor that came over from Japan after the DEWF tour - and immediately wished he hadn't. UT as expected pretty much romped over his opponent, and put Tenzan away with a Tombstone in a shade (ahem) under ten minutes. (match time:09:57) But ah, the post match festivities. Vader hit the ring and whipped UT into the buckle, and followed in with a Vaderlanche. Vader then began hitting UT with rapid fire rights and lefts - but then UT whipped *him* around and began giving him the same treatment. It was at about this point that The Usual Gang Of Idiots hit the ring and broke the two up. --------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF World Title Match* ? *challenger* vs. "Outsider" Kevin Nash (w/Paul Ellering) *champion* Nash hit the ring first, as determined as ever, taunting the fans every step of the way. But as he and Ellering were giving the fans the business, Nash's mystery opponent hit the ring to the *POP* of the night so far - PHIL LA FON??? La Fon clotheslined both Nash and Ellering from behind, and out Ellering went. Nash turned around and set himself to the task at hand. An opponent was an opponent, it made no difference who it was. Indeed, Nash used his considerable size advantage to its full - for about the first minute. La Fon used Nash's attempt at an over the shoulder backbreaker as a somersault to land on his feet behind Nash and planted the champ with a dragon suplex! Nash staggered to his feet and La Fon folded him up like an accordion with a kneelift to the midsection followed up with a DDT! La Fon mockingly put a scorpion deathlock on Nash, but that got broken up when Nash reached the ropes. La Fon whipped Nash into the ropes, but got reversed. No matter, he hit Nash right in the mouth with a back elbow off the middle rope on the rebound and Nash had to be wondering at that point "What the F*CK is going on here?". La Fon pressed his advantage for another couple minutes, but then got dropped off the ropes with a Nash lariat. To be truthful, Nash nailed the lariat out of sheer instinct. La Fon had done a pretty good job at that point of scrambling his brains but good. Nash went for the pin, but only got a two count. That was about all of LaFon for the next fifteen minutes. Nash battered his smaller opponent with every move in his considerable arsenal. But he made cardinal mistake. Every single time he had the chance to put La Fon away, he kept pulling him up. BAD move. The final minute saw Nash have no less than three opportunities for a pin - from a clothesline off the ropes, a backbreaker and his JackKnife power bomb. But he pulled La Fon up each time! Nash whipped La Fon in one last time - but got reversed! La Fon nailed Nash in the midsection with a *nasty* roundhouse kick that folded up the champ - then DROPPED him with a Tiger Driver! One - TWO - *THREE*!!!!!!! (match time:22:23) Announcer: Your winner, and NNNEEEWWWWWW DEWF World Heavyweight Championnn, PHILLL LA FONNNNNNN!!!! --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Schiavone: What an incredible night of action, fans! Kevin Nash can blame no one but himself and he knows it, for losing that match. Phil La Fon was a tag wrestler thrown into a title situation - and he took full advantage of Nash's sloppiness - and Kevin Nash knows it. Before we go, we want you to watch this piece of film from the site of the next DEWF Pay Per View, DEWF Rumble In The Jungle II. For the DEWF, I'm Tony Schiavone! (fade to a *nasty* looking area. It's very run down and you hear sirens everywhere. A voice comes up..) Voice: Boston. The Combat Zone. It's said that there are only two kinds of people in the Combat Zone - lunatics and corpses. Very often, they're the same thing. No sane person comes here if they can help it. (Paul E. steps in front of the camera) But then, nobody ever accused the DEWF of being sane, did they? (He walks off camera as the DEWF logo and the words "Rumble In The Jungle II - Live From The Combat Zone In Boston on October 4th, Only On Pay Per View" are slowly superimposed over the scene. That holds for a few seconds, and we then fade to black) ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
