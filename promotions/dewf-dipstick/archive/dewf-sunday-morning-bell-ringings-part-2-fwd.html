Sunday Morning Bell-Ringings Part 2!! (fwd)

Jan 26, 1997, 12:00:00 AM

Noah Raines: Well, we're back from that break. I think Dennis has wandered off to the merchandise stand again, so we'll start this match without him . . . ** The Monday Night Football theme plays ** Hubert Pubert: Announcing first, weighing in at 413 pounds, from the Bronx, and accompanied to the ring by Edna Todd, ELMO "COUCH POTATO" TODD!! ** The Todds come out carrying a small TV, a case of Idaho Spudwiser, and a whole lot of Cheetos. ** Noah Raines: I don't know what he plans to do with that TV. Watch the WWF, I guess. All I know is that this fed seems to have more than its share of fat drunks. Let's take it back down to Hubert Pubert. ** "I Just Can't Wait to be King" from the Lion King soundtrack plays ** Hubert Pubert: And his opponent, weighing in at 239 pounds, representing the Six People, from someplace he forgot, and accompanied to the ring by Maverick, Curtis Faust, Angel, Billy U. Badd, and The Irish Setter, KING OF SPADES! ** The Six People walk to the ring. ** Noah Raines: The Six People have already won one match here tonight and are going for another one. Well, it looks like the match is about to start. ** Ding-dong-ding-dong ** Noah Raines: Elmo Todd is sitting in the corner scarfing down Cheetos and watching that Touchless Car Wax infomercial on the TV with Edna. The King of Spades is coming over there and he . . . stops to watch TV? Apparently this infomercial has gotten a real cult following. They're showing the part where they set the police car on fire!!! COOL!!! Now Elmo looks up and sees his opponent. He gets up. Belly-to-back suplex by the Couch Potato! The King of Spades grabs him by the legs and pulls him down!! One . . . Two . . . Three . . NO!!! Edna Todd breaks the pin, with the pin. She has bonked the King of Spades over the head with the rolling pin!! Maverick and the rest of the Six People don't seem to like this as they go over to that corner. Maverick puts a Diamond Cutter on Edna Todd!! She's out for a while. Curtis Faust decides to switch the TV to PBS!! The Couch Potato is crying for Faust to turn it off and put on some real TV!!! The King of Spades gets up from the rolling-pin hit!! Enzuigiri on the Couch Potato!! He goes for the pin!!! One . . . Two . . . Kickout by Elmo Todd!! He wants to put the TV on the all-cockfighting channel, but the Six People are blocking the tuner!!! This is brutal!!! Everyone knows you can't make a couch potato watch PBS, even when "The Avengers" are on. Ooohh!!! Diana Rigg can kick really, REALLY high!!! ** Noah Raines goes into a Diana Rigg fantasy coma as Elmo "Couch Potato" Todd tosses Billy U. Badd away from the tuner. Angel, who is still blocking it, kicks him in the groin. The Irish Setter, also staring raptly at "The Avengers", bites Elmo Todd. Todd, realizing that he has rabies, starts screaming and trying to get Edna up. Edna gets up and bops the Setter, Billy U. Badd, and Maverick with the rolling pin and changes the channel to some nutso ultra-right talk show. ** Noah Raines: Oh, Diana . . . HEY!!! Who turned Diana Rigg off and put "Militia This Week" on? I mean, I'm a certified member of the lunatic fringe myself, but I want to see Diana Rigg, dammit!! Well, back to the match. The King of Spades is pouting in the middle of the ring!!! He apparently didn't want the channel changed, either. At least the whiny bastard has taste. Elmo "Couch Potato" Todd is going to mid-ring. The King of Spades bites him in the leg!! SMALL PACKAGE BY THE KING OF SPADES!!! The ref counts again. One . . . Two . . . Three . . . Well, it looks as if the Six People have put another notch in their belt. Let's go down to Hubert again. Hubert Pubert: Your winner, at 7:24, KING OF SPADES!!! Noah Raines: Where in the unholy name of Bill Gates is Dennis? Oh, well, the Six People are sticking around for this next match. Hubert should be announcing "The Referee" Curtis Faust's opponent right about now. ** "I'm Every Woman" by Aretha Franklin starts to play ** Hubert Pubert: "The Referee" Curtis Faust's opponent is, weighing in at 120 pounds, from Omaha, Nebraska, WIMPY FORD!!! ** Wimpy Ford comes out in full Urkel-esque nerd regalia ** Noah Raines: Well, Wimpy Ford doesn't look like much, but I'm sure that he's a good wrestler. Ford and Faust lock up. Faust easily wins the test of strength. Faust climbs to the top rope for a Frankensteiner on Ford!! He pins him!! One. . . Two . . . Wimpy Ford kicks out!! He goes for a flying cross-body block on Faust!! He just set himself up for a vicious sack-of-crap move by Faust. He's on the ground and he looks seriously annoyed. He's going for his finisher, the Wimpy Whack!! He cries for Faust not to hurt him!! Faust . . . AGREES!!! He backs off of Wimpy Ford and goes over to the referee. He kicks the copy of "Sesame Street Magazine" out of the referee's hand!! He climbs to the top rope!! He executes a Scorpio Splash on the ref!! WHAT'S GOING ON HERE??? The ref tries to call for the bell, but Curtis Faust plays some Sweet Groin Music on the ref's nutsack!! He tosses the referee out of the ring!!! The other five People are pounding on the referee as Curtis Faust calls for Wimpy Ford to hit him!!! Wimpy comes over and does just that!! Faust plays a few more bars of Sweet Groin Music on Wimpy and . . . CALLS FOR THE BELL?? He has disqualified Wimpy Ford!!! Hubert Pubert: Your winner, at 3:12, as the result of a disqualification, "THE REFEREE" CURTIS FAUST!!! Noah Raines: Well, the People have cleaned up here tonight. Now, security is clearing the ring as DEWF President Trillionaire Coot comes to announce the brackets for the Big Chrome Belt tournament to be held on Wednesday Night Weirdness live from the DipstickDome. . . Hubert Pubert: Everyone, welcome the President of Dipstick Championship E-Wrestling, TRILLIONAIRE COOT THOMPSON!!! ** The crowd pops muchly as Trillionaire Coot comes out in a hunter-green leisure suit with lots of gold chains around every part of his body. ** Trillionaire Coot: Hola, all of you fine folk here in Toadville, Washington!! ** The crowd starts to boo as Hubert Pubert corrects Trillionaire Coot ** Trillionaire Coot **holding a Frogtown Assassins baseball cap**: Oh, sorry, this is Frogtown, Oregon, home of my man Roddy Piper, who couldn't make it tonight, so I'll be doing this alone. Tonight we announce the first round pairings for the Big Chrome Belt Tournament to be held Wednesday in the DipstickDome!!! I will pick the first name out of the hat now . . . "THE REFEREE" CURTIS FAUST!!!! ** The crowd boos the heel ref/wrestler as he bows at ringside ** Trillionaire Coot: And his opponent shall be . . . "THE THINKER" LAWRENCE LOGIC!!!!! ** The intellectual gets scattered mixed reactions ** Trillionaire Coot: For the second match, the first wrestler shall be . . . ANGEL!!! ** She is catcalled as she poses for the crowd ** Trillionaire Coot: And her opponent is . . . THE BULL!!!! ** The crowd doesn't know who he is ** Trillionaire Coot: For the third match, the first wrestler shall be . . . MAVERICK!!! ** She doesn't bother to look up from her poker game with Billy U. Badd as she gets booed ** Trillionaire Coot: And her opponent is . . . MIGHTY MIGHTY UMPIRE!!!! ** The crowd is clueless ** Trillionaire Coot: For the final match, the first wrestler shall be . . . ELMO "COUCH POTATO" TODD!!!! ** He gets cheered as the people's champion ** Trillionaire Coot: And his opponent, "THE SCHOOLGIRL" KATHY BRIGGS!!! ** The crowd is again unknowing ** Trillionaire Coot: Well, those are the brackets for the tournament. I'll give the mike over to Hubert Pubert so he can do the introductions for our main event, Maverick versus "Substitute TA" Bonnie Burns. ** Trillionaire Coot leaves as "Another Brick In The Wall (Pt. 2)" by Pink Floyd begins to play ** Hubert Pubert: Introducing first, weighing, uh, not much, from Brian Bosworth Community College in Rathole, Oklahoma, "SUBSTITUTE TA" BONNIE BURNS!!!! ** "Substitute TA" Bonnie Burns comes out in typical schoolteacher garb, except that her dress has only one armhole. Why? She only needs one! ** Noah Raines: I'm getting seriously fed up with this!! Where in the ** he hits the bleep button ** is Dennis? Well, they say Substitute TA is a real high-flyer. Well, she'd better be, you can't grapple on the mat with one arm. I think Hubert is ready to introduce Maverick now. ** "Seven Seas of Rhye" by Queen fills the loudspeakers ** Hubert Pubert: And her opponent, weighing, uh, a bit more, from San Francisco, representing the Six People and accompanied to the ring by Angel, King of Spades, Curtis Faust, The Irish Setter, and Billy U. Badd, MAVERICK!!!! ** The Six People stroll down the aisle dragging someone else ** Noah Raines: What is this? They're carrying someone to the ring who's bound and gagged. I think it's the Sensei of the Jobroni Corporation, Crip Bodydonna!! IT IS CRIP BODYDONNA!!! Maverick takes the mike from Hubert Pubert and is about to speak! Maverick: Well, well, if it isn't that one-armed teacher. I believe you'll notice that your sensei is tied up here and realize that you pose no threat to me. Olga Piddledonna, you are not the Queen of that other federation!! I AM!!! You're just some tart with a swollen rear and I have your man to prove it!! Crip Bodydonna ** bound and gagged **: MmrmphhhuhDestroygjhgManhuhuhpshalljpmmfpay!!! Maverick: Now, let's start the match, shall we? ** the other five People carry Crip off ** ** Ding-Dong-Ding-Dong ** Noah Raines: Well, it looks like Maverick and Substitute TA are locking up. Maverick slugs TA in the face with her free hand and goes for an arm-drag takedown. The referee counts. One . . . Two . . . Three . . NO!! Substitute TA is up and she's headed for the turnbuckle. She's climbing the turnbuckle!! Shooting star press right on the back of Maverick!!! Maverick doesn't go down!! She's got Substitute TA in a bearhug!! The referee is checking to see if TA will submit. She won't!!! She wiggles out of the bearhug and nails Maverick with a Yakuza kick!! Maverick still will not go down!!! Where is that horny idiot Dennis? He'd enjoy this! ** Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly returns, carrying a rather familiar large round object in a bowling bag. ** Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: Look what I sto -- uh, found! Noah Raines: I don't care if you stole the moon, Dennis. Where in the heck have you been? Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly ** very softly **: Uh, the Six People's locker room. Noah Raines: Well, if you won't tell me now, I'll make you tell Coot later when he fires you. Let's get back to the match, shall we? Substitute TA outside the ropes going for her finishing move, the FrankenTeacher!!! She gets her legs around Maverick's neck, but can't take her down!!! Maverick is pulling her off!!! Maverick is going for The Final Ace!!! She's got it on!! Substitute TA screams "AUNT!!!"! It's over!!!!! Hubert Pubert: Your winner by submission at 8:39, MAVERICK!!!! ** The Six People began to celebrate in mid-ring by knocking Crip Bodydonna around as Noah turns to Dennis in the booth. ** Noah Raines: So, Dennis, what'd you find? Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: This. ** takes Lucille out of her bowling bag ** Noah Raines: But that's Mike Webber's bowling ball!!! Maverick stole that from Mike in that other federation!!! You'd better give that back to Maverick before the People pound us. Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: Nothing doing. This is going on my mantle right next to Arnold Palmer's driver and John McEnroe's racket. Noah Raines: You won't give it back? Well, then, we'd better get out of here. ** The screen fades to the credits: "DEWF, a division of the Jobroni Corporation, copyright 1997. http://members.tripod.com/~Coot/DEWF.html " as Noah and Dennis get the hell out of Frogtown. **
