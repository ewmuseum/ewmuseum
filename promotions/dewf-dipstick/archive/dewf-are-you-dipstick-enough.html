DEWF!!! ARE YOU DIPSTICK ENOUGH!?!

Feb 7, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you tired of "hardcore" this and "extreme" that? Sick of gimmicks that are actually good? Really annoyed with writing serious, portentous, pretentious RPs? Then I have the fed for you! Dipstick Championship E-Wrestling is a parody federation in the grand tradition of our arch-rivals at Mentally Challenged Wrestling, who just so happen are going bankrupt, have announcers no-showing, and a leader who secretly wants to join this organization, according to their brother's dog. As part of our schtick involves being their rival, I really don't want any workers that are already there and not planning to leave (unless your guys/gals are in need of a little extra mat time and they're good anyway), so if you're a MCW Freak, try topping your previous creation here. Let's show them that our guys are even more freaky and pathetic than theirs!!! There will be two weekly live cards (written by me and my sole co-writer Scott, I might need more writers later), Sunday Morning Bell-Ringings and Wednesday Night Weirdness. If you find yourself losing to Dwayne Casey Malone (official DEWF/MCW jobroni) in the opening matches at these shows, it's a reminder that you need to RP something badly before people start calling your character "that guy off the turnip truck." :) This WILL NEVER happen to you as long as you write something during your run here and at least do something to try to get into our infrequent PPVs. As far as RPs go, I want to see good ones, funny ones, ones that establish killer characters and I'm sure there will be some of those. But there will probably be some guy who thinks TaLkInG LiKe ThIs is a gimmick and forgets to RP, as well. Warning to anyone who doesn't want to write at least once in a green to remind me that you're not a jobber: You will die in a goofy gimmick match at the hands of "The Substitute TA" Bonnie Burns, Harry Horowitz, or DCM, who will NEVER EVER win over somebody who RPs, even if it's "EYE WILL WYN!!" every fourth even Tuesday. This fed will have four titles: The DEWF World Title, known as the "Big Chrome Belt."; The Bronzed Prosthetic Leg, a secondary singles title; The Ox-Yoke of Exceptional Teamwork, our tag team titles; and the DEWF Ladies' Championship Girdle. These will be filled whenever enough workers show up. Hopefully, this will actually get some of you interested in joining this e-fed. For character sheets and guidelines (there really aren't many of those. After all, the gimmicks are supposed to be outlandish), mail me at [detail withheld] . -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Well, here you go. This is the Official Dipstick Construction Kit(TM). Only three guidelines exist for characters. 1: I'm on a college gateway, so blatant racial, sexist, or homophobic slurs stop at my machine and go bye-bye immediately. Any gimmick that a normal individual with a soul would find absolutely repugnant won't go here. You can be outlandishly stereotypical (a la my jobber gangsta tag team the Ebonic Plague or MCW's Holden McGroin), just try not to seriously offend me. I laugh at dead baby and O. J. jokes and am definitely not easily offended, so this shouldn't be a problem with anything. 2: No finishers that any feasible human or alien medical staff couldn't fix. This applies to MK fatalities, decapitations, brain-sucking, and other things that are irreversibly fatal, even in Ariloulaleelay space. They can be goofy, insane, and physically impossible, but they must be survivable. 3: This is a parody fed!!!
