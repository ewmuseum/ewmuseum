Saturday Night Extreme for 10/11/97!!!

Oct 15, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fed head ; [detail withheld] (We open on a very somber Tony Schiavone standing in the middle of the ring. The mutants for once, are silent.) Schiavone: Good evening. As many of you know, a week ago this past Sunday "Flyin'" Brian Pillman passed away. At this time, I'd like to introduce to you the owner and operator of the Dream Extreme Wrestling Federation, Mr. Clay Evans. (Evans emerges from the back, to the respectful applause of the mutants in attendance) Evans: Hello. You know, it's hard sometimes to remember that these guys are just normal human beings. You see them in here, acting like they're about six fries short of a happy meal (crowd chuckles) and you forget. Then something like this happens and it's forced home on you. I won't bore you by reciting the facts of Brian's career that you haven't heard ten million times already since last weekend. I will tell you that the rest of this *month* in the DEWF is dedicated to the memory of Brian Pillman. (Some applause) So Brian my man, THIS MONTH'S FOR YOU!!! (Crowd goes BUGF*CK with cheers and we go to a very special DEWF opening montage, dedicated to Pillman set to the tune of "F*cking Hostile" by Pantera, his DEWF theme song. When it's over, Tony is still in the ring and the mutants are going *nuts* with chants of "DEWF! DEWF! DEWF!". "Dead Souls" by Joy Division plays over the arena PA system as Justin Cross makes his way to the interview area. He is alone.) Tony Schiavone : Justin Cross, one can't help note the fact that you are on your own this evening. Justin Cross : You put it very mildly, Tony. First, my manager Black Sarah was struck down in a cowardly attack by the Neighborhood of Evil. Then, on a night when I was not there to watch his back, my friend and partner Michael Monroe was taken apart in a combined attack by the Neighborhood of Evil and the Royal Assassins. Now I am all that is still standing of the Fear Machine. Well, tonight, the Fear Machine starts taking back what is ours. First victim : Chris Candito. Skip, you tried to destroy the Fear Machine before, and not only did you fail, you were run out of town. The last time you faced me, I destroyed you. So now you have to affiliate yourself with that cowardly pack of Jackals, the Neighborhood of Evil, to seek the revenge that you yourself were not man enough to achieve. You put the best friend I have out of action, and aim to get pay back by breaking as many of your bones as possible. And when I am done that, Mr. "No Cojones Necessary", I'm going to leave just on little tiny bit of you left unbroken. And do you know why I'm going to do that Chris? Not so that your girlfriend will have something to remember you by on days when it isn't so sunny. No, I'm doing that because you failed. If you think that you have seen the last of the Shadow, you are sorely mistaken. And when he returns ... which he will do when he has recovered and is good and ready ... there will be that little bit of you left, so that he can finish the job and destroy you. After Candito, we will work our way through the Neighborhood of Evil until we have broken them all, and then we will corner Kevin Sullivan and trap him like the rat he is, and we will rend him limb from limb until there won't be anything left for his *cough* Woman to identify. And once we are done that ... Lawler, make peace with your gods, little man, you are next. We are patient, Schiavone, and we don't care how long it takes ... but justice will be served! (Cross steps back and "Boogieman" by White Zombie rains down from the PA and the crowd is rockin' as the former tag champs emerge from the back with Raven... carrying a scarecrow on a cross, like you'd see in a cornfield??? Tony pokes at the straw filled thing) Gentlemen, I have to ask you - what *is* this thing? Crow 1 : (smiles grimly) I'm surprised at you, Tony. Haven't you ever seen a scarecrow before? Schiavone: Yes, but - Crow 2 : Tony Schiavone, this is what will be the instrument of Kevin Sullivan's ultimate destruction. This tattered scarecrow will avenge the Shadowchilde and she who walked in blackness, as will all of the Darkside. But when the shadow falls again, to paraphrase the poet, there will be "blood on the scarecrow, blood on the crown". Kevin Sullivan... Jerry Lawler... your doom is coming. Beware the shadows, scum. Raven : So it is written, so it shall come to pass. (Justin Cross takes the scarecrow and *spikes* the pointed bottom of the cross into the mat. The lights go out - and when they come back up Raven, The Crows and Justin Cross are all gone. Schiavone looks very shaken, and leaves the ring) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- 911 vs. "The Giant" Paul Lauria (w/Good Ol' J.R.) WELLLLLL *that* was fun, wasn't it? In probably the fastest match in DEWF history, 911 booted Lauria in the midsection and *planted* him with the Chokeslam in exactly seven seconds flat. (match time:00:07) But of course, here came "Hulk" Johnson from the back and he clipped Al's legs out from under him. He and Lauria were busily kicking the hell out of the big man when from the back came the masked man again, and the 2Wo bailed out but fast. MAN that guy had big arms... ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- *TRIANGLE MATCH FOR THE VACANT DEWF INTERCONTINENTAL TITLE* The Giant vs. Masahiro Chono vs. "Outsider" Scott Hall (w/Paul Ellering) Giant was the first man out, and the crowd gave *THUNDERING* chants of "Pillman! Pillman! Pillman!". Chono was next, also to respectful applause, followed by Hall and it was ON! Hall made his entrance by climbing to the top buckle from behind the Giant and executing a flying shoulderblock into the Giant's knees *from behind*, clipping the big man's legs out from underneath him! Hall followed up with an elbowsmash, seconded by a Chono elbow. Chono went for the cover on Giant, but got heaved clean out of the ring by Giant kicking out! Giant got to his feet - and went right back down again by a belly to belly suplex by Hall! Chono, back in the ring, locked Giant into a "octopus" style abdominal stretch ala Antonio Inoki. But The Giant was simply too big to lock it on properly, and the hold lasted a mere seven seconds. Chono staggered back from Giant getting out of the hold - right into an Outsider Edge from Hall! One, two, three! (match time:05:37) But there was still The Giant for Hall to deal with. Hall had two realistic courses open to him. One, go for the quick kill. Two, and the smarter one, use his speed advantage to stay out of the big man's reach and wear him down. Hall took the second course, and it payed off. Hall spent the next 15 plus minutes wearing Wight down, bit by bit. Yeah, Giant got his shots in, but Hall always recovered, and the extended time was taking it's toll on the big man. Then at about the 21:45 mark, Hall heaved The Giant out of the ring and distracted referee Slappy White. Out came Kevin Nash from the crowd - who brought the frickin' ROOF down by *JACK-KNIFING* the big man on the floor! Nash spatula-d Wight off the floor and rolled him back in the ring - one - two - THREE! (match time:22:24) Announcer: "Your winner, and NNNEEWWWW DEWF Intercontinental Champion - "Outsider" Scott Hallllll!!!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Schiavone is standing in the ring) Schiavone: Congratulations to Scott Hall for his big win. Fans, as you know, Scott Hall gave the Outsider Edge to Gary Hart some weeks ago and, regretfully, Gary will not be able to return to his duties as DEWF Commissioner. So, this morning, the DEWF Executive Board met in session to determine the new DEWF Commissioner. I'd like to introduce him at this time. He IS the thirteen time (crowd starts practically drowning Tony out with THUNDERING cheers) world heavyweight champion ("Also Sprach Zarathustra" begins playing as the crowd *LOSES* it). Ladies and gentlemen, the NEW DEWF Commissioner - THE "NATURE BOY" RIICCC FLAIIIIRRR!!!!! (The crowd is genuflecting their asses off as "The Nature Boy" struts his way down to ringside, "WHOOOOO"ing all the way. Flair hops in the ring with Tony) "Nature Boy", or should I say Commissioner Flair, welcome to the DEWF. (***POP***) Flair: Tony, you and I have known each other longer than some of the people in this audience have been alive. (Crowd laughs) So *you* can call me - Commissioner Flair. (Crowd laughs again) Gotcha! The first thing I wanna do is address a problem that's been happening way too much for my taste. I always have been the dirtiest player in the game. But everybody and his mother in this federation thinks it's alright to lay their hands on officials, even announcers like poor Joey Styles. (Crowd boos) As of right now, any wrestler in the DEWF who lays a hand on anyone other than another wrestler - nah, I'm not gonna fire you. I'll fine you, kick your ass myself - and THEN I'll fire you! (****POP**** of the gods) To paraphrase one tough son of a bitch - that's the bottom line, because Commissioner Flair SAID so! WHOOOOOOOOO!!!! (Flair leaves to the megamaxiPOP of the mutants) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Exorcist by Killing Joke blasts over the arena PA as Kevin Sullivan leads his men to the interview area.) Tony Schiavone : Kevin Sullivan, in my years as a broadcast journalist and as a wrestling fan, I have seen you pull some despicable stunts in your time, but in your actions the past few weeks, you have out done even yourself. I have never seen a more disgusting series of ambush attacks in my life, and I for one am appalled that I am even working for the same company as you do. KS (grinning like a hyaena) : Why thanks you very much, Mr. Schiavone, you are too kind. And all this time, I thought you didn't like me. But all flattery aside, I think I managed to get a message across, wouldn't you agree? It's always such a good feeling to mix business with pleasure. Now, after tonight, our first order of business, the destruction of the Fear Machine, will be complete, and we can move onto our second objective ... the eviceration of the Dark Side! Cross, you are the last man standing and it won't take too long for you to go down. Chris Candito : Cross, the last time we met you may have won the count, but I left you battered in the middle of the ring. If it wasn't for your punk partner Monroe, I would have finished you off then and there. Well, I took Shadowboy out for good this time, and now you have nobody to hide behind. Well, I'm going to put you in the hospital bed right beside him and you can talk about old times and the careers you might have had if you hadn't been so stupid as to cross ... heh heh heh ... the path of the perfect male specimen, yours truly, Chris Candito. TS : Sullivan, what did you mean about mixing business with pleasure ? KS : It should be obvious by now, Mr. Schiavone. You see, I said when I first arrived in the DEWF, that I was brought here by a friend. A friend who wanted me to help deal with a problem of his. That's business. It turned out that this problem became enough of a personal annoyance to me that I enjoyed eliminating the problem. That's pleasure. And so I owe a debt of gratitude to the man who gave me the chance to shed even more blood, the chance to deal with a thorn in my side as well as his ... Jerry "The King" Lawler! (The crowd boos ferociously as "Red" by King Crimson plays over the arena PA system, heralding Jerry Lawler and and the Royal Assassins procession to the interview area. A few fans throw cups and garbage at them and try to jump the guardrail to get at them, but are held back by security. The Royal Assassins and the Neighborhood of Evil all shake hands and slap each other on the back.) JL : Yes, Tony Schiavone, it was me! I was the genius who brought Kevin Sullivan and the Neighborhood of Evil to the DEWF. You see, when I first arrived in the DEWF, I brought a bunch of untried nobodies named the Fear Machine with me to see if they could make a name for themselves. Well, they turned out to be the most disloyal pack of ingrates I've ever encountered ... and I'm from Memphis! Well, when they turned their backs on the man who brought them to where they are today, I decided to be patient ... to let them feel more secure, and then to hit them when and where they were least expecting it ... because that way i would enjoy it more! And thanks to my friend Kevin Sullivan, I've achieved just that! Cross, Dark Side, this is your final curtain ... to quote one of your own, take your bow, and exit stage left! (The rest of the NOE and the Assassins do exit, but Sullivan and Candido stay behind as "Dead Souls" by Joy Division fills the arena and down comes Justin Cross, carrying the scarecrow from earlier...?) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- "The Ivory Tower" Justin Cross vs. Chris Candido (w/Kevin Sullivan) Candido nearly ended the match as soon as it started by reversin a whip into the ropes and nailing Cross with a powerslam for a two count. Candido followed up by throwing Cross outside the ring and whipping him into the guardrail. Candido threw Cross back in, slammed him, and nailed a flying legdrop for yet another two count. Candido followed up in quick succession with a dropkick and an elbowdrop. Candido went for another - but Cross rolled out of the way. Perhaps sensing trouble, Candido's stablemates Halloween and Hayabusa came down to ringside and made a great show of sitting quietly at ringside and minding their own business. Cross began beating the living daylights out of Candido, seemingly intent on caving in Candido's ribcage with rights and lefts to the body. Candido recovered long enough to throw Cross outside and then distracted the referee, allowing Hayabusa to *spike* Cross on the concrete with a DDT. Cross barely beat the count back in, and Candido got yet another two. It would have no doubt been a three, but Cross' foot was on the rope. Candido went for a piledriver, but held it too long and Cross backdropped him out of it. Cross hauled Candido to his feet and dropped him right back down again with a reverse neckbreaker! Candido still wasn't done though, and tried a dropkick - which failed miserably when Cross swatted him away like a mosquito. Candido tried to beg off, then suckered Cross into a whip off the ropes which backfired when *he* got nailed right in the mush with a flyhing forearm by Cross! Then Cross mocked Candido by planting *him* with a piledriver! Cross made the sign for the Falling Tower (Razor's Edge from the top), and set Candido on the top turnbuckle. Which, of course, served as a cue for the NOE and The Royal Assassins to hit the ring and beat the snot out of Cross. As usual, the miserable cowards ran like hell when the Dark Side hit the ring, making the sides even vaguely even. The Dark Side chased them out the back of the arena... and down came Commissioner Flair??? Flair: LAWLER! SULLIVAN! That's ENOUGH! You punks may have started this, but by God, "The Nature Boy" is gonna end it! At Rumble In The Jungle II, there WILL BE a WAR GAMES match (********POP*********) between The Dark Side - WHOOOO - The Fear Machine - WHOOOO - The Royal Assassins and The Neighborhood Of Evil! If by chance you don't like it, you can KISS my WHITE ASS!!! WHOOOOOO!!!! (Crowd LOSES it as Flair stomps off) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF WORLD TAG TEAM TITLES MATCH* The 2Wo (Hulk Johnson and Diesel, w/Good Ol' J.R.) *challengers* vs. The Road Warriors (Animal and Hawk, w/Paul Ellering) *champions* This was.. well, bizarre to say the least. The Roadies, for once, couldn't overpower their opponents, so they found themselves the faster team in the ring. Is it just me, or is there something wrong with that picture? :) The 2Wo aquitted themselves well (and I didn't even know they'd been charged - badumpCHING), but the end came at about the twenty four minute mark when Diesel had Animal set up for the JackKnife - and clotheslined out of his boots by Hawk, who came off the top rope! (match time:24:27) >From the back came the Powers Of Pain, who engaged in a staredown with the LOD before The Usual Gang Of Idiots (tm, Scott Kieth) broke it up before anything could happen. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE REMATCH* Phil La Fon *challenger* vs. Kevin Nash (w/Paul Ellering) *champion* Yet another case of WELLLLlllll THAT was fun, wasn't it? Nash whomped, stomped and romped over La Fon, planting him with the JackKnife in just under three minutes. (match time:02:47) Afterwards, Nash grabbed the mic. Nash: Oh, come ON! Is this jabroni the best you can come up with back there? Gimme some real competition, for chrissakes! (Out from the back comes Commissioner Flair) Flair: Big man, it seems to me like you need a little attitude adjustment. (Nash and Ellering make like "Ooooo.. we're SO scared") So I'm gonna give you the rest of the month off until Rumble In The Jungle. (Nash is like "Hey, I can live with that") You know why? I *just signed* you opponent for that night, and big man, you're gonna have all the competition you can handle. Nash: Well, "Nature Boy", who is it? (Nash, Ellering AND the crowd go stark raving out of their frigging minds as we "OH! OH, SHAWN!" blares over the PA and HERE COMES "THE HEARTBREAK KID" SHAWN MICHAELS TO RINGSIDE) Michaels: Big man, I've been listening to you make a royal ass out of yourself out here and it's gonna be MY pleasure to once again KICK YOUR SEVEN FOOT ASS!!! (Poppity-pop-pop-pop) At Rumble In The Jungle, it is just gonna be too SWEEEEETTTTTT!!!! (Nash and Ellering lose it again, right along with the crowd, as Flair and Michaels leave) Schiavone: (from the crows nest) Fans, what a turn of events we have here! Join me for a very special edition of Saturday Night Extreme this weekend where we will have for you the complete lineup of Rumble In The Jungle II, which will coming to you live from Boston's infamous Combat Zone! (fade to a piece of video. It's black and white, and obviously set in Boston's Combat Zone. We show clips and hear sound bites of news reports about all the bad craziness that usually goes on here) Voice: The Combat Zone. One of the most violent, totally out of control places in the world. (more sound clips and video of news reports) They say you have to be one of two things to be in the Combat Zone. (more clips) You're either dead, or out of your f'n mind. (Paul E. Dangerously steps from behind a burning car, obviously the voice) Paul E.: Well... we ain't dead. As for the rest? (smashes in one of the of burning's cars windows with a crowbar) Do the math. (fade to the logo for "DEWF Rumble In The Jungle II - Welcome To My Nightmare". Fade to black) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- -- 69th degree Bewilderbeest of the Serengetti Tradition Lover of the 318th degree Fieldmouse of Nimh of the Serengetti Tradition All hail the Zebra of the Nth degree!
