Saturday Night Extreme for 09/27/1997 (Tape delayed) *cough* :)

Oct 2, 1997, 12:00:00 AM

Fed head : [detail withheld] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- --- KS : One more thing. The last time we faced the Darkside, the Whore of Babylon sought to defile my physical person. I could say that I was going to take this out on her personally ... but I find other methods are more effective. She might be able to handle damage done to herself ... but what about someone she cares about? Her familial blood spilled? Think about it, dear, remember when you are weeping your eyes out that not only did you bring it on yourself, you brought it on your family as well. You can run, but you can't hide. *snip* Schiavone: Ooookkaayyyy! Fans, when we come back (Tony hears something in his earpiece) OH MY GOD!! QUICK - THE LOCKER ROOMS!!! (We cut to the back where through shaky camera work we see the dressing room door open and (of all damn people) Kevin Nash screaming for an ambulance. The camera guy pushes through - and we see *Black Sarah* hanging half out of a locker, bloody literally covering her entire face and her PVC suit in tatters. Blood is everywhere as we see Michael Monroe looking catatonic in the corner, sobbing at the sight. Suddenly, The Crows rush in and shove the cameraman out the door as Damage Control carts Black Sarah off to the hospital. We see a blood covered note fall out of Sarah's hand and the camera zooms in on it) "Whore Of Babylon - Tsk, tsk, m'dear. It's not NICE to fool with Mother Nature. Nor is it nice to stick your damned nose in where mortals fear to tread. All you hold dear will be claimed by the sacred river Alf - one by one by one. The delicious thing is, there's nothing you can do about it. You have deigned to interfere with the Neighborhood Of Evil. Suffer the consequences, bitch. 666-Cuddles-666, Kevin and the boys..." *snip* (Cut to the Locker room. Craig DiGeorge stands with a visibly distraught Michael Monroe, looking very much at the end of his rope. In the background, Succubus leans against the lockers, her face grim, shaking her head. The Crows, Raven, Justin Cross, Kevin Nash and various other wrestlers are muttering amongst themselves, looking uncomfortable.) CD : Michael Monroe, I know this is a rough time for you, but ... Micheal Monroe (his voice shaking) : You truly have a gift for understatement, Mr. DiGeorge. I have been through trauma in my time in the DEWF. I have been put in the hospital by Chris Canditio. I have seen ... Black Sarah taken out on a stretcher after an attack by Justin Hawk Bradshaw. But this ... this is ... Sullivan, you have no idea what you have done. To strike at one who has never done anything to you ... one so ... this is blood, Sullivan. Blood has stained your hands ... it can't be washed away ... soon you will find the blood you are drowning in is your own, Sullivan. I will not let this go unavenged, if I have to tear out you heart and feed it to you I will avenge her. There won't be enough of you left over for the Jackals to feed on when I am finished with you. This is blood, Sullivan ... CD : Michael, this doesn't bode well for your upcoming title defence against Gran Hamada - Monroe (grabbing DiGeorge's necktie and screaming) : DO YOU THINK I CARE ABOUT HAMADA? DO YOU THINK I CARE ABOUT F***ING HAMADA, DIGEORGE? DO YOU KNOW WHAT THAT WOMAN MEANS TO ME? (It takes the combined effort of Justin Cross, Raven, Tommy Dreamer, Eddie Guererro, Kevin Nash and 600lb Gorilla to peel Monroe off of Craig DiGeorge.) CD : *cough cough* Back to you, I guess , Tony. (Monroe slams a locker door shut and tears off out of the locker room.) Monroe (screaming) : SULLIVAN!!! (cut to the DEWF opening montage and the mutants are tearing the roof off for Tony Schiavone and the DEWF) Schiavone: WELCOME, wrestling fans to the DREAM EXTREME WRESTLING FEDERATION!!! (Crowd *BEPOPALOOLAS*) At this time - ("Exorcism" by Killing Joke blares over the Arena PA system, as Kevin Sullivan makes his wayto the interview area, Hayabusa, Joe Malenko, Halloween and the Powers of Pain close behind him. Kevin Sullivan is already laughing Maniacally.) Tony Schiavone : Kevin Sullivan, I think we al know that you were responsible for the disgusting actions perpetrated on Black Sarah last week. KS : Mr. Schiavone, if you think I am somehow to blame for Karma having an awfully wide range, you are mistaken. A certain woman seemed to be under the impression that she could lay hands on me and escape retribution. Not only was she mistaken, she had no idea how the retribution would take form. Not only did she bring her sins down upon herself, she brought them down upon her family. The Darkside, the Fear Machine, they claim to deal with dark forces. Well, you don't mess with dark forces, or you'll get yourself in over your head, and their are no forces darker than the Neighborhood of Evil! TS : You may be bringing retribution on yourself, I mean not only do you have the Darkside and the Fear Machine after you, you've upset the Legion of Doom. KS : Do you think I care about the Legion of Doom, Mr. Schiavone? The Legion of Doom and I go back a long way, and I am one of the few people who can claim to have engineered their downfall. Nash, if you don't like what I can do to a woman, you'd hate to see what I can do to a man! I've got two big bruisers here who nearly ended the carreer of Road Warrior Animal, and theyd love to get the chance to end your carreer. If you want to find somebody suicidal enough to join you against the Powers of Pain, bring him on. And as for the Fear Machine, well we have someone who knows all about young Mr. Monroe and is very interested in seeing him again. ("Beautiful Ones" by Suede plays over the arena PA as Chris Canditio, dressed in black trunks and a black robe, walks to the intervew area.) CC : Guess what, Monroe? I'M BAAACK! You hought you'd seen the last of me? Wishful thinking, Shadowboy. I put you in the hospital before, and I'm more than happy to help Kevin Sullivan do it again. You lay down the gauntlet to the Neighborhood of Evil, well you never know who is going to pick it up! (The Neighborhood of Evil return to the locker room, Sullivan and Canditio laughing all the way) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Masato Yakushiji vs. "The Giant" Paul Lauria (w/Good Ol' J.R.) The crowd was laughing hysterically as Lauria came down to ringside, threatening everyone and his mother with his "devastating chokeslam". Even Yakushiji was obviously trying to keep from cracking up. Lauria scored a couple of impressive moves right off the bat, dropping Yakushiji with a front slam and an elbowdrop off the ropes. Buuuuutttt that was about it because at that point "Frankenstein" by The Edgar Winter Group came over the PA - 911 IN DA HAYOUSSSEEE! :) The big man stepped over the top rope as Yakushiji wisely bailed out the other side, while 911 grabbed Lauria by the throat. Ross unwisely hit 911 in the back with a chair. Unwise because 911 (Lauria was still in his hand, mind you) merely reacted as if he'd been bothered by a fly. Ross tried his luck again - bad move. Bad move because 911 promptly spun around, dragging Lauria in his massive right hand - and grabbed *Ross* by the throat as the crowd went *nuts*. Only the timely appearance of "Hulk" Johnson saved the day for the 2Wo. Johnson kicked 911 in the - um - from behind, forcing him to drop Ross and Lauria. Johnson was in the process of dropping "Da Big Leg Bruvva" on 911 repeatedly when from the back came an enormous masked man, all in black, who threw Lauria over the top rope like a discus, Ross *through* the ropes and "Hulk" he whipped into the ropes and gave a big boot to the face, which sent "Hulk" out to the floor. The big man helped 911 to the back, while everyone wondered just who in the hell he was. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Suddenly, over the video wall came - D-Von : Can I get a WITness from the congreGAtion?? Buh-Buh: Hi honey, we're home! (laughs as the crowd goes *bananas*. Then, from behind the curtain comes Joel Gertner and The Dudley Boys as they head for the ring) Gertner: Ladies and gentlemen, I give you the finest wrestling family on the face of the earth, The Dudley's! And you thought the DEWF was extreme before? How could you possibly be extreme without us? That's right you can't. Of course, as I'm, sure you all know by now, these fine grapplers have Never been defeated on points! They are undefeated, and of course will remain that way for a long time. We open a challenge to any wrestler, single or tag team. The challenge is this: Challenge us to a match. If you can beat us on points, then we will leave the DEWF. I think we'll be here for a long time. Buh-Buh: Any takers? (All three laugh) You know, a wise man once said "be careful what you ask for - you just might get it." Get it they did because no sooner had the words left Buh-Buh's mouth than "Natural Born Killaz" blared over the PA and HERE CAME THE GANGSTAZ!!! The crowd went *nuclear* as The Dudleys bailed out, deftly avoiding getting the living crap beaten out of them. New Jack grabbed the mic as the crowd was just flat losing it. New Jack: Well, WELL! Look what we got us HERE!! Look to me like Moe, Larry and Curly din' want NO damn part of The Gangstaz! Well son, we turned the heat up, jus' a lil' bit. We gon' hit yo' ass wit' bricks an' sticks, an' bottles an' trash can lids. Why? `Cause The Gangstaz gettin' ready to ROCK the DEW-muthaf*ckin'-F one MO' time! (Mustafa grabs the mic and does The Mustafa Evil Laugh (tm) as "Natural Born Killaz" starts up again) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Headbangers (Mosh and Thrasher) vs. The bWo ("Big" Stevie Cool and Da Blue Guy) The mutants were on their feet for both teams, and the 'Bangers weren't about to let their DEWF debut go to waste. They domianted the first two minutes before Da Blue Guy reversed a whip off the ropes and nailed Thrasher with a fist to the midsection as he tagged out to Big Stevie Cool. The crowd went *nuts* as BSC and Da Blue Guy stuff Jack Knifed Thrasher. Stevie left the ring and snagged a ball bat, returned to the ring, and took a swipe at Thrasher with it - who ducked and grabbed the bat himself. Thrasher nailed Stevie right between the eyes with the ball bat, and Stevie began bleeding profusely. Thrasher tagged back out to Mosh and the two double back suplexed Stevie. But the crowd *really* lost it when the 'Bangers double whipped Stevie into the ropes and Mosh used Thrasher as a springboard to give Stevie a flying clothesline on the rebound! It wasn't until past the seven and a half minuet mark that Stevie was able to get much of anything going. Mosh had just whipped him hard into one turnbuckle and preceeded to try it again. It backfired when Stevie leapt onto the top buckle and nailed a *beautiful* moonsault press to drop Mosh in his tracks. The crowd was literally on it's feet as both men made their way to their respective corners. Mosh made the tag - but so did Stevie! Da Blue Guy was a House O' Fire (tm Jim Ross), dropping both 'Bangers in succession with stiff right hands. Mosh rolled out of the ring as Da Blue Guy *planted* Thrasher with a nasty Tiger Bomb. Stevie hurdled the ropes and took out a charging Mosh as Da Blue Guy went up top for the celebrated Meaniesault! But it just wasn't to be as none other than TAKA Michinoku roared down to ringside and blasted Da Blue Guy in the face with the 'Bangers CD player. Da Blue Guy fell *hard* back into the ring and Thrasher made the cover. (match time:10:12) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- "Outsider" Scott Hall (w/Paul Ellering) vs. Razor Ramon "Outsider" Scott Hall (w/Paul Ellering) vs. Razor Ramon (w/Good Ol'J.R.) Hall was *not* happy for two reasons - (1) he was still p*ssed off over being stripped of the title, and (2) the referee for this match was none other than the man who had cost the LOD a victory (or so it seemed) every single time they had lost recently - Nick Patrick! >From the opening bell, Hall was all over Razor like ugly on Abdullah, beating the living hell out of his "successor". Ramon was a bloody mess not even two minutes into the match and it only got worse from there. Hall was on the verge of finishing off Ramon and even had him in position for the Outsider Edge - when Patrick *blistered* him in the face with a chair! Ramon groggily seized the opportunity and set *Hall* for the *Razor's* Edge! He nailed it and Patrick made a *very* fast three and promptly fled the ring before Hall or Ellering could get their hands on him. Razor wasted no time fleeing, either. (match time:05:37) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Tony Schiavone is standing in the ring) Schiavone: Ladies and gentlemen, "The Man From The Dark Side" - THE UNDERTAKER!!! (The lights went low and the mutants went nuts as "Graveyard Symphony" played and UT made his usual slow march to the ring. (Once he got in the ring and raised the lights, Schiavone handed him the microphone - and got the hell out of Dodge. :)) Undertaker: Vader... you raised the wrath of the dark side when you dared laid hands on an innocent soul. Joey Styles did not deserve your cowardly actions - but I have set myself to avenge him. Therefore Vader, let us not play games. I challenge you - on Halloween Night, October 31st at Rumble In The Jungle II: Welcome To Our Nightmare... to a Violent Death Cage Match. The ropes are replaced by barbed wire... we wear glass studded straps on our wrists... a sealed cage is lowered over us... but a pinfall or submission does not end the match, oh no... the referee counts ten... and the man who cannot rise at that count... loses. There is only one way out for you, Vader... in a pine box. Your answer, Vader. (The lights go very quickly out - when they come back up - a wreath is in the ring bearing the message "Vader - Rest In Peace") ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF World Heavyweight Title Match* "Outsider" Kevin Nash (w/Paul Ellering) *challenger* vs. Phil La Fon (w/Doug Furnas) *champion* This was short, ugly and quick. It took Nash all of three minutes and thirty eight seconds to put La Fon away with La Boot To La Head. He wasn't going to fool around this time. (match time:03:38) But as per usual, the fun came *after* the match. The Powers Of Pain and Kevin Sullivan hit the ring. The P.O.P. ganged up on Nash, taking him down quick while Sullivan took out Ellering. >From the back came *The Road Warriors* who immediately began brawling all over the place with the Powers Of Pain, going through the crowd and out the back into the street where they fought all the way down Swanson street into the night. Back in the arena, *Michael Monroe* *flew* down the aisle and springboard plancha-d Sullivan off of Ellering. Nash wisely took Ellering to the back, but the rest of the N.O.E. came down and began ganging up on Monroe - along WITH The Royal Assassins lead by Jerry Lawler!!! It was plain for all to see that Lawler had been the one to bring Sullivan and company to the DEWF in the first place!!! They drug Monroe into the ring where Halloween and Hayabusa tied Monroe to "The Tree Of Woe" and Sullivan *planted* knee after knee after knee in Monroe's midsection. Chris Candito went up top as the NOE handed the unconscious Monroe up to him. Candito wasted no time in *BURYING* Monroe into the mat with that top rope powerbomb of his. To add a final touch, Joe Malenko slapped the Texas Cloverleaf on Monroe as the fans were going *nuclear*! The Royal Assassins merely stood by and cheered the N.O.E. on!!! >From the back came help - Sting, Raven and The Crows. The cowards cleared out plenty fast when they saw the sides were even vaguely even now. The Crows picked up the bloody, fallen Light Heavyweight Champion and in an eerie touch - all four carried him out the back to the locker rooms, held high over their heads, crucifixion style. When they were gone - the lights went black. Then ten ominous "BONG"s rained down in the darkness, like a ceremonial ten count for a fallen champion. When they came back - to the accompaniment of *fire* blazing from each cornerpost - Succubus was standing in the ring, mic in hand... Succubus: (eyes almost literally blazing) Sullivannnnnnn..... the sins of man are many, and are always punished in the end. But your sins, Kevin Sullivan - their punishment is going to swift, sure.. and final. Kevin Sullivan.. there is a difference between darkness... and evil. You are about to find out that your trangressions will show you that the difference can be *fatal*. Kevin Sullivan... rest.. in... HELLLLLLLLLL!!!!!! (The lights went black again and the show closes on that ominous note) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- -- 69th degree Bewilderbeest of the Serengetti Tradition Lover of the 249th degree Fieldmouse of Nimh of the Serengetti Tradition All hail the Zebra of the Nth degree!
