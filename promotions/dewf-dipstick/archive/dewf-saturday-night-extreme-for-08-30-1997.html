Saturday Night Extreme for 08/30/1997!

Sep 2, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fed head : [detail withheld] (open with a clip of Raven's interview from last week) Raven: Joey Styles... things in life do not often turn out as we expect them. I went into The Extreme Rumble fully expecting to gain the glory that has been mine before, in this very building - the world heavyweight championship. Shane Douglas gained that prize. So it was written, so it came to pass. But I have other matters that weigh on my soul. Joe Malenko... you betrayed me in the land of the rising sun. Now the twin spirits of vengeance, my brothers of the sky, will join me to tear the soul from your carcass and consign it to the flames that call for it to be theirs. So it is written, so it shall come to pass. But someday, Joey Styles, I will wear that gold. So I make a challenge to the winner of tonight's match to face me next week for the title. Quoth the Raven... nevermore. (cut to the show ending LOD interview from last week. Joey Styles is in the ring getting ready to wrap up the night's events when "We Are the Champions" by Queen plays. All the fans and Joey look back at the locker room, confused. That is, confused until the Legion of Doom walks out of the locker room smiling. Scott Hall has the IC Belt around his waist and Kevin Nash is carrying the World Title. As they reach the ring the chorus starts up. Styles tried to say something but Nash grabs the mic and he and Hall sing the chorus along with the music. As it finally finishes the music stops and Nash smiles as he waits for the crowd to die down) [KN] That's right, no more is Kevin Nash being screwed over, Kevin Nash is now doing the screwing, and that's gonna be all night, because I am the DEWF f*cking WORLD CHAMP!!! I've held the IC Title before, I tasted gold, and I wanted more, but I also wanted bigger, so I decided, I wanted the World Title! So now, I did it, I am the DEWF World Champ!! *snip* [PE] Hahahahahhahaha!!! We came, we saw, we kicked your asses!!! You people don't realize, the Legion of Doom is the MOST successfull stable in DEWF history!! We have held EVERY title except the newest one, the Trios Title. And it seems to me that we have four men left. So, to the champs, we make a challenge, how about defending the belts against three of the four left? But it doesn't matter, because tonight, the LOD takes Philly by storm, because we are CELEBRA--- (Ellering is cut off by "Come Out And Play (Keep 'Em Separated)" starting over the PA. The whole LOD looks to the eagles nest and their stands Raven, arms spread wide... on that, "Jesus Built My Hotrod" by Ministry begins playing as we head to the DEWF opening montage. As we open, Joey is as usual standing in the ring. "Iron Man" plays and Joey just stands there shaking his head) [JS] At this time it is my (rolls eyes) pleasure to welcome, representing the Legion of Doom, The Road Warriors and the DEWF World Champion, Kevin Nash. (The fans begin booing, and the Road Warriors emerge from the curtains first, followed by.....SCOTT HALL? Hall carries the DEWF IC Title and has a big grin on his face as he walks to the ring) [JS] I must say, this IS a surprise. Scott, just where is Kevin? [SH] Well Jojo, you see Kevin wanted to relax in the locker rooms before he rolls over Raven later on to keep his title. [JS] Well Scott, as tough as Kevin is, Raven is FAR from a push over. (As Joey finishes his statement "Come Out And Play (Keep 'Em Separated)" starts playing and the fans pop big time. Styles looks at the three in the ring, and they all look scared. Just then someone walks from the back dressed all in black with his hair over eyes. But he is too tall to be Raven. He steps into the ring and strikes Raven's arms out pose and flips his hair back. It's...) [JS] Kevin Nash!? What is the meaning of this!? [KN, laughing and grinning] Well Styles, I thought I would see just what the appeal is to Raven to walk around likes this, and I must say, I just don't see it. But it doesn't matter, as no one will hear much from him after tonight. I mean, come on, he's challenging me, the DEWF World Champion!! (The fans start booing madly at him until Nash, off mic starts yelling back "You're all marks! You're all marks!") [JS] Ok then, Hawk, Animal you guys are wrestling Blondes 3:16 later on in a grudge match, and the winners get a shot at the DEWF World Tag Titles. [H] WEEEEEELL, it seems that Austin and Pillman, the two EXTREME boys are having a little temper tantrum. What's the matter boys, you lose your little belts, worried about your tights falling down, and everyone seeing just how little you are!? Poor babies. Well, tonight, you boys are going DOWN, so don't worry about your little belts again. [A] Well, well, well, it looks like the two extreme "men" are nothing but whining little boys! What's the matter? Missing your titles? That's just TOO DAMN BAD because you gotta get through us to get them back, and you're sure as hell not gonna do that! ("Iron Man" plays again as the mutants go crazy booing the four as they leave the ring) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Mikey Whipwreck vs. "The 7-8-9 Kid" Al Snow (w/Jim Ross) Snow nearly got the pin right off the first move of the match, when he caught Mikey out of nowhere with a vicious leg lariat right in the face. Snow also caught a handful of tights, but still only got a two count. A minute or so later, Snow got yet another two when he hit Mikey with an enzoogory (tm Dok Hendrix). But as Snow was jawjacking with referee Jim Mollineaux, Mikey was going up top. When Snow turned around, he got caught right in the mouth with a flying dropkick! As Snow staggered to his feet, Mikey went for a FrankenMikey from the top rope - but Snow saw the move coming and turned it into a *stiff* powerbomb. The next ten minutes saw Snow dominating totally. But when he got the chance to go for the pin, which he did get, several times, he kept pulling Mikey up. It finally cost him. Snow had just hit yet another powerbomb on Mikey - and yet again pulled him up! Referee Mollineaux got ALL up in Snow's face - and when Snow pushed him, Mollineaux pushed back - right into a schoolboy from Mikey! One - two - THREE!!! (match time:13:12) The fun wasn't over, though. "Ahmedmania" Hulk Johnson stormed the ring and he and Snow began beating holy hell out of Mikey. They took turns dropping legs until over the PA came something that caused the fans to lose it - "Frankenstein" by The Edgar Winter Group!!!! *911 IN DA HOUSE!* Yep, 911 was back! A frantic Ross *tried* to warn his men, but they were having too good a time. Well, as 911 stepped over the rope "Ahmedmania" *did* see him and bailed - but Snow didn't! After dropping one final leg, Snow got up - turned around - and looked straight into the eyes of 911! Going UP! GOING down! *WHAM* CHOKESLAM TIME! 911 helped Mikey out of the ring as Ross and "Ahmedmania" tried to scrape Snow off the mat. --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Joey Styles : *sigh* To think this had to happen to me. Ladies and gentlemen, The Royal Assassins. (The crowd boos intensely as "Red" by King Crimson plays over the arena PA system. Jerry Lawler comes to the interview area grinning like the cat that ate the canary. He is followed by Justin Hawk Bradshaw, Diamond Dallas Page who wears his DEWF Trios belt, and the Razor Boys who wear the DEWF Tag belts around their waists and the Trios belts over their shoulders.) JS : What brings you lot out here, did Waffle House close up early tonight? JL : Very clever, Styles, Full House didn't know what they were missing when they didn't hire you as a writer. The fact is that the Royal Assassins go and do pretty much whatever they please these days. Our trophy case is so bright , we gotta wear shades. We've got the belts, we've got the victories, and all we have to do is take out some trash and we can say our work is done. Fear Machine, don't think for a second that we've forgotten about you. Your time will come, and our vengeance is going to be ... pradon my use of the word ... extreme. Los Gringos Locos Nuevos, it seems you just haven't learned your lesson yet. We've taken your belts, we've taken your pride, and we've taken whatever miniscule amount of pride that you have left. Keep coming back and making a nuisance of yourselves and we just might have to take you out permanently. And Darkside ... the big man here has a few words. Justin Hawk Bradshaw : Sting, you tried to stick you nose in our business, and look where it got you ... a one way ticket to the hospital. You like to wave that baseball bat around, but you didn't look so hot when it connected with your kneecaps, did you? Try it one more time, Marilyn Manson, and I'll cripple you for good! Paul Diamond : And speaking of cripples, it looks like the two cripples are facing the two facepainted has-beens for the chance to face us for our belts. Well, here's hoping you just kill each other ... and clear out the deadwood so that some real competition can come in! The Razor Boys ... and Al will back me up on this one ... have enough on our plates without having our time wasted on title defenses with fossils. The Razor Boys are what is happening now with tag team wrestling, and if we have to send you codgers back to the Home for the Terminally Washed up, then that's the way it will have to be! (Al Perez solemnly nods.) Diamond Dallas Page : Sweet 'n' Sour Jimmy Lowe, you flapped your gums once too often and look where it got you ... on the wrong end of the Diamond Cutter! Now that you're over and done with, DDP can get back to the business at hand, namely defending these belts. Now the LOD is making some noise about wanting a shot at the Trios titles ... sorry guys, but you're somewhere between the bWo and the Desperados on the list of contenders. And if the Joy Lucha club want to try and get the belts back, well they can take the spot behind you at the back of the line. Good gawd! Paul Diamond : Oh and Eddie ... give my regards to your charming family, will you? (The Royal Assassins head back to the locker room) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- "The Lost Soul" Sting vs. "Ahmedmania" Hulk Johnson (w/Jim Ross) The match never even got started - 911 came down and chased the 2Wo members from the ring right as Sting arrived. When the 2Wo and 911 were gone - Justin "Hawk" Bradshaw hit the ring and layed out Sting with a big lariat! Bradshaw drug Sting around the ring with his bullrope and ended by beating him even more senseless with Sting's own ballbat - and then left with it! --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Styles: What the hell kind of cowardly bullsh*t is Bradshaw trying to pull??? I - ("F*cking Hostile" by Pantera plays loudly as Steve Austin, Brian Pillman, and "Sweet" Jimmy Lowe come out. Lowe is accompanied by Kimberly Page, who is wearing really short shorts and a Lowe 6:9 t-shirt from a rival fed) [Joey] Well, it certainly seemed last week that the Underworld got it's ass handed to itsel...urrk (Lowe whips around and grabs Styles in a nervehold that paralyzes him...the Giant then comes out and takes over the throat grab...basically telling JoJo to shut up or suffer the consequences) [Lowe] That's better. Now then....first things first..."Dimed*&k" Dallas Page.....congratulations. You laid a beating on "The Gangster of Love". Of course, there are a half-dozen men who are BETTER THAN YOU, STRONGER THAN YOU, AND WHOM I RESPECT A LOT MORE, that have done that. And guess what.. I'M STILL HERE!!! And I hope that thought keeps you half as warm at night as Kimberly here has been keeping me.... c'mere sweetie.... (Kimberly basically shoves her tongue into Jimmy as the two kiss long and hard for about five seconds....Kimberly then turns to Jimmy's microphone.) [Kimberly] YYYYeeesssssssssssssssss.... [Austin] You see, Page, it's this simple. You, Perez, and Diamond pulled off the "on any given day" maxim......ONCE. But the sad truth is that you owe us a rematch regardless of how large a beating we lay on the Old Worriers tonight. Your clock is ticking, son, and there's a Time Bomb attached to it. [Pillman] You see, Diamond, Perez, you're nothing but a bunch of glorified jobbers reliving your Texas circuit glory attached to Jerry "It's not true love unless I can snap her pelvis in two" Lawler and Page. And when we get your sorry asses BACK into the ring, you're gonna find out that you don't ever, EVER, *bleep* with "Sweet" Jimmy. [Austin] Who knows, Page, if you and your boys piss us off enough, I may take your chippie here once Jimmy gets bored with her, stomp a mudhole in her ass to match the other two holes, and walk all three of them dry...although in one of the three I'd be walking a marathon!!!! [Lowe] LOD...."Precious" Paul Ellering.....it's time to send you one way back to the San Francisco bathhouse that spawned your sorry asses.....and that's the bottom line, cause "Sweet" Jimmy said so! [All] YYYYYeeeeeeeeessssssssss!!!!! (The Giant drops JoJo as they all leave) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Gangstaz vs. The 2Wo (Diesel and Razor Ramon w/Jim Ross) Diesel and Razor were standing smack in the middle of the ring, giving the 2Wo sign when "Natural Born Killaz" blared over the PA and here they came, Garbage Can O' Doom in tow. Razor tried for the Razor's Edge on Mustafa early on, but caught short when New Jack *keelhauled* him right in the face with a shot from a cookie sheet. Diesel showed he could take it to the extreme too. To wit, he nailed Saed right between the eyes with the ring bell at about the seven minute mark, busting him wide open. It got even worse for Mustafa a couple minutes later. Diesel brought the crowd to it's feet by setting up a table, then laying Saed across it. Diesel then picked up a baseball bat and nailed Saed right in the midsection with it, not once but twice! The table didn't actually come into play until two minutes later when Saed actually was able to nail Diesel between the eyes with a chair shot and then laid the big man across the table. Saed came off the apron Saed actually was able to nail Diesel between the eyes with a chair shot and then laid the big man across the table. Saed came off the apron with an elbowsmash - and went through the table himself when Diesel moved! But the tide turned when Diesel set up an identical table and tried the same move on Saed - and suffered the same fate when *Saed* moved and *he* went through the table. It was at the half hour point when it all came crashing down for the Gangstaz - literally. New Jack and 'Stafa hit the 187 (running powerslam from Saed and a flying chairshot from New Jack) on Razor. Ross, though, distracted referee Pee Wee Moore and that gave Diesel the opportunity to grab New Jack and nail him with the JackKnife! Diesel drug a groggy Razor on top and then laid out Saed as they got the one-two-THREE! (match time:29:23) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Joey Styles : Ladies and gentlemen ... the Fear Machine! (The crowd pops intensely as "Horror Head" by Curve plays over the arena PA system. Black Sarah leads her men, once again wearing a tight PVC outfit and black boots. Michael Monroe is wearing the DEWF Light Heavyweight Title belt, and Justin Cross follows close behind.) JS : Black Sarah, it looks like your man Michael Monroe might well have is work cut out for him tonight as he faces rising young Japanese talent Masato Yakushiji. Black Sarah : Joseph, don't think for a second that The Shadow knows nothing of Yakushiji-san. Michael has studied him well, knows his moves, his strengths ... and his limitations. He has studied all of the greats of Super J wresting, as well as younger talents such as his next opponents. There is no move that Yakushiji can perform that Micheal cannot counter, and there is no way that he will be able to escape the Shadowplay. Michael Monroe : Yakushiji, do not think that I have no respect you. I have seen you and know that you are capable of great things. But let us be honest here, you are the low man on the Seikigun totem. You've had your head kicked in by Men's Teioh and Dick Togo and now you've come all the way from Japan to face me. I'm not saying that victory over you will be easy, my friend, but I am saying it is certain. I know all about the Japanese style. I won this belt in Japan, and hoped that the attention I gained there would attract contenders from Japan, and you are the first to fall, Yakushiji. While you're at it, tell your friends Sasuke and Naniwa and Hamada to come after you. The more of them i beat, the more lustre it gives the title. Justin Cross : One more thing. Taz, we faced each other on your terms, in an I Quit match. You beat me, fair and square. Fair enough, I knew what the stipulations were when I signed the contract, I knew what I was getting myself into. But let's see if you dare face me on my terms. I'm more than just a bump, I am a roadblock on the path of Rage. Step into the Cage of Fear, Taz ... if you dare. *DEWF World Light Heavyweight Title Match* Masato Yakashiji *challenger* vs. "The Shadow" Michael Monroe (w/Black Sarah) *champion* Yakashiji displayed all of the sensational moves that made him the hottest rookie to come out of Michinoku Pro in quite some time. But he just couldn't counter the experience and aggressiveness of the champion. At the 6:01 mark, Monroe turned a missed springboard senton into the oppotunity to lock on the Shadowplay (Romero Chinlock). Yakashiji held on for an astounding 42 seconds before giving it up. (match time:06:43) Monroe helped the young man to his feet and bowed respectfully, raising his hand in a gesture of respect. The crowd gave both a standing ovation. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Crows (w/Succubus) vs. The Neighborhood Of Evil (Halloween and Hayabusa w/Kevin Sullivan) The NOE were first in the ring, enacting some bizarre ritual with Sullivan when over the PA came White Zombie's version of "Boogieman" and here came the ex-champs! But the NOE got first blood (literally) when Hayabusa nailed Crow 1 with a moonsault bodyblock from the top turnbuckle to the outside floor to open the match! Hayabusa missed his next move though, a senton from the ring apron to the floor when Crow 1 moved! Crow 1 bridged a table from the ring apron to the railing and set up Hayabusa on it! On cue, Crow 2 leapfrogged the top rope and elbowsmashed Hayabusa straight through the table! Crow 1 intercepted Halloween with a flying clothesline from the top, then pounded away on the masked man! He stopped as Halloween crawled back in, dropping a leg across Halloween's neck as he entered. Crow 1 even used the ropes for leverage, but still only got a two count. Hayabusa wasn't done, though. He hit Crow 1 with a German Suplex that folded him up like an accordion - but Crow 1 sat up, zombie style. Hayabusa wisely tagged out to Halloween while Crow 1 was catching his breath - and then the two dropped Crow 1 right back down with a spectacular flying double dropkick! It was pretty much all downhill from there for the ex-champs. Note I said, pretty much. The NOE totally dominated the next ten minutes, using split seconds tag team maneuvers and copious distraction from Kevin Sullivan to keep the edge. It was only when Halloween nailed the Pumpkinsault that Succubus saw her opening. She crept up on Sullivan - and sprayed him right in the eyes with a foul smelling red mist! The crowd went nuts as Sullivan rolled around in agony on the floor. The NOE unwisely berated Succubus for her interference. It was unwise because it gave Crow 2 (who had been on the receiving end of the Pumpkinsault) the time to make a switch with his partner, Crow 1! Halloween turned around - and got caught right in the mouth with a spin kick! Halloween staggered around as Crow 1 took out Hayabusa, giving the legal man Crow 2 the chance to come off the top - with a fire extingisher show right to the top of Halloween's head! But that still wasn't the end... Joe Malenko roared down to ringside and dropped Crow 2 with a DDT. That was when referee Paul Richards had enough and called for the DQ. That got *him* a DDT - and then the crowd went ballistic as "Come Out And Play (Keep 'Em Separated)" blared over the PA and here came Raven. (match time:15:08) The Crows and the NOE fought all the way to the back as Raven and Malenko wailed away on each other. Problem... That left Raven wide open for an attack from Kevin Nash! The big man hit the ring and chucked Malenko over the top rope like a shotput, then chokeslammed Raven! Philly's Finest hit the ring before Nash could do any more damage, but Raven had to be helped to the back. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Road Warriors vs. Blondes 3:16 The wasn't even close to being a wrestling match. It was a street fight, plain and simple. A street fight that lasted all of nine and a half minutes before The Razor Boys hit the ring and attacked all four men! That lasted about a minute before Gary Hart roared down to ringside, *irate*. Hart: STOP IT!! STOP THIS F*CKING BULLSH*T RIGHT F*CKING NOW!!! (Crowd POPS) IF YOU F*CKING COWARDS (Indicates The Razor Boys) THINK YOUR PUNK ASSES ARE GONNA GET OUT OF DEFENDING AGAINST EITHER OF THESE TEAMS BY ATTACKING THEM FROM BEHIND LIKE THE MOTHEF*CKING COWARDS YOU ARE (The Razor Boys looked stunned at Hart's vehemence. The Blondes and The Warriors are resting warily against the turnbuckles..), YOU GOT ANOTHER F*CKING THINK COMING! RIGHT NOW! RIGHT HERE! THIS IS RIGHT F*CKING NOW A THREE WAY DANCE FOR THE F*CKING TAG TITLES!!! (Crowd goes APESH*T asHart leaves - and in one of the funniest moments of the night, Animal and Austin look at each other, shrug, and lay into the champs) But the Boys bailed and fast, leaving the frustrated Warriors and Blondes to chase after them. Hart stormed off as well. --------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF World Heavyweight Title Match* Raven *challenger* vs. Kevin Nash (w/Paul Ellering) *champion* Raven staggered out from the back, still *clutching* his back. Nash wasn't far behind, and threw Raven right into the ring. Nash just *mauled* Raven. Raven got in exactly one move - a dropkick which missed when Nash swatted him away like a fly. Nash then mockingly JackKnifed Raven and got the pin.(match time:13:44) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Joey Styles stands in the ring ready for an interview) [JS] At this time my guest is a man who seems to be lost in the shuffle in the new attitude Legion of Doom. He is three time former DEWF World Champion, Vader! (Vader's WWF music starts up and the fans boo. Vader approaches the ring quickly, looking none too pleased. He rolls into the ring and Styles prepares to ask him a question but Vader grabs the mic) [V] What the f*ck did you just say Styles!? [JS] I said I was going to interview the three time former champ. [V] Bullsh*t!! You know what I mean! I mean the little "lost in the shuffle" part! [JS] Well you must admit Vader, since the LOD has changed it's attitude, you have taken a back seat to everyone else in the group. [V] Styles, you better f*cking take that back right now, or I'll show you who's gonna be left out!! [JS] Now calm down Leon, you know what I mean, you haven't been doing a whole lot since...(Vader grabs him by the throat) [V, growling] I said to take it the f*ck back Styles....now I'm mad. (With this he throws the mic down and tosses Styles into the corner by his throat. He then picks him up and punches him in the stomach) [V, off mic] IT'S VADER TIIIIIIME!!! (He pulls Styles in and then power bombs him. The crowd is in shock as Vader hauls the unconscious Styles to his feet for another powerbomb - and then the lights go out) *BONG* When they came back up, *The Undertaker* was standing directly behind Vader! He spun the big man around, making him let go of Styles and then chokeslammed him to the mat! Vader rolled to the outside, vowing revenge, as UT and Damage Control tended to the fallen Styles. On that we fade to black...
