Saturday Night Extreme for 08/23/1997!

Aug 28, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fed head : Clay Evans ( [detail withheld] ) ---- (Joey Styles is in the ring and "Iron Man" plays. Only, this time he was expecting it, as he was scheduled to interview some of the Legion of Doom) [JS] At this time it's my......(rolls his eyes) pleasure to introduce to you The Legion of Doom - The Outsiders, The Road Warriors and -- TAZ!!! (Nash, Hall, Hawk, and Animal step out of the curtains to the boos of the mutants. Nash wears a shirt that has the LOD drawn on the front, and on the back it has "You're all marks!! You're all marks!!" on it. Hall simply wears his trunks, and Hawk and Animal are dressed as they always have been) [JS] And what, may I ask, leads to you giving me the pleasure of interviewing you instead of you attacking me? [KN] Well JoJo, you see, we got bored with that routine, so we figured we would try it the normal way. Now, get on with the interview before we change our minds and kick your ass. [JS] Ok then, Kevin, first we'll discuss your match, or should I say matches tonight. First you wrestle Mr. Pogo, and then you wrestle Shane Douglas for the DEWF World Title. [KN] Well, yeah, I figured I would take care of both problems at once. First I take care of the guy who ONCE AGAIN screwed me out of the DEWF World Title, then I actually take the DEWF World Title!! As I see it, it will all be just too SWEEEEET!! [JS] Uh huh....ok. And now, Scott, you wrestle The Giant for the DEWF IC Title tonight, something your partner has held in the past. [SH] Dat's right mang. Joey mang, tonight Da Bad Guy, he is gonna roll over the big old Giant, just like Kevin and Vader did, and while I do it chico, I take da DEWF IC Title, just like Kevin did. You see, when you get in the ring with the Legion of Doom mang, you get in the ring with the best! [T] An I Quit Match? An I F'N Quit Match!? Justin Cross, you are in a no-win situation!! You must realize, I NEVER say I Quit! Taz does NOT tap out! However, I AM the master of the Tazmission, and big man, there is no way you will survive, and I WILL choke your ass OUT in this match!! The path of rage starts up again tonight, and it starts with YOU Cross!! [JS] Hawk, Animal, you don't wrestle tonight, why are you out here too?? [H] WEEEEELL! You see Joey, we're out here to talk about Blondes 3:16. Pillman, you have the mentality of a 10 year old! You think a fart joke is going to make us mad?? Punk, ask Da Gangstas what happens when you call us old? Last time they did it we took the DEWF World Tag Titles from them! [A] Austin, Pillman, you think you're extreme by farting into a mic? You know what's gonna be extreme? Well, to steal a line from our old face painted friends, WHEN WE KICK YER STINKIN' TEETH IN!!! [JS] I guess that just about wraps it up for the interview guys, any last comments? (Nash grabs the mic) [KN] Yeah JoJo, I think I liked it better the old way! (Nash gives Styles a little one handed shove, knocking him over as the four laugh and walk out, leaving the mic in front of Styles as we go to the DEWF opening montage. When we come back, Joey is in the ring still - and "Come Out And Play (Keep 'Em Separated)" begins playing and out comes Raven. Raven makes the crucifix sign to the crowd, who respond with the identical gesture) Raven: Joey Styles... things in life do not often turn out as we expect them. I went into The Extreme Rumble fully expecting to gain the glory that has been mine before, in this very building - the world heavyweight championship. Shane Douglas gained that prize. So it was written, so it came to pass. But I have other matters that weigh on my soul. Joe Malenko... you betrayed me in the land of the rising sun. Now the twin spirits of vengeance, my brothers of the sky, will join me to tear the soul from your carcass and consign it to the flames that call for it to be theirs. So it is written, so it shall come to pass. But someday, Joey Styles, I will wear that gold. So I make a challenge to the winner of tonight's match to face me next week for the title. Quoth the Raven... nevermore. (leaves as "Come Out And Play (Keep 'Em Separated)" begins playing again) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Mikey Whipwreck vs. "Ahmedmania" Hulk Johnson (w/Good Ol' J.R.) On the way to the ring, Ross promised he'd show the world the newest member of the 2Wo tonight. Johnson hammed it up to hell and gone at ringside, doing all his Hogan routines - which got him plastered to the floor when Mikey went up and over the top rope with a plancha, flattening the big man. Johnson immediately stormed to his feet, nostrils flaring, eyes bugging, pointing an accusing finger and trying not to laugh hysterically as the crowd started a chant of "Wubba Jubba! (clapclapclapclapclap) Wubba Jubba! (clapclapclapclapclap)" (tm SCDudley). Even Ross was having a hard time keeping a straight face. Johnson got back in the ring, followed by Mikey, and immediately whipped Whipwreck in for La Boot To La Head - which Mikey ducked and nailed a cross bodyblock for two. It was about this point that someone emerged from the crowd, wearing lightning bolt tights with "2Wo" emblazoned on the keister, a bandana, a really cheesy beard, and started doing karate poses. Sean Waltman? Nope. Al Snow. Or as Joey put it.... Styles: What the HELL....? I guess since Waltman's was 1-2-3, and the fans called Steve Corino the 4-5-6 Kid - I guess that makes Al the 7-8-9 Kid??? I guess he really *did* finally flip his beanie. At any rate, Johnson wasted no time in rushing Mikey - and got dropped like a bad habit when Mikey pasted him right in the mush with a spinning leg lariat. Mikey was going for a Superfly Splash when The 7-8-9 Kid chaired him and pulled "Ahmedmania" out of the ring, getting Mikey the DQ. (match time:04:26) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Kevin Nash (w/Paul Ellering) vs. Mr. Pogo To quote John Cleese, "Welllll, *that* was fun, wasn't it?" I won't bore you with details, but suffice it to say Pogo just flat got *mutilated* by Nash. One of the most horrific beatings in ring history finished with Nash Jack Knifing Pogo though a table, on the floor. Can *you* say "countout win for Mr. Nash", boys and girls? I *thought* you could. (match time:09:15) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- *SUBMISSION MATCH* "The Ivory Tower" Justin Cross (w/Black Sarah) vs. "The Human Suplex Machine" Taz (w/Paul Ellering) The LOD was just on fire tonight, and this match was no exception. Taz put on a wrestling clinic, where he had tried to brawl with Cross in their previous encounter. He wasn't about to make that same mistake. Cross gave it his best, as always, but the former IC champ was just no match for Taz in the grappling department. Taz kep his word, and slapped on the Tazmission for the win, about fifteen minutes in. (match time:14:46) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- *DEWF INTERCONTINENTAL TITLE MATCH* Scott Hall (w/Paul Ellering) *challenger* vs. The Giant (w/"Sweet" Jimmy Lowe) *champion* Ironically enough, the special guest referee for this match was none other than Nick Patrick, which led Joey to comment: Styles: How much you wanna bet Bischoff is having a heart attack right now? The champ almost got a cheap pin, early on, by hitting a thundering belly to belly and hooking the tights. But Patrick caught him at it and made him break it up. The Giant *cremated* Hall for a full nine plus minutes after that, mauling him in every way possible. Problem - he kept pulling Hall up at the last second. About 12:30 in, the two were out on the floor, where Giant was obviously going to Choke Slam Hall on the floor. Lowe blackjacked Ellering unconscious, which of course drew warnings from an irate Nick Patrick. Problem number two - what Giant didn't see was on the turnbuckle behind him - was *Tommy Dreamer*! Dreamer *flew* off the top and *BLISTERED* the champ in the back of the head with a chair shot, and then disappeared into the roaring crowd. Giant was out cold - but on the floor. Hall wasn't about to argue his good fortune though, and nearly gave himself a hernia getting Wight back into the ring. It was there that Hall got the pop of the night - by *NAILING* the Outsider Edge!!! Hall was clutching the small of his back in agony, but managed to make the cover at about the same time Patrick had more than enough of Lowe and *decked* him with a big right hand (which of course, drew a *monster* pop). Patrick made the entirely academic three count and the crowd went *ballistic*. (match time:13:36) Announcer: "Your winner, and NNNNEWWWWW DEWF Intercontinent Heavyweight Champion, SCOTTTT HALLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLL!" The bad luck for Jimmy Lowe wasn't done at that point, even, because as Hall left and Giant stumbled to the back - Lowe staggered to his feet at ringside. Unfortunately for "Sweet" Jimmy, DDP roared down the aisle and threw him into the ring where he promptly NAILED the Diamond Cutter!!! --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Diamond Dallas Page vs. "Sweet" Jimmy Lowe There wasn't much for Jim Mollineaux to do at this point but make the three count... which he did. :) (match time:00:44) That, of course (due to match stipulations), set up the next match - --------------------------------------------------------------------------- DEWF WORLD TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH The Razor Boys (Al Perez and Paul Diamond) w/Jerry "The King" Lawler *challengers* vs. Blondes 3:16 (w/"Sweet" Jimmy Lowe) As Damage Control hauled the unconscious Lowe away, the Blondes rushed the ring and began beating hell out of DDP. Bad move. The Razor Boys (co holders already of the DEWF Trios Titles with DDP) hit the ring and attacked *them*. All hell continued to break loose as *The Road Warriors* rushed ringside and began trashtalking the Blondes. The Razor Boys took full advantage, and dominated the match. Well, except for about the last minute when Diamond missed a spinwheel kick off the ropes and got clotheslined out of his boots by Austin. Pillman came in and whipped Diamond in, going for the "hotshot" part of the Blondes finisher, the "Suicide Blonde". Problem again. Perez clipped Austin in the back of the legs as across the way *Animal* yanked Pillman's ankle and hauled him outside. Thus, Diamond was able to hop up on the top rope and spring off with a high cross body onto the limping Austin! One - Two - THREE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (match time:16:44) Announcer: "Your winner, and NNNNNEEWWWW DEWF World Tag Team Champions - The RRRAAAZZZZOORRRRR Boys!" Things got even more nuts from here as *Los Gringos Locos Nuevos* hit the ring and brawled all the way to the back with the new champs and DDP as the Warriors brawled into the stands and out the back with the *incensed* Blondes! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- DEWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH Kevin Nash (w/Paul Ellering) *challenger* vs. "The Franchise" Shane Douglas (w/Francine) *challenger* Do I really have to describe how ugly this got? I mean, a fired up Nash just beat Douglas seven ways from Sunday. The LOD had been hot all night, and this was *definitely* no exception. Nash withstood several Douglas comeback attempts and put the champ away with a *NASTY* JackKnife to get the one - Two - THREE! (match time:12:03) Announcer: Your winner, and NNNNNEEEWWWWWWWWWWW DEWF World's Heavyweight Champion, KEVINNNNNN NASSSSSHHHHHHHHH!!!! --------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Joey Styles is in the ring getting ready to wrap up tonight's events when "We Are the Champions" by Queen plays. All the fans and Joey look back at the locker room, confused. That is, confused until the Legion of Doom walks out of the locker room smiling. Scott Hall has the IC Belt around his waist and Kevin Nash is carrying the World Title. As they reach the ring the chorus starts up. Styles tried to say something but Nash grabs the mic and he and Hall sing the chorus along with the music. As it finally finishes the music stops and Nash smiles as he waits for the crowd to die down) [KN] That's right, no more is Kevin Nash being screwed over, Kevin Nash is now doing the screwing, and that's gonna be all night, because I am the DEWF f*cking WORLD CHAMP!!! I've held the IC Title before, I tasted gold, and I wanted more, but I also wanted bigger, so I decided, I wanted the World Title! So now, I did it, I am the DEWF World Champ!! But that's not allllll!! Oh no, that is SWEEEET enough, but it's not all, my good buddy, Scott "Da Bad Guy" Hall is a new champ!! [SH] That's right chicos, Da Bad Guy is da new DEWF IC Champ!! Giant, you big dumb ox, you thought you could scare Da Bad Guy? I don't think so! I told you not to mess with da LOD punk, you didn't learn when Vader beat you down, you didn't learn when Da Big Guy beat you down, so you messed with everyone, including da LOD chico. So, we challenged and you took it. Well, as big as you are, you are about 16 pounds lighter chico, because the DEWF IC Belt is mine now! [PE] Hahahahahhahaha!!! We came, we saw, we kicked your asses!!! You people don't realize, the Legion of Doom is the MOST successfull stable in DEWF history!! We have held EVERY title except the newest one, the Trios Title. And it seems to me that we have four men left. So, to the champs, we make a challenge, how about defending the belts against three of the four left? But it doesn't matter, because tonight, the LOD takes Philly by storm, because we are CELEBRA--- (Ellering is cut off by "Come Out And Play (Keep 'Em Separated)" starting over the PA. The whole LOD looks to the eagles nest and their stands Raven, arms spread wide... on that, we fade to black)
