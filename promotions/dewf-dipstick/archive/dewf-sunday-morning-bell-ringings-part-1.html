Sunday Morning Bell-Ringings Part 1!

Jan 26, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** The scene opens in on a large church where a stereotypical hunchback is ringing the bell. As the shot zooms in, you realize that he's ringing the bell by hitting it with the head of a skinny jobber. Cut to the bell, on which is embossed "DEWF Sunday Morning Bell-Ringings" ** Noah Raines: Hello, I am Noah Raines. I took this job with Dipstick Championship E-Wrestling after being fired from my old position on KPU channel 77 as a weatherman. I don't know much about wrestling, so bear with me. Here on the premiere of Sunday Morning Bell-Ringings live from Hell Memorial Coliseum in Frogtown, Oregon, we'll see some great action from future we'll see some great action from future superstars like "The Thinker" Lawrence Logic, The Irish Setter, Billy U. Badd, Maverick, and Wimpy Ford. Now where is my partner? ** Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly, Noah's temporary partner, saunters into the booth ** Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly ** showing Noah a stolen wallet **: Say, do you want to buy this? It's genuine weasel-hide. Noah Raines: Uh, no thanks, Dennis, where'd you get that? I think the first match is about to start. Let's go to ring announcer Hubert Pubert for the introductions. ** "YMCA" plays as the crowd pops big in expectation of MCW superstar Holden McGroin ** Hubert Pubert: Weighing in at 211 pounds, from San Francisco, and accompanied to the ring by the "Bass Bastard" Leon Bubba Rundell, representing the Old Softcore Freaks, here is the "Butt Boarder" PHIL McCROTCH!! ** Phil McCrotch (aka Dwayne Casey Malone) comes out dressed in a Holden McGroin bodysuit in front of a crowd of 43 chanting "Fake!" ** Noah Raines: For those of you who don't know, Phil McCrotch is actually Dwayne Casey Malone of the Jobroni Corporation, who has apparently gotten a new gimmick. Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: He looks like he needs a pair of pants. Maybe I'll sell him these. ** holds up a pair of Levi jeans ** Noah Raines: How'd you steal those? Never mind, I don't want to know. ** "Think" by Aretha Franklin fills the loudspeakers ** Hubert Pubert: Weighing in at 229 pounds and hailing from The School of Thought, here is "THE THINKER" LAWRENCE LOGIC!!! ** The Thinker takes off his really thick glasses and puts them and a large dictionary in the corner. He is wearing his usual black trunks with "Pure Logic" on one leg and white shirt. ** Noah Raines: Logic is supposed to be an excellent scientific wrestler. Looks like a skinny little geek to me. Of course, Malone, uh, McCrotch is a skinny little geek, too. Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: Gee, are those glasses Ray-Bans? They'd look good in my collection. ** starts out of the booth, but is restrained by Noah Raines ** ** Ding-dong-ding-dong ** Noah Raines: Well, Logic and McCrotch are locking up to start the match. Logic with a , uh, I think it's called a leg sweep. He's arguing with McCrotch about something. The Thinker apparently saw the "butt boarder" in a bar last night with some hot local talent. He's arguing that McCrotch isn't really gay! Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly ** counting his watches **: One, two, . . . You know, I think Phil McCrotch knows he isn't really gay. After all, he is a practicing bigamist and everything. Noah Raines: Oh, will you shut up? He doesn't make enough money to support two wives. He's a jobber for us, for God's sake. Is that a Rolex Datejust? ** slaps himself ** Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: Yeah, it's a Rolex, only 200 bucks for you. I understand that both of Dwayne Malone's wives are wealthy divorce lawyers. Noah Raines ** shaking his head **: I feel really sorry for Dwayne, uh, Phil . . . Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: So do I. He's getting pounded on by the Thinker. Noah Raines: That's not what I meant, Dennis. Well, back to the match. Logic has McCrotch in a, um, ** consults his copy of "Mike Tenay's Moves Dictionary" ** half Boston Crab. Reversal by McCrotch. He's grabbing at the family jewels of Lawrence Logic. What's that he's saying? I think it was "I am so gay!!" He's twisting the Thinker's , uh, sensitive area. Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: Come on, Noah, you can say it, this is cable. He's got him by the balls!!! Oooh, that has to hurt!! Noah Raines: Logic doesn't seem to cotton to McCrotch's handling of it. He's going for a bulldog headlock. McCrotch ducks and Logic misses. McCrotch nearly takes down Logic with a tackle!!! They're backing up into the corner where the dictionary is located. McCrotch grabs the dictionary! Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: What's McCrotch doing? He's looking something up in the dictionary! He should be beating the Thinker over the head with it!!! Noah Raines: McCrotch is saying something to Logic. I think he just said "How do you spell cat?" This apparently amuses Logic, who's doubled over with laughter. Now he's getting up. He yells "C!!" and begins to cinch McCrotch in what I think he calls "The Logical Conclusion." The referee is checking to see if McCrotch is giving up. Logic yells "A!!" as he completes the hold. Now McCrotch is giving up!!! It's over!! Logic says, "T!!, you implausible imitation of a flagrant homosexual!". Let's go down to Hubert Pubert. Hubert Pubert: Your winner, at 7:57, "THE THINKER" LAWRENCE LOGIC!!!!! Noah Raines: Well, Lawrence Logic certainly looked impressive in that match. Of course, how can you not look impressive against Dwayne Casey Malone? Dennis, where the heck are you? Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly ** returning with the Big Chrome Belt (a three foot wide creation made out of chrome links with the DEWF logo on the front that could probably only be worn by someone larger than Blubber Mountain or Yokozuna) **: Say, Noah, how much will the fences pay for this sucker? Noah Raines: Uh, that's the DEWF's World Title belt, for which the tournament to decide its first owner will be bracketed live on this show later on. I don't think Trillionaire Coot would like it if you stole it so soon after he bought it for the company. Now we've got some tag action coming up next between the Irish Setter and Billy U. Badd of the Six People and the Genuine Article. Setter and Badd have looked good in the MCW, but that's no barometer of their success in this federation. Let's go down to Hubert Pubert for the introductions. ** "Too Drunk to Fuck" by The Dead Kennedys comes over the PA. ** Hubert Pubert: Introducing first, weighing in at 629 pounds, from Milwaukee, Wisconsin, and accompanied by Bud Wiser, here are Jack Daniels and Jim Beam, THE GENUINE ARTICLE!!! ** Daniels and Beam come out accompanied by Wiser, who looks like a bartender. They're wearing really filthy jeans and shirts and passing a bottle of Popov vodka between themselves. ** Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly ** holding his nose **: Oh, God, these guys smell horrible! They'd better bring some of that liquor in the booth, though, and put it right here in this glass. ** gets out an official Presidential shot glass ** Noah Raines: How'd you end up with one of those, may I ask? Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: I used to live next door to Gerald Ford. Noah Raines: Oh. Well, I think Hubert's ready to announce Badd and Setter now. ** "When Irish Eyes Are Smiling" and "Roadhouse Blues" by the Doors play at the same time. ** Hubert Pubert: And their opponents, weighing in at 525 pounds, representing the Six People, and accompanied to the ring by Angel, Maverick, Curtis Faust, , and the King of Spades, from the Baddlands, BILLY U. BADD!!!, and from Dublin ,Ireland, THE IRISH SETTER!!! ** The Six People come out in matching black tights with a gold VI on them ** ** Ding-dong-ding-dong ** Noah Raines: Well, it looks like the Irish Setter and Jim Beam are going to start things out in the match. Both of these guys are pretty damn big. This could be more interesting than the first match. Beam runs after Setter and starts whaling away at him! The Setter picks up Beam for a scoop slam. Daniels runs in and jumps on the Setter's back. He has taken him down! The referee counts. One . . . Two . . . Two and a half . . NO!! Billy U. Badd breaks the pin and goes after Jim Beam. He's going for a DDT! Beam's head hit the ring hard! Holy Tulsa, this guy just puked in the ring!! Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly ** examining a Faberge egg **: Well, that what he deserves for hogging all the liquor with his partner. What's he doing now? He's hugging the Irish Setter and saying "I love you, man!" and offering him some vodka. Noah Raines: Well, it looks like the Setter is a beer man, he's breaking the vodka bottle over Jim Beam's head! Jack Daniels runs in with another bottle!! He's offering it to Billy U. Badd! Badd takes a big gulp and thanks Daniels . . . with a punch in the nose. He's giving him some Atomic Noogies!! The Setter has Daniels up for a powerslam!! Bang, Daniels hit the mat. What the heck? Tell me he didn't!! Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: Uh, he didn't do what? Piss himself? He did that, all right. Noah Raines: OK, Dennis. Jack Daniels doesn't seem to be in very good condition here. He falls out of the ring, right next to "The Referee" Curtis Faust, who plays some Sweet Groin Music on Daniels' sack and rolls him back in. Beam is on the top rope!! This won't be pretty!! He lands on Angel!! She goes for the Devil's Claw!! If Jim Beam could feel pain, this would be hurting him. Meanwhile, the Setter hits Daniels with a running clothesline in the ring!! Setter doesn't seem to want to cover Daniels, but he's biting him!! Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: Maybe he wants to get a quick buzz. I bet that Daniels guy's blood is 80% alcohol. Noah Raines: It probably is, but you see, the Setter is rabid, as are apparently the King of Spades and Harry Horowitz, uh, Leon Bubba Rundell. It's been going around the freak-wrestling circuit. Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: Well, I hope all that vodka in Daniels' system kills the germs. He's getting mauled!! This stuff is so hardcore it's almost hard to believe this isn't a quality federation! Noah Raines: Well, all four of these competitors are rejects from larger corporation. The Genuine Article were fired for drinking too much, the Setter when he started demanding that the referee say "Good boy, Setter" and pat him after victories, and Badd for sleeping around with the bookers' wives. So it's not too surprising that they're good. Well, Beam is getting seriously worked over on the outside still. He's making a pass at Angel!! I don't feel sorry for him anymore. Five of the Six People are hammering him. Billy U. Badd rolls Beam back in the ring! He executes the Badd Bomb! He pins Jim Beam!!! The referee looks up from "Goodnight, Moon" and begins counting. One . . . Two . . . Three . . . . NO!!!! Beam kicks out. He's mumbling something about how great Billy U. Badd is. Badd sits there and poses. He doesn't see Jack Daniels coming up behind him and whacking him with a chair. Ooh, that had to hurt!! Badd is down!!! Beam bodyslams him as Daniels prepares to legdrop him in what they call the Tequila Slammer!! Daniels hits him!! One . . . Two . . . Three . . . Billy U. Badd kicks out!!! The Genuine Article apologize to him and hug him. Again, he thanks them and goes over and tags the Irish Setter. Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly ** examining a diamond the size of Ragland, Alabama **: I hope the dog-boy doesn't bite Jack Daniels again. It's bad enough that he gave him rabies already. Besides, Bud Wiser tossed me a can of Michelob. He's kicking the King of Spades on the outside of the ring. The King goes for the "I'm Winning" leg lock. Oooh, Bud Wiser is hurt!! You suck, whiny poker-boy!!! Noah Raines: Calm down, Dennis, it was only one beer. Meanwhile, in the ring, Jack Daniels has gotten the Irish Setter in, uh, that move the rich snobby WWF guy does, **Consults the moves book again** the Pedigree Piledriver and has slammed his head into the mat!! The Setter is down!!! The referee is now reading "Curious George". He looks away from it and counts. One . . . Two . . . Thr -- NO!!! Billy U. Badd breaks the pin!! The Setter gets up, he's got Jack Daniels up for the Setter-Plex . .. . . He's still got him up there . . .. . Daniels must think he can fly now . . . The Setter suplexes him down!! He makes the pin. The ref counts!!! One . . . Two . . . THREE!!!! It's over!! Let's get down to Hubert Pubert. Hubert Pubert: Your winners at 21:20, THE IRISH SETTER AND BILLY U. BADD!!! Dennis "The Klepto" O'Reilly: The dog-boy should be disqualified!!! If the ref wasn't doing the "I Can Read" course in mid-ring, the Genuine Article would've won! Noah Raines: Well, that may be the case, but Billy and the Setter head to the penny booth tonight!
