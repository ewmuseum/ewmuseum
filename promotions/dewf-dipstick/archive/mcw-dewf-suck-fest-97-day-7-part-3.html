Hubert Pubert: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for two out of three falls with a one hour time limit, and is a special 'Separation Anxiety' tag team contest. The rules are very simple. A steel cage with a partition in the centre will be erected, dividing the ring into two smaller 'rooms'. Each team will have one member in each 'room', and the fight will begin. When one of the men is pinned, he will be eliminated, and his opponent will go into the other room to join his partner in a 2-on-1 handicap match... Brad Storch: They're bringing the 'Separation Anxiety' cage now. Slick Dick: I heard that cage was designed especially for this one match.
