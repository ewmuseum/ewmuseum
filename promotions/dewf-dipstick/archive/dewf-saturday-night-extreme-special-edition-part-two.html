Schiavone: (Voiceover) Let's cut to six long months later. It's August, and The Crows are facing The Neighborhood Of Evil. This is the attack on Kevin Sullivan that apparently started the whole war all over again. (FROM DEWF SATURDAY NIGHT EXTREME - AUGUST 30TH, 1997) The Crows (w/Succubus) vs. The Neighborhood Of Evil (Halloween and Hayabusa w/Kevin Sullivan) The NOE were first in the ring, enacting some bizarre ritual with Sullivan when over the PA came White Zombie's version of "Boogieman" and here came the ex-champs! But the NOE got first blood (literally) when Hayabusa nailed Crow 1 with a moonsault bodyblock from the top turnbuckle to the outside floor to open the match! Hayabusa missed his next move though, a senton from the ring apron to the floor when Crow 1 moved! Crow 1 bridged a table from the ring apron to the railing and set up Hayabusa on it! On cue, Crow 2 leapfrogged the top rope and elbowsmashed Hayabusa straight through the table! Crow 1 intercepted Halloween with a flying clothesline from the top, then pounded away on the masked man! He stopped as Halloween crawled back in, dropping a leg across Halloween's neck as he entered. Crow 1 even used the ropes for leverage, but still only got a two count. Hayabusa wasn't done, though. He hit Crow 1 with a German Suplex that folded him up like an accordion - but Crow 1 sat up, zombie style. Hayabusa wisely tagged out to Halloween while Crow 1 was catching his breath - and then the two dropped Crow 1 right back down with a spectacular flying double dropkick! It was pretty much all downhill from there for the ex-champs. Note I said, pretty much. The NOE totally dominated the next ten minutes, using split seconds tag team maneuvers and copious distraction from Kevin Sullivan to keep the edge. It was only when Halloween nailed the Pumpkinsault that Succubus saw her opening. She crept up on Sullivan - and sprayed him right in the eyes with a foul smelling red mist! The crowd went nuts as Sullivan rolled around in agony on the floor. The NOE unwisely berated Succubus for her interference. It was unwise because it gave Crow 2 (who had been on the receiving end of the Pumpkinsault) the time to make a switch with his partner, Crow 1! Halloween turned around - and got caught right in the mouth with a spin kick! Halloween staggered around as Crow 1 took out Hayabusa, giving the legal man Crow 2 the chance to come off the top - with a fire extingisher shot right to the top of Halloween's head! But that still wasn't the end... Joe Malenko roared down to ringside and dropped Crow 2 with a DDT. That was when referee Paul Richards had enough and called for the DQ. That got *him* a DDT - and then the crowd went ballistic as "Come Out And Play (Keep 'Em Separated)" blared over the PA and here came Raven. (match time:15:08) The Crows and the NOE fought all the way to the back as Raven and Malenko wailed away on each other. Schiavone: (Voiceover) Next week came Kevin Sullivan's response. One thing that escaped no one's notice was that Sullivan left right after the match started... Crow 2 simultaneously tagged out to Raven, who was met by an armdrag from Halloween, who promptly tagged out to Joe Malenko. Malenko took Raven over and down with a *nice* hiptoss and got a one count. Malenko ran into the ropes and ducked a Raven clothesline, only to get hit with a textbook single leg takedown. Raven hauled Malenko to his feet and whipped him into the turnbuckle. Crow 2 entered the ring and nailed Malenko with a savate kick, and Raven followed up with a small package which got a two count. Malenko ran into the ropes and nailed Raven with a clothesline, followed with a hiptoss, a fistdrop, then went for another hiptoss only to get it countered by a backslide by Raven, which got a two count. Raven followed up with a German suplex, which got another two. Raven hauled Malenko to his feet and chopped him (Big "WHOOOOOO" from the Mutants), tagging out to Crow 2. Raven nailed Malenko with a backbreaker, and Crow 2 dropped down on Malenko with a senton. Halloween broke up the pinfall attempt, then he and Malenko double-whipped Crow 2 into the ropes. Crow 2 ducked and leveled *them* with a springboard twisting double clothesline on the rebound! (*POP*) Halloween rolled out as Crow 2 rushed Malenko - getting backdropped over the top rope to the floor for his troubles. Malenko quickly followed, and dropkicked Crow 2 right against the ring steps. Malenko snap suplexed Crow 2 on the floor, then whipped him into the guardrail. Malenko re-entered the ring, and tried to suplex Crow 2 back in. No go. Crow 2 suplexed *Malenko* from the inside *out* to the floor, and then acted as a springboard for a spectacular Sasuke Special from Crow 1 *onto* Malenko (**POP**)! Needless to say, Malenko spatula'd himself back into the ring and made the tag to Halloween. Crow 2 hopped the top rope and ran right into a chairshot to the face from Halloween. Halloween layed the chair across Crow 2, drew his finger across his throat, and went to the top. Halloween's mistake came when he paused at the top to taunt the fans by playing to Sullivan. Crow 2 *sat up* and grabbed the chair - then knocked Halloween clean off the top and into the first row with what has come to be known as The Chairshot From Hell! Crow 2 was still groggy though, and Halloween had plenty of time to crawl back over the rail and collapse into the ring. Referee Mark Curtis started the ten count as both men were on the canvas, and trying to make their respective tags. Crow 2 *and* Halloween made simultaneous tags, Halloween to Hayabusa and Crow 2 to Crow 1. Crow 1 hopped the top rope, ducked a spin kick from Hayabusa and dropped the masked man with a stream of red mist in the eyes! Halloween rushed back in, and got dropped with a leg lariat! *Malenko* tried his luck, only to be met by Raven with a DDT! The crowd was going *insane* as both Crows and Raven hauled the NOE and Malenko to their feet, and cinched them up in headlocks! *TRIPLE DDT*!!! Crow 1 promptly sprang to the top rope and brought the F'N house down with a Shooting Star Press on Hayabusa for the one-TWO-THREE!!! (match time:13:42) Schiavone: Ooookkaayyyy!
