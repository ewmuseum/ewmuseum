Steve Carey: Dave, quit denying what you really wanted to happen. You hated Cline, you wanted to see him dead. Just because you've grown a conscience, you wanna pin everything on poor little Strangler. Well it ain't gonna happen.
DS: Steve, I never told you to kill Cline. I never told you to even harm him. My expressed orders were fo ryou to scare the living piss out of him, why did you change the plan?
(Rothschild grabs the mic from Carey and talks.) Wayne Rothschild: The order came from me. I own 50% of this company and I have as much a say in what goes on here as you do. DS: Wayne, you were kind enough to give me back my rightful 50% share in this company, but now you want to use the other 50% to further your own causes? Sounds like a bad deal to me.
WR: Dave, it comes down to whether you are with us or against us. I want to know, Dave. DS: Wayne, if your business is killing and destroying people's reputations, I will have no part of that.
