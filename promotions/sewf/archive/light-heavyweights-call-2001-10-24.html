SEWF: Calling All Light Heavyweights

24 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi. I'm [name withheld]. You may know me as # one hundred something on the RSPWF 200...or as a member of SpikyJim's Hall of Fame, or maybe even as the guy once known as the "moment of arrogance" guy, perhaps as the handler of Starman in
RAW, or...you have no idea who the hell I am. But now, allow me to introduce myself all over again... I am [name withheld], current president of SEWF. I took over about 2 months ago for [name withheld], and I am of the opinion that we are going better than ever.
That said, I am currently looking to create a Light Heavyweight Division for the SEWF. I'm assuming that the weight range will probably fall somewhere around 240 as the maximum. We currently have
12 individuals who fall under that weight limit. But in order to get some firey competition, I want more. I'm greedy, what can I say?
At this point, you fall into one of two categories, probably... 1) You're wondering why the hell you clicked on this thread. 2) You want a webpage to see more. Well, if you're looking for a webpage, right now, you're, to quote my good friend [name withheld], SOL. Because we don't have one. Pretty top notch organization, eh?
I've got one being constructed currently, and it will be up and running soon, I hope :). So if you are interested... Please e-mail me at [detail withheld] And I will be GLAD to send you anything you need, such as an application, some sample cards, sample RPs from our roster etc. I'd love to get an LHW division off the ground, but I don't have enough guys as of right now.
And well...for old times sake...allow me to present to you... A moment of arrogance I am [name withheld] I am the greatest ever to lace up the keyboard I am the man who is so bright that the sun requested I tone it down, because I was ruining his image.
Alright, that's all. Thanks alot for your time guys. [name withheld] PS This does not mean we are not interested in NON Light Heavyweights. I'm looking for talent whenever/wherever. Alright. This is REALLY all now. Thanks again.
