SEWF Monday Night Mothership Preview

3 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That's right folks, coming to you live from the Molson Arena in Montreal, it's... MONDAY NIGHT MOTHERSHIP!! And it's the beginning of the SEWF World Title Tournament, with everyone raring to go for the gold.
We start off the night with a Fatal Four Way Dance for the last remaining spot in the tournament between Kujo, Jack Paine, Andrew Krumm, and Jay
Miller. There's no love lost between any of these four, and with a tournament match looming later in the night, anything can, and will, go.
Then finally, the first round of the tournament begins, when "The Foundation" Alex Robinson takes on "Superstar" Vince Jacobs. "The Foundation" has been known for manhandling his opponents and sending them packing, will the returning "Superstar" be the next on his list? Or will
Jacobs live up to his moniker and move onto the 2nd round?
Tommy Kain has seemingly found himself after spending 10 days in a German prison following the Octoberfest PPV. His opponent Syko is on another hot streak, coming off a victory against his former partner Andrew Krumm. The respect is there, the drive is there, but only one man can move on, and we'll find out who on Monday.
In a match that noone knows exactly who is gonna be in it, it's sure to be a shocker, as the winner of the Fatal 4-Way, gets to pull double duty and face a mystery replacement for the missing Adam Mitchell. President Thomas Casey is high on this man's return. Some people have been dropping names in speculation, but noone but Casey knows just who it is. Whoever wins gets to keep going in the tournament, and that's all that matters.
Smoove is the Extreme Television champion, with no signs of losing the belt anytime soon. After a series of wins, albeit controversial, he's earned his spot in the World Title tournament, and is looking to get more gold for his invading EWWF brethren. In round 1, he'll be put up against "The
All-American" Jeffrey Meyers. Can Jeffrey Meyers upset the champ? Or will this turncoat walk all over the All American into round number two?
One half of the SEWF Tag Team Champions, Ian Blade, was jumped last week after his match, by the newly formed EWWF forces. There's been talk of retirement, but Ian Blade has been a rock lately, and is scheduled to face another SEWF newcomer in Ryan Sanders. Sanders isn't taking his assignment lightly and has had some choice words for Blade this week. It's guaranteed to be a good one.
The other half of the Tag champs, Jeff Kane, was also a victim of last week's attack by the EWWF, and is looking to make an impact of his own. New to the SEWF, Sean 'XXXstacy' Stevens finally gets to show the SEWF just what he's made of. Both men come from SCW, both men know each other well. Will
Sean Stevens feel ecstacy after he makes short work of Jeff Kane? Or will
Jeff Kane 'Sensationalize' Mr. Stevens, and take another step in earning more SEWF gold?
Larry Gionet has been looking for a way to silence his critics and prove just how legendary he is. This week, he'll most certainly get the chance against the founding father of the EWWF movement, Simon Solkoff. Solkoff would love to have the SEWF World title in his control, but he's gotta get through Gionet and the tournament first. Can we expect him to pull a trick from his sleeve? Or will Gionet break him off a little legendary something something on behalf of the SEWF?
And closing out the brackets, it's 2 former World Champions making their re-debuts here in the SEWF. Supergay Superstar Tom Collins has come back to the SEWF after an extended absence. Jarred Matthews has his first serious match in months against the man with the poodle. Will Collins continue on his queer quest to be queen of the SEWF? Or can Jarred Matthews come through once again to climb his way back to the top?
ALL THIS AND MORE! Surprises, shockers, and a whole lotta Canadian beer! So tune in to MONDAY NIGHT MOTHERSHIP, eh?
