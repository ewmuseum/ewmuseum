Goodbye — the SEWF's closing letter

11 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all, The SEWF is over with and in a way, that makes me sad. It may sound stupid or trite, but the SEWF has been a pretty large part of the last 4-5 years of my life. 5 years is a long time to be committed to anything, let alone an e-fed in a world where e-feds can be born one week and die the next. This is also a world where loyalty is not always the first thing on everyone’s list. I have been lucky enough to run with some of you guys for that entire 5 year run, and most of you have never left to look for greener pastures. There are those that would rather look at what feds I’ve closed, what mistakes I’ve made. Each time I closed a fed, I did so after probably a month or so of thought. You all mean a lot to me and to shut you out again was hard each time I had to do it, but each time the closing was for the best. Sometimes you just run out of steam, and those are the times when this “sport”, this hobby, becomes a chore and a job. This is a game, this is supposed to be fun. Most of us have lost sight of that more than once, but for those that have probably years to go in this sport, don’t lose sight of that again. This is a sport, fed heads are not paid, your character is fictional, this is not life or death. Have fun. I’ve had run ins with a lot of you. Things have not always been smooth in the SEWF world, nor could they have been. Jason Cline got killed, Johnny Sledge left on sour terms, Frazer Fury was well...Frazer Fury. I’ve probably had character disputes with the majority of you all, and I lost count of the number of times I wished Kurt wasn’t such a prick. At any rate, I apologize to anyone that has felt wronged by me or slighted in any way by something I’ve been a part of. I didn’t open this fed to make enemies, but it happened anyway. I’m sorry. This is the end of the SEWF, but this is not the end of me. I’ll be around, online way too much as always. I may be involved in an e-fed venture here and there, but the SEWF has always been my success, my mark to leave on this sport. The odds of me running another fed someday are remote, but nothing’s impossible. Perhaps if I can find a solid co-fed head, 5-6 solid match writers and a core of guys ready to have fun, something will be possible. However, nothing will ever again be done under the SEWF name. The idea of doing a big flashback show of some of the great SEWF moments is also tempting, but the outside interest just hasn’t been there for that to be much more than a personal ego stroke. I’ve tried to avoid problems with ego my whole career, why start now :) From the days of Hollywood Phil and Helmut to the days of Destroyer, Caladan Moore and Jameson Spleen, to the days of Don Marvin, [name withheld], Tom Collins, Bushido, and a very young Mike Masters, to the days of Jay Lange, Jarred Matthews, Simon Solkoff and countless others, you all have made this worthwhile and entertaining to boot. The SEWF has been built on the work of others, I only guided the ship once in awhile. I’ve had dozens of match writers, countless wrestlers, a handful of VP’s, and they’ve all contributed to the SEWF in sometimes miraculous ways. I’ve seen probably more good will and chipping in than I’ll see at any point in what ever career path I choose. I’ve been blessed with strong people around me, and I’d like to take a minute to say thanks to as many people as I can think of, in no particular order. Ryab: Might as well start with you since you’ve been hanging out with me the longest. From Maniac, to Destroyer, to Adam Mitchell, I knew we’d make a world champ out of you eventually. Granted, I didn’t think it would take 3-4 SEWF’s to get it done, but what can ya do. You singled handedly made the SEWF a possibility. You’ve written probably close to a hundred matches for me, you’ve rp’d consistently, you’ve pitched in here and there, and I still haven’t managed to piss you off too badly. You’ve been the cornerstone of this little e-fed lore I’ve built up, and it was only logical that you take the next step and fed head a fed yourself. The EWWF is attracting more people each and every day, even people I’ve never heard of :) First and foremost, good luck with the EWWF, even though those four little letters still get the hatred going in me. You’re a great guy and I’ve been privileged to have worked with your for so long. Have fun with the joys and perks of being a fed head. Teddy: Good lord what a few years can do for a character and for a person. Your sample rp contained a talking monkey god, your early career featured a loss to someone who typed in all caps, and yet you came to be arguably the most important in my e-fed life over the past year or so. Running this last SEWF incarnation with you was probably the most fun I’ve ever had running a fed. We had talent, we had match writing, and with you, we had a booking style that made it interesting. You get an unfair advantage working where you do, so it’s no wonder that Manson and Pyro are two of my favorite characters of all time. The dumpster, the Vic Valence/Terminator feud, your overall vision for the SEWF, they will stand for a long time. You’re one of the best friends I’ve been able to meet through all of this e-fed stuff, and I had a great time talking strategy with you on those late nights. Good luck in whatever you do. Matt: If it hadn’t been for the last SEWF run, I wouldn’t have been able to see what a cool ass guy you are, and what twisted shit you can come up with. The rise of JM to world champion is probably my favorite SEWF story of this latest run. You came into SEWF and I didn’t know what to expect out of you. I saw mid card or tag team talent in you, but you totally shattered that. You put out one of the strongest months of rp’s I’ve ever seen and you deserved every minute you held the belt. On top of all that, talking hockey, chatting with Sarah, hearing about the baby, I’ve had a great time. I hope you got out of the SEWF one tenth of what you put into it, as well as out of me. Raymond: Speaking of fun pushes to the world title, you were one of the most fun. You came out of the feeder feds :) and rose to world champ in almost stunning fashion. During a time when Frazer Fury, Jason Cline, and Rob Dalton were making my e-fed life hell, you came in with an attitude that I desperately needed to see in a talented wrestler. You have never once complained to me about anything that has happened. You have put forth the same strong effort, you have consistently been a positive voice, and you have been a great friend. You are one of the “good guys” in this hobby and I hope that you go on to run another fed or two. You’ve got a great knack at being a fed head and it was my pleasure to hand over the SEWF to you for awhile. The nickname Superstar will forever belong to you. Aaron: As a wrestler, you’ve obviously been very successful and your writing has been very compelling. When you came into the SEWF for this last run, I was worried about how your ego would work within the SEWF. I had heard horror stories about your UWC run, and the last thing I needed was a morale problem 3 weeks into the fed. That being said, it’s been a privilege to know you. During the last couple years, if I needed a big name for some project, you were there. If I asked you to do something, you were all for it. You and I and everyone that paid attention knows that you are extremely talented. I respect you for not rushing your way to a world title when this SEWF opened. You took your time, you fleshed out a new character, you made those around you better...and then you won a world title. You are as good an SEWF historian as anyone I’ve ever met, and while I’m not sure I was ever able to give you something as good as the UWC run you had, it’s been great for me and I hope you feel the same way. Nick: You are hands down one of the oddest people I’ve ever met. It’s strange to think about you as a guy that came into the SEWF all those years ago and rose to world title status in rapid fashion. After all the homosexual business, the Poodle, and God knows what else, it’s strange to think all the way back to when you first entered the SEWF. Hell, you were VP for awhile. You’ve made my e-fed life certainly more interesting and infinitely more entertaining. You’re another person that I’ve been able to connect with outside of e-w talk all the time, and that’s what makes doing this worthwhile. You became one of the “Big Four” or “Big Five” or whoever we were counting as legends that day, and you deserved the legend status that you received. You’ve been willing to work, you’ve been taking on new challenges, and you’ve been having fun. Thank you for bringing that energy to the SEWF and to me, it’s been fun. JD: You will probably go down as the biggest legend I’ve ever had in a fed, and one of the greatest rp’ers I’ll ever find. When you came into the SEWF, you attacked things with an energy that surprised me. Most of the new applicants take a couple weeks to get used to things, to begin to find a niche, but not you. You came into the SEWF and began throwing angle ideas at me left and right. You continued to have something new bubbling just under the surface each and every week. One of the bigger regrets I had when closing the SEWF was that you were so close to beating Hellkid for the title. You’ve been an enigma over the last couple years, coming in and out of my life and my e-feds in such an unpredictable fashion that it sometimes drives me nuts. Real life has taken up more and more of your time and I can certainly appreciate that. I hear that you’re living in Columbus now, so maybe we’ll bump into each other someday soon. You are, without a doubt, a legend, and I hope that you gained as much from the SEWF or UWC as you put into either fed. Tha Deaner: Probably the fourth pillar of that “Big Four” that was sometimes talked about, you are an even bigger enigma than the man above you. I would get a letter here and there, probably just so you could prove to me that you were alive. You rose to SEWF World Champ in a fashion that surprised everyone. The day I put the belt on you was the day the SEWF turned a very important corner. Frazer Fury was pissed, which was fun, and I had a world champ that was proud to be the SEWF world champ. You became the strongest VP a man could have ever asked before and you generally made my e-wrestling life better in whatever way you contributed. I haven’t talked to you in awhile and whether you actually read this or not is unknown, but I hope you’re having a good time and being successful. If somehow this finds its way to you, drop me a line, just let me know you’re alive :) Kurt: Good lord what a run you had. You refused to come from the DECWF to the SEWF, thereby destroying the whole feeder fed concept I had in mind :) I was only able to land your services after the demise of the DECWF. You went on to produce some of the most entertaining rps I’ve ever read. The Trial of Brittany has always been my favorite rp series of all time and it’s a testament to your writing ability. After the UWC, getting a positive word out of you was like getting head out of my girlfriend, next to impossible. I was never able to coax you back into competition, and I know time constraints played a big part, maybe the only part in that. Your willingness to produce some material for the SEWF Finale was great of you and I’m sorry I didn’t get to use it. Maybe we can see Brittany in action someday somewhere else. Kurt, I’ve always enjoyed your presence and as much disgust as you’ve thrown at me over the years, I hope it was good natured at the core. If not, I’m sorry for whatever happened to make you feel that way. Good luck with your writing career and an endless search for redheads and a desk. Jason: Your love for e-wrestling yet disinterest in pro wrestling has always been intriguing to me. The way you rp’d Jason Lange and Fang was unique and different. I enjoyed reading your rps and I enjoyed having you in my fed. Your willingness to help out with the Finale tour speaks volumes about you and I’ve been privileged to deal with you over the years. Your transition from the most dominant tag team I’ve ever seen to one of the strongest world champions I’ve ever had was masterful. You deserve to be mentioned along with the other legends of the SEWF, and as much as you believe the contrary, you often are. Your work is intricate, it is complex, and it is often a masterpiece. You are a great rp’er, you are a great wrestler, you are a great person that I hope does well at that job you work too hard for sometimes :) Good luck and thank you. Art: I really had no clue what I was getting when you decided to join up with the SEWF. I was excited about a couple of other people that signed around the same time as you, and when I mentioned your name to a couple people, they went nuts. I heard about the wonders of Art for probably an hour, but really hadn’t seen you in action enough to really be sure of your talents. The way you came in and took charge in the SEWF was insanely impressive. You gave the US title more prestige than it could handle, and your devotion to it was compelling. I wanted to push you to the World title for months before you would finally allow the US belt out of your sight. You are a cool ass guy on top of all this, and I have loved chatting with ya. You are a big gun, you are a solid rp’er, and you’re a great guy. I can’t ask for much more in this game. Because we didn’t go through the whole tournament and award the SEWF titles for the last time, I’m giving you the SEWF US Title for you to do with as you please. I know it seems like a silly thing to say in a big farewell, but I wanted the Finale to produce sound homes for a title that I think means a lot. The SEWF US title is yours and I feel very good about its final resting place. Clint: The last time I really got into the wrestling side of e-wrestling, I drew the misfortune of facing you. You can rest easy with the knowledge that you sucked whatever desire I had to e-wrestle out of me when you beat me that week. I rp’d I felt as strong as I could have, and I did it every day for a week, and you were the better man. Years later you came to my fed under the same name and somehow coaxed me into pushing you to a world title :) You had a great run and you were always something different to read. You were and are distinct, as much a credit to your writing ability as to your character. Let me know how things are going on the indy scene. I always check the indy results for XTC, hope all is well. Tell Hellkid to get off the crack for me. Ryan Noonan (Ryan A): The Dirk Ryan bullet list was probably years ahead of its time. You gave me something I looked forward to reading each and every time you did it, and you gave me a character I could count on for a strong stance. You came back to the SEWF and I considered it one of my great recruiting triumphs to coax you back. I looked forward to reading some new Dirk Ryan material and I’m sorry that the Finale ended before I got to. You have always been one of my favorite characters, and one of my favorite people as well. The fact that you’re an aspiring sports writer just as myself is just another thing for us to chat about. Hopefully we get to do more of it sometime soon, take care. Tommy Kain: You have the worst catchphrase and you’ve held on to it for far too long. Minus that, you’re a great wrestler and a great guy. You are definitely one of the big names in the history of the SEWF, and probably the best tag team wrestlers I’ve ever seen. You play off of people well, you’ve complained very infrequently, and while I’ve taken exception to certain things you have said in the past, they are just that, in the past. Good luck with your e-wrestling career and with your life. Lloyd: Talk about being with me for a long time. I’ve forgotten some of the character names, but you and Ryab could chat for days about the wrestlers that have come and gone in the SEWF. I was disappointed that I couldn’t do a better job of getting you involved in this latest incarnation of the SEWF, but I’ve always enjoyed your presence. Quiet Man Maldor and others have given me someone I can always count on for a solid week of rp’ing and a solid week of effort. You’ve been one of the strongest competitors I’ve known and you will go down in my mind as one of the great parts of this hobby. Good luck in the future. Luni: The fact that the only names I’ve known for you are Luni and Shorty don’t tell the whole story of just what you and I have gone through in this game. You were a great VP for me for many months and one of the most creative e-wrestlers I could ever hope for. You came to the SEWF unsolicited. At the time, I was recruiting Misfit. He come over to the SEWF and said, hey, take a look at this kid, he’s pretty good. A few months later, Misfit was gone, but you stayed for the duration. You have been one of my good friends outside of this e-w realm, and I hope we can talk more often in the future. I know work and everything else is taking up most of your time, but we need to do some catching up. Thanks so much for being a part of the SEWF and for showing everyone just how fucked up you can be :) Garry: Of all the people I thought would represent the SEWF proudly, you were certainly not one of them. There was a time when I thought that you were the biggest prick I had ever met, trying to get the SEWF kicked out of the alliance and all. Then, years later, you reappear in my inbox, looking for a spot. I was hesitant and it turned out I never should have been. I think you also had reservations going in, but it truly looked like you grew to love the place. If you ever felt held back by me, I apologize, it was not my intention. You have worked harder than anyone for the SEWF over the past few years and don’t think that that has gone unnoticed. You make a great EWWF Champion, and I hope you have continued success there. Jaime: You are another guy that I didn’t really know what to make of for a long time. It sounds stupid, but I really grew to like you. You had some personal problems there for a bit, you had some drama, but you were always just a cool guy to talk to and I kinda wished you were around more to chat with. I haven’t talked to you in awhile, but I hope everything is going well for you. You are one of the unpredictable people in this sport, as was Reed, and I liked that, kept things exciting. Iron Man: I don’t really know why I titled yours Iron Man, I just always liked that nickname. You have amazed me over the years with your quality of work and commitment to the product. You and Jason gave me probably the most reliable couple people I could have ever hoped for during an e-w run. I could always count on you two for some quality material that people paid attention to. The way you chipped in to help with the SEWF Finale will always mean a lot to me. I appreciate the way that you took on that commitment even being fresh off of moving out of state. You are a good man and I hope the job that took you away from the SEWF is treating you well. Dan: You are one disturbed man. Raggy is one of the greatest e-w characters ever made and it’s a shame that the SEWF didn’t get to see more of him. However, what we did see was great and thanks for bringing him over. UWC was probably a more Raggy-like environment, but it was damn cool having you around. Have fun spreading your brand of havoc in the real world, I guarantee they’re not ready for it. Droo: You are fucking annoying. Grow up. John O.: Well, I regret the whole thing that led to you pulling Sledge out of the SEWF this latest run. I’m glad you came back for the Finale and we both put that behind us. You were always consistent and that’s a rare quality in the e-w world. You were a great world champ and it was great having you in the fed. Don’t be a stranger. Paul Gibney: I hope this makes its way to you, I’ll see if I can find an email for ya. At any rate, you wrote the funniest character I’ve ever seen in e-wrestling or outside of it. The interaction between Jameson Spleen and Jenkins was priceless and I still think about the character when I hear “The Girl From Ipanema.” Your career path over the last few years has been almost dizzying and I hope you hit it big one of these days. You certainly have the talent. Good luck and hope to chat with ya soon. James Dean: Nope, not that one. James, you are a hell of a guy. You and your RAW crew were a breath of fresh air for the couple weeks that they rp’d :) Seriously though, I always loved how you rp’d and how you got into the SEWF product after being involved with RAW for so long. You did wonders for the SEWF as far as recruiting and definitely even moreso now in how you’re helping Ryab with the EWWF. You are a great guy and I hope to talk to you more in the future. Marcus: You weren’t in the SEWF for more than a week, but I hope everything is well with you and that your personal problem is going as well as it conceivably could. Hope everything comes out alright. Dreamy: You are the first and last known “raver” in the SEWF. If I hear about you going to another foam party, I’m going to puke. You had a great enthusiasm and I’m sorry that we didn’t get to use your talents as much as I wanted. If I ever open up another fed, you better get your ass in it and rp for me. Few of your talent level have gotten off so leniently... Mr. Valence: You ARE good and if I have to tell you that one more time, I will hunt you down, rip out your intestinal track, stuff it with processed meat and feed it to the dogs down the street. You are plagued by your lack of confidence and if you would just shut up and get to writing rps and working at strats, you could be great. It’s a cliche, but if you think you are going to do poorly or that you are going to well, you’re right. Pick yourself up and do something great. Paine: You need to get your stuff straight man. You go off on these tangents about your real life in a forum that you really shouldn’t be and then do these crazy ass rps. You have a great amount of talent, you just need to focus it a little better. Contrary to what you’ve thought over the years, I’ve never disliked you or tried to pick on you. You’re a good guy, a good rp’er, and I think you’ll do well if you concentrate on keeping your rps focused a little bit more. Anyway, don’t listen to me, I’m just a washed up fed head. I enjoyed having you in the SEWF. Angel: We’ve come a long way from the Spanish Announcers Table. Auntie Path is one of the best characters I’ve ever seen in e-w. Your SEWF stay was fun and it pushed some buttons, which is always fun. On top of that you’re a good guy and easy to talk to. I always like people that can come in and do something that gets a rise out of people. You were pretty good at that, thanks for bringing it to the SEWF. Frank Demola: How little I knew ye, but what an effort you put in for the SEWF Finale. I never had you in a fed and yet you still tried to go after the SEWF Finale like you’d been there for decades. I admire the work you put in and will always appreciate it. Good luck in the EWWF. Jeremy: I haven’t talked to you in forever and I doubt if this will ever make its way to you, but if it does, I hope life is going well for you. You were always a pleasure to have in the SEWF and your readiness to come over to the SEWF after the demise of the UWC was very much appreciated. You worked hard to establish this fed all over again and I thank you for that. Hope life is treating you well. Guru: You are one fucked up guy, but you write the funniest rp’s I’ve ever read. Keep on going with your TVTC. Mike Sweetser: You are one of the newer additions to the whole SEWF realm, and it was a great addition. I know you liked rp’ing with Termie better, but Southward was always my favorite character of yours. I think at times you felt slighted in the SEWF and for that I apologize. I enjoyed your characters and thought that you should have kept Southward around longer, I could have pushed that guy for weeks. Anyway, hope life is good for you as well and that you enjoyed your stay in the SEWF. Thanks for everything. Jeff Kane: You were the worst rp’er in history, and it wasn’t your fault. You were pushed far too much in your early days and the success made you think that your style was better than it was. However, I always admired how you sucked it up and came to the SEWF with an attitude that told me you were going to work at getting better. You did just that. It is a credit to you as a person that you worked through those early problems, came into the SEWF without any ego, and worked at getting better. Good luck in the future. Brighty: I finally got you into the SEWF. It took years it seemed like, but damn if it didn’t happen. You are a great guy with a lot of talent and I hope you get your turn at the top someday. You’re easy to talk to, solid as an rp’er, you have the skills to do it, so do it. Teaming with you back in the day was fun as well :) Have fun with the future. Blade: Another of the young guys in the SEWF, you were definitely making strides before I left. As I understand it, you’ve done very well for yourself since. You were one of the most highly touted young guys that I recruited and you lived up to that. I hope you continue to get better and develop. Smoove: You fall into the same category as Blade. I left just as you were starting to hit your stride. I hope you do well with all of your e-w stuff and otherwise. You’re another quality guy and you deserve to do well. If I forgot someone, I truly apologize. Thank you to everyone that made my SEWF career what it was. I had a great time working with everyone and I hope to chat with all of you in the future. I’m not going into hibernation, so don’t be a stranger. My email is always [detail withheld] and my AIM name is [detail withheld] if you’re interested. Anyway, thanks again and good luck to all of you in the future. I’ll miss the SEWF. -Former SEWF Prez. [name withheld]-
