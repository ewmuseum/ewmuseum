SEWF call for wrestlers and writers, 29 January 2002

29 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello ladies and gentlemen. I am [name withheld]. Also known as Starman, in RAW, as SEWF President, and the "Moment of Arrogance" guy. I am here today to attempt to get you to join SEWF. But this is no regular recruiting pitch...oh no. Last time I provided statistics which warped the RSPWF Top 200 to suit my needs...those statistics are as follows. 1) There are 200 people on that Top 200. SEWF had 14 people listed. Only two feds had more! We had 14! That's 7% of the entire list! 7%! 2) Let's break it down a bit further, let's look solely at the top 40. SEWF had 5 people there. That's 5/40, 1/8...12.5% of the Top 40! Now, onto today's statistics. Many people are looking for a federation which will provide timely cards. SEWF provides timely cards. We had 1 card last week. It was scheduled for a Tuesday. That card came out on a Tuesday. That means that SEWF, last week, had 100% of it's cards on time. 100% people! You can't get better than that! 110% is a myth. 100% is perfect, last week, SEWF was perfect. Another thing that people are interested in...RPs. We had 30 RPs last week, for 6 matches with a total of 22 participants. Let's delve a bit deeper into that. That means we averaged 5 RPs per match. That's entertainment value right there...5 promos per match! BUT... If you're one of those people who doesn't have a whole lot of time on their hands...who is looking for something simple...we had 30 Rps between 22 people. That's an average of 1.36 Rps per person. It's not much...we're not asking for 5-10 RPs from you in a week. 1.36 isn't alot to ask! If you do 1, you're doing 73.5% of the average of what everyone else is doing. That's a C! That's a passing grade! If you do 2 RPs you're doing 1.47 times the average! You're a go-getter! If these statistics don't convince you to be a part of the SEWF RPing family, check out... http://sewfv2.cjb.net http://groups.yahoo.com/group/sewfv2 http://www.angelfire.com/pa5/carnegie/Preshow.htm Or email me at [detail withheld] for more information Now, for the writers, I'd like to recruit... Here are some statistics for you. We've got 1 every week writer. Me. We've got 1 contributing writer...who writes most weeks. That means we've got at most 2 writers in a week. If you join, that makes 3. That means you're 33.3% of the writing staff! If we've got 6 matches on a card...and you write one, you've written 16.6% of the entire SEWF product for the week! You've had an influence on 1/6 of the SEWF card! You're huge! You're important! You're a key player! If you want to feel important, and feel loved, join our writing staff. Now. That said, please ignore what is to follow. Your Moment of Arrogance. I am a man who whether soft like warm chocolate, or hard like steel. Your Girlfriend loves the way I feel. Between her legs, or in her mouth. She SCREAMS when I move it in, and out... Whether it's on the floor, on the bed, or on her knees... I'm what a girl wants, and what a girl needs. I am [name withheld] I am your hero, your idol, if I dare say so myself, and I do...the greatest ever to lace up the keyboard. JOIN SEWF! SEWF: So good we make Good and Plenty look Average and Plenty.
