SEWF State of the Fed, August 2001

3 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Apathy, it's a simple word but damn if it won't destroy everything in it's path in a hurry. That's what I've been feeling for the past couple days about the SEWF. I thought about closing shop more than once and even talked it over with a couple people I trust.
The SEWF has been in a bit of a disarray lately and I take responsiblity. I've been lazy with recruiting and timeliness and a lot of other things that have made the SEWF a quality fed to call home.
Then I got into the mode where I was cruising around the old old old SEWF site. It predates a lot of people here and I saw names like Luscious Luciano and Hellkid and Dean McGrath and Killer Christian Holmes and damn if I wasn't inspired. A lot of those guys I gave a break to, many made their own.
Right now, the SEWF is in the process of creating new legends, wrestlers and handlers that we can talk about after I close the SEWF sometime down the road. Jarred Matthews, Adam Mitchell, Art Blake,
Mike Masters, Phah Q, these guys are going to be legendary names. For the past couple months, it has felt a little forced to write an SEWF card or answer a question or deal with a handler. When I re-opened the SEWF last August, it was a joy. Serious, that sounds dumb and all, but it was kick ass to be able to play that role again and answer those questions and be a fed head again. I love it and I probably can't stay away from it for very long. We've all seen that phenomenon time and time again.
What the SEWF needs now is a rebirth. As in any rebirth, some changes will be in order: 1) [name withheld] is no longer Vice President. He is now Head Booker. The man excels at that area of e-wrestling and he deserves to have that role. Of course, I'll be right there with him, but it takes some of the strain of being a VP off of him and let's him do what he does best, think up weird ass shit for you all to watch.
2) Everyone who wants to remain on the active roster, MUST e-mail me at [detail withheld] and tell me so. Otherwise, you will not be booked, simple as that. 3) I am in search of a new Vice President. I'm hoping a current, veteran wrestler will step up to do this. This is not an easy job and should not be taken as such. I need my VP to write one match a week and be reliable in that. I also need him to take the occassional card and run it. That means someone has to be flexible, pretty pro-SEWF and a friend of mine :) I don't trust my fed to just anyone.
4) I will again be on the recruiting binge and I'm lookin for help. It is now the task of everyone here to recruit quality wrestlers. It is in NO ONE's interest to have the same 15 guys here from now til eternity, that can get stale in a hurry. We've added some great wrestlers like Dylan McCrea and Owen King and others. They are out there and the SEWF is a solid enough fed to attract the best, let's do that.
5) You must bitch. I'm not saying it needs to be constant or redundant or even often, but when I screw up I need to hear about it. It'll make me better on strats and matches and it will make me pay better attention to you and your character. Speak up.
6) The more match writers I have, the more matches I can book and the more activity this fed sees. More activity means a greater intensity during the week that will climax in a better card. I try my best to do my part. I have acknowledged fumbling the ball a little bit lately, but I've been here for a year and I'm not going anywhere. I need handler support both in consistent flashing and match writing to make this a feasible undertaking.
7) SEWF fucking rocks. Seriously. I've been running feds since 1996. This talent base has grown and matured over the past few years and we are as about as rock solid as you can find. That is phenomenal, given the fact that some of our local legends (Luciano, KCH, Hellkid, others) are absent. It is a testament to us as a group that we've sustained such a solid run. We have a mix of guys that have been with me for years and guys that are just coming into the fold. Guys like
[name withheld] and [name withheld] and poor Adam Mitchell who has been in a [name withheld] run fed every year since 96 are, a lot of the times, the ones I depend on most and the reason I still have the SEWF. Then, there are guys like the Matthews and Cliftons and Paines that have been with me for this entire SEWF run and have made it successful. Add in an Art Blake, who came in a few months ago, or a Simon Solkoff, who rediscovered e-wrestling, or even a Dylan McCrea, who is new to the SEWF and you've got one hell of a talented mix of e-wrestling handlers. I have a lot of pride in the SEWF, what we've accomplished, and the product we continue to produce. My hope is that you all have that same pride and carry that over into your dealings with me and the rest of the fed.
I think that'll do it for now. Match assignments have been sent and the card will come out on Sunday. A new card will be posted on Sunday and WE ARE FUCKING BACK. I'm not dicking around folks, I'm pumped and ready to go. Let's fuck some shit up.
-SEWF Prez [name withheld]- PS: If you are an old SEWF'er and have been sitting on the sidelines, get your ass back in the game. I told you we're gonna fuck some shit up. Seriously...
