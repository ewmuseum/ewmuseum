SEWF (2000–01) — News

SEWF Evolution: The Press Conference

9 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SEWF Evolution: The Press Conference -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Camera fades into a background of SEWF logos. SEWF Spokeswoman Alexandra Chase enters from stage left and sits down at a large conference table. He has a simple piece of paper in hand.)
[Alexandra Chase] Folks, I have about 394083 other things I could be doing, so this will be very short. I am here to announce the card for SEWF: Evolution, live on Pay-Per-View on July 15th, 2001. Here it goes.
We are going to start this puppy with a bang. Two four man elimination matches will open up the card. The winners of the two matches will then go on to the following Sunday Night Mothership and fight for a US title shot. Should prove to be some great action with some of the new fresh SEWF talent going at it. Match one will be:
Tyler Daniels vs. Archangel vs. Vince Jacobs vs. Neil McDougall Match two will be: Jaime Roberts vs. Owen King vs. Kintaro Nomura vs. Mike Bright After that hot start, rising star Dylan McCrea will be facing Kujo. An extreme title shot will be on the line and I think this will be a good test for McCrea, facing a man who was in his position not so long ago.
Should be a great match. In a feud that's been well documented over the past few weeks, match one takes place as Xian Pu vs. the Pyromaniac occurs. God knows what will happen in this one, with the ringside area sure to get a work out. As a result of the card you just witnessed, The Stalkers will take on
Terminator and Sean Southward for the right to face the Flaming Jet
Planes later on in the card. This should be a great match with two diverse styles in the ring.
Up next is another match that has come about because of this past
Mothership. Paine chose to stick his nose in the business of Gabriel
Ragnarok and may just pay a hefty price. He defended Kujo, but he'll defending his well being in this No Disqualification match.
In a battle that has been brewing since the very inception of the
SEWF. "Messiah" Ray Hagan will take on "Risque" Rick Wallace. These two are titans in the business and will collide in a big way this coming Sunday. There is no love lost here folks.
As per Mr. Shaw's orders, Tommy Kain will be challenging Authority member Simon Solkoff for his Extreme Title. This one may get bloody and may rival Gabby's tirade on Paine earlier on the card. The mops will be out in full force on Sunday, ladies and gentlemen.
We lower the cage on two men so full of hatred that's the only logical end. Mike Manson will take on Andrew Krumm in the doorless cage. If past weeks are any indication, this is going to be a brutal match, and with no door, a difficult one to win as well.
The Flaming Jet Planes, the now two time tag champions will defend their titles against the winner of The Stalkers and
Southward/Terminator. If the Jet Planes want to set another tag team record, they have to make it through Sunday night. We'll see if that happens.
Hot star Wyatt Wallace challengs Adam Mitchell for his United States title. Mitchell has been a marked man of late and could have a very precarious hold on the belt. However, Wallace may have some big butterflies in his first high profile match since being back. Should be interesting.
And finally, the big one. Art Blake will challenge Mike Masters for the SEWF World Title. You've seen the events of the past weeks, Art
Blake's phenomenal US title run and the subsequent events. You've also seen Masters duck Blake for the past few weeks. There will be no place to hide this coming Sunday. Should be a great match.
That's all folks. No quesitons, please, I must get back to work. (And as quickly as she popped out of stage left, she exits. Camera zoom on an SEWF logo, then fade to black.)
