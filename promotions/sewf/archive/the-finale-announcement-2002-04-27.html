SEWF: The Finale — announcement

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone, Ok, I've gotten a fair amount of feedback on the subject and I've made up my decision. This is what's going to happen: SEWF: The Finale A Tour To Crown The Final SEWF Champion...Ever Mid June - Early September The format for this tournament will be similar to the World Cup. Wrestlers will be grouped into groups of four. They will then fight each of the other people in their group. The titles on the line for this part of the fianl tour of the SEWF will be the SEWF World Title and the SEWF United States Title. The SEWF Extreme/TV Title and Tag Team Titles will be decided at my discretion on the final card. After the groups finish their competition, the top two in each group will advance to the World Title tournament. The bottom two will go into the US title tournament. Given the quality of the people that have applied thus far, I think this will work out just fine. The Extreme/TV title and Tag Team Titles will be contested by people that fall out of the tourneys. Here is how the dates break down: June 23 June 30 -Fight first in-group opponent, 8 matches per night July 7 July 14 -Fight second in-group opponent, 8 matches per night July 21 July 28 -Fight third in-group opponent, 8 matches per night August 4 -Round 1 of the United States Title Tournament August 11 -Round 1 of the World Title Tournament August 18 -Round 2 of both tournaments September 1 -SEWF Send Off Card w/tourney finals and more fun and hilarity These dates are subject to change, but I'm pretty sure this is the shape this tour will take. Now is the part where I tell you who all is involved at the moment. Those people confirmed as part of this little party: Adam Mitchell Art Blake Dirk Ryan DreamMaker Gabriel Ragnarok Higor Rosa Ian Blade Jack Paine Johnny Sledge Junya Kontani Marcus Payne Mike Bright Mike Manson Mike Masters Simon Solkoff Steve Maldor Tom Collins Vic Valence Please add your name to this list and respond with a big ol' yes today. This will be the final SEWF event ever and I'd really like to get as many people involved with it as possible. I'm also obviously going to need help with this, match writers in particular. If you're at all interested in helping out or participating, please email me as soon as you can at [detail withheld] . I hope to get a lot of interest going for the final ride of a project that has spanned almost 6 years of my life. Thanks. -SEWF Prez. [name withheld]-
