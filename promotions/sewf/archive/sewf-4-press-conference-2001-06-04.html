SEWF 4: The Press Conference

4 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SEWF 4: The Press Conference -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Fade into a backdrop featuring the SEWF logo. Dave Shaw enters the room from stage left and takes his place behind a podium. A sparse spattering of flash bulbs go off and Shaw begins to speak...)
[SEWF President Dave Shaw] Ladies and gentlemen, it seems we do one of these things every few months where I open a fed or close a fed. In a way, this press conference encompasses a little of both. Tonight we release upon a skeptical and sometimes unforgiving public the pinnacle of wrestling achievement, my personal last hurrah and what I believe to be the most talented, most exciting, and most riveting organization to ever hit the squared circle. Tonight, we unleash SEWF 4.
(Shaw takes a moment for a dramatic pause, Shattner style.) [SEWF President Dave Shaw] It has became painfully clear that the SEWF experiences its greatest success when I am at the helm. That is not arrogance in any way, though it is a favorite characteristic of mine, it is the simple truth. Cline took hold of this fed twice. Once he drove it into the ground and the second time he almost succeeded. The Authority stepped up and fed interest dwindled. Rothschild, well, God knows what may have happened. I am back at power in the SEWF and it begins on June
10th at SEWF 4.
(Pause.) [SEWF President Dave Shaw] So, with the hype we have generated and the healing process we have undergone for nearly a week and a half, the SEWF is ready to re-enter the fray of the wrestling world and deliver to the public what they want. We are a federation of immense talent, vivid story-telling and impossible standards. We will meet or exceed each and every goal I set for this federation, and it's only out of the respect I have for those involved that I make such strong statements. We are the SEWF. Some people realize just what that means, some people will just have to learn. It is a powerful statement. We are the SEWF.
(Pulls a folder out of his briefcase and sets it on the podium.) [SEWF President Dave Shaw] With that, I give to you the SEWF 4 card. It will be an extravaganza, an event of grand proportions. It will be the catalyst the SEWF needs for the most furious, productive, enthralling summer run any federation can even aspire to hope to have, and here it begins.
(Glances at the paper.) [SEWF President Dave Shaw] In the opening bout, Ian Blade, Jeff Kane and Smoove will do battle in what is being dubbed as a Relaunch Match. Aptly named given the nature of these three combatants and the junctures their respective careers are at right now. We expect great things out of this match.
Following up will be a match up of maybe the two greatest tag teams in the history of the business. We are matching up the Outpatients and the Flaming Jet Planes in what promises to be an intense, vicious battle, as well as one that I will enjoy watching. We all know it's
Steve Carey, maybe Jarred will take a shot at him for me.
Next up is Kujo and "Messiah" Ray Hagan. Kujo once again gets the shaft in facing Ragnarok, sorry bud, he just won't sign the contract.
Hagan stepped up to the plate and promises to take the kid down a notch. His words, not mine. We'll see how that one comes out.
In our first of four title matches, Simon Solkoff will defend his SEWF
Extreme Title against Jack Paine. My disgust for both men will hopefully be assuaged when they grate each others faces into cheese on the steel cage that will surround the ring. Did I mention that weapons will be placed in the cage as well? Winner is the one to get out of the doorless cage first. Should be fun.
In maybe the only match able to follow such a spectacle, the tag teams of Sex Stable 2001 and Jonny Troisi/Neil McDougall carry their feud one step further, now involving the SEWF Tag Team Champions Burt
Clifton and Tommy Kain. The title will be in the line and the interweaving of hatred should produce very compelling television, and possibly a new champion.
The US title goes up for grabs when the title shot that Mark Marleau regrettably stole from Sean Southward finally gets rewarded. I may not like the man but I live up to my ends of bargains. He will face Tom
Collins in a showdown that will not only test Collins as champion, but
Marleau as a viable contender. We shall see, we shall see.
In a match that I don't even want to touch, Gabriel Ragnarok will fight "Wild Horse" Jay Lange. Lange has been a cornerstone of the SEWF for years, but Ragnarok may be an entirely new opponent for him to face. Lange's family and friends have been affected by Ragnarok's outrages and one can only shudder at what Raggy may be thinking up for
Lange. These two men will be the first of two main events of the evening.
And finally we come to Mike Masters. Masters, I promised the people a match booked the night of the card and that's just what I'm going to give them. Not you or anyone else will know your opponent until that very night. I hope it keeps you up nights, I really do.
(Shaw smiles to himself a bit.) [SEWF President Dave Shaw] With that I will take about three or four questions and be gone. [Reporter Guy #1] You mentioned surprise returns on the web site, www.sewf.net , any clues and are they still planned as such? [SEWF President Dave Shaw] We will have returns, but the exact number is not yet certain. It will be a surprise filled evening, that's for sure.
[Reporter Lady #1] Will any of the surprise returns have a bearing on who faces Masters? Will Masters face one of these surprise returns? [SEWF President Dave Shaw] Tune in and find out. [Reporter Guy #2] Where is David Briceland? He's been scarce as of late. [SEWF President Dave Shaw] A fine question, um...Steve. He's been having some health problems as of late and I think he's gearing up for a strong run in the SEWF just as we are gearing up to have him.
(Pause.) [SEWF President Dave Shaw] Folks that's going to have to do it, there is business to take care of. I will see you all again I'm sure on June 10th. Goodnight.
