SEWF: The Finale — rules and last call

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SEWF'ers, We're hitting the home stretch here before the cards are announced. I will be releasing the first six cards on Tuesday, June 11th. Let me go through a few of the rules and regs surrounding this tournament. RPS: Will be sent to [detail withheld] You are allowed 2 rps a week, no more They will be distributed in packets, one on Tuesday, one on Thursday On card interviews will count towards your score, but not against your rp requirement Therefore, the max you can do for this tournament is 2 rps, 1 on card interview and a strat per week. You may do all of that (save the strat) on your off weeks as well and they will count towards your next match. I will be direct subscribing you all to a Yahoo Group for the dispersal of these packets. Strats: Primarily should be focused on your match, high points, game plan, all the normal stuff You may strat against other people that are fighting, but you will not affect the outcome of their match. This can be done to set up a big match on the final card should you be eliminated or to prolong an age old feud, whatever. I encourage you to use this and get involved in the very high caliber of entrants we've assembled here. Other: Be thinking about if you want to enter the tag team foray or not. The deadline for tag teams is August 1st, which is after all your in-group fighting, so you will know more or less where you and others stand. Recruit any old SEWF'ers you can find. If you know an email address, send them a little something. I have a wish list of participants: D. Bricland/LL Tommy Grayson Dean McGrath El Dorado Killer Christian Holmes Luni Phah Q Wyatt Wallace Probably more As you can see, there's still work to be done. VERY IMPORTANT: Last call for new entrants is Monday, June 10th Here is how the dates break down: June 23 June 30 -Fight first in-group opponent, 8 matches per night July 7 July 14 -Fight second in-group opponent, 8 matches per night July 21 July 28 -Fight third in-group opponent, 8 matches per night August 4 -Round 1 of the United States Title Tournament August 11 -Round 1 of the World Title Tournament August 18 -Round 2 of both tournaments September 1 -SEWF Send Off Card w/tourney finals and more fun and hilarity If there's something in here that I haven't covered or if you have any questions, please email me here and I'll correct it. I'm very much looking forward to this and I hope you all are too. -[name withheld]-
