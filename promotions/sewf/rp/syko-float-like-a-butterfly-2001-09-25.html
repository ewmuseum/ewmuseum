SEWF (2000–01) — Roleplay

Syko: Float Like a Butterfly, Sting Like a Bee

25 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Syko: The greatest cruiserweight in the world, undoubtedly, is me. _____ [And what is the absolute best in his craft doing right now? Talking to his agent.] Syko, on phone: "So he accepted?! Great, that's perfect..... I can smell buyrates going up as we speak. Ok, cameras just got here, i'll call you back later. Alright... see you there... later."
[Beep. Now a little more on our setting.... we're still in Indy, at one of the hotels in the area.... as the cameras close in, so does a local area girl, who looks to be of legal age.......]
Girl: SYKO!! Hi..... I was just wondering, could you give me your autograph? Syko: Sure can, sweet thing... got something for me to sign? [The girl lifts her shirt, and points to her left breast (as opposed to the right one), and smiles]
Girl: Right there. Syko: Nice. [Being the nice guy he is, Syko signs, and gives the girl a nice little kiss on the cheek.... The girl, naturally, faints onto a nearby couch....]
Matt Hammer: What the hell was that? Syko: Natural talent. So what're you here for? The xclusive first interview with Syko after his loss to Seek and Destroy last night? To get the first thoughts on Luscious Luciano himself, naming my opponent for this week, the man they call Smoove, the man that hit me in the head with a bat and helped to cost me the match? Or are you here to talk about the arguments going on in the locker room between me and Andrew?
MH: Actually, I came to address everything.... Syko: Good. Then here. Take this bag. [Syko hands Matt Hammer a large suitcase. Hammer seems to have a hard time with it...] MH: Hey, I'm no bagboy. Syko: You want the interview? MH: Of course. Syko: Then you're helping with my bags to the car. MH: Bastard. So anyway, where should we start? Syko: Well, how about we start with a little respect for the man that gave you the two greatest interviews of your SEWF career. I don't think ANYONE will ever forget that Hammerlock, and the interview later in that week. I mean, you've been giving me shit since you showed up here.... I mean, yeah,
I lost last night, but it's not like it's the end of the world... I'm not gonna just run away... And that's part of the reason that i'm DAMN happy I got Smoove this week, and defending my title.... He proved something last night, that through all the times he came out to caused trouble against people like Ian Blade and Jeff Kane.... He proved to LL, he proved to the people in the back, the front office, the fans who hate his guts, and he proved most importantly, to ME, that he is one hungry sonofabitch. He's been playing Ian Blade like a fiddle for the past month or so, weekly, coming out and interfering. It's about time the front office took notice and did soemthing about it, and gave this man a shot. And boy will he ever get it this week....
MH: But before we get too into that, I want to ask you about the partnership between you and Andrew Krumm. The match last night, and what went wrong....
[Now making his way through the parking lot, Syko continues.] Syko: I'll tell you what went wrong right now, we weren't on the same page. You could see it when we came out there, no music, no pyro, not a damn thing, just two guys who've been arguing for 2 weeks, having to team up against Seek and Destroy. {Video footage from the Mothership is shown] We would have won the match, because if you watch the match, you see me go up to the top, ready to launch the Certified Bomb, but Andrew whipped one of them into the ropes, it shook the turnbuckles, and I ended up in the back with an icepack on my balls. [Back to current visual.] Not exactly the best showing for us, now was it? This week, we're doing our own thing, he's got his match, and I've got mine. Hopefully in the coming weeks, we can work this all out. But as of right now, we're barely on speaking terms, and I'd like to thank Xian Pu for keeping the peace and a clear head throughout all of this. But for now, that's all I'm gonna say on the matter. Next question.
MH: Okay. As you know, Syko, the Extreme Television title has a bit of power to it, as the holder makes the stipulations for the title defenses.... what do you have in store for this week's defense against Smoove?
[Syko pauses briefly, standing in front of his car, giving the question a bit of thought before answering.]
Syko: Well, I haven't put too much thought into the exact stipulations.... but I will say this, there will be a special guest referee for this match, that I have handpicked from the SEWF roster. He is none other than the 7'2,
400+ pound monster known as IAN BLADE.
MH, putting the bags into the trunk: But that's insane, you can't possibly expect Ian Blade to call a fair match after all that Smoove has done to him!
Syko: So? It's not like I've never done anything bad to the man.... I mean, shit, I can think of at least 5 instances when I was SSW commisioner where I completely screwed the guy... titles, money, you name it. So I feel that
Ian'll do the right ting for himself, the fans, and this match..... As for any extra special stipulations..... I'll settle for.... hmm.... how 'bout we make it, FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE. Yeah, I think that's fair. Falls Count
Anywhere. In the street, the parking lot, Luciano's office, anywhere. MH: How do you figure that as fair, Syko? Syko: Well, you don't expect Smoove to beat me 1,2,3 in the middle of the ring, do you? MH: I don't know about that, Smoove has more than proved he can get the job done in the ring in recent weeks.
Syko: Yes. He has. Against peopel like Ian Blade, Jeff Kane, and whoever else he's been facing.... but never in his entire life has he stepped in the ring with a man of my caliber.... I mean, seriously, I'm the GREATEST cruiserweight to ever live! The fans love me, and I love the fans.... I'm driven by the people, the fans, the fame, fortune and glory..... this belt, this title, wasn't merely handed to me on a silver platter... I earned this title against 3 other men.... It symbolized my fame, my presence here in the
SEWF..... People RESPECT what I do in the ring, because I sure as hell know how to get things done in between, outside, and above those ropes.....
Unlike Smoove, I don't need to run into a match I have no true business in, every week, to show just how good I am... I get my airtime in my title matches, and delivering verbal beatings like this one throughout the week.... I MAKE PEOPLE CARE ABOUT PEOPLE LIKE YOU SMOOVE. And like it or lump it, I will beat you, I will remain the SEWF Extreme Television champion, and I will show you, just like I've shown everyone else, just why
I am the greatest opponent you'll EVER face here in the SEWF. You can have the commisioner respect you and give you a match like this. You can have
Thomas casey, personally, come out and kiss your ass on TV, saying how you've got a real shot at taking me out.... and quite frankly, you can be the greatest threat to my reign yet.... But I'm not ready to give up this title. Not now, and not to you. Come sunday night, you'll see just what lengths I'll go to to keep this gold around my waist. I refuse to go out without a fight, and without putting every last drop of blood, every last breath, every last piece of my being into this match... Rest assured,
Smoove.... if, by some VERY slim chance you beat me this week.... then you truly were the better man. Now if you don't mind, I've gotta get back to New
York to finalize a few deals. Believe me, I'm just getting warmed up,
Smoove. This week, you find out what Syko is all about. [Syko gets in the car and closes the door as Hammer speaks...] MH: Well, there you have it SEWF fans, Syko, the Extreme Television champion, giving his thoughts on everything. For the SEWF, this is Matt
Hammer. We now take you to more SEWF programming here on your SEWF network.
