Syko: Jeff Kane Appreciation Night

Yep, another one. I was drunk as hell when I did this. I know it's a blatant ripoff of Stone Cold Appreciation Night... but who doesn't appreciate Jeff Kane?
Well, besides everyone. Anyway, here it is in all it's drunken glory. ---------------- Messages Messages Help Reply | Forward | View Source | Unwrap Lines Message 1063 of 1124 | Previous | Next [ Up Thread ] Message Index Msg # From: "SEWF RP Guy" <[detail withheld]> Date: Sat Aug 25, 2001 7:29 pm Subject: [SEWF] Jeff Kane Appreciation Night [Ah yes, another day, another fade. Flushing, NY, smack dab in the middle of the dense asian community. And what do some asians like to do with their free time?]
[No genius, not download porn.] [No, not make Kung Pao Chicken. What the hell is wrong with you?] [Yes. Karaoke. So it's no surprise that Main Street has a few K-TV places scattered about. But we're only in one, and there is in fact a reason behind it. Syko is here. And there's a special occasion at hand, but i won't spoil the promo for ya. Go on now.]
Syko: "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Jeff Kane Appreciation Night!" Crowd: "BOOOOOO..." Syko: "Yes, I realize Jeff Kane truly does suck. But a sensational man such as him deserves a bit of recognition, after all, who else had the balls to even make a statement for this match against me for the title that's rightfully mine? Who else could have made a bid for what will become the greatest title the SEWF will ever know.... And who else draws more ire, hate, and death threats that Jeff Kane?? Huh? Nobody.
And that is why we have Jeff Kane Appreciation Night here at K-TV here on
Main Street." Crowd: "YAAAY." Syko: "So my first note of business, is in honor of this occasion.... HALF PRICE DRINKS FOR THE REST OF THE NIGHT! MY TREAT!" Crowd: "YAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAY!!!!" [Did we stumble into the old UWC crowd here or what? =P] Syko: "Second, I just wanna take a moment to reflect on Jeff, and what he's said.... Jeff, you claim you want the war to end all wars in that ring this
Sunday. I'll be straight with you, you're not gonna get the war to end all wars. I mean really.... It's you. The beating to end all beatings maybe, but most certainly not a war. Seriously, you're about as adept as a fish in a desert in a match against me. You wanna challenge my heart? You think that's where I lack compared to you? You think I don't want that title as much or more than you? Are you stupid?
Wait. Dumb question. We already know you are. You're determined. Good. I like to see that maybe you're growing a set.... but against me? Bro, my pubes are bigger than your set, don't come to me about determination. I'm coming back offa getting bucked during a time when i was just getting warmed up.... I was on my way to being something great in the SEWF.... now I'm back and if you think for one second I don't wanna return in grans style, kick the ass of you, JM, and TK, and take home a title that BELONGS AROUND MY WAIST..... then you got problems my friend.
You want a war though? You'll get one. You want the very best of Syko??? Brother, you can't HANDLE the very best of
Syko. But ask and ye shall recieve. And after it's all over, and you're in that semi-conscious state after I hit you off with one of my many and various moves of skill and grace... tell me then whether or not I had enough to beat you like the silly bitch you are.
But enough of that right now. Right now, I'd like to perform a song here at
K-TV.... it's based offa some other song you guys probably know, but I gave it that nice Syko touch, so here goes. Cue it up please? Thank you.
[Hey... is that Wind Beneath My Wings? Don't tell me Syko's gone soft?!? C'mon everyone, sing along anyway.] o/~ o/~ o/~ It must have been cold there on the canvas... To always have the lights on your face. You were content to take a loss, that's your way. You always were a big disgrace.... So I am the one with all the glory, While you are the one with all the pain. A ugly-ass face without the skills, for so long. A SENATIONAL grin to hide the pain. Did you ever know that you're a zeeeero, And everything noone wants to be? I can fly higher, cuz i'm SYKO... And you are the face beneath my knee. It might have appeared to go unnoticed, But I've got it all here in my head. I want you to know I know the truth, of course I know it. Everyone here wishes you were deaaaaaaad.... Did you ever know that you're a zeeeero, And everything noone wants to be? I can fly higher, cuz i'm SYKO... And you are the face beneath my knee. Did I ever tell you you're a jobber? You're everything, everything I wish I could kill. Oh, and I, I could fly higher CUZ I'M SYKO, And you are the face beneath my knee, [*snif* So touching.... so real.... so beautiful.... and a certain billion dollar ho can't butcher it..... life is grand...]
Crowd: *applauds and whistles* Syko: "Thank you, thank you.... you're all way too kind, really.... It's just a little something I wrote on the train over here..... Something that really captures the essence of the whole situation..... Now lets go on appreciating Jeff in the final moments of his career..... and PARTY LIKE
CRAZY!" Crowd: "YEAAAAH!!" [Fade out.... to audio!] Syko: "See you Sunday, Jeffy.... And for godsakes man, get yourself some gear, those sweats looked BEAT...."
