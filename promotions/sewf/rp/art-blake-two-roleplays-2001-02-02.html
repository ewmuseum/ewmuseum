SEWF (2000–01) — Roleplay

Art Blake: Two Roleplays

2 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The two roleplays that follow are a sample of the work I have done in the
SEWF. I chose not to write a new roleplay mostly because I'm not in the right frame of mind (read: I'm really rather cranky, and it wouldn't turn out very good).
I don't expect any of you to like the roleplays, so feel free to abuse them all you like, it's not going to hurt my feelings. All I ask is that you view them objectively, and judge them on their entertainment value alone.
I should also note that one of the roleplays contains characters that some might find offensive. I apologize in advance to anyone who takes offense at anything in the roleplay. The two characters in question are "ethnic" in nature, and are portrayed in an odd manner.
A couple of other quick notes before I get to the roleplays. This will help explain a few things that are left out because most of the people that read them originally knew the characters involved.
1. Bobo is my pet Chimpanze, who also acted as my manager at one point. 2. Fluffy Bunny #6, the mime, has Tourett's syndrome. 3. They're kind of long. I get carried away sometimes. With that said, here are the roleplays: ------------------------------------------------------------------ DISCLAIMER: What you are about to see is a satirical look at what could be an average production day in the SEWF. In no way is this meant to imply that anyone in the SEWF does produce their interviews in this manner. You have been warned.
[Fade in to that all to familiar white room. Standing in the center of the room, as we perceive it, is not Art Blake, as we have come to expect.
Instead, the man we currently see before us is considerably shorter, and skinny like a toothpick. He looks very uncomfortable in the tuxedo he is wearing, but still manages to smile genuinely. His hair is brown, and in a mop top style that reminds us all of the days when the Beatles were on the
Ed Sullivan Show.]
Lenny: "Good evening, my name is Leonard Hansen. Tonight on SEWF: Behind the Scenes we'll be taking a look at how the interviews you watch every day come to be. The subject for tonight's show is Art Blake."
[Lenny begins to walk to his right. The scenery changes abruptly from stark white to the dark, utilitarian images one would expect to find in a sound stage. Large lights shine on the white background where Lenny once stood.
Large thick cables run across the floor in every direction. People with clipboards scurry here and there. Other people cluster together, discussing elements of the script, and how to translate them into meaningful images.
Lenny stops at a long table upon which sit plates of cookies, and decanters full of premium gourmet coffee. He pours himself a coffee, cream, no sugar, then leans lightly against the table.]
Lenny: "Art and I have known each other for a long time. In fact, he got me my start in the wrestling business. I went on to disappoint him, and fans around the world. Some of you might remember me as Masturbating Lenny.
Well, needless to say, I was forced off of TV because parental groups the world over complained. They didn't think I should be encouraging that sort of behavior, so they put pressure on the promoters to get rid of me. I can'
t say I blame them, really. I did get a little carried away, and my talents are more suited to porno movies anyway.
So, lets get started, shall we?" [Cut to a small, smoke filled room. The once white walls are stained yellow by the nicotine from 8 monkeys and a chimpanzee all chain-smoking Camel
Straights. The monkeys are working feverishly at typewriters while the chimpanzee appears to be supervising, and is carrying a large whip, which he occasionally uses to motivate the monkeys.]
Lenny (VO): "If any of you have been wondering what ever happened to Bobo, here he is, supervising the crack team of writers who provide the dialog used in the interviews."
[One of the monkeys yanks the piece of paper from its typewriter, and hands it to Bobo. Bobo takes a drag from his cigarette as he looks over the paper. The monkey looks on nervously as it lights another cigarette off the smoldering remnants of the one dangling from his mouth. The chimp begins to screech wildly as it crumples up the paper. The other seven monkeys look up from their work to see Bobo savagely beating the poor monkey with the whip.
The monkey slinks back to its typewriter and begins typing furiously. The chimp glares at the other seven who immediately return to work.]
Lenny (VO): "Once the script is approved by Bobo, it is passed on to the director for review.
[Cut to a skinny man in a very loud shirt sitting in a director's chair on the sound stage. He wears on his head a black beret, a monocle over his right eye, and smokes cigarettes through a long black filter. He nods as he reads. A mime stands behind him, mimicking his every move.]
Alistair Cloudfruit: "Yes, I think I can work with this." [Alistair, the director, begins barking orders at any number of crewmembers. They begin scurrying around, gathering props and scenery, checking the lighting, positioning the cameras, and all of the other things that need to be taken care of for production to begin.]
[Cut to the exterior of the building. A dark green 1968 Mini Cooper 850 pulls into the parking lot at a high rate of speed. It screeches to a stop near the entrance to the building.]
Lenny (VO): "And then the star of the show arrives." [Art Blake steps out of the car, and locks the door. He wears his usual attire, shorts, t-shirt, Doc Marten's, sunglasses. His hair is combed straight back, as though he actually took some effort to do something with it this morning. He stands next to his car for a moment, adjusts his sunglasses, and takes a deep breath of the fresh air. He circles around the car, and heads towards the entrance to the building. As he passes the camera he nods at it in acknowledgement. He flings the door open dramatically, and disappears into the building.]
[Cut to the lobby of the building. Blake steps up to the receptionist's desk. The receptionist, a heavyset, unattractive woman with a gruff demeanor, answers phone calls.]
Thighs of Death: (into the telephone) "Blake Enterprises, what the hell do you want?" [She holds up a finger, indicating that she'll be with Blake in a moment.] T.o.D.: (into the telephone) "No, he's in a meeting right now, you gotta problem with that? I didn't think so."
[She begins to scribble something on a pad of paper.] T.o.D.: (into the telephone) "Yeah, I'll be sure he gets the message." [She slams the phone down, rips the piece of paper off the pad, and hands it to Blake.] T.o.D.: "Hey boss, they're ready for you in wardrobe." Blake: "I swear, if you weren't my sister, I'd have fired your ass a long time ago." [He looks over the message on the paper as he walks towards a set of double doors. He pulls open the door, crumples the note, and tosses it into a nearby potted plant. He continues down a long corridor, eventually turning into a large room filled with costumes and clothes of all different styles, shapes, and sizes. A midget in a black leather mask and bodysuit with all sorts of buckles and zippers, and clips on it sits at a table with a man of
Arabic descent in a Santa Claus costume. Upon the Santa's chest are strapped several sticks of dynamite and a makeshift timer. They appear to be playing scrabble, though neither one of them speaks English. They are arguing over use of the words "lo que", in which the q was placed upon a double letter score, and the l on a triple word score. The midget speaks exclusively in Spanish, and the Santa only knows Arabic. The Santa stands and points furiously at the bomb strapped to his chest, but the midget just mocks him. Enraged, he screams out "Praise Allah!" in perfect English as he attempts to detonate the bomb. The bomb fails to exploded, so he just nudges the scrabble board with his hand, messing up the letters.]
Lenny (VO): "Wardrobe is where things really begin to take shape. Here the clothing that Art wears is carefully selected by Enrique and Achmehd."
Blake: "What have you got for me today? The usual?" [Enrique shakes his head, and points over at a rack of costumes. Achmehd walks over to the rack, and pulls a large pink bunny costume from its hanger. Blake looks at the costume in disbelief.]
Blake: "You're not serious." [Enrique, the bondage midget, nods his head and hands Blake a copy of the script.] Blake: "Sweet zombie Jesus. Those writers are going to pay for this." [Cut to Alistair Cloudfruit sitting in his directors chair holding his fancy cigarette filter in his right hand. A copy of the day's script sits in his lap. The mime is still behind him, desperately trying to get out of an invisible cube.]
Cloudfruit: "I studied poetry in college. Actually, Fluffy Bunny #6 and I performed some of my original works on the beatnik coffee shop circuit a few years ago while I was working as a professional wrestler. Art got me the job as a wrestler, even though I was never really good at it, because he liked my poetry. When he came to me and asked if I'd direct his interviews
I was really excited. It gives me a chance to express my artistic vision in new ways."
[Blake marches onto the sound stage wearing the pink bunny costume. On his face is a rubber rabbit's nose, complete with whiskers. His front teeth have been augmented so that they appear elongated, much like a rabbits might be. Needless to say, he does not appear happy. He looks at Cloudfruit,
then raises his arms in exasperation. Cloudfruit picks up a megaphone, and begins barking orders again.] Lenny (VO): "Once all the preparations are made, filming can begin." Cloudfruit: "Okay everybody, places! Get those lights into position! Okay now, Art, in this scene your."
[Cut to Billy, the Spotty Faced Intern, sitting near the decanters of coffee. He is dressed in a lion costume, without the head on at the moment.
He takes a sip of coffee.] Billy: "Actually, my real name is Langdon Moore. The writers came up with the idea for Billy, and they figured I'd be a good person to play the part.
Problem was, I have a very good complexion. Now I spend 3 hours in makeup every day to achieve the spotty faced look Mr. Cloudfruit wants. Every day!
Even if I'm not going to be in the interview, I have to go through the makeup ritual. Can you believe it? He says that it will help me to get in character. What a bunch of crap! I need the money though, so I can't complain too much."
Cloudfruit: (from off camera) "Billy! You're presence is requested on the set!" Billy: "Oops, gotta go!" [He sets down the cup of coffee, and hefts the huge lions head over his own. He waves to the camera, then waddles off towards the set.] [Cut to Billy (in the lion suit) and Art (in the rabbit suit) standing in front of the white background. Both are holding copies of the script.]
Lenny (VO): "As filming progresses, it can take hours to get just the right feel for each scene. Alistair Cloudfruit goes to great lengths to get his actors to buy into the artistic vision."
Blake: "I can't read this line." Cloudfruit: "But the whole scene hinges on this line." Blake: "Yeah, well it's going to need to be rewritten. It's just not something I'd normally say."
Cloudfruit: "But this is not a normal situation. You're going to be fighting Tommy Grayson. You need to be fresh, and new. You can't say the same old things day after day. It gets predictable."
Blake: "So, that hasn't stopped Tommy from saying the same things day in and day out." Cloudfruit: "Yes, but you're different than he is." Blake: "True, but I know one thing, a mocha might make me more willing to say this dialog."
Cloudfruit: "BILLY!" [Billy, in the lion suit, jumps at the sound of his name. He looks at
Cloudfruit, though you can't really tell because of the costume.]
Cloudfruit: "Get Mr. Blake a mocha. NOW!" [Billy rushes off the set, and returns a few moments later carrying a white paper cup with a circular green logo. He hands the cup to Blake, who takes a sip, savoring its flavor.]
Blake: "Ahh, that's a good mocha. Unfortunately, it didn't make me want to say these lines. I'm going to go talk to the writers."
[Blake walks off the set carrying the script in one hand, and a mocha in the other. He exits the sound stage to the astonishment of Cloudfruit.
Cloudfruit quickly regains his composure as the mime fights against an incredibly strong wind, that no one else seems to have noticed.]
Cloudfruit: "Okay everyone, take 5. We've got a couple of problems with the script. We're going to have a talk with the writers, and see if we can work a few things out."
[Cloudfruit jumps out of his chair, and chases after Blake, followed by the mime, who appears to riding an invisible bicycle. The rest of the crew just stands around, talking amongst themselves.]
[Cut to Lenny standing next to a television monitor. He's still wearing the tuxedo, and still smiling.]
Lenny: "After several more rewrites, a final script is accepted by both the actors and the director. Once filming is complete, it goes to the editing room, where the raw footage is spliced together, and special effects are added. Then, after several hours of work, the team is ready to present the final product. Lets take a look."

The camera rushes towards the closing door, and is easily able to slide underneath it before it closes.]

Blake. Blake sighs, and picks up a mocha that had been waiting for him on a small table near the door into the house. He sips the mocha and feels energized. The garage, it should be mentioned, it very tidy, unlike the average American garage. On one side of the garage is parked a new Land
Rover Discovery, it's yellow. Don't ask why. Aside from the ladder hanging on one wall, the lawnmower, and a few miscellaneous tools here and there, the garage is nearly empty. Art opens the door to the house and peeks inside. He quietly closes the door, and whispers to the camera.]
Blake: "Shh, I told my wife I wouldn't bring any work home with me." [He takes another sip of the mocha.] "Now, Grayson's been pretty quiet today, so I don't have a while lot to say. I will say that he going to have to prepare better for this weeks match then he did for his match with Mitchell. Mitchell's got some good moves, I'll give him that, but Tommy seriously fucked up. He was practically begging
Mitchell to take that belt from him. In fact, I'm glad Tommy lost the US
Title. That takes the specter of it off of our match. Now I don't have to worry about people saying that I was only after him because he's the US
Champ."
[Blake pushes the button to open the garage door, and it can be heard opening behind the camera.]
"Fact is, I'm not about titles. I'm about kicking Tommy's ass. And anyone who tries to stop me, I'll kick their asses too. Yeah. And."
Female Voice From Inside the House: "Honey, who are you talking to?" Blake: "No one." [Blake motions for the cameraman to leave before they both get in trouble.] FVFITH: "You're not talking to that cameraman that's been hanging around outside all morning, are you?"

Fade to.]
[Lenny is still standing next to the video monitor. He turns to face the camera.] Lenny: "Amazing, isn't it? It takes a lot of work to generate one simple, two-minute interview. Think about that as you watch the other interviews this evening. Thanks for tuning in."

As we pass by Alistair Cloudfruit and Fluffy Bunny #6 (the mime), Fluffy begins cursing like a sailor as he struggles to pull some unseen object closer to him with an equally unseen rope. Cloudfruit laughs, and slaps his knee. Fade out.]
