Monikers and Blood Moon — a Tom Collins roleplay

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You've wanted the chance to see an RP. You wanna flame me and you wanna torch me. Here's my latest RP and the story. Tom Collins (moi) in the SEWF has been on a horrendous losing streak and is making a super heel turn. He's been a face forever and this RP complete/facilitates the turn. Be honest, I hope. But I just wanna see the reaction. FLAME ON!! =============================== [SEWF] Monikers and Blood Moon Tommy could've been called the luckiest boy in the world. He was 26 and had everything that Egri used to define happiness. Love. Hope. Health. A satisfactory position. He was wealthy, both in wallet and in respect. He saw his peers only by looking below, yet he knew the peak lay far ahead. There was so much gained, and yet so much more to be gained. And over the past months he metamorphosed. His grin faded, and, as another did long ago, he lost his smile. His appearance changed. His slender, manicured hands warped. His fingers seemed to square, resisting to taper until reaching chewed and uneven nails. Skin folded like amber waves over bony knuckles and cuticles splintered and cracked. Thick veins snaked under his skin, rough and dry. Between furrowed brows new lines emerged, dug deep like scars. His once blue eyes greyed and lines tapered off their ends, cutting his face. His lips resided now tightly pursed and chapped. Shoulders sagged, posture slumped. Shorter steps, deeper breaths. Consider him now. Alone in his car, a blood moon lurks over a weathered cityscape. The radio is silent now. Tires bouncing over the cracks and potholes as drums now. Squealing breaks and whining engines as voices now. Love...loneliness now. Hope...despair now. Health...pain, aged now. A satisfactory position...stepping stone now. Now. Now. Monikers like freeway's white stripes fly past; countless. Faggot. Superstud. World Champion. Legend. Steps through a rite of passage. A rite of passion. A heart bled dry and muscles worked dead and bones broken to powder and soul eroded to....what? A lifetime spent to build his career and his name. Where is Tom now? Where is the Faggot/Superstud? What closet does the World Champion hide in? In what shadows does a Legend creep? The shell they used to reside in now drives briskly along the Detroit River waterfront. The car pulls down a side street, flanked by broken houses, broken cars. The stench of broken dreams and broken lives rises ripe. Collins steps out after parking at the dead end and strolls to the embankment. The maple trees are spread eagle, naked in the wind. A fallen wedding gown of snow braces their trunks and mounds of exhaust blackened ice and slush corner driveways. The embankment is downtrodden, like the rest of the city. Frozen mud, littered with the remains of malt liquor and filet o fish wrappers that get hooked and swirled in the wind. An all too common postcard of Detroit. The Motor City!! The City that drives the world!! The Birthplace of the Assembly Line!! Mother of industrialization!! Collins' hair is whipped by a gust, he coughs. Heads crane from front doors. Black curious faces frame wide eyes, yellowed from crack cocaine, yellowed from despair like the yoke punctured in the frying pan. They stare in disbelief as this crazy Cracker/Honkey/Faggot/SuperstudWorldChampionLegend disrobes slowly. Catcalls soon follow. "You crazy, boy?!" "Ya'll gon' catch your death, son!" "White boy actin' a foo'!!" "Mama he takin' off his clothes!" Thomas Edward Collins removes his shoes and sets them on the hood of his car. Thomas Edward Collins removes his socks and places them, one at a time, inside his shoes. Thomas Edward Collins takes off his jacket and, folding it longways, rests it next to his shoes. Thomas Edward Collins pulls off his shirt and drapes it on his jacket. Thomas Edward Collins allows his jeans to fall. Thomas Edward Collins allows his underwear to fall over his jeans. "Holy shit, he nekkid!!" "Muthafucka! You fucked up!" "I"m-a call the gotdamn police!" "Crazy-ass cracker! I'll shoot ya ass, sick fuck!!" Unaware, maybe uncaring, maybe unpresent, he steps to the edge of the bank and looks down. The dirt and land is eroded. The warm Monday and Tuesday sun thawed the ice and it is yet to refreeze. Tree roots jut out from the land. Collins looks up at the blood moon - red and large. Ominous, maybe. It hangs inches over the Earth. Collins closes his eyes and leaps high and wide into the water. The river ripples. The waves creep out and dissipate, maybe. Or maybe they ride the crests of the ocean through the Great Lakes and the Saint Lawrence Seaway. From Detroit to the Pacific Ocean, and out. Maybe what he does now causes a tsunami in Japan. Cause and consequence. Action and reaction. "Oh shit!! He dead!!" "Mama, why that man jump in the river?" "Call the damn 911!" "Let that crazy cracker drown!" A second. Five. Ten. Twenty. Collins pops up. The crowd has gathered around his car and the River. They peer over the edge. One digs out his wallet and takes his money, credit cards. They silence as he climbs the embankment. He grasps the root and pulls up. His hands dig through the frozen clay. His feet push through. His chest drags up. His body heat thaws the earth and it dirties him. He stands, again, on the land. His breath steams out, even, paced. His palms bleed from the glass and ice. His arms and chest and legs and feet are coated in earth. In dirt. In clay. In shit. He is clean. His face is pink. His cheeks deep red. His teeth do not chatter. He does not shiver. He is clean. Cleansed. The people part. He is Moses. He is Jesus. His car keys have fallen next to his door. Still naked, still dirty, still cleansed he steps into his car and drives off, naked. He is a Faggot. He is a Superstud. He is a World Champion. He is a Legend. He is a man.
