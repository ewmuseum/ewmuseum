Hi. Here's an RP I wrote recently. I like it. People I've shown it to like it. Hope you like it too.
Critique it, praise it, criticize it for being "too graphic" and "it's unrealistic to assume they'd show that on television" or whatever. I just want to show off my work. Heh.
BACK STORY: Narcissa Vanderwal is a manager in SEWF and RAW. She likes to be "in control" of the situation, at all times. She's a sadistic, domineering, misandric lesbian. And she is very wealthy...though she technically had to find some patsy to marry to get her inheritance, but she did that. That's where Burt Clifton comes in. He's a heel now, under her influence, known as "The Winner"
Burt Clifton. And he's in the finals of the world title tournament in a couple weeks, and this week, is on the same four man team as the other finalist in another tournament, to crown a junior heavyweight champion. Now, naturally, there is of course much tension and verbal fighting between the guys. And so she's interjecting herself into it.
Enjoy. ++++++++++++++++++++++ [Fade in to a rather, ahem, shall we say graphic, visual. Narcissa
Vanderwal, Queen of Managers, Queen of Wrestling, Married Woman.]
[Explicitly groping one of Her maids.] [That's right. Laying out in front of Her Mistress on her back, displaying a trance-like motionlessness, as Narcissa runs her hands over her bare chest. Were it not for the close proximity of her white apron on the floor, it'd be impossible to tell she's a maid.]
[Narcissa is sitting on Her maid as if she were a low-to-the-ground stool, vigorously squeezing the proverbial melons as if testing their ripeness.
And, naturally, obscuring all nipplage, just to give all you pathetic
"men" out there blueballs.] [However, contrary to what one might expect, Narcissa doesn't seem to be very excited. There's a distinct aura of ennui about Her.]
Narcissa: This is hardly the stimulating diversion I had hoped it would be. [She stands up, and naturally the camera follows Her, leaving the passive nude plaything on the floor out of sight. Awwww.]
Narcissa: You're boring. [Her leg can be seen to move up and down, as if in a stomping motion, and the disctintive thud of a steel-toed unheeled boot coming down hard on human flesh is heard. Yet no yelp of protest.]
Narcissa: Now put your uniform on and leave. *Sigh* I can only hope
Toshiro is deriving more amusement from his toys than I am.

[Ha Ha Ha. Go take a nice, long, ice cold shower.] Narcissa: Yawn. [A long, silent, uncomfortable pause sets in. Something should be happening here. But isn't.]
[A true anticlimax after what was recently witnessed.] [The inaction continues.] [And continues.] [And continues some more.] [It's phenominally uninteresting.] Narcissa: Yawn. [My sentiments exactly.] Narcissa: You will not hear these words from my lips very often, world, because it just simply is rarely the case. But, being a higher form of life than all of you, I am capable of the act.
I made an error in judgment. I, Narcissa Vanderwal, made a mistake. I overestimated the mental aptitude of Simon Solkoff. [Narcissa begins to shake Her head reproachfully, making a "tsk" noise several times over. Her auburn hair, ever disciplined, stays mostly in place.]
Narcissa: You were so close Simon. So agonizingly, tantalizingly, frustratingly close. [She holds up her thumb and index finger to illustrate the short gap. Or perhaps it's an indicator of the length of Simon's phallus. Minute. Which would explain so much of his angry, hostile behaviror.] Narcissa: You don't quite grasp the reality of the situation, Simon. So close, but you're missing the keystone, and as any student of classical world archictecture knows, without a keystone, the arch falls apart.
So, for as close as you are to the truth, Simon, you are still in fact miles away. You have nothing.
Very similar to your World Title Quest experience. How thematic. Fitting for such an artificial character such as yourself. Simon Solkoff, you have lived far too long in a bubble world. There first came Twisted Chaos and your parody of a victory over David Briceland which you quickly deluded yourself into thinking was a legitimate victory, earned by your [She chuckles] ability.
From there, Michael graciously reached down into the mire of the lesser world, and ordained you for advancement, granting you the great privelige of being one of his footsoldiers under his command -- a foolish decision he would never have made had he been under my sage advice at the time.
It was charity on his part, simple charity. A gift. Again, you think you have earned it and that it was some sort of marker of your being special.
From there, Michael departed, and you fell in line behind that "Master tactician" the ever-mysterious Adam Mitchel.
[Her sarcasm is so thick it almost seems to be dripping from the wall, and leaking into and through each pixel on the screen. In other words,
Adam
Mitchell was a moron. As were all those who followed him...Hi Simon.] Narcissa: Anyone who plays second fiddle to Mitchell...oh, hello
Simon...can't be all that much. But once again, you, much like
Mitchell ahead of you, mistook your position at the top of the SEWF as an indicator of your greatness, and not a byproduct of a vaccum left by the absence of the true powerhouses of this company. Such as Michael. Such as
Briceland.
[She grins.] Narcissa: Such as myself. [Narcissa allows Herself to be consumed temporarily with the size and sheen of Her large-diamonded platinum wedding band.]
[After an unnecessarily long pause, She looks back up at the camera, mock-innocently.] Narcissa: And, latest of all, you've drawn false inferences from what
I
said about you to my husband. Poor, poor, misguided Simon. The bubble has grown ever larger since your arrival. Larger, and thicker. So thick in fact, that your vision of the world has become quite warped.
Welcome to Lecture Night, Simon. Welcome also to "Burst Your Bubble" Night. Simon, only one person has been able to excercise dominance over the SEWF when there was powerful resistance. When the SEWF was at its height, one person was the hand that rocked the cradle. One person truly ruled over all, when there was something worth ruling over. And I will now give you,
Simon Solkoff, three guesses as to who SHE was. [....] [Well, what are you waiting for? Guess. Come on, it's not too hard.] Narcissa: It was ME, Simon. The greatest of managers. The Queen of managers, and The Queen of professional wrestling. I assembled my team of three...took three pathetic individuals, plucked them out of the muck and mire of truly dreadfully bad wrestlers...and I made champions out of them.
Burt Clifton. Tommy Kain. Phah Q. Dickshine. The first two, a three time tag team championship combination, that *I* assembled, and I motivated, that
I made great. Look at Tommy Kain without me now! [She cackles, cuz, you know, Tommy Kain hasn't won a thing since leaving Her stable.] Narcissa: And the latter, Phah Q. Dickshine. A sideshow man. A gag. A character. Comic Relief that everyone knew would never ammount to anything significant. In essence, the lowest of the low. And under my command, he became World Champion. The longest reigning world champion in this company.
Longer than Lange, longer than Masters, longer than Blake, longer than Mitchell. And on paper, Phah clearly was the least of them all. So why did he ascend over the lot of them? Because, Simon, that is my power. I can make the least into the best. That was specifically why I chose Dickshine to be my World champion. To illustrate my greatness, my managerial power. Dickshine beat better men than you, Simon, because I controlled him. I did his thinking. I did his promotion. I crafted battle plans which even an imbecile could execute effectively -- as he proved -- and yet effective enough to carve out the longest run at the top of this company. I'm sure you remember that time,
Simon, seeing as you were part of the SEWF dominated by my creation.
And as for Clifton? I made him into the sniveling follower pack mule that you are all so familiar with. For that was what I wanted him to be in my system of Control over SEWF. World Champion. World Tag Team
Champions. No "stable" has ever held both concurrently, save for MINE. It's no accident Simon. And if you doubt my power? Look how I have molded Clifton to suit my needs once again! Now, I want a warrior. Now I want a Victory Machine in my hands. Now I want a World Champion again. And I shall have one again. And once again, I shall have reached into the muck and mire, plucked out a loser, and made a Winner out of him. A winner that will defeat you and all other pathetic examples of "men" in the SEWF...and then...
[She smiles a sickly grin, and then Her eyelids flutter as Her lips part slightly.] Narcissa: ...the same man who dominated all of you, will humbly kneel before me, in worship. The humilation you "macho" types will feel, will be utterly exquisite.
It is your moment in the sunshine that is over, Simon. Mine, in contrast, is just about to begin. The Queen is coming for Her throne, Simon.
