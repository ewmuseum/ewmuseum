Revolution E-Wrestling: ReWF F.A.Q.

Greetings, The following is the F.A.Q about the ReWF. It's a companion piece to my post
"Be Part Of The Gold Rush".

About The Revolution Simply put, the ReWF about you... The Handlers! A tremendous amount of input and respect is given to the handlers in the ReWF. We want you to help decide important decisions when it comes to the federation. After all, it's you who will make or break the ReWF! There's not much more I can honestly tell you.
In terms of what our main goal is? To make a fun, exciting and communicative environment for the handlers and staff.
To allow for the best action possible, the ReWF has opened up it's boundaries. Basically there are no rules in the ReWF. No countout and no DQ except at the referee's judgment. We are NOT a hardcore federation. However this allows the wrestlers to show off to the best of their ability and forces each one to use their surroundings, whatever it may be!
2. Who We Are The staff is made up several experienced e-wrestling members. I am the owner of the federation. I have had two successful stints as a fed head, combining close to two years. I've been involved in e-wrestling for close to four years now. I have also worked with several others fed in an executive manner. The vice president has even more experience then I do. He functioned in a V.P role for well over 3 years in a federation and did an excellent job. We also have a set of booker's and writers with a wealth of experience as well. Combine the entire experience of our staff and you have around 15 years in e-wrestling! We know what we are doing, we know how to do it and most of all, we WANT to do it for you!
3. What Kind Of Fed The ReWF is a role-playing fed. It's open book style which means we use a message board to roleplay so everybody can see the roleplay's has they happen. To exceed in the ReWF, you simply need to role-play and be involved.
Wins and losses are not the be all and end all here in the ReWF (which is why we don't keep official rankings, we do have unofficial ones though).
Even if you lose a match, it seldom will take away from your overall push as long as you worked hard for it.
Role-playing on the message board is not the only form of doing so. We also encourage "actions". Actions are anything you'd do on one of the ReWF shows.
Attacks, interference's, interviews and the like are all "actions". This is a form of role-playing as well. It's ipearitive to be involved in the shows as it will help you evolve your characters. There will be no Goldberg's here with "dead pushes". You need to work for everything you earn in the ReWF.
4. Requirements The main one is that you not take e-wrestling too seriously. It is a game. With games you, and those around you, are supposed to have fun. If the staff sees that you break the barrier between this being a game and taking it too "real life like", you will be terminiated on the spot. We require that you post at least one roleplay per roleplaying week. A roleplaying week is the defined as from the time your match is booked until the roleplay deadline for a given show. The final requirement is to have fun. If you are joining the ReWF, I trust you already have this in mind!
5. The Action What's an efed without its shows? The ReWF will have one weekly show. That is our main show which is broadcast throughout North America in syndicated markets. There will also be a house show each week. We will also hold
"Supercard's" from time to time. Finally, we will have Pay Per View's. Read on below to find out more about our shows.
RVT Warriors: This is our main weekly show broadcasted every Monday. The show is written in an unique format that jams alot of action and excitement but won't bore you to death. We are doing this because we want to try and get the entire fed involved in each show and so that each handler will know what's going on throughout the fed, not just in their match. The show will be a mixture of commentary and recap format. Basically the matches themselves will be in an in-depth form of recap. Before and after each match will be commentary by announcers as well as during any angle that is sent in. This will add to the shows and make sure to give every angle and match the play it deserves! It also makes sense since the shows are run in syndication and not shot live.
House Shows: The ReWF will also run a full slate of house shows. There will be at least one, possibly two, per week. To get on these shows, you need to roleplay well during any given week, consider it a bonus. The wrestler's will be facing either local wrestlers or as we like to call them, the
"talently challenged" wrestlers. hehe. You will get counted for a win on a house show (even though you aren't roleplaying specifically for any person on the show). Sometimes there will be a main event on the show involving a true, blue ReWF match. This will likely only be used to further an angle for a big match involving the participants or something of that nature. If you give no reason for roleplaying for any lengthy period of time, you may find yourself losing at a house show.
Pay Per View: The ReWF will also feature Pay-Per-View. Unlike the other feds where you plop down 30 bucks every 3-4 weeks, the ReWF PPV's will have meaning. They will come to you every 6-8 weeks to make sure each and every match has the build up and attention it deserves. PPV's in the ReWF are special events that's why PPV's will be done in full play by play commenary!
Cards: We will also run "SuperCards" every now and again. These will be in a different format then the rest. They will be shot live with commentary but aired as episodes of Warriors. Usually Supercards will be used for something special such as a tournament, a competition and things of that nature. The format is "joined in progress" commentary.
6. The Gold There will be 3 championship titles in the ReWF. We don't want to overkill the gold. This allows us to make sure each title means alot. Below are the titles.
Revolution Championship: This is the main title. Get this and it means you are top of the ReWF. The title will be decided in the "Gold Rush"
competition. Old School Championship: Whereas in other federations matches where there are no rules are something different, here they are the norm. Which means matches with tight rules, are the "something different". In all old school matches, there is countout and there is DQ. If you throw your opponent over the top rope, you are DQ'ed. The Old School title was decided in a Bunkhouse
Stampede match.
Revolution Tag Team Championships: Like the Revolution Championship, the team that gets these are on top of their game!
7. Application You are allowed two units in the ReWF. This means you can have two singles wrestlers, two tag teams, one singles wrestler and one tag team or two singles wrestlers that function as a tag team.
