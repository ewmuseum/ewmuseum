Revolution E-Wrestling: Week In Review #1

30 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings, It was a historic week in Revolution E-Wrestling as the ReWF presented its very first week of events!
It all started out in an action packed show at the Revolution Warehouse in
Boston. It was the first T.V Taping of "Warriors". Over 2, 500 people were on hand at the ReWF's homebase to see the debut of "Sweetness" Stevey Love's new promotion.
The ReWF made their intentions known from the get go. They want the wrestlers to use all their surroundings, take themselves to the limit all in the name of entertaining the fans. The show was a perfect example of that.
Hungry rookies, wily veterans, some looking to make up for past wrong doings, others looking to cement their name in the wrestling world. The ReWF roster came out and gave it all they had.
Broadcasters Sam Saunders and Denver Maddux called the action from the RTV studios. While everybody had the simple goal in mind of showing off what they could do, a couple of things stood out. The apparent developing war between The Foreign Policy team of Tommy Kaz and Paulo Sanchez and the duo of Scott Oakley and Shadowflare. As Sanchez faced Oakley, Kaz would interfere against his old nemesis. This brought out Oakley's good friend,
Shadowflare. The four men would come to blows again later in the night as
Shadowflare took on Kaz. With "Regulator" Izzy G sitting at ringside, Izzy got into a confrontation with Shad's manager. After attacking him, Izzy fled through the crowd with Shad hot on his heels. For some reason Kaz felt it acceptable to brutally attack the referee, this brought out Oakley to which
Sanchez ran out. After the Foreign Policy basted Oakley with their finisher.
The beating continued until Shadowflare stormed back to the ring. When all was said and done, all four men where laid out looking to the stars. Things look to intensify this week on Warriors as the two teams meet but there's a catch. Izzy G will be the special referee! Speaking of Mr. G. It's long be known to those who followed his career that he has had a long standing feud with Shadowflare. But Izzy made waves by attacking rookie Troy Gentry right before his match. When asked why he did it, he simply said "because he can".
Izzy was also present during both the Oakley/Sanchez match and the Kaz/Shadowflare match. He just observed until Shadowflare's manager shoved him, he then attacked "Bruce" and laid out the young kid. One must wonder exactly what he is up to and what is thinking?
From there the ReWF hit the road for the first time as they headed to Reading, Mass. to present their first house show. The show was an entertaining introduction to the ReWF for those in attendance. Many of those involved in Warriors were present but the main features were the debuts of several new ReWF faces and the first ever Old School match. Solitaire, Chris
Bevel, Alexander Dreamwalker and Zane Alexander all made their impressive debuts on the show. In the main event, Izzy G dispatched of "Cool Daddy
Dave" in the first ever Old School match. In addition to all that, the
Foreign Policy struck on both Oakley and Shadowflare. First they took out
Oakley after his match. Once Oakley was laid out, they had Shadowflare to themselves. He couldn't do much and was eventually nailed with their finisher. Things really heated up for the big tag team main event on next week's Warriors.
All in all, it was a fun and exciting week for the ReWF. The fans, the wrestlers and everybody involved had a blast as this young promotion took off. The action only looks to get better as time evolves. You still have time to be part of the action and enjoy one hell of a ride! Come on over and join the Revolution before you are left on the outside looking in!
ReWF Owner, "Sweetness" Stevey Love www.efeduniverse.com/rewf
