Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

SWF's earliest surviving broadcast, billed as the “very first edition” of Saturday Night Slam, was posted 13 January 1996 from the General Motors Place in Vancouver. Its hosts, Rod Pettingzoo and “Nacho Man” Sandy Ravage, opened the show with a ten-team tag battle royal to crown the promotion's first Tag Team Champions, plugging a call-in hotline for results and gossip along the way.

The battle royal came down to two teams, the Headhunters and a team called Snowstorm; the Headhunters' own manager shoved a nearly-eliminated partner back over the ropes to save the fall, and the Headhunters won the titles, then beat their fallen opponents down with a chair after the bell rang.

A further surviving broadcast, Wednesday Night War, posted 31 January 1996, put the SWF Television Championship on the line. The champion, the Canadian Crippler, retained it in a brawl against Pike “The Excellence” Walker that spilled through the crowd and onto the ring's own steel railing, finishing Walker with a sharpshooter after both men had already kicked out of a piledriver, a small package, and a second-rope belly-to-back suplex.

No document survives after that night.
