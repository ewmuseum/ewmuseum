Trading punches has never been Tsonda’s foray, but he attempts to keep pace with Gamble – however briefly – in an exchange of blows. Gamble quickly and easily takes the upper hand, using his knee to double Tsonda up for a sloppy, but effective, Powerbomb. He covers: One! Two! Thr-! So close, but not quite close enough! After slapping the mat in frustration Gamble complains to the referee about the count but, getting nowhere, he returns to Tsonda – stomping him a few times before mounting the turnbuckle. He sits waiting patiently for Tsonda to slowly rise, and primes himself ready to jump as Tsonda reaches a squatting position. It’s not a good day for the ‘little jesters’ though, as the Viet Viper lunges for the ropes from his low-level position, shaking Gamble down to fall rather, umm… ‘uncomfortably’. After a second to congratulate himself, Tsonda returns to the job at hand and takes a brief run-up to bound up and wrap his legs round Gamble’s head, falling back to take The Jester to the mat with a hurricarana, adapting his landing position to a kneeling pin. One! Two! Thr-! Gamble just gets a shoulder off the mat and no more! Tsonda slams Gamble’s head off the mat after the unsuccessful pin, adding a stomp to the mix once he stands – just for good measure. With a deep breath he mounts the turnbuckle he’d just rana’d from, checks behind and launches off with a superlative Model Citizen moonsault. But, just like the SSP, it’s all style and no substantial result as Tsonda crashes chest-first into Gamble’s raised knees. All the air in him is squeezed out on impact, leaving him to flop to the mat beside Gamble in a heap. All it takes is a draped arm, and that’s just as well because it’s all Gamble can manage to make the pin. One! Two! Three! WINNER: TONY ‘THE GRIN’ GAMBLE Exhaustedly Gamble stands and receives his retained belt, holding it aloft with a grin stretching right across his face. He rolls out the ring strolls past the growling Ellie, slinging the belt over his shoulder on his way up the ramp, leaving Tsonda to slowly come to and curse the result.
