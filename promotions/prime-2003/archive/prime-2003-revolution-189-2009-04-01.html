So he leans against the turnbuckle, huffing and puffing, and waiting for Tsonda to rise so they can keep the marathon match going. Outside, Hessian unleashes a forceful headbutt right into Rhine’s face, then shoves the stumbling New Life onto the announce table. He climbs up onto the table himself, although how it can support the almost six hundred pounds on it is baffling. Nick: That table could give way at any second! Richard: (mutters) Two weeks in a fuckin’ row… Nick: Last week, High Flyer decimated the table, and this time Hessian wants to break Jon Rhine in half by doing the same thing! As the Murder Show slowly pulls Rhine to his feet, there’s a disturbance in the crowd. Hitting a double axe handle to knock Rhine back off his feet, Hessian watches as the crowd parts, an obvious indication that someone is running towards the ring. Nick: I’m all too afraid I know who this is going to be. Richard: Oh, you didn’t know? Garbage Bag Johnny, too, watches as the disturbance comes closer, until the familiar locks of blond hair can be seen moving quickly towards the ring. Only one towhead that would make sense storming the ring for this match. Richard: Original Villain in the house, y’all! BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! That’s the reception that Las Vegas gives Jason Snow as they see him, Universal Title strapped around his waist, hop over the barricade and enter the ring area. Several fans in the first row offer up middle fingers and shout insults, but Snow’s eyes are only on the ring. Nick: Who’s Snow looking to take out? Tsonda? Johnny? Both? Richard: Any and everybody. Oh wait, he already did that by gunning down the entire roster. Confident that Snow’s not coming for him, Hessian turns back to the task at hand: ending Jon Rhine. He pulls the New Life up by the hair, taunting him with a vicious smile. The fans hate to see Rhine about to be done like this, and they chant with the intention of bringing him back from the dead. NEW LIFE! NEW LIFE! NEW LIFE! Richard: Your stupid heroes are dead, people. Rhine will probably be in a wheelchair after this, but he’ll have company, since Tsonda will be using a walker. Cut back to the ring, where Chandler Tsonda staggers to his feet in the middle of the squared circle. But he doesn’t see Jason Snow slide in behind him, and Garbage Bag Johnny certainly isn’t going to let him know that the Universal Champion is waiting behind him. Tsonda stares at Johnny, perhaps wondering why GBJ is yet to attack him. Snow winds up, taking two shuffle steps towards Tsonda… Richard: Snake Eyes! …but Tsonda’s not interested in being on the receiving end of that move ever again. Whether it was GBJ’s hesitation or some wrestling sixth sense, Tsonda simultaneously spins and ducks. Snow’s momentum carries him past Tsonda, and the crowd erupts as the lethal superkick misses its mark. WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Nick: Snow just missed with what surely would’ve been a killing stroke! And now your UltraViolence main event is in the ring together with no referee, and thus, no rules. Richard: Just how it’s gonna be at UltraViolence, I’d imagine. The only rule is win however you can. Johnny looks at Tsonda, who’s looking at Snow, whose gaze moves quickly from one challenger to the other. One or two or every motherfucker in the house has their flashbulb popping off. Nick: I can barely see with Hessian and Rhine in front of us! Ready to deliver a killing stroke of his own, Hessian finally lifts Jon Rhine up onto his shoulder. Seeing men go up onto the brawny shoulders of the beast is a preamble to his absolutely devastating finishing maneuver, and everyone in the place knows it. NEW LIFE! NEW LIFE! NEW LIFE! In the ring, no one makes a move. It’s almost perfectly still, except for the rapidly heaving chests of Garbage Bag Johnny and Chandler Tsonda. TSON-DA! TSON-DA! TSON-DA! Hessian lifts Jon Rhine, who seems nearly unconscious, as high as his tree-trunk arms will allow. He’s showing Las Vegas the destruction of Jon Rhine. NEW LIFE! NEW LIFE! NEW LIFE! Without warning, Tsonda charges Snow, spearing him to the mat and mounting the Universal Champion with punch after punch. TSON-DA! TSON-DA! TSON-DA! Hessian roars with delight as he holds Jon Rhine for all to see. A move for the truly desperate, Jon Rhine swings his leg out. You could call it a kick, but it’s really just a spasm. It’s all he’s got the strength for. NEW LIFE! NEW LIFE! NEW LIFE! As punch after punch lands on Snow, and the Universal Title lying unceremoniously in the middle of the ring, Tsonda takes a second’s time to stop punching and turn to Garbage Bag Johnny. His "stay the fuck out of my way" is loud enough that the first three rows hear it. GBJ watches for a moment, as Tsonda continues to mercilessly pummel Snow. TSON-DA! TSON-DA! TSON-DA! Call it a miracle. Call it an act of God or gods. Call it what you want, but somehow, Jon Rhine’s kick finds it mark on Hessian’s shin, and with a roar of pain, the beast drops Jon Rhine. ROAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAR! Nick: Rhine’s free! Rhine’s alive! It’s not two seconds that goes by before Jon Rhine wraps his right around Hessian’s chest, lifts the big man as quickly as he can (with no focus on getting airtime), and sits out, spiking him backwards through the announce table. Nick: RHINE REWIND! RHINE REWIND THROUGH THE DAMN TABLE! Richard: But…but…he’s…huge. Nick: Jon Rhine’s heart is bigger, and tonight, that’s what matters! Meanwhile, it looks like Chandler Tsonda might kill Snow with his bare hands! PRIME THAT SHIT! PRIME THAT SHIT! PRIME THAT SHIT! Stepping off of Snow for a moment, Tsonda grabs the Universal Title, shaking it in the face of Snow as he re-mounts the Original Villain and continues laying fists of fury into the prize-winning visage of the champion. Richard: How the HELL did Rhine lift Hessian for that move? Nick: There wasn’t much lift, but he used gravity to great effect, and as a result, our table is literally shattered. As the two announcers investigate the blast zone that used to be their announce table, fans count off Tsonda’s punches. ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! Richard: Yes! BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! A running knee strike from Garbage Bag Johnny to the side of Tsonda’s head knocks him off the Universal Champion and down to the mat. Nick: Garbage Bag Johnny didn’t like Tsonda treating him like a third wheel, and now the Model Citizen’s paying for it! Richard: I might like this vagrant, after all. Snow rolls to the other side of the ring, to catch his breath momentarily, as Garbage Bag Johnny lays into Tsonda with a set of vicious stomps. Richard: Kid chose the right side. It’s always better gettin’ cheddar with Jay Ess. Nick: I don’t think he chose a side except for his own. The Dirtiest Dude in PRIME pulls the Viet Viper up by the well-coiffed hair and whips him at the opposite rope. Catching Tsonda as he bounces off, GBJ nails a hip toss, as Tsonda’s crashes into the mat, landing painfully on his back. Nick: GBJ’s got a mean streak when you cross him, it seems. There’s not a moment for Tsonda to rest, as GBJ pulls him back to his feet. Neither man sees the stirring Snow in the background. The Universal Champion collects his prize, rubbing his jaw. He looks down at the title and then up at his two rivals, one old, one new. Richard: Someone’s about to pay right the hell now. Nick: This match has to end soon. Hessian and Jon Rhine are out here and can’t get medical attention as they’re technically still competing. As GBJ throws a right hand at Tsonda, the Original Villain charges, sneer a mile wide at the thought of these two daring to challenge him for his belt. Snow goes flying through the air, clobbering Garbage Bag Johnny in the back of the head with the Universal Title. ASS-HOLE! ASS-HOLE! ASS-HOLE! Nick: That bastard! Garbage Bag Johnny did nothing to him! Richard: He was in the way. That’s a sin punishable by getting your face knocked in with a title belt. Snow swings again with the title towards Tsonda, but the Model Citizen explodes out of the corner and hits Snow with a dropkick that sends the champion crashing to the concrete outside. WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Nick: Tsonda dodged that shot and tossed Snow from the ring! He’s on his ass where he belongs, after his behavior tonight! The Model Citizen, using the ropes to prop himself up, looks down at Garbage Bag Johnny. He heads to the corner, slowly climbing the turnbuckle, as Bernie Roberts staggers to his feet on the outside. Richard: I’m sure this will be good for his back. Steadying himself on his perch, Tsonda bends down and turns his back to GBJ, preparing for flight. Nick: The move that won him the Universal Title, coming right up! He explodes to full height and then flips backwards off the corner. This incarnation of Tsonda’s signature moonsault is less concerned with distance, since GBJ is already in the corner. No, this one’s about hangtime, about coming down as hard as possible, coinciding nicely with Bernie Roberts’s slow roll back into the ring. Nick: Model Citizen! As Tsonda connects with the moonsault, he yells loudly in pain. How do you think he fucked his back up in the first place? Grimacing in pain, he makes the cover, hooking the leg, and praying that GBJ will stay down. One… Two… Three! Richard: Shoulder up! Despite everything, Garbage Bag Johnny’s shoulder comes off the mat. It’s slight, almost unnoticeable, but it’s off. Unfortunately, it’s what we’d call too little, too late. "Welcome Home" hits the PA system, and EMT’s rush out instantaneously, most heading to the carnage of the Rhine/Hessian table-shattering (although one gets at your boy Bernie Roberts, he’s a trooper). Richard: Say it. Nick; Say the part how Rhine and Tsonda won, overcame completely unnecessary interference by Jason Snow, and put on a hell of a show? Richard: Yeah, whatever. His music is playing, but Tsonda doesn’t look like much of a victor, as he cringes in pain. On the outside, Jason Snow picks up the Universal Title, glaring at Tsonda. Nick: Folks, we’re running over and absolutely out of time. For Richard Parker, I’m Nick Stuart. Don’t miss us next week at the same time! Tsonda, huffing and puffing, looks down at Garbage Bag Johnny. His gaze then shifts to Snow and the Universal Title. He extends a slender finger towards Snow, in the specific direction of the Original Villain’s golden totem, as the PRIME logo pops up. The implication is clear: that belt is mine. Tsonda hates Snow. Snow hates Tsonda. But where does Garbage Bag Johnny fit in? Does tonight damn him to be a sworn enemy of both Tsonda and Snow? Despite that colossal unknown, one thing’s for sure. When it comes to the PRIME Universal Title… …three’s a crowd.
