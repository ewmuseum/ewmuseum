Paul Cain Biography Nicknames The Anarchist Hometown Denver, CO Birthday 28th July 1975 Height 6'3 Weight 235 Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography Paul Cain was a fluke. Before hitting back up with Power in the LSW, he was found by Johnny Holiday, wrestling in the wild and crazy world of Indy feds. He was a hit there, but didn’t have the marketing sense to get himself a contract with any of the big boys. Whilst bored with retirement, Holiday visited one of the bingo halls that was hosting Cain and his colleagues. Johnny loved the wild and almost dangerous style of the man he now calls "The Lethal Cocktail". Not only that but Cain’s subtle arrogance on the mic, yet sharp wit and coolness reminded Holiday of himself. Impressed, Johnny went backstage and got the details of the kid and promised him a start in the big-time. However, Holiday got distracted with his work alongside Theresa Angel and the now retired Power in Lone Star Wrestling. Cain patiently waited for his time, wowing the female crowd with his good looks and impressing the male crowd with his skills. With Power quitting to be with his girlfriend, Johnny made a few calls and got hooked up with CCW and where he would bring Cain. Cain had a majorly influential time in the CCW, becoming their top draw and winning the Mid-Atlantic Title and the Heavyweight Title. A stormy relationship with girlfriend and valet Maria Lopez, ended in a bitter split, with Maria leaving to be with Cain’s arch nemesis Rictor Arkane. Maria cited violence and abuse as the reason for the split and that was confirmed as partial truth recently. Eventually Cain departed from CCW after he helped to destroy the Arab movement "Jihad" led by Mohammed Hazeem, of which he was formerly a member. His departure was enforced shortly after becoming partial NWCal World Tag Champions with his partner SØren, when he was savagely attacked by a wrestler named Brandon Pride. A shattered left knee led Cain to surgery and a lengthy period absent. Paul returned to wrestling to the NWC South, however he was put back on the injured list by Raveller on his debut with the then National Wrestling Council. After further rehabilitation, Cain came back to NWC South a few months later to run his very own show, "The Cocktail Bar". This proved to be a real hit with the fans and led to some stormy confrontations between Cain and his old enemy Rictor Arkane, which included the revelation that Arkane had raped and assaulted Maria Lopez whilst in NWC South. This continued their intense rivalry which led to Paul Cain’s eventual wrestling return, which took place in the NWC Atlantic. Initially Paul Cain took on the role of manager to budding star Damien Kahn, who arrived in the NWC Atlantic with Paul. Kahn took the New England Title home but then was defeated by Halloween which led to a concentrated to destroy Arkane once and for all. However Cain soon became obsessed with Leonard Aarons and his apparent chokehold on Atlantic. Dissatisfied that Kahn was not doing a fair enough job with Aarons and Arkane, Paul gained medical clearance to wrestle once more, backed and supported by Maria Lopez’s sister Claudia. Cain waged heavy wars with Aarons, Heatwave and finally Arkane. After winning the NWC Atlantic Battle Royal to determine the Number One contender to Alec Spade’s World Title, Paul took the biggest match of his life, only to be defeated by Spade. This did not go un-noticed by the Atlantic and Cain was rewarded with a Triple Threat match against Arkane and Aarons for the NWC Altantic Heavyweight Title which was then won by Arkane. This set up a bloody and violent conclusion to their feud which saw Cain win the Heavyweight Title at Return To Purgatory 2004. Paul would no doubt claim this helped along Rictor Arkane’s departure from wrestling, although Leonard Aarons would argue differently. Paul would then suffer personal humiliation after Maria Lopez turned on him for not ‘killing’ Arkane as he requested. At the same time, the NWC closed forever and saw the arrival of AXIOM led by Jack Robinson. This coupled with Cain’s personal problems out of the ring saw a different set of performances from Paul. Incensed at the way AXIOM was being run and furious about the death of the NWC, Cain quit the company after losing the AXIOM World Title to Heatwave. Cain then arrived in PRIME and quickly set about making a stir. His main objective was to target the Dark Age and he set about attempting to rally the other PRIME workers against Vampir and his followers. It was however Vampir who got the last laugh, sacrificing Paul Cain at ReV 48 by carving the sign of the Pentagram on the 'Lethal Cocktail' chest. This saw the end of Cain's short career with PRIME before he returned in 2005 and went on to accompany Tony Gamble as his partner and manager. They continued their business association until Cain went on hiatus to deal with family matters outside of the squard circle. Upon Cain's return in October 2006, he has seen himself become more and more excluded from his locker room colleagues due to his bizarre behaviour. His extreme and violent feud with former partner still has no rhyme or reason to it which is what has put PRIME on full alert as Cain's lack of control becomes apparent. Having recently had a brush with the law as well, its clear that PRIME will have to keep a close eye on this suddenly ticking timebomb. Other Feds Seventh Circle Wrestling, AXIOM, NWC Atlantic, NWC South, CCW, NWCal, HWF, BWA. Indian Deathlock Leg Lock Entrance Music You Don't Know - Eminem featuring 50 Cent Ring Entrances The lights in the arena fade down to black as the introduction for "You Don't Know" begins. The initial instrumental kicks in and red strobe lights begin to flash rapidly. The flashing then ceases and pyro's explode either side of the entrance way as the beat kicks into life. Paul Cain walks slowly from the back and stands with his shaved head bowed at the start of the ramp. As Eminem and 50 lead the lyrics, Cain's head snaps up to face the ring as he begins to stride down the ramp. He's wearing a leather jacket on top of a T-shirt coupled with black pants and a scowl to match. As he makes his way to the squared circle, he blatantly ignores all of those around him as the hip hop anthem blares out with the bass extra high. He then sprints down the remainder of the ramp and dives under the ropes into the ring. He aims one of his recently developed glares in the direction of Vince Howard, as he removes his jacket and throws it down to the mat while power walking towards the nearest ring corner. He scales the top rope and removes his T-shirt, tossing it to the floor and then standing, glaring around the crowd with his arms by his side and fists clenched. Having soaked up the atmosphere around him, he drops down off the ropes and then turns around to lean against the turnbuckles casually with his arms draped on the ropes ready to spring at his opponent.
