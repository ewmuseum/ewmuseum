Simon Knox Biography Wrestler Name Simon Knox Nicknames The Providence Prince, the Golden Son Hometown Providence, Rhode Island Birthday October 5th, 1988 Height 6'1 Weight 235 lbs Fed Titles PRIME World Tag Team Championships (1x) Fed Awards None. Biography Simon is a jerk, just like Connor. Unlike Connor, Simon is responsible and not nearly as reckless. Simon has a cooler head than Connor, and he has a sort of a cool, detached personality. He is capable of easily no-selling or ignoring any threat or insult against him, even when he's being physically accosted (similar to Tony Gamble continuing to taunt Wade Elliot even despite Wade having him in a chokehold). This is actually part of his abilities as an actor, he can work himself into a sort of a "blank slate" and react calmly to events even when he might be in turmoil on both the inside and the outside. He can suddenly act different at a moment's notice, though it's often hard, if not impossible, to know if he's acting or he's really feeling that way. As a result of all of this, Simon is very hard to really rattle, and it's also hard to see what he's thinking. He'd be pretty good at poker, actually. Simon MAY get frustrated during matches and yell at the referee, but he never really loses his cool like the emotional and hot-tempered Connor would. There are two things, however, that Simon has a hard time tolerating. The first is a strike to the face. Simon, because he's a (aspiring) movie star with good looks, values his looks. A blow to the face will ensure that Simon will bail, and if enough facial strikes occur, he may try to walk out on the match. Despite this, Simon himself is willing to bleed for his craft, although he will certainly whine about it AFTER the match is over. The second is Miranda getting hurt. Like Connor, Simon really does care about her (and, in fact, has a one-sided crush on her). This is the best way to really pierce Simon's emotions and figure out what he's really thinking, although don't be surprised if he obliterates you with the Providence Knee Kick and maybe a piledriver or three directly afterwards. Other Moves WRESTLING STYLE Simon Knox is a "catch as catch can" wrestler, and his style is primarilly a pastiche of Shawn Michaels and the Rock. He wrestles a more deliberate pace, but can step it up and deliver some top-notch high spots when the mood is right. He has a great deal of substance in his style, he often focuses on a weakened part of his opponent's body or creates a weakness. He also expends his energy in a more conservative and deliberate way. He primarlly does this by keeping an opponent grounded and then keeping them there, but once that plan begins to falter, Simon begins to use medium-risk maneuvers in order to put them back on the mat, culminating possibly in the Providence Knee Kick. One of Simon's greatest attributes, which is both a strength and a weakness, is trying to demoralize an opponent mentally. He does this often by mocking his opponent on the ground, often while they're in a submission hold of some sort. It's a strength because, should the opponent be weak-minded enough, Simon can take full advantage of their emotional states and put them away. And it's a weakness because if Simon loses control of the match, the angry opponent can do that much more damage to him. Simon's most important attribute, however, is the fact that he is near-impossible to really rattle. The same tactics that Knox employs to demoralize his opponents will not normally work on him. He is unphased by most words or comments, and if they do have an effect on him, he hardly ever shows it because he tends to have the same cold, dismissive face on his face. In fact, the only real time his expression changes is if he begins to mock his opponent. Or he's in pain. Either/or. The only real way to rattle Simon is to either continuously kick out from his offense, which is including his Providence Knee Kick, or by the more extreme method of hurting Miranda O'Reily. Even when you keep coming back against Simon, it's hard to truly frustrate or rattle him unless he really doesn't like you. However, Miranda is a different story, as Simon's in love with her. Hurting her will really rattle him, though not in the same way it would rattle Connor. Whereas Connor would react by assaulting everyone responsible without mercy, Simon would be very stunned. From there, it's up in the air about how Simon will react. He may be more concerned about Miranda's health, but if it occurs during a match, Simon may decimate you with a Providence Knee Kick and then possibly follow up with a series of piledrivers. STRENGTHS - As mentioned above, Simon is incredibly hard (if not impossible under normal circumstances) to rattle. He is not overly cerebral, but he will stop to taunt and demoralize an opponent using his brand of sarcasm and off-color humor. He does this primarilly because he's an ass, and not because he particularly cares too much (under normal circumstances) how his opponent feels. - Simon is stronger than my average character, he is able to lift opponents up to about 280 lbs. onto his shoulders (fireman's carry position). I'm not saying he's the strongest guy in the roster, I'm saying that he's surprisingly strong for his size. However, it can be said that a lot of Simon's offense are the kind that don't require too much picking up. - For all of Simon's power, he has no loss of speed. While Simon is not as quick as Connor, he is fast and agile enough to properly execute a Busaiku Knee Kick and an asai moonsault. - Simon's ring presence is downright uncanny, and unless he's completely unconscious, he's going to find some sneaky way to get out of a sticky situation. - If your submission hold takes too long to apply, Simon's gonna counter it unless you deal him a heavy blow first. WEAKNESS - Simon is not as tough as a Wade Elliott or a Captain Justice/Soren Knox. He's not really cut out for Intense title matches, partially because he's got movie star looks and would like to not spoil that, and mostly because he's not that kind of brawler. You know how Shawn Michaels was in the first Hell in a Cell against Undertaker, right? Well, that's Simon Knox. - Simon may be pretty strong, but under no circumstances would he be able to defeat guys like Captain Justice, Wade Elliott, Tom Walczak, Logic, or Hoyt Williams with power alone. If he tries to match power for power against them, he's not going to win. - Simon is beset by gross cowardice. If the opponent tries to make a comeback, he's going to find any cheap, cowardly tactic to turn it around in his favor. If things get too hectic for him, he could bail from the match altogether if it doesn't matter to him (ie: when he's not challenging for a title). - Simon will tap out if he's beset by a submission hold while he's nowhere near the ropes, unless he's saved. - Oh yes, and by the way, Simon works "light". This isn't a weakness per se, but since some might consider it one, I'll explain: None of this MMA crap that 75% of PRIME uses now will apply to him (I'm looking at YOU: Shakur, Troy, Cozen, Silver, Rayne, Crucifix, etc.!). His brawling is of the standard variety and the only types of chokes you'll ever see him do are the illegal variety. He's a safe worker, he's not there to kick the crap out of people, he's there to be professional. Remember, you don't need to actually stiff your opponent for your offense to look good! PRIMARY FINISHER - P.K.K. -- Providence Knee Kick The Providence Knee Kick is KENTA's "Busaiku Knee Kick". The name, quite simply, refers to Simon's hometown of Providence. Considered to be the stronger of the individual finishers of the Princes, the P.K.K. is executed similarly to a front dropkick. However, rather than striking the opponent with the bottoms of his feet, he bends his right leg and impacts the opponent in the face/head region with his knee. Simon pretty much puts all of his force into it, which means that he often ends up stiffing an opponent when hitting them with the Knee Kick. He occasionally smacks his leg in midflight so as to create a loud smacking sound upon impact. The move usually looks very good and has a much better potential of ending a match than Connor's Condom Breaker. The dangerous part about the Knee Kick is that, essentially speaking, Simon can hit it at any point of the match and basically from anywhere. Apart from the standard running version, Simon can do it standing (without a running start, in other words), springboarding, off the top rope, off the apron, and can even hit a double P.K.K. by bending the other leg while attacking two opponents standing side by side. SECONDARY FINISHERS - Top rope elbow drop This is basically your classic elbow drop. It's very showy like HBK's, but unlike HBK, he doesn't go for it as a setup to the finisher, he goes for it in the sense that he'll want to win the match with it. It's only if the opponent kicks out that Simon will set up for the Providence Knee Kick. Simon usually sets this move up with either a scoop slam or a vertical suplex. The opponent usually stays down long enough for Simon to hit it, but if done too early, the opponent can catch him up top or move out of the way. - Jumping piledriver This is your classic piledriver, except that it's executed with a quick jump once the opponent is picked up, increasing the impact. Simon usually uses it on opponents of lesser or equal size to himself. Simon's piledriver is impressive, and can get wins at times when the Providence Knee Kick might not be nearly as effective. It can easily get a three count, especially if it's the spiked version that the Princes use. It's not like the Box Office Bomb, though, so it can still be kicked out of if Simon hadn't put you through a table or a steel chair or both in the course of driving you into the canvas. SIGNATURE MOVES - Golden Touch Mostly a "for show" move, like the People's Elbow or the Five Knuckle Shuffle. Simon slams the opponent down in the center of the ring, then he backs into the ropes, comes back, and makes like he's going to drop an elbow, but fakes it at the last second and simply kicks the opponent in the side of their head instead. <!-- RARE MOVES - Gold Standard This is Steve Knox's main finisher. BIG FINISHES - Double Standard The Double Standard is the second part of the Gold Standard chain, it is a crossface chickenwing suplex. Simon uses it to annihilate those pesky "I'm not submitting" jerks, and occasionally those jerks who refuse to give up even when they probably should. The Double Standard is basically a superfinish without it being one. It will usually get the three count, but after it's established as a finish, it can be kicked out of in major matches. It is sort of on the level of Kurt Angle's grapevined ankle lock... once it's locked in, it's over, and yet, Kurt only really does it for bigger matches. Unless you're the frickin' Undertaker or something. The Double Standard is, however, a touch out of character for Simon, so it's used only if the opponent won't tap to the Gold Standard. - The Necklacing This is a Coquina Clutch, and it was his father's finishing maneuver. For those unfamiliar, Simon wraps an arm around the opponent's neck and chokes the shit out of them (similar to a sleeperhold), all the while, he wraps his legs around the opponent's waist. The opponent will be down, usually in the center of the ring, while Simon wrenches the chokehold until the opponent either taps out or succumbs to the natural human need to breathe. This is the finisher that, to Simon, has the most history. If he applies it, he'd want it to mean something. As such, Simon's actually trained it to be the most unbreakable of his two rare submission finishers, to the point that if Simon decides to lock it in... that's it. You're either tapping out or passing out. Generally, Simon will use it in in one of two situations: 1) that extremely rare event where Simon uses the Double Standard... and the other guy kicks out or 2) Miranda was injured by his opponent in the past (maybe even *during* the match in question) and he wants to make you suffer. In the latter case, the hold will come only after Simon has obliterated you with the Providence Knee Kick and possibly a series of piledrivers. --> COMMON MOVES & MOVESET RULES - Flying forearm - Inverted atomic drop - Superplex - Asai moonsault - Swinging neckbreaker - Enzuigiri - Teardrop Suplex There are only two major notes to be aware of with Simon and his moves: 1) Simon's offense is rarely too complicated. Any move that goes from one and then transitions into another without being fairly fluid will probably not find its way into his offense. For example, a vertical suplex turned into a backbreaker is not a move that Simon will use any time soon. Much of Simon's offense is derived from a combination of what HBK could do and what the Rock could do, although I'd prefer that I never see Simon use a spinebuster. 2) Simon usually starts his singles matches trying to wear down an opponent. He is not apt for taking risks early in the contest unless he already wants to homicidally murder his opponent, and he usually saves any risky move for the middle and later portions of the match when an opponent is sufficently worn down. In terms of risky moves, he never does anything more complicated than that Asai moonsault of his.
