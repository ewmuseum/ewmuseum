Special Moves CHECK MWA Other Moves WRESTLING STYLE Garcia is a showman(and one showboating son of a gun); though he is indeed heel, he aspires to please the crowd. Night after night, he goes out there trying to out-perform whomever he's up against. He is a true student of the game, and is very skilled in the ring. Does a gorilla chest beat taunt, to the dismay of the crowd, multiple times throughout the duration of the match. Thrives off of the crowds boos and jeers. Dirty, and may quite possibly cheat every match. Most often when he is at a huge disadvantage. Very BRASH, SNEAKY and ARROGANT in and out of the ring. Known to throw a tantrum when things don't go his way in a match. Talks smack the duration of the match, whether he's boasting about how good he is or he's screaming in the opponents ear telling them to tap out. LOVES to FLY, hence his "MILE HIGH" nickname. Mixed with his lucha libre roots of wrestling, Garcia is a submission specialist and works on the opponents legs, knees, thighs and ankles before going for the quick kill, THE CHARLEY HORSE. Prefers to make an opponent tapout, but has plenty of moves in his arsenal for an alternate ending. COMMON MOVES Border 2 Border -Tree of Woe Corner to Corner Dropkick The Rise to Power -Tiger bomb. NICKNAME EXPLANATIONS (can be used for writing as well.) Self-proclaims himself the PINNACLE PERFORMER because as we know a pinnacle is a lofty peak or the highest point and Garcia believes that he can go toe to toe with anyone in the business. This along with the PERFECT 10 nickname is where his arrogance comes into play. Perfect 10 is simply for how pretty his moves are. He loves to add a little flare to each one which is why there are a lot of corkscrew like moves in his arsenal. Over the years in the Indy scene he has earned the nickname of DANGER for his ability to slap on a submission move without a moments notice and MILE HIGH because he loves to fly! The FOX nickname is derived from him being very tricky, cunning, and mischievous. GRAVITY-DEFYING is self explanatory!
