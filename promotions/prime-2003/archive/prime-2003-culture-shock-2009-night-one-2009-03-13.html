In time he sits up, and over his shoulder he stares at the new champion, holding a title that he’d defended so gracefully until just these short few moments ago. A title that he’d added both prestige and honour to, and a title now tore holes in his stomach in the clutches of another competitor. Perhaps there’s been no greater champion than Chandler Tsonda. In the opposite corner, the new champion brushes his sweaty hair away from his face, and drapes his gold over his shoulder. He turns around to find Tsonda across the ring, leaning against the turnbuckle, and the two lock eyes. Perhaps there are more battles to come.
