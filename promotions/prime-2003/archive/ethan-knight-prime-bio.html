Ethan Knight Biography Nicknames 'The Infinite' Hometown Chicago, Illinois Birthday November 12, 1980 Height 5'9 Weight 194 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Another of Violence Jack's top subordinates alongside Tully and Hemlocke. VJ is the brawler and mastermind, Tully the hard-hitting mat general, and Knight provides the over-the-top daredevil tricks and brash attitude to enrage opponents. Outside the arena, Knight is as sullen and stoic as Tully and VJ. But when the lights dim and it's showtime, the Ethan Knight of pre-Sect days rears his head. Other Feds RPW; tSC Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style Watching Ethan wrestle isn't dissimilar from watching the current crop of top indy stars and NWA X-Division standouts: he's technically sound, innovative, fearless and exciting. His strength is in his unpredictability/innovation and speed, above all. His arsenal is jammed with various suicidas and planchas to demonstrate his daredevil mentality. He also has a penchant for adding a slingshot to many more traditional moves to add leverage and impact. Knight's an ambitious youth whose style can summarized in one word: (Cue Matrix-style Keanu Reeves) Whoa. Think any combination of AJ Styles, Jerry Lynn, Petey Williams, Chris Sabin, Alex Shelley, Frankie Kazarian, etc. He shines the most in bouts involving 3 or more men, breaking out big spot after big spot that leaves the crowd breathless and awe-struck afterward. Like Karina Wolfenden, he uses a lot of springboard attacks and doesn't slow down. If he breaks out a submission then it's likely some spontaneous, wild-looking rarity that no one else around does. His offense is chock-full of high-flying attacks and he has an apparent fetish for neckbreakers (seemingly out of homage to his mentor, Violence Jack, and his Detonator variations). Whereas his mentor could care less about popping the crowd or invoking awe in the audience, Ethan is the polar opposite and his array of flashy maneuvers properly depicts that. Such is one reason for nickname, 'The Infinite'; the nickname is derived from his seemingless endless arsenal of techniques. Whereas his aerial finisher is a 630 senton, he's also broken out a 540 backflip legdrop and corkscrew 450 in the past to the awe of the fans, just to give an idea what this guy is capable of. Ethan is small in almost whatever company he works for, and as such he tends to be beaten around (and virtually twisted into a pretzel) like a rag-doll by the bigger men. His style is something of an obnoxious underdog who the fans love watching get beat up for his big mouth, but at the same time can't help but cheer for his comebacks. Also has an odd comedic side to his wrestling that I'll delve into another time. Finisher Move Cyclone Breaker; Cyclone Breaker '04; Knightfall Finisher Description Cyclone Breaker -- Ethan sets his opponent in a front facelock and throws their near arm over his shoulder, as if for a suplex. He then spins quickly to the side, snapping the opponent down on his back in a swift spinning neckbreaker variation. Cyclone Breaker '04 -- Updated version of the original. After applying the front facelock and throwing the victim's near arm across his shoulders, Ethan hoists up the opponent and slingshots their legs off the ropes as if for a slingshot suplex. As they bounce backward he twists his body and goes into an elevated version of the Cyclone Breaker that lends much more momentum and power to the move. Often he'll angle it so it resembles a neckbreaker/DDT combination. Knightfall -- 630 senton splash Finisher Setup Move Typically any of his neckbreakers (out of homage to his mentor, Violence Jack, who also has an array of neckbreaker variations in his Detonator set) Setup Description See move list Other Moves Hellbender - The newest additon to Ethan Knight's repertoire. Best described in one phrase as a swinging fireman's carry slam with a leghook. Essentially he leans forward as if about to lift the opponent into a fireman's carry, hooking the near leg at the same time. He then spins, executing a spinning neckbreaker. ((Spotlighted in the following video http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LawKpR-GM0o at the 1:49 mark.)) Moonlight Matinee - Knights brings his opponent off the top in a variation belly-to-belly superplex, and as he does so backflips himself and lands in a uranage. (NOTE: One-man Spanish Fly used by the SAT, also used by Frankie Kazarian as Flux Capacitor). Bear in mind that Ethan isn't very big and thus this is restricted to use only on other lightweights. Electric Chair Bomb - With the opponent sitting atop Knight's shoulders, facing the same direction as Ethan, he falls backward into a bridging powerbomb type of maneuver and holds the bridge for the pin. He typically grips both of their wrists as well for added support. Again, not used on anyone except lightweights. Behold the Infinite - Knight grabs his victim under the arms and hoists them up as if for a Sky High/Rydeen Bomb. In the air he shifts position and falls back, bringing them down face/headfirst in an elevated Flatliner of sorts. As a variation, Knight can execute the move from out of a fireman's carry instead of a Rydeen Bomb setup, throwing the opponent's legs and body out in front of him and catching them in the move upon descent. Mid-Knight Driver - Knight stands back to back with his victim, their body suspended with arms and legs trapped (think Widow's Peak setup). From there he spins slightly and simply releases the opponent and sits out, dropping them facefirst on the canvas. AKA modified Gory Bomb. Old One's Revenge - Ethan picks up his opponent in what could be described as position for an inverted fallaway slam. He throws the victim's body out in front, spins them, and falls forward while dropping them on their face in a facefirst uranage variation. Flying body press Missile dropkick Hurricanranna Tiger suplex Springboard plancha suicida Springboard shooting star suicida Standing corkscrew shooting star press (ala Jack Evans) Corkscrew plancha/suicida Split-legged moonsault Reverse hurricanranna Somersault legdrop Running handspring clothesline Springboard dropkick to back of opponent's head (w/ chair when possible) Slingshot swinging inverted DDT Spinning heel kick Springboard spinning heel kick Hangman's neckbreaker Inverted facelock swung into neckbreaker Cross-armed neckbreaker Cobra-clutch neckbreaker Side neckbreaker Double underhook swinging neckbreaker Other notes Ethan gets beaten up a lot. Badly. He's outsized by most of the roster and gets bullied around if he's caught by one of the bigger guys. Tends to take a hell of a lot of punishment but still escapes somehow. Very flexible and the more submission-oriented guys will probably make a pretzel out of him at one point or another. Doesn't take a tremendous effort to get him to tap out after he's been thoroughly dissected. Entrance Music 'Laid To Rest' --- Lamb Of God Ring Entrances Standard entrance is simply Knight walking from the back, theme music playing, jawing with fans as any loudmouth, arrogant heel usually does. Normal rope tests and some in-ring stretches and insult-trading with the ringside spectators follows. PPV and big-match entrance is more elaborate. When the mood is more dramatic, he comes out silently in the same vein as VJ and Tully, garbed in black robes with an army of faceless, hooded cult members to either side of the aisle and around the ring. Once isndie, he takes the center and slowly removes his hood, expressionless and eyes unblinking. Something of a 'It's game time.' entrance. The lights are dim and a spotlight illuminates the inner ring as he prepares to lower the hood.
