Pranks With Attitude Biography Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography We had everything. Then we lost it all. We lived the high life in Vegas, then the low life in whatever gutter was available. Wrestling was what we were all about. So now it's time that it became all we are about again. Other Feds PWA, NTICW, PCW, OSW, RPW Other Fed Awards voted Greatest Tag Team in WrestleOnline's end of year awards Finisher Name The Darker Side of Comedy Finisher Description Nerezza forces the opponent up and over his head in a Crucifix Powerbomb set-up whilst Gaz Green ascends a neighbouring turnbuckle. As Nerezza falls forward Gaz executes a Blockbuster Neckbreaker and both moves hit simultaneously. Setup Move Instruments of Destruction Setup Move Description Both Gaz and Nerezza send their opponent across the ring via an Irish-whip and as the unsuspecting victim comes back the pranksters simultaneously hit a double shoulder-block. However they aim low and come from the side somewhat making the point of impact, the ribcage. Move Set Individual Moves: See Individual bios. Tag Team Moves: A Gory Variation: Nerezza hoists the opponent up and over his head into the opening sequence of a Gorybomb or Widow's Peak. From there he executes the move by tossing the opponent face first towards the canvas, the variation comes into play with Gaz Green. The Jester has a number of options in which to inject himself into the move, hence 'Variation' - With good timing he could hit a Running Bulldog, standing in wait he could latch onto the opponent mid-move and hit an Implant DDT or simply guillotine the victim across the back of the neck with a Legdrop. Puppet Play: With the victim facing away from the corner (and usually following a tag), Nerezza will ascend the top rope and grab the victim's hands, lifting their feet off the floor and shaking them about, making them look like a limp marionette. Gaz takes the opponent's legs under his armpits and Nerezza lifts the opponent one more time, dropping him down (sometimes with his face impacting the turnbuckles) where Gaz locks in a Boston Crab. The Nutcracker: Gaz and Nerezza stand to an opponent's side, taking an arm each and swap places, wrapping the opponent's arms around himself, straitjacket style. Still facing away from each other, they'll then kick as hard as they can on the opponent's inside lower leg, forcing the victim into the splits or as close as they can into doing so. With the arms still held, sometime's they'll lift the opponent up and down, increasing the pressure. In either case, the move always ends with a goofy pose of P.W.A. holding their own groins with a comical 'Ooooh!' look on their faces. Entrance Music 'Where's Your Head At?!' by Basement Jaxx
