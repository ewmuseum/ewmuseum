The Freak, Himself Biography Wrestler Name The Freak, Himself Nicknames Freak-Face, Freaky, Did he really just say that shit? Hometown San Diego, California Birthday February, 23, 1986 Height 5'6 Weight 145 lbs Biography Layout of the Freak, Himself Mansion ((For RP Purposes)) Basement 1: A Torture Chamber. Harmony's interrogation room. Basement 2: A 5 room apartment, stocked with everything needed to survive the aftermath of an atomic bomb for a good twenty years. Basement 3: A hallway with four rooms. Each room expands for a good hundred acres each and have high cielings. Each of the rooms are living, breathing wildlife habitats sporting exotic and alien wildlife. Room 1: Desert/Plains Room 2: Rain Forrest Room 3: Mountain/Arctic Room 4: No fucking clue here... Basement 4: One gigantic room with an ocean habitat, once again made to feel like the real thing. Has a real beach, deeeeeep floors, and expands a great distance. The floors are specially thick and designed to hold the water from leaking. Basement 5: A Dungeon area, like a castle, sporting a labrynth of hallways and secret passageways. Basement 6: The Sphynx's training room. A humongous cavern with puzzle rooms and special training sectors. The X-Men would be jealous. Basement 7: A science lab. Has been active since before Freak even moved in. they even have aliens in a bottle! Born in southern California in the early eighties, The Freak, Himself had an odd life from the get go. While attending a dressup party full of cocaine, alcohol, and more sexual intercourse than Catholic girl in her first semester of college, his parents were split up amongst the crowd. In an error of judgement, more than likely caused by the aforementioned innebriates, Freak's mom ended up sleeping with a professional Robert Smith impersonator (the same costume his father had worn that night) in the pool room. Perhaps his father should have worn a flower on his shirt, or worn a more conspicuous shade of red lipstick, but the deed had been done. The fact that the guy was about a foot taller than Freak's father and a hell of a lot better looking should have tipped her off, but to this day she assures it was an honest mistake. So Freak's father turned out to be a bisexual roller skate waiter who called himself Jan on the weekends. Freak's father, though dismayed, stuck around to raise the Freak. Freak's parents were very open to whatever he wanted him to do, gently guiding him through life and letting him find his own way instead of pushing him into things. He dabbled in drugs and alcohol as a teenager, and was very active in the school's drama club and english program, but he always stood out. Constantly making jokes that made George Carlin blush, Freak was so offensive that it was almost cute. He never had a shortage of friends, but he always lacked true companionship and understanding. Two loves in his life changed this. Music and wrestling. In music, he was able to be as wild, crazy, and incorrect as he deemed necessary and he only got praise for it. In wrestling, he was able to entertain with a complete unwillingness for his own well being and an insanity streak that caused for some very memorable moments later in his career. Though music remains a passion to this day, it was wrestling that truly harbored him and his character. Freak began his professional wrestling career by attending a school offered to a fellow college classmate. His friend dropped out quick, but Freak had a knack for it from the get go. His teacher, an old luchadore called The Sphynx, took Freak under his wing after seeing his immense, though unconventional potential. Freak's wrestling training was inexpensive due to his rappatiore with Sphynx, and as soon as he was deemed ready, he was sent on his first wrestling tour. Placed in Europe, the tour would mark the beginning of a roller coaster wrestling career. A few months into his first stint in Europe, Freak was doing rather well. His spastic style and outlandish promos made him somewhat of a cult favorite, and though he lost more than he won, he was embraced wholeheartedly by the crowd. This ended rather quickly when, while doing a show in Spain, Freak participated in the Running of the Bulls and was gored and thrown through the window of an old watch shop. His head hit first, and for a time after this, Freak had a case of amnesia that took him out of wrestling for a good while. Though not knowing who he was effected him, he was goaded into wrestling by a fellow wrestler and friend, Leech, and soon joined a place he would call home for five years: The CWA. In the CWA, he was fairly bland at first due to his memory lost, not impressing anybody at all with his charisma. His wrestling skills were adequate, but he was used more as a tool to be fed to the wolves. He then met Dr. Henry Lescher, a manager in the CWA who dabbled in human experimentation, who managed an evil, volumptuous vixen named Lunacy muse. At Lescher's behest, Freak underwent a surgery that changed his life forever. A chip was implanted into his brain that brought back his memory in full. The side effects, however, were somewhat drastic. Freak's mild psychosis was enhanced to a somewhat extreme level. He became more entertaining than he was before, but his words and actions became so offensive that, while entertaining, many people were put off by his sincere insanity. Another side effect, one that caused more hinderance, came in the form of head shots. Any time Freak is hit too hard in the head, the chip tweaks out a bit. He takes on verious schizophrenic personalities when this happens in the ring. Sometimes (as in the case of the angry german body builder), they help him immensely, and others (when he thought he was a girl scout intent on meeting a cookie quote), they turn disasterous, however entertaining. The third side-effect, and perhaps the most life altering, was Freak's new ability to hear his own life's narrator (a feat shared by his later love and various other mentally unstable people that he'd met throughout his life). Freak and his inner voice act as eachothers best friends and worst tormentor, though Freak gets his fair share of off-putting looks when he talks to him in public. It was around this time that Freak was riding fairly well in the CWA. Winning a couple of titles put him in the limelight, as well as a developed affinity for garnering random sponsorships from companies to promote their products. Soon, he had developed his winning Cheesy Smile, which not only seemed to magically boost sales for whatever product he was promoting amongst the wrestling demographic, but also caused his bank account to rise faster than Ron Jeremy at a Playboy mansion pool party. Having a steady flow of such high quantities of money only caused Freak to get more wreckless in the ring (and out...), as he no longer relied on wrestling for money: only as a means to enjoy his life. Lunacy Muse almost destroyed that side of him, however. A competant wrestler in her own right, and a face of pure evil, Lunacy offered Freak the one thing he didnt have: understanding. She understood him and used that to her advantage, manipulating and seducing Freak into a shell of a man. While under her spell, Freak would go through spurts of anger so horrifying that he actually hospitilized his old team mate Leech and quit a faction he was a part of known as the 4:20 Flock. Lunacy made Freak think that she loved him, all the while Lunacy and Dr. Lescher were introducing a one Harmony Fallacia into the industry with dreams of making her as sick and sadistic as Lunacy. Two is always better than one. Freak finally caught on to the facade that was his relationship when he caught Lunacy seducing a new CWA wrestler in the same way that she had tried to seduce him. Freak snapped, his heart a shattered mess, and left her. He returned to his old ways, vowing never to let loneliness sway him again. Lunacy proved to be a formidable foe, but soon grew bored of Freak and moved on with her life. It was around this time that Freak experienced his first major injury since spain. He threw himself into many violent match situations to release some of the agressions caused by Lunacy, and in doing so, wound up in an electrified cage match. While trying to piss on an opponent who constantly referred to himself as "The Shit" (you know...to give him balance or something), Freak crossed streams with the fence and was thrown a long distance through a table and a gaurd rail. The accident, though brutal, only left the permanent injury of rare erectile dysfunction (though probably not as rare as he'd like you to think). As well, he broke his arm and fractured his hand so bad that a robotic pinky was installed. Kind of like Skywalker, but a lot less impressive. Though he is able to accomplish great feats of strength with his cyber-pinky, well....for god sakes, who really cares? After six months of rehabilitating his body and training, Freak returned to the CWA ring (now called the AW) with visions of grandeur in his head. Freak had long since decided that as soon as he won a world title, he would find a new place to settle down. After winning numerous Hardcore and TV belts, and a tag belt, he finally won his first title in the AW in an impressive match only two months after his return. Upon looking for a new place to live, he noticed that the old Lescher mansion was for sale, and upon checking it out, he found Harmony Fallacia, once a sweet, innocent newcomer to the wrestling industry, sitting on a floor in a dirty hospital gown giggling and murdering the rats that infested the place. Mind you, she did so out of simple curiosity, not malice. The tests that she had gone through while under the care of Lescher and Lunacy Muse had rendered her even more insane than him, and he took her with him to try and help rehabilitate her, knowing all to well what Lunacy could do to a person. Harmony had, (and still has, really), a knack of randomly experimenting on woodland creatures using toasters, high voltages, aerosol emmissions...whatever happens to cross her mind really, as a way to feed her curiosity. This phased Freak at first, though he grew to think it a cute little quirk instead of the monstrous act that most people do when they meet her. Freak soon bought a mansion in the outskirts of Corpus Christi, Texas for a good price and moved the two of them in. Upon inspecting the house, Freak noticed that the basement area was more than what it seemed, as it housed a plethora of torture tools and devices. Throughout the rest of his time in the AW, further exploration of the house revealed that it had apparently been owned by somebody very rich and into the strange, as the floors only descended deeper into the ground, each one much larger and much stranger than the last. A list of the floors and what they hold is as follows: Basement 1: A Torture Chamber. Harmony's interrogation room. A humongous cavern with puzzle rooms and special training sectors. The X-Men would be jealous. Basement 7: A science lab. Has been active since before Freak even moved in. they even have aliens in a bottle! He has yet to venture any deeper, but more rooms still await. The wildlife rooms caused Harmony to go on a masochistic rampage on endangered wildlife that lived there, and caused Freak to bar her from it. The science lab, in general caused in accident that first shrunk the Freak to the size of an ant before causing the Narrator to become a real person (an effect that, happily for both Freak and Narrator, wore off after a period of weeks). Regardless, many a fun time has been had exploring these floors, and many are sure to come. Harmony's rehabilition continued as Freak rose in the wrestling ranks, winning the tag belts with her in two different federations. Soon, the CIA showed up at the mansion for a strange visit. Seeing her "accidents" on TV and hearing about them through various inside sources sent shivers of both interest and fear down the spine of the government. Instead of detaining her, they offered her a job "interrogating" threats to the American way of life. Her special skills made her perfect for the job, and she has since cut back drastically on the unneeded torture of cuddly tree folk. Now she just tortures the hell out of terrorists and Wal-Mart protestors. After a brief stint in Leech's own fed, the NPA (where Freak won the world title twice, upon opening and re-opening), the place was temporarily taken over by a group called The Syndicate. After the The Syndicate saw that Freak had the most sway as far as a rebellion would be concerned, they had him "taken out", which means some huge bald guy hit him in the head and caused him to spaz out. For a few weeks, Freak lost the ability to hear Narrator, which drove Narrator slightly insane, and though Freak was no bad guy, he was devoid of basically any emotion other than loyalty to the Syndicate. Freak eventually came to his senses, but too late to spare Narrator, who has a much shorter fuse than he used to. He then returned to the AW to make a mark. A feud with the albino twin brothers, The Shmelkies (who weighed maybe a buck ten combined), caused Freak to dig deep and really find what he was made of. For some reason, the brothers and their manager, Adam West, struck an unheard of fear into Freak's heart. Three matches later, all of which saw Freak beating the brothers within fifteen seconds, he still hadn't quite gotten over his fear that they were really ressurected Norse gods out to devour his soul...but he'd made progress. After meeting up with the Sphynx again and recieving some training in various MMA and martial arts techniques (for protection against the damn albino Nords, as he claimed), Freak was becoming a formidable in ring opponent, and decided to once again shoot for AW gold by challenging the biggest, most vicious star in the AW. Rated R. Rated R and Freak brought the best out of eachother for their brief feud. Freak was fired up as he never had been before, and Rated R was so on his toes, never having dealt with an opponent quite like Freak, that the feud was a hot commodity. The brutallity the two dished out on eachother helped Freak two fold: by empowering his in-ring confidence, and by helping both him and Harmony to realize exactly what they would do without the other. Freak's victory over Rated R in their first outing was celebrated by the odd couple's first kiss, and they've been strangely in love ever since. However, the AW soon closed down, and unfinished business once again reared his ugly head. During his hiatus, Freak had been playing in a few fun bands to pass the time. Him and Harmony tried redecorating, but pigs blood made horrible wall paint and, well...we've all seen Carrie. He also prepared for an in ring return in his own odd way, knowing the itch would soon become unbearable. He smoked a bit less while rehashing some old moves, and started smoking more as soon as he realized how much he hated cardiovascular workouts (what better way to say "fuck you, treadmill than with a tasty Lucky Strike, no?). His ring attire also needed an upgrade. He started adding bright, flamboyant colors to his apparel, as well as forearm tassels, as he found them chique and unique. As well, he finally got his masters degree in Psychology (who knew?) and kept up with some of the celebrity friends (most of them psycopaths) who tended to stop in from time to time. However, his calling has always been wrestling, and he has returned once again. Though his confidence has depleted to a certain degree, his recent acquisition of the Boots of Higher Mediocrity +2 (aka, Doink the Clowns old wrestling boots), have given him a highly unrealistic sense of accomplishment that should help him in his future wrestling endeavors. He's still the same old Freak...ready to give it his all, however much (or little) that may be. Recently, the Freak, Himself and Harmony have reason to celebrate after finding out that the two are indeed expecting a child. With a spring marriage in the plans, and a name to be decided, the next nine months could very well decide the fate of mankind if Narrator's prophecy of imminant apocolypse comes to fruition. He does have a tendancy to overreact...though Harmony's real first name is Rosemary. In his own words, describing his career: "I've jobbed and won titles. I've been at the top, straddled the bottom, and ridden the middle lane. No, not Nathan Lane...though he did once come on to me at a Lion King premiere my mom took me to when I was nine. I'm an expert at fighting techniques the MMA wouldn't allow me to employ because they said, and I quote, "Stop! It looks like you're humping the guy. Nobody would EVER do that in a real fight!", "Jesus, will you please wrestle and stop testing the durability of his buttocks against your open palm?", and "The only people who would pay to see that live with their mothers and get movies about snuff films made about them". All very respectable comments which have led me to the conclusion that I'm unpredictable, I strike where least expected, and violent, lonely males tend to enjoy my wrestling...which is the demographic we aim for, right? And I'm told I'm extremely politically incorrect, but I'm an independant so that's probably A-OK. All in all, my proudest moments have been my multiple sponsorships that keep the cash flowing like a bullemic at an all you can eat Sizzler. Single handedly almost ending the career of...well...myself about one or two dozen times is a fairly noteworthy accomplishment as well. Finally standing up to those two ninety pound, albino balls of pure satanic evil known as the Shmelkies was probably the biggest accomplishment to date, as I smacked them around the ring as if they were mere mortals and not the Gods of lore I had envisioned from them. They probably just forgot their milk that day. Regardless...I have had a lustrous career, and for that, I thank me. Without me, none of it would have been possible, and quite frankly, had anyone else tried half of what I've gotten away with, they would be serving consecutive life sentences watching The Simple Life with Pauly Shore in some secret government facility that takes care of complete social failures. Just expect the unexpected, always double wrap it, and remember that there's nothing to fear but the Freak, Himself." And the rest, as they say, has yet to be written.....
