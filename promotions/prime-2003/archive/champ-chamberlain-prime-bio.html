Champ Chamberlain Biography Wrestler Name Champ Chamberlain Nicknames "The Center of Attention", "The Only Attraction", "The Man In Demand" Hometown New York City, New York Birthday September 25th, 1982 Height 6'2 Weight 230 lbs Fed Titles None as of yet Fed Awards None as of yet Biography Champ Chamberlain is unlike most of the people you’ll come across in the wrestling business, heck in life. While almost all wrestlers are drawn to power, glory, being in the spotlight there is only one thing that really drives Chamberlain and it’s the attention of the spotlight. He doesn’t just go after it, or like it, or want it. He absolutely is addicted to it, like a druggie hooker is addicted to crack kind of addiction. The spotlight, hell all lights, and eyes have to be focused on him at all times. If there is something going on that isn’t giving him face time, giving him time with everyone watching, he wants to know about it and he’s quite likely to try and find a way to put the attention on himself. Basically nothing is off limits for Champ. It’s an anything goes, no holds barred, take no prisoners, 24/7 job to make sure he has all the attention he craves. There aren’t going to be many people who can stand Champ, let alone actually like him, but you can’t tell him that. All the booing, the ugly looks, the beat downs, the cursing, and any other things the fans or fellow wrestlers can throw at him are simply, in Champs mind, their way of letting him know how much they love him. How much they have to be around him, be like him, how much they want to be him. He’s the Center of Attention and All Eyes Are On Him, baby! Other Feds N/a Other Fed Titles N/a Other Fed Awards N/a Wrestling Style Sports Entertainment, Cheating Finisher Move All Eyes On Me, Champ Stamp Finisher Description The Border City Stretch, The Backstabber Finisher Setup Move Any Setup Description ): Anything can be used to set up the finishes. Champ will often utilize some sort of cheating to put himself into position to get one of his finishers. Special Moves See Move List Other Moves Style Champ’s style is heavily influenced by the Sports Entertainment style of the real world today. He has loads of SE punches, kicks, and knees in his striking and will do a little bit of everything, in terms of moves, to win his matches. He adds that to his love for cheating at every oppurtunity. Strategy Champ’s only real stratedy is to make sure he looks as good as he possibly can, at every possible moment, during a match. He has no set route, or thought process, as to how he’ll get to a victory he just goes with the flow of the match, and the style(s) of his opponent(s) to get himself toward the goal of the W. He uses a healthy does of dirty tactics and he does, most of the time, try to employe the strategy of doing things to weaken the shoulder, neck, and back muscles of his opponents in order to set up his finishes to work as effectively as possible. Strengths - Very atheletic. - No morals, or Code of Honor, holding him back from doing anything and everything deemed necessary to win - Has a sadistic streak in him that will surprise people - Has a good mind for the ring, in terms of where he’s at and his surroundings, that is often a surpise to people cause he’s seen as such a self absorbed prick that he wouldn’t pay attention to such things. It’s survival at it’s finest when it comes to Champ in the ring. - Knows how and when to take advantage of a mistake. - So adapt at cheating that it’s hard to catch him in the act, but it does happen. Weaknesses - Loves the spotlight of showing off and showboating too much. It often leads to him losing control of situations. - While he’s a tough guy, and hates to lose, he doesn’t have that huge ‘Heart and Determination’ that you’ll find in the loveable faces. If someone locks him into a submission, in the center of the ring, and it’s clear pretty quickly that he has no way out he will NOT stay in the submission for long periods of time just avoid tapping. Live to screw someone out of a W another day, is his motto. - Can easily fall prey to a valet like Ellie, or other attractive females, trying to get his attention, as a distraction for their wrestlers, cause he’s so self absorbed he actually believes they’re just into him. - While he’s athletic, and well rounded, he’s not great at anything so he’ll struggle in areas of wrestling where his opponent is really an expert at it. For example if he’s facing a submissions expert he’ll struggle to fend off their submissions, or reverse them, and there for will have to use loads of rope breaks or blatant cheats to save himself. Flyers are going to be faster, more agile, and quicker than him so they can fly around the ring and give him loads of trouble. Things of that nature should be kept in mind. Regular Moves Forearm Smashes, Kicks and punches (Exaggerated Sports Entertainment Variety), Running back elbow, Running forearm, Various Suplex (Snap Suplex is his favorite and the one he hits to perfection), Side Russian Leg Sweep, Drop Kick Variations (Standing Drop Kick is his favorite), Atomic Drop, Knife Edge Chops, Fist drop, Running Clothesline, Cross-body Variations (Depends on size of opponent), Back-breaker, Standard DDT, Pump-Handle Slam, Scoop Slam, Standard Body Slam, Standard Leg and Elbow drop, Shoulder Breaker, Arm Drag Variations, Pile-driver, Jaw Breaker, Hip Toss, Monkey Flip, Arm Bar Variations, pretty much anything sports entertainment that can be thought of.
