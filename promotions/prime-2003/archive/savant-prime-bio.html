Savant Biography Wrestler Name Savant Nicknames 'The Crown Princess of the Dead Man's Hand,' 'The Purple-Haired Monster', 'The Tiny Terror', 'The Dreadlocked Deceiver'. Hometown Currently believed to reside in Paris, France. Birthday 6/18/1984 Height 5'5 Weight 129 lbs. Fed Titles Managed Lazarus in the NWC to the Diamond Wrestling Syndicate Crown Jewel. Biography The star protegee of SCCW mainstay, Alexandra 'Desade' Pierce, Savant is generally the quiet one of the Dead Man's Hand, the one sent in for the long-con. She's snide, mean, and utterly devoted to her mentor's cause. Other Feds Sin City Championship Wrestling, NWC: Pacific, Diamond Wrestling Syndicate Wrestling Style Street-fighting/extreme cheatification. Finisher Move The Sinker Finisher Description Planned to be the third in a chain of Hook, Line, and Sinker, the Sinker is the Sliding D (sliding forearm smash) popularized by Masato Tanaka. She'll often load her elbow pad with something hard before delivering it. Finisher Setup Move The Hook (a diving kneedrop bulldog aka the Calf Branding) and/or The Line (the Sliced Bread #2) Setup Description The Hook: Savant (seated on the top rope) puts her knee behind the head of her opponent, stepping off and riding them down, knee across the back of the neck. Special Moves The Spider's Kiss (the finisher of Alexandra Pierce) is something that all her girls are capable of -- it's a double-knee facebreaker. Other Moves Key things to remember: Savant isn't really skilled in a "wrestling" sense. She's not going to be able to play catch-as-catch-can with anyone, she can't fly like Lindsay Troy, and she can't punch like Cozen. Any fighting skills that Lauren has are directly as a result of her training by Alexandra Pierce, whose own wrestling style is more "eclectic" than anything. Her moves are designed to actively injure her opponent, not put them down for some arbitrary three-count, and this works really well if she's fighting you on the street (she's remarkably skilled with a weapon), but not so well in the ring -- this is why she usually leaves the DMH's in-ring action to Desade or Cozen. Entrance Music The beginning of Nine Inch Nails' 'My Violent Heart', melded with the Kidneythieves' 'Zerospace'. Ring Entrances Coming Soon!
