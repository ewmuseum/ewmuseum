In the event the hands do lock, then, victory is established.) Special Moves See Other Moves, please. Other Moves I. Short Intro to O'Neil. Jason O'Neil began life as the character named JNightmare. JNightmare was an average dark gimmick with a real penchant for pain, both inflicted, and inflicting. As time wore on, Nightmare became a bit overblown, thanks to the gimmicky, crappy Geocities, Angelfire, and Topcities feds of his day. I never much cared for the way the character went, and allowed his associations carry how the character developed, instead of developing him myself. His first taste of humanity was in GCW, run by Steven Caldera. He evolved from JNightmare, into "The Ticking Time Bomb" persona, which was more or less because of an association with Twisted, run by [name withheld] as you know as the handler of The New Victories. After being left by Twisted, he turned into this persona, complete with crappy facial tick, and horrible impact. Needless to say, it didn't last long. I turned from that, and completely immersed him into the Dark Gimmick side of his character, turning him from the former gimmick, into "The Dark Messiah". Rather than just call him that, I needed a name, and Jason O'Neil was born. The gimmick was fantastic, and was actually a well run Dark Gimmick. Rather than be sad, depressed, and upset about how life kicked him in the teeth, and he wrestled just to feel sorry for himself, TDM was dark and angry, because Wrestling sucked, especially the politics of GCW. He made it his goal to dismantle the place, person by person. As time wore on, O'Neil actually did help tear down GCW, and rebuilt it into an image of what he thought wrestling should be. Extreme, gritty, nasty and sick. Unfortunately, it didn't take O'Neil long to hear the call of another company, promising him they'd make him a star, and he left GCW before becoming the World Champion to enter FSW (The WO Era one). He also changed gimmicks, from the Dismantler of Dreams, The Dark Messiah, to The Scarecrow, a symbolic move in that he looked at himself still as a Messiah, but from the other side of the fence. It was the first time O'Neil was ever run as a face, and he went over like balls Marie. He went from Dark Gimmick, to a Dark Gimmick with a brighter twist. O'Neil had a feud with Angelo Deville that burnt down the house for agression, anger and hate between the two that is still evident to this day. He's part of the reason that O'Neil came to tSC under a hood. Currently, O'Neil is playing the part of a lovable loser type. Think Mick Foley, with a different gimmick. He's certainly not going to win tons and tons of matches, but he's going to be in every single one. He's not expecting to win, and he's not expecting to actually do much of anything, aside from entertain the crowd, and give a match people can be proud of. II. Psychology of Jason O'Neil. O'Neil believes in Honor over everything. He's not one that will take advantage of an opponent, and will rely on their mistakes to turn against them. Regardless, O'Neil will not allow a match to get out of control. If he feels things are getting too fierce, or an opponent is shooting, he'll lock on a painful submission hold for a bit, not to get someone to submit, but to remind them that he's not one an opponent wants to take liberties with. He also doesn't do the punch kick type stuff that others do. He's methodical in his approach. If he's going to try and finish a match with one finisher, he'll work over that part of the body until such time that he feels it's sufficiently worn down. Then, he'll lock in a submission hold, exploiting that body part. O'Neil wrestles in three phases: A. Feeling Out, B. The Wear Down, and C. The Finale. In section A, O'Neil is a bit more methodical, doing the common feel out kinds of moves. Section B, O'Neil will try and wear his opponent down, with moves that will target a certain bodily area, depending on weaknesses, prior injuries, etc. Section C, is just that. THe build up to the finale, and finishing moves. III. The Common Moves Suplexes O'Neil likes to fancy himself a master of the suplex. He's mastered a few too many, and as a result, only uses the following as a typical Wear Opponent Down part of the Match. a. Vertical Suplex (duh) b. Belly to Belly Suplex c. Belly to Back Suplex d. Pancake Suplex e. Side Suplex f. Gutwrench Suplex. Other. O'Neil is not a punch/kick sort of guy. He's more apt to apply the following holds instead of simply kicking or striking to wear down an opponent. a. Headlock, Any b. Armbar, Any c. Leg Takedown (Single, Double, cradle) d. Hiptoss > Armbar. e. Running Drop Toe Hold > Leg Bar. f. Irish Whip x4 >Running Knee Strike x4 > Follow Lariat IV.
