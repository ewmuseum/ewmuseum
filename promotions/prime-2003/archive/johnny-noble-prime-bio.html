Johnny Noble Biography Nicknames Mr. Incredible; Mr. Morality Hometown Tacoma, Washington Birthday Feb 1, 1953 Height 6'7' Weight 320 Fed Titles - Fed Awards - Biography PRIME History ReVolution 85 No Match Scheduled * Noble came down to the ring post-Main Event and kicked a whole bunch of ass. ReVolution 86 Johnny Noble vs. Baltasar (Single || Non-title) Winner = Baltasar ReVolution 87 Johnny Noble vs. Brian Arson (Single || Non-Title) Personal History: He was a wrestling champ in high school before enlisting to go to Vietnam to fight for his country. Then he came back and a VERY successful 15 year run, touring all over the world for various independant promotions. (What else was there in the 70's?) Before retiring in 1985 at the age of 33. Now his money's dried up and he needs work. He returns to wrestling, which is all he knows. But he doesn't know as much as he used to. These new-fangled moves like powerbombs and DDTs comfuse him and the idea of a 'tweener' boggles his mind. He's an old dog without the new tricks. He a super-face and acts as such. He lives and breaths God, Republicans and Americana. Picture Adam West, Hulk Hogan, Jim Duggan, James t. Kirk, and GI Joe all rolled into one person. mmhmm He loves telling people how he main evented for the biggest outdoor wrestling crowd the state of Washington has ever seen! Nearly 40,000 people were there! General Appearance: 'Superstar' Billy Graham as he looked in the 70's... but 30 yearsa older. Long hair with MASSSIVE SCOTTISH sideburns, standard blue trunks (with three large white stars on the front) and ring boots with white 'NOBLE' on the boots and back of the trunks. Red knee pads. White wrist tape. Always wears something to the ring that he can give away to the blind crippled kid who always happens to be front row. Other Feds Several dozen since the 70's. Other Fed Awards Numerous titles and acalaides Wrestling Style Old School/All Around/Big Man Finisher Move The Incredi-Slam Finisher Description Military Press Drop Finisher Setup Move Irish Whip Setup Description Optional Other Moves Trademark Move set: > Incredi-Boot: Big Boot to Face > Incredi-Plex: Stalling Vertical Suplex > Incredi-Splash: Body Splash (On VERY special/rare occasions he'll do it from the first, second, or *gasp* even top rope!!) Standard Move set: > Think Wrestlemania One > Suplexes > Clotheslines > Varied punches and kicks > Slams of all shapes and sizes (his finisher's a body slam, though. Be careful!) > Locks; head, arm, leg, wrist > Old School submissions (Boston Crab, Figure Four, etc) Gimmick Information: Cheesy! This man stopped wrestling in 1986 and he's picking up exactly where he left off. He has the cunning of Jake Roberts with all the gullibility of Hulk Hogan. He breathes crowd reaction. If they aren't behind him he has no power. *PLEASE DON'T NO-SELL THIS* Do: > Anything that would be uber babyface. > Except in the case of a squash he will almost always come from behind. After the halfway point of the match he'll always be down for a long two count. > For submissions his arm will always almost hit the mat on the third drop. > His come-from-behind wins will happen in the last minute of the match. He'll break out into a temporary rush of invincibility by throwing everyone and anyone off him Big Show-style. > Before each of the previous happenings the crowd HAS to start chanting for him. (Think Hulking-Up) Don't > Johnny will never eeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeever cheat. Under any circumstances. He won't thumb to the eye. He won't grab the shorts. He won't use a chair. The idea of a 'tweener' makes no sense to him. All he understands is mega-heel and mega-face. > No flying off the top rope. > No luchadore stuff. > Careful of modern wrestling moves we take for granted. Powerbombs were rare in the 80's and DDTs didn't show up until Jake the Snake so they were new on the scene. Not a part of the main arsenal. Think old-school, please. HAVE FUN I think this character could be a blast and a half to write with and for. So please, matchwriter, enjoy yourself. If you can stay within these guidelines then add anything else you want. You can make-up old matches for him. You can have a memory of him at a show when you were a kid. You can love him, hate him, or swear you've never heard of him. But, if you've never heard of him he'll tell you of his great exploits to try and jog your memory. Enjoy! Entrance Music Bill Conti - 'Gonna Fly Now' Ring Entrances (More of a template than a copy & paste) The trumpets sound and the audience explodes. The trumpets sound again and they're on their feet. The bass kicks in, the blue and white lights roam and flash about the arena, 'Mr. Incredible' Johnny Noble walks out and the roof comes off the building! He walks down wearing his latest merchandise, slapping hands, posing for photos, kissing babies. Then he stops at the base of the ringsteps, pulls off the t-shirt and gives it to the youngest ringside fan he can find. Then he gets in the ring and points to north side of the arena, beating his chest after to work the crowd. Then the east, then south, then west. He does a circular overhead motion (like a tornado) and beats his chest again with the whole arena being worked up. [Feel free to edit/alter and add specifics. Also, during the chest-beating/crowd-working part... that's when his opponants would go for the cheap shot.]
