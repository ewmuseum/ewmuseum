Around the corner, inside of the Lawlor Events Center, comes a black Mustang going as fast it possibly can inside of such a cramped area. The parking garage that leads right into the arena in Reno, Nevada, is filled with the screeching and the smell of burnt rubber quickly engulfs the garage. The Mustang is then seen parking into an empty space and out steps a man who has a determined look on his face. At the mere glimpse of his face, the fans know that tonight is going to be another interesting night. Inside of the ring, the fans immediately start chanting his name. DUSK! DUSK! DUSK! DUSK! Normally, Dusk would smile at the chants, but this time around he’s not in the mood. Instead, his mind is far away from the land of happiness. Instead, he’s focused. He’s determined. He’s been put in a mood that he hasn’t felt for a while now. Deep inside of him, he feels a fury that has long remained dormant. He can feel it swell inside of him to the point that he’s ready to explode. From the rented Mustang, he pulls out a leather bag and drops it to the ground. Then, he pulls out one of the most prized, and controversial, belts in the PTC world these days, the PTC Extreme Championship. The past week had been a whirlwind for him, having to spend a few days in the hospital to get all of his injuries checked out. This in turn had made PRIME commissioner Blaine Blair hesitant to book Dusk this week in a week where challengers for championship belts were being determined. At the hands of Clinton Sage, he had been demoralized and defeated. Eighteen staples were placed in his head to keep the wound created by slamming his head through the window of a car closed. On top of that, he had three bruised ribs which ached at every possible second. Because of all this, he had been left off the card. Instead, his enemies, Pierce Lavelle and Devin Shakur, had been given prime matches that could give them title shots. Dusk, on the other hand, had to sit on the sideline and watch. This made for an angry Dusk. One that was so angry that he had to make a return appearance at FUSE’s show, Killzone. He had made it a point to make sure that Sage felt his anger. After entering the ring and delivering the Lights Out, he felt that Sage knew exactly what state Dusk was in. This helped Dusk a little bit, but not nearly enough. Especially after the shitstorm he had gone through from FUSE members who felt disrespected by Dusk’s appearance. Dusk only had two words for them, two words which had become synonymous with PRIME. Fuck. You. As he picks his leather bag up off the ground and slings it across his shoulder, he turns around only to see a person he despises as much as he does Sage. Jonathan Winters. Winters was a complex man, and one who had caused Dusk quite a bit of problems since he had been here. Dusk: Are you not a man of my word? Winters: Do I even need to answer that?
