Osaka Street Cutters Biography Tag Team Name Osaka Street Cutters Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Combined Record: 2-0-1 ReV 216: W - Osaka Street Cutters/Jason Snow vs. Tyler Rayne/Chandler Tsonda/Bryan Dawkins ReV 217: W - Mach Hawke vs. Hessian ReV 218: NC - Osaka Street Cutters vs. Change in Spades (FRPW Tag Title Match) Other Feds Go-Rin Puroresu (Five Rings Professional Wrestling) Other Fed Titles FRPW Tag Team Champions: August 24, 2008 to Present Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style Stiff technical Finisher Name Various (See matchwriting aids) Finisher Description Various Setup Move Various Special Moves Stuffed high angle tiger suplex, Backbreaker held for a flying stomp, German suplex/leg drop combo, Bow and Arrow/Running SSP with knees, Double brainbuster, Double flapjack/Double kick Move Set When it comes to tag matches, the Osaka Street Cutters operate like a well-oiled machine, with the goal to isolate, dominate, and destroy either member of the opposition. Mach Hawke will almost always start the match, working a fast pace, looking to get the advantage, and bringing in Azuma when needed to do the heavy damage. Azuma will be really difficult for opponents to work over, as he's always fighting back at one hundred percent intensity, is unpredictable, and is a much stiffer worker than Hawke. Thus, opponents will likely focus their attacks on Hawke. When in control, the Cutters will utilize quick tags to stay fresh and overwhelm their opponent with double team maneuvers, looking for frequent covers. Sometimes they will utilize submissions in a further attempt to wear the opponent down. The Cutters do not have a set finisher as such, frequently ending matches with techniques from their own move sets. They do have a number of set double team techniques that they break out often. Double Team Maneuvers Stuffed tiger suplex: This is a possible finishing move. Azuma hooks both of the opponent's arms in a tiger suplex, placing his head in the center of their back and lifts them up in a high angle tiger suplex. Hawke comes from behind, pulling down their legs as they come over backwards to drive their heads into the mat with extra force. Backbreaker/Stomp: Hawke will catch the opponent in either a tilt-a-whirl or a pendulum backbreaker, holding them in position across his knee. Azuma will them run up, jump in the air, and come down with brutal stomp, driving a foot down on both sides of Hawke's knee into the chest and stomach of the opponent. Usually done from standing position, but if done from the top rope is a possible finishing move. Abdominal Stretch/Yakuza Kick: Hawke will trap the opponent in an abdominal stretch, exposing their face, while Azuma comes off the ropes to connect with a sick yakuza kick. Bow and Arrow Lock/Running SSP: Azuma will drive the opponent face first into the mat with a wheelbarrow slam, before quickly transitioning into a Bow and Arrow Lock. He'll pull them back so they're facing upward, while Hawke runs toward them, does a standing Shooting Star Press, tucks in his legs, and lands knees first across the chest of the opponent trapped in the Bow and Arrow. German suplex/Slingshot leg drop: Hawke will stand outside on the ring apron while Azuma slams the opponent backward with a bridging German suplex. At the moment of impact, Hawke will slingshot in to drop a leg across the face of the suplexed foe. Flapjack Kick: Both Cutters will whip the opponent into the ropes, catch them coming back with a big flapjack, tossing them high in the air, and meeting them on the way down with a pair of stiff kicks to the breadbasket. Entrance Music 'Cloud Connected' by In Flames Ring Entrances The lights in the arena dim, as the synthed-up guitars from the opening of In Flames "Cloud Connected" begin a slow build. Red, yellow, and green strobe lights shine upwards from the top of the entrance ramp, illuminating a cloud of smoke billowing from the ground. As the guitar riff turns heavy, three figures appear silhouetted in the color-shifting mist. (Match related commentary) The three men step through the mist, Mach Hawke at the forefront, black pants, black sleeveless shirt, and black wrestling mask, with Mamoru Azuma and Kaz Araki to his left and right respectively. Hawke and Azuma have their FRPW championship belts strapped across their waists. The lyrics to their theme song kick in as the three walk to the ring, ignoring the outstretched hands of the fans at ringside.
