Change In Spades Biography Tag Team Name Change In Spades Fed Titles PRIME Tag Team Championship Biography After being unceremoniously dismissed in March of 2008 by C.P. Cantrell, Nitz Donnelly took his time rehabilitating his left knee. It wasn't until early the next year... January to be exact that he came into contact with Leticia Mendoza in California. Her father runs a beach side hotel on Venice Beach and Leticia helps run a gym in the same area that teaches Mixed Martial Arts, etc.. While no one really knows where he came from, Nitz found his way to California instead of going back home to Boston. Removed from contact with his family months before, Nitz decided to look up his old childhood friend. Leticia welcomed The Ego with open arms and it was there in her gym that he met the former Custom CHAOS Champion of FUSE Vance Raymes. The Tortured Artist had been staying in the Los Angeles area since his departure from FUSE and met up with Leticia through as of yet unknown circumstances. Nitz was offered the use of a small beach house not far away and his California adventure had begun. After a lot of training, adventures and so forth, Leticia Mendoza toyed with Nitz about a possible return to the squared circle. Nitz scoffed at the idea but eventually changed his mind when Vance agreed to be his partner. The trio were eventually off to take PRIME by storm. Certainly, Raymes still had his fans from his FUSE days and Nitz was definitely no stranger to the landscape of PRIME. Put Leticia, a trained mixed martial artist in her own right, in their corner and there is no telling what will happen. Manager Leticia Mendoza Wrestling Style A power/highflying combination with some technical Finisher Name Ace of Spades Finisher Description Raymes lifts up his opponent into a stalling powerbomb. Donnelly comes flying off the ropes in a corkscrew and Raymes drops as soon as he comes close, hitting a combination powerbomb and corkscrew splash. Whomever is legal makes the pin, Donnelly can roll away for Raymes or keep the pin if he's legal. Setup Move Any Donnelly or Raymes setup is fine. Setup Move Description - Special Moves Be creative.
