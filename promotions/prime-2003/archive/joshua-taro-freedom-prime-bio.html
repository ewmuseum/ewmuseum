Joshua Taro Freedom Biography Wrestler Name Joshua Taro Freedom Nicknames JTF Hometown Niigata, Japan Birthday April 8th 1983 Height 5`11 Weight 213lbs Fed Titles 5 Star - Alias - Tag Biography Joshua born in Niigata Japan to a Yakuza boss spent his childhood surrounded by people trained in combat. At three Joshua took his first step on learning how to wrestle and fight his way through life. By six Joshua was competing in tournaments and winning due to his drive and the ever present pushing from his father. At Nine Joshua’s mother was killed before his eyes, and his father lost his strength. Being a devoted husband her murder pulled the blindness his life of crime had done to his family away. Seeking an out his farther over night removed Joshua from school and within three days they were on a plane heading for America. Their escape hadn’t been without revenge, Joshua witnessed up close and uncensored just who his father was and had been since he was a teenager. The men responsible for killing his mother where laid to rest before his very eyes, and Joshua had sat and smiled throughout. Once in America Joshua continued his own wrestling pilgrimage. Under the watchful eye of his father Joshua sort out wrestling companies and competed in some blood bathed matches with a smile. His wars raged through many professional companies and even came to a head when he was arrested on a double homicide charge. Released due to lack of Evidence Joshua took a leave of absents to regain his composer. Now colder than ever Joshua eyes a return, his bloody heeled and his mind once more focused on gaining the biggest price in wrestling, the world championship Other Feds 4CW, HWA Other Fed Titles 4CW - Universal Title.
