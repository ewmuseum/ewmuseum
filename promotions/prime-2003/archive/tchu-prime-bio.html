Entrance Music 'I Fucking Hate You' by Godsmack Ring Entrances Entrance Description - the lights in the arena go out. After a few seconds of silence and darkness, Godsmack's 'I Fucking Hate You' roars through the arena as blue lights begin to slowly blink on and off around the building (think HHH's entrance, only in varying shades of blue instead of red/green/yellow etc). As Tchu appears, emerging from the back, the crowd erupts and the Inhuman Being makes his way to the ring with methodical, determined steps. Once in the ring, Tchu will usually climb the middle turnbuckle in one corner of the ring and form a 'T' with his arms, then throw them apart with an animalistic 'roar', as the fans chant/sing along with the chorus of 'I fucking Hate You'.
