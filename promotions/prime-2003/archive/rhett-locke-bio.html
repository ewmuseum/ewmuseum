Rhett Locke Biography Wrestler Name Rhett Locke Nicknames The Albino, The Underdog Hometown Truth or Consequences, New Mexico. Birthday 2-28-78 Height 6'0 Weight 225 lbs Biography Rhett Locke is a man who has chosen to not divulge much into his past. But if you've ever heard of skeletons in your closet, this guy has the entire lost Mayan civilization in there. To those who do know his life before wrestling, he asks that everyone keep it quietly to themselves. To summarize it all in a sentence... He was depraved, raped and psychotic. Then came Old School Wrestling, his chance at a new life. At first it was all he could ask for. Holding the OSW World Title he was at the pennacle of the wrestling industry. He was a star. He made it. Hailed as a man who would do anything to win (which he would) with an extraordinary amount of willpower (which he posessed). But in a feud with Graphic Violence his mind slowly recoiled back into his schizophrenia, casting a shadow over the rest of the career. He would travel to a few more organizations before his disease overwhelmed him. He had spent the majority of the last three years of his life in a mental institution. With his disease back under control and at the age of 30, his intent is to rise back up the ranks of the wrestling world. With his skill still seemingly present, who is truly to deny him? Other Feds OSW, PWC, PIW, j0lt Other Fed Titles OSW International Champion, OSW World Champion, PWC Hardcore Champion (self-awarded), PIW Hardcore Champion Wrestling Style High Risk/Whatever It Takes Finisher Move Truth or Consequences Finisher Description Ace Crusher/Stone Cold Stunner Finisher Setup Move Black Mist Setup Description Rhett spits black mist out of his mouth into the face/eye area of his opponent Special Moves Fuck Yourself - A chairshot over the head of the opponent followed by a baseball swing with the chair to the skull on the way down. -- reserved for No DQ and similar gimmick matches. Other Moves Moonsault Dropkick Various Planchas Suicide Dive Anything he can do with a steel chair, he will do. DDT Dragon Sleeper Tarantula Hold Russian Leg Sweep Inverted Russian Leg Sweep Swinging Neckbreaker Baseball slide kick out of the ring. Match Psychology The fact of the matter is, Rhett rarely gives up. It is something deep inside him where he feels he has more willpower than anyone else on the planet. Most often than not, the referee will stop the match and give the victory to Rhett's opponent before he actually taps. Also, he will put his body through anything and everything to be the victor. That's why his wrestling style also shows "Whatever It Takes". If the only way Rhett can win is by launching himself into a brickwall from a cannon, he'll do it. Suicidal? He always has been. It also makes a lot of his matches just straight up spot fests. In addition, he will use the black mist spit to the eyes "technique" in every match he's in. And ALMOST always it's followed by Truth or Consequences. And as far as that move is concerned, he'll bust it out anywhere. Outside the ring over the announcers table, off the top rope, or just plain in the center of the ring. Doesn't matter. Ring Entrances No entrance is really the same.
