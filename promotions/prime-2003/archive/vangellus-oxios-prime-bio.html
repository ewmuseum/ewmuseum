Vangellus Oxios Biography Nicknames I like the classics. 'Sky High Oxios' and 'The Whole Freaking SHO' have done well for me. Hometown I'll claim my actual hometown of [detail withheld]. Canuckizzle in da hizzizzle, bitches! Birthday August 12, 1979. I won't give you my age, let you figure it out. Reading is fundamental, but so is math. Height A tall and healthy six feet four inches. I dwarf the entire population of New Zealand. Take THAT, Middle Earth! Weight Round and about probably 200 pounds maybe. Could be off by 5 pounds in either direction. Fed Titles Tag Team Titles. Twice. And it only took two pay-per-view appearances. Take THAT, Los Diablos! Fed Awards Most consecutive victories via knockout over kayfabe. Biography Vangellus Oxios was a nerd when he was younger. Well, not necessarily a nerd, since he wasn't the greatest in the acedemics department. More just geekish looking. He was tall and gangly, didn't really look all that good, and was way too goofy for his own good. That didn't help anything. When he graduated high school, he wanted to make a fresh start for himself. He left home and took fighting training. Boxing, kickboxing, marginal MMA... though he was still rather lanky, he made it work for him. What he would learn was that his entertaining brand of humor would delight the fans and frustrate his opponents. So he would go on to amass a respectable winning record in stand-up striking contests. He avoided grappling contests since that was not his forte. By becoming a public figure, he gained himself a great number of endorsement deals. He was quickly becoming one of the most marketable men in the world, and he had hundreds of products with his name and/or face attached to them. Seeking a new challenge, Oxios entered the world of professional wrestling early in 2004. Seeing it as a growing market, one where his face was not as well known, he wanted to jump in at the ground floor. Though he was some weeks late, he still made it 'early' in the history of Rush Pro Wrestling in Charlotte, North Carolina. Vangellus Oxios would debut against El Diablo in a #1 contendership match to the Heavyweight title. Although he didn't win, he still entertained and showed off his own special brand of combat. As a gimmick, he would list the names of moves based on product endorsements, and perform the moves related to those names throughout the course of a match. This would continue for a little while. Oxios had a losing record to start in RPW, but never lost his stride or his smile. And the fans had only good things to say about him. Even if he wasn't winning matches, he was still consistently one of the highlights of the show with his antics. Eventually, he would begin to amass wins, defeating the likes of Chris Storm, Hessian, Chris Collins, and so forth. His hard work paid off with a title win, capturing the Eclipse Title from indy sensation Gideon Poe in a three-way match against Aimz. The federation would close prior to Vangellus losing the title. Van took a year off from wrestling, during which time no one really knows what happened to him. But when he returned to wrestling along with Gabriel Brown (the former Invincible Gabe of RPW) to form a tag team, everyone knew that they were going to be in for a treat. Though Oxios's partner has changed, his dedication to the team has not, and he has reaffirmed it by capturing the Tag Team Titles twice, and vowing to revitalize the division along with Scott 'Rampage' Matthews. In addition he will now branch into singles wrestling after a tour in Japan that has seen an increase in his skill. What's changed? His technique. What's remaining the same? His fun-loving personality and his media-whoring. Oxios was always one to play to the almighty dollar, even if he doesn't come across as particularly greedy on-camera. To that end, he endorses hundreds of products the world over, and incorporates their names into his movelist. Other Feds RPW, bitches! Other Fed Awards A little thing called the RPW Eclipse Title. Ever heard of it? Wrestling Style Okay, I've gone through a little renovations in my repertoire since my time in Japan. I was always a great standing striker (really good with kicks and knocking peeps the hell out with punches). Add to that some high-flying excitement and some decent escape and reversal skill and you've got one hot talent in PRIME. Sorry, THE hot talent. Sorry, Scott. Finisher Move The Samuel L. Other Moves --FINISHER-- The Samuel L. Jackson: Super-stiff Spinning Backfist (In the front or the back) --SETUPS-- Manhattan Drop // Atomic Drop Regular Moves Kicks to the legs (immobilize opponents) Body punches (knock the wind from enemies) Palm Strikes (disorient opponents) Running Low Dropkick to the face (seated opponent) Rapid Double Stomps (face-up opponent) Signature Moves The Power of 4: (Named for the Schick Quattro razor) -- Back Kick to the gut >> Soccer Kick to the chest >> Roundhouse Kick >> Spinning Enzuigiri Black and Decker: (Named for the hardware company) -- Eye poke >> Haymaker >> High Uppercut Apple iGKO: ('I Got Knocked Out'; Named for Apple's iPod line) -- Superkick to the throat of male opponents; the 'adam's apple' Sunny Knee D: (Named for a citrus juice) -- Standing Shooting Star knees to the midsection Atkins Crash: (Named after the diet) -- With the opponent trapped in an upside-down facelock, Vangellus jumps up and throws his near leg over the face. He falls down with his groin (essentially his 'meat') on the victim's face, the weight going to crash the skull into the mat. He'll hook a leg and pin with it, grinding into the poor soul's face. Oxios has sworn never to perform this move on Scott Matthews under penalty of stabbing. Good Hands Plex: (Named after All-State Insurance) -- Oxios 'hugs' his opponent from behind, trapping the arms at the victim's side before arching back to perform the bridging German Suplex pin. No more damaging than a regular German but harder to escape the pin. Spicoli's Delight: (Named for the malt liquor product 'Colt 45') -- Vangellus lifts his opponent face-up onto his shoulder and falls to his knees with a Canadian Backbreaker Drop. Following this, he slumps the body forward and leans into a Backslide pin. Lite Brite Holiday: (Named after Hasbro children's toy) -- Oxios chains together a series of Northern Lights Suplexes, ending with a bridged pinning version. X-Boxed: (Named for the home console entertainment system) -- Vangellus stands to his opponent's side, facing in the opposite direction. Crossing the arms over the top of the chest, Oxios uses the trapped arms to assist in leveraging over his victim with a cross-armed STO. Touch The Sky: (Named for spiritualsky.com, a company that sells organic therapy products) -- One of Van's more acrobatic moves, it's an across the ring Springboard Diving Headbutt. Max Factor: (Named for the cosmetic line... which he apparently endorses despite it not being for males) -- Charging straight toward his enemy, he unleashes a move that's seen very much in 'tricking', but not in wrestling. Jumping into the air with a rapid spin, he goes 540 degrees before lashing out his leg with a Spinning Heel Kick. Special Notes -- Oxios is an expert kickboxer, capable of knocking out opponents simply with strong striking attacks. -- Possessing a thicker skull and membrane around his grey matter, Vangellus Oxios is able to no sell any attack to his skull. Attacks at the nose and chin affect him normally, though. -- Countering to the size of the skull, Oxios's neck is quite tender. Attacks at the neck are his weakness. -- While not the greatest catch wrestler in PRIME, Vangellus has developed an uncanny knack for escaping maneuvers used against him by his opponents. Most likely this is due to his weak neck, which can cause him to be pinned even if his opponent has not used a finisher. -- At nearly all times in a match, Oxios will be playing around. He's a very capable athlete, but still a comedy wrestler. If forced into a situation where he has to 'man up,' though, he's got the ring skill to take care of himself. Entrance Music I decided to go with something simple and instrumental. 'Dialogue with the Stars' by In Flames. No lyrics, bite me if you wanna sing along to it. I really wouldn't be listening to you anyway, honestly. And I say that because I love you.
