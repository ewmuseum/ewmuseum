Dani Furher and Hoyt Williams at ReVolution 61 (4/20/05). Defeated after 95 days. 10. Brandon Youngblood d. Nova at Colossus II (7/24/05). Stripped after 129 days.

Brandon Youngblood was fired from the company in November of 2005 and was stripped of the 5-Star Championship. At this time, the title was shelved temporarily, till being ‘re-debuted’ by Danny Ferguson, who laid claim to the championship prior to the Culture Shock PPV in winter of 2006.

11. Danny Ferguson d. Kyle Lamen at Culture Shock to officially be crowned champion (2/19/06).

With Ferguson’s win, the A-List claimed full possession of the title, sharing the glory and responsibility of title defenses, until Chandler Tsonda assumed full claim to the championship.

12. Jonathon Winters d. Chandler Tsonda at ReVolution 108 (10/13/06). Defeated after 236 days (as The A-List). 13. Tony Gamble d. Jonathon Winters and Chandler Tsonda at The Great American Nightmare (10/29/06).
Defeated after 16 days. 14. Sun Tzu d. Tony Gamble at ReVolution 117 (1/24/07). Defeated after 78 days. 15. Brandon Youngblood (2) d. Sun Tzu at ReVolution 124 (4/12/07). Defeated after 24 days. 16. Easton Hall at Colossus IV (8/5/07). Defeated after 73 days. 19. Jonathon Winters (2) d. Jason Snow at ReVolution 142 (10/17/07). Defeated after 18 days. 20. Brandon Youngblood (3) d. Jonathon Winters at The Great American Nightmare (11/4/07). Stripped after 87 days.

Brandon Youngblood was injured prior to going live on-air for ReVolution 150. Unable to defend his title, he was stripped of the 5-Star championship for the second time in his career. The title was then put up for grabs in a match featuring Tyler Rayne (fans choice) and Nitz Donnelly (winner of a qualifying match).
