Joey Melton Biography Nicknames The True Sound of the Ocean, the Vegas Merlin, Mr. Lindsay Troy Hometown New York, New York Birthday August 9, 1963 Height 6'1 Weight 218 Biography Born to a psychotic mother, and fathered by a traveling 'World's Tallest Man' carnie, opportunity was non-existent for Melton as a youth. Living in the slums of New York, Joey saw the worst in human nature and was forced to become a con artist to survive. Despite his surroundings, the boy blessed with staggering good looks, possessed an abundance of charm. An introduction that should have crippled Joey in the world, instead, fueled his desire to become one of the elite. At age 16, Melton legally separated from his mother, and married an aging real estate tycoon. Five years later, the family business declared bankruptcy, and his wife Alison left him in the middle of the night, never to be heard from again. Joey brought on a managing partner, who helped return the business to major profitability, but Melton sought to make his own mark in the world. A fan of professional wrestling, as it mirrored the carnival atmosphere of his upbringing, Joey spent the next two years training in fantasy wrestling camps, and working behind the scenes for upstate promotions. A promising young talent in 1988, Joey cut his teeth in fly-by-night promotions along the Atlantic Coast, booked wherever he’d be taken in. At 6’1, and just over two hundred pounds, Melton was a far cry from the steroid creations that were selling out arenas all over the world. In the late spring of ’88, he became the first World Champion of a Greensboro, NC based promotion called the CSWA. He hoped it’d be a quick stay, one that’d arm him with auditions for bigger companies. But, fate wanted Melton to stay in Greensboro. Over the next ten years the CSWA would become one of the biggest wrestling companies in the world, turning the men and women who started with the company into instant millionaires. By the late 1990’s, Melton had accomplished his dream of distancing himself from any remnants of his childhood life. Joey was one of the biggest names in the business, had millions at his fingertips, through wrestling and real estate ventures, but had succumbed to celebrity, and overnight success. A drug addict, by the turn of the millennium, Melton had burned most of his bridges in the States, and could only find work in Japan. While he’d befriend his best mate, 3-foot Adrian Evans (aka Little Voltron), on the Island, Melton was miserable, and longed to clean the smudges off his name, and become relevant again back home. One night in 2002 after a near fatal overdose, Melton desperate, and wasted, verbally assaulted fellow WORLD (the Japanese promotion employing him)LEAGUE worker Lindsay Troy at a Hotel bar. Not fully embarrassed, Joey threw up in Troy’s lap as she threatened to castrate him. Despite Melton’s boorish behavior, Lindsay was privy to previous interventions staged by some members of the WORLD LEAGUE crew on Melton’s behalf, and saw it in her heart to sober Joey up for the weekend. Once sober, she’d give him the choice. Rehab, or assault charges. The weekend was magical for both parties. A misogynist, Joey wasn’t used to having a woman own him by the balls. Apologetic, and with no expectations, he unloaded on Troy about his fears, his disappointment in life, and wanting to come back the right way. The weekend broke with both charmed, but horrified by the pretenses in which they got to know each other. As Troy flew back to America for work, Joey dedicated himself to cleaning up. And his newfound dedication would be tested early on. His ex-wife returned from the clear blue sky, waging a court battle that froze Joey’s assets and left him virtually penniless, after having wasted much of his wealth through the wrestling business over the years. Troy and Melton would meet again a year later in the NFW, Melton’s big ticket back to America and relevancy. Though in a committed relationship and repulsed by the arrogance clarity brought out of Melton, Lindsay agreed to manage Joey in the NFW mostly out of pity, and joy over having a man who once billed himself as the 'Sexual All-American' under her foot and at her mercy. Once her name was on the dotted line, Joey moved in to her Tampa estate: Guest bedroom, mind you. Over the last three years, they’ve proven opposites still attract. Melton won his legal battles, his battle with addiction, and Troy’s heart. Engaged in the summer of 2005, PRIME will be the first team Melton’s teamed in-ring with Troy since working together first in THE WORLD LEAGUE. Other Feds CSWA, fWo, NFW, EPW, Assorted Carnival Cruises throughout the year, and once he main evented in the Land Before Time before a wayward comet made transportation to the next town moot. Other Fed Awards First CSWA World Heavyweight Champion (3), CSWA UNIFIED World Heavyweight Champion (2), ULTRATITLE Winner (3, holds record for most times won), CSWA United States Champion, CSWA National Champion (4), CSWA IC Champion (7), CSWA Presidential Champion, CSWA Greensboro Champion, CSWA World Junior Champion (who knows), CSWA Tag Team Champion (3), ABWA Superheavyweight Champion, SUWF World Champion, PCWA World Champion, NFW Season 1 ULTRATITLE Final Four, EPW Tag Team Championship (with Cameron Cruise as The Cameron Cruise Project), ENN Tag Team of the Year (2004: The Cameron Cruise Project) Wrestling Style Mat Technician Finisher Move The Figure Four Finisher Description Come on, who hasn't applied this to someone before? First you knock said person to the ground. Then, you gently grab either their right or left leg, your discretion. Step over the raised leg, and turn 180 degrees. Raise free leg, then place bent leg of opponent over knee area of free leg, and fall down. Once to the mat, with your own leg, put pressure on hold. Repeat if necessary. I.E. until opponent passes out or says 'Uncle.' Finisher Setup Move He can strike from anywhere! At anytime! But, yeah probably works best if the opponent is backfirst on the mat. Just sayin'. Other Moves Trademark Move(s): Knife edge chops (STIFF!), Slingshot suplex, Bulldog, DDT, Nut shot, Dropkick, Elbow drop, Piledriver, Boston crab, STF, Sleeper, Double Chicken Wing, Brainbuster, Camel Clutch, Full Nelson, Adominal Stretch, Step-over Toe Hold, Backbreaker, Ref bump. Entrance Music 'Love is a Battlefield' -- Pat Benatar Ring Entrances Melton is pretty old-school. Basic color tights. Knee pads and boots. Fire, Water, Rain. Whatever you wanna do with the pyro he'll enjoy, so long as he's not set on fire or blinded by a laser. He's a Showman. He loves the moment. The world's his stage.
