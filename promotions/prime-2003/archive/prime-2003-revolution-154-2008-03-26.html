ReVolution 154 ---- Be The Change You Wish To See In The World We fade in to a room buried amongst the hallways and utility areas of the American Airlines Arena in Dallas. It's a makeshift office, the same layout of every makeshift office that PRIME has had in every arena on every tour. The same desk they always drag out of the truck. The same PRIME backdrop that hangs on the far wall. Everything is the same. However, a few notable accoutrements are missing. The fake exotic plants that Blaine Blair always liked are missing. The television and Nintendo Wii that Lisa Tyler kept as a personal effect is missing. Blaine and Lisa had been, along with every file, video and secretary, shipped to Chicago. Following the relocation of company headquarters, the new owners - now serving as PRIME's Board of Directors - hired Blaine and Lisa to office jobs, positions where your desk isn't the dinged-up mahogany monstrosity that gets loaded on and off the same truck in each new city. For their service and loyalty to PRIME, the former intern and former mic jockey were dragged up to corporate ladder, away from the rigors of the touring schedule. Gone with them were all the personal trinkets and bobbles that claimed this makeshift office as theirs. Dark, lonely and sterile, the room now only has one thing that makes it stand out. Faith Rodriguez, breezing through a copy of the night's program and schedule, sits patiently in one of the chairs opposite the business end of that familiar desk. She checks her watch and continues reading flipping to the next page as she lets out a loose sigh. She had been waiting too long. Faith Rodriguez: (muttering) Why does that little c*nt always get the good promos? You've played with enough girls, Rayne...maybe you need to meet a woman... Before she has time to check the time again, the door to the room swings open. Faith stands up abruptly, tossing the program onto the desk - presumably where she originally grabbed it from - and straightening out her skirt. She turns toward the open door. A man in his early-to-mid-thirties stands at the door, looking slightly confused. He scratches the back of his head, making sure not to mess up any of his meticulously-tussled blonde hair. It took the stylist a long time to make him look this disshevled, and he's not going to ruin that. Sporting a black suit with a tiger-striped orange tie, he takes a look at the name on the door - C.P. Cantrell, Executive Producer - and then takes a look at Faith, who was walking towards him with her hand outstretched. Man: Excuse me, are you C.P.? Faith Rodriguez: I- huh? She stops somewhat short, still leaving her hand hanging out there, so the man shakes it absently and walks into the room. Man: C.P. Cantrell, the new executive producer of ReVolution? Is that you? Man: That's funny, 'cause I did, too, but then I showed up at my office and you're here. I thought maybe my whole life until now was a lie. She needed to get things off on the right foot; that's why she'd waited in his office to begin with. Faith Rodriguez: Sorry, I just...I didn't want to miss my opportunity to welcome you to PRIME. You never know when that next call will come, after all. C.P. Cantrell: Thanks. If you're here to take a drink order, I'm all set. Still shaking as she retreats from the room, Faith can't help but break into tears. The new Executive Producer looks up past his program as she disappears through the doorway. He keeps his eyes on the entrance - and his new nameplate - for a few seconds longer, lost in thought. Maybe the emotional strain of his new position was now becoming clear. Maybe he is sensing that he just made the first of many difficult decisions that will cross his desk here in PRIME. Maybe he feels a little bad for the lady. Or maybe he just thinks they could use some more space after the second c-break. C.P. clicks his pen again and goes to work marking up the schedule further. He had a long night ahead, and if this show knew how to fix itself, he wouldn't even be here in this makeshift office behind some dinged up mahogany desk. He was the agent of change.
