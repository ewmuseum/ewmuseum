The Guardians Biography Biography DOMINIC: Dominic grew up without his father who left before he was born. His up bringing was rough living in Detroit, MI. His mother was rarely ever home forcing him to fend for himself at the age of eight when he learned to cook, clean, iron his clothes, and babysat himself. Dominic almost always got straight A’s in school, had a great outlook on life. He had dreams of going to law school and getting him and his mother out of the shit hole part of the city they lived in. Sadly when Dominic turned 13 his mother left him for good forcing Devereaux into a foster home. Dominic’s outlook on life changed from that moment on. He still got straight A’s through high school but every chance he got he decided to give teachers, students, and anyone else attitude. The school systems felt it would not benefit any parties involved to kick him out because of his exemplary grades. Dominic graduated early from high school and at the age of 17 he joined the United States Army. At first he had a tough time adjusting to the life style and his Drill Instructors would get on him constantly because of his attitude. Over time he grew more in tune with the style of living although that anger from being left behind so often built and built. He didn’t see the good in people, only waited until the people around him would turn their backs on him. A few years into his military career he took it a step up and moved into Special Forces, which is where he began learning all forms of fighting styles. After six years in the service he came out, moving straight into law enforcement. Just like in high school when he did his job thoroughly well but had a bad temper, he had the same problems in the force. His cop work was incredible but his temper could sometimes get in the way, until the day he was kicked off the force for reasons that were never released to the public. Knowing things would never work for him in a normal work environment so he got into the private investigator job. It was through this work, while going for a drug bust where he met his younger brother Stephen Devereaux, which he knew nothing about. Over the last few months the brothers have done their best to get closer, even deciding to get into the wrestling game as a tag team. The brothers are almost completely different in their views of others and what they consider justice. Stephen is more of a person who believe in the good in people and let the system get justice while Dominic takes a darker approach, thinking you have to take matters into your own hands … he is the judge, jury, and executioner. Today they are still learning each other, Dominic is still learning who he is and understanding and controlling that hatred that is built up inside. While he may have his heart in the right place, always wanting justice, his methods of getting to it may not be the best way to do it (or most legal).
