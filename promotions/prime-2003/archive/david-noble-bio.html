Other Feds NFW, fWo Other Fed Titles None Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style Innovative High-Flyer Finisher Move The Noble Ending Finisher Description Shiranui (Springboard backflip three-quarter facelock falling reverse DDT) Other Moves Matchwriting Aide Looks: David Noble is kind of a loner in his appearance. He's extremely lanky, having been made fun of his entire life as he has long arms, long legs, and long fingers and toes. He goes with a standard buzz cut as he enjoys having his hair very short. He has dark brown eyes though from time to time, he likes to put contacts in that change his eye color. Personality: David is very much a loner, to himself. He doesn't really interact with people backstage and keeps to himself. Out in the ring, he doesn't play up to the crowd very much. It's not to say he isn't charasmatic, but he just doesn't trust people and doesn't care to interact with them. Ring Attire: David wears a pair of black and white tights. On the outside of his left leg is his first name, David, with the letters on the top of each other like this: D A V I D While on the outside of his right leg it says Noble, in the same fashion. He doesn't wear any kind of shirt or anything. He also wears traditional wrestling boots and tapes up his fingers and wrist. Tactics/Style: David is a relatively new wrestler still learning the sport. From when he was young, David was always fast, and never scared to take a big risk, so when he started wrestling, he really took to being a high-flyer and putting his body on the line. He continues to learn though and knows that he needs to learn other styles to be effective since he can't fly forever. Strengths: Three strengths for David would have to be the fact that he's young. He's still learning and he loves to learn any and everything he can inside of the ring. He's also very much a thinker. He doesn't rush into any kind of situation and chooses his words very carefully. Finally, his other major strength would have to be his love for the sport. He loves, drinks, eats, and bleeds wrestling. Weaknessess: Three weaknesses would have to be the fact that he's inexperienced. He's never really wrestled before and is still learning the ropes. A veteran of the ring could abuse him pretty badly inside of that ring. Another weakness for him is that he's not very strong. He's a skinny kid who just doesn't have a lot of muscle to him. His final weakness would have to be the fact that while he's a thinker, he still wears his heart on his sleeve.
