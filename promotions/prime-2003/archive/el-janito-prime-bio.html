El Janito Biography Nicknames None. Hometown Beefville: City of the Annoyed, USA (may or may not exist) Birthday June 8th, 1977 Height 5'7 Weight 159 Fed Titles None, and we can all thank God for that. Fed Awards None. Biography Beef's tyranical reign over the other members of Mega Job started long ago when he partnered up with El Janito through some weird "rite of passage" involving a firework calling itself the "Goddess of Wrestling" and Jesus Christ, christening both himself and El Janito as Mega Job, a crime fighting unit. However, as time went on, Janito, Beef, and later Steve lost interest in crime fighting and instead became a camera crew. The members of Mega Job have a long-standing history with the Codemaster dating all the way back to 2004 when the Codemaster used them as his personal interview crew (citing that he dislikes both Kristen Jacobs AND Geoff Green). They were invited into the Blue Rogues to serve as a personal camera crew, though it's heavilly implied that Codemaster only invited them to serve as "cannon fodder". Other Feds The Asylum, DWF, IWO, IML, fWo, fWEo Other Fed Awards the Asylum Tag Champion (w/ Beef), the Asylum United Kingdom Champion, IML World Tag Team Champion (w/ Beef) Wrestling Style LUCHA LIBRE~! ....er, I mean, brawling disguised as lucha libre. Finisher Move The Five Tequila Frog Splash Finisher Description A frog splash. Finisher Setup Move Suplex Setup Description Janito kicks the opponent in the gut. Special Moves See move set. Other Moves El Janito s a comedy wrestler, through and through. When he's actually motivated, though, he can do some crazy high flying stunts like other Mexican wrestlers do. Although he sometimes calls himself a "lucha libre star", Janito's style is more of a mix of brawling and high flying. He's about the size of a Rey Mysterio, though, so his brawling is far weaker than Beef's. Janito usually takes absolute beatings throughout matches, hardly ever getting offense in until his last burst of energy allows him to make the hot tag to Beef. While Janito has been known to job to the weakest of moves (once jobbing to getting punched in the face by Ian Nackedy and Aaron Andrews), if he's really motivated, he has been known to survive some of the worst punishment in humanity long enough to tag Beef. Janito is a lord at selling, and he'll sell the right body part at the right time and he'll also oversell your finishers like he was just shot in the face with a shotgun, just because he can. Janito tends to talk to his opponents during matches, like Beef does. This usually earns him more beatings. --- Regular Moves Janito is more apt to just throw whatever he can at the opponent and say "to hell" with psychology. He's kind of small, so he doesn't do anything requiring lifting the opponent. - Punch - Kick - Dropkick - DDT - Tope suicida - Tope con hilo - Missile dropkick - Springboard dropkick - Springboard clothesline - Flying elbow drop - Hurricanrana - Springboard hurricanrana - Flying DDT - Fameasser Finisher - Five Tequila Frog Splash It's just a frog splash, not even really a five star one. Janito is kinda small, so he needs another big impact, like Beef's "Beef Grinder", for this to get a three. Otherwise, the opponent might still kick out.
