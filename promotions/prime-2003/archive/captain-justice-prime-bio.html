Captain Justice Biography Wrestler Name Captain Justice Nicknames Cap J., The Americanimal, The Heroic Hoss, The Hoss of a New Millenium, The Future of Sports Entertainment, The New Face of PRIME. Hometown The Great City of Gothametropolis Birthday September 16th, 1983 (Age: 24) Height 6'5 Weight 280 lbs. Fed Titles Coming soon, citizens! Fed Awards ^ Biography Brief Bio (real): -Soren is the oldest son of successful wrestler/actor Earl Knox. Earl died seven years ago in an accident on the set of one of his movies. -Soren has seen relatively little success in the wrestling business, but even in the wake of his newfound contract, he doesn't seem to care as he enjoys his strange and unique character. Brief Bio (as Justice): - Soren Knox was just a mild-mannered "reporter", before he gained his "powers" by drinking a "radioactive energy drink", thus turning him into Captain Justice. - Captain Justice's enemies are those who would dare attack the innocent! With his mighty "Arms of Justice", Captain Justice smites those evil-doers! - Captain Justice's ill-defined powers all have to do with his Arms of Justice. With them, he can bash people's faces in, tear phone books in half, and easily have his arms compared to skillets and other ordinary household objects! Watch as he lumbers around the ring! Marvel as he hits a generic big boots! Gasp as he delivers the chokeslam! He is Captain Justice! The Hoss of a New Millennium! -Managed by Mr. Silver, Chairman of PRIME, the dimwitted hero of justice is completely unaware he's being used for Mr. Silver's personal whims. Personality (real): - Soren is laidback about the business and he doesn't have an obsession with wrestling driving him quite like his manager does. He enjoys wrestling because of the fans, and not for the competition. - Of his three brothers (Steve and Simon), Soren is the most successful with a personal life, as he has a fiance: Rachel. He does not rub this into the faces of either of his brothers. -The obsession of Sonny in regards to professional wrestling manages to make him worry since his brother, Steve, is the same way. Personality (as Justice): - Captain Justice is a strong and brave individual! He smashes evil with his Arms of Justice! - That being said, Captain Justice is one gigantic simpleton who sees things in a totally black and white point of view. He believes it okay to harm women if they do something bad, he doesn't care about (or, more specifically, he's completely oblivious towards) the motives of those he smashes, weapons are fair game, and all in all, his behaviour lends him to not being the superhero he strives to be. Other Feds Yet again, coming soon! Other Fed Titles ^ Other Fed Awards ^ Manager Mr. Silver, Sports Entertainment Liasion of PRIME Wrestling Style Sports Entertainment Powerhouse Finisher Move The Long Arm of The Law/The Grand Seal Finisher Description Kenta Kobashi's Burning Lariat/Cobra Clutch Finisher Setup Move The Washington Monument Setup Description Spear; alternate out-of-nowhere finishing move. Special Moves Lots of punchy-kicky-stompy goodness and flashy signature moves (see movelist). Other Moves Style Power with a side of power dabbled in power and for a nice change of pace, a little more power. Pound-for-pound, you will not find a stronger body in PRIME than the Future of Sports Entertainment himself. Captain Justice relies on awesome displays of power and gaudy pageantry as opposed to any sort of “mat wrestling” or what have you. The moves he can do are mainly devastating slam moves and simplistic grinding submissions watered down with a heavy dose of “Sports Entertainment” brawling. While he knows some basic holds, what he does know is largely depreciated in favor of a more flashy style. Strategy In any regular match, Captain Justice makes with the big-time punchy-kicky-stompy Hogan-like maneuvers. You won’t see him bust out any of his heavy-duty moves unless he can counter his opponent’s moves early into something or the opponent’s one of those people designed to make your opponents look good. You know… jabronis. When it comes down to the middle and later stages of the match, Captain Justice relies heavily on his signature moves. So heavily, in fact, that they can be reversed by an opponent that’s seen him wrestle a few times. However, CJ is a simpleton and he’s incredibly stubborn. He works under the school of thought that "If this doesn’t smash you up, then I’ll try THIS." This isn't to say that he's predictable, either. He can change up his moves when needed, but that's a rarity. During PPV or title matches, he’ll go big time and take out all the stops (even delving into his rare-to-almost-never-seen high-flying arsenal (we’re talking Undertaker-level no-hands dives and such to the outside.) Think of him as an Undertaker-style performer where he makes some of those signature moves special because you never see him busting them out anywhere else but on a major show/title match/something equally important. If I catch you trying to make him do any of these things on a regular show, I WILL kill you. (*glares at Craig*) Strengths -Strong, STRONG, STRONG. Pound for pound, the strongest man in PRIME today. He can be lifted up, but he can also lift anybody up that's currently on the roster. His debut within the Halo slamming around guys like Wade Elliott and Killean Sirrajin proved that. -He truly is a juggernaut. Not like the red guy from Marvel comics, but in a sense that once he gets going in the ring, his momentum is incredibly difficult to stop. A rare combination of youth, explosiveness, and speed makes his offense very deadly. -Like Wade Elliott, One-Hit Kills are hard to come by for the big man. It may take a succession of moves strung together in order to put him away. Not to say you couldn’t surprise him with some sort of flash pin out of nowhere, but taking him straight on is ill-advised. -For a powerhouse, Captain Justice is a very well-rounded athlete. He’s only got the equivalent of a few years wrestling experience under his belt, but he’s capable of more than he lets on, surprising opponents. (ex., UltraViolence when CJ busted out a No-Hands Plancha, shocking all INCLUDING Sonny.) Underestimating him at all is not only ill-advised, it’s just stupid. -While he is a “superhero” he’s of the “Punisher” type of hero who believes that any means necessary is the right way to go to get his point across, committing extreme acts of violence without a second thought. He believes weapons are okay and he will strike women who get in his way. Weaknesses -He calls out the names of his signature moves prior to doing them, so it gives adept opponents a perfect time to counter his big hit maneuvers. -While possessing a great amount of mobility for a mountain of muscle, you won’t hear anybody use the phrase “quicker than a hiccup” to describe him. Flyers/bump takers give him all sorts of trouble due to their speed and propensity to take to the skies. -Tunnel vision. He may be a gullible idiot, but he’s not stupid… wait. Anyway, he doesn’t fall at all to distractions from referees, people on the PRIME*View, and other such diversions, but he suffers from tunnel vision. While focused solely on his opponent, he doesn’t notice things going on around the outside like valets scheming shit off-stage or a ref not being in the vicinity to count the fall, so if your buddy comes along with a chair or that trap you’ve set for CJ has an opportunity to be used, he will fall victim to it. -Opponents (particularly, vets with a leg up) can set their watch to Captain Justice’s movements because his sole gameplan is straightforward, thus is very predictable. -Will pause to gloat/taunt/laugh/photo-op when he’s in control. Regular Moves Liberal doses of “Main Event” punching, kicking, and stomping Bionic Elbow Body Avalanche Vertical (Stalling) Suplex Back Suplex Scoop Slam Big Boot Military Press Slam (capable of doing this to people in the 300-pound mark a la Goldberg) Shoulder Thrusts Shoulder Block Signature Moves The Liberty Bellclap (Modified Mongolian Chop; balls up fists, crushes opponent's head in between said fists) Golden Gate Bridge (Rotating Torture Rack; capable of lifting up to 400+ pounds, submission finisher) The Washington Monument (The most vicious Spear you’ll ever see.) American Revolution (Airplane Spin into a Flapjack) The Shot Heard ‘Round the World (Zidian Zidane-esque lunging headbutt to face/chest of opponent; either stationary or done to opponent coming off the ropes) Ten-Gun Salute (Series of ten STIFF Open-palmed chops in the corner) The Great American Drop (Samoan Drop) Fastball Special! (Running Turnbuckle Powerbomb) Holy Justice Slam (Over-the-Shoulder Powerslam) PPV/Title Match-only Spots Air America (No-Hands Plancha) Top Rope Splash Top Rope Moonsault Finishing Move The Long Arm of the Law (Kenta Kobashi’s Burning Lariat; palms opponent’s head and DECAPITATES them with a Lariat that can turn most folks inside out. Can also do a running variation akin to the Clothesline from Hell.) The Great Seal (Cobra Clutch Backbreaker, segued into a Cobra Clutch submission.)
