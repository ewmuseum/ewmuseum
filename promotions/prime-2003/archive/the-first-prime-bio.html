The First Biography Wrestler Name The First Nicknames The Outcast Hero, Painted-up Freak. Hometown Salem, Massachusetts Birthday 06/10/85 Height 6' Weight 200 lbs Biography An idiot kid who had a vision brought on by past life regression that led him and the person doing the session to believe he has the oldest soul in humanity. Has constructed a mythos around this belief and himself, and as such is The First, since he was the first soul to exist. Other Feds EPW, A1E Other Fed Titles EPW World Tag Champion (former) 2x UCW World Champion (Former) A1E Cyber Champion (Current) Manager Muse Wrestling Style High Flyer Finisher Move Cut The Thread/Slice of Death Finisher Description Front flip legdrop off the top rope/Fisherman's Suplex neckbreaker (Daffney's finisher in TNA) Finisher Setup Move Shining Wizard Setup Description a series of strikes or other moves gets the opponent to drop to one knee so they can be Shining Wizard'ed Special Moves Hand spring elbow into the corner and then after the opponent stumbles out of the corner, he’ll climb to the second rope and hit a shoulder block… Spits poison mist of various colors whenever it won’t get him DQ’ed… Whips opponent to the ropes and then Other Moves Pretty much anything that involves high risk. I am horrible with move-sets in general. I'm a WWE style "A few unique moves per guy and then a lot of punches, kicks and clotheslines" wrestling fan, so I won't begrudge you writing him doing anything, as long as it's not complex submissions. Entrance Music "To Die For" The Birthday Massacre Song Link http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=57BUcxEv_yM Ring Entrances "To Die For" hits and The First will come out from the curtain. He'll shout and yell at the crowd to get to their feet, and then he and Muse will make their way to the ring, high fiving as many fans as possible...Once at ringside he'll jump from the apron into the ring and land on the second rope, posing for the fans.
