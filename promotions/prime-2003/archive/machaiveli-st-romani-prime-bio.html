Machaiveli St. Romani Biography Nicknames The Good Shepherd - Master of the Arm Locks Hometown National Palace, Haiti Birthday May 2, 1979 Height 6 foot 3 inches Weight 236 lbs Fed Titles None. Fed Awards None. Biography "In an age where fans cheer the "Badass", Romani brings forth a breath of relief. Never, since the days of Rickey Steamboat, has their been a true loveable unhateable face…a guy the fans cheered because he was the pure definition of a good guy. He doesn’t curse, he doesn’t badmouth, he doesn’t downplay anyone, he tries to stick up for what’s right, he’s as friendly to the fans as you can get…and he gives the fans what they won’t each night inside of the ring, and that’s not just a well paced, story-telling match with the usual high risk. The funny thing about it, though…it’s not a gimmick at all." –Vangelus Olsig Disgruntled and Heartbroken, I watched as Rain droplets pelted the windowpanes, gouging thin canals alike. Thunder hummed softly through the velvet sky, setting the precedent for the night's mood. For this was the night in which the Sacar family learned the passing of their tier leader, my father Francois Sacar III. But as the water from the sky soiled the streets of Haiti, I felt a more passionate ire. My seventeen-year-old fist clinched, not in response to my father's passing...but unto the inheritance in which I was strained to partake. You see, my father was priest of a temple appropriately named 'The Tabernacle of Christ'. With his passing, I was obliged to inherit his estates, including the priesthood throne. My family was of high religion, so to watch their teened young heir ascend the throne brought forth a sense of passion. But all it brought me was Strife. Sixteen was the required age to enter Priesthood, so I was legally eligible, but the same could not be said of whether I was mentally eligible. For one year, I reigned and ruled the church, avoiding all controversy presenting itself and successfully leading the flock into a new era of faith. But then tragedy struck. Outcry for the sake of the children of Haiti was at an all time high. The corrupt government was cutting down education and forcing the children into labor at young ages. The same flock in which I'd successfully guided turned to me as their savior against the corrupt structure of government. I paced the floors, seeking a resolve to the issue. Nothing came....that is until I talked spiritually to my father. He led me to a sacred department in our home and introduced me to the Leopards Mask. The Leopards Mask was the mask my father wrestled with sixteen years before becoming a priest. He'd introduced me to a form of karate at an early age, so I was no stranger to the ring...but to sacrifice the throne of priesthood and don the mask was a risk I contemplated for quite sometime. But as I glanced outside of my bloodstained windows and witnessed the children, dying on the streets from exhaustion, the contemplation ceased. For I'd made up my mind. $100,000 was placed on the booker's table. I'd gotten the money from the church's finances, something I regretted having to do. That $100,000 dollars, though, placed me in the Annual King Haiti's Invitational Tournament. First round...success. Second round...squashed. Third round...repeated success. Fourth round... failure. The semi-finals brought forth defeat and a wasted $100,000 of the church's money. Upon learning of my contribution in such an act of violence, I was immediately excommunicated from the church and exiled from the National Palace. Thousands of fierce and venge-seeking Christians sought my very flesh. Dead or Alive posters bustled through the streets...even my immediate family turned their backs on me. So, seeking a way out of not only the controversy, but also out of death, I gave them what they wanted… I faked my death. They all believed it. With nothing but my father's Leopard Mask and a $5 bill, I managed to cross the border over into the United States. I legally changed my name to Machaivelli St. Romani, inspired by a Greek philosopher whom I've long admired. Finding work was near the brink of impossibility, but there was one thing I could always turn to... Professional Wrestling. Donning the Leopard mask in honor of my father and also to keep my identity hidden, I entered the independent scene, fancying a hybrid style of wrestling rarely known on the American soil. I trained at the AWWCWP wrestling dojo (trainees of such stars as AJ Styles, Mark Jindrak, etc.) to further sharpen my skills and traveled the country portraying them. I had no gimmick...just my uncanny recognition of my Lord and Savior. Apparently that was enough. I was known as the unhateable baby face. The fans cheered my success all through my matches and blew the roof off at the mere mention of my name. I was as pure of a face as you could get...and to this day I still maintain that status. I never sinned...I never partook in devious acts...I was the ultimate good guy. The modern day Rickey Steamboat. But as the Leopard Masked Super-Face... I sought a bigger prize... A bigger stage... I sought PRIME. Other Feds IWCP Kingsman championship | MCCW Southwest Championship Other Fed Awards IWCP Rising Star of the Year Wrestling Style Blended Culture (This is basically a mix of two diverse styles of wrestling, a strong technical style and a Lucha Libra style. One of his best-known attributes is pacing a match well, and what makes that attribute so grand is this blended mix. Unless otherwise notified, he will never charge directly into a match with high impact maneuvers, but instead will pick a designate a spot on his opponent and work on it, executing high impact signature moves at various points through the course of the match. He also seeks innovative maneuvers throughout the match, or moves you don’t see in everyday matches.) Finisher Move Leopards Claw Finisher Description As the master of the arm locks, it's only fitting that his finisher involve an arm lock in one form or another. This finishing maneuvar is a variation of the Crippler's Crossface where Romani applies a standing arm bar on the opponent before tackling him to the mat with that arm, placing the twisted arm in between his legs and applying the crossface. Simple...really. Finisher Setup Move Flatliner Setup Description Simple execution of the Flatliner, sometimes following up the maneuvar by rolling over into the Leopards Claw Special Moves The Master of the Arm Locks. He can and will use any and every arm lock in the book. It's automatic, really.
