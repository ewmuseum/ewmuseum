PRIME...Number One by Definition. ---- Tyler Rayne vs. Chainz A match between two of the most volatile forces in PRIME's history started out true to form, with Chainz getting a jump on Tyler Rayne before the bell. The Monster stormed from the back during Rayne's entrance and clocked him in the back of the head before shoving him into the steel stairs. Rayne stumbled around, leaning against the apron while Chainz slammed his 295 pound frame into The Underground Pimp. Bernie Roberts could do nothing but watch Chainz pummel Rayne on the outside. The match hadn't started yet and no way would he drop to the floor and try to reason with The Monster from Hell's Kitchen. Chainz put a series of elbows into Rayne's back before shoving him under the bottom rope and, officially, getting the match started. Once inside the ring, Chainz continued his assault on Rayne with a belly to belly side suplex. The Monster went for a quick cover, but Rayne pulled a shoulder out. Chainz decided to switch around and go for an armbar submission, but Rayne slithered out and got to his feet. Chainz got to his feet, but he was a bit too cocky in doing so and allowed Rayne to fire a set of knees into the forehead. Chainz stumbled back into the corner and was followed in by a dropsault. Rayne got to his feet just as Chainz came forward. The Underground Pimp dropped Chainz with a DDT, flipping himself in the air so his shins landed on Chainz' back. A taunting bow followed. Chainz didn't look too pleased with the show of disrespect. Rayne charged Chainz again, and The Monster was unable to fight off the charge. Rayne backed him into the corner, landing a strong knee to the forehead and a spinning back fist. Chainz shoved away, but Rayne landed a standing side kick followed by a running bulldog. Rayne went for a cover, but Chainz kicked out before three. Rayne didn't seem to mind inflicting more pain, rising to a standing position and landing a standing moonsault. Rayne quickly jumped back to his feet and followed through with a standing shooting star press for a cover. Again, Chainz kicked out before the three count. Rayne pulled Chainz up and went for an elbow, but The Monster countered with a drop toe hold and kicked Rayne in the back of his head. The Pimp was dazed, but Chainz didn't allow him a chance to shake the cobwebs loose. The Monster picked him from the canvas and slung him around in a German suplex. Rayne tried to escape, clubbing at the wrists, but Chainz wasn't in the mood to let Rayne and his free wheeling offense roam around the ring. Another German suplex put Rayne on his back/neck again. The Underground Pimp attempted another escape, but Chainz kept his grip tight and pulled Rayne back for the third consecutive suplex. He kept the body bridged and looked for a cover, but Rayne kicked out. Chainz pulled Rayne from the canvas and delivered a knee to the head. He followed up with another and then another for the set. Rayne backpedaled into the corner, twice swinging at air. Chainz landed a vicious uppercut and segued into a Death Valley Driver out of the corner. Once again, Rayne kicked out before three. Chainz grabbed Rayne by the left shoulder and hoisted him to a standing position. The Underground Pimp stood before Chainz for a few seconds while Chainz contemplated what would be next. Just as The Monster was about to land his signature spinebuster, Rayne dropped down and popped Chainz on the jaw with a jawbreaker. Chainz prevented himself from falling down, but his temper had been ignited and he wanted to finish Rayne. The Underground Pimp got a bit of a step on Chainz and was able to land the Un-fucking-believable flipping kick to the head. Adding an electric chair drop for good measure, Chainz was down on the canvas. Rayne went for the cover but did not get the win. At this stage of the match, both men were running on fumes. The winner would be the first person to land a finishing maneuver. Rayne beat Chainz to his feet and landed an elbow to the head. Chainz tried countering, but Rayne bobbed and weaved until he was able to land four more shots on the big man. Chainz was on spaghetti legs and Rayne knew he needed to capitalize on the chance. A whip into the ropes saw Chainz move the ring a few millimeters. The Underground Pimp ran, full head of steam, into the corner and went for the Foreplay. At the last split second, Chainz sidestepped, leaving Rayne to collide knees first with the turnbuckle. Before Rayne could even hit the deck, Chainz was already on the attack with a Half Nelson suplex. He went for the pinfall, but Rayne kicked out. Chainz pulled Rayne to his feet and went for the Chain Reaction, lifting Rayne into the air for the first powerbomb. The Monster had some difficulty pulling Rayne down, because The Underground Pimp locked on his Insult to Injury Triangle choke. Chainz, beast that he is, managed to stay standing for the first few seconds while Rayne had the hold on. Rayne tightened his legs and Chainz knew he was going to go down, so he pushed through and broke the Insult with the first half of the Chain Reaction. He pushed through a second time and completed the move, collapsing on top of Rayne to achieve the pinfall victory. Mills: Boda…Boda…can I get a word or two from you about what happened at the beginning of the show? The pissed off Legend stares down at Mills, scoffing and tries to walk past him. Mills: Chris, seriously, I’ve known you for a long time. What we all witnessed over the last month or so has effected you in a way that I can’t remember. But you’ve got to have some closure knowing that at UltraViolence, you’ll get to face your stalker finally. Boda puts his hand over his mouth, then strokes his goatee. Boda: Matt, you’re right, we’ve known each other for a long time. We started this company together many years ago. You’ve been there at my highest of highs…and now, this last month you’ve been there for my lowest of lows. I won’t lie to you, come UltraViolence, When my ‘stalker’ presents himself to me, there better be medical personnel standing by, because I will throw everything I know about this business out the window and go medieval on that poor bastard. Mills: So, you’re looking to hurt the person responsible then? Boda: What do you think? Now, listen, I’ve got to go. He rounds the corner and disappears from the camera's view. Mills: Well PRIME fans, it seems the mind games will finally come to an... Mills is interrupted by a loud crash and some screaming coming from inside Boda’s locker room. Mills turns the corner after Boda and jogs down the corridor towards his locker room. Matt reaches to open the door but is almost smacked in the face as it flies open. As Matt jumps back, Boda exits his locker room, with some baggage...and we’re not talking luggage. Boda is carrying a person clad all in black over his shoulders. Boda storms past Mills, who stares after him in shock. He looks into Boda's locker room, which has been completely ransacked. Red bullseyes have been spray painted onto the walls, one of which is unfinished. Down the hallway a door bangs open, which snaps Mills's head out of the room. He and the camera run after Boda and the mystery person. Eventually they reach the parking area where Boda has the trunk open. Mills: Chris, you can’t do that. Come on think about what you’re doing. Besides, you’ve got your stalker, let’s settle this now. Boda slams the trunk shut and turns to face Mills. Pounding from inside the car can be heard. Boda: That piece of shit said all would be solved at UltraViolence. So let it be. The pissed off giant punches the trunk lid. Boda: You hear that you piece of shit? You don’t know who you truly fucked with. You want to reveal yourself at the PPV? Fine. I’ll do it myself. You shouldn’t have gotten yourself caught. Matt tries to plead for Boda to think about what he’s doing, but it falls on deaf ears as Boda climbs into the driver’s seat and takes off out of the parking lot. As the tail lights fade to black, the camera catches a look of concern on Matt Mills's face. ---- The Stalwart Finals The Main Event of the evening had a number of different pros working in its favor. A member of the new guard, at least to those of Rage fandom, against one of the true Old Schoolers of PRIME. A clear cut babyface squaring off against one of the baddest, if not the baddest, heel in wrestling today. And the prospect of headlining a Pay-Per-View, a first for either man. The contest started off with a traditional collar and elbow lock-up with neither man gaining a sizable advantage. Rage would back Chainz a step and then Chainz would do the same to Rage. In each of their minds, they wanted to break and start over. They would let go right after the other would. After a few seconds, it became an issue of ego. Then, the lock-up became an issue of pride. Both men have easily ignitable tempers and now, they wanted to humiliate the other. Rage pushed in, reeling Chainz backwards. The Monster spun around and put Rage against the ropes. With so much on the line, both men knew a referee wouldn't be able to stop them. Rage and Chainz fell through the second rope, holding onto the collar and elbow while walking around the ring. Chainz looked for a kick to the body, but Rage kept enough distance to prevent him. Rage and Chainz kept the grip locked until reaching the announcers table. Richard bolted like a scared little child while Nick stepped to the side. Rage finally mustered enough strength to put Chainz on the table and unload with big rights and lefts. Chainz kicked Rage in the midsection and belted him with a right hand. He then scooped The Nicaraguan Nightmare into his arms and slammed him against the ring post. Chainz showed his raw strength by keeping Rage clutched and then slamming him again into the ring post. Rage was then tossed under the bottom rope. Chainz slid underneath and kept his focus on the back, dropping an elbow onto the kidneys. Rage tried to crawl away, get to the ropes, or find some other way of acquiring a respite, but Chainz wouldn't allow him. He pulled Rage back to center ring and hoisted him in the air, tilt a whirling him around into a back breaker. The Monster went for a cover, but Rage kicked out. Chainz continued to club the back, keeping Rage on his stomach with elbow, knee, and punch combinations. The Monster eventually decided to delve into his deep submission game, and wrenched on a Bow and Arrow. Rage grunted and groaned, looking for a way to free himself from the submission, but the strength of Chainz wasn't allowing The Nicaraguan Nightmare much room for escape. He wasn't getting much breathing room either. The Monster continued to wrench on the hold, but Rage refused to submit. After an almost Hurclean effort, Rage managed to maneuver over toward the ropes and grasp hold with his left wrist. Chainz was forced to break the hold, but not after doing substantial damage. The Monster kept on the attack, clubbing Rage and assaulting him all the way to a standing position. Rage fended Chainz off, delivering an elbow to the jaw, and landed a roaring elbow. The Nightmare knew Chainz would keep coming and succumbed to the 295 pound splash and overhead belly to belly suplex which followed. Chainz went for a cover, but Rage was able to kick out. Chainz put himself on the back of Rage and applied a camel clutch. Rage palmed the mat and desperately sought the bottom rope, but was unable to grab hold. Chainz kept the hold on for an additional half minute before dropping all of his weight down on the helpless Nightmare. Chainz applied a Half Nelson before pulling Rage up and then hoisted him in the air for a slam. Another cover, but another kickout. Rage's tournament life was still in tact. Chainz pulled Rage up and booted him in the midsection, looking to go for the finish. He'd spent too long already and two powerbombs on the back would be quite difficult for Rage to kick out of. He went to pull Rage up, but Rage refused to let go of the right leg. Chainz tried again, but Rage refused to relinquish. Chainz tried one more time, but Rage did the lifting by hoisting Chainz on his shoulder and throwing him back with a Samoan Drop. Desperate to keep his momentum, Rage grabbed hold of the leg and spun around, locking it with Sloan's left and falling backwards for a Figure Four. Now it was Sloan's turn to be threatened by submission. Rage pulled on the legs and applied all the pressure he could, but the back injuries from earlier plagued him. Chainz was able to get close to the ropes and reach back for a break. The Nightmare struggled to his feet, actually being beaten to a standing position by Chainz. The former Intense Champion clubbed Rage across the back and bounced off the ropes. Rage stumbled back a few steps into a reverse spear which put him on the canvas. Even someone attempting to hide the pain in their face couldn't do so after such a high impact maneuver. Rage was practically lying bait for The Monster, who hoisted Rage from the canvas and tried picking him up for the Chain Reaction. Rage couldn't block the move by grabbing Chainz leg, so instead he put his own leg under the bottom rope and resisted. Three pulls didn't get Chainz the desired result, so he went for a knee lift. Rage sprung from his position and managed to land an Alabama slam. Rage crawled over for a cover, but Sloan kicked out before the three count. Both men reached for the ropes to pull up and get back into the fight. Sloan charged first and landed a good right, but Rage countered. Sloan went back to the well another time, as did Rage. Rage looked to pull the ROAR out of his bag, but Sloan caught and then drove Rage into the turnbuckle. When both men came away from the buckle, Sloan tried to boot Rage in the midsection and hoist him up. He did manage to get him on the shoulders and almost drive him down, but Rage snuck rolled off Sloan's shoulders and connected with a ROAR. He went for the pinfall and got the victory, moving him into the Main Event of UltraViolence. Winner: Emilio Rage
