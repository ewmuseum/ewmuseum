ReVolution 121 Japan somewhere. ---- Extreme Times Call for Extreme Drama Dusk. The PTC Extreme Champion. He’s starting to feel the weight across his shoulders. By the minute, he’s gaining enemies from every corner. From PRIME. From PTC. As he steps out of his rental car he slings his black leather jacket back over his left shoulder while placing the Extreme Championship over his right shoulder. His eyes drop to the ground and with each step, he becomes more and more lost with his thoughts as he makes his way towards the door to the arena. However, as he reaches the door, he hears a voice come from behind him. “Well, if it isn’t the Extreme Champ.” The voice echoes in Dusk’s ears and he knew it immediately. Swiftly, he turns around and before him stands the former PTC Extreme Champion, but more importantly, the PRIME Universal Champion. Nova. Dusk’s bag drops from his shoulder in a hurry, readying himself. Nova: A little jumpy, don’t you think? Dusk: You seem to think that I don’t have a good reason to do so. Nova: Well, relax. You’re not important enough to warrant Deville risking his hair by hiding behind a dumpster, or Snow to lug his statue all the way out here for you to pay forced homage to. No, right now, in this parking lot, it’s just me and you. Dusk moves up to Nova, wanting the Universal Champion to look him dead in the eyes. Dusk: Well then, champ, I guess you’ve got my full attention. Nova: Glad to hear it. The reasons I’m waiting out here for you tonight are two-fold: first, to inflate your ego by congratulating you on winning that strap…and second, to deflate it by explaining to you that… Dusk: Save the bullshit for someone else, Nova. I know what you’re going to do. Try and downplay that I beat you for the title. Well, just to remind you, I not only beat you, but you can add Karina Wolfenden, Jake Hix, Easton Hall, and Jonathan Winters to that list. You might try to convince the fans that it’s all good. Hell, even do the same to your boys in the back. But, to me, I know the truth. I see the truth in your eyes because you’re scared by it. Nova’s eyes move away from Dusk, but Dusk snaps right back at him, making Nova look him right in the eyes. Dusk: Look at me. You had the balls to walk up to me out here, so have the balls to look the truth right in the face. It scares you losing to me, because you now have to wonder if you’re going to lose to your new friend, Tony Gamble, at Culture Shock. The thought that keeps running through your head is that you lost to me, so why can’t you lose to Gamble? Well, guess what Nova, you’re not foolin’ me. Because on any given night, you could lose to somebody and just lose it all. The Risen Star sparks a cigarette and makes no effort to blow the smoke away from the Extreme Champion’s face. Nova: Now take a breath and you listen to me for a second, Dr. Freud. Do you think, for one moment, that I give two shits about that title you’re parading around? I cashed in my rematch so I could be the one to shut Archie Mac down. He didn’t show. I wanted Youngblood. He backed out via bouncing on yet another company. I wanted to show that my original reign, the one they forced me to abdicate, wasn’t a fluke. The cigarette’s cherry lights up, and smoke drifts out of Nova’s nostrils. Ooh, I like that. “Nova’s nostrils.” Nova: But putting my ass on the line to defend it? WAKE UP, LITTLE SOLDIER. Your precious belt is defunct. They’re canning the division. Why would I go out of my way to defend that? So I can compete in the Unified Title match? Big. Fucking. Whoop. Everyone with a brain knows that PRIME’s Universal Title means more than any PTC belt. I’m King of the biggest show in town. Why be Emperor of the “other guys?” Dusk: Then it’s going to be an ugly sight when the only thing you care about is no longer in your hands. Because, if you ask me, Gamble has got your number and you don’t even know it yet. Nova: If Gamble could count, that might worry me. Silence. The two look at each other, burning a hole through one another. Dusk knows though that Nova didn’t stop him about winning the Extreme Championship. He stopped him for a completely different reason. Nova: I’ve been meaning to ask you…how did Jason Snow’s snakeskin taste? Dusk just stares ahead at Nova, knowing that’s not the topic either. Dusk: You high? You smoke too much today? I know you didn’t stop me to talk about me winning the Extreme Championship. I also know you didn’t come out here to rub it in my face that I lost to Jason Snow who had the grace and skill to simply fall on top of me after we superkicked each other. So, why don’t you just go ahead and rip off the band-aid. I tossed my hat into your little war and you’re here to scare me out of it. Nova: To the point. I can dig that. Now let me make mine. You’re right. I caught that little conversation you had with Troy…talking about all the honor and pride you have for a company you’ve been around for a length of a loud fart. Hell, when you came in, you wouldn’t shut up about freakin’ Global. You talk about how you can’t stand to see Lindsay Troy do this by herself any longer. So, here’s the million dollar question, Dusk, how is it going to feel when the temperature in this oven you’re so desperately to climb into gets turned up to a million degrees, and you’re standing in that ring and you’ve got the crazies surrounding you? The Universal Champion takes another drag. Nova: Because to get back to my point…Snow’s boot is only the beginning of what you have to look forward to if you try to hop into this little skirmish and pretend like you have a damn clue about anybody. Dusk’s red eyes start to flare up slightly as he looks at the champ, letting his words settle into him. Dusk: It’ll be just another day at the office. But, it all changes now Nova. You betrayed the fans and the fans deserve retribution. Nova: Pffft. I deserve retribution, against the man whose only reason to live, apparently, is my misery? Dusk: Just know this, you come at me, and I’ll ruin you. You need four other people to do it. I’ll do it myself. Nova: Does that make you feel like a roughneck, Dusk? Because you have convinced yourself that you can fight this war by yourself? Wake up. The guys you’re trying to fight have pedigrees longer than that green streak running up your back. Dusk: Keep telling yourself that. But, the worst has yet to come for you guys. Nova nods his head and looks to be turning away from Dusk when a question strikes his fancy. Nova: One question. You told Snow two weeks ago to just give you a reason to get involved with all of this. Mind telling what it was? Dusk: The simple fact that I could. Dusk then turns his back on Nova and picks up his leather bag. He’s done. He has other things to focus on as he walks towards the door. Nova just stands there, looking at him, and offers a parting shot. Nova: See you soon, Dusk. A few words of warning…I don’t usually roll alone. Angelo Deville: I liked that bit about the dumpster and my hair. Nova: I thought you might. Angelo Deville: So your Irish Porn Star plan, while it looked great on paper, lost something in translation. Nova: You’re right. Danny’s still here. Angelo Deville: Got a back-up plan? The Universal Champion lights another cigarette. Nova: Not a “back-up” plan…no backing up. We’re moving forward. Let’s up the ante.
