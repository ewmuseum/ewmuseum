Klaus Steinheil Biography Nicknames The Eastern Front Hometown Berlin, Germany Birthday 17/09/75 Height 6'9" Weight 295lbs Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography Klaus Steinheil, a man who for all extensive purposes, should not exist, before he had a name he was simply known as #273. He was given a name. Klaus was not chosen for the government program in the same way that Panzer was, nor did he volunteer. Klaus was created. A perfect clone of Kurt Panzer in every physical detail. Having noticed deficiencies with their first experiment, Panzer, they chose to not leave anything down to nature, to create the ultimate soldier themselves, learning from the mistakes that were already apparent in the five year old Kurt. Klaus was to be the Arian + model of the future, he would prove to be smarter than Panzer, at a young age he showed problem solving abilities far exceeding that of his originator. Yet at the same time showed a violent irratic streak which had not been experienced with Panzer at a similar age. As Klaus grew so did his irratic nature until he finally became aware of what he was, unlike Panzer who had simply followed the path laid before him, Steinheil began to question what he was, propaganda in the form of television and radio influenced his actions and he began to question the cause of the motives of his creators. The move itself is essentially a top rope version of the Howitzer, risking himself and his opponent, the move could quite literally be, do or die.
