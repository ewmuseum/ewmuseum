ReVolution 90 Osaka Dome, Osaka, Japan ---- Point Of Impact? More Like A Point Of No Return The echo of the pre-show fireworks successfully fufilling their point of purchase could mean only one thing. The cameras were rolling and the curtain on this particular sweat and blood stained stage had risen. In two hours time it would fall once again, only this time it also fell on PRIME's association with Playboy TV. Toshiaki Motoki could remember the moment when Sebastian Starr announced the image-scarring television deal from his hospital bed, one of two trump cards that was only surpassed by the revelation that his stake in the company had been handed over to a cat. It was a hammer blow, calculated with the utmost precision in order to shoot down Motoki's ambitions of a utopian wrestling promotion for the months and years that followed. After over two years of waiting, this stain on the canvas of PRIME's history was about to bleached from memory. Two years on however, his ambitions for the promotion were no longer so straight forward. What once was black and white, now existed as numerous shades of grey. Still though, a darker shade of grey was more appealing than a lighter one. Besides, they'd already delved too deeply into his dark designs to pull out now. There is no turning back.
