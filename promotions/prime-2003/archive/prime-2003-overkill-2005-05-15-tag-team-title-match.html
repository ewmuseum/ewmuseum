Your Soon-To-Be WotY Proudly Presents...

28 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
...from the PRIME payperview vault, aired live from Mexico for Overkill, Manifest Destiny (C) v. Los Diablos. The abridged backstory: After they were eliminated from the Dual Halo, Los Diablos broke back into the match and saved Emilio Rage from The Deville's evil clutches, sparing him the inevitable fate of the Soprano. Deville, naturally, didn't take kindly to this, and the following week had a match with Baltasar. Perhaps more importantly, he cut a promo with a chihuahua, which he launched at Angelus when the proud Mexicans came out to put a stop to the "culture-mockery". Angelus was bitten, The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid came out of nowhere to save Deville, and life went on. The HIT and Deville established a shaky alliance, teaming up to combat Los Diablos, who were enraged to discover that the chihuahua was rabid (Deville generously let them find out almost immediately, preventing any fatal effects). As a condition of teaming with him, Deville made The HIT revamp his image, modeling himself after his supposed mentor. Thus was Justin Williamson reborn. A match was scheduled between them for the vacant Tag Team Championship, and through bribery of the referee, Deville and Williamson won. A rematch was booked a couple of weeks later, but just before it was to air, Deville had Los Diablos falsely deported. In the ring, while it was happening, Williamson and Deville announced the birth of Manifest Destiny. Los Diablos made themselves a thorn in the side of the champions, who already had enough on their plate with their rampant distrust of one another. Baltasar and Angelus interfered in their matches and jumped them at every opportunity, prompting Hin See to schedule another rematch, the stipulations of which to be named by the challengers. Los Diablos, in response to the humiliation of being deported and to the ceaseless mockery of their country, set the location as a Mexican prison. The only way to win would be to lock the opposing team in a cell. A blueprint of the establishment can be found here: http://prime.e-wrestling.org/images/overkillprison.jpg The match: Nick: And I’m getting word that it’s time for the Tag Team Title match! We’re heading live to Mexico, Richard, where the teams have just arrived in the prison and we’re ready to get underway! Richard: Isn’t that a little redundant, Nick? Isn’t the whole of Mexico really just a big prison? The PRIMEtron flickers to snow, then we’re panning down a line of dilapidated cells, with old-fashioned sliding doors of long-rusted steel bars. Haggard inmates in navy slacks and off-white tank-tops glare from steel bunks attached to the walls, or with white knuckles clenched around the confining columns as if they might bend them and escape. Aside from a plastic blue bin pushed into a corner or under the bunk, the bunk itself, and a small aluminium toilet with a sink carved into the top, the prisoners are alone in the cells. Richard: Reminds me of my first apartment. All those damn Mexicans hiding in the closets. Nick: This is what it all comes down to, Richard. After months of sabotage from both sides, both personal and professional, it’s finally going to be settled . . . in a prison. The shot cuts to a large chamber dotted with steel picnic tables painted green and peeling. Grated staircases run in opposite directions from near the centre of the room, curling out toward the walls and giving way to catwalks that span nearly the length of the room. At either end of the catwalks are heavy doors leading to hallways with more cells. Directly beneath are their twins for the first floor. The longer walls are lined with cells on both levels. One of the shorter walls is a slick one-way mirror of plexiglass, with a door cut into the middle to give way to the main guard lounge. The other is plain concrete, half the length of its counterpart to give way to a staircase leading to the basement. Nick: I understand that the downstairs is off-limits, but the two main floors and the stairs connecting them are all fair game. Lots of potential for high spots and foreign objects. If those tables aren’t bolted to the floor, they could cause some serious damage. Richard: We have dozens of admittedly criminal, murderous Mexicans, probably all brandishing machetes, acting as lumberjacks in this match . . . and you see a threat in the tables? Baltasar and Angelus have their heads huddled together beside one of the stairways. Baltasar is nodding, but both men’s eyes remain focused, glaring across the chamber where Deville and Williamson are also waiting, angled away from one another so there may as well have been a wall separating them. Words are spilling from Baltasar’s mouth, but they are lost in the din of the prison, of the prisoners shouting threats at the gringos and even one another. Most of the cursing is in Spanish, but there’s no mistaking what it is. Richard: I think we’re establishing a precedent here. Nick: Yeah. Who ever heard of fighting in a prison? Richard: Stuff your sarcasm. I meant having a crowd debauched enough to be behind Los Diablos. The door in the ocean of plexiglass swings open suddenly, admitting a large, dark-skinned man in black dress pants and a collared black shirt. Despite the lack of sun, his eyes hide behind dark sunglasses, and a flat-topped full-rimmed black hat shelters most of his salt-and-pepper hair. He strides into the room as if he owns it, looking at neither the competitors nor the prisoners, entirely self-possessed. “Warden Ramirez” adorns his left breast in ostentatious white thread. The tag team titles dangle from his hands. Stopping directly in the centre of the room, the warden removes his shades and stares down his nose around the room, scanning the cells, looking almost hurt, trying to shame the prisoners to silence. The snickering and jeering grow louder. Warden Ramirez: Sounds to me like some of the residents are confused. His voice booms with authority, easily penetrating the general hubbub. Warden Ramirez: Sounds to me like they think that just because we brought a few extra cameras in we’re throwing order out to make room. The warden pauses, looking smug as the prisoners quiet down. A few persist, but without much zest. Warden Ramirez: Sounds to me like some of the residents might have gotten a bit too much sun this month. Maybe “fresh air” and “sunlight” should be added to their list of restrictions. That brings a heavy silence. Los Diablos and Manifest Destiny exchange bemused glances as the warden nods with satisfaction. Richard: Dear Hoyt! It is my first apartment! The warden eyes each team in turn, then addresses the air between them in his stately radio voice, doing his best to make every sentence a proclamation. Warden Ramirez: Welcome to Saint Jose Correctional Institute. My name is Warden Ramirez. If you address me, it will be as Warden Ramirez. Richard: Saint Jose? I’ll bet the prison has a rampant drug problem. Ramirez gives them a moment to try it out, then continues in the same vein when no one takes the bait. Warden Ramirez: I’ve been told that you were all informed of the rules by a Mr. Hin See. However, I’m going to re-state them now so there will be no confusion. He glares at the four men again through squinted eyes, as if relishing the idea of one of them daring to question the rules in his prison. A few catcalls descend from the upper cells, and Warden Ramirez’s mind’s eye carefully catalogues which and what before continuing on. Warden Ramirez: The four of you are to go at one another using anything short of blatant lethal force. In the event of blatant lethal force, officers of this prison – my men – will descend upon the criminal party with an apocalyptic wrath to make God cry mercy. Ramirez pauses to smile directly into the screen, nodding proudly, then quickly shifts back into character. Warden Ramirez: The inmates have been instructed not to touch any of you. However, this is merely a precaution, not a rule. We will not intervene should you provoke them and get too close. A chorus of whooping rises from the cells, interspersed by lewd threats and suggestions to Deville and Williamson. The Disciple meets their stares defiantly, but The Deville ignores them, instead grinning at Los Diablos as if it somehow bodes ill for them. Warden Ramirez: The match begins when the siren sounds. It ends when both members of a team are locked inside of the empty cells. There are only two empty cells, located at the centre of each hallway on the lower floor. He points to the doors flanking him like an airline steward, which give way to the U-shaped, cell-lined halls. Warden Ramirez: Officers with keys for the cells with be located in the guard squares. If a cell door is closed, only an officer will be able to open it. You will ask him, politely, to unlock the cell. Should any of you fail to be polite or get out of line, my men will have no choice but to correct your behaviour in the quickest, most effective way possible. His condescending smile beams his wish for that to happen. Again the bait is left unmolested. Warden Ramirez: The winning team will receive these championship belts. Ramirez raises the belts high for the teams to see, then, having said his piece, nods and begins striding down the length of the lounge back to the guard shack. At the door he pauses and turns back with a thoughtful mask, as if he’s just remembered something. A sudden mischievous smile betrays it as a flare for theatre. Warden Ramirez: I almost forgot. Hin See added a last minute stipulation, citing, and I quote, “because I think it’s funny.” That stipulation is as follows, and will be personally enforced by yours truly: The members of the losing team will remain here as residents in the care of Saint Jose Correctional Institute until the morning of the next ReVolution. The siren sounds as he disappears through the door, his grin nearly splitting his face. Nick: Hin See can’t do that! These men have committed no crime! Richard: … Nick: These men have been charged with no crime! The explosion of bloodlust from the prisoners is instantaneous, providing a school-yard backdrop as Deville steps onto the table immediately before him and smiles invitingly at Los Diablos. Nick: As harsh a stipulation as that is, it’s not going to have time to sink in! We’re already underway, and Deville has hopped atop one of the picnic tables, trying to nullify the size disadvantage! Richard: He probably just likes making Los Diablos look up at him. Baltasar says something to Angelus that brings a nod of agreement, then the two separate, circling out to flank the clustered members of Manifest Destiny. Nick: Los Diablos are looking to trap the champions between them . . . but Williamson isn’t going to wait! He’s charging Angelus! Launching himself from one of the benches, Williamson sails through the air and tackles Angelus, spilling both of them toward the southwest door. Angelus rolls through the momentum, mounting Williamson with a deluge of rights and lefts. Richard: But here comes Deville! He followed Williamson! With Baltasar hot on his heels, Deville takes to the air using the same bench Williamson did, sailing over and driving his elbow into the back of Angelus’ neck. The force squashes both Williamson and Angelus, but it also knocks Angelus free of Williamson, rolling him over to the cell beside the door. A hand reaches out to grab Angelus, but he manages to jerk away just as Williamson and Deville get to their feet. Nick: Baltasar’s got Deville by the scruff of the neck! Using his handful of suit to whip Deville back, Baltasar throws him into the picnic table. The back of Deville’s shins hit first, then his lower back smashes across the edge. Baltasar is there to meet him with a vicious front elbow when he pops up, then doubles him over with a knee to the nether regions. Angelus is having worse luck with Williamson, spinning out of the prisoner’s grasp only to get dumped with a belly-to-belly suplex. Both men are up immediately, trading punches. Williamson grabs one of Angelus’ arms and tries to whip him toward the cells, but Angelus overpowers him and reverses the move. Shoving Deville’s head between his legs, Baltasar takes a step back from the table to give himself room to heave Deville onto his shoulders. Deville plants a foot against the steel and drives himself forward, taking Baltasar down by the legs. The instinctual Boston crab follows in a heartbeat. Nick: A submission won’t do him any good, but the cramping of Baltasar’s back sure will! Richard: Not to mention the never-ending cramping of his brain. A tattooed forearm snakes between the bars beside Williamson just as he crashes into them, encircling his neck and choking him, holding him in place for Angelus. Angelus takes full advantage of the opportunity, cracking Williamson’s nose with a hard right fist, much to the vocal delight of the inmate aiding him. Nick: That criminal is holding Williamson captive! Richard: Yeah! And that poor Mexican in the cell isn’t doing him any favours either! Deville has a clear view of Williamson’s predicament, but he continues reefing back on Baltasar’s legs. Williamson can handle himself, after all. Shaking off the shot from Angelus, he grabs the wrist of the arm choking him and pulls it across himself, yanking the prisoner into the bars, then he cranks it hard the other way. There is a sickening crack of bones splintering when the upper arm hits the cell and the forearm keeps going, popping the elbow. Nick: Williamson just broke that guy’s arm! Richard: That Ramirez guy never said they couldn’t fight back! The inmate snatches his arm back through the bars, shrieking guttural curses as he cradles it against his ribs and hops around his cell. Angelus is all over Williamson as he stumbles forward, scooping him into a fireman’s carry before kicking out his legs and executing a Samoan drop. Nick: Angelus has noticed Deville . . . Deville releases the hold just as Angelus reaches him, but he’s too late to avoid the clothesline that sends him stumbling toward the picnic table. Rather than bang into it, Deville runs up and over the table, then turns and slams his hands down on it before giving Angelus the finger. Angelus considers him for a moment, giving Williamson the opportunity he needs to chopblock him from behind. Nick: Deville’s coming back over the table! Baltasar is still down! This could spell big trouble for Angelus! Richard: It better do it in crayon and all-caps if it wants him to understand. Deville drives a knee from the table into Angelus’ back as he gets up, then Williamson is dragging the masked monster toward the door by the foot. Angelus kicks at him with his free leg, but the blows make little impression on Williamson. Deville steps in and begins delivering kicks to the top of Angelus’ head, forcing him to concentrate there instead. Nick: Looks like the champions are thinking of ending this quickly. Deville falls on Angelus with both hands around his throat as Williamson releases the ankle and swings the door wide. A quick eye gouge knocks The Deville staggering back, giving Angelus room to rise, but Williamson locks him in a headlock and runs him into the wall between the door and the cell. Baltasar has gotten up and is charging Deville, but Angelo sees him in time to drop down and launch Baltasar overhead with a backdrop. Baltasar hurtles through the air and lands in the doorway just as the door swings closed. Kicking it open from his back, Baltasar spins to his feet and turns just in time to be tackled by Deville into the hallway. There is a sudden surge of volume as the prisoners in that hall explode, then it is muted by the door latching closed. Nick: Baltasar and Deville are alone in the southern section of the prison, where one of the empty cells resides. Williamson looks to follow, but Angelus has his ankle! Williamson spins and tries to stomp the arm free of him, but Angelus drives up and into him, scooping him onto a shoulder. The prisoners go berserk as Angelus saunters around in a small circle, holding Williamson like a sack, then pauses beside one of the tables and unleashes a primal roar. Crack! Richard: Holy shit! Williamson’s dead! Nick: That’s the most literal spinebuster I’ve ever seen! Angelus broke him in half! Crumpling from the edge of the table, Williamson flops around on the floor for a moment before going deathly still. Feeding off the rage of those around him, Angelus doesn’t relent, instead scooping Williamson back onto his shoulder and climbing up onto the top of the table. He shifts Williamson into a piledriver position, then spikes him down on the smooth metal surface. Williamson’s flops backward and his legs land off the end of the table, slingshotting the rest of his body to the floor. Baltasar and Deville dance circles around one another down the hallway, throwing shots, Baltasar trying to spin The Deville into the waiting feet and fists of those surrounding them, Angelo spinning nimbly out of the way and stinging him with quick counter-punches. Richard: Was that a flash of steel? I swear someone just slashed at Deville with a knife! Nick: It’s a prison, Richard! The inmates don’t have knives in their cells! Richard: It’s Mexico! Deville and Baltasar clench just as they enter the open square where two guards are on duty, sending them lunging to their feet with twin shouts of alarm as the wrestlers spill on top of the desk. The computer monitor shatters on the floor as The Deville climbs atop Baltasar, choking him against the wall. Richard: Hey! The guard who had been sitting in the desk chair, having recovered from the initial shock, is now ogling the fallen monitor. Outrage gives way to pure rage as he unbuttons the club at his hip, a thin black cylinder akin to a nightstick. Drawing it back like a tennis pro for a backhand, he lunges forward and slashes Deville across the back of his shoulders, knocking him off of Baltasar and to the floor. Richard: C’mon! Deville would have paid for it! Nick: I don’t think these guards care about The Deville’s money, Richard. Richard: That’s because you’re an idiot. The other guard puts his partner in a half-nelson, preventing him from going to finish Deville off, and the two have a short argument as Baltasar rolls off the desk and drops onto Deville with his knees. Grabbing a handful of slicked blonde hair, Baltasar climbs to his feet and rakes Deville’s eyes along the sharp wooden edge, then runs him face-first into the wall. Nick: Deville’s opened up! Blood gushes from a cut along Deville’s eyebrow as he stumbles back into the waiting hands of Baltasar, which wrap around his throat and heave him straight up in the air before slamming him down across the desk. The thin wood splinters under the force, cracking the desk in half, leaving Deville battered in its ruin. Richard: Baltasar’s going to get it now! The officers stop bickering and look at the wrecked table, then the formerly rational one brings out his own nightstick. The two advance on Baltasar, who has backed himself into the wall with his arms raised and is appealing to them in Spanish, but as they pass Deville they turn and fall on him, rapidly hammering him with the clubs as he tries desperately to cover his face. Baltasar seems stunned at first, then a knowing smile etches onto his face. He steps forward and rests a hand on one of the guards’ backs, then tells them he can handle it on his own. The guards give Deville a couple more shots for good measure, then straighten and back off, running their free hands through their hair and panting. Nick: I don’t know how Deville’s conscious, but he’s bleeding all over the place! Richard: What the hell was that? Did they mistake him for a piñata?! Those guards should be the ones behind bars! Back in the main room, Angelus has a half-conscious Williamson perched on his shoulders for a powerbomb. Directly in front of him is a trash can, its contents sprayed across the floor. The bomb brings more of a suctioned whoosh than a bang as Williamson is stuffed into the opening, his body folding like a suitcase as the force drives his backside almost to the bottom. His feet kick out the top impotently as Angelus laughs. Richard: Williamson is stuck in the can! That’s gold! Thinking the same thing, Angelus slides the trashcan across the floor to the side of one of the tables. Letting the barrel go, he moves around it and lifts the table, then strains to slide it over top of the can, cramming Williamson all the way in. Nick: And it doesn’t look like he’s going to get out! Angelus was barely able to lift that table with those tree trunks he calls arms! There’s no way Williamson will be able to kick his way free! Richard: Now they’re free to double-team Deville! Unless Angelus doesn’t know where they are! Apparently he does. After dusting off his hands and taking account of his surroundings, he makes a beeline for the southwest door, nodding to his bloodthirsty countrymen. Nick: This is the beginning of the end for Manifest Destiny! Los Diablos could finally get their hands on the titles! Baltasar is dragging Deville down the back hall towards the empty cell, watched smugly by the guards. The door to the cell is already open, and celebratory fists shaken through bars provide a fitting escort for Baltasar as he pulls Deville to the opening. Nick: Baltasar’s going to take Deville out of this thing right now! Then he and Angelus will be free to eliminate Williamson at their leisure! Richard: They’ll just roll him down to the other cell! Deville’s hand fires out at the last second and grasps the edge of the doorway, then his leg fires back and catches Baltasar on the kneecap. Deville spins around with a backhand, followed by an over-the-top shot to the eye, then spears Baltasar across the hall into the bars on the other side. Richard: No! He’s free! Nick: But he doesn’t have much time! Angelus will be here any second! Baltasar is on his feet before Deville, being far fresher, and elevates The Deville with a knee driven under the chin. Deville pops to his feet and staggers back, arms flailing for balance. Capitalizing, Baltasar steps forward and plants a shove on his chest, looking to knock him through the open doorway, but Deville grabs his arms and rolls down onto his back, flipping Baltasar over him and through the doorway. Nick: Baltasar’s in the cell! If Deville can close the door we’ve got a whole new ballgame! Richard: Shittiest. Metaphor. Ever. Deville grabs the door and slides it toward the wall, but Baltasar’s arm juts through the opening at the last second, making him scream as the door sandwiches it against the wall. Deville hauls the door back and crunches the arm again, but instead of pulling back to do it again, he pushes against the door with one hand and cracks Baltasar through the bars with the other. Then he’s absolutely mauled from the side as Angelus arrives like a rampaging bear. Nick: Baltasar’s saved! Deville took too long! He’s a dead man now! Baltasar slides the cage open and grabs his biceps, trying to massage the pain away. Deville has hiptossed Angelus and is driving boot after boot into his head, keeping him floored, but the constant glances over his shoulder give away his knowledge of his impending doom. He turns to swing at Baltasar as the monster closes in, but Baltasar deflects the blow and smashes Deville with one of his own, toppling him back over Angelus. Deville rolls through the fall, back-somersaulting to his feet, but as soon as he rises Baltasar levels him with a jumping boot to the face. Nick: Baltasar just leaped Angelus and almost decapitated Angelo! Deville’s bleeding all over the place! Orchestrating the affair, Baltasar orders Angelus to grab one of Deville’s legs, then grabs the other himself. They drag him back and through the open cell door, stopping only when Deville’s hands clamp onto the sides of the doorway. Richard: What a stupid move! If Deville breaks free and closes the door, it’s over! Nick: I don’t see that happening! Richard: He probably thinks Williamson is eliminated! With a great heave Los Diablos yank Deville into the air, snapping his grip. Nick: All three of them are in the cell now! Unless Williamson hulked up and broke free of his fake cell, I think it’s safe to say Deville’s looking at a week and a half in that real one! Deville’s head bounces off the concrete when he lands, and Los Diablos have a window to leave the cell, but they don’t. Instead, Baltasar pulls Deville up by the hair and looks up at the ceiling. With the limited space, they can’t pull off any of their finishing moves, but Baltasar compromises by turning Deville for a tombstone, and Angelus aids the cracking of Deville’s skull by jumping up and throwing down on his feet. Nick: Deville’s out cold! There’s no way he’s getting up from that! Richard: Where there’s a will . . . Will-less, Deville lays slumped on his back as Baltasar gets to his feet and shuffles out the door behind Angelus. Baltasar turns around and grabs the cell door, sneering down at Deville. Baltasar: Thanks for keeping the titles warm for us, gringo. The door grates closed and locks into place with a metallic snap, and Baltasar crumples to his knees, throwing his head back. Nick: Baltasar thinks Williamson is out, too! He thinks it’s over! Angelus quickly disabuses him of that notion, and Baltasar curses as he leaps to his feet, leading the way down the hall at a jog. With a laugh, Angelus tells him there’s no hurry, but rushes to keep up just the same. Nick: I almost hate to admit it, but Baltasar’s thinking like a champion tonight! Los Diablos have dominated this match, and they’re halfway to their dream! All that lies in their way now is Oscar the Disciple! Richard: . . . Why, Nick? Just . . . Why? Baltasar explodes into the main chamber, nearly kicking the door off the hinges, and begins scanning for Williamson. He’s halfway to the staircase when Angelus barrels through the door and shouts at him to stop, telling him to relax. Baltasar looks ready to bite Angelus’ head off, but his face blanks when he follows the direction Angelus is moving and spots the table propped on the garbage can. Baltasar: You’re shitting me . . . The inmates cheer as Angelus shoves the table from the trash can and looms over it, joined a moment later by Baltasar. Williamson struggles and tries to push himself up, but Baltasar leans down and drives him with a sharp fist. Angelus: He’s not getting out of there. Let’s just carry him to the other cell, essa. Baltasar nods his agreement, then shakes his head with a laugh. Muttering something under his breath about “stupid fucking gringos”, he seems confident and amused as he grabs one of the handles and threatens Williamson not to move. Nick: I’m gonna have to agree with Angelus! Williamson has no escape! Angelus grabs the other handle and hoists the can between he and his brother, then they begin shuffling toward the northwest door, looking to deliver Williamson to the last free cell like a package. In the other cell, Deville is on his knees with his head hanging, trying to dull the throbbing in his mind. It seems to work; pursing his lips together, he begins whistling something low, climbing unsteadily to his feet. He swoons and has to catch himself on the wall, but the whistled “The Devil Goes Down to Georgia” never hiccups, and after a brief instant he’s stumbling toward the bars, reaching inside his suit with his right hand. Richard: He brought a file! Good old Deville! Nick: Is he whistling? Indeed whistling, the blood-caked Deville pulls his hand out of his suit, and though it’s not a file he holds, the small blunt object does gleam feebly in the pale fluorescent light. Hooking his arm through the bars, Deville slides the key into place and unlocks the door with a dull click, then slides it open and straightens, paying special attention to smoothing back his hair, smearing his left temple crimson. Nick: Where the hell did he get a key?! It’s amazing what a bit of forethought, a plane ticket, and ten thousand dollars can do. The inmates who had been watching and taunting Deville are now crying out indignantly, shouting for the guards to come. Replacing the key to his pocket, Deville waits for them, pulling out a steel cylinder the size of a dumbbell bar. He flicks it down and it extends with a snap to the length of a baseball bat. Nick: He can’t be thinking what I think he’s thinking . . . Richard: No? Winding it back like it is a baseball bat, Deville leans on his back foot and bares his teeth in a snarl. Blood drips into his mouth, staining the pearly white grimace. When the first guard arrives and spins to face the open cell, Deville lunges through, slicing the rod down like a sword, splitting the officer’s forehead and sending him back into the far cell. The other officer shouts in surprise and reaches for his club, but Deville kicks him in the nuts and smashes the bar down across his ear, knocking him to his knees. Nick: Deville’s assaulting the guards! Richard: And the prisoners are helping him! The guard who had stumbled back against the bars is being choked by the inmate beyond. While his left forearm is cutting off the officer’s airway, his right hand scrambles around at his belt, wrestling with the officer’s hands for the club. Grabbing the inmates wrist, the officer changes gears and reaches for his radio, but Deville’s foot smashes it into the cell. It crackles as it bounces off the floor, but no alarmed voices pour through. Nick: What does Deville think he’s doing? There are at least twenty guards there, and some of them are armed with more than nightsticks! Richard: I don’t think they knows it’s happening! They must be watching Los Diablos and Williamson! Nick: Angelo better hope it stays that way! If one of them looks . . . Deville breaks the choked guard’s nose with an elbow, then reaches into his pocket and comes up with some loose coins and a key ring. The change he drops, but the key ring goes into his pants pocket. The other officer, bleeding from the ear, is just getting to his feet as The Deville cracks him under the jaw with a knee. Nick: That inmate has the nightstick! He’s choking the guard with it! Deville stomps on the kneed officer’s head as soon as he whips onto his back, then lashes him across the throat with his stainless steel staff. The guard gags as Deville reaches down and wraps a forearm around his throat, then drags him backward into the open cell, waiting until he’s out cold before relieving him of his keys and stepping back into the hallway. Nick: If this keeps up, Deville might be staying there a lot longer than a week! Richard: They hit him first, Nick! He’s allowed to defend himself! Deville lunges forward uber-defensively, driving another elbow into the already broken nose of the choked out guard. Shouting at the inmate to release him, Deville pries the bar from his throat and drags him across the hall to join his partner, then commandeers his radio. Whistling again as he emerges from the cell, Deville slams the door closed and re-straightens his hair. The inmate who had choked out the guard reaches through the bars toward Deville, pleading with him to let him free, telling Deville he owes him for his help. The Deville considers it a moment, rubbing his chin and frowning. Nick: There’s no way Deville can do this! He’ll be looking at life! Richard: Does Mexico even have laws? Grinning suddenly, Deville points beyond the prisoner to the sink in his cell. Angelo Deville: Wet some toilet paper for me and we’ll talk. Back in the main chamber, Baltasar has just set down the can with Williamson in it so he may open the door. As soon as he does, however, Williamson seizes the opportunity to throw his weight, knocking the barrel tumbling out of Angelus’ grasp and wedging himself free in a heap. Nick: Deville’s inciting a jailbreak and Williamson just broke free! He doesn’t look to limber, though! Williamson manages to kick the garbage canister away, but he can’t stop Baltasar from whipping him up and cracking his spine with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. Nick: Williamson might be free of the trash can, but he’s still heading for that cell! Richard: Unless Deville and a bunch of former inmates have anything to say about it! The southwest door bangs open as The Deville re-emerges on the scene, face washed mostly clean of blood, but he’s without a coterie of prisoners. Backing off as Baltasar re-establishes a vertical base, Deville measures his head perfectly before arcing down with the bar across his temple, sprawling and stunning the behemoth. We need to execute a plan. Williamson nods, resting his hands against his knees to catch his breath. Deville leans down and whispers into his ear, prompting more nods, then straightens and begins moving toward Baltasar. Angelo Deville: When I nod . . . Williamson doesn’t re-confirm his understanding, but instead goes after Angelus, who is leaning against the northwest door and holding his head. Baltasar is less coherent, offering insignificant, child-like resistance as Deville picks him up and guides him to the southern staircase. Smashing Baltasar’s face against the picnic table just beside the mouth of the stairs, Deville turns and makes eye contact with Williamson. Nick: There’s the nod . . . But what are they up to? If Deville really has freed those prisoners . . . “It’s simple,” The Deville had explained, his breathing just shy of panting. Deville takes the stairs two at a time, turning halfway up to remind Baltasar what a piece of shit he is, then disappears down one of the upper hallways. Baltasar is still shaky but he’s after him immediately, snarling as he propels himself up by the railing, pumping his massive arms like a running bear, devouring the staircase by leaps and bounds. Williamson makes the most of his headlock, running Angelus into one of the scant sections of concrete separating the cells. Hands nearly black with patterned ink reach out to grab Williamson through the bars, but the Disciple dances nimbly out of their range, trotting to the northwest door and yanking it wide, disappearing through it. Despite his head trauma, Angelus is up and in hot pursuit of Williamson before the door has swung shut. “Divide and conquer.” It was simple. Deville was to lure Baltasar upstairs while Williamson led Angelus through the crescent hallway on the ground floor and ambushed him at a corner. Using his speed to leave Baltasar far behind, Deville would circle back and come to Williamson’s aid, giving Manifest Destiny a brief but significant window to isolate and eliminate Angelus. Baltasar: Wait! The command comes as a hiss, with Baltasar throwing his voice to penetrate the din yet keeping it low enough that The Deville wouldn’t hear. Halfway through the door Angelus turns back, tilting his head quizzically at the sight of his adopted brother skittering down the stairs. Baltasar: Stupid fucking gringos . . . That’s all Baltasar has to say to get Angelus nodding. With a grin, Angelus disappears into the hallway, letting the door slam shut behind him as Baltasar races the length of the chamber to the northeast door and pulls it wide, lunging in to cut the Disciple off. Nick: Whatever the plan was, Los Diablos seems to have made it backfire! Williamson is now trapped between the Angelus and Baltasar, and Angelo is upstairs on the opposite side of the prison! A hushed silence falls over the main chamber as those in their cells try not to alert The Deville that his plan is backfiring. Of course, The Devil’s Don already knows. Strolling back into view on the catwalk, his legs seem in no hurry to deliver him to prevent his partner’s destruction. With a smug half-smile, Deville surveys the angry eyes peering out at him from sunken faces. His gaze locks on a younger man, sitting on his bunk with his fists pressed together in his lap, glaring back defiantly. Defiance gives way to suspicion as The Deville approaches, arching an eyebrow as his lips peel back to show a friendly, snow-white smile. Nick: That sonofabitch! What’s he doing? He’s leaving Williamson to get eliminated! In the northern hallway, Williamson has to circle all the way around to the far corner to avoid being betrayed by the leers of the guards on duty. When he turns the corner, instead of finding a place to deliver an ambush he finds himself the victim of one. Baltasar lunges into him with a knee to the gut, then drops him with a DDT as Angelus rounds the other corner and comes charging toward the melee. Nick: Williamson is surrounded now, and it doesn’t look like Los Diablos are suffering much from the punishment they took a few moments ago. Richard: And it doesn’t look like his partner is coming to help him. Williamson lurches up and aims a low-blow at Baltasar, but his hand is sandwiched between Baltasar’s knees, then a huge fist is knocking his head back. Angelus races in and plants a boot against his temple, knocking him sprawling against a cell. The prisoner inside quickly follows the example Angelus provided, but Baltasar robs him of the fun by grabbing Williamson by the hair and pulling him to a vertical base. Bending down, Baltasar shoves his head between Williamson’s legs and stands up, back body dropping him from a stand-still. Angelus is right behind to catch Williamson as he flips around, then he dives forward to obliterate the Disciple with a powerbomb. Nick: Doomsday Production! Los Diablos just hit their finisher on Williamson! Dragging Williamson’s limp but spasming body into the cell proves to be little hassle for the challengers, and when they leave the cell and slide the door into place, Williamson is barely stirring. The inmates urge them on between asking to be let free, and if either of Los Diablos have any sisters. Nick: And now we’re down to Los Diablos and The Deville, who is presumably laying in wait somewhere in the prison! Richard: With an army of inmates, Nick. You know it as well as I do. Los Diablos waste no time celebrating the elimination of Williamson. Taking off down the hall at a trot, they return the respectful nods from the guards as they pass, then make their way to the door leading to the main chamber. When the prisoners realize they’re drowning each other out, that their countrymen can’t make out what they’re saying, the yelling doesn’t stop, but arms start whipping out, pointing to the threat. Baltasar and Angelus slowly turn their heads to follow the desperately stretched fingers, scanning up the wall at their side before coming to rest on the corner railing of the southern balcony . . . . . . Where Teh D3v1ll3 is perched like Batman, the end of a length of ripped bedsheets tied into a rope clasped in his hands. The other end is fastened to the balcony railing above Los Diablos, and as their eyes and mouths explode in surprise, Teh D3v1ll3 descends like Tarzan from fifteen feet above, arcing down on his makeshift vine. Nick: What?! Richard: Teh D3v1ll3! The cheap Mexican sheets snap before Deville reaches them, but his momentum fires him forward, his body compressing as he slices the air, drawing himself into a ball. Los Diablos throw their arms up to catch him, to try to dampen the inevitable damage, but just shy of ground zero the Deville-bomb detonates, arms and legs like steel girders flailing out and scattering Baltasar and Angelus like pins. Nick: How did Deville talk a prisoner out of his bedsheets? Richard: Who cares? Everyone’s down! Deville went kamikaze! Somewhat refreshed from his break, The Deville is the first one up, wrapping his hands around Baltasar’s mask and pulling him to his feet. After feeding him a few shots to keep him loopy, Deville slings him over a shoulder and walks him over to the stairway. Baltasar tries to punch Deville as he’s set down on the floor just in front of the steps, but Deville easily blocks it and knifehands him in the throat, ascending two stairs before pulling Baltasar into him, twisting him into a pumphandle position. Nick: Deville just hit Baltasar with a piledriver from the steps! Richard: And he’s not finished! Deville snatches one of Baltasar’s ankles, but instead of going for The Soprano, he drags him over to a picnic table and releases him. Strolling around to the far side of the table, Deville grabs the bench and heaves up, resting briefly once he has it tilted on its side, but ultimately flipping it down atop Baltasar. Nick: Baltasar’s not locked in a garbage can, but it’ll do! For now, at least! Thinking along the same lines, Deville rushes back over to Angelus, who took the worst of the three man explosion, cracking his crown against the door. He’s still out of it when Deville scoops him into a fireman’s carry. Always erring on the side of safety, Deville still bashes his head against the wall as he takes him through the northwest door. Nick: Looks like Deville is hoping to free Williamson and leave Angelus in his place! What looked like a sure Los Diablos win is quickly evaporating in the wake of a Manifest Destiny title defence! Richard: I was never in doubt. Ever. The guards in the northwest square seem shocked to see Deville carrying Angelus toward them, but they hold their positions. The Deville doesn’t even look at them as he passes, turning the corner sharply before coming to a halt in front of Williamson, who is crouched in the far corner of his cell. Williamson scrambles to his feet when Deville pulls out his key and inserts it in the lock, then slides the door open. He slams Angelus to the ground just inside the door and gives him a kick, then spits on him before turning his gaze on Williamson. Angelo Deville: Let’s teach this piece of shit a lesson. He takes a step into the cell as Williamson yanks Angelus to his feet with a malicious grin, but as Williamson pulls Angelus deeper into the cell, The Deville hops back out and slams the door shut with damning authority. Williamson’s mouth gapes as he lets Angelus crumple to the floor. The Deville shrugs. Angelo Deville: You didn’t really think I was gonna wait around for you to knife me, did you? Laughing as if at a rich joke, Deville saunters away from the cell, ignoring the shouts of Williamson and the other inmates as he goes the opposite way of the guards, who are eyeing one another dubiously, as if to ask how the hell Deville opened the cell. Nick: I want to feel bad for Williamson, but he had to know it was coming! Richard: C’mon . . . He’s been begging for it since they met! Deville re-enters the main chamber to find Baltasar still down and under the table, but moaning and stirring. Rather than trying to finish him off, The Deville skirts around him and jogs across the room, disappearing through a door into the southern hallway. Nick: Please no . . . Please . . . Richard: Please yes . . . Reward my faith, Deville. After a dreadfully long moment, a piercing siren whoops through the building, and after another moment the door in the mirrored wall bursts open, spilling a dozen guards into the main chamber, led by Warden Ramirez. Richard: Jailbreak! Nick: There’s no way . . . With the blaring wails as their marching drums, the officers unfasten their quasi-nightsticks as they surround the southeast door. Every ten seconds or so another inmate joins the battle, until officers are brawling with prisoners all over the place. Nick: How the hell are we showing this on tv? Richard: Because this is payperview, Nick, and the name of said payperview happens to be Overkill. Meanwhile, back in the main chamber, Baltasar has just shifted the table off of him and is on his feet, looking around wildly for any sight of The Deville. Doors whip open, but guards rush out instead of his opponent, from every area but the lower south, two guards rushing over there. When the door whips open and they pile through, Baltasar catches sight of the brawl. Nick: He knows what’s going on now! But where’s Deville? The Deville appears through the southwest door with an expression of purest smugness on his face. It washes away when he sees Baltasar confronting him from the far end of the hall, squaring his shoulders like some old Western gone bad. The lapse doesn’t last, however. Angelo Deville: There’s no need for this, Baltasar. Strolling across the room toward Baltasar, his voice is as conversational as it is conspiratorial, as if he and Baltasar are old friends united under strange circumstances. Angelo Deville: In a few minutes this place will be overrun by the prisoners, and I seriously doubt they’re going to care that you were born here when the rioting and escaping begins. If we’re still here . . . Angelo shrugs as if to say he’ll probably be fine, but he worries for Baltasar’s safety. Angelo Deville: Listen . . . Both Williamson and Angelus are locked in the same cell. In half a minute, I’m going to walk right out the front door, disqualifying myself. You and Angelus will become the tag team champions, and if the place survives, that conniving Williamson will be stuck here for a week and a half while I skip the country. Worry mutates into a fallacious grin as Deville plants himself five feet in front of Baltasar and cocks his head to the side. Angelo Deville: The alternative is that I have to drag you through all those prisoners and guards fighting for their lives and lock you in the other cell. A couple of inmates, the ones with keys dangling from their grubby hands, bust through the door. One begins unlocking the cells right beside them, but the other bolts for the stairs, ready to kick the riot into full gear. Deville and Baltasar watch blankly as another prisoner comes out of his cell, screaming, and runs to join the fight. Richard: Please. It’s one little backwater prison in Mexico. Not even Mexicans care what happens in Mexico. Angelo Deville: The correct choice is obvious, Baltasar. Nodding with satisfaction, Deville begins walking past the huge Mexican monster. A heavy hand slapping against his chest stops him dead. Baltasar: No. Baltasar moves in front of Deville, looming over him and glaring down from his mask. Deville glares back, but it’s with more surprise than anything else. Baltasar: No way, Deville. I didn’t come here for you to give me the titles. I came here to teach you a lesson, to show my country the respect it deserves, the respect you try to rob. Those titles belong to me and Angelus, and tonight we’re taking them back. Baltasar shoves a sausage-sized finger into Deville’s chest. Baltasar: I came here to kick your ass, essa, and that’s what I’m going to do!
