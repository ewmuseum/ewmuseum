Colossus Night One ---- Rhodes "History?" As Michael Giacchino's "ROAR!" creeps to life, we fade from black to a shot of 5-Star Champion Tyler Rayne, belt slung loosely over his shoulder. We only see him from the waist up, standing in a completely black room with no other visibile attributes. He laughs softly under his breath and looks back at the camera. Tyler Rayne: History's easy. It cuts from Rayne to Lindsay Troy, arms folded, eyes piercing through the screen. Lindsay Troy: Anyone can be history. A series of quick, smooth fades show a litany of PRIMEates as they chime in. As the list goes on, the clips blend into each other, overlapping just slightly. Dusk: Shawn Stewart defeated Dusk. Killean Sirrajin: Adam defeated Killean Sirrajin. Nova: Ozric Mortimer defeated Nova. Chandler Tsonda: Damien Cruz defeated Chandler Tsonda. Tony Gamble: Danny Ferguson defeated Tony Gamble. Wade Elliott: Tony Gamble defeat'd Wade Elliot...like three fuckin' times. Devin Shakur: Wade Elliott defeated Devin Shakur...like three fucking times. Tyler Rayne: Tom Walczak (pause, deep breath) defeated Tyler Rayne. Lindsay Troy: Cozen defeated Lindsay Troy to become the new Universal Champion. The pace of cuts slows on Troy, then we fade quickly to Devin Shakur, the Universal belt tightly around his waist. Devin Shakur: But no one does what we do to become history. He sneers at the camera as we jump to Nova, who takes a drag from his cigarette before speaking. Nova: They don't sell copies of the record book. They sell posters of that time you won the belt... Killean Sirrajin: ...That time you won the tournament with your last ounce of strength... Tyler Rayne: ...That time you did that insane thing they've never seen before. The shots continue to cut between each wrestler as they speak, flowing into each other for complete sentences. Chandler Tsonda: Wrestling doesn't exist on facts or numbers. It exists on Moments. Dusk: On feats are so incredible that you wouldn't believe them if you hadn't seen them for yourself. Troy Douglas: On stories that will be told for decades after you're gone. Tony Gamble: This is a business built on hyperbole. Wade Elliott: History just ain't enough. Xavier Kannon: You step between the ropes to become something more than history. Jonathan Rhine: You step between the ropes to become legend. Dusk: To live on in tall tales and "did you see that"s... Killean Sirrajin: To be immortalized in the mouths and minds of the fans... Tyler Rayne: This isn't a glorious business. Nova: But it can offer you more glory than you'd ever imagine. Devin Shakur: We already know that we're destined to become history. Wade Elliott: An' we're sure as shit doomed ta repeat it. Troy Douglas: But on nights like tonight... Jonathan Rhine: And shows like Colossus... Lindsay Troy: We have the chance to transcend. Tony Gamble: To be something larger than life. Chandler Tsonda: To become myth. As Tsonda fades from the screen, the simultaneous voices of all the participating superstars chime in with the last line. "That's the hard part." From the titles, we BLAST into an overhead view of Bank of America Stadium in Charlotte, North Carolina. 80,000 screaming ant-sized fans are drowned out by a huge pyrotechnic display off the upper decks of the stadium. The rockets go streaking into the night sky in a collection of purples, reds, yellows and whites. It's no Opening Ceremony, but it gets the job done. As more fireworks explosions go off around the ring and ramp, we see the massive setup of the entryway for tonight's show. A 25-foot tall replica of the Greek Parthenon sits in the middle of the ramp, surrounded by a 20-foot high LED screen that stretches to either end of the 125-foot long stage. Above the LED screen, on either side of the Parthenon, two massive PRIME*View screens (about 50' x 30') are held up by Greek columns. Between the screens, hanging just above the building/entrance, is a massive "V" in the format of the Colossus V marketing efforts. The entire majestic setup is massive, stretching across the entire end zone of the stadium, but putting all the available space to good use. As the fireworks calm down, we're brought to a swooping shot around the stadium to show all the rabid fans who have converged upon the South for this monumental (get it? Parthenon? anyone?) event. Finally we settle on the announce desk, just beyond ringside, to the suit-and-tie combo of Richard Parker and Nick Stuart. Nick: WELCOME EVERYONE TO PRIME'S COLOSSUS FIVE! I'M NICK STUART! Richard: AND I'M RICHARD PARKER! Nick: NEARLY A YEAR OF PRIME PROGRAMMING HAS BEEN BUILDING TO THIS POINT, AND YOU CAN TELL BY THE SCREAMS OF THE FANS! I CAN'T EVEN HEAR MYSELF SPEAK! Richard: WHAT? Nick: EXACTLY, JUST READ OFF THE SCRIPT! Richard: THIS IS JUST NIGHT ONE OF A MASSIVE TWO-NIGHT, 13-MATCH AFFAIR! IT'S THE BIGGEST SHOW IN PRIME HISTORY, BAR NONE! Nick: THAT'S RIGHT! WE DON'T EVEN NEED TO TELL YOU WHAT'S ON TAP FOR TONIGHT AFTER ALL THE PROMOS, ALL THE COMMERCIALS AND ALL THE HYPE! WE'VE WAITED FOR TOO LONG TO REACH THIS POINT AND NOW THERE'S ONLY ONE THING LEFT TO DO! Richard: LET'S START THIS SUCKER OFF! ---- PosterBoy/DUI vs. Jason Natas/Mega Job Nick: Up first we’ve got what could turn out to be a really hot opener! After months of knocking the living daylights out of each other, Jason Natas will finally get to tango with Delta Upsilon Iota and Jimmy Bonafide. Richard: And don’t forget Natas’s – ahem - esteemed tag team partners, Mega Job. How do you see this one panning out, Nick? Nick: Well on recent match history alone you’ve got to give this one to Jason Natas. He’s beaten DUI on no less than two occasions recently and he also holds a recent pinfall victory over Bonafide. However, when you consider the sheer incompetence of his tag team partners for the night, the outcome doesn’t seem quite so certain… Richard: It’s doubtless that this is a tough one to call. But as much as it pains me to have to actually cheer for Mega Job tonight, I’ve gotta go with my boy Natas. This guy is a straight-up nightmare to compete against; he hits hard, fast, and with a ferocity that hasn’t been seen in the squared circle since Sonny Silver decided to hang up the boots. Nick: I dunno, Richard; there’s a lot of untapped potential in the alliance of DUI and Bonafide. The trio have displayed good chemistry over the past few weeks as they’ve worked together to take out Jason Natas. The picture on the other side couldn’t be any further removed; Natas hates the fact that he’s teaming with Mega Job tonight after his original partners, Risk & Reward, were shelved with long-term injuries. It’s essentially three-on-one tonight, Richard… I just can’t see how Natas is going to pull this one off. DING DING DING! Vince Howard: Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to Colossus V! A large and undeniably cheap pop rumbles throughout the Bank of America Stadium. Vince Howard: The following six-man tag team match is scheduled for one fall, and it is your opening match of the evening! Jay-Z ft. 3 Doors Down hits the PRIME speakers, the lights dim, and begin to flicker red as the fans begin to jump up and down with excitement. Holding up signs and, screaming at the top of there lungs. Doing what PRIME fans do best. Then Jimmy Bonafide slowly emerges out the back with a smile on his face and as always the wet towel over his head. He stops at the ramp way, looks from sided to side and then throws his hands over his head forming a X with his forearms. BOOOOOOOM! Two red pyros blast from behind him forming a X. He then continues walking down the rampway, towards the ring as the fans chant his name. Vince Howard: Introducing first… from The Bronx, New York! Weighing in at 211lbs… he is THE POSTERBOY… JIMMMMMYYYYY BBBOONNNNAAAAFIIIIDDDDEEEE! He finally slides into the ring under the ropes, and kicks flips back up too his feet. He runs too the turnbuckle jumps onto it and throws his hands up in the air making an X, two more pyro's blast, but this time from the two turnbuuckles behind him. He jump's down, as the light's in the arena turn on and he waits for his allies to arrive. Vince Howard: …aaaaaand his tag team partners! The crowd ripples with anticipation as Metallica’s "Master of Puppets" kicks in with full force. The tandem of Hank Cobb and Colby Korver step out at the top of the ramp, slapping hands together as the steady thrash metal rhythm pours from the speakers. "End of passion play, crumbling away, I'm your source of self-destruction! Veins that pump with fear, sucking darkest clear, Leading on your deaths construction! Taste me you will see, More is all you need, Dedicated to, How I'm killing you!" The fired-up members of Delta Upsilon Iota shake hands with a couple of fans on the way down before eventually sliding into the ring. Both men raises their arms in the air to draw further pops from the crowd. Vince Howard: Hailing from Orlando, Florida and Winston-Salem, North Carolina! Weighing in at a combined weight of 650lbs… the team of COLBY KORVER and HANK COBB… DELTAAAA UPPPPSSSILLLOOOOON IOOOOOTTTTTTAAAAA! Gunshots. "AHHHHHHHHH!" All excitement within the arena seems to die as "Natural Born Killaz" hits. El Janito, Beef and Steve leap out from the backstage area like they own the place. Beef is particularly animated, strumming an air guitar on his way down the ramp. Vince Howard: …aaaaaand their opponents! The Mega Job dream team finally reach the bottom of the ramp. Steve takes his position on the outside while Beef points at Bonafide and DUI in a Hulk Hogan style-e. Eventually Janito manages to convince his partner to enter the ring. Vince Howard: Hailing from Beefville, City of the Annoyed! Weighing in at a combined weight of 380lbs… in the ring… the team of EL JANITO and BEEF… MEEEEGGGAAAAAA JJJOOOOOOOOOBBBB! A chunky, slightly off-kilter guitar riff draws the first negative reaction of the evening as "Hail Destroyer" by Cancer Bats begins to play. "TEAR US DOWN!" The rhythm section kicks in with all the subtlety of a hang grenade. Jason Natas throws the curtains to the side and strides out onto the ramp, sneering at jeering crowd as they rain their bile down upon him. He wastes little time in beginning his descent and is soon approaching the ring with giant strides. Vince Howard: …aaaand finally! Their tag team partner, from New York City! Weighing in at 254lbs… he is THE-ANTI-SUPERSTAR… JASSSSOOOOON NNNNNAAAAAATTTTTTTTAAAAAAASSSSS! Jason sprints the final few feet to the ring and glides in under the bottom rope. Before his music even begins to fade he jumps and thrusts his way through El Janito and Beef, furiously throwing fists at DUI and Bonafide! Nick: And here we go! Richard: Looks like Jason Natas couldn’t wait for the bell! The DUI/Bonafide combination seem to struggle at first, taken aback by the sudden assault from the Big New Yorker, but soon enough the numbers game comes into play and they overpower the Anti-Superstar. Mega Job arrive late to the party and dive into the fight, Janito and Beef taking on Cobb and Korver respectively as Natas tackles Bonafide. Nick: This is carnage, Richard! Richard: The bell hasn’t even rung and already these six men are taking lumps out of each other! Nick: With the bad blood between these two we knew that this one wasn’t going to be pretty, but this is just out of control! The referee attempts to break it up but stands no chance against the six angry competitor. As certain individuals begin to take the advantage (note: NOT Mega Job), a veritable smorgasbord of referees and officials charge into the ring, dragging Cobb, Korver, Beef and El Janito away from each other and towards their respective corners. DING DING DING! Nick: And FINALLY we get the bell! Jason Natas refuses to let up his assault on Jimmy Bonafide, who he has backed up against the ropes. After throwing a couple of stiff shots into Bonafide’s gut, Natas grabs his fellow New Yorker by the arm and Irish Whips him across the ring, taking him down with a drop toe hold on the rebound! He flips Jimmy onto his back, before standing up and dropping his fist right into The PosterBoy’s face. Natas remains grounded and pulls Bonafide up to a seated position, locking in a rear naked choke. Richard: Good start from my man Jason Natas here! He’s taken Jimmy Bonafide down a peg, and now he’s choking the life out of him! Nick: But wait – PosterBoy’s fighting back! Indeed he is. Bonafide tenses his arm muscles and throws his elbow backwards into Natas’s side with such force that The Anti-Superstar recoils immediately, clutching his torso in pain. The agile Bonafide springs up to his feet, runs across the ring and propels himself back at Jason Natas, only to walk right into a discus clothesline! Richard: You speak too soon, Nick! Nick: And did you feel the force on that clothesline? Natas thrusting that big tree-trunk arm into Bonafide’s windpipe! Richard: Yeah, something tells me that ol’ Jimbo’s gonna be a little hoarse for the next few days! Bonafide shakes his head, trying to shrug off the grogginess. He’s struggling, though, and soon enough Natas hauls him to his feet, tearing his red bandanna from his head in the process! Jason hauls his opponent across to the corner and violently thrusts Jimmy’s head down into the turnbuckle. The grip of the big New Yorker is the only thing stopping Jimmy Bonafide from hitting the floor and soon enough Jason has Bonafide turned around, slumped in the corner, helpless to avoid the three strong shoulder thrusts to the gut! Nick: He sure is on fire tonight, Richard! Richard: Having Beef and El Janito as your tag team partners will do that to a man. Nick: Jason knows that if he’s going to win tonight he’s probably going to have to do it alone! He simply cannot let the frankly incompetent Mega Job combination get too much ring time if he wants to get one over on his rivals! Richard: And if there’s one man on the roster that can pull this one off, it’s Jason Natas. This guy is as tough as old boots, Nick, and he boasts one of the meanest right hooks in the business. What he may lack in technical fluidity and grace he more than makes up for in terms of pure unrelenting aggression. Nick: But let’s remember that Jimmy Bonafide is no slouch himself! This guy comes from a very credible background and has proven on more than one occasion that he is a handful inside the ring. He can cause Jason Natas some problems here… By now Jimmy Bonafide sits in the bottom turnbuckle. Natas takes a couple of steps backwards before charging forward, burying his knee around The PosterBoy’s facial features. But Jason does not yield; he pulls Bonafide up and brings him in close to the centre of the ring. With one hand he grabs Jimmy by the shoulder, pulling him forward, while with the other he viciously smashes his palm into Bonafide’s nose! Natas drops down to cover as Bonafide hits the canvas. Richard: Wham! Natas hooks the leg… …1! …2! Shoulder up! Nick: Quick kick out from Bonafide there. The strike from Natas was certainly forceful, but it’s going to take more than that to put The PosterBoy away! Richard: At this rate Bonafide might need to call in the plastic surgeon after this one! That’s two high impact blows to the face he’s taken in quick succession. Nick: Yeah, certainly not ideal for a man who calls himself "The PosterBoy"! The Anti-Superstar yanks Bonafide up again, this time backing him into his home corner. He makes an attempt to lash out at his adversary with a body kick, but suddenly a hand slaps down upon his shoulder… Nick: Uh-oh! Richard: He didn’t… did he? Fuming, Natas turns around as Beef enters the ring and flexes his muscles. Nick: He did! Beef just blind-tagged Jason Natas! Richard: And look at The Anti-Superstar, he’s pissed! Jason mouths the words "what the fuck?" and shakes his head, stalking menacingly towards Beef. However he is soon reprimanded by the referee who forces him out of the ring. Nick: Jason Natas did not want to be tagged out there, and who can blame him! He’d made a good start here against Jimmy Bonafide and now that’s all going to be shot to hell as Beef enters the fracas. Unfortunately for Beef, the distraction seems to have allowed Bonafide ample time to recover, and The PosterBoy swats away the Beefster’s attempted jab. Brushing Beef’s fist aside, Bonafide knees him in the gut before taking him to the floor. With Beef seated, Jimmy pulls his foot backwards before slamming it painfully into Beef’s kidneys, garnering a pop from the crowd! Nick: Well I guess Jimmy Bonafide lucked out with that one. He’d taken quite the beating from Jason Natas, and that little distraction has given him the time he needs to compose himself. Richard: Typical Mega Job! Messing everything up. God, why can’t they just die already? Nick: … Jesus, Richard. That’s a bit harsh, even for Mega Job… Richard: Nothing is too harsh when it comes to Mega Job. Trust me. Beef arches forward onto his hands and knees, trying to absorb some of the pain, but Jimmy Bonafide comes right back at him with a falling dropkick to the face, sending Beef rolling onto his back. Nick: And now Bonafide with the cover! …1! …2! No! Beef kicks out. Nick: That was a little too close for comfort. With Beef seemingly under his thumb Bonafide abandons his foe and walks over to the other side of the ring, slapping hands to the eager Colby Korver. Nick: Here comes Korver! Let’s see what the Frat Boy can do… Korver walks over towards Beef, who has now made it back up to his feet. Beef notices his opponent stopping dead in his tracks before him, so he flexes one of his arms, brings it up towards his face, and kisses the miniscule muscle that pops up. Richard: What. The hell. Is he doing? Colby Korver can’t help but laugh as Jason Natas slaps his forehead on the outside of the ring. The festivities don’t last for long, however, as Korver soon charges forward and decapitates (not literally, of course) Beef with a clothesline. Nick: Looks like Colby Korver has seen enough! Richard: Good, knock that Mega Job idiot on his ass! Nick: Wait, I thought you wanted Natas’s team to win? Richard: I do… but I wouldn’t mind seeing these two morons getting their asses whipped in the process! Almost completely no-selling the move, Beef pops back up, only to be levelled with a swift, snap STO from Korver who makes the cover. …1! …2! …aaaaand Beef with the shoulder up! Colby goes to work, standing up and slamming his boot down into Beef’s chest a couple of times for good measure. He follows up with an elbow drop, before bringing the Mega Job member back to his feet and putting him in a hammerlock. The hold is soon aborted, however, as Korver chooses to wrap his arms around Beef’s waist, before pulling him upwards and dropping him right down on his head with a back drop driver! Richard: Big, high impact move from the Frat Boy there, dropping Beef right on his head! Nick: You’ve gotta give these guys some credit. They might not be the flashiest tag team on the roster but Colby Korver and Hank Cobb can really fight! Richard: Like Bonafide they come from a pretty credible background. The thing that worries me though, Nick, is their seemingly haphazard approach to their careers. Nick: It can certainly be said that DUI try to enjoy life to the fullest here in PRIME, but I think it’s a little bit unfair to dismiss them as being a couple of drunks. They’ve shown over the past few weeks that when fired up, there’s not much that can stand in their way. And right now, Beef is in the firing line… Instead of making the pinfall attempt, Korver hauls Beef upwards again, this time whipping him off the ropes. On the rebound, Korver catches Beef and pulls him onto his shoulder into a fireman’s carry position that is soon dropped into a double knee gutbuster! Korver makes the cover… …1! …2! ... but at the very last second Jason Natas makes the save! Nick: Natas breaks it up! Richard: Smart thinking from Jase there… I thought Beef was done! Natas shakes his head as he strolls leisurely from the ring, ignoring the crude insults thrown at him from Colby Korver. The Frat Boy again tries to yank Beef upwards… but these time gets a kick in the nuts for his troubles! Richard: Wait a minute… did I just see what I think I did? Nick: Huh? Richard: Mega Job getting some offence in! Somebody call Jesus; the world’s about to end! Korver staggers forward, winded by the blow. He slaps the outstretched hand of his partner, Hank Cobb, as Beef simultaneously tags El Janito in, much to the chagrin of Jason Natas who stomps his foot into the apron through frustration of not being tagged in. Rather surprisingly it is Janito who gets the first shots in, slugging the giant Cobb in the gut a couple of times before bouncing off of the ropes. His luck quickly runs out, though, as Cobb catches the attempted cross body and tosses him backwards effortlessly with a fallaway slam! Nick: Wow! Would you look at the power of Hank Cobb! Richard: Haha, he just swatted El Janito like a fly! Nick: At 6’8" and almost 350lbs, Hank Cobb is quite clearly the powerhouse of Delta Upsilon Iota! And he just plucked El Janito out of thin air there… I don’t see how the Mega Job member can stand any chance against this behemoth. It seems that El Janito landed awkwardly as he arches his back, screaming out in pain. Hank Cobb shows no sympathy for his opponent’s plight and hauls him back up with one hand, before throwing him into the corner. After building up to the move for a good few seconds, Cobb finally unleashes an almighty knife-edge chop across Janito’s chest, the force of which causing many in attendance to cringe. Richard: Man, El Janito is getting SQUASHED! Nick: Massive impact from that chop! The miniscule El Janito is no match for Hank Cobb right now… Richard: Jason Natas needs to get in the ring, and quickly! DUI are taking Mega Job to pieces… this thing’s going to be over if The Anti-Superstar doesn’t get involved, Nick! Janito wails again and soon finds himself planted as Cobb grabs him by the throat and tosses him halfway across the ring. Jason Natas, by now, is absolutely livid, almost pulling his hair out thanks to the ineptitude of his tag team partner. Beef, meanwhile, as clapping and shouting, trying to stir El Janito into a comeback as Hank Cobb pulls him up by the scruff of the neck and smiles at Jason Natas. Nick: And look at Hank Cobb, teasing, mocking the big New Yorker! He knows that the match is his for the taking if he can keep Mega Job isolated inside the ring, and he knows that Jason Natas knows it, more importantly! Richard: Hank Cobb better watch out though. I’m not sure if inciting Jason Natas further is a good idea; the guy’s a ball of fire… he doesn’t need an excuse to go medieval on your ass, but Hank Cobb is giving him a pretty good one right here. Cobb grabs both of El Janito’s arms and pulls him up, only to slam him down with a giant Full Nelson Slam. He makes the cover… Nick: What impact! Richard: *groans* It’s over. …1! …2! …3? NO! El Janito kicks out! Nick: What?! How on earth did El Janito kick out from that one? Jason breathes a sigh of relief as his eyes again meet with Hank Cobb’s. The southerner points at Natas, motioning him to bring it on as he pulls Janito upwards again. Richard: Looks like big Hank Cobb wants to get him some of Jason Natas! He tosses El Janito towards the corner… but the tag that’s made isn’t the one he was looking for, as Beef suddenly re-enters the fray. Richard: Awwww hell! Not again! Nick: Folks, Jason Natas is FUMING! Hank Cobb just tossed El Janito to the side with hope of getting a piece of Natas, but it was Beef that made the tag! This one isn’t going to end well… Cobb shrugs as Beef walks towards him. The DUI member throws a fist into Beef’s jaw, but Beef once again completely ignores the blow. He walks away, shaking his head like a rabid dog, before turning and thrusting his finger at Cobb’s face. Beef: YOU! Beef counters the next blow, blocking it with his forearm, before slapping Cobb in the chest. Richard: … what is going on here?! Beef bounces off the ropes, raising his foot up in the air… … but the attempted Big Boot bounces off of Hank Cobb’s barrel chest, sending Beef falling backwards onto his ass. Beef seemingly fails to notice this, however, and attempts the leg drop anyway… even though Hank Cobb is standing above him, laughing at his hopeless opponent. Nick: Well, that was stupid. Richard: What do you expect… it’s Mega Job! Nick: I know, but jeesh, that was pushing the envelope even for Beef! Cobb stomps the back of Beef’s head before picking him up and dragging him across the ring. He slaps hands with Bonafide and the PosterBoy enters the ring, jabbing the Mega Job member in the face a couple of times, before flattening him with a rana. Bonafide makes the cover… …1! …2! No! Beef kicks out. Nick: For every minute that Mega Job spend in the ring you can feel this one slipping away from Jason Natas’s grasp! The New Yorker has had to watch on helplessly as his partners have taken one hell of a beating here, and DUI/Bonafide don’t look like letting up just yet! Bonafide pulls Beef upwards. He dazes him with an eye rake, before turning, leaping up, and springboarding from the middle rope with a dropkick that sends flashbulbs popping around the arena. He makes another cover. …1! …2! Broken up by Natas! Richard: FINALLY Natas makes his presence felt, hauling Bonafide up! But no sooner has Jason pulled Jimmy up than he has dropped him again with a gigantic European uppercut! To hales of boos Jason stomps away on Bonafide who rolls out of the ring, looking for a breather. Natas follows him out and continues the assault, clubbing him in the back before kneeing him in the jaw. Nick: And the action goes outside as it looks like Jason Natas has finally seen enough! Richard: But wait! Here comes Korver… WHAM! Korver absolutely FLATTENS Jason Natas, catching him off-guard with the clothesline. As Beef gets back up in the ring, Korver pulls Bonafide, the legal man, up, and slides him under the bottom rope. Nick: Korver evens up the odds by taking Natas out there. Richard: God-damn it, just when it looked like the tide was going to turn, Colby Korver has to go and ruin everything! On the outside Natas soon recovers from the clothesline and hops back up onto the ring apron. He briefly ponders attacking DUI, but decides against it for fear of disqualification. Meanwhile, Beef actually as the chance to mount some offence and takes it by mounting Jimmy Bonafide and punching him in the jaw a couple of times. Nick: Jimmy Bonafide has some work to do here if he wants to get back in the match. Richard: Yeah, he really got clocked by Natas on the outside there. Nick: This guy is certainly a capable athlete and he’s looked good thus far here tonight. He’s proven to us in his matches with Union Jack that he can really take it up a notch when he needs to, but right now he’s getting hammered away on by Beef… Beef runs against the ropes, this time nailing the leg drop. Ladies and Gentlemen, brace yourselves. Something truly insane is about to go down. Yeah, I can’t believe I’m typing this either… Beef makes the cover. …1! NO! Easy kickout from Bonafide! Richard: What, did Beef actually think he had a shot of winning this?! Beef pulls Jimmy Bonafide upwards and takes him towards the corner where the outstretched hands of Jason Natas and El Janito await. He reaches for Janito again, but his slap only connects with thin air as Natas elbows Janito in the face and makes the tag for himself! Richard: YES! Natas is in! AT LAST! Nick: He had to take out his own partner to get there, Richard, but Jason Natas is FINALLY back in this match! He’s been chomping at the bit for at least ten minutes now… Richard: Time for Jimmy Bonafide to get flattened! Natas first drags Bonafide away from the corner, just in case Beef tries anything. He knees him in the gut, sets him up in the centre of the ring by throwing his head under his arm and grabbing his belt, before pulling him backwards. NEW YORK MINUTE! Richard: That’s IT! Nick: Natas with the big finisher! …1! …2! … NO! Hank Cobb stomps Natas on the back, breaking the pinfall! Nick: And just when it looked like Jason Natas had this won, Hank Cobb breaks it up! The move is, of course, enough to incite Jason Natas further. Natas hops up, looking to get a piece of Hank Cobb, and begins to wildly throw his fists into the big man’s face! Cobb fights back, but not before Colby Korver enters the ring, making a two-on-one situation. Nick: Now DUI with the double team… Natas struggles with the power of the two Frat Boys and is soon the victim of a double Irish whip. But suddenly, out of nowhere, El Janito flies from the top rope, catching Hank Cobb with a missile dropkick as Beef spears Colby Korver to the mat! Richard: Wait, did Mega Job just do something USEFUL?! Nick: Looks like it! Jason seems more surprised than anyone else. He can hardly believe his eyes as Mega Job share a high five over their fallen adversaries. Not that it improves Natas’s opinion of them any, as he soon is in their facing, ordering them out of the ring. Nick: And now Jason Natas just wants to get on with this, Richard! Mega Job being to leave the ring, however, Natas suddenly catches the sight of Bonafide stirring out of the corner of his eye. He grabs Beef by the neck in the nick of time, tossing him right into Jimmy Bonafide’s flying boot as The PosterBoy connects with the superkick! Richard: Bwahahaha! Nick: Natas just sacrificed Beef for his own gain! Natas grabs Janito by the shirt as Bonafide stands over Beef, looking confused. He turns around, but it’s too late as Natas tosses the micro luchadore at Bonafide, knocking the PosterBoy off his feet. El Janito tries to make the cover, but Natas violently yanks him backwards, breaking the attempt. Richard: That was awesome!
