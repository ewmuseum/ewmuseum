Biography The following isn’t a match-writing aide so much as a character-writing aid. I don’t mind if anybody would like to use these characters for jobber fodder in tag matches, however ASK ME FIRST. They’ll be the Mean Street Posse to his Shane McMahon or if you want a better comparison, the Patterson/Briscoe stooges to his Vince McMahon. They’re a couple of posers from Fife, Washington, [detail withheld]. Here’s some information about each member of the FAM that you may find useful. The story of the Fife-Ass Mofos has origins that stretched all the way back to the mid-2006 era. Chet Rockmore and Mike Guttormson were a couple of wrestling students from places in North Carolina and South Carolina, respectively. Both had a fair amount of talent, but had been toiling away in the PRIME Wrestling Academy while talent like Alexander Kannon, Sun Tzu, and others surpassed them. Tired of it, they sought their big break and the call finally came. Sonny Silver had put out a good word for two people – any two people – to play his thugs. Nobody else had wanted to step in, considering playing a lackey hadn’t always worked out for some. Along came Chet and Mike. With management not opening their eyes, they figured "why the hell not?" Chet Rockmore and Mike Guttormson became brothers Slash McCoy and Burnie McCoy, old friends of Sonny’s from Washington. Despite the fact that A) Chet is a black man and Mike is a white man, B) They’ve never been to Washington, and C) they’d never met Sonny before in their frickin’ life, they took the job and now have been chose to safeguard a fake Chairman from certain destruction. Weep for them. Wrestling Style Slash McCoy = Aggressive Mat Technician.
