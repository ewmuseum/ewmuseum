The parking lot was full of various models of parked cars, but not a single sign of human life. A long black limo crept in and started its way towards the back of the arena. Relaxing in the backseat of said limo was the man known to fans as Toshiaki Motoki, the supposed exiled former dictator of PRIME. With Hin See’s passing, nothing stood in his way of returning. Motoki’s appearance on Revolution 87 gave evidence to that being the truth. Motoki closed his eyes and relaxed on the leather interior of the limo, putting his feet up on the seat. He was living the good life that he had grown accustomed to. “We’re here, Mr. Motoki,” the driver announced. “That’s fine,” he replied calmly. “However, I am in no rush to go inside yet. The driver just sat back in his chair, surprised beyond belief. Before his very eyes was the rogue ninja known as Xin Xin Xiong. There was a smirk on the masked man’s face, as he gazed into the windshield from a three-point stance. “Uh... sir...” the driver said softly. “There is a man on the hood.” “What?” Motoki asked, not opening his eyes. “There is a man...” he tried to reply. Before the driver could finish, X3 darted to the roof of the car and with one quick strike, drove his bo through the moon roof. Motoki’s eyes burst open and he jumped back in shock. Without warning, the man in the Kato mask shattered the back window and the two windows on each side of Motoki with the wooden weapon. Glass flew in all directions. Shards soon littered the floor of the limo and covered Toshiaki, from head to toe. Xiong leapt from the top of the car and landed soundly on the trunk, making the entire vehicle quake. “What the hell?” Motoki exclaimed pulling his arms down from their protective placement in front of his face. “Come to poppa,” X3 grinned evilly, as he gripped Motoki by his hair and dragged him halfway out of the back window. “Holy shit... it can’t be you,” Toshiaki said loudly, seeing his attacker for the first time. “You aren’t cleared to wrestle in America anymore! You were banned after trying to purposefully hurt those two men at a house show for AWC!” “Isn’t it funny how being banned doesn’t last that long,” X3 smirked and placed his boot on Motoki’s chest. “Now, I don’t know why you are here and really don’t give a flying fuck either. All I know is that you are going to be one dead fucktard, if I ever see you around here again. You got that?” “Go to...” Motoki growled and was slapped squarely in the head with the bo. “You really aren’t splitting atoms down there are you, Einstein?” Xiong asked mockingly before smacking Motoki again. “Stay the fuck away from PRIME or I am going to rip out your skull and use it as a fucking rice bowl. You feel me?” Before Toshiaki can respond, X3 walloped him in the head once more with the bo, knocking him out cold. The ninja casually hopped off the truck of the car and the driver met him. “You going to try something with those pseudopods of yours, lard ass?” X3 quizzed the portly black man before tapping his gut with his bo and knocking off his hat to which the driver just backed away. “That’s probably the smartest fucking decision you’ve ever made.” Xiong lurched toward the driver causing him to jump back before flipping his black and crimson hair away from his mask.
