ReVolution 97 Nassau Coliseum ---- Welcome To The Warzone "This way, Miss." If the wrestlers' entrance at the beginning of ReVolution 97 could be summed up in one word, it would be FORTIFIED (caps and bold inclusive). Ranks of security mimicked Roman Legions in formation, sporting state-of-the-art body armour, riot equipment, tear-gas, and batons. Decked out in black from head to foot, there's more than a hint of the imperial to them. Some eager men slap their batons against their gloved palms, not feeling their shift would have been worth it if they didn't get to at least beat a few from a rowdy mob into unconsciousness. Arriving just in time for opening bell, Karina Wolfenden displays her ID for the 3rd time. And, just like the previous two times, she has to explain why her hair is so different to that in the photo. Security: Proceed to checkpoint Delta. She thinks the whole thing is less verging on the ridiculous, than tumbling down it at a fair rate of knots. Naturally, the viewers can't see her thoughts, but the facial expression pretty much clues them in. Mocking the stuffy protocol, she salutes the guard, before striding across the 15 or so feet to the next mass of body-armoured personnel. Security: Documentation, please. Finally emptying out her pockets of all the triplicate paperwork that PRIME employees were advised to bring, Karina has the doors to the arena opened to her at last. But where military was the watchword before, she steps into the realm of bureaucracy. A map of the arena's backstage is drawn on a large flipchart, color coded to show those areas the general roster have access to. Directing operations, Blaine Blair has already discarded his jacket, and his shirt is stuck to his wiry frame with sweat. Pointing off in all directions, he somehow musters up the authoritative tone to boss around the security and production crew. Conversing with a couple of PRIME roster members, Blaine refers them to the map, and the one corridor coloured in green, and imaginatively labelled 'the corridor'. PRIME newcomer, Jonathan Winters, hangs back from the crowd of other members of the roster, clearly not wanting to be apart of anything that might involve conversing with anyone else. He is leant against the back wall with a completely placid expression liberally spread across his face, then, glancing at the numerous security flapping around the place and the general sense of chaos that is clearly enveloping the entire scene, he allows a smile and a brief shake of his head. Karina is led near and, around Winters' person, shoots him a sympathetic look. Karina Wolfenden: I know. Lame, huh? You thinking this isn't exactly what you signed up for either?
