ReVolution 199 ---- There's A New Sheriff In Town Earlier Tonight We open in a large, all-purpose room somewhere within the IZOD Center in East Rutherford, New Jersey. The conference room is filled to the gills with people, standing room only in fact. From the ring crew, to the production staff, to the talent roster, everyone is present. It’s not often that we have an ‘All Hands on Deck’ meeting before a show, but tonight that’s exactly what we have. Getting the wrestlers together in a confined space, with all the ego’s and testosterone running wild, is generally a Molotov cocktail just waiting to be lit. It’s even more dangerous as we lead up to the biggest pay per view of the year, Colossus VI. Almost the entire roster is present. Chandler Tsonda and Brian Dawkins huddle on one side of the room, shooting the breeze.
