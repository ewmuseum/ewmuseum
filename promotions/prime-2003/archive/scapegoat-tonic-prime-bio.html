Automobiles. The travel to a brighter day never seemed to fade away, no matter how hard Jeremy tried. He would hear of possible promise at a gym six-hundred miles away and become an overflowing popcorn tub of punctuality. He was one of those severely committed fella's who never seemed to glob through a web of distractions, He understood success and the way of life and how fast it can go at times. Jeremy slowed it down and cut out the unfortunate events of today, the hopeless romance of those sharing loneliness in the night, the evil dread that eats loneliness, and those who find themselves trapped within it. He took away the dispair, the obsolete, the unfinished and the disrespected and reformed his own establishment that had a mold of perfection tinting it from the dirge of society. He walked and walked through this paradise city and found himself on multiple occasions. He read himself like a book and carried out numerous acts of what would've been and what could've been. He traveled to different planets, saw his wife, kids and grandchildren. He ventured to a retirement center where he saw the recliner that would consume him sometime later. It was only seconds later that he found himself amidst thousands of stone structures, devoting themselves in memoir to those we've lost through time. His tombstone lay sunk amongst grass and soil and the date of his death blurred off. This appeared to be a message to Jeremy. A message that sent him over the edge. See, his dream was to entertain the world with true atheticism. His goal in life was to succeed in everything humanly possible. He wanted to be known as a great citizen and on top of all of this, he wanted to be a wrestler, and not just any wrestler. He wanted to be famous, he wanted to be critically claimed and honored. He would devote every drop of sweat in him to surrender to his ambition. This dream started when he was thirteen. Jeremy Lemon is now thirty years of age. He wrestles independently to random school events and is known to those in attendance as Scapegoat Tonic. Retreived through three - hidden - sources of general interest, the name stuck to Jeremy like a best-friend. It signified the thickness of piffle that he had to swim through to get where he was, and this wasn't even the crumbs falling from the cake. He was barely being paid enough to live off of and would, at times, go up to two days without a single bite to eat through matches. He didn't care. This is Jeremy Lemon's dream. This is Scapegoat Tonic's dream and he never thought he could ever be this fortunate again -- That was until his birthday. A special message shot into Jeremy's answer machine and a cooling chill ran like a tapeworm down his spine. Primetime was here and he managed to get there without losing a limb or breaking his neck in the process? Not bad... not bad at all. With Love. ScapeGoat2005.
