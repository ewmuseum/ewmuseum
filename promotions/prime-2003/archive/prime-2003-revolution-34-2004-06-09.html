Visiting Hours The monotonous beeping of the machinery around Taun Phams bedside had began to irritate his visitor who had been present in the room for at least twenty minutes by now. Pham was still comatose as a result of his inadvertent fall at End Game, his body's basic functions now being performed by the equipment around him. The Doctors could give no accurate predictions on when Pham would regain consciousness or even whether he would at all. Currently he lay in his state of vegetation, with the moonlight slithering through the blinds and illuminating the contours of his body as the time approached eleven o'clock on the night of Monday June the 7th. The rain was now steadily lapping at the glass as the visitor took up a position by the window, peering through the blinds to observe the situation outside of this sterilised environment. With a genuinely somber look on his face, Toshiaki Motoki turned away from the window to stand next to the bed, casting his shadow over Pham. Toshiaki Motoki: Rough night eh? No answer, not the Motoki expected one. Toshiaki Motoki: I was doing some research on your condition in my office suite last week in Pittsburgh.
