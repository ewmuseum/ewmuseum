ReVolution 93 ---- Just the Messenger Fury accompanied anticipation, breeding a firm kick that shoots open the locker room door of one Karina Wolfenden. Needless to say, that kick came as a result of the former Universal Champion herself, and her desire to get through yet another tedious night...at least to the point where her attacker would finally be unveiled. Her fiery pupils guides her to the inside of the room where she proceeds to toss her duffel bag into the nearest corner, flopping down on the wooden bench below. Rarely did she feel such anxiety, and unfamiliarity with the situation kept her on the verge, clenching her fist tightly, ready to explode at any given moment. Patience would be the virtue, though... Because in due time, her moment of reckoning would come. "Notice for a Misses Wolfenden." The stern voice yanks Karina out of her mellow train of thought, awakening her to the sight of a brown-clad messenger in the doorway of her locker room with a letter in hand. Without so much as a mumbling word, Karina extends her arm signaling for him to give her the letter. Detecting the value of her mood, he does so accordingly. But then he makes the same mistake that all messengers make... He extends his hand for a tip. One sharp glance courtesy of the K-Wolf, though, served as enough to force the messenger to lower his hand before continuing on his route. With him out of sight, the K-Wolf turns her attention towards the envelope in her hand. With no regards for securing the envelope, she rips it open and retrieves the contents which come in the form a single letter. With certainty, she reads through... Those who produce great fruits must first produce great labor. Tonight will witness your final test in your quest of conquering the fear in which I have placed before you. My identity is what you seek, and so is what you shall receive...but to receive such, you must put your wit to it's test. Proper solving of this riddle will lead you to my location. "Behind every great man is a great woman... And the Woman's role is which I play... Atleast in a Man's perspective." Good Luck And with that comes a furious anticipation that results in the crumpling of the letter And a spark in the K-Wolf. One month of battering and Psychological warfare had led her to this moment. A fight was what her attacker wanted from her... Killean didn’t figure he needed luck because it was only Clyde Walkins. But he went along with the idea anyways. Figuring it would only be a few choice people, he went to open the door. Killean was unfortunately being tied up by a couple of other individuals who said they knew Tyler Nelson. The PRIME Choice really didn’t want to mingle with “businessmen” today but he was literally pulled from the conversation. ‘Be calm…’ he told himself. ‘They are businessmen with Nelson… fuckin’ jerks in their monkey suits.’ Charity Manale: So girl, you happen to see anyone else you knew here? Killean turned to speak with Charity quickly, ignoring what the businessmen were saying. Charity Manale: Tess and I are going to catch up on old times. We’ll be back around later. Thirty seconds of waiting into a minute…a minute turns into a minute and thirty seconds…and before long, that minute and thirty seconds has escalated into two minutes. Not great TV, admittedly. That's when she begins to feel her fists slowly unclenching and her limbs slowly releasing themselves. Realization began to set in… Had he chickened out? Or had she just been made out as a fool? As she turned to walk away from the illuminated spot, she heard a sharp clinck sound behind her. She quickly jerks around expecting to find her attacker, but as she looks down at the pavement…all she spots is a lead pipe. A lead pipe with a note attached to it. Taking a moment to glance around her to see just who had dropped the pipe, she finds no one. In finding no one, she cautiously proceeds to pick up the lead pipe, snatching off the note to read its contents. As she glances over the note, she realizes that the note is a replica of the note she received earlier in the locker room that led her to this location. The only difference, though, was that this time the words at our original and latter destination were bolded. That's when it hit her. Their latter destination was last week right here in this parking lot…but their original destination was four weeks ago when he held her captive inside the trunk of a black vehicle. Suddenly, the roar of a vehicle from behind her grows gradually louder as she turns around before leaping to the right, barely dodging a charging black vehicle. The vehicle stops right in the middle of the spotlight as Karina slowly tries to gather herself before pushing herself back up to her feet and snatching up the lead pipe, twirling it like a baton. Slowly, she creeps over to the driver's window of the vehicle and sends the pipe shattering through the glass with a pump-kick, desperately reaching inside to retrieve the person whom almost ran her over. She finds nothing. Bewildered by this, Karina yanks open the door and looks down at the gas pedal to find an automatic pedal controller attached. Someone had set her up big time. As she pulled herself out of the driver's doorway, she recognized that this car was the same car from four weeks ago when she was placed in the trunk. With lead pipe in hand, she slowly makes her way to the back of the vehicle, yanking open the trunk…to once again find nothing. Or so she thought. Suddenly, her breaths grow short as she felt something choking her from behind. Sure enough, from behind her is a figure dressed in all black, choking the life out of Karina Wolfenden with a lead pipe of his own. "Did you really think I'd disappoint, Karina? Such a Martyr as you is not worthy of disappointment, but instead of death. I have half the mind to slew you in this very parking lot, because we all know that what you really fear is death. The last unknown. We all recognize that you fear that which you cannot control…but yet which instead controls you. So is why you also fear me, Karina." As he pulls tighter, she begins to swing wildly, trying any and everything to beat the attacker off of her…but to no avail. His screeching voice continues to ramble through her ears. "People respect and honor you as some sort of fearless daredevil…but not anymore. Why? Because I have exposed you to your fears…and now even they know that their fearless leader is afraid of one thing or another. You were supposed to be an immortal in their view, but I have exposed you as a fraud. You're as mortal as the sun is bright... just like the spotlight you've stomen from so many. Say goodbye to Hollywood, Karina." As she continues to kick wildly, she struggles to murmur some words. Karina: Why-…Are-…Y-… "SILENCE! We now recognize that you fear failure…and failure is exactly what this period of time in your illustrious career has brought you. This period of time is like the menstrual cycle of your career. Need I remind you that it was during this time - Overkill - that the immortal Karina Wolfenden suffered her first loss to Killean Sirrajin, and failed her fans by prematurely losing the Universal Championship? Failure during this period of time is what you've become…failure you shall remain…for your final test in is one in which even you cannot withstand." Suddenly, she scores with a soft shot to the side of his head…but it only serves to tighten his grip on her, choking the life out of her as she turns shades of blue. "For at Overkill…the same venue in which you suffered failure for the first time, you will embark on a tedious journey. You will enter a realm in which you have never entered before…a realm in which you dare not enter again. At Overkill, you will go toe to toe with your biggest fear… ME!" Suddenly, he drops the pipe and yanks her around so that she looks directly in his battered face. And as Karina does so, her mouth drops….Her attacker was none other than…. Vangelus Olsig. Hair Cut. Earrings Attached. A totally different Vangelus Olsig than any had known before. Before she could even react, Vangelus sends a back right hand shooting across her face, knocking her down to the concrete floor below. With her back against the ground, Vangelus stands over her…holding the Intense Championship in his hands. Vangelus Olsig: This permits me to do any and everything I desire to you, Karina…and at Overkill I will do just that. For at Overkill, you will face your final challenge…in the Intense Division. This isn't your comfort zone anymore…this is the most brutal environment you'll ever know. An environment in which blood and gore is not an option…its mandatory. Where the referee can only show mercy once matches are over, by calling for a stretcher. Slowly, this new sadistic version of the Intense Champion, Vangelus Olsig, pulls himself off of Karina Wolfenden, walking away from the scene with the Intense Championship over his shoulder and his prey, The K-Wolf, in shock. As he proceeds, he quotes the question that would plague everyone's mind. "How will the Almighty K-Wolf fare in an environment about which she knows nothing... and I know more than any man should ?"
