'Jammin' Jason Bass Biography Nicknames Jam, The Muther Plucker, The Five Stringer, The Major Rager on The Five String Muther Hometown Londonderry, Massachussets Birthday 3-19-73 Height 6'9' Weight 317 Biography Jasonito Curtis was born to an American father and Puerto Rican mother in the early 1970s in the state of Massachussets. At a young age he was exposed to the sounds of Black Sabbath, Led Zeppelin, and Rush due to his father being the frontman of a local band. He became a fan of rockin’ music at that very moment. He would often sit in the back of his garage and watch his father’s band rehearse and write songs, this is where he honed his knowledge of Rock. At the age of nine he picked up a dank, old Fender Precision four-string bass his old man had lying around the garage. Inspired by the sounds and performances of John Paul Jones, Geezer Butler, and Geddy Lee, along with CCR’s Doug Clifford and Skynyrd’s Leon Wilkinson and more recently Metallica’s Cliff Burton he taught himself to play the instrument. He spent every day after school locked in his room with an old record player listening to 45s of all the great rock bands he loved; honing his skills. Through out his teen years he played with local bands that ranged from bar room blues, southern rock, jazz, and all out thrash metal inspired by the movement of Metallica and Megadeth. At the age of 17, the year 1990, he packed his bags after finishing High School and moved to New York City; home of the least appreciated thrash band, Anthrax. Taking up odd jobs to make ends meet he became active in the local club scene; watching signed and up-and-coming bands perform and even set in with a few at the world famous CBGB. For the next eight years he played with different bands and got to meet a lot of people who would soon become famous in their own rights. It was on this eighth year that something finally happened. One September night in 1998 a local band had to duck out of a performance due to a Spinal Tap Moment. The owners were fearing an all-out riot from the patrons if a band didn’t take the stage. A young, 18 year old took the stage with nothing but a beat-up Strat and offered to save the day. After a drummer and keyboardist volunteered, Jam knew what had to be done. The near 7-footer climbed the stage and offered his skills on the Fiver. That night was the first time “Double E” Eric Evans and “Jammin’” Jason Bass met and performed together. The set began with a cover of Metallica’s “Seek and Destroy” and the rest became the legacy known as Seek and Destroy – Eric and Jason – Brothers of Rock. As for the rest of the history of Seek and Destroy, Vh1 recently filmed a “Behind The Music” spotlighting the band.
