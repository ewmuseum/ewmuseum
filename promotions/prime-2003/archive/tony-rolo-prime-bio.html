Tony Rolo Biography Nicknames The Specialist, PRIME's Goldenboy Hometown Chicago, IL Birthday 9/19/1971 Height 6'3'' Weight 250 lbs Fed Titles 2x Global Champion, Tag Team Champion (w/Joey Troy) Biography Tony Rolo began his wrestling career in a few small "feeder" feds around the age of 25. At the age of 28 he got his first big break by being recruited by IcWa. It was in IcWa that "The Specialist" began to make a name for himself. It's also where he met and feuded with the legendary Duke Williams. After IcWa's collapse, he found his way into iFWc, the former sister fed to IcWa. The significance of iFWc cannot be argued as far as Rolo's history goes, seeing that this is where he met his future partner and current best friend, Joey Troy. Unfortunately, iFWc suffered the same fate as IcWa, but in a surprising twist, IcWa was resurrected. IcWa became a prominent promotion during the early years of PTC. Because of this and Rolo's run at the top of IcWa once again, he became a household name in the wrestling industry. IcWa couldn't keep it's doors opened and had to shut down for a second time. The owner's, Cecil Cassidy (Mr. Bojangles original owner) and Joshua Alexander Xian (better know as Jax) put their resources together for one final run - Eternal Wrestling. Rolo, as he did time and time before, butted heads with management and eventually helped bring down the corrupted federation along with the infamous and mysterious GlobeTech, a company which provided Jax and Cassidy with electronic chips to plant in its wrestlers to locate them at all time, but more importantly control their actions to a degree. With Eternal Wrestling being no more, Rolo was once again without a home. After some time off, he wound up on the doorstep of $oldout Wrestling. This proved to be another significant point in his career, as this is where he met Sebastian Starr, the owner of $oldout Wrestling and future co-owner of PRIME. As was the case with every promotion Rolo was a part of in the past, he stayed with them until their doors closed. This brings us to the summer of 2003 and PRIME. Sebastian Starr approached Rolo with a lucrative contract offer as soon as the final papers came through to make PRIME official. He was the first member of PRIME's roster ever. He captured the Global Title from Lance Jackson in late 2003, only to lose it soon after to one of his all-time biggest rivals, Ian English. In 2004, Tony Rolo had a long and heated feud with Ignatius Lisieux and Toshiaki Motoki. In ended at Colossus I with Rolo recapturing the Global Title from Lisieux in an epic match. Later that year, Rolo lost the Global Title to another heated rival, Black Angel. Not long after, in early 2005 he was framed for the murder of Hin See. He was released and eventually returned to PRIME to seek answers to why he had moments of blackouts and uncontrollable fits of rage. He was suspecting GlobeTech to be active once again and seeking revenge for him bringing them down years ago. Unfortunately, due to family problems, Rolo retired from PRIME. Enter the present - Rolo returns at Culture Shock in 2008 for the Dual Halo. It's time to write another chapter in the history of PRIME's first Hall of Famer - and possibly his last.
