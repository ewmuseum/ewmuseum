Biography Ellis Easton was born to wealthy parents, his mother a film producer while his father is in Congress. Ellis attended Harvard for a year or so before transferring to NYU to attend film school. His higher education was on-again, off-again. It's unclear if he actually graduated. Ellis works as a model and sometime actor while living mainly off of checks from his seperated parents. A natural athlete and a gym rat, Ellis has decided that wrestling is this year's yoga. Whatever that means. Ellis Easton is shallow, vapid, and celebrity-obsessed. Some would say he lacks heart and soul. This is more true than it seems. Ellis is devoid of morality or human compassion. For him, going from being a superficial sycophant to a ruthless borderline psychopath requires no thought whatsoever, as does most of his life. Since joining PRIME, Ellis Easton has not bothered to hide his disdain for the industry he is using as a springboard to stardom. He degrades both the fans and his fellow wrestlers. Ellis has even admitting to only watching his own matches and regards PRIME's championships as being only slightly better than carnival prizes. The target of most of his venom has been Aimz. Ellis finds her daredevil attitude repulsive, although the true cause could be envy over the attention the Red Raver receives. So far they have exchanged victories with neither one up on the other. But Ellis Easton has made it clear that this animosity will not end until he proves who the true rising star of PRIME is. Entrance: The interior of the arena grows dark. Eyes hidden behind sunglasses, he makes his way to the ring like he was on a catwalk, Ellis ignores the fans, not even looking at them. He goes to the middle of the ring and stops, looking at the thousands of people in attendance with no trace of emotion on his chiseled features. Ellis removes his sunglasses and puts them into an inside pocket of his jacket. Then he takes the jacket off a nonchalantly tosses it to the ring announcer. Ellis pauses for a moment, then whips his pants off with one swift motion, revealing his gold tights as femimine squeals erupt for all over the arena. His lack of abilty to empathize with another human being makes him more than willing to commit acts of extreme ruthlessness without a thought. Ellis' body hasn't gone thought the injuries and wear-and-tear that the more seasoned wrestlers have contended with. His pretty boy nature and attitude could lead some to underestimate his physical capabilities and mean streak. Weaknesses: Ellis is a rookie, giving a vet a leg up.
