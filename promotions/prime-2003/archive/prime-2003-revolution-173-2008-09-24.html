ReVolution 173 ---- The Coldest Of Opens "Hartford, Connecticut." The scene opens up on those words, and the sound of a crowd of several thousand people all cheering because they recognize the voice that spoke those two words, and because, ohmaigawd, it's their town. The camera shows four individuals walking into the Hartford Civic Center in Hartford, Connecticut. One is a young man with long blonde hair and sunglasses, wearing a Celtics jersey (Kevin Garnett, #5) and a pair of jeans. The other is a young man with short black hair in a Celtics jersey (Paul Pierce, #34) and a pair of jeans. Walking close behind the two is a young woman wearing a ruffled and frilly red dress down to her ankles. Behind her was a large, bulky individual wearing the American flag over his head and over his back in the form of a cape. They are, in order of description: Simon Knox, Connor O'Reily, Miranda O'Reily, and Captain Justice. They are the Princes of New England. And we are still in Princes Country. Hence the crowd cheering their arrival.
