Culture Shock 2008 Night One ---- That Strange Feeling Of Anxiety We fade in to a greenish-white screen, a clinically sterile backdrop. It's the view under a microscope, a fact we become privy to when an eyedropper pushes into the scene and injects a small red dot of fluid into the middle of the pristine greenish-white. A voice speaks over the scene, slightly British (you know, just enough for people not to get offended) and academic in nature. "When introduced into a hostile environment, the agent experiences an averse reaction to its surroundings..." The red dot pulses and abandons its shape, seemingly under attack from its surroundings. "...a culture shock." The movement within the red dot becomes more vigorous, and it darts around the screen, fighting off the duress. "This failure to assimilate into the new atmosphere often leads to the destruction or dehibilitation of the agent." The red dot ceases moving and remains still, slowly shrinking as it is smothered by its environment. "However, in some cases the agent turns aggressive..." The dot suddenly bursts, the red color spilling out into the rest of the screen. "...and imposes its own properties onto the environment." The greenish-white parts of the screen all turn to a deep red, and the scene begins to fade out. Before we hit black, the guttural roar of "History's Stranglers" by The Bronx erupts over the narrator, and the CULTURE SHOCK: NIGHT ONE logo flies to the center of the screen. The logo cuts to a montage of clips highlighting the night's competitors.
