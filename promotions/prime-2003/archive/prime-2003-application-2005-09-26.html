application APPLICATIONS ARE OPEN AND WILL STAY OPEN... pretty much... Currently we are looking to fill 3-4 roster spots and once those are filled, we will still be accepting applications and storing them until further spots free up. So even if the roster gets filled to capacity, you can still apply and we'll simply get back to you when we have an opening and you can take it up then or simply tell us where to go and we'll pick the next most suitable applicant. Should you have any questions concerning the workings of PRIME or general advice prior to applying, feel free to fire an IM to Mat, our helpful talents relations type person at [detail withheld] First and foremost, I welcome you to the PRIME application page. Below are a few guidelines that we, the staff, feel you should understand before you submit an application. Luckily for you, I have summed these up in bullet point form for your convenience. (Yay!?) (o) Before you join PRIME, please make sure you understand and agree to the rules that have been drawn up. (o) When reviewing your application we will look at the quality of your writing. Please make sure you have filled in all of the fields listed below. We will especially look at the biography, character personality and the roleplay you submit (Please try to submit a recently written roleplay – that scores extra brownie points with us ). Furthermore please do not fill-in things such as "will write this when I'm accepted" or "this isn't my best RP, it's the only one I had on my computer that I wrote in 1998". (o) You must submit a role-play with the application. Any application, which does not include a role-play, will be over looked. We don't mind being sent direct links to RP's, but make sure they're not to boards that we can't view without registering. So, without further ado, I present to you, the application! Hurrah! To submit, cut and paste the form into to an e-mail, fill it in and send it HERE. Please title your application: Application | character name. HANDLER INFORMATION Name: E-Mail: IM: WRESTLER INFORMATION Wrestler Name: Real Name: (The characters, not yours) Allignment: (face / heel / neutral) Height: Weight: lbs Age: Weightclass: (Cruiserweight, Heavyweight or Super Heavyweight) Home Town: Wrestling Style: (All Round, Technical, Extreme, High Flyer, Brawler, Martial Arts or Powerhouse) Trademark Move(s): Description(s): Finishing Move: Description: Rare Move: (A move that is sparingly used, e.g. Brock Lesnars Shooting Star Press) Description: Past Federations: Story: Wrestlers history, gimmick and character should be shown here. (It is hard for us to run angles with a wrestler who has no gimmick or defining characteristics, etc) General Appearance: Just a fairly basic description of appearance and attire, nothing too indepth Additional Information: This section is a place where you can add any other details about your character that you feel would be helpful for us to know. It can be used to describe your characters gimmick, manager/valet details, past honours, where you think they can fit into PRIME, people you know already here, what you think you and your character can add to the fed, etc. Anything basically. ROLEPLAYING INFORMATION Sample Writing (RP): (This is essential for your application. You have to paste a RP here or, a link where I can find one of your RP's. So make them good) NOTE:: Please nothing under 20 lines, anything that short won't even be looked at.
