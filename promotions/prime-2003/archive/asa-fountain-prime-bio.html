Asa Fountain Biography Nicknames "The Fro," "The Funk Doctor," "The Ayatollah of Funk and Soula," "The Sultan of Soul," "The Jesus of Jive," "The Fromaniac," "The Most Happenin' Cat in PRIME," "Froses." Hometown New Orleans, Louisiana Birthday December 3rd, 1966 Height 6'2" Weight 220 lbs. Earth, Wind and Fire - Shining Star Other Feds Five Star Wrestling Tag Team Champion (with Jesse Ramey), Old School Wrestling Tag Team Champion (with William S. Powell) Other Fed Awards Primetime Wrestling Academy Graduate Wrestling Style Funkafied Brawling. Lots of claps, spins, dance moves and funky business. Crowd popping action. However, since Asa's now at the ripe age of forty, he's slowed down a little bit, so he's taken on a more boxer-like style. Finisher Move 'The Funkadelic' Finisher Description Asa whips the opponent off the ropes, turns away from them, sticks out his arm, catches them around the neck and drives them to the mat with a reverse DDT. It's a very fast, fluent, out-of-nowhere kind of maneuver. Finisher Setup Move Irish Whip Setup Description An Irish...Whip. Special Moves 'The Soul-Train,' 'The Ol' One-Two' Other Moves Asa's funkafied brawling style is quite a sight to behold. Lots of punching and such, more boxer-like than anything, with claps, spins, dance moves and all sorts of other debauchery thrown in the mix. Asa is the face of all faces, and thrives off of the crowd. If you need to relate this to an actual wrestler, think of The Rock. Strengths -Asa is a crowd pleaser, and when the crowd gets going, so does Asa, which results in fast, crowd popping rallies. -Asa has experience. He's been doing this for a long time, which gives him an edge over the younger fellas. -Asa has something to prove. He's been told time and time again that he's too old for the wrestling world, and that drives him. Weaknesses -Asa relies on the crowd to get him going. If they're not with them, and there's never been a case where they haven't been with him, then he can't get into his groove. -Asa has a lot of hair, which is very easy to get a good grip on. -Asa gets a little too focused on theatrics sometimes, and may not notice an attack from behind. -Asa has little to no aerial ability. He did at one time, but no longer, thanks to his age. -Asa is older. He gets winded a little faster, thanks to cigarettes and alcohol. Generally, he's not as tough as the younger bucks. Generic Maneuvers -DDT, reverse DDT, Neckbreaker, running swinging Neckbreaker, clothesline, snapmare, running clothesline, belly-to-belly suplex. Brawling Tactics -Boxing-style punches, uppercut, spear, chops, mounted punches, stomps, knees, elbows. Power Maneuvers -Spinebuster, Death Valley Driver, if the opponent is small enough. Top Rope Maneuvers -Elbow drop and legdrop. Submission Maneuvers -Sharpshooter. Weapons of Choice -The Gold Chain - The Gold Chain is a personal favorite of Asa's when it comes to the hardcore world. He'll use it to smash people in the face or choke em'. -The 'Fro Pick - Asa has a black afro-pick on him at all times, and when Asa gets real angry, he'll bust it out. He throws the thing, trying to stab the pointy pick-teeth into the opponent. This is stupidly rare. Ask for permission before using it. Signature Maneuvers -The Ol' One-Two - Asa puts the opponent in the corner, hits them with couple right hand jabs to the face, then steps back, spins around, and clobbers them in mouth with a discus punch. -The Soul Train - Asa knees the opponent in the gut, doubling them over. He then lifts an arm to the crowd, yells "whooooooooooo!," drapes his right leg over the back of the opponents head, and drops their noggin to the mat with a legdrop. Finishing Maneuvers -The Funkadelic - Asa whips the opponent off the ropes, turns away from them, sticks out his arm, catches them around the neck and drives them to the mat with a reverse DDT. The speed the opponent gains coming off the ropes causes this to be a very high angle reverse DDT, and the feeling of their head catapulting to the mat in such an abrupt manner leaves them with the notion they've been hit by a truck. It's a very fast, fluent, out-of-nowhere kind of maneuver, one to cause the crowd to erupt. Rare Maneuvers -The Stunner - On those rare, highly intense, long battle kind of moments, Asa has been known to pull out a stunner. The crowd absolutely frenzies when Asa connects. This is a pay-per-view finisher. Entrance Music 'Shining Star,' by Earth, Wind and Fire Ring Entrances A funky groove hits the speakers, and the crowd hops to their feet, knowing the arena is about to get a whole lot groovier. The Wal*Tron presenting the silhouettes of funkafied women dance on a psychedelic background. "When you wish upon a star... Your dreams will take you very far. But when you wish upon a dream... Life ain't always what it seems. What'd you see, on night clear... In the sky, so very dear?" Out of the curtain bounds Asa 'Fro' Fountain, a bright white smile on his face as he dances out to the top of the entrance ramp. "Fromania!" slams onto the Wal*Tron in green lettering, and the crowd erupts at the sight of the Sultan of Soul. "You're a shinin' star! No matta who you are! Shinin' bright to see! What you could truly be! What you could truly be!" The Funk Doctor grooves for the crowd a bit, then makes his way down the ramp, the silhouette dancing girls returning to the Wal*Tron. "Shinin' star star comes into view... To shine its watchful light on you. Gives you strength to carry on... Makes your body big n' strong. Born a man-child of the sun... Saw the work had just begun. The Fromaniac steps into the ring, makes his way to the far turnbuckle, hops onto the ropes and throws a fist in the air to the roaring crowd. "You're a shinin' star! No matta who you are! Shinin' bright to see! What you could truly be! You're a shinin' star! No matta who you are! Shinin' bright to see! What you could truly be! You're a shinin' star! No matta who you are! Shinin' bright to see! What you could truly be!" The Ayatollah of Funk' Soula hops off the turnbuckle and gets ready for action, hopping up and down and stretching his aging limbs. "Shinin' star for you to see, what your life can truly be! Shinin' star for you to see, what your life can truly be! Shinin' star for you to see, what your life can truly be!"
