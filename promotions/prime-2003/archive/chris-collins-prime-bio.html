Chris Collins Biography Nicknames 'Innovator of Offense', “The Surreal Deal”, “The Human Car Wreck”, “The King of the Impossible” Hometown Brooklyn, New York Birthday December 3rd, 1979 Height 5'8 Weight 210 Other Feds cW, ECWF, RaGE Other Fed Awards NBW Heavyweight Championship (1st), cW Classic Championship, cW Tag Championship, ECWF World Heavyweight Championship, ECWF Extreme Championship, ECWF Tag Championship, ECWF Group Championship, XCWF Junior-Heavyweight Championship, XCWF Extreme Championship Wrestling Style Cheater Finisher Move The Crash Landing Finisher Description Corner Front Flip Unprettier Finisher Setup Move High Impact (Turnaround 180 DDT planting the opponent right on the top of their head. Real High Impact.) The Tornado Whip Setup Description After being whipped into the ropes, Collins jumps onto the second rope and spin around with a Roundhouse Kick. Other Moves Style of Wrestling: Cheating. Yup, so there you have it. Chris Collins’ whole style of wrestling revolves around sly; sneaky tricks and cheating. Wrestling is in his blood so he’ll do anything to win a match. For example, sending a swift low blow to his opponent while the referee is distracted, or even playing possum after bringing an illegal weapon into the ring. He’s so manipulative and can usually swindle his way out of certain cheating situations, even after being caught. He’s a storyteller… wait no take that back. Chris is a LIAR, he’ll lie to your face. He knows it but will never admit it. ESPECIALLY, if it comes down to a match, or to protect a member of his family. He uses this as an advantage both in and out of the ring. He’ll lie to himself if he has to. You can’t trust him, and if you did you’ve made a BIG mistake. He hasn’t come to this business to make friends, he only cares about himself. Unless again if you’re a member of his family. If there is one thing we all know about Chris, it’s that he’s a family man, and he’ll do anything for them. When hearing about his cheating tactics, most would think that Chris couldn’t wrestle a lick. Those of you who may think that are very wrong. Chris has grace in the ring. He didn’t earn the names the Human Car Wreck and the King of the Impossible for nothing (“The impact from the amazing way he uses his body makes him a human car wreck”). He’s the Surreal Deal for Christ sakes! He’ll surprise you with many unique and inimitable wrestling maneuvers never before seen by the human eye. Chris is an Adrenaline junky and thrives off its rush to provide him the energy he needs to put on an outstanding worthwhile match. But moving back to the beginning, he’ll never win a match cleanly UNLESS he has a HUGE advantage over his opponent. Chris Collins is a cheater, innovator and the biggest most arrogant liar you’ll ever meet, all wrapped into one. Entrance: Match Psychology: Pinfalls: Collins puts his all into every pinfall because he feels that he has worked for it. Pinfalls will come quite often especially when Collins thinks the end is near or in desperation. Chris’ arrogant and cocky attitude will take a big effect in this area of wrestling. He’ll place his foot on you (ala Chris Jericho) and flex his muscles while the referee makes the count. Almost always, he’ll grab a bunch of his opponents tights, or place a hand or foot(feet) on the ropes to keep his opponent from kicking out. Tag Team: Chris Collins doesn’t give a shit about his partner, depending on who it is. He’s very confident in himself and will usually hog the match to himself, not tagging in his partner until he’s tired or feels like his work is done. If the tables are turned however, and his partner is hogging the match Chris will sit back laugh and watch. He’ll deny the tag of his partner, especially when he’s struggling. If he’s desperate for some action he’ll tag himself in whenever given the chance. Weaknesses: Collins is in love with high flying maneuvers. Even though they are highly effective when completed, there is also the risk of the opponent moving out of the way, getting the knee up, or intercepting him at just the last minute. This causes a weakness. Once Collins has been caught in one of these weaknesses, it is very easy for the opponent to take over the match with a submission or powerful type maneuver. Cheating is another weakness for him. Because of constant cheating, referee’s usually keeps a close eye on him. When he does decide to cheat there is a big risk of him getting caught or making an opening for the opponent to take a quick advantage. Larger Opponents: Collins stands at only five foot eight and weighs only two-hundred twenty pounds. Because of this there are many men in this sport who may be considered larger than he is. In cases like these, Collins will ALWAYS cheat his way to victory. No matter whom it is. If he’s fighting another heel, he’ll try to out-CHEAT them. High risk maneuvers will be rare, because of the risk of getting caught. Takedowns, springboards (he loves springboards) and other flashy type maneuvers will take a big affect. Smaller Opponents: If Collins is going against a smaller opponent, or one with a similar size of his; his striking and high flying abilities will come in to play the MOST. He’ll cheat like always, not taking the talent of his opponent for granted. Collins is addicted to cheating. ---------- Match Style: Without Momentum: When Collins is struggling high risk maneuvers will be very rare, BUT they could be used to get him out of very tight spots. Desperation striking attacks along with pinfalls and especially cheating (Get it now)will play a big role while struggling. With Momentum: Collins will kick things up a notch by keeping the crowd pumped(even though he's a heel he still likes to entertain) and into the action with high risk maneuvers and springboard attacks (he loves springboard attacks). With Momentum Collins will work parts of the body down such as the neck or legs to easily allow him to perform grapples. Taking Moves: Collins sells moves VERY well. He is known for putting many opponents over in his matches because of his strength in this area. He can sell even the worse manuevers. ---------- Signature Moves: World Crashing Down (Second rope springboard moonsault) (Almost always) Screaming SeaHawk (Modified Samurai Driver) Death Kneel (Seated Stunt Rider Stretch)(Submission) Shoot to Thrill (720 Diamond Dust (Rotation is Reverse Tornado DDT style) Falling Star Buster (Headbutt from top ropes.) The Decapitator (Superkick) The Catastrophe Ranas anywhere and everywhere (Always.) The Third Degree (Sting's Scorpian Death Lock) One thing to remember Chris Collins is addicted to cheating.
