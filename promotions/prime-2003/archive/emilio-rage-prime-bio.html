Finishing Move: The ROAR (Jumping Tornado DDT) Rare Move: Contra Crab (Boston Crab) Song Link http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w0bUgbbOp5w Ring Entrances The melodic verse of "Like the Angel" blares over the loudspeakers, causing PRIME-ates to momentarily set aside their bananas and stop swinging from branch-to-branch, in order to stand in raucous ovation for the approaching The Nicaraguan Nightmare. A fury of bright pyrotechnics explodes in continuity upward from the bottom of the entrance ramp to the top. The white sparks fizzle out just as a thick steam of dry ice rises to either side of the PRIME-A-Tron. The words "ETERNAL RAGE" flash in bloody red font and seemingly dripping off the screen as it fades away, replaced by the grinning countenance of Emilio Rage wearing his patented red bandana. "Viva la Revolución" flashes in electric font across his chest. Then, Emilio Rage himself steps out of the back and stands momentarily at the top of the ramp, hands on his hips. His shifts his intense brown eyes from left to right, in subtle acknowledgment of the cheering fans, before he turns his gaze downward toward the ring. He cracks his neck from side to side and jogs to the ring, slapping hands with a few fans along the way. Emilio uses his size and speed to vault over the top rope, landing with both feet on the apron to another chorus of cheers. After shadow boxing his way across the ring- evidently knocking the invisible foe out- Emilio settles back into the corner and bites his lip, staring holes through his opponent.
