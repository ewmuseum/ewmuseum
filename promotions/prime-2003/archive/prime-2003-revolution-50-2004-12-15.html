Date: 12/15/2004 Location: Skyreach Center, Edmonton, Alberta Announcers: Nick Stuart & Richard Parker It's Christmas time again! ReVolution is here and begging to be unwrapped. Tonight we have a PRIME first... the whole show has been booked at random... as opposed to the normal trend of just booking it while drunk. So grab ahold of your package and prepare to watch Playboy TV's festive offering of PRIME action... Christmas Comes Early! The Joy Of Giving Written By Sebastian It wasn't exactly Lapland, but Edmonton provided a suitable wintery setting for this festive end of year edition of ReVolution. Indeed, there is a definite cold snap in the Alberta air but instead of starting the show outside in the elements, we start ReVolution with the warming glow of a crackling fireplace. At the moment we're just focussed on the fireplace, although the sound of... I guess you could call it "merriment" can be heard off screen. As the view withdraws slowly, we now see a giant xmas tree next to the fireplace with several wrapped packages lying underneath (some of them opened) and a long table containing a varitable feast of xmas related treats such as mince pies, brandy, egg nog and xmas pudding. Despite the impression that we could be in the ideal American family home at this time of year, this scene is actually set in the VIP office/suite of PRIME co-owner Toshiaki Motoki. The illegitimate co-owner in waiting seems to be in a pretty good mood by his standards as he sips wine at his desk, drawing up the final plans for his contract signing ceremony that will end the show. Yes tonight here in Canada, Hin See will sign over the J-Tech share in PRIME that he originally held with his son Taun Pham, leaving the 45% stake of the company in the hands of his business partner Motoki... why wouldn't he be in a good mood? Hin See: Would you like to receive your present Tessa? The Asian Cowboy's voice was riddled with innuendo, as he offers his lap to the former RPW star. In the normal context of a working day, this would be a bit more disturbing. Tonight however, Hin See was dressed up as santa... a rather thin, short and Asian santa, but santa none the less. Why is Hin See dressed as santa you ask? Well if you'd have watched ReVolution three or four weeks ago, you would have known. This is the fiftieth edition of ReVolution, that's a whole forty nine other shows that have passed since PRIME kicked off with Tyler Davidson throwing Tony Rolo off of the stage back on ReVolution numero uno. Tonight in Edmonton, Christmas comes early for the PRIME lockeroom as each superstar gets to pick an xmas present from underneath the extravagent tree in Motoki's office. What's so special about that? Well each "gift" contains a match stipulation ranging from the biggest prize of Universal Title shot to a spot in a slightly absurd gimmick match. Once the superstar finds the contents, they would give the piece of paper inside to front office intern Blaine Blair who would find the number marked down and simply work out who had chosen what, then announce the matches throughout the show... just to keep the suspense level up. Miss Windsor approached Hin See, giving the Asian Cowboy a slight smile. Afterall there were still several packages underneath the tree meaning that she at least had some control over her destiny tonight, as opposed to simply opening the last few left over presents. Somewhere amongst the pile of wrapped boxes was small ticket of paper that would book one lucky superstar a Universal Title match against Black Angel. Tessa takes her position and reaches forward, selecting a large red parcel with a yellow bow around it from under the tree. Before setting about unwrapping, Tessa turns briefly to Hin See who is revelling in the moment... maybe a little too much. Tessa Windsor: (refering to the package) Is this a good choice? Hin See: Well put it this way, I don't think you'll be too disappointed. Now come on, who's next? Tessa unwraps her present as Paul Cain steps into camerashot and picks a blue and white package from under the tree. The Lethal Cocktail weighs up his choice for a moment... afterall this little festive gathering could shape the rest of his career. While he is still debating whether to stick with his present of choose another, Tessa retrieves her slip of paper and gets off of Hin See's lap in order to hand it to Blaine Blair. Pausing for a moment, Cain looks at Hin See's vacant lap and raises an eyebrow. Paul Cain: You don't seriously expect me to... Hin See: (shaking his head) I would prefer it if you didn't. This is a privilege reserved for a very small portion of the roster... Hardly disappointed, Cain decides that the package selected is good enough and shreds through the wrapping paper. He looks at the small slip of paper, holding it up to the light. Paul Cain: JC3259A2... and that's supposed to mean what exactly? Hin See: It's a code, just give the slip to Blaine over there and he'll work out what match you've selected and who you'll be facing... then it's just a case of waiting for the announcement... if you're lucky enough to receive a match that is. Turning past the buffet table, Cain approaches another desk where Blaine Blair is seated with various sheets of paper and a match planner tacked onto the wall behind him. While the intern fumbles around, our focus shifts back to Toshiaki Motoki who has briefly abandoned his ceremony plans to use the intercom. Voice of Secretary: Sir? Toshiaki Motoki: Has Nova arrived at the arena yet? Voice of Secretary: I'll check... Motoki sits on the edge of the desk next to the speaker, waiting. Voice of Secretary: Yes, sir. He's headed to the entrance now. Toshiaki Motoki: Have security escort him to my office, please. Voice of Secretary: Yes sir. Is that all Mr Motoki? Toshiaki Motoki: Actually no, is Karina Wolfenden here yet? Voice of Secretary: I don't think so sir, but I can check now if you'd like? Toshiaki Motoki: No don't worry, I heard that her time keeping isn't always one of her more prominent attributes... nevermind, it's not urgent... just make sure Nova is dealt with. Motoki terminates the conversation, a smile creeping over his face as he reaches across towards a plate containing his remaining mince pie. Before he can grab the festive treat, a furry little head bobs downwards and decides to sample it first. Mr Bojangles slowly rakes his tongue across the surface of the pie, licking off a furrow of the icing sugar. Cecil Cassidy's pet then looks up at Motoki and meows. Toshiaki Motoki: Not even you can spoil my mood night tonight you troublesome furball... Blaine! Looking up from his desk and dropping a wedge of paperwork in the process, Blaine awaits the co-owners imminent instruction. Toshiaki Motoki: Get me another mince pie, but first... lets get this show on the road. Cheers of approval can be heard eminating from inside the Skyreach Center. Toshiaki Motoki: What's our first match? Grabbing his notepad, Blaine refers to the match planner behind him and searches for the relevant labels to attach to it. Blaine Blair: First up Mr Motoki we have Vampir Nosferatu in a "Face A Legend Match"! The fans can be heard giving a mixed reaction. Booing for the former OSW blood sucker, but cheers for the somewhat mysterious matchtype... especially since no one in attendance seems to know exactly what a "face a legend match" entails. Obviously Vampir Nosferatu was hardly full of Christmas spirit, instead Tiamat was sent to select parcels for the four members of the Dark Age and it appeared that the fifteith edition of ReVolution would start of with one of the most controversial superstars on the payroll. The question remains however... just who is he facing? Scandal Of Passion? As if the forced conversation wasn't bad enough, especially for one as aloof as the K-Wolf, the smell just made it all the worse. Even with the infuriating beginnings of a cold, Karina could pick out at least 3 unsavoury smells, then hurriedly set about forgetting them with a less-than-complimentary facial expression. Driver: Here ya go, Miss. As soon as the cab comes to a halt, Karina hops right out, just about holding in the shudder as she instantly tries to blank out any memory of the drive. Without even looking at the driver, having found the sight of the back of his head enough of a turn-off, Karina just extends a couple of notes through his opening window. Karina Wolfenden: Keep the change. Driver: Much obliged, Miss. Walking away, Karina mutters under her breath. Karina Wolfenden: The pleasure was all yours... Hardly dressed for the weather, wearing just a long sleeved t-shirt and a pair of three-quarter-length jeans, Karina slings her rucksack over her shoulders and makes towards the wrestler entrance, while the cab speeds off into the night. Karina Wolfenden: Hopefully a hassle-free night awaits, for once. Mumbling to herself, Karina still thinks of last week's awkward encounter with the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid... the man her very risky top-rope Whirlwind Romance put on the shelf for the best part of a year. He wanted an apology, Karina wasn't about to deliver one. Their stubbornness cancelled one another out... but didn't negate the dispute. Rubbing her hands together for heat, while he breath turns instantly to a mist, Karina bounds up the stairs and pushes the doors open, ready to welcome a relaxing burst of heat. Karina Wolfenden: Oh... shit... But it wasn't a waft of warm air that was coming right for her... instead a packing crate, its wheels scratching along the tiled floor as it careered towards the wide-eyed K-Wolf. Aware of her surroundings, Karina knew the rails either side of the steps had her boxed in, but rather than spring up as an instinct, she crouches. Karina Wolfenden: Fuck this up, and I'll need that cab back for the hospital visit. Despite the rapid speed the crate approaches at, Karina thinks in slowed-down time, knowing she had to wait for the perfect moment... and then pray for a clean landing. Karina Wolfenden: Hyah! Showing the spring in her powerful thighs, Karina explodes up into a backflip, her sneakers' soles almost brushing against the edge of the crate as she flies into a graceful arc. Through focussed emerald eyes, Karina watches the bruised evening sky rotate through into the cold grey of the concrete steps, while the black crate tumbles down them. Landing in a crouch, Karina secures her footing just a few inches away from the bottom step, then looks curiously behind her to where the crate is beginning to slow to a halt. Suspicious, narrowed eyes then look up at the doorway, where nothing but the welcoming illumination from inside can be seen. Karina Wolfenden: Hmph... Dusting herself down, Karina then scrapes the fringe from her face as she warily scales the steps once more, before bounding through the door and scanning all around her in a split second. Clear to the left, clear to the right, clear ahead. Clear above. Karina Wolfenden: Huh? Looking down at her feet, Karina's analytical eyes are drawn to the mat just inside the door. Rather than the usual "WELCOME", it reads "DANGER: ONCOMING TRAFFIC!". Bordering the words are numerous calculations for velocity, acceleration, and angles of trajectory, a calling card that Karina didn't need to know who was responsible. Karina Wolfenden: Let me guess. Sighing, Karina walks over the mat, before kicking it back down the steps. She didn't even have to think about who put it there, or who set the crate on its path. But they weren't the most obvious things, far from it. The most glaringly obvious fact was that it wouldn't be the last time she'd need to have all her instincts working at maximum. Karina Wolfenden: Accidents happen... Clenching her fists, Karina strides furiously into the arena, each slapping of her sole against the tiled floor a warning for a certain somebody to get ready for a fight. Karina Wolfenden: ...deal with it. Before he can climb back to his feet, however, the guards move in, kicking and swinging with clubs. After a moment, Motoki holds up a hand. Toshiaki Motoki: That's enough. He holds the titles up, staring at them. Toshiaki Motoki: Did... did you draw on these? Nova: Ugh... a little bit, yes... Motoki shakes his head in disgust and sets the titles on his desk. Toshiaki Motoki: Get him out of here. The guards pick Nova up hastily and escort him out of the room. Nova wrenches free just stepping out the door and turns to stare defiantly at Motoki. Nova: You can take my Tag Titles...but you'll never take...my freed- One of the guards buries the end of his club into Nova's stomach, and they half-drag him out of the room. Motoki presses the intercom button. Voice of Secretary: Sir? Toshiaki Motoki: Get me some marker remover and brass polish, please... and someone to use them. Having witnessed the comotion the room is somewhat dead as the PRIME personnel currently in there all stand frozen to their respective spots. Except for The English Luchadore who despite not being involved in the draw, is helping himself to as much free food as he can get his grubby little British hands on. Hin See: I suppose Nova isn't getting that present now then? Toshiaki Motoki: No, he can have whatever discarded package is left over... hopefully it contain a rather unattractive stipulation and opponent. Scandal Of Passion? Part II Written By Tyler Lopez The fans have seen a lot of limousines pull up to drop off wrestlers or celebrities who are to be given backstage access. Perhaps they will only be allowed into the special VIP buffet or perhaps they'll be granted full access to everything backstage. Perhaps they won't even make it past the first line of security. Unlike most stars they have seen tonight, the next man pulls up, not in a limo but a motorcycle--a black 2005 Suzuki GSX1300R Hayabusa, for those who are curious. He wears all black. The helmet covering his face is even black. A valet walks towards this man. The staff must have known that it would be a few people who would decide not to come with a limo and thus that is why a valet was provided. Still this one doesn't quite know how to approach this man who made no movement other than taking a look around the facility. The crowd begins to grow quiet in anticipation waiting for the valet to talk to the rider of the motorcycle. Valet: Um, excuse me sir...should I take your vehicle for you? The man says nothing to the red hair freckled-face kid in the generic valet uniform. He instead continues to look at the back of the arena. Valet: Sir? The man turns the ignition off on his vehicle and kicks the kickstand down. He steps off of the cycle, looking even more dominant than before. We now notice that the man is wearing a black backpack that is hanging around one of his shoulders. He slowly removes the helmet from his head. We see shoulder length hair, light eyes, of this man. The fans hanging around this backstage area start to realize who they are seeing. Tyler Lopez has arrived at Revolution 50. Valet: You're...you're...The Scientific Apparatus. Tyler Lopez: I'm late. So take care of my bike...it's new. He gives away the key to the stunned redhead valet and begins to walk away. We finally see the words "Scientific Apparatus" in silver letters stitched behind his black leather jacket as he walks the red carpet. Lisa Tyler has made her way to the restricted entrance. She grabs her microphone and gets into position along side of the man as he walks towards the backstage area. She can't help herself from offering up a wide smile. Lisa Tyler: This is an amazing honor for both myself...and for all of the fans of PRIME... Ladies and Gentlemen...The Scientific Apparatus...Tyler Lopez!!! If Lopez is acknowledging the fact that he's likely being broadcast right here and now, he doesn't show it. He continues walking. Lisa, getting used to being ignored, keeps trying to start this interview. Lisa Tyler: Mr. Lopez, welcome to Revolution 50! I can honestly say that it is a surprise to be seeing you here. Lopez, it seems, will talk...but he won't stop walking. Tyler Lopez: Its...different...someone made it personal. Lisa Tyler: Lopez, who is it? Who was it that attacked Passion? The Apparatus doesn't stop walking, but it's obvious that he's pondering the proper answer to this question over the next few steps. Tyler Lopez: She won't tell me. But it will be made obvious, soon. Lisa is confused. Tyler Lopez: Tonight someone, will have to take the blame, and every week from here on out, until whomever it was makes themselves clear to me, anyone, everyone is responsible. Lopez breezes right past a second line of security without even so much as a second glance. The security station slows Lisa Tyler's camera crew down, so Lisa knows that this interview is about to end a bit prematurely. Lopez walks away. The camera pans back to Lisa Tyler who looks surprised. The camera goes back to show Tyler Lopez entering the loading dock area and disappearing behind those large doors.
