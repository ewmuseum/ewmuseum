Crucifix Biography Wrestler Name Crucifix Nicknames The Zen Assassin, Steel Serpent, War Machine Hometown Detroit, Michigan Birthday October 29 Height 6' Weight 196 lbs Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Wearing a mask to conceal his horribly burned face, David Cross took up the mantle of Crucifix when he joined the fWo. He brought with him a daredevil aerial style, mixed with his extensive martial arts background. He wanted to earn the respect of his peers in the locker room, and earn the cheers of the fans. He did manage to win the Hardcore Title from hardcore legend Hellrazor. Certainly the fans, or at least the others in the fWo, would respect him now. Neither happened. Disappointed, Crucifix took some time off to reflect on matters. During his time off, his friend (and original Crucifix) Tyler died with his son in a tragic car accident. Wracked with guilt, blaming himself for the accident, Crucifix returned to the fWo with bad intentions. Tyler had left an insurance policy naming Cross as the beneficiary. With the money, Cross had plastic surgery to repair his face. It was a blessing and a curse; every day, the guilt seemed to eat at his sanity a little more. Before he could completely unravel, the fWo closed their doors, leaving Crucifix alone once again. The fWo re-opened, and Crucifix made his return by going after the soon-to-be World Champion, Kodiak Vic Creed. Around this time, the fWo ran into trouble and was forced to put on shows outside the U.S. On March 16, 2003, a suicide bomber charged the arena during a card in Tel Aviv and blew it up, killing many civilians and fWo personnel. Crucifix was taken to a small medical facility by Isralei personnel who didn't speak English. The blast left Cross with damage to his right eye, as well as an oddly shaped scar (a "cross", ironically enough) on his face. Since returning from his injuries, his life hadn't become much easier. During his fWo stint, his mother had been murdered and his fiancee abandoned him. The pressure that had been building inside him had reached its breaking point. A human being could only take so much before he finally snapped. And snapped he did. While sitting in a church late one night, he took a shard of glass and carved off the cross on his face. He spent some time undergoing psychiatric evaluation. Once released, David befriended the son of former fWo World Champion, Kodiak Vic Creed – "The Mecca" Alex Creed. It was this friendship with Alex that would put him directly in harm's way. Vic wanted to wrestle his son and drive him out of the business. Alex refused to face his father, so Vic beat David to within an inch of his life. Later, to show that it wasn't personal, Vic paid for David's reconstructive surgery (earning him the Cher Award for "Most Cosmetic Surgery"). David finally realized that his world was spiralling out of control. He packed up his few belongings and travelled to Japan. He found the grassroots home of the martial art he grew up studying at the Temple of the Golden Phoenix. He redoubled his efforts and found the inner peace that he’d been searching for – the proverbial eye of the hurricane in his soul. Then he took a shotgun blast to the face. Wrestling under a mask once again, Crucifix has returned to professional wrestling. The question is: does his sanity lay in the eye of the hurricane... or has it become the hurricane itself? The key to the man is his deceptive strength, but mostly his lighting quick speed. Strikes: His martial arts style is something of a hybrid style. There are definite elements of Wing Chun as far as being sensitive to the attacker’s movements, as well as Muay Thai elbows and knees. The strikes are direct and devastating. Occasionally the movements are unorthodox, which keeps his attack from being too predictable. - Roundhouse Kick to the thigh/knee - Double-Tap Roundhouse Kick (kick to stomach / kick to head) - Rising Phoenix (Thai Knee - pulls opponent down while driving the knee up) - Burning Phoenix (Flying Thai Knee) - Shouldermount Headscissor / Punch – Elbow (Leaps onto opponent, headscissor, punches or elbows head until opponent falls) - Thousand Hand Strike, or THS (Wing Chun rapid fist strikes) Submissions: He will use some standard professional wrestling submissions, as well as more traditional joint manipulations. - C4 (Cross Camel Cobra Clutch - aka Cobra Clutch/Camel Clutch) - Fujiwara Armbar - Legbar - Juji Gatame armbar - Achilles Leg Lock Alternative: - Storm Hammer (Asai Moonsault into DDT) - Typhoon Tail (Reverse enzuigiri - kick to the face) - Fallen Angel (Springboard shooting star press) - Hadoken (Double palm strike as opponent comes off the ropes) - Rising Storm Kick (Backflip Kick) - Double Knee Drop off the Top Rope Entrance Music "Indestructible" by Disturbed Ring Entrances Blaring air raid sirens, machine guns firing, and other sounds of war shake the very foundation of the arena. A carpet of purple smoke begins to roll out from behind the curtain, thick and dark, swallowing the entryway and crawling down the aisle toward the ring. The lights flicker while the sounds of conflict continue to rumble, suddenly changing to the opening riffs of "Indestructible" by Disturbed. The remix cuts to the chorus – ## Indestructible Determination that is incorruptible From the other side A terror to behold Annihilation will be unavoidable Every broken enemy will know That their opponent had to be invincible Take a last look around while you’re alive I’m an indestructible master of war ## The curtains slowly part, and the Zen Assassin known as Crucifix almost seems to glide through the opening. His face, a patchwork quilt of flesh, reddish pink scar lines and stitches, is framed by curtains of long, dark purple hair. The Zen Assassin follows the rolling purple smoke down the aisle. He effortlessly hops up onto the ring apron, and then vaults over the top rope.
