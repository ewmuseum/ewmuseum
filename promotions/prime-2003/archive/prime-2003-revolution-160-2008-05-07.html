ReVolution 160 ---- Twisted Metal It’s a little cold outside the Bradley Center tonight, though the hundred or so PRIME fans jammed along the outside of the parking garage don’t seem to mind. They’re all jostling for position and buzzing about the possibility of getting a look at one of their favorite superstars arriving at the arena. Instead, they’re getting a good look at Angelica Brooks in a slinky black dress, (and it’s quite obvious that she’s feeling the cold tonight). Her fiery red hair blazes as a stark contrast to the dull gray tones of concrete surrounding her. Angelica Brooks: Nick, Richard, I’m standing out here in the parking garage to await the arrival of PRIME’s very own Southern Discomfort, Wade Elliott. Our spies saw his pick-up heading this way, and as soon as he arrives, I’ll be asking the Sparkplug about the altercation last week between himself and my…er, um…the 5-Star Champion, Tyler Rayne. Cut to ringside, where a half dozen screaming fans are trying to get their faces and signs on national television, bouncing and pushing behind the barricade separating from the announce table and the number one commentary team in sports entertainment, Nick Stuart and Richard Parker. Nick: Thank you, Angelica. Should make for an interesting interview. I know a lot of our fans have had some questions about that incident as well. Richard: That’s because our fans are morons. Nick: Rich! Richard: What are you, my mom? The fans should have seen this one coming. I know I did. Anyone who didn’t is an absolute idiot. I’m being told that Wade should be arriving… The world of professional wrestling is a lot like a genie offering infinite wishes. Ask and ye shall almost invariably receive. Take for instance this instance… where the very mention of Wade Elliott arriving to the arena is interrupted by the roar of his pick-up’s engine. A quick but definite murmur races through the crowd of fans packed around the outside, the mass beginning to surge closer to the edges of the concrete construction. Another mighty roar of the engine, (just barely audible over the Allman Brothers Band blasting from the speakers), and the unmistakable pick-up jolts to rest between two white lines. Angelica Brooks: Well, boys, as you can see, Wade has just arrived, so I’m gonna— It’s not the roar of an engine, but the screech of tires that interrupts the young woman this time. The camera pans to the source of the sound, a black Mustang kicking up smoke beneath spinning tires. The smell of burning rubber fills the air, as does the incessant roar of the car’s revving engine. The Mustang lurches forward, pedal to the metal as it races toward… Angelica Brooks: Oh my God… The Mustang hits the back of Wade’s truck, slamming dead-on into the driver’s side tire with such force that it bursts. The explosion echoes deafeningly within the concrete encasement. Fans dive backward, attempting to avoid fragments of fiberglass and steel that have loosed themselves from the two vehicles. Wade’s pick-up is sent into a tailspin, the driver of the attack vehicle jacking the e-brake and executing a sharp turn usually only possible in Vin Diesel movies. (You know, if the guy was still, um, making movies). Angelica Brooks: OhmyGodOhmyGodOhmyGodOhmyGod… The Mustang lurches forward again, this time slamming grill to grill into Wade’s truck. Sparks light the scene as the rim of that back tire skitters across the parking garage, neither vehicle stopping until the bed of the pick-up slams against a large concrete pillar, bringing both autos to an immediate halt. Nick: I can’t believe…we’ve…we’ve got to get someone out there right now. Angelica? Angelica! Can you tell us…can you see if Wade’s okay? The voice in her ear seems to wake the interviewer from her trance. Still shaking all over, she seems to at least make an attempt at collecting herself. Angelica Brooks: I…I don’t know. There’s too much smoke. Oh my God. Oh my God. Okay. Nick? I’m going to try and get a close…closer look. Nick: Be careful, Angie. We don’t even know what kind of lunatic is driving that— The passenger side door (conveniently on camera side) bursts open, a pair of black shit-stompin’ boots and dingy old jeans the first to slide themselves from the vehicle. The man rises to his full height, the white skull emblazoned on the chest of his t-shirt already dotted with blood. Veins bulge from the muscled, well-defined arm that hangs from the sleeveless Punisher tee. The arm shakes ever slightly, presumably from anger or perhaps shock, though it never refuses to release its white knuckle grip on the foot long lead pipe clutched in its fist. The ever-present stubble is not surrounding his usual smirk tonight, but rather a malicious and determined grimace. The usually brilliant and vibrant eyes are dark and narrow. Swallowed by the intent of violence. His hair, mussed as always, only adds to the crazed look about him. Oh, and did you happen to hear that loud sucking sound? Richard: Wow. I, uh…yeah. Didn’t see that one coming. It seems like time has frozen for a moment as everyone attempts to process this information. Tyler Rayne just ran down Wade Elliott. With a noticeable limp the 5-Star Champion takes one step forward, reigniting the natural processes of time. A few of the more die-hard Rayne fans give a little cheer. An equal amount of more stalwart fans boo the actions of someone they may have once considered a hero. Both cries are lost within the larger silence of shock. Angelica drops her mic and rushes forward, heels echoing inside the otherwise silent parking garage. Angelica Brooks: Ty! Ty! Oh my God! Tyler! What are you doing?! Rayne wipes the blood from his face before retaliating with a vicious slap that snaps Wade’s head to the right. Angelica Brooks: …no…please…Ty…no… Tyler Rayne: Fight me. Wade’s head lulls back across his shoulders. Another, even harder slap, echoes throughout the garage. Tyler Rayne: FIGHT ME, YOU FUCK!! The Sparkplug throws what might be one of the weakest, slowest punches of his entire life. Rayne ducks under it with too much ease, rocking Wade’s bobble head with a rising uppercut. The punch is more than enough to finish an already dazed Elliott, who begins slumping once again toward the ground. Unfortunately, Rayne catches him on the way down and throws all his weight, power, and hatred into a knee straight to the ribs. Tyler Rayne: FIGHT! Knee. Tyler Rayne: ME!! Knee. Wade spits up another mouthful of blood. Tyler Rayne: PRICK!!! The 5-Star Champion lets go, essentially dropping Wade Elliott back down to the concrete. Wade collapses upon the ground, hands lazily attempting to fight Rayne away as the latter leans in, snatching Wade around the jowls and forcing him to look into the cold, dead eyes of his attacker. Tyler Rayne: You asked for this, fuck. You did this. You wanted a fight with me. Well here you fucking go. Call me a cunt all you want, Country. From where I’m standin’… looks like you’re the pussy. Polo: Um…Mr…uh…C.P. wants to… Two fists wrap around the scruff of the polo.
