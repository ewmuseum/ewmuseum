ReVolution 84 ---- Mayhem and Insanity Lisa Tyler was walking out the doors into the parking area for the staff. Some cool air swirled in through the entrance giving the interviewer a chill. Lisa Tyler: Hurry up with that camera. It’s friggin’ cold out here. The cameraman follows her into the area. A few cars are parked in their assigned spots but there is absolutely no activity in the lot of any kind. Lisa Tyler: Damn, here I thought I would at least get an interview and all I have is an empty lot. Lisa throws her arms into the air and is pacing through the lot when a loud bang is heard. Both Lisa and the cameraman turn to watch the entrance as the squealing of car tires is heard. Then, a jet black Dodge Charger comes barreling through the entrance and makes its way towards the entrance, but there is a problem, someone is in the way. Lisa Tyler: HEY! WAAAAAA! Lisa dives out of the way as the car comes to a skidding stop right beside her. She picks herself up off the ground and brushes her hair away from her face. The door opens and she looks up from her position on her knees to see a silver baseball bat swinging in front of her face. Behind the bat stands Killean Sirrajin, sunglasses in place and hair tied back. The thick white bandages are still wrapped around his forehead, left over from the bell shot that sliced him open last week. Lisa makes her way to her feet and brushes herself off while giving The Supreme Machine supreme shit. Lisa Tyler: What are you trying to do… kill me?! Killean Sirrajin: Not you my dear. Lisa Tyler: What is that supposed to mean? Killean Sirrajin: It’s not you that I am here for Lisa. The person I am here for is far more evil and untrustworthy. Sirrajin walks towards the door but Lisa stops him again, this time with microphone in hand. Lisa Tyler: Who is it? Killean turns, laughing out loud this time. He can’t believe the stupidity in that question. Killean Sirrajin: Did I fuckin’ stutter? Better yet, could you really be that dumb blondie? It’s quite obvious to anyone who I am here to get. It’s quite obvious… The Supreme Machine grabs the front of the camera and looks right into it as he continues. Killean Sirrajin: … to everyone watching through this little thing we call technology that Killean Sirrajin is here tonight to take apart his old friend. To beat the living hell out of the man that betrayed him for the last time. Oooooo The Supreme Machine is coming Tchu. I’m coming… He fades away as he fades through the doors of the arena, leaving Lisa Tyler to wonder if Killean is actually sane anymore. Lisa Tyler: As you can see, Killean is on a mission for Tchu’s head and he seems to have lost all common sense! It was an all-around beneficial relationship, but the problem was that people had turned against him. Scott had come from his apartment in town to find his standard security detail waiting for him at the entrance to the gate. He finally saw why. When he arrived at the back entrance, he saw what was at the radio station as a warm-up. There was a veritable throng of protesters, all waving banners with his face, all chanting statements that were defaming, slanderous and all around painful for him to listen to. But he got out of his Jeep and slung his bag over his shoulder anyway. He had a show. The security formed around him, like a ring for protection. Oddly enough, this detail was to keep him in check as opposed to safe. But as they tried to make their way through the angry mass, his guard was pressed inward, slowing their progress to a crawl. Scott tried only to keep himself in check. He wouldn't lash out at the protesters. Suddenly, more security flooded out of the building and started shoving. The mob was pushed back, away from the beset group. Apparently PRIME didn't think highly of having their wrestler entrance blocked, because next to Scott's Jeep roared a pair of gray state trooper cruisers. The officers stepped out, tasers in hand and collapsible batons in the other. One hefted a megaphone. “Please step back. Do not block the entrance.” The boom backed the people off, but they kept shouting at Scott, bound and determined to make their hatred known. One object cracked him squarely in the head. He turned to see a vodka bottle on the ground, filled with red liquid. “That's for Del Marco!” screamed one woman. One of the troopers grabbed her and pulled her from the throng, slipping cuffs on her wrist. “Let me go! He's the danger! Arrest him!” Scott walked his way on into Rupp Arena.
