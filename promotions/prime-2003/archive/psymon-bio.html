Psymon Biography Nicknames 'The Monster'; 'Uncrowned Champion'; 'PRIME's Resident Psycho'; 'The Demented One'; 'The Extreme Icon' (though he despises it) Hometown Springfield, Illinois Birthday July 5, 1980 Height 6'0' Weight 240 Fed Titles None as of yet. Fed Awards None as of yet, but the night is young ;) Biography There's not much known about this poor, demented soul, but this is what we do know... Before Psymon was born, his mother, 18-year-old Alexis Falco, was raped in a dark alley. The next day, she was pregnant. She had thought about an abortion but thought against it, so she decided to put her unborn baby and put him or her up for adoption. She called up the Kreed family, who have helped her out for living on her own and independence. She told them that she was pregnant and asked them if they can have their child. Steven and Rachel Kreed were delighted to have her child, Steven the most. Why was he so excited to have Alexis' child? Finally, on July 5, 1980, the time had come. Around 1:15 am, she called up the Kreed family telling them that she was in labor. They rushed over to take Alexis to the hospital. Hours have passed and she was supplied with a safe dosage of drugs to block out the pain she had endured. Finally, 3:00 AM was time when the drugged-up Alexis gave birth to 'Simon', named after her dead brother, was born. He wasn't born as Simon Falco, no, the Kreed family signed over the rights to claim Simon, so he was born as Simon Kreed. Isn't that sweet? A family to take in a newborn child. Sort of a 'facism you can vote for' type deal. Wait, that didn't make any damn sense. Oh, well, let's get back on track, shall we? During his infant and toddler years, Simon was treated well, like he was apart of the family, and he had a sister to play with. Her name was Emily and she was 7 years older than he was. Everything was going just fine until Simon turned 7, that was when all the pain and torment happened. His adopted father constantly beat on him. His mother was no help, she would watch on in her self-amusement, fingering herself. After the pummeling that Simon would get, the adopted parents would fuck right in front of the haggard and beaten Simon. Steven and Rachel would neglect Simon weeks apon weeks, but where was his sister in all of this? Emily wasn't much help, she couldn't protect him, she would get a beating as well from her father if she tried to. After each beating Simon would get, voices would manifest themselves in his head, telling him to kill his parents and his sister. He ignored them. As Simon got older, his beatens became more severe; broken bones and several trips to the hospital. Still, Simon couldn't defend himself, he can't defend himself against a 6'6', 290-pound father. He has tried, but that got him nowhere, just more broken bones and trips to hospital. He could barely attend school after all the beatings he gets. He couldn't go 5 seconds without puking up blood in class, because the beatings are that bad. One day, as Steven was beating the fucking piss out of Simon, he admitted that he raped his mother. That's why he wanted to have Simon so badly. That enraged Simon and he punched his father hard in the jaw. That punch almost costed Simon's life...As a result of that beating (half his ripcage broken, punctured lung, grade three concussion and internal bleeding) Simon was laid away in the hospital for about...6 months, Psymon believes, looking back at that specific beating. By the time he was 15, he had fucking enough! He came home from school one day, went to the kitchen, pulled out a butcher knife and hacked up his father with it. Simon did have any problems since he split Steven's skull in two when Steven was sitting in his chair. Simon put his father's remains in the middle of the room and waited for his mother. Around 5 PM, Rachel came home and was greeted with a plastic bag over her head and three rapings before he broke her neck. She enjoyed the third rape before she died. He picked up his 5'8', 210-pound cunt of a mother and threw her on top of his father's butcher body. Then he realized that his 22-year-old sister is visiting the next. So, he removed his bloody clothes, showered and gets some rest before he can rape can kill his so-called 'sister.' Simon was up at 6 AM waiting for his sister's arrival. About 10 AM, Emily stepped inside and saw her parents' dead body in the middle of the house. Before she can scream, a butcher knife goes in her back. Afterwards, he raped her and decapitated her. After all was that done, he called the police, telling them that his voices told him to kill his family. A few hours later, he was sent to a mental institution for eighteen months. In his records, it shows that he was diagnosed with an acute case of psychosis and schizophrenia. When he was 16, he was released from the mental institution for being 'cured.' He decided to take up wrestling to work out his schizophrenic and psychotic tendencies on people. He trained for 9 months, doing everything he was told to do. Finally, on August 10, 1997 he has his first official match and almost killed his opponent, because of his schizophrenic and psychotic episodes. He was immediately fired after that match. He was enrolled in a few classes to control his schizophrenic and psychotic episodes. The classes worked rather well for him, and hasn't had an episode since. On his 20th b-day, July 5, 2000 (July 6 in Japan), was in Japan to face Daisuke Koriyama, one of the greatest wrestlers Japan. Before his match started, he met Yuriko Yakama in the backstage area. They talked for awhile, never really realized that they are falling in love just by talking to each other. Finally, the biggest match of his match is up, against Daisuke Koriyama. It was 54+ minutes of pure skill and pain. Back then, he was wearing faded blue denim shorts with an inseam 5 inches below his knees, black wrestling boots cut off below the calves, a black muscle shirt and a long-sleeve red flannel shirt, sort of a grungy, trailer park trashy look. After minutes passed, he was wrestling topless. Kensuke accidentally got a hold of his left nipple ring and ripped it off. Psymon (he called himself), didn't feel anything until he looked at his left pec, which was bleeding. He eventually lost the match and Daisuke respected his talent and him. Daisuke soon started an 'Ishiban' chant. 'Ishiban' is 'number one' in Japanese. This was, perhaps, the greatest event of his life. *UPDATE* On December 30, 2004, Psymon has released himself from his LOW contract. He is now at large to do as he pleases. *UPDATE* On February 12, 2005, Psymon was signed to the DWO. He is ready to compete in PRIME at Culture Shock, that will be held on February 12, 2006. Ring Entrance The area is swallowed up by darkness. Nothing can be seen nor heard for a few seconds as flashbulbs light the arena. About twenty seconds pass, there's a static sound coming from the Prime Tron, then snow appears on the Tron. A couple of seconds later, a blurred word appears on the Tron; it's fuzzy at first, but it slowly comes to focus. When the comes to focus, it reads this: LATHEM Finally, a soft drumming from a bass drum starts to beat. It's faint at first, then it becomes louder, along with some heavy guitar playing. When everything is finally crescendos, the front man utters two words over the PA system... NOTHING REMAINS! Explosions are heard and the lights come back on in the arena as 'Nothing Remains' by Chimaira starts to play. A figure, dressed in a hooded trench coat with a blood-spackled croquet mallet, standing on the rampway, looking out into the crowd. He flips back his hood, the man revealed is none other than the Monster himself, Psymon, and the fans emit their mixed reaction as Psymon walks down to the ring, walking along with the lyrics to the song. I shout these words to those who never listened; I pen this letter with the utmost conviction. It's been dark in here, cold and relentless its been too long, I can no longer fight this. Too late to change my mind. Nothing Remains I've silenced the pain. Psymon walks off the ramp and slides into the ring. He gets into the middle of the ring, raising his mallet in the air, as flaming pyro explode from all corners of the ring. Match-Writing Critiques on Psymon Psymon is a levelheaded and highly focus individual at the start of the match. But if he starts to lose the match or is making mistake after mistake, he'll slowly go off the deep end, become mad enough to the point where his monster personality comes out. His monster personality matches that of a serial killer's: cold, calculating, emotionless and more focused than before. Nothing will anger him since his anger content is completely full. When he becomes his monster personality, he fights with his opponent, using weapons and is out to kill his opponent, by any means necessary. When Psymon is his regular self, he's a stong-style technical wizard, minus striking. He likes to moves like his Rolling Thunder Lariat, all Suplexes and his submission moves. With having a strong, technical style, Psymon has the aggressiveness of Chris Benoit and the vicious tenacity of Kurt Angle. Being one of the smaller superstars - at 6'0' 240 pounds - Psymon is quite strong, as in he can throw 300-400 pound wrestlers like ragdolls with suplexes. Here's how Psymon matches up against the following opponent, comprising of their status and wrestling styles: Face Opponents Since Psymon is Neutral, he'll play the heel to his face opponent, making him more malicious and sadist than his counterpart. He'll bend the rules a bit, but he doesn't resort to cheating...unless he wants to. Heel Opponents Psymon will play the face to his heel counterpart. Psymon's more cautious when he takes on his counterpart. Neutral Opponents Psymon usually plays the heel to his neutral opponent, depending on how the opponent's mood is. Look at the previous status to determine Psymon's status against the neutral opponent. Powerhouse Opponents Powerhouse Opponents and Psymon's favorite. They're usually big, slow and have the intelligence quotent of a brick. He'll use his agility for his advantage, then pinpoint weaknessess that the opponent has, I.E. Dropkicking the opponent's knees from underneath him, then mat-wrestle the opponent down. Technical Opponents Technical Opponents can sometimes be Psymon's equal, since he's a technical wizard in the ring himself. Psymon'll try to simply out-wrestle his opponent. If not, he'll fight his opponent. High-Flying Opponents High-Flying opponents are Psymon's #1 mismatched opponent. People who are Cruiserweights or smaller than Psymon are faster than Psymon, and he can't keep up with people who are the equivilant to Rey Mysterio Jr. He won't block or evade missile attacks as much, but when he does, he can simply evade them or counter them. When Psymon has the advantage, he'll use Power Moves on his opponent. Brawling/Fighting Opponents Brawling Opponents are simple for Psymon: Out-wrestle the fuck outta them! Well-Rounded Opponents Well-Rounded Opponents can be a challenge to Psymon because, well...they're well-rounded. They're capable of doing everything and anything to Psymon, so Psymon becomes very cautious and studies his opponent as he wrestles him. Psymon has accumulated two injuries: his ribs (right half of ribcage) and now recently his left ankle. If Psymon's opponents have any chance to defeat Psymon, go after one of the two injuries or take them both head on. Entrance Music 'Nothing Remains' by Chimaira (Entrance in Move Set)
