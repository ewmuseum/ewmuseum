Tyler Lopez Biography Nicknames The Scientific Apparatus Hometown New Orleans, Louisiana Height 6'6' Weight 269 Biography Lopez, as explained in detail, is the epitome of everything that is cocky or arrogant in this world. Every woman's dream date and the top of every guys 'hate lists.' A talker of smack and a backer of all of it. Lopez has been in the world of wrestling for years now. He has a talent for the sport like few others however, Lopez has been through a hell of a lot in the past and is still adjusting to the changes that he's going through. The reason he wrestles is because it lets him vent his aggression and aggression is the only thing in the world he can rely upon. Born the older of two brothers in a New Orleans family, Lopez was trouble almost as soon as he breathed his first breath outside the womb. He quickly learned to ignore his parents and began running with a group of local hoods. Tyler discovered the joys of beating others up by practicing on his brothers, and then used his street skills to become a petty crook, making a living off stolen wallets and jewelry. His parents, hoping to bring some sort of stability and wisdom into his life, scrapped together enough money to send Tyler off to college. Once there, embraced his studies, and was an exemplary student. His parents' hopes for a well-adjusted son soon faded, though, as the Tyler Lopez that came home to meet them was still a hoodlum, just with a better vocabulary and a encyclopedic knowledge of Shakespeare. Tyler's life changed by a matter of mere chance. One day he happened to call a radio talk show that was giving away free tickets to a wrestling show in the local area. Tyler won the tickets and, learning early in life never to give away anything gotten for free, went to the show expecting to be bored out of his mind., yet he got a strike of revelation. He realized that his hobby of beating people bloody could actually lead him to a steady career that wouldn't end up with him staring out from between bars. He knew at that moment that his life had to be the life of a professional wrestler. The next phase of Lopez's career arrived: PRIME. For almost half a year, there was no trace of Tyler, but as 2004 began, Lopez began focusing on PRIME, watching what it was that PRIME had, or didn't have to offer him. When he made his PRIME debut Lopez seemed to be in the best shape of his career. He began to solidify his reputation by participating in vicious conflicts and making his debut in the biggest match PRIME has had to date, The Dual Halo. After a failed attempt at a successful debut, Lopez now has two goals: to become the most feared, and envied wrestler since the organization of PRIME, and to finally lay claim to the prize he has sought since his debut: the PRIME Global Championship.
