Matt Mills Biography Staffer Name Matt Mills Hometown Worcester, MA Birthday 4/18/81 Height 6'0 Weight 162lbs Biography Matt's been around PRIME longer than any other previous interviewer, barring his year-long absence after a suspension. In his late 30s, he's interviewed all types, and he's been put down and belittled by all types, so he has cause to be jaded. You wouldn't know it from seeing him at shows, though; after being brought back, he's dialing up the enthusiasm and turning the other cheek when wrestlers choose to take their frustrations out on him verbally. He's a deeply unhappy man, but when he's onscreen, we're talking Todd Pettingil levels of cheeriness. Ring Entrances N/A
