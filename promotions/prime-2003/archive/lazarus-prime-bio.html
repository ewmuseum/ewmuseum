Lazarus Biography Hometown Parts Unknown Birthday Sometime in 1978 Height 6'3' Weight 245 Biography Lazarus is the dark side of Michael Kriegman, feared by all his other personalities. Wrestling Style Submission, Technical, Striking Finisher Move SUBMIT! Finisher Description Dragon Clutch Finisher Setup Move The Culling (Full Nelson Faceplant) Other Moves Strengths: Unlike the other personas of Michael Kriegman, Lazarus is a skilled technician, well versed in painful submission holds and counterwrestling. He's also a powerful, and very stiff striker, his blows are crisp and well executed. There will be no wild haymakers that his other personas are known for, his blows will be targeted to deliver the most pain and damage possible. Of the personas, he is only second to the Trashcan Man himself when it comes to resiliency. While lacking Trashy's legendary endurance, he can and will shrug off a lot of damage. Weaknesses: Lazarus's biggest weakness is his desire to torture, humiliate, and destroy his opponent. He will not go for a cheap pinfall, he wants his opponent to submit to his will. There will be times he will be disqualified for refusing to break a hold, if he has someone locked into a submission that is extraordinarily painful, he will be hard pressed to release it before a five count. He will not cheat, but he will engage in other heelish tactics, ducking out of the ring to gain a breather, using the referee as a body shield, and the like. He also lacks even the rudimentary flying skills of the Trashcan Man or Mr Smiley Face, he would be completely lost on the top turnbuckle. Tactics: Lazarus likes to target the neck of his opponent. He'll use every neckbreaker, he'll lock on rear chinlocks, he'll drive elbows and knees into the back of his opponents neck, he'll use release german suplexes, overhead belly to belly suplexes, DDT's, and the like to do anything he can to have his oppoents land on their head to jam their neck. If this isn't working for some reason, he's always willing and able to abandon that plan to find another body part to weaken first. He'll go after an opponents knee with kicks, stomps, a variety of leg locks, shinbreakers, or he'll go for an arm or shoulder with a variety of armbars, knee smashes, arm wringers, shoulderbreakers... but eventually he will go back to that neck. Personality: Lazarus is an evil bastard, plain and simple. If he has the upperhand he will laugh in your face as he spits in your eye. He'll drag you up to his level by your hair right before he slaps the taste out of your mouth. He is there to punish and humiliate his opponents. If he loses the upperhand, he'll first try to play it off as amusing, but if it continues, he will fly into a rage of brutal punches, chops, and kicks until his opponent is back under control where he belongs.
