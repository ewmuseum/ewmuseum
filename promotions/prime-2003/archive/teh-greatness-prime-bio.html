Teh Greatness Biography Fed Titles No titles Fed Awards No awards Biography Teh Greatness. Vangellus Oxios and Scott 'Rampage' Matthews. They formed a team at the request of Oxios, who had lost his former partner Gabriel Brown. At first, they had problems working together. Lack of chemistry. Team cohesion was virtually nonexistant. Not that they would come to blows, but that they were rookie wrestlers who had not known each other beforehand. The climb to the top was slow, but it happened. They weren't really much of a tag team, as their matches constantly degenerated into roving brawls. Wins always came by Scott Matthews spearing his opponents, or Vangellus Oxios getting pinned before that could happen. But as they kept wrestling, they formed a friendship. And even if their wrestling had not greatly improved, they managed to win the PRIME tag team titles twice. The two friends secured the training services of Justin Williamson, former PRIME Tag Team Champion in his own right. The sky was the limit for them. Then, in no fault of either man, they were forced to retreat into protective custody. Scott's past altercations with the Cult of the Poison Tree had caught up with him in the form of testimony required in the case against the leader of the cult. Protective custody, isolation from society. No Teh Greatness. One month? Two months? Maybe more? To be gone from wrestling. As they say, out of sight, out of mind. The name 'Teh Greatness' would be off of the card, off of the public's lips. Something had to be done. Enter, the replacements... Other Feds First federation as a team Other Fed Awards None Finisher Name Various double-team techniques Finisher Description Either man refuses to be restricted to only one or two finishing techniques in singles competition. Likewise, they are proficient in ending matches in an instant with a number of attacks. Thus, the end can come from anywhere. They don't have double-team attacks planned, but the two have an intangible understanding that allows them to work well together as a team.
