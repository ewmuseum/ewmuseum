ReVolution 101 Quicken Loans Arena, Cleveland, Ohio ---- The Announcement. In the beginning, there was nothing. And then, God said, let there by pyro. And oh, what it display it was! Greens and reds and blues and purples, blowing into the air, as lights danced through the Quicken Loans Arena. The fans of PRIME were on their feet as the first Revolution was about to begin in Cleveland, Ohio. Home of Chet Worth. And then, the horns kicked in. And my shoe started to squeak. "The Science of Selling Yourself Short" rang through the arena, as the fans stood and screamed for their hometown boy. Chet Worth made his first appearance home as the CEO of PRIME, and they LOVED it. For his part, this was a different Chet Worth. Calmer. He was wearing a suit, but, sporting the Dana White casual, no-tie look. He's shaved his goatee completely off, looking fresh faced, but a tad awkward. In the past, Chet Worth would have simply walked to the ring, waited for his opponent, and walked away, win or lose. The new Chet Worth, took the time to slap hands with the fans, and shout encouragement at them before rolling into the ring surrounded by photographers. Worth stood, and threw two arms into the air, with a genuine smile on his face. The fans ate it up, and gave it right back to him. Chet Worth: HELLO, CLEVELAND! Worth stuck out his thumb, and put his tongue through his front teeth, giving a goofy, Mick Foley-esque smile as the fans ate up the Patented Foley Cheap Pop™. Chet Worth: ARE YOU READY FOR A NIGHT OF THE BEST WRESTLING IN THE WORLD?! Again, cheap pop, and a beauty. Foley would be proud. Chet Worth: Fans, last week, I made an announcement that literally shook the wrestling world to it's very core. And honestly, I can't tell you how pleased I am that each and every single one of you that have called, or sent emails to the PRIME offices to wish me luck, I can't tell you what that means to me. Yeah, it's going to be rough for a while. Yeah, I'm not going to do the right thing sometimes. But, I'm going to make sure that every week, each one of you are entertained by PRIME. The fans explode again, as Chet holds up his hand. Chet Worth: But, enough about me. Tonight, PRIME embarks on the second century mark, with an announcement of the main event for Colossus III. At the first Colossus, Tony Rolo... Oh, no he di-in't! The fans boo, mercilessly. Boo, Rolo! BOOO! Chet Worth: Tony Rolo beat Ignatius Lisieux for his second Universal Title reign on July 11th, at the Superdome in New Orleans. Last year, Hoyt Williams defeated Killean Sirrajin to win his first Universal Title. And while I could re-unite the losers of those two matches, we just saw the two biggest men from PRIME last week at Revolution 100, tear each other limb from limb. So, I had to make a decision, depending on if Ignatius Lisieux won or lost, who was going to be the man... ...or woman... to main event Colossus III, taking on Killean Sirrajin for the Universal Title. And I think, I made a good decision. LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, HE STANDS SIX FOOT TWO INCHES TALL! HE WEIGHS TWO HUNDRED AND FORTY TWO POUNDS. HE'S STARTED IN PRIME WITH AN UNPRECEDENTED 17-2 RECORD. HE IS THE INHUMAN BEING. HE. IS. TCHU! More pyro explodes, as Worth obviously spared no expense on his first moments as the CEO of PRIME being huge, a video package plays, set to Tchu's theme song "Pressure" by Staind. Tchu powerslams Chet Worth. Tchu decimates Smitty T. Duluth at ReV 75. ReV 83's victory over the Illustrious Face Eater. And then, triumph, winning the Universal Championship, only to lose it to Clyde Walkins the next week. His Dual Halo matchup, lasting just a few seconds shorter than Chet Worth as Killean Sirrrajin held his leg down, making him unable to kick out. And finally, his victory over Karina Wolfenden at ReV100. Tchu appears on the stage, and the fans explode. He goes ballistic, as pyro behind him explodes in rapid succession. He walks the aisle, dressed well, with a clean T-Shirt and a pair of pants, which is as good as it gets for Tchu. He enters the ring, and pulls the mic from the unsuspecting hands of Chet Worth. The Inhuman Being lifts the mic and holds out his empty hand, haulting the words of PRIME's new main man. Tchu: Before you call 'Killer' out here, I just wanna say a few things. Nick: A little out of the ordinary for The Inhuman Being. Typically, he's not the kind to say much, choosing to let the fight speak for him. Should be interesting to hear what he has to say. Richard: I, for one, prefer it when he keeps his mouth shut.  Tchu: Four years ago this month, The Supreme Machine made his professional wrestling debut in some little hundred degree high school gymnasium in San Diego.  And he did so, by attacking me from behind, breaking two ribs... all as a hired hitman.  Tchu: Four years... revenge has been festering inside me. Four long years... and at CIII, I'm collecting my payback with interest! You have a title around your waist that belongs to me, Killer, and in four weeks, I'm taking it back.  Once again, the Wal-Tron (Sponsored by Wal-Mart) lights up, and a video package of the career highlights of Killean Sirrajin. And after those three seconds are over, because Sirrajin sucks... Okay. I'm just kidding. Actually, there's a lot of stuff to go over here. There's his victories over Walkins, Lisieux, and others. There's his smug grin, Charity, and the rest. Chet Worth: Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome the Universal champion, standing at SIX FOOT FOUR INCHES TALL... weighing in at TWO HUNDRED AND NINETY SEVEN POUNDS... HE IS THE SUPREME MACHINE... HE. IS. KILLEAN SIRRAJIN! Nick: With Killean now in the ring, I have never heard a reaction like this in my life. Not in the days of Ian English, Vampir Nosferatu, Boda or Hoyt Williams, the fans have never been this vocal about their hate for one superstar!  Richard: The stupid people need an education! He’s the best we got. He’s the Universal Champion for a reason.  Killean stands in the ring, opposite Tchu with Chet Worth not far off either. He stands with a smile and then breaks out a golf clap. The fans reward this action by continuing their onslaught.  Killean Sirrajin: Bravo old friend. That was a well thought out and brief speech. Are you sure you have nothing else to say or can we actually return to reality?  Tchu: You're right. Speeches aren't really my thing. You're usually the one with a mic in hand, refusing to shut the fuck up. But, I'm done with the chatting... so we can get down to more important business if you'd like, champ. The Inhuman Being drops his mic and takes a step towards The PRIME Choice.  Killean Sirrajin: Whoa whoa whoa whoa whoa! Hold on there stud…  Sirrajin holds up his hand as a signal for Tchu to back up. But Tchu stands his ground, a mere four feet away.  Killean Sirrajin: Yeah, I can see that speeches aren’t your thing. So that’s just another thing I’m better at, including wrestling and being a father.  With no mic in his hand any longer, his words go unheard by all the fans in attendence but the cameraman is right on top of things, picking up the brief reply after the very personal attack from The PRIME Cut. Tchu: Is that all you do anymore?  Just stir up bullshit drama that's better suited for the Jerry Springer Show? You were a lot more impressive back before you began to think you were untouchable.  Killean Sirrajin: Anyways, Matt’s right. It’s been over four years since our first face to face meeting. He just won the Immortality Title in the Underground and I speared the ever living hell out of him. But the match never happened, something I have regretted ever since. You ended up being the luckiest man alive that day Matt. But now, four years later in PRIME, I’m the Champion and you’re the chump. No one wants a guy who couldn’t defeat Clyde Walkins as their Champion.  Flash bulbs from the press and the photographers flood the ring as Killean says his piece and Tchu is left to stir in his own juices.  Killean Sirrajin: Matt, you couldn’t beat me back then, no matter how hard you tried. Now, four years in the future, I have gotten exponentially better and you have stayed on the same level at best. Therefore, you won’t have a chance at Colossus. Two titans will clash for this… The Universal Champion and only I will come out victorious. You’ll have no self-worth and no championship when you walk in to your empty home with no family. You might want to buy a rope on the way home Matt. That’ll be more respectful than embarrassing yourself come August 13th. Chet Worth holds his hands up for a moment, cutting the two men off, as they stare each other down, while reaching out to take the microphone back from Tchu. Chet Worth: Guys, if I could interject for a moment, I can tell exactly what you're thinking. Both of you would love a shot at tearing the other one up, right here, right now. The fans pop huge as Tchu grins slightly, and Sirrajin nods, not taking an eye off each other. Chet Worth: Well, it's not going to happen. And that's not me trying to be a stupid heel, I just know that the moment each of you get into the ring, you're going to tear each other limb from limb. Once again, after a brief moment, the fans pop again. Chet Worth: I cannot allow that to happen before Colossus Three. Therefore, as the CEO of PRIME, I am making three decrees that each of you MUST adhere to. Number one. Your match at Colossus III will be no disqualification. Pop. Chet Worth: There will be no countout. Pop. Chet Worth: There will be no referee stoppage. Pop. Chet Worth: You two want to tear each other apart, you'll do it in the ring, and in the ring only, which brings me to decree number two: If either of you lay a hand on the other before the bell rings at Colossus, Killean, you will be stripped of your Universal Championship, and Tchu, you will never, ever, ever, ever get the chance to regain what you think is yours. Both men look at the CEO, warily, but nod, nonetheless. Chet Worth: Which, leads me to the final decree. Tempation, guys, is an awful, awful thing. I know how bad it can be. SO, to avoid that, until Colossus, neither of you will be allowed in the arena. You can have tonight, but, until August 13th, if I see you backstage, I will fine you... Tchu reaches out, and rips the microphone out of Chet Worth's hand, glaring at the CEO. Tchu: Then as far as I'm concerned, I'm working for free these next few weeks. Fine me. I don't care. But, I'll be here, every week. And I DARE YOU to stop me. The Anti-PRIMEate throws the mic down, and stares at Sirrajin as Chet Worth stands in amazement. Nick: What a main event! Richard: Tchu vs. Sirrajin for the first time ever! We'll be back! ---- "Best Friends" You could tell just by looking at Jonathan Winters that the smile plastered right across Buddy Kingfishers face was the very last thing he wanted to see before his first defence of the PRIME Internet title. However, it was quite transparent that Kingfisher doesn’t notice what everyone else blessed with the potent combination of sight and intelligence can plainly witness and so edges closer toward Winters, as opposed to the rest of the locker, who have been edging away from him for the majority of the afternoon. Rather predictably, Winters ignores Kingfisher completely, as he unpacks the PRIME Internet Title from his bag and gently cradles the belt as though it were a child. Kingfisher’s eyes widen at the sight of the gold and, noticing this, Winters turns his back on the simpleton hoping he might have the most basic concept of body language. But he doesn’t. Kingfisher: Excited? Winters ignores him, but Kingfisher’s smile remains as firm as ever. Kingfisher: I said, excited? Winters rests the Internet Title on the wooden bench right by his bag and goes about unpacking his wrestling tights, making sure that he's directly between Kingfisher and the title. Kingfisher’s face firstly contorts with confusion, but then ungracefully expands when realisation finally takes a hold - it’s a long time coming. Kingfisher: Oh yeah, I forgot. You’re ignorant, aren’t you? Winters doesn’t reply. Kingfisher: Well, I’m excited, it being my first title shot and all. I haven't had the chance to be a champion since...well since Core. I want to make a good impression on my fellow PRIME-ATES and all. Perhaps I’ll win first time like you... A grin slices its way across Winters face and he turns briefly to glare at Kingfisher. Winters: Doubtful. Kingfisher’s eyes widen and his arms are thrust outward, almost manically. Kingfisher: You spoke to me! YIPPIE!!! Winters sighs. Winters: I regretting it already... Kingfisher: I knew this day would eventually come! Then, without warning, Kingfisher lunges forward, catching Winters unawares, and envelopes him completely in a smothering embrace. He holds for the longest time, despite Winters’ misgiving and resistance. In the end, Winters must resort to shoving Kingfisher away to prise the man off. Once the embrace is broken, Winters glares at Kingfisher warily, idly brushing his now crumpled t-shirt down. Winters: Kingkisher... Kingfisher: Yes, friend? Winters actually grimaces at ‘friend’; both the use of it in a sentence and the actual word itself. Winters:...don’t ever do that again. Kingfisher: Understood. Winters shakes his head, turns his back on Kingfisher and goes about his business, leaving Kingfisher standing there looking incredibly awkward. Kingfisher: I’ll be going then...I have an important match to prepare for. Winters: I know you do. It’s against me... Kingfisher chuckles to himself, whilst making his way for the door, leaving Winters rolling his eyes. Then, as he unpacks, he halts dramatically. Something is wrong. For the longest time, it is difficult to decipher the problem, but then it hits; the PRIME Internet title is notorious by its absence. Winters: What the...? Frantically, Winters searches through his clothes and his travel bag, but already knows the inevitable truth; his mind just refuses to accept it. The belt is no longer on the wooden bench, it’s been stolen. Winters eyes widen at the prospect and he glares around the locker speculatively. Nobody else is paying much attention and whoever stole the damned thing isn’t stupid enough to be parading around with it. Angrily, Winters storms out of the locker room, in search of the covented gold. ---- Watch Out For A Falling Star EARLIER TONIGHT imposes itself on the bottom right of the screen as the outside of the arena is shown. A few wrestlers, doing their best to put the 'events that shall not be mentioned' behind them, break off and shake a few hands, sign posters, and briefly chat to their fans, before being subtly herded back into the building. Normality is slowly resuming under the watch of Chet Worth. I know... weird. With the rucksack a much lighter burden than the 3 falls Tchu now holds over her, the K-Wolf is far from bright and breezy as she walks into the low sun. Black all over, from vest-top to sneakers, only glowing orange hair looks anything other than sullen. "Karina!" "Over here!" "Sign my poster, please?!" Her fans do their best to yell over the boos and taunts of those who've sided with the camp of Tchu and Lisieux. Not in the most social of moods, Kari keeps her head down and keeps walking, a meet-and-greet down below 'head-butting sharp things' on her list of priorities. Her Brittney-eqsue shunning of the weather-suffering crowds creeps an air of hostility into the surroundings, which couples with the increasing security presence as showtime draws ever closer. But while one PRIME star is heading towards the building, another is heading out for some air. Had it been one of twenty or so other people, the event would pass without a batted eyelid... but fate had decreed that none other than the Inhuman being, Tchu, fancied a few minutes in the freshening evening air. Fate can be a pesky little fucker. On opposite sides of a railing, the K-Wolf and Tchu's eyes meet. Little of the tension their looks betrayed before their match at ReV100 seems to have been released, especially on the side of black and orange. With some of the fans able to catch sight of both superstars, the noise swells, more fans surging over to their side to catch a view. Tchu: S- He almost manages a syllable before... Karina Wolfenden: Don't. Tchu: Handshake's out of the question then? With a scowl so fierce that even the air around her was thinking better of sticking around, spite pours out. Karina Wolfenden: I'm sure tonight, when the cameras start rolling, you'll be back to playing the brave, valiant Tchu. Savoir of PRIME. The cold eyes of Tchu watch his fiery roster-mate stride past him, but not without adding a dose of reality to neutralise her acidity. Tchu: Right. God knows you’re innocent. I just dreamed that shot to the balls and thumb to the eyes. Fitting that you make me out to be the bad guy. All I did was say a man's name. Even with an exterior frosty enough to be classed as 'tundra', the Inhuman Being has his limits before he'll crack.
