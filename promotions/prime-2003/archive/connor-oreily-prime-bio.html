Connor O'Reily Biography Wrestler Name Connor O'Reily Nicknames The Boston Stallion Hometown Boston, Massachusetts Birthday January 12th, 1990 Height 5'11 Weight 217 lbs Fed Titles PRIME World Tag Team Championships (1x) Fed Awards None. Biography Connor O'Reily is the Irish-born half of the Princes of New England. He hails from Boston. Connor has never really known his biological parents, as his father left before he was born and his mother committed suicide when he was only three. Connor, his twin sister Miranda, and their brother Mark were all raised by their aunt and uncle (Caitlin and Barry Blair, respectfully). His uncle's love for wrestling eventually inspired Connor, notably irresponsible, to eventually pursue it at his occupation. However, he decided this when he was only fifteen and he dropped out of high school to join Curtis Brown's wrestling school. However, a combination of Connor's age, irresponsibility, and punkish attitude all got him booted from Brown's training camp with not enough information or training to make it. During this time, he met Simon Knox, who agreed to help Connor and took him to Joey Malone. Joey, with the help of Coral Avalon, assisted in training young Connor even in spite of his flaws, and managed to make him into a professional wrestler by the age of 18. Connor began teaming with Simon not long afterwards. Personality: Connor is a irresponsible and somewhat reckless youth. It is often implied and sometimes outright stated that his life would fall to ruin if Miranda O'Reily, his twin sister, were not constantly keeping an eye on him. He is so terrible at managing his own finances that he'll buy things on impulse if he has even a couple of dollars in his pocket. The entire reason why Miranda initially joined him in his wrestling insanity was because of this, and it's why she continues to have to be with him. Apart from his irresponsibility, Connor has a tendency to act like a jerk. He is a decidedly different kind of jerk than Simon is, in that he's more of the one of open hostility. He has an Irish temper and he's close to becoming an alcoholic, a combination that leaves him saying terrible things about a lot of people other than Miranda and Simon. Connor loses his temper frequently, often at bad times, and he seems to be the loose cannon of the Princes. Despite this, Connor can really think things through if he has to, though the results are often the result of his own irresponsible desires. Connor is also very cavalier, using his good looks and his undeniable charm (which he seems to turn on and off like a light switch) to win over girls, sleep with them, and then promptly leave. Connor does not tend to stay in long relationships and any that have lasted longer than a month have ended in disaster because his charm fades away admidst his irresponsible behaviour and the fact that he's really kind of an asshole. Though he manipulates her and occasionally treats her badly, Connor really does care about Miranda and goes insane with anger if she gets hurt. Other Moves WRESTLING STYLE Connor O'Reily is a high flying nutcase, to put it mildly. His style is definitely more "style over substance", and he is a flipping showoff. He shines in matches with lots of people involved, or hardcore-based matches, which allow him more room to be creative. Connor's style is a mix of Jeff Hardy, John Morrison, and Sabu. He has a lot of moves designed to just show off (like Morrison), but also a propency to put his body at risk in hardcore matches (like Sabu) and can do insane stunts involving ladders and high places that can leave fans gasping (like Hardy). All of this leads him to being the flashier member of the Princes, but probably not the one with longevity to his career. The saying "he's not quick, he's sudden" applies greatly to the way Connor paces his matches. Although Connor has tremendous stamina, he lacks the ability to pace himself in any way other than full throttle, which means that he's not usually the guy who can last too long in the ring without some kind of break in between. This makes him great for tag matches with a more methodical partner (like Simon Knox), as he can tag out to catch his breath from time to time. Connor likes to fly, but he doesn't like to give the fans a show straight away. Often, to be a jerk, he'll fake going to the air, only to stop short and just booting somebody in the head. It's only in big match situations that he'll go straight for the flying, as it's his desire to steal the show. Connor also has the propency to taunt his opponents when they're down. He's a showoff like RVD, but tends to not play to the crowd too much. STRENGTHS - Of the two Princes, Connor is the more dynamic and unpredictable of the two. It is his spontaneous nature that gives him an edge against more cerebral opponents who like to pick apart an opponent's weaknesses. Unlike others of his ilk, Connor doesn't have any sort of martial arts background (or discipline), so he doesn't normally unleash the tiger on his foes like, say, a Tyler Rayne. - He is also very quick and agile, enough that he can manage a standing shooting star press if he needs to. His most impressive feat of agility is a standing 450 splash. - Connor, unlike Simon, is very comfortable in hardcore-style matches, and can be quite creative in how he utilizes the enviroment. Once again, his spontaneity is what drives him in this regard. - Connor has a knack for is keeping an eye on what the opposing tag partner is doing. He likes to catch them with sudden cheapshots and then go back to pounding an opponent, often unimpeded by the referee who would suddenly be distracted by the other tag partner looking for retaliation. WEAKNESSES - First of all, he's a rookie with only about a year's experience as a competitor. He makes rookie mistakes at times, often forgetting to hook a leg or putting his head down too soon for a back body drop. He also has a tendency to take one risk too many. - Connor can hurt himself almost as much as he hurts his opponent with his flying techniques. He can also crash and burn miserably, leaving Simon or himself at the mercy of his opponents. - Connor is nowhere near as strong as Simon is and, in fact, he can't lift opponents that much larger than himself. - The most significant weakness is that Connor has an Irish temper, and he doesn't compose himself nearly as well as Simon does. Because he tends to wear his heart on his sleeve and doesn't exactly have a brain-mouth filter, he tends to make opponents more aggressive just by smacktalking them. His pride is much more easily wounded than Simon's, which leads him to attack wildly if thoroughly pissed off enough. - What makes him over-the-top angry is if his sister, Miranda, gets hurt somehow. In which case, Connor will abandon all reason, safety, and care about winning the match. He will go out of his way to annihilate the person or persons responsible for the act, likely getting himself disqualified in the melee. PRIMARY FINISHERS - Condom Breaker The Condom Breaker is John Morrison's "corkscrew neckbreaker". The name refers, of course, to Connor's cavalier lifestyle. Usually a blow applied after a boot in the gut, the Condom Breaker has an advantage in style and impact. It resembles a more fancy swinging neckbreaker, utilizing an almost cartwheel-like movement. As Connor himself is in the air for an extended period of time during the execution of the move, it increases the eventual impact of the move. It can be kicked out of, but that should be a relatively uncommon occurance. The Condom Breaker can usually come out of nowhere, but if the match is turning into a squash, Connor has no problem calling for it and pulling loads of theatrics before executing the move. - Crossfire Hurricane (Special) This is a split-legged corkscrew moonsault. The name is taken from the Rolling Stones' "Jumpin' Jack Flash". It's very fancy and very flashy. It's often used after an opponent is slammed to the canvas with a rolling fireman's carry slam or a front powerslam. The "Crossfire Hurricane Special" is used on those occasions where Connor might want to do it from a higher place, like the top of a ladder. In this case, he does not do the split-legged part of the move, he executes a Sky Twister Press instead. SIGNATURE MOVE - Shining Steiner With an opponent on one knee on the canvas, Connor runs directly at them and jumps onto their knee, then on top of their shoulders before snapping them over with a lightning fast hurricanrana. RARE MOVES - O'Reily Factor Pardon the name. XD This is an "Imploding Star Press", which is kinda like a reverse shooting star press. Connor faces away from the ring with an opponent close to the turnbuckles. Connor then leaps backwards, but does a 450 splash on his way down. It is often used in those rare occasions where everything else has failed.
