Neil Fracas Biography Nicknames Neil 'The Real (F#$king) Deal' Fracas Hometown Los Angeles, California formerly hailing out of New York City, New York Birthday March 1, 1972 Height 7 ft 2 Weight 359 Biography The Story of Neil Fracas Neil has been interested in sports and competition all his life. He was the guy that everyone wanted on his team. Neil was the BIG guy in the weight room that no one wanted to go near. In high school he was a star in both Football and Rugby. He was infamous in Hockey. Neil had a staggering 16 hours and 52 minutes of penalty time in just one season due to unnecessary roughness and checking penalties (This was a world record). This was usually after the opposition's lead scorer had been carried off the ice (see excerpt #1). Many people don't realize that Neil was a good guitarist and was part of several high school bands. He tried the local music circuit but the non stop drugs, beer, and sex distracted him from his studies so he cut out the drugs. He didn't smoke and found most of the drugs weren't any better then beer so it wasn't a problem. He quit the band after their drummer Eric Deering got busted for drug possession. It just wasn't fun anymore. (Plus the coach started enforcing drug testing so Neil had to 'study' for the upcoming test.) He graduated on a Football scholarship and was drafted by the University of New York. He found out that his reputation in Hockey was not a very positive one. He was banned from University Hockey for being excessively brutal with no control or remorse. This pissed Neil off a lot. To him the purpose of the game is to win not play nice with the little guys. He did very well in Football and had earned player of the year in his freshman year. He was earning his bachelor degree in Electronics, Computer Operation, and Programming. He was soon banned from Football because of a 'incident' between him and 8 other players from the University of Wisconsin. He ended up hospitalizing 3 of them in a fight after a tackle done by Neil (see excerpt #2). Thankfully there was no bleeding heart liberal yahoos thinking he is too rough for Amateur Wrestling. He studied Judo and Jiu Jitsu grapple styles. In 436 amateur matches Neil was undefeated. Neil saved a lot of money he won while betting on sports. It seems the bookie he dealt with was a little less then ethical. Neil was cut in for 10% of the profits to help influence the outcome of games. This was upped to 20% when he got the whole football team in on the deal. This was written in Neil's yearbook. “Man most feared by his own coaches”, “The F'in animal who drank a whole keg of Budweiser at a party and carried half of us out”, “I’ll never forget you laying out half of Wisconsin. It was worth $85 to see it”, and “You drank more then half the team and still drank us all under the table. Kick Ass!!!”. Neil decided to go on a trip to Thailand after graduation in 1989. He found out about all of the underground pit fights there. He had some Boxing experience. He knew a lot about wrestling due to his amateur background and how much he studied it on TV. He was familiar with the grappling techniques used in Judo and Jiu Jitsu. His first 2 matches he breezed through by Brute force and submissions. He was known for his brutal submission holds and the force he delivered his moves with. The style he used then was close to the style he would use later in his pro wrestling career. He also had a brutal right jab, which was slow but packed a lot of power. It was then he met Gideon (see excerpt #3). They talked and a friendship started. Both entered the Kumate (see excerpt #4). Neil made it to the quarterfinals where he was thrown out of the ring by a well-executed judo style arm drag. They watched each other's back and helped each other learn each other’s style and defenses against it. Neil gained a record of 76-3-33 KOs in almost a year there with 37 submission wins. He returned to New York with Gideon. He was considering entering the boxing circuit there. When he came back to NYC he started bouncing and working as a bounty hunter for a living (see excerpt #5). Gideon came to NYC to compete in the WKBA there. Gideon and Neil trained together. They regularly taught each other moves from their styles and defenses against them using their respective styles of combat. Gideon decided to go pursue a movie career and got Neil the bouncer job for the Hog Wallers Bar before he left. This was relatively high priced boredom with a bunch of yuppies who pretended they are bikers. Neil fit the biker image and never had to do much to convince people they should be leaving. Soon though WCCW came to town. 7 of their wrestlers came to the bar. When they started harassing the ladies Neil told 2 of them to leave. One said 'Do you have any idea who you are talking to you'. Neil said with a smirk 'Kid Flash a mid card jobber for WCCW with no real talent and Inferno who has won only 2 matches that I am aware of'. This started the fight that was to start his wrestling career. 3 of them were carried out and 2 more were injured. The next day after placing a call to Gideon from his jail cell he was approached by Richard Sanderson who posted his bail and given a 'job' offer as the new heel to take on the champ the 'Ironman' Silverstein for the WCCW World title. After talking it over with Gideon he decided to wrestle professionally with Gideon as his manager. Neil was looking forward to the match. That match was hyped from day one. Gideon helped promote Neil by supplying him with creative ideas (see excerpt #6). He even got Neil a spot on TV in LA to give a interview on his upcoming match. Gideon used his Hollywood notoriety to get several interview opportunities while Neil trained so he would be in shape for the match. Gideon got a lot of media interest in Neil just by being his manager and helped do the promo video for the match. It was supposed to be Neil jobbing a 'Hulk Hogan' style match according to Sanderson's script. Neil decided to do things “his way”. Neil went to the ring for the first time with Gideon by his side on October 12 1990 and told the crowd how he was going to put him in the Painkiller in under a minute. Then he did just that forcing a submission in 49 seconds. The crowd reacted very well to the debut cheering Neil. They saw a Neil come in and just lay waste to Silverstien with no effort. This infuriated Sanderson and broke the golden rule 'do what your promoter tells you'. Neil told him to calm down he would do the job better then Silverstein ever could. Sanderson saw a loose cannon that he could use to go on tour in Japan and get the IWGP world title belt. Finally he had the opportunity to get WCCW international recognition. It was talked over a while and Sanderson would vacate the WCCW World title and send Neil to Japan as “Punishment”. The matches in Japan are known as shoot fights where the victor is determined by skill. Gideon went with Neil to be his translator and to see old friends in Japan as well. Neil wrestled with rival stars from other American promotions. He fought Vader, Stieners, Road Warriors, Sting, Hogan, Chris Benoit who was known as 'the Pegasus Kid', 'Macho Man' Randy Savage, Dr Death, Scott 'Flash' Norton, Lex Luger, and the late Ravishing Rick Rude. The IWGPs stars were Masa Chono, Kenji Muto (Great Muta), Riki Choshu, Kensuki Sasaki, Frank Shamrock, Jado and Gedo, Jushin 'Thunder' Liger, Stan 'the Lariet' Hansen, Hayabusa, and many more not as familiar in the US. During this period he got to face the best competition. Neil got to polish his mike skills a lot. Sting said in an interview that Neil was the 'Real Deal' and that led to his current nickname. He was part of a group known outside of the ring as the 'Gaijin Gang'. This group was Neil, Vader, Scott Norton, Paul Orndorff, and Rick Rude. Neil was good friends with Vader and Rick Rude. During their time in Japan Neil teamed with Rude and Vader a lot. Vader and Neil won the IWGP tag belts against Masa Chono and Tenryu. They lost the titles 2 months later against the Road Warriors. He won the IWGP World title belt and defended it for 10 months. He was eventually beaten by a wrestler called Destroyer in a brutal match that was over an hour long. That match is still talked about today as one of the best fights in IWGP history. Rick Rude and Neil had a vicious feud with the Stieners and the Road Warriors (see excerpt #7). Neil and Rick Rude dominated the feuds winning all but 5 of their 46 matches (2 losses were by DQ). Both of them played the ref to their advantage at all times. During their last 17 matches their opponents only had a total of 9 successful tags. Now Neil had ring presence and charisma. He knew how to work the referee. He decided to return home as a full time wrestler now for the WCCW. Gideon and Neil decided it was time to win the WCCW tag belts in a handicap match. He took the WCCW tag belts in brutal fashion by beating the tag champs in a handicap match (see excerpt #8). Sanderson cut loose Neil to save his promotion. He claimed that Neil created an unsafe working environment for the other wrestlers. That was because the roster was pitifully low quality and had no wrestlers anywhere near Neil's level. He first tried to head to WCW because that is where Rick Rude said he should go. Eric Bischoff and Hogan blocked all of Neil's attempts to come to WCW to protect Hulk Hogan and some other unnamed 'Senior Tour' members (see excerpt #9). WWF was next but Vince didn't want to have a wrestler that he couldn't control. Neil wasn't about to go by any of the lame gimmick ideas that were put out by the WWF 'creative staff' (see excerpt #10). The Trucker and Bowler gimmicks really sucked. In retrospect they aren't half as bad as other personas that have been used in the WWF. He saw a plummer, Aldo Montoya, 123 Kid, Smoking Gunns, Quebecers, M.O.M., Akeem, Hulk H... and the list goes on. He had a brief stint in ECW but it didn't go anywhere and was nowhere near as well promoted as the big 2 promotions (see excerpt #11). He tried getting noticed by creating disturbances in the WWF+WCW but security was eventually able to prevent Neil from getting anywhere near the cameras. Still he had friends in WCW and WWF working to get him signed. He was approached by Beast in 1999 to work his promotion UJW. Neil and Beast had met through their common hatred of Hogan. Beast wanted someone to end Hogan's career and Neil seems to be the man to do it. Gideon came along with Neil to help manage his long time friend. Neil trained Gideon in his spare time and felt that Gideon would have a lot of fun in Pro Wrestling and needed someone he could trust and talk to. Neil had signed up and even was drafted by Beast for the Mental House before his debut match against Mankind. Beast set up the run in where Al Snow would be the first of many victims of 'The Crush'. Al Snow’s UJW career was over that night thanks to Neil powerslamming Al into a ladder. His UJW career has given him a record of 13-1 3draws. He has defended his Hard Core belt 8 times successfully so far. He is also a former Cruiser Weight champ and 2 time tag champ with Beast. The record books show his incredible success in UJW. He has beaten Hogan, Nash, Hall, Mankind, FA, Havoc, Buff, as the most well known victims. He gave the Cruiser Weight belt away since he was way too large to be the champion. The HC title he left with undefeated. During this time these were the people who really stuck out. Fear and his new manager Beast split up the Mental House. Beast's right hand man Executioner went along with Beast. Trash, Neil, and BW were the other half of MH. Neil's contract with UJW was up. After wrestling a few matches for Methos after his contract expired he left the UJW to settle the score in the LWA. He left on good terms with Methos despite being unwilling to put anyone over for the Hard core belt. Neil knew that Methos could take care of the federation while the Alliance went hunting. He followed his new enemies the Empire to the LWA. This resulted in The Alliance and their teammate Trash taking on the Empire as many as 7 on 2. With Rock and a guy called Sam Hickman. Trash and The Alliance were nearly always out numbered as many as 7 on 3. They did have 2 allies Jerry Silver and Raven. They learned what screw jobs were all about and gave as good as they got. The wrestlers were mostly from UJW. Here is what Neil thought of each of them. Neil won the Hard core championship, The tag titles with Gideon, and more recently the title he still owns the LWA World title. Neil fought one of the best matches in 2000. The Iron man match between Nemesis and Neil for the Hard core belt ended with Neil leading the total 8 to 7. Neil VS FA was the rematch everyone was waiting for. Neil proved that his victory over FA in UJW was not 'just a fluke' that he was world championship material. Despite winning the match Neil hasn't proven he is better then FA just that he was better that night. When the federation closed Neil still didn’t get his vengeance on Sam Hickman for the tag title loss He followed the fleeing Empire to ECWF where Neil was immediately barred from competition. The reasons for the barring were never mentioned. Mabye they were worried about his growing hostility. Who knows the reason Neil was never allowed to compete. One thing is certain they had forgotten about Gideon. Gideon had Neil nearly in the roster when the Empire fled for parts unknown. This got worse as Neil finally got the president's attention. Gideon prevented Neil from seriously hurting him but got both of them were banned from the federation. Gideon got revenge by shutting down the promotion. Unfortunately for ECWF the lawyers still have the federation locked in legal battles which may prevent it from ever opening again. Neil looked up an old drinking buddy of his Jeremy but there were problems getting the time for an appointment so Neil went to his old stomping grounds the UJW. The talent was nearly gone. Neil was disgusted at the guys who were getting pushed. Curtain jerkers and Jobbers all getting belts. The owner was wearing Neil's Hardcore belt like he won it. Neil was furious and wanted a piece of Methos for letting this crap happen. Jeremy saw the problems and got Gideon and Neil to join XWU instead. At the time there was a federation war between Methos and Butcher. During this time Butcher was murdered in his own office (didn't make this up). The part that really sold Neil was a chance to work with BW. They were part of the Mental House together and watched each others backs. She may be the only person besides Gideon that he respects. Neil was signed very quickly to join the war on UJW. When Neil was in the XWU he was picking his shots carefully. He was picking who would be the first victim of the man who is the best technition in the world today and the biggest, strongest, nastiest, most violent SOB in the world. Some said it would be Fast Fashion. Some said the Undertaker. Many think it will be whoever is the Hardcore champ. One thing is for certain the 'Real F$#%ing Deal' isn't there to sign autographs he plans to hurt someone BAD. The big problem is the police shut down the Federation after a few 'incidents' that couldn't be ignored like another murder and some strange disappearances. BW had committed herself to an insane asylum. Neil and Gideon left to avoid the problems. There were a lot of people there that could have done it. No one knows but the police will eventually find out. Neil can get in enough trouble on his own. Jeremy told him he was welcome to come back though. Neil was called up out of the blue by Beast. He listened as Beast asked him to join the Empire in Titan wrestling. Neil told him to go to hell in a very graphic and inventive way. On the night of their big debut in Titan Wrestling Neil was in Jail. He got in a brawl in WCW getting payback for Paul Orndorff. When Neil found out that Gideon had them join the Empire he was very very pissed off. Their debut was against the team of Revolution Next. They won by a forfeit which saved the Revolution Next from the Alliance Arm lock and being literally disarmed. They defended the belts against the World champ Xavier Cross and then Continental champ Red Falcon. They beat the 'Pittsburgh Iron men' Revolution Next. They humiliated them toying with them both using moves that were only meant to ridicule the Revolution Next. Trash substituted for the Alliances next defense against Xavier and the No Fear hardcore champ Damien. They executed the Power Neck breaker on Damien which they called the 'Nuclear Power Breaker'. The federation shut down a short time later. Surprisingly Neil only fought once in singles. Neil had opinions about the stars and he still always shoots. Where does the Baddest man walking the planet go now? Neil has been in the movie “Vampire Hunters” and more recently the movies “Lobo” and “Duke Nukem Forever”. He has made appearances in UJW. Mad Dog has been running his mouth too much for Neil to let slide. He has hospitalized Daz after a horrific beating in the back. He has jumped Mad Dog during his cage match. He also jumped 3 of the SOA at the same time in the locker room. Methos is trying to stop him. Neil’s true motives are unknown and his victim list is up to 5. Will Methos even allow Neil to compete in the Federation again. Does Neil really care anymore? BW called Neil and Gideon and told them about a new federation that she was competing in called the Pacific Pro Wrestling. Neil looked at the roster there was decent talent and he was bored. The Alliance dominated the tag division. There wasn’t a team that could come close to competing on their level. Neil, Gideon, BW, and Sean formed a stable that was unstoppable. During this time they debuted their new finisher “The G-Man Slaughter in the 3 rd degree” or simply the “3G”. Neil and Gideon could hit the 3G out of nowhere and nailed half the roster with it at one time or another (Most of the List: Ted Billings, Bird of Prey, Byron, Dean Bradfield, Jimmy Mayhem, Ash Silverman, Jezebel, Sammy, Bob, and Big Show). Violence Incorporated run rough shod over the competition. BW would become a hardcore icon for dominating the Hardcore division. This was where the 24/7 rule became the champion’s advantage. With the 24/7 rule BW lost the belt and V.Inc jumped the new champ and less then 2 minutes later BW was a 2 time Hardcore champion. Sean would have a great run as the PPW Champ until he came upon the man that he couldn’t beat Ash Silverman. Wrestling fans say it was this that led to the feelings of jealousy and insecurity that caused him to break up with V.Inc. BW would take the PPW World title on her very first try from Silverman. During the last months of PPW everyone knew that the company was going under. Everyone was looking for a new federation to go to. Neil and Gideon had some friends for Titan Wrestling that told them about a new promotion they were working called New Era Wrestling. Their debut in NEW was marked by some controversy. Their first match they had a shot for the NEW tag titles in a 4 team sudden death match. It was a match they never lost decided by Kriss Zander a man not even involved in the match. Neil didn’t much like a lot of the people there. He thought that Damien was still a complete idiot and there were a lot of other guys he didn’t like. One guy he didn’t respect was Kriss Zander for F$%king around in his title match. There were too many people that were green and didn’t even come close to respecting the business let alone being wrestlers. He pretty much started in a violent feud against The Soldier Boyz. Tru Soldier disrespected the memory of his close friend Richard Rood (Rick Rude). It isn’t very surprising that was “Go time” right there. Neil ended the career of Tru Soldier for what he said and this led him into a violent feud with Sherman Pain. It was one of the most expensive matches in NEW history when they took the match to the parking lot. Neil won 2 out of the 3 falls of a best of 3 Falls count anywhere match. Things went well for them until the rest of Violence Inc arrived. Caleb “The Canadian Heart Throb” Alan quickly lost interest and was only there to hurt people and feed his ego by appearing on TV. Stephanie (Black Widow) came in and John Blaze quickly turned her into a “T and A act” to gain ratings. Stephanie and The Alliance were very close. They weren’t tolerating that type of garbage from John Blaze. Stephanie had paid her dues and has even been a World Champion in other federations. In NEW she was being degraded as a wrestler and been stuck fighting the talentless whores and sluts that passed for a NEW Valet. They all left NEW and The Alliance dropped the tag titles and Gideon dropped the Xtreme title on the mat in the middle of the ring in a house show. There were several calls after that but The Alliance returned none of them. The Alliance doesn’t talk very often about the UWL and that is for good reason. It was one of the most dangerous federations to work in filled with wack jobs and psychopaths. In wrestling that is saying a hell of a lot. There was no concern for wrestlers health or the health of anyone else. This was pretty much the purgatory of wrestling. If you worked here you either were kicked out of everywhere else and McDonalds wasn’t hiring or you were a violent asshole that didn’t play well with others. Gideon and Neil didn’t even have to bother to try and keep from hurting people. Here it is encouraged to hurt your opponents bad. This is backyard wrestling with a budget. While federations like ECW had a love of the sport there was only one thing driving people here money and fame. They fit in well here but they knew they were just filling in time. When Gideon hospitalized one of the Announcers The Alliance got fired. Neil and Gideon couldn’t have cared less and hospitalizing one of the staff on TV was about the only way to actually get fired. The IWF was very interesting and by interesting criminally insane. One of the old enemies of The Alliance and Stephanie had resurfaced. Here is the idea someone had. Give a guy even more violent and angry then Neil Fracas a federation and tons of money. Did I mention he is a real big guy? Neil Fracas is a giant of a man at 7ft2 and 359 pounds of muscle. The Executioner is 7ft4, 412 pounds. He hangs guys by their necks with a rope because he can’t be bothered to learn a decent chokehold. He had the contracts of Neil Fracas, Gideon Fontaine, and Black Widow. He had a federation and could run it like a dictatorship and booked any matches that amused him. They also had a friend from PPW named Caleb Alan. It was also interesting when Executioner used his money to get in people to make the Alliance’s life hell. This was a time when Stephanie had to retire from wrestling since she was about 2 months pregnant. The Black Widow became an announcer. As for Caleb…… Well he had very little scruples to begin with and Executioner enjoyed letting him run wild. In fact he encouraged it by feeding his already monstrous ego. Caleb had a match and his opponent was beaten so badly it is assumed that Caleb killed him. That wrestler was never see again so it was never proven one way or another what happened though authorities spent 4 weeks trying to prove it was a murder. The backers of the federation withdrew their support of Executioner not wanting attention drawn to them by the authorities. The federation closed during that time. Caleb was never officially charged during that time since there was no evidence to prove anything. Gideon’s Lawyers argued that it was all a show and with no evidence to prove that it wasn’t fake. While Gideon and Neil were free agents they were called by Bryan Chase to rejoin NEW. Even though Gideon and Neil had walked out with Stephanie they still had earned respect of many of the wrestlers of NEW. When Bryan Chase became the man in charge he called up Neil and Gideon knowing what they brought to the table in terms of skill and intensity. They came in as the newest additions to Bryan Chase’s Billion Dollar Productions stable. This lasted all of 15 minutes when The Alliance showed Bryan Chase in no uncertain terms that they would not be dictated to. They did things on their own terms and Neil and Gideon laid out Bryan Chase in the center of the ring with their patented 3rd degree. This led to a feud with Bryan’s replacements in the BDP Stone Devane and Ryzen. Neil was a bit pissed off at Stone Devane for being too stiff so he pretty said screw it and he started throwing down. Luckily it was ended before it escalated. This led to Stone Devane and Neil becoming great friends later after all they had many thigns in common. Stephanie was wrestling again and in the National title mix. She came close to winning it against John Blaze. She also took Yamato Megumi to the limits in her match for his TV title. It was during this time that the Alliance met an upcoming tag team that could bring the fight to them on their terms. They were The Way Hetero Connection better known as Ed Gesumaria and Steve Gugliociello. They had 3 matches and each one was filled with an intensity not seen in years. The WHC took the first match and The Alliance’s tag titles. The Alliance took their rematch winning a 3 way table match beating them and the Warriors of Darkness. The 3rd match was to be a ladder match which had Steve and Gideon going to the hospital for observation after an incredible sitdown crucifix powerbomb off a ladder through a table on the outside. Ed would be injured and Neil suffered a knee injury. The Alliance won that day but that was the day the tag division died. The Alliance and WHC raised the bar so high there was no way that they could ever get up to it again. There was no one willing to go that far. Gideon became a manager during this time for Ed and Neil. While Neil, Gideon, and Stephanie were competing in NEW they were also competing in Core Wrestling. Neil, Stephanie, and Gideon still have bad feelings between towards Core’s management of Matt Lanier and Jason Hughes. There hasn’t been much said when the Alliance left Core. There wasn’t much said when Stephanie walked out. They have never hated working in a federation so much in their lives. You could go through the wrestling magazines over the past few years and see people going in and out of Core. Few stayed any length of time before leaving Core. Most never even got to the stage where they were there long enough to get their bio pages on the Core Wrestling website. Someday they will explain in great detail what happened. For now they are glad they aren’t there and pity the few friends that remain. Neil’s knee injury worsened and John Blaze had to step in to force Neil to get it fixed before he ended up permanently ruining it. Neil had come very close to being the new NEW world champ being the final man eliminated in the battle royal for the NEW title by Yamato Megumi. He was top contender for the National title and a favorite. He was pissed because he lost it all. Gideon was bouncing back from his narrow loss in his signature match the G-Man Challenge against Billy McStoner where he lost 2 to 3. He would beat Craig Clayborn and then get a title shot for the National Title VS Trendkiller. After taking advantage of a key mistake by Trendkiller when he brought a chair in the ring Gideon won the National title. It would be shortly after this where Gideon would have to step up to help run NEW. Knowing that a person running the federation should never be it’s champion he vacated his title and put it up for grabs. The idea was to give new talent a chance to prove themselves and give them a goal. It was also during this time that Gideon started to really hate wrestling. He hated the pettiness and the selfishness that had grown like a cancer. The finals were winding down and it would be Sean Page and Christian White but Craig Clayborn wanted it to be a 3 way match. Despite Gideon not wanting to be involved he was signed to the match and entered the match hating many things. He was in no frame of mind to be competing and angry. He let his NEW contract expire and he was under no obligation to even wrestle the match. It was assumed that Gideon would sign a new contract being one of NEW’s strongest supporters. Gideon of course wrestled the match. It never showed during the match but this would be the time when Gideon would decide to leave wrestling. Craig Clayborn and Bryan Chase struggled to keep NEW running but it was dying and everyone knew. They wanted the final card to be special and be one that will be talked about for decades. Gideon decided to comeback and would go for the tag titles one last time and help send them out in a blaze of glory but it never happened. Bryan Chase working to make that final card happen had died and NEW closed shortly after. Neil and Stephanie at this point had no interest in ever going back to wrestling. Gideon saw NEW reopened under new management and offered to help. The help was refused due to certain people bad mouthing Gideon. This management was doomed to fail. It took only 3 weeks. Craig Clayborn and Zakk Wylde tried to revive NEW and for a while it looked like they would succeed. After 2 weeks it started unraveling. The egos started again and Craig left. Gideon stepped up to help but Neither Zakk or Gideon had any stomach for politics and whining. The board of directors were brought in by a few unhappy roster members. It was over and it is likely that NEW will never open again. The memories will be there of a federation that stood for pride and sacrifice. Times when wrestling was what the wrestlers lived for. That time passed and on that day the Alliance turned their backs on the sports entertainment that wrestling had become.
