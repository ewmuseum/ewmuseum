Burnie McCoy Biography Nicknames The Snazzy One Hometown Fife, Washington (born in South Carolina) Birthday 6-12-79 Height 5'8" Weight 181 Fed Titles Bodyguard to the prestigious Mr. Silver, Chairman of PRIME Fed Awards Snazziest dressed man in this joint. Biography Burnie McCoy, born Mike Guttormson, was looking for the way out of the PRIME Wrestling Academy after seeing various other stars pass him by. Sonny Silver came to he and Chet "Slash McCoy" Rockmore for the part of bodyguards for his Silver Inc. empire. More than happy to do so, the two were finally promoted with talks of fame and fortune behind their wildest dreams. Of course, Sonny never said WHEN he would give them that. Other Feds N/A Other Fed Awards N/A Wrestling Style High Flyer/Bumptaker/Punching Bag Finisher Move The After-Burn Finisher Description Sliced Bread #2 (applies 3/4 facelock, runs up turnbuckle/ropes, backflips over opponent and drives them into mat with Reverse DDT) Finisher Setup Move Usually comes out of nowhere Setup Description See above. Special Moves Getting kicked around. Other Moves Common spots: Loads of cheapshots, basic punches and kicks, Dropkick variations, Swinging Neckbreaker, Flying Back Elbow, taking lots of bumps, bleeding a lot, basic high-flying moves (splashes, elbows, legdrops, moonsaults, etc.). BURNIE McCOY NOTES: -Burnie McCoy doesn't know the meaning of the words "shut up." He doesn't curse at all, but does paintbrush the back of his opponent's head which leads to him getting his ass kicked. -For the perfect comparison, think of Burnie McCoy as being a white version of Carlton Banks from "Fresh Prince of Bel-Air." He's preppy, fairly articulate, and basically a social outcast/dork. -He will cheat like nuts to keep the advantage in a match. He's quicker than a hiccup and hard to catch as well, making him at least dangerous in that department. Once you catch him, though, he can't take a lot of damage before he loses a match. Entrance Music "Money In The Bank" by Lil Scrappy Ring Entrances His entrance music hits. He comes to the ring. That's pretty much it. You figure it out.
