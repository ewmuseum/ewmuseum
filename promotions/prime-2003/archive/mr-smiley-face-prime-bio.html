Mr Smiley Face Biography Nicknames The Smilin' Dude, The Human Emoticon, MSF Hometown Parts Unknowns Birthday sometime in 1978 Height 6'3 Weight 245 lbs Biography The masked comedic persona of Michael Kriegman Wrestling Style Brawling, limited high flying, comedic chaos. Finisher Move Tickle Me, Jobber Finisher Description Tongan Death Grip Other Moves Gear: Mr Smiley Face ALWAYS wears a square cardboard bright yellow smiley face mask, black boots, black jeans, black gloves, and a black trenchcoat with an upside down smiley face button on the lapel and the words KICK YOURSELF in bright yellow letters on the back. He wears the trench open and a yellow shirt underneath Style: Brawling, limited high flying, comedic chaos. (Note that he doesn't do the high risk daredevil moves that Trash is known for, but when he does fly, it's a bit more graceful than the sloppiness that TCM's known for) Strengths: Mr Smiley Face's main strength is his unpredictability. You never know if that right hand is coming towards you to punch you in the face or give you a purple nurple. He's evil like that. Weaknesses: Mr Smiley Face lacks the resiliancy of the Trashcan Man. Same body, sure, but different mindset. He can't shrug off the punishment like Trash can. He also has a tendency to go for the laugh instead of the win. This can and has cost him matches. Standard moves: Flying Leg Lariat, Fisherman's Suplex, Enziguiri, Short Arm Clothesline, Samoan Drop, Double Arm DDT, Reverse DDT, Pumphandle Slam Trademark Moves Last Laugh: Somersault neckbreaker from the top rope(aka Blockbuster) Turn That Frown Upside Down: Tree of Woe followed by standing on an opponent's groin Doink: Same as TCM, some things are just truly universal What Comes After L?Bow!: An elbow drop where Mr Smiley Face will ask the crowd 'What comes after L? and will wait for the crowd to shout back 'Bow!' before dropping an elbow. Is about as effective as the People's Elbow should be, so it should be enough to finish off jobbers. Kick of CHEER~!: MSF will pull out a yellow Sharpie and draw a smiley face on the sole of his boot before delivering the big boot Finisher: Tickle Me Jobber (Tongan Death Grip) Rare Dagoth Slayer: EXTREMELY powerful unprotected pulling piledriver, the thighs are as far down on the neck as possible before the piledriver is delivered, forcing the head, neck, and spine to receive the full force blow. This is a VERY VERY rare move. Tactics: Chaos incarnate. Three Stooges meets The Joker.
