Wrestling Style Japanese light-heavyweight Finisher Move The Jack Lalanne Driver Finisher Description Sheerdrop pumphandle Omega Driver Finisher Setup Move Atomic Thunder Muffin (ATM) Setup Description Running double knee strike to the chest Other Moves 'Slingblade' Neckbreaker across the knee Fireman's carry into a neckbreaker across the knee Straightjacket/cross-arm neckbreaker Straightjacket head scissors Arm-trap belly to back suplex Top turnbuckle front flip cannonball splash Frankenberry (Frankensteiner, this is never held into the pinning rana... it also has the most amazing name for a move in the history of life) Yokosuka Cutter Kondo Clutch/Gorilla Clutch Inverted overdrive Hammerlock reverse Russian legsweep "Falcon Punch" -- A move he blatantly stole from Captain Falcon of Super Smash Brothers. It's a wild right handed haymaker. "The Liu Kang" (AKA: The Mortal Kombat 2) -- King Blueberry charges a cornered opponent before leaping into the air legs first, flailing them wildly in a bizarre attempt to kick his adversary. Tiger Feint Frankenberry (RARE) -- A combination Tiger Feint Kick (think "619"), though instead of striking his opponent in the head he'll use his momentum to execute a frankensteiner and pull his opponent through the ropes to the outside. Because of proximity to the ring and ring steps, there's a high chance of self-injury when pulling this off. "Jack Lalanne Driver, Late-Night Infomercial Edition" (RARE) -- Wrist-clutch Psycho Driver. This is an "extreme cases" move. If the normal JLD fails to put someone down, or if a heated feud is having its blow-off match, he'll go to this. NOTE: King Blueberry does not have any "my opponent doesn't kick out" moves, and he doesn't have any "unbreakable submission hold" moves either. Entrance Music "Go. Fighto.
