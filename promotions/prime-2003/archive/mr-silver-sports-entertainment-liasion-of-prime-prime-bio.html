"...a clinical sociopath... the description of which is fully elaborated on only within the confines of his deranged psyche. -C.P. Cantrell. The Hall of Famer lounges around PRIME generally acting as a second to CP Cantrell's machinations for retaining PRIME's top spot in the wrestling world via shenanigans, tomfoolery, and espousing the greatness of the Sports Entertainment lifestyle that has made him rich. Generally seen backstage, being laid-back with best friends Dametreyus Fuqueiwytas and Nova. While causing C.P. Cantrell many, many, many migraines. He's here. You're queer. Get used to it. Other Feds PWA, CDC, OSW/F/E/whatever other initials are there, tSC, fWo, moonlighting in LoC and ACW Other Fed Titles tSC Championship, fWo Tag Team Championship Other Fed Awards former ACW Commissioner, The Mark 2004 Co-Heel of the Year w/Craig Miles, The Mark 2005 Tag Team of the Year with Lindsay Troy. Wrestling Style Management Hard-Style: Fuckin' Badass. Special Moves Hitting you until you die from it. Other Moves Mr. Silver Moveset Nowadays, seeing Mr. Silver wrestle any sort of match is occasional and rare. Think Shane-O-Mac himself. Though he’s pretty much a deluded egomaniac and sits back letting his charge, Captain Justice do all the heavy lifting, there's zero doubt he can defend himself against just about anybody put across from him. He IS a former Universal and Intense Champion for a reason. Should the Sports Entertainment Liaison of PRIME find himself in a situation that requires some of his own personal finesse, one can liken his wrestling style to a hard-hitting version of a Sports Entertainer, making each blow sound incredibly stiff with proper contact. Silver, for all intents and purposes is a low-down, goddamn, bastard rotten cheater apt on attacking limbs and taking advantage of obvious weaknesses/injuries. He’s also great at selling in his own right, being a true veteran and selling everything up to and including comedy offense. He likes to talk loads of smack which can both be an advantage and detriment, depending largely on the opposition. Walking to and from the ring, Mr. Silver will always utilize the arm-flapping power-walk made famous by Vince McMahon, though he’ll relentlessly deny such a petty theft. He’s also prone to making fun of opponents and stealing occasional signature moves, even going so far as to mock-chant the other guy’s name in unison with the crowd when he’s in firm control. All this together makes Sonny a force that can defend himself, separating him from your token punching bag manager (though Sonny is often still the recipient of blunt offense from Captain Justice’s opponents if it means distracting the opponent long enough for his boy to get the victory) Regular Moves Going the route of the Sports Entertainer, much of Sonny’s moveset is comprised of lots of brawling and showy moves, although his blows (mainly his kicks) are among the most lethal in PRIME today and have knocked out people before. Here’s the main moves Sonny sticks to in his matches. -Punching -Kicking (of the Toshiaki Kawada variety. A succession of these will stop anybody in their tracks) -Gratuitous stomping -Vertical Suplex -Quick Back Suplex -German Suplex -Kneedrop to the head -Clothesline -Yakuza Kick -Scoop Slam -Pendulum Backbreaker -Swinging Neckbreaker -DDT -Armbars/legbars and lots of them. -Other "nothing fancy" rest holds like standard Boston Crabs, head/chinlocks, et al. Signature Moves -The Shitkicking A staple in the Silver moveset for going on four or so years but only recently named by some stupid fanboy in an e-mail, the move is the Facewash made popular by Samoa Joe. Running his boot along the opponents face propped in a corner, he bounces off the adjacent ropes and comes back, flooring the opponent with a nasty kick or knee to the head. Used in just about every match he’s ever competed in, this is a great close fall maneuver. -The Great Jesus Driver Not seen in some time, but worth mentioning, upon entry of the Mr. Silver, Chairman of PRIME gimmick, Sonny utilized the Uranage Suplex/Rock Bottom made famous by some jobber named Dwayne Johnson. It’s a rarity that he’ll pull this out anymore, but it’s a good one to note. Finishers - The Corona The signature high-impact Roundhouse to the head that has been a staple of the Silver offense for years. Doesn’t really see much use anymore since he’s not much for wrestling and Devin Shakur often utilizes a swifter, equally hard-hitting variation known as Good Times, Painful Memories. But don’t let that fool you into thinking that you won’t see it on the occasion Sonny wants to help CJ win a match by popping some guy upside the head. - The Saito Suplex This move – a high-angled belly-to-back suplex dumping the victim on the back of their head – has won many matches for Sonny. A personal favorite of his to bust out on opponents except Lindsay Troy who has an incredibly annoying penchant for kicking out of the goddamn thing. Nevertheless, a very potent finisher. Entrance Music 'The Shit' by Danger Mouse and Jemini. Cause he's the shit. And a bad motherfucker. Ring Entrances COMING SOON.... ISH.
