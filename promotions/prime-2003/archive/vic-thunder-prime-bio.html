Vic Thunder Biography Wrestler Name Vic Thunder Nicknames "The One Man Natural Disaster", "The Storm of the Century", "The Modern Titan" Hometown Ames, Iowa Birthday 12-11-84 Height 6'7 Weight 290 lbs. Biography A standout high school and collegiate wrestler and imposing physical specimen, Vic Thomas was a shoe in for the wrestling business. Victor entered training right out of college, then floundered around the indies for several months. He was raw, he was powerful, he was imposing...he was green. He made mistakes, and he never quite picked up the momentum and direction a powerhouse of his caliber was capable of. Enter Dave Snyder. The proverbial "agent to the stars" in the global Overzealous Pro Wrestling promotion, Snyder saw the raw and limitless potential in Victor Thomas. Snyder took Vic under his wing, using his influence and connections to strip away the young man-mountain's greenhorn tendencies and rookie mistakes, honing him into a true force of nature. Within a matter of months, Victor Thomas was dead and buried, and Vic Thunder was born. Snyder quickly worked out an exclusive deal with OPW (of which he got a massive percentage, of course), and his new monster went on a rampage, winning the World Heavyweight championship three times before injuring his knee in a defense against Antonio Chipotle, Jr. After several months of intense rehab and following the lapse of his OPW contract, Snyder now intends to lead Thunder to different pastures, and even greater heights elsewhere...namely PRIME. Other Feds OPW Other Fed Titles OPW World Heavyweight Champion (3x) Manager Dave Snyder Wrestling Style Pure Power with an underlying technical base. Athletically freakish in all aspects.
