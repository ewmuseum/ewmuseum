Nitz Donnelly He started out small, sending portable DVD players to interested opponents and walking around with an unmatched swagger. He was hellbent on backing up every ounce of trash talk inside the squared circle. After what some would consider a failed stint, Donnelly came back as part of Change in Spades with Vance Raymes and was one half of the final Tag Champions of PRIME. He has since gone on and carved out a successful singles career: finishing top 10 in the 2010 Dual Halo, rising to the top of the card, and winning the 5*Star Championship at ReVolution 234 against Boda. It is the first singles gold of his career. Tonight, he looks to add his name amongst legends. Violence Jack Widely considered one of the most dangerous men to ever step foot in a wrestling ring, or behind a microphone, The Pariah Saint is known for his brawling ability and endurance. The man can take a beating from anybody and keep on keeping on. Violence Jack has never had a successful PRIME stint, but if he came up with a pair of victories tonight, he would be in rare company. The Sentinel A man who has drawn confusion from fans and critics alike after announcing his retirement, but wrestling in the Jewel in the Crown tournament with a vigor unmatched by any other competitor in the tournament. He has run through his opponents like a hot knife through butter with an overwhelming amount of violence. Even with the stiff competition, some are considering him the favorite because of his relentless style in the ring. Alexandra Pierce One of PRIME's most polarizing and controversial figures. She was perhaps the most evil woman in wrestling for a stint when she was at the helm, so to speak, of SCCW. She won GTT7. She drew the ire of Lindsay Troy, Tyler Rayne, and others during a small invasion angle before Cataclysm back in the ReVolution 170s. There is no doubt she is one of the most devastating wrestlers on the roster when it comes to strikes, craftiness, and intuition.
