The Blue Scorpion Biography Wrestler Name The Blue Scorpion Nicknames The Ruthless Patriarch, Everyone's Favourite Underdog, Scorpio Hometown Leicester, England (now residing in Atlanta, GA) Birthday 11th May 1966 Height 6'2'' Weight 247lbs Biography Toothless, moody and aggressive, The Blue Scorpion (or Scorpio as he is affectionately known) has won respect from fans and fellow wrestlers alike with his no-nonsense attitude and ruthless in-ring style. Born and raised in the UK, Scorpio moved to America to follow his dreams of becoming a professional wrestler, going against the wishes of his father, who himself was a successful professional wrestler. Scorpio needed to be street-savvy just to survive in the cut-throat business, but he took that with him into pro-wrestling. The road to wrestling stardom was a long and arduous one for Scorpio, but he steadily rose up the ranks in several feds, yet never quite reached the big time in any of the companies he was with. Finally, after over twenty years in the business, Scorpio's most recent company also proved to be his most successful, as he dispelled his reputation as a journeyman wrestler and enjoyed a successful 18 months with Championship Wrestling Federation, becoming a 3-time tag team champion before finally winning the big one, and becoming a World Champion. Despite his success, Scorpio has become disillusioned with the new (mis-)management at CWF, and is looking to take his talents and experience elsewhere. Other Feds CWF (Championship Wrestling Federation), Hostility, GTWF (GameTalk Wrestling Federation) Other Fed Titles 3-time CWF Tag-Team Champion, 1-time CWF World Champion, 2-time GTWF Hardcore Champion Wrestling Style Brawler, but with a strong technical base Finisher Move Scorpion Claw Finisher Description High Impact Fisherman's Buster - a cross between a fisherman's suplex and a brainbuster. Special Moves Scorpion Sting - modified Belly-to-Belly in which Scorpio follows through with his opponent and lands on top of them as they hit the canvas, a la Shane Douglas Other Moves Sidewinder Suplex, Belly-to-Back Superplex, German Suplex with Bridge, Windup Headbutt, Figure-Four Leg Lock, Spear from the Top Rope, Successive Running Clotheslines, Successive Shoulder Thrusts into Corner, Implant DDT, Flying Forearm Smash, Tiger Suplex.
