Castor V Strife Biography Wrestler Name Castor V Strife Nicknames The God of Snuff, Emperor of Endings, The Final Scene;The GOLDEN DREAM! Alright, you don’t have to call him all that stuff. Hometown Hollywood, California Birthday 10/15/1980 Height 6'3 Weight 245 lbs. Fed Titles PRIME Universal Championship Biography Details regarding the early life of Castor V. Strife are sketchy at best. He was born on October 15, 1980 in Pavia, Italy, in the region of Lombardy . At the age of 10, his parents moved to Los Angeles, California before eventually retiring in Arizona. Castor, by then 16, was working as an intern for Metro-Goldwyn-Meyer and decided to stay in Hollywood to pursue his dream of being a famous director. By the time he was in film school, he was working weekends in a local wrestling promotion where he would do video distribution in exchange for booking. Quite the prodigy in the ring, he learned the ropes rather quickly and had several tryouts for big-time organizations by the end of college. Fast forward to 2010, Castor is a 9 year veteran of the business, as well as owner of an indie production studio, "Castor V. Strife Productions." His films have attained cult status in foreign markets, and has a niche in the United States. Strange days have found him, though, and he has reluctantly agreed to having a journalist write a feature series on his dark days in Hollywood, as well as his journey through unknown waters in PRIME. Other Feds NFW, A1E Other Fed Titles NFW TV Title, A1E Challenge Championship Other Fed Awards 2002 NFW Co-Rookie of the Year Wrestling Style Well rounded wrestler that is incredibly athletic for a 245 pounder. Style is akin to Lance Storm. This move is only to be used in very dramatic circumstances where a basic finisher isn’t enough.
