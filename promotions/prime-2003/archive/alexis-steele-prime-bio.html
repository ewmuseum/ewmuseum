Alexis Steele Biography Manager Name Alexis Steele Nicknames The Queen of Sexy Hometown Denver, CO Birthday 1/30/1989 Height 5'6 Weight 125 lbs Biography Alexis Steele formerly Alexis Gunnz is a former Covergirl, Playmate and International Model. Growing up in Denver, CO Alexis had everything. She had a loving family and a great neighborhood. Her mother was a Nuerologist and her father was a Surgical Doctor, both very prestigous in their professions. Once Alexis graduate high school she seeked out to pursue her already booming modeling career. She moved from home to New York and that is where her career sky rocketed. From magazine covers to big Modeling photos. Alexis was known everywhere by the age of 21. From playmate of the year to International model she was known by all as the girl every woman wanted to be and every man's fantasy. While on a trip to Atlanta she met Bishop Steele and they hit it off from day one which eventually grew into a promising relationship and marriage. The two took what they learned growing up and made what is now Perfection INC. A multi-billion dollar clothing company that Alexis is head of PR and Operations, these two are on the up and up. Now focused on Bishop's wrestling career, Alexis will be a problem for every female and their superstars simply because she is very attractive and has a I dont give a shit attitude. She is the fantasy of all and the bitch every woman hates. She is Alexis Steele.
