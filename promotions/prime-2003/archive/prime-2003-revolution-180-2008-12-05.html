As Ryane buckles forwards with the impact of the knee Vashaun catches him around the neck and slams him face first into the canvas with a vicious DDT. Rolling over he grabs one of Rayne’s legs and calls for the referee who slides quickly into position. Nick: Going for the quick win here. ONE … Richard: INCOMING!!! From out of nowhere … well, actually, from the top turnbuckle of the nearest corner … a semi-recovered Bryan Dawkins launches himself through the air with a frogsplash. TWO … Vashaun catches the movement out of the corner of his and tries to move out of the way – just a moment too late! Dawkin’s body crashes into the back of Vashaun and the two men’s bodies crush Rayne under their combined weight. The referee jumps back out of the way, his count interrupted, as the three men roll apart. Nick: Dawkin’s makes the save there! Richard: At the cost of his own body, though. Nick: Looks like a pile up on the highway in the ring … Richard: … without the cars! Nick: SIGH – yes, without the cars. Richard: So, really, nothing like a pile up then? Nick: How about it looks like three wrestlers just collided and are now collapsed on the canvas? Richard: Literal and factual … I like it! Dawkins is the first man back to his feet but he is holding his ribs and walking gingerly. As Vashaun gets back to his own feet Dawkins moves forwards and catches the unsuspecting opponent off his feet with a text book perfect arm drag, before dropping a leg across the face and head of the much larger man. Nick: Text book perfect arm drag there! Richard: Spooky – I felt like saying that exact same thing for some reason. Getting back to his feet as quickly as he can Dawkin’s nips up to the top rope once more and, taking a deep breath in, he looks up to the rafters before jumping into mid air and turning full circle. Nick: Moonsault! Richard: … or not! While Dawkins is in mid-air, his body flaying towards the prone Vashaun, Tyler Rayne – with almost superhuman resilience – has nipped to his feet, kicking a rising Vashaun in the back of the head on the way past, and run towards the hurtling Hawaiian. Jumping into the air, as Dawkins can do nothing other than mouth a horrified ‘no’, Rayne throws his whole body forwards and the two men meet in mid air. Unfortunately for Dawkins his face meets Rayne’s knees. Nick: Immovable object meets irresistible force! Richard: Which is which? Nick: Well as Rayne is the only one still able to see straight I think that he may just be both! Tyler Rayne staggers back to his feet and – in a display that doesn’t happen too often – the audience are on their feet cheering for the man. Unfortunately the same cannot be said for Dawkins who is lying in a crumpled heap on the canvas. Richard: Unbelievable – if I hadn’t sent that I don’t think that I would have believed it! Nick: I can’t believe that Rayne is on his feet! Richard: … and that isn’t all. Pulling the semi-conscious Dawkins back to his feet Rayne reaches under him and, hooking him into a variation of a standing small package, drops the Hawaiian viciously down onto the back of his head, holding on to the move as the referee slides into position. Nick: Dawkins has just been von Doom’d! ONE … TWO … THREE DING DING DING Vince Howard: The winner of this match … TYLER RAYNE!!! Nick: He did it! Tyler Rayne is the first Jewel in the Crown semi-finalist! Folks, we're out of time. Tune in for ReVolution 181, coming to you live from the BankAtlantic Center in Sunrise, Florida!
