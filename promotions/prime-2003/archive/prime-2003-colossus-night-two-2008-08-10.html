Colossus Night Two ---- YOU ARE NOT READY. As "Memorial" by Explosions In The Sky cues up, we see Charlotte's Bank of America Stadium...empty. A wide shot shows the venue all set for Night Two of PRIME's biggest show, but there are no fans there to enjoy it. Just a ring and an entrance and an empty, yawning stretch of seats. "You aren't ready." The weathered, almost raspy voice of "The Specialist", Tony Rolo, reverberates over the music. From the wide shot of the stadium, we cut to another shot, over the shoulder of the first-ever Hall of Fame competitor as he stands in the massive entryway of the Parthenon replica that sits atop the stage. His body is silhouetted against the bright lights illuminating the ring. "You never are." The full body of the music kicks in as Rolo just stands, staring out into the stadium. The scene cuts all the way across the venue, where Killean Sirrajin, dressed in street clothes, stands at the railing on the first row of the upper deck. He looks down toward the ring and tips his head to look over his trademark glasses. "You pretend that you are. You tell everyone that you've prepared for it like it was any other match. But it's not." Joey Troy sits in the front row, just feet from the ring. With his feet up on the rail, he leans back and eats a few pieces of popcorn from the jumbo-sized bag in the seat next to him. "Every stop, every show is an attempt to top yourself. Always getting bigger. Always getting better. You can't tell anyone you've been here before because you haven't. It's never been like this." Backstage, Hoyt Williams sits on a training table, eyes lost in thought, hair unkempt but clothes meticulously tailored. He stares at a stack of athletic tape on the bench next to him and sighs. "The butterflies will come, and the uneasiness will creep in. Any confidence you tried so hard to build will completely abandon you when you hear them." Back in the stadium, Karina Wolfenden stands in the ring and kicks absently at the bottom turnbuckle. Running a hand through multicolored hair, she spins slowly, looking out into the expanse of the stands. "Thousands of screaming fans...it can be the greatest sound in the world when you're in this ring. But for every minute before you step between the ropes, it's the most terrifying sound in the world." Outside, just beyond the stadium in the parking lot, Nova lights a cigarette and slams shut the door of his car. He looks up at the massive arena and the shadows cast by the light. "You doubt everything...the work you've done, the time you spent in the gym, the tape you watched. All of it leaves you in a matter of seconds." Ignatius Lisieux stands in the opposite end zone of the stadium, eyes transfixed on the sprawling entryway that stands 100 yards away. Hands in the pockets of his jeans, he seems almost serene...appreciative. "Say all you want about preparation - what could possibly prepare you for this, the biggest match of your life on the biggest stage? What in the world makes you think you're ready?" Back behind that massive entryway, Tchu sits in a folding chair just beyond the gorilla position. His head is down, looking at the floor, lost in thought. "So you welcome the nervousness and the fear, let them take you. It's natural. If you don't feel them every time you're about to perform, you're just...inhuman."
