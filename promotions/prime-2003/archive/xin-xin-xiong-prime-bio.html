Xin Xin Xiong Biography Nicknames X3, The Dark Hometown West Point, NY Birthday 12/2/80 Height 6' Weight 229 Biography Born and bred in West Point, NY until the age of 13. At that age, he was sent to Japan to study ninjitsu under his grandfather, Naito Futeki, as he was the last in a long line of masters in their clan. While in Japan, he worked as a janitor and member of the ring crew for the wrestling promotion Toryumon to pay the bills since his grandfather was as he put it 'a lazy bum' and didn't work. One day, he and one Nobuyoshi Nakamura (a.k.a. the famous Dragon Kid) got into a heated debate and X3 took out the masked fighter. This caught the attention of Ultimo Dragon who started to train the young ninja in the ways of puroresu. The upstart showed amazing prowless in the art of human chess and developed a strategy based off his favorite wrestler, Keiji Mutoh. In no time at all, Xiong had become a master at both ninjitsu and puroresu and things looked to be going well for him. That was until his father became deathly ill with brain cancer. He rushed back to America just in time to see his father one last time. He remained with his mother, paying the bills by working as a bouncer while wrestling on the independent circuit. He stepped into the ring, looking to show the world what a little mixture of old school martial arts and new school wrestling can do. However, the lies, coruption, and evil that he found forced him to return to his roots. Whether it is as an assassin for hire or vigilante at large, is yet to be seen...
