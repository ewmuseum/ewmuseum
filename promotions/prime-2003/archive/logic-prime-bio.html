Logic Biography Wrestler Name Logic Nicknames The Thinking Man's Grappler, The Pensive Punisher, the Squared Circle's Socratic, et al. Hometown Orlando, FL Birthday 4/18/81 Height 6'9 Weight 288 lbs Biography Coming Soon! Other Feds fWo, OCW, XUW Other Fed Titles XUW Television Champion (as Prof. Wisdom) Other Fed Awards The third-place finisher in the fWo Survivor IV contest. Wrestling Style Calculating technical powerhouse Finisher Move Proof Positive Finisher Description The best backdrop driver in the business. Finisher Setup Move Anything he can use to get Proof Positive set up. Setup Description Yeah. See above. Special Moves The Socratic Method (Two-Handed Choke Bomb -- debuted 5/26 at UltraViolence v. Killean Sirrajin) Other Moves Anytime Moves: HARD Straight Right, Palm Strikes, Delayed Vertical Suplex, Kneelifts, Powerslam, Big Boot, Bearhug, Sidewalk Slam, Corner Splash, Standing Splash Aerial Moves: Top Rope Splash, Top Rope Diving Clothesline, Vader-style Pump Splash Power Moves: Crucifix Powerbomb, Multiple Backbreaker, German Suplex, Sit-Down Spinebuster, Delayed Brainbuster, Death Valley Driver, Screwdriver, Press Slam Variants (to Samoan Drop, to Over-the-Shoulder Powerslam, to Gutbuster, to Backbreaker, to Death Valley Driver, etc) Submission Moves: Standing Anklelock, Crossface Chickenwing. Rare Moves (PPV or big match only): Elbow Suicida Setup and Finisher: See bio. Strengths: 1) Intellect. Logic's smart. SMART, smart. Genius IQ, member of Mensa, the whole nine yards. When it comes to gameplanning, there are few better than Logic. He's not going to make cardinal mistakes. No, he will make you make those mistakes instead. 2) Well-rounded. Not many 6'9" guys who have an anklelock, a crossface chickenwing, and a splash off the top rope in their arsenal. Logic does. His size makes even simple submission moves harder to escape, and a simple splash off the top potential death. He uses his power for more than just power moves, and his arsenal of moves reflects this. 3) Knock-Out Finisher. Proof Positive is a nuclear bomb in Logic's arsenal. He can pull it out quickly, from a lot of situations, and if he hits you with it you are most likely NOT getting up. Weaknesses: 1. Submissions. He doesn't tap out EASY, per se, but where someone like Lindsay Troy would fight tapping like Hell in a throwaway match on ReVolution, Logic will tap if he can't find a way out and fears the risk of lasting/permanent damage. Better to live to fight another day. 2. Rigid. As smart as he is, he is equally stubborn. He develops strategies based on probabilities and analysis. Opponents who take a lot of chances give him fits, as he will expect them to crash and burn more often than they likely will. Once he determines a plan, he will stick to it with almost slavish dedication, out of fear of improvising causing a loss. 3. Frustration. He is prone to being frustrated against particularly tenacious opponents, who can survive a lot of his big bombs. On the uber-rare occassion Proof Positive is kicked out of, he will lose his calm vaneer and become VERY vulnerable to quick flash pins. Flash pins in general are a weakness of his, especially once his head's starting to go in bad places. In-Ring Tendencies: 1. Logic will try to keep matches in the ring at all costs. He has very limited hardcore experience (mostly involving Lindsay Troy and Chinese throwing stars) and tries to avoid it if at all possible. Fewer variables that he has to keep track of within the squared circle. 2. Logic, not being an idiot, is pretty much immune to distractions by managers, people appearing on the tron, etc. If a referee is distracted, he will generally physically turn the referee around and make the official count falls. 3. His general gameplan is to start off slow, testing out an opponent with basic moves and strikes, building to his more potent offense. Once he gets a few close twos, he will move to try to finish with Proof Positive. Ring Entrances Coming Soon!
