Harmony Fallacia Biography Manager Name Harmony Fallacia Nicknames Harm Biography Before The Freak, Himself found Harmony in an abandoned mansion at the top of a hill, Harmony had connections with Dr. Lescher and Lunacy Muse. However, once Freak found the broken, slightly damaged girl eating off of empty plates and slipping every other step in tube socks and an undone straight jacket, Freak took her into his mansion to try and help her. From the get go, Harmony displayed an insatiable curiosity for the anatomy of living creatures. Many an animal fell by her bare hands, her bare hands holding a chainsaw, her bare hands holding a toaster…Harmony thought of more creative ways to experiment on living things and cause torture (though, by her own admission, she’s only curious and tried to end it quickly and as painless as possible…) than the writers of the Saw films. Freak tried shock treatment, drugs, and various other forms of treatment to stop Harmony’s homicidal tendencies toward the animal kingdom, but nothing changed. Slowly, however, Harmony’s growing knowledge seemed to bore her. Though still perky, insane, but completely loveable and intelligent, her urges to kill animals grew more sporadic. While in the NPA, Harmony was offered a job by the FBI, who had been following Harmony’s unorthodox torture exploits (including the PetSmart debacle where half of the animals in the store ended up dead by Harmony’s hands or the jaws of the released predators…poor bunny rabbits…), offered her a job in the secret Information sect of the FBI, which basically meant she got to torture suspected terrorists and criminals for information. Harmony jumped at the chance, and though Freak remains somewhat disturbed when she has to bring her work home, he’s happy she found something to do that she loves. Now that she’s been…mostly rehabilitated, Harmony will step back into the ring on a part time basis with now boyfriend Freak in the new tag team, Delirium, and will also accompany him to the ring on occasion. Harmony Fallacia has come along way since that mansion, but she’s a long way from done. Other Feds CWA, NPA, WFWA Other Fed Titles CWA Tag Team Champion X 1, NPA Tag Team Champion X1 Weapon Details Whatever the hell she can find, she can use...she toasted a gerbil, for crying out loud.
