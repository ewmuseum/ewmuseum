Skylar Green Biography Nicknames Cielo, Midori, SG Unlimited Hometown Fort Lauderdale, FL Birthday 4/29/78 => 27 Height 6'5' Weight 239 Fed Titles No titles Fed Awards No awards Biography Background Skylar Green was trained to wrestle in Japan, although he hasn't done much wrestling of any significane in that country. Remember the Toryumon 2000 Project (T2P)? Of course you don't. It's counted as the 'forgotten class' of dojo students. Those who weren't absorbed into the main Japan class simply disappeared from Japanese sight. And that is the case of Skylar. So though his first wrestling training was in Japan, as a professional he has wrestled primarily in the U.S., Italy, and Germany, though he has spent a few learning excursions in Mexico, Puerto Rico, and Canada. He's taken something of a two-month sabbatical to Brazil a couple of years ago, during which he chose to engage in some vale tudo training. Though not an expert in the combat form, he absorbed that training into his wrestling so that he has some chance at defending himself against more skilled fighters. He's become a better hand-to-hand combatant by facing greater hand-to-hand combatants than himself. He learned how to fly by dedicating a good chunk of his wrestling career specifically to competing with cruiserweights and trying to beat them at their own game. The mat-wrestling game is only up to that level because of the opponents whom he has faced and had to force himself to match. His reversal skills are only as good as they are due to the countless times that his own moves have been reversed. Green is a perennial student of wrestling, very much a professional when it comes to making himself better at his craft. At this point, it would be a laughable prospect to think that anyone would ever have to carry him in the ring. Athletics Sky was active in various athletic sports in high school and college. Though he was an avid runner in the middle school years, that was more of hobby undertaking. There were two major sports that he took part in during his more formidable academic years: volleyball and basketball. Through both of these sports, he tasted his first truly competitive spirit, but at the same time, there was too much of a team-oriented aspect to relate it directly to wrestling. What that part of his background did do for him was to allow him to work on his running speed and vertical leaping ability. After high school, Sky attempted to get into running and diving. He never made it onto the active teams, since he was neither fast enough, nor able to do what was necessary off of a diving board to get high scores. He did work on his cardiovascular conditioning, though, as well as improved his agility by leaps and bounds. That has stuck with him throughout his professional career. Martial Arts Green is not a martial arts master. At all. He still hits hard, though. That comes from his own professional drive to improve himself. His fighting training comes more from actively fighting against martial arts users and mixed martial arts fighters, thus forcing him to up his own game. He hasn't trained extensively in traditional martial arts or competitive mixed martial arts, but he has faced enough of those opponents to be able to create reasonably successful defense techniques and strategies against those kinds of opponents. Previous Wrestling Experience Skylar is best known for his time in PCW as 'The End', later wrestling under his own name. Beginning his career as a tall high-flyer, he was billed as a 6'8' cruiserweight when he was really a 6'5' heavyweight. His strength, speed, and agility worked well for him in this role. Eventually, though, he would switch to being billed as a heavyweight. Green quietly rose up the ranks over the course of a year, going from the Rising Star division to Continental Champion. Within 16 months, he was the World champion. He only had a handful of feuds, owing to his laid-back nature and ability to, for the most part, avoid making enemies. Though he had a superb gimmick in wrestling blind at all times (by special 'blacked-out' goggles, later in his career switching to white opaque contact lenses), he defined himself through his wrestling instead of through any gimmick. After a successful PCW career, Skylar Green took a temporary job as the head of Old School Fighting, but the company's lack of support for the brand quickly killed it. After that, a temporary stint in the resurrected PCW to work with Inphino Blitz (with whom he remains a friend). Following that, he appeared in two more federations. First, in Rush Pro Wrestling, wrestling primarily in tag teams even though he was a singles wrestler. He was making strides in the Mid-South division before the collapse of the organization. He seems to be tending toward a lighter weight, focusing more of his workouts on heavy cardiovascular aerobic exercise as opposed to weight training, though he does engage in that as well. He has spent his years in wrestling developing his body and style as such that he can match speed with anyone, while retaining strength and skill enough to have almost any attack available to him. Skylar is a very intelligent wrestler. He is like a chess master, seeing multiple different possibilities for attacks before doing them, and each branch then opens up more possibilities, all of which he can immediately perform. There is no delay to his chaining of attacks, and the movements that he does are decided upon on the fly so that his opponents have a very difficult time anticipating what he will do. Knowing that different opponents will have different body sizes and levels of agility and counter/reversal/escape ability, he will alter his moves to make it most difficult to escape. This will include cradling all suplexes, dead-lifting against agile opponents whenever possible to prevent 'flip-outs', and frequently changing one move into another before completion. In a match, there are things that Skylar will not do, period. These include: closed fists (even back fists), cheating, head drops, and harassing the referee. He's an honorable enough wrestler, and anything short of a clean win is unfulfilling to him. To that end, he will also refuse to end a match with a countout victory. One thing about Skylar is that though he is primarily a submission wrestler, he will attempt pinfalls constantly. These pins, however, come out of his holds; the purpose of these pinfalls is to make the opponent use their energy simply to kick out, he never pins out of desperation, but rather as an overall part of his strategy to wear down his opponent.
