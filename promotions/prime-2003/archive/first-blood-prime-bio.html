Pierce was the youngest of three siblings and his mother died in childbirth. This left his father to try and cope with having two sons and a daughter that he had no idea how to raise. This lead to strict, iron-fisted parenting. When his sister turned 16 she decided that she didn't want to live under her father's rules anymore and she moved in with her short-term boyfriend. No one has heard from her in years. Pierce's eldest brother was wrecked when his baby sister left. He turned to alcohol to ease the pain and died in an alcohol-related car accident. Pierce's father, being unable to cope with all the tragedy buried himself in his work and Daniel, not wanting to end up like his brother and sister decided to follow instead in his father's footsteps. He submerged himself in his studies. He was only 15 when his brother died and he took to high school as though it were all he had left - and maybe it was. He graduated at the top of his class and a full year early. He then went on to a large medical school in Montreal. Where, although he lacked passion, he excelled at everything, often to the point of separating himself from his peers - who were jealous of his achievement. After three years he joined a wrestling school there because he had a passion for the sport - unexplainable but clear. He balanced the two very successfully and graduated Magna Kum Laude from medical school. He then finished his wrestling training and graduated at the top of his class. Pierce's father died the day of his sons graduation ceremony from medical school. Although estranged from him Pierce still held his father close to his heart. When the old man died Pierce didn't know what to do. So he dove even deeper into wrestling. He was offered several contracts and was given a glowing reference from the school's trainers upon his graduation from the wrestling school. He chose to go with Pacific Coast Wrestling, a small, fledgling promotion that was just starting out in Vancouver, BC. There he quickly rose through the ranks of the organization via his unorthodox and often brutal fighting technique. This fighting style eventually earned him the nickname 'First Blood' after he consecutively drew blood from 43 opponents. He became and maintained the PCW Heavyweight Championship for almost 5 months but eventually tired of wrestling. Not wanting to leave the sport behind he bought out the company with the riches hand down to him in his father's will and ran it quite successfully for years with the help of some of it's top competitors. One man was particularly influential in the set-up of a merger that would be his legacy to leave the company. Johnny Postal helped Pierce arrange an inter-federation event between A CACC fed in the UK and his own, PCW in neutral territory - Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Upon completion of the architecture of the project Mr. Postal retired from wrestling, leaving his son, Brian Postal to carry on the family name. Pierce phoned Johnny up soon after and asked him if he wanted to main event the show in a tag team math with his son. Johnny thought it wasn't a good idea but Brian talked him into it. Pierce, trying to secure the UK investment drugged Johnny postal with a home-made concoction that would ensure a UK team victory. But something went wrong when Johnny Postal was killed by a young superstar-to-be, Aftermath during a moneouver gone wrong. After that the deal went south and Pierce was forced to sell off the federation and was out hundreds of thousands - or possibly millions of dollars. He had never experienced failure of this magnitude and blamed the four men involved in the main event Johnny Postal, Brian Postal, Aftermath and Prelude. Johnny Postal was dead, Prelude was taken out in the CACC parking lot three weeks later with an injury that would never properly heal but Aftermath and Brian disappeared. Nearly two years later the two appeared again, together in PRIME. Pierce devised a plan to see them both out of wrestling forever. So he befriended Brian Postal (known now as Driver) and sided against Aftermath (now known as Adam - the man Brian blamed for the death of his father). In the end Pierce got a beating but Driver and Adam both left the federation - for good. Pierce took over Driver's contract and now has his sights set high, Universally high and he won't stop until he gets what he wants - at any cost. Alternate Finisher: Higher Calibre (A jumping Diamond Cutter - A quick finisher without all the need for set-up) Trademark Moves: Bloody Sunday (An Uppercut Palm Strike - Most often done as a desperation move, almost always draws blood) The Exodus (A stalled Vertical Suplex spun out into a Reverse DDT) In-Ring Psychology: Pierce is not a Five Star Title contendor for a reason. He doesn't climb the turnbuckle or take high-risks (Save for extreme circumstances), he's out of his element in ladder matches and the like. He is a brawler first and a mat technician second. He has a good base of solid wrestling maneuvers (he is classically trained) but preferrs brute force and weaponry. Winning is the name of the game. Any means to that end are fine. He doesn't HAVE to cheat, but he will if that's necissary to win. Entrance Music Don't Drink The Water - Dave Matthews Band
