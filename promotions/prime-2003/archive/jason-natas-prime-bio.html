Jason Natas Biography Wrestler Name Jason Natas Nicknames The Anti-Superstar, Jase (informal) Hometown New York City Birthday 12th February, 1983 Height 6'4 Weight 254lbs Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Coming Soon! Other Feds None Other Fed Titles None Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style A stiff, proficient brawler with an impressive shoot-style striking repetoire. Check Matchwriting Aids for more details. Finisher Move New York Minute Finisher Description It’s essentially a more badass Evenflow DDT. Jason grabs the opponent in a front face lock before leaning the majority of his weight forward, grabbing the opponent's tights and snapping backwards viciously. Finisher Setup Move A series of jabs to the face followed by a haymaker. Setup Description A series of jabs to the face followed by a haymaker. Special Moves Rear Naked Choke, Fallaway Slam, Mule Kick, Rope Choke, Thesz Press, Curb Stomp and a wide variety of strikes. Other Moves STYLE - Jason's main aim inside the ring is to, frankly, bash the crap out of his opponent. Years of street-fighting have helped him on the road to becoming one of the most vicious strikers in the business. He primarily goes for fists, forearms and elbows but will quite happily kick you in the head if need be. - Technical wrestling isn't his forte. He's not fast, he can't mat wrestle and chain wrestling is completely out of the question. He relies of brute force and a cockroach-like resiliency to get by. - Despite his obvious lack of technical ability, his style is not as ramshackle as it once was. Training with Sonny Silver has amped his ruthlessness tenfold and left him more methodical and almost clinical in the way he goes about his business. In another life he could easily have been an MMA fighter or a boxer. - Jason absolutely despises the showmanship/entertainment side of the business. Thus, he will NEVER play to the crowd and will deliberately go out of his way to hurt anyone who does. He's a fighter, not a superstar. - Stiff. Like, Stan Hansen stiff. Put it this way; if Jason hits you, you fucking feel it. - There is nothing pretty about Jason Natas’ in-ring style whatsoever. He is your archetypical smashmouth wrestler; very aggressive and ‘in your face’. What he lacks in fluidity he makes up for in pure unrelenting hostility. - Put it this way; if a move can be described as "roughneck," Jason will probably use it. STRENGTHS: - Almost unsurpassed brawling skills. He hits hard, knows a vast array of strike moves and knows how to block and absorb blows. He knows when to go on the offensive and he knows when to go on the defensive. He'll quite happily put up his dukes and defend himself for the majority of the match if it'll tire his opponent out and allow him to sneak in a couple of deadly blows. - Jason Natas doesn't care about the element of celebrity that the sport brings; he will go into a clash with Jason Snow or Jay Phoenix without any awe and will treat them as he would an Enemigo. - Very high pain threshold and the ability to soak up damage like a sponge. He will go 'til his heart stops. - Relentless, wolf-like assault. He can strike fast and hard in quick flurries that are incredibly difficult to counter. WEAKNESSES - Jason is not technically gifted by a long shot. Because of this there is a long, long list of moves that he is unable to pull off, and he’s not very good at countering/wriggling out of submission holds either. If someone forces Jason into mat wrestling he’s fucked. - His temper. Natas often gets irritated when fighting opponents who refuse to adapt to his style (cruiserweights and high flyers particularly annoy him) which leads to him losing his cool. As a result he gets sloppy in the ring and starts making mistakes. - Completely non-existant aerial ability. - He's big, lumbering and slow. A very fast opponent can quite easily run rings around him.
