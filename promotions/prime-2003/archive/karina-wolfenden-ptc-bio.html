Karina Wolfenden Biography Nicknames The K-Wolf, the Eternal Sunset, PRIME's l'enfant terrible. Hometown Albany, New York. Birthday 22/12/81 : 25 Height 6'0 sans sneaker-lift. Weight 155-160 Fed Titles Universal Championship, (Unsanctioned) PRIME Hall of Fame Championship, Alias/Pulse Championship, Tag Team Champion (w/ Tessa Windsor), Mr Silver's PRIME Undisputed Amy Dumas Memorial Jizzbang Champion of the World. Fed Awards PRIME Hall of Fame, PRIME wrestler of the year (2005), equalled Rock Startling's record winning streak (10 matches), Dual Halo Winner (2005), Dual Halo 3rd place (2006 & 2007), PRIME Wrestler of the Week (numerous). Biography Deleted by Sun Tzu, who stated, "History is written by the victor." Other Feds PWC, RPW, Pro Wrestling AEON, short-term gigs with fWo, OSW & ACW. Other Fed Titles RPW Champion, RPW Eclipse Champion (x2) Other Fed Awards PTC Global Champion, finalist in the 2004 PTC Cruiserweight tourney, Winner of PTC's Seven of the Best 2005, Bracket finalist in GTT4, PTC Tag Team champion w/ Tessa Windsor. Wrestling Style Strike-centric Killyouintheface style. Finisher Move The C4 / C4 Mk 2 Finisher Description Flip-Over Kudo Driver / Sunset Bomb from the middle/top rope. Finisher Setup Move Um, yeah... Setup Description Pretty much anything. Special Moves Sonic... BOOOOOOOOOOOOOM~!~1#~~#1 (okay, none) Other Moves Strikes: Snap Kicks to thigh and side, Crescent Kicks, Spinning Backfists, Knees from a Muay Thai clinch, Double-Hit Roundhouse Kicks (kneeling opponent), Enziguri Kick, Enziguri Scissors Kick, Overhead Bicycle Kick, Spinning Enziguri Kick, Step-Up Enziguri, Springboard Spinning Enziguri Kick, Shining Wizard, Superwoman Punch, Moonsault Kick, Running Cartwheel Corner Kick, Handstand 619 (kneeling opponent), Running Pump Dropkick, Dropsault, Crane Kick, Koppou Kick, Running Double-Knee into the corner (often followed by inverted Lung-blower). Impact Moves: Asai DDT, The Whirlwind Romance (Tornado Seatbelt DDT), The Dual Halo (720 DDT). Grounded Opponent: Top Rope Double-Stomp, Curb Stomp, Asai Moonsault Senton, Standing Twisting 450 Splash, Standing Corkscrew Senton, Standing 450 Splash, Goodnight Moon (Asai Kneedrop), Teardrop Explodes (450 Splash). Opponent On Floor: Space Flying Tiger Drop, Top Rope Double Stomp, Unethical Experimental (Fosby Flop over the top rope to ringside), THE NEGASONIC LUPINE WARHEAD~! (Double-Fisted Torpedo Suicide Dive through the ropes). Submissions: Flip-Over Reverse Dragon Clutch (Applies front face-lock to kneeling opponent then flips over into bridge). Other: Top Rope Double-Stomp to opponent draped over the middle rope, Hanging Headscissors (Huricanrana over top rope, then holds onto top cable to choke opponent). Basics: Crowd-popping spot-monkey to top even the spottiest spot-monkies. Relies on strikes for majority of match. Knows how to take a fall, and relies on absorbing landings to keep her in matches. Annoyingly bendy and awkward for submissionists. Prefers short, high-tempo bouts, so will miss a few beats as things drag on. In peak physical condition, and a multi-disciplined athlete. Doesn't have a whole lot of different approaches to try if she's not getting anywhere. Doesn't so much defy gravity as flips it off, questions its Mother's honour, then spits on its grave. Entrance Music Overseer's 'Velocity Shift'. Ring Entrances The entire arena is plunged into darkness, leaving intense beams of white and purple to slash randomly across ringside. Pulses of synth then lay a trail for pounding drums, while licks of base trigger lights to flash in series around the arena. Joining in with the "WHOOO!" chants that escape from the beats, the fans rise in expectation of the entrance. 'SUPERCALIFRAGIALISTICWHENWEDROPWEGOBALLISTIC~!' A deafening eruption of white and purple pyro shoots out from either side of the curtain, unleashing Overseer's 'Velocity Shift' in full throughout the arena, speakers distorting with bass. A spotlight breaks from the swirling pattern around the arena to focus on the scaffolding above the entrance, where glimpses of a sleek figure slipping between the poles can be caught. Finally emerging from the steel jungle, the K-Wolf leaps over the PRIME logo and down onto the stage, long, patch-work coat rippling in the air behind her. As she lands, crouched, a final burst of white pyro shoots high into the arena from either side of the curtain, sending Wolfenden on her way down to the ring. "WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!" Gliding down the aisle, the K-Wolf reaches out either side of her to slap the out-reaching palms of the PRIME fans, before leaping up onto the apron . Vince Howard: Introducing first… from Albany, New York… weighing in at 159 pounds… she is THE K-WOLF… KARINA WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOLFENDEN!!! Gloved hands grab the top rope, slingshotting the K-Wolf into the cable, where she balances for just long enough to remove her purple-tinted sunglasses, clipping them onto the collar of her coat before hopping down into the ring.
