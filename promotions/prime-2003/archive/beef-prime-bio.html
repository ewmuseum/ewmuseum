Beef Biography Nicknames The Guy Who Always Talks About His Mean Beef Machine Hometown Beefville: City of the Annoyed, USA (may or may not exist) Birthday March 19th, 1979 Height 5'11 Weight 221 Fed Titles None. Fed Awards None. Biography Beef's tyranical reign over the other members of Mega Job started long ago when he partnered up with El Janito through some weird "rite of passage" involving a firework calling itself the "Goddess of Wrestling" and Jesus Christ, christening both himself and El Janito as Mega Job, a crime fighting unit. However, as time went on, Janito, Beef, and later Steve lost interest in crime fighting and instead became a camera crew. The members of Mega Job have a long-standing history with the Codemaster dating all the way back to 2004 when the Codemaster used them as his personal interview crew (citing that he dislikes both Kristen Jacobs AND Geoff Green). They were invited into the Blue Rogues to serve as a personal camera crew, though it's heavilly implied that Codemaster only invited them to serve as "cannon fodder". Other Feds The Asylum, fWo, ACW, IML, IWO, DWF Other Fed Awards the Asylum Tag Champion (w/ Janito), IML World Tag Team Champion (w/ Janito) Wrestling Style Brawler Finisher Move Beef Grinder Finisher Description Eye of the Hurricane Finisher Setup Move The Epic Beef Drop Setup Description Probably one of the most contrived moves in history, really. Special Moves Big boot and leg drop. Other Moves Beef is a comedy brawler, through and through. Though he acts and pretty much is an idiot, he can throw a decent punch if he really feels compelled to. In matches where Mega Job might actually feel motivated enough to fight back, Beef acts as the Robert Gibson of Mega Job, frequently taking the hot tag from Janito. Beef isn't apt to work on the limbs of opponents or take advantage of obvious weaknesses. This is not because he's honorable, but because he's really stupid about that. Despite that, he is excellent at selling like Janito is. Beef also tends to talk to his opponent during his matches, much like Janito does, and this usually earns him more than a few beatings. Being a chauvinist pig, Beef tends to especially hit on any female wrestler he ever encounters, often inviting them to take a ride on his "Mean Beef Machine", which of course, never goes well for him since... he's still a virgin. At times, Beef will "hulk up", go through Hogan's theatrics, and even use the big boot and leg drop. He tends to do this even on much larger opponents, so he often gets annihilated when he goes for the big boot against opponents much larger than him, like Logic. --- Regular Moves Since Beef doesn't offer much psychology, he just throws his moves out on a whim. They are the following: - Punch - Kick - Armdrag (he often actually shouts "ARMDRAG!" when he uses it) - Armbar - Dropkick - Vertical suplex - Back suplex - Backbreaker - Russian legsweep - Double underhook suplex - Missile dropkick - Scoop slam - Clothesline - Savate kick (like the superkick, but without that step forward) - Leg feed enzugiri - Chinlock / sleeper Signature Moves - The Epic Beef Drop This is also a leg drop, but done in the style of the People's Elbow. This move is exceedingly contrived and rarely hits. Beef sets it up with a scoop slam in the center of the ring, then bounces off the ropes once, hops over the opponent, and bounces off the ropes a second time. When he reaches his opponent again, he stops, does the Safety Dance, the Macarena, then the Charleston, before seguing into a Moonwalk. When he finishes the Moonwalk, he does four hops towards the opponent while screaming the letters of his name with each hop (in other words... "B!" "E!" "E!" "F"!) and then he drops the leg at the end of the fourth hop. Of course, by then, the opponent will have likely moved. Beef will STILL drop the leg even if he sees his opponent has long since moved, though. Because he's that stupid. - Hulk Up As mentioned before, Beef occasionally hulks up and goes into Hogan theatrics. The end result is that Beef goes for the big boot and the Atomic Leg Drop in succession. This usually won't get the three, but it's here. Finisher - Beef Grinder Beef grabs the opponent in an inverted facelock and then spins around and drops an elbow on the chest. In other words, this is Gregory Helms' "Eye of the Hurricane" or "Nightmare on Helms Street" finisher. Would theoretically used in combination with Janito's frog splash for a win, but Mega Job hardly wins matches.
