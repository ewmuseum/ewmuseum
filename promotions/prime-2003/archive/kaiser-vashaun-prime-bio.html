Troy Douglas(c) to win the Intense Championship via ref stoppage Wrestling Style Power/Technical Finisher Move Weight of the World Finisher Description (F-5 into a DDT type finish) Finisher Setup Move kick to the gut usually Setup Description a kick... to the gut Other Moves Primary Finisher: Weight of the World - Hoisting his opponent on his shoulders in fireman's carry position, Kaiser pushes off, spinning his foe out, however, he keeps his other arm locked around the opponent's neck/head and spikes their skull into the mat with a DDT-esque finish. Trademark moves/Other Finishers NRC3 (Now the Ref Counts Three) - Another possible finisher for Kaiser (though rarely used). KV locks in a gutwrench and hoists his opponent into the air, as if going for a gut-wrench-backbreaker, but instead of bringing the opponent's back across his knee, Kaiser releases his clench in mid-flight, letting his opponent fall uncontrolled. Dropping into standard backbreaker form (on one knee, other knee jutting out) Kaiser will allow the opponent to fall onto his leg, typically with the back of their head ricochetting off his knee. Style - Kaiser uses his strength and size to his advantage as often as possible. He is a high impact wrestler in every sense of the word, and looks to bring a "big fight" feel to all his matchs. Using a variety of slams and suplexes, Kaiser will try to literally break down his opponent. He's also talented enough to do some mat wrestling and submission wrestling. He'll rarely ever take to the air, and his strikes are almost always of the 'brawler' mentality more so than martial arts. Common Moves Snap Suplex Overhead Belly to Belly Suplex Overhead Choke-Toss Suplex Release German Suplex Head and Arm Suplex Saito Suplex Samoan Drop Tilt-A-Whirl backbreaker Full-Nelson Slam DDT Spinebuster (lifting his opponents by the legs, Kaiser will move one hand to their chest and push them down to the canvas with great force) Entrance Music Jackson, Mississippi by Kid Rock Ring Entrances The lights in the arena go out, casting the building in complete darkness. Then the PRIMEview comes to life, a black and white image of a raging fire acting as the only light. As the sounds of "Jackson, Mississippi" begin to play, the fire on the screen slowly starts to gain color, till burning an intense orangeish hue. When the heavy beat to the music kicks in, the PRIMEview is taken over by the black&white "KV" angel wing image, and Kaiser Vashaun emerges from the back, a white glowing spotlight highlighting his arrival. While making his way to the ring, the only light comes from the PRIMEview and the spotlight, which itself, blinks on and off, and fires from different locations in the arena. The result is an alternating image of compete darkness with that of Kaiser's journey occasionally being lit from above. The spotlight captures every second or third step he takes, giving Kaiser the appearance of moving without being seen. Once to the ring, Kaiser will step onto the ring apron and lean against the ropes. Standing under the spotlight, he will bow his head, looking towards the ground for a moment. As the lyrics blast out "I FEEL LIKE JACKSON, MISSISSIPPI!", Kaiser will quickly raise his head and fire his arms towards the rafters, roaring as the spotlight gives way to the arena lights which flicker rapidly, casting the ringside area in a white strobelight effect.
