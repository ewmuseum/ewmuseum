Johnny Raindance Biography Wrestler Name Johnny Raindance Nicknames J-Rain, Rainy-D, make up whatever you like Hometown Verona, Missouri via Mérida, Mexico Birthday September 10, 1980 Height 6 foot 3 Weight 227 lb. Fed Titles n/a Fed Awards n/a Biography Singles Record: 0-1-0 Tag Record: 0-0-0 PRIME Overall Record: 0-0-0 November 5, 2009 = Loss to Bryan Dawkins by pinfall (First Round of JitC) It's been seven years since Johnny Raindance exploded onto the wrestling scene starting in Puerto Rico. An unabashed babyface, the young man touted as the son of the Camal Mayan luchador dynastyin Mexico was much different than his predecessors. Acan, Chiccan and Kukulcan were known not only for their great connection with the fans and their highly ornate masks but also for their incredible stature. Not a one of the elder Camals stood below 6'10", and all weighed at least 280 pounds. With such a genetic pedigree, it struck watchers of lucha libre to see that Johnny, though not short by any means, was nonetheless the runt of the family. However, Raindance (real name Cizin) chose to eschew the family name at first, wishing to cut his teeth and gain recognition based solely on his own merits as an athlete rather than by association to his forefathers. To that end, using the ringname Johnny Raindance, the 4th generation Camal has endeavored to avoid working Mexico full time. Instead, he's spent much of his time traveling between Puerto Rico and Canada to ply his craft. Now the man seeks to enter the JitC after gaining a solid reputation as a hard worker, crowd-pleaser, company man and genuinely fun wrestler. Wrestling Style Speedy, defensive lucha libre Finisher Move 3 Seconds Mitnal (3SM) Finisher Description Swinging Shoulder Jawbreaker Finisher Setup Move Manhattan Drop // Rolling Sole Butt Kick Setup Description Inverted Atomic Drop // Spinning Back Kick to the bottom of the sternum Special Moves Altanero Camal (super finisher, very rarely done) Other Moves Style Notes: 1) Johnny knows how to fly, but won't unless he's sure the opponent is worn down enough for it to work. When he does, the flying is mostly basic; he knows better than to add unnecessary flair to attacks. 2) Johnny won't do anything that will gain him an instant DQ, but has no problem using the flexibility of a five-count or using the ringside area to his advantage. 3) When a match allows for anything, he'll go for the weapons without a second thought. 4) As a brawler Johnny doesn't mind punching, but he won't throw the first punch. Forearms and chops are in order until he takes a few fists to the face. 5) Johnny isn't the type of wrestler to single out a body part and beat it to a pulp. Rather, Raindance is more of a "fighting spirit" kind of wrestler. He keeps hitting you with whatever he can until you stop or he stops. 6) While Raindance's finisher, the 3SM, is a rather simple impact move that has served him well over the years, Johnny has no qualms about modifying it as necessary or following it up with an additional move to secure the victory. 7) Johnny is a luchador, which means that he will more often go for exotic submission maneuvers than simple ones. Still, he's better at doing submission holds than getting out of them. 8) Contact handler regarding Altanero Camal. Raindance hardly ever uses this move due to the highly dangerous nature of the technique. Aerials: Apron Running Senton Missile Dropkick Plancha Pescado Senton from the middle turnbuckle Springboard Back Elbow Fire in the Sky = Johnny hits a Guillotine Leg Drop from the top rope. Instead of lining up his victim for the attack, he'll turn his own body in the air as necessary. Impact Moves: Flying Bulldog Lariat Hangman's Neckbreaker Hip Toss Powerslam Mat Slam Sidewalk Slam Instant Karma = Snap Capture Suplex Rainmaker = Grabbing his victim's head, Johnny jumps up and places his knee against the side of the head by the ear. Raindance then falls backward so that when he hits the mat, his opponent's head will be jarred against his knee. Strikes: Baseball Slide Dropkick Discus Lariat European Uppercut Knee Drop (Flying) Forearm Smash Submissions: Armtrap Bow and Arrow Crooked Head Scissor Lock Inverted Facelock Surfboard Reverse Figure Four Leglock Underhook Indian Deathlock Danza Hurakan = With the victim bent over in front of Johnny, Raindance leans across the victim's lower back. Johnny scissors the near leg and picks up the far leg. Johnny then rolls onto his back and pulls down with both his legs and arms, forcing the victim's legs to do a split. Special: The Raindance = Kicking up his knees and shuffling his feet, Johnny performs a kind of dance that is little more than a hook for familiarity to the crowds. He has no preferred moments to use it, though he knows better than to do so when he has an opponent on the ropes or to take his eyes off his opponent for doing so. Pulled at the appropriate time, the Raindance may throw an opponent off of his plan, as the expectation would not be for an opponent to do something so seemingly senseless in a match. Vince Howard: From Verona, Missouri weighing 227 pounds... he is the only fourth generation professional wrestler in PRIME... Johnny Raindance! From the back comes a toned yet lanky competitor, an interesting sight in dusty black crew cut and a perfectly-groomed chin curtain of facial hair that is trimmed short and thin along his jawline. The most striking feature upon his face is a tribal-style half "black sun" tattoo over his left eye socket. The tattoo still stands out against his light, sun-bronzed skin. The long wrestling tights are split into quarters representing the four colors of the Mayan people's flag, with the front right leg in white and the front left leg in yellow, the back right in red and the back left in blue. White wrist tape and boots complete the outfit. With his music playing loudly throughout the arena, Johnny Raindance strides confidently to the ring.
