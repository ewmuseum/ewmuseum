Carlos Garcia Biography Wrestler Name Carlos Garcia Nicknames "The Pinnacle Performer", "DIRTY Sanchez", "Five-Star Superstar", "Your Feature Presentation", "Main Attraction". Hometown New York City, New York. Birthday 05/20/1982. Height 5 ft. 10 in. Weight 218 lbs Fed Titles None. Fed Awards None. Biography Coming Soon! Other Feds None. Other Fed Titles None. Other Fed Awards None. Manager Dixie Normas Wrestling Style Dirty showman/luchador. Finisher Move Get Rich or Die Trying. Finisher Description Somersault corkscrew senton. Finisher Setup Move Cash Flow. Superkick. Setup Description Superkick. Special Moves CHECK MWA! Other Moves Garcia is a showman; though he is indeed heel, he aspires to please the crowd. Night after night, he goes out there trying to out-perform whoever he is up against. He is a true student of the game, and is very skilled in the ring. Does a gorilla chest beat taunt, to the dismay of the crowd, multiple times throughout the duration of the match. Drives off of the crowds boos and jeers. Dirty, and may quite possibly cheat every match. Most often when he is at a huge disadvantage; which is where his name, "Dirty Sanchez" comes into play. Special Moves: *The Luxury Tax.- Stretch out frog splash. - The Rise to Power. - Spinning lifting sitout double underhook facebuster. The Paper Chase - Springboard spinning wheel kick to the back of the opponent's head. - For the Love of Money - Bridging dragon suplex. Common Moves: - The Matrix evasion. - Texas Cloverleaf. - Low blow. - Eye rack. - Thumb to the eye. - Various roll-ups while holding tights or with his feet on the ropes. - Kipup, usually into a frankensteiner. - Springboard seated senton. - Tilt-a-whirl headscissors takedown. - Hurricanrana. - Springboard moonsault. - Springboard frankensteiner. - Sunset Flip. - Pendulum backbreaker. - Russian Leg sweep. - Thesz Press. - Moonsault. - Dragon screw. - Tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. - Diving European Uppercut. - Small Package. - Rolling fireman's carry slam. - Dropsault. - Inverted Atomic Drop. - Springboard forearm strike. - Enzuigiri. - Tornado DDT. - Swinging neckbreaker.
