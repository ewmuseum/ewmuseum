ReVolution 181 ---- Philosopher of the People...or of the Well-Dressed To paraphrase Kermit the Frog, and contextualize him in a PRIME universe, it ain’t easy bein’ Mills. For all the hype dude has been getting, it’s been worth its weight in "unwarranted grief given to him." Every other week, he’s the butt of a Tyler Rayne entrance joke or the prop for a Jason Natas soliloquay. But this week, no mas. It’s with renewed zeal that Mills is strutting this week, showing unprecedented determination as he stalks this week’s interview victim. We even see a glimpse of Mills’ legendary unathleticism as he breaks into a jog, legs flying haphazardly to the side with each comically awkard step. "Hey! Hey! Chandler!" Cue the turning head of one PRIME Universal Champion, Chandler Tsonda. It’s street clothes tonight, which means the Model Citizen is in a black sweater and dark slacks, dressed down for the notoriously swanky Tsonda. He’s surrounded by a halo of interns, giving him a four-foot buffer to every side. Tsonda Intern # 78: Who’s that? Chandler Tsonda: He’s you in five years if you don’t shape up, 78. An audible gulp from the intern, and the group starts moving. Slowly, given the fact that they’re maintaining the ridiculous halo around the champion. Mills easily catches up, but seems perplexed by his inability to get to Tsonda. Matt Mills: Chandler, a word, please? Chandler Tsonda: Sorry, y’all, Chandler’s not here right now. But if you’d like to leave your name, number, and some other stuff I won’t listen to, do so after the beep. Tsonda Intern # 41: Beep! Matt Mills: They’re trained remarkably well. Chandler Tsonda: Well, duh. Untrained interns are feral beasts, Mills. Barely human. Matt Mills: So…can I get a word? Chandler Tsonda: Sure. Foppish. Feeling as though his debt to Mills is paid, the Universal Champion (and his halo of interns) turns to leave. Matt Mills: (shouts) Wait a damn second! Chandler Tsonda: ‘Scuse me, cowboy? Matt Mills: Chandler, I respect that you’ve got places to be, but it’s my job to get the scoop, and it’s part of your job to answer my questions. The Sultan of Style takes a long, hard glance at Mills, who may have just overstepped the boundaries of his relationship with the champ by about a football field. Tsonda Intern #31: Sir, should we initiate attack mode? Chandler Tsonda: Nah, it’s cool. Credit where credit’s due, the boy strapped on a pair this week. Fire away, Millsy, you just got yourself a hundred thirty second exclusive. Matt Mills: Why wouldn’t you just talk to me in the first place? Chandler Tsonda: Marginal benefit of me walking away versus talking to you? Very high. Marginal utility of being accosted by you, thus adding to my already-high stress level, versus just giving you what you want? You do to the math. Either way, Mills has his interview, and nothing’s going to stop him from getting the people their information. Stepping into his on-air personality, Mills turns to face the camera, while Tsonda steps out from the halo for the moment. Matt Mills: Chandler, the first thing I’ve gotta ask you is what on earth you’re doing using those interns as a human shield? "Isn’t it obvious?" Until the end of forever, that voice will be greeted by cheers. It’s just one of those things, that every time the indomitable Lindsay Troy hits the screen, the people go bonkers. Think of it as gravity: you don’t have to understand it, just accept that it’s happening. Matt Mills: Uhh….Ms. Troy? While the interviewer may feel comfortable accosting Tsonda, the fact that Lindsay Troy is a) a superduperstar and b) an ultimate babe (probably more the latter) has Mills somewhat tongue-tied. Plus, she’s not exactly in the day-to-day conversation of PRIME, given her current injury. So, even though they just had a run-in with each other a week ago, throw shock into the ring for possible reasons why Matt Mills is peanut-butter-on-the-roof-of-his-mouth status. Lindsay Troy: Channy’s all bent outta shape about being right. It was one thing for him to get on the soapbox and tell people that Danny was back, but it was entirely another thing to come face-to-face with his former best friend. Chandler Tsonda: Freudian analysis, too? What don’t you do? Lindsay Troy: Figured you might be in a talking mood after what happened on 179. Chandler Tsonda: You should’ve found me last week. Shit’s old hat, I’m over it. Lindsay Troy: Oh, God. See, y'know, that totally would have been a viable option for me, except you skipped the show last week. Chandler Tsonda: I didn’t skip the show, I was– Lindsay Troy: I’m sure there’s no proof that you weren’t here, but I know, Channers. I know that you damn sure weren’t gonna come to the show last week and give Danny another moment to bask in. Chandler Tsonda: I’m not scared of that ginger prick. Lindsay Troy: Probably true. His shortness is a thing of terror. But you are scared that he’s gonna steal your spotlight now that he’s back. Chandler Tsonda: And that’s unwarranted? Last I recall, he used his last "ohmygoodlookI’mbackagain" moment to jump me in the pecking order and get a shot at the Univeresal Title. Lindsay Troy: To no avail. I seem to remember someone else walking out of that match with the Universal Title. Chandler Tsonda: I’m here now, aren’t I? Lindsay Troy: True. I guess that’s the part I can’t quite figure out. I’m not saying that I agree with either of you, but why give him the opening he’s looking for? What do you have to gain by being here tonight? Chandler Tsonda: I could say the same for you. Lindsay Troy: Could, but you’d only do that to avoid answering my question. Chandler Tsonda: Touche, Queenie. The reason I’m here…is the same reason I’m always here, belt around my shoulder. I’m the best there is, and anyone who takes umbrage with that particular assessment of things can come find me. Lindsay Troy: Belt around the waist was always a much classier look. Chandler Tsonda: The royal house of Tsonda will have to respectfully disagree. It’s no small coincidence that, as two of PRIME’s most willing banterers go tete-a-tete, Tsonda readjusts the Universal Championship, propping it up onto his shoulder for all to see. Especially for Lindsay Troy to see. Lindsay Troy: I think your buddy might need some guidance. Chandler Tsonda: Ferguson’s way past getting help from a shrink. Lindsay Troy: I was talking about Dawkins. Chandler Tsonda: What about him? He’s doin’ great. Lindsay Troy: Really? You asked him recently? Chandler Tsonda: I…he’d tell me if there were problems. Lindsay Troy: If you’re as unsure as you sound, you might wanna get one of the Dior 'Droids to locate Dawkins on the GPS so you can ask him about that. The Queen crutches around the sea of Interns, heading off down the hall. Tsonda and Mills both watch her go, one staring at the back of her head, the other staring at that ass that sears corneas. Matt Mills: I hate to see her go, but Lord, to watch her leave...
