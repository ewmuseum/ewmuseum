Nitz Donnelly Biography Nicknames The Ego, The High Class Ass Hometown Now Residing in Venice Beach, CA (Hometown: Boston, MA) Birthday August 1, 1985 Height 6'1 Weight 224 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Other Feds None Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style High flyer with some technical ability Finisher Move Pride Before the Fall Finisher Description Nicholas hooks in a Devil's Lock DDT but instead of hooking his leg, he falls back swiftly, drilling his opponent into the canvas with great force. Smaller men would stand on their head for a moment. Others would fold like an accordian. This move can be executed in a heartbeat. Setup Description There doesn't need to be a set up, but this would work well. Nitz delivers a rib crushing twisting shin thrust. Other Moves Nitz has a total of 3 Finishers and one very rare move that spells the end. Pride Before the Fall - Described Above Flashing Exuberance (Top Rope Finisher) - Corkscrew 630. His opponent downed, he leaps from the top rope, going into a corkscrew and finishing with a Senton where he connects more with the upper part of his back then his neck. See... http://www.yucubetoo.com/moves/Corkscrew630.wmv AT Lock (Technical Submission Finisher) - Hard to explain. See http://www.yucubetoo.com/moves/at_lock.wmv Rare Finisher - The Sixth Sense (Top Rope or Springboard) - Donnelly goes into a trademark corkscrew with his opponent standing, facing away or towards him. He comes down and latches onto the head and drills him with a DDT. Sometimes, he can just pull him down roughly, executing a sloppy verson of the move with the same outcome. There has also been times when Donnelly catches them the wrong way and drops into a Reverse DDT with the move, but this can be dangerous as his opponents neck can be cricked the wrong way with his momentum coming down. Other Moves - Few men can fly around a wrestling ring as well as The Ego of New England. Endless training in Mexico and overseas in Japan has earned him his in-ring wings. Donnelly can execute basically any high flying high impact move you can think of. If you can think of it logically, he can pull it off. Fans can run out of film or burn out flash bulbs during his matches. He will always put his body on the line for a spot. He also throws discus style forearms and uppercut punches along with regular shots. Spinning kicks of anykind may also be used. Think London and Kendrick style. He also has some techinical ability. Besides his finisher, he can lock on wear down moves as well as get into some amateur technical wrestling. He also knows how to properly work on the neck with moves to soften his opponent up for Pride Before the Fall. Few power moves have been known to enter his arsenal. He is not behemoth strong, but he has a little power. Twisting powerslams, side slams and tilt a whirl backbreakers can be used on average sized opponents. For larger opponents, he will use his lighting quick speed to avoid any hard shots and try to chop the big men down at the knees/legs with any kicks or body impact moves he can think of.
