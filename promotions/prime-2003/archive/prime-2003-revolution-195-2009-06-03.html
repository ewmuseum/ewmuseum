PRIME...Number One by Definition. ---- Public Enemy Number One The world of professional wrestling has parameters in place that allow for graphic imitation of violence. Employees of the business are legally allowed to assault fellow roster members without police interference and incarceration. The circus atmosphere these competitors work under is hard for most to understand. An arena full of curious spectators watch the nightly battles, their reaction exuberantly happy if one of the good guys is victorious and seething should a villain have the last laugh. Yet, whenever the cameras shut off and the character evaporates from the performer, everybody acknowledges the journey made by both parties. The fans who got to see an amazing show are appreciative of the performers, and the wrestlers are grateful that fans continue to follow the product. At UltraViolence, every rule of the lifestyle was shattered in more ways than one for Tyler Rayne. He got on the wrong side of a deal and ended up paying the biggest price available, his career.
