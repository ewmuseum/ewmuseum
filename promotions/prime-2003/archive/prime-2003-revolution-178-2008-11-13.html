He gets to the arena before almost the entire roster, because oversight requires time. These are all part of Cantrell’s business credo. Yet even the shrewdest businessman is unprepared for what C.P. Cantrell sees as he approaches his office door. No know-how tells you what to do when your door has been crowbarred open.
