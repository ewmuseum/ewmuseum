Captain Suleimon Biography Nicknames The Sultan of Smackdown, the Pride of the Ottoman Empire, the Immolator of Istanbul, the Great Captain Hometown Istanbul, Turkey Birthday 11/1/1975 Height 5'10 Weight 198 lbs. Fed Titles none yet for PRIME Fed Awards none yet for PRIME Biography Captain Suleimon came from a modest upbringing in Turkey. Through his many adventures in his homeland, he became enamored with professional wrestling through an employer he had as a teenager, so much so that he went to train in Turkey's only professional wrestling school. After gaining notice from his instructor, one Malik Ismed Rahman, he came to America to wrestle in the indies. In his indie travels, he became so disgusted the Americans and Brits he was working with that his general patriotism for Turkey (specifically of its Ottoman past) turned into rampant xenophobia of anything not Turkish/Ottoman in nature. His avid reading of Ottoman Empire history took hold in his mind, so much to the point that he believes he's an Ottoman Captain. He first broke big in A1E, but didn't find much success there, eventually breaking his leg and compacting his tailbone. He faded in the background, becoming a road agent behind the scenes. (Although he did make a brief comeback as "Sully Man" in the short-lived UXW fed owned by current TEAM chairman Jess Chapel. Suleimon will deny anything having to do with this stint though). Finally, his injuries healed enough for him to want to get back in the ring. He turned his sights towards the Atlantic... the Atlantic Wrestling Club, that is. There, he continued to antagonize the Americans and English, the Christians and the non-religious, anyone he could rile up. His feud highlights include intense rivalries with Darcy Crisis, Red Rock, the Furious Fists of God and Jack Murphy. He later revealed an alliance with Murphy, an alliance they went to great lengths to conceal until Triangles 2006, the event that turned out to be the last one that AWC would ever host. With their dreams of dominating the Club dashed due to its closure, Suleimon and Murphy turn their sights to the only place on the PTC circuit that is bigger than their dearly departed home fed... ...PRIME. Special Moves see below Other Moves Wrestling Style: Suleimon is often referred to as the "Turkish Jeff Hardy," a label he hates because he's not a flaming homo :p. When applied to his workrate, it's an accurate portrayal. Suleimon often has no rhyme or reason as to what kind of offense he puts out other than "let's see what will killify the opponent the quickest." Anything high-risk will do. He only has one speciality submission that he uses with any regularity, and he's still trying to perfect it. Doesn't know the meaning of the phrase "work a body part." 3 Strengths 1 - Movement - Despite his history of injuries, Suleimon's got the deadly combination of speed and agility that lets him slink out of the most perilous of predicaments and avoid some pretty nasty beatings. 2 - Cheater - Suleimon isn't afraid to break the rules. Usually, this is a minus, but he's very good at not getting caught. 3 - Silver Tongue - Suleimon was a consensus top-2 promo in AWC and one of the best in the business. He knows how to get under your skin, and for some of the weaker-minded foes, it's a deal-breaker. However, against someone with a thick skin, and it's more an impetus to lay out an ass-whipping on the Sultan of Smackdown. 3 Weaknesses 1 - Prior Injuries/Size - Sully's not a large man, and he's been hurt before. Put him in against someone strong enough, and you can break him in half. 2 - Reckless style - His moveset is high risk/high reward. When there's no high reward, he just ends up hurting himself. 3 - Shitty wrestler - Asking Suleimon to counterwrestle is like asking Nova to give up pot. Movelist Favorite Moves - Tiger Suplex, Front Dropkick, Space Flying Tiger Drop, Missile Dropkick, Moonsault, Lionsault, Arm Dragon Screw, Enzugiri (offensive and defensive), Ranas of all shapes and sizes, Hennig Special (opponent is seated, Suleimon runs up from behind and does a flip-over neckbreaker), Guillotine Leg Drop, Flying Cross Chop, Running DDT, Chairshot Signature Moves/Alternate Finishers Sultanbreaker - Inverted Full Nelson Jawbreaker Turkish Tiger Tamer - Suleimon's only real submission move... the opponent is seated on the canvas, and Suleimon goes behind them, grabbing them in a double chickenwing (ala Tiger Suplex). He then nestles his head into the neck of the victim and flips over so that he's bridged over the seated opponent. It takes an amazing amount of balance, but it inflicts a ton of pain in the short time it's applied. Dangerous Tiger Suplex - A page out of Mike Randalls' book... this is the dangerous variant of the Tiger Suplex where Suleimon hits the victim's neck at a much sharper angle than his regular suplex Lay Out Neckbreaker - Known to CSWA fans as Troy Windham's SlackKnife, it's basically an inverted Diamond Cutter/Stone Cold Stunner done as a neckbreaker rather than a 3/4 front neckbreaker Arabian Facebuster - Guillotine leg drop... only Suleimon puts a chair under his legs as he's flying from the top. It hurts himself a good deal too. (NOTE: This move is death if you choose to use it in a match. Death. Do not be a dickface like Jeremy J. and have someone kick out of the regular version of htis move let alone the corkscrew somersault variant.) The Leap Across Continents - This is Suleimon's UBERFINISHER... Suleimon starts off on the top rope, back facing the ring. Opponent is prone on the mat. Suleimon leaps backwards, falling in forward rotation. After a 270 degree rotation, Suleimon lands with a Swanton variation on the victim. This move is only to be used in the biggest of big matches for Suleimon. Finisher The Whirling Dervish - Tornado Reverse DDT out of the corner. Usually set up by a hard Irish whip into the corner.
