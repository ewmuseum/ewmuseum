The Blue Rogues Biography Fed Titles PRIME World Tag Team Championship (1x) Fed Awards None. Biography See the individual bios of Coral Avalon and the Codemaster. K THX BYE. Other Feds fWo World Tag Team Champions Other Fed Awards fWo World Tag Team Champions Wrestling Style All-Arounder (Coral with the technical, CM with the brawling, they mix, it's fabulous.) Finisher Name The Lockheart Finisher Description Double (sandwich) enzugiri Setup Move Pretty much anything. Special Moves Some. Maybe. Move Set There exists six possible tag team combinations with the Blue Rogues' faction (Coral/Codemaster, Robert/Scott, Coral/Scott, Coral/Robert, Codemaster/Scott, Codemaster/Robert), but this guide will mainly describe the Coral/Codemaster team, as they're the only team that will be used in PRIME. Basically, you have two guys who mesh well in the ring as a team. Coral is the technical wrestler, using his wrestling ability to take down his foes. The Codemaster, meanwhile, is more of a sports entertainment-esque brawler who often uses cheap and dirty tactics to take advantage. They each cover for one another's weaknesses, as Coral is a much more well-rounded wrestler than Codemaster is, and the Codemaster has the willingness to use trickery and deceit to overcome his foes that Coral doesn't have. The two men are the same relative size, though Coral is slightly bigger. As they are both technically within the cruiserweight level, they are both fairly easilly thrown around by bigger opponents. While Coral can deal with larger opponents using his technical skill, Codemaster has to use his trickery in order to get by. Finally, despite Codemaster's skill in hardcore-type matches, the team functions much better in matches that maintain the basic rules of a tag team match. Anyway, on to the tactics. - Coral is usually the one who starts out. If he gains the advantage, the Codemaster often gets tagged in. If he doesn't get the advantage, the Codemaster tags in to try his luck. In matches that are of unimportance to the Codemaster, however, he will tag right back out to Coral the second he gets hit. - Usually, the Rogues will gain the advantage when either Coral hits a big move, when the Codemaster cheats his way to an advantage, or due to the intervention of one of the Entourage Rogues (ie: Robert or Scott). At this point, the Rogues cut the ring in half and pound the hapless victim into the dirt. - Coral doesn't do anything illegal, like take advantage of the Codemaster distracting the referee. However, nothing stops the Codemaster from such tactics, often taunting an opponent's partner into the ring, letting the referee take care of it, and then doing something not nice to the opponent. The Codemaster is also the type of guy who comes into the ring while the opponent is trying to make the tag in order to distract the opponent. - When the hot tag is inevitably made, it is often Coral who's the legal man and he's often the one who takes the full brunt of the punishment. There are occasions where the Codemaster gets the punishment he deserves, but mostly, it is Coral who takes it since he happens to be a sponge for punishment. Codemaster will regularly make the save on numerous pinfalls against Coral, in the meantime. - When the Rogues are poised to win, they will do so in one of four ways: 1) with the Codemaster's trickery followed by his Magnum Fang or something Coral does. 2) Coral's skill followed by either his Koji Clutch, Ratings Spike, or whatever else he feels like ending the match with (Coral has a knack for ending matches with anything credible, aside from his finishers, like hsi frog splash). 3) A finish using one of the two tag finishers. 4) Brutal interference from the outside Rogues, typically done using Robert Falk. - Keep in mind that Coral always adheres to his "code of honor", as explained in his match writer aide, and that means that under normal circumstances (ie: all the time)... he doesn't cheat, use weapons, drop people on their heads, or hit women. He's a goody-good face, even with this heel team, so treat him as such. The fans would honestly cheer the guy if he wasn't with the Rogues. Common Team Moves: Double leg lock, double snap suplex, double clothesline, double stungun, double Shoryuken (leaping uppercut), double STO, wishbone, Coral flapjacks the Codemaster onto the opponent, Coral wheelbarrow powerbombs the Codemaster onto the opponent. Strong Team Moves: - Ride The Lightning! The opponent is down. The Codemaster gets down on all hands and knees somewhere in front of his opponent. Coral gets a running start, springs off of CM's back, and lands a high flying move (it's variable, but often it's a senton) on the opponent. Think of this as being a more spectacular Poetry in Motion, as CM and Coral are basically doing it to a downed opponent instead of an opponent in a corner. - Bear Hug (Coral) + Reverse DDT (Codemaster) combination - Wheelbarrow Powerbomb (Coral) + DDT (Codemaster) combination - Wheelbarrow Powerbomb (Coral) + Superkick (Codemaster) combination - Doomsday Device + Missile Dropkick. Coral and CM are interchangable for this - Double superkick - Poetry In Motion, ending with a dropkick instead of a leg lariat. Both guys are interchangable for this. Finishers: - Lockheart This is a double enuzigiri. It is usually hit sandwich style to the sides of the opponent's head, though there are occasions where both men hit from the front and back of the opponent's head as well. This is the most frequently used of their two tag finishers, since it doesn't require the smaller Codemaster to lift anyone. - Mithril Spike This is an assisted Ratings Spike. Codemaster picks up the opponent in a kind of a bearhug, similar to that of how AMW used to set up the Death Sentence. Coral grabs the opponent's head from behind in an inverted facelock, and then Coral snaps them over with the Ratings Spike while Codemaster rolls with it. This is the less frequently used of their two tag finishers, but also the stronger. Shouldn't be kicked out of unless the other guy saves. Entrance Music 'Gone Guru' by Lifeseeker Ring Entrances I'm going to describe it, but this isn't a C/P job. So FU. No, not Nova's stable. Shut up. First, you're going to want to hit the "Gone Guru". The lights will dim, and a kind of a blue light will come from the entrance way while fog rises in that area. Then, several figures emerge from the fog. It'll usually start with El Janito and Steve on camera duty. They're playing the role of the paparazzi in this group, filiming the team as they come out of the fog. After all, it was MNM that gave me the idea of the Blue Rogues in the first place. After that, the two Falk Brothers emerge from the fog, walking on the sides of the ring and trying to keep fans from catching a glimpse of the team. Following that, it's Coral and the Codemaster themselves. Behind those two, Beef is walking along with Alexandria Malone, who may or may not be disgusted with Beef being... well, Beef... at the time. And there you have it. That's the entrance in a nutshell. I'm sure you feel enlightened now.
