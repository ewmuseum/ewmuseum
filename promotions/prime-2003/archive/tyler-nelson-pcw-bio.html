Tyler Nelson Biography Nicknames 'The Greediest Player in the Game'; 'The CEO' Hometown Dallas, TX Birthday 12/27/74 Height 6'1' Weight 225 Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography Tyler wasn’t born with a silver spoon in his mouth, and that festered in him throughout his childhood. He was greedy and selfish from the womb, and those traits only manifested themselves further as he grew up. During his high-school years, when other kids were working part-time jobs, Tyler was scheming ways to use others to make money for him. He became a master of manipulation and eventually started his own business before he even graduated from high school, Greed, INC. Tyler’s company was soon one of the largest real estate developers in Texas, and Tyler was making money hand over fist by using extortion, blackmail, and whatever other means necessary to get what he wanted. By the time he was 20, Tyler had disowned his middle-class parents and moved into a lavish estate on the outskirts of Dallas. He became involved in the behind-the-scenes political game, befriending the most powerful family in Texas….the Bush’s. Tyler was a driving force behind George W. Bush’s campaign for President and uses that considerable clout to further his own agendas. Tyler was a good athlete and by the nature of his own ego liked to compete….and always win. One day Tyler was flipping through the channels and came across a wrestling show for the Primetime Championship Wrestling promotion. He saw how many fans were at the show and immediately started to think about the money that must be involved in something as simplistic as beating the hell out of someone else. He immediately started training with some of the best in the business with a plan of climbing to the top of PCW, becoming its World Champion, and reaping the considerable monetary benefits that came with it. After entering PCW, Tyler had somewhat of an epiphany and decided that instead of waiting to become the World Champion, which wouldn’t really take all that long in his estimation, he should just buy PCW so he could have the cake and eat it, too. So with the help of Dan Hollywood, Chet King, and another who shall remain nameless, Tyler and his Media group purchased PCW from The Phantom Booker and took PCW to new heights….all the while fattening his already overflowing pockets. Life was good for Tyler for a long time. He even won the PCW World Title twice to boot. Then came the IRS, and everything started to unravel. Tyler went through several stages of near mental instability as he struggled to keep what he had so ruthlessly built…PCW, Greed, INC, and more importantly his money. Even the Bush family steered clear of Tyler during the investigations and consequent prison term. It was a country club prison, but prison nonetheless. For 2 years Tyler was incarcerated, but all the while he maintained his connections to the outside and when he finally was released he was already on his feet and back up to his old tricks. In addition to the real estate ventures he managed while in prison, Tyler purchased a railroad to serve as the backbone of his corporation. The railroad system in the US handles a large volume of goods….but they can’t even keep up with the demand. And of course, anyone who knows anything about business knows that a large demand leads to money, money, money….and Tyler is all about money. Unfortunately for Tyler, the economic climate in the world changed drastically. Real estate prices went in the tank, the stock market crashed, and George W. Bush was Constitutionally prohibited from running for a 3rd term in the White House. It all came crashing down for the Greediest Player in the Game, leaving him completely broke. Not getting any assistance from his old cronies, Tyler had to actually find a job. Not yet ready to don the Wal-Mart blue, Nelson went to one place he thought he might be able to weasel into....PRIME. He asked....no, begged...Lisa Tyler for an executive position in the company. She indeed offered him a job. As a backstage interviewer. Being desperate like he was, and thinking that he might be able to parlay that job into something more lucrative, Nelson agreed. As you can imagine, the backstage interviewer gig didn't sit well with Tyler. He craved more power, which brings more money, and made it his point to steamroll Lisa Tyler to get it. She was holding him back, keeping him under her thumb out of spite (in his mind) and he wasn't going to have it. Nelson started a vendetta against Lisa Tyler, even going as far as to change her travel plans and send her to Nicaragua in order to perform a hostile takeover of Revolution 199. This, of course, didn't sit too well with BLT, and she booked Nelson in a match against Killean Sirrajin (who Nelson attacked with a steel chair right after he announced his retirement in an attempt to get at Lisa Tyler) at Colossus VI.
