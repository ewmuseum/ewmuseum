Dusk Biography Nicknames The Lost Soul Hometown Los Angeles, CA Birthday February 18th, 1977 Height 6'4 Weight 250lb. Fed Titles PRIME Intense Title x 2 (Current) Fed Awards Dual Halo 2008 (#5) Other Fed Titles PTC Extreme Champion, PTC Infinite Gaunltet Champion Wrestling Style All Rounder Finisher Move Heartless Finisher Description Dragon Sleeper (Check MWA for more details) Finisher Setup Move Reverse DDT Setup Description Reverse DDT (Check MWA for more details Special Moves Please refer to the MWA Other Moves - After his match at GAN '08 against Cozen, Dusk suffered a severe injury to his left knee, an injury that needs surgery. Unfortunately, that surgery would also require him to hang up his boots for good. Unwilling to do that at this point, Dusk has decided to change his style and hopefully manage to stay in the ring for a few more years. Because of this, Dusk has changed his style from the high-flyer/brawler that he used to be to more a technical game, using his usual size advantage and knowledge for the ring instead of his daredevil routine. - Dusk is rather proficent in submission moves and suplexes. He knows wrestling inside and out, having been in the ring for the past sixteen years. When he's in a wrestling match, he's considered to be one of the best because of his proficency and ability. He will wear a wrestler down until they basically don't have a will to live. - Wrestlers will focus on his left knee, aware of his brutal match against Cozen, and the damage that was done to it. They might not know that his injury is so severe, but they'll focus heavily on it. Dusk will do just about anything to keep an opponent away from his knee. Even with his injury, Dusk is still very agile for a man his size. He's trained as a mat wrestler and withstanding pain for him is something he's used to due to his experience. - Dusk will revert to his "daredevil" status in very big matches. It's a rarity for him to do anyting that will put his knee in complete danger, but he spent a bulk of his career in Japan and Europe where he perfected his style. In a big match situation (ala Cozen-esque match) then he won't be afraid to pull those moves up to take his game to the next level. Otherwise, he tends to stay a bit more ground in his matches. General Move Set: DDT, Elbow Drop, Forearm Smash, Forearm Uppercut, Gutbuster, Hip Toss, Leg Sweet, Piledriver, Powerbomb, Shoulder Breaker Cruiserweight: (Will only perform these moves in a HUGE match setting due to knee injury.) Suicide Dive, Shooting Star Press, Moonsault, Crossbody, Springboard Bulldog, Diving Elbow Drop, Shooting Star Elbow Drop, Diving Headbutt, Hurricarana (Diving or Springboard), Frog Splash, Splash (360 degree, 450 degree, Corkscrew 450, Imploding 450, Corkscrew), Shooting Star Piledriver, Senton Bomb, Plancha, Heavyweight: Belly to Back Suplex, Belly to Back Brainbsuter Suplex, German Suplex, Full Nelson Suplex, Snap Suplex, Underhook Suplex, Vertical Suplex, Fisherman Suplex, Gutwrench Suplex, Tiger Suplex, Rolling Release Suplex Entrance Music 'I Dare You' by Shinedown
