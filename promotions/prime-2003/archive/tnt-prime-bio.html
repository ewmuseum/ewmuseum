TNT Biography Nicknames T, Anvil, the big man Hometown Veracruz, Mexico Birthday unknown Height 6'2 Weight 282 Biography TNT has a very murky background. His real name is still unknown even to his manager Jim Surge. Rumors have been trailing TNT that he worked the Mexican border for many years as a pollero, or coyote, smuggling Mexican nationals into the United States and that is the reason he feels compelled to remain as a masked wrestler with no identity. There are also some theories among the Detonators' rabid fan-club Los Bambitos that TNT isn't even Mexican, and that's why he sometimes makes a big show of displaying the Mexican flag. Jim Surge 'discovered' TNT in a literal barroom brawl where TNT beat the living hell out of two patrons giving him stick for wearing his mask out in public. TNT didn't reveal his real name but instead told Surge his initials were "T.N.T., like dynamite." TNT told Surge he had recently been competing in some independent Mexican wrestling federations and would love the chance to wrestle in the United States. Surge took TNT on and flew him to England where the Arizona rancher assisted his former teachers by helping expose TNT to the classical Cumberland and Westmoreland styles and a thorough review of the classic King's Road style. Opponents always seem surprised to see a masked Mexican break out the King's Road style, even when they know it's coming! Armed with his new training, TNT & Surge returned to Mexico to find the perfect partner for TNT and they did so in a luchadore league - another masked wrestler with the appropriate moniker of "Dynamite." Despite being the powerhouse, or perhaps because of it, TNT is the more laid-back member of the Detonators (if such a thing is possible with a team named after a device which causes explosions). It is more likely the product of his home region of Veracruz, a much less hectic and less congested place than Dynamite's hometown of Mexico City. TNT has always felt very fortunate to be teamed up with manager Jim Surge and is usually the peacekeeper during disagreements in the Dets' camp. Is the member of the team that never seems to lose his head despite the enormity or unexpectedness of a situation. TNT is not preachy, though, so don't expect any of his cool nature to rub off on Dynamite.
