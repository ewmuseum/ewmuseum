ReVolution 145 ---- Every Once In A Blue Moon If you asked Alex Creed a month ago if he'd be back in a wrestling ring, he'd flash a boyish grin and say, "Not any time soon." He was happy and enjoying success in his new ventures, making his own way in a different environment where the Creed name doesn't hold as much weight. But the wrestling itch does come back from time to time. And while Alex isn't that far removed from the squared-circle, the wrestling business now feels like a distant cousin compared to MMA world. The comfort of a different second skin had made him feel more at ease, but being backstage at the Air Canada centre means the third skin must be donned. He hopes it will still fit. The Mecca makes his way to catering, looking to snag a bottle of water before heading to the gorilla position. Mostly ignored by busy techies, he finds his way with little problem, fully intent on just grabbing what he needs and being on his way. Until a figure he hasn't seen in over a year catches his eye.
