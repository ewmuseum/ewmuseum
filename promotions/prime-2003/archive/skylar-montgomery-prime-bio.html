The Number 12 Other Moves Skylar doesn't really know how to wrestle but, like he's made of infinite monkeys on typewriters, is known to generate stunning offence on-the-fly. He is not so much athletic as he is charasmatic, but he disregards that fact as well as his own personal wellbeing whenever in the ring. Skylar is clumsy but energetic so will often botch attempted moves - styling it out like he meant to do that. "The Number 12" is Skylar's strongstyle move whereby he waits until the victim is on all fours, pushes off their back with one foot and comes down with a double-foot-stomp to the back of their skull "The Shooting Stardust" is the second move in this video - http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ufu7f9V5mPg "The <3 Punch" (less than three punch) is a slow, methodical punch to the heart of a running opponent. It is said that a human heart is the size of its owner's fist, and following this logic Skylar is trying to fit his knuckles into the perfect victim's heart like two halves of a locket - killing them.
