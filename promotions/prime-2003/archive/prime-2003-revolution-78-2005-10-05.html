ReVolution 78 ---- The Williamson Factor to the Tag Team Championship The air is already cool. As per the security-contracts that PRIME initiates with the arenas it uses for venues, they must blast the A/C around the entire ring area to 60 degrees and keep it that way in anticipation of the show. That is a bit on the chilly side, one must understand that it gets warm very quickly when the show starts. Between the hot lights, pyro, proximity of bodies, and warm food ordered from concession stands, if things weren't prepared ahead of time then PRIME would be a massive public sweat-fest (and not in a good way). Hours before the start of the show, we find Vangellus Oxios and Scott Matthews standing inside of the wrestling ring that has already been set up. The two are already wearing their wrestling gear. Not much to be said for Matthews, though; all he does is take off his shirt and he's "dressed" for the ring. Along with them, in his own wrestling tights, is none other than Justin Williamson. The same Justin Williamson who we formerly saw on television as part of the Manifest Destiny tag team with Angelo Deville. The same Williamson who turned away from Deville (a swerve, given that it would be expected based on character that Deville would be the one to first cut someone loose), resulting in the match in which Angelo got his revenge via Soprano victory. Williamson: Okay, do you know why I had you help set up the ring tonight? Scott stands off to the side, leaning against the ropes. Vangellus is seated on one of the top turnbuckle pads.
