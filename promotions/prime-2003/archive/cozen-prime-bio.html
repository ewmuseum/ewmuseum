Cozen Biography Wrestler Name Cozen Nicknames The Faceless Fighter; The Crazy (from Troy) Hometown Unknown. Birthday Unknown, appears to be 25-34 Height Unclear, no less than 5'11 Weight Not more than 210 lbs. Fed Titles Universal Champion (ReVolution 156 - UltraViolence 2008) Fed Awards Second place in the Dual Halo. Biography Cozen is a cipher, an identity without an identity. A life without a life. Other Feds None. Other Fed Titles None. Other Fed Awards None. Wrestling Style Martial Arts. And not the pansy-ass WWE-ized Martial Arts. We're talking the hoo, the ha, the hiya, and the kicking -- Cozen may be the best striker with boobs in the history of wrestling. Finisher Move The Piano Wire Finisher Description The half-nelson choke or katahajime. Finisher Setup Move The Terrible Lie (A flying butterfly kick) Setup Description A flying butterfly kick, the impact usually spinning the opponent around. Special Moves See 'Move Set' in Matchwriting Aids - nothing else has a badass name. She's also particularly adept at mimicking the not-completely-insane moves of some of the other competitiors. Other Moves Five Tips on How To Do The Cozen Thing: 1.) She punches and kicks (lots of kicks) more than DDTs and suplexes. An overabundance of the latter will make me frown. 2.) She's... she's like a Tekken or Virtua Fighter character, except one of those cool ones who can copy their opponents' moves (within size reason). 3.) Play smart, play S-Mart. She's not a super-genius or anything, but she'll be ready for her opponents, all evidence to the contrary in the promos and RPs. 4.) Cheating means "atemi" strikes to vulnerable parts and weapons. 5.) Seriously, that video game thing? Do that. Strikes: - Short, thrusting punches (not coming from expertise in straight kung fu, if Nick picks up on that -- it's a shorter, sharper style) - Savate kick (technically a chasse kick, and definitely not a superkick, as it's missing the sidestep) - Muay Thai style (kao tone) knee thrust - Knee-to-thigh Muay Thai strike in a clinch (kao noi - uncommon, but a good way to break a clinch) - Other kicks tend to come in circular, dancelike taekkyeon patterns, mostly side-to-side, usually leading with the left foot -- in stark contrast to the hard snap of the punches. - Stiff-fingered thrust to pressure points like the solar-plexus, under the chin, even the backs of the knees (commonly called "atemi") Grapples: - Rotational wristlock, often dragged over into a something American wrestling fans would call a cross-arm breaker, and if there happen to be a few kicks to the face as the opponent, then that's COMPLETELY unintentional, right? - Hyperflexing wrstlock (think the short-arm scissors with both hands - or look up "wristlock" on wikipedia) - Variety of judo-style throws, usually over the shoulder. - In a grapple, look for those fists and feet to be used more than anything else, because this isn't someone who's going to be able to whip out the power moves. Gain separation with something brutal but ugly, hit something prettier straight up. Entrance Music 'Coming Undone' by Korn (or, alternately, the music of whomever she's impersonating) Ring Entrances The PRIME*View goes dark, spiraling into images in half-remembered gray smoke. A sharply pointed jaw and a bright smile meet the light of house lights. At her left, a smallish blond man with a smile that makes you want to hit him. At her right, an Asian woman with auburn hair and a fine dress. For a moment, when she stops at the top of the rampway, hip cocked to the side, a grin that's like a knife slashes across her lips and she's Lindsay Troy, basking in the cheers. Except the cheers sound like this: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Vince Howard: She is the Faceless Fighter and the REIGNING! PRIME! UNIVERSAL! CHAMPION! Andreas and Siena follow in her wake; the former attempts (futilely) to lead the crowd in cheering. The latter is consumed with chatting up some men at ringside. Cozen hops gracefully to the ring apron, then launches herself bodily over the ringropes, coming up to a knee. Her teeth show in her grin as she tips her head to the side, like some kind of animal.
