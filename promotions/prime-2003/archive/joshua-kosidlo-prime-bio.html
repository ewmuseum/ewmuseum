Joshua Kosidlo Biography Wrestler Name Joshua Kosidlo Nicknames The Paladin, The Nightmare, The Ironman, The Man of 1000 Finishing Moves Hometown Rome, New York Birthday April 5th, 1972 Height 6'5 Weight 275 lbs Biography Born in the small town of Rome New York April 5th 1972. The Josh grew up a small town guy with big dreams. He was an avid wrestling fan since he can remember. Cheering on his heroes. Josh attended Dewitt Clinton Elementary School where he learned the basics of the world. When in 6th grade Josh remembers a love for acting brought upon by his teacher Antonio Lanzi. Josh then went off to Staley jr High where he became more developed into the wrestling world with Backyard wrestling. He remembers being most influenced about wrestling and the Hardcore greats by friend and Class mate Scott. He also learned to appreciate some of the best technical wrestlers from friend he worked with later on in a small time fed. Josh was top student there and star of the major sports teams. After his Junior high career was over he entered Rome Free Academy. There Josh and friend Scott began making plans in the future for Wrestling. Josh an active member of the RFA Key Club and School Choir was relatively a good kid. Josh here was also star of all the major sports teams. After graduating RFA Josh went to Harvard Law and graduated second of his class. Above him High School sweetheart Angelina Lambert, Who later moved away and they broke contact. He then went to work for a law firm. After a brief time there he realized the corruption within and left with a clear conscience. Josh then met up friends Rich, Eric and Scott and they discussed a wrestling college in Rochester. The three enrolled and upon graduating went their separate ways. The Josh started out in the Insane Wrestling Association as Vortex. He entered and was trained by current wrestler The Sandman. There he learned many technical moves and improved his abilities. This was the foundation of the Wrestler The Josh became. After many other feds, and many other title reigns The Josh hit a bump in the road. While in the Independent Wrestling Empire on Valentines Day of 2000 he killed a reporter. The Josh was found guilty but released under probation. Being the guy died in the hospital not at Joshua's hands he had a very short sentence. The Josh returned to action in the sW as a man of God. There he preached the words of God. He began a small cult following with Action Jackson and Memphis Rhaines. sW however became no more, and closed its doors. The Josh began wondering the wrestling world for a more suitable location. Finally after holding numerous championships in numerous federations, Joshua found his place in the Primetime Wrestling Academy. After only one match, that he lost to fellow former NTICW member Ken Kaze, he graduated. After graduation Josh got a call from NTICW fed head Blane Bishop to be appearing in the NTICW. The Josh took his offer, and immediately became a household name; he feuded there with wrestler Hessian. They were then named the best feud of NTICW, shortly after however the NTICW closed down. However while there Josh was able to pick up the United States Championship off the now current Global Title Holder Hessian, in what was called one of the best matches in the history of NTICW. Finally after a few meetings with PCW fed heads he had secured a spot in the PCW. However Josh's stay there was very short lived. Due to some backstage heat he was released from PCW and left the entire PTC for a good deal of time… However scouts from Global Wrestling had been looking into Josh, and for a while he didn't really want to go, but eventually gave in. While in GW Joshua hooked up with a girl named Trista, who for a long time there after became one of his good friends, and closest relations. During this time Joshua was working as hard as possible, and was actually working for two promotions on open contracts. In the second promotion however, he fell from a 60 ft drop flat onto his back. So needless to say due to injuries he had to step out of the ring for awhile. This occurred late July of 2002. Early November of last year Joshua was completely ready to return to the ring. After months of training he finally returned less than a year later to WWA. After only a few months there Joshua feel back into a recurring problem... Joshua had become addicted to drugs in his time off of wrestling. His main drug of choice was Coccaine. By this time Joshua's marriage was on the rocks. After nearly losing his wife and his two year old daughter Bonita he finally cleaned up his act. By the beginning of June 2006 Joshua had finally returned to Global Wrestling. The following year his wife gave birth to their son Jason, whom had to fight for his life. Joshua continued ressurecting his career, and at many points his family became second nature. Most recently he has discovered he will be having another child in the family, as his wife is currently due shortly into the new year. Career Joshua's career really began back in 1995 in a smaller promotion called Insane Wrestling Association. His time there was short lived, as the promotion didn't live for very long. After IWA he really didn't stay in one promotion for too long, until he came upon EFWO. Back in EFWO Joshua captured some of his most coveted title reigns, and really began to grow in popularity. It was in EFWO he was noticed by PCW, and signed to PWA. After a match in PWA with Ken Kaze, he was signed to NTICW where his first notable feud ever took place. Against Hessian. Hessian and Kosidlo, then wrestling as The Josh feuded over the NTICW United States Championship. The feud culiminated with Josh taking the NTICW United States Championship from Hessian. The feud had gained him much recognition, and catapaulted him into the World title scene, a title held by Xavier Kannon, one of the top men in the wrestling world at the time. Before Joshua could get his title shot however NTICW's doors were closed, and Josh was called up to PCW. The PCW years weren't the best of times for Josh, and after a contractual dispute was released from PCW after less than a month. When it seemed his career was almost over, Bubba McCoy came beckoning, and gave Joshua a chance when no one else would. Since then, Joshua has been in and out of Global, at one point even joining WWA for a couple of months until his drug use caused him to lose all focus. In the last year he returned to Global, and has begun a rise to stardom he has never experienced before. Back in April Joshua purchased fledgling Independent Promotion New-Found Wrestling and pitched a deal with the Global Board of Directors. The deal allowed Joshua to become a member of the board, and the board would get NFW as Global's new development territory. In early September Joshua Kosidlo and Global parted ways, NFW was closed and Kosidlo signed with FUSE. In FUSE his rivalry with Jacque Noreault began, again. The rivalry with Noreault catapaulted Kosidlo through the rankings of FUSE and allowed him to secure a shot at FUSE's Livewire Championship in the not too distant future. Other Feds PWA, NTICW, PCW, Global, WWA, FUSE Other Fed Titles NTICW U.S. Champion, Global PrimeStarr Champion, AWC LiveWire Champion, Global Tag Team Champion Wrestling Style Technical Powerhouse/Martial Artist/Submissionist Finisher Move Kosidlo Driver 07' Finisher Description An inverted F-5 Finisher Setup Move The Torture Rack Setup Description A torture rack, that leads into the Kosidlo Driver 07' Special Moves See Movelist Other Moves Overview: Joshua is a third degree black belt in Martial Arts, but has also studied basic mat wrestling, greco roman wrestling, and combat submission wrestling. He is a 12 year veteran to the business, and a fierce competitor. Joshua makes good use of his power, and his speed/agility to try and out maneuver his opponent, however when it comes to getting down and dirty Joshua is one of the best pure wrestlers in the business. He likes to wear his opponent down the best way possible, for a high flyer he likes to work on the legs with holds and impact moves. Powerhouses, he tries to take out their arms as soon as possible. Sumbissionists, he’ll take them move for move and do what he can to outsmart them. He may also employ some of his strength based attacks, and quick impact moves. Joshua is a near suplexing machine, any suplex that can be thought of, he will do. He also has a bad temper, if someone pulls something cheap in the ring, like an eye gouge, or thumb to the eye, it is likely to anger him. Once under his skin, he is like a beast, relentless and near impossible to put down. This quality in him comes through most often in either hardcore conditions, or against a major rival. Also surfaces when under a great deal of stress. When he is in this state, he will not stop until he cannot move any longer. Also when in this state, he will do whatever he can to put his opponent through as much harm as possible, even if it means hurting himself in the process. He has two weaknesses... the biggest being his right knee, he’s had a bad knee since child hood, every now and then it acts up, usually during cold weather. He may favor it, but will never show the weakness to his opponent. The other is his back, he had an operation on it almost three years ago now, and from time to time it can give him problems. Usually only if he takes a severe bump, or if he lands bad on an edge, like the barricade, stairs, a table or the turnbuckle. Trademark: Bottom of Reality: A Rock Bottom, but instead you wrap your hands around their neck and lift them sweeping their legs out and slamming them like a Rock Bottom. The Eternal Nightmare: A cobra clutch sleeper hold. One arm is behind the neck forcing the forearm there (left arm) and the other arm is wrapped around the chin/neck area, locking the hand into the other arm at the elbow, the opponet's right arm is up next to their neck so as it is not actually a choke hold, and you have their arm and neck in your right arm. The Nightmare Ends: The Eternal Nightmare shot back into a suplex. IronPlex: Ironman grabs his opponent in a reverse DDT set up. He lifts them up holding their tights as though he were delivering a reverse stalling suplex. He drops them down hard on their head and into a cover much like a jackhammer. Check Mate: A reverse DDT/Neckbreaker. Joshua hooks his opponent in a reverse DDT, quickly he twists and drops his opponent in a modified reverse neckbreaker, a lot like a twist of fate. Paladin Plunge: Psycho driver Fallen Angel V 1.0: Full Nelson Suplex/Slam off the top rope Fallen Angel V 2.0: In heated matches Joshua will set a table up outside of the ring where one side is on the apron and the other is on the ground, it will be at a slant. He will then perform the finishing move from the top rope to the outside of the ring through the table. This is the original way The Fallen Angel was done.
