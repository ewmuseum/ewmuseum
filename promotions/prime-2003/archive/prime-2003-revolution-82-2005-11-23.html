ReVolution 82 ---- The Pre-Show We open on a scene unfamiliar to usual television viewers of PRIME's ReVolution show. Something that no doubt made a few people switch the channel a couple of times and dig under the sofa for the TV Guide to make sure they got this right. But of course they did, because if you've got the Playboy Channel, you know for sure which channel it's on. There is no other option. But there it is, in the middle of the screen: a red carpet and two bleacher sections full of screaming fans. Before we have a chance to speculate what the fuck's going on, Kathy Griffin, D-list celebrity and regular red carpet annoyance, steps into the frame and shrieks...oh wait, that's her regular voice. Kathy Griffin: Welcome to the PRIME ReVolution pre-show! We're here to get the scoop on this evening's activities before everything gets underway. Ohh, I think someone's arriving right now! Let's see who it is! Wandering somewhat aimlessly through the weird setup outside the wrestler's entrance, the Illustrious Face-Eater walks down the carpet, all but ignoring the cheers of the crowd along the way. Griffin scampers up to him, and he cringes a little. Kathy Griffin: Facey! Facey! Over here, Facey! The Illustrious Face-Eater: You're the only one here. Kathy Griffin: Oh, right. Along the red carpet, the volume increases exponentially as a black limousine pulled up to the curb. Griffin, tripping over her dress to get in the view of the camera, squeals and tries to come up with a joke. Kathy Griffin: It looks like the star of our show has arrived. I don't see what everyone likes about him, though - redheads are such pussies... The door to the limo swings open and Danny Ferguson steps out, dressed far more formally than he ever has been. This is a different Danny Ferguson than we've seen before. This man has a level of confidence that usually seems forced and defensive.
