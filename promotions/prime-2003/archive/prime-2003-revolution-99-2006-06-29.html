Karina Wolfenden has just prevented Tchu from saving Ignatius Lisieux from a vicious attack and now we have EMT’s on the scene attending to the “Inhuman Being”… she doesn’t even care, look at her dammit. Richard: She was protecting Tchu, you idiot. She doesn’t want to hear any excuses come ReVolution One-Hundred, for her it’s all about proving that she’s still the best! Nick: Well these fans certainly don’t seem to agree- good God what do Poteet and Sirrajin have planned for Lisieux here!? I know we’ve seen Mr. French-Fry do some pretty amazing stuff since his return, but I just think this one’s a bridge too far for him right here… Nick: Nobody plays possum better than the French-Canadian, I just hope that’s all he’s doing… Killean Sirrajin: Are you ready for your surprise, Iggy? He sits back, with a contented smile on his face. Revolution 100 would be his coup de grace, where Killean Sirrajin would show his superiority over Ignatius Lisieux, and the show would go down in history as one of the greatest shows ever. Nelson's eyes go wide, before his face scrunches, and he walks out of the room, leaving Blair behind. And we cut the show here.
