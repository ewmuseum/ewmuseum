The Sentinel Biography Wrestler Name The Sentinel Nicknames The Sentinel Hometown New York Height 6'5 Weight 255 lbs Biography Bio: (short) On the heels of an unsuccessful business trip to England, the Sentinel, otherwise known to his close friends and family as Wolfgang Moriarty has decided to call it quits at age 42. He cites being a full time dad, and declining health as the reasons. But behind closed doors something is ailing him, something he has kept secret all this time. After announcing his retirement, only a few days later has rumor spread about a return. This Sentinel is very unlike Wolfgang. He is more violent, more aggressive, and each day seems to dive deeper into madness. Has Wolfgang really come back, or is it something else? Wrestling Style Submission specialist, roughhouse Finisher Move Penance Finisher Description Argentine Back Breaker (Think the torture Wrack) Finisher Setup Move Breaking Point Setup Description Spinebuster Special Moves Boston crab, one-legged boston crab,camel clutch, abdominal stretch Other Moves Wrestling Style: The Sentinel is a very aggressive wrestler, fighting an entire match almost purely on adrenaline. Rumors have it, it might be the cause of drug use? Or maybe something else? But he is known to know a good amount of submission holds, so consider him a submissions specialist. He also rarely climbs the ropes. He is very unrelenting so consider him testing a ref's patience constantly on such things as 5 second counts to release his opponents. Stand Alone Impact Moves DDT (any variation) Powerbomb Atomic Drop Suplex Body Slam Side Slam Back Breaker Pile Driver Moving Moves: Clothesline Back body drop Spinebuster Submission holds: Arm bar Single leg boston crab Camel clutch Knee to the middle of the back, and pulls back on the chin. Whatever it's called Elevated Boston Crab Scorpion deathlock Entrance Music Ghost N Stuff By Deadmau5 Song Link http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QV8eiSA4vqc Ring Entrances The lights dim down to a moderate darkness across the arena as "Ghost N Stuff" by Deadmau 5 blares across the arena. After a few moments, search lights scan the arena before all focusing at the entrance. The Sentinel steps out from behind the curtain in his wrestling outfit. The costume is a full body suit with mask. It's colors are midnight black with white tribal designs on the mask. The outfit is complete with high black boots and black elbow pads worn over the sleeves. The Sentinel stands still for a second staring straight ahead at the ring. He cares not for the overwhelming amount of jeering aimed at him, nor for the minute fanbase cheering him. His body is tense and he slowly makes his way to the ring. "Hailing from New York City, New York, and weighing in at two hundred and fifty five pounds...THE SENTINEL!!!!" The Sentinel reaches the ring and uses the stairs at the corner to get to the top. He enters through the middle ropes and heads straight for a corner. He leans back there and awaits for the start of the match.
