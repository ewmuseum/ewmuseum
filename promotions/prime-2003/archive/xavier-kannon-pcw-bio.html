Other Fed Titles PCW World, Continental, Cruiserweight, Tag, and Extreme Champion. OSW Old School Champion. Other Fed Awards PTC/WO/eWP Universal Champion, winner of PCW Alliances. Manager Ellie Wrestling Style Methodical brawler. Finisher Move The Hallmark. Finisher Description Cradle Piledriver. Finisher Setup Move The 7-10 Split (Uppercut Low-Blow). Special Moves See move-list Other Moves Strikes (Aimed at wearing down an opponent/making it easier to double them over for the Hallmark/weaken the neck for the Hallmark) String of left jabs followed by leaping forearm, flying forearm, eye-poke, raking eyes along the ropes, knees to the side with an opponent hooked in a quarter nelson, step-up knee strike, high flying knee, knee drop to the back of the neck, shoulders to the midsection in the corner, spear to the midsection in the corner, pendulum elbow drop to the back of the neck, smashing an opponent’s head into the turnbuckle while they’re trapped in a full-nelson. Impact (Targets ribs and neck to both make it easier to hit, and increase the effectiveness of, the Hallmark) quarter nelson lift into a gutbuster, fireman’s carry gutbuster, Russian Legsweep reverse DDT(normal SRLS only with head hooked under arm), double-underhook lift with opponent dropped neck-first onto knee, short double-underhook power bomb (more to flip them over for a pin), rolling neck-snap, hangman’s neckbreaker (also done to opponent sat on top rope), ‘rolling’ swinging neckbreakers, flying head-scissors (often done to opponents draped over the ropes to take them over and out), hot-shot, inverted stunner, backdrop suplex, dragon suplex (rare). Aerial (Gambling his body is better as a weapon than an opponent’s is as a shield, nothing has flips or spins) Slingshot plancha, between-the-ropes suicide dive, slingshot forearm, slingshot huricanrana to the outside (rare), generally leaping off anything high. Holds (To wear down the neck and ribs for same reason as impact moves) Dragon clutch (nearly always applied as a counter-hold), camel clutch in the ropes, grounded abdominal stretch. Ellie A constant, scheming presense at ringside, making the referee wish he had eyes in the back of his head. Will act as a Cheerleader to Kannon, and a mischief to his opponent. Entrance Music The Matthew Santos intro to Lupe Fiasco's Superstar leading into Rock is Dead by Marilyn Manson. Ring Entrances Cheering and applause flows in through the speakers to drown out the jeering of the crowd, and the opening chords of Superstar by Lupe Fiasco jab through the darkness. If you are what you say you are… a Superstar... …then have no fear… …the crowd is here… A lone spotlight shines brightly onto the stage, illuminating a blizzard of flickering golden confetti that falls down onto its focus. Spinning amid the golden storm, arms outstretched, stands Xavier Kannon, hooded jacket shrouding him as soaks up the abuse. …and the lights are on and they wanna show… …oh, oh, oh, oh yeaaaah! …yeah… yeah… yeah… With Matthew Santos’ vocal echoing into the distance, the screeching intro to Rock is Dead by Marilyn Manson deafens those unlucky enough to be near a speaker. As light floods back into the arena, Eleanor is shown standing in front of her man, leading him down the aisle as he tosses the hood of his jacket back. Striding with purpose down the aisle, Kannon gives as good as he gets to fans along the aisle, while a member of PRIME security either side of his wife fend off any gropes. Vince Howard: And his opponent, to be accompanied by ELEANOR… from Merlin Bay, Barbados by way of Clearwater, Florida… weighing in at 226 pounds… HE IS THE KING OF WRESTLING… GOLD PATRON MERITORIOUS XAAAAAVIER KAAAAAAANNON!!!
