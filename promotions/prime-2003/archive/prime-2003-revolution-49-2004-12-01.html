The Asylum Written By Ignatius Lisieux The armoured van that had sped away from ReVolution 48 was now on the interstate, apparently headed towards a local mental asylum in order to drop off a new patient, namely Ignatius Lisieux. Still travelling at break-neck speed, its weary occupants were being flung around the back, all of them trying their best not to bump into the still-unconscious PRIME superstar. Lisieux lay on a stretcher, firmly strapped into the white straight jacket and buckled down with an assortment of Velcro straps as his captors looked on anxiously. Two of them were guards and were wearing some form of security outfit, but it didn't look like traditional arena security or any form of judicial costume, so their identities were somewhat clouded. All that was known was that they seemed to be working underneath Toshiaki Motoki. The third man keeping a close eye on Lisieux was, by reading his nametag, Doctor Silverman. He was a middle-aged man with stern lines furrowed into his skin, bushy grey eyebrows and a receding hairline. His face told the tales of years of hard work and stress, mostly in the company of criminally insane human beings and he struggled to keep his eyes open in the late California night. The van was flung across a lane, causing his eyes to grow wide and he angrily slammed his fist against the front-end of the container. Doctor Silverman: Will you stop driving like a maniac! We can't afford to be pulled over with him on board! The two guards were noticeably taking aback by the fierce venom that shot out of the Doctor's mouth and they hurriedly averted their eyes from his gaze, both preferring to look up at the roof of the vehicle. Both men seemed relatively young, but were well-built and looked menacing. They both were white males with shaven heads, although one had far darker hair than the other and a freakishly large scar across his cheek. Silverman shot his glare upon the scarred guard's face... Doctor Silverman: You... where did you get that scar from? You look awfully familiar to me... The guard cautiously looked up and shrugged his shoulders, quickly changing his angle of sight and preferring then to stare down at the floor as his partner watched on anxiously. Guard One: I don't... I don't remember. It happened when I was very young, I think... He spoke with a thick New York accent and Silverman grinned menacingly upon hearing his voice, he seemed to be confirmed in his knowledge of the young security guard, but did not let on. Doctor Silverman: You're from New York, I take it? Silverman himself was British, from near London, to be precise. His accent had changed greatly since he moved to California over twenty years ago, but there was still a brazen hint of Cockney in his accent. Guard One: Yes sir. Doctor Silverman: Terrible place, terrible, awful place... full of crazy people. The doctor's sarcastic grin instilled more fear into the guards, for some unknown reason, but his grin soon changed to a stony glare as his patient's eyelids slowly began to twitch as the bright light of the interior of the van began to wake him. Silverman waited anxiously, looking on intently at the still carcass of Ignatius Lisieux... and then his eyes shot open. Ignatius Lisieux: Wha..? Where am I!? Pierce... I'm gonna kill you! Lisieux writhed around in a still position as Silverman returned to a grin at the incapacitated superstar. Ignatius was severely frustrated and he looked around, as if terrified, to try and get an explanation as to why he was wearing a straight jacket and being strapped into a stretcher... let alone why he could feel movement and hear the hum of an engine. Ignatius Lisieux: Argh... where the hell am I? Who the hell are you? Silverman was leaning over him now, his stale breath beating unrelentingly against the nostrils of the former Global Champion. Doctor Silverman: Ah, so you've finally decided to join us Mr. Larkin. Ignatius Lisieux: ...Where the hell am I? Silverman shook his head, obviously disappointed that Lisieux refused to enter into conversation with him, he reluctantly answered... Doctor Silverman: You're in the back of a state vehicle heading towards a mental asylum in the vicinity, your employee Toshiaki Motoki has had you committed. You can call me Doctor Silverman; I'll be taking care of you for the foreseeable future. Ignatius Lisieux: Asylum? There must have been some mistake... I'm not crazy! Lisieux wriggled futilely once more, taking deep, aggravated gulps of air... The two guards were refusing to watch, it seemed as though this was cutting close to the bone with the pair of them... there seemed to be an overall reluctance about what they were doing. Doctor Silverman: I'm sorry but I've looked at your records Mr. Larkin, you're a bread-basket... two personalities, heads or tails... attempted murder? Any of this ringing a bell with you? Ignatius Lisieux: None of that stuff was real, it was a gimmick... I did it for Motoki! Doctor Silverman: Excuse me while I add paranoia and denial to that list... now, for future reference, would you prefer to be called Larkin or Lisieux? Ignatius looked slightly puzzled, seeming to forget his plight since returning to PRIME around six weeks ago. He finally caught on and answered... Ignatius Lisieux: I'm Richard Larkin. Ignatius Lisieux is just a creation of PRIME and Toshiaki Motoki, a character in a soap opera... Doctor Silverman: I can see you're going to be quite the challenge Mr. Larkin, however, I don't expect you'll be with us for too long, we're running on somewhat of a deadline. Before the doctor could explain any further, the van screeched to a halt and the two guards set off into action. The scarred one stood up and headed out of the double doors towards the back of the van, closely followed by his partner as they prepared the ramp to unload Lisieux from the vehicle. Doctor Silverman: It looks like we're here... Excuse me one moment. Silverman's bad breath stopped inflicting itself upon Ignatius' sense of smell as he turned away and rustled around with some metallic equipment, before pulling out a syringe and without a word jabbed it into the side of the neck of Lisieux. Doctor Silverman: Back to sleep Mr. Larkin... I'm under strict orders to keep this asylum's location under wraps, we can't have anyone interfering now, can we? The former Global Champion's eyes rolled sickeningly backwards into his skull and a small dribble of saliva travelled down his cheek as he fell back into a state of comatose. Silverman looked down upon his subject with a satisfied look and patted him on the shoulder... Doctor Silverman: It will all be over in a week Ignatius and then you'll be back to top form, better than ever... just like what Mr. Motoki asked for. The Doctor smiled again towards Lisieux before achingly rising to his feet and heading out of the van. The final shot was of a knocked-out Ignatius Lisieux, still tightly restricted by Velcro strapping and a white straight jacket with closed eyes and drool covering the bottom half of his face... what awaited during the next week would have to be seen to be believed. Pain In The Neck Written By The HIT Parked in the lot of the Arco Arena was a school bus. This was odd. Most fans came in regular buses, not school buses. The driver of the vehicle stepped out, red afro making him stand out like a sore thumb. If that wasn’t enough, the scars that marbled his body like fat in a fine steak were readily apparent to anyone who looked at him. This was the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid. A man who worked in the opposite fields of teaching and pro wrestling. While his entire teaching career had been in one school in Utah, his wrestling career had taken him to six continents. And he had shed pints of blood on every single one of them. A veteran of the IWO, PCW, RPW, UWF, and the most sadistic matches each of those federations could devise, after nearly a year away from wrestling, the HIT had returned. He looked up at the marquee, almost unbelieving that he was here. His neck had been broken in a match, and he had been told he would never wrestle again. Doctors had never been able to explain the Trapezoid, and that would continue to be true, as he managed to get himself back in fighting shape. The HIT: Ah. Home. The Arco Arena was the first wrestling venue he had been to. He couldn’t take the pain of being around one of his twin loves without being able to participate. This week, there would be no match for the Trapezoid. He intended to go backstage and introduce himself. PRIME had some familiar faces from his PCW days, and a lot of young, talented competitors. As he slung his duffel bag over his shoulder, however, he saw on the Arco’s marquee the two words that he never wanted to see again. Karina Wolfenden. Karina Wolfenden had been signed by PRIME.
