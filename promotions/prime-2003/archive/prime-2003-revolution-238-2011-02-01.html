He backs up slowly, taking very, very careful aim. Nick: He couldn’t squash Seymour against the post, but he’s going to try it again here in the corner! Richard: Wait a minute, Almasy’s doing something… Nick: What do you mean he’s doing something? He’s leaning there in the corner half dead! Richard: No, look! Almasy’s fingers work frantically, hidden by his body, pulling at the lacing holding the top turnbuckle pad in place. Casting a quick glance over his shoulder, and spotting Walczak ready to charge, he redoubles his efforts. Nick: Almasy’s trying to set the trap for Walczak! Richard: If he can get that pad off and get out of the way in time he’s golden! If not, he’s squished! The Polish Punisher charges, coming closer and closer. Mere moments before impact, Almasy drops to the canvas, clutching his prize of the turnbuckle pad. Too late, Walczak realizes what has happened, but he can barely brace for impact when he crashes into the corner, the steel bolt of the turnbuckle making impact with his chest. Richard: Walczak just went chest first into that unprotected turnbuckle at full speed! His sternum hit the bolt, and it may be cracked! The sound of the air leaving the Polish Punisher’s body is a hideous, crunching noise. Walczak collapses to the canvas, the pain such that he may in fact be unconscious upon impact. Nick: Almasy bailed himself out here, big time! He removed the protective covering and managed to lure Walczak in! Staggering over to the downed Walczak’s body, Seymour turns to face away from him. Crouching low, Seymour leaps in the air for a moonsault, over-rotating it to drive both knees HARD into Walczak’s chest and ribcage area! Richard: Good LORD! What the Hell WAS THAT? Nick: Moonsault double kneedrop! Right on the injured chest and sternum of Walczak! My notes tell me that he calls that the Paradigm Shift! Keeping his kneeling position on Walczak’s chest, Almasy grabs both legs of the Polish Punisher, pulling them up, and forcing as much weight as he can down over Walczak’s shoulders. Bernie Roberts looks on disapprovingly, but considering Walczak propelled himself into the turnbuckle, there’s not much he can do but count. ONE! TWO! THREE! DING DING DING! Nick: Yes, but it *was* like Seymour took the pad off. "Otherworld" begins to play once more, and the shrieks of the fangirls scattered throughout the arena are overwhelmed by a nice, steady booing. Howard: Your winner of this contest, by pinfall…"The Dynast-King" Seymour Almasy! Vince Howard’s enthusiasm is at a lower ebb, as Bernie Roberts raises Almasy’s hand in the middle of the ring. Richard: He told Lindsay Troy that he wanted to put PRIME on notice, and I think he’s done just that tonight. He assaulted Jacob McKail, he had words with our brand new JitC, and he just beat an on-fire Polish Punisher. Nick: He has in fact done all of that, but he’s also written a Hell of a lot of checks tonight that I don’t think he can cash. At least, I hope to God he can’t. Richard: Sour grapes, Nick. Sour grapes. Don’t we have to go now, anyway? Nick: That we do. For my broadcast colleague, Richard Parker, I’m Nick Stuart. Good night from Paris, France! And so it is that ReVolution 238 ends on a most unusual sight: Seymour Almasy, a man who had once sworn never to work for PRIME, standing in the middle of the ring, drinking up the boos from his critics and the cheers from his fans, victorious. Just how long he remains so triumphant, though, may well depend on the man whom he blindsided so viciously minutes into the show.
