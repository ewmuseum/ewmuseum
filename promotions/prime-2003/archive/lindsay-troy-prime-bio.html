I'm used to carrying him by now...), The Most Pwntastic and Most Shit-Kickingest Hall of Famer Ever (EAT A DICK, SONNY!) Biography There's a not-so-little girl, Who has a lot of curls, Cascading down her shoulders from her head. And when she is good, She is very....very good. But when she is bad... She's just better. Richard Parker: She’s the devil Nick. That explains it all. Nemo me impune lacessit: No one provokes me with impunity. WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW (A matchwriting aide) Three Strengths: 1. Mat/Ring Presence: A ten year veteran of the sport, she doesn't make rookie mistakes (ie, crowd taunts, taking too much time to execute a move, etc.) Always willing to try new things to add to her arsenal. Works tight, and will only work stiff if someone agitates her to the point where she feels she needs to make a statement...which, given her personality, doesn't take very much at all. 2. Speed: She's a woman. She's not going to be able to throw around a 250 pound guy so she has to rely on her speed and quickness of executing moves to control the match. Has great stamina and has proven she can last through a marathon match (60+ minutes). 3. Attitude/Resiliency: No-nonsense, both in the ring and out. Doesn't take crap from anyone, nor does she just "go away" if attacked physically or verbally. Knows she's good...better than most. Carries herself in high regard. To date, has never tapped out and would rather pass out from the pain, or push herself to a threshold. Three Weaknesses: 1. Temper: Her mouth has been known to get her into trouble...a lot. Yet, she somehow welcomes this. She may just be a masochist.... 2. Gender: She's held her own against men on a daily in-ring basis, but size-wise she's not intimidating (outside of her height) and not as physically strong as her male counterparts. Has to compensate for this. 3. Nagging Injuries: Fifteen years + Crazy moveset = Wear and Tear. Not like she'd let on, of course. Opponents like to target her legs (can't fly if you can't stand). She may not be a spring chicken anymore, but she'll still be serving your ass up on silver platters, sir. Personality Quirks 1. A) At first outset, generally does NOT like other women in the business outside of staff members, thereby leading some to believe that she's a sort of female misogynist. There are exceptions to this rule. B) Has purposely injured other women (managers and "wrestlers") who have gotten involved in her matches. Her mindset is that if they're going to get involved then they'll be made aware of the consequences of doing so...and she won't hesitate. Injuries to others have included: broken bones, a broken nose, bruised trachea, concussions, comas. Characters with female managers should definitely be aware of this and be wary of her. Best bet: avoid her and, unless you have a death wish, don't piss her off. C) No longer considers herself a "champion of equality." After ten years, there's not a doubt in anyone's mind that she can't hang with the boys. 2. What she says and how she is perceived seem to be in opposition to one another. While her demeanor at times would lead you to believe she's skirting the line of heeldom, it's been widely known that she doesn't really check what she's saying before she says it. If Honesty's the best Policy, then she's subscribed to it for quite some time. Somehow, people love her for this. 3. In her life, has had a love/hate (ok, mostly hate) relationship with Canadians (with exceptions). Has never met a Russian (or a Commie in general) she actually liked. Bad history with both. 4. She's a full-blooded Italian. She's a hothead, has a temper and doesn't really think before she speaks. She is not easily intimidated and is quick to call bullshit on something. She's a smartass and a know-it-all with biting wit and sarcasm. 5. A woman of the world, she is very in-touch with her surroundings and is extremely bright. She's also a bit of a pop-culture whore. 6. Hard worker, knowledgeable about the business, a good backstage influence. Overall, she does get along well with others unless given a reason not to, and if you're lucky to be called her friend then you'll have a friend for life. Does some sort of training daily, whether it's sparring in a ring, weights in a gym or just going for a run if neither of the other options are available. In peak physical condition, which allows her to be ultra-flexible and have the muscles in her body built up for ultimate performance. Under normal circumstances, doesn't have a problem with authority as long as the authority is working toward a common good. 7. Has a tendency to change theme music on a whim, when the mood strikes her. 8. Always wears title belts around her waist. In-ring Tendencies 1. Fighting style: Very well-rounded. 20 years of martial arts training under her uncle. Graduated from Dean Malenko's school in Tampa, Florida at the age of 20 and started on the Florida indy circuit shortly after. Spent time in Japan, both for martial arts (pre-wrestling school) and wrestling (post-wrestling school). Other "education" was received from Xandor Alvarez (a lucha-libre promoter in Mexico who later took the style to California) and her peers and friends in the business. Since she's always experimenting and trying new things, her "education" will be complete upon her retirement from the business. 2. Likes to dictate a match's pace, generally starting out hot with hand and leg strikes and continuing through until the end with traditional wrestling moves and innovative high-flying offense. Will incorporate rest holds on the offensive if needed. Can and will hang with the best of them. She sometimes forgets she's only 170lbs. If she thinks an opponent is dogging her, or not taking her seriously, or trying to throw the match, she'll go into shoot-style uber-stiff mode, and when you cry and whine about how the big, mean, bitchy woman hurt you, she'll maul you again for acting like a pussy. 3. She can execute any and all power moves on opponents in her weight class (no larger than 220lbs) without feeling much fatigue. For opponents 221-250lbs she can hit some power moves (suplexes, for example...nothing stalling or delayed) but will have something taken out of her. Even though she's one of, if not *the* strongest woman in the business, there's no way she's German suplexing a guy who's 290 pounds. C'mon now, who does she look like...KSZ? ;) 4. Will generally not taunt an opponent unless she feels it necessary. While she's sure of herself, she doesn't look to be cocky in the ring. Cockiness leads to missed opportunities. 5. Will cheat if necessary. Sometimes, though, someone just needs to get a chair wrapped around their skull. 6. Is a pure definition of a pain sponge, being flexible enough to withstand an ungodly amount of pain and having enough presence of mind to try and counter, break free or get to the ropes. Would rather pass out than tap out, but this isn't to say that she never will. 7. Can and isn't afraid to brawl. Doesn't shy away from hardcore-style matches. If she's the first to enter the ring, she will sometimes launch an aerial assault on her opponent as they're approaching from the ramp. 8. Doesn't really like using low-blows unless she finds it extremely necessary. They're a bit outplayed when they're used a ton of times during a match. Finishers Any one of her signature moves doubles as a finisher. Throughout her career, she's continuously added to her arsenal in order to have some variety. The Ultimate Finishers should always be a match-ender, especially the Crowning Glory...she's used that one since the very beginning of her career. Ring Attire Since her war with Cozen, her attire has changed. She now wears halter tops that secure around her neck and also just below her bust-line to show off her well-sculpted abs. They're tight-fitting, enabling her to still wear a sports-bra underneath. Design is generally Royalty-themed (crown, crown and scepter, crossed swords with a crown, etc.), but she has been known to wear custom-made T-Shirts with pop-culture or other snarky references on them. Pants are generally black and whatever color to match her top. Tailored fit to hug her curves, with a boot-cut for the leg. Boots are steel-toed shit-kickers, and her pants fall over the top of them. Heavily taped hands and arms up to her elbows (for handing Chinese throwing stars and wrapping barbed wire up her arms). Hair is pulled back in a pony-tail.
