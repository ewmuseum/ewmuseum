Razor Blade Biography Nicknames The Hardcore Legend, Raz, RFB Hometown Houston, Texas Birthday 08/14/59 Height 6'2 Weight 257 Fed Titles n/a Fed Awards n/a Biography To this day no one truly knows Razor Blade’s real name, all that is known is that early in his career he legally changed his name to Razor Blade. Razor Blade started wrestling young in his life at around the age of fifteen in his hometown of Chicago, Illinois. He wrestled with friends in backyard matches and what have you . At the age of Seventeen Razor Blade started to train on his own with lots of work outs and weight lifting in a local gym At this age, Razor Blade was pure muscle and would attend wrestling venues that came his way in Chi Town as he loved the sport of wrestling and had high hopes of one day being a wrestler himself. Three years later Razor had gained a lot of city and street fame in Chicago as a backyard wrestler with a lot of underground video tapes and what not. At the same time Razor would work independent shows jobbing to the indy stars that would stop by when they were in Chicago. It wasn’t until one time a promotion stopped by that Razor Blade really worked the crowd despite the loss, that the promoter offered him a deal. His first big chance, or so he thought, never the less it was the change that set him up for many years to come. The promoter also had a promotion based in Japan, and with the brawling tactics that Razor Blade possessed he had a good feeling that Raz would be a good fit. The promoter offered Razor Blade the deal to work and wrestle in Japan and if he were to make a name for himself that he would then have him shipped back to the United States to pursue his career. While in Chicago, Razor Blade had a girlfriend since his second year out of high school that often got overlooked due to his wrestling obsession. Her name? Jessica. Jessica however was still in high school. Jessica would later become his wrestling valet and better known to the wrestling world and industry as Soultress. Before Razor’s departure for Japan the two married under her parents legal consent. Razor left Soultress to stay in the States due to her needing to finish up highschool. So Razor Blade left for Japan with hopes of success and a quick return home. Whilst in Japan the Japanese vice promoter issued him a manager in which was the day that he met Bob. Razor Blade enjoyed lots of success with Bob at his side, thus the reasoning he has kept Bob at his side ever since as the two have become close friends. Ever hear the term over-qualified? Yeah, well that is exactly what happened to Razor Blade. Razor Blade ended up spending the first seven years of his career in Japan competing in some of the most hardcore matches ever to exist. This is where Razor Blade was deemed the Hardcore Legend when he won three Death Match tournaments three years in a row. Razor Blade won numerous titles and awards whilst in Japan, and most notably his one month shy, four year undefeated streak. Razor Blade became a fan favorite in Japan and humorously enough they would chant “Lazor Brade”. Razor also obviously had to make Japan a home because of the long stay in Japan, Tokyo to be exact. Of course the seven years weren’t spent alone as after the first year he had Soultress flown over and the two were together ever since. While in Japan Razor worked some matches with an established Mexican star by the name of Francisco Santiago, later to have a daughter by the name of Nicky Santiago. The two feuded in what seemed was a real feud but what many don’t know, and still don’t know is that in retrospect Razor Blade and Francisco Santiago were really in fact friends. Moving on, I’m sure of course you figured out that the first year afterwards he wasn’t granted back a deal in the United States. He continued his career in Japan where he began to be quite fond of it while continuing to make a name for himself. However, I did say seven years, and at the age of twenty seven the now Hardcore Icon and scarred up somewhat seasoned veteran was offered a deal with the UXCW. The UXCW was not what Razor had hoped for as it was an Indy promotion that lacked a lot of funding and shortly after his stay it fail victim to bankruptcy. Already back in the States with his wife Soultress Razor Blade toyed around with numerous Indy promotions picking up work now off of his established name in Japan. With UXCW and the Indy promotions combined (BAWF, ICWO, ARSF) Razor accumulated the following as listed. - 4 Hardcore Equivalent Titles. - 1 Intercontinental Equivalent Title. - 2 Tag Team Titles. - 3 World Titles. After touring the Indy promotions above for a little over a year Razor Blade was now becoming a hot commodity for up and coming federations to snag talent like himself. Razor Blade was offered a deal to the WeWF. Finally, a chance at some steady work, Razor Blade gladly agreed upon the contract that was a step in the right direction in his eyes. The WeWF was monumental for a lot of reasons. Most importantly, Razor Blade ran ruffshot in the federation and at one point capturing every single belt making it his second grand slam federation as far as capturing all titles. Secondly, Razor Blade met not only Big Poppa in the WeWF but Supernova as well. The three of them along with a few others formed a stable in T f’n A. T f’n A stood for Total Fuckin’ Anarchy. The stable dominated the federation and many other federations to come. While Razor Blade hit it off well with Big Poppa and Supernova he had now met his two best friends in wrestling. Little did he know that he would one day be looking at that last comment on the opposite end of the spectrum, absolutely vice versa. At a point Razor Blade and his group of fan favorite rebels had large disagreements with the powers that be and so they took their said fame to a federation by the name of XWL. There Razor Blade became the World Champ in no time, but once more Razor made a bad decision in his career as he left the WeWF for a federation that he thought was promising. The only thing it promised was the owner skipping out without paying the wrestlers their due pay. Now that Razor had convinced his friends to follow him they were a little upset, as their name was nowhere built to the magnitude that Razor’s was as he was already a ten year plus veteran while they were rookies in the industry. Bob put out word that Razor Blade was out of work and shortly after he had quite a few Indy promotions looking at him along with one somewhat nationally known federation the CWF. The CWF didnt yet have a television contract although it was rumored to get one soon with the success it had endured. Razor Blade had money put away for himself and his wife, Soultress. However, he knew his friends didn’t. Razor Blade took the offer for CWF under one condition, that they hire his friends. After a given tryout and Big Poppa and Supernova proved worthy the CWF had no quams at hiring them as well as Razor Blade. CWF didn’t know that in actuaility Razor Blade didn’t many plans of staying with the said federation as he only signed a six month contract and when the time was up, he moved on. Razor Blade, Big Poppa and Supernova alike joined up with Painkiller’s Five Star Wrestling (FSW). There Razor Blade helped bring the young , up and coming mainstream fed to glory as it had its own tv deal and monthly pay-per-views. Razor Blade formed a nice comradery with Pk and still has to this day. Razor Blade had lots of success in Five Star Wrestling including the longest win streak which coincidentally was an undefeated streak at that. FSW was also the place that Razor and Bob met Bald Wall, a homeless guy hiding behind a dumpster after a show, from there on out they took him in. Of course he was more so a retarded guy but never the less they felt sorry for the young’n (In his twenties) After Five Star Wrestling fame Razor Blade took a break from the wrestling scene to spend more time with his wife Jessica (Soultress). Big Poppa went on to bigger things on his scale while Supernova shortly after took a break as well. In FSW Razor Blade met the likes of Jake Keeton, Max Danger, EOE, Mike Marvelous, “The Idol” Jacob Jackson, and many others that would make an impact his career. After sixteen years in the business, Razor took his one year leave of absence as what he thought was his retirement. Shortly after that year with not much to do at home and a nice amount of money in the bank but not nearly enough to last a life time, Soultress nodded her head in approval as Razor laced up those boots again. Razor Blade signed with gfwaOMEGA better known as gO. gO was another Indy fed, at this point in time even with the fame not many federations were looking at the thirty seven year old wrestler. However, Max Danger the owner of gO took knew Razor Blade’s work ethic from FSW and despite the critics he took him in. Razor Blade excelled in gO becoming a major player and this is where Razor Blade contacted a big name at the time, Doozer. Razor Blade and Doozer seemed to see eye to eye on lots of things and before you knew it some strings were pulled and Doozer was in gO as well. This is where The S.H.I.T. began. Razor Blade and Doozer created the tag team claiming that they each were so good and impressive that they could beat any two wrestlers by themselves. Thus, The S.H.I.T. Single Handedly Impressive Team. Razor Blade to this day has never lost a tag team match up with his only notable tag team partners being Big Poppa and Doozer. Razor Blade met a lot of people in gO and had a lot of fun, Indy promotion or not. Razor’s knee started to bother him and so he took a few months out of action to rehab and in this short amount of time the federation went under more or less. With a bit more recognition to the wrestling world that his age didn’t make a difference he quickly found work in another Indy promotion where he worked a good amount of shows for a couple of months before he was tired of it all. It was then, that Razor Blade decided he would try his own hand a a promotion. Advantage Wrestling was born. Razor called up all the people he met through out his career, and accumulating the likes of Jake Keeton, Big Poppa, Sapphire, Supernova, Max Danger, Creature, Stone Devane, Rock Startling and many more created his company. Advantage Wrestling was semi successful and closed abruptly after a feud between Razor Blade and a wrestler in the federation Chris Rickter got out of hand. Rickter’s girlfriend was pregnant and she jumped on Razor Blade’s back in a scuffle and ended up getting thrown to the ground in a quick reaction which caused her a miscarriage. Rickters revenge? A stiff ddt to Soultress that put her in a coma for months. With that Razor Blade let his company crumble as he spent time with Soultress hoping she would awake. She never did, and not long after she past away. Razor couldn’t bare to see her dead body as he just signed papers and had a closed casket funeral which all of his wrestling friends attended in his time of pain and grief. Shortly after the funeral, the calls stopped. His friends didn’t call anymore to see how he was doing, they went on with their lives as if Razor Blade never existed. Big Poppa joined up with AW’s vice president’s rebound federation, Empire. Supernova left to New Era Wrestling (NEW) and others left to the two feds alike. Razor Blade drank his way into his own misery. After months of being an alcoholic Razor Blade sobered up and decided he would get revenge on those who where not their for him during his time of need. With that being said Razor Blade returned to the sport of professional wrestling. Razor Blade made an impact by signing with Empire and declaring a state of war against those the betrayed him. Of course however declaring anything against the top fan favorites in the federation automatically sent him on a course of hate and despise from the fans. Razor Blade plagued Empire week after week causing huge rivalries with one time best friend Big Poppa and his girlfriend, Sapphire. Razor Blade made a hitlist and started to widdle away on it one by one knocking out Jake Keeton and many others. Razor Blade lost for this first time in his career to Big Poppa at one of it’s pay-per-view events but won the war claiming two more victories over him later, one of which to be a #1 contendership to the World Title. The World Title wasn’t what thrived Razor Blade as he was now a man built on hate, he didn’t care about any gold, he’d won more belts then most men have belts in their closets. Razor Blade was here for revenge and he was a mad man acting more crazy with each week that past. To make matters worse a lawyer showed up at the door of Razor Blade’s home in Houston, Texas exactly one year after Soultress’ death. The lawyer bare in hand an envelope. Strangely enough this lawyer was hired by Soultress he quoted. “This was told to be given to you if Jessica were to ever die at a young age.” Soultress died at the age of Thirty-six as Razor was Forty-two. Razor Spike - He is slowly moving away from the Razor Spike though to his new move, Heaven to Hell. Never the less, the Razor Spike is the move that Owen Hart did to Steve Austin at Summer Slam 97 I believe. Although Owen broke Austin’s neck the move can still break anyone’s neck if not done properly. If and when the drop of impact is made the opponent’s head is sticking out of the bottom of the enclosed legs than there is a good chance of it breaking or seriously injuring someone by the neck. Razor however had perfected it where its much like the tombstone where the opponent’s head never actually has true hard impact with the mat. If this move is done, and your writing it in, be sure to know they are NOT going to kick out of this move unless I have given you permission. Razor Spike Variations - There really isn’t any true variations, the only variations are just from like on top of things, like, from the 2nd rope or top rope (Very rare, and for more lax feds, not tSC) Off of apron, on top of chair, etc. Heaven to Hell - Razor Blade will lift the opponent over his back much like the Razor’s Edge, however, he will do this inversely ... so the opponents stomach is on his back. Razor will then throw them but in air catch them by the head executing a diamond cutter. With bigger opponents, he may not do the lifting part and just grab them by the inside shoulders and lift them that way, instead of putting them in between his legs and doing the whole lift an flip over concept. The lift is genuinely thought as lifting them to Heaven and then the drop of the diamond cutter is plunging them to Hell, thus, Heaven to Hell. People can kick out of this move, although slightly rare, but it can be done, and would most likely be saved for a ppv or a very important match. Heaven to Hell Variations - Variations depend a lot on weight as the smaller the opponent the more ways Razor Blade would be able to perform the move as they would be easier to lift. Against a small opponent he can counter an Asai moonsault by catching them over his shoulders and then executing the move. For mid size wrestlers and small wrestlers he can catch them off of a double axe handle smash (top rope, apron to outside, etc.) and execute the move. Combination, Jawbreaker > Heaven to Hell. Submission Finisher - If the time comes where Razor Blade needs a submission to win the match then he will most notably use the sharpshooter.
