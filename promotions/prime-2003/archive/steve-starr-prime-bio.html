Steve Starr Biography Wrestler Name Steve Starr Nicknames None Hometown Kansas City, MO Birthday 06-26-1981 Height 5'11 Weight 215 lbs Fed Titles PRIME Intense Champion(1x) Fed Awards N/A Biography Steve Starr was once on the fast track to superstardom. As a member of PRIME in its early years, Steve burst onto the scene with an amazing repertoire of aerial maneuvers that left fans everywhere salivating for more. After quickly establishing himself with signature victories over some established superstars, Steve was quickly becoming a force in the professional wrestling world. That is until he aligned himself with TDK Inc., a sports agency firm that convinced him to become their first professional wrestling client. Much of the success that Steve had earned had gone to his head, and he began to lose sight of why he was a wrestler in the first place. It was never about the money, the fame, the flashy lifestyle that he loved to portray; Steve Starr just loved to impress people. He was all about putting on a show, telling a story, producing high-quality bouts. Steve Starr was all about showmanship, and he knew just how to market himself to the people.
