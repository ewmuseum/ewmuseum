Alliance Wrestling Style: Neil and Gideon have several wrestling styles to choose from so what they do one match may vary greatly from a previous match. They take great pride in being prepared for an opponent and study them inside and out to be prepared for their big moves. One thing that is standard is that they are very familiar with each other’s styles so there isn’t as much communication needed. They generally have a gameplan set before going to the ring as well as a back up plan to switch to if the first plan isn’t working as well as it should. The Alliance has 4 wrestling styles which they use regularly. 1: Power/Technical style: This is the standard style and in many cases the default for the Alliance. Neil uses Power and Technical moves while Gideon uses Striking and Submissions. 1: This can change a bit depending on who starts or what is needed. Gideon wears down a limb and tries to set up a submission. Neil uses his power to beat the opponent down with high impact technical moves. While this is pretty much the same style they use in singles Neil will switch to help Gideon wear down a limb for a submission. Gideon also will switch over to use High Impact technical moves to help slow down opponents and wear them down. 2: Showboating: This is focused mainly on showing up an opponent. The Alliance uses this style when they want to prove a point or annoy the opponents. 2: This is demoralizing because the Alliance are pretty much non stop verbal abuse. They will take an opponent down and lock in a submission and they will say anything they feel will annoy the person because they are helpless. They won’t fail to point this out to the guy on the outside just begging him to come in the ring to shut them up. If they can provoke the opponent into coming into the ring neither Gideon or Neil have any qualms about burying a “Greco Roman right legged front kick to the ribs” (Groin kick) while the refs back is turned. After dropping the opponent they lock in a ridiculous submission hold and get the referee to ask the opponent if he submits while smiling at the opponent outside the ring and if Neil or Gideon has a hand free they give a hand gesture to the opponent outside the ring to emphasize the point. 3: Submission/Counter style: The purpose of this style is to take someone out in the ring legally or to demoralize because not only are you countering them you are weakening them and put the fear of submissions in their mind. They will get a submission anyway they can. Not used often unless they have an agenda in the match. 3: This style is very self-explanatory. This is totally devastating against a big man. They counter moves and strikes and weaken them for a submission. If someone goes for a shin kick Gideon would legdrag them or use a dragon screw legwhip. If a clothesline is attempted then a single armbreaker or Painkiller armbar might be applied. Person tries a move or a strike and is put into a submission or taken down. When an opponent leaves their arm open Gideon will go after it with the Painkiller. The slower the move the easier it is to employ this style. Gideon loves to demoralize opponents by stopping their offense dead in its tracks. Neil while not as quick at countering has the advantage of sheer strength to force counters regardless of the opponent trying to prevent the counters. 4: Street Fight style: The purpose is to just to beat people up. No style just 2 men beating someone up. 4: (Neil): This is a style you do not want to see. If he is going street fight style he is out to seriously hurt someone REAL bad. There is nothing pretty or flashy here. He starts driving knee after knee into the opponent. It doesn’t matter if it is to the head or the ribs he gets an opening and he is going to drive a knee somewhere. If he decides to take the person down then he is likely going to start raining punches to the guy’s head to mess them up. This style can be countered but Neil will take a hit to do damage if he has to. 4: (Gideon): This is perhaps the most brutal of Gideon’s styles. Gideon’s striking has been honed over nearly 30 years. Gideon’s elbow strikes are not generally planing on knocking out opponents. If Gideon throws elbows he wants to bust the opponent up. Knee lifts and shin kicks to the ribs are designed to demoralize the opponent by making it hard for them to breathe. Shin kicks are to weaken the opponent’s legs so they can’t move and eventually not even be able to stand. The ones to really worry about are the punches and kicks. They are designed just to flat out knock the person out. If you are not knocked out by a punch or kick it is likely because Gideon wants to hit you again. Entrance Music Reckoning Day by Megadeth
