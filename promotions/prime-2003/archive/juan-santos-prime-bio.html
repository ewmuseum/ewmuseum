Juan Santos Biography Hometown Long Island, NY Birthday August 17, 1977 Height 6' 4' Weight 248 Fed Titles JITC 2005 winner. What? >=) Biography First off, this character was made years ago, so if the story comes off as clichés, then blame the evolution of E-wrestling, :p Shit, I’ve made so many changes to this character, I don’t know where to begin. Okay, Syko…er Juan Santos, was orphaned at birth against his underage mother’s wishes. Due to difficulties and the overwhelming authority of her parents, Juan’s mother, Marissa Santos, was forced to leave Juan at an orphanage and later on leave him for good. Juan was transferred throughout any orphanages in the Tri-State area, with the majority of the time spent in the St. Patrick’s Orphanage in New York. Like so many would-be orphans, Juan never knew his mother, or his father, and presumed that neither was worth looking for so he never set out on a mission to find his parents. After he was done being moved from foster home to foster home, Juan attempted to try to get his life in track by going to community college and getting a degree in mechanics. Yet, his short temper and not-for-me attitude led him to multiple suspensions, and eventually expulsion from school. Juan became very delinquent, often getting arrested for robbery and aggravated assault. He continued that path until he came about a local wrestling gym. With no where to turn for work, he agreed to do the wrestling gig and in a year made enough to get by. Eventually, when he turned 23, Juan caught the eye of a passing federation talent seeker, from the sWo. Juan’s beginnings in the sWo were very forgettable. Blotched spots. Awkward rants. Very little fan base. At the brink of unemployment, Juan stepped up a bit, dubbing himself Syko Arsonist, and burning his opponents, in response to a fire he had survived in his earlier years. The gimmick didn’t quite catch on, but he was getting the attention of the roster, and more importantly the fans. Over a year or so, he was revered as one of the greatest technical wrestlers the sWo had ever seen. His new image became so powerful, that no one really called him Juan anymore, but Syko Arsonist. His success in the ring had become that much significant. Juan Santos continued to live the fast life – getting money, getting laid, getting drunk. He also appeared on several other federations’ shows and for a while it seemed that this was all he was set to do. During one of his few federation mainstays, Juan became enthralled with a woman who was assigned to be his manager and personal trainer. She was called Emilia Del Fuego (go figure). Their relationship started out slowly, but then materialized into the most significant relationship Juan Santos had ever had. Their relationship reached a climax two years after their first date, with Juan proposing marriage to her, and she accepted. However, she was brutally murdered in cold blood before his very eyes. When things couldn’t get worst, Juan received the news that Emilia was pregnant with his son during the time of her cold murder. From that moment on, Juan turned into an emotional wreck and isolated himself from the public. He was often invited to appear in many events for healthy bonuses, but declined the majority of them. The impact of losing his love, and his unborn son, nearly drove him into madness and he became the apathetic person he is known as now. Within two years, he nearly spent all he had earned through lucrative contracts, although he still maintains quite a bit amount of money. Finally in the late spring of 2004, Juan Santos was invited to participate in Prime Time Central’s first annual Cruiserweight Tournament. He lost weight of course – mostly to neglect and not keeping in shape. He reached the semi-finals of that tournament, but received a far greater impact: his real mother had actually found him. At the age of 43, Marissa had spent half her life for her soon. Her initial visit was met with hatred, disbelief, and denial. After several tests, and attempted conversations, Juan finally accepted Marissa as his mother. Other Feds Just know that I noshowed all of them and those I did show up I used stolen work. Superflex Tornado DDT off 2nd ropes SET UP MOVE/TRADEMARK MOVE The Recurring Nightmare Description: Juan Santos sets up the victim in a standard powerbomb setup. He lifts the victim up and snaps them down quickly, while still maintaining his hands locked around the victim's waist. He lifts the victim up and slams them down two more times. Juan can then connect straight into the finisher, Asylum Grounds, or go for the pin here. FINISHER The Asylum Grounds Description: Having the majority of your moves deal damage to the back area has its rewards here. Juan hooks the victim's legs and turns him over in standard Boston Crab-style. However he steps further back and elevates the legs. At times it may seem as if the hold applies pressure to the victim's legs as well. OTHER SUPERMOVES The Emerald Frosion Executes a side Michinoku Driver-like slam. The EF is one of the more versatile supermoves as it can be executed from a standstill or a reversal from an aerial attack. Dementia Shoulder neck breaker. Can be done from a front face lock hold, or as a ducked under reversal. WRITER'S GUIDE Juan Santos is a technical/submissionist wrestler. Obviously his stragety (I hate spelling that word) changes depending on the size of the opponent - anyone at around 280 and below he can do power moves, especially suplexes and powerbombs. For wrestlers larger than that, he sticks mainly to impact moves, ala DDTs, and submissaion holds.. For an opponent around his weight range... ..fights the more balanced. Alternates between power moves, impact moves, and submissions. Always concentrates on the back area, and thus performs more back breaking moves than anything else. Begins to use submissions, only late in a match when both appear exhausted. For an opponent over his weight limit (290+)... ...won't use powermoves, like superflexes, duh. But will rely heavily on impact moves that don't require to much lifting, like DDT's and such. Juan Santos also relies on more streetfighting techniques and tries to keep opponents down with any of his submission moves. For any opponent... ...the strategy is to work on the back area. Plain and simple. For opponents he can do powermoves on, he shies away from any super moves, ala turnbuckl - except in the most marquee of matches. IN RING PSYCHOLOGY Juan Santos maintains a calm psychology. He rarely gets upset and will never use any methods of cheating out a win. He is very calculating in his attack, opting to go for quick impactr moves in the beginning. His tactics are to out stamina an opponent by outwrestling him, providing enough of a window to take out a backside with the Recurring Nightmare or Asylum Grounds. His entrance to the ring is also caluclating., He doesn't showboat and walks sternly to the ring. Entrance Music 'Paso Doble' by Orquesta Guayacan
