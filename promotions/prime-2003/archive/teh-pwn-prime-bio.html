TEH PWN!~ Biography Fed Titles n/a Fed Awards n/a Other Feds Gabriel Brown (RPW, NFW) // Vangellus Oxios (RPW) Other Fed Awards Gabriel -- RPW Tag Team Championship, RPW World Championship // Vangellus Oxios -- RPW Eclipse Championship Finisher Name See Moveset Finisher Description See Moveset Setup Move See Moveset Setup Move Description See Moveset Move Set Tag Team Strategy What must be understood about this tag team is that Gabriel Brown is the one who holds it all together. He is a great wrestler on his own, but throughout his career he has functioned more as a tag team specialist and it shows. While Vangellus Oxios is capable of defending himself, he's not as proficient in tag team wrestling, and dso Gabriel is the one who sets the attack strategy and he is more likely to be the one to call moves in advance. Oxios is much more of a 'What the hell?' kind of individual, leading him to improvise double-team moves more than his partner, who has planned out some of their attacks. Together, they will primarily attack the head (as Oxios has a harder time targeting limbs). Gabriel may target another part of the opponents' anatomies so that there are multiple damaged areas to exploit. Oxios works exclusively to attack the head and neck, although he has some token high-flying moves which land on the torso. The two have individual finishers, but it is unlikely that they will ever develop a true 'tag team' finisher. Gabriel has formulated a number of finisher-worthy attack options for them, and Vangellus just might make up something on the fly that will get the job done. The techniques below are those that Brown has had the two practire together, honing the execution to near-perfection. Anything that is especially damaging and potentially gaining of a three count is marked with an asterisk. Anything that will definitely keep down an opponent is marked with two asterisks. The base attack is that move from which the double-team attack branches. The subheadings come for the accompanying attack done by the other member of the team.
