ReVolution 148 ---- Your Turn Before any pyro. Before the screaming fans. Before a panoramic shot of all the clever (and sometimes crude) signs in the arena, we open on the scene of PRIME’s official ‘man-in-charge’, sitting behind his desk, looking sharp in a pinstriped black suit. Blaine Blair: Good evening Ladies and Gentlemen. As you all know, we’re here tonight for ReVolution 148, and with a bit of quick, simple math, many of you have probably already deduced that the 150th edition of ReVolution is just around the corner. In fact, two weeks from tonight, PRIME will celebrate the epic occasion by doing something we’ve never done before. Blair gives a tug to his suit as he straightens his posture, preparing for the impending announcement. Blair: Those of us in the PRIME front office are going to take a vacation of sorts. What does this have to do with the 150th episode of ReVolution? Simple. With our ‘take a step back approach’, someone will have to decide just exactly how the historic edition of ReVolution should go down. And that someone… Leaning forward, Blaine points his finger straight into the lens of the camera. Blair:… is you. With a smile, PRIME’s CEO lays it all out. Blair: It’s time for you, the fan, to Vote the ReVolution. Every match on the card will in some way shape or form be influenced by the decision of the public. On this blockbuster night, your voice will be heard. Oh… and titles will be on the line. Without further delay… I give to you the ReVolution 150 card… in its half-finished state. Lifting a piece of paper from his desk, Blair begins to read from the list. Blair: The Intense Championship will be on the line as former champion Wade Elliott gets his rematch against the reigning champ, and 2006 JitC winner, Tony Gamble. And you, the fans, will decide if the contest will be a Last Man Standing, TLC, or Barb-wire Rope match. Somewhere the sick twisted minds of fans are already plotting the most savage encounter to plug the intense superstars into. Blair: The 5-Star title will be on the line when Brandon Youngblood defends against the fans choice of either Tyler Rayne, Nitz Donnelly, or Johnny Noble. The two superstars not selected will square off in a one-on-one match of their own, with winner earning a shot at the 5-Star belt down the road. Blaine pauses to clear his throat. Blair: And last but not least, the tag and Universal titles. Winning the JitC means a lot of things. It means a piece of immortality. It means a guaranteed spot in the CS main event. It means a shot at the Universal title at that event. But it doesn’t mean the first crack at the champ. Instead, Chandler Tsonda will special referee a match in whichSonny Silver will defend the top prize in the business in a triple threat contest against the fans choice of Lindsay Troy, Devin Shakur, Hoyt Williams, and superstar to be named The two highest vote getters will challenge for the Universal title while the two other superstars will be square off for the right to compete for the Universal title at UltraViolence as one-quarter of a fatal four-way. Blaine lays the piece of paper on his desk and smiles into the camera. Blair: Voting will begin this weekend. This is your chance to make the calls at one of the biggest ReVolutions in PRIME’s existence. Your chance to make history.
