Kenjiro Ito Biography Nicknames The Future of Wrestling, The Cocky Osakan, Bad Will Ambassador Hometown Osaka, Japan Birthday April 30th, 1979 Height 6'4' Weight 258 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Kenjiro Ito Match History: IOW International (IOWi): Coming Soon Jolt Wrestling: (Jolt): Coming Soon All-Star Championship Wrestling (ACW): Coming Soon PRIME Wrestling (PRIME): 1-2-0 Culture Shock 2006 Tokyo, Japan The Dual Halo Match Winner: Chet Worth (Kenjiro Ito eliminated by Ignatius Lisieux) ReVolution 91 Tacoma, Washington Kenjiro Ito vs. Kyle Lamen Winner: Kenjiro Ito via pinfall with Ito's Ice Age. Other Feds Jolt Wrestling, IOWi, with guest appearances in All-Star Championship Wrestling Other Fed Awards Jolt Ambassador Champion, Jolt Triple Crown Champion Wrestling Style High impact Japanese strong style Finisher Move Ito's Ill Awesomeness Finisher Description Pyramid Driver (High Angle Cross-Arm Sitout Powerbomb) Finisher Setup Move Bakemono Lariat Setup Description Hard, charging left-armed lariat Other Moves Bold denotes signature moves. Bold + Italic denotes rare moves. Anytime Moves: Knifeedge chops, forearm/elbow strikes, fireman's carry, elbow/knee/legdrops, DDT, vertical suplex, hiptoss, armdrags, basic arm/leg/neck submissions, bodyslam, backbreaker. Strikes: Running Yakuza Kick, shotei, Roaring Elbow, Flash Frozen (Running Yakuza kick with the opponent facing the turnbuckle, driving their face into the buckle, Bakemono Lariat (STIFF left-armed lariat) Power Moves: Military Press Backbreaker, Thunderfire Powerbomb, Piledriver, Elevated Double Arm DDT, Full Nelson Slam, Chokeslam, Reverse Chokeslam, Tiger Driver, The Home Islands (Four rolling vertical suplexes. Ito calls out the name of each Japanese home island as he executes the suplexes, ie: 'KYUSHU! SHIKOKU! HONSHU! HOKKAIDO!') Suplexes: Gutwrench Suplex, bridging Belly To Back suplex, Belly to Belly Suplex (side and overhead varieties), German Suplex (bridging and release), release Tiger Suplex, release Dragon Suplex, Blizzard Suplex (Bridging Exploder Suplex),Rolling Blizzard Suplexes, Top Rope Blizzard Suplex, Apron-to-Floor Blizzard Suplex Submissions: Fujiwara Armbar, Jujigatame, Dragon Sleeper, Bow and Arrow Lock, ITO Special (Powerbomb into Texas Cloverleaf) Aerial: Top Rope Diving Shoulderblock, Top Rope Flying Legdrop Finishing Moves: 1. Ito's Ill Awesomeness (The IIA): Kenjiro Ito's patented Pyramid Driver (Cross-Arm High Angle Sitout Powerbomb). Thus far, the IIA has not failed the arrogant Osakan, meaning victory every time he has executed it. Making the move more deadly is that Kenjiro has developed variants for it, designed to counter opposing moves directly into the IIA. The acronym has been redone to reflect his status as the CEO of Awesome. VARIANTS: - Elevated-Style IIA: Innovated against former PRIME superstar Aimz. In this variant, Ito simply catches an opponent out of a hurricanrana, crosses their arms while on his shoulders, and throws them down forcefully with the IIA. - Captured-Style IIA: Created against his current ally, Sonny Silver. Designed to counter a high kick. If the opponent throws one, Ito catches it, as if for a Capture Suplex, and then crosses the opponent's arms, lifting them in the air and nailing them with a fall-forward variant of the IIA, driving the opponent on their head. - Avalanche-Style IIA: Also known as Ito's Ice AVALANCHE, this is the typical IIA off the top rope. It won him the Jolt Triple Crown on their last ever show. Fear it like death. 2. Pearl Harbor: Kenjiro's highly insensitive name for his alternate finisher. A Dick Togo-style top rope senton bomb, he likes to execute it with the opponent face down, driving all of his weight into the back of the victim's head.
