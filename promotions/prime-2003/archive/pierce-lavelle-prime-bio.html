Pierce Lavelle Biography Hometown Harrisburg, PA Birthday 12th August 1982 Height 6ft2 Weight 235lbs Fed Titles Intense Title Biography Lavelle was raised in a small town called Williamsburg in the capital of Pennsylvania with his father Keith Lavelle and his mother Marie Bernstein. Lavelle was born on the 12th August 1982 in Mercy Day hospital with his twin sister, Rebecca Lavelle. His father’s side of the family were Roman Catholics and his mother’s side were Jewish. Pierce and his sister were baptised Catholic. At 16 Rebecca decided to convert to the Jewish religion and Pierce became an atheist, deciding to believe in neither. Pierce was an average student in all his classes but he was a star athlete on the wrestling mats. His sister on the other hand was studious and also took part-time work in the student centre aiding others in their work. Having failed his Calculus exam, he was sent to be tutored by his own sister leading him to pass his finals and go to College along with his sister. On one winter’s day while the twins were out late celebrating. Marie came home to find her husband and their Lawyer bent over the living room table and that was the end of that star-studded relationship. With the divorce finalised, Marie took a profound hatred toward Keith and Pierce and his sister began staying with Marie’s mother, Grandma Bernstein. It soon became apparent that Pierce would become the black sheep. Rebecca became the good daughter, studying her religion, attending mass every week with her mother and grandmother and talking in Hebrew and Pierce would spend time listening to metal and watching programs and ignoring the gibberish that would emerge when he got in trouble. With his uncle and aunt now in town, Pierce had dropped out of College and began to aid his Uncle Jorg Bernstein in his Mechanics shop while his sister began to date ,Joey Mayr, the baker’s son and married a Jewish man with inheritance to his name – making her once again the perfect daughter. Pierce soon moved out of the small town and joined the wrestling business. With the start of his career beginning in the AWC, his efforts were thwarted by Alexander Strider. Strider proved to be more than a match for Lavelle who held mere basics in wrestling when he first joined. Through months of training and sparing, Lavelle went on to become the transatlantic champion having defeated Strider at Solarized. His career was almost shortened by his near death experience as he was shot by a quaint but versatile mafia gang in Albany New York headed by Marvin Harrington whom under the FBI’s duress died after escaping. From here on he began to make a name for himself with wrestling and became a more confident person. The long stint and his career in the AWC were not easy. Although having held the transatlantic title three times and being enrolled into the hall of fame, his career was stunted by the Prometheus incident and his unlikely foe, The Illustrious Face Eater. Through the ramblings of a crazed man and the beliefs of Sasha Volkyeva Lavelle became accused of taking the Prometheus drug to enhance his wrestling performance. This brief pause in his career was taken out of court with a not guilty verdict through his lawyer, Lois Wood. The accusations didn’t stop him and he came back with a vengeance that would soon lead to his involvement in the Empire and Kassidy Drake's end. Although his one year career was short and his wrestling brought the slightest disgrace to his mother Lavelle managed to make a name for himself and recent months saw him end his career with the Triangles bash along with many of his colleagues in the defeat of the Empire. Life now leads Lavelle back to that small town in Harrisburg Pennsylvania and on his way to starting a career in Prime. Russian Legsweep STO Wrestling Style: Pierce Lavelle is very methodical and quick about the way he approaches wrestling. He focuses mainly on technical submission moves and power. Using high-flying for entertainment value and also desperation when an opponent regains momentum outside the ring, or underneath him. At the beginning of a match, he’ll scout out his opponents movements – weakness’s and strength’s – he’ll play it easy at the beginning, working on the softer areas of wrestling. Middle of the match is where the fun will begin – he’ll begin to use his technical and submission moves to out manoeuvre his opponent, playing games with his opponents mind. Keeping a quick speed momentum throughout, tiring his opponent while working an area of weakness. If the opponent begins to cop-on to the wrestling style, he’ll switch immediately to Power, to hurt, weaken and confuse his opponent. If the opponent regains momentum and soon takes over the offensive, Pierce will use desperation moves – high flying, quick tosses and power moves that allow for a quick upper hand to allow him to turn the tables once more. Nearing the end of the match, he’ll remain focused on the weaker points, usually an arm, neck or shoulder – allows him to apply a stronger move effective hold with his finishing move. He’ll set his opponent up and then connect the Whiplash, usually causing his opponent to tap-out or be pinned for the three count.
