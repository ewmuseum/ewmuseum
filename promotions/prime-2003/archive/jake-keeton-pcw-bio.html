Jake Keeton Biography Nicknames The Solution, Tradition's Trademark Hometown Lexington, KY Birthday Jan. 1st, 1977 Height 5'9' Weight 217lbs Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography : “When it comes to this business, you name it and I've probably done it. I've gone from wrestling in front of 10 people at the National Guard Armory in Cynthiana, Kentucky to winning World Titles in front of sell out crowds at Madison Square Garden, and back again. I've wrestled in front of a hundred rednecks all packed into a flea market and in front of the truest wrestling fans in the world in Japan. I've been to the top and below the bottom, and I'll rise right back to the top.” – Jake Keeton Jake Keeton was born June 9, 1977 at the University of Kentucky Hospital. His father was a drunk and left when he was seven years old leaving his mother to raise him and his younger brother. His mother worked hard and had two wonderfull sons. Jake excelled in all sports in Junior High School. In High School he was all-state in Basketball and Football as a freshman. When his junior year was set to begin the Football coach told him he was to skinny for football, and the Basketball coach said he was to short for Basketball. Jake began working out hard every day and soon found his sport, Wrestling. Jake began wrestling mid way through the season of his junior year. He won the state championship and went on to the Nationals, coming in second. By his senior year he was the top amateur wrestler in the world, and won the 1996 NCAA Championship at 180.5 lbs. After a disappointing knee injury caused him to place 8th in the 1996 Olympic games. Jake received a full scholarship to the University of Mississippi but would have been forced to sit out his freshman season due to the injury. A regional pro-wrestling promoter, known only as XXX, saw Jake in the gym one day. XXX offered Jake the type of money that would be unthinkable to a rookie in pro wrestling. Jake quickly dropped out of college for the spotlight of the wrestling business. 10 years and some 47 titles later, he’s still going. From horrible gimmicks, being labeled as nothing more than a good Cruiserweight wrestler, to being one of the most hated men in the business as Advantage Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion, he’s had an unforgettable career. The revelation in 2002 that he had a son he had no idea about who’s mother had just died brought a change of heart to the bitter Keeton. When his son was taken away from him by the courts he entered a downward spiral of drinking and pain killers. After almost dying in detox he spent close to a year in rehab. When he began getting back in shape for the business and trying to raise his son with only the help of a his mother and a few close friends he realized he needed to set an example for the little boy and do the right things not only in life but in his chosen profession. After a shocking upset of Shane Steele, the most dominant champion in Power Surge Wrestling history, Jake captured his second PSW championship, this time with the support of the fans behind him. Outside the ring his life was falling apart again though. A week after he won the title his son was once again taken away from him by the courts and PSW filled for Chapter 13 Bankruptcy. And this is where the story picks up… Other Feds PSW, Advantage Wrestling, PCW(Pro and Primetime), PWC, Empire, Titans of Wrestling, XwC, TWC, many many more. Other Fed Awards Advantage Wrestling World Champion, Advantage Wrestling North American Champion, Advantage Wrestling Tag Team Champions(w/ Chris Rickter), Advantage Wrestling Hall of Fame, Advantage Wrestling Co-Wrestler of the Year(2002 I think), Power Surge Wrestling World Champion(x2), Power Surge Wrestling Television Champion(x3), Power Surge Wrestling Tag Team Champion(x2 w/ Johnny Vain and Chris Rickter), Power Surge Wrestling Hall of Fame, Primetime Championship Wrestling Cruiserweight Champion(x2), those are the most meaningfull but there are many more I can't recall. Wrestling Style All Round- Technical, Old School and High Risk mixture Finisher Move 1. Star Crusher 2. Detox Finisher Description 1. Front Flipping Fisherman Buster(It can be done) 2. Dragon Clutch Finisher Setup Move Depends on situation Setup Description Depends on situation Other Moves Keeton is smaller than most of his opponents, so he must use his speed and quick thinking to get through his matches. This used to mean by any means neccesarry but recently he's made a commitment to do the right thing and won't cheat unless the opponent has been cheating for much of the match. His stamina is incredible and he isn't very prone to submissions because of his ability to withstand punishment long enough to reach the ropes. His weaknesses are his head from multiple concussions and his right arm which was broken by Razor Blade and put Jake on the shelf for close to a year. Against opponents of smaller or around the same size he will attempt to over power them and ground them with mat wrestling and various suplexes and slams. Larger opponents may still fall victim to the mat wrestling and variety of suplexes it just won't be as pretty. This is when he takes to the air. His finishers, The Star Crusher and Detox are both used equally. The Star Crusher, due to the strength it takes and the flip means it can't be performed on opponents over 250lbs or taller than 6'5'. Detox however has no restrictions. Trademark Moves: Wasted Hopes (Shootingstar Legdrop) Tri-Plex (Series of Tiger Suplexes, over head release on the third if the opponent is light enough.)
