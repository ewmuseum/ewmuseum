ReVolution 249 ---- One Last Time? The camera opens on a wide shot, Vince Howard standing in the center of the ring, surrounded by a packed-to-capacity crowd in the Philips Arena. As the fans in Atlanta buzz, ready for the second stop on PRIME’s farewell tour to begin, Howard raises a microphone to his lips, ready to utter the first words of ReVolution 249, a right usually reserved for Nick Stuart. Howard: Ladies and gentlemen… Those three words are enough to get the crowd making noise. What emerges from behind the curtain doesn’t seem like an icon. A once-great warrior looks more like a broken man, a shell of his former self.
