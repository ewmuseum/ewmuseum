Gabriel Brown Biography Nicknames The Near Future, PRIME's Golden Child (Still used. Shut it, Nova! You're a pothead! ;-) ), Invincible Gabe (by those who obviously have a deathwish) Hometown Pikeville, KY Birthday 1/1/85 Height 5'9' Weight 212 lbs. Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography A long time ago, this was but a man whose sole purpose was to entertain. Invincible Gabe, he was called. Master of the L33T. Owner of the most bizarre set of sideburns since that stupid beard-like thing HHH has nowadays. Those days are dead. From the ashes rises a man who is now true to himself. He gives no quarters and expects no favors. Think of him what you will. He certainly doesn't care what you think. He isn't another in a long list of those who pass themselves off as The Future. He is The Near Future. For his time on high is imminent. ...Oh, and for some reason he seems to tolerate Vangellus Oxios. What a weird boy. Other Feds RPW, WBG, NFW Season II (current) Other Fed Awards RPW World Champ, RPW Tag Champ (x2), WBG International Champion, OSW Tag Team Champion (defunct title, won from Forsaken at RPW's The Kannon Effect.) Wrestling Style The Ultimate In Ass-Kicking Technology. The Human Swiss Army Knife. Any fancy term for 'All-rounder' basically. Think of him as one part Chris Benoit, one part Chris Sabin. Finisher Move Tornado DDT (All variations), 450 Splash, Cattle Mutilation, Legsweep DDT. Finisher Description All his finishers are as such. He's not the type to name moves. Finisher Setup Move Any and all. Setup Description What he said. Other Moves Other Moves: All moves are categorized under different types. Thanks to Dillon for allowing me use of this system which I find pleasing to the human eye. :-P Strikes: Punches, Kicks, Chops, Elbow Smashes, Knees, Rolling Koppou Kick, Dropkicks of all varieties, Dropsault (lands on feet after hitting the move), Enzuigiris of all varieties, Gamengiri, Dragon Whip, Backflip kick in the corner, Dangerous III (Roaring Elbow; tribute to one of his trainers, Max Danger.) Pins: Back Slide, Oklahoma Roll, Small Package, any headscissors/rana variation turned into a pin, Side Cradle Roll (a la Chris Sabin), La Majistral. Aerial: Moonsault variations, Splashes, Elbows, Suicidas, Springboards, what have you. Holds: (Just about everything done here sets up the Cattle Mutilation) Armbar, stepover armbar, hammerlock, keylock, Jujigatame(crucifix armbreaker), Wakigatame (Fujiwara armbar), crossface chickenwing, double chickenwing, Buffalo Sleeper, Rings of Saturn, Stranglehold Gamma(stepover armbar w/ neck submission), Triangle Hold, Mexican Surfboard, Romero Chinlock, Flying Armbar, Dragon Sleeper, Dragon Sleeper w/ leg scissors, Single Leg Crab (both regular and elevated), Chinlocks, Figure-Four Leg Lock, STF, Crucifix Kneebreaker, Goku-Raku Stretch, Back-Mounted Dragon Sleeper. Takedowns: All variants of the Russian Legsweep, Snapmare takedown, arm drag takedown, fireman's carry, waistlock takedown followed by a face lock on the mat, drop toe hold, go-behind takedown, side arm takedown, side headlock takedown, single leg takedown, double leg tackle takedown, headlock takeover, single leg trip, double leg trip, arm wringer takedown, Dragon screw, arm dragon screw, Leg Dragon Screw. Breakers: All variations of the Neckbreaker. A few backbreakers here and there, but only against smaller guys. Slams: STO, Standing Sunset Flip Powerbomb (Amazing Red's Code Red), German Suplex, Tiger Suplex, Back Drop Elbow Driver, Side Effect, Body Slam, Any kind of slam if he can hit it on the opponent. Drops: All variations of the DDT, all variations of the Single Arm DDT, Complete Shot/Flatliner, Rocker Dropper. While all these moves Gabriel can do, he is not limited to just these. He can do virtually anything you can think of, but of course use common sense. He won't be powerbombing Hoyt, Boda, or Azala any time soon and don't make him try it, either or I make with the stabbing. :-P More to come...
