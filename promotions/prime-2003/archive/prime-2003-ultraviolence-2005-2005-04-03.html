UltraViolence 2005 Fleet Center, Boston, Massachusetts ---- Introduction Every PPV needs a flash introduction sequence and Ultra Violence is no different as the latest production package starts to roll, breaking the viewer in with some tarnished black and white footage of various violent acts throughout history. The footage flickers in and out with the void inbetween clips filled by a black screen, which eventually takes over as the footage ceases, although the audio is still live. Deprived of visual stimuli, a disturbing montage of sounds come to the forefront, firing the mind into overdrive trying to identify each one from anguished cries and screams, to the sound of steel scraping against flesh or bone's breaking like twigs. Uneased by the symphony of suffering, it's a shock to the senses as the screen suddenly pulses, throwing up a brief glimpse of some suitable PRIME footage, before fading again. Each flash of film seems to highlight a more violent occurence within the promotion, such as the Dark Age's very first acts of bloodlust against Ignatius Lisieux, to Boda attempting to hang Vampir Nosferatu from the cable looped over a TV camera crane, to Los Diablos throwing Angelo Deville about the parking lot as recently as last week. The pulses quicken before finally the footage comes to an end and the screen seems to travel along the breadth of a large mesh panel, it's color soaked in ultra violet light before we finally stop with the Ultra Violence logo in the center of the screen with one final anguished yelp fading in the background. "BOOM! BANG! BOOM-BA-BOOM!" A variety of violet, silver and white pyro lights up the inside of the darkened Fleet Center as we cut to the LIVE feed in Boston. After about $200 bucks worth goes up in smoke, the lights are raised and the PRIME-ates in attendance begin to go wild. Not wild as in girls at mardi gras wild, but roar with anticipation whilst waving various signs and merchandise for the panning cameras. Ignoring the attention-seekers for a moment, we cast our gaze onto the Ultra Violence entrance set, which is a pretty basic construction compared to some of PRIME's more lavish examples. The PRIME-A-Tron occupies the center, suspended above the entrance with an oval covering, whilst the actual entrance itself ressembles the kinda of space tunnel design as seen on NWA-TNA Impact. Mesh fencing surrounds the sides by the entranceway, as well as a few (presumably empty) dull grey oil drums and stacking crates with the rest of the backdrop consisting of some basic large steel framework panels, tinted purple from the strobe lighting. Heading down the dark grey steel rampway, the camera circles the squared circle, which is decked out with black posts, violet buckle pads and dark grey ropes as well as grey ring mat and purple a apron that has the UV logo and show sponsors plastered all over it. Finally it's time to head over to our announce duo, their table situated on the stage for the third supercard in succession as Nick and Richard prepare their notes for the impending show.
