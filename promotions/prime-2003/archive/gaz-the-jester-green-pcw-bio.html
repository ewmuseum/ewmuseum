Gaz 'The Jester' Green Biography Nicknames The Emerald Menace, The Prince Of Pranks, Original Prankster, King Midas Of Tag... Hometown The City Of Sin (Las Vegas, Nevada) Birthday 23rd July 1979 Height 5' 11' Weight 226 Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography Gaz Green first wrestled under the name of The New Blood in a small-time company. During his tenure there, the company's owner disappeared and his position was usurped by his brother. Furious with his treatment of the wrestlers, The New Blood declared war on him and overwhelmed him. An ultimatum was issued - the company's new owner, Gaz Green, would disappear from the face of the earth. Gaz agreed and gave up his identity, changing his name and fleeing. The New Blood then masqueraded as the owner until he could locate the original owner and restore the company to him. However, upon his departure, he kept the name of Gaz Green, adding the nickname of "The Jester" from his entertaining manner of mocking his opponents in a stand-up style. Upon his acceptance to Hessian's semester of the Primetime Wrestling Academy, Gaz began to emphasize himself with his outfit now being a snappy 3-piece suit, sunglasses and even green hair dye to accentuate his naturally green eyes. It was by chance that he was randomly paired with the other up-and-comer, Nascosto Nerezza, where the two formed a synergetic performance in their first tag-team match against Redemption Song. Upon their graduation, the two took their tag team name as a tribute to show their gratitude to the academy. Their backstage antics had made them known in the locker room as practical jokers, leading to the abbreviation of P.W.A. - Pranks With Attitude. The colour green is associated with envy and Gaz is no exception. Whether it's a championship belt, another wrestler's wife or a position of power, Gaz will try to steal or equal it, having had affairs with Alexander Miyagawa's wife Natalia as well as literally snatching Chris Storm's bride-to-be, Tessa Windsor at the altar. Gaz lives by the phrase "There's no such thing as no such thing", a self-serving motto that implies too much is never enough. While his eyes, hair and outfits are green, women are usually helpless to his silver tongue. Interpret that as you will. Nerezza and Gaz were once part of the WO interpromotional scene up until the sudden implosion it suffered. Working in PCW, OSE and as representatives of the academy that spawned them, they made their way to RPW. However, their creativity was being stifled. The same old faces, no fresh blood, eventually they tried a new territory but found Civil Disobedience waiting for them in PRIME. For a time, the team parted ways, until sheer boredom forced them back to PRIME. Nerezza first infiltrated the company posing as "N", homage to the movie V For Vendetta, where he tested the waters by campaigning for fairness and no-nonsense wrestling. The backlash from the fans choosing excitement over the rules was the perfect excuse he needed. On Rev 124, Nerezza and Gaz marked their return to the ring as official, blindsiding the team of Pajaro and White Tiger. Delivering a bitter and venomous speech about how the fans would get exactly what they asked for, P.W.A. have set their sights on PRIME's tag team championship belts to prove that their time away from the ring has had no ill effect on their skills inside it. Other Feds 5 x PCW Tag Team Champion (w/ Nerezza) - PCW Continental Champion (Last ever) Other Fed Awards voted PTC/ WO Greatest Tag Team Wrestling Style Counter and reversal specialist Finisher Move The Last Laugh Finisher Description Full-Nelson Facebuster Finisher Setup Move The Most Electrifying Move In PRIMEtime! Setup Description The Jester applies an electrical charge to the temple of his opponent courtesy of his notorious hand-buzzer. The shock should stun it's victim long enough for Gaz to apply the opening sequence of his finisher, The Last Laugh (Full-Nelson) Other Moves Submission Moves~ Entrapment: Elevated Boston Crab with The Jester's knee imbedded into the base of his opponents spine. Lion Tamer, not The Walls Of Jericho. Blood Rush: Corner Entangled Dragon-Sleeper. With an opponent straddled on the top turnbuckle facing away from the ring, Gaz will climb alongside and apply a Dragon-Sleeper, bare in mind that both combatants fall backwards into what appears to be a Tree Of Woah predicament. Since this uses the ropes for leverage Gaz cannot score a victory and would need to release the hold at the referee's five count. The Fastest Armbar In The West: I'd love to say this is some variation of the boring armbar in which Gaz dazzles the crowd but in reality it's simply what it says on the tin. It's only claim to fame and thus why it even reaches Gaz Green's movelist is that it once defeated Eddie Mandrake in 0.95 seconds, the quickest victory ever recorded in Primetime Championship Wrestling. Cheating/ Illegal Tactics~ The Most Electifying Move In PRIMEtime!: See 'Set Up Move Description'. Seen Green: Tajiri style green mist that temporarily blinds the opponent. ARMAGEDDON~!: Think William Regal's brass knuckle shot with a slight twist. Gaz turns his hand-buzzer around and connects with a HUGE haymaker that electifies the opponent, the move is called the ARMEGGEDON~! because if it hits, it's the end of the world. This should result in victory. Highflying/ Aerial Moves~ Las Vegas ReVolution: It's the Jester's own interpretation of the Asai Moonsault. With the opponent downed in the centre of the ring, Gaz races across and uses the ropes for leverage, allowing himself to vault backwards and execute what most people refer to as 'The Lionsault'. Whiplash Effect: If both Gaz and his opponent are atop of a turnbuckle still on their vertical bases then The Emerald Menace will vault off executing a swinging neckbreaker. However, if the opponent is groggy in the middle of the ring and Gaz is perched up high, he'll leap off and catch his victim via the throat region with a blockbuster neckbreaker instead. Entrance Music "Feel Good Inc." by Gorillaz Ring Entrances A long cackle breaks out over the speakers as Gaz Green struts out, lowering his shades and giving a grin to the cameras. Rolling his shoulders forward, he then strolls merrily down the ramp to the ring, onto the apron and grips the top rope. In a display of athleticism, he jumps up, lands sitting on the top rope, then bounces off into the ring and bows. Running to the other side of the ring and climbing the turnbuckles, he removes his shades and throws them into the crowd, then repeats his shoulder-rolling pose.
