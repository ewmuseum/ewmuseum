The Educator Biography Hometown Surrey, England Birthday 11/06/1978 Height 6'4' Weight 220 Biography Adam Masters was a former school teacher who was discovered by a man named Harry Shins and trained by Max Thomps. It was Shins idea that Masters would eventually develop into the Kurt Angle or Triple H of England and be the leading name in the BWF (British Wrestling Foundation), however several things changed those plans. First off, Shins was forced out of the BWF group. Second, Masters began hating pro wrestling, particularly when he was asked to lose and began to pine for his school teaching days. Masters hates American wrestling and wrestlers. He calls many American wrestlers 'phonies.' Masters was discovered by Shins in 1995 in a wresting tournament and made his pro debut the following year at the age of 19. He worked as a jobber for a few years and eventually knew he would have to begin whoring himself to an American audience if he was going to make any real money, joining such federations as DWF and iCON. He returned to England amidst a big build-up in April 2000 by pinning Bungle, the British Bear. Masters was, and still is to some degree, tentative against Americans and although he has very good technique, his best matches are often against other British opponents. Masters was a consistent top-level performer until he took Shins advice and joined the EWF (English Wrestling Federation, a new group Shins was forming, to be its top star on April 10, 2002. Masters was given the new group's International title by defeating the late Disciple X in Madison Square Garden, however while on tour, the relations between Shins investors and Shins himself worsened and eventually ended, leaving Masters on tour and being asked to do jobs for several wrestlers far below his level of talent. Masters returned embittered, and tried to turn the EWF (then re-naming it Extreme Wrestling Federation) into the hardest fighting group in the world in order to make sure American showman type wrestlers stayed away. But by the end of 2002, the original EWF had broken up. On December 2nd of that year, Adam Masters had a match with a wrestler name Super Samson that went horribly wrong. Masters threw several intentional kicks to Samsons groin. The referee disqualified both wrestlers because the match was getting way out of control. Masters was fired from the EWF. Samson eventually left the EWF on December 15, 2002 and the promotion folded. Masters returned to the BWF in January 2003. A match against The Extremist in April 2003 resulted in a shoot when both wrestlers refused to cooperate with each other. On June 12, 2003, Masters faced one of the BWF wrestlers he respected, Tony Fudge. During the match, Masters accidentally hit Fudge with a shoot kick to the face causing Fudge to bleed profusely. The match ended in a double knockout. 2003 proved to be Masters final hurrah in the British Wrestling Foundation. On November 19, 2003, Masters teamed with Orlando Kingsley and Nobby Tanks to face Mr Suit, Hero Strings and Rico Lopez. During the match, while Lopez was applying the Scorpion Deathlock on Kingsley, Masters walked in the ring and kicked Lopez in the cheek from the blind side, breaking Lopez' orbital bone. As a result of all this, Masters was suspended and then fired, once again, in December of that year. Now Masters realises that his reputation in the whole of Britain is a wrestler has been ruined, he has had to swallow his pride and once again do exactly what all his morals go against.
