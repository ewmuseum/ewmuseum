Eli VanNess Biography Wrestler Name Eli VanNess Nicknames So Sly Hometown Baltimore, Maryland Birthday 8/8/87 Height 6'4'' Weight 246 lbs Biography Eli and Felix were both Fraternity brothers in Sigma Alpha Epsilon, and were legends on the Loyola Campus Party scene. Neither one wanted to hold down a real job so they went into the business of arranging parties for both their fraternity and others. In the case of parties where freshman were involved, they were liquor runners and usually supplied all alcohol to the underage party-goers. On one fateful night a lit cigarette caused a fellow fraternity house to go up into flames. The cigarette belonged to freshman Chris Daniels, heir to the Jack Daniels fortune. In lieu of facing arson charges he quickly talked to the police about Eli and Felix's lucrative "speakeasy" scheme and was let off the hook. Eli and Felix were held accountable for the house and the fines that went with misdemeanor endangering minors and serving alcohol without a liquor license. Heavily in debt, the boys were expelled from school and faced an uphill battle to pay their dues. Life gave them a break when Eli came across a PRIME add looking for tag team wrestlers, something both had dabbled in at the beginning of college and which Eli had excelled in high school. With not much to lose the boys threw their hats into the ring, hoping to earn a quick dollar.
