Richard: Beautiful Lie on the way, if she lands this one then it’s good night, Irene. Shakur has no idea what’s awaiting him behind spin number 1, and he’s not going to know for a good long while. Stumbling up to his feet in a drunken boxer’s stance, he wildly swings into the air, giving some air molecules a good roughing, but nobody of significance. His body on the other hand might have a slight indication, which would explain his legs going out from underneath him before Cozen can make contact. Nick: SHAKUR DUCKED! Cozen dangling like a wet spaghetti noodle over the side of a counter and Shakur dead asleep, so out of it that not even a tornado would wake him up.
