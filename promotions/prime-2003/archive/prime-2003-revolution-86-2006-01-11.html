ReVolution 86 The Pepsi Center: Albany, New York ---- Whatever Happened To PCW? He Did... We open ReVolution with the grinning face of Tyler Nelson, co-owner of PRIME. He sits comfortably behind a hand-made Amish oak desk, dressed in a tailor-made black suit with a button up white shirt underneath contrasted by a bright red tie. On the wall behind him is a PRIME banner, and underneath that is a shelf containing three items in protective glass cases. They look similar to championship title belts, but are extremely rusted, bent, and broken. Nelson continues with a half-cocked grin as he gazes into the camera, beginning to speak. Tyler: Good evening. In case you've been living under a rock for the past month, or worse yet, losing the Universal Title to Clyde Walkins, I am the new co-owner of PRIME. With that being said, I felt it was necessary for me to bring you a State of PRIME address. A lot has happened in recent weeks, and if those changes are any indication of what the future holds for PRIME, I think we can all agree I will have a VERY good year! Nelson's grin widens as we can faintly hear some boos from inside the arena. Tyler: There are a few things I want to address tonight, so I'll cut right to the chase. First off, PRIME has recently undergone the passing of former co-owner, Hin See. Many of you may see his death as being bad, or untimely, but I think that exactly the opposite is the case. I mean, Hin See did all he could for PRIME, but he'd taken the ship as far as he could. It was time for someone to step in who knew how to get ratings, bring in the top talent, and perhaps most importantly, make the most money. Who better to do that than ME? The verbal pat on the back falls upon deaf ears inside the arena. A quick cut to inside the Pepsi Arena shows that the fans are booing emphatically as they watch on the PRIME-a-tron. Back in the office, Nelson motions over his shoulder to the items on the shelf behind him. Tyler: It's like a renaissance of sorts for me. Owning PRIME now reminds me of the days when I owned PCW, which pwned Sebastian Starr's previous creation JEW and its subsequent incarnations. I even have these nice souvenirs to commemorate my domination, given to me by Stewart Hix as he tried to beg his way back into PCW. The camera zooms in somewhat to reveal that Nelson has indeed encased the nearly destroyed JEW2 Tag Team, United States, and World title belts shipped to him by Stewart Hix almost five years ago. The leather straps look to have been chewed on my some sort of rodent, and the metal on the belts is rusted clean through in some spots, but there is still enough left to identify them. The crowd in the arena continues its chorus of boos. Tyler: Enough about the past, let's talk about PRIME's future. Now that the old man's gone, we can get down to the business of taking PRIME in a new direction....a more profitable direction. The first thing we're going to have to do is put a new face on PRIME, namely it's Universal Champion. I don't know who in the hell even booked Clyde freakin Walkins in a title match in the first place, but I'll be damned if my PRIME has some fool running around with its top prize. We need someone marketable, someone good looking, someone....like me... Nelson flashes a huge grin and winks at the camera. Tyler: ....acting as PRIME's ambassador to the world. Nelson rises from his chair and walks around to the front of the desk, then lifts a leg and sits on the edge of it. The door can hit you in the ass on the way out for all I care. I don't cater to all you people like Hin See did. The camera zooms in somewhat in a rather stern face from Nelson. Tyler: That should just about cover that. Panning back, the arrogant smirk returns to Nelson's visage. To this day some people would still insist that Toshiaki Motoki had a heart as black as the suit he'd just draped over the chair in his expensive Kobe-based luxery apartment. The suit was now rather crumpled, not that it concerned the reinstated co-owner a whole lot. With any luck, he wouldn't be requiring it for another funeral in the near future. Around about this time one year ago, he would have almost savoured Hin See's remains being covered in earth and left to rot. It was only twelve months ago that the Asian Cowboy tore everything away from his former business partner, stabbing him in the back on LIVE television and exiling him. Those actions left Motoki a broken, yet focussed man, toiling away in this very room as he tried to concoct various elaborate schemes that would prise Hin See from power. In the end he settled on purchasing the identity of a little-known wrestler and duped his way into the Dual Halo with the intention of using his victory "wish" to gain full ownership of the company. In practice, Quartz "The Digital Luchador" didn't survive for long in the structure his mind had conceived nearly two years earlier. The mere fact that he was willing to put himself at such physical risk, illustrated his desperate plight. Yes, twelve months ago Toshiaki Motoki would have stood by the side of his mentors grave with a smile as bright and glorious as the rising sun itself. That however, wasn't the case now. In truth, the range of emotions he experienced as Hin See's meagre, charred remains were condemned to the earth held no pleasure for him. He doubted if the police's forensic team could even distinguish the pieces of Hin See from the rest of the wreckage and as an act of either compassion or convenience, simply gave his son a momento to bury. As the phrase goes, a lot of water had passed underneath the bridge since then. For one, he'd saved the life of Hin See's son... or at least, that's what both the Asian Cowboy and Taun Pham believed. The NetBIOS chip in Pham's head was basically dormant at this point in time. Whatever GlobeTech's controversial unit could do to influence the co-owner, it was debateable that it would have the abilty to override the pure grief that Pham was experiencing. Originally Motoki had intended to use the chip to destroy Pham and his father in due course, eroding their relationship, before envoking his full retribution for the wrongs carried out against him all those months ago. Even though he wanted them to suffer and even though he was yet to actually finalise what price the pair deserved to pay, the act of extinguishing a life was something that even Motoki believed he was not capable of. Somebody else however, had decided to take that liberty and although they might not have known it, ended Motoki's quest for vengeance in the process. Walking across main living quarters of his apartment, the co-owner moved a handful of files from the table in front of him. He needed the space for whatever meal of convenience his freezer still contained. The files themselves related to PRIME, something that Motoki was guilty of letting slip to the back of his mind for periods of his unexpected stay in Japan. He knew there was an event to unfold in his absence and he was also well aware that leaving Blaine Blair with the instructions to moderate any decisions made by Tyler Nelson, was most likely a futile act. If the worst came to the worst, he'd simply have to deal with the mess that greeted him when he returned to the states. He was also well aware that however decided to take Hin See's life, remained undiscovered. That meant that the person who had shattered his vow of revenge was still walking amongst them... And that in turn meant, Toshiaki Motoki intended to make said individual suffer the consequences had been reserved for Hin See and his son. His fifteen minutes in the spotlight, however, had somehow remained behind the curtain. It was like it had all happened so fast, that no one had even seen it.
