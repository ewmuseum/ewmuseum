Mega Job Biography Tag Team Name Mega Job Fed Titles They were totally part of the Blue Rogues entourage when they were tag champs. Does that count? Fed Awards None. Biography They're Mega Job. What, you want more? Other Feds IWO, IML, DWF, the Asylum, fWo, fWEo, NFW, and moonlit in other promotions like 21W, Action! Wrestling, ACW, and the Squared Circle Other Fed Titles IML World Tag Titles, the Asylum Team Titles, the Asylum UK Title (Janito) Other Fed Awards None. Wrestling Style Basic brawling with comedy Finisher Name Unnamed Finisher Description Eye of the Hurricane by Beef, followed by the Five Tequila Frog Splash by Janito Setup Move None. Setup Move Description None. Special Moves See moveset. Move Set You can look at Beef's and Janito's bios for moveset stuff, though I note that they're... y'know. Idiots. --- For the most part, Mega Job are comedy wrestlers. They act like idiots because they are idiots, and they frequently get the crap kicked out of them for being idiots. They've been used as jobbers in the past, and are quite aware of their use in this regard. That's not to say that they can't fight back on occasion. Beef is a somewhat competent brawler and Janito is a somewhat competent high flyer. If they're not trying to be jobbers, then they can at least look like underdogs against their opponents. Janito is almost always the punching bag, both in and out of the ring. He can take an absolute beating, but if he's motivated enough to do so, he can survive long enough to make the hot tag to Beef. Beef, when hot tagged, is usually a house of fire. As he is based partially on Hulk Hogan, he may occasionally "hulk up", ignore the opponent's offense, and then come back with a big boot and leg drop. This never gets the pin unless there was some sort of interference between the boot and the leg drop. Also, Beef is only 5'11" and still a cruiserweight, so Beef tends to get obliterated trying to do this on somebody the size of, say, Logic. Mega Job usually, if they're actually going to win, finish by a fluke rollup. Occasionally, though, they may use a combination of their singles signature moves as a finish. Entrance Music 'Natural Born Killaz' by Dr. Dre and Ice Cube Ring Entrances Alright. I'm going to level with you. They're Mega Job. They're a ridiculous virgin, a Hispanic who speaks no Hispanic, and a midget with a ball-busting lariat. If you can't come up with an entrance based solely off of this information, then I've got no hope for you. This means YOU, Chris.
