Ian Nackedy Biography Wrestler Name Ian Nackedy Nicknames “The SuperJerk,” “The Random Renegade,” “The King of Crappy Nicknames” Hometown New Orleans, LA Birthday 9/16/1982 Height 6'0 Weight 209 lbs Biography Ian Nackedy was born somewhere and did some stuff before one day wanting to wrestle like his big brother in the NWC. While he was by no means good at it, he eventually got better and started winning matches with his friend and adopted brother Gildenstern (whose last name was lost in Hurricane Andrew). Using the innovative “Gimmick-Of-The-Week,” in which the two would come out every week dressed as something different, the two won many matches and owned tons of noobs and won the NWC World Heavyweight Titles, holding them longer than anyone ever did or will (because the NWC died in a horrible grease fire accident). Ian Nackedy was content to live in the past and rub his replica belts every day without needing any present glory…until he ran out of money. Now he’s trying to wrestle again.
