Stephen Devereaux Biography Nicknames None Hometown Chicago Birthday 1984 Height 6 Feet Weight 220 Biography Some say Stephen was born ready to be a cop. Even after his mother was gunned down in the line of duty Stephen made it his mission to join the force, and he was welcomed with open arms into his new family. Throughout Stephens life in which he maintained exceptional grades and was heavily involved in sports, Stephen had another calling, a more personal calling. Stephen knew he had a brother, this much his mother had told him, Stephen's ambition in life aside from putting on the uniform was finding his brother. Stephen never thought his two goals would meet abruptly one cold night during a drug bust, When Stephen chased down a rather fast perp and tackled him to the ground he found his own eyes staring back at him! Imagine his surprise when he learned his brother was a pirvate detective operating not far from Stephen! Stephen came to discover however that his brother had a dark past and went about serving justice in his own way that didn't always sit well with the younger Devereaux brother who liked to play it by the book. Despite this the brothers quickly developed their own kind of bond, and when they discussed a career in wrestling to keep themselves in top shape and share a common interest Stephen managed to use one of his connections to get them into PRIME.
