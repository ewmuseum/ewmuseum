Diego Delgado Biography Nicknames The Latin Disciple, King Diego, El Delgado, Latin King of Kings, ALKD, Militainment Man. Hometown Born in San Juan, Puerto Rico... resides in Chicago, Illinois. Birthday 07/26/1982 Height 6'2 Weight 245 lbs. Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Bio coming soon... Match History: PRIME: 1-3-1 ReV 110: Diego Delgado def. Delgado Other Feds None Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style Technical/Brawler Finisher Move Coronation Finisher Description A delayed butterfly suplex, into a spinning implant DDT. Finisher Setup Move The Crown Setup Description A series of punches to the side of the head, followed by a spinning kick to the stomach doubling the opponent over. Other Moves Strikes: Stiff-arm Clothesline Spear Knife-Edge Chops Superkick Spinning Haymaker -Diego will focus his strikes on the opponents legs at the beginning of the match, but once he gains momentum he will start to focus on the head and neck region. Submission: Anklelock Sharpshooter Basic Sleeper Chokehold (Usually to buy himself time.) Arm-Bar Crossface Elevated Single-Leg Boston Crab -He will primarily use submissions as a way to slow down his opponent and wear them down, not to try and make them submit. Impact Moves: Reverse DDT Samoan Drop Diamond Cutter Elevated Fireman's Carry Running Powerslam Neckbreaker Sit-out Powerbomb Variety of Spinebusters Fisherman's Suplex STO Belly-to-Belly Suplex Atomic Drop -Has a wide array of Suplex moves to his name, would prefer to slow the match down and hit high impact moves. Trademark Moves: 1. Cannonized [Anklelock] 2. Crossin' the Border [Running Powerslam] Strategy: -Diego likes to slow the match down with an array of high impact moves and submission holds. Has been trained in the technical style of wrestling and likes to avoid matches where the opponent will try to 'out-quick' him. He will attempt to play to the crowd at all times, although he will never take to the turnbuckles. Entrance Music 'Enemies' by Wayne Wonder Ring Entrances The slow tempo bass of 'Enemies' by Wayne Wonder pounds through the sound system, before quickly cutting into the rhythm of the song. As Diego makes his way onto the stage, surrounded by a sheet of sparks falling from the entrance way, the flag of Puerto Rico sits majestically on the Wal*Tron. Vince Howard: Now coming to the ring, hailing from San Juan, Puerto Rico... he is the Latin Disciple and the self proclaimed Latin King of Kings... DIEEEEEGO DELGAAADOOO. Sporting the two trademark yellow bandanas covering nearly all of his face, Diego proceeds towards the ring, slapping the hands of a few fans leaning over the railing on the way to the ring. He hops up into the ring, and onto the nearest turnbuckle as he crosses both his arms across his chest and extends his fingers giving his usual sign.
