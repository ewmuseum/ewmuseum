Mark Blackwolf Biography Nicknames 'The Enforcer' Hometown Boston, Massachusetts Birthday 5th December 1977 Height 6'11'' Weight 400 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography PRIME Debut Match - Tropical Turmoil 2005 Tom Walczak and Mark Blackwolf b. Dave Gibson and Kyle Lamen. ReVolution 75 Hoyt Williams and Mark Blackwolf b. Tom Walczak and Christopher (More to be added when it happens....) Wrestling Style Powerhouse/Brawler (Look at Move Set for Entrance) Finisher Move Death From Above Finisher Description An inverted powerbomb: Blackwolf sets his opponent up for a powerbomb the normal way, only instead of dropping his opponent on their back he drops them on their face. Finisher Setup Move Big Boot Setup Description Doesn't need describing! Other Moves Loads of variations of suplexs. Nearly always uses Power style moves, for example: Body Press Slams, DDT's, Powerbombs, etc. Never climbs to the top rope (It would probably take his fat ass 10 minutes to get up there). Will use submissions when the time is right, but never uses them in the early goings of a match. Blackwolf once had a spell in the Ultimate Fighting Championship and due to this he uses a lot of strikes and brawling type moves, he lets everybody know that the rules are to be obeyed, but for some reason it doesn't seem to count when it is him breaking them, very hypercritical.
