ReVolution 79 ---- Being a Smart Mark If their movements summed up Karina Wolfenden and Amy Campbell any more perfectly, someone would surely have to shed a tear. Heading to the same destinations, only in opposite directions, and for an equally opposing reason. Not even having had time to dump off her bags, Amy was locked on course, only having one person on her mind all through the ride to the Kemper Arena. Having even arrived early - well, on time, so early for her - just to make sure her words were heard, the K-Wolf too sought out the man that both women so desired to see. And which lucky fella was looking like fulfilling the horny net-geek fanboy's dream of being double-teamed by the K-Wolf and the Red Raver? Not who you'd think... "Mark Blackwolf?" Karina delivers the words as much as a statement than a question, leaving the targeted employee looking blankly back at her. Not following up, Kari simply guides the puppet-strings of his brain with a tilting of the head and narrowing of feline eyes. Employee: Oh, yeah, he's just down there... saw him about half a minute ago. Another subtle adjustment tries to prise the extra information she needs from the worker, before Kari gives up with a sigh, exclaimated further by hands thumping down into pockets. Karina Wolfenden: And... he looks like? The previously blank look on the employees face turns into bemusement at her question... especially since once people see Mark Blackwolf, they tend not to forget him. Generic, blend-into-the-background type he most certainly is not. Employee: ...you, er, don't know what he lo- A typically Karina roll of the eyes precedes the cutting off of the young man's words. Karina Wolfenden: There are wayyy too many people around here for me to care to remember. People want me to know their names, get a sticker and a marker, then slap it on your forehead. I'm not asking for a charcoal portrait, the basics. After taking a few moments to diplomatically think how best to describe the Enforcer, the employee then takes a stab at it. Employee: Well, he's about 7 feet tall, probably over 400 pounds... 100 of it in his gut. About as furry as a mohair carpet, tattoos everywhere, long black hair, red eyes... oh, and purple lips. There's a silence as Kari's head drops just a little, tempted to slump into her hands as she strides off, information provided.. Karina Wolfenden: Sometimes I really hate wrestling... As the K-Wolf strolls the corridor, voices begin to drift around the corner, slowing her pace and stealing her ear. "Listen up, it's pretty simple..." Amy Campbell. No mistaking the voice, the accent, the inflection, the delivery. "At the moment, come King of Kings, you're looking like kicking your heels on the sidelines, handing out water and hot towels to Hoyt. Is that what you really wanted coming into PRIME, to be on a few TV shows and skip the big paydays, or worse... be shoved into some filler match people will remember less than how salty their popcorn was?" The words fire off like a Tommy-Gun, pauses after questions filled before an answer could even be formulated. "But... if Cain wasn't tied up with the Jewel in the Crown tourney after tonight, that's your PPV match in the bag right there." Stretching, curious fingers turns to fists as she hears the Red Raver's whisperings in the ear of Mark Blackwolf, and the hint of an affirmative grunt that followed. "Now, I'm not saying that Karina hasn't got things covered on the match front, but she's got a habit of forgetting that muscles don't stretch as far as the horizon, or twist more than a few times. So if she does crash and burn out there, I'm sure the referee could find a lil' something to be distracted by, allowing you to... how shall we put it... 'book' your PPV slot?" Through the plaster, Karina could almost feel the beaming smile from Amy that she'd put up to compliment to niceties... and that would probably sour the moment Mark left her sight. "Well, Aimz... I think you might be right." Kari could hear the enamoured tone to his voice, leaving her dropping back against the wall, a smile conflicted with the shake of the head, half impressed at how tightly Aimz had managed to twist the Enforcer around her middle finger. "It tends to happen more than some people think. Now, I have to go and make sure I'm their waiting for ol' Frostybox at King of Kings. So, later." As soon as Amy turns the corner, the false exterior is cleansed in a heartbeat, shook loose by a shudder. For a moment, she halts her step, getting an odd feeling tapping her shoulder, as if someone was creeping around behind her. A glance back shows that not even Blackwolf is in her wake, leaving Aimz to head off up the corridor, grumbling about the things she lowers herself to do in the wrestling business. 10 seconds or so later, Blackwolf's feet stamp around the corner, his mammoth bulk almost filling the corridor. "It's time to be smart, Mark..." Mark Blackwolf's red eyes shoot open as his colossal frame spins sharply to face the K-Wolf, who'd unfurled herself from overhead to touch down behind him. Mark Blackwolf: Where th-... what th-... who t- Rather than waiting for him to get a whole sentence out, Kari splices her own words into his. Because, Monkeyboy, if I see anyone but a ring announcer, a timekeeper, and maybe those ker-RAZY Spanish guys they give a table to down at ringside tonight when I fight Cain... well... Karina Wolfenden: Yes, you're bigger than my fridge, congratulations. Now, I'm guessing you could probably punch through that wall, let alone my face, but...
