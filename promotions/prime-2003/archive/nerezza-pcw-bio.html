Nerezza Biography Nicknames Rezz, The Human Horror Film Hometown London, UK Birthday 29th May Height 6'7 Weight 251 Fed Titles What's a title? Fed Awards ...huh? Biography With the parting of P.W.A. So many years ago, Nerezza found himself at a loss. Without his partner and inspiration, Nerezza tried to reinvent himself several times. Burying himself under various masks and costumes to try and change his persona, none lasted long and Nerezza couldn't resist unmasking himself. The russian BDSM sadist, Bastion Cossack was no more than an anagram of "Nascosto Is Back". The "freedom fighter", N, who stood for fair play and justice in matches. As his career began to take a downward turn, Nerezza realised that the core of the problem wasn't that Nerezza was the persona that resurfaced but that everything else had become a persona and Nerezza had taken over as the face beneath the mask. Nerezza stepped back and realised for a moment that he had allowed his life to be consumed by the character. The pigmentation treatment to turn his skin grey. The plastic surgery to alter his face. The dental work that gave him his shark-smiled mouth. With no alternatice but to rid himself of the character, "Rezz" walks a razor's edge between reassuming the moniker to entertain the fans that refuse to let him go and being swallowed up by a figment of his own imagination once more. Other Feds PCW Tag Team Champion x 5 (with Gaz Green as P.W.A) Other Fed Awards Wall Dating agency manager of the year 2003-2004, making more people kiss brick than anyone else. Wrestling Style Chain-wrestler, submissionist, corner specialist Finisher Move Ichigeki Hissatsu '09 (One-shot Fatal Technique '09) Finisher Description With the opponent doubled over, Nerezza wraps his arms around their chest, lifts them so they face up then swiftly drops down with his legs outstretched backwards, drilling the neck and shoulders into the mat. Finisher Setup Move Shortarm Knee Setup Description Nerezza begins an Irish Whip, but holds onto his opponent's arm and drags them back towards him and buries his knee into their abdominal region Special Moves See below Other Moves From Bad To Worse - With the opponent down, Nerezza will pick up an ankle, turn his back on his opponent and lean forwards, whipping the opponent over his head and into the mat face first. The Lecter Lecture - With the opponent sitting, Nerezza stands behind them and reaches with his right arm to take their left and pull it across their own neck. Then with his left arm, he pulls their right arm and drags it into their mouth, then applies pressure. If the opponent will not open their mouth, Nerezza will 'encourage' them with several sharp kicks to the lower back and butt. Dragon Sleeper Arm Trap Neckbreaker (AKA Regal Cutter) Elevated DDT with the opponent sitting on the turnbuckle Nerezza is at his most dangerous when an opponent is in the corner, using every move he can to keep them there - elbows, knees, bearhugs using the corner post itself, shoulder rams, you name it. Another thing Nerezza is fond of is chaining moves. He will go from a Back Drop into a German Suplex to an Electric Chair Drop without allowing his opponent to escape or recover. Entrance Music Basement Jaxx - Song Link http://www.goear.com/listenwin.php?v=4b33b93 Ring Entrances The opening words of "WHEEEERE'S YOUR HEEEAAD AT?" strike the arena and Nerezza crawls out onto the ramp on all fours, hiding his face behind his hair. Pressing his body low to the ground, he does a few pushups and then leaps to his feet. With his arms raised up, he stands there shakes his head as the adrenaline begins to build inside him. Emitting a loud shriek, Nerezza sprints to the ring and slides under the bottom rope, back onto all fours. Crawling to the corner, he climbs the turnbuckles and poses with his arms raised again, completing his ritual.
