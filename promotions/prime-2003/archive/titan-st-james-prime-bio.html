Titan St. James Biography Nicknames Shadow Mountain Hometown Jerusalem, Israel Birthday 2/1/1976 Height 7'1 Weight 395 Biography As a result of the attempts to create luxury housing along the Gaza Strip, a pair of well-to-do Americans took up Israeli citizenship and join the financial venture. Unfortunately, the result was a collection of posh apartments that couldn’t be given away, and thus, the St. James’s had to almost start over. Their son came up, born in a Tel Aviv hospital, never knowing life outside of the Middle East. He watched as the world exploded around him, day by day, and strove to maintain order in his own life. Always a large child, he played whatever sports he could, using his strength to every advantage possible. Every person is required to join the Israeli Military, or the IDF. This is where the young man earned his nickname, which has now come to be pretty much his real name through consistant use and the Titan came to be. Titan had hit his full height at 7’1", and was unsuitable for front line action and was instead recruited into the MPs, where he was meant to keep order within the military. Titan learned there not how to kill, but how to restrain and interrogate, giving his huge size even more pluses as he learned to us intimidation and a surprising prowess in restraining holds. During his tenure in the military, he also became an instructor in Krav Maga, the unarmed combat style created by and for the IDF. Titan learned principles of efficiency, directiveness and practicality to every facet of his life, especially the physical aspects. Titan opted for an extended stay in the MP’s until, finally, he decided to leave the IDF and pursue ventures elsewhere. But after maintaining order in the military for so long, where could he go? His first trip to the United States came not much later. It was his decision to continue a life of law enforcement, but for him, the best way to continue his life was to work with the FBI. Part of his training into the new realm of the FBI involved grappling training, which was meant more to subdue a subject that Krav Maga's aim of quick and destructive incapacitation. It was here that Titan learned how to wrestle in a manner prominently seen in his ringwork. After being assigned to protect Vangellus Oxios, Scott Matthews and Matthews' family and fiance, the DA assigned to the case they were under protection for was shot. Titan took the initiative and sent the entire group to Japan for further protection. Feeling a sense of obligation, Titan has moved onto the PRIME roster to fill the void left when he sent two of its members away. Wrestling Style bigman with simple twist Finisher Move Shadow Mountain Bomb, Titan's Hammer Finisher Description Shadow Mountain Bomb Finisher Setup Move See bio Special Moves Peace Maker, IMI, Joshua's Trumpet Other Moves Titan prefers more simple, effective moves. He has, in his repitoire, nothing as complex as a powerbomb. In his mind, too much can go wrong, as often does with wily opponents. Lifting, dropping and throwing are his bread and butter. Moves such as uranages, suplexes and slams are moves you’ll see out of this big man. Powerbombs, he feels, are contrived and entirely too complicated. You will see Titan throw attacks, but nothing complicated; Krav Maga taught Titan how to punch, kick, headbutt and elbow, and rarely will it take more than one of any of those to stun an opponent or even knock them flat. His grappling training from the FBI taught him throws to full exploit his size and strength. Moveset (including, but not limited to) -vertical suplex -belly to belly suplex -belly to back suplex -belly to back slam -one-handed bodyslam -hammerlock -choke lift -sidewalk slam -spinebuster -back body drop -fireman’s carry -powerslam -snake eyes -bearhug slam Signature Moves: Peace Maker -Imagine a uranage (Rock Bottom) from a man that’s 7’1. Titan won’t fall all the way with the move, instead dropping to one knee at the conclusion of the move. Variants on this move include (but are not limited to): Short arm, catching a flying opponent, or off of an irish whip. IMI -Yes, Titan is a big man grappler. He does use typically big man moves, with some exceptions, and this is NOT an exception. Titan’s big hand will grab the throat of the opponent, hoist high and proceed to slam the man straight down with a fearsome, "Better-than-Kane’s" chokeslam. It has been occasioned to see a double IMI, one man in each hand, which, without a doubt, is quite impressive. Joshua's Trumpet -Named for the tactics that fell Jericho, Titan took a move that gained him a one shot victory against Silvio Fiore into a signature of his. Sure, a superkick from a seven foot giant is going to be easy to avoid, but if you don't... Finisher: Shadow Mountain Bomb Titan wasn't the only nickname given to the big man in his time in the military. In fact, as part of being in the MPs, his intimidation and psychological training dubbed him another name, used as his code designation during more sensitive periods: Shadow Mountain. Titan recognized in his wrestling training that powerbombs were effective, yet felt the standard powerbomb (or throwing piledriver as purists would say) was entirely too contrived. Instead, he adapted the Rydeen Bomb, known to D-Lo Brown fans as the Sky High, and dubbed it the Shadow Mountain Bomb. To go from a standing position to eight feet in the air, supported only by the huge man's grip on your ribcage is bad enough. However, when he sits down and flattens you out in powerbomb style, it's even worse. Super Finisher: Titan’s Hammer -In a departure from most big men, Titan’s big move is one that, in truth, is starkly simple. Known in Japan as the Emerald Fusion, Titan will hoist his opponent on his shoulder, facedown, and then drop the man, neckfirst, to the mat. Titan has the ability to hoist anyone he’s ever met into this position. Should the opponent be small, the height of the drop will be the significant factor, but if the opponent is large, then the person’s own mass is what will put him out. Should he have multiple opponents who are small enough, a double Titan’s Hammer is not out of the question (best example being a pair of crusiers that tried moonsault bodyblocks and ended up being caught on Titan’s broad shoulders). Titan St. James MWA Basic Notes: -Titan is a big man, easily considered a giant. Remember when Paul Wight was in WCW as the Giant? Remember when he first got there? That's about the body type we're thinking here. He's in a habit of keeping in shape. -Titan is massively strong. He can be picked up, but he can pick up about anybody, too. Style: -Titan is mainly a striker. He'll swing big fists, kick hard, really club with those forearms, throw knees and you'll see him headbutt as well. For him, hitting the opponent is the primary means of offense, while doing moves is really secondary. Early match offense: -Titan will start just pummeling the crap out of the guy. You'll see him pitch his opponent in the corner and start laying in with the knees and forearms, possibly an irish whip into a big lariat and so on. But early in the match, the only moves you're going to see are simple ones. Back body drops, hiptosses and such. Midmatch offense: -At this point, Titan starts in with harder offense. He'll use big boots and stiff elbows when the opponents come off the ropes. It's at this point you'll see him break out other moves. Vertical suplex, backdrop suplex, sidewalk slams and so on. Late match offense: -Here's where Titan is probably pissed and wants to hurt the little sucker. Spinebusters, flapjacks, gorilla presses and powerslams will come up. He'll also really sling the guy into the turnbuckles and fling them like ragdolls. It's at this time in the match that his trademark moves will come out as well. Finishing notes: -Titan usually finishes folks off with the Shadow Mountain Bomb. Bear in mind, it's at least a seven foot high powerbomb, usually higher. He will only go to the Titan's Hammer when things are dire and, as his uberfinish, isn't kicked out of. Entrance Music "Shadows" by 12 Stones
