Nick: Ward is setting up for something big right here and the floor is shaking as the fans go wild!! Richard: I can barely hear myself Nick! The downfall comes in three parts. First, Hessian removes the shirt from his face and tosses it aside, his face turned down into a sour grimace as thick ropey strands of congealed blood smear his face. As he wipes it from his face Ward strikes out, delivering a kick to the back of Hessian's knee and dropping the giant to both knees. Next comes the sleeper hold which he easily applies on the kneeling giant. Nick: Downfall! He's setting up for his finisher! Hessian is done for! Richard: We're gonna have a three-time Universal Champion! The Inhuman Being puts everything into the most important sleeper hold of his career. The throbbing pain in his head and his dulled senses are momentarily heightened by a mighty kick of adrenaline, and pulling back on the submission Ward forces Hessian up to his feet for a second before reaching down deep and roaring out as the alpha male as he executes the neckbreaker that drills the champion hard against the canvas. Nick: DOWNFALL! GO FOR THE PIN!!
