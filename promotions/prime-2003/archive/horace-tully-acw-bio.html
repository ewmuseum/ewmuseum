Horace Tully Biography Nicknames 'The Adamant'; 'Reverend'; 'Mr. Wrestling'; 'Heart of Burning' (picked up in Japan recently) Hometown Dover, Delaware Birthday July 18, 1978 Height 6'1 Weight 221 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Horace Tully is the right-hand man of the infamous Bruce 'Violence Jack' Shanahan, diabolical mastermind of the Sect of Black Wisdom. Many believe he is currently being groomed by VJ as his successor in the wrestling world come the day of his inevitable retirement, and if Tully's in-ring skill is any indication, Violence Jack has chosen his man wisely. Whereas VJ dominates all around him with his dark persona and stamina and Ethan Knight with the flashy, brash attitude of an outspoken upstart, Tully is the consummate wrestler. He's been praised as one of the most overlooked up-and-comers by many of the most trusted judges of talent in the business and this acclaim hasn't gone unnoticed by Tully himself. He was the one to dub himself 'Mr Wrestling' and intends on using PRIME as a platform to prove he's not full of hot air. Other Feds RPW; tSC; ACW Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style Tully shows a variety of wrestling disciplines in his ringwork. Most notably is his pure technical wrestling ability, chaining together moves with ease and fluidity (especially in the early stages of a contest). He can also support his groundwork with any number of stiff strikes, notably a barrage of forearms and elbows. Tully's matches tend to start with back-and-forth chain-wrestling exchanges in the feeling-out stages, wearing down the opposition and gradually progressing to more high impact maneuvers as the bout continues. He tends to win with one out of his impressive array of submissions, but he has won many a match with his ultra-stiff Lariat and The Horror Out Of Dover maneuvers. His style is mega-stiff with the flashy Dragon Gate style present in many of his high-impact moves. Finisher Move The Everlasting; Anaconda Vice; Unhallowed Finisher Description The Everlasting -- Tully maneuvers behind his opponent and locks both arms in a double chickenwing, as if to execute a Tiger suplex. He instead instead forces his opponent facedown on the mat, and then flips over in a bridging full nelson submission. Variety of takedowns, armlocks, leglocks and submissions. Signature moves Shinjuku Explosion -- Newest maneuver in his arsenal picked up on a recent Japanese tour. Double underhook lift into a powerbomb, ending in a sitout facebuster. Ending is much like his Demon-Slayer. Demon-Slayer -- Tully lifts his opponent into a vertical suplex, corkscrews around, and sits-out, dropping his opponent face and chest-first onto the mat. It Came From Yuggoth -- Crossed-arms Northern Lights Bomb. Lariat -- Aggressive, attacking clothesline in the vein of a Stan Hansen, Samoa Joe or Homicide, and the type that has all the potential in the world to end a match in the late stages and even KO the recipient. Before using this move Tully will telegraph it by removing the elbow pad on the arm he's going to use before hitting the ropes and throwing every bit of force he has into it. Out of all his impact moves, this one sees the most use as an effective finisher. The Horror Out of Dover -- Pumphandle lift into an inverted Michinoku Driver-style faceplant, or even headdrop in semi-rare situations. Puts opponents away nicely in long matches. The headdrop version doesn't happen nearly as often as the standard but is more-than-effective match-ender when its pulled out. Unholy Roller -- Tully grabs the opponent in a rear waistlock, runs toward the far corner, and rolls them backwards. Instead of stopping in a rollup, he continues to roll through and uses the momentum to deliver a high-impact German suplex. He'll hold the bridge for the pin about 25% of the time. Other notes Tully loves to stand and exchange strikes. It's the meat-and-potatoes of his offense. His more spirited exchanges are like something straight out of NOAH or New Japan and exchanges with anyone of similar in-ring mentality result in some brutal mid-ring salvos. A side effect of this is that his matches tend to end with himself, his opponent, or both leaving the ring afterward with bloody noses, busted lips, etc. and virtually blanketed in ice-packs when they're settled in the back. He trained and wrestled extensively overseas and brings that same hard-hitting discipline into the PRIME forum. Most of his matches are battles of attrition involving long striking sequences. When not ruining the complexions of the poor saps he's assigned to wrestle, he finds a body part and focuses on it. And by focuses on it, I mean he relentlessly beats on it until said limb/area is dust. His usual focus is the shoulders and tricep region in preparation for The Everlasting or Anaconda Vice. Doesn't target the neck too often, but those who receive that treatment are usually on his hit list and can expect a few of his more devastating impact moves later. When it's business as usual, he goes after the arm/shoulder or leg/knee. Entrance Music Slayer -- 'Dead Skin Mask'
