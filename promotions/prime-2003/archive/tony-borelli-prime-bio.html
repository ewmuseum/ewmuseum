Other Moves Borelli is a technical assassin in the ring hence him earning the name, "The Technical Assassin," clever huh? Having an amateur wrestling background he incorporates a lot of chain wrestling and holds into his technique. He's a submission specialist and works on the opponents legs and ankle before going for the quick kill, his many submissions.
