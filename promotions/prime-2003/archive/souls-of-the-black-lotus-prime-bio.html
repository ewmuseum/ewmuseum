Souls Of The Black Lotus Biography Fed Titles none as of yet Fed Awards none as of yet Biography Both brothers were born in the Shao-Lin Temple on Mt. Kidake, Japan. As children they excelled in the ways of the Black Lotus, which was instructed by only the strongest moncs. They were instructed specially until they were 18 years old and were told the real truth behind their lives. They were told that they were sons of a great Yakuza warlord. And that at ages 19 and 17 they were to be sent into their fathers service as street warriors. Reluctantly, the brothers said goodbye to all they had ever known to aid their fathers cause. For several years they served their father. However, one faithful day, when their father sent them on a mission, when they found a scroll written in some ancient markings. The two brothers knowing only one place who might know these markings they took the scroll to the Shao Lin Temple where they spent their childhood. After many hours of studying the scroll, the elder monc, had found out the true meaning of the scroll. And traced it's origin to the creators of the Forgotten Arts. The scroll told of two warriors who were destined to be the bringers of the Black Lotus to the western world. After weeks of studying and researching the eldest and wisest monc, had decided that the Nakazura brothers were the two brother warriors destined to bring the Arts of the Black Lotus to Western civilization. He contacted the brothers and their father. And with some convincing the Nakazura's father allowed two of his best street warriors to go into final preparation for their journey. The two brothers spent months and months of final preparation for their journey into the North American wrestling world. Where they plan to bring the power of Shao Lin and Forgotten Arts into Western Culture. Now after a devastating return home to find their father dead and his empire destroyed they returned to GW. To turn over a new leaf, and a quest for vengeance. Soon afterwards, the two brothers found out who truly was behind the death of their father. None other then Upper Echelon member, Maverick Reno. A key rival to the tag team, soon upon their revelation. Reno struck again. In a blood bath that ensued outside the wrestling ring, and led to Reno's immediate departure from Global Wrestling, Maverick killed the younger of the two brothers; Jushin Nakazura. Ichiro was devestated and heartbroken, despite his thirst for Reno's blood, he was never again cleared to return to a Global Wrestling ring. He returned home to Japan. Another place where he had nothing, he went about his business as a normal citizen. No killing, no debt collection, no illegal activities. It took several months, but the rise of violence in the street and the buzz of the city went down, other Yakuza bosses realized that Ichiro was done with his former life. He was a man of peace. One day during a Kickboxing tournament, Ichiro discovered his new partner. Hiatsu Myagiwa, the man was similar in build to his younger brother and former partner, and Ichiro sensed a strong inner strength inside the young man, a certain Zen. Ichiro approached the man, who although a mute, sensed a strong friendship in Ichiro's words, and to this day, Ichiro is the only one who he has ever confided words to. Ichiro trained Hiatsu to be his new partner, now with a new partner and protege, he has the tools necessary to return to the United States. To show the strength of his ancient religion...
