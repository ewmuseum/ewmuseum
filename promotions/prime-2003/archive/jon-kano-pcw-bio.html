Jon Kano Biography Nicknames 'The Man who Rocketh the Mosteth' 'The REAL Legend' Hometown Las Vegas, Nevada Birthday February 19, 1976 Height 6'10' Weight 315 Biography Jonathan Marcus Kano was born at Our Lady of the Worthless Miracle Hospital in Las Vegas, Nevada, on February 19, 1976, to Jennifer and Marcus Kano. He was their second and final child. Their first son, Ken, was two years older that Jon. Jon and Ken were as close as two brothers could be. They both excelled in high school sports, with Ken lettering in basketball, baseball, and track. Jon lettered in baseball, basketball, and football. Following graduation, Ken enlisted in the Army as a demolition specialist, Jon followed suit two years later. Then it happened, Ken was killed in a freak accident involving a supposed dud mine. Jon was devastated, his world was over. The only person who could talk to Jon and get through to him was his lifelong love, Kristin. After Ken's death, Jon was discharged from the Army and began to look for work. He decided to dedicate himself to his brother's true love, professional wrestling. The rest as they say is history. Jon Kano founded Kano Enterprises, Inc., a Las Vegas-based real estate firm, in 1997, and has since made millions and millions of dollars off of this business. He wrestles now, much to the dismay of his business partners, friends, and family. Jon Kano was made in PCW. He battled everyone who was anyone in PCW. He beat many of them. He had his ups and downs in PCW but was the face of the company for nearly the entire time he was associated with PCW. He is a first ballot Hall of Famer, and one of the most respected wrestlers today. Having said this, he still wants more. He claims that wrestling is a drug and this is the only way to get his fix. His wife, Kristin, and baby boy, surely don't want Jon to keep wrestling, but Jon just keeps one keeping on like only this REAL legend can. Now, as he faces new challenges in PRIME, he looks to build on his legendary past, and make a new legendary future. When Jon Kano joined PRIME, it was at Culture Shock '05. He was a surprise brought in by his long time friend, Boda, to combat the Dark Age. This all took a bad turn when Boda turned his back on Kano and joined the Dark Age, beating Kano down in the process. Kano won't soon forget the embarrassment of being played like that. He now has even more motivation to become a legend here in PRIME and plans to do it at the expense of his one time best friend, Boda, and the Dark Age. Kano and Boda were entangled in a bitter feud for most of the first half of 2005 with both men beating the living hell out of each other. During a brawl on ReVolution, they brawled to the parking lot, where Boda was run over by a truck passing by. Boda was barely clinging to life and Kano felt obligated to come to his aid. Since this chapter in Kano and Boda's long history, Kano has been trying to focus back on his career in PRIME, namely building himself back up to legendary status.
