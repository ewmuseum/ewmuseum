Devin Shakur Biography Nicknames The Devil in Black, The Natural Born Heel, Shak Diesel, Shak Daddy Hometown Raleigh, North Carolina Birthday 12/8/1978 Height 6'2 Weight 214 Fed Titles 2x Intense Champion, 1x Universal Champion Fed Awards The Bestest Heel Of All Timez! Biography Devin Shakur's biography is documented in the destruction of others. Other Feds None Other Fed Titles The only one Shakur won isn't worth discussing. Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style He's the best striker in PRIME/w Kurt Angle suplex ability Finisher Move Holy Shit Finisher Description I'll describe that one in the MWA Finisher Setup Move Good Times Painful Memories Setup Description Standing roundhouse kick to the side of the head (See Mirko Crocop for an idea if you aren't smart on the ways of MMA. This is a knockout kick.) Special Moves They'll be done in the MWA Other Moves Updated: 1/18/11 Arm strikes -Right punch -Left punch -Elbow -Forearm smash -Boxing jab -Knife edge chop -Open hand palm strike -Slap (his actually hurt though) -European uppercut -Spinning backfist Leg Strikes -Shin kicks to the side of the leg -Karate front kick -Standing knee to the body (with opponent standing near) -Roundhouse kick to the sternum -Spinning back kick -Knees to the body (with the opponent down) -Knees from a Muay Thai clinch. -He will basically use his knees and arms in any fashion that adheres to the disciplines of martial arts. He's not a novice and if he encounters another martial artist, will be well versed in whatever style they implement. Suplex list -Vertical suplex -Back suplex -Reverse vertical suplex -Snap suplex -Belly to belly side suplex -Belly to belly overhead suplex -Gutwrench suplex -Double underhook suplex -Fisherman suplex -Half nelson suplex -Full Nelson suplex Submission list: These are all legit submissions, none of the fakeness of wrestling surrounds them. -Armbar -Triangle choke -Gogoplata -Heel hook -Ankle lock -Peruvian necktie -Shoulder lock -Figure Four leg lock -Sharpshooter -Kneebar -Rear naked choke Trademark: (Use on anybody under 330 pounds as finisher) Good Times Painful Memories (Standing roundhouse kick to the side of the head. It knocks you out. Cold.) Possible Alternate Finisher: Quick and Painful Description: Shakur locks his arms around the opponents neck in a Muay Thai clinch and slams his knee into their head three times. These are not fast knees, but slow and very powerful. There's really not much of a counter out of the Muay Thai clinch unless the opponent is very familiar with martial arts. Once the first one hits, they are pretty much out since he's putting all of his weight into each shot. He's looking to knock someone unconscious when these are done. Finisher: Holy Shit Shakur goes to the top rope and locks the opponent in position for a Pepsi Plunge (Top Rope pedigree). They both release in position, but during the flight of the move, Shakur tilts the opponent downward so they are completely upside down with their head looking straight at the mat. Shakur sticks his knee out and lets it collide with their head. Summarization 1st position: Top rope pedigree 2nd position: Opponents arms locked behind back, head tilted downward as if they are going to be dropped in a double underhook piledriver. 3rd position: Same double underhook piledriver position, but Shakur extends his knee forward and puts it into the top of their head. Nobody gets up from this. Nobody. If you write an opponent kicking out of this, I'll tie you to the bed stomach first, strip you naked, and let a horny and drunk Aaron Dillon have his way with you. Strengths -Striking: Shakur is not intelligent in the ways of a professional wrestler. Meaning that, he's not going to try and wear people down with headlocks, or put on a clinic with chain wrestling. He's going to try and physically hurt you, break you mentally, and then physically destroy you. It's his only objective. -Focus: When he wants to, Shakur can drain out the crowd long enough to defeat an opponent. He'll no sell valets at ringside, despite attractiveness, and ignore anybody observing him from ringside/apron/crowd (unless from the Tyler Rayne contingent) -Smart: Shakur is intuitive in the wrestling ring. Again, not intelligent like a wrestler, but he's a great psychologist and one of the best at anticipating what's coming next. -Psychologist: Much like Violence Jack, he can get inside an opponent's head during a match. He's willing to do so because it furthers his focus on the opponent and makes him less likely to lose his focus. Weaknesses -Obsession with Revenge: He doesn't want to just win the battle, he wants to win the war. He'll go to whatever lengths necessary to achieve that goal. If, in a match, someone comes out to ringside who he has a beef with, he's likely to get distracted and focus more attention on them than his opponent, unless that opponent is someone from the Tyler Rayne brigade. -Broken left ankle: Courtesy of Tyler Rayne at Great American Nightmare 2008. -Body: If someone tries to rip his shirt off, he'll divert attention from whatever else is going on to try and keep it on. He doesn't want people seeing his scarred body, and his shirtless form has only been seen twice by PRIME fans. -Long matches: He hasn't had a full wrestling schedule in quite a while, and doesn't want to go into a 30 minute war with an opponent. He wants to end a match quick, in under 15 minutes. If it goes past 20, he's going to get winded. -Fast opponents: With the right amount of taunting, high flying ability, and pace, Shakur can be thrown off his game and get a little desperate. Entrance Music 'Black Hole Sun' by Peter Frampton Ring Entrances The devil is and has always been a gentleman. The irony of this quote is that the man about to emerge from behind the black curtain is far from a gentleman. In fact, he's likely to go down as the biggest asshole to ever walk through any wrestling federation. BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! And everybody knows who he is. Every light in the building shuts off. It's pitch black. All the lights shut off. Pitch black. No one can see two inches ahead. The hatred for this "gentleman" is only magnified when Peter Frampton's fingers glide over an acoustic guitar in the fashion of Chris Cornell. A cascading wave of black pyrotechnics sends smoke out into the audience. A pair of dark brown eyes then invade the PRIME*View. The eyes blink six times before being replaced by the man's name. [SHAKUR] The curtain is tossed aside. With a long black leather trench coat covering his body, Devin Shakur steps into full view and cracks a wry smile. No entrance video highlighting his accolades assists him to the ring. He doesn't need one. With his long and macabre history in PRIME, a video wouldn't do him justice. Shakur hears every insult within earshot. Nobody is shy about expressing his or her feelings toward The Devil in Black. His eyes shift back and forth to gauge reactions. He's a fan of what he sees: ignorant masses not capable of understanding his great wisdom or wrestling talent. His feet tap the ringside mat and his walk becomes military like, continuing until he reaches the base of the ring steps. He stands in front of the steel, taunting any and everybody by leaving his back exposed and arms at his sides. He casually walks up the stairs and steps between the second and third ropes. The ring spotlights focus on him while he removes his coat, folds it, and hands it off to a ringside technician. Shakur is clad from head to toe in Armani's blackest and finest. He stands in his corner and rocks back and forth waiting for the bell.
