Jumpin' Jack Flash Biography Nicknames The Self Proclaimed Sexiest Man Alive Hometown Parts Unknown Birthday Sometime in 1978 Height 6'3' Weight 245 Biography The flamboyant persona of Michael Kriegman Wrestling Style High Flying, some striking, Spot Fu Finisher Move Jumpin' Jack Splash Finisher Description Five And A Half Star Frog Splash (yes, it's just that much better) Other Moves Strengths: Jack's biggest strength is his sense of balance and amazing vertical leap. Unlike the Trashcan Man, when Jack takes to the air it's graceful, it's crisp, and it's highly effective. He's also a bit faster to react than the Trashcan Man, avoiding strikes as opposed to absorbing them. Weaknesses: Like Mr Smiley Face, Jack lacks the Trashcan Man's resiliency. He's a novice in the ring, having no idea how to take advantage of an opponent's weakness. He's inherited some of Trashy's counterwrestling skills, he can wriggle out of some of the slower set up power moves and the like, but again a technician who can ground him is going to beat him every time. And if Trashy's prone to bouts of Spot Fu, Jack is suffering a terminal case of it. His moves are over elaborate and many times unnecessarily complicated. Trademark Moves: Jack Be Nimble: Jack will apply an armringer and walk the ropes. Once he's halfway between turnbuckles, he'll turn with his back facing the center of the ring, still holding his opponent's arm. He'll drop down, catching the back his thighs with the top rope and slingshot backwards into a split legged moonsault, catching his opponent's head on the way down delivering a rolling DDT. He'll immediately spring to his feet and run to the turnbuckle to follow up with... Jack Be Quick: Running up the turnbuckle, Jack will leap backwards without looking to deliver a corkscrew senton bomb. Marking His Territory: He will attempt this move at LEAST once a match. Kicking his opponent in the gut, he'll spin behind them, bending them over by applying a hammerlock with his left hand. He'll then lick the palm of his right hand and deliver a resounding smack to his opponent's ass. Face To Face With Greatness: The Bronco Buster Crossfire Hurricane: Sending his oppoents into the ropes, he'll run for the other side, leaping up to the second rope and springboarding off, twisting in mid air to catch his returning foe with a headscissors takeover. X Marks The Spot: He'll deliver a diagonal knife edge chop with his right and, then cross it with another chop with his left. He'll follow that up with a flying roundhouse kick to the spot in the middle of the chest where the strikes would cross if they left marks (preferably around the solar plexus) Doink: Continuing the tradition, the standard Three Stooges poke to the eyes that transcends all of Michael Kriegman's personalities. Standard Moves: Arm Drag Hip Toss Hurricanrannas Moonsaults (all varieties, from standing to springboard to split legged to Asai) Slingshot Shooting Star Press (note that he won't do a normal one from a standing position on the top turnbuckle... it's too common) Flying Leg Lariat Spinning Heel Kick Front Somersault into a clothesline Kick forward into a somersault legdrop Drop kick Seated drop kick Corkscrew Plancha Knife edge chops. Finishing Move: Jumpin' Jack Splash. A Five And A Half Star Frog Splash. It's just that much higher than Rob Van Dam's, and he doesn't do any pussy overselling of it, instead he goes right into a pin. Tactics: Jack relies on his speed, high flying, and balance to get through matches. He's a huge fan of springboard maneuvers, moonsaults, flying roundhouse kicks, hurricanrannas, you name it, he'll try to bounce off a rope first. He always prefers to do a kip up instead of climbing to his feet if at all possible. He doesn't like simplistic moves. Why do a simple leg drop when you can do a spinning leg drop, or a somersault leg drop? Why do a simple clotheline when you can leap into the air with a flying leg lariat, or by rolling forward, springing out of the somersault with a flying clothesline? Spotfest is the name of the game. He'll always try to get a successful Marking His Territory on an opponent, just to hear the crowd pop. He will stop to absorb crowd reaction, he hungers for the spotlight and adoration of the fans. He'll never cheat by using a weapon. He might do a roll up by pulling down the tights... but if he does, he's going to pull them all the way, showing as much of his opponent's ass as he possibly can. Ring Entrances Ring Entrance: The lights go completely out as Keith Richards' familiar opening riff begins. The bass line kicks in as a fury of yellow, orange, and red pyro explodes over the entrance ramp. I was born in a cross-fire hurricane And I howled at my ma in the driving rain, But its all right now, in fact, it's a gas! But its all right. I'm Jumpin' Jack Flash, Its a gas! gas! gas! The smoke clears and we see Jumpin' Jack Flash, back turned to the audience, showing the words "SEXIEST MAN ALIVE" spelled out in red sequins on the back of his pristine white leather trenchcoat. He slowly spreads his arms and spins around before doing only what we could call a dancing strut as he makes his way down the ramp. I was raised by a toothless, bearded hag, I was schooled with a strap right across my back, But its all right now, in fact, it's a gas! But its all right, I'm Jumpin' Jack Flash, Its a gas! gas! gas! He leaps up on the apron, spinning back around as he shrugs off his coat, revealing the skintight red, orange, and yellow jumpsuit beneath, slit to the navel, glitter sparkling on his bare chest as he soaks in the response of the crowd. I was drowned, I was washed up and left for dead. I fell down to my feet and I saw they bled. I frowned at the crumbs of a crust of bread. Yeah, yeah, yeah I was crowned with a spike right through my head. But its all right now, in fact, it's a gas! But its all right, I'm Jumpin' Jack Flash, Its a gas! gas! gas! Grabbing the top rope, he pulls himself up to the top rope and twists all in one smooth motion, balancing for the briefest of moments on the top rope before he springs into a backflip, landing on his feet, his arms again thrown wide as his confident grin sweeps out over the audience. Jumpin' Jack Flash, it's a gas Jumpin' Jack Flash, it's a gas Jumpin' Jack Flash, it's a gas Jumpin' Jack Flash, it's a gas Jumpin' Jack Flash, it's a gas The music fades away and Jack is ready to go.
