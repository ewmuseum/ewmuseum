Gabriel Afeaki Biography Nicknames GA, The Fijian Lion, The Animal, Hometown Savu Savu, Fiji Birthday 1980 Height 6'3 Weight 287lbs Biography born under starred skies life, death and life again, friend his faith, extinguished. a new light, fears... faced... fervent. gone. shadows. Other Feds AWC Relentless Title, L2E Heavyweight Champion Wrestling Style An explosive Vale Tudo (literal: 'no holds barred') blend comprising of; Judo, Muay Boran and Taekwondo. Finisher Move Fijian Leg Lock // Strikes Finisher Description A Heel Hook // Various Muay Boran Knees from the clinch, or a Chuck Lidell-esque right hand bomb. Finisher Setup Move the Fi.Ji.T (fee.jee.tee) Setup Description An Ankle Pick Takedown Special Moves Located In Matchwriting Aids. Other Moves Elements of the Character: GA in essence is a thoroughbred MMA athlete. His gameplan comprises integral elements of the MMA genre: strikes, takedowns and submissions. Gabriel is brutally powerful, maintains a high intensity and loves to submit opponents. Therefore, when drawing wrestling comparisons Angle, Lesnar and Shamrock spring to mind. A master of the Abu Dhabi Combat Club scene, his mastery of Judo, Muay Boran, Taekwondo and all things Submission enables Gabs a masterful command of his craft: he is an undefeated mixed martial artist, with his explosive Vale Tudo style sweeping all before him. Gabriel is a very fluid and agile fighter. His quickness and strength are his biggest weapons, however his immense technique is a facet often overlooked due to the sheer physicality he possesses. Explosive and enigmatic, the Fijian Lion likes to put on a show. Ring Style: GA rarely ever uses the turnbuckles, he is not concerned with aerial assaults in the slightest. Although agile, he is uncomfortable with mounting turnbuckle attacks. Due to his noble sense of competition he will also rarely cheat to gain an upper hand or resort to underhand tactics. Relying on his power, speed and technique, Afeaki will look to use any of his strengths to take his opponent apart piecemeal. Strengths:GA is an amazingly powerful animal and will look to impose his physical will on an opponent from the offset. A superb striker in the mould of Chuck Liddell/Mirko Cro Cop, anyone willing to stand toe to toe with GA will more than likely meet an early end. However, this is not Gab's uppermost intention: he has a plethora of takedowns, suplexes, throws and trips all at his disposal. His intention is to physically batter the opponent by continually wearing them down with a modified 'ground and pound' tactic. Looking to suplex, throw, slam his opponent into a weakened position to start his submission work. He is blessed with great speed and quickness for someone so big. His power and explosiveness are the key aspects of his article. Weaknesses: No man is without his flaws and Gabs in the ring has weaknesses to exploit. GA is a showman and thusly has been known to try and please the crowd a little too much instead of getting the job done. Gabs is not a natural wrestler, he is a fighter, thus an in ring tactician can hold a huge advantage over Afeaki. ---------- The Move List Judo: Judo forms the major part of Gabriel's takedown offence. Using sweeps, throws and trips Gabriel likes to slam his opponent to the ground. GA is a 9th grade Black Belt, and is well versed in all Judo styles. He uses some throws as a basis for submissions, while others work merely as a method to inflict pain. GA is nefarious in his delivery. Judo Techniques - Ashi Guruma (Leg Wheel), Daki Wakare (High Lift and Separate: used to set up head attacks or arm submissions.), Hane Goshi (Spring Hip Throw), Harai Goshi (Sweeping Hip Throw), Harai Tsurikimo Miashi (Lifting Pulling Foot Sweep), Ippon Seoinage (One Arm Shoulder Throw), Kibishi Gaeshi (Heel Trip Reversal), Kosoto Gari (Small Outside Reap), O Guruma (Large Wheel), Osoto Gari (Large Outer Reaping), Sukui Nage (Scoop Throw), Sumi Gaeshi (Corner Reversal), Tomoe Mage (Circle Throw), Ura Nage (Rear Throw), Yama Arashi (Mountain Storm), Yoko Gake (Side Hook). Muay Boran: Muay Boran is the predecessor of Muay Thai and works to maximise the infliction of damage each strike can impact. The salient notion being the use of extremely powerful elbow and knee techniques/combos with a view to incapacitate the opponent as swiftly as possible. Muay Boran Techniques Kicks: The Teep (foot jab), Roundhouse, Revers (thrust kick), Crescent.Elbows: Horizontal, Diagonal-upwards, Diagonal-downwards, Uppercut, Downward, Backward-spinning and Flying. GA throws elbows nefariously.Knees: Kao Dode (Jumping knee strike), Kao Loi (Flying knee strike), Kao Tone (Straight knee strike). Taekwondo: GA uses a loose Taekwondo ethic, heel kicks, and jabs form a backbone of his offense. General MMA: Although GA is heavily influenced by his backgrounds in his various art forms, cage fighting required him to learn other basic techniques to employ. These ranged from takedowns to strikes. MMA Takedowns Double Leg Takedown (GA opts for a double leg takedown that resembles a Batista spinebuster.), Single Leg Takedown (One leg pick up, and dump.) Submissions: You know how people have a thing about cars? Or bikes... or you know... a thing... well Gabriel's thing is submission holds. Don't ask. They just are. Achilles Lock, Anaconda Choke, Bicep Slicer, Guillotine Choke (Variations), Knee Bar (Variations), Arm Bar (Variations), Triangle Hold, Katahajime. The Wrestling: Gabi is new to the wrestling game, but has found himself accustomed to use certain moves. Suplexes - Using similar skills found in Judo to lift and throw an opponent, Gabi has taken several suplexes and incorporated them into his Vale Tudo style. Belly-to-back Cradle Suplex, Millennium Suplex, Tiger Suplex, Belly-to-back Flipping Throw Suplex, Tiger Suplex '85, Taz-mission Suplex, Belly-to-back Hammerlock Suplex, German Suplex, Belly-to-belly Suplex, Head-and-arm Suplex, Belly-to-belly Overhead Release Suplex, Cradle Suplex, Leg Capture Suplex, Snap Suplex, Soviet Judo Suplex.Other Moves - Charging Clothesline (a favourite spot where GA will explode from the ropes with a hellacious clothesline), Fireman's Carry Takedown, Arm Drag, Dragon Screw, Big Back Body Drop Entrance Music Tha King by T.I. Ring Entrances The arena descends into darkness... a few moments later, a black void greets the eyes of the fans as their gaze wanders toward the video screen. Immediately, there are two sets of silver points a few inches away from each other drawing outlines of the human eye. When the drawing is complete, the outlines open, revealing a pair of red eyes with white irises and no pupils. The infectious beats of T.I.'s 'Tha King' pervade the airwaves. ... and in case you forgot... I'm tha King! The eyes flash to a dazzling white as pyro explodes with Gabriel Afeaki bounding out, hoody adorned, his rippling physique glistening in the flashbulbs. THE FIJIAN LION HAS ARRIVED.... Afeaki walks with a relaxed step towards the ring and slowly makes his ascent up the ring steps.
