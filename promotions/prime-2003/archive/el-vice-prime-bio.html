El Vice Biography Nicknames El Rey Hometown El Paso, Texas Birthday 10.14.1982. Height 5'11' Weight 207 Fed Titles n/a Fed Awards n/a Biography El Vice was born in El Paso, Texas under mysterious circumstances. With no father, and a mother who didn't know how to speak English, El Vice faced a hard future. He graduated high school at the top of his class, with barely enough money to buy books and clothes. What's more impressive is that El Vice did not manage to master the English language until age twenty, and even now speaks a form of spanglish. Shortly afterward, his mother died of undiagnosed cancer. El Vice couldn't afford college, so he began working various jobs (he was an Elvis impersonator for a traveling circus) for people until eventually he got enough money to attend wrestling school at the age of twenty. He began wrestling locally in Texas as an Elvis impersonator, impressing crowds with his spanglish songs. The charismatic star went back to a more prestigious wrestling school at the age of 21, and graduated to a higher level of competition, Core Wrestling. When officials would not allow him to sing one of his more controversial songs, he demanded a trade to PRIME, before his first match.
