ReVolution 135 Rose Garden Arena: Portland, Oregon ---- The Greatest Show On Earth Welcome one and welcome all to ReVolution 135! … But wait, where’s the entrance music? The badass theme song that opens every segment? Gone? Nay, but rather than open to the legions of screaming fans filling out the very last show before PRIME’s biggest event of the calendar year, we open to the visage of none other than potential Universal Title contender, Sonny Silver calmly walking down the ramp. No music, no flashy lights, no pyro. Just the apathetic stare of Sonny – steel chair in hand – heading towards the ringside area. Nick: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, WELCOME TO REVOLUTION’S ONE-HUNDRED, THIRTY-FIFTH BROADCAST AND… Wait, what the hell is this? Why’s Sonny Silver coming down to ringside?! Richard: SONNY SILVER! He’s coming down here to deliver me from your horrid broadcasting one and for all! Let’s see you do play-by-play with that chair lodged up your ass, Stuart! But instead of opting to no doubt expel some of his current intensity upon the likes of either Nick or Richard, Sonny simply props the chair in between the ring and the announce table and calmly sits down. His poker face tells the audience nothing, nor does seating position. He stares off into space, snarling like a dog watching for oncoming trespassers in his yard. Nick: This… is eerie to say the least. What business does he have out here, we’re not scheduled for any interview tonight. Richard: Who cares? You don’t ask the mentally unbalanced man why he’s got a gun, you shut up and do as he says, lest you want to find yourself getting capped. Nick: Ladies and gents, tonight, we’ve got a great show for you tonight! In a MASSIVE Four-team match right before the biggest show of the year, we’ve got the team of Universal Champion Lindsay Troy and Intense Champion Pierce Lavelle going up against the teams of Nova and Dusk, Sonny Silver and Shakur for the second week in a row, and Danny Ferguson teaming with Jonathon Winters! Richard: Awesome! The faces gonna die! And then, finally, the music hits. Except it's not Otep's "T.R.I.C.", the usual ReVolution opening theme. It's not a song that anyone in the arena could possibly enjoy. The tacky beat and obnoxious techno thump of Daft Punk's "Da Funk" evoked painful memories to PRIME fans, memories of late 2005 and early 2006, and the perpetual annoyance that was Danny Ferguson, Chandler Tsonda and the Illustrious Face-Eater. Collectively, they were the A-List, and they were a pain in the ass. Silent and otherwise cheers of joy went up from the masses as the world's most narcissistic stable exploded along with the metaphor that was Facey's PRIME exit strategy at Colossus III. With their apparent lynchpin reduced to smithereens, the List was sent into a downward spiral that included Ferguson's already well-publicized incarceration and Tsonda's flameout and disappearance months later. The group that had at various times attempted to Freebird their way through not one, not two, but THREE separate title reigns had lost basically everything in the wake of PRIME's biggest show. And now, with the anniversary of their anti-tipping point looming and Colossus IV around the corner, the strewn debris of the fractured supergroup had done their best T-1000 and re-formed, fully developed, as if nothing had ever changed. Fortunately for all of us, though, plenty HAD changed during the unexpected sabbatical. Ferguson had spent the entirety of the year playing grown-up alongside an all-star team of some of PRIME's biggest heroes. Tsonda had spent his time off pulling a Lebron and trying his hand at becoming a global brand. And Facey, well...if I said he had changed at all, would you ever know the difference? Yes, on paper this was the same A-List. But this was not your older-by-one-year brother's A-List. This was 2.0. Which is why the song that rips through the P.A. system - and your delicate ears - is not "Da Funk", but THA REEEEEEEEEEMIIIIIIIIIIIIIIX! Take it away, Chemical Brothers... The lights go out in the arena, save for a few Hollywood-esque white spotlights that circle the crowd. The entrance ramp is lit red from below to give it that familiar catwalk carpet look. Eventually, the spotlights settle on the curtain, and it parts like the stage curtain at a theater. There, in all their glory or some reasonable facsimile thereof, stand Chandler Tsonda, The Illustrious Face-Eater and Danny Ferguson. The reaction is immediate, passionate and...kind of mixed. Nick: Am I crazy, or are some people actually cheering for these guys? Richard: No! They can't! How dare they! Nick: It's like going to a concert of a band you've loved and seeing a bunch of 14-year-old girls in line, isn't it? Richard: Harumph. The three men stroll out onto the stage and collect a fair amount of cheers with their regular serving of boos. Free of their own respective hangers-on, they make their way down the ramp. It’s not so much gloating as showboating or hamming it up. At the bottom of the ramp, as Facey and Tsonda roll into the ring and begin demanding a mic, Danny pauses. He exchanges a glare with Sonny Silver, seated just feet away from him. Sonny might smirk and talk a little smack on any other night, but this time he just rubs his casted hand with the free one and sneers at the Superstar. Finally, Danny jumps up on the apron and steps into the ring as the Illustrious Face-Eater takes the microphone. The Illustrious Face-Eater: You know, everyone's said to me 'Why, Face? Why go back to those assholes after all that they've done to you?' Danny Ferguson: Funny, I thought they were saying that to me. The Illustrious Face-Eater: They're saying it to all of us, Danny. You know why? Because they're scared. They saw a so-called 'super-group' performing at their lowest quality tier for the last six months, needing to bolster their rankings when the going got tough. And now that threat has dissolved, it's like Monsoon Season is starting all over again. They're scared, Danny, because they remember what it was like having the three most happenin' dudes kicking tail on a weekly basis, and they don't want that time-period to return. That sort of shit is what sets the excellent apart from the peons. It's what separates the A-List from the rest. Talent. Perseverance. And most of all, Wits. Why can't I get play from these slutty interviewers we've got running around while these rookies get their dicks sucked? AND WHY THE HELL ARE THE THREE BIGGEST, MOST SELF-RIGHTEOUS-YET-SICKENINGLY-TALENTED ASSHOLES ON THE ROSTER WORKING TOGETHER!? It just works, alright? You think I'd have it any other way? And I know them just the same. And if there's anything I can trust, it's that Danny Ferguson will be Danny Ferguson, and that Chandler Tsonda will always remain Chandler Tsonda. We may not 'love' each othere. But there isn't any agenda here, folks. You don't need to worry. We're not trying to take over PRIME via some hostile takeover. We've been there. We know control. This time? This time it's all about each other. A BROTHERHOOD. No games to play, no politicking with Nelson or Worth. This time, it's all about success by any means necessary. And to stay there, we're going to have to watch each other's back. And there's no confusion this time. This isn't the "where did you put the car keys--oh I thought you had them" A-List of yesteryear. The Illustrious Face-Eater: THIS IS THE NEW AND IMPROVED STAB YOU WITH A RUSTY COKE-CAN A-LIST. And AIN'T NO ONE gonna stop us. Not Nova, under perjury of getting another head-shot to the balls. Not Sonny Silver, WHOM WE ALL FORGOT, GOT HIS BITCH ASS HANDED TO HIM FROM BEYOND THE GRAVE, COURTESY OF MOI. Not even Lindsay Troy, who seems adamant on giving Aimz a run for her money. I MEAN, FUCKING SERIOUSLY, WHAT IS THIS? THE FWO? IS IT, LIKE, A FUCKING REQUIREMENT TO RUN A ROMANCE ANGLE WITH THAT BITCH TO GET TO THE TOP?! Chandler pulls Facey in for a hug, whispering in his ear. The Illustrious Face-Eater: NO, I WILL NOT CALM DOWN! She's going through a divorce, Sonny's acting like a jilted ex, fucking KILLEAN turned into an awkward pre-teen girl whenever they were in the same room together, and now some new kid's getting hand-jobs from her on roadtrips! Hell, EVEN DANNY'S GETTING SOME FUCKING PLAY. BUT THE SECOND I SUGGEST 'THREE MEN AND A SUPER BABY' WITH DEVILLE AND ROLLINS TO HER, HER FUCKING PLATE GETS FULL?! FUCK THA-- The Model Citizen steals the stick and begins to address his adoring fans (certainly not for the first time). Chandler Tsonda: What my partner is trying to say is that PRIME has been squandering great potential. The status quo has become mediocrity on all levels of the operation. Some diehard PRIME marks boo, but the crowd seems somewhat interested in this atypically thoughtful (for the Model Citizen) critique. Chandler Tsonda: When I left to conquer the commercial world, Matt Ward was a fearsome force. Granted, I beat him the only time I faced him but he notched wins like Snow notches homosexual encounters with strangers. Pause for the wit to sink in. Chandler Tsonda: And now?! He’s midcard slumming with Titan St. James…REALLY?! TITAN SAINT JAMES?! The guy who fell off the face of the map after losing to me for approximately eighteen straight ReVs? The guy who manages to counter big and stupid with timid and unentertaining? Fantastic. The Sultan of Style shakes his head just thinking about PRIME’s former greatness. I don’t know about anyone on this roster or in this crowd, but Lindsay Troy doesn’t scream dominant champion to me. In fact…wait a tick, has she ever defended the damn thing? I hope everyone’s got their Tivo ™ set… Cheesy promotional turn and grin for the camera. *sigh* Capitalism at its best. It is what it is. Everything's just a rehash, a repeat or a retread. There's no wrong in going back to what works, is there? Danny Ferguson: But what kills me, Nov...what kills me is that you NEVER. SAW IT. COMING. Some boos flutter out for this one, undoubtedly for the same reason anyone has booed Danny in the past few weeks - for a seemingly unwarranted and mean-spirited attack on their recovered hero, the Risen Star. Danny Ferguson: It's funny that you referenced Kyle Lamen two weeks ago, because it was 19 months and 48 episodes of ReVolution ago, at 86, that we ran that act on Kyle. I’ve learned to do what Hollywood always told me I should do, to shut out emotion or care and think about the self when it becomes necessary. I’ve learned to look another man in the eye and not give a damn about him. I am not the triggerman type - they told me that in my first audition. Well, when I did auditions, anyway...
