Robert Falk Biography Nicknames The Destruction Device, Mendez (the Codemaster's nickname for him) Hometown Colorado Springs, Colorado Birthday August 1st, 1980 Height 6'10 Weight 357 lbs. Federation Awards None. Biography The massive younger brother of Scott Falk. Robert and his brother came under the direction of a man named Daniel Phillips a few years ago, who trained them to become wrestlers. The goal of Daniel was to use them as a way of further destroying Joey Malone's legacy by having the two brothers destroy the two students that Joey Malone personally trained: the Codemaster and Coral Avalon. Daniel's plans went awry, however, when both Falk brothers not only failed to destroy the two Malone trainees, but even befriended one of them (Codemaster). Eventually, when the Codemaster decided he needed help in the fWo, he called in both Falk Brothers to serve in his new faction, the Blue Rogues. The Falks have remained by the Codemaster's side ever since. Other Feds None. Other Federation Awards None. Weapon Details He, himself, is a weapon. Management Style Enforcer / Powerful Special Moves Black Hole Slam / Full Metal Jacket (straightjacket suplex) Other Tactics Robert Falk, if it hasn't been made clear, is a wrestler who uses absolutely ferocious and unmatched power to decimate foes. His power is enormous and he can lift an opponent weighing five hundred pounds onto his shoulders if he needed to. He can furthermore lift two opponents at once, and in some rare cases, even three. Robert also has a frightening amount of speed due to his long legs, he can build up a lot of forward momentum to the point of being difficult to stop once he's in motion. He uses this forward momentum to power his absolutely devastating Clothesline From Colorado, or some of his other moves. Robert's weakness is that his center of gravity is actually quite high since most of his muscle mass is centered around his upper body. This means that if one targetted his legs, he would be put off balance and sent to the canvas. Furthermore, Robert isn't used to opponents capable of matching his power (ie: Vic Creed), which can lead to unexpected consequences. Robert typically dominates his matches with powerful attacks, heeding nothing towards psychology. His psychology and mindset in a match is very simple: destroy, DESTROY, *DESTROY*. Once he feels the opponent has been pounded into putty enough, he typically finishes with the Full Metal Jacket. If the opponent fights back and comes at him with a run, that's usually when Robert ends the match with the Black Hole Slam instead. Ring Entrances See Blue Rogues.
