Dave Gibson Biography Nicknames Mr. Old School, Gibbo Hometown Charlotte, North Carolina Birthday May 22, 1979 Height 6-1 Weight 228 Fed Titles None... yet Fed Awards None... yet Biography After successful runs in IOW, jOlt, and OSE, Gibson was forced to retire in January of 2004 following a second severe neck injury. Now, he’s risking it all to make a comeback. While he’s never won a world championship, Gibson has been made his fair share of enemies during his career. Gibson is best known perhaps for his work in OSW. The first three months of his tenure there were consumed by a rivalry with Hessian—during which Gibson broke Hessian’s arm and the Goliath went into Gibson’s home and confronted his elderly mother. The hatred between these two still burns brightly. During that feud, Gibson began to tag with Clay Vendeta as the Throwbacks, and they won the tag team titles in the federation’s first and only four-team TLC Match against Civil Disobedience, Ronin and Da Westsidaz. Just weeks later, though, Gibson betrayed CCV and joined the offshoot of OSW, New School Wrestling. Once NSW was defeated, Gibson became embroiled in a rivalry with Timo Bolamba that lasted from the spring of 2003 until OSW finally folded in January of 2004. It enveloped Gibson’s stable, Power Play, as well as Timo’s recincarnated Asylum before devolving into a one-on-one affair. During their rivalry, Gibson inflicted a knee injury on Timo that eventually ended his career, while the Sellout himself began to feel the early effects of neck trouble. Power Play did not last, though. After spending a good two months trying to bring the late Matt Register into the fold, the Apex Predator took over the stable—betraying Gibson and delivering a piledriver onto his OSE title belt. Gibson made his return and the two embarked on a brief and intense rivalry, capped by Gibson’s victory over Register in a brutal Strap Match. 7 His body may have failed, but one of Gibson's most marketable characteristics--his skill on the mic-- led him back to the world of pro wrestling. He signed a contract with RPW... as the host of the regular interview segment, 'School's In Session.' Gibson uses this forum as his own personal soapbox--while ripping all those who offend his old-school sensibilities. Not long after, he entered the fray in RPW as a manager. First, he brought Shawn Stewart into the federation, steering him through a brutal three-way feud against Cisco Milloy and Steven Silver. Then, Mr. Old School added Exceptional Dave Hurst to the fray before truly upping the ante and forming the first large stable in RPW history. Gibson amassed the services of Chris Storm, the Marvelous Brothers, Robbie Decker and the mysterious bodyguard known only as Pain, joining that group with Stewart and Hurst to form Old School Elite. The partnership between Gibson and Stewart did not last, though. The Panther chafed under Gibson’s aggressive “win at all costs” philosophy, and finally quit the stable—only to be beat down by his former teammates. In the ensuing weeks, Gibson used a hidden clause in their contract to severely garnish Stewart’s wages, and even put together some misleading footage to make Stewart’s new wife believe he had been cheating on her with prostitutes. The issue between these two has never been resolved. After RPW closed, Gibson went to tSc where he became manager for the Flying Frenchie. Gibson spent most of his time behind the scenes, trying to broker lucrative endorsement contracts and television appearances for his new client. Since departing that now-closed federation, Gibson has opened a modest wrestling school in his hometown of Charlotte.
