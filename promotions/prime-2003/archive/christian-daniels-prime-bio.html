Christian Daniels — PRIME biography

Christian Daniels Biography Wrestler Name Christian Daniels Nicknames The Biker Hometown Raleigh, North Carolina Birthday 2/19/69 (40 years old) Height 7'0 Weight 302 lbs Biography Christian will never divulge information about his childhood or life, but through public and police records, it is established that he is the leader of the North Carolina motorcycle group known as 'Death'. When he was 20, both of his parents were murdered in a mafia hit and he became head honcho of the family. He burglarized, robbed, manipulated, and did whatever was necessary to ensure survival for himself and his family. Somewhere along the way, he adopted Devin Shakur into the fold and made him one of the Smith family and a member of Death. Wrestling held his interest for a brief stint in 2002 where he became an HAW Tag Champion with brother Chris 'Icon' Smith. He fled the country in 2006 to avoid capture after a statewide scandal involving the murders of Michael Connors, Lance Robinson, Marcus Galloway, and Daniel Williams. In late 2007, whatever remaining evidence against the Smith family and Christian was stolen and never recovered by the Raleigh Police Department. The evidence remains missing to this day. While suspicion remains high, there is no concrete evidence tying them to any of the four aforementioned murders. Christian returned in 2008 after coaching Devin Shakur through murdering the Braintrust when the second generation of Connors, Larry and Steven, were getting too close to Devin. Widely considered to be one of the most powerful men in the state of North Carolina, he's the typical Hells Angel type that nobody wants to encounter. While suspected of an immense amount of murders, none have ever been able to hold water in a court of law and he has gotten away without charges on every case against him. He is essentially a free man but always chased by authorities who hold a grudge against him. He is a legend throughout the state and known throughout the country as one of the most vicious and violent men around. He would rather use his fists than his mouth and never thinks twice about beating someone to a pulp. Even at the age of 40, he continues to go strong in his involvement with Death and coaching Devin Shakur through life. His purpose in PRIME is to ensure that Tyler Rayne does not get the final word in the war against his younger brother. Power: He's 7 feet and 300 pounds so he's more than capable of lifting anybody on the roster off the ground and throwing them around (Hessian included). He might not be able to swing them around repeatedly like a Brock Lesnar type, but he can certainly lift them without straining his body. 2. Striking/Stand up game: He's a brawler, the man who taught PRIME's toughest striker, Devin Shakur, how to box and hone his martial arts skills. He knows how to hit, where to hit, and how to hit accurately. His boxing ability is second to none and if he gets in a groove, many wrestlers on the roster aren't going to be left standing. 3. Pain Threshold: While he's aged and his body has a lot of wear and tear, it will take a lot to keep him down for an extended period of time. Through his biker days, he's been through the worst of the worst and come out on top. 4. Ring Intelligence/Composure: Christian has seen the ins and outs of the wrestling business and come up with through a family chock full of wrestlers. He's been in the business himself for a time and knows to keep his cool. He won't freak out and go after the referee. He's a business first kind of wrestler. Will always focus on the opponent. Weaknesses 1. Stamina: He is 40 years old and doesn't have the stamina to go out and wrestle hour long matches every single night at the pace of the PRIME roster. He's got an old school mentality of slowing beating an opponent and ending the match inside of about 10 to 12 minutes. He can, and will, get fatigued if the match goes longer than 20-22 minutes. There's a difference between being able to take pain, a strength, and losing ability from exhaustion. 2. Lack of Speed: He's got great instincts, but he's not going to be able to keep up with a Bryan Dawkins kind of wrestler in the ring. He will have definite problems with a wrestler who isn't afraid to throw his body around and use quickness. 3. Legs and Back: He is not immune to the cons of being a big man and having gone through a ton of fights in his life. His legs are not in the greatest of shape and neither is his back. If a wrestler targets these areas and brings Christian to the ground, he will more than likely be in trouble as that is not how he is accustomed to fighting. 4. Pride: Normally, this is considered a strength but not in Christian's case. Even if a wrestler threatens to break his limbs, he will more often let them rather than submit and end the match. He despises any form of submission as that goes against his core philosophy of survival. He will instruct nobody to throw in the towel for him and allow himself to get beaten to a pulp before conceding defeat verbally. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- In Ring Style: Starting off the match: In the first few moments of the match, Christian will feel out his opposition and allow them to take command of the match. He will want to see where the match is going, how fast the pace is, and what he needs to do to adapt. Once these variables are accounted, he will go after the opponent. Considering his immense background in boxing, he will want to start off a lot of his matches by throwing punches. Even if a wrestler puts up a guard or defense, Christian can get through the small cracks. The likes of Wade Elliott and Jason Natas might be able to hold their own against him for a little while, but not for the duration of the match. Middle of the Match: If Christian remains in control, at about the five or six minute mark is when he's going to elect to bring in the power moves: Slams, suplexes, and so forth. He will want to wear down the opponent and work them through slowly into the finish. If Christian is on the defensive (at most any point in the match): He will try and use his boxing to get back in control, or take a calculated risk and kick the opponent away with his legs. Elbow strikes and forearms are likely counters for him as that takes the focus off his legs. He does have extraordinary martial arts ability, but unlike Devin Shakur does not choose to display those skills unless absolutely necessary. His legs are banged up so unless he senses that someone could put him away, there will be no Gogoplatas or Triangle Chokes applied. End of the Match: The biggest impact moves possible. He will look to drop the opponent on their head and neck. If an opponent has a bad back, he will utilize moves that cater to their injured area. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Moveset Boxing Set -Jabs -Hooks -Uppercuts -Crosses His reach is massive due to his height, but he's not afraid to come inside and hammer somebody. He prefers to pepper somebody with jabs and get them into a brawling game where he's more comfortable. Striking -Elbows -Forearms -Body kicks (only to the body, not the head or legs) He will not deliver a headbutt.
