Idiots. He turns his attention back to Tchu, but now the tide appears to be turning yet again when Tchu starts to adjust his body position, sliding free of the submission. Sonny tries to throw a right hand, but the Inhuman Being cuts him off at the pass and fires a right of his own. Silver bounces off the ropes and comes back again, but Tchu once again blocks and answers with a second right. A third soupbone finally knocks the Universal Champion on his ass, making the crowd go wild! Nick: Tchu’s coming back into this! We’ve seen him go through hell and come back stronger than ever! Richard: I’ll admit, I never thought he’d last this long with all those punishing maneuvers Sonny’s been throwing at him, but he’s proven me wrong! Silver rises to his feet, but a Back Elbow from the former Uni Champ puts him down. Rinse, repeat and Sonny goes down again. Tchu whips him into the ropes, but Sonny reverses, attempting to send the Inhuman Being into the corner. Tchu stops himself just short and throws a HUGE elbow into the head of Sonny, stunning him and making him wobble around in a daze. Nick: What a shot! Grabbing hold of Sonny, he grabs him by the head and DRIVES him damn near through the mat and to China with the DDT! Richard: No! Nick: Yes! Great DDT hit with perfection by Tchu, but he may not be able to capitalize! Sure enough, the quick dose of adrenaline had worn off, allowing Sonny almost a welcome reprieve. Tchu cradles his back with the free hand, crawling over to Silver and hooking a leg. ONE! TWO! THR… KICK OUT! Sonny defiantly shoots the shoulder up, deflating the crowd in the process. Tchu brings Sonny to a vertical base and throws a succession of Knife-Edge Chops into his chest before whipping him. Silver reverses… URANAGE BACKBREAKER! Nick: Damn! Just when you think Tchu was about to fight his way back into this match, Sonny just cut off his momentum completely with the Uranage into the Backbreaker! Richard: Love that man, Nick! Just when you think you have him down for the count, Sonny shows just why he’s the Universal Champion with moves like that! Sonny holds the back of his head in pain following the brutal backbreaker, but Tchu looks much worse for wear. Having his spine pounded into a fine powder does not do wonders for his title aspirations. Placing him into the corner, Sonny buries the heel of his boot into Tchu’s skull several times before getting a running start off the ropes… Richard: FACEWASH! Nick: DAMN, THAT WAS HIGH-IMPACT! TCHU MAY BE OUTCOLD! Nick Stuart’s statement may ring true as the camera gets a close-up of the glassy-eyed look on Tchu’s face as The Five-Star Ring General pulls him out of the corner. Sonny hooks a leg and cradles the head along with it. ONE! TWO! THR… NO! Tchu barely shows the strength to kick out, but that tells Sonny the story that Tchu’s chances of winning the title have dwindled down severely since the onset of the match. Busted open hardway from the forehead after the sickening boot to the head, Silver screams bloody murder at The Inhuman Being as he tries to fight through his daze. Silver: Come on, motherfucker! Get up! He throws a few boots to soften up the back of his rival. Seeing another opportunity to put the match away, Sonny takes hold of two of Tchu’s feet and looks out to the Canadian fans before attempting to intertwine his legs. Fervently, Tchu tries to fight out of his grip, but Sonny buries a few boots into the abdomen to keep him from fighting back. He manages to turn Tchu over… Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Making use of the very sacrilegious move known as the Sharpshooter (yeah, he’s not Bret, so they hate it of course.) Sonny sneers and pulls back on the legs, putting pressure onto the back of PRIME’s Wrecking Ball. Nick: Great strategic use of the Sharpshooter, though one would surmise he didn’t do this to endear himself to the fans. Richard: He’s got him dead to rights, Nick! Tchu ain’t going anywhere now that he has all that pressure bearing down on his back. Almost reveling in their hatred, Sonny smiles as Tchu raises a hand, looking like he’s ready to tap. The referee goes to check on him, but Tchu puts the hand down only to try and grab for the ropes. Silver looks at the referee and glares. Silver: Ring the fucking bell! Like you haven’t done it before! But since this is neither Montreal OR the WWE and the referee isn’t Earl Hebner, he does no such thing. While Sonny bickers with the ref some more, Tchu screams gutturally and claws violently for the nearest set of ropes. He pulls Sonny with him. Inch by inch, the ropes get closer to his grip until he can put two fingers around it… Nick: He got it! He got the ropes! Richard: Do your job, referee! Ring the goddamn bell! He used the Sharpshooter! In Canada! Don’t you know how this works?! Sonny holds onto the submission until the referee starts making the count. Just before five, Sonny releases the hold, choosing not to take the chickenshit way out and get himself disqualified. People bashed his reign enough as is; he didn’t want to go out like that. He wanted the goddamn victory. Measuring up Tchu, Sonny holds a hand out, ready to possibly decapitate him with a Lariat. With the aid of the ropes, Tchu puts himself right in Sonny’s crosshairs. He runs… he leaps… Nick: Here comes the Lariat… NO! Richard: NO! TCHU COUNTERS! Nick: Not just ANY COUNTER! That was Killean Sirrajin’s Supreme Justice! Tchu manages to counter Sonny’s Lariat with the Running STO that his long-time friend/enemy Killean Sirrajin made famous throughout PRIME history. Down and out for the moment, the referee begins the mandatory ten-count when neither Sonny nor Tchu are able to stand. Nick: You talk about Sonny showing off a brilliant counter, WHAT a move. I’m sure Killean Sirrajin’s watching backstage, smiling. Richard: That wasn’t his move! What business does he have using it? Nick: Tchu’s back has been worked over in the last near ten minutes, making moves like his Inhuman Treatment and the Broken Soul difficult. So Tchu found a way to work around it! Brilliant strategy! Now, can he capitalize? The referee is in the middle of his count, now reaching five. Determined not to be the first match to end on a simple countout since the Mesozoic Era, Tchu is the first one to move, trying to fight through the searing pain his back is in. Sonny follows shortly, just barely managing to get onto his knees. They reach seven and finally Tchu’s the first to a standing position, ducking underneath a Clothesline from Sonny to come back and connect… FLYING ELBOW! Holding his back briefly, Tchu scrambles to his feet again, grabs Sonny by the head, turn him around and SPIKE him into the mat with the Hangman’s Neckbreaker! Wiping some blood out of his eye, Tchu grabs Sonny by the hair and starts wailing on the champion with a volley of rights, each harder than the last. Before the referee even has the chance to count, Tchu picks both he and Sonny up and hurls the Universal Champion into the nearest corner, damn near taking his head off with a Clothesline in the corner! Nick: A new life has picked up from Tchu! He’s so close to the unheard of third Universal Title reign, he can taste it! The Dayton native follows suit and throws Sonny cross-corner, looking to follow up with a second clothesline, but a hard elbow stuns the Inhuman Being. Sonny runs at him, but Tchu buries a foot in his gut. He puts him down… PULLING PILEDRIVER! Sonny folds over from the gruesome impact and flops over onto his back while Tchu still holds his back, still feeling the effects of Sonny’s offense. He leaps for another cover. ONE! TWO! THREE… NO! Nick: SO close off the Pulling Piledriver, but that move had to take a lot out of both men. Tchu clenches a fist and waits for Sonny to rise once again, licking his chops as he does so. He waves at Sonny to bring it and wraps a hand around his throat… DOWNFALL… NO! Sonny, thinking great strategy, sits down and rolls Tchu up into a pinning predicament… ONE! TWO! NO! Gritting his own teeth now, Sonny pulls his kneepad down and slaps a bare knee, ready to strike. Tchu rises and sees the oncoming Knee Strike… DUCKED! Nick: FTW! TCHU’S GOT HIM LOCKED IN THE FTW! Richard: DON’T GO INTO THE LIGHT, SONNY! DON’T GO INTO THE LIGHT! Finding his oxygen supply now being robbed, Sonny thrashes his arms around, trying to get to any set of ropes, but the Inhuman Being doesn’t relent and tries his damnedest to keep Sonny restrained in the Katahajime submission! Silver, face turning blue quickly, thinks quickly. Nick: What’s he doing? Sonny wisely makes his way up the turnbuckle and uses the momentum to quickly push himself backward, landing into a second pinning predicament on Tchu! ONE! TWO! THR… NO! Richard: Smart thinking! Either Tchu would have to release the submission or suffer a defeat because he was too stubborn. Sonny, coughing up a lung, tries to gather his wits, but Tchu’s already there to bury a foot in his gut to cut him off at the pass. He bounces off the ropes and attempts a Running Knee Strike of his own, but Silver sees Tchu from the corner of his eye and lets Tchu pass. On the rebound, he throws a HARD Punt Kick directly into the former Uni Champ’s back! Letting out a loud roar, Sonny wastes no time measuring him up… Richard: CORONA! OUT OF NOWHERE! The stiffest High Roundhouse Kick that professional wrestling has ever seen connects square in the jaw of the Inhuman Being. Slashing a thumb across his throat, Sonny hurriedly picks Tchu up by the waist and throws him back. SAITO SUPLEX! Nick: There’s the Saito Suplex! Sonny took advantage of that sore back of Tchu’s and finally landed the killing blow! Sonny hooks the leg, praying to your deity (or deities) of choice that this would be over. ONE! TWO! THREE! BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Silver doesn’t even move his weight off Tchu right away considering how much he’s spent. The referee is handed the Universal Title and puts the most treasured prize in the sport in his hand. For nearly thirty minutes, the two raised hell, but like any physical masterpiece there could only be one winner. Vince Howard: HERE IS YOUR WINNER AND STILL PRIME UNIVERSAL CHAMPION… SONNY SILVER! Both men tore their bodies apart for a prize they felt worth more than life itself. Sonny picks himself up using the ropes to steady his weary body and raises the title high over his head, looking at the fallen Tchu. Nick: WHAT A MATCH! These guys have put on a damn clinic and Tchu has proven here why he’s a Hall of Fame-calibur athlete and one of PRIME’s most decorated superstars! Richard: That’s all well and good, but Sonny hung in there with Tchu the entire way and even scored the pinfall victory over him! Only a handful of people could claim that feat! How true that was… Want to meet the other guy on PRIME’s roster that could boast that? “Welcome Home” by Coheed and Cambria. Sonny’s celebration is very short-lived as he turns his head to the entryway. Just moments ago, he’d managed to overcome the greatest odds ever stacked against him, but now, he was to look to the future. Chandler Tsonda does nothing, says nothing, but calmly walks to the ring and comes face-to-face with the man that, much like himself, had been through physical hell to capture something near and dear to him A man that was battle-tested himself by winning two matches against an incredible gamut of credible opponents – each JitC-worthy in their own right – The Sultan of Style took a look at the future that awaited him in just two months time at Culture Shock. Sonny gazes once at his title, then back to Tsonda. Nick: CHANDLER TSONDA, OUR 2007 JEWEL IN THE CROWN WINNER IS WASTING NO TIME LOOKING ON AT THE PRIZE THAT HE NOW HAS A SHOT FOR AT CULTURE SHOCK! The two competitors lock eyes, both prepared to travel down two very distinct roads and arrive at the same destination. Nick: Ladies and gentlemen, thank you so much for inviting us into your homes and for joining us on another great Pay-Per-View broadcast with King of Kings! For Richard Parker this is Nick Stuart saying good night, Merry Christmas, and a Happy New Year! The final scene ends with neither man backing down from their respective stances. If this was the way 2007 was going to end, then 2008 was going to open up with one hell of a bang. FIN.
