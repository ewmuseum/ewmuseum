Since Danny Ferguson's declaration to avoid wrestling until the Dual Halo match on Night Two of the CS Pay-Per-View, the locker room was antsy. Plenty of people dedicate themselves to winning the coveted Golden Ticket, and with just weeks to go, the game always turns ugly.
