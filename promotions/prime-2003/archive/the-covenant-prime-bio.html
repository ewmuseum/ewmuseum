The Covenant Biography Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Other Feds RPW; tSC Other Fed Awards None Wrestling Style Moderated hybrid of both men's individual styles. Tully brings the power, striking, and submissions while Knight uses speed and high-flying artistry. The duo recently completed a lengthy tour of Japan (hence their extended PRIME absence) and have honed their tag prowess immensely. Finisher Name Ride of the Damned; Flight of the Damned (primary; see move list) Finisher Description 1) Each man stands to opposite sides of the opponent and grasp one of their arms, hoisting them up before spiking them into the mat in what can best be described as a double-team crucifix powerbomb or piledriver. Known to many fans as the Backseat Boyz' T-Gimmick maneuver. Won't be used on larger opponents as Ethan's size won't allow his contribution on bigger men. 2) Tully ascends to the 2nd or 3rd turnbuckle and Ethan hoists up the opponent to pass off to him as if for a super powerbomb. Tully holds them up as he stands to his full height, on the top if possible for maximum elevation. Knight then uses the top side rope as a springboard, leaping up and catching the captive opponent in an inverted hurricanrana Setup Move Any of their tag signature moves (except The Madness From Beyond) Setup Move Description See move list Special Moves See movset Move Set [b]UnCreation[/b] - Tully hoists the opponent into a position sitting atop his shoulders, grabbing the wrists for support. He falls to a sitting position, dropping the opponent forward and facefirst to the mat in an electric chair drop, just as Knight leaps up and catches them with his knees to drive them into the ribs/chest of the victim. [b]The Madness From Beyond[/b] - More of a series of quick and hard hitting attacks performed with precise teamwork. It starts with a German suplex by Tully, and as the opponent rolls backwards their limp body falls into the grip of a waiting Ethan Knight (he often grabs the back of their legs and pulls them into position for the next part himself). He pivots slightly before hoisting the victim into an inverted suplex. As the opponent falls, his legs are caught by Tully. Tully alters his positioning so that when the opponent falls, he's caught in a cutter (by Knight) and a gutbuster variation (by Tully). Knight springs to his feet and runs off the ropes while Tully holds the victim by the legs (with them still on their stomach), and Ethan delivers a somersault senton splash. He rolls and locks on a camel clutch just as Tully comes back with a sitting dropkick to the face. This technique may see alterations as time goes on, but that is its current incarnation. While brutal in its relentless execution, Tully and Knight always strike a long, arrogant pose immediately afterward and therefore remove almost any probability of gaining the win after this technique/combo. [b]Virtue Of The Vicious[/b] - Tully lifts the opponent up as if for a backdrop suplex, but then drops them forward to land in a sitting facefirst powerbomb. He stands while still holding their body, leaving them suspended with body running parallel to the mat (as if doubled over, slightly elevated). Ethan then lunges off the top rope and catches them in a falling DDT as Horace releases them. In the late stages of the match this can easily end things if no save is made. (NOTE: The facefirst bomb attack isn't part of the attack as much as it's the primary set-up. This spot is usually telegraphed by a slightly groggy Knight being near a corner at the same time that an opponent is mid-ring with Tully, most typically being caught after a missed clothesline attempt on the latter and driven down with the facefirst powerbomb.) [b]Nether-Bomb[/b] - Tully hoists the opponent up into a powerbomb with victim atop his shoulders. Knight, atop the turnbuckle or top side rope and behind the victim, grabs the victim's arms from behind and crosses them so they are straitjacketed. Tully pivots and delivers a fall-forward powerbomb while Ethan goes for the ride, keeping the arms in place as he helps spike the opponent into the mat. A variation of this sees Knight drive his knees into the back of the opponent instead of just going along for the ride, executing a Backcracker maneuver as opposed to a flat powerbomb. While not a move per se, Tully will use his own body as a launching pad for his daredevil partner as necessary (from his shoulders, off his back while on all fours, throwing him like a projectile >=), etc). [b]High Heavens Burning[/b] - Tully lifts the opponent onto his shoulders, both facing the same direction. Knight scales the top rope and stands behind Tully/victim. He leaps off the top and grabs the opponent in a tornado DDT just as Tully falls forward in an electric chair drop, driving the opponent to the mat. Another brutal attack that will quite likely end the bout if the pinfall attempt isn't broken up. [b]KT-Buster[/b] - Spiral double-team fisherman's buster. Another potential match-ender in the late stages. Won't be used on large opponents as Knight doesn't have the power to contribute as much. Ethan locks on a hangman's neckbreaker and Tully uses his hands to boost Ethan up and backflip into a DDT. This move is best described as a partner-assisted inverted Sliced Bread #2 (hangman's neckbreaker into DDT as opposed to 3/4 facelock into reverse DDT). This move is a common spot in their matches but yet officially untitled. Simply referred to as an assisted backflip DDT by commentators in small indies they've worked together. Entrance Music Either man's theme, randomly selected. If accompanied by Violence Jack, his is used.
