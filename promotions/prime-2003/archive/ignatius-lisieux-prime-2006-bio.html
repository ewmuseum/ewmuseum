Entrance: The arena is plunged into pitch-black as camera bulbs shoot off in a vain attempt to shed some light, electronic voices from all continents fade-into a jet-style whoosh and then into a cymbal splash complete with parallel white and pink pyrotechnic explosion as the fans rise to their feet and emit their very own sound explosion for “Surfing with the Alien' by Joe Satriani. Pink and white strobe lights slash through the arena as the entrance video for Ignatius Lisieux commences on the PRIME-a-TRON and the French-Canadian strolls confidently out onto the middle of the stage. Lisieux raises his arms into a crucifix pose as white explosions fire off down the centre of the ramp, the crowd gasp in shock and awe as the smoke begins to clear and the MVP marches down purposefully towards the squared circle. Once there, Lisieux hops up onto the apron and mounts the nearest turnbuckle to raise the crucifix pose once more as the lights are restored to the arena and Satriani’s wailing guitar fades to silence.
