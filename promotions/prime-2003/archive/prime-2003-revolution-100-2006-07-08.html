ReVolution 100 Michigan Staduim - Ann Arbor, Michigan ---- Revolution's Greatest Match - Angelo Deville vs. Xavier Kannon It began with desire. It was a different time, a different world. It was a different Universe. The legends of today walked a landscape without borders, browsing countries without curtains. Rather than a system of independent federations, there was a symbiotic alliance of federations under one system. The gladiators knew who championed the other arenas, and more importantly, they knew how and why. Each champion proclaimed dominance with the arrogant certainty of their own name, beating their proverbial chests with the titles they lorded, laying down vague challenges of supremacy they knew would never be answered. Despite the transparency between the kingdoms of the land, the walls they erected were impermeable, sectioned off by barriers of exclusivity. That was the rule, and it was adhered to as strictly as the pecking orders in the fallacious federations that honoured skill and talent as tertiary considerations. As with all rules, there were exceptions. The GTT extravaganzas. PCW Alliances. The Universal Title. When the braying of the empty boasters subsided, there were two men, like moving monolithic towers of unmitigated majesty, who reigned supreme. One of them was Angelo Deville. The other was Xavier Kannon. Their careers paralleled one another, from the bottom of the PWA to the apex of the Universe, from the shadows of obscurity to the gleaming envy of every man and woman who ever found themselves surrounded by ring-ropes. They were both derisively called The Chosen by those who never were. They were both called The Golden Child by painted pieces of coal. They were the best, and while there were those too consumed by ego to admit it publicly, there were none too deluded to deny it in their own mind. The inevitable showdown was written in the stars, outshining them, dulling them with jealousy. While other matches ascended with hype, theirs would transcend it - theirs would settle it to supernova dust. When the Universe bore witness to the collision at PCW Alliances, they believed they were watching the culmination of legend, but in reality it was an orchestration of succession. Xavier Kannon considered it a passing of the torch. Angelo Deville considered it the extinguishing of the light. Desire evolved to maniacal obsession. A chase began that would last for years, unrealized, pushing each man into retirement and crumbling entire federations underfoot. Kannon seemed intent on taunting Deville, retracing his steps the moment he set down a new path, and Deville seemed intent on his destruction, cutting off those paths with vicious brutality and unconscionable acts. In the ring, however, the only blows they exchanged were unsanctioned or verbal. It seemed the parallel was destined to persevere, always surging forward but never crossing. The match an entire Universe had yearned for was damned to remain a dream, lost in the annals of "what if" and "do you think" . . . Until now. The opening drums of Sympathy for the Devil swell the already standing masses to a cacophony of wild shouting, their cheering and jeering intermingling to create an indistinguishable pandemonium of noise. Crimson fireworks explode from cannons set about the stage to coincide with Mick Jagger's "Woo-woo!"'s as the instruments crescendo. Smoke from the blasts suffuses the entryway with a choking mist, a fog that takes on a look of phantom blood as the PRIME-a-tron is washed with red. Vince Howard: La-dies and gen-tle-men! It's TIME! Impossibly, the crowd grows louder. Please allow me to introduce myself . . . I'm a man of wealth and taste. I've been around for a long, long year . . . Stole many a man's soul and faith. Vince Howard: Our main event tonight has been years in the making, and has pulled from retirement one of the most celebrated combatants to ever grace our sport! After countless challenges and dogged pursuit failed, one million dollars has accomplished the otherwise unthinkable! Live from ReVolution in The Arrowhead Pond, PRIME is proud to present to you: The Million Dollar Match! Through the smoke a silhouette is spied, an Apollonian form striding with the arrogance and self-assurance of a beloved king in his home country. The red glow casts macabre projections dancing across the mist, shadows that reach out and chill the audience as they've swallowed countless competitors whole. Pleased to meet you . . . Hope you guessed my name! Vince Howard: Introducing first from parts unknown in Canada, he weighs in at two-hundred-forty-four pounds! He's known as The Devil's Don! The first-ever PTC Universal Champion! The Crimson Angel! He is . . . ANGEL-OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO DEEEEEEEEEEE-VIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIILLE! The haze swirls as The Deville throws his arms to the sides and twirls slowly in place, surveying with a satisfied eye the ocean of hungry, anticipative faces. They crave this contest almost as much as he himself, but where they are the unwitting scorn that made it nigh impossible, he is the man who made it happen, the harbinger of fate. He takes his time descending the ramp, knots of muscle rippling the Kannon-designed interpretation of his trademark tights with each destiny-preying step. A smile - not a smirk - dances across the sharp angles of his face, and in his azure eyes there shines an excitement typically reserved for the young and uninitiated. While his pace is slow, his pulse and breathing are not; The Deville is trembling, a coiled spring waiting to explode. He'd abandoned more than the majority of professional wrestlers achieve in their lifetime for the simple opportunity to have this match. But it wasn't enough. He'd won tournaments and toppled fables with one match as the ultimate goal, but it wasn't enough. He'd challenged, burned, framed, sabotaged, insulted, embarrassed, and extorted, but still, it wasn't enough. No amount of goading or taunting would pull his white whale to the surface, no amount of scheming or planning would let him realize his dream. People told him to give it up, pleaded with him to abandon it, but that isn't in The Deville's character. He can't give up. It's anathema to him. When it seemed his resources were exhausted, he shook out his sleeve and unveiled his trump card. For him, money is plentiful and easy to obtain, something so abundant he feels little more than contempt for it; for Kannon, it's a new lease on life, the gateway to the peaceful retirement of his fantasy. Deville's held back from making it this simple for the longest time, hoping against hope that they could meet through the spirit of candid competitiveness . . . but now that the offer is on the table, he doesn't feel cheated or cheapened in the slightest. Not like before. Not like in the aftermath of the charade. A million dollars for one match might seem excessive, but The Deville would part with ten times that amount without batting an eyelash. The money found Kannon's account as soon as his bank opened last Thursday morning. Deville won't miss it; it's the most fulfilling purchase he's ever made. He slides under the ropes as if plunging into the fountain of youth, springing up and to his feet in one smooth motion, ready to realize the dream he refuses to be denied. Pacing the ring like a lion on the prowl, he isn't just electrified, he's electricity, excitedly seeking a conductor. The fans are still shouting themselves hoarse, but the volume wavers briefly as The Deville's theme cuts off. It begins quietly somewhere in the upper rows and spreads like bacteria in a tepid pool, and in the ring Deville's smile grows appreciatively, knowing it's deserved, honoured to hear it reverberating all around him. "KANNON! KANNON! KANNON!" He's never before wrestled in a PRIME ring, but there isn't a fan in attendance who hasn't at least heard whispers of the legend, and most of them horde a collection of fond personal memories, stories of what they once witnessed that they'll animatedly unleash at the slightest provocation. "KANNON! KANNON! KANNON!" Mrs. Kannon-Hall leads the way onto the stage as Rock Is Dead foreshadows the long-awaited arrival, clashing with the harmonic chanting. Apprehension stains Eleanor's face like a layer of make-up when she spies Deville in the ring, but it washes away with a buoyant smile as she leaps into the air accompanied by a blue and white palisade of lights, volatile columns of brilliant energy that seem to suspend her at the focus. Vince Howard: And his opponent! Accompanied by his beautiful wife, Eleanor, he hails from Merlin Bay, Barbados, and weighs in at two-hundred-twenty-one pounds! He's carried more World Titles than Aimz has diseases! The final PTC Universal Champion! He is . . . X-AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA-VIER KAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAN-NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOON! Anger doesn't begin to describe the menagerie of baleful emotion seething from Kannon's entire being as he joins his wife on the stage. He hates being here. He loathes being introduced as Xavier Kannon, despises the cheering and adoration the name inspires. It isn't him; it's a gimmick he adopted, a puppet's cloth he wore, a false idol that left him perpetually dependent on pills for respite from the pain. He wishes they would forget about it so that he could grant himself the same reprieve. But one man is viciously intent on preventing that from happening. Kannon doesn't want to be here, but it's his only path to peace, the sole sojourn that will end The Deville's torment. He doesn't care about the fans chanting for him. He doesn't care about all the extra people tuning into ReVolution just to see him in action one last time. All he cares about is clearing away the sword that's dangled overhead for the last three years, then living peacefully with his family on the money this night has earned him. He wastes no time pandering as he strides purposefully down the rampway, unwavering eyes locked on Deville, his mind singularly focussed on how to end the match as quickly as possible. Eleanor tries to give him some advice, but he brushes her off and slides into the ring, already motioning for Roberts to ring the bell, still glaring across the ring as if at his damnation. The audience quiets to the fabled dull roar as the bell chimes and the two step forward from their respective corners, eyes locked in a duel to precede the hell their bodies will endure. Smiling with what can only be described as giddiness, Deville circles the man he superceded in wrestling's folklore, feigning a dash in with almost every step. Loosening up his shoulders, Kannon carries the stance of a man who's wrestled only five times in the last eighteen months, cold steel skittish over reacquainting with the forge. The Deville's flicking hands goad Kannon on, aided by the insatiable hunger in his eyes, while the out-of-retirement superstar licks his lips and buys one last moment to check his pads and tape. Nick: Are we on? Can you hear me? Richard: I've been able to hear you bellyaching this entire time, twit. Nick: Sorry for the silence, folks, but we seemed to be experiencing technical difficulties. Richard: Don't apologize. No one's upset about missing your trite observations. Heck, every night when I show up here, my highlight comes in the few moments before you sit down, with the hope that some calamity befell you that would prevent you from joining me. Nick: Thanks, Richard. That gets me right here. Anyway, whatever the problem was, we're back now, and just in time to call this match! I know you all witnessed it for yourselves, so instead of going back over it, this is the only mention I'll give to their electrified entrance! With a savage snarl Kannon surges suddenly forward like an irate rhino, drawing Deville to the centre of the ring, where they clash with a collar and elbow tie-up. The fans pop madly for the first joust, while Eleanor leans on the apron, hammering palms on the canvas to urge her husband on. Giving up both height and weight, Kannon is forced back, but drawing on the heart that made him famous, he digs his heels in, leaving Deville to force him downwards instead. Nick: And the battle of the titans has begun just as it should, with a traditional grapple in the centre of the ring! Deville is clearly the strong of the two, but wrestling is about oh so much more than strength! The muscles that once bulged through Kannon's skin work behind the scenes, forcing him back up, before he and the Crimson Angel shove the other off, declaring it a stalemate. Kannon loosens his neck while Angelo wipes his palms on his tights, making it look as if Kannon's perspiration forced him to accept the draw. Nick: Leave it to Deville to make an excuse! Richard: What are you talking about? Kannon's been sweating bullets since the challenge was made two weeks ago - maybe even three years ago! I'm surprised Deville was able to grab him at all! Spoiling the façade by flashing a respectful grin, Deville spreads his arms, daring Kannon to test his luck one more time. Once again, the legendary duo lock up, Kannon's desire to show up Deville allowing him to overcome the size deficit, spinning Deville into a hammerlock, wrenching at his arm. Kannon instinctively tries to hook Angelo's leg with his to keep him under control, but The Deville reacts too quickly, flipping him with a snapmare. Defying his very nature, stunning the crowd to silence, Deville resists the urge to decimate Kannon's exposed back. He looms over Kannon with his grin still etched in place, but instead of offering taunting words or a cheapshot, The Deville extends a hand to lever Kannon to his feet. Richard: Please be a trap . . . Please be a trap . . . Rolling backwards, Kannon's legs hook themselves around a shocked Deville's neck, then pull him over with a rolling headscissors. Nick: Looks like Deville had every intention of being the gentleman, but Kannon was quick to remind him that there's no place for gentlemen in the squared circle! As Deville rolls back to his feet, disgruntled, Xavier executes a kip-up that defies fans' expectations. Despite not having the agility he used to, Kannon's return to his feet leaves Deville frowning appreciatively. His narrowed eyes and tight smile tell the story on the off-chance Deville missed it: he doesn't need his charity. Shrugging it off in typical nonchalant fashion, Deville adjusts the waistband of his tights, then again takes to circling Kannon. Soaking in the expectation oozing from the crowd, Deville takes his sweet time with the predatory antics, chronicling every second distinctly in his mind. Kannon isn't so appreciative of the situation; with a grim look of determination, he cuts off The Deville's circling, lunging for another tie-up. Ducking smoothly under it, Deville pops up behind him, and when XK turns, sums up their relationship to this point with a sharp slap right across the mouth. "OOOOOOOOOH!" The warning smirk across Deville's thin lips doesn't take up residence for too long, however, before Kannon returns the favour, the impact echoing out over the hushed crowd. Fuming from the sting, Deville replies with a forearm, only for Kannon to absorb it, then light up the Crimson Angel's chest with a chop. Deville grimaces as he flings a right hand at Kannon's jaw, connecting, forcing the Barbados dweller to stagger back. Before Deville can press the advantage, Kannon launches into a roaring elbow that catches Angelo mid-step, knocking him down to the mat. As Deville sits up, Kannon surges forward with a knee that Angelo barely avoids by throwing himself back down. Kannon aims a stomp at the exposed chest, but Deville seizes his ankle and twists, then drives his shoulder into the back of Kannon's knee, collapsing him. The transition into an ankle lock is instinctive, but before Bernie Roberts can even begin to bend, Deville releases the hold and skips backward to give Kannon room. Obviously, he isn't looking to end the match. Nick: And again Deville backs off, letting Kannon regain the feeling of his surroundings! Whether he wants this to be a dream match or not, he'd better stop giving his opponent so many liberties! Richard: He shouldn't be giving any liberties! Break his back, Deville! Stop screwing around! Kannon levels him with a glare as he regains his feet, then grabs the top rope and yanks back on it, stretching as the crowd settles into rhythmic clapping. As they go to lock up again, Deville steals a pace on Kannon, taking him down with a fluid armdrag. Barring the arm, Deville pushes his shin down against Xavier's face, letting him know the pain he could be applying without actually enforcing it. Revelling in his dominance, Deville sneers and offers a few cutting words before Kannon manages to squirm loose, rolling to his feet once more. Nick: Since when is Deville so sporting? Richard: I don't know, but he'd better knock it off! I don't want to have to cheer on Kannon! Feeling it all coming back to him, absorbing the energy of the audience, drawing on the countless hours of tangling he's experienced, Kannon drops back to his knees as Deville advances, taking him over with a fireman's carry . . . but The Devil's Don again snatches the arm, this time putting pressure behind the armbar. Kannon favours Deville with a grin of his own as he simply stretches his right foot out, draping it over the bottom rope he senses is there. Nick: Some of the viewers at home probably think Kannon is rusty, but the presence of mind he's cultivated over the years isn't so easily clouded! With each passing second something old comes back to him and instinct begins to take over! Looking pleased that Kannon is rediscovering himself, Deville releases grandly and climbs to his feet, lifting his arms in forbearance as he steps back, once more gifting Kannon the chance to rise. As soon as Kannon's hand finds the ropes, however, Deville rings his ears with a surgical boot that knocks him temporarily senseless. Slapping the apron with outrage, Eleanor tells Angelo exactly what she thinks of him as he pushes his boot down into the back of her husband's neck, choking him on the middle rope. Roberts pinwheels his arm in front of Deville's face, counting the five, and Deville milks every nanosecond he can, inciting a warning before finally backing off to let Kannon rise. Nick: So much for sportsmanship! I guess Deville figures Kannon's warmed up enough now! Richard: It's about time! Holding his throat and sucking in some deep breaths, Kannon pulls himself up, nodding at Deville as if to say he'd have done the same thing. Flourishing an indulgent bow, Deville then motions for a test of strength in the middle of the ring, which Ellie rants at her husband to refuse. Never one to listen to reason, Kannon looks at the hand, then raises his own, whipping his head around to poll the crowd. Nick: What a deafening reaction! There's no doubt the fans want to see it, and frankly, so do I! The two slink forward eyeing the other with tangible distrust, fingers wriggling, until they're face to face. Deville proffers a lordly grin as their hands fuse together, then raises his other arm high overhead. Looking Deville right in the eye, Kannon begins lifting his hand to meet it, then stabs suddenly forward, driving the fingers into Deville's left socket. Nick: What?! It's Kannon with the cheap shot! The fans pop for the usually jeer-worthy act, cheering on Kannon as he follows the eye gouge with a string of forearms. Nick: And now he's pressing the advantage, knocking Deville back across the ring! Grabbing Deville's wrist, Kannon whips the Crimson Angel into the ropes, then meets him with a running forearm that fells him. As if he's allergic to the canvas, Deville shoots back up and at Kannon, only to be taken over with a falling hiptoss. With Deville on his back, Kannon grabs both of his arms and entwines them as Deville rises, crossing them behind his neck. Before Angelo can even try to squirm free, Kannon drops onto his back, raising both knees so that they embed into Deville's spine upon impact. Seeing Kannon back in full flow raises the volume of the crowd as they watch him transition into a full nelson, rolling Deville over onto his front and trying to yank his upperbody off the mat. Deville squirms an arm free, but Kannon spins around from the half-nelson he managed to keep on, converting it to a front facelock. Deville surges up with a growl, finally losing himself in the dance, but in so doing allows Kannon to switch into a double underhook. Deville bucks his body, trying to lift Kannon over, only for XK to step through and grapevine the leg. Flinging himself back, he plants Deville's forehead into the canvas with a bridging DDT. The fans pop for the crude move, while Kannon hurriedly rolls Deville over and hooks the far leg. One! . Tw-NO! Nick: That pinfall never had a chance, but it seems to have awoken The Deville! Thrashing around under Kannon, Deville breaks free and bails to the outside, killing the momentum while also letting Kannon revel in the adoration. Normally he'd be angry, but The Deville is smiling as he circles around the ring, shaking the cobwebs clear . . . until he looks up and finds himself staring into the beaming face of Eleanor Kannon-Hall, who, in a throwback to Ellie~!, favours him with a condescending raspberry. Nick: There's certainly no love lost there, Richard. No matter what sort of relationship Angelo envisions having with Xavier, it's safe to say that his wife will never forget the scheming, not to mention her public dismissal! Richard: Who cares what she forgets or remembers? Deville used her to get what he wanted, and, well, he got what he wanted. After blowing her a derisive kiss, Deville hops onto the apron with his knee, but Kannon is already there to meet him, driving a boot through the ropes into Deville's gut. Deville slumps forward over the middle cable as Kannon speeds across the ring and rebounds off the ropes, returning a second later to deliver a solid knee to Deville's forehead. A stunned Angelo grabs onto the ropes to avoid tumbling to the floor, but Kannon sees an opportunity and isn't about to pass it up. Testing his agility, Kannon drops out over the top rope, seizing Deville and prying him from the ropes with a sunset flip powerbomb. Nick: Oh! Did you see that?! Kannon just flipped out of the ring like a Vergomov! Screwing his face up, Deville feels the impact shudder up his spine from the concrete through the thin mats. Nick: Whatever respite Deville was offering Kannon in the beginning, the favour isn't being returned! It was Deville who made the match happen, but at this point Kannon's taking it much more seriously! Richard: I've figured it out. The Deville's just screwing around. He wants Kannon to feel he's doing well. Eleanor doesn't bother to hide her delight as Deville bridges in pain, but she obeys her husband's command to back around the corner. Deville crawls to the rail, pulling himself up, only for Kannon to connect with a vicious yakuza kick to the side of his head, slumping Angelo over the barricade. Nick: He almost decapitated Deville with that kick! The Devil's Don is down and loopy! Richard: Pfft. Lining Deville up, Kannon flings himself into a roaring forearm, only for Deville to jab a boot out, connecting with his ribs. Having followed Kannon's lead in succumbing to instinct, Deville slaps on a front facelock that he quickly transitions, taking Kannon swiftly over with a snap suplex that jackknifes his back across the scantly padded blockade. "OOOOOOOOOH!" Nick: No! Please no! Kannon's injury is as well-kept a secret as Nicole Brown-Simpson's real killer, and as he topples unmoving to the floor, an eerie quiet descends in the wake of the snap and ensuing moan. Abject horror washes over Deville's face as he realizes what he's done, and Eleanor's shriek easily carries to the uppermost rows without the aid of a microphone. Eleanor Kannon-Hall: You BASTARD! The venomous accusation is the last of the attention she pays him, turning her worry instead to her husband, collapsing on him, cupping his head in her hands. Deville staggers back, stupefied with disbelieving revulsion, slowly shaking his head in a soundless apology that he knows can never be accepted. He didn't want this. All he wanted was to earn the respect of a mentor, to engage in honest competition with one of the few men who truly awed him in the ring, the only man he ever strived to emulate and measure up to. He would give anything, risk anything, to achieve the dream, but he never imagined it would end like this. Nick: Please tell me we're not seeing what I think we're seeing . . . It was Kannon's injured back that forced him out of the sport years ago, and while his heart brought him back time and time again, everyone knew it was only a matter of time if he kept subjecting it to jeopardy . . . Richard: I told you the cripple should have stayed home. Nick: Richard! What the hell is wrong with you?! Adrenaline flees Deville's body in a cold, sickening wave, supplanting the excited shaking with trembling wrought of uncompromising terror. Kannon's eyes are clamped shut, and so seem his ears as his wife's cries rain down on him without effect. Deville can't hear what she's saying, but the agony in her voice compounds his own. She doesn't deserve this. Christian certainly doesn't deserve this. He should have listened to them and ignored himself. He should have crippled that little voice in the back of his head that demanded he find out, that niggling uncertainty and insecurity that compelled him to make the match happen at any cost. Instead he's crippled his better and turned his dream into a nightmare; he's incurred a cost that is too much to bear. A million dollars wouldn't buy back Christian his mobility, his independence. It wouldn't- Eleanor cries out with delight as Kannon's eyes flutter open and he tries to sit up. She falls on him again, pleading with him to stay down, but with a kiss to assure her he's okay, he guides her off of him and climbs to his feet, twisting his torso to test his limberness. Nick: Thank god! I thought we'd witnessed a tragic end, but it seems the bullet has been dodged! "KANNON! KANNON! KANNON!" Deville has to remind himself to breathe as Kannon's eyes fall on him. They should be filled with hate, with loathing, but instead his eternal rival seems to be measuring. Angelo Deville: Christian . . . Clearing his throat in a vain attempt to strengthen his voice, The Deville takes a step toward XK, reaching out tentatively to assure him there's no attack coming. Angelo Deville: Let's stop this. It's not worth it. I . . . I'm sorry I brought you here. I'm sorry for everything. All I wanted was the experience you'd granted so many others, the thrill of . . . it's not important. Not as important as your . . . it's not important. I'm sorry, Christian. With the crowd chanting and cheering in the background, Kannon regards Deville implacably, his only movement the periodic blinking of his eyes. Eleanor's consoling hand finds her husband's shoulder, telling him it's alright as her whispers urge him to accept the offer. "KANNON! KANNON! KANNON!" Angelo opens his mouth to speak again, but no words come out; none have the chance. Eleanor staggers back with a knowing, bitten off curse as Kannon's fist crashes into Deville's pearly whites, knocking him back against the ring. "KANNON! KANNON! KANNON!" Spitting blood from a severely lacerated lip, Deville brings a shaky hand up to his mouth, running his thumb along the inside, where his upper teeth sliced an half-inch-long gash. Xavier Kannon: Don't worry about me, Angelo; my back is fine. Worry about yourself. Despite the shock, Deville flashes a crimson grin as Kannon lunges forward with a knee, dodging at the last second, grabbing the back of Kannon's head and using his momentum to whip him into the ringpost. Stumbling back from the impact, Kannon finds Deville all too agreeable, snagging and planting him with an inverted DDT. Nick: I don't know what that exchange was about, but the fight's back on! Eleanor surges forward and pounds her fists against Deville's broad back, but it's water to a duck's. He spins into her, his grin still evident, and scoops her onto a shoulder, carrying her to the barricade and setting her in the front row. She stammers indignantly, but he silences her with an admonishing finger and a deadly glare. Whether she likes it or not, this is between him and her husband alone. Nick: That's one way to remove a manager from the equation, though I don't imagine the effects will be lasting! Richard: Security! Security! Check that woman for a ticket! The distraction is enough to give Kannon time to rise, and as Deville turns back XK laces him with another pulverizing right hand, then smashes his face on the apron and rolls him into the ring. Deville reels smoothly to his feet and dances back from the ropes, granting Kannon one final reprieve as he slides in behind him. Nick: With blood pouring from his mouth and Kannon seemingly in perfect health, it's safe to say that Deville is done teasing! He's dead serious now, realizing the match he's waited a lifetime for! The crowd goes wild as the staredown begins anew. The smiles are gone, replaced by dogged determination, forgotten in the glory of battle that should have overwhelmed all else from the get-go. Eleanor skitters back over the barrier with a choice finger for The Deville, but he doesn't notice as he and Kannon dance a slow concentric circle, locked in provisional stalemate. Kannon makes the first move, launching himself into the ropes, tearing back toward Deville, who waits in the centre of the ring. They explode together, trying to bull the other over, but each man simply staggers back, neither falling. A brief grudging glare, then Angelo follows suit, and again the two detonate in a flailing of arms, neither going down, neither getting the better. Nick: Like glaciers colliding, neither man budges! Simultaneously they launch into opposite ropes, Kannon ducking a clothesline as they pass, then trying for one of his own. His arm grazes the back of Deville's head as The Crimson Angel twists, tangling his arm around Kannon's, spinning into his back with a roar and the rapid succession of brutal neck shots that complete the Tommy Gun. Richard: Lower! Punish the back! He converts it to a full nelson, looking for the slam, but Kannon reaches back and hooks his head with both arms, kicking his legs out to shatter Deville's jaw on his crown. Scrambling to their feet, they collide again, Deville trying for an Irish whip but finding it reversed. Deville goes airborne as Kannon steps into him, heaving him overhead with a belly-to-belly suplex that rattles the ring. Kannon is on him immediately, burying his hand in Deville's mop of blonde hair, pulling him to his feet. A quick neckbreaker is followed by a whip into the corner, followed by a clothesline, then Kannon is scaling his way to the middle rope, thrusting a fist high for the crowd to cheer before bringing it down on The Deville's temple. Nick: Kannon's back in the groove now, getting the crowd behind him with this series of shots! Angelo looks like a bobblehead! Excitedly counting along with the flurry of falling right hands, the crowd is silenced with another moan as Deville pitches into Kannon, cradling his back, rushing out of the corner with a hellacious spinebuster. Kannon tries scrambling back out of range along the mat, but the ravenous Deville hooks his ankles and turns him, locking him in the agony of a Boston crab at the dead centre of the ring. Nick: You've got your wish, Richard! Deville's generosity has gone the way of the dodo as he targets Kannon's weakness! Richard: It's about time!
