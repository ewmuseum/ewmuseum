Move Set Rare finisher: GRAND FINALE~! (Somersault leg drop / Shooting star press elbow drop combo) Signature Tag Moves: High Time (Double Suplex) Roman Rings (Double 619 kick) Flashing Light (Double Super kick) Igor Arefiev: Wrestling Style: High flying show off In-Ring: Igor is a liability in-ring. He is crazy and manic. He never rests unless his body forces him. He has had no formal wrestling training and will use the ropes to his advantage being a trapeze artist and all. Igor will jump to the top rope, never climb and always springboard off, except in the case of the High Wire moves. He will attempt to fight half of the match with high flying moves. Igor realises he will rarely, if ever, be bigger and stronger than his opponents so his aim to wear them out. Run them around and then use their weight to crash to the mat. Running DDT’s and neck breakers are the order of the day. He will never punch. It isn’t worth it. But his kicks are stiff and hurt. They are generally aimed at the hip region except when delivering a super kick to the chin. Igor can take numerous bumps. He has fallen lots of times as a trapeze artist and high wire walker and can take a lot of impacts. To out do each other, both wrestlers will try to pull off each other’s finishers if the chance arises. This is only if the match is of importance and they want to rub it there partner’s face. DO’S and DON’TS: - Will only pin after his finisher. Never attempts a quick win. And try not to have people kicking out of La Nouba just yet. - will tap. Submission training was productive for Igor and he feels comfortable trying them but cannot survive them. - will never try to beat someone at their own game. He won’t try submission holds on a mat technician. He has a game plan, stick to it. - will never cheat. NEVER. NEVER. Common Moves: Every kind of DDT possible, especially a crucifix DDT, Springboard dropkick, Lionsault to standing opponents, hip kick, knee kick, leg sweep, drop toe hold, corkscrew moonsault, springboard leg drop, springboard elbow drop, springboard everything. Basic submission moves, eg armbar, sleeperhold. Signature Moves: High Wire #1 (Walks along the top rope before cart wheeling in mid air to land on the opponent’s neck with his leg driving it into the mat.) High Wire #2 (Walks along the top rope before cart wheeling into the downed opponent with a leg drop.) No Strings Attached (While running at the opponent, Igor uses their body to run “up” them, placing a foot on their knee and spring boarding himself onto their shoulders. There, while completing a somersault the springboard began; he grabs their head and brings them down in a neck breaker.) Wheel In Motion (Cartwheel super kick) Set-Up Move: Crucifix DDT / No Strings Attached Finisher: La Nouba (Somersault leg drop) Constantino Zergolov: Wrestling Style: High flying show off In-Ring: Constantino is an amateur in-ring. He is careful yet still manic. He will never rest for fear of being caught by the bigger opponent and thrown around. He has had no formal wrestling training and will use the ropes to his advantage being a specialised trapeze artist. However he likes to survey things from the top rope and will rest himself for a second before leaping off. He will jump to the ropes but steady himself before hitting a move. He will attempt to fight half of the match with high flying moves. Constantino realises he will rarely, if ever, be bigger and stronger than his opponents so his aim to wear them out. Run them around and then use their weight to crash to the mat. Running DDT’s and neck breakers are the order of the day. Constantino has a tendency to fight like-for-like unlike his tag partner. If a mat technician tries submission holds, Constantino will try to out do them. He will always fail. He will never punch. It isn’t worth it. But his kicks are stiff and hurt. They are generally aimed at the hip region except when delivering a super kick to the chin. Constantino can take numerous bumps. He has fallen lots of times as a trapeze artist and can take a lot of impacts. To out do each other, both wrestlers will try to pull off each other’s finishers if the chance arises. This is only if the match is of importance and they want to rub it there partner’s face. DO’S and DON’TS: - always likes an early pin. He will try to finish matches early. - throws his whole body into the pin. Will himself in the opponent hooking both legs and then pressing heavy on the chest. - will tap. Submission training didn't go well and he fears submission moves and will panic to get out of them. - will never cheat. NEVER. EVER. Common Moves: Every kind of DDT possible, especially tornado DDT, stiff kick to hip, stiff kick to knee, moonsault cross body, jumping cross body, dropkick, jumping dropkick, roundhouse kick, every kind of neck breaker Signature Moves: No String Attached (While running at the opponent, Constantino uses their body to run “up” them, placing a foot on their knee and spring boarding himself onto their shoulders. There, while completing a somersault the springboard began; he grabs their head and brings them down in a neck breaker (Generally Igor's move but Constantino has been known to perform it)) Ayala Memory (Dudley Dog) Simet Memory (RKO from a running start) Kalinin Troupe (Stardust Press – Frog Splash Combo) Set-Up Move: No Strings Attached / Tornado DDT Finisher: Los Quiros (450 Elbow splash) Rare Finisher: Grand Quiros (Double somersault splash) ENTRANCE: The arena is plunged into complete darkness. Feedback. Quick guitar riff. 'Plug In Baby' by Muse kicks in with a thunderous roar as Igor Arefiev and Constantino Zergolov spring onto the stage. The Russian pair run down the stage and then jump cleanly onto the ring apron and somersault in unison over the top rope. Then, after climibing on opposite top ropes, the pair backflip towards each other landing back-to-back in the centre of the ring.
