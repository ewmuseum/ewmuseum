UltraViolence 2007 Scottrade Center: St. Louis, Missouri ---- And So It Begins... The PRIME logo fades to a first-person view of the streets of ancient Rome. Street-traders flood the air with their promises of far-flung exotic goods from the most distant corners of the Empire, while duelling strings fight for attention, and whatever reward their music may receive from entertained passers by. "In ancient Rome, the Coliseum was the site of gladiatorial combat." The view then leaves the streets, thrusting high towards the clear blue sky to join a flock of birds overhead, and their view of the Coliseum below. Even hundreds of feet above the battle, the sound of iron on iron cuts the air, broken only by curdling screams as iron slices flash. "Fighters renowned throughout the Empire entertained tens of thousands of blood-thirsty spectators, their swords and spears glistening with blood as they hovered over their fallen opponent, awaiting the verdict of the thumb." Dive-bombing down into a sweep of the arena, the victorious combatant tosses his helmet aside and plants his foot into his maimed victim’s chest. The thumb turns down, and so does his sword thrust. "But tonight, it won’t be the noble warriors of the Coliseum that PRIME’s wrestlers will pay homage to…" Packed like veal calves into the stadium stands, football supporters unleash a barrage of flares, staining the air a violent red as their banners unfurl. "In modern Rome, the stadiums are the site of visceral combat." Shields clutched tightly, perspex visors down, riot police lead a cavalry charge of batons down the stand, ploughing into the sea of fans below. "Hooligans infamous throughout a nation, vilified by millions but hero-worshipped by others, their raw knuckles wet with blood as they indulge their most basic, dominant, aggressive instincts." Their standards and banners still flying, bare fists and steel toe-caps do battle against baton and shield, passion waging war against order as the flare smoke swirls amidst them. "ULTRAS." Head split open by a baton blow, blood cascading down his face, an Ultra spits crimson mucus out along with a tooth, snarling as he throws his head back and joins back in with the chanting that bellows out from the stand. "And tonight, it is these modern gladiators that PRIME’s wrestlers will resemble, as frustrations are vented, scores are settled, betrayals are punished, and hate is unleashed… …in UltraViolence." As the shot zooms in on ripped skin, split lips, black eyes and missing teeth, the view is bleached by fierce pyro explosions, acting as a screen wash that takes us to the interior of the Scottrade Centre.
