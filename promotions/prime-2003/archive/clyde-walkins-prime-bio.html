Clyde Walkins Biography Nicknames Clyder-Man (alter-ego), the Clyde of Clydes, One of a Clyde Hometown Winnipeg, Manitoba Birthday January 9th, 1961 Height 6'2' Weight 310 Fed Titles PRIME Tag Champion (w/Farwell), PRIME Universal Champion Fed Awards 5th Place Wrestler of the Week (ReVolution 83), 1st Place Wrestler of the Week (ReVolution 85), First Man in PRIME to Defeat Tchu (Yes, I'm listing that as an award, because I am that petty. Oh, Tchu was at nine wins when it happened, too), 4th Place Wrestler of the Week (ReVolution 86) Biography Clyde is a black hole of copyright infringement and general stupidity, except now that's all wrapped in 300+ pounds of buttery lard. Other Feds OSW, RPW Other Fed Awards OSW Tag Champion (w/Farwell), RPW Tag Champion (w/Farwell) Wrestling Style 75% fat guy stumbling around the ring, 5% actual technical wrestling knowledge, 20% using his weight to hurt his opponent Finisher Move Cyclyde Finisher Description In one fluid motion, the opponent is lifted off his feet in a double chickenwing, before being swung out and spun around into an elevated Reverse Underhook DDT - in other words, it's an elevated Unprettier Finisher Setup Move Clyde Crusher (Three-quarter face lock bulldog) Setup Description Three-quarter face lock bulldog Special Moves Suiclyde is Painless (Second rope Samoan drop) Other Moves Clyde Effect (Side Effect) Scoop Slam into Leg Drop Back Suplex Standing Elbow Drop Sweet Ball Music (Clyde takes a pounding and then comes back off the ropes with an 'unexpected' flying forearm. He heads to the nearest corner and begins warming up the band as the fans come to their feet, before delivering a Superkick straight to his opponent's groin as he stands and turns to face Clyde.
