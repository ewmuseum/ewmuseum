N Biography Nicknames The Terrorist Hometown Unknown Birthday Unknown Height Unknown Weight Unknown Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography N is considered by the majority of PRIME's staff, including some of the locker room, to be a menace and a nuisance. Appearing first on Revolution 110, he has run a short campaign of propaganda for PRIME to become more sportsmanlike and stop cheating. Angered by the use of referee distractions or hidden weapons, N called for the fans to rally against what he called a robbery of their money to see a cheating constest, falsely advertised as wrestling. In an effort to pacify him, as well as challenging his manliness at the same time, Chet Worth pardoned him of his previous transgressions if he was willing to join the company as an active wrestler and prove that he could survive without trying to bend the rules. N accepted, in return for the promise that any non-wrestling staff give him his privacy. N is now considered a part of PRIME's roster, but the enmity he gained in his previous acts may prove to make him wish he had never become so... Other Feds Not applicable Other Fed Awards Not applicable Wrestling Style Absolute fairness. Will not cheat under any circumstances. EVER. Finisher Move The N-Terrogator Finisher Description With the opponent laying face down, N bends their knees upwards and sits behind the opponent, scissoring their legs with his. Getting into a kneeling position, he takes the opponent's arms by the wrists and pushes them forwards, toward the head. N will then use his body weight to tip forward, applying immense pressure to the victim's upper arms and shoulders while keeping their legs trapped.
