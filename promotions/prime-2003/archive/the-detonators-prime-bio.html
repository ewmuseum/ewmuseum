The Detonators Biography Biography The Detonators were brought together for the first time in a wrestling ring in the spring of 2004 although rumors have swirled about them having worked together prior to that as professional polleros, or coyotes, smuggling Mexican nationals into the United States. Despite the fact that some long-time fans suspect that TNT is actually an American and not Mexican, they have achieved a level of folk-hero status in their home country due to their backgrounds that has inspired legions of fanatical supporters akin to Robin Hood’s Merry Men. The so-called Los Bambitos, the largest and most organized of the "ultra" supporter groups, is a motley collection of 30 fans that travel to every Dets appearance in six decomposing 1968 Volkswagen microbuses. The Bambitos usually gobble up ringside seats and act on The Dets’ behalf in any way possible, provoked or not. Despite the differences in height and weight, the masked TNT and Dynamite dress in identical ring attire and have mastered the no-tag swap out. They have worked together for so long and know each other so well that they have come to understand that things don’t always work out inside the ring despite the best of planning and so will never sell being unhappy with the other member in the ring whatever the incident. They also understand that past achievements mean nothing and they are looking to use their opportunity in PRIME to continue a long tradition of great PTC tag teams from Civil Disobedience to Havoc to American Ronin by working hard and keeping their heads down. TNT has never been seen inside or out of the ring without a mask on. Some speculation there is that he’s paranoid about possible illegal activity in his past and prefers the mystery of the mask. Others just think he’s a weirdo. Dynamite on the other hand does remove his mask when not in the ring like a normal person. The Detonators were discovered by former PCW wrestler and successful Arizona cattle rancher Jim Surge while Surge was on a long bender mourning the early retirement of his half-brother and tag team partner due to injury. Even though Surge doesn’t always see eye-to-eye with his young charges (Dynamite in particular) they know they have his unyielding support both strategically and financially. Surge loves to work the backstage scene while TNT and Dynamite usually keep focused on in-ring business and do what their manager suggests. Surge and TNT have a better relationship than Surge and Dynamite due to Dynamite’s hyper, reactionary personality. Surge has at times in the past undercut Dynamite by bringing ideas to TNT prior to presenting it to both Detonators at the same time, as is the unspoken code. When The Dets are lacking for motivation Surge has no problem turning the heat on himself to get a spark from his team. The Detonators travel from show to show in a luxury tour bus paid for and maintained by Surge. The bus is a two-story, 50-foot-long Wanderlodge SuperKing, with two 40" LCD TVs, a musical horn that plays Bad Religion’s "Big Bang" (The Dets’ entrance music), a whirlpool and fitness center, and on the upper level full sleeping and washing quarters. Los Bambitos follow the gleaming black bus in their ratty old Volkswagen busses pretty much everywhere it goes. Dynamite is the most friendly member of the Detonators group towards Los Bambitos, going out of his way to secure them front row seats to matches and provide them with other signs of appreciation for their unflagging dedication. Surge and TNT don’t avoid Los Bambitos but certainly don’t go out of their way to do anything for them. They basically treat them as fans. TIMELINE July, 2005 - Contract signed with World Wrestling Alliance. Made PTC debut on WWA Meltdown. It is a successful debut, ending the WWA careers of Scottywood and John Hitchin in the process with a piledriver off the ring apron to Hitchin. August, 2005 - Jim Surge famously appears from under the ring dressed as "Nitro, the third Detonator," in a clash against the legendary duo Havoc. February, 2006 - The Detonators achieve their biggest victory to date at Total Meltdown, winning the WWA tag straps from 5-time champion and long-time nemesis Havoc. Spring, 2006 - TNT and Dynamite dump Jim Surge publicly and then, directionless, they are forced into a period of inactivity. TNT begins the process of repairing The Dets' relationship with their former manager. Fall, 2006 - Unknown to the public TNT and Dynamite are back in training under Surge. January, 2007 - Surge forces The Dets to crawl back to him publicly. To further the paybacks he devises a plot for them to humiliate themselves working a horrible Russian gimmick as the comedy team Tempered Steel (Anvil and Hammer). The plot backfires when Tempered Steel are wildly popular and win their match in a squash at Rage in the Cage X. May, 2007 - Surge and The Detonators announce that they had let their WWA contracts expire and that they had signed with PRIME. BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Four M-80 firecrackers, the equivalent of a full stick of dynamite, startle the crowd out of their chatter or whatever had been occupying their attention. The words "The Detonators" appear on the screens in stylized red letters on top of a continuing loop of explosions as Bad Religion's classic "Big Bang" blares over the PA. 30 Mexican fans seated up at ringside wearing sombreros, panchos and moustaches stand up and begin to throw their sombreros into the air with loud whoops and shouts. The Detonators get a bit of heat from the crowd and one or two cheers from those twisted people that love those for whom no love is deserved. Big bang, big crunch, you know there's no free lunch. Kneel down and pray, here comes your judgment day. Big crunch, you know, it's gonna be quite a show. What comes around always goes around, yeah. Out through the backstage curtain step TNT & Dynamite with their arms raised and outstretched and they are greeted with a casual chorus of booing fans. The Detonators’ manager Jim Surge walks out silently behind them and the three pose for a moment before Surge makes his way slowly down to the ringside area, taking some time to taunt the fans a bit as he goes. The Detonators charge down the ramp past him and slide through the ropes and up onto their feet in the ring. Vince Howard: Ladies and gentlemen, introducing, at a combined weight of 515 pounds, from Veracruz and Mexico City, Mexico respectively and accompanied to the ring by their manager Jim Surge, they are TNT and Dynamite – The Detonators!
