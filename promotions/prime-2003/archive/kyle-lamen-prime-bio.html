Fed Awards Weekly Rankings: #2 [ReV 86], #3 [ReV 71] Biography 'Get busy living, or get busy dying.' --Kyle Lamen The following is taken from: USA Today Sports Section, January 1, 2007. Young Wrestling Superstar Passes Away by Pro Wrestling Correspondent Mark Salada The wrestling world lost on of it's best and brightest young stars yesterday, as former industry stalwart and rising superstar Kyle Lamen died on an operating table yesterday afternoon. Kyle was only 25 years of age, and is survived by his father, retired wrestler Johnny Lamen, his mother, his half-sister, and, by some reports, one child. Lamen was entrenched in the wrestling business since high school, being trained New Jersey's famous Core Wrestling Academy by his longtime friend and mentor, “The Jersey Jackhammer” Jason Core. While Core was unavailable for comment, reporters have seen him visibly grieving over the young man he treated as a son. Originally going by the moniker “Kyle Velocity,” Lamen enjoyed success to some extent or another in many of the important federations of his time. Making his television debut at the height of wrestling juggernaut Primetime Championship Wrestling's dominance, he later moved on to cult phenomenon Cyberspace Championship Wrestling, and the immensely popular, albeit short-lived 2002 incarnation of Five Star Wrestling. After a two to three year sabbatical, he returned to the sport at PRIME's Colossus 2 event, where he has competed until his death, in addition to short stints with Core Wrestling, and the newest incarnation of CCW, as well as competing in some of the major non-federated events of 2006. Over the last year, Kyle Lamen was made aware of a cancerous tumor sitting precariously on his brain stem, and decided to put off the dangerous surgery until the afternoon of December 31. He continued to wrestle professionally to the very end, with his last match being at PRIME's Great American Nightmare 2006, where he was presented with one of the most touching farewells in the history of this business. Kyle's surgery was set to be held at a clinic in New Hampshire, near the site of the controversial Arovnik Institute for Pain Management. During the removal of the tumor, Kyle Jonathan Cameron Lamen died on the operating table, There is no word as of yet if a tribute show is being planned, and there will be a public funeral held at Bevis Funeral Home in Tallahassee, Florida, near the home of Mr. Lamen's family. While he has a strong technical background, this is primarily used for escaping holds, creating reversals, and giving him the balance needed to perform some technically proficient impact moves. No submissions. Ever. Strikes are kicks, forearms, and elbow strikes, unless we're finishing a feud. Are we? Tends to play the face-in-peril a lot. Lives and dies on crowd reactions.
