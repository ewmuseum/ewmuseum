Tyler O'Neill Biography Nicknames Tye, TO, Quiet Guy Hometown San Diego, CA Birthday March 15, 1983 (22) Height 5'11 Weight 195 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography All people know is that one day, this kid that looks like he could model, is seen in a wrestling ring. Sights were made and labels were created for Tyler, yet no one knows where he came from; and keeping it that way has only made this enigma more interesting. They soon found him in the RPC hosted tournament, Face Off where he was defeated in the 3rd round by 'The Ravishing Jew Destroyer' Mitch Goldstein. After his defeat, he was never seen or heard from. Some say he quit, some think he committed suicide because of his troubled past, but the majority of people know not to assume anything from Tyler because he is incredibly unpredictable. Other Feds LOW (Annihilation), VW (Icon), 360WE (Skylar Brooks) Other Fed Awards LOW: World Champion, Xtreme Champion (x2), 1 of 4 Hall Of Fame Inductees. VW: Tag Team Champion (w/ James Friend) Wrestling Style High Flyer / Technical Specialist Finisher Move The Shining Guardian Finisher Description With the opponent lying face up on the mat, Tyler would quickly jump up onto the top rope and with no hesitation whatsoever, with his back to the opponent, Tyler performs a backflip and corkscrew combination that looks phenomenal through the air, and lands on the opponent with both of his elbows and knees in the opponents neck/ abdomen section. He then goes for a cradling pin. Finisher Setup Move The Skyline Setup Description Tyler runs towards the ropes and jumps up to the top rope and ‘springs’ backwards over the standing opponents shoulders and on his way down, grabs the opponent by his head and pulls him straight down and backwards hard to the mat with the opponents neck striking the ground first. Tyler would land on his ass in a sitting position with his legs extended. Can also be used for a pin combination. Other Moves Rare Move: Fateless (Possibly the most sadistic move of Tyler O’Neill is rarely used, but has been a trademark of Tyler’s since the origination of growing up fighting on the streets of San Diego. The opponent is fatigued and sitting in the corner of the ring either by collapsing or very few times, Tyler dragging them there. Tyler would then take two steps backwards and then very quickly rush forward and kick his opponent square in the face with the bottom of his boot with great speed and force and the opponents head would snap back into the ring post knocking him out cold. Tyler wouldn’t even bother pinning. It’s moves like this that make people curious of what goes through Tyler’s head at times.) --- Other Moves Coming Soon. Entrance Music 'Your Sword Vs. My Dagger' by Silverstein
