Chet Worth Biography Nicknames CASH Worth, Worth Millions, Worth While, The Worth Hometown Cleveland, Ohio Birthday January 7, 1963 Height 6'3' Weight 271+ Fed Titles None Fed Awards 2006 Dual Halo Winner. Other Feds GCW, RPW, FSW, tSC Other Fed Awards Jeff's Original Bitch. Wrestling Style Brawler/Bully Finisher Move The Redemption Finisher Description Vertical Hanging Suplex to DDT (Lifts to the top, holds it, brings down face first into a DDT type move) Finisher Setup Move The Retribution Setup Description A gut buster. Other Moves Trademarks Say Uncle: Crucifix Pin setup, but, rather than a pin, Worth gives his opponent a wet willy. As they struggle, he turns it into an armbar, and continues the ear assault. Purple Nurple: From behind, Worth grabs the nipples of his opponent, and twists them. As he does, he'll lift under the armpits, and and throw the opponent backwards, usually into his knee. Repeatedly. Show Off : Used against a smaller opponent, and usually as a setup for a bigger spot to fall victim to, Worth will back his opponent into the corner, and do one of three things: 1) He'll pull them in and out of the corner, pushing them back into the turnbuckle. There's no reason why, and there's nothing he's setting up for here. He's just bullying. 2) If he's been angered by his opponent, he'll chop them, like the Big Show does, but, not quite as loud. He will however, make a show of it. 3) If he's really pissed, he'll go Stone Cold, and stomp a mudhole in someone's ass. That's followed up by Worth reaching into his pants, pulling some of the sweat off his sack, and slapping the opponent in the face with a modified stink palm. (An absolutely rare move.) Submissions Triangle Choke - If you don't know, click here. Key Lock - Description. They do it better than I can. Stump Puller - Worth puts his opponent in a seated position on the mat. He'll then put his legs around the head of his opponent, as if he were going to sit on his shoulders. He then reaches down, and grabs the left leg of the opponent, and pulls it into the body of the opponent, stretching the holy crap out of his hamstring. The Power Move Section Choke Shove - Worth thinks he's bigger than he is. So, his chokeslam is more like a Choke shove. Usually, he'll cut a leg out too. Belly to Back Suplex - This move is rather Stiener-esque in nature. it's more of a throw than a suplex, and a more agile wrestler will land on their feet, more than likely. Fall Away Slam - An homage to the late Scott Hall (who's a bit of an idol for Worth, and not really dead), Worth will execute the Fall Away exactly like Hall did/does, but more of the Razor Ramon slant of the dusting off of the hands first. The Chained Type Moves OCD #1 - Chet Worth will use three consecutive punches (always R, R, L) followed by an Irish Whip into a short clothesline, three more punches while the opponent is on the ground, followed by a pinfall. In the event any one of these are broken up, reversed, or otherwise done in a different manner, Worth will become enraged, and make a bit, momentum changing mistake. The Alternate Finishers Worth Millions - Your standard Million Dollar Dream. With a smaller opponent, he'll lift them, and swing them back and forth, possibly separating a shoulder. With a larger opponent, he won't even try it. Ten Thousand Words - Against a Larger opponent (+30lbs or more), Worth will launch into a devistating finisher sequence. Part 1 - A kick to the jewels, taking the opponent down to a knee. Part 2 - A left, right, left hook, right hook, left cross, right cross combination to the head of the big man. Part 3 - Worth backs off, comes off the ropes to the left of the opponent, and then to the right. After coming of the right ropes, Worth runs at the opponent, and puts a right hand to their face, knocking them over, a la the Piston Charge from Mike Tyson's Punch Out!!. Part 4 - The win. Entrance Music None. Ring Entrances Chet simply emerges from the curtain and walks to the ring. Nothing elaborate, though he may stop to wave to or slap hands with fans at ringside, depending on the situation.
