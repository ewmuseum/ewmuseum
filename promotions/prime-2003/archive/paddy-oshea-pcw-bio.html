Paddy O'Shea Biography Nicknames The Irishman, The Unrepentant Fenian Bastard, The Man From The Caravan Hometown Galway, Ireland Birthday 5/11/69 Height 5'9 Weight 180 Fed Titles - Fed Awards - Biography Paddy hails from the sleepy town of Athenry in the mountainous regions of Galway, Ireland. Son to a life long alcoholic, Paddy quickly learned how to cut corners and earn easy money, whether this was through deceit or force. His childhood was one of confusion with his mother and father speaking only rarely to each other because of their lifelong hate for one another. They only compromised on one thing - sex is good, hence the reason Paddy has eight other siblings (Old Papa O'Shea cut corners too with the condoms). O'Sheas perseption of the family unit was therefore disrupted and Paddy found commit in all it's forms, quite difficult. This is the key reason for his lack of ability to commit to even a lasting friendship and also his quick temper which developed from this. As a teenager, his father taught him (in some of his few moments of soberness) some of the worst lessons that can be taught - take what you can and take what you can't. Growing up therefore, Paddy was known to sell, wheel, deal and flog anything of his fancy, just to fuel his desire to take despite having no commitment to the evils of money. As he entered his twenties, he entered into the circle of bare-knuckle boxing into his hometown. His status was quickly raised a result of his shockingly powerful left hook and high pain threshold. His status soon caught the attention of a PCW scout who was actually in the local city of Cork to watch an up and coming star. After a shaky trial period, Paddy found his feet in the sport of wrestling only to have his career postponed by a fractured foot. With only a terminated contract in his pocket, Paddy turned his new talent to here - the AWC. However recent days have seen him in pursuit of a new challenge. And only PRIME seemed to offer that for him...
