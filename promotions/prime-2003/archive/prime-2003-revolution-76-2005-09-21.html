ReVolution 76 ---- The Sensei of Backstage Unrest He'd been back for only a matter of weeks, yet already Toshiaki Motoki's presence had left a destabilising aura behind the scenes. The more seasoned superstars were wary of him from his previous run, whilst the newer recruits had got wind of these discontented mutterings, not to mention word of the latest resignation winging its way to Hin See's desk. Oh for what it is to be proceeded by ones reputation. It hadn't been easy to forge such a legacy of mistrust and fear, but the former legal advisor had excelled in his chosen field. Just ask Carl Ca$h, whom Motoki had beaten to a bloody pulp in the center of the squared circle before firing him on the spot from which he laid. Ask Mr Starr, whom Motoki set the monstrous duo of Kincade and The First on, leaving the co-founder of a PRIME barely clinging on to life after the most brutal of assaults in his very own St Louis home. Ask Rimian who's public torching was considered "good TV" by the GlobeTech investor. Ask Ignatius Lisieux, Emilio Rage, Paul Cain, Jon Kano and anyone else who suffered at the hands of the Dark Age after Motoki brought Vampir Nosferatu from out of the wilderness and introduced the infamous former OSW bloodsucker to PRIME like one would lethally inject an inmate on death row. Add these and numerous other instances together and the rationale behind the consensus of doubt clouding the locker room isn't too hard to fathom. Just ask Mr Bojangles. The four-legged co-owner is occupying in his own space backstage, his small form currently sitting on top of a wooden packing crate. If it wasn't for "Sinister" Steven Lee's mistake and the basic incompetence of a Canadian truck driver, Bojangles would have been history by now. Instead with a little plucky courage and a huge dose of luck, Unstoppable Eugene and his mother found the valuable little furball in an animal shelter, just a week or so away from being put down and brought him back home with the intention of cashing in (well on Eugene's mother's part anyway). Motoki wanted to put an end to the Bojangles/Pham charade without getting his hands dirty and no amount of charmwashed wording was going to convince the feline that the man who was approaching him here in Orlando was any different to the one who tried to take all nine of his lives just over a year ago. "MMMMMMEEEEEEEEEEEOOOOOOOOOOOOOWWWWWWW!" Scooping the unaware "Little Eugene" up and off of the crate, Motoki cradles Cecil Cassidy's cat in his arms as he continues to walk through the lobby-like area backstage. It's early in the show, so the general scene backstage is the same as every Wednesday night with various superstars and employees milling around and making up the backdrop as the disgraced former co-owner makes his way through. Placing both of his hands around the midsection of Mr Bojangles, Motoki adopts the same tone of voice you might use when lovingly talking to a small infant... see "patronising".
