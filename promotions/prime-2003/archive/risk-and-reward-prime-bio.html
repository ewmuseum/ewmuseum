Risk and Reward Biography Tag Team Name Risk and Reward Fed Titles N/A Fed Awards N/A Biography Tyler East and Aaron Nelson attended high school in Easton, PA together. Their friendship grew while in school as did their love for wrestling. They primarily followed the indy scene as they finished out high school and headed into college. After graduating college together, they both headed to the Chikara Wrestle Factory to train for their dream. After months of hard work and invaluable training from "Lightning" Mike Quackenbush and Chris Hero, the two debuted for Chikara. After numerous months of wrestling each other on shows, their chemistry really came together when they teamed up. They've been on a tear in the Independent tag team scene since, wrestling pre-show matches for Ring of Honor, multiple Florida Impact Pro and IWA Mid-South shows.
