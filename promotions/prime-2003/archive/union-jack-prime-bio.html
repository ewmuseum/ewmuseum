Setup Description He can hit the move from almost anywhere! Other Moves Submission Finisher: "The Manchester Stretch": Crippler Crossface "The Royal Revolution" -- Basically, a delayed spinning release belling to back suplex. UJ hoists the opponent up by the waist, spins on the spot and releases his grip in mid-air. "A Very British Make-Over" -- Opponent seated at turnbuckle, with UJ positioned at the turnbuckle to the right of him. UJ charges and places both feet into the opponents face, while smoothly sliding out of the ring through the ropes. "British Bulldog" -- Running Bulldog. "By Royal Appointment" -- Running knee drop. Commonly Used Moves: Striking -- European Uppercuts, Stiff Knife Edge Chops and headbutts mostly. Impact -- Belly to Belly suplex, gut wrench suplex, exploding t-bone suplex, northern lights suplex, snap suplex, piledriver, spinning spine buster, ddt. Submission: cobra clutch, Indian deathlock, Mexican surfboard, STF, Fujiwara armbar, sharpshooter. Ring Entrances Then, ‘The Sovereign of PRIME’ emerges onto the entrance ramp way, his own personal security detail leading the way, with his ever-faithful butler, Jeeves walking right along side him and two orange-tanned and white teeth brunettes following him along, both holding onto the end of his sequined union jack cape. Vince Howard: Introducing, on his singles debut, standing at six feet tall, weighing in at two-hundred and twenty pounds, UNIONNNNN JAAAAAACCCCKKKK!!!!!!!!!!!!! BOOOOOOOOO!!!!! Union Jack makes it to the ring, his security details making sure that his opponent remains on his own side of the ring, whilst Jeeves has a few words with the referee. The girls disrobe their sovereign in one swift movement and as all of Union Jack’s minions evacuate the immediate area.
