Adam Biography Nicknames 'The Number One Son', 'The Eternal Underdog' Hometown Glasgow, Scotland Birthday Feb 8th 1978 Height 6'04 Weight 264lbs Fed Titles Alias Champion (current), Tag Team Champion [with the English Luchador] (Sept '03ish) Fed Awards Longest Time in '04 Dual Halo Other Feds NTICW, FWF, FWWF Other Fed Awards N/A Wrestling Style Unconventional, innovative, determined & resourceful - semi-powerhouse into submission. Takes opponent to ground with power moves then wears them down with attacks and holds. Finisher Move Fall From Grace - Shawn Michaels-style ½-ring crossing elbow drop Finisher Description Shawn Michaels-style ½-ring crossing elbow drop Finisher Setup Move Anything to set the opponent up flat on their back in the middle of the ring Setup Description Slams, suplexes, powerbombs, a signature move... Other Moves Signatures - Original Sin: the opponent is grabbed in a Rock Bottom position, Adam ducks behind and locks on a Half Nelson, tying up the opponents other arm. Very effective sleeper-type move Two variation continuations: 1) carried on down to the ground to wear opponent down more 2) dropped suddenly (Rear Naked Choke Drop style) for maximum impact - Beyond Belief: Double-Underhook Suplex taken vertical, stalled, then dropped into a devastating Dangerous Driver, landing Adam on his knees and the opponent straight on their head - Wrath: sudden Three-Point-Charge throat strike, normally hit when opponent is trying to stand Adam up or when the opponent is running towards Adam, definite tide-turner Commonly-Used Moves - Dragon Screw (sometimes on into knee-lock) - Arm Drag to Arm Bar - Float-over Suplex - Swinging Neckbreaker - Shin Breaker - Pendulum Backbreaker - Arm-Wrench followed up with a knee to the side - Mid-section kick to Apocalypse Jaw-breaker (Standard Stunner) - Fireman carry to slam - Top Rope Superplex - Texas Cloverleaf - Running DDT - DDT held on into a chokehold - Stiff back-elbow to jaw - Running Neckbreaker Drop - Standing Dragon Sleeper - European Uppercut - Kneeling Powerbomb - Forearm to jaw - Knee to gut - Pumphandle Drop to pin - Half-crab - Buffalo Sleeper Hold - Crooked Figure Four Rare Moves (Normally only pulled out the bag in desperation, or under extreme circumstances) - High Diving Moonsault - Single Knee Drop to throat - Elbow to the back of the head - Standing Figure Four Leg Lock - Blind Faith: with the opponent set up on the top turnbuckle, facing into the ring, Adam climbs up and grabs them in a Tiger Suplex. He slips his feet over the top rope, but hooked inside the second rope before launching the opponent into the mat and, more-often-than-not, a three count. Leaves Adam vulnerable if there is a kick-out as he's stuck in a position similar to the Tree Of Woe - Turmoil: an arm-wrench to an opponent in the corner is continued when Adam bounces from the second rope on one side of the corner and leaps over the top rope on the other side. Lands him on the outside, bringing the opponent's arm down on the tope rope in a near-dislocating wrench Personality Self-assured and confident, but not to the extent of being cocky. If the match calls for it he can be nasty in the ring, but he has a streak of good conscience. Mainly looks out for number one but has a degree of camaraderie for anyone he’s comfortable working with. Takes risks if the pay-off’s good, both in-ring and out. He's self-conscious of any attention directed his way by the crowd, only just egtting used to being regarded as the Face he seems to be taken as. Favourite Match Type Anything which allows him to use his wide array of normally illegal holds and chokes; No Holds Barred - taken literally. Entrance Music Instruction. - (I Am So Fucking) Great Ring Entrances A searing guitar riff tears through the arena, signalling the arrival of PRIME's Scottish contingent. 'One-two-three-four!' He strides out from behind the curtain at the count, title strap over his shoulder, and stalks his way down the ramp, hand slaps the whole way. 'Let's get something straight; You are gonna be nothing... And I am already Great' After a quick roll into the ring he bounds up from turnbuckle to turnbuckle, shouting along with Arty Shepard's cries; 'You are gonna be nothing... And I am so fucking Great!' He hands the strap to the timekeeper and leans in his corner, tying his hair in a ponytail as the music fades down.
