Sun Tzu Biography Nicknames The Artist of War, The Chinese Air Force, Mao's Little Red Bitch, Sunny (which she hates), The Rabid Panda (which she hates even more) Hometown Beijing, the People's Republic of China Birthday April 23, 1981 (for legal purposes, actual DOB is classified) Height 5' 8.5 Weight 143 lbs. Fed Titles Intense champion (10/29/06-1/10/07) 5 Star champion (1/24/07-04/12/07) Biography Much of the early history, and including the birth name, of the woman known as Sun Tzu is a mystery, even to her. What is know is that she is a product of a highly secretive program conducted by the Chinese military, the purpose of which was the creation of the ultimate soldier. The Ministry of Propaganda discovered her existence and recruited her for a rather unusual mission... to take part in American Professional Wrestling. The goal is to score a propaganda victory when a Chinese woman proves her dominance in a fight, especially against American men. Sun Tzu has embraced this mission and looks forward to the competition, not just out of patriotism, but due to her own violent nature.
