ReVolution 155 ---- A Kind of Troy Evening Fade up from black to cheering: an explosive roar from inside the Ford Center in Oklahoma City. What's the reason for this cheer, you ask? Is it that we've got an arena filled with hepped-up, liquored-up wrestling fans spoiling for a fight? Could it be because someone instructed the crowd to scream their fool heads off, right before the cameras went on? Either of these could be the case, sure -- if you're a cynical wrestling fan. We like to think it's the shiny gold belt that we start with a focus on. "PRIME," it reads. "UNIVERSAL CHAMPIONSHIP." It also has a lady's name on it, but, really, if we have to tell you whose name that is, you're probably watching the wrong goddamn show. Newly rehired interviewers generally get shitty jobs, and, for Matt Mills, that means "hang around outside and wait for the important people to show up." The tall woman in the "Yes, The Gold Is Impressive, But My Eyes are Up Here" t-shirt? She qualifies. Matt Mills: Lindsay! Lindz, just a moment of your time! She's taller than Matt is (especially in boots), but that's okay: she's taller than a lot of people around here (just ask Tony Gamble). It's a voice that can't be mistaken; loud, brash, cut through with that cocky half-smile that just refuses to go away. Lindsay Troy: Matt Mills! How are you? Great to see you back. Mills: Thanks, it's great to be back, believe me. I thought I was going to go out of my mind at home. Troy: I can only imagine. Her smile is easy, friendly. She claps Matt on the shoulder. Mills: Devin Shakur has claimed to be the voice of the people, that he'll "rescue" PRIME from you. Troy: Devin Shakur is the voice of the people the same way that Tom Cruise is the "voice" of scientology. Mills: This isn't even mentioning Chandler Tsonda or Tyler Rayne and their actions against Danny Ferguson at the Dual Halo. Troy: It's not, and we're not going to. Rayne and I spoke last week. Chandler and I haven't spoken since -- it's a conversation I'm looking forward to in the same way I look forward to a Wayans Brothers movie. Mills: And Cozen? Troy: Who? You know, generally have a Lindsay Fucking Troy kind of evening. You stand here and -- I don't know. Try to make sense out of a Team V.I.A.G.R.A. interview or something. That sound good to you? The sudden about face from chipper to snippy catches the man in the suit off-guard -- the audience, too, gauging from their quiet "whoa!" She doesn't wait for or give him a chance to give an answer, though, turning smartly on heel and toe towards the arena. Matt's left standing alone, staring blindly after the Universal Champion.
