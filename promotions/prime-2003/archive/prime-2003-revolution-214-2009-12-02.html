We can be fair about the fact that people who choose this career are not the most balanced of individuals. Life on the road can eat at what fragile bits of sanity remain. Consider this… PRIME is an entertainment juggernaut. Each edition of ReVolution draws in thousands of live attendees and millions of viewers at home. The professional wrestling business pulls in hundreds of millions of dollars annually. Like any good business, PRIME thrives on brand recognition. When fans show up at the arena, or tune their televisions to FX for the night, they are welcomed by a familiar and comforting sight. The stage set-up. The ring placement. The announce team. ReVolution is a visually recognizable product. To accomplish this, things must remain as consistent as possible. Now consider the corridor appearing on the PRIME*View or a loyal television set at this exact moment. The same black production crates are stacked against the dull walls. The same nameless crew members shuffle through the scene, all buzzing about the same business that preoccupies them every week. The neutral colored paint on the wall. The fluorescent lights. It all looks near identical to what was on television last week. It may as well be the same damn arena. Of course, it is not. Last week was Tennessee. This week is North Dakota. It is a twenty hour drive from one to the other. Five hour flight. Regardless of how each member of the PRIME roster (and staff) decides to arrive at the next destination… they have to get there somehow. Which means that a journey of some sort has been made.
