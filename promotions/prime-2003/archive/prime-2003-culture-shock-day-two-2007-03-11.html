Culture Shock Day Two, 11 March 2007

11 March 2007

Culture Shock Day Two Guangdong Olympic Stadium, Guangzhou, China ---- The Dual Halo Finale. Under the clear blue Guangzhou morning sky, the Dual Halos stand tall within the Olympic stadium. A circular cell around each of the two rings, steel platform taking away half of the ringside area, and two perilous rings of upper-level platform looking ominously down into the ring below. In and around the Halos, the entire PRIME refereeing roster stand ready for their toughest test of the year, while between the Halos, Vince Howard stands. Vince Howard: Ladies and Gentlemen… PRIME welcomes you to the DUAL HALO!!! As Howard reads out the rules, including some changes brought in for 2007, they flash up on the screen. 1. Two wrestlers start in Halo #1. 2. Every two minutes, another wrestler will enter… and this year, will be allowed to pick their Halo. 3. Between the two Halos is a tunnel, that this year, will open and close at random intervals. Vince Howard: And with no further dally, it’s time to find out who the first entrants are… There’s a familiar hush descending upon the stadium, a buzz sifting down through the crisp morning air. That hum of anticipation, once a year, where the wrestler with the longest haul through the Halo reveals his shortest straw. Whispers carry on the breeze, impatient yells pepper the stands, and even Vince Howard mouths, "c’mon…" Wait by Earshot. A theme no PRIME fan expected to hear grace the promotions PA system ever again. The man blackballed by the company. The backstage cancer. The controversial ’only Diamond in the sport’. Say hello to BRANDON YOUNGBLOOD. The crowd explode, although given the release of pent-up energy, they’d cheer anyone not on their country’s ‘death list’. Wrists and fists taped ready for war, head freshly shaven, Youngblood strides out from the back, pulling his singlet straps onto his broad shoulders. Cracking his neck, then his taped knuckles, Youngblood walks dead-centre down the wide aisle, hands staying down despite the fans almost tumbling over the barriers. The door to Halo #1 is opened for him by Thomas Giles, who seems less than thrilled to see the pure wrestling master back in the promotion that slapped a big pink slip on his shiny forehead. After leaping up onto the apron, Youngblood steps into the ring, relishing the chance to run the PRIME ropes one more time. With his theme subsiding, Youngblood becomes one of the fans, awaiting the wrestler who’ll face just as long a battle as himself to win the Halo, but would do so without the ‘winning from #1’ kudos. Crouching, his taped hands beckon his first opponent out, ready to Suplex and Spinebuster his way through every wrester PRIME holds the paperwork for, after tearing up his. Velocity Shift by Overseer. The first to step into the cages with Youngblood? 2005 Dual Halo winner, 3rd place in the 2006 Dual Halo. KARINA WOLFENDEN. A playful smile breaks across Youngblood’s face as he pulls his elbow pads into place, the limbers up ready for a wrestling lesson. With rapturous jeers distorting the stadium’s speakers for the woman declared an enemy of the people, Karina forgoes her usual showy entrance, throwing the curtain aside as she steps out into the cauldron of hate. With PRIME security again earning every cent, Wolfenden briskly clears the aisle and steps into the structure she’s made her second home in PRIME. Rolling his knuckles, BY steps back, inviting Wolfenden into the ring, all the time mouthing off about what happened last time she tried to hang with him in PRIME’s squared circle. Not holding back any, Wolfenden springboards off with a leapfrog as Youngblood rushes her. Touching down, the K-Wolf immediately turns and fires off a Snap Kick, catching the outside of Brandon’s knee just as he puts his weight on it to turn. Seeing the leg buckle, Wolfenden thumps another two kicks against the joint, before surging forward to smash her feet into his back with a Pump Dropkick. Knocked into the corner, BY turns to face Karina, who’s already launched at him, driving both knees into chest, before gripping around his bald head to pull him down into an Inverted Lungblower. As BY bounces back to his feet, dropping against the buckles, Wolfenden kips-up, before hopping onto the middle rope, then flinging herself up high, lashing her feet back and forth to pepper Brandon’s torso with kicks. But, as she lands, BY puts his aches to the back of his mind and grabs the nimble K-Wolf, before hurling her overhead and into the buckles, where her foot catches just under the top connector. Backing away, Youngblood flicks his ‘ruthless’ switch to the on position, landing a flurry of harsh stomps to Karina’s body, but as he goes to follow in with a shoulder charge, the wily Wolfenden winces through the pain to sit up, leaving the only Diamond in the sport to smash his shoulder against the post. Rolling down to the canvas off BY’s back, Kari again hops onto the middle rope, using it as a launch platform to fly high and land a Double Stomp to the small of Brandon’s back. As he collapses back onto the canvas, Wolfenden shows that not even Communist gravity can hold her back, scoring a standing Twisting Corkscrew Moonsault across BY’s chest. "ONE! TW-NO!" Before Bernie Roberts can even get to two, Youngblood Pushes Wolfenden up and off through the ropes, dumping her down on the floor. Breaking away from their cheer at Youngblood kicking out, the Chinese fans begin the countdown to the next entrant, who’ll face the choice of making it a threesome… or waiting for someone else to come play with them. Down on the canvas, BY looks towards the stage, while down on the floor, Kari drags herself up using the cage to get a line of sight. They didn’t need to look, as the song says it all. Sympathy for the Devil by the Rolling Stones. >=) The smile emerges through the curtain a few seconds before the man. ANGELO DEVILLE. The Cadillac of Wrestling saunters down the aisle, shrugging off the hostile reaction he faces for shunning the stalker-ish advances of their beloved Sun Tzu. Facing two opened cage door, Deville flashes a smug grin at the rising Youngblood and opts for Halo #2, making himself at home by lounging atop the corner. With Brandon distracted by the man he jostled with many times during their spells atop PRIME, Karina springboards back in with a twist, before launching off to ensnare him with the appropriately-named Dual Halo, but as she spins through to complete the 720 rotation, By pulled her back down to the ground, then launches her overhead with a Northern Lights Suplex. "ONE! TWO! T-NO!" Under the entertained eye of Angelo Deville, Karina shoots a shoulder up, but in doing so, gives the limb to Youngblood. Capturing it right away, Brandon goes for the Armbar, forcing Wolfenden to entwine her fingers, desperately trying not to let her arm be extended. Contorting on the canvas, Kari kicks at out BY, landing a blind shot to the temple to break his hold. Vulnerable on the canvas, Wolfenden scrambles up, only for BY to be there waiting with a European Uppercut that launches her back against the turnbuckles, where a rare Yakuza Kick from the only Diamond in the Sport swats her head to the side. Giving a round of mock applause, Deville’s attention is then snapped away by the countdown. Ducking a shoulder, Youngblood lifts the dazed K-Wolf up into a Fireman’s Carry as the clock hits 00:00. After giving it a few seconds to see who was next to join the fray, Youngblood tosses Kari up over his head and guides her down into a Backbreaker. Still, no music. Deville goads Youngblood with a yawn as he digs his knee into the small of Karina’s back, torturing her with a Bow and Arrow hold. Still, no music. Jawing back at Deville through the Halo cages, Youngblood synchs the hold in tighter, just as the #4 entrant makes his silent, yet hardly low key, arrival. 2006 Dual Halo winner. CHET WORTH. And so Deville’s attention is finally perked. The man who, up until 10 days ago had been the CEO of the sports entertainment juggernaut, would step into the cages as just another member of the payroll… and once with decidedly fewer friends than when he did the same thing one year ago. Dropping down off the ropes, Deville sits on the middle cable, inviting Worth to join him in Halo #2, only for BY to make a counter-offer, releasing Kari to ask the man who rejected numerous return applications to come see what skills his roster were missing out on. At the doors, both officials order Worth to pick a Halo, no longer offering him the perks of an executive role. Much to Deville’s sneering disapproval, Worth enters Halo #1, where Youngblood backs away from the ropes, then drops down to his knees, letting Worth know that it’s going to the ground, and it’s going to hurt. Heading up the steps, the greying Worth keeps his stare locked on Brandon, showing no fear, and not giving up and inch to the intimidation… something made all the easier by the sight of Wolfenden rising behind him. Holding her lower back, Wolfenden doesn’t go for her usual high-flying approach, and instead just smacks a Spinning Backfist against the side of By’s head. Caught off-guard, BY stumbles forward into Worth, who stings him with a right jab. A left and a right follow, before Worth whips BY into the ropes to scythe him down with a Clothesline, felling him for Wolfenden to land a picture-perfect 450 Splash. "ONE! TW-NO!" Lonely in his Halo, Deville stamps along the metal flooring, trying to bribe the referees outside to open the tunnel a little early. Although never exactly bosom buddies, Wolfenden and Worth forgo any professional disrespect they share to work over the man previously blacklisted from the promotion. Scooping BY up, Worth shuffled his feet into position, then tosses him overhead with a Fallaway Slam, dusting his hands off while Wolfenden fulfils her aerial obligation with an Asai Moonsault Senton. "ONE! TWO! T-NO!" As Deville paces back and forth like a frustrated zoo animal, the crowd start to count down to the next entrant. Occupying themselves with trying to shatter the only Diamond in the sport, Wolfenden and Worth pay little attention, but both can’t resist cocking an interested eyebrow as the music hits. The Enemy by Godsmack. Possibly - nay, definitely - the most decorated woman in professional wrestling. One half of the PRIME tag team champions. The Queen of the Ring. Next in line to be Queen of the Halos? LINDSAY TROY. The trademark grin returns to Deville’s face as he can already sense her fist coming right for his face. Action time. Not exactly one to shy away from battle, Troy heads down the aisle as quickly as one can go without breaking into a gallop, but as she goes to dive head-long into Deville’s cage, Worth beckons her to Halo #1. Trying to pretend she didn’t hear him, Troy rubs her hands together at the prospect of getting in a locked cage with Deville, but again Worth yells, gesturing madly for her to join him. Shaking his head, Deville orders Worth to shut it, but desperate to be joined by one of his only true allies left, Worth finally succeeds in luring the QotR into his cage. Angered, as well as bored, Deville kicks out at the cage, then stabs a finger towards the distant Worth, putting him on notice. Joining Worth’s side, Troy nudges Wolfenden out of her path before nailing a front-flip Legdrop across BY’s throat. Springing up, Troy again barges right through Wolfenden to line Youngblood up, prompting Wolfenden to shove her back. Before Worth can step in, Troy replies to the shove with interest, landing a Snap Kick to the side that almost has Kari curling around her shin. An uppercut connects to the stooped over K-Wolf, before Troy grabs leg and hoists Kari up, back peddling to dump her over the ropes and onto the steel flooring. Seeing his chance to get back into things, Youngblood explodes back up with a Roaring Elbow that topples Worth, only for his momentum to carry him into a Roundhouse Spinning Backheel Kick from Troy. Managing to land on her feet, the Queen of the Ring heads straight to the top, keeping her focus despite distractions from Deville. Showcasing her high-flying abilities, Troy flings herself up into a Shooting Star Press, balling herself up and then exploding out to hit a Frog Splash across Brandon’s chest. "ONE! TWO! THR-NO!" But, just as it looks like she might’ve rid PRIME of Youngblood for a second time, Wolfenden slingshots herself in, bringing her knee down into the back of Troy’s neck. Rising, Kari looks down with spite at the Queen of the Ring, then up to the stage as the countdown begins once again. Seeing Worth rise, Kari rushes him with a flying knee, connecting to the jaw. Spinning back around, Wolfenden sees BY rise… …and offers him a helping hand. As Youngblood looks up with understandable mistrust, the countdown reaches 00:00 once again, and the Halos are given their 6th entrant. Faint by Linkin Park. The man whose scarred and battered body survived almost a full year as Intense Champion. The Prince of Delusion. VANGELUS OLSIG. Drawn to caged violence like moths to a flame, or Blaine Blair to a ‘matching shirt and tie’ sale, Olsig sprints down the aisle and finally grants Deville his wish. Entering in his usual untraditional manner, Olsig springboards onto the top rope, spins, then launches off at Deville with a Moonsault, knocking the Cadillac of Wrestling down. Staying on Deville, Olsig lands a flurry of forearms, only for Deville to stay calm and spin around into a Headscissors. Rolling his legs up, Olsig kicks out of the hold and goes to get back to his, but Deville’s fists are as quick as usual, and catch him across the jaw. Knowing the former Intense Champion will be much easier prey when taken out of the air, Deville lands a kick to the back of Olsig’s hamstring, then two more, tightening the muscle and taking the leg out from under his opponent. Taking the foot, Deville grapevines the leg, then leaps up into a kneedrop, twisting Olsig’s joint into a torturous angle. In Halo #1, Youngblood has accepted Wolfenden’s hand, and the unlikely allies go to set about Worth and Troy, pausing, then switching over so that Kari gets the ex-CEO, and Brandon can test out Troy. A Snap Kick across the chest from Wolfenden knocks Worth back through the ropes, sending him tumbling out onto the steel platform. Backing away across the ring, Kari sprints back towards him, keeping half an eye on Youngblood, before launching herself over the top rope with the Negasonic Lupine Warhead, torpedoing into Worth then crashing into the cage. Managing to parry a retaliatory chop, Youngblood throws Troy overhead with a belly to Belly Suplex, sending her over the ropes and down onto the steel… her foot smashing down against the back of Wolfenden’s head as she rises. In Halo #1, Deville continues to mercilessly target the knee and leg of he injury-ravaged Olsig, scoring with a Shinbreaker, then a Chop-block that has Olsig grinding his teeth through the pain. Dragging the record-breaking former Intense champion to the ropes, Deville rests his foot on the middle cable, before stomping into the side of the joint, again bending the leg to a wicked angle. Keeping it on place, Deville steps up onto the rope and goes to drop back down onto the knee once again, only for Olsig to kick up into Angelo’s groin. Doubling over, Deville staggers away, while Olsig has to use the ropes to drag his tortured body up, still feeling the effects of his Malicious Championship loss to Easton Hall. Gritting his teeth through the pain down below, Deville rushes back at Olsig, but instinct cuts in as he’s swept up into a Tilt-a-Whirl Backbreaker, but before Olsig can complete the move, his knee buckles, the injury worsened as Angelo crashing down into it. Seizing his chance, Deville grabs the leg and sits into a Half Crab, pulling back mercilessly on the hold as Olsig grabs the bottom rope on vain. As Deville shuffles around to twist the limb as well as tear at it, the injury-ravaged Olsig is forced to tap. Eliminated: Vangelus Olsig Just as one leaves, another gets their cue to enter as the clock ticks down to 00.00 just as Olsig’s hand slaps the mat. The buzz of anticipation gives way to eager silence. Silence that’s ruptured by I Fucking Hate You by Godsmack. Even Deville can’t help but let out an ‘Ohhhhhh’ as things get turned up a notch. TCHU Storming out from the back, the cold eyes of Tchu target Deville like snipers’ lenses. To a giant roar from the Chinese PRIME fans, the Inhuman Being strides down the aisle, past Olsig, and right into Halo #2. All caught up in their own wars, nobody even notices the passageway opening up. Expressionless, Tchu steps through the ropes, and meets Deville in the middle of the ring, a violent brawl erupting within the confines of the cage. A right hand from Tchu rocks Deville, who replies with a knee, knocking wind out of the Inhuman Being. A European Uppercut puts last years Halo runner-up back in control, only for Deville to play his Joker with a thumb to the eye. Ignoring his blindness, Tchu throws his right foot at Deville, catching the Devil’s Don square in the face and knocking him down. As Tchu rubs his eyes in Halo #2, Brandon Youngblood crouches on the steel flooring around Halo #1, lining her up, before rushing at her with a Spear. As her head clears, Troy slides into a Drop Toe Hold, Clotheslining BY across the middle cable. With BY slumping down to the bottom rope, the Queen of the Ring pounces, entwining them both around the bottom cable with a Crossface, burning BY’s eyes with the plastic coating of the ropes in lieu of her favoured barbed wire. Back in Halo #2, Tchu continues his vengeful onslaught against Angelo Deville, whipping the Crimson Angel into the ropes, before hoisting him up, then driving him down with a Samoan Drop. Staying sat back Deville, Tchu hooks the leg. "ONE! TW-NO!" Rather than kicking out, Deville traps Tchu’s right arm between his legs, before hooking the left with his own. Before Tchu can squirm loose, Deville unleashes a brutal string of hammer-fists to Tchu’s unprotected face. With blood running from Tchu’s nose, Deville finally releases him, then lands a trio of stomps right to the face as he reaches his feet. Back outside Halo #1, Troy tries to pull even harder on the rope-assisted Crossface, only for Wolfenden to drop back into her kicking stance ready to break it up, prompting a rising Worth to grab her by the hair and toss her back into the ring. As Troy pulls back until the rope can go no tighter, the force prises Troy’s fingers apart, freeing Youngblood from the torturous hold. With Worth keeping Wolfenden from disrupting Troy’s attack on Brandon, the countdown reaches zero and entrant #8 joins the fun. As hard as they try, all those still able to raise their heads turn an eye and an ear to the stage. While the City Sleeps by MC 900 Ft Jesus. The PyroCore Legend. THE TRASHCAN MAN As fast as the admittedly un-athletic Michael Kriegman’s legs can carry him, Trashy rushes down to the Halo’s picking #1 as opposed to disrupting the bloody brawl between Deville and Tchu. Entering the action just as Worth is about to lift Karina off her feet for a Choke Toss, TCM spins him around and tries to cave in Chet’s skull with a Bionic Elbow. As the former CEO’s legs weaken beneath him, Trashy connects with a boot to the gut, then drives him into the canvas with a DDT. Looking up to see who was her saviour, Kari’s blurred vision sees the trademark orange glove reach out to her. In Halo #2, Deville shows up the brutal side that lurks under his calm exterior. Having dragged Tchu out through the ropes, Deville throws him headlong into the ladder up to the upper level. Chocking Tchu on one of the ladder’s rungs, Deville then leaves his head poking through, so he can connect with a boot. As Tchu drops out, Deville grabs a handful of hair, dragging the Inhuman Being to the cage, and raking the steel across his face. Back in Halo #1, the Trashcan Man and the K-Wolf take turns in landing shots on Worth’s still ample torso. Locking in a Full Nelson, TCM heaves Worth up, before sitting out and driving the ex-CEO’s tailbone into the canvas, while Wolfenden follows up with a Dropkick to the exposed face. Too focussed on Youngblood to help out her former boss, Troy land a hard Backfist Strike to Brandon, knocking him down to one knee and giving herself time to climb the ladder. Stopping near the top, but not yet tempted by the upper level, Troy waits for BY to enter her crosshairs, before flying off to land on his shoulders for a Huricanrana. But as she goes to snap back, BY grabs her shirt by the sleeves, blocking her. Despite two hand punches rocking Youngblood, he manages to charge the cage, crushing Troy against the steel, before turning and brutally Powerbombing her against the ladder. As a grimacing Troy slides down the steel, Youngblood mops the sweat from his brow, before landing a hard stomp. As Both Wolfenden and Youngblood make unsuccessful pinning attempts, the two minutes expires, and entrant #9 gets his cue. Now You‘re A Man by DVDA. The most unlikeliest of former PRIME Universal Champions. Clyder-man, Clyder-man, he can do anything a Clyder can. CLYDE WALKINS For some unknown reason, Walkins appears to be HUGE in China, and his almost carried down to the ring on a wave of cheers from the crowd. Reaching the Halos, Walkins looks back and forth, taking his time in making his pick… testing the referees’ patience, before they both bundle him into Halo #2 and lock the door. Wisely not announcing his entry too loudly, Walkins sneaks into the ring just as Deville’s boot tries to push Tchu’s face through the steel mesh. Motioning rolling his sleeves up, Walkins then clenches his fists, indicating for Deville that he’s ready to lend a hand. Humouring the Forsaken member, Deville holds the limp Tchu up, leaving him unable to stop Walkins as he shows off why they call him Clyder-man and hastily climbs the ladder up to the second level, before wiping his forehead in relieved fashion. Using the brief distraction, Tchu reaches back and grabs Deville’s head, dropping him into a Jawbreaker. Back in Halo #1, Youngblood heads back into the ring, rushing up behind Wolfenden to level her with a forearm smash from behind. As Trashy goes to avenge his ally, Youngblood ducks a shoulder, lifting Trashy up then driving him down with a trademark Spinebuster. Surging back up, Youngblood roars, before stalking a rising Worth, who in turn takes another massive Spinebuster. "ONE! TWO! TH-NO!" Showing the same survival instincts that won him the 2005 Halo, Worth shoots a shoulder up, leaving Youngblood to instead drop across Trashy and hook the leg. "ONE! TW-NO!" Rising back to his feet, again having to crack his neck after the punishment from Troy, Youngblood is met half-way by a Shining Wizard from a fuming Wolfenden just as the countdown to entrant #10 concludes. Black Mass by Danzig. Unfamiliar music to those only following PRIME, but to wrestling fans with a broader knowledge guess it right away. The Japanese Firecracker. The man whose FUSE Livewire Title run put the belt on the global map. TSUYOSHI TANAKA Down the aisle Tanaka rushes, not seemingly caring which Halo he’ll call home as he heads into #2. Wisely avoiding the battle still raging on between Deville and Tchu, Tanaka heads up the ladder to confront Walkins, prompting Clyder-man into action. As Tanaka reaches the top of the ladder, Walkins tries to stamp his fingers, but the Japanese Firecracker managers to switch grips in the blink of an eye, only for Walkins to change tactics and stomp his forehead. As Tanaka drops backwards, he’s wisely hooks his legs around a ladder’s rung, letting him sit back up and sweep Clyde’s legs just as the ex-Universal champion thinks he’s thwarted the break to his upper-level kingdom. Walkins springs back up, only to suddenly take a look down, before wisely retreating away from the edge. Not showing the same concern for his health, Tanaka hops up onto the platform, then unleashes a string of Knife-edged Chops to Walkins’ chest. Below them, Tchu punishes Deville with a release German Suplex onto the steel flooring, before mounting Deville to land a string of punches, then just resorting to smashing the back of the Devil’s Don’s head against the metal. In Halo #1, the four PRIMEates put their own personal mistrusts and dislikes aside for a minute or so, circling Youngblood. A Roundhouse Kick from Troy knocks the only Diamond in the sport into a Spinning Backfist from Wolfenden, swatting him into a right jab from Worth, which bounces him into a Bionic Elbow from Trashcan Man. On the upper-level of Halo #2, Tanaka and Walkins trade Chops, only for Walkins to step back from a strike from Tanaka, letting him scoop up the FUSE, star, then send them both crashing down onto the platform with a Clyde Effect. As he lands, the platform’s little bit of give bounces Clyde a little closer to the edge, again sending him scrambling back as close to the cage as he can get. With Tanaka down, Walkins venture foot or so away from the cage as he then pretends to pump up his sneakers, before landing the Clyde Knuckle Shuffle. As the Chinese fans hush for the dangerous, high-above-the-ring action, the countdown to entrant #11 reaches 00:00. Jungle Boogie by Kool and the Gang. The morning sun catches the gold plate around the entrant’s waist, but the glimmer is eclipsed by that coming off his teeth. The PRIME Intense Champion. The Sultan of Soul. ASA FOUNTAN To a gigantic pop from the fans, his reward for vanquishing Sonny Silver at night one, Asa rushes down the aisle, handing his belt to the officials before entering Halo #1 to join Youngblood, the K-Wolf, Worth, Troy, and Trashy. Miraculously managing not to have turned on one another for more than a minute, the 4 PRIMEates in Halo #1 push the groggy Youngblood to Asa to take his turn. Pushing Brandon into the corner, Asa lands a trio of right jabs, before felling BY with a Discus Punch, With Youngblood down, Troy turns and doubles the K-Wolf over, before driving her down in the blink of an eye with a Fisherman’s Neckbreaker. Before Trashy can react, she’s back on her feet, ducking his swing and pulling him down with a Cobra Clutch Legsweep. As Worth gives her a judging look, she shrugs. A Baseball Slide knocks Trashy down to the outside of the ring, the Queen of the Ring then conquers the air, sending the crowd into a raptures by nailing a spot-on Space Flying Tiger Drop. In Halo #2, Deville’s thumb lodges itself in Tchu’s eye-socket, then swivels, as if trying to gauge the ball out. As Tchu drops back off Deville, the Devil’s Don pounces, driving his knee into Tchu’s groin as their brawl descends further into brutality. Above them, Tanaka has regained control, sending Walkins crashing back against the cage with a Side Russian Legsweep, then flinging his right foot up into the ex-Universal champion’s face. With Walkins groggy, Tanaka reaches back to grab a ¾ Nelson, before walking up the cage and swinging down to drive Clyde’s face down into the platform. While below them Deville lands a short right to the nose of Tchu, freeing the blood flow a little more, the countdown to the next entrant reaches zero. Map of the Problematique by Muse. Out through the curtain bursts AWC Roll of Legends member, and 3-time AWC Transatlantic Champion. PIERCE LAVELLE Running down to ringside, Lavelle seems un-fazed by either option, but chooses Halo #2. As he slips in under the bottom rope, Pierce heads right for Tchu and Deville, only to have Tanaka plummet down from the upper level as Walkins scores a low-blow, then pushes his teetering opponent backwards. As Tanaka crashes down at his feet, Lavelle seems almost apologetic in pulling hip up for the Whiplash, Powerbombing Tsuyoshi into the buckles before making the pin. "ONE! TWO! THREE!" Eliminated: Tsuyoshi Tanaka Backing away to safety, Walkins peeks over the ledge of the upper level, looking a little relieved. Below them, Deville and Tchu’s legs can barely keep solid beneath them, such is the beating they’ve given one another, but Lavelle has no sympathy for their efforts and exertions, taking both down with a Flying Clothesline over the top rope. AWC’s biggest star stays right on 2 of PRIME’s biggest, pulling Deville up, then scoring an Atomic Drop that sends him bouncing off into the cage. Showing no favouritism, Pierce turns his attention to the Inhuman Being, whipping him into the outside of the ropes, then hoisting Tchu up into a Flapjack, stepping aside to leave him crashing down into the steel. Back in Halo #1, Worth and Troy’s alliance sees them dominant, with Trashy down on the outside, Youngblood slumped in the corner, Wolfenden down in the middle of the ring, and Asa hooked for a Double Suplex that they execute with perfect timing. With Worth making sure Asa stays in place, Troy lands across his chest with an Asai Moonsault. "ONE! TWO! TH-NO!" With the confidence of his Intense title win still strong in him, Asa bursts a shoulder up off the mat. As Worth breaks off to stomp Youngblood back to the canvas, the countdown reaches it’s natural conclusion. Puritania by Dimmu Borgir. Entrant #13. Unlucky for some. The leader of the Sect of Black Wisdom. Bruce Shanahan. VIOLENCE JACK Shaven headed, tattoos engulfing his torso, and fists taped, the Bringer of the Black Gospel appears ready for war. Picking Halo #2, Jack spins Lavelle round and slashes at his chest with a Knife-Edges Chop, before connecting with a knee to the gut. Hooking Pierce’s head, Jack heaves him up for a Suplex, before bringing the AWC Legend down ribs-first onto the top rope, then sending him dropping back into the ring with a Kneelift. Seeing the chance to earn himself a big scalp, Jack drags Tchu up, looking as if he’s going to Suplex the Inhuman being as well… before dropping into a Brainbuster. Swinging around, Jack hooks the leg, as even more blood trickles from Tchu’s head. "ONE! TWO! THRE-NO!" Growling, Jack goes to drag Tchu back up, but his attention is grabbed when the fans suddenly up their volume. Having finally shuffled to the edge of the upper-level, Walkins swallows down his nerves, then flies off with the Clyde Star Frog Splash onto Lavelle. "ONE! TWO! THRE-NO!" Anything PRIME’s megastar can do, AWC’s can seemingly match. Back in Halo #1, Troy takes Asa over with a Snapmare, sitting the Intense champion up for Worth to rush him with a knee… only for Karina Wolfenden to springboard off the top and connect with a Leaping Side Kick, almost snapping Worth over backwards.
