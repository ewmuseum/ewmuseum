ReVolution 191 ---- So, This Is How She's Going To Play It? The sound of squealing tires echoes throughout the parking garage area of the Pepsi Center. An engine revs from off camera, followed by more sounds of tires fighting against pavement. Soon a set of headlights come speeding into view, leading the way for a yellow taxi.
