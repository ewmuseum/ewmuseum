Malik Roland Biography Nicknames The Mammoth Hometown Brooklyn, New York Birthday June 13th, 1975 Height 6'4 Weight 290 Fed Titles Not Applicable At This Time Fed Awards Not Applicable At This Time Biography Malik Roland also known to the wrestling world as 'The Mammoth' was born on one of the islands in New York known as Brooklyn. There he grew up, fighting off the temptations of gang life and the constant peer pressure. Unlike many wrestlers his dream wasn't to be a wrestler, hell he doesn't bleed wrestling to this very day. He didn't have any dreams, he was a young man without focus and there came Richard Silver. Roland was abnormally a large kid for his age, and Silver took notice of this. Usually the Silver's didn't train outside of their families but he took an exception. Richard coax Malik into training him along side his son Sonny Silver (whom also is a multiple time World Champion in his own right).
