ReVolution 104 Orlando ---- Looking For A Fight The scene opens with the shot of a thousand screaming faces, each one lit up from the glow of fireworks that can be heard erupting from just off screen. The fans scream at the top of their lungs in anticipation, trying desperately to make their voices heard over the recurring explosions. Suddenly, a familiar voice is heard over the shot. Nick: Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome to ReVolution 104! We’re coming to you LIVE from the spectacular city of Orlando, Florida… and we’re just ten days removed from the biggest event in the history of PRIME’s existence. Colossus III is officially in the record books, and what a night it was! Now the shot switches to our old friends, Nick and Richard, tucked away comfortably behind their announce table, but battling against the crowd to try and make sure their commentary is actually audible to the fans watching at home on the FX network. Richard: You can say that again! I’m still trying to come wrap my mind around everything that went down at CIII. The A-List were screwed out of their tag team titles, the K-Wolf thankfully retired Iggy, Tchu got luckier than a man hitting the lotto in back to back weeks, and Facey exploded and totally stole Lamen’s “I’m dying from cancer” heat! Nick: Do you have any decency? Richard: Minimal amounts. Not enough to actually create any tact. Nick: At least your honest. PRIME’s official play-by-play guy looks at his partner in crime and shakes his head in dismay, then turns his attention back to the camera just in front of his mug. Nick: And just ten days removed from Colossus… we’ve got a helluva show in store for you tonight! There’s bound to be some fallout from the big event, not too mention the fact that… what was supposed to be a CIII match… Titan Vs Ivan, one on one in a reinforced ring… will be happening tonight, LIVE on ReVolution! On top of that, a Hall of Fame ceremony is scheduled for Karina Wolfenden! Richard: I… Before the color commentator can give his two cents, the lights in the arena vanish, plunging the building into darkness. The anxious roar of the fans begins to quiet down as they try and figure out what they are in store for. Nick: What’s going on here? His question is answered. In unison, blue lights begin to blink on and off as the sounds of Godsmacks’ “I Fucking Hate You” rips through the arena speakers. Immediately, the fans explode into a deafening ovation, pumping their fists into the air. Nick: What a way to kick off the show… our NEW Universal Champion is about to make his presence known! Richard: Just hearing you say that makes me want to throw up. From the backstage, The Inhuman Being emerges, Universal title firmly across his left shoulder. With his appearance, the cheers from the fans seem to somehow manage to double in volume. Nick: What an ovation! I think the arena walls are going to collapse. Richard: Oh god… I can feel the vomit rising into my mouth. Tchu begins his march towards the squared circle, as the fans chant the chorus of the song, pumping their fists with each cry of “I fucking hate you!”. Richard: Listen to these heathens! Nick: God forbid they cheer for someone you personally don’t like. Tchu makes his way into the ring, stepping between the middle and top ropes. Straight to the opposite set of cables, he holds out his hand and asks for a microphone as his music fades away and the lights return to normal. Tchu: First things first… As The Inhuman Being tries to get around to the first piece of business, the fans continue their loud showing of approval. Tchu: …I’m not the kinda guy to come out here and get down on my knees and start slurping away… so I’ll make this quick. Iggy… The crowd pops for the mention of newly-former PRIME superstar. Tchu: … I don’t like what went down at CIII. The K-Wolf got her panties in a bunch and started demanding that you had to go and Worth basically just laid down and let her have her way… and I think that’s bullshit, man. Another loud explosion from the crowd, who seems to agree with The Anti-PRIME’s sentiments 100%. Tchu: You were a helluva competitor, Iggy… and wherever you go from here, I hope things go well for ya. With a pause, The Inhuman Being lowers the mic and adjusts the championship strap across his shoulder, a cold look of rage forming across his face as he paces across the ring. Tchu: Ok, enough blowin’ smoke up people’s asses. Ten days ago, not too far from here, after four years… I stepped into the ring against The Supreme Machine. And at the end of the night, beaten, bruised, bloody… I stood over his broken body and held this title high in the air, just like I said I would. Richard: Jesus, would you listen to this guy? How self-indulgent can you be? And you say the A-List is arrogant? Nick: I’ve never claimed Tchu was the humblest, most honorable of men. Just a great competitor who… Richard: Save it. I’m sure he’ll tell us anyways. Tchu: Rumor has it, Killean isn’t here tonight, and won’t be showing up anytime this evening. The fans pop… most of them anyways, glad that they won’t have to listen to the meGaHeEL!@~ Killean rant and whine… others, however, boo. They must have been expecting the clichéd post-PPV free TeeVee main event rematch. Tchu: That’s fine. But whenever Killer does decide to show his face around here… I want him to know that if he wants to step back into the ring with me and beat the hell out of each other again… (Tchu pauses, then turns to look into one of the cameras around ringside)… you just come find me, Killean. Another loud burst of cheers from the fans, looking forward to a potential rematch, and loving the fighting, albeit somewhat arrogant and nasty, spirit of The Inhuman Being. Tchu: One last thing before I shut my mouth and leave… cuz there’s two mastodons scheduled to kick the crap out of each other tonight, and I want to see that as much as all of you. I want to make it clear that I’m not gonna sit on my ass and defend this belt once every four months. Richard: What an asshole. Tchu: I said it before and I’ll say it again; If The PRIME Choice wants a rematch, Killean knows where to come find me. And if he’s out there watching… anytime, anywhere, one on one, if The Deville wants a piece of The Inhuman Being… I’ll be standing right here. The self-proclaimed Anti-PRIME points his finger down to the center of the squared circle, and the fans have a brief mark-out moment, as “I Fucking Hate You” once again roars through the rafters of the arena. The Inhuman Being drops the mic and rolls out of the ring, heading up the aisle to disappear into the backstage. Richard: Did he just say Ang… Nick: I think he did.
