The Princes Of New England Biography Tag Team Name The Princes Of New England Fed Titles PRIME World Tag Team Championships (1x) Fed Awards Wrestler(s) of the Week (Rev 166) Biography Simon met Connor by chance as Connor was thrown out of his wrestling academy by Curtis "the Hammer" Brown. For better or for worse, they've been together ever since. Other Feds the Squared Circle (post-TV) Other Fed Titles tSC Tag Team Championships (thrice), tSC Evolutionary Title (Simon only) Other Fed Awards None. Manager Miranda O'Reily Wrestling Style All-Around (somewhat of a hybrid of the Midnight Express and the Briscoe Brothers, using quick tags and sometimes elaborate double teams) Finisher Name The Boston-Providence Intersection Knee Kick (commonly called the B-PICK) Finisher Description Busaiku Knee Kick / chop block combination Setup Move Whatever they want. They're Princes. They don't have to conform to your... setups. Move Set The Princes of New England wrestle a style akin to traditional heel tag teams, primarilly the Midnight Express. They coexist quite well, and wear matching tights to signify just how well they get along. The team is cohesive enough that they know when the other might be in trouble in the ring, and enough of a group of jackasses to not only keep an opponent isolated from his tag partner, but also keep the fans hating them by... well. Being jackasses. The team typically doesn't care which one starts, though both members have separate gameplans on the whole when they start a match... Connor likes to start fast and avoid a ground match, while Simon wrestles more well-roundedly, but displays moments of gross arrogance and cowardice at times (particularly with regards to getting hit in the face). Once an advantage is secured through either legitimate or, oftentimes, nefarious means, the team makes quick tags designed to keep the fresh man in, and make use of varied double teams. They like to distract the referee during crucial moments, so that the referee doesn't catch a hot tag being made. The Princes use varied and often unique double teams, much like the Briscoe Brothers. It can be said that although Connor and Simon are dangerous by themselves, they are a truly terrifying team when together. The Princes usually look for Simon's P.K.K. or the stronger B-PICK to finish the match, but they can pretty much use anything that's reasonably strong to put it away. TEAM FINISHERS - The Boston-Providence Intersection Knee Kick (B-PIKK, or known more commonly as the B-PICK. Since that looks better.) A sort of a new-school version of the Midnight Express's "Double Goozle". While Connor takes out an opponent's legs from behind using a chop block, Simon obliterates the opponent from the front with the Providence Knee Kick. The end result is that the opponent not only takes the impact of the P.K.K., but they also land on their upper back or the back of the head first. This will often knock an opponent completely out, so nine times out of ten, this is usually enough to put away an opponent. The B-PICK has a variation where Simon hits them from behind with the P.K.K. and Connor trips them from the front. This hurts more. ALTERNATE FINISHERS These are moves that are born out of their Midnight Express-based roots. They can finish matches, too, though generally speaking, the B-PICK is their preferred finish and the Celtic Pride is their killer finish. - Ode To The Patriots This is a combination of a vertical suplex and a missile dropkick, a sort of a new-agey Rockerplex. Simon grabs the intended target and lifts them into a stalling vertical suplex, and then Connor comes off the top rope (or springboards in) and hits the opponent with the missile dropkick as Simon hits the suplex. Sometimes, they can be knocked completely off of Simon's shoulders for a much more brutal landing, but this is very rare and pretty much a botch. - Boston Massacre A double fisherman buster. Basically, they both grab the guy in a front facelock, throwing his arms over their shoulders. Then they suspend him in the air briefly by each grabbing his legs. They lift him up, and then drive him down with the buster. Some other specifics about entrance and overall design, though: - Every time the words "Cult of Personality" are sung (specifically at the moment of the word "Cult"), every light in the ring suddenly blazes white light, filling the dark arena with light briefly. The lights blaze for a second each time it happens, then it goes back to black. Those familiar with the song... yes, that'd be we'd have some epileptic shock if the entrance ever stretches to the last minute of the song. =P - The Princes themselves have a tendency to wear matching, or at least color-coordinated, outfits even when not wrestling. They both wear short tights with a sort of a flame design. They are normally white with the flames being colord, and the back of the tights have crowns on the back, the same color as the flames. Their main colors are blue, purple, and red. - For pay-per-views, they use special color combinations, and they are the following: Culture Shock (Day One) - Main color black, flame/crown color red. Culture Shock (Day Two) - Main color black, flame/crown color orange. UltraViolence - Main color white, flame/crown color green. Colossus - Main color white, flame/crown color silver. Dice, Dollars, And Tragedy - Main color light blue, flame/crown color navy blue. Great American Nightmare - Main color white, flame/crown color red on the left side and blue on the right side. King of Kings - Main color white, flame/crown color gold. - Miranda wears whatever she feels like wearing, and is only really coordinated with the Princes for pay-per-views. She's really very fashionable, but never dresses very revealingly. Like the Princes, she steps it up for special occasions, wearing lavish dresses that are color coordinated with whatever the Princes are wearing. - Connor jumps over the top rope. Simon enters normally, though he holds the ropes open for Miranda to come into the ring too. They often jump onto the turnbuckles closest to the main camera and play towards IT, rather than the crowd in attendance. Miranda stays in the center of the ring and claps proudly for both guys, before leaving the ring before the match starts, or staying behind the Princes if it's an in-ring confrontation.
