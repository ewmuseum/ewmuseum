Gonzo Biography Nicknames None Hometown Bat Country Birthday Unknown Height 5'11 Weight 266 Fed Titles None Fed Awards None Biography Who is the man known as Gonzo? An orphan adopted by a Mexican illegal immigrant dishwasher and Las Vegas showgirl with bisexual tendencies and a predilection for barbiturates? The descendant of Samoan royalty, still plotting to return to his native island and stage a coup and thus reclaim his family birthright? A rugged man who was welcomed into the Hell’s Angels due to his love of mayhem, the open road and mescaline? The answer is none of the above… and all! The most important thing to know about me is that it is impossible to predict the unpredictable. One day, I may come to the ring and put on a five-star classic. The next, I may blow off my match altogether to go to a cockfight, then to Denny’s. Three-cheese omelet? Carpe diem! In this era of Christian conservatism and a stress on morals, debauchery and hedonism are my yin and yang, the Holy Mother and Blessed Whore. My pockets brim with illegally begotten prescription drugs, uppers, downers and hallucinogens like a colorfully-festooned Santa dredged up from the depths of Hell to serve as the hook-up within an Elvis Hawaiian movie that never ends! Alcohol: wine or beer? Yes please! With three shots of Everclear! Boola boola! Have I ever stepped in the ring? P’shaw! I’ll have you know I trained at the jiggly teat of Afa the Samoan and once kicked the shit out of George Hackenschmidt’s third cousin, twice removed, after that dastardly momser tried to pass off oregano for marijuana! It’s so hard to make a proper damn spaghetti sauce! The brawl ended when Canadian Mounties with a penchant for sadism decided to play a Tito Puente solo with their truncheons against our skulls. I come to PRIME with a quasi-Ivy League-caliber education, a GED Master’s degree in street fighting and a car trunk full of firearms. Lock and load, kiddies! Absolute truth is a very rare and dangerous commodity in the context of professional wrestling. And we all know how the truth hurts.
