Brian Postal Biography Wrestler Name Brian Postal Nicknames - Hometown Toronto, Ontario, Canada Birthday July 10, 1982 Height 6'4 Weight 236 lbs. Fed Titles - Fed Awards - Biography Brian's history is largely unknown. He has a strong amateur background and, according to some high school friends, has been involved in some street fighting rings. He also served as a session musician in Southern Ontario and in greater Detroit. Brian showed up to an open audition for PRIME and recruiters saw something they liked. Now he has a shot at the bigs. Other Feds - Other Fed Titles - Other Fed Awards - Wrestling Style Grappler, Brawler, High-Risk Finisher Move Postal Driver Finisher Description Inverted Olympic Slam (Opp. lands on face instead of back) Finisher Setup Move The Gut Kick.
