Date: Tax Day I think I’m uninspired this week and a little frustrated. I feel like my feud with Nine is going well yet the criminals in PRIME’s press office are ignoring it. Perhaps it’s just crying and moaning but if I’m not going to cry and moan for myself, who the hell is going to do it for me? Once your mother is dead, you’re very lucky in this life if you are one of the few who can find somebody who really truly supports you. It’s sad but true. This week is the half way point till the point when I actually step in the ring with Nine and break his ass rookie down. My problem with him is the dude can’t take a joke and in a profession where most of the time you are traveling and miserable it’s a must that a joke can be taken. Its history, its tradition it’s the way it fucking has been and always will be. So much shit in this world around me is changing and very little of that change do I welcome or like. This is one of those things I refuse to let go and Nine will soon learn that. Tyler Nelson is back!! The bitch is back in town. How I feel about this is really mixed. I guess the best way to describe the feel is that of two old soldiers from different sides getting together years after the war. I have respect for him and I know where he’s coming from. At the same time he knows the same about me. We both are old dogs struggling for the few scraps the young lions toss to the side. Some days I feel like asking him to join me and forming an old school stable fighting for our ways. The other part of me wants to cut the tip of his tongue off and tape his mouth shut so he drowns on his own blood. I’ v been thinking a lot about death after my pecker failed with a hooker two nights ago. I was at a bar that at best was a dive and at worse condemned. I wanted some blow but my guy Sweet Pete was nowhere to be found. More and more these day’s people I need are harder to be found. I’m not sure what that means but something tells me it isn’t good. Anyway I started drinking and drinking hard. By my tenth shot at the final stroke of the first hour of my adventure I was drunk. The kind of drunk where the room spins and all function ceases to exist. The kind of drunk that if you’re lucky enough you’ll end up puking in the toilet instead of all over somebody at the bar. Christ was catholic and Catholics like to drink I know he died for my sins but in some ways I think at times I pay for his. I puked and it felt fucking amazing. The aftertaste burnt and the shit coming out of my nose was frightening but all function was backed. I returned to the bar and ordered water as I jammed out to some Credence on the juke box. That’s when I saw a piece of ass that would make a homo hard and an old man smitten. God was I smitten. I wanted to fuck her many times over and now my night and perhaps my life had purpose. I moved in like a horny cicada bug after a 17 year slumber rubbing my legs and jiggling my balls. She was a near perfect twenty something Barbie doll replica. I impressed her, I seduced her, and then I took her home. This is when it all falls apart. My pecked would not wake up. He was sleeping, drunk, or most likely slacking off on the job. My jerk off pecker wouldn’t get up no matter how hard I beat it. Trust me I beat it like it was Nine with no legs. It failed me. Tradition was broken. Now I’m tired, depressed, and as I already told you completely and utterly uninspired. This week I don't know who I will call friend or who I will face. Just a random vote by random people so I leave you a random rambling from a random yet Malicious Guy. DW =w=
