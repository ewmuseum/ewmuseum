101 Ways to beat Karina

5 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th September 2005 An opening winning streak of two had welcomed Jason Jameson back into wrestling comfortably and the youngster could have been accused of some relaxed in his position. But then he was notified of his opening King of Kings opponent… Karina Wolfenden. Sitting at the table in his kitchen, Jason exhaled a sigh of worry. He was putting the final touches to his instructions for his shoe store and to his mom before he headed down to Revolution. With PRIME travelling from Hawaii to Miami for this week’s ReVolution, The Missing Ideal decided to spend a couple of days at home and check up on things. But as he checked up on every aspect of his former life in Canada over the previous two days The Missing Ideal was introduced to numerous theories on how he could beat Karina. “Go for her titties…” beamed Justin, the kid that was now running Jason’s shop. Those four words had welcomed Jason into the premises and headed for the office area. The shop looked clean and the shoes looked stocked. Everything was in order although Justin seemed to have part of Karina’s body on his mind. “What?” asked a confused Jason slipping into his leather chair in his office. “On Wednesday, against Karina…” grinned Justin. “What?” This was Jason’s first introduction to a theory and he was a little bemused by Justin as he had been expecting a report on the state of business and the store. “Me and the guys were talking,” When Justin applied for his job he had made it obvious that he was a huge wrestling fan and even remembered Jason from NTICW. Justin also proved to be a great employee and the pair, along with some of Justin’s friends would often discuss wrestling matters especially the great matches that ‘didn’t’ involve The Star, “and we have realised that if you go into that match like you did with PWA, the match will be over so much quicker than that one and Civil D will nowhere in sight…” “Okay…” Jason lay back in his seat. Justin and his friends were like anti-bookers. They sat around and bitched about every decision that was made by the creative teams of federations and of course made it clear they could do a better job. “So we think you should go for her bosoms. Try to go for a German suplex or something and grab a feel of those babies…” Justin’s grin grew and grew as he explained his plan for defeating the voluptuous superstar. “You want me to grope my opponent?” The Star’s face was awash with disgust and confusion. Justin’s plan made him slightly nauseous and also left him in wonderment as too what exactly he was doing with his days. Justin nodded his teenage grin ever present. Jason dared not look towards his pants for fear of a stirring in them that an employer should not see. “How feeling her breasts help me win?” questioned Jason. He knew the answer would not please him in the slightest but he had become curious as to the cogs that turned in the head of the young man that he had left his store in the filthy hands of. “Well, to be honest it won’t guarantee that you will win but at least by grabbing her boobs you will distract her and give you a window of opportunity to possibly beat her, and hell even if you don’t, it’s going to be the only chance you will ever get to feel those beautiful things…” There he was. The cogs were on the table and they were the cogs of your typical teenage boy. “Okay…” Jason exhaled deeply as he listened to Justin’s proposal and then sat forward in the chair. Theory #1 had made itself apparent to Jameson and he was only just back home, “…so how has business been?” The business report was good. Back in real-time, the Australian born wrestler stood up from the kitchen table. His mother was at work and he wanted to be gone before she returned. She wasn’t pleased that he was back in wrestling but at least she had attempted to sound supportive. “I like Justin’s idea,” she confessed as Jason told her the story of his day. After returning from checking out the business, Jason was still a little shell shocked about Justin’s idea. Jason was standing at the open fire as he told his mother the story and snapped his neck in her direction at her response. “What?” For the second time in less than two hours Jason was completely stumped for a reasonable thought. “Go for her boobs…” Susan clarified, but Jason was already receiving the message that his mother was backing him to effectively molest his opponent to win the King of Kings opening round match. Standing at the fire, Jason studied his mother’s expression. She was genuine. “I’m a woman…” Susan began as she realised her son’s apparent disgust at the idea. Jason nodded. He couldn’t really argue that she was in fact a woman. “…and I have boobies…” Jason refused to nod here. First of all his mother was drawing attention to her breast, something a son should never look at and secondly, she said the word “boobies.” “…and I know how much it hurts to get hit in them.” … “But he’s not telling me to hit her in the boobs,” responded Jason, still uneasy about the idea, “he just wants me to grab her by them.” “It still hurts…” added Jason’s mother in immediate response to his protests. And so it was. Less than twelve hours into his trip home and already two people had voiced their opinions on defeating the Alias Champion. Lifting his bag from the hall floor, Jason flicked the front door open. Stepping out of the house towards his hire car, Jeff’s theory hit him. “Give them an ultimatum…” suggested Jeff as he sat at the poker table with Jason, Brian and Steve. They were all school mates and had grown up on the same street. The other three still lived in their home district of Toronto, but were very successful in their own right. Two hours of poker and they had spoken about nothing else. Everything that came from their three mouths had been to do with PRIME and more specifically Karina Wolfenden. Yes, nothing else of any other importance was happening in their lives. “Explain…” Looking up from his five cards that he was using to bluff his way to winning, Jason was preparing himself to hear another theory. Brian and Steve also figure their attention on Jeff as he sits up straight in the chair to prepare his explanation. “Right… You see I was thinking about it…” began Jeff. “You see there’s your first problem… You thought about it!” interrupted Brian, raising a weak laugh from the other two, yet un-phased Jeff continued. “You go down to the ring and tell everyone that if you don’t win you are going to kill yourself… or maybe even set yourself alight. You’re guaranteed to win… Ultimatums are always good… Everybody loves ultimatums…” Silence. “That’s interesting…” commented Sean as he finally broke the disapproving silence surrounding the table. “Dude that sucks…” added Brian. “ReVolution is aired on Playboy TV,” finally answered Jason, “the PRIME officials and the network have seen crucifixions before; I think setting myself alight would be a good thing for ratings… Not a bad thing… So they would probably enjoy seeing me lose.” And so Jeff was silent as he rose the betting by $0.50. His theory had been shot by the three men especially Jason. “You see,” suddenly began Steve, “all you have to do is cop a good feel of her assets…” Jason sighed. Here was the third person to suggest this. As the PRIME superstar groaned, the other two men gave a whimper of approval each. “Oh god no…” snapped Jameson immediately. Having heard the suggestion twice already, Jason didn’t need three sex-starved twenty-something’s breathing heavily while considering the perfectly formed assets that Karina Wolfenden possessed. Getting into the hire car, Jason had a big smile across his face. Despite everyone’s obsession with the biggest match of his career, it was good to be home but to be fair he couldn’t have been blamed to expect at least more suggestions for ways to beat Karina. “KICK HER IN THE FANNY!” What the hell? Looking around from the driver’s seat Jason tried to find out where the previous screamed sentence had came from. Opening the door of the car, his mother came running up to him as he looked behind the car. “What?” It was really the only question that Jason could comprehend asking as his mother reached the door. She had pulled up in her car as Jason had put in a CD, and had screamed as she got out of the car. Slightly out of breath, Susan caught her breath but continuing. “I was talking to the girls at work about the theory that Justin came up with… and they suggested you kick her in her… ‘Special place’ and that will really hurt…” And so finally it had come. The third theory for beating Karina was incredibly similar to the most popular idea but still Jason was pleased his mother was back to see him off, even if it was to produce on an incredibly embarrassing and awkward moment for her only son. Jason nodded to his mother. He would take the idea on board. Susan nodded to her son. There would be no hug. No good bye. Susan Jameson still didn’t want her son to go, but then again she wanted her son to succeed if he assisted on remaining a wrestler. Okay so it wasn’t 101… But three is a start… Right?
