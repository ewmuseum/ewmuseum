Heelies, Kids, Security, and Snowstorms. Things Trying To Keep Silver and Shakur Down! (ReV 135 Joint producshizzle)

22 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-22-03 "YOU’VE… GOTTA BE FUCKING KIDDING ME!" A clenched fist rattled the thick windowpane looking out to the snow-covered runways. Sonny Silver furrowed his eyebrows and glanced down at the floor because the sight of improbable July snow hating his voyage made his head hurt. The murmurs of hundreds of bitchy people talking about the freak snowstorm wasn’t really doing anything to block out the pain, so a nice burst of rock music was in order. He plugged in his earphones and prepared to crank that shit until they exploded with Sully Erna’s crazy wailing, but stopped when a familiar hand tapped him on the shoulder. "This BETTER be about… oh, hey, Devin." Devin Shakur stood in front of his tag team partner with a look that read "I have been violated." He threw his bags down in front of Silver and glared straight out the same frost-covered window. "Now I’ve HAD it. First when going to check in, my electronic ticket almost fucks itself up, apparently capitalistic computers are sensitive to threats of violently getting killed in the face so that eventually worked. Right after that I have to go through the metal detectors…Sunny and I have read through the Kama Sutra and I don’t even know some of the shit they did to me! Now we’re stuck in this miserable prison because of an "objective weather system"? Bitch please. This is just the next obstacle to attempt and dethrone communism from PRIME. It ain’t happening! I might as well just threaten set off a damn bomb to make this day perfect! Everybody within earshot of that last comment decided to stare at Commie Emo. "IT’S SNOWING OUTSIDE IN THE MIDDLE OF FUCKING JULY, IDIOTS! DON’T LOOK AT ME!" While some of the fellow disgruntled passengers could only stand by and watch either the freak occurrence outside or the freak occurrence of the two tall people screaming obscenities, Sonny shook his head in disbelief of the situation. "Well, while you were over in security meeting your next ex-wife, some kids were racing around here on those fucking Heelie things while I’m trying to think. I swear, the next time I see one of those punk teens riding around, I’m tripping them." Placing arms behind his back like a token Bond villain peering out a window, he let out a sigh and looked straight up into the thick patch of gray clouds. "Fuck Al Gore and global warming, this is all YOUR fault," he muttered to the Almighty. Things didn’t seem to be going right at all for him lately. Having a big match before Colossus instead of getting the time to plan. Not to mention there was still the matter of him even getting a spot on the Colossus card that was weighing heavily on his mind. He knew it and the stupid bitch knew it, too, but right now he had more pressing concerns. BZZZZZZZZZZT. BZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZT. The OTHER stupid bitch calling. Sonny looked at his cell and saw "Blaine" pop up on the Caller ID. With a cough, he looked back to Devin. "Shakur, you want this?" Devin was too busy breathing on the chilled glass, writing "GET ME OUT OF HERE" on the frost to really pay attention to anything else right now. Next to it, a picture of a stick figure hanging himself. "Cheery." Sonny picked up the phone at long last and let out a sigh before putting the receiver to his ear. "This is Sonny Silver. He cannot come to the phone right now, as he is trapped in a snowstorm with the ignorant hoi polloi. His tummy’s rumbling and with people trapped around him, cannibalism may be likely. If you feel you’ve reached this message in error, hang up and try again. On three now. One… two…" "Sonny, I-" CLICK. "Faggot," he said before tossing the phone back in the confines of his jeans pocket. Shakur awoke from his trance long enough to raise an eyebrow. "Who the hell was that?" "Blaine. He and Lisa have been wasting my fucking minutes trying to find our whereabouts. I swear, the next time I hear Blaine’s nasal-ass voice, my foot’s gonna be meeting where about the dick and the balls connect." "Hey, hey, hey, relax, dude," PRIME’s resident communist said in an attempt to settle his tag partner’s nerves. "First off, we’ve gotta talk some serious strategy. Beating up frat boys was one thing, but as much as I detest these sons of bitches, they’ve all got their own chances of winning. The Universal and Intense Champions got paired up, Nova and Dusk have teamed up before successfully with Nova never dropping a tag team match and… I dunno, Ferguson will just talk everybody to death while Jonathon Winters is busy trying to find the other two dimensions of his character underneath the ring or something. We’ve…" Before another word could be mustered from the breath of Devin Shakur, a loud THUNK sound against the glass pane cut him off. Their eyes focused on a boy appearing to be no more than ten, sitting by an obese adult – his father – engrossed in the local newspaper. The boy continued to bounce a tiny blue rubber ball obnoxiously. "Hey, stop that!" The father took his eye off the paper one second to scold his son, then continued on. After the kid stopped, Devin turned his attention back to Sonny. "Before I was rudely interrupted by the Condom Ad over there, we’ve got to keep our minds on this, man. There’s NO reason we can’t pull out this victory. We’re easily the best strikers not only in this match, but in all of PRIME itself." Sonny nodded. "True." "We’ve already notched a tag team victory under our belt. Teaming up is still fresh in our heads." "Aye." "We’re also easily, the most ruthless duo of callous killing machines ever paired in the same corner." A smile formed. "Right-o." "And… we’re both former Intense Champions. We know how to swing momentum our way via blunt force and how to use whatever weapons possible to get the job done." Smile? Gone. Devin thought about why the look on Sonny’s face was telling him "you just put a bullet in my heart," but when he thought back… "Oh, right, that Mr. Silver Capitalist shit… sorry, bro." "AHHHHHHHHHHHH!" Sonny kicked an empty seat near one of the gates and let out a huff of hate-fueled air. That STUPID moniker again. That stupid façade of a man that wasn’t him was going to haunt him. It was a certainty he was prepared for… but to still hear about anything accomplished by that… THING. That bothered him to the extreme, knowing he may not be able to match that success. Trying to regain his composure, he rubbed his temples with his two fingers on either hand. After a few moments, Devin patted him on the shoulder quasi-sympathetically and chuckled a little. "It’s a good thing you don’t have your gun. Let’s just sit down. We’ve gotta sit back and think." Several moments passed as he calmed down. Both he and Devin grabbed their stuff and took two seats near their gate to keep up with any flight updates. Almost as if this was a contest to see how over the edge both competitors could get, another little kid of about twelve years in age walked up to the pair and stood in front of them. Shakur really did not want to get kicked out of the airport, so he decided not to indulge the kid in whatever whimsical endeavor he was thinking. The kid moved within inches of Sonny Silver’s face, raised his left pointer finger up in the air, and put it on his temple, a parody of the familiar pose Sonny did. This goes on for about fifteen seconds before Sonny broke the tension. "What…" The fanboy decided to play along verbatim with Silver. "The…Shit?" The kid’s friends just a couple of feet behind him busted out into hysterical laughter, and applauded their ballsy friend. Then the kid took a step back from Silver, stood in place for a second, and raised his hands high into the air to imitate the Old Skool Mic. He cupped his hand into a ball and put it very close to his mouth. "SSSSSSSSSSSSSSSILVER!" "OHHHHH SNAP! MR SILVER, CHAIRMAN OF GETTING PWNED!" The kid’s friends enthusiastically shouted. Silver looked over at Shakur and REALLY wondered where his gun was. The kid proceeded to bravely walk near Shakur, stand in front of him, and pull two objects out of his back pocket…Devin Shakur and Sun Tzu action figures. Placing the figures a couple of feet apart, both made a beeline for one another and started to make out. A play by play commentary with the kid was provided from Shakur’s perspective. "Mmm, I’m emo! I’m emo!" One of the kid’s friends rolled down on the floor and started to hit his fist in overzealous laughter. The instigator backed up a couple of feet and held the action figure up to the real life Shakur’s face. "The Devin Shakur action figure…WITH WRIST CUTTING ACTION GRIPS! OH YEAH BOI!" Before the friends could get another barrel of laughs in, Shakur and Silver each grabbed the kid with one arm and yanked him in close. The amusement drained out of the kid’s face very quickly and was replaced by horror. Shakur decided to scare the shit out of him. "Get your miserable, pathetic ass away from us or we’re going to tie you up in the fucking airplane propellers!" The kid ran away to his friends who collectively hauled ass away from the two perturbed wrestlers. Shakur and Silver exchange sighs of relief before turning toward each other to talk strategy. "Alright, the first-" Shakur could barely get a word out before he felt a respectable thunk against the back of his chair. Silver quickly flung his head around to see what the commotion was, only to find the kid that was earlier throwing the ball against the glass now throwing the ball against Shakur’s chair. A long glare from Sonny stops the kid. "Sorry, you were saying-" The kid once again slammed the ball into a chair, but this time that distinction fell upon Sonny himself. A glare from Shakur froze the boy in place. Sonny was getting more and more agitated by the second. A man who needed to embark on the ultimate journey couldn’t afford distractions any more. This "world against me" shit stops NOW. With lightning fast accuracy, Sonny reached behind him and took the ball from the kid before the next thunk could manifest. A look of shock registered on the kid’s face before tears form in his eyes. While the boy ran off, presumably to go cry to the father, he started to bounce the ball in his hand. Wednesday was weighing heavily on his mind as it would be the first time he’d step into the ring with Troy since OverKill... he could think. "Okay, that does it," Sonny continued to throw the ball up and down in his own hands. "How about we just fuck up everyone in sight? That’s never failed us yet." "Deal." Content with their tried-and-true methods, the two kicked back in their seats and waited out the storm. Just then, a teenager of about seventeen on Heelies skated through the runways, laughing to himself and having a jolly old time. "Case in point…" The Universal Title hopeful said as he gently let the ball fall from his grip. The teen tried his best to skirt around the ball that suddenly appeared in his line of sight, but as he went to correct his trajectory, he stumbled and crashed right into a crowd of several people. While the boy apologized up and down and the victims of his wayward skate recovered just enough to yell at him, Silver and Shakur stifled their laughter before kicking their feet up against their own luggage. Sonny, for the first time in several weeks, let out a sly grin as he looked ahead to the future. "Oh, yeah. We’re ready."
