An Introduction To The Devil That Never Goes Away.

13 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-13-05 *Silently, tenderly. I can feel my fingers stabbing into the wounds I've created. Don't even assume sympathy from me. You're like a candle being lit on a windy day. You're due to blow at any time and it was useless lighting the flame in the first place. My given mission was to destroy you and still is. I have no regrets for burning you and sending to a hospital. I have no regrets for making PRIME pay hefty hospital bills because it was worth it in the end. It was worth seeing your petty expressions of pain as they strolled you out of that arena on a stretcher. It was always my plan to revive what I started one day, but I never thought it would take this long. People have plummeted from airplanes and recovered quicker than you, Ignatius. A couple burns here and there physically stopped you for nearly a year? I guess it doesn't matter because you've finally returned. Everyone is cheering. There are people masturbating to your debut at ReV right now. You're in your glory moment, Ignatius, and I hope it feels great. I feel great, too. You know why? Because for the first time ever, I'm going to officially obliterate you. * The algid and audible streets of Chicago never seemed so bleak and offbeat to me. The sun is hiding behind a mountain of skyscrapers and the rain is pounding the streets, commuters and pedestrians into submission. Strangely, there aren't that many pedestrians, though. The sidewalks are abandoned, numerous buses are parked on sides of streets without anyone behind the wheels, and the air had a clotted taste to it; as if the city was burning. All of this and more exposed the true side of Chicago. Where evil rests and crime feeds. This wasn't some playground inside a newly built McDonald’s; this was inner-city realism at its best. Everything seemed so disconnected to me. I felt like some kid just turned on his video game console and started me up again. Life didn't feel like life. It felt more like a virtual reality than anything. How did I get to Chicago in the first place? Every night has this drunken experience to it, where I end up in front of some strange place in a strange land that I've never been to before. This is nothing new, per say, but it always has the same effect to me. I struggle to pick myself up and look to see if there's anybody around that can explain. After a couple of minutes of screaming help I come to the conclusion that there truly isn't anyone out there, and if there was, why would they know about my situation? Then I start to walk around until I eminently collapse and wake up within the skate park of blankets inside my bed. Unfortunately, it didn't go that smooth this time. There was a direct singe of pain running down my right leg and I refused to look at it in fear of how bad it could be. I sprinted down the street, past a couple of buses, and noticed an actual human being sitting on a park bench, looking towards the sky. I walked over to him and quietly sat down next to him. 'Excuse me, I was wondering if you could give me some assistance?' 'It was up there to the right of the moon. It flew into the bowels of the streets.' That's all I had to hear. That sounded just like, well, it sounded like someone I was familiar with. 'Is that why Chicago's a ghost town now?' He didn't respond. He just kept looking at the sky. This was such a surreal moment. Some 65 year-old, gray-haired stranger just told me he saw Slavon flying through the skies. Is he the reason I was here? Had I started taking drugs? What the fuck was going on? I started to sprint farther down the street and reached Twisted Angel's All-Night Pub. Its 'open' sign was hanging on the door and there was lights decorating the bar inside. I figured, at this point, it was worth a shot. I walked over to the door and tried to open it. Locked. 'Excuse me? Is there anybody in there?' I moved closer to the glass and clasped my hands over my eyes to see well. There was a young woman, black haired with a maroon-colored lipstick on, wiping the bar stools. I was seconds away from knocking on the glass until I noticed the handle of a gun in her back pocket. A bartender carrying a gun while on the job? It didn't make any sense to me at all. I was almost convinced that if she did, in fact, shoot me I'd jump back up, as if I had springs, like a cartoon character. This was something out of the science fiction channel. I made my mind up rather quickly and tapped the glass with my index and middle fingers. The woman, beautifully startled but not startled enough to shoot me dead, stood still. 'What do you want?' 'Sorry for bothering you. I just need some help.' 'Are you a human?' '..What?' 'Answer the question!' 'All right, all right. Yes, I'm human..' She walked over to the door and unlocked it. I opened it up and entered without saying a word to her at first. There were bodies everywhere. Lying on all ends of the floors, slouched over the chairs and frozen in mid-lean over a couple of pool tables. I backed away until I hit a wall. Catching my breath, she took it upon herself to start. 'Wow, you really must be a human.' 'What the fuck are you talking about?! Yes! I'm a human. You're a human. What's going on?' A stone expression planted across her face. Her eyes like two ocular plaques of ice. She grabbed me by the arm and dragged me over to the bar where a cordless radio was sitting. She turned up and I listened in utter fear. This is WBRD-5 reporting breaking news to all of you. Questions have been flying in from everywhere about the random explosions, buildings collapsing and the mysterious spotting of an unknown figure flying in the sky. We, unfortunately, don't have answers for you. This is a warning, I repeat, warning to most of Illinois. Take caution by staying in your houses and letting whatever this phenomenon is pass. Thank you. 'Okay, listen, I know who this guy is.' 'He's altering minds. All of these people in here? They all turned into these mentally drained monsters. They tried to kill me..' 'We need to get out of here. I don't care if you know the guy. I've got a car in the back.' 'Where are we gonna go? Thing is, I just woke up in the middle of fucking Chicago without a single memory as to how I got here in the first place. Now, I hear that Slavon is here trashing Chicago and altering people's train of thought?' 'Slavon. His name is Slavon?' 'Yes.' 'Is he some mad scientist? Or an Arab terrorist? Or just some rich guy with a jetpack who went insane?' 'I don't know. He could be any of them, I suppose. It all depends on what he wants to do with his time here.' 'We'll go as far as we can.' I accepted the proposal. It just seemed a bit pressured. Such a delicate situation and she's already letting me inside her car? Why was she inside the bar wiping tables in the first place? Was this some kind of ploy to bring me closer to the eve of destruction? Was I being played like a record over and over again? This couldn't have been actually happening. I was sleeping. I am sleeping. I know what I'll do! I'll count to three, snap my fingers and hope that this nightmare goes away. Count to three, snap my fingers and hope that this nightmare goes away! One. Two. Three. Nothing? Still inside the bar? You fucking retard. I kicked open the door and jogged to the back of the parking lot. There was a list of options. Take a ladder to a roof to scope out the scene more than I have. Hide inside another building, what looked to be an apartment building, and keep quiet until the damage is completely done, or jump into one of seven running cars. Seven running cars? I walked over to one of them and peaked in, only to see a key jammed into the ignition. I lifted the handle and the door opened without much assistance at all. There were definitely seven running cars around me, all vacant. I shifted my attention to the bartender running towards me. I shook my head and held my hand up. 'I'm sorry. I can't trust anyone right now. I have to do this on my own. All of these cars are running. Take one and may be I'll catch up with you once all of this is over.' She continued to run towards me until I slid into the car and quickly slammed the shifter into 'reverse'. 'Please, don't go without me!' 'I have to. I think I may have started this.' 'What? What are you ta---' The car screeched as I watched a beam of red light shoot down from the sky and impale the bartender from the back and out her chest, exposing a massive burn and a gaping hole that was the size of a walnut around. She held her index finger up to the sky as her eyes rolled to the back of her head. I rolled down my window and looked up to the sky. What I saw was nothing I could ever imagine in a million years. A rainbow of inverted colors being decorated by a laser-shooting psychopath in the skies. Bodies, lightning, energy and misery from above. I resumed backing out, and much quicker this time, when a corpse crashed down on the roof of the car. I swerved until I reached the opening and slammed my foot down on the gas pedal, sending the corpse rolling down my windshield and landing on the pavement with an ear-popping slap. I took a tire melting left down Highchest Terrace and swerved through a couple of orange construction cones. Of course, I unintentionally blocked out the fact that they were, indeed, construction cones and moments later I found myself riding in a four-wheel death machine. The road, up ahead, was carved like a pumpkin for what seemed to be pipeline work. I reached an onramp to a bridge and noticed just why they had the orange cones up in the first place. A major gap in the middle of a once-connected road. The bridge was out and I was mindlessly defying it without knowing so. My leg felt like it was on fire and I could no longer turn away the pain. I lifted my pant leg up and noticed, to my flabbergast, that an 'S' had been branded into my leg. That motherfucker had set me up completely. He's the reason I was here in the first place and why I was about to run my car into the Goddamn ocean. This whole day, this entire scenario, was mapped out by Slavon himself. 'Is this how you want to die, Nicholas?' I turned my head and saw the cigar smoking, leg-crossing, pale-skinned son-of-a-bitch himself, Slavon, sitting in the passenger seat. A seatbelt made a diagonal cross over his chest and the smoke he exhaled filled the car like a smoke grenade. 'This is how I want you to die.' 'How so? By driving the car over a bridge into the freezing waters below? Don't be ignorant, Nick. You'll only do harm to yourself.' 'I don't care anymore. The whole realization that today was planned by you has convinced me that I'll never see the light at the end of the tunnel.' 'Probably. Then again, I don't hate you. You hate me. Technically, I've never done anything wrong to you.' 'You've made me do wrong things to myself, Slavon. All of this. The phony waitress. The bridge under construction. The bodies falling from the sky. This is all of your doing.' 'True, but my only intention was to create brief entertainment.' I turn my head to see if he's cracking up yet, but a stern expression remains. It seems as if that's all that remains. I turn around and notice that Chicago has become normal again. There's pedestrians walking dogs and stores are open. There's even a dozen cops behind me with their lights on. Wait, cops? I brake the car and police vehicles follow suit. 'Look what you've done.' 'You want everything to be better?' 'What's better to you?' 'This..' This is WBRD-5 reporting breaking news. To further our report of Chicago being under attack, we've been told that things have suddenly changed and changed for the better. People are leaving their houses and stores are opened for business. There hasn't been another report of any mysterious individuals flying in the sky and you're going into a cave where only the good die young. Where only the good die young. Where only the good die young. WAKE UP! Cold sweat. The sound of a million clocks recording different times simultaneously. The hour seems to go slowly and my closed eyes have nothing more to see today. Like a written chapter in an unfinished book, things have unfolded like nothing I've ever seen. I can't explain any of this anymore. If this is boggling all of you? Then it's boggling all of us. Seventeen Ozric Mortimer’s. Seventeen Nicholas Brockens. Seventeen disconnected souls marching through an ocean of sand. It wasn't hot enough to be the desert, but what else could it be? The land was flat. Skulls and bones were scattered in various spots. I couldn't tell if this was I dying or if this was I on drugs. I didn't want to know anymore. I rested my knees on the lumpiness of sand as the satanic sunrays peeled my skin and made me severely nauseous. 'Will you do what I say now, Ozric?' I didn't respond. I fought him off as best as I could. I couldn't let him back in again. I made such an incredible, miraculous return to normality; where I was accepted as a sane individual and given opportunity after opportunity to succeed. I couldn't let him back in. 'Will you do what I say now, Nicholas?' 'You can't control me.' The massive machine I once thought I defeated stood before me. Its red eyes glared at me and ripped me apart from the inside. Digging my weakened fingers into the pool of sand, I struggled to escape the motions of going back. After all, this is why they discovered a past and a present. 'Look around you, Mortimer.' He held his hands up, like Jesus Christ on the cross. 'I created all of this for one day to simply show you what I can do. Imagine what I can create for your eternity, Ozric. Don't fool with the masses..' I couldn't convince myself that what I was doing was right. He'd kill me if tried to run. Then again, where could I run? This was his own virtual design. I was inside his own reality without even knowing when it happened. Good God, if you are there, save me. I don't know what I'm about to do. I just don't know anymore. Stopping, I rolled myself around and faced Slavon; no longer a machine, but a powerful mind-controlling man. 'You win, father.' 'An eternus sententia lies in manuum of meus vox.' With that, I felt my body relax and become energy, like light. I could feel myself spinning a million miles an hour before I slammed into Slavon's structure and disintegrated. Pieces of my soul fell like shrapnel from his mouth and in less than a minute I was back in realm of Chicago, lying against a park bench with sweat streaming down my forehead. The day had turned to night, just like me. I could feel the reverb of an evil spirit inside of me and wanted to cry because of it. This was, in fact, the beginning of an end. *
