Date: And I didn't have time for my second idea This is the story of Devin Shakur A man who lacks purpose Ever since he became naked, void of Championship belt He's been in dire need of guidance In search of direction to show him the way However, he always ends up stuck inside a circle that he cannot escape The variables of the story are interchangeable Because the end result is always the same October 10th, 2008 Cataclysm In the center of a heated Boston crowd, Devin Shakur and Lance Marshall were stuck in the heat of battle. Twenty thousand plus cheered for Devin to continue his fall from grace. An additional loss would fully encase Commie Emo in his new home, the shell of his former self However, the former PRIME Universal Champion had other plans and just sacrificed his head by slamming it into Marshall's to keep fighting for victory. Great fucking Caesar, that son of a bitch has a harder head than I thought! Ugh, Steve better have stolen that Vicoden from Nova like I asked. Gotta finish this. Gotta finish this. Gotta finish this. Come forward you big fucking ogre, I got a present for you. Marshall staggered forward, clearly on his last legs. Shakur cocked his right hand back and waited for 'The Lion' to place himself in just the right spot before unloading the punch. "BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO" Marshall fell to a knee and rubbed at his jaw. Shakur cracked a smile and aired out his hand before shifting six paces to the left and planting his feet. Keep booing me all you want you stupid pricks because in about five seconds none of it will matter. Marshall rose up to his feet, oblivious of the fate that awaited when he becomes fully erect. Devin Shakur never needed extra time to leave his mark on an opponent. BOOM! Outta here. The shin collided with Marshall's head, rendering him loopier than a roller coaster. The impact resonated throughout the building and caused fans to instinctively cringe for fear that Marshall might have suffered a concussion. Adrenaline pumped through Shakur's body, a sense of elation that always overcame him whenever a win was about to occur. WHAT? YOU'VE GOTTA BE KIDDING! One aberration decided to spoil his party in the form of a weary Lance Marshall, still standing much to the shock of Devin Shakur, Boston, worldwide audiences, and the announce team. Nick: OH MY GOD! HE'S STILL STANDING! HOW IS THAT POSSIBLE? Eugene: He took a...Dusk pill? Am I reading that right? HE'S ON STEROIDS! STEROIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIDS! THIS IS BULLSHIT! HE'S GOT A METAL PLATE IN HIS FUCKING HEAD! HE...HE...AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! An enraged Devin Shakur bulrushed ahead, looking for the knockout blow. "OHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH" "NICE, DUDE!" Drunk patrons inside of a local Raleigh bar hooted and hollered at Devin being launched halfway across the ring by The Lion. They jumped all over each other and spilt beer on fellow enthusiastic, now angry and wet, sports fans. Quickly scurrying over to salvage a chance at picking up a victory over the former Universal Champion, Marshall threw his arm over Shakur. One person sitting to the left of the bartender, about to fall off his stool, protested the decision to continue showing the match. "Shut that shit off, shut that shit off, nobody wanna see that." Any guesses as to who the voice belonged to? "S'amiscariage of FUCKIN' justice I tell ya-HICCUP-big bastard oughta gone down." When Devin showed up two hours ago with the idea to get wasted, he expected a quiet evening of venting to the bartender about his wrestling woes and berating waitresses for not being Chinese enough. Little did he know that three different parties had chosen to invade his privacy, and the majority of them happened to be avid wrestling fans. "I betcha I get that there victory because he failed the drug test." Shakur said, slipping into a seldom seen Southern drawl. He managed to work out the kinks and eliminate the accent on camera, but his true self comes out whenever he threw back a little of the sauce (Read: Eight beers). One of the clearly intoxicated corporate businessmen shouted over his right shoulder, "GO BACK TO CHINA, EMO!" His equally trashed friends chimed in, cackling like a bunch of hyenas, which didn't soothe the bar folk who weren't drunk enough yet . Shakur grunted and clutched his bottle tighter, digging his fingers underneath the edge of the label. The boisterous drunk continued, "You ain't got nothin' left anymore, Emo. You used to be able to knock somebody's head smooth off but now you can't even beat somebody from Shit City Championship Wrestling." Devin muttered under his breath, "Just leave me alone, what I ever done to ya?" But the party of five continued to egg him on, determined to exploit Devin's mental breakdown, "Hell I bet those two little doucheshits that follow you around, what are their names, Janitor and Vegetable, could kick you up and down the block now." "You ought to just lay on your back whenever the bell rings, would make your life a lot easier." "Didn't you used to tell your ex that, dude?" "I bet that's what Sunny tells Dev over there." "OHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH" Devin dropped his head down and stared at an imperfection in the mahogany bar wood. He didn't want to lose his temper and endure even more unwanted scrutiny for his actions. The bartender glared over at the party, a silent plea to get them to stop. She didn't want any trouble so close to the end of her shift and seeing the unrequited assault, she knew things could get volatile in a hurry. However, if any of them saw the glare, they paid it no mind. "Man, Shakur, you should just put a mask on and call yourself Enemigo Emo." "Baby Dusk could kick his ass with the spring loaded superkick!" "Man, I'll bet you could kick his ass, Jimmy and you ain't worked out in about what, six years?" "Hell, I bet you I could. HEY SHAKUR! GET ON OVER HERE SO I CAN BEAT YER ASS AND THEN I'MA TAKE THAT DOLL YOU CALL A GIRLFRIEND'N INTRODUCE HER TO THE JIMMEISTER!" Unfortunately, Devin turned around just in the nick of time to see Jimmy and his fat blob of a body thrusting forward in a sexual nature. It was the straw that broke the camel's back, or the beer bottle's body across the bar. Charging forward like an enraged bull, Devin attempted to cut Jimmy across the throat with one swift motion. Much to the delight of his friends, who were still observing rather than protecting, Jimmy sidestepped Devin and let him rush to a stop, the neck of the beer bottle stuck three inches deep in the wall. "That's enough!" shouted the bartender, looking around for anybody who was willing to help defuse the situation. "Watch this," Jimmy said, taking an empty pitcher from the table and lumbering ahead. His plan was to hit Devin on the head and back him against the wall, using his girth to manipulate the fight to his advantage. Yanking the bottle and shards of wood out of the wall, Devin turned around and saw the near three hundred pound man charging straight for him. The glass instinctively dropped to the ground and his arm swung forward. The patrons at the bar were stunned at the resolution that followed. Jimmy barely had time to get his pitcher fully cocked when Devin's right hand collided with his jaw. Unlike what Jimmy saw on television, there wasn't a voluntarily backlash where his head whipped around and everybody in the bar jeered. Instead, he dropped to the ground stiff as a corpse. His friends were all flabbergasted. Jimmy had a reputation for having a high threshold for pain while inebriated, and he was just dropped with a single punch. Maybe the rumors about Devin Shakur's demise were greatly exaggerated. The drunken crowd rose from their chairs, brandishing weapons and desiring to avenge their unconscious friend. There were still six of them left and Shakur looked desperate, like a caged animal. He might be able to hold them off for a while, but the numbers game would eventually catch up. They all stalked ahead, licking their lips at the thought of striking a blow that would make Devin realize that he wasn't as tough as he thought. Well, they were, until a mysterious hand emerged from behind Devin and took him by the collar, forcefully dragging him down the back stairs of the bar and into a waiting car. The driver popped open the door, shoved Devin inside, and shuffled around to his side of the vehicle. With the intoxicated mob in pursuit, the enigmatic accomplice floored the accelerator and rode off down the alley, making a quick left and leaving the drunken businessmen to throw their beer bottles against the ground in disgust. Devin looked around the car like a curious dog wondering where it was being taken. Just a minute ago he was standing in the corner of a bar ready to stand up for himself. Next thing he knew, he was crammed inside of a car rubbing at his collarbone. The driver said, pointing to the seat belt hastily, "Buckle up, I'm not doing a song and dance for your sake to get out of a ticket." Shakur felt around for the belt, "GAMBLE, whadda hell? I had them pieces of shit under control...And where the hell you takin' me?" Tony Gamble kept one hand firmly on the steering wheel while practically buckling Devin up himself. "I'll bet a pile of money up to my chin you would end up in a Torres Wilson expose next week if I hadn't jumped in there." "...So you'd be bettin' about, what, fifteen'er twenty cents?" "Can the short jokes, drunk, you look like a twenty year old mop that hasn't been cleaned." "Jeez, man, what's with all the hostility? Wha'de I ever do to you? I'm just out tryin' to have a good time and you end up ruinin' it." "I saw the whole thing, Dev. I doubt Amateur Night at the Apollo and seeing the Chris Farley pelvic thrust is considered good time." "That fat fuck had it comin' when I laid his ass out." "Devin, what the hell are you doing to yourself? We've been to that bar plenty of times ever since you showed up in PRIME, but we never went there after a loss. You had to know they were going to give you the business for losing to Marshall." "I at least figured they'd give me some respect, being as I am a local legend and all." "Ric Flair is a statewide legend and his daughter's boyfriend doesn't have a problem giving him a shiner during a fight." Shakur was verbally defeated and threw his arms up in the air defiantly, recoiling them instantly after smashing into the ceiling. "Why you gotta be such a party pooper, Gambs? I done thought you were cool and now you go and play this hogwash." Gamble looked over at Shakur and smacked him hard across the face. Shakur swung wildly in retaliation, but Gamble was able to avoid the contact, leaving Shakur to smash the steel of his headrest. "First of all, drop the Wade Elliott or I'm stopping the car right now." "I ain't done nothin-" Gamble tapped the brakes. "Man, stop this right now." Gamble tapped them another time. Shakur conceded, "Alright, fine. You never told me where you were taking me." "I'm taking you home so you can sober up and get a fucking grip," said the uncharacteristically serious Grin, "I don't know what's up with you but you need to take a step back." "I think I'm doing just fine, thank you very much. I have another shot at the strap and I intend to make Tsonda my bitch." "Devin, face facts, you couldn't make a female dog your bitch right now if you had a ball and some Kibbles and Bits." Gamble replied, making a left and heading for the long stretch of road just before Devin's house. "You need to get your head back in the game otherwise you are going to end up being traded to GCW or some backyard wrestling federation for a couple of prospects." "My head is in the game. I'm Main Eventing a Pay-Per-View." Devin retorted, smiling and nodding his head. "I've seen you after some bad losses before, man. Hell, you hold the distinct privilege of losing a Championship on a major Pay-Per-View to Dusk. DUSK." Devin gave Tony a confused look, putting his thumb underneath his chin to make sure he heard that right. "Dude...You lost a belt to him too." Gamble delivered another smack to Devin, this time hitting him in the back of the head, "Are you going to bring schematics into this? The point is, I've seen you handle big losses a lot better than this. You always got back up on the horse and moved forward. You never let the pressure and all the haters bother you before, but now that you lost the strap you are looking sadder than the sixteen year old bimbo I just jacked this car from." Shakur pointed his finger in Gamble's face, "Don't underestimate me Gamble, the last guy who did that ended up flat on his back in the middle of a bar." Gamble brought the car to a stop in front of Devin's house, ignoring the barking and scratching of the Doberman watch dogs at the doors. "You are beating up local hicks in a bar and considering that an accomplishment. That's how far you've fallen, man. The horse you keep getting back up on? Now you are out in the desert on a horse with no name. I'm worried about you. I think you should drop out of the title match and have a little you time." "WHAT? NO WAY MAN! This is my chance to get back to the top, back to where I belong, and I'm not bowing out for anybody or anything!" Devin said, failing to notice his spit flying all over the windshield. Gamble sighed and put his hand on Devin's shoulder, shaking him back and forth a little while reaching into his pants pocket with his available arm. "What's going to happen to you when you lose that one, Devin? You'll end up even worse off than you are now. I don't know what that entails and I really don't want to find out. Right now, you can't beat the dead side of a jobber and you think your going to suddenly become the best wrestler in the world...Pfft, please." Shakur responded by putting his hand on Gamble's shoulder, "Everybody doubted me the first time Gambs, and then UH-OH, EMO'S GOT A BELT!" "Devin, look, I'm going to give you something that's gotten me through a lot of tough times." Gamble said, retrieving a long gold necklace from his pocket with a diamond cross at the very end. "It's definitely not easy being me and sometimes I let that fact get to my head a little too much. It's something that's been passed down to me and I think you need the luck that it brings a lot more than I do." Gamble placed the necklace into Shakur's hand and ushered him out the door into the waiting paws of his dogs. Off in the distance, Gamble and Shakur both heard a screen door slamming behind and saw a hulking figure slowly walk toward the car. The dogs immediately rushed over to him and jumped up to the top of his legs. He bypassed the pooches and took a hold of Devin's right arm. "Make sure he gets some sleep and puts some ice on his punching hand." Christian Daniels leaned down into the car, sticking his head uncomfortably close to Tony's. "Yeah, I'll take care of it, thanks little man." Christian turned, yanking Devin toward the house while Commie Emo waved at Gamble. The Grin looked concerned at watching his cherished gold necklace dangling from Devin's head. His dogs had a keen eye, swatting at the gem like it was a play toy. He wondered if Devin would ever learn to cherish the gifts that he had and the gifts he was given. Tony shook his head vehemently when the necklace showed up at his front door three days later.
