Date: 16/02/06 Bless Me, Father, For I Have Sinned.Written: 15th February, 2006. 'It was me on that road,Still you couldn't see me,And then flashlights... and explosions.' - Röyksopp. I - Averitia.Greed. It is the last words you say to somebody that rubber-stamps you memory of them. So many endure life with the burden of knowing they parted with their loved one the final time on bad terms, bidding them farewell with words of anger or spite. How they'd give anything they owned, or would ever accrue in their lives, to go back and replace the barbed, verbal attack with one of affection. To go through life trying to make amends for a discretion you can never truly right would be a cross most simply couldn't bare. Most, naturally, betraying that there are certain exceptions. 'If a single cent is a second late, I walk.' Words spat out in spiteful frustration, half uttered as a relief that a seemingly vast expanse of disagreement had somehow been closed... no matter how fragile the grip, or frayed the stitches that held it in place. They were the last words that Karina Wolfenden said to Hin See, the owner and founder of PRIME, before his untimely death at the Palace of Auburn Hills when PRIME waved mournfully goodbye to 2005 at the Great American Nightmare. 'Take a seat.' Having decreed the meeting didn't warrant any special attire, Karina Wolfenden didn't just look like a fish out of water, but more akin to a Guppy tossed flapping onto the scorching sands of the Sahara. Across the vast, divisive table from a crack team of crisp suits, Karina tucked her sunglasses over multicoloured hair, then slithered free of her Parka coat's sleeves. Cross-legged in the thoroughly modern, ergonomic chair, Karina sat, attire comprised of bright orange cargo pants, and what appeared to be a treble layer of strappy tops that criss-crossed black, peach, and turquoise over her shoulders. A far cry from the rank-and-file pinstripe opposite. Leading from the front, Hin See and Taun Pham shuffled closer to the table, neatly arranging the papers before them. 'Can we hurry this along a little?' she appealed with a mischievous half-smile. 'I'm meeting someone from the fWo at midday, and I promised I'd call back AWC's talent relations guy before lunch.' Safe to say, the K-Wolf's passage back into PRIME wasn't to be an easy one... for either side. As both sides launched volleys across the battleground of the flawless mahogany oval, the low morning sun gradually clawed itself to the top of the floor-to-ceiling windows, hampering See's tactic of distracting Kari with its untainted heat and blinding glare. One by one, See and Pham dismissed their snappily-dressed legal guardians to other appointments, leaving the K-Wolf with a small sense of victory every time the door slammed shut. 'Are there any more of them hidden under the table?' Wolfenden took great delight in enquiring with a definitive mocking tone to her laid-back delivery as she saw only Pham and See remained. Both exhaling as thoughtfully as audibly from their nostrils, Hin See and Taun Pham looked to one another, than back to the numbers on the table before them. 'You do realise,' Pham began, launching a futile bid to reason with Karina, 'that with bonuses, and all clauses triggered, this would make you the highest paid member of the PRIME roster, right? I really do want you to actually think about that. YOU would be taking home more money, pre-taxes and deductions, than ANYONE else in the PRIME locker room.' Shooting a peculiar look back across the negotiating table, Kari simply shrugged, wondering what the fuss was about. 'Works for me.' 'Yeahhh...' Pham pondered, looking right back into her fierce emerald eyes, which were unfaltering in their gaze. 'That doesn't really work for us.' Shuffling forward in her seat, Kari dropped her sunglasses back down onto the bridge of her nose, then checked her watch. 'Oh well,' went the dismissive sigh. 'Call down to reception and have them get a Cab ready for me then.' Planting her palms on the arm rests, Wolfenden lifted herself up, then pushed off to land on her feet. With the appeal to remain which she had thoroughly expected not forthcoming, Wolfenden swiped her coat from the now vacant chair, before striding towards the door just as the sun finally ascended over the floor's windows. 'That's not to say there isn't still some room for negotiation...' Pham finally caved in by saying, rising from his seat. Only the door was privy to the self-assured grin that broke like a dawn most glorious across the K-Wolf's bright pink lips. Spinning on her toes, Kari strode back to the table, pushing her chair aside, before parking her derrière on the mahogany. Tucking her knees up to her chin, she span on the heavily polished woodwork, before simply sitting cross-legged in-front of her former employers. 'Then let's finish this so I can blow-off the rest of my appointments today,' she delivered with unusual enthusiasm. 'I'd rather take a nap than endure more cab and rail journeys today.' See looked like he'd just swallowed a wasp, face souring as it began to dawn on him that - in all likelihood - he'd be signing off the demands placed before him. He'd be signing them off, then downing a few stiff drinks to try and numb the sense of defeat. If that failed, he'd probably slap himself across the face. The thought-process must've run in the family, as Pham felt no different. 'In the past year...' Pham began, not mirroring the eagerness exuding from the K-Wolf. 'In the past year, you've forced us to take a 10 minute break on a LIVE television presentation when you decided to instigate a mini-riot in the crowd...' Not letting the accusations go without defence, Kari shrugged off each one with her own justifications. 'Lame security isn't my bad.' 'And then you almost broke a referee's cheekbone when he tried to break up a fight between you and Justin Williamson,' Pham continued down the list with. 'Actually,' Kari halted him with, index finger conducting. 'It was the H.I.T.' 'They were the same person,' the far-from-amused Taun informed her, as Kari had to do a double-take, before deciding she didn't really care about either of them anyway. 'And then we had you delaying the announcement of your Dual Halo prize because you had to leave early for a flight, us not being able to book you in the opening match of any show due to your tardine-' 'Yeah...' Kari again chipped in with. 'But is that you not booking me early because I'm usually late, or is it me being late BECAUSE you never book me early?' 'The former,' Hin See delivered in stony, no-nonsense fashion. 'And that's not even mentioning the numerous interviews that have found their way back to us where you've been less than flat-' Pham went to continue his accusations with, before Karina finally tired of the negativity and repetition. 'Bored now,' she decreed. Taken aback by the rather unorthodox negotiating techniques of Karina Wolfenden, not to mention the fact that most would have normally resorted to begging, bribes, and offerage of first borns to land a PRIME contract, the duo of Pham and See ground their teeth. Adjusting his collar, as if to flush a little frustrated colour from his reddening face, See pursed his lips, just about tolerating the sight of Wolfenden. 'Like me or not,' she began, before realising that really wasn't a choice for the two men across the table from her. 'Okay, I know you both pretty much hate me, but PRIME is a business, and one which, in the last year, I've done my part in making bigger.' See and Pham, out of the corners of their eyes, shared a knowing look, half wishing the fact wasn't so truthful. 'You signed me for pretty much peanuts while I was doing things, winning matches, and taking honours people on treble my bank couldn't manage on their best day,' Karina continued, no hint of humble shame to be found on any of her voice's frequencies. 'So consider a decent fraction of that amount as money you owe me from the last year.' 'Well, yes,' Pham did his best to acknowledge but not outright agree. 'You did become, maybe, one of our to-' 'Maybe, schmaybe,' Karina almost sounded insulted at having to dismiss. 'I became your poster-child for PRIME, and you know it. I don't think Rolo, Troy, and English exactly propelled ReV to high ratings sat at home in their retirement. You canned Youngblood, Aimz threw in the towel, Killean is a doubt for the next 6 months, and Nova still has 6 months of injury himself to recover from. You both know, no matter how begrudgingly, that I'm worth every cent of what's scribbled on that little bit of paper there... so knock your pride back and admit that it's what you need to pay me.' Ushering a response with a raising of the eyebrows, Kari waited impatiently as Pham and See took turns in sliding the paperwork back and forth between them. 'Knock $75,000 off basic and into appearance based,' Pham demanded as much as suggested. 'Another $25,000 into the bonuses, then throw in 6 more house shows a year, and no other wrestling bookings in the 2 weeks before each PPV.' 'Eh,' Kari shrugged. 'Whatever, that'll do.' Pham's eyes narrowed as the K-Wolf responded to agreeing what had the potential to become PRIME's most lucrative contract with 'whatever'. Greed was apparently the dominant virtue to gratitude. As the debris settled in the twilight of 2005, the outpourings of grief came from all quarters of the PRIME roster. Old arguments were cast off into the realm of unforgotten, carried away by the sad tides of water under the bridge. Kind words and blessings were lavished upon Hin See's memory, while Karina Wolfenden's blinkered world welled up its own special reflection. 'I think he still had my pen.' II - Superbia.Pride. 'Where are the paramedics?' one demanded to know, the deep, booming quality to his voice freeing it from the hum. 'She shouldn't be moving!' a woman then exclaimed, bringing the histrionics. A token gesture, a throwaway plaything to brutalise until her use had expired. With legs barely able to support her, knees shifting weight back and forth as if she were careering down the slopes, Karina ripped the Velcro of her gloves, before flinging them down at her sides. After flexing pins-and-needles ravaged fingers, she rummaged through her choppy mess of blonde, pink, and blue hair, creating a cloud of dust, not to mention a mini meteor shower of plaster chunks. Tongue lapped any blood still being forced out through her cut bottom lip as deep breaths did their valiant breathe to ease the sensation that her skull was about to implode... which, in turn, only aggravated throbbing ribs. A gutsy cough resonated agonisingly through her body once more, sending more blood splattering down at her feet. 'We should really do something,' one bystander appealed to the others who didn't heed the K-Wolf's barked directive. Wounded pride demanded its vengeance, on the most primal of levels. An eye for an eye. Sadly, the fiery determination hadn't fully consulted the weary body. 'Oh shi-' one of the do-gooders didn't even have time to fully exclaim as Karina's legs went limp below her, the body's damage threshold tired of flashing red before it simply cut her off.
