A Super Introduction

9 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/9/07 SOUND AND THE FURY Introduction pieces are for noobs Welcome to the wet, wild, and wonderful world of Sound and the Fury. I will be your host and ever-present narrator God Jenkins, and I will guide you through your journey. At the moment we don’t have a focus quite yet, so let me pass the time by filling you in on exactly who our mild-mannered tag team is. Ian Nackedy and Gildenstern are two fine, upstanding young gentlemen who observe laws and national holidays. They aim to compete in PRIME wrestling to fight the wrongs of the world, to defeat the evil-doers and bring them to justice. They are marauders for a great cause, and they are the pinnacle of morality and equity. They… We have feed? Oh, good. As I was saying, they… They… They’re attacking a guy in a hospital? “Talk, you villain, talk!” Two young men stand in a hospital room, leaning over a patient who is summarily not moving a muscle, as men in comas (and women in comas as well, to be equal opportunists) are wont to do. The one closer to the patient is smacking him across the face and the other one is watching the door. Did I mention that they’re dressed as superheroes? Yeah, well they are. That’s funny, all my talk of justice, marauders, evil-doers…and I didn’t even have a clue. Really. Anyway. The smaller one, who is apprehending the comatose villain, whispers, “I know what secrets you’re hiding, but we have ways of making people talk.” An evil smile sprouts under the mask covering his eyes and doing a great job of shielding his identity as Ian Nackedy. Ian has on a blue spandex shirt and red tights, with a big yellow SD about five inches off center on his chest. Mustn’t have gotten a good seamstress to make his costume. Pity. His partner in herodom (is that a word? Spellcheck has its doubts, but I don’t care), Gildenstern, is looking through the whitewashed door and continually looking back to see if Ian has been successful in waking the villain. Gildenstern is not wearing any sort of mask to protect his identity, but we can only assume he wears glasses on a normal daily basis that cause people not to notice. He is currently wearing a red jumpsuit with the letters CC sticking out in blue on his chest. After a minute, he says, “Clear!” and pulls a shade over the window, then moving over to the bed. As Ian and Gil stand over their nemesis, they look at each other and nod. They know that in that look a thousand words can be said to their unity, their friendship, and just exactly how much porn the other is watching every night keeping them awake. They then look down at the patient, ready to do justice… Until the patient bats his eyes, looking around in wonder. He’s woken from his coma! Hooray! “What…what is this?” the groggy man asks, his eyes trying to focus correctly. He rubs them to make sure he’s not imagining the two superheroes in his room. “Is this…am I dead?” “No, but you will be if you don’t talk,” Ian growls, putting his hand in a karate-chop position to the patient’s neck. This causes the man’s eyes, which have been barely open due to his recent battle with being in a coma, to snap open wide. “Wha…who are you?” Gil pipes up. “We are the superheroes who will bring the world to justice, killing every innocent person if we have to!” “Gil, too much,” Ian whispers. “Don’t say my true name in front of the villain, I have to keep my secret identity!” Gil suddenly covers his face in shame. Ian shrugs. “He’s not going to tell anyone, he’s in a coma, remember?” Then Ian looks over to the formerly comatose patient, who is now very awake and very confused. “Coma? I was in a coma? What happened?” Ian ignores him and looks to Gildenstern. “I’m sorry, Gildenstern, I won’t say your real name again.” “AGH!” Gildenstern shouts, slamming his hand down in frustration… Directly into the patient’s nether regions. “AAAHHHH!” the patient screams, pressing the help button once he realizes that he is not dead, he is only in a hospital and picked the wrong time to wake up from a coma (if he had woken up three days ago around 3:00 PM, he would’ve woken up in the middle of Nurse Layla’s sponge bath. Of course, the day after at the same time he would’ve woken up in the middle of Nurse Carl’s sponge bath. Not sure if this situation is worse or not). Ian and Gil look at him pressing the help button and pause. Gildenstern shouts, “He’s calling for reinforcements! Bring out the weapons!” “I didn’t bring any weapons,” Ian says, hanging his head. “What? Why?” “I didn’t have enough room in my spandex.” Gildenstern looks around nervously. “The window!” They both turn and open the window, stepping out onto the ledge and looking over. Seems this hospital has four floors, and they were at the top. “Shit,” says Ian, lighting up a cigarette and looking down uneasily. Gildenstern, however, smiles. “What are you so happy about?” “Forgetting something?” asks the taller man in return, tugging on his cape and smiling. Ian smiles back, and the two leap off of the building, picking up the air beneath their capes and taking off into the beautiful sky… Okay, fine, they crashed to the street below. Lucky there was that awning there. Ian and Gildenstern dust themselves off and look around, noticing the looks from everyone, as it is not a very common thing to see superheroes fall off of buildings. After exchanging an uneasy look, Ian shouts, “Don’t do drugs, children!” and the two run to a nearby alley. Our didactic duo stops in the alley and catch their breath, looking around to make sure they’re alone. “What do we do now?” Gil asks. Uh…guys? The two superheroes look up. “Who’s there?” asks Ian. It’s me, God Jenkins. Remember? The two heroes hug each other in excitement. “God Jenkins! You’re alive!” shouts Ian. Gil contributes, “I knew I heard that faint rumbling in the air of someone talking about us.” There’s a pause. “What are you doing here, God?” Ian is the first to ask. Well, you guys needed a narrator again, since you’re wrestling again. Gil and Ian look at each other, confused. “Wrestling again? Really? I never really liked wrestling,” Gil says. “Well, I put an application to PRIME awhile back, but I didn’t think we’d be accepted. They like serious people, I thought they’d laugh us out of there.” Ian shrugs. “What about that Tony Gamble guy? He’s smiling all the time.” It’s a deformity, dipshit. Now you guys had better prepare for your match. I can’t believe you guys didn’t know I was narrating you. You mean to tell me you were dressed up as superheroes without knowing you had a match coming up? Ian and Gil look to each other, then look up and shake their heads. “No idea,” says Ian, “But it’s pretty convenient we already have a gimmick picked out.” “Captain Crackhead away!” shouts Gildenstern, and Ian follows him out of the alley. …wait. Captain Crackhead? Cut. Now we’re here in a local gym, where the two debuting superstars are getting ready for their coming match with theUPstarts (is it really spelled like that? Hm.) by lifting weights, doing sit-ups, and all that other stuff to prepare for a… Where the fuck are these guys? Ian? Gil? Goddammit, where did they go now? Cut. We find our superheroes…in Alabama? What are you guys doing? “Preparing for our match,” offers Ian off-handedly. Gildenstern and Ian are sitting at the Dairy Queen, waiting in a booth, their fingers tapping together impatiently. “When is she coming?” asks Gil. When is who coming? “Hey, boys,” a southern drawl answers, and the female following it is about 5’6, has blonde hair and blue eyes. She’s a cutie, if I do say so myself in my all-knowing narrator-ness. She waves to Ian and Gil and sits down in the booth with them. “I’ve missed you boys so – HYDROSTATICBUNTCAKE! – much.” Oh, and she has Tourette’s. Her name is Nate. Yes, Nate. It’s a family name. This is preparing for your match, guys? “Totally,” Ian says, staring at his former girlfriend and beauty. “In order to fully get back into the wrestling business, we need to get back our supporting characters. It’ll make us focused…determined.” Ian gurgles. “Your boobies have gotten bigger, Nate.” Nate giggles, running her hands through her hair. “So what’s with the outfits, boys?” she says, slightly twitching. “We’re superheroes!” says Gildenstern proudly, pumping his hand on his chest. “I’m Captain Crackhead and he’s Super Dirk.” Nate nods, then looks confused. “Wait…Super Dirk? What’s a dirk?” “It’s a knife,” answers Ian, looking defensive. “A knife? That’s what your – COMCASTCABLEFUCKBEANLEOTARD! – name means? Super Knife?” Nate looks quite unimpressed. “Hey, Nate, knives are dangerous, okay? Do you want a knife stabbing you? A SUPER knife, at that?” Ian feigns stabbing the girl, looking quite menacing as he does so. Nate looks uneasy. “I guess not. How did you come up with the name, anyway?” Ian shrugs. “Picked it out of the dictionary.” “You have no idea how close he was to being Sober Diarrhea,” Gil laughs, enjoying a dip cone. Nate nods, think pauses for a second as if thinking. Then, she winces. “ASSPUNCHINGGRANDFATHERCLOCK!” After a brief pause, she continues. “Well, guys, if you want me back, you have to prove your love to me.” Ian and Gildenstern lean in, interested. “How?” they sing in unison. “You guys haven’t lost your singing voices. Anyway, in order to show me how much you love me, you must bring to me a big bowl of – PLAGUEINFESTEDMONKEYCRAP!” Ian and Gil look at each other uneasily. “Uh, no thanks, we’ll find another tourette’s inflicted girl with a man’s name willing to have sex with us. Later.” Nate shakes her head. “I wasn’t done yet. In order to prove your love, you must bring me a big bowl of strawberry ice cream from the counter.” Ian blinks. A few times. “Wait…that’s all? Just some strawberry ice cream?” “I’d greatly appreciate it.” As Ian and Gil walk up to the counter, they look at each other and smile. “What an easy quest, Captain Crackhead!” Ian says. “I think we’re ready for wrestling again.” “That’ll be $6.45,” says the Alabaman Dairy Queen worker. Ian digs in his pockets, but comes up empty. Gildenstern does the same. “We’re really ready for wrestling again,” Ian says as he grabs the bowl and runs, Gildenstern grabbing Nate as they run out the store, screaming “SOUND AND THE FURY AWAY!” Oh, you two. Fade.
