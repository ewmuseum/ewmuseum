Land of 1000 Slaughters

16 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 16, 2006 what have you done to me?a miasma of betrayal as emotions writhe.once we savored bliss, innocent and virginal,but your heart paled. a vengeful morass of blood -tears follow darkness, follow death, love condemned.in a torrent of righteousness, I condemn you. -Olsig Emotions drained as I retired from writing for the night. An existence that once held favor had turned into that of consistent trial. Strong emotions expressed on a blood-stained napkin in the form of poetry...the only saving grace from death. Prowess to understand a nature of retrieval saved an otherwise tarnished life. A miasma resulting in betrayal and ultimate destruction. A blood sport that I thought I knew so well...was only a figment of my imagination. A sport I thought was so outstretched from the realm of reality, was closer than I could have ever imagined. It all sent me raveling into a place I did not want to be.... But favor was not an option, presently. The warm sensation rustled my lungs as I took a drag of the cigarette. Complications of life, resulting in an abyss of tribulation. In a heedless moment, I’d went from five-star hotels, glistened to their core with glamour...to a place where you’d be lucky to leave with your clothes still on you back. This was certainly not the most pleasant nor satisfying feature in the world, but neither was life. I’d learned to cope with it and make due. Another drag brought forth cautious reminisce of the previous weeks events. Two Million dollars...they robbed me of Two Millions Dollars. The type of cash that was as valuable to a lowly professional wrestler as myself...gone. In an instants time, I went from mediocre living, to depression...all because of a clown. A clown operated by a cruel, devious chronic. Pshh...Life. I took a drag of the remaining contents of the cigarette before disposing of it and grinding it with my boot. Utter depression had become more than a state of mind for me...it’d become a state of living. Robbed by life. Robbed of the opportunity to continue the hunt for my father. Robbed of the opportunity to live a life of grandeur. Robbed, simply, of opportunity. The Philosopher who crowned the phrase Desperate times call for desperate measures couldn’t be more correct. If only he could fathom the extent of desperate measures... I pushed myself off of the tarnished couch and walked into the only bedroom in the entire apartment. I’d customized it to fill a new spiritual aspect of my life. Candles ignited in every corner with a platform holding a ballista...a weapon used in ancient times by the great Oceanic Warrior, Genghis Khan. The crossbow-shaped mangonel represented the blood-creed brought forth by the Oceanic Warrior in his crusades to the top. I kneeled before the platform, chanting an ancient phrase once repeated by Genghis Khan himself.... The greatest happiness isTo vanquish your enemies,To chase them before you,To rob them of their wealth,To see those dear to themBathed in tears, to clasp toYour bosom their wivesAnd daughters. The prose summed up the bloodthirsty aura that one of the barbarous and anarchic Calvary faith must maintain in order to remain as so. My loyalty and dedication to this Calvary faith is what kept me living throughout most of the day. It had become a method of escape...escape from trials...escape from tribulation...escape from life. I’d once traveled down this road with a man that went by the name of Vampir Nosferatu...but the faith in which he summoned was one of false claims as well as false prophets. This new found salvation was one that would usher in a new era in my life...one that would be trouble free through the faith and spirit of the great Oceanic Warrior. Something still wasn’t right though... When releasing emotions through poems failed...when righteous prayer unto the Oceanic Warrior failed...I turned to a much greater addiction. One that nearly cost me my life a little over a year ago...but even in knowing that, I still turned to it because as of then I really didn’t care whether I lived or whether I died. All I wanted was release from this torture...release from this abyss of darkness...release from this miasma of agony. I sought that release in any manner possible. I rubbed my hands through my hair and made my way over to the dresser, yanking open the top drawer and retrieving the needle. It was half-full...just the right amount. As I glanced at its contents, though, a voice in the back of my head beckoned for me to not go through with the act....anyone who knew the history of Vangelus Olsig knew why. This very needle nearly killed me as I headed into a Universal Title Match with Killean Sirrajin. I was suspended from PRIME for weeks in order to rehab...and even in knowing all of that...I injected the needle anyway. I smiled as the warm fluid swam throughout my veins, creating a tickling sensation that I’d been missing for far too long. Memories of depression and death faded as the warm flow trickled my veins, giving me a sense of neutrality that I’d longed for so many years. In that moment, as the fluid ran its course, I felt no worries...depression and heartache were only figments of the imagination...the events of the previous week that had placed me in the state of mind I’d currently faced ceased to exist...I was simply neutral without a care in the world. ...A feeling that would soon fade as well. Suddenly, a strain took over. I felt my joints lock up in my arm as well as my neck. My lungs felt as if they’d completely collapsed, which ultimately choked the life out of me. My body began to shake fiercely as I dropped the needle to the mat and struggled to grab a hold of the edge of the dresser. Once grasping the edge, I begin trying to pull myself up...but the shaking was just too great and it kept me grounded. I felt my breaths running short as I grabbed my neck in panic and began struggling to gasp air. And then... Knock Knock Knock The knock came at the door, which threw me into even more of a panic. I didn’t know whom was on the other side of the door, but I knew that if they saw me in this state then they’d be sure to call some sort of official. After which, word would leak back to PRIME headquarters and I’d probably be out of a job for good. No... I wouldn’t let it end that way. Against my bodies shaking will, I pushed myself up to my feet and quickly snatched up my trench coat, placing it on as quickly as my fiercely shaking body would allow in order to hide the shaking from the person on the other side of the door. Slowly but surely I made my way over to the door and opened it, revealing on the other side my trainer...Scott Washington. “Vangy...my boy!” he yelled out, taking the liberty to welcome himself into the room. I didn’t bother stopping him because I was too busy trying to control my shaking. As I gasped for air, Scott took notice...turning around to look at me with a worried look on his face. “You okay, Vangelus? You look sick or something...” He stated, squinching to get a better look at the man before him. “I-I’m...I’m fine.” I struggled to respond. “You surely don’t look fine. You sure y-...” “I SAID IM FINE!” I yelled out in frustration, but the aftermath of it made me wish I hadn’t. Scott, however, took a hint and moved on to another subject. “Ugh...Okay. Well, anyway, here are your wrestling trunks. I couldn’t get the blood stain from last week out, but it shouldn’t be that noticeable.” Scott stated, handing me the trunks. As I looked at the trunks though, it became as if reality was suddenly replace with painful images of the brutal beatdown suffered at the hands of Ozric Mortimer which caused the blood stain in the first place. It wasn’t just images of last weeks beatdown, though...it was images of countless beatings suffered at the hands of that psycho clown. Last week topped it all though...two million dollars basically placed in the hands of Tyler Nelson and a beating that couldn’t even be removed from my wrestling trunks. And the worst part of it all... Ozric was Still in my hair. I handed Scott back the trunks... “Keep them...they’re no use to me anymore.” I stated, returning back to reality which ultimately forced me to return back to controlling an uncontrollable shaking. As I handed him the trunks, Scott saw the shaking a bit and became even more curious... “No use? Why not? Vangelus...you sure you okay?” He stated. “I SAID I-...I said I’m fine.” I stated, trying to calm my anger. As my breaths grew shorter, though, so did my frustration level. The will to end it all became nearly too tempting to resist. “Then tell me what your problem is, Vangelus...” Scott stated, more concerned than ever. “I don’t have a problem Scott. You saw what happened last week...this idiotic sport is starting to ruin my life and has me on the verge of killing myself. I don’t even like wrestling, but yet I do it anyway to pay the bills. But when I’m robbed of a bulk of my finances and served probably one of the worst beatings I’ve ever encountered in my life...It makes you wo-...” Suddenly I stopped, dropping to my knees and grabbing my neck as the need for breath reached an all time high. I felt as if I was on the verge of dying right their on the spot. The worst part about it...Scott was no longer just concerned...he knew what was going on. “Oh my g-...I’m calling an ambulance...” “NO!” I used all of my strength to yell out. “GET OFF YOUR HIGH HORSE OLSIG! YOU NEED HELP!” Scott stated. I didn’t care though...I began to push myself up as Scott immediately rushed over to try and help me up. With his assistance I reached my feet and stumbled into the kitchen as breathing became even more difficult. Finally I reached my destination, though, standing in front of the kitchen drawer and yanking it open in desperation withdrawing a butcher knife... And in one swift motion... “VANGELUS NOOO!!!” Scott ran over and grabbed the knife as the edge stood sparkling mere centimeters away from my heart. With my struggling for breath, I couldn’t fight back as he yanked the knife out of my hand and tossed it to the ground. “What’s wrong with you Olsig!? I know you may not be living the grandeur life right now but this isn’t the way to do it!” Scott yelled out in my face. “J-j-just g-...give me...i-it.” I struggled to say... “Fine...you want it? Here.” Scott kicked the knife in my direction. “If you want to take the pussy way out then be my guest. But let me enlighten you on a few things...while you may be going through some trials and tribulations, just remember that there are still people out their who depend on you. Fans depend on you to give them an entertaining match every night...executives depend on you to help feed their family...and do you remember Kacey” ...Suddenly, I took notice. Shift Kacey was the little 12 year old girl to whom I dedicated my Intense Championship win. She was the victim of an attack by a racist nazi group and was my biggest fan ever. I still remember those words she mumbled towards me as she laid still in that hospital bed on the verge of death... ”Promise me...you’ll continue the fight.” Shift End “...Burn in hell Olsig. If this is how you won’t to do it, by God I hope you burn in hell.” stated Scott, turning around and exiting the apartment leaving me with the toughest decision of my life. Get Help... Or Simply End it... The choice to simply end it seemed the more obvious... But as I picked up the knife and stared at my reflection through it... All I could see was Kacey... All I could hear was that soft voice echoing throughout my head... Continue the fight... Continue the fight... Continue the fight... So I struggled to dial 911... I was going to be in Philadelphia Wednesday Night to face Paddy... Even if it would be my last time.
