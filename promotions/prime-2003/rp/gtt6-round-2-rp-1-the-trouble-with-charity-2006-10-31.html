GTT6 - Round 2 RP 1 - The Trouble with Charity

31 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/31/06 ReVolution 109Saturday, October 21, 2006 The atmosphere inside the Xcel Energy Center in St. Paul, Minnesota was electric. The fans were on their feet all night long and the show still had about thirty minutes to go. The main event between Tchu and Chandler Tsonda hadn’t even happened yet. The show was progressing well but after a certain in ring piece by the 5-Star Champion and his new manager, one man had left his skybox and was packing it in for the night. Accompanied by one of St. Paul’s finest officers, Killean Sirrajin had taken offense to Jonathon Winters’ unkind words and thought it better to remove his presence from the building for the rest of the evening. He had his gym bag hanging from his shoulder as he made his way towards the staff exit to the less than classy police cruiser that was patiently waiting outside. As he trudged through the backstage area, he encountered many of PRIME’s unsung heroes. Farewells were expressed from the many members of the ring crew and plenty of the technical crew who were on break. A heel he may be, but a respected backstage leader was Killean’s true role off screen. For over a year now, members of the roster had asked The PRIME Cut for his two cents on certain situations and some younger and less exposed members sought his wisdom for ways of getting exposed. Contrary to popular belief, Killean was not a man who worked close with the members of the creative of writing team. He had some creative say in the angles that he was to partake in, but beyond that, he was willing to deal with any hand that was dealt to him. His reliability to fulfill his duties when called upon was what gave Sirrajin his unscathed backstage image. With the constant thoughts of prosecution running through his head, PRIME was the only place that felt like home to him. However, there was one loose end to be tied up behind the scenes and away from the cameras, even extending outside the boundaries of the company itself. As he approached the back door, his gaze managed to direct itself down the last hallway before the exit. Perhaps he was looking for one more kind face to say his weekly goodbyes to before adventuring into the outside world. There, he was treated as a common criminal and a disruptive member of a supposedly peaceful society. He caught the image of a female appearing from a side dressing room, surrounded by CSC Security for perhaps the final time. CSC security had been a constant irritation in the backstage area for quite sometime now. The talent felt as if their bodies were covered in fly paper for the yellow shirts worn by CSC. As the security spread out a little more, the image of the raven-haired female was unmistakable even to the lower intellectual group of wrestling marks. But to Killean, the image was one of blinding pain, blistering rage and literal helplessness. Standing a matter of twenty-five feet away from him was Charity Manale, saying her goodbyes to an unknown entity still lingering inside the dressing room she had just appeared from. Figuring it to be Jonathon Winters, Killean started to gingerly set one foot after another in the direction of what he still thought to be his girlfriend and the chosen mother to his only daughter. The officer who accompanied him however decided to defuse the potentially explosive situation before it even had a chance of manifesting itself. “Killean, you can’t go that way. We have to leave the building this instant.” The middle aged officer said. Normally, one on one a police man had no chance of detaining someone of Killean’s size and stature. However, James O’Reilly happened to be a large individual in his own right. He had the ability to hinder Sirrajin’s plans long enough to gain the attention of some of the guards that were surrounding the female vixen. It took only a couple seconds for Killean to shave a good ten feet off the distance between himself and his female counterpart, but O’Reilly managed to slow Killean down faster than the superstar expected. He still pressed towards his goal, exerting extra strength every passing second. “Charity…” Killean whispered to himself. As he looked at his love, she didn’t seem to be the same person that he remembered. Her mannerisms seemed altered and her smile was not as enticing as it once was. Her street clothes had changed as well to a more conservative approach from her usually outgoing style. She had changed and he had begun to realize that as moments went by, he was losing her more and more. His breathing had become distressed and his heart rate increased to an extraordinarily high intensity. O’Reilly caught wind of this and realized it’d best to get the attention of a couple CSC guards while he could. “Hey!” O’Reilly yelled. Immediately, two yellow shirts turned their focus to Killean and the officer and they moved to intercept the distraught hall of fame talent. Charity had turned to survey the situation briefly but then turned away, managing at all costs to avoid eye contact with the man that still loved her. Killean grew further agitated with the fruitlessness of the situation and began to struggle with all the will he had. “CHARITY!!!” Sweat began to bead on his forehead, his muscles beginning to ache from the non stop struggle he was forced to endure to attain his goal. But as the two yellow shirts arrived to assist in detaining Killean, he managed one final struggle and many yells of desperation and heartbreak. “CHARITY!!! CHARITY PLEASE….” He forced himself forward with the last bit of energy he had, his body shaking like a leaf about ready to fall from a tree on a brisk fall day. His sunglasses toppled from his face as he struggled, falling to the floor unscathed. Charity moved farther and farther away without looking back, and Killean’s hopes of solving his personal issues were floating away with her. As the guards and the police officer managed to gain control of the situation, Killean stepped back once; the sound of glass crushing under his feet reverberated for a second throughout the hollow corridor. He was finally out of energy from fighting against the three individuals and dropped to the floor on his hands and knees. Killean Sirrajin may have been a heartless beast in the minds of many fans throughout the world who watched him weekly on TV, but the character he played didn’t reflect his true personality or his true feelings. He was exhausted… the mere moments of emotional conflict and struggle taking it’s toll faster than a grueling one hour wrestling match. He uncharacteristically threw himself back against the wall, a single tear of horror and depression streaming down his cheek. As he watched Charity disappear around the corner, his final cry of despair was bone chilling to any individual with a heart. “CHAR… I…IT…TY!!!” He began to sob uncontrollably, his head finally ending up buried in his arms as he curled up into a ball with his back against the wall. He could feel the cold, rough stone through the fabric of his shirt which made him shiver. He was left to sit for only a moment, before Officer O’Reilly and the two yellow shirts picked him up to his feet and literally began to drag him off down the hallway, his foot coming to rest once more on the already shattered red lenses of his sunglasses. The sharp jagged pieces of red glass scraped on the concrete and came to rest in twice as many pieces as before... broken... He couldn't help but think about how Charity had not returned from her supposed retreat just before his match in GTT took place. ~o~ Monday, October 30, 2006 11:30 AM Looking out the window of the rather disorganized loft, a blanket of white could be seen falling from the sky. It was the first real snowfall of the season in Winnipeg and it showed absolutely no signs of letting up until later in the day. The lock on the door clicked once and the door swung open. Killean stepped inside accompanied by Officer Jacobs. “I could have sworn I locked that door…” Killean thought. Both stomped their feet on the small rug situated by the door to remove any snow that gathered on their shoes. “It’s damn cold outside! If I had any idea the snow would be flying, I would have packed a little better.” He dropped his bag near the door causing some flakes of snow leap off the material. He took off his rather thin coat and rubbed his arms to try to regain some heat in his body. “Winnipeg weather… gotta love it.” Jacobs commented while brushing the excess flakes of snow from her hat and coat. “So let me get this straight…” Killean checked his answering machine for messages and when he found none, he turned back to Lynn and continued. “For the last week and a half, I have been calling the police, speaking to sergeants, lieutenants, and any other officer I could find and they were of no help at all. I have been trying to find out more info about this cult and this Frederick Harris psycho fuckin’ leader and nothing. Why can’t I get any help from the police?” “Well in all fairness, they did give you the name of the leader of said cult.” Jacobs replies winning a glare from Sirrajin and a twirl of his index finger while whistling. “Oh yippee! I know the name of the fucktard that runs the shit, but for the life of me, I can’t locate them. Yeah, that does me lots of good.” Killean began to dial the phone, being put on hold as soon as the line picks up. Hey, the police were busy people you know? “Killean, the problem is that there hasn’t been a crime committed. You yourself said that it was Charity’s own wishes to stay behind at the camp. The police aren’t going to send out a troop of men to investigate something that is not a danger to society.” Lynn jumped as Killean slammed the phone on the table. He gave her a rather upset look. “Not a danger? My girlfriend is out there somewhere, marching around with these fools and I have no idea where she is? Her parents have no idea where she is? I see her at ReVolution 109 and she ignores me? She acted like I never existed…” Killean paused and fixed his eyes on Lynn’s. “…and that hurt me.” His head turned away from her briefly but returned as his self-consciousness shone through. “How many other people’s wives, girlfriends and daughters could be out there Lynn? How is that not a dang…” Killean was interrupted by the sound of someone clearing their throat. He was staring right at Lynn so it couldn’t possibly be her. Standing in the doorway to the bedroom carrying a bag was the center piece of the whole conversation. The jaws of both Killean and Lynn dropped to the floor as Charity made her presence felt. It was like a shot to the chin of Killean to see Charity standing there, but he quickly regained his composure and tried to make his way over to her. She raised her hand and took a few steps over to the CD cabinet, taking a few CD’s and depositing them in her carry bag. “Surprised to see me here?” She asked, flicking her hair from her face as she turned to finally acknowledge Killean’s existence. “I… uh….” Killean was half a man at this point. After the way Charity acted at ReVolution 109 and at The Great American Nightmare the previous night, his pride was crushed. He watched her from his private skybox hanging off the arm of Jonathon Winters. He watched her giving him knowing looks and subtle winks multiple times when she stood at ringside. He had an ace in the hole however. Charity had no idea he was in the building last night. “Where you coming from?” She asked as she gathered some DVD’s from the opposite shelf. Lynn was quick to respond. “We’re coming from…” Killean cut her off “FR-from a preliminary hearing. It was scheduled this morning.” He was quick to act, knowing that his presence at the PRIME Pay-Per View was unknown to pretty much almost everyone except Chet Worth and some PRIME staffers. He gave Lynn a knowing look and she nodded, agreeing with Killean’s story. “I see… so you going to jail or not?” Her voice was cold and the feeling was missing. It was like he was listening to a corpse speak. She felt distant, almost inhuman in her actions and her words. “Charity… I…” But he was cut off again. “You nothing Killean. I have finally found something I am happy to be involved in and I won’t let you take that from me.” She made her way over to the closet and gathered a few outdoor sweaters and stuffed them in her bag. She zipped it up and then took her winter jacket and slipped it on in a hurry. Killean meanwhile had his head hung low and he made his way over near where she was standing. “What about… us?” His voice was desperate with a sense of urgency. He was searching for the solution but just looking into Charity’s cold and uninviting eyes seemed to tell him the answer. “US?! Us you ask?! Well let me tell you about US Killean! We are almost non existent. We have been for a long time. You know what I’m talking about. You had such a hard on by the idea of beating up on Matt that I was a non factor! You even went as far as to have me parade around and call out Mary Ward. MARY!!! The wife of your former friend. You made me say that she wasn’t a supportive wife or a good woman. It was enough to make me sick. Mary knows what I really thought. I spoke to her shortly after Colossus and we cleared the air. I don’t hate her and she doesn’t hate me. But Killean…” The tension in the room was so thick. The scene was reminiscent of a brutal one sided boxing match. Killean was the underdog, taking a beating and standing on rubber legs waiting for the eventual knockout blow. Lynn Jacobs was forced to stand by and watch the lopsided fight. Her emotions were mostly in check but she had to feel a little for Killean as he was getting verbally attacked and emotionally dismantled by the relentless charge. As for Charity, she was the fist swinging champion, striking blow after blow like an animal fighting for its first meal in days. She just wouldn’t quit and wound up to deliver the final blow. “… I HATE YOU!” Ouch. “I HATE everything you stand for!” Double Ouch. “And I especially HATE that you have pushed me away for your own gains.” That’s three. The ref should be stopping the fight. “You never seemed to care a lick for me!” Ummmm… “You neglected my needs while I cared for your daughter!” Ok, that’s enough… the bell rang already. “And I love her to death but even she isn’t enough to make you look good anymore!” Damn, talk about beating a dead horse. Killean was done, his body was lifeless and the only thing keeping him on his feet was his unconscious balance. Lynn was still cringing at the verbal massacre she just witnessed. He was always strong in situations of physical tryst but emotionally he was never a very tough man. In fact, if Charity knew anything about Killean, it was his weaknesses and sure enough she had no problem locating them. After his wife passed away, he was a mental wreck and sought attention from a skilled doctor and he happened to run into Matt Ward. Matt helped him through difficult issues many times and Killean thought he was a better man for it. It was those talks that gave him strength when Matt turned on him. But without constant help, Killean was beginning to turn into a mess of thoughts. Defeated and broken, he slumped to the floor on his knees. The Supreme Machine of the wrestling ring was a vulnerable human being outside of it. “Charity… ple… I…” His voice barely had any tone to it. He was almost whispering, looking up at his girlfriend with a twinkle in his eye. Charity had a reputation to be a very caring and open person and that was one of the main reasons that Killean felt as strong as he did for her. But now, she had changed. “You are nothing but an embarrassment to yourself and everyone around you! I’m going to go where I am appreciated and I get attention.” She turned away from the groveling mess that Sirrajin had become. She took her bag and made her way to the door. Killean suddenly sprung to life and tried to grab her but Lynn was quick to jump in the middle to separate the quarreling couple. Killean was too strong for her but she focused on getting Charity out the door instead. She then called for help. “DAVE! Get in here now!” From the hallway, Officer Carlyle emerged and made his way around the departing Charity. He then helped Lynn restrain the struggling Killean as he tried to look out into the hallway. Charity turned back once more. A bright flash caught Killean’s eye. The gold necklace, the one that Killean had given her when he first told her that he loved her was still hanging around her neck. Killean took it as a sign of hope that the Charity of old was still in her body. He had a chance… “Look what you’ve become and brought yourself to… you’d never survive in jail. You don’t have the balls anymore. So pray that you don’t end up there.” She turned and made her way for the stairs. Killean slouched a little in the grasp of both officers but jumped once more and gave it one last effort. “CHARITY!!! Don’t go now! PLEASE!” She disappeared down the stairwell and Killean dropped back from the grasp of Lynn and Dave. He came to rest, hanging over the arm of the couch in the main room. He was moaning the word ‘No’ over and over again as he tried to catch his breath. Tears began streaming down his cheeks. Killean clumsily stumbled to the window, wiping the tears away. He caught a glimpse of Charity putting her bag in the trunk of an unmarked four door black sedan. She walked to the passenger side back door and opened it. Killean could have sworn that she looked up at him one last time before disappearing into the car. His eyes lit up as he watched, pressing his hand against the glass like he was trying to communicate. But it was soon a moot point as the car drove down the road and faded into the thick wall of falling snow. He rested his head against the window briefly, the hand that he pressed to the glass sliding down to his side. He pulled himself away from the window and turned back to where Lynn Jacobs and David Carlyle were standing. He was a mess. The image in his face was clear. He hadn’t slept well in over a week and he wasn’t about to start anytime soon. “Perhaps we should…” Lynn began but Killean, slumping to sit on the couch finished the sentence for her. “Leave me alone.” The two officers didn’t move at first. Lynn didn’t know if Killean really meant it or not. Perhaps it was a call for help. “Leave me… NOW!” Killean’s voice showed more life within that short demand than it had during the entire conversation with Charity. Lynn gave David a pat on the back and they both walked to the door. She took one last look at Killean and left his loft. Once Killean knew he was alone; he leaned his head against the back of the couch and openly sobbed. It wasn’t as if he wanted to protect the last little bit of his pride. No it wasn’t that at all. In the weeks since Charity had left, he had become what he was just shortly after his wife passed away. He was a mess of emotions that he just couldn’t get a grasp on. He was a big man with an even bigger heart. He was more human than he ever let on while in a wrestling ring. ~o~ Monday, October 30, 2006 4:15 PM “Really? Just outside of Fountain City, Indiana… great, that’s what I wanted to know.” Killean was speaking on the phone with a security official from the Winnipeg International Airport. He had gotten to know him when he was detained and questioned at the airport just prior to getting arrested. He was hitting the tail end of the call when Vegas walked through the door. Killean waved for him to get a drink and sit as he was just finishing the conversation. “She hopped a plane only an hour ago? Headed for Indianapolis… Alright, thanks for your help.” Killean hung up the phone and finished writing down the information. Millions of thoughts were running through his mind but he honestly felt a little better about the whole situation. He was only a few hours removed from the confrontation with Charity, but while he was given time alone to think he realized he needed to handle the situation step by step and he couldn’t just rush into anything. But he had one problem. That’s where Vegas entered into the picture, or at least that’s where Killean hoped he would. He rose from his seat and joined Vegas at the island table, cracking a cold beer that Vegas knew he would want. “Dave…” Killean started, immediately gaining the attention of his best friend and winning a slight look of hostility. “Dude, you know I hate my first name…” “That should prove to you how important this is to me.” Pointing out the obvious, Sirrajin turned to take a quick drink of his beer. He then turned back to Vegas. “I found out where the cult is. This guy I knew from Airport security spotted Charity and that asshole Frederick Harris boarding a flight just over an hour ago bound for Indianapolis. He managed to get his hands on a flyer that they dropped on the ground. They’re in Fountain City, Indiana. But I have a problem and that’s what I called you here for.” Killean stopped, taking a deep breath and trying to contain all this thoughts from spilling out in one big mess. Vegas meanwhile sipped on his drink and he was stuck on Killean’s every word. However, he hoped that Killean wouldn’t ask for a favor that he just couldn’t do. Killean kept going. “Anyways, I called you here to ask you a big favor. I know I don’t ask for much and sometimes it doesn’t seem I appreciate the help that everyone offers, but I do. I really do and being in this state…” Killean reached down and tapped the ankle bracelet he was wearing, a constant reminder the terrible state that he was in. Shrugging off the feeling of confinement, he finally asked the favor. “…has really reminded me that my friends are all I have and I sometimes take things for granted. I’d be ready to do this myself in a heart beat but as you see I’m kinda grounded to my room for being a bad boy so to speak. I respectfully ask if you could help me with Charity and go down to Fountain City. I know it’s a lot to ask, but I’ll bank roll the entire thing…” “Ugh Kill, I was hoping you wouldn’t ask me that…” Vegas paused after cutting Killean off. He put his head in his hands and sighed briefly and then shook his head. “Dude, without you at the club I have double the workload on my hands. I have gotten some help from a few guys in getting the loads out and on time but I’m struggling down there. Without you, the place is barely surviving. If I was to take off for a few days, we’d be sunk. We wouldn’t make loads and we would lose contracts. There is no way…” He rose from his seats and wiped his palm across his forehead in frustration. Killean had asked him for the one thing that he just couldn’t provide. He kind of knew what Killean would ask when he overheard him talking on the phone when he walked in. Really, what could Vegas do? Their delivery service needed him and even though the strip club was well managed, Killean or Vegas were always there at the start of the evening to ensure things ran smoothly. What options were left? More importantly, where in the heck could Killean turn? “I know… you were just the only person that I could ask. But I know that the club relies on you with me not able to be there.” Killean leaned down and rested his forehead on the counter in between his arms. He kind of felt like a heel for asking such a large favor, but he remembered that Vegas always told him not to hold back, that he could ask him for anything. Vegas came around and rested his arm around Killean, trying to comfort his best friend. He wanted to be there for Killean, after all, what were friends for? Killean had done so much for him, biggest of all was Killean fronting him the money to invest in their business venture. Killean also put up the down payment on a house for him when he moved to Winnipeg. It had all been repaid but Vegas wanted to return the favor properly since he knew that he wouldn’t have been able to make it when he moved from San Diego by himself. “Man if I could I would… in a second and you know that.” Killean nodded in response. “I just can’t leave the club behind for any length of time. I’m over here right now on crossover time. The last loads went out and in about half an hour, the business in the club will pick up and I have to be around while that starts. I… damnit I hate myself for leaving you high and dry on this.” “Whoa, it’s not your fault man. Really, I’ll just have to find another way… Somehow I HAVE to find another way.” Killean gave Vegas a small hug of friendship, showing that there were absolutely no hard feelings. Their friendship went back for years. David Vegas was there for him when he first wanted to start wrestling many years back. They first met when Killean began to attend the gym that Vegas managed. Dave even dated Killean’s sister Summer at one point but it had been over four years since Summer passed away. Vegas and Killean swore to always be friends and that they wouldn’t let a soul, living or dead come between them. Killean saw him more like a brother and he knew the feeling was more than mutual. “Listen man, I should head back to the club. I need to balance the books for today’s shipments that went out. It’s gonna take me a little bit. But keep me posted.” Vegas patted Killean on the shoulder and he returned the favor by patting Vegas’ hand. He nodded and Vegas walked to the door. “By the way…” Vegas said as he opened it a crack. “Indianapolis is maybe an hour away from Ohio isn’t it? Don’t you know someone there?” Killean’s eyes lit up as he snapped his head to look at the closing door. Vegas was gone, but his words were replaying in his mind like a broken record. It wasn’t possible was it? Ohio? Mary and Matt? He had contacted Mary the middle of last week and they had been playing telephone tag since. Perhaps Mary was waiting until Matt was out of the house before daring to answer the phone with Killean on the other end. Either way, he wasn’t really calling to talk to Mary. Killean’s road in life had always been bumpy, but now it was getting so bad he realized that he couldn’t do it all on his own anymore. Matt had been there many times in the past and maybe he could convince his former friend to let bygones be bygones. It was a long shot and he was willing to take it. He was willing to try anything. But this? Could he really call Mary back and ask her to at least check this out for him? He was looking on his laptop as the thoughts swam through his already flooded mind. Fountain City turned out to be only a short distance from the Ohio and Indiana state line. According to his mapping, Dayton… the hometown of Mary and Matt Ward was only an hour or so away from Fountain City. The distance was at least realistic. But how would Mary explain a day trip like that? Their daughter Georgie would need a sitter and Matt would definitely insist that she not go alone in any case. He was protective that way, just like she was in regards to his wrestling. As it turned out, Killean just couldn’t come up with any viable ideas or alternatives. The police had already declined to help in so many words. Vegas was out… so that just left one option. He rubbed the skin just underneath the ankle bracelet, cursing it and his situation up the river. He thought out loud. “What a fuckin’ pickle...”
