Date: Yesterday He had spent most of the day attempting to keep her happy, but had no success. The lack of visual stimulation had certainly contributed to Desiree’s crankiness. Baby’s like those kind of things. It could be anything. A simple dishcloth being flopped around with a large spoon would do the trick. Or so he thought. Listening to her unpleasant cries as he was flailing the cloth back and forth in her view, he finally gave up dropping the inane device on the table. The smack that the plastic spoon caused only worsened her contempt for the current situation. She screamed louder. 'Damn girl you got some vocal chords on you.' It was a sarcastic response to her constant crying, but a truthful one as well. Killean picked her up from the basket and held her in his left arm. His right arm frantically reached for the cordless phone on the island table. He dialed quickly, not missing a beat like he had dialed the number millions of times in the past. After a few rings he started prancing around impatiently. Finally, he heard someone pick up. 'Hello?' 'Viv I am so glad to hear your voice!' He exclaimed, trying to hide most of the excitement he had for successfully reaching someone he thought of as a mother. She cared for his home in San Diego. But it really wasn’t his home anymore. For the return of her great housekeeping services and the constant parental inspiration she was, he pretty much paid for the home while she could call it her own. But it was the parental inspiration that prompted him to call her this time. 'My boy, what could be such an emergency?' 'You are never going to believe this.' It’s true she wouldn’t. He had never expected it himself, but he was now a father. Granted he had already scheduled a paternity test to put to rest his constant doubts that she was actually his. He could almost sense it when he looked into her eyes that she was indefinitely a Sirrajin, but he needed to quench his thirst for the undeniable truth. Meanwhile he spent much of ten minutes explaining his little emergency to Vivian and she listened every way. She did have a smart comment for him though. 'Well baby I knew all those nights with ladies would catch up to ya.' He could hear her laugh on the other end of the line but she knew that he needed assistance and she was willing to do everything to help. 'I can be there tomorrow my dear.' She said, moving as she spoke to mobilize herself to get packed up for a visit to a chilly, snow laden city of Winnipeg. 'Jesus thank you Vivian. I’ll book you a flight leaving in the morning immediately. Consider it done.' Killean was relieved, yet it was only 2:30 PM in the afternoon and he had twenty-four hours to go until he could expect any type of assistance. After a goodbye sequence, Killean dropped the phone on the kitchen counter. Desiree had managed to fall asleep in Killean’s arms for a short while, giving him another sign that she was his even more. Why he had doubts he never knew. Maybe it was still his immature personality shining through. Constant images of Maury Povich haunted his mind along with the ever popular line of, 'I’m not that baby’s daddy!' He scared himself a little and swore he would never do that. Desiree had grown on him in the last day since he found her. He had taken a quick run to the corner store with her to get a small amount of supplies to last but he was quickly running low. He figured it was time to take the car and go to the supermarket to get more things, including some toys and other things for Desiree. He grabbed his coat and his keys and made his way to the door. He closed it behind him before realizing what he had done. He opened the door again slowly and peaked around the corner to look at Desiree. 'How could I?' he thought to himself as he made his way over to pick up the basket she was resting in. He wrapped her tight in an extra blanket after he fitted her with the little coat that he picked up for her yesterday. He was about ready to leave again, this time with Desiree when he realized his trip to the store in the car would be a tad difficult. 'Now where are you gonna sit Dizzy?' He asked her, never expecting an answer but it felt comfortable to talk with her anyways. He remembered that his neighbors had a younger child and figured he would ask to borrow their car seat. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ YouIn your shellAre you waiting for someone to rescue you From yourselfDon't be disappointed when no one comes Don't blame me you didn't get itDon't blame me you didn't get itDon't blame me you didn't get it I already told you that falling is easyIt's getting back up that becomes the problemBecomes the problemIf you don't believe you can find a way outYou've become the problemBecome the problem ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ He continued to strap the car seat that contained his new daughter into the car as people who entered the car park watched him. He knew plenty of the people and some even looked at him strange knowing that he didn’t have a live in girlfriend, nor did he have a daughter before yesterday. Finally tightening the last strap, he was convinced that Desiree was safe and secure and he pinched her little cheeks and she let out a giggle in delight. He shut the door and shook his head, having a genuine large smile on his face for the first time in a long time. Rounding the car and climbing in to the driver’s seat, he double checked to make sure she was secure and began to pull out of the car park. Pulling on to the street, he figured he should put on some music. But then he thought that Desiree might not like the loud sounds that bands like A Perfect Circle or Disturbed might play. So he figured he would switch through the local radio stations and let her take her pick. Looking at her in the rear view mirror, he began to switch stations. He found a jazz station but she made a little whimper and changed it. Next he put on a classic rock station and she whimpered again. Then he located a country station and cringed. He then heard Desiree in the back crying and smiled. 'My kinda kid.' He quickly switched to the local pop station and Kelly Clarkson was on. Desiree seemed to calm down and break a cute little smile which Killean took notice of in the mirror. He continued to drive listening to music that most men would not admit to liking. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ YouAll aloneAre you waiting for someone to make you whole?Can't you see?Aren't you tired of this dysfunctional routine? During the day, that was where all the old people went, spending most of the afternoon almost running into people with their carts. The older people were to be respected, yet they couldn’t drive, walk across the street at the proper time or ride their scooters on the sidewalk. Many a time did Killean almost run over older people on their scooters when he was driving. They had the nerve to run around on those motorized wheelchairs and do it in the middle of the road. But all was not that bad. Sure he was surrounded by old people. Sure, he had no idea what to by for Desiree to keep her occupied. But he had to admit, he had fun doing it. He took an inventory in the cart and counted the toys, the books and the other play things that he was almost certain she would enjoy. Of course, nothing she could choke on. That wouldn’t be safe and he was a safe kinda guy! He then looked to his right and found a Sponge Bon Square Pants CD. He didn’t know many kids but it was obvious that she would like it. He handed it to her to hold on to and she smiled, sticking the corner of the case in her mouth to suck on. 'Pacifiers…' He said, wondering where in the store he could go to find the baby products. He made his way aimlessly around the store, Desiree still happily sucking on the end of the CD case that contained Sponge Bob’s delightful songs. As he turned a corner, he caught eye of the baby wipes and stopped to take a look. A couple women came from an adjacent aisle and saw the single daddy and his cute daughter sitting in the cart. One was rather well endowed and the other was short yet attractive. They approached the cart and started making faces at then happy child. 'Ohhhhhh what a cutie! She looks so happy shopping with her daddy!' The well endowed woman said, taking another sip of the slurpee that she had in her hand. He friend agreed. 'Look at those cheeks, they are so squishy!' Killean turned to see the women paying attention to Desiree, pinching her cheeks and tickling her under her chin. Killean stood frozen, loading some wipes and diapers into the remaining space in the cart that was not occupied by toys. 'Ladies, could you tell me where I could find the pacifiers?' Killean questioned, trying to look as innocent as he could. 'First child?' The well endowed woman asked. Killean just nodded and the woman and her friend giggled. 'Hopefully not the last.' The other whispered back. They giggled again and led the way with a curled finger. Daddy couldn’t help but smile at his daughter who giggled herself and sucked on the CD case. He rubbed her hair. 'Good girl. Keep it up.' ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Killean was relieved to be on his way after dropping well over five-hundred dollars at the store on Desiree. He had promised her that he would play her new CD on the way home and if it was at all possible, she complained that he hadn’t started it yet. She cried constantly in her car seat, waiting for Killean to start the CD. He was fumbling with the wrapping on the disc, trying to drive at the same time. 'Just wait, just wait I’ll play it don’t worry.' He really didn’t think that would help and he was right. She was being just as loud as before and she seemed a bit older than she really was. She was trying to communicate more than most three month olds usually did. After stopping at a light, he finally managed to wrestle (no pun intended) the CD from the plastic wrapping and quickly opened the case and slid the CD into the car stereo. He turned the sound up a tad and soon enough, Desiree was being calmed by the children’s music that she so craved. She pointed at her daddy in the rear view mirror and broke a playful smile, making Killean smile in return. A car pulled up beside him as he was playfully making faces at her in the mirror making her giggle with delight. The lady looked over and saw him making the faces with his tongue hanging out and she laughed, also hearing the music leaking out through the partially open window. Killean caught a glimpse of her laughing as he made small farting noises with his tongue out still. He blushed and then looked at Desiree and continued his playfulness. It must have been a sight to see a husky man with sunglasses and a beard making playful faces and listening to Sponge Bob. She looked back to see Desiree looking out the back window at her. The lady then made a funny face and Desiree happily obliged and began to giggle again. The lady smiled and then waved, driving off as the light was now green. Killean then started to drive, looking at Desiree in the mirror again. 'You sure make an impression on people don’t you Dizzy?' But she wasn’t listening. She was listening to her music and her eyelids were fluttering. He was on his way with Desiree to get tested for paternity. But somehow when he looked at her in the mirror, he knew that he didn’t need the test. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 'The hint was there Mr. Archer. It was hidden in the message. What’s yours son?' ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The moon was bright and was shining directly in the window into the spare bedroom reflecting off the polished wooden rail of the new crib that Killean assembled for Desiree. He was in this for the long run and decided to quickly get all the necessary equipment for her. After all, she couldn’t continue to sleep in the cramped basket she was found in on his doorstep only a short thirty hours earlier. She was sucking her thumb and resting on her back. He had been trying to get her to sleep for almost an hour and he thought he finally succeeded. It finally began to hit him. After the entire day, it never made a lasting impression on him until now. He had a daughter. Killean Sirrajin, the man called 'The Supreme Machine', the man who less than two weeks earlier had left his former friend in a bloody mess in front of his wife on television after the most savage attack Playboy TV had ever seen, was a father. He had the responsibility of caring for himself and another human life. He had always dreamed about having children and being there to care for them one day. But he had also envisioned a mother being there as well, helping to care for their children. He dug into his pocket and pulled out the yellow piece of paper and read it over. He smiled and shook his head, still sort of in denial that he had now run into such a large responsibility. He let the paper go and it slowly fluttered to the floor. He was happy, truly happy for the first time in a long time. After long hours of waiting but sort of knowing the truth, the test results were handed to him with a smile from the lab technician. It was official, Desiree was his. Little Desiree was a Sirrajin. He looked out the window towards the moon and dangled his arms into the crib. After looking into the sun and fading away for a moment, he was brought back by a warm little touch on his index finger. He looked down to see the twinkle in the little girl’s eye, the light of the moon reflecting from her gorgeous eyes into his. She giggled and tugged lightly on his finger, trying to play. But Killean took it as a sign. His daughter had come home and accepted Killean as her father. He smiled down into the crib and it was always as he pictured it. He had the most beautiful daughter in the world and he couldn’t be more delighted. Lyrics from Staind – Falling
