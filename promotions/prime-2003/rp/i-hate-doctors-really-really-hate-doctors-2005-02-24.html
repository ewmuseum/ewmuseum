I hate doctors, really really hate doctors.

24 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/24/05 ~Health. It is said that if you have your health, you are doing just fine. Some people are healthy by definition, they don't smoke, drink rarely, exercise daily, and eat right. They are rarely sick. Others are sick all the time, these people usually don't take care of themselves. They don't take care of their body, so their body takes care of them. Jon Kano falls into both categories. He works out religiously, but he drinks. He eats pretty decently, but he smokes a pack of cigarettes a day.~ *The scene opens up inside of a doctor's office examination room. Jon Kano, dressed in a light brown Armani suit with a grey, silk tie and black dress shoes with a shine on them that would make Stevie Wonder shade his eyes, is pacing in the room. One can only guess that he is pacing because of anxiety of the doctor. He begins to look around the room, looking for something to entertain himself with. As he rummages through a cupboard, he finds various medical tools. First, he finds a pair of forceps, he looks at them for a second, chuckles, and puts them back. Suddenly, the doctor walks in. Kano slams the cupboard door shut and tries to make it look like he wasn't doing anything. The doctor, a middle-age man with salt and pepper hair and a slight gut and of an average height, is wearing a white lab coat with his stethescope draped over his neck. The two men shake hands and the doctor sits on his stool as Kano sits on the exam table.* Doctor: Well, Jon, I got the results of all of your tests and such back. Kano: Hey, Doc, not a 'Hi, Jon,' 'Hello, Mr. Kano,' or even a, 'It's nice to see you, Jon.' Nice bedside manner, so to speak. Doctor (sarcastically): Oh I am so sorry. Kano: Are all the tests good? Doctor (getting serious): Well yes, but no. *Kano gets a confused, yet worried look on his face.* Kano: What? It can't be both. It is either one or the other. Doctor: As far as the blood tests, they are all fine. I am glad that you got your knee done last year, though. I saw the surgeon's notes on that procedure, he took a hell of a lot of scar tissue out of your left knee. Kano: Yeah, I know that. Is that it? Doctor: No, I also am still concerned about your neck. Your C-5 vertabra is healed, but I still strongly suggest you not wrestle. Kano: Doc, that is ancient history. It was over three years ago since that Russian son of a bitch cracked my neck. And next time I see him, I will return the favor. Doctor: Jon, damnit, I have known you since you were ten years old, and I have been your doctor for fifteen of those years. Your neck, as strong as it may be, can't take a whole lot more punishment. I remember when you first started wrestling and you said you didn't want to be one of those wrestlers who ended up leaving the business in a wheelchair because they didn't know when to quit. *Kano stands up off the table and is getting visibly angry.* Kano: God damnit, George. I am only twenty-nine. I am just now getting as good as I can get. I ain't quitting now, so I can wonder for the rest of my life what could have been. And before you say that I don't need the money, it has never been about the money, shit, I would wrestle for free. Hell, I have wrestled for free. *The doctor is visibly upset and begins to raise his voice to counteract Jon Kano's loudness.* Doctor: Listen to me, Jon. This is what is going to happen. One night you will be wrestling a match, maybe a month from now, maybe ten years from now. You will be wrestling this match and your opponent goes for maybe, I don't know, a powerbomb. He grabs you and lifts you up, but you slip and land just right on your neck, back, or head. And then you live the rest of your life in a wheelchair. And what if Kristin and the baby are watching this happen? Kano: Listen, doc. *Kano demeanor has changed back to his usual calm, laid-back style as he sits back onto the table.* Kano: I know that can happen, but hell I could take a header down the stairs one morning when I have a hangover and the same thing will be the result. Doc: Well, Jon, that is another thing I need to talk to you about. You drink too much, and you need to quit smoking. Kano: I know, I know. But what the hell, you only live once, well unless you are a Buddhist. *Kano's trademark smirk comes across his face.* Doc: Well, Jon, I can see I am not going to talk any sense into you, so I guess I'll see you around. Kano: Six months, right? I'll see ya then to have this conversation once again. Doctor: Wait, Jon. How in the hell are you not hurt from Culture Shock? *Kano's smirk goes away.* Kano: I think all of the pain from Culture Shock went to a place you can't see. I am going to get retribution, it may not be today or tomorrow, but Christ himself as my witness, I will seriously f*ck Boda's world up and his little Dark Age buddies, too. Now doctor, I say good day. *Kano gets off the exam table, shakes hands with the good doctor, and leaves the room.* Doctor: Give them hell, kid. *The scene fades to black.* ~Kano has his health, at least physically. The question is, do you?~
