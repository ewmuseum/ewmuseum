The time has come for my turn

10 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/10/2005 ~Jon Kano has been up and down the road a few times. He has seen the world too many times to count. In his travels, he has seen way too much of way too many things in his relatively short life span of less than thirty years. When Kano was in the Army for two years prior to being discharged due to his brother's untimely death, he was stationed for a time in Serbia, as part of a UN peacekeeping force, during the 'ethnic cleansing' done by Slobodan Milosevic in the mid 90s. He saw the horrors of war and what hatred can do. He remembers a small girl, of about seven or eight years of age, laying in the street of a village, dead. She was beaten to death and the sight almost made Kano throw up right then and there. Hell, he was only nineteen then. He had barely been outside of the United States, just for a couple trips to Mexico. Then he was thrust into this position of being exposed to death. He was happy in the Army. Hell, his dad had just retired a Master Chief from the US Navy, four months after Jon enlisted in the Army. Jon Kano would have gladly been a career Army man. He loved his job, which was demolition. That all changed when death hit home and took his brother, Ken, whom was also an Army demolition expert. Kano's life changed forever. He lost his only sibling and his best friend. The only comfort was his then-girlfriend, now wife Kristin. Well there was that, and the bottle, whose love affair with Kano has become something of legend. After Ken died, Jon worked a couple odd jobs. He worked as bouncer at a couple clubs and as a waiter for a short time. All the while, taking a class here and there in business at the local community college in Las Vegas. He then took a personal loan out and invested in some real estate, in two months, he had doubled up. He continued to make wise investments in Las Vegas real estate and in the surrounding areas. That is how he made his fortune, well that and wrestling. Kano began wrestling as a tribute to his brother, never knowing that he was so damn good at it. The rest as they say is history.~ *The scene opens up inside of a crude wrestling training center. This place doesn't have world-class facilites. It has three rings each one about twenty feet from the others. Along one wall is different weight lifting equipment, universal machines and free weights. Along the reverse wall are treadmills, stationary bikes, and punching bags. There are about a dozen young men, four in each ring, practicing their wrestling skills. Watching over these young wrestlers is one trainer in each ring, with an older man, of about 70 years of age with slightly graying and balding hair, supporting himself with a cane on the gym floor.* Old man (yelling at the trainer in the closest ring): Damnit, Tony. Get those bastards to actually throw a chop, instead of pussyfooting it. *As the trainer begins to move towards the students to instruct them, the side door of the gym opens. You cannot see whom is entering the gym, as the sunlight coming from the door is absolutely blinding. The old man shields his eyes, but still tries to make out who is entering his training facility.* Old man: Who is there? Identify yourself. *The man entering takes two steps in and reveals himself as Jon Kano, decked out in a 'REAL LEGEND' t-shirt and dark blue jeans. Kano is smiling from ear to ear as he steps closer to the old man.* Old man (whom by now is smiling as much as Kano): Jon!!! Why are you here? I mean, oh hell, come here. *The two men embrace each other with a friendly hug.* Kano: Mickey, I figured I would drop in and see how everything is going. How are you, my friend? *Mickey leans a little more heavily on his cane and gives a good cough, as old men seem to do.* Mickey: Well, Jon. The diabetes is getting to me a little bit. I have been watching you on television, Jon. What the hell are you trying to prove? *Kano is taken aback by his old friend's comment. He puts a bit of space between himself and Mickey.* Kano: What ever do you mean, sir? Mickey: Jon, you were my best student. I trained wrestlers who wrestle great, but you were a great wrestler. You have made me so proud with every title you have won and the worldwide acclaim. And I fear you are pissing it all away. You can't stand alone in your fight against the Dark Age, Jon. *Kano's jovial face, and his face of happiness disappears, being replaced by a scowl.* Kano: Mickey, I know you love me like I was your son and you have taught me a great deal, nearly all that I know. You must understand, the Dark Age is pure evil. I know I am not as pure as the driven snow, but compared to the Dark Age, I am a saint. They are out to dominate PRIME, and I simply cannot allow that. Mickey (raising his voice): Damnit, Jon. Listen to me, you hard-headed son of a bitch. You can't beat them on your own. Kano (raising his voice to match the octave of Mickey's): You aren't listening, Mickey. I have to do this. I am not on my own. Everyone in PRIME who has any self-respect is standing firm against the Dark Age. I just choose to walk alone and not subscribe to any of these factions fighting for their own interests in PRIME. *As the two men shout, all activity in the training building stops as trainees and trainers alike stare at the shouting match. Suddenly the two men shouting notice what is going on and stop. They look at their spectators.* Mickey: Hey, you little bastards why don't you get back to work. That is why none of you will be as big as Kano here. Kano: Now, Mickey, you don't know that. Mickey: Well why don't you motivate them a little bit? *Kano sighs and motions for all the wrestlers-in-training to get on the floor and have a seat. All of these wrestlers-in-training oblige as Mickey and his trainers lean against the far wall, all men listening intently to Kano's forthcoming words.* Kano: I met Mickey when I was twenty years old. My brother had died earlier that year and I was having a hard time dealing with that and being discharged from the Army. I was bouncing around, working different jobs, really not enjoying life. My brother and I were wrestling fans growing up and I mostly came in here that fateful day on a whim. I was in great physical shape, but Mickey still made me work. He, at times, made me wish I was dead. There were times when I was completely tapped out, had nothing left both physically and mentally. There was one time I was close to that door and I was going to quit. Mickey, remember what you said to me? *Mickey pushes himself away from the wall and leans heavily on his cane.* Mickey: Hell yes. I told you to go, to get the hell out of my gym, that I didn't want your pansy ass in here if you couldn't cut it. *Kano looks at his teacher with pure admiration and smiles.* Kano: And I turned around and busted my ass for the rest of the time. This is not an easy business, guys. I thought it would be pretty easy. You don't realize if you make it to a larger federation, you are on the road 200 days plus a year. You are always on the go, got little privacy. It is very rare that I can go out to eat with my wife and eat in peace. I get approached by fans wanting a picture or autograph, and I always try to do that. I look at the fans and know that without them I wouldn't be getting a paycheck from wrestling. I love doing this business. Son, you look like you have a question. *Kano is staring at this super-skinny young man wearing baggy workout clothes and looks like he is about 18 years old, tops.* Skinny-kid (in a nervous, squeeky voice): Uhhh, Mr. Kano, sir, uhhh, aren't you scared? I mean, I know you are a great wrestler but at ReVolution you got a steel cage match with Boda, aren't you afraid? *Kano's expression turns to a bit angrier.* Kano: Let me tell you and everyone else in this gym something. I fear no man or group of men. I have beaten Boda before, I can do it again. He says he is going to cripple me. That, I will admit, catches my attention. Hell, you ask any professional wrestler what their biggest fear is, and they will say being crippled in the ring. We have all seen someone be crippled in our careers and any decent wrestler would never threaten to cripple another. So to answer your question, I don't fear people like Boda and the rest of the Dark Age, but I do fear being crippled for life. That is a possibility, I ain't gonna deny it. Boda and myself are to step into that ring and grind each other's face into hamburger against the cage. Skinny-kid: But, Mr. Kano, what do you think will happen at ReVolution? Kano: Same thing that always happens when Boda and me wrestle each other. We will put on an instant classic and both of us will end up in the hospital that night. I hope the fans watching ReV at home set TiVo to record this because it is sure to be a great one. It is going to be brutal, bloody, and you will see things that you never thought you would see. Boda wants to cripple me and I just want to beat the shit out of him to prove my point. Skinny-kid: What point is that, Mr. Kano? Kano: That I have been, am, and always will be the better Mogul. That is the debate that has raged since he and I stopped tagging together, who was better Kano or Boda? I am, and I will prove it at ReVolution. That son of a bitch may as well go fishing with Guido in the dingy, because he is dead to me. He played me like a fool back at Culture Shock, punking me out in front of thousands of people. He embarassed me while my wife was watching at home. He wants to end my career and cripple me inside of a cage, that is just fine. I have heard this speech before from way too many people who said they were going to take me out and here I am, standing right here, passing a little wisdom onto the next generation of possible superstars and legends. You guys have an opportunity to do something great and live the life of fame and fortune. There is a price to pay, and you will pay that price throughout your career. At ReVolution, Boda and I will both pay the price, but when all the dust has settled, when all the smoke has cleared, there will only be one of us with our arm raised in victory. Any guesses on who that will be? *The gym is perfectly dead quiet, you can almost hear a pin drop.* Kano: Well, I can't say that I blame you guys not saying anything. I mean hell, Boda and I have taken turns beating the hell out of each other. We could wrestle a hundred times and we would each win fifty. We are very closely matched, but this time, inside of that demonic, sadistic steel cage, it is my turn to humiliate Boda. It is my turn to make him feel the pain of having someone who was as close to you as a brother beat you senseless while thousands of people watch. It will be my turn to make Boda scream in pain and wish to God that he wasn't born. And it will be my turn to show absolutely no mercy despite what past we might have. And it will be my turn to prove I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano's words echo through the gym and the young trainees wonder what is coming next in the epic Kano/Boda saga. They will have to tune into ReVolution to find out just like the rest of us.* ~Well, hell. I think Kano is a little motivated, don't you? Will Boda be ready for the awakened giant? Will Kano be able to deliver on his words? The only thing that is for sure is that at this week's edition of ReVolution, inside of a steel cage, all hell will break loose.~
