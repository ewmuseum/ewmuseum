Slowly a chill washed of her, like someone just walked on her grave. Becky Schulman had seen well-behaved children and some children that, well, required a little more attention. But Becky Schulman never thought of any child as being bad. However, the calm, poised young boy, all dressed up sharp for his first day at a new school, effected her like no child had before. He looked at her without kindness, malice, affection, annoyance, respect, or dread. He looked at her… like she was nothing. Like she was reflection on glass.
