The Drought (.05)

20 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/20/07 The dull white paper sparks to red, igniting beneath the heat of flame and smoldering to cold, grey ash within the same quickened heartbeat. A sharp intake of breath brings the red ring dangerously close to the brown filter of the cigarette. Ashen paper crumbles as two weather-worn, though well-manicured, fingers pinch the filter, pulling it from the dry, chapped lips. Smoke billows from his nostrils like the chimneys of an old Dick Van Dyke movie. The nose slightly flattened and just noticeably crooked from over a dozen times of being broken. The cigarette butt flips endlessly upon its arc, scattering tiny blasts of glowing red ash across the sand when it crashes down. A few scarce drops of rain splatter down upon the sand as well, creating a set of impact craters within the sand that trace along the same line as the arc of the cigarette. Tyler Rayne raises his eyes to the sky, the beginning traces of redness slipping through the veins of those orbs. The sky is clear. Beautiful. A heavy sigh lifts his battle-scarred chest. His head drops, eyes falling to gaze despondently at the hands hanging over the worn knees of his jeans. The palms of both hands are stained, a dark pigment that splashes through the gaps between fingers to paint the back of his hands with the fading trails of dried blood. The slightest stream of crimson dribbles down the knuckle of his left forefinger, conspiring to form a lone drop of blood at the tip of the finger. He watches as the drop increases in size, dangling precariously from its precipice before both size and gravity prove too powerful to be denied. The drop of blood takes a short fall to the sand, blasting away the tiny bits of grain to form a familiar looking divot. The bloodshot eyes roll upward, moving from this fresh crater to those left by the raindrops only a minute earlier. Tyler’s head tilts backward, staring blankly back into the cloudless sky. The head falls again. Eyes droop to the fingers once more. Another drop of blood forming at the tip of his finger. In this though, hidden behind a crimson tint and blurred by the filter of a liquid looking window, Tyler Rayne swears that he can see a face within the blood. A tortured, agonized face screaming from within the droplet. Trapped. The blood falls. Tiny fists beat against the inside of the droplet. The droplet explodes upon the sand like a liquid bomb, displacing even more grains of sand from the beach. And with the rending of that droplet comes an ear piercing, spine tingling shriek. It is as though the contorted face within is now released, free to relinquish its final gift unto the world before death. It is a cry he has not forgotten. A voice forever etched within the darkened recesses of memory. Tyler’s eyes squeeze tight, creasing the darkened circles beneath them. He takes a handful of deep, intentional breaths. His pulse begins to steady once again. The tears he fights at all hours of the day are kept at bay once more. The left hand flexes, closing into a tight fist before opening wide, fingers splayed, and then closing once again. His throat bobs as he swallows fear. The eyes open slowly, and though he knows no longer to trust these senses, finds his sanity once again hinges upon the truth which they reveal. His hands are clean. No drops of blood. No dark red stains spreading through the gaps of his fingers. The sand is as pristine as ever. No craters or divots from apparently imagined drops of blood. His head falls into his hands, eyes staring dully into a spot in the sand that he only thought had existed. He senses more than notices the presence of someone beside him. Though he did not hear them approach, Tyler can feel them there. Watching. “What’s a matter, kid? Seein’ things again?” The ill-intentioned glee within the voice is impossible to mask. Tyler suppresses the urge to vomit. “Just…just go away. Please.” The maniacal, condescending laugh sends an involuntary shiver through his body. “Oh, well, since you asked nicely and all…” Another chilling cackle. Tyler swallows once again, shakily pushing himself to his feet. He rubs sand from his palms onto his jeans and turns to meet eye-to-lifeless eye with his alter ego. The twisted, broken fragments of skull contort themselves into a smile. Tyler once again suppresses the bile rising from within. “Go fuck yourself. I’m tired of this…shit.” He steps forward, passing through the figment and toward the sliding glass entry of his modest beach house. The lights are off. The main room darkened. Instinct and memory guide him past the edges of the glass coffee table. Around the corner of the long leather couch. Measured steps guide him into a short hallway lined with movie posters. He glances into the frames as he passes, noting the reflection of his own hallucinated visage following behind. “Go fuck myself, eh? What? Can’t get it from that dyke, so you figure you’ll start making passes at your imaginary friend? Well, sorry to disappoint, pal, but I don’t swing that—“ Tyler spins on a heel, turning quickly and defiantly on his imagination. He stares into the endless black pits of MDK’s eyes, a fire burning within his own. “GET. OUT. OF. MY. FUCKING. HEAD!!” “Oh, don’t I wish I could. All this mamby-pamby crying bullshit is about to drive me crazy. You think I like this? You think I want to be stuck with some pussy ass mother fucker gonna bitch and moan about killin’ a few meaningless Yakuza? We did a good thing in Hawaii and—“ “We murdered people.” “It’s what we do.” “Not anymore. Not for fifteen god damn years til you--” “Til you, Mr. Blame Game. Let’s not forget that was your god damn finger behind the trigger. Your hand on the fucking knife. You killed every last one of those cock sucks, and for god damn good reason. World’s better without ‘em. Sooner you figure that out, the sooner both you and I—“ “There is no you and I.” “No you and I? What the fuck you think this is, kid? You can’t exactly break up with me, can you? Can’t just fuckin’ leave me behind. This is us til the end, son. Nothin’ you can do ‘cept—“ The image vanishes and Tyler focuses himself enough to see the large, dark object sailing at him. Thankfully, a fine set of honed reflexes puts him out of the path of destruction. The heavy thud behind him implies the kind of density associated with a rock. Perhaps a broken chunk of concrete. The tinkling of shattered glass still echoes in his ears. Tyler steps forward, removing himself from the darkest reaches of the hallway to inspect the figure that now stands framed within the jagged edges of what remains of his sliding glass door. “You do have a key, love. Gave it to you for a fuckin’ reason.” Fragments of glass crunch to dust beneath the long stiletto of her knee-high boots. Her raven hair falls in tresses around her shoulders, outlining the barely contained rage beneath her usually awe-inspiring features. Katterina Wylde stands silently imposing, nothing more than the tightening balls of her fists to indicate she’s even alive and responsive. “Not really in the mood for games, Katt. You obviously came in with somethin’ on your mind. So speak your piece and be about your fuckin’ merry.” “You took the job.” “I take lots of jobs.” A sudden gust of wind blows through the broken door, whipping her hair about like a nest of furious snakes. The force is strong enough to send him back about half a step. Glass swirls around her ankles. “Oh. That job. Duke finally went and spilled the beans, then, eh? Actually lasted longer than I—” “I won’t let you do it.” “Well, then I suppose it’s a good thing I don’t need your fuckin’ permission.” “He’s my father.” “He’s a murderer.” “So are you.” “She’s got ya there.” “And you aren’t?” “This isn’t about me.” “Beg the fuckin’ differ. Seems about the moment you start throwin’ rocks through my window, it becomes a whole hell of a lot about you.” “I won’t let you.” “We’ve had this chat before, Katt. Far too many times in fact. Gettin’ kinda old.” “Bored now?” The smile she offers lacks any sort of good nature or merriment. It is pure, unadulterated malice. With a primal, animalistic roar the woman leaps over couch, fingernails lashing forward in a pounce that harkens to her namesake. The attack, though frighteningly fast, is easy to predict. Defend. He drops to his back on the hallway floor, hands clutching her wrists as she floats over. The soles of boots planting firmly into her abdomen. All goes according to plan, except the one tiny detail of her sharpened nails slicing through the flesh of his forearms as he throws her over. Tyler groans in pain, releasing his grip on the Dark Angel immediately. She tumbles down the hall. He stands, wincing at the six inch gashes bleeding out his forearms. “Ooh. Little kitty grew some claws. Think maybe you can grow some balls to match?” Tyler responds with a sudden loss of breath, his entire body bending in the wrong direction as Katterina tackles him from behind. He hits the ground hard, damn near biting through his tongue. “Katt, I really—” The world about him swirls with blackness a second after the point of her elbow stuns the back of his head. He feels her claws ripping through his scalp. Fingers snatching a handful of hair. His forehead slams into the carpet with such force, that though only for a second, he does in fact black out. It’s the second meeting of face to floor that brings him back to consciousness. “Bitch is probably gonna kill ya, kid. Fuckin’ shit way to go, you ask me. Which you didn’t, but…” Tyler roars in defiance, throwing his considerable eighty or more pound weight advantage into a back elbow that effectively knocks Katterina from her dominant position. Her back slams against the corner of the wall at a weird angle, leaving her arched and vulnerable on the floor. Tyler only half jumps from his position on the ground. Just enough to get some elevation on the dropkick he plants into her midsection, once again bending Katt’s back across that corner. Her cry of pain is cut short by a knee to the throat. He presses harder. Nearly hard enough to snap her neck. Her fingers grasp out, clawing and scratching at his face. Deep serrations begin to peel from his flesh, though he pays them no mind. His own fingers are playing about the young girl’s face. Finding that spot, there, at the corner of her eyes. Thumbs pressing inward, painfully increasing the pressure within. Forcing her eyes out by sheer displacement. “Now that’s what I’m fuckin’ talking about!” The toe of her boot connects soundly with the back of his head. It’s a struggle to keep his eyes from rolling up in the back of his head. He falls, fighting for consciousness, beside her on the floor. It’s almost impossible to breath. Blackness clouds his peripheral vision, threatening to engulf him entirely. He vaguely realizes she is standing to his feet, just as he is getting to his own. He only barely sees her rushing at him, instinct more than conscious decision allowing him to counter her with a weak but effective hip toss. She tumbles over the back of the couch. Somewhere far off he hears the sound of breaking glass. A memory, perhaps. Or…no. The coffee table. She must have… The couch tips with her added weight, Katterina using the momentum of that fall to multiply the ferocity of her own attack. She tackles Tyler yet again, sending them both stumbling back into, and through, the wall behind. A second of dazed confusion ends with her fist against his jaw. He falls back onto the bed and she pounces, mounting atop him once again. “Now this is some action. Think you can get her to wiggle a bit while she’s there?” The fist comes fast and clean and hard enough to break his nose. The black turns to red and searing pain shoots through every neuron in his head. He barely feels the next two punches through the haze. “Wish I could say it was nice knowin’ ya, but, well, you know.” His hand shoots forward, fingers jabbing at full force into her throat. He hears the choked gasp for breath that offers him those precious few seconds to turn the fight. He sees nothing but red. Hands feel about her body, tracing up her well-defined arms to take a firm grasp of her shoulders. Using them as a fulcrum, he pulls himself up while pulling her down, bringing them both toward a collision that shatters her nose. Thick, warm blood splatters upon his face and chest. He feels the weight of her sliding away and then himself tumbles backward off the edge of the bed. He crawls, blinded with pain, to the wall, hands reaching up to grasp at anything to help him stand. Fingers find purchase on something solid, but as soon as he applies his weight, the structure rips from the wall and topples upon him. Something long and heavy crashes atop his head, splitting the scalp instantly and flashing him with blinding white pain. The pain is a catalyst, causing him to jump to his feet. She tackles again, catching him in the side and probably cracking a few ribs. The combined weight knocks them through the walls of this obviously poorly constructed house. His other side slams wholly and quite painfully into the bathroom sink. Legs give out beneath him and it is only his grip on the sides of the porcelain that keep him standing. Katterina, on the other hand, continues in her downward motion, head bouncing off the toilet seat. She falls, barely conscious, against the tub. He pushes himself away from the sink, letting gravity carry him toward the gaping hole in his wall. Though most of his body refuses to respond, his arms still work well enough to clutch the wall, keeping him up. He looks down at the bleeding and bruised body of his best friend. The one woman who’s always stood by him. Always been at his side. Had his back. Tears well in his eyes. He squeezes them shut, attempting to swallow the emotion. But the boot Katterina lifts into his groin leaves no room in Tyler’s throat for anything other than his own testicles. He can’t even keep himself from releasing a little dribble of vomit. He crashes forward, falling over Katterina’s body and tumbling into the tub. His wrist cracks against the faucet. Possibly broken. She turns, using the shower curtain to pull herself along. Even her light frame, however, is too much weight to support, and the curtain rips from the rings, falling like a shroud around her. Tyler takes his advantage, wrapping the curtain tight around her head and wasting what little energy he has left to punch the woman into submission. He relents when she finally stops moving. Minutes pass as he sits in the tub, matching his own breathing to the slow, steady rise of her body beneath the curtain. After what could have been an eternity, he seems to have gathered enough energy to pull himself from the tub, stumbling once again to that hole in the wall. A rustling from the tub brings his attention back behind, where Katterina is somehow rising to her feet as well. “Good god damn. Finish this bitch and be done with it. I’m tired of fuckin’ around.” Exhausted, completely unable to comprehend this entire situation, his head falls, dangling. There at his feet, amidst the rubble and wreckage of the bathroom wall, rests the object that split his skull. Something he’d had hanging on the wall of his bedroom. Dazed and unbalanced, Tyler carefully reaches down to pry the object from the debris. The katana Katt had given him for his thirtieth birthday. “Do it.” He doesn’t remember the sword being unsheathed. Doesn’t remember turning to stalk toward his friend and confidant. Suddenly he’s just there, standing before the bleeding and unrelenting figure of a woman who just tried to kill him. Almost succeeded. There’s a sword in his hand and then the point of it is in her gut. It slides deep and easy. Her eyes widen, blood boils over the cusp of her lips and suddenly Tyler Rayne is back in the moment. He is right there in the bathroom. Perfectly conscious. Perfectly aware. And there’s Katterina, sinking into his tub with a katana in her stomach. He turns for the bathroom door, managing to fall to his knees just before the vomit can explode from within. He falls face first to the carpet. Cold. “Tyler? Ty! Ty, what the—Oh my goddess.” The voice is familiar. Angelic even. He can’t make out a face. A form of any kind. All he can see is Katterina Wylde, dying slowly in his bathtub. The body to which the voice belongs rushes past him, the fleeting scent of lotus blossoms reinvigorating him. “Milena…” “Jesus. What the fuck did you do?” He rises to a sitting position, eyes focusing as she stalks from the bathroom. Her arm wrenches back, her fist and the handgun clutched tightly within knocking Tyler back to the floor. He watches one of his teeth bounce across the carpet. “What the fuck did you DO?” She stands over him, barrel wavering above his face. Her hands are shaking. Or maybe he is. Maybe it’s both. “Well come on. Do it then. Fuckin’ kill me.” The shaking increases. “Do it, kid. Just fuckin’…COME ON!!” His tears wash the dried blood from his cheeks. He can’t really see anything more than the endless maw of the gun’s barrel. He hears her, though, sobbing at the other end of the weapon. “DO IT YOU FUCKIN’ CUNT!” The gun drops atop him. The sound of her footfalls fade away. His hands reach blindly, grasping, finally wrapping around the barrel of the gun. He pulls it close. Pulls it onto his chest. Adjusts his grip. The barrel touches his tongue, reminding him of younger, more innocent years. Sucking on a penny to see if it would give you a shock. It’s disgusting and if he had the strength to do, he’d probably vomit again. But the most he can manage is the twitch of a finger. Enough to put a little pressure on the trigger. Enough to make the mechanism respond. The click of an empty chamber is the most disappointing sound he’s ever heard. Denied. Deprived. His only escape. The blackness cannot be held at bay any longer. It swirls and engulfs him. Claims his consciousness. And as he passes into sleep, Tyler Rayne can’t help but think that he’s lost his only chance. His one opportunity to end the pain. The anguish. The heartache. God if he could just end it all…
