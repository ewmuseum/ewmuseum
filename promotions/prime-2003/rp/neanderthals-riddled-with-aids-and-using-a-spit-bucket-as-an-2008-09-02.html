Neanderthals riddled with AIDS and Using a Spit Bucket as an offensive weapon...

2 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/09/2008 “What kind of place is this, Jeeves?” Union Jack scrunches his face up in disgust as the two of them wander further into the gymnasium, trying desperately to ignore the stench of shite and body odour whilst at the same time trying desperately not to touch or to bump into anything. All around the place, huge Neanderthals loitering around the weights section all turned to glare at them. One even growled. Jeeves sighs. “It’s a gym, sir.” “Looks more like a gigantic lavatory,” Union Jack notes, stopping dead as something crunches underfoot. He looks downward, unsure of what to expect and rather bizarrely finds himself amazed that’s a bunch of used needles. “What the bloody hell...?” Jeeves raises a speculative eyebrow. “Used needles, sir. It’s best we don’t delve too deeply into why they’re littering the floor.” Union Jack shakes his head. “I don’t care why they’re there Jeeves! What if one of these things had pierced my foot and one of these gobshites had AIDS?” “I wouldn’t worry about it sir,” Jeeves tells him, eying up offended faces looming dangerously closer. “I hardly think anyone in here has AIDS.” “You don’t think?” Union Jack questions, still irate. He points at a particularly massive individual stood in the wrestling ring right in front of them. “He alone looks like a massive poof! I can’t imagine how many back door burglar’s are lurking around this establishment!” “WHAT DID HE SAY?” The man in the ring booms. “You heard me, you nufter!” Union Jack replies. “Think you can spread AIDS around the place and get away with it, do you? They should string people like you up by your bollocks and let rats have their way with you!” The big man climbs out of the ring, with great difficulty it should be noted, and plods over towards them, the ground rumbling with each new footstep. He stops right before them and crosses his arms pointedly, looking at each of them in turn. “I’m sorry, I didn’t quite catch that?” “Deaf as well as a bum fiddler, eh?” Union Jack questions. “I’m surprise you’ve worked out which end of the needle to inject yourself with!” “Right, that’s it!” The Neanderthal lunges forward to make a grab at Union Jack, but ‘Our Sovereign’ manages to haul Jeeves in the way and take a quick step backwards as the much larger man grabs his ever faithful butler by the throat. “Deal with this, would you Jeeves?” Union Jack asks. “I’ve got a migraine.” Jeeves desperately attempts to sigh, but with the mammoths ham-hocks wrapped around his throat he can’t. He simply replies: “Very--hnnnhhh--good, sir!” Whilst Jeeves gets the life choked out of him, Union Jack sneaks around the back of the rather large individual and lines him up before lunging forward and placing him boot right between the gargantuan gobshite’s legs. The man releases his vice-like grip and Jeeves falls to the floor gagging, whilst Union Jack reaches down for the spit bucket by the side of the ring and cringing at the sight of the contents, smashes the metal saliva container down hard atop of the suspected homosexuals head. He keels over to the floor, unconscious. As Jeeves picks himself up from the floor, Union Jack glares at him shaking his head with disappointment. “You realise that this just isn’t good enough, don’t you Jeeves?” Brushing himself down, the ever faithful butler develops a befuddled expression. “What isn’t good enough, sir?” Union Jack rolls his eyes. “Your performance with that bloody hoodie was fubbing ridiculous! You think Delta Upsilon, um, er...Salamander or whatever those frightful commoners are called, will be defeated as easily as this AIDS ridden nufter?” Jeeves simply stares at blankly. “Do you think the threat of fisticuffs will seriously dissuade them?” “You’re speaking as though it were who challenger those two rapscallions to do battle,” Jeeves responds. “But don’t worry, I used to wrestle at local carnivals in exchange for toffee. I’m more than capable of handling myself in the squared-circle.” “You used to wrestle for toffee?” Union Jack questions. “How old were you?” “Twenty-four.” Union Jack shakes his head and mutters, “Jesus Christ. Anyway, what finishing manoeuvre do you intend to use to take down these DUI idiots?” Jeeves ponders the question for a time. “The Armbar.” Union Jack simply blinks. “The Armbar? What is this, 1912? The Armbar wouldn‘t stop your mother sleeping with the caller to have her numbers at Bingo come up!” Jeeves ignores the insult, something he’s become adept at during his employ, and raises a questioning eyebrow. “And what precisely do you suggest, sir?” Union Jack shrugs and glares at the downed AIDS riddled Neanderthal still lying prone upon the floor. “A kick to groin seems to be effective against even the larger opponent.” “Next,” Union Jack begins, grabbing for a broom that rested right next to where the spit bucket once lay, “we see how tough you are, just in case things take a turn for the worse.” “What do you mean--” Before the sentence can be finished, Union Jack smashes the broom over the top of his butler’s head, sending his south in the blink an eye. Grinning at the broken broomstick in his hands, Union Jack decides to change the subject entirely. “So, I was thinking we really must come up with a tag team name. I--” He pauses, realising there’s no response. He turns to see Jeeves still lying prone on the floor. “Jeeves?” There’s still no response and Jeeves still hasn’t budged an inch. “Bugger!”
