Fatherhood - Chapter 3 - Now What?

21 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 21, 2008 THOMAS MEMORIAL HOSPITAL CHARLESTON, WEST VIRIGINA AUGUST 21, 2008 AFTER REVOLUTION 169 I couldn’t remember much about the drive over here was the fact that she was silent, and I was freaking out. I remember glancing down from the blur of passing tail lights to see my speedometer, where the small needle was quickly approaching 120 miles per hour. I would glance at the rear view mirror every few seconds to check for cops, but that was clearly the least of my worries. As I quickly veered off the highway onto the exit where a small blue sign pointed out the way to Thomas Memorial Hospital, I hit traffic. I remember muttering a word that I use pretty rarely, especially around Nikkie. “Fuck…” I couldn’t see far enough ahead to figure out what was causing the backup, thanks to the exit road veering around to the right. Again, I muttered that four-letter word that I truly hated using, but for obvious reasons, I couldn’t care less at the moment. “Fuck…” After waiting in traffic for a few moments, I just couldn’t wait anymore. I pulled the Civic onto the side of the road, and although repeatedly running over those rumble strips was incredibly annoying, it was worth it. “Ah…” A moan escaped Nikkie’s mouth, and I picked up speed and was passing cars with ease, aside from those damn rumble strips. I turned right off of the exit, and sped toward the sign with the illuminated lettering reading “THOMAS MEMORIAL HOSPITAL” on it. “Here we go, Nik. Almost there,” I whispered as I pulled up to the Emergency Room drop-off. I parked the car, simultaneously opening the door and darting around to the other side. I opened her door and picked her up, holding her closely in my arms. I pushed the door closed with my hip as I rushed toward the doors. The automatic doors slid open as I jogged into the lobby of the ER. Luckily, there were only a few people in the waiting room, so I ran right up to the desk, where a dainty Latino nurse sat, shuffling through some paperwork. Upon seeing me, and more importantly, Nikkie, her expression morphed from a straight face to one of concern. “Sir, what happened here?” Her voice was calm, but obviously concerned. I took a deep breath. “She got hit.” The nurse looked puzzled. “By what?” I almost got frustrated until I remembered ”Not everyone in the world watches PRIME.” I took another deep breath and exhaled slowly. “A knee. A bruh’s knee. It was intended for me, but I ducked and she wasn’t so lucky.” I started to panic as the time began to pass. The nurse still looked confused, but I had to explain. “I wrestle. We were over in the Civic Center tonight, and this bruh’s got it out for me. He was aimin’ for me, but like I said, I ducked and she got hit. She’s pregnant, and needs to get checked out.” As soon as the words “she’s pregnant” left my mouth, the urgency in her body language was obviously upped, as she sent out a page instantly. “Okay, we’ve got a team coming down now. Sir, calm down, she’s going to be in good hands.” Before I knew it, there was a team of nurses, both male and female, ready to take my fiancée away from me. Although the nurse assured me that everything was going to be alright, it was hard to believe. I grew up learning to not trust doctors, and to this day, I’m still pretty hesitant to trust a doctor unless I know exactly who they are, what kinda history they’ve got, and the like. Hell, I had to do days of research between ReVs before I agreed to take Nikkie to Dr. Southwick. As the team of nurses took Nikkie away, I started to follow. I mean, in House, E.R., Scrubs, and the rest of those hospital sit-coms and dramas, the loved ones of the patients could usually follow the group into whatever room they were taking the patient into. However, the last nurse looked back and held her hand out, as if to stop me. She frowned, and started to walk away. “We’ll come and get you when she can be seen.” I was crushed, not being able to follow her, but I could do nothing. “’Kay…” My voice trailed off as the swinging doors rocked back and forth after the nurse’s departure. Again, I muttered the word… “Fuck…” * * * I actually fell asleep after downing four cinnamon sugar Pop-Tarts and a chocolate milk from the vending machine. I’d have preferred fresh pineapple, but hey, I won’t complain. When I finally woke up, I wiped that annoying crust that forms on your eyes away, and sat upright. I figured I was asleep for a couple of hours, because it sure felt like it, but it turned out that it had only been about 45 minutes. After a couple of moments, that same nurse who first waited on us when we came into the ER hung up the phone and smiled at me. “You can go back now. She’s in room 165. It’ll be down the hall and to your left.” I smiled and quickly thanked her as I rushed through the doors. “Thanks.” I briskly walked down the hall, glancing at the passing room numbers as I moved along. I was only in the low 100’s. I began to jog since I was still pretty far away. Within half of a minute, I hit the 160’s. Finally, after hitting 165, I pulled a left and quickly walked into the room. There she was. She had an IV in one arm, and a sling around the other, which was obviously the side that Rhett had collided with. She was out like a light, so I just sat down next to her in an awfully uncomfortable chair and grasped her hand. I whispered in her general direction. “Things are gonna be alright hun, I promise…” I took a deep breath. “…and as for Rhett Locke? He’s done. Just wait.” Shortly afterward, a nurse walked into the room and pulled her chart from the end of her bed, examining it closely before turning in my direction. I pre-empted her by asking the first question. “So what’s wrong with her?” She glanced at the chart again, and then looked back at me. “Well, she’s got a fractured humerus, as well as a grade-one concussion. Other than that, she’s got a couple of bumps and bruises, but she’ll be alright.” I sighed deeply. “And how about the baby. She’s pregnant, ya know?” The nurse smiled at me. “Like I said, she’s going to be fine, and the baby will be too. You shouldn’t have anything to worry about.” The nurse put Nikkie’s chart back onto the end of her bed and left the room, leaving me alone with my fiancée. I smiled, relieved that my fiancée and our unborn child would be alright even after the encounter with Locke earlier. Locke. The name just made my insides boil. Not many people can push my buttons like this, but one thing I grew up practicing in my household was that NOBODY comes before family, and NOBODY messes with family without repercussions. And Rhett Locke? He messed with my new family. A million things were going through my head. I was replaying the situation over and over, wondering how I could have prevented the situation from happening. My thoughts varied from how I could have kept the situation from happening to how I was going to react to Rhett. After a few moments, the only thing I could focus on was revenge. That freak could have harmed my fiancée to the point that my unborn child would STAY unborn, and I wasn’t going to have that go un-noticed. The one question I had to ask myself was simple… “…now what?”
