Against All Odds

14 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1.14.06 Another week and yet another loss that seemed to be the pattern for my career, as a wrestler. Week in and week out the win column stayed the same but it seemed like the loss column seemed to increase by at least one each week I guess I have found my home in PRIME, the bottom of the barrel, the person all the new comers face and get their first win against, only increasing their ego and deflating mine. I have found my position and I guess it is just time to be content with it. Boredom is setting in I sit here reflecting on my career, I ponder and wonder where I have gone wrong. But that right there is my problem, I over think. I’m constantly trying to weigh out where I went wrong, what I need to do next and how to fix the previous mistakes. Well that is the actual problem I was always too worried then when I took a step with my left I should have actually been leading with my right foot. But that needs to change, and too do that I need to take my mind off of my career, off of wrestling all together, actually. I sit down turn on the television to try and see what is on. I flip through the channels. Nothing is on, and then I slowly look over my DVD collection, what could I watch? I mean what haven’t I seen that I own? The answer to that is nothing. All these movies I have seen over and over again, I mean a couple of them are worth watching again but I think I want to explore my options a little more. I slowly get up and walk over to my phone, I start going through the phone book on my cellular device. The names went on and on, Aaron, Adam, Dave, Josh, Jon, Shawn etcetera. As I was just reaching to the end of my phone book with no actually ideas in sight my phone started to ring in my hand. The screen read Incoming Call Meaning the number was blocked or unavailable, seeing as I was bored I figured I would answer it. Hell, a couple of minutes playing around with a telemarketer would be a good time. After the fourth ring I picked it up, “Hello?” Expecting the voice of salesman I was surprised to hear the voice of a friend, the voice of an old acquaintance, Jimmy. “Hey Peter old Buddy.” “Jimmy what is going on buddy? Long time no talk huh?” I was pleasantly surprised to hear from him, thinking maybe tonight is the night I finally go out and do something instead of sitting around and wallowing in my own self pity. “Well I was able to watch last weeks match you had there with Chet Worth and the rest of those guys.” Right when he said those words my heart started to drop, I had hoped this conversation would have nothing to do with wrestling; I had hope we could forget about our careers and just hang out and have a good time. “And I must say I was pretty impressed.” Shocked by his last statement the only thing I was able to utter was, “Really?” “Yeah, P.V. you had a great fricken fight on your hands, I mean you out smarted everyone by climbing to the top and taking everyone down, I don’t think Chet Worth would have had a chance to win if you hadn’t knocked him and everyone else out for him.” I could tell by the sound in Jimmy’s voice he was being sincere, he must have watched it and actually been routing for me. That actually makes me feel a whole lot better. “I know, I was a little scared going for such a high risk maneuver, but I knew if I didn’t I wouldn’t even have a chance.” I said that proudly, I had made the decision that night to do something risky, and I was uncertain till this point if it was worth doing, since I had lost the match, but judging from Jimmy’s reaction it was worth it. “Anyways, the reason for me calling wasn’t to break balls about the match you had last week, it was to invite you out with some of the guys we used to hang out with. We are trying to get together and start a poker night every week if possible. Are you interested?” “But of course.” “I assume you know how to play Texas Hold Em.” “Who doesn’t these days?” This invitation to go play poker might just be what I am looking for to help me get my mind off of wrestling and start thinking about some other things. We both chuckled at that last statement getting little humor, but the chuckle wasn’t completely sincere for either of us I think, they each sounded half hearted like we knew the joke wasn’t all that funny but we did it to amuse each other. We then said our good byes and agreed on a time and place. We then parted ways for now, a friendship that once was is now beginning to look like it might be intact once again. I think this friendship I had lost will be the one thing I need again in the future for happiness. No longer will I look at my success as the gauge of my happiness, but for once I might actually be genuinely happy again. Arrival He had told me to arrive as close to six thirty as possible. Well I arrived at the destination right around six twenty nine or at least that is what my fancy Rolex said. As I looked down to see the time I realized that the Rolex was just one of many things I had purchased to try and bring myself happiness. As I stand at the footstep of his door I realize that it isn’t these material things that make me happy it is the friends I have, and the relationships I have with others. Knocking at his door I reflect the thoughts I just had and it made me realize that this call from Jimmy was the dawning of a new day. Sitting at the door step no longer then a minute, it was opened by a five foot six man who is built rather large. The man I remember from back in the day was relatively the same, only a little larger in size. He greeted me with a warm heart back then and I hoped he would do the same for me this evening. As we said our hello’s we made our way through his nicely kept apartment. He told me how his girlfriend always made sure to leave on poker nights or he would have introduced me to her. We got a small amount amusement from the snide remark about his girlfriend knowing her place. We continued to walk through what seemed to be a rather large apartment for the area it was in. As I briskly walked I noticed the numerous pictures of him and his girlfriend looking all happy, plastered all over the walls. I went from this new happy go lucky feeling to anger, within a matter of moments. Seeing these pictures only reminded me of what I had and more importantly what I had most recently lost. I quickly snap out of that frame of mind and remember the happy place, the new me, the excited life of Peter Vetra. Finally we enter our destination, as I look in the room I see a poker table set up; a couple of guys from my old school are sitting around the table. I recognize them all, there is Matt, Sean, Kyle, and then a guy we called Rocco. All four of them were quality shits as we would have said in high school but not my closest of friends. We all said our hellos, some wanted a hand shake and others just received the nod, you know the one where you walked down the hallways and you saw someone who’s name you knew and knew a little about them but were not a friend enough to get the daps? Well you’d give them the nod a simple shake of the head acknowledging them but not giving them the whole time of day. Some of these guys got the nod. I sat down in the only free chair and was offered a beer by Jimmy, not to be rude I was obliged to accept. The foreign beverage known as a Corona had a lime inserted into its neck and was handed to me. What a smooth taste it was, good ole Mexican piss I thought to myself as I puckered my lips at the sourness of the lime juices on the rim. “Thank You,” I was rather quick to give my gratitude as I was looking at having a new friend and I wasn’t about to ruin that chance by being rude. I slowly sipped on the beer like the other gentlemen did and we talked leisurely. “Peter, I tell everyone six thirty so everyone arrives on time for some pizza and beer, we will actually start the game in a half hour or so.” Just as Jimmy finished explaining the reason we hadn’t started the game yet the doorbell rang. Jimmy was quick to his feet and received the Dominos pizza from the delivery guy and gave him a hefty tip. Jimmy came back over to the table and an all out pig out fest started; pizza, wings, beer, and even chips what more could a man ask for? The beginning of the Game The pizza, beer, wings and of course the chips were all placed to the side out of the way. The food was great, a nice chance to mingle and learn what had happen to these guys since the high school days, but it was time to get down to business, it was time to start the game we had all came for. The pot was rather hefty we had a twenty five dollar buy in and we had all paid right away. One hundred and twenty five dollars for first and a twenty five for second, not that great but at least you get your money back for second. The chips, poker chips that is not those pesky salt and vinegar chips we were eating earlier, were all passed out. My money accounted for in front of me, but the real question was how long could I hold onto it? The first hand was dealt, so was the second, nothing really spectacular a bunch of guys who know how to play poker doing so but having a good time at doing it. See the subtle part about this is we weren’t just playing poker. We were bonding, now guys won’t admit to it but we are. I mean we just don’t give it fancy words and we don’t actually admit to it but each guy knows it is going on and knows even more not to mention it. See that is the difference between men and women, we have respect for each other to do what we have to do and enjoy each others company. Women have to call it a slumber party and speak about it for days after. They sit there and dwell on saying how well they got to know so and so this time they go together. See if men did that we would be shot by another man for being gay. But I guess that is just one of the many differences between men and women. The night went on and slowly people started bowing out, the realization I might have a chance became greater, but just as it did the amount of beer left in that fridge got smaller. So I wasn’t too sure I could win being this tipsy. But as I sat back and relaxed, watching Jimmy and Rocco go heads up, balls to the wall, in other words both are all in. The winner of this contest would be heads up with me, one on one, mano et mano. Meaning I would at least get my money back, better yet I might win a hundred dollars. But as I sat here that wasn’t really my focus, I was more interested in the time I was having, a blast. I wasn’t thinking about my problems but actually accomplishing many things with my personality. Jimmy and I went heads up after he had won the pot from Rocco with a full house. Now it was me versus him, it always leads back to competition, only this was a little friendly competition for some fun. After going back and forth a couple of times Jimmy went all in off the cards that were dealt. I had enough to cover him, but was it a risk I wanted to take? I slowly flipped up my two cards and saw something to my amazement. A red Ace, followed by a black Ace, pocket Aces a hand that any poker player knows is one of the best if not the best hands in poker. I had to follow him in. What a hard decision… but as I pushed my chips in I was confident. I flipped my cards over awaiting the flop. He slowly flipped his over to reveal an Ace King combo both suited. The moment of truth had come, the flop came. Queen, ten following the suit that Jimmy withheld. The third card however was the three of hearts, not really helping anyone. As we sat and waited for the turn I noticed he had the best chances, either the Royal Straight Flush or just a Flush either way I was doomed. The turn came I couldn’t look, I turned away not paying attention the noise from around the room engulfed my ear drums, none of them signifying who it helped. That’s because it helped no one, I stood up as I wanted the last card to help me in the worse way. At this point it wasn’t about the money it was about the intensity, it was about the friendly competition and it was about having a good time. But it just turns out that the good time was either at the hands of first place, or first place loser. Who was who all depending on which card fell. The dealer flopped down the river, the fourth and final ace. I had won with three of a kind, he had nothing. At this moment I was fully engulfed in joy, I had won, but the most important part was I had made some friends. The money was exchanged, hands were shaken, and invites were given back for the next week. Something I graciously accepted. What a night I had, maybe things were starting to turn up for me, I guess time will only tell but I know I’m a hell of a lot happier now that I’m enjoying life and not wasting it away drowning myself in self pity. What a night… A time I will remember for a long… long time…. Well at least till next time… Next week, I look forward to it…
