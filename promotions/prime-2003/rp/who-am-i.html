Who Am I?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Every Moment of Everyday until Colossus Who Am I? Sometimes, the mind floats around it's thoughts and ponders it's existance and the existance of the the physical being it's attached to. Surely, everyone at some point in time has questioned why they are put on this planet and what their exact purpose is. Religion and other beliefs can alter this perception for many, but most just come to accept it without any real reason to fall back on. It makes you wonder why we're put here to work in order to live. Even the necessities of our life such as food requires money. There are some people who don't work, who don't have that nine-to-fiver to lean back on and they struggle by, some starving themselves because these afformentioned necessities are out of reach. There are some in that situation who really do want to work but they are not given the chance because they are not qualified enough. These people feel bad that they can't do what they believe they are put here to do. On a similar wavelength, there is regret for other actions as well. Heinous acts such as murder, rape and molestation to just name a few. But most of the time when one human being physically or emotionally harms another, there is that hint of regret. Sure their words may not address it and their faces may mask it, but deep down everyone feels something. You, Captain Justice have felt this I'm sure. As much as you may say you don't, you certainly regret... even just for a second what you did to Killean Sirrajin in the ring. Physcial beatings drawing some blood and causing some bumps and bruises are one thing. But to hang another person by their neck with a chain is not just a slight. It's not even a brain fart. It's, for lack of a better term, absoulutely disgusting. You knew this even before Mr. Silver told you to go ahead with it. Yet you still performed this dastardly deed. Why? What did you get out of it? Don't claim for one split second that it satisfied you in any way. A spoken claim of a similar nature would force me to call you a liar. I know better than that Ceej. In reality, you felt bad that you almost took the life of another person, even if that wasn't the original intention. Given a few more moments, there wouldn't be a Colossus match taking place and you'd be behind bars giving favors to a heavily tattooed man named Gruff to befriend him so he wouldn't steal your shitty food. Oh, who am I kidding? Convicted criminals are treated better in this day in age than most normal folk. Free food paid for by the innocent tax payers and an education to use for when you are eventually released on what they consider good behaviour. Yet there are others out there willing to learn and they work their asses off to struggle to put food on the table. They are forced to enjoy frozen hotdogs cooked on a cheap barbeque littered with hot spots meanwhile behind those cold steel bars they are being asked how they'd like their steak done. Ok, maybe I'm exaggerating just a little bit but I'm kind of along the right line of thought. You know Mr. Justice that Killean Sirrajin was almost dead and you know the basics of his recovery. Regardless if it was supposed to go down that way or not, PRIME officials felt obligated to notify everyone backstage of his condition. What you don't know however is the details behind it all, the feelings going through his and his loved ones minds just a short while after it all went down. Or do you? I'm willing to bet you've at least wondered what you caused exactly. |~o~| “His emergency contact is in Canada!” One nurse claimed. “Who cares where his emergency contact lives! They should still receive a call.” The doctor quipped in return. The nurse ran ahead to her station as the doctor flipped a few pages in a folder. “Damn newbies.” Killean Sirrajin was being wheeled down the cold white hallway of the hospital. He had bandages around his neck and a neck brace surrounding it all. He also had bandages around his forehead. The doctor seemed extremely concerned as he looked over the medical history he managed to obtain. “He's had clots before. Gotta make sure to closely monitor his medication levels.” He mumbled to himself. Turning a final corner, they brought the Hall of Famer into the observation room. But as soon as they burst through the doors, it was like Sirrajin came back to life. He was thrown into a full blown coughing fit, spitting up phlegm and blood all over himself. He couldn't sit up and immediately, the doctor feared choking. “We need more oxygen here stat!” He called over to the nurses standing at their station. They sprung into action, but it wasn't soon enough. Sirrajin was conscious enough to realize he was choking himself so with instinct, he moved his arms to his neck, finding the brace there. Before he could try to tear at it, the doctor acted. “I'm not sure you remember what happened to you, but your neck is badly damaged... NO NO! Put those hands down at your side. We gotta make sure your neck is ok before we remove the brace.” Sirrajin complied with the orders to leave the neck brace as the oxygen was hooked up and flowing. His coughing subsided and the nurse cleaned him of whatever fluids he managed to cough up. Not exactly a job for someone with a weak stomach. The nurse who made the phone calls home for Killean came back into the Observation Area only a moment later. “His family physician wants him air lifted home soon as possible.” She stated as soon as she was withing respectable speaking distance. “Sounds good to me. Nothing would be better for him than getting him home.” “Bullshit.” A deep raspy voice rang out form behind them. They both turned to look at their patient. “Got a show to be at next week.” He had to try to speak multiple times even with the doctor urging him not to, but eventually he got what he needed to say out. “You gotta be kidding me. You're in no shape to do any such thing and you won't be for a couple weeks.” But as Killean tried to shake his head again, the doctor thought better of arguing at the moment. Killean couldn't even talk properly, nevermind go to a show in 7 days. “Look, we gotta roll you over for X-Rays and stuff. Your voice is terribly effected by the events of tonight. Let's just say you're a lucky man Mr. Sirrajin.” |~o~| Given the fact that you were made privy to this information by PRIME Staff, doesn't it turn your stomach Captain that you were the cause of all that pain and suffering? Perhaps it doesn't... your job after all is to inflict pain according to yourself and Mr. Silver, but I don't think you had any acts of murder on your mind either. But that was just for starters. You probably haven't thought about the fact that you almost stole a little girls father... you almost took the life of someones significant other. But the worst is that you came nothing short of a pubic hair away from orphaning a little girl who loves her Dad very much. Fatherless daughters are one of the ultimate sins in life and you almost added to the problem. But enough of the guilt trip, cause it may not even be getting through that thick skull of yours. As much as I try, you may not even listen. That's ok though. I'm fairly certain Killean is willing to do everything he can to beat it into you, regardless of his physical condition. Thing is, you've dealt the changing blow in this war you're fighting. It's gone from being a little personal to as personal as it gets. Some have even considered it to be along the lines of the Killean and Brandon Youngblood war that mercifully ended at Ultra Violence last year. Sirrajin made Youngblood pass out to win the 5-Star Championship... and which title was it that you let slip through your fingers recently? Few people have even compared it to such legendary feuds as Lindsay Troy and Nova, Lindsay Troy and Sonny Silver or even Killean Sirrajin and Tchu. The Imhuman Being and The Supreme Machine took each other to the limit countless times outside of the ring and after four years of buildup, their match tore the house down at Colossus III. Now, here we are two years in the future and you've made thing personal again. Regardless of the outcome of the match, you will be a changed individual on the inside even if your physical features won't allow anyone to notice. You'll know it and that's all you'll need. Be real about it all, after Colossus, you'll be beaten and bruised. Killean already is and he isn't going to go down without a hell of a fight, even ifit may seem hopeless on his part. Oddsmakers would have you as the current favorite because of your actions at 167 and the follow up at 168. The man was all offense and had no defense in the tank. So once you saw the opening you struck and medical attention was once again required. Problem is, you've most likely given a man the time he needs to recover enough, something you may come to regret even more than your actions. As each passing hour ticks by on the countdown to Colossus, Sirrajin is on the mend. Good news for him and bad news for you unfortunately. |~o~| “Should you really be doing that?” Jenny said as she stood in the doorway of the exercise room eaning against the door frame. Killean was curling a very large weight bar, the veins in his neck sticking out amidst the brusing that looked like a purple scarf. He was sweating, like had been relentlessly performing this task for quite sometime. “Gotta be in top shape for this one.” It was a simple answer, one that he had an easier time expressing as his voice was given time to rest. Docs said the gravelly rasp that he was speaking with since the incident may or may not dissipate. Only time would really tell. “You gotta come home from this one baby. You almost didn...” “Enough. I don't wanna speak about such a thing. I'm here and I always will be if I get what I want.” He put down the weights carefully, watching to not bend or move in any odd way as to hurt himself. He rubbed his right palm on the back of his neck, wincing a little as the sharp pain cut through him, but it was much better than it used to be. By the time Colossus came around, he should be in good shape to go. “It's not always up to you, when are you gonna learn that?” “Look, I'll crawl home with my lips if I have to ok? I'd do that for that little girl in there and for you.” He walked over to the treadmill and started the belt. He started at a brisk walk, fully intending to jog with it in a few short moments. “I know that. But I have to ask you... what was it like? Like what happened?” Killean began to jog and scrunched up his face upon hearing the question, confused at what Jenny meant. “You've lost me. What was what like?” “You know... almost ummm...” “You can't be seriously asking me that.” “Well you know, people always wonder about their own mortality and what happens and stuff.” Jenny says as she sat on one of the weight benches. Killean stopped the treadmill and saw what Jenny was saying. He walked over and sat opposite of her on another bench. “Honestly, there is only a bit that I know of. If anything else was happening, I forgot about it.” He looked to the ground for a moment, searching for the words to explain his ordeal. He then looked straight at Jenny while he spoke. “When people say that your life flashes before your eyes, they're right. It certainly did for me, but it was weird. It was almost like the volume of a home theatre system being turned down and the picture getting darker every second. I couldn't hear the people around me and I couldn't hear the crowd. Soon all I heard was the steady heartbeat in my ears getting slower and slower. Soon, it was gone and white was all I saw and then it seemed I fell into a deep sleep. Next thing I knew I was in an ambulance. I was told I was awake as they were wheeling me out of the arena, but I don't remember any of it. I also heard Lindsay was there... her and Dam apparently helped me down, but I don't know anything of it. It's almost everything after the first struggle didn't happen.” “You... haven't seen the video?” “To be brutally honest, it scared the shit out of me, so I've avoided seeing it even once.” He hung his head, having problems dealing with his own mortality again. The old scar on his throat was from a drug dealer slitting his neck. It was different back then cause he never lost consciousness. It was only minutes later in the living room of his loft that Killean decided to watch the video. Once he was revived, he turned the video off and looked over to Jenny who was sitting beside him. “Well?” Jenny asked. “I feel... good.” “Huh? How does that work.” “I'm back here aren't I?” He got up and walked over to the window to look outside. He never turned back as he continued. “Obviously it wasn't my time and now I'm here with you and with Dizzy. Much like this scar around my neck from before, it wasn't my time then either. I'm just happy to be home, to see my little girl and to see the people I love.” “What are you gonna say about Justice or Silver?” “Say? What could I possibly say to them? 'Ooooo hey you almost killed me and now I'm gonna kick your ass!' Nah, that's not what this is anymore. This has gone far beyond some wrestling feud, some money making scheme for a wrestling company for the big payoff at the big event. I'm not gonna go cutting some promo saying how much I'll make him suffer for what he did. Words can't describe this. Some things can be explained, cleared up with a few words... this can't. So it's more along the lines of WHAT I'm gonna DO.” He looked directly back at Jenny when he finished the last line. “And that would be what?” “Have fun.” “Excuse me?” “Have fun. It's all that really matters. Like I said this isn't about a wrestling match anymore. I'm not going out there to win or lose. I'm simply gonna hurt him, no matter what it takes. I guarantee Justice will be hurt in some way come the close of the event.” Killean shook his head and smiled a little before he started to walk off towards the hallway. “Where you going?” Jenny asked, standing to follow. Killean didn't answer, he simply made a direct beeline for Desiree's room, where she was sleeping quietly. He knew a child of her age couldn't comprehend what was happening him. It was best too, cause it's gone beyond personal. |~o~| Mr. Silver asked you to do what you did Captain Justice and you took no time to think. I happen to know beyond a shadow of a doubt that if you had thought about what you were being asked for even one second, you wouldn't have gone through with it. Instead, you're blind. You can't act for yourself. Silver is pushing the buttons to run The Americanimal roughshod over the PRIME roster because he is physically able. You're allowing yourself to be controlled. You're merely, a robot. You're a pawn, used in this game of chess we call Sports Entertainment. Silver thinks he is the King of PRIME and you're thinking along that line of thought only because he is. But in reality, you don't think too highly of yourself do you? Of course not. You don't think highly of what you did? Certainly you don't. You even went to far as to finish the job, to let the man dangle there, the life being removed from his body. You took away Killean's ability to say at the end of the day, “You didn't finish the job, I'm still breathing.” So he'll come back and look to decimate you. You know it and I know it. How do I know it? Who am I? Stop looking in the mirror and realize what your conscience is saying to you.
