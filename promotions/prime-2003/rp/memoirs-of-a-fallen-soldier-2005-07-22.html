Memoirs of a Fallen Soldier

22 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-22-05 ”Fall 2001”: Memoirs of a Fallen Soldier Solitude transpires amongst this barren land so simply deemed Tahj. A span of centuries have culminated into one standard kingdom, corrupted with blood shed and hatred for the common man. This land of Tahj was once a bold place, brought together by the mutual joy of its citizens, only to be torn down by the ever so familiar themes of Jealousy, Greed, and of course the envy of them all…Power. The joy that formed a once unbreakable bond tarnished with the rise of gang wars and power hungry tyrants. The joy that we so once held, never to be seen again. The only remains of those wonderful times are the blood that we shed, fighting to keep it stable. A fight that would later prove meaningless. 2 years ago, I was granted the totalitarian throne due to my father’s unfortunate death. Since I was his son, I was forced to inherit the throne and lead this wild pack of sheep. With my inheritance came the Jealousy and the envy of those who opposed the throne. Many wanted my position…many were willing to kill for my position…many have tried to kill for my position…many have failed. For through Trials and Tribulations alike, I have held firm to the blood oath that I granted my father upon taking this throne…That I would lead it with the dignity and honor that he detained all the while avenging his death with the killing of his attacker. The latter I had yet to conquer, but the other I had. Many have opposed me during my reign, but none far more powerful than Roman Esposito III. Anyone who knows the story of Roman Esposito knows how dangerous of a crime boss he is. Taking the risk of sounding far too lenient, Roman Esposito is without a doubt the most powerful man in Tahj. At the snap of his finger, things happen. His enemies are granted death with the whisper of his breath. He is a dangerous man…far more dangerous than I…and he wants what I have. But despite contrary belief, I own this land and I will protect it from any and all adversaries at any and all cost. For far too long I have fought to keep it tamed, never will I allow someone of his magnitude to strip me of what I have built. For I am Vangelus Olsig. And this is my terrain. Samuel S. Tarver. June 19th, 1997 Samuel Tarver witnessed the crime that would shape the face of Tahj for eternity. For on this day, Tarver bore witness to Roman Esposito killing off Judy Blanchard…his then Fiancée and FBI Detective. Roman is said to have lured her into the bedroom before drawing out a glock and staining the sheets with her blood. Only problem is that he did it inside of Blanchard’s penthouse…the same penthouse that had surveillance camera’s at every peak. The killing was caught on tape and after being informed of this, Esposito quickly disposed of the tape. Tarver is the only person who knows where that tape is hidden. He contains the information we need to bring Roman down. We have Roman and Tarver in the same courtroom, attempting to pry the right information that would lead us into bringing Esposito to justice. Neither will budge. We know the obvious reason as to why Roman won’t budge…Tarver on the other hand; he wants to gain a big payday from all of this. He believes that if he supplies the information, then he is entitled to millions piled upon millions. Were not talking about your simple 25 mil…we’re talking 99 mil…a year. Money the government can’t spend on one person…but it seems to be the only way. “Mr. Tarver” spoke Attorney Windham “The government is willing to offer you a mighty fine wager for the information you withhold. Your signature on the dotted line is all it would take for you to receive your money and for us to bring down Mr. Esposito.” “…99 Million per year or nothing.” Responded Tarver. “Mr. Tarver, lets be real here. The government does not have 99 million dollars to waste on one man per year. Can we please step back into the realm of sanity?” Attorney Windham spoke, attempting to portray himself as a caring man. But just like everyone else who knew him, we all knew all he cared about was bringing Roman down…avenging the alleged death of his daughter, Sara, who is said to have been in cahoots with Roman. “Fine. 80 Million Per year. I mean, that’s how much athletes are getting paid in these ages, so why not a man who has the key that will rid this city of its most notorious virus.” Responded Tarver, a glee of bliss in his eye, knowing that whichever way the cheek was turned, he was in for a mighty fine payday. Attorney Windham sighs, knowing that his attempts to compromise are being blatantly shot down. He slowly walks over to his table, leaning in and discussing something with two other fellows, one being District Attorney Charles Rancid and city treasurer Melissa D. Hall. Finally he lifts his head and walks back over to the bench to face Tarver. “Mr. Tarver. The court has decided to grant you 70 million dollars per year for the information you withhold. If, however, any signs of false promotion is recognized, you will be sentenced to extended time in prison.” Attorney Windham stated, with the cracks in his voice showing his displeasure in the act. “Fair Enough” replied Tarver. “Now where do I si-…OOPH!” A quick flash. A loud bang. A big gasp from everyone in the courtroom as the body of Samuel S. Tarver fell forward revealing the burning hole in the back of his head. The blood seared out of the hole, spreading across the courtroom floor as flocks of people flee the courtroom, screaming and yelling in terror. Security desperately searched for the culprit behind the shooting as officers gathered around the body, attempting to make a quick recovery. But as they did…Roman Esposito simply sat back in his chair, a spectator to the events…smiling…knowing that the only man who held enough evidence to convict him had been shot and killed. He had been saved by a bullet. “What do you mean he’s dead!?” Vangelus Olsig pounded the table in furious anger, questioning District Attorney Charles Rancid. Attorney Rancid was startled at the sudden outburst displayed by Olsig, but by all means understood the cause behind it all. Learning that the only evidence good enough to bring Esposito down had been killed was something that Olsig did not take kindly too. For years, Esposito had caused the Olsig family long hard aches and pain. Even his father fell victim to Esposito, and to this day Vangelus still holds him as the number one suspect in the death of his father. “We still can’t piece the puzzle together. He agreed to the compromise and right there in mid sentence, BAM...he was gone.” Expressed Rancid with a less then appalled look on his face. Vangelus paced the room slowly, wishing that this was only a dream. He took a deep sigh and rubbed his hands through his hair, finally securing himself in one spot by sitting on the desk and staring a hole through Attorney Rancid, whilst shaking his head. “You mean to tell me, masses of people gathered in one court room and no one saw the shooter?” Olsig asked. “The only thing we found was a .22 gun with the serial number scraped off and the handle taped. Whoever it is surely is a smart one. He scraped off the serial number so that we could not trace the gun…and he taped the handle so that his fingerprints could not be traced. One can only be lead to believe that he was wearing a glove to further disguise his prints.” Said District Attorney Rancid, the worry on his face just as evident as that on Olsigs. Olsig took another deep breath and stood up from his desk. He walked over to the window and glared outside at the city in which he held in the palm of his hands, and out there in his city was a virus that would inevitable concluding with his death. Olsig knew the price, but he brushed it off…for anything that was worth having was worth fighting for, and that’s exactly what he planned on doing…fighting. “…Where was Roman during all of this.” Olsig asked, his tone lowering as he attempted to piece together the pieces of the puzzle right there on the spot. “I can’t really give you an exact location sir…I was too busy trying to tend to the fallen Mister Samuel Tarver. Why is this of such an importance?” Rancid asked, standing up from his chair and glaring at the Olsig. “It’s simple. The killing occurred as soon as Tarver agreed to the compromise. Obviously Roman Esposito was behind it all. He wanted to kill of Tarver to spare the evidence and ultimately his life.” Olsig stated, thinking that he had the answer all worked out. “While that may be true, Sir, Roman Esposito is a man of difficult stature. He would not have constructed a crime such as this where he was the obvious culprit. He is more complicated than that.” Rancid affirmed. Finally Olsig turned around and walked back over to his desk. He snatched open the drawer and pulled out his keys. “He’s fooled you into believing that so that when he does construct such a simplistic crime, you’ll flee from suspecting him. Nonetheless, I’m about to gather some answers.” Olsig stated, making his way to a file cabinet standing beside the door. “Sir Olsig…how do you plan on going about that?” asked Rancid. His answer came as he watched Olsig open up the file cabinet and pull out a handgun, tucking it in his back pocket and snatching on his trench coat, throwing it on. “I’m going to get the answer directly from the source.” Olsig replied. “Surely you’re not about to m-…” “Yes. I’m going to meet Roman Esposito.” The sound of the door slamming closed lowered the head of District Attorney Rancid. He looked over at the desk of Olsig and spotted a picture of Olsig as an infant being held in the arms of his father Czar Olsig. “You are very much like your father Sir Olsig...” Rancid stated, shaking his head and placing the picture back on the desk. He walks over to the coat rack and grabs his coat, opening the door to make his exit. “…Very Much.” And Darkness. Midnight. It was nothing more than mid-day to the citizens of Tahj. For midnight was the time in which most of the happenings…happened. The city still sunk in the blaring sirens of various police cars, all still on the hunt for the man who shot Samuel Tarver dead in the middle of City Court. The only evidence that was found was a .22 gun with the serial number scraped off and the handle taped. But now, in the midst of the night, one man sought the answers to this case. One man…One firm man. Roman sat in his bed in the middle of his penthouse, glaring at the big screen TV that dwelled in reports of the earlier happenings. Footage after Footage of the shooting occurred on the screen as reporters from all over the country attempted to analyze and dissect the purpose behind it all. It all made Roman smile, because he knew that at the end of the day, no matter how many reports were given, no matter how many people dissected the killing, at the end of the day Samuel S. Tarver was the dead and so was the evidence that would ultimately land him a spot in the State Penn. He was off the hook. “More scotch, father?” asked Roberto Esposito, the only son of Roman Esposito. He brought a tray to his father’s bedside with a bottle of the countries finest scotch and two glasses set up in only the most conjure display. “Please, Roberto. I’ve been downing Scotch all evening. Do your pop a favor and head down to my favorite hub and grab me a bottle of thunderbird. I feel like stepping out of the realm a bit to celebrate my victory over the government, so to speak.” Roman replied with a smile. “But father the local hub is way d-…” “I SAID GO!” screamed Roman…his blaring screams shaking the room and the soul of his Son. Esposito always treated his son as an outsider, using his son as somewhat of a “servant” to do his dirt work or the work that he just didn’t feel like doing. His son let him do it to him. Without another word, Roberto walked out of the room taking the platter of scotch with him. Inside of the room, Esposito positioned himself in a more comfortable position to review the news on the Big screen television. His eyelids grew heavy as sleep beckoned. Finally, unable to fight it off anymore, he closed his eyes and submitted to the overbearing will to sleep. “Roman…Lets talk.” He peeled his eyes open with his first vision being that of Vangelus Olsig standing over him with a less than sappy look on his face. Roman jumped up, looking into the eyes of his adversary, a hint of fear evident in his crackling voice. “What are you doing in my home.” Roman stated, his mouth frowning up as he peers into the scarred face of the man who has plagued his career in crime with trouble amongst arrival. “Samuel S. Tarver. Killed in the middle of city court, ironically as soon as he began to testify against you. Its obvious you did it…its obvious why you did it…now I want to know who pulled the trigger.” Olsig spoke with as serious a look on the Tyrant’s face as possible. “What are you talking about…I had nothing to do with it.” Roman retorted, the innocent look on his face almost believable. Olsig reaches out and grabs Esposito by the collar, bringing him in closer so that the two see eye to eye. “Don’t play games with me Roman. The evidence is there. Now tell me who pulled the trigger or I’ll pull one of my own.” Vangelus stated. He slowly reaches into his back pocket and retrieved the glock, quickly flashing it so that Esposito recognized it. Olsig could feel the pounding pump of Roman’s heart as well as feel the fear amongst his body heat. “Do it Vangelus…I dare you. You made an oath to your father to bring justice to this city, but by shooting and killing me without any evidence that I was behind the Court room attacks then you’ll be no different than I. You’ll be a criminal just like I am. You’ll have broken the blood oath you gave your father and you’ll burn in hell. So pull the trigger, Vangelus. My death lined up with yours is a risk I am willing to take.” Esposito stated, smiling as he saw his words take their purposed toll on Vangelus Olsig. “Just tell me who pulled the trigger and this encounter will have never happened.” Olsig stated, the dim in his eyes mounting. “For the last time…I do not know. I had nothing to d-…ARGH!” Blood splashed against the carpeted floor as a result of a straight punch from the fist of Vangelus Olsig, knocking Roman unto the ground with blood soaring from his nose. Olsig stands over the fallen body of Esposito. “Screw with me and I’ll Kill you.” Gone. Outside now, Olsig made his way down the sidewalk of the city, rubbing his hands from the effects of the punch. A scream…A gunshot…Olsig runs into the alley from which the scream came from. Right there in the alley, laid out cold soaking in a pool of blood… Roberto Esposito. Obviously there was a serial killer roaming the city somewhere, and he had struck Esposito’s only son. Olsig knew that it was the same killer as before by the .22 gun that stood beside the body of Roberto. He picked up the gun and checked for the scraped serial number…it was there. He checked for the tape handle…it was there. He tucked the gun in his coat pocket for further evidence before continuing to examine the fallen body. But as Olsig stood over the dead body of Roberto Esposito, he pondered… Would Roman Esposito kill his own son? Footsteps. They came from around the corner. Olsig heard them and ran off. He heard something bounce off of the concrete ground…something like metal, but he didn’t stop to see what it was, he just continued to run. One of Esposito’s guards who had heard the shot ran across the corner just in time to see Olsig turn the corner on the other side of the alley. He looked down at the body of Roberto and cringed. But he noticed something… A Cross Medallion... The same type Olsig wore. He reached down and picked it up, stuffing it inside of his pocket as he ran to inform Roman Esposito that his son was dead. Fear troubled his heart, but he had to do it… Had to… Ashes to Ashes Tears of sorrow as a mass of people gathered around the gravesite of the fallen Roberto Esposito. His aunts cried tears of sorrow, looking to the males to hold them during their time of grief. The uncles and other males tried to comfort the females, but fell victim to tears as well. A sad day in the Esposito empire as they had lost another one to a .22 gun. And all the while… Roman stood there. Calm. Vangelus Olsig and District Attorney Charles Rancid stood a few feet away from the gathered masses under a tree, observing the funeral service and any activities that might come along with it. Olsig reminisced on hearing the gun shot and watching Roberto die. He thought to himself…what if he would have gotten there earlier…maybe he could have stopped it. Olsig never had any problems with Roberto, seeing as Roberto was nothing more than an innocent pawn trapped in Roman’s game of human chess. To watch him die struck Olsig hard…very hard. Dust to Dust It brought forth suspicious judgment to both Olsig and Attorney Rancid as to how well Roman Esposito was taking the death of his son. Only made the fact that he was behind these random killings. But the question still remained…would Roman Esposito pull the trigger on his own son? If Olsig knew Esposito like he thought he knew him…if all of the stories his father told him of Esposito were indeed true…then he wouldn’t be surprised by it. But as he glared into Roman’s eyes that night…he felt a hint of truth. It was the only thing keeping him from pulling the trigger that would ultimately end Roman for good. Roman stood before everyone watching as they lowered his son’s casket into the ground. It had been a closed casket funeral…the body was too horrific to be displayed in public like so. All that was left were memories. Olsig could understand Roman’s calm nature during the ceremony, seeing as Roman never displayed any love for his son. He only treated him as a servant. But on this day, although it hadn’t been expressed in words nor tears…Roman Esposito was distressed. Finally, the body reached its destined placement six feet under. Members of the family gathered around, saying their final words and throwing roses unto the casket in the ground. Roman was the last one to do so. He took one last look at his son’s casket before turning away and walking off. A couple of people patted him on the back, whispering kind words to comfort him. He brushed them off. Suddenly, Roman stopped to greet the coroner of this ceremony and his best friend…Ralph Bradley. Ralph knew of Roman’s wealth, but charged nothing to bury his son. The two had grown up together…they had become wealthy together…they were the best of friends. …They were. “Roman…I have something very disturbing to tell you.” Stated Bradley. “I’ve been hearing a lot of that lately…go ahead.” Replied Roman. “Roberto is n-…” “…” That oh so familiar bang rang throughout the mourning air as every ducked in terror, including Roman. After assuming that the shooter had fled, Roman stood back up in an upright position…but he felt something at his feet. He looked down… Blood… A bullet… A .22 with scraped serial number and taped handle… …Ralph Bradley. The serial Killer now deemed as the “Discriminant Killer” for his random and hurried killings had stuck again. And just like the previous two killings…they all linked someway to Roman Esposito. Masses of people rushed unto the scene attempting to revive Bradley, but it was too late. He was a goner. A funeral…planned at a funeral. But as Olsig sat back and watched the activities, he began to ponder… Who is the Discriminant Killer…? What’s his purpose...? …Why has he targeted Roman Esposito…? 8:00 p.m. District Attorney Charles Rancid sat at his desk, filling out some paperwork…possibly on all of the new happenings surrounding him. This Discriminant Killer case was getting more confusing by the day. First he killed Samuel Tarver which ultimately saved Roman Esposito from prison. But now he’s targeting Roman’s friends and family? No matter how the pieces were placed together, it didn’t make any sense. In a sense, Attorney Rancid sort of felt for Roman Esposito. He had never seen Roman in such a depressed state as he was now. It was because Roman knew that this Discriminant Killer was still on the loose, targeting members of his family and his friends. Roman knew that in the end…the only person that would be left to target would be him. That fact did not sit well with Roman Esposito. Attorney Rancid placed his pen down for a moment and began to think. Maybe the Discriminant Killer killed off Samuel Tarver so that he wouldn’t testify which would ultimately keep Roman out of jail and allow him to kill Esposito. Rancid shook his head, garnishing a headache form trying to calculate the minimum evidence. He just hoped that it would end soon. …Hoped. “Let’s talk Rancid.” Attorney Rancid knew that deep toned raspy voice from anywhere. He looked up and saw Roman Esposito standing over his desk, looking down at Rancid. No guards, no protection, no weapons…just a depressed and vengeful Roman Esposito. The look on his face said it all as he held a letter in his hand and something else that could not be recognized at this point. “I know who the Discriminant Killer is.” Spoke Esposito. This immediately drew the attention of attorney Rancid as he slowly but surely stood up from his desk and peered into the eyes of his opposing foe. “W-Who…Who is it?” Rancid stumbled, getting the words out. “You know him better than anyone else.” Esposito said, a hint of smile crossing his face. He opened his palms and placed a letter and a cross medallion on the desk. “Vangelus Olsig is the Discriminant Killer.” Roman stated with an evil glare in his eyes. Attorney Rancid said nothing as he looked down at the desk, peering at the cross medallion and the letter. He picked up the letter and read its short content. These Killings won’t last long…they’ll stop with you. Rancid let the note fall to the ground before allowing his hands to ball up in a fist, but he quickly released it, holding his anger to his best ability. “What evidence do you have?” Rancid asked…the look of anger still present on his face. Esposito stepped forward a bit to come closer in contact with Rancid. “That medallion…his medallion…was found at the sight of my sons killing, covered in blood.” Esposito stated firmly. “That’s not enough evidence to convict someone.” Attorney Rancid replied, turning his back to Esposito and staring out of the same glass window that Olsig stared out of, into the city of Tahj. Through the reflection of the window, he could see Esposito behind him smiling a bit. “What, Attorney? Your heart to weak to handle the fact that your boy is a killer? Face facts Rancid…the evidence is there…so are you going to do your job and arrest him o-…” “SHUT UP!”Shouted Rancid. “Since you have all the answers, how about you tell me why he killed Samuel Tarver…tell me why he killed your son…TELL ME WHY THE FUCK HE KILLED CORONER BRADLEY!” Rancid ranted, the tension building up like fire inside of his heart. “Its simple. Its not secret that Olsig wants me dead. He killed Tarver because he knew that if Tarver was to testify…I would have only received prison time…something I could have easily escaped. He knew that if I were to be convicted of a crime like killing Tarver, I would receive a death sentence. Therefore he shot Tarver knowing that all of the obvious blame would fall on me. He continued on to kill my son because he knew It would draw me in…which it did. Add that in with the fact that his medallion was found at THAT site. Then he killed Bradley because he knew it would draw me in even further…which it did. He’s got me, Rancid, and he’s reeling me in for the big moment when SPLAT! He sends the bullet through MY chest.” Rancid said nothing as he continued to look through the window at the city. It had begun to rain…Mother Nature’s poor attempt at washing away the sin in this city. His fists clamped once again as he turned around to swing at Roman, but Roman caught the fist and held it in his hands, staring deep into the eyes of Rancid. “The evidence is there…Do your job Attorney…Arrest Vangelus Olsig. You read the note…These killings won’t last much longer…he’s coming for me NOW! IM going to be the next victim…and if I am, the blame will fall on your hand Attorney. Do your job and arrest this man.” Rancid released his fist and resisted the urge to knock Esposito’s teeth down his throat. He simply turned around to glare out of the window once again before murmuring his final words. “…Get out.” The hint of smile that was once present on the face of Roman Esposito washed away. Roman shook his head and simply made his way to the door. He opened the door, but stood in the door way, shooting one more phrase at Rancid before exiting. “The blood’s on your hands, Rancid. The blood’s on your hands.” And with that, he exited. As soon as he did, District Attorney Rancid turned around and pounded a fist into the desk, anger boiling and ever so present. He picked up his hand and rubbed it through his gray hair before walking over to the file cabinet that stood beside the door. He opened it up and gasped as he peered inside. …a .22 gun. Scraped Serial Number… Taped Handle… …Vangelus Olsig. District Attorney Rancid lowered his head before raising it once again to study the .22. His Medallion at the scene of the crime…his hatred for Roman Esposito…and a .22 gun, the same one that the Discriminant Killer used…it all lead Attorney Rancid to one conclusion. ...What if Esposito was right? The rain had drowned up from yesterday, and the sun blared creating that uneasy wet-yet-hot feeling. It didn’t matter to Olsig though, as he continued to browse down the streets. It had become a daily routine for him to walk down to the local hub every evening for a couple of shots. …This evening would be a bit different from the rest, though. Olsig felt the metal object slap him in the back of the head, sending him down to the concrete floor below. He looked up to see 4 masked men, stomping the living hell out of him while one pounded him with a lead pipe. Two of the men picked him up and held him place as the lead pipe armed man ran and took him out with the pipe and continued to beat him until blood splattered his face. “That’s it…he’s done…grab him.” Shouted one of the men as the rest followed, picking Olsig up and dragging him. Only seconds passed before the beating rendered Vangelus Olsig… Unconscious. “Drop him right there” It was the first audible sound Olsig heard as he peeled his eyes open and regained conscious. His blurry sight made things difficult to vision, but after a while it began to clear up. He realized that he was inside of the Alley in which Roberto Esposito had been shot. “H-How did I get here? Who’s behind this?” He shouted…and his question was quickly answered…not with words, but with a face. …District Attorney Charles Rancid. And behind him was none other than Roman Esposito. Roman placed his hand on the shoulder of Rancid who stood there, glaring at Olsig with a look of pure hatred in his eye. “Great job, Attorney Rancid.” Esposito said, smiling as he paced the scene glaring down at the helpless and chained body of Vangelus Olsig. “Now Vangelus…we know all about your little secret. It wasn’t so difficult to piece together the pieces of the puzzle. First, Samuel S. Tarver…then my son Roberto Esposito right here in this very alley…and last but not least, my best friend…one of my only friends, Coroner Bradley. You have caused me loads of pain these past few days. You were deemed the Discriminant Killer for your random and rushed killings. You disposed of three, and you thought that would open the door for you to lay waste to me…but your planned backfired and now its you who will Die.” Vangelus didn’t say a word, instead he just shot a mean glance over at Attorney Rancid who returned the same glance. Finally he spoke up. “How could you betray me…how could you convict me of something like this!? You’re a backstabber and I’m going to K-…” His speech was interrupted by the bottom of Esposito’s boot. “Shut up, Olsig. District Attorney Charles Rancid is just doing his job…which is convicting you for the murders of Samuel S. Tarver, Roberto Esposito, and Ralph Bradley. And now I must do my job of making you pay for those crimes.” He reached into his back pocket and retrieved the same .22 gun that had been found earlier, pointing the cold barrel into the face of Vangelus Olsig. “Tell your Daddy I say Hi when you reach Hell.” …BANG! Everyone in the alley ducked and looked to the far end of the alley where the shot had come from. They saw a man turn the corner as the guards began to chase him. They reached a tall fence signaling a dead end. The guards grabbed the man, restraining him so that he dropped his gun. A .22 gun. District Attorney Rancid reached into his coat and retrieved a flashlight, shining it on the face of the man revealing to everyone the True Discriminant Killer. …Roberto Esposito. “I am the Discriminant Killer.” Roberto calmly stated as the Guards held him in place. Roman’s mouth hung wide open as he dropped the gun and slowly walked over to his son who was supposedly dead. “Ro-Roberto….This ca-can’t be…You’re Dead!” “I am the Discriminant Killer” Roberto Repeated. “B-But how…WHY!?” “For years, father, you have neglected me. You are growing in age and when you die I’ll have nothing because you never thought of me enough to grant me anything in your will. So I killed Samuel Tarver because I knew that if he were to testify…you would only go to prison. I wanted you Dead so that I could inherit your estates. I faked my death so that I would not become a suspect. That body that you buried was wax…and Coroner Bradley knew it. That was the disturbing news he was going to tell you. The news he promised that he would keep secret. I knew that he would break his promise, so I put an end to him before he could rat off his mouth. And now…I was going to end you right here but I missed…I fucking missed. You’re lucky Father…I had you…This isn’t over.” Esposito ran his hands through his hair, not believing what he had been hearing. As he thought over things, Attorney Rancid walked over to Olsig, releasing him from his chains and pleading apologies. There attention focused on the situation at hand, though as finally, Roman’s hint of confusion turned into pure genuine anger. He raised the gun that he was going to blast Vangelus with and pointed it at his son. “…you were always ungrateful.” He said, ready to pull the trigger, but Attorney Rancid ran and grabbed Esposito’s hand, stopping the shot. “LET GO!” Esposito screamed, managing to placed his finger on the trigger during the scrap. BANG! Thump. The body of Rancid fell to the ground with a hole in his chest. Olsig screamed and crashed right by the fallen body, shaking Rancid attempting to revive him…but to no avail. Rancid was still alive, but struggling to stay that way. He managed to push out a few words to his loyal partner of the past two years. “…I’m……Sorry.” Olsig felt his weight shift into that of dead weight, and then did he know that District Attorney Rancid…was dead. He lowered his head, trying to hide the tears…but they came flowing like a river. Attorney Rancid had been Olsig’s partner in justice for two years.
