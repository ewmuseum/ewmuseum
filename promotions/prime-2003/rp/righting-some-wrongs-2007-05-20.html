Righting Some Wrongs

Date: 05/20/07 Sunday, May 20, 2007 It was turning out to be a lazy day in the house. Killean knew he had things to do, but he kept hesitating, getting more comfortable on the couch with Desiree who was playing with her new teddy bear. She giggled at the motions it was making which Killean thought was cute because she was making the bear move. He smiled and messed her hair a little, but he couldn’t win the attention of the occupied youngster. He was a lucky man to have a daughter like her. With all the problems that had gone down in the last two years, Desiree still seemed like a normal fun loving child. Sure she never knew the crazy, out of her damn mind mother, but Killean still thought the episode of Stacey breaking in to the house to try and take Desiree away would have some kind of effect on her. Too bad his mind couldn’t exactly block out that day much like it seems Desiree’s did. Then there was the whole Charity problem and his own drug problem and near death experience to boot. He had put Desiree through a lot, even though her young mind couldn’t process the drugs and the scar that he now wore like a badge for her. It was now there to remind him that he would never do anything so stupid again, nearly orphaning his daughter once already. He sat for a few minutes more, ignoring the TV in favour of watching Desiree play. Finally, he decided to get on with his day and pulled Desiree up, who in turn pulled the bear with her. She hugged it close, intent on taking it where ever she was going with her Dad. "Good thing we don’t have pets sweetie. You’d squeeze their eyes out." ~<&>~ "THERE’S MY BABY GIRL!" Vegas’ voice boomed out as he rose from his chair to greet Killean and Desiree. He lifted the little girl out of Killean’s arms and held her up high, before bringing him down and giving her a kiss. Desiree loved her uncle Vegas, not at all intimidated by his rough exterior. She took to him quickly in the beginning and it’s been smooth sailing with them ever since. "Man, you are lookin’ good lately." Killean said to his friend/pretty much family member. "Dude, I’m in the best shape of my life and I owe it all to you. Been eating better with the food guide you gave me and that workout you e-mailed me was a blessing. I owe most of my recovery from my heart attack to you!" "Nonsense. You owe your recovery to yourself and Hanna. You had a wife who stuck by you and you realized you needed to take better care of yourself. You always worked out but..." Killean was cut off. "Yeah yeah, I didn’t eat as well as I exercised. I know. But now things are different man. I could even take you out." He laughed in his deep voice. "Pfft! Dream on." Killean replied. Desiree meanwhile was playing with the gold chain around Vegas’ neck when the music from the club got louder in the office. Rich McGregg, also known as Big Mac around the office walked in. He was the club’s head manager, behind Killean of course. But recently Killean once again scaled back and took a more silent role in that area as well, much like he did last year in the shipping end. "Hey boss. Look, Maryanne just quit. Something with her not wanting to take her clothes off to pay for University anymore or some shit and..." "WHOA!" Both Killean and Vegas yelled, the latter moving to cover the little girls ears. "Not in front of the little one you dip!" He said as Desiree had a lost look on her face. "S... sorry guys. It slipped out. Won’t happen again!" Big Mac said as he put some papers on Vegas’ desk. Vegas removed his hands from Desiree’s ears and she seemed grateful that she could hear again. "She quit, well that’s just great." Vegas sighed; leaning back in his chair with Desiree snuggled close to him. "We need to find someone else." "Instead of pointing out the obvious genius, do something about it. Hanna told me you like wearing a thong. Get out there!" Killean ribbed his good friend, winning a hard laugh from Big Mac. Vegas just shook his head. "Yeah, and Mac here could be the beanbag chair, the fat fu... uhhhh guy. Yeah." He sighed, almost slipping in front of Desiree too. "Actually, I lost some weight boss. I used to be 345 and now I’m down to..." "I’m just bustin’ your balls you retard! Put an ad in the paper for a new girl." Vegas replied, sending Mac away. Killean looked over at Desiree, who was still having fun playing with Dave’s chain. "Man, one dancer won’t make a lick of difference." "Perhaps, but three quitting in the span of a week will." Vegas replied. He didn’t tell Killean about the other two girls, because he figured he could handle it by himself. "Three?! Who the heck else quit?" "The other day, both Stephanie and Danielynn took off because they were being bugged by a couple drunks. Kind of a crappy way to leave since it’s to be expected though. Not like they touched them." Vegas explained, clearly shaken that they were now presented with a bad situation. Killean was about to reply when bursting through the door to the club was Terri, one of the strippers, stark naked, yelling and holding a pair of pink g-string panties in her hand. "What is this crap Mac just told me?! I can’t work overtime tonight!" "Hey! JESUS CHRIST!" Vegas covered the eyes of Desiree and tried to hide her from the nudity in the room. He finally ended up putting her under his desk for the moment, giving her a fuzzy blue marker to play with. Desiree didn’t complain and looked at the object in awe. "Terri, what the hell?! You come in here buck naked in front of my kid?" "How was I supposed to know she was here? Anyways, I can’t work tonight!" She replied, giving off an expression mixed with anger and depression. Killean waved off her lack of apology and started to think of something. "Look Terri, with Steph, Dani and now Mary quitting, we need everyone we can get. We’ll pay extra of cour..." But he was cut off by Killean, standing between the naked girl and a husky club owner. "Don’t worry about it Vegas." "What? What are you talking about Kill? We need her." Vegas tried to explain, but Killean’s mind was working fast. "Terri, have you ever thought of becoming a waitress?" "I’ve tried but no one would hire me. Most places won’t hire anyone with visible tattoos." She said, stumbling on her high heels as she slipped on her underwear in the process. "Gotcha. Thanks Terri and no you don’t have to work overtime tonight. We’ll make due with who we have." "Hey, thanks boss!" She replied, turning on her heel and making her way for the door. "Oh and tell Mac to forget the newspaper listing." He finished just before she closed the door. Vegas looked at his friend like he was crazy. "What the heck was that all about man? You want this place to suffer?" He questioned Sirrajin, but The Supreme Machine didn’t miss a beat. "Seriously, how has the club been doing Dave?" "Steadily declining unfortunately. But what can we do about it?" Vegas asked, putting his hands on the desk in front of him. "It’s time to put some fresh thought into this business dude. Let me take Desiree home and get a sitter, you order the pizza and I’ll grab some beer on the way back in. It’s time for a friggin’ meeting." Killean smiled. "Yeah, cause I wanna know what’s on your... oh damn..." Vegas sighed as he looked under the desk at Desiree, still holding the fuzzy marker. "What?" Vegas grabbed a hold of Desiree, turned her around and stood her on the desk. She was holding the fuzzy blue marker, minus a lid now and she had blue marker all over her face. She was smiling at her Dad, whose smile faded away. "What the hell man? That shit’s permanent isn’t it?" Killean raised his voice, looking over his daughter to see marks all over her arms too. He grabbed the cap from her left hand, putting it back on the marker in her right and he returned the marker to the desk. "Where’d you get such a stupid marker from anyways?" "One of the girls gave it to me. I think it was Yolanda." He said in a rather quiet tone. "Jesus, I’ll be back." He swiftly took Desiree off the desk and headed for the back door. Vegas blew the ink covered little girl a kiss and went back to some paperwork. ~<&>~ Killean, Vegas and Mac were all huddled around a desk in the office. There was a pizza box thrown to the side and another one opened on top of the opposite desk. Mac reached around to grab another slice as Killean cracked another beer. "Man, this is a perfect idea!" Vegas exclaimed, clearly liking the way the meeting was going. "See, we can have open mic nights and local bands from all over town can come in and showcase their talent. We’ll turn the current stage into a great sound stage. The girls who are stripping now can become waitresses, clothed, but leaving little to the imagination with skimpy outfits that we provide. Then, a couple nights a week we can have comedy nights and even a Coyote night with all the waitresses dancing clothed once again. We’ll attract numerous girls when they know that stripping is not involved. Seriously, this place will be rockin’!" Killean stated, taking a long sip from his beer after we are done. "Wow, I’ve always wanted to manage a club like this. But I was never given the chance." Mac said after he swallowed the massive bite of pizza he took. "Well Mac, you’re gettin’ the chance now. You’ll have help too. Once we get the finances squared away, we’ll close the club, renovate and have a grand re-opening. I’ll be around now and then to help like always and Vegas will have one big bear sized hand in there too." "Damn rights. This is gonna be off the fuckin’ hook!" Vegas said, pounding his fist into the desk and falling back into his chair with his arms behind his head. "Mac, go out and tell the girls to get in here right away. They are all still here so let’s get this over with." Killean said, putting the almost empty beer down on the desk. Mac meanwhile did as he was told and disappeared from the office. Killean sat down on the chair on the other side of the desk. "See, told you everything would work out in the end." "You certainly did. The renovations won’t even cost that much. I got a guy." Vegas said, but Killean was not convinced. "You mean the same guy you brought in to fix the door to the john?" Killean questioned, pointing out the rather decrepit door hanging off one of the hinges. He smiled, clearly badgering his friend and business partner. "Anyways, this is great. We’ll bring in a whole new audience and we’ll have a first class night spot. It’s perfect." Vegas said, studying Killean’s actions. He seemed a little bit lost, even though he masterminded the entire idea for the club. "How you gonna balance this new thing and wrestling?" "Oh, I’ll find a way... always have, haven’t I?" He smiled, knowing that some personal time would be cut back for a while but it was just too good an opportunity to pass up. They both grabbed their beers and clicked the cans together in a celebration of sorts. Mac made his way back in the room with the scantily clad women following closely behind. Killean went around the desk and stood along side Vegas. "Girls, we have some great news..." ~<&>~ "Seriously, I could see Boston winning the pennant this year, but the Jays will give them a challenge. Halladay just needs to come back and they need to get some real offense going. Then they’ll be unstoppable." Sirrajin said as he curled one of his arm weights. He was on the phone to none other than Lindsay Troy, something that had become common place once a week before a PRIME event. Killean and Lindsay had developed a rather odd relationship in the span of the last couple months as they battled Fuck You with Tchu and Danny. They were friendly but not really considered friends, even though these phone calls were a constant thing. So how did Killean feel about it? Comfortable actually. Normally, Killean was a ladies man and would try to win them over. Most of the time he was successful and most of the time, he considered them notches on his head board. But Lindsay was different. He didn’t feel like moving in on her. Sure he had some feelings, but they weren’t the same as he usually feels. In truth, he had no idea how Lindsay felt about him. Were they friends to her? Maybe just acquaintances? He wanted to dig a bit deeper into things, but he knew it would probably be suicide since she was so fresh off her relationship with Joey Melton. So he always moved around it, like so... "Anyways, what are you doing after ReVolution on Wednesday?" He never gave a reason for the question, but once again, this was something that was common place. On top of this, she normally came back with the same response. "Depends... why?" So how did he respond? Like a man trying to get his first date. "Oh, no reason. Just wondering is all." It was pathetic. It sounded like some sort of teenage crush or something. He normally felt very confident around the women, but something about Lindz made him... lose that confidence. He had spent some time thinking about it and still hadn’t come to a proper conclusion. Soon enough, the phone call ended and Killean walked over to tend to Desiree and a little pudding mishap she had. While he was wiping her face, the phone rang again. Lindsay had a tenancy to call back when the Red Sox sealed a game to quickly say something about it and then hang up. It was a tease, but just another of those odd things happening more and more. Killean finished wiping the pudding from his daughter’s mouth, but sure enough, it would be back there a few short moments later. He picked the phone back up and answered directly. "Lemme guess... Ramirez with a Grand Slam, right Lindsay?" "Hello?" An unrecognized female voice said on the line. "Uhhh... sorry, yes?" "Is this Mister Kill... Killee..." "This is Killean Sirrajin if you are looking for him." "Mr. Sirrajin, we have received a special request to contact you. My name is Frieda and I’m a nurse here at the Health Sciences Center. We have a patient by the name of Thomas Sirrajin asking for you." His heart sank immediately. It was like his childhood all over again and just like back them, off he went when his father called. ~<&>~ It had taken me a half an hour to get the sitter over, but as soon as she arrived, I was out the door quicker than a hiccup. My mind was swimming with so many thoughts; I slammed the phone down on the nurse when she said my father’s name. I didn’t even ask what it was all about. I just called the sitter, set Desiree up for bed and bolted out the door when she got there. But really, why was I here? Why was I in this crummy waiting room when my Father of all people told me to show up? I always said that I would never again listen to one of his demands, but here I was. It was just like old time. He barked an order and I followed, like a lost puppy. I don’t know what made me do it, but I grabbed his patch from his Vietnam days that he had left me at the bar back in December of 2005. I remembered that day like it was yesterday and once again I swore that if I ever saw him again, he would live to regret that moment. Sadly, I had a funny feeling I would go back on that promise too. Frieda the nurse came out to get me. I was being brought to his room. This made my heart race. What would I do when I got in there? Would I snap and do something I’d regret? Or would I stand there like a goon, not knowing what to do or say? As Frieda turned a corner, I was looking at the patch in my hand. His name was clearly embroidered on it along with "1st Lieutenant". I don’t know what made me keep it that day, but I surely knew that one day in the future I might need it. Looked like that was the day. As she came to a stop in front of the door, I took a deep breath and felt very light headed. I kept my composure and waltzed into the room like I had something to prove, something to say. But when I laid eyes on my surroundings within those disgusting white hospital walls, my resolve immediately weakened. There, in a bed, strapped to numerous machines was Thomas Sirrajin, my Father. He air tubes in his nose and an IV in his arm. All the monitors were reading normal, but he sure didn’t look it. His comb over was no longer a comb over. His hair was matted and greasy. His bald spot was easily visible and reflecting light from the ceiling. He looked like utter hell. But in truth, that’s not where I grew weak. I was in the company of two people I had never seen before in my life. One was an older lady, just like my Father and the other was someone who was a little younger than I was it seemed. But I had no idea what I was dealing with. "You must be Killean. I’m so glad you came. My name’s Hilary, I’m Thomas’ wife." She reached out to take my hand but I pulled it back with a nervous shake. This woman was technically my step mother. I must have had the fear of ten spirits in my eye cause she stood back a bit and dropped her hands to her side. "Thomas was asking for you by name and I did everything I could to find you. This is Cadence, our daughter." She stood to greet me. My half sister that I never even known I had was standing to greet me. What else could I do, but slump down in the nearest chair in a dizzy spell, trying to process the information given to me. It was just too much to handle at that particular moment. "I’m sorry..." I said, trying to regain some sort of control. "This is just so much... I didn’t want..." "... to be here, yeah I figured." A raspy voice said from the other end of the room. He was awake, dad was awake and I was looking at him with a mixture of rage, anger and sympathy. Yeah, I felt sympathy for the mean who left me and Summer for dead all those years back. My sister never made it here, but I sure did and I was wishing I was elsewhere. "You always were a... hard ass..." He coughed in the middle of his sentences. I was drawn to him, something drew me to him. Regardless of the fact that I hated this man with all my being, I was moving to him. I got up from the chair and walked over to his bed side. What was getting into me? "I..." I stammered and stumbled, not knowing what to say. "Just listen to me..." I felt like screaming no, like I did plenty of times as a child. But something made me keep my composure. "I asked for you... to be... here. I wanted to see you... one last time..." It was hard to understand him, but I got the gist of it all. He continued on, talking about the past and the present and what he wanted from the future. He asked me if I could watch over Hilary and Cadence for him. What was I a baby sitter? But it was nothing like that. For the first time in my life, I saw the signs of weakness and defeat in his eyes. He was in the middle of a sentence when the inevitable happened though. "You couldn’t love me and your... mother... but love them, accept..." He choked a little and started shaking. He was quite obviously going into arrest. The machines began to go crazy and I could hear nurses and doctors running towards the room. I stumbled back a little, watching on in absolute horror as he convulsed on the bed. As heart monitor was flat lining. His breathing had stopped and Hilary was hugging Cadence close, both of them crying as they watched the events unfold. It was something children and even most adults shouldn’t see. I turned my head away and hung it over the sink in the room. I felt like I was going to be sick. Finally, without turning back to look, the commotion had stopped the crying continued and the flat line of the heart monitor went completely dead. "I’m calling it..." Said one of the doctors. I always said that my Father deserved to die for what he did. But I never thought in a million years I would witness his brutal demise. It seemed to change me. ~<&>~ I was dizzy, thoughtless and I seemed to smell like a cheap liquor store. No, maybe that was the smell in the room. I think I was sitting in The Woodbine Hotel, the same grubby bar that my Dad surprised me in back in 2005. I had his patch grasped tightly in my hand as I slugged back drink after drink, leaving a fifty dollar bill on the bar. I was gone, pissed out of my face. I didn’t know which was way left or right anymore. I leaned my head t the side, feeling like I was about to vomit. But in my blurred vision, I could see the figure of a female approach. Who was that? I couldn’t see. Damn beer bottle glasses. She said she would take me home and get me out of there before I caused a problem for them or myself. She helped me off the seat and I stumbled along, almost falling against the walls a couple times. It seemed like hours later, but we were at the door to my loft. I remembered that Desiree was with Vegas cause the sitter had to go home. This woman dug my keys out of my pocket and used them to open the door. ~<&>~ With a yawn, he made his way to the bathroom. He scratched his stomach a little and still felt quite woozy. He knew he had a hangover. When he was done, he washed his hands and strode off back to the bedroom, robe in tow, partially hanging off his shoulders. He yawned deeply and rubbed his eyes, clearing his vision to see the last thing he needed to see at that moment. "Mornin’ sunshine!" Stacey said as she sat up in the bed, holding the blanket over her nude body. The last thing he remembered was hitting the floor, literally collapsing in the doorway.
