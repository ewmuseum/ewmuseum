Celebrate Good Times, C'mon!

16 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Aug. 16, 2005 When you make a small but substantial chunk of change wrestling—then piss it all away—you learn to appreciate the simple pleasures in life. For you, there’s always been one. The feel of a good glass in your hand. Not one of those cheap little jobs you get at a bar, but a REAL glass. One with some weight to it, some character, some design etched into the base or the side that lets you know “Hey, I’m different!” This particular glass has a series of indentations running along the bottom perimeter, creating a nice ridged feeling against your palm as you carry it through the living room. It’s got weight. It’s got character. It’s got icecubes inside—god, is there anything better than the sound of ice cubes pinging off the bottom of a glass? Has there EVER been a more inviting sound? So here you go, walking in your best casual shirt and holding one of these quality glasses and part of you starts wondering how much better this glass would feel in your hand if you had some bourbon to mix in the soda currently fizzing happily as it surrounds and slowly starts to dissolve those aforementioned ice cubes. Naaa, best not to focus on that. Luckily, at this party there’s plenty to divert your attention. First and foremost? The high quality human scenery at this shindig—with the finest stuff predictably of the estrogenical variety. You watch as they saunter and strut past on impossibly high platform heels in outfits that leave little to the imagination, flaunting their objects—which are, for the most part, foreign—in the direction of you and every other red-blooded male and a few curiously minded females (not that there’s anything wrong with that—as long as they let you watch) who are all too willing to take notice. There are worse ways to spend a few days off. You can thank your super-agent, Ricky Oliveros, for the invitation. Good ole Ricky still hadn’t given up on getting you to do some endorsements—and if he can work shoots and meetings during your off weeks in PRIME, you’ve decided to give it some serious thought. Meanwhile, he’s tried to help sway you with the same things that have worked for talent agents and salesmen of all kind since one caveman first tried to get another caveman to trade in his club for the newer, sleeker model. Pussy and alcohol. Or, in your case, the PROXIMITY of pussy and alcohol—since you had no intention of indulging in either tonight. You returned home from the latest ReVolution with a headache and a nagging desire to exact quick and bloody retribution that was three times as irksome as a throbbing noggin. Speaking of throbbing, are those girls making out over there?! Focus, man! You’ve got some introspection to do! You could fully expect to get smacked around by wrestlers like Brandon Youngblood or Karina Wolfenden. At the very least, they could give you a good fight. Even Los Diablos—as much as you hate to admit it—could go toe to toe with you and lord knows that you hadn’t seen anyone take that many bruises from Mexicans in masks since the time you stumbled across that BDSM porno online from the dungeon somewhere in Tijuana. But this was different, dammit! This was two… two… NEWBIES! Oh sure, Tom Walczak had been around the block a few times. He’d also been to the police station, the courthouse and the Federal Pound-Me-In-The-Ass Prison… all after entering enshrinement in the “Most Overrated OSW Champions Ever” pantheon where he and Scott Taylor could keep one another company for the rest of recorded time. Then there was Blackwolf—even though you had the feeling by looking at his waistline that a ring name of “Porkchop” might be more anatomically accurate. The point is they left you laying with about as much thought wasted after the fact as the jobbiest jobber in the history of jobbiness. They did it because they said you were an obstacle. They did it because they thought you were in the way. They did it because they were new, and wanted to make an impact. Not long ago the same could be said about you. Hell, the same could be said about you right NOW. It’s not like your neck is any stronger than it was when you first set foot in PRIME. It’s not like you’ve won any titles, competed in any main events. What’s been your claim to fame, really? Winning the I4 competition? HA! Edwards has some skills but his team was comprised of what’s left of Magnum Wolf and the Flying Borscht Brothers. Meanwhile, you had the Lotus guys on your team—able to disappear from a federation without anyone noticing! OOOH, fear their magic… So you earned a spot on the roster, and what did people say? “Azala’s the star of the I4 group!” “No, Easton! He’s destined for greatness!” “Azala!” “Easton!” “Azala!” “Easton!” “Azala!” “Easton!” All of it left you feeling as neglected as a Circuit City in Amish country. There WAS your win over Brandon Youngblood. But then he had to go and shit on it, didn’t he? He had to go and call it a fluke. You knew it wasn’t a fluke. HE knew it wasn’t a fluke. And he knew that you knew that he knew it wasn’t a fluke. But it got people talking… again. About how you weren’t in his league. Nevermind that you left Youngblood’s arm attached to its shoulder joint with the structural integrity of a soapbox racer constructed of paper clips and snot. The whispers started again. “Gibbo’s still just a midcarder. Youngblood so totally pwned him in that match!” That’s why this week is so important. That’s why you need to beat Ellis Easton. People think he’s the next big thing? Destined for greatness? If you have your way, the only thing he’ll be destined for is stitches. You down the last of your soda and set your glass on the strategically placed coaster on the table. Thinking about your perpetual status flying under the radar of most PRIME fans has diminished your desire for the party. “Hey there,” a female voice speaking with a highly unnecessary husky tone breathes close to your left ear breaking your internal monologue which seems catchier than usual tonight. You turn to survey her. Long-haired brunette. Tight black dress in a room full of tight little black dresses. She gives you a confident smile and, knowing that you’re looking (Christ, how can you NOT look), she lets out a sigh, arching her back ever so slightly to accentuate the twins that evidently are the source of so much pride. And somewhere, God looks down and says “I didn’t make those…” No thanks. You duck your head and walk off leaving her standing there. Harsh? Maybe. But when you’re contemplating a match with an egomaniac—an egomaniac who everyone thinks is BETTER than you—it makes you a little short on tact. As you head for the door, disconnected images—sensory snapshots, really—swim through your brain. You imagine the feel of your hand, wrapped up in Easton’s long blond hair down to the roots—and the sudden freeing jerk as you Beal him across the ring and yank out a big hunk of his beloved locks. You imagine him screaming in pain, and how it will sound like music. You imagine the sight of his cold, arrogant, handsome face contorted in pain and obscured by blood spilt at your hand. And you smile. Later, after you get back to the hotel, you ponder if she wonders why that guy was grinning like an idiot as you ran over her. “HEY!” It comes out like more of a squeak, but the solid thud that followed brought you back to attention. “Aw, shit.” You extend a hand and help her to her feet. “Sorry about that, I was ju—” You find yourself staring into a very appealing female face. A very familiar female face. A very FAMOUS female face. QUICK! Think of something clever to say! This is your one chance, man! “I was just leaving.” Dumbass… She winces, then shrugs. “Oh well. I think I’ll live. You hurt?” You start to answer before you realize she’s laughing at you. Forcing a smile onto your face, you run a hand through your close-cropped hair. Years from now, assuming you ever have any grandchildren—or children to sire the grandchildren, for that matter—you’ll be sure to tell them about the time Jessica Alba busted your balls. You realize it’s the only time the famous actress and your genitalia will ever come into even metaphorical contact. “Leaving so soon? This a bad party?” “No, I’m just not in a very festive mood. I don’t even know whose house it is. My agent thought it’d be a good idea to show me a good time, but he bailed out 20 minutes after we got here.” “Pfft. Agents. Isn’t that just like ’em?” “Heh, yeah… Well, see ya. I’m heading back to the hotel. Gotta get up and train tomorrow.” “Movie role?” “Naaaa, I’m… oh what the hell I’m a pro wrestler. In PRIME.” “They the ones on Playboy?” Sweet Jews for Jesus, she’s heard of us. “Uh… yeah.” “They give you guys free trips to the Mansion for that? You know, the Playboy Mansion?” “Um… no. No, they don’t.” “Hmmmmm, pity.” Why is she standing here? Why don’t you make a move? Sometimes you wish your penis wouldn’t try and talk to your brain. This is one of those times. “Like I said, I better get going. Gonna be hard to kick Easton’s ass if I’m not ready.” You turn and start down the driveway when— “ELLIS Easton?!?!” You turn back to face the movie star. “You know him? Don’t tell me you watch.” “Well, Derek was a wrestling fan but…” she blushes. “No. But I know HIM.” The emphasis is punctuated by an eye roll. “He slept with my assistant. Both of them! At the same time! Then they started fighting over him and both quit and it’s SO hard to get good help in this town. Asshole…” Then she cocks her head, looks at you and says something you never expected. “Do me a favor?” Awww yeah. Here’s your chance. Work it smooth now. “Kay.” GODDAMMIT YOU ARE USELESS!” “Kick that poser in the nuts for me?” “Jessica, it’s what I do best.” You couldn’t ask for a better exit line.
