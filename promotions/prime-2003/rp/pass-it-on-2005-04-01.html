Pass It On

1 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/01/2005 Begin. Video Feed. The first time we saw this park months ago, Tyler Lopez was giving a lecture about what turning his back on Tyler would mean. It was very dark and ominous and parks weren’t made for that sort of thing so today is a new day. Today is turning into quite the little project, in fact. This is not the sort of project that is going to be changing any games but we do what we can here. As the camera focuses the very same swing set that supposed PRIME mega villain Tyler Lopez occupied the other day is being used by children in the background. In the foreground, there is something entirely different going on. THERE IS BALLOONS. There is also a man in a red shirt. The man is of course The Scientific Apparatus and to his right are balloons. All kinds of balloons and a banner that says “Happy Birthday!” This is going to be some kind of party. People are setting up stuff all over. The party still isn’t for a few days. Tyler, on the other hand, is doing nothing of the sort. He’s smiling at the camera like an idiot. Lopez: Before I start, I can’t help but mention that my promo today feels strangely devalued. Aimz has reached into my soul and damaged it to the very core. But enough about that. Lopez starts to walk to the right and the camera follows him. A strange man is lurking behind the Apparatus. Lopez: So Mortimer, the gist of this is I’m going to beat you in a wrestling match…AND THE FOLLOWING IS WHY! Yes, I have always found that to be a particularly effective here in the PRIME. As Lopez keeps walking, the camera focuses in on the man that was lurking behind him. He starts walking in the other direction and we just follow him. It is Tyler's lanky pool boy. He’s got a rather big head since he is on television. The camera keeps following him. There is old cheesy detective music in the background. He was originally here to blow up balloons. Pool boy: I tell you what, back in the day, none of this bullshit would have ever happened. No one knows anything about respect anymore. I toured the council like a destructive circle and did I ever get tired? You bet your new age ass I didn’t. Back in the day, Jesus Crucified Christ did they used to suck. Lopez: [OSV] Uh, what is this, exactly. The camera turns to find Tyler, and when it goes back to Pool boy, he is already running away. Lopez: [shaking his head] This could have been a well-coordinated diversion to give me more time to counter-act that impressive Mortimer smack talk, or maybe the world has just gone stupid. Maybe Tyler. Maybe. Earlier. “You go all out for this thing, huh.” “Is there any other way to do anything, Passion?” In some ways, this is mirroring the last piece you were subjected to. We go from the park to the kitchen. The Tyler and Passion kitchen, that is. Description is terribly unnecessary because we’ve already been here before lots of times. It’s just the sort of kitchen that rich white people live in. Passion is in a puffy royal purple robe, and Lopezis dressed for the day in an old Tantalus t-shirt and blue jeans. She is putting dishes in the dishwasher. Passion: I suppose not. But usually, someone’s duty as a protégé isn’t blowing up balloons. Lopez: He’s just helping out. You’re going to be on balloon duty in a half hour, we’re doing this in shifts. Passion gives him an odd glance. Lopez: So, have you given any more thought to my idea? Passion: [walking away] Not really. Lopez follows. Lopez: In the long run, this is a good thing. This place is all about attention. Attention, attention. Passion: Maybe that’s just all that it is about to you. If any other girl would have said that, it might have come off cold. But Passion’s whimsical smile takes the edge off of it. She likes giving him a hard time more than about anything. Lopez mumbles something in response, before becoming more audible. Lopez: So if Dead Laughing can’t show up for the party to do balloon animals and miming and stuff, I’m going to be forced to call another entertainment. Perhaps… Dani Furher. Passion: I really don’t think so. Fade. There are cute little noises and what comes first is an image of the most famous videogame villain of all time. There is Bowser and Mario is really giving him a hard time of it on the screen. This is the Super Mario Brothers game that came with the Super Nintendo system. Some called this the greatest Mario game of all time. We pan back and this is being contained on a large TV in one of the many large rooms in the Lopez estate. Lopez: [VO] Money, Power, Respect is my boss music, and my energy level is why higher than yours. I could have used a more obscure reference than Bowser and all the fucking geeks out there would have probably gotten it too, but I’m more into catering to the masses…anyway. So this is Bowser and he has spikes on his back. The guy in the red shirt is Mario. Panning back even more, there is Tyler who has just returned from the park for a little rest and relaxation. Planning a birthday party can be a lot of work. So, he’s sitting in a bean bag chair. He’s holding a super Nintendo controller. He doesn’t have any of those new systems. Lopez: Basically, Mario faces Bowser in all the video games. Bowser and Mario used to face off a million times a day when these games were popular. It goes like this, Bowser wins and wins and wins until Mario wins once and then the kid doesn’t play the game anymore because quitting while you are ahead is generally the thing to do. It makes me wonder why the game wasn’t name after Bowser in the first place. He’s the only one with perseverance. He has to put up Mario charging the fort a million times to save some Princess that he misplaces all the time. Truth be told, I think Mario loses the princess on purpose because what would he be without a princess to save, hmm? But you, three, Aimz, Furher and even you Mortimer, you are a different bird. Clearly. But nonetheless, my energy meter is still higher than yours. Lopez hits the pause button. The pause button is to the right of the select button. What in the hell did the select button ever do, anyway? I mean, come on.. Lopez: I can’t disagree with a lot of what you say. Surely, I got some things about you wrong in my promo because no one knows someone quite like his or herself. But just the same, it is what makes you right that is your disadvantage. But we aren’t in disagreement everywhere. The entire point of what I said, is despite how much I damaged you in the past…and despite the fact that this is the biggest opportunity you’ve been given in a long time, you still aren’t motivated. And you politely agreed, which is nice, because I hate arguing. Lopez smiles. Lopez: You complain about having to wrestle the likes of each other, and when you trade him in for a much more established Apparatus…you look a gift horse in the mouth. You say, “I don’t have to be anything special to beat you”. You weren’t anything special against anyone and look how that turned out. It takes a supreme effort to beat me, and I don’t say this in gloating fashion. It’s going to take everything you got and them some. It’s the then some that I think you’re going to have trouble with. Do you think I’m just going to slip on a banana peel in the ring? You have to be special every goddamn time you get in that ring or someone is going to beat you. Just ask Killean Sirrajin and he'll tell you that he loses to people that suck with frequency. If you weren’t such a pompous ass you’d see that I am your Bowser, and you are my Mario, and you aren’t going to get a chance to reset the Nintendo if are losing. Your very nature contradicts itself. You have given up on success, but you throw a fit when the booking committee treats you this way. But I suppose everything is ok, and we can all just light a candle for you three tonight because your…real! Lopez shakes his head, laughing in cruel fashion. Lopez: So we were all doing you a favor, hmm? Stripping away the fake macho attitudes…yeah. That’s all professional wrestling is really. Running around in tights that you never would be caught dead in anywhere else. That’s all this has ever been. There are exceptions, naturally. My love for another human being was used against me and her head was practically impaled with ringsteps. I know this lesson more than anyone. If you think letting the real you out was some great career move, then you haven’t really been paying attention. Lopez un-pauses the game and his attention doesn’t really seem to be on the camera anymore. He’s locked in as you might imagine. Later he’ll play NHL ’94 for Genesis. He will not play Shaq-Fu. Lopez: All of you cute and amusing failures have to stick together, I guess. Fade. Lopez: [VO] Bowser and the turtle looking bastards with the wings gangfucked the princess, pass it on. End.
