Stepping Stones Too Glory

2010
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/11/2010 Down Time I. The camera flickers on as the scene is set inside the lavish home of Prime Superstar Bishop Steele. Bishop comes walking down the stairs dry his hands as if he just came out of the bathroom. Walking into the kitchen he heads towards the refrigerator looking for a beverage. Questioning his thirst for something cold, he reaches for a Miller Lite. Popping the cap and taking a drink, he smiles as the drink quenches his thirst. Lowering the bottle he looks outside and locks eyes with his gorgeous wife Alexis. She is tanning outside by the pool in a two piece red bikini. Laying in her lounge chair the sun glistens off her body making Bishop’s little man rise to the occasion. Bishop sits his Miller Lite down and heads towards the patio doors. Walking outside he takes off his shirt, showing off his nice body and walks over towards Alexis. He sits down next to Alexis and reaches for the bottle of tanning lotion. Alexis knows that Bishop is sitting next to her. “I wonder if he is going to put lotion on me.” Alexis wonders. “You’re looking a little burnt sweetie.” “Well maybe you should put some lotion on me then big guy.” Bishop smirks and starts putting lotion on Alexis. Massaging her body while rubbing lotion on her body, Alexis’s eyes roll into the back of her head as she enjoys the soft touch of her husband. 5 minutes pass by and by that time the two of them are so engaged into each other they ignore the home phone ringing like crazy. Eventually Javier the Steele’s butler answers the phone. “Steele residents, this Javier speaking how may I help you?” “Yes this is Prime management; I’m looking for Bishop Steele.” “One moment please.” Javier walks towards the patio doors and looks out spotting Bishop and Alexis embracing each other by the pool. Javier smirks and walks back over to the phone. “Umm Mr. Steele is preoccupied at the moment. Can I take a message?” “Well it is really important that I speak with him, so just have him call me back. My name is Blaine Blair. My direct number is [detail withheld]. Have him call me back as soon as possible. It regards his upcoming match and tournament information.” “I will forward this information to him quickly sir.” “Thank you and have a nice day.” “You’re welcome. Bye now.” Javier hangs up the phone and takes the message to Bishop Poolside. Interrupting Bishop and Alexis’s alone time Javier hands Bishop the message. “What is this Javier, when did he call?” “He just called about 4 minutes ago.” “Umm why didn’t you tell me he was on the phone?” “Well sir when I came out here to tell you that the phone was for you, you was embraced in Mrs. Steele’s arms engaging in adult acts and I figured you didn’t want to be bothered at the moment.” “Well you get a reprieve this time Javier, but next time just tell me; this is kind of important to me and my career.” “Will do sir.” Javier walks off as Bishop stands up. He smiles at Alexis and heads inside. Alexis lies back down and continues her relaxing in the sun as Bishop walks off towards the house. Walking through the large double patio doors, he grabs his Miller Lite and walks towards his office. Taking a few drinks while walking down the hall, he turns the corner and walks into his office. He shuts the door and sits behind his desk. He dials the number given to him and awaits an answer. While waiting for an answer he opens his right top drawer and grabs a Cuban cigar out of his box. He splits it and then reaches for a secret compartment in the same drawer and grabs a small bag filled with kin bud. He starts breaking up some kin bud and putting it into his Cuban when Blaine answers the call. “Hello this Blaine Blair, who am I speaking too?” “What’s good Blaine, this is Bishop. My butler told me you called.” “Yeah I have some information about your match this week on Rev 223, which will be your first round of the tournament.” “Well hold that thought for just one minute Blaine, I got do something really quick.” Bishop finishes rolling up his blunt and grabs his lighter. Sparking up his blunt he leans back into his chair and grabs the phone again. Puffing on the blunt he continues his conversation. “Alright go ahead Blaine, feed me the info.” “Alright this week you will face Vic Thunder and Hush in your first tournament match and if you win of course you will move on to the next round.” “So is there anything you can tell me about these opponents of mine?” “Honestly I don’t know anything more than you do and are you smoking right now, because it sounds like breathing in the phone.” “Hell yeah fool, I’m smoking some of that good shit right now.” “Hey you better not be smoking weed, because if you are you will get dropped in a heartbeat.” “Look man calm down it is a natural herb that grows from the ground, I just happen to light it on fire to see what it does and man it does some freaky shit and smells amazing.” “Whatever just don’t do that shit around other management figures because they will definitely get your ass.” “Look Blaine I got this and my opponents will see why I’m True Perfection.” “Well I will see you at Rev 223 and good luck in your match.” “My opponents need the luck not me.” Bishop hangs up the phone and continues to puff on his blunt. While chilling in his office Alexis comes walking in with a furious look on her face. “Umm why are you in here smoking that good shit and not telling me you are smoking.” “I didn’t think you wanted any baby, my bad.” “You know that you can’t smoke that in here and I wouldn’t smell it.” “Sorry, here take some hits then babe.” Alexis grabs the blunt and starts puffing on it as Bishop Turns on his computer and pulls up his twitter page. He logs in and starts blogging. 6:55 pm. 5/10/2010 You know who it is once again. It’s your boy sitting here at his home relaxing with my wife smoking a fat beezy. Anyways come check me out at Rev 223 Izod Center in East Rutherford, New Jersey. Watch as I smash my two other competitors to move on to the next round. Counting on you guys to get this out there on Twitter, Facebook, and other blogging sites and letting everyone know that Perfection INC is here to stay. Bishop logs out of Twitter and heads over to PerfectionINC.com to see how many fans have been on his site. “Wow babe, what the hell is going on with my fans? I have only had about 2500 hits today which is way under my average.” “Win this match at Rev 223 and I bet your site booms with hits and comments from all over.” “You think so sweetie?” “Trust me; I have been in this type of industry before. When I was just starting out in modeling it took me a while before I got the response that I have now. Once you hit that big spot or big show, everyone seems to be interested in you and your phone won’t stop ringing.” “I’ll take your word for it. Anyways I need to head out. Need to get some training in before my match.” “Who are you going to train with?” “Well seeing that this is a triple threat match and I have no idea who my opponents are or do I know anything about them, I’m basically starting fresh and fighting this one blind. So anyone I train with will help me just as much as anyone else.” “I think you should train with Team Xtreme Mayhem.” “My MMA trainers. Why them?” “Well when you are doing MMA, you didn’t really know much about your opponents then. Also you are trying to make a statement to Prime and the roster that you are not someone they should take lightly. So why not brutally beat your opponents in your match to shut everyone up.” “You know I knew there was a distinct reason why we are such a good team.” “Was there any doubt.” “Not for one second. Anyways I gotta go, but I love you and I will see you when I get back.” “You want any more of this blunt?” “Just save it for later tonight, because we getting freaky tonight.” “Oh really, well then leave the credit card so I can get something nice for tonight then.” “Ha ha your nuts use your card.” “Whatever.” Bishop walks out leaving his card on the desk. He puts his shirt on and heads towards the garage door. Grabbing his motorcycle keys on the way out, he gets on his Suzuki GSXR 750 and starts it up. Revving the engine and rumbling the tools on the wall he speeds out of the garage and heads down the driveway. Popping a wheelie he turns right out of the driveway and races off down the street towards Xtreme Mayhem Gym. The camera fades away……. Preparation II. Arriving at Xtreme Mayhem Gym, Bishop pulls in to the parking lot and parks by the front door. Easily he can see the sign that says no parking. Bishop shrugs his shoulders and walks into the building. Strutting as he walks passed the cage and other fighters training he walks into Mike Lowry his old training partner. “You got to be kidding me. Bishop is that you?” “What’s crackin Mike?” “Not shit man how have you’ve been?” “I’ve been good and I see you’re still doing you thang here at the gym.” “Of course man. I even got a shot at the Middle Weight Title next week.” “Are you serious? Congrats man. Hey is Harry here?” “Yeah he is up in the office.” “Alright well it was good seeing you, but don’t get too comfortable, because I want to see what you got.” “Oh you don’t want any of this. I ain’t anything like I was back when you was fighting here.” “We’ll see youngster, we’ll see.” Bishop smirks at Mike as he lowers his shades revealing his eyes. They both glare at each other as Bishop flexes and heads upstairs to Harry Smith’s office. Bishop knocks. “Come in.” Bishop walks in and starts laughing. “Look if you are going to be an asshole, I really don’t have time for bullshit, so get out of my office.” “You know you’re just as lazy as you was back when you coached me.” Harry puts down his clipboard and looks up, only to chuckle at the presence of Bishop. “What in the hell are you doing here?” “Come to get some training from one of the best in the business.” “I thought I was a lazy piece of shit?” “Oh you are, but that doesn’t mean you don’t know what you are doing.” “How have you been Bishop?” “Good can’t complain.” “So you need some training, if heard you right.” “Yeah I got a Triple Threat match against some opponents that I know nothing about.” “Well that would be a good thing to know because it at least gives us something to go off of.” “But that’s why you are the best. I know you got some guys here with different styles that will give me options on what to do, plus I figured I would just use my MMA skills to dominate this match anyways.” “That is if you still got your MMA skills.” “Shit I’m Perfection Harry, can’t anybody touch me and you know it.” “Well go get dressed because I got just the warm-up for you.” Bishop smirks and heads out of the office down to the locker room. Harry shakes his head as he walks down behind him. Gathering all his students and fighters by the cage for Bishop’s training session, Harry speaks to the guys he wants to train with Bishop during this session. Bishop comes out in his fighting gear prepared for his test. Bishop’s first opponent is Jeremy Hanson, a new upcoming fighter looking to makes his mark in the MMA world. “This will be a three round warm-up fight. They will be 5 minute rounds.” “You ready for this old man.” “You shouldn’t talk shit kid, because it will come back and haunt you, trust me.” “Ha ha we’ll see.” Bishop smirks and gets ready for the bell. Round 1 Jeremy Hanson wraps around "True Perfection’s" waist, and drags him to his back, taking side control "Hammer" slides his knee across the body of Bishop and takes full mount. Jeremy secures his position. "True Perfection” gives his back up trying to escape the mount. "Hammer" peppers Bishop Steele with several shots to the face. Bishop starts to wiggle out of back control; Jeremy Hanson lets him escape putting them both on their feet. Round 1 comes to a close. Round 2 "True Perfection" dodges a cross from Jeremy Hanson, and hits him with a stiff right hook. Steele attempts a single leg, but a scramble takes place getting both fighters to their feet. Jeremy Hanson connects with a series of punches forcing "True Perfection" into a clinch. Hanson receives numerous knees to the thighs from Bishop. Steele has double under hooks, locks across the body, and takes Jeremy down to the mat, landing in great side control. Hanson tries to get back to full guard, but has to settle with half guard. Round 2 comes to a close. Round 3 Jeremy Hanson throws a strike that Bishop Steele slips and then uses a Kosoto Gake trip and falls into Hanson’s guard. "True Perfection" drops some good elbows while being caught in the guard. Bishop takes a risk going for a leglock from being caught in "hammers" guard. It doesn't pay off as "hammer" makes his way to standing. The fighters circle each other. "Hammer" takes a heavy kick to the face forcing him to stumble. Steele comes forward, throwing a short hook that distracts Hanson. Steele then grabs an overhook and uses a small outside-hook throw and takes side control. "Hammer" takes a few knees to the body while under Bishop Steele’s side control. Bishop slides his knee across the body of "hammer" and takes full mount. He then takes his back and locks him a rear naked choke. Jeremy is forced to tap out. After the match Bishop stands up helping Jeremy up on the way. They shake hands as Bishop Whispers in his ear. “I told you not to talk shit just yet, but good fight, you got real skills.” Jeremy acknowledges Bishop’s words and heads out of the cage. “There will be a 15 minute intermission and then we will go to the next fight.” Bishop walks out and sits down next to Harry and Mike. “I see you haven’t loss your step any.” “I told you I was Perfection and can’t anybody stop me.” “Yeah, yeah well you are a little rusty and you need to defend the takedowns more, which means in your match you need to take the initiative and never give your opponents the opportunity to take the upper hand.” “So you want me to make the first move and then capitalize on that.” “You got it.” “Alright then let’s do this.” Bishop stands up and heads back into the cage. His next opponent is Anderson Riley; he is currently ranked #2 in the world and is fighting for the Welterweight title next week. Anderson steps into the cage ready. He glares at Bishop and Bishop just shrugs his shoulders as if he careless of his ranking and gets ready. The ref signals to begin. Round 1 Bishop keeps Anderson Riley at a distance with some well timed jabs. "True Perfection" parries Riley’s jab and connects with a cross. Riley is staggered by a jab to the chin. Anderson attempts a lazy shot, which Steele sprawls, eventually taking Anderson’s back. Round 1 comes to a close. Round 2 Riley connects with a sweeping kick at the leg of Bishop Steele. Anderson takes a heavy kick to the arm attempting to block his head. Riley circles Bishop, landing jabs and crosses at will. Anderson weaves through a jab-cross combo from "True Perfection" but is met with a powerful shot to the ribs that knocks the wind out of him. "True Perfection" throws an uppercut, then a cross that knocks Anderson out. Bishop stands over Anderson and smirks. Anderson is slow to get up and is helped out of the cage by his fellow partners. Bishop stays in the ring awaiting his next opponent. As Bishop stands there his next opponent comes out. Luke Blair comes out who is a heavyweight with a lot of power. Bishop bounces around and waits for the ref to signal the start of the fight. Round 1 Steele snaps back the head of Blair with a stiff jab. Steele shoots a takedown from a distance putting Luke Blair on the ground. Blair attempts an armbar. Bishop times a spin perfectly landing a strong overhand as he works his way into Blair’s guard. Blair works for a triangle but Steele throws Luke’s straight leg over his head and makes it to half guard. Bishop tries to pull his leg out of half guard, but no luck. "True Perfection" slips out of half guard and into side control. Blair gives his back up in an attempt to get out of side control. Blair starts to wiggle out of back control; Bishop Steele lets him escape putting them both on their feet. "True Perfection" stuffs a shot from Luke and hits him with a hard hook to the temple and pushes him to take his guard. Round 1 comes to a close. Round 2 The fighters circle each other. Blair stuffs a takedown but is backed into a corner and must slide into guard to avoid being picked up. Luke shifts his hips to get better position in the guard. Blair sinks a Komura, but cannot finish. He settles for a switch into mount. Blair pulls Steele close to avoid a flurry of strikes. Blair tries to slip out the back, but Bishop Locks in an inverted triangle choke. Luke Blair taps. Bishop helps Luke up and shows his sportsmanship. Blair walks out of the ring with Bishop right behind him. Bishop sits back down in his chair next to Harry and drinks some water. “Not bad Bishop, not bad at all.” “I told you I’m the best.” “Ha you may be the best against these fighters but let’s see if you can match up with someone that knows your style.” “What are you talking about?” “Your next opponent will be Mike Lowry your old training partner.” “Ha ha you must be joking.” “Nah I’m serious. What you nervous?” “Man I’m never nervous nor am I worried about his no talent ass.” “Well then get in there cause its time.” Bishop stands up and looks into the cage and Mike. Mike smiles with a cocky smirk as Bishop steps in. Shaking his head Bishop stands across from Mike with a look of determination. The ref signals to begin. Round 1 Lowry takes a punch and goes to the mat, Steele pounces into side control. Lowry recovers quickly. “True Perfection” slides his knee across the body of Lowry and takes full mount. "The Viper" lands a good elbow as "True Perfection" lowers his torso. "The Viper" gets pummeled by the fists of "True Perfection". "The Viper" gives up his back in an attempt to escape, but doesn't quite make it. Bishop tries to set in a rear naked choke, but can't quite finish it. Mike Lowry tries to explode out the backdoor but Bishop Steele sinks in both hooks, continually landing outside strikes. Mike Lowry falls flat to protect himself. Round 1 is over. Round 2 The fighters circle each other. "True Perfection" stuffs a shot from "The Viper" and hits him with a hard hook to the temple and pushes him to take his guard. "True Perfection dodges a few strikes from Lowry and lands an overhand and an elbow. A can opener from Bishop Steele opens up Lowry’s guard. Bishop Steele lands two quick strikes as he moves into half guard. Bishop Steele tries to pull his leg out of half guard, but no luck. Mike tries to slip his leg out to get full guard, but is having difficulty. Lowry tries to slip his leg out to get full guard, but is having difficulty. The ref stops the fight as the round comes to an end. Round 3 Bishop Steele connects with a series of punches forcing Mike Lowry into a clinch. Bishop gets double underhooks, and unleashes unanswered knees to the midsection. Steele escapes the clinch and creates some distance. Steele circles Lowry, mixing jabs and leg kicks. Steele circles "The Viper", mixing jabs with body shots. Lowry keeps Steele at a distance with some well timed jabs. Mike presses forward and grazes "True Perfection" with a combo. "True Perfection" wants none of the stand up and clinches with Mike to then savagely, throws him down to the ground. Bishop lays into "The Viper" with some stomps. "The Viper" can't handle it as the ref stops the fight. Bishop looks at Mike and smirks. Mike stands up and shakes hands with Bishop. They walk out of the cage and sit down. “Honestly Bishop you are ready for that match. I mean you just dominated some of our best fighters. I really don’t see any of your opponents being able to handle you in that match.” “Yeah it felt good to get back into the cage and to be honest I miss it a lot, but I’ve moved on to a new chapter and I appreciate the training session.” “Hey man anytime you know where to find us.” “Well thanks for everything, but I have to go. Need to pack and get ready to head to New Jersey for my match. Wish me luck and good luck to you Mike and you Luke in your title matches next week.” “Go kick some ass.” Bishop smirks as he heads back to the locker room. Harry smiles as the camera fades away…….. Final Thoughts III. The scene opens as Bishop Steele is sitting out on the balcony of his hotel. He has a Jack Daniels and Coke in his hand as he looks out at the New Jersey city. The sounds of music and people partying up in the city echo throughout the city. Bishop stands up and leans over the balcony while taking a sip of JD and Coke. “Pretty soon I will be home. I will be in the one place where I feel no one can’t touch me or even compare to me. That very place will inside the IZOD Center fighting for my chance to move on in the tournament. But this tournament is not just any tournament and this match is not just any match. You see this is my chance not only to get a title shot, but to also prove that I belong here. That I’m someone this company can count on to put fans in the seats and sell out venues around the world. That I can be a household name that many will talk about for years to come and in order to do that I have to not only win but I have to dominate my opponents. And that is what I plan to do. You see Vic Thunder and Hush, I understand that you have just as much to gain as I do, but I also understand what it takes to be the best and maybe you do too, but I can’t worry about that. All I can do is go into this match with my game plan and agenda. Many of my fans are expecting me to come out here and talk about you too and how I will do this and that, but what does that accomplish. What I will do will be showcased in the match not in words, because unless you can back them up, then they are just meaningless expressions of what you said you would do. I don’t know anything about you two, but I do know you are standing in my way of becoming a champion and a contender for more things in the future. With that being said I will leave you with this. In this match coming with a fuel and desire to be the best and if one of you even thinks about getting in my way of being the best, just know I will do whatever it takes to make my dream a reality. You two are just mere stepping stones to my glory and it’s time for me to use those steps to get that much closer to my goal. See you in the ring.” Bishop takes a drink of his JD and Coke and then walks back into his room. He shuts the patio doors as the camera fades away………
