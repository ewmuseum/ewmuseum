Such a thing is beyond the scope not only of this medium, but of your foolish mind. Instead we will attempt to familiarize you with some basic rules of operation, to gift you a skimming insight into the thought process of a man so dangerous that someone once said of him: “Holy shit! Did you see that?! He just--” Naturally, that someone died before he could finish testifying to whatever it was he saw. Don’t worry. He went easily, painlessly, and like you, without a clue. ~><>…<><~ Lie. Always.* Even if there’s no reason to do so, lie. Even if you find yourself conversing with the Almighty, lie. Even if you’re going to get caught eventually, lie. Even if that eventuality is only thirty seconds away. Five seconds. One second. Half a second. The abysmally miniscule length of time you entertained the notion of cornering The Deville. *Sometimes a small or seeming truth is required to facilitate a bigger lie. Lordship. Never questioned. Never allowed to appear to be in question. Even remotely. Ever. Example: Deville at prestigious banquet. Head of the table. Chair a bit higher. Bigger cup with better brew. Brighter light overhead. Etc. Goes to bathroom. Everyone stands in reverence as he saunters out. Darth Vader exit-music. Rose-bearers. Etc. Bush enters and sits in his seat. Deville returns, approaches the interloper, arches a demanding eyebrow. Offender stands up, apologizes, offers to shake hand. After a long stare The Deville finally acquiesces, giving his hand so his ring may be kissed. “And stop being such a pacifistic pussy about things all the time.” Eloquence is wit. Brevity suffers from chronic constipation. Every time you speak, even if only of the weather, it’s incomparably important, and your audience is hanging on your every word. Why say in three words what you can stretch into thirty, simultaneously delighting and pissing off everyone in the process? Swerve. A straight drive is never fun or entertaining, no matter the destination or how many mundane detours through the countryside you take. Not even if you’re drunk. . . . Well, okay, then it’s a little fun. But as fun as swerving around, screwing with people? Not a chance. Example: It’s a nice sunny day. We’re on a pleasant residential street, with yawning trees on every manicured lawn and a car in every driveway. A bunch of kids are playing hockey in the middle of the road. The same road Deville is cruising down. “Car!” one shouts, and the fat kid that has to play goalie picks up the net, taking it to the curb. Deville nods as he rolls on by, enjoying the scenery, especially the leggy lass sashaying along on the sidewalk, gyrating her ass – which juts out like an air-conditioner – like she’s wearing high-heels. As if she can feel his lecherous, lascivious gaze, she peers over her shoulder, and at just that moment The Deville jerks the wheel toward her. With a horrified scream, she flees up the nearest property, arms flailing. “Ahahahahahahahahahaha!” cackles Deville as he straightens the vehicle, feeling tears welling up in his eyes. “She almost climbed that tree! Run! Hahahahahaha!” Always be the flame, never the moth. Casanova said that, but it doesn’t only apply to romance with the fairer sex, or even to getting laid. Let people’s enthusiasm and attraction be their downfall; if you wait long enough, they’ll come to you, and then you consume. Or, in the case of nice tits and a delectable backside, you devour. Subtlety is King. Even if you later have to point it out. Example: You didn't notice that last section was to deride whatshisname for showing up in PRIME, did you? Formula is for babies. Formulae for math. Grow the fuck out of it already. Step beyond the box. Apologies are not to absolve guilt, but to inspire it. If you think saying sorry makes you feel better or shows character, you’re a sucker. In these days of moral relativism the criminal is the victim and the victim is the criminal. Anything can be rationalized, everything can be excused and explained. Don’t be sorry for what you are or what you did; be sorry for what they made you, for what they made you do. Personal accountability for wrongdoing, they say? Apologize for not having the pious upbringing they did. Do random badass shit. It keeps people on their toes. Example: Deville and Rollins are backstage, with The Renegade lecturing Deville about how he’s been slipping lately. “Hold that thought,” The Deville interrupts with a grin, plucking a phone from his pocket. A handful of seconds and some number-punching later he’s addressing the receiver. “Hey, it’s me. Yeah. Six balloons popped. No, helium. Blame the gypsies, man. It’s always the damn gypsies. Two smokes, three beers, and deer antlers. Yeah. Take it easy.” With The Renegade regarding him cockeyed, Deville folds up the phone and tucks it back into his jacket. “The fuck was that?” queries Rich, arms spreading. “Someone’s dead in five minutes.” “What?! Who?” “How the hell should I know?” Pay no attention to the man behind the curtain. You are the man behind the curtain. Except you’re not a hapless pussy. Whenever something good happens, even if you were ignorant of it beforehand, once it goes down you’re responsible – in fact, it was your plan all along. Without you it could not have happened. And if someone ever pulls back the shade, make sure the last thing they see is the business end of your shotgun. Don’t quote song lyrics. Unless they really, really own someone. ~><>…<><~ The author of this book, giddy with excitement, approaches The Deville with his masterpiece, hoping he’ll appreciate the brilliance and allow the both of them to cash in. Nah. More like a roleplay.” “A wh--” Yeah. See the foreword.
