Momentum Means Something Different To Him

undated

Date: 04/28 and 04/29 Who is this Faceless Fighter I speak of? No one knows, nor do I care to hazard a guess. I do however know who it’s not. It’s not someone with a child, with any kind of life to address. It’s not a person with love or remorse. No, this soul lost in the annals of time is not I, I know who I am. I’m a legend. You’re a poser. |~o~| Monday, in Minneapolis It had gotten out to the public, somehow, the arrival time of United Airlines flight 65 from Dayton, Ohio. The Lindbergh terminal was the final destination of wrestling’s “Supreme Machine”. Unfortunately it had become common knowledge and anyone who was set to attend Revolution in two days found time to get out of either their cushy, high paying or breakneck, slave wage jobs. Hardcore fans and part time marks blending into one, hoping to get as much as an autograph or as little as a quick view of a wrestling icon. Normally it wouldn’t be such a big deal to mingle with some fans or to whip out a Sharpie to scribble on some 8x11 photo. In fact, under normal conditions, he would be looking forward to it. Everything. The whistles from the single women... the hollers from the more hardcore fans. On this day however, Desiree Sirrajin was quite upset with the entire hubbub around her. “Dizzy, it’s ok. Really, it’s… EXCUSE ME!” A man with a backward ball cap stepped back on her purse, which had landed on the floor while trying to console the crying child. Jenny held the littlest Sirrajin in her arms, trying to dry her tears as the crowd in front of them got louder. She picked up her now footprint covered purse from the tile floor and returned to her vacant seat on the bench with Desiree in her lap. Suddenly the murmur of the crowd grew to the volume of a few hundred separate discussions as an unmistakeable black, shiny head of hair came into view at the top of the escalators. People began to push forward a little, almost abandoning the area around Desiree and Jenny, triggering Jenny back to her feet again to catch sight of her man. The oval shape of his sunglasses was next to appear followed by the rest of his features until finally he stepped foot on the escalator descending from the balcony above. Almost out of nowhere, a few suited guards made their way to the front of the escalator, blocking the way so the group of people could move forward no more. As the escalator lowered Sirrajin to the ground below, the crowd began to cheer and clap, welcoming the legend with open arms. Through screams of his name, Jenny knew it would be futile to try and get Sirrajin’s attention. Instead, she stood off to the side with Desiree and waited until he came closer. A few other guards appeared and created somewhat of a bubble in front of the escalator for Killean to walk through. As soon as he stood on the hard tile the posters, magazines, newspaper articles and other memorabilia shook from the hands of happy fans, trying to get a John Hancock from the former Universal Champion. Killean was led by one rather large guard into the bubble created by the other guards, but that didn’t stop him from shaking hands with certain fans trying to reach out. The ever present Sharpie emerged from his pocket as he gave a few free signatures to the fans being contained. Finally, after a few dozen scribbles, the guards do their job like their kids were being put through college by this one single security gig. They back the fans away further, trying to maintain some sort of order. One other guard happened to address the audience. “This isn’t a publicity appearance! If you want to see Mr. Sirrajin in person, an autograph signing exhibition is set for tomorrow outside the Foot Locker in the Mall of America!” As he finished his speech, other guards began to distribute flyers. Killean made his way through the cheering audience, getting closer to Desiree and Jenny. Finally deciding it a good time to make her presence known, Jenny threw her arm in the air, waving out to try and catch the eye of her man. Looking through the crowd, his vision locks on the redhead in the corner, holding his pride and joy. He points her out to the guard leading him and he makes a beeline in that direction. The crowd moves out of the way at the request of the suits in front and finally, he is reunited with his loved ones for the first time in over two weeks. Desiree holds out her hands and her Father is only too happy to oblige, sharing a hug with his daughter and then leaning down to hug Jenny as well. Flashbulbs light up the corner of the terminal as they have since the PRIMEate made his arrival. Killean grabbed her hand and they started to make their way out of the area, even as chants of “KILLEAN” ring out behind them. The security team keeps the crowd at bay while another couple guards accompany the family through the Lindbergh terminal towards the limosine waiting outside It took a few minutes for the crowd to finally disappear in the rear view mirrors of the limo, but the pressure was off and Sirrajin could finally relax his shoulders. He reached into the fridge and grabbed a bottle of water for himself and Jenny as Desiree was happily sucking away at her juice box. “That was the worst one yet.” Jenny said between sips. The driver raised the dark separation window, giving his occupants their privacy. “That? That was nothing Jen. Back when I was champion, the crowds attracted in big cities were 3 times that size. Some people wouldn’t take no for an answer either. These people were tame.” As he spoke, Jenny opened her eyes wide, ignoring her water for the moment to glare into the now uncovered eyes of her main squeeze. “You can’t be serious. How do you do it? Why do you do it? I couldn’t handle that all the time.” She waved her hand a little, trying to dismiss the topic. Why does he do it? A question that even he may not know the answer to. Sure he made the decision to return to the ring based on his undying hunger to be a part of what seems to be a part of his soul. Soul… perhaps it’s a topic too out of reach for certain people. |~o~| To be vacant of a soul is to lack direction. To be devoid of all feeling and emotion. Sure, in the end you may wear a mask and put on a hell of an act, but it only fools the shallow and easy follower. When you begin to play your mind games, some people may grow nervous, some impatient and some completely tired. Whichever they grow, you win. You throw them off their game just enough to pounce and devour. It’s been your game since day one. I’ve had that game played with me and I have also been the host of such games. So I’ve seen it and done it all and the difference Wednesday will not be your soulless lack of remorse. No no Cozen. The difference will be my hunger and my experience. The difference will be my apparent ability to stop momentum dead in its tracks, disabling its ability to follow the course laid out by its conductor. Do your homework and catch up. In fact I’ll provide you with the information myself. Kincade – A seemingly unstoppable monster, a man rumored to not feel pain. He fell to me at Colossus I, relinquishing the Intense Title to my hands for the first time. Karina Wolfenden – She was a smash mouth, I’ll killyouintheface talent who lived up to her every word. A tag title reign, a Dual Halo win and a Universal Title reign all in her first 10 matches, all wins. She had the momentum of a runaway 18 wheeler, barrelling through everyone in her path. But she met that impenetrable brick wall known as The Supreme Machine at Ultra Violence 2005 having her shoulders counted down for three… having to watch as her title was handed to me to hold high. The most prestigious title in this business… and now it is housed around the waist of, well… a waste. Clyde Walkins – The man was a walking, talking, breathing miracle… although he couldn’t do all three at the same time. He still managed to shock the world by downing Tchu after nine straight wins and a Universal Championship for the man to become known as PRIME’s Wrecking Ball. So Mr. Miracle had somehow held that championship for numerous months, before finally being introduced to his death sentence. Ultra Violence 2006 was the stage and it was my night, grasping the Universal Championship for the second time in my career. Sure I was not proud of being a cheap shot artist, but I did whatever I could to get the job done. Brandon Youngblood – He was PRIME’s Pariah. Built like a brick shit house, the man has a jaw that could crush third world countries and he could never keep said jaw clamped to his upper teeth. He talked a bad game and also played a bad game in the ring. He came back with momentum, winning the 5-Star title he valued so much. It was a title he never lost in the first place. He was set to go the distance again, to get another shot at Universal glory. But he never thought the trump card would be a factor. I beat him to a bloody pulp and made him pass out, a feat no other man had accomplished. I took his title and I took his pride. All that was left was a broken and beaten shell of a man who was considered the Only Diamond in the Sport. I beg to differ. Easton Hall – He was a beast in his own right, taking the PRIME stage by storm and winning the Alias Title early in his PRIME tenure. He was seen as unstoppable, a man who has success hardwired into his genes. But again, the freight train was removed from its tracks when I decided to take the Alias title from him to complete my collection of every singles title in PRIME. The only person to ever claim the Grand Slam. Do you see what you are up against? You have momentum and I feast on it. You have a championship that I eventually want back. You have the one thing that brings me to that next level. You have the hype behind you. Crushing you and breaking you will be my pleasure, to reveal to the world the truth that our Universal Champion is not all IT’S cracked up to be. |~o~| Tuesday Night, Following Autograph Session Rewind… Play. Rewind… Play. Rewind… Play. Rewind… One of the most respected technological advances of today has to belong to digital video recording. It’s being used in the fast paced world of sports as their official means of instant replay. It’s also being used in many a home as the official means of getting off to girl on girl pornography. On this occasion though, even instant replay can’t possibly lead you to answers pertaining to a certain Universal Champion. “Katahajime huh?” Killean studies the tape of Cozen in the ring. The multiple times she applied the submission to individuals in the Halo to choking out Lindsay. She also happened to take out a former number one contender and JITC winner. “I’ve experienced that a time or two before, from people stronger and more technically apt than you.” He was more like mumbling to himself, to avoid waking up Desiree and Jenny in the next room. “Have to do better than that.” Unfortunately, there wasn’t much to study. With only three official matches under it’s belt, studying Cozen took about the amount of time an eighth grader wanted to put into studying French. Just a few seconds of studying short clips made Killean fall tired of thinking about ReVolution 159. But yet, he couldn’t stop. This… this thing took out Lindsay, who was nearly unstoppable. It all came to a head at the autograph signing. Fans lined up for hundreds of feet, all coming to see the former Universal Champion. Yet just when he thought he’d seen it all, a Cozen fan was next in line. A makeshift Lindsay Troy wig, similar costume yet half normal, this guy looked like just as much of a freak as Cozen did. He was speaking in tongues as he placed a small picture on the table in front of The Supreme Machine. Killean never looked at the photo, just taking the Sharpie to it as he stared at the wonder standing in front of him. He even recognized a few of the lines he was using. He had heard them just recently… “Ever going to understand what you're facing. I am not a little girl lost…” To… I don't walk down paths or roads. Make them myself, littered with lies. You think I am something that can be quantified, counted. "There is precisely 34.6% craziness in that one." You see what I want to show, but there's so much inside I won't. Come near me again, ruin my plans, step on my heart, and I will have added you as a waypoint on my road. A sign to weary travelers. I am champion, my friend. No time for losers. Maybe next year.” Lines intended for Dusk last week, ring a true tone this week and in weeks past as well. Such words effect everyone who has and will step in the ring with Cozen. So as this guy finished spouting off his mouth in front of the table, a fan finally came forward and pushed the freak out of the way. As he did so, the fan took his picture straight out of Killean’s hands. He caught a quick glimpse of it, enough to realize that he was signing a 4x8 picture of his own defeat in the Dual Halo. Mind games, not only from Cozen, but fans that he never thought existed. He threw his face into his hands, letting out a deep sigh. Long night… longer night tomorrow. He reached up to close the laptop, but a folder on the desktop caught his eye. It was simply labelled “Amy”. He moved the arrow over to the folder and clicked to open it. He then clicked on a file and clicked to open it. A grainy, dark video popped up on the screen. It looked like a wrestling ring, but with how dark the video was one couldn’t be sure. Killean knew what it was though and through the rough image, swashes of red could be seen moving about the screen. For the first time all night, Killean smiled. As the video played, a light turned on and a shadow was cast over his back from behind. He stopped the video quickly and turned to face the light, shielding his eyes a little bit. “Shouldn’t you be in bed?” Jenny asked, making her way to Killean and giving him a small hug and a peck on the cheek from behind. “Just uhhh… lookin’ over some highlights for tomorrow night.” “You’re wasting your time. Just go out there and do what you do best. There isn’t even that much to look over.” Jenny poured a small glass of water from the pitcher in the small fridge and returned it, closing the door and darkening the room a little bit more. Killean closed the “Amy” folder and then the lid on his computer. “You’re right. I need some rest anyways.” |~o~| Confusion at its finest. One woman tells me to keep it simple and the other one will make things that much more complicated when I stand across the squared circle from her. Women… It’s a well known fact that I stop momentum, but one of my favorite things to do is to shake down people from the tree of success when it’s clear they haven’t totally proven themselves. Brandon Youngblood thought he was a big deal until he passed out face down on the canvas. So instead, I’m not just going to beat you, I’m going to rub your face in it like the filthy little animal you are. Lives without souls never lie on. They die forever, left to rot in limbo. Faceless Fighters may win at first, but are destined to lose. This will be your fate Cozen. Like the many who have fallen before you, the momentum… Stops… Here.
