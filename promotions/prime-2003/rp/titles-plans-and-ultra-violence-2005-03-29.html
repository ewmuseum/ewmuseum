Titles, plans, and Ultra Violence

29 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-29-05 Angelo Deville has done it again. Every since Culture Shock the Crimson Angel has antagonized the team of Los Diablos. Whether he has verbally insulted their heritage with cracks about Mexico, attacked them physically with brass knuckles or dogs with Rabies, or simply by mocking them with Mexican body guards that he used in an attempt to imply that Mexican labor is both cheap and worthless, he has gotten under their skin in some way. So you could have easily forgiven Los Diablos, especially Baltasar who seems to have far more problems with Deville than anyone at the moment, if they believed that Deville couldn't stoop any lower. Los Diablos, Baltasar in particular, couldn't have been anymore wrong. For on ReVolution 59 Deville went that little extra and stooped even further. He went down the one road no one, least of all Los Diablos, would have ever expected him to go. Joining in with his new found friend Justin Williamson, better known to the world as The HIT, has decided that he must take from them the most important thing in to them in their careers. Angelo Deville, the Crimson Angel, the man with enough ego and arrogance to fill up twenty federations, has decided he wants to take what belongs to Los Diablos. The PRIME Tag Team Championships. The symbols of excellence that belongs only to the very best tag team in PRIME. The very prize that has already been stolen from PRIME's best tag team on one occassion already... is now the target of Deville's desire. The titles that are now the one thing that Deville believes he can take away from Los Diablos...that will hurt them the most. Well if there is one thing that Los Diablos has become good at it's turning back the challenges to their dreams. They have always found their way around the road blocks that have been placed before them, in an attempt to stop them from reaching their ultimate goal. A goal they have been on the path to achieving for quite some time now. A goal that saw a rather large road block to it...before the goal could even be set. Flash back to several years ago. 'I'm going to become a tag team professional wrestler and finally make this family proud!' Angelus proudly announced as he walked into the living room of his families very small home. They were pretty poor...mainly due to the insane amounts of Tequilla his father drank all the time. The man who spent the vast majority of his life in the local cantina, was also the one person that Angelus had always wanted to impress. To make him proud of the son that he always seemed to hate...rather than love. Unfortunately Tequilla seemed to be the only thing that ever really made the man happy. 'Is that so essa...and just who will be your partner in this little endevour,' Hector enquired from his son. Hector, a stocky but stout man, was already well lit for the day, having already visited the cantina earlier that day. Angelus looked into his fathers eyes and could tell that it wasn't pride or real intrest that had caused him to ask the question. Angelus had been beaten enough times by his father in the past, to know what that look in Hectors eyes meant. 'Jorge is going to train and then team with me. He is my best friend after all and has always been really intrested in doing something in the wrestling industry,' Angelus began explaining while his eyes darted about the room, trying desperately inside his head to figure out how he was going to avoid the event that was sure to come. 'I see,' Hector replied slowly as he stood up from out of his chair. 'I could have swore, puta madra, I told you not to be hanging out with that boy any longer.' Hector finished off his complaint while moving toward Angelus and removing his brown leather belt at the same time. Maria, his wife and Angelus' mother, quickly stood up and moved in between the two in an attempt to talk Hector out of the act he was about to commit. 'Hector please let the boy alone. He only wants to make you proud that your his father. Lets all be happy together on this occassion,' she pleaded with him. A bright smile coming across her face, while fear ran through her body. Hector stopped, right in front of Maria, and simply shook his head slowly as a small smile cracked over his face. 'Perhaps you are right chica,' he began, before suddenly lashing out with a hard back hand that caught Maria across her cheek. The force of the blow knocked her to the floor where she began to cry immediately and shake from the fear. 'NO!' It was the only word Angelus managed to scream out before rushing to his mother and covering her up with his own body. Prepared to take the beating in her place since she had only attempted to save her son. Hector certainly didn't disappoint as he began to wildly beat his son. Alternating between vile and stingings lashes of the belt across his back, arms, and neck...with brutal and vicious kicks to the ribs and legs. Slowly it began to sink in that this might be the very last beating that he would ever have to worry about taking. That the old man had finally snapped enough, and was just drunk enough, that he would beat him to death and take him out of this horrible life he was living. But then it was over. Suddenly, without warning, the beating stopped. The next few moments were an absolute blur for Angelus as he attempted to see through the askew vision that had befallen him from the pain of all the punishment he had taken. As he look up from his mother, and tried to focus, he saw the large outline frame of his best friend, Jorge, beating the hell out of his father. Even at this time the young man who would grow up to become Baltasar was a very large individual. Standing in at over six feet tall and weighing in around two hundred and fifty pounds. Jorge had long been a very stout, powerful, and angry young man, steming all the way back to having lost his own father several years before. He had always praised Angelus, and even envied him at times, cause he still had both his parents. Believing that Angelus had the perfect life that he still wished he had himself. That is until he started noticing the bruises popping up on Angelus more frequently. With every knew one they also seemed to get larger and a darker purple every time as well. 'Where do you keep getting all those damned bruises, essa,' Jorge would question his good friend on several occassions, each time knowing the real answer, but also knowing that Angelus would come up with some new excuse, story, or action that would make Angelus sound like he did something really cool to get them. Trying to impress Jorge in any way possible, cause he wanted to be more like his best friend. 'I know where you really get them from, Angelus, and may God have mercy on your father if he is ever stupid enough to do that when I am around...he'll pay in his own blood for it.' It was a promise that Jorge had made him, and Angelus knew he meant it. More importantly he knew that his father, drunk and all, was smart enough to know it as well. Hector had heard all the same stories and things about Jorge that everyone else around Mexico had heard. Since the loss of his father Jorge had become an uncontrolable person, most of the time, who would snap at the slightest of things. Hector knew this and as such had always acted like a father of the year canidate when Jorge was around. Of course that had always meant a far worse beating when Jorge would finally not be around, a way for Hector to take out his loss of guts and pride on his son. After several moments, blood covering his chest and hands, Jorge stood up and simply stared down at the brutally beaten Hector. Contempt filled Jorge's eyes and Hector just simply let his head roll to the side, to avoid the shame of the gaze. Jorge finally stood up and went over to check on his good friend and his mother. 'You alright hermano,' was the question that came from Jorge...a knowing smile on his face as he could read the fear in their eyes, 'He won't be bothering either of you again. Hector, pride shattered and manhood destroyed, had other plans in mind. No, he wasn't going to beat on Angelus or his wife any longer, but he was sure as hell going to make the man who turned him into a fool in front of his family pay. With all the attention off of him he pulled himself up, using his chair for leverage, and grabbed the wooden baseball bat that always set beside his chair. 'NO!' Once again Angelus screamed out the only thing he could...a second too late...as Hector brought the bat down across the lower back and rib area of the giant young man named Jorge. Jorge roared in pain as he slumped to the floor in serious pain from the shot that could have sent a baseball flying out of any baseball stadium in the world. A sick grin slowly came over the face of Hector as he lifted the bat over his head, ready to put an end to the bane of his existence, and ending the only person who really had ever cared about what happened to his son. 'Sleep tight puta,' were the words that Hector chose as he prepared to blast Jorge with the final shot that would end his life. Before he could complete the swing, however, he suddenly had a feeling shoot through his stomach and ribs like that of a mack truck making its way through them. The force of the shot took him to the floor in, trying to clutch his injured mid section, but unable to as the form of his son, Angelus, defending himself for the very first time in his life...was perched on top of him and was already unloading the most vile lefts and rights a person had ever unleashed on someone. Years of pain, humilation, and suffering came flowing from Angelus with every crunch of his knuckles against his fathers face...screaming like a person who was filled by demonic power...his anger and hate taking over his actions and consuming him. Finally Jorge was able to get up to his feet and pull his friend off...not a moment too soon...as much more and he would have surely killed his father that day. Tears filled up his eyes as Jorge hugged him and reasured him. 'Fear not hermano...he won't ever lay a hand on you again.' End flash back Those words have proven to ring true to this very day. Angelus and his mother moved out of the house and went to Mexico City, where Angelus began his wrestling trainning with Jorge...while his mother worked as a waitress. Hector remained a drunk to his final day, when he slapped a waitress in the cantina he frequented. Her husband, a hitman for a well known drug lord outside of Mexico, took exception to this...taking out his frustrations on Hector. Now with the Tag Titles within their grasp, the symbols that represent all their hard work and sacrifice, Los Diablos faces the roadblock that Deville would love to be. Taking the stamp of all their hard work from them would make Deville as happy as he could possibly be. Those titles are nothing more than mere trinkets to a man like Deville, simply extra spoils along the path of destroying the faith of yet another competitor. Mere pawns to use in order to screw with Los Diablos even further, and continue to do his best to one up them. While Los Diablos continues to attempt to establish themselves as the premier tag team, not only in PRIME, but the world...trying to prove that tag team wrestling is still important and worthy of being part of...Deville is plotting to destroy that goal. To take the symbols of the division and ruin it even further. To Deville tag team wrestling is nothing more than a secondary care, if he cares about it at all, that he can use to rid PRIME of Los Diablos. No tag titles could mean no tag division...No division means no Los Diablos...No Los Diablos means that Deville will have won. He would once again prove that he is the only 'Devil' to be feared in any federations. A man with no compassion, no care for other human life, and no remorse for the things that happen to others... Or is he really that man? Flash back to a few days before ReVolution 59 The shot opens us to outside the PRIME offices, in the parking lot area, on a clear and beautiful March day. Standing in the lot, with a few PRIME camera men, is Baltasar, dressed in blue jeans...a black Imperium T-Shirt, and his wrestling mask....he smirks, slightly, while trying to hide the annoyance he is feeling from having to cut a promo, at the order of Hin See, in the damn parking lot of the companies main offices. 'You gringos about ready with that damned camera equipment? I am getting a little tired of standing here and wasting my time with you,' Baltasar shot out, annoyance clear on his tone, as the impatience of the crews actions began to over take him. 'Yes sir, Mr. Baltasar, we are just about ready now. Sorry it's taking so long, but we don't usually have to drag all this stuff out into the parking lot, when we have a perfectly good room inside the building,' the man behind one of the cameras began to explain before he was cut off by the more than cranky Red Devil. 'NO KIDDING GRINGO! This is nothing more than another attempt by Hin See and his filthy son to undermine the careers of Los Diablos. He thinks he is going to teach me a lesson with this bullshit, does he essa?' Baltasar asked, clearly with no intent to get an answer. The question was rhetorical anyway and an answer meant nothing more to him than these employees jobs did, 'You know in Mexico this kinda stuff doesn't happen. They know how to treat their stars there and all the employees value their jobs, so they do the best work possible. You God forsaken Americans could learn something from that sorta work ethic.' A smirk crawled over the brown, tan, skin of the self proclaimed Red Devil. Finally the crew began to signal that it was time for him to cut his promo and he let out a sigh that indicated that it was about time they got their stuff together. 'Angelo Deville...The Crimson Angel...the thorn in my side for too long now. You, my dear gringo, have caused me far more grief than you should be allowed. Every time I turn around your off running your mouth about how you are such a victim. How you've been cheated and screwed with by Los Diablos. How your panties were just on so damn tight in the Duel Halo that you couldn't compete properly, and now have to blame your defeat on us. Essa I've got a headache the size of the damned Rio Grande...from having to listen to your constant bitching and whinning like a little girl.' Baltasar pauses for a moment, rubbing his temples for effect, before turning his gaze back to the camera and smiling. Just as he is about to continue the sounds of someone elses voice are heard, off in the parking lot, and it gets Baltasar's attention. He turns and begins to head in the direction of the commotion. 'You gringos follow me. We're going to find out just what the hell is going on around here and put a stop to it.' The crew do as they are ordered and begin to follow Baltasar across the lot as he stalks toward the sound that has gained his attention. A little in front of them he rounds the corner of a building and the crew is forced to speed up to try and keep up. Before they round the corner, however, Baltasar comes back around the corner of the building with a smile on his face and puts his hand up to stop them. 'Listen very close puta madras. I am only going to say this once, and if you screw this up, I'll make your lives so damn miserable that you'll quit and go to work for that shit hole Core Wrestling. You understand me gringos?' The camera men merely nod their heads in understandment, trying not to piss off the Red Devil any further than they already have on this day with their slow work. 'Good. Now you follow me around this corner and I want you to keep that footage recording. I'm about to get some very useful information...very useful information indeed.' The men begin following Baltasar around the corner, their cameras held into perfect position to record anything that is going on within reasonable distance. Rounding the corner we find... End Flashback Oh the wonders of modern technology. The wonderful things you can capture on video these days, to archive, and use for all times. There are days, every now and again, when you just have to be completely thankful for the wonderful good fortune that our dear Lord gives us. PRAISE HOYT! I know, gringos, that your just dying to know what is on that tape. Rest assured you will find out all in due time. It is the type of information that will rock the foundation of a person... Allowing the ruin of the best laid plans.
