The Monster Within [JitC]

16 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/16/07 "No man, for any considerable period, can wear one face to himself and another to the multitude, without finally getting bewildered as to which may be true." -Nathaniel Hawthorne ***October 28, 2007*** "Jack and Jill went up the hill to fetch a pale of water!" "Jack fell down and broke his crown!" "How does the rest of it go?" "I dunno." They were both five, still locked into a magical world of creation and roses. Blues Clues was staunchly exploratory, and they were learning Spanish on Sesame Street. Jackie and Meadow were the son and daughter of a middle class family who had escaped the City of Angels, finding a home in a much simpler place. Here, they could let their children run and play on the rolling hills without fear of pedophiles and gangster rap destroying their youth. They were white, suburbanite to the mocha latte marrow inside their bones. Mom and Pops sat on a blanket, toasting cans of Coke as if it meant something. Their children were running around without a care in the world. They would speak French, the mother thought silently to herself while her husband wanted the pair to become doctors. They had never had to struggle for anything in their lives, and as such had no concept of what adversity was. They wished for diversity at the same time that they wished against socialized health care. God had a place in their home, and piety would give everyone in the family a spiritual identity. Everything else could be fixed with cash. "AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!" It was in unison, the scream ripping the couple from their day dreaming of better days back into the current. They had been Generation Xers who now had morphed into their parents before them. They thought the worst, running to the aid of their children. When they got there, they both gasped as Meadow cried crocodile tears. The odor of rot was intense. ***October 26, 2007*** In the subsequent months after his near death experience, Brandon Youngblood had a rough time dealing with reality. The callousness of what had happened put a strain on him emotionally, causing him to withdraw even further into his own self because that made the most sense. His fingers twitched nervously at random points, and a cold sheet of perspiration accompanied any thoughts of fear. Youngblood hated to feel helpless in ANY situation, but the timing of it made him believe it was a causality. He had begun to change as a human being, almost to the point where he thought himself to have the qualities of a loving and caring entity. But the barrel of a gun fixed squarely towards his skull changed all that, and only served him as far as it was an excuse to get rid of his dojo without much notice. The sun had yet to rise, but when it did, he would greet it with bloodshot eyes. It was one of those nights for him, where every single time he closed his heavy eye lids he would find himself frozen in a slice of his own demise, the brute fear crashing into him at a truck like force through a hazy stare. If there was any way he could describe it to someone (which he wouldn't), it would be as though it was stylized violence through a stained glass window. All the weird colors swimming a whirlpool inside his conscious as he stared up at a street light, the pack of niggers doing their best to contain blackface smiles. In other such instances, he was front row center to his own posthumous celebration. The burial was weird, as when he passed on he had left instructions the he wished to be cremated. In the current climate Brandon found himself, he wasn't sure if the thought itself was morbid or simply birthed from a tragic reality. With a long box of faux wood colored mahogany playing as the centerpiece, his death would be mourned by few because there were so few out there that Brandon would let into his inner circle. There was something about the floral decor that adorned the top of the casket, the mixture of atypical red roses and the like that pained him. He couldn't quite put his finger on it. The casket would change, the setting would as well (from one church he remembered to another one from his childhood). But those red roses, they lingered throughout each and every nightmare he had As the sun rose out far in the corner of the Los Angeles sky, his hands were wrapped around the metallic handle of a gun. His eyes felt dry as his throat, the haze of a seventh consecutive cigarette still present as he lit an eighth. The smoke did not help his eyes, rather it stung them. He had never really owned a weapon, nor was he a connoisseur of fire arms, he just knew that this cannon in his hands was supposed to punch a nickel sized hole through whatever he shot towards. He was not afraid to admit to himself that he wanted to kill them. His wife did not understand, and as such he decided to not force her to worry her pretty little head . He could not gather the words about how he felt his manhood had been raped, the chilled sense that at a moment's notice his life was over. It was redundant, and it was true that had it happened at the end of last year, he more than likely would have welcomed the end with a restrained embrace. She laid tossing and turning in their marriage bed, one that had already been built of thorns, unease resting firmly in her mind. Brandon never did the best job hiding the fact that something was troubling him. He would just be light lipped and like to believe he was able to put on a dog and pony show for an audience of frothing idiots. Lindsay's eye was trained on signs, and ever since she had arrived in Los Angeles to go to university. there was a myriad of little ticks that let her know that Brandon was mentally checked out of this world and into his own. She didn't know about the gun, or the fact that if he had the chance, he would deal out a punishment just to ease his troubled mind. The cops hadn't done anything. Someone would have to. *** "That'll be ten fifty." It came out in a burst, his exhausted mind preparing for yet another day working the cash register at a gas station that was around the Hills. Nobody recognized his face. Why would they? In a place like this, actors were a penny a hundred, fleeting fame figured unkindly to the unremarkable. And any remarkable skills Alastair had dissipated through heavy narcotics and alcohol. He was strung out, stone sober but the cravings for a needle were driving him mad. His hair seemed a greasy mess, his face pale and his skin constantly clammy. He had been repeatedly told not to smoke in the glassed off register area, but he was making barely above minimum wage for less than twenty hours of work a week. His job was replaceable, as was the income he garnered from it. The rest of his rent and legal fees came in the guise of a liquor store job in the bad part of town. That was dangerous, yet he did it anyway. There was no bullet proof glass separating him from the animals there. And he wouldn't want it, given his own psychological make up. But the fact of the matter was, Nagatsuka had finally hit bottom, and even salvation in the form of rehab was not going to save him from falling through the drain and down the pipe towards the septic tank of Hell. He had a rough time making change for whatever reason, and would short people constantly because they never went through and counted until they were in their cars. To most, a nickel wasn't a big deal, but to him it was expounded by the fact that he was being yelled at for having more in his register than the machine said he should. This made his supervisor think that Al was stealing, and that brought piss testers in on an almost weekly basis. Scratching his throat, Alastair dreamed of better days. Of cutting through the sky and crashing upon someone in some opaque maneuver that not even he could describe. He was a child of time, and his had passed him by. After he had gotten out of rehab, he hoped to get back on the Indy circuit. Perhaps go back to wrestling for decent money in Japan. Anything was better than this. But every single promoter that had once plead loyalty to him now were not returning phone calls. No place in California was willing to book him. As it went on, Al realized what had happened. "I see you again...I'm turning you in to the police." And Brandon didn't only mean "I see you again" in the sense that if the two would cross paths in the grocery store or if Al came to him begging for forgiveness. What it meant was that Brandon was going to do whatever he could to distance himself from his former wrestling brother. It left a sour taste in Al's mouth, as it was he who had picked Brandon up out of the gutter in oh five, helping him get back on his feet. Al had heard about Brandon being jumped outside his own wrestling training dojo, and didn't know how to think about it. He had introduced Tayral Brown into that world, only to have Brandon break the gangsta's ankle to the point where the nigger was still walking with a limp. Al had nothing to do with it, and had done his best to stay clean and sober and all that. But the feelings of loss were to the point where they were unbearable. He needed a bone. Something. Anything. He left his spot, peeling off the Shell over shirt and tossing it to the floor. The sun was just rising. *** The phone rang three times before Brandon answered it. And when he did, his ears were greeted by a static wind. The tone was one he wished to forget, but one he know he never could. "Brandon...we need to talk about this. About everything. About the shit that happened between us. I'm sorry man...I..." Brandon was resolute in his silence, the grip on his gun unconsciously growing hard. The skyline was growing red, he thought. In reality, it was his mind clouding his judgment in a heady haze that hindered any sort of real rational thought. All he had was his emotions and his instincts now as he was confronted with a slice of his past that he wished had been buried. "I know you hate me man. But we got to settle this. It ain't fair that you done done this to me man...especially after all the shit we been through. How I saved your life when you needed it." Brandon didn't hear the ending words, just the part about settling 'this'. His heart raced. "I know man...I know. I just wanna talk...I know you're in a bad spot. I know you closed the BloodWorks. Just...I'm gonna be around the old spot man. You wanna pick me up...we can go out to the country and have it out. That's my final offer." "Be there at eight at night." The words surprised Brandon, and before he could take them back, he had gone to flipping his phone shut. He rose up, his brain a mess of tangled weeds. Why there? he thought. Into the country? It all became crystal in Brandon's mind. He knew he needed gloves. ***October 28, 2007*** Pops reconsidered his original thoughts as to whether he wanted Jackie or Meadow to become doctors. What he saw before him was a twisted mess of flesh, with pale skin that looked a hue blue. It was male, what resembled a face planted firmly down into the hard ground. He stole a peek at the face for a moment, seeing flies congregate around the opened mouth of whoever it was that had died. The flies themselves were like dots in the landscape, making this sick pastoral painting all the more cryptic. The smell caused them to shudder, and they wanted to run away. But they couldn't. The flies buzzed around the still pink tongue, and it was absolutely alien to see a human body not move to such contact. The body itself was twisted and contorted, which meant that whoever it was must have died in tremendous pain. When the flies started reaching towards the face, Jackie moved over to get a better look. His mother would later chastise him, but at the moment she was a frozen pillar, her shock at seeing a dead body giving her a gargoyle glare. Jackie realized that the eyes were still open, and a little maggot was wrenching around on the side of the eyeball. There was the hole through the back of of the mouth. For the first time since he was a baby, Jackie threw up. The image of the mangled skull would say with him for the rest of his life. ***October 26, 2007*** It had taken Brandon all day to garner the courage necessary to do what he felt he had to do. His hands had a pair of fine leather gloves, his body adorned with a smoky pinstriped suit. The reason for the dress was simple: he figured it would be the last thing he would ever wear. And for whatever reason, he felt it best to be well suited for when the end would come. In the nightmares, he always wore a gaudy black number with a crimson tie that just looked ugly, for whatever reason, in his head. It smelled of a set up. Al was a Trojan horse set to finish the job. And Brandon could hardly say he was surprised. At home, he acted odd around Lindsay and Melissa, and they knew something was amiss by just how on edge Brandon seemed to be. At the same time, Lindsay was more preoccupied with other issues, stemming from her roommate Aurora Andrews. The two had grown close, as Brandon would know it, but the pair had a huge fight which caused Melissa to crash at the apartment for a few days. He made sure, five times, to tell Melissa that he loved her, but refused to kiss her as he left in the dead of night on 'personal business'. While they were in Los Angeles, Brandon wasn't living in Hollywood, and thought that the script had yet to be written for what would transpire. The drive went by like a breeze, and once he arrived at the vacant lot that once housed his wrestling legacy, he was greeted by Alastair Nagatsuka's chain smoking form. He wasn't dressed for the occasion, and for whatever reason he sat alone on the steps next to the spot. Brandon swallowed hard. It had been the first time since it had happened that he had as much as been a block within the place. He'd made others pack his stuff and lock it into storage. One day, Brandon would put it up for sale, but he was far too afraid, and the very sight of anything having to do with the place brought a sort of queer rush that made his knees weak. He honked his horn once, the lights flashing bright as Alastair made his way over. Jeans and a hoodie with a big pocket that Brandon swore sank a little too far down for his liking. The door opened, and before Youngblood knew it, he was side by side with a man who assuredly wanted to kill him. "So...where you wanna do this?" Al looked over, kind of shocked by the question. He had forgotten about the country statement, and figured if anything, it would go down in the car around here. "Oh...I...where ever man. Where did I say?" "The country. You said the country." "Fine. Let's go to the country." ***October 28, 2007*** "You calling somebody?!" The shrieks from the mother were piercing her husband's calm demeanor, getting to the very inside of him and rousing all sorts of angry feelings. Her voice, once associated with beauty, now had all that gentleness of a brier patch. "I'M CALLING! I'M CALLING!" "YOU DON'T HAVE TO YELL AT ME!" The pair had corralled their children as best as they could, threatening to take their toys away if they didn't stay away from the body. They shifted uneasily, the very image of death's personification having shook them down to their very roots. "Jackie..." "Yeah?" "What...what was that?" "A monster." Tears started to well up in his eyes again, his face flushed bright red. The wrinkles that accompanied a child's cry was not a pretty sight because of the mask like contortions their flesh seemed capable of. "A monster had to do that." ***October 26, 2007*** The drive was uneasy the whole away. Brandon had traveled down narrow dirt roads, curling around and around and around until he found a new one which took the pair even further into seclusion. Dust kicked up underneath the treads of the tires as they spun, tossing rocks up at such a velocity where the pings of stone on metal were audible within the vehicle itself. Brandon took a violent turn off the road after seeing grassy hills to the side. The night sky made things all the more difficult, but the lights from the car allowed him to recognize that this place was okay for what he had in mind. Putting the car in park, Brandon turned off the ignition and looked over towards Alastair. "Get the fuck out." Al looked over, his brow arched. The drive had brought about a myriad of exclamation points in his head, but for whatever reason, he chose not to speak up. "Huh?" "Get the fuck out!" Spit carried with the words, drenching them with a hot hatred that hurdled into Nagatsuka's ears and brought with them a sick bed of disastrous connotations. Alastair followed his command, pushing through the door and making his way out to the field on uneven footing. "Why we out here Brand? I don't get it?" "Don't play coy with my...you stupid fuck!" Brandon followed with him, staring a hole into Al's head. "You gonna sag that heat you packin' some more, eh? EH?! How fuckin' dumb you think I am Al? Huh? HUH?!" Alastair was caught off guard. "What the fuck are you talking about Brandon?!" "You brought them fuckin' niggers into my fuckin' world. And what happens? WHAT HAPPENS?! I should be dead right now Al. I SHOULD BE DEAD!" "How is this on me?!" Brandon had reached his gloved hand to the back of his waist, ready to draw. Ready to end it all and put to bed the nightmares and the insecurities. He drew the gun, pointing it towards Alastair's chest. Nagatsuka wished it had been some sort of mirage. But it wasn't. The world melted away, and all that was left was this growing pit in the bottom of his stomach, draining color and reason from him. "That's...that's murder Brandon." "No Al." His face turned to a sneer, his grip growing ever steady on the handgun. "It's street justice. I took out that nigger trash, and they come and jump me. Are you gonna tell me that they didn't know when I was gonna leave...eh? Eh?! THEY WAITED FOR ME AL! THEY WAITED FOR ME!" "I'm sorry Brandon..." Alastair was doing the best he could not to simply break down. His nervousness caused him to grab at his own neck, scratching and clawing at it with a frantic pitch. "I'm fucking' sorry man...I didn't do nothin'." "BULLSHIT!" Brandon was incensed now, his steps growing ever closer. Silence permeated their stances. Alastair fell to the Earth's floor, stumbling and flopping around as if he was a fish out of water. Brandon motioned for Alastair to get up once, but he shied away. The butt of the gun collided with his cheek, forcing whimpers and cries to come from the mouth of the man who had helped Brandon up off his own feet countless times. Brandon grabbed him by the scruff of the neck, trying to bring him up to a stand. Instead, it only worked to cause Al to scamper a few feet away. He was frozen solid, unsure of what he should do. Brandon pulled away from the scene long enough to go to the back of his car, popping the trunk. He shuffled around in the messy back end of his automobile, grabbing the handle of a shovel he had put in just hours earlier. He grabbed the base of the spade and slammed the trunk shut. Brandon stalked his way back to the growing crime scene, tossing the shovel in front of the prone Alastair Nagatsuka. "So...are you going to dig my grave too?" The question was preposterous. Al looked over at the ground, trying to see if he could garner a response of some sort for Brandon. Youngblood just stood there before him, the space between them stagnant. "Do I look that inhuman to you?" For whatever reason, the words had power. Alastair looked up towards him, their glares meeting in form of territorial combat. The man before each of them was completely foreign, completely alien to the point where it made them both forget their past camaraderie, only remembering the now. "If you don't stand up, I'm going to beat you to death with the fuckin' shovel. Now GET! UP!" "Do it Brandon. FUCKING DO IT! JUST KNOW I DIDN'T HAVE ANYTHING TO DO WITH THIS!" "THE FUCK YOU DIDN'T!" "FINE!" Alastair stood up, his lips quivering. His entire life flashed before his eyes, showing him all the failures in a steady fast forward. His life had peaked before he had ever reached adulthood, and now he was just a mess of human waste. He hated himself, hated what he had become. A junkie who had sold his friends down the river. It wasn't that he had done anything to bring about Brandon's assault directly, but the events that transpired had been a direct causality of what he had done. Tayral Brown, heroin, all of it. "I don't even fucking care anymore. Believe what you wanna believe!" "I KNOW IT WAS YOU!" Brandon was growing uneasy with all this. He couldn't bring himself to pull that trigger, nor did he even know if he wanted to at this point because there was no turning back from it. Tears of fear, of desolation, streaked down his cheeks. "I LOVED YOU! HOW COULD YOU DO THIS TO ME?!" "PULL THE FUCKING TRIGGER BRANDON!" "NO...GODDAMMIT...no..." he pulled away, the gun dropping to his side. Alastair had no intention of letting the gun go to waste. Ripping it free from Brandon's hand, Alastair began running away, running off up the hills. Brandon, shocked, chased after him for whatever reason. He didn't know why. The thought that Alastair would shoot him did not resonate in his mind until it was too late. BAM! Alastair fell to the ground, tumbling forward down the hill until what remained of his face planted into the ground in front of him. Brandon did not know what happened, even when he saw the body with half a head. Tissue and bone were like broken puzzle pieces that could never be put back together. Youngblood fell silent, unable to put the suicide into any sort of realistic context. After all this time, this is how it would end. He pulled back, trying not to become this ghastly monster, but it didn't even matter. And another black rose bloomed in Hell tonight.
