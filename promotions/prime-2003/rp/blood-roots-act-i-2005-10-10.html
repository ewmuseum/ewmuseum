Blood Roots: Act I

10 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.10.05 ['Blood Roots: Act I'] The Man is losing his touch... He can't win a match to save his life... He brings disgrace to the Intense Championship... Mr. Olsig, all of this alchohol you are consuming is damaging your life span... Introducing First...Vangelus Olsig... You'll never make it in this business, boy... Your country needs you... Vangelus Olsig has been eliminated... Why won't you grow up... When will he learn... I hate you... *'Argh!'* The cold glass shattered in the palm of my sweaty hands. Blood cut through pale skin, seeping and staining the carpet below. Though present blood, I felt no pain. Over the years I had become numb to such small threats. But while the blood crept through my open wound, I felt a much bigger threat. The one pounding my head. A night filled with erotic favors, drugs, and extosy brought forth a morning filled with the common headache and the feeling of naseua. But with it came blood shot memories. Memories and glimpses of the past few weeks. Memories of droughts inside of the squared circle, a time in my life in which victory seem so far out of reach. Glimpses of common life drama, from being informed that my drug issue was damaging my life span, to being given the ever so infamous 'I hate you' speech by random woman number 2 who's name I care not to remember. I snagged a stained T-Shirt from the edge of the Sofa's arm in which I sat on, using it to whipe the blood that stained my sweaty palm. Times in my life in which dying seemed like the best option of relief. But as usual, when trouble presented itself, something or someone always came along to make it that much worse. From being pushed to the edge of suicide to taking up a life of sheer violence, I had seen it all...but none more blatantly unsolvable than this. What was supposed to be a reward in claiming the Intense Champion has only brought forth night after night of bruises to accomodate my already scarred life. Along with that came the pressure of being a true champion and living up to the expectation set by holders prior. The Olsig's Ladder challenge was my key to escaping it all...constructed in hopes that someone would relieve my tarnished soul of one anchor. The substances contained in the alchohol that once graced my glass cup, pre-shattering, now begin to creep into my exposed wounds, creating a burning sting unlike any other I'd felt before. I snagged the blood soaked T-Shirt and wrapped it around the palm of my hand, in hopes that it would soothe some of the pain. In a sense, the burning was actually a relief...as was anything that countered this torturing headache and nauseating sensation. I pressed the T-Shirt deeper into my palm with my vacant hand, attempting to numb some of the pain temporarily. It worked, but as stated before...only temporarily. It wasn't so much the alchohol and extosy from the night before that caused such a dramatic headache, but rather the voices that were pounding through. Voices of hate, voices of doubt, voices of crtisiscm, regret, sorrow, madness...like the laugh a sinister clown, it throbbed making me want to scream out into the abyss, but I reconsidered in knowing that screaming would only welcome even more pain. I heard a knock at the front door, but my will to answer it plunged. Suddenly, a common knock transformed into an urgent banging, making me wonder if it was as urgent as the person on the other side of the door was making it sound. Nonetheless, I pushed myself off of the couch and stumbled weairly to the door. I unwrapped the T-Shirt from my palm and tossed it unto the ground, figuring that the person at the door couldn't be anyone important in whom I would need to clean up for. Though mild pain, I grabbed the door knob and yanked open the door...but as soon as I did, a figure fail dead into my arms. I caught what, at first glance, appeared to be an elderly man in my arms and dragged him fully into the home, laying him gently on the solid cold ground below. I looked into his eyes...they were bloodshot red, and halfway closed. He was panting deeply, and his heart acted as if it was attempting to break out of his chest. I squinched, trying to recognize the man through his battered state. With a couple of glances, and whatever my tortured memory would allow, I finally labled the man as Bishop Walter Abraham, the authorized priest of Tahj, Columbia. He was my main inspiration through tough times serving as the Tyrant of the Tahj Empire in which I inherited from my father's death, before ultimately being accused of blasphemy and exiled from the territory. Anyone who knows the story of Vangelus Olsig, knows of these times...And they also know of my deep desire to erase them from my life forever. But like always, something presented itself which kept me from doing such... God only knew what that something would be this time. 'B-Bishop Abraham...W-What happened?' I asked, terrified at the condition of the man who's only purpose in Tahj was to relieve the tortured souls of those citizens through spiritual comfort. He was the kindest man you'd ever meet, and the only reason that kept me from utter meltdown while serving my father's reign. If it wasn't for him, my decension from the throne would have occured much earlier rather than later...I told him a long time that I'd do anything for him. I meant it. 'Ma-Master Olsig. Y-You must h-help us.' He stated, struggling to get the words out of his system. I held his cold sweaty hand in the palm of my equally sweaty one. I could feel him shaking in terror, and his breath smelled of horror. He was sweating as if he'd just taken part in a marathon, and his bloodshot eyes made it difficult for me to even bear looking at the wretched soul of this poor man. I didn't want to question him too much, because by the looks of things, he was on the verge of death and even dialogue was hard for him to produce at this point. ...But I had to know. 'Bishop...w-what's going on? Is It...Is it something to do with Tahj? Who did this to you?' I stated, distraught at the fact that I had to sit here watching a man die right in my presence, and knowing that there was nothing I could do about it. 'Sir...A demon has...taken over Eastern Tahj. H-he is not of this world. H-he has no heart...n-no regard for the common man. Thousands are dying, sickness...torture...y-you're the only one that can help us. Asc-ascend the throne, Sir. Please...f-for the peoples sake.' He stated in the most convicing manner that any man in his condition could. I had a hard time understanding exactly what he was speaking of, but through headache and nausea alike I attempted to decipher it. He spoke of a demon...but the only person I could figure him speaking of was my step brother, King Cerasani Olsig. The story of Cerasani is rather simple. He's the child of my father and the mistress in whom he cheated on my mother with. Since he was also a son of my father, he fell in the inheritence category right after me. When I was exiled, he automatically ascended the throne. Why, was the question though. Why would he bring such torture upon the people of Tahj? 'Bishop...What am I supposed to do?' I asked, hoping that the man before me would not cease to exist before I could gather some more information out of his frail soul. I could see his eyelids growing heavy from the wake of death, and his body resembling the feel of an ice cube. His lips parted slowly and he spoke what would be his last words before ultimately ceasing... '...D-do...what...you must.' He stated, his breath smelling of sheer death. After that was spoken, His eyelids shut eternally and his body in my arms suddenly turned into dead weight. Right there in my presence...in my arms, a man who was the inspiration of my life had ceased. The thought alone brought forth terror in my heart, but to actually witness it brought forth a sense of torture in which could not be described. His dying wish was for me to save the people of Tahj... I had to grant him that.... Somehow. A emergency plane flight brought me here to my home of Tahj. From the moment I stepped foot unto the land in which I once ruled, I knew something was wrong. The smell in the air was one in which I would never forget. It was a smell that made my gradually healing naseau seem like a bed of roses. The scent of death mixed in with the scent of sickness all to accumulate in one enormous bulk of suffering. It was mid-day and there was a silence in the air that sent chills up my spine. Eastern Tahj was a city of hard labor...so the fact that no one was working finalised the ideal that something in this town was wrong. It had been a long time since I had to acquire the use of my .22 glock, but I made sure to clutch it tightly as I crept over the dry land of a city in which I once called my own. As I glanced over everything, I saw the same scenario I had witnessed upon leaving this place. Dry blood spilled out across the desert-like lands. Random bodies, scrolled across the dirt, unrecognizable by known. And the eerie silence did little to calm the already disasterous wasteland. I wanted to vomit courtesy of the stench in the air, but I held my ground. Suddenly I heard something behind me. Quickly, I wrapped my palm around the .22 glock located in my right pocket and made a swift turn around to meet the predator face to face, only it wasn't exactly as I expected. I felt a tug at my pants leg and glanced down to witness a man who's shirt had been torn from his torso revealing deep scars and dry blood that covered his body like paint. I looked into his eyes which were filled with tears that fell into his bloody mouth. He parted his lips, shaking ferociously as he reached up in a vain attempt to plead for my assistance. The sight alone made me want to break down in tears, but I knew I had to hold my ground. Screaming was a ever-so present option, but I held my ground for the sake of the dying man before me. He extended his trembling hand as far he could as he lie there on the soil. '...H-Help M-Me...' He stated, as if his voice had already died out. In disbelief, I extended my hand welcoming him the opportunity to grasp it and pull himself up. He attempted to do just that, but as soon as the tip of his hand touched mine, the trembling stopped. The deep breathing ceased. I was too late. This man had died right here before my very eyes. I raised my hand and placed it over my throbbing head before sending a fierce fist in a nearby dying tree, screaming out into the abyss... 'DAMNIT!' I screamed out. This couldn't be happening. This city...my city...had been plagued with mass holocaust. Someone...no...something was terrorizing this place. Something that was above mankind. No common man could do this alone. There was a greater being somewhere among the stars whom were killing these people. A greater being who's wrath had taken its toll on innocent people whom's only purpose in life was to serve their master faithfully. I had to get to the bottom of this, but deep down I only feared what was at the bottom waiting for me. I rapidly stormed through the land, my destination set on the Drakhoan Palace...the kingdom in which I once called home. The Drakhoan Palace is the place in which the Tyrant of Eastern Tahj resides, similar to the white house of today's modern government. I had to know just exactly was going on, and no other source was more reliable than that of Cerasini Olsig. Hate had kept us apart for many years, but this wasn't about my inner feelings towards him. This was about the people whom, even though betrayed by, I stilled cared for. In me remained that inner justice to defend the helpless and innocent of this world...and no longer could I push that inner justice aside with simple words of comfort. For the sake of Bishop Abraham...for the sake of Eastern Tahj...for the sake of my damned father...I had to do something, and to my best ability I was going to make sure that something was right. Finally I reached the Drakhoan Palace, stepping over ceased body guards and into the open door of the drakhoan palace. I heard absolutely nothing, which was as menacing a sound as the screams of terror in which I had witnessed prior. I called out into the nothing, but just as nothing presented itself, nothing responded. Being in this place once more brought back deep memories. Most were memories of terror, but some brought forth joy. Some were filled with good times...but they were scarce and irrelevant at the present time. Slowly, I crept through the palace, hand steadily placed on the glock in my pocket just in case any danger may present itself. Though mild, the feeling of naseua and the throbbing headache was still present and did little to advance my will of continueing on this death journey, but as I thought over the bloodshed and the sheer violence, something inside of me just wouldn't let me turn around. I slowly ascended up the steps ready to enter the master bedroom of Cerasani Olsig in which I prayed to God was still alive for me to gather some sort of information out of. Finally I reached the top of the massive amount of steps and crept to the door of the master bedroom. The door was closed...I didn't know whether to take that as a good sign or a bad one. I pressed my ear up against the door in an attempt to make sure that there was no violent action of any sorts occuring on the opposite side. After a quick assurance, I pushed the door open and stepped inside. Even more present than before was the smell of death and torture. I glanced down at the cold ground below to see the body of Mariah, the house maid laid out...although no blood seemed to be seeping out of her body. She must have been one of the few who ceased due to illness instead of torture. I glanced over at the opposite end of the ground to witness Bayo, the palace chef laid out with a butcher knife stuck dead in his heart. The sight of the blood that caressed the blade was sickening, so I quickly shifted my head...but after doing so I quickly regretted my decision. Simply because, as I shifted my head towards the other side of the room, I saw Cerasani, my step brother, with his head hanging out of the window. He seemed not to notice my presence, but the realization that he was still alive brought me joy...only to later bring me discomfort. I made my way over to the window in which his head hung out of and tapped him on the shoulder. Upon doing so, he quickly yanked himself around in a defensive stance fearing that I may be one of the attackers. As he turned around, I stated deep into his cloudy eyes which seemed to be caving in. His face was battered with scars and so was his body, but the one thing that made me the most sick was the sight of blood covering his mouth. He was spitting out blood...so much that it led me to believe that he was on the verge of death from such a massive amount of blood loss. Nonetheless, he stated into my peering eyes with just as much bewilderment as I towards him. His lips slowly parted to speak... '...B-Brother? Is that...is that you?' He asked, trembling ferociously from the bulk of blood he had loss. It had made him so weak that he could barely support his own weight, but he was still alive and thats all that mattered. Though I held an inner hate for the man, I couldn't bare to see my own flesh and blood in such a state. He fell foward as I caught him in my arms and rubbed my hand over his forhead in an attempt to comfort him. 'Yes, Cerasani...its me. Tell me what happened here.' I stated, hoping to garnish some sort of information out of him before he reached what seemed like an inevitable death. Suddenly he pushed me away and fell to the ground, unable to support his own weight. Trembling, he pushed himself upwards and pointed a finger towards me as if accusing me of a crime. '...Y-You did this...' He stated, taking me by suprise. I was shocked to find out that my own flesh and blood accussed my of such a massive holocaust. How could he turly believe deep down in his frailed heart that I was the cause of all this destruction. Nonetheless, he continued on. '...H-Hell awaits y-you...br-brother. Our bl-blood...is on your h-ha...' Before he could even finish the statement, he fell face first unto the ground below. I leaped over to grab him, shaking him in a vain attempt to wake him up. I placed my palm on his heart and felt nothing...that feeling alone made me scream out into the bloody terror that had ceased my country. Tears gathered inside of my eyes. I had promised myself that I would keep my cool, but the scent...the blood...the scenery just wouldn't cooperate. I held just as much hate in my heart for my brother as I did for my father...he was the one who brainwashed my people into exiling me, but to sit there and watch him on the verge of death accuse me was something that I could no longer bear. I dropped his dead weighted body unto the cold ground below and buried my face in the palm of my hands as a puddle of tears formed inside of my palms. Words could not describe the great loss I felt...and even though crying was a sign of cowardness, or weakness, my present state of mind...my present state of torture just wouldn't push forth the will to fight back the tears. I was a man who had grown weak...yes...but in order to grow stronger I had to leave the tears of sorrow in their proper place. Vangelus, you are our new leader... I present to you...Lord Olsig! What would your father think... I will conquer the throne...brother... EXILE! I'm sorry to say this Sir...but you're banished from Tahj... As different voices of the past plagued my mind, I finally lifted my face out of the palm of my hands. Upon doing so, I witness something shiny on the ground before me. It had not been there before or else I would have noticed. I wiped the tears from my eyes in order to view the object more clearly. I picked it up, glancing at it strangely. It was some sort of medallion...it looked so familiar, but yet with my mind clogged with so much pain and torture I could not properly place it. A medallion, crafted in the form of a dragon. Suddenly, as I held the medallion in my hand, I heard a voice speak out... Vengeance will be crafted...when you least expect it. '...No.' I stated as things suddenly began to become more clear. I now knew the reasoning behind this...I knew whom had constructed this plan...and the thought alone made me cringe in anger, but also in fear. Anger boiled so to the point where I began to shake and crushed the medallion in the palm of my hand. Watching as the rubble of the medallion sprinkled the cold ground below, I slowly pushed myself up to my feet. Things were so blurry, but suddenly they have become so clear. One man had been the cause of this Holocaust... It was time to to end this for good. Having to step into this place once again brought forth a sense of bad memories. It was a place in which I knew all too well, and the place in which I sought to put an end to the torture that had presented my life for so long. I pushed the door open and stepped inside of the dark place. I could hear the humming of rituals as the only source of candle light came from that of lit candles that illuminated the long dark hallway. It had been so long since I had to grace this place, but yet that same eerie feeling overcame me still. I glanced at the walls, observing crypted letters which only held the meaning of death, blood, and sacrafice. I heard the screams of terror, even from the hallway alone. The smell in this place was of a erotic scent. It was not the same scent which was present in Tahj...the scent of death, no. It was a scent of erotic sin. A scent that was filled with evil and which made my heart clamp in fear of the unknown. Finally I reached a tomb door with more encrypted symbols. This time, I knew what the symbols stood for...and the sheer thought of it made me shiver in fear. Taking a deep breath, I pushed open the tomb to reveal a dark room. Aboslutely no light...just pure darkness. In the darkness, I could still hear the soft humming of rituals which seemed to be sung by death angels. I gently closed the tomb behind me and made my way down the dark pathway. Though no light graced this place, there was still no way you could get lost. It was a straight hallway with the most narrow of hallways. Not to mention that it was exhausting to walk across because of the distance, but I knew that this was the only way to get my life back in order. Finally I reached the end of the hallway where a seat made of the finest stone sat on an elevated platform surrounded by Candle which illuminated it. The back of the seat was turned towards me, therefore I could not see the face of hte man whom sat in it...but I knew all too well whom was in that seat. I hesitated to speak, but the night was growing old and I knew there was little time to be wasted. I couldn't stand living this way anymore, and I had to get it over with one way or another. '...I have come to beg your forgiveness. You promised me that you would grant my vengeance when I least expected it...and you have delivered. My in-ring life has been in a drought as of late. I can't even defeat newcomers such as Jonothan Revolte anymore. My personal life has been a wreck. The doctors have informed me that in a years time I could be dead...The one woman I ever cared for was stripped from me...and my country...my dear hometown has been torn apart by a mass holocaust in which I know was only of your doing. My life is a wreck...I can't continue to live this way. Never in my life have I wanted death so badly...but I do. Only you can wash away this torture. Please...forgive me. I have a big title defense this week...I need my life back. Relieve me of this hell!' I lowered my head as the being sitting in that chair absolutely stated nothing. I knew that my presence in this place was unwelcome and also I knew of the high threat that I held in being here. But I stood steadfast, waiting for the answer from the man who would one day seal my fate if I continued to live my life under his reign of terror. Suddenly I heard the tapping of his palms against the arm of his chair, and I found joy in knowing that he was going to respond. 'What you did to me was uncalled for...am I right?' '...Y-Yes, I kn-..' 'What you did to me was unwelcomed and blatantly offensive.' '...Yo're right, bu-...' 'YOU BETRAYED ME, AND NOW YOU WANT FORGIVENESS!' '...Y-Y-Yes...' '...Well Vangelus. Forgiveness is not so easy to come by. You can't just grace my presence and ask for forgiveness and expect it to be handed to you on a silver platter. You know me better than that, Vangelus. You know what you must do, Vangelus. I'm not asking for your soul in the ring...but outside of the ring, you belong to me. Only through this will you recieve your life back and be relieved of this hell. Sell me your soul Vangelus...only then will you be relinquished from my wrath.' I took a deep breath and ran my hand through my hair in depression. Never had the term selling your soul been more meaningful, but as so I was doing just that. It was the only way to be relinquished, and I knew I had to do it. I began to think of what Bishop Abraham told me before he graced his death bed...Do what you must. And now...this was what I had to do. '...Yes...Sir Nosferatu.' To Be Continued.
