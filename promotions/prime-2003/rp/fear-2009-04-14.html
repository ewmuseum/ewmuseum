Fear

14 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14th April 2009 “Do you think he's ready for this? This isn't the bush leagues any more. If he was to mess up now it could blow the entire operation out of the water.” The voice was cold and filled with worry, as though the thought had rounded his head countless times, but only just been allowed to breathe life. A man middle-aged no more than fifty years old was sat with a face which more than matched the tone of his voice. A furrowed brow was the only notable feature in an otherwise plain face. A simple tailored black suit was his only distinguishing feature, which in the same notion would allow him to blend into almost any situation he chose. “You keep giving me the stuff and we'll have to see won't we? If nothing else we will know if we can slip under the biggest radar of them all.” A soft female voice, totally calm and in control. As though she didn't have a worry in the world. Her body language would belie that though. Her eyes were darting from side to side as she fought all her urges to bite her immaculately manicured nails. The woman was Claire Saunders. A woman who in the past had been known as someone who didn't take any shit. She was the best at what she did, taking control of situations and getting things done quickly and efficiently. She despised dilly dallying and going around the houses but was a firm believer in the fact that if you wanted something done, then you went to someone like her. “How do you mean?” The tailored suited man's voice was suddenly drawn with intrigue, as though there was a factor in the equation that he had not considered originally. “Well lets put it this way. PRIME is the big time do you really think they would want the press of steroid abuse within their ranks? Of course not there will be tests on a regular basis. This isn't performing in school gyms any more. If your product is as good as you say, now is the time we will find out.” The harshness of her voice did not reflect her thoughts at this particular moment in time. For a start she was terrified. It hadn't always been like this. In the past she prided herself on being a bitch, believing the term to be an endearment as opposed to insult. She read it as something which showed she had power and anyone that didn't like it was simply jealous. Of course things changed as they inevitably do. So when she was given this latest assignment she felt as though her world had been turned upside down and she had lost herself, or at least the mask that she portrayed to the world. “We will be in touch soon for an update. This is a crucial time Claire, I trust that you won't mess this up. The effects are starting to be well documented and my superiors are happy with the results. Lets not disappoint them now.” And with that the black suited man rose to his feet and left Claire sat silently, much in the same way he always did. In the same way that he had done years ago when he first approached her. Considering she had worked for him for sometime now, she knew very little about her employers, other than not to ask questions. If that golden rule was adhered to then the money continued to flow. The dirty dirty money. The company had approached her due to her business acumen, her ability to get close to people and manipulate them. Yet as time had passed by she found that she may not have been as savvy as first thought. She was a big fish in a small pond, as soon as was dumped into the ocean the enormity of the situation had dawned on her. She was scared and trapped. Yet if she showed even the slightest inch of weakness, they would pounce and destroy her. The company had showed in the past that they were entirely heartless and that people were expendable. The trials had not been going on long and as expected there were a few side effects. Most notably Chris having a heart attack. In all her time with the company she had bit her tongue. Held the moral high ground, yet when she saw Meat have the heart attack in front of her, she had panicked. Not once since its inception had anyone been hurt, this was a step too far and she had been to see her bosses, the results not only did not sit well with her. “He nearly died, how can you sit there and still think that this is going to work? We are not gods!” Her eyes were filling with tears as she spoke causing her emerald eyes to sparkle. She was physically shaking. Scared and frightened at what she had witnessed. Ok it was possibly a shade over dramatic, but it was true, they were playing with peoples lives and existences it was just wrong. However it was nowhere near as wrong as the answer that was just about to be delivered “No, we simply create them.”  The truth of the matter was that the man who spoke the words fundamentally believed every single thing he was saying. There were no delusions of grandeur, he had been caught up in his own web of ideas to the extent where the truth was no longer applicable. Only his own ideas were fact and any evidence to the contrary would be ignored. A man with that kind of mind, was the most dangerous thing imaginable. “If he dies....” “Then we will find another.” She was frightened and there was nothing that she could do about it. ~@~@~@~@~@~ The mobile phone had been ringing for sometime now. He had stared at it as it vibrated repeatedly on the oak surface of his bed side table and chosen to ignore it. It wasn't that he didn't want to speak to the person on the other end, far from it. He loved her with all his heart and there in lay the problem. It continued to ring for a few more seconds before stopping still and lifeless. The screen read 19 missed calls. As silence descended on the room once more Chris allowed himself to sigh quietly to the world in general, taking in his surroundings he could have been anywhere in the world. Hotel rooms were all the same to him now, as soon as you shut the door behind you you became another number, another slab of meat. Resting his head gently on the pillow he turned to look out of the window, the no expense spared view of the car park greeted him as he scanned across the view before him. His eyes fell upon a young couple, couldn't have been more than twenty years old, the perfect picture of loves young dream. A single solitary tear fell from his eye and landed on the pillow next to him, quickly absorbed into the fabric. “ I know Chris. But I miss you. When are we going to be able to spend some time together?” The voice belonged to Kelly. Chris' fiancée one true love and the woman who was carrying his child. They had been childhood sweethearts and had gone to the same college. The child was an accident, but that had not stopped the two of them preparing for the future. The love that was apparent for the unborn child bloomed like a sunflower in spring and everyone close to the pair commented on how they both radiated happiness. “We have the rest of our lives together Kelly, me you and the little one. Now though I need to hit the showers and head to work.” “Hey, it's not going to be like this forever. We'll both graduate, we'll get good jobs and we will be happy. We just need to take the hit right now. I love you ok.” “I love you too Chris, I hope you know that.” And she did love him, so very much but she also missed him. As it stood at the moment they barely spent any time together, he worked every hour that god sent to save up for their unborn child and even that wasn't enough. When you counted his college wrestling and college work on top of that, there simply weren't enough hours in the day. It wasn't that she felt abandoned. She just wanted her man back, at least for a little while. Chris kissed Kelly tenderly before smiling and dashing off to the showers, she watched him until he was out of sight before allowing herself to slump back down to a seated position. She had barely taken a breath before a figure looming over her had blocked out her light. “I'm sorry to bother you, but I couldn't help but overhear your conversation there. It can't be easy, a couple at college with a baby in the way, especially with all the costs and the time. I was wondering if I could help in anyway.” Kelly squinted at the man but he had not discernible features, he was all intensive purposes, normal or whatever classed for normal these days. A man in a tailored black suit. “Sure like someone is going to wave a magic wand and make everything all better.” “Not quite a magic wand. You look thirsty. Lets talk over a coffee....” Things had been so much simpler then before that day and before his life had changed. Sat alone with his thoughts was the worst time. It gave him that open door to the world that he had left behind, the happiness and family he had turned his back on and for what a simple promise of an easy life? He didn't know what he was any more other than a corporate puppet being played on all sides. The one thing driving him forwards was knowing that he was in control, that he was doing this for all the right reasons. Yet as every day passed, the reasons became blurred the chemicals coursing through his veins and muscles not only numbed the pain, but also the mind. Why didn't he pick up the phone when Kelly called him? Why was he unable to just tell her how much he loved her and missed her? Why couldn't he just tell them to go to hell and get his life back in order? Questions that surged through his mind whenever there wasn't the sweet background noise of life around. They were questions that he had been asking more and more, with every missed call the answer became clearer. As every second of vibration began to cut through his body like a knife, the deep sensation of burning pain that encompassed his chest. He resented her. He resented the fact that she lived a normal life. He resented the fact that it was her that forced him down this route. It was her that forced him to willingly poison his body. To make it so that the sensation of pain was something that came and went like a summers breeze. It was one of the side effects of the drugs that he willingly allowed himself to be violated with. When he was in the ring the closest he came to pain was a slight tingling sensation, it was the days after when the physical pain came, coupled with the mental anguish of the torture that was his life. Another sigh and empty hollow sigh, coupled with more tears, there was no noise coming from Chris just the free flow of tears as they began to make his pillow sodden. When he had been approached initially it was to better his families life, sure he had been given dossiers on what to expect but it takes a special sort of man to allow himself to be drugged freely. To know that the woman that was sat opposite you, who accompanied you to the ring was happily pumping you full of shit, but lacking the evidence to bring her down. So day after day he suffered in silence, resenting the woman who pretended to be looking out for his best interests. The woman he had pretended to be in love with. It was probably karma, he had cheated on the woman he loved in the name of a job. Had build an entire new life away from her, for all intensive purposes abandoned his wife and child and just sent them the pay check every month, as though that made everything alright in the end. His mind often wondered to what his child was doing, if he would grow up knowing his father at all. If he returned home would his son be scared of the strange man baring gifts. It was an all too real fear. All he had to do was blow everything out in the water, he could return to his way of life, back to his family before it was too late. If he just ended the game he would be able to take pride in his job, take pride in his position in PRIME. But why was he there? Was this all part of the game as well? A game which he was quickly losing. “Why are you smiling at me like that?” Asked Claire, almost too suspiciously as she handed Chris his meal. “Sorry I didn't even realise I was smiling, just nice to be cooked a meal once in a while, you know this living on the road life style doesn't really agree with me.” Chris said as he looked down at the steaming roast in front of him, this was it he knew it, he would finish this meal and then leave himself in the hands of fate. “Eat up, we don't want it getting cold now do we, anyway I figured with the biggest match of your career you could do with a proper meal in you.” Claire spoke to Chris, but her eyes not once met his and remained transfixed on the meal that lay before him, almost willing him to eat it and have done with it. Chris rammed a large portion of roast beef into his mouth a chewed vigorously, the gravy and meat juice trickling down his throat with ease, it tasted delicious and his previous statement was not a lie at all, he had missed a good home meal. He waited for a moment as Claire watched on a little too expectantly, as though she was waiting for something to happen. In Chris' head he too was waiting for something to happen, but he wasn't sure what. But there was nothing, not even the slightest bit of gas. “How is it?” Claire asked “Delicious” Chris replied. Both said nothing more, neither wanting to play their cards. Yet at the same time as the two of them sat across from each other at dinner, their eyes often caught each other, before swiftly looking away. Who knew what? Who was playing the games? With every glance further questions were raised but remained in silence, for now at least. Deep inside Chris, chemicals were already beginning to flood his blood stream as it flowed freely around his body, nothing would happen initially, but soon it would kick in. Liquid desire to succeed, pure strength and aggression would team at every sinew of his body, make him push himself to his very physical limits, until there was nothing left but a shell, a machine. Someone so focused on the goal at hand that anything would be deemed as expendable to get there. Or anyone The phone rang again, the vibrations sawing through every sinew of his body. The tears began to flow more freely as he buried his head deep within the pillow, trying to drown out the noise before letting out an anguished scream. He was frightened and there was nothing he could do about it. ~@~@~@~@~ Half way across the country, a woman sat alone crying desperately clutching at the phone in her hand. Begging praying for it to be answered, for days she had been trying to contact him to no avail. She needed him now more than ever. She needed her husband to comfort her, to tell her everything was going to be ok. She needed to be held in his strong arms. She needed to be told that everything was ok. She needed to be lied to because as she strained and lifted her head. Her eyes were met with an empty children's room, a few toys scattered around as though the room had not been touched, kept as a shrine almost. She needed to be told why her son had died. Because she was frightened and there was nothing that anyone could do about it.
