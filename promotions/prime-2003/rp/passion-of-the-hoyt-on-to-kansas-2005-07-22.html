Passion of the Hoyt: “On To Kansas”

22 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/22/05 Narrator: Ahhh Buckaroo’s its scamp Williams time once more. This time the story isn’t about me and chasing women, although this tale is about a woman or I should say her wedding ring. I know you’re probably trying to figure out what the hell old Duke is doing as the narrator for Hoyt’s world title promo. To be honest I was wondering that myself when I was asked to do it. But I got to drinking and I got to thinking that sometimes a story is just a good story regardless of your personal feelings toward the characters. So here I am and here we go. Before I get into the “Passion of the Hoyt” let me tell you a little bit about war. Around 1858 my great country, the United States of America had massive tensions between the states. I guess we really weren’t the United States quite yet. You see the North wanted to free slaves and have an open trade market while the south whose main trade was cotton needed the slaves to keep their economy viable. A war was on the horizon and everybody knew it. Territory was key, as land meant soldiers and supplies. So before the war a propaganda campaign began to snag up the last remaining states. Kansas was one of these unclaimed states and both the civil and union army’s wanted Kansas on their side. So leaders from both sides sent crews to Kansas under the spoken command of “On To Kansas”. It became the fight before the war to claim the upper hand before the actual warfare began. In wrestling there are plenty of fights before the war some are physical, others are psychological, the rest are just games. If you can get into your opponent’s head you gain land. You gain an edge that keeps your enemy awake at night when you’re resting soundly. That is what my son did in this tale of “On To Kansas” where he claims the upper hand, which will deliver him the victory in the war come Colossus. Sun Zu thought he mastered the “Art of War” and in many ways he did. But Hoss that was before the Williams’ clan was born and hell followed. Ahhh look at me here I am rambling about God knows what. Let’s just get to this tale that I hope makes you laugh, cry, and even think a little bit. The story starts at an airport in Chicago on a less then perfect day. You see the city is under a heat advisory putting everybody in a bad mood. Now if you have air-conditioning it’s not a big deal. However as Hoyt sits in the airport waiting for a woman he’s less then pleased to see, a concert is going on promoted by him and carried on every major religious network in the world. Four nations were taking part and the devil was doing his part creating heat waves in all regions the concerts were happening. Now these concerts aren’t really important to the story but understand the stress they are creating. Nigar Aid is a global concert to raise money for Nigar’s and their seeds. I know what you’re thinking why help those people abroad when we have so many who need help here in the states? I thought that too but the RISP and IMPERIUM are all about the aid of Nigar. I guess the country is known for producing Niger seeds and the white doves that the RISP owners have want to get the seeds cheaper. So they are rising money to open up a factory in Nigar ran and worked by Nigar’s. By the way before you get your liberal panties in an up roar know its pronounced (Nyjer) cus the first time I read this script I almost punched the guy who handed it to me. My son Hoyt already had a headache as a couple local crazy Nigar’s heard about the concert. They were upset that a bunch of the Christian world’s best-known bands like Larry Garcia and the Grateful Living, Jesus Phish, Arvil McClean, Insane Clergy Posse, and K.I.S.S: Killean Induces Snoring & Sleep, would rock on through the night while the local Niger’s were starving. I guess in many ways they had a point. So while these problems exist Hoyt sits at this airport waiting for a fight with Mary with Her Cherry whose leaking cherry juice if you know what I mean. Do I have to spell it out? She’s on the rag, or at least Hoyt thinks she is. Added to that the actress Betty White a big time fiscal supporter of the RISP is on her way to spend a few days with Mary and Hoyt. This is Hoyt’s nightmare day as he is worrying about this concert, the heat, Mary and her leaky cherry, and putting up with Betty White for a week. How does it turn out? Read and learn. Mary: Will you relax? Hoyt: Are you kidding we’re waiting for Betty White while my concert is going to hell. How are we going to raise money to keep the pets of white suburban church goers happy if we can’t buy a factory in Niger? Plus did you forget I have a shot, which may be my last, at Killean a few days from now? Mary: RELAX. Mrs. White donated a lot of money to us and she’s a great women. Hoyt: This is the lady who was on the Golden Girls? Mary: Yea, Betty White. Hoyt: Jesus Christ! Mary: No, Betty White. Narrator: Hoyt stands up and paces in front of the giant glass window that has a backdrop of airplanes. The massive eagles of the sky we call airplanes will never be looked at the same after 9-11. I tell you the A-rab’s bitch and moan about getting nasty looks from American’s but for my dollar I look at them fellows the same as I always have. Now the airplanes they got it rough. Let’s get back to Hoyt who’s yet to figure out how to “take the match home” at the pay-per-view. That is some wrestling jargon for you as that’s the ref call to end the match. I’ll shut up now and let the story unfold. Hoyt: I need to do something in the next few days that will not only take Killean off his game but also make him manic. Mary: Another miracle? Hoyt sighs as he sits back down slapping his palms on his knees in very causal manner. Hoyt: No it needs to be something that can’t be chalked up as dumb luck. It needs to be in your face and personal. Mary: Maybe Betty White will have an idea. Hoyt: Yea and I’m Randy Jackson. As a plane pulls up and begins to prepare for departing passengers both Mary and Hoyt sit in silence and watch. Hoyt’s mind is going a hundred miles a minute as is Mary’s. Mary has a surprise of her own and she’s not sure how Hoyt is going to take it. The silence isn’t really silence as an airport isn’t a library but conversation between the two has noticeable ceased. Mary stands up in her blue jeans and t-shirt with her ample biblical breasts busting out of her “Jesus Saves” t-shirt. Hoyt takes a moment to admire her womanly figure, which includes an ass that even Gandhi would eat. The flood of people pouring out of gate number thirty-three breaks his gaze at her Garden of Eden turning his interest to all the different walks of life exiting the plane. Hoyt: I’m glad I have a private Jet, public planes must reek. All those foreign people jammed into a small tube with no fresh air. Yuck! Mary: Wow Betty looks great! There she is. Hoyt puts on his instamatic grin as he greets the old bag with a few kind words and a hug. Mary then hugs Betty hard the two embrace like old friends. Once they break the lock Betty gives Hoyt a look, then a glance down at her bags like, “what are you waiting for stupid”. This doesn’t ease Hoyt’s resentment to the unwanted celebrity houseguest but the joy on Mary’s face and the fact that he thinks she’s Bloody Mary prevents him from saying what he thinks. Hoyt: My friends Peter, Baltasar, Angelus are waiting in the Imperium Cadillac. Betty: No limo? I guess times are hard. Hoyt: It’s in the shop. Betty: I can’t wait to meet the boy’s of Imperium they all have gold. Well except you. Hoyt: Fu…. Mary kicks Hoyt in the shin hard as he stifles his anger for a moment and picks up Betty White’s bags which weigh about as much as Boda’s ass. Very, very, very, heavy. Hoyt: Fullow me. Betty: It’s pronounced “follow”, honey. But close enough. Mary: We watched Lake Placid last night WOW what a movie. Hoyt: I don’t know why it didn’t get an Oscar. Mary: Don’t patronize me son. That movie was GOD awful. Sort of like your win/loss record on the TV. Hoyt: I’m Mr. Pay-Per-View I only perform when the money is on the line. Mary: Let’s hope you don’t get another “flu” Mr. Pay-Per-View. Betty is greeting by IMPERIUM with open arms. The guys all seem to enjoy Mrs. White and she seems to really enjoy them. She sits in back with Los D and Mary while Vetra sits passenger in the front as Hoyt is in the captains chair. The crew share laughs, story’s, and bust open the mini-bar in the back of the Cadillac driven by Hoyt who is not partaking in this fun. Betty: Can you slow down? This isn’t Indianapolis. Hoyt: I’m doing the limit. Betty: Maybe your speedometer is busted. You’re going too fast. Hoyt: We’re fine. Vetra: Maybe you are driving a little fast Hoyt. Hoyt: Sell out. I’m sure Mrs. White wants to get to her hotel room as soon as possible. Mary: Hotel? Hoyt: Yea, you know the place where out of town guest stay. Mary: She’s staying with us I prepared the guest room. Mary and Betty are holding hands with Betty giving Mary a warm rub of her upper hand. This catches Baltasar’s attention as he nudges Angelus with his leg. The too stare attentively as they take mental notes of this strange activity. It’s clear to both men observing that Mary and Betty are more then just friends who have never met. Hoyt: I’m sure a hotel would be much more comfortable. Betty: I’m STAYING with you two. The Cadillac goes as silent as tomb as nobody wants to speak during this tension. Hoyt: Why don’t I drop you two ladies off at the house while the boy’s and I check out things at the concert site? I’ll then pick you two lady’s up at eight for dinner just the three of us. Mary: Sounds great. Hoyt puts the pedal to the metal as he can’t get to the house fast enough to drop off the estrogen in the vehicle. He’s dying for a little alone time with the boy’s so he can plot his game plan for the MAIN EVENT at the BIGGEST PAY-PER-VIEW of PRIME’S calendar year. The match being only a few days away Hoyt really needs to figure out the perfect idea that will lead to the destruction of Killean. Baltasar: Man I really could go for some eggnog. Vetra: It’s July? Baltasar: Why is precious eggnog only available during Christmas? I think it’s a conspiracy. Betty: It’s the fucking homeless. Angelus: Huh? Betty: Oh yea the dairy produces have to make extra milk to give to the homeless shelters. That extra milk could be used to make eggnog so people like us who could afford it can enjoy it. But no it goes to the filthy poor. We need to eradicate them. Baltasar: Of coarse, its Satan’s way of making us good Mexican-American’s who enjoy the holiday treat suffer while the greedy poor drink milk. You are a smart women Betty. Although it sounds like something that Hoyt would say he isn’t impressed. He seems irate that his friends are hanging on Betty White’s words. He’s the one who’s supposed to give the political opinions not Betty. What the hell does she know she was a Golden Girl for God’s sake! Hoyt: WHAT A MORON!!! HEY BUDDY WHAT THE HELL IS A MATTER WITH YOU. STUPID JERK! An explosion of pent up anger is ejected in the direction of the man in front of Hoyt. The guy in the green car which still fashions a “Kerry/Edwards” losing bumper sticker has been driving the limit slowing Hoyt down. Now instead of making the last light before his home he is stuck at the red light with this guy still in front of him. He is raging yelling at the top of his lungs giving the guy the finger and NOT laying off his horn. Baltasar: Chill on the horn holmes. Mary: Relax. Too late as a knock is heard on the driver’s side window a cop stands there waiting for Hoyt to reply. Now Hoyt is not the best when it comes to dealing with the police. He rolls down his window and confronts the motorcycle cop who just happened to pull up next to the raging Hoyt witnessing the whole incident. Hoyt: This moron needs a ticket for recklessly driving to slow. Cop: Out of the car sir. Hoyt: Do you know who I am? Hoyt exits the car and walks a bit to close to the cop who now has his gun out. Cop: On the ground. A few minutes later we find Hoyt lying on the concrete with his hands cuffed behind his back. The rest of the group sits on the curb watching. Time flies by as Hoyt just lies with his face burning on the black asphalt. His anger is almost as hot as the sun. An hour passes before the issue is resolved. Cop: I’m sorry it was a case of mistaken identity. The cop begins to un-cuff Hoyt letting him stand up. Hoyt: WHAT DO YOU MEAN? Cop: Well I saw the Jesus fish on the back of your car. Hoyt: Right. Cop: I saw the What Would Jesus Do bumper sticker. I saw the proud Christian license plate holder. I saw the pro-life symbol. The RISP logo. Hoyt: You have a point? Cop: Then I saw a mad man in the front seat not acting like a good bible toting Christian so I assumed the car was stolen. You get my point? Hoyt: I think so. Cop: Then you’re free to go. Oh and Betty thanks for the autograph and cookie recipe!! You’re the best. The crew gets back in the car and begins rolling towards the house this time Hoyt is driving like a saint. Betty: I told you that you needed to slow down. The gang arrives at the nice suburban home of Hoyt Williams and Mary with Her Cherry. It’s what you would expect American flag in the front lawn, white picket fence, and Mexican mowed green grass. Hoyt kisses Mary good bye and helps Betty get her crap out of his trunk. He gets back in and drives off with just Imperium. Hoyt: House guest from hell. Baltasar: Dude, I don’t know how to say this. Angelus gives Baltasar a serious yet smirking look. Baltasar: Ang and I are privy to some new information. We saw some shit and I’m not sure how to tell you. Hoyt: Nothing can piss me off more then I already am right now so spill it. Baltasar: Ang and I saw…Betty and Mary…holding hands and hugging a lot. Hoyt: So? Baltasar: You’re not following I’m talking about A LOT of hand holding. To be blunt, dude they are a lesbian couple. Hoyt goes silent as he stares at Baltasar from the rearview mirror. Angelus: It’s true man I’ve seen a lot of lesbian’s in my time and I have to agree with my partner. She’s a pink soldier. Narrator: How about that nugget of information? Do you believe it? I don’t, but then again I did see some strange behavior between Mary and Betty. I guess anything is possible these days where it’s hip to be a muff diver. Hell I was diving in muff long before it was hip but this story isn’t about me. It’s about a ring and a new beginning but we’ll get to that. Right now Hoyt has more on his plate then an obese man at a buffet. After spending the next few hours pissed at the world Hoyt checked in on his concert. To his surprise all is well and the thing is going to end with out incident. Now if only he could get his life back on track. With the possibly that Mary has changed teams plus the added fact that Betty is annoying to him and still here one would assume that all hell is about to break loose. Let’s get back on our journey where we find Hoyt at a fancy restaurant having dinner with Mary and Betty. He is dressed for the occasion in an all white suit while the other two are dressed in gorgeous dinner gowns. Mary: So then he went on TV and said he banged his dead wife in heaven. Betty: You ask God to take out his friend, and then you visit his dead wife in heaven and fuck her? I think you have done enough to get under his skin. Hoyt: So you’re married to Barry White, how’s that going. Betty: Barry White is black. Hoyt: Oh so you’re racially cool and what not? Betty White is getting upset as she butters her bread. Betty: No I’m not racially cool…. Hoyt: Lots of fights huh? Betty: NO I WAS NEVER MARRIED TO BARRY WHITE. We just have the same last name. Hoyt: Oh. Hoyt seems disappointed to find out Betty isn’t married as he is still trying to figure out if what Baltasar said is true or just a figment of his over active tequila driven imagination. Hoyt: So are you looking for a guy? Betty: No I’m not interested anymore. Hoyt: Not interested anymore? What does that mean? Betty: When you get to a certain age it no longer matters. Hoyt: Oh. Mary: I could go for some fish. Hoyt: WHAT? Mary: I have a craving for fish. Hoyt: Why? You hate fish? Betty: People’s tastes change. Now Hoyt is convinced that he has lost Mary to a lesbian relationship to Betty White. Baltasar must have been right. Hoyt: Mary’s taste doesn’t just change she likes sausage. All the buttering of bread has stopped as the voices have been raised a bit and there is fire in the eyes of all three dinners at the Williams table. Betty: What have I done to her? You’re the reason her taste has changed to fish. Hoyt: Me? Betty: Well, more so what you did in the bedroom. Hoyt: I wasn’t good enough? Mary: Too good. Hoyt: What is going on here? What are we talking about? Betty: You should have married her first. Hoyt: Now you’re going to marry her? How dare you try to take my lady with your lesbian tactics? Hoyt: Your mother? Why didn’t you tell me?? Mary: I wasn’t sure how you were going to take it. Plus there is more. Hoyt: Oh? Mary: Hoyt my appetite has changed because I’m pregnant. After pulling off a successful awareness concert selling a ton of stupid plastic trendy bracelets for major bucks to the nitwit drone like general public. All I know is I will be ordering the Pay-Per-View. Must see TV if you ask me. That my friend’s is the honky tonk truth. =w=
