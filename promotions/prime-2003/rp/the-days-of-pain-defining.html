Date: Now “You wanna do what?” Hailey was in the kitchen, fixing something warm to drink as her brother called out from the living area. Some cool air came in through the patio door. Nitz liked it cool inside his home, something his visitors didn’t take kindly to. Hailey was wearing a thick sweater, trying to keep herself warm, although to his credit Nitz decided to close the patio door for her. What a nice guy. “I want to come with you when you leave for El Paso. Texas is such a beautiful state, I’d feel like I’m missing out if you went and I didn’t.” She said, dipping the tea bag into the hot water. She moved to the cupboard, looking for the sugar. “Ok, so you wanna come with. That’s cool, but what are you gonna do down there when I’m getting ready for the show?” “I’m sure there’s plenty for a single gal to do in El Paso little bro.” She laughed a little, winning an unseen roll of the eyes from Nitz. “Sugar?” “In the cabinet beside the fridge Hail.” “No, do you want sugar in your tea?” She asked. “Yeah... three.” Nitz got out of his chair and walked to the side credenza. He pulled the cell phone charger into the outlet and then plugged the other end into his cell phone, resting it on the surface. He sighed. “Alright. If you wanna come you can. But let’s set some ground rules here. You get your own room. I don’t room with anyone from the show, even if I have to pay the extra money to do it. I definitely don’t wanna share a room with my bad habits sister.” “Oh, bad habits huh? If you weren’t seeing Rachelle, you’d have girls coming in and out of your room constantly. You couldn’t pay ME to share a room with you. So don’t worry about that.” Hailey laughed again as she stepped into the living area and sat on the couch across from her brother. She placed the two mugs on the table on top of the coasters and continued to stir her own. “Yeah well if things keep going, Rachelle and I could be done.” “What? What happened?” Hailey asked, seeming to be legitimately concerned with the sudden change in the discussion. “She’s just acting weird.” “Do you know why?” “Nah, but I can bet that it has to do with Chad’s decision to move back out here.” “Well, Chad’s moving back here because he got another business opportunity.” Hailey explained, but Nitz is clearly shaking his head no. “Bullshit he did. This guy can get a business opportunity anywhere these days it seems. With this country going into recession, people are losing their homes and their jobs but he’s thriving. It doesn’t make any damn sense.” “Come on Nick. Why do you think he has to have another agenda?” “Simple. I’ve never trusted the fucker since he left in the first place.” “Maybe he’s trying to make things right again.” Hailey said, poking her brother in the shoulder, trying to show signs of humor. The Ego was having none if it though. “Yeah, and Bush is a good President. Come on Hailey, he’s luring you in too. I know it.” Hailey let out a deep sigh and took a sip of her tea. She wondered just when Nicholas would let things go and try to patch things up with Chad. It was a problem that had gone on for far too long. |~o~| ~Back in Portland~ Chad Donnely was almost done packing up his desk. His book cases behind his desk were already empty and his files were almost all boxed up. But he wasn’t using the care that a normal person would use. Instead, he was not organizing and just loading up the boxes in a random fashion. Most of the items that used to sit atop his desk were also packed away. There wasn’t much to go. “Almost done in here Chad?” His co-worker DeeAnn poked her head through the door to check on him. They become friends immediately upon his move out to Portland. “Getting there. Kind of upsetting, but I made my choice.” DeeAnn watched him shake his head as he spoke, wondering if he really thought his decision through. She came through the door, the regular hustle and bustle of the office going on as normal in the background. “You know, don’t mind my asking, but why are you doing this? I thought you came out here to get away from home. Not to mention, what are you doing for work?” She was being extra inquizzitive, however if there had been a fly on the wall, Nitz and their family back home would soon know that Chad did not have a business opportunity in Boston. “I need to go back... right some wrongs and tie up some loose ends. You know how it is... things bother you until they wear you down and you have to confront them.” “But what are you gonna do for work?” DeeAnn asked, picking up and studying a crystal paperweight that has a falcon engraved into it. “I have plenty of money to live off of right now and then once I tie up the loose ends, I may just work part time at home. Or I might come back here. You never know, but I’m not worried.” DeeAnn had an innocent look on her face, trying to pay attention to what her friend was saying and observe the gorgeous paperweight in her hand. She had always been rather envious of Chad’s quick rise in the company, surpassing her and becoming an executive. She saw the crystal paperweight as a token of his success, that something as silly as a paperweight could be so expensive and beautiful. “You can keep that if you want. Not like I’ll be needing it.” “Wow, thanks Chad. I’d better get back though. Come see me before you go?” He nodded in response, turning to his window and looking out at the busy city below as DeeAnn went back to work. Soon it would be back to Boston. Back to home. Back to tie up the loose ends and rid himself of some guilt.
