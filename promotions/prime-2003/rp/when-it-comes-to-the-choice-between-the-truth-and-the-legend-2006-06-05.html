When it comes to the choice between the truth and the legend, always choose the legend (ReV 96 RP)

5 June 2006

Date: 05/06/06 This was ready a few days ago, but I didn't know if it was meant to be a 'Public' RP one a 'secret' one - so I just PM'd it to Pete. Apparently it should've been up here too, so it's here now. Two Years Ago – Colossus I Beaten, bruised and broken, but with head held high, Adam walks back through the curtain of the PRIME stage for the last time – or so he’s said to the fans. He pours a bottle of water over his jet-black hair, nods his acknowledgement for a match well fought to Black Angel, then starts to walk back round toward his lockerroom. Once round the corner, out the sight of his opponent there’s an instant change in the Scot’s manner – far from the melancholy demeanour of a man beaten in his last match, leaving the ring forever, he instead had a spring to his step; he was a man who knew he had a future no-one else had any inkling about. Voice: “It’s a full moon.” Adam stops in his tracks, a smile washing slowly over his face as he answers. Adam: “They’re coming to get me, when the night falls.” A man in a dark suit walks out the shadows of a doorway to Adam’s left and extends a hand. Adam: Samson Hayes, isn’t it? The man nods as Adam takes his hand and shakes it firmly. Samson: Come this way. He pushes open the door he’d been standing in front of and leads Adam through. It’s a side-door to the parking lot, where there’s a black Lincoln with tinted windows waiting with the engine running. Samson opens the back door and allows Adam to step in. Fifteen Months Ago – Nevada Desert Adam: You’re shitting me... Area 51? The black Humvee he was riding in pulled up to the entrance gate and the driver (to whom Adam had addressed his astounded question) flashed and identification pass. It didn’t take long for the gates before them to open, letting them drive through. The driver, Adam’s mentor and overseer, Samson Hayes, glanced across at the bewildered Scot. Samson: Remember... Rule 3 – Never appear surprised. Expect everything. Adam closes his jaw, which had been literally hanging open, slightly sheepish at his lapse in basic training – nine months solid and he messed up one of the basic principles. The inside of the car remained silent until they reached the main building, when Samson nodded for Adam to get out. Adam: Just me? Samson merely nodded. Adam: Well... I guess I’ll see you. He stepped out, walked the few feet across the blisteringly hot tarmac to only door nearby then stepped through and into his future. One Year Ago – Underground, Area 51 Sitting strapped into a chair, eyes held open with ‘A Clockwork Orange’ style machinery while a film plays at incredible speed before him, lies Adam. Every major event in the United States Of America’s history has just been flickered at him as the lights in the small, antiseptically clean room start to come up. A man in a white coat walks in through a near-imperceptible door somewhere in the smooth white walls. Doctor: And so completes day three of your viewing schedule. He administers a few drops of liquid to the Scot’s dry eyeballs before starts to he unhook the apparatus, first from the eyes then the restraining straps from the wrists and ankles. Adam blinks repeatedly, a few drops running down his cheeks like tears. Doctor: Your training for the ‘Shock Trooper’ program is now ready to begin. Adam shakily steps off the chair, still blinking. The doctor appears to take no notice as he strolls to the unmarked door again, it slides open just as he reaches it. Doctor: Come. In the next room there’s a table set up, with a stack of A4 paper approximately a foot high sitting in the middle of it. The Doctor pulls up a chair in front of the stack and motions for Adam to follow suit, only too happy to rest his unsteady legs Adam obliges. Doctor: Now, these are your release forms – you need to sign away a few rights before embarking on the program. First, He pulls the top sheet off the pile, hands Adam a pen from his shirt pocket, and points to the dotted line at the bottom. Doctor: you agree to any slight adverse physical effects that any treatment or training may cause you, for example: hair loss or colour change; minor physical markings or burns; retinal colour change; loos of one or many teeth. Adam mutely signs the form, it’s then slid into a fine slot in the table where there’s a quiet whirring sound as it’s taken in and processed by the table. The next sheet is peeled from the pile and laid before the two men. Doctor: This one is to agree to the reimbursement by the agency for any major physical effects that any treatment or training many cause you, for example: loss of the use of limbs; loss of limbs; partial or full paralysis; blindness; deafness etc. Again Adam signs, again the form is fed into the table and again another sheet is laid before him. Doctor: You agree to any intravenous, under-skin or otherwise internally-placed tracking chip placement. And so it goes on. Six Months Ago – Deep Underground, Area 51 Adam: My mind, body and soul are solely property of the United States’ Central Intelligence Agency, Sir! My role in life is to serve, by any means necessary and possible, this fine country by means of this Agency. I am advanced beyond any of my peers and I and entirely grateful for these superior abilities, sir! He’s standing, dressed in full army gear, gun on shoulder, eyes straight ahead, inside an Official’s office. The man in front of him is highly, highly decorated. He nods approvingly and extends a hand. Official: Congratulations, son – you are now officially a member of the Central Intelligence Agency’s Shock Trooper unit and now a United States ‘citizen’. He chuckles slightly at the word citizen, quite the understatement in this case. Official: Now we need to discuss your objectives. He waves a hand to tell Adam to stand down and take a seat opposite him. Adam sits his gun across his lap as he takes the seat. Official: You’ll receive the information now, then spend the next month preparing for your re-integration. Adam: Re-integration? Official: Yep; you’re going back to PRIME.
