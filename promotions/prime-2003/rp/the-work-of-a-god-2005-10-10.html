The Work Of A God

10 October 2005

Date: 10 October 05 The Passion: Day 1 – 'One Small Step For Man'             It didn't take me long to bask in the feeling of my stomach flipping or my nerves twitching, but it's almost what I expected. She was right where she told me she'd be; underneath the third tree from the left, in the very back of the large concrete structure. The peaks of sunlight contrasted flawlessly with her bright red hair as they snuck through the dense branches and leaves of the tree. It was a picture perfect moment, only without a camera. Typical.             I proceeded to the woman who I met once, but a woman who I've seen many times before. I'm not one to impose, but it was she who told me to visit. It was simply an offer I couldn't refuse. The moment I made movement her way, I was spotted. I was hesitant to put my hand up and wave, and the fact that I stuttered in the process of lifting my arm was enough for possibly her and I to realize that I am an idiot. I never was slick around the ladies. Go me.             I think the smirk on her face was enough for me to not turn around and leave and after what seemed like the most demeaning walk ever, I stood within feet of her and she greeted me with my name. I'm not sure if I was shocked to the fact that she remembered my name, or if it was the fact that she was incredibly stunning. It had to have been the name; I only told her once and I was sure of the fact that I'm not one to show much emotion towards people.              She spun around quickly as I met up with her and lead me into a single door that was propped open by a cinder block. You never really saw much of this on Playboy late at night; people running around for interviews, wrestlers having coffee with each other, and especially something like 'Aimz' running around with a 'boy'. I wasn't used to the fact that people were pointing at me with sour looks on the face, nor was I used to someone helping me. Then she came.              Amy and I ran into Karina. I recognized her from her numerous appearances on live television and the more than enough fills in the newspapers. I envied what she did in the ring, but I despised her ill attitude. I would have settled with saying hello to her, but that initial glare almost made me snap. I guess those anger management classes did come in handy because I just watched the performance as Amy fired back rebuttals better than I have ever seen dished out during an Aimz/K-Wolf confrontation. It didn't take Karina long to leave which shortly opened the opportunity for some food. Flips Page Amy suggested the chicken. It sucked, but I pretended to like it.              Leaning up against the wall, the conversation during the meal was interesting and helpful. She told me things about the characters that you'd only know when you stood toe to toe with them in the ring or if you happened to be their sex therapist. Interesting was the easiest way to describe it. Conversation got cut short when she suddenly leaped up with the words… Oh how did she put it? 'Oh fucking shit, I'm in the first fucking match.' Yes, that was it.              She pointed me towards a television that was propped up in the corner directly behind me. As soon as I turned my head back towards the direction where she stood three seconds ago, she was running around the corner with both of her hands on her baggy pants; one hand on each pant leg, lifted high enough to expose her ankles the moment prior to her running out of sight. Did she just pull a lousy prank on me?             I turned my head back around if it happened that she was pointing at something else, but the only thing there was a small television that was showing a commercial for tampons. At the time, I was about to give it up for that girl, she was one up on me. I began to make my way from the food and back to my car. I never did take practical jokes well. But then I heard it…             A melody that I've heard when I seen her practicing the other day at the gym. It was Hush's 'Rock Shit' – supposedly Amy’s new theme while she made her way to the ring. Of course! She wanted me to watch her match. I don't remember taking my eyes off the match, nor do I remember her performing one move incorrectly. I never was a fan of perfection, but this was entirely different. Flips Page             She went along to beat Kyle Lamen in that match and I knew that I had to find some way to congratulate her. For about an hour, I felt like I was walking around a place I had no clue how to get around. The fact that people stared at me didn't make me at all comfortable. I figured that she could have gone to her designated room after the match and probably fell asleep from exhaustion. I knocked on her door quietly, not to be rude, but loud enough for anyone inside to hear. I swore I knocked at least five times – four times too many for my liking.             How could she have forgotten about me? I turned away from the door and sat in a chair nearby. It was awfully quiet. I would have thought that I was at the wrong door if it weren't for the nameplate next to the door that read 'Aimz'. Maybe I was foolish; I mean this stuff always seemed to happen to me. The little tour was nice, the conversation was great, and the food was alright, but once again, in the end I'm left alone trying to figure out what the fuck happened. It's time to go home. --Tyler             Continued! That sneaky little woman! About five minutes ago I made my way to the car, feeling like a complete bag of ass. I knew I wasn't parked illegally, unless a parking permit is required for all PRIME superstars to park back here, because a small piece of paper fluttered in the wind which was held down by my left windshield wiper. I proceeded to it with a bit of anger and prepared myself for the damage. It cost... …Absolutely nothing.             It wasn't a ticket after all. I mean what ticket reads, 'I'd never leave a man behind…call me ;-)' followed by nine digits. The truth was, with that little stunt, she did have one on me. --Tyler Book Close The Story: 'This work which I have begun makes good my promise. . . In it, I am undertaking nothing less than the task of defending the glorious City of God against those who prefer their own gods to the Founder.' (Augstine, 426/1958: p. 39) “I never thought of myself as a religious person, but the fact I do take the time to believe, pray, and confess, I guess you could say that I am.” Voice quiet, yet distinct, Tyler O’Neill sits inside a small booth with his head down and his hands placed atop his thighs, back straight against the wall behind him. Artificial lighting passed through a grid-like window on the door entrance of the booth which passed light through the locks of his hair into his eyes. Tyler squints as he stares down towards the ground. “One needs not to express love for God to others all the time. Expressing love for him within you is all that matters.” Another quiet voice is directed towards Tyler from an adjacent room. Between the two rooms was another small grid-like window and the silhouette of another man is seen in that room. What came clear was that Tyler was in a confession booth, but the reasons why were unknown. “Father, sometimes it seems that at times I can not control my anger. It seems that a type of evil enters my body, but the evil is not of which would be pressed upon by the Devil.” Tyler takes in a deep breath and exhales a subtle sigh. He runs his fingers through his hair and picks up his head and stares forward with a cold, dense expression. “Please continue my son, you have my ears. Be aware though, that there is no evil, which is not sent from the Devil himself. Evil arises from Hell to plague the Earth to compete with God himself, but it is us who must strengthen the will of our father within this religion.” The priest replies calmly, eager to hear this ‘confession’ of Tyler’s. “No Father, as much as I respect your judgment because of who you are and what you do, I know what I feel. Maybe it’s not evil that has bestowed itself upon me, but whatever it is, it’s something that I haven’t been able to find an answer to for at least seven years. This feeling that I hold inside of me urges me to just unleash everything and snap. I’ve actually done it before and after I’ve done what my body has fledged me to do, I don’t often remember much. Before any counseling, I have hurt many people and have done some not-so-great things.” The priest is quick to reply. “Then you have come to the right place my son. When was your last confession?” Tyler shakes his head and squints his eyes. He is beginning to become emotional. Only on rare occasions this happens; but it also happens in front people he is calm enough to talk to. “No Father, this time I have not come to confess. I don’t regret much of anything I’ve ever done. I’ve come here solely for advice, guidance, and possibly some answers.” The priest replies in a calm tone. “Advice I must, guidance I will, and answers I may.” Tyler reveals another sigh and begins to speak. His words distinct with curiosity. “Father, when I begin to get that urge inside of me to hurt someone, it’s usually directed towards someone of wrong doing; a criminal of sorts. When I see a weaker kid get beaten up by a stronger bully; when I hear a woman screaming because she’s getting raped in the distance; or even when I hear about another innocent man being murdered for his watch, the anger inside of me fills up and I just want to take it out on every low standard, no class, scumbag of a human being who does these things to people and just beat the living shi—“ Quick to interfere, the Priest responds. “Please my son, calm yourself and relax. I am almost for certain that any docile man or woman would be ill willed towards anything that performs acts such as these. Sins of others shall not motivate others to sin as well. That is how the Devil works on the Earth; using one’s sin to create sin with another. Be faithful in our Lord for he will help these people in the dark and show them the light.” Tyler turns his head towards the priest’s booth, disgruntled by something on his mind. “It’s it possible though, that He could have pushed me to serve him by ridding of the one’s who serve to the Devil? When I lose consciousness and when it feels that it’s not my arm swinging, or not my fist punching, I begin to wonder things like that. I feel that sometimes, when I’m fighting, I have been given the power of a God to better serve him and his world.” The priest strikes back with a mild sense of anger. “That’s blasphemy at its entirety.” Tyler grunts softly while shaking his head in disapproval. “How can it be blasphemous if it is He and only He whom I look up to and it is intended to better serve his world?” This time, it is the priest who releases a sigh and he replies to Tyler’s question with a subtle, yet demanding tone. “Because you’re pretending to be a God! You are attempting to convince yourself, me, and others around you that you have the power of a God. Doing so very noticeably conflicts with the Ten Commandments. That is not the work of God, but it is the work of the Devil and you must be cleansed instantly.” Tyler becomes louder as anger begins to taint the tone of his voice. “I never said I was a God and my faith is too strong for the Devil to do anything to me, I swear of it.! It’s taken me over twenty years to start on the right path that was chosen for me by Him and I will follow this path until it leads me to a place where I have no where left to go but the sky. A misguided step would be entirely unnecessary and critical to my mission. Thank you for the advice Father.” Tyler begins stand up and cracks the door open making it possible for the light to enter the booth; striking the old, wrinkled face of the priest who yells out to Tyler. “Why would he talk to you?” Tyler turns his head and smirks, soon replying. “I don’t hear him talking to me, but I feel him and that’s enough for me to keep my head up and take that next step forward. Now if you excuse me father, I have a job to train for... …and it starts at PRIME.” Before the priest could say another word, Tyler has left the booth, door swinging open and the sound of him exiting through the main church doors is heard in the near distance. As the confessional booth door slowly swings shut, the priest is seen with his head down, his hands to his face and mumbling the words… “Oh Great Lord, help this young man.”
