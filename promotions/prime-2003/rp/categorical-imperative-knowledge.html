Date: Who cares The categorical imperative was thought up by philosopher Immanuel Kant, and is focal to his interpretation of the world. In his own words the definition states: "Act only according to that maxim whereby you can at the same time will that it should become a universal law." No one person can be exempt from the imperative just because they think they are special. Paramount to the categoricalness of the imperative is the term absolute. The line is incapable of being bent in any capacity whether logic, emotion, morality, or society be the attempted manipulator. How one wishes to go about achieving the imperative is left open to interpretation. Devin Shakur’s categorical imperative states that in order to achieve enlightenment, one must find true happiness. In his own mind, this is how one can become Buddha. Rule 1- All knowledge must be set before the subject in a lucid manner so that the subject understands their options. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Reflecting January 20th, 1991 Time: Midnight My fingers trembled as I wore the tag of rebel. Chris and Jason made such a mundane request for my presence to acquaint with theirs at the stroke of midnight. To them right now I am nothing more than a superficial entity who goes by a name. What is in a superficial name? Nothing more than letters strung together by the brain. I learned how to be deceptively quiet in my spare time. The floor in my room was wooden and there was a certain portion on the upper left side that was creaky so I practiced there for hours on end. If I made one or two minute sounds, my father would not be able to constitute that I was trying to escape. Right before leaving, I said a prayer to a woman named Harriet Tubman, requesting stealth silence in my journey. The first significant obstacle was the door that went to the basement. It had a rather loud sound whenever someone opened or closed it. My father went down there to make his furniture. I slept down there in a casket that had my name etched into the stitching for years earlier in my childhood so I knew the layout very well. My fragile fingers applied extra pressure on the knob, my attempt to prevent the sound from manifesting.. Once I made the fateful turn my breath was taken inward, eyes shut, and ears perked for the sound of inevitability… But it never came, the door refused to emit sound. That accomplished, I set upon to do the reciprocal of the previous action. I went to close the massive device. With each inch that passed, I feared my father’s eyes lurked. The light slowly oppressed and the door came to a complete shut, success! The next part was about as easy as taking a breath, moving down the stairs in the darkness. My knowledge of the practical might have been ignorant, but in the companion of darkness I held prestige. Down the thirteen steps and onto the basement floor I went. With a quick glance to my right, I saw the casket that I once called home. It still held an ominous presence to me and I would never have any Stockholm syndrome while in its vicinity. I reached the door that went outside of the house. My hand once again applied pressure, the door once again opened, and I once again escaped out into my vast opaque friend. Driven by exploration, I threw myself up the stairs and dashed across the front lawn with cat like quickness. The rest of the journey was merely the twelve hundred step program to liberation. When I came upon the Smith’s driveway I was entranced by the beauty of their house. The light blue color made it seem like heaven on earth. Inside I heard a television that rumbled with activity, intoxicating everybody with its imagery. One window was open so I could see five people inside: Chris, Jason, Brandon, Matthew, and someone new. The man wore a contraption upon his head that appeared to be a decorated red cover for his head, revealing only the slightest strand of jet black hair. Sixteen years later he still wears the same red bandana. His arms were covered with bright pictured literature: A demon that ran from the top to the middle, a flaming skull, a chain wrapped around his entire arm, and the letters DEATH in bold red. A jean jacket and a face wise beyond its years were the last things that I saw from my vantage point. His instincts proved to be accurate, as within twenty seconds of my arrival upon the driveway, he took a hold of Chris’s shoulder and pointed directly at me. Chris beckoned me down with that folded up right hand so I rushed down the driveway, past the cars, up their stairs, and into the living room. Upon entering their home I felt like an actor on a stage for the first time, all eyes fixated upon me. The man that identified me in the driveway was drastically taller than the four counterparts in the room, by almost two complete feet. After introductions, I found that he called himself Christian Daniels and was the older brother of Chris, Brandon, and Matt. "Sit, we’re playing Super Mario World," Jason said with the extended Super Nintendo remote in hand. I had never laid eyes upon the device before, something no bigger than my petite hand, yet it could provide hours upon hours of entertainment. I grasped a hold of the remote, looked down at the buttons, and stared at the screen. A little red man stood and I had the option of taking him to the right or to the left. They taught me how to play the game. Having never had assistance in anything made this experience the first real amount of a word that I never knew existed before. A word so simplistic in the English language, yet capable of rendering people helpless to its spell…Fun. I was having an immense amount of it. After two hours with the game, my eyes stared at the clock to see that it was 2 AM. In fear, I walked over to the window and stared at my home. The much larger Christian came up behind me and looked out in the same direction that I was looking. "What you trying to make?" he asked me, unaware of my ignorance with police and mafia vernacular. Trying to sound intelligent, I blurted out a reply of "My house, I can’t really see if any lights are on." Christian moved his hands to a C shape, put them to the sides of his head, looked deep into the night, and shook his head, "No lights, you are still good." He then slapped me on the back. The blow was completely unexpected so my reaction was following suit, a face of anguish and trying to soothe my wound. "Crap, sorry about that. Hang on, I’ll go get some ice." Christian walked into the kitchen, opened the freezer, grabbed a small blue pack, and tossed it to me. I caught the object and placed it upon my back. It was at that moment Christian’s eyebrows burrowed down. He walked up to me and removed the ice pack while staring at my shirt. "What the hell…?" was all that he managed to say before he left the room, walked down the hall, and returned with a small white box that said First Aid Kit. At this point the reason for his concern slowly dripped down my back. The only word I muttered was goddammit! This turned out to be the most enlightening moment of my life to date. "Alright, the rest of you get out of here, go to bed, now!" Christian said, and then shuffled the other four out of the room. The only thing that their brains comprehended was that an accident had occurred. Christian knew much more however. He took control of the situation, dropped me down to the couch and sat beside me. With one swift motion, he pulled my shirt up over my head. His next reaction was like that of someone who stared at Medusa, a stone face. If one knows Christian Daniels at all, they know that he is not easily shocked in any capacity. The man has encountered the reaper so many times that he has plagiarized many of the intimidating characteristics. Yet, on this night nothing came out of his mouth but two words "Holy…shit". This was the first sign of concern that had ever interacted itself upon my being. Christian put his massive frame on the floor in an Indian style position, looked into my eyes, and posed the question "How did all of that happen?" The only response I gave was quivering while tears flowed from my face. Another fact about Christian Daniels is that he is not one to show legitimate concern for anybody other than his own family. Upon seeing my body and my reaction, a sense of responsibility overtook him. The Smith’s mother, Jacqueline had died not that long ago and ever since then, he had to become the parent of four younger brothers. His massive right hand came up to my face. I moved backwards in an attempt to shun him, for I knew not what his intentions were. "Hey, hey, Devin, I’m not going to strike you," he said, and then he held both hands in the air. After a second, his right hand once again moved up to my face and I allowed him to wipe the tears from my face. "Good, good, relax. Nobody’s going to hurt you, you understand?", I managed to nod and slowly the shaking disintegrated. Christian reached over to the table near the couch, grabbed a box of tissues, handed them to me, allowed me to wipe my face. Once I had done such, he once again posed the question of "How did all of this happen?" The tears still flowed but I somehow formulated the answer, "My father". His expression was unchanged so that was more than likely the answer he anticipated. He handed me another tissue and once again coerced me into looking at him, "Why does he do this to you?" What would follow next was an answer that threw Christian aback, "He says…", I salvaged my raped innocence for just a second longer, "That I killed my mother". At this point my hands went over my eyes and the tears managed to win their battle against my will. Christian put his right hand on my knee, yet did not remove my hands, "Alright, good, you are doing good Devin. It’s going to help you in the long run to talk about these things. Almost like thera…It will be almost like a confessional. Do you know what that is?" I responded with a nod and continued, "…He also says that I am the Devil’s son sent to Earth to further ago…ago…n", Christian finished off the sentence with disgust, "Agonize him." "So he ends up doing this to you because of that?" Christian shot back before he stood up, and now received the panorama view of my back. His head shook back and forth and his face grimaced with agony. "How did your mother die?" he said before putting the ice pack down the middle of my back, cooling off some of the wounds from my past years. "I…I don’t know. I never saw her." Christian nodded at my comment and once again sat down beside me. "Does your father say that he is doing God’s work by punishing you?" Again, I responded with a nod. The next words that came out of my mouth had to be the most complex Freudian slip of all time "HE MADE ME SLEEP IN A WOODEN BOX WITH MY NAME ON IT!" The blood went out of Christian’s face immediately. He knew various methods of torture even at the age of 19, but this was something that never crossed his mind. Hell, this was something that was downright absurd. He put his face into his hands, just like me, and pondered: Why was this brought upon me? How could a father do this to a child? How in the hell is Devin still standing? "Alright, Devin…I’ll tell you what I’m going to do. Tonight, before you go back over there, I’m going to teach you a couple of defensive moves so that you can better ensure your safety. What he’s doing to you…It’s not right. You are a very brave man for being able to withstand all of this abuse. At your age, I don’t think that I could have gone through what you are going through. We’re going to straighten this out one way or the other." "First thing we are going to do is go down in the basement. I have a punching bag down there, we’re going to build up your strength. Second thing we are going to do is sharpen your mind. Even a man like your father, strong as he might be, has weaknesses on his body. We all have them and he’s not out of that group. The third thing that you need to learn is where to go if you get a chance to run. If neither of the first two work, you need a third plan. You know where our house is. I want that to be the place where you run if you have to. If none of us are home, the houses that are next to ours should have people inside of them. You can go in there and wait for one of us to get home. Alright? Come on, we’re going to get this going right now." I pushed my body up to a standing position alongside of him, my shirt slung over the right shoulder, and walked out of the living room. When I re-emerged later that morning it was like I had seen the light. I benefited from the experience and the groundwork had been laid for the start of my evolution. The equilibrium swung its mighty pendulum however. When I returned home, my father was awake and saw me come up through the basement door. After a short interrogation, he got the information out of me as to my whereabouts, and he gave me the worst beating of my life. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Part II- January 20th, 1991 Time: 3 PM Wandering eyes illuminated me to the power of strength, but not the strength of the expected. Once my father managed to ascertain which house that I went to earlier this morning, he decided to pay the residents a visit. He walked across our yard with a blithering amount of rage and discontent, curious to know what mind or minds could propaganda his son to commit such an act of domesticated treason. He would find out and deal with them accordingly, or so he assumed. He knocked upon their door with the intent of encountering a small child, one that he could easily bring forth the illusions and lies that he brought forth to me. Instead, he encountered a man who was taller and stronger in Christian. Until I saw them facing one another, I never saw my father show a sense of being overwhelmed. In later years, I would be told from Christian that the conversation they had was as followed: "Hello, is there a Chris Smith here?" "Not home yet, I‘ll be of assistance though. What can I do for you?" "Yes, I believe the other evening that you had a young child come over and stay in your home for a number of hours." "Little kid about yon high, black hair, looks kind of like…You" "Yes, that would be my child Devin. I’m Anthony Shakur, his father." "Is that so…Well now ain’t this a treat?" "I’m sorry?" "Yeah, I met your kid earlier and we had a rather interesting time with one another." "How do you mean?" "For a father you sure are acting retarded in the face of the truth." "Son, I don’t-" "You ever call me son again and I’ll knock you the fuck out! Don’t try to call me the son of the Devil like you do Devin." At this point, I saw my father taken aback, but slowly the rage flushed back into his face. "What did you just say?" "I didn’t stutter dumbass, I know what you do to Devin and I know why you do it. It’s taking every fiber of my being not to beat the shit out of you right now." "He did something against the will of G-" "Get that religious bullshit out of my face. You beat him because he killed your wife? Man, take a step back and say that to my face, see how much sense it makes." "He did! How else can you explain it? God was-" "Stop bringing God into the equation. I have limited medical training but I’ll take a stab and say that she died of excess bleeding during the birth. THAT AIN’T THE KID’S FAULT! IT’S A COMMON FUCKING PROBLEM!" It is at this point Christian walked completely out onto the porch and looked down at my father. His fists were balled up and my father showed the first signs of fear. "I also know you made the kid sleep in a casket…That is more than enough than I need to beat you into oblivion without feeling an ounce of moral remorse." "What the hell do you know about parenting?" "I am a fucking parent you idiot. I raise my four brothers that live in this house. I go out and make the money to put food on their plate and I DAMN sure don’t treat them like goddamn slaves! You can’t legitimately justify putting a belt to that kid unless he does something against the rules. Beating him just because he’s alive is motherfucking appalling. You are REAL fucking lucky I don’t go to the police and turn your ass in. Hell, the kid is so fucking socially inept that with a little bit of talking, the police would arrest your ass and put you in the pokey." The vigor with which the words escaped his mouth apparently got to my father. Christian told me that at this point the started to sweat, and I could see that he was adjusting his collar through the window. "And if you got in the pokey, boy…Or should I say…Son…I know plenty of people on the inside that would make your life far more miserable than you make Devin’s. You’ll be trying to hang yourself in your cell every free second that you get, but you won’t be able to. You want to go biblical? I think you should be publically crucified for the shit you pull with that kid. The only thing that he wants to do is live and you are taking that away from him." My father knew that intimidation would not work at this point so he had to take the verbal abuse. "You want me to tell you what we did? I taught the kid some self defense. I taught him where the weaknesses on your body are, and I told him where to run if you keep doing the shit you are doing. So lemme tell you what’s gonna happen son, you are going to grow a set, man up, and take care of that kid the RIGHT way. If you don’t…Trust me, I’ll know about it. If he wants to go outside, you let him. If he wants to go somewhere, you take him. No questions asked, no argument made. He’s going to come over here whenever he wants and if he wants to stay the night then you are going to let him. The kid needs some fucking exposure to the outside world, keeping him inside that house is going to make him kill himself before the end of the year." At this point I saw Christian grab my father by the collar of his shirt, yank him up off the ground a good six inches, and ensure that their eyes were level with one another. "Do I make myself perfectly clear…Or do I need to give you a taste of your own medicine?" My father shook his head no and Christian threw him to the ground. Not another word was said. Christian walked up the stairs and back into the house. My father picked himself up off the ground and walked back to our home. I was free…The knowledge would soon come to my window.
