Date: ??? The scene opens in a dark and dingy hotel room. We see a man sitting down on the edge of the bed all dressed in black, he has a hood on so his face is not visible at the moment. He has a duffle bag full of money, with a 9 mm pistol laid on top of all that money. The television in the hotel room turns on, as the man stares into the television set. DUAL HALO!!! "You got to respect the supreme cruelty of March Madness." In the empty Arena Monterrey in Mexico, only the looming menace of the Dual Halo keeps the voice from echoing from one side of the venue to the other. The words vibrate just slightly against the plexiglass walls, giving them a far more booming effect than any microphone can provide. "Sixty-something teams go in...Only one leaves happy. The Halo's a lot like that." The source of the voice stands at the base of the Halo, staring up at the metal balcony on the second level. The faint light in the arena glistens off the green-and-gold satin-style robe hanging loosely on his shoulders. Besides that and a few glimpses of his red hair, we don't see much more of the man. "Eighth place out of fifty-something guys in 2006 isn’t too bad. Neither is second place out of the same number in 2007. Problem is, this is a black-and-white industry. If you aren't the guy getting his music played as the crowd files out of the building, you may as well have been the first guy tossed. One winner, fifty-something losers." He takes off the robe and tosses it to the side, where it hangs on a barricade to the side of the Halo. After bouncing back and forth a few times, he heads toward the door. "You go into this match looking for your legacy. Second-place doesn't get remembered. No, the only way to immortality is to be the last man standing...the sole survivor." Shrouded mostly in shadows, the man steps inside the Dual Halo, and the door slams shut behind him. The television turns off, and the man removes the hood off of his head. It's PosterBoy one of the 40 men who will be included in this crazy match. Poster Boy put's his face in his hands and takes a deep breath in and slowly exhales. He takes his face out of his hands, and closes the duffle bag. He gets up from the bed with duffle bag in hand, and slowly walks to the table that is in the middle of a small kitchen. He throws the duffle bag on top of the table and takes a seat in a wooden chair. He reaches deep down in his pocket of his hood and pulls out a pack of Newport cigarettes. He pat's the pack down, opens the wrap and box and takes one out with his mouth. He closes the box up and put's it back into his hoody. He light's it up with a lighter that was laying on the middle of the table. He takes a couple of drags from the cancer stick that kills millions of people each year. "So a dual halo open invitational match, Isn’t that something. Oh! And the winner gets to sign with no other then the biggest fed around PRIME. That sound so tempting, there is no way I can turn down such an offer like that. I mean this is PRIME, all the big names wrestled in that very ring, such as Killean Sirrajin, Ian English, Ignatius Lisieux and Tony Rolo…and many others. This is it. This is my ticket out the hood. I need to step up to the plate and show everyone that I have what it takes to come out victorious." He say's while he continues to smoke his cigarette. (Cell Phone Rings) …RING… …RING… …RING… "Hello." Poster Boy answers. "Yo! What's up Jay, are you ready to do this?" The other man asks on the other side of the phone. "Ready, as I will ever be." He replies back. "Good…good. That's what I like to hear." The man replies back to Poster Boy. "When we going to do this?" Poster Boy asks. "I’m coming to pick you up in 10 minutes I just got on the highway." He says. "Okay. Honk your horn when you’re out front and I will come out." Poster Boy replies. "No problem homie." (End) Poster Boy get's up from the wooden seat put's out his cigarette and walks up to the mini fridge that is in the hotel room and pulls out a 40 0z. He opens it and begins to guzzle it down. He walks back to the chair and sits down. He open's the duffle bag reaches in and grabs the 9 mm that was in the bag. He lays the gun on the table and closes up the duffle bag. He grabs the gun takes it off safety and cocks it back. He then stuffs it in the back of his pants. He puts his hand over his face and begins to think… BLAM BLAM BLAM BLAM We see Poster Boy kneeling on the ground over his dead cousin’s body. Poster Boy holding his cousin close to him begins to speak to him knowing these are the last words that they will probably speak to each other. “Don’t worry….Everything it going to be all right junior.” Poster Boy says’s as he tries to calm his cousin down. “I…..I…..I don’t want to die.” Junior mumbles out of his mouth. “Don’t worry you’re going to a better place junior.” He tells him. Sirens in the background begin to get closer as the eye’s of his cousins closes one last time. HONK HONK HONK HONK Poster Boy snaps out of his horrible nightmare that he will never forget. He gets up from the wooden chair, grabs the black duffle bag and heads towards out the door of his hotel room to meet his friend down stairs. The camera fades to black as the door closes. **************************************************************************************************************************** 1 HOUR LATER **************************************************************************************************************************** The scene open’s back up in an abandon alley way behind a building. We see a black expedition slowly entering the alley way. It’s stops and the camera cut’s into the truck. We see Poster Boy sitting in the passenger seat and his friend AR (Assault Rifle) sitting in the driver seat. Poster Boy and AR grew up with each other and knew each other since they were young. They use to talk about how they were going to live the glamour life and get rich quick but none of that has happen yet……… “Yo! This is the building right here.” AR tells Poster Boy. “Are you sure he is in here? I want this to be a 123 thing. Drop off the money gets the stuff and bounce...” He says. “Yeah, that’s exactly what it’s going to be 123.” AR explains to him. “AR……….Before we go in here there is something I need to tell you.” Poster Boy says’s with a look of concern on his face. “What happen blood?” AR tells him. “This right here is going to be it.” He say’s “What you mean, this right here is going to be it?” AR asks. “All…..this right here. This is going to be my last deal and I’m out. I’m tired of having to look behind my back when I walk down the street. I’m tired of having to sleep with one eye open and my glock in my had. I’m just tired. This is the same way I lost my cousin junior….so once we do this, that it I’m done. There need to be another way.” Poster Boy explains to AR. “Look man I know you are still mad about losing your cousin junior, but that’s the way shit goes. You know how the street’s is. If I can’t get you ima get your man. But that isn’t any reason for you to bitch out on me and stay fuck everything. Everything we hustled for…everything we grinded for…throw everything away like that. Nah man. Ima keep riding till I D.I.E.” AR say’s with a smile on his face. “Yeah…what ever man. Let’s just hurry up and do this shit. Where they going to be at? He asks? “Well they are going to meet us on the roof of the building.” AR tells him. “The Roof…what kind of shit is that? Sound funny man. You’re ready to go?” “As ready as I will ever be.” He says. They give each other a handshake and check there glocks. Poster Boy puts on some black gloves and grabs the black duffle bag with all the money. They both get out the car and close their door. They walk towards the back entrance of the building and enter. They walk towards the elevator and press the top floor (22). The elevator finally stops at the top floor and opens it door. Poster Boy and AR come out of the elevator and walk up a flight of stairs that lead you to the roof top. AR opens the door and they walk out onto the roof. The roof door slams behind them and they see two Cuban guy’s on the roof. Both men wearing bright colorful suits, both also have their pistol in hand. They all make contact with one another….. “So you are AR…The man who names himself after an Assault Rifle.” He say’s as both Cuban men begin to laugh. “Man, $hut the f*ck up. We aren’t here to have a conversation, we are here for business.” AR say’s with a serious look on his face. “Hey…man calm down no need for that type of language I was only playing around.” The Cuban guy’s explains to himself. “Man I aint here for games. Give me the stuff so we can go, I have a wife at home that I need to satisfy. I told her I was going to the store.” AR say’s. “Do you have the money?” He asks? “Yeah…We got the money. Toss over the stuff and we will toss you the money.” AR say’s The Cuban man tosses a book bag to AR, AR opens the book bag and finds over a quarter pound of marijuana. AR looks at Poster Boy. Poster Boy tossed the book bag towards the two Cuban guys’. One of them walks towards the duffle bag and grabs it. Kneels to the floor opens it and see’s all the money. He smiles and looks at his Cuban friend just in time to see a bullet pierce his skull from AR’s glock 9. BLAM…. His friend rises from his knees and points the gun at AR. AR tries to shoot the 1st shot but his processed glock jams on him. “F8CK” AR say’s as a bullet pierces his skull from the already upset Cuban. Poster Boy see’s his friend drop right in front of him. Poster Boy takes of his glock 9 mm and begins to shoot at the Cuban guy who has no turned his back and his now running towards the roof door with the money. Poster Boy catches him in the shoulder while he was running. Poster Boy run’s up to his body; the Cuban is still alive breathing heavily. Poster Boy pistol wipes him and grabs the duffle bag full of money and goes back into the building toward the elevator. “You fu8king spick.” The Cuban guy mumbles out his mouth while the blood continues to trickle down the side of his mouth. The elevator finally makes it to the top floor. He enters the elevator and presses the bottom floor the door closes and as it closes he takes off his black gloves opens the duffle bag and throws it in there including the gun. He closes the bag and stands up. The elevator stop’s at the 5th floor and in comes an elderly lady. She enters the elevator and presses the 4th floor. Poster Boy looks around and cant believe it……… ”Hey how you doing sonny?” The old lady asks as she turns around. “I’m doing fine, thanks for asking.” Poster Boy answers’s with a sarcastic tone of voice. The elevator door closes, and goes down one floor and opens back up for the elderly women. “Have a nice day sonny.” She says as she exits the elevator. “Yeah…yeah whatever.” Poster Boy replies as he presses the elevator close button nearly catching the lady. The elevator door closes and finally makes it to the 1st floor. He walks towards the back exit, walks to the truck and opens the driver’s door. He tosses the bag of money in the passenger seat. He turns on the car, and puts in a cd in the CD player “Kayne West-You can’t tell me nothing” begins to play as you can hears sirens get closer. Poster Boy see’s a bum looking in the garbage, Poster Boy opens the duffle bag and grabs a stack of money and tosses it to the bum as he pulls off and the camera fades. “THANK YOU……………” The man yells out. -Fin.
