Control Issues

21 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/21/09 “Are we there yet?” Tyler asks, sounding exactly like a whiny seven year old. The King of Greed sits in the backseat of the car, his chin propped up on the palm of his hand and his forehead resting against the window. He looks tired and defeated. They had been driving around all day long looking at houses. And by driving around and looking I mean that literally; Tyler had refused to get out of the car and actually go in many of the houses. None of them met his high standards for a place of residence, although they were all in his meager price range. Before taking his vacation to Club Fed, Tyler Nelson was used to living in palatial mansions with hundreds of rooms and acres of land. He had tennis and basketball courts, swimming pools groundskeepers, and any number of other amenities. He lived like a king; perhaps better than some monarchs. Those days were long gone, however, and Tyler knew he needed to come to grips with that. That didn’t mean it was going to be easy. The graphite metallic Nissan Altima pulls into the driveway of a one story ranch home with a single car garage. Tyler leans to his left so that he can see out the windshield, peeking between the two front seats. He sighs heavily as he gazes upon the house and how unappealing it seems to him. The real estate agent, Rita Hanson, turns and looks over her right shoulder. She forces a smile on her face, almost grimacing from the effort. “Well, what do you think?” she asks. Rita’s dirty blonde hair was pulled tightly into a bun earlier this morning, but now some strands are starting to come loose and dangle freely. As she looks into Tyler’s eyes, she peers through a pair of professional looking designer frame glasses. “Is this the guest house?” Tyler quips. Rita hangs her head and sighs. Mr. Simmons, who is seated in the front passenger seat, squirms as he struggles to turn around. He cranes to see his boss over the headrest of his seat. “It wouldn’t hurt to at least go in and take a look, you know.” The head of Tyler’s one man security team tries to provide a voice of reason. Tyler protests for the sake of protesting. “That looks like a place where my gardener would stay, Simmons! I’m not a Goddamn gardener! I’m an American citizen for God’s sake!” “I fully realize that, Tyler, but you don’t really have a lot of options. I know you know you can’t afford much more than this, so you need to just suck it up and start looking at some of these.” The Greediest Player in the Game, who at last check was a grown-up, folds his arms and juts his lower lip out in a full blown pout. Rita rolls her eyes and looks at Simmons. The bodyguard gently waves his hand, motioning for Rita to get out of the car. “Can you just give us a second, please? We’ll be up in a minute.” Rita grabs a binder and her purse before climbing out of the driver’s seat. She shuts the door behind her and walks toward the house. “Aren’t you tired of living out of a suitcase?” Simmons asks his boss. “I know I damn sure am. You’ve only gone into a handful of these houses today, and I think even then it was only to use the bathroom. You have to get over the fact that you can’t live how you used to.” “I know that!” Tyler barks. “Don’t you think I know that?” “Then why are you making this so hard?” “I’m better than this. We both know that. There is no way that I should have to live in these substandard conditions. I’M TYLER FUCKING NELSON!” Simmons nervously looks around, making sure that there weren’t any children nearby. Simmons knew that Tyler’s huge ego was the biggest reason for his reluctance. Unfortunately for the security guard, there wasn’t much he could do about that. He’s known Tyler for quite a long time, and one of the things that Tyler hated the most was not being in control. Without a substantial bankroll at his disposal, Tyler had little control over anything right now. “You’ve been a little more on edge than usual this week. Wouldn’t have anything to do with your match on 207, would it?” Tyler twitches angrily in his seat, his upper lip quivering in disgust. “Oh, you mean the curtain jerker against Douglas? That one? It might have a little to do with that. I’m the number one contender for the biggest title in PRIME and she’s got me working a glorified dark match? There is no way I should be opening up shows. Do you realize how much that affects my pay? ” “Isn’t that what you would do?” “Don’t try and reverse psychologize me.” Tyler points an irritated finger at Simmons. “You’re not a shrink and I don’t need one. You’re supposed to be a yes-man, dammit. Act like one.” The bodyguard looks over his right shoulder and sees Rita standing in front of the house, tapping her foot. “Can we at least just go inside this one? I think we owe Rita that for putting up with you all day.” Nelson scowls at Simmons, but he’s probably right. Besides, Rita wasn’t half bad looking. Maybe if he played his cards right, Tyler thought, he just might get to have a main event of his own against the cougar. “Did you see how she was looking at me earlier?” The King of Greed looks up at the impatiently waiting real estate agent. “Like she wanted to vomit?” “No, before that.” A slight smirk of confidence sneaks across the lips of Tyler. “She was giving me the eye…right after breakfast.” He’s always fancied himself a hit with the ladies. But then again, he always had money for the ladies to swoon over. “I think she was looking for you to leave the tip.” Simmons jostles around in his seat once more so that he can get out of the car. He opens Tyler’s door for him, and the two men head up to the house. Rita gets an almost genuine smile on her face, pleased that Tyler is actually going to look inside the house. The three of them disappear inside.
