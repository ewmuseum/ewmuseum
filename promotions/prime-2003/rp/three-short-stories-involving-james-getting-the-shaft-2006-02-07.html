Three Short Stories Involving James Getting The Shaft

7 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 7th, 2006 So, here it is. D-Day. H-Hour. S-Second. Or something. Whatever it is, it’s here, and that’s all you need to know. Right about now, you might be asking yourself a couple questions. Mainly, why have I chosen to go back to my old monologue intro when the only time I ever won using it was when the other guy no-showed? I’ll tell you why. You see, rather than pull a Simpsons and completely disregard pertinent information, I prefer to educate my fans as much as possible, so that they may fully enjoy what I write without having to worry about little (in the case of The Simpsons, re: gigantic) pieces of the plot seemingly not fitting together. That, and the last time I figured something was common knowledge, I got knocked out of a tournament. That’s right, I’m Bret Harting this shit. And if anyone refers to it as anything other than The Incident from this point onward, I’ll punch you in the eye. With that said, perhaps now you’ll understand why I’m about spoon-feed you every tidbit of info, no matter how truly insignificant, until your eyes bleed and your soul begs for mercy. And then I’ll sleep with the closest living female relation to you. Because that’s how I roll, etcetera, etcetera. Oh, look at the time. Monologue’s over now, and with just too little time left to let you all know why I’m back to doing it again. What a shame. … Yeah, I’m just way too lazy to be bitter. Plot holes, ahoy! Narcolepsy: More Of A Bitch Than Your Mother-In-Law He just closed his eyes. He could feel the fogginess of the dream world settling over his brain. He could hear the voices of parents he couldn’t fully remember anymore. He could smell something baking in the kitchen. He embraced this bitter happiness. KERR-ASH! He could kill Clyde for waking him up again. Bang! James’ left eye slowly peeked open, secretly wishing that the noises coming from downstairs involved Clyde being stabbed by someone big and angry. Thud! His other eyelid quickly lifted, a look of shock and awe! on his face. That last one had sounded suspiciously like the antique bookshelf he inherited from his aunt a few years back. Other Assorted Noises! Sleep wasn’t coming back for a while, so James did what any sensible man would do when placed in his position. He got out of bed, put some clothes on, and pulled a shotgun out of the closet. Whether it was someone in the house, or Clyde screwing around at three in the morning, somebody was getting shot. Farwell leveled the shotgun and began walking down the stairs in his robe, slippers and nightcap, resembling what you’d expect Ward Beaver to look like if he finally just up and lost it one night. A twisted grin on his face, he took each stair with a deliberate patience, soaking in the precursor to murder. As another probably-somewhat-expensive object crashed to the floor, he even went as far as to start singing a little tune to himself. Farwell: If you go down in the woods today, you’re sure of a big surprise. If you go down in the woods today, you’d better go in disguise. For, every bear that ever there was, shall get shot in the ass, because today’s the day that I KILL YOUR BITCH ASS DEAD! Swinging around the corner, James shouted out the last line while unloading a blast into the far wall, clipping Clyde with a bullet as the PRIME Universal Champion slid into the kitchen after trying to run away. Holding the back of his upper thigh as blood dripped through the tear in his pants, Clyde sat with his back against the wall, pleading with James to stop. Walkins: Don’t shoot! It’s me, Clyde! A second shot hit the wall just inches above Clyde’s head. Farwell: Oops. Sorry about that, Clyde. Did I hit you? James waited a few seconds before asking again. Farwell: Clyde? You alright, buddy? Again, no answer. James pumped his fist in hushed victory, before setting the gun down to go check out his kill. Big mistake. There was no one on the other side of the wall, and just as James realized he’d been duped, Clyde jumped out in front of him, wearing a hockey mask while brandishing a machete. The blade plunged into Farwell’s chest, forcing him to spew blood all over the mask and front of Clyde’s shirt, and the last thing he heard was a voice from behind the bloodstained mask. Walkins: I’m Jason Voorhees, bitch! And the last thing James thought of before he closed his eyes wasn’t that he’d just been killed, nor was it wondering how he’d let himself be fooled so easily, and it wasn’t even regret over the fact that he was murdered by Clyde. No. The final thought he had was that Jason isn’t supposed to talk. Funny how things go, isn’t it? Of course, it’d be a lot funnier if it wasn’t just a dream. But, then again, I don’t think I’ll need to make that leap over to killing off characters in order to keep things fresh until about the second or third year of writing this stuff. … Oh. Right. Well, looks like the above scenario isn’t too far off now. Sorry, James, but you are the weakest link. Start dying, dammit! Too Clyde For TV~! James knew he shouldn’t really be mad at Clyde for killing him. After all, it was only a dream, and he had shot Clyde first. But, still…something about being shanked by that dumb son of a bitch pissed him off more than his gruff demeanor let on. In laymen’s (Kyle Lamen, that is) terms: the smile on his face wasn’t a smile, but a grimace forged from within the deepest depths of his black soul. He shoveled a spoonful of Rice Krispies into his mouth while glaring across the table at Clyde, who responded with a wide grin, revealing a traffic accident of breakfast cereals in his throat. James cringed, the hate in his heart growing ever stronger, but Clyde kept on staring at him and smiling. It was getting kind of creepy. Finally, James had enough. There was only so much one man could take before breakfast. Farwell: Ok, Clyde, what is it? What retarded surprise do you have in store for me today? No, wait--don’t tell me! Let me guess…you rented our house out to a band of gypsies in exchange for tickets to a circus that doesn’t even exist! No, no…that’s way too intelligent for you. You probably did something even stupider, like volunteer the two of us to be male prostitutes, where fantastic hijinks promptly ensue as we try and fulfill the nonsexual fantasies of our clients in order to not catch an STD or something. I don’t know…I fell asleep halfway through the movie. James stopped himself once he looked at Clyde and saw an even more vacant stare than usual. A roll of the eyes preceded James’ attempt at giving it the old college try for a second time. Farwell: Just…tell me what you’ve done, so I can at least expect it somewhat when it happens. I promise not to be mad, I just don’t want to fall into anywhere or get hit by anything today. Please, Clyde…you owe me this much. Walkins: I got us on Springer! Clyde beamed as he shouted at James in delight, nearly deafening him. Farwell: Wait…you did what?! Walkins: Well, I figured with everything we’ve been through lately, and all our problems, we could use a little help. Farwell: And that’s why you decided to take us on the worst show in the world, so that a bunch of inbred hillbillies can have something new to jerk themselves off over? Clyde nodded. Walkins: But, I had to tell him we were gay, or it was no deal. So, flame it up a bit, alright? James’ jaw dropped open in disbelief. Him, gay? Ha! That would be like calling Liberace straight. Farwell: Forget that. We are not posing as a gay couple. First off, we’re not gay. Well, at least I’m not. You’re about as queer as a Martin Lawrence Oscar nomination. Hell, if someone looked up the word ‘gay’ in the dictionary, they’d see your face beside the definition. Cut to a dictionary being opened, its pages flipping from one side to the other, gradually slowing to a stop at the requested page as the view scrolled down to the word ‘gay’. gayadj. gay•er, gay•est 1. Showing or characterized by cheerfulness and lighthearted excitement; merry.2. Enjoys a good cock now and again. And beside both bullets was Clyde’s face done in Che Guevara-style outline. Back to The Forsaken. Farwell: Furthermore, Jerry Springer hasn’t been cool since the last millennium. In the world of entertainment, Springer is about as respected as Courtney Love is sexually attractive. There’s no way in hell I’m doing his show. Walkins: Oh, I think I can change your mind… From under the table, Clyde produced a bottle of chloroform and damp rag, a maniacal look in his eye. Slowly raising the items, he got up out of his chair and approached James, reaching out to him. Walkins: If you come with me on the show, I’ll give you this bottle of chloroform and wet cloth. Three dollar value, yours free. Farwell: No. Walkins: Plan B. Commence the drugging! Farwell: Wha?! Before James could react, Clyde covered his face with the smelly rag, streaking chloroform across his lips and in his mouth. Finally succumbing to the disgusting piece of tarnished fabric, James dropped face-first into his cereal, while Clyde placed his hands on his hips and smiled proudly. A job well done. Eight hours later… A groggy James Farwell slowly came to, the ache in his skull subsiding as his blurred vision cleared up enough to allow him to take in his surroundings. Farwell: Wh-where am I? Have I been raped? Seated beside him, Clyde’s voice pierced James’ eardrum like a thousand tiny shards of really obnoxious glass. Walkins: Only by the low, low prices found at Orbitz.com! Orbitz: The Freshmaker! He rubbed the back of his head. This had to be some insane dream. And were they on an airplane? Farwell: Oh, god…I feel sick. Where are we? Walkins: We’re on a private jet commissioned by PRIME to take us to Springer! James looked around. Surely, this was no private jet. People sat all around them, some sick, some even looking like hobos. And…was that the sound of children crying? Son of a bitch! Farwell: This is no private jet! This is coach! Just then, a raggedy little orphan approached James, coughing into his scarf while tugging on the pant leg of the PRIME Tag Champion. Orphan: Spare some change, mister? James immediately pulled his leg away, looking down in horror and disgust. Farwell: Why are you even on a plane?! None of this is making any sense! One of the security guards on the plane saw the commotion and walked over to see what was going on. Leaning over Clyde’s seat, he watched James for a moment, then spoke in the direction of the Universal Champ. Security: Sir, is there a problem? Does your father need medication of some sort? Farwell: I’m not his father! Security: Sorry. Does your grandfa- Farwell: Say it, and I’ll cut you! Brandishing one of the plastic knives from the two meals Clyde had greedily eaten for himself while James lay unconscious next to him, Farwell took a few swipes at the guard before being felled by a blast from a stun gun to the nipple. He shook violently in his seat, then settled back into a mild coma, while Clyde thanked the guard for his help. All in a day’s work, he said. All in a day’s work, indeed. Three Hours Later…In The Green Room… Farwell: Ow…my head. Where am I? Have I been raped again? James massaged his aching skull, but the pain numbed when he looked over and saw what was on the television in the corner of the room. The Jerry Springer Show set, and Clyde seated on the stage. At the bottom of the screen, the caption read: My Gay Lover Treats Me Bad Because I’m Better Than Him. Farwell: Son. Of. A. Bitch. Outside the green room, the rest of the world (well, at least the parts of it that were related to their sexual partners) watched as Jerry Springer did the recap and introduced James to a chorus of boos from the white trash audience. Springer: Our guest today says that his gay lover treats him badly because he’s not as successful. They now fight all the time, and don’t even sleep in the same bed anymore. It’s gotten so bad, there has even been some physical abuse involved. But, as if that wasn’t enough, our guest told me just moments ago that both he and his lover were raised as brothers since childhood. The hicks jeered on the unknown lover even louder, while Clyde sat there and looked pleased with what he’d done. Springer: Ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce to you…James Farwell! Generic Springer music hit, bringing James into view as some women in the crowd stood up and shouted profanities at him. James responded in kind. Farwell: Bitch! You don’t tell me what to do! This is my body, and I LOVE showing it off! Butch feminine voice and all, Farwell licked his finger and slapped his ass, tying his shirt in a knot while grinding the set. Farwell: Oh, yeah! You like that? Take a good look, ‘cause it’s the closest you’ll ever get to this! James grinded some more. Farwell: Uh-huh! Uh-huh! You wouldn’t know what to do with m-…wait, what the fuck am I doing? Seeing James snap out of the white trash trance, Clyde knew he had little time with which to entirely win over the crowd. The card he held up his sleeve was placed upon the table. Walkins: He shot me in the ass! Everyone started booing. Clyde smiled, for he knew he had won. Farwell: That doesn’t count! It was in a dream! Crowd: Slut! Slut! Slut! Slut! James had enough. In a fit of rage, he grabbed the nearest thing he could find, and hurled it at Clyde. The object being a chair. Which Clyde ducked. And subsequently hit Jerry. Which caused Steve to leap into action. And subsequently hit James. Then, all was black. Shreaky Saturday Farwell: Do you know how many times I’ve been rendered unconscious in 2006 alone, thanks to you? Walkins: Well, I know it was twelve in 1962, so…zero? Farwell: Fourteen times, and that’s not counting the Sabbath. I’m required by law to write off any and all unconsciousness on that day as spiritual meditation. It’s how I retain my status as a church, but that’s neither here nor there. Walkins: Ah. So, that’s why you have all those homeless people over on Wednesday nights. You probably wash their feet down in that basement, don’t you? Farwell: No, I drug them and sell them to the butcher for meat, but thanks for the alibi. Clyde didn’t know whether to take that as a joke or not. After all, James had been acting awfully strange lately, seemingly more tense than usual. And then there was that rash of missing people in the area. Could James really be the Slum Side Stalker? Clyde had to find out more, and there was only one way to do that. Walkins: Quick, James! Touch my ball. Instinctively, James drew his hand back. Walkins: You have to touch it, or it won’t work properly! Farwell: Jesus! No! Moving further away from Clyde, James looked for a way out. The stairs were the only place he could go in time to get away, and Clyde began positioning himself in front of the only exit within reach. Clyde lunged forward, James hopped to the side and hit the ground running, taking the stairs three at a time. Puffing on the floor, Clyde tilted his head back and watched James disappear into his room. Walkins: Your hand shall caress my ball before the day is out! Mark my words! Sometime Later… Walkins: James, I’m done with the ball thing now, so you can come out of your room. I baked you a blueberry pie! Farwell: I’m allergic to blueberries! Clyde rammed himself up against the door, trying to force it open. Walkins: Please! Just give it a little squeeze! Even just a tickle. Farwell: I’ll never touch it! Never! Walkins: Palm my ball! A scraping sound alarmed James, causing him to jump out of bed and rush over to the door, where Clyde attempted to jam the pie underneath it. Walkins: Just have a little taste of my ball pie! Unbeknownst to Clyde - what, with the lower intellect and all - James’ door didn’t lock. It simply opened outwards, the house having been built by crack addicts in the ‘70s, back when they tried to make druggies work for their fix instead of just handing it to them, and on an ancient retarded Indian burial ground. James kicked the door open, the wood connecting with the side of Clyde’s face, sending him back down the stairs and out of James’ hair for the time being. The blueberry pie lay crushed on the floor. Some More Time Later… Clyde hadn’t tried to make James touch his ball for the last few hours. Maybe he’d given up. Or, perhaps he was planning something even more sinister. Maybe he was still out cold at the bottom of the stairs. It had been a while since James last checked on him, after all. But, a knocking on his bedroom door soon revealed the retard’s whereabouts. Farwell: I already told you, I’m not touching your ball. Walkins: Please, James. The old Chinese lady said we both had to touch it for the magic to work. I need to see how the other half lives. Farwell: What the hell are you talking about? What old lady? Walkins: The old lady that gave it to me. James couldn’t make heads or tails of this shit. Farwell: So, you got a magic ball from some old Chinese lady? Is that what happened? Walkins: Yes! And for us to switch places, we both need to touch the aforementioned magic ball. Farwell: Why the hell would I want to change places with you? That’s like trading a Cadillac in for a Mazda. Walkins: Because! Just go along with it, ok? Farwell: No! Back in the hole with you. James kicked the door open again, cracking Clyde in the forehead and sending him toppling backwards down the stairs. Farwell: Peace and quiet, at last. A Little While Longer… James peeked his head outside his door. Clyde was nowhere to be seen. Good. James needed to take a piss, but there was no way he was willing to risk trading places with Clyde, even if it meant peeing out the window and onto the neighbor’s lawn. Again. Checking both ends of the hall, James sprinted towards the bathroom, counting down the steps until he was safely inside. The door locked, and the piss was promptly taken after he searched the area for any signs of Clydemydia. His eye caught sight of the last few squares of toilet paper left on the roll, and he thanked the sweet lord that he didn’t have to take a dump. Once he opened the door, however, all relief left his body as he came face to face with his arch nemesis, tag team partner, and brother all rolled into one. Farwell: So…we meet again. Walkins: Don’t worry, I’m unarmed. I busted my ball when I fell down the stairs that last time. A sad look on his face, Clyde pouted and held out his hands to show James the two halves of the ball, the magic fading from it before his very eyes. Walkins: I’m here now on a mission of peace and friendship. In order to restore relations on both sides of the border, I must utilize your facilities to the fullest extent of my being. Farwell: Fine. Whatever. Just don’t take a shit, because we’ve only got three squares of toilet paper left, and I’m not going back out to the store tonight. Walkins: Don’t worry, James. I’ve seen the commercials. Is it Charmin? Farwell: I…I think so. Walkins: Then, three squares are more than enough to tackle my toughest messes! A bear told me so. Pushing past James, Clyde shut the door behind him, the lock popping into place. For the first time that night, James felt happy at finally having won even the tiniest of battles against Clyde. Six minutes elapsed, and then James heard Clyde’s panicked voice from the bathroom. Walkins: Jaaaaaaames. Grumbling to himself while he put a coat on, James grabbed the car keys off the kitchen counter and stepped out the front door into the cold and dark night. He’d been bested again.
