Lessons in Passing

4 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 4th ''You went to fWo, you were a fighter! There was nothing you couldn't do, nobody you couldn't beat... until you let that 'hero' pick you up. I'd like to show you a trend, Amy...'' He was elderly - maybe eighty and laboring for breath but, for some reason, his words were strong. He was an image from her childhood... someone she'd never forget. He'd lived through two wars and dedicated his life to fighting conformity until the very last minute. The first and last person she respected. ''You made friends there, didn't you?'' he spat, ''Some valet, these big men who thought they were much more than they'll ever be. You were the youngest, most nimble and cunning of all of them... but what happened? You fell in love. You got married. You got pregnant; You peetered out. You went from main event to the girl with the cute pants, you failed.'' You failed. ''You came to PRIME, slow starting, and then gained intense momentum when you pushed that second 'love' away. You became focused on what you know keeps you alive - the fighting spirit you've had since birth. You're not a social creature by nature, Amy... why can't you learn that?! You're angry, you're aggressive and brash. You're difficult to get along with, and you're a fighter. You're not meant to talk or kiss, you're meant to kick and scream through hoardes of lesser individuals who live in the web you keep getting caught in. You fail when you have a human life - your drugs, love, friends, money... didn't you notice that? Once you're living through those chemicals, those people and ideals, you become sloppy... too human; NOTHING.'' ''It's n---'' ''It is like that. You let your head go and you lose. You lose to Karina, you lose to the Trashcan Man and whoever else comes along. Drugs, friends, 'love'; Weaknesses, and little more. You're only meant to do one thing on this earth, Amelia... the purpose of your existance, the 'meaning of life' - it's to fight. Kill or be killed, your job isn't to entertain the masses - it's to stand against all odds and be the strongest person prevailing through a sea of bodies that dance to some twisted little life game. That makes you the best woman, person, human being that you can be. To fight and to prevail... not to get caught up in the nuances of life that destroy anybody under you.'' ''But grandpa, I...'' ''But nothing. I'm not your grandfather, I'm your subconscious. I'm you. But it figures, you never could tell the difference between a real man and a dream...'' Train. Fight. Rise. She woke up in a cold, unrelenting sweat that threatened to send her shivering away if she left the comfort of those damp covers. The images would soon fade, but they were burned into memory now. Just in case, she reached to the bedside table and retrieved her journal... but that was far more than a dream, so much more than those little pages could hold. Amy had taught herself a life lesson that was far beyond the capabilities of her conscious self; How to be Aimz, how to survive in this race and how not to live. It had been forced, but that only meant it was time to listen to herself - not the candy-coated shell, but what lingered beneath in who she truly was. She was filled with purpose as much as sadness at the image of her grandfather that would teach those lessons, but rose past that to sleep again... letting that eerie message simmer menacingly in her psyche while the promise of a two week vacation from PRIME smiled in front of her. Rest. Recover. Get your sorry ass ready...
