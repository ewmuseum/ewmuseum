The Explanation (Finding a G/f Part III)

15 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/15/05 The Explanation Sitting here battered and bruised, I think to myself did I do the right thing? I mean last week I gave the girl back, but it was at the expense of someone’s job. Now I have forced Brandon Youngblood from PRIME, how is he expected to pay for his family? How is he supposed to get another career? Should I even be worrying about such a thing? I don’t know but to some degree I feel bad. Maybe the only reason I gave her back was I know how it feels to want something and not get what you want in return. Maybe the real reason of my actions comes from my Melissa, the girl who I had sent a personal invitation to come to the show and see as my guest but decided not to show. Was I wrong to take my aggression out on Brandon Youngblood and Melissa Garvin? Probably but what is done is done, the real problem I am face with now is, where is my Melissa? Why did she not come to the show? Maybe she had to work, maybe she had previous plans. Or maybe she just didn’t want to see me. At this point I can see myself slowly falling further and further into depression. The multitudes of reasons she did not show are flying about a mile of minute, each one of them is a little more drastic and unbelievable then the first one. Until I finally try and make myself believe that she didn’t make it because of something drastic, or even worse some sort of death, not hers of course but maybe a great aunt or uncle or something. My mind is spinning off into a world I don’t like. Although she is all I can think about I can not build up the nerve to call her and confront her about her absence from the event. I guess all I can do is wait and see what happens next time we meet, hoping on one level that the thoughts in my head are all wrong, but yet on a lower more sadistic level hoping she has a great excuse and maybe death is the only acceptable one. Luckily my phone started to vibrate in my pocket, maybe the girl is making the first strive to speak with me, or is she will calling making me have the nerve to speak with her. Pulling the phone out from my pocket I look at the caller I.D., then under my breath I murmur, “Incoming Call.” The only time that happens is when the number is blocked, it has to be her. I hate when this happens, the turmoil between answering and not answering are tearing me apart. “Hello,” I answered not confidently, but hoping it was her, otherwise knowing I should have allowed it to go to voicemail. But I knew if I missed her call and she went to voicemail I once again would be in the same boat as before, trying to get the courage to call her. “Hello Peter;” the innocent voice of the girl I had been awaiting for rang though my ears as if I had just heard the most peaceful song ever written. She continued on, “I’m sorry I was unable to make it to your event.” The eagerness of hearing her voice does not succumb his anger for not telling she was not showing up. He tries to hide his anger when he responds to her, “It’s not a big deal, I am sure you have a reason; you had to work or there was a death in the family or something like that.” As much as I tried to not show any anger it was next to impossible. “Well yes, I had to work. I’m sorry I couldn’t make it and I will make it up to you Peter.” She spoke the words in a rushed manner, making it obvious she sensed the anger in my tone. This made her voice quiver just a little at the sounds of certain words, I noticed she had taken a grasp to my tone and played it for what I could. I started breathing a little easier, now I knew the real reason why she didn’t show up. “That is fine, thank you for at least considering coming to see me at work.” “I really am sorry, Peter. There was no way of getting out of it before the show. I do hope you are not mad at me. I will be watching this week on the Playboy Channel to see you.” I could tell she was starting to breathe easier; her voice was calming down and becoming peaceful like it always was. I could sense the actual sincerity in her voice, making my blood pressure slowly drop and making me a happier person, but the one thing that still bothered me was she didn’t let me know she was not going to appear. “How come you never called? I mean you didn’t show up and that is not a big deal, but the problem I had was I didn’t even hear from you to let me know you weren’t coming.” “Peter, I am sorry. Things go busy and I was unable to get in touch with you, that was something I felt shouldn’t have been left on a voicemail.” It was easier for me to tell as she spoke her words that her honesty was coming through. In all actuality it made me want her a little more. Isn’t it sometimes strange how things happen like that? I had to try and play it out like it was no big deal, but deep inside I was still a little angry. “Melissa, it’s ok I just hope to get to see you next time I am back in Massachusetts, hopefully after Wednesday I will be able to get sometime off and come and see you.” I was now opening myself up to see if she really wanted a relationship or if I was just something she wanted on the side. “Peter, I would like that. I have to be running, give me a call later in the week when you know what time you are going to be back around.” Her voice made things all better; I had gone from a raging lunatic to a man in love again all with in a matter of minutes. “Melissa, thank you. I will get in touch with you later on.” I had finally shown the real me to her, she was now one of the few that had ever been let into my feelings, but I think that was just a trait she carried with her beautiful smile and sense of honesty. “Thank you, for what?” I could tell she honestly did not know what I was thanking her for, but I did want to leave it at that. “One day you will understand baby, don’t you worry. Don’t work to hard.” With that I ended our conversation, the dial tone was something I did not want to hear as I could have spoken with her all day. Knowing our time was growing thin on the phone it was best to end it there before we got into anything else. I guess it was for the better.
