A Thousand Words: I

8 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/08/06 CLICK. I’m gonna be trying something new that my therapist wanted me to try out for a few weeks. Should I like it, I’ll stick with it. Should I not, this fucker’s going in the trashcan. He wanted me to record my thoughts when I finally snag myself a moment of solitude. Naturally, I’m a busy guy. The professional wrestling business is something that’s been here for me my entire life. My grandfather and father – God rest their souls – did it. I’ve been doing it for just a little south of twenty years and counting. But… shit’s been getting to me. Real bad. It’s awful pretentious of me to say that I’m famous. Some know me on the street and ask for an autograph. Some don’t. Whatever, it’s their bag, not mine. However, I’ve made a pretty decent living in this business. I’ve made enough where if I were to retire right now, it wouldn’t be a problem. But I’ve got an itch for this business that keeps me doing what I do. And that itch is being conflicted by family. I’ve had so many wars of attrition with my own wife, Eme. Our relationship didn’t start on the greatest of notes. Hell, she was an easy fuck after a friend of mine in another federation didn’t like her. But we always had this bond to one another; it’s genuinely unexplainable. You don’t know why or you don’t know how, but it’s all there. You feel it in the deepest reaches of your heart and that everything just falls into place. …That and a condom broke or her pill was defective. Whichever. We hang out, we get along great, she becomes my best friend and knowing she’s the one person that I can confide in. We get married and have a baby girl, Gabrielle. We buy a nice place near her firm and she takes care of my child while we’re out on the road. But… She NAGS. All the goddamn time. I’m so sick of the fights. She begs me to stop what I’m doing CONSTANTLY. She begs me to come home and forget this silly dream of mine that leads me away from family for more than 2/3 of my year. It’s a crock. To put it bluntly, I’m sick and tired of being sick and tired. It’s not like I ask her to give up HER dreams. I don’t ask her to quit working at that stupid firm where there’s a bunch of single, lusting, perverted dominant fuckhead males that sneak a glance at her ass every time she reaches for the Brunstein Case in the bottom drawer. You’ve heard all the stupid lawyer jokes, but that’s all they do; they fuck you over or fuck you THEN fuck you over. I can’t even begin to count the number of times I’ve hung up the phone on account of a “bad connection,” quote, unquote. The fighting makes me sick. Sometimes… I wish I stayed abstinent. It was perfect. Of course, I had to give up the temptations of the flesh in order to continue with my business, but that was what I had to live with. I wouldn’t be one of those deadbeats that forget all about their kids while they mangle up three to four groupies at a time in their bedroom – that’s just not right. Luckily I’ve avoided all that. I’ve got great willpower, but just listening to her bitching… Sometimes, I wish I never met Eme at all. Stupid ring rats. A pox on all of you. CLICK. --------------------------------------------------- Taking a moment to admire the low, yet captivating amber hue of the setting sun from his deck, Sonny let out a low sigh before unwrapping a Snickers bar. From the heights of his deck on the twelfth story of the Holiday Inn, eyes continued to wander the world below. Most were concluding their business, others getting ready to take place in the tumultuous style known by many as the nightlife. Who knows what the fuck else they could be up to. Bet they didn’t have have problems… or if they did, they just hid shit like that real well. After three or so more bites, he simply pitched the wrapper over the side of the deck and watched it float lifelessly on currents of air. “Lucky mother…” That was all he mumbled to himself before the form of three bodies on the deck next to him took his attention. He eyed the family there, a mother and a father with a kid. The parents appeared to be incredibly young for such an undertaking, but when that kind of responsibility is thrust upon you, you take it no matter what. The boy in the family managed to spot Sonny in the corner of his eye and waved at him. What else could he do but wave back? He’s not a TOTAL asshole; A curmudgeon at best, maybe. The parents laughed before their eyes went back to the sight that was the sun setting over Atlanta. Silver was just about to retire back to the darker depths of his room, but stopped and did a double-take over to the parents; both had red hair, the kid was brown hair… and considerably darker than either parent. “Milkman baby…” he chortled to himself before going back in. Sure, it could’ve been an adopted baby, but why the hell would kids that appear THAT young adopt? All that crap aside, his day was over and he still had a day or two more before he had to report to the arena to work his match with Sun Tzu. Ah, yes. He heard many tales of the talented young girl from China with a mysterious “let’s get down to business” aura about her. She was undefeated thus far in wrestling and she clearly had the charisma and talent to go far. Shit, she already had a title around her waist not more than a month and a half into her PRIME tenure. And this world tour thing; this was going to be great. Sure, he’d wrestled occasionally in several different countries, but an entire world tour was just off the cha---WHOA, WHOA, WHOA! The fuck? How’d he go from silently lamenting about his problems with Eme to jumping back to wrestling? With another hard sigh, he sat on the edge of the bed while facing a blank TV screen. He gazed into arbitrary nothingness and let his mind travel again. Part of him knew why he was like this. Most people that come into this business are empowered with the idea of taking charge of their destinies when they leap out onto that brave new world 200 days out of the year… Sonny was OBSESSED with it. He knew he was. It almost broke his heart to see that his job gave him much more solidarity within himself and meaning than his own love, flesh, and blood ever have. He rolled over the bed to pull a Coke bottle out from the mini-bar next to him. Wrestling around his pocket until he found that specialized key chain that Eme got him for a welcome home gift (a metallic clock/bottle opener/knife gizmo that read, “Sonny, My Love” on the side) he pulled out the bottle opener and ripped it clean off before downing about a quarter of the bottle in a big gulp. Placing it on the nightstand, he figured he could possibly get out of shitty real life for an hour or two. Sonny got up and went for one of his bags. From it he procured a portable DVD player and the second season of House. He still had a few episodes to catch, but it was really getting good. Was Foreman going to live or die when it came to that fucked-up virus that already killed their initial patient? Several moments later after struggling with crap problems involving not being able to get the player to read the disc, Disc 6 of House, Season Two was on for display and for Sonny to finish. Anything House said would make him laugh. It always did. “There… Euphoria… Part Two…Play All…” Sonny set the DVD player on the nightstand and adjusted himself on the bed so he may finally finish out his season of one of his favorite shows (until Heroes started to show up on DVD. Then fuck House.) …………………………………….- -……………………………………. CLINK. CLINK. CLINK. CLINK. A lazy eye unhurriedly came to life with only the light of a portable gadget at its bedside. The once tranquil room now a cornucopia of annoying racket. Clinking, tapping, children laughing blissfully. The sounds around him made him jump up from his slumber. Maybe one of the neighbors was still up at this time of night. What fucking time was it, anyway? A shift to the right gave him his answer. 10:08 PM Awfully late for children to be up at this time of night. Plus, there was something about the noise. Familiar, perhaps? It didn’t sound like it was coming from any other room in the vicinity, but rather in one focused point in his very room. Maybe the neighbors were pedophiles? No, that image was put out of his head quickly. He turned to his nightstand and opened the drawer to look for his car keys. He needed to get out and go somewhere… no idea where, but anywhere but here right now. The sounds were driving him nuts. The jingling of the car keys ceased when the laughter of a child continued to disrupt his train of thought. Sonny shook his head and made a vain attempt to clean his ear with his finger to make sure he wasn’t just hearing things or going insane. CLINK. CLINK. CLINK. CLINK. More noise. More shit. Silver reached for his wallet and did an about face towards the door. Couldn’t be booze, he hadn’t touched any for quite a few days now. Not drugs, he’d only made a stop at 7-11 to get some donuts earlier. As he rose from the bed, he lost his grip on his wallet and watched the contents spill out onto the floor. He kicked the bed in frustration and cursed his clumsiness before picking credit cards, old receipts and tattered bills. Sonny stopped when he finally came across the last of his belongings: an old picture that his wife took with their daughter, Gabrielle. It was one of those family portraits done at some craphole like Sears. He didn’t know where or care for that matter, but it was always on him. He turned to place it back in his wallet, but halted at once when he heard a voice. “Listen.” It was almost quiet. A hushed whisper, but he distinctly heard the word “listen.” Okay, that was it. Drugs. It had to be drugs. He scanned the room quickly, but he was the sole body occupying his room. This was some spooky shit. He shook his head and headed towards the bathroom. Whatever the hell was going on was most likely the by-product of him working too hard and stressing out over the last series of fights with the wife. Screw this crap, he thought to himself. He set the photograph on the counter next to him before turning on the faucet. Both hands were cupped to take in as much cold water as humanly possible. Sonny splashed himself in the face vehemently several times. Droplets were flying all over the place and he was making a mess in a bathroom that was not his, but fuck that. That’s what those stupid foreigners get paid to do. He doused himself with the bone-chilling water in an immense attempt to fight back what appeared to be a bout with the crazies. After letting the serenity of the now-quiet room set in for a few moments, he picked up a towel and wiped himself clean before rolling it into a ball and discarding it on the floor next to the tub. He picked up the picture of family and continued to walk back to his bed and be done with this absurdity… “Change.” No, he didn’t say that. He didn’t say anything. Sonny’s head darted around the room, looking for whatever said that word. He distinctly heard somebody telling him to “change.” This was getting downright screwy. “Who’s there?!” he called out into the darkness, but whatever apparition he was hearing before didn’t’ appear to be there now. “KEEP IT DOWN IN THERE, ASSHOLE, WE’RE TRYING TO SLEEP!” Came the voice of a disturbed neighbor, partly muffled by the rather thin walls. “SORRY! …fucko,” he muttered the last part under his breath. “That’s what we’re trying to tell you about.” Sonny nearly jumped out of his skin when he heard it for a third time. He was getting incredibly panicky at this juncture and whatever was in here with him was something he couldn’t fathom. “Get a Goddamn grip, Son…” he reasoned with himself to keep sane. “You’re buggin’ out.” “I assure you, you are perfectly sane.” Now, the voice was definitely something he’d heard before. He knew that voice… but… no fucking way. She was thousands of miles away in Hawaii. Eme. He looked down at the picture of his wife and child once more and let out a heavy sigh. Seriously. This shit wasn’t happening. That bout with the crazies that he didn’t want to lose? He was not just losing it, but it practically owned him. His eyes virtually burned a whole through the photo while his mind hypothesized in a thousand different directions. Whatever force had manifested itself here tonight was beyond his understanding, so he did what any person would do in this situation. “I’ll ask again… who’s there?” Sonny did a 180 to see if anything was going to jump up and try to eat his brains or something. “Come on. Clearly, I’m suffering from a bout of stress-induced delirium so if you’re gonna show up and make me go out of my gourd, let’s just get this over with so I can finally get some sleep, albeit in a padded room!” “Look down, Sonny.” Carefully, he did so. He licked his dry lips, then let out yet another sigh before realizing what was going on before him. That picture of Eme and Gabrielle. This… was surreal. He took a gulp before crashing back down on the edge of his bed, sending the whole other side popping up. “What the Hell are you doing to me, Eme?” Sonny spoke softly to the picture in his hands. “It’s not about what your family is doing to you. It’s about what you’re doing to yourself.” With a gaze to the sky and a silent “this can’t be happening” under his breath, he looked at the picture that was somehow conversing with him. “So you’re looking to find a new way to nag me and you got it. Ha ha, joke’s on me, I’m rolling back over and going to sleep now...” “STOP!” He did as commanded, his own personal reluctance notwithstanding. “Sonny, I’m your wife. And you’re my husband. I love you, but I’m not going to sit idly by while you head down a darker path that not even you can escape from. You’re heading in a very bad direction right now, Sonny. You have to know that in some part of your heart. Your family needs you.” He put the picture on the nightstand and buried his face in the balls of his fists, still trying to wrap his mind around the events transpiring around him. After what appeared to be many moments of silence, he finally mustered up some words. “This is garbage,” he fumed quietly. “The only reason I’m imagining this shit now is because of the fight I had with my wife mere days ago – my REAL wife, not a three by three glossy. She wanted me to stay another day or two during the holidays, but I had a schedule that I had to stick to and she knew that. Then she lost her nerve, as I was about to leave… there were tears in her eyes that I haven’t seen since her mother passed away last year. She was on bended knee to get me to stay an extra two days, but I had to get back to work. I was there for her on Christmas and New Year’s Eve and Day. Isn’t that enough?” “No.” Sonny stared off into space again, the last syllable really hitting him hard. “The way that you speak of me leads me to believe that you only see being with your family as a chore rather than as your love in life. You were supposed to retire last year and be with your family. But the second somebody offers you a job at stardom, you’re quick to leave me and your baby girl and happily march back to work because it involves this stupid sport.” That struck another nerve. “Don’t you even FUCKING think of degrading my job, Eme! You know that the second we started a family that I was going to be married to this business. You KNOW that all I’m doing, I’m doing for you.” “I wouldn’t be here talking to you if you knew that that was true, Sonny. You’re doing this for yourself. You’re out there, night after night, instead of being at home with us. You’re thousands of miles away because people out there seem to like you when the people that LOVE you are left at home to sit and wonder when you’re coming back to us. You fool yourself night after night, convincing yourself that what you’re doing and how you do it is perfectly okay. It isn’t, Sonny Gabriel Silver. It damn sure isn’t. You’re abusive to those around you, you neglect others, and you’re more infatuated with what people you don’t know think about you. You’re married to your fame and your infamy, Sonny. Not to me.” He just sat there, taking every last word into his being. Sonny simply had no quip or retort for what this spirit was saying to him. “The real kicker of this relationship? You wanted to pursue me in the first place, Sonny. Not the other way around.” “Bullshit,” he finally broke his silence. “You wanted Dave Morey, you didn’t want anything to do with me.” “What about the note on the plane you had Steven write for me because you aren’t half as eloquent as he is? You tried to pass it off as your own and tried to let me down easy when I didn’t view you in that light in the first place?” Sonny’s eyes bulged. He never ONCE told Eme about that. In fact, it escaped his memory till the other day when he was pondering through his old belongings at home. An old love note from an older girlfriend reminded him of that. What’s more… he tore that note up. “Check your bag.” In trepidation, he did what was asked of him and snatched up his gym bag. He tore through most of the contents – gym clothes, mp3 player, other DVDs, water bottle – before finally coming across a folded up scrap of paper. Not torn, either. He knew he didn’t write shit on a letter, but this couldn’t be it. But there it all was in black and white for him to see: --- Dearest Eme, The realm of love has never been something I was very good at. I have done nothing in my life outside of this business, so I hope that you know that the following things that I am about to tell you are for your own good. Stop. Please. Ms. Ke’ala, you seem like a very attractive young woman, but to pursue any kind of love interest outside this business would be a path that could not be traveled without dire consequence. You have your life. I would have mine. I know almost nothing about you. You seem like a very sweet girl with a world full of promise. I felt something every time that our eyes met when you would sit in that front row. It made this heart smile to know that somebody could ever take an interest in me, but I’m afraid that if what you are seeking from me is of a romantic nature, I’m afraid that it cannot happen. Long ago, this happened to me. I hurt the one I loved because I could not separate the worlds of business and pleasure. I left her behind. A sweet girl much like yourself that didn’t need to experience the tumultuous burden that was being away from her beau for months at a time. Please realize that the words I am using upon you are not words of callousness, but of regret. Had I chosen a different profession that had not involved myself traveling constantly around the U.S. and the world itself, this letter would have never seen the light of day. I would’ve loved to experience a new world of sensations with a delicate flower such as yourself, but regrettably I do not believe that I can return any of your feelings. At least not right now. Eme Ke’ala, please take this in stride. I do not wish to harm your feelings, but I simply cannot string you along if you feel this way. It would be completely unfair to the both of us to sit her and wonder what could be when this wall exists that prevents me from finding happiness with you. Believe me when I say this, dear Eme. Somewhere out there, there is a man for Eme Ke’ala. The man you seek just cannot be Sonny Gabriel Silver. Take care in all that you do and best of luck to you in this long road that we call life. It is for the best. --Sonny --- There it was, plain as day. He looked back and forth between picture and letter, still confused as to how this was happening. “That note set in place events that eventually led to us getting together… but that’s not why I’ve come to you in this time of need. I don’t want to fight with you, Sonny. Not now. I’m just telling you something important, so listen up.” He turned his head off to the side and nodded solemnly. “You’ve got to change. Something, anything. If you blow off your family – the ones you love – you’ll suffer the consequences. You’ll be left without anybody to care for you. If you turn your back and something happens… we’re not going to help you. The events are already setting in motion, but you can avoid all that IF you change.” Sonny folded his arms and took another heavy breath, gazing off into space. It’s clear that he needed a few moments to let this all sink in. He had to ask, though. “What kind of events?” “That, I cannot say. Just know that what I’m telling you is the honest truth. You just have to realize that hurting your family is also going to hurt yourself in the long run. Things will happen to you and nobody will be around to help you if you don’t stop to think about the road you’re traveling on.” He nodded again. This entire disarray going on around him had opened his eyes. Sonny Gabriel Silver knew then what he had to do to save his own life. With a hop off the bed, he slipped on a black bathrobe and headed out into the chilly Atlanta night. Sonny stared at the sights on the concrete below, admiring the various lights and sounds. A smile now adorned on his face, he turned back to the picture of Eme and Gabrielle and picked it up. “You’re right. I know what needs to be done, Eme. Thank you.” That was all he said before he went back out to the balcony and stood over the Atlanta skyline. He cast his gaze upon the ground below and laughed to himself. “This is really nice, you know. I wish you really could be here, Eme. Gabrielle. I wish that every day, I could be holding my daughter in my arms while I’m out here traveling the world and taking in cultures of all kindFUCK YOU!” Sonny RIPPED the picture in two and tossed the remnants over the edge, watching them fall back down to the Earth. “STOP!” Eme’s voice cried out to the sky, but ultimately fell only on Sonny’s deaf ears as he turned his back to the skyline and retired to his bedroom. He shook his head and then crashed back down on the bed, taking the remote in his hands to turn on the TV. He angrily muttered to himself and “Talking pictures. What a bunch of fucking rubbish. I know I love my family, I don’t need a talking picture to tell me that kind of crap.” …………………………………….- -…………………………………… A form jolted up in the obscurity of a pitch-black room, drenched in a cold sweat. A few heavy breaths could be heard, trying to calm down and alleviate the shock from such a surreal dream. What time was it, anyway? 12:19 AM The form turned their head and rubbed their temples to try and wake up. It flicked on the small porcelain lamp next to the bed and took a moment to admire a portrait on the side of the bed. “Please come home… Come home, Sonny.” Eme Ke’ala ran a hand through her jet-black hair and held the picture of her husband contemplatively. After letting a million different thoughts of dread and doubt race through her head, she simply turned the lights off and rolled over. Gabrielle was fast asleep in her crib. Lucky baby, Eme thought to herself as she smiled at her baby girl one last time before rolling over. All was quiet now in the house as she and sleep tried to find each other, but not before she heard a voice from the room that made her eyes bolt open. “Help me, Eme.”
