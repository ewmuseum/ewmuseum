Surrendering Yourself

8 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 8th, 2007 Johannesburg, South Africa Tuesday, January 23rd, 2007 The beady brown eyes before him were strong, resilient, and resolute. Craig could tell all of this just by looking at him. Even though he didn’t like the kind of work Johnny did, whatever he did, he admired the fact that he had the cajones to back it up instead of being shifty and unsure about himself. After an encounter in Atlanta a week back, he had been put in touch with Johnny Black here in South Africa. Johnny was Craig’s only link to Daniel Santiago, the man who had Craig’s daughter for some reason. Craig’s red eyes darted to the bar behind Black’s right shoulder and noticed that there was nobody sitting there as well. This made him rather curious as to why the restaurant, wafting with wonderful and delectable smells, was so empty. “So, before we move forward, why is it empty in here? A place like this, you would think it would have some customers,” Craig said to Johnny who just curled his lips rather slightly. He looked around the establishment and then back at Craig with a curious look upon his face. “That’s a simple question to answer. As you probably assumed as you walked into here, I own the place. I dictate when it’s open for business. For our little meeting, I’ve dictated that it’s to be closed. Don’t need people snooping around and listening in to our conversation, now do we? No, I didn’t think so. Thus, it shall open once it’s finished. For that very reason, let’s move forward with our business,” Johnny said with a slight South African accent to him. He hadn’t assumed Johnny, an older man who could’ve fit right in any major city in the United States, was from South Africa. “So, tell me, what’s going to be so dangerous about Santiago? Sure, he’s the boss of the criminal underworld, but what else is to him?” Craig asked, deciding to jump right into the meat of the conversation. His tone was steady, but his palms were growing sweatier by the minute. He wanted to be done with this and well on his way. “Just the boss of the criminal underworld? You seem far too calm for this. So, I have to ask myself, who exactly are you? I mean, I know you’re a wrestler and all, but this. These are things you don’t concern yourself with. No, you concern yourself with posh rooms and fancy dining. This, you know nothing about, so you shouldn’t try to act as if you know something about what it is that Daniel Santiago truly rules,” Black responded with harshness in his tone. Yet, Craig wasn’t affected by his comments. Instead, he intensified his stare as he looked right at Black. “And yet, you actually act as if you know something about me. So, let me ask you a question. Have you ever seen a man’s eyes explode into fire?” he asked, a slightly smile creeping across his face. Johnny sat across him, noticing the red eyes of Craig, and just shook his head. Craig raised his eyebrows quickly, acknowledging that. “Trust me; you don’t want to see them do that. I know no boundaries when it comes to my daughter, Johnny. So, you tell me what I need to know, and we shall be on our merry way. So far, I’ve done what you asked and come alone. Now, you need to live up to your end of the bargain,” Craig told him, his tone stern enough to tell Black that he was done with this conversation and wanted to be on his way. Black sat up a little bit more and pointed at a waitress to come over. A short, blonde Caucasian girl walked over with a slight pep to her step. Johnny looked her up and down right quick before smiling at her. “Let me get a Jack and Coke, and for our friend here, he would like,” he began to say, and then he turned to Craig. Yet, Craig sat there silent, never removing his eyes from Johnny. Johnny just nodded his head as he looked back at the waitress. “Well, I guess he’ll take nothing,” he finished telling her and she just simply nodded her head and walked away to get his drink. The two then locked eyes again and it appeared that it was time to get down to business. Black reached into his jacket pocket and pulled out a small piece of paper that he immediately handed to Craig. “This is the address to the place where you’ll find Santiago. Now, let me tell you this, you going in and causing a noise will be a bad idea for you, your daughter, and everyone else. Wouldn’t recommend it. This is place where you will find Daniel once a week, on Friday’s, where he hosts some event. They call it The Arena. It’s there that you will get his attention. You do whatever it takes, and he’ll start taking notice. Use that to your advantage and you might actually have a shot at getting your daughter back,” Black said solemnly and quietly. Craig took the piece of paper and simply glanced at it. The location was in Amsterdam. He looked back up at Black, questions still lingering in his mind. “What is it that I’ll be doing at this Arena?” Craig asked, at a total lost as to what that all meant. The look on Black’s face gave it all away though. “I couldn’t tell you even if I knew. Never been there, only heard the stories from it, and trust me, the stories are never good. You’re risking your life, but if you think it’s worth it, then so be it,” Black said, Craig’s question going unanswered. Craig looked at the piece of paper again and decided he had gotten everything he needed. He started to slide out from the booth, but Black’s hand went over Craig’s hand to stop him. “You do me a favor though. You take Santiago out for me. I did give you this information after all. It would be a nice little even trade,” Black concluded and Craig just sat there, starting straight into the eyes of Black. Johnny was ever the interesting character and Craig was trying to figure out what game he was playing, but just from his expressions, his eyes, and his body language, he was getting nothing. “I’m not doing your dirty work. You want to know what I consider a fair trade? Me not ripping your throat out and serving it to everyone here,” Craig said before tearing his hand away from Black’s grasp and getting up. Before Craig even had the chance to turn around and leave though, Black was already making his final threat against Craig. “You don’t do this for me, and your crusade to save your daughter will end before it’s started. You understand me?” Black said with such coldness to him that it almost made Craig jump at him right in the middle of the restaurant. Instead, Craig just stood there and remained silent. He had to restrain himself or it could turn ugly for him. He immediately turned away from Johnny and ran right into the waitress who was returning with Black’s drink. Craig’s left arm quickly shot out and grabbed the drink out of midair before it crashed to the ground. The waitress looked up at Craig, a little awe struck, as he returned the glass towards her. Not even waiting for a compliment from the woman, Craig walked away from her and the restaurant. As he exited the restaurant, he felt the weight of the world collapse upon his shoulders. Things were going to get uglier before they got better. ---- Dallas, Texas Thursday, January 25th, 2007 Shattered hopes and dreams laid at Brady’s feet. He had hoped that the alcohol would’ve killed enough cells so that he didn’t remember what happened last night, but sure enough, he remembered everything. He remembered his fight with his wife, the subsequent betting a dime on Paul Cain to defeat Dusk in PRIME’s Revolution the previous night, and he definitely remembered Dusk beating Paul Cain thus causing Brady to lose a thousand dollars. A thousand dollars that he didn’t have. A thousand dollars bet that he had made in a fit of rage. Yet, he couldn’t tell his wife about it. She would flip on him He pulled himself off of the brown couch that he had fallen asleep on and saw the empty liquor bottles sitting there from the binge drinking he had done right after Dusk pinned Cain. Brady shook his head as he looked down at the liquor bottles and picked them up. In the back of his mind, he knew he needed to clear them out before Jessica found them laying there. If there was one thing that Jessica hated more than seeing Brady bet on something, it was seeing him drunk off his ass. As he grabbed the bottles and walked to the kitchen, he had a premonition that something bad was about to happen. He couldn’t be more on the money. As he entered the kitchen, he saw Jessica standing right there with a stare so strong that he could feel his insides melting away. Brady gulped slightly as he walked with his head down towards the recycling bins in the corner of the kitchen that was across from her. Yet, he never reached it as she immediately started yelling at him. “What the hell are you doing?!” she screamed, her tone so aggressive that it frightened Brady. He considered himself a man, but he was almost certain that Jessica was Satan in female form at times. His feet dragged him over to the recycling bin and he dropped the bottles in there, hoping that she was done yelling at him. It wasn’t even close. “You were drinking in our house and you just left the bottles laying out Brady! We have a daughter! She could’ve come down and seen her father drunk off his ass! She could’ve hurt herself with the bottle or heaven forbid, she could’ve sat down and had a drink with her father! I’m sure you would’ve loved that one, turning your daughter into an alcoholic at the age of nine!” she continued to yell, each insult hitting Brady in the heart. Nothing was farther from the truth. He never wanted his daughter to turn into him. As he looked up at her, he saw the anger etched into her face and her hair tied up into a ponytail. He would do anything to make it all just stop. “So, explain to me why you were drinking?” she said, her tone a little bit more calm as she was no longer yelling. He gulped hard as he desperately didn’t want to answer this question, but he had dug himself this hole. He had to recover from it and find a good lie out of it. The only problem was that if he lied then she would definitely know it, but he had to risk it. “What?” he said, as he was to buy some time. He knew it wouldn’t work though. Instead, she walked over to him, wanting him to look her in the eyes. “Don’t what me! You heard me perfectly fine. I didn’t stammer. So stop trying to buy time to come up with a lie and tell me the damn truth,” she said, her tone becoming harsher and softer at the same time. She obviously didn’t want to wake up Rose, their daughter. He had already been caught in a lie, even before he started to tell it to her. “I was… gambling,” he said, taking a moment to say the final word as he contemplated going through with the lie. It was at the last minute though that he decided just to take his lumps like a man and take the verbal bashing from his wife. Her eyes though almost made him wish he had just stabbed himself instead of telling her that he had gambled the night before. “How much did you lose?” she asked, already knowing that he had lost or else he wouldn’t have drunken himself to the point that he passed out on the couch. He stood there, wishing that she would leave the subject alone. “You just don’t want to know,” he responded, knowing that wasn’t the best answer to give, but also knowing that she really wouldn’t be happy with the answer. Instead, she folded her arms and looked straight at him, demanding an answer without so much as saying a word. “A… thousand dollars,” he said, the last word getting stuck in his throat. Immediately, his eyes darted down as he knew what was coming next. A verbal thrashing, yelling to another magnitude, and he couldn’t bear to look at her. Instead, she just walked away from him, and he thought he had escaped. He was wrong. “You’re pathetic,” she said as he passed him and walked out of the kitchen. More than anything, that hurt him the most. He had failed in her eyes and he had to make it up to her. He would do exactly that, but how, he wasn’t quite sure yet at this point. Whatever it was though, he had to do it in a hurry and within a certain amount of time. ---- Amsterdam, Netherlands Thursday, February 1st, 2007 Daniel Santiago was a man who loved the power he had earned. For the past three years, he had ruled the underworld with an iron fist, but he did so in a way that everyone was happy with how business was ran and they all made their money. Of course, Daniel made the most, but he was also the boss. That was the way that things should be run. As he sat there, reading a book, he couldn’t quite concentrate on the book. Instead, he was thinking about the different things he was taking care of. Drug deals, weapon shipments, and most importantly, the black market. It was the black market that took Daniel the longest to control, but it was also the most important aspect of his business. Without it, he was just another name. This way, he saw everything that happened in his world. He had come from a poor family, his father never around, and his mother trying to take care of him and his sister in Cuba with three jobs. Yet, it never mattered. She was always behind no matter what it was. Daniel had learned to despise her at a young age and hadn’t shed a tear when he found out that she had died when he was eighteen, twenty years ago now. Instead, he had grown to be a wise and rich man. Though, not as rich as he was now. Instead of feeling sorrow, he was happy with her out of his life. While he still kept in touch with his sister, it was something that was there just to entertain him when he was bored or lonely, which was a rarity for him. Instead, he kept himself busy, with operations in every continent except for Antarctica. He was a happy man, but with a busy mind as he could feel the information in his head. He was happy with that though. He wouldn’t have it any other way. “Daniel,” a voice echoed throughout the room. Daniel’s blue eyes glanced up from the book that he was reading and saw his assistant, Penelope, walking into his office. He smiled at her as he put the book down and sat up for their daily meeting. “Penelope, as always, a pleasure to see you,” he said, his Cuban accent just rolling off of his tongue. She smiled as she sat down across from him. “Now, tell me, how goes it?” he asks her, hoping that all news was good news. Unfortunately, he knew that not to be the case in the line of business he was in. “Things don’t look too good. We’ve got some people stepping out of line. Namely, Ethan White and Frank Sebastian. It seems like they want just a little more share of the money then they originally agreed to,” she told him, disappointed in her voice. He just simply shook his head, and placed his hand on top of her’s. “No, don’t worry yourself about any of this. I will take care of this,” he told her, soothing her very fears. But, she wasn’t easily assured. “It’s my job to worry about this Daniel, and it’s my job to take care of this,” she told him, her posture rather tense. Daniel just chuckled at this. “Yes, it is, but in this regards, let me show you how I take care of things. I shall show you what I do with people who don’t like to honor their contracts. They’re going to be at The Arena tomorrow, correct?” he asked her, and she just simply nodded. “Good, then they shall see a sign of Daniel Santiago that most people don’t get to see. She smiled back at him as she got back up and walked out. As she did so, he watched her, amazed by her beauty. Then, he was lost in his thoughts as he knew what he would have to do. He would have to make someone an example to show White and Sebastian. They would learn what it was to deal with him and then try to change the terms. ---- Amsterdam, Netherlands Friday, February 2nd, 2007 Craig briskly walked down the street towards the place only known to him as The Arena. As he turned the corner, he was rather surprised to see a line of people running down the street filled with people trying to get inside of it. An illuminated sign gave it all away as it said The Arena on it. Craig assumed he was at the right place and quickly walked up to the place, trying to see if he could bypass everyone trying to get inside. As he reached the front, a large bodyguard, though smaller then Craig, walked up to him to stop him. Craig looked him up and down, but knew he couldn’t start anything with the bodyguard. “U wilt me binnen laten?” Craig asked, surprising even the bodyguard with his Dutch. (Editor’s Note: It says will you let me in and it’s probably a shitty translation. Leave it be!) Yet, the guard doesn’t even flinch an inch as Craig stands there, looking him straight in the eyes. Then, Craig reaches into his pocket and pulls out a wad of cash. The guard takes it without hesitation and let’s Craig in. If there was one thing that was universal, it was cash. As Craig walked into the small, dank building, he immediately wondered what the place was all about. He could see people filtering through the hallway and then he saw a fleet of stairs, one set heading upstairs and another heading downstairs. Craig decided to go upstairs so he could remain unseen by most people. As he walked upstairs, he could hear a large crowd inside yelling, and some kind of pounding inside. He slowly walked up the stairs and everything got louder and louder with each step. As he walked through the threshold, he was taken aback by what he saw. Below him was a large cage with two men fighting inside of it. Weapons were scattered around the floor of the building and the two men were going it at with their bare hands. Blood was spilling out one of the men and onto the concrete floor. Around him, people were urging them on, wanting to see one of the two men die right before their very eyes. Craig couldn’t believe what was going on. His eyes immediately dashed across the room and he saw a man. A man he knew immediately without question, just by the look in his eyes. Daniel Santiago. Part of him wanted to rush around the room and just kill him, but that would get him nowhere. Judging by the security around the place, it would do nothing more then get Craig killed. Instead, he knew what it would take to impress Santiago. As he looked across, he saw a few women standing by him or sitting next to him. None of them looked like Sarah though. Instead of lingering around, he decided to get it over with as he walked back down the steps. As he reached the bottom of the steps, he saw two men head down a separate hall and he followed them. He could tell that he was heading in the right direction as he got further away from the action. At the end of the hall was a woman sitting behind a table and a list on it. He looked at the young woman who eagerly looked at Craig, wondering if he was interested. “How can I help you?” she asked him, and Craig could feel the glee dripping from her voice. It made him sick. But, he had to do this if he wanted to get his daughter back. “I’m signing up. Put me up against the best you’ve got,” he said, knowing he could very well be throwing away his entire life right there and now. Yet, it didn’t matter to him. For Craig, his back was up against the wall and that’s when he did things at his best. Santiago would never know what hit him. To Be Continued
