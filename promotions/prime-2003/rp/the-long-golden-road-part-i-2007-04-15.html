A view from the top of the arena from the rafters highlighted the internet based GWF fans, who lacked mass but not vocal ability. Two wrestlers battled in the middle of the ring. Pain. Pride. Passion. The atmosphere of the YMCA in Santa Monica was electric. The voices of GWF’s broadcast team, Milt ‘Bull’ Backer and Jimmy Hughes, call the action: Hughes: BEAR GOES FOR THE WIN! ONE! TWO! Bull: No way… THREE-NO! Bull: Barely kicked out, DIOS MI! Hughes: That was for all of our Hispanic fans around the ‘net. Munson had brought WHAM! CRACK! Hughes: Oh that son of- WHAM! Just showed Munson where he gets the name, blindsiding him from behind. Bull: YEAH! Take him out, Tony! The fans are gonna love this… Bear, slumped over the ropes, staggered up to find a crouching Tony WHAM!, setting up for his WHAM!kick. He slid in as Bear turned, flicking his foot up to the head. Bear, ducking down and lighting his thick arm up, caught WHAM! In a Death Valley Driver. Hughes: BIG BEAR DROP! WHAM!’s neck folded backward, slamming the GWF champion into the mat. Bear rolled around, holding his back as an untimely cramp came. Bull: This is ridiculous! What a cheapskate. Bull had wrapped the chair around Bear’s big dome, silencing the crowd’s cheers and chants. He spun around as he raised the dented and mangled chair in the air – to more cheers. BEAR’S GONE...WIIILD!! Bear grabbed the limp and dazed Bull by his shirt, holding him up by brute force. He turned the man around, lifted him up with a choke slam, which sent him flailing down to the arena floor – igniting a pop that only an internet hero could garner. Hughes: Ladies and gentleman… we remember the late Bull Backer… what a Dumas. ONE! TWO! THREE! Hughes: The crowning moment… You’re new, and by no means unproven, GWF Heavyweight Champion… BEAR MUN-SON! BEAR MUN-SON! BEAR MUN-SON! Hughes: These two may need medical treatment… The broadcast faded off to Bear Munson, entrenched in the stands with fans and friends, hoisting the belt high above his head for the very first time. --------------------------------- Munson Monsoon stood in the elevator, a bit cramped and uncomfortable. His shoulders were slumped, conforming to the space to give a slightly elderly woman and suited man their space also. He sighed as another floor passed.
