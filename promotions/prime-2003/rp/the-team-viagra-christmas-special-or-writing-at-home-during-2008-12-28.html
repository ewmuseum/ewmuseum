The Team VIAGRA Christmas Special or Writing at Home During the Holidays is not Conductive to Writing

28 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/28/08 Darkness. Stagehand(OS): Lights! A spotlight turns on, illuminating a hardwood stage. Stagehand(OS): Camera! An image of a camera, it's red light blinks on. Stagehand(OS): Cue snow! A light snowfall begins to precipitate. Stagehand(OS): ACTION~! Stepping forward into the spotlight is none other than the Neighborhood Lunatic, High Flyer. He wears a Santa cap and a large full body Frosty the Snowman costume. His wild white hair streaks out of the corners of his hood, his face the only visible part of his body. He holds a condenser microphone in his hands that looks like a carrot, and sticks it onto his nose with a smirk. The soft chords of Frank Sinatra begin to play... High Flyer(Singing): Have yourself... a merry (rushed)belated Christmas... Flyer shrugs. High Flyer(s): We're a couple days late. Flyer sways his head to his side. The house lights come on, as the spotlight diffuses. High Flyer(s): From now on all your troubles will be stuff we hate... The backup singers, Dusk, Tony Davis, and Mary-Lynn Mayweather, are dressed in Santa outfits. Tony Davis however, is so aluft he misses his cue. Dusk/MLM: Ahhhhh! High Flyer(s): Have yourself... a merry belated Christmas... MLM: Merry Belated Christmas. Flyer shrugs his shoulders as he sings. High Flyer(s): The rest of PRIME is gay... Mary-Lynn Mayweather reacts to this in a horrifying manner and does not continue with the back up vocals. She crosses her arms in protest. Dusk however, is ready to jump in. MLM(mumbles): Slander! Dusk(s): Especially Tyler Rayne. Flyer smiles, and continues, cutting off the last "ne" of Dusk's speech. High Flyer(s): Please don't watch this Christmas special in May! MLM/Dusk(s): Ahhhh! Flyer walks over, passing by his backup singers. He walks off the stage, and directly into a Winter Wonderland from the 1920's. Old cottages covered in snow. Additional snowman reside all over the place, and begin to back up dance in a swaying fashion. High Flyer(s): Here we are, days 'for King of Kings. Flyer motions behind him as if he were Vanna White. High Flyer(s): Already opened presents... all I got was Viagra and this hooker I paid in cents Dusk & MLM continue to back up sing, repeating the following line. MLM/Dusk(s): Oooooh. Merry Christmas... Merry Christmas... Flyer pauses, and begins to speak. High Flyer: Folks. I apologize for the vulgarity. Wait. No I don't. Flyer takes a deep inhale and continues to sing. High Flyer(s):Christmas has been stolen by the Princes. Flyer lowers his head in a somber tone. High Flyer(s): With Christmas gone My Christmas gift to everyone A close up, as Flyer whispers into the camera. High Flyer: Except Boston. High Flyer(s): Is to shoot the Princes in their head first like Han. With resolve, Flyer takes a deep inhale, and the music slowly comes to a halt. FADE OUT. We return, to see none other than Connor O'Reily and Simon Knox, wringing their hands maniacally. They are each colored green and dressed as if they were the Grinch. Miranda O'Reily is there as well, standing in front of a box marked "Viagra Christmas." Simon Knox in particular tosses his head back and lets out a blood curdling cackle. Miranda frowns. Miranda: Why are we cackling? Simon Knox: Because if we can't steal those tag team straps, the lest we can do is steal Christmas! Connor O'Reily: I think the more appropriate question is why are we here? Simon goes to answer but can not. Instead, all he can do is cackle. And so, Connor joins in. Miranda half smiles, and shakes her head. Fade out, and return to the stage. High Flyer, Tony Davis, Mary-Lynn Mayweather, and Dusk sit together on stage. Tony Davis smokes out of a corn cob pipe, hacking up a lung with every inhale. Mary-Lynn Mayweather is closest to a roaring fire, which is actually made out of cardboard. Dusk: So, what's your plan to rescue Christmas from the Princes? Flyer lowers his head, shaking it from side to side. He sighs. High Flyer: I don't know to be honest. My initial instinct was to release bears on them. And then I thought I'd buy them beers. Enough beers to make them drunk enough not to realize a bear is mauling them. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: As your lawyer, I'd advise you against that. High Flyer: You advise me against everything fun. Tony Davis raises his head, seemingly coherent. Tony Davis: So... I shouldn't have released those bears? Mary-Lynn Mayweather: NO! Oh God! Somebody save the Princes! Cut to the Princes. Simon Knox calmly reads a newspaper. Miranda and Connor are quietly playing a game of war in the corner. They flip numerous cards in succession, each grabbing their pile when they win. Simon Knox lowers his paper and tilts his head to the side. Simon Knox: Is anybody else expecting something dangerous to happen? He shrugs, and continues reading his newspaper. Cut back to the stage, where the scene is the same as before. Flyer snatches the corn cob pipe out of Davis' hands and takes a toke. He sighs. High Flyer: Tony. Did you deliver the bears before you released them? Tony Davis: Deliver? Suddenly, two bears rush on stage. They each have a beer in their hand. Mary-Lynn Mayweather shrieks and trips as she rushes off stage. Dusk stands to his feet with fists balled. Dusk: Just like anyone else, I'LL PUNCH YOU IN YOUR FACE! The two bears stand on their hind legs, and stare at one another. They look back to Dusk, and then back to each other. Suddenly, a shotgun blast rings out, blowing a hole through one of the bear's chests. The other shockingly grabs his compadre, cradling her in his hands. He looks up, and BAM. Yet another shotgun to the face. He falls, slumping to the stage. Standing behind the bears with said shotgun and dressed to the teeth in ammunition is none other than Stephen Colbert. A backlight illuminates him. High Flyer: STEPHEN COLBERT?! Stephen rips off his ammo, clanking it to the floor. Stephen Colbert: Bears and a treacherous Christmas Special? HOW DARE YOU COMPETE WITH ME DURING CHRISTMAS TIME! Stephen begins to fire indiscriminately. Flyer tackles Dusk out of a shotgun blast, and they scramble away. Tony Davis meanwhile, hasn't noticed. Stephen tilts his head toward Tony. Stephen Colbert: Shouldn't you be running? Tony finally acknowledges Stephen's presence, and smiles. Tony Davis: Hey! It's Rambo! Maybe YOU can help us get Christmas back! Stephen Colbert: Why not buy my Christmas Special? Not only do you get Christmas, but every proceed obtained from the purchase of my Colbert Christmas goes to the needy. Tony Davis: Really?! How needy!? Stephen Colbert: Pretty needy. I could use a Jacuzzi. Tony Davis: Sold! How much is it? A hundred!? Here's two! Tony hands Stephen two hundred dollar bills. He raises them high above his head. Tony Davis: Hey! Flyer! I saved Christmas! Tony rushes off to find the rest of his friends, leaving only Stephen Colbert behind. Stephen Colbert: Hello PRIME. With this abortion of a Christmas special over, I must say, you should all feel cheated. Cheated enough out of Christmas to buy my special! Only... He looks around, side to side in a panic. Stephen Colbert: Two hundred dollars. YES! TWO HUNDRED DOLLARS! Buy it now! For capitalism! AND ME! Slowly fade out to a commercial break, which includes, what? A commercial for Stephen Colbert's Colbert Christmas! That's what! Afterwards, we fade in, to see none other than Flyer standing in front of a green screen. White snow falls in the background. High Flyer: Have you ever had the need for snow? Perhaps you're a small child, desperate to have off from school? Cut to a child, praying to God. Child: Please! Have the school catch fire! I want to play in the playground! Cut back to Flyer. High Flyer: Well, now you don't have to buy a book of matches and a blowtorch Jimmy! Only dial 1-800-SNOW-TIME! You don't have to dial the E, but you can if you want! Cut back to the child, covered in snow. Child: Thank you Flyer! High Flyer: Remember! You don't have to go to juvy, to get out of school! Flyer smiles, beaming to the camera. Fade out. We return, to yet another commercial. Tony Davis holds two canisters of the hair removal cream nads in his hands. He giggles. And then laughs uncontrollably. A number flashes on screen, "1-800-Buy-Nads." And Davis just continues to laugh. Fade out. And yet another commercial, as Mary-Lynn Mayweather now appears, dressed to the teeth in attorney's garb. She sits behind a nice oak desk, in front of a large bookshelf, her numerous(some fake) law degrees hanging around her. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Have you ever been convicted of a crime? Well, I can't help you. But if you're being charged with a crime, from murder to rape to petty theft, come down to me, Mary-Lynn Mayweather, attorney at law. Yes! I will fight for you. And win lose or draw, you pay the same! Wait, no, that's not right. She frowns. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Can we go again? The camera shakes no, and a number flashes on screen. "1-800-Buy-Nads." Mary-Lynn Mayweather: ... That can't be right. She sighs. Fade out. Fade back in to our final commercial. Dusk stands, holding a Baby Dusk doll in his hands. Dusk: Whatever you do. Don't buy this cute Baby Dusk doll. He squeezes it so the eyes bulge out of it's skull. Dusk: Or I will punch you in the face. Dusk smiles, throwing out a thumbs up. A number starts to appear on screen. Dusk: NO! NO! Put that number off! Dusk charges toward the camera, and lays one thick punch into the lens, causing it to shatter to the floor. Satisfied, Dusk stomps on the camera, and we see nothing but static. Fade back in, as High Flyer stands in front of a PRIME banner. He's dressed in his normal ring attire. High Flyer: I would like to apologize for the last portion for the moments you found unhumorous. Any humor you found was purely accidental, because to be honest, I've been a bit distracted .Christmas time is important to me and my family, even though I can't be around them. Granted, I did buy myself a high powered telescope so I could see Allocca open the PS3 I bought for him, and the swarm of bees I bought for my Ex-Wife. Toss in the Philadelphia Eagles precarious Play off position and their monumental win against the Dallas Cowboys, and I must say, I've been quite distracted. Flyer crosses his arms. High Flyer: But now, with just hours until the Jewel in the Crown, I've got nothing else on my mind except tearing Tyler Rayne's spine out of his body. Kicking Jason Snow's face clean off his, well, face. And Rhett Locke? No offense, but I don't think you're going to get past Snow. I mean, everyone just circle jerks around that guy's immensely talented prose and theatrically gripping narratives. It's not like the rest of us had a chance, no? I mean, he's a part of a novel, what do I got? Some lame Christmas Special that never really got off the ground. And what happened to the Princes of New England's cameo? There's no foresight to anything you saw today. And I'm okay with that. Because I didn't spend my time getting into fanatical situations. I spent my time preparing, watching footage, capturing weaknesses and figuring out the perfect way I can kick you all in your face. I hope you all have dentist's appointments for December 29th, just in case. Flyer grips the side of his head. High Flyer: I also rammed my ear into a sharp drape earlier, and all I can think about is the pain. OH GOD MY EARS HURT. It's like tiny paper cuts being constantly rubbed by my hair! How I curse my lengthy locks of heavenly beauty! CURSE THEE! He runs off screaming. Fade out.
