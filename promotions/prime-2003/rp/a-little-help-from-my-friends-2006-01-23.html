A Little Help From My Friends

23 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-23-06 NORTH PACIFIC WRESTLING ACADEMY The huge sign adorned an otherwise unimpressive warehouse, over by the waterfront fish district in East Seattle, and only five or six cars were parked in the lot. All the cars parked around were pretty similar...they hadn't been washed in a while, and they all looked older. As Kyle winnowed his way through the parking lot, he kicked the tires on an '02 Jeep Cherokee with a CWA sticker on the back bumper, and a PRIME VIP Parking sticker on the windshield. That's Jason for you. He still hasn't taken that damn sticker off his car. One match in the federation, and he wears it like a badge of pride. He never changes. As Kyle finally reached the front door, he saw a worn yellowed flyer posted. Every wrestling school Jason operated...the first Core Wrestling Academy in Red Bank, the second one in Tallahassee, and now this one, had this same sign on the front door: ”Upon entering this door, you sacrifice a part of yourself. Nothing good is easy.” - Jason Core – Head Trainer: Core Wrestling Academy/North Pacific Wrestling Academy Kyle shook his head, and chuckled a bit to himself. Pops, you ain't kidding. He swung the big metal door open, and stepped into the warehouse, which was bathed in cheap flourescent light. Inside, a few old wrestling rings were spattered with blood and sweat (probably tears as well) and bits and pieces of equipment were scattered around the warehouse. Inside, jumping rope were a few students. A few were Asian, primarily due to the history of the school and it’s location. Kyle knew all three of them. They’d taken to calling themselves the PWC: the Pacific Wrestling Club, despite the fact that one of them hadn’t taken a booking in three-plus years. They were Kensuke Suzahara (the one who didn’t wrestle anymore), Kenichi Kazuyo (the one that wore stupid sunglasses), and Jiro Sennosuke (the one that Kyle trained in Japan). And Kyle Lamen hadn’t been so happy to see three Japanese men in, well, ever. Kensuke, the one on the left, stopped jumping rope immediately as he saw Kyle, then gave him a quick embrace. Kenichi (KK) stopped jumping rope too, primarily because he’s lazy…and Jiro kept jumping, but smiled in Kyle’s direction, as the sweat dripped off his face. “Kyle…my man! How’s it hangin’?” Kensuke chirped up, leaving Kyle just to scratch the back of his head. Kensuke noticed that he’d started doing this whenever anyone asked him how he was doing. “Not too bad, Kensuke. Could be worse.” This is true. Kyle’d been feeling pretty good the last few days. “I’m a millionaire now.” Jiro stopped jumping rope. Kensuke almost fell over. “Um, you take TERRIBLE care of your money Kyle…and with all of your medical bills…” Kensuke stopped for a moment. “Two words, Ken…Atlantic City.” Kyle just grinned. Kenichi Kazuyo shook his head, and started wandering off towards the showers. “Fuckin’ Jersey.” Kazuyo disappeared into the locker room, without even a backwards look at Kyle. “What’s wrong with him!? He’s not usually like that.” Kyle asked Kensuke, who pulled him closer and got near his ear. Kensuke nodded. “Uh, yeah. His tag team partner disappeared. Danny skipped town, and hasn’t talked to anyone. Kenichi was talking about going off to find him.” Kyle’s ears perked up. “Crazy. Hey, is Jason around?” Jiro jumped into the conversation. “Uh, huh. But he’s pretty bummed out about his prize student disappearing as well.” Kyle smiled. “I’ll cheer him up a little, then.” Kensuke threw an arm around Kyle in a friendly manner, pulling his attention more towards the front of the building, then started off towards Jason Core’s office. “Let’s go, then.” Kyle was wondering what exactly the hurry was, and paused a moment, before Jiro approached Kyle. Jiro murmured into Kyle’s ear. “He’s trying to prevent you from looking at the back ring.” What Kyle saw caught him completely off guard. He caught a glimpse of Sarah McIntyre, his former personal assistant across the expanse of the warehouse floor. She was in one of the three rings, decked out in shorts, sports bra, pads from head to toe, and appeared to be leaping over a wooden box, and landing solidly on her back. The classic Jason Core beginner wrestler's training. Don't say anything. Don't open your mouth, Kyle. Say nothing. Say nothing. “Hey! Sarah!” Moron. Sarah sat up, and looked over in the direction of Kyle, an shot him an exasperated look. She pulled off the foam sparring helmet she was wearing, and chucked it through the ropes, before rising to her feet and exiting the ring...away from Kyle's general direction. Mmm. That went about as well as expected, didn't it? Kyle made his way into Jason Core’s office, following Kensuke. Jiro took off in the other direction, perhaps he said something to Kyle, but he missed it in his ruminations over Sarah. Jason’s office was functional, organized, and barely decorated with memorabilia from his twenty-year run in wrestling. The crowning achievement of the room was a PCW Extreme Championship Belt in a glass case attached to the white concrete walls. Jason Core was standing next to his chair, leaning on his cane. For the last eight months or so, he’d been stuck using the cane after an attack by Hoyt Williams. This was the impetus that caused Kyle to try and join PRIME, and led him to where he is today. So yeah, I guess you can thank this guy. Despite his injury, Jason was still hale and solid, with the body of a man ten years younger, despite the battle scars. He clapped Kyle on the shoulder in a friendly manner of a favorite uncle. “How’s it going, kid?” Jason said, smiling happily. Kensuke kicked back in the other comfortable chair in the room, and Kyle was left standing on his own, hands in pockets. “Well, boss…I’ve got some good news. I’m gonna donate fifty-thousand dollars to the school.” Jason sat back down promptly. “Are you serious?” he asked Kyle, and Kyle just nodded.“I’ve got some ideas for some investments…some things that should pay off pretty well before I kick off. I’m gonna need some help from you guys, though.” Jason just nodded. “Anything you need, kiddo. Right, Kensuke?” Kensuke nodded too, but looked a bit apprehensive. “I’ve come into some cash, and I want to spend it in a way that means something, not like the way Tyler Nelson spends it.” Jason Core’s eyes lit up. “Tyler Fucking Nelson. I remember when I used to mess with that guy, and I’ve got an idea that might help you out.” Kyle assisted Jason towards the door and waved Kensuke to come with. “Man, am I ever lucky to have friends like you guys. People I can trust. After we straighten this out, I’ll probably head out to see a specialist about my tumor…” Kyle said on the way out. After Kyle and Jason left the room, Kensuke pulled out his cellphone, and hammered in a few numbers. The phone rang once, and then Kensuke spoke into it. “Hey, Paul…I think we might have a bit of a problem.”
