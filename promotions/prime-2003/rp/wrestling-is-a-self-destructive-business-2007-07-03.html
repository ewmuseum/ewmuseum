Wrestling Is A Self-Destructive Business

3 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 3rd, 2007 The road is an excessively torturous endeavor to overcome. Professional wrestling, on the whole, is an extreme hazard to go through in the first place. The physical toll matches tend to have on people, not to mention the constant travel and the sheer amount of times one wrestles in a month makes things more difficult on the wrestlers involved. Wrestlers are often separated from their families for long periods of time, and they rarely get to see home. Being at the top meant getting the most money, and the most exposure. However, it also meant being a strong political player, a guy who can not only work the crowd, but work the back office as well. It also meant that there's a high chance that the person on top is on something. While any professional athlete would be bound to have their demons, professional wrestling was a virtual playground of Satan himself. Wrestlers are less scrutinized than most, they don't endure being under the media microscope quite like, say, a Kobe Bryant. However, ask them to give someone like Tchu the same sort of treatment that they give Kobe, and they'll probably laugh. The sports media don't treat professional wrestling in the same sort of way as they would baseball or basketball. They consider it inferior. Fake. And why wouldn't they? Professional wrestling is filled with freaks of all kinds. Point me to a wrestler that's normal and well-adjusted and I'll point you to a flying pig farm. If a wrestler's not on steroids, they're probably on some other drug. If they're not on drugs, they're probably getting blowjobs from every ring rat on the planet. If not that, then they're clearly messed up in the head. And if they're not, then they're just plain lacking in common sense in the first place. To give you an idea of the lack of common sense in some wrestlers, I present to you one Coral Avalon. Coral Avalon wasn't in this business because he wanted to win titles or be successful. I know, I know, that goes against the logic of pretty much every wrestler on Earth, but Coral's that kind of guy. The reason why Coral got into the business was to become a great performer in the ring, and to be someone respected for his work there. Coral genuinely enjoyed performing in the ring, and always managed to stay in ring shape in order to perform. Part of his ability to endure it was Annabelle Summers' willingness to travel with him. Most people would think that it would ruin Coral's pace to bring the girlfriend unit with him most of the time, but to tell the truth, Annabelle wasn't moderating Coral. Coral already did that to himself with many various personal ethics, including, but not limited to: no performance enhancement drugs, no drugs period, no drinking, and no smoking. That being said, it didn't completely absolve the rest of the Blue Rogues from partaking in insanities. Coral and Annabelle sat in the back of the Delphinus, the Codemaster's nickname for the custom bus that he extorted negotiated from Blaine. Coral was in his normal out-of-ring gear, which usually consisted of a T-shirt of some kind (in this case, something Japanese he picked up in Japan) and a pair of jeans. Annabelle wore a purple tanktop and a flowy black skirt. Annabelle was curled up with her legs on the seat, and most of her body leaning against Coral's. They were watching with a mixture of surprise and fright, because the one thing you never want to have happen was occuring right in front of them. That's right. Mega Job found the beer. God help us all. As you can probably guess, Beef and Janito were even less "there" to begin with. El Janito was missing his shirt. Beef was missing his pants. Neither event was really something anyone ever needed. Ever. The Codemaster and Steve, meanwhile, were smoking marijuana (something they did once a month). Apparently, it's some sort of thing that graduates of the Craig Miles Professional Heel Academy did. Regardless, they're not gonna be doing much for the next couple of hours, so we'll be ignoring them for a bit. By the way, they're wearing casual clothing. I'll let you determine what that means. Neither of the Falks were in the bus, they apparently stepped out. Which was probably either smart of them, or they're probably out doing something worse than what the people on the bus were doing. Let's not dwell on it, especially if any of the rumors about Scott Falk's dick have any truth to them. Alexandria walked onto the bus in the middle of the commotion, wearing a green string tanktop and black leather pants. Compared to the rest of the lot, she wasn't doing anything reckless. Except wear leather pants. But hey, she's nineteen, she's still got time to be young and stupid. Right? Beef sauntered over to leathery Alexandria. "HEEEEEY! Wanna sex?" Beef asked. His words were slurred, his grammar was terrible, and he smelled of cheap alcohol and... what amounted to about a week of not knowing what a shower was. Alexandria's response was simple and direct. She pushed Beef out of her way and went to the only sane or coherent people on the bus, those being Coral and Annabelle. "Oh, come on!" Beef objected. He held his arms out, not even bothering to hide the fact that his pants were *still* AWOL. Rumor has it that they were captured by the Germans (not Das Korps) and held hostage as prisoners of war. Then again, the very idea of Germans capturing a pair of pants and treating them as POWs was a very frightening prospect in of itself. "You take the Beef Machine Mean?" Beef asked. If that statement made any sense to you, I again have a flying pig farm to show you. Thunk. Alexandria lobbed one of Annabelle's shoes at Beef's forehead, knocking him out for the night. Yes, Beef can be knocked out by a flying (fashionable but affordable) shoe. It's science. You can't fight it. Alexandria sighed, and turned to the couple, gesturing in the direction that Mega Job had been staggering around, "Why are you two in here and dealing with that mess?" "To tell you the truth, they're kinda scary like that." Coral explained, "Annabelle's been all clingy to me for a while." Annabelle didn't look up, but she did acknowledge Coral's statement, "I can't help it. You're comfortable." Alexandria slapped her forehead, "Can't you guys, like, get a room or something? All this lovey-dovey stuff you guys do drives me crazy. I haven't even had a boyfriend worth spending time with, you know." "You could have me!" Beef shouted from the floor. Thunk. Alexandria threw Annabelle's *other* shoe at Beef, and down he went again. Coral sighed, "Alex, we're not at a gas station, not a hotel, and we're gonna be stuck here until we get to the town. Getting a room is impossible." "I'm just resting, anyway." Annabelle said, with a tired sigh, "Travelling as much as we do takes a toll." Coral glanced over at Annabelle with worry, as he reached around her with his near arm and wrapped it around her. Although this forced Annabelle to adjust and find a new spot to rest her head, she was overall comfortable with this change. Coral then looked down towards where Anna's head rested. "You don't have to travel with me, you know." Coral said, "You're under no obligation." "I wouldn't be with you if I stopped now." Annabelle said, "So, I'll just stay here." Alexandria facepalmed, "Again. Get a room, you guys." Coral turned to Alexandria, "So, Alex... I gotta ask, since I've not had a chance to really talk to you... why'd you come with us? You don't ever seem that interested in wrestling." "I'm not." Alexandria said, "Sure, I keep in shape and I know the ropes, but... I'm underutilized for a reason. I'm not that fond of this business." "But... you keep coming with us." Coral said. Alexandria crossed her arms, and looked up at the ceiling of the bus. She thought about it for a few seconds, before she answered without moving her head back down, "I have questions. About this business. About why it did what it did to my father, and my brother. Dad was an alcoholic, treated my mother poorly, and didn't abandon us solely out of guilt. Big brother had been alcoholic before, but he sobered up. But he has this damaged knee that's now become a limp, and his loyal to his wife to the point that I think he's guilty about something. Joey never answered my questions himself, so... I came along to see if I could find them myself." Coral asked, "Have you?" "I wouldn't still be here if I did. I'm still missing something." Alexandria said. "Missing something?" Coral asked, "Alex, this business is pretty self-destructive. I've seen people and friends break down all around me. My best friend's lost his whole family because of this business and nearly broke his back because he feared having to retire *because of it*. Ironically, he's now kinda retired himself. Daniel Phillips, your trainer, became misanthropic solely because of the way he was treated by this business. Anthason's got issues all the way around." Coral sighed. "If you're not ready for it, it'll swallow you whole. Make you empty. That sort of thing." Coral said. There was a pause. "You know, you're kinda creepy when you start talking like that." Alexandria said. Coral nodded, "Yeah, people don't like it when I actually talk for some reason." Coral then paused and put his other arm around Alexandria. And for this briefest of moments, Coral was the biggest pimp in the Blue Rogues. Envy him, mortals. "Joey wasn't immune to self-destruction, himself. He suffered a knee injury, continued working for years without rest afterwards, and by the middle of 2003, he could barely walk and *had* to retire. He turned to alcohol as a way to dull his pain without resorting to pain killers, which made him a pretty lousy person to be around. And he loved this business to the point that it nearly killed him and his marriage." Coral said. Alexandria thought about it. "If you know that this business is self-destructive, why keep doing it?" Alexandria asked. "Because this is my dream job." Coral replied. Alexandria placed her hands in her face, "I don't get it." "...That's why you had to find out for yourself, right?" Coral asked. "I... guess." Alexandria replied, before she moved Coral's arm off of her and stood up, "I think I'll lie down. My head hurts from all this thinking you're making me do." "Lie down with me?" Beef asked, still on the floor. Alexandria looked around for another shoe to throw, but finding none, she simply walked up and kicked him in the head before going to lie down. Annabelle, still curled up next to Coral, looked up at him, "Do you want to get something to eat before we start driving again?" "Sure." Coral said. The two uncurled themselves from each other's embrace. Annabelle carefully walked around Beef so he couldn't see up her skirt, and took her shoes as she went. After the two passed, Alexandria lifted her head up to watch them leave, and smiled to herself. "I'd fall for a guy like him."
