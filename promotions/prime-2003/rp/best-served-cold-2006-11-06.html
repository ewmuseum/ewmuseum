Best served cold

6 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 November 2006 Looking down on her firstborn’s blood-covered head, as she held his gaping mouth up to her nipple, Eve probably wasn’t thinking that she was looking into the eyes of a murderer as she gazed at Cain. To be honest, and taking into account that they didn’t have any entonox way back then, she probably wasn’t thinking anything more profound than ‘Thank God that is over’* If, however, she had been fully compos mentis at the time I would be willing to bet my soul on the fact that she wasn’t mulling over career options and deciding on fratricide for her little boy. I obviously assume that it would be Eve making those sort of decisions for her son simply because she is blamed for everything else; Adam was either far too innocent or a moron but, either way, nothing was ever his fault … but I digress. To cut a long story short – ok, shorter than it feasibly could be – fast forward a decade or two and we find Cain and his baby brother Abel. “Hey, why do I have these ringlets and you get the cool blonde hair? – DIE!” “Hey, He liked your offering better than mine, that isn’t fair – DIE!” “Hey, how come you get to bang our hotter sister and I get suck with the moose? – DIE!” Any of the three alleged statements from Cain could be the basis for the truth of raison d’etre, so to speak, behind the murder of his brother. Cain, supposedly, was darker of complexion, bearded and brown of eye. Abel, on the other hand, was fair, blond, and blue-eyed; even in the dim and distant past Hollywood style beauty sold more than hard work and talent, go figure. When called by God to give an offering Cain gave what he could, some fruit and grain from the land that he had tilled himself while Able handed over the first born kid from his flock; you know, the flock that he had done nothing but watch from the sidelines while the rams did all the real work. God, of course, goes for the ‘bling’ and favours Abel’s offering over Cain’s own. Then there comes the matter of marriage. Now the Bible, of course, cannot be wrong so when it came time for Adam and Eve’s darling and beloved children to marry they had to keep it all in the family. Literally. A little known fact is that Cain and Abel were both twins, just not to each other. The ‘good’ Book states that both Cain and Abel had twin sisters and so that neither one married too close to their own ‘blood’ it was much better that they marry the other’s sister. … still with me? Incest is ok, kids, as long as it isn’t too closely related. So marrying your twin sister is a ‘no no’ because obviously that would be gross, wouldn’t it? Marrying your brother’s twin sister, however, is just fine and dandy. Don’t forget, the Bible says it is true, so therefore it must be. Anyhoo, I digress again. The bottom line is that Eve decided … yeah, the blame goes to her again, Adam was created by God so therefore is only a dupe in all of this … that Cain would marry Abel’s twin and vice versa. Now one of these girls was hot and the other not, and guess which one Cain was meant to marry? Yeah, you got it. Unnamed sister number two. Abel, of course, not only got the cake but got to eat it too!** So, whether it came down to jealousy of his brother’s looks, jealousy of his brother’s favouritism in the eyes of God, or jealousy of his brother’s hottie bride to be it … allegedly … boils down to the fact that Cain killed his brother, Abel, out of jealousy. To get revenge on his brother because he was jealous. Me? I don’t personally think that is fair, you know. End of the day we have a little Book, written about two thousand years ago, that tells us that Cain was a bad boy. Where is the evidence … where is the proof? Habeas corpus, baby. You may have noticed, the more observant among you anyway, that I have an … affinity if you will, for Cain. Well, I cannot deny that, so won’t even try. I do feel a certain ‘kinship’ for the man, blighted as he is by reputation that doesn’t seem fairly deserved at all. Trust me, I know how that feels. Growing up I was much like Cain. I don’t actually know what my mother thought about me when she looked at me, I don’t even know if she cuckolded my father like Eve did to Adam. I never knew them, you see, they were dead before I was old enough to know anything. I don’t even remember what they looked like, what she smelled like, even. Hey, don’t start feeling sorry for me now; I don’t want your pity. Keep it for those that actually need it! Anyway I spent most of my life in the shadows. Literally and figuratively. My grandfather and brother were the only family that I ever knew and to be honest only one of them even cared to acknowledge my existence at all. My brother, my darling brother. I can remember sitting inside the dark room, the smell of smoke and embers burning my nose as tears rolled down my face. It is weird that I cannot remember my mother, cannot remember anything earlier than that at all, but I can remember that one moment. My ‘birth’ if you will. Like a duckling bonding with the first thing that it sees I remember looking into the piercing green eyes of the figure above me and knowing him, feeling him. Loving him. From that moment on I adored my brother. My grandfather was always too busy for me, too busy to notice let alone care, and so it fell to my brother to raise me; to care for me; to love me. No one else mattered to me; no one else even seemed to notice me. I was always the shadow to his light, the ash to his sparks, the embers to his glorious inferno. For twenty years I walked behind him, always two steps behind him and watched – in awe, like everyone else – as he flew, on wings of fire, to the heights. I knew, in that moment, that I would never fly, my wings had been broken, stunted, never growing in the darkness of his shadow. So, that is why I have such an affinity for Cain. You see I don’t know if he is guilty of what he was accused; I don’t even care, really. I know that I am. I know that, when the opportunity came, I took it. I killed my brother. Gladly. I stepped out of the shadows and into the light, in his place. In my place! Why am I telling you all of this, Winters? It is simple, my friend. You see I just wanted you to know what is coming for you. They say that revenge is a dish best served cold, do they not? Well I disagree with that. I think that it should be served hot; it should burn. You will feel my heat, Winters, and you will pay for the sins of your past. So, Winters, just remember this. Cain was brought up to love his brother and yet he killed him; I did love my brother and still I killed him. You, Winters … I don’t even like you! / / / * I have taken the liberty of translating that from Aramaic for you all and don’t worry about Eve blaspheming like that, I am pretty sure that God already had her down as a ‘bad’ person. ** Yeah there is an innuendo in there but c’mon if incest is ok then surely so is a bit of cunnilingus!
