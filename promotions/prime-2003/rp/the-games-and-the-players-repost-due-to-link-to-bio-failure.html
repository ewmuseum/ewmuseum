Date: Repost I am reposting this because for some reason it hasn't got a link from the Alliance bio page. ### Disclaimer: A lot of the earlier bits are mainly for background and shows Neil and Gideon off camera as well. By separating things into televised and non televised I’m going to be using the camera as a 3rd person to be introduced to the scenes when relevant. Neil Whittaker and Neil Fracas act similar but are different in many ways while Gideon will consistently be a ruthless asshole. ### Last week was the Alliance’s first wrestling match in a long time. The debut was hyped on the Internet. They were painted as superstars. They were the larger then life conquerors walking roughshod over all before them. They were the men that succeeded at all costs and always found a way to win. On the rare times they lost a battle they eventually won the war. Was October 6, 2003 really that long ago? People have forgotten just what the Alliance meant and the ruthlessness they stood for. When you have the average wrestling fan thinking that sports entertainment is wrestling and getting wowed by any match that actually contains wrestling moves you know that it has been WAY too long. Neil and Gideon aren’t vain enough to think that they can single handedly bring back wrestling. The days of miracles and Moses parting the sea are long since passed and buried in the sands of time. The Alliance are made up of two men that are more stubborn then the norm. Neil Whittaker and Gideon Fontaine are thinking men. Where others give up and fail they find a way to succeed. Are they better then most. A case can be made that they are because they are well trained and great at what they do but the most important thing beyond all the skill and physical conditioning is the fact is that they are willing to work. Kids today just don’t have that same drive or respect. They want the easy road paved for them. They say that a journey begins with a single step towards your destination and ends with a single step as well. They forget all the steps it takes to get to where you end. Is it the short path paved and lined with pretty flowers or is it the path that has to be cleared by hand, every inch EARNED by the sweat of your brow. Sports entertainers thrive on the pretty path put before them. To them it should be easy and everything handed to them. The wrestlers are the ones that make their own path. If you ever heard the stories about paying your dues you know just what I mean. 50 dollars after getting hurt in the ring, Unable to sleep because of pain. A loaf of bread and peanut butter because you can’t afford anything else. 15 hours of driving. 7 guys in one hotel room. Relationships ended because you are never home. Families left behind. Drugs for everything from pain to entertainment. Seeing friends hurt or even dead before the age of 40. This is referred to as “Paying your dues”. Sports Entertainers just don’t “get” it.Sports Entertainers will never “get” it.They need to learn the hard way.This is not a game.This is not a place to play.This is life.For some it is “The life”.When you end the day broken and hurt.When you see nothing but pain.When you see the years wasted.When you see the years you will never enjoy.When you get up and do it all over again.That is when you “get” it. ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** Gideon Fontaine is in his office. Neil comes walking into the office with a noticeable limp. As soon as Neil shuts the door he starts walking normally. This may seem a bit odd and it is but nothing is ever random when it comes to the Alliance. There is plans in motion.. What those plans are of course vary. Gideon: Did you go to the doctor Neil. Neil: Yup. Gideon: Excellent. The rumors should be circulating the Internet as we speak. I made sure that an anonymous tip had taken pictures of you going to the doctor. Neil: So other then faking a knee injury what is the plan? Gideon: I was thinking a knee brace with some heavy metal supports. Neil: No. Gideon: No? Neil: Against the Seek and Destroy clowns I couldn’t care less. Driving the metal brace into their ribs with knee lifts is a hell of an idea and I like the chance to make those idiots suffer. Still ain’t going to use it against Titan or Noble. Gideon: I have a plan for the 4 way match. Neil: Why the hell is Titan against 3 teams anyway. Gideon: Because he’s a monster Neil. Neil: He is what less then 40 pounds heavier then me and he’s a monster? Gideon: Yes. Neil: Even though I’m about an inch taller I’m a big man but because he has about 40 pounds more he’s a monster? Gideon: Yes. Neil: This make any sense to you? Gideon: Obviously you don’t understand marketing Neil. Either Motoki or Tyler decided that Titan St. James is a monster so he is a monster. Guys like you are big men until the time the front office decides you are a monster. He will be booked as the unstoppable juggernaut and placed in matches where even if he was the most incompetent inbred sloth in the world he would still look like a million bucks because his opponent would be a joke not getting in any offense. Neil: I hate marketing. So what’s the plan and is this an elimination or fatal 4 way? Gideon: Best to assume it is only a single fall. I’ve done a little scouting. I like Titan and Noble. They are actual wrestlers though Nobel does seem to have some Sports Entertainment tendencies. As for Gonzo he seems to be one of those backyard wack jobs. Wait long enough and he will do something stupid that takes himself out. Unless of course he is facing Jason or Eric because I doubt they could react to anything he does enough to capitalize. Neil: I took a look at Titan’s match. He likes to brawl but I noticed from his eyes that he has some skills. Gideon: What did you notice. Neil: I saw the “look”. He studied his opponent for weaknesses and his gaze seemed to be looking at the arms and legs as well as focusing on Killian’s chest and head like a boxer would. My guess is military training there. If it is what I think it is we best sharpen up our striking defense. Gideon: Could be a pain in the ass. Best to keep him occupied with the other 4 guys. There are a handful of military martial arts and if he is using Krav Maga some of the strikes can be damn deadly. I’ll face him if it comes down to it. He will have a very hard time striking me because even with his training I have nearly 30 years of training in the martial arts and I can trap his neck, leg, or arm for a submission. Neil: Think I’ll call dibs on Gonzo. I can counter damn near anything he does and drive him into the mat. Should be interesting to improvise a few Fracasplexes from any of those 760 mc flippysaults those backyard idiots do. Gideon: I think we should focus on using alternate moves as a finisher. If we hit the 3rd Degree you know they will break the pin because it is too well known. If we use any of our regular finishers the same thing will happen there. Neil: So we hype up how deadly the 3rd Degree is? Gideon: Exactly. Have them looking for the 3rd Degree and have them scratching their heads when we get a pinfall with something else. Neil: Seems solid to me. You ever get in touch with Tyler yet? Gideon: I left a voice mail on his answering machine. I’d have sent a thank you card but I think humiliating Seek and Destroy was thanks enough. Neil: What are we going to do for an interview segment? Gideon: Annoy the living hell out of Seek and Destroy. I have my creative working on the final revisions of a promotional video as well. Neil: Any good? Gideon: Lets say that men that intimidate other men in washrooms leave all sort of options open for ridicule. Neil and Gideon start laughing their heads off. Neil: What about Titan, Noble, and Gonzo. Gideon: I think we should pretend we are overlooking Nobel and Gonzo so they feel a bit of overconfidence that we are ignoring them. Might lead to mistakes we can exploit. As for Titan well might as well give him some respect because he at least is attempting to be a wrestler. He is paying his dues and working to get better so I respect that. Neil: Better then the roided up jackasses coming into the ring these days that are bigger then their IQs. You catch that idiot Ferguson’s steroid cracks? Gideon: Yup and it is sad that he has so little mike skills that he has to go for the cheapest possible heat. The thing that made my ears perk up is when he referred to Titan as a 7 ft Jew. Really the only thing that didn’t come out of his lips was calling someone gay. Neil: Doubt he could pass a urine test because the bitch squats to take a leak. Gideon: His time will come sooner or later and on that day I will have some truly interesting material for Fergie. Let’s go to the studio and cut a promo. ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** The Alliance enters the interview area and is waiting for things to be set up. There is a few last minute ideas getting tossed around and they are snickering a bit. The lighting is in place and the cameramen have the cameras ready to record. They give the Alliance the countdown. 5… 4… 3… 2… 1……… Gideon: As predicted we won. Anyone that has been around this business wasn’t surprised. Let’s go through the reasons we won. We are bigger. We are better trained. We are smarter. And of course the 2 most important reasons we won is because they are Seek and Destroy which pretty much guarantees a win for their opponents and because they had made an enemy that they really did not need. Neil: Before our match you were even stupider then I expected going after Tyler Nelson. Gideon: When we heard about that we naturally had to switch gears a bit. You see a man like Mr. Nelson isn’t going to let that slide without a receipt of some sort down the road. We decided on toning down our match into a bit of advertising so to speak. We wanted to show that we were 2 men that could take care of business in a very short time. Neil: Plus we noticed the ref change before the match started. Gideon: Do you gentlemen even bother reading the cards or simply mark down your names with an X. When the ref is switched at the last moment and there were no previous injuries to the ref that really should have tipped you off that something may be up. Tyler’s new ref did very well that match and was perfectly positioned for the illegal switch. Neil: You would almost think we had it planned wouldn’t you. Gideon: Of course we didn’t need the help but why pass up an opportunity like that to embarrass and humble you two. When you talk smack and attempt to throw your weight around you had better have something to back it up. You claimed to have a mental edge. By beating Baltasar at his own game you thought you were able to go toe to toe with anyone. We of course had to show you the difference between the mental games of an amateur like yourselves and what true professionals like Neil and myself can do. Now onto Neil’s knee and this weeks match. Neil: The knee got cramped up a bit but I haven’t been wrestling for about 2 years either. Grats you managed to cramp up my knee but you idiots still don’t get it. A real wrestler will work hurt in the ring. I’ve seen broken arms, broken ribs, broken necks, broken feet, broken hands, ripped quads, concussions of various types, and gallons of blood spilt. I’ve worked with my knee so bad I couldn’t bend it. I’ve had a mild concussion. Hell I’ve even been knocked clean out. I picked you up for the 3rd Degree with a bad knee because a real wrestler guts things out and finishes the match. Gideon: You gentlemen should all thank Neil this week. You see I wanted to set in motion an excuse for Neil to have on a metal kneebrace going into the 4 way match. It is already circulating the Internet about Neil’s knee injury but Neil stopped that idea because he has respect for both Titan St. James and Johnny Noble. Knee lifts into the ribs with that metal brace I’m told feel like a baseball bat and it would have made things very very painful for someone. Neil: We don’t need a shortcut. We can hit the 3rd Degree out of nowhere and end a match so suddenly that just like Seek and Destroy you will be wondering what hit you. Now for that Gonzo wack job. Gideon: You appear to be a backyard wrestler. There is very little substance just a guy who knows just enough to be dangerous and THINK he is a wrestler. Neil: I’m looking forward to seeing you in the ring kid. It isn’t because I like you it is because you are an idiot that hasn’t got enough training. Every high risk move you do opens you up to a half dozen counters and when you try to do something I'll shut you down and plant you hard in the canvas. I’ll give you credit for at least learning some wrestling. Though if you don’t know how to protect yourself from a move you are going to get hurt. I can brawl with the best of them and you are barely average. Gideon: The problem with backyard wrestling isn’t that the wrestlers don’t have heart or any skill. It is that they do not have any training. They are a danger to themselves and anyone else because they really haven’t got a clue other then what they see. It is a lot like a monkey at a typewriter typing away. No more no less. Neil: Since we actually like Titan St. James and Johnny Nobel we aren’t going to go the trash talk route. Hell make it real short here and say may the best men when. You manage to win then we will get you in the rematch. Gideon: Now for some fun. Neil and Gideon grab cardboard cutouts of both Eric Evans and Jason Bass. They set them up near the camera in plain view. Gideon goes over to one of the lighting guys. Gideon takes a 100 dollar bill and a note out of his billfold. Gideon: What is your name? ???: Travis Gideon: We need you to read this for us while looking at the cut outs of Seek and Destroy. The 100 is your payment for helping us. Travis: Sure. Gideon: Excellent. As you can tell this is a 100% improvement in Seek and Destroy where they are actually 2 dimensional instead of the 1 we are used to. You see there has been a great injustice on Revolution 91. Danny Ferguson came within an eyelash of getting the cheap heat trifecta. Neil: Couldn’t believe it until I saw with my own eyes that there was an idiot like that in PRIME. Gideon: We will make the dream happen Danny. You will get the trifecta of cheap heat and complete the Triple Crown of mediocrity. Neil: When Gideon talks about cheap heat he is talking about things so low that even the most pathetic idiot can always say something to piss people off. We call it the minor leagues promo material. Gideon: Indeed. Danny opens strong with a steroid reference. He has the eye of the tiger there and I quote “The nation is in a steroid crisis, do you really want the belts on some of these Baby Hueys?” My god an opener with a steroid comment directed at the big men. This man is a solid pro going to the steroid reference so fast. Neil: Then you get the idiot saying “Hey look, a 7-foot Jew. Do you come with a punchline or do I get to make one up on my own.”. Within a minute he went for a steroid reference and a racist slur. Gideon: Since there were so many people there and you never had the opportunity at the time I have spared no expense in bringing in a highly trained actor to reenact that event and give the PRIME fans a chance to see you complete the cheap heat trifecta. Travis can you look at the cut outs of Eric and Jason and deliver your lines please. Travis: Then you have these 2 fudge packers trying to manhandle T-Nel in an Oz scene in the bathroom. Neil: Ain’t a cheap heat trifecta without Fergie calling someone gay. Gideon: We have made the vision complete we have shown the A-list star delivering the F class promos from cheap heat 101 and the trifecta of mediocrity. Thank you for your help Travis. Now we will see you gentlemen at Revolution 92. Neil: All it will take is one 3rd Degree. We hit that it’s over. The cameramen motions that’s a wrap. Neil and Gideon are snickering as they talk over plans for Revolution 92. They walk away and begin their preparations for what we in the business refer to as a cluster f$%k match. 4 teams, 3 Losers, and 2 teams not even involved in the decision.
