Losing a little more

2 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/02/06 Culture Shock is coming and to the fans of PRIME it would seem like it couldn't be coming at a worse time for Baltasar. Ok so the fans don't really care that it's bad timing for him. More accurately they are probably praising the fact that Culture Shock is just around the corner and coming up fast. I mean it works out perfect for them right? Dual Halo will be on display for the third time and it will come right in the middle of an awful streak for The Red Devil. Yay! Yippie! The wicked Red Devil is DEAD! Don't get ahead of yourselves, with the premature celebrating of The Red Devil's demise, peons. It isn't exactly like you really have anything to celebrate about. We all know Baltasar is in the middle of a 1-3 run since his return, which doesn't sound to impressive when looked at through the eyes of a fan. In fact it sounds down right Peter Vetra style awful. Still the fact remains that you can't name one of those losses that is actually the fault of The Red Devil. He wasn't pinned for any of those losses. Was he? No. He wasn't. You'd like to be able to say he was but you can't. Since it didn't happen. Tough luck. Move to the back of the line and cry about it some more. He gave up? What do you mean he gave up? Oh that time. The one where he supposedly gave up to that poor submission move. Yet if you people weren't so stupid, to a degree that only , then you'd have realized he had no choice but to tap. He had a cramp in his right testicle. He had to give up so he could stand up immediately, without having to take the time to pick up two PRIME tag team jerk offs, and walk that cramp out. I mean come on now, have you ever had a cramp in one of your testicles? It hurts like hell right? Wait a minute. Why am I even bothering to ask any of you people? The idea that you'd know anything about what your nuts are for, or that they are even there for that matter, is as insane an idea as Kyle Lamen getting laid. Even with that cancer eating his body into nothing he hasn't got a prayer of using his penis on a woman. Unless of course he paid that woman a hundred dollar bill before hand and it just so happens that riding charity cases is what she does for a living. Maybe a woman like that. Certainly not a self respecting one though. Now I'm off track. Where was I again? Oh yeah. Culture Shock. That little Pay-Per-View thing that PRIME has coming up. Dual Halo and all that. You know what I'm talking about so I don't really have to set the stage for you. Even as stupid as some of you people are the very least I can do is give you credit for knowing about the up coming event that PRIME is throwing. The only people not fortunate enough to know about Culture Shock and Dual Halo matches are those silly enough to tune into AWC shows. We all know we don't have any losers like that here. Except for Face-Eater...oh and Paddy O'Shea. But neither of those two matter anyway. When was the last time O'Shea did anything that people actually bothered to take notice of and talk about? Win a GTT match you say? Bah I haven't the time to keep up with such low level events. Now if we were talking about something like Seven of the Best, Infinite Gauntlet, or that old PCW thing called Alliances then you'd have something. So yeah. He hasn't done anything. I mean it isn't like he beat Rich Rollins or something in the tournament. That would NEVER happen. You know. Just learn to live with it if you don't. Obviously Face-Eater isn't exactly someone to hang your hat on for ratings. Unless you really enjoy PRIME's version of a B-rated after school special known as The A-List . It's like the A-Team only more light on the team, heavy on the gay, and certainly scaled down for the three fans that they actually do entertain each week. Those fans being the same people who were the stars of Brokeback Mountain. WOOOO! Gay entertainment PRIME certainly is on it's way to attracting that ever important 18-34 STRAIGHT male demographic. Way to go! Que the cheesy thumbs up and all that jazz. Once again I been led off course by the hijinks of PRIME superstars. I tend to do that from time to time when the subject matter I have to cover is so...for the sake of not being mean we'll say intriguing . Now let's get back to Culture Shock shall we? That event where Baltasar is going to win this little thing known as The Dual Halo, much like he won The Fallen Halo last summer, and walk out of Culture Shock with the prize of all prizes. The Golden Ticket, so to speak. No not that lame ass Golden Ticket that gets you into the Wanka Factory and allows you to rub elbows with a tool bag like Johnny Depp while he does his best Michael Jackson impression. That sort of Golden Ticket is for people who love Golden Showers. Give that ticket to Danny Ferguson. He'll drink it up. The real Golden Ticket, however, is the prize that the Halo winner receives. The one that allows them to receive any wish their heart desires. Some people choose things that are small and insignificant, like title shots. They waste their pick by choosing with their heart instead of their head. That or the wish their empty, evil, uncaring chest desires. In the case that they are like Karina and have no heart or brain with which to make choices with. Just ice cold jealousy running through their blood. Oh yeah you better believe she is jealous. Completely jealous of The Red Devil. Then again who wouldn't be? Just shut up. You don't buy her being jealous? Are you a daily subscriber to Around The Horn? This isn't Buy or Sell time morons. You will simply listen to what I have to say and believe it as closer to the Gospel truth than anything that Hoyt Williams has ever said in his miserable life. She is jealous. Jealous that he attacked her, not cause he thought she was a target to make a name off of, but cause she was nothing more than a pawn to get the attention of The Deville. That is what eats at her. Karina is an egomaniac. Don't you people realize that? She has to be the center of attention at all times. What you actually thought that Aimz hating her was simply her trying to make a name for herself? Please people. She was doing her best, albeit not nearly good enough, attempt to save you people from the maniac of ego that is known as Karina. Yeah you can laugh about it all you want. The truth hurts don't it? Can't stand the fact that you're precious Karina is no better than say...Tyler Nelson. Except that Tyler Nelson is actually pretty cool. I mean he has an ego the size of the state of Texas. He should though. He is rich. He has power. He has the ability to make careers or end them. You don’t have any of those things or abilities. So cram it! Anyway. Back to K-Wolf. There is a reason that she spit in the face of The Deville. That she took advantage of his emotions and destroyed his big moment. Then again how big could that moment really be? I mean he was beating up on a guy with a completely destroyed back. A guy who hadn't been anything important in the wrestling world in quite some time, at least not as a competitive wrestler. Was that really all that special a moment she ruined? For arguments sake we'll give Angelo Deville the credit he wants for that huge victory and say that it really was. We all know the real truth about it so it's alright in this case to adhere to a little white lie for the sake of pissing on the notion that Karina is anything but a greedy, envious, jealousy driven cock sheath. She was envious of the amount of attention that a guy like Deville could garner while facing a former wrestler with a bad back. Jealousy, folks, is a very powerful emotion. Just as STD's are a powerful deterrent, for anyone with half an ounce of sense, to avoid having sex with women who have been on drugs, had multiple sex partners, or just generally look like the trash that someone scraped out of the gutter. Please no Aimz jokes at this time. Those are so 2005. Speaking of jokes that brings me to the current booking pleasures of PRIME. With Culture Shock just a couple weeks away it was the “brilliant” idea of PRIME suits to stick Baltasar in a handicap match against Cruz and Vetra. Does this sound like something to “wow” the crowd with to anyone else? *Crickets chirping can now be imagined in the background.* My thoughts exactly. In all honesty with a match of that caliber everything that needs to be said about it probably already has been said. ***** Pitch black. No sound. Empty. The feeling that his soul was indeed gone for good. Hours before the sound of a slamming door and the site of tears streaming down a beautiful face had left that feeling behind. Along with others. Hurt. Sorrow. She was gone. This wasn't some trip to the store. It wasn't a few hours of shopping with her mother that took her out into the world without him. No it was Baltasar who'd given her the reason. The fire and drive for him to be respected and noticed, in the world of wrestling, had forced her out the door. She loved him. He knew it. Even sitting there, alone in the dark of his living room, he knew she loved him. He knew that in all honesty this was hurting her even more than it was hurting him. He was crushed though he'd more than likely never show it. She'd left him there as payment for his actions over the recent weeks. Payment for making her life uncomfortable. The only medicine to easing the pain that his career decisions were causing. 'What the hell am I doing?' Silence once more. ***** 'What the hell are you doing?' Her words were filled with anger and grief. The pain of what he'd done only a couple of weeks prior still covering her every word. Plaguing her every thought and every passing moment of her life since his return to PRIME. 'What? I'm doing my job. Making a living. You have to be respected to be the best and make the real money.' His words came out not without the confidence of total belief in them backing them. Even in his mind he knew the way he was thinking was flawed. 'You'll trade life long friends for the glory of being considered in the same breath as Karina or Angelo Deville?' Her disgust was as clear as the light blue sky above them. The neighbors, who weren't at work or off shopping, were either standing in their front yards or watching from the safety of their homes. A circus for all the people in their community to watch. 'If that is what it takes to get to the top. It puts me closer to where I need to be. To where I can take care of this family forever.' 'Bullshit! This isn't about providing. We were doing just fine without you destroying our lives. Do you realize I get called every single day by former friends, people more like family, cursing me for what you've done. How could you? You've turned your back on the two people who cared about you more than anyone in this world outside of myself.' He blinked. Making sure to show no signs of any of this affecting him. He'd never let her, or anyone else for that matter, know that he regretted what he'd done. Felt like the fool he was for turning his back on Enrique and Angelus in the persuit of personal glory. 'You don't even care do you? I don't even know who the hell you are anymore!' He stared into the raging brown eyes of LaCosta. His wife, the love of his life, and his best friend. She meant the world to him. Still she didn't really know him. Didn't really know how he truly felt about these type of things. He'd never even let her that close. Never would either. 'What I care about is removing all obstacles from my path. I don't need people holding me back when I've got a reputation to build.' 'Reputation? That is what this about? You want people to speak your name in hushed whispers like other people? I guess it means the world to you that they fear your every move cause of how you'll treat everyone around you.' 'Yes. Professional wrestling isn't a fairytale world. People don't get what they deserve by sitting back and waiting for someone to decide they have earned it. You have to make people take notice. You have to take what you want.' 'What is it that you want? Fame? Fortune? Respect? Fear?' 'All of the above.' 'At what cost? Will you sacrifice our marriage for these things?' He was stunned. He had to take a moment to let it sink in. She'd never brought up something like this before. 'What are you saying?' 'I'm telling you that if you continue to pursue your career this way then I'm gone. I won't stand by and watch you hurt all those who loved you before you were the world famous Baltasar!' His eyes narrowed. How dare she mock him like that? Who was she to question his choices in the wrestling business. The guilt he felt for those decisions only moments before seemed to fade entirely. Replaced by a rage building in him. How dare she threaten to leave him for trying to better his standing in wrestling. 'Nothing is keeping you here.' Coldly he threw her a set of keys to their Jeep Cherokee. She blinked. Tears filling in her eyes. He simply stood there staring. Believing she'd never leave. Even if she did she'd only get a couple blocks away and she'd be right back. 'Fuck you Montrell.' **** Peter Vetra and Cruz you've come at a bad time. A really bad time. I have nothing in my life now but pain. Pain that I am more than willing to share with others. You two are just as good as anyone, at this point, to share that pain with. I've made my bed and now I have to lay in it. Something like that right? It was my actions, decisions, and inability to admit my wrongs that have led me to this moment. I'm alone. Angelus and Enrique have been removed from my life. Removed from anything that even resembles caring about me any longer. My wife is gone. Staying with her mother now. Hell for all I even know it could be for good. I've sacrificed everything for this. I've given up all that I once had in an attempt to gain what I believe is more. That brass ring awaits me just over the near horizon. Cruz earned the 'right' to have a handicap match with anyone. Bet when he won that he had no idea he'd be dragging his fee for six months on it and then being forced into the ring with a pissed of Red Devil. Vetra, my old partner and friend, this is truly a bad spot for you. The truth be told I have no ill will toward you. I have no real desire to futher inflict punishment upon you. I used you as a messenger to Hoyt Williams. I'm sure you're pissed off about that. Pissed off that you've been used as the whipping boy in my quest to show Hoyt that I don't need him or to prove anything to him. Fair enough. Now we both have to do something we don't like. I have to destroy someone I really have no ill will toward and you have to be the target of that destruction. Don't blame me. Blame whoever it is in PRIME that hates you. This is one of the few times lately where I can say what I have to do isn't my decision and has nothing to do with being my fault. At any rate, no matter who is at fault, I will do what I have to in order to secure a victory. It's my last shot to get ready before Culture Shock and The Dual Halo. So take advantage of the moment boys cause for a very short time you get to be my personal training tools for Culture Shock. Before I go there is one other pressing matter on my mind at this point. A little matter of being made to look like a completely useless fool on the last edition of ReVolution. Yes, Nova, I haven't forgot. I haven't simply shrugged it off and decided to move on and let it be. You've got a mark on your chest from this point forward. A huge mark that I'll make sure to try my hardest to punch a hole through during the Halo contest. Once again my partners were right there to let me down, as usual, but it was you Nova who tried to take the greatest advantage of me. Trying to use me as your personal stepping stone back to the spot in the company that you believe you were at before suffering an injury. I'm not you're stone however Nova. I'm not the guy you'll be making your mark on and then simply walking off into the sunset of the PRIME main event. There are no free passes for this ride dear boy. At Halo I'll be attempting to show you what the true meaning of an injury is. It won't be that school girl type of beating you took from Youngblood. This one is of the more lasting flavor. Ask Angelus. He'll tell you how it goes. Well...he would if he could talk.
