Nova Battles the Legendary Legends (Wetherlution Invitational)

14 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/14/05 “Nova! Nova, wake up!” Nova winces under the glare of the fluorescent overhead lights as he opens his eyes slowly. “Nova! Come on!” Nova springs up, disoriented and unsure of where he is. A small, fat man in a black shirt with headphones on is standing in front of him. Nova stares at him for a moment, trying to place him… …Fred. The guy’s name is Fred. Nova remembers now. One night after a show, Nova was walking out and managed to catch some toppling boxes of equipment that, if spilled, would cost the techie who hadn’t heard them slipping away his job. Fred was grateful, of course, and offered Nova a few rounds at the pub a few blocks over after he got off his shift. Nova got wasted. Fred was so drunk, he… “Nova! Come on, man!” Nova comes to, staring at Fred’s sweaty, furrowed brow. “Jesus, Fred…what is it? Spill some equipment or something?” Fred doesn’t bite. “Har, har…you’re on, man! It’s out to get time there, time to get there out, nam!” Nova cocks an eyebrow and stares at Fred. There’s something not right, but he can’t place it. He turns to look at the stairs leading to the entrance ramp. It’s bathed completely in darkness… …but what really sets Nova off is the silence…absolute silence…no buzz from the crowd, no drunken random cheers… Nova pops his neck and turns back to Fred. “Alright, I guess I better get out the-“ Nova stops as Fred’s bright blue eyes seem to burn into his skin. I must be going crazy…weren’t his eyes brown before? “GO! GO! Get out in the you should there, man!” Fred’s insistent nagging doesn’t miss a beat, and he begins physically pushing Nova towards the stage. “Hey, chill out, Fred-O…I’m going, I’m going.” Startled, Nova swats Fred away and jogs up the steps. He stands just behind the curtain, waiting to hear the intro of “Heaven’s A Lie” for the nine-millionth time. … Nothing. Still silence. “Sound check! Fuckin’ sound check, please! Where’s my music?!” Nova looks around, but no one is there, a stark contrast to the lively hustle-and-bustle of the normal PRIME backstage show crew. Nova hears steps running toward him from the hallway and breathes a sigh of relief…someone’s finally coming to tell him what the hell is going on. “Bout time, guys…I was shitting a brick waiting for you mother-fuc-“ Fred explodes into view, his face dark red, veins bulging out of the sides of his head. “I TOLD YOU TO GO! GO! GO! GET DOWN THE TO THE RING DOWN RIGHT NOW!!” Nova stumbles backward defensively, and falls through the curtain, onto the stage. He braces himself for laughter, or boos, or maybe applause if he plays it off right… …but there’s nothing. He stands up slowly, sweat soaking his headband as he stares at the surreal picture in front of him. A packed arena of 20,000 plus… …and none of them are looking at him. He gets up and waits a moment, but still no one seems to see him. Slowly, he begins walking down the ramp. CLANK! CLANK! CLANK! It is the first time Nova has ever heard his footsteps on the metal walkway leading to the ring…even in his professional infancy, the crowd’s general buzz drowned out most other noise. Nova makes eye contact with a man and waves at him. The man waves back. Thank God! Someone sees me! The man keeps waving and his eyes stay on the same position even as Nova walks in front of him. Nova turns to follow his line of sight and his shoulders slump in disappointment as he sees that the man was just trying to get the camera’s attention. Nova makes it to the ring and slides in, standing up once inside and tossing some phantom punches to loosen his shoulders up. He looks over at the announcer’s table. Nick is picking his nose and Richard is spilling a chili cheese coney on his lap and screaming about said coney’s unreasonably hot temperature…but neither are looking at him, and neither are talking into their microphones. BOOM!!! Pyrotechnics explode on the ramp, shattering the thick silence like a sledgehammer across a plate glass window. “MONEY!” BOOM! “POWER!” BOOM! “RESPECT!” BOOM! The fans come alive, echoing a mixture of boos and applause as Tyler Lopez appears at the top of the ramp. Yes, it’s strange to Nova that after his own surreal experience, everything for Lopez seems to be normal…but what’s even stranger is that Richard and Nick’s microphone dialogue has begun scrolling down the giant videoscreen above the entrance ramp, as white text with their names high-lighted in blue. Nick: Tyler Lopez is… Richard: A total turd-bomb. I hope he dies. Nick: Kind of harsh there, Rich… Richard: If by harsh, you mean the cool slang “harsh,” then yeah…I’m totally harsh. Tyler Lopez begins making his way down the ramp…but about half-way to the ring, all of a sudden the guardrails collapse and a cross-section of fans on both sides ambush Lopez, sharpened bamboo shafts raised above their heads as they begin goring him with their make-shift weapons. Lopez’s screams for mercy can barely be heard above the bloodthirsty roar of the crowd. A trickling stream of blood oozes its way down the ramp to the ring as Nova stands in the ring in horror. Richard: You know…I didn’t think it would really happen. Can I be sued for this? Nick: I don’t think so…want any of my Pepsi? It’s diet, and you know I hate diet drinks. Richard: Yeah, I’ll have some. Their tribal lynching complete, the fans move back to their seats and re-erect the guardrails. As Lopez’s corpse twitches on the entrance ramp, all eyes turn to the top of the arena. Nick: Could it be?! The fans pop so loudly upon seeing Tony Rolo appear at the very top of the stadium seating aisles that the roof of the arena shatters and the debris is carried off in the wind. Dark storm-clouds brew over the opening created by the Tony Rolo MARK-OUT~! And before Nova can even get a handle on the situation, heavy rain begins pouring down through the hole. Nick: THE LEGEND HIMSELF! TONY ROLO! THE LEGEND IS HERE TONIGHT, FOLKS! Rolo begins climbing down through rows and rows of fans, yanking up anyone in his path and Rolo-plexing them. Bodies begin flying in every direction as Rolo Rolo-plexes his way toward the ring. Nick: ROLOPLEX! ROLOPLEX!! ROLOPLEX~!! A woman holds out her baby boy for Tony Rolo to kiss, and he grabs her around the waist, Roloplexing her into Row VV Seat 31 before catching her child in mid-air and Roloplexing him into a tub of nachos. Nick: ROLOMANIA! THE LEGEND! THE LEGEND! On the other side of the arena commotion is being caused as Jon Kano appears in the corresponding opposite location that Rolo first was seen. Nick: JON KANO! JON KANO! THE PCW LEGEND IS HERE! “P-C-W! P-C-W! P-C-W!” The crowd begins a “P-C-W” chant so overwhelming in its ferocity that if there was a roof left on the arena, it would probably be blown off again. Jon Kano plunges headfirst down through the crowd, Kano-spearing fans left and right like an enraged, horny, pregnant rhinoceros. Nick: KANOSPEAR! KANOSPEAR FROM THE LEGEND! Richard: AW-BUH-GU-AWD! As Rolo and Kano plow through the crowd on a collision course for the ring, the “Eternal Punk” Shane Wethers appears at the top of the entrance ramp, fuming with anger, a microphone in his hand. “Excuse me! Excuse me!” Shane screams as he hops up and down, obviously flustered, “this is MY invitational! MINE! Someone pay attention to me! Who cares about Rolo?! Who cares about Kano?! Who the hell ca-“ Wethers is interrupted as one of the beams snaps and falls off the top of the entrance, crushing him where he stands. Nick: That support beam just TELEGRAPHED a body splash onto the Eternal Punk! Nova stands in the center of the ring, helpless as he watches Rolo and Kano maul a path to the ring. Each man comes exploding through the guardrails, and Rolo grabs one of the cameramen, Rolo-plexing him so hard that he bounces off the protective mats and flies all the way through the gaping hole in the ceiling of the arena. Nick: LEGENDS~! Nova watches as both men charge the ring on opposite sides of him. “Oh, shit…” Kano catches Nova first, Kano-spearing him so hard that his torso completely separates. Nova’s upper half lands with a nasty SPLAT! on the far side of the ring. Richard: That Kanospear looked almost as treacherous as the one Kano gave Unknown PCW Alumnus during Obscure PCW Wrestling Show in Nostalgic Yesteryear! Nick: Almost, Richard! This match is friggin’ LEGENDARY! Nova lies choking on the mat, trying with his arms to keep his guts from pouring out in a steaming heap onto the mat. His vision clouded, Nova can make Rolo grabbing his lower half on the other side of the ring and Rolo-plexing it before wrapping up one of the dismembered legs with a schoolboy pin. Wesley James climbs out from under the ring and slides in to make the cover… “One! Two! Three!” Nick: Nova’s dismembered leg has been eliminated! NO ONE survives the Roloplex! Richard: And how, Nick! And how! Nova sees Rolo coming towards him, and feels himself lifted into the air. He catches one last glimpse of the mat before Rolo brings him down on his head. Nick: ROLOPLEX!!! “ROLOPLEX!!” Nova shoots upright in bed, clutching at his stomach. He feels around for a minute… …it’s all there. Nova breathes a sigh of relief and lies back against his pillow. “Honey?” Ariel shouts from the bathroom, “are you alright?” “Yeah,” Nova replies, “I’m…I’m fine. Why?” “You screamed something just now,” she explains, “I dunno…I thought it sounded like ‘Rolodex,’ but that wouldn’t make sense…we don’t have one. I remember you telling me you thought they were ‘really gay.’” “I did,” Nova confirms, “I…I don’t know what I was saying. Probably just babbling…” Nova rolls over to look at the alarm clock. 11:35 AM “Shit!” he hisses, throwing back the covers and jumping out of bed, “Ariel, why’d you let me sleep in?” “You said you were meeting her at 12:30,” comes Ariel’s reply from the bathroom. “I said ‘11:30!’” Nova groans through gritted teeth, trying not to yell. “Oh, shit, Chris, I’m sorry.” “Don’t worry, it’s fine,” Nova says as he yanks a pair of jeans on, “she can wait a few minutes.” He pulls on a white t-shirt and grabs his jacket from off the chair by the bed. He walks into the bathroom where his wife is straightening her long dark hair…much longer than when they split four months prior. She puts down the iron and walks over to him, placing her hands on his cheeks. “Don’t leave without any answers, okay?” “I won’t,” Nova assures her before giving her a kiss, “love you.” “Love you, too,” Ariel echoes, “see you this afternoon?” “You bet,” Nova says with a wink before turning to leave. He scans the park as he steps out of Ariel’s car. He takes off his sunglasses and puts them in his jacket pocket. She said she’d be by the swings, and since she was the one who contacted him, he figured she wasn’t gonna no-show on him. A waving arm from the area near the pavilion catches his eye… …and he can see that it’s her. Liza Williams. Nova’s stomach churns. It had been over four months since he had seen the woman who at one time had his complete confidence and trust. Their last meeting still haunted him at night. Nova stands frozen in the doorway to the room. Liza stands in front of him, panting, blood pouring out of her nose and onto her dress. She had just broken the dress strap to her right shoulder, and punched herself in the face. Nova could say nothing, think nothing…no time to contemplate being set up, or the ramifications of said setup… …the only thought was to get out of there. Quickly. NOW. “STOP! PLEASE! HELP! SOMEBODY PLEASE HELP!” That fateful night had started the descent into madness, the result of which was Nova’s divorce, restraining order from his house, and financial liquidation. And now he can see her waving at him from a playground. Now he can finally get some answers. Nova forces himself to walk over to her, wincing with each step as her image becomes clearer and clearer, more and more like the woman who once stood in the kitchen with his wife and drank beer and laughed. They all used to laugh a lot. Nova fights a double take as he gets close enough to really make her out… …she’s different. Much different. Her radiant, sunny beauty seems a distant memory, replaced by haggard features and age brought on by stress and too many late nights. She’s smoking a cigarette…so Nova smokes a cigarette. She looks up at him from her seat at a painted wooden bench, her eyes watery and her mascara runny. “Hi, Nova.” “Liza,” Nova says coldly. She doesn’t deserve the liberty of congeniality, and Nova is giving no quarter on this cold Detroit day. “I’m glad you agreed to see me, Nova,” she says before taking a drag of her cigarette. “What’s this about, Liza?” Nova asks, cutting to the chase, “why did you ask to see me?” “I needed to clear the air,” Liza replies softly. “Good luck,” Nova spits bitterly. “Please,” Liza says, holding up a hand, “Nova, I’m sorry.” “Well, you’ll excuse me if…,” Nova begins, but she interrupts him. “Nova, I don’t expect you to forgive me, but please listen. I was an idiot, okay? I was never a writer. I was a stupid girl who made her way to Calgary to try and be a model or get into show business or something like that…you’ve heard the story a thousand times. Anyway, of course, I didn’t get any offers…there were girls all over the city starving and trying to find work, most of them better looking than me…so when a guy came to me and told me he’d pay me 50,000 dollars to find you and trick you into thinking I was a pro-wrestling writer and then screw you over, I jumped at the opportunity...” “I’m sorry, am I supposed to be sympathetic to any of this?” Nova hisses. “No, just listen, okay?” Liza begs, tears welling up in her eyes, “I didn’t know you. I didn’t know your family. To me, it was a chance to make quick money and the livelihood of some schmuck didn’t matter to me. But then I got to know you…” “And you did it anyway!” Nova screams, his eyes wide with anger, “how, Liza?! How, after you stayed at our home, and played with my fucking daughter! How could you do it to Ariel?!” Liza tries unsuccessfully to mask a sob. “I…I don’t know. I was confused…I thought I was too far into it to back out…and I was scared. I had already spent some of the money, and I thought if I tried to back out, the man…” “Big Poppa?” Nova asks. “Yeah, dark hair, orange sunglasses, big guy?” Liza asks. “That’s him,” Nova replies coolly, rage collecting like thunderclouds in his eyes. “Well, I was afraid if I backed out, he’d want all the money back, and I didn’t have it,” Liza continues, “I know there aren’t excuses, and I’m not trying to make any. But that’s the truth. So I went through with the plan.” “You sure did,” Nova mutters. Liza winces at the jab before speaking again. “I’m not asking for forgiveness or friendship. I just…I just wanted to tell you about it.” “Well, now I know,” Nova says, tossing his butt before lighting a fresh one. He turns and begins to walk away. “Nova!” Liza calls, and Nova turns around to look at her, “I just want you to know, I gave the money I had back to him. I couldn’t bear it.” Nova stares at her for a moment before reaching into his pocket and pulling out his sunglasses. He puts them on his face and takes a long drag from his cigarette. “You’ve should’ve kept the money.” With that, he turns and walks back to the car. Liza watches him go, and when he slams the car door behind him, she lays her head in her arms and begins to cry. That night, Nova lies in bed, one arm stretched across underneath his wife. She rolls over and looks into his eyes. “So did you get the answers you were looking for?” Nova looks over at the dresser, at a picture cased in a black frame of the Supreem Regime at the end of 2003 or beginning of 2004 in Advantage Wrestling’s third go-round. Four people, all soaked in sweat but jubilant in victory, stand in the center of the image. A grin crosses Nova’s face as he remembers one of the happiest, most professionally significant times of his life. To the left in the picture is Nova’s old tag-team partner, Teemu McCorde, the AW North American Title slung over his shoulder. To the right is Sapphire, at that time Big Poppa’s girlfriend, with one of the Tag Titles around her waist. Nova stands in the center, his long hair dripping with water and sweat, the AW World Title over his shoulder, and behind him towers Big Poppa, one hand on Nova’s shoulder and the other clutching the other half of the Tag Titles. Nova’s eyes settle on that image of the group, but more importantly, of him and Big Poppa, back when they could trust each other with anything. “Nova?” “Wha?” Nova asks, having accidentally tuned out his wife. “I said, did you get the answers you were looking for?” Ariel repeats. Nova’s eyes still rest on the image from what seems now like an alternate universe. “There’s only person who has the answers I’m looking for…and it’s gonna take more than a chat at the playground to get them, I think.” Nova rolls over and drapes his other arm around his wife. In every really big-game situation I’ve been thrust into since arriving in PRIME, I’ve failed. Sure, I won the 5-Star Title, but the others weren’t on their games, and the match itself was a complete cluster-fuck. There’s a difference between being told the night of that you’ve got a TLC match against Opponents X, Y, Z…it’s another altogether to know your foe, feel the hype, and then deliver on it. I’ve yet to deliver on it. I’ve yet to beat one of the Bodas or Emilio Rages or Vampir Nosferatus. I eliminated Rolo in the Halo match, but I couldn’t even deliver on it, being eliminated by Aimz shortly after and watching my shot at immortality dissipate with the three count. With ReVolution 58 arrives another opportunity to move up a step. I face…well, Tyler Lopez and Shane Wethers… ...but more importantly, Tony Rolo and Jon Kano. I don’t know much about Jon, other than that he comes with credentials from one of the most hallowed battlegrounds in our sport’s history. What I know about Rolo boils down to this: After this week, Karina Wolfenden might be Universal Champion, and she’s hands-down the most dominant force in PRIME right now… …but “The Specialist” is the most respected. When people look at Tony Rolo, they see a man who has fought every war in this company’s short but storied history. Two times the holder of the now-defunct Global Title, alongside a Tag Title reign with Joey Troy…the first inductee into PRIME’s Hall of Fame…responsible for the most-remembered moments in PRIME’s history… …he’s done it, man. He’s done everything that all of the newcomers to PRIME don’t even dare to dream about…and he’s still here, fighting to keep his spot at the top, and doing a damn admirable job of it. And on Wednesday, he’ll be fighting me. The list of people who have beaten Tony Rolo is shorter than his win-loss record would indicate. That list includes names like Ian English, Rock Startling, Vampir Nosferatu, Black Angel…the men whose names came, in their own ways, to define the legacy of this company. Will I take my place among them? I don’t know. I really don’t. What I do know is that sooner or later, I WILL capitalize on one of these opportunities…and then another, and then another, and then another. I will see the top before I leave PRIME. I will affect change in this company.
