Born of a Broken Man

Date: Look inside Reflecting on December 16th, 1993 Time: 8 AM Sleeping on the floor was nothing new to me. I always enjoyed the company of the Smith’s, so whenever I went over to their home that was a sacrifice I made. The previous evening was full of entertainment and excitement, another positive about spending the night at their home. That was the dosage of becoming humanized I craved. Around this time, a phone call would enter the kitchen, from my father, telling me to gather my things up and head on back home. But I didn‘t have a home to go back to anymore. I threw open the front door and greeted the world with a thunderous yawn and outstretched arms. Looking straight ahead I could see a couple of cars passing by on the road, the occupants inside going off to provide for their own. Next, I poignantly turned to my left and saw my old residence in the distance. Inside, I knew that a dark demon was resting in his proper place. Anthony…My father…would never again get to avenge mother’s death on my being again. The monster that he helped create turned against him with his own blade of hatred. I was what I always wanted to be…Emancipated. The light at the end of the tunnel was far, far away from me at this point…However the door is locked behind me and I swallowed the key. There was no going back from here. My eyes closed and I returned inside of the house to help make breakfast with my new family. ------------------------------------------------------------ Time: 3 PM I sat on the couch and watched my favorite afternoon program while Christian sat on the opposite end of the couch. The program featured a 12 year old kid trying to struggle through the different phases of life. In the episode, the kid named Douglas Yancey Funnie is worried that his green skinned nemesis, Roger Klotz, has a crush on his sister Judith Funnie. To Doug, his sister was hard enough to manage on her own, and if his nemesis were to become her boyfriend that would cause him to pull out the few strands of hair on his head. The episode was fittingly titled ‘Doug’s Worst Nightmare‘. "If only life was like a cartoon, eh Christian?" "Now that would be something. Our lives would be pre-determined. We would not have to bother fighting for our survival, and not have to look over our shoulder every three seconds…Nice sense of finality to boot." I gave a nod and sipped some of my Pepsi. The house was completely quiet with the kids outside playing in a neighborhood snowball fight. Christian suggested that I stay behind so that we could have a talk about a couple of things. Taking a mental guess, I assumed that we were not going to discuss the differences between cartoon reality and actual reality. "You know Devin, I know that you are still getting over what happened with your father, but soon you are going to have to start pulling in some money." And there it was, "What, like get a job?" "Something like that" "None of the other kids in the house have jobs." "Yeah, but they are in school, and you’ve never been within ten miles of a school, there’s a difference." "Aren’t you making enough to provide for all of us? I doubt that I’ll require that much." "I’m at the point now Devin where I’m either going to be making a massive amount of money in the next couple of months, or I’m going to be flat broke. I want to have all of the bases covered just in case. You also have an immense amount of time to dedicate to making money, whereas if I stuck one of the others in a job, it would probably bring their grades down." "What is it exactly that you do anyway that would mean you making either a lot or no money? I thought jobs had a consistent amount of money coming in every week." Christian wanted to give a smug laugh, I saw it, but he decided to hold back. "Let me put it to you this way…The kind of work that I do isn’t necessarily on the books of the work force. You know that John Gotti guy that we saw on the television a couple of years back, went to prison? I do that kind of stuff only on a more local scale." I did my best to hold in my expression of shock, but the small smirk that appeared on Christian’s face indicated that bits of it surfaced. An attempt to dwell on his occupation did not occur. Christian kept talking. "Dev, the world is like a tree and you cannot walk up to it and take whatever money that you wish. You have to battle to the death with that one limb and come out the victor with your last ounce of strength. Then the next day you have to go back and do it all over again." I gave another nod, and took a swig of the Pepsi. This was not the conversation that I wanted to be having at that particular moment. You usually know when you are ready to take the next step on this journey, and I did not feel mentally that I was there. I was far from being ready to take a forward step. "Soon you are going to have to get on your feet, that is a must. I’ll be there to help you along the way, but you will have to be able to handle yourself after a while. I won‘t whip you with a belt and make you sleep in a casket if you fail, but you might end up wishing that would happen. You’ve got some great talents inside of you that most out there don’t have, so I’m going to treat you like you should be treated rather than oppress you." With Christian, I soon found out that when he wanted to talk, the man was going to keep talking. I could not get a word in at all, so finally I conceded. "The road that you are going to go down is going to be one where rules do not necessarily apply as they would for the average John Doe. Think of it like this…If you play your cards right, you won’t have to wrestle with that limb. Instead, you will have an axe to cut it off with and take all the money." "That seems like the most reasonable solution. Why doesn’t everybody do it?" "Heh, my boy there are consequences for everything. The consequences of being caught in this kind of anarchy would be most unimpressive." "Did you just have a Bill and Ted moment there?" "I believe so." ------------------------------------------------------------ Time: 8 PM "What do you see when you look at that?" "A rather nice looking house" When Christian told me that we were going sight-seeing, I jumped at the chance to do some open road exploring, another luxury that I never got to experience under the tyranny of Anthony Shakur. For twenty minutes we drove down back roads that I never knew existed. The car came to a stop at the end of an abandoned street. We started walking away from street, almost a good mile and a half, until we got to the destination, a ditch across from a driveway that leads down into a massive house. From the looks of it, the occupants inside must be rather happy inside their kingdom. Christian gave a half-frown on the left side of his face. "Pretend…That you are in a war and this is the house of the enemy." "Ok" "What do you see now?" I switched my eyes to see the nuisances and liabilities that the house presented. No longer did I look at the home as luxury. Instead, I saw it as a fortress of opportunity. "There are a lot of thick trees around the house, giving a lot of vantage points for an opportunity to spy." "Good, keep going." "Behind the house the only thing that I can see is woods. That would make a tremendous means of entering and escaping without being detected from the front." "Point on, point on, if you will also notice that their lights do not cover all of the yard. You could get right up to their trees and, unless they look hard, you aren’t going to be seen. Beats the hell out of having to climb up a tree to gather intel." "Right" "Alright, let’s continue with this. You want to go inside the enemy’s house and get a first hand account of what they have. Where are your entrances?" "Front and back door, also the windows." "What else do the windows present? Assume that you don’t want to be seen once inside." "You would have to make sure that they are all covered so that nobody would be able to see you moving." "Correct again" My mind started to ponder the situation: A car ride alone with Christian, not telling Chris and the rest of the gang where we were going, and talking about "war". Not to mention the fact that we were standing across from a house I‘d never seen before. Finally I decided to ask, "What exactly is the point of all this?" Christian didn’t even blink out of place. "Devin, remember earlier when we talked about the world being a tree, and how I would take you down the road to obtain an axe to chop the limb off?" "I remember" "Well this is how one avoids the stress of wrestling with the limb. Hypothetical question, would you rather make 50 dollars a day, 9 hours a day, 5 days a week, or would you rather make thousands in fifteen minutes?" Realization hit me like a tidal wave. I couldn’t speak, but Christian could. "This is how you achieve the latter, and you have already eliminated the majority of exterior problems. Now all you have to do is study the people inside." I could not believe that I had been deceived that easily, and by someone not even ten years my senior. "And that’s what I’m going to do now, right?" "Precisely" This would be my opportunity The age old question of morality came up and spewed from my mouth. "Isn’t it wrong to steal from other people?" Christian gave the smile that a parent would give to a child before manipulating their argument to a way that the child would agree with. "The way that I look at it Devin, it is all about necessity. Now let’s take a look at this place: Two floors, about three or four acres on the entire property and this has got to cost somewhere in the six figure range. I would garner to say about one quarter of a million. The people who have that kind of monetary rolling in, they don’t NEED all of the things inside of that house. No, they WANT to have them, but need is not in their vocabulary that often these days. Alright, Now let’s look at you…You ain’t got shit. You have no money to your name. You have no toys to call your own. You have absolutely nothing that you can say "Hey, that’s mine." Who do you think NEEDS a couple grand more, rich people who have money coming out the wazoo, or fifteen year old kid who would be homeless if circumstances were crueler? I really couldn’t retort his argument in any capacity. I needed the money more at the time. I didn’t have a foundation and the people across the street almost had a legacy. Christian folded his arms across his chest and looked down at me. "Yeah, you know I’m right on this. I will come back to pick you up in a couple of hours. I want you to mentally record everything that you see inside of the house, get as close as you can, but don’t overdo it. You have vision right there with the binocs, use it wisely. Remember Devin, you need this, they don’t." Christian turned on his right foot and started to walk away from the scene, leaving me all alone with a pair of binoculars and a family whose lives I would soon attempt to destroy. My eyes stayed fixated upon him until the darkness consumed him. I would be in for one hell of a litmus test. I also saw a brief glimpse of the light at the end of my tunnel. I would have to become Christian Daniels in order to survive in this world. Yet I will do it because I do not want to become my father. A broken man. I grabbed the binoculars and crossed the street toward the large trees that surrounded the home.
