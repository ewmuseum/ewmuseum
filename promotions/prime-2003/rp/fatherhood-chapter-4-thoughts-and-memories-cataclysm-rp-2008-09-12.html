Fatherhood - Chapter 4 - Thoughts and Memories (Cataclysm RP)

12 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 12, 2008 SIVEC HOUSEHOLD HILO, HAWAII SEPTEMBER 12, 2008 I had to leave Nikkie in the hospital in Charleston after her injury in late August. It was now mid-September, and she had just come home. I had to go out on the road, taking care of my PRIME obligations: house shows, autograph sessions, and ReVolution tapings. Add that to this debacle we’ve got going on with those bruhs over at SCCW, and I’ve got a packed schedule. She wasn’t doing too well, and it was more psychological than physical. Her injuries weren’t awful, but they certainly weren’t pleasant either. A broken arm and a concussion aren’t fun things to try to live through, especially whilst being pregnant at the same time. I wouldn’t know, but I’m just assuming. I pulled up to her parents’ house and put the car in park; I hadn’t seen or talked to her parents since Nikkie told me she was pregnant, so I had to take a second to collect my thoughts. I scanned the outside of the house, and was glad to see that her father’s 2001 Ford pickup truck wasn’t sitting in the driveway beside me, meaning he wasn’t home. Now, he always liked me, but I’m not sure if that’d change now that I was the one who got his only daughter impregnated. The good news for me was that he wasn’t here, and I’d only have to deal with her mom. “Bryyyyyyyyyyyyyyyan!” I heard the high-pitched voice of Nikkie’s mom, and shot a look over toward the front door of the house, where Pamela Sivec was standing, waving her arms frantically. She always loved me. Pam is a middle age woman who stands at about 5’4” and her physique is much like her daughter’s. She’s short, but well rounded, with curves to DIE for. I’m tellin’ ya, bruh, these two are a perfect example of the proverbial apple not fallin’ far from the tree, if ya know what I mean. Her shoulder-length brown hair was highlighted with blonde accents and put up in a ponytail. Every time I see her, I hope that when they say that if ya look at a girl’s mother, that’s what she’s gonna look like at that age, they’re right. ‘Cuz I’m not gonna lie, she’s a pretty attractive older lady. But I digress. I opened the door and hopped out of the Civic, flashing a smile and sending a wave back to my future mother-in-law. She was all smiles as I closed the door and walked toward the door. Upon hitting the porch, Pam extended her arms for a hug, and I happily obliged. She embraced me, and exchanged greetings again. “How are you doin’, honey? Everything alright?” I smiled and put my arm around her. “Yeah, I’m dealin’. How are you guys doin’? Handlin’ everything alright?” Pam cracked a smile and reassured me. “Yeah, we’re all gonna be fine, you just wait.” She lead me into the house and shut the door behind us. I walked into their familiar foyer, but noticed a few changes since the last time I had been in the house. New carpeting and new wallpaper and paint had been added to the house, giving it a much more upbeat, happy tone. There was some new furniture in the living room, which was where Pam lead me to. We sat down on the couch, and I gazed around the room, lookin’ at the different decorations Mrs. Sivec had pieced throughout the living room. There were pictures of Nikkie growing up, pictures from the Sivec wedding, pictures from Nikkie’s prom, and one picture that particularly stood out… …a picture from the night where this all started. The night before I left to train in Honolulu for the Dual Halo. “You remember taking that picture?” Pam must’ve seen me eyeing the picture up. “Oh, yeah I do.” LIE. I was loaded at my party. It was my last hurrah as a “normal citizen” in Hilo, and I was gonna go out with a bang…a “bang” turned out to be pretty literal. I didn’t remember taking the picture at all. Hell, I didn’t even remember having sex with Nikkie until she told me the first time I came home. “That was at my going away party, right?” Pam nodded her head happily. “Took that right before you and Nikkie left the party, Bryan. I’m assuming that’s when…” As her voice trailed off, I felt a rush of blood flow into my face. I was blushing like crazy. I couldn’t lie to her, you know? What was I gonna say? ”Oh, no Pam. I wasn’t the bruh who got your daughter pregnant. That was that OTHER guy.” The next few moments seemed like an absolute eternity. I simply continued to try to pass the time by observing the new fixtures inside the living room, fiddling with my hands, and trying desperately to stray from anymore awkward conversation. Finally, Pam spoke up. “Bryan, Nikkie’s in her room. If she’s sleeping, don’t wake her, but otherwise, I’m sure she’d like to see you.” I nodded my head and got up from the couch, thinking to myself… ”Thank God…that was almost bad.” * * * I crept up the stairs slowly but surely and finally got to her room. The door was cracked, so I slowly opened it and tiptoed inside. Nikkie’s eyes were closed and her sling-enclosed arm was laying on her enlarged stomach. She looked so peaceful; I couldn’t help but to smile. I slowly walked closer to the bed until finally sitting down in the rather comfy chair positioned at the side of the bed. I sunk down into the recliner and propped my feet up. My eyes then focused on my sleeping fiancée, resting so peacefully in the bed. But then, the thoughts began to creep into my mind. The flashbacks to the night Rhett attacked the two of us… ”Hey Dawkins!” Locke’s final words before his attack on us echoed through my mind. I must’ve twitched or something, because after hearing Locke’s words in my head, Nikkie turned her head to look at me. “Hey you,” she cooed as a small smile appeared on her face. It was good to see her smile again, because it had been so long. I smiled back, and grabbed a hold of her hand on her good arm. “Hey. How you feeling today?” Her smile remained upon her face as she turned her head to face me. “I’m doing pretty well. A little sore, but good.” After rubbing her enlarged stomach, she smiled and looked back at me. “This little guy in here is really startin’ to get on my nerves. Little…bruh…won’t stop kickin’ in there! I guess he wants out.” Hearing her use the word “bruh” provoked a chuckle out of me, which I appreciated. This was what I loved about Nikkie; her ability to always find a way to cheer me up and make me smile, no matter what the situation was amazing to me. That’s something you don’t find in many, if any, people anymore. “Well, sooner or later he’s gonna come out, and believe me, we’ll both be much happier once that happens.” I was talking with some hesitation and had an unsure tone to my voice, something Nikkie wasn’t used to. Another one of her abilities is that she can immediately tell when I’m lying, unsure, or worried about something. Apparently she’s got a way to read me; maybe it’s the way I talk, maybe it’s the way I carry myself, maybe it’s the expression on my face. I don’t know what it is, but she’s just got that amazing ability to figure out when I’m not being that lovable Hawaiian that everyone seems to love anymore. “Bry, what’s wrong?” I sighed, knowing that I can’t lie to her. “This whole thing with Locke. That was just unreal. That bruh had no reason to come after me when you were around. Ya know, I figured he might have a little more manhood than that.” My anger was starting to intensify, and Nikkie could yet again tell. The smile on her face morphed into a frown, and she started to pull her hand away. I simply continued. “I mean, he could’ve KILLED you, Nikkie! KILLED! You wouldn’t be a mom, you wouldn’t be a wife, I wouldn’t be a dad, and I’d be a widower! There’s just no reason he had to bring you into this. I’M the one he has the problem with, not you!” “Bryan, I know…but things don’t always work out how they should. I mean, look at us.” She paused and grabbed a hold of my hand again. I just stared blankly off into space. “Six and a half months ago, did you expect to be a soon-to-be father? If you say yes, I’m gonna have to call you a liar, hun, because I know for sure that I had absolutely no plans to have a baby pop out of me in November.” My scowl turned into a straight face. I couldn’t tell her she was wrong, because she wasn’t. “Yeah…you’re right. But when I see you, all I can think of is Locke, and what that freak did to you. I can’t let that go unpunished, ya know? He put both of your lives in danger, and that just can’t go unnoticed. I can’t handle it. He’s going to pay, and he’s never going to forget it.” Nikkie frowned, and squeezed my hand harder. “Bryan, just be careful. I’m still here, and I’m not leaving you. I’ll never leave you. I love you, don’t ever forget that. Just BE CAREFUL.” Be careful? For what he did to Nikkie, I’m willing to throw caution to the wind. Rhett Locke deserves to die. DIE. And ya know what, bruh? I’m gonna do everything in my power to make sure he comes DAMN close to death. Nikkie kept a tight grip on my hand and we sat there in silence for a few moments, thinking about our situation, our baby, each other, and other things. My other thing? Rhett Locke.
