Cruz Control

2 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-2-06 You lose. I mean, the biggest match of your life. AGAIN. You've got a new manager. You're a fantastic pure wrestler, because you've been trained by the best. Why can't you seal the deal? What is keeping you from reaching that upper echelon? It's not a glass ceiling, that's for damn sure. You've built your own ceiling, and it feels like it's made out of lead and goddamned Kryptonite. Oh, and you lost the one title you'd had, that Infinite Gauntlet. ...to a guy who thinks that he's a vampire. FUCK. So you've got two options. Your first option? Crawl into your bed. Cry about it a little bit. Feel very, very sorry for yourself. You insist over and over again that when you wrestle Chandler Tsonda for the Five-Star Title that things will be different. But they won't be. Because you're not changing anything. Or there's the second option. You call former PRIMEate and current ACW superstar Shawn Stewart. You offer to pay him a handsome amount of money to teach you how to hit people. Hard. He tells you that you have a strong wrestling background, but you brawl like a little girl. You're a wrestler, not a fighter. You train, and train, and train. You work yourself to exhaustion...and then you start to pick stuff up. For the first time in your life, you learn how to REALLY punch someone. Not a stunning move to set up your hurricanrana. You learn how to follow through, and break a jaw. How to move after the punch so that they don't see you coming on your next attack. Shawn works you to the bone, and it pays off. You walk into the panther's den, and you come out alive. Beaten, battered, but whole and alive. And then, on a clear Wednesday night, you beat Damien Cruz within an inch of conciousness. Not because you want to. Not because you need to. Because you can. And because there's a wild, angry animal inside you that's tearing at your guts to escape. You're going to send a message to your father, that his genetics, what makes him a Lamen...it runs in you as well. You're going to send a message to PRIME. You are no longer to be trifled with. The next splash page with a picture of your face beaten to hell on the website...well, you'll be smiling in that picture. You're going to send a message to Chandler Tsonda. A message that if he steps into the ring with you, you're going to take something from him. It may be the A-List's Five Star Title. But it also may be his model good looks. It may also be a pint of blood. And it may be all of the above.
