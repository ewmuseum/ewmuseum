Bringing The Band Back Together (Part 4, Mega Job: The Epic Tag Team)

21 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 21st, 2007 Mega Job. Words fail to truly describe them. Actually, when words encounter Mega Job, they usually run away in the opposite direction with their arms flailing in the air while screaming in terror. Mega Job was, in fact, a tandem. More particularly, a trio. They consisted of Beef (the Slightly Annoyed), El Janito (the Mexican Stereotype), and Steve (the Rambling Communist). They work as a pseudo film crew, after badly botching their previous line of work: superheroes. Yes, that's right, that's how intelligent they are. They actually thought being a superhero would get them money and hot chicks. Like Tony Stark (Iron Man). Except that they missed the part where Tony Stark was already ridiculously rich and the fact that he's an alcoholic douchebag. But hey, let's not fault Mega Job for trying. I mean, they went the extra mile and wore actual superhero suits. Made of spandex. They're totally not gay. That's why El Janito was wearing a pirate's hat. ... ...Work with me here, people! A year ago, Beef had decided that since they were a film crew and not a superhero team, that they should convert their "Job Cave" (in reality, it's just an abandoned warehouse in downtown Beefville that Mega Job took residence in) into the "Job Studio". Basically, the only thing they changed was that they were trying to build movie sets and using the cameras and costumes they stole from the fWo on their way out of the promotion in order to create their super special awesome movies. Including "You Have The Right To Remain Bad IV: This Time, Whatever Was Previously Personal Is More Personal Than It Ever Was Before". If you're wondering what the other three were all about, let's just say that they all involved "Cocky" Craig Miles and shouldn't be viewed by anyone in the history of EVER. Except [name withheld]. At the moment, however, Beef had a new vision. A vision that he had long since held off until he could find a boat that wasn't so completely ruined that it couldn't be used. After all, it's not like Mega Job could ever afford a decent living space, much less a real boat. They had to find one in a junkyard and yoink it. (A note to new readers: In the literary works of [name withheld] (read: crap written when bored at five in the morning), to "yoink" means to "steal".) As you may have guessed, Mega Job don't exactly do a whole lot of things that involve "not stealing". This brought me to Beef and his vision, and El Janito and his pirate outfit. Beef wanted a movie about pirates. He wanted to call it "Pirates of the Carribean", but when he found that some douchebags beat him to that name, he renamed it "Pirates of the Great Lakes". Not nearly as snappy or witty, but ultimately better in his mind. The cue cards of the movie even have "Carribean" lined out and have "Great Lakes" written above it. Meanwhile, El Janito wasn't too pleased about being cast as "Shankey the Openly Gay Pirate". He was given this role after El Janito wanted to include a straw hat as his choice of headgear for the movie, and Beef simply snatched it away claiming that "pirates don't wear straw hats". Neverthless, ever the consumate professional, El Janito stood on the front of the boat and pointed toward the open seas (or concrete) with his rapier (or broomstick made to look like a sword) held. "Avast, ye scoundrels, lest I penetrate you with my sword!" El Janito claimed. Beef broke down in hysterical laughter. Janito, flustered, threw down his "sword" and his hat and stormed off the set, "I'll be in my trailer!" Beef raised his megaphone and shouted into it, "YOU DON'T HAVE A TRAILER!" "Then I'll be in my bed! Crying in a fetal position!" Janito exclaimed, before he crawled into bed, curled up into a fetal position, and started crying to himself. Beef sighed and raised his arm into the air as if he was going to grab something, but nothing was there. However, he held the position, and eventually, an unknown man appeared with coffee. This man was certainly not Steve, because Steve was a midget. This man will be henceforth known as Grundle McMiles... the only man so low on life's totem pole that he has to be an assistant to BEEF. "Grundle," Beef said, as he took a swig of his coffee, "Whenever you decide to try and make it in this business of ours, don't be like that guy." "It's Bill, sir. Bill McDou--" the assistant attempted to say, but Beef cut him off by putting his megaphone right in Grundle's face and yelling. "WHEN I WANT YOUR OPINION, MCMILES, I'LL GIVE IT TO YOU!" he shouted. This caused "Grundle" to be blown away about twenty feet and into a wall, where he made a Grundle McMiles-shaped dent in the wall before collapsing onto the ground and sleeping the sleep of the knocked unconscious. By the way, in Beefville, some laws of physics were outlawed. Seriously, check the Beefville Hall of Records. It was passed in 2001 by Mayor Chester McCheese and the City Council. Look that shit up. Beef sighed as the door of the Job Studio was suddenly kicked open. The Job Studio door was well-known for usually having to be opened with a swift kick. The person who did the kicking was a cute girl, Alexandria Malone, who simply walked in and surveyed the room she had walked in on. Since the last time we saw Alexandria, she had taken her hair out of its ponytail and let it fall straight down to her shoulders. She wore a long-sleeved black shirt and a pair of jeans, with a set of black boots worn underneath the jeans. "Holy..." Beef said, staring at the girl. He rubbed his eyes just to make sure, adjusted the beret he wore on his head, and knew what he had to do. "Janito! JANITO!" Beef shouted, excitedly, turning towards where Janito was laying. Janito popped up from his fetal position and glared at Beef, "What!? Can't you see I'm all mopey?!" "Janito! Look! An honest-to-God hot chick! In our pad!" Beef said, pointing frantically at Alexandria, while completing failing to notice that Codemaster and the Falk Brothers had walked in behind Alexandria. El Janito had suddenly pulled a 180° turn from his previously somber mood, then proceeded to set a land speed record by a Mexican by going from his bed to by Beef's side and gawking at Alexandria in about 1.4 seconds flat. In all of the confusion of this, Steve had walked over to Alexandria, while eating what appeared to be a bagel. Alexandria, who had been distracted by Beef and Janito's antics, had no idea that Steve was checking her out from behind and from the side. Steve swallowed the part of the bagel he had been chewing, raised a thumbs up, and pronounced his verdict. "NINE." Steve said. Steve's comment surprised Alexandria, because she didn't notice Steve, nor did she expect that a person like him would have a voice quite like his combination of gravel and sonic waves. "Did you hear that, Beef?!" Janito shouted, excitedly. "I think I did, Janito! Steve says she's a 'nine'!" Beef said, with equal excitement. The two adult-sized members of Mega Job joined hands and did a little square dance right then and there. As Alexandria crossed her arms and tried to restrain herself from destroying all of humanity, the Codemaster and Scott Falk walked up to Steve and squated down next to him. "Hey, Fujin, how've you been?" the Codemaster asked, holding out his hand for a high five, which he got from Steve. "AWESOME." Steve said. He pointed at Alexandria, who was just a little bit confused. "You have a good eye, my little friend!" the Codemaster said, before he turned to Scott Falk, "Doesn't he, Scotty?" "Fuck you, she's mine." Scott Falk said, bluntly, to Steve, with his eyes narrowed. This prompted Alexandria to kick Scott right in the ribs. Hard. Then Beef and El Janito came running over to "defend Alexandria's honor" (in reality, they both wanted her) by beating down Scott Falk with stomps. "You know," Beef said, as he stomped, "This feels weird somehow." "Yeah," El Janito commented, as he also stomped, "It's almost as if we're not meant to do this." At about that time, Robert Falk casually came over, grabbed Beef and El Janito by their heads with one hand each, and then casually clonked them together like coconuts, causing both of them to slump to the ground. Robert then helped Scott to his feet. The Codemaster stood up and stood next to Alexandria, before he looked down at his little friend, "I assume, Steve, that you're the only one smart enough to figure out why we're here?" Steve thought about it, before he offered his opinion, "HOOKERS?" "No, Steve." the Codemaster said. Steve placed his hand on his chin, as if he really was thinking about it, before he offered a new suggestion, "BEER?" "Alex is eighteen, and everyone else," the Codemaster said, as he glanced in the direction of both Falk Brothers, El Janito, and Beef, "...is simply too dangerous to have drinking alcohol." Steve nodded sagely, knowing that the alcohol intolerance of El Janito in particular has gotten Mega Job in trouble with the local gang of Yugoslavians on more than one occasion. Which is funny because Yugoslavia doesn't even freaking exist any more. "BAND?" Steve offered next. "Yes. Yes, that's right. We're putting the band back together." the Codemaster said, before he pointed at Steve, "Are you a bad enough dude to join our party?" "INDEED." Steve said. The Codemaster gestured and Beef and El Janito, who just got the crap stomped out of them by Scott Falk in retaliation for their own stomping of him earlier. "And them? They're not bad enough dudes or anything, but I can always use their cannon fodder... I mean, help." the Codemaster asked. Alexandria stared at him, disbelieving that he'd bring Mega Job into the stable JUST for the sole purpose of taking beatings. "Yes! We'll go!" Beef screamed, though he wanted to come only because it'd be the closest thing to action (being in the same stable as Alexandria) he'd ever get. Alexandria continued staring at the Codemaster, causing him to turn and go, "What?" "Cannon fodder?" she asked. "Hey. Wrestling a rough business. Besides, these guys are malleable and bounce really well." the Codemaster said, gesturing at Mega Job. "In bed!" Beef shouted in response to that, causing Scott to "accidently" kick him in the head. He even said "oops" after he did it. The Codemaster put his arm around Alexandria (causing notable jealousy from the other Rogues), while Alex just went with it, "Alex, we've pretty much reunited nearly the entire crew, now. With you, that'd make for seven people." "Nearly? That implies that there's still someone missing." Alexandria said. The Codemaster sighed, and removed his arm from Alexandria's shoulders. He yet again pulled out his notepad from his coat, and after making a few marks on it, he showed it to Alexandria. Alexandria looked at the note, blinked three times, and seemed genuinely surprised, "Him?" "That's right." The Codemaster had adjusted his note since we last saw it, and now he had made a real list of people he wanted in his group, and had striked out all of the people he had picked up.
