Precio De Opportunidad - Tres

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Continuous Precio de Oportunidad - Tres LAST TIME... Suddenly, a pair of hands appears on the partition between stalls. In a quick motion, the boy in the hooded sweatshirt vaults the wall into Reggie's stall. A sudden grunting yelp is heard upon contact, and then silence. CUT. EL SPIFFY The Spiffy is immortal, friend. I taunt death like it's a little brother. Even if it grows bigger and more powerful than I am, I know that it can't get the better of me. CUT. 76933: So as long as the kid's just following, we can't touch him? 52112: Bingo. But once they open a line of communication, then everything is in danger, and we become involved. CUT. MAN IN SUIT (sighing) I anticipated that you wouldn't take us up on that offer, and that's why I've instructed El Spiffy to deliver his farewell speech to the fans at tonight's show. CUT. The young man pounces from his seat and reaches inside his coat, causing them to stop in their tracks. YOUNG MAN I'll give you a hint. It's not a fucking nine-iron. CUT. MATT You've just made a deal to bring El Spiffy to America, and now you have no Spiffy. I need a job in America. CUT. 52112: In other words, El Spiffy IV, meet El Spiffy V. Danny's eyes widen as he glances back and forth between 'Luis' and 'Juan Carlos.' FADE OUT. PRECIO DE OPPORTUNIDAD - TRES FADE IN. INT. UNNAMED AMERICAN SPORTS ARENA - NIGHT Several crew members move around equipment in the calmed aftermath of a professional wrestling show. How do we know it's a pro wrestling show? Bear with me, dammit, where the hell else would it be? Maybe you didn't notice the 'RUSH Wrestling' logo on that crate over by the wall, did you? Yeah, maybe you pay attention next time instead of nitpicking. Damn e-smarks. The RUSH branding puts is in the year 2002, three and a half years prior. A few of the wrestlers are shuffling towards the door. Jonathan Drachen is preparing for his night gig at the pizza place. Chris Matthews is rifling through some unattended bags. Through this dispersing crowd comes El Spiffy, who by now, has become known as El Spiffy!. Also, by know, you should know the drill about the man under the mask. At the moment, though, we're the only ones who can identify him. Spiffy! ignores the crew working around him and makes a beeline for the back door. He doesn't make it, though, and I think we all knew that. JEREMY 'KRAYZIE' HOWARD (O.S.) Spiff! Spiffy! Hey, Spiff! RUSH owner and operator Jeremy 'Krayzie' Howard, a notable from sister organization PCW, jogs into the frame as El Spiffy! slows down reluctantly. He turns to face his boss, who is beaming. JEREMY 'KRAYZIE' HOWARD Good stuff tonight, man! People love this stuff between you and Statuz Quo! I've got a great idea for next week, though, as long as you think you've got another show stealing performance like that in you! El Spiffy!, a little more somber than most times we've seen him, sighs and nods. JEREMY 'KRAYZIE' HOWARD You doing ok, kid? I'm not overworking you, am I? Because I can hold you out for a while if you need a breather. Spiffy! shakes his head. EL SPIFFY! No...I- JUAN CARLOS SMITH (O.S.) Of COURSE he'll be ready... Juan Carlos Smith, El Spiffy!'s co-manager and a member of his personal Spanish announce team, walks into the frame and puts a hand on his exhausted client's shoulder. El Spiffy! looks considerably less pleased to see him than you'd expect. Despite the attire - the sombrero and fake mustache in his hand and the bright blue suit, the identity of this man is unmistakable. It is the same agent, 52112, who had been following Danny and Luis back in the present time. After shaking hands with Howard, he smiles and continues his thought. JUAN CARLOS SMITH He just needs some sleep. He'll be raring to go in seven days. Spiffy! only nods in agreement. Howard gives a caring smirk to both guys. He starts to turn away, then stops. JEREMY 'KRAYZIE' HOWARD You guys got plans tonight? I think a few of us are going to hit a bar before the ship sails tomorrow morning. JUAN CARLOS SMITH (looking at Spiffy!) Well, he's got a car waiting for him. Flying...home. I'll take you up on that, though. I've got a few ideas to throw your way. JEREMY 'KRAYZIE' HOWARD Sure thing, man. I'll catch you once we're packed up here. Giving a wave and a farewell to Spiffy!, Howard disappears in the direction he came. Smith steers the luchador towards the door, and the two disappear outside. The shot follows... EXT. OUTSIDE OF THE ARENA - CONTINUOUS The same gold El Camino we saw at the start of this series idles a few yards away. The two men progress towards it, and the conversation is entirely one-sided. JUAN CARLOS SMITH A cab will pick you up in LA. Your hotel is pretty much walking distance from the Center. Remember the schedule: Once your match is over, you've got to be back at the airport in a few hours so you can catch up with the tour. El Spiffy! grunts affirmatively. As they approach the car, a portly, unkempt man in a Corona t-shirt leans out. JULIO DIAZ PEREZ O'NEIL Where you been? JUAN CARLOS SMITH Chatting with the Boss. I'm going to get in his ear while you're driving Ms. Daisy. JULIO DIAZ PEREZ O'NEIL At a bar? JUAN CARLOS SMITH (nodding) I'll let you know where it is. The fat man sits back in the car, content. Slinging a bag into the back seat, El Spiffy! opens the door to climb in. Juan Carlos approaches him and stops him from entering. Looking around the parking lot to ensure relative privacy, he begins untying the back of the El Spiffy! mask. JUAN CARLOS SMITH You know not to run, right? Another affirmative grunt from El Spiffy!. A close-up SHOT on the manager's hands, unfastening the headgear with relative ease, shows the tattoo of an analog clock face on the inside of his wrist. JUAN CARLOS SMITH Good. We wouldn't want to have to tighten the leash. Done tying, he pats Spiffy! on the shoulder and leads him into the car. As Juan Carlos pulls away, he brings the mask with him and quickly pockets it. The camera follows the door of the car as it shuts. Through the passenger-side window, we see Julio staring across the way at his passenger, Matt Register. The bags under Register's eyes are visible even at night. He lets his head slump back against the headrest. Julio shifts the car into gear. JULIO DIAZ PEREZ O'NEIL You look like shit, kid. Matt closes his eyes and doesn't respond. The El Camino pulls off into the night, and the camera follows, slowly panning upwards and outwards. Juan Carlos Smith stands in the lot by himself, watching the taillights disappear. For the first time, our narrator joins us. VOICE The third quality of the Spiffy hosts, the third characteristic that all Spiffys hold, is a chronic, destructive dedication - a willingness to sacrifice anything and everything for that extra chance at success and fame. Sometimes the harshest of Evil traits is simply a perversion of noble intentions. The scene darkens until only the red rear lights of the car and the blue suit of the Watcher can be seen. CUT TO: INT. ALLAN'S OFFICE - DAY Danny and company, despite having the resources to surround and subdue the young man has them presumably at gunpoint, stays back and safe. They seek instead to protect their star, who is perfectly fine with that. His eyes betray his confusion, though, as they dart back and forth between the two agents, 52112 and 76933, and the young man, who has recently been introduced to us as Luis Alazar. DANNY FERGUSON I have a question... The three newcomers look to Danny, and Luis, who clearly has control of the floor, gives him a wave with his concealed gun hand, signalling Danny to proceed. It might have been a bad idea. DANNY FERGUSON Why the FUCK should I care who this dickface is? Luckily, Luis has a sense of humor, and only gives the remark a short smirk. I mean, you'd have to have a sense of humor to trail a guy through the streets of LA and then ambush his manager in the john and steal the dude's clothes just so you can 'have a conversation.' Of course, it also means you're probably batshit crazy, so let's see where this is going. Luis turns his head towards 52112, who both he and Danny have identified as 'Juan Carlos.' LUIS ALAZAR I thought you couldn't get involved yet. 52112 Someone's gotta keep you from dressing like that. (pause, when no one's amused) You've made active contact with another member of the project, and that's a no-no, Luis. DANNY FERGUSON Project? LUIS ALAZAR (ignoring him) Doesn't he have to be receptive to make it active contact? DANNY FERGUSON What FUCKING project?! Luis gives a nod towards Danny to try and emphasize his point. 52112 rolls his eyes. Danny, as you could guess, is not amused. DANNY FERGUSON Excuse, me, fuc- Luis turns straight towards Danny, making sure to point the gun hand at him. LUIS ALAZAR (interrupting) Does the gun mean anything to you? I'm asking questions right now, superstar, so I'm afraid you'll have to shut the fuck up. 76933 Come on, punk, we all know you don't have a gun in there. Let's give this up and avoid some ugliness, huh? Obviously the Ferguson crew is just as confused as ever. Danny, not thinking safely, has drifted from the side of his bodyguards, who are individually plotting their own hero moments. Luis doesn't react to being called out by the agent, but lowers the hand that would have been holding the gun. He backs off a little. LUIS ALAZAR Where's Julio? Pause. The two agents look at each other before 52112 finally answers, forgoing any circumventing language in favor of a rare commodity - the truth. 52112 Transferred. Luis nods, satisfied with himself. LUIS ALAZAR And this guy? 76933 None of your business, kid. He starts to move forward, but Luis backs away, closer to Danny. He suddenly looks very worried. 52112 Relax, Luis. He's a new Watcher. LUIS ALAZAR I see. So he's expendable. 52112 furrows his brow at the comment, but that's as long of a reaction as he can get. Before 76933 advances one more step, a shot rings out, blowing a hole in the pocket of Luis' jacket, and consequently, in the large agent's face. The man drops to the ground and everyone in the room instinctively reacts. The other agent dashes for him, and the bodyguards both dive for Danny, but he's already gone. Luis pulls a small firearm from the jacket pocket and tosses it on the ground next to the agent as he drags Ferguson for the door. The two former El Spiffys disappear into the hallway amidst the ensuing chaos. CUT TO: EXT. LOS ANGELES - DAY It's mid-day in LA (we can lay by the bay and roll in some hay, what do you say? I just may!), so the usual orange glow is somewhat subdued in lieu of SEARING HOT SUNLIGHT. The shot starts in the sky and slowly moves down to the street. It's about as hopping as a kid who's never been to Los Angeles in his life would write it to look like. In fact, you could picture it just as you do in the movies, because that's what I did when I wrote this. Off in the distance, the STAPLES Center has a buzz to it, a glow that is usually reserved for Lakers games, because if you remember, Shaq's still wearing purple and yellow, looking like the ugliest Easter Bunny to ever pin Boda. Maybe second ugliest, but we're not here to debate. VOICE The mask is known to cling to its host, to not let them go. But it knows just as well that the men cannot survive without it. Like an abusive husband, it knows to let go...they will come back once they realize there's nowhere TO go. We move down the street, taking in the scene. Some young women in sundresses with shopping bags walk down the street, and give some very uppity looks towards a group of scantily clad prostitutes standing at a corner. A billboard above that corner touts 'Novocaine', the new movie from Steve Martin that's sure to bring him back to prominence. 'Outstanding!' says the schmuck from USA Today. At the end of the block, a smallish family-owned - or at least manufactured to look that way - diner sticks out amongst the rest of the glitz. We begin to zoom in, and pretty soon it becomes obvious why: the familiar face in the window facing the street. The same harrowed and drained look pulls on the face of Matt Register. The sandwich in front of him is only a quarter eaten, as his eyes keep getting drawn out. When we get close, we can see the reflection of the STAPLES Center in the window, conveniently in the area where he's staring. As he watches, though, his eyelids sink, and he fights off sleep, shaking out the cobwebs. WAITRESS (from behind him) You need anything else, hun? MATT REGISTER Nah. Thanks. He doesn't bother to turn around. As the apron - the only thing of the waitress we see at his level - moves away, Matt runs a hand over the faint stubble growing in on his usually bald head. Maybe it's a fashion statement, or maybe it's something he picked up to get the mask on easier. Maybe he stopped caring about how he looked a long time ago, because he's not the one buying t-shirts and tickets. Although, when it comes to Matt Register, nobody's buying t-shirts or tickets. Before he can drift off again, his lack-of-focus is broken by another body sliding into the booth across from him. He turns slightly to see who it is. Danny Ferguson, looking ages younger and a lot more chipper than we've ever seen him, beams at the wrestler. DANNY FERGUSON Hi. Matt snorts and turns back to the window. MATT REGISTER You want something? DANNY FERGUSON I know you. MATT REGISTER (not even looking back, sighing) No, you don't. DANNY FERGUSON Yes I do. Matt closes his eyes for a second, which quickly turns into five seconds. Without opening them again, he answers. MATT REGISTER No. You think I'm Jason Snow or Tom Lexian or Eli Flair or one of the other wrestlers in town, but you obviously don't watch and you're just some jock-sniffing homo who wants an autograph to show to his friends and hang on the wall like he knows what he's talking about. But I'm not any of them. My Hancock wouldn't get you enough to make back the fee it costs to post on eBay. There's a pause. If you didn't know better, you'd think that the Wrestling God III tournament, affectionately known as RPG3, was Register's last hurrah, his final shot at making it as himself in the business instead of hamming it up under a hood. You'd think he was ready to quit. And maybe he was. But that wasn't stopping Danny Ferguson, who leans in closer and drops his voice. DANNY FERGUSON You're El Spiffy. Matt doesn't turn from the window, but his eyes lock on Ferguson immediately. Danny's smile widens. DANNY FERGUSON Name's Danny Ferguson. I'm an actor. Remember 'Four Is A Crowd' from the 80s? MATT REGISTER (warily giving Danny his attention) My mom used to watch it religiously. DANNY FERGUSON Well, that's me! Or it was. MATT REGISTER So you're a former actor. DANNY FERGUSON I'm between jobs. MATT REGISTER Right. DANNY FERGUSON Anyway, I did a couple of b-movies with 'Mr. Smooth' David Wayne a few years ago, and he turned me on to UCW. I know all about you and El Spiffy. MATT REGISTER It's El Spiffy!. DANNY FERGUSON Whatever. Matt finally turns back to the table, knowing full well that he's not going to end this conversation until he gives it his undivided attention. MATT REGISTER Back to my original question: Do you want something? Danny freezes, trying to resist what he knows is the natural answer. DANNY FERGUSON (giving in) Yes. Satisfied with his assessment, Matt signals for his check. Danny scrambles to cover. DANNY FERGUSON But so do you. You're splitting time between here and RUSH so you can get the name out there two different ways. That's cool if you like doing the Christopher Daniels thing, but I can see that it's wearing on you. You need the load lightened. MATT REGISTER (tapping his fingers on the table) And you can help? Danny leans in and lowers his voice again. DANNY FERGUSON Smooth trained me. I can work, but I can't do it as myself. Washed-up actor-turned-wrestler? They'd laugh me out of this town. I need a gimmick. Matt doesn't react, and Danny senses he's losing his audience. DANNY FERGUSON I'll pay you. The waitress brings the check and he backs off a little, reaching into his back pocket. He produces a wallet and a checkbook. Grabbing the check, he hands it back to the woman with a credit card pulled from the wallet. He gives her a wink and sends her on the way. Matt doesn't even protest. Pride and money have funny interactions. MATT REGISTER I thought you were between jobs? DANNY FERGUSON I still get 'Crowd' royalty checks. I never said I was hurting for money...I just want more of it. MATT REGISTER I can certainly respect that. The shot pushes back and out through the window as Matt seems a little more at ease. MATT REGISTER Let's talk business. The shot pans away from the diner and back out towards the STAPLES Center as we... CUT TO: EXT. LOS ANGELES - DAY The stairwell to the building that houses Allan North - and at the moment, Danny Ferguson's bodyguards, a naked Reggie Delray, a pair of mysterious 'Watchers' and at least one discharged bullet - opens out onto the back alley, and as the door blows open, Luis Alazar comes storming out, with a rather harrowed Danny Ferguson in tow. As they step outside, Danny pulls away, bringing Luis' escape to a brief standstill. Confused, and although he won't admit it, a little frightened, Danny questions his kidnapper. DANNY FERGUSON What the fuck are you doing? Luis is less angry and more flabbergasted. He holds his hands out to his sides, but then begins stripping off the clothes he was wearing, which if you remember, originally belong to Reggie Delray. LUIS ALAZAR Buying time. Taking the next step. Controlling the situation. I don't know, pick your favorite euphemism. DANNY FERGUSON (confused) I...but... Once the disguise is off, and Luis stands in a black band t shirt (Streetlight Manifesto) and his cargos, he gives Danny undivided attention. LUIS ALAZAR We've got a while before they chase us. The Watchers' top priority is discretion. Juan Carlos will have to clean his partner's face off the walls before he can release the hounds. C'mon, let's go. He starts walking, tossing the clothes into a dumpster. He walks a few steps, then turns to see that Danny hasn't moved. He walks those few steps back. LUIS ALAZAR You coming? DANNY FERGUSON Why the hell should I be coming with you? You don't have a gun anymore. I can go back upstairs. Luis just laughs. LUIS ALAZAR Well, first, I hope you'd remember about two minutes ago and what happened to the last guy who questioned whether or not I had a gun. There are a lot of pockets on these shorts. He starts to walk away again, but stops, in the same spot that he had earlier. DANNY FERGUSON And second? LUIS ALAZAR (without turning) Excuse me? DANNY FERGUSON You can't say 'first' without having a 'second.' What's your second reason? Luis turns back again, but doesn't move. LUIS ALAZAR Second...you've got other questions to ask besides 'why should you follow me?' The two make silent eye contact for a second, and then Danny begins walking forward, towards him. DANNY FERGUSON What the hell are the Watchers? CUT TO: EXT. LOS ANGELES STREET - DAY We move down the street, taking in the scene. Some scantily clad young women with shopping bags walk down the street, and give some very uppity looks towards a group of prostitutes standing at a corner, dressed more conservatively in sundresses. A billboard above that corner touts 'Shopgirl', the new movie from Steve Martin that's sure to bring him back to prominence. 'Outstanding!' says the schmuck from USA Today. A run-down but familiar-looking diner sits at the corner, but we don't get a good look at it before Danny and Luis emerge from the alleyway next to it. Luis makes a break for a bus that slowly rolls to a stop just down the road, and Danny follows. LUIS ALAZAR Probably the first time you've been on public transportation, huh? Normally, the question would get a snappy answer, but these are special circumstances for Danny, who is surprisingly candid. DANNY FERGUSON Not all of my shoes have been made of alligator, if you know what I mean. Luis turns back to him and nods before climbing onto the bus. Not wanting to look around for fear of being recognizes, Danny follows. FADE TO: EXT. PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION - CONTINUOUS The men shuffle to the back, amongst a sizeable crowd. As usual, there are only single seats available, because of all those jackasses who get on busses and require their own two seat pair to sit by themselves and stair out the window. But I digress. Danny takes a seat, and Luis takes the one behind him. Neither man is keen on getting noticed amongst the crowd, but there isn't a lot of time to wait. DANNY FERGUSON Now are you going to explain what the fuck is going on? LUIS ALAZAR Ok, here's what I know... Uh-oh, sounds like time for a flashback recap. We've certainly done a lot of that, and we already know what he knows, so let's try something new. CUT TO: INT. HOTEL ROOM - DAY If you haven't figured it out yet, we won't be leaving LA for the remainder of this hefty read, so don't get your traveling pants on. The hotel room in question is that of Matt Register, who is in an awfully chipper mood compared to, oh, every other time we've met him. He's looking out the window, once again staring down the street at the STAPLES Center, but the sense of longing that he reeked of earlier has disappeared. MATT REGISTER HEY, DAN-O! What's going on? He pulls away from the window to reveal himself on a cell phone. Nodding a little with the responses on the other end, he seems awfully quick to want to speak again. MATT REGISTER Right, awesome, sure, glad to hear it. Got the check in the mail. I haven't talked to Juan Carlos or Julio yet, but I don't expect them to be a problem. He paces the room a little bit before pausing to stare at the mirror in the room. MATT REGISTER Well, they won't be happy about my disappearance, but I've got a new...business arrangement that will avoid any trouble. He holds up a small credit-card-like object in front of him. The back is plain white with a magnetic strip, but as he turns it over, we see the front as far more interesting. A large, gothic-looking 'C' adorns the front of the card, along with the label 'Cronus Enterprises.' After observing the card, presumably for the 23rd time, he tosses it back onto a dresser. MATT REGISTER Yeah, just signed it today. Anyway, once you show up with the note from me, they'll pretty much have no choice but to give you the hood. It's a big match this week, Danny. You'd better be up to it. He does that awkward smile-nod-open-mouth thing that everyone does when they try to close a phone conversation with forced pleasantries. MATT REGISTER All right, good luck with it. Glad we could work this out. Maybe I'll catch you out there on the road someday. Take care of yourself, Spiffy!. He finishes it off with the fake smile and flips the phone closed. Heading back towards the window to observe the street below, he looks down at his hand, and twists the new gold and black ring on his finger. The ring features the same gothic 'C' logo from the card. The wheels are turning in Matt's head as we change to a new SHOT outside the hotel. It zooms away, slowly leaving he and the hotel in the distance. VOICE The dedication of the hosts is a sickness, an absence of logic and rationality. There becomes but one priority, and everything else is pushed to the wayside in its favor. Matt walks away from the window and out of our view. It's the last we will see of him. CUT TO: EXT. PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION - DAY The bus chugs along in the present day. Luis and Danny do their best to keep out of sight. Amongst the crowd. You might think their conversation is a little odd to hold in public, but you've obviously never been on a civic bus before. Everyone's saying some crazy shit. Our story barely cracks the top three for this particular route. LUIS ALAZAR ...so Register signed his life away to this 'Cronus Enterprises' company. One year later, he's hawking MetaTrim, that ephedra based supplement that killed all the high school kids. Two years later, he drops dead himself. Danny's been watching the city go by as they pull away from the water and move inland. As you could probably guess, he's not in a good place right now. A few ticks of silence pass, and then he turns his head back towards Luis, who sits in the seat behind him. DANNY FERGUSON And? LUIS ALAZAR (incredulous) And? AND? That's it! AND that's it! Don't you get it? Rolling his eyes, Danny turns back around and rests his head against the seat. He tries to humor the kid. After all, he has been kidnapped...and from the sound of his, his saviors might be more dangerous than the kidnapper. DANNY FERGUSON Sorry, kid. It's a great story. Somebody should write a script about it. (pause) Thing is, though, there's no lead role, no featured character that I can play. So unless you think we can write me in, I don't see what this has to do with me. LUIS ALAZAR Because you heard it speak to you. Danny doesn't answer. He doesn't even turn to acknowledge Luis, and the young man takes it as an affirmative. Now that his audience is once again captive in all senses of the word, he can elaborate. LUIS ALAZAR You heard the voice, and you know something's going on. And you still get that feeling at the base of your neck. The feeling that you never got away from the mask, no matter how far you ran. I know it's there, because if it wasn't, I would've been shot dead by one of your lackeys, or I'd have a cadre of LA policemen handy when we step off this bus. He pauses to let the moment sink in. A large number of people rise from their seats to shuffle off at this next stop, in front of the STAPLES Center. Luis sits back, letting his words sink in as the people move off the bus. Danny's eyes drift outside again. The sun has almost set and the exterior lights on the arena make it stand out along the horizon. After a few seconds of idling alongside the scene of the crime, the bus creaks, heaves and pulls away. Luis doesn't even lean forward to re-address Danny. They're alone at the end of the bus, so all he does is speak. LUIS ALAZAR Thing is, all these years of paying other people to do your thinking for you has made you soft. Did you notice Juan Carlos' hand? CUT TO: INT. OFFICE - EARLIER 52112, aka Juan Carlos, wrings his hands while speaking to Luis. SHOT closer, the same clip, but zoomed in. SHOT closer again, a repetition with all focus on the hands. As the right hand comes around into focus, a gold and black ring with the gothic 'C' logo is clearly visible. CUT TO: INT. PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION - EVENING LUIS ALAZAR They work for the same people. The Watchers are Cronus employees. The Spiffy thing, it's a setup. Whatever it is the mask does, it breaks you, it destroys your spirit. All of the Spiffys encounter a crippling setback, be it injury or death or simply poverty. You're broken, and then this Cronus company, whatever they are, swoop in to pick you up. Danny's been quiet until now, but without breaking the stare he's locked on the front of the bus, he lets Luis know that he's paying attention. DANNY FERGUSON So they're just handlers? They just follow the mask and wait for us to fall? Behind him, Luis nods, but doesn't give any other affirmation. LUIS ALAZAR El Spiffy makes us into stars...then it makes us into pawns. He punctuates his statement by rising from his seat. He grabs Danny's arm and pulls him up with him. Although the actor is far more receptive to his lead at the moment, Luis doesn't want to take any chances. The bus creaks to a stop in an nondescript section of the city, and the two men are the only ones waiting to get out. CUT TO: EXT. LOS ANGELES - NIGHT Luis and Danny step off the bus, with the younger kidnapper in a slight lead. He walks with a purpose, prompting Danny to raise questions. DANNY FERGUSON Where are we going, by the way? LUIS ALAZAR Anywhere. After a few more awkward steps, he pitches into a sports bar, dragging Ferguson behind him. CUT TO: INT. BAR - CONTINUOUS Without a word to the other three people in the place, he slides into a booth. Danny joins him. LUIS ALAZAR Like I said, they're going to find us. I'm not trying to run. We both know that it doesn't get us anywhere. DANNY FERGUSON (confused) I don't understand. Why did you pick here to stop? LUIS ALAZAR I need them to work for it if they want to find us. This buys more time. DANNY FERGUSON For what? More stories? LUIS ALAZAR You could say that. But I've said enough. I'm done talking. He leans forward and grabs at the chain around Danny's neck. Ferguson pulls away. At the end of the chain, now resting in Luis' hand, is a gold and black Cronus ring. LUIS ALAZAR Now it's time to for me to listen. Danny closes his eyes, unsure of what to do now that his secret is revealed. Luis lets go of the ring, and both men lean back in their booths. This could take a while. FADE OUT. TO BE CONTINUED.
