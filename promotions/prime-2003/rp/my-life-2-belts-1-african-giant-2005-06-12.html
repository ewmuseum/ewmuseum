My Life = 2 Belts + 1 African Giant

12 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/12/05 “NO!” Nick’s verbal protest echoes Nova’s own sentiments as he wobbles on the edge of the cage, arms flailing. As the tapes (and thousands of personal photographs, as well) will attest to, he does not regain his balance and rejoin the other competitors. He falls. Even in the split-second of clarity allowed to Nova during the fall that, pardon the cliché, seems to last forever, he can tell that he’s in relatively deep shit. He shouldn’t be falling here. He shouldn’t… A flash of light, all the colors of the rainbow, explodes in Nova’s mind, and he hits the surface of the water, shattering the calm with a deafening KER-SPLASH! He looks around…the water doesn’t sting his eyes. He can see clearly… …and immediately regrets the ability to do so. “Mmmrrpph!” comes his muffled cry as he stares down into the aquatic abyss, where, to his horror, an endless number of bodies float quietly in the deep, dark blue. They’re all shapes and sizes, mostly adult males, Nova can tell. The bodies aren’t necessarily in and of themselves the source of Nova’s terror, however. It’s what they’re wearing. Most all of them are dressed in tights or looser fitting pants, boots, many with elbow pads, wrist-bands, finger-less gloves, some bald and some with long, flowing hair that swirls above their heads. They’re all wrestlers…dead wrestlers. Nova recognizes faces from the past, friends…and enemies…people who met their deaths in the ring from freak accidents, people found dead in hotel rooms next to bottles of Jack and Vicodin… …the lost sons and daughters of “sports entertainment.” Floating in the wet infinite, their heads turn toward Nova, and his skin prickles with fear as he hears a low moan. “Stay. Staaaaay.” He swims toward the surface of the water frantically as corpses begin floating up in a giant vortex shape after him. “Staaaay, Novaaaa….staaaay.” His head explodes above the surface. “Aaahhh-uuhh!” Nova’s head shoots up as EMTs attempt to move him onto a stretcher. “Whoa!” one of them cries, taken off-guard, “Nova, be still! Please, be still!” He puts his hands on Nova’s shoulders and gently holds him against the back of the stretcher. Another medic shines a small metal flashlight into Nova’s eyes and braces him around the neck with a hand. “Nova, listen…we don’t know the damage that’s been done to your head. You have to be still.” As they strap him down underneath a wool blanket, Nova shivers violently. “I can’t stay.” “What?” one of the medics asks, leaning in close to hear Nova’s hoarse whisper, “Nova, what did you say?” “I can’t,” Nova rasps again, as his eyes roll back in his head. He lays back against the stretcher, and a moment later, the trembling ceases. “Nova!” the medic says, clapping his hands in front of Nova’s face and causing his eyes to open slightly, “you can’t sleep now, okay? You might have a concussion, and we can’t have you nodding off, so just be still and try to stay as focused as possible, alright? Tell me about your family.” But Nova isn’t biting, and his eyes soon close again. “Shit,” the EMT on the right hisses, “we gotta get him to the hospital.” They hoist up the stretcher. The arena is emptied of most of the fans, a few groups of stragglers hanging back to watch the progress with Nova. The EMTs march around the side of the ring and begin walking the stretcher up the entrance ramp. CRACK! Mid-step, the stretcher snaps loudly in half, the straps coming undone as Nova tumbles to the steel-grated floor of the ramp. “Fuck, John!” the EMT at the head of the stretcher snaps at his colleague, “I thought you said you fixed it!” “I thought I did fix it!” the other EMT replies defensively. “Well, what the fuck are we gonna do now?!” the accusing medic cries loudly, “it’ll take twenty minutes to get another one out here, and this guy is hurt!” “Azala do it.” Both the medics turn their heads to stare incredulously at the 6’10”, 478 lb. African Nightmare in front of them. “Azala do it,” Azala Zameer repeats slowly, “move now.” Azala moves toward Nova’s prone form, but one of the medics, identified by the EMT still fuming as “John,” steps in front of the Big Snack Daddy. “This man is hurt. We have to take him to the hospital.” “Azala do that,” Zameer insists, his big brown eyes locked on his friend, “Azala carry Nova to white truck.” “For God’s sake, John,” the other medic says desperately, “get out of the fucking way.” John obeys, and steps aside as Zameer bends down and hoists Nova up in his arms, cradling him almost like a baby. Without another word, Azala turns and begins marching up the entrance ramp. The African Nightmare tromps clumsily through the narrow hallways of the backstage area, earning confused stares from people who only see a huge black man carrying a large white man, who, in his battered state with long hair and beard resembles a homeless person. As Zameer makes a turn, Nova stirs again. “Unngh…uh…can’t stay. I can’t stay.” Oblivious to what Nova means, Azala smiles down at him and continues walking. “No. No, Nova go get help. Can’t stay.” Rounding the last corner, Zameer sees the waiting ambulance and bounds toward it excitedly. He hops up into the back of it, Nova still in his arms, and the entire truck sinks down on the tires three or four inches with a loud groan. “Go!” Azala trumpets triumphantly. The ambulance remains stationary. Azala looks around, the smile still frozen on his face. “GO!” A man comes into the view of the back of the truck, dressed all in blue. “Is…is someone back there?” His eyes widen as he stares at Zameer cradling Nova’s limp form. “Nova need help!” Azala pleads, confused as to why the ambulance hasn’t embarked yet. “Holy shit!” the man exclaims, seeing the blood crusted to Nova’s scalp around where a patch of hair has been ripped out. “I wasn’t expected the EMTs with the wrestler for another couple of minutes! Wait…where are the…” “GOOO!!” Zameer roars, his eyes widening with anger, and the man scampers around to the driver’s side of the ambulance and leaps in, firing the ignition and practically skidding out of the parking lot. ------*~*~*------ Mount Sinai Hospital, Toronto, ON Nova opens his eyes slowly, squinting painfully under the bright, sterile glow of fluorescent lighting. He can feel the back of his head dully pounding, as though it should be racking him with jolts of intense pain…but it’s as though someone has unplugged the cord for the synapses linking his nerves to the pain receptors in his brain. Blessed be you, liquid hydrocodeine. Things appear blurry, but they’re moving slowly back into focus. How many times has Nova woken up in a hospital bed to see his wife’s face over his own, shoulder-length black hair draped down over her tear-streaked cheeks? Too many. But unless his wife, over the course of the week, has put on 350 pounds and become a black man, it isn’t his wife’s face Nova sees, but that of the man quickly becoming one of his best friends… …Azala Zameer. Zameer peers down happily at Nova, a broad, toothy smile spreading over his wide face. “Nova awake!” “Ugh,” Nova groans, “Hey, Z. What…what’s shakin’?” “Hey! HEY!” Zameer yells, turning away for a moment, “Nova awake!” The sounds of footsteps follow, and Nova looks up to see Ariel approaching his bed, his chart in her hands and her eyes puffy and red. “Hey,” he says, “when…how did you…” She interrupts him, leaning down gently to hug him by the shoulders and give him a quick kiss. “How are you feeling?” “Thankfully, I’m not feeling much of anything at the moment,” Nova replies with a slight grin. “How did this happen?” Ariel asks softly. “Well,” Nova begins with a grin, “I was on my way to falling off a steel cage, right? And this damn support got in the way. I mean, I honked my horn and everything, but you kn-” “This isn’t funny, Chris,” Ariel says coldly, and the smile fades somewhat from Nova’s face. “Chris? That Nova’s name?” Azala asks, obviously confused. “‘Chris’ is my real name,” Nova explains, turning to Zameer, “‘Nova’ is my wrestling monik…say, I don’t suppose we can smoke in here, can we?” “Did you know you’re booked again for next week?” Ariel chimes back in, the expression on her face darkening by the minute. “How Nova get two names?” Zameer asks, his brow furrowed, “Azala only have one.” “Am I?” Nova asks, turning back to his wife, “against who?” “Does it matter?” Ariel says angrily, “you need to tell them to fuck off.” “Who against?” Nova persists, sitting up slightly in his bed. “Azala have two names if he count both words,” Zameer continues, holding up two fingers and staring at them, “Azala…Zameer…” “Justin Williamson,” Ariel mutters, giving in to Nova’s inquiring stare, “it’s a 5-Star Title defense, or so I heard.” “I’ve gotta be there,” Nova says solemnly. “What?” Ariel replies, her jaw dropping open. “Ariel, this isn’t as bad as it looks,” Nova says, gesturing to the bandage around his forehead. “How the fuck do you know?!” Ariel cries, “you just woke up!” “I have to defend the title,” Nova asserts, “I have to, Ariel. I haven’t defended it yet since I’ve had it…” “Chris,” Ariel interrupts. “…I don’t want anyone to be able to say I wasn’t a great champion,” Nova continues. “Chris,” Ariel butts in again. “…and Williamson, now there’s a win that will solidify me as a champion,” Nova goes on, “he’s an upper tier athlete, and a successful defense will really open people’s…” “Chris!” Ariel shouts, and Nova stops mid-sentence, “I think we need to talk.” Her eyes wander over to Zameer, who’s busy contemplating whether or not he might be able to ingest the x-rays of Nova’s skull. “Hey, nurse!” Nova says loudly, and a nurse walks over. Nova grabs his wallet off the counter-top next to his bed and removes a twenty-dollar bill. “Can you change this for quarters somewhere and show my friend to the snack machines?” The nurse smiles warmly and leads Azala off down the hallway by the hand. Ariel has her head down, a hand rubbing the back of her neck. “What do we need to talk about?” Nova asks, worry creasing his brow. When Ariel looks up, tears are once again threatening to run down her rosy-hued cheeks. “I don’t think this is gonna work, Chris.” “What?” Nova says, aghast, “what…what do you mean?” “Us,” Ariel replies, trying to choke back a sob, “I mean I don’t think we’re gonna work this out.” “No, hey, wait,” Nova says, his eyes widening, “Ariel, no. We are gonna work this out. Hey, listen…fuck the match. I’ll call the front office right now and tell them to lick my balls. Where’s the phone? I’ll…” “No, Chris,” Ariel says, softly, her battle with the tears lost as her cheeks glisten. “N-no?” Nova repeats, the look of disbelief on his face echoed in his voice. “No,” Ariel replies firmly, a sob squeaking out despite her best efforts to maintain composure. “Ariel, are you…leaving me?” Nova asks, the pallor in his face becoming more and more pronounced. Ariel lowers her head again. “Yes.” “Oh, my God,” Nova says, leaning his head back against the pillow as his own eyes begin to get misty, “oh, God…” Ariel takes his hand. “This isn’t your fault, Chris…not really. I knew when I met you that wrestling was your life. I thought…I don’t know, I thought that it might change when we got married. And then I thought it might change when I got pregnant. But you can’t change, I know that now. You are who you are, and who you are is Nova. Nova isn’t a part of my husband Chris. Chris is a part of Nova.” Nova’s thumb and index finger massage the bridge of his nose as his head shakes slowly back and forth. “You’re not really married to me, Nova,” Ariel says, using for the first time ever Nova’s wrestling name instead of his real one, “your real marriage is to the ring, and those fans that you love so much that you would give your health and your life for them…and I think…I think that’s what’s going to eventually happen.” Azala Zameer’s laughter can be heard faintly from the hallway where he plugs quarter after quarter into the snack machine and retrieves the goodie of his choosing. Ariel stands up from her chair and leans over Nova, a few tears dripping from her chin onto his chest. “I think it’s time to see things for what they really are. I just wanted something that I couldn’t have.” “And what’s that?” Nova croaks out, a tear of his own rolling down into his beard. Her reply is simple: “You.” Ariel gently kisses his bandaged forehead and steps back, grabbing her purse. She walks toward the door and turns around at the last second. “This isn’t the goodbye…but it’s a goodbye.” With that, she walks away. Nova buries his head in his hands. “Shit. Shit…” Azala re-emerges in the hospital room, a load of snacks in his arms. “Azala have Mr. Goodbar for Nova!” Nova tries to smile as more tears roll down his cheeks. “Thanks, buddy.” ------*~*~*------ Nova doesn’t say much as he and Zameer smoke a joint, sitting on a bench in the park next to the hospital. Zameer takes a pull befitting lungs his size, the end of the spleef lighting up like a Christmas tree, and then hands it to Nova, his chest puffed out as he holds in the smoke. When he speaks, it billows out of his mouth. “Nova’s head okay?” Nova exhales into the evening sky, his eyes on the admittedly gorgeous sunset. “I’ve got a pretty thick skull.” He takes another rip from the joint and hands it back to Azala. “Nothing like BC bud, huh?” The wide grin on the Snack Daddy’s face reciprocates his satisfaction with the Canadian herb. Nova looks again at the sunset, and the neon lights of the downtown area. He looks back at Zameer, who’s unrolling the roach and putting it into a small glass bowl. Azala gazes up at him. Nova isn’t smiling, but there are signs of life in his eyes, something Zameer hasn’t seen Ariel left a few hours ago. “You wanna hit the bars, Z?” Her Majesty’s Royal Pub, downtown Toronto… “And the real funny part here,” Nova slurs, clumsily setting a shot glass down on the counter, “because, believe you me, there is a funny part…is that there’s no paper work! You wannano why?!” Zameer just frowns uncomfortably at Nova, seeing the bad shape that his friend is in. “Yeah, you do! Yeah, you do!” Nova shouts in that way that suggests he’s lost control of the volume of his voice, “it’s because…drum roll…bum, bum, bumbumbum…” Nova slaps his hands on the bar-top several times, causing other patrons to stare over at him. “…We never officially got re-married after she left me the first time!” Nova lays his head down on the bar for a moment, and a low moan escapes his lips. “Iddn’t that pathetic? Whose wife leaves them twice! Mine!” “Nova,” Azala interjects, putting a massive hand on Nova’s shoulder, “Nova maybe have enough to drink now?” “Ha!” Nova squawks, his head shooting up, “HA! This is you gotta ROLL when you hits the bar with me! Barkeep! BARKEEP!” The bartender looks over at Nova, an annoyed expression on his face. “What?” “I been doin’ a number on these shots!” Nova exclaims, gesturing to the collection of empty shot glasses gathered around him on the bar-top. “I couldn’t tell,” the bartender replies sarcastically. “Couldn’t you, though?” Nova replies merrily, though his eyes narrow a bit at the bartender’s apparent attitude, “well, I been thinking…why not just gimme a bottle of Jack and save yerself some trouble? I can pour my own shots!” “I’m not giving you a bottle of anything but water,” the bartender replies. Nova climbs out of his stool and stumbles a few feet away from the bar. “Is that so? Well…well…you, sir…” Nova looks over at Zameer and points a finger at him. “And you, too…you’re in on this…both of you…together…” But before he can finish what he wanted to say, Nova falls backwards through a table that’s thankfully unattended. The patrons of the bar move back, forming a circle around him. “His skin is turning blue-ish,” a woman says, her voice filling with worry. “Shit!” the bartender curses as he picks up the phone, “I didn’t think he had enough to drink to give himself fuckin’ alcohol poisoning!” Three minutes later, for the second time in less than twenty four hours, Azala Zameer carries Nova out to a waiting ambulance. ------*~*~*------ Mount Sinai Hospital, Toronto, ON “Ugh…uuuugghh…” Nova opens his eyes slowly, wondering why jolts of pain actually are racking his his body this time. “Sorry. No pain-killers when you’re in for alcohol poisoning. What the fuck were you thinking?” Nova looks down at his gowned form lying in the same hospital he had left only hours ago, and then up at the doctor standing over him. “The bartender was worried sick. He didn’t know about the blood you had lost at the show…but you did.” “Is Azala Zameer here?” Nova rasps, his throat painfully dry. “Who?” the doctor asks. “The big African guy who eats a lot,” Nova elaborates. “No, he rode in the ambulance here, but left a little while ago,” the doctor answers, “we’re giving you fluids now. I’d recommend staying the night and leaving tomorrow, just to be on the safe side.” The doctor taps his clipboard against his hand once and then turns and walks to the door. He looks back at Nova, an oddly confused expression on his face. “You’re lucky. You could have died, Mr. Vega.” He walks out the door and down the hall. Nova shuts his eyes and takes a deep breath. Maybe I wanted to die.
