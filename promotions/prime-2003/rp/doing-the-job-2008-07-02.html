Doing the Job

2 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 2nd, 2008 The camera opens in the living room of the Delta Upsilon Iota fraternity house. And although the sun vacated its perch hours ago, leaving the bright July moon in control of this beautiful summer evening, the residents of this eclectic abode have only just begun to tackle today’s designated workload: the utter and complete decimation of Mega Job. With a pair of sizable and upwardly expanding empty beer can mountains on either side of a worn leather couch, it is painfully obvious that tonight’s only physical exercise of consequence is the twelve ounce curl. But for tonight, the frat boy extraordinaire Colby Korver and the massive southerner Hank Cobb seem content to handle merely the verbal side of things. Although it remains to be seen how capably they will realize this goal, considering the copious amount of alcohol being consumed. But by god they’re gonna try. One, the walking personification of every meathead frat guy you’ve ever had the misfortune of running into while out on the town. While many students are so bold as to value academic degrees or annual salaries, Korver instead measures his worth by taking the empty beer cans and handles of liquor on his floor and multiplying that number by the notches on his bedpost. Unfortunately for him, however, if you took that astronomical number and multiplied it by Delta Upsilon Iota’s victories as of late…you’d have exactly zero. The other, a good ol' county boy, raised in a southern home with a firm belief in holding doors for women, Sunday mornings in church, and Copenhagen tobacco. Blue collar in every sense of the word, this North Carolinian sporadically rubs his sore right knee, the result of a career-ending football injury that consequently set in motion his circuitous route to the squared circle. So with liquid courage coursing through their veins, they begin to deliver what they hope will be the promo of a lifetime. That is, if you’re the aquatic animal the Gastrotrich and only have a life expectancy of three days. Any longer than that, however, and chances are that you’ll hear a better promo at some point and time. Because the frat boys only have one point to make at this particular time. With this particular level of intoxication. Korver: "We’re gonna whoop your fucking asses. Because we’re fighters, and you’re just a god damn camera crew." Cobb: "Amen, brother. Amen." Fade to Black Korver: "And your name sucks too." Cobb: "Got ‘em."
