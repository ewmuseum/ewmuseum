The Preggo Trilogy: Damned from the Start.

Date: CWMC Final a the future... Adam sits in the front office of Brookwood Elementary much like he has many times before. Planted directly under the faculty's bulletin board, his legs dangle from his seat as he impatiently kicks at the air. In these specific times of boredom, there's a routine he's come to develop with the Principals' secretary; he attempts to maintain eye contact with her for as long as he can, staring daggers into her eyes and down her throat. Miss K, after less than thirty seconds of indulging him, leans forward and squints her eyes. "Don't give me that look, Adam. No one put you in this mess but yourself." The mess she refers to is the one opposite of the wall he sits against. While he can find some amusement in testing the limits of teachers coming to check the bulletin board, these times spent right outside the office of Vice-Principal Viscarelli (pronounced: Fish-A-Relly) are never ideal pastimes. The door to his right swings open and Adam turns his head to see Viscarelli peek his head out of frame. A look of joy creeps across his face, almost as if the man finds amusement in his position; doling out punishment to children. Viscarelli and Adam had danced this little dance before. It'll always be a different song, but the steps are always the same. "Come on in, son." Mr. Viscarelli says, but Adam is already jumping off of the seat. He enters the room to see Corbin Hunsaker, his most hated classmate, sitting to the left of the VP's desk. Both Corbin and Ms. Salah, their Social Studies teacher standing behind Mr. Viscarelli's seat, appear as if they're waiting for Adam as he takes the only empty chair. Viscarelli shuts the door behind them and returns to his plush leather recliner tucked away snugly behind his large Oak desk. "Ah.... I think we all know why we're here." Viscarelli begins by joining his hands at his chin. "Something bogus, that's what." Ms. Salah gasps. "Adam Davi--" "It's alright, Ms. Salah." "Alright?! I'm wounded!!" Corbin screams while displaying a wound on his hand. Wound, of course, meaning small abrasion on the knuckles. Adam, of course, couldn't give two shits. "You kicked it first, R-TARD." "What did you say, Adam?" Viscarelli asks rather sternly. After a moment of back-tracking, the boy decides to forget the part about R-tards. "Look, Fish, here's how it breaks down: I'm strollin' towards the spider-bars, 'kay? And right when I'm about to hop into the sand-pit, I look down to hop over the curb, and what do I notice? I stop myself saying 'Whoa whoa whoa, Adam! Don't get ahead of yourself—your shoes are untied!' Of course, safety comes first, so I decide to take a moment and tie my shoes. My cousin taught me this method in First; you know the one, around the loop and through the hole. I'm just about to pull my laces tight when—WHACK—my workspace is violated by this SCOOBY DOO OBSESSED LOSER AND HIS SNOWBALL-KICKING WAYS!" "SCOOBY DOO DOES NOT SUCK; YOU DO." "THAT CARTOON IS, LIKE, A HUNDRED YEARS OLD, YOU FAG. AND IF YOU KICK A SNOWBALL AT ME AGAIN, I SWEAR TO GOD I'LL PEE IN YOUR LUNCH BOX." "BOYS." Viscarelli says, commanding immediate silence. After a moment of awkwardness, Adam laughs nervously. "Sorry about that, Fish. I know you told me to work on my anger issues, but sometimes counting to ten takes too long-" "Adam, both Corbin and Ms. Salah say they saw you take the snowball and throw it at Corbin." "He made me BLEED." "OH SHUT UP. You were making fun of my aim until I told you your hand was bleeding." Slowly, a grin creeps across Adam's face. "Then you started crying like Dana Smith when her underwear was bleeding." "Young man, I don't think you-" Mr. Viscarelli attempts to talk some sense into Adam, but his words have struck a chord in Ms. Salah that it is impossible for her emotions to remain in check. "Listen, you little shit, Dana Smith is traveling the path God intended. Her body is metamorphosing into that of a woman's and what event you horrifically referenced is simply a side effect of said changes." "I don't know what you just said but when I asked her where she put the Band-Aid, she told me to follow her to the Cubby Room so she could show me." The room goes quiet (Corbin's eyes go wide) as both Ms. Salah and Mr. Viscarelli find difficulty in swallowing the lump suddenly lodged in their throats. "Let's just say she used waaaaaaaaaaaaay too much Neosporin for my liking." "....We'll discuss that later—right now, we're here to-" "YOUNG MAN, YOUR CORRUPTED HEART MUST ALLOW ITSELF TO ACCEPT THE LOVE OF JESU-" "Ms. Salah, restrain yourself!" Viscarelli jumps to his feet and places his hands on the Teacher's shoulders. She is quite beside herself, looking more than ready to leap at Adam with a switchblade aimed for his throat. While the VP whispers a few words to Ms. Salah, Corbin manages to whisper to Adam quietly, "I heard she bleed for, like, a week." Adam nods. "Listen. Boys. Whether one of you kicked it at the other first or one of you threw it first, violence was enacted. And school policy holds a strict stance against violence. I'm afraid I'm be forced to act accordingly." Viscarelli takes a pen from a cup rested on his desk and marks down a few finishing touches on the punishment slips sitting in front of him. "Corbin, you kicked the snowball first? That's instigation, and for that you will receive two demerits." "TWO DEMERITS?!" Corbin cries. "TWO DEMERITS?!" Adam protests. "Adam? For retaliation, not to mention the infliction of an injury, you will receive three demerits. This places you at a total of five demerits for the quarter." Removing his prescription specs, Viscarelli gives a vigorous rubdown to one of his eyeballs. He says with feigned sincerity, "I'm sorry, but we're going to have to call your father." "THAT'S SO GAY, I DIDN'T EVEN DO ANYTHING! Suddenly it's a crime to throw a snowball out of your way when you're trying to tie your shoe after some JERKWAD decides to kick it at you?! CONSPIRACY, I SAY. MS. SALAH HATES ME BECAUSE I'M NOT MORMON!" "Adam, that's enough." "IT WASN'T EVEN MY FAULT!" "You say that every time," Viscarelli responds. "BUT THIS IS FUGGIN' RIDICULOUS, FISH!" "ADAM." "What?! I SAID 'FUGGIN'!" "Adam, please sit outside and wait for your father to arrive. Corbin, you may return to class." Leaping to the floor from his seat at the side of the Principal's desk, Corbin begrudgingly strolls past Adam. His plan has backfired, and now he's left with two demerits. Adam, however, is left with consequences far more dire than anyone can imagine. Though calls to his dad have not been uncommon throughout his Elementary career, this hasn't made them any less dreadful. The faculty has no idea either. Most times, Viscarelli and the teachers toy with Adam's emotions and allow him to tell his side of the story. It doesn't matter how elaborate or convincing the story is—he's always guilty. Adam is just lucky that this time, they decided to fuck him straight. But the punishment from school is bittersweet compared to the hell awaiting him on the drive home with his father. It's a hell he's constantly forced to await, planted on a chair with his legs hanging above the ground, sharing dirty looks with Miss K the secretary. Of course, you shouldn't feel bad. This kid has been damned from the start. ---- It hasn't been thirty-seconds and already Adam's father is destroying the foundation of his self-esteem. To compensate, Adam turns up the volume on the radio deck that sits permanently fixed on classic rock. The melodic tune playing quickly captures his attention, and for the rest of the song he willingly places his focus elsewhere. After a few glances towards his son, swaying rhythmically to the music, his father can't help but regard him strangely. "Were you sniffing the god damn Elmers again?" Adam doesn't pay attention, so dad sends a smack to the back of his head. "Ow!" "The hell is wrong with you?" "I didn't even do anything! I chucked a stupid snowball out of my way!" "Hmph." Adam's dad chuckles and tightens his grip on the steering wheel. "The story is always the same with you. You never do anything, but you're always the one in trouble. I'd rather my son have an ounce of integrity and admit his crimes than lie to his father like a coward." By now he's realized that there will be no escaping the ridicule his father seemingly loves to put him through. He folds his arms and waits for dad's next blow. But of course he never anticipates it to be a physical one. "OUCH!" He yelps after his father smacks the back of his head. "You sneaking NyQuil again, boy?" "NO! Gosh, I just like Rilo Kiley, okay?" "If you're doing drugs, I swear to God--" "I'M NOT DOING DRUGS. I'M FUGGIN' TEN." Dad smacks Adam again. "Watch that 'fuggin' shit." Adam doesn't respond, learning that the only way he'd survive the car ride home is by keeping his mouth shut. Just a couple of miles left that he needs to strong for. He can manage; he just can't take his father's bait. "Your mother would have a fit if she could see you today." Father coldly states as he flicks the left turning blinker. Adam wants to say something but knows he shouldn't. Home is only a few blocks away now. He just needs to hang in there. But after what dad said... he just can't resist. "Mom would have probably le-OOFT." Adam's father takes a left turn rather quickly, whipping Adam's small frame across the seat of his SUV and sending him crashing into the door. "Sorry, son—you were saying something?" ---- Adam sits in Social Studies with his head rested on his hands, his mind easily wandering away from the lesson before him. The previous night once again brought a dream he's had often times before, with each instance gradually becoming stranger and stranger. Mom would wake him up and ask him what he wants to eat for his birthday dinner, to which Adam would always respond 'Hamburger Casserole'. Of course, she'd joke like she didn't have the ingredients to make it at hand, and suddenly, just like clockwork, the oven would go off. Adam would run through the kitchen, pull the door open and there it would be; a dish full of his mother's greatest concoction. When she'd ask if dad can be allowed to have a plate, Adam would answer no and continue eating as if nothing was ever said. The rest of the dream would follow a routine as well, consisting of receiving the floating Skateboard from Back to the Future II which would lead into him ripping down the Alpine Slide in Park City. Afterwards, his mother would meet him at a secret underwater Dave & Busters where she would drink her special drink and he'd play futuristic video games that could never exist until he wakes up. Repetitive, yes, but always entertaining. If anything, Adam's only regret was that he never remembered to say goodbye to his mother. His eyes would always flutter open right when he'd be in the middle of a game and once again he'd leave his mom's side without so much as a word. Last night, however, was different. When his mother asked if father was allowed to have a plate and Adam said no, things became a lot darker. The door to the pantry began shaking until mom became so annoyed that she felt compelled to get the commotion to stop. She opened the door to reveal Adam's father, locked behind cell-bars with his arms and legs shackled. "I want a plate." He said, to which mom turned to Adam to get a response. "Of course not! It's just—you know! It's our thing." Mom turned back to dad and shared with him a disheartened look. Dad's face quickly grew bitter and tight. He gripped the restraining bars and shook vigorously, once again disrupting the scene. "You're not my son." Dad said to Adam, prompting Mom to slam the door shut. Adam's eyes stayed fixed on the door, but just as it clicked closed did the commotion end. The walls stopped rattling and the raucous noise faded, but Adam kept his eyes on the door. His mother motioned back over to his side. She leaned over and lightly kissed his cheek. Then she whispered into his ear. "Don't worry, Adam. He just doesn't understand the bond that we have." And that's when Adam woke up. "Adam!" "What's that?" "Why aren't you singing along?" Adam realizes he should have been paying attention, as his entire class is staring at them for interrupting their harmony. "I'm sorry, I wasn't-" "-Paying attention. You don't want to ruin the concert tomorrow, do you?" "....Sorry, Ms. Salah." Adam says halfheartedly. "Okay, now that we're all hopefully on the same page, let's take it from the top." A few students clear their throats while Ms. Salah, at the front of the classroom, begins conducting her students with a pencil in hand as if it were a baton. Pioneers! Pioneers! We're grateful for the courageous Pioneers They persevered for all and crossed benevolent frontiers We're grateful for the Utah Pioneers It's two lines into the chorus that Adam remembers why he began daydreaming in the first place: because this song is gay. "Ms. Salah!" he calls out, again interrupting. "MS. SALAH." "Adam, at this rate you're never going to be in the running for that solo you wa-" "Can I just, like, not sing this one at all?" A few of the kids look on with confused expressions while Ms. Salah's simply reads of shock. "Pardon? Is there something wrong?" "I just don't want to sing it." "Why wouldn't you want to sing it?" A classmate snobbishly asks. "Adam, it's apart of our history. This state was founded-" "By Mormons, I know. You remind us of it every day." The students gasp. Ms. Salah reacts in a much more dramatic fashion. "Young man, I don't know what you're getting at." "Alls I'm saying is that I'm not Mormon, so I should be able to exclude myself. I mean, what happened to the separation of Church and State? I thought we weren't allowed to say the Pledge of Allegiance in school." Everyone in the room is rendered speechless. While most of the kids have no idea what he's talking about, Ms. Salah is more than stunned. "Young man, I don't know where you hear such horrible things. But if you EVER repeat them in my classroom again, we'll be calling your father. And we both know that he doesn't want to be bothered." "I just don't want to sing this stupid song!" "YOU DON'T HAVE A CHOICE. THIS IS YOUR HISTORY AND YOU WILL LEARN IT, YOU WILL SING IT, AND YOU WILL BE PROUD. ARE WE UNDERSTOOD?" ".....yes." "I ASKED IF WE ARE UNDERSTOOD!" "YES MAM." "GOOD. NOW I'M GOING TO GO USE THE RESTROOM, AND BY THE TIME I GET BACK, YOU'D BETTER BE IN PERFECT SYNC WITH THE REST OF THE CLASS OR YOU'LL BE SPENDING ANOTHER AFTERNOON IN MR. VISCARELLI'S OFFICE, WAITING FOR YOUR FATHER TO PICK YOU UP. AND HE WON'T LIKE TO HEAR WHAT I HAVE TO SAY WHEN HE GETS HERE." Ms. Salah storms out, leaving the entire class with their mouths agape. Just when they think she's left them alone, she peaks her head back in. "PERFECT SYNC!" The door slams again. "What a bitch." Adam says aloud, causing some of the kids to stir to motion. "That's a bad word!" "You'd better sit down, Adam. She's already mad at you." Another classmate calls out to him. "Like she's the only one who has to take a piss?" "But she has to give you a hall-pass!" The children notice he's acting strangely as he doesn't head to the front door. Instead he goes to Ms. Salah's desk and pulls out her chair. "I'm not using the bathroom, dummy." Jumping on top of Ms. Salah's desk, Adam drops his drawers around his ankles and begins to let it fly. A suitable pool of urine collects itself in the cup of Ms. Salah's seat. Once drained, Adam pulls his pants back up and jumps back to the ground, turning to face his classmates. Some of their expressions read of disgust. Some others find it humorous. There are even a couple of kids who have closed their eyes to pray. There are no doubts that each of them are equally shocked. But Adam doesn't mind himself with their reactions. He calmly and coolly strolls back to his desk, offering the classmates a "shhhhhhh" while his finger covers his mouth. Right as he sits down, Ms. Salah re-enters the room. "Alright class," Her tone and expression are hardly as intense as they were before she left. Apparently, a quick, private one-on-one with the J-Man is all it takes to restore her composure. "Let's take it one more time from the top. Adam: no monkey business." Adam smiles as he watches Ms. Salah pull out her chair and prepare to sit down. Everyone is staring at her. The kids are all frozen in their stares, waiting for her to take her seat; they could care less for what directions she imparts on them. "What are you waiting for? Start at the begin-." Her phrase is cut short as she plummets into her chair. A wetness immediately soaks her backside. All of her confusions quickly subside as the stench hits her nostrils. Ms. Salah knows what has transpired, though she still doesn't want to believe it. She's been violated. Tricked. Harassed by one of her students. The victim of a prank that's more despicable than clever. With an ass soaked in urine, her lips begin to quiver. She can barely mutter the words she intends to, but all it takes is the first spoken syllable. "....Wh-who..." And everyone--EVERYONE--turns to point out Adam. "NARCS! YOU'RE ALL NARCS!" Don't feel sorry for him. He never stood a chance to begin with.
