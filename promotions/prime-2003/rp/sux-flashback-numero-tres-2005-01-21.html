Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/21/2005 The second promo of the fourway match. Pajaro and Tiger would be victorious and earn the right to challenge Clique Clique Boom for the PTC tag titles thanks to this win. NOTE: The following promotion takes place before the UWF Impy Awards. Just in case you really worry about the continuity of a couple of jobbers like the Masked Monkeys. And if you do... GET A WOMAN!!! On the steps of a courthouse in Chicago, UWF hotshot reporter "Snap" Meyer is all hepped up on goofballs to report late-breaking news! "Snap": Hey ho, all you wrestling maniacs! "Snap" Meyer here with the story of the century! Inside those halls of justice, the Masked Monkeys are in the trial of their lives! Their very identity is at stake! All because of a lawsuit filed by the Gay and Lesbian Association of Monkeys claiming the Masked Monkeys defame real monkeys across the world! And all this right before they have a HUGE inter-fed match against three teams from Lights Out Wrestling! For more into this, I’ve got with me someone who knows the defendants very well… Plucky the Cockfighting Duck! The camera pans back to show a small white duck standing next to the reporter. “Snap”: So, Plucky… can you give us some insight on the mindset of the Masked Monkeys? Plucky: Quack. “Snap”: Uh… could you expand on that? Plucky: Quack quack. “Snap”: Gee… I see to be having trouble hearing you. Could you repeat that? Plucky: Quack quack. “Snap”: Riiiiiiiiiight… Just then, a phone rings. Looking perplexed, “Snap” Meyer reaches into his jacket and takes out his cell. “Snap”: Hello? Oh, hello, Mr. Hayes. I’m sorry, but I’m doing a live interview. Yes, I know you’re in charge of the UWF, Mr. Hayes. Yes, I know that means you’re my boss, Mr. Hayes. Yes, I know that means you can make me interview Clyde about his scars before he’s had his coffee while I’m dressed as a Catholic school girl, Mr. Hayes. I would love to talk to you right now, Mr. Hayes. Why am I interviewing a duck? He hangs out with the Masked Monkeys, sir. Yes, I know ducks can’t talk, but I thought this one might, Mr. Hayes. We’ve had a talking monkey and a talking turtle in the UWF, sir. Yes, the UWF Gorilla can’t talk, Mr. Hayes. Yes, Petey the PG Tips Chimp never talked, Mr. Hayes. Yes, the Phenomenal Camels never talked either, Mr. Hayes. I’ve never heard Tsunami Oni’s Komodo dragon talk, sir, but you must admit, Rodzilla only shows up to bite people in the groin and you can’t talk with some guy’s genitals in your mouth. No, I’m not saying you enjoy having male genitals in your mouth, sir. Yes, you are a red-blooded sex machine, Mr. Hayes. No, I’m not hitting on you, sir. Yes, I would lik to get back on topic too, Mr. Hayes. Yes, it was my fault, sir. No, I’ve never seen Plucky the Cockfighting Duck talk, Mr. Hayes. But Pájaro de Presa seems to understand him, sir. Yes, I know Pájaro de Presa is an idiot, Mr. Hayes. I’ll end the interview right now, Mr. Hayes. The red-faced reporter puts his phone away. “Snap”; Uh, looks like it’s time for us to head into the courtroom, folks! Thank you for your time, Plucky the Cockfighting Duck! Plucky: Quack. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ That was the way-cool scene change special effect. Now the action is in the courthouse where we find those dashing defendants sitting behind a table… the Masked Monkeys! And look! It’s their attorney, UWF senior referee, Jerry “Pops” Kohler. “Pops” is looking snappy wearing a suit instead of his zebra stripes. And at the other table are the plaintiffs. It’s GLAM. They pretty much look like monkeys. Except they’re gay and lesbian. You can’t really tell which is which. Both genders look the same. Hey, they’re monkeys. And they got their lawyer with them, Johnny Cockroach. He looks like a cockroach. Except he’s wearing a double-breasted suit with a power tie. Easily the most intimidating cockroach ever. He could totally kick the ass of that dude in that Kafka story. He was a bitch. The bailiff, an attractive woman in her late 30’s, announces to arrival of the judge. Bailiff: All rise! Everybody in the courtroom stands. Which leads to the discovery that Jerry “Pops” Kohler is once again without pants. The jacket and tie still look snappy. Bailiff: The honorable Hannibal Mental presiding! A man in a black robe enters. He looks like Judge Mills Lane except without the pretty boy good looks. White Tiger turns to his partner. White Tiger: So this guy is Judge Mental? Alas for White Tiger, the judge heard him. He stares at the luchador with eyes filled with fire and brimstone. Judge Mental: Silence, you miscreant! I can tell by the look of you that you’re guilty! Guilty! You’ll rot in prison for the rest of your life! Bailiff: This is a civil case, your honor. Judge Mental: I knew that! The judge sits and the rest of the people do the same. Except for “Pops.” Bailiff: You can sit down, sir. “Pops”: Eh, I like standing. Bailiff: Sir, you have no pants on. Sit down or I will pepper spray you. “Pops” chooses to sit. Judge: Plaintiff! Call your first witness! Johnny Cockroach: He’s fresh and he’s funky! He’s Spiff the Magic Monkey! Asylum Wrestling Federation legend and confidant to Mike Montgomery, Spiff the Magic Monkey takes the stand and gets sworn in. Johnny Cockroach: Please state your name. Say it loud, you’re monkey and proud! Spiff: My name is Spiff the Magic Monkey and I have been employed by the UWF. Johnny Cockroach: The same UWF that employees these so-called Masked Monkeys? The insect attorney gestures at the two Masked Monkeys with a dramatic flourish. Well, as dramatic as a cockroach can be. Spiff: Yes, the same federation. Johnny Cockroach: And would you tell the court the role you played at the UWF Pay-Per-View called FANdemonium? Spiff: Well, there was this match called the Flaming Monkey match. The first one to light a monkey on fire and hit his opponent with it would win. And I was to be that monkey. A gasp is heard in the courtroom at this startling revelation. Johnny Cockroach: This ain’t no game, they tried to set him aflame! Judge Mental pounds his gavel to restore order to the shocked courtroom. Judge Mental: Order! Continue, Mr… the Magic Monkey. Spiff: Yes, one of the wrestlers came close to setting me on fire, but I was able to commandeer the gasoline and lighter and escape from the arena. Johnny Cockroach: He stared death in the face and got the hell out of that place! So you mean to tell me the UWF thinks so little of monkeys that they find it amusing to set them on fire? Spiff: Yes. Yes, they do. Which is why I’m pursuing a separate lawsuit of my own. The way the UWF treats monkeys is appalling. It’s inhuman. I am so glad my gay and lesbian brothers and sisters have stood up to defend the image of monkeys everywhere! The members of GLAM stand of, hooting and clapping. Judge Mental pounds his gavel gain. Judge Mental: Order! Order! You will not make a monk… You will not make a mockery of this courtroom! Stick to the questions! Johnny Cockroach: I have no further questions for this witness, your honor. Judge Mental: Defendant, you may cross-examine. Much to the chagrin of everyone in the courtroom, “Pops” rises. The UWF senior referee walks over to the witness stand with a serious look. His steel gray eyes betray no emotion as he stares at Spiff the Magic Monkey. Then “Pops” reaches out and starts tickling Spiff. “Pops”: Who’s a cute monkey? Who’s a cute monkey? Spiff giggles and squirms in the witness stand. Spiff: HAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! CUT IT OUT!!! Judge Mental: Mr. Kohler, you must refrain from tickling the witness! Now, ask a question! “Pops”: How did I get here? This isn’t the Chinese buffet… Judge Mental: Next witness! As “Pops” wanders back to his eat, Spiff the Magic Monkey leaves the stand. Johnny Cockroach: The plaintiff calls its next witness… White Tiger! Another shocked gasp! White Tiger looks perplexed but takes the stand and gets sworn in. Johnny Cockroach: Please state your name. White Tiger: Uh… White Tiger. Johnny Cockroach: Your real name, please. White Tiger: But… I’m a luchador. I just can’t give my real name out! Johnny Cockroach: How about you just whisper it to the judge? Standing up, White Tiger whispers to the judge. Judge Mental: Your name is Oscar? Like the Grouch? Now I know why you wear a mask! Ha! The whole courtroom is cracking up. Well, except for an annoyed White Tiger. After a good chortle, things settle back down. Johnny Cockroach: Give us a history of the Masked Monkeys. Don’t be lax, give us all the facts! White Tiger: Well, shortly after I joined the UWF, I had the chance to compete in something called Stable Wars, where groups of wrestlers compete to get the matches of their choice. The original group was me, the Trailer Park Luchador, and a guy from Japan called Ryusei. We dubbed ourselves “Los Trios Mascada,” because Ryusei and the TPL didn’t know anything about speaking Spanish. They thought it meant “the Masked Trio” or something. We needed a fourth member, so we added Giovanni, who renamed himself El Turtlé. Even thought there were four of us know we kept the name. But then some idiot janitor came up with a nickname for us… the Masked Monkeys. Johnny Cockroach: Was it THIS idiot janitor? He holds up a photo that looks like this… (It's a picture of Ryan-o in all his Affleckesque glory. Bastard took it down later so the link doesn't work.) White Tiger: Yes, that's him. Judge Mental: He looks familiar. White Tiger: You probably saw him on the news. He used to be an actor in, uh, adult movies. Johnny Cockroach: He was no Rip Torn, he was in porn! White Tiger: You kinda stretched for that one. Johnny Cockroach: Just tell us more about this janitor. White Tiger: Well, he wasn't just a porn star. He was a GAY porn star. Judge Mental: I did see him on the news. What was his name again? White Tiger: Uh, I don't think that would be appropriate. Johnny Cockroach: We're all grown ups, son. Give us the 411! White Tiger: Well, he performed under the name... Ben Assfucked. Judge Mental: Ben Assfucked? White Tiger: Yeah, that was his name in gay porn. He made movies like Armageddon My Butt Banged, Good Will Hunting For Some Lube, and Jersey Girl With A Penis. It was all mentioned in his True Hollywood Story. Johnny Cockroach: But he ain't taking money shots no more, the only wood he grabs is when he mops the floor! White Tiger: Right. See, Ben Assfucked came down with chronic impotence. Nothing could help him, uhhh... rise to his former heights. Not Viagra, not Horny Goat Weed, nothing. He was arrested for causing a disturbance at the Washington Monument. That's how he made the news. Ben Assfucked said the monument mocked him with it's, uh, "erect phallic majesty," as he put it. Eventually he was released and got a job as a janitor with the UWF. Johnny Cockroach: That's where you got the name "Masked Monkeys?" White Tiger: Right. I never asked for the name. It doesn't exactly inspire respect. Johnny Cockroach: The Masked Monkey name brought you nothing but shame! White Tiger: Pretty much. I left the UWF for a time to pursue acting in Mexico, but that didn't work out. I came a little while back and got pretty much buried. All the luchadores were forced to work security. That is, when we weren't losing to someone. Johnny Cockroach: All the luchadores? White Tiger: Well, there was me, and the TPL. Plus El Turtlé. And a couple other guys joined up, El Chupacabra and Crab Mask Jr. Then another luchdor came in, this one from 4WW. Pájaro de Presa. People remembered the "Masked Monkeys" nickname and this time it stuck. Even when Pájaro and I tagged up, we got slapped with the name. Johnny Cockroach: I notice you dress like a tiger. White Tiger: Well, yeah. Johnny Cockroach: And your partner is dressed like a retarded bird. White Tiger: That's pretty accurate. Johnny Cockroach: Plus a fella in a crab mask, not to mention a turtle. An actual turtle. White Tiger: That's correct. Johnny Cockroach: Not a single monkey among you. So why the name? White Tiger: Well, it was meant to be a put-down because we're, you know, jobbers. We lose. We lose a lot. It's what we do. Johnny Cockroach: And you don't like the name? White Tiger: Not really. Before this whole Masked Monkey thing, I was getting shots at the World and Intercontinental titles. Now I'm stuck trying to help my partner find his duck. It's embarrassing, no one will take a Masked Monkey seriously. Johnny Cockroach: Stuck with a name he didn't choose and forced to always lose! No more questions! Judge Mental: The defense may cross-examine. Jerry "Pops" Kohler walks up to the witness stand and casts a kindly eye upon White Tiger. "Pops": Son, I know this is difficult for you. I admire your honesty. I've just got one question for you. Look deep within your heart and find the answer. White Tiger: Sure, "Pops." "Pops": Now... where are my pants? White Tiger: I don't know, "Pops." I told you that this morning when we picked you up. "Pops": Darn it! I was hoping they might have shown up. No further questions, your honor. The judge gives White Tiger permission to step down. He doesn't look too thrilled as he sits next to his partner. Judge Mental: Any more witnesses, Mr. Cockroach? Johnny Cockroach: Red rover, red rover, our part is over! Judge Mental: A simple "no" would've worked just fine. Defense, call your first witness. "Pops": PÁJARO DE PRESA! PÁJARO DE PRESA! Pájaro: I'm right here, "Pops!" Sitting next to you! "Pops": Oh. I thought you were one of those hallucina-thingies my family says I get. Judge Mental: Just have the witness take the stand. Pájaro de Presa trots over to the witness stand. The bailiff has him put one hand on the Bible and raise the other. Bailiff: Do you swear to tell the truth, the whole truth, and nothing but the truth... so help you God? Pájaro: I swear. Just seeing you in that uniform gives me a half-chubbie, mamacita! Bailiff: Some things you can keep to yourself. The bailiff walks off as "Pops" approaches the stand. "Pops": Mr. de Presa... Suddenly, "Pops" spins and bends over. He pulls down his underwear to show Pájaro de Presa his wrinkled ass. "Pops": Should I get this boil lanced? It hurts like the dickens when I sit down. Pájaro: Madre de dios! That's a big on! You gotta get that taken care of! Will it cost much? "Pops": Nah, I get medical. Pájaro: Really? I just get paid in stuffed jalapenos and caramel shakes. Judge Mental: Mr. Kohler, please pull up your underwear. "Pops": Fine, fine. The senior referee does as the judge tells him. Judge Mental: Do you have any questions for this witness that doesn't involve exposing yourself? "Pops": Yes, I do, your honor. Mr. de Presa... Pájaro: Aw shucks, you can call me Pájaro. "Pops": Thanks, Pájaro. Now... do you like me? Pájaro" Of course I like you, "Pops!" You're a swell guy! You're the only one that doesn't call me names! Like "Birdbrain" or "You Stupid Retarded Birdbrain." "Pops": Wow! I feel the same about you! My family never calls me on the phone. Mr. Hayes wants me to take mandatory retirement. And I'll let you in on a secret... Pájaro: What? "Pops": I don't think my pants like me either. That's why I can never find them! Pájaro: That does make sense. I tell you what, "Pops," let's go make some s'mores! "Pops": Yippee! Pájaro de Presa stands up to leave. That's when he gets clocked on the head by a gavel. Pájaro: Hey! We'll save some for you! Judge Mental: There is a trial going on. Mr. Kohler, are you done with your questions? "Pops": I've got a friend! Judge Mental: So that's a yes. Mr. Cockroach, care to cross-examine? Johnny Cockroach: Just one question for the witness, you honor. Mr. de Presa, are you an idiot? Pájaro: Huh? Johnny Cockroach: No further questions, your honor. Judge Mental: The witness may step down. Pájaro de Presa and Jerry "Pops" Kohler skip back to their seats holding hands. Judge Mental: Time for closing arguments. Plaintiff... you are first. Johnny Cockroach: As you saw, your honor, the UWF has a history of monkey abuse. They are considered objects of ridicule and even targets of violence. The Masked Monkeys were given that name as a form of mockery, even one of their own members said so. It was bad enough when they were just running around their own neck of the woods. But now this so-called Greatest Enhancement Talent Tag Team in PTC has been popping all over the place, spreading their shameful lies against monkeys. Clearly, this is a case of a fine, furry people being degraded and debased. Do it for GLAM, do it for Spiff, let justice be served and find for the plaintiff! Judge Mental: Thank you, Mr. Cockroach. And now it’s time for your closing statement, Mr. Kohler. Jerry “Pops” Kohler stands. “Pops”: In 1983, I dryhumped Carol Channing in a bathroom at Denny’s in Toledo! Jerry “Pops” Kohler sits. Judge Mental: All right then. I will retire to my chambers now. After long and thoughtful consideration, I will return with my decision. The judge rises and heads into his chambers. Closing the door, he opens his black robe. Judge Mental adjusts the red lace thong and garter belt he's wearing. Judge Mental: Ah... much better! The judge closes his robes and heads back to the courtroom. They go through that "all rise" crap again. Judge Mental: After much deliberation, I have decided the evidence is clear. The UWF does treat monkeys as objects of scorn. So much so that they dub other objects of scorn, such as bottom-of-the-barrel luchadores “monkeys” as an insult. This behavior clearly shows an active role of the UWF in desecrating the good name of monkeys everywhere. I find in favor of the plaintiff and sentence the defendants to death by lethal injection! Another shocked gasp from the courtroom! Pájaro and White Tiger don’t seem to like the judge’s decision very much. Bailiff: Your honor… civil case… Judge Mental: Oh, right. I rule that the Masked Monkeys may no longer use that name! SLAM! Down goes the gavel and there is massive clapping and hooting from all the monkeys in the courtroom. The former Masked Monkeys stand with their lawyer. White Tiger: Well, that’s that. Monkeys no more. Now what do we do? Pájaro: The only thing we can do… FIESTA~! Suddenly, a mariachi band bursts through the doors of the courtroom! A piñata drops from the ceiling along with streams and confetti! As the band plays, a Shakira impersonator, who most likely has a penis, sings “Whenever, Wherever!” Humans and monkeys are gettin’ their dance on! Pájaro de Presa is does the lambada with the bailiff! White Tiger: Uhhh… don’t we have that Fatal Fourway match with those teams from LOW? And now we don’t have a name. Hearing his partner, Pájaro de Presa raises his hand and all the music and dancing stops. Pájaro: You are right. It is time we got back in touch with our Latino roots. From now on we shall be… LOS MUCHO MACHO MÁSCARAS!!! White Tiger: Well, OK, I guess. Pájaro: And now… FIESTA~! FOREVER~! And that’s exactly what they did.
