A Tale of Two Countries...

Date: Various Date: July 23, 2007 Location: Polish Market, Gdansk, Poland Walczak walks into the grocery store looking at his shopping list, hoping that he has not left anything off. He grabs a shopping cart, and makes his way to the produce section, specifically towards the tomatoes picking up a couple, and tossing them into a bag. He sees an employee sweeping to floor, and tries to grab his attention. “Excuse me…” “Yes can I……” The boy turns around and is immediately taken aback by the Punisher. The boy cannot be more than sixteen years old, and is suffering from a rather bad case of acne. He stops mid sentence, and fixes his glasses. He wipes his hands on his already dirty smock before once again turning his attention towards Walczak. “My God, you are Tom Walczak. I watch PRIME just so I can see what you’re up to. You’re a hero around these parts, do you know that?” “Thanks for the compliment, but I just do what I can you know. Try to make the people of Poland proud.” “You sure do that, but is there anything that I can help you with, to make your shopping trip a little bit better.” “Yeah, actually I was just wondering how fresh these vegetables were.” “We just got them in today. We get a new shipment fresh in everyday, everything that’s a day old we throw out. They just get battered, and we just don’t think anything that’s left over is fit to sell.” “Just what I wanted to hear, nothing better than fresh fruit and vegetables.” “If you don’t mind me asking, could I possibly get an autograph?” “Sure, no problem got a pen and paper.” The boy nervously searches through his smock, and finds a pen and a notepad. He hands it over to Walczak his hands still shaking as Walczak takes the notepad away from him. “Who should I make it out to?” “You could make it out to Michal, yeah Michal is fine.” Walczak quickly scribbles some words of encouragement to the boy, and hands the notepad back to the boy. A huge smile comes over the face of the boy, and he immediately scurries off to show off the autograph to his friends. Walczak gathers up some more vegetables, onions, cucumbers, and some beets before he to makes his way to another part of the store. As he makes his way up and down the aisles there is something that catches his attention. His nose leads the way as he makes his way towards the back of the store. Walczak comes to the bakery, and finds a variety of freshly baked breads and pastries. He sees that the kitchen is still hard at work, and makes his way over to the bakers. “What time do you guys get here to make the bread every morning?” Two of the bakers are in the back unloading another fresh batch of bread, and one of them makes their way towards the front, wiping his hands of dough before speaking with Walczak. “Sorry I didn’t catch that.” “I was just thinking that you and your partner would have to get here pretty early in the morning to get the bread ready for sale.” “Yeah we usually get here around 4:30 in the morning, giving us a couple of hours to get the bread ready for the public.” “Where do you guys order the dough from?” The man gives a short little laugh, that may come off as even a bit cocky before answering Walczk. “Order, we don’t order the dough. We’d be fired if we did. No we get here early in order to prepare the dough from scratch every day. That’s what makes it taste so good, if we used pre-made dough, than we wouldn’t be able to look at ourselves in the mirror.” “Shit, that must take a lot out of you. Well, at least your work doesn’t go unnoticed. I am gonna take a couple loaves of bread home with me, and don’t be shocked if I come back tomorrow to get some more.” Walczak shakes the hand of the man in the kitchen, almost as if to say congratulations on a job well done. He grabs three different loaves of bread, bringing each one close to his nose, and breathing in deeply before placing the bread in the cart. Walczak, looks down at his list, and makes his way over to the butcher taking a look at all the various meat products hidden behind the glass. “Can I help you with anything sir?” A hefty butcher makes his way over to Walczak, his white apron covered in blood, and innards. “Quick question, where does the meat you sell come from?” “Well, the livestock comes to us fresh from the local farms, and all the cold cuts, kielbasa and such we make here everyday.” “Seems to be a common theme, freshness.” “We wouldn’t be a respectable market if we didn’t any other way.” “That’s what I like to here. Let me get a whole chicken, four sticks of kielbasa, and five pounds of wolowina.” The butcher goes off to prepare Walczak’s order, and Walczak just stands there once again looking at the variety of proteins the butcher has to offer. Walczak is not used to this type of freshness in America, and he feels like he is a little kid again able to get all this quality food on a daily basis. A couple of minutes pass, and the butcher comes back over to Walczak with his freshly packaged meats. “There you go.” “Thanks.” Walczak gets his meet, and now makes his way over to his favorite place in the market…The Beer Aisle. There are piles and piles of beer, cases upon cases, and all the various Polish varieties are represented, Zywiec, Hevelius, Warka, and Tyskie among them. This is what I like to see, real beer. Anything drink that I can possibly want is here. A smile comes across the face of Walczak, and he leaves his shopping cart alone right next to a stack of Zywiec. A few seconds later Walczak comes back to the spot pushing a brand new empty cart in front of him. He loads up his cart, all with beer, and not repeating a single variety, until the cart is nearly overflowing. Walczak pushes both of his shopping cart, one hand on each, and makes his way over to the cashier. A little boy talking on his cell phone, watches as Walczak slowly approaches him. The boy is tiny in stature, and must have heard about Walczak’s presence from his friend over in produce. Walczak starts unloading his cart, and the boy quick starts tallying up the item. Another cashier comes over, and starts to bag the groceries. “Did you enjoy your shopping experience Mr. Walczak?” Walczak looks up at the boy who even though he gazes admiringly at Walczak, continues to scan his items. “Yeah, it was great I miss the quality of food back in the states, so it’s refreshing to get this kind of quality.” “Good to hear, your total comes to 180 zloty.” “Excuse me.” “Is there a problem?” “No, it just seems a little low, did you scan all of the items.” The boy looks over at the items, and signals to Walczak that he did in fact scan all of the items. Walczak is still shocked that with all that he had bought his total still seems to be the equivalent of 70 American dollars. “Here you go, keep the rest for yourself.” Walczak hands the boy two crisp hundred dollar notes. The boy is shocked, but shakes the hand of Walczak to show his thanks. Walczak leaves the store pushing both his carts, pleased at the service he had just received. Date: July 30, 2007 Location: Associated Market, Brooklyn, New York Walczak walks into the grocery store utterly disgusted at the establishment. The floor is cover in dust, and pieces of food that look like they haven’t been swept for at least a month. The shopping carts are all over the place no genuine order whatsoever. He grabs one, and has to empty all the coupon books, and food remnants. Walczak slowly makes his way over to the produce section, each step he takes, the look of disgust seems to grow on his face. He finally enters the produce section, and as he goes to grab some tomatoes a number of flies come up in the air making their way towards Walczak’s face. “What the fuck was that. Don’t they monitor this shit?” Walczak looks around for someone to help him, but no one is anywhere near the section. He makes his way over to the next aisle and finally finds someone stocking shelves. The boy has long, greasy, hair, and he sits disinterested on a box of canned soup, putting a can up on the shelf at the pace of about one every five minutes. “Excuse me, do you work here?” “Yeah I work here, would I be wearing this dumbass apron if I wasn’t.” “You better watch your mouth. Aren’t you paid to help the customer? I suggest you do your job.” “Whatever you say boss. What can I help you with my liege?” Walczak does not at all appreciate the heavy sarcasm in the boy’s voice, but decides not to take any further action. “Why are there flies in the produce section, and it’s not just one or two, they are all over the fucking place.” “I don’t know, I don’t work in that section, if you want ask John, the fat black guy that’s his section.” “Well, where is John? ‘ “Beats me, probably either taking a shit, or stuffing his fat face.” “Great that’s a lot of help. Do you at least know when the last time you guys got your shipment in.” “Some time last week.” “So none of that shit is fresh?” “Hell no, we just spray it with water every morning.” Walczak shakes his head, leaving the boy to get back to the half assed effort he was putting in to stocking the shelves. He thinks better of getting any vegetables, and makes his way over to look for some bread. He walks up and down looking for a bakery, but can’t find one, finally he gets to the last aisle, and sees a couple of shelves filled with bread. “What the fuck is this? Wonder Bread, what the fuck is Wonder Bread.” Walczak picks up package after package, of bread, but doesn’t seem to be satisfied. He squeezes leaving massive paw prints in the packages, but still does not put any of the bread in his. “Are you kidding me there is not one loaf of fresh baked bread in this entire fucking store. NOT ONE!!!” Walczak screams catching the attention of a mother and child at the other end of the aisle. The mother takes her son, and quickly gets out of the aisle fearful of what Walczak may do next. Walczak to leaves the aisle, yet again leaving his cart empty. Visibly angered, he searches for something edible within the store. He gets to the back of the store, and sees freezers stacked with piles and piles of prepackaged meats. He picks one up, and sees that most of the expiration dates come up within the next two or three days. Walczak keeps searching, but he can’t find anything that he deems adequate. He finally sees a man unloading packages of meat into a nearly empty freezer, and hopes that these are of the fresher variety. “Thank God, maybe I’ll be able to finally get something fresh in this store.” “Fresh? Ha, buddy you’re dreaming if you think this meat is fresh. I just brought this stuff out from the back. Hell, I don’t think that we have had a shipment since the middle of last week.” “That’s great.” “Yeah, I know how….” Walczak turns his back on the man, obviously not caring what he has to say. He makes his way towards the last aisle, and hopes that it can ease his nerves, as well as salvage the trip. He sees cases of beer line up the freezers, and walks down the line to browse the selection. Coors Light….Miller Lite…Budweiser…Bud Light…Heineken, but nothing that quite catches his eye. He goes up and down the aisle making sure that he has not missed anything, but still he cannot find what he is looking for. An employee runs by him, and Walczak whistles loudly to catch his attention. The man is dressed in a golf shirt, that has the word ‘MANAGER’ stitched across it. “Do you have an area where you guys hold your good beer?” “Good beer, we got the best right here, Heineken, Beck, Amstel Light. We actually have a pretty large selection.” “What about Polish Beer?” “I didn’t even know Poland made beer, I thought it was all vodka.” “YOU WHAT!!!” The man is taken aback, bringing his hand to his chest as Walczak snarls at him. Walczak’s eyes flare up as he back the man down, until the man’s back is nearly parallel with the floor. “Poland, my friend, makes the best beer in the world, and I suggest that if you want to broaden your horizons, as well as customers, you get some here as quick as possible.” “I…I…I…well, I will look into it, but I really have to get going.” The man runs off avoiding any further contact with the Polish giant. Walczak looks, at the cases of beers, and even though the selection is not up to par, the thought of staying in the Unites States, without the help of alcohol is unbearable. He grabs three cases of Budweiser, and makes his way, unhappily, over to checkout. A young Latina girl is standing with her back towards the register talking with one of her friends. Walczak unloads his cart, and waits for the girl to do her job. “Cough…Cough…Excuse me, but are you going to scan my items.” “Take it easy, gimme a second. Yea, so I can’t believe that shit that Ray was pullin, I am not just some ho, like Tanesha, who gonna give it up like dat.” “I know, right.” “I don’t want to interrupt, what I am sure is a very important conversation, but I am in a bit of a hurry.” “Jeez, why you all up on me like this, I am not your slave, or nothing like dat. Alright Jackie, I’ll talk to you later, cuz SOME PEOPLE, can’t wait.” The girl says nothing to Walczak, but does scan the items through. She has a bit of trouble with the last case, and shakes it, making what is sure to be a foamy surprise for Walczak. “You got ID.” “Yeah, but do you really think I am under twenty-one.” “If you talk to me like that again, I am gonna kick you out, GOT THAT.” Walczak reaches for his wallet, and hands over his ID, but if it not had been for the way the sisters in the orphanage had raised him, he would have surely struck the bitch down. “Your total is $55.32.” “Are you sure you got that right?” “Yeah, I don’t make mistakes mister, so pay up or get out.” Walczak hands the money over to the girl, and gladly picks up his beer making his way out of the store. The experience had been much different that that only days earlier in Poland, and only further brings down Walczak’s opinion of American culture. Date: July 25, 2007 Location: Walczak’s Estate, Gdansk Poland It is midday, on an overcast day, so Walczak has decided to stay at home, and catch up on some soccer action, something that he feels is greatly undermined in the United States. Today is a special day, because Cracovia is playing Wisla Krakow, and while Walczak is not from the South of Poland, he knows that this is the fiercest rivalry in all of Poland. He cracks open a fresh beer, and leans back in his recliner, awaiting the opening whistle, and just as Tomasz Moskala, gets set to kick off, Walczak’s doorbell rings. “God Dammit, can’t I have one day where I just relax.” Walczak gets up making his way to the door, placing his beer on a table on the way out. He opens the door and sees a short little man with graying hair standing in front of him. The man is wearing a khakis, and a light yellow polo t-shirt, dark sunglasses hanging off of them. “Mr. Wolecki, to what do I owe the pleasure.” “Tomasz, I hope that I am not disturbing you, but I just want to let you know that a couple of kids have been messing around in the empty pool in your backyard when you’re not here.” “What do you mean, like skateboarding and stuff?” “Yes, that and bike riding. They know that you are here only once in a while, and feel that they can take advantage of it. I felt I just had to let you know, just incase you want to take some precautions against it.” “Yeah, thanks for the heads up, I guess I should just get someone to come in and fill it up. I am gonna be here a lot more now, so why not enjoy my time here.” “Probably a good idea. By the way, how is it going in the States, I hear that you are some kind of big shot there.” “I don’t know about being a big shot, but I will say the one good thing about going to the states is that it gives me the money to enjoy my life here.” “Who knew something good could ever come out of the states.” The two men share a laugh as the much smaller man slaps Walczak on the shoulder. After the laughter has died down Walczak’s neighbor puts his glasses, off finally taking his hand of the big man. “Alright Tom, I have to get going, I gotta meet with little Timmy’s daycare. Got the tyke a camera for his birthday, and what does the kid do with it. Hide out in the girl’s bathroom taken upskirt shots.” “You got quite the kid there John.” “That’s the other thing whenever the kid excels in anything, school, sports, it’s her kid, but now we got a meeting with his teacher, and all of a sudden it’s MY KID. Can you believe that?” “Sound’s rough, but I won’t keep you any longer, don’t want Timmy getting in any more trouble.” “You said it.” Walczak shuts the door as his neighbor walks away down his walkway. He shakes his head, and goes back to watching the game and enjoying his beer. You really don’t meet people like this back in the states. Come to think of it I don’t think that I have liked any of my neighbors in all the years that I have lived in America. In America, you’re lucky to get one of your neighbors to wave to you, maybe say hello, but to come over to you and say someone is messing with your shit. No way, if it doesn’t bother them, than it doesn’t matter. Walczak doesn’t let anymore thoughts of the country across the Atlantic fill his head. He takes a sip of his beer, and focuses on what is really important, the game. Date: July 31, 2007 Location: Walczak’s Home, Queens, New York Walczak jogs down the stairs, pulling a black t-shirt over his head. Having just showered after a long workout from the gym he wants nothing more than to just sit down and relax. He picks up the remote, and flips to ESPN, hoping to get some good sports action going. “Welcome back, to the 2007 World Series of Poker, I’m Lon McEachern, joined by Norman Chad.” “You know Lon, the last time…” Walczak changes the channel before he can hear Norm Chad make a comment about one of his ex wives. He goes down one channel hoping to find something better to watch on ESPN 2. “Your word is floccinaucinihilipilification.” “Floccinaucinihilipilification, could I have the defini...” Pre-pubescent children spelling words that no one ever uses, is not Walczak’s idea of entertainment, so he quickly flipping over to the Fox Sports Channel, but just as he might have a glimmer of hope with a sports countdown, he hears something crash outside. He runs over to his window, and sees that there is a construction crew next door, knocking down the fence. He immediately slips on a pair of flip flops, and heads outside. “Hey, HEY, WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU GUYS DOING?!?!?!?” A heavy set man with a hard hat turns around to meet Walczak who is quickly closing the gap between the two men. “We got a work order here, and we are building an extension on your neighbor’s house, and this fence is just gonna get in the way.” “Yeah, I understand that, but this is my fence, and you don’t just take down peoples shit without asking them first.” “Not according to Joey, he said that this side is his, and told us to just knock it down.” “Well, get Joey out here, because I think that we are gonna have a problem.” The man cups his hands around his mouth calling Joey over. The man jogs over, he is near Walczak’s age, and is wearing jeans and a flannel t-shirt both torn up. His brown hair is covered in bits of debris, and he has a smile on his face as he sticks his hand out to Walczak. “How’s it going man?” Walczak looks down at the man’s hand deciding not to take before addressing him. “What’s going on, you decide to knock down my fence and not tell me that you’re doing it.’ “I didn’t have to tell you that I was knocking it down. IT’S MY FENCE.” “HOW THE HELL CAN YOU SAY THAT THIS IS YOUR FENCE!!!” “You know what, I am gonna solve this real quick. I am gonna get the plans and show you that this is my fence.” Joey leaves the scene, and makes his way back into his house. The project manager not seeming to want any part in this confrontation and makes his way towards some of his workers unloading a nearby van. Minutes later Joey emerges from the front door carrying the plans in his hand. “Now, I’ll show you that you are fucking wrong.” He unrolls the plans showing the houses and their respective property lines. “If you look right here, it clearly shows that this fence is mine, not yours.” “Where do I look?” “Right he…” Joey pauses mid sentence realizing that he was in fact wrong about the property line. In fact about a foot of the property beyond the fence belongs to Walczak as well. “Get your shit off of my property, and put the fence back up.” “Hold on one second, can’t we work this out.” “Yeah, we can work it out by you just putting the God Damn fence up.” Walczak turns to walk away as Joey angrily crumple up the plans in his hands. “Get back here, I am gonna tear this fence down whether you like it or not, and believe me even if you call the cops they’re not gonna do shit.” Walczak stops in his tracks slowly turning back towards his not so friendly neighbor. “If you do anything to that fence, but put it back up you won’t have to worry about the cops because I will come over there and kick your ass myself.” He gets right up into the face of Joey looking down at the man. “You don’t scare me. I know people.” “All the people you know won’t be able to help you if I get my hands on you.” “You don’t know what you’ve gotten yourself into.” Walczak turns away not saying another word, as the men have already started putting the fence back up. Date: August 1, 2007 Location: Walczak’s Home, Queens, New York The ringing of keys is heard outside as Walczak tries to get into his house. After a couple of seconds the ringing stops and the door handle turns, showing Walczak coming through the door with a handful of mail. He throws the mail on top of a nearby table, a wrestling magazine lays on top. Walczak goes to put his gym bag down, and rushes back to pick up the wrestling magazine. He flips over to the middle of the magazine, where a picture of him Powerbombing Tyler Rayne takes center stage. The article reads: “Tom Walczak enters this weekend against two familiar foes. Tyler Rayne and Big Bear Munson Monsoon will be staring at Walczak from the across the ring, as these competitors once again lock horns. Walczak has gotten the better of Rayne over the past couple of months, but this week he will have to deal with the uncertainty of tag partner Tony “The Grin” Gamble… Walczak stops reading the article, and closes the magazine back up. A smile comes over his face as he thinks of the pain that he has inflicted on Tyler Rayne for over a month. It seems like Rayne just can’t get a clue. He gets beat on week after week, and just can’t stay down. I’d expect that from Monsoon who is as dumb as they fucking come, but Rayne, I thought he was smarter than that. Rayne should have just dropped out after finding out the competition had been changed. I mean come one, I didn’t think that it would take Lisa Tyler this long to figure out that it was insane to give Rayne a chance to win a title shot of any kind. Walczak walks away from the mail, turning on the television to once again see a pleasant sight. The preview for Colossus graces the TV screen, and the image of Walczak once again forcefully slamming Rayne onto the mat appears. “That sure is a pretty sight, me pounding on Rayne just never gets old.” Date: July 26, 2007 Location: John Paul II Stadium, Krakow, Poland “For the first time in PRIME history we are broadcasting live from Poland, and the crowd is loving the display they are getting from their hero, Tom “The Polish Punisher” Walczak.” Just as Nick finishes talking the scene shifts to the center of the ring where Walczak holds Tyler Rayne high above his head. He slams him down to the mat with force, causing Rayne to grab his back in pain. “These two have a storied past, but Walczak seems to be in total control tonight not letting Rayne get and ounce of breathing space.” Walczak props Rayne up, seeing as the man cannot stand on his own. He puts Rayne’s head in between his legs and the crowd knows exactly what is coming next. “POLSKA GURA CLAP..CLAP..CLAPCLAPCLAP…. POLSKA GURA CLAP..CLAP..CLAPCLAPCLAP…. “ “Here it comes the POLISH POWERBOMB.” Walczak lifts Rayne high into the air slamming him down with a thud that echoes throughout the arena. Walczak looks over his fallen opponent and goes down to make the cover. ONE… TWO….. Ring…Ring…Ring…. The sound of an alarm clock rings through a bedroom, and we now see that what had just happened was just a figment of Walczak’s imagination. Walczak sits up turning off the alarm clock, and throwing his covers off of him.
