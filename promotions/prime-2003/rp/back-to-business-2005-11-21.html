21 November 2005

Date: 11/21/05 We find ourselves in a very…basic place. Bare walls, a mixture of concrete and green tiling which looks as though it has seen distinctly better days. Two large, industrial sized fans which look just about enough to cool the cavernous space before us. A large boxing/wrestling ring spanning about half of the left-hand side of the space, currently occupied by two rather formidably built gentlemen (one white, one Hispanic) sparring in full equipment. A cordon of battle-hardened weight benches, currently being manned by: a very Teutonic looking bloke lifting more weight in one hand (both hands being occupied) than the average individual could lift with the aid of all four major limbs and a couple of frat boys screaming each other on through a set of benchpresses. But it’s the other end of the gym where our attention is focused, on the clutch of boxing equipment taking up residence on the right hand side of the gym. The speed bags, the jump rope area, the heavy bags. On one heavy bag in particular, in front of which the gentlemen we have come here to see has currently taken up residence. He’s currently wearing a pair of high top sneakers, a white muscle shirt and a pair of olive drab shorts. Tyler Lopez's hands are gloved and he’s wailing away at the bag with a speed that his size would seem to belie. He’s also hitting it with enough force to suggest that the bag has somehow managed to insult his wife, his entire immediate family and his hometown. A voice pipes up from out of camera range. It is obviously English, the sort of English accent that even the untrained ear would immediately recognize as not being “upper class”. OSV: Now what, exactly, did this poor piece of canvas and stuffing do to you? A slight shift of camera angle and we now get to see the man behind the voice. He’s tall, dark haired, dressed very casually in a tank top and a pair of gray sweatpants. He’s well-built, not the size of the gentleman on the other end of the bag but big enough so that he’s managing to hold onto the heavy bag in the face of Tyler’s continued assault without looking as though it’s doing him too much damage. The man’s name is Ian. He runs the joint. Lopez: Nothing. I’m just in the mood to hit something this week. I: And this is different from how you usually are in what way, exactly? Lopez continues pounding away at the bag. Lopez: Very funny. I’m just feeling the need to work off some aggression, especially after the events of the last couple of months. I: You and half the world, mate. Seems like everyone in Manhattan’s come in here lately, asking about signing up for a membership. Lopez: They do know where they’re trying to sign up, right? I: Har-de-har. And yeah, they do. That’s the point. They’re all full of piss and vinegar and testosterone, they’re looking for somewhere…gods, what was the term that one bloke used…oh yeah, “butch”. At this, Lopez has to stop and laugh. Lopez: You’re kidding. I: Bloody wish I was. These stockbroker types showing up, looking like the most physical thing they’ve ever done in their lives is pushing around paper…now they suddenly wanna become Action Man. Lopez: Any success stories? I: Well, considering most of em take one look at guys like Eddie or Gunther over there and piss themselves in fear…so yeah, actually, we have signed up one or two. At this, Ian flashes Lopez a grin that could distinctly be described as “shit-eating”. I: So, how’s you? You and the missus ok? Lopez: Bout as well as could be expected, I guess. We were both at home when it happened…saw most of it on CNN…most of the time we just stood there in shock… I: Yeah, I know what you mean. I thought I’d gotten used to that sort of shit from back home but… Lopez shakes his head slightly, as if clearing out his head. Lopez: Anyway…we should probably stop talking about it. I: Why? This getting ya down? Or do you just wanna hit something some more. LopezM: Nah, I’ll probably get accused of trying to exploit my “distinctly masculine accessibility”. Ian is, officially, confused. I: Wha? Lopez: That is, if I haven’t “pontificated interminably” already. I: Ok, now I’m getting worried. Yer getting poncey on me. Lopez laughs. Lopez: Sorry. It’s just that I have a match coming up. I: Knew it! You only ever start sounding like this when some ponce git starts trying to get all flash. Who is it this time? Lopez: I’m feeling the need to hit something again. The two men resume their positions from earlier and begin again. I: Who is it this time? O'Neil? Lopez: Nope. I: Not a tag match again, is it? Lopez: Well, no. I: All right then, who? Lopez: A lot of guys. Its a Battle Royal. It takes Ian a second. I: Again? Lopez: Formerly, I would agree. But since I lack anytype of angle, and the PRIME administration has yet to give me a run, what can I say. I: So what was all that shit about “distinctly masculine accessibility”? Lopez: Some people think I’m trying to paint myself as some sort of super-hero, some sort of ultra-man of the people. Captain Joe Public. I: They ever consider the fact that you could just be, well…you? Lopez: Nope. They are being pushed into the box of trying to represent the sort of “evil” that stopped being scary back when Alice Cooper was still selling well. I don’t think he has all that great a grasp on the concept of “honesty”. I: So most of them lie? Lopez: That’s part of it. Here’s an example: remember what happened after Katrina? I: You still showed up to wrestle. Lopez: Men who claims to give no credence to the notions of “good” and “evil” are attempting to qualitatively judge how much of one or the other I have within me. I: Or, in English…he thinks he can judge something in you that he doesn’t even believe in? Lopez: Yup. And he doesn’t see the irony in this at all. Then again, he doesn’t seem to have a real clear grasp on what he says. I: Such as? Lopez: How long did it take me to say this sentence. I: About five seconds. Lopez: Some would call that “pontificating interminably”. Ian shoots Lopez a blank look. Lopez: Talking too much. I: You’re joking. Lopez: Nope. Having been called arrogant and a “self-doubting maggot” I: You been slagging anyone in particular off any? Lopez: Nope. I: Why? Lopez: Because that would be dumb. I’ve seen all of my opponents in action. For all their personal defects…and they are legions, let me assure you…they can wrestle. I’m well aware I am gonna have to give everything I have in there. But I think I’ve got one thing working in my favor. I: Whuzzat? Lopez: I think I've pissed half of them off. I: That’s a good thing? Lopez: Uh huh. See, most of the locker room consist of wrestlers prides themselves on being above it all. Nothing cracks their resolve, they never show emotion, they’re always the ice king. But in the last week or so, I saw it. A little crack. A little chink in the armor. In everyone on the roster, they are distracted. I: This could just make him want to hurt you even worse, y’know? Lopez: Good. Lopez and Ian pause for a second. I: Sounds like you’ve got a hell of a match ahead of you. Lopez: I do. I: So we’d better get back to work, then. (Ian gives Lopez a smack upside the head.) Come on, back to it! And as the two men get back to business, we slowly fade out until we’re back to black.
