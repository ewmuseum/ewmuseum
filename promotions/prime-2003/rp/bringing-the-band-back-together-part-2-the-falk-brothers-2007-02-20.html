Bringing The Band Back Together (Part 2, the Falk Brothers)

20 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 20th, 2007 The Falk Brothers. They were legitimately brothers, with Scott Falk being the older brother of Robert Falk. Despite being brothers, they were like night and day between each other. Robert Falk was a quieter and more reserved individual, usually speaking only when spoken to or whenever something seemed important enough to talk about. Scott Falk was a loud, boastful, rude, egomaniacal jackass who always spoke, even when not spoken to, and even when it might be considered rude or inappropriate for him to say something. On top of that, Scott wasn't exactly very bright compared to his brother. Robert Falk was a big, powerful guy. He was almost seven feet tall and over three hundred pounds, and was noted for being big and muscular, yet possessing the speed needed to land much more forceful blows on opponents. Scott Falk was a small, speedy guy who often thinks he's bigger than he is. While he's good at striking and flying, he was often thrown around like a ragdoll. Right now, the two brothers were sitting at a diner somewhere in the city of Phoenix. They have yet to be served anything except some coffee for Scott (which he likely never needed in the first place) and some tea for Robert. Scott wore a simple white T-shirt and a pair of jeans, and he also wore some bandana on his head that's meant to make him look "street", but really makes him look like a "wigger". Robert wore a black Hawaiian shirt over a white tanktop and black pants. Sitting across from the mismatched brothers was Allen Brown, referred to henceforth as "the Codemaster". The Codemaster wore a black jacket, a white T-shirt with a 1-Up Mushroom logo on it, and a pair of jeans. Robert, after sipping his tea, commented on the two pieces of paper that the Codemaster had passed forward along the table, "I thought you outgrew taking advantage of idiots." The Codemaster smiled his wily (like Dr. Wily) grin at the younger Falk, "You kiddin'? They're right there. Waitin' to be taken advantage of. I swear to Heat Man and Mantorok, if I didn't take advantage of people slower than a brain damaged Zangief..." Scott was unusually quiet. Usually, he'd be the first to jump right in with some lewd comment about doing one's mother or some impossible tale about plowing some woman from behind with his unusually large penis. Usually, it's impossible because Scott sometimes claims that his penis is so long that when he inserts it into a woman's butt, it comes out at her mouth. Which is just gross. Finally, he spoke up. "You're fucking kidding me, right?" he asked, "How long have we been following you, motherfucker? What have we seen out of it? I mean, goddammit, I haven't seen a single hot chick since we started fucking following you!" The Codemaster thought about it for a few seconds, putting his hand on his chin. "So, your argument for why you don't want to be in our supergroup is that there won't be a hot chick involved." "Fuckingiven!" the diminuitive Falk said, crossing his arms defiantly and not even caring at the fact that his invented word had gotten him stares from everywhere around him. Bothered by the stares, Scott looked around and shouted, "What? You want me to start dickslapping the whole lot of you? Go back to your stupid daily life bullshit!" Everyone reluctantly did as told. "That's a shame." the Codemaster said, sighing and crossing his arms. He reached into the pocket of the coat he was wearing, and pulled out a small picture, "I was gonna go get this person next, too." The Codemaster placed the picture on the table and slid it across the table. Scott looked down at the picture, and saw a very pretty black-haired girl who probably wasn't even out of her teens yet. Robert leaned over and saw the person in the picture, and commented, "Alexandria?" "Your recognition skills are on the level today." the Codemaster said, with a nod towards the larger, but younger Falk. "Why her?" Scott asked, picking up the picture and giving it a glare, "I mean, yeah, I'd fuck her harder than a diamond, but won't Joey object?" The Codemaster sighed as he lurched forward and snatched the picture from Scott's hand, "He can't do a damn thing." He then placed the picture back in his pocket, "The princess is eighteen. She can come to another castle at any time she feels like. Given how much of a free spirit she is, I would imagine it'd just take only a tiny bit of my usual trickery to get her to come along for the our epic quest." The Codemaster then gave a wink. "So. Does that convince you two to journey with me into that terrible thing known as professional wrestling?" the man known in some circles as the Breaker of Kingdom Hearts asked. Robert casually drank some more tea. He then picked up a small napkin and patted the area around his mouth and lips with it. He quietly set the napkin down and looked at Codemaster, "I'm in." Scott did a double take. His brother made a decision before he did? Madness. MADNESS, I TELL YOU! Eventually, Scott sulked, laid his arms and much of his upper body on the table, and let out a pretty big sigh, "Fucking fine. I'm in, too. Can't do much without this big lug, anyway." The Codemaster smirked. He pulled out a notepad and pen from his jacket's pocket. The only thing written on this notepad, at the top, were the following two words: BLUE ROGUES Below that, the Codemaster had written two things. Robert Falk. Scott Falk. The Codemaster smirked and got up from his seat, and looked down at the two brothers that still remained seated. "Well, boys. What's say we go find ourselves a very cute fourth member, shall we?"
