Memories and Plumbing, a great combination!

19 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th September, 2005 (Note: Sorry about this. This must be one of the worst RP's I've ever written and I want to apologise to Jamester for not giving him more of a battle.) Saturday 17th September, 2:34pm '40-0 and Andre Agassi is now........' 'And thats another base hit.......' 'You can't do that. That has to be a penalty.....' Mark was sat on the edge of his bed, flicking through the channels his illegal cable box could receive, trying to find something to keep his short attention span interested. He had been scanning the channels for a good ten minutes, before he found something that he wanted to watch. 'We are now set for a light heavyweight title match between Terry Martin and the champion Chuck Liddell' UFC: Ultimate Fighting Championship, now this was something that brought back a lot of memories. He missed being inside that octagon, but he was a member of PRIME now and he couldn't have been more happier, although it was good to look back sometimes and imagine where he could have gone if he had stuck to his MMA (mixed martial arts) career. Mark had perfected the art of Kuk Sool Wan while a part of the UFC fighting circuit, which was a mixture of hand strikes and throws. A lot of fighters who specialised in Kuk Sool Wan also used a lot of kicks, but Mark hadn't quite perfected that style of Kuk Sool Wan, preferring to use his tremendous strength to strike his opponent down to the floor and mounting them to finish them off with some killer blows. He had a record of 13-2 before finally bowing out after the death of his parents, but he was still good friends with a lot of the fighters on the circuit and had often thought about returning to his MMA career. That wasn't an option anymore, but he knew if anything we're to happen to his professional wrestling career he would be welcome back to the UFC with open arms and this made him smile a little as he sat watching Chuck Liddell make his way down to the cage. Mark was always good friends with Chuck Liddell, but he had never had the oppurtunity to fight him due to Mark being 364 lbs at the time of his MMA career and being classed as a super heavyweight (obviously) and Chuck being only 204 lbs and classed as a light heavyweight. It was always Mark's dream to fight Liddell, but it would have to remain a dream, it would never have happened. Mark glanced at his watch and stood up to his feet, the plumbers we're expected to be at his home between 2:00 and 3:00 pm and he wanted to be downstairs to allow them to get to work. He hadn't had a proper shower for weeks, using the PRIME showers in the meantime and he couldn't wait to finally have his restroom finished. Blackwolf: 'Oh man, I was looking forward to that fight, Oh well nevermind I'm sure it will be replayed again some other time.' Mark flicked his TV off and made his way downstairs. It was another fifteen minutes before their was finally a knock on the door, which would have given him adequate time to watch the contest and this pissed him off a little bit, but his anger was subsided when he opened the door to reveal a gorgeous looking blonde haired woman. Blackwolf: 'Wow! Umm.....can I....erm.....can I help you?' Woman: 'You needed a restroom installing?' Mark looked around like the plumbers we're suddenley going to show up, but there was no movement on the street. He scratched his head in confusion before looking back at the woman, who was becoming restless stood in the drive waiting to be allowed in. Blackwolf: 'Yeah I did, what would you know about that?' Woman: 'I'm the plumber, I'm here to install your restroom.' Blackwolf: 'Your the plumber? But your a woman.' The tall and gorgeous blonde just shakes her head in disgust. Woman: 'Your all the same aren't you? Every man I've been to has said the same, but your a woman, YES I'M A WOMAN!, is there something wrong with that? Can women not the job properley? You stereotypical....... Blackwolf: 'Ok....Ok, I just thought it would be a man, but no problem, if they can do the job then I wouldn't care if it was a monkey with a toolbox at my door.' Woman: 'Really?' Blackwolf: 'Well maybe not a monkey, but........nevermind. Is it just you, do you not have anybody else with you?' Woman: 'Yeah, my van is just around the corner. My girls are getting the equipment ready to bring in here.' Blackwolf: 'Your girls?' Woman: 'Yeah, my girls. There's me, Sandra and Jessica. Don't worry we're professionals, you'll have your shower up today if you want to take one, but the rest of the room may take a couple of days.' Blackwolf: 'Ok, I'll leave the door open, just make your way in when your ready, its the second door on your left upstairs. By the way what's your name?' Woman: 'Ali, pleased to meet you, Mark isn't it?' Blackwolf: 'Yeah.' Ali the plumber made her way back around the street and towards her van as Mark made his way back inside the house and upstairs again towards his room. The fight might have still been on and he may just be able to catch the ending of it. It was a championship match and thus lasted five rounds and not the normal three round limit. Each round lasted five minuted and five times five was twenty five, or it was the last time Mark went to school which was......well lets not go into that here, its kind of embarrassing. Mark pushed his bedroom door open and jumped onto the bed, actually he sat down on the bed, if he'd of leaped onto his bed he wouldn't have had one left. He grabbed the remote and turned the TV off standby. The fight was still going and it was into the last round, yes he'd made it and he would get to see the end of one of the most anticipated UFC matches in history. Chuck was bleeding from the mouth and Martin didn't appear to have a single scratch on his buffed up body, wait let me repeat that, his well toned body, wait......oh shit! Blackwolf: 'Come on Chuck, you can take this punk. Right, left, right, left, come on man!' Liddell was shaken up, but with only 1:32 left in the round Chuck did exactly what Mark told him to do as he unloaded right hands, followed by left hands and a sharp kick to the head took Terry Martin down to the canvas. Liddell then leaped onto Martin and began to unloaded fist after fist into the face of Martin causing official Big John McCarthy to step in and put an end to the contest, declearing Liddell the winner. Mark leaped up from his bed and punched the air in delight. This was only Martin's second ever loss and it couldn't have come at a worse time for the Chicago native as he looked to have Liddell beaten before the barrage from Chuck had knocked him down and out. Ali ran into Mark's room with a look of worry on her face. Ali: 'Is something wrong?' Mark gave a little chuckle at the plumber before pointing at the TV. Blackwolf: 'No, Liddell has just won his fight, I was just cheering him on, nothing to get worried about.' Ali: 'Liddell?' Blackwolf: 'He's a UFC fighter. I used to be on the circuit too before my parents death.' Ali dropped her head down. Ali: 'I'm sorry, I didn't know' Blackwolf: 'Don't be, forget about it.' Mark began to shadow box before turning back to Ali who was still stood in the door, looking down down at the floor. Blackwolf: 'Anyway, I have to go get ready for my match this week. I'll be in the back garden if you want me.' Ali: 'Your match? But I thought you said you weren't in the UFC anymore' Blackwolf: 'I'm not, I'm on PRIME, and I have a match this week with Jesse Jamester and I need to get ready. The guy hasn't lost a match yet and its going to be really tough, nothing I can't handle, but I still need to make sure my minds focused.' Mark made his way past Ali and out onto the landing. Blackwolf: 'So the shower will be done tonight?' Ali: 'Yeah it should be.' Blackwolf: 'Great, cos' I'll really need one after this.'
