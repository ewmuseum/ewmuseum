Date: ReV 180 -November 17, 2002 “Why’s it always so fucking dreary on your birthday?” Tyler Rayne had his back to her, watching the tourists as they mulled about the grey Boston afternoon. Coats bundled and scarves wrapped tight, the visitors hustled from one tiny shop to another, scurrying to get souvenirs and chowder. Tyler watched it all with mild amusement and, though he’d never admit it, a certain amount of longing. Normalcy. How quaint. How… foreign. “It’s November, Ty. November is dreary. Plus, you brought me to New England of all fucking places. Leave it to a couple religious zealouts to settle down in the only place more depressing than Britain herself. Jolly ol’ England my ass.” “I like England.” “You also run around in a Halloween mask and get sweaty with men in their underwear.” “Point. Bitchy. Sarcastic. Stereotypically over-simplistic. But still. Point.” “What do you think of this one?” He turned from the window, from the envious draw of mundane life, and smiled at his little sister. It was a smile that, in years later, would become synonymous with the man himself. With the sudden onset of thousands of female orgasms. For now, though, it was simply the expression he flashed when faced with a particularly unavoidable dilemma: loving someone so utterly and completely ridiculous as Rena. Her blonde hair poked in odd places from the pink, knitted snow cap shoved down tight on her head. Blue eyes blazed with amusement under the pink fur (At least he thought it was fur. Looked a hell of a lot more like Christmas garland…) that outlined the bottom of the cap. It looked like some sort of creatures, bunnies?, had been stitched into the sides. Little mutated rabbits bounding around the pink landscape like a bizarre kindergarten art masterpiece. A pink string dangled from the top of the cap, bouncing a big white puff ball in front of her nose. Rena turned her lips upward, eyes nearly crossed as she focused intently on the giant fuzz and tried to blow it away. “You look ridiculous.” “I look amazing.” “Amazingly ridiculous.” “Ooo. Good one. Keep using zingers like that at work and they’ll be handing you a world championship in no time.” The tip of her tongue poked between her lips for only a moment. A squint-eyed, juvenile moment of sibling annoyance. He rolled his eyes and tried not to laugh. She turned her head back over her shoulder, casting a seductive gaze into the mirror behind her. “Pretty sexy, huh?” “Yeah. Totally. You know, if I was into that sort of thing.” “Incest?” “Retards.” “Tyler!” She grabbed the nearest thing to her, a pair of thick winter gloves, and chucked them at him. He laughed and feigned a frightened defense, letting the gloves smack harmlessly off his leather jacket and down to the floor. He snatched them from the ground and walked up behind her to hang them back on the rack. Unfortunately, Rena was still admiring herself in the mirror. Which must have meant she was blocking his attempt to be a responsible customer. Why else would he have had reason to tuck a shoulder and check the poor girl a good six inches across the floor? She stumbled, arms whirling out in front of her to gain her balance. “You ass!” “What?” “What? What! I’ll show you what…” He turned just in time to catch the half-hearted attempt at her retaliation. She wrenched her wrist from his grasp and swung with the other hand. He was just quick enough to jump back. Rena pursued, swinging wildly at her older brother. Tyler just smiled and backtracked across the shop, effortlessly swatting away her meager attempts to land… whatever it was she was trying to do. “You’re such a pansy.” The rubber sole of her shoe found a swift and sudden stop against his shin. “You little brat…” It’s a natural reaction. Someone kicks you in the shin, you bend to grab it. Tyler wasn’t exactly a towering man, but he was still a good six inches or so taller than his little sister. So as he bent to massage the wound, Rena leapt into the air, wrapping her arm around his neck and wrenching her big brother around in a headlock. “Who’s the pansy now, Ty?” *AHEM* “Is there a problem back there?” The shopkeeper was a mole of an old woman. Liver spots and Coke bottle glasses. Her call from the front counter was raspy, but still managed to carry a certain tinge of that matriarchal authority. Both Tyler and Rena immediately found their postures perfect, hands working overtime to smooth the creases in their clothes. Eyes to the floor. “Way to go, Ty.” “Oh hush with you.” He snatched the cap from her head and began to walk toward the counter. “C’mon. Let’s go.” “Sudden rush to leave, huh? ‘Fraid someone might have seen you get your ass kicked by your little sister?” “First off… you didn’t kick my ass. Second off, isn’t a soul in Boston’d recognize me without my mask on.” “Who is it again you’re wrestling this weekend? Maybe I should just go in your place…” Tyler threw the cap up on the counter. “Se7yn.” “Which one’s that again?” “Guy who thinks he’s a vampire.” She laughed. “Right. What’s his deal, anyway?” “Other than bein’ bat shit crazy? Fuck if I know. Hell, I wish the guy was a vampire. Little supernatural power would probably make him a better wrestler. Better, of course, being a relative term here, but… oh, and the flowers there.” He was sifting through a wad of bills he’d pulled from his pocket and only nodded toward the shelves behind the old woman. He decided to look up when the frail old woman refused to budge. “The daisies. Please.” “Aww. You remembered.” “I’m sweet like that.” “You have your moments.” The old woman carefully pulled a bundle of vibrantly pink daises from their resting place and set them on the counter. Tyler smiled and passed them on to Rena, who was positively beaming. “Gerbers. My favorite.” “I know.” “Thirty-two fifty.” Tyler handed the woman two twenty dollar bills, stuffed the other cash back down in his pocket, and yanked the cap from the counter. “Keep it. Let’s roll, kid.” “Kid? I’m like, three years younger than you.” “Younger. Which would make you my kid sister, now wouldn’t it?” “Whatever.” Even though she gave him a playful shove, she made no attempt to push him away as he stuffed the pink atrocity down on her head. With arm wrapped around her shoulder, Tyler Rayne led his little sister out into the chilly Boston afternoon. “Happy Birthday, Rena.” “Thanks, Ty. Love?” “Love.” “Good. Me too.” ---- -November 17, 2008 His left knee throbbed a prophetic warning of rain. A distant, rolling thunder echoed the sentiment. Tyler Rayne kneeled there in the closely manicured grass, hand shaking with the unsteady rhythm of grief. The beautiful orange petals of the daisies scraped and beat across the thin green paper they’d been wrapped in. He crouched as still the stone that jutted from the ground before him. Unblinking eyes transfixed upon the words that his mind, to this day, still had trouble comprehending. Rena Elizabeth Studd November 17, 1974 – November 17, 2004 Katterina Wylde hovered silently above him. The fringes of her black dress fluttered in a shallow breeze. Nature continued to move around them. Time, however, seemed frozen in this moment. He hadn’t moved for minutes. Seemed like hours to her. Just… staring. There were no words that she could offer. No sympathy or comfort that seemed appropriate. After all these years, he just couldn’t let go. Couldn’t get over… She stiffened as she felt him approach. The soft crunch of shoes against the grass. If Tyler heard him coming, he gave no indication. When he heard the voice, however, there was a sudden and immediate rigidity to his posture. “The fuck are you doing here?” The flower stems crumpled in the excruciating crush of his fist. Tyler heard the crackle of paper and had to concentrate very hard to force himself to ease the grip. Every muscle in his body tightened. A spring loaded trap waiting anxiously to be unleashed. Deep breaths. Calming breaths. Not here. Not on this ground. Not in front of Rena. “She was my sister.” “She was my wife.” Now he stood. Tyler rose to his full height, which, in fairness, isn’t incredibly intimidating. The icy stone glare he turns upon his former brother-in-law? Downright fucking frightening. Heated enough to wilt the flowers he’d just set on his sister’s grave. Tony Studd had seen this all before, though. Hell, he’d spent the better part of his very short career as a professional wrestler seeking that glare. Sinking to unmentionable depths of depravity to earn Tyler’s ire. It had made for scintillating television. Guaranteed ratings and money like you wouldn’t believe. It also turned out to be a shit storm of an idea when Tony later fell in love with Rena. Marrying your arch nemesis’ sister never tended to work out well. This was no exception. “You’re in violation of your restraining order.” “Fuck yourself, Tony. You saw me here. You approached me.” “Damn right I did. So I could tell you to leave. Now.” “Guys. Ty…” “Stay out of this bitch.” He didn’t even take his eyes from Tyler to say it. “Talk to her like that again and you’ll be experiencing all sorts of flashbacks as to why you retired. Restraining order or not.” “What is it with you? Always stickin’ up for this stray. It’s bad enough you used her to replace Rena, bu-” “The fuck did you just say?” “You heard me.” “Don’t you ever think for one fucking second I would replace Rena wi-” “You’re such a fucking ingrate, Rayne. Always were. You think we didn’t notice? All you ever wanted to do was gallivant around the world with the fucking carpet muncher here. You know what Rena wanted? All she wanted. You to settle down. To stop… moving. Stop fucking. Just… stop. Be still. Be normal. But you couldn’t give it up, could you? You just couldn’t fucking quit. What is it, Rayne? Huh? The money? The fame? The sluts? Why couldn’t you just do the one thing, the one fucking thing your sister asked of you? You loved ‘er so god damned much…” Words were unable to form in the mind of Tyler Rayne. There was no proper response. There was no explanation or answer for the questions. There was only a barely controlled rage. A pulsing, furious hatred racing through his veins. Invigorating his muscles. It was all he could do to keep from tearing Tony apart. Katterina wasn’t faring much better herself. Not here. Not on this ground. Not in front of Rena. Discretion, they say, is the better part of valor. Not that Rayne would have ever known… but for once in his life… for this one time… maybe it was time to give the theory a test. He stepped to the side. Attempted to walk away from his former brother-in-law. His former nemesis. Attempted to just let it all go. A firm hand against his chest stopped all those attempts pretty damn flat. “I’ll tell you what it is. See, I finally figured you out. You actually like this shit? You get your fuckin’ rocks off on it? The blood. The violence. The pain. You’re some sorta fetish junkie. A fuckin’ freak. All you know is suffering. Pain and misery. It’s all you’ve ever been good for. You’ve spent all your fucking life looking for it. Picking fights. Jumping off of cages and scaffolds and Lord knows what else. All that fucking bullshit. All to feed that emptiness. That… hole. You’re a disease, Rayne. A fucking parasite. You just suck the life out of those around you. Dragging everyone into your misery. Your… depression. You’re the God damn Plague. Everything you touch, you destroy. Everyone close to you…” For the first time, Tony takes his eyes from Tyler’s. He lets them drift slowly down to the engraving. “…dies.” His lip quivers. Something gleans in the corner of his eye. He can no longer meet Tyler’s stare. “You killed her. You and your sick, depraved life. You did this to her. To my Rena. You put here there. You fuck. You sick fuck. It should’ve been you. It should’ve fucking been you. You know that? It should’ve been you.” Tony Studd melted. His hand slid from Tyler’s chest. His legs folded beneath him. Knees settled in the grass. Head hung low. Tyler didn’t move. He didn’t look down. Didn’t offer anything but a quiet admittance. “You’re right. It should have been me.” “Ty…” “Just… just leave. Get the fuck outta here. I never… I never wanna see you again.” For the first time in their lives, Tyler Rayne and Tony Studd agree on two things in a row. “Me neither.” “And you…” Tony doesn’t look at Katterina when he speaks to her, but there’s no mistaking the fact that he is. “I’d leave ‘im if I were you. Less you wanna end up like Rena. Like that Jap he left in Tokyo. He’ll be the death of you. Just like he was the death of her. The death of us. You killed me. The day you killed her. ‘Spose that was always what you wanted, really. Well, congratu-fuckin-lations. You took my life, Rayne. You… just leave me here. Where I belong. Just leave me. Just leave…” Under any other circumstances, the tortures Katterina Wylde would have inflicted for such transgressions were too numerous and obscene to tell. But she knew this wasn’t her fight. This wasn’t her call. She was simply here as a friend. For Tyler. To support him. So when he walked away, she had no choice but to follow. There was nothing there worth really fighting for anyway. Only the haunting ghosts of the dead. And Tyler was carrying those with him. Wherever they went. “Why’s it always so fucking dreary on your birthday?”</
