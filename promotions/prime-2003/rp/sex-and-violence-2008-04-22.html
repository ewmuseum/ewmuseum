Sex and Violence

22 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd April She wasn’t perfect by any means; the fringe of her voluminous brown hair ran along her forehead on a surely-unintentional slope, more than a couple of her teeth were crooked, and she certainly didn’t have a Hollywood complexion. That didn’t change the fact that she was a cut above the women he usually shared a bed with; at least he didn’t have to pay to be with this one. Her apartment was clean and bright. The white walls were tastefully decorated with various items of modern art, and there was not an item sat upon the laminate wood flooring that did not seem out of place. The views from the giant floor-to-roof windows in the living area were spectacular, especially at this time of day. Thousands of tiny lights lit up the night sky across the cityscape, as the headlights of a hundred vehicles darted through the sleeping city’s streets. Compared to his place, this flat was utopian. Not for the first time in the past few weeks, Jason found himself in an unusual – but rather pleasant – situation. Before, a woman of Adelina Tuyet’s calibre wouldn’t have stopped to look twice if she passed Jason Natas in the street. Actually, she would… just to turn her nose up and sneer at his general disregard for his own appearance. Then again, Jason wasn’t a competitor in the biggest wrestling promotion in the country a couple of weeks ago. What a difference a little bit of fame makes. Slowly he approached her from behind and gently wrapped his heavily-tattooed arms around the purple, silk nightgown that she had wrapped her petite frame in. As she looked out across the city, she took one final sip from the glass of wine in her hand, before rubbing her, little, soft hand against the rough on Jason Natas’s arm. Their reflection in the mirror wasn’t exactly picture perfect. A burly, rugged professional wrestler who had lived life on the streets and had the scars to prove it, cradling a reasonably-wealthy white collar lady (who had probably never done an honest day’s graft in her entire life) in his arms. Jason didn’t care though; tonight belonged to them. Sure, he may up and leave out of the blue in the morning, but that didn’t matter. He didn’t want to hear about this woman’s problems, he didn’t want to be a shoulder to cry on, all he asked for was one night. Just one night; is that so much to ask for? THUMP! The first knock hit the door hard, startling both Adelina and Jason. The Anti-Superstar broke his embrace and turned on his heel as the doorframe rattled with the force of a second knock. He glanced at a nearby clock; “who the fuck knocks on a door at half-past fuckin’ midnight?” he snorted. He made no attempt to tone his guttural lexical choice down, even in the presence of a member of the fairer sex. “Adelina, c’mon, open up,” a loud voice slurred from the other side of the door. Immediately a look came across her face that told a hundred stories, and none of them good. “Oh shit, not again,” she said, all in a fluster. She immediately darted across towards the door as the person on the other side knocked again, and took a look through the peep-hole. “God-damn it!” she barked, throwing a fist of her own against the door in frustration. “Whoa whoa, slow down a second hun,” uttered Natas, attempting to calm her down a little. It wasn’t one of his strong points. “Let me in! C’mon! We just want to have a little fun…” the voice spluttered once again, met by a cackle of laughter from at least two other males. Adelina took a step away from the door; the mere sound of his voice sent shivers running up and down her spine. “It’s this guy, Ray. We dated, now he won’t leave me alone, he’s obsessed, I swear,” she trembled. As she spoke, Jason could tell that Adelina was almost petrified of the faceless Ray. Clearly that relationship hadn’t ended on a positive note. “We gotta find a way to get rid of him…” The knocking wouldn’t stop. It was beginning to give Jason a headache. “Let ‘em in, I can handle ‘em,” Natas announced, flashing Adelina a slight sneer from the corner of his mouth. “Are you crazy?! There’s three of them at least! You don’t know what these guys’ll do to you… to us…” Natas didn’t want to hear it; he placed his hands firmly against her shoulders in effort to calm her down. “I said let ‘em in. I’ve dealt with worse than a bunch of boozed-up cronies before…” Amazingly, she seemed to have all the confidence in the world in Jason; he was, after all, a man who had made a career out of throwing his fist into the faces of other men. As Jason sneaked off into the nearby bathroom, she gingerly stepped forward and unlocked the door. In stepped a grinning, tall gentleman, flanked by two others. Ray, the focal point of the group, wore a long, black leather coat that was opened up to reveal a wine-stained sky-blue shirt below. From the gap between the door that he was peering through, Jason could see that the guy was an absolute mess. Thousands of split ends sprung from his greasy, unkempt mop of long, black hair, and the few teeth that were still in his mouth were yellow, some of them black. “Heeeyyy, Adelina, baby,” he said as he attempted to swing an arm around her. She was having none of it, and promptly pushed the intoxicated scruffball away from her. “We’re headin’ out into the town; I think you should join us…” The alcohol in his breath stung her nostrils, causing her to recoil immediately. “Screw you, Ray,” she retorted defiantly, knowing that Jason was lurking in the background. “I’m not going anywhere…” “Ooohhh, this cat’s got claws,” Ray chuckled as he took a swig on the half-empty bottle of Jack Daniels in his hand. After passing the bottle on to one of his associates, he clenched a fist and immediately swung at Adelina. It connected with her left cheek perfectly, sending her toppling to the floor in a heap, much to the amusement of Ray and his boys. “Hold the fort, guys, I gotta take a leak…” The sight of Ray lurching towards the bathroom satisfied Ray; he’d successfully managed to suppress the urge to rush out and attack the guy after seeing what he’d done to Adelina. Now he’d get his chance. Ray slid into the bathroom, locking the door behind him, and stood over the toilet bowl, preparing to unzip. Before he could get any further, Natas sprung from the shower cubicle behind him and wrapped his left forearms around Ray’s throat. Not strong enough to choke the guy, but certainly enough to hurt him a little. In his right hand, Jason raised up a discarded razorblade, holding it precariously close to the drunkard’s eyeball. “The fuck are you? Bitch’s new boyfriend?” snarled Ray. The level of his intoxication was so great that he seemed unphased by the fact that Jason was within but a couple of millimetres from carving into his eye. “Worse,” was Jason’s simple response as he threw his knee into the villain’s back, sending him falling forwards, his chin narrowly missing the toilet. Dropping the blade to the ground, Natas knelt down, grabbed the drunk by his greasy locks, and immediately forced his head downwards into the rancid toilet water. Ray thrust and threw his arms around the place, frantically trying to get Jason to let go, but it was to no avail. Eventually Jason brought the gasping drunkard up for a little air; he didn’t want to kill the guy, just send a message. “How’s my fuckin’ piss taste? Huh?” he asked Ray, who spat out a jet of toilet water through his rotting teeth. “Suggest you stay away from Adelina, ya prick, or I’m gonna have to take this up a level, ya hear me?” Still unable to find words through his sharp intakes of breath, Ray nodded and opened his eyes for the first time since emerging from the bowl. Jason could see that he had gotten through to the scumbag. “Good, guess I won’t have to cut out yer fuckin’ eyeballs just yet then,” he said, standing up. He slid the latch on the toilet door open, before grabbing Ray by the neck and unceremoniously dumping him out onto the floor of the living area. Immediately his sidekicks recoiled; clearly disheartened by the defeated figure cast by their soaking, downed leader. The disgusting toilet water dripped from Ray’s hair and faced as he attempted to scramble up to his feet. “Don’t think these boys’ll be troublin’ you any longer,” he said to Adelina. She was crying, and a sizeable bruise was beginning to form under her right eye. She rose to her feet at the sight of Natas, and grabbed his arm for security. Jason, meanwhile, shifted his glance to Ray’s partners. The cocksure attitude that they had entered the room with had all but deserted them now that a 6’4” professional wrestler had just put the fear of god into their leader. “Run along now boys…” And that they did. They pulled Ray to his feet, but as soon as he regained his vertical base he pushed them away. “I’ll get you, bitch, this dick can’t be around all the time,” he blurted out, pointing a finger at Adelina. Soon enough all three of them were out the door, which Ray took the liberty to slam forcefully behind him. “Thank you, Jase,” she mumbled, turning to face The Anti-Superstar. “Told ya, ain’t nothin’ I ain’t done before.” “They’ll come back though… when you’re not here.” “We’ll see about that.”
