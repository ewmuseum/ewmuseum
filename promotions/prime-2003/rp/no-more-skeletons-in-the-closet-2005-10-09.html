No More Skeletons In The Closet

9 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/10/05 “I have THE biggest match in my career coming up and you bring me to New York City?! I hate this place, it’s dirty, it’s noisy and the people here are fucking miserable!” Paul Cain jammed his hands deep into his leather jacket pockets as he put his head back down so that his hair could cover the frustrated look on his face. Damien Kahn walked alongside him in a grey hooded coat and black sweatpants as they patrolled the streets of Brooklyn under the cover of darkness. He shot a look of impatience in the direction of Cain who blanked him in return. After all the traipsing around that they had done in Denver, Kahn felt it was about time that Paul followed HIS direction for a change. “Stop whining like a bitch, I’m well aware that Karina is your biggest match to date and that’s the whole point of us being here. Now stop wasting your energy in complaining and keep walking, we’re almost there now.” Paul grumbled something under his breath and begrudgingly accepted the instruction. They had completed their tour of Denver and exorcised the ghosts of Paul Cain’s past yet Damien had insisted that there was one thing left to do but wouldn’t say what. This had become even more confusing when he then produced two tickets to New York City, a place that Paul had only had a fleeting encounter with. All that he had remembered of it were the ‘hood-rats’, the gangsters and the amount of money it had cost him to take control of Damien’s services from the gang lord he was working for. When they flew out of New York, Paul had resolved he would never come back to such a place of ill repute, it wasn’t classy enough for him. Now though he found himself back in Damien Kahn’s past and he knew that he did not have time for this. “I just don’t see the point of this, I’ve never felt better thanks to the eradication of my so called mother, I’m feeling great so why on earth are you trying to ruin my mood by bringing me here?” As he said this, Paul found himself looking to the left and right of him more than he ever did during that Battle Royal a couple of weeks ago. He caught a glare off a young Mexican man with shaved hair and goatee stubble who held a look that could kill. Cain showed no fear and returned the expression only he actually meant it. Damien looked to his right and noticed this silent altercation that was going on. “Stay out of trouble, we don’t have time for extra curricular activity, we’ve got an appointment to keep.” The words from Damien were enough to snap Paul out of his aggressive distraction and reminded him that his question had gone unanswered. It felt like they had walked for miles as they rounded yet another street corner and Paul was getting tired of this very quickly. “You haven’t answered my question D-Money!” Damien then suddenly came to a halt and turned to face a run down abandoned building. He slowly pulled his hood back and exhaled loudly as he took in the sight before him. Folding his arms, he stood in silence as if he had seen something magical before him. Paul Cain was simply becoming more agitated and more confused by the moment. “We’re here.” Damien’s words were sombre and quietly spoken as Paul arrived by his side with a frown on his face. He was far from impressed and mused that at least he had taken Damien to some impressive locations such as a decent bar and a strip club too when they were ‘off duty’. Now the best he got was a building that looked condemned and probably should have been torn down a long time ago. “You brought me…HERE…for…THIS?!”Again, Damien ignored his impatient associate and walked over to the doorway of the building. This place was hardly secure as Damien easily pushed the door open which was loose on it’s hinges. Cain sighed deeply and followed with great reluctance as he imagined Karina Wolfenden working out, preparing herself stronger than ever in attempt to be the winner that took all. As he walked, he considered Karina working out in a whole different light for a moment. “Boy do I wish I could see THAT.” He smacked himself on the forehead to snap himself out of this fantasizing. Even he realised that if he became fixated by her obvious attractiveness, he may as well declare the match lost already and he was better off concentrating on the damp and dark building he had just stepped into. It was obvious from the amount of broken down doors in the hallway that this was an old apartment building, judging by the rubbish strewn around it now seemed to be a refuge for the homeless. A man like Cain could not be in a more alien environment than this. “Don’t tell me D-Money, you saw this place and you thought of my mother, right?” Damien stopped at the door which held the number ‘8’ on it. He looked down at the floor for a moment and then turned to Paul slowly with a sombre expression on his face. “This isn’t the time to make jokes, man. You think you’ve exorcised all your ghosts and you believe you are some invincible force now? There is one test you have not passed, one ghost you have not defeated and until you have done that, you are not ready to face Karina.” As Kahn turned back to face the apartment door, Paul felt the rage surging up through his body. If he had had a mirror, he would have seen his face go as close to bright red as humanly possible. He knew he was ready, PRIME knew he was ready, who was Damien to say that this was not the time? Paul had cast aside the demons of his ‘mother’ and managed to see his grandmother before her final days. He had even made peace with Jesus Christ himself in a crusade to rid the world of the evil of Imperium. What more did Paul Cain have to do to prove himself to those doubters? “WHO…THE FUCK…are you to tell me I’m…NOT…READY?!” Calmly, Damien opened the door to the apartment which like everywhere else in this dirt-ridden filth complex, was not exactly secure. He then spun around and grabbed Paul by the collar of his jacket and threw him into the apartment with brute force. Cain could be heard grunting as he fell hard onto the cold floor inside. Damien took hold of the door and went to slam it shut. “I’m the man that has the key to your success, that’s who! Now go meet your final demon!” Then the door crashed shut throwing Paul into darkness. ******************************************************************************************************************************************** “Well, well, well…Kahn wasn’t lying then, the worm really did come out of his hole.” Whilst the place was not recognisable, it took Cain literally no longer than a couple of seconds to recognise the voice of Claudia Lopez. He groaned under his breath as he realised now what Kahn meant by the final demon and the last test to pass. He was taken back to the memory of assaulting Claudia in the bar and causing her to miscarry their baby. He had not laid his hands on a woman since and in his own mind he wondered just how difficult it would be to do the same to Karina. Although he could not see her, knowing she was there sent a shiver up his spine for this was the worst demon of all. “Claudia…I…” She hushed him snappily and then the room was suddenly basked in light as she lit a candle and set it on the mantelpiece of the lounge they had both found themselves in. As he laid his eyes on for the first time in over two months, he noticed that she was back to her old self. She was power dressing with a sharp black suit jacket and pants with a white blouse underneath. Her hair was tied up and although he could not make out everything in the limited light, she had clearly gone to great lengths to get inside his head. “Take your filthy fucking eyes off me.” She could obviously feel his eyes on her and her tone was sharp and cold. More candles were lit and the room soon lit up exposing a largely empty space except for a couple of rickety chairs. Paul looked away from her as he felt uncontrollable fits of guilt every time he laid his eyes upon her. She turned around and folded her arms with a look of defiance and there was no doubt that she was carrying fire in her belly once again. “The man who usually has so much to say for himself…Is now standing like a fucking mannequin…Come on Paulie, speak up, I can’t hear you!” His head remained bowed as he glanced up at her and recognised the same feisty woman that he had first met yet she had never directed her full aggression at him. He used to be the one person that could quell those flames and the one man that could reduce Claudia to a submissive and subservient female. Now however she looked to be free of those mental shackles as she approached him and the viciously slapped him across the face. “You deserve a hell of a lot more than that! Come on Paul, let’s talk! I mean, where do we start, how about the time that you smashed that bottle over my head, kicked me in the stomach and caused my own flesh and blood to fucking DIE?!” Cain visibly flinched more from the words than the slap as he remembered that night with a shudder. His eyes were pleading and apologetic yet he struggled to find the right words and his mouth felt dry. “I…I was in a bad place Claudia…I wasn’t thinking straight…I wasn’t myself back then…Every night I wake up and feel sick about what I did to you…To your child…To OUR child…I let myself get obsessed with the wrong things as usual…I-I’m so sorry…” She scoffed at the attempt of apology and waved him away dismissively. It was quite apparent that a simple gesture of words would not be enough to calm her and to erase the memory. She flounced around the room for a moment as she thought and then spun around to verbally attack him again. “You’re so sorry are you? Paul, you don’t have a clue do you? I’m a LOPEZ female, please don’t make us out to be stupid because that’s the LAST thing we are. There was a time there where YES I did have feelings for you, but the majority of that time…I knew I could get whatever I wanted from you…Because of all the evil, sick things you did to my SISTER…I was your second chance wasn’t I, Paul?! You blew it though didn’t you, just like all the important things in your life, you ruin them and your chance for forgiveness was blown too!” Cain’s expression changed to one of confusion as the words came from her mouth. He had believed that there was genuine love there and only when the Dark Age attempted to put an end to his career, did disaster actually strike. Yet now it appeared that Claudia had been playing her own game too, one which ensured she got everything she wanted whilst appearing to be fulfilling the role of attentive and loyal female. How wrong he had been and now he began to see that Claudia was just like her sister, bitter and twisted. “But I thought…I thought everything was real…?”Her laughter was mocking and sinister as she poked fun at Paul’s beliefs on what had existed in the past. Suddenly she stopped and approached him again with a furious and cold look on her face. “You assault Maria Lopez and then expect her sister, me, to fall in love with you?! What kind of ‘B’ movie script are you working from, Paul?! I wasn’t in it for the love, I was in it to get everything I could out of you and it worked! You were a champion after all, you made me money and you got me the spotlight, modelling jobs, interviews, I loved every second of it! I had every intention of making as much out of you as I could because you fucking deseved it! My plan had been to drop you from the greatest height I possibly could, but then…Oh yeah…you really did hit the coup de gras didn’t you…You went and got me pregnant…Well fucking done Paul!” It was obvious that Paul couldn’t believe what he was hearing as he ran his hands through his hair with his eyes wide open. He was desperate for her to stop but she wouldn’t as she came closer and began to mix the soft whispering of venomous words with the fierce yell of a wronged party, into his ear. “You were a NOTHING in PRIME just like you always will be! It was obvious to me that you were running out of use in my life and I was ready to drop you like the bad habit you had become. Then you managed it Paul, you managed to do the one thing that would break me down! You gave me a kid and I weakened, a Lopez woman should never fucking weaken! I started to doubt myself and what I had done to you, you ruined everything Paul!” Paul had been staring straight ahead at all times as she had lectured but now he slowly turned to look her deeply in the eyes. “So it was all bullshit, just to get ‘revenge’ for your precious…CRAZY…sister?” She smiled at him in a patronising manner and ruffled the top of his hair without any comeback. “Aw how cute! You only just worked that out? You stupid egotistical bastard! But then hey what are you worried about, you took the only thing worth all this shit, away from me, so it’s not like you didn’t get your revenge, inadvertently anyhow!” He couldn’t take his eyes off of her as the coin began to drop in his mind. All the time that he had been consumed by guilt, this woman had been playing the ultimate con artist. It was funny how everything came back to a female, from his mother to Claudia to Karina Wolfenden. Would the later be just another example of why not to trust a woman? As the question ran around his head, it then dawned him again what Claudia had done. “I AM sorry about what I did…But…none of it was real…was it?” She shook her head at him with anger in her eyes. “The only thing that was real, was the amount that I wanted to hurt you and destroy you. Now that you took the one good thing away from me, I hope that you burn in hell…slowly!” He had spent so long feeling guilty but now he looked at her in a completely different light. She had lied to him and played with him for all this time and now the attack he had made on her felt almost deserved, justified. This was the final demon that he had to face and she had shown her true colours to him with her black heart. He could feel his own anger building again and he spat the words out to her. “You’re just like Madusa aren’t you, a sick and twisted creature of hatred. You’ve lost your power, bitch! You don’t have anything over me anymore and all because you showed me the real you, well done darling, you just blew your own plan! I’m sorry for what I did, but I’m no longer sorry for you, just for the unborn child that had done nothing wrong but be caught in a twisted situation.” He edged over to her and stroked her left cheek with his hand slowly. “When I drop Karina on her pretty little head, I’ll be thinking of you and wishing you were there. Fuck you bitch, you’ve lost your leash on me!” That same hand that had been gently stroking her pulled back and then slapped her across the face. She staggered back and he smiled in satisfaction at having finally broken her hold over him. The exorcism was now truly complete as there were no more ghosts to face and now he could look to the future without having to look over his shoulder. “I’ll save a spot for you in my victory speech…SWEETHEART.” He turned away from her and slowly made his way to the door as she screamed out to him. “FUCK YOU! YOU’LL GET WHAT YOU DESERVE! ONE DAY! ONE DAY YOU BASTARD!” As he opened the door, he looked back at her and shook his head. “What do I do to women to make them want to fight me all the time? Sheesh.” Paul walked out of the door and slammed it shut hard, leaving Claudia screaming at the top of her lungs just like he had left his mother. Another fine mess that had just been resolved. ******************************************************************************************************************************************** As Paul Cain appeared again, Damien was leaning against the dusty wall of the building with a cigarette in his mouth as per usual. He seemed rather ignorant of the fact that the whole building could easily go up in flames with his smoking. Still, that was typical of Damien keeping cool and calm and not overly concerning himself with things that didn’t need to be thought of. When Cain stood before him, Damien noted the smile on his face and realised the test must have gone well. “So…are ALL the demons exorcised now?” Paul raised an eyebrow at him and looked to the door of the apartment. “You set all this up didn’t you?” Damien idly flicked some ash onto the floor and brushed over it with his boot. “Let’s just say I found out before you, the truth behind Claudia Lopez and I thought you deserved to see it too. After all, I didn’t want you having any doubts about laying out a woman again, because if you had hesitations, Karina would finish you without thinking about it. NOW…I know you’re ready man…So let’s see about making her… Cain smiled and touched his fist against Damien’s as they spoke the final words. “OUR…BITCH!” With two big grins, they walked away from the scene with Claudia’s wailing still being heard in the background as the scene fades out to black.
