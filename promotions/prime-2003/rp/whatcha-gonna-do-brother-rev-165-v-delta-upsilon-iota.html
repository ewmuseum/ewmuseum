WHATCHA GONNA DO, BROTHER? (Rev 165 v. Delta Upsilon Iota)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: TIME HAS NO MEANING. We see Beef. This can't end well. BEEF: WELL, YOU KNOW SOMETHIN', MEAN GENE SNITSKY... The camera pans to the right, showing that Gene Snitsky is standing next to Beef. For some reason, this is the Snitsky of 2005, rather than the Snitsky of today. SNITSKY: It wasn't my fault. He leaves. Beef ignores the fact that he is gone. BEEF: As I look about this sea of Beefamaniacs, I realize that they demand the attention of the great, mighty Beef! With my ten and a half inch earthworms at my disposal, I could topple a tower... of styrofoam cups! Lift up... the largest pillow on my bed! And crack... a cube of ice with my forehead! Beef flexes. BEEF: Beefamania runs through these veins, brother! With the red and chartreuse runnin' wild all over my veins, I shall topple the styrofoam cups of Colby Korver! I shall lift the large pillow known as Hank Cobb! And then have a nice cup of tea with Dusk. He seems like a nice guy. Except for the part where he superkicks people for no reason and is mentally unstable. I mean, just the other day, I was talking to Devin Shakur, and he was like, "Yeah, Dusk is a stupid bastardhead". Hm. Maybe we're not talking about the same guy... well, anyway! I went to Devin Shakur, and I asked of him, "But isn't Dusk a swell guy!?". And then he looked at the Beefster for a very long time... and then he kicked me in the nuts! And then I fell over and clutched my nuts. Beef frowns. BEEF: That really hurt, you douche. Beef sighs and proceeds. BEEF: But as I laid there and clutched my nuts, I realized something transient. I realized that while I might be here, I'm actually not! And if I'm not actually here, why am I here? Then I realized that the whole exercise is pointless. We should just hang out with Devin Shakur all day and talk about rhyming stuff. Like how a cow hit me in the brow while riding a snow plow while I heard a cat's meow and I should really stop now. Flex. BEEF: But just when things looked their bleakest... I looked up, and I saw it! It was Beefamania! And a great ship made out of Beefamania was made! And thus, I, the scion of Beefamania, saved the world through doing nothing except look at an image I saw in the sky in one of my drug-induced hallucinations! Flex, again. BEEF: DUI, brother... uh... BROTHERS... uh... WHATEVER YOU GUYS ACTUALLY ARE! WHATCHA GONNA DO WHEN BEEFAMANIA RUNS WILD OVER YOU?! Fade.
