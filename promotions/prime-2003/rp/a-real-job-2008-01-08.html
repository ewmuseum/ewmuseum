A Real Job

8 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/08/08 INT. BASEMENT -- November 23rd, 2002 Flashback. Jack Harmen has already had an illustrious career wrestling on a national promotion in the Internet Wrestling Organization. However, in October, he'd recently been defeated by Simon Seaman in what was considered a retirement match. With the end of jOlt, the man known across the country as High Flyer was out of a job. Before he would head to Action! to wrestle Joey Malone, Coral Avalon's trainer, High Flyer spent most of his time in this basement. He would often find himself here during times on unemployment. It wasn't a particularly nice basement. Nor was it really grimy. It was just a regular concreted basement. Laundry machines off to the side, a bunch of piled up junk no one's used in years tossed in a corner... Of course, Tony Davis calls these mementos of the past 'buried treasure'. Jack Harmen didn't exactly sit around on a couch all day either. He wasn't a mooper. At this very moment, he was plugging in an amp that didn't go up to 11, but had the number scratched onto it's metal plating anyway. And no, Jack Harmen had never played a guitar before. STRUMMMMMM~! "Hey, that's pretty cool." STRUMMMMMM~! Tony Davis lifted two trash cans and placed them in the center of the room. He raised a hammer which had a small cloth wrapped around its tip. HIGH FLYER I didn't know you could play drums. TONY DAVIS I can't. HIGH FLYER I meant you being physically able to. With your shoulder. Are you allowed? TONY DAVIS I'm pretty sure doctors tell you what you can and can't do when you leave their care, so that you remember not to do it. (Pause) Or if they don't they should. HIGH FLYER I buy that. STRUMMM~!! RAT-TA-TATAT! "JACK!" Normally, that would have been the voice of the ever pleasant Kate Young-Harmen. Today, it was the voice of a frumpled, disheveled mess of a woman. But she had every right to be. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN DO YOU KNOW WHAT TIME IT IS! HiGH FLYER Sure. (Flyer points over his shoulder to a clock) It's 3. We're okay. We waited till now so we wouldn't wake the late risers. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN God. (Shakes her head in disbelief, quickens speech) This is why people don't live in basements. (pause, leans into Jack's face) THIS IS WHY PEOPLE DON'T LIVE IN BASEMENTS! (She sighs) Can you go back to work now? HIGH FLYER But you haven't heard that me and Tony are starting a band! TONY DAVIS Yeah! You don't know that we're in a band now. HIGH FLYER I think she knows NOW. I just told her! TONY DAVIS Well, my now was for a now a few moments ago. High Flyer gives Tony a glare. TONY DAVIS (sighs) Do we need to get into this every time I use the word now? KATE YOUNG-HARMEN It's three in the FUCKING MORNING! Kate exits back the way from which she came, slamming the door behind her. She yells loud enough to be heard through what High Flyer once believed to be a sound proof concrete bomb shelter. It certainly wasn't doing it's advertised job. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN Go to sleep! HIGH FLYER But I'm not in a sleeper hold! Tony Davis quickly locks on a sleeper; High Flyer passes out after just a bit of a struggle. Tony Davis wiggles, shaking frantically around. TONY DAVIS Oh god! Who's gonna put me to sleep now! Tony quickly looks around, and picks up a rope conveniently tied in a noose. TONY DAVIS This can work. INT. BASEMENT - March 23rd, 2003 High Flyer had recently become employed by Action! Wrestling, and asked for his release when Atlantic Wrestling Club picked up steam. As the World Champion, he felt his efforts should have been concentrated to the aWc promotion. However, after losing his championship, he went nuts and destroyed a production truck. This halted new tapings of this promotion until they could recover funds to return to action. And so, Flyer and Tony Davis once again find themselves returned to the basement. Kate Young-Harmen calmly walks through the basement door, carrying a load of laundry. Trying not to pay attention to her current husband, she loads her machine quietly. BANG! POP~! BANG~! Kate drops the laundry basket to the floor, and almost falls herself, avoiding a rocketing firework that ricocheted off the stone wall. Over in the corner, High Flyer and Tony Davis were lighting off fireworks, with Jack's Toddler son looking through his wooden old crib. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN Jack! You're putting Allocca in danger! HIGH FLYER He's two, he knows how to duck. TONY DAVIS Believe me; he's much safer in that makeshift prison than we are out here. I've got like, three burns on my thigh already-Wanna see?! Tony yanks at his jeans, not giving Kate a moment to answer. He lowers them slightly to show a burn on what could only be described as where the leg meets the crotch. Kate quickly threw her hands up and looked away, startled. HIGH FLYER Yeah, I mean, If I shoot a firework, and it starts rocketing toward a baby's crib, its' got a fifty fifty shot of hitting a bar instead. I've got to think that God makes babies lucky. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN You don't believe in God! HIGH FLYER Well, Satan can't corrupt pure innocence, can he?! He has to wait until we ripen! KATE YOUNG-HARMEN WOULD YOU PLEASE, just LOOK, for work! HIGH FLYER You never asked us how we paid for the fireworks. TONY DAVIS We sold our instruments! (Proudly) I sold my drums! HIGH FLYER Oh yeah, we need to buy new trash cans. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN Is that how you got this fifty inch television set I now see that we didn't have before? HIGH FLYER Oh no. I stole that. From a store. For its retail price. TONY DAVIS Wasn't so much a steal as it was a barter. But they took paper for it! What saps! INT. BASEMENT - DECEMBER 26th, 2003 As Christmas approaches, fWo released it's wrestler's to the comfort of their home for the holidays. They would routinely allow their employees time to recover before a three consecutive night show called Countdown which leads the promotion into the new year. High Flyer truly nests inside of that basement of the Harmen Household. At this point, Tony Davis' ears have what appear to be protected eye goggles covering them, and their fifty inch television has since been sold. Taking a swig of beer, Jack elmer's glues the final picture of his collage, that of Sarah Michelle Gellar holding a stake. He raises his artwork high to admire it. Tony attempts to look as well, but sighs as if he failed, even though it was only feet from his eyes. Kate Young-Harmen enters, once again carrying laundry. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN What are you idiots doing today instead of working? HIGH FLYER Well, I made a collage. FLYER holds up his collage of media icons. There isn't a single family photo included. TONY DAVIS I've been working on regaining my eye sight since the fifty inch TV made my eyes bleed. I figure I should just not use them for a while, but I can't use them even if I wanted to, so I don't know if I'm actually healing or just still in pain. It's like a roller coaster that you don't know has hit its peak yet or not. HIGH FLYER That's not what a roller coaster's like at all. TONY DAVIS Yeah dude, people say life's a roller coaster, and that you never know you're on a downswing until you're comin' up on an upswing! My eyes are so a roller coaster of pain! HIGH FLYER You need to crack a physics book. Or go to a theme park. Or watch video of someone else going to a theme park. Choose any of these options. TONY DAVIS Let's go to the theme park! HIGH FLYER Sorry honey, can't look for a job now, gotta show Tony physics at the theme park! And so quickly, Team VIAGRA departs, leaving Kate once again alone, sighing in frustration. INT. LIVING ROOM - DECEMBER 17TH, 1999 Having just about completed his first year inside of the Internet Wrestling Organization, High Flyer was celebrating his 24th birthday. He had received a gift from his wife that allowed him to enter a small business fair. Here, he would have been able to find his career. Only he found a career in selling snow. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN You're selling WHAT?! HIGH FLYER Snow. Y'know. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN Fly! You're selling drugs?! HIGH FLYER (Quickly) Why does everyone think that! No! I'm selling snow from the sky. You know, white pure heavenly bliss of fluffy flakes slowly floating through the air? KATE YOUNG-HARMEN All I hear is cocaine. HIGH FLYER No, see! I bought this awesome weather machine! And it's got a remote trigger! KATE YOUNG-HARMEN Why do you need a remote trigger? HIGH FLYER I send this puppy in the mail to Mexico, bam, they see snow and think the world's ending because, *laughs* It NEVER snows in MEXICO! I mean, it's MEXICO! So y'know, we go down there, say we'll make the devil's dandruff stop, and BAMO! We're King and Queen of Mexico! KATE YOUNG-HARMEN I... I want you to find a real job Jack. HIGH FLYER This is a real job. So's my gig with the IWO. EXT. WALKWAY PATIO -- DUSK -- PRESENT High Flyer rests against the wall of his home, leaning onto his foundation for support. He rolls his eyes as Kate stomps her heel in a flurry of anger. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN (sighs) I've said for years that you've been too smart to just be a professional rag doll for a living. You're a great man Jack. It's the reason I fell in love with you. But you can't even see that anymore. You hide, and you joke, and you put the blame on ANYONE within earshot! Craig Miles was RIGHT! Jack winces. He reaches into his pocket and pulls out a pack of cigarettes. Shaking, he lights the tip, and takes the first drag. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN I'm naming our daughter Young. Flyer promptly coughs out the first drag of the cigarette, fumbling with it so that it falls. Flyer catches it before it hits the ground, but burns himself in the process, and places the cigarette back in his lips. HIGH FLYER You're a b-... (he sighs, shoulders lower) What's her first name? KATE YOUNG-HARMEN Veronica. Jack can't help but let out a soft smile. He shakes his head and kicks himself off the wall of his home. HIGH FLYER I know I can't go forever Kate. And crap... every year you say that speech I'm one year closer to the end. And I know I gotta have a gameplan. Because our bodies are held together by duct tape and Elmer’s glue and we have the HMO of an African country at our disposal. Wrestling isn't as glamorous as it ever was in the past; it isn't as... engaging as it was to be honest Kate. But I still love this sport, and I know that when I'm 70, I'll look back to this moment, and ask myself Why I'm not in that ring, at least once a week, flying around the ring like only I can for as long as I can. KATE YOUNG-HAMREN Then WHAT?! HIGH FLYER What? I dunno. Commentate? Train other's in my footsteps? Run my own promotion? KATE YOUNG-HARMEN Why Jack! Why! You're still putting your body on the line for a community that DESTROYS it's stars. And you STILL want to be apart of this world?! I mean, just look at you! HIGH FLYER Hey! I'm still young and sexy! KATE YOUNG-HARMEN You're not as young as you should be. Kate folds her arms, and there's an awkward silence. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN You really think you're going to live to seventy? HIGH FLYER Yeah... why? Have you heard something different? KATE YOUNG-HARMEN Nothing direct. HIGH FLYER That's a relief. Another awkward pause. Flyer shuffles in his place. HIGH FLYER Tony and I are going to Los Angeles. We're going to start our PRIME careers. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN Have fun. HIGH FLYER I'm not just doing this for myself! KATE YOUNG-HARMEN That's all you ever do things for! HIGH FLYER NAH-UH! Allocca wants to see me on TV more than ANYTHING, EVERY week. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN He'd rather see you in real life everyday than once a week on the boob tube. HIGH FLYER Have you asked him that? Because he thinks daddy smells. KATE YOUNG-HARMEN It's because you're smoking like a chimney! I thought you stopped fifteen years ago! HIGH FLYER Yeah, well. I'm not taking this whole sepera- KATE YOUNG Divorce. HIGH FLYER Right, Divorce. It's like bad peach cobbler in my stomach or too much whisky or tequila after drinking beer. I could go on. KATE YOUNG Please don't. HIGH FLYER See, I don't get that. When we were married, you'd let me go off, and you found me hilarious. KATE YOUNG Well, I was drunk. HIGH FLYER For all of it? KATE YOUNG Most of it. HIGH FLYER Even when pregnant? KATE YOUNG You just want to see me arrested for harming a fetus, don't you? HIGH FLYER Not especially. No. In fact, on my list of things that I wish would happen to you, that's all the way at number 948 between arm chewed off by a weasel and squashed to death by an extraterrestrial who would then revive you in a slightly mutated and zombie looking life form. Whe- KATE YOUNG If you're leaving us, you should just go. Flyer stops his story, and takes a deep sigh. He nods his head, and turns away, leaving off into the shadows of what is now night. Kate sighs, and lowers her head. A small tear falls down her right cheek, the one closest to the camera. In the distance, we see a shadowed figure stop, and turn, before shouting. HIGH FLYER You know, Number one on that list is us together again! Kate laughs, and Flyer walks completely off into the distance.
