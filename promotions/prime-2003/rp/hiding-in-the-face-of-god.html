Hiding in the Face of God

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Revolution 194 Knock. Knock. Knock. \"Come in!\" David shouted as he grunted through another sit-up, his face drenched in sweat. His eyes glanced over to the girl who knelt beside him with an eager look on her face. \"MacKenzie, keep counting.\" She nodded her head as David pushed through another sit-up and the door to his room opened up. In the threshold of the door stood Samantha with a worrisome look on her face. \"Its family meeting time, David,\" she informed him to which he responded with a slight nod of his head as he completed another sit-up and felt the burning sensation in his abdomen. \"Are you coming?\" \"In a few minutes, just a little busy here if you didn\'t notice,\" he responded with irritation in his voice. MacKenzie looked up at her oldest sister and grinned at her. \"How many is he up to \'Kenzie?\" Samantha asked her and she thought for a moment. \"Like, ten.\" David looked over at his sister with a furrowed look on his face. \"Hey!\" he yelled at her in a joking manner. She giggled as MacKenzie looked back at Samantha while David finished another sit-up. \"He\'s got like 277 done or something in that area.\" \"You know,\" David began as he struggled through another one as he felt his body as it resisted in protest. \"I don\'t know what I\'m buying you that new bicycle for if you\'re not even going to do a good job at count--ahhh!\" he grunted once again as he somehow kept going with his exercise. Samantha then stepped into the room before she kicked David in the side of his ribs. “Let’s go,” she said and it was obvious to David that Samantha was not in the best of moods at this moment. He brushed it aside though as he shook his head and continued with his work-out plan. “David, your phone is ringing,” MacKenzie told him and David paused in the middle of a sit-up to see who it was calling him. On the caller ID screen it read Mary-Lynn Mayweather. David paused for a brief moment as the two girls in his room looked at him for what felt like an eternity. David tapped ignore. “Got a new girlfriend?” MacKenzie asked as David placed the phone down, and she giggled throughout her entire question. David shot her a menacing look before he went and completed his previous sit-up. “No.” Samantha sighed as she watched David do three more sit-ups before she lost her cool. “I thought the purpose of us setting a time for this meeting was so that you’d be there for it,” she grilled him and David rolled his eyes. “Look, can you give me a few more minutes. I need to get to three hundred, which might take a while if Mac doesn’t do her job and count them,” David responded in kind. “You’re at 295! I remember now!” David chuckled as he completed another one and felt the glare from his sister. A moment later she left without another word. MacKenzie stood up as she made a mental note of 297. “She didn’t seem too happy, did she?” she asked him. “As of late, no. But, don’t you worry your pretty little mind about it, MacKenzie,” he told her and she nodded her head as he completed number 298. “So, was that your girlfriend?” David paused for a minute as he tried his best to define what exactly Mary-Lynn was to him. “No. She’s just a friend. My partner in the ring.” MacKenzie seemed to understand that without needing David to go into further detail. “Do you have a boyfriend, Mac?” he asked her and she moved her foot around just a little bit with a tepidness to her. As he finished number 300, he sat up and looked at her. “Well?” “No.” David smiled at her as he rose to his feet and tapped MacKenzie on the head before David kissed her on the top of the head. “Good, keep it that way, alright?” She nodded her head in agreement as David grabbed a nearby towel and wiped the sweat off of his face. “Alright, you stay in here. Let the grown-ups talk while I try not to fall asleep.” MacKenzie remained silent though as David opened the door and. As MacKenzie watched him walk away, anxiety filled her chest. Just like every other time she had watched him walk away over the past few days. *** The tension in the room was undeniable. There was blame to be had and anger to be dealt, but somehow all of the pressure had remained contained over these couple of days. David walked into the room, no shirt on, and sat on the floor in the corner as he looked up at Samantha and nodded his head. “Let’s do this.” Andrea, Sarah, and Rebecca looked over David, unaware of his late arrival, and smiled at him. Andrea looked over at David. “Thanks for showing up,” Andrea voiced her discontent with David. “Whoa, whoa,” David began as he held his hands up high. “I’m pretty sure that I didn’t sign up for my second boxing match with Andrea. So, if this is all that it’s going to be--,” but before David could finish he was cut off by Rebecca who had a rather impatient look in her eyes. “David, relax. Now. I don’t think Andrea came all this way to have to deal with your attitude and resentment of her. I’m certain she had to take valuable time away from her job at the law firm to just deal with this, so let her say what she wants to say,” Rebecca informed David who sighed before his head dropped towards the ground as Andrea blushed. “Mom, I don’t think that was all necessary. I just think we need to talk about things, because in a day or so David, you’re going to be heading back to Los Angeles. We don’t even know if we’re going to see or hear from you again, and I think all of this has to be resolved. It didn’t get the chance to be resolved when you first came back because of what happened to Dad,” Andrea began as David’s eyes met her’s. Samantha moved to the edge of the couch and looked over at David. “I think we deserve an honest explanation of why you left, David,” she told him and David looked around the room hoping that someone would come to his defense. “Samantha, I can’t honestly tell if you’re happy that I’m back or that you think I can offer you closure on something. I left for my reasons and my reasons only,” David shot back, a hint of venom had slipped into his voice. Andrea looked over at Samantha and it was obvious that she was trying to get her to hold back. “Okay, fair enough,” she said and Samantha shot back with a look of contempt. “Now, we need to discuss that you took Mom with you to Los Angeles and kept her away from us for months,” Andrea started to bring up until the matriarch rose her hand. “That was all my doing, Andrea, and I think we don’t need to harp on David about that either--,” Rebecca began before David cut her off. “I’m sorry. I truly am. I should’ve relented a little bit and brought you guys out to see her. I think that would’ve been best and we wouldn’t have this riff that we do now amongst everyone.” The words even surprised David even though he said them and everyone looked around the room. It was obvious that he was sincere in his message and everyone simply accepted it even if they still held some resentment for the move. Andrea sat on the edge of the table and looked at everyone. “I’ve been talking to Mom and just discussing the changes this family has seen recently. Dad passing away, Mom not being here, and David getting some rather lucrative jobs as of late. Dad, he left us a lot of money, but we haven’t been able to go through the will yet because of everything that’s been happening. But, something we threw into the air, with some hesitation though, is that we move out to Los Angeles. To be with David,” she suggested and a face of shock was written all over David’s face. He began to open his mouth, but quickly shut it. “I think it’s a good idea.” David’s eyes quickly snapped over to the person who had voiced their opinion and found Sarah sitting there. “Sarah? What about school? Life? I mean, you want to give everything up just like that?” David asked her and she nodded her head. “We’re a family. Yet, we haven’t been in such a long time. School and childhood friends, those are things that can be replaced. Family can’t, and we have to start fixing that before it’s too late,” Sarah elaborated and David couldn’t find the words to argue with her. He looked over at Samantha who nodded her head in agreement. “Well,” David said as he sat there. He knew that when he came home that everything would change. He never imagined it would be like this though *** A small clock in the kitchen startled David as it went off due to it being the top of a new hour. David glanced at the clock and could barely read the time through his blurry eyes. He cursed under his breath as he clumsily grabbed the glass on the edge of the table and finished off the contents. As he went to place it back on the table, he missed his spot, and the glass crashed onto the tile below and splintered across the floor. “Fuck,” David mumbled as he bent over to grab the shards of glass, but fell off of his chair instead and slammed both of his knees into the tile. He took a deep breath in as the pain and embarrassment of falling out of the chair irked him. He picked up a few pieces of the glass before another light flickered on. “What the hell is going on in here?” Rebecca whispered violently as she looked at David who could barely keep his head straight on his shoulders. David’s eyes grew narrow as he looked over at his Mom who hurried over to where he was. “David, why is there glass all over the ground?” David looked around and picked a few of the pieces up before he tried to get back up to his feet. “Fell,” he responded simply and threw the glass into the trashcan. Rebecca walked over to the refrigerator and found a broom and dustpan there as she began to sweep up the broken glass. Instead of helping his mother clean up the glass, David pulled another glass out of the cabinet and poured himself another glass of Bourbon. “Jesus,” his mother said as she watched the nearly full glass turn empty in the blink of an eye. David though never even noticed it as he began to pour himself another glass. “David!” Rebecca scorned him as she dropped the broom and yanked the glass from David’s hand who grabbed it back from her. “Just leave me alone,” he said as he slurred his words and continued to pour his glass of alcohol. Rebecca only looked at her son with disgust. “Damnit, David, you’re drunk off your ass, aren’t you?” she questioned him and David took a long look at her. “Not even going to lie to you, Mom,” he began to say before he took another sip of his Bourbon. As he felt it swim down his throat he looked back at her with a puzzled look on his face. “What was I saying?” Rebecca sighed before she grabbed the glass and discarded the contents down the sink. David looked like he was ready to fight with his mother, but decided against it as he sat at the table with a look of anger on his face. “I can’t believe you, David,” she said and David just shook his head. “Mom, trust me, it’s nothing.” Rebecca scoffed at the notion. “Like hell it is. What if MacKenzie saw you like this? Huh?!” she yelled back at him, but her attempt at shaming him didn’t work in the least bit. “Whatever.” Rebecca took a long look at her son and just shook her head. “You know, since I’ve gotten back I’ve had time to do a lot of thinking. Especially about how you guilted and tricked me into going into that clinic. Told me it would be best for me. Ever since then, I’ve been protecting you from your sisters about it, because they could just tell that I was lying. No more now though, David. You’re fighting your own battles,” Rebecca dismissed him in a fit of anger. Deep down, Rebecca regretted what she had told him. “Mom,” he began, but she held his hand up to him. “I didn’t want to tell you this David, but do you remember when you asked me if I was mad at you about what happened? At the time, I told you I wasn’t. I lied. I hate you for taking me from my family the way that you did. I hate you for what you did to Shawn. But, your sisters, they revere you. Trust me, I don’t. I hate you,” she replied in a biting tone. David could only watch as she walked out of the kitchen and away from his point of view. His eyes shifted ever so slowly to the table and he felt the words hit him in the face like a ten ton truck. “Thanks Mom, thanks a lot.” *** “Hey, you got a moment?” The voice of Andrea cut through the daydreaming mind of David. He looked over at his sister with a hesitance as a litany of bags were packed and placed right by the front door of the Noble house. In less than an hour, they would pack everything into a van and drive to the airport to begin the rest of their lives. MacKenzie had cried, but knew it was for the best, especially after her sisters told her of all the fun she would have when she lived there. “What’s up, Andrea?” he said through an exacerbated breath. He was in no mood to talk as the pounding sensation in his skull mixed with the venomous words his mother spoke just hours beforehand had put him in a rather annoyed mood. “I was thinking,” she began as she looked at the ground with a certain hesitance to her. “You’re starting to make some pretty good headway into the business right now. But, I’ve been talking to Samantha and she tells me that you don’t have an agent, manager, or even a lawyer to look out for you.” David nodded his head in agreement. “What’s your point?” “What if I filled those slots for you?” she asked him and it confused David for a minute who slowly began to shake his head. “Wait, I thought you had a job. In Europe. Making a massive amount of money,” he began to riddle off the top of his head the various things that had been thrown in his face the past few days as it pertained to his younger sister. Andrea began to nod her with apprehension behind it all. “I do, well,” she caught herself. “I did. I got fired about a week ago. Downsizing.” David sat there, rather shocked by the bombshell that Andrea had just laid on him. “So, I’m going to assume that Mom knows nothing about this. Or Samantha for that matter,” he said and she nodded her head in agreement. A slight smile appeared on David’s face. “I fail to see how this is in the least bit funny.” Andrea shot him a dirty look and David immediately backpedaled. “Sorry,” he told her and she shrugged her shoulders. “Look, if you need a job, why work with me? I mean, I know you love me and all, but we get on each other’s nerves on an hourly basis. How would this even work?” Andrea squirmed for a minute. She desperately needed the job. “I’ll make it work. Just, please David, I need your help here,” she pleaded with him and David took a minute to think about it. At the end of the day though, it was family and he couldn’t let her down. “Fine, you got it. I just made a deal with the devil, but you got the job.” She screamed at the top of her lungs before she wrapped her arms around David’s neck. The two hugged each other for the first time in what felt like a lifetime. As they broke it off, Andrea gave David a kiss on the cheek. “Thanks,” she told him and he thought nothing of it. Then, his phone rang again and he looked at it. It was Mary-Lynn. “Answer it, Craig. I’m your manager, and you should do it,” she told him. David looked at his sister and contemplated firing her right then and there. He stood up and walked outside of the house with a pensive look on his face as he accepted her call.
