April Fools

1 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 1, 2008 Thank God the gym in his hotel had a heavy bag. Once in a while, Troy needed to do something other than tick off the miles on the treadmill. Sometimes, the stress of it all meant you couldn't just wait for the cameras to turn on and the bell to ring before you just needed to hit something. So, iPod blasting music in his ears, Troy Douglas took out his frustrations on the hanging bag, battering it with every available appendage. Hey, he had a lot of frustration. To be more specific, he had a lot of nerves. For a guy who had been in the wrestling business for eight years and had just beaten a Hall of Famer in his PRIME debut, Troy knew that he had been thrown headfirst into the lion's den when it came to his second straight appearance on ReVolution. As soon as he'd gotten the call and was told who he'd be in the ring with, Troy immediately bolted out of his room, headed to the elevator and ridden the six floors down to the gym. Not one man. Not two. Three of the best that PRIME had to offer, all standing on opposite corners of the ring from him. He needed to hit something hard enough that he knew he could pull it off when the time to go came around. The names, faces and credentials of his opponents, they flashed in his mind as he unloaded on the bag. Easton Hall. A man on a monster roll, seemingly on a collision course with Tyler Rayne for the 5-Star Title down the road. BOOM! He connected with a left jab and followed with an overhand right. Tony Gamble. The former Intense champ, and a guy desperate to regain his stature after being unseated at Culture Shock. THWACK! A hard roundhouse kick with his right leg sent ripples of dust flying off the canvas exterior of the bag. Dusk. The brand new Intense champion, and the man who just happened to end Troy's big to upset the Dual Halo. BANG! He grabbed the bag and drilled it with a flying knee so hard that the bag nearly broke loose from the chains keeping it in place. Lucky for him, the gym was empty, or else he was sure the noise he was making would attract a good deal of attention. The third-floor gym at the Loews Oklahoma City wasn't normally the home of a lot of high impact training. Eh. First time for everything. "Welcome back my friends to the show that never ends. We're so glad you could attend, come inside, come in--" Click. He didn't have anything against Emerson, Lake and Palmer's "Karn Evil 9", but he had to go get ready for another show that was about to start. And it was guaranteed to blow your head apart, too. ******* The tights hung in the same spot he'd hung them in every closet of every hotel that he'd ever been to. Third hanger from the left, next to his leather jacket. Black boots sitting directly underneath them, black kick pads outlined with white laying right next to those. Superstition? Maybe. Routine? Definitely. He guessed he probably got into that kind of regimented lifestyle because of football. Every locker he'd ever put his gear into, from junior high all the way to the NFL, was set up the exact same way. The helment had to be facing the same direction. The jersey was always hung on the exact same peg, each set of pads had their own designated spot, and the shoes sat right at the bottom, toes pointing out. It was probably Troy's way of coping with the randomness of the world of professional sports. With so much damn uncertainty awaiting him, he always needed something to be consistent, to be sure. He needed an anchor to keep his head from wandering off his goal. That's why it pissed him off so damn much the day someone decided it would be a fun little joke to go and rearrange things on him. Four years ago, the locker room of Empire Pro Wrestling was a hell of a place to be a part of. The rising stars of the wrestling world, and Troy was right there among them. And his meticulous nature with his locker had become something of a running joke. He wasn't sure who had done it, but when he got back from the catering area that day in Minneapolis, everything was out of order. One of his boots was sitting on top of his locker, the other had been indiscriminately placed on a shelf on the opposite side of the room. His tights, which had been cleaned and pressed earlier in the day, lay in a heap in front of his locker, the wrinkles settling in by the moment. And his jacket. Where was the jacket? He'd taken a lot of flak for carrying a heavy, tan leather jacket with him no matter the weather, but it was a constant part of his wardrobe nonetheless. That jacket was the one tie to his late father that he brought on the road with him. It was an 18th birthday present, and that jacket had been along for the ride on some of the biggest moments of his life. And now, it was missing. Troy didn't normally get angry, but this one pulled the fucking trigger. What had he said, exactly? Oh, yeah. "Goddamnit! If someone doesn't tell me where my fucking jacket is in five fucking seconds, I'm going to turn everybody's insides into their motherfucking outsides!" Wow. Looking back, that was probably a little harsh. Then, he looked at his cell phone and saw the date, and his apoplexy turned to jags of laughter. April Fucking First. From the hallway, he heard a group of several people absolutely howling with laughter as they walked in, carrying his jacket like it was a prized trophy, their voices echoing with a constant chorus of those two annoying little words. "APRIL FOOLS!!!!" "I am going to fucking KILL each and every one of you." Good times. Sometimes, they seemed to have happened a thousand years ago. But, as he saw the date on the small calendar on his bedside table, memories like those kept flooding back like they'd only happened the day before. April First. Sometimes, things just moved in circles, didn't they? ReVolution 155 wasn't for another day. But, that didn't mean he couldn't play his own little April Fools joke on national TV, did it? After all, a day late only meant it would be a day more unexpected. And, he wouldn't be moving anybody's jacket or putting live maggots in somebody's gear bag. He'd just take one more shocking step up PRIME's ladder of success. So what if he'd been saddled with facing three men with a far higher PRIME profile than him? It hadn't stopped him before. Troy chuckled and slid onto his bed, ready to grab the phone and order up some room service. But, just before he picked up the phone, "Karn Evil 9" popped into his head one more time. "Come inside, you'll get your money's worth. Greatest show, in heaven or on Earth." Gotta see the show, kids. If he had his way, there wouldn't be anything better. April Fools.
