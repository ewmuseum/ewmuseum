Date: ReVolution 152 The words hit like a sack of bricks. Not one of those light sacks of bricks, but like a heavy duty sack of bricks. Like the mother of all sack of bricks. You know that sack of bricks that falls on Wily E. Coyote. That one. Got it? Good. "Now, I want to set up an appointment in the next couple of days with a specialist," Warren begins to say, but the words never quite hit Craig fully. Instead, he's in a stupor as he contemplates what all of this means to him and to his career. "I-I-I'm sorry." The words just stumble out of Craig's mouth, still in awe and shock. Warren looks up at Craig with troublesome eyes and notices that he's moving a little fast for Craig. "No, I'm sorry. I didn't anticipate your reaction and that's my fault. I know this is a lot to take in." Craig shakes his head as he tries to figure everything out. None of it makes sense. He lowers his head as he tries desperately to clear his mind, but it's not working. He just wants to punch something in the worst way. All of his life, he'd worked and worked, stayed in good health, and kept himself clean only for this to blindside him. He'd been able to beat everyone in his way, and this time, he wouldn't. There was no opponent in this life except for him. "How bad is it?" Craig's eyes look up at the doctor who calmly sits back in his plush leather chair. His hands fold over each other and he places them underneath his chin as he contemplates his word. Craig looks at him skillfully, trying to read his mind, but the doctor is extremely guarded. "That's why you need to see a specialist. I don't generally handle MS cases. It's just a very risky field that has so many vagaries in it. It's extremely difficult to say." "I know I should be concerned with my health, but my career is my life--" Before Craig can even finish his statement, the doctor holds up his left and inches closer to Craig. "You need to retire." "No. No. No. I refuse!" The anger just bursts out of Craig as he can't believe someone is recommending that he leave the one thing that's always been there for him. A veteran for over fifteen years now, Craig is at the prime of his career and doesn't see any sign of slowing down ahead of him. He can't leave. Not now. "You need to think about your wellbeing at this point. Constant fatigue, your joints aching, nausea, burning sensations all across your body, these are all things that will happen. Not maybe, it is. You could suffer from paralysis at times, the inability to walk or control your motor functions. Competing in the ring is only going to exacerbate those issues." "There has to be another way. I can't walk away, I just can't." His voice trembles as he sees the reality staring him right in the face. "Ultimately, the decision is up to you. That's why I need you to see this specialist so he can do some further tests. If you want, I can find someone who'll be discreet on this sub--" "No, you're handling this. This can't get out. Not yet. Not ever." Warren nods his head as he knew this would come. He silently gives up the fight of trying to make Craig see a specialist and starts jotting down a few notes on what he should do next with Craig. He begins scribbling a variety of tests so he can see the extent of the damage. "Okay, then you need to resign yourself to the fact that you're going to need to be in town a little bit more. You need to slow down your schedule so you can at least take a few tests that're vital to this. If you want me to continue on this case, you're going to need to take the medications I tell you and follow my instructions exactly. I'm jeopardizing my career for you at this point." "Thanks." "It's no problem. Craig, I need you to seriously consider the retirement option. I know it hurts to even think like that, but if you don't, I don't know how long you're going to have to live. Don't you have anybody in your life that you care about more than this?" Craig doesn't answer. Instead, he stands up and extends his arm. Warren quickly takes it and then Craig begins to walk out of the door. "Call me Doc, when you're ready to start these tests." His hand grips the doorknob and pulls the door open. As he walks out, it's almost as if the world is a different hue to his eyes. It all seems so much darker now. ****~~**** "H to the izz-O, V to the izz-A. Fo' shizzle my nizzle used to dribble down in VA!" The words of Jay-Z blasts out of the windows as Craig races down highway 420 with the windows down and the wind racing through his hair as the top on his convertible is down. He moves in and out of the different lanes as he expertly dodges the slower drivers on the road. This dark sense of foreboding just washes over him and it's a feeling that he can't quite shake. It's almost like a staring match with Death, and Death was just starting to win. Yet, Craig remained determined to continue to fight. "Brrrrrring!" Craig glances over to his cell phone and notices that Katie is calling him. He thinks about it for a second, but throws the phone into the backseat and continues to race down the street. As he reaches the end of the highway, he veers off on one of the exits. He then shoots down another side street and pulls his car off to the side of the road. The click of a hinge is heard as he opens the door and the crackle of the gravel echoes throughout the empty alley way. Each step feels like it's last as Craig walks aimlessly and wreckless. His right hand is curled into a ball as he keeps walking, a plan in his mind, but one that's still a secret to him. As he turns the corner, a bridge stands before him, overlooking a large lake. His jaw tightens as he walks up to the edge of the bridge and grabs the railing above him. He pulls himself up and stands on the edge of it, on the edge of death. His eyes scan the field before him and he knows that this is his battlefield. Craig then looks up at the sky and can feel the anger that seers his blood. "You want to test me?! YOU WANT TO TEST ME?!" No answer. He didn't expect one. "You want to see me suffer? Want to see me crumble before your might?! You lost the war and you want to take it out on me? Well, you want to know what I think about that?" Both of his hands shoot straight up as he gives the sky the one finger salute. Anger is replaced by fury. "That's right, FUCK YOU! I don't play your games, not anymore! So test me, you dirty fucker. Because, I'm not playing it. I'm ending these sick games right now and there's not a damn thing you can do about it, is there? Nothing!" He yells at the top of his lungs and wonders in the back of his mind if he's even listening. "Stop me if you can, but I'm going to the hell that you've already lost me to. You can't stop me!" He then closes his piercing red eyes and then drops his head. He's resigned himself to this much. If they wanted to take away the things he loved, then he would end the games right then and there. His legs feel eager, ready to get it over with. His mind begins to play the scene out in his mind, hitting the unforgiving water, the water filling his lungs, his lungs expanding past the point of healthy, and exploding into nothingness. The pain would be unbearable, but he would block it out like he's done with pain all of his life. This would be it. It was time to end the silly games. "Excuse me? Are you alright?" The voice cuts through his thoughts like a katana. He turns ever so slightly, wondering who it is that's behind. A fourteen year old kid just stands there, dumbfounded. Craig wonders where the kid came from and then looks up at the sky. "You play dirty." Craig looks back at the lanky, short haired boy, and hops off the railing. "Hey." "You're--!" "Yeah, I am. What're you doing around here?" "Just going home, sir." "Alright then, get on home. Take it easy." The kid though is dumbfounded as Craig walks past him and back to his convertible. The tears well up behind his eyes, but he keeps them there. No more weakness. If he wanted a fight, he would get a fight. He sticks the key into the ignition and hears the engine come to life. Just like his life. ****~~**** "You thought about committing suicide?" "Yeah." "Why?" Craig sits back in the chair, Silvia glaring at him with some intense interest. "How can this get any better? It can't, right? Haven't I effectively lost?" Silvia writes something on her legal pad, before moving a few strands of her brown hair out of her face. "Is that what you think? If anything, you've got a chance to start your life fresh. Find out the answers that've been haunting you for so many years, accomplish your goals with a renewed vigor. You're going to live each day from here on out not knowing if it'll be your last one or if tomorrow you'll never be able to walk again. I don't think you should retire. Instead, I think you should fight harder and keep pushing yourself. You're not the kind of person to play it safe. You live life recklessly. It's why you compete like you do, throwing all caution in the wind. It's what has made you the, so called, Icon of PRIME. Why change that?" Craig swings his head from left to right, letting her words just settle in. His left hand grips the wooden chair hard and he can feel a small part of it just crumble in his hands. He knows that she speaks the truth. It doesn't really surprise him. Instead, he starts mapping things out in his head. His red eyes look at her, a million questions lingering in the balance. "Don't I have an obligation to PRIME? To the fans?" "You do. You have an obligation to the fans to perform each and every night. That's very true. But, you're going to continue to do that regardless. To PRIME? Yeah, you really do. But, you have to weigh that in your mind. The right thing to do would be to tell them. But, if you do that, they'll put you on the shelf. So, you have to balance that out for yourself. But, this is your life, why should they slow you down?" "Yeah." "I want you to do something, Craig." "What?" "Make a list of things you want to accomplish before you die, and bring it back here. We'll go over those things." Craig nods his head before he gets up out of his chair and begins to walk to the door, another session over with. As he reaches the door though, she stops him. "I have one question for you, Craig." He looks at her, a puzzled look on her face. "Why come here? You hate this place? Why here?" Craig's tongue runs over his lips softly, the answer staring him dead in the face. "I'm tired of running away." Silvia nods her head, approving of this answer. She writes something else down on the legal pad. "In that case, if you're really done running away from things, the first thing on that list should be to find your parents." He remains silent. Craig turns around, grabs the door, and opens it. The world seemed a little darker to him. ****~~**** Twenty minutes. That's how long Craig's been sitting in the driveway of his home, unable to get out of his car. As the seconds roll past him, his mind works furiously to figure out what to tell Katie. Can he tell her? He already knows the answer to that one. But, it worries him more than anything to tell her and know she'll change on him in a heartbeat. He wants to keep this one close and guarded to him, but the last time he did that, she left. Could he survive that again? That's one question he doesn't know the answer to. His hand sits tensely on the door handle, trying feverishly to decide on something. As he pulls the door handle, he knows the answer. The door swings open and he steps out onto the paved driveway. His neck begins to tense up as he takes a step closer to his house. His own house. He decides to brave it and begins to walk to the door. As he reaches the door, it opens and Katie's standing there. "Hey." She looks at him, scared. In the back of his mind, she wonders if she should tell Craig what she knows. Instead, she decides to wait until he tells her, wondering if he ever will. For Craig, his insides begin to churn, his emotions tearing at him, ripping him apart. Warmness comes over him, making him feel safe and confident. The thought of having MS leaves his thoughts for the first time in hours. "Hey." A smile comes over him and it relaxes her slightly. "What's going on?" Just the mere sight of her has calmed him down as well, as if he knows everything's going to be alright." "Nothing. Just ready to start life over?" Katie looks puzzled. "Yeah?" "Yeah." He then steps inside and shuts the door closed. The world seemed to be a little brighter now. To Be Continued
