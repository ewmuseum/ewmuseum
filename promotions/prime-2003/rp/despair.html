Despair

1 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/1/07, the night after Rev 118 Despair. If a painting could be taken right now of this scene, it would be entitled Despair. The not so tastefully cheery hotel room provides a stark contrast to it's occupant. The pale blue walls, cheerfully canary yellow sheets, and vase of brightly colored artificial flowers only draw more attention to the sheer misery of the man sprawling on the floor, his back against the wall and his legs akimbo. A haze of smoke rises from the lit cigarette between his lips, the overflowing ashtray and empty pack next to him attesting to the length of time he'd been in that same position. He stares with open longing at the minibar across the room, it's closed door taunting him with its highly overpriced and undersized bottles of fermented panacea. He takes a long drag of his cigarette and suddenly clutches at his head as surges of agony pound against his head like waves crashing against the reef. His jaw clenches as he rocks back and forth, riding out this latest attack. It finally fades to a low throb, and the Trashcan Man sighs in relief. Wiping away the sweat on his brow, he stubs out the cigarrete, ignoring the embers that drift to the salmon colored carpet, leaving a few more singed spots. With a practiced flick of his burn scarred hand, he lights his battered silver Zippo and lights another, leaning back as he takes a deep drag, holding the smoke in his lungs for a good thirty seconds before finally expelling it in a long breath. It's getting worse. Strangely, the mental voice of Mr Smiley Face is muted, lacking it's usual grating tinge of hysteria and completely unaccompanied by the normal bout of high pitched cackling. Instead, an unaccustomed somberness belays the Smilin' Dude's fears. The Trashcan Man nods and takes another drag before answering out loud. "Never was like this before, Smiley. The attacks... they're so erratic. It's not as..." He falters to a halt as he searches for the word. Rhythmic? "That's it. The pain was far worse towards the end last time, but you knew when it was coming. These random blasts... they're scaring the hell out of me. But not as much as what happened last night does." Trash... that wasn't you... "But it almost was, Smiley. Just for a moment... for one second it was me. When Lazarus had my lighter in front of Karina... he let me out for just a second... and I almost... I almost..." But you didn't. You pulled it back, Trash. "It's the chip, Smiley. That taser blast Karina hit us with activated the chip. It's getting harder to hold on... Not only to my mind... but my humanity. Laz... he almost... he MERGED with me... The chip's misfiring and Lazarus is bleeding into me... Don't worry, Trash. Kremmy called and said... well, he just said "KREMMY!" but you know what he means. Trashy is forced to chuckle breifly as fond memories of one of his only friends come to him. Kremmy was a genius trapped in a crippled, hydrocephalic body. The accumulation of cerebrospinal fluid had enlarged his skull and permanently damaged both his motor skills to the point of his usual confinement to a wheelchair as well as the speech center of his brain so that he could only say his name and a few unintelligible grunts that apparently only the Trashcan Man could interpret. Kremmy had somehow turned his disability into an asset, forming a world famous grunge rock band named Kremmy And The Lords Of The Underworld. Shouting out "KREMMY" at the top of his lungs during breaks in the bands verses, he had become a rock and roll icon, beloved by millions worldwide. On the way he had become PCW's first FSW (For Short Wrestlers) Champion, somehow coming out of his wheelchair and taking out Ministreak with his Kremmy's Special DVD to win the Midget Brawl For All. "Remember the look on Jeff Marx's face when Kremmy signed his paycheck in crayon?" Priceless as a Ming vase... Trash, what are you doing? The hardcore legend rises to his feet, brushing off the light layer of ash off his jeans. Scratching at the very light mat of hair on his bare scarred chest, he stretches out a bit. "Goddammit, this itches. Jack, why do you keep doing this, it itches like crazy when it grows back!" Sure, if you let it grow back in. I told you to keep me waxed and smooth. And quit smoking, you're going to stain me teeth again! "Fuck you, Jack, you spent a ton of my money on that. And dammit, these are MY teeth, not yours!" And now our teeth can warn passing ships about shoals. This oh so interesting internal dialogue is interrupting by a knock at the door. "Senor Kriegman"? Trashy opens the door to find a well dressed middled aged Spanish man in front of him holding a package wrapped in brown paper. "We have a delivery for you, senor," he proclaimed, brandishing the package. With a brief nod, the Trashcan Man takes the package and hands the hotel employee a crumpled bill, the denomination hidden in the folds. He closes the door and sits on the garishly yellow sheets covering his bed, holding the package in his hands. "Finally," he mutters as he tears the brown paper off. Opening the cardboard box inside he pulls out an innocuous black plastic piece of equipment. Rather plain, it just has a few buttons and a dial on the front. It looks so simple... but the Trashcan Man cradles it carefully in the palm of his burn scarred hand like it was the Holy Grail itself. It could be his salvation as it once was his damnation. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- BANG! BANG! BANG! The Trashcan Man's head jerks up off the ratty mattress and stares around wildly at the sudden, loud noise. He moans at the jagged spikes of pain in his skull that beat a rapid tattoo against his temples. Reaching up, he runs his fingers through the side of his beard, trying to clear out the chunks of dried vomit and drool that he had drunkenly passed out in. He grabs the mostly empty bottle of Evan Williams on the floor and tilts his head back, letting the rotgut pour down his throat, trying to wash the sour taste out of his mouth and kill the ever present migraine he had been living with for months. BANG! BANG! BANG! "I know you're in dere!" shouts a voice through the door. "You owe me last month's rent, ya drunken bum! And dis month's too! You better have to me by Friday, our your out! I don't care how big you are, I'll get the cops in here, ya hear me?!" The Trashcan Man groans weakly and drops back down to the bare mattress. The sudden movement causes the bile and whiskey in his stomach to rebel and he leans over, vomiting up cheap booze and what was left of a frozen burrito he had gnawed on the night before after the microwave in the hotel room had stopped working. Blinking at the disgusting pool in front of him, he slid onto his side and tried to let oblivion fall over him. He desperately needed the black velvet curtain of unconciousness to draw itself over the constant pain he had been in for the past six months. The pain that had started as a dull headache a year or so before when he was in Primetime Championship Wrestling and had eventually driven him out of a newly created Old School Wrestling, where he had to give up a lucrative contract and main event status because he couldn't make it through a show without drinking enough to kill the screaming pain in his head. He finally started to slip into slumber when he heard the quiet jingle of a keyring. A scraping noise caused him to blearily look up to see the doorknob turning. Rising to his feet, the 300 pound Trashcan Man leaned up against the wall and clenched his meaty fist. "You sumbitch... I'll get yer rent... jus' let me 'loan...." he sputtered out as he tried to straighen up. The door opened and Trashy tried to lurch forward, but instead fell onto his hand and knees with a groan. From this vantage point he started to look up to see a pair of black shoes, polished to a deep shine. He rose up to just his knees, his eyes following up the black slacks, belt, and expertly tailored suit jacket until he met a pair of brown eyes. "It's... it's you," he slurred, trying to focus. "Wh... what are you doin' here?" Even white teeth show in a small smile from behind an immaculately groomed black goatee. "Why Michael... where else would I be?" A well manicured hand reaches down and fondly pushes the hair out the Trashcan Man's face. "M... Michael? Why you call me Michael? My name's Trash... Trashcan Man..." Ignoring his question, the man in black looks around the shabby accommodations, his nose wrinkling in disgust. "Michael, Michael, Michael... how ever did you let yourself come to this?" "It hurtsh..." cries out the Trashcan Man. "It hurtsh shooo bad... Can't make it shtop!" Alan Kriegman smiles down at the drunken hardcore legend. "Yes, I suppose the chip's final stage of powering up would hurt... and I don't think you could make it stop. Not without some seriously invasive brain surgery... and I think you'd end up a drooling vegatable if someone tried." Reaching into his inside pocket, the evil supergenius pulls out a small black box. "Would you like it to stop, Michael?" Trashy blinks up at Kriegman, tears welling in his eyes. "Yes... please make it stop!" he demands. Kriegman's smile grows a little wider. "Quid pro quo, Michael. I do this for you, you must do something for me." "Anything!" "Anything? You will serve me, and only me. You will be my faithful right hand? My sword and shield against those who oppose me?" "Yes! Yes, please!" Alan Kriegman's smile grows even wider as he kneels down in front of the begging Trashcan Man. "Call me... Master." Synapses fire as post hypnotic suggestions are triggered. Boosted by the chip in his head, the Trashcan Man has no choice... indeed, he's not even aware that there is the possibility of a choice... "Master!" The man in black rises back to his feet as he gazes down upon the hardcore legend who meets his gaze adoringly... but the adoration fades as another wave of pain washes over him. Kriegman rests his hand lightly on the snarled and shaggy mane on top of the Trashcan Man's head. "Trash... are you ready for the pain to end?" The Trashcan Man just nods quickly, his eyes pleading with his master. "Then... you know what I want to hear. Four little words, Trash... they will be your salvation. Say them now!" Again, synapses fire as suggestions buried deep into the pyromaniacs mind are uncovered. His eyes widen as the words come to him. "My... life... for you?" Alan Kriegman starts to laugh that oh so famous evil laugh of his... "My life for you!" Kriegman laughs even harder as he pushes a button on the remote. Trashy jaw drops as the pain fades away. In gratitude, he falls to his belly and starts rubbing his cheek on Alan Kriegman's shoe as he shouts his new mantra over and over again with the man in black's laughter ringing in his ears. "MY LIFE FOR YOU! MY LIFE FOR YOU! MY LIFE FOR YOU!!!!!!" "MUAHAHAHAHAHAAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!" --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- His adopted father's sinister laugh echoing in his memories, the Trashcan Man runs his fingers lightly over the box in his hands. Biting his lip with concern, he turns his face away before pressing one of the buttons on the remote... to no effect. "But... it should've done something?!" He presses another with the same result... and another... and another. He starts jamming his finger into the box wildly, panic rising as nothing seems to have any effect on the slowly building pain in his head. He falls to his knees with a deep cry as he hears another sinister voice in his head. Your life is for me now, Trash. And there's nothing you can do to stop it. The Trashcan Man wails in despair as Lazarus's laughter, so eerily similar to that of his former master, echos in his mind.
