Introducing Scotty

3 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/3/05 He slapped the bell on the front desk, its sound annoying the occupants of the lobby. Then he slapped the bell again, and again, and again… the ringing echoing through the air like the rapid fire of machine gun… a very wimpy, irritating machine gun. “Sir,” an elderly woman emerged from the back and made her way towards the desk, “it’s really only necessary to hit the bell once. We’ll respond as soon as we’re able to. It’s quite an annoyance to our other guests who may be in the lobby or dining area of the hotel…” “Good point. But then again, I’m paying as much as they are to stay here, and I find the incessant ringing quite amusing. It reminds me of my days in wrestling. When that bell would sound, and I would look around at the screaming fans, and think to myself… ‘god dammit, how did I lose again?!’” “Sir, how can I help you?” The woman reached out and placed her hand over the bell, sliding it back across the oak surface, and placing it behind the desk, out of sight and reach. “You should have a room for me here. A nice one.” “Name?” “Last name is ‘Ward’.” He watched as the woman punched a few buttons on the keyboard, heard a ‘beep’ and saw the glow of the monitor change colors in the lense of the woman’s glasses. “Ok, are you Scott, Dennis, or John?” “Which one has the best room reserved?” “Sir..” “Scott.” “Is that your name, or your choice?” “It’s Scott.” The woman went back to work on the computer, took care of the necessary odds and ends, handed Scott his room key, told him the number, check out times, this and that and yadda yadda yadda… that’s about what he heard of it anyway. He took the elevator up to the seventh floor and found his room. Trip Sevens. It was a simple room. A bed, two side tables, a small table in the corner of the room for use of a laptop, and a dresser complete with television opposite the bed. For a guy who had used to frequently blow his weekly paycheck in a night at the blackjack table… love had settled him down… and made him cheap. Scott grabbed the remote and kicked back on the bed. Slowly, he started to flip through the channels, hoping his tight spending would still allow him a room with HBO, or Showtime, or Cinemax… anything he might be able to catch a boob or two on. With nothing of interest on at the time, Scotty muted the TV and pulled out his cell phone and pressed ‘3’ to speed dial his brother’s number. After seeing what had happened at ReV 76, his head had nearly exploded. He hadn’t even known his big brother had stepped back into the ring, and he sure as hell wasn’t sure why he saw him in that ring beheading one of his closest friends in the business. A couple days after he had seen the show, Scotty had called Matt and left him a message, letting him know that he would be in town and thought they should get together. They hadn’t really talked since Scotty had moved away a few months earlier thanks to Laura’s promotion. Scott had taken the time to secure a backstage pass for the next PRIME show. His former run in the industry, not too mention the fact that he had once written for a popular Pro Wrestling magazine, had given him more than one or two ‘ins’ with the business. The phone rang twice, then Matt’s voice mail kicked in. He rambled on for a little bit about nothing of any real relative importance, just letting Matt know that he had arrived in town, but closed the conversation with all hereally needed to say… “Anyways, I’ll be at the PRIME show this week, got hooked up with a backstage pass and stuff. Having old friends in the business kicks ass, dude. I’ll see you at ReV, bro.” Scott closed the phone and went back to his channel surfing. The rate for the room wasn’t THAT cheap… there had to be a good channel or two somewhere.
