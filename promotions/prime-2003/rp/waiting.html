Date: Two Ok so there was that whole part I wrote in a previous RP about Sun Tzu leaving Devin. Yeah, forget that ever happened. I went back and erased it. Why? Because I effin wanted to. So as far as the reader goes, Sun Tzu and Devin Shakur are all happy lovebirdy still. ------------------------------------------------------------- Previously in the Devin Shakur Story… Business partners decided to see other people Michael DeAngelis fires Devin Shakur from his crime family after failing to whack Tony Gamble in a timely fashion. But not before sending their best regards as a Thank You Moments after stepping out of his Lamborghini, Shakur stands on his front porch and watches his prized vehicle erupt in a fireball. A family plotted to save their beloved friend Michael Blaze stands amongst the Smith family and reveals a devious scheme to get inside The Braintrust. Going to the ends of the Earth Devin Shakur’s guardian angel, Rachel Smith, purchases an RPG in a back alley for $50,000 And showing that love knows no bounds Rachel fires the RPG into a suburban home, sending the house into flames while concerned citizens scream, because two women just went inside the home. But everybody isn’t always let in on the plan Devin sits agape on his living room couch watching the house fire. Matthew Williams, a local news reporter, interviews the two homeowners. While transformed by the news, Devin’s phone rings causing him to hit the ceiling. On the other end, the raspy voice of Christian Daniels lends knowledge. "Kid, we got some splaining to do." ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ April 1st, 2008 12:45 AM Devin hated waiting, no matter the circumstance. Time had a way of toying with his mind in a capacity that often left him yearning for resolution. Whether it was something simple like Sun Tzu getting her hair done so the two could have a night out at the weekly Mao Zedong Enthusiast Poker Game, or more complicated like waiting on the weekly update regarding Michael DeAngelis. No matter what, Devin felt eyes of the unknown watching him, eager for the opportune moment to strike. He sat up in bed for the seventh time that night because sensitive ears perked at a noise outside. Due to his defensive nature, Devin rolled out of bed and slowly trudged toward the window to get a peak at the development. At any moment he could have to fight for his life, so he grasped a hold of Sunny’s ‘Kookoo Bear’ handgun and cocked the weapon. The disturbance was nothing more than a bird rustling his birdhouse on Shakur’s fence. With a groan, Devin put the weapon back on the counter and begrudgingly crawled back into bed for more snooze. He needed to get more than a couple hours sleep heading into active competition against Killean Sirrajin on Revolution 155. Peeling the red covers forward, Devin coyly tried to sneak back into bed without waking Sun Tzu. He had not succeeded the six previous times and tried to learn from each of his mistakes. If he woke his sweetie another time, who knows what she might do. Watching her eyes the entire time, Devin put his left leg onto the bed and pushed the rest of himself upward with his right. All was going according to plan and he was able to situate himself, throwing the covers back over his body without causing a ruckus. Until that good for nothing nightshirt caused him to absentmindedly lift up and remove the itching wrinkle. Sun Tzu shrieked, leaped out of bed with a vengeance, and reached for a hold of something on her nightstand. Mr. Snugglepants, the other gun in the house. BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! "AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH" "AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH" Shakur dropped down to the floor, taking the entire bedspread with him in an attempt to avoid untimely death. Sun Tzu stopped her rampage long enough to flick on the lights. She saw her hubby covered in satin sheets on the floor and the wall littered with bullet holes. Sun Tzu: STOP WAKING ME UP! Devin Shakur: I- Sun Tzu: YOU, NOTHING! Every night it’s the same thing. Are you waiting on The Boogeyman to walk over here and get you? Devin Shakur: Come to think of it, Silver might send him over to get back at me for not- BOOM! Shakur stopped talking. Sun Tzu: I’m going to go sleep on the couch until you get this sleeping impotence problem cured. At least that way I can avoid your capitalist agenda of hogging all the bed covers for yourself. Sun Tzu slammed the bedroom door, leaving little specks of ceiling to fall atop Devin's poodle like head of hair. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ April 1st, 2008 2 AM Devin was drifting off to sleep for the umpteenth time. He could actually see layers of darkness while staring at the ceiling fan. This was what Commie Emo wanted all along, solitude. A good seven hours of bliss and his batteries would be recharged to the fullest, ready to tackle the challenges of many tomorrows. Unfortunately, someone did not wish for Devin to have peace. The telephone rang, shooting Devin out of bed and almost making him perform the 'Dwayne Johnson Stunner' selling job. Quickly flipping on a side lamp, he frantically looked around for the phone, falling off the bed headfirst when a fraction of the ringing annoyance caught his eye underneath the nightstand. Unfortunately, someone else’s sleep was also interrupted. Sun Tzu: WHAT THE HELL DO YOU WANT, HEATHEN? THIS ISN’T MADAM YES, YES AND I’M NOT GETTING YOU OFF FOR 2.99 A MINUTE! PEOPLE HERE ARE TRYING TO SLEEP! Devin nervously put the phone up to an ear, wondering who could withstand that verbal onslaught and still remain on the line. "Well, I’ll be glad to note that girlie, now is Devin around? This is a business call." Devin had to speak up before these two got into a heated argument. Devin Shakur: Honey, I got it. Sun Tzu: I’m about to come in there and have your balls on a platter with some hot sauce if this happens again. Sun Tzu clicked off the phone and threw her head back down on the couch. Devin Shakur: Sorry about that Christian, she uh- Christian Daniels, the leader of the Death crime organization and Devin Shakur’s longtime friend. He was also the man helping Devin fight The Braintrust and Michael DeAngelis. Christian: You spent half a year going after her? Devin Shakur: I was staring at the back a lot of times if you know what I mean. Christian Daniels: Nothing wrong with that so long as the front doesn’t talk, you know? Devin Shakur: Yeah, yeah. So why are you calling this late, anyhow? Christian Daniels: Well I assumed, wrongly, that you weren’t sleeping. You’ve always had a problem with that and it’s daytime over here. Anyway, this really can’t wait. We’re all but ready to move in on The Braintrust. Devin Shakur: Really? That’s great news. Christian Daniels: Fucking right it is. Give us a couple more weeks to get plans finalized and you will have all the necessary information in order to begin your phase of the plan. Those morons aren’t going to have a clue in hell what hit them, shit they already don’t now. Devin Shakur: Fabulous. You know I’ll be ready. Christian Daniels: I’ll hit you up with the details when we’ve gotten them finalized. Devin Shakur: Good deal, good deal. Christian Daniels: Alright, sleep tight kid. With a double click, both parties hung up on their ends and went back to living their lives as normal. Well, about as normal as serial murderers and mobster wrestling celebrities can. Flicking the phone like a poker player mucking a losing hand, Devin rested his head on the pillow. He reminisced about that faithful conversation that started the madness all those months ago. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ October 16th, 2007 Christian Daniels: Kid, we got some splaining to do. Devin sat on his couch and watched firefighters desperately trying to put out the raging inferno before them. Neighbors watched the spectacle unfold from their porches and in the streets. Police had to hold portions of the gathering crowd back who managed to slip around the yellow tape. Devin Shakur: W...What the hell? Did you have something to do with this? Christian Daniels: You don’t win anything for guessing that correctly, but job well done nonetheless. Devin Shakur: The fuck is wrong with you? Christian Daniels: All part of a grand and masterful plan to get you out of trouble with the Braintrust. Devin Shakur: Start talking, and make it snappy. Christian Daniels: Calm down sissy, they aren’t going to come knocking your door down. As far as the public is concerned, nobody of importance actually died in that explosion. Devin Shakur: Who died there, reporter said two women went into the house. Christian Daniels: Valerie and Shannon of course, who do you think? Devin Shakur: Then who the fuck is that on television? Christian Daniels: Valerie and Shannon. Devin Shakur: ...Don’t play stupid with me. Christian Daniels: Don’t have to. You already do that for me. Devin Shakur: Goddammit, Christian, this shit isn’t funny. Christian Daniels: Is to me, and I’m the one holding the cards. Devin Shakur: Man, just tell me what’s going on. Christian Daniels: Alright, alright. You see, ever since you killed the Braintrust trigger man, Nathan Murray, nice job on that by the way, those two have been out to get you. They don’t want their friend to die in vain so they were going to whack you whenever they got the opportunity. I mean, shit, they already tried to take you out once on the highway and Rachel had to bail your ass out. Devin Shakur: So...You got to them first? I’m still lost here. Christian Daniels: Might not have been the most stealth manner, but we got the job done. Devin Shakur: So then why the bloody shit am I seeing their faces on the television OUTSIDE the house? Christian Daniels: You give me a minute, Sherlock, and I’ll divulge that information. Now, Michael came up with this brilliant scheme that I agreed to. Devin Shakur: ...Were you drunk or something? Christian Daniels: Not the point, but yes. For starters, the best way to get a game plan on the other team is to have a look at their playbook. Devin Shakur: Alright... Christian Daniels: So we gotta do that. Now, we coulda gone down the traditional road of robbing the office and taking all the pertinent information, but these kids have some talent so they can adapt. Instead, we felt the best road would be to stay one step ahead of their adaptation. Devin Shakur: Still lost here. Christian Daniels: You are intimate with Valerie and Shannon’s faces. Devin Shakur: Yeah. Christian Daniels: Rewind your DVR and take a good look at them during that interview. See if you notice anything different. Devin reluctantly grabbed a hold of his DVR remote and hit the rewind button on the lower left side, coming to a stop right before Valerie and Shannon began to talk to Matthew Williams. He studied the faces intently, scared at whatever findings may arise. For ten seconds, nothing really stood out at him until he got a look at Valerie. Something seemed to be off. To normal people, one would think she’s just having a bad makeup and hair day. To a man who has spent a year haunted by her image, little imperfections make his blood inquisitive. Devin Shakur: ...What the hell? On the other end, Devin heard Christian chuckling with delight. Christian Daniels: You figured it out yet, Einstein? Devin looked closer and harder. Devin Shakur: ...Is that...Oh, you are fucking with me. Christian Daniels: Yep, Mallory jumped at the prospect to play Valerie, and Shannon is played by someone we met when trying to escape the States. You wouldn’t know her. Devin Shakur: All this trouble just to get me out of it? Christian Daniels: Well, by coming after you they are disrespecting us. We might have left there a year ago but nobody takes our fucking turf. Now, stop distracting me. They are going to spend the next God knows how long getting to know the boss, the modus operandi, and what the guy is all about. I’ve already got them wired up and ready to go so whatever information they get is going to be put right into my lap. The lackeys over here will filter whatever comes through in their own way, and we’ll continue to formulate courses of action for them to take. Devin Shakur: So what do you want me to do? Christian Daniels: Your part won’t come for a long, long time so for now, relish in the freedom of not having to deal with those crazy cunts. You’ve put up with their bullshit for about a year, and I think you can handle DeAngelis on your own. Focus on stalling that Paisan long enough for us to deal with this, and then we’ll tend to his stupid ass. Devin Shakur: What will I end up having to do? Christian Daniels: You’ve done something like this before so it’s not going to be out of your range. Devin Shakur: Tell me Christian Daniels: You’ll become all emo internal on me if I do. Devin Shakur: I still want to know. Christian Daniels: Ok, you asked for it. Well we make a big dramatic scene involving them one day during normal operations. They allow you access to the building, you get past the guards, meet up with the boss, and blow his brains out. Devin went dead silent. He really didn’t like to be known as a killer, but aligning himself with the Smith family at an early age made murder a given at some point in his life. Christian Daniels: Yeeeeeah, just as I suspected. Devin Shakur: No, no, I can do it. Christian Daniels: I’m not questioning whether or not you can, but you’ll be freaking out until you have to do it. Devin Shakur: Listen, you said to focus on DeAngelis and that’s what I’ll do. Just be ready when you call right? Christian Daniels: That’s the plan. Devin Shakur: So that’s what I’m going to do. I got shit around here to keep me occupied. I’m a big time wrestler now. Christian Daniels: Yeah, one who can’t win at Pay-Per-Views. Devin Shakur: You wait and see I’m going to show this Ferguson kid what’s up. Christian Daniels: Well you are a better mobster than wrestler so think about that if you ever decide to quit. Devin Shakur: Fat chance. Christian Daniels: Just be ready kid. Devin Shakur: I will Click ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ April 1st, 2008 Devin felt a trickle of sweat go down the front of his head while adjusting positions underneath the covers. Having flashbacks was something Devin hated, but he really couldn’t avoid them. This was the life he had gotten himself into and there was wasn’t any method of diversion available. The time was drawing near and soon he would wish he could embody another person. The waiting game was on once again. Now he had to wrestle his body in order to get a little sleep, to escape from the overwhelming anxiety. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ April 1st, 2008 10 AM Devin rubbed the sleep away from his eyes and stretched weary arms toward the sky. He only managed to get about four hours of sleep in total for the whole night. That was an improvement, but he would still need to pound a massive quantity of energy drinks in order to feel alert enough to get through the day. Throwing open his bedroom door, his eyebrows lifted in surprise when he didn’t see Sun Tzu ranting and raving in front of the television. Usually, she spent the entire morning watching 24 Hour News Networks and screaming about how America was nothing in comparison to China. Devin shrugged and began a drowsy walk toward the refrigerator. He launched open the door and took out an Amp energy drink, popped the top, and started chugging the 16 ounces of refreshment down. Thirty seconds later, Shakur slammed the can down onto the ground and jogged over toward the front door to get the morning paper, feeling like a new man. Expecting to find a headline about world politics at his feet, Devin was confounded when he saw a white box staring back at him. Kneeling down to examine the package, Devin saw that the box was addressed to him but had no return address on it. Curiosity killed the cat as he picked up the package and began to shake it in his hands. There wasn’t much rattling, leading Devin to the conclusion that something big was inside. He decided to drag the trash in later, and started tearing into the package. Ripping the tape off, Devin flung open the top. What he saw inside made him go white as a ghost and drop the box, recoiling in horror. The world around him started to spin. He struggled to gain a balance by placing his hand up against the side of the house. Nothing could have prepared him for such a shock. This couldn’t be happening to him. Slowly sinking down deeper and deeper toward the ground, Devin shook his head vehemently while staring at the open box, and more specifically what was inside. The head of Sun Tzu Cradling his head into his hands, Devin started wailing out tears of agony. She might have been a belligerent communist and a real pain in the ass, but who would want to kill such an angelic person? He could not think of someone who held that much hatred for his love. He sat there for what felt like an eternity, helpless before the cruelty. "Why...Why..." She was the one he came home to at night and told about his problems. After the initial wave of lust faded between the two when she left PRIME, she managed to still love him. After seeing his secrets, her heart did not shrink. Once he revealed his story to her, she still kissed his cheek at night and called him honey. Despite their obvious differences in taste, they could still wrap an arm around one another and kiss. Now one of his closest friends had been ripped away. Devin removed his hands and wiped the tears descending down his cheeks. Having lost a girlfriend in a violent capacity before, reliving this moment was even more poignant. With his body beginning to shake, Devin tried getting back up to go inside and make the necessary phone calls. Then something caught his attention, a note on the side of the box. Devin yanked it away from the cardboard. He ripped off a side of the white envelope and unfolded the note to see if any insight could be gained into who committed this atrocious deed. Dear Devin, "April Fools. That isn’t really the head of your girlfriend in there, just some skank we found on the street that looked a lot like here. Sorry about your car by the way, hopefully you have insurance to get another one." Signed, Michael DeAngelis PS: We’re coming for you. Next time this won’t be a joke. Devin stood in front of his house with the note crumpling in his right hand. Rage began to flow as he allowed tears to drop down toward the cement. He violently kicked the box out of his sight and stared ahead, eyes burning like saucers. If this is what DeAngelis considered a joke, wait until he saw what the retaliation would be.
