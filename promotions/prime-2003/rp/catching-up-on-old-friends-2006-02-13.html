Catching Up On Old Friends

13 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13/02/2006 “If my wrestling career was represented by a piece of furniture, that furniture would be the dustiest item you ever saw. I’ve been stagnating for the last three months and quite frankly, I’m getting bored. I need something to do, something to get my blood running again and I really don’t care what it is. I’ve heard about the Dual Halo and how it’s like the proverbial rainbow that has a pot of gold at the end. Would I like my hands on that bounty? Of course I would. Will I get it? Of course I won’t. Do I care? No. So long as I leave the Halo having hurt someone, then I’ll be popping champagne corks all night long. Because THAT…is how I’ll know I’m alive…And THAT…is how I’ll know…I’m back.” Paul Cain on the Dual Halo experience. Date: Friday 18th November 2005.Location: Columbus, OHTime: 10.30am When you live your life out of a hotel room, it can be a very cold and lonely experience. There are no loved ones to keep you company and no children to put a smile on your face. Non celebrities claim that those in the glare of the spotlight have a wonderful time with their money and champagne lifestyle. That might be true for some but for others it’s a prison sentence and there is the category that Paul Cain himself has fallen into. He sat on the edge of the rickety and uncomfortable bed with a letter in both hands. He had not been focussed on his work for a few weeks since the failing health and eventual death of his grandmother and in many ways, escaping the dog eat dog world of professional wrestling might have been good for him. He had found his tolerance levels for the inflated ego’s and the self importance of those around him had greatly fallen. This weakness had been highlighted after Cain had ignored the odds that went against him the night before when Mark Blackwolf was made to pay for Hoyt Williams’ verbal desecration of his grandmothers’ grave. The weak elastic that held Paul’s temper in check had dramatically snapped and that had cost PRIME two of their workers. Despite this, Paul had sworn to himself immediately after that he would not leave PRIME. He could not afford to because he had to keep going for his own health and sanity. The brief moments where he had had time by himself were the moments that he had turned to the bottle. His grandmother was the only flesh and blood that had been in his life with the rest of his family members estranged from him. She had made him strong and kept him whole despite the distance that had kept them apart in recent years. Without her, he felt the weakness growing inside him which led the alcohol to dull the senses and the pain. Nobody could reach him until it was all too late. The curtains of the hotel room were barely open allowing only a sliver of light to shine through. Cain had not bothered shaving in the last couple of days, his hair was slightly greasy and his eyes bleary. A bottle of scotch was lying empty on its side in the middle of the floor and the remainder of last nights room service sat on a table by the bed. He opened the letter again and his heart was heavy as he sighed deeply. In a monotone and depressed voice, he read the document out to himself as he broke the rooms’ silence which had become deafening. “Dear Mr Cain, During the night of Wednesday, 16th November 2005, PRIME officials were disturbed to have witnessed an altercation involving yourself and members of the Imperium group. During this situation one such member, Mark Blackwolf, was severely injured after your actions which saw him be thrown down one of the arenas lift shafts. On initial examination, we are led to believe that Mr Blackwolfs injuries are not permanent however they are long term and he has consequently terminated his contract with PRIME. Whilst PRIME officials understand the difficult personal circumstances you are currently experiencing, we cannot possibly accept the kind of response that you carried out in retaliation for the words of the Imperium group. Therefore we must take the right and sensible course of action that will be in the best interests of you, the remainder of the PRIME roster, the fans and the staff of PRIME. It is our decision that you should be suspended from all activities relating to this company which will come into immediate affect. We would like to stress that PRIME take no pleasure in making this decision and that if you are able to prove to us that you are psychologically fit to return to active duty then we will discuss this with you accordingly. In the meantime we strongly advise for your own health and safety that you give yourself the opportunity to grieve privately and to recover from your ordeal. I speak for the rest of the PRIME management when I say that I wish you well in your future endeavours and that I hope you manage to demonstrate to us that you are ready to return to the ring in the months to come. Regards Hin See.” Paul dropped the letter into his lap and stared down at it with wide and glassy eyes. He knew that whilst Hin See had decided his future for him, others would be living their lives with no consequence. With that one night and one action, Cain had lost another thing that held him together. Only this time he had not lost it at all, Hin See had stolen it from him with a lack of compassion and understanding. Everything that was keeping him whole was being torn away from him and Paul Cain was losing the will to live. He stood up and picked his bag up off the floor and kicked the bottle out of his way. He didn’t care how he smelt or how he looked because he just wanted to get away but he didn’t know what from. As he took hold of his belongings and reached for the door handle, he stopped and turned to the letter that had fallen to the floor. He slowly walked over to it and crouched down, picking it up between his thumb and forefinger. Then with a twisted smile, he pulled a lighter out of his pocket and lit the flame, touching it to the corner of the paper. He tossed the letter back down and turned to the door as the document started to slowly burn. Throwing the door open, Cain strode down the corridor and as he walked past a fire alarm, he threw his fist out and smashed the glass, triggering the warning as he kept walking. “I hope you fucking burn in hell, Hin See.” Scene fades out to black. Date: Monday 19th December 2005.Location: Denver, COTime: 00:15am It had been the worst of times but now it was the best of times. Paul Cain had retreated to his home in Denver in order to find some kind of peace and to ease his frustrations. The bar trade had of course quietly celebrated his arrival as they knew that a down and out celebrity would be good for profits. They were of course proved right as Cain had spent a number of nights in the last month propping up the bar, accompanying his drinking with a high level of self pity. Now though he had done away with the unkempt and shabby exterior and was almost back to his old self. As he sat at the bar with a huge smile on his face, Cain was approached by a lady wearing a long flowing purple dress and equally lengthy coat to match. In her hand she carried a bottle of champagne with her hair cascading down underneath the hood that covered her head. She pulled out the stool that was next to Paul and delicately placed herself down on it. Beckoning the bartender to her, she gestured for him to open the bottle and motioned for two glasses with her fingers. Cain did not look at her but addressed her calmly and slowly with that smile still etched on his face. “You again, hey Violet? You don’t give up easily, do you?” She smiled from beyond the hooded coat and passed a glass of bubbling champagne to Paul. “Violet Rayne never gives up on a worthy cause.” Cain’s attention is focussed on the TV screen above the bar which is showing graphic footage of the death of Hin See. Mockingly, he raises his glass to toast the passing of PRIME’s lead figure and winks at the screen. “Good luck with all YOUR future endeavours Hin See…Oh shit, that’s right, you don’t have a future.” He laughs quietly to himself and takes a slow sip of the alcoholic beverage. Violet Rayne studies him closely as she runs her fingers idly along the rim of her glass. “Now that he’s out of the way, maybe you and I can talk business?” Cain scoffs dismissively at her and rolls his eyes. “Please don’t spoil this wonderful evening with your sales pitch.” Refusing to be denied this opportunity, she continues unabated with her tone one of insistence and urgency. “Paul, together we can get your life back. All you have to do is say yes and I will make sure that PRIME listen to me and that we get your career back on track. With Hin See out of the picture, it will be a piece of cake and for the sake of one three letter word, you can get the chance to finish what you started. You know as well as I do that there are people in that company who look at you as if you are a nobody, something they just stepped in and not the man that has been a multiple World Champion wherever he has gone. When you got suspended Paul, nobody cared, they just shrugged their shoulders and got on with it. This is your chance to get back and show them who you are and by God, if you can’t get a piece of gold, you can at least make sure that they know WHO you are.” Finally at the end of this tirade, Paul turns to her with a look of complete apathy and disinterest. “I have one quite major problem with that though.” Nervously she takes a sip of her drink knowing full well that this problem could jeopardise the deal. “What’s that?” He again returns his gaze to the TV treating her in the same way that she claimed PRIME treated him. “You’re a blood sucking parasite, also known as a woman.”Her eyes widen as she is taken aback by this sweeping statement but then she draws breath and quickly regains her composure. “It’s not your money or blood that I’m after Paul, it’s the resources of PRIME that I’m looking to tap into. With Hin See gone, Tyler Nelson is in the frame for being the head honcho and believe me, I’m sure that he and I can speak the same language.” Leaning his hand on his knee, he turns around and shakes his head at her again. “No you don’t understand do you? Women don’t like me and I don’t like them. One girlfriend tried to kill me and the other went crazy, can you blame me for being just a TINY bit sceptical? I can tell you now what will happen, I agree to you being my agent and then after a while things will get complicated with us and you’ll either stitch me up or mess with my head! When it comes to women, it’s like all of my dealings with them are scripted and I end up having another neurotic psycho on my list. My luck is so bad you couldn’t write about it!” She smirks at him and his obvious arrogance that he thinks their relationship will become more than business like. “Believe me darling, you have no problems with me, trust me on that one.” With that, she pulls out a letter which she passes across the bar to him. He looks down at it suspiciously and after a moment, decides to take the plunge and accept it. “This is the contract I’ve drawn up for you….Now I KNOW your past, Paul…I KNOW your history…So, with that in mind…IF I cannot get you and Damien Kahn back into PRIME…Then the contract will be ruled null and void…You can walk right away from me and our dealings will be finished with.” He taps the letter against his hand and raises his eyebrows thoughtfully at her offer. Without taking his eyes off the contract, he addresses her proposition. “You GUARANTEE you can get us back into PRIME?” With a smile, she sips her drink and looks up at the screen. “It’s as certain as death.” He raises his glass and taps it against hers with a matching grin. “Well if that’s the case….I’ll drink to that.” Scene fades out to black. Date: Monday 13th February 2006.Location: Denver International AirportTime: 07.30am “Flight UA 875, United Airlines. Departing from Denver International Airport and arriving at Narita Airport, Tokyo is due to depart at 8.47am.” The formal voice of the airport staff member boomed out over the white noise that were passengers busily trying to find their way. It was a scene of organised chaos as people attempted to get their luggage checked and onto the planes. Paul Cain however was not so stressed as he stood in line with his baggage, his faithful colleague Damien Kahn by his side. Things were a lot different now as Cain was showing off his Armani taste again and wearing that look of arrogance that had followed him during the good times. Damien Kahn leaned forward on his baggage trolley watching the world go by. “She had better have sorted out the first class, as there ain’t no way we’re sitting with the rats.” Paul turned around and smiled wryly at Kahn. “I thought you were from the ‘hood and used to being with rats.” The dry comment was met with a glare. Cain certainly knew how to press Damien’s buttons and it amused him to no end. He was just glad to have reason to smile again. Before Damien could make a retort, he was interrupted by Violet Rayne making her way over to the duo. She handed back their tickets and looked at them both intensely. “Your tickets have been upgraded to first class, all care of PRIME and more specifically, Tyler of course. HOWEVER…Just remember why you are going out there, okay? Do NOT screw up and do NOT go looking for personal glory…Go out there, do what you have to do, do it well and come home again. Remember, Tyler just wants a job done and done to a high standard, if you boys get distracted and screw up, then you’ll put me in a difficult position and I’ll have to do some fast talking to get us out of the shit…Do you get me?” The frustration on Kahn’s face is evident and not helped by the fact that an elderly woman suddenly rams her trolley into the back of his leg. He casts an evil look and she quickly apologises. After making sure that the woman has got the message, he returns his attention to Violet and mimics her before offering a stern warning. “Do you get me? Of course we GET YOU…I don’t listen to no bitch though, we’ll do what we gotta do but we’ll do it our way…Do you GET…ME?” Cain shakes his head vehemently and holds up a hand to Damien in order to silence him. Paul had already spotted the repulsion in Violet’s eyes at the dissension. “Easy big man, easy…Violet here has ALL of our best interests at heart…Let’s just enjoy ourselves…It’s not work that we gotta do…It’s fun...Let’s just have a ball D-Money…Chill out…Have us some sushi…A bit of geisha…Do the hit…And then come home again…Easy money in the bank D…Easy money…” He glances in Violet’s direction and notices she has been appeased by his compliance. Damien however continues to look mildly disgruntled at having to follow the orders of a woman. In an attempt to distract himself, Kahn addresses Violet again in a calmer manner. “So where’s Gamble at?” “I’ll have him in Tokyo, you don’t have to worry about a thing, it’s all in hand.” Noticing the queue has begun to move along, Cain decides to step in between what resembles an old bickering married couple. “He’ll be there…Now let’s get our shit together and go make Tyler proud.” Cain smiles at Damien and pats him on the shoulder before they troop out leaving Violet to find Tony Gamble. Scene fades out to black.
