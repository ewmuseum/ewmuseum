Date: ... “Like a fart in the wind…” Devin Shakur flicked the off switch on his radio, effectively ending the primitive moment of intellectual comedy. Today was a day of intimate reflection, only 24 hours before he would have to depart on the most grueling journey of his nine month professional wrestling career. The coveted Intense Championship would be on the line against Wade Elliott, and then along with nine other individuals, he would engage in combat for the most illustrious prize in the sport, the Universal Championship. It was not a matter of if Devin was ready. The amount of physical punishment his body had endured over 28 years cannot be measured. He is a legitimate freak of nature under the black suit. A thousand nuclear bombs of rage have exploded on his body and he still remains in one piece. What could ten people in a wrestling ring over two matches do to him? Yes, for Commie Emo, it wasn’t a matter of getting ready. The task at hand was… Getting ready Sitting on the rooftop of his Raleigh mansion, Devin stared out at the heavenly view that 15 million dollars bought. A glass of lemonade and the Intense Championship rested on a small table to his left, and a green photo album sat in his lap. Whenever troubling times surfaced, the roof was his sanctuary and the album of selected photos from his library was his guiding light. From seemingly nowhere, Shakur started to speak into the day. “I don’t know, it’s just what in the hell would they say today if they saw what I do?” The album flung opened and the first picture Devin saw was of his mother and father posing for their best Christmas card in 1975. It almost brought a smile to his face to see them living in innocence, before the chaos of a child shattered them at the seams. “Maybe Mom would be proud of me, follow your dreams and what not. I figured she was that kind of person to say that you could be whatever you wanted to be, you had the talent inside of you, blah, blah, blah. My father on the other hand, that piece of shit wouldn’t be proud if I figured out the cure for cancer, AIDS, built a flying car, was the first man to mars, won the lottery, married the most beautiful woman in the world, became famous as one can get, and had my face on a box of Wheaties.” Imagine that for a second, Devin Shakur’s face on a box of Wheaties. Funny ain’t it? Dusk, don’t make that your next gimmick, for our sake, please. Devin flipped to the next image in the album, one of him and Christian Daniels sitting side by side on the Smith family couch back in the day. At the time Devin looked so tame, not knowing that soon his mind would be warped into that of a psychopath. “I remember what Christian told me right before I got into PRIME…Don’t get too close to anybody. HA, oh man I’m going to get fucked up whenever he brings Sunny into a conversation. Damn, I can hear him now…” In perhaps the worst impression of a seven foot biker with a North Carolina accent, Devin began to re-enact the speaking part of Christian. “What in the hell were you thinking boy? Ain’t you got any idea how to do a job? Go in there, watch Gamble, get his shit down, and then whack him. Back when I was your age, I used the head up here.” Devin pointed to the head on his shoulders. “And now the head down here…” Devin threw his right foot out and simulated getting hit in the nuts, almost falling out of the chair onto the hot cement. He cackled with laughter, something seldom seem anywhere, before getting back to his ritualistic routine of viewing photos. “Tremendous.” A turn of the page produced a photo of Sun Tzu lying on his master bed. With pride, a smirk emerged on his face when he looked at his girlfriend. Her tight body, firm ass, and those “fuck me” eyes could draw him away from anything at any given time. It was almost as if his mind was pried apart, and all of the components of a dream woman were concocted into a living breathing person. “God, she’s hot man…It’s like…I don’t have any clue what to do with her really. Everything I want is everything that she is. I told her that I loved her and that I couldn’t spend another second without her in my life. I get her, right, and what happens, can’t tell her that I love her.” Accomplishing the task of having a self conversation, Devin shouted back. “Man, what the fuck is wrong with you? You are going to give up that to go back to the way you were?” “Dude, the last time I tried to love someone…” A sick feeling embedded itself in Devin’s stomach at this point. He flipped to the one page that he hated to look at, yet he always brought it to the album, his first wife Kimberly Crawford. During his time in the joint, they were married, and he was scheduled to spend the rest of his life with her. Then she was savagely murdered. The case still remains open, but Devin doesn’t discuss the matter with anyone. Whenever someone had the guts to ask about it, Devin only gave them a one line response. “Gotta keep moving on…Gotta keep moving on.” And that’s what he intended to do. Quickly he turned to another page. Devin’s face went from malcontent to a happy regret. It was a portrait of his CEO party for ATI. Everybody around him was drunk and festive, even Devin himself managed to crack a smile for the cameraman. At 27, he was a business prodigy with the mind to change the world. Then he got smacked in the back of the head with a pipe and forced to join PRIME. “The man has a funny way of working. Shit, I was THERE dude, everything in the world at my fingertips, coulda been sniffing Bill Gates exhaust fumes with how much potential I had…” He snorts loudly. “…And now…” A glance over at the Intense Championship “Now I’m Commie Fucking Emo…What the fuck happened to me?” Devin threw his hands up in surrender. “Ridiculous.” Devin’s eyes locked upon a picture of a lighter moment in his life. One night during Nova’s long Universal title reign, Devin snuck into his locker room and found “the belt”. He had a staff member take a picture of him modeling the championship. Yes, it was indeed a faux, but still a nice image to one day hang in the trophy room. “I’m not going to remain someone’s goddamn puppet for the rest of my life, you know? Ever since I came into this world, I’ve been a bitch. Whether it was for my father to beat on me, for Christian to have another man in the field, the guards to have another inmate to break up before putting in D block, or for some no name organization, the mob, and biker gang to use me and kill one of their past problems. Me, me personally you ask? I ain’t got fucking shit.” Devin flipped two middle fingers to the world around him from his high perch before taking a sip of lemonade. “Really, I just want to see into the future for a couple seconds. Get a glimpse of where I’m going to be in five years. I’ve done all this, gone through all this shit, and for what? To kill some Italian midget who works in a wrestling organization? That is going to be the apex of my life? You have got to be motherfucking joking me…I’ma become an optimist for the first time in my life, but I’m supposed to do greater things than that. That’d mean all this bullshit I’ve done is just wasted time.” Devin spat to the side before taking another sip of lemonade and continuing on his rant. “Ain’t nobody expecting me to walk out of DDT with either belt on my shoulder. I beat Elliott and then I’ve broken the mold of fragile Intense Champions. The championship would actually have some cred again and that’d be something that I could attach my name on…” Devin leaned up in the lawn chair and stared down deep into the Universal Championship that resided in the photograph. He looked like a child setting his eyes upon the end of a beautiful rainbow. “But if I win the Universal Championship…Goddamn son, I’d be the fucking man! Everybody will be looking at me as the best wrestling superstar in the world…Billboards, buses, talk show appearances, fuck that I’d BE on that box of Wheaties baby.” The euphoria exited just as quickly as it entered. The realization hit Devin in the face like a ton of bricks. “Heh, question is…Would I even know how to handle that? I ain’t ever been any kind of real leader before.” Devin leaned back in the chair and folded his hands across his stomach. He allowed the world to pass him by for a couple of seconds thinking about the prospect of becoming the top dog on the block. Is this all worth it? They say when the time is right that you’ll know… Devin sure as hell hoped that time was soon. Devin felt a hand caress his shoulder, a figure slump into his lap, a pair of lips passionately kiss his, and an ignorant woman rest herself into his body. “Hey baby…” He said to Sunny before staring off into the distance. The wind flipped to the last remaining page of the small photo album. A little sticker with the title “Reserved” was resting comfortably at the peak. Something to hope for
