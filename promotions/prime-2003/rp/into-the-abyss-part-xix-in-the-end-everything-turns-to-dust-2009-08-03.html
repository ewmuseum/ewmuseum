Date: 03-08-09 Up until now... They met in the time before Hessian came to PRIME, at the tail end of 2006 to be precise. Von Kelsig, attempting to lead a normal life away from the ring had acquired a property with help of an investment from one Bruce Shanahan. Renovating the old office building into a nightspot for the perverse, Von Kelsig successfully ran the business through the year until one day he was kidnapped by thugs working for a man named Reuben Blanco. Blanco, he later discovered, was a big time player in the drug trafficking and illegal armaments trades in South America, looking to expand operations to the States. The reason for his kidnapping? Blanco employed a number of loan sharks to help fund his operations, one of which was a James Carlisle, a man Von Kelsig had crossed years before and done out of a substantial sum of money. Thus, looking to reclaim the lost money Blanco held Von Kelsig against his will in his Detroit safehouse. What followed was several weeks of torture, taking the form of Von Kelsig effectively being force-fed heroin and other hallucinogenics while Blanco demanded he return his money, be it in bank notes or the deeds to his nightclub property. His methods ensured Von Kelsig would either comply, or die on the outside. Von Kelsig never faltered however, refusing to cooperate under such circumstances and give in to such a heinous man. It was at this time he first laid eyes on Gloria Carmichael, a runner for Blanco. She was an emaciated, track-ridden junkie with wily white hair and sunken eyes. Seeing her as his only way out, Von Kelsig romanced her with the idea of a better life outside of the hell she was living. Being promised so much by such a symbol of power such as he was, Gloria aided Von Kelsig in his escape and together they fled Blanco's clutches thanks in part to one Tyler Rayne, completely by coincidence. They promptly disappeared, out of sight out of mind. Their first struggle was to overcome the addiction to the drugs Blanco had supplied, an uphill struggle that they eventually conquered thanks to a spell in a rehabilitation clinic. Walking out clean and truly free from Blanco's wrath, Angelus Von Kelsig and Gloria Carmichael set about making a life for themselves, travelling for a while before settling in Memphis, Tennessee. While he got back to wrestling the indy circuit in a bid to make money, Gloria picked up two jobs working as a salon receptionist and took part time shifts working in a bar. Being clean for so long led to her tidying up her appearance and turning from a scabby ghoul to a sultry looking female. They soon fell in love. Two years passed had passed since Von Kelsig had been kidnapped and subjected to the unspeakable horror he faced, when he made the decision to contact PRIME, the last company he worked for before going on hiatus. Thanks to a little intervention from the influential Rayne Man, Hessian was hired with no mention of his previous indiscretions and once again the Murder Show rolled on. Juggling the rigours of a life on the road with a blossoming relationship was always going to be tough. Being gone for so long, Gloria eventually found companionship in the form of her salon boss – Larry DeLancy. This led to her questioning her being with Von Kelsig, who regardless of his stature and wealth seemed hesitant to quit life in the ring to settle down and start a family. This story finds Von Kelsig finally making that decision, after all the years of suffering and hard work. A decision that will change his life forever.
