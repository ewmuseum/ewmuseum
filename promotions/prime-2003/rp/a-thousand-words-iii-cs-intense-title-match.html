Date: Lotsa days involved n' shit! *click* I... Uh... I spoke to Eme last night for the first time in a week or so. I mean, sure I have no choice but to fulfill those fatherly and husbandly duties and call at least every other day to let the family know I'm still alive while I'm on this World Tour. But... how do I put this? Things have not been good for me in the last week or so when it comes to my home life. Apparently, Eme couldn't keep her Goddamn mouth shut and she started spilling off our private problems to my family. I can't stand it. My cell's getting bombarded with whiny phone messages from Steven telling me that I need to find guidance and sit down with Eme, blah, blah, blah, I never asked that giant goody-good. Rex and Tim won't leave me alone, either. I leave those two to run the Silver Lining Gym and I find that business has gone down in the last year since I opened that place up. Hey, guys? Word of advice: A little less whining, a little more working. I'm fine and I don't need any of them sticking their big, fat noses where they don't belong. I sigh to myself constantly when I'm alone flying the skies or taking the cab rides alone here. I keep telling Eme that we'll deal with all this as soon as I get back to the States. It's just one last show, then she and I can have it out. No, scratch that. She can have it out, I'll roll my eyes and nod my head as if I'm truly listening, then we'll both dismiss this entire argument just as we've done before and we'll be our happy little family again. I feel like she's starting to calm down a little bit. I mean, sure she called my brothers and my own mother complaining that I have problems, but it's all gravy. I've always been the Alpha Dog of this family and to them, my word has always been my bond. They'll have no reason to doubt me, I can get on my merry way, Eme and Gaby can continue to live in the lap of luxury and all will be right with the world. Then I can forget about these stupid exercises recording myself talking. It's annoying. I'm tired of seeing this shrink and as soon as this trip is over, I ain't having any more of it. I gotta say, though, this World Tour experience has been nothing short of incredible. All the sights I've seen, the people I've met, the other cultures I've come across. All of it, just one great time. Getting to be part of such a grand event is a joy in my career that I'll cherish always. What... What the...? Goddamn, can't Steven leave me alone? They all want to be wrestlers, too. They're just jealous that I made it to the big-time while Steven's still toiling over in Japan and Rex and Tim crapped out, so they're only good enough to work at my gym... those two were always like dogs, begging for scraps at my table. Of course, I would never say this kind of stuff to their face because they're all sensitive. Truth hurts, eh? One thing that I've noticed lately is that those dreams where my wife and daughter are calling out to me from a picture that I've always kept in my wallet have lessened in frequency over the last few weeks. In fact, it's been a good two weeks and I haven't heard hide or hair of Eme's nagging me in said dreams. Though, the frequency of the dreams where the very title I won – my first singles championship in nearly three year– The Intense Title, has gone up. It's there telling me all the time all the stuff that I've known all along: I'm a frickin' success now. And I did it all my own. Why would Eme want to rob me of that kind of joy? I'm not a preacher man nor a real firm believer in the power of the Lord, but if I remember correctly, under His power, husband and wife are to love one another unquestioningly and uncritically. They would die for one another. I would gladly take a bullet for my wife if it meant preserving her life for that much longer. She knows this, but because I'm out here on the road, I'm labeled as selfish. Egocentric. Self-Centered. Living in a state in which the only being that exists for all to see is me. I call bullshit. The being I am today can't possibly be the way she depicts me in her mind. I, a man willing to do anything for his wife, is labeled as such while SHE wants ME to give up the one thing I've been good at in life and say goodbye to it. To my original point, somebody that loves me unquestioningly and uncritically would not ask such a massive task of their loved one. It pains me to say this, but the fear that Eme has either stopped loving me or never really loved me at all has crossed my mind more than once. I mean, why else would she press on with this fruitless argument? You can't ask somebody to give up all their dreams just to fulfill your selfish needs if you really love that person. It's just not right. But as I said, it's in the back of my mind. Eme Ke'ala was in her bedroom, having just gotten out of the shower to prepare for yet another agonizing day at work. They'd said everything they felt they needed to say about how they've felt lately, but like the infamous Cuban Missile Crisis, tensions remained high with either side not budging from their stance on things. With a grunt, she slammed the cordless phone back into the wall and let herself crash back onto the bed. Eme then pondered whether or not she should go into work feeling this way. It was a good thing that Gaby wasn't able to speak yet, because if she were able to fathom the kinds of things that had been going around in the Silver-Ke'ala Household, the words "Bad dad" or "Selfish Prick" might come to mind. Eme made no sound, but she mouthed the word "Wow." She'd NEVER thought anything that hostile towards him before. She paused for a moment, then tried to take her mind off thinking something like that; she let the last month of events play through her head. Her work life, things had been fine while Sonny was doing his wrestling gigs overseas. Two big victories for her firm, a new promotion that she just got by now working directly under one of the Senior Partners as his secretary, and praise from her co-workers. Plus, that bitch in accounting that always had a grudge against her got fired, because, she, took was working directly under one of the Senior Partners, but in a whole other way. As she continued to wait for the babysitter's knock at the door, her mind just couldn't escape it. She had just referred to her husband as a selfish prick. For months, she begged him to stay with the family since he'd never done that for more than a month at a time, but the more she thought about the comment – and this made her feel just a little bit horrible to admit – the more it made sense. She did all the work around the house. She was the one that paid all the Goddamn bills and mortgage and all he did was fly around the world pretending to be something he wasn't. It seemed he cared more about his stupid job than for his own family and she felt stupid for not realizing this sooner. "AAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!" escaped from her mouth before she picked up one of her books and chucked it HARD across the room, knocking several make-up products off her vanity mirror stand. More angry thoughts began to race through her head. What she REALLY wanted to do at that moment was pick up the phone and for the first time in months, REALLY scream at her husband for everything he'd put the family through until both his ears started to bleed. She'd been upset and she'd been sorrowful about all this, but this anger was a new sensation that was festering itself into something she didn't like. Eme finally sat up and started to fix up her stand again. She glanced at her favorite box of perfume – Dolce and Gabbana Light Blue – and put the container back upright, thankful it hadn't broken and overtook the room with its scent. She straightened it all up and cleared off the top of the stand so nothing was left on it. Her head suddenly snapped up and turned back to her phone. There was always somebody for her to call in these situations, but until now she'd only thought about how much she wanted to keep these problems between she and Sonny. There was no sense in getting other people involved, but it was clear things were not going to get better any time soon without some sort of divine intervention. Given Eme wasn't especially religious herself, she would have to resort to the next best thing. Eme took the phone in her hand again and flipped through their address book she picked up from the nightstand. Frantically, she ran her thumb through the book until coming across the number she wanted and dialed. After hearing a few rings, she was delighted to hear a deep voice present itself politely on the other end? "Hello?" "Is this Steven?" "This is him." Eme smiled when she got the person she was looking for. "Steven, if you got time, I have to talk to you. Steven hit the "Off" button on the phone and put it back on the charger before making his way back to bed. On the way upstairs, every last word that Eme had told him from surreal dreams with talking pictures to her constant bouts with stress to their little arguments. Steven tried with all his might not to wake her up when he tried to gracefully set nearly 6'5" and 280 pounds of his own muscular frame next to a mere 113-pound 5'4" Asian woman. Thankfully, Achika was one of the world's leading heavy sleepers so when he managed to cuddle up next to her, he gently kissed her on the forehead and took her form up underneath his entire arm. The seconds soon became minutes, the minutes soon became hours and Steven was no closer to finding sleep than he had after his phone conversation. By this time, his girlfriend was in whatever deep slumber she had maintained before while Steven kept his mind at bay with one of the vast collection of books he kept at bedside on a miniature bookshelf. He thought this book on Campaign Finance Reform that Sonny once got him as a gag gift (it was actually to be used as a doorstop) would've put him to sleep, but poor Steven's Low Latent Inhibition allowed him to retain such useless knowledge. Hell, it was even starting to sound intriguing. "Kay, that's enough of that rubbish," he muttered before tossing it down on the bed. Steven let himself drift off again, folding his arms in another attempt to make sleep come to him. Then he started concentrating again on all the things that Eme had told him concerning that dream. She truly was worried for her husband, but she didn't appear to be in a position to do anything about it. Sonny had all but blocked her out. Steven clinched his arms tighter and soon entrenched himself in deep thought. Steven began to think about just how he could approach his brother in this situation. Their relationship as it pertained just the two of them was not on the best of terms ever since Steven got Sonny blacklisted from a federation they wrestled in together, The Wrestling Battle Ground, when Sonny was sleeping with the owner's daughters. Steven would make a little comment here, Sonny would blow it off and that's just the way it was, so speaking to him by his lonesome was absolutely out of the question. Then his mind took into account how he could help, if at all. The relationship between Sonny and Eme didn't occur like any normal one. She'd followed his friend, Dave Morey, on the road, but the two ended up falling for one another. Sonny got her pregnant and agreed to marry her, hoping one day they would love each other. And for a time, everything seemed to be all right, but he knew the day would come when either Sonny resented Eme for having and choosing to keep this child that neither initially wanted or Eme would get mad for being away. From his point of view... both catastrophic events were taking place. Quickly, Steven rolled out of bed again, but stopped when he realized he almost woke up Achika. Lucky for him, all she did was roll back over and return to sleep. Silver was about to head for the door, but then walked over to his girlfriend's side of the bed and planted another kiss, this time on her lips. In a soft whisper while kneeling closer to her, he spoke. "Baby, I hope whatever pitfalls plague my brother don't befall us," he smiled at her, then got back up and gently shut the bedroom door behind him before heading back downstairs. It was a certainty: approaching this thing by himself was not just inadvisable, it was a suicide mission. He needed help from family. He made his way back to the living room and picked up the phone. He reached into one of the many drawers in the kitchen and came back out with an address book of his own. Steven thumbed through it until coming across the number for The Silver Lining Gym. By that wonderful invention called Pacific Standard Time, he determined that the boys would be at work, at least setting up the ring mats before they took on their first class at 12:00. He still had a little bit of time to get to them. Slowly, he dialed the phone and thought about just how he was going to present this. "Hi, our brother's making his wife upset and I think he's crazy, too." "Hey, how's the weather. Oh, by the way, Sonny's being a gigantic douche to his wife." Lovely, but no. He asked in that slightly nasally voice Steven knew all too well. "Hey, Tim. How are things?" "Good, man, but ain't it awful late over there?" "Yes, but... I have a good reason. Tim nodded in response to all that Steven was telling him, then scratched his natty black hair. "Okay. Well, dude, don't be afraid to call if you hear anything else. If Sonny or Eme need us, we gotta be there." "Dude... personally, I feel like what goes on between Sonny and Eme should stay between Sonny and Eme, you know?
