Tension in the Air

21 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/21/09 Dixie Normas didn’t take no for an answer. If she wanted it, more than likely she had it. And if she didn’t, it was only a matter of time before she did. The world was hers for the taking and she dared anyone to stand in her way. -------- Dixie Normas brushed her long blonde hair away from her forehead, as if to clear her thoughts. She stood across the desk from her father, hand on hip bitch stance, waiting for his reply. “And why are you doing this again, Dixie” her father asked brow furrowing in concern. “I mean seriously. You don’t know a thing about wrestling, and I’m afraid you’re going to get yourself seriously hurt. How about you hop on board with me at Helix? You‘ll have your own office, an assistant, the works. You‘ll be rolling in the big money in no time.” Dixie chuckled and a smile crept upon her face. “Dad,” she pleaded, giving him the best puppy dog-eyes she could come up with. “You know how much this means to me. Besides I won’t even be anywhere near the ring.” It was a lie of course but Dixie didn’t care. There were more important things at stake here. Fame was so close that she could taste it. She didn’t want to waste her life in some cramped office all day; even if the pay was good. She knelt over and planted a kiss on his cheek to make her point that much more clear. She was a daddy’s girl and an only child so she was spoiled rotten. As if on cue, he smiled and sighed, pulling his checkbook out of his organizer. “Alright, alright,” he said putting his hands up in defense. “You win. If it means that much to you, I’m game. Let’s do it.” He scribbled his signature on the check and handed it over to her. “Here’s a blank check, do what you’d like.” Money was no option. Bill Becker was very wealthy. Filthy rich, in fact. He was chairman of San Francisco’s most renowned private equity firm, Helix Capitol. The company had just finished raising one of the largest private equity funds in history; twenty billion dollars. It was all in a days work. Bill had only wished that, Dixie would grow up and someday follow in his footsteps. Just like he’d followed in his own father’s footsteps. Famous himself, and doing his best to stay out of the watchful eye of the public, he didn’t quite understand her fascination with it. “Thanks, daddy” she said practically jumping up and down. “You’re the best.” She kissed him on the cheek once more and turned to walk for the door. “Uhh, Dixie,” he called out for her. “Please try to stay out of trouble.’ She smiled innocently, her laugh anything but innocent as she somehow managed to weasel her way out of the awkward situation. “Why of course, daddy! Anything you want.” Who was she kidding? Trouble was her middle name and it just so happened to be her favorite thing to get into. Besides shopping that is. Dixie folded the check and placed it into her back pocket as she exited the room. “Well, that was easy,” she said as she pulled her Blackberry from her Louis Vuitton bag. She had the money. Now all she needed were the slaves, err… wrestlers. --------------------- Harry Cox opened the door to the pink limousine. Mercedes, the eager Shih Tzu, greeted him enthusiastically with licks and nuzzles and was rewarded just as profusely with generous pats. “Dixie, what a pleasant surprise it is to see you aga-…” “Save it for someone who gives a damn, Harry,” Dixie interrupted pushing the door of the limousine into him. “I’m not here for the formalities. I’m here to talk business, it’s a go.” Harry was a friend to Bill Becker, Dixie’s father. A former wrestler himself, and now an owner of one of the most famous power gyms in California--thank to Bill--, Dixie contacted him with her idea of starting a tag team. At first he thought it was ridiculous but she was just so damn persistent. She had to have what she wanted. Plus Harry owed Bill. “And just what are you referring to exactly?” “Project fame, you jackass. Look, I have no time to stand around and chit chat. Do you still have those guys lined up for me or not?” Dixie grabbed Mercedes off of the limousine’s leather seat closing the door with the heel of her foot. She began towards his mansion which wasn’t even a third of the size of hers. Harry chuckled scratching his hairy chin sagely. “As a matter of fact, they’re here now.” “Really,” her eyes lit up like a child’s on Christmas. Everything was beginning to fall into place and pretty soon her dream would come true. Harry nodded. “They are actually looking forward to meeting with you.” “But how’d you…” “Bill called and gave me the good news before you got here. I wanted to have everything ready for you when you came.” “Why, Harry,” she began. “I don’t know what to say.” “Maybe thank you, would be a nice start?” Dixie thought for a moment, and then looked him in the eye and said, “Umm… sorry not my style.” Harry slapped himself across the face chuckling once more and lead the way to his humble abode. -------------------- “Fellas, meet your new boss.” In unison Carlos Garcia and Tony Borelli turned away from the fifty-two inch flat screen television. Carlos Garcia’s heart sank to the floor at the sight of sweet, little Dixie. She was a looker. In great shape. Nice long legs with a matching set of nice perky boobs. She had the blondest hair and the greenest eyes one could only wish for. Topped off with a little junk in the trunk. Back to Carlos. He hopped up off the couch so fast that he tripped over his feet. Highly embarrassed he caught her grin of delight. So did Harry. He couldn’t remember the last time she smiled. Maybe this would be good for her after all. Or maybe she was just laughing at how stupid Carlos looked in those cowboy boots and that stupid little cowboy hat. Who ever heard of a Mexican cowboy before. Anyway. Carlos poked out his little hand and tried to introduce himself. “Name’s Carlos, chica.” “I’m sorry but I don’t shake hands,” she said flatly. He froze, his hand still in the air. More embarrassed now than before. “But the name is Dixie. Dixie Normas.” “Dicks Enormous,” Tony Borelli’s gruff voice echoed sarcastically across the room. “What type of shit is that? What were you raised by wolves or something? Who‘d name a child that?” “Excuse me,” Dixie exclaimed. “What did you say you little Italian meatball?” “What are you, deaf?” “What’re you stupid?” Dixie turned towards Harry. “Who is this character? How dare he insult me like that,” she roared flames appearing from her clenched fists. “What is this Harry? Some sort of sick joke? I don‘t know what type of operation you run here but I am not interested.” Dixie turned to leave. “You were right, Harry,” Borelli said. “She is good.” “Fiesty! Just like I like em’,” Carlos said. “Huh?” Dixie stood, dog in hand confused. She looked at Harry and then over at the members of her ’Trust Fund’ who were now both grinning. “You passed the test,” Harry told her. “Tony refuses to work with someone soft and Carlos seems to have the hots for you. No surprise there.” Dixie stood lost for words, confusion still reigning over her. This was turning out to be one weird day.
