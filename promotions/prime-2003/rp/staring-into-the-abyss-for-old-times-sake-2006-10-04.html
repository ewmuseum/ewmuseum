Staring Into The Abyss (for old times sake)

4 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/4/06 The small bar was only seven miles east of Houston, but it was far from the Texas mindset. On the walls were pictures of great revolutionaries. From Che Gueverra to Malcolm X, to Zapata himself. Damien Cruz found the place by accident, but returned there at every chance he could. Sitting at the bar, he sipped a rum and coke, and watched a satellite feed of  Iraqi war footage. He was scheduled to do a speaking tour of colleges to get people registered to vote, but that was a lifetime away. He had a much larger purpose to deal with. In a few hours, he would stare down and defend his place in PRIME against a man few people could claim dominance over. It was a task he was ready for, and one he was looking forward to. He realized that the cameraman that was assigned to get his reactions was impatient, so he spoke his mind. ' Face-Eater, if you can glean anything about me from my actions, you will realize that I am a man of few indulgences. For the most part, I take only what I need, I live only within my means, I exist only to serve a purpose, and I say only what I intend to do. So if I ever sound arrogant, or boastful, I suggest that you may be misunderstanding me.' He sips his drink, and points to the picture of Malcolm X on a nearby wall. ' I am a fan of that man for a simple reason. He did not believe that another man's impression of you should influence who you allowed yourself to be. He once stated that the Black Man's plight is not the struggle against the White Man, but the struggle against being what The White Man assumed you would be. He was a brilliant man, but was killed by his own people. The same can be said for Che Gueverra, and even for Christ. My point is, those who stand out tend to scare those who don't. And even among those who claim to see your vision, there is a threshold of brilliance that they will not tolerate. Once you evolve beyond your followers' limits, you risk making them feel betrayed. I would imagine you can see what I'm driving at. It's time to take your personal evolution beyond the limitations of the typical people you once surrounded yourself with. And Sunday will be your baptism of fire to a new beginning. ' The Latin Assassin rolled up the sleeves of his white dress shirt, and exposed a tattoo of a smiling buddha. ' I received this one when I was wrestling in Japan. Now while so many people see The Buddha as a symbol of a jolly god, I see him as a representation of satisfaction. What he taught can be summed up in a simple phrase; do not persue what you cannot attain. Many regard that to mean that we should persue nothing because everything is fleeting. I don't think of it in those terms. I prefer to think that life is more interesting when you have a higher sense of purpose. And this week, stepping into that ring with you, Facey, is my higher purpose.' The Latin Assassin drops a twenty on the bar, and carries his drink to a table. He sits, and casually continues what he is saying. ' I will admit something to you, Face. I honestly considered making this week in PRIME my last. In fact, I considered leaving the business all together. I grew bored. In the time I've been in this business, I told myself that I would do it long enough to get out of my small-town life in Cielo Azul. Then I made it to Japan. Then I told myself I would do it long enough to make my wealth and help my community back home. I put every penny I had into a bank account and opened two schools and a clinic in Mexico. Then I came to the US and told myself that I would do it only long enough to earn a decent retirement. As of today, my retirement is pretty much secure. So you have to wonder why I continue to pull on my pads and allow myself to be beaten like a dog week after week. THere must be something outside of the simple pursuit of wealth that drives me. Now if you've paid attention to me long enough, you already know what that is. Face, this is my life. I step into that ring for the same reason many men break soil and others start families. There is an art to what I do. There is a dedication to physical and spiritual completeness that will not allow me to rest. And the more I think about my future in PRIME, that fire is fed and the flames lick the base of my skull.' His smile has faded, and is replaced with the stare of a man who has found God. ' I will bring you to the edge of what you thought you were capable of, and then I'm going to push harder. If you want to see the depth and the breadth of your instinct, I will show it to you. That is a solemn vow that I keep. I have watched you make a mockery of this sport with your attempts at theatrics. And I will forgive them just so long as you will forgive my unrelenting desire to strip away all else but the man underneath. It's time to put to action what you're so artfully put to words in the past. Within each of us, Facey, is the ability to be a martyr. Within us, as well, is the ability to be a catalyst. We can evolve beyond the level that so many others wallow in. We can push ourselves, and this art form, to a plane that will send the typical men running. We have, if you are serious, the potential to add to the epic history that has carried PRIME head and shoulders above what so many others claim to be. So make a vow, right now, to push me further, and I will do the same for you. Evolve past the literature of human ability, and show me the visceral definition of it. If you do that, if you make my every atom rage against the desire to shut down, that I will promise you that you and I will be forever changed. And in the end, the course of human evolution will be brought to the doorsteps of those who question us. I hope that you are ready for such a transformation, because the process can destroy those who can't face it's taxation on the body, mind, and spirit. Facey, when you and I are standing inside that ring, the Gods will be forced to pay attention. And I will, before Gods and men alike, force you to prove the very words you've spoken.' In that dark bar seven miles outside of Houston, Damien Cruz remembered why he broke the threshold of that ring so many years before. It all came rushing back to him like a pentacost. The sight of his Coming Battle, like the smell of the sea, or the sight of a faded poster, made everything almost fogotten very real once again.
