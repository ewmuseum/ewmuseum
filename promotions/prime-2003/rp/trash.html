Date: Archiving 155 Stuff (Fade in on Clearwater Florida, home of Scientology and the United States base of PRIME’s Xavier Kannon. Speaking of the Gold Patron Meritorious , we find him standing atop the steps of the city’s gargantuan Scientology flag-building. At his side, as always, is Eleanor, whose wardrobe appears to be counting down to the fourth of July by adding more stars and stripes every day, while also managing to consist of less and less fabric.) Kannon: PRIME’s Stooting Stars and Armbars, Wednesday 2 July… War Games. And how fitting is it that I stand here, just feet away from the doors to ultimate enlightenment. Behind these doors is where we fight our war against intolerance and ignorance. A fight that we will win, no matter what obstacles are placed in our way. Why? I’ll tell you why. Because I have a dream. I have a dream that one day the world will rise up and overthrow the out-dated and faith-sapping Churches that have left your minds tainted and unquestioning. I have a dream that in streets across the world, the children of Scientologists can play with the children of… well, other Scientologists, since the Neanderthals we’re surrounded by will surely reach an evolutionary dead-end and pancake against it at 100 miles and hour. (Ellie claps her hands to demonstrate.) Kannon: This dream will come about because of men like me. Men who will form the Vanguard in our crusade against spiritual charlatans. Just as I will lead my team to victory on Wednesday night. (Beside him, Ellie twirls her hands around before pointing to her husband and mouthing, ‘His team!’) Kannon: Inside the cages of War Games, my allies may fall, but they will sacrifice their health knowing that it is for a noble cause… my victory, as I prove to my people that I will lead them to the promised land. (His chest puffs out and his drops his head back, staring up at some distant point high on the skyline in heroic fashion.) Kannon: For those who will watch in two days time, let me tell you now why our victory is assured before the cages even lower around the ring. Focus. Determination. Concentration. Leadership. All things sorely lacking in our opponents. (Backing him up, Ellie nods to the camera, her expression communicating to the viewer that it’s a sad but true fact.) Kannon: As Tony Gamble, The Codemaster and Champ Chamberlain line up behind me, they know that they’re being commanded by a ring general unrivalled in the history of the business. A man that will stop at nothing, will turn back from nobody, to achieve his goal. And who do our opponents have as a standard-bearer? Wade Elliot. (Ellie shudders at the thought of his… grittiness.) Kannon: I don’t think I need say another word… but I will. What has the whole wrestling universe been talking about for the past fortnight? I’ll give you a clue, it isn’t Elliott’s dog licking his rear-end backstage while his master tries to do the same to Lindsay Troy. They’re talking about what I did to Jonathan Rhine. (Just to mock the incident further, Ellie pretends that she has a lash stuck in her right eye.) Kannon: They’re talking about me taking his eye. They’re talking about me taking a chunk out of the competition’s champion. They’re talking about me making a statement of intent. They’re also asking why I did it… and the answer is simple. He wronged me. But Elliott wouldn’t understand that. You don’t remember back to when your balls were still in the sack and you weren’t fighting for first place in Lindsay’s line to get them back, do you Wade? That’s why you’d better get ready to apologise to your team for caring more about Dusk trying to roofie Troy’s lights out or Rayne pimping her ‘underground’ than winning the match for them. (Just the thought of Kannon’s suggestions make Ellie fight the urge to heave.) Kannon: So while you’re trying to decide if you should be on ReVolution or Grey’s Anatomy, Wade, I’ll be there ready to beat you so damn hard that you’ll flashback to Daddy getting the belt out and his drunk pals bruising your ‘Bama… and by ‘Bama, I obviously mean shithole. (Just off his shoulder, Ellie feigns a wince at the low blow.) Kannon: I’ll make my dream reality, Wade. But your team winning on Wednesday? Afraid you’ll just have to keep dreaming. (Fade out on the smug couple turning from the steps and heading through the Scientology’s flag-building’s doors.) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = (Fade in on Xavier Kannon sunken deeply into an armchair within the lobby of Clearwater’s Scientology flag-building. He offers slow, mocking applause to the camera.) Kannon: Wade Elliott, I can honestly say you’ve shocked me. Seriously. Under that furrowed, missing-link brow of yours, behind those dull, crossed eyes, inside that thick skull, you actually figured something out. (He leans in so close to the camera that his breath almost steams up the lens.) Kannon: I, Xavier Kannon, am a cunt. (A click of his fingers brings a caption up on the screen that reads XAVIER KANNON - CUNT.) Kannon: Don’t go feeling all proud of yourself like you’ve just learned to write all joined up though, Wade. That fact should have you pissing the bed at night like you were 13 again, afraid the door would creak open after you‘d gone to sleep. (Dropping back deep into his chair, Kannon unbuttons his jacket and lets it hang open to reveal a slogan across his t-shirt that simply reads c n u t… a play on f c u k rather than a typo.) Kannon: Being a cunt means I’m the lowest of the low, means I’ll sink deeper than anyone else on the roster, means I’ll go that step further after all the motherfuckers and shit-stains have said, ”that’s enough.” (He executes a sudden clap, prompting two banners behind him to unfurl, revealing intricate, woven designs… all around a four letter word.) C U N T Kannon: Me being a cunt means that what happens in the cages on Wednesday should be the least of you worries. (Calling the camera back in close, Kannon’s voice takes on a sinister tone, a fixed stare into the lens piling weight onto his words.) Kannon: If I were only a prick, I’d likely just slash the tyres on your Arkansas Chugabug. If I was a mere fucker, I’d put the headlights through and piss in the fuel tank. But I’m not a prick, and I’m not a fucker. You said it yourself, Wade… I’m a cunt. Being a cunt means I’ll cut your brake lines. Being a cunt means you’ll be unconscious on the floor before you even realise I’m behind you. Being a cunt means I’ll kick that mangy mutt of yours down the corridor when it sits there whimpering in the pool of blood around your head. (Even after the outburst, there still seems to be one last thing the Scientologist needs to get off his chest.) Kannon: Oh, and just for the record… I’m not just a cunt, Wade. I’m THE cunt. (Fade out on Kannon appearing proud of the fact.) = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = (Fade in on The King Of Wrestling Xavier Kannon sat in the departure lounge of Miami International Airport. From the airport’s bookstore he’s managed to find the little-known self-improvement book that is ‘Leadership for Rednecks’. XK looks up from attentively reading the book, feigning surprise at the camera being there.) Kannon: What can I say, Wade? Bang-up job of rallying your team around you on the eve of the match. I’d grab a copy of this if I were you. $4.99. Only a bit more expensive than your whole wardrobe. (He slams the book shut and tosses it onto the seat beside him.) Kannon: But, hey, you got one of your team to show up. Shame it was only ‘the other one, yeah him, the fourth guy’. Simply put, one won’t be enough, Wade. But I can’t say I blame Channy T and Dawkins. If I was told I had to follow you into a War Games match, I’d be at the terminal flying out to Barbados and watching the slaughter you’ll lead them into from my lounge. (Placing both hands behind his head, XK stretches out in his seat, mimicking settling down to watch ReVolution at home.) Kannon: Oh, and while I’m sure Rossi brought some truly inspirational words with him, I’m afraid I didn’t hear a word. Common problem with having reached such a heightened state of enlightenment. I simply exist mentally and spiritually on a plane where the jibes of gutter-scum like Simply Beautiful can’t penetrate… oh, and right there is where he’d have made a penetration joke. See, who needs to hear when you can simply predict? Let’s see… probably called me gay, too. But, will I be drawn to replying? No. But I urge all of PRIME’s homosexual demographic to write to FX, and voice your disapproval at Rossi using your lifestyle as an insult. I myself face persecution each and every day, and it’s time to say that enough is enough. Write, e-mail, telephone, take action to get the prejudiced words of Simply Beautiful taken off the air… oh, but wait until Thursday. No point in writing to have him removed if he’s in traction before anyone even reads it. (To aid the viewer, the website, e-mail and postal address of the FX network pops up on the screen, together with the telephone number.) Kannon: So, where are the rest of your boys, Wade? (Mocking the ‘Bama Bruiser, the Gold Patron Meritorious looks all around, shading his eyes as if he’s hunting them out in the distance.) Kannon: Tsonda? Channy? Oh, wait, here you are. Fashionably late, should have known it. You were smart enough to hire Ellie as a manager once, so this should make blinding sense to you. Channy, just look at the billboards and watch the commercials… Colossus is coming, and you’re in the main event. Or, at least you will be if you can still feel your limbs after Shooting Stars and Armbars is over. See, you don’t need to walk into War Games, Channy. You’ve nothing to gain and everything to lose. So, if I’m somehow preoccupied enough destroying your team mates to let you pin Champ, Codey or Gamble… so what? You’re the #1 Contender. But I get you up for the Hallmark on the second rope, so high that your hair barely brushes the canvas, you can kiss that Colossus main event goodbye. Hell, you can kiss standing up to take a piss goodbye because you’ll be spinning around in a D&G wheelchair. You have that much faith in Wade’s leadership? (A shake of the head communicates, ‘I thought not.’) Kannon: Dawkins? Bry? You there? If you’re smart too, you’ll relapse into the trusty ‘forgot I had a match’ routine and get your Hawaiian ass flyin‘ back home. Only thing that’ll be hangin’ loose if you follow Elliott into battle is your head after I Hallmark your neck into bone-dust. (He slams a fist down onto his knee, mimicking Dawkins’ skull thumping off the mat.) Kannon: So, Chandler, Bryan? I’d say I’ll see you tomorrow… but I have a sneaking suspicion there might be some horrendous traffic around the arena or a cancelled flight… don’t you? Funny how that’ll only effect one team. Crazy, huh? (Fade out on Kannon flashing a sly grin at the camera as he receives his boarding call.)
