10 Steps To Anger Management

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/10/06 I lost. The big return. The whole world was going to tremble at the feet of the angry giant. The former PRIME Tag Team Champion who was out to prove he wasn't going to be a push over this time around. Oh yeah I had big plans. HUGE plans. No way were people going to make fun of me this time around. No way could anyone over look me for some other shining, rising, promsing star. This time it would be different. This time it would be me who everyone was talking about. Well they are talking alright. See it wasn't just the loss. In fact it wasn't even the fact that I lost that people are talking. I'm a realist when it comes to professional wrestling. I want to be the best, make no mistakes about that essa, but I'm also no fool. Nobody wins all the time. No one is perfect, no matter how much we'd all like to believe what we tell one another. Losing is just part of the job. You can't get out of it. You can't run from it. You can't hide from it. I'll lose again at some point. We all do it. So you accept the fact that you lose some and you win some. The only thing that really matters is how you lose. Did you hold your head up high? Did you show pride in the fight you put up? Can you look at yourself in the mirror and know you gave it your all to win the match? Yet I can't look at myself in the mirror essa and know that I did all I could have. I haven't been able to get up this week without feeling sick to my stomach. I didn't just lose a match last week. I lost some of my pride. I lost some of my dignity. I lost a little part of my manhood. I lost. 'I give...I give.' Those words haunt me. That simple little series of words have mocked me every single moment of my life, sleeping or awake, since ReVolution last week. Every single breath I take they are there screaming into my mind about how I quit. How I let someone else break me. So I have to ask myself why? Why did I lose essa? Why did I give up when I've never done that in my life? Why would I allow myself to be humilated like that on live television? Losing I can get over. Losing I can move past and correct. Losing I can correct and avenge. Quitting is something entirely different. Quitting is something that sticks with in you, eating at your soul like the worst flesh eating virus. Quitting is the kind of thing that can destroy a man. So why would I do it? Why would I allow myself to be put into this uncomfortable situation? Does this mean I've lost everything? Has my 'big' return been derailed before it has even gotten under way? Sure maybe some will point out the fact that I destroyed Karina after that. I guess they could point out that I did a pretty fair job of taking my frustrations out on people during the brawl at the end of the night. I say that is all a big steaming load of horse shit essa! There is no way around it. I can't deny it any longer. There can be only one thing that is holding me back now. I've been searching for answers all week long. Trying to piece together the puzzle. Trying to find the link to the person or people I can blame for something like this happening. Karma. It has caught up with me. The way I act. The fact that I let my anger get the best of me. Hell the truth is I let it control me. I've become what I hate the most in the world. I've allowed myself to act like the one person I never wanted to be like. I've become my father. Well like him only way better looking. But the point is that I've allowed my anger to control me. Maybe it is time to change. ***** The meeting room was large and bright. The walls were painted white and were pretty much bare except for the occassional poster, cork board, or flyer. Next to the entrance stood a large display center with lots of pamplets, booklets, and other various reading material on the subject at hand. Anger Management help was something Baltasar wasn't exactly thrilled to be in the need of. Growing up with an abusive, drinking, angry father had made him withdraw from sharing with others. He didn't believe in telling others his problems or letting them get to know about what was on his mind. 'Pissing and moaning about problems to others is the sign of a pussy, essa.' His father had said on more than one occassion. Just one of the many wonderful ways that his father attempted to ruin his life. The joy of degrading his son, wife, or practically anyone else for the fun of it was a wonderful past time for his father. He could still remember the first time the thought of seeking counceling for his fathers drinking and anger problems was ever brought up in their household. It wasn't a very entertaining day in the household. Unless of course you were his father. At the age of twelve, however, Baltasar wasn't exactly in position to do much in the terms of 'having fun'. 'How many beers have you had Jorge?' Constantina asked. The beautiful wife of Jorge was truly worried about her husbands health. Drinking all the time, getting into fights in bars, and acting foolish was bad enough when they were young and just married. Now with their twelve year old son, Montrell, things needed to change. They needed to change in a hurry. 'Seven, eight, ten. I don't know. I don't have the time to keep track. Only a chica would be keeping count of how many they drink.' Jorge replied before taking yet another chug of his beer,nearly finishing this one off as well. 'Jorge I've been thinking.' She began before Jorge began to laugh and gave her a little shove as he walked past her to the kitchen to get another beer. 'You don't even need to finish that sentence for me to know that you've already made a mistake chica. Thinking is something a woman isn't suppose to do. You're job is to cook dinner, clean house, and suck cock when I tell you to. Nothing more.' Tears began to form in her eyes. He only spoke to her like this after he'd drank several beers. He was getting drunk, like every day, even though you couldn't tell it if you didn't know. He drank so much that it took quite a few before his speech would noticably slur. 'Jorge you need help. We can't live like this anymore. We've got a son. You've got responsibility.' 'RESPONSIBILITY! WHAT THE FUCK DO YOU KNOW ABOUT IT WHORE!' Just like that he was turning ugly. The anger and drinking completely taking over his mind. In the time it would take a person to snap their fingers he went from getting another beer to now yelling at her and holding her, by the throat, up against one of the walls of the kitchen. 'YOU DON'T EVER TALK DOWN TO ME WHORE! I OWN YOU! YOU DO AS I SAY!' Finally he let her go and she fell to the floor, gasping for air, tears streaming down her face. Between the choking and crying Jorge simply reached casually for another beer. As he drank it down he noticed his son, sitting in the corner of the hall, with his head tucked up near his knees. Crying and in fear. Jorge simply smirked as he looked toward him. 'Pissing and moaning about problems to another person is a sign of a pussy essa.' So much for bringing up the topic of getting help and talking about your problems. All you got was tears and seeing your mother get man handled by the man who was suppose to love her, care about her, and protect her. ***** Baltasar sat in the frong row of the meeting. Quietly listening to the person who was leading the class. Supposedly a person who'd once had anger problems themselves but had conquered it. Maybe he could help him over come his anger issues. Maybe he could help remove the one thing from Baltasar that was reminded him of his father. If the anger was gone then his father would be completely dead to him. 'We have a great crowd here tonight. I'd like to start out by asking everyone to stand up and tell us your name, what you do for a living, and what made you come here tonight. I'd like to start right over here with you sir.' Baltasar sat there listening. Silently cursing himself for coming to this place. Now he was going to have to stand up and tell all these people, who he'd never met, what his problems were. Some where deep down in his soul he almost felt as if he really was a pussy for being here. After a few short moments it came to his turn. The man at the front pointed to him and without even really thinking about it Baltasar stood up. Eyeing everyone in the room, all of whom had their eyes fixed on him. He could feel the sweat already forming on his body as he finally was able to begin to speak. 'My name is Baltasar and I'm a professional wrestler. I work for the federation known as PRIME.' 'Is Baltasar your real name sir?' The man leading the class, whose name was Adam, asked him as he stared. 'Not my legal real name but the name I choose to go by.' 'Why is that? Do you hide from your real name for a reason?' 'No. What kind of question is that?' Baltasar hissed. He could feel his anger already welling up inside of him. 'Usually when people go by different names it is cause they have issues they want to get away from. That is why we are here. To help people through those issues.' 'I don't have issues with my name. I am a wrestler. It is what I go by for my job and it is just something I've gotten used to. Nothing more. Are you done asking me stupid questions now?' 'This is good. I can tell you're getting angry. That is alright. We're here to get to the bottom of your problems. Let it all out. Tell us what you are running from.' 'Essa do you have a hearing problem? I said I ain't running from anything. I don't have issues with my name. It's Montrell in case you were actually wondering. I came here tonight, Baltasar began before taking a deep breath and then exhaling. 'I don't really know why I came here tonight. It was clearly a mistake.' Baltasar picked up his jacket from the back of his chair and was turning to head out when he was stopped dead in his tracks by hand on his shoulder and the voice of Adam. 'Let us help you Montrell. Tell us what your problem is. Where does the source of your anger come from? You can only heal through the help of others.' Suddenly the room got really hot. His temper was quickly rising up to dangerous levels. He didn't like having people try to force their ways into his life. Adam though he meant well was doing exactly that. Pushing his way in. He didn't belong. No matter whether Baltasar had come here on his own or not. 'First off I said this was a mistake. Secondly don't every lay your hands on me again. I don't like it when people who I don't know touch me.' Baltasar hissed as he slowly turned to look down into the eyes of the smaller man. Adam took a small step back, taken aback a bit by the look in Baltasar's eyes, but still appeared undetered. 'So you have issues with touching. Maybe your anger centers around that. Did you get hit as a child? Maybe you had an abusive father?' Suddenly rage filled the eyes and body of The Red Devil as he lunged forward. Grabbing the man by the throat and pushing him backwards. The fire flashing in his eyes. 'Some people just don't know when to shut up!' The Red Devil stated in a very angry voice as he continued to lead Adam, flailing arms and all, backwards toward his podium. The people in the meeting were all standing at this point, in shock, at what they were seeing. A moment later The Red Devil and discarded Adam into his podium, knocking it over, and leaving the man gasping for air. Tears of fear coming from his eyes as he clawed at his throat in an attempt to some how get the air to flow into his lungs faster. Baltasar simply stood there,staring, before finally turning around to look at all the people in the meeting who were standing in shock. Mouths open, hands on their faces or head, in complete shock. After a snort, that would remind you of a satisfied bull, he started to make his way back down the aisle and out of the building. 'This is for pussies.' Maybe his father was right about one thing in his sorry and pathetic life. Maybe having other people help you with your problems was a sign of weakness. The truly strong, mentally and physically, in life deal with things on their own. They way they are suppose to. Baltasar was going to be no different. Giving up last week would be the last time the world would ever see weakness out of him. From now on it was a whole new Baltasar. The Red Devil had accepted the part of his past that he most wanted to run from. No more running. No more confiding. Time to adapt. Time to put that anger to good use and for his own personal gain. If he was going to be stuck with something from his father he might as well make the most out of it. *****
