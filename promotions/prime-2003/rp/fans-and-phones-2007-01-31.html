Fans and phones

31 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/31/07 While January's not exactly tourist season, there's definately some foot traffic on Montcada Street in the Gothic area of Barcelona. And cameras hung around necks, the gawking at the beautiful architecture, and of course the raised voices speaking a mishmosh of languages and dialects scream that this is in fact a tourist attraction. The late afternoon suns shines down with little warmth on this cold, blustery day. Breath condenses in the air in short sputtering bursts as people stamp around, hugging themselves for warmth in the slowly moving line. Two young Japanese men,looking to be in their early twenties occasionally glance over at a man with shaggy brown hair standing in a phone booth across the street, digging through his pocket. They argue with each other animatedly, gesturing wildly, the one trying to convince the other of something. The optimistic one finally leaves his place in line and begins to stride across the street. His friend sighs and then follows him. They reach the phone booth just as the figure inside pulls his orange gloved hand out of his pocket, holding a small handful of coins. He picks one and stares at it blankly before shrugging and turning back towards the pay phone. He starts to put a coin in when he's interrupted by a knocking on the phone booth door. Turning he looks to see the two men, one beaming confidently, the other nervously edging away, doing the embarrased short apologetic bows that the always courteous Japanese are known for. He opesn the door with an eyebrow raised as the young Japanese man greets him. "You're him! You're... Trash Can Man! I... Toshi... this my friend Takeshi... We big fans! See!" With that, Toshi unzips his jacket to show, of all things, an old PCW Asylum shirt with the Trashcan Man's face, as well as that of JJ, BANG! Davenport, and goggle wearing Miyagawa. Trashy shakes his head and smiles. "I can't believe any of those still exist." He reaches out and fingers the material on Toshi's shirt. "Where'd you find that old rag?" Toshi shouts out "Ebay!" before jumping up and down excitedly as his friend tries to calm him down. "Toshi, you look like a fool! I am so sorry for my friend, sir." Toshi completely nosells Takeshi's attempt at calming down, continuing to jump up and down and clapping. "It is! It's really Trashcan Man-san! " He suddenly stops, his smile disappearing, replaced by a grim look. In a deep voice he intones, "Don't play with the fire! You might get burned up!" The grim look swiches right back to his excited grin. "Pretty good, yes?!" The hardcore legend has to chuckle at the earnest face. "Close enough. You guys gonna be at the show tomorrow?" Takeshi speaks up. "Hai! We come to Spain on school trip, but we get away tomorrow night to see wrestling show!" "Trashcan Man-san is going to beat up big Bastille guy good!" Toshi starts to pantomime some punches. Trashy takes a step back, holding up a hand defensively with a smile on his face. "Watch out there, big guy, you look like you could get a little hardcore your..." He stops in midsentence, leaning up against a wall, his gloved hand reaching up to massage at his temple. Toshi, a concerned frown on his face, takes a step forward but the Trashcan Man waves him off with his free hand, breathing heavily for a few moments before lowering his hand. "Trashcan Man-san... ok? Anything we can do?" asked Toshi. "Nah, it's ok, just a big of a migraine, that's all. Listen... can I ask you guys to... I really got to make this call..." "No problem, Trashcan Man-san," Takeshi assures him. "Come on, Toshi, we need to get back in line." "Ok. You win good tomorrow, Trashcan Man-san!" Toshi throws up a fist. "I AM NOT A SANITATION ENGINEER!" With that, the two fans wander back across the street and get back in line. Trashy watches them go before turning back to his phone. Slipping in a few coins, he waits for a dial tone and punches in a phone number. He leans against the wall of the booth, running a hand through his hair as he listens to the low ringing noise. "Pick up, please Suzanne..." he mutter as he continues to wait. Finally he hears a click and starts to speak, only to be cut off by the answering machine. Hi, this is the home of Suzanne and Eddie, we're not in right now, please leave a message and we'll try to get back to you just as soon as we can... BEEEEP The hardcore legend sighs, clenching his fist in his hair before beginning to speak. "Suzanne... it's me, Tra... um, Michael. I... I just... I'm outside the Picasso Museum in Barcelona and I know you said you always... dammit... Listen, I just wanted to check on you... and I wanted to hear how Eddie was doing. I know I left you in a bad spot... but I'm trying to get better. I know thing's between us will never... I... I really want to be in Eddie's... I want to be a good father, you know." He sighs again, this time wiping a tear from his cheek before speaking again. "I've sent the rest of the back child support to your lawyer... and I'm working now so I'll be getting them to you on time... Please pick up, Suzanne, please... I'm so sorry..." He hangs up the phone and leans his arm against the wall and resting his face in its crook as his shoulders expand and contract in great sobs. He cries for about a minute before he straightens up, oblivious to the stares the 6'3" sniffling American is receiving by passers by. He starts to open the door before crying out again, this time in pain as it feels like hot knives are being driven into his skull. He throws himself back into phone booth, slamming his fist into his own head as he slumps down onto the floor. Waves of agony wash over him as he clutches at his head, muttering obscenities as passers by hurry on their way, not wanting to get involved with the crazy American, who's most likely on drugs and carrying a gun like most Americans do. Finally the pain fades back to a dull throb and the Trashcan Man rises to his feet. He blindly shoves more money into the phone as he dials another number. He waits, hears a message, and speaks into the phone again... "Kremmy, it's Trash. The chip... it's been activated. You gotta send me the remote I gave you, right away. You've got my hotel room, get it here right away. Hurry Kremmy..." He hangs up and slumps back down to the ground, hoping beyond hope that he can hold out for the next few days while he waits for an old friend to come through. He had to hold out... the alternative was unbearable. Because last time the mind control chip in his head was activated... the Trashcan Man killed his best friend.
