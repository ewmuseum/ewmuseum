Destined to Lose it All

15 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/15/06 It was a fine mess… a beautiful mess… that he was in. Or at least… that’s what Marc said. While Matt had stopped to analyze his predicament and come to the realization that he was neck deep in a brutal mess with four of the most dangerous and talented men in the business, Marc Avit had been busy encouraging him to lead the war. “I’m telling you, Matt. This is great stuff. I told you this would all work out.” Marc twirled his fork in rapid form, then shoveled more than a mouthful of spaggetti into his mouth. “I’m a genius”. Through slurred words, he gave sauce smothered smile. “What are you talking about?! I’ve got my wife worried that I’m getting involved in the biggest battle of my life when I should be winding my career down. I’m spending all my time trying to stay in contact with Lindsay and Killean so that we can keep some sort of game plan in order. I’m ignoring my fam…” “C’mon, Matt. Sacrifices have to be made. You’re looking at the downside of things. If you take the time to check out the glass and open yourself to the concept of ‘half full’… you’d see that you’re leading the resistance against four legends of the industry. Have you heard the fans going nuts for you recently? Have you taken the time to check the Nielson’s and see that you’ve been in some of the highest rated wrestling segments in TV history?” “So getting the shit kicked out of me in the middle of the ring… I should just ignore that because, after all, that quarter hour did a an 8.0?” Matt shook his head, ripped his knife through the prongs of his fork and shredding his chicken breast more than cutting it. “You have fun explaining that one to my wife, Marc.” “Hey, fuck you, Matt.” Marc paused only long enough to unhinge his jaw in snake-like fashion and shove what appeared to be half a loaf of garlic bread in his mouth. “Yo flamer, hit on me.” “Did you just say, ‘Yo, flamer, hit on me’?” “No.” Marc chewed for several seconds, wearing out his gums, then swallowed and spoke… this time without the jumbled mess of garlic, dough and saliva. “I said… Don’t blame this on me. The fact is… for all the pushing I’ve done to help get you into the spotlight, spearheading the war against FU… it was your crazy mind that decided it would be a good idea to call out The Deville. That was all you, Matt. You went out to the middle of the ring on national television and called out The Cadillac of Wrestling. And whaddayaknow… a couple weeks later, he was back and immediately a thorn in your side. All I did was push you to take on his little gang of buddies and give Nova this rematch.” “I’m beginning to wonder if I should just fire you.” “Try it.” Marc finished off his spagetti, sucking in the last of the long noodles, its tip flipping sauce everywhere as it whipped towards his lips. “Try it and see what happens.” no·va [noh-vuh] noun - a star that suddenly becomes thousands of times brighter and then gradually fades * * * Some concepts in life are hard to grasp. Some ideas fall flat or backfire on the individual that beget their existence. Take betrayal for instance. Every day, someone stabs somebody in the back. In other cases, there are people serving life sentences behind cold and lifeless steel because of betrayals they never committed. ‘The Rising Star’ was certainly not the former. In fact, he was very much a victim of the latter. Betrayal was a broken promise… for instance… a man promising his wife that he would retire from his dangerous profession, then turning around and diving even deeper into its grasp. Betrayal Betrayal was the creation of a façade. A distraction or feel good moment set up only to fuel the anguish of the burn of the knife as it dug deep in the flesh. Something like… a close friend smiling and making claims of ‘teamwork’ only to wrap a steel chair around the skull of his comrade just minutes later in the night. The Inhuman Being was certainly familiar with the concept of betrayal. On occasions too tedious to count, he had been the betrayer. Where he lacked experience with the subject… was on being the betrayed. And make no mistake… Nova had not betrayed him. The Rising Star had owed him nothing. Perhaps a friendship had started to form in the midst of a shared problem by the name of Olsig… but no promises for any sort of foundation had been made. No façade or false teamwork had been forged. Nothing of the sort. Plain and simply put… Nova didn’t betray Tchu. He probably didn’t even really betray the fans. Perhaps, the truth was the fans had betrayed Nova. They had claimed support for him over the course of years, then cheered his defeat with thunderous delight at The Great American Nightmare. They had chosen The Inhuman Being over the The Rising Star. For all the love and admiration they claimed… they had smiled and applauded while one man had robbed him of his destiny. Destiny. That was a truly intriguing idea. Destiny was a concept that no one could agree on. Some people thought it possible, but unlikely. Others believed firmly that every man had path laid out in front of him before his time and that fate would lead him down that path. Still, others thought it the stupidest, most asinine idea every fabricated from man’s mind. Destiny was even more polarizing than religion. Many people believed in God, many didn’t, many remained unsure. But all the same could be said about destiny, and even within specific camps, like individuals couldn’t agree. Some people believed in God and believed he had a plan for every one of his being. Some people believed in God, but believed in free will… that every man was capable of making his own decisions and crafting his own life. Destiny was too complicated to be real. What if two men were destined to be the first man to walk on the moon? Only one can be the first. What if Neil Armstrong wasn’t either one of those men. What if, in all their greed, those two men destroyed each other? And then there’s the problem of destiny in the hands of another being. When a person’s destiny requires experiencing, doing, or a capturing something that belongs to another… when it lies in the hands of another… is it truly destiny? When one man has control in the path another man travels in life… destiny dies. Perhaps, people just have it wrong. Maybe destiny exists… its just not what it seems. des·ti·ny [des-tuh-nee] noun - the power or agency that determines the course of events. * * * “No. I’m not doing this to piss you off. I’m doing this because it needs to be done.” Matt held his hands out in pleading fashion, but Mary just shook her head. “Ok… that… and because I’m an idiot and listened to Marc.” “What the hell are you talking about?” “I’m in a bad spot and its my fault… but I promise… I’m not going to go out there and get myself killed. And if I do… sue Marc.” “Matthew,” Mary took a step towards him and put her hands on his face, one on each cheek. She steadied his trembling and looked him solid in the eyes. “I’m concerned about you. You haven’t been making sense lately… you seem far more worried about wrestling than you ever have… and that doesn’t make me feel any better. I worry enough for the both of us. If you’re going to wrestle, I need you to stay strong and at least make me feel a little at ease.” “Jesus Christ… stop guilt tripping the guy, kiss him, support him… and shuddup!” Marc leaned forward, peeling himself from against the wall and strode to the middle of the room, interjecting himself in the middle of the couples conversation. “Just say ‘ok, do what ya gotta do, baby!’ and the let the man be.” Marc turned to Matt. “Are you serious, you just shrug your shoulders for her and say ‘sorry, yes dear, as you wish’?!” “Butt out, Marc” “Matthew… look at me when you’re talking.” Mary reached out and turned his chin, moving his gaze back into her eyes. “Tell me what’s going on, baby.” “I dunno…” “C’mon, Matt! Just say it! Get some balls!” Marc should have had pom-poms and a skirt. “I…” Matt looked back over his shoulder at Marc, then turned to Mary. “I’m going to King of Kings and I’m going to do the same damn thing I did at The Great American Nightmare.” “Good man!” Marc fired the thumbs up in his other half’s direction. Matt embraced Mary, a confident smile forming on his face. Mary wrapped her arms tightly around Matthew, afraid, confusion filling her eyes as she stared at the empty space behind him. Tchu [choo] noun - 1. The power or agency that determines the course of events 2. The one left shining when the star fades away.
