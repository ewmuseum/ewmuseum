Date: 166 BEEP BEEP BEEP We see Jimmy Bonafide, laying down on a hospital bed unconscious with tubes in his arm and in his nose. Then a young white nurse opens the door and enters the room, she grabs the chart that is at the end of his bed and reads it. She put’s it back, and walks up to bonafide. She then grabs a white towel that is laying open a steel tray on top of a small desk. She place’s it on to the forehead of Bonafide and whips the sweat right off of his head. She places the towel back onto the tray, and exits the room. BEEP BEEP BEEP BEEP The door open’s up for a second time, but this time it isn’t the young white nurse. Its two undercover detectives with a little white girl. They enter the room and the door closes, they walk up two the bed, and they begin to talk. “So is the man, sweetie?” “Yes, that is him mister.” “Are you sure it’s him?” The 2nd detective asks. “Yes. That’s him.” “So this is the man, who saved you life? He is the one who tried to save you from getting shot in that drive by?” “Yes, I was playing with my friends in the park next to the basketball courts. When these 3 men jumped out of a jeep with black mask’s on and start shooting our way, but they weren’t aiming for us. They we shooting at this group of guys that were playing basketball, listening to music and smoking weed. I guess we were just at the wrong place at the wrong time. Thank god no one got hurt, I couldn’t move I was in shock I felt like my feet were glued too the floor.” “Well you sure were at the wrong place, at the wrong time. Now as for no one being hurt, I wish I can say yes no one did get hurt. But there were two boys who got hit, some kids name Chich, and Chong. Ever heard of them?” “No, officer. All I know is, this is the man who saved my life. If it wasn’t for him I wouldn’t be here now.” “Well okay. Thank you for your time, we can leave now. We have a lot of paper work too fill out.” They all exit the room. As the door closes bonafide fingers begin to move slowly. [[FLASHBACK]] The scene opens up in a little deli store, bonafide enters the store and buys a colt 45. While he pulls out the money out his shorts to pay for his colt 45. He over hears two guys talking. “Yo, we gonna get him. Payback is a son of a bitch.” “What, what do you mean you seen him?” “Yeah, I just saw him.” “Where did you see him?” “He is at the park next too the basketball courts.” “Yeah, well let’s get him. What are we waiting for?” The two men rush out the store, in a hurry. Bonafide drops the money for the beer on the counter and walks out the store right behind them. The two men quickly jump into an all black expedition jeep. Bonafide, walks by the jeep and continues to walk up the block towards the park. He hears the engine of the jeep turn on he begins too walk a little faster towards the park, where he see’s a group of guys chilling playing basket ball and listing too music. And not too far are some little kids that are playing with a jump rope. He enters the park, and the black jeep begins to slowly drive up the block. Bonafide opens the beer and continue to walk towards the lil’ kids that are playing with the jump rope. The tires of the jeep are heard screeching towards the park, Bonafide turns around and see’s the widow of the jeep slowly lowering. He turns back around and runs and jumps to cover the girl who was about to jump. 8 shots ring out of 9mm clearing out the whole park, and leaving two men dead. And one with minor injuries. [[/FLASHBACK]] Bonafide wakes up, in a cold sweat and takes off all the tubes that were attached too him. He begins to breath heavy as the camera begins to fade to black. -Fin.
