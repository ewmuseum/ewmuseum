I Am Not My Brother's Keeper

11 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/11/05 ”I am not my brother’s keeper. I am, thou, my brother’s destroyer, his hatred, his angst, and his desire to eliminate his enemy. I am everything my brother wants to be, but isn’t strong enough to become. I am a champion. A man of my own destiny. I have never let another man, woman or child decide my fate. I have always caused the biggest ripples in the lake. Just like the night I convinced you Jon to come to PRIME to help me. The waves are still lapping the shore on that one.” ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- DATE: Saturday, April 9th, 2005LOCATION: Las Vegas, NevadaTIME: 7:15 pm We are inside of a casino. This is made evident by the people plopping money into the one-arm bandits just in front of us. We see old ladies jumping up and down as the win at video poker. Waitresses walk around, selling their wares. The action is hot, the money is hard to win. Except one table. “Hit me.” A familiar voice is heard. Ah, a typical sound in The City of Lights. The place where what happens here, stays here. The city where businessmen come to spend their company’s money, using it as collateral until they hit is big. Many of them don’t, and their souls are said to still haunt the casinos they lost it all in. “Dealer has 19, player has 20. Player wins. Good hand sir.” The blackjack dealer says. Most people come to Las Vegas for pleasure. It’s either a vacation with the family, friends or loved ones. Many come here to get married, choosing The King over a preacher at home. One thing is for sure, this city is like a drug. Once it’s in your system, it’s hell to get it out. “I’ll stand.” This time, we see the source of that familiar voice. Sitting at the blackjack table is the biggest problem that Jon Kano has ever faced. Chris Sloboda. But at second glance, we notice that it’s not the Boda we have seen in this business for the past 5 years. The blonde hair is gone, now a shade of midnight black. “Dealer hits.” The dealer takes a card. ”I’ve been here for two days now. Taking a break from my routine life of human sacrifices and animalistic rituals. I told Vampire and Furher that I would catch up to them in Seattle, in time to continue our reign of tyranny over PRIME. And I’ve been pretty lucky at the tables the last two days. “ “Dealer stands at 18. Player has 19. Player wins. Another good hand sir.” Says the blackjack dealer, this time with less enthusiasm as before. “Woo….” The crowd behind the big man whoops it up as Boda wins his hand. ”And I’ve gathered a crowd of onlookers. Businessmen that wish their luck were as good as mine. Gold digging bitches that want some of my money I’ve won over the last two days. But, I have other motives for being here the last two days.” “Excuse me sir. Phone call for you.” A voice from off to the left side says. Boda stands from the table. “Well, it’s been fun. But that’s the phone call I was waiting for.” The big man takes his collection of chips off the table. The crowd around him starts to disperse. A few females try to talk to Chris, but he brushes them off. Boda tosses the dealer a 1000-dollar chip. “Thanks for the fun.” Walking off from the table, Boda follows the well-dressed man that informed him about his phone call. After a walk across the casino floor, Boda stops and picks up the house phone. “Hello” Boda says. “Hello Mr. Sloboda. This is Robert Jenkins, from Kano Enterprises. I heard back from the owners of that house I showed you, and they said yes. 10.5 million is exactly perfect for them.” The caller says. Boda stands still, looking out of the glass walls that show the main drag of Las Vegas. “So, would you like to meet with me and the owner on Monday and close the deal?” “Well, Robert. I appreciate all you did for me these last two days.” Chris smiles as he talks. “I really like that house, but unfortunately, I took a phone call earlier, and I have to fly out of town tonight. I have a 11 pm flight, to go to Orlando. One of my children is very sick and I need to go tend to her. I’m sorry.” “Uhm…..” Robert gulps into the phone. “Are you sure? You mean you’re not going to buy the house? Damn, I thought you were a sure deal, and you would have been my first deal. Man, this sucks.” “Come on Robert.” Chris curls his lips. “How long you been in real estate? One month you told me. Well, you’ll sell a house sooner or later. You’re a natural. Just bad timing on my part man. Sorry.” “Well…. Okay. Now I guess Mr. Kano will have to wait for some new neighbors. And here I was so looking forward to attending our annual barbeque at Mr. Kano’s home, and you being there.” Robert seems a little happier. “I hope you’re kid is okay. And have a safe flight.” After hanging up, I’m immediately brought into an evil smile. “Does this kid not know who I really am? And here he is, showing me Kano’s house. It couldn’t have been any easier, could it have been?” We fade out with Boda smiling as he heads outside the casino and hailing a taxi. As an yellow and white-checkered cab pulls up, Chris opens the rear passenger side door, stepping in and taking a seat in the back all by himself. “Hey, I’m heading to………..” And the feed ends. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Date: Saturday, April 9th, 2005Location: Outside the Kano Residence, Las Vegas, NevadaTime: 10:12 pm The feed returns, showing the new location. A huge mansion outside of Las Vegas. The lights of the city glow in the distance, as Boda stands outside. ”Jon…. I know you’re watching this, since I am sending this to you courtesy of UPS. I’m sure you’re screaming at the television screen right now, as I stand outside, in the shadows, of your beautiful home in Las Vegas. And boy… Kristin looks lovely tonight.” The camera pans in, through the living room window, as Kristin, the wife of Jon Kano, sits on the couch, enjoying a hot beverage. Kristin looks up, out the window, as Boda moves back into the shadows. ”Wooo… that was a close call Jon. She almost saw me. Now Jon, you have to have figured out by now that I didn’t fly out to Vegas to spend money in a casino and to buy the home next door to you, now did ya? Well, I did plan on spending money at the casino, but I had other motives, other ideas for why I pretended to want to buy the house next door to you. Beside…. That house is a dump compared to my place out in Orlando.” We are shown the house next door. ”And I knew that by talking to the newest member of your company, that I’d be almost hand delivered to the location of your home. Since you have kept it a secret from me for the past 2 years. But wait Jon… what is this? An empty medicine bottle in my hand? And, look it’s sleeping pills too. But, Jon… I’m not tired. But, I know someone who might be.” The camera zooms back inside the house through the window. Kristin is seen now yawning on the couch, the hot beverage mug in her hand slipping, slipping, till she drops it on the floor. Shooting back up, we see Kristin is now asleep on the couch. ”That didn’t take long. Now, Jon, what should I do? What should I do? I know…. I’ll go check on her and make sure she’s all alright. After all, Jon, we were once like brothers, right? You’ve been talking up a storm about how you practically helped raise my little girls. So, brother…. Why shouldn’t I go inside and make sure my… sister… is okay.” Boda walks out of the shadows and through the front yard. Coming to the main entrance, Boda turns the knob and is surprised it is unlocked. ”Jon, you aught to tell you wife that she shouldn’t leave the doors unlocked. Especially at night, when you’re on the road. She might get in trouble you know. She might not know who will just walk right in.” Walking into the main foyer of the residence, Boda looks around, to make sure there isn’t any unsuspecting surprises, like a housekeeper, maid, dog with big teeth. When the coast is clear, he continues his trek to the living room, where he stops behind the couch that Kristin is asleep on. ”Look at her. She really is a sleeping beauty Jon. I always told you you did well for yourself with her.” Boda strokes his hand across her head, brushing her hair to the side. “When’s the last time you did this to her Jon? Before Culture Shock? Before I convinced you, my ‘brother’, to come to Japan and help me defeat my enemy. Did you caress your precious Kristin the night before you found out that you are my real enemy? Maybe I’ll do you a favor Jon. Maybe I’ll bring Kristin to Seattle with me, and reunite you two. After all, she is so innocent in this all, all she wants to see is her husband, you, safe and sound. So why not? And after you kiss her and caress her one more time, I think we could top the reunion off with a crucifixion. Yeah, Kristin would look good hanging from the cross.” Boda stands and mocks Jesus on the cross. Only with a sadistic smile that Jesus never had. Chris looks down a hallway just off to the left. “Jon, is that the way to your son’s room? Maybe I’ll go pay Junior a little visit.” Chris heads off down the hallway, stopping outside a room with a wooden placard that reads Jon Jr. Opening the door slowly, Boda pops his head in to see the spawn of Kano asleep in his bed. The big man steps in, quietly, and stands inside the door. “Look at him Jon. Your son. When’s the last time you played with him? Was it the day before you were destined to become my whooping boy in PRIME? Well, how’d you like to see him in Seattle? Because I can arrange that. Hell, mom and son would look great on the cross. And it’d make for a perfect sacrifice. Shh…. I should be a little quieter. He’s stirring, and well, I am the stuff that nightmares are made of.” Chris opens the door and steps back out into the hallway. “Don’t think for one minute Jon, as you watch this and are now screaming at the screen for me to leave you son out of this, that I won’t sacrifice him. DID YOU FORGET I USED MY OWN KIDS TO GET WHERE I AM? I don’t think you forgot that. After all, you came out on the ReVolution after Culture Shock and were awe stricken and shocked that I, you best friend for years, you brother, the man you looked up to for years, would do that to his own two girls. The same girls you played with when you stayed in Orlando with me. DON’T FORGET IT, I DID WHAT I DID TO GET WHERE I AM, AND I’LL DO IT AGAIN.” Chris turns his head, to look down the hallway. And the big man smiles at what he sees. For at the end of the hallway, is a glass case, with Army memorabilia. “Well, well….. Jon…. You really did love your brother Ken didn’t you?” Chris walks down the hallway and stops in front of the case. Inside we see various medals, photos and memorabilia of Ken Kano, the brother of Jon that dies in a freak demolitions accident in the Army. Chris opens the case and takes out the dog tags that hang from the top. “Jon, you don’t have to worry about Jon Jr or Kristin. They’re safe. I just came here to show you that you really don’t know me, and that you better really start fearing me. You’ve beaten me before, I’ve whooped your ass too. We’ve had battles, we’ve waged war…. But this week, at ReVolution…. The animal that I have become will be to much for you to handle.” Chris puts the dog tags over his head and around his neck. “Because the last thing you want to be is stuck in a steel cage with a man that doesn’t care. I could easily end your happiness and family right here, right now. But I don’t care if you’re happy or not. I don’t need your family to be in Seattle for a crucifixion. Because Jon…. Whether I beat you or not…. And I’m positive I will…. You can bet on one thing after our match is over……That I’ll hang you from our cross personally, and crucify you in the name of The Dark Age. And these dog tags will be placed around your neck so when you go to Hell… your brother Ken can recognize you. So it is written….. You better bet your ass it will be done. See you in Seattle….. Brother.” And just like that, we are left in complete darkness.
