Thou Shall Not Murder

16 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 16th, 2007 "Sit down." The voice rings hallow, void of emotion, but the meaning behind it is rather real. With the cocked barrel staring him straight in the face, not flinching in the slightest, Craig has no choice but to heed the words of the man behind the gun, and sit in the chair behind him. Yet, Craig’s eyes are locked fiercely on the man holding the gun, a man he thought he had rid himself of; a detective known simply to him as Daniel. Daniel: You’re a fool. Craig: I’m thinking that I’m not inclined to disagree with you as you point that gun in my face. Daniel: The rest of you… His eyes move towards Nicole, Katie, and Dave, anger radiating from them, and they remain frozen in time, too scared to move. Daniel: Sit down. Craig: Leave them out of this. Daniel: Sit! Down! Craig: I said leave them out of this! Daniel: Sit the FUCK down! Craig: LEAVE THEM OUT OF THIS! With the roar from his voice, Craig leaps out of his chair, but the gun stares him straight in the face. Daniel is cool, calm, and collected as he stares Craig down. Craig wants to act, wants to fight back, but he’s held away at an arm’s length, stuck between life and death. If Daniel kills him, then there will be nothing to stop him from killing those that he loves. He has to remain calm and let the actions run its course because Craig’s the only who can get them out of this situation alive. Daniel: You might want to start choosing your actions very wisely here, Craig. There’s only one way that this movie ends, and it’s not the way you will want it to be. Craig: This script’s been written for a long time Daniel, so if you’re going to do it, then just get it over with. Daniel: Not going to be that easy, Craig. So sit down. Craig looks at Daniel and then at Nicole who is visibly frightened by the scene around her. He then sits down once again, his body tense, and ready for action. Daniel: I imagine you’re not used to being told what to do. You do whatever the hell you feel like, whenever the hell you feel like it. Not tonight though. Tonight, you come down from your majestic mountain and get the punishment that you’ve deserved for such a long time. Craig: And you’re the executioner? The Prince of Peace? Who gave you that kind of power in life which you so richly don’t deserve? Daniel: No one gave it, Craig. Instead, I took it FOR myself. I took it UPON myself. Craig: You took it upon yourself? Daniel: To do what countless others have been unable to do; kill you. Nicole: No… Craig: Nicole… Nicole: No, who is this man? What the hell is going on--? Yet, she never gets a chance to finish her thoughts as the man produces another gun from his belt and points it at her. The anger inside of Craig’s chest just roars, but he is able to keep it maintained. Craig: Daniel, you’re issue is with me. Leave her out of it. Daniel looks back at Craig. Daniel: No, Craig. Your death won’t be satisfying enough. Destroying the precious little world that you’ve cultivated and maintained is going to be the sweetest part of that. After all of the lives that you’ve destroyed, it will be sweet irony. Craig: Once again, you’ve taken it upon yourself to be Judge, Jury, and Executioner? Weren’t satisfied with the judge’s remarks of how your case was so loosely put together? Daniel: Screw her! She was too blind to see the monster that sits at the foot of the devil! Craig: And yet you can see him? Daniel: He sits before me, doesn’t he? Craig just sits there in silence. Daniel: Now tell them to sit down; before I have to shoot somebody. Craig looks over at Nicole and Katie, and nods his head. Craig: Just do as he says, it’s going to be okay. Daniel: Like hell it will. Nicole and Katie reluctantly sit down in chairs, but Dave just remains standing. Craig looks at Dave awkwardly and then Daniel looks at Dave before looking back at Craig. Daniel: What, is he an idi--? But, he never gets a chance to finish that statement as Dave rushes at Daniel. He catches Daniel off guard, but Daniel quickly regains his balance and slams the butt of the gun across Dave’s face as he slumps to the ground in a world of pain. Craig just watches on, unable to move as the gun remains pointed in his face. Daniel: What kind of STUPIDITY do you surround yourself with, Craig?! Huh?! Daniel then points the other gun right at Dave and looks intent on pulling the trigger. Craig: What’re you doing Daniel?! Daniel: He doesn’t deserve to live anymore! Craig: You’re not the Daniel I once knew. Daniel: And you’re not the man I originally thought you were either. Craig: You originally thought that I kidnapped or killed somebody. Daniel: And then you came out to be an honest and honorable man, or so I thought. Craig: Daniel… Daniel: But, you weren’t were you? Craig: Daniel, don’t. Daniel looks at Craig and then at Nicole. Daniel: You know that I’ve been watching you, Craig? Of course not. Though, this morning you finally had the weird feeling you get when you feel like you’re being watched. I just chuckled to myself as I watched you as you looked around in vein, hoping to find what was throwing you off. For a week, I’ve just watched and waited, learning everything there is to know about you. Daniel then looks at Katie. Daniel: Long time, Katie. How have you been? Katie: Go to hell. Daniel: Oh, I will be going to hell, trust me. Still harboring that crush on Craig? Katie’s look turns to ice. Daniel: Will you still be harboring it if you knew that Craig was--? Craig: Daniel! Daniel: Will you still be harboring it if you knew that Craig was fucking the girl sitting next to you? The look on Katie’s face is priceless as she looks at Craig in shock, almost not wanting to believe it. Craig sighs loudly as he looks down at the ground, hoping to have told Katie later when he thought she’d be able to handle it a little better. Katie: Craig… He doesn’t respond. Katie: Craig, look at me, damnit! He continues to look at the ground. In front of him, Craig can feel Daniel’s smile filling the entire room. In the pit of his stomach, Craig wishes he could smack it off. Daniel’s going to enjoy doing this, shredding each facet of his life before brutally ending it. Katie: Nicole? I thought you were my friend! How long?! Nicole: Katie… Katie: No, damnit! Don’t Katie me! Don’t talk to me like that, as if you’re my friend! Nicole: Katie, please… Katie: No. Craig! The fire in his eyes has turned cold. He looks up at Katie and as she looks at him, the both share the same coldness. Craig: A month. Katie: You kept this from me for a month? You told me this would be a business dinner and it was really a date?! Daniel: Isn’t this beautiful? The attention then returns to Daniel who stands in the middle of the room, beaming. Craig: Are you happy now? Daniel: Not in the least bit. Craig: Then, let’s talk about you, Daniel. Daniel: Craig, we’re not here for me. Craig: Oh, but we are. This whole thing, Daniel, was written in stone. Daniel: Just like your wife’s murder was? Once again, silence fills the room as Nicole’s eyes begin dashing all around, lost by Daniel’s words. Nicole: What? You had a wife? Daniel: Oh, surprise, surprise. The secrets that lies hidden in Craig’s closet. Didn’t decide to share that with the woman who shares your bed? When did you feel that would come up? After you get married? When your hand was around her throat and suffocating the life out of her? The look on Craig’s face is murderous as he just eyes Daniel with malice. Daniel: Nicole, I’m going to be your friend and confidant right now. Let me tell you about the man that you’re crazy for. Did he tell you that he used to be essentially a hit man for a crime syndicate? The number of people he’s murdered over the years is over a hundred people. Then, he murdered the one person who loved him despite him not having a soul. Nicole then looks at Craig who just sits there, as the rage and anger just continues to intensify. Nicole: Is that true, Craig? He doesn’t answer. Nicole: Damnit, don’t ignore me! I trusted you! He looks down at the ground and then back up at Daniel. Craig: Happy now? Daniel: Oh, one more piece to the puzzle and happiness will be mine, Craig. Craig then looks at Nicole. Craig: It’s true. It’s all true. I could come up with a litany of reasons, but the truth is that I did it. I have to live with those demons in my life every single day. Not a day goes by that it doesn’t make me ill. Every day I live, hoping to erase those demons, but it never happens. I don’t get to live a day guilt free, Nicole. I didn’t tell you because I’m not interested in sharing that piece of me. You shouldn’t have to be burden with my past. Nicole: Bullshit, you don’t get to make that decision for me! You don’t get to make me fall for you and then hit me with the ugly truths whenever the hell you feel like it! Craig: Don’t Nicole; you don’t know what it’s like! Nicole: That’s because you chose not to share it with me! Craig: Then you try to be me. I was fucking brainwashed and this piece of shit before me decides to throw it in my face?! I didn’t have control over anything and yet I still have to live with the truths and consequences of it all! You don’t, Nicole. So hate me all you want now, but I was trying to protect you from it. Nicole: I’m grown, Craig. I don’t need you to protect me. Daniel: Oh now, lovers, lovers, let’s not get all testy. We haven’t reached our grand finale quite yet. His voice is dry and serious though, contradictory of his words. Craig: You’re right. Let us go to the grand finale. Daniel: Accepting of death so easily? Craig: You think it makes you a better person to kill me? To ruin my life? Daniel: You RUINED mine! Craig: Tough shit. Live with it. Daniel: What did you say to me? Craig: You heard me. Daniel: Big words coming from a man whose death will send him to the depths of hell. Remember the fifth commandment? Thou Shall Not Kill! Craig: It’s Thou Shall Not Murder! Craig once again explodes out of his chair. Craig: You want to prosecute me, that’s fine. At least make sure you know what the hell you’re doing it for! Do not make this a religious mission and misquote the Ten Commandments. Do not stare me in the face and tell me what I did was murder because it was the complete opposite! There was never any malice in my heart, Daniel, and that’s the difference between you and me. I see it in your eyes. A killer can always read another killer. The difference between you and I is that you’ve committed murder with pure malice in your heart. Another silence fills the room, but now there’s a certain fire to Craig as he stares down the gun, knowing he can win now. Craig: All of this that you’ve done, shredding my life into pieces, you did it with malice. I might have ruined your life, but never did I have any intent on doing so. You went about it the complete opposite. Two men walking different paths in life and yet it’s you, the one destined to do good that has gone down the wrong path. Not me. The one mistake you made, Daniel, is that you took away the things that I live for. Daniel: What do you mean? Craig: You’ve alienated the people I love and trust. Now, I have nothing to live for. You know how dangerous I am when I do have something to live for? Imagine how dangerous I am when I have nothing to live for. Daniel: Shut up. Craig: Tell me about the person, Daniel! Daniel: Shut up! Craig: Tell me about them! It was a she, wasn’t it?! Daniel: I swear, if you don’t shut up! Craig: Live with it, Daniel! LIVE WITH IT! Admit to yourself what you did! Daniel: I swear, right fucking now, shut up! Craig: You can’t do it, can you? You can’t live with what you’ve done! You think it’s easy to blame me, but what’s going to happen when you kill me? You think that guilt is just going to wash away? Trust me, it’s not. You’re going to have to live with it every single day. Except yours will be worse. Killing hundreds with no malice and killing one person with pure malice in your heart is the exact same kind of pain. Daniel: I told YOU! SHUT! THE! FUCK! UP! The cold metal presses against Craig’s forehead and he’s certain that death awaits him. Yet, he doesn’t flinch or fight back. He stands there strong and valiant as he stares into the soul of a man that’s shattered. It’s what murder does to a person. Before, Daniel was calm and collected. Not anymore. His hand is shaking and his lips are tight as he fights to keep his eyes open. Craig: Do it. Katie: Craig… Craig: No more, Katie. Daniel, do it. If you really believe that it’ll set you free, then do it. But, I promise you, it won’t. That pain will never go away Daniel. Daniel just looks at Craig. Daniel: Yes, it will. I know it will. Craig: You’re going to revenge their deaths? Daniel: Yes. Craig: These people who have killed for pleasure, sold drugs to kids, and were basically the bottom of the rung low-life’s? You want to revenge their lives. Daniel: I— Craig: You don’t. I know you don’t. But, go ahead and do it Daniel. Instead, tears begin to stream down Daniel’s face. Craig: You can’t though. One, because you can’t bring yourself to do it again. You’ve tried to block it out, but you can’t can you? Daniel shakes his head. Craig: Two, because that’s not how the script goes, Daniel. You don’t kill me. It’s just not the way it’s meant to be. Daniel slowly lowers the gun, but Craig doesn’t make any kind of movements. Craig: So, what’re you going to do? Daniel: The only thing I can do, Craig. Daniel then puts the gun to his head. Craig: What’re you doing?! Daniel: You’ll never forget this one, Craig. You’ll never forget me. My death will be on your conscious until the day you die. You’ll remember the one life you ruined forever. Craig: Daniel— Daniel: It’s how the script was written Craig. This is one script you’ll always remember. Then, the bullet explodes out of the chamber, sending a loud echo throughout the house. Katie and Nicole flinch away, unable to watch it all happen. Craig though just stands there and looks at the man lying in the pool of blood at his feet. Craig: No, I won’t. Craig then looks over at Katie and Nicole. He doesn’t know what to say them as they’re visibly shaken from the entire ordeal. Katie’s the first one to look at him and then looks down at Dave who is in sheer disbelief. Katie: You need help up? Dave: Yeah. Katie slowly helps Dave up as Craig just stands in the middle of it all, gone from their consciousness for now. Dave has a black bruise on his jaw, but nothing appears broken. Katie walks with Dave up to the door, but stops next to Craig for one moment. Craig: Katie… Yet, she doesn’t respond as she just walks away and out of the door with Dave. Craig can only shake his head. He turns and looks at Nicole who is just standing there. Craig wishes he could find the words to make everything right with her. Yet, there are none. Nicole: I fell for you Craig and you broke my heart. Craig: Nicole. Nicole: No. I thought you were a good man, Craig. I really thought you were. Maybe misunderstood and very protective of yourself, but never did I imagine this. I just… can’t. Craig: Nicole that was a past that I’m leaving behind me. I didn’t have complete co— Nicole: If you didn't have complete control, then why didn’t you tell me? A question he doesn’t have an answer for. Nicole stands there for a second before walking up to Craig. They look at each other before she walks away, forever. As the door slams shut, he knows that Daniel’s done exactly what he planned from the very start. Destroy everything he truly loved. He’d have to start all over. The End.
