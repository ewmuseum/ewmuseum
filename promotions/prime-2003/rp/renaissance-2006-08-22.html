Renaissance

22 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/22/2006 'Their minds have been poisoned by lies. Untruths spread by a faithless regime seeking to spread not salvation but fear. And the saddest fact...' Violence Jack turned to face his stoic disciples. They hung on every word of his wicked sermon, a gospel disputed by any who called themselve sane. But those under his sway were assuredly not completely rational. The acts of violence and blasphemy in the Sect of Black Wisdom's past echoed the depths of their depravity. He shook his head, despondent.'The saddest fact is that the regime I speak of was created amidst a dark era of humanity where the world cried out for a modicum of solace. Those who call our doctrine a collection of evil lies continue to follow a dead system instituted to offer solace, control, and fear. And they were admittedly brilliant in maintaining all three simultaneously.' Bruce Shanahan was preparing to leave Wyatt Manor, the Sect's home and sacred hall, for PRIME's next show. He realized the importance of keeping his legion of apostles convicted to the cause in his absence, and had set about delivering his last address before he departed the mansion with his top subordinates, Ethan Knight and Horace Tully. Even now, as he stood at the pulpit in the spacious conference chamber, two of his followers waited outside with bags stashed in the car. Knight and Tully, the most trusted of Shanahan's dark cult, acted as sentinels to either side of their conniving mastermind. His body was beginning to break down after so many years of abuse, but there was no doubt his mind was as sharp as ever. Tully's eyes passed across the hooded congregation just as VJ turned to look on him, swiveling around to look upon Knight as well His expression was one of approval. 'Believers will never die. We will only go on to serve even more in the world after this, as we set foot upon the courts of the great daemon-sultan Himself. Some of us will simply be more in favor than others. We attain that favor through faith, through dedication, and through destroying those who may undermine what we seek to bring forth into this world that has been stricken by greed and heresy.' He paused, his venomous words bringing further hope to a gathering of the damned, many young and coerced by a man who appealed to their angst and rage. He grinned, his upper lip twisted into a vile symbol of the shadowy dogma he preached. 'Tomorrow we continue to show the world what empowers our crusade for the truth. And through Brother Knight, they shall see not him, but Them From Beyond the Stars. They will watch the harlot Lindsay Troy fall beneath our strength, and when that jackal falls silent and can howl no longer, we will tear her apart. And through our judgment, people shall come to slowly see the truth. They will see that though reality may be bleak, the illusion cannot be hidden forever. And only by pulling back that veil of perceived comfort will they truly be awakened.' Nods from the assembly became murmured agreements, and those murmurs evolved into a deafening chorus of confirmation, some of the more avid of the masses echoing primal bellows that should have been produced from the jaws of an enraged beast as opposed to the mouth of a man. Tully and Knight watched with pride, Shanahan musing on his next words as the thunder of his zealots drowned out his words. The swelling in his heart compensated for the lack of sanity in his mind. 'We stand at the threshold of a new renaissance! Soon we will hold the key to breaking down the walls that have confined our kind for so long and we will bring a world long-beguiled back to its senses! The guilty shall suffer for their ignorance and the favored shall prosper! What remains shall be nothing but a burnt-out necropolis. The Old Ones' memorial to a damned human race as they reign from thrones beyond the heavens.' Knight's eyes deviated from Shanahan's rabid flock to a fresco covering most of the wall behind him. It was a portrait of Nyarlathotep in his Black Pharaoh form, a burning landscape set in the background. As Violence Jack continued his fiery speech, Ethan focused on the humanoid avatar of the monstrous god and the scorched tomb he would leave in his wake. He thought of the doubters and the naysayers, of the wretched idealists and hate-driven zealots. And above all, right now, could visualize Lindsay Troy. He could picture that smirking bitch's face as her and Melton condemned the Great Old Ones and spit on Their gospel. And how she mocked them with that belt on her shoulder... 'Brother Knight!' He shrugged off the infuriating image and looked to Tully and Shanahan. They all stared at him as if he'd just gunned down the president. For a moment he found himself wondering why, but soon found himself stumbling over words instead. It had taken him a long moment to spy to hole in the wall. The one he had put there only moments ago, distant in his fury. Distant in his thirst for righteousness against the nonbelievers. He found himself trying to stammer out an apology only to be cut off by a wave of the hand from Father Shanahan. He didn't look the least bit ruffled by the damage to his glorious mural. In fact, he looked more than satisfied. 'Let PRIME feel the terror we can and shall unleash. In time new blood will rally to our cause and support the true power of the universe. I implore you all to share the conviction that Brother Knight has proven, and together...' He turned to Knight and Tully. His smile was broad, the grin of a man both joyous and maniacal at the same time. '...PRIME shall fall under the dominion of The Sect of Black Wisdom and the Old Ones. Blessed are we, the damned. Amen.' Their time had nearly arrived. And it was Ethan who would start the revolution. He would quell the laughter by strangling the life from Lindsay Troy.
