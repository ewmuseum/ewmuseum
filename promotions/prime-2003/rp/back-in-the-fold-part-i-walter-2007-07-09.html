Back In The Fold Part I: Walter

9 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th July 2007 Out of the corner of his eye, he realised that Walter was still staring right at him with that stupid grin he always wore hung gormlessly across his face shamelessly. Winters couldn’t help but sigh and shake his head; if Walter had once again been drafted into Cornelius Stakiri’s inner cabal that could possibly spell doom for them all… “Am I offending you, Mr. Winters?” Stakiri questioned, his fire-filled eyes aimed directly at him. Winters shook his head no and made damn sure his eyes didn’t wander back over to Walter’s general vicinity. “Good,” Stakiri said. “I take it I now have your approval to continue?” The sarcastic undertones of Stakiri’s voice were not lost on Winters, he just simply ignored them. The way he saw it, his employer had every right to be pissed off with him, especially considering their recent unstable relationship and the whole Aeryn situation. “We all need to take a step back and focus,” Stakiri continued, unlatching his eyes from Winters vicinity and his gaze now wandering the room. “The advances those OmniTech-sponsored thugs made last month have only just been recovered and at great expense. I think it’s safe to assume that a reprisal will be heading our way soon.” Stakiri had been talking at them for the better part of an hour already and Winters was bored, had been for the better part of fifty-nine minutes. What their illustrious employer wasn’t telling them, was that the enemy hadn’t been doing anything of any note for the better part of three weeks now and that situation didn’t look like changing anytime soon. Once their undercover mole had failed in her task to assassinate Stakiri outright, it seemed as though the OmniTech funded opposition didn’t have any more plays to make. Much to Winters dismay, that undercover mole turned out to be his supposed lover, Aeryn, the woman who originally brought Winters into the fold in the first place and after subduing her attack, Stakiri’s men had come to the conclusion that her mind had been damaged, perhaps even irrevocably. Turned out, OmniTech had used advanced brainwashing techniques the likes of which the CIA were reportedly experimenting with and they’d wedged in there pretty good by all accounts; they couldn’t remove the programming without removing the majority of Aeryn’s vital memories. Of course, Stakiri’s natural reaction was to forget about trying to restore her to her former self and simply dissect her; to just carve her brain up like she was some damn lab right and extract the relevant information right then and there. Winters politely informed Stakiri that wasn’t happening and when his employer tried anyway, Winters took her away from them and went on the lamb. The thing of it was, Winters needed Stakiri just as much as his illustrious needed him and a couple of weeks later, the two of them had managed to broker a deal of sorts. Stakiri’s highly paid researches will try to sift through what’s left of Aeryn’s programming and the possible vital information regarding the enemy held therein in a far more humane way than slicing her head open and scrutinising the contents of her brain. As part the deal, Stakiri had brought Winters back to the fold to aid in his war, but that didn’t mean he was out of Stakiri’s bad books. Far from it. Knowing Stakiri, he’ll utilise Winters special talents and then find a way to fuck him over in conjunction with his services. He always was a devious old bastard. “So I want extra patrols out there,” Stakiri said. “I want a report on everything, and I do mean everything, that’s happening on MY streets and I want to see any such reprisals coming a mile away.” He paused and glanced around the room pointedly. “Do I make myself clear?” “Yes, sir!!!!,” the majority of the room responded. Winters simply nodded along and filtered out of the room, desperately trying to exit the confined space before Walter even got close. “Mr. Winters,” Stakiri called after him, just centimetres from the door. Winters sighed and turned, begrudgingly. “Yeah?” Stakiri shot him his trademark shark-like grin. “I have a little task for you…” Winters raised a speculative eyebrow, also noting that Walter had now positioned himself right next to him. Winters ignored him. “What is it?” “You know Walter I take it?” His employer asked, his grin still vicious. Winters glared at Walter for a second and then back at Stakiri. “No.” “Aw, man!” Walters sparked into life. “How can you say that, man? I hang at the company gym all the time! Don’t you remember me? I‘m your biggest fan, I--” “Walter, you weigh three hundred pounds,” Stakiri interjected. “You’ve never been near a gym in your life and we both know it.” Winters sighed and lowered his head. “No, he does. He stands by the water cooler and talks at people.” Walter winked at both Stakiri and Winters in unison, before adding, “I’m also there for the ladies!” “Walter, the last time you had sex with anything or anyone was when your sister was still alive,” Stakiri responded. Walter lowered his head sullenly. “Yes, Mr. Stakiri.” “Now,” Stakiri continued unabashed, “I’m putting the two of you on patrol tonight.” He closed in on Winters and patted him on the shoulder. “He’s my sisters boy. You’ll watch out for him, won’t you?” Winters sighed. “Whatever.” Walter punched the air triumphantly. “YES!!!!” *** “Look, Winters!” Walter said, his arm firmly wrenched around his hapless victims neck. “I got one! Let’s get him!” Winters sighed and folded his arms. “You think so?” Walter glared at him grinning, planting a couple of well-placed punches to the gut of his victim. “Definitely OmniTech! I can smell those bastards a mile away!” Winters rubbed the bridge of his nose soothingly and shook his head. “So you think he’s an undercover agent posing as a homeless guy then, huh?” Walter’s brow furrowed and released the man from the headlock. “You don’t?” “I think OmniTech are a little more cunning than that,” Winters educated, “don’t you?” Walter sighed and allowed the hobo to break free of his fat-handed grasp. “I guess. So Johnny, what happened on Revolution One-two-two was pretty wild, huh?” Winters shook his head, jammed both hands into his thin jacket pocket and walked on along the street. They’d been out there an hour already and during which time, Walter hadn’t shut up about how much of a fan he was of Winters and his wrestling career. Winters was hating every damn minute of it. “I mean you got right into the crowd and everything,” Walter continued. “A few of those fuckwits in Pheonix even got a few bruises along the way, didn’t they? Man, I bet you enjoyed that the best, right? Am I right?” Winters nodded thoughtfully. “Well at one point, I grabbed this chair and--” Winters was interrupted by a masked individual suddenly springing out the shadowy alleyway. Walter glared at Winters reaction questioningly, just before the stealthy individual grabbed the fat man around the neck and hauled him backwards. “Get your red commie hands off me you homosexual donkey fuckers!” Walter kicked and screamed all the way. “Fucking great,” Winters sighed, strolling forward in pursuit. Just as he did, another black garbed individual appeared. Then another and another. Soon there were half a dozen and Walter’s curses were fading somewhere in the distance. Winters did not halt in his tracks immediately, but was quickly forced as a roundhouse kick of one of them pounded against his chest. It was good technique, Winters knew that much and it was a safe to bet that the rest of the fuckers were just as were just as well verse.
