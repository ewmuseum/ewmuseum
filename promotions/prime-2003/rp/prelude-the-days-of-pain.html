Prelude: The Days of Pain

Date: Various times Sometime around Christmas ”Elf elf baby!” STRIKE! “Are you still playing that game?” A smiling Rachelle said as she was wrapping the last couple Christmas presents as she sat on the floor. “Hey, Elf Bowling rules and you should be happy that I’m actually getting in the holiday mood.” “I am.” Rachelle stood up, stretched and made her way over to the computer where the Ego was sitting. She wrapped her arms around his neck, helping to break his concentration and causing him to bowl a split of the rather silly looking elves who proceeded to moon him when he missed. “I’m also happy that you decided to finally invite your parents over to your place. I have to say it was kinda weird that your own parents had no idea where you lived.” “Believe me, I had my reasons for that.” SPARE! “I don’t get involved with that.” She mumbled as she walked back over to the finished gift, which she whisked away and put under the tree. “But what about Chad?” “What the fuck about him? He won’t be here this holiday either. Apparently he is coming soon after the holidays.” “Well don’t get too bent out of shape. You’ve knocked his block off according to your sister once already. This extra training would allow you to hurt him pretty bad.” Rachelle came back to sit on the desk beside Nitz, crossing her legs. “He opens his mouth, he fuckin’ loses teeth. It’s really that simple.” “That’s not very festive baby.” She said, teasing him a little. She knew his limits. “Then let me play Elf Bowling a little longer and maybe I’ll calm down to a point where I don’t wanna see him in pain.” “STRIKE!” “Fair enough.” With a kiss on the cheek, she proceeded to the kitchen to check on the food. “What time did your parents say they were coming?” “Hey Santa, I’ve got crabs!” “Excuse me?!” Rachelle questioned. |~o~| January 3, 2008 “Hit it! Hit it!!!” *thump thump thump* “Stop screaming in my damn ear Blair.” Nitz said as he smashed his fists into the punching dummy. It wobbled around a little before he wound up and fired half a dozen more shots into the body and the face of it. The sweat was beading on his forehead and glistening on his bare arms. There was no question about it, Donnelly was in the best shape of his life. Besides a short break over the holidays, Nitz was training non-stop. Blaine had developed the program based on the Ego’s physical weakness. His strength was never a strong point, but Blaine had changed that. Much of the same could be said about his striking and his ground game. He knew that Donnelly could wear guys out and knock them down with his speed and aerial talent, but he needed the ability to finish the job. Nothing works better than a good ground and pound. He’d need more than just speed the first ReVolution back anyways. “I’ve got you up against Walczak the first ReV back on the 9th.” Blair stated, moving to watch Donnelly place a powerful kick into the side of the dummy three times. “He won’t know what hit him.” Nitz threw numerous shots into the rubber face. He finally finished it up with a lethal uppercut. Blaine was proud of himself. He was never the wrestling kind of guy, he just fell into the gig in PRIME by accident. Now he was the boss and his confidence was high. He knew that he could get Donnelly into better shape. He didn’t like to admit it, but he saw the potential of the high flyer when he first showed up. But his mouth and his hate of people had held him back. He didn’t want anyone’s help. Finally, he surrendered and Blair began that task of moulding the Ego into a new kind of animal. He was happy with his work. The smile was always... “What the fuck is with that shit eating grin?” Nitz said, looking straight into the eyes of the CEO. Blaine broke out of his trance and watched his trainee throw more punches at the dummy with determination on his face. “Nothing. But I do have to wonder what’s going through that crazy head of yours as you obliterate that training dummy.” Blaine asked as he circled the area once again. “You don’t wanna know.” “Sure I do.” “Go get some glasses, a blonde wig and one of your shirts to dress this thing up with and you’ll see what I think about.” “I see. Not too thankful of the help I’m giving you huh?” Blair questioned as he crossed his arms. “Sure I am. Everyone has their different ways of being thankful though. Ask my brother.” “Again, I don’t get involved with family matters of my wrestlers.” Blair grabbed a water bottle and handed it to Donnelly as he finished his routine. “But my sister said you were cute.” “You... serious?” Blaine said, looking somewhat hopeful that Nitz was telling the truth. Nitz threw the empty water bottle to the side. “Yeah fuckin’ right. HA!” Donnelly laughed as he threw more punches into his own personal image of a Blaine Blair punching dummy. |~o~| January 9, 2008 He got to the arena early on purpose. He didn’t want to be followed, questioned or bothered. He didn’t want Faith trying to find him again to ask more questions or perhaps to flash her tits at him for a story. Mind you, after what The Ego said to her previously, she may not be coming around him much in the near future. He broke through the double doors with his bag at his side. The flaps of the leather coat flopped around has he walked. It was a powerful and confident stride. Quite different from the night he entered an arena in Canada to fold to the pressures of the CEO. Some even wondered that with his new found fitness, how long he would be “loyal” to Blair. Donnelly was certain though, Blair got him where he was and a contract was within his reach. He wasn’t about to risk the guaranteed payday. As he approached the dressing room that he would be using, his cell phone started to go off. He ducked into the room swiftly and shut the door behind him. He reached into his inner pocket and flipped open the phone that displayed Rachelle’s number. “What the hell? I told you I didn’t want to be seen before my match. Half the staff probably heard my phone go off!” “Sorry babe, I just needed to tell you something. Chad is in town.” Rachelle said from the other end. She was still in Boston, but there were plans in place for her to join him on the upcoming road trip. “And I care why?” “Just figured you should know. He said something to your mom about taking a leave from his work in Seattle and staying here a bit longer.” The signal started to fade in and out but The Ego certainly could make out the words. “Great. All the more reason to NOT want to fuckin’ come home after this show for a quick visit.” “He sounded a little weird though babe. Your Dad was even wondering if something else was wrong.” She said with a little worry in her voice. She remembered all too well how Chad reacted when he found out the hard way that his brother was dating his ex. “The bastards got a whole whack of shit wrong with him. I’m hardly surprised.” He said as he set his coat and bag down on the bench beside the locker in his room. “Either way, good luck tonight and I’ll see you back here in a couple days.” She said as she blew him a kiss and they said their goodbyes. His brother was back in town and sounded a tad bit screwed up. But he couldn’t let that bother him. That would be later. Now was the time to worry about Walczak and his first match of the year, not to mention to unleash his new found fury. Home had to wait. This was the first night of the best years of his career.
