To be Ace.

20 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.20.07 “Do you know what you’re going to say?” “What?” The producer, had caught Harley off guard, he was daydreaming. “Do you know what you’re going to say? What you’re going to talk about?” Harley glanced at him a moment, puzzled. It took him a moment to gather up his thoughts to know what the producer was talking about. “Yeah… I don’t.” “Lovely,” the producer said. “What? You expect me to have it all written down in a little paper and be practicing it over and over in my mind or something?” Harley shot back. “that’s not how I do business. I’m a master procrastinator, if I do anything in advance, it ends up sucking. I need to be in front of the camera, ready to shoot, and if everything goes well, then it does. If it doesn’t, then it doesn’t. I can’t be bothered to practicing this bullsh*t.” “Then I can’t be bothered filming it.” Harley was angered at first, then calmed down and smiled. He had a point. “Touché.” “Alright, you ready to shoot now?” “I’m never ready, give me the five count before starting.” 5… 4… 3… 2… 1… ***** "When you're looking into the wrestling world from a bird's-eye view, everything looks so simple." "You have your separate little kingdoms spanning the world, some smaller than others, some don't even quantify as kingdoms, they quantify as villages that don't really matter in the grand scheme of things, these villages can come and go, and no one will notice, or more appropriately, care, what happens to these places. Of course all these kingdoms and villages across the wrestling world all claim to have a champion. They all have their knight-in-shining-armor, who they believe can defeat the other champions from other kingdoms, so they decree him, not only the champion of their village slash kingdom, they decree him the champion... of the world." "They don't even have to believe that their champion can defeat other champions, all they have to believe is that their kingdom, their small little world... this world that encapsulates their lives and makes them believe is safe and the most powerful of kingdoms, is all that matters, and that's why he's their champion, is World Champion. Of course, it's debatable whether or not he's the Champion of the World, as in the Earth... World, or Champion of their World, their little blocked off world where they feel safe and comfortable and dare not move outside the protective glass orb…” ***** “Ok, Cut!” The Producer had cut Harley off mid-sentence. “What the hell’s wrong with you?!” Harley was exasperated. “What the hell is wrong with me? I’m asking the same thing about you. I don’t what drug you’re on right now, Harley, but if it’s anything stronger than Mary Jane, then you shouldn’t be shooting. That promo was bordering on Ultimate Warrior status, all you were missing was a rant about the heavens and the gods, which you probably would have gone into if I didn’t stop you.” Harley chuckled at the Ultimate Warrior reference. “It was a masterpiece.” The producer raised an eyebrow. “Nah, I’m kidding, it sucked. But you know I suck at these right?” The producer stayed silent. He had seen this kid go from a stuttering, mumbling rookie who sweated bucketloads before, during, and after each promo he cut to a refined wordsmith… well ok, that’s an exaggeration. He saw him become a micworker that could get by and do a good enough job of putting over himself, his opponent, and the event they were participating in. So it wasn’t that he agreed with him, it was just that he always gave him his way. “Yeah,” the producer smiled. “You do suck.” Harley, who had been feeling awkward after the producer took a long time to respond, smiled back. “Alright then, help a brother out,” Harley said. “What should I say? Alex Creed sucks, Harley Douglas for Life?” Harley flashed the 4 and L symbols with each hand. “Hmm, that could work,” the producer nodded. “If this was 1998.” “Shoot, I was like ten years old back then, that’s definitely some outdated material. Well, I’m definitely not having any ideas coming to me. We should just look over some of my older material.” “What for?” the producer questioned him, stretching his hands behind his head. “Recycle an idea, a good idea, or reuse some of the elements I’ve previously used.” The producer shook his head at the thought of that. “That’s cheap, and you know it.” “Well, that’s true but,” Harley wasn’t going to be shot down that easily. “Everyone does it. Its what makes people successful. Everyone has a basic formula that they know works because they’ve done it before, because its been successful before, and they stick to it. It doesn’t matter if its wrestling a match, doing a powerpoint presentation, filing away their comic books, taking an exam, or doing a public speech. You look at the most successful wrestlers in PRIME, in TEAM, or any other place I’ve wrestled and they always wrestle the same way when its permitted by their opponent. And why do they do that? Cause it works, cause they have a lot of wins, titles and achievements under that formula. You don’t tinker with something that works, you just don’t. If some lame ass reverse DDT or something always wins you a match, you keep using it until it doesn’t work.” “Alright,” the producer sighed. “Whatever works for you, its still cheap though.” “Get out of here. You just make sure that camera is running once I’m through watching these videos of me.” “Yeah, yeah.” “Hey! TEAM IV or TEAM V?” “Uhhh,” the producer looked down, thinking. “TEAM IV, you stunk up the joint in TEAM V.” “Brutal.” ***** 5… 4… 3… 2… 1… “For those who don’t know, now you will.” The camera faded into Harley Ace Douglas, with his trademark bomber jacket and aviators. He just liked the look, nothing to do with anything. “I’m Harley… Ace… Douglas. That’s my real name, no name changes or gimmicks involved in that. I’m nineteen years old, and I’ve been wrestling for the past seven months. I’m a former TEAM Free For All… tilde~ bang! Champion, and I recently competed in both the TEAM Dupree Cup Tournament and the TEAM Tournament of Champions, safe to say… I would’ve won the Dupree Cup if I hadn’t had some ham n’ eggars as teammates… and well, the Tournament of Champions was won by Nova, I mean… come on. That guy’s like Superman on acid. Now that the formalities are set aside and you know who I am and why it’s important you listen to me, let me begin.” Harley took off his sunglasses and put them away. “It seems I’ve wound myself up in another of these tournaments, the Jewel in the Crown tournament to be precise and, much like the Dupree Cup or the Tournament of Champions, I’m impressed by the caliber of the wrestlers involved. Sure, you have your Joe Bloes… I’m looking at you, Easton Hall…” Harley grinned and winked at the camera. “Rounding out the tournament, but all in all, I’m impressed.” “And I have reasons to be impressed, because this isn’t a PRIME only tournament, no, they’ve gone out of their way to bring in outsiders, worthy outsiders, to fill the gaps and truly make this a prestigious tournament, a tournament that will culminate at King of Kings.” Harley glanced up at the ceiling momentarily, gathering his thoughts. “Oh how lovely it would be to be King, I guess, I don’t know. Sounds like hard work, being a King. And it seems to me like too much of a hassle, too many expectations, too many demands from too many different parties. No, I’d prefer not to be king, thank you very much. I’d rather be what I am right now, Ace. Harley Ace. I’ve been doing a good job up until now and unlike Kings, which are rampant across the wrestling world, there is only ever one true Ace, a high Ace… a Harley Ace. Demands and expectations be damned when you’re the Ace.” “They be damned.” ***** “Aaaand Cut!” “Yeah, I was done like 10 seconds ago,” Harley snorted. “Closing shot, brother, closing shot.” “Alright, I forgive you…” Harley smiled at the producer. “But only because you’re my baby brother. Now let’s go get some pizza or something. What'd you think of my work, Jacob?" Jacob grinned mischievously. "It sucked."
