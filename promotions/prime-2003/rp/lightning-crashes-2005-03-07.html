Lightning crashes

7 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03-07-05 *FADE IN* It’s a dark and rainy night, as the sky is brilliantly lit up with the awesome effects of lightning. As the thunder crackles through the darkness a woman screaming is heard in the near distance. The scene pans in, through the darkness, which is lit up every ten to fifteen seconds by the lightning storm. As the sky comes to life from the lightning, it is clear that we are in some sort of forest. Another lightning strike. Another thunder clap. Another scream. The camera moves through the woods ever so faster, as if to hurriedly reach the source of the screaming. As the scene moves faster, it seems as if the lightning and thunder comes at quicker intervals. Suddenly a bolt of lightning strikes the ground, five feet from the camera. As the picture returns to normal from being scrambled by the electricity, we hear another scream, right in front of us. Coming from inside a cabin a mere 50 feet from where the lightning struck. We pan to a window, small and frosted, where light from within the cabin can be seen flickering. Bright, dark, bright, soft brightness, complete darkness. As the rain collects on the camera lens, the forest gets uncomfortably silent. KER-RASHHHHHHHHHH!!!!! Another close lightning strike. ‘AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH’ Another scream from a woman, coming from the cabin in front of us. We slowly are taken to the front of the cabin, to the entrance door. As if by some mystical force, the door creaks open, slowly, agonizing. Another lightning strike. Another scream. The door is slammed shut, as the person filming this promo wants to get out of the weather. But was it really smart to go into a dingy, dark cabin, where nothing but the sounds of a woman screaming has been heard for the last few minutes? “Whose there?” A voice cries out, from another room. Looking around, we see a kitchen, living room and bedroom, all made up from the main room of this cabin. Across the way, another door, where a light shines from the cracks in the door. In the corner, the television flickers, revealing itself as the source of the light shining through the window a few moments ago. The old cabin creaks as the lightning and thunder storm continues outside. And suddenly, silence. Which is broken by the sound of water being flushed. “Vamp… Tiamat… you guys out there?” Again the voice calls out. “Come on… it’s time to put our master plan into effect. Guys…” The only other door in the cabin opens, and standing in the doorway is The Universal Champion, Boda. “Oh, it’s you. Well, it’s about damn time. Welcome to my own little private Idaho. This is where I go, when I need to have some alone time, away from the rest of the world.” Boda walks across the room and sits down on the couch in front of the television. “You’re just in time. Sorry I didn’t great you at the door, but I was busy taking a Kano and I had to make sure I wiped my Rage so I don’t get any skid marks in my underwear.” The Universal Champ picks up a remote and points it at the television and VDR sitting on top of it. “Well, I’m still waiting for the boys to show up…. So I’ve been busy scouting my opponent for this week.” Boda flips the VCR on, and the screen comes to life with a video of The English Luchador in action. The match he’s watching doesn’t last long, as EL is pinned after a few moments. As EL recovers, he screams, a loud, ear piercing, blood curdling scream. Just like a woman. lightning crashes…….. Thunder claps……….. The English Luchador screams like a little bitch……….. “And this coming ReVolution…. The Dark Ages will unleash a fury, like that PRIME has yet to see. For Culture Shock was only the beginning to an end. The end of fertility. The end of sanity. The end of humanity as we know it. The seeds of ReVolution were planted long ago, and though they sprouted a short time ago, this week, we shall water the plant of Evolution.” Boda smiles sadistically. “English Luchador, you mean shit to me. You will be an example to the masses of PRIME, as to what The Dark Ages can and will do now that we are ONE. This week, leave the small children and elderly at home, for I promise you an event that is not for the young or the weak of heart. Now, before Vampir arrives and steals your soul…. Be gone. That is a warning.” And as Boda turns to watch the television again, the camera man takes head and runs out the door, into the rainy, dark night. lightning crashes………. Thunder claps…………. And the camera man just pissed his pants as he ran into Vampir waiting outside the door. *FADE OUT*
