Accusations of Destruction

31 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/31/2008 Monday, March 31, 2008 "Ugh Dizz… please go to sleep…" Jenny was standing over Desiree’s rather new big girl bed. The littlest Sirrajin was quite unsettled and upset, like she knew something was wrong. Killean was not home yet and obviously he didn’t know the news broke yet. Heck, when she saw it on the TV, she couldn’t believe it either and she saw it with her own two eyes. Vegas saw it too and he was on his way to their loft post haste. Finally, Desiree was starting to calm down and get a little settled in, her cute little eyes fluttering open and shut, until they finally stayed there. Jenny then heard a knock on the door, running quickly to answer it before the foreign noise woke up the slumbering child. She opened the door and the large black man burst through, clearly frustrated with what they heard. "I had to leave Big Mac in charge of the club! What the hell was I thinkin’?" He said with his deep and booming voice as he stepped through the door. Jenny tapped him on the shoulder to stop him and put a finger over her mouth. "Veg, shhhhh! Dizzy just got to sleep." Hanna, Vegas’ wife also walked through the door, clearly taking Jenny off guard. "I thought you were working today Hanna." She gave her friend a hug, Hanna visibly upset at the news. "I had to find out for myself Jen. I won’t believe it until I hear it from Killean himself." "That fuck is my friend, but I’ll be damned if I’m gonna go easy on him for this crap! Performance enhancers or not, I’ll beat his face in until he hits rehab again!" Vegas was practically glowing red from holding back the full growl in his voice. "Again?" Jenny questioned, clearly wondering what Vegas was talking about. "I uhhhh… damnit." "Well Jen…" Hanna started, clearly giving her husband a stern look as they all made their way to the sofa. "Killean was addicted to pain killers at one time. Not exactly his fault as the stupid doctor just kept prescribing them. But he had a hand in it and it almost killed him. You know about the scar on his neck, but you didn’t know all the details. So we forced him to go to rehab." "Yeah he knew then and he’ll know now!" Vegas said, getting back up from the couch just seconds after sitting down. He was clearly bothered by the news. "Hun, we really need to stop the accusing before we find out the truth from him." "Damn guy will have some ‘splainin’ to do! No doubt!" He made his way for the kitchen to grab a drink, leaving the two ladies alone. "I know one way to find out for sure." Hanna said. Jenny looked up at her friend, clearly trying to find out what she was getting at before she asked but then she had to relent. "What’s that?" "Well… has the sex been better? Last longer?" Hanna said, giggling as she pushed the last words out. "HANNA!" Jenny quickly covered her mouth, worrying about raising her voice and waking Desiree. "What’d I miss?" Vegas came back into the room, taking a sip from a beer. The girls then both began to laugh. The fun jab really lightened the mood for them. But Vegas… "THIS AIN’T THE TIME FOR LAUGHIN’ DAMNIT!" |~o~| The gym was busy, a little extra busy as there appeared to be a class going on for aerobics and they had a deal on a free workout with personal trainer for one hour. Plenty of people were sitting down at desks discussing possible new memberships. Normally, Killean worked out at home. But he listens to his music rather loud so the gym was the perfect alternative if Jenny wanted a quiet day at home. He was finishing his workout when he noticed people looking at him, some giggling and some giving rather disgusted looks. He really couldn’t figure out what it was all about because it was just not the normal gawking people did when they noticed someone semi-famous. Earlier in the day, he signed a few autographs outside the gym as he arrived but recently, no one approached him. Instead, the laughs and the looks of death were all he saw. He shook his head and quickly finished his workout. As he made his way towards the change room, one of the people on an exercise bike was watching a television. The words scrolling on the bottom of the screen were, "More Drugs in Wrestling". Killean’s picture happened to also be on the screen. The man on the bike stopped pedaling, looking in the direction of the change room as he saw The Supreme Machine disappear around the corner. |~o~| He hit the button on the remote and the trunk popped open. He lifted his gym bag from the trunk and shut the lid. He made his way for the elevator, happy to be home to relax before having to go to the airport. He made it a habit in the past to leave Monday night and he was doing just that. He pressed the button and the elevator doors closed and he started to ascend. ‘Only a couple floors and then some rest.’ He thought to himself. In a few more seconds, the doors opened and he walked down the hall to his loft. As he approached the door, he overheard Jenny talking with someone. He then heard Vegas’ booming voice coming from the room along with the light and weak voice of his wife Hanna. Company… just what he needed. Vegas was like his brother, but rest was just the first thing on his mind. He turned the knob and opened the door, coming into the room. Jenny and guests turned and met his gaze as he dropped his gym bag and removed his boots. Within a split second, the room went silent and their stares said something was wrong. But in his mind, it could wait until later. "What… the gym bag some kinda cover up?" Vegas said, trying to get to his feet. Hanna swiftly grabbed his arm and pulled him back down, basically warning him to shut up. She was tougher than she looked. "That didn’t make much sense, but whatever. I’m going to lie down." "Hun, we need to talk." Jenny tried to stop him, but he just kept going. "It can wait until I get up then. We’ll all get some dinner." "THIS ISN’T GONNA WAIT!" Vegas exploded off the couch, this time escaping the grasp of his wife. She didn’t have much of a chance at that point and just braced herself for a potential explosion. Killean stopped and turned to look at Vegas, almost looking insulted by the tone of his friend. "What the hell is your problem?" "Yeah, like ya don’t know. We got ya into rehab before and got ya all fixed up. All that just for ya to fuck things up and drug the shit outta your body again?" Bluntness aside, Vegas’ tone was very serious. "What exactly are you trying to say, Dave?" Killean walked around the couch, stopping to stand beside Jenny. She sat, trying to catch Killean’s gaze with her own but his eyes were glaring at his friend now. "Y’ALL FUCKIN’ DRUGGED UP AGAIN AIN’T YA?" The Supreme Machine’s eyes burst open wide, like he had been punched in the stomach. His feet stayed glued to the floor where they were but his body wanted to charge at the insult hurled his way. "Back off! Accuse me of that again and I’ll skull fuck you in your sleep!" "A liar too now… wonderful." Vegas said, turning his head in all directions. Killean meanwhile removed the nails from his feet and began to approach. Jenny stood up immediately as did Hanna to ensure they would come between any possible scuffle. "After all I’ve been through, HOW DARE YOU accuse me of something so asinine! Wake up you prick, I’ve been clean since then." "That’s not what the TV is saying. So who’re we to believe?" Replied Vegas, calming down and not yelling. Killean stared daggers at him. He then narrowed his eyes suspiciously. "What in the world are you talking about? Jenny, what the hell is all this?" "Look." Jenny said, turning on the TV to the news channel. A press conference was being held for PRIME Wrestling. Killean sat down on the couch with Jenny and Hanna. Vegas stayed standing. Lisa Tyler, Vice President of Talent Relations was delivering a short speech. It’s a replay that was halfway over when they tuned in, but Jenny turned the volume up so they could catch the rest. "PRIME does not forsake its own," Tyler said. "However, in this instance, I had to consider the well-being and reputation of the rest of our roster. Ultimately, it was more important to me to remove the cloud of suspicion over their heads, even if it came at the expense of identifying Killean Sirrajin directly." The headline on the bottom of the screen is the same as in the gym. The broadcaster then chimes in. "Once again, we’re watching Lisa Tyler from PRIME Wrestling on the now confirmed positive drug test administered on Hall of Fame Superstar Killean Sirrajin. Sirrajin is Canadian, twenty-nine years of age and has been around PRIME on and off since 2003. This is the first time we know of that he has tested positive, unless details of such a test were not made public in the past. Sirrajin has not been suspended and will still appear in Oklahoma City this Wednesday. The only information we know about the drug that came up on the test was that it was NOT an anabolic steroid, but some other performance enhancing drug." Jenny muted the newscast to break Killean out of the trance he was in. He was staring blankly at the screen. He then looked to the floor and then all around his peripheral vision without moving his head. He seemed speechless. "So, what’s your defence to that?" Vegas said, crossing his arms. Killean’s head twisted around in an instant, determined to make his brother believe. "I swear on my Father’s grave, my Sister’s grave and Kira’s grave along with my life and Desiree’s life that I have done nothing! Any drug test I take HAS to be clean." "But you heard i…" Vegas was cut off. "BUT NOTHING DAVE! It’s not true and someone is lying!" The room fell immediately silent. Sirrajin was breathing rather heavy, trying to get a hold on his emotions. It had been months since he had been in the ring, now it only took just over 2 weeks to be completely shrouded in controversy. "You obviously don’t get it. It’s not April Fool’s Day until tomorrow. Everything I’ve worked for in the past, the present and in the future has been and will be destroyed with this. Why would I do something so stupid? My career is on the line and this will ruin me! Understand for fuck sakes, once drugs enter the equation, no one person is ever the same again!" He took a step back and sat on the couch, burying his head in his hands. A sudden burst of crying emanated from Desiree’s room and immediately, Jenny and Hanna went to tend to her. It was no doubt the constant yelling that woke the child. Vegas approached Sirrajin and cautiously sat at his side. "Look man, I’m sorry. I didn’t know what to think and I guess what happened before just caught up with me." Killean took a deep breath and lifted his head. "It’s alright. I know if the roles were reversed I probably would have lost control like a jackass too." He broke a small smile but it quickly faded, turning to rub his forehead. "Heh, glad you accepted my apology. You’re not gonna skull fuck me when I sleep are you?" Killean turned back to Vegas and got off the couch when he noticed Jenny and Hanna coming back with Desiree. "I don’t think you have anything to worry about man." Desiree reached out her arms as Jenny approached Killean. Tears had been streaming from her bloodshot eyes. "Daddy…" Killean took her up in his arms and they both smiled a little. No matter how cloudy the day was his darling daughter could always bring some sun shine. "It’s ok baby girl. I know there was yelling and a bunch of bad words. I’m sorry for that." "I told Dave that we should give you the benefit of the doubt. Ya big lug!" Hanna playfully smacked her man on the arm, giving him that ‘You’re not getting any tonight’ kind of look. "So now what are you going to do?" Jenny asked, taking a seat on the couch. Killean mulled it all over in his head. What could he do really? Stand up; tell people that it’s not true? What good would that do? The modern media made it obvious that many people made up their minds even before all the facts were present. All he knew was that he didn’t take part in anything that he was being accused of. The main difference between him and say Roger Clemens was one simple thing. Killean was not lying, nor did he have a reason to. "He’s gonna go out there on Wednesday and kick some ass, that’s what!" Vegas piped up, clearly not minding the young ears in the room. Hanna slapped him again. "Oops, sorry Diz." "Look, you may be right but if I just go out there and take out Shakur, people will be saying that the #1 contender just got owned by a drugged up muscle head and where will that get me? I’m just gonna do what I need to do. I’m going out there, hopefully with the crowds support and I’m gonna do what I do best. I’m gonna do what I have never needed performance enhancing. I’m gonna wrestle and I’m gonna win. In due time, everyone will realize that they made a huge mistake. I don’t need help with this." The room fell silent again. Eyes shifted all around the room when finally, everyone’s eyes rested on Killean as he reached his free arm to grab his cell phone. "Gonna call your lawyer aren’t ya?" Vegas smirked a little. "Damn straight." No help indeed. Welcome back to PRIME, Killean.
