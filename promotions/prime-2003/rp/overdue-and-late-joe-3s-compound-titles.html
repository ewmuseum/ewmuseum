Date: Right after Colossus August 11ish, 2008 Charlotte, NC Post-Colossus Overall, the bar in the Omni Charlotte Hotel was nothing to write home in a tizzy about. It's nice for sure, no guest staying there would dispute that, but it looked like nearly every other upscale hotel bar that the PRIME contingency had stayed in thus far this year. In fact, if one were to ask Tyler Rayne about it, the Golden Boy would state that the only spectacular thing about it was the person who summoned him there and who already had a bottle of beer waiting for his arrival. And, he would say it with that irrepressible smile of his. "Ya know, I think I've reached a point where there's only so many posh hotels and swanky hotel bars I can sit in before it all just... loses its luster. Of course, your pretty little ass does put a nice polish on the package." For her part, Lindsay Troy rolled her eyes at the all-too predictable comment and kicked the empty chair away from the table before he could pull it away himself. Rayne smirked and plopped himself down. His battle-scarred hand reached for the bottle of beer. "You said you wanted to talk, Tyler," she sighed, the weariness from the fight with Cozen still apparent in her voice. She ached all over. "Where I come from, that was talkin', sweetheart." Another roll of the eyes. Another sigh. "You sounded sincere about this at the arena. I can go get what passes for lametarded 'smooth talk' at the dive down the street if that's all you're going to feed me here." "Relax, will ya? Jesus, Uni. Take all the fun out of my life. So... yeah. I figured we should talk. You know. About... stuff. Things. Considering we didn't get that opportunity given the circumstances with Country and the Hillbillies." "Alright then." Her pint-sized glass of rum and coke was lifted to her lips and a generous sip taken. "Start talking." "You could make this easier for me. A little less bitch would be nice. How's he doing? Recovering well?" "Not for anything, Tyler," Lindsay put the glass back on the coaster, "but I went to you with a problem and was completely sincere about it. Your response was, well...deserved, but then you attacked me in the elevator. And just when I thought you'd show me the same sincerity I gave you, you proceed to be glib all over again. So, pardon me if I'm just a little bit sore and a little bit tired, both from my match and from trying to get a read on you." That sigh again. She rubbed her temples. "Wade's fine, I guess, but him taking on Locke wasn't the smartest thing to do all things considering. He doesn't ever talk about what ails him, and I used my one 'You're going to the hospital to get checked out, goddamnit!' card for the year, so I have to take him at his word that he's OK." "Kind of why I brought it up. You came to me with a problem. Why? Certainly didn't feel the need to speak to me any time recently. Or even acknowledge my existence for that matter. You've got Rider and Killy and all kinds of good friends to help you out of a jam. So why me? Especially when it comes to helping Country." "I told you why I couldn't." "Right," he scoffed. "Everyone else had too much at stake to risk it. But Tyler Rayne? Well, let's put his life on the line for some douche he doesn't even like all that much. Not like he's got his own problems to worry about. That's about the gist of it?" "Oh, right," Troy nodded, not in acquiescence but to start her engines for a full-blown fight. "Your problems. Like keeping your rebound chick's pussy wet. I'm sure that was no problem at all for you, like it was no problem at all to badmouth my name on national television because of something you did that I rightly didn't like. I'm sure you slept pretty well all those nights, starting with the night you hit Wade with your car right up until poor little me came knocking humbly at your hotel room door. But hey, I gave you an out. You didn't take it. You opted to help me. So what's that say about you and how much you enjoy putting your life on the line, huh?" "I'll tell you what I'm gonna do." Tyler leaned forward in his chair, dusty brown orbs narrowed almost to slits, voice dropped with anger. "I'ma loan you a couple thousand bucks, 'cause I'm a nice guy like that. I'ma point you in the right direction. You take the money and buy yourself a fucking clue. Open your eyes, pull the fuckin' stick out your ass, and actually look at the situation in hand. You dropped me in a hotel hallway. Left me there without a word. Didn't speak to me for a fuckin' month and then came knockin' on my door, asking for my help to save some cunt drip mother fucker you know damn well I'd just as soon see rot. Now ask yourself, love... why the hell would I put my life on the line like that? What does it say about me? You came to my door. You asked for help. I never asked for an explanation. I never asked for an apology. I asked for five minutes to lace my boots. You think you're so fuckin' smart, Uni, answer your own gods damned question." "What's that say about you? YOU'RE TYLER RAYNE. THIS IS WHAT YOU DO. You know why I went to you? Because I knew, deep down, that Wade was bad off. I knew, deep down, that if I went on my own and found him, that someone might be there waiting for me. And wouldn't you fucking know it? I was right. But gee, maybe if I had Mr. Fucking Ninja Fighter Man to help me out, then maybe I could get out of there in one piece and I could get whatever was left of Wade out too. Now, I'm sorry I did.." A long, long swig of alcohol. "You know what Tyler Rayne doesn't do? Tyler Rayne doesn't see how his bad his actions hurt someone he supposedly cared about. Tyler Rayne can't even fathom it, because when the chips are down, Tyler Rayne has one focus and that is the Survival of Tyler Rayne. And he didn't just do it to Wade. He did something pretty shitty to Danny Ferguson too, without much of an apology for either scenario. Oh, but blame me. Put it all on me, Tyler, don't even begin to see how I was justified in leaving your ass without a word, when all you gave me was a bunch of spoon-fed bullshit that never meant a goddamn thing. You never needed to ask for an apology, because you'd never in a million years feel free to give one of your own. But at least I'm the better person, right? I apologized. I said I was sorry. Oh! And I didn't have to go running into the arms of anyone else during that little interlude of ours. Let's just keep tallying up the brownie points for me!" Lindsay stood up so hard, so fast, that her chair not only toppled over, but it also knocked over a chair behind her. "You take your couple thou, you call up Shakur and you ask him where the cleanest whorehouse in this town is. Consider it me giving you my permission to going back to kissing, dating, fucking within your social class, because you and I both know the latter never happened, and now it never will." With the eyes of the few stragglers on her, the Queen spun on her heel and marched toward the lobby. Tyler stood up and followed her out, his yells unable to slow her down. "Gee, and when everyone told me you're a callous, cold-hearted bitch I figured they were just jealous. Turns out, though... You want an apology for Hollywood? Fine. I told you it was never my intention to put him on the shelf. I reacted to a situation. It's what I do. It's why you came to me when you needed help with Wade. Get up off your high horse. Cut the double standard bullshit. It's dandy to use Mr. Go Kill Go when you need him to save your friend's ass because he's too stupid to keep himself out of trouble... but you don't want me doin' the same for other people. That's what it was, wasn't it? I saved Tink from Ferg the same way I saved Country for you. But sure... I'm sorry 'bout what happened to him. Wasn't lookin' to take the man's livelihood. I'm sorry. Turns out I'm an asshole. Go fuckin' figure, huh." Finally, he caught up to her at the elevators and pulled her back from a door about to open. "You're hangin' all this shit over my head, Uni. Comes down to it? You still came back to me. I hit your friend Wade with a car. Maybe that was a bit overzealous. Figure savin' his life oughta go a long way toward makin' amends for that. You drop wrath and fury down on me for that... and I can understand. I would've done the same. But don't look down on me for giving it back. We're not the type of people to take shit. Even if we do deserve it. You wouldn't. I wouldn't. Let it go. You can walk around and be pissy about the fact that I slept with 'Chelle... or you can look at the fact that she came to me after you left. And the moment you arrived... I walked away. We've both done some really stupid fucking shit. We're stubborn, egotistical stupid fuckin' assholes. For all of it... much as you seem to think you should hate me... you're still here talking to me. Whether you wanna admit it or not, that counts for something. I can't apologize for who I am. I can't promise it won't happen again. Not any more than you can. All I can do is say I never wanted to hurt you. And I'm sorry that I did. And... we've really made a fucking scene here, haven't we?" Troy's eyes darted about: back to the bar area, to the lobby, many more eyes on her than she felt comfortable with in this situation. The sigh that escaped from her lips was jagged, embarrassed and emotional. "I shouldn't have expected you to be broken up over me and not find someone to fill the void. Doesn't matter who went to who, it still happened. I could've shacked up with Vega and I had half a mind to, just to spite you. But I...I don't, can't, do shit like that. Not to him, or you. And I don't know if--," she buried her face in her hands. "We caused a scene. I didn't want it to happen like this. Can't take it back now, and I can't fix it." "Fix? C'mon, kid, look who you're talkin' to. Isn't that what I do best? Well, actually... fucking things up is pretty much what I do best. But the two of us are just stubborn enough to try anyway, aren't we?" Her fingers tangled themselves in her hair, as Rayne slipped his hands around the curves of her hips. "I want another chance." She nodded, slipped her hands down to her sides, and offered him a weak smile. "You're going to have to do something first." "Here I thought you'd never ask..." He pulled her into him, but her left hand slipped into her pants pocket and pulled out her phone, which she placed against his chest to create a buffer. "You're going to have to start apologizing to more people than just me. Call it part of your penance." Her finger brushed over one of the keys to bring up a number placed on speed-dial. "You can start with Danny."
