Mid Life Crisis - 'The Time is Now'

18 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/4/07 ‘Are you sure that this is going to help get me over?’ ‘Of course Jack, would I tell a lie?’ Well…when you’re talking about Dave Price, anything’s possible. Currently, I was standing in some elegant department store, looking totally out of place in my hulking two hundred and eighty pound frame, knocking into racks and generally looking like a bumbling idiot. Then again, the stupider I acted, the greater the chance I might actually be kicked out and get some peace from the situation. You’d think that someone buying you clothes and paying for it on the company account no less would be grand…but then you remember you’re subject to what his personal taste is. Suits. ‘Listen Jack, you’ve got to appear more approachable and amicable to those who are going to be flocking under your banner. With the way we’ve got this story planned out for you, you’ve got to look like you mean business. Jeans and a shirt just doesn’t cut it anymore because you look like everybody else. Now this…’ He points to the manila suit barely draped over me. ‘…this will give you the business approach. You look around and remember the last half hour of conversation. Who exactly bothered to listen to your opinion? Did anybody in this store ask did you like or dislike what was happening? Do you know why they didn’t listen?’ I just sighed, I wasn’t up for playing games. Price was in his element and was perfectly capable of having a conversation with himself. ‘They didn’t listen because you were unimportant. You don’t have any presence other than the fact that you’re big. In fact, several of them probably think you’re an annoyance and can’t wait to get you out of here. It’s all in the swagger Jack, you’ve got to know your business and then act it. Isn’t that right Dante?’ Ah Dante, my image consultant. The slim, almost anorexic man child peeked up from near my crotch, yes my crotch, where he’d been stitching the trousers to fit my build. You’d think there’d be time for that afterwards but he insisted on the grounds that they’d be a better fit for my package. Oh yes, he said package. When I looked down at him tending to his work, it looked more like he was drooling at being so close to the groin of a man that tended to be well oiled on a weekly basis. I noted to myself that in the future I would try to stay away from Price on the nights of ReVolution because I knew Dante would be with him. A flamboyant image consultant was not conducive to a good match build up. ‘So you see Jack, trust me on this one, you’re going to be a much more important looking figure on the shows from now on.’ Apparently Dave had continued speaking. Oh well, I figured it’d be in his priceless dossier so I could catch up on things later. He wasn’t finished either apparently. ‘We’ve got big plans for you Jack, let me tell you that and it’s the little things like that which will allow us to get the plan going. After all, when I meet with the committee today…’ ‘Today? You’re meeting them today?’ ‘Oh yes, they need a progress report on what’s going on.’ He said it flippantly, like I should’ve already known what was going on. With the way my face looked, clearly I didn’t know, at least that’s what he figured with his follow up. ‘Didn’t I tell you this?’ ‘No, no you didn’t.’ ‘Oh, well I guess I must have told Barney to let you in on the proceedings. I must remember to give out to him then.’ He shrugged a little and looked more pressingly towards Dante. ‘Are we done? I have to make this meeting on time you know.’ ‘Just done!’ He shouted, but it was muffled under fabric. With a sigh from down there (I really didn’t want to know what for) he jumped up to his feet and twirled before giving some jazz hands at me. ‘Excellent. Jack, just get changed so that we can chalk this one up shall we?’ I have no idea how I got myself into this mess. Actually maybe I do. It’s Barney’s goddamn fault. *** So we’re finally leaving the department store in Denver and between the two guys walking behind me, it really is ridiculous. Dante is skipping, and I seriously mean skipping, behind whilst Dave is jabbering into his cell phone with flailing arms. It’s not that impressive until you see him flailing both arms in the air while still talking. That’s pretty impressive. But you could say that I was trying to keep a low profile as we headed for the limousine that Price insisted on going around in. Despite the absolute cheap ass ending by Youngblood and being denied a shot at the title, which of course Youngblood won, Price and Barney had both intimated to me that my enthusiasm and energy was getting across to the local fan. They said interest in my appearances for certain avenues were being opened up and I only needed to keep up with the amount of effort I was putting in. For their part, I could agree that I certainly felt energised and more at one with the wrestling right now. It wasn’t forced or textbook like it had been for the past month previous. It was starting to feel exciting to me again, even after twenty five years in the business. The crowd was giving me the goose bumps again. And it felt great. We neared the limousine, the lion and tin man behind me, still flailing and pirouetting like the best of them when I bumped into someone unexpectedly. The person flew back from me, almost falling to their feet but I managed to catch them. It was just a boy, maybe about thirteen or fourteen and frankly I could’ve crushed him with my hand if necessary. He coughed as I pulled him up from the half-sitting position he was in. ‘You alright kid?’ Then I noticed something. His eyes were going wide and I mean real wide. They were like the kind bugs had when they looked like they were going to pop right out. His baby blue eyes were glazed over. ‘What’s the matter? You alright?’ Finally, he blurted out a quick sentence. ‘Ohmygod you’re JackMurphy!’ I reeled a little at this and looked cautiously at the boy. Then I heard the words which would change things forever. ‘Can I have your autograph?’ Wow. ‘Eh sure kid.’ He whipped out a pen and paper (because everybody apparently carries them in Denver) and he had my signature in no time. Because of the commotion caused by the kid, several others wandered over and it seemed that they too knew who I was. An impromptu signing session emerged around me and I was swamped with requests. Amidst it all I can remember signing a lot of things… …even a pair of breasts if I’m remembering correctly. Throughout all this the boy was still standing beside me, waiting for the crowd to disperse and get another chance to talk to me. I managed to get him to move a little bit with me and I looked at him. ‘Jack, I hope you get revenge for what Youngblood did to you. You didn’t deserve that. I’ll be rooting for you.’ Without another word the kid went on his way again, clutching the autograph dearly and I felt…good for once. *** Click. ‘Hey Jess, you’ll never guess what just happened!’ Cue a mumbling of words from Jessica, apparently in the middle of eating something. I threw out the event with blistering speed, mimicking the boy in his eagerness and luck at finding me. ‘That’s great honey! I’m so happy to hear that things are picking up for you.’ Another bit of rummaging on the phone. I pressed her to see how she was but she wasn’t too interested. She casually brushed me off as quickly as possible, citing business reasons for this. Click. *** ‘I can’t believe it, I just can’t believe it.’ Dave looked at me with a smirk and a smile all at once as I giddily bounced in my seat. ‘You see, I told you there’d be changes to how things are going Jack and this was the first payoff. People are noticing you just like I said they would and it’s going to get even better than that. You know I was just on the phone?’ ‘Yeah?’ I was still hyped up from the experience and would’ve listened to a children’s story with glee right now. ‘It was from Muscle Max3000. They’ve got a new product coming out and they’d like to have you, be the main person in the advertising campaign.’ ‘Really?’ ‘Really.’ ‘That’s awesome. You know I was starting to doubt but…’ He stopped me with the flick of a wrist. Nothing more, just a flick to quieten me down. It irritated me deep down but I let him have his way, conscious of the fact that he was providing me with this new lease of life. He had the power to give and take after all and I was pretty sure that he would exercise both sides of that power if necessary. ‘There will be no doubt that I can deliver everything I said and more alright Jack? Just believe in me and everything will be fine. I am Dave Price after all.’ The rest of the journey to the hotel in Denver was pretty quiet, the odd yelp from Dante who was flabbergasted that he got to be in the limousine with us. It was almost like he was expecting a restraining order or something. Despite all this I felt good. Things were going well, I had a programme slot on the show regularly, I had options, I had opportunities and I had the possibilities to go as far as I wanted. All thanks to that man beside me. Hmm, this was almost going too well. *** ‘So…how are things going Dave?’ ‘They’re coming along fine. Murphy has complete trust in my actions and right now he’ll jump through hoops to continue along the way. He has our complete support with whatever we choose to do with his character.’ It’s a nicely lit dining room where Price finds himself talking with the big boys. They’re all casually walking around as they drop in to ask questions of him, the one person sitting in the middle of the room in a really uncomfortable chair. He scoffed at it in particular because it was dirtying his designer suit. Another booker swooped in. ‘Don’t forget the trust you have in us David. Even with all your power you cannot forget that we can just as easily take it away if we so wish.’ ‘I haven’t forgotten.’ ‘These plans…do you think they’re going to work?’ ‘Of course. I wouldn’t be suggesting them otherwise.’ ‘I see…there are going to have to be some alterations.’ He looked around anxiously, unsure of what to do. Price was able to command the situation when he had lackeys behind him to support his position. He sort of fed off their ass-kissing energy in order to make himself a better talker. So in this position Dave was slightly out of his league. ‘Uhm, of course. Whatever you think is necessary for the company.’ ‘Yes, for the company David, that’s what it’s about isn’t it?’ He hid his head, bowing it out of sight and breathed a reply close to his chest. He hoped that his ambitions hadn’t gotten the best of him yet. It was too early and there hadn’t been enough planning laid in place. He needed more time. A hand gripped his shoulder. ‘We’ll let you continue…just make sure you realise who’s in charge ok?’ A woosh of air emanated from Price. *** ‘So, how’d it go.’ Dave was looking nonchalantly at us as he sauntered out. Barney had joined me in waiting and he was munching on some jerky. Price shrugged his shoulders and kept walking. ‘How’d it go?’ He’d walked right by me and was about to leave the building before he finally turned on his heels and looked at me. ‘You’re still in this. We just need a continued show of faith in this. Winning at the next show is important for the overall scheme. We’ve got till UltraViolence to prove we mean business.’ ‘Right that shouldn’t be a problem.’ ‘It better not Jack, it better not.’ A wave of exultation went over me and I was so pleased with the outcome. I had my lease and my chance…and I was going to take it.
