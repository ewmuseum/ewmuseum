The Surface and the Interior

Date: WHAT Engaged in the ebb and flow of a balmy New Jersey night, Devin Shakur strolled hand in hand with Sun Tzu through Six Flags Great Adventure theme park. The craziest celebrity Communist couple was indulging in a rare night out on the town, away from the spotlights of the wrestling world. With Devin being a prominent star of PRIME, the couple rarely had available time to spend together, let alone set up an actual date. From The Boardwalk to Johnny Rockets through fifteen different roller coaster rides, they basked in the happiness of each other's company. Nobody had been able to pry their hands apart for the entire three hours they spent together. Many times throughout the night they stole kisses from one another, trying to top each other's previous performance. Unfortunately, for fellow theme park goers, the couple wasn't as kind to others. Frequently, they taunted people who decided to scream their lungs out in the middle of a ride. Both found the wailing to be incredibly annoying and dealt with it the only way a former and current wrestler could. There shouldn't be a happier man on the planet than Devin Shakur. At least on the surface. Beneath the huge grin and frisky hands was a man struggling to find his place in the world. The professional life which he had worked so hard to master was on the verge of collapse. Torn and having lost his Championship belt, he needed a spark to fully resurrect the beast that once raged inside. Cataclysm and Lance Marshall should provide accommodations to the aforementioned quagmires. Nestling himself close to Sunny, Devin pulled the steel bar down over both of them and prepared for Batman: The Ride. It didn't take long for the initial jolt to pull Devin into a contemplative state, one that was long overdue. To him, this ride signified much more than three minutes of thrills and chills. The roller coaster was an emblem of his adventures over the past two months. With the ride creaking up to an apex, Devin fixated a hard stare down on the protective bar. He wasn't thinking about the inevitable drop or the newlywed couple behind him clutching one another tightly. Sun Tzu running her delicate fingers along his palm wasn't even eliciting a reaction. Instead, he reverted back to May 25 of 2008. Wild whipping hair and body tight outfits clouded his eyes while he astutely maneuvered to avoid the owners powerful limbs. His body already felt like it had been stung by a horde of wild bees whose nest had been shattered with a baseball bat. UltraViolence was supposed to be the shining moment of his life, amidst a sea of flashbulbs in Wrigley Field and the rage of rivals Cozen and Lindsay Troy. For months, he'd built himself as the heir to Troy's Universal Championship, and now he was either going to hold the belt high above his head or push a button inside a hospital to release more painkillers. Neither had given him the desired opening and time was of the essence. Devin's right cheek turned red from a stifling kick courtesy of The Queen. She was gaining the upper hand and wanted to finish the fight. Through blurred vision, he could see the blood lust in her eyes. No opening was going to manifest and if he didn't act soon, he would need to recreate the journey all over again. Eighteen months of labor would be fruitless. The train was leaving the tracks. Suddenly, his eyes spotted a figure cocked and ready to unload. Moving through the air with the grace of Jet Li, Cozen re-entered the picture in a dominant fashion. Devin froze, incapable of deducing a legitimate escape. She was going to hit the money shot whether he liked it or not. With the roller coaster coming to a complete stop to prepare passengers for the adrenaline rush, Devin kept his gaze downward, barely acknowledging Sun Tzu leaning her head onto his shoulder. There was nothing left to lose at this point. Should Devin elect to do nothing, he would be giving himself away. Seeing the impact resonate throughout Lindsay Troy's body, his auto-pilot activated and caused him to leap through the air haphazardly. Thirty five seconds later, flashbulbs poured in from all angles and his hands became fifteen pounds heavier. Meanwhile back in reality, his voice imitated, verbatim, the first sentiment of reflection after acquiring the belt. "No shit" Sun Tzu inquisitively gazed over into Devin's eyes and asked, "What sweetie?" Before Devin could awaken from his trance to answer her, a cacophony of screams invaded his ears. Devin wasn't prepared for the reaction, evident by plugging his ears with his fingers. A camera stationed nearby snapped a souvenir picture which would be available for purchase after the ride. The one hundred foot descent lasted only five seconds, but having the volume tuned down made the segue easier into the next facet of Devin's journey. Even though he tried to avoid the spotlight and screams, his best efforts were thwarted. The Universal Championship cast him in an echelon that the former APT CEO hadn't experienced in his life. Now, wherever he went, he was the object of affection. Even donning sunglasses and a sports cap couldn't make the number of fans dwindle. Most just wanted to get in his face and say Lindsay Troy would rise to the top once again. However, others were more interested in making his business acquaintance. Sponsorship deals came left and right. People were throwing everything but the kitchen sink just to get a moment of his "valuable" time. And it changed him. He went against the values and morals that made him one of the most dangerous men to step through the halls of PRIME. Instead of keeping his laser like focus solely on the Championship, his attention wandered. While in the limelight, he played the role of jester to perfection, making Tony Gamble look like Viggo Mortensen. So enamored by his newfound wealth, Devin didn't give an ounce of legitimacy to his next title defense, Chandler Tsonda. Preoccupied with the potential of screwing around rather than getting into the gym and pouring buckets of sweat, Devin didn't see the potential of losing. Instead, he saw dollar signs from the huge Colossus Main Event payday and the ability to bath in the spotlight, pushing his stock even higher into the ranks of immortality. Instead, he was left screaming a single word when Chandler Tsonda refused to die, and he knew the run was over. His sentiment was echoed by a few passengers when the coaster roughly settled back onto a straight path. "FUCK" Thundering along at a frantic pace, Devin needed a moment to recuperate. He needed time to gather his thoughts. To plot the next course of action. Take the necessary steps to get back on the road that led to another shot at the Universal Championship. Unfortunately, time wasn't willing to allocate anything, and left him straggling behind. The victim of a Chandler Tsonda coup. Critics and hindsight prophets decided to cast their wisdom. "I saw it coming" "I did too" "Dude, how could you not?" No two people cope with a devastating loss in the same fashion. Some get right back on the horse and put it out of their mind. Like it never happened. They are still the best at their profession regardless of what happened on one single night. Devin, on the other hand, slid into a role that he never imagined conceivable, living out his gimmick of being "Emo". He refused to leave his home, obsessed with replaying the images over and over again on his giant plasma screen television. He should have put Tsonda out. Finished him with the kick when he had the opportunity. But he didn't. He played the villain in Chandler Tsonda's fairytale dream and succumbed to the classical ending of falling before the hero. Whipping around a corner, ignoring the wind throw his hair in every conceivable direction, Devin zoned in on two people carelessly laughing three rows behind him . Their tone reminded him of a familiar voice, one that would never let Devin live down the biggest loss of his career. "AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAH! YOU LOST! HOW YA FEEL ABOUT THAT, BRUH!" Bryan Dawkins. Chandler Tsonda's right hand man was there every step of the way, even when the cameras chose not to highlight their skirmishes. Others saw fit to chime in and beat Devin while he was down. None of them ever accomplished something of that magnitude so they never got to feel the heartbreaking loss either. No sweat off their backs. None of it mattered to them. He was the asshole. He was the douchebag. He was supposed to take the loss. Call it a fluke. Say Tsonda got lucky. Pull out his rematch clause and continue psychologically tormenting the roster. Instead at ReVolution 171, Devin drowned in his own regret and the pouring rain of Buffalo, New York. He felt there was no hope in making an attempt to become a two-time Universal Champion. Now, Tyler Rayne was running operations at his digression. He had the option of booking the next Universal title fiasco and the two were in a weird place. The probability that he would even be in consideration sunk faster a desperate person trapped in quicksand. Then the wrestling gods decided to throw an awkward bone, one Devin didn't recognize until all of the pieces had fallen into place at the conclusion of ReVolution 170. Mayhem Desade Angelica Brooks And most importantly, yet again, Tyler Rayne Each of them deserved their own special loop on the roller coaster. Desade found the torture of PRIMEates to be rib breaking hilarious. Angelica Brooks happened to be a close "associate" (read: frequent one night stand) of Tyler Rayne. Mayhem just happened to be the giant mastodon who saw fit to rip Angelica through the hood of a car with a powerbomb. Tyler Rayne happened to be very pissed off and in need of running roughshod through the enemy. He needed the help of someone who he could depend upon. Someone who, when the time was right, could be a lethal force in his own right without the assistance of weaponry. Near 171's conclusion, Devin Shakur was quenching his thirst with a Code Red Mountain Dew in the right place at the right time. He was offered a bat and a chance to get a guaranteed spot for the Universal Championship at Great American Nightmare, the Pay-Per-View where he made his stamp on the wrestling world one year ago by knocking out Danny Ferguson. All he had to do was help Tyler Rayne knock around some "SCCW cum wads." The ride stopped yet again, continuing on a slow build to elate the passengers. Devin's depression slowly subsided. Frequently, he thought about fading off into the night and never returning. Now, he was once again in the mix, an opportunity to be on the tip of everybody's tongue one more time. Another chance to hold the one thing that mattered to him almost as much as the woman purring like a kitten next to him. He didn't hesitate and neither did the ride, quickly rising up to another apex. Now Devin couldn't focus on one specific moment during the descent. He was faced with an entirely new dilemma, one he wasn't exactly sure how he fell into. A hulking figure stood before him with the name Lance Marshall, full of rage and desiring to get revenge for being clocked two different times courtesy of Commie Emo. Literally capable of breaking skulls with one punch, most men in the wrestling business aren't going to stand up to Marshall, especially not one in the mindset of Devin Shakur. To the honest shock of nobody, Revolution 172 saw the return of Devin Shakur, the douchebag. The one who made his name by ridiculing the entire roster because he felt superior to them in every way. The one who tirelessly sought the Universal Championship even when he was looked upon as fodder for Lindsay Troy and Cozen. The man who could deliver mind blowing and brain stopping blows with just the spin of his body and the rise of his boot. The only question that remained was, "Could he still go?" Nobody boasted like Devin Shakur. He purposely emitted a goofy exterior in order to throw his opponents off and make them believe they have all the control in the world. He was a distinct and unique prick that loves every part of filling his role. To the casual fan, Lance Marshall bought the twenty five cent fictional island set in paradise and now was flying there to meet his demise. That's on the surface, though. But, did he have anything still left in the wrestling tank? The last time Devin was seen in the ring, a Mexican wrestling jobber took him to the limit, dominate every aspect of the match, and almost achieve a pinfall victory over the former Universal Champion. Lance Marshall is more skilled than from a Mexican jobber. Lance Marshall is not a test for someone who hasn't competitively given it their best in the last month and a half. Lance Marshall is a former Universal Champion. Lance Marshall is a measuring stick. Lance Marshall is a fucking BEAST One with the motivation and the means to make an example out of Devin Shakur before the eyes of two different wrestling promotion and millions of inquisitive fans. He had to be there one hundred percent in order to have a shot at knocking The Lion out with his head kick. Removing his eyes from the bar, Devin looked down and saw a dark and long tunnel awaiting. Sixty feet was all that stood between the forty-eight riders and the unknown. Devin's face, stuck deep in thought, changed into a hearty smile. He reached across the car and took the hand of his girlfriend so that the two could equally share in the experience. What awaited him on the other side nobody knew. Like a top level poker player, Devin wasn't giving any reads or tells. Maybe Devin didn't even know. But all he needed was to have people guessing. Thinking just a little bit too long. Wondering a little too much. It's how he's made his living for the past twenty months.
