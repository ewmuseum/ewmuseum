Two men in suits with briefcases walked in, one shaking a balled up fist as though the contact he'd made with Locke's face hurt him just a bit. Their sunglasses hid their demeanor. They were nearly identical in the sense of their uniform, including their short, parted and brown hair. Rhett scrambled to his feet and ran towards the kitchen. There was no house phone, though his cellphone was plugged in and charging. He tried to turn it on. The two men marched towards him casually, the one who punched him ripped the phone out of the Albino's hand violently. He removed the battery, put it back together and handed it back to Locke. For the first time in a long time, those layers of pride were pulled back and the true Rhett Christopher Locke was uncovered: a frightened lamb... Aware, and awaiting slaughter. Tears started to fill the Albino's eyes. "Don't cry." One said. "Yes, we will apply no further harm unless you do not cooperate," replied the Other. Rhett finally yanked the needle out of his arm. One and the Other looked at each other and grinned, before turning back to Locke. "Cooperate with what?" As though that was their cue they both opened their briefcases and removed papers, extending them out to him as though it were some foreign intelligence and he was the President. They were silent as he pulled the papers from their hands. Their grips were so firm and he was so weak from the heroin riding through his bloodstream that even that small action took effort. His eyes grew even wider (though his pupils were already as wide as could be) with each paper he skimmed over. The jist of things were there. Every piece of dirt on Rhett Locke's character, every incriminating act he had been a part of was documented and neatly organized in order of occurance. "...am I going to Jail?" One turned to the Other. They smiled. "No. We're not involved with the Authorities," One said. "Actually," the Other imposed. "We're part of something much greater. And with these papers in our hands, so are you." Rhett was a ballsy kinds of tom cat, "These papers aren't in your hands." He began tearing them into pieces. One and the Other looked at each other, a Cheshire grin occupying each face. "Perhaps you're too much of a recluse to understand that there are devices called Hard Drives, now. You know, where people save information they've recorded and whathaveyou?" Rhett stopped his actions, realizing he must look like a real fool. "Okay... so... you've got me--" "By the balls?" The Other interrupted. "...Something like that." One started, "Please understand that we are not Bullshitters, Rhett. "Actually," the Other divulged, "Someone else is blackmailing you." "True." Rhett questioned, "Your employer?" "True." They both stated. "...What do I have to do?" "Kill." One said plainly.
