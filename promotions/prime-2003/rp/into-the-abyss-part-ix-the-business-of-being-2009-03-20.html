Into The Abyss: Part IX - The Business Of Being

20 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20-03-09 December 12th, 2006 5 Days Before Von Kelsig's Release... "You must understand that I do not do business with just anyone." Doe spoke softly, almost in a whisper. "Certainly sir. Suffice to say I am not just 'anyone'. What would you like to know?" Blanco replied. They stepped back into Reuben's office, Doe relaxing in a faux leather recliner while Blanco sat upright at his desk, hands clasped around a glass paperweight in front of him. From the dank and bare dungeon-like room down the hall to this simple prim and proper office Doe regarded the change in scenery as a testament to Reuben's ability to handle business, in every sense. Moments ago he had walked in on Blanco as the giant of a man snapped Von Kelsig's fingers like kindling and now he regarded Reuben as a calm, down-to-earth office figurehead. Doe observed this to be an extension of the manner in which Reuben dealt with his business, evidenced further by his effortlessly taking the monstrous Von Kelsig and whittling him down to something bare and raw and naked. Something weak. All the while establishing himself as an almost barbarous rival to some of Detroit's most notorious drug dealers, Reuben gave every impression that he was most definitely bankable. On the other hand, Blanco felt threatened and humbled being in the presence of a man who his sources told him had been the premier gun-runner in a number of states for a number of years. His sources had never let him down yet. "Before we get to the guts of this conversation I want to ask you about Von Kelsig." Doe began. "What of him?" Reuben asked. "You plan on using him to part-finance our venture?" said Doe, and Blanco nodded in retort. "Then I want his file, or whatever you keep in the way of details. I want it all, and I don't just want a cheap copy of it. I want assurance that the income is constant, legitimate and above all to my satisfaction. Yes?" Doe raised his eyebrows, though under the shadow of his fedora all Reuben could go on was tone of voice. "Yes, understood. You'll do well to note that I am a very flexible partner and accommodating in every sense of the word. " Reuben drew his hands back from the paperweight and rubbed them together with a confident smile upon his face. "Excellent...under the circumstances you can understand I'd prefer it if I had control over this particular situation, though I would appreciate it if Von Kelsig was left under the impression he answered to you..." Blanco happily nodded in agreement as Doe sat upright and perched forward onto Reuben's desk. "I want you to release him." Reuben's featured almost shot straight off his face. "Wh-What??" He cried. "I want you to let him go. He is too headstrong to keep here. Give him up and he will be as confused as you are now. Remind him of the terms of his stay here, remind him just how much control you have over him, then just let him go...and have one of your men shadow him." Doe continued. "..."Now, this proposition..." He changed the subject with such immediacy Blanco was too overwhelmed by his authoritative nature to answer back, and sat back in his chair placing his arms by his side and relaxing in a defiant show of fearlessness, unafraid of any questions in the face of such a lucrative opportunity. "...I'm aware of your reputation south of the border, the kind of rapport you hold with suppliers and traffickers is unheard of in my circle. Given your intention to expand I feel we should explore the possibility of a conglomeration." Doe licked his lips and tipped his hat a little lower over his eyes. "I can guarantee if we merge there will be few men short of the authorities who will be of any threat to us. Combining the routes and contacts I've established in the narcotics trade with the prowess and efficiency of your heavy weapons empire would be like...like..." "Like making Superman immune to Kryptonite." Doe interrupted as Reuben fantasised about the prospective deal. "I've worked very hard and very ruthlessly to get to where I am today and there are few rivals who can offer the kind of service I do in America. You know yourself how fickle trading can be between individuals, to be able to safeguard transactions and deals with a partnership such as this would make our operation run flawlessly......however..." Doe held his breath and caressed his jawline with one gloved hand while Reuben sat before him, heart pounding rapidly in his chest in anticipation. "I must ask...you have complete and total authority over all proceedings in your organization I assume?" Doe asked, continuing his line of questioning as Reuben cocked an inquisitive eyebrow. "By which I mean, does anyone else have their thumb in the plum pudding or is this as lucrative a deal for me as it is for you?" Doe squinted his eyes and leaned forward an inch towards Reuben, who replied with a casual crossing of the leg, resting his clasped hands atop his knee. "The only other dealing I have is internationally. You would be my exclusive partner with regards to business in South America and the States." He replied with that confident smile. "Is that so? Well, in the interest of further development...just who do you deal with internationally?" Doe asked, shooting back a devious grin of his own that Reuben could make out, noting that the smile wrinkled at his cheek below a scar under Doe's right eye... "Well, for that kind of information I'm afraid I'll need a decision on where we stand. It'd be foolish to consider divulging such information to-..." Reuben stopped, cut off suddenly by Doe. "Oh we have a deal Blanco! We have a deal." He hastened, extending his hand in a rash manner and shaking Reuben's. "Now who is your international contact?" Doe pushed, straightening his back and cocking his head slightly. "No one knows his name..." Reuben began, detecting a less than subtle hint of mounting anxiety in Doe's change in posture. Something about this conversation was gauging his interest, though it wasn't the prospect of global expansion. "He calls himself Se7yn........." Doe's face slackened, any trace of a smile wiped clean as the words that escaped Blanco's mouth plastered a vacant expression over his grin. His eyes glanced to the side before meeting Reuben's once more as he sat back slowly, placing his hands on the armrests of the recliner and tilting his head back just enough to let Blanco catch a glimpse of his face in its arrested state. He would've had the looks of a film star if it wasn't for that long scar running through his right eye, Blanco thought. ---- December 29th, 2006... She held her arm loosely in the crook of his elbow as they left the hospital in Saint Johnsbury. It appeared she was supporting him but the sensation rippling across her skin told her she was doting on him. They stepped into a waiting people carrier as the clouds shifted across a grey sky and threatened to powder the land with a light flurry of snowflakes. "That went better than expected." Von Kelsig chuckled, taking care to elevate his hands. This was his third trip to the hospital, and like the two previous visits he left with both hands stinging like lightning and a stiff upper lip holding his head high. To be seen by a doctor known by any name other than Menendez was a joy, and being able to pass off the horrific injuries as a holiday-related accident left Von Kelsig with a great sense of relief. For now he was more than willing to sweep the events of the last couple of months under the rug and attempt to get his life back on track. "Yeah, thanks for picking up the tab for me on the Bupe..." Gloria smiled sweetly at the giant. In the days following his release both he and Gloria had spent most of their time in separate rooms like grotesque maggots in the metaphorical chrysalis. Swimming on sheets of satin and bile and sweat as their guts contorted and their brains burned in their skulls, the withdrawal sunk in deeper with what felt like each passing hour. Seeing the full extent of his injuries upon arrival, Bruce Shanahan made time between engagements in his schedule to set Von Kelsig and Gloria up with an appointment to be seen by a doctor at the hospital in Saint Johnsbury who he knew from experience could provide treatment with no probing questions asked. "Is there enough of that stuff for the two of us?" Von Kelsig asked, gesturing to the paper bag in Gloria's grasp as they sat in the back of the cab and took off for Montpelier. Von Kelsig had his wounds treated and was prescribed the appropriate antibiotics, while Gloria was given a supply of Buprenorphine after an emergency appointment with a counsellor from the Howard Center in South Burlington who was at the hospital to promote the clinic. Von Kelsig paid for her prescription and they took their leave. "Not really. They gave me a week's worth and told me to pick up the prescription at the local pharmacy when I run out. Two sublingual pills every day, morning and night for two weeks...what does sublingual mean?" She asked innocently, while Von Kelsig studied the road as the cab took them back home. "I think it means you sit the pill under your tongue and let it dissolve..." Von Kelsig replied with a concerned look on his face as he turned to his wild-haired angel. "...What am I going to do? I can't get a job if there are drugs in my system and if I put myself into rehab it'll be on my medical file that I was prescribed something for heroin addiction." He sighed and shifted in his seat, feeling cramped even in the spacious cab of the people carrier. Gloria stared at the floor and contemplated their situation before producing the box of pills from the paper bag and popping one Buprenorphine into her mouth, manoeuvring it underneath her tongue as they drove across the countryside towards Wyatt Manor. "Hey this stuff's pretty good..." Gloria smiled, nearly five minutes after taking the pill. "Tastes kinda bland but it's almost dissolved and my stomach feels better already!" A pleasant sensation swam through his gut as butterflies erupted from within, momentarily drowning out the pounding savagery of withdrawal in his own torso. He loved that innocent smile of hers, such a pure emotion. Gloria wasn't old, barely middle-aged in fact, but her looks betrayed her youth thanks to the effects of being addicted to heroin. She wasn't beautiful, nor ugly...just average...and innocent. Perhaps it was her purity he was attracted to, he found it endearing that she didn't know what some words meant or how certain things worked or why some people did the things they did. He embraced her naivety and felt the need to care for her more when she needed help. Gloria on the other hand saw a pillar of strength in Von Kelsig and endured the first few nights of agony as the withdrawal symptoms tore at her insides and her mind, though never once did she so much as contemplate running away in a bid to score drugs. "Nearly there." Von Kelsig remarked as the cab came to a T junction, bearing left along a straight country road. Beyond the field to their right lay the dead forest they had both crossed from the village of Brayston, which lay on the opposite side of the woods. Bruce had told them to leave the woods on the opposite side from which they had entered so as not to further provoke the villagers. Taking care not to bump his hands against any of the dead trees, Von Kelsig traversed the woodland with Gloria and emerged on the other side to find a back road running the length of the woods to a junction just on the horizon, nestled between two fields the whole way along. Trudging over the grass they left the field by way of an old rusted gate and walked a couple hundred yards towards the junction to find a lay-by and a bus stop on the opposite side of the road. Though it appeared neglected, sure enough a bus appeared within twenty minutes of them arriving, which took them on their way to the hospital. That same bus stop now inched closer and closer away from the horizon as the cab approached it from the south. "This your stop?" Asked the taxi driver, a small pale middle-aged man with a beer gut and a head of tufty greying hair poking out from underneath a farmer's cap. "Yeah right here, thanks." Von Kelsig called out as the cab swerved across the road and came to a stop in the lay-by. As the fare flashed up on the little counter on the dashboard, Von Kelsig turned to Gloria and smiled through a sigh. "Would you mind?" He cocked his leg up and gestured to his pocket. "My wallet?" "How did you pay for our treatment if you can't get your hands into your pockets?" Gloria asked, chuckling softly as she reached for the wallet. "Ahh, the helpful doctor handled that problem!" He replied as Gloria pulled the wallet from the pocket and counted out the cash for the fare. Hopping out, she held the door open as Von Kelsig slid across the seat and exited the same way. As the cab set off down the road in search of another fare, they glanced across the field to the dead woods. Popping the wallet back into Von Kelsig's pocket Gloria led the way back towards the Manor the way they had come earlier in the day. "So, how are you feeling now?" Gloria asked as she cranked open the rusted gate and ushered Von Kelsig into the field. "My hands are still throbbing and damned itchy since the bandages got changed." He replied as they made their way towards the woods. "My stomach is still turning and I got this horrible sour taste in my mouth like battery acid." "Maybe if we ask Jack to send one of his helpers to the hospital he could get the prescription for Bupe and just get it for you that way...so you don't have to worry about people finding out about what happened!" Gloria continued, much to the surprise of Von Kelsig who managed a chortle as they entered the woods. "Maybe. I don't want to impose on Bruce too much, he's kind enough to let us stay at his home...I don't want it to seem like we're taking over and giving orders." He said slowly and thoughtfully, focusing on the uneven ground as the roots seemed to grab at his feet. "You can only ask." Gloria added. "Yeah...guess you're right." He agreed, as they passed through a small clearing and made out the silhouette of the Manor on the hill beyond the edge of the woods up ahead. They trundled on, Von Kelsig shuddering nervously as his left hand came perilously close to being jagged by a branch jutting down from the bare canopy of the woods. A few minutes of silence passed before Gloria built up enough courage to ask her next question, which she phrased as casually as she could while she climbed over a felled tree. "So y'know we've known each other for a little bit now and been through some shit and all.........but I realized a while ago I don't even know your name, your first name I mean." "I don't know your last name, Gloria..." Von Kelsig replied without missing a beat, covering his embarrassment with a smirk as he followed her over the fallen tree. "You first!" She turned and playfully kicked mud at him as he heaved one tree trunk of a leg over the thick branches of another on the ground. Stopping in front of the log he smiled as she took a step back towards him and smiled brightly, while he stood straight and bowed his head. "Very well, madame." He began. "You may call me Angelus, Angelus Von Kelsig." "And you may call me Gloria Carmichael. No fancy middle name like you I'm afraid, sir!" She stood straight and winked and for a moment they stared at one another in silence. "Pleased to meet you Gloria." Von Kelsig said softly, and right there in the serene and surreal dead woods they came to know each other. With a wink of his own, Von Kelsig and Gloria continued on towards the Manor. As they stepped over the broken wrought-iron fence and made their way up the cracked pavement of the driveway a figure emerged from the tree line on the opposite side that led to Brayston. Hurrying up the hill the figure chased after them as they walked past the statues of Cthulhu and came to the front door. To their left they noticed the curtains behind one of the large ground floor windows flapping shut before the front door opened in front of them a few moments after, and they were greeted by Bruce Shanahan. "Hello Jack!" Von Kelsig called out. "Everything went-..." "Who's that??" Shanahan cut him off, thrusting a finger out at the figure making its way up the driveway to the front door where they now stood. Turning back, a shiver ran down Von Kelsig's spine as he noticed the cloaked figure advancing towards them. On closer inspection he found the cloak to be a trench coat, while a fedora adorned the head of the man who was only fifty yards or so from them now. "Von Kelsig when I agreed to let you stay here I specifically asked that you never divulge the whereabouts of this Manor to anyone! Who is it?!" Bruce said, the frustration building in his voice. "I don't know, Bruce!" Von Kelsig snapped back. "We came from the other side of the tree line, we weren't followed and I never told anyone where I was staying! Nor did she!" He gestured to Gloria, who had taken a step behind Von Kelsig while Bruce stepped out onto the front porch, closing the door behind him. Together they watched as the figure approached, stopping several feet short of the front door. The tension thickened the air like smog as Bruce kept an eye on the stranger, while Von Kelsig immediately took a step towards the figure who stood a whole foot and an inch shorter than his gargantuan 7'2" frame. "Who are you?" He demanded in his most imposing manner, though he knew he recognized the intruder from somewhere. "Who am I?" The figure replied, his tone of voice and manner completely different to what it had been in the past. Tilting his fedora away from his face and holding his hands out by his side in a show of submission at the obvious signs of aggression he awaited Von Kelsig's response. "I'm the bastard negotiated your release from Reuben Blanco. Show a little respect, eh?" Von Kelsig cocked his head, his eyes narrowing as the stranger took a step forward. "I believe he referred to me as John Doe. Stupid alias from a stupid thug. Trust when I say I have no real associations, or desire to associate, with Blanco." Von Kelsig's heartbeat quickened as John took another step forward, removing his fedora. The seamless jump from character took him aback. "Blanco and I have business to deal. Something personal that only he can help with. Quite unfortunate, but he did mention you a time or two. When I actually saw you, well... couldn't help but lend a hand." Bruce tapped Gloria on the shoulder and ushered her inside the Manor, leaving Von Kelsig and 'John Doe' on the front step as the snow began to fall. "Why should I believe you? How do I know you're not here on behalf of Reuben?" Von Kelsig asked, clenching his open hand into a fist. "Because if I was, Von Kelsig, that pretty little thing of yours would be dead by now. Or dying in slow and terribly painful ways, depending on my mood." Doe spoke honestly, and Von Kelsig grit his teeth and shook at the thought. "So WHO are you?!" He snapped, stepping chest-to-face with Doe and raising his fist to John's eye level. "The name's Rayne." He paused, looking Von Kelsig in the eye and taking a deep breath. "Tyler Rayne. You and I, my friend... we have business to discuss."
