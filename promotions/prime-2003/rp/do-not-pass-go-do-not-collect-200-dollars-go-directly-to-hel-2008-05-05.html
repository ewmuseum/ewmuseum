Do not pass go, do not collect 200 dollars, go directly to hell.

5 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-5-08 Simply American Beauty Hoyt Williams kicks a diet coke can down a slummy street on Chicago’s South side. He is wearing jeans, a t-shirt, and tan boots. The voice over of Hoyt’s voice speaks as Hoyt continues kicking the can. Hoyt Williams (V.O.): The world is a lost cause in the eyes of God, and I’m now the dark angel here to carry out justice. Hoyt watches as a whore gets into a car and slowly drive away. Hoyt Williams (V.O.): Whores whose innocents have long been lost along with the values and self-respect in toe with the rest of humanity. Dangling their hypnotic flesh melons like neon signs advertising, “Where open, come on in”. Selling sultry sin feeding their habit of addiction by adding up dick, after dick, and disease, after disease, to earn enough cash to praise their god known as Heroine. Hoyt watches as a whore buys a vile of crack from a corner dealer. He just keeps kicking the can down the street now with a little more force. Hoyt Williams (V.O.): I hope she got a receipt! No returns, no refunds on the streets of addiction. It’s a fine ecosystem that can’t be broken by law, nor changed by structure. The whores are the bottom feeders who ‘earn’ the money from the rich white sinners who don’t get enough from their uppity prude wives. The sluts then take that money, and use it on the drugs sold by the middle men and tools of the gangs who run the streets. Where is God? Where is Satan? Where is humanity? Perhaps they are all in an ally somewhere sharing a needle laying lifeless lost to the world and forgotten by the American dream. Hoyt stops and watches as the sun vanishes behind a dark cloud, as the warmth of spring falls back to the cold of the winter. Hoyt Williams (V.O.): America was once the one nation under God, but it’s since fallen. It has given up on its dream of hard work fighting for the dream of a better tomorrow. Nobody dreams anymore, and nobody works anymore. Who has time for faith when the new Grand Theft Auto is on the market? Why read the bible, why dream of tomorrow, why work for mankind when you can sit on your ass and live a violent and morally bankrupt life in a world of fiction? The rain starts to falls as Hoyt just watches as the rain soaks his face. Hoyt Williams (V.O.): Man fought to remove the shackles of slavery for the dream of freedom. Yet now they have given up. Nobody works for themselves but rather a task master. Even those who think they work for themselves really serve the protective whip of the master known as Government. If you’re good they will give you health care, protection, and even food if you want. Thank you master! The personal savior of PRIME looks up to the sky once again this time lifting his arms and reciting out loud Exodus 20:2–17. Hoyt Williams: I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery; Do not have any other gods before me. You shall not make for yourself an idol, whether in the form of anything that is in heaven above, or that is on the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth. You shall not bow down to them or worship them; for I the Lord your God am a jealous God, punishing children for the iniquity of parents, to the third and the fourth generation of those who reject me, but showing steadfast love to the thousandth generation of those who love me and keep my commandments. You shall not make wrongful use of the name of the Lord your God, for the Lord will not acquit anyone who misuses his name. Remember the Sabbath day and keep it holy. For six days you shall labour and do all your work. But the seventh day is a Sabbath to the Lord your God; you shall not do any work—you, your son or your daughter, your male or female slave, your livestock, or the alien resident in your towns. For in six days the Lord made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that is in them, but rested the seventh day; therefore the Lord blessed the Sabbath day and consecrated it. Honor your father and your mother, so that your days may be long in the land that the Lord your God is giving you. You shall not murder. You shall not commit adultery. You shall not steal. You shall not bear false witness against your neighbour. You shall not covet your neighbour’s house; you shall not covet your neighbour’s wife, or male or female slave, or ox, or donkey, or anything that belongs to your neighbour. A young black men walks up to Hoyt slowly looking at him like he’s crazy. Leroy: Damn dawg take it down a notch you trying to get us busted? Hoyt Williams: The world is busted so what does it matter? Leroy: Shit negro, I’m not going to get into this shit with you. You’re bat shit crazy, and that’s enough for me to stay the fuck away. Now you got the green or do I need to split this scene? Hoyt Williams: I got the cash if you got my green. Leroy: A ounce of the finest weed and X in the city!! Hoyt Williams: Very good. Leroy: You want to ride the cocaine train? Hoyt Williams: No, I need to keep my head in the game this week. Leroy: So is Armageddon coming? Hoyt Williams: Like Peter North. Leroy: Who? Hoyt Williams: Never mind. I need your judgment on something. Leroy: I’m a dealer not a healer or a sage. Hoyt Williams: Just fucking listen. I was playing monopoly last night with a buddy of mine. I had his ass beat as I was the king of Marvin Gardens. You dig? Leroy: Monopoly? Fucking white people! Hoyt Williams: Don’t play the race card, just listen. So I have his ass broke as an honest man in the ghetto. He’s hanging on to his dignity as best he can but he’s one rent away from losing. So he rolls the dice and lands on Luxury Tax and has to pay the 75 dollar fee. Leroy: Are we really talking about this? Hoyt Williams: So this guy can’t pay his taxes, you know? Leroy: Should get my motherfucking accountant I haven’t paid taxes since the first Snoop Dawg album dropped. Hoyt Williams: So he says he lost but I didn’t beat him but rather the game. Leroy: I would have beat his ass with the ironing token. Hoyt Williams: So I won, right? The game can’t win it’s him verses me not us versus the board. Leroy: You smoke before we meet up? Hoyt Williams: Damn right. But really I won, not him? Leroy: Shit. White people and their problems. Hoyt Williams: You know God doesn’t see white nor black. Leroy: No the honkie in the sky too busy playing Monopoly or some other cracker shit. Learn to play craps you bitches. Hoyt Williams: Ok, what ever. Leroy: Is it true you have a match this week? Hoyt Williams: What is truth? Leroy: It’s the motherfucking truth slick. Hoyt Williams: Oh, well then yes. Leroy: Hoyt has risen!! Well go kick some ass. Hoyt Williams: The book of Hoyt is not finished yet as a new chapter begins. Simply Beautiful will be simply punished with pain and suffering at the hands of god. No daygo is going to mess up this heavenly body. Leroy: Good luck. Hoyt Williams: You don’t need luck when you have God on your side. =w=
