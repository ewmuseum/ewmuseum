Into The Abyss: Part XXIV - The Evil That Men Do

25 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25-09-09 The speedometer taunted him as he fought to keep the Buick at a steady 60mph, the needle dancing over the line mockingly. Coasting past the slower traffic Detective Barnes kept his eyes fixed ahead, expertly weaving his way down South State Road 63. His partner Greco studied the horizon, as anxious as a child on their way to Disney World desperate to see the rising spires of Cinderella's castle. "Are we there yet?" Barnes asked, juggling the steering wheel between his knees while he fumbled around for his cigarettes. Greco consulted the road map, running a finger along I-70 and down Road 63 to roughly where they were. "'Bout a half hour, roughly. Keep an eye out for the turn onto Bureau Drive." He replied. In truth he had no idea how far it was. His time on the journey had been spent studying Reuben Blanco's profile, and trying to figure out some kind of connection with Von Kelsig before they even arrived at the federal penitentiary. Barnes meanwhile was focused solely on putting the 150 or so miles behind them to further this infernal case of theirs. Neither detective had rested since pulling up Blanco's file. While their contact in Detroit had continued to follow Von Kelsig on to Calgary, Greco and Barnes had researched as much as they could while the Captain of the precinct put word through to Terre Haute about their impending arrival. Here they were now, cruising towards the first solid lead of the case. Barnes felt his heart thumping in his chest, willing him on. He was good at what he did, he worked hard to get to where he was today. That's why Julian Greco had been assigned as his partner, to learn from him and some day become the kind of detective he was. His palms felt damp against the wheel, the anticipation incredible. Only twice in his career had a case slipped through his fingers; in the first instance a serial rapist who believed he was sowing God's seed had, to his shock, committed suicide after picking up a nasty infection from one of his victims. The second involved a paedophile who fell to a lynch mob before he could make an arrest. Barnes put his all into each and every case, and reminiscing about those two losses served to push him harder to snare Von Kelsig. His Captain was intent on closing the case when forensics turned up nothing at the crime scene, but his persistence paid off when he convinced his superior that Von Kelsig must have played a part in the deaths of Gloria Carmichael and Larry DeLancy. "Hey, take it easy man..." Greco muttered as Barnes overtook an old Ford pick-up and roared back into the lane before an oncoming MACK truck put a stop to their sleuthing once and for all. Greco waited for a retort, and swallowed nervously when Barnes simply wiped the sweat from his forehead and pushed harder on the accelerator. "Where's this damn turn?" Barnes growled after fifteen minutes. "If we've missed it so help me Go-..." "There it is!" Greco whooped excitedly, seeing the unmistakable outline of the federal penitentiary on the horizon. "Where?! How far??" Barnes snapped back, looking desperately for the junction. "No, no. Not the turn, the federal penitentiary. I can make it out over there, we're nearly there!" said Greco, a relieved smile spread across his clean-shaven face. To be sure, he ran his index fingers under his bifocals and wiped his eyes, blinking rapidly and staring at the horizon. Ahead of them, not several miles away loomed the monolithic walls of Terre Haute Federal Correctional Complex. Surrounded by acres of arid land it truly captured, quite literally, the essence of freedom in every sense. To be on the outside looking in the surrounding area represented a void, a nothingness at the centre of which was one paltry sliver of existence, which in itself was a dark hole from which no man could escape. To be on the inside looking out, that same endless spread of land represented limitless possibilities beyond the federal penitentiary walls. It was as a blank canvas upon which any inmate could paint his hopes and dreams from the ground up, though he might never realize it. Spying the junction ahead, Barnes bit his tongue and pushed the Buick hard. The needle practically jumped past 60mph as he swerved past several cars and shifted down the gears on the approach, eliciting a pained bellow from the guts of the Buick. Turning off the main road onto Bureau, the Buick kicked up a cloud of dust as Barnes excitedly sped towards the federal penitentiary. Greco hastily organized all his files back into a bundle and slotted them back into their folder, eager to pursue their lead. Of the three hundred plus spaces available in the parking lot, only seventy or so were occupied. Spying a free space directly in front of the main entrance, Barnes parked the Buick up and sighed with relief. "Let's do this." He said, only to be met with the sound of the passenger door closing as Greco got out and stretched his legs. Barnes followed suit, taking a moment to relish the feeling of his muscles tightening and relaxing. Greco held his folder underarm, waiting patiently for the next order from his partner. "Alright," Barnes muttered, staring up at the ominous walls behind the front building, "watch and learn buddy. Watch and learn..." ---- "......All questions must be submitted through the Deputy Captain. The questioning will take place in the Special Confinement Unit, or SCU. You will have no contact whatsoever with the inmate, so please ensure everything you require is presented to the Deputy. Any questions?" The crippling monotony of the federal penitentiary seemed to infect even the tone of voice of the Operations Lieutenant. Barnes and Greco strode alongside the O.L. as he sounded off a list of rules and regulations. Barnes was all-too familiar with the procedure, and listened intently as they stopped outside a locker room. "Make your way inside and remove your jackets and loose objects from your persons. I'll take that folder off you for now." said the Lieutenant, holding a hand out for Greco to pass him the files. The detective immediately complied, fazed slightly by the military precision with which this complex seemed to run by. The detectives emerged a few minutes later, stripped of anything that wasn't hiding their shame. Handing the folder back to Greco, the Lieutenant took both locker keys off of them and marched off down the hall. "You'll get the keys back upon leaving the SCU." He growled, tossing them under the gap of a steel mesh-enforced plexiglass window, behind which a sullen-faced middle aged woman with a beehive and tribal tattoos covering her forearms received and deposited them in the key box. The three men passed through two sets of secure doors as they waded deeper into the belly of the beast. With nothing to stimulate them, Greco and Barnes stared ahead at what lay beyond the next secure entry, only to be disappointed with the same beige hallways reeking of the same bland detergents. Clutching the folder tightly, Greco began asking himself questions in an attempt to understand the procedures of Terre Haute. Facts and plausibilities kept him occupied while they traversed the lifeless corridors of the federal penitentiary. "Why can't we interview Blanco-..." "The inmate." The Lieutenant interrupted. "Sorry, why can't we interview the inmate ourselves...or say his name?" Greco said, adding an extra query to the question after being cut off so abruptly. "The inmate whom you require information from is high risk. He will be detained here until his dying day, however his accomplices, employees, partners and such like are still roaming around out there. Manipulation is this particular inmate's weapon of choice. Some choose to bully their way to a dominant role, others use more devious tactics. This inmate in particular uses every trick in the book to get messages to the outside, to relay orders. To combat this we don't let him communicate with anyone outside of the warden or the guards. Any visit he gets is closely monitored and recorded. All visitors are vetted before entry into the SCU..." "So what's the problem with us interviewing him? You've got our credentials and whatnot..." Greco replied, somewhat insubordinately. "Like I said," the Lieutenant continued, "the inmate has far too many connections for us to take a risk. Eight months ago he nearly got out after relaying messages to his visitors in code, telling them to target the guards in an escape bid. As for his title? Unless confined to the SCU no one is permitted to speak his name or any business pertaining to him. This complex has ears Detective, and none of them can be trusted. Ergo, no one outside of personnel is to know the inmate even exists. The sooner people forget about him the easier it is for us to do our job. Does that answer your question, Detective?" Greco nodded satisfactorily, rolling his eyes when the Lieutenant turned away and stopped at the next secure entry, staring up at a surveillance camera and awaiting confirmation to continue. In the next corridor the detectives perked up at the sight of a uniformed officer and an armed guard standing in front of a cell door. The sign above them read "Solitary Confinement Unit". "OK. This is where I hand you over to Deputy Captain Bryce. Good luck with your investigation." said the Lieutenant, saluting the Deputy and spinning on his heels. "Detectives, I am Deputy Captain Bryce and I will be handling the questioning of the inmate." He began as a loud buzzer prompted the Lieutenant's exit. "I'm Detective Barnes, this is Detective Greco." Barnes replied, shaking hands with the Deputy. "Unfortunately we haven't prepared any written documentation for the questioning, we were under the impression we would personally be conducting the interview." Bryce narrowed his eyes at Barnes, alert to the sudden possibility that the supposed Detectives could be accomplices of Blanco. Their credentials checked out perfectly, but Bryce never made Deputy Captain by ignoring his gut. "Tell you what. There's a tannoy in the visitor's room. You can relay your questions to me and I'll interview the inmate myself. You can monitor the phone line and take what information you need." "Alright, that works." Barnes nodded gruffly. "I wasn't asking your opinion, Detective." Bryce snapped back. Holding his hands up innocently, Barnes bowed his head and waited as the Deputy Captain buzzed them through the cell door and took them to the office overlooking the visitor's room. Another officer sat at the control desk, operating the CCTV and setting up the audio surveillance for the room. "I'll be on Line One, Davis." Bryce said authoritatively. The officer at the desk nodded obediently and went about his duties while the Deputy Captain turned back to the Detectives. "Davis will record the interview for you. You speak into this microphone only when prompted. The glass in here is mirrored so you're invisible to the inmate, however if you speak into the microphone without being prompted there's a good chance he'll hear you and make this process very difficult for us. Blanco knows nothing other than he is needed to answer a few questions, let's keep it that way gentlemen, OK?" Greco shot Barnes a nervous look, to which his partner retorted with a stern narrowing of the eyes. Bryce studied their eventual nods of agreement and left for the visitor's room. Officer Davis motioned for them to take a seat by his side at the main console, while he tinkered with a joystick and settled the camera view on the first booth in the room. Grabbing the microphone from the corner of his desk he placed it between the detectives, motioning to the button at its base. "Push this when you want to speak to the Deputy Captain. After the inmate has answered I'll mute the call so you can ask your next question. Clear?" Again the detectives nodded as Bryce took his place at booth one. Greco watched the monitor with Davis while Barnes leaned over and peered out of the glass into the visitor's room below. The video feed he noticed had no time delay, and responded the very same second that Bryce moved. The room was designed for non-contact. The walls were stark white, though appeared a shadowy grey in the low light. A partition separated the inmates from the visitors, spaced into individual booths each with a telephone hanging on either side of the thick glass windows, criss-crossed with durable steel mesh through the glass itself. Protection guaranteed. "You shut off visiting hours for us to conduct this interview?" Barnes asked gruffly. "Of course," replied Davis, "the warden doesn't want anyone eavesdropping on this." Barnes shuffled his feet, gazing over the many controls on the console. "Hm. Bet that sat well with the other lags." A buzzer went off and a door on the opposite side of the Deputy Captain opened. Greco and Barnes stared slack-jawed as the monstrous frame of Reuben Blanco plodded into view. In his glory days he wore the finest suits tailored personally for his bulk by high profile designers. He made sure he was immaculate so that his partners, employees and peers knew he ran a tight ship. Appearance was everything on the street, but now? Now he wore the trademark lime shirt and trousers of the Terre Haute correctional complex. Creased and ill-fitting, the clothing quite obviously ired the giant. "Jesus..." Barnes gasped, taken aback by the sheer size of the convicted felon. Greco could only watch in silence as their lead took a seat across from Bryce, holding a shark-like stare with the Deputy Captain. He had memorized all the facts about Blanco, and noted his tremendous build from his stats. To see him in person left both detectives breathless. Blanco was a mountain of a man, or more accurately, a volcano. His neck was a large roll of fat wobbling over an equally flabby torso. "Christ...he is one fat son of a bitch." Barnes whispered. "...He's a goddamn giant." Greco added, comparing the stature of the crime lord to Bryce who was at best 5'10" and 200lbs. Blanco appeared to be twice the size in every aspect, and then Greco thought back to the meeting with Von Kelsig, and how huge he was in person. Blanco looked as though he might equal or best the wrestler in terms of height and weight. The weight was a definite. Blanco ran a hand through his thin head of hair, slicking back the already greasy and greying strands. He wore wiry spectacles that magnified his almost alien gaze. Davis watched as both men picked up their telephone receivers simultaneously and at the same time opened the line for them to communicate. "Hello Deputy." Blanco growled, gripping the receiver tightly in his ham fist so much so that Bryce could hear plastic cracking through his earpiece. He could tell from Reuben's tone that the giant was in a mood for fucking around, and took a moment to compose himself before replying. "We need you to answer some questions for me, Blanco." He said dryly. "Oh, really?" Blanco answered back surprised, "About what? Do you want me to confess to some other shit I didn't do and tag on another several life sentences to the three I got already?" "No, we need information on something." "Who's we?" Blanco asked, glancing up at the mirrored glass behind Bryce. "The penitentiary." Bryce shot back, not missing a beat. Up in the surveillance room the two detectives shared a look of anxiety. "Well shoot, Deputy. Make a nice change to talk about something other than death threats or laundry duty for once..." The process began. First, Davis cut the connection between the two phones and motioned for Barnes and Greco to push the mic button and pitch their first question to Bryce. After releasing it, Davis resumed communications in the visitor's room. It was a trying task, but for the sake of keeping Blanco in line it had to be done. "Do you know an Angelus Von Kelsig?" Bryce asked eventually, easily covering the silence with a piercing stare and a stony disposition. "You're shitting me..." Blanco replied, his jaw hanging in disbelief and his eyes wide with shock. "Tell me you didn't just say that name." "Sorry to say I did," Bryce said, pulling himself closer to the glass, "do you know that name?" Blanco rubbed his eyes and sighed. Visibly frustrated, the giant let himself calm down knowing full well if he did anything out of order he'd be locked back up into solitary in the blink of an eye. "Yeah I know that name. Angelus Von Kelsig. The wrestler?" "Don't act dumb on this one Blanco," Bryce warned him, "what do you know about him? Did you two have business together?" "Somewhat. Von Kelsig and I met a few years back. He put one of my associates out of business and I took it upon myself to claim the losses incurred. Things got a little heavy-handed when he refused to take responsibility, and before he could recompense me he disappeared and took one of my people with him." "Can you elaborate on that?" Bryce asked after a moment's pause to receive the next question from Barnes. "What is there to elaborate? I had this girl working for me, running errands for me. She was a real trick, looked like a Living Dead Doll. Had a filthy mouth on her too. Come to think of it she was just filthy all over. Von Kelsig got a hard-on for her and disappeared with her when our little business arrangement went sour." "Her name?" Bryce said abruptly. "Gloria. Gloria Carmichael." Reuben replied, the information running off his tongue as though it had happened yesterday. None of the men attending the interview could know just how deeply those names were bored into Blanco's brain. Bryce lowered the receiver for a moment, staring at the ground as though contemplating his next move. Blanco couldn't tell that he was waiting for the next question from two detectives hidden behind the mirrored screen. "What was Von Kelsig like? What do you recall of his character?" The memories came flooding back to Blanco. Seeing the pitiful giant tied to a chair in his own headquarters, soiling himself when he wasn't crying like a bitch or bleating like a lost lamb. He remembered the sounds of Von Kelsig's screams, the smell of his flesh burning and the look in his eyes right before they rolled into the back of his head as he injected dose after dose of heroin into the poor bastard's veins. "He was...reserved." Blanco finally answered, "Mostly quiet, never spoke out of turn, very placid. Your average Boy Scout. My only problem with him is that he screwed me on our business deal." Up in the surveillance room, Greco ran his hand through his wax-styled hair and turned to Barnes with a sigh. "Ask him if Von Kelsig ever approached him about any 'work', or if any of his contacts ever dealt with him in the past." A moment of silence ensued. Blanco examined Bryce through the glass, wishing he could just let rip on the true nature of their dealings together. Unbeknownst to Bryce, the sudden twinkle in Reuben's eye was a flash of ecstasy, knowing he had a secret the Feds would never know and if the day ever came when he escaped this nightmare he would get his revenge on the man who wronged him. He wasn't going to let on one goddamn iota to this Deputy Prick. "Did Von Kelsig ever have any business with yourself or any of your associates at any time?" Bryce said, relaying the question from above. "Can you elaborate on that?" Blanco grinned, playing the Deputy Captain at his own game, or what he thought was the Deputy's game. "Did Angelus Von Kelsig at any time solicit you or your men into accepting a contract for a hit?" Blanco lost his breath. Yet again in the space of one conversation he was dumbfounded by what he was hearing. What the hell had Von Kelsig done? His twisted mind began to work its magic, and while the Deputy Captain waited for an answer Blanco orchestrated a scenario in his mind, working up an instantaneous plot to get more information. "...He might have done." Came the callous retort. Bryce would have taken the hesitation as a sign of something evil at work, but with two Memphis detectives squawking over the tannoy all of a sudden he dismissed any potential plotting as a sign of simple cooperation. "Who did he approach and what did he ask? Be very specific." Bryce explained. A wry grin spread across Blanco's fat face, his eyes narrowing almost to slits as he gleefully considered his next move. "Deputy...please don't mistake this for contempt..but what is this information worth exactly?" A chill ran down the Deputy Captain's spine. He confronted insolence like this on a daily basis, and had been warned by his superior to punish any attempt at blackmail with brutal force. Watching from the surveillance room, Davis was unnerved by the situation, even though he witnessed events like this as often as Bryce. Barnes and Greco meanwhile were falling over themselves to squeeze Blanco for more information, completely unaware of what was running through the minds of the two Terre Haute personnel. "He knows who Von Kelsig contracted. He is guilty! Tell him to ask Blanco for the name, we need a name!" Greco yelled excitedly. Davis swatted his hand as it came perilously close to opening the comms in the visitor's room. "Just slow down there partner," Barnes said in a long drawl not unlike The Duke, "we need to tread carefully here. If we get one name from this scumbag we've bagged ourselves a double homicide." Down in the visitor's room Bryce stared a hole through Blanco, while the fat bastard could only grin back, knowing he had a hand to play now. "Blanco, might I remind you that these calls are recorded and any attempt at blackmailing an officer will result in severe punishment beyond what you can comprehend." The Deputy Captain's words snapped Barnes and Greco out of their daydream of capturing Von Kelsig, and clenching his fists Barnes seethed at the prospect of losing his lead. "We can't lose this. No way in hell can we..." He muttered under his breath, before pressing the mic button in, "Bryce we need a name here. If we don't get something from Blanco we lose this case and a potential murderer walks free from any prosecution. If you have to lie to him to get the information then do it. Jesus tell him he's getting out today, tell him anything! Just get that name." The Deputy Captain massaged his temples with the thumb and middle finger of his free hand. Blanco remained stoically poised, with that grin tattooed to his face. Something was about to give, he could feel it. "If you know the name of the contact Von Kelsig used, I need it. Now. I know you know you've got an edge here, and I-..." "And what? What can you do for a man who is facing a century of jail time? I give you a name and for what? It won't buy me freedom. It probably wouldn't even buy me an extra five minutes in the yard! Either way I lose, I'm gonna get a beasting whether I comply or not, Deputy. Now the way I see it you need information from me, and I'll happily help you out..." "But..." Bryce tempted fate. "But...I want something in return. If I give you the information you need I want Von Kelsig." Blanco hissed, coming to the conclusion that if he could never again taste the sweet freedom of the criminal underworld then his request would at least be enough to keep him content for the duration of his stay. "What the hell are you on about?" Bryce groaned, sensing disappointment coming the way of Barnes and Greco. "If Von Kelsig is behind a contract killing, then he's going to do some hard time, isn't he?" Blanco almost chuckled at the thought. "Yes." "Then he serves it here. I want Angelus Von Kelsig detained at Terre Haute. He's the reason for so much of my misery, and if I'm going to die in here I want something to occupy myself with." "You're out of your fucking min-..." "Take it or leave it Deputy," Blanco snarled, "like I said I got nothing to lose, and you apparently have something to gain. All I'm asking for is a cell mate to keep me company, is that too much to ask?" Bryce lowered the phone once more, ignoring the demands of Barnes and Greco booming throughout the room to go on his own judgement. Taking into account the needs of the detectives, Bryce made his own decision. "Alright. Deal. If Von Kelsig is found guilty of anything worth coming here for I'll make sure he bunks with you. You have my word." Reuben Blanco licked his lips and tapped his feet against the tiled floor, eager to seal the deal. In truth, he knew nothing of what Von Kelsig was involved in, but if playing along here meant a chance at revenge against the big son of a bitch then he would play ball, if only for a little while. "OK, Deputy. The contact's name is Menendez. Guillermo Menendez. Gil for short. He was my personal physician when I was on the outside, did a few jobs on the side here and there when he wasn't playing Doctor. After I got busted he went into hiding. You'll find him in Albuquerque." "Thank you," Bryce replied, "I'll do my best to honour our deal. You can go now." "You make sure he comes here...y'hear?" Blanco said menacingly into the receiver as Bryce hung up, "You make sure he comes right here! We got a deal Deputy! Don't fuck me on this!" The Deputy had already left the room before Blanco could finish his sentence. Suddenly he was shaking, and placing the phone back on its hook he got up from his seat and shuffled back out of the door where three armed guards waited to re-shackle him and transport him back to his cell. As composed as he was during the questioning, months of being locked up were evidently taking their toll on him as he struggled to catch his breath and contain his excitement. "I'm gonna get him...I'm finally gonna get him..." He muttered to himself as he felt the cold steel wrap around his wrists yet again. ---- Up in the surveillance room, Barnes and Greco high-fived each other while Davis smiled, caught up in the moment of triumph for the detectives. The door opened behind them and Bryce walked in, clapping his hands and grinning. "You got what you wanted I take it?" He asked sarcastically. "Deputy you have NO idea!" Barnes replied excitedly, slapping his thigh and rising up out of his chair. "Sir, thank you so much for your help." Greco added somewhat stoically, "You've helped us tremendously." The detectives shook hands with Davis and then Bryce, then took their leave of the SCU, accompanied by the Deputy to the front door after collecting their things from the locker room. They had a spring in their step as they made their way back to the Buick, Barnes practically skipping back to the car. "We need to get to New Mexico and find this Menendez guy. A double murder conviction is riding on this one, Greco..." "I can't believe Blanco gave up the name of the hitman. We got this in the bag for sure, don't we?" Greco replied, raising an inquisitive eyebrow. Barnes was quick to slap him on the shoulder jovially, exuding confidence with every gesture. "Oh, we've got him Greco. We've got him." Greco slouched in his seat, thoroughly reassured by Barnes's confidence. As they sped away from the federal penitentiary Barnes chuckled under his breath, ecstatic at the thought of bringing the case which had now been ongoing for over a month to a close. "Von Kelsig's a dead man walking. Trust me."
