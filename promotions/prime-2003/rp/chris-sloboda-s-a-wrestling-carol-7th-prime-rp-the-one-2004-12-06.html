Date: 12-06-04 It’s a mere 11 days till that special time of year, when little boys and girls get what they want. From a jolly fat man, on a sled, with flying reindeer none the less. Some say Santa is fake, some say he’s real. It’s also less than 24 hours till a special event, when big boys… and maybe big girls, get what they want. From a not so jolly Chinese man, behind a big desk, with yes men at his beck and call. Of course, I’m referring to ReVolution 50, The X-mas Special. A ReVolution where no man, or woman, on PRIME’s roster knows if he or she is wrestling. Nor do they know who they are wrestling. And further more, they don’t know if it’s for any of the 5 titles in PRIME or if it’s a match for nothing at all.Date: December 14th, 2004.Time: 11:30 pmPlace: The Orlando residence of Chris Sloboda, aka BodaWe open with a shot of the beautiful Athena, in a low-cut, high rising negligee. She’s busy decorating the Christmas tree in front of the bay windows of the living room. She’s carefully putting on the tinsel, as the family dog, a rottweiler pup of a mere 6 months, attacks said tinsel. “Can you come get Murphy out of here please?”, Athena shouts out. Murphy licks her right leg, which produces a giggle from Athena. “I love you to Murphy, but you need to get out of here, and let me finish this tree.” Athena leans down, petting Murphy on his young head.“What’s a matter?” Chris enters the living room, a glass in one hand, a cookie in the other. His wrists still bandaged, an after affect of the crucifixion from Rev. 48. “Is my little buddy getting in the way?” Chris puts the cup down on a glass table, and calls Murphy over. The little pup hops up on his back legs, trying to get up as high as he can to his master. “Come on little guy, it’s bed time. We’ve got an early flight tomorrow.” Chris takes Murphy in his arms, and starts to leave the room. Murphy takes the cookie right out of Chris’ hand, and hops down, running off across the living room to another side of the house. Chris shakes his head, then turns to kiss Athena. “Don’t be up too late; the flight is at 8 am.” Chris suddenly seems less than enthused.“I know you hate flying. Don’t let it get you down.”. Athena smiles at her man. “It’s not the flight. It’s tomorrow night’s show.” Chris turns to leave, getting but a few steps away.“What do you mean?” Athena puts down the decoration box, looking for an answer to what is bothering her man. “What can be bothering you about tomorrow night’s show?”“Don’t worry about it. It’s nothing really. I just need to sleep.” Chris tries to duck out of a solid answer. Athena heads over to her man, grabbing him by the hand. Chris flinches, the pain of being nailed to the mat two weeks ago still prevalent. “I’m just…. I’m just….” Chris looks at his two bandaged wrists “…I’m just not in the mood to be there Hun.” Athena places her head on Chris’ chest. “I know what that son of a bitch Vampir did two weeks ago has played a toll on you, both mentally and physically. But you can’t let that hamper any chances you have to come back to PRIME tomorrow night. Don’t let this opportunity pass you up. You have a chance tomorrow to win gold.”“Yeah, it’d be nice…. but it’s not going to happen. I could show up and wrestle some rookie trying to make a name for himself. Hell, I could show up, and not get booked at all. There is no guarantee tomorrow. And you know damn well, that if I do get a title match, that I’ll never win the title because Vampir won’t let it be. He’ll stop at nothing to finish what he started two weeks ago.” Chris walks off, heading to the bedroom area of the house. “As far as I’m concerned…. I’m not even going to show up tomorrow night. I say Bah humbug to ReVolution 50, Bah humbug to a title shot…. and Bah humbug to Vampir spoiling any shot I’ll ever have in PRIME to win some gold.” Chris looks at his wrists once more. “And Bah humbug to me even wrestling ever again”*****SLAM*****As the bedroom door slams, Athena sighs. “I wish the spirit of wrestling was not taken from you Christopher. You’re just not yourself anymore. Damn that Vampir, damn that demonic freak.” Murphy comes out of hiding, licking Athena on her right leg. “Well, boy…. it looks like we’re not going tomorrow. I might as well finish this tree tonight.” Athena picks up the decoration box, pulling out some ornaments to hang from the tree. “I’ll let Chris fall asleep before I crawl in with him.”The scene leaves the ever beautiful Athena, shifting through the house. It stops briefly at the bedroom that Mackenzie and Carleigh are sleeping in. They sleep peacefully in the dark room, the only light is that of the E.K.G machines that are monitoring their heartbeats, pulses and respiratory rates. Leaving the angels, the scene now settles inside the master bedroom, where tossing and turning on the king size waterbed is Chris. But the sleeping problems last but a few minutes, as a voice quietly calls out Chris’ name.“Chris…..Chris….” Chris sits up in the bed. His eyes half opened. “Athena?” Chris looks around the room, looking for his young woman. Not seeing her, he lays down again.“Chris…..Chris…..wake your ass up.”Again the PCW Hall of Famer sits straight up. He looks around, his gaze stopping in the far corner.
