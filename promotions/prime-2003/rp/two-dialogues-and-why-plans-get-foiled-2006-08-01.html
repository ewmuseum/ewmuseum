Two Dialogues And Why Plans Get Foiled

1 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/1/2006 “First it’s a Saturday night and you feel cool: like a gangster or a rock star.” -Jim Carroll It’s a Saturday night and I feel fuckin’ cool: like a gangster or a rock star. All these fine people are my brothers and sisters in arms. Like a cavalry of junkies we ride out on this sea of haze. It’s like Tinseltown; we’re our own government, schools, entertainment, and police force. Some of us know when and how to push the envelope; others are fickle and demanding of the prize. “Dude, why are you mumbling a monologue? Just pass the fucking hookah.” Okay, fine, we’re just in a hookah bar, but we’re still kinda fucked up. “Yeah…who are you anyway?” It’s another voice chiming in. Apparently, these faux socialites don’t know exactly who the eff I am. Please, like I want to be taking hits of hookah with some summer session SDSU kids. All of a sudden, it’s all the rage to have, like, “a pay-per-view match.” I swear to God, man: fuck Colossus. It’s a piss-poor PPV and no matter what Danny or Facey says, I’m a bigger person for not buying into the hype. I mean, does anyone even remember what happened at Colossus last year? “Old guy dressed like a chick: chill with the soliloquies. I’m trying to rip on this Dutch apple and you’re killing the vibe.” Fine, here’s the goddamn story. * * * * * 2:15 PM-I try Danny’s phone a bunch of times, but all I get is that annoying ass “ringback tone” of Eminem’s “Ass Like That”. He’s said to me a couple times that he’s going to be in the studio today and it’s not like I’ve got anything better to do. It’s a Saturday afternoon and they don’t start serving Long Island Iced Tea until dinner time at any respectable place. So I’ll take the drive down to see him; don’t ask me why he’s taping in San Diego. I don’t pretend to know what those dickheads in Hollywood are up to. 2:42 PM-The heat is oppressive and the traffic throws me into a murderous rage. These people are lucky I don’t own a fucking sidearm because I’d put down the window and let them hear a few precious seconds of this sweet Craig David jam before I euthanized all of them. I hate fucking traffic. I hate fucking heat. This is supposedly the greatest country in the world and we all forget how to live as humans and revert to playing with our dicks and making cave art as soon as the thermometer hits ninety. 2:46 PM-I hope global warming wipes California and these fucking yuppies off the planet. That’s right; it’s 2006 and I’m bringing yuppie jokes back. I’m about to straight Lebanon the woman in front of me talking on her cell phone. 3:00 PM-I pick up the Sidekick, only to hear Danny’s voice crackling in and out. I ponder larger issues, like the future of the human race and who the hell told this woman in front of me that it’s okay to wear that horrid orange blouse 3:22 PM: Dialogue One -After vanquishing the hordes of unintelligent drivers, I make it to the studio. I don’t know what they call it, but it’s that place they showed on Entourage; the one where they lay down the final audio cuts. I don’t think they’re done filming yet, but I also don’t know shit about movies. I climb the stairs and encounter a pretty blond receptionist. It takes a second before that smile goes south and I realize that the “Superstar” has probably already “helped her get her foot in in Hollywood.” Yes, I realize that every promo I do features me checking out a girl. It’s better than all those gay ones that Johnny Noble does about checking out guys and titty-fucking the American flag and…stuff. “C’mon bitch, get out here; you’re my new best friend,” I crack.“Jesus,” he sighs, “the hell is wrong with you? If I wanted you on the final audio cut, I’d have advertised in the GBSA newsletter.” Of course, that shithead can’t take a joke. He gets majorly moody around his little “pet project.” He gets all exasperated, pissing and moaning about something like I ruined his best take yet for the turning point in Funk’s early adulthood. I’m one more mean jab away from making a joke about just pirating the movie off of Limewire and posting anonymously forums that his movie sucks. If he takes it in good humor, then I’ll take that as permission to actually do it. What are friends for? “Danny, I’m trying to plan a big ass house party.” “Bullet point number one: I’m not going to another ‘golf pros and tennis hoes’ party.” “C’mon dude,” I plead, “that was a classic!” Danny looks over at the fuming studio exec who’s sitting in on the scene and throws up the universal signal for “five minutes.” We walk out into the hall, where we end up facing a massive paper cutout of him as Terry Funk. “Are you kidding me?” “You’re just mad I’m gonna sell more merch than you..” “YEAH MAYBE IF YOU ADVERTISE IN THE GBSA NEWSLETTER. Cock.” I know what you’re thinking: Danny just got served. I one hundred percent agree.” While he polishes the cutout with some spit, I spill the beans about the massive throwdown that will be officially sponsored by the A-List. “Next Saturday, I’m gonna string kegs like fuckin’ ornaments around the apartment. And I’ll be naked in the bathroom, taking a dip in a big ass bath of Vanilla Smirnoff.” “One problem. Well, two problems: that sounds like a night best suited for Chandler and the first season of Queer As Folk and some of us have shit to do on Sunday. Ya know, like RE-retire that cockjuggler Lamen.” “So?” “So it’s the biggest pay-per-view of the year. If you wanna sit at home and watch anime instead of defending the title that I saved from the abyss, then don’t expect me to join in.” “Whatever, dude, Facey will party with me any day of the week.” “That’s great; the stable presence in your life is a guy who wears a cape.” 3:37 PM-Danny goes back to his box-office failure. I’m in an even pissier mood than when I showed up. As a matter of convenience for this promo, I just happen to know that the Face is in the area. He’s the one guy who won’t let me down. He would get cocked with me on a Saturday if he had to give a eulogy on Sunday morning; the Face is a PTP’er. 3:40 PM The car pulls up at this wonderful little delicatessen. A guy named Ira runs the place and it’s as quaint as you’ll find in San Diego. No, seriously? Face is in a strip club. 3:43 PM The strip club is that eerie kind of dark where it’s just a small enough amount of light to really annoy you. For instance, reading the newspaper in here: forget about it. A really smart car dealer would take his customers to a strip club to sign the contracts and put in all sorts of sweet clauses like “Your wife has to break me off some dome, provided that the undersigned’s spouse is a 6.5 or higher.” 3:47 PM-Dialogue Two Avoiding several close encounters of the clitoral kind, I make my way over to the Illustrious Eater of Faces. Like the man I know and semi-respect, he’s wearing his cape and mask. He’s even got his Intense Title in hand. At the moment, he’s demanding that one of the dancers…well…show his title belt a good time. “Fiend, you will succumb to its golden guiles. Now GYRATE!” “Face.” “Sorry kid, no autographs.” “Wait…what?” “Okay fine, Yang, but no pictures.” “Face, it’s me.” “Stonewall Jackson?” “So I guess you’ve been lacing with PCP?” “It’s dark as balls in here. Fuck if I can tell this random person in front of me from that douchebag hanger-on Chandler Tsonda.” “Face, it’s Chandler.” Silence. This great man selects his words from deep in the heart. “Oh. Hi.” “Listen, I’m tossing a bash that’s gonna blow up like ass next Saturday. I need you to roll deep with me, since Danny’s on his high horse about the whole Colossus thing.” “Got bud?” “Check.” “Underage chicks?” “Check.” “Another ten million dollars for me?” “Che-wait, where the hell is that, by the way?” “Can’t help you, broheim. I made a pledge to myself that I’d spend the entire weekend leading up to Colossus getting too blazed to feel pain.” “You don’t even care about wrestling, though. I can honestly say I think you only hang around PRIME because they keep paying you and because you like pissing people off.” “Oh, totally. Fuck wrestling. But fuck Nova harder. Fuck him with a poison-tipped saber dildo. Thus, I gotta hulk up with some ‘ponic. Why don’t you just fight Noble?” “No way, man.” “Well, whatever, you’re gonna be lonely at home in the rice pad by yourself.” “Christ, I’m friends with two allegedly big-time party guys and now you’re both into taking PRIME more seriously than our friendship.” “Fuckin’ tragic, I know. But you should see the fan mail I’ve been getting about this match. Mad bitches sending me nudes all like ‘Facey, we’ll suck your dick off if you beat that pole-smoking long-haired cocksmooch.’ I’ve been investigating like whoa on mySpace and these offers seem legit.” “Fine. Be that way.” 3:43 PM-I’m road raged to a dangerous extent. I only pray that a stroller pulls out in front of me. Who are those two clowns to tell me what I should do with my Sunday, liberated from the tyrant clutches of PRIME? I’m not giving goddamn Johnny Noble a title shot. Over my dead body.
