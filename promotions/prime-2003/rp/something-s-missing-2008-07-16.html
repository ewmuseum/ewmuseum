Something's missing...

16 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/16/08 Tony Rolo swings his legs over the side of his bed and lets out a big, loud yawn. He looks over to the clock that displays 8:53. Rolo: Aw fuck! I’m supposed to be at the airport to catch my flight for ReVolution in an hour. Damn it! He leaps to his feet and begins to run around his room frantically. The Specialist pulls his gym bag from the closet and starts opening up drawer after drawer, feverishly hurrying to stuff clothes into the bag and get a move on so he doesn’t miss his flight. But for some reason, as he opens the third drawer, he stops. Rolo looks around the room, obviously confused. Rolo: What the fuck? He closes that drawer and looks in the drawer beneath it, but it doesn’t appear he’s found what he’s looking for. Rolo: Where the fuck are my underwear? Then the little bubble forms above Tony’s head with a light bulb inside of it. Rolo: JOOOOOEEEEYYYYY!!!! ____________________________________________________ Tony is now in the back of a cab on his way to O’Hare Airport to catch a flight to Alabama for ReVolution 167. He’s got his cell phone pressed up tightly against his right ear and the former two time PRIME Global Champion looks none too happy. If this were a movie, this is where the screen would split in two to show who is on the other end of this call. Ah, fuck it, the screen splits. This is my roleplay, I’ll do what I want. Rolo: What the fuck did you do with my underwear? Joey: What? Rolo: Every single pair of underwear I have is missing… I want to know what you did with them! Joey: Woah, woah, woah… hold on here for a second. All of your panties go missing and you automatically think I had something to do with it? Rolo: You know it, dipshit! Joey: What the hell would I want with your underwear? Rolo: I don’t know, you fucking freak! If you even wear one pair of those, I swear you and John Wayne Bobbit will have a lot to talk about. Joey: I didn’t take them so I could wear them. Rolo: So you did take them! Joey covers the mouthpiece of the phone. Joey: FUCK! Rolo: Hello!? Joey: Yeah, yeah, so I stole your underwear. Rolo: For what? Joey: To sell. Rolo: You’re gonna sell my underwear? Joey: I already did. Rolo: To who? Joey: The Salvation Army. Rolo: You sold my underwear to the Salvation Army? Why? Joey: I needed money. Rolo: What for? Joey: Pot. Rolo: Seriously? Joey: Yeah. Rolo: This is just fucking fantastic. I have no fucking underwear to wear to ReVolution now, I’m already late, and I won’t have time to stop to buy any. How the fuck am I supposed to wrestle my match without underwear? I hope you’re proud of yourself! Joey: Actually, I am. I was able to buy twice as much Kentucky Blue Grass as I thought I would. I never knew boxers could fetch so well on the second hand black market. And are you trying to tell me that the key to you beating some dude named Champ is underwear? Because if that’s what you’re telling me… well, you’re a gaywad. Rolo: You know that I have a chaffing problem! And you stole another man’s underwear and I’m the gaywad? I swear I’m going to fucking strangle you when I see you. Joey: Whatever… I gotta go pack this bowl. Good luck beating Champ while going commando. Rolo: Hold on, I’m not done with you yet! Joey: Yes you are. Later douchebag… Rolo: Wait! Joey hangs up the phone, leaving Rolo angry, frustrated, and feeling a draft up his shorts.
