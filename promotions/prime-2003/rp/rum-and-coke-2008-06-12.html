Rum and Coke

12 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 12, 2008 Interesting fact number six seventy-two: Indianapolis has only recently passed legislation prohibiting the smoking of cigarettes inside bars and restaurants. The addendum was protested and argued more vehemently than issues on either gay marriage or the placement of the Ten Commandments within government institutions. The Midwest isn’t exactly what we’d refer to as progressive. Theoretically, the passing of such legislation should work as a sort of negative reinforcement to keep people from smoking. In practice, however, it just sends droves of nicotine addicts to the streets, sidewalks and patios of bars and restaurants the city over. Point? If you’re looking for the most happening party in Indy, all you need do is find the secluded terrace packed fence-to-fence with smokers. Tonight, that would be The Great White, a relatively new establishment with three whole floors of non-stop debauchery. That’s about two more stories than ninety percent of the bars in the whole state of Indiana offer, so it should come as no surprise that The Great White has quickly become the place to be. Of course that only means the arrival of PRIME’s preeminent partier is nigh. In fact, that Yellow Cab pulling up to the curb right about now? Yeah, well there’s a pair of military-issue shit stompers drifting out the door and putting sole to concrete. The figure clad in such appropriate club gear is the 5-Star Champion and current subject of many hushed whispers between patrons waiting for their turn to enter the club. Tyler Rayne steps to the side of the cab, adjusting the black blazer over his white graphic tee. He pulls a pack of Camels from the inside of his blazer and lights one up. A pair of knee-high stilettos fall gracefully upon the pavement beside him. Lithe fingers tipped with deep crimson nails clutch the cab’s door frame as Michelle Chase pulls herself from the vehicle. Her tight black skirt reveals but an inch or two of leg between its hem and those leather boots. Dark hair cascades about her shoulders, swishing across the denim jacket covering her simple black halter. Rayne gives her a longing once-over, smiling with approval before handing her the cigarette. Can’t seem to quit putting stuff in her mouth, can he? Michelle steps into stride behind him, the pair bypassing a line of waiting party-goers as they head straight for the door. Rayne exchanges a rather complicated handshake with the large bouncer monitoring the entrance, inciting another rush of slightly more agitated whispers to sweep through the line. “You look busy, Cole.” The bouncer nods, his gaze passing through the gathered masses waiting for entry. “All thanks ta yer friends, bro.” “My friends?” “Yeah. Still workin’ for PRIME, aint’cha?” “Last I checked.” “So…ya came fer the party then, right?” Tyler’s lack of a response is answer enough. The bouncer shifts uncomfortably, fingers playing at a sudden itch on the back of his head. “Shit, man. We jus’ figured they’s with you. Fuckin’ whole mess of ‘em up on three. Bitch came an’ rented out the whole floor.” The 5-Star Champion nods. He rubs a hand across the perma-stubble before looking at the bouncer through the corner of his eye. “Tall dyke with a red dye-job?” “Didn’t see ‘er myself, bro.” Tyler sighs, hand once more rubbing across his face. He nods, more to himself than anyone else, then looks back up at the bouncer. “I’ll go talk to Ken. See if he knows anything. You, uh…take it easy. And say hi to the girls for me, will ya?” “Word, son. Keep it real.” The same intricate handshake that greeted them serves as a parting. Tyler and Michelle step into the club, the former immediately shoving his way through the crowd to the stairs. It’s an effort for his date to keep up, and even more of one for her to get a word in his ear. “What the hell was that about?” “Not rightly sure. Feel like a drink?” “Don’t I always?” He laughs. The second floor is smaller, less crowded than the first. Much easier to navigate their way to the bar. Tyler’s eyes dart about the room. He observes every face. Notes the position and posture of each body within the room. Proximity to the bar. To the person next to them. To the floor or the nearest toilet. In an instant, he catalogues as much information as possible, registering every possible detail with precision. Particularly the slumped shoulders of the red-haired girl at the corner of the bar. She slouches on her stool, finger tracing lazily around the rim of her empty glass. Her creamy skin, exposed through the open back of her sleek black dress, serves as an unlikely canvas for the Floyd-like light show flashing about the dance floor behind her. Tyler presses forward, quietly sidling up behind the woman as he throws a wave to the bartender. “Jesus, B. You look like your prom date just ran off with Shakur.” The surprised whelp Angelica Brooks releases is lost behind the poignant and thought-provoking lyrics of Soulja Boy. She nearly topples them both to the floor when she leaps from her stool to give him a hug. “Ty! Thank God! This night has been so horrible.” He looks from the glossy polymer glazing her eyes to the empty row of glasses at the bar in front of her. “I can see that. Heard there was a party here?” The very mention of such things puts Angelica back in a tizzy. She huffs, crossing her arms over her chest and plopping back down, quite angrily, on her bar stool. “Yeah. Lots of fun. Stupid bitch left me off the god damn guest list.” Angelica plants a palm on the bar and leans forward, elevating herself a bit so she can furiously jab a finger up at the next floor. “FUCKING WHORE!!” Tyler rolls his eyes, both hands on Angelica’s shoulders to pull her back down to the barstool. Michelle gives him an amused look. Her hand tugs at the waistline of his jeans, fingers brushing across his skin as she slides around behind him and up to the bar. He smiles, but never pulls his eyes from the obviously intoxicated Angelica. “Troy.” He doesn’t ask the question, but rather makes a statement of the rather obvious variety. Though, just in case he was mistaken, Angelica reassures his assessment with a vigorous nod of her head. “Stupid…dyke. Didn’t put my name on the guest list. Even…Mills got to go up there. But nooo-ooo-oooh. Not Angelica. She’s too pretty to be up at the party and steal all the sunshine from Miss Holier Than Thou Lindsay Fucking Lesbian Troy.” “Three rum and coke. Three shots of Jager.” Tyler pats Angelica on the knee, a gesture meant to suggest a bit of calm. His hand lingers, however, when he turns to thank Michelle for making the order. Almost immediately, Angelica’s own hand is there on top of his, caressing his fingers. “Couldn’t wait to order, huh?” “You seemed a little… preoccupied. Figured I could find a way to entertain myself.” “You’re a doll, you know that?” “Yes. I do.” The shots appear first, Tyler and Michelle quickly snatching two of them. Down the hatch and back on the counter before the bartender finishes the first rum and coke. Tyler looks back at Angelica. She smiles at him and tries to guide his hand a little farther up her leg than just the knee. He resists, surprisingly enough, and turns back to Michelle. “I think she’s had enough.” All the excuse he’ll need to take that third shot. The bartender passes the three drinks across the counter before giving Rayne a questioning glance. “Been awhile since I’ve seen you behind the bar, Ken.” “Been awhile since we’ve been this busy. Word spread about yer lil’ gatherin’ upstairs an’ everyone swings by ta try an’ schmooze with their favorite PRIMEate. Little late, though, arent’cha? Not like you ta miss yer own party, Rayne.” “Not my party.” “Serious?” “As a heart attack.” “Fuck, man. Girl said she was from PRIME. I jus’ figured…well, you know. Friends of yers or somethin’.” “Might be. I’m gonna go up and take a peek. Put these fine ladies on my tab, will ya, Ken?” “Sure thing.” The two exchange a nod before the bartender slides off to handle his other customers. “You be good to watch the kid for a bit?” Michelle gives a skeptical look over his shoulder. Angelica is staring out onto the dance floor, bobbing her head to the super swingin’ sounds of Nelly Furtado. “If I have to.” He leans forward to whisper a reply in her ear. Oddly enough, as he does so, his hand just so happens to slip into the opening of her skirt and slide on up her thigh… “I’ll make it worth your while.” With the stilettos, she’s a little bit taller than him. So when Michelle leans forward to whisper back in his ear, her entire body lowers to accommodate the height differential. This serves to slide his hand a bit further up her thigh than just the tease he had intended. Turns out that skirt is the only thing she’s wearing… “I know you will.” She offers a quick peck on the cheek and a longer nibble of his ear lobe as she brushes by him to drag Angelica out onto the dance floor. Tyler turns to watch the both of them leave, all manner of inappropriate thoughts flittering through his mind. The rum and coke is gone nearly as quick as the shot of Jager, and then he’s off for the stairs again. The third floor is sectioned off with a velvet rope. A man roughly the size of a Volkswagon checks names against the list on his clipboard, turning away far more people than he ends up letting in. A few lucky individuals are sporting VIP bracelets, allowing them full access to the private party. Rayne waits patiently in line, exchanging a single look with the bouncer, who frowns slightly upon sight of the 5-Star Champion. After the last of the wannabes are turned away, Tyler finally ascends the last step to the very exclusive third floor party. “Name’s not on the list, bro.” “Figured as much. You know what the party’s for?” “Dude up in the corner.” The bouncer points up in the direction of…a flock of scantily clad females giggling and fawning over…some dude who’s covered in a flock of scantily clad females giggling and fawning over him. “Name?” “Dametreyus … um… Foosh… Farc… uh…Funk? …” “Fuqueiawytas.” “Yeah. Uh…that. You goin’ in?” “Got some ladies waiting on me, but I figured I should at least say hi.” The bouncer nods and removes the velvet rope restricting entry. “Tough break the other night, man. Ya know we’re always rootin’ for ya here.” Tyler shrugs and steps up on the third floor. “Well, you know what they say, Marcus. Can’t win ‘em all.” Without another word on the matter, the 5-Star Champion pushes his way into the throng. He recognizes various faces from the PRIME staff. Make-up ladies and wardrobe mongers and nameless technicians. A few well-dressed kids off in the corner that might be some of Tsonda’s interns. Troy Douglas ordering drinks at the far end of the bar. Simply Beautiful chatting it up with some smokin’ hotties at a secluded table. Tyler nods in time with the infectious beats of Lil’ Wayne. He makes his way to the bar, smiling at the small twist of fortune that has him stepping up behind yet another despondent PRIME interview personality. However unintentional this second run-in may be. The man at the bar is so lost in thought, though, he doesn’t even notice “Three shots of Jager, a bottle of Petrone, and a rum and coke for ol’ Mills here.” The interviewer stirs, shaking from his internal struggle to stare slack-jawed at Rayne. “Close the cock trap, kid. It’s unbecoming.” “Uh…yeah. Right. Thanks for the drink.” “No problem.” Tyler’s eyes narrow as he watches the bartender work his magic. Or at least, that’s the way it would appear to any hapless sap who happened to be keeping tabs on the Golden Boy. His true gaze, however, falls somewhere beyond the bartender to the mirror behind him. A reflection of the entire room for him to peruse. Observe. Catalogue. He notes the slight increase in proper posture on this floor. The way everyone seems to be carrying themselves a little bit higher. Chests puffed a little bit farther. A bunch of very special people consorting with equally special individuals in an extremely exclusive part of the club reserved just for them. There’s an aura of superiority about the room. The people. The former Universal Champion Lindsay Troy. That hair. That dress. That body… “Jesus Christ.” “Wha—” Mills eyes dart about the room, looking for the source of Rayne’s quiet exclamation. Tyler slides a hundred dollar bill across the counter in exchange for the drinks, setting both the rum and coke and a shot of Jager in front of Mills. “Nothin’, kid. Bottoms up.” Rayne takes the first shot of Jager before Mills has even turned back around. The two clink glasses to finish the last two shots. The interviewer’s eyes fall back down to his glass. The champions eyes pretend to watch the bartender as he works. She’s talking to Dam. There’s an unmistakable twinkle in her eye for the briefest of moments. A fleeting second of genuine happiness that twists Tyler’s stomach in ways he’d rather not admit. “So how’s the party been?” “Oh, it’s great. Ya know this is uh…this is a really great place. I’m having a great time. Everyone’s just been so gr—” “Stop saying great.” She leans in close to whisper something in Dam’s ear. The dress glides across her body as she shifts. How easily it would glide right off. Onto his floor. Into his bed… “Sorry. I just…um…this isn’t exactly my kind of party.” “Aw, come on. You tellin’ me that Matt Mills isn’t a hip-hop fan?” She’s taken a lot of shit for the hair. She takes a lot of shit for everything. It’s part of what makes her so gods damned alluring. Her perseverance. Tenacity. The hair… he likes it. Not that he could ever tell her, but… “I’m more of a hole-in-the-wall kinda guy. Juke box in the corner and as little lighting as possible.” “Sounds like my kinda dive. You just gotta find ya a woman, Mills. That always makes for a better party.” “Easy for you to say.” She rises, pulling away from Dam’s ear. She looks gorgeous in that dress. He watches the relieved faces of the fawning women as the former champ leaves the new Director of Security to enjoy his party. The envy they’re trying to play off. Pretending to ignore how easily she stole his attention. How captivating she is. Just gotta find you a woman. That always makes for a better party. “Yeah. It is easy for me to say.” Tyler snatches the bottle of Petrone from the counter, along with two of those shot glasses, and leaves Mills without an explanation. Walking at a slightly brisker pace than he’d originally intended, Rayne quickly covers the distance between himself and PRIME’s new Director of Security. “Congrats on the promotion. Also…hell of a party.” “Thanks, Boss. Didn’t know you was comin’.” “Neither did I.” The gaggle of women parts ways for Rayne as if he were Moses. Or, more likely, as if he were some dude with an unopened bottle of Petrone come to join the party. Tyler pours two shots of Petrone and hands one to Dam. His gaze washes over the bevy of beauties and back to the bar, where Lindsay Troy and Wade Elliott have tucked themselves into a secluded little corner of their own. Well, Wade seems to be sharing with a few smokin’ hotties of his own, but… the infamous smile spreads itself across his face without much thought. A natural reaction to the situation. “Guess my invite must’ve gotten lost in the mail.” “Don’t go startin’ no trouble, Rayne.” Tyler turns his attention back to the man of the hour, a genuinely amused smile on his face. He pours them both another shot. “Me? Trouble? Come on, Dam. You know me better than that.” The newest edition to the PRIME security staff responds with only a raised eyebrow. Tyler nods knowingly and hands the next two shots to a couple of the girls. “Bottle’s yours, bro. Present to go along with the promotion. But uh…yeah. Not lookin’ to start any trouble for ya. I’ll see ya at the show, right?” Dam nods and offers a closed fist. Tyler responds in kind. Pound it. The 5-Star Champion, not about to forget his reputation, flashes the harem with that irresistible smile and an accompanying wink. “Ladies.” The stairs are his escape. He doesn’t have to look at her again. Doesn’t have to ignore that twisting acid bath of jealousy that boils his stomach every time he looks at her. Thinks about her going home with someone else. No. He’s got Michelle. Someone who actually wants him. Accepts him for who he is. He snatches a glass from a wandering waitress, absently tossing a bill onto her tray in exchange for the drink. Half of it’s gone in the first gulp. Drown out the desire. There’s no reason to look at her. No need to draw attention. Just go back down to Michelle and fuck the unforgiving shit out of her in a bathroom stall. Yeah. Just… …he turns and raises his glass in the direction of the former Universal Champion. The smug, patented smile plastered on his face. His mask. His secret. Fuck her. What was so special about Lindsay Troy, anyway? Fuck her. Michelle was waiting downstairs. And last he’d checked, she was only wearing that skirt… Fuck her.
