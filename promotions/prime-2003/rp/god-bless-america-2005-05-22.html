God Bless America

22 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-22-05 Could it be that Boda is so stupid he doesn’t understand the force he’s up against? Perhaps it’s all the times he’s been hit in his head that makes his judgment a little foggy. I have to admit in my last two hardcore battles I too have taken my share of Lewinski shots putting me on the same track as Muhammad Ali, in more ways then one. Unlike the selfish overweight has been they call Boda my goals are not based in vanity. You see a voice came to me last night as I tried to sleep off the hardcore hangover I was feeling after my historic victory at the Pay-Per-View. It was the voice of God and he was pissed. It appears the big man upstairs wants me to win the Universal title on the biggest show of PRIME’S calendar year. When a telemarketer calls you it’s natural to hang up. When a pesky Jehovah witness shows up at your door it’s not rude to throw your hot coffee at their face. When you’re in a match with Furher it’s not silly to chalk it up as an instant win. However when God tells you to do something you do it baby. It’s not like I wasn’t going to anyways, this just speeds up the process at bit. First thing I need to do is take Boda out of the picture. This guy has had more shots then Aimz on a Heroine binge to prove himself as champion. Even Al Gore was only given one chance before they put him out to pasture and for as much as I hate people named Al, Boda is ten times worse. Hoyt Williams: Can you please chew a little less loud sinner. My head hurts and now I’m stuck on a plane in coach since Los Diablo have the Jet and this stupid flight was booked last minute next to a cow. The obese women in the purple dress next to me is chewing peanuts with a horrid grinding sound more annoying then Fran Dessers voice. What is it with fat people and the color purple? As one of the least holy of colors why in God’s name do fat people want to look like Barney the dinosaur? I need to put that on my questions to ask Boda. Fat Lady: I’m nervous about flying. Hoyt Williams: Yea me too, I wonder if this thing is like an elevator and has a weight limit. My God you must be pushing three hundred L-B’s. I guess a little Hoyt goes a long way as the woman huffed and puffed and sulked to her self in the seat next to me. I just hope her puff stays up top because a bottom puff from this sow would make the gas chamber turn green. Hell for all I know she might be a biological terrorist. Folded into the seat pocket in front of me was a magazine. Unfortunately it was full of over priced crap like tanning machines. Why would anybody pay to tan when God gives it to us for free? Then again why would anybody fly coach voluntarily? The plane landed at Midway and I got off with out any incident from the lady’s ass. I waited patently for my luggage on the turn style conveyor in front of me. A pretty woman who smelled like she bathed in lilies was standing waiting for her luggage as well. Hoyt Williams: You can learn a lot about life from an airport. Lady: Oh yea? Hoyt Williams: Sure, you see it all comes around in the end. The lady hardly chuckled I guess she left her sense of humor on the plane. People don’t laugh like they used too. I guess when the price of gasoline costs more then a loaf of bread it’s hard to find humor in the irony of life but that didn’t stop people in the 1930’s. People have become so consumed with the devils toys around them they are too busy to talk or smile. The cold spring wind of Chicago felt good as it blew softly on my bruised body. Chicago is an acquired taste much like apricots. But those who enjoy it stay regular, much like apricots for that matter. She can be harsh and mean but when she wants to look good no city compare. My limo was waiting for me ready to take me back to the RISP headquarters just outside of the city. It was time to start my planning on how to get into the head of Killean and rid myself of Boda. Wrestling is more about politics then it is about fists and I’m a master at both but this level of the game would take some help. There was only one guy who would able to take my game planning to the next level. A master of the con and the ring he was the guy who could help me. So after a short call on my cell phone I had the limo driver divert my trip to William Perry’s refrigerator. If you want to pack your colon their isn’t a better restaurant around Sitting in the corner booth was the guy who was going to help me achieve God’s goal. Despite his skin color and bride of Frankenstein hairdo nobody was shrewder or more experienced at mind games and title fights. Mr. Ron King. Ron King: God bless America!! God bless you champion!! Ron gave me a hard hug causing me to scrap myself on his sequence jean jacket covered in American flags. The guy had a fashion sense even I, wrestling’s best dressed could learn from. Already at the table in front of him was the game plan that would take out Boda and destroy Killean. My track record the last few months was more then impressive. I damn near ended the career of Trashcan Man twice and embarrassed Token Weed so badly I doubt we will ever see his face in PRIME again. I told you a little Hoyt goes a long way. With Imperium and Ron King by my side winning the mind battle over the next month shouldn’t be hard. Ron King: Boda deserves another shot at the gold about as much as kid who loses a finger to a fan deserves an air conditioner. It just doesn’t make sense. Wrestling’s greatest promoter had a way with words that I too could learn from. For the next hour we went over stats, matches, and the history of both Boda and Killean. Ron showed me the importance of taking out Boda this Tuesday showing to not only the owner, the fans, but more so to Boda himself that he was as worthless as a Furher autograph on E-bay. Hoyt Williams: So do I take my case to management this week to let them know that I should be the number one contender? Ron King: Take hell, you ARE the number one contender and don’t forget that champ. You tell the people that you are the number one and you get Killean to believe it and you will be ready to rock and roll. Boda is old cold coffee while you’re a fresh steaming pot of Java. Nobody likes old coffee that’s been sitting around and NOBODY will drink old cold coffee. His pot is going down the sink and the new brew must move in. Any dime and nickel promoter can see that and so can the fans. GOD BLESS AMERICA HOYT YOUR GOD’S CHAMPION, and an AMERICAN should be universal champion. AM I WRONG? Hoyt Williams: Well… Ron King: AM I WRONG? Hoyt Williams: Hades NO!! You’re right I’m the champion of the people their personal savior and I deserve that title. Boda be damned. Ron King: Tuesday night you and the Vet are going to demolish the oversized oaf for good. You have a brother in your corner in Vetra while Boda has Furher the Brooklyn Brawler of PRIME. I’m an old black man and I can kick the hell out of Furher. The Dark Age passed a long time ago they stepped on a rainbow and are now playing with Jesus. You know that and they know that. They are dead throw the dirt on them just as Killean did to Boda last Sunday and show the world Imperium’s dominance. The time is now CHAMP take it and sell or become cold coffee nobody wants. Hoyt Williams: I’m not old coffee I’m a steaming pot of Jesus Java! Ron King: Lets print up the Jesus Java shirts and get a brewing. =w=
