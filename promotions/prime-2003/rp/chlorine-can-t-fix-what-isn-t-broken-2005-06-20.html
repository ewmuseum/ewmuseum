Chlorine Can't Fix What Isn't Broken

Date: June 20th ONE: Little Secrecies ''I've been having some fucked up dreams lately. Crazy sci-fi movie type shit. Could be the drinking, but I dunno...'' Amy stretched her legs out from underneath the covers, speaking to what seemed like nobody in particular - maybe herself. ''Eh, it's not like it's killing me,'' she grumbled, picking up a small pamphlet from the bedside table, ''Hey, looks like room service here's pretty good... want anything?'' ''I'll pass.'' came a voice from behind the hotel's half-closed bathroom door. Not just any voice, either... While it appeared that Amy Campbell had decidedly taken a lazy day away to clear her head by staying half-asleep on the mattress, Killean Sirrajin stepped out into her room wearing little more than a towel and caused that notion to fly away quickly. ''Not that I'm complaining,'' began the redhead, ''But I think we hit the bottle a little too hard last night...'' ''Not that I'm complaining,'' Killean responded, ''But you're an animal! Look at these scratches on my back!'' Amy smiled and rubbed her eyes before relaxing back against the pillow she still hadn't left. This was a confusing and sudden situation, but hardly anything she'd take to worrying about. Once more, she smiled, ''Casualties of war... you shouldn't have expected any less. You really think I'd crawl in bed drunk and not leave a mark?'' Killean sprawled out over the sheets beside her, smiling back ''At least you missed my tats, I wouldn't want to pay the money to have those fixed. I can honestly say this whole scene was the last thing I expected, but definitely not the last thing on my mind since I met you.'' ''Of course it wasn't the last thing on your mind. You're a guy, and I'm supposedly easy. The thing people don't get is... well, everybody needs to get out of their mind once in a while, you know?'' Amy sighed, ''Short of the hardest of drugs, very little can give me the right physical high. Yeah, it's pretty slutty of me to have gotten drunk and hopped into bed with you, but it was a HELL of a lot less destructive than trying to pull myself into some sort of long term relationship and getting hurt in the end, or shooting up some chemical. How can you cheat if we're barely attached, you know what I mean? This... well, it's convenience.'' She sighed, not quite sure what else to say. Killean was mulling over the discussion while admiring the outline of her form underneath the bedspread, which only served to put a different tension in the air of that awkward silence. No less, Amy would stutter on... ''I... I mean, look at us! We're hardly a pair, just two dece--two INCREDIBLY sexual individuals--and, uhh... well, you hardly know me. We just... you know... last night, and... yeah.'' All Sirrajin could do was chuckle at her stumbling form of explaination - for what, he wasn't sure. ''You try to say a lot, did you ever notice that?'' ''I kind of... uh, I tend to talk at length about myself. It kinda dampens my problems down, I guess...'' her eyes met his, noting the confusion in them, ''Err, nevermind. I sound like a fool.'' He laughed, ''Not at all, actually. You're really different, right down to the accent... which I was actually about to question. Where d--'' ''I'm French,'' Aimz interjected, ''Never actually learned English until my family moved to a place with no immersion program. Luckily, I pick up on things quickly... it did make school a bitch of a place for a while, though. By the time I knew enough of the language to get the grades, I was already getting suspended for... well... doing things my way.'' ''I'm assuming that involved its fair share of violence.'' ''Yeah, and poor attendance. School just didn't turn my crank... sitting around never did. On a positive note, school taught me how to tune irritating figures of authority out.'' ''So that's how you deal with Hin See!'' They both chuckled but Killean continued soon after, ''This will sound odd, but... you know, laying around's really a great way to get to know you.'' ''That's because I never learned how to shut the fuck up.'' the redhead smirked, ''Besides, I'm pretty comfortable with you. I mean, not in that 'I want to have your kids' way, but...'' Killean raised his hand and smiled, '' 'Nuff said. I know what you mean.'' ''I know you do, but I'm having a little bit of an issue here...'' ''Such as...?'' Amy cringed, ''Nobody can know that... well, whatever this is... happened, okay? I just... I really can't afford to look worse than I already do. I mean, you're the champion - you've beat Karina, and I just lost horribly to h--'' ''Lemme interrupt for a second.'' The little redhead's stomach knotted while Killean said his peace. She had a feeling that she knew what he was about to ask. The same thing Jeph would have... ''...Can we leave her out of this?'' he queried, ''I know you're still hurting and all, but the last thing I think either of us needs to be thinking about in bed is... ugh... you-know-who.'' ''Frostybox? Yeah, I guess she'd kinda ruin the party,'' Amy offered, ''But as I was saying... not telling about this and all...'' ''Don't worry about it. I know how these things look to the general population of idiots, so don't expect me to walk into the lockerroom like I've got another notch in my belt.'' TWO: The Force Of KinClickclickclick... ''...Did you order room service?'' ''No...'' mumbled a confused Campbell, wrapping herself in a sheet as she unhappily rose from bed to check the peep hole of the hotel's door. That wasn't necessary, however, since the caller made himself known well before she could reach the door. 'Ames! It's Dave. Wanna, uh... lemme in?' ''Shit!'' she hissed, turning back toward Killean with wide eyes, ''Mo--fuck... just, umm... bathroom, now!'' Sirrajin nodded and took a dive toward the shower he'd just come out of, closing and locking the door behind him as Amy tried staving Gibson off for another moment, ''Just a sec, Gibbo. Gotta... uhh... need to get some clothes, yeah...'' Straightening the bedsheets without a thought of what she'd just said, the little redhead clicked open the door - still clad only in the hotel's fine linen. At first, Dave's face lights up in a smile-but turns to a puzzled frown as he steps into the room. ''All right, what's going on?'' Gibson folds his arms, an item in a plastic bag hanging from the grasp of his left hand. ''And don't even think about playing innocent with me. Finding a naked Amy wrapped up in a bed sheet is like finding Michael Jackson leaving a nursery school - you just KNOW somebody's been up to no good.'' Gibson keeps his stern look, but it quickly dissolves into a smile. ''Dammit… I can't play bad cop with you. We are NEVER allowed to play poker together. Seriously though, what's up with the hot chick burrito look you've got going on?'' Amy swallowed hard and readied an explaination that she couldn't put out in enough words, ''I... uh, what - I'm not allowed to sleep naked? It's not my fault that you like to prance in here at all hours of the morning, expecting me to be awake.'' ''First of all, it's three o'clock. Second thing is that you told me you needed to get some clothes on... but a shirt's the last thing I'm lookin' at right now. Spill it.'' The redhead was frenzied now, groping for an excuse, ''Have you been drinking, Dave? Yeesh, you're way too hard on me when you get like this.'' Amy's friend stood there, silent, his jaw clenching and unclenching a few times as his nostrils flared. Gibson? Out drinking? Probably the wrong choice of words. ''No,'' Dave said, clearly enunciating each letter. ''I. Have not. Been. Drinking. I had something I wanted to show you, but maybe this isn't the best time. Neverm-'' CLATTER Halfway turned to the door, Gibson slowly swivels to face the bathroom door. ''What was that?'' ''My shampoo probably fell into the tub or somethin', I never set it down properly,'' Amy mused, ''But look, I didn't mean the whole drinking thing... just kinda wasn't sure what else to sa--- hey, no need to check in there!'' She dove to Gibson as he reached for the bathroom's door handle, ''Relax, man... it wasn't anything. Now, back to me being a bitch...'' But Dave Gibson, his jaw set in determination, was already reaching for the doorknob… Before a slim hand flew up against that side of the doorway, and its mate pressed itself against the other side. There she stood, the Red Raver, arms extended, blocking Dave Gibson from the bathroom door and trying to keep her sheet from revealing any salacious bits at the same time. ''That sounded pretty loud for a shampoo bottle,'' Gibson casually says. ''LOOK, Dave, don't change the subject,'' she replied. ''I'm bus-EEEP!'' Amy's reply got cut off as Gibson bent and hoisted the Red Raver over his shoulder so he could freely twist the knob, pushing the door open. And just as suddenly as Gibson moved, the strength fell out of his arms. Amy twisted out of his grasp and dropped back to her feet on the carpet, anxiously looking back over her shoulder and then back at Dave Gibson and back again -like a tennis fan with the worst seat EVER for Wimbledon. She stood there as Gibson surveyed the current, and at the moment very barely dressed Universal Champion Killean Sirrajin staring back at him from inside the bathroom. Amy braced herself, awaiting the explosion. The verbal tirade. The profanity. Maybe even a punch or two aimed at the champ. But instead, a tight-lipped Gibson stood motionless, soaking in every atom of the scene in front of him. It had finally happened. Dave Gibson… had been rendered speechless. There was still his right hand, however, currently clenched in a white-knuckled fist at his side. Campbell's face scrunched up as she pressed fingers to her temple to stifle the intense headache that had just presented itself. No explaination would do, but she had to try... ''Look, Dave...'' she squirmed, ''You're my best friend, I would've told you really soon. It's just... this, it just happened, you know? And I didn't want anybody to know about it until I knew how to deal with it. It wouldn't do public opinion of me any good at all, and it might even hurt Killean's reputation. That, and I didn't need you thinking less of me or making any assumptions that I was... well... the kind of person who engages in this stuff, and...'' Suddenly, Gibson turned and tossed the bagged object in his hand against the wall. Amy flinched as soon as she heard glass shatter. Instead of even screaming, Mr. Old School turned right for the hallway, further grinding whatever was in the bag under his heel, before he slammed the door shut. ''Want me to get him back?'' Sirrajin's head cocked to one side. ''No... Jesus! He gets so fucking sensitive...'' Amy shook her head, ''We really never should've done this.'' Killean put a hand on her shoulder that got shrugged off, ''Hey, it's not YOUR fault he's unhappy. He can't deal with his emotions, and that's his problem. I mean, come on... it's not like you were obligated to call the guy three seconds after I fell asleep trying to explain yourself.'' ''That doesn't mean I had to be dishonest when he came in here.'' ''We all make mistakes, Amy.'' When Amy picked up the gift Gibson had crushed, she found a blown up photograph of her as a child, with her brothers... back at the cottage. Back home. That meant something to her, he'd have known it - and now it was nothing more than an 8x10 torn by shards of contempt. She turned to face Killean with a half-hearted sneer, ''And I've made too many lately. Could you just... I dunno, just let me clear my head a bit? I need some time. Just... please... don't be here when I get back.'' ''Amy, I...'' Too late. If she wanted to disappear, that damned redhead sure knew how to high-tail it out of a scene. THREE: Washing It DownShe felt at home floating on the surface of the water. Peaceful. Serene. Alone. Awake in a substance that consumed every inch of her. It had been like this her entire life... the water was a way to escape, to test the limits of her lungs and muscles and, most of all, to collect herself. Under any other circumstances, she'd be irritated by the slack-jawed gawkers that were permanently glued to their poolside seats, but it was a calm Amy Campbell who chose to ignore them. She had far better things to concern herself with - she'd gotten involved in the emotions and lives of others once more, and there was no way for it to be resolved without pain. Gibson was already hurt, and there was no telling what Killean'd been feeling since she abandoned him in her hotel room with a stern request for his expulsion. Only one thing rang through in all of it - Nobody could talk. This was one of those weaknesses she thought she'd learned to avoid since Karina Wolfenden, but that obviously wasn't so. People expected her to be shacking up among the ranks, it was a ridiculously weak public opinion that was held by the 'right' people, thus bought by their idiotic followers... the chubby, cheeto-breathed dopers who stunk up every wrestling arena across America. She couldn't have them be right, couldn't let this tarnish her further... not with Ellis Easton in the seat to beat this week, and ESPECIALLY not after her small downfall a few weeks prior. Amy dipped her head beneath the pool's cool surface. This was a tiny funnel cloud that couldn't turn into a tornado. Not with the brushfires she already had to snuff out. Yet again, Aimz had absolutely no idea what laid ahead of her at ReVolution. And, once more, it was something she could barely control. All she could hope to do was keep a handle on her performance in the ring against whatever fodder they'd shoved her way this week. No amount of training would help handle the other inconveniences that sat in the middle of her roadway, the least she could do was use it against the only factor she had say in the outcome of.
