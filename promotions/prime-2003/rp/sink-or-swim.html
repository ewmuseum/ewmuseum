Sink or Swim

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: when? Everything is always simpler in black and white. Good and evil. Light and dark. Life and death. Choice: live or die? Questions like this are made light in a rhetorical sense; that answer you are so certain to be true, that you just have to scoff when people ask because they should know just as much as you do. “Why the fuck would you ask me such a stupid question?” It all comes down to life, right? What can you do to be affective in a world filled with one-trick ponies? What precautions do you need to take to make sure you stand out in the crowd? Whatever you may say to that, it’s all easier than doing nothing. Why not say ‘die’? What’s the harm that could follow? It’s so much easier to sit on your ass and let the world happen. To sit, watch, wait and die. To accept that what you have is what there is, tossing aside any notions that insinuates just a little bit more hard work to amount to anything In death comes a certain acceptance of bleakness and mediocrity that wouldn’t come at any other time. In the choice of sitting idle, doing nothing, and letting change occur out of its’ own will; one is making the choice in death. Some people can sit content in waiting for death. I call these people Catholics. That was never the lifestyle for me though. I didn’t like letting things happen, especially when shit never “came to me,” if you know what I’m saying. Life wasn’t exactly rewarding, sitting by as a spectator in a sport filled with action. It was time to make moves; put plans in motion. Picture this: You’re in a world of seven-foot-something, three-hundred-pound giants, and you’re barely breaking the six-foot mark. You’re limbs are compared to toothpicks on national television when standing next to these guys. You wear a slapstick mask and cape, go by a stupid moniker because you hope you’ll turn a few heads. And when no one’s looking, you hit him in the place where they’d least expect it. That isn’t sitting idly and letting the world happen. That’s, what I like to call, getting shit done. If you’ll hear me out, I’d like to explain. It all relates back to this very basic ideal my father has had instilled in me since day one. My mother was never there to combat his notions, because—well, quite frankly, I don’t even remember my mother. All I do remember was my father caring for me. Daddy would tell me bedtime stories or quotes from famous and smart people. Little anecdotes. Old scriptures. Fables. Family history. All designed to mold my frame of mind to revolve around a certain concept; Survival of the fittest. It’s a way of life that is, in essence, very chameleonic in how it is addressed. Most people have often found it paraphrased as “Be the best you can be,” or “Sink or swim,” or “Do or do not, there is no try.” Many cultures’ foundations are based on this simple concept of hard work. Others often adopt the ideal as one of their own, once witnessing the progress that such standards yield. Most commonly referred to as the theory of Natural Selection, it is an order that has been seen time and time again. At first it was regarded to economics; and how the free market system leaves only enough breathing room for those able to fight for breath. But later it was commonly referred to the progression of humankind through Darwin’s Theory of Evolution. It doesn’t matter whether you believe in Darwinism or not, I’m not about to get into any argument over God or creation or any of that babble. History has taught us; through the Holocaust, struggles over the state of Palestine, and the “modernization” of Africa, that if there’s one thing on the human race’s mind, it’s proving that you’re the best. And if there are no UFOs attacking major cities or gigantic alien threats of global destruction to keep the people united, they’re going to start feeling the need to prove ‘who’s best’ to each other. “White power.” “Black pride.” “Arriba la raza.” “Go Go Jew-Jew Juice!” “Whatever the hell Muslims say?” They all have this nationalistic pride that keeps them from respecting anyone but themselves. Whether you’re blowing someone’s church up or gatting up the north side of Crenshaw Boulevard, it all goes back to the basic concept of showing everyone else exactly who the fucking boss is. My dad told me, that’s what ‘Survival of the fittest’ meant. And he told me to live by these guidelines in everything I did. He taught me to understand that the weak do not prosper from letting things happen. Destiny is a silver-lined curtain to distract your eye and make you fall. It raises false hope. It is the strong who grab the moment and make it their own. Trust me; these ideals aren’t the easiest to live by. There’s the constant need of always staying on your toes and always sticking to your convictions. Everyone else has to recognize you for what you are; a death-bringer of maximum destruction and eternal hell-fire, forever raining an evil upon their soul until their will to live is no more! I try to display this ideal in my everyday life. The way I walk and talk, the things I say, the activities I choose to partake in. Everything adds up to create the entity you see before you; the Illustrious Face-Eater. I’d like to think there are two sides of my personality, however blended they may be. Even when I’m out living my day-to-day life during the week as Adam Dick, my standards do not change. It’s either kill or be killed in my world. Take aim and go for the throat. I find out that bitches are jocking my dick, I’m going to be all over that shit. It’s how I’ve always been; able to make the best of any situation. Even if I’m being hustled, I still lay that shit out on wax, if you know what I mean. Although that sleazy and creepy manager guy from the roleplay two week’s ago (the one I SCHMAKT Tsonda’s slanted ass with) tried to play me for a fool, I was still able to benefit. It was at his little public speaking gig that I met the woman of my dreams, Chelsea Vice, when she decided to rip me open a new asshole by way of verbal humiliation. And you know what? I wouldn’t have it any other way. Sure, she may seem like the predatory type, but are we forgetting who lassoed that girl in and brought her down for the kill? Chelsea knows she’s dating a famous superstar, but already she thinks she’s some sort of royalty or something. We’re about a week-and-a-half into our relationship and already she’s feeling the need to tell me everything that I do on-camera is bullshit, my gimmick is terrible and I should get a formidable plan set-up as soon as possible. Bitch, don’t you know that the Face is FULL of formidable plans? As the Illustrious Face-Eater, everyone sees my undying passion for success and for admiration. I show every single one of my peers why I am their equal—and then some—and I show every single one of the fans that I’m just as fun to watch as the “Menopaunic Lupine” Karina Wolfenden. All of the seemingly crazy things that I say and do, I assure you, are NOT random acts of mindless indulgence. Everything I do, I do it for a reason. From the Danza gig, to everything I’ve done to Danny Ferguson and winning the Pulse Title; all of it was to show everyone that I’m not what they expect. I’m not the Super Fucking Duper Champion for a stupid reason, you know. I work just as hard as the Vangelus Olsigs and Jesse Jamesters, if not harder than them! Every single one of the champions in this promotion likes to walk around with an asshole in the middle of their forehead, telling tall-tales of achievements, ones they feel actually matter. Do you understand that I don’t care what the Immortal Championship or the Underground is? Who Killean Sirrajin’s trainer, Sephiroth, is? I don’t give a fuck about you winning Jewel in the Crown. You can rightly shut the fuck up, because everyone is sick of hearing about feds that they don’t know about. No one wants to HEAR a history they can’t SEE. And more importantly, you can take that seven-and-oh record and shove it down Hoyt Williams’ throat because that’s the only place where your hype will pay off. When it comes to facing Tchu, it comes down to the very essence of sink or swim. All the matches I was in before hand were always in the hands of someone else. When it came down to the end of the day, it was guys like the Forsaken who were able to help me in my cause. At least that was one case, but other times it was down to every man for themselves amongst a pack filled with Chandler Tsonda and Paddy O’Shea and Tyler O’Neil. This, it has come down to two people. For the first time in my career, it feels like everything is on my shoulders. Because this isn’t AWC where I’m wrestling in disillusioned nine-man matches where the fans and booker alike feel the need to shoot themselves in the foot. This is down to the Jewel in the Crown of 2005 and the Super Fucking Duper Champion. People don’t understand our situation, as they don’t think there’s any kind of history between us. How could this be such a big match? Well, aside from two undefeated records, there is so much more at stake. This is more than the main event at ReVolution. This is what will tell everyone, had I made my arrival just four weeks earlier, that I would have been the undefeated man that Tchu is today. Could the Illustrious Face-Eater have been Jewel in the Crown given the chance? There isn’t a fucking second spent on ReVolution where that thought doesn’t run through my mind. Has everyone forgotten my debut already? For a second, the internet called me the ‘hype-thief’. So when I came out to interrupt a stupid Tchu speech, he must have been web-surfing that day. He must have clicked a few links to do research on this new hire, and found out that I’ve been in AWC. In short, he must have been ready for me. So when he physically destroyed me by way of chair shots and suplexes, he knew exactly what would get under my skin. His last words, yuck. They make me cringe. ”Helluva debut, kid.” Yeah, no shit. Because you fucking ruined it, you inconsiderate prick-faced asshole. And if he thought next that I would forget about his fucking time spent on denting my skill, he’s wrong. Because the Face doesn’t forget baby, and he certainly doesn’t forgive. The only thing that’s left coming is a sour end to that run you’ve held onto so dearly. You think the momentum of winning the most coveted tournament in professional wrestling today is enough to stop the political and philosophical movement that is Super Fucking Duper? I’ve thwarted terrorist attacks at bus-stops that have been more elaborate than the trite that comes out of your mouth each week, Dr. Ward, so don’t even think your psychological, analytical bullshit will wear on me. I’m fucking unique and there are no maps of the human brain that could even relate you to what I’m thinking. Do you honestly think my drive lies in some bullshit I tried to pass off earlier as ‘proving to yourself that you’re the best’? Hell no! My drive lies in, what I call, a singular and personal conflict. Because in the sport of professional wrestling, there is usually one obstacle in between you and victory: your opponent. My tactic has always been to find that drive that will excel me past my opponent. And Tchu, you don’t even have any idea as to what I could be thinking. Ask your buddy Killean, he might remember. Revolution 4 Wrestling; R4W? Do you remember that? How about a wrestler named Eddy Estrada. He went by a name called FILTH. Now Eddy Estrada is more familiar to this circle as Deft, and his intentions were entirely different when going by that moniker. But when you knew him, Dr. Ward, I think you knew him pretty well. It was a glass floor match, I’m pretty sure. Filth versus Tchu for the R4W Heavyweight Championship. You remember who won, don’t you? Of course you do, that belt is still hanging up in your fucking trophy case. I don’t know if you know this, but I was trained by a guy named Eddy Estrada. Yes, the one and the same. And if there’s anything I learned by the Karate Kid movies, it’s that taking on your mentor’s challenges is always the most logical action to make once you’re on your own. So Tchu, when I think of this match, I don’t think of a collision between two new-comers who had the handle to make a splash. I don’t think of this as a bout between two champions in their own right. I’m thinking of this as the Illustrious Face-Eater, defending the cause of good in his battle against the evil Tchu who doesn’t deserve the golden touch he’s been granted. As far back as you and I are concerned, we have a legacy to maintain. Because you beat Deft. And you beat his mentor, Jace, before him. My odds aren’t exactly looking good. Does that mean I’m going to stand idly, and let life happen? Let Tchu get yet another win over my lineage? Fuck no. I’m the Moment Thief here. I’m the one that’s going to raise everyone’s eyebrows. Make them say “Should Tchu REALLY be Jewel in the Crown?” It all comes down to this moment at ReVolution. Who wins? Who lives? It’s all or nothing, for me. To you, this is just another win in your column. Another scrawny masked dude who thinks he has a shot at denting your record. But you don’t understand. And neither does anyone else who can sit back and watch the world happen, but this is it for me. This is my shot. The Super Fucking Duper Title isn’t on the line, but you know what? This isn’t about any titles. What it comes down to, is a simple fact for me. Do I have what it takes to swim? Or am I going to be left sinking? I’m afraid I don’t know the answer. I’m neither a mind-reader or a psychic nor am I blessed with the gift of foresight nor can I time-travel. Although I would really like to time-travel (Who the fuck wouldn’t?), I wouldn’t take any of these options even if I could. Because that means I’m just sitting back and watching what happens; knowing before hand if it’ll even be worth it to try. I don’t want to do that. I’ll just wait until the struggle occurs, and then I’ll make sure that there can only be one outcome. VICTORY, for me. Because there is nothing else that can matter. It comes down to whether or not I have what it takes; and if I don’t, then shit, I don’t know. It’s sort of like wondering if there is a God, and if Heaven exists. I’m counting on winning; I don’t know what lies waiting for me if I lose. Just like Christians, they go into this whole Jesus thing not knowing if he actually existed before hand. All we have are writings and records that say a man lived a life as Jesus, but there is nothing that can tell you for sure or not that he is the Son of God, the Saviour of Humanity. All that the Christians have to understand the concept of afterlife is faith. They don’t know for sure, but there is this certainty that tells them no matter what the outcome, they’re going to be okay. I guess you could say I have faith. Faith in myself that I am going to win. Faith in my abilities that they can carry me to victory. Because when it comes down to it, there is no one here but me. No one on this fucking earth shares a breath that is as valuable as my own. It’s time to be the Jesus Christ of my own situation. Because if I can’t even save myself, who the fuck else will?
