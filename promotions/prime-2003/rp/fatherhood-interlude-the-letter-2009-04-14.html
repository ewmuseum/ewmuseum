Fatherhood - Interlude - The Letter

14 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 14, 2009 From TheSmokingGun.com The following letter has been leaked to us here at The Smoking Gun, and won’t be found anywhere else on the net! We just received this within the past hour, and we’ll have a chat regarding this on this upcoming Friday, April 17 at 8PM. Be sure to log in and get the latest news, rumors, and dirt on Bryan Dawkins, PRIME, and your other favorite wrestling information! ---- Mom, First off, I know your attorney advised me to stay out of touch with you guys, but honestly I could care less about what she says. I’ve already had the love of my life taken away from me before I had a chance to appreciate her, and now I’m having Nicholas taken away from me by people who don’t know what’s best for him? This is bullshit. Melinda doesn’t know us. She doesn’t know the family. She doesn’t know me, and apparently neither do you or dad, because otherwise you’d know that I’m more than capable of caring for Nicholas and giving him the life that he needs and deserves. You guys act like I haven’t overcome adversity before. Don’t you think leaving Hawaii and going out on the road full-time, trying to make a name for myself every week counts as overcoming adversity? Or how about continuing to do my job while knowing that my fiancée died because I was too wrapped up in my job? I’m pretty certain that after everything I’ve been through in the last year, I can handle taking care of my son as well. There’s no need at all to get the law involved in this, because while in the end, you might gain a grandson, you’ll lose your own son in the process. Mark my words. I love Nicholas. He’s the reason I wake up every morning anymore, and he’s the reason I’m still employed with PRIME, making a living for myself so that I can give him everything he needs to be more successful than I am. I really hope you guys think about what you’re doing before you make this decision, because if you really take a minute and think about it, you’ll realize that Nikkie would want her fiancée and Nicholas’ father to raise her son. And, in case you guys have forgotten, I’M NICHOLAS’ FATHER, NOT YOU. If this is dad’s way of getting back at me for leaving him back in Hawaii, then have him confront me directly about it. I hope he realizes that ruining my life by taking away my son isn’t going to only get back at me, but it’ll also cause the family to lose one member. I left Hawaii to make a name for myself, something I obviously wasn’t going to do with dad peeking over my shoulder at every move, criticizing me the whole way. If he wants to get me, have him attack me, not something that’s going to ruin my life. Dad doesn’t have to know about this letter, but if you’re the loving, caring mother that I remember, you’ll nudge him and be the voice of reason for him. If you guys decide in the end that you don’t wanna go through with this and come to terms face-to-face, without any bullshit mediators, let me know. You know where to find me, and you know how to get ahold of me if you decide to make the right decision. You don’t have to go through with this. We can settle this on our own, and I think everyone in this situation knows this. I love you, mom, and hope we can figure this out without going through all of the bullshit that we’ve been drug through already. Love, Bryan</align>
