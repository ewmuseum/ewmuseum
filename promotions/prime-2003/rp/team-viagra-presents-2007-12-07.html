Team VIAGRA Presents: ♦

7 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec 7th, 2007 ♦ "Come in!" Lisa Tyler shuffles papers on her desk. At the time, she had been Vice President of Talent Relations, and was spearheading the charge on recruiting Killean Sirrajin. However, Lisa had not placed all her eggs in one basket. Entering into her makeshift office is a bright white haired individual. He wears a large smile on his face, and quickly shuffles into his wooden uncomfortable chair, opposite Lisa. He stands to his feet awkwardly, shaking Lisa's extended hand, as they then both take their seat. Lisa Tyler: Welcome Jack. High Flyer: Hello there. High Flyer looks off to his side, checking out her extensive library. High Flyer: You could do with some dvd's. Lisa Tyler: You could do with some more books. High Flyer: I've had my share. Flyer hasn't returned Lisa's gaze during this entire time. He continues to look up and down. High Flyer: So... I'm not exactly sure why I'm here. Are you going to give me some free money? Lisa blinks. Lisa Tyler: That's not exactly how this buiness works. High Flyer: Oh really? Because I did get three million for a single match once. Think you could top that? Flyer finally takes a moment to look at Lisa. High Flyer: Of course, I was ordered to injure the guy. That's why the sum was so large. Lisa Tyler stares, not sure whether to take the Lunatic's nickname seriously. Quickly, Flyer bursts into laughter. High Flyer: You should see your face. Oh wait. Flyer picks up his cell phone. He snaps her picture quickly. He then starts to press some numbers on his phone. High Flyer: What's your number. You'll wanna myspace this. It's freakin' great. Lisa Tyler: I've been told you have ADD. High Flyer: My doctor's a quack. I pay him in toiletries and fake insider tips on horse racing. Like how Smarty Jones was actually a mutated Centaur from the year 300 and would never lose to a meager and common horse! Lisa Tyler: You know why I asked you here. High Flyer: Sure. Flyer sniffles. He pulls out a flyer, promoting the King of Kings event. High Flyer: I assume this is you. Lisa Tyler: It is, in a small fashion. High Flyer: Sonny Silver's your world champ? Lisa Tyler: Yeah. High Flyer: Man I'd really like to smack him once. I mean, I know his mouth is his bit, but y'know, he still really makes me want to smack him. I had to withhold myself during our entire Countdown match. Especially since I knew he was leaving and I knew it'd be my last oppertuinity. Then fWo fell apart, and part of me always regretted not just headbutting him once for real. Lisa Tyler looks from side to side awkwardly. Lisa Tyler: Uh... High Flyer: I'm kidding. I like Sonny. Okay, no, I don't like Sonny. I don't think anyone can LIKE Sonny. They can respect Sonny. Maybe even trust Sonny if your goals line up. But bah. Whatever. I'm on a tangent. So, you want me in PRIME, that it? Lisa Tyler: Well, you're highly respected around the locker room. Willing to do whatever it takes to put over a story and ultimately a team player. You've been in this business for over a decade, and you were apart of the federation that spawned PCW as it's main competition, which eventually spawned PRIME today. And I know it's not the IWO Jack... High Flyer looks down, and stares off into his mind. Lisa Tyler: But PRIME is a great chance for you to retool yourself. To give your career new life. I know you've been in no brand Wrestling. You've been running your training school. Flyer looks up, finally curious as to Lisa's extensive knowledge on himself. Lisa Tyler: I do my research Jack. Do you? Flyer smiles and stands. Lisa does the same. High Flyer: Well, I just came here because of your voice. Like a god damn Siren I tell you. Listen, let's make this simple. I don't do this deal without Tony or Mary. You hear? I'll take a pay cut from my normal rate if that's what it takes, but I don't go anywhere without them. Lisa stops, and thinks. Lisa Tyler: ... Deal. They shake hands. After a few moments, they stand apart and it slowly becomes awkward. Flyer rubs the back of his neck. High Flyer: So... King of Kings? Lisa Tyler: You'll be there, no? High Flyer: Well, we'll mail it in. Tyler's eyes narrow, as Flyer turns and walks out of the room. Two weeks later, and Team VIAGRA mailed in their "Written Statement from a Member of the WGA."
