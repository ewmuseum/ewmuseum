Preggos are Distracting.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: CWMC Round 4a A large, circular Table sits in perfect harmony amidst the dark abyss. Made of ebony, the Table would be invisible against the backdrop of nothingness, be it not for the eerie, iridescent glow material objects hold in this absence of light. This black Table sees use for only one purpose, and on nights such as this... requires such use. Lust sits in her plush chair at the head of the Table, bringing forth the beginning of the Gathering much like she has time before. Since the moment she found herself in the Lead she also found that her appointed chair contours to her body with each rising Sun. Control has always been the most potent of gifts. Now that it's in her grasp, she has no intention of releasing it. The aides sit quiet as they await her word. Gluttony, whose productivity has fractioned since relinquishing control, looks on with the same contentious regard as Wrath – a man whom he never imagined sympathizing with. "By now, we all know why we're here. Do we understand?" Greed and Envy both nod in response, while the rest give their answers with silence. "Excellent. To begin, I will open the floor to anyone so daring to take it. In regards to our progress, is there a concern that you'd wish to share?" While no one is first to speak so quickly, there is a slight disturbance amongst the pitch black. Each of them would love to spill their discontent. "I assure you, gentlemen, that there will be no ill-will towards anyone who wishes to voice their thoughts." "WASTING TIME." Someone blurts out, though Lust isn't paying attention to see who. She doesn't need to see. It's obvious who spoke by the content alone. "And I suppose you'd prefer the Morning Star sitting on his ass, taking bong-hits all day, like you had it before?" Lust replies to Gluttony with a biting tongue, a response that doesn't sit well with Pride. "'No ill-will,' eh, Luxuria?" Envy succeeds Pride's sentiments. "This isn't time to change the subject. It's your inability, Luxuria. You say since taking lead that we've prospered. How? How are we better where we are now than where we were years ago?" "We are closer to our goa-" "WE ARE CLOSER TO FUCKING AN INSIGNIFICANT PREGNANT WENCH. AND WHY? ALL BECAUSE YOU PUSH US." As Envy finally sits back down in lieu of his tirade, Pride places a sympathizing hand on his shoulder. "What Invidia is trying to say is that he doesn't see the point. He doesn't see why we've wasted this much time on such frivolous matters when we were inches from our goal. The Morning Star's loins are potent. If we allow it, he'll be prime for seeking a ripe mate." "Agreed." Wrath finally speaks. "Let's be done with this Preggo-fucking madness and move on! Enough wasting time; NOW IS THE HOUR OF DEATHBRINGING! NOW IS THE HOUR OF THE APOCOLYPSE!" Greed pounds his fist on the ebony Table. "Ira's dramatics aside, we thirst for greatness! Our appetites cannot be sated by these mortal achievements and your pesky amusements, Luxuria. All it takes is for us to grasp at the Heavens' golden ring and snatch it to descend Humanity into the chaos it so rightly deserves. For eternity, we have waited for this moment, and now it's here! Let us take it. Let us seize this opportunity and bring on the Fall. Please, permit us to do what destiny asks of us!" "Okay, okay, okay, look." Sloth pulls his feet from the Table, tipping his hat from his sight having finally awoken from his nap. "I know it seems like I don't care much, but this plan is bogus. Ripe loins? Bringing on the fall of Humanity?" Searching for the right words, Sloth can only chuckle to himself. "I'm sorry, but I'm with Lux. Let's just wait until the Preggo births then knock her up again." "Because, Acedia, we've been focused on that wench for far too long. Where has she gotten us? Where has she gotten HIM? Nowhere! Not an ounce of success or progress towards our goal. He's even losing his focus on the tournament. So far we've gained nothing from this venture." Gluttony says in response. "Like you were doing better in Lux's spot?" Sloth rolls his eyes. "Look, I'm not saying we're in a great way right now. We're not exactly doing bad either. But personally, I've still got some shit I'd like to accomplish with the Morning Star before we end everything, and once this Apocalypse gets started there ain't no turning back. I mean, do we REALLY want the end of the world to go down so soon?" "SOON? We've waited half of an eternity for this moment!" Wrath screams. "So why are you so anxious? It's going to take time for the Morning Star to find a suitable host, anyways. Not to mention the Nine Months of Housing until the Anti-Christ is even born. And then what? No one knows! We don't even know if we'll return to Nothingness!" Sloth gestures to the abyss surrounding them. His words aren't met kindly. Everyone waits to hit him with their response, but surprisingly, Lust is the first to speak. "No, they're right, Acedia. We have lost sight of our goal." The statement surprises her counsel. For many reasons, no one anticipated Lust to change her direction. "Tonight you have reminded me why our work is so important. Henceforth, we shall quit fooling around with the Pregnant Girl and commence towards our goal as planned. Rest easy, Gentlemen." Some members at the Table remain surprised. The others, satisfied. Wrath and Gluttony stay upset, much like they have been since Lust began directing them. They do realize that there is nothing more to say. Their course has been set. "We look to the Glory of the Morning Star. Meeting adjourned."
