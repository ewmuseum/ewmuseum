Reconciled

16 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 16, 2008 Beyond being a top-class athlete and world-renowned ladies man, Tyler Rayne likes to consider himself something of a connoisseur of beers. And the piss colored concoction splashing against the grimy film of his unwashed glass, with all the viscosity of that motor oil advertisers like to pander on other, less entertaining wrestling programs, barely passed muster to be considered more than the excrement its color resembled. However, the bartender was a fan of PRIME, and Tyler Rayne firmly believed in never turning down a free drink. No matter how much it made his cheeks pucker like sucking on sour asshole. Not that he would know. He sits in the far back corner of this quiet, dingy little bar, a booth to himself from which he can see the entrance. To an extent, anyway, as there’s a thick haze of smoke rising from what few tables are actually occupied. The ventilation in the den is obviously of the same caliber as the beer. He pulls his expectant gaze from the entrance, letting it fall to the filthy glass cupped between his hands. A small smile creases the lines of his face at the thought of having fingernails again. “Hey.” The voice is like a siren’s call, beautiful and terrible all at the same time. Yes, he’s been expecting her. Yes, he’s been waiting here in this rundown, half-assed little shithole for a chance to apologize. He’s wanted nothing but to see her since that moment three weeks back. Yet, here she was, and it was the most nerve-wracking, stomach twisting situation he could ever recall being in. Slowly, his eyes wander upward, noting the usual black leather ensemble. The sharp, beautiful features. Those captivating, frightening eyes. A ruffled mess of raven black hair. “Hey.” A second passes in uncomfortable silence before she sits, her hand immediately reaching for the unopened bottle of Tequila on the table. “Katt, I…you look good.” She slams the empty shot glass down as her response, the remnants of alcohol splashing just over the lip and onto her finger. Another dose is coming just as quickly. “Thanks for…thanks for seeing me. I…ya know, I’ve barely been able to sleep.” “Big match this week?” “Well, yeah, but that’s not…” “You fucked that girl yet? What’s ‘er name? Troy?” “No. I haven’t. … She’s not that type of girl.” “Does she like sex?” “ ’Scuse me.” “Sex. The physical act of making love. Coitus. Does she like it?” “I thought we were talking about me trying to kill you.” “She’s not interested in sex?” “You mean coitus?” At this, they both smile, eyes moving from their respective bottles to finally meet. And in that, the tension is gone. “You really were out of your mind, weren’t you?” “You have no idea.” “Sure I do. Remember The Fallen?” “Point. So what now?” “Thought you had a date.” “It’s not a date.” “Still jerkin’ you around, huh? I don’t understand why you just don’t fuck that cute little Brooks girl. Kid’s got a tongue like a giraffe.” Tyler cocks his head to the side, a curious eyebrow and amused smile turning in the Dark Angel’s direction. “And how would you know?” “I…heard?” “That so?” “Well, okay, so maybe kids her age like to experiment. I mean, it’s not my fault you’re always inviting her over for drinks.” “I’m not inviting her over. She just…shows up.” “Same difference." “So…we’re good?” “Aren’t we always?” “Well, yeah, but I’ve never tried to kill you before.” She laughs, offering a non-committed shrug to go with her shot. “First time for everything. ‘Sides, after what they put you through, stands to reason you’d go a bit wacky. Not like I haven’t gone a little wicked wicca Willow a few times myself, ya know?” “I know.” Silence. They drink. “Thank you, Katt. I…I really don’t know what I’d do without you.” “Probably get drunk a lot less.” “Maybe. Lindsay can hold her own, though. Never figured her for a drinker, but…” “You’ve really got it bad for this girl, don’t you?” “More than I’d like to admit. Or she’d like to know, for that matter. Speaking of, I should probably get going. We’re supposed to be meeting soon." “Right.” He stands, pulling out a handful of cash to leave on the table. For a moment he lingers, considering a hundred different things he wants to say. Instead, he just settles for walking away. Well, almost. “Oh, and Ty.” “Yeah?” “I understand. I mean, I don’t like it…but if he’d murdered my sister, I ‘d want revenge, too.” He nods. “Ya know, this’ll be the first time we went to a bar and didn’t get into some kind of fight.” “Like I said, first time for everything. I’ll see you later, Mr. Rayne.” “RAYNE?!” A chair scrapes across the floor, an old, unshaven drunk stumbling from his table to point an unsteady finger in Tyler’s direction. “Tyler fucking Rayne? You’re a suck cocking son of a dick, you know that? I lost two hundred bucks that night you beat Shunny Shliver. And now…I wants it back.” Tyler shrugs. “Then come and get it, asshole.” With a guttural, savage war cry the drunkard rushes forward, damn near falling on his face before he even reaches Rayne. Tyler waits patiently for the bum to get a little closer, lowers a shoulder, lifts the bastard by his ankles and drops him face first down on the table. Tyler spins, fast, raising his right leg as the entire left side of his body drops. His left palm touches the dirt-covered floor, providing the right amount of balance for his right knee to rise way above normal levels of flexibility and crack the filthy drunk right in the back of his head. Previously unnamed asshole drops like Nitz on his knees before Blaine Blair. “Well, so much for that.” “Figures. You should scram, now. Wouldn’t want to keep the Queen waiting.” And with a final chuckle, Tyler Rayne does indeed scram.
