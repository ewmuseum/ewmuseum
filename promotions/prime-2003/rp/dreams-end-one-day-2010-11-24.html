Dreams end one day

24 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/24/10 The scene opens up to a dimly lit, worn down gym. The air is musty and the smell is that of a high school locker room. Scattered across the gym, there lie various weight sets and machines and in the middle of it all is a beat down wrestling ring. You can hear loud grunts coming from the furthest corner of the gym. The sound is that of a man pushing himself to the limits, as the camera pans over you see Drake Daniels bench pressing. He is putting up damn near 120 pounds over his body weight, as the sweat just continually pours from his face. He finally finishes the last rep of his set as he sets the weight back on the ledge and sits up slowly. He starts to wipe the sweat from his face with the back of his hand. Then he reaches for his water bottle and takes a swig, swishing it around his mouth before he spits it on to the filthy gym floor. He then stands up and takes off his now drenched gray tank top, using it as a towel to wipe down the rest of himself. In doing so he reveals a scar on the left side of his chest right on top of where his heart is. After wiping the beads of sweat off himself he turns around. He then starts to stare at himself in the mirror located on the back wall. His eyes are fixated on the scar as he is gently rubbing over it with his hand. He then slowly starts to go in to flashback mode. Flashback A drunken Drake Daniels with tears running down his faces is stumbling around his kitchen, knocking over papers and pretty much anything that stands in his way. He falls to the ground in from of the kitchen sink, but uses the counter top to hoist himself back up, as he leans over the sink he begins to vomit out all the toxicants he had digested through out the night. Once he is finished he wipes his mouth removing about eighty percent of the vomit from his face. He looks over towards the knife rack and start to make his way. He grabs out a sharp knife from the rack. He takes the handle of the knife with to hands and raises it above his chest, with a painful smile on his chest he begins to dig the knife into his chest, slowly and painfully he starts to carve a heart into his chest. The blood is now pouring out of his chest, as the alcohol has made his blood so thin. His body covered in blood falls to the kitchen floor. Drake with a grin still on his face lies on his kitchen floor in a puddle of his own blood. Drake Daniels “WHY!” He yells out loud as he slowly starts to go in and out of it. Rolling around slightly on the floor he continues to yell that, till finally he stops moving. This brings Drake back to present time as he shakes his head and stops touching the scar on his chest. He then moves over to the preacher curl set up. He gets himself in place as he begins his set, starring at himself in the mirror as he begins his work out; he starts to talk to himself. Drake Daniels “You’re the best!” “Come On!” “Don’t give up now!” The veins in his biceps are bugling out and he pushes himself harder and harder. The sweat is starting to drip with a constant flow once more and Drake’s face is getting more and more beat red. He finally brings up his last one and as he holds it for a minute his face looks like it could burst at any moment. He lets go dropping the weight to the ground and setting off another flashback. Flashback It starts off with Drake hitting the mat hard Man “That’s a boy keep em coming like that and you’ll be in the ring in no time kid.” Drake smiles at the man as he continues to takes bumps. Drake’s mind has wandered back to when he was first starting out in the wrestling business. He first few months in Fabulous Andy’s wrestling school weren’t your ideal setting. The school was worn down and not many people were paying to join. The school was rough in the summer there was no cooling system and in the winter the only thing that could keep you warm was running laps around the outside of the ring. Andy was an old school mind, in a new school era. People had stopped caring about the sport and more about the glitz and glamor of it all, Andy shied away from teaching wrestlers how to get over with gimmicks and drilled it into them that mat skills and technique would get them far. Drake loved working under the teachings of Andy and took everything Andy said and soaked it up like a sponge. The scene continues on with memories Drake has of things Andy used to teach him back in the day. From learning his first suplexs to them tagging together in Drake's first match ever. New Scene The scene cuts to what looks like an old broiler room. The air has the smell of dirt and mildew in it, the lights are flickering off and on and you can see steam leaking from the pipes. The sound of knocking can be heard on the pipes as the camera picks up a silhouette. It starts to grow as it moves towards the camera view. A voice starts to come in, as the body slowly creeps into the picture. Drake Daniels “Tick Tock...Tick Tock. That's the sound a time running out on my opponents this week." Drake grins "My time here in Prime can either be lengthy or short this week will decided all that. When I was asked about why I entered this tournament it was simple to prove to everyone that Drake Daniels still has "it". I've been on a uphill battle over the last few months since my return to pro-wrestling, and this could be my chance to level out and become noteworthy all over again. I've struggled like many men before me, but those men quit and I will not. This is my time and my opportunity to show who am I. So guys be ready to either have your dreams killed or end up just another pig in my slaughterhouse." The scene fades to black.
