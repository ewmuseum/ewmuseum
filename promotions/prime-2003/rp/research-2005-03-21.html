Research

21 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/21/05 “I can’t believe I’ve fallen this far. I KNOW I haven’t been doing so great so far, but THIS?!” We open up to see the Trashcan Man sitting on a black leather couch in a tastefully (in a bachelor sort of way) decorated living room. He’s looking anxiously at a telephone that’s sitting on a smoked glass and chrome coffee table. He stabs out a cigarette into the overflowing ashtray sitting next to the phone, already reaching for another one from the crumpled pack lying on the arm rest next to him as he continues his tirade. “It’s the show before the pay per view… and you’ve got me scheduled JERKING THE CURTAIN?!” He flicks open his battered silver Zippo with a practiced flick and lights up his cigarette with a burn scarred hand, taking a deep drag before exhaling a cloud of smoke. If his tone of voice wasn’t enough of a clue, we can get an idea of the level of his agitation by the way he is constantly flicking the butt of his smoke, sending a sprinkle of ashes all over the table hitting everything but the ashtray he’s aiming for. “W-W-Well sir… w-w-we wanted to start the show with a bang… A b-b-big star s-s-such as yourself…” The ever nervous and usually perspiring PRIME intern Blaine Blair is interrupted by the hardcore legend. “You’ve said that three times now, Blaine. Let me talk to Hin See. I’ve got to get this straightened out. I haven’t been stuck in an opening match since the early days of PCW. That’s where you stick the jobbers so the late comers won’t miss anything good.” “T-T-That’s n-n-not true! W-W-We wanted to get the crowd f-f-fired up by h-h-having a big n-n-name like you start the show.” The Trashcan Man leans forward, resting his elbows on his knees as he takes another drag. “I’m more than a big name, dammit! I’m one of the hardest working men in our business. Didn’t you see that match my first week with The HIT and that Razor guy? Now put Hin See on the phone. I’ve paid my dues, and I deserve better than this!” “I’m s-s-sorry sir… he’s not taking phone calls right now… he’s in a meeting. B-b-but I assure you that this is nothing against you…” He’s interrupted again by Trashy, who leans forward so far that he actually rises from the couch, bending over so his face is mere inches from the phone. “I put asses in seats and put shoulders to canvas, little man.” “Y-y-you haven’t been d-d-doing much of putting shoulders to canvas s-s-since you’ve came to PRIME” A shocked look comes over the hardcore legend’s face at the amazingly uncustomary response. He slowly puts out his cigarette as he slumps back down in the couch, his jaw coming closed with an audible click. “M-M-Mr Trashcan Man… sir? I-I-I’m sorry sir… p-p-please don’t get me fired…” All we can here is leather creaking as the Trashcan Man leans back, running his fingers through his hair. “S-s-sir?” “You’re right Blaine. You’re very right. I’m sorry that I yelled at you.” “It’s okay sir…” The intern’s voice is cut off as Trashy leans over and hits the disconnect button before leaning back again. “Damn me, the kid’s right. I haven’t done a damn thing since I’ve gotten here. Sure I’ve put up some good fights… but not really.” I hate to say this Trash… but so far your heart hasn’t been into this. Although it’s pumped enough of your blood all over the place lately. “You’re right, Smiley. You don’t think…” That you’ve bought into this whole legend crap that people have been spewing like Bill Clinton into the mouth of a waiting intern? “Not exactly how I’d put it… but yeah. I’ve been walking into the ring pretty much expecting these guys to lay down for me. I haven’t even been researching my opponents.” Perhaps we should fix that then… to the Laptop of Hardcore™ old chum! “Hey, there’s now way I’m Robin in this relationship buddy…” With that the Trashcan Man gets up and dashes into his study. Well, if you can call it a study. It DOES have a bookshelf, but it’s mainly filled with comic books. It’s more of a shrine to his career, really, as posters of the hardcore legend’s exploits cover the walls. A still of a bloodied Trashcan Man standing triumphant holding his first PCW Extreme title as a very angry Boda looks on sits next to a frozen shot of Trashy putting Ivan Stanislav’s head through the mat with a slingshot DDT in OSW. A replica PCW World title is hung above two posters, one showing the Trashcan Man being handed the PCW world title as he stands over the defeated Tyler Nelson, the other showing a bloodied, barely conscious TCM being supported by Alexander Miyagawa and Brock Newbludd as Alan Kriegman, coldly smiling, is handing the PCW World title to him after his victory in the dual tiered cage match ending the 64 man tournament. The square yellow cardboard mask of Mr Smiley Face is hung above the Smilin’ Dude’s customary black trenchcoat. Beside it on it’s own rack rests the nail studded barbed wire wrapped engine of hardcore destruction known as Bob. Trashy sits down at a computer desk covered with framed photos of him standing with several incarnations of Asylum in both PCW and OSW, as well as pictures of him standing with Ed and Tempest. “Let’s fire this badboy up.” Atomic engines to power… turbines to speed! “What’s with the Batman references lately?” I’m feeling nostalgic from when you actually won matches. Wasn’t that back when Adam West was Batman? “Real funny Smiley…” Trashy reaches for the duct tape covered Laptop of Hardcore™, a present from Tempest back in OSW. The sound of Godzilla screaming greets him as Windows XP fires up. “I’ve got a match… a hardcore match with Shane Wethers… what do we know about this guy?” Didn’t he sing It’s Raining Men? “That’s the Weather Girls.” All that can be heard is the clicking of a mouse as the Trashcan Man navigates his way through PRIME’s website. Um… Trash? How do you know who sung It’s Raining Men? Is there something you want to tell me? “Bite me, Smiley. Steven Smith was my commissioner in OSW. You know we had to hear that damn song constantly. I actually had to renegotiate the contract with the record label for rights to that song.” He frowns for a moment. “Wow… they STILL haven’t put up my picture in the biography section. I’m going to have to get on someone about that… Ah, here we go… Shane Wethers.” Looks like he’s coming soon, too. “Yeah, and he’s been here longer than I have. Wonder who he pissed off… Wow… this guy was a PWA graduate!” Man, we dodged a bullet with that one, didn’t we? “No kidding, I still can’t believe the Phantom Booker wanted ME to run that place when it first opened up. We want these kids to learn the fundamentals, not the best way to stitch yourself up after a stiff chair shot.” He clicks around a bit more on the site. “Hmmm… brawler and extreme high flyer… what the hell does extreme high flying mean?” He likes Mountain Dew on long airplane flights? Ding! Hello, stewardess. Can I have a Mountain Dew. I want to make this flight EXTREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEME!!! Trashy chuckles as he continues to peruse the limited biography. “Ok… he uses a double arm DDT as a finisher… that’s a good move but easy enough to counter, I can take those all day… says here he calls himself the Eternal Punk.” What, he likes to steal handbags from immortal old women? “I don’t know… huh? He’s a punk… but his entrance music’s My Generation from Limp Biskit? LIMP FUCKING BISKIT?!! How the hell does he get off calling himself a punk. No Sex Pistols? No Dead Kennedys? Hell, no Dead Milkmen?! Jesus, if this guy ever ran into a real punk they’d headbutt him and take his milk money… Do you realize what this means?” Trashy leans back and shakes his head. “I’m being booked against a poser. A Hot Topic loving Green Day worshipping POSER! Man, I know I’ve lost my first two matches, but COME ON!” Why are you such an expert on punk culture? “Not claiming to be, but I know a poser when I see one. This guy probably makes Duke Williams look like Sid Vicious.” Speaking of Duke… I’ve got a plan for his favorite son. Trashy grins in mischievous delight. “Do you now? Think we can get him to put his title on the line at the next PPV?” Oh, I’m sure we can talk him into it. And if not, well, we’ll still have some fun. “Tell me more.” We fade to black as the Trashcan Man’s grin widens in glee.
