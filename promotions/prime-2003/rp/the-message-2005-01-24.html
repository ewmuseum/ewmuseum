The Message

24 January 2005

Date: 1/24/05 January 24, 2005: Friends it is time I get some! Darkness has fallen, with the sudden drop in temperature along with the wind chill the current air temperature is at a whooping 19 degrees. Raleigh, North Carolina is a rather chilly for this time of the year. But is sure a lot nicer then the North East is having at the current moment. A local church which is under construction at this current moment is the current sight we see. Inside this church is a single candle burning; the candle illuminates the whole building. The single candle shows the draped tarps and curtains to cover the newly finished pews. Over by the candle sits a single man, this man is propping himself up by holding one of those pews while sitting upon an empty bucket. “A single candle might mean nothing to many of you. But to me, a single candle shows a large amount about myself. I have since placed my self into solitude. I have put myself into many situations I am not pleased about recently and have made many stupid mistakes; the time has come for a change.” The man who is all alone sitting on the bucket picks his head up. The face is showing an immense amount of stress. The face is showing many wrinkles from the weariness. “I am going to entrust a select man with my trust, I am going to instill my career in this man. This is one of the few men I have entrusted before, but in the time of need I crumbled. I am now hands and knees hoping for this person to instill their trust in me, as I am currently putting my trust in him.” The man mid sentence gets off the bucket and gets on his knees and puts his arms into his air. The man looks rather odd but being alone in the church he is unnoticeable to many. “It is time for my acceptance of friendship.” As he speaks those words he reaches out to the single candle burning and lights one of the many other candles that are around. “Soon these candles may or may not be lit showing my friendships, only time will tell.” The man stands up and blows both candles out, he steps away from the small bursts of smoking now blowing the smoke into his face. He turns away and much like the previous lit candles he slowly fades into darkness. Journal Entry 3.0 Poor, Poor, Oscar Hernandez My only friend who was originally pushed upon me by the former dictator, Motokio, Oscar Hernandez, a man who has tried to form a friendship with me, but yet I pushed him away like he was that extra bit of salt left on the table after dinner. The time will come where Oscar might get his revenge but I honestly think he will be take some time and realize that he is in need of me also. We are going to bond our friendship and it will happen this week on ReVolution. We will show the amount of friendship when we step into the ring this week. After many weeks of fighting we are going to bond once again, in the ring. It will be nothing like the stories of Troy and Rolo bonding, but more of a bonding of a tag team that might be the future of PRIME’s Tag Team division. As I have said many times before only time will tell, and to be honest this week is one instance that is completely true. We are going to see Oscar and I, tag up and face Brandon Youngblood and a man who he does not even know. Sometimes just knowing the person makes that much of a difference, Oscar and I will show that this week. January 25, 2005: Tides have changed around here. PRIME superstars com and go, and many do not last longer then a few months from the physical pain and agony that is achieved from wrestling, especially here in PRIME. We are located at arena where many crews are setting up for this weeks Revolution. Backstage many of the newer roster members are getting ready for this week’s show. Like many of them Peter Vetra is backstage stretching and working out. “This week I am stuck in the ring with a man who is new to the federation, a man who like many of the others will not last long here in PRIME, they are not strong enough they are not of the highest wrestling capabilities as we are. Jeff Garvin, The Squared Circle did not get you ready for what you are about to go through this week. We say we are number one by definition and we mean it to the fullest extent.” Peter breathes in and looks back up. “What makes you think you are any different then the many men that have fallen before you? We have seen wrestlers come and go, Vince Jones, Bastion Cossack, hell even Joey Troy could only take it for so long. What makes you think you are strong enough to hang in the ring with Oscar and me? We are two of the longest reigning wrestlers in this company, competition is tough and we both are planning on showing you this week. Time has come for us; we will show the world what we are capable of.” Peter Vetra a man of the uttermost respect looks down from facing the camera and turns his head slightly. Shaking it ever so slightly he looks back up and you see a tear come down from his eye. The camera fades to black. Journal Entry 3.1: Why did I do that? Many people will question the tear on my face during the latest interview, well what people need to realize is Peter Vetra is human also. I am a human being and I am going to show the world such. I too have feelings and the thought of being a loner the rest of my life sickens me. I can only hope that Oscar is going to take me back and accept me as a friend, we will see that this week. The times have changed and I am now the person looking for the respect of people and the friendship of others when just a few months ago Oscar and I were forced to be friends. That evening a friendship will either blossom or it will fail, many people will see it right before their eyes. Will you be watching?
