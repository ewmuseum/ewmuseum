The Confession: Part III Finale - Pulling The Plug

14 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14-11-10 They'll do anything to keep from getting caught, they'll do anything to get away if they do...and they always keep mementos. This is right. What I'm doing is right. I know he's guilty. He knows he's guilty. This is right. This is justice...please God don't let anyone find out about this. This is justice. I'm right. He repeated the mantra over and over in his head as he played over exposed nerve endings with the blood-crusted blade. Daniels had done the hard work for him, carving him up nice and good so that all Von Kelsig had to do was put the tiniest bit of pressure on the flesh to get a reaction. He spent a shaky half hour teasing the steel over different wounds, asking nothing and simply enjoying the grimace on Michael's face as he poked and prodded him. The way he squirmed and whimpered under the knife gave him satisfaction. Initially, Michael had screamed. It shocked Von Kelsig just how loudly he screamed. Daniels spent most of the night cutting him, and Michael let him know how it felt. The giant had to cover his ears at times when the volume became so great it vibrated through his skull. The Biker didn't seem fazed by the sound whatsoever, but commanded the giant to go check on their other guest down in the basement. He even went as far as to glimpse out the window at the street below, and though no one was around to hear the cries it felt like someone, somehow, knew what was going on in the abandoned house. Until he reached the basement the giant could hear howling through the walls. Closing the heavy door behind him he tread silently down the stairs and across to the door to Tracy's room. He held his breath and listened carefully, content that nothing discernible could be heard from above. His own heartbeat was louder than the faintest muffle that permeated the walls. When he walked in he noticed magazines she had been given were stacked next to the bed, and sat atop them was a digital radio complete with a thin layer of dust on top. She sat bolt upright at the sight of him. “What's going on?” “Nothing, I just came down to check on you. How you doing?” “I wanna know what's going on. Where's Michael?” “He's fine. He-” “How long is this going to take? How long have we even been here now?” “Few days. I know it's a drag but you need to bear with us. It's this or the police...and police means prison.” He gave her a moment to let the fact sink in, watching as she gazed about the room and sighed, trying to hold back the waterworks. Her lip quivered and she held a hand up to cover her mouth, but managed to swallow the lump in her throat and blink away the tears welling in her eyes. Composing herself, she straightened out her blouse and rubbed her hands on her leggings then sniffed. “Can I at least see him? Is he okay?” “He's just fine...” World's biggest lie, and he had to focus his facial muscles to hide it from Michael's wife. “...but you can't see him.” “Why not?! I want to see my husband! Why won't you let me see him??” “Because this is the way it has to be,” he said with authority. “I know you don't understand any of this but you need to trust me. When the time is right I'll let you see Michael. For now though, just sit tight and stop worrying. You understand?” “I just want to see my husband...” Tracy huffed under her breath, turning away from the giant and lying in the foetal position on her mattress. He left her like that, sulking into her pillow. The absence of screaming on the way back upstairs worried him, until he made out gurgling and drawn out groaning coming from the room. As he walked in Daniels spun around with the knife held up, then seeing the anxious giant straightened up and wiped his brow. The blade was thick with crimson goop, but the Biker's hands were clean. Surgical. Von Kelsig was impressed. “S'yer turn now. Good luck.” The knife was in his hand before he had a chance to reply. Daniels moved aside and headed for the door, revealing the mess he had made on the table. Von Kelsig gasped, eyes fixed on the quivering mass. “Wait, where are you going?” “Why? Ya wan'me ta hold yer fuckin' hand'r somethin'?” Daniels said, glancing back as he clutched the doorknob in hand. “No but...look at the fucking state of him man. The hell am I supposed to do now? Did you even ask him anything? Did he say anything?” The Biker pulled him close, his hand wrapped tightly around the giant's bicep to emphasize his point like a parent scolding their child. “Ya got alcohol, salt, my knife and,” he paused, reaching into his pocket and producing a lighter which he pushed into Von Kelsig's free hand, “this. I set him up for ya, and yer lucky I got the common fuckin' courtesy ta do that. Take 'at shit an' conduct yer li'l interrogation. When yer finished I'll help ya get rid of 'im...” That sent a chill up the giant's spine. In the heat of dealing with the current situation he hadn't stopped to think about what would happen next. In the dark recesses of his mind, in that portion of his psyche where the Reuben Blanco saga and other such misdeeds resided, a bad thought had snuck into his reason. Murder. It didn't even shock him that that conclusion had come automatically. What else could be done in this predicament? Pretty pleases weren't going to cut it this time. Not with Michael Sloan. Murder was an option. Watching Daniels slink out of the room like he had nothing to do with what was happening made him realize as much. It may have meant straining the ties with Shakur, but it was possible. The giant had seen enough of the underworld in his time, would it kill him to be an accessory to murder? The whole reason Daniels was even here was down to shady extra-curricular activities within the PRIME family. If anyone knew anything about the goings on outside the ring they didn't open their mouths, and if they did they would probably find themselves in the situation Von Kelsig was in right now. But what about Tracy? A loose end like that could bring hell to his doorstep. People like Tracy Sloan were what stopped PRIME's deviants being outright criminals, well, with one glaring omission lying before him. She couldn't go down like Michael should. The giant would have to find another way. Michael's eyes rolled into the back of his head as Von Kelsig asked him, “ready to talk?” He flicked the blade back and forth in front of him, getting a feel for the weapon. It seemed kind of redundant he thought given that there weren't many places left on Sloan's person to inflict more wounds. Daniels was a master craftsman. He stepped forward, standing over Michael and dangling the knife blade down over a long slice across his left pectoral. “Tell me the truth about Gloria.” It wasn't so much of a request as a warning, and when Michael continued to ignore him the giant dipped the knife into the wound about half an inch, then twisted. The reaction was immediate; every muscle tensing in his body and his eyes rolling back around. His face reddened so much so that the giant thought he might burst, and a hopeless shriek struggled to part pursed lips. “I can't hear you Michael,” he said softly. He twisted the blade further, prompting Michael to scream. A spray of crimson saliva flew from his mouth into the air before settling again in a spatter over his face. He cried in agony as Von Kelsig removed the knife and slotted it into a puncture wound in Michael's navel. He shivered at the thought of how it must feel, but once more twisted the blade and widened the hole. The noise that escaped Michael's throat was more of a gurgled choking than a scream. Von Kelsig noticed that the tip of his tongue was stuck between his teeth, oozing blood. Removing the knife, the giant moved to the head of the table and angrily thumped a fist down onto Sloan's mouth, jarring the muscle prompting a squeal from his captive. “I'm not going to stop until you start talking.” Both wounds where he had twisted the blade had begun bleeding again, a viscous fluid more closely resembling poison than blood flowing over Michael's bruised skin. He stopped to watch it for a moment, and realized that it wouldn't take long before Michael wouldn't be able to say anything before he bled out, at the rate he wanted to go with the knife. The giant glanced back at the salt and alcohol sat by the window. He couldn't help but smile as he reached down and retrieved the salt, pouring an ounce into his palm and holding it above Michael's body. “You're sure about this? I could stop right now and get you help if you want...just tell me it was you that did Gloria in. I know there's no other explanation. Something as personal as that note I found doesn't belong in your possession, not even by accident. So come on...tell me something I already know.” Michael's eyes were shut tight again as he slapped the handful of salt into the chest wound. A plume of crimson soaked through the white crystals, dissolving them into the flesh. Michael shut his eyes tighter and made a sound that Von Kelsig likened to a goat getting its throat cut. There was no way to tell what the sick fuck was thinking, but when that devilish smile tried to creep across his lips again Von Kelsig poured out some salt onto the blade, coating it before plunging it back into Michael's belly button and stirring it like a teaspoon. “GYYYEAAAAAAAAAAAARRRGGGHHHH!!” The giant was startled as Michael suddenly lurched at him, grabbing his arm with both hands and pulling it up and taking the knife with it. The blood soaking his hands made it easy to slither down Von Kelsig's wrist and clamp them over the knife. He felt as though he were watching it through someone else's eyes as the monstrous form spat and gurgled, trying to prise his fingers off of the handle. “FUCK OFF!” Pulling his hands free of Michael the giant smashed a fist across the bridge of his nose, dropping Sloan back to the table. “That how you wanna play it?? Fine by me!” Grabbing Michael's right wrist, Von Kelsig began slashing at his already wounded hands. Cutting into the fingers, knuckles, nails and palm until it resembled red ribbon rather than skin. As Michael's head lolled groggily from side to side he was powerless to stop the giant pouring out more salt onto his hand, smearing it in and clutching it tight in his own paw, working the condiment into agonizing pain. He didn't stop to see how Michael was reacting, instead moving to the other hand and cutting it up just as worse before pouring out even more salt and working it into the flesh. Glancing at the torso, Von Kelsig shrugged and popped the cap off the container and dumped the rest of the salt over his body. Massaging it firmly into all the nooks and crannies, he cured Sloan like a slab of beef. Stepping back and wiping his hands off he admired his handiwork as Michael began spasmodically twitching from the waist up, his legs futilely kicking out. “Let's see you try that again motherfucker. Just try it.” Gnarled fingers reached up at him. The mess on his hands had formed into a creamy pink dripping foam. He tried to sit up, groaning like an undead corpse yearning for its next meal. Von Kelsig had seen violence before, he'd inflicted severe injury on his fellow man for the love of the fans, but the sight before him was enough to turn him off violence. It was as though Michael were melting before his eyes as gradually his entire upper body began fizzing and bubbling. “Stop...stop...please...pleeease...” The thought was dead in an instant as Michael appealed to him with tears in his eyes. His lip petted even under the strain of the cuts tearing at the skin, he pleaded with the giant in silence as his body continued to twitch and spasm. “I'LL STOP WHEN YOU FUCKING ADMIT THAT YOU KILLED GLORIA!! DO YOU UNDERSTAND ME??” Michael broke down. His eyes welled up and his laboured breaths fuelled the pathetic groans emanating from his bloody throat. Gradually his arms fell limply by his sides and he lay back on the table. His taut wounds relaxed as he did, but that only aggravated the salt invading his flesh. His chest rose and fell quickly and he clenched his teeth as waves of hurt washed over him. “I...didn't...do...anything...I didn't...I didn't...I swear...” Insane he thought, that one so sick could be reduced to such a pitiful sight. The fire in his belly roared that this was what he deserved, but the chill creeping back up his spine again tapped him on the back of the head and whispered that perhaps he had it all wrong. “I don't...know what you're...talking about...you got it all...all wrong...pleeease...” He was convincing. Too convincing. It made the giant sick to his stomach to see such false innocence. Had he scrambled Michael's brains so much that the bastard couldn't even remember laughing through half of his torment? Why laugh in the face of someone's suffering? A punch to the jaw stunned Michael, but his response stunned the giant. He must have been in more pain than he had ever felt before, but the way he articulated himself with such clarity all of a sudden scared Von Kelsig. “You won't get away with this! You fucking accuse me of something I never did and you...” he stopped to take a look at himself, his voice cracking, “fucking did this to me...fucking lunatic. You and that fucking prick biker. You're dead. Hear me? Dead.” Sighing, the giant sauntered over to the window and picked up the bottles of alcohol. Michael's threats were a muffling reverberation against the cogs turning in his mind, and when he stood over Sloan and twisted the caps off of the bottles and splashed the liquid all over him he couldn't help but greet the howling with a smile. When he thumbed the wheel back on Daniels' lighter he held the flame close so Michael knew he wasn't fooling around. “You don't have the balls...” “Don't I? Be doing you a favour with all that blood spilling out of you to cauterize those wounds of yours.” “Then fucking do it. Go on! I don't have a fucking thing to say to you Von Kelsig. You fucked up in a bad way today and if you thought I was going to say anything about whatever the fuck's going on here after cutting me up, you were wrong. What difference is it going to make if put that flame on me? Huh? Don't you think if I knew anything I would've told you before you did this to me??” He held up his hands, fighting the pain that ate at his flesh, to make his point. “Think about it. This isn't you Von Kelsig. Someone's set me up. We all saw you get arrested at Two-Oh-Three. We all heard the rumours...” His tone was fervent now, and his tongue tripped over his sentences as he forced them out as quickly as possible to make his point between bouts of pain stinging him all over. “...What's to say someone else in the company didn't set you up for a fall? Why would I? We did our thing, I played my hand and you yours and we finished it at Colossus. Sure I wanted you out of PRIME, I'll admit it. I wanted to be the number one Monster, and I pulled my tricks to do it. But what you're talking about? I'm a married man Von Kelsig. How could I be party to something like that? How could I even condone that kind of shit when I'm with Tracy. If something happened to her I'd-...” “Kill whoever was responsible?” Michael had always thought of Von Kelsig as a beast of burden, just a dumb bovine specimen to be driven like cattle by superior individuals like him for his own personal gain. During their feud he didn't think much of Von Kelsig's methods of retaliation to his mind games. Hessian was all about brawn over brains. So he was justified in his wariness over the steely glare in the giant's eyes when he interrupted him then. “NO!” He yelped frantically as Von Kelsig dropped the Zippo onto his belly. He cowered as a burning sensation ripped through his body. Slapping at his torso he tried to put out the flames eating at his flesh while the giant laughed at him. Michael opened his eyes and glanced at the damage and felt anger rising. The giant had faked the drop. The lighter hit the floor with a thud, and he realized that the only pain he had felt was his body burning at the slightest move he made. The combination of the cuts mixed with citric acid, salt and alcohol had put his nervous system into overdrive, and he had been fooled because of it. Moreover when he looked up at the giant to convey his anger he found Von Kelsig walking to the door. “What are you doing?” “Nothing. Just when you said there about Tracy...gave me an idea.” “DON'T YOU FUCKING-” but the giant was already gone, and as Michael summoned all his strength to stop Von Kelsig all he could do was flop to the floor in a writhing heap, gasping and reaching for the closed door. He could take the pain he was in, of that there was no question. He'd been carved up like a Christmas turkey and sure, it hurt like fuck, but he never let on about anything. He could take it. But Von Kelsig had already used Tracy as a pawn in his defence on ReVolution, and the look in his eye and the way he said it when he walked off told Michael that she wasn't out of the meat grinder yet. Suddenly his vision blurred, and after managing to crawl all of one foot his brain told him he'd had enough for now and shut off, plunging him into unconsciousness. ---- “Finished a'ready?” Daniels asked from the bottom of the stairs. “I thought you were leaving?” Von Kelsig replied, pushing past the Biker and storming down the hall. “I heard yer up'ere. Stuck aroun'in case ya couldn't handle'a li'l interrogation.” The giant stopped short of the basement door and stomped back towards Daniels, pulling him by the arm. “You ain't going nowhere. Come with me, we need to talk.” He struggled at first, but the more responsibility Von Kelsig took of the situation the less work Daniels would have to do afterwards. He might have been in the zone right now but his conscience couldn't handle the baggage that came with what was going on, and when he doubted himself Daniels could offload the extra onto him and walk away clean. “What're ye up ta now?” “We're getting his wife. He didn't say fuck all to you and all he did was blubber like a fucking baby to me. I know it's all a goddamn act but he won't talk. Doesn't give a fuck what we do to him but I'll bet he'll give a fuck if it's his wife taking one for the team.” Daniels stopped the giant in his tracks and spun him around so they were face to face. He leaned in close and kept his tone calm so as not to alert Tracy or Michael, who was too busy laid out on the floor groaning to hear anyway. “She ain't part'a the deal. She gets involved'n I'm out, un'erstand?” “Two things Christian, one; I've got a plan for Tracy. Two......how far afield do your connections stretch?” The Biker didn't have to guess what the giant had in store for the petite blonde, but why ask about his connections? Part of the deal was to disregard any and all custom surrounding the incident at Shakur's home in exchange for help with Sloan, and that deal was made between Devin and Von Kelsig with Daniels acting as overseer. That's why he was here now and not Shakur, the Biker was the real tie to the underworld and now the giant was trying to take advantage of that. “Why'd ya wanna know a thing like'at?” “Well,” Von Kelsig said with a furtive glance, “I was thinking about what happens when we're done with him.” “Ya don't wanna kill'im now?” “No, not if this plan works. If this works...I want him to live forever. That means sending him far, far away.” ---- Tracy Sloan’s high pitched screams pierced Michael’s black heart. The love of his life, the one and only, was crying out in pain. Michael Sloan could endure an ungodly amount of human suffering to his body. He had fought and scratched his way to the door when he heard her screaming, not caring that his wounds would pick up all manner of infection after being dragged across the floor. The screams and suffering of others meant little to nothing. But her? She was precious and his, all his. Inside he knew this day would come. For more than a quarter of a century he had barricaded his feelings from the world knowing they would lead to his undoing, but he couldn’t keep himself from loving her. It was a weakness, everyone had one. Michael thought he was above it. What purpose did love have in his life? Yet, as her pitiful yelp of pain echoed in his head his heart began to melt. The stairs echoed with sounds of knocking, dragging and muffled cries. Desperately he reached up for the doorknob, gritting his teeth and fighting the bursts of pain that emanated whenever he moved. As the last knock sounded and he knew they were on the landing beyond the door he felt an inhuman rage fill his heart. It burned as brightly as the agony racking his body, and when there was silence and whispering just outside the room he was blinded by the great desire to snuff out the man who would hurt his Tracy. His hand touched the doorknob at the same time her screaming became strained shrieking. The door banged and bent inwards, and after every thunderous jolt the screams turned to broken breaths. He stopped breathing, focusing every little bit of energy he had left into getting that door open, but as the wood began to splinter and he could see shadows dancing through the cracks he still couldn't find the strength to open the door. Suddenly a hole was blown through the middle of the door, and a bloodied blonde head dangled through, gazing at him through lifeless eyes. Now his heart stopped, and for a moment he was truly lost. He gazed into those beautiful dead eyes and felt himself being sucked into the floor. But when the pupil flickered back to life and glanced at him and her mouth jerked as if to call for help he flew forward ignoring the pain he was in and reached up to her. In an instant the head disappeared back through the hole, which just as quickly filled with shadow that met his hand with the force of a concrete wall when he hit it. That's when he began breathing again, and felt his heart pump life through him once more... When he opened his eyes and the phosphenes cleared from his sight he knew he had been dreaming, though he was lost in a womb of darkness. A glance to the right found the faintest square of light fighting through the thick black. The window. He must have been out for four or five hours. “YYYAAAAAAAAAAAARGHHHH!!!” The scream deafened him, caused him to jump out of his skin and sent the tingling sting of goose flesh over him. It came from directly in front of him, no more than a few inches from his face. He could smell the stale odour of Tracy's morning breath, a smell he'd come to love lying next to her for so many mornings. He wanted to welcome her to a new day, but he had just woken from a dream into what seemed like another. His body still pulsed with a familiar agony, and from that he couldn't even tell if he was lying down or sitting up. Still he fought to see through the night under the weight of a mighty headache bearing down on his brow. “Nononononono, please I-YYYAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARGHHHHH” The scream was so loud this time it practically blew out his eardrums. Something wet spattered across his face over the dried blood crusting over his wounds, and he drew great breaths to gauge what kind of fluid had hit him. To his horror he smelled copper. He could hear her silent attacker pressing and squeezing her, but for the life of him couldn't tell what kind of action it signalled or where on her body. His heart was battering so loudly inside his chest that he wished he weren't cursed with this human body, just long enough to rescue Tracy. His angel. He tried to reach out but he couldn't even feel his hands now, the wounds inflicted having long since numbed his nerves. “Tracy.” A fierce light burned his eyes, and as the salty tears stung them he forced them open to watch as the flame from the lighter ran over the outline of a female form. It started low at her slender smooth legs, working up to a tight little pair of denim shorts hugging her buttocks. The light passed over her exposed midriff, and Michael watched as she flinched when the flame came too close. Next it lit up her buxom chest, her pushed up cleavage appearing to heave quicker as the flame danced before them. Then he saw her face. Only for a moment, but he saw her. Her delicate jawline. The rouge of her soft lips. Her button nose. A flash of her sparkling eyes, glimmering with tears. With fear. Then darkness again. “No! No! N-YAAAAAARGHH!!” This time the pain knocked the pause into her. Every hitch of her breath squeezed a little more life from Sloan's blackened soul. When she started sobbing he tried again to move and still couldn't shake the paralytic grip on his nerves. He felt beaten. He had tried to convince Von Kelsig he was innocent, but he knew the giant wouldn't give up now. He had done this to Tracy, and all because he couldn't leave Gloria without taking a souvenir. At the time it filled him with a sense of power so deep and erotic he imagined himself a God. Now that thrill, that lust had cost him the only thing in life he ever truly loved. The click and swish of the knife blade being flicked open was the last straw. As Tracy's breathing became frantic and she readied herself to scream again he steeled himself for the end. “Alright,” he whispered. She was plan B through Z, had it not worked he wouldn't have known where to turn next. After seeing him smile his way through the physical torture he didn’t think the psycho would ever crack. He too prepared himself for what was to come. “Alright what?” Sloan coughed, blood most likely, before responding, “What you want to know.” No one moved. Barely a breath uttered. Only the dancing flame, mocking Sloan, that bobbed and waved before him. “Go on.” Michael bit his lip and stared at the empty space above the amber glow around the giant's mouth, into two glowing white spots that flicked back and forth, gazing back at his own lighted face. “This is between you and me, no one else.” Von Kelsig flicked the lid back over on the Zippo and nudged the darkness to his left. Understand?” Christian Daniels mumbled something under his breath. Michael listened as Tracy's breathing became erratic, and she whispered her protest as the Biker pulled her away towards the door. It screeched as it opened, a sliver of light spilling into the room interrupted by two shadows before it slammed shut again. Now they were alone. Two monsters cloaked in darkness, the black void around them filled with so much tension that it stifled their breath. Like a switch flicking in his head, Michael was ready. For Tracy's sake. As long as she was safe it didn't matter what happened to him. He could take it. His voice took an ominous tone, as if it were filling the entire room. Von Kelsig had to fight every instinct in his body not to check behind him. “So what do you want to know?” “Did you kill her?” There was a soft laugh from the corner, “You’ll have to be more specific than that. Who?” “Don't act the cunt,” the giant replied, hinting at Tracy. “Gloria. Did you have anything to do with her death?” “Yes.” He was prepared for that answer. From the start he had been building to this point where he would find out the truth, he just didn't bank on it coming so quickly and...so proudly. This wasn't a feud between wrestlers on a television show any more. This was beyond personal. This was inhuman. He wasn't dealing with a man any more. “Just like that?” Sloan chuckled, “What? Can't handle the truth now?” He couldn’t hold back. Grasping around in the darkness he finally found Sloan’s face and sunk his fingers into the cheeks, digging his nails into the wounds and struggling to hold on as Michael replied with a sinister high-pitched giggle. He felt soiled just touching his skin. “No...what I can't handle is how fucking proud you sound right now.” Dejected, the giant released his grip on Michael's face and wiped his fingers against his thigh. Sloan took a breath, his face filling with a biting pain once more, only now his psychosis was numbing the hurt for him. Von Kelsig's head was filling with hundreds of questions at once, but he could only voice them at their most common base. “What happened?” Michael snorted air from his nostrils, his pride inflating at the sound of Von Kelsig breaking down piece by piece. “You and the drink, old pal, don’t mix well. Remember when we met up before Colossus? You drank and blubbered about your cheating skank and I ate up every word you had to say. It wasn’t hard to find them, or even find time to take care of them. When I peered deep into her eyes I saw the fear; not for her safety...but for that of the life inside her. I'm no doctor, but I know that glow when I see it. Like fire to a moth.” Von Kelsig was frozen with horror. Suddenly he felt all too vulnerable and he wished he had left the light on. He had just stumbled into Michael's realm. The dark of night. The unknown. He was face to face with the monster under the bed. “Usually I’m prepared for these things, but for some reason that night I wasn't. My knife, I hadn’t cleaned or sharpened it for some time. Have you ever tried splitting a rib cage with dull instruments? It isn’t easy let me tell you.” He stuttered, more from disbelief than nerves, “w..wh...why?” “Because I could, because I wanted to, and because I needed something to do before Colossus.” “You can't be serious. No way. You're fucking sick but...” “Oh come on, don’t act so surprised. All the whispers and the little rumours floating around backstage, you didn’t think they were all made up did you?” “How the hell could PRIME hire someone like you?” Sloan laughed. “The board of PRIME is filled with simpletons too blinded by greed to see the real picture. From Blaine Blair to Lisa Tyler to Devin Shakur. They want ratings, they want money. All else be damned. I could’ve slaughtered half a country for all they care; as long as their pockets were stuffed they were happy.” The giant wanted desperately to stop talking, but he was hypnotized. Do you honestly think I would kill for the sake of some fictional feud? He was an over-the-hill professional wrestler struggling to live a life of his own, and now he was in over his head. But still he couldn't bring himself to stop. “How many? How many innocent people have you murdered? How many lives have you ruined?” “Don’t be so melodramatic. Ruined? The people I killed didn’t have lives worth living. They needed to die and they wanted to die, I just facilitated the process.” Von Kelsig asked again, “How many?” Michael Sloan sighed, “I don’t know. I lost count years ago. That’s like asking how many stars there are in the sky. You’d need more hands and feet to count I’d say. The children are my favourite. The way they pop like blisters. You press and squeeze and when the blood and the pus comes out there’s no greater, or tighter, feeling in the world. You'll never know the joy until you try it for yourself.” Hot bile splashed the back of his throat, and Michael grinned at the sound of the giant choking back the vomit. They sat in silence for a few minutes before Von Kelsig found it within himself to continue. He didn't want to think about that last comment, but he wanted to know everything now. He had to know everything now. This was no longer just about his family. “You enjoy it? You enjoy killing these people? Killing Gloria?” Sloan pondered for a moment. “I am who I am...wouldn’t change it for the world. I’ve lived a life people can barely even imagine. I’ve done things you can't even comprehend. I’ve seen things you wouldn’t believe. I've stared into the eyes of Satan and watched him turn away. I've spat in the face of God. I am what no one else can be, and in the end I win. I always win Von Kelsig.” “And just how the fuck do you figure that?” “Because in the end I will have something that you will never know again in your life. True happiness.” That was a slap in the face that Von Kelsig couldn't take. He couldn’t refute Michael's claim. He struggled his entire life to find true happiness, and when he found it Michael Sloan took it away as though he were the master of fate and destiny. He would never find that kind of happiness again, but he could also ensure that Michael never felt it again. “You might be right Sloan, I won't find happiness. Not after this. That make you happy? That what you want to hear? That you took so much from me, that you got to sit there with a fucking smile on your face and tell me how you ruined my fucking life? You're right, I'll never find the kind of happiness I had with Gloria again...” It was then that the muffled cry broke through the silence, as though it had been birthed from the void. It quickly escalated into frantic whimpering, snivelling cries fraught with tears and hopelessness and heartache. Then the faint square of light to Michael's right became a blinding torch in his face and he had to turn away to let his eyes adjust. Sloan glanced back to see sunlight pouring into the room. He hadn't been unconscious for as long as he'd thought. The cover of night was nothing more than a ruse, orchestrated in part by the man holding the light blocking curtain in his hand, Christian Daniels. The Biker stared in silent disgust at Sloan, but it was the weeping figure at his feet that put the fear of God into him as Von Kelsig finished his sentence. “...but judging by her tears Michael, neither will you.” The revelation of Michael's true identity had disturbed Von Kelsig on a whole other level. But the look on the psycho's face when he realized the love of his life had been party to the confession the entire time offered the giant a grain of solace in a storm of shit. Michael's voice wavered and for the first time in his life he knew fear. A gripping fear of complete failure, of foolishness in the face of genius, of the unknown and most importantly, fear of the loss of the only thing precious in his life. “Tracy...” She never took her hands away from her face, only moving them up from her mouth and through tears and saliva and mucus asking, “is it true?” He didn't know what to say and as he panicked words came spilling out in a futile bid to try and patch things up. He had a better chance of patching up the Grand Canyon with a band-aid. “Baby, I’ve done wrong. It's...I’ve lied to you, yes.” She sprang up quickly, startling him. “But I’ve never lied about loving you! Tracy listen to me!” There was nothing he could do. Through clenched teeth and eyes of fire she plucked the diamond ring from her finger and hurled the band at her husband with a scream of hatred. She continued to shriek and cry, struggling to stand on her own two legs as she bolted out of the room, her world thrown into chaos. Daniels followed her with a nod to Von Kelsig, who stared Sloan over with a happy smile etched across his face. Once more they were left in silence. Michael glanced down for the first time since he had collapsed earlier and found that he was sitting upright, and had been bound to his chair by cable ties that bit into his flesh. That meant the giant could confidently kneel down in front of the monster and express his satisfaction. “You know...the more I think about it, things between me and Gloria were great, but we both knew it wasn't going to work out. She hung in there for me, she owed me as much after I-...well that doesn't matter. Point is I let her go, and only when I found out I could've been a daddy did I go over the edge. You're to blame for that Michael, and I'm going to have to live my life knowing that, but after seeing that? Well, things won't be so unbearable for me now that I know you've lost just as much as I have. I've heard things today that no man should ever have to know, and there's only one way to deal with something like that...” Michael looked up, his eyes wet. Then his bottom lip trembled and Von Kelsig felt power surge from the floor all the way up his spine and spread a tingling feeling through his brain before he shivered. He had gotten equal, and now he was going to win outright. Sloan laid his head back, ready to welcome his fate. “Go on then, finish it. Kill me.” “Kill you? Are you mad?!” Von Kelsig laughed, “That'd be a reprieve for you. Death is the only reward for a man who's done the things that you have. There's no chance you're ever going to get that reward, Sloan. No, you're going to live. You're going to do life. Multiple times and maybe forever. Every single second of every single day of the rest of your life you're going to remember this moment. You're going to remember the pain and anguish and the hurt you made me feel and you are going to suffer it like no one has suffered it before. Even if you forgive yourself, and hopefully you won’t...I know something that will never leave you for as long as you live, and that mean that I win, Michael. I win.” A single tear rolled down Sloan's cheek, and he bowed his head in defeat. Moments later Christian Daniels re-entered the room carrying rolls of chains, a black hood and a body bag. “Y'ready?” “Yeah. Time to flush this piece of shit away...and get on with my life.” ---- Several weeks later The voices sounded like they were coming from all around him, haunting him in the stifling humidity and a miasma of pungent and sickening smells that had been rotting for longer than anyone cared to remember. Even the rats feared to tread where the ghosts of men lay, in this place where humanity was a distant memory and survival was only temporary. Of the minds that entered this unholy place very few had remained intact, and only by remembering the world outside of walls covered in bloody scratch marks. Even then those men would eventually beg for their release of this mortal coil and wind up reverting back to a primeval way of life that no human eye should ever have to look upon. In the darkest, furthest reaches of this living hell there lay shaking a foetal figure. In silence and darkness he had suffered since that fateful day when his world came crashing down upon him, and now he lay amongst the rubble begging for his end. Every so often he would stop to listen to the incessant wailing of the insane around him to drown out his own thoughts. Snatches of some language he didn't recognize, or couldn't decipher, scared him. He had been banished to a place where all he had left was the pain and suffering and the memory of losing everything he ever held dear. Sometimes, he thought he could understand what the voices were saying, and imagined them to be tormenting him. Sometimes he screamed back, and though he heard those other voices being silenced by an unknown hand, his was allowed to echo through the night unabated. He threw himself at unseen walls and laughed maniacally at nothing in particular. He beat the floor with his feet and did things to himself with his own hands to reassure himself he was alive. And always, throughout the everlasting limbo of what his existence had become, always he clutched in his gnarled fist a band of diamond and gold, to remind him of why he was here, and more importantly...who had put him here.
