This Is Who They Are

Date: Varied He sits in clothes covering his body, the scene around him reminding him of hell. The helmet on his head seems to be present only because it's legally required. He doubts that this simple piece of thick plastic could save him from any real danger. Especially not in a place like this. A simple trip could result in a broken neck, or worse. People had burns on their arms from when they forgot to wore their protective gear, or chose not to wear it. They had to wear it - covering their arms, their limbs, their hands, in a place were things were hot enough to melt, hot enough to eat through skin, hot enough to eat through bone. He was in hell, because that was what this entire place was. Fire and darkness and suffering because somebody else said that it was supposed to happen. Sure, it paid well, and it was something that he could do well, but it still was a type of underworld. It was, like the real hell, a necessary place for the cogs of existence or society to keep going. For the first year, he wasn't even concerned that he was working in hell. The duties changed every day he was at work, and it was a two-week rotation, so he could keep doing something new to the point where it wouldn't get old. But hell is not just a way for the wicked to be punished. It was also for the apathetic to get what they wanted. And due to his continued employment, he found apathy even in the change. For the rotation became a set aspect of his job, and he knew every job he would have, and on what day, for three months in advance. He stopped caring, and just did the job. He lost his passion, and so he had to find something else about which he was passionate. And so he came to this business of entertainment, this business to find a home in Detroit, in Philadelphia, and in Canada, all within a reasonable drive from his home. It became his only passion after a while, and instead of finding a family and children, he found the road, in pursuit of the next show, and the next meal, and the next paycheck. Somehow, even though this could become repetitive, it never became boring. This is who Jack Conway is. When the riots started, he was in school. He couldn't understand why there was such a commotion about it. Blacks, hispanics, Asians, even white kids, all got beaten by cops in LA. This was no different - a black man mouthed off to the police, and instead of the long arm of the law, the black guy got the hard-toed boot. But somehow, because this black man was rich, because this black man was famous, it got publicized, and an entire group's rage about their plight raged on to the streets. He knew that he should have been a part of it. There were a lot of people fighting each other over race before the riots, and now, he got threatened just because he was Hispanic. He got threatened because he was born to the wrong parents, he was expected to be friends with certain people because he was born to the right parents, and he just wanted to get away from it all. The whole time that he heard slurs tossed around, he wanted to lash out, and a few times he did. But eventually, he decided that he couldn't handle it any more. He decided to just find something that didn't have any connection to any of it. He found himself in a place that joined the entire world, and he dreamt of a day of unity, of hope, of peace. He had wood and water, and it was the way that he could let all of it go. He went to the sand, he went to the waves, and he went to find hope. Hope of a better time. His journey to the wrestling industry was one of following a surfer friend to a training session one winter where the waves were dead and the water was colder than usual. He showed a natural prowess for it, and began to train. He met his current ally at a special training session, and they somehow forged a bond. This is who Luis Gallegos is. These two men are the most unlikely of friends, but they are men that have come through hardship to do what they love. They have been locked in cages that nobody could see, that none could perceive, and another such cage, or even two of them, is not going to come between that friendship. They will do what they can to win the Dual Halo, but they will not sacrifice their alliance. They are The Union, and Culture Shock seems to be a show name made for them. They fight, and they win. This is who they are.
