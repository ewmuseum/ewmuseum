Things Fall Apart (ReV 111, JITC Round One vs. El Rayo Azul VI)

13 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/13/06 Nova’s house, Parts Unknown, 4/20/94 “You ready for this? You ready to see this monument to technological badassery? This veritable Atlas who shall surely carry me, as though I were the world resting on its back, to the new era of electronic prosperity? This…” “Chris, come on with it already. Goddamn.” An irritated frown creases the face of a much younger, baby-faced Nova, who stands in front of his friend, arms outstretched as he milks the anticipatory moment for all it’s worth. “Alright, Jesus. You got somewhere to be or something? A hot date? Yeah, that must be it. You can’t stay over very long and smoke my weed and check out what I’ve got to show you because you’re…hehehe…you’re going on a date…ah, hehehe…with…with…the CAPTAIN OF THE CHEERLEADING TEAHHHH AHAHAHAHA!!!” “Alright, point taken, damn,” the other boy says, shoulders slumping in teenage self-loathing as the future Rising Star continues to howl at his joke. “Ahhh, okay,” Nova says, wiping an eye, “now if you’re done rushing me, I’ll show it to you.” Nova disappears behind his desk and retrieves a heavy black bag, setting it gingerly in his desk chair. Unzipping it as though he were retrieving the Holy Grail from inside, Nova slowly reaches his hands in and pulls out a clunky piece of machinery, placing it on his desk and turning proudly to catch the reaction on his friend’s face. “Whoooooooooooaaaaahhh,” his friend mutters, the elongation both a sign of his awe and how much weed has been smoked in this room so far during the stoner holiday. “Yeah, man…it’s called a laptop computer,” Nova explains, “and it’s fucking ri-di-cu-lous. I can fold this screen up…like so…and press the power button…like so…and as long as there’s a place to plug its battery in, I can take this computer with me wherever I go! It’s fucking portable, d00d!” The Windows 3.1 start-up window appears on-screen, and the boys stare on, entranced. “Dude, let me try…,” Nova’s friend mumbles, and his arm stretches forward towards the machine, knocking over a bottle of Shasta Black Cherry cola onto the keyboard. “Okay,” Nova replies warily, “but please be care-AHHH!! ZACH, WHAT HAVE YOU DONE?!” The Windows screen shorts out, and a small POOF! of smoke burps out of the machine’s interior as systems fail. Nova can only stare for a moment, his jaw dropping down to Hell for a moment and dipping up some magma like a ladle. “Dude…dude, I’m…it was…,” Zach stammers, but Nova doesn’t hear him, his eyes still locked on the expensive drink coaster/desk ornament sitting where his laptop used to be. “Chris!” comes a deeper voice from the hallway outside, “I’m coming up! Is your laptop on? I want to play that Wolfenstein game again!” “Shit, it’s my dad!” Nova hisses, slapping his friend upside the head, “what the fuck are we gonna do? He’ll kill me and maim you if he finds out what happened!” A light bulb suddenly comes on above Nova’s head. No, really, it was getting dark, Zach turned on the overhead bulb. Knock, knock. The door creaks open, and Nova’s foster father steps inside, pushing aside thick long strands of grey hair as he grins with anticipation. “Time to kill some Nazis. Is it turned on? Hey, Zach.” “H-h-hey, Mr. Vega,” Zach replies, his skin white. “Aww, sorry, Dad,” Nova says, stepping in front of the machine quickly, “I’m lettin’ ‘er rest for a while. Hey, speaking of a little R & R, today is Four-Twenty, you know.” “I know it,” Nova’s father says, scratching his beard, “but I didn’t manage to grab up any weed before I came home.” Grinning from ear-to-ear, Nova holds out his hand, unfurling a sack of the straight-up bubonic fluffy nuggets. “I gotcha, Dad. I gotcha. Let’s go downstairs and twist up a fatty.” His attention diverted, Mr. Vega turns to walk downstairs and the boys quickly follow him. Nova’s Hotel Room, Sacramento, CA, 11/13/06 He learned long ago that leaving drinks next to the computer can only produce epic disaster, but that doesn’t stop Nova tonight as he wields a bottle of Wild Turkey 101 in one hand and clicks furiously on a computer mouse with the other as a cigarette burns in the ashtray on the desk. He’s naked. It’s one of those nights. Bookings had been released a few hours prior for PRIME’s ReVolution 111, and all the rumors Nova had heard from whispers backstage had been instantly realized in front of his eyes as the magic of high-speed internet drove the news home like a stake to the heart. OPENING CONTEST: EL RAYO AZUL VI VS. NOVA “Mother-fucking cock-sucker piece of…goddammit!” As the paint peels away from the walls, Nova kicks the front right leg of the desk as hard as he can… “AAAAAHHHGGH!!” …but now he’s just pissed-off with a slight limp. That’s honestly the worst, isn’t it? When you’re so pissed you have to hit something, but it really hurts, so it just turns out to be gasoline for your bonfire of rage? Anyone else ever feel that way? Nova takes a swig from the bottle and slams it down on the desk. He grabs the cigarette out of the ash tray and takes a drag before flinging it across the room. It bounces off the wall in a shower of sparks and lands harmlessly on the floor, burning a small hole in the crappy mass-manufactured carpet print and giving it character. The Rising Star pushes back from the desk, a small trail of blood following his foot across the floor. His head falls forward into his hands and his fingers push up into his thick mane of blonde hair. His body shakes as he sits hunched-over, perhaps because the blood coursing through his veins is boiling. Wild Turkey’s been known to turn men into monsters, but Nova’s no lightweight, and he knows as well as anyone else that alcohol only brings out what deep inside you’re already feeling. And the Rising Star, watching his dreams fall apart like turkey from a Crock Pot, is feeling very, very angry. Hands shaking, he reaches for the herb-sack and rolling papers on the desk. Grabbing them, he tries to pull a few buds out to break up, but he’s still trembling so hard that he drops them, eliciting more curses from the former Universal Title contender. Things Fall Apart. That had been the name of a book Nova read in high school about Africa. It seems so vivid to the Rising Star in this moment when he is seemingly surrounded by blown opportunities. Taking a deep breath, Nova concentrates on the task at hand. He manages to roll the joint at last and sets it on the desk-top while he opts out of the straight-from-the-bottle strategy and into the Wild Turkey a la Rocks camp. He takes a sip from the glass and sparks the joint, exhaling a thick cloud of smoke. The Rising Star rubs his temples and takes another long, deep breath. For now, he’ll bottle up the rage and let ‘er ride. Come Wednesday, he’ll uncork it.
