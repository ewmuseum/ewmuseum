The Depraved and Rotten Road to Alabama

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 9-10 "I'll see you around," Rhett said to DUI as he walked off. Quietly he lit a cigarette. He wasn't very keen on doing drugs at the workplace but fuck, he won... might as well party. Even if it's just himself. The clank of his heels hitting the ground were haunting the empty walls. The backstage area during an event at a certain point of the night always looks like a barren wasteland, a place with once bustling commerce. Now red Solo plastic cups littered the floor like relics in a pyramid. The fact of the matter was that Locke wasn't much in to sticking around for the Universal Title Match. Disrespectful? A little bit. Rhett wasn't in PRIME for the camaraderie. He was there for restoration... a revival of sorts. Just as he reached the door to the parking lot, The Albino was stopped by a rather hippy looking fellow holding a mop. "Hey, Rhett Locke." Rhett turned, stopping to speak to whomever caught him. The man seemed Irish in descent, dreadlocked red hair and a red goatee. Otherwise he was the typical janitor with his cliche grey jumpsuit. Rhett seemed in an unnatural hurry and didn't answer quite quickly. So the janitor continued. He whispered what he really had to say, "Do you dose man? I watched you for a looong time," the Janitor shook his finger at Rhett and grinned, "...and I always thought you were a doser." Dosing. Did every drug lover recognize each other? Hardly. More often than not though, people that dose are more likely to feel like they have a groove to the whole "Living On The Planet Earth" thing and they have everything figured out. Rhett always had a theory that people who have a "holier than thou" perspective on life were naturally drawn to acid because it makes you feel like you know every minute detail of every mundane aspect of everything. It validated their opinions on themselves already. "What's your name?" Rhett asked the apparent hipster. The janitor grinned and started chuckling a little bit, "Rob man. It's on my uhh... my badge here." Two pale (though not nearly as pale as Rhett's) digits pointed to the badge on his jumpsuit. True. "Yeah, I dose. How much do you charge?" Rob clapped his hands and started laughing, "I knew it man. I fuckin' kneeew you fuckin' dosed I fuckin' knew it." Rhett seemed slightly annoyed, "How much? What the fuck... man?" Rob waved his hands in a stopping motion. "Well... dude, where are you going man? Where are you headed?" Rhett understood now. This guy was a leech. Rob was just a dickhead striking up conversation and then hitching a ride. Locke shrugged his shoulders in a "fuck it-esque" motion and turned to walk off. Then Rob said something that peaked The Underdogs interest: "I've got a briefcase full. Where you headed man?" Now to some people this might seem like a surface statement. Rhett had a way of reading between the lines. "Your briefcase. Is it on you?" Rob frowned, "Naw man... we'd have to pick it up. But if you have a car it's on the way man." Rhett sighed, it seemed like everything in the fucking world had a catch. "Well do you HAVE a fucking briefcase or not?" "I HAVE A FUCKING BRIEF_CASE." "...Where's it at?" "On the way man... on the fucking way to the interstate highway." Rhett sighed once more, throwing the cigarette butt to the ground. Rob looked at that action with disdain as any self-respecting janitor would. "Let's go." ------------------------------------ "So," Rob said in an inhale simultaneously passing the joint to Rhett. "Where exactly are you going, man?" Rob's curiosity seemed to come natural as though he had no idea what the concept of asking too many questions was. Though with Rhett, unless you started breaking through his surface, he'd answer anything. "Alabama man." Rhett said, coughing and offering the little cigarette back to the janitor. "Cool. Gonna stop through Mississippi?" Locke grinned, "Yep." "Cool. Care to drop me off there?" "Nope." "Cool." Rob replied. He finished the roach and threw it in an empty soda can. "So, who knew Rhett Locke drives a fucking red Civic man?" Bursting out into high-induced laughter, Rob took a second to compose himself. Rhett replied, "It's a rental car. What the fuck is your last name anyway, Rob?" Rob took a moment to stop laughing enough to reply, "Nicholson." "Rob Nicholson?" "Yeah." Rhett laughed a little, "Alright." Rob pointed to a road off to the right, "Hey, pull down this road. Briefcase is this way." Rhett followed his directions well. The truth was he was very high when he pulled down that road in the rented Civic. So high he forgot the name of the street. He parked in the cul-de-sac. Rob stepped out and started jogging toward a house. "I'll be back real fast. Stay put." Rhett sat for a moment. Sure, he would just chill. No problem. The neighborhood was seedy but Locke lived somewhere even seedier so there was nothing to worry about. It was a fine thing to just recline in the seat with the air condition hitting you directly in the face. He was a zen like state until he heard gun shots. What would happen next was a bit of an adrenaline rushed blur for Locke. Rob Nicholson the Janitor came barrelling out of the house like a demon from a hellgate, briefcase and a gun in his hand. "GO! GO!" "What the fuck!?! WHAT THE FUCK!?!" Rhett was exasperated. "GOD DAMNIT YOU BASTARD, GO!" Nicholson yelled back. Locke nearly fishtailed the vehicle in place, slinging it in drive and going full blast out of the road and back on the Highway. Everything seemed clear. "Phew!" Rob sighed. "Intense shit man! The interstate's right up here." Rhett sat there in a quiet shock for a moment, just driving. Then his fist slammed down on the horn. It was a relentless, awful sound for three seconds. "No! No! Fuck that! Did you kill someone!? Am I a fucking... Rob... if I'm a fucking accomplice to murder, I swear I'll kill you." "RELAX! I didn't kill anyone. Robbed? ...Yeah. I robbed them. But of drugs. So who can they report it to? We're in the clear. You should just chill." Rob snapped open the briefcase. It was a postman's dream... or worst nightmare. Stamp after stamp after stamp of acid on sheet after sheet after sheet. "Want a drop?" "What the fuck do you mean if I want a drop? You just fucking robbed, what I assume to be a god damn drug dealer and you want me to take this?" Without having to answer a single question, Rob's offer was accepted. Rhett tore off four stamps and placed them on his tongue. Nicholson laughed, "That's the spirit," and did three himself. "I say we go apeshit." Rhett shrugged, "Might as well. It's a long way to Mississippi." ------------------------------------ A few hours later Rhett had all but forgotten about Nicholson robbing the dealer and was peaking in a very pleasant and exciting fashion. "No Quarter" by Led Zeppelin was blaring through the stock Civic speakers and the road resembled a snake. It slithered in front of the vehicle, pulsing prismatic rainbows everytime John Bonham's stick crashed against a cymbal. He smiled, this was cool. Rob, meanwhile, was having a god awful time. Acid had a way of making someone dwell on any particular thought. Even bad ones and you can't escape them. Trying to get away from the thought only makes for a frustrating, upsetting endeavor. Rhett looked over at Rob, who was fetal in the seat and rather unhappy with the whole thing. "You have to face your demons Robert. Robert, face your demons. You can't just fucking... you know... not face them 'cause the acid... you know, you don't escape the uhm..." Rob looked at Rhett, disgusted. "You have no IDEA what's happening to me!" Rhett's words suddenly became very direct. "You're a panicked asshole who stole drugs from very real people who will hurt you if they find you." Rob didn't seem willing to accept it. He held his hands over his ears and closed his eyes, wincing in imaginary pain. Too bad for Nicholson, Rhett had made it his purpose now to make him face his fears. "Robert. Look you silly bastard. You know, life man... life's crazy, you know that. Sometimes you do some very retarded shit. Sometimes you just do that man. You just do. Now, if you walk around this trip in a state of denial you're going to have a horrible time. If you accept the fact that you're the dick who stole this shit and you're going to have to live with it... fuck man, you know, we're going to be fucked up for quite a bit longer and you'll have great fun." "Good point." Rob said. He seemed to be calming down. "Can we stop at a store? For a cigarillo? I think I can roll." The rest of the car ride until they reached the store took place in silence but for the blaring of Jimmy Hendrix's cover of "All Along The Watchtower." At the store it was a relatively quick trip in and out for Nicholson, who seemed to be calming down already. Sometimes, when you're in a bad state of mind it's best if you don't trip at all. However, Nicholson ran out of the store and motioned for Rhett to come in. Rhett stepped out. Upon walking in the store, Nicholson showed him what seemed to be a very free-spirited chick. She was blonde and fairly attractive. "She wants to ride to Alabama man. Her name is Mona. And hey, I wanna go to Alabama too dude... look at her, she's beautiful. I told her we're crab fishermen." "Crab Fishermen?" "Yeah. I'll introduce you." Rob walked up to her and gestured to Rhett. "This is Rhett Locke." "Rhett Locke? That's a wrestler, not a crab fisherman." "Well, you see, I crabfish during my off time." Rhett said, going along with it. "So, you're an albino?" She butted in. Rhett nodded his head. "I need a ride to Albama... you headed that way?" Rhett finally offered, "You know I can't take either of you back. I can drop you off on the way there... but once you get to Alabama, you're fucked on a ride home. I'm stating it now." "It's cool." Rob and Mona said in unison. Mona added, "I don't have any gas money but I've got another white girl with me, too." Rhett read between the lines. He snorted in out of a natural reaction he most likely didn't even notice. "Okay, let's go." Mona got in the backseat, Rob in the front. On the road, Mona was working out the coke like a true fiend can. Her hair tied back, a credit card in her hand and a CD-R case for a surface. She offered Rhett two lines. He inhaled them with the straw he'd used for a very long time. He had started calling it lucky. Locke drug a pale finger across where his lines used to sit, rubbing the excess that stuck to his fingertip on his gums and teeth. Thinking about it, it wasn't as clean as the image portrays. "I think," Mona offered, who looked like she had trapped the devil in those suggestive and green eyes. "We should get a hotel." ------------------------------------ Mona checked them into the Hotel Room (Room 419 at the Jamison Inn at the state line of Mississippi) things were going fine. Rob Nicholson, Rhett Locke and Mona Porter were dosed out of their minds. Rhett was running around in a demon mask, Mona was chasing him trying to hug him and Rob was standing in the corner giggling and laughing at the two. "Mona, I really like you," Rob said. His eyes were practically bugging out of his head. Mona laughed, "I really like Rhett." Rhett threw in, "I really like Satan." Rhett fell on the ground giggling. Apparently they had not yet peaked on the second wave. Rob glanced at Mona, "I'm serious. I'm really into you." Mona stopped what she was doing. The problem with assholes is that they always ruin a good time. Always. Mona shrugged, the poor honest bitch. "I'm not really into you though." Rhett stayed silent. The demon mask was still on his face. It was an old fashioned, rubbery halloween demon mask... horns and all. Rob sighed, "Why the fuck not?" "...Because you come off like a liar." Rob looked at Mona, then looked at Rhett. Locke took notice that a certain look came over Nicholson's eyes. Something a bit primal, a bit sadistic. Then his eyes quickly welled up, like an outpouring was to begin. He looked at Rhett, "I killed that dealer Rhett. I killed him. He was a 21 year old kid and I fucking killed him. And I'm sorry dude, he owed me money. He owed me money, see... and so I had to kill him because he didn't have money. I took his acid and he drew the gun... he drew it first, man. I just... shot." There was a quiet in the room that seemed cast by wizards or sorcerors or some other sort of fucked up magic using bastard. A silence so gloomy that it could only impend sinister dealings. Finally, it was Mona that said something. "You have to turn yourself in." Rob jumped up and pointed at her, "Shut your fucking mouth bitch." Mona insisted, "Turn yourself in." Nicholson became infuriated and drew the gun and pointed it at her. Rhett still didn't move or speak. "You will shut the fuck up. And I am not going to jail." Rhett glanced at him, finally saying something. "Man, you're fucked up and weird. You looked like a hippy, you mentioned dosing and I thought you were cool. You're not a hippy, you're a piece of shit. A fucking janitor who wants to hustle big time drug dealers and then kills them. Basically." "No! No! Fuck that!" "I'm fucking leaving." Rob was being childish, "Fuck you Rhett you're going to turn me in. But if you do it I sware to God I'll take you down with me" Locke yelled at him, "Are you a god damned idiot!?! Has this acid made us all a bunch of raving lunatics, or just you!? You're a fucking moron and I'm leaving." Mona was about to say something but Rhett grabbed her hand. "She's coming to. Mona, you're not turning him in and Rob you are not shoo--" BAM! Rhett ducked out of a natural reaction. Mona's body fell limp. He looked at his arm. Blood and other matter covered it. He looked at Mona. She'd been shot in the head. Rhett stared at Rob, fear sweeping across his face. He was peaking on the acid. He thought Rob was shaking but he couldn't tell for sure. Now it looked like Rob was wearing the devil mask. Or was he the devil? "I didn't mean to... It was an accident." Time seemed to slow down as the Devil said that. Damn, the devil looks insane. "A Lot like the mask I'm wearing..." Rob was confused, "What?" Mona's dead. The pacing of Time increased back to normal. "...Fuck!" Rhett burst through the door and outside. Luckily he was parked almost right outside the door. Rob ran out. He looked like shit. "Dude you have to help me!!" Rhett was already in the car. The adrenaline was overpowering his brain. His hands shook violently before he could crank the car. Nicholson stumbled outside and pointed the gun. Locke threw the car in drive. Rob fired. He was so fucked up he missed the car. Rhett knew then that it must've been an accident that he shot Mona. But that didn't matter now. As he drove away, Rhett figured that he would make it out of this clean. Should his name come up, Rhett figured it wouldn't matter. They'd see the gun, run it. It'd match Rob's fingerprints. They'd see the briefcase full of stamps and assume it was a drug dealer gone insane on his own drugs. It was, basically. So with his head full of acid and his mouth sewn shut, Rhett drove to Alabama. No one would hear about this. No one.
