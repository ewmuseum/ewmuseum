Date: July 10, 2009 "God damnit..." A voice is muffled from behind an old wooden bedroom door. Outside a cat stares curiously in the direction, just a few mere feet away from it. The expression CATastrophe's face shows that he's probably not entirely sure if there is a person on the other side or if the door itself is talking. "Where in the hell did I put that top?!" Hearing it once again the curvaceous calico puts its paws up on the door and begins scratching vigorously. The paint was already chipped away from the area, covered with deep scratch marks into light brown wood. Ripping and splintering of wood now muted the frantic sounds of searching on the inside. Drawers opening and closing in a fever pitch, the scene inside surely resembled that of a man trapped inside a glass phone booth of dollar bills blowing around like a cyclone. Reaching, Scrambling, Desperate. How can you call it a lifestyle if you don't live your life in style? How can you call it a lifestyle if you don't life your life in style? "Is that the phone?!" She screams from the other side of the door. Soon enough it's jolted open, leaving the rather plump feline to go tumbling forward into the room. So much for always landing on his feet. Stepping over him was Elise Aresel Nguyen, dressed with a pair of designer jeans and a black tank top. Her body was certainly rocking as she jogged barefoot into the living area where the television still stayed on the Sex And The City Season 2 DVD Menu. "Where are you?!" Elise proclaims out loud as she shoves away trash from the coffee table and even pulls up the couch cushions. A gumshoe she is not. With the cushions now scattered across the living room she takes a step back in frustration before screaming out in pain. "What the...." Looking down at the ground she sees the object of her desire, under her foot surely next to a bruise of matching shape and size. "Hello?" Elise answers confused as she steps over the cushions back towards 'where the magic happens.' Admittedly there hasn't been a whole lot of magic lately, but that's by choice. Obviously, now that she's a big superstar there's going to be lots of paparazzi around. She has to be more selective of who she brings back to the palace. If it's just anyone, she'll be on the cover of every gossip mag for sure. Actually... that's not such a bad idea... "Emily! I haven't heard from you in forever... are you still with Hawaiian Tropics?" She asks with an incredibly fake voice. You can't tell from her infliction how much she hates talking to this woman, but it's certainly obvious from her facial expression. They don't match in the slightest. CATastrophe bolts out of Elise's path as fast as a football shaped cat with tiny legs can. Here is your hint: It's not very fast... but it's hilarious. "So you're a fashion model now?" Elise rolls her eyes mocking her own fake excitement, "That's fantastic!" Looking around Elise pulls the phone away from her ear and places it on the nightstand as she pull open drawer after drawer after drawer of expensive, top-of-the-line designer clothing. Yanking out a small gray baby tee she tosses it into a suitcase next. Grabbing the phone back up and putting it next to her ear, she finds out she just missed about 45 seconds of rambling. Trying the zip the luggage shut, she finds that it can't quite hold as much as she wants to bring. That's become pretty typical now that she's on the road. "Mark Elling?!" She answers stopping in her tracks, "I haven't heard that name in years." Now she's paying attention. "I am going to be in New York City for work." She answers, "Surely you don't think..." She's obviously cut off as she jumps up into the air and lands on the luggage, using her body to hold it down while she tries to zip it shut. "It's a pretty big city." Elise answers in what might be the understatement of the day, "There's no way he finds me..." The zipper begins to move. Score one for overloading! "What ever happened to him?" Elise paused for a moment and thought to herself. The final bell of Wall Street signaled the end of the week for a group of some of the richest men in America. As stocks soared, one man in-particular had a grin from ear to ear. His close cut light brown hair blew in the summer breeze as he walked down the steps of the stock market. He had just made $65,000 in his biggest day ever. Wearing what he would now refer to as his lucky navy blue suit along with matching colored tie, he couldn't help but be taken in by his surroundings. Who knew he'd ever be standing in the middle of the greatest city on Earth on such a beautiful day? It was such a far cry from growing up outside of Detroit, Michigan to a middle income family who made their living off the automotive industry. A family of factory workers who busted their ass so that some day their child could get a good education at a private school before going to Yale on a full-ride scholarship. Just a few years later here he was... rich beyond his wildest dream, one of the fastest rising young stock brokers in the world. He was making his father proud, who to this day still insisted on working in the same factory he'd worked in for 25 years. Shunning the retirement he could easily take due to his son's succeeds. The Elling men were certainly never ones to take handouts. This particular Friday felt different though, it was something in the air. Today started out great, but it was only going to get better from here. He was going to go home and kick back for a few hours before hitting the shower and going for a night on the town with his buddies Doug and Jack. Hopefully one of these days he'd find the woman of his dreams, just like his father did 28 years ago tomorrow. He'd have to give his parents a phone call and wish them a happy anniversary and give them the good news. They were going on a cruise of Alaska in two months. The vacation of their dreams. All expenses paid. Today was definit... "OW!" A yelp broke the daydreaming of Mark Elling as he made his way towards his car. Looking down he saw a young woman on the ground holding her shoulder. Horrified about his realization that he'd knocked her to the ground, quickly he scrambled to the ground to help her up to her feet. "I'm so sorry ma'am!" He explained, obvious shame resonated in his voice, "I should've paid more attention to where I was walking. I had no idea you were even ther..." "It's okay..." She said before looking up at the man before her. Their eyes locked for a few seconds and it was almost like they stood still in time. He was mesmerized by how beautiful she was. The woman before him would've fit in perfectly on a runway in Paris, or on the cover of a magazine, or even on a wall in a gallery of the most beautiful artist pieces the world had ever known. His mouth grew dry and his throat closed tight. He was speechless. "I probably ran into you." "No.. no." He finally spoke, "Don't apologize. Completely my fault." "I'll get out of your way," She spoke in a coyish manner, "You all look like you're in a pretty big hurry to get out of here." "Where are you going?" Mark responded almost before she finished her sentence. He had to get to know her better, "I could give you a lift. I know car isn't exactly the easiest way around this town but I don't live in the city and I have some time to burn until I really have to do anything." "Actually, I have no idea." She admitted with a smile, "I just moved here last week, and I was just wandering around town trying to find something worthwhile." "Really? Where are you from?" "I was born in Havana, Cuba." She answered, "But I just moved out here from San Francisco, on business." "You're a model aren't you?" He couldn't help but guess, "You have to be." "No, but that's flattering." She admitted, "I'm a secretary, actually." "You have to be kidding." "I'm not. I wish I was. Being a model sounds more exciting." "You could do it, if you wanted to. You're beautiful." At that moment it was almost like they were alone on the busy streets of New York City. Not a sound, nor smell, nor sight caught their attention except for each other. It was a tunnel vision most people like to call Love At First Sight. As awkward as the situation was, everything felt perfect. It all fell into place almost like it was destined to do so. This was a once in a lifetime experience that people grow up telling their children about. "Thank you." She blushed a bit, "I don't think I ever caught your name." "My name is Mark Elling." He said, "I don't believe I caught yours either." "My name is Elise..." She answered, "Elise Nguyen. You know, I could use a ride back home, I'm not even quite sure where I am." "It'll be my pleasure." Mark said with a grin, "Follow me, I'll make sure you get home safe." ---- Chapter Two: The World Is Her Oyster ---- Elise did make it back to her apartment safe that night, but Mark Elling missed his man-date with his friends. They spent the entire night learning about each other. Ordering pizza and watching a movie, they shared stories of their lives and cracked jokes that made them laugh until they cried. Mark drove home back to the outskirts of New York City at 7am. Wrapping himself in his blankets, he fell asleep dreaming about her... not being able to get over how crazy he was about Elise Aresel Nguyen. He called her first thing in the morning, and they spent nearly every waking second in each other's arms that weekend. Mark would tell you it was the greatest weekend of his entire life. Never had he fallen for a girl so hard and so fast. After just a few weeks he was ready to spend the rest of his life feeling the way he did at that moment. The feeling of ecstasy made the weeks blur into months. Their time apart felt like a lifetime, and their precious moments together felt like the blink of an eye. His life had turned into a dream. He had everything he ever wanted. He had money, a great family, and he was in love. What more could a man ask for in his life? "I can't believe you haven't been up here before..." Mark's voice could be heard as the elevator doors opened. Stepping out onto a large viewing area snow was falling from as far as the eye could see. If it wasn't for the brilliant lights, you might not have been able to see anything at all. Bundled up in a sky blue jacket, scarf, gloves, and hat combo Elise walked out behind him looking all around her carrying two shopping bags. "I've only been here a few months." Elise responded, "It gets really cold here." "You get used to it..." Mark explained, "It might be cold but it's beautiful, come over here and look." Reluctantly Elise walked over towards the guardrail surrounding the edge where Mark stood. Coming up beside him she stopped and he put his arm around her. Looking over across New York City her jaw dropped. Lights burned brilliantly in the night over the horizon. It was almost like you could see the entire world from up on top of the Empire State Building. She couldn't help but gawk. He was right, it was absolutely amazing. Elise almost dropped her bags in awe. "Beautiful, isn't it?" Mark looked down at her with a smile. His breath wavered in the breeze as he looked down at the thing he loved the most in the world, looking across the city he loved the most. "It is beautiful. It looks nothing like Seattle." She mentioned. "I thought you said you were from San Francisco?" He questioned with a smile. "I am!" She poked him with his elbow, "I was just on top of the space needle in Seattle once..." "The view is almost as beautiful as you." He responded quickly, leaning over and kissing her on the forehead. "So it has to be a lot better than the space needle." "It's amazing!" Elise looked back at him with a big smile. "It can all be yours too." Mark stated, showcasing the entire city like he was one of Barker's Beauties on the Price Is Right. "All of it. I'd buy you that building over there, if you wanted it. Or that entire block. We could buy where we're at right now and build our house up here. Wake up every morning to this view. A view of everything that you could ever want." "You don't have that kind of money." "Of course I don't, no one does." Mark responded, "But if you looked at me and told me you wanted it, I'd find a way. If you told me you wanted the moon, I'd find out how to get it for you tomorrow. The way you make me feel, I can't even explain. I've never been this in love. This is what's really amazing, the way I feel inside." "But you don't have to..." "No, I want to." Mark answered, "I want you to have everything that your heart has ever desired. You're the most beautiful woman in the entire world, and you deserve the best of everything. That's why I think you could look into moving out of that ratty apartment you have on Long Island." "I don't really have anywhere else to go..." Elise responded. "You could come move in with me." Mark said excitedly, "We could spend every moment together, when we're not at work. Waking up to your face every morning would make me the happiest man on Earth. I'd even make you breakfast. What do you say?" Elise paused for a moment, thinking about it. "I don't want to be a burden." Elise answered, "I don't even have a car. How would I get to work everyday?" "I'll buy you a car." Mark said with a smile, "I'll buy you another Gucci purse to match, and whatever else you want to have to accessorize." "Haha, you're going to go broke doing all that stuff." "If I do, I'll be broke and happy." Mark said, "As long as I have you." "Yeah..." Elise said with a big grin, "Me too. I'm already broke and happy." "Lets change that." Mark said. "Alright!" Elise said, "I'll do it! Just knock off all that sappy stuff!" Mark laughed and couldn't contain his excitement. He was almost like a little boy when she agreed. Do you remember that feeling you used to have on Christmas when you woke up all by yourself and walked into the living room to see all the presents under the tree. It was like that... only ten times better. "If you insist." Mark said as he held Elise tight next to him, "I'll be over this weekend to help you move your things. I can't wait to tell my parents. They love you to death." "Don't forget to tell your mother I said hello." Elise stated, "If you keep forgetting she's going to think I hate her." "I won't forget this time." Mark said, "Lets get off here. We have two DVDs at home and a pizza place waiting to bring us dinner." "I can't wait..." Elise said, picking up her bags full of designer purses and clothing. Not to mention the jewelry. When he said she could have anything she wanted, he meant it. Dogs, Cats, Clothes, Jewelry, Cars, and Stereos... the world was her oyster. She pointed, she got it. It was almost like being a princess without the responsibility. Most girls dream of growing up and living the life she was living right now, and she wouldn't have it any other way. ---- Chapter Three: The Proposal ---- Elise moved her stuff into his house that weekend, and they spent the night in each other's arms. As time went on they grew closer and closer. She ended up getting that car she was promised and matching purse, of course. He made her breakfast every morning before they left for work, she was treated like a queen. Everyday they spent together seemed like it was just another great day in what would hopefully be the rest of their lives. Over the next few months the weather began to change. Snow flurries turned into showers. The leaves began to grow back onto the trees, and the birds and the bees came out in full force. As the weather grew warmer, Mark decided he wasn't content with her just living with him. He wanted it all. He wanted to spend the rest of their lives together. Everyday after work he'd head to a different diamond store in search for the perfect engagement ring. On his sixth stop he found one that just screamed her name. He picked it out and got it fitted. That March he sent out invitations to get the entire family together. Mark Elling was going to make Elise Nguyen his bride, and he wanted to shout it from a mountain top. This dinner was the first day of the rest of their lives... It was a beautiful April afternoon. The showers from the past week had cleared the way for amazing weather. The sun rose high over the picnic tables in the backyard, all with umbrellas to give the family proper shade. From the table Mark Elling looked across his beautiful yard. His little cousins were running around, playing a game of pickup football with his nephews. His nieces had taken this time to pick from flowers out of the garden Elise had started around the back of the house. As he looked down the table, four generations of Elling's made themselves at home. Everyone from his grandparents to his older sister's children had gathered for sort of a vacation/family reunion. The sliding glass door to the back of his house slid open and out came the woman of his dreams. Dressed in a blue-violet spring dress and a pair of matching shoes, she made her entrance with a smile. It was almost like everyone stopped to watch while she slowly walked across the yard towards the tables. Elise was pretty sure even one of Mark's uncles got hit for staring a little too hard. "There you are!" Mark exclaimed standing up, "I've been waiting for you." "A girl needs her time to get ready." Elise answered with a smirk. Mark kissed her on the cheek. "You're just fashionably late. You look amazing." "I try." Elise stated before sitting down at the table next to the man she called hers. He wrapped his arm around her as they looked around at all the people who'd gathered for the event. She would be lying if she said she wasn't just a little nervous. There were a lot of people to impress. "You look beautiful Elise." Mark's sister Diane spoke to her. "Thanks." She answered with a smile, "You too." "Why thank you!" Diane answered, "Where did you get that dress?" "This?" Elise answered, obviously knowing the answer to her own question, "I think I got this on a business trip." "A business trip?" Mark questioned, "Secretaries get to go on business trips? I think I need to get a job where you're working." "It wasn't really a business trip as much as it was a get together with me and a few of the girls..." Elise defended, "We like to go out and have a good time. They're like family to me." "You never really talk to your family, do you?" Mark questioned, "I think I've only seen you talk to your mother once." "We had a bit of a falling out." She answered, "I'm not exactly following in the family business. Now that I have all of you, I have all the family I think I'll ever need." Diane and Mark's mother gushed as Elise's comment. As she laid on the charm Mark stood up from his seat unexpectedly holding a glass and a spoon. Tapping the spoon on the side of the glass, the dinging got everyone's attention and almost all the conversations seemed to stop immediately. They all looked at Mark, wearing a grin from ear to ear. Dressed in a yellow polo and a pair of khakis, he adjusted his sunglasses as he looked over his entire family. Old and new. "On that note..." Mark announced, "I have a question to ask." Everyone looked back at him, ready for the big moment. Elise was confused as she looked up at the man she'd lived with the past couple of months. Looking down at her, it became clear that she was now the center of attention. Reaching into his pocket, Mark pulled out a velvet black case and got down on one knee. The entire family began to speculate and gasp as he took off his sunglasses, and looked up at Elise with his hazel colored eyes. "Elise..." Mark started, opening the box to reveal an incredibly diamond ring, "These last seven months have been the best months of my life. With you by my side I wake up every morning with a smile on my face because you're still there. I want to spend the rest of my life with you next to me. Elise Aresel Nguyen... will you marry me?" She paused for a moment, biting her lip before finally looking into his eyes and giving him the answer he'd been hoping to hear. "Absolutely. Yes!" She said, taking the ring out of the box and putting it on her finger. Everyone clapped and cheered for the newly engaged couple as Mark pulled Elise up out of her chair and gave her a big hug and a kiss. Wishes of congratulations came from every direction as they looked into each others eyes. This moment was perfect. After all, it was everything she'd wanted it to be. The past seven months went just like thought it would. As all of Mark Elling's hopes and dreams came true, as did Elise Ares'. It was a shame they were on different pages all along... because this story, like most, doesn't have a happy ending. Life rarely does. Unless you're Elise Ares. ---- "After that... I never saw him again." Elise spoke, her face completely unapologetic. The story obviously didn't effect her at all, she seemed quite proud really. The ranting from the other end of the phone was almost loud enough to make The Wolf In Sheep's Clothing pull the phone away. Emily had such an annoyingly shrill voice... what was the big deal anyway? A girl had to get by. "I gathered all my belongings while he was at work, rented a truck to drive across country, and cleared out our bank account on the way out of town." She almost smiled with glee, "Then I made my way to Austin. Where I got my first gig in professional wrestling. Everything worked out... I haven't worried about money in years." "No I didn't care." Elise seemed confused, "Are you kidding me? I netted almost $200,000 from that. That money got me started in my career and look at where I am now! Might be one of the best decisions I ever made." "No, I have no idea if he's still alive. Why did you hear he killed himself or something?" There was a long pause. "Well, it's good you haven't heard anything... then what's the worry?" Elise got off the top of the luggage to try and pull the rest of the zipper around. "No I was never a secretary, I was there with Hawaiian Tropics for the winter." She responded finally, "God no. I'd never be a secretary. That's so degrading. I'd never stoop that low." "Kazys?!" She answered shocked, "Ew, no. He's not my boyfriend, so I wouldn't do it to him... plus he doesn't have any money. He's my bodyguard, I pay him. A girl's gotta have some protection in this world. Especially a girl like me." Struggling with the last part, Elise scrunches her face together to get it zipped all the way. "I have my share of cash, I didn't spend much while I was with Hawaiian Tropics. Everything was paid for." Elise waited for Emily to quit blabbing, "Of course I haven't made it last this long, actually I have a lot more than I ever saved back from that place." Jumping off the bed she glanced into the living room to see CATastrophe laying on top of one of the couch cushions now all over the living room floor. "How? Hehe..." Elise smirked, "You can't possibly think that Mark Elling was the only one." "There was one in Austin too... and San Francisco... Seattle... I lived in Denver for a bit." Elise stopped to think, "But Miami has always been home. You can't live and play in the same city. You'd never get away with it." "No, Kazys has no idea." Elise answered, "He thinks my father and I don't want to kill each other with blunt objects and I'm a spoiled little daddy's girl. As far as he knows, everything I have came from daddy's pockets. He hasn't questioned me much about the shabby apartment, it's not like he's my boyfriend. It's strictly professional. Now that we're in PRIME, my payments to him are coming straight from them. I'm not even paying out of pocket anymore." "Not enough, they have no clue what a girl of my caliber deserves." Elise paused, "I figured I'll deal with the pedestrian pay and shoddy accommodations for now. In a few years, I'll be the highest paid talent that place has ever seen. You'll see." "What if Kaz finds out the truth?" Elise stopped for a moment, almost as if she came to the startling realization that Kazys was going to be around for a long time and eventually... he would know everything there is to know about her. She didn't really have an answer to that question, "I'm sure he'll understand that I don't just use people..." KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! The knock on her apartment door startled her almost enough to make her jump in the air. Slipping her feet into some sandals she had lying on the foot of her bed, she was ready to jump on the plane to New York City. "Sorry, there was a knock." Elise gathered herself together as she pulled the luggage off of her bed, "Like I was saying I don't just use people... I'm just a girl who knows what she wants, and trust me. I get whatever I want. Whenever I want." Emily begins to answer but Elise interrupts her. "Listen, I'd love to chat but I have a plane to catch." KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! "Damnit Kaz, I'm coming!" Elise screams, "Yeah you too hun, bye!" With that Elise slides her phone shut and shoves it into her pocket before saying something under her breath. "Gah, stupid bitch is making me late..." Walking quickly across the living room area, Elise makes sure to say goodbye to CATastrophe who will be well taken care of by the neighbor while she was going being famous. I'm sure you were concerned. After maneuvering the obstacle course that was now her living room, Elise pulled the door open to find Kazys Jankauskas standing on the other side. "We're running late." Kaz states. "I know, I know..." Elise answered frustrated, "This girl I used to know called me and wouldn't get off the phone." "You have friends?" "Of course I have friends! What's that supposed to mean?" "Nothing, you just never talk about them." "Well, there's a lot of things I don't talk about..." Elise said, which as much as she runs her mouth seemed to shock Kaz. Imagine if she did talk about everything. His ears would probably be bleeding. "You have any friends in New York City?" "Nope." Elise answered, "I do know this great restaurant down the street from The Empire State building though. Make sure we have a perimeter when we go there though. There are a lot of obsessive crazies in that town. I don't want them drooling all over me, that's how people get sick... and PRIME can't afford for me to get sick. People will riot in the streets." "Yeah..." Kaz just agreed, it was almost pointless to argue at this point. While she rambled about how awesome she was, in the back of her mind she was thinking about Emily and Mark Elling, and wondered if he knew about her. Or if somehow he'd end up being at the show tonight. What would she think if he was sitting in the front row staring at her? How would she react? It didn't take her long to think about it. In a way, she sort've hoped he was sitting in the front row watching. There is nothing more she'd love to do than to show him how right he was about her greatness. Then take the time remind him why he'd never be good enough for a girl like her. Just like everyone else in the crowd.
