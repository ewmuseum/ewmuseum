"Roots" Part One: Knowledge is a Double-Edged Sword...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: REV 143 Prologue Cornelius Stakiri: Jonathan Winters employer, big money industrial pioneer and domineering underworld mob boss, is at war. The Enemy: A rival business organisation, known as OmniTech, threatens both Stakiri’s legal and less than legal enterprises aggressively and without remorse. The First Casualty: Aeryn Sun, Jonathan Winters long-time girlfriend and co-worker in Cornelius Stakiri’s elite inner circle. She was sent undercover to infiltrate the enemy, but failed. They caught her, broker her and re-programmed her mind using a combination of powerful drugs and hypnotherapy, then promptly set her on Stakiri. The Result: Crisis averted, the only way to prevent Aeryn from harming herself (or anyone else for that matter) and to destroy the deeply implanted programming was to wiper her mind entirely, including all the inside information pertaining to the enemy she‘d acquired during her capture and infiltration. The Future: Now the war has reached a critical juncture and Stakiri needs said information regarding the enemy desperately. The good news is Aeryn’s memories are beginning to return to her… Rebirth Two Years Ago Somewhere in Kansas… His eyes flickered open and his body convulsed as if it were coming to life for the first time in an age. He couldn’t help his heavy breathing nor blinking wildly whilst his eyes slowly adjusted to the light. With instinctive panic, he desperately attempted to pick himself up from the ground, but quickly discovered he couldn’t even move. There was no pain, he was sure of that, there was only numbness; universal numbness and at this juncture, he considered that to be worse. Or more accurately, his instincts did. Within seconds, his eyes adjusted to the light and he was able to make out the glistening white clouds, against the beautiful backdrop of a pristine blue midmorning sky, all framed by the dominating stalks of what appeared to be corn. Where the hell am I? He silently questioned, sifting his mind for answers. However, there was no information to sift through, his mind was completely blank and only now he realised it. His raced away and all of a sudden a better question struck him, Who the hell am I? Later he would adopt the name Jonathan Winters, but he never did find an answer to his question… Memory Progression Last Week Stakiri Towers, New York City… “I don’t like it,” Stakiri began, shaking his head to further his point, “I don’t like it one little bit.” He sat behind his desk, fingers arched and glared out of the pristine view of the New York skyline out of his office window; arguably the best view in the city. Dr. Langstrom nodded. “I understand that sir, but I don’t think we have many options left available too us.” Stakiri maintained his troubled expression, though Dr. Langstrom could not see it. “There must be some other way to do this!” “I’m certain there is,” Dr. Langstrom replied. “Our intensive memory progression techniques are working, sir, but the problem is, they’re not working fast enough for the deadlines you‘ve set…” Stakiri shook his head. “The deadlines aren’t extendable. That’s something beyond my control now.” Dr. Langstrom sighed. “Then this is the only option we have left.” Stakiri nodded absently, still deep in thought. “But what if Winters begins to remember his shaded past throughout this entire affair, Langstrom? What will we do then? He is vital to this war and we simply can’t afford to lose him!” “The memory wipe has lasted this long, sir,” Dr. Langstrom responded, obviously clutching at straws, “it is extremely unlikely that after all of this time his old memories will suddenly begin to seep through into his conscious thought.” “But he’s never been exposed to this level of nostalgia before, nor possible links to the past,” Stakiri advised, “besides, if what we have planned does work and Aeryn’s memories do begin to return, what’s to stop her from simply telling Winters what has occurred? After all, she has been around for the majority of this new life and the old one and knows all of our dirty little secrets.” “Perhaps that’s the chance we’ll have to take,” Dr. Langstrom replied. Stakiri nodded a second time. “Perhaps there‘s another option that we have yet to consider. Get Walter.” A Spanner in the Works Present Day Jonathan Winters Apartment, New York City… “Why the hell do we have to go to Pheonix again?” Aeryn questioned, gently placing a neatly folded t-shirt into her carry-all. Winters sighed. He stood across from her on the other side of their bed arranging his own clothes ready for the trip. It’d been a week since Stakiri had instructed him to accompany Aeryn on her little journey of nostalgic discovery and ever since then she’d been tense about it, worried even. “You know the deal,” he told her. “Stakiri needs information you may have locked in your head and this is the only way to get it.” “I know but why couldn’t Stakiri just tell me about my past?” She enquired. “Why the hell do I have to go all the way to Arizona to do it?” “Those white coats of Stakiri’s reckon words aren’t gonna cut it,” Winters explained. “You need to be there. You need to smell the same smells, hear the same noises and see the same things as you did way back when Stakiri first recruited you.” Aeryn nodded. Of course, she already knew all of this. Stakiri and his scientists had explained it to her in great detail, but it was trust issue with them. Winters was the only one she really trusted after Stakiri had threatened to dispose of her services back when the enemy initially wiped her memory and hearing it confirmed by him, told Aeryn it was the right course of action. “I know,” she admitted, “I just--” “You’re scared,” Winters interrupted with a knowing smile, “I get that. If I were in your position, I doubt I‘d feel any different.” She smiled a warm smile. “But you are, aren’t you?” Winters glared at her blankly. “I am…what?” “In the same position as me,” she said. “Two years ago you woke up in some field in Kansas. You have no idea who you were prior to then, you have no idea what you did or where you’ve been. The way you and Stakiri tell it, our paths must have crossed at some point.” Winters considered the notion for a second. It wasn’t the most unreasonable idea he’d ever heard…But his considerations were cut decidedly short, as suddenly there came a heavy-handed pounding on his apartment door. Aeryn shot him a startled expression and he readied himself for action. Had the enemy finally come to finish it? Had they finally decided to attack? No. If it were them, they wouldn’t be knocking on the door. They’d simply slit his throat in the middle of the night like the cowards he already knew them to be. Their actions were never direct, they were at an angle and always twisted. “Come on, man!” The call came. “I know your in there!!!” Winters shook his head and sighed, unlocked the door and then opened it. “Jesus Christ, Walter!” Walter instantly developed an innocent expression and shrugged. “What did I do this time?” Winters sighed. He didn’t have time for him, not now - not ever really…“What the hell do you want, Walter? We’re about to leave…” It was only then Winters noted a hefty-looking travel bag draped over Walter’s right shoulder and a gormless grin plastered right across his face. “No. Fuck no.” “What?” Walter questioned. “Fuck you! You aren’t coming with us…” Winters told him. Walter sighed. “Pfft…like it was my choice to accompany you two losers to the middle of nowhere!” Winters developed a confused expression. “You mean…” “Yeah,” Walter interrupted, barging past Winters in the doorway and promptly slumping down into the nearest comfortable chair he could find, “Stakiri called my ass up and demanded that I come with you. Don’t ask me why, because I sure as hell don’t know. How long will this thing take anyhow?” Winters sighed and shook his head. It was going to be one of those weeks, he just knew it. To Be Continued…
