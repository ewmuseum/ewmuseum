Changes: Nothing and Everything

29 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/29/09 \"What are…\" Jonathan Rhine starts, but he cannot finish. Looking into her eyes has always stopped him cold. \"Hey, Jon,\" says Sarah Riley, the woman who went from being his psychologist to his lover to one of his worst enemies, all in a matter of months. Her voice is low, her brows furrowed, but her eyes are staring at him, strong as ever. \"Can we talk?\" Jonathan looks to his friend, Aaron \"Dith\" Timble, for some sort of explanation. Surely he knows what this woman has put Jon through. Surely he knows that putting the two of them together cannot possibly end well. The smile says it all, but Dith decides to say it anyway: \"Yeah, I know. Don’t care. You kids have fun.\" As Aaron Dith walks out of the dive they have found themselves in, Jonathan Rhine looks from his friend to his former enemy, and his eyes concede defeat. \"What do you want, Sarah?\" Sarah Riley smiles simply. \"I just want what you want, Jonathan. I want you to be happy. The only difference is you have no idea how to do that, and I do.\" ---- Trust is something that’s very hard to come by for you in recent years. It used to be in your nature to trust and believe in the goodness of humanity. You were raised in a small town in southern Louisiana, and that sort of thing just came with the territory. It was normal to accept things at face value, and that’s pretty much what you did for the first twenty-one years of your life. Then you got into professional wrestling, where such a benefit not only doesn’t exist, but where people with those values are basically chewed up and spit out. Your easy-going, trustworthy (naïve?) nature was basically ruined in your time at FUSE and SCCW, and to be perfectly honest this woman was one of the main destroyers. You opened up to her, let her in to all of your deep-seated fears and insecurities. And she used them against you, like most of the people you’ve faced in the ring. So as you stare across the table at her, looking into those eyes, you really don’t know if you can take anything she says at face value. You haven’t been able to trust anyone since, and even if you decided to open up again, you’re not sure she would be the first on your list. But you’re still desperate. You’re still looking for answers. And most importantly, you’re looking for someone who’s there. And that’s what she is. So you talk. ---- \"What do you know that I don’t?\" Jonathan asks. \"I know that you’ve convinced yourself that you’re in a funk and that there isn’t anything you can do about it. You’ve also convinced yourself that it has somehow affected your performance in PRIME although you haven’t lost yet, not to mention your incredibly impressive stint in the Dual Halo. On top of that, you’ve completely pushed Katie away in some sort of noble gesture, and…\" Jonathan, who had been going with Sarah on her entire rant, suddenly cuts her off. \"No, no, no. You don’t understand. She cut me off. She pushed me away. She said she didn’t want to be with me because I was dangerous to both her and to myself.\" Sarah Riley smiles, shaking her head then immediately running a hand through her hair. \"If you’ve got the strength to kick out at 2, are you going to wait until it’s too late to kick out?\" \"Wha?\" Jonathan is always flabbergasted how she jumped so quickly between the real world and the wrestling world in their sessions. \"It’s a straightforward question. Your shoulder is down, 1, 2, what do you do?\" Jonathan Rhine’s response is slow and the tiniest bit condescending. \"I…kick out. So?\" \"So you don’t give up, even if you’re down. Right?\" Jon sighs, leaning back in his chair. \"Yeah, I get it.\" Again, Sarah shakes her head. \"Apparently you don’t. You always preach that the thing that sets you apart from any other wrestler is your determination. How you never stop fighting for something you believe in. How if something is in your sights, or someone is in your way, until you get what you want you will go as far as it takes, do whatever you can.\" Jon waves his hand dismissively. \"Yeah, yeah, I’m resilient.\" \"But not for the important things, Jon.\" Once again, Rhine is confused. \"What?\" \"Resiliency in this case should be telling Katie that you love her, that you want to make it work, that you won’t stop until the two of you are together again. You have no problem shouting all of this stuff into a microphone, but do you really believe it? Do you really think that you’d do anything to be with Katie?\" His frustration boiling over, Jonathan slams his fist into the table, causing the other few patrons in the bar to look at him, startled. \"It doesn’t matter what I believe, Sarah! It doesn’t matter that I’d drag myself to the ends of the earth to be with her, because she wouldn’t do the same. She doesn’t want me, not in my current state. Not in the mess I’m in.\" Instead of escalating her voice in response to his, Sarah Riley reaches across and grabs his hand to get his attention. \"Jon, I want you to listen very closely. If you don’t hear anything else, hear this.\" Leaning forward, she whispers in his ear. \"You’re not in any mess. Nothing is keeping you down by yourself. There is absolutely nothing wrong with you.\" ---- As soon as she said it, it hit you. She’s absolutely right. You’ve been so worked up about how sad you are, how you’re languishing in mediocrity and depression, that you haven’t even stopped to consider that maybe this situation isn’t as bad as you’ve made it. Maybe you’re not a wreck. Maybe you just made yourself feel like you were to make up for getting back into the game. It makes sense when you think about it: you felt guilty for joining PRIME because Katie didn’t approve, so you convinced yourself that you were really depressed. But you weren’t depressed, you just made yourself that way as some form of penance to her. As if feeling bad about it would make it okay that you went back into the profession that drove the two of you apart. But in the big picture, Sarah Riley is right. Nothing is really wrong with you. You’re not at a bad stage of your life. Even when things get rough at work, you find ways to handle it (although you know you’re going to have to eventually answer for what you did to Hessian). But for some reason you’ve been wasting your past few months drowning in your own pity. You’ve decided that stops now. So you thank Sarah for her time, leave the bar, and get to a private spot as soon as possible so you can call Katie and finally try to make things right. ---- \"…Jon? What is it?\" Katie Malick’s voice is low, slightly annoyed, and most importantly slurred sleepily. It makes Jon remember that it’s a lot later than he thought. \"Sorry, Katie. I didn’t mean to call so late.\" \"Obviously you did,\" says Katie, waking up and gaining steam. \"Well, yeah, I did.\" Rhine chuckles nervously, then continues. \"But I just…I wanted to call you and tell you…I’m kicking out.\" \"What?\" \"I’m kicking out. I’m not giving up. I’m not going to stop fighting for you, Katie. I’ll never stop. Because after everything that has happened, I’ve spent a lot of time thinking about all the bad in my life. But there isn’t anything bad in my life, there’s just a lack of a good thing. The best thing that ever happened to me.\" There’s a long pause before Katie says, slowly, \"Jon…\" \"I know, I know. You don’t want to be with me when I wrestle. And I get that. But the thing you need to know is there are two things I love more than anything in the world. One of them is my job, and the other is you. You told me that I could basically have one or the other. And I’ve been struggling recently, and I didn’t know why. But now I know it’s because I can’t have one or the other. I need both. I need wrestling, and I need you. And I can’t deal with just having wrestling. That’s not enough.\" After another pause, Katie sighs. \"I need you too. More than anything. I just…I don’t know if we can leap right in again.\" \"We don’t have to,\" says Rhine quickly, trying to hide his smile but not quite succeeding. \"We can start out slowly. Just talk a little, see each other a few times. It’ll be like the beginning all over again.\" He can’t see it, but he can practically hear Katie’s smile over the phone. \"That sounds like a great idea. How about Ultraviolence for a first date?\" \"Really?\" Jon shakes his head in disbelief. \"Really. I want to embrace you, all of you. What you love is a part of that. So I want to be there at Ultraviolence when you win that title.\" \"I…I don’t know what to say.\" Katie chuckles, then says, \"You can say goodnight, Jon. It’s really late. But we’ll talk about it tomorrow.\" \"Bye.\" Jon hangs up the phone, then looks around him to make sure no one is around before pumping his fist in the air a la Tiger Woods. \"Yes!\" he screams. \"Told you it would work out,\" a voice behind him rings. Jon turns to see Aaron Timble, his friend and self-professed life coach, with his arms wide. \"You didn’t believe me.\" Rhine smiles and puts his phone in his pocket as he walks towards the Deer in the Headlights. \"You’re right. I thought you were crazy for dragging me into a bar and shoving my ex-lover down my throat.\" \"But you sure were wrong, weren’t you?\" Aaron’s smile is toothy and wide. \"No, I wasn’t.\" \"Wh-\" is all Dith gets out before Rhine sends him to the ground with a huge left hand. The New Life kneels next to his body and leans in. \"You helped, but you also put me through hell because you didn’t like being bullied at the academy. You wanted to get revenge on me, not see me through to the other side. So I’ve made progress, and I’ve gotten back on solid footing. And for that I thank you.\" Rhine wipes some blood from Dith’s face. \"But for trying to make me see whatever faults you see in me through your jealous eyes, I gave you this. And if you ever try to get revenge on me again, I’ll give you much, much worse.\" Jonathan Rhine stands up, the smile on his face unwavering. Nothing has really changed in the last few minutes, except his knuckle hurts a little. He’s still not quite sure what direction his career is heading in. He’s still not really with Katie. Everything is the same. But for the first time in months, things feel right.
