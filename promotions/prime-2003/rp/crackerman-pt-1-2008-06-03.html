Crackerman (pt. 1)

3 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd June NOW Jason Natas stumbled from his bedroom just after midday, clothed only from the waist down and still half-asleep. He’d been out of town for a few days following PRIME’s UltraViolence pay-per-view, and it sure felt good to wake up in his own apartment, regardless of its state. Since dragging himself from the gutter and attempting to forge a career as a professional wrestler, Jason had noticed that the place did look a heck of a lot cleaner than it did before. Instead of leaving various cans, wrappers and bottles discarded on the stained carpets he now made a conscious effort to dispose of them straight away, and he’d also found the time to even give the main living area a fresh lick of white paint. The walls were bare and boring, but at least it was a step up from the peeling, cracked mess that they were before. He’d also managed to fill the place with a few expensive trinkets over the past couple of months as well. For the first time in what seemed like an eternity he possessed a brand spanking new television, which looked ridiculously out-of-place sitting in the corner of the room on an old, run-down dresser. Other notable recent purchases include a new sofa to replace the piss-stained, rotten mess of before, and a new fridge freezer stoked to the brim with expensive foods. He mightn’t have had the best of times in the wrestling ring, but the lifestyle was definitely something he’d taken a shining to. The Anti-Superstar treaded barefoot into the kitchen, and pulled a carton of Tropicana from his brand spanking new fridge. The almost-acidic orange juice stung the back of his throat a first, but after a couple of hearty gulps it was replaced by a soothing burst of citrus. Jason kneeled down to put the carton back in the fridge, before slamming the door closed. Unfortunately, he stood up too fast and felt a shooing pain sting him in the back of his head. Gurning, he rubbed the palm of his hand against the sore patch in a vain attempt to numb the pain. His damn head had been stinging since his loss at UltraViolence against Bryan Dawkins, and it was beginning to royally piss him off. The PRIME doctors had told him it would clear up naturally soon enough, but that was four day ago and still he felt that no progress was being made. Never mind, he thought, just keep swallowin’ them painkillers, doctor know best. Physical ailments weren’t the only injuries he had sustained at UltraViolence; his pride had also taken one hell of a battering. Bryan Dawkins, a man whom he loathed, caught him off guard and picked up the victory, and it made Jase sick to the very pit of his stomach. He couldn’t stand the annoying little shit-eater and his nauseating “hang loose” attitude. This was one loss that was going to take a little while to get over. As he tossed a couple of painkillers down his throat, Jason’s mind began to wander to ReVolution 161. He would be taking on the tag team of DUI alongside newcomer Torres Wilson.; a golden opportunity to redeem himself to PRIME following a less than impressive star to his career. He began thinking of ways to prepare; he’d hit the streets for a run later on, then maybe head to the gym and lift a few weights. Slowly but surely, he began planning his day out, hour-by-hour… Little did he know that a gigantic spanner was about to be thrown into the works. ***** THEN ”Ya see this, fucker?” the balaclava-clad perpetrator barked into Jason’s ear, pointing around at the remains of his decimated personal possessions. “This is what ya get, boy. Fuckin’ messin’ with the wrong people here; time to settle yer debt…” Jason was paying absolutely no attention to the words that this prick was spewing at him; he was more concerned with trying to pull the slackened noose back over his head. “Oi! Quit squirming, or we’ll have to do things the hard way!” the fatass behind him announced as he prodded the barrel of his gun painfully into the small of Natas’ back. Grudgingly, the Anti-Superstar did as he was asked, instead letting the rough rope cut away at the skin around his neck. Through squinted eyes he could see that his apartment had been completely obliterated; just about everything of value of owned had been rendered useless… his couch had been torn to shreds, holes had been kicked in the walls, and his television and hi-fi lay in bits around the floor. Not only that but these two bastards had even taken the liberty to eat his food and piss on the carpet. Fuckin’ pricks, if only he could cave both of their skulls in right now… … but he couldn’t. Here he was, helpless, standing tiptoed on a small table to prevent the noose around his neck tightening and removing him from this mortal coil. “C’mon man, let’s just end this fuckin’ shit right now!” urged the fatter of the two assailants, slurring his words through mouthfuls of balaclava. His slightly leaner accomplice nodded, realising that they had been there for well over and hour already. “Alright Natas,” he began mocking as he climbed up onto Jason’ table. “Any last words?” Jason growled, and thought about how he would give ANYTHING to wipe the smile from this smug motherfucker at this very moment. “Fuck you,” were the only words he could utter. Cliched, but thinking up some cool, witty final words when you’re a matter of seconds from the other side is a pretty hard thing to do in practice. Hopping down from the table, the skinnier guy nodded at his accomplice. However, before he could kick the table away… THUMP! THUMP! “NYPD! MOVE AWAY FROM THE DOOR!” CRACK! The sound of a door behind violently forced from it’s hinges piercing the ear as the boys in blue poured through the door and descended upon this scene of absolute carnage like flies to dogshit. “PUT THE GUN DOWN, FUCKER!” screamed an over-zealous cop at fatass. Fatass thought quickly, however, and instead of putting the gun away he savagely kicked the table away from underneath Jason, tensing the noose and beginning to choke the life from the big New Yorker. Without warning one of the police officers fired a bullet straight into fatass’s shoulder, sending the colossal criminal crashing to the ground lack a sack of potatoes. Shouting various testosterone-fuelled yells, the cops quickly subdued the two assailants while another one quickly cut Jason down with a pocket knife. “MEDIC! WE NEED A MEDIC!” were the last words that Jason heard as he slipped out of consciousness. ***** NOW “GOD-FUCKING-DAMNIT!” a distressed Mr. Loftman screamed as he through his foot into the wall. He wasn’t the only one in the room near boiling point; his colleagues – Mr. Jewel and Mr. Najera – both exuded auras that defied their usually cold-as-ice demeanours. “We take this guy from the streets, give him the opportunity of a lifetime, and then we find out about this! I knew that taking this guy on was a mistake, I fucking knew it!” The partner of the Potemkin Management Group turned and slammed his fist into the glass-top table; he hit it with such force that he was quite lucky not to break it. “Alright man, let’s not get carried away here,” Mr. Najera said, unfortunately his attempt to calm his business partner down seemed to fall on deaf ears. “CALM DOWN!?” bellowed Loftman as if Najera had just defiled his mother. “This shit could have SERIOUS consequences! We’re talking jail-time here, guys! Jason, you fucking moron!” he cursed Jason even thought he wasn’t in the room. “Come on now, maybe we should hear Jason’s side of the story…” piped in Mr. Jewel. “Fuck that! I say we fire the guy!” retorted Loftman violently. Mr. Najera leaned across the table, holding his head in his hands. He knew that a giant shitstorm was about to descend upon all three of the men in this room – and Jason Natas. He was just as pissed-off as his colleague, he just had a good way of hiding it. “We can’ do that, man, the compensation will be ridiculous.” “I don’t care! I’ll pay it myself if I have to!” “But what if it’s bullshit?” suggested Mr. Jewel. He attempted to explain further, but he soon found Loftman wagging today’s New York Times in front of his face furiously. “Look man, it’s here in PLAIN. FUCKING. ENGLISH…” Now it was Jewel’s turn to sigh. This was going to be difficult. ***** THEN “Jason? Are you awake?” The surprisingly soft words of his father seemed to hang in the air as they filtered into Jason’s stream of consciousness. “Jason?” Finally he awoke from his extended slumber. His body was so full of aches and pains that he felt like he had been chewed up and spat out by a giant, and he didn’t exactly look like a million dollars either. The skin around his neck was badly burned from where the noose had chafed against it; in fact, the doctors said he was lucky not to have broken it, given the sudden force at which the table had been kicked from beneath his feet. Not only that, but his face was littered with tonnes of tiny cuts and bruises, and a nice big shiner was beginning to form around his left eye. It took him a while to realise that he was indeed lying in a hospital bed. It was that soulless, sterile smell that did it for him first as it wafted up his nostrils and into his lungs. Then, as his vision recovered, he noticed the unblemished curtain drawn around his bed, and the stack of machinery to the side of his bed, beeping and flashing like some kind of robot disco. His father sighed. “Dang, son, look at the state of you…” he mused. Jason couldn’t find the energy to reply; he tried to speak, but his throat felt like he had just swallowed a handful of razorblades. “You’ve got to quit this fucking shit,” continued his father, his tone suddenly taking a turn for the worse. “You’re a fucking NATAS, son! You shouldn’t be lyin’ around in some hospital bed like a scolded dog. Get a fucking grip of your life and get OUT of this messed-up fucking shit you’re involved him…” It didn’t surprise him to hear such harsh words coming from his father; he’d gotten used to it growing up, and his coarse massacring of the English language had rubbed off on him. Either way, when he heard his Dad pleading with him in the only way he knew how, he knew immediately that some changes had to be made. ***** NOW ”POWER! POWER! HAS ALWAYS BEEN ABUSED! WORK, DEBT, POVERTY, TAXES! LIFE’S OPPRESSIVE RULES!” The larynx-shredding roar of Sick Of It All vocalist Lou Koller forced it’s way out of Jason’s newly-purchased iPod at a near-deafening decibel. As far as Jason was concerned, hardcore punk was the perfect soundtrack to a dreary afternoon jogging through the streets of the Bronx district of New York; the ugly collision of breakneck drumbeats, gutwrenching screams and harsh guitar riffs pumping an insurmountable amount of adrenaline through his veins. The streets were quiet today; hardly surprising given the fact that the heavens had opened four or five hours ago and the downpour hadn’t let up for a moment since then. Not that that put Jason off of his daily jog; in fact, he preferred it when the streets weren’t so busy, less chance of bumping into someone he doesn’t particularly care for… Stopping at a crossroads, Jason took a minute to catch his breath as he leaned against a nearby lamppost. The traffic was as heavy as usual, and so loud that he could faintly hear it over the top of his maxed-out mp3 player. As he stood there in the rain he immediately rued his choice of clothing; his grey, unbranded hooded sweatshirt was soaked right through to the skin, amplifying the coldness of the East coast wind and sending a shiver up his spine every time traffic lights forced him to take a break. Noticing the traffic lights turning back to red, Jason pulled tight on the sweatshirt’s cords, decreasing the size of the little cloth porthole he saw the world through. The changing of the sign from “DON’T WALK” to “WALK” across the road was met with great relief from The Anti-Superstar as he slowly begin to build pace again, squinting to prevent any raindrops from flying straight into his eyes. However, no sooner had he got back into his stride than something in the window of Digby’s Electrical Store caught his eye. Jason stopped in his tracks, backtracking a couple of steps to get a good view of the giant plasma television screen in the window. Immediately thoughts of a not-so-glamorous past filled his head as he saw his visage appear on the television screen, staring back at him. The guys breaking into his apartment, the hanging, the hospital room… they all seemed like they happened just yesterday. Glancing at the TV in disbelief, Jason had to rub his eyes, just to make sure he wasn’t seeing things. When he re-opened them it was still there… WORLD EXCLUSIVE: PRIME SUPERSTAR NATAS A DRUG DEALER? There, in plain English, a damning headline scrolled across the bottom of ESPN. Not only that but much of the right hand side of the screen was taken up by a PRIME promotional shot of The Anti-Superstar himself. He felt hi cell phone vibrating in his pocket before he even had time to let his jaw drop. Immediately he pulled his headphones from his ear, looking down at his phone as the word “POTEMKIN” flashed across its screen. Gingerly he pulled the phone to his ear; he was going to have some explaining to do. “Yeah, what is it?” he growled as a scowl began to fold itself across his brow. He immediately recognised the Hispanic accent volleying a furious diatribe at him from the other end of the phone. This wasn’t going to be pretty.
