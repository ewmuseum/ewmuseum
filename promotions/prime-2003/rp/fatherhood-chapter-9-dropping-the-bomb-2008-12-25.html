Fatherhood - Chapter 9 - Dropping the Bomb

25 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 25, 2008 THURSDAY DECEMBER 25, 2008 DAWKINS HOUSEHOLD 11:35 AM “Your father’s downstairs.” Sarah, Bryan’s mother, was sitting on the living room couch when her son entered the house for the first time since Nikkie’s death. Between visiting Nicholas at the hospital and touring the country with PRIME, there wasn’t much time for Bryan to make it home. He had last seen them the day of Nikkie’s funeral, but hadn’t had time to go to the house after due to obligations pertaining to the funeral and Nikkie’s family. Did he feel bad about it? Absolutely. In the past few months, Bryan’s father’s health had been declining. After hitting the age of 65, things started to gradually slide downhill for him. The hips needed to be replaced, the knees and elbows were stiff, and normal acts of life were now chores. “How’s he doin’, mom?” “Oh, he’s himself, ya know? Older, crankier, but himself.” Bryan flashed a half-smile. He had left home on bad terms. In Kenneth’s eyes, leaving home was a huge injustice to the family. According to him, Bryan was needed around the house, and there was a main event spot open for him in the company where Bryan had been working, and where Kenneth made the Dawkins name known around the wrestling world, Hawaii Championship Wrestling. But Bryan had different plans, and Kenneth never accepted the fact that Bryan was on his own now, and needed to make his own decisions. When Bryan left, Kenneth predicted that his son’s tenure in PRIME would be just like the ones in every other promotion he had joined, aside from HCW: a few weeks on the road, then back out on the street, looking for a new job. In HCW, Bryan was tenured, and even somewhat respected in the locker room, despite the many lackluster performances he put on. Most said it was due to the immense pressure put on him by his father’s legacy with the company, and in the wrestling world in general, and most believe that Bryan would agree with that statement. As Bryan made the trip down the short hallway, he couldn’t help to think about how he could very easily rub the fact that he made a name for himself somewhere NOT named HCW, and WITHOUT his father’s help into his face, but that idea was quickly dismissed due to the unspoken respect and reverence he had toward his father. He found the door to the basement and opened it slowly, listening for any sound of life coming from below. A soft murmur of voices, presumably coming from the basement television, drifted up the stairway, acknowledging the fact that someone was down there. He tiptoed down the stairs to find his father sprawled out on the couch, almost lifeless, staring blankly into the vibrant colors of the television. Trying not to wake him if he was sleeping, Bryan quickly yet quietly walked over to the arm of the chair, and was about to take a seat when… “Bryan…s’that you?” Kenneth’s voice was deep and raspy, aged by many packs of cigarettes and bottles of whiskey out on the road with his fellow wrestlers throughout the years. He glanced toward his son, who had almost fallen over out of surprise, and re-fixated his eyes on the television in front of him. Bryan took a seat on the recliner next to the couch and sighed. “Yeah dad, figured I’d stop by and…” Kenneth interjected. “…what? Come see us for the first time in MONTHS?! I see PRIME is treatin’ you pretty well, flyin’ you all over the country as you please. Flyin’ to your shows, then back home to see your son, then back out to your PRIME gigs. Must be nice.” “But pop…” “No, no ‘but pop’ today, Bryan. You know, it’d be real nice to see you once in a while.” “I’ve been tryin’ to for months, pop. It’s just been so busy with Nikkie’s death, Nicholas’ birth, helpin’ Nikkie’s parents with everything. Hasn’t been easy, ya know?” Kenneth sighed. “YOU HAVE FAMILY HERE TOO, BRYAN. YOU EVER THINK OF COMING TO US FOR HELP?” Hearing Kenneth raise his voice was nothing new for Bryan. In fact, it finally felt like he was back home, only now, the voice reprimanding him was weaker and less forceful than the one he remembered from the last time he had heard him like this. When Bryan was in HCW, Kenneth accompanied him to ringside more often than not, to give Bryan pointers and manage him through most of his matches. Unfortunately, the whole “managing” part turned into a screaming session which usually ended up with Kenneth embarrassing Bryan to the point where he’d lose concentration and consequently, the match. “I’ve…just been busy, pop. I’m sorry.” … … Silence. … … “Listen, I didn’t come here for you guys to forgive me. I know I messed up and I should be home more, but I haven’t been thinkin’ straight all the time lately. You guys are just as important as anyone else in my life right now, and I need you guys to just be behind me through this stuff.” Kenneth wasn’t flinching. Continuing his lifeless stare into the television, Bryan kept going. “I just need you guys to know that Nicholas is coming out on the road with me when he gets out of the hospital. I’m not asking anything from you guys. This is me tellin’ you that when he gets out, he’s gonna be with his dad. I’m gonna raise him how I need to, I’m gonna take care of him, and everything’s gonna be fine. I just needed you guys to know that.” The television no longer held Kenneth’s attention; it was now solely on his son. “That’s such a stupid idea, Bryan. You have NO idea what you’re doing. Nicholas needs a stable home environment to thrive in. He needs to live in a home, not whatever hotel PRIME books for you that week! You’re crazy, Bryan. I knew you weren’t the sharpest knife in the drawer when you decided to knock Nikkie up and take this job traveling all over God knows where, but come on. Raising your child out on the road with you? That’s just plain stupid, Bryan, and you know it.” Bryan sighed. “That child will NEVER grow up to be functional in society. You’re going to mess this up, Bryan, just like you’ve messed up everything else in your life. You bring a kid into this world WAY before you’re ready to do so, and now you decide to bring him out on the road with you. Yeah, it sounds like a great way to bring yourself and Nicholas closer together as he grows up, but you’re not gonna be able to raise him like that. No way.” But when Kenneth turned to face his son, he was gone. * * * SIVEC HOUSEHOLD 1:00 PM “Bryan, talk to us. What’s wrong?” Pam was concerned about Bryan. He was somewhere between a fit of tears and a fit of rage, and not like himself. He burst through the door of the Sivec house, and stormed into the living room, searching for Nikkie’s parents. Bryan’s father’s words had seemingly pushed him over the edge. “My father. He just doesn’t understand. I need to be with Nicholas. Other things have been my priority as of late. I can’t please everyone. WHY CAN’T EVERYONE JUST UNDERSTAND THAT I WANT WHAT’S BEST FOR ME AND MY SON?!” Now Pam and Mike were really worried. They had never seen him like this, and it was a troubling sight. “What are you talking about, Bryan? We all want you to be with Nicholas too. He’s your son, that’s your right.” Calming down a little bit, and subsequently worrying, Bryan took a deep breath before exhaling and dropping the bomb on Pam and Mike. “You guys don’t understand. When Nicholas gets to the point where he can leave the hospital, I’m giving him a home.” Pam and Mike looked at each other quizzically. “Yeah, we know. He’s gonna live with us, and we’re okay with that.” Bryan just shook his head somberly. “No.” The confused glance of Pam and Mike shifted from each other to Bryan. “What are you talking about, Bryan?” “I’m saying that when he’s good to leave the hospital, he’s coming with me. He’s gonna live out on the road, in PRIME, with me.” Blank stares were pasted across the faces of Nikkie’s parents, who were now in an incredibly confused state of mind. One minute, they were the future caregivers for their grandchild, and the next, he’s going to live out on the road with his father. “Yeah, I’m sorry for not talking to you about this earlier, but I really think this is gonna be best for Nicholas. I need him. He’s my son, and I need to take care of him and watch him grow up to be a man. And more importantly, he needs me. I don’t want Nicholas to be one of those boys who grows up never seeing his father because of work, so I’m fixing that problem as soon as I can.” Pam and Mike were silent. Not because they had nothing to say, but because the words just would not come out. “I appreciate everything you guys have given to me throughout the last few months, and I promise that you’ll get more than your fair share of time with your grandson, but he’s living with his father, and that’s all there is to it. I’m going to provide for him the best I can, and if the time comes where I am without a shadow of a doubt not caring for him the way I should, I’ll hand him over to people who I know can do that job effectively.” Bryan took a deep breath and exhaled slowly. “That would be you two. I love you both, and I love your daughter. Regardless of what happens, I’ll forever appreciate everything you guys have given me, and hope that you’ll be there, should I need you guys down the road. I really hope you guys understand where I’m comin’ from, and don’t hold this against me, whether you think this is a smart decision or not.” After a few moments of silence and mental pondering, Mike stood up and advanced toward Bryan, grasping him on the shoulders and locking eyes with him. “Bryan, we don’t think this is the ideal situation for anyone here, but we trust your judgment on this one. We realize you feel the need to be near Nicholas at all times, and will respect that wholeheartedly. We just want him to have a good life, and we really think you can give that to him. We just ask one thing of you, and that is that you take care of our grandson with utmost care. You know that we’ll always be here for you two, day or night, rain or shine, so never shy away from coming and asking for help.” Bryan smiled. “Boy, Bryan…Nikkie would be so proud of you two. She’s lookin’ down on you smilin’. Take care of our boy, Bryan. We know you can do it.”
