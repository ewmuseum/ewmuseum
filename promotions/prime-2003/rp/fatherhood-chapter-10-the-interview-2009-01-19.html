Fatherhood - Chapter 10 - The Interview

19 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 19, 2009 MONDAY JANUARY 19, 2009 KWXX RADIO STATION HILO, HAWAII 4:05 PM "Ahola my friends, and welcome back to the Afternoon Show here on KWXX Radio, 94.7 FM. This is Kaohu James, playin’ all of your favorite music for ya this afternoon. It’s a calm 71 degrees outside the studio right now on this Monday afternoon, but with the arrival of my special guest, I’m sure things are about to heat up." KWXX is the main Top 40 radio station in the city of Hilo, repeatedly playing all of the new hits that have just gone mainstream over the past few weeks, much to the delight of high schoolers everywhere. The station has a talented group of on-air personalities, the most vocal and colorful being Kaohu ‘K.J.’ James, who hosts the Afternoon Show. Filled with borderline-racy skits, jokes, and more early-adult themed entertainment, James is a popular choice for most radio listeners on the island. Being such a big name on the island, he has no problem bringing in other big names to spend time with him on air, discussing everything from politics to sports to internet tomfoolery. "Many of you might recognize this man as the one who throws his body around in the squared circle, touring from week to week around this great nation of ours with the pro wrestling company PRIME. Some of you might know him as ‘The Flyin’ Hawaiian.’ Yeah, I’m talkin’ about our hometown boy, Bryyyyyyyannnnnn Dawwwwwwwkinnnnnnns!" Dawkins sat cheerfully in the comfortable padded barstool next to KJ, sipping on a pineapple juice and listening intently to his introduction through his headphones. Although listeners at home wouldn’t see it, he greeted KJ on-air with a handshake followed by a brief man-hug, showing that this was indeed not the first time these two had met. Settling back down into his chair, Dawkins adjusted his earphones and addressed the eager listeners. "Hey bruh, thanks for havin’ me. It’s been a while, but I’m glad to be back on." James flashed a quick smile toward Dawkins before diving straight into the heavy stuff—the PRIME stuff. "No problem, ya know it’s always good to have a homebody on the show. Especially a homebody who happens to be the numba one contenda for the 5 Star Title! Tell us about how ya got there." Cracking a modest smile, Bryan Dawkins took a minute to take a long drink from his juice, breaking the number one rule of radio broadcasting: never allow there to be dead air time. "Well ya know, bruh, it hasn’t been easy. I’ve been with the company for almost a year now, and I’m just startin’ to find my groove, if ya know what I mean. Just startin’ to get a feel for where I’m at in the grand scheme of things; what I can and can’t do, and what I should and shouldn’t do, ya know?" Extending a clenched fist toward Dawkins, James responded quickly. "Amen, bruh. Amen." "Yeah, so a couple’a weeks ago I faced Dusk, who’s no longer with us, in a match to figure out which one of us would become the new number one contender for the 5 Star Title, ya know?" Receiving a nod from KJ, Dawkins continued, jokingly. "I think we all knew how that’d turn out, right bruh?" Quickly following Dawkins’ one-liner, KJ cued up a laughter soundbyte used by radio personalities nationwide. "Yeah, we all had all of the faith in the world in ya, Bryan. So what’s next on the agenda? Gonna be takin’ that title away anytime soon?" Luckily, Bryan was prepared for these questions, and he responded with his calm, yet lighthearted demeanor. "Well, if I see Flyer in the ring again like I did last week, I’m pretty sure I’ll be makin’ my way back to Hilo with that gold wrapped around my waist. Maybe then some people would actually be listenin’ to the show, haha." "Yeah, yeah, yeah…reason nobody’s listenin’ right now is cuz of you, bruh, not me. Normally I’ve got tons of listeners and callers phoning in to talk to Hilo’s number one man, but apparently nobody wants to talk to PRIME’s hottest right now." Glancing over at the call board, KJ soon realized that he was quite wrong. Or maybe he was just pulling Dawkins’ chain. I guess the world will never know. "Alright well it’s lookin’ like we’ve gotta run to commercial real quick, but we’ll be right back here on the Afternoon show with KJ James and PRIME’s Bryan Dawkins here on KWXX Radio." With that, the illuminated "On Air" sign shut off, and both James and Dawkins removed their headphones and jumped from their seats to stretch for a second. "So Bryan, how long ya in town for?" "Ya know, bruh…couple days before I gotta head back out on the road again. But really, most of my travelin’ is done through the air anymore. PRIME’s real good about that, flyin’ me home whenever I need to see Nicholas or the family—" "—I don’t mean to cut ya off, but we’ve got about 10 seconds ‘til we go back on the air. You good?" With a nod, Dawkins slipped the headphones back on, just in time to catch KJ’s first words. "And we’re back on the Afternoon Show with Bryan Dawkins of PRIME fame." "Now now, bruh…fame? I wouldn’t exactly go that far, haha." "Oh c’mon bro, ya know you’ve hit the big time with that place. All ya gotta do now is caplitalize." With a smile, Bryan replied. "Much easier said than done, bruh. Muuuuuuch easier." Taking a sip from his pineapple juice while KJ rambled on about the random happenings on this side of the island, Bryan faded off for a second, letting his thoughts drift away to somewhere far, far away from him… THURSDAY OCTOBER 2, 2008 US AIR FLIGHT 724 FROM LOS ANGELES TO HILO 1:03 PM Bryan couldn’t help but to toss and turn whilst attempting to catch some shuteye on this plane ride. The love of his life and mother of his first-born child was dead, and he was thousands of miles away. Add to that the fact that he wasn’t able to be reached in this time of need, and you’ve got a few very good reasons why the young man found it nearly impossible to find that golden state where sleep is perfectly attainable. Covered up by one of those cheap imitation-flannel blankets that are provided on extensive flights and resting his head on a pillow that felt like it was filled with packing peanuts, Bryan rolled from one side of his body to the other, unable to find that spot that felt "just right." The fact of the matter is that finding that spot was going to be very difficult in the upcoming months. Coping with death isn’t easy, especially when it was uncontrollable, unexpected, and especially when the deceased’s loved ones feel as though they should be held accountable for the situation. Bryan felt like he shouldn’t have been on the road with PRIME. Had he not been in New York City for ReVolution that week, he would’ve been at home, ready to take care of Nikkie when she went into premature labor. But he wasn’t. And in his mind, he was completely liable for what happened to her. She died because of his commitment to work, and his lack of commitment to her and the baby. The question of "was this my fault" didn’t even need to be brought up in his thoughts, because the answer was a resounding "yes," and there was nothing that anyone could possibly say to convince him otherwise. That afternoon on the plane, Bryan Dawkins sincerely considered leaving wrestling altogether. He was blaming his fiancee’s death on his commitment to his job, and the only way that he felt that he could redeem himself would be to abandon his job and go home to Hilo, where he was needed by his family, and those he left behind. He would make up for the wrongs he had committed when Nikkie died, and somehow the universe would re-align and peace would enter his life again. The flight was only supposed to last 3 hours that afternoon. To Bryan Dawkins, it felt like 3 years. MONDAY JANUARY 19, 2009 KWXX RADIO STATION HILO, HAWAII 4:25 PM "Whoaaaa, Bry…you alright, bro? Look a little spaced out there." KJ cued up the laughter soundbyte, which spooked Dawkins from his daydream. "Yeah bruh, I’m here." "So what’s on your mind? Tell us what’s goin’ on with Bryan Dawkins outside the ring…" Dawkins’ mind quickly flashed back to the daydream. "Uh…well as most people know, the past few months have been rocky for me. In early October, I had a son, but unfortunately lost my fiancée due to complications during premature birth. I’m doin’—" KJ interrupted. "Alright folks, sorry to interrupt Bryan again, but we’ve gotta cut to a commercial real quick-like. Be back in a quick second!" Calling up a 30 second commercial and focusing his eyes on a confused Bryan Dawkins, KJ questioned if Dawkins really wanted to discuss this. "Bryan, you really wanna go there? I mean, I was cool with us keepin’ it nice and light with some jokes, some wrestlin’ talk, and that kinda stuff. Ya don’t gotta go into this if ya don’t wanna." "Yeah bruh, it’s cool. I need to let everyone know how I’m feelin’, though. Holdin’ it in ain’t gonna help anyone." With a reassuring nod, KJ turned up the mics just as the "On Air" light turned on again. "Alright sorry about that, folks, but we were havin’ some technical difficulties here in the studio that we had to get fixed up so that we could continue on for your listenin’ pleasure. We’re here with Bryan Dawkins, who was tellin’ us about the life he lives outside the squared circle." "Yeah bruh, like I was sayin’ before we had to cut to commercial…I’m doin’ alright. Nicholas, my son, is doin’ really well. Little bruh should be ready to leave the hospital in another month or so." "That’s good to hear, bro. So does this mean you’ll be makin’ more trips to the island to see the little guy, or what?" Dawkins cracked his big toothy smile and spoke with incredible pride. "Nah bruh, Nicholas is gonna live with me, out on the road. I’ve got a couple friends that are with the company that are gonna help out when I can’t be with him, plus I’ve been throwin’ around the idea of gettin’ a full-time nanny to come out on the road with us when we do shows so that Nicholas at least has some sort of permanent female figure in his life." "Ah, sounds like gettin’ that father-son bond goin’ is gonna be a top priority for ya." "Yeah bruh, definitely. I want Nicholas to see what dad does for a livin’. Even if he goes down another path in life when he gets older, that’s cool. I just want him to know what his dad does, how I made a living, ya know? And with any luck, yeah, he’ll become the third generation of the Dawkins wrestling family. But hey, he’s not even four months old yet. He’s got time to figure out what he wants to do." "Of course, bro, of course. And why wouldn’t any young boy wanna grow up backstage at wrestling events? I can’t think of any that wouldn’t love to grow up that way." "Eh, it’s mainly about bein’ together for these early parts of his life. I mean now, with him still bein’ in the hospital and everything, I can’t very well be home to see him every day, just ‘cuz of work, ya know? Eventually we’ll see each other every day, and that’s somethin’ I’m definitely excited for. And I’m sure his mom would be thrilled that father and son are gonna be able to live together, despite the situation." "I’m sure she would, Bryan. It’s a little crazy, but this sounds like it could work for you guys. Just gotta be careful , ya know? With all of the perks this could bring, there are gonna be negatives. But I’m sure you’ve thought that through, bruh. You’re good like that." Dawkins smiled uneasily with those last few lines. Sure, he had thought it through before, but was this really the right decision? His gut feeling said yes. But the more he thought about it, the more questions arose. "Thanks for comin’ on the show, Bryan. I really appreciate it, bruh." "Yeah bruh…uh, no problem. Glad ya had me on…" Dawkins’ voice trailed off while KJ transitioned into the next part of his show. Bryan slowly removed his headphones while gazing off into nowhere, seemingly lost in thought. Slowly but surely, the questions and doubt that his father had planted in his head last month were starting to creep in and make sense. Bryan needed to make a decision. And fast.
