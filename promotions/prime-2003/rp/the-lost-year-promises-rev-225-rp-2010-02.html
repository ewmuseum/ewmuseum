The Lost Year: Promises (ReV 225 RP)

February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February, 2010 Late February, 2010 Winter in Texas could never be construed as frigid, but there was a definite coolness in the training center on Dan Ryan's expansive Texas compound. The Houston native had opted to build a facility on the grounds of his home to optimize both his privacy and the convenience of catering to his training schedule. Dan stood next to a folding chair, sunglasses propped up on his head and his arms hanging loosely by his sides. He stared intently ahead, where in a ring towards the far back of the building his sister-in-law, Lindsay Troy, was sparring with a trainer. So focused was he on Troy's workout that he almost didn't hear the door behind him open. He glanced over his shoulder to see his wife Alaina and daughter Cecilia enter. CeCe held a piece of paper in her hand and she waved it as she ran towards him. Alaina, on the other hand, was juggling her purse, briefcase, CeCe's backpack and an iced coffee and was much slower to reach him. "Daddy look what I did!" Dan scooped Cecilia up in his arms and looked at the drawing in her tiny hands. She beamed and he smiled warmly. "Well, that's just beautiful, Ceese. I know the exact spot on the fridge where I'm going to put that." Alaina stopped next to Dan and dropped the bags to the floor. She watched her sister for a moment and creased her eyebrows together. "How's she doing?" Dan tilted his head to look down at his wife. Her bottom lip was caught in-between her teeth; a noticeable concerned trait. "Been going slow with it, just like most days," he replied. "She'll get there, eventually." "Daddy I wanna go watch!" Alaina said nothing as Dan lowered Cecilia from his arms. She put her picture on the chair, then hopped towards to the ring where Lindsay had resumed her work with the sparring mitts. "I've seen that look enough times to know that it's not one I'm all that fond of seeing. What's going on, Alaina." "She's not ready for this yet, Dan." The admission came very softly. Alaina shuffled her feet, sighed and crossed her arms over her chest. "Every time she's taken time away, whenever she's been ready to start getting back into the swing of things she's never gone slow. Not when Jaime died, not when she had knee surgeries, not when Brent got sick the first time, not when she got divorced from Joey. None of those times nor any other has she ever not come out firing on all cylinders." Alaina motioned to the ring with her hand for added emphasis. "This isn't her. I'm aware that I don't know every specific little detail that you or her or anybody else employs when preparing for the jobs you do but I know my sister better than anyone. I'm telling you, she's not ready. And who knows if she'll ever be." "The fact that she's actually wanting to get back in the gym and work on cardio and weights and sparring should be enough of a sign that Lindsay's serious about getting back into the swing of things," Dan said. "She's been staying with us for how long now?" "Since Christmas." "And the past two weeks have been the only time she's shown any spark of enthusiasm towards doing anything wrestling related. That says something." Dan took a step closer to his wife and laid a reassuring hand on her shoulder. The gesture was returned with Alaina's hand covering his. "Alaina, I'm never going to pretend that I know Lindsay better than you and I know that she's bounced back from some pretty serious shit in her life. She just faced something that's worse than all of it and to even see her in there, no matter what pace it's at, is huge. I'm not trying to rush her or trying to dictate any pace that she's not setting herself. I'm just an observer until she says differently." The youngest Troy looked over to her shoulder and squeezed her husband's hand. "You need to promise me something, Dan." "Anything." "And you need to keep it." Dan furrowed his brow and cocked his head in curiosity. "Since when have I not kept a promise to you?" "There was that time you said you were going to retire." He rolled his eyes and chuckled. "I said I'd cut back on my obligations, not ret--." "You said retire," Alaina smirked, slightly. "I'm not holding it against you. Just listen, alright?" She looked to her sister, who had paused her workout in the ring and was instead looking down at CeCe. "Wherever she goes, and whatever she does from here on out, you need to go with her. I know she has Tyler, but I'm still in the process of warming up to the notion of her being serious with someone given the Melton disaster. You need to watch out for her, Dan, because I'm not so sure she'll be watching out for herself." Dan kissed the top of Alaina's head and rested his forehead against her blond tresses. "I promise," he muttered against the curve of her ear. "Good," came the whispered reply. A pair of footsteps approached from up ahead and caused Alaina and Dan to glance forward. Both Lindsay and Cecilia were coming towards them, Cecilia bouncing with the same youthful exuberance as before and Lindsay much more slowly and passively. CeCe grabbed her mother's hand while Lindsay grabbed the towel that she had thrown over the back of Dan's chair. "You all done for the day?" Alaina asked her sister. "Yeah." Lindsay wiped her face and tossed the towel over her shoulder. "I, um..." she stole a quick glance down at Cecilia, then lifted her eyes back to her sister and brother-in-law. "I'm just not feeling it right now, y'know?" The once Queen of the Ring scratched the back of her head and made her way toward the exit with hands shoved into her pockets and head bent, quite like in defeat.
