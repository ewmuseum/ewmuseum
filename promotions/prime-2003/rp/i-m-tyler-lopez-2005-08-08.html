I'm Tyler Lopez

8 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/08/2005 ”I've put in so many enigmas and puzzles that it will keep the professors busy for centuries arguing over what I meant, and that's the only way of insuring one's mortality.' James Joyce We fade outside of the New Orleans, Louisiana urban mansion in which our local paragon of virtue inhabits. The mansion is a mansion. Outside of the home is a white van, you can call this van a ‘painters’ van if you will. We know this because outside of the van in large almost crayon like writing reads, “Guzzy’s Painting Services” This broken down van looks like it also doubles as a home. All we know is, that it’s parked next to Lopez’s 2006 platinum Lamborghini, and that the front door is slightly ajar, which means we can assume “The Apparatus” is indeed home. We slowly peer inside the door and we see not much has changed from last time in completely furnished home. Sitting in one of the white recliners is “The Scientific Apparatus” Tyler Lopez, he’s plopped down in front of the 42' plasma TV. On the television is the re-run of “PRIME's Colossus”, Lopez’s of course watching it. Not that normal watching, he’s giving that now strange glazed over glare that we’ve seen in the last few weeks, creepy shit. Behind the TV, is a man, a young looking man, looks like he used to be a woman, he probably gets drunk off Coronas, e-wrestles and lives in that van parked outside. He has a ‘painters’ suit on, and a piece of canvas in front of him and a ‘house painting’ paint brush and a few smaller ones by his side. He’s surrounded by a bucket of paint, that is all. For lack of a better name, we’ll call this man, “Thomas Morris. “How much longer is this going to take?” Lopez continues to stare at the young painter, questioning him, never once glancing away from the TV as the painter boy flashes a ‘five’ towards Lopez, meaning five minutes we assume. “Good, once you’re done, leave and never come back. You never came here, you don’t know who I am, disavow all knowledge of this day and this event ever happening, and make sure the door is locked on your way out.” Lopez continues to stare as the young painter works feverishly, thrashing paint all over the place. From behind Lopez, slowly strolling out of the kitchen is Passion. She is wearing normal house clothes. Passion glances at the painter and gives a fake smile, and then watches over Lopez’s shoulder as he glares at the television. Passion: I still cannot believe that it was you Tyler. And your explanation, worthless, you are so lucky I am a forgiving woman. Now, you’ve watched this one thousand times now, nothings going to change. Speaking in a trance like tone. Lopez: My huge opportunity, and I lost, I lost babe. Did you hear the people cheering though, chanting my name? Did you hear their reaction when I destroyed Jackson Darilek? Well, did you? Now for anyone whose seen the match, you know, no such move happened, but if you haven’t seen the match, you might not be sure. Well, Passion has seen the match, and she knows not only did that happen, but also Lopez damn sure didn’t do it. Passion: Sure, I saw it, what about it though? Lopez: That was the biggest opportunity I have ever had in this damn organization. The biggest stage of them all, and I dropped the ball. There’s no way, no way this can continue. It is my time. I have been here longer than half the guys in the main event. I have worked my ass off for two years straight. How many of these guys can withstand what I have gone through? How many? Passion: [A little more leery] None, not any of them. Lopez: Damn right, and do you hear me out here crying like that little Vaginal Spasm Kelly Flawless, hell no! Do you hear me out here bringing up every old accomplishment, every old memory, every old family member, searching just searching for a reason to say, ‘I’m better than you’. Because that’s all half the locker room does, boys like Kelly Flawless sit back and a try to get sympathy, how pathetic is that? Lopez's face fills with disgust, as he watches himself run and hide on the television. Lopez: [Scoffing] I’d never, ever, stoop to the levels that half these losers in PRIME do, this business is dirty, back stabbing and immoral, if it wasn’t for people like me around here, it’d become a shell of it's former self, filled with dictators and people who have no respect for this business. People who'd retire with World Championships, on a whim. [Pause] Luckily, I'm hear and that won't happen. A place where women get kidnapped, wives get killed, children get drug into the ring and almost power bombed by the owners, best friends come and make the save, champions forfeit their titles only to come back six months later under a mask and hire a manager whose name is a noun and then live life happily ever after. [Awkward pause] Lopez: But I’m above that, and boys like the New Breed, boys like Kelly Flawless make me sick, he makes me sick. Every time he opens his mouth he makes me sick. You Kelly Flawless aren't a worthwhile challenger, you are sorriest excuse for a man I’ve ever seen. What type of man would just allow himself to walk into a PRIME, wrestle one damn match, and then allow himself to leap frog over all the hard working talent currently in the region and challenge Tyler Lopez, when he hasn’t earned or worked one damn minute for it? I'm not an opener Kelly, I should be the damn main event, and you are no where close to being on my level. Lopez glances over into one of the many standing mirrors. The reflection of him beams right back at him as he snarls. Lopez: You make me sick! He might as well have just walked in off the streets and been allowed to be handed the damn title, of course a man like him would probably accept it, you disgust me. You’re just a back stabber, you’re out to get me, to be like me, just like the rest of them, you think you’re better than everybody, and it’s disgusting. Lopez glances at the painter boy whose grabbed his buckets and slowly started to exit, just staring at ‘The Apparatus’ as he leaves, fucking bum. Lopez glances at him and then television, as he watches himself get manhandled. Lopez: And for those reasons and many more that I can’t think of just yet, is why I’m going to destroy you at Flawless. Not because I hate you, but because I’m better than you, I’m better than you on pure genetics alone, and no matter how many people you have protecting you, no matter how many secret alliances you form, and I know you people are plotting against me, it won’t matter, and I’m not worried about it, not in the least. Because I’ll walk in a legend, and I’ll walk out just the same, and why? Because I’m “The Scientific Apparatus”, I’m Tyler Lopez, and Kelly Flawless, you are not! Because I know you need this win, your whole career, your whole identity is built on material things, on achievements, and I’m not about that. Lopez states this so matter of factly, it even catches him off guard. As doing this whole rant, the look on Passion’s face has gotten more concerned. Passion looks down at Tyler, whose breathing is panted and spastic. Lopez: Passion… Passion: Yes Tyler? Lopez: We need to find that army, and I know just the place to do it. His tone defeated; not like the ‘Apparatus’ we’ve known for so long. Passion just sighs as Lopez gets up and walks towards the front door in a daze as he grabs his jacket. Passion just looks as she looks curiously at the canvas still standing in the room. Passion slowly peers around the corner and looks at the canvas and her face just crinkles, as she doesn’t know what to make of it. Like a piece of Picasso’s art, the little gay college student who lives inside of his van, just painted an amazing portrait. Down to every fine detail, Passion just stares in amazement as on the canvas sits a life size and life like portrait Of the PRIME's Alias Title Belt And underneath the portrait an inscription reads simply… …I’M THE SCIENTIFIC APPARATUS, I’M TYLER LOPEZ? Passion is more than confused than ever before…
