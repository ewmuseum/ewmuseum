I'm Groping The Deadline!

30 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 30th, 2006 He’d finally been beaten, and the title he loved so dearly would no longer cover his shame while he slept at night. Clyde sat in the backseat of the Clyder-Mobile, after having been driven home by a faceless moron, as he liked to call them - sometimes even to their faces - and wondered if he’d ever get another chance to hold that championship. Truth be told, he loved the adoration of the fans, and the fame, and the popularity that came with it. Not to mention the money. And, the cult. And, the women. Although Clyde didn’t know what to do with them - or so James suspected - he did have his fair share of womanly admirers, who would stop by late at night for hot sex and end up having to play Candy Land until Clyde fell asleep. Even some men, too. But, deep down, despite all the glory and power he held in his hands, Clyde was still one of the greatest people you could ever know. Don’t believe me? He’ll tell you, himself. So, why was it that, on the day he was meant to prove his greatness and bring joy to the world, Clyde was felled by the man known as Killean Sirrajin? Surely, the Fates had conspired against him, and robbed him of his destiny. And, so began his quest. He would not know it now, but, in a matter of days, he would begin waging a war on the Fates that would span the length of time, itself. And, that…is what brings us to our opening montage. Enjoy. I know I will. Ra - “I Lost Everything Today” The first day after losing the Universal Title, Clyde sits on the front porch of his home, as a heavy rain pours down on him. James appears after a few seconds, asking Clyde if he wants an umbrella; Clyde just shakes his head without looking up. After James goes back in the house, a group of kids run up the road, and, for some reason, two of them begin simulating the end of the main event of Ultra Violence. As Kid Clyde taps out, and the others stand around pointing and laughing at him, Clyde jumps to his feet and runs back into the house, sobbing into his hands. I lost everything today,I got beaten, so I ran away A few days later, Clyde stands beside the mayor of the town, a saddened look on his face. The mayor, short and fat, with a wet-looking toupee covering an otherwise bald head, shakes his rosy jowls in disappointment at Walkins. Some words are said, then the key to the city is forcibly removed from Clyde’s hands by several large guards, who then present it to the new hero of the town. A cardboard cutout of Killean Sirrajin. Everyone claps, and Clyde looks pretty suicidal. I lost everything today,I can’t find my way to yesterday Later on, Clyde is at a fair, while James attempts to cheer him up after his recent run of bad luck. A giant cotton candy in hand, James calls for Clyde after he dismounts a miniature pony, motioning for the depressed former champion to have his favorite treat following a horseback ride. But, as luck would have it, Clyde’s finger touches the pony’s puckering hole when he spins around in excitement over the prospect of cotton candy, causing a chain reaction in the horse’s central nervous system, which culminate in both back legs kicking Clyde in the kidneys. Clyde flies forward a few feet, landing unconscious in the nearby dirt, while a squad of paramedics quickly rush over and start performing CPR on him. I lost everything today,Out of time, and it is not worth trying Clyde stands in front of the toilet in his bathroom, gingerly pulling his shirt up to reveal bandages wrapping around his torso, cringing when he lifts his arm too high. Rubbing his kidneys with one hand, he carefully unzips his jeans, hissing in pain as the stream starts. The stream of blood, that is. As he lowers the lid after finishing up, Clyde looks into the mirror behind the toilet, seeing a shell of his former self. A single tear drops onto the handle as he flushes. I lost everything today,Lost it all, and I just feel like dying - Shrouded in darkness, save for the consistent flickering of a lone strobe light, the room smells like a mixture of cheese and urinary tract infection; an unknown source blasts your typical gothic music from its speakers. Probably Dimmu Borgir, or something fun like that. The beat of the drum matches the flicker of the light, illuminating the faces of three dark souls on a couch, and the bodies of a pair near the back of the room. Lying on their backs beside each other, they could easily be mistaken for clones of the trio on the couch; each one wearing white and black makeup, purely black clothing, with dyed black hair. It looked like the locker room of the DWO would if that shit were real. Bobbing their heads in unison, they each begin reciting lines of improvisational poetry, going from the left of the room to the right. As expected, it’s dark, crappy, and almost inaudible above the sound of the music. First Goth: Life is pain, pain is darkness, darkness is life. Second Goth: My wrist is a lawn that begs to be cut. Third Goth: I drink from the Cup of Anguish; I drink from nothing. The next Goth, lying on the floor beside the final one, stutters as he tries to think of something compelling and inspiring to say. Eyes wide, he turns his head back and forth, hoping to find some sort of muse; he finds none. A last ditch effort sees him closing his eyes and saying the first thing that comes to mind. Fourth Goth: It burns when I pee, and I think I swallowed a bug on the ride over. Before the last one can talk, the music grinds to a halt, and the lights turn on. The two Goths in the back sit up, their heads turning with the heads of their brethren, hatefully glaring at the pair of legs coming down the wooden stairs. As James Farwell enters the picture, he quickly takes in the room in one fell swoop: creaky brown stairs, tacky fake wood floor, patched off-yellow couch; some might call the color beige; and sky blue walls with white trim bordering the little basement positioned just under the front entrance of the house. A small rug covers the floor under the two at the back. Farwell: Have any of you guys seen someone named Clyde? About yay-high, blond hair, blue eyes, might be wearing a loincloth. He left a note saying he was going out with his new friend, Alex, and Alex’s mom told me they might be here. Fifth Goth: My name isn’t Alex! It’s Darkraven Nightblood! James very slowly raises his left eyebrow, then looks to the trio on the ‘70s-style couch. Farwell: So, Clyde is here? First Goth: His name’s not Clyde, anymore; he’s Dark Clyde now, and he doesn’t want anything to do with you. Farwell: …Dark Clyde? Listen, is he here, or not? I’m supposed to take him to Chuck E. Cheese’s tonight, and...wait--are you the fuckin’ Crow? The first Goth, looking pretty similar to Sting, furrows his brow and shoots an angry look towards James, but the fourth Goth draws the attention away from him. Fourth Goth: I no longer feel the need for a place Where A Kid Can Be A Kid®, James. My pit of despair is never-ending, and my shallow heart breaks with every word you say. Leave now, and cure me of my pain. Farwell: Damn it, Clyde; what’d you do to your hair? I thought you told me you weren’t gay! Third Goth: We’re not gay. God hates fags, and queering don’t make the world work! So says our hero, Lord Warrior. A shiver runs up Farwell’s spine, and he turns away from the Goths on the couch, looking back at Clyde. White makeup covers his face, and the black design is done ala Peter Criss, lending a slightly comical effect to the appearance. James tries to stifle a laugh. Farwell: Heh…ahem…Clyde, I know the past few days have been extremely tough for you, so I want to do all I can to help you get through it as quickly as possible. Come on, let’s go get some pizza. Dark Clyde: No. Not now, not ever. This is my tomb, and I shall lay here until I die. First Goth: Dude, don’t die on my rug. James’ tolerance for this crap has nearly run out, causing his otherwise light skin to turn a reddish color, and he finds himself walking over and grabbing Clyde by the arm before he even realizes it. Clyde puts up a valiant fight, but James proves too strong for him, dragging the depressed former champion past his new friends and up the stairs, keeping the Goths on their asses with a single angry glance. After the two are up the stairs and out of the house, an elderly woman comes walking down the stairs, carrying a tray of orange juice and chocolate chip cookies. Old Woman: Henry, I thought you and your nice friends might like some juice and cookies. Growing boys need juice and cookies, you know. First Goth: Grandma! I told you not to come down here when my friends are over! Old Woman: Oh, Henry! You know I don’t care what you do down here, just as long as you clean up after you’re done. It doesn’t make me love you any less, deary. First Goth: We’re not gay, grandma! And, turn the music back on when you go upstairs. Old Woman: Just call if you need any condoms, dear. As the old lady shuffles back up the stairs after leaving the juice and cookies, the Goths sit around staring at each other for a moment, before the lights and music go back to how they were, and everyone slowly starts bobbing their heads again. - The shower runs in the room across the hall from his, as Clyde drearily writes a haiku in his diary. “Fun Times At Chuck E. Cheese’s - May 25th, 2006 Woe becomes of me,Ordered a pizza, no cheese;I don’t deserve it” After that, a few scribbled drawings of people hanging themselves and getting stabbed fill the rest of the page. Lying on his stomach with his legs bent back and intertwined, Clyde doodles a fresh sketch, lolling his head from side to side. In the bathroom, James turns the faucets off, and the stream of hot water pouring down on him comes to an abrupt halt. James quickly dries himself from top to bottom, then steps out of the tub, giving his crotch a good toweling off before throwing the towel onto the face of a little dead girl with empty sockets where eyes should be. Having just missed witnessing a horrific scene, James turns and grabs his toothbrush, smearing a thick helping of Colgate onto the bristles, scrubbing his teeth as the fog on the mirror begins to dissipate. A demonic portal leading into hell reveals damned souls reaching out for James, their bony hands drawing ever closer to his face. Suddenly, he spits and rinses, plunking the toothbrush back down on the edge of the sink, before turning and leaving the bathroom without looking up. The demonic skeletons pull back into the mirror, and the towel drops to the floor as the little girl presumably disappears from beneath it. In his room, James finally puts on some damn pants, and takes a quick second to admire himself in the mirror. He flexes a few times, winking at himself and taking up a pose. Farwell: Hey, sexy. What’s a hot number like yourself doing in a place like this? I bet all the girls want a piece of you. Oh, yeah! Behind him, the mirror shows a demon crawling down the ceiling on invisible stairs, approaching James as he continues flexing and posing, unaware of the danger. Just as it raises a claw to strike, the button of James’ jeans pops off, causing him to bend down to get it; the claw of the demon harmlessly punctures the mirror. James instinctually jumps back, avoiding the shower of glass, then stares wide-eyed at the hole in the mirror. He looks around the room, but finds nothing. Farwell: Queer… Wary of the things going on around him for the first time, James takes another look around the room, then slowly walks away. Entering Clyde’s room without knocking, James shuts the door behind him, his face a little more pale than usual. Farwell: Have you noticed anything odd lately? Dark Clyde: What do you mean? Clyde responds without looking up. Farwell: Portals to hell, restless spirits, demons and the like… Dark Clyde: Our world exists alongside another unlike our own; one that is never meant to cross into ours. It is a world filled with pain and suffering, and I channel that same agony through my very essence. What you see are the manifestations of everything wrong this world has ever done to me. Clyde pauses, then finally looks up at James. Dark Clyde: Also, I did some stuff to a skeleton that might have caused it. Farwell: Listen, Clyde; you’ve gotta snap out of this funk. We’ve got a match coming up against a new team in a few days, and the only way to rebound from everything is to grab a win. We need momentum, damn it! Dark Clyde: How can I care about winning a meaningless match when I’ve already lost something more important than life, itself? That title was my everything, and, now that it’s gone, I have no reason to go on. Farwell: If you won’t do it for me, at least do it for the cult. Those faceless morons depend on you, Clyde; they worship the ground you walk on! You need to be the one to lead them to glory! James holds for dramatic effect. Farwell: …You need to lead yourself to glory. Tears well up in his eyes, and Clyde has to brush them away in order to avoid smearing his makeup. Dark Clyde: You’re right, James…you’re right. The only way thing to do after the horse kicks you in the kidneys is to get back up and pee blood, and get kicked by the horse again! Farwell: Yes! Exactly! Wait--what? Dark Clyde: You’ve opened my eyes to the truth once more. Thank you, Brother James. I shall lead my followers to the promise land, and take their money without mercy! Clyde pumps his fist into the air. Dark Clyde: Also, we’re an illegitimate church, not a cult. Remember that. Giving a thumbs-up to James, Clyde hops off the bed and runs to the bathroom, turning on the water and unknowingly grabbing the towel that James just used. After several seconds, Clyde manages to scrub away the final remnants of his dark days, leaving the bathroom with a fresh face that smells suspiciously like crotch. Back inside the bathroom, the mirror shows another horrific picture: Dark Clyde, but…actually evil-looking. Oh noes? Oh noes. - The following day… A burger flips into the air, and the smell of barbeque fills the entire neighborhood. Situated in the backyard, one of Clyde’s followers turns another burger over on the grill, as the entire ‘church’ mingles with each other in white robes. After several seconds, Clyde takes stage at the front of the yard, standing on a cardboard box. Walkins: Friends, family, faceless morons…I welcome you here today to tell you the Truth of Truths. God’s gold has been lost, but only because the power it held has now been transferred to me; I am the Chosen One, and God has shown me my destiny. I will lead you to heaven, and I will shield you from the ills in life! You will live forever by my side, for I am God’s Chosen Warrior, and his will is my own! As the crowd cheers, one of the followers in back raises his hand, which Clyde acknowledges with a wave of the arm. Follower: Uh…I don’t mean to interrupt, but I think Agnes just died. Anything you can do about it? A circle starts to form around an old lady lying on the ground, looking decidedly dead. Some of the followers appear grossed out by it. Walkins: People, people! Unbelievers shall fall within our ranks, but the holy shall survive! Just try to step around the bodies as they drop. While the sermon continues, a black woman from across the street stares out her window with a pair of binoculars, watching in horror as people in white sheets appear to heil each other, and the body of a dead woman lies amongst them. The whole smell of something burning doesn’t help anything, either. Without looking away, the woman calls back to her husband. Woman: Honey, I think we’re gonna have to move again…
