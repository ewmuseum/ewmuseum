Date: 2010 THE AVANT GARDE SUICIDAL IMAGINARY MAN SKYLAR MONTGOMERY WILL BEGIN IN 5 4 3 2 1 GO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! SKYLAR: (smoking a cigarette) Yeah, I'm in love now. RUDIE: HAH! Hazel don't like you. SKYLAR: Not with her... the girl's imaginary. RUDIE & CHAZ: What? SKYLAR: You know, she's not actually real, she only exists to me. CHAZ: So basically you're not in love, you're alone. SKYLAR: No, I'm still in love. I'm telling everyone that I'm in love and acting like I'm in love so I am. CHAZ: But the girl's... in your imagination. SKYLAR: Yeah we still have a relationship, it just means I listen to slow songs now and cum a healthy amount of times. RUDIE: What the fuck Skylar, you're an emo fag. Punch him. (Chaz punches Skylar) ...8 minutes later... SKYLAR NARRATOR: Me and Chaz have nothing in common besides the fact that we both know that fat bastard, Rudie. That, and we do both seem to have a common interest in looking cool and dressing sexy; but when Rudie isn't there to play the odd-one-out our act becomes limited to just taking turns to ask eachother "Where's that fat bastard got to now?" - which is actually a surprisingly entertaining party-piece and 1-in-10 times leads to a new conversation sparking from an onlooker who: (a), simply must know more about this 'fat bastard' or (b), has seen him and is definite that's who you were referring to. SKYLAR: He's That Fat Black Bastard, Rudie Bane*. (They find Rudie sat at a table finishing off a sale for a gram of coke. He slaps Skylar's shoulder heartily. Cold-heartily. Laughs, as Sky and Chaz awkwardly squeeze into the round-table-bench fusion and instantly begin toying with the ashtray and salt shaker. Rudie gives a mandatory gossip about his last customer) CHAZ: I saw one chick I went to school with breastfeeding today. Is it perverted that I got turned on by that? SKYLAR: No. Breast. Baby. What's not to get turned on by. RUDIE: Aw Monty you're a sick bastard. (Skylar nods) ...15 minutes later... RANDOM GUY WHO'S SAT AT OUR TABLE: (holding a pint) Yeah I don't really like the Tories myself, they're scum really. SKYLAR: (Turning to camera) Oh so he's decided to express left-wing political views to pander to fat black guy at the table. Nice. It's a shame none of these people would actually fight if I was to say "TORIES CLEARLY DIDN'T WIN. THE WHOLE SPLIT-DECISION GIMMICK WAS A BLATANT SHAM, LET'S REVOLT NOW!". Obviously. But fuck politics, that shit bores me; it's just un-entertaining people telling you that they're not entertaining you but they deserve to be on TV because they own you. Fucking pigs. Heh. My views are so left-wing they're extreme. RANDOMER: I'll tell you who I voted for. The Monster Raving Loony Party (pause for reaction) heard of them? Hahaha, they're awesome right, one of their policies right is that all currency should be replaced by.... chocolate money. RUDIE: HAHA IMAGINE THAT!! You could just eat man's money. SKYLAR: Heh, yeah, I'd be like "I needed that to buy chocolate!" (Everyone laughs) RANDOMER: (turning to Skylar) Hey you're a pretty funny dude. I'm Keith, what's your name? SKYLAR: Skylar. RANDOM GUY: Nice to meet you, Skylar. So what are you guys up to now then? (The Protagonist mumbles vowels) CHAZ: We're just about to boot actually mate, see you in a bit. RANDOMER: Oh. Well can I get one of your numbers or something? Skylar, do you have Twitter? SKYLAR: Fuck that. You can follow me if you want...just not on twitter. (As they walk out of the beergarden and back into the pub, Skylar's own last words echo in his head. That was a pretty good line, wasn't it?) ---- ************ Im the kinda guy people know is a star but don't know why. It doesn't take them long to figure out, though. Once they see how I move and my blood-spattered and torn jeans they quickly start acting a little bit more suicidal themselves. ************ ---- FOLLOW ME... NOT ON TWITTER. I am Skylar Montgomery and, yes I am a virgin (I like to consider myself celibate – could get bitches, choose not to). And yes I am a low personal-hygiene having, skunk-smoking virgin who still lives with his mum. I am basically a kid gimmick, but way more realistic than GCW's Clyde Fox. My left arm is the ruins of razorblade city – I have to keep that covered up most times. I throw on my long-sleeved Manchester United top, and don't even bother to do more than one double-take at my reflection, as I pass the mirror to pick up an empty cigarette packet (ten Marlboro Gold, one of many kinds to choose from on my desk), my keys, slip on my trainers and then go. When I'm outside I trace the mapped-out route of my local London area. This is the route explained in *my verses for the GTT7 first-round job I took to James von Doom. Basically – every night I go to all the afro-carribean barbershops after closing hours and scavenge the abundance of spliff-ends they have neglected to kill. They like to smoke outside of the shop, see. Sometimes there's even a nearly entire spliff sat waiting in the ashtray, the window ledge or on the weights bench. Today is one of those days. I drop that badboy into the cigarette box and, even so, proceed to continue filling it to the brim with the remaining slimmer pickings. To the brim I tell you. Eventually I have to remove the most prided conquest from the box as it's getting all crumpled up, so I stick it behind my ear like the badarse anti-hero that I am right now. Continue my travels to the second spot on route when interrupted by my phone ringing that starry polyphonic melody of it's. I answer and it's Roger; he's with Lacey and Louie The Dyke (two characters that were also introduced in the GTT7 verses*). They're in Shoreditch, it is Friday. I dismiss them because I am busy. But once I've completed the route and I pass the bustop on the way home their voices echo in my head and I decide I need to be less predictable and more impulsive. So I get a lighter off some nerd at the bustop, pull that spliff from behind my ear like magic and smoke that shit. Stand away from the nerd so he doesn't ask for tokes. Yeah, I've got my flasher in my pocket. I'm gonna wait for the bus. And I'm smoking the spliff, of course. Double-toking and triple-toking to maximise my high. I'm so high. The bus pulls up. The nerds get on. I get on behind them, flash the driver my flasher like psychic paper in Doctor Who and I walk on and go upstairs. He didn't even see the bottom-half of that spliff still tucked into my palm, still alive when I put it to my lips. I kick back in the front seat on the top deck of the empty red bus, smoke my spliff and continue being me. I don't have credit on my mobile so that's why this is impulsive. I dial the free reverse-charge number (in yankland you call that 'collect call') and send it the way of my mum. Yes I have a mum. I am a kid gimmick. You don't need any details about my mum because she doesn't exist – she's imaginary too. As you know of Wade Wilson and The Joker, my backstory is also one to vary from time to time. So tomorrow she could not even exist. But right now she's here on the phone to me, bitching about how I'm always mooching off her and how I should top my phone up on credit.... or, atleast she would be if she answered the reverse charge call. Still ringing. Dammit, I hit the red button and stop trying. I was gonna ask her to call Roger or Lacey for me, but now this is gonna be truly impulsive. I sit back in the bus seat and can do nothing but anticipate. The spliff is gone. We're here. I get off the bus. Walk to outside of the club they said they were in and find a corner to lean. People stare at me because I have bright ginger hair and the most distinguishable face you'll never see. I'm just zoning out right now. I used to call that "going to my imagination", but some time during my JUST stint I realised that anology was incorrect - I'm actually staying right where I am. And so now the things that usually only happened to me in flashbacks and dream-sequences happen to me in everyday life. I am snapped out of my zone by a shot of white light. People are taking pictures in my direction again. They start posing and making a show of it – I just think "Narrativium". They love London so much right now; to me it's just home. My mobile's polyphonic themetune wails again and one of them bursts into uncontrollable laughter. I answer it. It's mum. I tell her to call Roger for me and to tell him to call me. I wait a second. Roger calls me. He's inside the club and he's not coming out because he has plenty more "Love Heart" ecstasy pills left to sell. I tell him to call Lacey and Louie The Dyke, find out where they are, then call me back. I wait a second. He calls me back and tells me they aren't answering their phone. I feel pissed. I told Roger to call me back at the end of that last conversation but he never does. I stand around, leaning in my corner and not feeling that pissed off really. A two guys and a girl group have been staring at me the whole time; talking amongst themselves. "I've lost my friends" "Do you know where there is an after party?" Literally throwing sentences out of their mouths as bait to get my attention. I think they want me to talk to them. So I do - now I have a free cigarette. I use it to give me the steam I need to start walking away from the club, past the shop that everyone's crowded around and back to the bus stop from wence I came. I'm just gonna go back home - never pay to club and feeling too lazy to sneak in. When I sit at the bustop passing cars beep their horns and I don't know why. NOHOMO. All these guys are fags and I know I'm ridiculously hot looking; but I'm not some kind of Tyler Rayne who actually encourages the closet-faggots to look his way. NOHOMO. Another car beeps. I just swing on my seat and whistle loudly because I'm using a kid gimmick. I understand the burden of maintaining a precarious male ego, and I understand what happens when someone much cooler than you comes along and you can't even bring yourself to envy them. But don't ever become gay for characters like Tyler Rayne or Andy Murray; trust me they're faggots. If you ever feel weak, feminine and disempowered. Just completely disband of your male ego. Disband of your pride, the ideals of self. Then FOLLOW ME *beeeeeep* ...NOT ON TWITTER.
