The World Crumbles...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ReVolution 155 Seventeen tears. That’s the number of tears that are clinging to the emptiness that is your tear ducts. Your head is buried into a soft pillow, the pain and exhaustion becoming far too much for you this morning. Other mornings, you've been able to dig down deep and find the strength to help you get out of bed, but this morning has become a herculean effort. Your feet dangle off the edge of the bed, feeling weightless and heavy at the same time. Do something already, do something! They yell at you to do something, but you ignore their cat calls, your mind too wrapped up in the constant pain and agony. Your left arm twitches. Damn, third time in the past week. You can feel the bile crawl up your esophagus and just burn in the back of your throat as you try to hold it down. Your right hand grips the sheets as if you're in the middle of passionate love making, but this couldn't be further from that everlasting bliss. It calls you out each and every time, and you continue to cower in its presence. The weight of it rests on your shoulders, taunting you, screaming at you to fight, but you refuse to do so; instead allowing yourself to hide in fear. Sickening is what it is. But, you don't have the courage to keep fighting it. THUMP! BANG You close your eyes from the pain as your shin collides into the hardwood floors. Your teeth clang into one another, sending a shiver up your spine. For some reason, you just thought it'd be a smart idea to roll off the bed, and then realized that maybe it wasn't that great of an idea. The words just sit on the edge of your breath as you think them, but can't find it in you to use that much energy. The palms of your hands are sweating onto the dark-hued floor as you desperately try to push yourself into the corner. Beads of sweat roll down your forehead, begging to be wiped off, clawing at your forehead in an attempt to be noticed, but it's the furthest thing from your mind as you try to just make it to the bathroom and let it all out. You can feel the muscles tightening in your arms and you think that you're moving, but in all reality you're still in the same place. Thwarted again. KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK! Part of your mind seems to think that you must be delusional as your eyes dart to the door of your bedroom, wondering who it could honestly be knocking at your door. You tell yourself that no one's there. Nobody could've heard you fall out of your bed and care about it, could they? You shake your head, pleading with yourself to be stronger than this, to fight through the pain. How much longer though can you really fight through it? You've fought through so much as of late; can you really do this once again? "Are you okay in there?" The sweet voice rips through your consciousness, almost arousing you from the haziness that you're mired in. You once again glance at the door and can feel it bending and twisting before your very eyes. The image is surreal and unbelievable as you close your eyes and shake your head, trying your best to grab onto reality and keep you there. When you open your eyes you see it as it actually is, a door. You try to remember the words that were yelled at you moments ago, but they're fleeting with each possible second. Your voice is dry as you open your parched lips and try to focus on the task at hand. "Yeah... yeah." The words trip over themselves as they leave you, and you vaguely recall even thinking about speaking. The doctor told you it would be this bad. The drugs would leave you in this state of mind that you couldn't figure out reality from fantasy. Your neck is stiff as you look to your right and see a burning hole of fire in the sky above. Armageddon has come to get you and you have no way to fight back. You quickly rock to the right side of your body, hoping to protect yourself, but you can feel it coming closer and closer, its eye focus entirely on you without any other thought in its mind. The hot breath upon your neck sends goose bumps up and down your arms as you pray for forgiveness, to let you live, but you know it's futile. Saliva drips down onto your neck and you shiver from it, knowing the moment is close. And then nothing. You ponder in your mind if it's worth looking death in its face when you hear another knock on the door. "What's wrong? Why'd you scream?!" Scream? You don't remember screaming as you open your eyes and look around before realizing that you're alone in the room. Your left hand, still twitching, grabs the edge of the bed and you try to pull yourself up to your feet, but it's no use. If you had any energy, any strength left in your body then it's all being used just keeping you alive. The burning sensation in the back of your throat returns as the back of your head collides into the wall directly behind you. Consciousness begins to fade, but you grab onto the last thread of reality that you can see and open your eyes again against all common sense as the pain floods your senses. "No, I'm fine." The voice though sounds like you just spoke the words in your mind. Your right hand stretches out and grabs onto the edge of a metal dresser and you begin to pull yourself up. Part of you is amazed at the fact that you can even pull yourself up. Eventually, you're standing up even as the world spins around you. You're certain though it'll go away in just mere seconds as you stretch out your toes, feeling very distant to them. Come back, little toes, come back. You breathe in, the air feeling good inside of your shrieking lungs before you go to take a step forward. CRASH! A lifetime passes before you open your eyes again, or, at least it feels like a lifetime. As your eyes open, you can see the dust all around you, threatening to take over your body. You try to yell for help, but nothing seems to be coming out. You try to roll over but everything just aches. You make a mental note, one that you're certain that you'll never remember, to get off of these drugs before they kill you. Somehow you find some extra strength residing in a cavity of your body and try to pull your head off the ground, but it proves to be harder than it looks. Your head sticks to the ground and you can't for the life of you figure out why it is. Your free hand comes across your head, hoping to snatch the stickiness off, but it's no use as you realize that it's not something silly like gum or paste, but blood. A gash several inches long resides on your head now and the blood continues to pour out of it. You struggle to keep your eyes open, but you can feel the life being sucked out of you. CRACK! You have no heavenly idea where the noise comes from, but within seconds you can feel hands wrapped around your torso. "Come on, let me help." For some reason, you fight though. You refuse to fight, not longer will you fight. "Stop fighting. You're bleeding. Let's get you up off the ground." Instead of fighting any longer, you give in, and let the person help you. For so long you've been proud and kept your head held up high. You'd refuse to ask for your help, satisfied and destined to fight your own battles yourself. Now though, you realize that you can't do everything by yourself. You've kept this secret to yourself for long enough and you know it's time to confide in someone else. The hands hold you steady as you get to your feet. Another wave of nausea hits you as stumble forward, but fall hard on your knees. Your mind screams out loud, but you're not certain if the person helping you can hear your screams. You begin to crawl, but their hands are wrapped around you, and help you up to your feet again. They drag you with all of their might to the toilet where you can just feel your intestines ready to pour out in endless ecstasy. The chunks of food, green in color, splash into the water. Yellow liquid continues without end in sight. You can feel the life being drained out of you as you just continue to vomit. After a minute, you wonder how there's anything left in you as you feel more explode out of your stomach, hurling out of your throat like a space shuttle. You want it to be over, but it never ends. Instead, you continue to feel the painful sensation up and down your chest, tormenting you. It laughs at you and it begins to enrage you. A few more moments pass before you're satisfied that you're completely drained. You can feel the life being sucked out of your body once again, but this time you have someone to hold you up as they drag you to the floor and keep their arms wrapped around you. Your secret sits confidently on your chest, ready to be exposed as you know that you now need some help. Your head droops though as you fend off sleep to the best of your abilities. "You feel better? You shake your head no. How could you possibly feel better after all of that? You struggle to keep your head up as you open your eyes and try to look at them. Yet, everything comes out blurry as you have to blink a couple of times just to figure out who's standing there right before you. As the picture focuses in, you see the loving face right before you, and you know it's safe. Everything's going to be alright. It's the one person who'd stand by you in the face of anything. "Mama...," The words feel weird in your throat as your voice trembles from the fright of telling her the truth. "Yes, angel." Her words soothe your nerves as you can feel the strength returning to you. "I have something to tell you." As your mind focuses solely on the secret burning a hole through your heart, you can feel yourself getting nervous again. Will she still love me? You're certain of the answer, but still, that chance lies there before your very eyes. Failure. "What is it, Nicole?" You swallow hard, fighting with yourself. Then, you think about him. Craig. The love for him is uncanny and your heart breaks as you think about him, without him. Eventually, you win out as you look her in the eyes. "I'm pregnant." The fear grips you, but only for a moment. The image of Craig, holding you in his arms, fills your entire mind. Then, she kisses your forehead and you feel as if everything's going to be alright in the world. Except, it's really not.
