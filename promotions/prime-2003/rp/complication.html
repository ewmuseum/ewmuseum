Far as I’m concerned, it’s all the same. I hate this state anyway.” Keiko looked over her son’s shoulder at the, ahem, *unique* individual that was Sonny Gabriel Silver. “Is this your friend,” she asked Kenjiro, who promptly nodded. “Mom, this is Sonny. He likes to be called Mr. Silver, though.” In an attempt at cultural sensitivity, Sonny bowed. “He’s helped me out a lot,” ITO explained. “We work together in PRIME.” “How nice! I’m glad my son found someone like him to associate with. ITO managed to choke out a “we’ll be right back” long enough for both men to head off in the direction of the bathroom, only busting out in laughing fits when safely around the corner. I don’t know why I never told her. Maybe it’s because I didn’t think she could take it. She tried so hard to raise me. I was her only son, her only child. Her treasure. She doesn’t know what sports entertainment is.” “Poor deprived woman,” Sonny said, with a shake of his head. “In my opinion, you haven’t really lived until you’ve watched me decapitate some jobber with a chairshot.” “Heh. Hopefully, one day, when she realizes what I am, she’ll look back on that and know that my heart isn’t entirely black.
