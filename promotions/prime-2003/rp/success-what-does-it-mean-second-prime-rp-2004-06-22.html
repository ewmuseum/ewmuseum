Success, What does it mean? [Second PRIME RP]

22 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06-22-04 A great wrestling career has always been centered around one thing. Success. There are many different levels of success. Winning a particular championship makes you successful. It’s the ultimate reward to a great feud or angle. You win the title, you’re successful. But it doesn’t stop there. You need to retain that title, defending it against all comers. That’s makes you a successful champion. I’ve been successful in being a champion. The list is getting to big. Check my bio, it’s faster to read that than to have me spell out all I’ve done over the last four or five years in wrestling. I’ve been a successful champion before. But does that truly make me successful? Then there’s the successfulness [if that’s even the word I am looking for] of fighting off the demons that are associated with this business. I’m talking about the drugs and the women. A lot of guys get addicted to the pills, some to the point that they don’t know when one day ends and the next begins. I’ve been lucky in this aspect. I’ve only suffered one major injury, a torn shoulder back 3 years ago. I fought it off with a bit of surgery, a lot of ice pads and some Tylenol. Other wrestlers aren’t that lucky though. I know guys that get addicted to the Vicadin or the Oxycottens they get prescribed for an ingrown toenail. Weak willed bastards, that’s what they are. They have found no success against the Demon Lord of Pain Killers. I have on the other hand. Then there are the women… Yes, women. Now, I know what you all are thinking…… Boda, you have a reputation of being a womanizer. Yes, I do….. When I’m playing Boda. When I’m home, away from the rigors of the business, I’m not whoring myself out to the hordes of female fans I have. In the ring, I am the master of the Boda-Gasm… a phrase known near and far. But up to 4 months ago, I was a married man. And that marriage was made known in character too. The ‘womanizing’ was part of a gimmick. It had nothing to do with the divorce. I knew the sanctuary of marriage, and I followed it to a T. Stacey and I sited irreconcilable differences when we mutually agreed to the divorce. So, therefore, I have been successful against the demons of our sport. I don’t fall into their traps. But does that truly make me successful? I’ve never rated my success on these factors really. I’ve always considered the most important factor for considering myself a success in this business to be how the fans respond to me. In my opinion, that is the only truly way to know you’ve made a name for yourself in this business. When I first started in the indy feds of Florida, I got little to no reaction. Then a few months in, the fans booed me… as was wanted as part of my gimmick. Then in PCW, it took half a year for me to get over with the fans. It truly took me turning heel, joining The Red Army to really get the response that I wanted. I was successful in getting booed out of every arena. The fans hated me, and they weren’t afraid to let me know it. As a face, I’m welcomed by one and all. Boda has become a common place name in the annals of wrestling history. Whether I’m a face or a heel, the fans know respond to me. That’s what makes me truly successful. The day I can’t get over, and the fans forget about me, I will not be a success anymore. Coming into PRIME, I didn’t know how the fans would react to me. I didn’t know how much of an impact I was going to make my first month here. I’m about to step into the ring, in my second officially booked match in PRIME. I’ve been in 3 other matches really. I answered Hoyt’s open ‘Sinner’ challenge, to which he was successful in tucking tail and running, stating some bullshit stipulation. Then I beat Kincade, The Intense Champion in my debut match. I won because I was successful in keeping Kincade unable to move, outside the ring, as I got back before the 10 count. Last week, I answered another open challenge, this time to replace the injured Tony Rolo in the main event. During that Six-Pack Challenge, I was quite successful in kicking a lot of ass. Had it not been for that obvious fit of jealousy displayed by Kasper Sky, I would have won that match. I’m going to get off of Kasper for now, as I have a stiff competition to concentrate on this coming week. Another man that has been successful in PRIME. Steven Lee. Okay, so you’re not a huge success. Don’t let it get you down son. Some day, you maybe good. But not this week, I won’t allow it to be so. I don’t know a whole hell of a lot about you Steven, hell no one knows much. You seem to want to hide your past from everyone, stating it is the past, and you want to make your future…. blah, blah, blah. What ever. You are obviously afraid of what your past holds. So you won’t let us, your co-workers, and the fans know what it is. What are your hiding Steven, that makes you so Sinister? A murder, child molestation, you’re a transsexual. What are you trying to hide? I know…. you once had emergency surgery to remove 4 gecko lizards from your anus after a gay sex party. NO, scratch that…. I know…..you went to jail for raping a retarded monkey. Well, that might not be it, but since you’re denying letting the past be known, then I’m going to go with that theory. So, Steven, you too are a successful man in your own rights. You’re a success at being a sick perverted monkey fucker. I can live with that. I’d hide it too. That’s definitely something you don’t want to let out in the open. Steven…..in all honesty, though, I wish I knew what you were thinking right now. You’re about to jump into the ring with me, a man that is very successful in this business. I don’t get beaten easily, as you’ll soon find out. But there is hope for you, a tiny, glimmering sliver of hope. Beat me, and you too can be a success….. Or at least a step in the right direction of successfulness. [There’s that word again, I doubt it’s in Webster’s, but who cares.] But, since you’re afraid to let your past be known…… I know you’re afraid to be a success. Steven, try not to be afraid. It’ll only increase the pain this week.
