All I Never Wanted (ReV 120, Universal Title Match)

20 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/02/07 “So here’s where I tell you about how my life revolves around the Universal championship, how I’ve wanted to hold that gold strap since I was a little boy and how I’d sacrifice everything I own, live for and hold dear to get it. That’s about how it goes, right?” With a questioning raise of his eyebrows PRIME’s immediate Universal Championship contender, sole Scottish contingent and generally overlooked workhorse, Adam, lets a sly grin slowly ease its way across his face. He leans back, shirt off in the blazing heat of the Australian summer, and rolls his shoulders ’til they crack. “No, if this was a match I actually gave a flying fuck about winning I’d have been in hours and hours of meetings with the storyboarders and scriptwriters, planning yet another surreal, epic adventure that – Lord knows – would have been much more interesting than any of my usual day-to-day shite that no-one gives a rat’s arse about listening to.” He sneers a little at this as he lights a roll-up and takes a draw. He stands up from leaning back against the railing around the balcony he’s sitting on, squinting in the sunlight even behind his dark sunglasses. “Christ, as it was I still had to deal with a barrage of ideas from those guys: ‘We could have you waking up on the bus to Battle Royale, realising you’d not actually won it but it was a dream you had on the way there as an effect of the knock-out gas they use – you remember, like in the movie?’; ‘Why not skip forward a few years in the legacy of Zenith and have you ready to knock the pretender from the throne to reclaim the ultimate prestige in Ignatius’ honour?’; ‘A return to the West, the Stranger meets Wyatt Earp and challenges him to duel – it could totally work!’.” He spits off the balcony as he starts to walk, heading for the relative cool of the shade inside his hotel room. “Then Steve suggested rolling them all together to have a Western-themed Battle Royale based in a medieval village, complete with zombies and Nazis and cockroaches that talk… but Facey dropped in through the ventilation duct at that point and kidnapped him, shouting something about not letting us steal his ideas as he dragged Steve out the window…” Adam shakes his head slowly as he looks off into middle-distance, his brow slightly furrowed as he recalls the bizarre incident. He snaps back to reality quickly, though, the knowing smile on his face again in an instant. He pinches the roll-up between his lips and talks from one side of his mouth as he has literally to peel the baseball cap from his forehead to take it off. “But yeah the guys were honestly considering asking Chet for some serious fucking money to entice Iggy, Black Angel and the like to come back for cameos in a dream-sequence thing to encourage me – and I quote from the provisional script – to ‘leap the last hurdle and shed the final bonds of the Eternal Underdog tag’. Jesus titty-fucking Christ… fuck that.” He tosses the cap aside and runs his hands through his sweat-soaked hair, shaking them by his side in disgust once they’re suitably drenched. He curses the heat under his breath before finally taking another drag and removing the roll-up from his lips. “But I’m taking a wild guess there are more than just a few people currently thinking ‘Hold up, rewind… he doesn’t want the Universal Championship?’. I mean, when I was a kid I had the same dream that everyone who watched wrestling in the eighties did – to get up on that big stage and bodyslam someone for the World Championship. I believed I’d get a belt, the belt, until I was seventeen, when I actually got into the sport and saw I was nothing special in a backwater outfit running in a gym hall we couldn’t afford to cover renting two nights a week.” He shrugs a little and grabs a short-sleeved shirt from a clothes-hanger, he thinks twice about it and tosses it on the bed, opting to pick up a towel to wipe the sweat from his back down a little before letting the cotton shirt soak it all up. “Sure, I struck it lucky time after time, hopping from one promotion at just the right moment to land on my feet in another. But I never thought ‘Hey, I’m gonna take this place by storm and be the best’ and I never did. Call it a self-fulfilling prophecy, say that I never worked as hard as I could or that I gave up on myself – whatever. All it boils down to is that I was realistic about my prospects and abandoned a dream when it became obvious it wasn’t just unlikely but completely unattainable.” He throws the sodden towel aside and returns to dress in the shirt, frowning a little as the material sticks to his clammy skin. “I could tell you a story, confide a secret or pull a stunt to show you that the Universal Championship is everything in my life… but I won’t, because it isn’t. I don’t want what a win would bestow upon me, I saw what the Halo did to Chet and I don’t envy him in the least.” Adam shakes his head in disbelief and walks out toward the balcony again, leaning on the railing to look out over the city of Melbourne. “I’ve been on the brink of a Universal title shot twice here, no – once, the other was a Global contendership. That’s how fucking long I’ve been here. And, yeah, I’ll admit it; I was psyched about it the second time round. I had the idea in my head that I might actually get through JITC and walk out with a guaranteed shot at the title. Then I was twisted and contorted in ways I ain’t never seen and that moment of mocking self-doubt kicked in when I was aching worse than I ever had before. It whispered in my ear, told me I should’ve known I wasn’t good enough. I fought it, beat it away and reversed hold after hold but it was always there. And eventually… I listened to it.” He slaps the rail beside him three times in quick succession. “That’s all it took, I was out – I believed in something stupid I shouldn’t have and when I lost faith in that I gave up. It suddenly dawned on me that I’m unknown for a reason. I’m underrated… but not that much. I’m the underdog through and through: how could I be the champion like that? My first defence – which the challenger would be the obvious favourite, of course – would be against a man who beat me two months ago in order to get his shot at the title I’d hold. How much of a head-fuck would that be?” He draws the last from the burned-down stub and flicks it off the balcony, breathing the smoke out slowly. “Don’t get me wrong, I’m not walking out there to lie down and get pinned so I can stroll up the ramp and clock my afterward card for another day’s pay – far from it. I’m not giving anything less than my best. Why? Because Nova surrendered every single thing he owned and loved to get that belt. Just think for a minute how frightening a thought that is – that someone would turn their back on everything in their life for the sake of a piece of gold with their name on it? Now, think about what losing that belt to someone who had no interest at all in holding it would do to him.” He chortles slightly at the thought. “There’s no way I deserve that gold, not a chance in hell… Even if the rest of Fuck You beat the living shit out of me as soon as the bell rang, stole the belt back and paralysed me right there and then in the ring – he’d still know: Nova would still see my shit-eating grin reflected every time he looked at himself in that shiny gold strap, and every time he saw that he’d know that – without even really trying to – I did what he devoted his life to. I really want to see what that could do to a man…” He straightens up and flexes back, letting the sun hit him full on. “And then there’s what it’d do to PRIME itself: can you imagine what that would be like? Having a champion who really didn’t give a shit that he’s the company’s figurehead; someone who couldn’t care less about the belt around his waist, the one thing above all others that that symbolised everything the place is? Man… I’d love to find out.” With that he smiles again: a wide, knowing and certain grin.
