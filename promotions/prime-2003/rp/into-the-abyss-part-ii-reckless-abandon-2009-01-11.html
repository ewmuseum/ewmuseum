Into The Abyss: Part II - Reckless Abandon

11 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-01-09 A sullen, sunken-eyed gaze belonging to a gruff old bear of a man peered back from behind the misted glass of the mirror, mottled with specks of mold or even moss. The toilet was small, dank and had a reek of waste floating in the air even by the frosted glass window that had been left open allowing a breeze to waft in. Strange he thought, considering the glass was broken at the bottom right corner whether by fist or rock and thus negating any need surely to open it. He didn't care enough to so much as shrug off the notion as he studied himself. Not so much through vanity as curiosity did he find himself in front of the mirror more and more these days, pondering the network of creases and lines that had crept from the corners of his eyes and his forehead over the years like tendrils. He imagined the skin beneath his rough auburn beard wouldn't be any better and commended himself on letting it grow out, a decision made passively a few years ago before he was hired by Rush Pro Wrestling in an effort to recapture any air of menace he had lost from the intimidating mask days. A beard gave the impression of a primal rage; of your skull being smashed under a barrage of fists by an animalistic force...as opposed to being assimilated by the gas mask-wearing Hessian of NTICW, or eaten alive by the steel-skulled Hessian of PCW, a piece of attire reminiscent of Machine from the Family in Judge Dredd. In OSW being skinned alive and having your tendons sliced out and used to string some kind of hellish guitar was the impression given by the Predator/Snaggletooth amalgamation design and of course he still bore the humility from calls of "Get over here!!" and "FATALITY!" thrown from the crowd during matches in RPW thanks to the slice of metal he called a mask that was all-too familiar to Mortal Kombat fanatics as that of Kano. Nowadays a beard sufficed; less maintenance and management couldn't lay claim to it as a piece of merchandise...unless the Phantom Booker reared his ugly head again. Of all the earnings and achievements over the years only the reputation and scars remained, a shame considering with every promotion that hired him more scars meant more cash. If only those were dollars spackled and scored across his body and face from barbed wire, nails, fire, steel, bamboo, electricity and any other conceivable conductor of pain he'd be a walking million dollar man. The notion pulled the corner of his mouth into a grin as he examined the last of the blemishes and marks in view before the sleeve of his coat and the crook of his elbow drew his gaze. The marks were long since healed but the memory burned like an open wound treated with a splash of lime and a sprinkling of salt. Grunting with a snarl, he twisted both taps and washed his hands under the hot, ignoring his fingertips as they foamed with soap. He cooled his face with the cold and grabbed a handful of paper towels from the dispenser hanging squint on the wall and dried his face and hands. He regarded it for a moment, and something about hanging sidelong and forlornly around a toilet all day brought a depressing sense of foreboding to him. Sighing, he tossed the towels into the haven of cleanliness that was a beige bin with a flip-lid decorated with yellowed crust and dark red blotches and exited the men's room as discreetly as he'd entered. As always he left the diner with few glancing at him other than to remark upon his leave that he was one "big ugly sumbitch" to whoever cared to listen. He hadn't met many truckers who were fans of pro wrestling, and those whose sons travelled with them and knew who he was had stopped asking him for autographs for little Billy or Tommy a few months after RPW had closed its doors, ironically the addition of a beard didn't help his profile much. Traversing the courtyard past the fuel pumps the frost crunched underneath him where grit hadn't been thrown down the night before. A plume of steam rose from his nostrils into the cold air as he exhaled, wrapping his heavy leather overcoat tightly around him and nestling his gloved hands into his oxters, while his bald head lay bare to the elements. It took three attempts to slam the door of his rental car shut, and two to get the engine of the '99 Toyota Camry spluttering to life. With a wipe of the sleeve down the windscreen he jacked the heating up full and pulled out of the diner back onto another highway, heading down another road for another hundred miles to spend another night in another motel before another day dawned. Then he would step into the ring, face his opponent and it would all be worth it. He had embraced the spotlight upon many a return before but this time would be different. No fanfare and no fireworks, just in the back door quietly and let the infection spread from there. He always remembered PRIME as a good company to work for and one phone call set him on his way to coming back from hiatus. It didn't matter who was still there or who was champion or any of that, all that mattered is that he could return to what he loved after so long and still make an impact, and this time he'd leave the old flamboyant Hessian at the door on his way in. A nice reminder to anyone coming in after him however that they were still dealing with the Hardcore Monster that had ruled so violently through the years, he was far from over the hill and the volcano that lay dormant in his mind had erupted. All it took was one Christmas tree hitting one random guy to get noticed, and as he drove on he knew he was scheduled to wrestle against that same unfortunate bastard underneath the tree along with someone else, the name of whom escaped him. A few years ago he would have heard which match he was scheduled in and who with and been able to rattle off strengths, weaknesses and statistics in his head. Irritatingly as the years passed by so did facts like seconds on a clock. With his first match back a triple threat to bite into, he was becoming a little more excited every few miles and the little details like the number of opponents or even their names didn't matter when second nature would kick in and turn those names into statistics on a hospital clipboard. A little further down the road he began to overtake one of the 18-wheelers carrying ring equipment for PRIME and felt that little whoop of camaraderie at seeing a fellow employee on the road. That sensation never died from his time away from the ring, his was a life on the road like many others and on the occasions no one wanted to car pool even a glimpse of a familiar face put a smile on his knowing they were all one big convoy rolling like thunder into the next town. Flicking the wiper blades on to their highest setting they waved back and forth rapidly flinging slush and dirt from the spray of the MACK truck, and with a toot of the horn and a quick wave he pulled back onto his side of the road and sped on along with only his thoughts for company. The drive was pretty dull after that. But it was the most nonchalant action that caught his attention, he wouldn't have even remembered doing it if the fabric of the sleeve of his sweater hadn't snagged on a hair on his arm as he raised it from the gearstick to the wheel. He winced, scratching his elbow and cursing the damned sweater and the goddamn winter, but the pain brought it back to him suddenly. A memory of a pain not unlike what he had just experienced, and then the boredom of the drive didn't matter any more as the haunting images came back to him and he remembered all too well where the pain came from. "Wakey, wakey. Eggs and bacey!" In the next instant he felt his wrist being grabbed and turned over. Almost immediately he began whimpering, groaning too as his stiff neck creaked from the awkward angle he slept at sitting up. Tears welled in his eyes which, were they ever introduced to adequate light again, would have been ringed with a deep shade of purple and slightly crusted around the eyelids. Something stung his arm and all of a sudden he felt the sickening rush again. The first time it happened he had been ill and vomited down the front of his shirt, only to have it cut off of his body where he sat leaving him topless and smelling like sick, still crying and dribbling saliva down his chin that was riddled with slivers of bright yellow bile. This time he felt hot flushes throughout his body, while the air was cool his mind clouded over and sweat began to seep from his pores. He choked back the urge to vomit, but all he could manage anyway was to dry heave and feel his throat close up and contort as though it were being played like a broken accordion. Someone chuckled in the darkness in front of him and he felt the warmth wash over him once more before it bled out of his groin and stained his jeans. The thick odour of urine clung to his face and he sneered in disgust and inhaled sharply for a breath before he heard the sound of footsteps moving behind him and the familiar click of the light being switched on. It took longer than usual for him to adjust to the brilliant light of what was an energy saving 30 watt light bulb. He would have given anything to be able to rub his eyes at that moment, but he forced the thought from his mind as that voice whispered gently in his ear. "Breakfast is served." He had endured the torment so far and the heinous act of being drugged almost tore away the last shred of moral fibre. He had endured so many ups and so many more downs in life however that sometimes he couldn't tell whether he was back up or still down. This though was the final straw, the bottom of the barrel and the end of the line. He understood perfectly well the consequences and repercussions of his lifestyle and the business he dabbled in but never did he imagine it would be this bad. Now he was as low as he had ever been and the only direction was up. He wasn't giving anything away to anyone, he was just a shell of a man now. For the time being. "You know, I thought you would have cracked by now. Hell, I didn't even imagine it'd come to this! My plan was to bring you in, ask a few questions...maybe knock you around some then get what I wanted and be back in Mexico in time for happy hour. But for some reason you're guarding information from me, which I am entitled to, which is rightfully mine. I'm telling you this...again...because today we make preparations. Today is the last day I'm going to put up with this shit, and today you tell me what I want to know, understand?" A sucker punch to the back of the head dazed him before he could focus in the light and colours danced before him again, ugly flying bruises of purple and green and red swimming in his head as he closed his eyes shut and heard the light switch clicking again. He was alone. He knew exactly what his captor wanted, but he'd die before giving it up out of pride alone. After RPW he had pondered alternative sources of income and with a little research and a cash injection from Bruce Shanahan, one Violence Jack to those in the industry, had refurbished an old office block in Detroit and re-invented it as a nightclub, as the Underworld. A fitting name given the spacious hidden basement Von Kelsig had discovered a couple of weeks into renovating which would later house all manner of perverse machinery and answer the aching need of a few of the Motor City's more perverse culture vultures. One early morning after locking up he had been jumped from behind by someone hiding in a restroom, or so he'd imagined. It had happened quickly and the next thing he knew he was tied up to a chair in a dark room. While dazed and in transit to his current location he could make out talk of money and old debt, and a name that sent a chill through his bones which pieced the puzzle together a little better. James Carlisle. He couldn't remember the date, the time or where it had happened but he remembered as vividly as high definition the noise that had gurgled from Carlisle's throat and the shape his neck had twisted into as he had the life squeezed from him. He remembered the look of fear in a dying man's eyes and he thought at that moment he was free from the ghosts that had haunted him ever since the day his family had died. Everyone has their demons and waking up in that room on the first day he wondered now if they too had theirs. It was obvious Carlisle was in charge of whatever operation he was running but he had to answer to someone, in a dog eat dog world like his everyone did. Now that dog was chewing on him like a bone, trying to suck every last bit of marrow right out of him. Between being sick and coming down and feeling like shit Von Kelsig thought about what he had done. He had cut off a very large branch on the money tree. Whether Carlisle had reported back to his superior before his death or someone else knew something, Von Kelsig had been targeted by this man who had left him here like this rather than turn him into the police or kill him. This man knew who he was or what he was worth or something of value about him to keep him for himself, there was a lot of lost profit to be reclaimed. Being told it could have been anyone else in his shoes only lead him to believe the debt ran back to his father and in that moment the pride swelled and he was glad to be here instead of his old man, few could take what he was enduring now. Between these sparks of knowledge and the horrible form of torture he was suffering he sat in silence and let himself be lost in the darkness. Hours passed and the sickness faded like a dying flame, he was almost at rest and perhaps it had been calculated by his captor that he would be at this stage when he chose to burst back into the room, throwing the light switch and smashing something metal into Von Kelsig's mouth as the light dazzled him yet again. The force drove him backwards, tipping him over onto his back on the floor with a crack. His head smacked off the floor and never in his life could he think back to a time when so much pain hit him so hard so fast all at once, a shocking revelation given a past like his. "Time to face the music my good man." Came the dry tone of his tormentor. Taking a deep breath Von Kelsig could barely squint at the light shining down on him when he suddenly gasped as freezing cold water was poured over him. A sponge smacked him in the face as his captor scrubbed hard, wiping away the sleep and crust and dried bits of crap that clung to his face. "I want you to be nice and fresh for what's about to happen, so you won't think it's some kind of fuzzy wuzzy fuckin' nightmare and maybe you'll get the hint that I'm not fucking around any more." Spluttering on a nose full of water and shaking badly from the cold, Von Kelsig looked up at his captor and was shocked to see his face. He gazed at the man as he washed off the last of the dirt from his face and grabbed the chair, hauling Von Kelsig back to an upright position. Still he couldn't take his eyes off this man. He was almost as tall as Von Kelsig and a lot wider. A hell of a lot wider. His head was a fleshy pock-marked mass atop a neck hidden somewhere beneath a roll of stubbled flab under his chin. Wire-framed glasses straddled a thick nose set in a snarl above a gnarled mouth of yellowish teeth and a greasy fat bottom lip. His eyes were wide and his pupils tiny, and a head of thinning hair had been greased back with what Von Kelsig imagined at first to be boot polish. He wore a pair of boots in the style of Cat or Timberland though the logo was hidden by the bottoms of his blue stonewashed jeans. A red and black chequered shirt adorned his body, the front marked here and there with food stains and something darker and more clotted that didn't have an edible look about it. He looked scarily like Principal McVicker from the Beavis and Butthead cartoons. "Take a long hard fucking look pal. Why not? Dead men tell no tales. Get an eyeful while you still can you piece of shit." His captor spat at him. "Who are you?" Von Kelsig managed to croak after a few minutes. "The grim fucking reaper you asshole! You have no idea who I am but you're about to find out what I'm capable of!" Gaily he produced what Von Kelsig realized had hit him when his captor had first entered. A wielding torch. Retrieving a zippo from his pants pocket, the man in the chequered shirt turned on the gas and lit the flame in front of Von Kelsig's face, his eyes widening in stark realization. "Last chance. Jimmy Carlisle's last report to me mentioned dealing with you in a bad way. Jimmy Carlisle turns up dead and I have to hear from one of his fucking goons who heard it through a goddamn crack whore that she seen a guy looks just like you all dressed up like the devil leaving his place. That's how I know it was you, and that's why I'm the only one that knows it. I know all about your shithead father and his money troubles, I know what happened to them and I know you're the dumb fuck that walked out of it all alive. You and your father owed Jimmy a lot of money...my money. Word gets out he's been got good and half his clients skip town faster than a cockroach in the light. The other half either paid up or disappeared and turned up again looking like Jimmy, if you catch my drift. Then we hear from some skank about you and that's when it clicks. Do you know how much money you cost me?" said the man in the chequered shirt. "A lot?" Von Kelsig groaned and held back a raspy laugh. He was answered with a boot to the face that once again put him on his face. His captor stood over him, and grabbed hold of his left hand, staring at the fingers while glancing back at the flame dancing from the torch. "A whole fucking lot. But see that's where it gets interesting, because I know who you are and I know what you do for a living and I damn sure know how much you make doing it. That's what I'm interested in, because I know you can pay me back for what you owe and for all the hassle you've caused me if you don't feel like paying me what you owe. I only want to know one thing and you better pray...because that's all you can do now, is pray...that the next answer you give is the right one, because if it isn't I'm going to turn you into paste and plaster my fucking walls with you. Where. Is. My. Money?" His voice was higher than Von Kelsig imagined a man of his stature would sound, but some kind of guttural warbling noise made him sound like he was gargling with twisted metal. Von Kelsig closed his eyes for a moment. When he re-opened them the man in the chequered shirt had turned a nozzle on the torch which changed the flame from a sloppy yellow to a sharp blue. His glare hadn't let up one bit and he bore a hole straight through Von Kelsig's face. He stared at the man and felt cold and empty inside. His body knew what the answer would be before his mind did, or at least the adrenaline surging up his legs and down his back and arms would have him believe. Perhaps that was just the flame licking at his fingertips that did it. He didn't know how much money was lost with the life of James Carlisle but he knew it was enough that a mob boss, or whatever this man was, was enough to warrant threatening him with a lingering death sentence. For a moment Von Kelsig saw something flutter in his captor's eyes. Then it was gone and a smile hid behind a gaunt, cut up mouth. He had gambled with his life before and he was still alive, and against worse than a wieding torch. He hadn't given anything away yet and in reality he didn't have much of anything to lose now. He closed his eyes, rested his head on the floor and said it with a smile. "I don't owe you one goddamn penny." The man in the chequered shirt winced, his face going blank for a second before he straightened up and tightened his grip on the torch. "We'll start with the fingerprints. Then the dental records. Then I redecorate." As the flesh bubbled and turned black under the nails, Von Kelsig's screams echoed out like a wailing banshee. Those within earshot only shuddered when the noise faded shortly after and the smell of burnt meat filled the air. The man in the chequered shirt emerged after a while sweating and shaking with rage. Something else fluttered in his eyes.
