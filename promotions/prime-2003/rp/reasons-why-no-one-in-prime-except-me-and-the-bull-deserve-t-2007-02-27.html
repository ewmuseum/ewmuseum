Reasons Why No One In PRIME Except Me (and the Bull) Deserve This (Dual Halo)

27 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/07 The following is a promotional appearance by Captain Suleimon to prepare for the Dual Halo Match. A promo. For those too far removed from the spirit of wrestling, it's where a wrestler talks about the match, his opponent, or in this case opponents, and basically lays the verbal smackdown on what it needs to be laid down upon. The following may be offensive to those with no brain. You have been forewarned. Listen closely and I shall ask you a question that I shall reprise at the end and answer for you beyond any shadow of a doubt. "What do you call fifty-five men and women in the same place at the same time?" Ponder upon that for awhile. Fifty-five men and women. One match, with the prize being anything that your heart desires. Fifty-five men and women, and only the hearts of two at the very most shall be worthy of attaining this Golden Ticket. Who are they? More interesting the question though, who is not worthy of the Ticket? There are so many... Like Jake Hix, Tommy Osbourne, John Adams and AJ Palmer. None of you deserve to be within fifty yards of the Golden Ticket, you who carry the stench of that second-rate company, latching onto PRIME with your envious tentacles. You wish to make names for yourselves because your actions in your own landfill don't register even a blip on the wrestling world. Well you know what? You're but the barnacles on the side of this sinking ship, and I'm prepared to scrape you off. The same goes for anyone else from outside of this realm, especially you, Promo. You come here, trying to ride my coattails, bringing your false brand of pain. Sir, you don't know pain. You don't know hardcore. The only thing you know is failure. That is what you shall experience. Azala Zameer... I'm sorry, did you roam back into the ring in South Africa after you got lost in the Kalahari foraging for gazelles? I'm sorry you wandered too far south, but you can't eat anyone in the ring, especially in China, where they shoot on sight for jaywalking. Jonathan Winters and Dusk... fighting over a finishing move, and that finishing move is a kick? A mere kick to the face? Bah! I've suffered boils more painful than the kind of kick you two superficial attention whores are bickering about. What's next, a duel over who gets the rights to the "bitchslap" from hell? Or will you argue over the eye poke? You're both pathetic, and I will rain down much more effective and devastating moves upon both of you than your silly little superkick. It's like you're two mongoloid Greeks fighting over a spear when you're facing the Ottoman cannons of conquest. You especially Dusk, you think you're the cock of the walk because you won the Extreme Championship. I've seen more extreme bowl movements after eating Indian food. You hit a woman with a chair and think that qualifies you as hardcore. Peon, if you want to see true devastation with a chair, you can see it firsthand upon your own person by myself. Violence Jack... you poison the minds of people with your talk of Black Wisdom and the Old Ones. Your psychobabble sounds so much similar to the drones of the imams and clerics calling for these brainless Arabs and Palestinians to blow themselves up. They waste perfectly good manpower in sacrifice in ascription to religion. You make those who follow you sacrificial lambs to further your own twisted goals. The reason why you and these suicide bombers are so unsuccessful is because the men they sacrifice could be used as soldiers. Because you fail to realize this, you shall fail. Vangelus Olsig... heh, go listen to your Taking Back Sunday LP, cut yourself and spare us the indignity of your depressing presence. Trashcan Man, or whoever the hell you decide you are today, you are the paragon of the failure of the American medical system. Instead of looking to treat solely the bodily war wounds, we make up these mental illnesses and try to treat them with therapy. Instead, these patients are confused and they're made to think things that aren't true. So you get psychopaths like you, Kriegman. You don't know who you are. Would you be the same under Ottoman rule? No, either your charade would have been found out and scared out of you, or you would have been executed. Simple as that. So you can thank your lucky stars that you live in the "land of the free," because it has given you a chance to think your charade is cute. It ends here though as you meet the swift, steel fist of Ottoman power. Shawn Stewart... you're not a wrestler. This is a wrestling match. Therefore, you do not belong. Go home nicely, or else I shall forcibly make you. Devin Shakur... I still haven't forgotten the mockery you made my ISTANBULLSHITTIN' segment. All you had to do was one thing, one blasted thing. Cooperate and step off of your belligerence. We didn't want to pummel you, but you gave no choice, and all because you had to try and engage in a pissing contest with my cohort. Was pride really worth it? Was your need to upstage everything that our former company stood for that great that it was worth the bruises, the blood, the pain? You were foolish then and you are foolish now, especially if you believe you are out of danger. I have a long memory, and your sentence isn't nearly complete. I shall make sure you never get the chance to get one thing you desire let alone anything you want. Rich Rollins... please tell me when the Renegade decides he wants to return. Until then, he gets no mind from me. Jason O'Neil? N? Diego Delgado? El Rayo Azul? Bastille? Paul Cain? Who? Malachi... you believe you are God, a fairy tale, a being created to give small children hope for the cruel world that awaits them. You know what? I can agree with that, because your talent is a fairy tale. Pierce Lavelle... a man who got everything in this industry by cheating. You attained your first success under the serum. You came back and the only way you could get back was by aligning yourself with corruption. Now, here in PRIME the best you can muster are lame pickup attempts to hide your latent homosexuality. Is there anyone more pathetic? Pierce, I'm making it a point to destroy you and expose your career as the fraud it really is. Easton Hall... you have tasted my wrath before, and you're not done receiving it yet. Everything you've done to me over these last weeks shall be repaid in this match. After I'm done with you, you'll wish you had gone to prison. Garbage Bag Johnny... I hope you wash your body as your brain has been washed. Drones don't win these kinds of matches; they're fresh meat to be eliminated. Danny Ferguson... you are the epitome of American politics, like your Democratic Presidential candidate John Kerry. Your wishy-washiness shall be your undoing. You tried to get in on FUCK YOU!, but you were rebuked. Now, not even those who despise them will trust you. You belong more in the fake hills of Hollywood. It's just too bad that no studio in their right mind would take your ugly mug on. Asa Fountain and Sonny Silver... if I wanted to watch cartoons, I'd tune into your Saturday morning drivel. Jay Phoenix... 2001 called, it said you ceased being relevant then. Your fifteen minutes have long since passed. Chainz... the raping competition is happening at Sing-Sing this weekend. Hurry up and you might be able to assume the last catcher's position, because you certainly don't belong in a wrestling ring. The Ottoman justice system would have cut your dick off a long time ago. I may do just that if I can be assured that my hands won't melt in the process. Gabriel Afeaki, another cheater... the Prometheus-induced beating you gave me still stings. I still maintain I would have crushed you had you not been hopped up on the serum. Now, I will prove beyond the shadow of a doubt that you are a fraud since your precious serum supply has dried up. Adam... I've already reserved your room at New Bedlam. Tony Gamble, Jewel in the Crown... funny, the crown must be a piece of costume jewelry since you're a cubic zirconia at best. If you're what passes as royalty in these parts, then it should be easy to take your mantle next year. You wannabe gangster, don't you know this place already has its quotient of faux-Mafiosi? If you're the caliber gangster that you are wrestler, you'd have been shot a long time ago. Sun Tzu, Lindsay Troy, Karina Wolfenden... sorry, the moment I acknowledge any of you, I acknowledge that women have a place in the same ring as men, and that's just wrong. Go get knocked up and watch your degenerate lesbians on television and leave the warfare to the professionals. Killean Sirrajin... I think you had the right idea when you turned tail and ran the last time, ooh, I'm sorry, when you "got jumped." I'm sorry, I'm not up on my lingo for beastiality with moose. You're a washed up hack and your best days have passed. Face-Eater... all the weed in the world can't dull the pain that I'm going to dole out enough for you not to scream bloody horror. Jason Snow, Nova, Angelo Deville... the circle-jerk known as FUCK YOU! You men are the biggest joke of them all. You get together and demand that everyone stand and take notice of you, but what have you actually done since you got together? You beat another washed-up has-been for his title, won a sham tournament and basically bored everyone to death with your opiate quality ring spots. I'm sure you're impressing the fat and lonely Internet smarks with your witty comments, but they carry no weight with those who matter. You three men are in for a wake-up call. Then, the only ones who will be saying FUCK YOU are me and Murphy. Finally, Chet Worth, you overweight slob. You allowed this company to deteriorate the way it has. You are the reason that Murphy and myself need to come in here and purge this place. It's all on your head, although it was in sad shape last year. I mean, you won this match last year, right? Pitiful. This year though, I am going to show you how it's done. I'm going to show you how a real man wins this event and then I will show you firsthand who the only real talent in your company aside from Murphy is. Here's a hint... it's not Ferguson. So, now that that's out of the way, the only ones who are worthy of winning, me and Jack Murphy. When we are the last two in the ring, you shall all see an exhibition of what wrestling should be. Pure, unadulterated, unfettered by the fecal matter that people like the rest of this company has dumped on it. He shall not lie down for me. I shall not lie down for him. It is our destiny to make all of you heathens realize what wrestling is. It's not swerves, lesbian kisses, on-screen rape, soap opera drama... it's wrestling. Come Dual Halo... we're going to show you what you've all forgotten, show you why we're the only ones worthy of the Golden Ticket.
