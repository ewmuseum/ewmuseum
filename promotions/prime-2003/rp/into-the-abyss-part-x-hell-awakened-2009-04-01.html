Into The Abyss: Part X - Hell Awakened

1 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-04-09 December 29th, 2006 The man calling himself Tyler Rayne had proven his intentions to be noble on the front step of Wyatt Manor. The sincerity etched in the stranger's face with every crease and expression he made was apparent to Von Kelsig. It took slightly more time to convince Violence Jack to allow Rayne into his abode, Shanahan eventually accepted that Von Kelsig's judgement could be trusted. He had no reason to doubt him, although he had his rules and as Rayne entered his homestead, Bruce took Von Kelsig to one side to inform him of them. "When I offered my home as asylum to you in any plight, I made it perfectly clear that no one other than yourself and your partner were to know the whereabouts of this Manor." Bruce's words hissed under his breath as Rayne observed the great hall beyond a door to his right, while a claustrophobia-inducing hallway lay to his left. "But Bruce, I-..." Von Kelsig uttered those few words before Jack made his retort, squinting up at him with anger in his eyes. "I don't care if you had nothing to do with it. As far as I'm concerned this location could very well be compromised, and for what? No, I've made my decision..." Bruce sighed, and leaned in to Von Kelsig. From the corner of his eye Rayne spied Jack whispering to Von Kelsig, and held his breath as the giant of a man stood tall and statuesque with the exception of a head bowed down and weighted by guilt. Watching as his host shuffled off towards the great hall, Von Kelsig glanced up at Rayne and huffed, flinching suddenly as a bolt of pain ran through his gut and the back of his eye sockets began pulsing. Holding a bandaged hand out towards the hallway he gestured to his guest. "Excuse me, I have something to take care of first before we sit down, the sitting room is third door on the left if you'd like to make your way through..." He held his manners together with threads of sublimity, hoping to leave a less bitter taste after their none-too-sweet introduction. Tipping the fedora resting on his head, Tyler Rayne made his way down the hall. As soon as he disappeared into the sitting room, Von Kelsig darted forward into the hallway, stumbling into the wall and disrupting a rich tapestry depicting a great ancient war. Taking no heed of his error he fumbled at the door knob of the second door on the right and fell onto the cold marble floor of one of the many ground floor bathrooms. Kicking the door shut with his heel, he crawled forward and flipped the seat of the toilet up, wrapping his fingers around the bowl and pulling his head into its embrace. Moments after he spewed up bile his eyes rolled into the back of his head and he twisted around, knocking his head on the toilet bowl as he fell unconscious on the floor. ---- A pinprick. One tiny little sting. Just a nip. The thought of an injection scared him. The consequences frightened him more than that of half the bumps he had taken in his storied wrestling career. As a wrestler he had taken falls from heights upwards of thirty feet onto plywood tables barely an inch thick and burning with fire. From that he got back to his feet and scaled the cage to the top where the match was taking place to finish what he had started. He was a warrior. A goddamn force of nature. Suffering was bedfellow to bloodlust as he participated in wars where adversaries used his own vicious weapons against him; most notably a barbed wire gauntlet slipped over the foot of one opponent and used to annihilate his nether regions with a kick heard for miles around. He had launched his nemeses from sickening heights on many occasions with the mark of vengeance scarred upon their flesh. He had tasted the truly vile nature encapsulated in his enemy's mind and fed upon it to unleash retribution. Throughout his career he endured falls and cuts and breaks and fractures and performed breathtaking feats of agility and violence and won and lost and charged through everything in his path in the name of greatness. He was hardcore to the bone and was feared and respected for his actions in and out of the ring for the better part of a decade...and yet... A pinprick. One tiny little sting. Just a nip. In the split second he had felt that needle pierce his skin all the past melted away, every achievement and scar and injury that he had prided himself upon inflicting became false, almost embarassing. Spots and bumps were planned and executed with perfection, but the outcome was accepted no matter the intensity. The day Reuben Blanco forced heroin on him was the day he knew real pain. Nothing in the world could compare to the shock, the realization that came with a dose of that lethal toxin. No ache or agony could hurt more than descending into the gaping maw, growing darker as he fell from grace to a plain far below any such semblance of an existence, all with one hit of heroin. For all the hurt he had suffered and inflicted in his wrestling career, the single act of a needle delivering that poison made it all pointless, worthless and painless. The thought of the drug was filthier than anything he could imagine, and it truly sickened him to the core. He knew now what Hell truly was. ---- He awoke from the blackout moments after hitting the floor. Dusting himself off and taking copious gulps of water from the sink, he shakily rose to his feet and faintly grabbed the sink with his bandaged hands soaking, veins thick and dark blue beneath. Gazing into his reflection he choked back the shock at the sight of two blackened eyes staring back at him. For all intents and purposes he was as a drug addict. The only thing missing was the desire to give in to the hunger. Attempts to secure methadone or any similar weaning drug had been futile, without him putting his own name to a prescription he had no chance of obtaining any help. But he had Gloria, and he had determination. If he had that much he could survive this, couldn't he? Thinking of Gloria he splashed water on his face and spat away the excess into the sink before turning back to the door. He hoped at that moment that his guest had a damn good reason for being here. ---- "Got a funny way of working..." Tyler Rayne shifted on the black high-back leather chair as the flames licked at the mantle of the grand fireplace. He watched the logs popping and cracking, spitting sparks against the sooty brickwork of the pit. His breaths were measured, he was in no rush and it was this methodical calm that reassured Von Kelsig of Rayne's business here. "Life, that is. Must suck, y'know? Getting away from the ring and stepping back into the real world, cosy new life and then......" Tyler broke off, forcing a whoosh of air from his lungs and raising pinched fingers before snapping his hand open. The gesture was all too clear; Von Kelsig's dreams had gone up in a puff of smoke. He knew this all too well as he glanced at the floor and sighed, placing a hand over his gut. He had reason to be disheartened. Bruce had not taken kindly to Rayne's appearance at the Manor and had asked him to take his leave at the earliest convenience. He understood Bruce's frustrations and agreed to go as soon as Rayne had explained himself. He very rarely felt guilty, but asking Violence Jack to allow this stranger into his sanctum made his chest tighten and his face blush with compunction. Remembering the earlier discussions brought shame upon him. "I'm well aware of how much it sucks, Rayne." Von Kelsig replied dryly, clutching his stomach now and wincing as it wrenched painfully again. "Wrestling's a way of life...giving that up musta been like trying to stay off the smack, huh?" Rayne leaned forward into the firelight, cocking an eyebrow. "Don't go there." Von Kelsig grit his teeth as his muscles ached and his guts twisted. "Just get on with whatever you're here for..." "But I am, V.K., I am. See from where I'm sitting you're up shit creek without a paddle. You spent the best part of the last three months out of your tits on junk because you pissed off some uppity drug dealer. You had a new life, new focus and he took it all away to settle some old debt, and now you got nothing but cravings. PRIME won't take you back like this and you damn sure don't have the funds left to start over. That about right?" Von Kelsig snapped from his gaze and locked eyes with Tyler at the mention of PRIME. "How did you know I worked for PRIME?" He asked softly and slowly as his stomach turned and his oesophagus burned with a bad case of heartburn. "Google. There was one official site, two unofficial, a fan club and a bunch of forums all had information on a seven footer called Von Kelsig wrestled under the name of 'Hessian'. After that there was a bunch of pages with German scat and sacks and...well...you get the point." Rayne crossed his legs and clasped his hands, twiddling his thumbs and flashing a smile at Von Kelsig as his throat burned and his mouth flooded with saliva. The fire hissed as he spat into it. "So what? I'm an ex-wrestler that got screwed over just because I wanted a normal life. What the hell does that matter to you? Who the hell are you anyway, Rayne?" Von Kelsig asked, sneering at Tyler. "Would you believe I work for PRIME too?" Rayne replied, and Von Kelsig bolted upright in his chair, his shadowed face sporadically flashing with firelight and revealing a curious sneer. "Bullshit. The hell are you doing with the likes of Blanco if you're a wrestler?" He snorted. "Christ, are you slow or something? I could ask the same of you. I got my own business with Blanco, fuck all to do with you. The fact you're here and not rotting in a gutter should be your only concern. It's a gods damned miracle I even found you! Thank Reuben Blanco's ineptitude for that one. Fact of the matter is though, we're both from the same circle and by sheer chance and a bit of luck you're here and alive and well...and you owe me. Big Time." Rayne smirked, and Von Kelsig clenched his fists. "Fine." He swallowed his pride, nearly choking on his words. "You saved my skin, I owe you. What the hell am I supposed to do now though? Hm? I got no money, a habit that I never asked for and now thanks to you showing up here I've got nowhere to stay. How am I to pay a debt of gratitude when I have nothing?" Von Kelsig stared at the flames, his sullen gaze illuminated in the dancing light. "Well...two things." Rayne began. "Firstly, I can think of plenty uses for the man that ended the career of Vampir Nosferatu..." Von Kelsig knew he was being baited with that little ditty, but curiosity gripped him. "Trust me when I say I can find a way for you to repay me. Secondly..." Rayne reached into his pants pocket and retrieved a brown envelope. Von Kelsig watched with bated breath as Tyler leaned forward and held the envelope out to him. "Take it." He offered it openly. Sitting forward, Von Kelsig took the envelope and sat back in the chair, carefully tearing it down the side and working his thumbnail between the paper to recover whatever it contained. Tyler's smile grew as he studied Von Kelsig's face. Producing a folded white slip of paper, Von Kelsig crumpled the envelope and tossed it in the fire before opening the contents. His eyes grew wide and a soft exhalation escaped his lips as he read what was on it. "What the hell is th-..." He started, only to be interrupted by Rayne. "It's a cheque. Y'know all the money Reuben stole from you, that he made from selling off that property of yours? He's using it to finance what he thinks is a joint operation between his good drug dealing self and a gun runner by name of 'John Doe'. Me." Rayne pointed both thumbs at his face and smiled cockily. "With the set-up I've created it'll take months, maybe years for him to figure out he was paying two wrestlers for the privilege of doing business in a bunch of dead end zones around the States. You get your money, I get a cut, Blanco gets what he thinks is better business and we all live happily ever after." Von Kelsig ran his thumb along the long number written on the cheque. There were a couple more zeroes trailing the sum that turned the corner of his mouth to the beginnings of a smile. It was almost too good to be true. "What happens when he figures out he's been done?" He asked, glancing up at Rayne. "Then he gets done over. Like I said he's a two-bit crim, he's not John fuckin' Gotti. You're gonna take that money and make you and your little girlfriend nice and comfortable." Rayne stood up and observed two oil paintings hanging either side of the fireplace, one of Ry'leh and the other of Cthulhu. "Why don't you keep it? You can have it for getting us away from Blanco." Von Kelsig offered the cheque back, only for Rayne to hold a hand out, pushing the slip of paper away. "Your money's no good to me. What I want is for you to start by getting clean...then begin training." Von Kelsig furrowed his brow as Rayne turned his attention back to the big man. "Yeah, you heard. Get good and strong, go wrestle freelance around the indy circuit for a bit and work off that ring rust. When the time is right I'll come calling and then I'll be cashing in the ginormous favour you owe me. How's that sound, Hessian?" The fire lit up Rayne's eyes as he pronounced the name of the Murder Show, and Von Kelsig felt a shudder run up his spine. Hessian. He never thought he'd hear that name again, had forgotten the prestige it carried. Fear was a calling card with that name scrawled across it in blood. The combination of Rayne's voice and the fire burning in his eyes had awoken something in Von Kelsig. The unrelenting pain that surged through his body with the nagging craving was overwhelmed by another sensation in his head, the bubbling adrenaline building up and bursting through his veins as the legacy that name carried flooded back in his mind. The roar of the crowd. That overpowering call to greatness brought goosebumps to his flesh. His name had been roared by millions in adulation. The more his ears rang and threatened to burst from the sheer volume, the closer he felt to being a walking God. He remembered the people calling his name, and the very acts he committed to be rewarded justly. "Sounds good to me." Von Kelsig grinned, and rose to his feet as Rayne extended a hand. He took it in his own and they shook. "Good deal." Rayne said and turned away from Von Kelsig, lifting his hat and coat from the stand by the door and preparing to leave. "There's just one thing..." Von Kelsig uttered, taking a step towards Rayne. "What's that?" He replied, adjusting the fedora on his head. "No one ever finds out about the drugs, yeah?" It was more of a plea than a request, Rayne could tell by the look in Von Kelsig's eyes. "Yeah." He nodded in retort. With that, Von Kelsig escorted Tyler Rayne from the premises of Wyatt Manor. As he bid farewell to the man who had effectively saved his life, Von Kelsig felt a pair of hands slide around his waist, gripping around his hips and hugging him tightly. He turned slowly to find Gloria peeking up at him, her soft eyes complimenting the quaint smile on her lips. "Who was he?" She asked, sinking her face into his stomach and peering up at him. "His name's Tyler Rayne." Von Kelsig replied softly, bending down and holding Gloria's head in his large bandaged hands, gently stroking her hair with his thumbs before lovingly placing a kiss upon her brow. He could feel her gaze pulling him in, and felt the desire to lose himself in those big beautiful eyes. "He just gave me...us...a second chance."
