Becoming His Own Man

Date: My name is Craig I- The Duplicates "Goddammit, where the hell could he have hidden those papers? We’ve looked all over the office." Mallory Daniels poked her head out from underneath a mahogany desk, sweat covering her brow, disgust in her eyes. This was the fourth time she had torn through the drawers of her boss in an attempt to find documentation on his real identity. A calming voice from across the room attempted to soothe Mallory. "Stop being so belligerent, he could have all of it at home." Mallory’s palms pressed against the desk allowing her body to push off. Standing to her full five feet ten inches, her slender arms flew into the air. "Jennifer, he stays in this office approximately 20.3 hours a day, only going home to change clothe-" Jennifer Thomas rolled her eyes and walked to an overturned black trashcan, lifting up two used napkins. "I don’t need the stats, girl. Less talk more looking, yeah?" Mallory couldn’t let this rest, her anxiety wouldn’t allow such. "He’s probably in a surveillance room watching us right now and laughing. I wouldn’t be surprised if he’s holding the files we need right this instant." Jennifer put a hand across her forehead and said a silent prayer under her breath. "You and I both saw him leave, get into his limo, and drive off. If he comes back, security will let us know." "How can we trust secur-" Dusting her hands off and walking over to Mallory, Jennifer placed a reassuring hand on her shoulder. "Girl, your paranoia is driving me up the wall. They trust because they are ignorant. They haven’t taken the time to dissect our facial features so they can’t see the little surgical scars left behind. They won’t see us naked so they can’t see those scars either. Just relax and keep looking." Mallory gave a nod while Jennifer focused her attention on one of the six level bookshelves in the office. She started from the bottom row holding books one at a time upside down in search for loose papers. In hindsight, she really couldn’t blame Mallory for being paranoid. After all, if any of her delusional hypotheticals were right, she would be more sought after. Mallory was the wife of a fugitive serial killer and an accessory to a laundry list of other homicides brought on by the Smith family. Every United States law enforcement agency would give an arm and a leg in order to strap her down on a gurney and administer a lethal injection. If Jennifer got caught, she would get identity theft and nothing more. These two were crucial in the plan of Christian Daniels to help Devin Shakur overthrow this enigmatic figurehead. Eighteen months ago, Devin was kidnapped by members of the Braintrust and ordered to enter PRIME to find and destroy Tony Gamble. His own life was the cost should the mission not fully materialize. In retaliation, Devin enlisted the Smith family for assistance and the two sides waged war ever since. Christian took his time in delivering the first effective measure, eliminating Nathan Murray from the triumvirate. This left Shannon and Valerie to counterstrike. Incapable of deciphering the channels Devin was using for communication, they decided to rid of their problem the hard way. Multiple attempts were made on their part to kill Devin, but he had another ace up his sleeve in Rachel Smith, Christian’s younger sister. She thwarted every attempt by Valerie and Shannon, sending them back to the drawing board each time to formulate another attack. Hating to be on defense, Christian set off to have Rachel excommunicate Shannon and Valerie. In a back alley, she purchased a rocket launcher. Later that night both women were incinerated upon impact when the rocket collided with their house. No doubt the boss would have a raging fit if all three of his henchmen were taken out. Christian anticipated this avenue and had a plan waiting in the wings. Mallory and Jennifer, having recovered from surgery to reconstruct their bodies, rushed to the house claiming to be Shannon and Valerie. Down to the smallest detail, their appearances mirrored the Braintrust duo. Neighbors couldn’t tell the difference. Police officers were oblivious. Everybody was clueless. Even the boss wasn’t suspicious of the switch. Their task was to use the assumed identities as status, find out the operations and future plans, communicate with Devin and allow him to slip through the holes. Then when the time was right, get into the boss’s office and find out his true identity. Tonight was the night. Bossman, as he was nicknamed by the Duplicates, left for a few hours to take a cat nap and change into different clothing, leaving his apprentices to guard the ship. Now The Duplicates were in a bind and running out of time. Mallory rifle kicked the rolling office chair over onto its side and rubbed sweaty hands against her dress pants. "If you were him, where would you hide something that is of dire importance to you?" Jennifer spun around holding a law book by the hardback ends, letting all the pages hang down. "It definitely wouldn’t be showing for the naked eye to accidentally find." Mallory slid her feet across the carpet, looking for any fallacies in the giant lion design. She studied every inch, sometimes running over an area twice. Her eyes were clogged in a sea of red carpet while Jennifer rearranged books on the shelves, trying to find a secret room that might generate answers. "He’s going to be back any minute. We have to find something soon." "Girl, have a little faith. Nobody is perfect, we’re bound to find something if we look hard enough. I bet its right under our noses." "Fuck faith, we need luck." Mallory used her hands to trace lines along portions of the carpet. Jennifer moved to another bookshelf and started rummaging through volumes and anthologies. Nothing new was presenting itself and cheerful optimism was growing thin with her counterpart. Strolling over to the northwest end of the carpet, Mallory let out a disappointing sigh. They were taking all of the risk without getting any reward. On top of that, they would have to reorganize everything in the office how they found it, which would be another strenuous endeavor. "Let’s face it Jen," Mallory said, casually leaning up against the wall, inches away from a painting of six dogs crowded around a poker table. "We haven’t got shit and we aren’t going to get shit! Let’s just start cleaning up. If he comes back and sees his office fucked up like this, we’re going to have a lot of explaining to do." Her hand absentmindedly slipped to the left and into the painting. She couldn’t prevent herself from pressing against the portrait and attempting to save balance before tumbling to the ground. Jennifer looked over at the painting with eyes the size of basketballs. "Oh...My...God" Picking herself off the ground, Mallory turned around and saw the damage that had been done. The flimsy painting had a giant hole in the center, voiding the poker table. But that wasn’t what their eyes were transfixed upon. They saw a small metallic box behind the painting. Jennifer rushed over eagerly, launching the destroyed portrait onto the carpet to get at the box. Mallory swung a stack of papers off Bossman’s desk while Jennifer placed the box down and undid the latches. This was the moment they had been waiting for. Their fingers trembled slightly and they could each hear their heartbeat inside their head. Jennifer threw open the top and Mallory reached inside to pull out the documents. What they found was private and pertinent personal information about the individual they only knew as ‘Bossman’. His life was in their hands, literally, and Jennifer began scanning through a bunch of files. Mallory ripped open a manila envelope and held a lamented birth certificate to the light. "Jesus Christ" Once her eyes made contact with the name, she could have been declared legally dead. "What, what? Let me se-" Jennifer eyes caught the name and she imitated Mallory’s stance verbatim. Shocked eyes met one another and they raced from the room to their office. Nobody would believe this. Both nonchalantly stepped onto the painting, further damaging the once prized wall decoration. II- The Smith Family "Sevens and fours my good man, eat it and give those chips." "Are you fucking serious? How can you make a call with that when I bumped under the gun?" "You only raised the minimum. I’m not into that position bullshit! Fuck what those people on TV say." Around a smoke filled dimly lit room sat the Smith family on a Saturday night. Jason Hardy flipped his cards into the center of the table and put joyful hands around two hundred chips. Chris and Brandon Smith argued back and forth about the schematics of the hand while Michael Blaze pointed and laughed. Christian Daniels sat on a couch watching The Ultimate Fighter and throwing back a beer. He isn’t in the mood to deal with superficial shenanigans, especially after ordering Mallory and Jennifer to have some information ASAP. Jason looked over at the seven foot behemoth and beckoned him over with a friendly hand. "Come on man, they probably aren’t going to have anything before the night is over. Just play a couple hands. We’ll do 7 card stud if you want." Christian shook his head and took another swig of beer, sneaking a look at the telephone. He removed the biker bandana from his head and fidgeted with the ponytail until his hair was released, flowing down to the middle of his back. Staring at the ceiling fan, he tapped his foot impatiently on the old wooden table. Michael Blaze looked over at the television and stared back at Christian, pointing to the clicker on the top right. "You mind if I turn this?" Christian dismissively waved his hand and took another shot of beer, looking for the caller ID to light up with a blue or red color. Michael turned the television to channel 72 and saw a familiar face on the screen. "IT’S DEVIN EVERYBODY! Christian turned a lazy eye toward the screen, keeping his attention on the phone. Chris, Brandon, and company all slid their chairs around and like a group of fraternity brothers egged Devin on in his fight against Cozen and Lindsay Troy. With everybody in the group holding a prior wrestling background, advice should be sound and useful from this pack. "GO FOR THE LEGS, KILLER!" Chris shouted enthusiastically. "Fuck that, are you high, go for the boobs. They won’t see that coming!" Jason shouted back. "Is that one with the awful looking pants taller than Devin?" Brandon remarked. Devin ate a knee into the ribs from Cozen and a left from Troy. Screams continued to emit from the group of rowdy brothers. Amidst the cacophony, a small ring resonated through the kitchen. Christian swung his legs off the table and rushed over to the phone, picking it up. "What’s the word?" Christian put a finger in his right ear to drown out the noise from his brothers. He nodded encouragingly four times before removing his hand around his ear and circling it forward, indicating he was already sick of the formalities. "Come on Mal, give me a fucking name." Another moment elapses before a much louder sound emanated from the receiver. Christian stood dumbfounded, his beer bottle slamming against the table but not shattering. Chris and Jason removed themselves from the television fight and move closer to Christian. It’s not like him to show any kind of real emotion in a defensive manner, let alone enough for a bottle of beer to slip out of his hand. "Are you absolutely sure, Mal...Alright, I’ll get to work on it." Christian turned the phone off and took a deep breath. Leaning over the table, his face was bright red with a combination of rage and embarrassment. Chris interjected himself into Christian’s view with a hand wave. "What happened, dude?" "Come on, spill it" Jason added Christian wound his tattooed right arm back and violently slammed knuckle into wood. The television paused and all uninterested parties changed their tune. A heavy blood pool began to form on the white island table. "Who was it, man?" Chris asked again Christian ran bloody fingers through his greasy hair and started to realign his previous ponytail. "...Anthony" Everybody in the room looked at one another inquisitively. They all tried to recall that name. Was it a relative of someone they killed looking for some revenge, a jealous husband, someone from one of the plethora of law enforcement agencies? Christian pointed to the television at Devin Shakur. Chris thought out loud "Anth...NO" Brandon looked at the television and back at Chris "He’s d-" Jason pointed at Christian "You-" Christian finished with a dejected "Yeah" before taking a hold of the phone and storming out of the kitchen onto the back porch, already punching numbers. Everybody else looks at Devin on the television being escorted to the back by security immediately after the conclusion of Revolution 160. III- Devin Shakur With the assistance of security and referees from the back, a battered and bloody Devin Shakur tried to walk on his own without crumbling into a heap. The kicks and punches of Lindsay Troy and Cozen weren’t exactly those of a five year old on the playground. He would have to be in the hospital yet again, which had become his second home away from the Raleigh mansion. Taking another couple steps on his own, the referees began to give the Number One contender a little breathing room. Still clutching his ribs, Devin managed to make his way through the curtain and down the nearby stairs. Throwing his right hand back, Devin resisted any further assistance and used the wall as his means of support to his locker room. Thankfully, he got a spot real close to the gorilla position. Spitting blood behind him, Devin knew this one would hurt for the next couple days. He had received worst beatings but never before a match so vital to his career. All of 2008 was based around this match and optimal performance was a necessity just for survival. He would need to make sure that Sunny had the hot tub working before he got back to the house. After throwing open the locker room door, a light buzzing garnered Devin’s attention, causing his eyes to scan around the room for the source. Getting louder by the second, his eyes moved back toward his jacket. He reached inside to retrieve a cellular phone. With a grunt, Devin stretched out on the couch and flipped open the phone without bothering to look at the caller ID. "This better be fucking brilliant because I’m about to pass out and have a wet dream." "I’ve got a name on Braintrust Boss." "Christian, shit!" Devin quickly rose to a sitting position, repressing a cry of pain in order to swing his legs around onto the ground. "Who is it?" Christian sat against his porch door, eyes nonchalantly fixed on the ocean waves crashing into the shore. "You won’t believe it when I tell you." "CD, man, I trust you. Is it Gamble?" "No, far worse than that" "DeAngelis?" "Worse" Devin burrowed his eyebrows in confusion and put his right fist on his chin in contemplation. There wasn’t anybody around who could be worse than either DeAngelis or Gamble. Nobody had that kind of pull in his life. "Well I’m fucking stumped then, tell me." "...It’s your father" Medusa’s glare couldn’t turn anybody more rigid than Devin Shakur right now. He couldn’t think. He couldn’t breathe. He couldn’t see anything. He couldn’t hear anything. Everything became a blur. Keeping its erect form, his body tilted sideways off the couch onto the floor. With no wherewithal for protection, Devin’s head slammed viciously into the cement. Christian put his hands over his face and let the cellular phone bounce off the chipped wood. "Oh God, SOMEONE GET A MEDIC IN HERE! SHAKUR IS DOWN AND BLEEDING!" Through the window Chris and Michael stared at Christian muttering under his breath. "We shoulda finished the job. We shoulda finished the job. We shoulda finished the job." IV- Devin Shakur Devin didn’t remember what cards he played on the nurses and doctors that allowed him to leave Milwaukee and return home to Raleigh. Honestly, none of that mattered. Material injuries that appeared on his body were irrelevant to the information that just invaded his life. The enigmatic individual who had been manipulated Devin for the last eighteen months was his own father. One both he and Christian presumed had died over 15 years ago when Devin stabbed him repeatedly and placed him inside a coffin. "How...De...The fuck?" Lying sprawled across his master bed, Devin was still at a loss for words. He still looked identical to the way people saw him exit Revolution 160. The trademark black dress shirt clung to his body along with scabs and bruises all over his face courtesy of Lindsay Troy and Cozen. Devin held an aching hand against his right side while staring aimlessly at the ceiling above. "Dev...Huuuuuuu! I’m as lost as you are. I dunno what the hell to tell you and I have the documents in front of me." Christian sat on the same back porch, having not left since Chris and Jason managed to talk him into a quasi comfortable chair. Nothing would be comfortable to the burly biker for a long time. Grasping a bottle of vodka in his left hand, Christian cradled the phone between his ear and shoulder while staring down at a list of papers. "He looked dead to me when I came over there: wasn’t breathing, wasn’t moving, wasn’t doing any of that. You did all that you were supposed to do to kill a man." Devin scoffed while switching positions to his back on the bed. "The man was dead, Christian. I...Shit, do you know how blood I was covered in? Nobody could survive all those stabs." "...Like I said, I dunno what to tell you. Even at 15, you ain’t have to cut him deep to get results. The amount of times alone...He shoulda lost enough blood to...Dugh! Christian launches a steel toed boot into a helpless table some eight feet away, shattering a potted plant in the process. He reached over and violently grabbed the bottle of near empty bottle of vodka and swigged the remaining contents. Devin shared the frustration of his mentor. "All we had to do was check one more goddamnfucking time and none of this bullshit would fucking be fucking happening. I could have fucking kept going as a goddamn CEO, living life man, and instead that Sumbitch can’t keep his fucking nose out of my shit." "..." Christian flicked the bottle of vodka over his shoulder, not concerned about its eventual destruction. Rocking back and forth, both men were at a loss for words after Devin’s profane outburst. Christian had never lost a battle before or been drawn into a sequel from anybody. He always had the answers to every question and plans for every scenario. Now his mind was like scattered pieces of a jigsaw puzzle across the living room table. An eternity seemed to pass before Devin composed himself enough to speak. "...So what the hell do we do now?" All Devin could hear was screeching from the rocking chair Christian sat in. The big man put his legs on the wooden railing and dropped his rugged face into his chest, looking for some possible resolution to the dilemma. Massaging his temple did little good to ease the rage, but he believed an answer would materialize if enough of a window was left opened for lucid ideas to enter. For five minutes he held that position, eliminating all deterrents from his mind. He needed an element of surprise that would prevent Anthony Shakur from digging into his pockets to find another way at his son. There had to be one quick attack that was all or nothing, consider everything and risk the consequences in order to obtain liberation. That’s when the solution presented itself. Before blurting the answer out, Christian realized he would be putting a lot of heads on the chopping block. Should this idea fail, the entire plan would blow up in his face. If Anthony would go this far to torment his own son, what would he do to the people who assisted Devin? "...Alright, I’ve got something." "Let me hear it, man." Christian exhaled deeply and held his tongue for as long as possible. "You are gonna have to go there and meet him face to face, and end it for good." Like a fifty foot wave crashing onto a surfer, Devin was rocked hard. He wanted to curl up into a ball and crawl underneath a rock after hearing Christian utter those words. Devin groaned loudly and uncomfortably turned onto his other side, facing a plain white wall. Words couldn’t formulate in his mind for his mouth to speak, so Devin became captivated with the blandness of the wall. "I really don’t like it anymore than you but we can’t leave shit to chance this time." Logically, Christian was right and Devin knew it. If Anthony managed to slip through the cracks one more time, a scorched Earth philosophy would be kindergarten compared to what he would unleash. "All you gotta do is kill the sumbitch. You don’t gotta look hard for motivation. He abused you fifteen years. I think you can find it in you to do what you gotta do." With tears streaming down his cheeks, Devin shifted his gaze from the wall back to the ceiling. The fact that Anthony still remained alive was unsettling unto itself. Now, he would have to confront the man who made him wanted to commit suicide on so many cold nights. All of those painful memories would manipulate their way back to the forefront. Clearing the tears from his face temporarily, Devin began coughing. The front of his head throbbed, an indication that a stress headache was inevitable. With his voice a little higher in pitch than he would like, Devin finally responded. "Yeah" Christian breathed a little sigh of relief. "Just...Leave everything else to me. I’ll call the girls and have shit set up so you only have to go in there and handle your business." "Yeah" "I’ll call you when everything is set..." Christian went to hang up, his finger was ready to press down on the End button, but something drew the phone back up to his ear. He had broken Devin down with this blitzkrieg of information and in order to fulfill the task ahead, he had to be built back up. "You can do this, I believe in you. We all do. You know we wouldn’t have taken you in all those years ago if we didn’t believe you had something special. You’ve come a long way Devin. I’m more proud of you can than anybody can imagine. Once you get done with this, we’ll bring you down and all of us can kick it like the old days. You remember those? All day we’d sit around, pop beer, play cards, hit 100 miles on the bikes..." Through the tears and emotional stress, a smile force its way to the surface. Devin kept his right hand underneath his eyes to wipe away the tears but he was smiling. "I remember" "Now make this happen for me." Christian finally hit End on his end while Devin rose to his feet and left the room. He needed solitude. V- Devin Shakur Stamping out remnants of Marlboro cigarette against his wrestling boot, Devin breathed heavily into the Raleigh afternoon. Smoking wasn’t something he did often, but desperate times called for desperate measures. In just a few short hours he would have to endure one of the most gut wrenching moments of his life. Devin would have to become Frankenstein and kill his creator. From the rooftop, Devin saw the comings and goings of the neighborhood. In a lot off to the left, a group of little kids were playing baseball. No one could bribe the smile away from their faces as they ran around the bases and taunted opposing team members with school yard chants. A few yards behind the home plate fence, a boy and a girl leaned up against a concession stand. The girl dropped her head and twisted her body away from the boy while giggling. He leaned in closer and gave a peck on the cheek. She managed to break free from his grasp, but the persistent little boy started chasing her around the concession stand. Lighting up another cigarette and looking to the right, Devin saw a group of teenagers with spiked hair standing beside the half pipe of a skate park. They rocked out playing air guitar while other kids performed 360s and whatever innovative things they could do with a skateboard. Devin flicked the cigarette over the side and dug his hands into blood stained Armani dress pants. Looking straight ahead, Devin saw a massive group of almost adults gathered around a swimming pool behind a large two story home. Every inch of the backyard was littered with kids shouting and jumping around. One kid emerged from the balcony of the second floor and proceeded to climb atop the wooden railing. With his friends screaming "JUMP, JUMP, JUMP", the kid leaped into the air and plummeted down into the deep end of the pool. A collective "YEAH" emerged from everybody around the pool. All this time Devin’s eyes have been cold. Inside the pockets of his pants, his hands slowly began to ball into fists and the shocked nerves he once possessed melted away. From behind, Sun Tzu wrapped her arms around Devin’s waist. He barely noticed her presence, eyes never leaving the sight of the college party being held. All of the sounds from every little niche faded away, leaving Devin with only his thoughts. You took all of this away from me, Dad. You destroyed my chance to be a normal kid because of your inability to be a good parent. Devin removed his hands from inside the pockets and wrapped them around Sun Tzu’s. You aren’t going to take away what I have now. I’m ready to end you once and for all. When Devin heard the phone ring inside the house, he knew who it was and what he had to do. VI- Gaining courage The putrid smell of cigarettes emanated from Devin’s pickup truck while he drove toward the office of the Braintrust. Christian told him to drive something that would look like an ordinary employee car so he wouldn’t stand out. Hanging a quick left in order to beat the red light, Devin saw the very top floors of the building ahead. It would only be two or three minutes before he would encounter his father for the first time in over fifteen years. Devin removed his right hand from the steering wheel and undid the top button on his dress shirt. After burrowing his eyebrows in confusion about the need to perform the aforementioned task, Devin adjusted the air conditioner in the truck up three notches to ‘High’. When his hand moved back to reaffirm hold of the steering wheel, Devin noticed his fingers had a slight tremble about them. He used his other hand to smack at the knuckles, believing that would eliminate the nuisance. After switching lanes and increasing his speed to 45, Devin held his hand away from the wheel again. This time, every part of his hand was shaking. Devin smacked harder and clutched the steering wheel tight. Making one more lane shift, Devin came to a halt at the stoplight which led to the private road of the giant campus. The uncomfortable silence began to manifest while Devin tapped the wheel impatiently. Up to this point, his mind had been focused solely on destroying his father. Doubt slowly slithered into his mind and all of the irrelevant questions he had tried to repress finally had their moment in the spotlight. What will I say when I see him? What will he look like? Has he seen me on television? Does he know about Christian? Will I have the ability to go through with this? What will happen if I don’t? I can turn back now. No, you can’t. What will Christian say? "SHUT UP! SHUT UP! SHUT UP!" Devin threw fists into the sides of his head in an attempt to eliminate his overactive imagination. BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! "HEY! MOVE YOUR ASS, YA IDIOT! THE LIGHT TURNED GREEN TEN YEARS AGO!" Devin looked into his rear view mirror to see an angry motorist waving his fist in the air. He stepped on the gas pedal and gunned the pickup around the corner and down a winding road that led to a small security tower. Ahead of him, two cars were moving slowly and waving to the guard, who responded with the same gesture. Playing it as cool as he possibly could, Devin fell in line with the other cars and repeated their gesture to the guard. He seemed too worried with what was playing on the small television set behind him to do any thorough identification checks. Everything from this point to finding the building was rudimentary. He followed the map exactly how Christian instructed him to do so: Three lefts, go straight after the third left until a giant blue diamond appears, hang a right and that’s the building. Parking in a visitor’s space, Devin put his foot on the brake, shifted the gear into Park, turned the truck off, and brought up the emergency break. Trying to convince himself that no preparation time was necessary, he opened the driver’s side door and stepped onto the pavement. Alright, this is it Thanks to spaghetti legs, the rest of his body proceeded to join him on said pavement. His legs fell completely out from underneath him. Scrambling to ensure nobody saw his blunder Devin jumped back into the truck and shut the door. This was going to take a little more time. Looking into the backseat, Devin found something that would ease his fears and make the process a little easier, a man by the name of Jim Beam. VII- The End of the Road Fifteen minutes and the equivalent of three drinks later, Devin stepped from the vehicle and managed to stay on his feet. Turning around and shutting the door, he walked the two hundred feet to the front of the building relatively flawless. He even managed to open the door without quagmire. Once inside, a familiar face immediately caught his attention. Standing twenty feet in front of him was one of the women who had attempted to put him six feet under. Her green eyes, mixture of red and black hair, and a perfect imitation of Devin’s dress attire was something that would never be eviscerated from his memory. It took her walking up and wrapping her arms around his neck for Devin to remember that the real Shannon Austin had been blown up in a house fire. Mallory Daniels occupied her identity for the time being. Her mouth moved directly beside his ear and she whispered "Fifth floor all the way in the back." Devin nodded and moved Mallory aside, wanting to get this over with sooner rather than later. Rounding the corner, Devin punched the Up arrow on the elevator and walked inside. After hitting the fifth floor button, Devin had the unenviable task of waiting on the elevator doors to close. Even after repeatedly hitting the necessary arrow, they wouldn’t go any faster for him. Sneering in disgust, Devin continued to press the button at a rapid fire pace until the doors finally sealed shut. With his body somehow staying perfectly still, the journey ended rather quickly compared to the buttons fiasco. Before Devin could crack two knuckles on his right hand, the elevator doors flew open. Once he stepped onto the carpet, Devin felt in dire need of a blanket despite the fact he was wearing long sleeves. Refusing to take in the scenery, Devin started walking out briskly, gaining ground and passing by a plethora of doors with employee names that meant nothing to him. Two men passed him in the hallway, exchanging a friendly wave. Devin scoffed sardonically and continued on his way, making a left when office doors turned into dead end walls. Picking up the pace once again, he looked back and forth from door to door looking for any indication that Anthony was around. The first six doors resulted in nothing, but the seventh stopped Devin in his tracks. Straight ahead, he saw the name tag on the door that read ‘Boss’. The son stood there for a few moments, testing his legs to ensure their stability. When he was confident they would carry him through the door, Devin walked the remaining fifteen feet and grasped the door handle. Before he could open the door, something jolted his hand away. Turning to the side, Devin smacked himself in the head and tried to mentally gain control. Emotions had a cruel way of playing with the body and Devin did not want to become a victim any longer to their manipulation. The child wanted to leave, run away, and cower underneath the bed in hopes that Father wouldn’t come looking for him with his leather belt. The man didn’t run. The man didn’t hide. The man took action. Devin Shakur was a man. He slung the door open and firmly slammed the apparatus behind him. Devin walked out into a giant open office. Straight ahead was a single giant window that spanned almost an entire football field. To his left was a plush desk with an exquisite leather chair. Over on the right sat three different fish tanks. Behind Devin fifteen televisions covered various news outlets from around the world. "I told you Valerie those papers were to be delivered at the end of the day!" Emerging from the bathroom with a paper towel washing his hands was a man in his late 50s trying to project the image that he was only thirty five: Slicked back thinning hair that was only black due to hair dye products, a five o clock shadow that didn’t really complement his pale skin tone, and freshly pressed business attire. He looked like a million bucks. He rounded the corner and still hadn’t looked straight ahead to see what awaited him. "Now if you’ll please le-" Anthony finally gazed upward at his son. In all of his years, he never anticipated this meeting. Regardless of disguise, Devin would always know what his father looked like: Brown eyes which always glared at him each night telling him that he was a mistake and never should have been born, yellow teeth that sometimes bit deep into his skin, large callous hands that left bruises for days at a time on his body, and the cackling voice that haunted Devin on many sleepless nights. Devin stood there like a soldier, unflinching with his trained eyes locked squarely on Anthony Shakur. Anthony returned the glare but with far more quizzical intentions than Devin. Father tried to extend an olive branch, "Son..." "...Anthony" Devin wasn’t taking any offers. "What in God’s name are you doing here? How did you find me?" "You know why I’m here." Anthony started to size up his son, looking at him from head to toe. Dressed in all black and looking two shades paler than Edward Scissorhands, Anthony had a lot to grasp in a very short amount of time. Taking two steps forward to close the gap, he extended his arms forward and tried to hug Devin. "Son..." Devin reared back his right fist and with all the power he could muster, extended it forward into his father’s jaw. Anthony was taken completely by surprised and completely off his feet, dropping to the floor and retreating against the wall. He held his jaw and looked up at his son in shock. "You aren’t in control anymore, Anthony. I am my own man now and I make my own way." Devin cocked his right foot back and extended it forward, connecting soundly with Anthony’s sternum. Clutching at his blue dress shirt, Anthony fell backwards and started to hack up a storm. Stopping now wasn’t in the cards. Showing the relentless he received from his father, Devin threw another kick which connected with flab on the stomach. Anthony was helpless and far too captivated in the moment to adequately put up a defense. "You want to know what my first memory was, Anthony? You brought me down a flight of stairs..." Devin unleashed a furious kick to each of Anthony’s legs, causing him to grasp at aching hamstrings. "I was crying, trying to get away because the temperature was too cold. You said that was alright because people like me should be privileged to get anything." Another stinging shot to the middle of Anthony’s spine resonated through the office. "We turned the corner and I saw some of the furniture you made for the local town folk. Amazing, you would make them furniture for nothing but you wouldn’t even look at me with a smile." Devin swung his foot around and drove the heel of his boot down into Anthony’s mouth. The screams for help were useless. Blood began to seep into the carpet, and Anthony reluctantly swallowed three of his own teeth. The blank stare of his son continued to advance. "You swung me around and told me that this would be my new sleeping quarters. And do you want to know what you did after that?" Devin methodically walked around to the front side of his curled up father and grabbed him by the collar, lifting him off the ground effortlessly. Anthony made an effort to get away but Devin slammed him hard into the wall. "You opened a five foot tall door and forced me to look inside at a coffin. You told me that was where I was going to sleep." Devin’s enraged right hand clutched his father’s throat. Losing strength and blood quickly, Anthony tried to fend off Devin with weak shots to the forearm and wrist. The strength Devin contained was unbelievable. Anthony tried to squirm his way out, but Devin kept him in line with a knee to the thigh. "YOU MADE ME SLEEP IN A GODDAMN COFFIN, ANTHONY!" Throwing his head forward, Devin connected in the precise spot that he wanted to, shattering Anthony’s nasal bone. "YOU TOLD ME I WAS A MISTAKE!" Putting both hands around Anthony’s throat, Devin yanked his father a foot off the ground and launched him across the room into the televisions. Four screens shattered immediately, their sharp fragments digging into Anthony’s skin. "And you said that you were just doing the Lord’s work, Anthony. That’s how you justified every single act you ever did to me. I was Satan’s son. Mom being killed was a sign that I was nothing more than scum? I heard about what you tried to do to me in the hospital right after I was born. You wanted your priest to perform an exorcism on me." "Dev, Son, listen I can explain." The front of a boot smashed into Anthony’s mouth yet again, spewing droplets of blood onto the walls and Devin’s face. Devin reloaded his foot again and planted it firmly on the jaw, knocking Anthony an iota from unconscious. "You don’t have the right to play God, Anthony. Mom died because of you. I went to prison for seven years because of you. I hate staring at myself in the mirror everyday because of you. I’ve managed to make it back to the top and you try to bring me down with this Braintrust bullshit?" Anthony tried to formulate a response, but all he could generate was a loud wheezing sound. Devin bent down and backhanded Anthony, sending his head to the floor along with the rest of his body. "I didn’t say you could talk. You did all the talking when you attempted to bring me down. When you had lackeys out trying to put bullets in my head. I AM NOT YOUR SON ANYMORE, ANTHONY! I AM DEVIN MICHAEL SHAKUR! YOU..." For the first time in a long time, Devin started cackling loudly with laughter. Anthony could only look on perplexed while Devin almost fell backwards hysterically. Anthony tried to slide out from underneath. Devin shot back to life and put his eyes no more than two inches from Anthony’s face. "You...Are...Nothing" Devin delivered another violent headbutt and clutched seven inches of glass from a destroyed television. He spun the shard in his fingers while waiting for Anthony to open his eyes once again. Trying to clutch at his forehead, Anthony attempted to move his arms. Devin brought the shard of glass down into each arm, causing Anthony to let out a frighteningly loud scream. "Nobody can hear you Anthony. Valerie isn’t coming to save you. Shannon isn’t running in here with a gun. It’s only you and me...Just like it used to be." Anthony opened his eyes and saw the lightning bolt shaped glass in his son’s hand. "In the name of the father..." Devin swung the shard directly into Anthony’s left eye. His entire body tried to buck Devin off, but it was of no use. The necessary strength to attempt any legitimate defensive move was long gone. "The son..." Devin removed the shard and brought it down onto Anthony’s right eye. Once again, Anthony tried to fight his way to safety, but Devin wasn’t allowing him any wiggle room. Not after he managed to escape death the last time. "And the Holy Spirit..." Sliding the glass down Anthony’s face, Devin made a nice little incision along the nose and across the lips. He lifted the shard up a little before bringing it across Anthony’s throat. With a quick slice, Devin split his father open from ear to ear and stood up. Anthony flopped like a fish out of water while Devin remained stagnant with the shard in his hand, ready to inflict more damage if necessary. Slowly, Anthony Shakur began to fade into the night. Devin crouched down and put two fingers against his father’s neck, checking for a pulse. There wasn’t one to be found. The son rose from the ashes and dropped the shard of glass across his father’s lifeless chest. "Amen" Devin Shakur spun on his right foot and walked out of the office, never looking back.
