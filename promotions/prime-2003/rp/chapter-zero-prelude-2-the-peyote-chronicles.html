Chapter Zero, Prelude 2 [The Peyote Chronicles]

Date: Sometime between May 4th and Early June Brandon did not know what the ball was made of, only that it sounded wooden as it jumped along the spinning wheel. It let along an annoyingly dull cackle, the rotation of the roulette moving at a snails pace. He had money to burn, but desired to hold a majority of it. He did not want this trip to bankrupt him. And when he got in a hole, he only dug it deeper as he tried to pull himself out of it. The monochrome suit, at one time pristine, was ruffled from celebrations at the blackjack table. But in typical fashion, boom became bust for him, and suddenly he found himself scrapping his nails into the carpet. As if it a green vortex attempting to swallow him whole. A former desert dweller, the Youngblood's had put it behind themselves somewhere between addictions and domestic disputes. But as sands in the hour glass turn, a new chance is born. And in Vegas, there was plenty of sand. The individual grains almost numbered the amount of degenerates who were foregoing future stability in a haze of booze. He'd been turned on to it in his various cross country travels, those vast desolate places. He had built a palace in a desert paradise in the Mojave desert, but when the marriage between Brandon and Melissa dissolved, it was as if the place was filled with too much foreboding to make the place inhabitable. She clung to his right arm with hers, and he could feel her flushed alabaster skin even through the fabric. They were linked once again, now stronger than before because they had fallen. Because they had, indeed, lost the status and had to rebuild. This was for them. No longer love struck children, the pair embarked upon their second journey of marriage with a sense of grandeur. Thirty six red was the hope. Wood hit wood (it sure as hell sounded like it to him) in bullet time. Sweat poured gently down his furrowed brow as he intently stared downward. "Come on baby...daddy needs a new pair of shoes!" It was the wrong game for that, but it did not matter to him. Part of the reason he never had real success in gambling was because he never sat down to try and learn the nuances of the games. He did not know how to play poker, which drew laughs from many people he knew simply because it had become such a big thing in the past few years. But he played it. Lost money on it every time too. It was not like stocks and commodities. That was his realm. That's where their future had been strategically placed because it was a fail safe. Then again, a certain type of competitive frugality came to the surface when he started seeming chips going from his pot and into the dealer's hands. She was elegant. No longer was it the bridal dress, but rather a pleasant black velvet number with a modest cut to the skirt. "Are we gonna get some after this Mister Diamond?" She had a way with her words, especially when her mind had been touched by the numbing hand of brandy. Brandon pulled her tight. Pulled her close in, tipped her slightly, and kissed her pert lips. The scene most have been absolutely intoxicating, because everyone within a fifty foot radius could not help but look away. "No more bets." The dealer blurted out after stealing a glance down her v-cut. He was envious. And around and around the ball went, the wheel getting one last tug before a release. Brandon almost dropped her at this point, his mind fogged by Jack Daniels. He pulled her up, breaking off the embrace in an almost rude manner that left her standing there in longing. "COME ON!" Came the forceful roar from the Diamond, booming over the herds around him. The ball slowly rolled around, landing on twenty one black. A curse word escaped his lips, and he punched his thigh. "It's not that bad Brandon." She said, literally yanking him from the table. Her embrace was as warm as her skin. He knew because he found his head trapped between her extended arms. Stepping tip toed on stilettos, she gave him a rough and drunken kiss. The rest was a blur until they somehow magically arrived in their suite. Their lusting hungry forced their hand, as desire powered them on to disrobe the other. It was with sloppy wet lipped kisses and groping that they found themselves locked with just their undergarments on. Brandon, upon unclasping her bra, picked her up and tossed her onto the bed. She bounced, letting out a childish peel of giggles that only served his libido more. Through the blur, a memory peaked her interest. As Brandon was about to prowl upon a jungle of cotton, he found his wife on her feet. He laid there in mock anger, which seemed to only cause her more excitement. She reached for her jaw, subconsciously letting her fingers glide across the slight dimpled scar that they both would have loved to forget. In her hands was a small plastic bag with two weed colored tablets. At least they looked like tablets. "You said we could..." a giggle fluttered in the air as she pulled the top open. She scampered to the bathroom, grabbing a Styrofoam cup and proceeded to fill it with water. Brandon merely rested his head against the pillow, contemplating whether this was the right thing to do or not. Addiction had plagued him. Turned him into a beast that could not control himself even as he mutilated himself and those around him. Would peyote be any different? But Melissa had wanted to try it, and he conceded with terms of their agreement to be named later. It struck him as odd. Maybe it was because they had already broken through the formalities, but this just felt like another day to him. It was not the fact that he did not love her. It was the fact that he did not have the same butterflies in his stomach as she ambled down the aisle. This one lacked the connotations the last one did. It was not done over a fallen house, rather upon it's raised ground. Melissa's beauty radiated through every pore. Brandon had always thought she was a Goddess, so that thought process was cliché for him. But when she was in his arms, nothing was a cliché. The feral beast turned to a man, that constipated glare dissipated into normal human qualities. Cup in tow, she placed her drug on her extended tongue and took a gulp. She cringed in disgust, the idea coming quick to her that perhaps it was not a good idea to taste it. She nearly took another swig of water to rinse her mouth of the foul, lingering bitterness. She tossed his to him, scrunching up close after he picked it off the sheets. Laying down next to him, she handed him the water. Brandon was apprehensive, but her smile forced his fingers to allude the stop button. And in a gulp of water, Brandon and Melissa were inaugurated into a new drug culture. "Let's just play for a little bit...let it take effect." Melissa's voice came out in a breathless whisper. Brandon could not help but feel disappointed, but then she reached into his boxer shorts the notion left his mind. Deviousness filled the air for forty five minutes. When their perception became fuzzy, they sprung into action. And so it was, ironically, that Brandon and Melissa Youngblood consummated their union while under the influence of hallucinogens. Brandon Three in the morning, or four. Maybe it was five. The moon still hung in the sky like a retched disease, it's light hitting the creases in the drapes. It filled the room. The swimming room. Brandon could sense something was completely amiss inside his body, but could not move himself at a quick enough pace to do anything about it for a while. His body was freezing, even with a pair of blankets covering him. His teeth chattered, his flesh beady. When the rush came over his stomach, he bolted out of bed, taking the blankets with him as he rushed towards the bathroom. He did not even turn the light on. He just went on instinct, pulling up the toilet seat and let loose a barrage of lobster and baked potato...or what was left of it. "I know it was you Fredo." The unmistakable line from the Godfather II came from the television. Another dry heave of bile, and Brandon heard it again. And again. "I know it was you Fredo." "I know it was you Fredo." "I know it was you Fredo." Michael Corleone grabs onto his older brother and kisses him. It was as if it was on a loop. Brandon picked himself off the bathroom tile and went to see if Melissa was ready for another go. When he turned his head around the doorway, there was nothing but a sea of blackness. It made him feel uncomfortable, because he felt like he was being pulled away from this world and into another. The television was not on and neither was the moon. What was on was the bathroom light. But it was not like the one he remembered when he needed to empty his bowels after check in. This one was fluorescent and hummed like a bug zapper. Through the haze, Brandon did not think he had turned it on. He hadn't His father did. "Hey dad." It was conversational. "I'm dead." His skin was a sort of lifeless pale that is almost indescribable. It looked like rough putty. His lips were dripping mildew onto the cream colored tiles, which evaporated in the spots his decay landed. Underneath that, Brandon could see a starry sky, like the world had been flipped upside down. "I know." "So is he." He pointed to the side, his fingernail six inches long and brittle yellow. Brandon did now know how the world around him had moved so much. All he knew is that the shower curtain was drawn shut. He forcefully pulled down, ripping the plastic linen and leaving plastic rings along the steel bar that went across the shower area. And there, laying in a pull of black tar was Al. Good old Al. A smile was on his face. The tar pool that his limbs slightly stuck out of also had stars, but it boiled. It would splatter onto the floor, eroding away the ground beneath him on contact. He stared at the man who had brought him into this world perplexed. "Why?" "Like I need to tell you..." his voice trailed off with a mad cackle. And then, he was gone in a cloud of ash which left a stain. He did not necessarily care all that much because he left him just as he had in life...without him knowing. Alastair moved slightly, the swishing noise causing Brandon to look over. His partner for so long was zoned out, his eyes rolled to the back of his head. Youngblood reached his hand downward, taking careful effort not to touch the pool of darkness. Nagatsuka just stared at him, his lips pressed together. "Why'd you kill yourself?" It was an honest question. "You did." It was an honest answer. Brandon stood there for a moment, nothing making sense. "You aren't dead. I punched you." Alastair moaned in satisfaction, a syringe bubbling up. The stars dissipated from the tar. "I killed myself." "Then why did you blame me?" "I didn't off myself like your old man." Brandon's arms were too heavy to respond. In his carelessness, the tar reached out and grabbed hold of his wrist. It was sucking him in. "I don't understand." Brandon forcefully tried to pulled himself out of the grasp of the tar, which took on a life of it's own. Like a beast, it let off a roar. His nerves felt assaulted, heat stabbing him like miniature pitchforks. They filled his nerves. After it had chewed through his arm, he felt nothing. "You will." Laughing ensued. And that black pool, Brandon tried to pull himself from it. But he continued to be pulled in. He became frantic, but in his madness his other arm touched down onto the tar. Alastair's arm grabbed onto his throat, choking him. He knew if he could use his arms, he could break free. It felt like a noose. He was slowly being strangled to death. Melissa's tortured face came as a visage of white sprinkled powder. Her eyes were gone, as were the contours of her face. The image lacked definition, but it was her essence. He knew it. The grip of death settled in, his face going a tense purple. The sound of a baby crying filled the air. His head was dunked into the tar. And then it was over.
