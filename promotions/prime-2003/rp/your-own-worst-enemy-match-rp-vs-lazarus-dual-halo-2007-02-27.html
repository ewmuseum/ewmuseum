Your Own Worst Enemy (Match RP - vs. Lazarus, Dual Halo)

27 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/02/07 I Chest puffed out, shoulders back, Chet Worth is ready for war. Black and green face-paint is thickly plastered across the olive skin of his face, while his rich black hair, fading at the edges, is held back by a Rambo-esque headband. Loose Army fatigues envelop his body, visually adding back the pounds he’s shed since retiring from the ring. Protruding from one of his several hundred various pockets is a “Wilderness Survival Guide”, and hanging down from his belt is a comically-oversized Swiss Army knife, seemingly large enough to house every possible implement from a corkscrew to a Samurai sword. The eyes of one of his PRIMEates scan him up and down, brows contorting into a bemused look. “Chester,” Karina Wolfenden’s suitably unimpressed words stab, “you do realise that there are no witnesses… and that should worry you, right?” Not sharing the PRIME CEO’s enthusiasm for dressing up, the K-Wolf has simply mustered up a pair of faded Khaki tech-pants to compliment her usual attire. Having adopted the fallen remains of a once mighty tree to lounge on, she lets out an over-exaggerated yawn, stretching her whole body out, right to the tips of fingers and toes. “We won’t be alone for long, Ms Wolfenden,” he dryly states while checking his watch. “You’re not the only one in need of this little bonding trip.” Around a year ago, Worth shocked the wrestling world by emerging victorious from PRIME’s Dual Halo, just as the young woman gazing daggers at him had done the year before that. His prize was a golden ticket that would grant him anything he wished for in PRIME. He could have chosen a shot at the Universal Title, could have set his career up for any kind of personal glory… …but instead, his golden ticket turned into his Albatross. While the man he pinned to win the Halo went on to capture the biggest belt in PRIME at the biggest show in PRIME history, Worth sacrificed his own rapidly-rising career to save the promotion from Tyler Nelson. And his reward for such a selfless act? Ungrateful workers, outrageous contract demands, anti-authority trouble-makers, numerous fines for excessive violence, threats to close down shows due to dangerous stunts, roster members thrown in prison, and a locker-room who’d gladly tear each other limb from limb if you gave them so much as an inkling of a motive. “I’m not quite feeling the logic of this, Chester,” the K-Wolf’s mocking tone carries through the crisp, pine-scented air. “Forcing folks who pretty much can’t stand each other to haul ass out of bed at half seven to spend a little quality time together actually makes me want to kill them in the face just that little bit more… ideally, after breakfast and a few more hours in bed.” Safe to say, Karina Wolfenden wasn’t one of those employees he held up as a shining example of what he wants from a PRIMEate. “Yeah,” Chet ponders under his breath while adjusting his head-band, “it’s almost as if there are people on the roster I want to punish under the guise of employee relations…” It’s ticking towards eight o’clock, of the ante meridiem variety, as the sun has finally hauled itself up the branches to the treetops of the forest. But, before the light can flood down into the clearing and defrost the icy hint to the air, it gets a little frostier. For ‘a little frostier’, see ‘absolute zero’. “Chet, give me the co-ordinates so I can get an airlift out of here.” The sharp sound of fallen branches snapping under moody stomps filters through the forest, announcing the arrival of the next PRIMEate Worth felt could benefit from the bonding experience. There’s something to the voice that grabs Karina’s far-wandering attention, causing her head to snap around. Blowing black, blonde and purple fringe from her view, the sight that meets her instantly forces a scowl to break across her face. “Chester…” she hisses. “Um, Worth?” the new arrival aims at the CEO, hands planting on hips. Eyes locked in a duel with Wolfenden is a woman just a couple of years younger, decked out in black and hot pink. Vivid green eyes threaten to erupt with emerald fire as she and the K-Wolf look as if they’re about to start wailing like fighting cats, blonde and pink hair restrained by two plaits to ensure nothing breaks her stare. She was the woman who limped down the aisle at her first Culture Shock, outfit shredded from her earlier opponent wrapping himself in barbed-wire, to snatch sensational victory from many snapping jaws of defeat. She’s the over-achieving sibling all others are compared to, much to their chagrin. “Karina Wolfenden 2007,” Chet announces with glee, hands rubbing together in that ‘plan comes together’ way, “meet Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden.” II “No, no, no, no, no, no, no,” Karina Wolfenden 2007 protests, shaking her head as she swings down from her lounge seat in the fallen tree. Dropping back down, her black, white, and purple coat flaps behind her for the 10 feet until she touches down in the leaf litter. “No.” She holds her finger up to pause the proceedings for a little longer. “No.” Stepping back a few paces, just in case the static in the air erupts into a lightning bolt aimed squarely at the Worth Family Jewels, Chet reclines against a tree, folding his arms and enjoying the show. “That what you say nowadays when they ask if you want a win?” Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden can’t resist goading herself with. “Oh, go get knocked up,” is spat back with venom by Karina Wolfenden 2007. “Yeaaaaaah,” Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden drags out, “that wasn’t actually me, that was Karangelo Wolfendeville, when she suddenly made all those Italian sausage jokes the in-thing on the PRIME message boards.” Waiting for the Technicolor hair-pulling to start, and wondering if he should have engineered a mud-pit, Worth continues to watch on as the two abrasive personalities grate at one another. Silence returns for a second or two as the pair, separated by two eventful years, look each other up and down. “Wow,” remarks Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden, drawing first. “I put on weight.” “Not really a huge price to pay in return for NOT looking like a pre-pubescent boy,” is the barbed retort from Karina Wolfenden 2007. “You’ll find out for yourself when you finally stop knocking back the diet pills.” After each lands a job, they back off just a little, feet almost scraping the leaves below them, like a bull lining up its charge. Then, a little spark seems to ignite in Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden, and a sly grin creeps towards the corner of her mouth. “What?” Karina Wolfenden 2007 demands to know, puzzled. Raising her right hand, Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden flexes her fingers, before suddenly whipping her palm back to strike herself across the face. As the echo sounds out around the clearing, Karina Wolfenden 2007’s face is swatted to the side. “Owww!” winces, rubbing her left cheek as the sting spreads across the skin. “Can’t believe you didn’t work that one out first,” boasts Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden, pain numbed by expectation. “I must be getting slow in my old ag-Ghar!” Before she can finish, Karina Wolfenden 2007 swings her right leg out, then kicks it back into her left shin. Biting her bottom lip through the pain pulsing up and down her leg, Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden clenches her fist, ready to strike, only for Karina Wolfenden 2007 to again swing out her leg. “Kinda takes the fun out of it when I know all your weak points,” Karina Wolfenden 2007 laments. “Funny,“ Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden shrugs, “I was just thinking it would be fun to find out what new ones I have to look forward to. Or, I could just climb the nearest tree, leap from the top, and see if there are any that give you lower odds of survival.” Let’s face it, she never seemed like a woman who liked anyone a whole lot, including herself. Jealousy of past accomplishments can be a nagging insecurity, while always wondering if it’s downhill from here on in is an ever-present fear. Mix them up, and, well, you have this. The grinding of teeth and cracking of knuckles is heard above the ambient wildlife noise of the forest as the pair of feuding personalities continue to joust their death-stare. The hot pink and black of a career’s apex outshining the white, black, and purple of its apparent nadir. All under the eager gaze of Chet “Cheshire Cat” Worth. The shimmer of the morning sun on his bleached teeth seems to distract both the warring parties, and their head snap around in unison, a Medusa’s glare from each freezing the PRIME CEO where he stands. “Does my knee to your groin count as ‘bonding’?” Karina Wolfenden 2007 enquires of Worth. “Really, Karina…” Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden is quick to dismiss, puffing air out through her nose. “Odds are he’ll catch your leg and make you tap. Seems the fashion of the year.” “Oh, shut up,” Karina Wolfenden 2007 barks, already tiring of the jibes by her more successful sibling-personality. Despite their bickering, the pair of Wolfendens close in on Worth, who, backed against his tree, holds his hands up, trying to diffuse the sparking personalities. “Hey,” he urges, adopting a calming tone to his voice, “let’s remember this is all about coming together as a team here, girls. I‘ve even invited someone that I‘m not a big fan o-” “Wow, way to feel wanted…” Stepping out from the trees behind Worth, sleek form complimented by slimming black, is Colossus III Karina Wolfenden, complete with aura that could curdle milk. Bright orange hair catches the new sun’s rays, spreading a gaudy glow across the clearing. “Check my back for knives, will you?” Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden asks of her 2007 personality. “Only if you’ll check mine,” reciprocates the mistrust. Having heard it all before, Colossus III Karina Wolfenden motions brushing off her shoulder, before hopping up onto the tree-branch lounge that Karina Wolfenden 2007 had earlier vacated. “Oh, Chet, before I forget,” the most loathsome of all the Wolfenden personalities punctuates with a jab of the index finger, “since these little happy-happy-fun-fun let’s-all-hold-hands outings aren’t in my contract, I’m billing you for time and mileage.” “We’ve agreed to go halves on paying the mileage to send her back…” The woman previously referred to as Karangelo Wolfendeville strides out into the clearing, dye-free brown hair as natural as her surroundings. Following her, thus justifying the ‘we’, is PRIME Debutant Karina Wolfenden, who single-handedly lowers the level of cynicism in the clearing. “I really think the money would be better used to buy your dignity back,” Colossus III Karina Wolfenden aloofly remarks toward Karangelo Wolfendeville, not even making eye-contact. “That chip on your shoulder come with any dip?” PRIME Debutant Karina Wolfenden refreshes the air by quipping. “Something not too sour, if you can manage that?” “Oh, go pose with some merchandised-up kid, Mrs Smiley Face,” Colossus III Karina Wolfenden sighs. “I’m sure Chet here has a camera to catch the oh so unforced moment.” With a quintet of warring Wolfendens spread out into a circle around the clearing, Chet Worth finally strides out into the middle, before gesturing towards a bush. A pair of nervous eyes peek out from the foliage, before a cautious head hesitantly bobs out of its leafy turtle-shell. “I thought we were going with the ‘sedate them’ plan…” a jumpy Blaine Blair questions his employer. “They’re more entertaining this way,” Worth unashamedly announces, spreading his arms wide to encompass all of the five violent females. Almost anchored to the forest’s leafy carpet by a backpack that practically doubles his weight, Blaine Blaire shuffles out from the undergrowth, then stumbles like a hunchback over to his master. “At least all they’re saying isn’t ‘Wolfenden’ over and over…” Blaine grumbles under the sound of clanging pots and pants, struggling to stand up straight with the weight on his back. “Now, there are meant to be six of you,” Chet resumes his announcement with, “but, let’s face it, you’ve never exactly been the most reliable of people. No sense in waiting around though, as all this means is that one of you lucky girls will only have to team up with one other of yourselves… if that made any sense.” “Is this even legal?” Colossus III Karina Wolfenden demands to know. “As in, the legal sense of legal?” “The PRIME legal department are on holiday,” Chet gladly reveals, “so the usual procedure is we assume it’s legal and pay the fines later.” A circle of uneasy looks orbit’s the PRIME CEO. “So, let the games begin.” III Megaphone in hand, Chet Worth stands atop a massive mound of dirt, into which he drives a flagpole, adorned by a standard of the PRIME logo. By now his few streaks of paint have turned into full-face camouflage, and two paint-ball guns, taped side-by-side Aliens-style hang from his shoulder. Raising the megaphone to his mouth, Worth gives the tree-line a quick scan, before making his announcement. “On Blaine’s whistle, the game will begin,” Chet declares, peeking down at his assistant. “Capture the flag rules will in force. If you have any questions, now’s the time to ask…” Gesturing down to Blair, Chet asks that he hold up his walkie-talkie. “Can we keep what we kill?” Colossus III Karina Wolfenden’s voice is heard asking over the crackle of the walkie-talkie. “I like souvenirs.” “My cell has no signal,” follows Karangelo Wolfendeville. “I need to know if I get a call.” “Their team’s colours go better with my hair,” protests PRIME Debutant Karina Wolfenden, “but I guess I’ll live.” “What’s the prize for winning, again?” Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden asks. “You win your very own ‘ego-massager,” crackles the envious retort from Karina Wolfenden 2007. Shaking his head, Worth lowers his megaphone and calls down to Blaine. “Just blow the damn whistle…” IV The shrill sound of the whistle cuts through the forest, forcing a wincing Karina Wolfenden 2007 to drop the walkie-talkie she still has held to her ear. Ignoring the ringing-eared plight of her team-mate, Colossus III Karina Wolfenden adopts a leadership role, calling the less bull-headed PRIME Debutant Karina Wolfenden to heel. “Right, time to put our numbers advantage to use,” Colossus III Karina Wolfenden declares. “The two of you take a flank each, if you’re fast enough you can both get a good position to pick both of them off from high ground while I make the charge through the middle for the flag.” Not just letting her authoritative words do the talking, Colossus III Karina Wolfenden scratches her plan into the mud under the leaf litter with a discarded branch. Slashing X’s to mark their positions, sweeping lines topped off with arrows mark the play routes. “Um,” Karina Wolfenden 2007 vocally ponders, “who exactly put you in charge?” “I did,” comes the blunt reply, “you were busy deafening yourself.” “Look,” PRIME Debutant Karina Wolfenden wedges herself between them by uttering, playing the diplomacy card. “Maybe we should toss a coin or something?” “And if the coin lands on her head, knocking her out, we might stand a chance of winning,” fires venomously from Karina Wolfenden 2007’s lips. “Oh, please,” the bickering continues with, as Colossus III Karina Wolfenden naturally bites at the goading bait, “you just standing there lowers our combined winning percentage by about 15.” “You know what?” Karina Wolfenden 2007 proclaims, striding off. “We’ll go with your idea, since it involves me not being here listening to you. Just watch out for friendly fire, since obviously I’m a giant liability.” With Karina Wolfenden 2007 being less than stealthy as she storms off, PRIME Debutant Karina Wolfenden looks quizzically at her self-appointed leader. “Can someone explain the bonding part of pretending to shoot each other?” she has to ask, not quite getting it. “Gets it out of our system for if we ever have live ammo,” is the best guess Colossus III Karina Wolfenden can make. “Go, that flank.” V “They’re blatantly going to try and outflank us,” Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden predicts right on the button, feet crunching leaves below as she paces back and forth to visualise her contemplation. “It’s what I’d do.” It’s then that a gust of wind imposes itself upon the forest, whistling through tree-tops and leaving the canopy shaking in its wake. “You up for a climb?” Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden then asks, light bulb glowing above her blonde and pink head. But, while a flash of inspiration may have overcome Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden, her team-mate seems to have a darker attraction as her thoughts visibly wander. “Oh, PLEASE say you’re not thinking about him,” she demands of Karangelo Wolfendeville. “If you are, please kindly shoot yourself in the mouth with a paintball.” “Hard to see why your single,” is Karangelo Wolfendeville’s sarcasm-laced retort as her attention is realigned with her team-mate’s. “Anyway, he’s just misunderstood.” Not registering the reply from Karangelo Wolfendeville, Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden looks up to the treetops, a sheet of leafy green cracked by the sunlight. “As I was saying,” she softly growls. “They’ll head for the high ground on each flank, so we’ll go for the even higher ground. Take the tree-top route, take out whoever’s making their charge for the flag, and drop down to take the prize.” “Angelo has a tree right outside his window…” the distant Karangelo Wolfendeville swoons. “You are so lame,” huffs Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden, before pointing up the hefty trunk. “Climb.” VI “We watched Crouching Tiger, Hidden Dragon the other night,” Karangelo Wolfendeville dreamily recollects, “wouldn’t it be romantic to fight on the tips of tree branches like that?” Under cover of the tree canopy, the pair of Wolfendens hop from branch to branch, pausing only to pick the one best to hold their weight. “Please stop talking,” Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden instructs. “Me vomiting might give away our position.” “You need to get laid,” is Karangelo Wolfendeville’s diagnosis. “You need to get sterilised,” bounces back at her. Tiring of her besotted team-mate, Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden makes the leap to the next tree, touching down crouched, then hopping to a platform nearer the trunk to clear the landing pad for Karangelo Wolfendeville. Holding up a hand, Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden signals for her soppier personality to pause, having spotted something below. Pressing herself back against the branch, Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden drops her head to the left for just a split-second, confirming her sighting. With a mischievous smile breaking across her lips, she then snaps the flat palm into a thumbs up. “Angelo!” Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden then exclaims, just as her team-mate goes to launch. Distracted, Karangelo Wolfendeville’s push-off is weak, and as she reaches out for her branch, she catches sight of a smug wave from her team-mate, before watching her disappear over the horizon of her own plummeting vision. With her wits about her, Karangelo Wolfendeville nails her landing, absorbing the shock of the landing through her leg muscles… *Puht-Puht* …only to get double-tapped in the chest by Colossus III Karina Wolfenden… *Puht-Puht-Puht* …who in turn has her hair splattered yellow from above by Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden. After finding just enough balance to give the pair of disgruntled personalities below a mocking bow, Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden makes her treetop beeline for the flag. Swinging around the trunk, she flings herself onto the staunchest of branches, using it as a springboard to leap the distance to a lower tree. Clamping gloved hands around the next branch, Dual Halo 2005 Winner Karina Wolfenden uses her momentum to swing through before detaching herself, sending her spinning through into a spot-on landing at the top of the flag mound. “Nice try, losers!” is as classy a victory speech as she can muster, before… *Puht* …a single shot explodes against her chest. “Bitch,” is the rather less joyous remark that follows. As the three fallen Wolfenden eras descend upon the mound, Karina Wolfenden 2007 and PRIME Debutant Karina Wolfenden emerge from the forest either side, each dotted with paint splatters, and looking none too happy about it. “And the winner, and sole survivor…” Chet Worth declares from his megaphone, doing his best ‘The Fink’ impression. Emerging over the horizon of dirt comes a choppy mane of lilac, purple, and black hair, followed by a black and white gloved hand that clamps purposefully around the flagpole. Chet Worth clears his throat. “…Dual Halo Winner 2007 Karina Wolfenden!”
