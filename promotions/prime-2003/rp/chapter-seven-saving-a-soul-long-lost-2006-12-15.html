Chapter Seven: Saving a Soul Long Lost

15 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 15th, 2006 Chapter Seven Saving a Soul Long Lost It had been weeks since I had become part of this world. The incident at the church had surely gotten me some press, especially since it was a PRIME superstar that had done the evil doings. Unfortunately, I had been banned from churches all across the world, and they had posted security guards at the doors of each one. I cared not though. Those in the churches were far too ignorant to hear my words. Instead, I had focused my efforts elsewhere. I had quickly realized that yelling at people couldn’t help me in the long run. People were adverse to such trivial things and would quickly close up if they felt like someone was yelling at them and damning them to hell for the lives they had lead. My anger had slowly started to subside after my first few weeks, but in a corner of my soul it still rested there ready to be unleashed. I kept it there for the wrestling matches so that I had something to help prove my case to the world. Victories would soon gain people upon my side. I knew that. Yet, it was going to be a long, arduous road ahead for me. However, I would not let them down. Around this time, King of Kings 2006 was creeping up upon me. The first time I would ever compete at a Pay-Per-View. The nerves hadn’t hit me yet, but I was susceptible to human conditions. So, surely enough, I would start to become nervous when the days grew closer. In my short time here, I had just followed wherever PRIME told me to go. Some shows I hadn’t appeared on while others I appeared on quite a bit. It was a mixed bag depending on my mood and what they had planned for me. Living in hotel rooms and eating on the road had made me tired. So much so that I had started to lose track of my mission. Thankfully, it was a woman that would help me find my way back to where I needed to go. Two weeks prior, I had been in Rutherford, New Jersey on another stop of the PRIME tour. It was there that I met a woman that needed my help. It was she that I would find my place in this world and how I could help those find their paths in life. ---- November 27th, 2006 The sounds and echoes had started to filter into my room rather rudely. As my weary eyes opened up and looked to the clock, I realized that the red lights were telling me that it was merely 2AM in the morning. It would be two days before I had to compete anywhere, but I wanted my sleep. Living on the road was taking more and more out of me each day, and without my sleep I couldn’t continue my journey. The noises outside quickly turned to screams, and I started to roll out of bed. What could be the problem, I wondered to myself, wishing I could kill each one of these mortals who had awoken me from peaceful slumber. That was one of the things that I was adjusting to in my short time here. Having to sleep when I hadn’t slept for thousands of years was taking a toll on me and was something that I was still not used to. I cursed myself for making humans have to sleep as I pulled back the curtain and noticed that a woman was standing roughly ten feet away from me, yelling with another man who looked to be of the violent sort. A loud crack echoed throughout the nearly vacant parking lot and it didn’t take me long to realize that the man had slapped the woman with the palm of his hand. The woman’s head just snapped back from the force as she stumbled backwards. After a few steps, her back slammed hard into the wall next to my hotel room. How could this be? Why had this man hit her? I had to stop this, that’s all I knew. Immediately, I threw open the door, and looked at both parties. The man just glared at me as if his stare alone would be enough to make me go back inside of my room and ignore the happenings outside. He was a foolish man for thinking so. My eyes immediately darted to the woman and looked at her battered body. I wasn’t sure if this man had caused all of the bruising that was on her body, but I knew a few of them had to be from him. She looked at me with such sorrow in my eyes that it nearly broke my heart. People thought of me as a cruel God, but when I had to look into those eyes and see her soul the way it was, it truly hurt me. I didn’t want to ever see any of my children go through such pain. “Get back inside!” the man yelled at me, but I stood my ground as I walked outside of my room. My eyes glanced over to him for a brief moment and I saw astonishment etched on his face. He couldn’t believe that I had stuck my nose into his business and I knew that this would turn for the worse sooner then later. I walked up to the woman, her blonde hair looking mattered as if she hadn’t washed in a few days. As I drew closer to her, I immediately noticed her blue eyes. With it being dark out, it was hard to notice something like these unique pair of eyes because of the fact that the sun wasn’t beaming and allowing the colors to show brightly. It was amazing though to see that her eyes were different shades of blue. Her left eye alone had three different shades of blue and the same was true for her right eye, yet neither eye shared the same shade of blue. It was truly something I hadn’t seen in a long time. I stood there, stupefied for a moment, as if I had seen a ghost. The women’s face had now grown to fear as she had no idea what exactly I was doing. The eyes had been familiar, but I couldn’t quite put my finger on it. Unfortunately, I wouldn’t have much time to figure it out as I heard the footsteps, so softly, of the man creep up behind me. My stupor quickly left me as I looked at the woman who was fearful of the man coming up behind him. I had to quickly put her fear to rest. Within mere seconds, I could feel the heat radiating from the man. It was then that I knew I had to strike. In a flash, my right elbow struck up and connected with the man right in the throat. I knew that his throat had immediately closed up from the blow. Making sure that he would have problems breathing for a little bit longer, I kept my right leg planted as I swiveled my hips, and kicked him in the stomach, removing any air he may have had left. The man immediately went down to the ground as I walked over to him, pulled back, and slammed my fist into the side of his jaw, creating a sickening crack sound that told me I had broken his jaw. My job was done. He was rendered useless. Without wasting any time, I turned back to the woman, and walked up to her. Tears had started to creep out of her eyes and race down her cheek. I took her by the arm and walked her over to my hotel room. Hopefully, she would relax and let me help clean her up. For whatever reason, the anger in my heart started to melt as I found myself starting to care for this woman who looked as if she had a rough time as of late. As we walked into my room and I shut the door, she immediately came up on me, and tried to pull my shorts down. My arms shot out and grabbed her on the shoulders to stop her as she had a lustful look in her eyes. “What’re you doing?” I asked her, my first words all evening. She looked baffled by all of this though as she backed up, unsure of what I wanted from her. It was then I realized what she was as I looked at her body. Bruises on her arms. Her blonde hair, which should be normally full of life, was incredibly thin. Near her wrists, I saw weakened veins. This woman was a prostitute and even worse, a drug addict. “Let me help you,” I told her as she just stood there. I was unsure if she knew that I had recognized what she was yet she didn’t decline my help as I walked into the bathroom. I looked around for some towels and found a few. I turned on the faucet in the bathroom, and started to dampen each towel to help clean her up. Unfortunately, it would be all for naught. As I turned back around to see where she was, I realized that she had left. Instantly, I rushed out of the bathroom to look for her. The door to the hotel room was open and I raced out of it to see if I could spot her, yet, it was no use. The street surrounding the hotel was unfathomably dark and this woman obviously knew her way around these streets. I cursed underneath my breath as I turned away from the street and started to walk back inside of my hotel room. It was then that something caught my eyes. It was a piece of paper on the ground, close to where the woman had stood while I fought off the man. I walked up slowly and kneeled besides it to pick it up. As I picked it up and turned it over, I was shocked by what I saw. It was a photo. ---- That’s how it had begun. Ever since then, I had been searching for that woman. Instead of immediately going to the next city that I had to wrestle in, I had spent my time in Rutherford, New Jersey looking for this woman. All I had to go by was a picture of her and a vague recollection of where she could be. Unfortunately, that had proven useless as in two weeks time I had yet to find this woman. It wouldn’t be long before I had to travel to Columbus, Ohio for the King of Kings Pay-Per-View. Yet, I was determined to find this woman. I had to find this woman. Her eyes, they were familiar, and I had to find out why. Apparently, this was to be my lucky day. I had turned the corner and there she was, standing down the street talking to another man. A man much unlike the man I had seen her with in the parking lot two weeks prior. Instead, this man was dressed up professionally. I could once again see the fear in her eyes. I began to walk closer to them, but the man was making her get into a car and before I had a chance to catch up to them they were driving off. I had finally found this woman and just like that, she was gone. I was beginning to lose hope that I would be able to help this woman. I needed to help this woman. To the right of me, a bell rang loudly, and as I turned my head I saw a door opening to a local restaurant. Out stepped a large man with a small boy who looked content with himself. As I looked at the boy, I realized that he looked familiar, and quickly fished the picture out of my pocket. It was him. The boy in the picture was the boy walking right in front of me. It was her son. To Be Continued. ---- An excerpt from the upcoming book, The World Through The Eyes of God, penned by Malachi. To be released in Winter 2007.
