Oh, Jonny Boy

9 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/09/08. “Oh, Jonny Boy…” Clutching a half-full bottle of Krug Clos du Mesnil champagne (since Cristal is what passé was before it became passé, which was freaking ages ago), a boisterous Amy Campbell staggers out of Buffalo’s SubZero club. One bleary eye leering out through a mess of hot-cherry-red fringe, the Sin City champion looks over her bare, inked shoulder to see a blurry interpretation of her fellow revellers follow. “The bell, the bell is ringing… Joining her in the more-mocking-than-mournful chorus, having just about held onto his belief in ‘clarity of mind’ by the tips of his fingernails, is a merry Xavier Kannon. Under his tattooed arm is Eleanor, whose penchant for drinking her own bodyweight in cocktails has pretty much reduced her appearance to that of ‘The Lohan Look’. It’s, like, totally not in this season. “I, like, totally want another Banarita,” she softly squeaks. “Oooh, no… Kir Royal. What was that one that was, like, totally on fire and stuff?” “That wasn‘t a cocktail,” grumbles a trailing Rush Mason, sporting two less eyebrows than when he was last seen. “That was you trying to blow out a candle after knocking back an El Presidente.” Swinging her bottle of Krug up, Amy nearly loses her balance, sending a glug of champagne down past her gaping mouth and across her chest. A little confused as to why there was nothing to swallow down, the inebriated Sin City champion quickly gets over it them stamps a demanding foot into the bubbling puddle. “More. Singing.” “Only you two knew the words,” the teetotal Isabelle Kannon chimes in, traipsing up the steps behind them. “Well, two lines of them.” If she seems a little less ‘spirited’ than the others, that would be down to spending a lovely couple of minutes holding back Ellie’s platinum hair as she puked up a Paralyzer, and being asked ‘aren’t you the chick from Paramore?’ forty one times. “I’m the one ending Patch’s career on Monday, and I say… More. Singing.” Amy demands, regardless. “From buckle to buckle, and down to ringside…” Trundling out after the odd mix of Sinners and Kannons is a crooning William S. Powell, weighed down by the jackets that the others (sans Izzy) forgot they’d arrived with. “YES!“ the Sin City Champion roars in approval. “Willy, I knew you were invited by someone I invited for a reason!” Much to Isabelle’s awkwardness, Aimz throws an inked arm around her. “You’re eye is gone, and your career is failing…” As Powell sings of The New Life’s impending doom, Aimz takes another swig of Krug, then tosses it to Kannon. After toasting the Sin City Champion with a raising of the bottle, the Gold Patron Meritorious empties the last of it down his throat, despite Ellie weakly clawing at it like a kitten at a piece of string. “It’s you, it’s you must go… and Aimz must survive.” “All together now!” Kannon and Campbell call in stereo, while stone-cold-sober Isabelle gives a wincing ‘I’m having fun, really’ look as Aimz raucously shakes her into song. “Oh, Jonny Boy… the bell, the bell is riiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiinging!” “Whooooooooo!” a more-than-a-little-tipsy Ellie yelps in approval, still transfixed on the now empty champagne bottle. “We, like, totally love you and stuff, Buffalo… Stuffalo! Gimme bottle…” “I hate drinking with white people,” Mason confides in Powell. “Right, my chariot awaits,” Amy announces as the black Limo across the street sounds its horn. “See you north of the border for Retirerhine-a-po-one-eyedloser! Did that even rhyme with Lollapalooza? Eh, who cares.” Waving to Kannon and his entourage, the inebriated Sin City Champion backs into the street, the shrill horn of the car whose path she’s staggered into barely registering. “Why are we going to Mexico…” a half-asleep Ellie mumbles. “Rush… hand here?” the Gold Patron Meritorious asks, passing off his near-comatose wife to his burly agent. “Need to… how do I put this?” “Golden Pissing Alleytorious?” offers Isabelle, more than a little disgusted at her own suggestion. “No. Forget I even thought of that.” “Winner!” her brother declares as he takes lunging, off-balance steps towards the nearest alleyway. “You’ve put on weight,” a distant Ellie observes as she hugs on to Mason. “And blackness.” It would be safe to say that Rush Mason preferred every other time a paralytic-drunk little blonde white girl had latched onto him. Well, except that time she turned out to be sixteen. Less said about that… “Ever feel we’re the only normal ones?” Powell brings the schmooze on to Isabelle with. “My brother said that if you ever looked at me like that, I was to cry rape as a formality,” is her instant, biting cock-block. “Touché,” the Master Debater concedes. “Um, like, what is taking him so long?” Ellie slurs, showing she at least has a little awareness about her… but very few inhibitions. “Not like it has that far to travel if you, like, get what I mean and stuff…” The looks on their faces as she giggles? Isabelle = I so didn’t need to know that. Mason = I knew that was a sock. Powell = Oh, please keep talking. Sadly for Powell, those are the last words Eleanor can muster before she flops limply forward, Mason only just able to catch the Preppy Princess before she face-plants into the sidewalk. As for her husband? “You know I can’t go with big black guys watching me, Rush,” Kannon snaps back to the large, dark figure looming over him. “Don’t make me mark my territory, buddy.” It’s rumoured that once a male Scientologist reaches the level of Operating Thetan, he finally ascends to a spiritual level above bodily-function jokes. Or at least nobody has ever heard Tom jest about crossing streams. “Def’ntly ain’tcho buddy.” Kannon’s forehead crunches into the wall, expelling mortar dust and redbrick chips. As if charges had been set around his ankles, the Gold Patron Meritorious’ foundations crumble, leaving him in a heap at the feet of Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas. That would be Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas, sporting a ‘Gawd damn…‘ expression and currently wishing he’d have waited for the trickling sound to cease. “Yo payin’ fo’dese sneakers, too.” . . . “Oh, Jonny Boy… the bell, the bell is ringing!” A mumbling Xavier Kannon comes to, collapsed face struggling to heave up his sandbagging eyelids. His faced is carved up by a trickle of crusted blood originating from his forehead, and two narrow beams of amber lights which slash at his cloudy eyes. “Ugh…” the Gold Patron Meritorious groans, his skull still rattling from being bounced off the wall hours earlier. “Mornin’, sunshine.” A black, wrecking ball of a fist ploughs into Kannon’s stomach, forcing a sickening wrench up his gullet and out his dry, sticky throat. Rocking franticly back and forth on the wooden chair that he’s bound to, a heaving Kannon gargles on stomach acid, leaving a dark, stewed-tea liquid dribbling down his chin. “Hchyuk uuugh,” Kannon wheezes, spraying a mist of brown gut-sewage. The man standing above him is a Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas devoid of all joviality, any showman’s sparkle he once had to his eyes drowned out by menacing shadow. Stretching his body up straight, PRIME’s ‘Sherriff’ morphs his cuddliness into pure, intimidating bulk. Grinding teeth sheathed behind pursed lips, only Dam’s eyes are visible, and they broadcast an ominous signal. “Dinnut listen t’whats I told ya, huh?” Dam probes, going the verbal route while a gasping Kannon rides out the agonising after-shocks of the punch. “All ya hads t’do was keep yo mouth shut fo’one night.” A high-pitched, squealing breath whistles into Kannon’s lungs as he slowly raises his head, revealing a snarl that morphs his stained features into something almost supernatural. “You’re finished in this business,” the King of Wrestling venomously hisses. “Fin. Ished.” After a chillingly nonchalant shrug of his shoulders, Fuqueiawytas again clubs his balled fist deep into Kannon’s gut, sending nerves into spasm that almost turn the Scientologist inside out. Kannon’s facial muscles tense up as he tries to lock down the pain, fingernails split as they dig into the wooden base of the chair, and an agonised, ghostly hum echoes behind clamped-down lips. “I took dat right there as a threat,” states Dam, his face filling Kannon’s view, but just far enough back to avoid the frenetic headbutt attempts. “Unless yo’aint realised, yo ain’t in nooooooooo posh’un ta be makin’ dem.” A snatched glance either side of him by the bound and tremoring Gold Patron Meritorious certainly supports Dam’s assessment. Nothing but a grim sea of dusty concrete floor surrounds him, the world outside of cloaked in darkness. “This is how the Cantrell regime works then?” Xavier almost takes a sick delight in judging, despite his predicament. “Nah,” Dam denies with a slow, deliberate shaking of the head. “Dis is how I work when I be needin’ ta…. and I be needin’ ta know what yo lil‘ friends got‘n store fo‘us. Ain‘t nuttin‘ like got did t‘Angelica happen‘n on my guard ‘gain.” A wrenching, breathless wheeze explodes out from Kannon, rocking him on his chair. Then another dry heave, then… … laughter. “Heh… heh… hah… haha… ha ha hahahah…” Kannon splutters, his face teetering between sickened amusement and agony as his stomach pulses. “Heh… heh.” The King of Wrestling apparently finding Angelica Brookes’ hospitalisation hilarious snaps the last, fraying fibre of Dametreyus’ patience. “Yo deserve ‘dis,” is Dam’s judgement. Like a discuss thrower, Fuqueiawytas launches a wild haymaker into Kannon’s gut which sends the legs of the chair scuffing along the concrete before they catch and topple backwards. Helplessly strapped in for the ride, the back of Kannon’s skull bounces off the cold concrete. Feeling retreats from his hands and feet, all the way back to his head and neck, rendering his limbs heavy and numb. “Heh… heh… hah…” Xavier groggily coughs, unaware that a pool of sticky crimson is welling around his head. “Y’mind tellin’ me what’s so damn funny?” is Dam’s short-of-patience request as he plants his boot on the edge of the chair’s seat, leaving no doubt that it’s a stop-off on the way to Kannon’s groin. “You…” whispers a ghostly, wide-eyed Kannon. “You let them iiiiiiiiiiiiiin.” “What’cho say?” prods Dam, brow furrowed. Silence. “What. Cho. SAY!” Three hundred and fifty pounds press down through the seat of the chair, splitting and fracturing it between the King of Wrestling’s legs. “You let them in, Dam…” goads Kannon with a wheezing chuckle. “After you barred Aimz last month, I called in, through official channels, my whole allocation of passes for the next show.” This time it’s Dam’s turn to fall silent. “Now, my memory might be a bit foggy,” jabs the pained King of Wrestling with a ‘wonder why that could be’ tone, “but I remember a certain overweight odd-job telling me ‘ya made yo point, now here’re yo damn passes… ain’t like yo got no friends to take’em off ya’… or words to that effect.” Through flaring nostrils, a stewing Fuqueiawytas puffs out a lungful of air. “Guess what, Dam?” mocks a grinning Kannon, his sick satisfaction at breaking Dametreyus’ spirit soothing his aches. “I found some friends. Oh, and they appreciated you meeting them at the door… a nice personal touch.” Inner-turmoil boiling over, Dam expels a grizzly roar and lurches down to grab the Gold Patron Meritorious by the throat. A rag doll would have put up more of a fight as the PRIME security chief heaves a collared Kannon up by the neck. “Yo ain’t sherkin’ tha blame fo’dis,” a deep growl warns as Dam swings the unopposing XK into the building’s corrugated steel wall. “Yo gonna get strung up at ReV.” Dried blood and stomach juices painting his lips a sickly brown, the King of Wrestling battles Dam’s strangulation with just a grin… but still succeeds in loosening the asphyxiating hold on his neck. “Tell ‘em, Dam,” Kannon wheezes, dropping into a pulpy heap. “Tell ‘em all how The Hand were able to assault Maywhatever and Angelina, I’m sure they’ll forgive you… just like they did on Coliseum 2000. Tell ‘em ALL.” Despite having to battle through his body’s desire to wince, Kannon manages to shoot the most derisive smirk his purple, swollen face can muster. “Yo gonna wake up at yo hotel, an’ none o’this ever get’n talked ‘bout… ‘k?” It’s a smirk that Fuqueiawytas can only deal with by stamping his piss-stained Air Force One down into. “Next time, I don’t loosen ma grip.”
