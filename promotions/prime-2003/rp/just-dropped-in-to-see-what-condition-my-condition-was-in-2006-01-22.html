Just Dropped In (To See What Condition My Condition Was In)

22 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-22-06 (REV match rp) “I pushed my soul in a deep dark hole and then I followed it inI watched myself crawlin' out as I was a-crawlin' inI got up so tight I couldn't unwindI saw so much I broke my mindI just dropped in to see what condition my condition was in”-- Mickey Newbury When you achieve the best your career has to offer where do you go from the top? I can tell you it is indeed lonely at the top but the view is nice. Unfortunately the only direction to go is down, down, down into the waiting ring of fire. As a matter of fact when most guys drop the top belt they fade away in a pseudo-retirement or simply vanish. Why not, after all you burn yourself out getting there driving and giving your damndest along the way. Then once you’re there, you’re criticized by amateurs who will never see the title who also have forgoten all you have given to achieve it. Then you lose it which is very disheartening and burn out ensues. You can surround yourself with so-called-friends who are more of a pain in the ass wanting to talk about the one thing you don’t want to talk about which is wrestling and losing. You can’t get your mind off of your fall and looking ahead at the hill you ponder if the fight is still left in you to climb it again. That is indeed where I am and I really don’t give a shit what other people think. If I quit that’s my call not anybody else, and if i stay then I stay. I’m not going to apologize for my lack of decision because what ever I decide will be final. If I leave I will never come back as heaven awaits me. I will leave my earth body and retire forever in the kingdom of the lord in perfect tranquility away from the pain of this world. Job was a saint and his thick skin is a bible tale of faith is awsome, but I’m no saint. Understand I play a character who claims to a God or a saint but he is nothing more then everything I want to be. He is my escape, he is an extreme of the good and bad that my soul has to offer. The truth is I can no longer continue the image he portrays because I’m no longer where he is in my life. There is only so much you take before the body gives up and the soul wants out. The death of my mother caused so much pain inside of me I can’t express it in words. As a matter of fact instead of dealing with my pain I dug deeper into my work and career goals putting grief on a back burner for many years. But grief and pain never go away they stay with you until you are down and out, then it kicks you and kicks you hard. You see for me once the job was taken away all I was left with was dark thoughts and the need to escape reality. Then that escape turns into a nightmare manifested by child’s play and the need to create drama by people so far gone life to them is the fantasy. That has left me with nowhere to escape and reality has to be met head on. Then people wonder why I hold a grudge or simple want to cut ties with those who disrupted my world for their own selfish endeavors. Go away I’m done dealing with you. To tell you the truth I want to cry but I can’t, I never could and I’m not sure I ever will be able to. Visions of better times in my life don’t come to me as warm memory’s but rather taunts of happiness I will never feel or see again. Cherish every hug you get from your mother as nobody will ever hug you that way again and when its gone pal, its gone. So that leaves me sinking into an abyss of alcohol, food, and other addictions of pleasure that create another kind of fantasy world only with too real results. The plus side is they are never late. Understand this, I’m not braggin’, or complainin’, or looking for your sympathy and support. If that’s what you think this is then you are a fucking fool who isn’t comprehending a word I’m saying. I’m just talkin’ to myself man to man and you happen to be listening in. I’m a man in pain who would end his life if I wasn’t a man of God. It’s the great compromise faith over freedom. How long do we accept pain before we fight back or sub come? Plus if my mother killed her self, does that mean she isn’t in heaven as the bible tells me? If she isn’t there then what the hell am I working to get there for? Why do I have to put on this smile and face the world that really isn’t one of God nor or faith? Why do I have to carry this fucking cross on my back if Jesus did indeed die for my sins, and if he did that then he must have died for my mothers too, right? Confused? Fuck you so am I and there in lies the problem. I’m all alone and deeply in pain yet my job is to entertain and make people laugh. How fucked up is that? Why do I do it?? Why do I find so much pleasure in making others laugh when I can’t crack a fucking smile myself??? I’ve lived my life in stealth and secrets preventing me from creating a relationship. I’ve killed men in cold blood in acts of my government and for the continued cause of freedom of democracy. Yet my emotions are suppressed like a Muslim women in Iran and I can’t free them no matter how hard I try. So here I am at the cross roads of life with a tough decision. Jump off the bridge and end it all or cross it and continue on. The answer is yet to come to me so right now I just sitting here in the grass and watching the clouds hoping for a sunny tommrow. (Hoyt lays in his bed as the clock reads well past two o’clock. His beard is ungroomed, its clear he is gaining weight, and the smell on him tells of a tale which dosn’t include a chapter on showering. He was supposed to work out today with old friends but the want or the need to isn’t there.) Hoyt Williams: I have to piss. (One of the few activists of normalcy still structured in his life, the morning piss. He watches as the stream disrupts the still toilet water sending ripples though the bowl much like the ripples and waves that has put Hoyt into this terrible funk. Hoyt brushes his teeth then heads to the living room where he looks out the window towards the sky. He falls on his knees talking to his father in the heavens.) Hoyt Williams: I have a big match coming up with a mix of partners I have never worked with before. That is the least of my worries but maybe a prayer is what I need. Lord Jesus Christ, King of kings, you have power to breath life into our body’s yet you give us the power to take that breath away. That’s fucked up if you don’t mind me saying. You know me as you know the rest of the world around us. You know what I think, what I write, and my deepest and most hidden thoughts. Tonight I’m not asking to be judged, for I know I’m a sinner. Tonight I’m asking you to take this pain from me that haunts my soul. You know how weak I have become, both in body and soul. I beg of you to offer me strength, O Lord, in my weakness and remove this hurt from me as I can’t take it any longer. Grant me this request even if I’m not worthy of it. I know your love is there for me, and I want to take it. Thank you Lord I would not ask if I did not need it. =w=
