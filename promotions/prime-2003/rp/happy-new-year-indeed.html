Happy New Year....inDEED!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 010206 Happy New Year. It means different things to different people. For some, it’s just a reason to party. For others, it’s a way to start anew with resolutions of change which, while earnest in their intention, are rarely kept up with. Many people see it as just another year taken from their lives. Most will take some combination of the three. For Tyler Nelson, though, it means that he’s back on top once again. He may not have a gold belt around his waist to signify his physical dominance over professional wrestling…..yet, but he has something that is infinitely more powerful. A piece of paper. Not just any ordinary piece of paper, however. A piece of paper that designates him as ten percent owner of PRIME. A piece of paper that says he can now call shots and make decisions in PRIME. A piece of paper that gives him power over the entire roster, including the new Universal Champion. A piece of paper that, with the passing of Hin See, would now let him put his own stamp on what was once a bitter rival…..PRIME. But was it real? Was this document, tattered as it was, authentic? Was it the same piece of paper that Ian English has previously misplaced? Could that Mick bartender really have given up something so valuable for pocket change if it was legitimate? It would be a master stroke by Emilio Rage if he could have come up with such a scheme in order to get Tyler to put custody of his daughter on the line, but was Rage that smart? Nah, he couldn’t be…..could he? And what of Emilio and Sylvia Rage? Last we saw them both were being attended to by medical personnel after a terrific fall from the ladder to the floor below, leaving Tyler as the victor of the ‘Nicaraguan Death Match’. Must be a tough one for Rage to swallow….losing his match, losing his bargaining chip, and losing out on the chance to get his daughter back. It was a touching scene, though, as they were reunited at the top of the ladder and on their way to victory. Or so they thought. Guess Tyler had other ideas, as he usually does. Back to the real issue…ten percent of PRIME. Something this important has to be verified. There can’t be any doubts about the validity of the document in order for Tyler to be able to execute his plans and make it a Happy New Year…inDEED. Dr. Ed Dominguez’s office in Dallas, TX…PRIME’s insurance company’s designated site of a follow up visit for Tyler Nelson to check on the injuries sustained during the brutal ‘Nicaraguan Death Match’. Tyler sits on the padded table inside the lavishly decorated office dressed in a designer hospital-style gown. A large bandage covers Tyler’s forehead where the skin was lacerated severely at The Great American Nightmare. His face still shows a few bumps and a yellow-purple hue hangs below each eye. Various plants and flowers decorate the brightly lit upscale room, with a large aquarium full of exotic fish and other sea life taking up the whole wall on the right side of the room. The Greediest Player in the Game looks up impatiently at the wall clock. Tyler: For Christ’s sake! The outburst is followed by a few inaudible mumbles about how important he and his time are and how he should have been seen by now. He fails to realize how similar his personality is to that of a doctor….both have God complexes and feel that they can do things whenever they damn well feel like it. Comes with being great, I suppose. Finally the door opens and in walks Dr. Dominguez, a typical looking Hispanic man standing about six feet tall with black hair, and fair, tan skin. Looking the doctor part in his long white lab coat with white shirt, brown tie, and tan pants underneath, he strides into the room with a corny smile on his face. Dr. Ed: Hello again, Tyler. Are you feeling any better since your last visit? Tyler: Yeah, I’d feel a lot better if you got your ass in gear and got this over with. I’ve been sitting in here for a Goddamn half an hour waiting on you! The good doctor continues to smile, almost like he’s been sedated. A typical fake doctor smile. Dr. Ed: You’re not the only patient I have to see, Mr. Nelson. Nelson points an accusing finger at the doctor. Tyler: I’m not paying you to see other patients, doc! I’m paying you to see ME! I wouldn’t even be here if it weren’t for that Goddamn insurance company. Now let’s get this over with, I’ve got plenty of better things to be doing right about now. Dr. Ed: Very well. I’m sure that nine year old little Johnny with the broken neck down the hall will understand. Tyler: Give him an extra sucker when he leaves. The doctor sighs, still smiling though, and washes his hands before placing the ear pieces of his stethoscope in place. He breathes on the end of the stethoscope to try and warm it up. Dr. Ed: Now this might be a little chilly. Nelson furrows his brow. Tyler: Get on with it already! The doctor presses the stethoscope against Tyler’s back, causing the CEO to stiffen up from the cold metal against his skin. Dr. Ed: Take a few deep breaths. Nelson breathes heavily in, then out, about 4 times before the doctor is satisfied. Dr. Ed: Everything sounds good there. The doctor takes out a pen light and checks it against his hand. Dr. Ed: Have you had any more headaches or blurry vision? Tyler: Only the headache I’m getting right now. Dr. Ed: Hehe…very funny. Look straight ahead, please. Dominguez shines the light into Nelson’s eyes, dilating his pupils somewhat. He finishes up with both eyes and places the light back into his coat pocket. Tyler blinks exaggeratedly as he tries to regain the focus in his eyes. Tyler: Jesus, doc…you trying to look at my brain or something? Dr. Ed: Have to make sure everything checks out medically if I’m going to clear you to wrestle this week. Tyler: Well, I feel fine. I beat the hell out of Emilio Rage and won part ownership in PRIME. How could I NOT feel fine? The smarmy smirk returns to Nelson’s greedy visage as he thinks about the bounty he won at TGAN. The doctor continues to check Nelson’s vitals and reflexes. Tyler: And besides, it’s not like I have a grueling match coming up or anything. Granted, there’s potential there, but Hoyt is on a suicide watch since he lost his belt, who knows if Nova will get off the herbs long enough to even remember he has a match, and Deville knows what side his bread is buttered on so I doubt he’ll be a problem. Sounds like a cake walk after you think about it. Dr. Ed: And how is your little girl? I heard about the nasty fall she took from the ladder. Nelson shrugs nonchalantly. Tyler: Don’t know. I haven’t seen or heard from her. I think the hospital tried to call me with an update once or twice, but I’ve had more important things to deal with. The smile fades from the doctor’s face and is replaced by a look of shock. Dr. Ed: More important than your daughter? Tyler: Well, she’s not really my daughter anyway. And besides, did you see the way she betrayed me? She was going to help that Nicaraguan buffoon win the match! I think that the correct punishment was administered. Dr. Ed: I see. The odd smile returns to the face of the doctor as he opens a drawer in the counter under the sink. He grabs a pair of rubber gloves and a tube of KY Jelly. Dr. Ed: Your records indicate that you haven’t had your prostate checked in quite some time, Mr. Nelson. The CEO’s face goes flush with horror. His jaw drops and he throws his hands out in front of him. Tyler: Waaaait a minute! You are NOT going to do THAT! I have a female doctor that takes care of that, among other things. Dr. Ed: When PRIME assigned me to your case they said that you needed to undergo a THOROUGH physical before you could be released. The doctor squirts a glob of the lubricant on his hand and walks toward Nelson, that stupid ass smile still on his face. Moving outside the office we see Mrs. Beasley quickly approaching the room dressed in a dark green feminine business suit. She anxiously raps on the door just before hearing a high-pitched shriek from inside. Tyler: EEEEGAD!! Dr. Ed: Come in! Mrs. Beasley looks puzzled but opens the door anyway. The news she has simply cannot wait. She rethinks that when she sees Tyler standing and bent over the table, the doctor behind him with two fingers getting ready to move against the current of traffic on the exit only lane of the Hershey highway. She quickly covers her eyes and turns away. Mrs. Beasley: Oh God!! I’m sorry, Mr. Nelson! I’ll come back in a minute. An uncomfortable Tyler groans and motions for her to approach. Tyler: It’s too late for that now. Please tell me you have some good news that will take my mind off of this. The personal assistant doesn’t budge from her position. Mrs. Beasley: I heard back from the lab at Texas A&M. The document is authentic. You are officially the owner of ten percent of PRIME! Before Tyler can get too excited, Dr. Ed shoves the fingers in. Dr. Ed: You’re going to feel a little bit of pressure. Nelson can’t even respond to the outstanding piece of news he just heard. He just has to stand there and try not to cry. There will be time for celebrating later. Happy New Year….inDEED!
