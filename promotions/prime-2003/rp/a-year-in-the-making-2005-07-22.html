A year in the making...

22 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd July 2005 Just over twelve months ago a pair of Russian circus performers joined the ranks of Global Wrestling and started their wrestling career in the Silverdome in Pontiac, Michigan with an amazing debut of speed, flying ability and a general care free attitude as regards to their bodies. Now a year on, in the same Hilton Suite as their first wrestling hotel, Igor Arefiev and Constantino Zergolov sit at a table, preparing for the biggest night of their lives and ultimately their first ever title shot. “Do you remember that night?” asked Igor as the best friends sat over their lunch. The meal consisted of chicken and bacon in a tomato and herb sauce on a bed of fresh pasta. The fact that Silverdome was their debut has failed to escape the two young Russians as they ate in the dining room on Auburn Hills. Zergolov nodded. Both men were silent, the events of that night floating through their mind, both men playing back the same moments in their heads at the same time. Global Wrestling was good to The Flying Vergomovs. They had been given a chance to home their skills at a training camp and then be promoted. Global Wrestling allowed the two men to get a basic knowledge of technical skills and some submission holds. But the one the training camp gave Igor and Constantino was free reign. When they were practising and even competing in exhibition and dark matches Igor and Constantino were allowed to try anything they wanted. What followed was the basis for The Flying Vergomovs in-ring style. Entering the ring over the top rope at all points, walking along the top rope and somersaulting off, using each other as perches to jump from, double somersaults in a splash instead of just one, running up turnbuckles and jumping off, it all started in Global Wrestling and any ripples they caused in the wrestling industry was due to that training. The opening night of The Flying Vergomovs’ wrestling career continued to echo through the minds of Igor and in turn Constantino. 19th July 2004 in the Silverdome, Michigan the Flying Vergomovz as they were called then began their wrestling careers in Michigan. The debut match lasted all of ten seconds. Double dropkicks and then double 450 splashes on the opposing tag team and TFV had their first match victory and more importantly their first squash. Granted their debut didn’t showcase their flair and style along with their skill but it proved it would mean they could get the job done. “Yeah I remember…” came the audible reply from Zergolov. He had just finished a mouthful of lunch and a little tomato sauce was left on his cheek. The night had meant more to the pair than the ten second match would have you believe. Granted the match allowed the pair to get used to working a television show without having to put on a huge match, but the occasion really got the most of The Flying Vergomovs. Walking into the Silverdome over 12 months ago, Arefiev and Zergolov were like deer caught in some headlights. The arena was huge. Ask anyone and they will agree. The Silverdome is one of the grandest scales that someone will ever compete. Just standing there, being amongst the wrestlers was a realisation for the two Russians. As they stood and watched the likes of Jesse Jamester and Josh train, Igor realised a dream had been fulfilled. The dream that would mean financially security fro the young Russian had been easily achieved. One year’s employment with the three federations that the pair had worked with had set them up nicely. They had earned more than they could ever have imagined while remaining circus performers. Igor and Constantino had bought a house in California, an apartment in New York and could retire on Monday morning after Colossus, safe in the knowledge of a relaxing retirement. But something had happened in the past twelve months. Something had changed in the line of thought that the two men embarked on. They had different perceptions on the wrestling business and the world of wrestling. Wrestling was now something to be conquered. The Flying Vergomovs now care about their success in the wrestling business. They have turned from money hungry young men that were only out to get a pay packet from a wrestling promotion into a tag team that want to be the pinnacle of their discipline. Sitting at the table, both men were aware of the other’s change of opinion. The looked up at each other as they came to the same conclusion. Wrestling was no longer a gravy train to be ridden by The Flying Vergomovs. Wrestling was now their career. Wrestling had become their life. Neither man would be happy until they had tamed wrestling in the same manner that they had conquered the circus world. And the planned taming begins on Sunday. The training was complete. The homework was done. The Halo had been examined. The Flying Vergomovs knew the task in hand. “Do you think we can do this?” asked Constantino. His plate is empty as he sits over the table digesting his food. His eyes lock with his tag team partner as the question lingers in the air. Both men cast their minds back to the weeks training session. Together TFV had perfected a Falling Halo training schedule. Every possibility and angle had been considered. The problems posed by the big men were noted and plans made to counteract them. On Monday morning, Igor Arefiev and Constantino Zergolov had arrived in the early hours to a large junkyard. While there Igor had arranged some very large oil drums to be set up. The mission of the pair was to knock over the securely grounded drums. Easier said than done for two men that struggle to weigh two hundred pounds each as they used dropkicks and running cuddles, I mean spears to try and tip the balance. Eventually the pair worked up some tag team manoeuvres that would knock Azalea Zameer and Los Diablos safely to the mat. Then that afternoon came the simple task to effectively beat the big men, running away. That afternoon at Pontiac Municipal Golf Course where Igor had sweet talked the greens keeper, TFV were sprinting up and down the fairways. They both knew that one-on-one versus the Killer Klown, Igor or Constantino had about as much chance as a snowball in hell. And after day one, they had perfected some methods to deal with the men that were twice their size. All the small men in the match had been studied and any problems they might have caused were anticipated and prepared for. And so came the second day of training and so came the weight room. The plan for the small guys was to match them pound-for-pound. Lifting weights and working on their strength to give them something. Looking around the match, Igor and Constantino noticed that most of the small guys can take the big bumps. All the small guys can speed up the tempo of the match. Therefore their plan was to hit them with something they weren’t expecting, power. Day two came and went and the weights were lifted and put down. The strength was built up and the hard hitting moves were perfected. Individually The Flying Vergomovs were ready for every member of this match. They are ready for this match and this without the help of the great manager James von Doom. But they both knew that there would sixteen men trapped inside the Halo on Sunday night. Queue the bar brawl. This part was the brainchild of Constantino. When they entered the match attacks would be raining down on everyone or so they expected and to prepare they wanted to be in the same kind of situation, well at least Constantino thought so. He knew that they needed to be prepared for multiple attacks from all directions and taking different shapes. So he decided to start a barroom brawl in a local bar. Of course, Igor, who thought they were out for a quiet drink, knew nothing of the plan as the brawl started and began fighting for his life. Constantino used it as a learning curve watching where the attacks on him and others were focused from whereas Igor was just swinging his arms wildly. Eventually they decided to leave, leaving the fight in full swing. A small cut on Igor’s left arm was the only injury as the pair vacated the delightful premises. Igor found out the idea behind Zergolov’s argument with the guy that brushed past him, and after some disapproval realised it seemed like a pretty decent idea. The next task that The Flying Vergomovs considered was the actual surroundings for the match, the Halo. After spending hours of day four watching the two Dual Halo matches, the Flying Vergomovs had scouted the whole surroundings. The Halo was one of their least worries. They knew that only two other men involved in the Falling Halo match had been inside the Halo so it would be a learning curve for most if not all. They studied the videos anyway, just to have a feel for the insides of the huge structure. And that afternoon, the second part of the surroundings was considered, the Silverdome. The Flying Vergomovs paid it a visit during the week just to get used to the grand scale once more before the Falling Halo. As they walked through the door, Igor felt like a beginner again. The emotions of the Global debut came flooding back as the pair walked around some of the higher seats. They looked around the huge building as memories of their ten second match filled their minds. Having Colossus II at the Silverdome made sense to them. It was the end of their first year as professional wrestlers. Colossus II would be the beginning of their next chapter and what better place to do it than where they began. The magnitude of the Silverdome didn’t deter The Flying Vergomovs. The magnitude would be a spur to The Flying Vergomovs, a spur to the thought of just the heights that Igor Arefiev and Constantino Zergolov could achieve as wrestlers. Both men will deny tears were shed, but they were, as they left the Silverdome feeling incredibly confident about having their match there. And finally, on day five, The Flying Vergomovs prepared for their biggest task of the Falling Halo training, gaining weight. The final training session was Igor’s idea. He bought two pouches and filled them with weights. At first, Constantino approached the idea bemused. Igor then explained that if they wanted to be Champions they had to get used to carrying a little extra weight. And with that, the training scheme the pair had devised had been revised in their minds. “Yeah,” nodded Igor, “I know we can win…” adding a smile to his comment looking at Zergolov. Constantino smiled as well as the pair got up from the table. The Flying Vergomovs appearing at Colossus II was a year in the making. Their first shot at some Tag Team Gold was a year in the making. Hopefully the biggest victory of their careers was a year in the making.
