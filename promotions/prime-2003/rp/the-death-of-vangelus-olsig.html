It was a difficult night for me, attempting to balance the task assigned to me with the death of my fiancee. The fact that I hadn't even informed my son yet didn't help matters any, either. If anything, it worsened them simply because now I was forced to fight back emotions and think on the fly in order to keep things hidden. I was putting Dillon to bed. He'd fallen asleep in my arms while watching a movie. Afterwards, I made my way downstairs in order to grab a snack per usual. The tears were still there...the heartache was still there, but it all subdued at the sight of Vangelus Olsig, the 62 year old, former wrestler who'd befriended Richard many years ago, bursting through my front door with a .22 magnum in hand. "PUT YOUR HANDS UP!" he shouted, prompting me to do so immediately. I couldn't say a word. I was in complete shock as he held the gun pointed to my head. Slowly, he reached over and switched off every light except for a very dim one. Quickly, he grabbed me and wrapped his arm around my neck, restraining me as he pressed the cold barrel of the gun against my temple. "I know everything, Sherri. How DARE you kill not only your fiancee, but a dear friend of mine." I couldn't speak...he was choking the life out of me. "ANSWER ME!" Still I couldn't speak. Suddenly, the front door burst open once more. This time it was Thomson. According to Thomson, all he could see was my body, which was illuminated by the dim light, and the gun pressed against the side of my head. "WHAT TH-..." He immediately stopped himself and yanked out a gun of his own, blasting three shots. Blam, Blam, Blam! The sound of the blasts still ring throughout my head. Suddenly, I felt the choke hold loosen and the gun withdraw. Vangelus crumbled to the ground, underneath the dim light. Thomson panicked. I panicked. Vangelus Olsig died. "Thank you, Mrs. Polzine." ---- Two years ago, I lost my brother. Harvey admitted to killing him. This morning, we lost Harvey. I admit to killing both him and Richard. Harvey, because he killed my brother. Richard, because he took my woman. The world doesn't know that, however. And they never will. BLAM!
