Living.

12 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-12-05 [ 'Living' ] I know what you're thinking. Vangelus Olsig... The Lackey to the Dark Age. The pet of Vampir Nosferatu. The body shield of Boda. I don't blame you. Because to exposed judgement, I am just that. Killean Sirrajin showed me last week the error of my ways. He showed me the fault in the Dark Age in which I had been so blind to. He showed me what it would be like to be... Normal. Instead of portraying a punk teenager who only lives for the motivations of blood and suffering from others. For that Killean... I thank you. Killean Sirrjain also is the one in who's shadow I stand. He is the top dog, and I am but a mere obstacle. To him, and to the world, Im am nothing more than a warm up. Something to boost interest in the Revolution main event. For portraying me as such Killean... I hate you. I hate you for thinking of me as that little 'Kid' who hangs out with the vampire and the fossil. I hate you for making me look like some nobody who needs council support. I hate you for portraying me as a lost soul, seeking destination. I hate you for making the world think the same. You sought to restore faith in my torn soul... But instead you only restored the depression which brought me to this state in the first place. You wanted to make yourself look like the hero. Make yourself look like the good guy. All while everyone threw their jokes and laughter upon my shoulders. The poor helpless kid who couldn't win to save his life. The poor helpless kid who's name has to be put in headlines when he actually wins a match. The poor helpless kid... Who stands no chance in hell of competing for the Universal Championship. I am more than what you have made me out to be. I am more than what you have made so many, even myself, believe. I am not the helpless brat anymore. I am not the Lackey anymore. I am not Dani Furher anymore. I am Vangelus Dante' Olsig. A man seeking a legacy which had been stripped away from me the moment I joined the Dark Age. I can't promise a win at Revolution. But I can promise revenge. Revenge for the hell you have put me through. Revenge for the image you have painted of me. Revenge...for making me hate me. ----- I remember that day. The fire ripped through the curtains like a pack of wild sheep as I stood there as a spectator to the madness. Solemn, I said nothing whilst a smile crept unto my face. I stood there, at the foot of my parents' burning bed, crying out inside of my tortured soul for my mother...my real mother. The woman who raised me up from humble beginnings and never gave up on me, no matter what the situation. The woman who rubbed my ailing belly as I lay awake in pain. The woman that I hadn't wanted to lose. No one heard my crying, though. Not the lawyer, not the judge, not the child psychiatrist who insisted I would survive trauma and ultimately be a better person. No one ever heard the cries of this nameless, faceless, helpless little eight-year old boy who should have never lost his mommy. The helplessness expired in due time as I watched the painful images of my past burn before my very eyes. The fire danced across the room, creeping up the walls. I found salvation in the fact that Laura Roberts and Timothy Roberts, these imposters of my real family, would be burning in hell where they both belonged. As the flames danced towards me, I ran into the hell hole that Laura and Tim had given me as a room. I opened the closet, peering inside before pulling up the dashboard retrieving a brown cardboard box. I opened it up and pulled out the only solace I had left. The only emblem I could call my own that provided me with the joyful memories of my 'real' mother, not these no sell imposters. I took the locket necklace and placed it around my neck before turning around just in time to see the flames making there way near me. I grabbed the blanket off of my bed wrapping it around my barren body as I ran directly through the fire unto the outside of the room. From here, I sprinted down the steps and into the living room, taking a seat in the wooden rocking chair and glaring through the glass window at the outside where the snow fell ever so serene. I hugged the locket close to my chest as I rocked back and forth imagining myself inside of the warm comforting arms of my mother as she caressed me with her sweet melody... 'Hush little baby don't say a word...' The fire began to break through the wood of the ceiling crushing tiny bits of the forsaken wood down unto the even more cloaked floor below. I felt the warm wood fall unto my shoulder, but only took it as the warmth that my mother withheld. 'Momma's gonna buy you a mocking bird...' A tear streamed down my right cheek. My mother should have still been here...this shouldn't have been some type of irrelevant dream...it should hold credibility. But it doesn't and the very thought pissed me off to no end. 'And if that mocking bird don't sing...' As sorrow filled my trouble soul, I found comfort in the sound of the wooden ceiling caving in over my head, for I knew that just above, those hellions who mocked themselves as my real parents...who used my weakened and battered spirit as a heir of their entertainment purposes, were up there swimming in the flames of death. In this I found salvation. 'Momma's gonna buy you a diamond ring...' My eye lids peeled open as I quickly surveyed my surroundings. From instant assumption, I was lying on my back in my Boston home. I attempted to think of my reason for being here, but it hurt to think. I rolled over to my side and at instant glance spotted a needle. A drug enhanced needle. No longer did I need to think. Over the past couple of days I had become a slave to this demon drug, submitting to its every request and yielding to its every desire. When the realization that I was a lacky to the dark age set in, I turned to the drug for relief. When my career looked dim, I turned to the drug for comfort. When burdens grew heavy, I turned to the drug for salvation. Pretty soon I had known it as my only friend. But look what it had done to me. It had torn my already battered life apart like an untamed beast. What I once perceived as mere self-pity had turned into depression. What I once perceived as bad luck had quickly transformed into a lifestyle. This drug had taken my already torn life and ripped apart any hopes of its revival. This was no way to live. I needed an escape. The previous time I had attained to this point, I turned to this drug as my escape...but now the drug had become the main accomplice behind my concoction of depression. I needed a better escape. A Permanent escape. I turned stomach first on the trashed living room floor and pushed myself up to my knees. I slowly began to climb with every bone in my body throbbing with a colossal mass of pain. It was the type of the pain that could not be described in words...you would have to feel it to understand it. But through the pain, I continued to crawl for I knew that all of the pain...not just in my body, but also the pain of life was soon going to end. All I needed was that escape...and the troubles of this world would be nothing but a distant memory left to burn in the deep gallows of Hell. finally I managed to reach the dresser after crawling for what seemed like an eternity. I pulled myself up and looked at myself in the mirror that presented itself. I examined my reflection, observing the face of a man who had nothing left to live for. Usually you would think that a guy who was on the verge of a world title shot would be smiling right about now...but not me...for I had no reason to smile. My life was nothing more than an eternal bliss coped with Hell's most fiery vehemence. It seemed as if I was just a mere spectator in life, taking notes in hope of one day becoming a part of said factor. Not even the Universal Championship could restore my joy. I reached in the drawer and withdrew a cocked pistol. This was going to be my escape from the chasm so boorishly named life. For the first time in years, a smile crept unto my battered face, and this time...it was real. Not a painted smile, a real smile. Why was I smiling, you ask? For the simple fact that I had been assured the proper assurance that this life in which I so heartily concurred was amongst seconds of ending. A 25 year span of the oh so familiar themes of Death, Drugs, Sex, Violence...they were all coming to an end with the pulling of this trigger. That’s all it was going to take to finally be relieved of this prison. I placed the gun's cold barrel against my forehead before taking a deep breath. 'Good Bye world.' I mumbled the words and slowly began to press the trigger. Suddenly, I looked down at the counter once again and spotted a piece of paper sitting under a coaster. I stopped in my tracks, delaying my slow and painless death for a few moments to indulge the contents of this note. I placed the gun down for a moment and retrieved the note. To: Dani Furher Hey Dani! I must say that I am your biggest fan. I love the way you fly across the ring with such fluid motions and your match last week with Killean was awesome! You have a big Universal Championship match this week and I know that you can pull it off. Please Dani...I want to see an underdog prevail for once. Win this one...for me. Thanks. Signed David Peterson. A fan letter? I had never received a fan letter before. It wasn't the thought that someone actually liked me enough to write me, though...it was the fact that someone actually believed in me. It was the fact that one person decided to go against the grain of what everyone else was saying and actually install faith in me. Suddenly the bad hand life had dealt to me began to take a turn for the better. It wasn't the best thing that could happen to a person, but at least I now had something to live for. I was going to step inside of that ring and deal him the biggest shock of his life. I knew he underestimated me. I knew he expected little from me...and to be quite honest, I expected little from myself as well. But what I did expect was that I was going to put up a fight. Win or lose, the name Vangelus Olsig was going to ring throughout that arena as the boy with heart. I took the gun and placed it back inside of the drawer...I was no longer going to need it. My life had all new meaning now. David Peterson... My savior from death. ----- Take me to the water. I want to be baptized. Baptized for placing myself in a hell hole. Baptized for playing third fiddle to the Dark Age, thus downing my true potential. Baptized... for relinquishing unto Killean Sirrajin the torment in which he has forced upon me. Forgive me father... for I know not what I do. ----- The air in here was thick. Places such as this crafted a sick emotion in the core of my stomach. I didn't know what it was...maybe it was the fact that I was out in public for the first time in nearly two weeks. My life, which hung barely by the strings of a barrel, took an immediate and bravura turn for the best as I received the news that I, Vangelus D. Olsig, had a fan who believed in me. On paper it may seem dull and an obvious plea for sorrow, but in my heart...it was the one thing I needed to keep me going. Never in my life had someone actually believed in me...even going to the lengths of making sure I knew it. Laugh if you will, but my life now had brand new meaning...And with this new meaning, I felt an inner peace which could very well carry me to victory against a man whom one day ago I would never have even dreamed of beating. Funny how much a difference life holds when you're on camera and when you're all alone. Here I am again, in the middle of a local Starbucks, wandering off in space, thinking about a distant future which has yet to even come. It is this in which has brought me to such an awry of mental breakdown. Its always been said that your best friend is your mind...True enough in a sense. But to me, my mind was nothing but an escape from the abyss of this tired and war torn world. I picked up the cup of cappuccino and pressed the tip of the lid against my mouth, sipping the creamed fevered liquid before placing the cup down. I placed my tongue on my bottom lip, savoring any left over flavor from the steamed cappuccino. Things in life were certainly looking up for me. I wasn't craving a Portentous life filled with the earth's dearest perks...just something simple. Something...decent. I think its safe to say that I have that now. I looked down and checked my watch. It was about time to head out. I grabbed my coat and motioned to stand when all of the sudden I heard a sweet and serene voice near my ear. I looked up and saw something that life had been depriving me of for way too long....a woman. Not just any woman...a woman with such beauty that it seemed as if it was carefully crafted by the worlds finest. I placed my coat down figuring that I would stick around for a while. 'Ugh...Mister? Is Anyone sitting here.' I snapped out of my state of admiring her beauty and recognized her question. I answered her stating that no one was sitting there as she took a seat in the chair directly in front of me. Why had life been so cruel to me? Didn't matter now, though, because I had the upper hand on life now and this array of artwork that came in the form of the woman sitting in front of me was more than enough evidence of that. 'Wow...its really crowded in here today, eh?' 'Yea...it really is.' 'So what is it you do for a living Mister...' 'Olsig. Vangelus Olsig' 'Ah...Mister Olsig. What is it you do for a living?' '...' I paused for a moment. She had asked me a question that, for the moment, I didn't even know the answer to. Yea you could call me a professional wrestler, but when you look at it, am I really a professional wrestler or just an actor? A Winner or a gimmick? Being in the Dark Age made things lean more towards the latter. 'I'm a professional wrestler.' I pressed out anyway. I mean, what else was I going to tell her I was...a clown? 'Wow...really? I love to watch wrestling. Who do you work for?' 'A company by the name of PRIME.' 'That is awesome. I'll have to check you out sometime.' 'Yea.' Check me out eh? Well she couldn't possibly be checking me out more than I was checking her out. I smiled thinking that maybe I had got off on the right foot for once in my life. She seemed to be very fascinated in my chosen profession, so I continued to entertain her notion with tales of my past...a fictional past of course, seeing as in reality I had really achieved little o boast about. 'World Champion? Wow, you're awesome!' Yep...It was definitely fictional. I continued to entertain her with On-the-spot stories as time pressed on. I was scheduled to be catching an early flight to Canada in order to prepare for the event...but obviously the scheduled time in which I was supposed to be there had past. I was all caught up in this dwelling and didn't wont to let go of the moment...not even for one second. 'So, Vangelus. Do you like to read?' 'I love to read.' 'Awesome!' By the way...she was a blonde. You can tell by the random expressions of excitement. 'I wrote this book...its entitled a Desperate Song. Would you care to read it?' 'Desperate song, huh? Funny how related I feel to that.' 'Excuse me?' 'Ah...nothing. Sure, I would love to read your book.' 'Awesome!' She reached down into her bag to retrieve the book as I wiped the sweat from my eyebrows. That was a close one...my big mouth nearly had this woman believing I was a depressed maniac. Wait a minute...I am a depressed maniac. Well at least now I was a maniac with a femme attraction to the women of the world. I patiently waited as she searched thoroughly for the book. Finally she popped up with the book in hand, reaching across the table to grant me with it. I looked at the cover...a clown weeping. Much relation to myself...a clown. Weeping. 'Vangelus?' I jerked my head up as she called my name. I had become so involved in the cover of the book that I didn't hear a word she had said unto me. 'I asked if you would like a cup of coffee?' 'Ah...no thanks. I must be leaving. I have a flight I’m supposed to meet.' 'Oh Okay. Well, here’s my number. Call me when you're finished with the book.' My eyes grew wide. I thought life had treated me with desert just through the option of talking to this Golden beauty...but now...her phone number as well? Sure it may not seem like much to the mortal man, but after being trapped in a cerebral closet for 25 years forced by life to play the role of a desperate little boy...It had become a big deal to me. She took out a pen and wrote her number on the back of a business card. I stood over her, watching as her writing flowed so beautifully. She handed me the card. 'There ya go. Maybe we could hook up sometime.' 'Right.' I smiled and took the card and began to walk out of the door. If this was how life was going to be treating me from now on, then I was definitely looking forward to tomorrow, and the day after, and the day after, and the da-...ah well you get the jest. ----- You killed Dani Furher. Yet you gave birth to Vangelus Olsig. You killed a weak and tempting man. Yet you gave birth to a strong man with a mind of his own. You killed a lacky with nothing more to live for than the approval of his peers...Boda...and Vampir Nosferatu. Yet you gave birth to a man who tells them both to go to hell. You killed the the man everyone bounced on to get to the top. Yet you gave birth to a man who is already on top. For that I can never repay you. But for causing this strife...you can repay me. The belt means little... Your blood means everything. ----- Moments passed as I finally withdrew the needle from my arm. The effects still took its toll, though, as I tossed the needle to the ground and fell back unto my king sized bed looking up at the ceiling. I may have had a meaningful life, but I was still an addict. Deep Thoughts began to ponder...flashbacks of painful memoirs. But I always found that same comfort in this feeling. I began to think of Revolution...only three days away. I began to think of Killean Sirrajin's faithful fall from grace...only three days away. But most importantly, I began to think of the Universal Belt, My Universal Belt...only three days away. The thought alone brought forth a rare joy that had been missing from my battered soul for much, much too long. I was not a huge fan of life, but these things drew me closer and closer into the fold. I could barely wait. The thing that meant so much to me was finally coming back home...and as a complimentary prize, I get to see Killean Sirrajin bask in the oh so familiar pool defeat. Ah, Life is sweet. I closed my eyes, hoping to gain a measure of rest...but that became short lived as I felt a cool chill embrace my body. I brushed it off, imagining it as maybe the wind from outside creeping through a peak. The chill, though, came once again…this time with even more fury. I peeled my eyes open and sat up, surveying my surroundings to find nothing. I started to lay back down, but then I heard a creek sound. I looked over to the door of my room and saw that it was opening, the only problem, though, was that no one was opening it...It was opening by itself. Chill Bumps invaded my entire body as I knew not what to expect. What was going on...that was the question that tainted my soul. 'Hello?' I screamed out...but no answer. I spoke out once again, asking if anyone was there...but the same result. The cool wind began to blow with an incomprehensible fiery as a mild whispering hiss began to sound throughout the room. I jumped to my feet, grabbing my knife from the top of my dresser and began to slowly tip my way over to the door. Before stepping fully through the doorway, I took a slow peak making sure that no danger was on the other side. After confirmation, I stepped through the doorway not knowing what to expect. I looked all around me, but nothing was there. It was if the demon drug in which I had become slain to was playing some sick cruel joke on me. I began to hear a tapping noise as I jerked around swinging the knife...but it was nothing but the ticking of the granddaddy clock. To say I was in a state of paranoia would be an understatement. Without warning nor expectation, I heard someone whisper my name. The voice was ever so familiar, but I could not make out where it was coming from. I heard it again and begin to yell out, asking who was there and where they were, but the voice gave no specific answer...instead it called my name once again, this time not whispering it but yelling it. 'Over here' it said afterwards. I looked behind me at the dress up mirror that stood there to lay claim to the face of the last man I would have expected to see on this day...My father. Background Check. My father was an evil man...he played a significant role in the death of my dear mother. I can still remember those screams, every single night after he would return home from work and find no dinner on the table. They were not his screams, they were her screams. Her screams as a result of his constant beatings. The smacks and the kicks rang through my ears. The blood that covered her face. I couldn't take it...I crumbled to my knee's holding my head, trying to shake off the images, but they only grew worse. It wasn't her fault that we had no dinner, we simply could not afford it. The blood, the tears, the screams, the agony, It all began to pound my head forcing painful memories. I screamed. 'Dani...look at you. What have you become.' Suddenly the images stopped as I heard his voice. I lifted my head up and looked at him standing before me in that mirror. Was this a sign? I had made a promise to myself never to follow in the footsteps of him, but now as I looked through the mirror, I saw his reflection instead of my own. Putting the pieces together, I began to understand it all...Deep down, I was just another version of something I promised myself I would never become...My Father. 'My name is Vangelus Olsig...not Dani.' 'Your name will always be Dani to me.' He said. But it was not my father. For the man's voice had changed. It was now a deep toned voice which had my bones trembling with every echo. My eyes widened as the figure in the mirror stepped forward...and it was not my father at all. Vampir Nosferatu. 'You have grown weak Dani, and I know the reason why. You have been brain washed, lied to, and swiveled into believing that you're above the Dark Age. Dani, you're not above the Dark Age...you ARE the Dark Age. You can't escape that...no matter how hard you try. The sooner you realize this, the sooner you shall be saved from this torture.' I began to shake my head. I did not want to believe what he was saying, but something kept nagging at me on the inside, telling me that his words...his dreadful words...they were all true. Suddenly he grabbed his masked and ripped it off of his face. Needles Scharf. My alter ego. The man who told me that HE was Dani Furher. The man...or should I say spirit, who told me that HE was the cause of my blood spree. He now stood before me, as the culprit of it all. 'I can see the rejection on your face. You don't want to believe this...but believe it, because it has become more than evident. Remember this...' That’s when it came. His image faded and was then replaced with the Image of Jessica, my long deceased lover. Anyone who knows the story of Vangelus Olsig knows about Jessica. They know about how much I loved her. They know about how I treated her. And most importantly, they know about how my actions drew her into suicide. Hatred began to boil inside of my body as I saw her standing there holding a gun up to her head. Suddenly a loud bang rang through the air nearly shaking the ground. The next image was Jessica lying on the floor...blood leaking from her forehead. I cupped my hands over my mouth, not wanting to believe what I just saw. The images didn't stop there as I saw Needles walk back unto the scene. He stood over the lifeless body of Jessica as his cold eyes met mine with a sickening smile on his face. 'You see Vangelus...when you drew your dear wife into suicide, you took the first step in welcoming me into your mind. But what do we have here? You wanting to change your name...wanting to make the world believe that you have finally overcome your demons and turned back into the man you were born as? You think that now, since you have your little Universal title shot that you're mister big shot, eh? Well guess what...you can change your name to whatever you want...but you will ALWAYS be Dani Furher...' I screamed and through my fist into the mirror sending the glass shattering everywhere thus destroying the image of Needles. I looked at my fist which was now covered in blood. This is what I had become. I had been driven into a state on insanity. The Universal Title was now my only savior from throwing myself into the grave. Without it, I would surely crumble. Without it, I would surely suffer. Without it, I would surely reach that breaking point. That’s why I was going to head into Revolution Wednesday with a fury that no man has ever witnessed. Wednesday was the night in which I either gain it all, or lose it all. Killean Sirrajin may have the favor on his side, but I have an advocate that no man can conquer. My time in the spotlight is on a hasty debut. I know that my chance has come...and I'll fight with every limb to obtain it. ----- Theres a part of me that wants to rip you limb from limb. Scattering your blood all across the canvas for all of the world to see. Exposing you of your fault. I know this because it is building up inside of me. Burning inside of me. Tearing away at my heart like a ruthless animal. Slowly this wall of seclusion is dissoluting. Slowly what little mercy I had for you is clawing away. Slowly, my hatred for you is growing. I tried to keep it as simply business. But No. I tried to keep it on a respecful level. But No. I tried to tame this inner hatred. But No. Your ego had to tarnish it all. Your inner desire to drag me across that ring and batter me prevented it. Through doing this, you have drained me of my soul. Drained me of any signs of mercy. And with that draining comes the fact that I can and will do anything to you. My words, to you, are meaningless... But my actions will be authentic. In Three days... In front of your own people. On your night. I will embarass you, shaming your legacy and creating one of my own. You have given me this opporutunity... I will not dissapoint.
