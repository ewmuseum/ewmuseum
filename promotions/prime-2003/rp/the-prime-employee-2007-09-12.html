The PRIME Employee

12 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-12-07 Like a fart in the wind… That’s how your hopes will vanish if you don’t follow this guide, future PRIMEates! SALUTATIONS! Welcome to the Sports Entertainment conglomerate called PRIME where our modus operandi is to create the stars of tomorrow and put out the best damn product, bar none. Before you begin today’s lesson, know that undergoing lengthy training methods in order to carve your niche into the annals of wrestling godhood are a thing of the past. Spend countless hours in a gym memorizing monotonous routines? Who does that? NOT US, THAT’S WHO! Working hard and paying your dues? Fuck that! In today’s fast-paced world, the only absolute certainly is that there are no certainties. As the premier "build ‘em up and ship ‘em out" facility of Sports Entertainment, we’ve effectively learned through trial and error what will get you to celebrity in this business lickedy-split. For proper tutelage, we’ve streamlined extensive training guides into a straightforward series of dos and don’ts that’ll give you the know-how to avoid those horrible procrastination habits and start you on the path to success! It is our hope this guide serves as the conduit to which you may fulfill your dreams of one day attaining fame and greatness. Familiarize yourselves with these basic rules of operation and perhaps it could be YOU with your mug plastered in between Nova and Tchu at the next Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony! To properly elucidate our guide, let’s look at our shining examples: MEET THE PLAYERS These two men from very different walks of life traveled dissimilar roads in order to arrive at their destinations. The path of Mr. Silver, Chairman of PRIME was predestined when he set foot in our doors. Signed immediately, he was over like parachute pants. With charisma to burn, an astounding ring presence, a nonpareil drive to succeed, and a cornucopia of catchphrases, superstardom was his oyster and titles his pearls. Some internet wrestling communities referred to him as "1337HARDKOR~11!!11!" Sidelined several months ago with an arm injury at OverKill, his return is a big question mark, but the impact left upon our fans and fellow performers alike during his tenure was undoubtedly an unforgettable one. Sonny Gabriel Silver joined our ranks right after OverKill, making egregious claims of deserving the hallowed spot at the top of Mt. PRIME while falsely extolling his own abilities. He subjected many to stories of wrestling in irrelevant indy circles for twenty years and got X-Pac Heat quickly. Like a child, the baby was ignored until he lashed out with a temper; kicking, fussing, and generally being a nuisance backstage. Baby got his way and when he got his chance to drink from the cup of stardom at Colossus IV, he choked and subsequently went on to create some lackluster performances over the following month. He thinks he’ll win the Universal Title come DDT, but success is to him what virginity is to Paris Hilton: had it once long ago and lost it just as quickly. There you have it. One good. One bad. Mr. Silver did everything correctly to get here and was handsomely rewarded and revered by his peers. Sonny Silver, in contrast, generally whined the whole way and wasted his time trying to forge his own destiny rather than take to heart our helpful tips and bypass all the tragedies that have shaped him into the Walking Angry Bitch Monster we see today. If only he’d listened. DOS AND DON’TS And now, the crème de la crème of our guide. Now that you’ve been properly greeted and you’ve met our players, it’s time to divulge the secrets that separate mediocrity from magnificence. Study these rules closely and greatness will be yours in no time! DO As you’re told when it comes to your promos. Read from the cue cards they’ve worked hard to prepare. Trust the people that write your dialogue. There’s a reason they were given this job: THEY KNOW WHAT THEY’RE DOING. Example: Blaine Blair and Mr. Silver iron out the details of a promo. Blair hands him a sheet of paper and jabs his thumb to accentuate the importance of certain parts. Blaine: Now, make some bold proclamation like… uh, "I’m Str8 Outta Compton, Bitch." Then wait for laughter. Mr. Silver: I’m Str8 Outta Compton, Bitch! Blaine(laughing): Wonderful! DON’T Cut the same promo, week in and week out. You’ll lose fan interest quickly. Example: Sonny filming a promo for the upcoming Roulette match. Sonny: I’ve been here twenty years! I deserve this shot! I should have won at Colossus! I should’ve won at Colossus! I should’ve… *vinyl rips off-camera* Sonny: Aw, fuck. Record skipped again. DO Engage in trash talk in a variety of ways. Whether heel or face, trash-talking is a great utility for retaining attention. Keep it both gripping and entertaining. Example: Battle Raps. Mr. Silver cuts one on opponent. You think you can insult me? I’m the guy with the nutsack! Y’all should go to the sperm bank If you wanna get your comeback. DON’T Recycle the same trash talk. Silver: I’m gonna hurt you! Your title will be mine! Then… then I’m gonna get all pissed, make mean faces in the camera… no, wait, I’ll do that before… I’ll talk about your dead parents! OOOH, SCARY! DO Give clever names to your signature moves. Dragon Sleeper with Hammerlock? Nah, to Mr. Silver, that’s The Human Torture Chamber! Ura-nage Suplex? GAY. The Great Jesus Driver sounds catchier. That stupid Frog Splash? That’s now a 90-Degree Flying Space Pressure Drop Hold the Mayo! Catchin’ on now? DON’T Use the same restholds for ten minutes. We cater to an audience that’s of a much grander and more prosperous scale than those that masturbate to Samoa Joe matches with poor resolution. While we’re on the subject, hitting people all match is only cool WHEN THE SAP IS INTERESTING! You look like a putz when you put on the same boring angry face. DO Make all eyes on you. Your entrance must captivate the eyes. People call you pretentious? No, you’re flashy! So what if you just started? Don’t be a fucking tightwad and go balls-to-the-wall with your pyro. Lasers, pyro wheels, the whole nine. Select music with lyrics that REALLY pwninate someone or depict your personality well. Get some rapping midgets, too. They’re a keeper. DON’T Use generic red flames. EVERYONE uses flames. Also, do not be wishy-washy with entrance themes. That just gives people migraines and it wastes PRIME’s budget on royalties. DO Prepare for change. Be a Human Swiss Army Knife at the ready; always become accustomed to any situation. The key to preserving longevity is how well you can adapt in an ever-changing business. DON’T Be formulaic. Formula is for toddlers and scientists and I don’t see a bottle in your mouth or you adorning a lab coat, Einstein. DO Remember there are no friends when it comes to championships. What you do outside the confines of the squared circle is your prerogative, but inside is where conflicts lie. You’re all here for one goal and that goal is to succeed, not to start The Superfriends v2.0. DON’T Make friends at all if you can avoid it. There’s others reading this, too, and if you don’t take to heart we have to say, they might. DO Execute a nice swerve when an apposite situation presents itself. You’re now immersed in a world embodying Darwinism and you must do what’s best for #1 (Ex: You!) if you want to continue thriving: Example: The break-up of Silver & Gold after 2½ years: Gamble hits the ropes, and just as he bounces back... CLOTHESLINE!!! Nick: ... Richard: ... Troy hits the mat hard as Sonny shakes his arm out, half-smiling. Nick: THAT DAMN SILVER JUST CLOTHESLINED HIS OWN PARTNER! WHAT THE HELL IS HE THINKING?! Richard: I think he's thinking that Fuck You! needs a little Silver Lining. DON’T Try to kid yourself by saying "people are really good on the inside." People are merely afraid to act any other way than what’s perceived as "good" to avoid mutually assured destruction. Don’t take ten years to realize the fact. DO Be ready to miss countless holidays, special occasions, firsts (baby’s first steps, words, birthdays, etc.). This job is your mistress now. That obsessive, loving gaze usually reserved for your wives and significant others should be saved only for her now. DON’T Make a personal pact to yourself to abstain from intimacy while you travel these roads, then renege on your stance for a night of passion, ESPECIALLY with faulty prophylactics. Otherwise, you create a child you never wanted in the first place and because of Federal Law, are forced to love the mistakes you made and be ensnared into a shotgun wedding that will crumble under the weight of passion for your OTHER job. DO Brace for the fact that no matter what alignment you wish to portray, many will think of you as a bad person. Prepare to be judged once you claim success. Prepare for every last minute aspect of your life to be put under the microscope. Prepare to be treated like some sideshow attraction and be thought of as a steroid-injecting, hooker-banging, spouse-choking, hard-partying freak of nature with nobody’s cares taken into consideration save your own. DON’T Crack under pressure and succumb to everything the media accuses you of doing. Trust us; you don’t want to crack under the pressure. You will not like what you’ll find when you hit rock bottom and for some, there’s no way back to the top. DO Bask in every last achievement you create. Let those serve you as a testament to your capabilities. There is no greater feeling than to know you’ve made something of yourself. DON’T Sit idly by and wonder what could’ve been. "What could’ve been" doesn’t exist. "What is here now" does. DO Make your dream a reality. Make sure that you get back what you’ve put in and never let anyone or anything deter your ambitions. DON’T Let a dream become a baneful obsession. Don’t measure your career by the skulls you’ve cracked. Don’t lead a career based off infamy. Don’t shut out the people that love you because some aren’t even lucky enough to have that. Don’t be too stubborn to accept help from them because your pride controls your every move. DO Leave if you’re miserable. DON’T Keep going until wrestling robs you of everything, including your happiness.
