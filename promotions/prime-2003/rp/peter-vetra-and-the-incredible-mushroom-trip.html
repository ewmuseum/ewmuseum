Peter Vetra and the Incredible Mushroom Trip

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: for along ass time The newest substance in my life Ever get to that point in life where it is intolerable? I mean really intolerable, so bad you think suicide is the only way out? Well it looks like I have reached that point, or so I think. Hell, a couple of weeks ago I lost all my friends in Imperium, and for fucksake Hoyt Williams didn’t have the balls to tell me, he needed to email me. An E-MAIL? What is that shit? Slowly I have found out what life is really about, slowly I have found out that it is not worth living the way I used to live it. Have fun; enjoy your time… because it might be your last chance, your only chance for salvation. It wasn’t until I meet someone and a certain substance did my eyes open to this new way of life. It was like these two things were my new best friends and yet also my enemies, but it will take me weeks to find that out. Hell, one has already proven they are my mortal enemy, not just a simple battle that I need to fight. That’s right I am talking about you Adam, Illustrious Face Eater you make us all call you. It’s your fault, all your fault. A few weeks back, a simple accident caused us to run into each other, at that point you introduced me to another enemy that I’m going to try and knock off in the next couple of weeks, but it will be impossible, it will only lead me further and further down the wrong trail of life. MARIJUANA…. The illegal substance which now controls my daily functions, I have let it become something I need, something I want to need. But if I never met you, I would have never become a fiend, never have become addicted. Yes, I used that word, if only I had stopped there, I wouldn’t have had any problems. But yet again I made the wrong decision. An even newer substance… I don’t like where there is going… As I smoked this blunt to my head, of course I’m using a Honey Dutch, is there any other way to smoke? Nah, not really, I mean just because Facey taught me how to smoke, doesn’t mean I need to smoke pussy ass wraps like he does. Fuck Platinum Watermelon!!!! As I sit here inhaling large amounts of THC, my body is becoming more relaxed, my mind and body are both in a different state, and they are both equal but at the same time extremely different. Slowly I start to rock in my chair; it is a rocking chair you know. With each rock backward and forward my momentum is building after doing this a couple of times the momentum is way too much for my stoned body to handle and it’s almost like I can’t stop my body when the chair is switching its momentum backwards. Well I was able to save myself that time, but the next time it switched momentum I fell right of the chair. And when I fell, I fell hard. I made a THUD sound and did it ever so much hurt, well I guess it really didn’t hurt that bad at the his moment, but I sure as hell can bet its going to hurt real bad tomorrow. As I sit on the floor in a stoner like fashion, you all know what I am talking about don’t make me describe it, we all know you are all pot heads. I look around the room of my apartment, the room is like it would usually be, maybe it is a little dirtier then I like it to be, but hell shit happens. Then I saw it… On top of the counter it laid. A bag filled with my next substance of choice. No, I’m not expanding into the realms of LSD or heroine, hell I am not even trying ecstasy. The bag of mushrooms, or as I will call them through out my experience “shrooms”, were just staring at me, yelling and screaming “EAT ME” , metaphorically of course. It was hard for me to refuse, but at first I did. But however after a beer I was no longer looking to refuse them, but instead enjoy them. Once the beer and THC mixed my mind was no longer in a safe state, I made a bad decision, a real bad decision something I wouldn’t have consider it to be bad idea till hours later. But when I did I would be in trouble. Those same shrooms that were calling me earlier, were still screaming out my name. Begging, calling, enticing me to eat them, and in this state of mind I was going oblige. For anyone who has done them before, the first time is not always your best. I was about to find out how my first time, was going to be. On the table laid the eighth, I ate them, all of them. Another bad decision, never having done shrooms before I didn’t think it would be a bad idea, yet again something I will realize later, it’s a horrible idea to eat that many for your first time. But I did it, I had no idea what this night was about to become… but I could only hope the beer, weed, and shrooms would all mix. If not I’m about to see how bad a trip can really be.60 Minutes later… What a waste of money, nothing is kicking in. This shit sucks if I was in my right mind I would go and kick that drug dealer who sold this to me ass. I mean he told me this shit would fuck me up and nothing is even happening. Oh well… I guess I will just watch a movie. Slowly I stand up and finally the effects are kicking in, the room is a little wobbly, or maybe it was me. The carpet on the floor looks like a bunch of grape vines trying to attack me one leg after another. I guess this shit might have been worth it. I continue to stumble over to my movie rack, looking through the titles the all seemed a little blurry but still interesting. Finally I got to the film that by the end of the night would be my doom. Charlie and the Chocolate Factory, no not that old ass version with Gene Wilder, but the new trippy and fucked up version with Johnny Depp. Some how I manage to get the DVD into the player and got the movie started, I got my glass of orange juice, what you didn’t know that the acid makes the trip more fun? That is the last thing I remember, before it started happening, the trip that is. No longer was I in the right state of mind, instead I saw myself in the movie, I was Charlie Bucket. Fuck for Christ sake the opening credits said Charlie Bucket is being played by Peter Vetra, now that is fucked up. Cue in the cheesy music, I don’t mean like boring shit the music that sounds like bells and whistles and all that other shit you hear in a factory. But the funny part is as I listen to it I start to think of Dr. Seuss, but truth has it he has nothing to do with this movie. The scene cuts and moves fast, sending my brain almost into convulsions, I hear little kids whining but it seems like they are almost singing, gears are turning the bass is killing my head. What do I see is the real question? Well it is simply answered by chocolate, and lots of it. Hundreds upon hundreds of bars of chocolate, in every stage; the creating, the cooling, and even the packaging, with each bar being precisely like the one before it. However there was a couple, actually scratch that, more precisely five bars that were wrapped differently. These were all placed with a special gold piece of paper inside, what did they lead to? No one but he himself knew, he being Willy Wonka the magical chocolateir. Those five “special” tickets were placed in boxes and shipped off around the world; the boxes were labeled to such places as England, United States, Germany, France, and even that hell hole of a country called Ireland. These boxes may or may not contain those golden tickets, only time would tell. Now comes the part where the narrator starts talking but if you have ever done mushrooms you will know anything that is boring and doesn’t keep your attention, so you don’t pay attention to. So there I sit staring at the plant at the wall because the roots coming to get me is far more interesting then watching Grandpa Joe talking about how we are worthless and all we eat is cabbage soup. The plant starts to dance and it is entertaining me for the time being. Tickets Found!!! I start to pay attention now that the first ticket is found and as I sit there and watch the television spin into a circle again it seems as if I am back into the actual movie. There I am sitting in the cottage with my family watching the television or as you British people call it the tube or tele. The first winner is announced; Illustrious Face Eater from Utah, the snow capped mountains of the great north-west. My face is showing that I’m shocked to see a familiar face winning such a great thing, a chocolate bar and a chance to meet Willy Wonka. There he is standing holding a blunt in his hand and all he can say is, “I had the munchies” A good reason for someone to win a special prize, no not really, just another young American who is driven by the munchies to eat after smoking too much pot. A chance in a life time for this poor…poor… stoned man. As I stare at the television watching the news I am glued like the head of Willy Wonka on my toothpaste factory I had made. The second winner was announced, my head turned and twitched with the anticipation of the next winner. Danny Ferguson, a rising star from Hollywood, yet another American. There seems to be a path starting to form. There he stands in his suit and tie thinking this is just another way to get his name on television, just another great way for exposure. There he sat staring at the camera and when asked how he was able to accomplish such a feat all he responded with was, “All I had to do was tell my interns and agents I wanted to get that ticket and boom I had one within a day and a half.” More news that only disappointed me, this guy didn’t deserve it not nearly as much as I did, he didn’t even look for the ticket himself that is cheating. But it didn’t take long for another ticket winner to be produced, number three. That much closer to me not winning, something I am almost used to in life. However before I could start to grieve the third winner was announced and there he stood all decked up looking like Elvis. Long flowing hair and a beard upon his chin made this man unmistakable. Hoyt Williams, the son of God, stood next to his father Duke Williams as they held the third ticket. All that was uttered from either man was, “Praise God our savoir.” Duke looked like he always did that he had ate all the chocolate bars and Hoyt took all the credit. The two of them stood gleaming, Duke still munching on a chocolate bar. Without any notice the screen shot to a new person, a fourth ticket had been found. Standing in front of the television I was watching a man who looked more like a giant then a human being. The man known as to the world as Psymon, his giant hands which could almost be referred to as baseball mitts are holding onto the fourth golden ticket. There I sat around my family, depressed, I had not won a ticket and I have already gotten the one bar of chocolate I was expected to get for my birthday. Oh well let us just sit here and wait for another winner. Fuck, in real life I’m loser but hell even in my tripping dreams I am loser. What a life? I left my cottage and went to look for some change; maybe I could buy won more Wonka Bar and not be a loser. There I go again being selfish only looking for self gratification and not looking to better the family. My venture didn’t go very far, the word on the street was the fifth ticket had been found. I rushed like many of the others to the near by corner store where I watched the news. My eyes were glued to the window, both arms pushing and shoving the others who were trying to get me away so they themselves could get a better look. As I stood in amazement, the fifth person was someone else I knew. What was going on, every winner was a member of PRIME, and there I stood watching the television as they interview Paddy O’Shea with his big cock, yes I am talking about his pet, and not the one Prince refers to in many songs. Bewildered, down in the dumps, what other words could I use to explain my emotions? I was just down and out; I thought this has to be some kind of conspiracy. I start to walk away giving the masses what they wanted, a closer look at a winner. I sulked all the way home, until something caught my eye. In the storm drain there it laid a crisp five dollar bill. I reached in and grab the bill, once I had I ran to the local store. I wanted a chocolate bar, fuck giving the family something good for dinner, hell I’m a kid, what can be expected of me? As I enter the shop which is crowded but nothing compared to what I have seen the last couple of days over this candy craze. As I walk up to the check out I over hear a lady reading a newspaper, “BREAKING NEWS: Fifth Ticket a Fraud. Found by Irish Gypsy.” I look shocked and try and get a peak but I was too short, however I did hear another man say, “What else do you expect from and Irishman!?” Another man is quick to jump down his throat. As the two of them are fighting I’m graciously receiving the candy bar from the clerk. With the news of a fake ticket there is still a chance that this last candy bar could hold the ticket. But why should I get my hopes up? Slowly I pull back the wrap, slowly with every second my heart increasing its pace. Finally I open it to reveal I’m actually the fifth ticket holder, I’m the lucky boy that I always wanted to be. I ran home as fast as I could to try and tell my parents, although along the way I was bombarded with people trying to purchase the ticket from me, something I was heavily contemplating. Running home I was out of breath, but breaking through the door was something I longed to do. When that door busted open I couldn’t even get the words out of my mouth to tell my family what had happened, all I did was wave the ticket in the air. Around me the family became over joyed thus embracing me as the fifth ticket winner. I stood in shock not knowing what to do but happy I had finally won something. All that was left was finding a way to win the special prize the bottom of the ticket spoke of. Sleeping that night was almost unbearable, but I got a couple of hours, most of the anticipation I had was more about seeing what the special prize is, not seeing some chocolate factory. Finally it came, I was outside the factory, no I don’t mean I was standing outside the gate like all those other common folk, I was on the inside standing next to four other men, I was fairly acquainted with. I mean we have Facey, Psymon, Hoyt Williams, and Danny Ferguson. That right there is a cast of men. As the clock ticked on the dot the front doors opened to reveal a stage. Anyone who has seen the Tim Burton version knows what would happen next. But with my mind in state of hallucinogens and disbelief, I couldn’t think about listening to those fucking dolls sing that song and then catch on fire. The weird part is I was imagining those dolls catching on fire long before it actually happened. Then it came, the clapping, the entrance of Willy Wonka. There he stood on the side of the five us who were just looking at shock at the stage were now looking in shock at the man standing next to us. Standing near six foot four and almost another half of foot with his big hat and goofy glasses, there he was the amazing chocolateir. He welcomes us to the wonderful factory and we start the tour. I am trying to leave some of the lame details out for you guys. Once inside it doesn’t take long for us to get acquainted and not even five seconds after dropping his coat on the floor like told Danny Ferguson chimes in with a question for the lead man, Willy Wonka, “So what is this special prize? I am all about winning things.” Wonka comes to an abrupt stop. He pivots on one foot and turns to face the equally as tall “superstar”. Wonka looks at him face to face for a second and then takes his glasses and slides them down his face, doing this exposes his face not as the actor, Johnny Depp but PRIME CEO Tyler Nelson. Tyler lets off an evil laugh for a moment along with a sly grin. “I’m glad you asked that, Mr. Ferguson, I presume. “ As Willy, I mean Tyler Nelson starts to look around he can easily point everybody out and why they are here. After he finishes doing that he continues to explain the prize. “See Danny, I always enjoyed the story of Willy Wonka, so I got in touch with his people and as you know we created new people. I spoke with him about this idea. I wanted to give a tour out to some of the PRIME wrestlers, only difference is I am not about to show my factory to you guys, I am showing you the new Duel Halo. Please follow me, and see it.” “But sir you still haven’t told us the grand prize.” A stoned Facey intervenes trying to sound nice to the superior figure. “Well, my friend, it is quite simple. Who ever does not break my trust and stays with us in the magical ride we will be the winner of the Halo. But we have lots of time and little things to see…. Wait” Tyler Nelson does the hand movement Willy does in the movie. “Switch that around. Lets go!” At this point if you are not following the story but are more interested in the state I am in as a real person and not in the movie, it is rather simple I’m passed out in the chair feeling a complete body high, my mind is in a different universe one that which created what you are seeing right now. But enough with the boring details of how I ‘m slowly seeping into one of the worst trips ever, but lets worry about the fun my brain is having at my body’s expense. We are now back into what at first seemed like a factory but as we look around we can actually tell it is the Duel Halo. It is still being constructed as we speak and there are millions of “umpa loompas” working on it. Only so it coincides with this tripping sequence we don’t see a bunch of little immigrants… well actually we do, it seems Tyler has shrunk down a million little Baltasars and put them to work, they work just like a Mexican should. “Let me show you the beginning ladies and gentlemen, “Tyler looks around nervously, “Actually we don’t that Karina Wolfenden or any other females in our presence so I guess I can just say gentlemen.” Tyler starts to walk over to the first door which will allow us to enter, as we all get in and Tyler Nelson starts to show us the nooks and crannies, or the in and outs of the business we see Facey starting to trail behind. Shortly the smoke detectors are starting to go off. Tyler Nelson looks around with a fierce look on his face. Then standing in the back of the crowd is Facey, quietly puffing on a blunt. The group turns and looks at him. All he is able to say is, “What, I didn’t think you’d mind me puffing a blunt.” Tyler Nelson looks disappointed, he reaches in his top pocket and gets a whistle, he blows it and a couple of the Baltasar miniatures come over and take the blunt from Facey, they put it out. And knock him over so they can carry him out. They sing a comical little song about smoking and not smoking in public. This is actually part of the irony of this story almost everybody on the tour smokes, but yet Facey gets in trouble. After the little Baltasars sing their song they start to carry away Facey and all you can hear as they throw him out the first door they can find is, “What about my sack…..” With that there is a silence and everyone turns to Tyler to look at what is going on. His response is simple and fast, “That’s what you get when you use illegal substances, no chance at winning the Duel Halo. But we must continue on.” As we slowly make our way through the Halo’s different parts we see each person eyeing one another trying to get it so they have the upper hand in the competition to be the winner of the Halo. We stop and Tyler is starting to talk about the steel and how it is built. How to use it your advantage, as this is going on we see Danny Ferguson start snapping pictures. It doesn’t take long for him to get the attention of Tyler. “What are you doing?” “Nothing, taking notes.” “You shall not!” Tyler is stern that he doesn’t want any of the pictures of the new Halo being leaked to the press before the event happens. Well it is too late for that as Danny has the state of art technology and has already sent the images forwarded to his press agent. Tyler finds this out, he blows his little whistle again, but before they come and do their song Tyler takes a moment to speak. “It looks like you can’t be trusted and with this I shall remove you from having any further knowledge of the Halo before hand. Be gone.” After Tyler finishes his statement the little Baltasars or as you might like to call the umpa loompas take away the body of Danny Ferguson. As we continue on to the next part of the Halo it is quite obvious that there is only a couple people left, Psymon, Hoyt Williams, and of course me. Hell I’m the main character of the story if I was out already it would not be very interesting now would it?Tyler turns and looks at the remaining three, “Is there anything you three would like to tell me before we continue on. Because the pact is quickly disappearing and who will be next? I don’t know but I soon shall have only one left and he will be the winner of the 2006 Duel Halo.” We then go ahead a little bit where we see the four men; Tyler Nelson, Peter Vetra, Hoyt Williams, and Psymon standing at the second level of the first Duel Halo. Tyler is explaining something and boredom looks to have forced its way onto the face of Psymon. Psymon looks at Willy or Tyler how ever you prefer to refer to him and utters the following words, “Where is the bathroom?” Tyler looks at him and has a little Baltasar come and lead him to the bathroom. “There is no time to wait for him, let us move onward.” We continue to walk into the second section of the Halo, it’s not long before the small Baltasar that went to bring Psymon is back, or at least the one we think brought Psymon, Tyler leans down nods his head respectively. It’s not long before Tyler stands erect again and speaks, “Well it seems we are one man less.” Look of confusion has set in on the faces of Hoyt and I... Willy Wonka… I mean Tyler Nelson starts to speak again, “It looks like Mr. Psymon looked for more excitement in his life; my friend said Psymon was taking a long time and when he walked in he was watching him do a line of cocaine on the bathroom sink. I can’t have my Halo champion be a coke head. I see we still have two worthy winners, so let us continue.” I sit there and look at my one time friend, now do I consider him a foe or not is something to be decided at a later date. But my mind is playing tricks on my like it has been for the last couple of hours since I took that bag. Nonetheless we continue on making our way into the second part of the Halo. This part is very similar to the first but only difference is the type of staging and what not. Tyler Nelson is standing in front of us showing another secret some other way to put us ahead of the rest of the pack. As Tyler is just about to start another sentence we hear, ‘Personal Jesus' by Depeche Mode. Hoyt reaches into his robe, pulls out his phone, quickly he answers. Tyler Nelson’s face drops, not expecting have one of his contenders be so rude especially when he was trying to teach them the ropes and all the secrets. Tyler just stares at Hoyt in disbelief, finally he gets enough courage to utter the words, “I can’t believe you, what do you think you are the Son of God?” Realizing what he just said Tyler tries to take back the words he just uttered but to no avail they were let out. Hoyt just looks at him, “Hold on.” He was speaking to the person he was on the phone with, he then turns and face Tyler. “Excuse me sir, this could very well be God himself, how do you know? You may have just bought yourself a one way trip to hell.” Tyler quickly intervenes,”I think I already have a place reserved there, so no worries about that. But for now…” before Tyler even finishes his last sentence he is blowing on his magical whistle and along come the many miniature Baltasars. They escort Mr. Williams out; Tyler is left there alone with me. “Well lets continue on…” Tyler/Willy starts to walk on then he realizes that he left all alone with just me. “Well son it looks like you have won the special prize.” Now this is usually the part of the movie where they would go to the glass elevator and start pushing buttons and what not, but when you are doped up on so many drugs that doesn’t happen, instead Tyler reaches out and gives me a piece of paper. Quickly I unravel it, too my surprise I’m handed a diagram and plans to the whole construction of the Halo. “Here, you will find every hiding spot, and every nook and cranny.” I can feel my jaw drop just as Charlie Bucket’s did during the making of the movie, finding out he just inherited a Chocolate Factory. “Peter, there is something you need to understand, it’s not how you win the match, but that point you won the match.” I grin slyly, slowly understanding the true meaning of this little adventure I was on. Tyler looks at me, “It seems you were the only formidable one out of these other men to deserve the win. So I have given you as much as I can to try and get you the win, you must utilize it to your own ability.” Back to Reality With that it was all over, I found myself sitting back in my chair dripping in my own sweat. I look over at the clock and notice I was on the trip for a good 6 hours. What a fucking night I thought to myself, the effects still fucking with my brain. “That’s enough for one night.” If that was only the truth for me, I might be ok. I get up from my chair still a little stoned but more importantly seeing more and more things that I usually wouldn’t, like the pet lizard in the corner. I don’t own a lizard, fuck I hate reptiles; maybe I should go to bed. I walk over to the table, roll another joint, which I of course smoke, thus making that lizard in the corner start to dance around like the Geicko lizard impersonating Adam Dick wrestling. Which in turn is a bunch of uncoordinated gestures in which look almost like a Mexican Bull fighter, he must have taken lessens for Los Diablos. Mixing weed and shrooms might not be the best idea. Slowly I stumble to my room where I pass out with my only hopes are to wake up in the morning, and have a slight idea of what the fuck happened this evening.
