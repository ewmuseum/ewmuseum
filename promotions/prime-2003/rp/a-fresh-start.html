A Fresh Start

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ReVolution 195 David pulled the tape off his wrists with his teeth and felt the exhaustion as it wracked his body. Blood had stained into his beaten face, his chest, and across both of his arms. War had been waged in the Target Center and for David, even an hour after the main event had ended, he wasn’t quite ready to leave the battleground. Part of him had felt like he sold his soul, but he knew he had to do whatever it took to walk out of that match a winner. Now, in two months time, he would have a date with a man that could be considered a legend. David’s face looked tiresome and weary. He turned on the faucet rather slowly before he splashed cold water against it. The droplets of water felt good against his face, a stark contrast to the pain he had felt earlier after a battery of fists had assaulted it. “You’re not looking that great.” The voice came from behind him and as David looked into the mirror he saw Mary-Lynn standing there. A bag was in her left hand while there was a sultry look in her eyes. Noble turned around and looked at the beautiful woman and saw that there was no smile on her face. “Saw your match tonight, nicely done,” she offered before she dropped the bag onto one of the benches. “Thanks,” David responded as he leaned against the nearby wall and folded his arms across his chest. Somehow, he had gone the entire evening without running into Mary-Lynn, but deep down he knew eventually fate would intervene and force these two together for the first time in weeks. Her eyes looked over David and she shook her head. “Man, they got you pretty good tonight,” Mary-Lynn remarked softly as she pointed out the various wounds over his body, but David just shrugged his shoulders. “If you ask me, they got it bad,” David responded as he alluded to the fact that he walked out the winner of the match. Mary-Lynn chuckled as David moved closer to her and sat on a chair just a few feet away from her. “So…” The uncomfortable silence had gotten to David as his eyes looked down at the ground as he desperately searched for a way out. “So, you’ve done a pretty good job of avoiding me and my phone calls the past few weeks.” “I wouldn’t--,” Mary-Lynn held her finger up. “You can save the sorry excuses for another day. I’m not in the mood to hear them. I just want the truth from you and I want it now, David. Was I just a fling or what?” David sighed. “You’re not just a fling.” The words came out muttered, more so than he had intended, but Mary-Lynn managed to make sense of what he was saying. Her hands moved to her hips and she looked at him through her designer glasses. “Then, what am I?” she asked him again and David sighed once again as he sat up in his chair and looked Mary-Lynn in the face. “It’s not a matter of what you are to me, Mary-Lynn, it has more to do with what’s going on in my life,” David began as he stood up. His eyes, meanwhile, never moved from Mary-Lynn. “When we…” His voice then trailed off as he began to pull his bag off of the ground and closer to him. “We slept together, that’s what the kids are calling it these days, David.” Sarcasm. Cute. “Yeah, when we did that.” It rolled off of his tongue weird. “I wasn’t in the best mindset. I just reacted the wrong way and didn’t mean to drag you into the general craziness that is my life.” Mary-Lynn chortled at the thought. “I’m a big girl, David. I can handle myself. But, let me ask you something.” David pulled his shirt over his head before she decided to continue. “Do you think that I’m a good looking woman?” David thought about that question for a minute, more so because of the sheer shock of her asking him. “Yeah, of course.” A smile appeared on her face as she inched closer to David. “Did we have a good time that evening?” A grin appeared on her face. “Oh, yeah.” The sexy red-head took another step towards David who was still lost by what she was doing. “Do you want to kiss me?” “I’m sorry?” Mary-Lynn smiled again as she leaned forward and her soft, red lips met with those of David. The two kissed for a moment as their eyes closed and the world seemingly disappeared around them. A moment passed before they broke their warm embrace and the two looked at each other with softness. “I accept your apology, and yes, I will go out on a date with you Tuesday evening,” she whispered to him before she spun on her spot and began to walk away. “Um--,” David started, but found himself at a loss for words. Mary-Lynn lifted her bag off of the bench and proceeded to David’s locker room door. In one split moment, she opened the door, and left David there all by himself. The young man had to sit down on the bench next to him as he was breathless as well by the succession of events that had just taken place. Life was good. *** “Seriously, Samantha, how much crap do you have?” Two suitcases rested uncomfortably in the arms of David who walked through the spacious bedroom. Surrounded in clutter, the oldest Noble looked up from her project and glanced over at her brother with a wide smile on her face. “Gotta have my life, little brother, especially now that we live here with you.” David leaned into the doorframe. “So,” David began as he looked around at the room. “Are you going to be able to get your wedding planning business back on the ground here in Los Angeles? Because, you know, I can make some calls--,” but before he could finish, Samantha had already begun talking over him. “Think everything’s going to go just fine. Got some good leads from some of my contacts back home, and hopefully, I can pick things up here,” she let him in on her plans before Samantha pulled herself away from unpacking to look at David. “I mean, at the end of the day, that’s not nearly as important as the family is, you know?” David knew exactly what she meant, but didn’t voice his opinion in that regard. Silence, at first. “Dad, he would love this house, you know?” David just shrugged his shoulders. “You found one hell of a place for us, David.” David smiled at his sister, but offered no more words as his hands slid into the pockets of his blue jeans and he exited the room. His head lowered as he walked down the hallway and his shoulder grazed his Mom who looked at him with a troubled look on her face. “Everything okay?” No compassion from her though. David ignored her though as he bolted down the set of stairs and into the large living room where Andrea and MacKenzie were working at placing that Noble charm on the house. “Looking good,” David remarked as a smile appeared on his face. MacKenzie stood from her spot and ran over to David to give him a hug. He lifted her into the air and kissed her on the cheek. “Glad you love it!” she responded cheerfully and the smile on David’s face just grew wider. Andrea looked over at David and felt a warmness in her heart as she watched the two. Her eyes moved around the living room and found her mother hidden at the top of the stairs. Before she could open her mouth though, the matriarch had disappeared and left Andrea confused as to the look on her face. “David, get over here,” Andrea told her newest client and David returned MacKenzie to the ground. Noble then looked over at his sister and gave her a slight scowl which was met with a slight roll of the eyes. Andrea had been attempting to hang some drapes, but placed them to the ground as they moved to the corner out of earshot of MacKenzie. “I’ve been trying to find the way to talk to you about this, especially since you’re starting to take over everything in the family. I got a call from Dad’s lawyer this morning,” she told him and David took a deep breath in his as his eyes moved to the ground. “What’d he want?” “We need to go over Dad’s will.” Andrea felt the tension in David’s face. The pain was even worse. “No, we don’t. His assets, we don’t need them. So, you take care of that, okay?” “Alright, I’ll see what I can do.” David and Andrea’s eyes met, and the two decided to leave that subject at that. “So, you guys going to be okay tonight? I’ve gotta do some stuff tonight,” David told Andrea who’s eyebrows lifted ever so slightly and confusion set in on her face. “That’s not a problem, but what have you got?” “I--, um, I’ve got a date.” “A date?! Oh man!” Andrea shrieked in response and then quickly covered her mouth when she realized that the entire household had just heard her. David shot her a dirty look in the blink of an eye as he shook his head in disbelief. “A date?!” this time though the voice belonged to MacKenzie and then Sarah joined in as she walked in from the kitchen with an utter look of surprise on her face. David felt his face turn red and he greatly wished that he had either kept his mouth shut or that he could disappear right then and there. “So, who’s the lucky girl? Come on, spill the beans. Is it Mary-Lynn? God, I hope it’s Mary-Lynn.” David glared at Sarah, who was far too excited about this notion. “First off, I’m not one of your High School friends that you’re gossiping with. Second of all, um, yeah. It’s with Mary-Lynn.” David’s eyes shot to the floor. “I knew it was only a matter of time before you two hit it off. Maaaaaaan, you better not blow this.” More sarcasm didn’t sit well with David. He began to pull open the door and heard them snicker. “I hate all of you. I just want you to know that.” David stepped outside after the small joke and felt this sense of happiness over take him. He was going to have his first date with Mary-Lynn, and he was going to try his hardest not to blow it. Most importantly though, he had his family back. *** David sat inside of the rather nice restaurant and looked uncomfortable. Mary-Lynn had picked the restaurant and informed him to dress nice, which he had done perfectly. A few minutes had ticked off the clock since he had been seated and David found himself glancing at his watch every few seconds. Still no sign of Mary-Lynn though. Noble had to deal with the constant stares as people recognized him and he wondered to himself if Mary-Lynn had done this on purpose. Another minute passed by and David felt the sweat building up on his hands. It irked him slightly because of his usual confidence when it came to women. Yet, Mary-Lynn, she was different, and he knew it. Noble’s eyes continually glanced towards the entrance and felt his heart jump each time the door opened only to reveal that it wasn’t Mary-Lynn. A waiter walked by him and checked to ensure he was alright. Another minute passed and the door opened once again. This time, Mary-Lynn stepped through it. David felt the air leave his lungs as the lovely red-head looked stunning. She wore a cute black dress that stopped at the middle of her thigh. Her hair was meticulously done and when her eyes landed upon David, she lit up the entire restaurant with her patented smile. “Hey,” she said slyly as she approached her seat and took it. Mary-Lynn’s eyes never left David and the two felt like they were taking a step in the right direction. The waiter quickly approached and laid out a napkin across Mayweather’s lap as he handed David the wine list. “What would you two like to drink tonight? It’s on the house.” David looked at the list though and appeared lost. “Well, if it’s on the house,” Mary-Lynn interjected with a calm voice. “Then the house should surprise us on this particular evening.” The waiter nodded his head before he left the two and Mary-Lynn placed her hand close to the middle of the table. “Look at you, ordering the waiter around.” David joked, an attempt to loosen the mood. A moment later, the waiter returned with a bottle of nondescript wine, and he began to pour it for the two. Another waiter approached and placed an order flatbread on the table as David just looked on in amazement. Mary-Lynn just chuckled as she opened her mouth, “I’ve gotta do this more often. These people are falling all over themselves for us.” It was true. “You a fan of this place?” Her face said no. “Good, because if so, I might have to be a little worried as to who’s bringing you to places like this. Might not be able to withstand the competition.” Mary-Lynn blushed at the thought. “I don’t think you have anything to worry about, David,” she informed him and David felt comforted in that thought. “So, how’s the family doing? Everything going smoothly at the new house?” David moved his head side to side for a moment as he placed a piece of bread on Mayweather’s plate. “It’s… different. I can’t hide as much from them any longer. They’re not the easiest bunch to deal with you, you know?” Mary-Lynn took a bite of her bread, but her eyes never wavered from David. “When do I get to meet them?” He paused as she knew he would. “Whenever you want.” That surprised Mary-Lynn. “So, you bought them a house, but you’re still going to live in that small apartment?” she asked once again and David shrugged his shoulders. “We’ll see. Being on the road all of the time, not certain I see the reason to get a bigger place. Especially since my sisters are going to want me over more often. But, we’ll see, I guess,” David responded in a noncommittal tone. Mary-Lynn then took a sip of her wine. “You happy to be here?” “Yeah.” No hesitation. Mary-Lynn smiled at that and David looked at her hand before placing his on top of her’s. “I’m happier than I’ve been in a long time.” For the first time in a while, things finally felt like they were going right in his life.
