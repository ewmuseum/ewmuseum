The Authentic Karina Wolfenden Culture Shock Experience

15 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02-15-06 The Authentic February 10th, 2006: PRIME Headquarters ‘I need a copy of the travel expenses made last year during the week before Culture Shock by Karina.’ The Red Devil more or less demanded of the PRIME employee sitting behind the desk in front of him. This was a very unusual trip for Baltasar as he’d never been one to ever bother with coming to the travel and expense department of PRIME. He usually just faxed all his plans to them and never bothered with it again as he knew they’d simply take care of it. ‘I’m sorry sir but we aren’t suppose...’ the employee began before Baltasar placed his hand in front of her face to stop her from talking. He didn’t quite touch her face, as that would of possibly been grounds for a lawsuit, but put it close enough that nearly hear entire face was covered from view by his massive hand. ‘Look,’ he began in a clear and calm tone. Almost eerie in the way that he was so calm yet the intent of his message so clear. ‘I’m a busy man and I don’t have time to stand around here making small talk with you on the finer points of companies rules and regulations. Just give me the damn information and I’ll be on my way.’ ‘I’m not suppose to do that sir.' She once again stated when Baltasar lowered his hand. He simply sighed as he leaned down a bit to get closer to her face. ‘Here is how this works. You give me the information. I walk out of the room and nothing has to be destroyed in the process. That is the easy way.’ He said before standing back up and placing his arms over his massive chest. ‘Besides you won’t get into any trouble. Just tell Tyler Nelson, or whoever it is you’ll have to answer to, that I threatened to destroy not only the office building but put you in a hospital if you didn’t do as I asked. I’m sure the knowledge that you saved him tons of money by not having to replace all the computers and junk in this office will keep him from firing you.’ ‘If it doesn’t?’ She asked in the most serious tone she could muster given the situation. ‘Then you won’t be any worse off then you’ll be if I don’t get what I’m looking for today.’ The lady shook her head slowly in acknowledgment before turning to her computer screen and beginning to type feverishly on the keyboard. The Red Devil could only smile inside. It wasn’t like he’d expected any other reaction. He knew he’d left her with little choice but was still a tiny bit surprised that he didn’t have to smash anything just to prove the point that he would do it, if needed. ‘It’s nice to see that PRIME has a few people working for them that don’t need to have something proven to them before they do their job.’ A few moments later the sound of a printer on the left side of her desk coming to life can be heard through out the room. A moment later the lady hands a white piece of print paper to The Red Devil with the list he was asking for. A small smile comes across his face as he folds the paper up, sticks it in the inside pocket of his leather jacket, and simply turns and walks out of the office. *** The obsession has taken hold of me entirely at this point. Sitting in my window seat, staring out at the light blue sky, I have nothing but time to think. Sapporo, the site of last years Culture Shock, is my destination. In my mind it is the only logical starting point for winning this years Dual Halo match up. There isn’t going to be much more to it than that seeing as how it's pretty clear Karina hated Japan. The one and only item on the list for her travel in Japan is a hotel room. A simple task of sleeping in the same hotel for an evening. As I watch the clouds go by from the tiny window to the outside world I feel the obsession griping my heart and soul even tighter. Like a Demon clutching at my soul with everything it has in order to control my fate. Everything I want is tied up in this years Culture Shock. To be mentioned in the same breath as Tony Rolo, Angelo Deville, Hoyt Williams, and even Karina Wolfenden I must finally take that giant step forward. Make my way up to the all important top level, that the superstar talent fights on, by carving my own path to the top. Culture Shock presents that chance for me in spades. There will never be a better moment to put that stamp on my PRIME career than now. Dual Halo, one on one with Karina, millions of fans watching it world wide. When I think about it like that then the pressure sets down squarely on my shoulder likes a million pound weight. This brings up the obvious questions of doubt. Where to go from here if I'm unable to do what I've said I would. How do I manage the rest of my PRIME career if I'm unable to win the Dual Halo like I predicted was going to be so easy for me to do? I know that they will never let me forget it if I walk out of Culture Shock without a victory in the Dual Halo. The truth is that by my own ignorance and design I've almost all but promised myself a loss in the Dual Halo. These doubts, and more, weighting heavily on my mind cause of one person. One singular person was able to single handily put my confidence on shaky ground and could potentially ruin my victory. The one thorn in my side that took me from the highest of confidence in winning the Dual Halo only for mere seconds later to send it crashing down on the jagged rocks of their ego. No doubt it's a gift she has for crashing parties and ruining moments. Dual Halo is hard enough to win normally, and I've already got the added problem of having absolutely everyone in PRIME against me at this point, and now my back is against a wall. I've been backed into the proverbial corner with this 'challenge'. The mocking tone in which the match was laid out still sticks into me like a thousand knives. Clearly there is plenty of belief that I have zero chance of walking out of Culture Shock with a victory of any kind. No one believes I'm going to win the main event now. Hell if I'm totally honest with myself I have to doubt my chances at this point. I probably wasn't even a real threat, in most minds, before I allowed myself to be duped into a match up with her. Any match would be bad news for Culture Shock. Even facing someone like Damian Cruz or Paul Cain prior to the Dual Halo would be a bad idea. It simply isn't smart business on my part. This match is differen though. It’s far worse and of a much great magnitude than just any other match up I could have had prior to the main event. It isn’t just about getting through it unscathed. I'm no fool I know I won't walk out of an encounter with Karina at one hundred percent. It simply won't be possible. She tried to kill Angelo when they faced and she was sleeping with him then. What does that say for a guy like me when I know she hates my guts? It all adds up to one thing. My Dual Halo chances placed in a great deal of peril. The respect and victory I've so desperately craved is now being jeopardized by my over zealous pride and ego. Everything I've been working so hard to attain could possibly be for naught. I'm in danger of failing before I even get a good foot hold on the mountain. It’s all in great doubt and my attempt to finally prove myself is now being over shadowed by the unmeasurable ego of one person. Hard to imagine someone having a large ego or chip on their shoulder than myself but it has happened. One single person. One person I could have certainly done without being upstaged by at this point. It isn't exactly like she needs to prove herself going into the Dual Halo. She won it already. She did this all last year. This year isn't really about winning for her. It's about humiliating me and making me look like a fool. Damn her. I'm a bad guy. I'll admit that to myself. I have no choice but to admit it to the world due to my course of actions. Yet as bad as I am there is no way I hold a candle to her. I don't try and act like I'm doing the 'right thing' when I want to destroy the dreams or aspirations of others. I'll spend this entire week worrying about failure and what kind of mess I've gotten myself into and all the while she'll be smiling. She'll be playing the 'hero' of the people. Content in the knowledge that this isn't about doing the right thing. This isn't about giving me a shot to prove myself and be respected. This is something with far less glitz and glamour than that. This is her way of upstaging everyone again. Her way of trying to make everyone else feel like shit so she can feed her monsterous ego. This isn't about respect for her. She has it and she knows it. Events like Culture Shock are about two things to Karina. Greed and shitting on the hard work of others. Pure and simple. She just has to have the spotlight at all times so there for makes sure to upstage any and all. If she beats me before the Dual Halo then she is set. Even if she doesn't win she still has that Ace up her sleeve. She won it last year so it don't matter. 'I still beat The Red Devil' she'll be able to brag. She'll use it as a crutch to why she didn't win the Dual Halo. Being the hero was just 'too much' for her this year. While she has that I have nothing. Beating Karina, while respectable, isn't going to save me in this case. If I beat her but still fail to win the main event then I have doomed myself to the fate of a joke. 'Sure you beat Karina but you still aren't as good as her. She won her match AND won the Dual Halo last year.' It will be their battle cry for the next year. Wow you won but you didn't do what you said you'd do. You weren't able to complete the feat that Karina was able to in your position last year. I'm doomed. My fate sealed. All at the hands of one person. 'Damn her.' Karina Wolfenden She has some how managed to become your mortal enemy. That one nagging punch line you can't get away from. It's like the Angelo Deville situation all over again only this time even more humiliating. It's not cause she is a woman, you've fought plenty of tough women in the wrestling business. This one is different. This one brings out something in you that even The Deville himself couldn't bring out of you. Fear. You guessed it. You’re afraid. You know it. I know it. The world knows it. It’s written all over your face. With every move you’ve made, ever word you’ve uttered, and ever excuse you’ve conjured up all you’ve done is betray your secret. You’re afraid to fail. Afraid that you’ll be wrong and the whole world will have every reason to point in your direction and laugh. There are no excuses left to make. You can’t beat up another partner and put them in a wheelchair claiming they cost you once again. You can’t take it out on Hoyt Williams and the fact that The Imperium didn’t want you anymore. Hell Hoyt Williams didn’t even want The Imperium anymore, period, so all he did was actually give you a blessing. Instead of emailing you, that you’re services were no longer needed, he had the guts to tell you to your face. This isn't like failing to Deville or any host of others. You had a crutch. A back up plan. It wasn't your fault. It was always the fault of your partner Angelus. What do you think you're going to do now if you don't beat Karina? Blame it on him for being in the wheelchair you put him in? Nope this less than enviable situation is of your own design. Now you’re out there on display. You’re going to walk to the ring with that big chance to finally grab that respect and honor you’ve talked so much about. It’s your time to shine and to prove that everything you’ve been claiming you could do are true. It won’t just be words when it’s all said and done. Now it’s going to be facts. At least that is what you're hoping and praying is going to be the case. That is if you pull it off and are able to over come that fear which is making it's home inside of you. Karina is waiting on the other side of the door you are trembling about opening. Waiting for you to open it, step through, and prove to her that you deserve to be more than the punch line to another racist joke about Mexico and it’s people. The weight of the entire country now rest squarely on your shoulders just as you’ve claimed these last few weeks to be how you wanted it to be. The chance to pass magnificently is just on the other side of that door. Just wanted you wanted right? The ideal situation of putting everything on you and being able to come out looking like a million bucks when it's all said and done. Are you man enough to do it? Can you walk into the fires of the personal Hell that you’ve built for yourself and come out on the other side unharmed? The time to find out is coming. Time to fight for it all. Time to prove everything or fail miserably at all that you dreamed to achieve. Check in time. Ante up and win the ultimate prize. *** February 9th, 2006 'You're late.' The Red Devil remarks as a small framed, middle aged, man walks up to him at the hotel bar. 'I know. I'm terribly sorry about that. Things just kept piling up on me. Traffic, my boss,' the man beings before being cut off by the wave of The Red Devil's hand. 'It's not important. Just sit down.' The man does so as he takes a notepad and pencil out of the black bag he is carrying then carefully sits it down at the foot of his stool. 'I'd like to thank you once more for your time today. It isn't often that I get the chance to speak with a top star in a company like PRIME right before one of their biggest events.' The words coming from the local newspaper reporter simply slid in one ear drum and rang their way right out the other. Baltasar wasn't intrested in this little 'interview' and was only doing so cause PRIME had held him to it. He needed to get some sleep and be up early enough for a trip to the PRIME offices before catching his flight out, a week early, to Japan. Instead he finds himself having to sit here, at the hotel lounge, drinking tequila and giving a pointless interview. 'Your welcome. Before we get started though I'd like to ask you a question.' Baltasar states with about as much enthusiasm as that of some one getting ready to have their teeth drilled by their dentist. 'Shoot.' Comes the quick reply of the middle aged, twelve year veteran, sports writer . You could even say it was an excited response to the question. I guess reporters don't get asked to many questions by those who are being interviewed. No doubt nights like this are exactly why Karina avoids the media and anything even resembling interviews and things of that nature. You have to imagine that sometimes it must be really great to have her attitude. 'Do you even watch wrestling?' Baltasar asks as he takes another shot of his tequila. 'Sometimes but not very often. Why?' The reporter, whose name is Dan by the way, asks with a raise of his eyebrow. Clearly he wasn't prepared for this question. 'Just curious. Do you like doing your job Dan?' Baltasar asks while leaning back up against the bar.'I love doing my job. It's all I've ever dreamed of doing. Sports writing is in my blood.' Dan responds with a smile. 'Tequila is in my blood,' Baltasar quickly replies before taking another drink, 'but that isn't really important. I love my job too. It's all I can remember ever seriously considering doing for a living. Are there things you don't like about your choice of profession?' He asks as he stares Dan in the eyes. 'Things I don't...' Dan pauses for a moment. 'I'm not sure what any of this has to do with interviewing you about Culture Shock.' 'Just bare with me a few more moments and you'll see perfectly clear how it ties into Culture Shock.' 'Alright. Well to tell you the truth I don't like some of the long trips that go into doing this. Traveling is a pain and there are always delays to think about. So I guess I'd say that is the one factor, besides the fairly low pay, that I don't really care for.' Dan replies as he puts his notepad and pencil down on the bar next to them. 'Then we understand each other already. The traveling in wrestling is murder. Long plane flights, bad food, different time zones, vastly different cultures. It's the really bad side to this business. Takes us away from our families, makes us hate what we do sometimes, and even most of all makes us hate people like you.' Baltasar replies. 'People like me? What did I do?' Dan askes clearly taken back by the words of The Red Devil. 'Personally you've done nothing. You are just doing your job. What you don't understand is that things like this, small as they might seem to others, are delays for a wrestler. Sitting here with you delays my going to bed. That then delays my getting up in the morning. Throws a monkey wrench into my entire operation. Know what I mean?' Baltasar asks with a small smile on his face. 'Yes I believe I do. I guess I should just go then.' Dan says as he gets up off the bar stool and starts to collect his things.That was easy enough. Quick and to the point and now it is all over. Just the way Karina would have handled this in order to get back to what she'd rather be doing. 'Wrong.' If only it were that simple. Baltasar reaches out and grabs Dan by the shirt collar, lifts him up, and sets him back down on his bar stool. A look of absolute shock, and even some amazement, crosses over his face. 'You just said I was...' Dan starts to say before being cut off. 'I know what I was saying. You aren't listening. You are already here so there is no need for you to leave. You've already delayed me. More accurately PRIME and your bosses have. So since your here I thought I'd simply give you that piece of information, for free, so that you can better accomidate others like me in the future. Get what I'm saying now?' Baltasar asks before drinking down another huge gulp from his Tequila bottle. 'Yes sir. I'll remember to take other peoples schedules into account when setting this sort of thing up in the future.' Dan replies while adjusting himself on the stool. 'Good man. You're already light years ahead of anyone else in your profession then. Now let's get on with this. What did you have to ask me? Maybe I'll learn something here tonight as well.' Baltasar says with the slightest of a grin. Maybe this wasn't going to be such a hassle after all. If everything went right this would be over in the next half hour and things would be back on their way. No huge delays and no having to answer the angry phone calls from people like Tyler Nelson for being like Karina and refusing to full fill interviews and press time. He wants to be like Karina in the wrestling ring. To have her type of success with in the confines of the ring and the prestige that goes with it. He just doesn't desire to be anything like her outside of it. Culture Shock The Land of The Rising Sun. In our business you'll hear many wrestlers, tons of them actually, speak about hating Japan. They don't like the travel, they don't like the food, and they don't like the vastly different culture of the country. Personally I love it. In Japan people are treated with a greater level of respect than they are in the United States or even my home country of Mexico. While I'm not the God here in Japan, that I am back home in Mexico, I'm certainly treated with a great deal of respect. The fans over here, even for a guy who is the 'bad guy' like me, are extremely respectful and take a great deal of pride in being honorable and hospitable to athletes like myself. The trips to Japan could stand to happen more and more often for PRIME events if you ask me. Though no one does cause no one in PRIME cares what I have to say. I'm just The Red Devil who gets pissed off when he doesn't get his way. I'm there for use in the lastest 'jobber' or 'You might be a Mexican if' jokes that people seem so fond of throwing around. In Japan it's completely different. Here I am revered. I am treated with respect and honor. In fact if I could die, be reborn, and come back a superstar of at least the same level, if not higher, I'd want to be reborn right here in Japan. This country is the next best thing after being at home. 'Ohayoo, Montrell.' 'Ohayoo, Hiroshi.' 'How've you been?' Hiroshi asked. It had indeed been a while since their last visit. I had first met him almost a year ago when I took part in the Atlantic City show for Core Wrestling. That night I had faced, the then Legacy Champion of Core, a man named Munson as well as, PrimeTime Central Legend, Seymour Almasy in a triple threat. It was billed as one of the two main events and called a super bout. Hiroshi had been there for a match earlier in the card with, if memory serves me correct, Josh of Global Wrestling. We struck up a conversation and have since become fairly good friends. In many ways he is someone that I can still look to for advice. Very similiar in many ways and having gone through many of the same struggles in the American wrestling enviroment makes it very easy for us to connect. 'I've been good. Things could be better but that is how life goes.' 'What are you in Japan for?' 'PRIME is putting on their yearly Pay-Per-View, Culture Shock. I'm in the main event, with everyone else, taking part in the Dual Halo.' 'Confident in winning?' he asks. 'To be honest not really. I was, a couple weeks ago, but now I've went out and lost my head again. Accepted a singles match prior in the event with Karina Wolfenden.' 'So?' Hiroshi responded. He has always been pretty good for shrugging off the important implications that befall a match up like this. It's his own unique way of simply telling you, without having to say it, that you're acting like an idiot. Still I always bite. 'So that is probably going to wind up costing me the Dual Halo victory. You've never been in one so I don't expect you to really understand the implications of this. The Dual Halo is evil. It takes away your soul, when trying to only win in it, as it is. Now I have to do my best to beat Karina in order to not look foolish heading into the Halo and there for have zero shot of being a hundred percent. I've pretty much given up my Halo victory and the ultimate wish that comes along with it.' 'Given it up for what?' He asks. Clearly he knows but is trying to get me to finally openly admit what I've known to be the truth for about a week now. I was foolish to accept the match but did so out of desire to put a win over Karina next to my PRIME legacy. 'For a victory over Karina. Can you believe that? I've probably given up any hope of winning Halo and thus the right to have anything I want just so I can face her and win. That is like giving away the keys to controling the universe in favor of becoming a celebrity.' 'Possibly. You know I'd never have chosen this path but then again I don't desire victory over a singlar person at this point in my career. For you this Karina thing has some how become personal. It clouds your judgement.' I shook my head. I couldn't argue the point. It was all fact. This had some how become personal. What had once simply been a message to another wrestler, to get their attention, had suddenly taken the majority of mine. It had certainly stolen my focus away from winning the Dual Halo match up. Stole it away from winning that match about twelve seconds after I declared it was my ultimate goal. 'You're right. She is a personal issue with me. I'm not even sure why. She hasn't ever been a factor in my career to this point, at least not a major one. In fact she only even became part of my career, in a major way, cause I used her as a pawn. My desire to finally face Angelo Deville, and destroy him and all the bullshit that has gone on from losing to him, in war to end all wars. She was just there. Yet the moment she took the time to respond, by kicking my face in, Angelo was all but forgotten and my focus became humilating her.' 'Will winning this match humilate her?' 'Probably not. If she loses to me she'll shrug it off. She has won so much in her career that a loss to someone like me won't derail her or become an obsession for her. In my mind it will be enough. I'll have proven my point by defeating her.' 'Then what's the point? You must have some sort of reason for it meaning something to you.' He says with a folding of his arms. The problem is that is a question that even I can't answer. I don't know why beating her means so much. I'm not that important in her universe. Just another chump wrestler for her to put down before she jumps on her bike and goes off on her next ski trip. 'I honestly can't tell you why. I don't even know. Which is why this is so stupid. I have given up even caring about winning Halo in order to defeat someone who won't even take notice of it when it's over. I could rub it in her face every single ReVolution after that and it simply wouldn't matter. Yet even with that knowledge I'm drawn to defeating her. Drawn to it much like I was drawn to beating Deville.' 'Only with him he took notice of you. Making you look foolish became his focus. What is hers?' 'Making money is her focus. I guess. That or simply pissing people off with her indifference toward everything not Karina. I can't really get a read on her personally. Frankly I don't really want to know much about her. I just want to know enough about her to know that when it's over I've beaten her. Simple as that for me I suppose.' 'You're in a dangerous situation for your career Montrell. Focusing on one person yet having no idea why. You should know better than that. I believe Munson has a victory over you for that very reason. Too much focus on Almasy cause he is a legend.' BINGO. 'Exactly. I've become obsessed with defeating legends and people with that aura about their name. Almasy, Deville, even Youngblood were people I obsessed over beating during the week before those matches. Deville of course was the worst but that is what Karina has become. The next legend I'm obsessed with defeating.' 'Some time soon my dear friend you'd better learn to let that line of thinking go. Beating a legend is nice but it's one person. It's one singular victory. Even a string of those doesn't add up to the same type of legacy that a person gets when they win all the big time matches. Things like Halo, Jewel In The Crown, and the Universal Championship are what people in PRIME are remembered for. The truth is that at the rate you're going when it's all over Munson will once again have a victory over you. As the bigger legend in PRIME for having won the JITC.' He was right. My whole PRIME career had been about beating the 'names'. It has become my sole focus and has caused me to lose site of my own career. To lose site of climbing the ladder and achieving the other great moments I should be aspiring to. Culture Shock is indeed going to be a history making event. Karina and Baltasar one on one, the Dual Halo, and more importantly. The final event were I focus on beating one 'legend' instead of focusing on my entire career. This is the final time that one person gets all my attention. Soak it up Karina. This ends at Culture Shock. Experience If there is one saving grace for me as I walk out for Culture Shock this weekend it's in the experience I have not only at this event but in this enviroment. I've been to this point before and I know what the dance is about. I know the steps and the routine that goes into preparing for an event like this. My only solice in this entire messy situation is the simple truth that I've been here before and I know what to do. I've competed in the Dual Halo and done fairly well. More importantly I've won the only Fallen Halo match up in PRIME history. I've been locked inside this type of structure twice and I've walked away the victor of it before. There is no surprise element to this anymore. I know what it's all about. I know that I'm going to bleed, to get beat on by every person who I've ever wronged in PRIME. I know that I'll be one of the biggest targets in the match up and not just cause I'm one of the biggest people. I painted this bulls eye on my own back. I set the course of this match up long ago. It's by my own design and the work of my own hands that I'm going to have to endure the worst beating I've ever taken in my life in order to walk out of the demonic structure with a smile on my face. I haven't got any friends left. There won't be any one in that structure looking to save me or give me a hand. Tyler Nelson, the greediest and most ruthless person this business knows, is going to have far more support that I will. Still I can't complain. I did this of my own free will and thinking. I put myself behind the eight ball in this situation to see if I could come out with any honor. Failing to beat both Karina and win the Dual Halo is a sharp sword that will cut into me many times over in my career if for no other reason than it didn't have to happen in the first place. I didn't have to accept that match. I could have turned her down in order to pursue what is suppose to be The Ultimate Prize. Anything I want in the form of a granted wish for surviving The Dual Halo. Yet I didn't turn her down. I didn't do the smart thing and walk away from it with the secure knowledge that I'd still get my chance to top her. By eliminating her from the Halo. So why would I have taken the match? I've spent this whole week pondering that very question and I've only been able to come up with one logical answer. The divine truth, if you will, to why I've painted myself into this corner that doesn't seem to have any chance of ending well for me.She is my Ultimate Prize. There is no other explanation. I went after her, in the beginning, in order to get to Angelo Deville. She was a pawn in my plot to once again plague The Crimson Angel. Some where along the way it all changed. She became my focus and the fact that Angelo has suddenly went AWOL never even factored into my mind. Once it was clear she wasn't going to be a real drawing point to luring out Deville I could have simply left her be. Let her have that attack on me in the bathroom and called it even. I mean it's clear I brought that on. It could have ended right there. Deville was my main focus right? WRONG. I was a fool to deny it to myself before. Karina is, was, and has been my focus since my return. I've tried to play it off. I've tried to make The Dual Halo more important. I have even attempted to put her out of my mind in order to focus on this Hood character for the last few weeks and it's simply not possible. As much as I'd like to deny it she is my focus. She is the prize I seek. Beating her is more important to me than winning The Dual Halo, than finding out who is behind the Hood, than winning the Universal Championship. It's been there all along. Right there in the back of my brain waiting to be expressed but be pushed back in a vain attempt to pretend that defeating a woman wasn't that important to me. Make no mistake about it I want to win the Halo. Everybody wants to win the Halo. If they don't they wouldn't be entering it. It's too dangerous an enviroment to enter if you simply don't care about surviving it. I want to win it. Just not nearly as much as I want to get my hand raised one on one against Karina. Karina is going to get my full attention for our match. She'll get everything I have and then some of what I don't have in my attempt to defeat her. I don't even care if that puts me in The Dual Halo as a wounded target. It's all part of the process. She is the goal, the X factor, if you will. Winning Halo would be a great accomplishment but truthfully would be completely hollow if it came after a loss to her. She is the measuring stick, much as I hate to admit it even to myself, of what is success in PRIME. Going toe to toe with Karina and walking away the winner... That is like winning 10 Dual Halo matches. Nothing more needs to be said than that. Time to ante up and claim that ultimate prize.
