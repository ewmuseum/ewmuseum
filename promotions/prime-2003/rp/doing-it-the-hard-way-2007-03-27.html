Doing It The Hard Way

27 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 27th, 2007 AMSTERDAM, NETHERLANDS FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 2ND, 2007 “I know you,” a voice spoke, waking Craig out of his stupor. Even though his eyes had been opened, he wasn’t anywhere close to this world. Instead, his thoughts had led him far away from here. As he looked up at the stocky man in front of him, he didn’t look familiar in the least bit. Craig sat up, looking around, and wondering if there was anybody else in the room with him. Silence. Good, Craig thought to himself as he looked back at the man. “I’m sorry, you do?” Craig asked, wondering how the man knew him. The man just simply shook his head and laughed. Craig was baffled. With many of his memories still missing, it could be entirely possible that he knew who this person was, yet most of his life was shrouded in darkness to him. The memories he did have were just recent and had destroyed so much of his life that he wished he could give them back. He could only imagine what else had happened in his life up to this point. “Not literally, but I’ve seen you before. On the television. You’re that big superstar in the wrestling world, right? Dusk?” the man asked him, looking rather impressed at who he was speaking to. Craig sighed out of relief, but then realized that his plan had a hole in it. People would recognize him. Someone would look at him, realize who he was, and word would get out. Most importantly, Daniel Santiago would notice who he was immediately, and it would all be over for Craig. He hadn’t thought this out too well he saw, and had to do something in a hurry. “Most people find themselves here because they owe somebody something. I look at you and I don’t have that feeling about you. To me, it seems like you’re trying to get something back. Unfortunately to you, the people here are going to recognize you. If they love this stuff, then they love what you do for a job. You need a different identity,” he said and Craig wondered what the man meant. Craig’s red eyes followed the man as he walked up to a locker across from where Craig was sitting and opened it slowly. After a few seconds, the man pulled out a black mask and tossed it at Craig. “They eat this kind of stuff up, and it lets you do what you need to do in private. Something tells me that you’re interested in being unnoticed and sneak up on whoever owes you what they owe you. The name’s Rudy. I just hope I don’t have to fight you tonight,” he said before handing out his hand towards Craig. He was hesitant to take it though as he looked down at the soft black mask in his hands. He shifted the mask to his right hand and shook with his left, allowing the man to leave for the evening. Craig had almost made a colossal mistake. He began to doubt himself and whether or not he should go through with this. But, with each negative thought, he could only think about his daughter. He needed her and vice versa. If there was one thing he needed to accomplish before he died, it would be bringing her back into his life, no matter how much she hated him for not being there. With the mask in his hands, he pulls it over his face. Soon, it would be showtime. It was time to show who was in charge around here. As he stood up he saw a mirror right in front of him. In it was a man he didn’t recognize. Even with the mask off, the eyes he looked into were foreign to him. His place in the world was mysterious to him and no matter what he tried to do, it continued to elude him. Apparently he had an important place in the world, but he could care less at this point. His life was empty without his daughter. He needed someone in his life to save him from the inevitable darkness creeping up on him. Craig could see it surrounding him, just waiting to engulf him whole. So far he had fought it off. He had to. His eyes close slowly. His senses grew keener as the seconds passed him by. The cold air from the steel vents above him blew viciously upon him. Thump. Thump. Thump. He could feel his heart beat rhythmically, never skipping a beat. It was the only part of him that felt warm. Everything else was ice cold. He had an inkling that it had always been that way for him. Years had been stolen from him and he desperately wanted to have them back. Yet, he couldn’t. He kept looking backwards when he needed to look forward. As he felt the darkness creep up on him, he steeled himself. The darkness though was inviting. He could let himself go and be gone forever, no longer having to worry about his place in the world or his memories or his shattered heart. Yet, it would be unlike him. As much as he didn’t know himself, he knew giving up was unlike him. He had yet to give up, and he wasn’t about to start now. Not when so much was still on the line. ---- DALLAS, TEXAS FEBRUARY 7TH, 2007 It was beginning to look ugly for Brady. Things just weren’t going his way. His betting addiction was getting worse and worse by the day. So much so that he was avoiding his wife as much as he possibly could. After he had bet a thousand dollars on Cain defeating Dusk, and subsequently losing it all, he had become obsessed. The following week, he had bet against Dusk in an event known as the Infinite Gauntlet. Of course, Dusk had somehow found a way to win the match. That was another thousand he had washed down the drain. He needed to win his money back, but it just wasn’t happening. As he sat in his home he began to think of different ways to get the money back. He couldn’t get a second job and he couldn’t take out a loan. Jessica couldn’t know what he had done or that would be the end of them. Everything he had worked so hard for would be gone in a moment. After the last time, she looked like she was going to murder him right then and there. Since then, they hadn’t spoken to each other outside of little things like what they were going to eat for dinner and how their daughter, Rose, was doing. Otherwise, it was an icy silence between the two lovers. With no other escape clause, he had turned back to gambling, and he wasn’t doing himself any favors. The hole he was digging was getting worse and worse. But, he had to get the money back. He desperately needed that money back. So far, he was down two thousand. He could easily make that back. All he had to do was be smart about it. Sooner or later, his luck would change. Above him, he could hear footsteps. Someone was coming downstairs. As he turned his body, he saw a small little girl racing down the steps. He smiled. As she turned the corner, he saw Rose’s beautiful face and every worry he had simply went away. She had the biggest smile on her face as she walked up to her father. “Hey daddy,” she said gleefully. Without a moment’s hesitation, he swept her into her arms and kissed her on the cheek. Immediately, she kissed him right back on the cheek and he sat her down next to him. He handed her the remote and she turned on the television before flipping through the various channels she loved to watch. As she settled on a station, she turned to her father and looked to be in an inquisitive mood. “Daddy, why are you and mommy fighting?” she asked, a question that he knew would come sooner or later. He quickly thought of the various answers he could give her. Some of the answers, like because your mom is a bitch, or because daddy just happens to be a fuck up were easily dismissed. Instead, he decided to take the easy approach. He knew his daughter was intelligent, but she would quickly drop it if he gave her an answer that seemed plausible. “Because people fight, sweetie. But, don’t you worry about your mommy and me, we’ll be okay. She loves your daddy very much, and your daddy loves her very much as well. It’s just part of the world,” he told her, thinking that would solve everything. Yet, it didn’t. Instead, her little eyes continued to be fixated upon him. He didn’t know what she was thinking in that mind of hers, but it worried him some. “Then why does mommy cry so much? Is crying normal too? Is crying over money normal?” she asked, not accusing him of anything, but actually interested. His throat constricted at hearing these questions though. Sweat slowly started to pour from his forehead and he looked at the television, hoping he could just ignore the questions. She was so much like her mother it almost sickened him. Yet, he could still feel her glare upon his face. The tension was becoming worse and instead of answering her question, he kissed her on the forehead and got up off the couch. Jessica had been hiding from him and now he knew why. He always knew why though, but he didn’t realize it had become this bad. He climbed up the stairs and could hear her sobbing from the bathroom. Cautiously, he walks over to the door and pushes it open. Sitting on the floor is Jessica, just balling her eyes out. Slowly, she looks up at him with a look that pierces Brady’s heart. “Our own daughter hears you crying and she has to ask me why. What am I supposed to do about that? You belittle me in front of her and now you’re crying over the money. You slowly turn her against me, and that’s not fair to me,” he tells her, and she just simply shakes her head. “That’s not why I’m crying. I’m crying because I’m pregnant again, Brady. I’m pregnant with another child and you’re being a fool with the money!” she yells at him, taking Brady’s breath away. He had been using protection and she was pregnant. It was almost like a Friend’s episode (you know, the one where Ross and Joey find out that condoms only work like 98% of the time. Work with me here). Slowly, he steps back and turns away from his wife. The pressure upon him was even worse than before. He bounds down the steps and immediately goes outside. The air feels good against his lungs, but every inch of his body still feels like it’s ready to explode. Feverishly, he reaches for his cell phone, needing to take care of this immediately. After dialing a phone number in, he hears the phone ring. After a few rings, the phone is answered. “Hello?” comes from the other line and he knows that he’s got the right person on the line. “Hey, it’s me, Brady. I need to put down another bet, but I need you to front me some money,” Brady says. “Come on now Brady, you know you’re not the most reliable when it comes to bets,” he responds, causing Brady to feel enraged. “I need this. I need to make my money back. Just put me down for two thousand on the Extreme Title match. Put it on… Karina Wolfenden,” Brady says and he knows this one will work. A hall of famer would easily win this match To Brady, his luck is changing. Unfortunately, he just doesn’t know how wrong he is. ---- AMSTERDAM, NETHERLANDS FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 2ND, 2007 Craig stood in the hallway, getting ready for his first match ever in the Arena. It scarred him slightly. He knew he would have to control himself in this venue. If he didn’t, if he lost himself, then he would do something unthinkable and unimaginable. He couldn’t allow for that to happen. Above him sat Daniel Santiago, he knew that much. What he didn’t know was what Santiago was capable of. He tried to fathom the idea, but he wasn’t given the time to do that. A woman was walking towards him. “You ready?” she asked him, flinching at the sight of the mask. “Yeah, let’s do it,” he told her and she just nodded her head before she turned away from him. He followed her through the hallway. At the end of it was a small cage door. As he reached it, it slowly rose and he stepped into the arena. It was unlike anything he had imagined. As his feet stepped onto the concrete floor, he knew it was going to be an experience that he would never forget. He could hear the crowd above him, but it was different then the crowd that PRIME attracted. These people were violent and brutal. They said foul things at him and just looked ready for someone to kill him. Yet, he had no desire to be killed. “Introducing first, a new member of the Arena, he is… THE MASKED AVENGER!” the announcer said, taking Craig by surprise. He really hoped that name wouldn’t stick because it was honestly the worst name he had ever heard. The fans immediately jeered him and he didn’t have the slightest clue as to why. As he turned around, waiting for his opponent to appear, he got the picture why. The cage before him opened and out walked a man that dwarfed Craig, which isn’t an easy feat in the least bit. Craig stood there, wondering what exactly he had gotten himself up to. “And his opponent, the reigning Arena champion, he is mighty, he is ferocious, he is… MAMMOTH!” the announcer yelled out and the fans in the stadium immediately exploded into a frenzy. Apparently, they loved this Mammoth guy, even with the horrible name. Craig was far from a small person, but compared to this guy, he was. He easily stood over seven feet tall and three hundred pounds. As Craig looked up into the crowd, he saw a smile on Santiago’s face. However, next to him though stood a man that looked frightened. It immediately dawned upon Craig. It wasn’t so much as to punish Craig, but to teach the man an example of what could happen if you cross Santiago. This infuriated Craig. He was ready to show who he was to Santiago and show that he was no pawn in his game. A loud bell sounded, signaling the beginning of the match. The fans above yelled at Mammoth to kill Craig, which Craig had no intention of letting happen. The beast started to walk towards him and Craig showed no fear. He had gone up against monsters before and this would be no different. Craig quickly slammed an elbow into the man’s stomach, and nearly collapsed from the pain. It felt like he had slammed his elbow into steel or stone. As Craig walked away, he could see the fans laughing at Craig’s pain. This didn’t make it any easier on Craig, who wasn’t used to being humiliated like this. As he turned around, he was met with a fist to the jaw from the beast, which sent Craig to the ground in a world of hurt. “Okay, let’s not do that again,” Craig mumbled to himself as he pulled himself back up. However, he didn’t get very far as Mammoth kicked him into the ribs, which sent Craig flying into the nearby wall. Things were just getting worse and worse for Craig who was trying his best to fight back. After getting his air back, he started to pull himself up. He could sense Mammoth coming upon and telegraphed his foot coming right for him. This time, Craig caught it, and with his legs, he knocked Mammoth down to the mat with a leg sweep. This tiniest of moves shocked the crowd. He knew that they hadn’t seen something like this happen to Mammoth, and knew that the challenge ahead of him was going to be one of difficulty. Mammoth wasn’t down for long though as he immediately got back up and grabbed Craig by the throat and slammed him into the closest wall. He held him there, squeezing the life out of Craig, who wasn’t too happy with this in the least bit. With Mammoth’s hand around his throat, he thrashed violently, hoping to get out of this predicament. It wasn’t helping any though as Mammoth just tightened his grip. Before Craig knew it, he was being tossed back onto the ground with such velocity that it slightly dazed Craig. As he got his bearings back, he noticed someone throwing a sword into the ring. Who would throw a sword into a ring, Craig thought to himself, and as he looked up he saw that it was Santiago himself. Almost immediately, Mammoth grabbed the sword and picked it up. Things were beginning to look ugly for Craig as Mammoth walked over towards him and slammed his foot into his chest, just leaving it there. Slowly, Mammoth put the sword up to Craig’s throat and looked down upon him. The fans above him knew what was going to happen next. Mammoth was going to end Craig’s life, right there and then. Craig wasn’t too much of a fan of this. Yet, he couldn’t budge for the life of him. Slowly, Mammoth raised the sword up into the air, and Craig was sure this was the end of it. Within moments, he could feel the air from the sword moving right towards him. TO BE CONTINUED
