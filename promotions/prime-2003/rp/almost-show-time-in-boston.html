Almost Show Time in Boston

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Before the show It was less than a week away. Even though he had worked in the Indy Circuit for a long time, he had never actually performed in front of a hometown audience. On top of it all, almost double the audience would be seeing him step into the squared circle. GCW certainly had their own loyal audience that bought every Pay-Per-View and PRIME had a large following of their own. If there was a definition of "Grandest Stage", this would be it. It’s his time to shine now. |~o~| Monday, October 29, 2007 "Well look at you two!" Mikey busted through the kitchen door and into the main dining area. Nitz and his new main squeeze Rachelle were sitting side by side at his usual table not far from the kitchen. They had one of their hands in the other and were laughing when he came through the door. "Bout time ya fuck! Thought I was gonna die and rot before we got served!" Nitz commented and smiled, the ribbing becoming somewhat common now. Mikey gave Nitz a shot in the arm. "Smart ass... what attracted you to this schlep honey?" He questioned Rachelle, winning a laugh from the pretty young lady. "His quick wit is certainly sexy." She said, patting the hand of Donnelly. "So this isn’t like the first meal together is it? Cause I’ve got candles and shit, but they are buried in mothballs in the back." He smiled. "Fuck no, what the hell do you think? Bring a chick here on a first date and I’d never win her over." The Ego was on a roll with the ribbing. Normal people would look and see one man insulting another. But that was the kind of bond that Nitz and Mikey had. They could throw up some of the worst comments and win nothing but laughs from the other. "Keep it up dude if you wanna go spilling out of that window back there. Heh, so what am I gettin’ the cute couple?" "Nicholas told me about the chowder here and I couldn’t resist." Rachelle said as she folded up her menu and placed it in front of her. "Two bowls and these waters would do good man." Nitz said taking a sip of the water served by the one of the other waitresses when they sat down. "You betcha! We’re making a fresh batch right now for the lunch rush so it’ll be ready in a few." He took a seat opposite Nitz and Rachelle and struck up a conversation. "Into stealin’ the brother’s woman now huh?" He playfully slapped Nitz on the arm. "Fuck him man. Douche leaves for Portland without so much as a ‘Thanks for the memories’ to her and expects any type of sympathy? Yeah sure, when I lose my backbone perhaps." Nitz waves off the mention of his brother as Rachelle doesn’t seem overly concerned about the topic of her ex leaving. "I’ve come to terms with it all. It took me a while but that’s when I decided to look Nicholas up." She said. "Yeah, you shoulda seen this guy crawlin’ all up my ass trying to figure out who was writing these notes to him. He was scared to death HAHA!" Mikey shared a laugh with Rachelle and Nitz but Nitz stopped just a second later, casting a joking glare in Mikey’s direction. "I just wanted to have some fun with him. He knows that now. I was nothing but a babbling idiot in the past in front of him and I have no idea why. Maybe I had a thing for him all along but I could never talk to him. Now since everything is out in the open, he can’t shut me up." She smiled during the explanation numerous times as Donnelly took another long drink of his water, almost emptying the glass. "Sweetie, this guy has the biggest mouth in all of New England. It’s nothing short of a miracle you can talk without being interrupted so you must be something special." Mikey said as he got up from his seat to make his way back to the kitchen. Nitz stopped him. "Dude, I almost forgot. These are for you." The Ego handed his friend a couple of tickets. "They’re for the show this weekend. I got some for the whole family so you’ll all be there." "Sweetness man! For that, lunch is on me. I’ll even bring some fresh baked bread." He took off to the kitchen leaving Nitz and Rachelle to themselves. "That was nice of you." Rachelle said, after taking a sip of her own water. "Well how many times do I get the chance to perform in town? It’s a rarity. Did you get the digital camera yet?" "No, it’s on order. I’m supposed to get it Thursday afternoon. Don’t worry; I’ll have it in plenty of time for the show." She said, patting him on the shoulder. "Hope so... how much time you got left for lunch?" "Gotta be back at the office in about half an hour." "Plenty of time, you’ll enjoy this chowder." He smiled as he leaned back in his chair. |~o~| "You gotta be fuckin’ kidding me!" With the obligatory curse word, Nitz paced around his room, talking into his cell phone. "Not kidding..." the female voice on the other end said, recognized instantly as his sister Hailey. "What do you want me to say Nick? Chad said he is coming back to town again and wants to see the show Sunday. He said he’d be here by Saturday. I guess that punch in the head you gave him made him want to see you more." "Stop it with the god damn jokes Hail. What about Rachelle? I wasn’t ready to be confronted abut this just yet, not BEFORE the show this weekend anyways. That fuckhead doesn’t work anymore?" "He has a lot of free time on his hands now that his company got a hold of the big account. Big payouts equals big time to spare little brother." She said, apparently washing dishes in the background as she talked. The plates and cutlery could be heard banging together. "Yeah fine, free time... so he needs to get his ass out here to see a damn wrestling show? To see me with Rachelle? What’s that gonna do to her?" "Nick, sometimes problems come up. I can’t fix it and I don’t control Chad. If he wants to come out here, he will. He bought a ticket for the event already." "Well that’s just fuckin’ dandy. Bet you spilled the beans as to what section you’re all sitting in huh?" "How could I not?" Hailey replied, a crash heard in the background as she was talking. "FUCK! I couldn’t let him sit alone. He got a ticket right behind us all." "So he could stab everyone in the back again no doubt..." "Look little bro, I need to go. I don’t have time to argue about this as I get glass in my foot from a broken glass. I’ll call you back after." She hung up. "FUCK SAKES!" Nitz yelled to no one in particular as he tossed his cell phone on the couch and fell back beside it. He threw his head back and covered his face with his hands, unable to get Chad out of his mind again. Rachelle was now with Nitz and Chad had no idea. He would know soon enough, there was just no way to hide it. Certainly he couldn’t explain her attendance at the show Sunday without giving something away. The last important family gathering she was at was Christmas and even then Chad couldn’t believe she was just invited for the heck of it. He had a complex where he doesn’t believe in ex’s, close to the family or not, attending anything the family puts on. It’s a fact that would make life difficult for Nitz in the next coming days. |~o~| Friday, November 2, 2007 The cool November wind was blowing the last few leaves around the streets. The street lights were shining brightly, illuminating the ground and the few pieces of trash that were lying around. Mice and rats could be heard scrounging around in the alley, but those sounds were quickly replaced with the cool breeze whipping around the corner from the building in front of them as they walked. Nitz had his hands in his pockets and Rachelle was walking by his side, he arms wrapped around one of his. Their legs were moving in sync with the right first, followed by the left. Her healed boots were clapping against the sidewalk. "So Mr. Ego as they call you... why did you get into wrestling?" She asked, smiling has she whipped her hair back out of her eyes. The breeze did her no favors however and the same strands were falling back over her face again. "Why? There really is no reason why. It kinda just came up. I always watched it when I was a kid and there was this school. I was too young at the time to learn there so I practised at home. Mom always bugged me that I was gonna put my eye out or some shit and of course I didn’t listen. Fortunately I kept my eyes and when I was young enough, I took off to Japan and Mexico to train and perform. When I got back in the country, I had a few calls from a local Indy guy. The rest as they say is history." "Alright then, why do you stay in it when you can get hurt?" "Simple really..." He paused as he took his hand out of his pocket and scratched his upper lip. "I love the rush. The energy that runs through your veins when you are performing in front of people is insane. I supposed you could compare it to the job of a daredevil or a stuntman or some fuckin’ crazy ass gig like that." They shared a laugh. Surprisingly, Rachelle wasn’t at all shocked or appalled at the mouth Nitz had on him. This wasn’t even the half of it either. She watched PRIME and knew how he talked on there and she happened to mention that he was quite clever in some of the random phrases he managed to make up. "So am I coming up for a drink?" Rachelle smiled as she talked innocently while attempting to close the front of Nitz’s coat, one big flirty gesture he noticed she did a lot. "Yeah, tonight is the best night. Chad will be around tomorrow sometime and sneaking around is not one of my specialties. Normally I like everyone to know what woman I’m with." He said while he walked her up the stairs. He used his key on the front door and held it open for her as they made their way to the elevator. He presses the button to open the doors and they went inside, sharing another smile together. He pressed the button for his floor and the doors shut. When they arrived on his floor, they made their way down the hall to his suite located in the corner of the building. When he got there, he put his key in the deadbolt, but it wouldn’t turn. "That’s odd; my deadbolt was locked when I left..." "Maybe you just forgot." "That..." He said as he turned the knob. "Or maybe Hailey let herself in with her key." He opened the door and noticed the light on in the family room. Rachelle was still on his arm and walked in with him, closing the door behind them. "Hailey, is that you?" They made their way to the family room, hearing someone get up off the couch. They approached the doorway. "Look bro I think we need to talk about a couple things." A voice said from beyond the wall and it certainly wasn’t Hailey’s. "Hailey gave me her key and told me to make my way over. I told her we need to put things behind us." Chad said as Nitz and Rachelle came into view. Chad lifted his head and was staring in the eyes of not only his brother, but his Ex Girlfriend too. Both had a look of shock on their face as they stood in the doorway and Chad stood stoic, not knowing what to say. "Chad... I..." Rachelle started to say but was cut off by Nitz. "Don’t say a word Rachelle. He shouldn’t even be here." "Yeah well forgive me for trying to make amends with you little brother." Chad said as he grabbed his coat. "But it looks like nothing is out of bounds for you. Not even Rachelle. You really..." "FUC K YOU CHAD!" Nitz yelled, tossing his keys down the hall in anger. "You really are a piece of fuckin’ trash huh? You treat this girl like shit when you leave while she deserves nothing but the best and you hope to be treated with respect through it all? Fuck that, she looked me up ok." Chad pursed his lips and nodded his head, making his way between Nitz and Rachelle without as much as a word until he was half way to the door. "You know I come here and I hope to patch things up and then see you wrestle Sunday. But I take an early flight and everything blows up in my face huh?" He said as he searched for his own keys for his rental car. "Probably were gonna play dumb with me the whole weekend, pretend that you two aren’t an item." He took a step to Rachelle. "Probably were fuckin’ him behind my back before huh? Whore..." SNAP! Nitz drove forward and tackled Chad to the floor and began trying to punch him in the face. Chad was blocking but it was all a blur to him as his brother seemed like a man possessed. Rachelle rushed over and tried to pull Nitz off his brother. "Get off Nicholas, don’t worry about him! He isn’t worth it..." Nitz rolled to the side, bringing Rachelle down to the ground by force as Chad backed to the door on his ass by way of his hands. He had a spot of blood trickling down his lip as he wiped it away. "Not worth it? Well fuck you babe, you missed the boat and this yacht has long since left the building. So stick with Nicky here and live in this hell hole while I go back home to my lap of luxury in Portland after this weekend." He got up and walked to the door, opening it. "Yeah, I’ll still be there Sunday. Hope I see you there." He said to Rachelle as he walked out and slammed the door. Nitz was still breathing heavy, rage overtaking him. On the way to the ground after the tackle, The Ego caught his arm on the sharp end of a table and ripped his coat. He also sliced his arm a little and blood was trickling through the opening that was now in the leather. "Jesus, that’s a nice cut. Let’s bandage that up... come on." Rachelle said, trying to get Nitz up. He got up but the first thing he did was walk to the door and lock the deadbolt after putting the chain over. He then took a look at his arm before letting Rachelle tend to it.
