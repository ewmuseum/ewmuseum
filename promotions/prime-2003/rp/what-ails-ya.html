What Ails Ya

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Late FADE IN: INT. STUDIO - DAY MICHAEL MANN Cut! We spared you of the trite 'show you a scene that seems unordinary and then swerve you to show that it's just a movie' scene to open this chapter, so you owe us a thank you. Instead, we join the area just after the scene, and we find our hero, Danny Ferguson, already walking off the set. He's dressed in the wardrobe guy's best impersonation of original Terry Funk West Texas attire...which coincidentally, is a pretty good replica. You expect nothing less with Michael Mann involved. Something else you come to expect is a rigid shooting schedule, so as Danny weaves his way through the woodwork, wires and wannabes who pepper the area, we're treated to some news from the famed director. MICHAEL MANN Okay, take fifteen, everyone, and I don't mean twenty. I mean fourteen and thirty seconds. We're going to nail this stuff today. Danny's shoulders sag as he hears the notice, and he limps over to a corner, plopping down in chair with his name on it as some weekend stripper with a cosmetics degree checks his foundation for any sweat marks. Off to the side, his manager, Allan North, is patting a rolled up copy of the script in his palm, Paul Ellering-style. Of course, the reference is lost on him. Hell, it's probably lost on you, too, you nu-era flippity flop ROH-loving bitch. DANNY FERGUSON This guy's killing me, Al. ALLAN NORTH He's a taskmaster, that's for sure. DANNY FERGUSON We must have shot five hours of footage today. How can we possibly have more? Is this a movie or a miniseries. ALLAN NORTH Take it easy, champ - we're only in the early years. There's at least another month or two before we wrap this. DANNY FERGUSON At this pace? Two months at this pace? He runs a hand across his forehead, effectively ruining the makeup artist's work. She swoops back in for a touch-up, but he disposes of her with a startlingly-bitchy wave-off. ALLAN NORTH It'll pay off, and you know it. DANNY FERGUSON Yeah, in the fall. What the hell am I doing now? I need some instant gratification here, Allan. I need a damn break. (beat) Call PRIME. Have them take me off Revolution this week. Allan's gaze turns puzzled, and he chuckles a little. ALLAN NORTH You're joking, right? DANNY FERGUSON Do I look like I'm joking? When I'm joking, I either slap you on the shoulder or 'accidentally' smack you in the nuts, don't I? Do you see me doing either of those things right now? ALLAN NORTH This week is the first set of in ring scenes, remember? DANNY FERGUSON No, I don't remember. You're my fucking calendar, REMEMBER? I pay you to keep track of this kind of stuff. ALLAN NORTH Right, it's just that you talked about it last week, and... DANNY FERGUSON I talk about a lot of things that I don't remember the next day. Just ask Teri Hatcher. Over Allan's shoulder, Danny notices Michael Mann finishing up with a crew member and starting to move towards his chair. DANNY FERGUSON Fucking hell, North, this asshole's coming over here. ALLAN NORTH No big deal, we can talk to- DANNY FERGUSON If he gives me one more fucking pointer about how to stand like a motherfucking cowboy, I swear to Christ I'll- (smiling as Mann is within earshot) Mikey, buddy, are you not seeing enough of me already? The three men laugh as genuinely as you'd expect from three men in their respective positions. Around them, the 'normal' people bustle in preparation for the continuation of filming. MICHAEL MANN Just wanted to check in on how you felt about the posture thing we spoke of before. DANNY FERGUSON (beaming) I think I'm getting it. The hips, right? MICHAEL MANN Right, the hips. A little bit of the back, too, which is what I thought you- DANNY FERGUSON Say, Mike, I've got a question. We're shooting at PRIME on Wednesday, right? MICHAEL MANN I believe so, yeah. DANNY FERGUSON (eyeing Allan) Yeah, got it in my calendar and everything. Hey, so my guy there says you haven't contacted them about an opponent? Do we need to find someone? Cause they've got this Japanese guy who I think would be great for...I dunno, one of the Japanese guys in the script. Plus I know a dude who's part Vietnamese, but I can tell him to skip his next few tanning sessions and he'd be ready to go, too, we just need... He trails off as his speech is trampled underfoot by Mann, who speaks with a little more authority. MICHAEL MANN Actually, I was told that we're bringing your opponent with us. Danny and Allan both look confused, and Mann senses the next question. He waves over a PA and grabs the clipboard from their hand, flipping through it multiple times before settling on a page. DANNY FERGUSON So which historic opponent are we going with? Tsuruta? Johnny Valentine? Dick Murdoch? MICHAEL MANN Are you familiar with Mark Sandusky? DANNY FERGUSON Sand - who the f...no, sorry, I can't place it. MICHAEL MANN Hmm. Well, his son, Kevin, auditioned really well, and requested the opportunity to play his father, who he says - and we backed this up ourselves - that his father wrestled around the same time Terry was getting started. While Mann delivers that last part, Danny is looking over his shoulder at Allan, mouthing the name 'Sandusky?' to an empty response from his manager. DANNY FERGUSON Michael, I don't know if- MICHAEL MANN Not a problem, Danny. His in-ring skills are what impressed me - we can worry about continuity afterwards. The real movie magic happens in the editing room anyway. Before Danny can say anything else, someone back near the set calls Michael's name and he quietly excuses himself, making sure to point at his watch and smile before he goes, playfully reminding his star of the end of break time. DANNY FERGUSON (watching him go) Then why don't you edit my goddamned cowboy stance in later, you piece of- ALLAN NORTH Let's save that stuff for the DVD extras, huh? DANNY FERGUSON If he keeps this shit up... (beat) Who the motherfuck is Sandusky? ALLAN NORTH When you go back to filming, I'm going to slip out and call Reggie. Have him search through his tape library and pull something. DANNY FERGUSON Have him call the offices, too. I don't want to be booked on Rev 96. I want a damn week off. ALLAN NORTH I'll see what I can do. DANNY FERGUSON Don't see. Do it. (beat) Do it. He stands up from his chair, snaps for the makeup girl, and makes his way back towards the set. Allan already has the phone out, and makes his way towards the door. ALLAN NORTH Who the hell is Kevin Sandusky? FADE OUT.
