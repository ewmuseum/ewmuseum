Walking on the wild side

22 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd July 2005 Sob what did we do in the months between leaving Vegas and arriving here, you might ask? From the side of Gaz Green… Well, if I do say so myself, squire, I found myself cut out to be in the adult movie industry. Applications went well, physical shape couldn’t have been better and the obvious was sufficient. However, it would be a mere 2 months into my career that things went wrong while working on an alternate video. To be entitled Amazon Dom 3, I was to play an explorer that would be subjected to physical abuse by a powerful woman. Despite the residents of Great Britain having to endure this every day for several years thanks to Thatcher, I guess some people can’t get enough. So it was a certain day that I, Gaz Green, working under the porn star name of Big Bad Dave, made my way to a set located in some rather thick forestry. While not the jungles of Africa, I doubted in my mind that anyone would care if it was a lesser or greater spotted frog in the background, especially seeing the only wildlife in the video would involve chicks and a snake. It was around 1am and we were on the set, awaiting our cues. Here I was, dressed in my Indiana Jones garb, complete with the whip that my co-star was supposed to tie me up with later on. And that was when I saw… The Beast. Stepping out of her trailer and into the sunlight, I stood in awe. The smallest sentence I could use to effectively describe her would be “Stanislav in drag”. Her face would be rather attractive at first – a pair of baby blues and a pretty pair of pink lips, all capped off with long platinum blonde hair. Then you would hit the chin… something I wish I had done sooner. Needless to say, I had seen incarnations of Batman with more feminine features. Then you see the neck, which I would probably fail to completely wrap both my hands around from thumb to middle finger. The rest was tanned and oiled lumps and bumps of muscle, which looked more like someone had rolled a carrot in gravy and glued it to others. Covering her was a leopard-skin bikini and loincloth, never enough for my liking. If I were the director instead of the actor, I would have recommended Haz-Mat suits for us all, just to be sure! Quiet fell on the set and I gradually made my way through the flora, hacking away at various branches and such while looking for nothing in particular. All of a sudden, the she-beast pounced on me behind. I threw the machete aside as soon as she grabbed my arm to make it look as though it had been forced from my hand. At this point, she pinned me down by my wrists and started growling like a tiger. I did not have the situation in mind as I signed up for the career, but here I was and there was nothing I could do about it. Adrenaline was pumping and acting was the last thing on my mind as the gorilla of a woman began to tear at my shirt, slapping my face as she did. The blows were real and I began flashing back to my time in the ring, something I had put behind myself. Despite this, she was forcing me to remember the hits I’d taken between the four ropes. As she stood up, I couldn’t help myself and instinct took over. She turned, looking for the whip. My body moved on it’s own and immediately hit a drop toe hold, taking her down. Luckily, she kept in character and growled, wielding the whip and ready to lash me. With no option, I began to run towards a tree, quickly shimmying up the bark and retreating to the safety of a branch. It was strong enough to support me and luckily the she-beast below was in no condition to climb trees. Instead, she aimed whiplashes at my feet. I presumed her plan would be to knock me down and catch me where upon I would once again be subject to her domination. Again, my reflexes took over and I found myself leaping off the branch and planting my legs around her head, rotating backwards and forming a perfect head scissors, whereby the momentum of the move flipped her over into an impressive Hurricanrana. While it wasn’t enough to knock her out, the roles were reversed and now I was in control. I rushed over to her and while she lay on her back, quickly turned my own back on her and leapt into the air, landing stomach first over her body into a Moonsault. Like I’d done so many times before, I hooked the leg for the pin but instead of the 3-count, the only thing in my ears when I finally regained control was the director, who had apparently been screaming “CUT!” for minutes. ~ And from my side… Nascosto Nerezza. Arkham Zoo was the place where I had resumed active employment. It had been sold on TV and radio as a “theme zoo”, comprising of part freak show, part wildlife reserve. I found myself at home among the quadruplet of nocturnal houses, reptile farms and insect hives. There was an eerie comfort in seeing other unusual creatures that was only slightly spoiled by the other staff. Nightmarish clowns, classic universal monsters, neo-gothic-industrial sub-genre body modification punks… the animals may have been the rarer inhabitants of the zoo, but by far, not the weirdest. I had personally been assigned care of the “Nature’s Mistakes” exhibit. It was hardly Nature’s mistake that had driven the animals to be this way - a mutant snake that had grown two heads, fused together with a solitary cyclopean eye planted in the middle was more the result of nuclear testing or the dumping of radioactive waste than Gaia’s folly. Nevertheless, it was a marvel to behold. Other such animals were less freakish or deformed and naturally a simple fork in evolution’s path. The ultimate anticlimax was a carnivorous plant that was kept in a caged enclosure “for the safety of our visitors”. Upon asking my superiors what to feed it, it was revealed that the plant was not carnivorous at all, but the aroma it emitted was enough to make the punters think otherwise. She was right, too… nicknamed the “Corpse Flower”; it smelled like a rotting carcass. The reputation and secret of the plant were both respectively publicized and guarded, with the reason of it being fed before opening and after closing times in order to prevent it attacking anyone during the day. It was precisely four days since Gaz had applied to the adult entertainment industry that I led my first group around the exhibit. The clientele, as it were, were no different from any normal zoo. A lucky dip of society, from the average family, the curious professor and hiding at the back, the 30-year-old Star Trek fan. While there no Klingons present, I prayed to whichever god I was supposed to, hoping they would prevent him comparing any animal to something he’d seen in a Sci-Fi channel. My uniform was… amusing, to say the least. My appearance was to be of some sort of noble demon, comprising of a red velvet mantle and beneath it, a full leather bodysuit with various straps, buckles, spikes and skulls adorning it. I was instructed to speak in a generic and vague mid-European accent… something I practiced but to this day, never mastering it. But nobody seemed to mind nor care. The actual interior of my dominion, if such word was correct, was extremely well designed. A haunting chorus of tortured groans and rattlesnake-style jitters provided the ambience, broken up by the occasional roar or screech. The youngest of the bunch, a pre-teen boy who I would later come to know as James (or Jim, as he refused to be called by his full name), was also perhaps the bravest. I first noticed it as we encountered a clear glass tank containing a spiny and elongated crab. He pressed up against the tank, staring eye to eye with the hideous crustacean. “And this is something that came from who knows where… perhaps the radiation that the Japanese were plagued by during World War II didn’t create one giant monster in the form of Godzilla, but millions of these monstrosities to make up for it. Imagine taking a brief trip out to see in your yacht, only to suddenly feel a small STING in your ankle. You turn around there they are… by the score… all swarming over your possessions, tearing your ship to pieces with their lethal spines. You think the only way out is to dive overboard but then you see them in the water – ten times the number on your ship.” “How do they taste?” the boy asked, interrupting me. “James, please, let the man finish.” His mother scolded him. “Ahahaha, so curious, little one.” I chortled, gently leading him away from the glass. “I’m sure they wonder the exact same thing of you.” Then the exhibit I loathed the most… the Corpse Flower. Three feet across and stuck in the cage, I began my usual routine of hyping the plant to be more than it was. “Now, ladies and gentlemen…” I took a small bow to the child. “You must stay back from this. I’m sure many of you can already smell the remains of the creature’s final meal.” The small crowd nodded their heads in agreement, adding a few comments on what it smelled like and pinching their noses shut. I can safely say that no place on earth smelled worse than Skitzo’s former residence, a place I had built a tolerance to over a period of time, which provided me the stomach to stay around the plant. “This is the Corpse Plant. We have to feed it before we open and after we close to make sure it doesn’t get peckish. The cage is there for both your safety and mine. Even though we bring such things here, we cannot bend them completely to our will. This particularly nasty species of carnivorous plant grows only in the deadly Sumatran rainforests, where it eats almost anything… birds, insects, and snakes… and apparently, it has a taste for primates. I will make no secret of it that I am not the first person to introduce this plant to you, but I am certainly more careful than our last one.” “It doesn’t look so scary…” Jim piped up again, stepping ahead of me and looking directly at it. “Hold your stride, young lad. Perhaps some day, you may grow up to be strong enough to bring another of this foul flora to us. But for now, he who fights and runs away…” Again, the boy interrupted me, but not with words. In a quixotic dash, he had pulled a sabre from a suit of armour and ran towards the plant. I was struck with a decision that would have to be made in a millisecond; Either to try and hold an impulsive boy back and try to avoid being stabbed, or join in and help him hack up the plant to preserve it’s secret and the reputation of the establishment even at the cost of the plant itself. Wrestling is in the blood. I ducked my head and quickly rushed at him, grabbing the hand that held the sword and squeezing it. He was struggling, refusing to let go for whatever reason a child might consider valid. I brought his arm behind him into a hammerlock, finally getting the sword from his hand and thankfully leading him back to the group. He was beginning to cry, but thankfully I hadn’t gone overboard. His parents quickly shuffled away, dragging him by the arm and being far rougher than I was with him. However, I looked back to the plant and shrieked in horror. The sword, in the scuffle, had been kicked across the floor and the blade had slid through the bars of the mesh cage, severing the root. The plant’s main protrusion had bent up against the bars as it slouched to one side like a dead man trying to stay on his feet. This was bad. ~ We wound up back at our cheap motel with not much aside from the cockroaches for company. “So you ended up killing a rare man-eating plant?” Gaz asked, plucking the leaves from his hair. “And you broke character, made the dominatrix tap out and only got away with it because the director understood your background.” Nerezza gruffly sighed. These four walls had held us too long. An epiphany came over us… we were never cut out to be zookeepers, or porn stars, plumbers or flight attendants. For 4 years, we’d only been good at one thing. It would forever surface in our souls every time we got a little excited because our bodies were programmed for it. We were wrestlers. We ARE wrestlers. No more sitting and watching mindless cable TV. No more wearing ridiculous cartoon costumes… well, maybe some more. Our home was the squared circle, not the diamond motel. We searched for new places under disguises, with Nerezza finally nailing PRIME under the pseudonym Bastion Cossack. We waited, working off the ring rust, trying to rebuild ourselves. And we promised ourselves that no matter how many losses, how many humiliations we suffered, we’d be staying. And win or lose… we’d leave the ring laughing all the way.
