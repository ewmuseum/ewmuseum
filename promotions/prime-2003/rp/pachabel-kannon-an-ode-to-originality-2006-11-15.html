Pachabel Kannon: An Ode to Originality

15 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-15-06 The stage is set for a PRIMEtime coup; The lights are slowly dimming down. The time has come for something new. Watch! I’ll make these Lemmings drown. You can’t defeat me in deceit, Nor through pure strength alone. The more this poem grows offbeat, The more outraged my tone. Surname earned, or surname stole? I really can’t decide. Your young career I will extol, And slap you twice cockeyed. I can’t abide a man like you With such pure pedigree; A man who lacks a single shred Of originality. So come on Alex, earn your name, Before that rumbling in your belly Becomes a bit of yellow-tail, As your run off to snuggle Ellie~! See, I don’t fuck with yellow-tail It’s a shitty Australian wine. It’s time to jump, to jive and wail, Across your splintered spine. So listen well, you Kannon brat, I’m not your fucking mother. I’m the swatter, you’re the gnat, You I’ll cripple and no other. It’s a statement, see, for you to be A symbol of might and moxie. It’s cute and sad, but unfortunately I’ll still beat your ass by proxy. School is now in session boy, And best you bite your tongue. I hate to see them go so soon; Careers are killed too young. But it’s the nature of the beast my friend, So your pain I’ll take for free. With hands outstretched I vow to lend You all the help shown me. Which was none. Fuck you, son. Now I’m done. ~snapplause~
