Parties, Women and Booze... what a Champion!

undated

Date: ??? The count was still lingering in his head and it was part of the aura surrounding him. He could still picture the fans, stunned into silence, pondering what Killean had said previous to the very entertaining main event at Ultra Violence. Karina Wolfenden and Killean Sirrajin… it was most definitely a match that was bound to go down in the history books as two promising young talents collided in their “prime” so to speak. The fans finally broke the silence mere seconds later, rising to their feet to give both combatants their just deserves. It was a day that would live in the mind of the Supreme Machine forever. It was his second chance at wrestling that put him in the position to even be considered a top contender for the top prize in PRIME. It was only a short ten months earlier that Killean had joined PRIME and impressed many with his power, speed and pure talent. It took ten months of pain, torture, brutality and emotional murder to get his shot at the Universal Championship. The first time he had that shot, his dreams were shattered as the one, two and three were counted in favor of Boda. But the second time was not going to be wasted. It could be considered the count heard ‘round the world when all was said and done. ONE… TWO… THREE!!!! He could hear the referee’s hand striking the mat, counting the shoulders of the K-Wolf down for the first time in PRIME. It truly was a night to remember. Karina Wolfenden lost for the first time in a PRIME ring and Emilio Rage beat Vampir Nosferatu but it seemed as if he paid with his life. But the biggest of all for Sirrajin was that he now had the chance to hold aloft the top championship in a mainstream federation. The PRIME Universal Championship eluded him once, but now it was in his tight grasp and he would have to be killed before it would be pried from his powerful hands again. It was Thursday before Killean finally returned to his home in San Diego. Only after all the wild parties and get togethers were over, it had finally sunk in what he had accomplished. The parties were nothing more than a celebration and an excuse to get “off the handle” drunk. Sirrajin even knew that he had slept with a couple women at the conclusion of a few wild nights, something that he was not proud of in the least. He never even knew their names. But they sure gave him a ride, similar to the whirlwind roller coaster he was experiencing after the biggest victory in his career. He was on top of the mountain and a person for rookies to look up to. Yeah, some role model huh? Sleeping with random women and getting piss drunk for a few nights straight was something that Killean didn’t want to make a part of his routine. Opening the door to the house, he was greeted with a powerful aroma. Putting his bag on the floor by the door, he anxiously breathed in the wonderful fragrance. It was a fragrance that he had not smelled in quite some time. A home cooked meal. The housekeeper Vivian appeared from the kitchen and held her arms out to greet Killean and welcome him home. “Welcome home hun!” the older woman sounded ecstatic to see Sirrajin return home. She definitely knew what had happened at Ultra Violence. How could she not? She had been telling Killean that she would watch it on pay per view just so she could see him win the big one. “Good to be home.” Sirrajin sounded tired and he was ready to just crash and fall asleep on the couch. But how could he resist the food? The aroma of the meal was the only thing that was currently keeping him awake. “What is that smell? Is that…” “Yes my dear, its hot wings and pan fries. I had to make one of your favorite meals for yew when I heard yew was comin’ home.” Killean looked down to the older woman and smiled genuinely. Coming home to a wonderful meal was one of the only things he looked forward to anymore in San Diego. His life was pretty much elsewhere now. Killean leaned down and hugged Vivian, thanking her for constantly thinking of him. She was much more than just a housekeeper to him; she was family and was one of the best friends in his life. Later on, Killean and Vivian were sitting at the table and Killean was chewing on one of the last chicken wings left on his plate. He certainly enjoyed the meal, something that he made obvious with a thank you now and then and the constant satisfying murmurs that he purposely made while he ate. Vivian was pleased and had finished the small amount she had gathered for herself. She was a larger woman but eating too much was definitely not the reason for her extra weight. Sirrajin even noted in the past that doesn’t even eat enough to feed a bird. Killean was finishing the last of his meal and talking with Vivian when the topic of his emotional help and Kira came up in the conversation. It certainly was not a surprise for Vivian to talk about Kira and Killean always talked openly about Kira with her. But he had just gotten off the biggest night in his career and really didn’t want to get back to the old grind and get back to his normal life so to speak. For the last week, he had ridden the wave of success and let it take him wherever it did. Whatever happened, he took it all in and let it slide. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Sitting in the large bedroom, Killean was digging through a few boxes and bags. The windows in the room were open letting in the fresh air and also ventilating some of the stale dust that was kicked up. The boxes were not opened in over a year and the bags stood in the closet gathering all kinds of dust. Opening all the boxes and bags kicked up all the dust and made The Supreme Machine cough. But nothing was going to keep him from reliving the moments of his past with his lovely wife. It was all Vivian’s idea. She mentioned earlier to Sirrajin that he should begin to dig through the memories and begin to let go some more. Some personal items of Kira’s were no longer needed. It was the really sentimental items that Killean really needed to keep around. There was no point in letting these other items sit around and collect more dust when other people could put good use to some of the clothes. It could all go to the less fortunate. Kira would have wanted it that way after all. She was always a giving person in the sense that she was well off, she felt it to be unfair and did everything she could to level out the lives of others as well. Good Will was certainly looking for donations and Sirrajin was more than happy to donate some clothing and knickknacks for the greater good. After going through the clothes and deciding what was going to be given to Good Will, Sirrajin came across some real sensitive things. Kira was buried with her wedding ring and engagement ring, but some other items that Killean gave to her were in the jewelry box that he happened to dig out of the closet. A beautiful silver necklace with diamond pendant was the first item that Killean held high. He held it up and in front of the lamp on the end table and it reflected the light towards Killean and the ceiling of the bedroom. It created many different colors and was certainly an item that Killean was keeping. He also came across some gold bracelets and other miscellaneous rings and necklaces. Sirrajin finally decided that he was going to keep them all and just give away the clothes he picked out. He saved some other clothing such as Kira’s wedding dress and other favorite tops. After looking through about 12 different boxes and bags, Killean was feeling extremely tired. He leaned back against the headboard with a pillow. He didn’t expect to doze off but as soon as he shut his eyes, he was out like a light. Vivian took a quick look around the corner and noticed Killean asleep. Snoring ever so gently, Killean was the covered by a small blanket that was hanging over a chair in the corner. Vivian then turned out the light and made her way to her room down the hall, closing the bedroom door halfway on the way out. She happened to stop and take notice that Killean had separated some clothes to give away. She smiled and knew that it was finally happening and Killean was moving forward with his life after all this time. An hour or so later, Killean was startled out of his slumber by a noise emanating from down the hall. He heard something fall and make a clang when it hit the ground. A mere few seconds later, the sound of shattering glass filled the room and Killean jumped to life. He stepped quietly towards the door and grabbed the bright silver baseball bat from behind it. He looked quickly around the corner and noticed a couple shadows making their way towards his room. He pressed his body against the wall beside the door and waiting patiently for it to burst open. He saw a glove covered hand pressing against the door and when it pushed the door open, Killean swung, connecting with the wall first, but he was then pushed back into the darkness of the room. The figure made its way through the door, ready to engage in a territorial fight, one Killean would not lose easily. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Thursday, April 7, 2005 12:30 PM Pacific Time(Filmed before Killean returned home) Killean was walking to the bar at the local beach. The sun was shining and it was definitely warmer than normal for that time of year. He decided to stop and enjoy the weather before returning home and returning to his personal life amidst the celebration he had been living for the last few days instead. He grabbed a short glass of rum and coke with ice and made his way over to a rather vacant spot on the beach. He removed his shirt, revealing his well built body and the tattoos that covered his right and left arms. He sat down on the sand, sipping at the drink and then proceeding to speak his mind. “What a glorious week it has been. It’s been full of parties, drinking and perhaps some activities with some ladies that I am not exactly proud of. Hell if anyone did what I did Sunday, then I am sure some of the same events would have taken place. But the main point is I did what I said I would do at Ultra Violence.” Killean looked to be reveling in his win and the glory that has surrounded him. Never to be one to rub in a victory, Killean quickly made his point and moved on. “The Universal Championship is in good hands and I can guarantee that the fans will be proud of their champion. But I have held the Intense Title for quite sometime and I cannot help but think that people will begin to see it in a different light. People may say, ‘How can the Universal Champion defend the Intense Title as well?’ But the answer is simple. I am not a champion like any other. The Supreme Machine will defend both titles with honor and will remove anyone in his path. This brings me to my first opponent, Hoyt Williams of the Imperium.” Killean took a large sip of his drink after he stopped speaking. He took a few moments to soak in some of the sun, and kept his gaze skyward. “Hoyt Williams, the supposed Son of God himself. Well Hoyt, we have met in the ring many times in the past. A few times you got the upper hand on me but I can assure it will not be repeated here and now. As I recall, the last time you and I locked horns was on ReVolution 50 and I proceeded to shock the living hell out of you and four others with a power source I found after I crashed through the ring. I was not about to let you get your grubby hands on a prize of that magnitude. But things have changed and now I am the Universal and Intense Champions. I am the top of the food chain and I am looking down from my perch, ready to pounce. You have been spending your entire time here in PRIME trying to catch the runaway freight train known as Killean Sirrajin. But every time you put out an arm to jump on the final rail car, you stumble and land flat on your face on the train tracks of your career.” Killean stood up and made his way over to the bar again to replenish his beverage but continued on his verbal tirade of PRIME’s Pontiff. “It has been nothing but an upward battle for you since the day I set foot in PRIME and you can’t deny that. You figured that God could help you and that you are here to invoke God’s Will on PRIME. But I can tell you this, ‘God’s Will’ will not be enough to stop me. I am untouchable. Boda will learn that for the sneak attack on me after my match at Ultra Violence. But first, I must lay to rest God’s phony servant. Hoyt Williams will fall at ReVolution this week, being only the next in line to experience the power of The Supreme Machine. Bring your Imperium, for I will run through them all if necessary. It would be the worst mistake a group of people could make. Wrestling immortality has beckoned and I have answered the call.” Killean had received his refill and he was making his way back across the beach to pick his shirt off the sand, tossing the empty cup into the trash bin. After plucking the shirt from the ground, he straightened his sunglasses and made his way to the parking lot to finally go home and relax after a long week. He deserved it.
