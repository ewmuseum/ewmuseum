Life changes Part 1

24 November 2010

Date: 11/24/10 The First woke up, his head swimming, he wasn’t hung over, but he wasn’t refreshed from his sleep either, and then the memories of what happened the night before started to flood back into his mind. The night was a party a Thanksgiving themed indy show in Albany New York, close enough for his friends from the New England indy scene to come in, and big enough that a bunch of other talent was able to meet up. First had been brought in as a special attraction and worked a match with J1D, a stoner with a huge internet fan base, in First’s mind, they tore the house down. After the show a bunch of the guys hit a bar and everyone got themselves good and plastered, First ran into his friend Eddie “The Fire” Burns, who was already drunk. First decided to drink with him as Eddie got himself even more hammered. The two of them enjoyed stupid drunk conversation for a while when Eddie then ruined everything. “Man, you got a great deal with Rozy man…I mean, you get to screw Muse and all the ring rats and stuff, and she’s free for whatever, but man I’d be kinda upset if I didn’t know if my kid was my own.” He said as he took another pull from a beer. “What the fuck are you talking about?” First replied. “What? I mean, you’ve been screwing Muse this whole time right?” Eddie said, his eyes glazed from drinking, he looked completely baffled by First’s protests. “No…I haven’t, and what do you mean my kid might not be my own?” First said, the haze of his drunkenness lifting as he focused his gaze upon Eddie. “Look man, I mean..Everyone talks about it…I…I thought this was just common knowledge and just what you were doing with your personal life…I didn’t mean anything by it.” “You’re calling my wife a whore?!” First yelled. Eddie fell back out of his chair and crashed to the ground hard, “Ah fuck, Burns can’t hold his shit together now we’re all getting kicked out.” Shouted Felix Red from the back, Felix was practically invulnerable to anything and his drug binges the stuff of legend. He’s left the majors a year or so ago when most thought his addictions made him a bad investment, but he still worked an indy gig now and then. True to Felix’s word, the bartender cut off the gaggle of wrestlers, and soon enough everyone was finding cabs or driving drunk back to hotels or the houses of the conquest of the night. First thought about getting into a fight with Eddie, but something held him back, something more than just the fact that getting into a bar fight would be poor conduct for one in the professional spotlight, he didn’t want to be suspended from any of the companies he worked for , but more than that, Eddie said everything he said as if it was the gospel truth, First wondered if he really was that out of the loop, was his wife really having an affair or affairs on him behind his back, did she and everyone else think he was sleeping with Muse? Now he was awake and he had to deal with all this. He was thankful that Muse had been out at gig after the show. One of the many bands she had played in had a concert in town and the drummer got food poisoning, so she came in and performed with them again. If she had been there Eddie would have gotten his head caved in, Muse wasn’t one to have people make comments about who she was sleeping with, since she was very open about all that, she might screw around, but she did so out in the open and she wasn’t going to steal another woman’s husband. First showered, got dressed, he had the next three days off before he had to do radio interviews to build up the major PRIME tournament that was happening in a week. He didn’t know what to do with himself, but he had to do something, so he called his friend Will. “Hey bro, what’s going on?” Will said as he answered the phone, Will had been living in Salem for the past year after being medically discharged from the military for a back injury he suffered in Afghanistan. “Not much, I have to ask you, is my wife fucking around on me?” First replied, not a lot of small talk in this conversation, just getting to the point as it were, First hoped for a quick dismissal, a scoff or a laugh, the deafening silence on the other end of the phone put a knot in his stomach. Finally after agonizing moments Will spoke “You think I’m fucking her?” he said, he sounded weirdly defensive, maybe he was fucking her, or maybe First was just being paranoid, he couldn’t even keep his thoughts together, he felt like he was going insane. “No, I didn’t say that, what I did say is, was asking if my wife was fucking around on me, now I have to ask if she’s fucking around on me with you.” He didn’t even want an answer, First felt so hurt, his life was shattering in front of him, he thought back to when he met Rozy, at the movie theater in the Liberty Tree Mall, he forgot what movie he was going to go see, but he was there by himself and he saw her, all goth’ed out, tight fitting black clothing, curvy as hell, hair jet black, light purple eye shadow, she was a revelation. He watched her for five minutes, and no man was around her, and more than that, she was staring at him too. They watched a movie and about the only thing that kept them from having sex that night was First’s complicated non-relationship with Muse, which gnawed on him and made him feel guilty, but that guilt went away after a few more dates and soon he and Rozy were a couple, and Muse was a friend, a friend who hung out with the two of them for a year before she left to go on tour with x.E.R.O.x a garage band she’d been in that played local gigs and was now doing a national tour in support of an album bring promoted by some random label or another. Then was a simpler time, Rozy and him just screwed around, hung out, talked about souls, spirits, the meaninglessness of religion, life, death, everything. She went on the road with him for a while in the indy leagues, but she hated the crowds, for every ten guys that wanted to have sex with her, the one that called her fat or a cunt was the one that she heard, she couldn’t take the abuse. Nights after he fought, win or lose, were spent with her in tears over the things people said, she started having panic attacks and stress from it, and so she stopped being his valet. Will was stammering, sputtering on the phone, First wasn’t even listening to the words, just the tone of his voice was enough to convict him, He thought about how he couldn’t have seen all this coming, how he didn’t know his own life, his wife. Then he went back to that day when Eleanor was supposedly conceived. He remembered Rozy throwing herself at him, which was odd, normally by that point of the relationship he’d been the one that had to get the ball rolling when it came to sex, that day she seemed really intent on getting laid, now he looked at it and saw it as her attempting to cover for the fact that she was already pregnant and just wanted him to sleep with her so she’d have some justification in claiming the child was his. He’d been away for three weeks, and they hadn’t slept together for the week before that because he’d had a case of strep throat and she avoided him like the plague to avoid getting it herself. His child wasn’t his he winced as he thought about it. In his ear Will kept stammering, kept on talking now he sounded pleading, almost like he was begging forgiveness as he talked. Maybe he should bother listening to what Will was saying, but he didn’t think he needed to, cause the ugly truth was already out there, he didn’t know or really even care about what Will had to say. Then he randomly heard “Look man, you’ve been cheating on her this whole time in public with Muse, what did you expect to happen?!” and then he turned the phone off. He didn’t need to hear anymore. Why did his own wife not trust him, why did everyone think he was sleeping with Muse. First buried his face in his hands, was this all his fault, was hanging out with Muse and all this suspicion his fault? Did he bring all this on himself? He and Muse did have a chemistry about them, but he’d never done anything untoward with her on camera, they never even had a serious kiss in all the time. Muse was beautiful and maybe it’s just expected of the guy with the beautiful lady to be sleeping with her, but Muse was his friend and Rozy’s friend for years, hell Rozy and Muse had even made out before, Rozy had even brought up the idea of a threesome, but it never happened. He called Muse and asked her to meet him at a restaurant at the hotel, he didn’t even know what he was going to say to her, or what to talk to her about. Was he going to tell her he never wanted to see her again? End his friendship with her to try to save his marriage? He didn’t know what to think. She walked into the room, her hair today dyed a platinum blonde, wearing a low cut shirt and tight blue jeans, he looked at her and remembered the chubby girl she was in high school, she was still overweight when she left to go on tour, he remembered the text message all those years later “Hey Mr. Rock Star, lemme hang with you on the road, my band sucks, I’mma be your hot chick side kick!” and he relied “So what you look like?” because she was sweet and nice, but she wasn’t hot, that much was for sure. She replied with a full frontal nude photo that showed off all of her very in shape body. “Good enough for you?!” was the message with it, and with that, well, she got the gig. And here she was, this crazy, sexy girl who he’d spent the last two years with on the road, he’d had all the highs and lows of his career shared with her, spend all that time with her, had so much fun, cried many tears, and now she was munching on some toast, having no idea of events of the last night, or this morning. First looked at her, he knew how he felt now, he felt like a fool that he never admitted it to himself before, but now just looking at her, he knew what he had to say. No small talk here either, much like with Will before, he got right to the point. “Muse, do you still love me?”
