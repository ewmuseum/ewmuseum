Date: Various Fruit or Consequences The Official King Blueberry Blog I Burst in Your Mouth Like "Gushers" 3:12pm Friday, March 6, 2009 - 102 Comments You know, I probably should have picked a different title. That one looks a little, well, let’s just go with "risqué" and call it a day. And before anyone says anything about it, I am well aware that I can change it, but that would require clicking and I don’t really feel up to doing much of that right now. I also realize that in the time it’s taken me to justify myself to the faceless internet masses that I could have very well clicked in the ‘subject’ box and changed it to something else. I didn’t. Instead I chose to argue with you, and I don’t even know you, so take that to the bank and smoke it. By the way, hi. In case you haven’t been paying attention I should probably tell you that I’m awesome. I should probably also tell you that I’m King Blueberry, which if you read anything in that little box to the right (or is it left? - damn layouts and their DIVs, always mocking me!) you would know already. I should also probably tell you that I am by no means Jared Sykes, regardless of what that douchebag might try and tell you. Rest assured he is a douchebag - a very strong, handsome, agile, athletic, charismatic, funny, and charming douchebag, but a douchebag nonetheless. Where the hell was I? Ah! I got it! I was about to tell everyone that after a few months away to deal with some personal stuff, and by "personal stuff" I mean "none of your damned business, Chester." If you really want to know, just tell yourself that I was cryogenically flash-frozen and accidentally stored in the freezer case of your local Stop ‘n Shop. It won’t be true, but if it helps you sleep through the night then I’m all for it. Anyway, I’m back now, and in a few days I have to get on a plane to Russia to compete in the Dual Halo. (Note: My agent has strongly advised me to NOT say that I’m getting on a plane to Commietopia to compete in the Human Habitrail, so I’m not going to do that, even though I just did.) I’m expecting to kick serious ass as a result of my intense workout regime that revolves around eating walnuts by the fistful as I watch that episode of The Wonder Years where Wheezer and Puffy get sucked up into the vacuum cleaner. I wonder if I can sneak a vacuum past customs. * * * * * Leaving on a Jet Plane 9:34am Wednesday, March 11, 2009 - 3 Comments They seem to take exception to dudes in masks trying to get through security, so the guards and I had words. Even though I asked for a run-down cabin in the middle of the snowy wilds, where I would be free to do sit-ups while suspended from a wooden beam, he apparently decided I would be better suited in a classy hotel with a real gym. Note to self: find mustachioed Russian to chase me in old Ford while I run through the snow carrying logs. * * * * * He is not a man... 4:21pm Thursday, March 12, 2009 - 44 Comments Found a swarthy-looking guy with a kickin’ moustache, but couldn’t convince him to chase me through the snow in his car. Hung on for as long as I could, even wailed on a French guy, and then T-Rayne decided it’d be an awesome idea to drop me on my head. It didn’t tickle. In case you’ve ever wondered what it’s like to get VON DOOM’d, here’s how it goes down. First your head goes WHACK against the ground. Then your eyes go all googily, and your entire life - and oddly enough, the lives of five hundred and twelve supporting characters, they all flash before your eyes. Because your eyes have gone wonky it looks kind of bizarre. You might even think you see a werewolf or two. It’s not the impact that beats you. Hell, I get whacked in the skull all the time. What beats you is the fact that it somehow unleashes a tidal wave of chaos directly into your brain, causing it to overheat and sputter out. You can’t kick out because your neurons have gone on strike.
