Storytime, Kiddos.

21 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 21, 2007 You really believe you can pull this off? Pull what off? The Whole proving that the Tag Titles are superior to the Universal Title ordeal. Am I? It's written in stone, my friend. I'm laying out the facts. The general public has no other choice but to soak it in as gospel. What makes you so sure? They don't call me the Prince of Delusion for nothing, Machaiveli. In fact, let me share a story with you. Oh goody... Oh goody, indeed. I bet you didn't know I once led a wrestling promotion, did you? I'm afraid to ask why you once led one and are no longer doing so. Honestly, I pushed my abilities of delusion much too far. I constructed a 'work' that I assumed would draw the biggest ratings boost in the history of the promotion. The downside came when I attempted to sacrifice the safety and well-being of my employees in order to do so. Listen as I tell you the story. --- The last time the AWCP presented a ‘worked’ angle was during the entire Dane Mitchell-Seth Black fiasco. The fans grabbed hold of the thought that Dane was sleeping with Black's wife and thusly bit every ounce of bait thrown at them. Ultimately, it transformed the entire angle into something truly magical. I personally, however, saw so many informalities involved in that angle, that I knew without a shadow of doubt that no one had the balls to attempt anything similar again. I was wrong. Not only did I come in contact with someone who had the balls to attempt it, but who also had the balls big enough to amplify it. Former independent legend, Hector Fernandez, without even knowing it, was that man’s victim and that man was none other than...well...me. You hosted Hector “The Blade” Fernandez? Indeed I did. So there we were in Mobile, Alabama set to open the night’s show. Management had finalized the card and Hector was given no other choice but to abide by the match I'd assigned to him. Anger was much too loose of a term to describe The Blade's mood that night. Pissed off was even too flattering of a description. Hector was on an entirely different scale. In fact, Hector was so hot that Alvin Washington, the jobber that I'd cleverly booked him in a dark match against, nearly dumped the entire plan in fear that Hector's anger would turn fatal. Yes, the kid literally thought that Flair was going to murder him in that ring. The plan I speak of, you ask? Simple. Hector adored my father, but hated me with every ounce of blood in his body. I'm not exaggerating when I say the guy more than likely would have murdered me if given the opportunity. Once I took over the reigns of ACWP, I made it my sole duty to capitalize on that hatred. I booked Hector and myself in a sort of Austin-McMahon type ordeal, but I refused to let it remain strictly In character. Everyone knew that Hector hated my guts and therefore I attempted to piss him off so much to the point that he would literally shoot on me during interviews and stiff me whenever we engaged in physical contact. I wanted people to see this thing as real. I wanted to bring that 'Loose-Cannon' feel back to professional wrestling, if you know what I mean. Booking this 'legend' in a dark match was my first step in that operation. Booking him to lose said dark match was my second, or so he thought. I wasn't stupid. I wasn't going to really kill the moral of a legend by allowing him to lose to a jobber. He didn't know that, however. What was really set to happen was for Alvin to take an injury bump half way into the match thus causing him to have to call the match in the ring in favor of Hector. Simple, really. Therefore, the introduction to the night’s show was made and the two went out there and began their battle. The match started off with Hector Fernandez locking Washington up in a collar and elbow tie up. Following that was a side headlock which only resulted in Hector being pushed off into the ropes. On the rebound, Alvin took Hector down with a stiff shoulder block but Fernandez immediately popped right back up and responded with a round of chops that really worked the crowd. From there on out, it was all Hector as he chopped, slapped, suplexed, and headlocked the kid all across the ring. At one point I began to wonder if the match was originally planned to resemble such a ‘squash’ or if Hector had literally taken over. About four minutes into the match, Alvin found himself lifted into the air in suplex position. With a tad bit more eagerness than usual, Hector dropped Alvin with the suplex. Suddenly, Alvin clutched his ankle. Something was wrong. Alvin wasn’t set to take the injury bump until at least another five minutes. Figuring that he simply wanted to get the match over, I brushed it off. But as Hector reached down to pick him up, the kid literally found himself unable to stand. He was legitimately injured. Hector had brought the suplex down so suddenly that Alvin was rendered unable to straighten up and therefore landed on his ankle under the weight of his entire body. After realizing that Alvin could not stand, Hector locked in his trademark figure four leglock. Immediately, the referee called for the bell and awarded Hector the match as EMT’s rushed down to ringside. As if he had little regret for what he’d just caused, Hector simply walked away from the scene. Actually, stormed would probably be the more fitting term. Oh boy…were things about to erupt. As Alvin hopped backstage at the aid of the EMT’s, he ran into a complaining Hector Gonzalez who was seemingly yelling at one of the booking team members concerning the match he’d just taken competed in. I hadn’t arrived on the scene at this point, so I’m not exactly sure who he was yelling at nor what he was yelling about. When I did arrive, however, things had already begun to escalate. “YOU CLUMSY PIECE OF GARBAGE! YOU COULD HAVE ENDED MY CAREER OUT THERE!” “WHO ARE YOU YELLING, AT CHICO? YOU’D BETTER BE GLAD I DIDN’T END YOUR CAREER OUT THERE. YOU DON’T HAVE ANY BUSINESS IN THE RING WITH A LEGEND LIKE ME ANYWAY AND I JUST PROVED THAT!” “THE ONLY THING YOU PROVED WAS HOW MUCH OF A SELFISH PRICK YOU ARE. SIT YOUR OLD ASS DOWN AND LET SOME NEW BLOOD TAKE OVER!” “I GOT YOUR OLD ASS, PUNK!” *SMACK!* …Hector punched him. From that point on, it was a full fledged brawl. EMT’s quickly stepped in order to save Alvin from furthering the injury on his ankle, but the two or three of them were simply not enough. For a one legged guy, I must say Alvin held his own. One of his wild punches actually caused a cut to arise on the forehead of Hector thus drawing blood. After about an estimated twenty seconds of all out brawling, enough wrestlers and officials had finally assembled in order to break up the fight. All the while being carried down the hallway, Hector could be heard cursing and screaming at the top of his lungs. Alvin, on the other hand, was simply trying to get at Fernandez, but the combination of an injured ankle and the wrestlers holding him back would prevent that. Thankfully, my father wasn’t in attendance. He would have lost it. The fight, while controversial, didn’t concern me as much as the aftermath of it. After fifteen minutes of reconciling with Alvin, I shifted over into the locker room of Hector Fernandez who, alongside several officials, stood screaming at several executives and members of the booking staff. He was talking at such a random pace that I could not catch everything he said, but the basis of it was: “I’M NOT GOING TO SIT HERE AND LET THAT PIECE OF TRASH JOB ME ALL ACROSS THIS FED! I’M OUT OF THIS SINKING SHIP! WHEN YOU IDIOTS DECIDE TO WISEN UP AND DUMP THAT PIECE OF TRASH OLSIG, THEN YOU CAN GIVE ME A CALL!” And with officials on his tail attempting to reason with him, Hector stormed out of the building. I watched as one of those ‘consequences’ I feared would occur through this whole ordeal unfolded before my very eyes. Work or no work, the guys weren’t going to be very happy about losing a star such as Fernandez. Immediately, I called up my Assistant, Danny Taylor. “Hello?” “Danny…we have a problem.” “What’s going on?” “It’s Hernandez. I don’t have time to describe it in full, but during his match with Alvin, he suplexed Alvin and completely blew out the kid’s ankle. The match ended about five minutes earlier than it should have and a fight erupted between the two backstage.” “Wait, a fight? You mean yelling and screaming, right?” “Yeah…after the fists were thrown.” “Fists!?” “Yes, fists…and now Hector has stormed out the building and vowed not to return until we get rid of me.” “You’ve got to be kidding me.” “You’ve got to think of something. I can't allow this thing to unfold and get out of hand.” “…” “Taylor? What are we going to do?” “Okay, Okay. Remember how I told you that we'd ultimately have to sway this thing towards Hernandez receiving a World Title shot sometime down the road?” “Yes.” “Well, scrap that. Tell them to tell Flair that he’s going to fight for, and win, the World Heavyweight title next week.” I paused for a moment. “…excuse me?” “Please don’t make me repeat myself.” Once more, I paused. “Taylor...I'm not so sure about this.” “It's the only way we'll be able to get Hector back.” Before I could hang up the phone good, I was approached by some random reporter. How he’d gotten back here is beyond my guess. “Excuse me Mister Olsig, do you have any information on the Fernandez situation?” Before I could tell the jerk to back off, I thought about the plan for a moment. I wanted this work to be as real as possible. If word got out that Hector walked out, it’d be all across the dirtsheets. It’d be the weekend’s biggest story and even more importantly, it’d add fuel to the fire that I was trying to build. I mean, what harm could it do? The next show was already sold out anyway. With that in mind, I told the reporter everything. My plan succeeded. That angle alone brought in more profits than any other in the history of the AWCP. The board eventually found out that it was a work, however, and planned to engage in a vote that would force me to step down as head of the company. I resigned a week before in order to counter the attack. --- Your story intrigues me. Think you still have the wit to pull such a thing off again? Convincing the world that the Tag Team Titles are more prestigious than the Universal Title will prove effortless, my friend. And if it doesn't. It will. But what if it doesn't. It will. It just mi-... It...will. This time next week, the world will be groveling at our feet all the while the Universal Championship and the Universal Champion will be but a forgotten memory. It begins this week as we down another tag team alongside that Silver fellow. He might not approve of your little promo from last week. Who cares. It was true. I exposed him as the fraud he is. This week, we do it again as we carry him through victory. And after that? Lindsay Troy. The only way to prove that our Tag Belts are superior to her Universal Belt is to prove that we are superior to her. Sound like a plan? Sounds like a plan.
