Failed Attempts

1 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 1st From the back of the room comes a large thud. A few solemn footsteps resonate through the dark room. No lights. Nothing visible in the slightest respect yet... something moves. "Have you heard the story of a man named Hank Cobb?" With the flick of a match, the bright, beard of Veljumin Rippa flares into the picture and then out. Gentle flames show his most grim features. His face fills with a wide smile. "It is one that is very familiar." Rippa’s thick Swedish accent echoes off the acoustic Oak. "The man we all grow to love; the blue-collar Carolina boy who came from such a blessed home..." Rippa snarls. "Only to find himself smack-dab in-the-middle of a war." "You see, the only thing Hank Cobb has done is insert himself into a warzone. A warzone in which he will lose and lose violently. Hank has come across the Shark’s Tooth a few times, sure. Has he ever walked away the bigger, better man? In the win loss column, sure. In the 'I-am-not-injured' column? Hardly." Rippa adjust his presence in the dim, dim candle light. "Each time Wicked Ways puts their stamp on PRIME’s talent, we’ve left too many scars and bruises to be ignored anymore. For Claude Christ’s Sake – we put Tyler Rayne through a window. The kind you can see through but not punch out. That type of window. We've put down DUI, the Union, and even some of the major pawns got their licks during the Halo." He grins. "But then there’s this... soap opera. I'm talking about the DUI, Wicked Ways saga that continues on - week after week. Last week you wanted us to step up to the plate..." He shakes his head slightly. "And here we are...you and me, Hankford Cobbins. This time; Cobb versus Rippa won't just be a match. They're going to call it a slaughter in the lawsuit. The same way I came into PRIME – stomping that has-been drunk partner of yours – I will come out of ReVolution 189." "The title match last month was our's-to-lose until your last-ditch effort to embarrass yourselves worked flawlessly. Since then you’ve just been on a downward spiral; both of you have. While Duke runs off, he lives the kids to defend the fort? Ha! Imbecile." Veljumin paces around the room for a bit. He raises a finger. "But I’m willing to put you on your head once again to impress the fans. Why?" Rippa shrugs. "Why not? That’s what I say. I say we go out there and give it our all – my best and your worst – what do we have to lose? I only have things to gain, like wins and money and fans; and you, Mr. Cobb, have already done enough damage to yourself – nobody cares how you look!" He laughs and snickers. "So why-" The lights burst on. The Swede crumbles into a ball, clutching his eyes. Turning backward, he spots a fiery little man staggering into the room. He removes the burning object from his mouth. "Ripps! What da feck you doin’ in the dahk?!" Scraps runs to his partner and clutches him by the collar. "I just tried some crazy shit, man. I mean like – CRRRRAAAAAA..." Scraps stands still for a long pause while Rippa wonders what happened to his lighting. "ZEE! I was down with PJ and Sam, right?" Scraps begins to shake frantically. "And then we were all, HEY! Let’s do this pill my boy gave me." Rippa stares at Scraps who seems like he did exactly that – took some pill. "I was all, sure sure!" Rippa groans. "I can do anything you guys can! PJ says, here's some Hydro. Except it wasn't hyrdo; no, no, no! That was like... WHAM!" Another groan. "I am fuckin TRIPPIN BALLS, dude. Like, ROLLIN-TRIPPIN-TWEAKIN BALL-ZEEZ!" "Dude..." Scraps stops moving and stares at the walls. His eyes begins to grow wide and he gets a rather telling smile on his face. "When did the walls start moving?" Rippa looks to the camera. "See you soon, Cobb." Veljumin grips his partner under the arm and begins to walk out with him. Scraps turns around in curiosity. "Hank Cobb- is here!? No way! We hate that guy. But Duke would be cool to party with...in this moving room, no doubt!"
