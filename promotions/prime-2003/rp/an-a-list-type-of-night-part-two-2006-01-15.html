An A-List Type Of Night: Part Two

15 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/15/06 I can't believe I dragged Tsonda here. And now I can't believe that motherfucker just passed out on some guy's shoes. Before I stoop to pick him up, let's write in a flashback here and turn this bitch into a prequel. About six days ago I got some great news. Reggie called me while I was shooting a promo for Spike TV. The new ReVolution card had been released, and I didn't have to wrestle a walking press nightmare. The absence of the former tag champions, Teh Greatness - easily one of the top seven worst team names I've ever heard - had been extended to 'indefinite,' and the upcoming show would pit us against Skylar Green and Shawn Stewart instead. Don't get me wrong - I don't know or care how those two are in the ring. That's why I pay Reggie for the other six days of the week. My problem was in the possibility of locking up with Scott Matthews, who you may have seen on 20/20 or Nightline or one of those other shows that people watch to pretend they're informed about the world. 'Rampage' may as well have been nicknamed 'The Guy Who Set Wrestling Back 50 Years.' If some casual fan watched ANY of the media coverage he'd been getting in the second half of '05, they'd think we still ran shows in circus tents. If I had just taken time off to deal with my 'violence' issues, then I didn't need to be lined up across the ring with a guy whose issues would set my issues on fire, rip them in half, toss them in a wood chipper and run over the remains with a rusty steamroller. And that's saying nothing of his partner, who's fast becoming the next Andy Dick around these places. Which, sadly, puts him right about at my level. Anyway, I mention that only because the relief lasted 2.3 minutes. Before I was even done with Reggie, Allan, my agent, called me on the other cell. His news was not so bright. The new scriptwriter for Double Down, the guy we hired to CLEAN UP the previous draft, was out. Some 'insider' website - a Hollywood smark - reported that he'd been picked up in San Fran for crack possession. Normally a Dot Com reports means shit to us, but the feeling around H-town when the guy signed on was that he took the job to pay for crack. Oh blah dee, oh blah dah. Allan had already started to put out feelers for an alternative project. We'd passed up a lot of deals waiting for the sequel to materialize. Now I needed to give up my dream of starting a franchise and just look for some work. If we weren't careful, 'actor' and 'wrestler' were going to switch places on either side of the backslash. That's a line we would never be able to return from. In other words, my career had just been fucked up, so I needed to go out and follow suit. So our newest ally, or as I like to call him, our newest not-our-enemy-anymore-guy, the Face-Eater, left a call with Vicki, my manager, about a party. At least that's what she made out under the heavy breathing and...other noises. Something Danza was throwing. I blew it off initially - word came around during a 'rough time' for me and Tony. But he was a pal of Facey, and I was still on thin ice with the press. They were waiting for a chance to put me back on the cover and deep six my ever-increasing popularity among the 18-to-34 demographic. So I told her to tell him to tell him that we'll be there. And if Facey and I are supposed to be there, I need to bring Chandler, too. He's not the most...socially adjusted of people. I'd say he's a Southern drawl and a pair of round eyes away from having 'Manning' as a last name. BUT, if I get him liquored up, I thought, then he'll be a little more friendly. Of course, at the time, I was expecting a 'take his shirt off in the middle of a party and eventually get a handjob from some MTV girl' kind of friendly. What I got was the 'throw up at the feet of that guy from 24' friendly. Which is actually not friendly at all. 'I'b surry,' he drools as he tries to get back to his feet. As I approach the scene, the guy he threw up on is backing away, repulsed by the whole thing. 'This is disgusting!' he repeats out loud, apparently so horrified that he won't just WALK THE FUCK AWAY. 'Please. You get thrown up on by a bonafide celeb every day? Those shoes will be on eBay tomorrow afternoon and you know it.' 'Excuse me?' he demands, incredulous. 'You think I'm just some ordinary guy here to schmooze?' 'If you're not, then you did some damn good research to prepare for the role, 'cause you look like a fucking putz.' I sit Chandler down at the barstool. His head falls back a little and he winks at the skank at the counter. At 'putz,' though, he snapped back forward. 'Danny,' he slurs, making sure to repeat it two more times in case I didn't hear him, 'thisiTobBell.' 'Huh?' 'Tobm Bell.' 'The running back from the Broncos?' 'The star of SAW!' the dude exclaims before turning on his heel and heading for the door. 'You'll be hearing from my people!' 'Only if I choose to listen,' I grunt as the door shuts behind him. Back at the bar, Chandler is spitting game at the keep. Heavy on the spitting, light on the game. I should've stuck around at that club a little bit longer, and let 'Natasha' take care of him. The boy's not gay, he's just a metrosexual who's not getting laid. Probably would've lightened up a bit, and then we would have never leapfrogged the cutoff point to land where we are now. Besides, the pair of tits working on me was a leading candidate for Sunday. She wasn't as hot as Thursday, but hell, it's the weekend, I can cut loose. 'I'ma be muuvie sturr tooo!' he proclaims to the help. I tense up a little and try to signal the girl, try to cut the conversation off. Apparently she doesn't recognize the head-shake-and-throat-slash gesture as a universal no. I guess in whorespeak it's 'you can't refuse because I have a knife.' Whatever the reason, she leans in and offers an encouraging 'Oh yeah?' to my company. He nods. 'Danny gonna putmeinone.' Well, shit. I guess we need to take another step back and talk about another reason Chandler's here. You probably think that we just teamed up out of the blue, that we're like any other ordinary people with a like mind who join forces. But we're not ordinary people. We're famous people. You don't have a like mind with another famous person because everyone's fucked up and primarily self-motivated, to say the least. Chandler didn't just hop on Team Ferguson. We made a deal. If he sticks with me, I'll get him back into the celeb lifestyle. He'd been hit hard when that dump in Jersey went under, and he needed an in. I was an in. The deal was simple: his support and partnership in exchange for movie roles. Turn him from a model slash wrestler into a model slash actor slash wrestler. He was to be the Clint to my Ron, or the four guys that Adam Sandler puts in all his flicks to my Adam Sandler. Conveniently enough, all four of those guys are schmoozing with Chan at the bar right now while I pace the room, thinking up contingency plan. Double Down was going to be Chandler's silver screen debut. For a brief moment, I consider the possibility that my 'write a good-looking Asian guy into the script' note to the writer that pushed him over the edge. So with Double Down on the backburner and no replacement in sight, I'm not holding up my end of the bargain. I needed to break it to him easily, so he'd decide to stay on board while we looked for another movie. So that's a problem...but not as big of a problem as Chan making eyes at this bartender. Christ, going home with a service worker is like going to a baseball game and watching it all on the TV of your skybox. I guess I don't expect most of you to get that analogy. Regardless, I can't let that happen. 'Cut him off,' I tell her curtly. She gives me a half smile and nods. 'At least until he's speaking full sentences again. Then give him four more of what you gave him in the first place.' She shoots me a concerned look. What the fuck does she think this is, a Freddie Prinze, Jr. movie? No ugly ducklings love stories here, sweetheart. 'I'll make sure he gets home. Thanks.' With a pat on the bar, I head out for another part of the room. We may as well go all in on this one - get him to black out and forget what happened when he wakes up. If he flips out about the movie thing later, I'll just say I mentioned it to him tonight. He'll be too embarassed to pursue the issue. Now, the matter at hand is to give the boy a good morning when he eventually does wake up. There's a group huddling by the couch. I pick up my beer cup on the way over and sit down like I had been there from the start. '...and then I say, 'Fuck you, buddy, I don't need a license!'' Everybody laughs. So do I. It's the guy that plays Johnny Drama telling the story, but I couldn't give two shits about him. It's his company I'm interested in. So let's fast forward to the part where we get introduced. 'This is Taylor and this is Alex. They're from Laguna Beach, the MTV show.' It's funny. You actually DO hear that cash register sound when it's appropriate. I look them both up and down. The blonde one is hotter. I should give her to C, but he's too plastered to know the difference. Besides, I still need a Sunday. Hell, if I play this right, I might get two Sundays...only if I can convince the brunette to keep Little Chan out of her major orifices. That's just gross. After some chatting, I take the girls with me. It's ok, the Johnny Drama guy wasn't getting them anyway - they were just waiting to be picked up by someone better (isn't every girl?). Walk them both over to Chandler. He's still talking to that fucking bartender. I need someone to set the pick. Someone with no tact...someone who would like nothing better than to cause misery... 'WHO'S THE FUCKING PARTY BOSS, BITCHES?!?' The Illustrious Face-Eater, everyone. 'Fergie! You shitheads made it! Where's Danza?' 'I haven't seen him all night.' I hadn't. One of the few truths I've dropped this evening. 'You want to help me out with something?' A few minutes later and Facey's conducting a taste test and keeping the bartender busy. She rolls her eyes and looks over at Chandler. He's not paying her a bit of attention, though, and that's probably because of the barely-legal brunette massaging his knee. Couple of hours and the night will be a success after all. I've gotta shake a few hands before I call it a night. Make sure people know that I need a new project without actually saying I need a new project. Now that Facey's here, I don't have to stick around for the Danza appearance. He's six hours late as is, and it's his fucking party. I give the girls a signal. The one standing between C's legs leans in even further, which seems impossible without them physically conjoining. She whispers something in his ear, and he nods. Time to go. I produce a pair of keys to two adjoining rooms, elsewhere in the hotel. Chandler leaves without even looking at that bartender again, despite the puppy dog shit she's pulling as we leave. Sure, both of our acting careers have been pulled out from under us, and sure, we've got our first main event match coming up in the next few days. Right now it's looking like a pretty good night. Of course, this is just another Sunday...if you're on the List.
