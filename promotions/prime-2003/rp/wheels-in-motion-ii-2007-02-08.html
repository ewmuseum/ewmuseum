Wheels In Motion(ii)

8 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8th February 2007 “Sir…” the words stammered horribly from the lips of Armando De La Juego, they hinted at that element of fear that engulfs you when meeting someone in a position of power. He began once more, “Sir… I’m afraid we have a situation.” Reclining deep into his leather chair Carlos Lauaki looked every bit the archetypal general: except he held a power so commanding not even the Fijian government could touch him. For his was the power of fear and respect, the kind that Tony Montana had and lost, the kind every Goodfella dreamt of – except this was no dream, it was as tangible as the lines of cocaine that funded his empire, as tangible as the bodies he’d disposed of, as tangible as the very fear in this room. Lauaki did not move and rested his chin on one hand, “What seems to be the problem Arman? If you tell me it’s something to do with the cooking I swear before almighty God I will castrate you and use your balls as paper weights… not that they’d hold down much paper mind.” “It’s… it’s…” “For fucks sake Arman, spit it out.” “Lisa…” Right there and then the most powerful man walking the green pastures of Fiji was reduced to tatters. His face construed everything a father would go through in the same situation; he was a cold heartless bastard, but when it’s family concerned every person is the same. “Wha… what about her? Where is she? Is she okay?” “She… she…” Lauaki grew agitated, leaping from his seat and collaring Armando… “You’ll tell me what you know, and you’ll tell me now… where is my daughter?!” “I don’t know how to put this but she’s… she’s gone Carlos. No one’s seen her… no one knows where she went. She was meant to go shopping this morning, but we checked in with her girl friends and not one of them has seen her for weeks…” Lauaki’s face dropped a thousand storeys, there wasn’t much he’d cared about in this world, well… people wise. “Are you fucking sure no one’s seen her? Fucking positive?” “Positive Carlos… absolutely certain.” “Fuck…” ---- She felt so small… so innocent… so totally involved when wrapped up in his arms. She’d longed for this day for what seemed like an eternity. It was a risk, perhaps one of the biggest risks she’d ever taken, but the heart wants what the heart wants… the plane was only small but well equipped to take them the full distance. No one would suspect a thing – especially not Carlos. It was too far gone now though to actually care about consequences, he was certified dead in prison, he is certified dead for making contact, and if possible he’d die a thousand deaths if Lauaki discovered his deepest fears had been realised. Lisa looked up into the eyes of Afeaki and he gave her that same warm look back that he’d given her since the day they met and right there she knew things would work out. It was a side of him she’d always seen, a side that the world had though could not possibly exist, but a side that right now under the starry skies was more salient than ever before. He’d never experienced true love. His parents died when he was still a cub. Love for GA was an all or nothing, paternal or true love… there were no in betweens. ---- It's been a while since I've written anything about anything that my mind feels as tangled as the drain of my bath tub, confused by all the shed strayed hairs that have no problem leaving my scalp. For a while now, I had been existing in a timeless time where clocks have no hands and every moment fell into my hands to play with. It's Fiji and I'm sitting on flight number two, somewhat drowsed by substance. Across from me are two girls. Dressed like 1997, conversing in vintage language that belongs in the time of old Arabic movies. “Abyad weswed” Confusion occurs. It is the Egyptian dialect but so unalike the reality that it is today. It was as if they had never left the flight two to nowhere. Three days of a blurred joy ride, elevating from one world to the next. Looking at myself objectively. In the third person. and I'm back to the O'clocks. My body recovering from severe abuse. Verbal I think. Where twelve personalities lived in the same home, beneath the veins and bloodstream, debating on whether which one should be allowed to stay in the apartment above. The mind. In the end. When it's over. But then again we all know it's temporary... I come about to Fiji. Which for some reason doesn't seem to have its initial appeal. The original charm has left the house of the Lion. Why did I care so much? I couldn’t tell you, I’m not that articulate. You’d see soon enough… So very soon. Yet you ask her why why did you die... my Fiji...... why?
