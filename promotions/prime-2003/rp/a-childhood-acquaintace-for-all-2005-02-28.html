A childhood acquaintace for all!

28 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28/05 The following one liner was taken from Joey Troy, off AIM. doo-doo-dooo-doo, Inspector Gadget... doo-doo-dooo DOoooo, doo doo The grey hat of Inspector Gadget is shown walking down the street; he is also decked out in his other nifty gadgets he has incorporated into his attire. He is all alone, no Rolo, no Troy just Inspector Gadget walking down the street. The jacket is fitting a little tight today, actually the more and more you look at it the man resembles nothing of Matthew Broderick, and when you actually think about it why is this Inspector Gadget even walking down the streets? All these are thoughts that are currently being pondered by the most intelligent people, you know those guys who write the fortune cookies and who also wrote the Chinese proverbs? Yeah, them they are pondering it. Getting strange looks from everyone who is walking down the street this Inspector Gadget imposter hand starts to ring. After getting all flustered the man figures out to answer it. 'Hello?' 'Peter Vetra, where are you? You sound like you are under water.' 'Hey man, don't say my name some one might be listening, I'm currently walking the streets of down town Arkansas. Do you have everything lined up?' Peter asks quickly as he turns his head around nervously wondering if anyone actually heard his name being released into public. 'Yeah, man things are set to go and you will make him feel your mark after this ReVolution!' The man whose voice is unfamiliar continues to speak but the imposter Inspector Gadget, Peter Vetra or should we just call him Inspector Vetra. Finally when the man stops speaking Inspector Vetra is able to get a couple of words in. 'Man I really appreciate it, but I have to go, what if the Chief calls me and needs someone to go solve a crime? This is a business phone you know!' 'Sorry, I guess I will just catch you later Inspector Vetra.' The man lets out a chuckle.” I guess you are not afraid of being caught as an imposter again, I mean you were already caught once, Mr. Illuminati!' With that the dial tone is heard, and Peter Vetra puts down his hand trying to hang the phone up. 'Well, I got the suit; I got the gig I mind as well try out the equipment. GO GO GADGET ROLLER SKATES!' Peter Vetra utters the words and the roller skates descend from the bottom of his shoes and he starts to skate his way down the street. 'WoW these things actually work; I guess I am not going to regret renting this suit!' Peter continues to skate his way through the small crowd that continues to grow as they watch their childhood television cartoon hero in real life. But all of a sudden a crack is seen in the cement sidewalk and the klutz hits the crack head on and is sent spinning into the local trash cans making a ruckus. Little birdies appear around his head, as he tries to shake some sense into himself. Just as he is starting to see straight again his hand starts to ring again. 'Man I am going to have to get used to this hand/phone thing!' 'Hello?' 'Inspector Vetra? Are you there?' A deep familiar voice is heard on the other end a voice we have grown to love with this cartoon series. 'Ahh yes, Chief. What can I do for you?' 'Vetra, we have a serious case, here Claw has just gained control over Karina Wolfenden. You need to go and get her! As you know this message will self destruct in 5 seconds.' Inspect Vetra crumbles put his hand phone away and crumbles the piece of paper that had magically appeared for him to read off of. He throws the paper into the garbage cans and helps himself up and begins to skate away from the crash scene starting to look for Karina. 4... 3... 2... 1... BOOM!?!!? A cloud of smoke engulfs the area that Inspector Vetra was just in, and magically just like the cartoon the Chief happened to be in that exact spot and the cloud explodes on him. ------------------------SCENE CHANGE------------------------ The silver claw is seen stroking the usual cat, than face not shown like the numerous times in the past. 'I will get you Inspector Vetra, I already have your little friend Karina, I will get you!!' ------------------------SCENE CHANGE------------------------ Inspector Vetra is seen back at his house where he is accompanied by his niece Rolo and her little mutt Troy. 'Uncle Vetra, I want to go with you. You never let me go.' Rolo tries to get her uncle to allow her to join on the little escapade he is about go on. But as usual he is stern and is not hearing it. 'Rolo I think it would be best if I went on this alone, you and Troy can stay here.” Whispering under her breath Penny states, “That’s what you think.” “GO GO GADGET CAR!” Peter calls for his car to come and out from the garage it appears. He gets inside the car and starts to rev the engine a little bit. “Penny you behave, I will see you in a little while I have to go save a “friend”” “OK, Uncle Gadget be safe.” Penny shows her concern but as he is looking she and Troy hop in the trunk of the car. Inspector Vetra plays with a few buttons on his screen to try and find out where they are holding the hostage Karina Wolfenden at. The location is then disclosed to him through the monitor but all he sees is the directions and a recent picture. “The abandon boat yard, I am on my way!” ------------------------SCENE CHANGE------------------------ We now switch to an abandon boat yard; we see a hanging Karina Wolfenden tied to a beam by her hands. Don’t these kidnappers know that she is deadly with her feet? Needless to say she is spinning and trying to kick all of the kidnappers but to avail she just gets tired. The kidnappers are a bunch of red haired emo/ punk chicks. They are out to get Karina for her well known wrestling ability. No wonder they can get her, there are about 4 of them and only one of her. It doesn’t take a rocket scientist to figure out that the odds are against her. After they have watched her tire out they decide to start their subtle attacks on her. They start whipping her and they seem to be enjoying every bit of it, they are fetish freaks what can I say? ------------------------SCENE CHANGE------------------------ The Inspector mobile slides into the abandon boat yard no more then ten minutes later. Inspector Vetra is the first to get out still unaware that Penny and Troy are inside his trunk. He gets out of the car and decides he needs an aerial view of the area. “Go Go Gadget Copter!” With the end being uttered two handles come out and reaches down for Peter Vetra to grab a hold of and they are followed by the propellers. He pushes on the buttons and he is propelled up into the sky and starts to hover over the building. Troy and Rolo quickly sneak out from the trunk as he is doing this and decide they need to take the ground attack and watch out for Uncle Gadget we know he is unable to do this on his own. As they sneak around the back. As they sneak around the back Inspector Vetra is flying overhead and sees that there is an opening in the middle of the building. He starts to descend into the building as he is noticed by the members of the kidnappers. Just as this is happening Troy and Rolo are finding their way into place. “Troy go and find a way to save Uncle Vetra, I am going to call the Chief and get him here!” Rolo makes the phone call to the Chief and discloses the location and the Chief says they are on their way. Inspector Vetra continues to descend and once of the punk red head get in the way he lands on her and accidentally cuts off her head. Inspector Vetra has only been fighting crime for a couple of hours and has already committed one of his own! The blood is spewing everywhere and he is not quick to get to his feet. As Inspector Vetra is trying to regain his sense Troy has found a way to get the rest of them, you hear a swoosh noise as numerous sand bags are seen flying down from the ceiling. With them each hitting another emo punk kidnapper they are sent to the ground and Troy has just helped Uncle Gadget stop the criminals. It seems like it was just in time as the sounds of cop sirens are heard and they enter the building through the back entrance. The first one out of his car is the Chief. By this time Troy and Rolo are already back hiding in the car knowing they have done their job. “Great Job Inspector Vetra, you kidnapped the Emo/Punk kidnappers we have been trying to catch them for months for many silly pity crimes. But you did it!” The Chief is all congratulating knowing Rolo and Troy had something to do with it as always but not wanting Gadget to know. “Alright guys let her down and take her down to the station for questioning and lock the others up!” The chief is used to barking orders and this time is no different. But he is interrupted by Inspector Vetra, “Chief if you don’t mind I would like to take the victim back myself.” “No problem at all Gadget fine work as always.” The crew continues to clean up the scene and Karina Wolfenden is let down from the rafter, she is helped into Gadget’s car and thy drive away. ------------------------SCENE CHANGE------------------------ “BLAST YOU GADGET! YOU FOILED MY PLANS AGAIN!” The big claw we saw earlier hits the desk over and over again scaring his cat away from the desk and onto his shoulder. “I will get you one of these days Gadget!” ------------------------SCENE CHANGE------------------------ We are now back at Gadget’s house he seems to be making Karina comfortable in his bedroom a scary thought all together. He offers her some wine and it seems like he is trying to work his magic. She starts to drink the wine but before she can finish the words, “ GO Go Gadget Dick are uttered.” The face upon Karina is light up and her eye balls almost jump right out of their sockets. The scene fades away as the rest is best left to imagination…. BUZZZZZ…………….BUZZZZZZZZZZZZ…. The annoying sounds of an alarm clock are heard. A hand turns and slaps the button to shut it off. Peter Vetra uncovers his head from under the sheets and starts to take the sleepys out of his eyes. “Man what a dream I had last night! I don’t know how I am going to be able to step into the ring with her now!” Peter snickers and rolls back over and try’s to fall back asleep knowing he should get up and study film. I would like to thank the influences from Joey Troy and Angelo Deville on some parts they know them, and I want Deville to mark out like he said he would! If you do not know who Inspector Gadget it is please by the cartoon!
