Go South Young Man (match RP)

8 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/8/05 It’s the pits of hell and we know this because Bill Mahr is in the red house, blacks are all police officers, and whites are working the crack houses when not being tormented by Satan. That and its hot as hell there! Hoyt is walking down the street continually being jabbed in the ass by a pitchfork by an angry Hitler yelling in Germen. Hoyt Williams: Take me to Satan! Hitler: Haben Sie noch ein anderes? Hoyt Williams: Stop poking me. Hitler: Haben Sie noch ein anderes? Hitler keeps jabbing Hoyt causing him to bleed and become weak. Hoyt Williams: Take me to your leader. Hitler points to a hippie commune just over the tracks. Hoyt Williams: I dig that stash man! Hitler: Gute Reise! Hoyt walks over to where all the hippies are playing bag folk songs. The music sounds like fingernails on a chalk board. George McGovern greets Hoyt at the entrance. George McGovern: Hoyt we’ve been expecting you. Hoyt Williams: George McGovern! You’re not dead? George McGovern: Yes but my career is. Now lets take you to Satan himself. Hoyt Williams: It’s hot in here. George McGovern: You should stay tonight we are having a Beatles reunion concert! Hoyt Williams: Sorry I’m an Elvis man. George McGovern: Oh yea well he’s upstairs. The two men walk to Satan’s office and knock on the door. Hitler keeps jabbing Hoyt driving him crazy like McGovern whistles “Blown’ In The Wind” the door opens slowly. Hoyt Williams: This should be interesting. The door swings and their he stands the evilest man in the world. The most disgusting figure one can imagine. So gross his sight can make the average man go blind. Hoyt Williams: Michael Moore? Satan: Go south young man, haven’t you been told? Hell is full of whiskey, women, and gold. Hoyt Williams: I should have known that you were Michael Moore. Satan: Don’t be silly this is one of my many forms although I do use this one on earth, yes. Hoyt Williams: This is hell? Satan: Hell is different for everyone. Depending what your fears and loathing are. Hoyt Williams: My promo feeling like it’s something Clyde came up with is hell enough for me. Lets get down to brass tacks shall we. Satan: So be it. Would you like some tea? Tony Rolo: FUCK YOU COCKSUCK FAG!!! I STIRRED YOUR TEA WITH MY COCK. The image of Tony Rolo walks up with a glass of cool ice tea. Hoyt Williams: No thank you. Tony Rolo: Only gays turn down drinks you motherfucker. Satan: Anyways. What is it you want. Hoyt Williams: I want to look you in the eyes and ask you to pull back your frontal assault make the US secular. In return I will give you China. Satan: Are you kidding me? I already have China. I’m taking GOD’s USA and there is nothing you can do to stop me. Hoyt Williams: I’m done trying to save. Satan: Then perhaps you would like to join me?? Hoyt Williams: Not a chance in Heaven. No, I’ve already done what I came here to do. I’ve recruited some of your top me. Satan: YOU DIDN’T!?! Hoyt Williams: I did big poppa and now the games begin don’t they. Prepare for war hippie. A flash of white covers the screen as it all disappears.
