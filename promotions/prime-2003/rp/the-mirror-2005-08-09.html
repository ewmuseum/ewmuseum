The Mirror

9 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-9-05 08.07.05. New Fucking Jersey. Have you ever woken up in the morning too embarassed to look at yourself in the mirror? Stood in front of the damn thing just staring into the sink and last week's gob of toothpaste that's turned into a teal rock. You hope, for like five or ten seconds, that something about you, something intangible, and possibly unreal, with will change, and you'll be filled with a stark clarity? A calmness, a light, and a peace. And you'll be able to look back into your own eyes again? If not, then you're not Kyle Lamen. And you're not a goddamned coward. Kyle stood in front of his bathroom mirror, staring into the sink. Deville. He absently scratched at an ear, carefully avoiding looking up from his focus point in the bottom of the porcelain sink. Did you REALLY think that you could fly under the radar? That a man of his obsessiveness, his clarity wouldn't notice? Wouldn't pick up the fact that now you're coming into HIS home federation, into HIS ring, and trying to pass yourself off as a professional wrestler again? Fuck me, Kyle. You're older, but you're not any smarter than you were before. It's not a particularly nice bathroom. The third-floor loft above Jack's Music Shop in Red Bank, New Jersey...came with a great view and hardwood floors, but the bathrooms...well, nothing's quite perfect. Fight it. Fight the urge to think about something else. Stand up and be a man, for once in your life. Get to the goddamned point of this. Right, back to Kyle focusing on that glob of toothpaste that looks like it's fossilized in the bottom of the sink. Deville. There're a number of people that you owe apologies to...and Angelo Deville is one of them. To be fair, he's probably the most dangerous of the lot...no. Don't get into that now. Stay focused. You say the things you need to say in your head...then you say them to Deville. That's the way this works. You come back to wrestling, because you need the money, yes. Because you need the outlet for your energies, yes. But primarily, to try and do the things that you were too pathetic to do before. A lot of people talk about how amateur wrestling made them learn about being a man. And deep down, Kyle...you still feel like a child. Because you've neglected to take full responsibility for your actions. Neglected to offer full disclosure to the people who deserve it. To Kyle, the glob in it's position started to look like it was moving. His eyes, still tired from the all-nighter that he pulled with the band, saw the slight lines go hazy... Whatever the physical reasons, the fact is...when you left the business all those years ago, you did it in the wrong way. You took for granted the people that you worked with, you took for granted your management, and you spit on all the people that helped you be the success that you were. Instead of offering thanks, offering apologies at the time, when they would've been received a little better, you disappeared. Went off to England, then China and India, then Japan. You ran away like a scared child. The toothpaste seemed to melt, and Kyle couldn't take it anymore. He just shut his eyes entirely, blocking out the whole of the bathroom, his focus object, everything. STOP MAKING EXCUSES. His eyes flashed open, and the toothpaste still rested in the sink. Unmoving. Do you have any idea how hypocritical you sound to these people? Claiming to be all for the moment. Saying that you've got no regrets? Son, you've got more regrets than times you've changed your hair color over the years. That's what this is supposed to be about. Giving up those regrets. And you can't move past them unless you confront them... The...thing...at the bottom of the sink wavered, mocking him. You just need to suck it up. Admit to Angelo that you're wrong. You were wrong. You ARE wrong. Everything in life is about making mistakes, and then not making them again, but if you continue to avoid the situation, then God only knows how much worse you'll make this all. Kyle had to close his eyes again. Remember? Remember all the times you've ran from failure? Ran from success? Remember how the hobbies, the sidelong distractions, everything manages to distract you...keep you from the things you really want? Do you remember THAT? Kyle remembers. 10.11.04. Tokyo, Japan. The lights were low in PUB, a bar chain in Japan that offered something that kind of looked like an English pub, but managed to at the same time be completely dissimilar. The flat-screen TV, playing Star Wars Episode 1, might've had something to do with it. “Kyle, you will not believe this...” Naota, Kyle's bandmate in American Ninja, and the only other American (albeit Asian-American) in the band sidled next to where Kyle sat, sipping on his beer. Naota kept wearing this goofy-looking tiger-print bathrobe out in public, and his hair was long and shaggy, but whenever anyone tried to tell him that he looked like a hobo, he insisted that he was just trendy ahead of the curve. “Isawa-chan, she says that there's a party down at train station in Shinjuku, and that Kurosaki-sama is there!” Kyle's eyes widened. He looked much the same as he did during his appearances in PRIME, with the exception of his hair being dyed a fire-like red and orange blend. Same guy, different hair color. Story of his life. “Kurosaki-sama? THE Kurosaki-sama?” Kyle's face was lined with skepticism. No way was a pop idol, even one of minor popularity at one of Isawa-chan's station parties. “No, Kyle...the other Kurosaki-sama. The one that you DON'T keep a poster of like a schoolboy. The one that you DON'T keep trying to convince us is musically legitimate, even though...let's face it...she's not.” He just liked the music, he told himself. It had nothing to do with the amazing brown eyes, the charming smile, and the curves that were featured in several magazines and fan publications. You can't even fathom the fact that MAYBE...you were goofy enough to fall in love with a picture, and it made you renounce your musical convictions, if only for her. “So, can we go now?” Naota shook Kyle on the shoulder, anxious to be up and out. God, was that bathrobe ridiculous looking. “No, you know that Isawa-chan is just trying to get attention again. I'm tired, and I don't feel like showing up.” Naota looked at Kyle as if he'd grown a second head out of his neck. “CAN WE JUST GET OUT OF HERE, TA!?” Pushing his beer away from him, and across the table, Kyle stood up and started weaving through the tables towards the door to the pub. Naota followed behind, with a sour look on his face. 08.07.05. New Fucking Jersey. You big fat loser, you fell in love with a picture in a magazine. Kyle can't take the sight of the sink much longer, shutting his eyes and turning away from the mirror entirely. And if that wasn't enough, you were offered a place with her. Didn't even have to work for it, someone just TOLD you that you could meet her...at least be at a party she was at. And what do you do? You go home. Because you don't have the guts to try. You can't fail, you can't do anything. And it's like failing all the time. It's the same thing with Angelo. It's the same thing with any of the others. Kyle covers his face with his hands...and scrunches his hands up, his fingers starting to dig into his skin and leave red marks. If you want to move on with your life, stop living in the past and worrying about the future, you need to be real. You need to put these past things behind you, and you need to stop doing them TODAY. Put together a goddamned list if you have to. Kyle started fishing around in his pockets, and pulled out his copy of the card for the upcoming ReVolution that he copied down from his answering machine a few hours before. It simply read: Olsig -Williams – Deville in big, bold Sharpie letters. You can't live with yourself the way things are now. You'll eat out your own insides, fail at wrestling, music, love and life itself. If ever there was a time for you to make the change, and change your life...now is the time. Do it. Kyle took his finger, and pointed slowly at the word “Olsig”. Forget about him right now. You can't beat him...you can't beat anyone on your own until you get past these demons. And what of it if you did? What if you took that Intense Title from him, even? Would you choke and lose it three days later, like your title in PCW? Would you leave the company after your big win, like in FSW? You're older now, Kyle. Grow the fuck up. His finger travels to “Williams.” Oh great. Little Kyle wants to give Hoyt a spanking. Like you're this great big moral authority. Hoyt's wrong, and you're right, and you'll punish him like the right righteous wrath of God, huh? Terrific. Jason Core's real proud of you, kid. Why don't you do something that'll REALLY make him proud, and grind it out for twenty years, train a hundred people who're working in the business RIGHT NOW, and stop running. The finger stops...hesitates. Hoyt hurt someone you love. Don't beat around the bush. He's a big dumb redneck with a mouth and an evangelism problem...but when you get right down to it, you will NEVER be in the moral right against anyone. Give it up. This is about revenge, and proving you're better than the big, tough bully. But unless you do what needs to be done, you've got no chance to fight him. You'll always be fighting a losing battle. The finger still doesn't move. Suck it up. So what if he's the representative of everything that you hate about yourself. A competitor that you respect FIERCELY. The best in the business, someone you admire...and you dicked him over. I mean, REALLY dicked him over. He did you a huge favor, whether you like to admit it or not, and you did worse than spitting in his face over it. No matter what he's done in the past, he did right by you, and you repaid him with NOTHING. Less than nothing. The finger shook, but hovered. KYLE. TONIGHT IS THE NIGHT WHERE YOU GROW UP. WHERE YOU BECOME BIGGER THAN THE TRIVIAL BULLSHIT THAT YOU'VE LET TURN YOU INTO THIS BIG, WEAK DOUCHEBAG. It moves to Deville, steady at first, but then shaking. You WILL confront him. And then, maybe, you'll think highly enough of yourself to actually step into that ring, against a roster that doesn't respect you, not because of them, but because you don't ACTUALLY deserve it. Kyle crumples the paper in his shaking fists, actually letting beads of sweat fall from his face and dampening the paper. Tonight is the first night of the rest of your life. One resolution, now you must act on it. And truly act, not just avoid him...avoid the people you felt you needed to avoid. You WILL take responsibility for your past, and face all of the consequences. And someday, maybe you'll rise above the empty, hollow, cowardly person you are, and become something better. Something more real, and something that the fans of PRIME, and your co-workers can admire. Kyle turned back to the sink, and back to the stupid gob of toothpaste that started this as he tried to get to sleep an hour and a half ago. “I'm sorry.” The toothpaste isn't the one who's going to forgive you. Kyle looks up, eyes barely making it to the bottom of the mirror, before he speaks. “Angelo, I'm sorry.” Good. Now if you can get together the balls to do it in person, maybe you'll actually have it in you to look at yourself in the mirror again. Or, maybe he'll kill you. We'll find out soon enough, won't we, Kyle. He nods. And Kyle Lamen starts scrubbing his sink clean.
