A New Chapter Begins

17 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 17, 2007 “Sir would you like another drink?” the bartender’s voice asked him for easily the fifth time that evening. Craig just looked up at the man with a look in his soft red eyes that told the man not only yes, but not to ask him again for the rest of the evening. The bartender understood this look and poured Craig yet another drink to which Craig picked it up and drank it within seconds. As Craig placed the now empty glass back down on the wood grain counter the bartender proceeded to pour him another drink before proceeding with some of his other customers. Around Craig, people were lost in their own conversations. Some noticed who he was and were frankly surprised to see him at the bar, especially with the way he was drinking. Yet, Craig could care less. He wanted to dull the pain that he was feeling. In less than twenty-four hours, Craig would be making his debut against the likes of Garbage Bag Johnny and the Illustrious Face- Eater, superstars when compared to Craig. Even though he had made quite a dent in Global Wrestling, this was when Craig would find out if he truly had the stuff to compete against the legends of the wrestling industry. Yet, Craig wasn’t the least bit nervous or scared. Instead, he embraced it much like he had embraced every challenge before him. Tomorrow evening would be no different. Not if he had any say in it at least. Craig’s right hand grasped at the glass, picked it up, and drank the contents down once again. He had no idea what the drink was as he had told the bartender to surprise him with a drink, but whatever it was, it was doing the trick for Craig. His heart continued to beat, but it felt hollow to him. The events of the past few months had seen to that. Now, he had sat in a hotel bar, the Four Seasons nonetheless, wondering what to make of his life. He couldn’t even put words or thoughts to the events of the past few weeks that had changed his outlook on the world around him, and he didn’t attempt to. That’s what the alcohol was for. To make him forget. He had gone through so much to get his memories back and already he wanted them gone. Nobody dared sit next to him. A large, intoxicated man was not one many wanted to associate themselves with. Especially one that happened to wrestle on a weekly basis and could fight with the best of them. No, Craig had been a loner and always had been a loner. That’s why it surprised him when a sultry woman came up behind him and decided to sit down in the stool next to his. Craig just simply looked over at her as she looked back at him and just smiled. She was a knock out, that was for sure as she had long blonde hair that went down to the middle of her back and blue eyes that were the most beautiful that Craig had ever seen. He looked back at the bartender who approached the woman, but she waved him away before he had the chance to ask her what she wanted to drink. “Rough day?” she asked him, obviously oblivious to whom he was. Craig just shook his head as the bartender poured him yet another drink. The woman just watched intently as Craig drank down the drink once again with such reckless abandon. She found the man to be rather attractive and she hated to be alone at night. “I like this bar. It always makes me feel like I’m more than who I really am,” the woman told Craig, hoping to strike up a conversation with him. Craig looked back at her, and noticed the blue dress she was wearing, or wasn’t wearing to be more accurate. The dress showed off some of her rather better features about herself. The woman noticed Craig checking her out and knew that she had him hooked. Craig looked back up into her eyes, and continued to ignore the conversation she was intent on having. This frustrated her slightly as she went over and touched him on the shoulder gracefully. “The name is Marie, what’s yours?” she asked him softly. He just chuckled as he saw where this was going. He looked back at her and gave her a look that sent shivers up and down her spine. “You want no part of me. Trust me,” he uttered to her, but then he caught himself as he held his hand up as if to apologize to her. “Forgive me, I’ve just had a little bit too much to drink tonight and I’ve probably been ruder then I normally am,” he told her as if he was waking up out of his stupor. She smiled as she knew she had connected with him. It was one of the things she did best. “It’s okay,” she had kindly as she looked at Craig, wondering what he was all about. “I understand that sometimes we all have things that we need to wipe from our memories. I hope you don’t mind if I join you,” she said to him to which Craig just responded with a simple nod that made her more comfortable with the situation she had entered into. Craig didn’t know what attracted her to him, but for right now he tried to push that out of his mind. Instead, he wanted to just enjoy the company. “Buy me a drink?” she asked him and Craig just simply looked up at the bartender who understood that look as well. The bartender knew that this was a popular hook-up for the classier clientele that tended to frequent this bar. In the back of his mind, he realized that these two would share a few drinks, and go back to their rooms where they would fuck like crazy. A smile crept across his face for a brief second as he had been the recipient of a few of them himself. It paid to be the bartender sometimes. Sometimes, people were just interested in a little companionship. “What would it be?” the bartender asked the lovely woman who continued to look at Craig. She then looked at the bartender before thinking briefly about what she actually wanted. “Let me get a Cosmopolitan, please,” she said and within a few seconds she had exactly that. She sipped at it and was rather happy with the taste of it. “So, what’s your name?” she asked Craig who had also been handed another drink. He drank slowly, trying to hold off on the question as long as possible. While he was interested in the company, he wasn’t interested in the small talk that went along with it. Yet, he knew that the woman had to know his name at least, and as he finished his drink he looked at her with the same look he had before. “Craig,” he finally responded to her as she smiled back at him for answering one of her questions. Then, he looked up at the bartender who once again refilled his drink, not caring that he was probably drunk out of his mind at this point. As Craig went to pick up his drink this time though, he accidentally knocked it over and the drink went all over the woman’s dress. “Oh God!” she yelled out in shock as the cold alcohol seeped into her clothes and onto her skin. She smiled at Craig who looked at her apologetically. She stood up as a few others looked at the situation starting at the bar. The bartender immediately handed her a few napkins to help with the mess, but she obviously needed a new dress at this point. Craig got up in a hurry to help the woman out as well seeing as how he had spilled the drink on her. “Please, let me help you,” he responded to her, embarrassed at what he had done. She shook her head as she just wanted to leave the hotel and take care of this herself. She was embarrassed as well at what happened and was now rather wet from the proceedings. She started to walk away from Craig, who decided to follow her in an attempt to help her out. “Listen, I’m really sorry,” Craig said to the woman as they exited the bar. She continued to ignore him though, until he grabbed her by the arm and he pulled her into him. “Let’s go back to my room, get you out of these wet clothes, and I’ll buy you a new dress. Fair enough?” he asked of her. She looked around and then nodded her head. “Sure, that’ll be fine,” she said before the duo walked towards the elevator and went up to Craig’s room. Craig let her into his room and she immediately went to the restroom. Craig, on the other hand, proceeded to the chair in his lavish suite at the Four Seasons. Inside of the bathroom, the woman immediately disrobed and took the robe that was hanging up on one of the hooks to wear for the rest of the evening while he took care of the cleaning of her dress. She then exited the bathroom to see Craig sitting down in the chair. For the first time, she noticed just how nice the room was. Easily the nicest hotel room she had ever stepped into. “So, what is it that you do?” she asked as her eyes darted around the room. Craig looked at her with anew interest as he stood up and walked towards him. “Some things,” he responded cryptically. If there was anything that Marie enjoyed, it was a mysterious man. She smiled at him as she stood there in her robe. “My clothes are in the bathroom. You going to call room service so they can take care of them?” she asked of him as he nodded his head that he would indeed call room service. However, he just stared at her as she smiled back once again and started to undo her robe. He knew what she wanted and vice versa. Craig was no stranger to hotel bars and women. Yet, this time it was a little different as she untied her robe and dropped it at his feet. She was certainly beautiful, but he had no interest in her to tell the truth. “While we wait for my clothes to be cleaned, how about we do something to pass the time?” she said to him and he nodded his head. “Sounds like a good idea,” he responded before grabbing her by the neck and tossing her on the bed, his hand still wrapped around her throat. This wasn’t she had in mind though. “Why don’t we start with you telling me where Johnny Black is? Because, I want my daughter back,” he told her, the fear starting to set into her eyes. This wasn’t what she expected at all. Yet, all Craig cared about was getting his daughter back at any cost necessary. To Be Continued
