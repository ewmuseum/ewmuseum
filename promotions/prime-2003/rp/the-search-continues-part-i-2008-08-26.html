The Search Continues... (Part I)

26 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 26th, 2008 The camera opens in the living room of the Delta Upsilon Iota fraternity house. Because from the looks of things, with an enormous tower of empty cases of beer stacked high in one corner and a dozen or more full garbage bags of empty beer bottles, some actually still intact, occupying the other, there is little doubt that humans are not the only species to exist, and probably thrive, within these worn and splintered walls. On the black leather couch in the middle of the room sits both halves of Delta Upsilon Iota. The sultan of chauvinism, Colby Korver, sits comfortably, with an open bottle of Bud Light in one hand…and an unopened bottle of Bud Light in the other, Korver stares vacantly at the television screen across from him, which remains off. An obviously expensive high definition set, it seems quite out of place when juxtaposed with the stack of old Playboy and Sports Illustrated magazines wedged under the stand upon which it sits. Although the engineering behind this makeshift construction seems suspect at best, it is nonetheless doing a fine job of supporting the stand…so far. Next to him rests the gentle giant and native North Carolinian, Hank Cobb, who also holds two bottles of Bud Light. While one is being rapidly emptied, the other is instead being hastily refilled, courtesy of the dip can of Kodiak tightly packed behind Cobb’s lower lip. Finishing off a Bud Light and subsequently shattering its glassy remains against the far wall, Korver quickly reloads courtesy of the nearby beer cooler. Twisting off the top, Korver takes a long swig before shooting a question at his tag team partner. "You know what I think, big guy?" Depositing another two or three ounces in his trusty dip cup, Cobb responds in turn. "What’s that, Korv?" Korver leans forward and places both beer bottles in front of him on the floor, freeing up his arms to assist his voice in painting a vivid picture of his plans. "I think we need to start streamlining this whole manager selection process. Perhaps if we screen potential applicants ahead of time, we can avoid any further interviews like last week." Cobb winces at the mere mention of his disastrous booking of the "overly enthusiastic" Andrea Lafayette for a managerial interview. "Well I think you may be on to somethin’ after all. From the looks of it, we’re fixin’ to have ourselves quite a cast of characters tryin’ to hitch their wagons to the Delta Upsilon Iota gravy train. Seems to me like being mighty particular with those fixin’ to do the hitchin’ is our best plan of attack." "Exactly, so now the question is, what kind of criteria do we want to put in place in order to down our pool of possible selections?" "Don’t know 'bout you, but I was always taught that when you’re fixin’ to figure somethin’ out for yourself, it’s always best to start at the beginning." Korver rolls his eyes and takes another pull of Bud Light. "Do you even have any idea what you just said?" "Well of course I do, jackass, seein’ as how I’m the one who just said it." "And how does what you just said have anything to do with our search for a new manager?" "I didn’t say it did. I was just givin’ you a piece of advice that I always found helpful." Korver throws his head back and takes a quick inventory of the ceiling above. "Well in the future, can you please restrict your southern ramblings to the topic at hand?" Cobb nods his head in approval and enjoys some Bud Light as well. "Reckon I can do that for you, brother. So what do you think the applicant guidelines should be?" Korver clears his throat before beginning what he plans on being a rapid succession of suggestions. "No women, because they belong in the kitchen with the rest of the household appliances. No fa…" "Alright buddy, that’s enough." "What do you mean that’s enough?" "I mean that my dear grand momma Clara is fixin’ to roll over in her grave, and you didn’t even get to your second sentence." "Which reminds me, no old people either. The last thing we need is someone slow and dangerous behind the wheel who’s gonna stroke out the first haymaker that gets thrown." "Korver! This conversation is over." "And no rednecks either, one more of you hillbilly’s around and I’d feel like I traded in my wrestling boots for a camper in the Talladega infield." "A boy can dream, can’t he?" "I dream of a non-retarded partner sometimes. Does that count?" "So do I, Korver. So do I." "Which reminds me, speaking of retards…"
