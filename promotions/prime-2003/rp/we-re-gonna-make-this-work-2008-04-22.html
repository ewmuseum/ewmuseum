We're Gonna Make This Work

22 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/22/08 The sun shined down on the crisp, cool water of the beach somewhere between Hilo and Papaikou, Hawaii. Since joining PRIME, Bryan hadn’t had much free time, so any small period of time that he could take off he spent wisely. There was a good breeze blowing in towards the shore, and the waves were peaking at five or six feet a piece. It was a good day to be out on the water, and a good time for Bryan to clear his head of all of his recent distractions. Bryan had been coming to this spot since he had learned to surf around the age of five. Peter, his grandfather, had actually taught him the basics, due to his father being off on tour with HCW for a majority of his childhood. So in all reality, surfing, not wrestling, had been Bryan’s first love and passion. With time, however, that would change, and Bryan would follow in his father’s footsteps. It took a couple tries to get back up on the board after such a long time of being away from the water, but soon Bryan caught back on, and was gliding along the clear water like he hadn’t lost a step. Meanwhile, on the beach, Bryan’s cell phone vibrated wildly in his back pack. He was far away from being within earshot of the phone, so it was nearly two hours before he checked it. After drying himself off and plopping down onto his towel to admire the horizon, Bryan remembered his phone. “Ah, one missed call. A new voicemail too, eh?” Dawkins listened to the message on the phone. At first, the voice was unfamiliar, but after the first couple lines, it became clear… “Hey Bry. I know it’s only been a couple months, but it feels like I haven’t seen you forever. Do me a favor and give me a call back so we can meet up if you’re not busy the next time you come home. We’ve got a bit of catching up to do…love ya, kid.” It was Nikkie, one of Bryan’s closer friends throughout high school and what would have been his college years. At a couple points over that time, Bryan wanted to take things a step further in their relationship, but things could never work out; he was her “best friend” and it would just be “too awkward.” Bryan understood, but after the attempt at taking things further with her, they lost touch with one another. The phone calls became less frequent, and they hardly saw each other anymore…except for the night of the party. FOUR MONTHS EARLIER… The Dawkins household was THE place to be that night. It was the night they found out that Bryan was going to make an attempt to get back into wrestling. He was leaving to go train in California with one of his father’s old friends, and the entire town was excited; Bryan was getting one more shot to make a name for himself away from the island. The was a bar for mixed drinks set up in the back yard, kegs ‘a plenty scattered around the property, a fresh pig roasted on a spit in the far corner, and scantily-clad girls roaming around EVERYWHERE. It was a full out luau, and it was all for the Flyin’ Hawaiian. Bryan had consumed more than his fair share of alcohol over the course of the night, and to be honest, most of the people in attendance had as well. As things started to wind down and people began to drunkenly stumble their respective ways home, things started to heat up between Bryan and Nikkie. It started with a hug that was supposed to be a “good luck, I’m gonna miss you” hug, but turned into a whole lot more. It escalated to a kiss, followed by some body exploration, which eventually lead to clothing being removed, and…well, I think you’ve pretty much got the picture after that. What? You don’t? Well let’s just say they didn’t lie in the bed and tell each other ghost stories. It was supposed to be closure for the two. Bryan could get it (whatever it was) out of his system, figuratively and literally, and Nikkie could have the satisfaction of knowing that Bryan could get over her and they could both move in their separate ways. Or so they thought. PRESENT DAY Bryan decided to call Nikkie back. It had been a while since they’d seen each other, and he figured it would be a good idea to see how she was doing. “Aloha bud, how ya doin’?” “Oh, Bryan? You got my message?” “That I did…what’s up?” “Oh, well…I hadn’t seen you since the night your folks had the party at the house and…well, I missed you. There’s a lot to talk about.” “Yeah, missed ya too, Nik. It’s been hard not seeing my…” Bryan grimaced upon mentioning the next few words. “…best friend in so long.” “So Bry, are you busy? Actually, are you at home?” “Yeah, I’m actually at home for a day or two. I just got done surfing at my spot; ya know, the one between Hilo and Papaikou?” “I know the place. Gonna be there for a while?” “Actually I was plannin’ on goin’ home, but I can wait if ya want.” “I can be there in ten minutes. See you there?” “Yeah, I’ll be here.” “’Kay, bye.” And with that, she hung up. After a few moments of watching the sun reflect off of the bright blue Hawaiian water, Bryan heard the shuffling footsteps of someone coming through the sand behind him, and soon felt a finger tapping lightly on his shoulder. It was Nikkie… …an enlarged Nikkie. …a pregnant Nikkie. She used to be a very athletic girl, always in shape and working on making her body even more perfect than it already was. Her shoulder length brown hair was tied up in a pony tail, and she was still as cute as she was the last time he saw her. Only now, she was pregnant. She couldn’t help but notice his unwavering attention at her ever-growing body, so she decided to just spit out the obvious. “Bryan, I’m pregnant.” Bryan, still staring in awe at the spot where a new life was being raised, was shocked. “…with who? Who’s the father?” A tear formed at the corner of her right eye and slid down her cheek. She did her best to brush it away without Bryan noticing, but it didn’t work. “C’mon, Nik. Who’s the dad? When did this happen?” “…it’s you, Bryan. The night of the party at your place.” Bryan’s look of shock turned into one of sheer disbelief. His eyes grew wide, and his jaw hung open. This couldn’t be true, he thought. He had been drinking heavily that night, but things didn’t go that far, did they? It was Nikkie, it couldn’t have happened. They were just friends, and friends don’t do that. And even if they did, he SURELY would’ve used a condom, right? Right. No, actually, wrong. “No, that can’t be right. That never happened with us. And even if it would have, I would’ve been safe about it.” “Well apparently you weren’t, Bryan. Because here I am, VERY pregnant, and you’re the father. I’m not mad, I’m not upset, and this is gonna be fine. I just need you to know that this is happening, and I’m having this baby, whether you like it or not.” Bryan was still in shock. His attention had moved from Nikkie to the horizon, but the same expression was painted across his face. He tried to muster up the words to say, but they just weren’t coming out. She sat down beside him and he put his arm around her. They were both speechless, even though there were so many words that needed to be said. However, that conversation was simply not happening at that moment. Bryan was leaving in a day or two to leave for Nebraska, but he vowed to come back directly after the show to tend to the business at hand. Finally, Bryan came up with the only thing he could possibly say at the moment… “…somehow, we’re gonna make this work.”
