Fatherhood - Chapter 13 - Change of Plans (Dual Halo & 5 Star Title Match vs. High Flyer RP)

7 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 7, 2009 WEDNESDAY FEBRUARY 25, 2009 SULLIVAN ARENA – ANCHORAGE , ALASKA FOLLOWING REVOLUTION 187 “Good to have ya back, Chan.” Dawkins limped over to Tsonda, who was sitting on a nearby bench in Rayne’s rather large locker room. The adrenaline was still pumping throughout the Champ’s body, and Dawkins envied him. The adrenaline had worn off about halfway into the match against Snow and Shakur. The Flyin’ Hawaiian had a thick patch of gauze on his forehead, masking the gash across the edge of his hairline. “Yeah kid, it won’t feel too good tomorrow, but right now it doesn’t feel bad. You alright?” Tsonda’s voice showed a hint of concern for his young protégé. Being matched up one-on-one isn’t exactly the most ideal situation for anyone, much less a young, budding star who had been preparing as though he was going to have a partner in the match. “Hawaii’s gonna be fine. You don’t give the kid enough credit, Tink.” Even Rayne was being supportive. The bond that had grown between three of the top talents in PRIME was ever-growing, and always getting stronger. Sure, they’ve had their rough patches, but like all strong units, they stuck together and found a way to persevere. They were family. “He’s right, bruh. Gimme a week or so, and I’ll be ready to begin that record setting 5 Star reign that I’ve been talkin’ about since Great American Nightmare. Sorry Ty, but that record of yours has gotta go.” Dawkins flashed that quirky smile of his that couldn’t help but to brighten the entire room up. Tsonda stood up and winced; the ribs were nowhere near being fully healed, and every day his body found ways to let him know. Meanwhile, Dawkins quickly was filling his gym bag. Rayne and Tsonda were still in attire from ReV, while Dawkins speedily packed. “Jeez, what’s the rush, Hawaii?” Dawkins flashed that grin that only made its appearance when he was talking about Nicholas. “Goin’ back home, bruh. Nicholas is getting’ released this week. I’m hopin’ to bring him next weekend to see what his dad’s work is all about.” “Now kid, I know havin’ my godson around is only gonna bring more motivation, but you gotta be careful. Next weekend is gonna be one of the biggest challenges of your life, and you need to be avoiding distractions.” Dawkins’ grin slowly subsided, and he slumped back onto the bench. “Listen, what I’m trying to say is that you gotta be careful because even though you’re gonna be pumped that Nick is around, you still have business to take care of. If you want Nick to have that good life you’ve been talkin’ about since he came into this world, you’re gonna have to do your job out in that ring.” “He’s right, Hawaii. I know havin’ the kid here is gonna be one of the shining moments of your life, but at the end of the day, you’ve still got a job to do.” Dawkins nodded and flashed a halfhearted grin at his friends. “I know what’cha mean, bruhs. But this is important to me and I think, if anything, this is just gonna gimme more motivation to go out and make a statement.” Glancing at each other, Tsonda and Rayne shrug simultaneously and gave their blessing to the young Hawaiian. They warned him, and if he was going to learn anything from the situation, Dawkins needed to find out by himself. And with that, he was off. * * * SATURDAY MARCH 7, 2009 BRYAN DAWKINS’ APARTMENT HILO, HAWAII 5:35 PM Dawkins laid passed out on the living room sofa after a long week of packing Nicholas’ things up. For only being a few months old, Bryan’s son sure had a lot of possessions. The television was on in front of him, and the sound of Tony Kornheiser and Michael Wilbon filled the room with a soft murmur. Every so often, Bryan would flinch from the deep bruises embedded in his ribcage, or he would, without thinking, raise his hand to his head to softly rub the slowly healing wound on his forehead. After a few moments, Dawkins sat up, groggy, and slowly rubbed his eyes in hopes of waking up quicker. Reaching out to the small coffee table in front of the sofa, Dawkins could feel the deep bruises giving him signals that moving in that direction wasn’t a good idea at that time. He flinched, brought a hand to his ribs cautiously, as if that would instantly halt the pain, and exhaled deeply. He slowly made his way to the bathroom, and forty minutes later, emerged from the shower a revitalized, yet still badly beaten man. He took a few minutes to grab a quick drink, down a few slices of pineapple, and find his sunglasses before heading out to the garage to fetch his car. It was time to go see Nicholas. * * * SATURDAY MARCH 7, 2009 HILO MEDICAL CENTER ROOM 3233 6:03 PM “What the…” Dawkins’ expression was that of pure shock as he stood in the doorway to room 3233, only to find that Nicholas Chandler Dawkins was not present. Nor were any of his things. His crib was gone, all of the medical machines were gone, all of the decorations, pictures, and little nick nacks. All gone. Turning around quickly, Dawkins sought off to find the nearest person who could give him answers. He rushed toward the nurses desk located a few hundred feet down the hall from room 3233, and asked the first person he found. Marnee. “Where’s Nicholas?” A look of confusion from Marnee prompted clarification from Bryan. “Where’s my son? He’s not in his room!” “I..I don’t know, Bryan.” Bryan’s breathing was quickening, and sweat was starting to form around the gauze affixed to his brow. “What do you mean you don’t know? YOU WORK HERE.” Marnee took a step back. “I mean, I don’t know where they took Nicholas. All I know is that I saw some of the doctors, along with a woman I never saw on this floor before, down by Nicholas’ room. Next thing I know, I went to check on him and everything was gone. “Why didn’t I know about this, Marnee?“ Marnee shrugged and took another step back. “I don’t know, Bryan. I didn’t even know about it…” Just as Bryan was about to storm back down the hallway in pursuit of his child, an older nurse stopped him. “You’re Nicholas’ dad?” Bryan nodded and looked at Marnee in confusion. “Here. They told us to give this to you if you came around.” The older nurse, Carla, handed Dawkins an envelope and turned away. “What is it?” Walking away, Carla shrugged. “Don’t know. Didn’t really feel like I needed to ask.” Dawkins sighed. “Marnee, you have any idea what this is?” Shrugging, Marnee stepped closer to inspect the contents of the letter with Bryan. “No.” Dawkins opened the letter and read the first line to himself. ”Dear Mr. Dawkins: We regret to inform you that because of an impending custody battle between yourself and the Dawkins and Sivec families, your son, Nicholas Chandler Dawkins, will not be eligible to be released this upcoming week like previously planned.” A tear formed in the corner of Dawkins’ right eye, and slowly began to run down his face. His breath was taken away, and the sheer shock of the information left him weak in the knees. Bryan let the paper slowly slip out of his fingertips and stared ahead blankly, while Marnee quickly snatched the paper out of the air, not letting it touch the ground. She inspected it closely while Dawkins continued to stare off in his trance. “Bryan, look. You can still go see him.” Snapping out of the stupor, Dawkins breathed deeply, sending the sound of sniffles echoing throughout the hospital hallways. As he began to respond, Dawkins seethed. “Why wasn’t I notified sooner?” Dawkins’ breathing was again quickening, as the sadness and shock of the situation turned into anger and rage. “This is fucking ridiculous. Nicholas is MY son, and MY son should be able to go home with HIS father whenever HE’s ready! I wanna know who left this letter here. Does she work for the hospital? Or is she from the outside? Marnee, I NEED to know. I need to straighten this out.” Carla, from the other side of the nurses’ station, chimed in. “She has darker skin, dark brown hair, and was wearing a white dress shirt with a navy blue skirt. Not really tall, but a little on the heavier side. That’s all I remember. Sorry, hun.” Carla turned back around and Bryan glanced at Marnee. She shrugged, and Dawkins stormed off down the hallway. He knew that there was one person who would know who was behind this. * * * SATURDAY MARCH 7, 2009 DAWKINS HOUSEHOLD 6:57 PM Dawkins’ Honda Civic screeched to a halt in his parents’ driveway, and without taking the keys from the vehicle, Bryan jumped out of the car and made a b-line for the front door. He glanced in front of the house, where a brand new BMW was parked. Nobody his family knew had the kind of money for that kind of high-priced car. His heart was pumping terribly quickly, almost as if he was competing in one of his matches, and adrenaline pumped through his body as he threw open the front door. “What the fuck is going on?” Bryan rounded the corner into the family den only to see his parents sitting on the couch across from a short, slightly overweight woman wearing a white dress shirt and blue skirt. Carla was right. “Who are you?” “Melinda Anderson, Child Custody Attorney. I’m going to be representing your parents and the Sivec family in the case. I’m assuming you’re Bryan. You must have gotten my letter.” Bryan, clenching his fists, was steaming in rage. “You think I’d be here for any other reason?” Just as Melinda was about to address Bryan again, he turned to his parents. “What the fuck is wrong with you two? We can’t settle this ourselves? That’s such bullshit. You’re supposed to be my family, and you go behind my back and get the law involved in something that we could have settled over a fucking dinner between us and Nikkie’s parents. You guys are ridiculous, you know that?” Unable to control himself, Dawkins began pacing back and forth, nostrils flaring, teeth gritting, and writhing his hands continuously. “Seriously, I don’t get it. Why do you guys not understand that I can handle taking care of Nicholas and myself at the same time? Other single parents find a way to do it all the time, yet when I decide to go out and make a decision about my life that doesn’t involve you guys, you go and get people involved who don’t understand that I can take care of my son, no matter what you guys or anyone else thinks.” Out of breath, Dawkins stopped pacing for a moment before resuming. “I want you guys to really think about what you’re doin’, because believe me when I tell you that if you go through with this, Nicholas’ and my relationship with you guys is OVER. This is unforgivable, because no matter how right you guys think you are, there is no possible way that taking a father’s son from him is the right thing to do. I can take care of him, and after I go into court and prove that to whoever I need to, you guys will never get the chance to show me that you deserve to be the part of his life that grandparents are supposed to be.” Leaning up against a near wall, Dawkins rested his voice and his breathing began to slow again. Melinda began to stir, attracting attention from Bryan. “Bryan, I think it’d be wise for you to stay out of contact with your parents until the case is taken care of. This isn’t going to be a fun matter for anyone, and like you’ve just shown, emotions are going to be at an all time high during this entire ordeal.” Melinda stood up and began approaching Bryan, who threw a hand up, warning her to keep her distance. “But I really think the best thing for everyone would be for there to be limited to no contact between yourself and the grandparents of your child. Also, I think it’d be wise to come up with a lawyer so that you can defend yourself properly. Hopefully, if all goes well, we can settle this so that all parties involved get at least some of what they want, and we can all go home happy.” By the time the word “happy” left Melinda’s mouth, Dawkins was already out in the driveway. Bryan took one last look toward the house, knowing very well that it could be the last time that he made his presence there. Wiping a lone tear from his cheek, Bryan got into his car and sped away from the house. His plans had changed, and now there was a battle to partake in. And while Bryan begins his journey toward Russia to fight for dominance in his workplace, in the coming months, he will begin a longwinded fight for something so much more important to him. His son.
