Greatest... promo... ever

27 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/05 Nick: Welcome back to PRIME’s flagship show, ReVolution! We’ve got a great match in progress between Mexican legend El Jobberifico and that classic brawler, Random Jobber #2! Richard: Great match, snooze fest, whatever you want to call it. I’m just waiting for a run in! In the ring, Random Jobber #2 has the masked sensation El Jobberifico in a powerful headlock, that he’s maintained for the past ten minutes. Nick: This is truly a showing of ring mastery. Richard: He’s got him in a headlock! How’s that ring mastery? Nick: It’s methodical! Richard: What? God, will someone please do a run in now?! The lights go out in the arena. Richard: FINALLY! Nick: Oh no, who could be coming out to interrupt this catch as catch can classic?! Richard: Seriously… do you touch yourself in the dark while reading old Ross Reports? Nick: I have no idea what you’re talking about. The lights take on a flickering reddish cast as “While The City Sleeps” by MC 900 Ft Jesus starts to play over the arena speakers. The crowd leaps to its feet as the Trashcan Man, steel garbage receptacle held high steps out onto the entrance ramp. Richard: Dammit! Can we go back to the jobbers showing their mastery of one single resthold? Nick: OH MY GOD! TRASHCAN MAN IS HERE IN PRIME! HE’S HERE IN PRIME! Trash sprints down the ramp, trashcan in hand as the oblivious jobbers continue to work their methodical pace… which involves possibly the greatest of all Luchadores El Jobberifico sittng on the mat while darling of the Internet Random Jobber #2 is on his knees beside him, headlock firmly in place. The hardcore legend slides into the ring, lifting his namesake weapon up on high before bringing it down upon the unsuspecting skull of future Hall Of Famer Random Jobber #2 who slumps over in a heap. Nick: OH MY GOD! HE’S BROKEN HIM IN HALF!! Richard: Seriously, stop it. It’s creeping me out. Masked marvel El Jobberifico, freed from that punishing resthold, springs to his feet as only a Mexican in a mask can. The high flying masked supserstar runs, leaping up in an amazing feat of agility, springing off the top rope into some sort of twisty somersault gravity defying manuever at the Trashcan Man, who swats him out of the air with his dented trashcan, sending him hurtling through the ropes and out onto the floor below. Nick: BRILLIANT COUNTER BY THE TECHNICAL MASTER! Richard: Technical master? He hit him with a trashcan! The hardcore legend reaches into his pocket and pulls out a microphone. Trashcan Man: I FRIGGIN’ RULE AS YOU CAN SEE~! Complete silence falls over the arena. If you listen closely, you can hear crickets chirping. It’s that quiet. Finally, we hear a single chuckle… followed by another… and a HUGE swell of laughter washes over the dumbstruck Trashcan Man as the crowd roars it’s amusement. Trashcan Man: What’s so damn funny? A chant breaks out…. Crowd: He-Man! He-Man! He-Man! Trashy looks down in shock to see that his pants are gone. From the waist down he’s clad in only his boots… and He-Man and the Masters of the Universe Underoos. Richard: Now that’s something I never ever wanted to see. Nick: HE-MAN! HE-MAN! STONE COLD! STONE COLD! PAY PER VIEW! PAY PER VIEW! “ARRRRGH!” The Trashcan Man suddenly sits up in his bed, covered in a cold sweat. “What in the hell was THAT all about?” he mutters to himself as he blearily tries to shake the cobwebs of that horrible, horrible nightmare away. “Who the hell cuts an in ring promo? Why did Nick sound like JR? Why the hell was I wearing He-Man Underoos?! His mouth opens wide as he emits a jaw creaking yawn, slowly stretching out the kinks. He winces as he tries to extend his right arm fully, the elbow being wrapped in bandages, evidence of his death defying leap from the top of the Halo structure onto Hoyt Williams’ golden Cadillac. “Seems to be healing up ok. The doc did a good job with those stitches. At least there wasn’t that much glass to pull out this time.” He scratches at his shaggy torso with his horribly burn scarred hands, several scars showing through his chest hair silent testimony to the brutal battles he had endured throughout his career. Rise and shine, sleepy head. That was definitely an entertaining dream you gifted us with this morning. Trashy shakes his head as he hears a familiar high pitched cackling in his head. “Do you have to laugh so damn loud, Smiley? That dream was unnerving enough as it is without you giggling in my head before my first cup of coffee” But it was He-Man Underoos! The look on your face... The hardcore legend winces a bit as the cackling in his head intensifies. Throwing off his dark blue comforter, he swings his legs out and stands up, making his way across his hardwood floor to the kitchen. “Lay off Smiley. We’ve got a flight to Little Rock tonight, and I’d like to get some more work done on that piece in the scrapyard before we go.” He starts to make a pot of coffee as he talks to the voice in his head, pausing only to hitch up his flannel pajama bottoms. I still don’t think this is such a good idea, Trash. Why not stick to doing the metal scrap art you’ve been working on. It’s not like we need the money, and we both like putting those sculptures together. Trashy sighs as he reaches into the fridge for some half and half. He takes a careful sniff, his brow furrowing for a moment before he shrugs and sets it on the counter. “We’ve been over this, Smiley. It’s not enough any more. I’ve been getting too restless, and I’ve got to find a better outlet. Remember how close I came to setting that old warehouse on fire last month? You were barely able to talk me out of it.” It’s amazing what threats of constantly humming the chicken dance for all eternity will do to you, isn’t it? But Trash… you know the barriers are getting weaker. I can feel HIM starting to poke around. And I know you do too. He’s not pushing to break out yet… but he’s testing The last thing we need to be is distracted. You know how emotional you get when this business is concerned. Placing his palms flat on the counter in front of him, the Trashcan Man hangs his head low for a moment. “I need this, Smiley. You know I do. I’m not on any big crusades this time, I just need the release that wrestling gives. Didn’t you see how happy I was fighting the HIT? Man, it’s been so long since I’ve tangled with someone who can go toe to toe with me like that…” He smiles, raising his head. “Leaping off that cage, trying to drop the elbow on him… that rush… it was like it used to be. It just felt so good to be doing that again. The crowd screaming… everything was right once again.” Don’t say I didn’t warn ya, Trash. I’ll do what I can in here to keep things level… try not to go off on one of your crusades again. I don’t think I can handle the big fight against the evil powers trying to take over whatever fed we’re in. It’s all just so… unfun. The hardcore legend chuckles. “No promises, Smiley, but we’ll see. We’ve got that match this week with the HIT and Razor Blade… and they’re nothing but a couple of hardcore brawlers like me. No crusading there, just a good old fashioned brawl. I owe HIT a good kicking for jumping me after the Halo match… I just wanna know… who names their kid Razor Blade? That’s about the most retarded name I’ve seen since… He quirks an eyebrow. “Trashcan Man and Mr Smiley Face?” Exactly. I’m jealous. I wanted to be named after a Vanilla Ice lyric too! Mr Smiley Face starts singing the riff from Ice, Ice, Baby in Trashy’s head as we fade to black…
