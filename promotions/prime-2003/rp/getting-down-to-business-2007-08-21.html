Getting Down to Business.

21 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 21, 2007 Tom Walczak lies down on a bed in a hotel room thinking of what he had just seen on the television. Walczak has just learned that the man who had sent him here to Russia is also one if its most wanted criminals. Walczak is not new to the dangers of the criminal world, but he has vowed to stay out of it. He has spent time in jail on numerous occasions, and to say it was an undesirable experience would be an understatement. A dilemma now faces Walczak. He has always wanted to find out what had happened to his parents, what background he came from, but in doing so it could take him down a path he had already traveled, and one to which he does not want to return. RING…..RING….RING…. The phone takes Walczak away from his train of thought. He looks around the room for the phone, finally seeing it on the table next to the television. He runs over to the table and picks up the phone. “Hello?” “It’s Dmitry, I will be outside of the hotel room in ten minutes. Be outside, and bring everything that was in the suitcase.” “But all that was in the….” CLICK…. Dmitry hangs up and a dial tone is all that is heard through Walczak’s receiver. He slams down the phone angered that every time he tries to find something out he is cut short. Walczak is rarely in a situation where he is not the one in power, but for the past couple of weeks he has been. “Fuck me….” Everything in Walczak’s head is telling him to get out, not to do this, but in his gut he knows that he must continue. He has had numerous chances to back out, but now he has come too far. There is nothing more to do but go forward. Walczak gets up and throwing his towel on the bed he starts to get ready. He puts on nothing more than a pair of jeans and a black t-shirt that barely makes its way around his bulky chest. Walczak picks up the briefcase off of the floor and places it on the bed. His palms are sweaty and his hands shake as he goes to open the suitcase. The gun stares him in the face, it is the last thing stopping him from making the choice. Walczak looks over at the clock radio sitting on the dresser next to the bed, time is running out. “Fuck it…” Walczak picks up the gun and after making sure it’s loaded he places it in securely in his jeans. As he walks out the door it is obvious that he has made his choice, and where it will lead him only time will tell…. ________________________________________________________________ Walczak waits outside of the hotel. It has been longer than ten minutes; closer to half an hour and still Dmitry is not there. He is no longer worried about what he has to do, but the wait is killing him. Patience has never been a virtue that Walczak has had, and it does not seem that he has acquired it in the short time that he has been in Russia. “Get in.” Dmitry shouts to Walczak from inside his black Lincoln Town Car. Knowing that the wait is over, Walczak gets into the car taking a seat in the front next to Dmitry. “Before we go any further you need to tell me exactly what’s going on, and what I am doing here. I came to this country, not knowing anything and I still don’t know anything.” “You don’t have to know anything. You are here to provide muscle, and that’s it.” “I need…” “You need to be quiet. If you insist on knowing more than when we are done with our meeting I’ll tell you, but it will only be a liability on your part.” “What meeting?” Dmitry does not answer Walczak, and just keeps on driving. He weaves in an out of traffic trying to make up for the time he lost picking up Walczak. “What meeting?” Walczak asks one more time, but again he receives no response. Anger keeps building up inside of Walczak. He feels as though he is being disrespected, but he knows that there is little that he could do to stop it. The car comes to a stop in front a little café in the center of St. Petersburg. It does not look like the place that shady business dealings would take place, so it somewhat catches Walczak off guard. “You will follow me inside, and say nothing. If anything starts to go wrong I feel that you will know what to do.” Even though Walczak really does not know what to do he shakes his head. Asking questions has not gained him any further knowledge, so why add more stress? Dmitry walks outside handing the keys to his car to a valet. Walczak takes a deep breath and follows Dmitry into the café. The outside of the café gives the appearance of a rustic and homely place, but as Walczak walks inside it is obvious that this is not the case. The floor is made up of pristine marble, and designs made from gold adorn it. The walls are covered in large paintings and ornate mirrors. Elegance is the only word that could describe the inside of the café. “Zenstov…” A large man wearing a black pinstripe suit and snakeskin boots makes his way towards Walczak and Dmitry. He looks at Dmitry, but pays no attention to Walczak, almost as if he has not yet recognized the two of them are together. “What are you doing here Michal? We are supposed to have a meeting with Janek.” “Go upstairs, they are waiting.” Dmitry goes forward, and motions for Walczak to follow him, but as Walczak does the palm of the large man sinks firmly into the chest of Walczak. “You will not be going any further.” “What is the meaning of this? He is here on direct orders from Nickolaj…” Walczak raises a finger to Dmitry causing him to stop speaking. He then slowly pushes the hand of the large man away from his chest, and looks down at the man. The man is around six foot two in height and he carries a solid two hundred and fifty pounds on his frame, but next to Walczak he looks like an average sized man. “I am supposed to be present for this meeting, that is what I was told to do, and it’s not going to change because you said so.” “That is where you are mistaken…” The man slowly pulls away his jacket revealing a holster and a gun. This is not the way Walczak wanted to start things off. Getting out of this meeting unscathed is how Walczak had hoped to get through the day, but it does not look like this will be the case. “That is what I thought.” It seems that the man has taken Walczak’s silence as a sign of obedience, but that is not the case. A swift kick to the man’s abdomen bends him over, and a knee to the jaw sends the man flat to the floor. Walczak steps over the body of his man digging his size eighteen boot into the throat of the helpless man. “Do you have a problem with me going up there now?” Nothing more than a couple of groans and mumbles come out of the mouth of the man, and Walczak smiles at him before allowing him to breathe and following Dmitry upstairs. “It seems like you do know what you’re doing.” For the first time since he had met Dmitry, he had broken into a smile. When they get up the stairs Dmitry knocks on the door. A couple of seconds pass before a beautiful woman comes to open it. She is wearing a form fitting black dress, and he straight blonde hair comes down to her shoulders. “How are you Mr. Zenstov?” “I am well, where is Janek?” “He is not here today, but I have all the information, and was Michal not with you.” “Michal is currently indisposed, but I do not think that I heard your right. Did you say that Janek is not here?” “I did but like I said I have all of the information.” “Well, than we will be leaving. Tell Janek to call me when he comes back.” “Mr. Zenstov, you must….” Walczak follows Dmitry back down the stairs neither of them saying another word to the woman. Walczak has wasted most of his time in Russia accomplishing nothing, but he still has no new information. “So, do you still want to find out more about why you are here?” The question catches Walczak off guard and it takes a couple of seconds before he can think of anything to say. “Uhh….yeah…yes I want to find out all that I can.” “Alright let’s go get something to eat.” Walczak follows Dmitry outside, and they wait for the valet to bring out their car. ____________________________________________________________________ The drive away from the café was much more relaxed, and Dmitry had even turned on the radio and offered Walczak a smoke. Dmitry gets off of the highway and drives into a small rural town outside of St. Petersburg. Walczak looks around at the surroundings, much of which remind him of his youth in Poland. There are men plowing the fields, and woman tending to farm animals. Even the children get involved taking care of smaller domestic chores. Dmitry pulls the car into a small dirt lot that only has one other car in it. The lot is behind what seems to be a rather old family restaurant. “This is the best restaurant in the world. The food is unbeatable.” Walczak smiles, but he highly doubts that the best food in the world is inside this little shack. They get inside, and Walczak immediately notices the difference from the metropolitan café. The floors are dusty, and wooden. The tables are also made of wood, and look worn down, as if they had been there since the restaurants inception. All in all it has a very rustic feel. “Natalia.” Dmitry makes his way over to an older lady who seems to be the proprietor of the restaurant. She wears a body length dress, and her head is covered in a sarong. Her hands seem to be covered in flower, but she still grabs Dmitry’s cheek before planting a kiss on the man’s cheek. “Dmitry, how are you? Who is your friend?” Dmitry introduces Walczak, and the lady brings him in for a hug treating him as if he were family. She brings the two over to a table and makes her way back to the kitchen. “Natalia is my aunt, she always takes care of me, and I have never met a better cook.” “She seems like a great lady.” “Now, I ask you one more time. Do you want to find out more? Before you answer, know that if you know more than ignorance will not be able to protect you.” The thought of not knowing what kind of situation he is involved frightens him more than the possibility of realizing how deeply involved he is in the situation. “I want to know.” Dmitry takes a deep breath, almost as if he was hoping that Walczak would not want to find out anything. “The man who we are dealing with is responsible for the death of your parents…”
