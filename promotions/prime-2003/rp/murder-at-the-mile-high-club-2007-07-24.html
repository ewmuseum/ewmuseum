Murder at the Mile High Club

24 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/24/07 Another night, another crazy day at the office, Craig thinks to himself as he stands outside of the Cow Palace in San Francisco. Just a few short hours ago, another edition of ReVolution had gone out over the air, and Craig had been tied to the three men he hated the most at this moment in time; Devin Shakur, Pierce Lavelle, and Jonathan Winters. Now, he’s waiting for a taxi cab to come pick him up so that he can head out of town for a bevy of different appearances and events before he heads to Portland for the penultimate showing of ReVolution where he’ll compete in a huge tag team match with Nova as his tag team partner. The race to Colossus is coming upon him, and he’s a little worried as to whether or not he’ll perform to his liking. A few minutes pass and finally, with the obnoxious glare of brights that should have been clicked off and a screeeeeech of its tires, the taxi cab shows up. It slowly pulls up to Craig, who immediately walks to the trunk of the taxi cab before throwing his stuff in. He’s learned to pack lightly with the whirwind schedule he keeps at this point, so all he has is a simple black leather bag. Then he slams the trunk shut, and he heads to the seat right behind the driver. As his hand pulls on the lever to open the door, he hears a voice yelling out at him. "Hey, hold on!" Craig looks up to see a man rushing out of the arena with two bags in his arms. As the figure gets closer, he notices that it is none other than former Universal Champion and his unexpected tag partner, the Risen Star. Craig takes a deep breath as he walks over to his colleague and stares at him. "You can't get your own taxi cab?" Craig asks him and Nova just glances at him before opening the trunk and throwing his things in there. He slams it emphatically. “Given how late it is, and that the taxi schedule I checked said they stop running in…well, one minute ago…I’d say no, I can’t get a cab,” Nova says, his eyes locked on the red coals on either side of Dusk’s nose. The Risen Star walks around to the other side of the cab and climbs in. Dusk does the same, and there’s a moment of awkward silence as the cab rolls on into the night. Nova decides to break it. “So…where to for you?” “I'm heading to the airport," Craig replies before looking ahead to the cab driver. "Look, take 4th Street to Long Avenue.” The cabbie immediately starts pulling off. Nova cocks an eyebrow and clears his throat. “Actually, I’m headed to the airport, too, and I’m pretty sure it ain’t on 4th-and-Long.” Dusk turns back to Nova, leaning his head quizzically at the Risen Star. “Oh, I’m pretty sure it is. I mean, I flew in from there not a couple of days ago.” “Yeah, yeah, me too,” Nova says dismissively before leaning up towards the cabbie, “and trust me, this ain’t the way.” “Please!” Dusk protests, “Like your memory is a credible source for navigation! How many joints have you smoked today?” “Seve-hey! What difference does it make?” Nova says, “I’m telling you, the airport is in the other direction!” He taps on the cab driver’s window. “Hey! Hey, Cochise! Turn this wagon around!” “What the hell are you doing, man?” Dusk groans, rubbing a hand against his temple, “it’s too late for this shit. I’m exhausted, and you’re hijacking my fucking cab.” “I’m not hijacking shi-listen, take us down 17th street to the 420,” Nova tells the cabbie mid-sentence with Dusk, “this asshole next to me is delirious, took too many shots to the head. He was sending you on a wild goose chase.” “Yeah,” Dusk chimes in with a snort, “we’ll see who sends who on a wild goose chase.” With that, the former Global star relents, leaning back in his seat as Nova directs the cabbie down a series of side roads before they break out onto a long, two-line stretch that ends in the distance with a series of lights around a building complex, illuminating a line of planes. “Oh…oh, I’m sorry, Craiggles, are those planes I see at that enormous building up ahead there?” Nova mocks, jabbing Dusk in the side with his elbow. “Are they, Craiggles? Because I can’t really see that well, I need you to use your laser eyes to look for me.” Dusk shoves him away grumpily. “Don’t call me ‘Craiggles’…and yes, those are fucking airplanes, but that doesn’t look a thing like the airport I flew into.” “Oh, doesn’t it, now?” Nova says, rolling his eyes, “Hmmm, must be a magic airport that just appeared here out of nowhere! It isn’t CRAIG’s airport! Ha, ha, ha, ha! What a schmuck!” The cab drives along the road until the lines divide around a tiny check-point station. Rolling through, the cab squeals to a stop as two men dressed in security uniforms flash lights into the car. “What are you doing here?” one of them demands gruffly, “you aren’t…” The lights shine through the back window at Nova and Dusk, who both wince and attempt to shield their eyes. One of the guards’ eyes flash with recognition, and he slaps the other one on the chest. “Hey! Hey, check it out! These guys are wrestlers!” “No shit?” The other one says, peeking around to get a good look himself. “Hey, guys,” Nova says, leaning over Dusk slightly and peeking up at the suited men, “we kinda gotta get a move on, ya know? We’re on a schedule here.” Their laughter ceases, and they resume the serious airs of dutiful sentries. “Yes, sir,” one of them barks, his eyes staring straight ahead in a vacant fashion that the Risen Star guesses is probably only half theatrical. “Go on ahead, sirs. They must’ve been expecting you for a while.” The cab rolls on ahead, and Nova cocks an eyebrow at his unexpected partner/cab buddy. “That’s a weird thing to say.” “This whole situation is weird,” Dusk replies, rolling his eyes for the fiftieth time in under an hour. “Eh, who cares, we’re at the airport, aren’t we? Eh?” Nova says with a grin, “using MY directions. Yep, just another area of life where Nova > Dusk. You hear that, cabbie? I own this guy! Every day of the week!” “Yeah, yeah,” Dusk grumbles, “we’re here, asshole. Climb out and grab your shit.” “With pleasure!” a jovial Risen Star exclaims amiably. They walk around to the back of the cab and grab their bags before looking at one another. Awkwardly. For, like, almost a minute. “What are you looking at me for?” Nova asks. “What are you looking at me for?” Dusk replies, “I was looking at you because you were looking at me!” “I was looking at you, sir, to signal you to walk around front and pay the man,” Nova explains matter-of-factly. “WHAT?!” Dusk cries indignantly, “dude, I was waiting for you to pay him! You hitched a ride in my cab!” “YOUR directions to the airport blew ass!” Nova fires back, “I got us here, I’m the Man, now you pay the man. Chop, chop. Plus I don’t have any cash.” “I don’t believe this,” Dusk growls as he walks around to the side of the cab and hands the cabbie a few bills, “let’s get the fuck inside before I miss my plane and this whole ordeal will have been for nothing.” “Agreed,” Nova concurs with a triumphant grin. They approach a set of electronic sliding doors, which open for them and lead into a marble hallway. Fake plants and trees of various…um…varieties…are positioned decoratively around them…but there’s no one in sight. “Damn, I knew you were catching the RED EYE, but where are the people?!” Nova exclaims before turning to the former Intense Champion, “you get that one? Get it? ‘Red-Eye’?” Dusk just stares blankly at him before shaking his head and moving on. “I have a really shitty feeling about this, Nov, I gotta tell ya. If you led us astray here, I’m gonna be seriously pissed off. Fair warning.” Nova brushes off Dusk’s threatening tone and they continue to walk down the corridor until it opens up into a vast room with staircases in the distance and booths set up alongside its left side…all abandoned. “There’s…nobody,” Nova says in almost a hushed whisper. “There’s…fucking…NOBODY!” Dusk shouts, his voice echoing off the walls, “FUCK! Where are we?! I don’t hear my plane boarding, Nova! I don’t hear anything! Where did you lead us?” “Pretty lattice-work, though,” Nova says offhandedly, lighting a cigarette. Outside… A man sits in a director’s chair next to a series of tripod-mounted cameras, all focused on the outside of the “airport.” He’s talking to a buxom blonde. “I gotta pat myself on the back for this one,” he boasts, “I wanted the perfect set for MURDER IN THE MILE HIGH CLUB, and I got the rental on this old abandoned airport for a SONG.” A stagehand runs up, interrupting him. “Sir, ready to shoot the snow scene.” “Then roll with it,” the director says with a wave of his hand, his eyes still locked on the boobs in front of him. “Sir, I think maybe I saw a few guys go inside the building,” the stagehand says awkwardly, scratching his neck. The director turns towards him grumpily. “I think maybe you should quit wasting my time and roll scene. Dump that fake snow, boy! Dump it good!” “Alright…,” the stagehand says with a shrug before running off. Inside… Nova stretches out on a bench, smoking his cigarette, as Dusk paces around nervously. “Dude, what the fuck?” Craig vents, “I’ve tried every phone in this dump, and none of them are connected! Where are we?” “Chill out,” Nova says with a shrug, “all that pacing is making me antsy.” “ANTSY?!” Dusk shouts, “Dude, my plane is…(looking down at his watch)…FUCK, IT’S TAKING OFF NOW!” “So you missed it,” Nova replies, “big deal. Happens to me all the time.” Dusk’s face turns the color of his eyes as he looks down on Nova, trembling. “I’m. Getting. Out of here. NOW. Enjoy your stay in Bates’ Fucking Airport.” He begins stomping off, and Nova shoots up from his prone position. “Hold up, don’t leave me in here! Dude, I’m deathly afraid of zombies, everyone knows that!” Dusk spins around. “Actually, I don’t think anyone knows that.” “Well then I just shared some privileged information with you!” Nova quips, “so wait a second and I’ll come with…” As he says this, several loud BOOMS are heard, and suddenly white blankets all the windows around them. Dusk’s jaw drops. “Is that…is that fucking…SNOW?!” He sprints towards the door, but after trying it, it won’t budge. “It is fucking SNOW!” "Global Warming. That's some shit," Nova says, walking away from the door. "Seriously, San Francisco in the middle of summer? Al Gore might be on to something," Craig says as he follows the Risen Star. “What the hell are we gonna do now? I can’t believe this!” A wry grin comes over Nova’s face. “I’ve got a few ideas.” Dusk looks at him suspiciously. “I don't know what you guys did in Fuck You, but I'm definitely not game for some ass play shit.” “It’s kind of like that,” Nova says, “except replace ‘ass’ with ‘smoking,’ and replace ‘play’ with ‘weed.’” He unfurls a large sack of ganja, much to his partner’s surprise. Dusk eyes the bag before looking around. “Well, shit, I mean I guess there isn’t much better to do…and if PRIME drug-tested, I think you would have been fired, like, thirty months ago.” “That’s the spirit!” Nova says, raising his fist in the air and flashing the rock symbol. m/ TEN MINUTES LATER m/ VRRROOOOOOOOMMMM!!! “WHEEEEEEEEEE!!” Nova squeals as he flies down one of the airport’s abandoned hallways on one of the motorized carts they use to chauffeur old people around. “I CAN’T BELIEVE WE FOUND THESE!!” Dusk is in close pursuit, the whole of his eyes now matching the middle. “I KNOW~! THIS IS GREAT! I’M COMIN’ FOR YA!!” “OHHHHH NO YOU’RE NOT!” Nova shouts back before zooming on, his head still turned back around to face his pursuer, “YOU CAN’T CATCH ME, YOU SCUM-SUCKING BOTTO-” “NOVA, LOOK OUT!!!” Dusk shrieks, and Nova spins back around just in time to see a group of plastic curtains hanging down over the wall directly in front of him. “AAAAAIIIEEEE!!!!” Cart and all, Nova plows through the curtains…only to drop at least twenty feet, down onto a huge mound of fake snow. Dusk halts his cart and runs over, brushing his way through all the plastic until he comes to a ledge overlooking the outside of the building. Nova is buried up to his head in the white powdery stuff, and the cart is all-but-submerged, one wheel poking out of the snow still spinning. “DUDE!” Dusk shouts, “You found the outside! Way to go!” Nova blows a patch of the fake snow off of his head. “This reminds me of my clubbing days.” "Clubbing, fuck, I used to sing at the club. Man, those were the days," Dusk mutters to himself, well, at least he thinks he mutters it to himself. Nova can only stare at Dusk, trying to get a mental image of Dusk singing on stage in the middle of a club, but fails each time he tries to. Too much weed. Seriously. "Yeah, that's great and all man, but we're outside--wait--- what the fuck... why are you climbing on top of the cart? Dude, just--what--ugh," Nova stammers towards the end as his eyes watch as Dusk climbs on top of the car, obviously thinking he's at the club all over again. Within seconds, Dusk looks out, seemingly reliving his days, and just starts to sing. "AND I--EIEIEIEIEIEIEIEIEIEIEIEIEIEI- WILL ALWAYS-YS LOVE YOU-OOOOOOOHOHOHOHOHOHOH! OH YES! AND I- OH I WILL- ALWAYS LOVE YOUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUU!" Dusk yells out at the top of his lungs, Nova only capable of looking on and seeing that putting the weed in this boy might not've been the best idea after all. Nova then walks over to Dusk and grabs him, but begins to fall as Dusk leaps off the top of the thing, thinking that Nova wants to play a game. "Ah, fuck dude, calm the fuck down! Yelling and shit, that was not singing." Dusk then rolls over and looks at Nova, sadness in his eyes. "Oh, shit, I'm sorry dude. I didn't realize--you know-- your singing--," he stammers once again, but then loses his train of thought as he wonders where he can get some food. "This doesn't taste like snow," Dusk says, throwing even Nova off guard. "What? You sure?" he asks, leaning down to pick up the snow in his hands. "Fuck, you're right, it tastes like--." "SNOW CONES!" the two of them yell at the same time, thinking they've entered a magical land of pounds after pones of the sweet, luscious, and delicious snow cones that you used to get as a kid that tasted like sweet heaven. They begin to pour the snow in their mouth, not even thinking of the reality that snow doesn't taste like snow cones. Far from it. More like water with piss in it, even if it hasn't been pissed in. After a few moments, Dusk starts to slow down almost as if the realization seems to hit him. "Wait... since when does snow taste like a snow cone?" he asks and Nova just gives him the finger and keeps going at it. Dusk loses his train of thought and starts eating it again. Yet, Nova starts to think about that question and stops eating it. He looks up at Dusk with horror in his eyes. "NOOOOOO! STOP! THAT'S HOW THEY MAKE ZOMBIES YOU FUCKER! NOOOOOOOOO!"Nova then rushes over to Dusk and tackles him, wanting Dusk to stop eating the zombie juice. Dusk, obviously not liking the fact that he was tackled, looks up at Nova who is starting at Dusk, hoping he saved him in time. Yet, Nova is frightened as he sees Dusk's eyes turn bright red. "Fuck! I'm too late! Get away from me you zombie!" Nova then gets off of Dusk and starts running for his life, knowing that it's all over for him. Dusk can only look on, wondering what the hell is wrong with Nova. "Hey, fuck face, my eyes are naturally red!" Nova then stops in his tracks and looks back at Dusk with a puzzling look on his face. "Oh, yeah, that's right. I forgot about that. Silly me." "Trix are for kids," Dusk mutters under his breath as he gets back up and looks at Nova who is still cautious of him. "Pussy. Now, wait, why does this taste like snow cones? Where the fuck are we?" he asks and no sooner does he get his answer when the director yells out. "CUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUT!" Both Dusk and Nova look over at the director who looks furious even though he is staring at a pair of boobs in his face. Even that is something that Dusk and Nova can't fathom. They walk over to the director, wondering what the hell is going on. "Yeah, what the fuck is this?" Dusk asks and the director finally breaks his stare as he looks at Nova and Dusk with an indignant look on his face. "It's my next big thriller, MURDER IN THE MILE HIGH CLUB! It's going to make millions at the box office. But, you two have RUINED it! Just demolished it," he tells them, nearly breaking into tears. Dusk just looks at Nova and seemingly wants to smack the shit out of him. "See what you got us into?!" he yells at Nova who looks at him like he's crazy. "Me! I didn't do anything! This is all of your fault," Nova responds, refusing to take the blame for this. "Yes, you did! You gave the directions!" Dusk yells back, the fury starting to take over. "No I did--, oh, wait, I kind of did didn't I," Nova says, realizing that he did indeed give the instructions. "Great, just great. I can't believe you! How could you screw this up?" Dusk says, starting to walk away from Nova, ready to go home. Nova starts following after him, not wanting to lose his partner in crime for the evening. "Wait, you can't leave me! Come on, it was a mistake!" Yet, Dusk keeps walking away and Nova can only watch. Then, he gets an idea. "AND I-IEIEIEIEIEIEIEIEIEIEIEIEIE- WILL ALWAYS LOVE YOUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUU!" Nova starts to sing to Dusk who stops in his tracks and looks bact at Nova who can only smile. Then, he looks back at the director who isn't quite sure what he's just seen. "Gets them every time," he tells the director before running after Dusk as the two men go back inside to get their things. And smoke more weed. The End.
