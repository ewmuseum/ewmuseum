Devin Shakur sits on a private jet, high above the sky, in his leather reclining chair reminiscing about the last couple of days. They have been a tumultuous period in his life, nothing that he hasn’t experienced in the past, but he hates living in this corner of hell. Someone had attempted to end Shakur life, dead, and it scared the living daylights out of him. For the incessant amount of times Shakur has claimed to be death proof, all of that invincibility washed away like sand once he was pitted on the highway. His mind flashed back to one of the many times during his childhood in which he was beaten profusely for whatever his father deemed appropriate. Thankfully, someone out there thwarted his enemies attempt to bring him six feet under. There is no way Shakur falls from grace this quickly, and not without a fight. Nobody has ever threatened his life before and gotten away with their crime in the end. There might be a period of months, years, or even a decade before the revenge strikes the perpetrator down, but it will always come. Commie Emo doesn’t forgive and he definitely does not forget. Shakur knows that the upcoming weeks are going to be a pain in the ass, literally and figuratively. The escalation in this war has started to pick up, and he is the individual who started the mayhem. Nobody expected him to pull off that kind of maneuver, murder, and the unpredictability is what he needs to keep an edge on the competition. If he fails, Shakur could lose everything and never having the privilege to open his eyes the next morning. At this point, that is unacceptable. He believes that if the enemy is kept on their toes, they will not have adequate time to prepare a counterstrike which is effective, that tiny bit of optimism that keeps Commie Emo in this fight. Well that, and he has heartless bastards working in his corner to help him rid of the opposition. He needs to take this flight, clear his head, and get ready for the next phase of destruction. Times are certainly not going to get any easier. Shakur looks up about fifty feet and sees a bottle of Jack Daniels resting on the bar. He gives a smirk and starts walking up to pour himself a glass. …There are some who choose to sit above the field, and plot… ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Team: DeAngelis Crime Family “You know Boss…This could be a good thing.” “…How much of the white powder did you do earlier? Tell me how in the fuck this could be a good thing!” “Well…” Michael DeAngelis wishes to believe that his lackey, Joey Turassi, is going to emit a rare moment of genius that will make the entire dilemma look like hogwash. Unfortunately, he knows Joey and realizes that killing rather than intelligent conversation is his forte. “I’m listening,” A perturbed Michael stamps his foot on the wooden floor. Joey takes another second to mull a potential argument over in his head, before shoving forth a pointer finger. “Ah, Devin can keep tabs on Tony better this way.” Michael gives Joey the most incredulous look ever permeated on a human face. “You really are proud of yourself for concocting that argument aren’t you Joey?” “It makes sense,” Joey replies, before nodding his head in agreement with himself. Trying to stay calm, Michael says, “Well you know what would make sense to me, Joey?” “What’s that Boss?” “I would rather have a cold blooded assassin who spends all night and all day conjuring up ways to destroy the one thorn in my side RATHER THAN SKIPPING DOWN THE HALL WITH OOMPA FUCKING LOOMPAS HOLDING A BOOM BOX BEHIND THEM!” …Others become consumed by trivial matters… DeAngelis massages his temple with his right hand and paces around the room like a madman without a prayer. “Christian said that Shakur was one of the best out there, that he got results done without asking many questions. Hell, it looked to me like the perfect marriage. Gamble would be done in by someone on the inside and nobody would ask serious questions about it. They run a television show, who would suspect the guy that pretends to be emo? But NOOOOOOOOO, he has to go and change his tactics around…Fucking idiot.” In an attempt to console his don, Joey throws himself under the bus. “Maybe Boss…He thought up a plan.” Michael stops dead and stares into the now timid eyes of his lackey. “Joey, get it through your skull for just a second…He…Works…For…Me. Much like you, I don’t pay him to think or to crack jokes. When you work for Michael DeAngelis, you have a certain stature expected of you, and Shakur has fucking termites all throughout his legitimacy right now.” “Well what are you going to do Boss?” Michael walks back over to his seat and plops down into it. “I’m going to lay it out straight for Shakur, he either runs the script that is written for him or I will rewrite the damn thing, and whack him in the process.” “Would you like to call him and say that or fly him in personally?” A malicious grin forms on Michael’s face. “I would LOVE the latter.” “I’ll get right on it.” Joey goes to exit the office, but Michael stops him in his tracks. “Oh, and Joey, has the package been sent?” “Yes, it has boss.” “Outstanding” Joey walks out while Michael folds his hands on the table and gives a wry smirk. …And wish to terminate them before dealing with business... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Team: Braintrust Inside of a luxury mansion, two bitter women stomp around an office with vengeance and torture weighing heavily on their minds. Seldom one will find that they are in a content mood, but this is an aberration of normality. Both of these women are on the defensive, and need to come up with a course of action effective immediately. The circumstances call for it. Shannon Austin and Valerie Swanson are not going to let Nathan Murray, their fellow Braintrust compatriot, die in vain. Neither is their boss who has the back of his leather chair facing the two firecrackers. “I know that you ladies are in the mood for blood, but we cannot do anything yet.” Shannon stops dead and stares at the chair. “Are you kidding me?” “I’m afraid not, dear.” Valerie jumps in, just as angry as her counterpart. “Do you realize what you are saying? To just let this rest and go about business as usual? Are you out of your fucking mind?” …Some consume their minds with revenge… The boss remains cooler than the other side of the pillow. “I never said that we were going to let it rest. I just said that we are going to stand down for the time being.” Valerie on the other hand, remains as red hot as mercury. “How are we going to do…No wait, WHY are we doing that?” “Val, what you are doing is invoking the movie strategy. We’re not going to rush in with our guns blazing red with rage. That is how you get killed, and we don’t get a second take if we botch this.” “I understand that, but-“ Now, the boss decides it is time to assert his control. “No, I really don’t think you do. A cop in real life doesn’t put his gun in front of him and walk into a room. That is like putting bells on your body and trying to move around without making a peep. Devin is going to be lying in wait for us to come back at him a second time. He expects it out of us by now, and I don’t want to give him anymore fuel to indulge on. Yes, we lost one of our own because of his lusting fantasies, but casualties are to be expected.” Shannon attempts to stick up for her tag team partner. “And so is retaliation. I say we take that Asian slut of a girlfriend out, that would get his attention.” The boss sarcastically retorts, “Yes, because that is just what we want. Devin to come in screaming mad with God knows who behind him, looking for our heads.” “I wouldn’t mind that actually, would solve a lot of our problems.” “No, it wouldn’t Shannon. Perhaps you don’t remember, but someone almost murdered the two of you after y’all ran Devin off the road. Whoever he has working with him, they are serious hotheads who aren’t afraid to make a move on a whim.” Valerie interjects, “So what are you s-“ “Nathan was a good friend to both of you, I understand that, but you are still angry. You still want to act because common sense hasn’t kicked in yet. If we hang back and rest on the laurels for a couple of ticks, then we can see the playing field after the smoke clears. The war is starting and I want to make the first move, but I don’t want to get caught in a mouse trap. Do you comprehend that? Reluctantly, both women agree. …While others with a...clearer head, hold onto their hand for one more round of betting... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Team: Smith “We fucked up…Bad” In life, Christian Daniels does not often get bested by anybody or anything. He is too strong, too smart, or too intuitive to be deceived. But this time was different. The Braintrust wanted Shakur's blood on their hands and he almost allowed it to happen. If it had not been for younger sister Rachel, let's just say a lot more people would probably be dead right now. “Man, you really need to lighten up.” Chris Smith attempts to console his older brother with a glass of Jack Daniels. The seven foot behemoth slides it back down the bar before fixating his eyes on the ground. “He was that close Con, that motherfucking close. We’ve both had guns pointed at our head, knives touching bones, and we’ve stayed far away from death.” Christian looks over at Chris before placing his feet across the two other bar chairs. …And finally, there are those who are stuck in the past… Chris doesn’t change tone, “Well what this gives us the chance to do is go forward with our strategy. I’m sure they can be neutraliz-“ Neither does Christian, who cuts his younger brother off. “Wow, for someone who systematically plotted to kill his second wife, you sure lost what you once had.” Jason Hardy looks up from his newspaper “…Damn” Christian stands up, showing his sheer dominance over his younger brother. “We’re not going to sit around on our hands and go numb. If they are going to attempt and knock him out when THEY are the ones who hired him in the first place, I don’t know what they are capable of next.” A defiant Chris looks the necessary ten inches up at the tattooed giant that is his brother. “You ever talk to me like that again and I’ll fucking cut your throat when you sleep, no joke. What I’m trying to get at is, if they wanted to off Devin don’t you think they would have done it by now?” “Seriously, don’t put that thought in my head. That’s something we don’t need.” “But look at it from a logical perspective, Jason back me up on this.” Deciding it is not the time to look up from his newspaper Jason replies “Don’t put me in the middle of this. I want to keep my throat and wife.” Christian presents his counter, “The point is Con they did something. I’m not a genius on the subject, but usually you wait until AFTER your hired hitman kills his victim before killing the hitman himself.” “Well the hitman doesn’t usually quench his thirst for death by shooting one of the company’s yes men.” Michael Blaze slams down the glass of Jack Daniels previously poured for Christian and decides to intervene into the scene. “Alright, alright, break this dick measuring contest up right now. You both are on the same side. Now let’s face facts…” Michael throws out his left pointer finger. “One, Devin decided to take matters into his own hands and kill Nathan. In the world of mafia philosophy, that is not going to go unpunished. We should be thanking our lucky stars that they didn’t slit Devy’s throat later that night, or pull heat outside a PRIME venue ala 1985 Paul Castellano Sparks Steak House style. Can we agree on that?” Both belligerent brothers reluctantly agree. …Who cannot let go despite influential persuasion... The left middle finger comes out next, as Michael uses his right hand to move his long blonde hair behind his ears. “Two, yes they attempted to send Devin to sleep with the fishes, but look at where they did it and how. It was on a highway near his house. You are going to tell me that wasn’t a moment of passion? True, he offed someone that was very dear and near to their organization, but he got them off their game plan. We can use that to our advantages.” Michael thrusts out the triumvirate finger. “Three, we didn’t lose anything. Sure, Devin is out 300 g’s for a car, but that is external pish-posh. An abrasion and a scratch here, but he’s still in the ballgame.” Christian looks over at the business major of the group. “Alright, I’ll give you some crumbs there, that’s an argument. But how in the hell are we going to pull something? You know they are going to be on high alert all over the place.” For the first time in a long time, Michael Blaze formulates an evil smirk on his face. This is so abnormal that Christian and Chris both look at each other like a computer nerd in between the spread legs of a w…Ok, bad example, but the point is established. “I got some people that are sages in the field of the unexpected. We are going to hit them where it hurts, and we’re going to hit them hard. If they want a war, then they have got one. Somebody get Rach on the phone, I got a plan in my noodle.” …But some see the prize and move forward… Jason flips a cell phone to Michael Blaze and the Fried Chicken King of the World starts to work on his plan. Christian puts his hands under his head. “If he’s got a scheme…Shit, someone pass me that Jack.” ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ …Battle lines have a funny way of being drawn… …No two are ever identical in shape or structure… …But once the plans are set into motion... Team: ...Rachel A crisp wind whispers over downtown Raleigh and all is quiet. It is so calm that nothing is stirring, not even a mouse. Well, unless you count the two individuals standing behind a warehouse attempting to be inconspicuous. One is a woman who stands about 5’8 tall, and looks like a dead ringer for Kate Beckinsale, circa Underworld. She is Rachel Smith, Devin Shakur’s guardian angel. The other is a man dressed in an Armani suit that could pass for five billion dollars in human skin. A transaction transpires between the pair, with the man receiving a large brown envelope, and the woman receiving a black carrying case. Both parties look inside of their respective compartments to find their desired prizes. For the man, fifty thousand dollars For Rachel, a rocket launcher “How good is it?” Rachel asks, her eyes transfixed on the carrying case, staring at the contents with an inspector’s eye. “It’ll do…” The man draws a pause and smiles down at his creation, almost like a father talking about his son. “…What you need it to do.” Through her raven hair, Rachel stares into the man’s eyes to get any kind of information about his words. With his debonair arrogance, the man inquires “How good a shot are you?” “The best,” She replies, placing her hands on the case. “Then you don’t have a thing to worry about, my dear.” Rachel takes the case and places it under her right arm, giving the man a nod before walking off. …There is no going back
