Preggos are to be Conquered.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: CWMC Round 4c The moon is nothing but a sliver--not even noticeable in a passing glance--and provides no light to this gloomy evening. Arizona is a desert, and while Flagstaff passes through the seasons somewhat unaffected, during the summer she too suffers the same heat from the unforgiving sun that her sisters do. People assume Arizona to be a dry heat. The problem is, they've never lived here. During Monsoon Season the consistent showers, that are at other times rare in the desert, evaporate into the warm air. When the rain clouds depart, moisture begins to permeate and leaves the natives widely unpleased. It isn't just the locals that get upset. Danny Ferguson, a visitor to this state, expected nothing more than Melanoma-inducing infrared and Mexicans. Instead, he's greeted with humidity and pine trees. On the premises of the local Embassy Suites he enters a room on the ground floor, because hotels in Flagstaff rarely go higher than a second story. At first he's hesitant, as most hotel doors remain locked while the room he enters is propped open by a portion of the comforter. He opens the door wide to allow in a beam from the streetlight, revealing the space inside to be completely trashed. When he peeks his head in, he finds the air has a sickeningly foul stench. "My God," he says upon entering, retrieving a handkerchief from his jacket pocket to filter his breathing. The door slams behind him, and once again he finds himself in the absence of light. While darkness is difficult to navigate, the situation isn't helped with the humidity causing Danny's tailored suit to stick like another layer of skin. "I know you're in here, Face. What the fuck!?" He calls out, his arms swinging wildly to maintain balance as he stumbles over random clutter."Get your ass out here! You think this is a gesture out of kindness? Get real, dip-shit Ð I'd sooner resuscitate an O.D.ing Courtney Love than check and see how you're doing. We're teammates. My success is intricately linked to yours, and when you have problems, the List has problems." For a split-second, Ferguson sees movement in the darkness; a collage of black and lacking colors with a shadow passing through. He ceases his movements, hoping to amuse his partner enough to reply. "Come on, Dick, this isn't like you. You want people to call you Emo and shit? Start sending you iTunes Gifts in the e-mail? You can download that 'Hey There Delilah' song and be like every other asshole, jumping on the bandwagon more than two years after the song's release. Then you can compare playlists with Vangelus Olsig and paint your fingernails black. You'd like that, wouldn't you?" He shouldn't taunt the darkness as he does, but there is a confidence about Danny Ferguson that never falters, even against the unknown. Maybe it's the fact that the man lurking in the shadows weighs half of his amount, but in the back of his mind he knows not to put anything past the Face-Eater. He's somewhat upset at himself for appearing, believing the trip to be worthless, until he sees a figure in the room. Looming, not entirely massive but scary all the same (who knows? He might have a knife), the man moves in the dark with ease Ð unaffected by the clutter on the ground. "I've failed, Danny. I... couldn't seal the deal." "Failed?!" Ferguson begins laughing. "You are the Face-Eater! People cower in the presence of your one-hundred-forty-pound mass! And you're afraid of two Global jobbers and the Mom from 'What's Eating Gilbert Grape'?" "What? Fuck that, I'll kick those fools' asses." The shadows reply. "'Was gonna say, that's not the Face-Eater I know. He'd threaten to shoot their prostates with a bottle-rocket, or something." "That's actually not a bad idea." A lighter ignites in the shadows, revealing the tiny frame of the Face-Eater with a joint dangling between his lips. After a moment, a smoking cherry is all that's seen in the darkness, as well as the common stream of smoke that graces the moonlight, piercing through the front window. "Thing is, that's not what I'm worried about." The shadows take a deep breath. "There's this Preggo bitch. And I've been trying hard, Danny--real hard--but no matter what, I can't seem to put my hand down her pants! At the rate I've been progressing, I'll see the fetus' first birthday before I see bush." Danny scoffs in reply to the whines of the shadow. "Pish posh! This kind of talk is for people like Peter Vetra, not successful winners!" "You don't know what it's like! She's all infested with estrogen or whatever. Like, all she did was cry about her missing boyfriend, and now she won't stop yapping about her baby's health. It's getting pretty depressing." "You know what those sound like, my good friend? Excuses. You're just saying this because you're getting lazy -- because you're slipping. You found solace in your reputation as 'Facey, the PreggoSlayer,' but now you're letting the renown slip through your fingers. What happened to you? I thought you wanted to be the world's most famous Fucker of Pregnant Women?!" "I still do! It's just...." The shadows take a looooooong, cherry-burning breath. "It doesn't sound like you do." Danny turns away to head back outside, standing from his seat amongst the junk. "You were once on the path to become a Legend, Adam; perhaps the greatest this planet has EVER seen." Danny takes a few stumbling steps, injuring his shin and knees in the process, but he ignores the pain in an effort of maintaining the dramatic tension between he and his partner. "It's time for you to make the choice." The door slams shut, leaving the shadows alone... to breathe, thinking their thoughts.
