How Veljumin Rippa Came To Be

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1997 In Sweden, green hillsides and mountainous landscapes create sweeping, vast terrains in a country known mostly of its’ hockey and fish. Definitely not know for its’ world-class wrestlers. On this beautiful Swedish day, however, school is not in session as many young boys and girls run along the hillside. Many are holding hands and clutching each other in romantic embrace. The innocent lips of kids in love meet as if it will be the last chance. Helga: Oh, Hans. I love you. I can’t wait to go off to Africa and join the Peace Corps together. Hans: And I, love you, my sweet Swedish princess. Everybody leaving Sweden after school is so great! The couple kiss a kiss that could knock the Earth off its’ axis and then run over the hill, out of sight. One young man walks along the hillside with his burly frown stretched over his mug. His deep blue eyes carry a look of sadness and being alone. He runs a hand through his thick blond mane and looks to the skies. Veljumin: I hope I find love some day, Lord. Will you help me? Please help me. Hans and Helga bump into Veljumin. Hans smirks at the burly brute as he passes and his gal scoffs along with him. Hans: S’cuse us, Minny. We’re off to go be in love. Helga: Deep in love! Blahhh! Veljumin’s shoulders drop as does his self-esteem. His first inclination is to run home. His next is to pummel everyone he sees on this dreary day. He notices a rain cloud roll in. Dark, thick, and full of rain it stops over the big boy and pours down a rain that only Zeus himself could stir up. Veljumin: I hate love. Suddenly, a stone hammer slams into the side of Veljumin’s head. His eyes roll into the back of his head and he begins to seize and shake on the ground for a while. The apparent owner of said hammer comes running up. Veljumin rolls over and shakes his head as the lousy no-gooder kneels in concern. Veljumin: What the Hells? The last person who hit me like that ended up a dead... Veljumin throws his arm up to block the sun from blinding him anymore. He even lost his train of thought. The blinding light disappears to Veljumin’s surprise. His curious eyes peer over his sleeve, in fear it may just be a passing Condor. Veljumin: …girl? The sweet Swedish accent cues a giggle from the girl. She kneels down and lends him a hand. Unfortunately for her, this boy is bigger than a baby bear, and quickly yanks her down to the ground. She doesn’t appear to mind though. Veljumin: I think I have blood in my brains. Heidi: Let me look. She touched the side of his head to start a grimacing hiss from the brute. A frown and pout smears across Veljumin’s innocent young face. She leans in, softly, and kisses it. Veljumin stops everything and stares at her. She is smiling at him. Veljumin: You are quite beautiful, miss. Do you do that often? Heidi: (curious pause) You’ll waste your breath to flatter me, boy. I’m leaving for Africa in three days and- Veljumin: I meant the hammer. Is that how you meet all the boys? Heidi looked down at the hammer and grinned. She had forgot all about it but Veljumin had to get a better take on this girl. This had never happened before. They shared a long silent moment that seemingly explained everything. Veljumin lost his frown and scowl. She removed her gloves and dropped the hammer. Heidi: (spiteful eye) What’s your name? Veljumin pulled his hand away from the side of his head and lay back as the girl lay down beside him. She looked deep into his eyes and smiled. Veljumin perches his head upon his fist. Veljumin: Veljumin.., Veljumin Rippa. My friends call me Ripp. What do your friends call you? Heidi: Heidi. You can call me anything you like, though… She leans in again and kisses Veljumin’s cheek. This time she grabs his hand and stands up. Rising to his feet, Veljumin follows her as she begins to run up the hillside. He stops abruptly and jerks her directly into his solid frame. His eyes look over the hill. He looks down at her. Veljumin: Is this... are we... Heidi: I’ll always be your girl Veljumin. Veljumin: Africa? Heidi: Africa is only six months. I will be back before the snow falls and we can snuggle up beside the fire, together. Veljumin glares into the distance. Her warm, soft hands on his cheek cause his head to drop down and match his eyes with hers. She smiles. Heidi: I’m coming back Veljumin. Because I love you. A strong, proud smile spreads across the blond brute’s mug. He grabs her hands. Veljumin: I’ll always love you, Heidi. Always. EIGHTEEN MONTHS LATER... Veljumin sits in a dark, desolate room with nothing but magazines, torn up pieces of paper and empty bottles of Svedka strewn around. His hair is ratted and tattered. His shirt is wrinkled and crinkled. His pants are missing. Replacing them are a pair of red boxer shorts and mid-height white socks. A knock comes to the door. His head snaps to it, peering to his doorway. He leaps out of bed like an owl in the night, soaring through the hallway and to his apartment doorway where he, and everything else, remain quiet. He peers ever so gently through the eye hole. His eyes roll back. He swings the door open. Frans: Hey, Veljumin! How’s it going pal? You don’t look so good. Veljumin: What’s the point of your visit, little man? Frans: Well, you know, still wanting to see how things are going with you and the whole not going crazy talk we had a few weeks ago. You know, going crazy over a woman is no way to live life, is the saying. Veljumin: I’m not going crazy! Veljumin pounds his clenched fists into the wall, shattering several chips of paint onto the floor. Frans: I don’t think she’s coming back, man. That’s all I’m trying to say. Veljumin looks at his long time friend with a glare for a second then changes it to appreciation. Veljumin: You’re right, Frans. You’ve been a good friend. I must say love is a bitch I intend to fix. Frans: To fix? Come on, guy. That’s no way to look at it. There’s plenty of fish out there in the deep blue sea, is the way the song goes. Some joy for you and me! Love is right around the corner. Veljumin’s rock-hard fist crashes in Frans’ glasses and bend the frame so far in he looks like an RV antennae. Veljumin: Fuck love. And fuck joy. Let’s spread...some pain. Rippa’s expression is cold, callused, and unchanged. He glares at his friend slowly slipping down the wall as his face makes that squeeling sound all the way down. He walks out of his residence and into the public domain as one pissed-off, heart-broken son-of-a-bitch who just might be big enough to take down an entire hockey team himself. The elevator ding caught his attention as a similarly sized man appeared after the doors slid open. A grin spread over his face as the man’s eyes filled with fear. Veljumin: You... like pain?
