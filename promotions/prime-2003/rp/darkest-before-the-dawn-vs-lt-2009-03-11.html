Darkest Before the Dawn (Vs LT)

11 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 11th The Afternoon Session "Has anyone ever told you your head is sorta shaped like a watermelon?" "No." "Yeah… like one that’s been turned to stand on its end." "Might explain why I’ve been having those nightmares about Gallagher." "Perhaps." Dr. Ward leaned back in his chair and continued to stare at Kaiser’s dome. "Or like... Bert. You’ve got a head shaped like Bert." "Bert?" "From Sesame Street. Ya know… Bert and Ernie." "What?! I don’t look like Bert!" "Little bit. Very similar cranial structure. A tall, elongated head." "You are a horrible psychologist." Kaiser sunk back in the couch, folded his arms across his chest and pouted. "Aren’t you supposed to help me feel better about my life?" "Sorry." Dr. Ward unfolded his notepad and clicked his pen. " What do you want to talk about?" "How bout a few pointers on what I need to do to beat Lindsay Troy at Culture Shock." "Oh, you can’t beat her." "What?" "You can’t beat her." "What makes you say that?" Kaiser leaned forward, either having trouble hearing the responses… or closing the distance so that he could easily punch the doctor as soon as he’d answered. "Kaiser, last year at this time, Lindz beat me. Fair and square… in the middle of the ring, just minutes before the Halo kicked-off. And since I’m undoubtedly better than you… A=B=C and so on and so on till we get to the conclusion that you’re shit outta luck." "You’re better than me?" "Well, I dunno about right now. My knees are shot and I haven’t wrestled a match in a full year. I did give Chandler Tsonda some advice that helped him beat Tyler Rayne a few weeks ago. But that’s the closest I’ve come to a wrestling ring in 12 months." "I can’t believe you helped Chandler Tsonda." "He just wanted a little advice to help him break Nova’s record-long Universal title reign." The doctor folded his arms and smirked. "And Nova is a dirty hippy douchebag who beat me like 34 times, got me stoned once from just being near his bus, and also got me kidnapped by Jack Sparrow… so I was glad to help Chan beat his record." "What does any of this have to do with me?" "Nothing, why?" "Because I’m not paying you one-fifty an hour to talk about you." "We can return to the subject of you your oddly shaped head, but you didn’t seem too happy about that earlier." "Can we return to the discussion of what I’m going to do to Lindsay Troy at Culture Shock?" "What are you going to do to Lindz at Culture Shock?" "I’m going to make a name for myself at her expense. I’m going to use her spine as a stepping stone to the main event. I’m going to make her pay for assaulting me before the Last Man Standing Match at ReV 187, and it’s not about getting even… this is about getting ahead. I’m going to beat her, not out of anger, but out of respect for her accomplishments, and what crushing her will mean." Kaiser leaned back against the couch, content with the cap he was about to put on his response. "I’m going to pin her in the middle of the ring. Defeat the Queen. I’m going to generally do everything you couldn’t." "Kaiser, there’s more wrong with you than I thought." ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The Misunderstanding in the Morning "Debo, you’re killing me." Kaiser adjusted the Bluetooth ear piece, and slipped his cell phone into the pocket of his jeans as he headed across the parking lot and into the bank. On the other end of the conversation, Ronald Debrosse, Kaiser’s rather lousy agent, pleaded his case. "I know, Kais. I’m working on it. It’s just that… I may have complicated things when putting your contract together. "Complicated? This should be cut and dry, Debo." Kaiser entered the bank and began to maneuver his way through the rope maze, taking his place behind an elderly woman waiting at the front of the line. "If I held a title for 200 or more days, I’d be due a $25,000 bonus. I had the Intense title for 200 days. I’m due twenty-five grand." " Well, when typing the contract up, it’s possible I made a mistake with the number of zeroes involved." "Beg pardon?" Kaiser shoved his hands into the pockets of his leather jacket, growing frustrated with the conversation and the lack of movement in the short line ahead of him. "Kaiser, I screwed up the ‘zero’s in your contract." "You mean I’m only getting $2500?" "No no. The contract says $25,000. But I may have added a zero with the number of days…" "What?" "Technically, your contract states that you’re due a bonus of twenty-five grand at the end of a title reign lasting two-thousand days or longer." "Two thousand?!" "Yeah, guess my finger got stuck on the zero key a little too long. So, ya know… 200…2000." "Arrrgghhh!" For the first time since entering the bank, Kaiser lost his cool, raising his voice from its whispered state. Jamming his finger forward in his pocket, pointing for added emphasis (that Debo couldn’t see), Kaiser went off. "If I don’t get all the money, I swear to Christ I’m gonna kill you!" "Shit! He’s gotta gun!" Kaiser heard the scream and lifted his gaze, locking eyes with the Teller in front of him. He followed the look of panic on the man’s face, tracing the bank employee’s stare to Kaiser’s hand in his jacket pocket. Looking down, Kaiser noticed his finger jutting an infamous point through the lining of the coat, and he looked back up at the Teller to explain his case. "Wait a min…" It was too late. An alarm rang out as bank employees hit the floor, hiding behind the tall counter. "Please don’t shoot!" "I’m not going to…" Kaiser stopped and pulled his hand from his pocket. "It’s not a gun. I’m not trying to rob…" "Dude, what’s goin on? Sounds pretty crazy on your end." "I can’t talk, Debo." "Why not?" "Cuz apparently I’m robbing a bank." "Be patient, Kais! I’ll get you the twenty-five grand. You don’t have to resort to this!" "You’ll never get away with this, man. Think better of it!" The Teller seemed to be gaining courage. "You’re right." "Of course I’m right. Wait… what?" The Teller, hands raised above his head in hostage position (even though no one had told him to do so) scratched his scalp. "What did you say?" "Have you shot anyone yet, Kais?" "I’m just gonna go ahead and leave now." Kaiser said it to his agent and the bank-teller, slowly turning and heading for the exit, before the police arrived. "No wonder I’m in therapy." ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Back to the Afternoon Session "People in Ohio are crazy." "People everywhere are crazy." Dr. Ward peeled the wrapper from a Snicker’s bar and took a bite, talking while he chewed. "You ought to see how they drive in Michigan. And in Montana… everyone owns a 4x4 pick-up truck that’s worth more than the shithole shanty they live in. Every group of people everywhere has something about them that makes them nuts to the rest of the world. You’re a professional wrestler. I can promise you the everybody thinks you’re crazy." "They may be right about that." Kaiser stared off at one of the pictures in the doctor’s office. "You’ll be fine. You’re on the right track… taken the first step. You had a breakthrough yesterday, and I felt we needed to capitalize on that." "And so you squeezed me in for another hour this afternoon." "Yup. And I’ve heard a lot of good things today." "Well it hasn’t been a good day." Kaiser shot an annoyed look at Dr. Ward "Sounds like I’m out twenty-five grand because my agent can’t let up on the ‘0’ key. I damn near ended up in a scuffle with the cops, and you claim I can’t beat Troy. Oh… and the thing about my misshaped head, too." "You need to put this thing with Lindz behind you." "How do I do that when the match is just around the corner?" "Simple… you understand that you screwed up. You apologize for being a jackass to her, you let her know that she’ll never have another problem from you… and then you go out there and try to be the best wrestler who’s ever laced a pair of boots. If that’s enough… great. If ya come up short… the world keeps spinning on its axis. I promise you." "I guess you are an expert on getting beaten by LT." "Yup." The doctor folded his notepad closed and tossed it on his desk. "So believe me when I say… it’ll all be fine, and it will get better. You’re on the right track." "Doesn’t feel that way." "And it may not at Culture Shock if you step in that ring and come up short against Lindz." "Feels like things are getting worse." "Then you know you’re doin’ something right, cuz things always get worse before they get better." ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The Shitty End to a Crap-Filled Day Kaiser stepped out of the small café, letting loose a satisfied sigh, thanks to one delicious buffalo chicken flatbread sandwich. As he made his way up the city street, he fumbled with the keys to his rental car, trying to unlock the door as he approached. "What the hell?" Just as the headlights flashed to signal the car had unlocked, Kaiser noticed a small piece of paper stuck under the driver’s side wiper. Plucking the object free, he opened to find a ticket staring back at him. Immediately, his eyes shot to the parking meter, which displayed a large red "EXPIRED" where he’d hoped to find two or three minutes remaining. "Shit! You’ve got to be kidding me. Could this day get any worse?" In frustration, Kaiser wadded up the ticket and marched in front of his rental, heading for a trashcan on the sidewalk. Tossing the meter-maid’s fine into the garbage, Kaiser smirked. "Three or four lousy minutes." Satisfied with his act of defiance, Kaiser turned to head back for the car. And then something caught his eye. Stuck to the light post next to the trashcan, amongst the flyers advertising countless bands playing at every bar in town, was a single image of a different sort, posted for the safety of the city, courtesy of law enforcement. One artist’s sketch, and the pertinent information to accompany it. With a sour taste in his mouth, Kaiser headed back for his car, unlatching the driver’s side door. "I don’t fucking look like that."
