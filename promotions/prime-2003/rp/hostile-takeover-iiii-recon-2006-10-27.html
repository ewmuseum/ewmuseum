Hostile Takeover IIII: Recon

27 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/10/2006 The icy night air cuts into me like a machete through hot butter, as I walk the same streets the fourth time over. My thin black jacket does nothing to keep the cold out and I’m beginning to wonder why I bothered putting it all, on for the good it’s doing me. However, in this area of the city, I could hardly walk around in my knee-length woollen business coat. Such a display of opulence will make me stick out like a sore thumb and tonight, I’m going for stealth rather than comfort. Right about now, Aeryn’s deeply embedded within the new gang that’s sprouted up over the last month and I’m here to back her up. Thing of it is, they’d only let one inside and so I have to wander the streets and wait for her to emerge from the lion’s. I would complain that I don’t want to be here, because I have better things to do and a rather important match at Great American Nightmare, but I don’t really feel too put out by this. Normally, when Stakiri’s ropes me into one of his schemes, I do it kicking and screaming, but this time, I don’t have the energy -- nor the desire. Hell, the more time I get to spend with Aeryn, the better. That area avenue of pleasure has dried up for me recently and I have no idea why. I would suggest that it has something to do with Charity Manale, but there something there long before that slut wandered aimlessly into my life. Regardless, I knew she didn’t like the situation between Charity and I and I can’t blame her. Charity does have something of a reputation when it comes to men and her being my manager automatically puts the notion of a new fuck buddy for Jonathan Winters to play with into Aeryn’s head. It’s an understandable, but it isn’t like that. I’m only keeping her around to watch my back and to royally piss Killean Sirrajin off. I’ve told her as much numerous times and I think, deep down Aeryn believes me. So what the fuck is her problem? I shake the thoughts away. I’ve been trying to figure out how Aeryn operates for the better part of two years now and truth be told, I’m no closer to attaining the goal now, than I was then. I probably never will understand her, but I can live with that. I round the same corner for the fifth time that night and I sigh. Fuck. It shouldn’t be taking this long. Aeryn should’ve been out of there fifteen minutes ago. I can’t walk past the place again, even though they’re not the sharpest knives in the draw, they’re bound to make me out sooner or later. Fuck. Where is she? For the past month or so, my employer, Cornelius Stakiri, had been targeted by a rival organisation and earmarked for a hostile takeover of sorts. Might sound on the level when it is cast in such a way, but when it‘s put another; a rival company is trying to muscle in on his territory and he wants to hold onto his power regardless of the consequences. Pure and simple. Stakiri is a wealthy business man with fingers in a lot of pies and most of his legal and illegal business gets done on the streets, with the aid of his enforcers and a ruthless streak that’d make Pol Pott look like Ghandi. Stakiri had been through things such many times before and fought, maimed or killed any opposition that stood in his way, but whoever’s behind the current string of attacks is a completely different animal. They’ve already managed to break into Stakiri Towers and get to my illustrious employer with relative ease that made Stakiri’s set up look like amateur hour. It embarrassed him more than anything else he’d ever experience, I wager, and now Stakiri wanted his revenge. Stakiri had received word from the streets that a new gang that’s arrived in town may have substantial backing that could lead him to whoever the hell was behind all those attacks and Aeryn had gone into lion’s den, so to speak, to gather sufficient information. The place will be hit by a few of Stakiri’s hired hands later on that night. I stick my head around the corner of the building and study the place intently. There’s no sign of life at all. What the fuck is she doing in there? Is she in trouble? Should I go in and check it out? No. Aeryn can take care of herself and she’s more than capable of getting out of most sticky situations. If I storm in there like John Wayne with nothing to lose, I could blow the whole thing and get us both killed. I lean back against the wall again and allow my mind to become occupied on more important things. The Great American Nightmare to start with. I am to defend my newly won PRIME Five-Star title against the likes of Tony Gamble and it’s former master, Chandler Tsonda. Gamble and Tsonda again? Seriously? I’ve fought them both before and I’ve defeated them without any great difficulty. The only difference this time is, I have to face them both at the same time. The bookers probably arranged the match, thinking this will be more interesting than all of the other battles we three have fought combined. But it won’t. The outcome will remain the same. Another win for Jonathan Winters. You didn’t need to be clairvoyant to see that little snippet in the pipelines. I mean look at them. Gamble is just a glorified thief with delusions of capability. And Chandler Tsonda, well he’s something else entirely. Some weird Vietnamese guy, a little too much into his feminine side, who I can’t for the life of me figure out. But then, I don’t need to figure him out. I just need to knock him on his ass and get the three count. It’s that simple, or it should be. But things have a tendency to get real complicated, real fast most of the time. It’s Sirrajin. He’s a wildcard in all of this. I spoke out last week and showed that fucker precisely who I am and that I’m not in awe of his over-inflated ego. In his own deluded little mind, he may think he’s the messiah PRIME has been looking for, but in reality, he’s nothing more than a convicted thug with delusions of greatness. He’s done things in the past, won the PRIME Universal title or some such. But what has he done recently? What has he really accomplished in the last few months? The answer is nothing. But how could he? His ass has been in the slammer. Like I say, a convicted thug with delusions of greatness... BOOM! “What the fuck?” I found my muttering, despite the fact that I knew precisely what it was already. Gunfire. BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! I glance around the corner of the building and see Aeryn charging at me full pace, a number of heavies chasing her, handguns glistening in the moonlight. Fuck. As she passes me, she shoots me a playful grin. She’s a wild one, there’s no denying it. “Running would be a good idea...” I match her pace and don’t attempt to look back. Why should I? By the time we disappear up the alleyway and take the fire escape up to the roof of a nearby building, they haven’t got a fucking clue what’s going on. Like I say, not the sharpest knives in the draw. From the building top, we glare down upon them as they wander around aimlessly, scratching their heads wondered what the hell is going on. They don’t even entertain the notion of the building tops. It gets even funnier when the police sirens draw closer and they panic, scattering like frightened mice. And is this was the best muscle the rival company could buy? What is the world of organised crime coming to... “Are you alright?” I ask her, only just noting the crimson stain upon the right arm of her white cotton jacket. She notes my gaze. “I’m fine,” she assures me. Assures me? She’s the one who’s been shot... “It’s just a flesh wound.” “All the same,” I respond, reluctant to wrench my eyes away from the wound. “You need medical attention. I’ll call Stakiri and have his doctor meet us at the office. He’ll probably want to debrief you anyway.” She sighs. “Fuck...” I frown, noting that she’s avoiding eye contact with me. “What?” “I didn’t get much,” she tells me. “They blew my cover too quickly.” “Stakiri’s going to go ballistic...” I add. She nods and looks down at her feet. It’s hard to tell whether she’s actually afraid or disappointed with herself. I suspect it’s the latter. I put my arm around her, making sure there’s no contact with her wound. “...but we’ll deal with that when we come to it. She looks him in the eye’s for the first time and smiles. “So why are you keeping that slut as you’re manager again?” And I thought things were going well. Fuck...
