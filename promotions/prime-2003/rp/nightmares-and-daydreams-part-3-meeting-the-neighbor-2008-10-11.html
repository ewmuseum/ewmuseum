Nightmares and Daydreams - Part 3 - Meeting the Neighbor

11 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 11, 2008 It was simple really. The balled up fist was in no way intended to find it target on his friend's face. That slimy little toad had slipped away and ran out the door amidst the chaos. The bartender was now calming down the rest of the patrons as Killean helped Vegas back to his feet. It was a shot that would have broken the jaw of any normal man and had it found its intended target, there would have been need for an ambulance to be called. "Good thing you don't have a glass jaw." "No shit." Vegas said, clearing the cobwebs from his still dazed head. Killean looked through the crowd towards the door. It had closed only seconds earlier. "Ok, you know I always stick up for you when something goes down, but what the fuck was that?" "I think he knew..." "Uhhhh, knew what exactly? Naw, he couldn't know. How could he man? Clear your head cause that's stupid." "Things are about to change I think... and not for the better." |~o~| A Few Hours Earlier... "I've done all I can Killean. There is no way for me to know who took those pictures of you proposing to Jenny." Frederick Taman had done all the digging he could for The Supreme Machine, but unfortunately he came up empty handed. "What do you mean? You found out the photos were taken from an apartment block across the way." "As I told you, the owner of the suite is an older man who doesn't even watch wrestling. For all we know, he could have been paid off by the real culprit. It's his business what he does with his own apartment. Would have been a small price for someone to spend to get these pictures though. Heard they were attracting large offers." Taman forced himself to keep quiet after seeing the raw emotion in the eyes of The PRIME Choice. He was engaged, it was supposed to be the precursor to the happiest moments of his life. Instead the moments were tainted thus far, looking like a far distant hope instead of the near reality. "Freddie, what is happening to me?" Killean threw his arms into the air as he got off his chair. "I go back to PRIME because I miss it and now, months later, I have some crazy psycho person stalking me and taking pictures of my private life? I'm used to being in the spotlight, used to being the center of attention and normally I'm talking to you because I'm trying to get out of some stupid fucked up situation I caused. Now look at things... I'm here because I'm the victim! Because Jenny is a victim. What a terrific start to this engagement huh?" "It could be worse Killean." "Oh come on. Look can I use your phone to call Jenny about this? My cell is in the car and she wanted me to call after I talked to you." Taman pointed to the phone on his desk as he vacated the office. Killean sat on the edge of the cherrywood, glad to be given the privacy he needed without asking for it. He quickly dialed his home number. Meanwhile... The final curler came tumbling down from her head, her gorgeous locks of red now hanging with a touch of bob. The slight waves were exactly what she was looking for as she observed her handiwork in the mirror. She smiled lightly, opening her makeup bag to quickly apply a light touch up to certain spots she may have missed earlier. The phone rings as she retrieves what she wants from her bag. She brings the portable mirror into the other room with her, setting the phone to speaker so she would still have both hands free. "Hello?" "Hey baby." Killean said from the other end, his voice sounding slightly distraught. "What's the matter Kill?" "Ahhhhh everything. Freddie came up empty... there's nothing he could do." "You're worried about this so much hun. Why bother with this anyways? It was just pictures, it's not like someone invaded our place." Jenny said, pausing in the middle to apply a nice shiny coat of lipstick. She certainly was beginning to look the part of a happy spouse. "It was our privacy Jenny. We were photographed in our own home!" "It could be worse Killean." The Engaged superstar paused long enough to prevent himself from tossing the headset through the office window. He wound up but didn't throw the receiver, instead holding it back up to his ear. It caused a bit of static through the speaker, so Jenny had a clue what he was doing. She smiled a little. "Why does everyone keep saying that?!" "Cause it's true.... ah shit!" "Jenny, what was that?" "I dropped my makeup on the table in front of the phone." "You know how much I hate that speaker phone. I almost wanna slap Vegas and Freddie when they use it." "Chill baby. I'm just getting ready to go out. I met the neighbor and we're going for coffee. She seems like a really nice person. You should meet her." Jenny got up from the table quickly to brush off her clothes. She was interested in looking absolutely perfect, something she always prided herself in. "I gotta go see Vegas at the club. Maybe some other time." "You guys live at that club!" The frustration in her voice was obvious. Killean being the smart ass he was decided to use her own words against her. "It could be worse." "Oooooo. How'd I know you'd say that?" Jenny said, playfully hanging up the phone on Killean. She whistled a little, putting the little odds and ends together so she could leave. Desiree was not a worry. The cute little Sirrajin was spending a couple days with her Aunt and Grandmother. As Jenny shut the door behind her, the eyes on a picture on the far wall rolled a little and became dark once again, like a slight light was running through them for a moment. On the other side of the wall, Jordan also checked her makeup in her tall mirror. She kissed one spot in the middle of it, leaving a red lip print behind. "Oh dearest Killean... you're right. Things could be worse. And they're about to be." |~o~| A Few Minutes Later... Jenny was downstairs by her car. She rolled up her sleeve to check her watch. As soon as she did this, the parkade door opened and with clapping heels Jordan made her way into the lot. Her freshly dyed auburn hair with red highlights was lining her face. She had a pair of rectangular sunglasses on with a medium length black overcoat. She was obviously wearing a skirt underneath as her stocking covered legs fluidly carried her over to where Jenny was standing. The soon to be Mrs. Sirrajin greeted her neighbor with a smile. "Hello Jenny." Jordan said as she approached to shake hands. "Hi... I'm sorry I don't think I caught your name before. How silly." "Oh yes, I apologize." Jordan moved her hair away from her eyes and took off the sunglasses. Her eyes were somewhat cold with a blue twinkle. "It's Chasey, spelled exactly like it sounds. Like Chasey Lain the porn star." She shook Jenny's hand again, obviously content that she could get away with the outright lie. "Nice to meet you Chasey. I'm sorry I haven't watched much pornography so I don't know who she is." Jenny replied, jumping back a second later because a ruffling sound startled her. It came from Jor... Chasey's large bag. The small head of her chihuahua popped out from inside and barked lightly, licking it's lips once again after finishing a treat. Chasey petted her. "Oh I'm sorry, this is Trixy. She normally comes with me everywhere I go." Jenny looked curiously at the dog and then smiled and petted her little head as well. Trixy licked Jenny's hand, clearly enjoying the attention. Oh if dogs could talk, Jordan would be outed then and there. "So, I know we said Starbucks before, but what about Earl's or something. I didn't really have any lunch and it is practically dinner time." Chasey said as jenny stood up straight again, smiling brightly. "Terrific idea. I could do with something to eat too." "By the way, that's a beautiful dress you have on." Chasey said, leading her new friend into her car with a hand on the small of her back. Meanwhile... The door beeped after the code was correctly entered and Killean leisurely entered the club. Vegas was standing at the door to the main club room, watching the set up for the band playing tonight. Large banners over the stage signaled that the Weakerthans were playing tonight, a huge victory for the club. The smile couldn't be wiped off the face of Vegas. Killean approached from behind, patting his friend on the shoulder. "Lookin' good. This should be a hot show." Vegas turned and kept the smile on his face. "Hot? HOT?! This is gonna be off the hook man, I can't wait." He paused, patting Killean on the shoulder in return. He made his way back into the office, closing the door to the club behind him. "What about you? Your banking situation getting sorted. You know I we can't keep these money transfers going on for much longer." "End of the month is what they told me. My credit cards have been reinstated and paid off. Got my cell turned back on along with Jenny's. By the way I'll bring back the club phone tomorrow." Killean went to look out the window into the back, the delivery trucks parked for yet another weekend. He seemed satisfied with the cash flow this week, taking a quick glance at the chalkboard. Vegas poured him a drink and handed the half full glass to Sirrajin. The PRIME Choice took a quick sip and nodded. "It's all coming back together. We can make a final transfer in a couple days, a tad bit bigger than the last. Then we shut it down completely. If my accounts are not cleared up by then, my lawyer will get them open pretty damn quick." "Yeah, sounds good man. How is Freddie?" "He's good but get this... he can't do anything about the picture deal." "I told you that before." Vegas said , taking a long sip from his drink and clearing his throat right after. "Something about bypassing privacy laws for photographers. Why do you think countless pictures of celebrities in their homes are allowed in the tabloids? It's almost the same deal here." "Whatever, I'll get over it. It just pisses me off." "It's cool dude. I'm sure it can't really get any worse now." |~o~| A Couple Hours Later... BANG The door flew open and the knob would have gone through the wall had it not been for the rubber stopper on it. Chasey helped Jenny into the room, as Jenny's foot caught the door, sending it crashing into the wall. "I don't think Killean would want you to wreck your place." Chasey said, helping Jenny over to the couch. The engaged redhead shook her head, trying to catch her bearings. "I'm sorry, I'm not normally like this after only a couple glasses of wine. I must be tired or something." Jenny said, clearly intoxicated from their dinner out. "It's alright..." Chasey said, slipping a blister pack out of her coat pocket. "But you're home now, so no harm in having one more right?" "Why not?" Jenny replied, kicking off her shoes into the middle of the living area. She relaxed on the couch, a little too much for the dress she was wearing. Let's just say she wasn't sitting very lady like. In the kitchen, Chasey was pouring another glass of wine for her and her new friend. The blister pack burst open and instead of a pill, a powder poured out into the glass of wine intended for Jenny. She walked back to the living area and handed Jenny her glass. Jenny smiled and took three large sips from the glass. Clearly becoming more and more intoxicated by the minute, Jenny was turning into someone other than herself. "So, this Chasey Lain, she hot?" "Huh?" Chasey said, clearly taken by surprise by the situation. The powder was working much faster than she bargained on. "Oh, yeah, I think she is anyways. Not that I'm really into women though." "Got a few videos you could put on?" Jenny asked, taking another sip from her wine, leaving the glass a bit more than half empty. She had a devilish smile on her face, clearly not of her own accord. "I might have something. Let me go check." Chasey said, getting off the couch and moving towards the door. Jenny leaned back on the couch laughing a little bit to herself. "Cheap date..." Jordan smiled. |~o~| Back At the Club... Killean was watching the first crowd of the night filter into the club. The WeakerThans were due on stage in less than two hours and Happy Hour was to last until the first guitar rift. Everyone came in quickly, wanting to take advantage of the cheaper drinks before the show started. A few people even had t-shirts, most likely purchased from the band's other concerts or their website. "Dude, this is gonna be a crazy night. How much security do we have?" "No need to worry Killer. The security detail has been doubled for this one. Everything will be perfectly fine." Killean nodded, noticing that it was dark outside and that Jenny hadn't called lately. He just decided to check in with her. He dialed the home phone and waited patiently, taking a sip from another drink. The phone continued ringing, not attracting an answer from the other end. So Killean hung up quickly and dialed Jenny's cell number. It rang a few times and then a click was heard. Instead of the usual cheerful greeting, all Killean heard was a woman's voice. Then he heard a woman moaning out loud. He didn't know what to think, so he called her name a couple times. "Jenny... Jenny is that you? Are you there?" He paused, winning a glance from Vegas. "Jenny? What the..." He hung up the phone, hearing the talking and the moans from a couple of women. "The heck was that?" "It sounded like Jenny was watching a porno or something. She only does that after she's had too much to drink. I'd better go check on her." Killean said, pocketing the cell phone and downing the remainder of his drink. He made his way to the door. "Yeah sure man. I know what you're going to do. Just make sure you're back before the band kicks off!" "No I'm not doing that and definitely. I wouldn't miss it." |~o~| At the Loft... Killean turned his key in the door and opened it. No light from the inside greeted him upon entry. He stepped inside and closed it quietly. He caught eye of a little bit of light coming from the bedroom. So he made his way over there without taking off his shoes, hoping that if Jenny was sleeping that he wouldn't wake her with the footsteps he took. Turning the corner, the TV was the only source of light in the room. On the bed laid Jenny, partially unclothed, her bra the only article of clothing remaining on her. Her messed up hair lined the pillow she was laying on and an empty bottle of wine was on the bed beside her. Killean looked under the blanket, so find that the loose and unclasped bra was indeed the only article of clothing she was wearing. Jenny's cell phone was at the foot of the bed, open and turned on explaining no one answering his calls before. The TV was glowing with an adult movie featuring Chasey Lain. Being a man, Killean would recognize her anywhere. He shook his head and went to go turn off the television but something on the TV stand caught his eye. It looked like a stack of pictures, digitally printed just recently. He picked up the photos and they were rather dark. But he could make out the naked figures of two women. As he flipped through them, he became even more confused. Jenny was in a few of them and so was another woman. These were not old photos. Killean knew that Jenny had purchased the bra only a week earlier and the bra was featured in the first few photos until it disappeared. The photo's were rather adult in nature, Killean looking more confused by the minute. As he flipped through the last few pictures, a bright item caught his eye on the floor. The light from the TV wasn't enough to illuminate it properly but he could tell it seemed to be a bright pink. He put the pictures on the bed and leaned down, picking up the item and quickly dropping it again once he realized just what it was. It bounced and rolled along the floor near his feet as he stepped over to lean down and rub Jenny's hair. "Jenny..." He said as he rubbed her hair a little bit. He moved the wine bottle over to the other side of the bed so he could sit down for a minute. She must have gotten incredibly drunk and Killean really didn't know how he was feeling at that moment. He knew Jenny was not a cheater, nor was she the kind of girl to watch porn unless she had a good amount of alcohol in her system. He didn't know what to think, so he did the safest thing he could do. He leaned down and planted a small kiss on her forehead, the redhead moving a bit in her sleep and quickly settling back into a quiet still slumber. He would wait until the morning to even bring up the issue clearly seeing that Jenny was in no position to discuss just what could have happened. What's worse, Killean wasn't about to do next door and confront the neighbor. He doesn't even know who she is, much less her name or sexual orientation for that matter. He just shook his head and walked back out of the door. He had to get back to the club, but now this situation was weighing heavily on his mind. |~o~| At the Club... Back to the Present Killean walked into the office, catching Vegas off guard by slamming the door. The PRIME Choice quickly grabbed the bottle of liquor off his friend's desk and opened it. Without even pouring it into a glass, he took a few large swigs of the fluid, stopping to cough and catch his breath. The alcohol clearly burned his throat on the way down. "You might wanna take it easy dude, you'll be passed out before the concert starts." "Whatever..." Killean said, taking a few more swigs before returning the bottle to the desk, a good portion less alcohol was now contained inside. He shook his head and made his way over to his chair. "Jenny is the only thing on my mind right now." "Why, what's up? She sick?" "Sick? You could say that. You could also say absolutely wasted on wine, half naked in the bed at home and pictures of her and our 'neighbor' in the bed using a cute little pink sex toy." He made sure to use the quotation hand symbol as he referred to their neighbor, who he still didn't know from Adam. "Excuse me?" Vegas replied, obviously taken back by the weird situation just presented to him. "What, do I need to spell it out? The porno movie I heard over the phone wasn't just the movie playing in the background." "Serious?" "I saw the pictures Dave. Clear as day." Killean said. Vegas didn't say anything right away and then smiled brightly. "ALRIGHT Jenny! Hey, you got some of those pictures with you? I've been bugging my wife to play for both teams for years!" "You can't be serious!" Killean said, obviously agitated by the outburst from his friend. "Oh come on Killer. We're both men, what guy wouldn't wanna see his woman with another woman? It's the ultimate man's fantasy. She took pictures for you to see! Damn man! At least it wasn't another guy right?" "Yeah I guess that's... wait... NO!" Killean moves to smack Dave on the shoulder, trying to hold back a little laugh that the alochol may have brought on. "Shaddu..." He stopped before he could finish the statement. A ruckus from the club was heard in the office and Killean was in no mood for there to be an issue. He signaled to the door. "Gimme a minute man, I'll be right there." Killean broke through the door, most of the people calm and drinking their drinks, waiting for the band to start playing in the next few minutes. Killean noticed a small disturbance in the corner of the club and it just seemed to be nothing more than a drunk patron bugging the people around him. Killean made his way over to the man who was stumbling around a little bit, the people around him pushing him away. Killean took the man by the shoulder and spun him around. "Hey it's youuuuuuuu..." The man said, finishing his statement with drool coming out of his mouth. "I don't know you, but you're causing a disturbance in my club." "I know you though." The drunk said with a hiccup. "How's the wife man?" "Excuse me?" Killean's anger intensified immediately upon hearing the last drunken remark. "She need visits or what? I could do it, I have more time than youuuuuu..." He stumbled and hiccuped again. Vegas made his way over behind the guy and grabbed him by the shoulder. Killean was seeing red after the few off color remarks and didn't notice Vegas behind the man. He grabbed the drunk by the throat and wound up to deliver a punch right to his face. But in the tussle, the guy managed to duck and Killean struck Vegas right on the jaw, sending him down to the ground like a sack of bricks. The drunk guy pushed his way through the crowd, quickly wanting to make an exit from the building. It was simple really. The balled up fist was in no way intended to find it target on his friend's face. That slimy little toad had slipped away and ran out the door amidst the chaos. The bartender was now calming down the rest of the patrons as Killean helped Vegas back to his feet. It was a shot that would have broken the jaw of any normal man and had it found its intended target, there would have been need for an ambulance to be called. "Good thing you don't have a glass jaw." "No shit." Vegas said, clearing the cobwebs from his still dazed head. Killean looked through the crowd towards the door. It had closed only seconds earlier. "Ok, you know I always stick up for you when something goes down, but what the fuck was that?" "I think he knew..." "Uhhhh, knew what exactly? Naw, he couldn't know. How could he man? Clear your head cause that's stupid." "Things are about to change I think... and not for the better." Killean continued to eye the door as Vegas rubbed his jaw lightly. The opening to the song, "I Hate Winnipeg" started up, winning the attention of the rowdy audience as the house lights dimmed and the WeakerThans were ready to make an appearance.
