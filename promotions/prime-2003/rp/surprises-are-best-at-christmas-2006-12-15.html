Surprises are Best at Christmas

15 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-15-2006 December 10, 2006 A few months ago back around Colossus III, not a soul on the face of the earth would have believed that Matt Ward would be in the home of Killean Sirrajin. Something similar could be said for Lindsay Troy as well. None of them really got along, although Killean always felt some sort of connection to Lindsay. He couldn’t put his finger on it but it was there. As for Matt, back those few short months ago, he was prepared to end the career of the PRIME Hall of Fame Superstar. All of the above would not be expected. And around Christmas time, those odds drop even more. But something that everyone in wrestling knew is that you should always expect the unexpected. “Dude, look I’m gonna drop this if you don’t get over here.” Matt said as he was lying on his stomach up in the dusty closet. He was holding up a larger box. The closet wasn’t your typical linen or storage closet. In the front, coats and sweaters hung for easy access to accommodate the unpredictably cold Winnipeg winters. But this closet looked wider and deeper to the blind eye. There was a small ladder to the left that went up to where Matt was lying. He could almost stand on the shelf if he wanted to. And if the closet seemed deeper, Mat was lying down flat on his stomach, his legs stretching back into the closet, never touching a wall. It was a very large storage unit, but difficult for one person to use. “Hold on, this other box is heavy…” Killean replied as he stopped to put another box on the floor. There was something slowly leaking out of the bottom corner of the box. “Damn Charity and her paint sets.” He cursed in a whisper as he wiped the paint from his hands onto the old sweats he wore. He made his way back to the closet but with a word from Matt, he was too late… kinda. “Oh shi…” Matt said as the box slipped from his grasp. It was top heavy so it flipped over before it was to hit the ground. Only it didn’t hit the ground. Instead, the box came crashing down on top of Killean’s head and shoulders. The box wasn’t heavy so Matt wasn’t concerned about Killean being hurt. But the box was an awkward shape and that’s why it slipped from his grip. As the box tumbled over Killean and to the floor, numerous different small items and containers spilled out of it. One such container broke open and showered Killean in… “Fuckin’ sparkles!” Killean’s profanity was rather loud as he glowed in the sunlight that poured in through the large picture windows. Matt could not keep from smirking at the sight of his gleaming friend. “Charity like scrap booking much?” He said as he learned back to grab another box. Killean was not going to be ready for this one, so he balanced it on the shelf so it wouldn’t fall. The Supreme Machine was spitting out sparkles from his mouth and dusting off his face and hair but it wasn’t doing him much good. The work made him sweat a little and caused the unwanted debris to stick to him like glue. “Do you really need to ask?” Killean said as he dealt with his glitter problem. He reached his shiny hands up into the closet to grab the other box that Matt handed down to him. That process went off without a hitch this time. “Two left up here. Looks like Christmas decorations. Seems like we got to the end of Charity’s stuff.” Matt said as he moved to the back of the storage closet. “Might as well pass them down too. Gotta put up the decorations anyways.” Yes it was a very odd situation to witness. But one question remained. Where is Lindsay Troy in all this? “She seems to be sleeping now…” Lindsay said as she walked out of the far bedroom taking a sip of her water. As soon as the shimmering Supreme Machine came into her view, she spit her water out across the room. Killean pointed at her. “Not. One. Word.” He stated with a look of half seriousness and half playfulness. Lindsay held back some form of laughter as Killean went back to the closet to receive the last two boxes Matt was passing down. “Didn’t plan on it… sunshine.” She smirked to herself as she went to the kitchen to get a napkin to clean the water she spilt. Killean placed the last two boxes on the floor in a different spot meant for the Christmas decorations. Instead of taking the ladder down from the closet, Matt just slipped off the shelf and came to the floor with a slight thud. He walked over to where Killean was standing observing the boxes that belonged to Charity. “Charity had a lot of stuff here. Didn’t she take any of it with her?” He questioned as he opened up another box that belonged to her. The box was filled with more scrap booking stuff and some other knickknacks. “No…” Killean replied in a rather soft tone. Matt knew that Charity leaving the way she did bothered Killean to no end and he wasn’t about to push the issue to get any more of an answer. “You know, your daughter is cute.” The Queen of the Ring said as she tossed the wet napkin into the trash bin by the counter and made her way back into the living area. “She isn’t Charity’s though?” “THAT… is a long ass story.” Matt replied for Killean, who nodded in agreement. “Let’s not get into that right now…” He paused, helping Killean move one heavy box that was awkward to lift. It contained more of Charity’s things, all in smaller boxes. “What are you gonna do with all this stuff?” “Most of it is going into Christmas Hampers. I’ve already sent some turkey dinner packages to them. I do it every year.” “Nothing in here is special to her I hope…” Matt said as he moved another box. “Nah. Just her scrap booking stuff and some other things. That big box has some personal items from her mom. I talked to them the other day and they told me to send everything like that back to them. Everything else I could give away to people who need it and can use it.” “But what if…” Matt paused again, still not wanting to strike any chords with Sirrajin. But Killean just shook his head, looking a little more in control of the situation. “Her parents even said I should get rid of the stuff. Leaving the way she did, she didn’t rush to take any of it. So since it’s the Christmas season, there are people who would give anything to have this stuff to make some homemade gifts for their families.” “How many turkeys did you give in?” Matt was always amazed with the generosity that Killean had. Even when they were at each others throats, he knew Killean went home and did everything he could to help the less fortunate. “Vegas and I got together and sent in twenty five turkeys each with all the trimmings this year. We only did twenty last year each last year. So we decided to do a little more.” “One of those is gonna show up on my doorstep I hope.” Killean and Matt turned to look at Lindsay after the unexpected comment. Killean smiled but Lindsay just stood looking at the TV. Something was really bugging her about the situation she found herself in. Killean really didn’t know any of the history of Troy, but as soon as he invited them back to his place and paid for the plane tickets, she seemed to be on edge. Even when he went to pick them up at the airport, she seemed to be extra careful, stopping to look at every little thing that caught her eye like someone was following her or something. Killean had to admit that it must have looked strange for two fairly large men with tattoos partially showing to climb into a long black limousine with one of the tallest women anyone had ever seen. But Killean shook his head away from the sight line of Lindsay, wondering just what it was his new “friend” was hiding. After all, if it was that serious, she wouldn’t have come at all… would she? “Well, if you guys wanna sit and watch TV, I’m gonna start putting up some decoration and in about an hour I’m gonna start on dinner.” Killean said as he opened a box and began to sift through the different lights and garland that was contained inside. “Mind if I help? I really don’t feel like watching TV right now.” Matt said as he opened another box and found the decorations used for the tree. Killean shrugged as they began to unpack their respective boxes. He hadn’t seen some of the decorations for quite a few years because he didn’t decorate on his own last year. He was away participating in the GTT tournament and Vegas and Vivian were taking care of Desiree when they put up the decorations. Although it didn’t bother him, he enjoyed being involved in the decoration process instead. It just made it seem like Christmas more to him. King of Kings would be the last event before the holidays and he couldn’t wait to spend it with Desiree and some of his friends. Perhaps Matt would be there somehow too, but he wasn’t about to push the boundaries of their newly repaired friendship. As Killean hung a pine cone wreath on a nearby wall opposite the kitchen area, Matt was organizing the different things on the couch where Lindsay suddenly was missing from. He was going to ask Killean where the tree was when he heard a door slam from down the hallway. No words were said as both men stood and watched Lindsay come into view carrying the medium sized tied up natural tree with her. There was an empty spot in the room that Killean moved a chair from earlier and she just guessed that the tree was supposed to go there. Killean had also moved the tree to the closet down the hall earlier when they all arrived to make more room for the cleaning spree in the closet. No words were exchanged between the group as they just continued with what they were doing. Every now and then, small smiles were exchanged but not much more. ~x~ Christmas of 2004 it was. I was still living in San Diego at the time and in years past I would have spent the holiday season there. But that year was different. I had gotten together with all my friends before I boarded a plane and made my way for Dayton, Ohio. I was going to spend Christmas with the Ward family. Mary and Matt sat around the large dining room table as the lights from the tree and the numerous decorations shone brightly. The house was beautiful, well kept and suited them to a tee. I kind of felt out of place. This was supposed to be a family Christmas to them and when Matt invited me to be with them, I couldn’t help but think it was partial pity for my situation and that I would get in the way. For the longest time, Matt had been my doctor, my psychologist. He had always made himself available for me whenever I needed to talk about my problems in or out of the ring. It was getting a little bit pricey and inconvenient at times to make my way to Dayton when I was trying to get home to San Diego, but honestly it didn’t matter. I had the money and Matt was always someone I could talk to. To be honest, if it wasn’t for Matt back then, I don’t know where I’d be. I could be lying in a gutter somewhere, never accomplishing my titles in PRIME, never hearing the fans chanting my name. I could be burning in the depths of hell if I believed such a place existed, which I don’t. I could be somewhere else, hiding from my life and anyone who ever existed in it. Really, I could be almost anywhere. However I wouldn’t bet on being where I am right now. That much I do owe to Matt and to some extent Mary as well. Back then, we couldn’t have been tighter. The trials and tribulations that we experienced in the Underground when I was basically hired to take him out were put behind us. I didn’t ask for Matt to be my psychologist, instead I was referred to him. I didn’t catch it when I heard the name Dr. Ward. But I sure found out when I heard his voice as I walked into the office. I saw nothing, but I knew something was up. He turned in his large chair and revealed himself to me. I couldn’t believe it but then I didn’t change what happened and even now I wouldn’t go back and change it if I could. As I sat at the dinner table and shared a wonderful turkey dinner with the Ward’s, I couldn’t help but feel lucky. Lucky to have friends like Matt and Mary and lucky to share the holidays with a couple people that meant so much to me. Sure Matt and I had a falling out when he came to PRIME. Sure we almost killed each other in the ring and out of it. The physicality’s were consistent and brutal, but yet we still felt close in a way. We confused plenty of people when we were seen in the backstage area talking like we had always got along. I have to admit that it was one of the strangest and yet one of the most rewarding friendships I ever had. Crushing one another was the only thing on our minds at Colossus Three… well that and either capturing or keeping the Universal Championship. I gave him my all and yet I still couldn’t keep him down. Finally, he beat me. Yes, I admit it. Matthew Ward pinned my shoulder to the mat for three on the grandest stage of them all. Why can I admit it? Because it’s the truth and my respect for Matt grew leaps and bounds that night. I had spent all of my days from the time he appeared in PRIME until Colossus trying to make him go home. I tried to hurt him enough so that he would leave and be a husband and a father. I tried to help Mary out as I knew she wanted him home. But that night, I realized that I was wrong. Matt was in the business for a reason. The fucker was damn near unbeatable. I had stepped into the ring with him for a couple minutes only once in a tag team match. But Matt and I never were in the ring, one on one in our careers. Over four years passed and nothing. I’m glad to be in the position I am in now. I have once again become friends with Matt Ward, the guy who was there for me when I needed him most. I was friends with Mary again, although I hadn’t seen her since before Colossus. I talked to her on the phone, asking her for help in getting information about Charity while I was being held for a crime I was eventually excused of. I had been reunited with my daughter a couple days before I picked Lindsay and Matt up at the airport. Desiree was my life, my reason for existence. Last year, I had quickly fallen in love with her and accepted her as my own when she was abandoned by her mother. Nothing much else can be said on the issue other than I am ecstatic to be spending my second Christmas with her. The second of hopefully a lifetime more to come. ~x~ The Next Day… Killean had dropped off Matt and Lindsay at the airport only thirty minutes earlier to catch their respective flights. He then decided that for the first time since his house arrest was lifted, he would go to into work. He knew that Vegas would be happy to see him and he knew that the remaining staff members would not expect to see him. He pulled up to the back door of the club and got out of his car. He stood, looking at the building for almost a minute. He couldn’t believe it had been months since he had last been there. His life was finally returning to him after months of wondering if he would ever be free to do whatever he wanted to do again. As soon as he opened the door, numerous heads turned in his direction. There was a couple seconds of silence as he walked through the door. Finally, everyone approached him and welcomed him back. Vegas was slow to get to his feet, still weakened from the heart attack suffered not long ago. He gave Killean a quick hug and had to make his way to his seat again. Killean meanwhile dropped his gym bag on his desk that was now covered in paperwork and dust. Everyone stood around in an awkward moment, almost waiting for Killean to say something. As he sat in his chair and put his feet up, he looked up and froze for a minute. He looked at them all and shrugged his shoulders. Killeah had been known to take control at work when he was there. He was after all the senior partner in the business and had the most money invested in it. But he looked confused, wondering why they all turned back to him as soon as he walked through the door. He then smiles… “Look guys, I haven’t been around here in months. I know how I have been in the past but don’t look at me. Look to Vegas over here. If it wasn’t for him, this place wouldn’t have kept going while I was gone.” He leaned back in his chair and watched as Vegas handed out the deliveries. When all the drivers were gone, Vegas looked over to him with confusion. “What was that about?” “You heard what I said. If it wasn’t for you, we wouldn’t have this business. We wouldn’t have a business for me to come back to.” He explained as he put his feet down and turned towards his friend. “How are you feeling?” “Not too bad, trying to get some of my energy back.” “Well you’re about to be feeling a little better.” Killean reaches into his gym bag and provides a few sheets of paper and a pen. He gets up and makes his way over to sit in front of Vegas’ desk. He puts the papers down on the table and begins to sign one. “What’s that?” Vegas asked in a very curious tone. “Remember back when you came here and we started this whole shindig up? You had to borrow the money from me. To this day, through the money we earn you are paying me back. But consider those days gone man. I own this business 80-20. Consider that to be a part of the past my friend.” He signs another paper. “This form explains two things. First, consider the money you owe to me as paid in full. Second? Consider yourself the majority owner in this little business venture of ours. It’s now 75-25 in your favor. I will become more of a silent partner in most of this. I’ll take more control of the club instead of the delivery service to give you some breathing room. And don’t worry dude. I’ll be here if you need me… anytime.” He signs the third and final paper and turns the forms the other way to have Vegas take a look. His eyes are almost popping out of his head. “But… WHY?! I don’t understand. You helped me get into this and now you are just handing majority to me?” Vegas said. Killean walks to the window and looks out, seeing fresh snow falling from the sky, making a blanket on the road and the cars parked in the lot. He sees his drivers outside, enjoying the fresh snow fall, knowing that Christmas is not too far away. “Why? Two reasons. You have kept this place afloat for the past few months. You deserve it. And…” He pauses and turns back to his gym bag, picking it off the desk and heading for the basement where they have their own personal gym. “… its Christmas Dave. Merry Christmas.” Vegas can’t believe his luck. He didn’t want to tell Killean but he was struggling as of late making ends meat since he was absent from work due to his heart attack. He couldn’t have gotten a better gift. “I have a match to train for. This white fluffy stuff falling outside is not the only Snow I’ll be seeing this week.” He goes for the door but Vegas stops him. “Wait, what’s this third form for?” “The drivers… it’s for their bonus, out of my pocket.” He disappears into the basement and shuts the door behind him. Vegas reads over the third and final form closely. His eyes almost bug out of his head. ”This form certifies that every driver employed will receive a Christmas bonus of $5,000.00 a piece on December 18, 2006. Also, drivers are to submit all receipts for Christmas gifts bought for their family to be reimbursed before the New Year.” Merry Christmas indeed.
