Answering the haters [My First PRIME RP]

7 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06-07-2004 *Every time I get employed by a new company, I get flack from the wrestlers in the back. Sure, there are a few guys that’ll be cool to me, welcoming me into the family. But those guys are few and far between. But no matter where I end up at, I hear the voices of the doubters, the naysayers and straight up haters.* “Boda isn’t anything. He’s been handed everything he’s ever won.” “Who the hell does he think he is? Waltzing into OUR fed, and getting a shot at the top right off the bat.” “He’s nothing. A shell of his former self. I can’t believe he’s being made into such a big deal.” “PCW’s washed up, and so is he.” *Yeah, I’ve heard those and many more ever since I lost a ‘Loser Leaves PCW match’ two… almost three years ago, and have taken employment with other federations. But this is a business. I can see why I get treated like this. Hell, it’s been two weeks since I debuted in PRIME. I’ve got a match this week, against a huge son of a bitch, who happens to be the Intense Champion…………… I can see how some guys in the back could get angry.* “WHAT THE HELL? He’s been here two weeks, and gets a huge match against a champion?” “Where’s my title shot? I’ve been here since the beginning of PRIME, and have not gotten a shot at that title.” “I hope Kincade kicks his ass back into retirement. He doesn’t deserve to come in here, make all that noise like he’s someone that should be respected, and get a shot at a champ” “I JUST WISH HE WOULD DIE” *See, it’s not easy being me. I’ve either pissed everyone off, or it’s all about jealousy. I’ve taken it verbally in every fed I’ve kicked ass in. And dammit… that’s the main point… I’ve come, kicked ass and taken the verbal abuse while kicking that ass. So, I think it’s time I addressed the masses. Let’s get it all out on the table. Because, quite frankly, I think I’ve proven myself over the past 4 years of my career. But some, especially those in PRIME, don’t think so. They think I deserve a spot on the bottom rung, and would prefer to see me work my way up.* “Boda isn’t anything. He’s been handed everything he’s ever won.” Yeah, that’s right. Ever since I was a white, poor kid, growing up in the blackest neighborhood of Newburgh NY, this was true. And yes, it was the blackest neighborhood, because even in the daytime… it was dark out. There were that many African Americans living there. And three white, poor people. I was handed everything, even back then….. I was handed an ass kicking every day. I was handed a childhood of low self esteem. I was handed bread for dinner…because mom couldn’t afford anything else. Get my point? I took it upon myself to make something. I became a high school stand out at basketball. I got myself into University of North Carolina, I helped win a NCAA title for The Tarheels. I made myself good enough to be drafted into the NBA by the Orlando Magic. I got myself kicked out of the NBA, and I got myself into wrestling. I took shit, and turned it into gold. Some one didn’t just walk up to me one day, and hand this life to me. I EARNED IT. I MADE IT POSSIBLE. Ohhh…… They were talking about my wrestling career…. Not my entire life? Well, from day one in PCW, back under the Phantom Booker reign, I was nothing but a near 7 foot jobber. I got handed a lot of beatings. I got handed a lot of humiliation. The fans thought I was a joke, the front office didn’t take me seriously. HELL, I was HANDED a loss to SHAQUILE O’NEAL for Christ’s Sake. Yeah, I got handed EVERTHING on a silver platter in PCW……….. PLEASE. I got handed….. well….. actually I got walloped with a NBA backboard by WILL PERDUE. Yeah, I was ALWAYS treated great. I jobbed in PCW for a whole year. I was treated like a 6’11 pussy. I was handed the title of PCW’s BITCH. But after taking beating after beating, jobbing to the top echelon all the time, I decided enough was enough. I made the front office realize my full potential. I made them notice me. The Phantom Booker didn’t wake me up one day and say ‘Hey, Chris…. You’ve taken a ton of shit from us here. Well, here you go, you’re the main event now.’ There was no magic wand, no higher powers at work. I busted my ass and made the fans and the powers that be realize just what I had to offer….. I did it…. It wasn’t granted to me. And come OSW…. I wasn’t handed the World Title. All right, I know it looked that way on television, because Tyler Nelson was a little bitch. He didn’t want to face me, he knew he couldn’t beat me straight up. So, because he has some sort of deal, he was able to do what he wanted to do. And he was leaving for PWC anyhow…. He didn’t want to go out with some broken body parts, so he took the bitch way out, and laid down. But dammit….. I worked my ass off. I took the flak from the guys backstage, who thought I was in way over my head. I took EVERY challenge thrown at me. I beat two, three guys at a time to retain my World title there. I took a hurricarana from Scott Taylor, off the stage, twenty feet to hard, cold left center field of Safeco Field in Seattle….. got taken away on a stretcher, was thrown down the ramp STRAPPED to that stretcher…. And still walked away with the win. And let me tell you…. I’ve fought Ivan Stanislav….. Meanstreak…..Propaganda….. Vampir Nosferatu…..Duke Williams….. and they were nothing compared to that 20 foot bump. I damn near got crippled that night. But I got up… fought through the pain, and won the match….1…2..3. I wasn’t handed the win…. I earned it. That bump was nasty. Unpredicted. Painful. But I got up, and handed something to my opponent. A Bounced Czech and no title belt. “Who the hell does he think he is? Waltzing into OUR fed, and getting a shot at the top right off the bat.” Quite frankly, this one gets to me a bit. And it goes back to the last statement that I just covered. I think I’m one tough son of a bitch. I think I worked my way into the annals of wrestling lore. I don’t think I am bigger than any one man, but I didn’t work hard, making a name for myself to let it all slip away. I go into each fed I’ve worked for since PCW’s original days, and I continue to make a name for myself. And ya know what I’ve heard over the years? The fans appreciation. The fans love a hard worker, rather he’s an asshole heel, or a cool face. I’ve gotten a following of hardcore fans over the years. So, where ever I go, they turn to and watch. So I’m not going to let the fans down, and have them watch Boda work his way up again. I did it once in PCW, and it was a miserable, long year. I don’t waltz. Hell, I don’t even dance…. Once maybe, but I was drunk. Nothing worse than a near 7 foot muscular man, under the influence of alcohol, trying to do the cabbage patch. Yeah not pretty. And I certainly don’t DEMAND a top spot when I sign a contract to work for a company. I let my actions speak for themselves. I don’t like to sit backstage, and watch the world go round. I like to jump on and enjoy the ride. That’s why I made such an impact two weeks ago. I didn’t step on anyone’s toes to get noticed…. I jumped right over them, while they sat back, making excuses for being held down on the mid to low card. That’s just me… I like to make an impact, week in… week out. Maybe it’s addiction. Maybe it’s a bit of ego… but I like it when the fans cheer for me, because I made them want to cheer for me. And sitting around, backstage, doesn’t make the fans want to cheer for you. I didn’t waltz, fox trot, square dance, running man or even tootsie roll my way to the top of PRIME. I exploded onto the scene and made a bunch of noise. I wasn’t handed this match with Kincade…. I was given it, after I gave Kincade something he wasn’t expecting. ME. MY FOOT. HIS ASS. “He’s nothing. A shell of his former self. I can’t believe he’s being made into such a big deal.” First off, they are all right. I’m not a shell of my former self. I used to be a big time, curtain jerking jobber. That was a long time ago. I shredded that shell, like a snake sheds his skin. I don’t even want to look at tapes of that shell. I didn’t like it, and I make sure, where ever I go, that I never wear it again. As far as being a nothing….. I guess accolade don’t mean a lot anymore. I’ve won the PCW World title twice, the PCW Tag titles twice [with different partners], the PCW Continental title, PCW Extreme title. All those after shedding that former shell. I’m a Hall of Famer that the fans love. I won’t even go into the other feds I’ve won titles in. Go watch a tape of me, check my biography. I’m for real. I’m more than a nothing…. I’m a something to reckon with. I don’t back down from anyone, I look a challenge straight in the eyes and tell it to come on and get some. I still, as much as I love it, find it hard to believe the fans love me the way they do. I never expected to be looked at as a hero. The fans have made me a really big deal. Matter of fact, they make everyone a really big deal. This business is all about how the fans react to you. And the fans love me, and they pop for me, and they treat me like a hero. I didn’t tell FSW, OSW PRIME or any other federation to make me out to be the main event star from the moment I step foot into the company. The fans did it. They hype me anywhere I go. You don’t think I should be a big deal….. blame the fans, not me. “PCW’s washed up, and so is he.” Indeed, PCW is washed up. It was watered down in its ill fated attempt at a comeback. When I first left PCW, I heard that a lot. I heard that since the stars like Meanstreak, Jeremy Howard, Ivan Stanislav all left, that PCW wasn’t a powerhouse like it was. Well, that wasn’t true then. PCW was still the original product, and it was still running strong. The only time PCW became washed up, was when it failed to return earlier this year. As far as me being washed up? BULLSHIT. I’m far from washed up. Matter of fact, I’m still new, and in the package. Matter of fact, Kincade found out two weeks ago just how ‘washed up’ I am. And now this week, he’ll find out some more that just because I’ve been through wars and battles the last four years, that doesn’t mean I’m ‘washed up’. The Intense Champion maybe taking me lightly, but hey…. To each his own. I’m too big to be taken too lightly. *Damn that was a lot to speak on. Listen, the point I’m trying to make is this. I’ve been around the block. I’ve overcome a lot to get where I am today. I don’t stand back, and watch the world pass me by. I make the world watch as I pass it by. And this week, in my first official PRIME match, I’m going to step toe to toe with you Kincade and………… Wait, that’s in Charlotte, North Carolina. Damn, Kincade….. there is something I aught to tell you about myself. I played college ball up the road from Charlotte. In Chapel Hill. March of this year, one of my last PCW shows….. The University of North Carolina retired my college jersey in a ceremony held before our show. Yeah, I know what you’re thinking big guy. “Who cares?” Well….. son, you aught to care. You see, I’m from Orlando. That’s my home, but Chapel Hill is my second home. And the fans in North Carolina love me. Charlotte is Boda country. And you know what…. I hate letting the fans down. So, if you got an A game….. Leave that at home. Because it’s going to take a lot more than an A game to beat me and pin my shoulders in North Carolina. I’ll see you in Charlotte son.* The end………. *What? There’s more? I didn’t answer the PRIME wrestler’s statements? Well, look at that, I forgot about those. Well then, hand tight. I’ll give you answers, retorts and so much more.* “WHAT THE HELL? He’s been here two weeks, and gets a huge match against a champion?” Hell is a funny place. I’ve never been there personally, but seems that it’s always around me. And now, I come to PRIME, and it seems like Hell is here too. Well, at least the others think its Hell. I think PRIME is a great place so far. Not Heaven, but certainly not Hell. As far as this huge match goes. I didn’t say ‘Hey, I won’t sign with PRIME unless I got a big huge debut match’. Not my style. I said it before. I come to make noise. Two weeks ago, I did. Slamming Kincade like that made a lot of noise. Hell, it hurt my ears a bit. The impact of his 300 plus pounds, slamming back first onto the mat. That was some thunderous boom. I didn’t ask for this match. Kincade did. Seems he wasn’t too happy about being manhandled like yesterdays trash. The fans loved it. I found it refreshing. The critics ate it up. I guess a bruised ego isn’t Kincade’s thing. So, he wants to prove to everyone that I’m a nothing. Kincade wants to squash me and put me out of PRIME, before I even get unpacked. Well, sonny…. I don’t know a whole hell of a lot about ya, but that tackle and Bounced Czech wasn’t personal. The ass whooping, Boda Bomb and Boda Drop that’ll be delivered to you this week….. take that very personal. And it will be hand delivered with expert care. “Where’s my title shot? I’ve been here since the beginning of PRIME, and have not gotten a shot at that title.” Don’t ask me where your title shot is. I don’t make the bookings. Stop bitching too. Two things you need to know….. First off….. This match is not a title shot. I could care less right now about the Intense Championship. Kincade is obviously afraid of ending up on the receiving end of a Boda Drop, or else he’d put that title on the line. Don’t really say I blame the guy any way. With the way everyone is bitching about me coming here, I’d hate to be him, and leave Charlotte the ex-Intense Champion. Second off…….. Congratulations on your loyalty to PRIME. You’ve stuck around, sat back and watched PRIME evolve. And now, that I’m here; you want to bitch about me getting a ‘title shot’. Well, stop bitching, and look in the mirror. Maybe the reason you haven’t gotten a title shot yet is that…. maybe you suck? I’ve said it before, and I’ll say it again. This is a business. You’ve got to take the bull by the horns, and make people notice you. I’ve made a name for myself because I do just that. I get up, and take charge. And I don’t suck. “I hope Kincade kicks his ass back into retirement. He doesn’t deserve to come in here, make all that noise like he’s someone that should be respected, and get a shot at a champ” I hope Kincade kicks his ass too. I’d be pretty impressed to see him do it. That’s a very flexible man if he can. Oh, they were talking about Kincade kicking MY ass? Well, then that changes every thing. Listen. Stop being bitter towards me. I’ve proven over and over today that I deserve my accolades. That I work hard, to get to where I am all the time. If I came here and got a World Title shot, and won it in my first match, well, then that would be fishy. But I didn’t. I didn’t ask for it, nor have I ever asked for it. And once again, this isn’t a title shot against Kincade. He didn’t want to put the title on the line, because he was expecting the worse outcome for him. I don’t blame him. And I’m not interested in the Intense title right now. As far as him kicking my ass back into retirement. Hey, I’m not walking into Charlotte and expecting this man to lie down for me. He isn’t Tyler Nelson. He’s a big man, a strong man. So am I. I’m expecting a battle. With no holds barred. He’s the Intense champion for a reason. Because he’s a monster. Because he’s Intense enough to be that champion. So, yes, I’m expecting a battle in the ring. I’m looking forward to it. Him kicking my ass back into retirement? Not going to happen. I’m far from ready to retire again. Been there, done that…. Got bored really quickly. And besides….. I’ve got a lot of noise left in me to make in PRIME. And who knows. After I beat Kincade, I might get a title shot at him. But it doesn’t matter to me either way. Title shot or none. I’m still going to prove to all you in the back, that I’m to be taken VERY SERIOUSLY. I’ll show everyone why I should be RESPECTED. Because I earn it. Everyday. “I JUST WISH HE WOULD DIE” Okay, who said this one? I’ve been here two weeks, and you people want me dead? Wait………. Sorry, that didn’t come from any of you doubters and haters in the locker room. That’s from the ex-wife. She’s a bit bitter at times. Just because I was pleasing a fan…. Female fan…… hottie, with blonde hair on top…… and I bet if it grew back in, it’d be blonde down below too……38DD……19 years old…….could suck a bowling ball through 50 feet of……….. STOP. Sorry. I was just getting carried away. Where was I? [but dammit, that little hottie was really, really good. Definitely worth the grand a month in child support and alimony.] The ex-wife. Well, shit happens. And unlike Kobe, I didn’t try to make up for my wrong doings with a million dollar diamond ring. I screwed up. Cheated on the wife, and she left me. No jewelry for her, but that fine blonde got a pearl necklace that she really enjoyed. *Okay, so I didn’t need to dwell that much. Sue me. You people wanted to know about me. You PRIME stars wanted to learn about why I am here, why I make so much noise. Well, now you know. Oh, and Kincade. That A plus game….. Definitely won’t be enough. Try again. And, I’ll see you in BODA COUNTRY son. You better be ready. I AM. *
