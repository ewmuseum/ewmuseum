Chasing Dreams: Part Seven

11 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 11, 2007 [Editor's Note: This story was originally to be posted last month for the Dual Halo. Unfortunately, this part of the story is just massive and was a story I was trying to tell while over in Global. It's a beast to tackle and I don't want to post a novel. So, I'll be splitting this up into at least two parts. The continuation of my other story I've been telling will be completed soon as well. Just have to tell this part of the story to move forward. Thanks and Sorry. Craig] ---- Previously: Eight months ago, Craig seemingly waked up from a two year coma. The only problem was that it wasn’t a coma as much as it was him not remembering a single thing. To his friends and family, he had disappeared off the face of the planet. To Craig, he had no recollection of his entire history. It wasn’t until July 1st, 2006 when his entire world was turned upside down. It was then, in the early hours of the morning that he met Wayne Arthur Silsbee, a known rapist and murderer. Silsbee had been involved in an argument with his girlfriend Rebecca, and Craig had decided to step in between them. Unfortunately for Craig, he made an awful decision in doing so as Silsbee not only nearly killed Craig, but he would end up killing Rebecca who he had tried to change for. Yet, he couldn’t give up his murderous way he would soon find out. Unfortunately for Craig, as he laid out in the hospital wondering who he was, it would only get worse for him as he was the initial suspect in FBI Agent Daniel Rand’s investigation into what happened to Rebecca. On top of that, Craig would find out that he was married to a woman named Jocelyn White. A woman he couldn’t remember. A woman who had secrets about Craig that not even Craig knows about. She supported him though during it all as Craig was able to clear his name relatively quickly thanks to a letter from Silsbee who was finally outing himself to the world. Rand, who had previously been on Silsbee’s case, was embarrassed by this and renewed his efforts to take Silsbee down. Craig would be released from the hospital where he would go back to Orlando, FL with Jocelyn who was his only link to his previous life. As much as he knew that she was hiding things from him, he needed her more than anyone in the world. If he wanted to get back his memories, he needed her. Thankfully for him, she supported him in his endeavors and pushed him back to wrestling to help find his memories. For Jocelyn, she needed him to remember his past because it held the key to something that even Jocelyn had stayed silent about thus far. Craig would return to wrestling under his old guise of Dusk, and join up with Global Wrestling where he became an instant star, defeating all in his way. However, Craig still wanted his memories, and he had to gain them at any cost. Thus, he searched out for someone who could help him. That person ended up being Master Kosen Thibaut who was trying to not only help Craig retrieve his memories, but find out who he truly is as a person. Thus far, it had been a long road for both men, but Thibaut could feel that the light at the end of the tunnel was getting closer for Craig. To do so though, Craig would have to confront his demons. One of them was Wayne Arthur Silsbee who had left the body of Rebecca at the temple that Thibaut taught at. This would enrage Craig to the point where he had to kill Silsbee or else let others suffer because Craig couldn’t kill him the first time they had met. With Silsbee’s blood on his hands, Craig found himself in the arms of Jocelyn who was scared of what Craig would find out once he got his memories back. So much so that she had hired a detective, Steve, to keep tabs on him. However, he had failed in his job thus far. Thus, it’s Jocelyn’s mother’s job to make sure that he carries out his task. To kill Craig. This is only the beginning for Craig. He knows deep down that the road to find out who he truly is will be a treacherous one, but it is one that he must complete. Even if it kills him. ---- December 2006 The cold had seeped into his bones, yet it didn’t faze Craig as he stood outside of the temple in Monte Visto. It had been a few weeks since he had been here, and truthfully he didn’t miss it one bit. One half of Craig wanted nothing more than to find out his past, remember who he truly was, but the other half of him was scared of what he could find. Craig had just started to piece together his life once again, and he felt that the light at the end of the tunnel would shatter everything into millions of pieces. Yet, he knew he had to continue. He had a daughter that he wanted to see and he hadn’t the slightest clue as to where she could be. There were things he had to repair and he couldn’t do that without his memories. Without them, he was nothing. Even if they ruined everything, he would have to find the inner strength to move forward without the familiarity around him. Craig stood outside of the temple, wearing multiple layers of clothes as the snow slowly fluttered downwards from the heavenly skies and onto the top of his head. He could feel the dampness from the snow as it melted on his scalp. One thing that Craig wished he had was longer hair right now as his current hairstyle, nearly bald as he had taken a liking to extremely short hair, did nothing to help shelter him from the cold. One thing that was certain to Craig was that he had always hated the cold weather. Nothing he tried could change that reality. To the world around him, Craig was dressed in a pair of blue jeans, a black turtleneck sweater, and a long black leather trench coat. Thankfully for Craig, after the first time he had made his way to the temple he decided to make sure he wore more clothing to block out the raging winds. The weather on top of the mountain was known to be rather arduous. After a few minutes, Craig decided it would be a good time to go inside of the temple, and slowly walked towards the door. As he reached it though, the door opened for him, and he saw a man he had never seen before in his life. With dark black hair, this man was the first man he had actually seen in the temple thus far. Craig just simply nodded towards the man who led him into the temple that was relatively empty. Even though Craig hadn’t been there for weeks, he could still feel the somber mood inside of the temple after the death of one of the students there. Silsbee had made his way to the temple with the body of Rebecca Lansing and in the process had killed one of the students there. It was a harrowing situation for Craig who in that moment realized what he had to do, and he did just that as he murdered Silsbee for the crimes he had committed. To his right was a staircase leading to several of the rooms, including his, in the temple. At the top of the stone staircase was a woman he had grown to despise despite his short time there. Melody, with a uniqueness to her that added to her overall beauty, looked down upon him with a stonewalled face that easily covered her feelings for Craig returning to the temple. She slowly walked down the steps as the man who had let him into the temple reached for his bag, which Craig surrendered to him. As Craig looked around the temple, he could tell it wasn’t quite the place to celebrate Christmas, which were just mere days away. He nodded his head at this assessment before turning back towards the stone staircase to see that Melody had reached the bottom of the stairs and was making her way towards Craig. “I see you’ve decided to return,” Melody said to Craig in a disdainful way. Craig just smiled back at her, letting his thoughts form in a way that he could return her sentiments towards him. “Oh, I didn’t realize you had missed me so much,” Craig replied sarcastically towards her, keeping the smile on her face. She though did not return the sentiment. Instead, she turned her back towards him and started to walk way without uttering another word towards him. Craig knew why she was so cold towards him. “You can’t blame me for what happened, Melody,” he began to say to her. Yet, before he could even finish his though, she had turned around and rushed back towards him with eyes of fury. Craig stood his ground though as he had finally realized that he couldn’t put everyone else’s burdens on his own shoulders. He wasn’t responsible for everyone else in the world, and he wouldn’t be held responsible for what Silsbee did, regardless of how guilty he felt. “Yes I can. You brought a foreigner to our home, and look what he did,” she breathed at him in a way that struck Craig as plain odd. He noticed that she was having troubles controlling her emotions and almost couldn’t speak the words that she just had basically muttered. Craig just shook his head at her. “No, I brought nobody here. You want to continue to blame me? Fine. Yet, you should realize that I don’t have control over everyone around in the world. None of us do. Yet, you go ahead and blame me if it makes you feel better. But, you keep placing your anger towards me, and I will slam it right back in your face,” Craig said in retaliation. Then, a voice rang from above. “Enough,” he said and before Craig even looked up, he knew it was Master Thibaut. Craig’s eyes averted back towards the staircase, and his assumptions were correct as the Master stood there looking down upon both of them. Melody and Craig both looked up at him as if they were almost sorry for the disturbance they had created. Without speaking another word, the Master had started to walk down the stairs. The anger in his eyes was evident. Evident enough that everyone left the room immediately, leaving just Craig, Melody, and Master Thibaut. Whatever it was that he had to say, it was going to be bad. ---- A Few Hours Later It tore at him like a million pieces of shattered glass piercing his skin at the same time. His red eyes exploded open as he felt like his skull was being torn open and his brain examined for personal use. Craig’s hands immediately shot to his head as he tried to make the pain stop, but it was no use. He didn’t have the slightest clue as to why he was in pain or where the pain was coming from, but he just needed it to stop in the worse way possible. The pain shot through his body, making every joint in his body scream out in pain. His body thrashed from the pain as he rolled off the bed, sending a large thud sound throughout the entire room. “Oh God,” he whispered loudly, feeling the pain throughout his entire body. He felt like it was death descending upon him, but he knew it couldn’t possibly be. He was at a loss for words as to what this feeling was and why it was causing so much agony to him. Craig slowly starts to pull himself up to his feet, hoping to find some kind of relief somehow. Knowing he needs help of some sort, he slowly starts to move towards the door to his room when things start flashing before his very eyes. Memories. The first one is a memory that leaves him speechless. He can’t find the words or fathom the reality that the memory is. One of Jocelyn placing a gun to Craig’s chest and pulling the trigger. It was so vivid. He could hear the clicking of the gun, the voice of Jocelyn as she chuckled from Craig hitting the ground. The echoes of her feet as she walked away, the pained gasps he made as he could feel the life being sucked out of him, it all hit him at once. His memories were flooding back to him without any sense of warning. The tears starting to well up in his eyes as he wished that it would just end. His hand touches the cold, metal doorknob as another memory hits him right after the first memory is inserted into his memory. This one is of Jocelyn, standing over him after some kind of fight. He can hear her words as if they’re being spoken in present time. I killed her. That’s what she says with no remorse to her voice. Immediately, Craig knows that Jocelyn means that she killed his first wife. He doesn’t know how he knows, but he knows regardless. It tears at him. He wants it to end. The world that he knew was no longer that world. Instead, it was a cruel joke being played upon him. The memories continue to hit him in succession as he pulls open the door and falls outside of his room. His knees hit the stone floor and immediately it echoes throughout the temple. He can hear people waking up, wondering what exactly is going on. To the right of him, the door to the Master’s room creaks open, but he can’t bear to look at him. The pressure in his head, right behind his eyes, is too much and he has to keep his eyes closed as the memories keep exploding into his head and inserting themselves where they belong as if they were files on a computer that he had restored. Master Thibaut slowly walks up to him, hoping to help him to the best of his abilities, but knows that Craig has to go through this on his own. “I’m sorry,” the Master says to Craig, but Craig can’t make a comment. He can barely move. Yet, he can feel the Master kneeling next to him and he can feel the pairs of eyes upon him as the pain just worsens. He wants it to end. Craig desperately wants to stick a knife through his heart and end it all. The pain of knowing the betrayal that he’s experienced by Jocelyn alone is much more than the pain that he’s feeling from the memories exploding into his head. The Master continues to kneel there, trying to console Craig, but it’s no use. “The pain will subside soon,” the Master says, and a stray thought enters into Craig’s mind. He knew about this. He knew this would happen. Yet, Craig can’t do anything about it as the pain continues. It takes Craig a few minutes to realize though that the physical pain is no longer there. It’s just the emotional, mental, and psychological pain. It was the worse kind of pain he had ever experienced before. He felt like everything he knew was shredded as if it was a piece of paper that someone had written upon. “What’d you do to me?” Craig finally manages to ask, hoping that he can finally get some answers. The Master just remains there, without speaking a word. Craig starts to look towards him, finally finding the strength to look at the Master. “What happened to me?” Craig pleads with him, knowing that he has the answers. The Master just looks on frightened. “It was ten years ago. You came here ten years ago and made me do this. You made me put a spell upon you that would have you go through a considerable amount of pain if you ever were to lose your memories. The first part was killing. You had to kill over and over again for your memories to come back to you. You had me do this because you knew that if you lost your memories, you lost them for a reason,” the Master said, despair in his voice. Craig was having trouble picturing himself coming in here and having the Master do this to him, to cause him so much pain. “But, why? Why would I do this to myself?” he asked, perplexed by everything. “Because, you told me the secrets you housed in your head, were far too important for you to even get them back if you ever lost them without making sure you were the same person you had been before,” the Master explained to Craig and Craig just couldn’t talk anymore. The reality was starting to hit him. He knew what Jocelyn had done to him and he wanted death. Yet, he couldn’t bring death to him. So, he would bring death to her. ---- Elsewhere. Two Days Later. Wayne Arthur Silsbee’s body laid there in the morgue, motionless. Detective Daniel Rand could only stand over it, looking down upon it with a sense of relief that the case he had been building upon him was now finally shut. The case that had taken over a year of his life away from him had been solved with a solution as simple as death. Rand owed Craig one. He had made sure to do what Rand couldn’t do and left it ambiguous enough so that Craig couldn’t have any charges filed against him. That made Rand even happier as he would’ve hated to do something like that. With the paperwork sitting next to him, Rand knew that it was time to move on to the next aspect of his life. One that would bring hopefully more enjoyment out of him. Yet, he felt something in the back of his head that wouldn’t make that possible. He happened to get the cases that were next to impossible. He just knew that the next one would be just like that. As he sat there, his heart no longer feeling heavy by the pressure of trying to find Silsbee, he wondered if he could take a vacation. With no open case on his desk, he could probably sneak one in with the New Year right around the corner. Slowly, his eyes glanced down to his watch. Soon, it would be Christmas. Once again, he would be spending it alone. “Well, Silsbee, you fucking bastard, it’s just you and me. For years, you tortured people. Now, you got what’s coming to you. Merry Fuckin’ Christmas,” Rand said, hatred spewing from his voice. Then, he picked up the file and started walking out of the cold room, hoping to get a few drinks in him before he would call it a night. As he stepped out into the frigid, Washington D.C. air, his cell phone began to ring. He glanced down and saw that it was an unknown number. Immediately, he answered it, wondering to himself who it could possibly be. “Hello,” he answered briskly, hoping that it was a wrong number or something along those lines. Unfortunately, it was no such thing. On the other end was a woman who sounded like they had been crying. He knew the voice immediately. “Jocelyn, what’s wrong?” he asked her, concerned with what happened to make her cry. Having met her before, he knew that it had to be something major. He started to fear for Craig’s life. Something must’ve happened. He listened intently as she told him what had happened and what was going to happen. Fear struck his eyes. He had to leave immediately for Los Angeles. “I’ll be there soon. Don’t you fret. If he doesn’t know where you are now, he won’t be able to find you before I get there,” he said into the phone before hanging up to it. Jocelyn needed help and it was his job to do exactly that. Unfortunately, it was the kind of help that she would need that worried him. Because it would tear him in half, a feeling that he had never experienced before in his life. ---- Mere Hours Later The thunder crackled violently outside of Jocelyn’s hotel room as she sat in the soft, comfortable bed in silence. Her thoughts were consuming her as ever since she had heard from hr mother that Craig was coming for her she had been paralyzed with fear. So often over the past few weeks, she had flipped flopped on life and death. At times, she wanted to live. At other times, she wanted death. Now though, she didn’t know which one she really wanted. Everything that she had done up to this point, including sending out Shawn to kill Craig had built up to this point. The crescendo had to reach a limit before it came back down. She knew that one way or another, this would be that very moment. Whenever he reached her door and decided to take her life that would be it. It made her ill. Her insides churned around and around, and she felt like she wanted to throw up. Yet, she didn’t. It was a feeling you get before singing in front of millions or a feeling you get before you face that judgment of whether or not you’re going to be sentenced to death by a judge and jury. It was painful. It was sickening. It was torturous. BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! Her soft brown eyes quickly darted over to the door of her hotel room, the source of the pounding. She could feel the fear caught in her throat as her body fought valiantly for air. The fear struck her harder than she thought it would when that moment came. He was standing out there, ready for her. She would ask him for forgiveness, but she would not get it. She knew that much. He would ask her questions, trying to figure out pieces to the puzzle, but she wasn’t sure she could give him what he was looking for. His anger would grow. His confusion would multiply. He would be wondering about his purpose in the world and the truth of it all, and not even she could give that to him .It was something he had found once before, and it was something he would have to find again. Even if giving him the answer meant saving her life, she knew the destiny of Craig, and it couldn’t be tampered with. She knew her destiny and had tried to change it. It was how she found herself in this predicament. “Who is it?” she finally managed to squeeze out of her voice, but it came out as nervous and fearful, which she had hoped it wouldn’t. The agony came with the silence as she sat there, waiting for Craig’s voice to boom through the door, telling her to open it. To her surprise though, it was a much softer, kinder voice that spoke to her. “Room Service,” the person on the other side of the mahogany door said. The fear subsided. Jocelyn slowly rolled off the bed, remembering that she had ordered some wine to try and calm her nerves. That very thought had left her mind though when she heard the knocking on the door. Instead, her mind had focused on Craig, like it had been for hours now. With a few, gentle steps across the white carpet, she reached her destination and pulled the door open. Her jaw dropped though as she looked outside and saw her worse nightmare standing there, waiting for her. His red eyes blazed like a nighttime fire as they pierced a hole right through her heart. “Craig…,” was all she could manage to say, the very sight of him making her almost drop to her knees.
