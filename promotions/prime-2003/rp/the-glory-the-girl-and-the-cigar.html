The Glory, the Girl and The Cigar

Date: Today Friday, June 24, 2005 The setting sun was shining brightly against the open waters. The waves, loud yet calm were lightly plummeting close to a set of feet as they walked. It would be the last time for quite some time that these feet would walk the beach, touch the sand and feel the crispness of the ocean water in San Diego. The end of an era was coming for The Sirrajin surname, even though there was thought to be only one holder of that name remaining. The name holds quite a bit of history and unfortunately, only one person could claim to have brought the name any success. Not delving too far into the family tree, the parents Sirrajin were nothing short of a cookie cutter family. Yet they were not much more than that either. Summer Sirrajin, loving sister to Killean, had passed on resulting from complications with cancer. Not to forget one of the most important people in the life of the last remaining Sirrajin, Kira. Needless to say she was not born with the name but with the way she lived her life, Killean thought she made the perfect Sirrajin, at least in his eyes. But unfortunately, tragedy struck another fatal blow and Kira was also taken before her time along with the couple’s unborn daughter. Killean had come to think that the Sirrajin surname had somewhat of a curse at that point. All hope seemed to have dwindled and all fears seemed to have surfaced. But even then, Killean fought through all the emotions and through all of the turmoil. He faced his fears and he had defeated them. He now had a very successful career and he finally had returned to his real home in Winnipeg. Now walking through the sands on the beach in San Diego, he knew he would miss it but then he knew he would be making a new start in a place he loved just that much more. Earlier in the day, Killean and Amy were beginning to remove the few things from his house in San Diego that he needed for his new home in Winnipeg. Sirrajin had found and purchased a loft in the heart of downtown Winnipeg via phone from Buffalo. He had yet to see it except for in pictures but it seemed like the perfect place. He didn’t want anything too big because he was currently living alone. With Amy in the picture in his personal life, he had wanted to get something cozy yet something big enough that two people could share should the situation arise. But it had yet to arise. Killean and Amy were just starting their relationship together. Feelings had blossomed following that now epic night they spent in each others company but they had yet to evolve into anything more than a dating relationship. Killean had the idea for the loft in the back of his mind and he hadn’t shared the thought with Amy for fear of scaring her off. He was not even ready to move to any other level with her yet and he certainly knew that she wasn’t. As the final pieces had made their way into the moving truck, Killean was doing the finishing touches on hooking up his car to the back to pull it to Winnipeg. He was just checking the lights to ensure they worked when Amy came along side the open door of the car where Killean was currently sitting. “Phew. I think that was the last of it.” Amy sighed, trying to catch her breath from making quite a few trips to the upper level of the house. “Just out of curiosity, what is in the attic? I saw quite a few boxes stacked up there.” Killean knew the question was going to come sometime when Amy had finally been to his home in San Diego. But he saw no point in avoiding it, seeing it as a stepping stone to move forward. “Kira’s things are all up there. I had done some cleaning in the previous weeks to try and minimize her presence in the house. Even though she is gone, I had to pack up quite a few things just so I would not constantly be reminded of her. It has helped and I have been left with memories that I remember only when my own memory retrieves them. It’s actually comfortable controlling it.” He thought he was rambling on and on as he played with switches on the dash board, but Amy had been listening to every word. She was interested in Killeans life before her, or before PRIME. But Amy thought the discussion had quickly turned too serious. So what better way to lighten up the mood than with a joke right? “Oh Mr. Fixer of all things that hurt, please share some more of your mind boggling ideas.” She laughed as she said it and Killean turned to her, smirking and playfully poking the dashing redhead in the ribs. Yes, this is how the new couple got along in their first week together. Killean was the willing participant in the relationship but it takes two to tango. It was always going to be up to Amy where she wanted to take the rather surprising events of the previous week. But deep down, Killean could not help but wonder if this is what Amy really wanted. She laughed, she played, she joked and she had fun with Killean. The time they had spent together as of late was nothing short of amazing in both of their minds. They got to know each other pretty well. But that was also causing Killean to think that Amy could have been a tad confused when they made the decision to become an item. Getting to know her also involved understanding her. He was not quite to that level as of yet but he had come to understand a few things and he could see some confusion on Amy’s part. But he wasn’t worrying about it too much because a previous conversation covered a very important part of communication in the relationship. It was agreed that no feelings would be hidden from the other. If Amy felt different about the relationship, she would tell Killean. But it was the fact that Amy felt this way since the beginning and truthfully, that’s not considered a change… is it? “Amy, thank you so much for helping me. I cannot thank you enough.” He turns to hug her and she hugs him back, trying to see who could squeeze more even though she had no intentions on matching his strength. “You’re welcome. This was actually fun and I enjoyed spending the time with you.” She smiled genuinely at Sirrajin and he returned the smile. “Plus it keeps my mind off other things. I explained to you what gives me that certain ‘high’ and I guess one of those things is also human contact that helps me avoid lonesome which in turn keeps me away from… you know…” “Say no more Amy.” Killean sounded comforting and caring, something he had been yearning to be since his last days with his deceased wife. Amy completed him, well almost completed him. Yet there was still something missing and no doubt was attributed to the possible confusion of the Red Raver. ~o~ Dream Sequence ~o~ It was ReVolution 68, it was the Arrowhead Pond and it was a hell of a night. Te crowd was on its feet and Killean Sirrajin was standing tall in the ring after delivering a mind numbing spine buster. Brandon Youngblood was outside the ring battling with Nova and Vangelus Olsig was just recovering from said spine buster. But it was being lined up. The crowd knew it was coming. Nick: I can feel it; he is readying this arena for The Supreme Justice! Just as The Universal Champion was about to charge at Olsig to finish the match, the crowd reacted in confusion as Amy Campbell, the Red Raver, entered the ring and stood between the two men. Killean looked confused; staring into the eyes of Aimz, the woman he called his girlfriend. Olsig was still trying to brace himself on his feet, leaning on the corner turn buckle for support. Aimz was about to out the secret. She was about to reveal to the world that she was with the Universal Champion. Killean could not believe his eyes. Aimz came closer and in a heartbeat it all came crashing down. She reeled back and planted a swift kick with her right leg where no male would want to be kicked. The crowd gasped and Killean fell to his knees, holding his jewels and trying to protect them from further annihilation. Aimz drove out of the ring and just as fast drove back in, steel chair in hand. Olsig could not believe his eyes and the referee in the match was too busy outside the ring trying to break up the scuffle with Brandon Youngblood and Nova. Aimz steadied herself and just as swiftly as she brought her foot up into Killean’s nether regions, she spanked the fragile Sirrajin across the face with the cold steel. It ricocheted off his face and flew out of Amy’s hands and landed outside the ring. The image was played in his mind millions of times. The sweat, the blood and the tears all pouring from the Supreme Machine after that destructive chair shot. But it wouldn’t stop. It just kept getting faster and faster. The chair shots were getting more and more forceful. Aimz delivered it, why did she do it? Why did she betray him? Why? WHY AMY WHY? Suddenly, a startled and sweaty Killean woke from his slumber, sitting up like a dagger in bed. He had a shocked look on his face and his breathing was quickened. He felt like he had had a stroke. But it was just all a terrible dream, wasn’t it? He looked down at his hands and then looked around the hotel room that he was staying in. He was on his way back to Winnipeg. Amy was not there with him. She wanted to head home and Killean got her the plane ticket. Looking beside him confirmed just that. But why this dream? Why did his mind wander enough in sleep to show Amy, the girl he now trusted, betraying him? It was an image he could not dismiss from his mind. The walls appeared to be moving and the foundation appeared to be shaking. Everything may have been still, but to Killean, the ground moved. His world was being shaken and it was about to fall from its axis. Sirrajin began to panic and he held his head in his hands, hoping for it to stop. But the world was not being friendly that night and it did not stop. He fell back against the pillow holding his head and screaming for it to stop. “Stop! God damn it stop this! Please!” He begged and pleaded but it was no use. The intensity of his thoughts hit him like a ton of bricks and he was down and out. He had lost a piece of his heart before when his sister was taken from him because of a disgusting disease that has no cure. He had lost a piece of his heart before when his wife was taken from him, months before she was going to give birth to their first child, a daughter. But there was not reason for all this to happen. All Killean ever wanted was an explanation, a reason as to why the most important people in his life had to be taken from him at such a young age. His wife, his sister, his unborn daughter. It was all too surreal to return, flooding back to his memory once more. But the heartbreak was the worst part. But now, with confusion hanging over his head, would he lose another person he came to care about? Amy was an incredible young woman. She has worked her way into the heart of Sirrajin. He knew he was falling for her. But this time, he had to brace himself for impact. No, no one was going to be taken from him like before. It would not happen again and he would see to that. He would not see Amy until ReVolution on Wednesday but that was good enough. If Amy was confused, it was coming from her mouth. She would explain and she would release it all. Killean would not have another person he cared about snatched away without any explanation. An explanation he wanted? An explanation he may get come ReVolution. Sunday, June 26, 2005 8:00 AM Killean was driving down Interstate 15 just outside of Salt Lake City, Utah. After a terrible nights sleep, he was sucking back some cappuccino he purchased at the last truck stop he saw. Making it to Winnipeg was his goal and he would then fly out to Anaheim for ReVolution. But he was driving his moving truck, pulling behind a lifetime of memories and his pride and joy, his Chrysler Crossfire. But moving was not the only thing on his mind. “You know, I would love to shake someone’s hand right now.” He was speaking as he was doing his best to avoid the wrath of the terrible drivers he has experienced in Utah thus far. “Someone has stolen God’s Title. What a great gift just mere weeks before the big match at Colossus II. Hoyt Williams and Killean Sirrajin will face off once more on the grandest stage of them all. But Hoyt will not have his precious title that was supposedly given to him buy his father Duk… I mean his religious father. Now Lord knows I am not a religious man, but bow to the person I will who stole that piece of tin obviously manufactured by the same cheap company that designs Karina Wolfenden’s ten cent suits.” The Champion took a break from speaking to honk his horn at a jackass in a little Honda who just cut him off. One thing he hated more than religion was a snotty little kid driving his jap crap car like an absolute psycho. “Hey I’ll be honest, I wish I had that fake, cubic zirconia encrusted piece of pleather sitting right here in my gym bag, but I don’t. I wish I had it sitting back somewhere in this moving van but I don’t. I even wish I had it dragging on a chain behind my beautiful car back there but hey, I can only do so much. I will still shake the hand of the person who did it, if they decide to ever come forward.” Killean could not help but shake his head and smile. As if the loss to Olsig wasn’t enough, Hoyt now lost his title. What a blessing for Killean come Colossus. Hoyt would be way off his game. He just knew he would be victorious at Colossus. It was destiny for him. “More pressing issues are to be dealt with. Brandon Youngblood is my partner in the friend or foe match come ReVolution. Nova and Vangelus Olsig had better ensure they are not foes because Youngblood and I have an important thing in common, respect for the squared circle. That will be the battleground for us come Wednesday. Now given that Angelo Deville seemed to coax a cripple out of retirement for one last time, the show will be stolen as two accomplished athletes and wrestlers attempt to overcome a chain smoker and a reborn child yet to fully mature. I mean truth be told, Nova smokes more cigarettes than Karina smokes of Angelo’s half sized Cuban cigar. But we all know why it is only half sized, because that damn black window Karina ate the other half!” Killean stopped at a gas station just off the highway before Salt Lake City. He parked the moving truck in a secluded area. “All joking aside really, what is his match really about? It’s about respect. The main point is that Youngblood and I have respect for the business, respect for each other and respect for our talent. Nova, what have you and Olsig got? Yeah that’s right, just as much as Hoyt has around his waist in gold… absolutely nothing. End of story.” Killean hops out of the truck and walks through the hot Utah sun towards the gas station, having the match in mind. But he also had Amy in the back of his mind and perhaps in his heart as well. What was she thinking?
