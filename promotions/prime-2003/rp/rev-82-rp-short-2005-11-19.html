ReV 82 RP (short)

Date: 11-19-05 I bet you’ve all been wondering where I’ve been in the last couple weeks. Well, two weeks ago, I was involved in a horrific car accident on the way to ReVolution 80. Some jack-off cut in front of me, making me crash into a semi. A ten-car pileup pursued, killing three people while injuring nine, and I was one of the nine people injured. I managed to walk away from the wreckage, breaking one rib and cracking two on the right side. I swear my ribs are the bane of my existence. Now, two weeks later, against doctor’s orders, I’m coming back to face Paul Cain in some ultra violent, cheese-tastic match directed by action director, Quentin Tarantino. Danny Ferguson has gone off the deep end on this, making this week ReVolution Hollywood-esque. We need a Hollywood-esque show like we need a fucking hole in our head; it’s fucking useless. But who am I to complain? I’ve been itching to go back into the ring for two weeks, whether I was injured or not. So I’ll participate in Danny’s little game. Might as well give myself something to do: savagely destroying people left and right, climbing further up the ranks to superiority. If it means playing Hollywood, then I must. No one’s going to pilfer my greatness without an altercation, and Paul Cain is in my way to greatness. Tomorrow night, he and I will participate in a bloodbath. That’s not a threat...it’s a promise... Lights... Camera... Action! Where am I? What am I doing here? Those were the words floating in my head as I came two, finding myself in a concrete room. It appeared to be the size of a small basement. There was a TV setting on a stand with a Nintendo gaming console underneath it, resting against the left wall, with a very worn couch in front of it. There was a refrigerator resting in the corner of the right wall, humming gently to break the silence of the room. On the south wall, there was a rickety-looking shelf filled with non-perishable food items, cans and other delectables. And on the north wall, there was the entrance to the basement. The entrance was pitch black, as if all the all the lights upstairs were turned off--- Wait a minute, I’m in my old basement! I’m in my childhood basement! But how could that be? How did I end up here? I thought I was at my home, mediating. Ah, yes. I soon remembered that I was in my sub-conscious, mediating, but why did my mind simulate my old basement from my childhood house? I didn’t know. Perhaps if I leave the basement, I’d wake up, return to my own reality. I was about to leave the basement until something caught my eye. It was a naked body of a boy, about thirteen years of age, with his wrists tied to a hanging apparatus above him. His head hung forever. His feet dangled a few inches off the ground. I slowly walked toward the body. Upon further inspection, I discovered who it was, taking aback at the discovery in front of me. The boy was me. I felt a scream forming in my throat, choking it back down as best as I could. The scream escaped as a whimper, as my eyes searched the teenaged version of me. Memories came flooding back; there were days when I was in the basement, naked and defenseless, waiting for whatever punishment that was about to come. I would hang there all day, sometimes all night, wanting to scream, but no one would hear me. No one would come to my aid after my punishment was felt. I never did anything to Steven or Rachel to make them mad. All I did was mind my own business, then they throw me down the basement, strip me naked and tie my wrists to the ceiling. “You will stay here and think about what you did, you fuckin’ whelp!” Steven would scream at me. There were only two punishments: physical and sexual. Steven would come down to the basement, use me as his personal punching bag. All I could do was give into the pain, or pass out from it. Then he would chuckle, say some obscenities to me, get a beer from the frig and go back upstairs. Rachel’s punishment was a lot worse. Sure, Steven would use his fist, but Rachel would use a large, rubber phallus. She would strap it on, sodomize me until I either bled from my rectum or ejaculated, and she would laugh at me. Laugh at me and hold her massive, rubber dick in the air, taunting me, belittling me until I was broken mentally. From a sexual standpoint, I would feel inferior. Images of Steven assaulting me... Images of Rachel laughing victoriously, holding the bloodied rubber strap-on in the air... Get out, get out, GET OUT! The images were gone, but the body of my former self remained in the room. The next thing I see was a red line, starting from the sternum and going all the way down to the abdomen. To me, it looked like someone was making a slow incision from the sternum to the abdomen. What struck me as odd was that no one was in the room with me. All of a sudden an arm shot out from inside the body, making me jumping back a few feet. Another arm appeared, as if someone was trying to pull himself out of the body. A few seconds later, another figured emerged from the teenaged body, dripping blood, intestines and other gory entrails. The figure was me, except the face took on a demonic look. His eyeballs were black, with red irises and orange pupils. He was walking toward me now, staring at me with his fiery eyes, chuckling at me as he ventured closer. A malicious smile curled the ends of his lips. “Welcome to my humble abode, Simon,” the demon said. “Your humble abode?” I replied, looking at the demon in front of me. “I believe this ‘humble abode’ is the far regions of my mind. Why are you here? And why did you simulate this place in my head?” The demon stared at me, his smile broadening. “Because this was your home. You had good memories here---well, I had good memories here, after I made you kill your foster parents and sister.” “After you told me to,” I replied bitterly. “Really? Sure, I played a huge role into the murder of your family, but think about it, Simon, how did I come to play? Why did you start hearing voices after your parents beat you on a regular basis? It was those years of abuse that made me, and you retaliated, Simon. I pushed you into the right direction. Now, I’ve brought you here to put an end to this.” I looked at the demon. Put an end to this? What did he mean? Did he really thing he could kill me in my mediated world? “You can’t hurt me here,” I replied with a sternness I wasn’t feeling. “Oh, but I can,” the demon replied. “When you enter into your sub-conscious, it’s all legal. Lest you forget, the body cannot live without the mind. You’ve been trying to get rid of me for years, keeping me in a quarantined portion of your mind. But I broke out, broke out and I’m here to haunt you all over again. Now I’m here to return the favor!” He started to roar, then started to change in front of my eyes. He was morphing into the man I despised for eight years of my life: Steven Kreed, my father. Now the demon had taken the shape of Steven Kreed, standing six feet, six inches tall, weighing over three hundred pounds of beer belly. His eyes were blue, his head was bald and he reeked the smell of death. His head was sewn on, as stitching went vertically down his face. Even his limps were sewn back on after I chopped him into six pieces a decade ago. “Hello, you fuckin’ whelp,” Steven said, looking down at me. “We meet again. Didja miss me?” I swallowed a large lump in my throat, staring at the man who once killed me by picking me up over his head and throwing me down the stairs, where I broke the right half of my ribcage. If it weren’t for Emily Kreed finding me in the basement, I would’ve died there, choking on my own blood. “Can’t say that I have,” I finally replied. “It’s a pity that you have to haunt me ten years after I killed you!” “You brought it on yourself,” but I immediately cut him off. “No, you brought it on yourself! I never done anything to do to receive eight years of abuse. What the fuck that I ever do to you?” Steven smiled, exposing all of his yellow teeth. “Because I wanted to,” he answered. “I wanted to give you a taste of what my old man did to me. My little brother would get me in trouble, and I never did anything wrong either, but did my old man believe me? No! He would pound me good, call me a fuckin’ whelp, and pound me some more. I wanted to kill my little brother for what he did to me, but he died before I got the chance. I turned my attention on you. When you got older, the pain would begin. Unfortunately, I didn’t get the job done the first time. Now, I’m gonna put you away for good!” With one felt swoop, I felt the hardest backhand I’d ever experienced, sending me a few feet backward, crashing into the TV and stand. I gingerly got up, looking at the man with a fiery rage. “That was a big mistake,” I roared, charging at the tub of lard. I threw a punch at him, expecting to hit the fat fucker, but I hit nothing but air. To my surprise, he moved to my right with lightning speed reflexes. “Too slow!” I heard him say. Then he kicked me hard in the ribs, grabbed me by the throat, and tossed me into the rickety shelf, having it collapse around me. He’s too fast, I thought to myself. I can’t defeat him! “You’re right,” Steven said, as if he were reading my mind. “I’m too fast for you. You can’t lay a fuckin’ finger on me. I told I was gonna put you away and I’m gonna fulfill my wish!” He leapt up in the air, and in slow motion, he pressed all of his three hundred plus-pound weight on top of me. I’m trapped...there’s nowhere I can go, no one to help me. I started to scream. His large palm was coming toward my face. Ultimately, I succumbed to the darkness... Darkness and screaming.
