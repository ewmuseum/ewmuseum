At The Movies (Match RP - ReV 71)

1 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 1, 2005 Who am I? A question asked a million different times of a million different people. Soul-searching, self-seeking people unsure of why they are here or what, exactly, the meaning of life is. What’s the point of it all really? A chunk of rock spinning around a sphere of burning gases… who cares? Everyone has asked this question of themselves at least once. “First Blood” Daniel Pierce, though, has asked it a million different times of himself. Who am I? An orphan, a failure, and a wrestler. Who am I? An Orphan. Daniel’s father, Darren, died the day of his son’s graduation from Medical School. Daniel was crushed. Daniel had been surrounded by so much tragedy and death in his life that he sought to make his father happy through his success and achievements. He was Valedictorian of High School class a full year ahead of schedule and he graduated Magna Kum Laude from Medical School. Nothing he did could ever seem to earn him a “well done” or “good job” from his dad, no, his father. There’s a difference between the two and Daniel had never known the former. Maybe if he had he wouldn’t feel the incessant need to validate himself, he wouldn’t constantly feel inadequate, and he wouldn’t always have to overcompensate. Maybe he could just be content. Ahh, contentment – could such bliss e’er be achieved? Daniel Pierce doubted it. Who am I? A Failure. The sweet song of success always seemed greeted by the deflating discord of downfall. You already read how Darren Pierce never affirmed his son’s achievements – no gifts, no speeches, no hugs, not even a simple pat on the back. As good as Pierce could do it was never enough. Even though he had experienced much success in his life there was failure. The name “First Blood” was given to him after drawing blood from 43 consecutive opponents… he only beat 20 of them. He was Pacific Coast Wrestling’s Heavyweight Champion for 5 months with only a handful of title defenses and his loss finally coming at the hands of a punk kid. A part of the story Pierce would rather have forgotten. But instead he bought the fed out and released the kid. Revenge for the loss? Yes. Release from his shortcoming? No. Not even close. Even in PRIME where he reigned with Ignatius Lisieux as Tag Champion for 43 days his success was moderate. A record of six wins and 11 losses haunted him. He couldn’t escape it. He’d had a record four-match win streak, but he’d also had a four-match lose streak, capped off at Colossus II against Vangeleus Olsig and Trashcan Man. He’d have a shot at Trashcan Man this week, but Olsig would have to wait for another day. Of course that’s not where Pierce’s revenge list ended, but it had to start somewhere. And so many people from the list had tucked tail and ran that he’d likely never have his shot at them anyhow. Boda and Vampir for pushing him aside during his brief stint on the Dark Age. Ignatius Lisieux/Richard Larken for allowing Pierce to carry them through the Tag Division then turning around and beating him in a First Blood Match – insult and injury. Then, of course, there was Killean Sirrajin. The recently retired former Universal Champion who beat Blood in the Glass Ring Match. These men would go by unavenged… but Trash and Olsig… their time was coming. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~Our Hero, Daniel “First Blood” Pierce is sitting in front of his computer monitor watching tapes of his matches, looking for flaws, looking for holes in his strategy. He has his heart set on making his game the best it can be. But Eve is none too impressed. Eve: Daniel Pierce! You get your butt off that couch and take me out! First Blood cocks an eyebrow and spins ‘round in his chair to look at the face from whence the voice came. Pierce: What are we dating now? You can boss me around? Eve puts her hands on her hips and gives him ‘the look’. Pierce: Where would be go anyway? You know I’m broke. I’ve been out on injury for longer than Scott Woods has been losing matches. I’m broke-ass poor! Eve: Now that’s just not true. You’re sitting on tones of money left ot you by your father. Now take me out! Pierce spins back around in his chair. Pierce: You still haven’t told me where we might be going. Eve will have none of it and turns Daniel’s chair around again to face her and she offers up a suggestion. Eve: How about the movies? Pierce: Fine, I guess… which one? A gleam appears in Eve’s eye as her choice of film comes bubbling out of her. Eve: Must Love Dogs!! Pierce: What? Is this going to be like The Sisterhood Of The Travelling Pants? Pierce replies with a wince on his face and a groan in his voice. But Eve remains undeterred. Eve: God I hope so. That was such a good movie. And she wore the pants to the wedding. So sweet. No, this one’s about a girl who’s recently divorced and teaches kindergarten and all her friends want to set her up but she’s all like, “No way!” and still everyone’s trying. So anyway her sister eventually sets her up on the internet and then this guy— Pierce: Meets her, they have a good date, they have a couple more dates and then, OH NO, another guy comes along!! Will she go with him or stay with the first guy? It’s looks bleak until the last five minutes of the movie when she races across town to stop him from getting on that train!! Eve: So romantic. Pierce: This sound like the chick-flick of all times. Did the new guy, Kyle Lamen talk you into this? ‘Cause he’s a little on the fruity side… Eve, rather than replying, smacks Pierce on the shoulder and walks out of the room. Eve: The show starts in 45 minutes. I suggest you get ready now. Pierce: But I’m studying for the enormous Battle Royal coming up this week! Eve is now even within eyesight of Pierce anymore, but she calls back from around the corner someplace. Eve: You need a break from all that, Daniel! GET READY! Pierce rolls his eyes and shuts down the computer. He walks over to his coat which is laid across a chair at the other end of the computer room and reaches into the pocket. He pulls out a prescription drug bottle, pops the top and downs one of the pills… it’ll be a long night. He may need help retaining his sanity. Pierce slips on the jacket, completing the look of his slick red silk suit, and waits at the base of the stairs for Eve. Evenutally she comes down wearing a suit of her own, black pin-stripe and smiles at Pierce. Pierce: Damn! You look sharper than Trashcan Man’s decline of success! Eve, slightly confused at how to respond since she was the one to promote and endorse Trash’s bid for the Intense Title, laughs it off and hooks his arm with hers and allows him to escort her out the door. We’ll fast forward here, because no one needs to hear about starting the car and driving down the road uneventfully. We’ll try to keep this interesting and at a reasonable length, we’ll leave rambling and snowballing to Dave Gibson. ;) Some Time Later… Eve: There’s a spot! Pierce maneuvers the car to try and get to the spot, he pulls up and… Pierce: It’s handicapped like Tyler Lopez! Eve: Let’s just go park towards the back! Wouldn’t that be easier? Pierce sneers, not necessarily at Eve, but at the situation and maybe a little at her constant naggings. Pierce: Eve, darling, could you please just let me park that gaddam car!? Eve: If you keep this up I won’t let you kiss me at the end of the date. Pierce: Now I know how Wolfenden made Deville feel. That’s cold. I don’t think you were gonna let me kiss you anyway. Eve: Neither was Karina. Eve smiles coyly, and contently, knowing that she’d cleverly de-escalated her date for the evening. Eve gestures at a spot in the next lane that an older man is just about to pull out of. Pierce whips the car around in response and cuts off several vehicles to ensure the spot. He stops to the left of the other car and puts his blinker on. Just as he does the old man put his hand on the shifter and looks up into his rearview mirror. Old Man (John): What’s this? His wife looks up and sees the car waiting to pull in. Martha: What is it Jonathan? John: This guy wants my spot. Martha: So give it to him. We don’t need it anymore. John: Yeah, but he can’t just come claim my spot. I haven’t even left it yet! This… this! This just will not do! Honey, hand me my roadmap! Martha, confused, rifles through the glove box and finds two, very poorly folded, roadmaps. Martha: Which one do you want I have two— John: Both, please. Back in Pierce’s car he’s beginning to get upset that this is taking so long. Pierce: Good gawd! This is taking forever! It’s like waiting for Paul Cain to do something noteworthy. C’mon!! HOOOOOONK!! HOOOOOONK!! John: Oh, yeah? That’s it! I’m re-folding these roadmaps perfectly. I don’t care if it takes 30 minutes! Martha: Could you at least turn the engine off? Gas is at 103.9 per litre. John nods and turns the engine off the goes happily, and angrily, about folding his maps. Martha rolls her eyes at her husband of 30 years, thinking he would rather drive away than turn the engine off. Martha: John you can’t possibly be serious. Let’s go, please. We had a wonderful evening, let’s not ruin it. John: Wonderful evening!? HA! That was scarcely enjoyable! I paid $25 for the tickets, $20 for the food and I had to watch Must Love Dogs – a movie only slightly more enjoyable than reading the transcript of a Peter Vetra promo! This is my fun for the night! Martha: Fine. Are you going to be long? Should I call a cab? Eve is equally frustrated in the other car. Eve: Daniel! He obviously doesn’t want to give you this spot. Let’s just take the one over there, it’s just as good AND it’s closer! Pierce: Yes, Eve, but I’ve already claimed THIS spot. He has to come out eventually! Eve: He’s old. He could die right now. Pierce: I can wait. Eve: The movie starts in ten minutes! Let’s go, please? Pierce doesn’t even answer this time. He just stares intently at the car… his anger ever growing. His eyes widening… watching as the old man continues folding his roadmaps! What… the… HELL!!?? Pierce: I’m going over there! Eve: Daniel please! But before she could even get the words out Daniel’s door was opened and he was outside the car. He walks with purpose over to the man’s car. Martha: Great! Now he’s coming over here. John: He is!? John gets up and pops over his door, he grabs the doorframe and pulls himself up out of the car with a groan. John: You wanna fight to impress that girl, sonny? Don’t be a hero, now! Pierce: Let’s go, Geriatric Man! Dum-da-da-dum! Pierce walks in with his right fist, pounding the old man in the gut and jawing him at the same time. Pierce: Up! A punch to the gut! Pierce: Up! Another punch to the gut! John: AND AWAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAYYYY!!! A swift cane to the side of the head. John smiles and gets back in his car and drives away as Eve runs over to sit beside her fallen date. Eve: Sorry, Daniel, but you got beat like Token Weed. Pierce: I thought you were going to sayJohn Edwards. Eve: That would just be mean. A smile is exchanged as the welt starts to form on Pierce’s head. Eve: Can we park the car now? Eve helps Pierce to his feet. Pierce: Do you think I have a concussion? Eve: How about we test that out by getting you to park the car? Pierce consents... and parks the car. Then the two get out and stroll into the theatre. Pierce: Hey Eve, are you sure you don’t wanna see the Dukes? Eve: You just want to oogle Jessica Simpson! Pierce tries to play dumb, but an actor he isn’t. Pierce: Wha—is she in this? I wanted to see the good ol’ boys! Eve: The Flying Vergomovs? Pierce: The only thing I like about them is that they provide me with a solid restroom break. They’re always good for one or two minutes. Eve rolls her eyes, double-checks the show time and physically pushes Pierce up to the counter and orders for him. Eve: He’ll take two for Must Love Dogs. The tickets are printed and handed to Pierce but Eve quickly snatches them and walks over to the ticket-taker. Pierce opens his wallet and pays out the $25 for a show he doesn’t even really want to see. Alas… women have that control over us as men. If the battle of the sexes were a wrestling match, men would be The Super Duper Masked Jobber king III. Pierce catches up with Eve and enters the main part of the theatre. Eve walks up to the snack bar followed shortly by Pierce, still skeptical at the whole thing. Eve: Aren’t you going to ask me what I want? Pierce: Aren’t you going to tell me anyway? Eve: No. Pierce: What do you want? Eve: Nothing. Pierce: What? But you jus— Eve: I can’t afford to get fat. Besides, it’s your money. Pierce: Are we seriously doing this? What do you want? Eve: I said nothing… besides I don’t have any money. Pirece rolls his eyes and grits his teeth. His head still hurts from the old man’s cane shot and he would really rather just get the whole movie over with. Pierce: Okay, let’s just suppose for a minute that you had your own money… what would you buy with it? Eve: If I had my own money… not yours? Pierce: Yes. Eve: A large popcorn and drink combo. Pierce: But that’s like 15 dollars! Eve: Hey, it’s my money. Plus it comes with a free Kit Kat bar! Pierce: Gimmie a break! Eve: Ha ha! That was lame, Mr.Lame-O! In her head she imagined that joke being like putting Vangellus Oxios, Clyde Walkins, James Farwell, Tom Walczak and Kan Kaze in a ring and watching the look at each other all unsure of what to do next. (Hey, it’s hard coming up with stuff for everyone when there’s eleventy-billion new people in this match and a few don’t even have bios yet… slackers!) [Eleventy-billion’s not even a number.] (Yet!) [*rolls eyes*] Cashier: Hello, sir. Would you like to try our Famous Feast Special? Pierce: Yeah, no. I’ll just get the large popcorn-drink combo. Please. The teenage lackey who bears a striking resemblance to Chris Collins, and moves about as quickly, goes about getting the popcorn as another grease-laden teen pours a watered-down fountain drink. The cashier places both items on the counter. Cashier: Thank-you. NEXT!! Pierce starts to walk away when Eve notices they’ve forgotten her Kit Kat. Eve: Hey, they forgot my Kit Kat. Can you go grab it? Pierce rolls his eyes and hands Eve the popcorn and soda then walks back to the counter, amid hoards of people, he fights through the sea of humanity to get to the counter. Pierce: What are you guys giving away some Captian Morgan or something? Cashier: You mean from the Pirates Of The Carribean? Pierce: No. That was Jack Sparrow. Cashier: Oh, right… then you must mean the wrestler. Pierce: He’s a wrestler? No! I was looking for a shot of rum. This bloody movie will be the longest 90 minutes of my life. I’m sure of it. Cashier: What can I do for you? Pierce: My Kit Kat bar? Cashier: I gave it to you. Blood’s blood is beginning to boil. He reaches across the counter and grabs the pimply young man by the stupid bow-tie he’s wearing and pulls him up off his feet and halfway over the counter. Pierce: What’s your name, punk? Cashier: Angelus… Pierce: Angelus!? What the hell kind of stupid name is that? Your parents must have hated you! The only thing stupider than that name would have to be… I don’t know… Baltasar! Bwahahahhaha! Cashier: I’m sure I gave you that candy bar, sir… Pierce: Look, I’m sure you were a mistake and your parents couldn’t be arsed thinking up a name for you so they threw a dart at a name board. So listen, Accident, I want you to get me my Kit Kat before I go on a bloody murderous Rampage! [Oh come on! That was terrible!] (Shut up! You’re ruining my promo!) The Cashier quickly grabs the Kit Kat once he is released and Pierce straightens his own suit as he leaves with the chocolate. He meets up with Eve again. Eve: Did he give you a hard time? Pierce: Sure, but I just roughed him up a bit like the outlaw, Jesse Jamester. Eve: I think you’re talking about Jesse James… I don’t think Jesse Jamester even exists. Pierce: Probably not. Okay… They’re standing in front of the theatre, Pierce is over-dramatizing, fighting it… and eventually he gives in. Pierce: Okay… let’s go. He holds his breath and enters the theatre. Pierce: I can’t believe how many people are in this theatre! Eve: It’s a good movie. Pierce: You haven’t even seen it yet. Eve: It doesn’t matter. Pierce: I’d rather watch Killer Klowns From outer Space! Eve: That’s a movie. Pierce: An MGM classic. What’d you think it was a wrestler? Only the most desperate of wrestlers would name themselves after a 1980’s B-Movie! HA! Hey There’s a seat! Pierce gestures up towards the middle, there’s a seat at either end of the row. Eve: Those are two single seats! Pierce: Yeah, but they’re in the same row. C’mon. Eve: I’ll wait here. Pierce ignores Eve and makes his way up as the first commercials start. He sees a flaming metrosexual sitting near the end. Pierce: Hey! Assuming the… man… didn’t hear him properly he whispers a little louder. Pierce: Hey! The man still doesn’t turn. Pierce: Hey Ellis Easton!! The man looks. Metro: Yeth? Pierce tries his best to get around the lisp without laughing… as stereotypical as it is. Pierce: Can your row move down a bit? Metro: Do I look like I control my row? Thir, you’ll simply have to thit thomwhere elth. Pierce readies himself for a fight. He winds up his… popcorn? Dammit! His hands are full. This’ll have to wait for another night. He got lucky just like Civil Disobedience when they won that match. He’ll get his though… maybe the parking lot… Eve: Daniel! Over here! I got us seats! Eve steps into the row and sits in the first available seat and starts opening her Kit Kat. Pierce starts in second and as he tries to climb over Eve he loses his footing and starts to fall, he trips and lands on a fat man who smells badly of B.O. and looks like Zameer, but without all the African Warpaint or whatever that is… Pierce loses the popcorn but saves the soda as he jumps up to his feet. He got up faster than JDash after finding out she was only 16. Eve: Aww, Daniel, now you’ll have to go get a new popcorn. Bring the bag, tell them you dropped it. Pierce: I dropped it!? You’re supposed to move in! You don’t sit in the first seat! You move in to the far seat! I didn’t drop it! I was tripped. Eve: Fine you were tripped. Now how about you take a trip down to the concession stand and get me a new popcorn? *sigh* So Pierce goes back down the steps. Back down like Jon Kano – Whole Lotta Hype; Whole Lotta Disappointment – that was his motto. But then again, Pierce was only in it for the popcorn. … Pierce returns with the popcorn, buttery-salty-licious. He VERY carefully steps through the aisle and carefully sits down to… enjoy (?) the movie. Pierce: You know… it did cost me $15 dollars for this stuff, but it’s really quit big… it almost seems worth it. Eve: Yeah. … About seven eighths of the movie has passed and Pierce is starting to realize why the popcorn is so salty… because the salt makes you thirsty. That’s where the drink comes in. And there was AMPLE drink. And the drink fills your bladder. And the bladder makes you want to go tinkle. Ahh…. So the plot is revealed. Pierce: Eve? Eve: Yeah? Pierce: I gotta go pee. Eve: So go. Pierce: But… Eve: Are you… are you enjoying the movie!? The glee is so abundant that she almost can’t keep her voice down. Pierce: Shh… no! Afraid that he has become some kind of she-man like Kelly Flawless he resists the urge to admit that he might actually have a genuine interest in the outcome of the movie. But still, he finds it hard to pull himself away from the screen, but his bladder… he can’t fight it… OH FOR THE LOVE OF HOYT!! He springs up from his seat and makes a mad dash for the washroom. He finally gets there and he lets fly! Pierce: Ohhhhhh yeahhhhhh….. And it keeps on coming. And coming. And coming. Pierce: Seriously, this is worse than a Pranks With Attitude promo. Pierce finishes and washes his hands and makes his way back to the theatre just as everyone else is leaving. Pierce: (Under his breath) Dammit. Eve: are you upset that you missed the end of the movie? Pierce: No way! The pair leave the theatre together and as they do Pierce sneakily looks back and checks future show times… not that he’s going to go see it. He’s manly, like Tony Rolo and his evil twin, Damien Cruz. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Who am I? A Wrestler. He’s drawn blood in 13 out of 17 matches. Tony Rolo, Black Angel and Hoyt Williams – all Universal Champions at one point or another – all have lost to First Blood. He’s not the best there is, was or ever will be. He’s not the be-all, end-all. But he does his damnedest. He gives his all and nothing more can be expected but that. He’s not Rock Startling, Ignatius Lisieux or Angelo Deville and he doesn’t want to be. He’s Daniel Pierce, dammit, and that exactly where he wants to be. This week 36 men and women will step between the ropes and when it’s all said and done only one will stand tall as the victor. Sure he wasn’t the favourite. He wasn’t a Karina Wolfenden or a Dave “Gibbo” Gibson. This isn’t about comparisons, though. This isn’t about what’s happened in the past. This is about a new thing. This is about right now. He’s a fighter. He’s not a quitter. It’s about defining himself each and every day, each and every fight. Every time he steps between those ropes the legacy is ridden, it’s written. This week won’t be another page in the same, old, boring chapter. This week we’re starting from scratch. Who am I? A Winner.
