Date: 03-20-05 :::: The battle lines are now drawn. There is a new group in PRIME with every intent of being the absolute best. A group of men who are bonded by a common cause, one supreme goal. Those being the ability to dominate and rule the whole of PRIME...as the most influential force in the company, since the first incarnation of the group known as TRUE...that Rock Startling was the leader of. Some have said that the Dark Age are the most feared group to ever run through PRIME. The Dark Age is lead by the most cruel and evil leader to ever form a group of athletes in a wrestling company for one goal. The funny thing is, and maybe I missed it, but exactly at what point has the Dark Age been on the same page? Since the arrival of my team, Los Diablos, into PRIME the Dark Age has been a revolving door or men. Every single one 'supposedly' on the same page, fighting for the same cause, wanting the same thing. Then ten minutes later we find The Dark Age beating the hell out of them, cause that common bond...wasn't so common after all. Look at the list of people...Ozric, First Blood, Shadow...all three have joined and either disappeared, left, or been taken out in the last two months. Ozric is a WAY bigger and more feared star, now that he isn't part of the revolving door that is The Dark Age. Now you add Boda to the team. The man who was the Universal Champion. The guy who was suppose to lead The Dark Age on a revolution with his position and power in the company. Well excuse me but did I miss something here? Is that not Boda who doesn't ever seem to be involved when the Dark Age are attacking someone, branding them, or doing whatever other deed they usually do? I mean I haven't seen Boda take part in a single thing that the entire 'team' seems to be taking part in. Now you've got Vampir, one of Hoyt and Baltasar's opponents this week, seemingly taking a back seat to Boda. The Soul Stalker himself, the guy who just two months ago everyone was suppose to fear as the most dominate enity in the company, now taking a backseat to the 'former' Universal Champion. Don't get me wrong here, essa, we all know Vampir is tough. The Imperium realizes that he is no push over in the ring. The man is strong both physically and mentally. His team, however, well that is an entirely different story. Let me take this moment to congradulate Boda and Vampir on their masterful work over the last several months. I mean who else could have pulled off the most shocking event in PRIME's history then them two. Just look at the facts here, homes. Everyone, I mean everyone including myself and Los Diablos, believed that Boda and Vampir were blood rivals. Men who would go to their graves doing their best to destroy one another. Congrats on absolutely pulling the wool over all our eyes, homes. Faking the whole thing in order to secure the Universal Title. The position Boda in the top spot in the company, and making sure that the Universal title stayed in the Dark Ages camp for a long time. That was a brilliant plan. The plan that was going to insure you guys kept the gold in your camp, litterly forever. Wait...what do you mean you lost the belt already? Didn't Boda just get that belt three weeks ago? Don't tell me that the K-Wolf actually took the belt from the 'all mighty' Dark Ages already. Excuse me why I laugh for a moment... HAHAHHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHA!!! Wow homes! Some plan. All those months. All those beatings. All that blood lost and time setting up the swerve. And K-Wolf comes right in and ruins it in a matter of seconds. Yeah...you guys are really a real 'super' power group to be feared. Never mind the Dark Age, PRIMEates, you'd be better of skipping to the end and fearing K-Wolf herself. See that vacant area on the right shoulder of Boda now? Take a close look. Pull your camera shot back, focus on it. What have you got? That's right. The spot that the Universal title sat, after months of planning, for all of three weeks. Lost in the very first title defense, of the oh so awe inspiring reign of the essa who refers to himself as the Big Bad Boda Daddy, to the fans choice of PRIME, K-Wolf. So this bring us to this week...ReVolution 59 gringos...where the Imperium will go head up with the two longest standing wrestling members of The Dark Age...Vampir and Dani. No easy task in the ring, that is for sure essa, but nothing we sweat. The Imperium, unlike the Dark Age, is an actual team. We are there for one another. There are no ego's running this cause. Not to mention this will give myself a chance to stick it to Tiamat this time around. I tip my hat to you, gringo, as you pulled out a great plan last week. Smashing your own man in the face with a chair, to get Hoyt and Baltasar removed from ringside, was a stroke of genius. I'd almost want your autograph, after that, if you weren't running around with a bunch of gringo's who couldnt find their ass with both hands...much less lead a cause. This week, Tiamat, step aside and watch how a real pro takes care of business. Maybe, just maybe, if you play close attention essa you might be able to turn your sinking ship around and make them into something that resembles more of a unit. Then again...homes...it's quite possible that your 'team' has already sunk so far that there is no hope. The Dark Age appears to be like the Titanic, gringo's. A whole lot of hype about how it can't be destroyed...yet it continues to sink further and further every single week. And just like the Titanic it only took a single iceburg to send it going straight to the ocean bottom...That iceburg goes by the name of K-Wolf. The Dark Age...essa don't even bother. The Imperium is the present and the future in PRIME...and this week... We will prove it. :::: 'Your going to be free to go today, Angelus, but your still not released to wrestle until at least next week,' Doctor Robert Jones informed Angelus and his friends Baltasar and Morales. 'Will I be cleared for the Pay-Per-View the week after homes?' Angelus questioned as he hoped off the side of the bed, extending himself to his full height of six feet, six inches tall. 'You'll be cleared to wrestle by then, yes. I'd recommend that you not do too much this week though,' Doctor Jones began before being cut off by Morales. 'Don't worry gringo, he won't be doing to much this week. Baltasar and myself have everything covered this week,' Morales explained to the doctor as the members of Los Diablos stood, side by side, for the first time in almost two weeks. 'Good to hear. He doesn't need much stress. Take it light this week, and good luck in your future. I'd also recommend you stay away from whatever individual put you in this position, Angelus. The man is clearly unstable.' Doctor Jones explained before shaking the hands of everyone and leaving the room. 'That gringo is crazy if he thinks I'm letting this go,' Angelus began to say before Baltasar cuts him off with a ton that reflects his current mood toward The Crimson Angel. 'You don't worry about that gringo. He is going to pay for what he did to you, and for his cowardly attack on me last week,' Baltasar said as he rubbed his mask, where the bump on his forehead was, and then the place where he had to get stiches just under his lower lip, from the shots by the brass knuckles. 'I don't know, homes, I don't like the idea of you tangling with that sick freak on your own,' Angelus began to agrue, clearly only out of concern for his best friend. Baltasar simply smiled at his buddy, while patting him on the back. 'Have no fear, essa, I have things under control. Besides I am not worried about Angelo. Last week he proved exactly what type of man he is. He is nothing more than a coward, hiding behind the exterior of someone who should be feared. He knows he wants no part of me, and he is doing his best to avoid me. This week, I turn the tables in my favor.' Baltasar finished with a satisfied grin on his face. 'Boys I know that this week is going to be difficult in terms of emotion, but this will be a very successful week for everyone. The Imperium will take it's place as the dominate group in PRIME,' Morales began as the three men started to head out of the room and toward the elevator. 'Wait if I am not cleared to fight who are you teaming with Baltasar?' Angelus questioned. The Beast was all about helping out the team, yet he still felt a little distant. Not sure if he could quite trust the rest of the group fully yet. 'Senor Williams. Hoyt and myself will strike a message for The Imperium by taking down Dani and Vampir,' Baltasar said quite confidently before Angelus stopped him. 'Vampir! Your facing the leader of The Dark Age and I'm not going to be cleared to stand by your side! I don't like this homes, I don't like it one damn bit.' Angelus growled, quickly becoming very upset over the situation that had been caused by Deville. 'Angelus, my boy, I wouldn't worry too much about that at all. Hoyt is a very capable partner for our friend. And I have plans for you two that are very important anyway. We have some unfinished business reguarding our tag team titles to take care of this week.' Enrique explained as they reached the elevators. 'Above all else, my friend, I am going to even the score a bit with Angelo this week. He will feel my wrath, I promise you that essa!,' the words roared from Baltasar's mouth as they three good friends hit the first floor button and the doors slide closed in front of them, ending the scene.
