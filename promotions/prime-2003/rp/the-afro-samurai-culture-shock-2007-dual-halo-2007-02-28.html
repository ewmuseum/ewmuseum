The Afro Samurai (Culture Shock 2007, Dual Halo)

28 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 28th, 2007 If the enemy thinks of the mountains, attack like the sea; and if he thinks of the sea, attack like the mountains. -Miyamoto Musashi, 1584 - 1645 The brilliant song of a Japanese Tree-Swallow sang throughout the forest. The pine needles of the towering evergreens were damp with early morning dew, and a heavy mist danced on the woodland floor. A dark-skinned foot, shining with moisture and bound to a wooden sandal, took a light and calculated step forest’s bed of fallen brown needles. A second followed, planting itself next to the first, and a pair of strong legs, draped with the skirt of an olive green hakama, bent low to meet the ground. A slender hand reach for the earth, taking the needles between its thumb and forefinger, and raising it to a dark, inquisitive face. He sniffed with a wide nose, studied with deep brown eyes. He released the needles, allowing them to return to their home. He rose, resting his left hand upon the pommel of his katana, displaying a brilliant green and yellow weave along its handle. His right hand teased the end of his bushy beard, then ran itself through his immense, dense, and perfectly round mane as his eyes panned across the woodland landscape. The Afro Samurai His steps were as silent as a feline’s as he made way through the trees. His face was that of stone, his focus unparalleled. It wasn’t long before he found himself in a very small clearing. He stopped. Something darted behind a small bunch of trees. The Afro Samurai gripped the handle of his blade, his eyes trained to the left. The whirring sounds of spinning metal sped toward him. With blinding speed he unsheathed his blade and swung; up, across, down, each swing creating a shower of sparks and deterring the course of a shuriken. Silence once more. The samurai stood still, bent low with blade across him in a defensive stance, his eyes studying the trees from which the assailant had attacked. Another assault from the right, another attack thwarted by the shining blade of a katana. "You can’t keep runnin’ forever, brotha," said The Afro Samurai, taking a steady step to the right. "Shows what you know," answered a voice from the trees. "I’ve been chasin’ you all over this island." "I’ll see you chase me all over the world, foolish samurai." Above. The dark figure jumped from limb to limb, disappearing behind the branches. "I’ll see that justice is served," said the samurai, turning and taking soft steps in the opposite direction. "Pah! Your samurai code of ‘honor’ and ‘justice’ makes me gag, fool. I deserve no justice." "You dishonored my name, jive turkey. You made an attempt on my life." "I’d make attempts on the lives of all samurai, would that I could." "That so…" The Afro Samurai focused his attention on a smaller pine tree. "It is," continued the mysterious figure. "Where does this ‘code’ of yours get you? Nowhere. It is a burden. A weight that prevents you from assessing a situation!" "Keep talkin’, brotha. Keep runnin’ your mouth so I can find out where you ARE!" The samurai struck a mighty blow to the trunk of the small pine, cleaving it with one stroke. It fell heavily to the forest floor, sending a spray of moist soil and pine needles into the air. The warning cries of birds echoed throughout the woodlands, and then silence once again. "Ha ha! Clever, samurai. Clever. But I am no amateur." "I’m aware, my man. But that don’t change the fact that I’ma bury your coward ass," replied the samurai, eyes studying the trees. "Ah, he plays the ‘coward’ card! Why am I such a coward to you, Afro Samurai? Because I run? Because I hide? Because I make attempts on your life when you least expect it?" "Because you’re afraid to face me, eye to eye, jive turkey," was the samurai’s response. "And that, that is why your code holds you back, because you deny yourself the ability to take others by surprise." "There’s a difference between takin’ a brotha by surprise and stabbin’ him in the heart while he sleeps." "No. There isn’t." Silence. The Afro Samurai stood strong, blade held high. "You forget another flaw to your character, samurai." "Yeah? Enlighten me." "You grow old." "Some things get better with age." "Most things don’t. In fact, I doubt you’re even strong enough to take me down." "Like you already said, jive turkey. I ain’t no amateur." "THEN PROVE ME WRONG, HONORABLE SAMURAI!" A black blur dove from the trees, ninja sword leading the way. The Afro Samurai quickly parried the attack, and the ninja just as quickly sprung back to the sanctuary of the forest canopy. "HA HA HA! There is some life in you, isn’t there, samurai?" "Come on down and face me on the ground, brotha. I’ll show you just how much life I have." "You see, there is another flaw, Afro Samurai. You are only able to fight what you see in front of you. But what if one was to attack you from BEHIND!?" The ninja dove from the bushes behind The Afro Samurai, blade finding a path toward his spine. The samurai was quicker still, swinging his blade behind him to thwart the attack. The ring of steel on steel sang through the trees as the ninja retreated back into the brush. "I can keep this up, ninja," said the samurai, blade held high before him. "It’s only a matter of time before we fight to the death." "Of that I am sure, samurai. But to frustrate you brings me a joy you could never understand." "You’re wastin’ your time, my man. If there’s one thing the code teaches us it’s patience, and I got a whole lot to spare when dealin’ with you." "So you say. But I sincerely doubt that my silly games and petty speeches haven’t worn on you somewhat." "And that, my brotha, is what holds you back!" The Afro Samurai cleaved a nearby pine with his blade, but as the tree began to fall he took another mighty swing, cutting it from the opposite end, causing it to buckle and fall in the opposite direction than it had began. It thundered to the ground, and out of that tree, falling into the center of the clearing was the ninja, landing on his feet and right fist. He lifted his head to the samurai, and this time did not run. He stood, covered in ninja garb. The Afro Samurai took a weary step toward him. "Let us end this, honorable samurai." "This is the place, jive turkey." "Take your honor to your grave!" The ninja sprang, and as did The Afro Samurai. The met in mid air, katana on ninja sword. The battle was furious, masterful swordsmanship credited to both combatants. The ninja swung wide, slicing the samurai’s hakama. The Afro Samurai retaliated, tripping the ninja to the ground. He turned his blade in his hand and thrust downward, but the ninja spun out of harms way. With a terrifying war-cry the samurai charged the ninja, blade high. He swung string, a diagonal downward strike intended for the ninja’s wrapped head. The ninja dodged the attack, scurrying to the samurai’s back and planting a foot in his spine, sending him into the trees. "Did I not say you grew old, samurai!?" "Shut your bitch-ass and fight, motha fucka!" The ninja leapt once more, meeting the samurai’s blade into the density of the woods. The Afro Samurai was able to turn the ninja, pushing him back further and further. Their blades sang through the trees, sparks illuminating the darkness of dusk. Quite often a swing would fell a tree, but the warriors would not lose their focus. Their blades smashed into one another again and again. Finally the ninja found opportunity. His foot found The Afro Samurai’s knee vulnerable, and with a swift strike the samurai found himself on one knee. The ninja swung wide, blade on plane with the samurai’s sweating neck. "Die with your honor, samurai!" But it was not meant to be. The Afro Samurai parried the blow with the back of his blade, and once thwarted he thrust forward, the tip of his katana piercing the ninja’s ribs and finding home in his heart. He held still, the ninja’s eyes open wide as he lay prone with the blade in his chest. He dropped his samurai sword as blood pooled around the wound. "Farewell, jive turkey. You were a great warrior." "Before I die, *wheeze* s-samurai. May I ask you one l-l-l-laaast question?" "What is it, brotha?" "W-would you…like some…tea?" "Huh?" * * * * * * * "I ask, ‘would you like some tea?’" "Oh, um…yeah. Thanks, cat daddy. Sorry, I was day-dreamin'." The small, old Japanese man smiled and bowed, handing a tea cup and plate to Asa ‘Fro’ Fountain. Asa, however clumsily, returned the favor, then turned to witness the Japanese sunset on the small porch, overlooking a garden. "I wanted to thank you again, Mr. Iwahara. I coulda stayed back in the city, nice suite an’ all that. But, I needed to get away, come spend some time the country side, ya dig?" "Oh, yes. I ‘dig,’" replied Mr. Iwahara. "Thanks for takin’ me in for a few days. Um…‘Konichiwa...’" "No no. ‘Gurache.’" "Ger-ah-chay?" "Yes, yes! ‘Gurache!’" "Alright, I dig, I dig." Asa sipped from his tea, staring at the pink sunset over the green, Japanese hills. "Your name, it have meaning in Japanese," said Mr. Iwahara. "Yeah?" replied Asa, taking genuine interest. "Yes. ‘Ah-sa’ mean ‘healer.’ "Healer, huh?" "Hai." Asa took one last sip of tea, placing the cup on the tiny plate and setting it to the side. "Well, that’s a good thing, my man, cuz PRIME needs a whole lotta healin’ that I’ma have to take care of."
