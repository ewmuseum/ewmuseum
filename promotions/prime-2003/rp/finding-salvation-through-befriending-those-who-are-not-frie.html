Finding Salvation Through Befriending Those Who Are Not Friends (Or, Finding Nova) - Prologue

Date: Today. "I don't understand why you gotta go back, Jim." "It's Chet, Dad. Not Jim." "Whatever. Your mother named you Jim, I'll call you Jim." "You named me Chet. After your dad, Dad." "I did?" "Yeah." "Fuck you, get your mother." And so began the same conversation that Chet had with his father between April 6th, 2007 and today. It didn't matter anymore. What was a strong recognition at the beginning of the year, deteriorated into a bare glimmer in his father's eyes, when the wind blew just right. And, since April 6th, Chet stood, waiting for his father to realize that he was his son, his wife was dead, and the dementia eating his brain was real. "Mom's dead, Dad. Remember?" The fury of the seventy-seven year old man lasted for four seconds, long enough for him to reach up and slap his son across the face, and sit down again, as Chet stood dumbstruck. "Get your fucking mother, boy!" "I can't, Dad. She's dead." Another slap, this one bloodying Chet's lip. "GET YOUR MOTHER, JIM!" "Goddamnit, Dad. She's dead." This time, the arm was caught in mid-slap, Chet pushing his father into his chair, his emotions boiling over, tears flowing from his eyes, his face turning red with anger, and his heart sinking, realizing that who was once his father was now just a shell of the man who taught him to throw a ball, light a barbecue, and gave him his first beer. Chet stood up, after his father's body relaxed, and looked out the window, trying to regain his composure, trying to concentrate on a single emotion. "I'm doing this because this is goodbye, Dad. Now that you're gone, there's nothing left for me. Jason had a choice. He had someone left, and he chose to push her away to redeem himself. Me? I've helped people my entire life. It's time to help myself. I'm forty-four years old, and I want to be my own man, now." He looked at where his father slumped in the chair his eyes staring at him, fixated on Chet's position, and cried. "I'm sorry, Dad. I really am. But, it's for the best. They'll be here to take you in ten minutes. But, I can't do this anymore." A moment later, he grabbed his jacket, and closed the door, as the weak voice called out behind him "Goodbye, Chet." Chet Worth was free. He pulled out a pack of cigarettes, put one to his lips and lit it as he looked down the street. Taking a deep drag, he pulled his cell phone out of his pocket and dialed a number that would likely change his life. "Maryanne? Hi, this is Chet Worth. I'm well, thanks. Can you get me the phone number for Nova's last known location? I need to find him. Thanks, Maryanne." Chet Worth + Nova = ??? Find out, next time!
