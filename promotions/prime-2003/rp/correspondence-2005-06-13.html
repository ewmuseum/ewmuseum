Correspondence

13 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/13/05 Alan, How does it feel, Alan? How does it feel to be proven wrong once again? Did you think I'd just come crawling back to you? Oh, please Master, show me the way? I hope your so called connections have told you what happened this last week. I did it. I beat FIVE other people. I won. And I'm a champion again. And where are you? Oh, that's right. In prison. Where I put you! You see, Alan, there's nothing wrong with me. Sure I'm beat up, bruised up, bloodied up, burned up. But aren't I always? And you... You... You tried once again to play your mind games with me. It WON'T WORK!!!! I'm better than you. I'm smarter. I'm craftier. I'm stronger. I'm even more sane. More sane than I've ever been in my life. You keep trying to pull your little strings, but there's not much you can do when you're locked up, is there? No one knows that better than I. You say I'm losing control? HAH! You don't understand, Alan. You don't know me. You think you do, but you don't. Sure I went through a bad spot there. We all do. All of us NORMAL people. I'm normal, Alan. I'm just a guy. And, guess what? No more voices. Surprised? You see, I'm a winner now. I don't need Mr Smiley Face anymore. My life is exactly how it should be. So Mr Smiley Face is gone. No more laughing in my head. No more stupid grin. I've buried him deep. He knows I'm better now and he's gone away. And as for Him... my dark side... the demon, as the priest that raised me put it... he's locked away as well. Last week I went through my last trial. As I fell through the flaming glass floor, I had an epiphany. You always act surprised when I use big words, you know that Alan? It's one of the things I dislike about you. You don't realize how smart I am. You asked what I did during the time between OSW and PRIME. Well, one of the things I did was have my intelligence tested. Do you know what the results were? I'm a genius. IQ of 163. Not bad for a mind controlled puppet, no? And realizing that, I took the money I've made over the years and invested. Do you know how wealthy I am now? Does the word multi-millionare mean anything to you? Yes, Alan. I've survived your games. I've thrived. I'm better than you. I don't need you. I will never need you. I don't need anyone. THAT was my epiphany Father. You. Mr Smiley Face. Him. You were all just anchors dragging me down. Ballast that I've thrown away so I can fly higher than ever before. Do you get it yet? Despite all that has been done to me over the years. The beatings. The burning. The brainwashing. The drugs. The electric shock. The chip you put in my head... I'm still the man I should be. Master of my fate, decider of my own destiny. I'm a champion, Father. Aren't you proud? You've called me a weapon over and over again. And I am a weapon. But I wield myself. I hope you are able to watch me this week, Father. I pray that after this letter, you do whatever scheming is necessary to convince your keepers to let you watch me. Boda. Kano. Hoyt. I face them this week. It's funny, isn't it? You mocked these three men in the letter you sent to me. And now I'll destroy them. I'll prove to you and to everyone else that I've returned to myself. I've thrown off my chains. Their destruction will be my flag of freedom. And freedom is sweet. And that's a sweetness you'll never know again. I've won. I'm better than you in every way. No one can stop me now. Nothing can stop me now. Your 'son',Michael Kriegman 'Trash, listen to me!' shouted Mr Smiley Face through his cupped hands. 'We're not ok, you dumb bastard!' He hops off a boulder, dust raising in a cloud around his feet as he lands on a dry, barren surface. Around him is nothing but cracked earth. Desolation reigns over the Mindscape. Green lighting flashes in the dark gray clouds over head. A brisk wind whips the Smilin' Dude's leather trenchcoat away from his lean body as he spins around to face the squat stone tower, the only landmark other than a few boulders strewn haphazardly about the mental construct of Michael Kriegman's fractured mind. The tower were He resides, locked away. For now. High pitched cackling is barely heard over the wailing winds as Mr Smiley Face lets loose his frustration at his inability to get through to the Trashcan Man. It's been almost a full week since he was able to pierce the barrier to speak to Trashy. He can only watch helplessly as the Trashcan Man lives his self deluding lie. For while the barrier between the Trashcan Man and his other personalities have inexplicably grown stronger, the rest of his mental barriers continue to weaken. Smiley's losing control. The Mindscape is starting to take a life of its own. And while He stays complacent and quiet in the tower, the newest personality is swelling. 'Dammit, Trash, we're like a pregnant Catholic in here! We can't kill this guy before he's born, and we don't know who he is! I need your help!' His shouts go unanswered as they have all week long. Ever since the instant the Trashcan Man was declared winner of the Glass House brawl, the changes in the Mindscape took effect. Cackling wildly again, the Smilin' Dude makes his way threw the blasted landscape to the tower. He places his black gloved hand on the old crumbling stonework, feeling around for cracks. 'This is somehow your doing, isn't it Atropos?' mutters Mr Smiley Face, using the name given to the dark personality by Alan Kriegman. 'Your prison still feels strong... but I know it's you.' If Mr Smiley Face is waiting for an answer, he waits in vain as the only sound is the wailing wind. He shakes his head before cupping his hands around his mouth again, pleading to the Trashcan Man to listen to him. His world is crumbling, and it appears he's powerless to stop it. In the dimness of the tower, a figure reclines on a large, high backed chair. No details can be seen in this darkness, just vague shapes. A brief flash of white gleams from the figures teeth as his lips curl in a small smile. 'Keep trying, Lachesis' says the figure in a soft voice. 'Like the last flutters of a butterfly as its wings are pinned to a display case, your struggles are meaningless. But just as the butterfly uses the last of its life to escape its fate in a futile attempt at freedom, you will do the same as you try to break through. My time is coming, and as Clotho's letter so eloquently stated... no one can stop me now. Nothing can stop me now.' The figure steeples his fingers together and chuckles throatily as we fade to black.
