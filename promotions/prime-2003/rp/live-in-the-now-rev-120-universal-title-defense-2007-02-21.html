Live in the Now (ReV 120, Universal Title Defense)

21 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/21/07 My past is my wisdom to use today. . . my future is my wisdom yet to experience. Be in the present because that is where life resides. - Gene Oliver Travel Inn Hotel, Melbourne, Australia, February 12, 2007 OH YEAH! FEEL THAT BURN! LOVE IT! LOVE IT, YOU SONUVABITCH! HOLY GOD, THE ENDORPHINS! WHAT A HIGH! THIS IS GREAT! WHAT ON GOD’S CREATION COULD POSSIBLY BE MORE COMPLETELY SATISFYING THAN THIS?! … Oh, who am I kidding? Exercise blows. Nova hangs from a bar wedged into the doorjamb of his hotel room. He’s wearing boots whose metal hooks latch onto the bar, and he’s doing reps pulling his upper torso as far forward as he can. Each time he does, he catches a glimpse of the #1 Contender at Culture Shock, in action at King of Kings in his defeat of Angelo Deville to win the Jewel in the Crown tournament, courtesy of the flat-screen mounted above the door. Nova pulls himself up, and it’s Tony Gamble reversing an Atomic Drop into a Spike DDT. He drops back down, beads of sweat rolling down (or up?) his cheeks into the short, ragged blonde hair that has grown back in place of the mane he once took so much pride in. His beard is growing back as well, thin brown stubble now covering his face below the nose. It feels right. The "Holocaust Victim" thing needed to happen. It was a rough time, one that the Risen Star has basically blocked out of his mind because dwelling on that kind of human failure will drive a man insane, but it’s not a look he wants to keep. It isn’t familiar. Familiar is good these days. Another inverted sit-up, and Tony Gamble is vaulting himself out of the ring, twisting in mid-air before slamming into Wrestling’s Cadillac, driving both of them into the guardrail. Nova maintains the position for a second, body trembling, and then lets himself fall back down, abs stretching out as his knuckles drag on the carpet. Breathing hard, he reaches out and grabs the bowl and the lighter from the ground. Still upside-down, he rips the unlit green and places the piece back on the carpet, holding the smoke until his brain screams for oxygen (it’s already getting plenty of blood). Blowing smoke out of his nostrils, Nova stares blankly at the floral pattern that decorates the comforter on his bed. Mmmm…that’s pretty. The flower over by the nightstand looks like an angry turtle. Pulling himself back up, he sees Deville stomp approximately 850 mud-holes in "The Grin" before re-introducing him to the mat by way of the Evenflow DDT. That one makes Nova smile, high or not. Nova lowers himself down slowly, letting his abs absorb the weight as his vision is once again flipped over. This time his eyes fall on the other television in the room. Onscreen, four men dressed in suits sit around a panel setting, not wholly unlike ESPN’s SportsCenter. "Welcome back, fans, and it looks like we turn our attention now to monolith wrestling organization PRIME, and the card for the 120th edition of ReVolution," a man with slicked black hair says as he shuffles around papers in his hands, "and what a card it promises to be! Very solid contenders lined up for every title, beginning with the…" "If I could just interrupt you there," another man says, extending his arm, "I know we normally start at the bottom and work our way to the top, but I have to say, this Universal Title match is interesting on a number of levels, and I can’t get my mind off it…" Nova shakes his head and goes to pull himself back up, but can’t help pause as he hears the others chime in. "I know what you mean," another chimes in, "factions within PRIME aside, Nova is a good Universal Champion. He defended the belt against one of the men slated to be PRIME’s next generation of Devilles and Wolfendens in Jonathon Winters, as well as a host of other defenses that he either won or finagled his way through…I mean, he’s technically already the defending-est Universal Champion in PRIME’s history…but I don’t know if he’s ready for this match." With a dismissive snort, Nova pulls himself back up, and his eyes fall on Gamble as he locks the Crossface Chickenwing onto the Devil’s Don. Deville writhes on the mat, searching for his out. The Risen Star zones out for a moment as he stares past the image of a determined Tony Gamble, until the image itself fades and thousands of tiny pixels shimmer in Nova’s vision. Letting himself drop, the voices of the commentators on the other television return. "Look, all I’m saying is, I think Nova’s focus is on Tony Gamble at Culture Shock, not Adam at ReVolution 120, and that’s a mistake," one of the commentators defends. "I personally don’t see how," another interjects, "Let’s be realistic, Nova is at his best right now…yeah, I know, the list of real challengers to his belt hasn’t on the whole been daunting, but let’s face facts, he beat a man who had, what, three singles losses in 2006? His life is that belt, and Gamble is the threat…not Adam." "You don’t see how?" the man from before retorts, "someone else already made the mistake Nova is making right now, and that’s Tchu, the man you lauded a moment ago. Yeah, he had three singles losses in…" Nova wrenches himself back up, and Tony Gamble has the hold locked in, the pressure placed across Deville’s carotid artery causing the Crimson Angel’s face to turn exactly that color. Another moment passes, and Nova slowly lowers himself down, grabbing around until he has a pack of Camel Lights in his grasp. He lights one, and exhales a cloud of cigarette smoke as his focus once again shifts to the other set. "That’s a good point," the man who spoke first says, "No one would’ve expected…well, let’s just roll the clip from file…" A box in the corner of the screen enlarges to replace the commentators, an image from ReVolution 85 frozen on-screen. It comes to life. Clyde Walkins has the double chickenwing locked on Tchu from behind, but the Inhuman Being appears to block it, hoisting the challenger overhead for a Vertebreaker. Clyde drops behind to the surprise of everyone in the world watching…surprise surpassed only by the moment of shock that follows as Clyde hooks Tchu again and plants him with the Cyclide. One! Two! THREE!! The video disappears, replaced by the commentators. "Tchu’s first loss in PRIME, the Universal Title that he had to defeat practically the entire roster to get a shot at gone," the man speaking from before says, "I’d say it’s a great precedent for the situation we have at hand. Tchu didn’t see Culture Shock as champion, and if he doesn’t get his mind off Tony Gamble and on the former Alias Champion, Nova might not either." "Exactly," another supports, "Adam is no slouch, guys. He’s widely recognized as one of the most underrated stars on PRIME’s roster for a reason. The guy took Tony Gamble to the limit in a match at King of Kings that, in my opinion, certainly rivaled that evening’s main event. The Scot was inches away from moving on himself to the JitC finals, and…" Scooping up the remote off the carpet, Nova shuts off the voices of the commentators. He doesn’t need them. The seeds of their doubt are causing the voices in his head to begin chipping away at his confidence much more effectively than any group of panelists could ever do. The Risen Star puts out his cigarette in an ashtray resting on the carpet and pulls himself back up, and on-screen the fans in Ohio are losing it as Gamble and Deville both lie on the mat, utterly exhausted. The voice of Vince Howard is audible over the crowd. "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN... YOUR TWO THOUSAND AND SIX JEWEL IN THE CROWN CHAMPION... THE MAN WHO WILL GO ON TO FACE THE NEW UNIVERSAL CHAMPION NOVA AT CULTURE SHOCK...TONY "THE GRIN" GAMBLE!" Gritting his teeth, Nova wrenches himself up further and hits the eject button on the VCR installed under the flat-screen. The tape pops out and Nova swipes at it, yanking it from the player before collapsing back to a swinging position a foot or two off the ground. With great strain Nova pushes one leg up, unhooking it from the bar and then sliding it over the top around the back of his knee-bend. Putting the pressure all on that leg he unhooks his other and then swings himself up, grabbing hold of the bar before falling backwards and landing on his knees… …on top of the cigarettes. "Awww, fuck." Nova climbs to his feet, slipping out of the boots and plopping down on the edge of the bed to take a breather. After allowing his muscles a momentary rest, he stands up and goes to a large duffel bag in the corner. Sifting through the tapes, he recovers one with a white label that has ReVolution 113 scrawled across it in Sharpie. Walking over to the VCR on wobbly legs, he pops the tape in and hits fast-forward, stopping it only once the Jewel in the Crown matches are underway. Stepping back, he sits down on the edge of the bed again and watches as Digital Mortality spears Adam to the mat in the opening moments of their bout. He wants Gamble. Tony Gamble is, at the moment, more representative of the "next generation" than anyone else in the company. Nova wants to shut him down, to prove that he’s no transitional champion, that it isn’t "their" PRIME... …not yet. It’s still his. As much as he wants to concentrate on the match on-screen, on how Adam moves, attacks, counters…he can’t. The voices of the commentators return. "His life is that belt…" Suddenly the voice of the Trashcan Man, of all people, pops into the Universal Champion’s head. "MY LIFE FOR YOU! MY LIFE FOR YOU!!" Nova shakes his head, a sad grin spreading over his face. Trashy is certifiably psychotic, and pledges unwilling allegiance to entities within himself that overpower the weaker, kinder persona. The Risen Star pledges allegiance to a bundle of leather and metal. "My life for you." Who’s more fucked up?
