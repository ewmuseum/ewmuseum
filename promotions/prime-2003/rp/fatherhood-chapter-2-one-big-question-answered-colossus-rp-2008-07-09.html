Fatherhood - Chapter 2 - One BIG Question Answered (Colossus RP)

9 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 9, 2008 “Nicole?” the nurse called into the crowded waiting room. No one got up, although a few of the women glanced at the girl. She repeated. “Nicole Sivec?” Nikkie was deep in thought, pondering those same questions over and over again… How would Bryan react after seeing the ultrasound? Is the baby going to be healthy? Am I going to make it through the pregnancy? Is he going to stick around after the baby is born? If he does stick around, how is he going to keep wrestling AND be a good father at the same time? Was her apartment going to be big enough for her and the baby? Chances are, most of those questions weren’t going to be answered today. Nikkie was still deep in thought when the nurse called her name a third time. “Is there a Nicole Sivec in the room?” Bryan glanced over at the mother of his unborn child. “Are we gonna go in, or are we just gonna sit in the waitin’ room all afternoon?” He smiled. A shaky, unsure voice escaped her lips as she stood up. “Uh, yeah. That’s me. Let’s go, Bry.” She paced gingerly across the waiting room toward the nurse. Bryan followed closely behind her, almost as if he would be ready to catch her, should she fall. Actually, if she would fall, he would catch her. He was her safety net. Her protection. Her guardian… …her love. --------------------------------------------- A nervous energy swept through Bryan’s girlfriend as they waited to see the doctor. Dr. Richard Southwick was a renown gynecologist who had worked with many famous couples through the pregnancy stages, and most, if not all, were more than satisfied with their experience with him. The door to their room opened and a dashing middle-age man with a mild resemblance to Tom Selleck entered the room. Had this been a sitcom filmed before a live studio audience, this would be the point where all of the middle-age women in the audience remove their undergarments and launch them in his general direction. I digress. “Miss Nicole Sivec. I hear you’re dating Brian Dawkins. Is this true?” questioned the dashing doctor, as he scanned Nikkie’s chart. For the first time since Bryan’s arrival at the airport, Nikkie was sporting a half-sincere smile. Proudly, she answered yes and squeezed her love’s hand. Having him here with her was such a relief, compared to being mostly alone through this whole process, with Bryan on the road with PRIME nearly every weekend during the calendar year. Bryan smiled and studied the doctor. For some reason, something didn’t sit right with him about this guy. “So, uh…who’s this? A brother? Cousin? Friend?” The question was directed to Nikkie, but was about Bryan. Staring back at Southwick with a deeply confused look on her face, Nikkie replied, “That’s Bryan…my boyfriend.” Dr. Southwick visually examined Dawkins briefly and proceeded. “Nope. This can’t be Brian Dawkins. Impossible.” See, what Nikkie and Bryan don’t know is that Richard Southwick is an avid football fan. More specifically, he’s an avid Philadelphia Eagles fan. Needless to say, there was a mix-up. “Yes, this is my boyfriend. Bryan Dawkins. He’s an athlete.” Dawkins, meanwhile, has been silent through all of this. Although he’s not the sweetest coconut in the palm tree, he pretty much figured out what was going on. Bryan had dealt with this for years, during his stays in Hawaii Championship Wrestling, High Risk Wrestling, the various independent promotions in England, and now PRIME. The first thing that people think when they hear “Bryan/Brian Dawkins” is the football player for the Philadelphia Eagles. The Flyin’ Hawaiian, however, was out to change that. Southwick studied Dawkins yet again. “I’m pretty sure this guy doesn’t play Strong Safety for the Philadelphia Eagles.” Richard glances back at Dawkins. “Do you?” The Flyin’ Hawaiian just grins and shakes his head “no” to Southwick’s question. He wasn’t about to piss the best gynecologist in Hawaii off, leaving his child’s fate in some hack’s hands. No way. “Actually, bruh, I don’t. I wrestle for the most successful wrestling organization on the globe today, PRIME. If that’s not up to your standards of celebrities and football players, then bruh, we might have a little bit of a problem.” Dawkins’ tone begins to get more serious as the conversation continues. “I’m pretty much aware that you only accepted us right off the street due to the fact that you thought my fiancée—“ WHOA. WAIT. HOLD UP. Fiancée? Nikkie’s mind was just blown into a million places at once. Is he serious? Nikkie hadn’t even considered the fact that Bryan may want to marry her. Is this just because of the baby? She only thought he was around due to the fact that they were in this situation. DID HE JUST (INDIRECTLY) PROPOSE TO ME? Yeah, he did. “—was dating the starting Strong Safety for your beloved football team. Well, I’m sorry to burst your bubble, bruh, but that’s not the case. I’m a wrestler, and a pretty damn good one at that, and I’m pretty sure it’d be in your best interests as a doctor to continue this checkup as if I was your beloved Brian Dawkins. Whaddaya say, bruh?” Tom Selleck—I mean, Dr. Southwick looked directly into Dawkins’ eyes with an intensity and fire that the Flyin’ Hawaiian had only seen in men he stepped into the squared circle with. For a second, Bryan thought that the gynecologist was moments away from taking a shot at him, when out of nowhere… “Okay, sounds good. How about we get you all prepped for your ultrasound so we can get a look at this?” Southwick placed Nikkie’s chart on the table next to her and went searching for a nurse that could prepare the soon-to-be mother to see her child for the first time. --------------------------------------------- After watching Nikkie go through things that only a woman who really desires to be a mother would go through to prepare for the ultrasound, Southwick finally re-entered the exam room to begin the procedure. “You two ready?” Holding a tight grip on one another’s hands, the two soon-to-be parents nodded their heads with anticipation of seeing their first baby. A year ago, neither of these two would have imagined themselves in this position, but here they were, making the best of a very awkward situation between two close friends-turned-lovers. After placing a lubricant on the LAT (linear array transducer), Dr. Southwick placed the instrument on Nikkie’s exposed stomach, causing the screen to blur and adjust itself. Just seeing the screen come to life was cause for excitement between Dawkins and Nikkie, who could have only dreamt about moments like this. After moving the LAT to different places on Nikkie’s abdomen, the picture became more and more clear. With each movement, another feature would appear. An arm here, the head there, a leg here, the torso there, and so on. Finally, after what seemed like hours, a recognizable picture of a fetus was shown on the screen. Dawkins was overtaken by a feeling of sheer joy upon seeing the screen. It was an unimaginable feeling for the young PRIMEate, one that he honestly hadn’t expected to even think about for at least another eight to ten years. “Bruh…it’s amazing.” The statement wasn’t really directed to Southwick, or to Nikkie. Just a general statement. “I know, babe. I know.” Nikkie’s grip on Bryan’s hand did nothing but intensify as the pictures on the screen changed with each movement of Southwick’s hand. For a moment, the doctor couldn’t help but pause and take notice of the way these two looked at each other. Sure, most couples who came into his office were happy together, and more than thrilled to be having a child together…but these two. These two were different. It was simply apparent in the way they looked deep into each other’s eyes, knowing that they were there for each other through whatever ups and downs this experience might give them. These two were in love, and he knew it. He knew it because a lot of the couples who he dealt with, especially ones involved in accidental pregnancies like Bryan and Nikkie’s, simply weren’t in love. Simply put, you can tell when a couple is truly in love with one another, and you can definitely tell when a couple is in it because they have to be. This wasn’t one of those cases. Before resuming, Dr. Southwick asked the most important question he had asked to the couple all day. “Would you two like to know the sex of the child?” Without hesitation. “Yes…” and now with Bryan’s touch of personality, “…bruh.” And with that, and a seemingly magic flinch of the doctor’s wrist, a tiny blip on the screen appeared. It was barely visible at first, but as Richard did his magic, the shape of a small male reproductive organ began to clear itself into full view. Silently, a tear fell down Bryan’s cheek…a tear which the Flyin’ Hawaiian nonchalantly brushed off before Nikkie or Southwick could catch it. There was gonna be a little Flyin’ Hawaiian runnin’ around the Dawkins household before he knew it, and the idea of that completely overjoyed Bryan. Meanwhile, Nikkie felt no need to hide her emotions, breaking out into tears upon sight of her child’s gender. No matter what the sex of the child, she was going to be happy. Either way, the child was either going to have a strong mother to follow after, should it be a girl, or a fearless father, should it be a boy. “Nikkie, Bryan, your son looks to be a very healthy baby, and I’m sure we’re not going to have any complications over the next few months. I’m gonna send a nurse in here to get you all cleaned up, Nikkie. I’ll be seeing the both of you a lot more down the road. Take care.” And with that, Bryan and Nikkie were left in solitude in their exam room to take in the amazing moment they witnessed not ten minutes earlier. Bryan clutched Nikkie’s hand firmly and gazed into here green eyes, which stared right back into him. “Did you mean what you said earlier…to the doctor?” “Huh?” “Bryan, you called me your fiancée. That doesn’t just happen out of nowhere. Did you mean it?” Without hesitation, Bryan replied. “Of course I did. And before you ask, it has nothing to do with the baby. If anything, our child has helped me figure out that you really are the girl I want to spend the rest of my life with. So…” Nikkie was on the verge of crying again. Her eyeliner was already running, her eyes semi-red and puffy from the last outpour, she was overjoyed. “Yes. I’ll marry you, Bryan.” --------------------------------------------- After receiving pictures of the baby Dawkins, Bryan and Nikkie were headed home on the same highway which brought them to the office. The beautiful Hawaiian countryside just complimented the wonderful day that the two had. Out of nowhere, another question sprung from the Flyin’ Hawaiian’s mouth. “So, you wanna hop my flight with me and catch Colossus? We can give the little Dawkins a close look at what daddy does.” Nikkie didn’t fancy going to Bryan’s wrestling events; she never had in the past. However, after all they had been through, and all of the changes that had gone down between them, she figured it couldn’t hurt. “Sure, why not. The kid’s probably gonna be a wrestler too, anyway.”
