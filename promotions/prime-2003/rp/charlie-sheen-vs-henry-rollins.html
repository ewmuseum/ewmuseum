CHAPTER ONEEyes opened. The shuffle against her room's door must have woken her up. That, or whoever had closed the door... And, in all likelyhood, the culprit behind the exit was the same person who'd left such a warm imprint on the mattress beside her. In the unhinging silence that ensued once the rustling in the hotel's hallway stopped, a young woman was left to test her sobriety in the dark - swinging her arm around in hopes of eventually reaching the bedside lamp. Lighting revealed that her vision was still dragging and fuzzy, which meant that the tequila she hadn't stopped tasting still had a harsh hold over her brain. Worse than the hazy vision was the hair that tickled her bare shoulder, stuck to the pillow beside her. She plucked it from the cotton and grew startled upon a quick examination... ''I haven't had blonde in my hair for months...'' Killean didn't have highlights, and it was too long to belong to any man she could remember off-hand. Just then, the warmth of the bed space next to her grew ten times as significant, but any and all attempts to sit up were slaughtered mercilessly by the dizziness only found at the bottom of a bottle - so she scanned the room from a sleeping position. There was a black elastic hair tie on her bedside table, and she stretched it between her fingers when she picked it up, noting the yellow and pink strands that had been tangled in it. She did have pink in her hair... but the blonde was still unexplained. Blonde and pink. Blonde and pink... Blonde and... Very suddenly, Aimz was sober as a priest. Her head, however, was still spinning - just not from the alcohol. ''Oooooh no no no...'' she grumbled to herself, violently shaking her head, ''Nonononononoooo. That didn't...'' Hours before, she'd ambled into a bar where she knew the crew members would be having drinks after King Of Kings. She had, however, forgotten that Karina Wolfenden usually accompanied them. Amy had sat down next to a lighting technician she'd known from one of her first federations and started chit-chatting about the various rage-inducing match outcomes of the evening when the K-Wolf herself walked back from the washrooms and sat down a table away from the redhead, not noticing her presence at first. That ignorance could be attributed to her red eyes and the whiskey sour in her hand... CHAPTER FOUR''I didn't know she drank,'' Amy observed to her friend, chuckling ''Look at Little Miss Athlete...'' It was in that moment that Karina noticed her and... ignored her completely. She downed her drink rather swiftly and stood, excusing herself for another moment in which Amy thought she'd make hasty exit. Instead, it seemed she was just going for a refill. Amy watched, but avoided any direct eye contact when Wolfenden began her trek back from the bartender. Aimz hadn't expected the delivery she was about to get, though. While the crew continued talking amongst themselves, most already too drunk to note the obvious tension of having Amy Campbell and Karina Wolfenden in one room, the K-Wolf snuck around beside Amy, taking a moment to laugh and whisper in her ear... ''Looking to drink away your loss?'' She'd placed a vodka tonic in front of Aimz, decidedly sitting directly across from her rival in a rather smug move. Amy rolled her eyes, but Karina was perfectly willing to speak... ''Hoyt's still champion, you know.'' she grinned, ''Shouldn't you be off somewhere, trying to ride his moustache?'' ''You don't have to buy me a drink to insult me. I'm a big girl, I can take it.'' ''I bought you a drink so I'd have an excuse to sit and insult you within earshot. See everybody else?'' Amy shook her head, ''They're too drunk to notice anything so long as you and I pretend this is a friendly conversation...'' ''And that's the idea.'' ''Free shots and cheap shots,'' Aimz sighed, sipping the tonic, ''That idea's a little too bright to be yours.'' ''Oh no, it's all mine,'' Karina smiled amicably, ''Just like coming here was your own idea.'' ''I came here to have fun with the crew.'' ''Really? And which one did you intend to go home with? Chris from ring assembly has a keychain with your photo on it - he's as good a candidate as any.'' ''Fuck you. Up the ass, and much harder than Deville ever could.'' ''I loathe you.'' ''It's mutual.'' Karina stood, smiled again and ended their 'friendly' conversation by returning to the table she'd shared with her actual friends from the crew. She stared hatefully at Amy, who was looking right back, but neither of them would give the other the upper hand by leaving the little pub. It was a war of wits... It would be lost by the one who passed out first, since neither would move otherwise, and things tipped drastically into the K-Wolf's favor when one of the men from the medical department started buying rounds of tequila shots. Still, neither woman was willing to abandon the vicious staring contest. It was simply one more thing to be won or lost between them. CHAPTER THREEAimz' brow furrowed. It couldn't have been... ...Right? She'd called the front desk to get Karina's room number. Not that she thought they'd... ahem, just to check on the K-Wolf and make sure she'd gotten back from the bar safely. Yeah, that was it. It had turned out that Wolfenden had never checked in. Ever. More confusing was that the last call Amy had recieved was from Karina. She remembered enough to know that Wolfenden had called her from across the bar to, once more, note that she loathed her in the most amusing way possible. They'd still been starting at one another with a hatred masked by smiles and little waves for the sake of the crew. It was actually pretty funny to those around them. The memory goaded Amy into checking her recieved calls, dialing the last number that had called only to get no answer, not even a voicemail. In the one moment she needed Karina Wolfenden, at least for the sake of cross-referencing their memories of the evening, it appeared to Aimz that the K-Wolf had dropped off of the face of the planet. Then again, if... that... had happened, Kari was probably off somewhere to wash away as many layers of her skin as humanly possible. And it wasn't like Aimz enjoyed the hazed memories, either. If there was ANY moment that she needed her brain to work, it was that one. Oh well. Her flight was in the morning. If what she thought had occured really did, and it wasn't just some sick joke her brain was playing on her, Karina would likely contact her. That, or they'd both act on the instincts to avoid one another at all possible costs - in fact, it seemed that Wolfenden was already doing so. That was good. After all, such things didn't warrant conversation or rehashing... right? CHAPTER FOURSenses are all anyone has tonight. It's bright, only thanks to the moon, but it's still night and darkness never fails to greet anyone whose eyes dare to open at this hour. One eye is dark as the shadows around it, but its partner captures every tiny ray of light coming in through the window, which casts its own shadow courteosy of the rain pouring down its panes in buckets. Earth and ice illuminating in the joint-popping cold of early November in Atlantic Canada, the central nervous system of the elements' owner only rousing itself to life after a droplet of water soaks the lips it commands. Niceties aside, Amy Campbell's awake and about to be rather unhappy about it. To her left is a bedside table with a lamp and glass of water set on it. Her right yields a pillow on which she'd set her cell phone and a stuffed Ewok that was a constant in her luggage and sleep routine, unknown to most of her peers and friends. One side, light. The other, contact. Either way? Comfort. She clears her throat groggily and presses down on the cell phone, shielding her eyes from the initial glare of the screen and only casting a glance when they've adjusted to the pale moonlight and flourescent glow of the phone. Seeking nobody, she goes through her regular midnight-waking routine and notes the time - 2:34 AM. Yes, she'd gone to sleep early... but woke up all too soon and, to little surprise, it didn't take her long to notice another drop of water fall from the ceiling - this time onto the tip of her nose, forcing her to momentarily squeeze her eyes shut when the fractions of rain slid down the bridge of her nose and into the hollows of her eyes. Sleep is hard fought in the moment that her eyelids are bonded, but she rubs the water away and pulls herself back to consciousness swiftly. As far as she could remember, her apartment's water wasn't stored in the ceiling, which means that the drip is a leak and the leak is a problem. One that woke her up, no less. The cold drip makes it apparent to Amy that the space beside her was actually recently warmed by a human body. In her groggy awakening, she'd forgotten that Killean was, in fact, there with her... somewhere. ''Ungh...'' she groans, half-whispering into the darkness, ''Kill?'' Hearing footsteps on the hardwood floor, she nervously rolls out of bed - putting her waterglass below the leaking ceiling and preparing herself for whatever might lurk in the darkness. ''Boo!'' She squeaks and jumps a solid foot into the air when Sirrajin speaks up, laughing and wrapping arms around her shakey figure in the moonlight, knowing fully well from many secret nights spent near her that Amy is afraid of the dark. He offers an explaination, still amused by her shivering, ''I was just trying to find your flashlight. Relax, no boogie men here.'' ''You're bad enough,'' she snuffs, writhing in his grasp and swatting his bicep, ''That was riculously unfunny.'' Killean's still chuckling, and Aimz feels his lips brush her cheek apologetically... but that doesn't change that he IS still laughing. She grumbles, decidedly catty in response to his mean joking, ''Seriously, I bet you wouldn't do this to Lisa Tyler!'' The shock of the comment rang in his ear. He backs away from her a little but he holds her shoulders with his strong hands. He looks deep into her eyes, trying to get a sense of what she's thinking, but to little avail. ''Why do you keep bringing her up? It's bad enough that I gave her a ride to her hotel, but since then she's been attached to me worse than a spider monkey. I try to get rid of her but she's way too persistant. I have no interest in her whatsoever. And at least I'm not the one suddenly befriending a certain frostybox. She finally thaw out enough for your taste?'' ''Never say that again. I don't even wanna know where you HEARD that I'm at all friendly with her.'' Amy, despite her fear of the darkness, pulls away from Killean entirely and excuses her behavior by brushing over it entirely, ''It's simply not true. And back to the ACTUAL subject... sorry, but I don't buy that you - a professional wrestler - don't know how to make someone go away. From what I hear, you don't protest much at all when Lisa's around... that doesn't sound like someone you're 'trying to get rid of'. Maybe you like the attention, or maybe you're trying to make me jealous, hmm? We aren't really in a boyfriend, girlfriend sort of thing, after all...'' Even though she pulled away, Killean closes the distance between them, and she can see his eyebrows stilt by the glow of the moon. He quickly reminds her that if she isn't above being catty, then neither is he. ''Yeah, we aren't in a boyfriend girlfriend sorta thing, but you know damn well that I'd like to be. You just keep avoiding the whole subject, Amy. You afraid of commitment? Lisa means nothing to me and if I could make her disappear, I would. Perhaps you could bitch slap her for me?'' Amy snarls, ''If beating her wouldn't scream 'HEY WORLD, I'M BANGING KILLEAN!', I'd gladly to it. And why the hell would I be afraid of commitment?'' As soon as she said that, she knew that she didn't want him to answer. It was too easy, too painful... but it won't stop him from saying what's on his mind. ''Look, I wasn't the one wanting to keep our relationship secret from everyone. That was a death trap in the first place. I've always wanted a full relationship with you but you never even want to discuss it. Should I even mention that previously, you slept with me and then told me we shouldn't see each other anymore? What kind of signals are you sending out?'' ''Since you've apparenly become an expert, why don't you tell me?'' ''You want me to tell you? If you don't know, how could I? Something strange has been going on with you lately. I never know where you stand and when we make arrangements, you change that without even telling me. Honestly, am I the only one you're 'banging'?'' ''How can you even ask me that? I've been...'' she sighs, ''Fuck, that's selfish talk again. But NO, I haven't been making lo---errr--- fooling around with anyone else.'' After correcting what she saw as a possibly too-serious slip in wording, Amy falls backwards onto her couch, barely able to see Killean without electricity to power the lights. She looks confused but, more than anything, she's trying to cover something... ''Well how about this. After King of Kings, I came to your hotel room as we agreed upon and all I see is a 'Do not disturb' sign. Then I tried to call your room from my cell phone and heard it ringing from outside the door but guess what? You didn't answer. Not even the front desk could get a hold of you. So since the hotel bar was open, I went to have a drink. But all I heard was that two women, both with wildly colored hair were in there earlier and they appeared to be quite wasted. The bartender said they were quite into each other, too. I mean hell, I'm a guy and I like the whole girl on girl thing, but Karina?'' He shivers at the thought he had burned into his mind. For a second, Amy thanks god for the darkness, so Killean can't see how red her cheeks just became. ''I... look...'' She steps to her door and, despite the darkness, seems to have no problem pointing outside of it. ''There's the door. I'll see you at the show.''
