Worst case scenario

23 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd August 2003 So, how do Pranks With Attitude start the day? A 6am run replacing the newspapers with fly paper? A vigorous effort to stack all the trash in the street in the middle of the road? In the imagination of many, Nerezza kicks off his coffin lid and shouts out loud, bringing Gaz down, fully dressed and ready for pranking. But in reality, the ringing of the doorbell at 7:34am barely touched their ears. Their rented accommodation was no longer the lofty penthouses of Vegas, but now a simple and lowly excuse for a house located in the east end of London. 2 bedrooms, a joined dining room and kitchen, a bathroom and an overgrown, unattractive garden. Until PRIME had restored their financial security, it was where they’d have to live. A second doorbell chime rang out through the house. Gaz stirred on his mattress, then rolled over towards the wall, facing it and mumbling. “Rezz… get that would you?” In the next room over, Nerezza remained completely motionless beneath the huge, bright pink and fluffy cover. Where the pillows were, his boots protruded. For some bizarre reason, his bedside table had a picture of Tom Jones in a frame with cracked glass. “I’m busy.” Came the reply. “Doing what?” Gaz shouted back. “…” “Get the door.” Gaz said, lying on his back, mouth wide open with a yawn. “Wait – this is a Monday. It’s your turn.” Nerezza said, throwing the covers off and sitting up, staring at the wall where Gaz seemed to be talking from. “I got the door twice yesterday. You owe me one.” Gaz quickly took his words back as a few chunks of plaster and wallpaper sprayed out from the wall. He sat up quickly and spat the remnants of dust and debris from his lips, wiping his eyes free of the same stuff. Emerging from the wall was Nerezza’s left hand, balled into a fist with one thin, bony middle finger extended. Not to be taken lightly, Gaz reached under the bed and took out a small bag, first pulling out a welding mask… “We had a stern agreement. It goes by days, not times. It’s like how I do the cooking and you do the goldfish and the garden. Except the first day, you wanted goldfish pie with extra roses. And… what’s that clicking sound? Are you feeding the chipmunk Red Bull again?” Nerezza withdrew his hand and peeked through the hole he had just made. “If he goes on another rampage, I’m not going to use the cheese grater this ti… OH SHIT!” The vampire ducked just in time as a huge burst of flames burst through the hole, just clearing the top of his head. Gaz’s arm followed through with a blowtorch in hand, sweeping left and right to make his point. A few seconds later, Nerezza was crawling along the floor in a dark red bathrobe towards the stairs. However, before he managed to reach the first step, a huge crash from downstairs startled him. Peering around the corner, he saw the front door, taken off the hinges, being thrown in sideways and a monstrous, inhuman shape following it. Clad in hunks of burlap and paper held together with tape, it staggered in like a drunken scarecrow, muttering obscenities before falling flat on its face. Nerezza stayed rooted to the spot, watching the immobile creature before scuttling back to Gaz’s door, standing and knocking on it gently. “Gaz… Gaz… come here and be quiet…” “HUH!?” Gaz bellowed as he slung the door open, with a pair of toothbrushes sticking from each corner of his mouth and an unbelievable amount of toothpaste running down his chin. “Rezz?” “Shhhh!” Nerezza held a finger up to his lips then pointed to the staircase, whispering to Gaz. “There’s some big sack man down there.” “Magnum Wolf?” Gaz recoiled in surprise. “No, a walking sack!” Nerezza corrected him. “Magnum Wolf?” “A person inside a sack that was walking about and has now collapsed on the floor.” Scratching his head in confusion, Gaz peered around the top of the staircase and into the dining room below. Sure enough, a motionless man of burlap, paper and tape lay in the middle of the floor, silent aside from the occasional grunt that sounded a little like swearing. Gaz dashed into his room, spat the toothbrushes into the sink and wiped his face down. Gazing down again, Gaz began to descend the stairs slowly and quietly while Nerezza waited at the top. As soon as he hit the bottom step, Gaz put spring into his step and jumped over the offending mess of humanoid scrap on the floor and snatched a banana from the kitchen table. Within seconds, he had rolled next to the sack-man and shoved the banana into his temple. “Don’t move or I pull the trigger!” Gaz cried out loud. Seeing his partner had got the situation under control, Nerezza made his way down the stairs, picking up the door and shaking his head, crudely stuffing it up against the frame in an attempt to at least make the house look secure. Dusting his hands off, he leaned up against the wall of the dining room and crossed his arms, staring at the intruder. Gaz looked at him, then pushed the banana harder into the temple of the burlap invader upon a nod from Nerezza. “Don’t try any sudden moves…” Nerezza warned the hostage. “What did you come here for?” Gaz demanded. “I have a delivery to make!” came a voice that sounded like audio sandpaper. “This is the residence of Mr Gareth Green and Nascosto Nerezza, right?” “Yeah…” Gaz answered, still highly suspicious. There were many who decided that the best form of revenge in P.W.A. was a counter-prank. However, nobody had ever come close to successfully turning the tables on them. “Who sent you then?” “Check the sender label!” “No, you tell us!” “It’s a surprise!” Nerezza stood over the mysterious intruder brandishing the fruit bowl Gaz took the banana from, nodding to Gaz that the first sign of trouble would lead to several weeks of the offender’s inability to wash his hair without getting bloody hands. “Where’s the sender’s address?” Gaz said, pulling the banana away. “On the rear, where you’re supposed to write it, stupid!” Gaz frowned, then shrugged and grinned malevolently and he lifted a section of the sack up, then yanked it towards the “parcel’s” head, giving him a wedgie that the resulting yelp confirmed had the desired effect. “Let’s see… Rezz, this is apparently from Hessian! Says so right here, sent from the club he owns. Even has his real name.” Gaz chirped. “Why would he send us a person though?” Nerezza raised an eyebrow. “I get my blood delivered under medical conditions. I don’t hunt anymore. Unless…” “Oh, no…” Gaz dropped the banana and sat back, his face in his palms. “Oh, YETH!” Standing up, the sack-man began to claw away at his costume, ripping away sections of burlap and tearing tape apart. Bit by bit, shred by shred, the person’s identity became clearer. The jean shorts, the ponytail, the scruffy beard and the bizarre look in his eyes. “HEEEEEEERE’S WILLY!” Gaz and Nerezza instantly covered their eyes and turned away, until they remembered his real name. William Williams, better known to wrestling fans, sex offender registers and a good deal of the mafia and Al Qaeda as… “SKITZO!” the pranksters cried in unison. “But why the hell were you all wrapped up like that?” Gaz pointed at the rags on the floor. “You look like something out of Arkham!” Nerezza couldn’t help but look at himself, then Gaz and finally Skitzo in turn, almost coughing at the irony. “Well, I made a little mistake. You see, when Hessian started up the club, you donated a load of money to him, right, Drac?” Skitzo slobbered, wiping away a few leftover threads from his face. Nerezza resisted the urge to bend his Skitzo’s knees forward and nodded. “Yes, that was back when I was living under different circumstances. Around $13,000, give or take.” Suddenly, the vampire’s eyes lit up. “You’ve come to tell me he’s made it back and you came to deliver it?” “YES INDEEDY SIR DO!” Skitzo proudly proclaimed, raising his index finger into the air. “…” “Well where is it?” Gaz looked about the discarded wrapping on the floor, nudging it with his foot and seeing nothing. “Well…” Skitzo began, picking his nose in front of them. “You see, as I was packing it, one of me got confused.” “Screw you, you messed it up!” Skitzo snapped at himself. “Don’t blame me because you can’t read!” retorted Skitzo, pointing an accusing finger back. “NEITHER CAN YOU!” cried Skitzo, scandalised. “That’s hardly the point!” “Don’t change the subject, fool!” “Oh, you want some, do you?” “Hey, I didn’t ask to be involved in this!” “QUIET YOU!” Before Skitzo could land the haymaker he had planned for himself, Nerezza clamped his fingers around the psychological explosion’s wrist and got right in his face, baring as many teeth as his split-open mouth would show. “SILENCE, ALL OF YOU! …Where is my money?” “Well…” Skitzo began. “Like I said, there was an administration error and because of SOMEONE, we mixed up the instructions to remain in the office and the instructions to send the money in an extremely well-concealed package. So while I came here in this…” “The money is on the other side of the Atlantic?” Gaz interrupted. “You could put it like that…” Skitzo smiled apologetically. “But the good news is I was supposed to come over the next week anyway, so when someone sees the $13,000 laying there, they’ll send it along anyway!” Gaz and Nerezza exchanged glances, nodded and advanced forward. Grabbing Skitzo by the arms and legs, they carried him down the corridor leading to the front do… er… frame. Nerezza gave the detached door a kick, knocking it through the doorway and into the street. “Skitzo, listen carefully.” Nerezza instructed. “We’re going to send you on a very special mission.” “In three parts, no less!” Gaz joined in. “Message received, over!” Skitzo saluted. “First!” Gaz shouted, as he and Nerezza dropped Skitzo down into a fierce Double Atomic Drop, then raised him back up. “FIX THE DOOR!” “Second!” Nerezza roared, as they brought Skitzo down for a second time onto their knees. “FIX MY MONEY!” “And third!?” Skitzo squealed and he struggled in the air, followed by P.W.A. heaving him out of the doorway and onto the road. “FIX YOUR FREAKING HEAD!”
