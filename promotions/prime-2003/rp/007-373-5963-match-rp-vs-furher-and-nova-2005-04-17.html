007 373 5963 (Match RP vs Furher and Nova

17 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-17-05 It’s the club house of God’s stable known to the wrestling world as Imperium. The camera shows the wood paneled walls as they are decorated with pictures of Imperium members dealing out damage. There is a huge leather couch in the center of the room which is where Hoyt is sitting in the center with Vetra to his right and Baltasar to his left. Morales is leaning on the arm rest of the coach as Angelus is watching from behind the sofa. Vetra: This is it you’re the champ you can’t let this guy stop you. Morales: Match of your life. Baltasar: This guy is nothing Holmes that title is as good as yours. Angelus: Knock his ass out champ. Hoyt stares with determination as he enters the match of his young career. What happens will dictate his future and goals with in the company. The pressure is starting to sink into the confident mind of the Pontiff. Hoyt: I don’t know, I think I should back out now well I have a chance. Baltasar: What are you a Conchuda or God’s champion? Vetra: We are so close to taking this thing. The camera pans back exposing the giant plasma TV where on the screen lists Hoyt’s next opponent not Nova or Furher but the drunken giant known as Soda Popinski. Hoyt has taken on the role of Little Mac in the Nintendo game Punch Out. His only guide on this journey is his overweight black trainer known as Doc. It’s been a battle to get this far first they had to find the old gray box known as the Nintendo. After two minutes of setting it up Imperium spent the next two hours blowing into the cartage and slamming it into the Nintendo trying to get it to work. It wasn’t until Vetra rubbed the bottom of the game with alcohol until it worked. Morales: That Middle Eastern oil monger Bald Bull tired to take you out. Yes he knocked you around a bit until you mastered the three-hop stomach punch of doom. Then you sent his ass packing man. Your going to do the same with the alcoholic Popinski! Hoyt: This isn’t “Glass Doc” or the evil German Von Kiser with his evil mustache and scary laugh. This is a drunken Russian know as SODA!! Nothing is more satanic then Soda is you know how many calories and how much sugar is in one can of Soda and this guy is named after that vile substance. I’m going to win this match in the name of God like I do all my battles. Little Mac isn’t going to be shut down by some over rated booze lover. Bring it on. The cheers from the video game crowd can be heard as the match starts. Quickly Soda begins dominating the match absolutely destroying Little Mac. Angelus: Oh shit man! He’s kicking your ass Holmes. Three big uppercuts from Popinski sends Hoyt to the mat for an 8 count. He gets up and does some dodging being saved by the bell. Hoyt: I’m done. Angelus: Let me try I can take him. Baltasar: Hoyt has to finish his turn he’s not done yet. Morales: Oh damn Hoyt did you see what he said about you? 'I can't drive, so I'm gonna walk all over you!” Hoyt: Oh hell no he thinks he’s going to take me out? I’m God’s Champion I can’t lose. Vetra: What happened against Killean? Hoyt: He cheated by pulling my tights and hitting me with a loaded forearm. He should drop his title for his dirty tactics. Angelus: I saw that too man he had a hand full of tights. Morales: Your manager/trainer Doc has got some advice for you. Little Mac: 'His defense is too tough, doc!' Doc: 'Don't give up, Mac! He has a weakness........' Hoyt: Fat Doc is the only black man to ever inspire me. Together him and I are going to win this damn game and become the champion. You know Doc is dedicated; by the way he throws on that hot pink jogging outfit to ride a bike while I jog behind him. What a true black hero! Morales: As a manager he is an inspiration to all of us. I wonder what ever happened to him? Vetra: Don’t you know who that is? That’s obviously Reginald VelJohnson better known as Steve Urkel’s buddy the fat over weight cop Carl Winslow on Family Matters. Baltasar: How do you know this shit man? Vetra: I’m well read and deadly. Morales: I know that I rode with this Chulo after stopping at the Burrito King! Deadly is an understatement. The entire stable laughs at the statement as Vetra smiles and flips off Morales. Hoyt: ENOUGH!! Time for round two. Hoyt begins the match and gets his ass kicked and is sent to the match in the first thirty seconds. It looks all about over as Little Mac barley gets up in time. Angelus: Super Mario is counting fast. That fat whoop is trying to screw you. Hoyt: It’s ok we got Luigi on our side and he’s sick of Mario’s ego. I can guarantee Luigi will ref in the next version of this game. Hoyt risks Nintendo finger wrapping on the A and B button getting Little Mac back into the fight. He sticks and moves and jabs hard. Vetra: You GOT HIM!! Morales gets off the arm rest of the couch and walks over to the stereo system in the corner. He hits play on the tape play as the theme music from “Rocky” begins to play. Baltasar: You got him champ! Angelus: It’s over. Morales: Tyson here we come. As the music plays Hoyt takes down Soda Popinski and doesn’t stop there. Hoyt as Little Mac then steam rolls over the Turkish Terror called Bald Bull Two before pounding down with ease the Spanish dancer Don Flamenco, Hoyt gets the knock with out the left right technique. Then the music fades as the tension is built. Hoyt: 240 pounds of blackness from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania “Mr. Sandman” is a monster. Angelus: This guys only been taken down twice Holmes maybe you should let me play and rest your Nintendo hand. Hoyt: Callused thumbs and fatigue aside I’m going to take this black bastard down. A black boxer? That’s absurd and there is no place for him in the real world or a video game. He’s about to be taken down by Little Mac and with Doc in my corner how can I be stopped? Morales: Lets get it on. Vetra: Watch the sneaky ref Mario he may try to throw the fight with a fast count again. The first round begins and again Little Hoyt Mac is taken to school by the boxing legend. Hoyt is taken down one time in Round 1 and returns the favor zero times. In his picture between the round Little Hoyt Mac is busted up where as Mr. Sandman is fine. Mr. Sandman: 'Hey! Mac baby… Say goodnight!' Hoyt: Is he talking some kind of black slang towards me? This guy is dead I’ll just get some advice from my ever trusty black manager Doc Lewis. Morales: The greatest corner man in the history of the sport. Little Mac: 'Help! Doc!!' Doc Lewis: 'Join the Nintendo fun club today! Mac.' Silence fills the room as Hoyt drops the small controller to the floor. He looks shocked. Hoyt: My manager just sold me out! Join the Nintendo Fun Club? What is that some kind of black stable with black Nintendo characters like Mr. Sandman, Mike Tyson, ahhhh Bowser….and ahhh I think that’s all they got. Doc is a flipping sell out and I’m never going to trust another black man again. Hoyt enters the second round with a black cloud over his head and is again taken to town. The bell saves him as one more hit and it would have been TKO Hoyt. Hoyt: This isn’t good. I don’t need Doc Lewis I have Jesus Christ in my corner I’m going to rain the supremacy of God down on this colored fellow with no mercy. Vengeance in the name of the Lord will be served as I will strike down this sinner with furious blows and biblical jabs. Darky will go down quicker then Nova in a title fight. Heaven is UNLEASHED!! Vetra: Now your talking!! Morales: I’ll be your manager Holmes. Hoyt: I’ll watch my wallet. The guys laugh as Hoyt unleashes a great flood of domination taking down Sandman by TKO in round three. Super Macho man is next but God isn’t on his side as Hoyt KO’s him in three to become boxings champion of the world. I young upstart Mike Tyson tries to unseat the Litte Mac “The Miracle” with quick skill and hard punches. After beating Tyson Little Mac retires and writes a book about his greatness and his GOD Hoyt Williams. Morales: You are the champion, champion!! Imperium conquers again like the Spanish Conquistadors or the biblical Four Horsemen before us. Hoyt: Our greatness is unmatched and the same will be witnessed as I take out not one but two so called contenders for my 5-star championship. Kid’s, it’s not going to happen as the chapter Hoyt in the RISP bible is being written. Nova you called me out once and I walked out Champion. Furher how many times do I have to make you beg to GOD for mercy as I beat your little butt down. I am greatness and I am heaven sent. I am your savior and I am your champion. Deny it or accept it but understand you can’t beat me. I control the assets of PRIME and I control it’s titles and direction. If you think that is false then so mote it be. But come this week you two sinners are going to feel biblical power and might and the wrath of Hoyt. Now excuse me while I beat a false God in Rygar with nothing but this control, a yo-yo, a grappling hook, and God’s will. I’ll pray for you two later. =w=
