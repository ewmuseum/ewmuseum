Hi my name is...

28 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-28-2010 All this madness began because I finally gave in to Toad’s complaining. None of this would have happened if not for him. Maybe I’m getting a little ahead of myself, so let’s set things up, it’s 2003, I’m 16 years old, your typical high school outcast, with my gang of outcast friends, Will, a cool guy, not a weirdo like the rest of us, but for whatever reason he just doesn’t fit in with anyone else, Jeff and his girlfriend Rebecca, she hung around us all, Jeff and Toad both wanted to date her, she picked Jeff, that’s how things go, she doesn’t put out for him, which is a source of constant whining from Jeff. Toad is Toad, he makes me look feeble in gothdom, he’s been suspended a few times for wearing to much make up or putting on outrageous amounts of metal spikes on gloves, necklaces, all that stuff. Then there is me, Brian, but people tend to call me Nads, or Balls. My last name is Nadalny which got shortened to Nads, and from there the easy transition to Balls was made. My mom hates that my friends call me these names, I don’t care, one name is as good as another. One day we were on the grounds at the Salem Willows, loitering the summer away, I wasn’t needed at my dad’s garage that day so I could hang out with everyone. We were small talking, shooting the shit as usual, before Toad got us back to his favorite subject which is souls. There was a man named Richard Kimble in Salem, he did past life regressions, you went in and he’d hypnotize you and get you to go back to past lives, Weird stuff would be revealed now and then, like people who had phobias about dogs would find out they had been killed by a dog in a past life, spooky stuff. But that wasn’t why Toad wanted me to see this guy this was how we as a group measured ourselves, the age of our souls. I mean everyone else is driving cars, partying, doing drugs, getting drunk, and having sex. What do we got? We got old souls! Take that motherfuckers! Kimble had put Toad way out in the lead he had a soul that dated back to sometime around the birth of Christ. Everyone else was newborn babies compared to him, the only other person in our group who hasn’t seen Kimble was Rebecca, and that was because she had already constructed a past life situation, putting herself in ancient Egypt. This is how all women are, you ask any girl about her old soul and she’ll tell you she lived in Egypt. They are all full of shit, Egypt is a lie. “You going to see the man or what?” Toad finally brought the hammer down. I’d been avoiding seeing Kimble for months, why I was avoiding this I can’t say, I guess I just didn’t want to deal with the hassle and having to pay the 40 bucks, which seemed pretty steep just for a guy to hypnotize me and then ask me a bunch of questions that I wouldn’t even remember. “I’ll go if Becca goes.” I said trying to dodge yet another effort by Toad to get me to go. “Sure, I’ll do it if it means you’re going.” She replied back quickly, I was stunned, now I was boxed in, I had to do it. “Great, I’ll call him and find out when he’s free, lemme know when you’re not working for your dad and we’ll get you two scheduled.” Toad said he was so happy this would finally end all the foolishness of our group. Every now and then Will or Jeff would tease Toad about the potential for me or Rebecca to be older than him, it really got to him. Toad was about 5’3”, and he was about 50 or so pounds overweight, he was very Toad like, right now I couldn’t recall his given name if you asked me. About all he had was our little group, and in it he was the great diviner of knowledge and wisdom, he told us all how the universe worked, the sights he’d seen in his countless past lives. This was the one thing in this world he could hang his hat on. And then July 6th 2003, everything changed. I walked into Mr. Kimble’s office with Rebecca, he was there, older man in his early 50’s I’d say, he did everything occult based you can think of. Tarot card readings, séances, ritual purging of places or people who believed themselves cursed, you name it he did it. Past life regression was just one of his many services. “Hello Brian, Rebecca.” He said warmly as we got settled into the room. “Who wants to go first?” he asked. “Let’s get this over with.” I replied, and with that I was led into a different room with a large couch, like one a shrink would use. “OK Brian, I want you to relax…Stare up at the ceiling and focus only on one spot…Feel all the tension drift away from your body…Feel everything relax…Now move your focus from the ceiling, a point halfway from you to the ceiling…And feel how calm you are…” as he said these things, I could feel myself going away. Where I was going, I had no idea. I was drifting in space there was nothing around me anywhere. I wasn’t seeing anything nor was I hearing anything, I was merely conscious. I was awash in a blue light, how I knew it was blue or that it was light I can’t say. I felt like I was merged with the universe, it was the most peaceful relaxed feeling I had ever experienced. Supposedly during this time I was talking with Mr. Kimble about what was happening to me, but I don’t recall saying anything, I do remember the light slowly changing colors, from blue to light blue, and then again to white. Something happened and the next thing I knew, I heard his voice again “Brian, I want you to come back to your body, come back into the office where we are at now.” It was relaxed as it had been when he put me under, and I followed him, followed his voice, and the next thing I knew, I was back in his office, when I looked at him he appeared flustered. “What’s going on?” I asked him, confused. “Brian, you were under for almost two hours, I’ve never had anyone experience the things you said you had seen. You didn’t even find a past life in the time you were hypnotized, you were somewhere else completely.” He was sweating now. “Did I do something wrong?” I said, nervous that I’d somehow screwed things up. “No, no you did everything you were supposed to. I just don’t know what to make of it. I’ve already had to cancel Rebecca’s reading and a couple tarot card sessions due to the time I spent with you, don’t worry I’m not charging you extra. We need to meet again, I won’t charge. Something is very interesting about you, and I need to know what.” He said. I felt weird, it was one thing to be special, but it was another thing to feel like I was a project for him to study. “Dad normally gives me Wednesday’s off, so I can come by anytime on Wednesday if you want me to stop in.” I said, I felt better being the one to schedule things. “That would be fine, how about 4 PM?” he replied. “Sure” I said and so we’d meet again, six days from now and finally unravel the mystery off the vision from my regression and what it all meant. Rebecca was thrilled she had avoided being forced to admit she wasn’t from royal blood in Egypt and the story of my regression become all the rage in our little group. Everyone speculated that it was some great sign of importance and meaning, that I’d transcended humanity. Toad was upset he lashed out against everyone, called the story “Bullshit” that I was lying to Mr. Kimble about what I saw. I visited him the day before my second session. He was holed up in his parent’s basement, and he’d gotten his hands on some beer and had drunk himself into a stupor by the time I saw him. “You OK man?” I asked, about as stupid a question as a person could ask, given the situation, but there are really no good questions that I could think of. “Fuck you, you poser bitch!” was his well thought out reply. “You and you’re fucking blue light…You think you’re better than me now don’t you?! DON’T YOU?!” he shouted. “No, dude, I’m not better than you.” I said quietly, I’d never really cared about all this, it was a game, just something silly to talk about, spirits, souls, boasting you’ve been alive for 2,000 years or whatever. But it was all Toad had, and I’d taken it from him, he was crushed, and I felt awful about it. “Look man, who the hell knows what the light means, could mean nothing, could mean I never had a past life and all I know about is wherever the hell it is souls are formed.” I suggested “I could be a virgin to planet earth, breaking my humanity cherry right here and now.” I said, which drew a laugh from Toad. “Look man, this isn’t a contest at least it isn’t to me. Toad, you were in on the Council of Nicaea. For the last 1,700 years people been following what you said about Jesus, isn’t that enough?” I asked, oh to get him started on the Council of Nicaea that was to invite hours of conversation about the most unknown stuff you can even fathom in Christian lore. “Yeah, that is pretty cool I guess.” He replied while taking another drink from a can of bud. His dad doesn’t really care if he drinks, and with his mom off working this evening odds are dad let him get hammered to shut him up. Toad’s dad Roger really doesn’t know what to make of the lot of us nuts, I figure he thinks this is all a phase will grow out of. “I always knew you were gonna be the one to get me” he said. “I could just feel it man. You, you man, you’re fucking old.” Toad said he looked upset, but calmer than before. “What about Becca? She’s from Egypt after all.” I said wryly, he snorted, than held his nose. I nearly got him to laugh beer out his nose that has to be a good sign. “If every bitch who claimed she sucked King Tut’s dick really did then he’d have had half the world population guzzling his cum.” He shot back, I laughed, oh Egypt, you are the gift that keeps on giving. “So what’s going to happen tomorrow?” he asked. “I have no idea, guess I’ll go back and find out what the light is all about, getting to the bottom of it all.” I said, shrugging, I had gotten to his house late and was on curfew after a few nights to many being out. “I gotta run man, parents all over me, you know” I said. “Yeah, thanks for stopping in. You figure this shit out.” Toad said. “After tomorrow, you ain’t gonna be Nads no more, I’mma call you Old Man, old man.” he laughed as he said it. He was wrong about the Old Man part, but after that day, I did end up with a new name. The First.
