1. Scary 2. Shaggy (match rp)

10 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-10-05 Nova03-12-2005, 05:17 AM1. Scary2. Shaggy It’s your typical poolroom in the back of a private club. All five members of Imperium are in the small wood panel room engaged in a game of pool. The teams have been set as Williams/Vetra are taking on Los Diablo. Vetra: Why do I always get stuck with Hoyt on my team? Baltasar: Me and Angelus are tag partners we have a bond amigo. Angelus breaks as the balls go flying and a stripe rolls in. At the end of the table stands Morales, he is holding on to the money that is being wagered counting it again and again. Vetra: Looks like were solids. Hoyt remember how to play? Hoyt: I take the stick and poke it into the balls trying to knock one of our color in. Vetra: All of them except the black one, avoid the 8 ball. Hoyt: Just like in life I stay away from the blacks! Vetra: We are SO FUCKING screwed. In enters a pissed off black man with a big afro and a Jamaican accent. It’s the dancehall reggae singer known as Shaggy. Shaggy: What’s up with all this racist ass shit I hear coming from this cracker ass room? Hoyt: We’re ok on drinks thank you. Hoyt waves off the black singer as if he’s some kind of club servant. Shaggy: OH HELL NO!!! Baltasar: Hey you’re Shaggy!! Hoyt: I would say…shouldn’t you have a suit on, and a hair cut to be working here? Shaggy: You best be getting your oversized friend to back off before I put a cap in his ass. Hoyt: That would be an odd place to wear a ball cap…you blacks and your styles what will you think of next! Vetra: He means no disrespect Shaggy he’s just a bit….out there. Shaggy: His ass is going to be out there in a minute if he don’t back down. How come you big Mexicans have masks on? Your not about to jack this place are you? Angelus: We’re wrestlers and our identity is custom kept secret in Mexican folklore. Shaggy: I’m down with that. In enters a man of about six foot in height and thin as a post. He is wearing an Olive V-neck T-shirt, brown jeans, and black shoes. The man has some scruff on his chain and a sloppy bowl cut hairdo on his brown head. The man looks lost. Man: Like has anybody seen Imperium man? Vetra: Who’s asking? Man: My name is Norville Rogers but my friends call me Shaggy. Shaggy: My name is Shaggy man. Shaggy-white: LIKE WOW!!! Me and you might be related. Morales: He’s black and your white, a first name doesn’t make you related holms. Shaggy-white: Oh man that’s like such a bummer. Shaggy-black: This is one fucked up group of crackers!! What’s next a dog named Scooby? Shaggy-white: I had a dog named Scooby but he died last year! It’s a sore subject because his replacement Scrappy was a dud of a dog, man. Anyways I’m here because you’re usual RISP/Imperium private jet plane pilot Fred has food poisoning and we were buddies from high school so he like asked me to fill in for him. Morales: Is the plane ready to go to the Vatican man? Shaggy-white: ZOINKS! It sure is. I’m just going to light up a quick dub first. Hoyt: Let us pay our respects to the pope and go to his funeral. Shaggy-black: You crackers are going to see the Pope? Hoyt: Oh yea him and I are old friends I need to say good-bye. Shaggy-black: That’s dope man!! You g’s got a private plane…that’s sick! Hoyt: I don’t see what’s so sick about it nobody has gotten ill on it. Shaggy-black: Let me ride along and things will get ill! I’m down for seeing the pope! Morales: Sure man, come on board. Hoyt: When is this man going to bring us a drink? Two hours later the seven men are on the luxurious plane that looks more like a flying living room. Vetara and Hoyt are playing chess while Morales is asleep on the sofa. Angelus and the black Shaggy are watching a movie on the plasma TV. Shaggy white is flying the plane, smoking a joint, and is unseen in the cockpit. Shaggy-black: Yo this plane is boombastic!!! Hoyt: No, we don’t allow Muslims on here. No worries about bombs. IS he going to get me a drink or what? Vetra: Your move. Hoyt: I know!! I’m thinking over here. You know, I have a lot on my mind with Killian this week. Last time we locked up I kicked his ass but this is for the world to see. This is in homage of the Pope and MY greatness. This will not only put Imperium on the map it will highlight OUR commitment to excellence. Vetra: You’ll do fine you’re Imperium. Hoyt: Darn toot’en! Shaggy-black: Did you just say darn toot’en? What is this 1964? Hoyt: I’m sorry I’m not all shizzle this and shizzle that and down with the g-unit and pump shoes. Shaggy-black: Well you made it to 1980, congrats. Morales: Keep it down over there man; I’m taking a siesta! A few hours later the 7up club make it to the Vatican. They are greeted by a high-ranking cardinal that looks like Don Rickles who escorts them to their sleeping units in the scary old Vatican castle. Thunder rattles the building as a storm is brewing outside. Shaggy-white: ZOINKS!!! What was that? Cardinal Rickles: It was God blowing gas he ate Mexican tonight. It’s thunder you puck head there is storm on the horizon. By the way who picks your cloths Stevie Wonder? Shaggy-white: Like I do man! Cardinal Rickles: What is this the United Nations? We got Africa, Mexico, San Francisco, and roidville. A door to one of the doors swings open scary the 8 men in the hallway. Entering into the hallway wearing Sponge Bob pajamas is President George W. Bush. Bush: Cardinal Rickles, my wife and I just saw a ghost!! Cardinal Rickles: Now if we can just find some Weapons of Mass destruction we might have something here. Nobody laughs as President Bush’s lips get tight in a Texas snarl. Cardinal Rickles: Who died? What is this a funeral…oh wait it is. Sorry. Bush: Hoyt is that you? The two men hug as they size each other up. Bush: I haven’t seen you since I needed your help to catch Sad-DAMN. How you been brother? Hoyt: You know how hard war is but I appreciate your help in the battle with Satan. Bush: That’s what I’m here for. If you need help with Killian this week just give me a holler. Hoyt: Still watching the wrestling I see!! Bush: Me and Laura never miss it! HE HE HE Hoyt: These are my Imperium brothers this is Baltasar, Morales, and Angelus. Bush: My brother Jeb is married to a Mexican women! Good people….good, good, people God bless you. President Bush shakes hands with Los D and their manager. Hoyt: Peter Vetra over here. Bush: The silent assassin I see. Hoyt: Not sure who these people are, but they’re both named Shaggy. Shaggy-black: What up G it’s an honor to meet you. Bush: G-Dub if you will. Shaggy-white: WOW this is like so cool you and I went to the same school! Bush: HE HE HE HE HE! We did that’s great! Out of the walls enters a mystic looking free roaming vapor that bares a resemblance to the Pope. Ghost John Paul: LEAVE THIS CASTLE AND NEVER RETURN!! I HAVE SPOKEN TO GOD AND HE WANTS YOU ALL TO GIVE UP THE CULTURE WAR AND RETIRE!! WHOOO WHHOOOO WHOOOOOO The spirit moves in closer as the 9 men start to run in separate directions. Cardinal Rickles and President Bush run off, Los D and Morales go another way, Vetra and Shaggy White go another, while Hoyt and Shaggy black run to a big black door. We rejoin Peter Vetra and Shaggy white as they are in a dinning room with a fireplace. Vetra: Why does this shit always happen to me? Shaggy-white: You too, like I thought I was the only one. Vetra: I think we’re safe now…. The ghost comes through the wall and slowly starts to sneak up on Vetra. Shaggy notices the ghost but is too petrified with fear to tell Vetra. The Shagster is shaking uncontrollably and biting his nails. Vetra: …no way is the Ghost Pope going to get us in here I locked that door with a bolt. If he does I may just have to kick his ass and show him why they call me the silent assassin. I ain’t afraid of no ghost they can’t hurt you. They are nothing…what….what is it….huh? Peter Vetra turns his head slowly to see the scary ghost. Like a bolt of lighting Vetra turns to run as fast as he can but ends up crashing into Shaggy knocking them both out. The camera then goes into the piano room where Hoyt and black shaggy are rigging up some kind of device. Hoyt: You see my urban educated black friend when the Polish Ghost Pope enters the door this string will catch sending the piano flying down and knocking him out. Then we will get to the bottom of this mystery. Shaggy-black: I just came here to see the Pope not some Ghost. Ghosts are white people shit you never see no movies with blacks and Ghosts. Black folk ain’t that crazy. Shit last time I chill with white folk in a castle. Hoyt: Well I need you to bait the pope in here so why don’t you start singing. Do you know any Sammy Davis Jr. songs he was black! Shaggy-black: I know how to put my foot in your ass. I’m not going to be no bait. The doorknob starts to turn. Shaggy-black: Forget this shit! Shaggy backs up, as Hoyt gets ready for his trap to work. The door swings open and a piano comes crashing down Hoyt runs up to see the Pope ghost only to find Cardinal Rickles crashed and out of it. Hoyt: Oh damn. In walks President Bush in his perfectly pressed PJ’s. Bush: No his mouth was getting to me anyways. How are you guys doing? Hoyt: Ok Mr. President. A second later Los D and Morales run into the room. Morales: Did you get the ghost man? We heard a crash. Hoyt: No, it was Rickles. Morales: Good he was making fun of Mexicans! The guys congregate in the center of the room when out of nowhere enters the Popetergeist. Ghost John Paul: I warned you now you will pay!! You should have taken out the feeding tube while you had a chance because now I’m going to put you all on one. America makes me sick, and the RISP is a con, not to mention Imperium’s weakness. YOU ALL ARE ABOUT TO DIE!!! From out of nowhere enters Vetra and White Shaggy dressed as Old Italian barbers. Shaggy-white: A free-a-hair cut for-a-ghost Popes!! Vetra: And-a-free a shave if your dead! Ghost John Paul: I’m a ghost pope!! Shaggy-white: Well then-a-sit-a-down! Vetra pulls a barbers chair out of a corner and sharpens his razor. Shaggy begins to cut the Popes hair as Vetra applies the shaving cream. He covers the ghosts face in cream. Ghost John Paul: Hey…HEY…wait a minute. Before he can recover the 8 men jump him and hold him down. Hoyt: So Mr. Pope you thought you could scare off the American’s did you??? You thought you could out smart us. Not a chance. Shaggy-black: I don’t think that’s a real ghost it looks like a mask. Hoyt: Pease what do you know. Bush: I think the colored fellow is right. Angelus bends down and rips off the ghost mask to reveal….. Everybody: DON KNOTTS!! Don Knotts: What the hell did you expect?? I need work and nobody has been calling me for movies, TV, or even wrestling promo’s. Last week Hoyt you did a spin off of three’s company and I didn’t get a call. No way was I going to let you do a Shaggy promo with out me. I’ve had enough I’m putting my foot down. Hoyt: How could you do this to us Don!! This is terrible now were going to have to arrest you and throw you in jail and your so old your going to die there. Shaggy-black: That’s not Don Knotts. Hoyt: Sure it is look at him! Bush: I think the black guy who didn’t vote for me is right. Angelus bends over and rips off the mask to reveal…… Everybody: French President Jacques Chirac!!!! Hoyt: Why President Chirac. President Chirac: Because I hate Boosh and America and all their religion. I hate you Hoyt for fighting the liberals and I would have gotten away with this if it wasn’t for you pesky kids!! Shaggy-white: I like hate the French ZOINK! Bush: ME TOO HE HE HE HE HE Vetra: What a waste of a trip. Bush: Lets get some sleep and pay our respects to the real Pope tomorrow while Jacq strap here spends the night in jail waiting for his global trial. Then I’m going to give you guys all the Congressional Medal of Honor for foiling his scheme! You don’t mess with the USA, Texas, or Imperium!! Now have a good night. Hoyt: I have a match against Killian where this Congressional Medal of Honor winner will show the world once again why I’m God’s champion. Why God’s title is the top title in Prime and why Imperium is in absolute power. =w=
