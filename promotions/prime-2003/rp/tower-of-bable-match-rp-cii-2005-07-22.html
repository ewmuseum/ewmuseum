Tower Of Bable (Match RP - CII)

22 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 22, 2005 Part I – The Prologue Every man has a breaking point; the point at which physical, mental or emotional strength gives way under stress. Different people can responds to various levels of stimuli before reaching the proverbial ‘end of their rope’ but where that rope’s end lies, and for whom, is the real conundrum. Maybe an unkempt house. Perhaps the death of a loved one. Even prolonged or sudden financial struggle or collapse is as likely a reason as any other. But for “First Blood” Daniel Pierce it would be something altogether different. Part II – The Breaking Point (A Flashback) ReVolution 56 Still lacing the ring with his red fluids, Sirrajin scoops Pierce up and slugs him with a couple of weary right hands, before motioning for an irish whip. First Blood puts his foot down though and counters by pulling the champion into a desperation short arm clothesline. Determined that not having his newfound stablemates at ringside would be a factor, Pierce lets out an anguished cry and spits a mouthful of blood airward as he almost appears to be preparing for the Supreme Justice. Nick: Don't tell me he's going to steal the Supreme Justice and use it against Killean Sirrajin! Richard: Yep, that's exactly what he's doing. First Blood is about to win the Intense Title with the champion's very own finishing move! Taking the necessary run-up, First Blood moves in to kick Sirrajin's legs from under him, but the Supreme Machine counters with a textbook inside cradle. That's always the drawback of trying to use the moves of others... they normally know every possible counter and escape outlet to boot. Nick: Supr... wait, it's countered! Inside cradle! Inside cradle! One!...Two!...Three! The ALLTEL Arena roars with approval as Sirrajin releases the awkward looking pinning combination, allowing the official to raise his blood plastered hand in victory. First Blood on the other hand simply sits up, staring blankly into the distance as crimson continues to trickle down his face while the champion is re-acquainted with his title belt. WINNER: KILLEAN SIRRAJIN Nick: Killean Sirrajin has seen off the challenge of First Blood as well as the dangers of competing in that glass ring to retain the Intense Title, although that probably still seems scant consolation after what happened at Culture Shock. Richard: He should just be thankful he's still got that belt, First Blood was so close to a deserved win, only to be robbed... his time will come Nick, trust me... his time will come. Nick: Well that remains to be seen… Part III – The Prologue Continued For Daniel Pierce it was failure that drove him to the brink… failure that pushed him to the edge… failure that caused him to skirt insanity. Never had he studied his opponent as hard or wanted a win as much. It didn’t matter, though. He would be beaten by an Inside Cradle Pin! How ridiculous! How preposterous! The simplest of all wrestling moves, a maneuver that any student would be taught in his first week! And a sloppy one at that. A bloodied and beaten Killean capitalized on a small error and cost Pierce the belt, his pride and a vital win. The gold and the glory Pierce could have overcome, but the win that he felt he deserved – to have that slip away in the blink of an eye was… was… It’s said that life is 10% what happens and 90% how we react to it. Part IV – The Reaction (Another Flashback) ReVolution 59 We find ourselves in a darkened room. There is a beautiful leather couch against the eastern-most wall and an entertainment center against the northern-most, to the south a doorway and foyer, and to the west a hallway and a window. The window serves as the only source of light. The luminescence of the moon cascades throughout the room, in the center of which sits a well-known man; none other then Our Hero, Daniel Pierce. He sits, slouched, in a Lay-Z-Boy recliner wearing his usual red slacks and a white tank top. There’s something in his hands, we can’t quite tell what it is, it’s the focus of his attention, though. Our first thought is that he is plotting something, likely against Killean Sirrajin for retaining the Intense Title. But upon further inspection we can see that he is lost, stumbling around in a world, which exists purely in his own head. His eyes are welled up with tears. This may be the weakest we’ve ever been allowed to see this hardened Dark Ager. Finally we get to see his hands - still covered in lacerations from the Glass Ring match, the FAILED Glass Ring match. But what really catches our attention is the contents of his right hand – a 9mm Beretta. Pierce stares sadistically at the weapon. Not smiling, not showing any pleasure at all, but intent. What is he planning to do with that firearm? He holds it up to his face, examining it. After a few moments he jams the barrel of the gun deep into the underside of his jaw. He tilts his head back and closes his eyes. As he tightens his finger around the trigger the shot whips over to the door behind him as it flings open. Eve: DANIEL!!! 'BANG!' Simultaneously the scene washes out to a glowing white with Eve sprinting towards Daniel's chair as the sound of the gun still echos... Part V – The Part you Didn’t See The bullet tore through the flesh at the bottom of his mouth and blew a hole in his tongue before shattering almost all the teeth on the left side of his mouth and then, finally exiting through his left cheek, causing minimal damage to the Maxilla (the upper-jaw bone, the lower is called the Mandible bone). Eve changed her direction mid-run and grabbed the phone. She dialed 911 as fast as she could. There was so much blood. She’d never quite seen anything like it. In 6 years of wrestling nothing had ever grabbed her quite as harshly. The reality was too much for her, in fact. But she struggled through the phone call and waited, waited what seemed like forever for the ambulance to show up. Immediately Pierce was rushed to the hospital, as was Eve – for a self-requested psychiatric examination. After 11 hours in surgery Pierce had his jaw rebuilt and new teeth put in, and his tongue sewn together - he was functionally fixed and that was all the government was willing to foot the bill for. Anything further would have to be taken to a private institution. Pierce found the best plastic surgeon available in Canada and had a consultation with him to re-construct the skin on his face and make him physically ‘normal’. After several surgeries and several weeks he had been rebuilt to his satisfaction, but mentally he was destroyed. Eve had been released to return to PRIME after just two weeks, but Pierce would take a little longer. 4 months. 16 weeks. 112 days. 2688 hours before he was released to return to a wrestling ring. It was a long, long time since ReVolution 56 and a long, long time was more than enough to train. He felt stronger, faster, tougher and his PRIME lay in front of him. Part VI – The Tower Of Bable Genesis11:1-9 1 Now the whole world had one language and a common speech. 2 As men moved eastward, they found a plain in Shinar and settled there. 3 They said to each other, 'Come, let's make bricks and bake them thoroughly.' They used brick instead of stone, and tar for mortar. 4 Then they said, 'Come, let us build ourselves a city, with a tower that reaches to the heavens, so that we may make a name for ourselves and not be scattered over the face of the whole earth.' 5 But the LORD came down to see the city and the tower that the men were building. 6 The LORD said, 'If as one people speaking the same language they have begun to do this, then nothing they plan to do will be impossible for them. (NIV – Emphasis Added) Part VII – The Here And Now Pierce reaches up and twists the air vent to the “ON” position. After a brief, hiss-free moment, he sits back in his seat and sighs to himself. Pierce: No air. God damn, it’s hot in here. I need rest and a proper flight. I can’t be unsettled. It’s my big return and one-year anniversary this week. Colossus II. Nothing else compares. It’s going to be the biggest card of the year, the biggest show of the year and the return of the greatest wrestler PRIME has ever seen. A bit dramatic, perhaps, but Pierce has his doubts. This serves, really, as an avenue to build himself up and hype himself for the match. It was a big one. His last Intense Title match was his last match ever… until now. This one coming up wouldn’t be against Killean, though. Killean had moved along. A 4 month set-back had prevented Pierce from doing the same, but thanks to his connections with Eve, a good woman who was willing to give up her position in order for Daniel to have another title shot and a chance to set himself back on the path to the top of the fed, he would start right where he’d left off. Stewardess: Sir, could you please put your seat upright for takeoff? Pierce: (Nodding, thinking more about his return and his past than the words of the flight attendant.) Sure. And even as mush as he felt had had earned/deserved the shot he still felt that he could blow it and be knocked back down to the level of… Peter Vetra or John Edwards. And who would be his opponents? Dani Furher and Trashcan Man. Stewardess: Sir? Anything to drink for the flight? Pierce: I’ll take… a Coke, I guess. The stewardess happily handed him the beverage and he drank ¾ of it in one huge gulp. The latter was new blood. Pierce had known nothing of him. All he did know was what he’d seen on ReVolutions. TCM had an impressive losing streak, broken after the Overkill PPV in the epic Glass House Match. He’d retained in only one match against Token Weed which he’d won in what Pierce was sure was a PRIME record 30 second match. Pierce crushed the can in his hand and threw the crumpled bit of aluminum into the aisle. The flight attendant gave him an angry look as she picked it up, but he was vastly oblivious to it. The only other thing Pierce knew about Trashcan Man was that he, too, was slowly drifting into insanity. Treading ground Pierce himself had walked. In so far, in fact, that an entirely new personality was coming out. The one referred to as Mr. Smiley Face. What an interesting situation to be in. Pierce was sure this man was a weakest link in the chain of this match… mentally for sure. Physically? Logistically? Spiritually? Who is to say? Pierce fidgeted with the air vent again, tapping it and twisting it, jiggling it… nothing worked. But the man in the seat beside him got very angry. Man: Do you mind, buddy? Pierce: It’s just… it’s so hot. Man: Do you feel a need to share this misery? Pierce gritted his porcelain teeth and bit his scarred tongue. Instead of engaging this man any further Daniel simply pressed the Call Button. The other… Dani Furher… or Vangelus Olsig. SIDEBARWhat’s with the name changes!? Can’t we all just stick to one name and be done with it? Was your birth name not good enough for you people? Unless your father named his son ‘Sue’ deal with it!/SIDEBAR Stewardess: Yes sir, how can I help you? Pierce: My air vent is broken. Stewardess: I’m sorry sir, there’s nothing I can do for you. This flight is sold out, I can’t change your seat. Pierce: There must be someone who’s not using their vent that could change with me. Stewardess: There’s only just over an hour left. just sit tight and We’ll get you to your destination. She begins to walk away, but Pierce insists. Pierce: I really would prefer if you could— Man: I really would prefer if you could SIT DOWN! Pierce sits in his seat… trying to get comfortable… only an hour left. The two had never met one-on-one before. That last time the two were in the ring together, the ONLY time the two were in the ring together was a battle royal for the Tag Titles on ReVolution 44 where Furher was eliminated by Peter Vetra and Tyler Lopez… a match that First Blood went on to win. But back then Dani was little more than a curtain-jerker, and punk kid and lap-boy for the Dark Age. Now he’s come to a place of prominence in the fed and has established himself. Even losing a close-fought battle against Killean Sirajin for the Universal Title. Maybe the two had more in common than Pierce had first thought. But that was neither here nor there. After their altercation last week Pierce felt that Olsig had something special coming his way. But damn if it wasn’t hot… Anyway… Pierce had done enough thinking. He needed to get rested. The plane would be landing soon and he wanted to cram in as much sleep as he could before the match and pre-match training. He leaned his chair back and closed his eyes. He knew he could come back. He knew nothing could separate him from the win he deserved so badly. Nothing. Man: Hey fella, you got your arm on my arm rest. Pierce tries to be diplomatic. Pierce: It’s an arm rest… so I’m resting my arm. Man: Yeah, it’s an arm rest… MY arm rest. Move your arm, fella. Pierce: I’ll tell you what… switch me seats and I’ll let you have both arm rests in exchange for your air vent. Man: I don’t wanna move. YOU move. The lady in the aisle seat is starting to get upset at this point. Woman: Would you two shut up!? Pierce rolls his eyes and grits his teeth. The heat… unbearable… Man: So, mac… Pierce: That’s IT!! First Blood braced himself against the window and started punching and kicking the man until he had to get up to move out of the way, falling over the woman, who, then, also got kicked. Pierce kept swinging fist and foot until both the man and the woman had left or fallen into the aisle. Then he turned up all the arms rests and laid across the three seats with the vents open all the way. And when the people started complaining he put on a headset and listened to Elton John singing, “I’m Still Standing” he felt tall and proud and unstoppable. ZAA-ZAPZAP-ZAAAAP-ZAP-ZAP One of the flight attendants tazered Pierce into submission and shoved him back into his seat where he drooled over himself for the rest of the flight. Stewardess: We’ll let the police and the airport authority deal with him in Michigan… Part VIII – The Rest Of The Story Genesis 11:7-9 7 Come, let us go down and confuse their language so they will not understand each other.' 8 So the LORD scattered them from there over all the earth, and they stopped building the city. 9 That is why it was called Babel —because there the LORD confused the language of the whole world. From there the LORD scattered them over the face of the whole earth. (NIV) Part IX – The Prologue Fate is not something left under the control of man. God is where he is for a reason. All our hopes and dreams hinge on an unseen force. And when we get too high, when our tower starts to reach into the heavens He’ll decide it’s time to put our little wants to bed. Winning, losing, titles… they’re all part of the game. They’re all pieces of the puzzle. “First Blood” can only hope that Sunday is his day and that God is smiling on him. Although… last time he took F.A.T.E. into his own hands he carried Ignatius Lisieux through a 43-days reign as Tag Team champions.
