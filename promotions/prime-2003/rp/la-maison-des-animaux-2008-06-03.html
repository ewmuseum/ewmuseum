La Maison des Animaux

3 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 3rd, 2008 An expansive living room replete with PCU, Animal House and Old School posters, a case of Red Bull, already on ice, conveniently positioned next to a handle of fine Barton vodka and not one, but two glistening kegs can only mean one thing… …the frat boys of Delta Upsilon Iota have set up shop. And judging from the cavalcade of young women strutting about, the shop is already open for business. Comfortably situated in a black leather recliner sits the essence of fratastic himself, Colby Korver. With a blonde on one armrest and a brunette on the other, he does not seem to be in any immediate danger of losing the questionable reputation that he has spent the last seven years of college so carefully constructing. On the other side of the room, stands Hank Cobb, who at the moment seems only concerned with the contents of the room’s refrigerator. “Hey Korv, did you do somethin’ with my fried chicken, cause I swear I put it in here before I left to do my liftin’ earlier?” Korver takes yet another long swig of keg beer before answering. “Bro, you were hogging all the shelf space, what did you expect me to do?” “You tossed my sweet mama’s fried chicken for Smirnoff Ice and Arbor Mist?” “Bitches gotta drink too, big fella. This is no time for selfishness, we’re a tag team remember?” The brunette begins to take umbrage to the sexist remark, until Korver points out the Smirnoff Ice she is so thoroughly enjoying, sending her right back to incessant, albeit inebriated, fawning. “And remember when you threw out my Aunt Janetta’s potato salad and I told you that the next time you pulled that shit it would be your last day walkin’ without a limp?” “I dare you, Cobb. And just so your big country ass understands how serious I really am, I double dog dare ya.” Before the incensed redneck can return fire, verbally or physically, the door opens and a nasally voice comes leaking through. “And I, kind sirs, dare both of you pertinacious pugilists to abandon your asinine arguments and instead meditate on a mere morsel of my mental meanderings.” “So I gather you called him after all, Korv?” “You ‘reckon correct, huckleberry.” And with that, enters an absolute shoe-in for his high school’s “Least Likely to be Involved in Professional Wrestling” superlative. Standing less than five and a half feet tall and weighing in at little more than a hondo, this bookworm has surely stumbled into the wrong locker room, and would probably be wise to stage a hasty retreat, before his premature ejection is involuntary. Of course, things are not always as they seem. “Fukstick!!!” The aforementioned Mr. Packer sports an immaculately pressed white oxford and khaki pants combination, smartly complimented with a powder blue sweater vest and black wire-rim spectacles. Clearing his throat, he addresses his expectant audience. “Gentlemen, if I have beseeched you once, I have beseeched you an innumerable amount of times, will you please kindly refrain from utilizing the altogether unfortunate acronym that my parents so tragically selected?” “Well lil’ buddy, I reckon they shoulda thought out Franklin Ulysses Katzenberg a lil’ more carefully, huh? “A positively precise postulate, Mr. Cobb, but my moniker is far from the most imperative matter at hand.” And almost on cue, as so many happenings in the world of professional wrestling seem to be, booms out yet another unknown voice from the hallway. “Speaking of hands, you fools finally ready to admit that you could use some lessons from the greatest thing to happen to boxing since taped fists?” And as this understated introduction draws to its conclusion, into the house steps a young African-American male whose percentage body fat appears to be inversely proportional to his percentage confidence. “Cause it’s about time me and Franklin Roosevelt over here dropped some knowledge on you white boys.” After another futile attempt to clear the nasally quality from his enunciation, Franklin delivers his farewell address. “It is readily apparent that you cretins have chosen…poorly…to immerse yourselves in decadence and frivolity this evening. But come tomorrow, expect to be awakened at dawn’s outset, for our arrival shall mark the outset of the renaissance of your fledging careers, and I suggest that you ready yourselves not only physically, but mentally as well. For as President Washington so astutely observed, “Ninety-nine percent of the failures come from people who have the habit of making excuses.” With that, Fuksti…er…Franklin Ulysses Katzenberg spins on his heels and exits the premises, leaving the frat boys of Delta Upsilon Iota both speechless, and fairly amused. But before they can gather together their thoughts, tonight’s second visitor offers his own. “And although I, Dwight Tidwell, have every intention of using my expertise to whip you crackers into shape...” Tidwell glances around the room at the myriad of lovely young ladies. “…it can certainly wait until tomorrow. Yo C, throw a Heineken and one of these dime-pieces my way. Cause ya boy heard these white girls be getting crazy.” As Korver sends a green bottle flying, Cobb can only shake in head in amazement. “And here I thought things were already pretty interestin’ round here.” And as the frat house again descends into utter and absolute debauchery, the new brains of the operation shuffles down the steps outside, but not before issuing one final reflection. “Gentiles.”
