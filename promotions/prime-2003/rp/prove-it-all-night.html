Prove It All Night

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 and 4 Days Before UV A lot of times, guys are content with a win here and there against top talent. It keeps the paychecks rolling in, and guarentees you more TV time and exposure. For SB, it's not about the money. When you're a self-made millionaire through the stock market (and a little bit of help from dear old dad), and the owner of such fine establishments as Rossi Classics fine Menswear and SB's Italian Bistro, it's NEVER about the money. A lot of people have trouble understanding why SB does what he does. He could just kick back and relax if he wants, rather than risk his neck in rings around the world. NAPW paid him peanuts, but he didn't miss a show save for a near-fatal car accident. Dan Ryan made his career a living hell, but he never took his ball and went home. When other guys with his level of financial security would have packed it in, he stuck through it and came out better off for it. Now, with Angelo Frazetti, his closest friend and trainer back at his side to keep him hungry it's only a matter of time before he's in the title hunt in PRIME. But titles and glory are for another day. Ultraviolence isn't going to be flashy. It's going to be brutal, sluggish, and ugly as hell. Andrew Rossi - the rejuvenated, re-focused Andrew Rossi - is more than ready. At ReVolution 160, SB proved that he wasn't just flapping gums when he promised to get back to the basics and it showed with a thrilling victory over PRIME Hall of Famer Hoyt Williams. The time to feel good about it is over though. For this playboy, the real test is next. Last time he threw away an opportunity to beat Tony Rolo because he tried to prove a point, a point that ended up being moot. The best revenge isn't to embarrass him or even to maim him - it's to beat him in the middle of a PRIME ring, the same ring where he made his name and elevated PRIME as a company from infancy to an international power. If SB can do that to Rolo, it'll hurt more than any kick to the nether region could ever do. If SB wins this Falls Count Anywhere match at UV, then he'll prove once and for all that he can win on the big stage - the biggest knock on his career thus far. As Rossi fires through rounds of push-ups, Angelo sits on a lawn chair inside of his small, smoky, crowded gymnasium. There's young wrestlers working out on the bench press, a few MMA fighters sparring in Ring B, and The NFW's Dream Team are quietly getting a workout in before their next NFW house show. "The Runaway Train" Brian Duncan, the bigger member of the team, walks over and lets out a laugh. Duncan: How many you up to, Hollywood? SB: (grunting) Can't talk now, Bry. Duncan: (to Angelo) How many, Ange? Angelo: Three hundred. We're busy, come back in a half hour. Duncan: Son of a bitch! I couldn't even get out 50 'for I started hackin' up a lung! "The Natural" Mike Gordey, his tag partner and himself a student of both Angelo and SB, calls over from across the gym. Gordey: That's why you gotta cut it out with the booze and the cigars. Duncan: (without turning) Ahh fuck off junior. I don't need to worry about none of that shit. I kicked ass just fine when you were out hurt. Angelo: Go finish your sets, then come back over and we'll talk. Duncan: You got it, pops. Duncan walks back over to his partner, who tosses him a 30 pound dumb bell that Duncan catches as if it were a set of keys. Back on the other side of the gym, SB finishes his set. SB: How many was that? Angelo: You weren't keeping track? SB: I assumed you were. You just told Brian that I was up to three-hundo. Angelo: I was just tryin' to get rid of 'em. Pain in the ass, he is. I think you're closer to five. SB: All right, we're half way home. Then we have crunches and some sparring. Angelo: You want to spar? Today? The shows only five days away! Besides, I got nobody here who can mime for Rolo. I'd need to make a phone call and I honestly don't feel like callin' in any favors right now, this place is a fuckin' zoo and Duncan won't stop hitting on the receptionists. SB: They don't like him? Angelo: He smells like piss-soaked loaf of garlic bread that somebody ashed on. What the hell do you think? SB: Point taken. All right, so I'll make the call. Go take care of Brian. Angelo: Good, cause he's givin' me ulcers lately. Angelo heads over to the front desk, where the 6 foot 10, 300+ pound Duncan is curling a fifty five pound dumb bell, with his leg up on chair so as to give Debbie, the receptionist, a better view of his crotch. Duncan: So, when does Angelo unchain ya? Eight o'clock at Hooters sound about right? I'll pay for the drinks, sugarloaf. Debbie: Actually, I'm engaged. Duncan: Get the fuck outta here, really? You want me to kick his ass? Debbie: Why would I want that?!? Duncan: Cause I'm your soul mate? And he's gettin' in the way of me and a nice retirement house - maybe a few kids? Angelo reaches up and smacks Duncan across the back of the head, and moves the chair quickly causing him to nearly fall over. Duncan: The fuck are you doin' pops? I'm mackin' it over 'ere! Angelo: Can't you see she don't like you? Where the hell is Mike? Duncan: Who cares about Mike, you're breakin' up a beautiful thing. Angelo: Debbie, get back to work. (to Duncan) Get over here, I gotta show ya something. ==================================================== Outside, SB is on the phone with Angelo's close friend and a fellow trainer, Greg Haggar SB: Yeah, this is Andrew Rossi calling on behalf of Angelo Frazetti Haggar: How ya doin', kid. What can I do ya for? SB: I need a wrestler who can mime Tony Rolo for me tonight. Haggar: Hmm...you ever heard of a real young kid, Mike Gordey? SB: Uhh...I trained him. Haggar: Get the fuck out! SB: Yeah, I did. Angelo helped, but it was pretty much all me. Haggar: What's that? I can't hear ya too well, I'm breakin' up. SB hangs up the phone and heads back inside. ==================================================== Back inside, Angelo is talking to Mike Gordey while Duncan does an incline bench press. SB walks over to them, with a funny looking grin on his face. SB: Guess who my guy is? Angelo: Who? I'm not good at this shit. SB: Mike. Mike: I don't know either, I'm not from around here. SB: No, I mean you Mike. He told me to get you to help out. Mike: But...I never even seen Rolo wrestle in person! I can't mime the guy on short notice. SB: You still have the tapes we went over Sunday night in your office? Mike: Yeah, there's a few. Your first match with him, and his second Global Championship win. It's probably more of a help for you to watch the match he had with Andrew, Mikey. Mike: But I have my own match to worry about. I don't think I have time to help you out, Hollywood. SB: Ah c'mon! I trained ya, ya bastard! Mike: Fine. But you owe me a favor. (points at Angelo) Tonight. Angelo: Yeah, what's that? Mike: Get me and Brian dates with your receptionists. Angelo: No. Fucking. Way. SB stucks out arm out to hold back Angelo, who looks ready to rip Mike's head off. SB: Can you do it for me, Mikey? Mike: Sure. Hey, Brian! Duncan: Yeah? Mike: You still got that green suit? Duncan: It's a winner, of course I still got it. Mike: Good, cause we got ourselves a date tonight. Angelo shakes his head, and SB wraps an arm around his shoulder and musses up his hair. ==================================================== The Next Day Simply Beautiful stands in front of a camera in PRIME HQ. He looks intense, as intense as he's ever been. This match is huge - his first one on one match at a PRIME PPV. He's hit the big leagues now, and it hasn't been easy. He hasn't allowed himself to be booked in one easy match, and hasn't asked for any kind of reward for his grueling schedule. All he wants is the competition, the one thing in this world than drives him like nothing else. SB: Here we are. Just four days away from my biggest match yet in PRIME. I can spit out all the cliches I want, but just mentioning cliches is cliche in it's own right. I've been preparing like this is my last match, because due to the extreme nature of the night as a whole it very well could be. You're getting SB, Tony, at the very best I've ever been. I'm in top shape. My mind isn't being clouded by anything - I can thank an old friend for that. All I'm thinking about is our match. I can say a lot of things to drag you down, but why would I do that? You're above me, Rolo. You've got all the things I want. Words won't change that. This match, though, very well could. I can leapfrog you and head towards the top. I can suck out all the heat of every match you've ever had, and make it all mine. I can make your career...mine. But I'm no copycat. I'm going to do it my way. Hard work. Determination. And a passion that burns so hot it sears my skin. I said it before, but I'll say it again. I have re-invented myself once again - and I AM the future of PRIME. When it's done, no hard feelings, Tony. Let's just go steal the show, like we're both used to doing. But don't imagine you'll walk out the winner. It's destiny, and mine is yet to be written. SB spreads his arms wide, his muscles glistening in the light. SB: And page one begins at UltraViolence. FTB
