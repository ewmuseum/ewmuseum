Of All Time

27 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/27/2005 “Maybe I did overestimate you Ventra. I figured one thing I could count on this week was that I’d be up against a seasoned veteran, a man that didn’t make rookie mistakes. Yet it seems that I have you so badly on the bubble, that you’re pulling the little “let me spell it out real clear for you because you suck” routine. C’mon Peter. I’ve seen it happen and I’m sure you’ve seen it happen a thousand times. A guy tries to make a point that doesn’t go through, and so all he has left is to taunt the opposition about being stupid and not understanding the English language.” “If you think that @#%$’s going to work with me Ventra, then don’t even bother showing up at Revolution, because Pete, it’s not a question anymore of whether I’m going to win. No my friend, it’s now a question of how badly I’m going to thoroughly embarrass your soon to be swollen ego.” Fade Up. Welcome to the Anaheim Marriott, a luxurious hotel. The lobby is busy with many people bustling in and out of the hotel. As it gets closer and closer to the big event known as Revolution, the hotel has gotten busier and busier, up until last night when it filled the rest of its vacant rooms with people from all over the country gathering to see this PRIME event. Wrestlers, managers, bodyguards, promoters, fans, and even crazy amish people have been spotted heading in and out of the huge glass doors that serve as the entrance to the hotel. It is these doors we now find ourselves at as a limousine pulls up to the front of the hotel. Two bellboys hurry to the limo as they have learned that people arriving in cars such as a limo tend to tip very well. However, that isn’t the case for this limo as the doors open and out steps “The Scientific Apparatus” Tyler Lopez, by himself. He’s wearing a business suit. Lopez is quick to make his way inside the hotel. Heading towards the elevator, Lopez is stopped by a beautiful blonde working at the front desk. Slightly irritated, yet remaining calm, Lopez switches directions and heads over to the front desk. “Sorry sir, but there was a man here about an hour ago looking for you. He said it’s very important and told me to inform you that he would be in the dining room at 7:00 pm, and advised you be there pronto.” Lopez seems a bit puzzled. “What did this man look like?” “Well he looked like he’s another one of you wrestlers. He seemed a bit familiar like he’s been on posters a lot around these parts, but I don’t know, I don’t pay much attention to wrestling. Lopez nods. “If he stops by before 7, tell him I will meet him.” Lopez doesn’t even stick around to listen to the lady answer him as he is already headed for the elevators. He makes his way into an elevator with a couple other people and turns around to wait for the doors to close. Cut. We’re now inside of a hotel suite, presumably that of Lopez’s himself. The shower is running in the background as we glance around the room to find one of the nicer hotel rooms you’ll ever see. Even though it’s a quarter past six, in the summer months, it is still very light outside. The sun is seen making its way behind one of the tall skyscrapers, leaving a shadow over much of downtown Anaheim. As we glance out the window, the shower stops running and we hear a few noises before seeing Tyler Lopez step out of the bathroom in a white bathrobe. He makes his way over to the bed and lies down, propping his head up against two pillows. “Has the PRIME really moved on Peter Ventra? You did what I said. We never had to fight one on one Peter. Peter you didn't want to fight me because you knew I would win. Or at least were devoid of that famous confidence. Have you taken a look at the other wrestlers competing in PRIME. Hell, the what I did last week is all that the locker room seems to want to talk about. Even on my worst day, I'm still what everyone talks about. That’s power Pete. Something contrary to your popular belief, I’ve shown all week I have, and you don’t.” Lopez nods. “Apparently there is something in me. Maybe it’s the same thing PRIME saw in me when they told me I was wanted by PRIME, the same PRIME many men always wished that could join, but never had what it takes.” Lopez shrugs. “But whatever Pete, continue to think I’m nothing. Continue to spend the entire week trying to prove with words that I was a weak link. Since you obviously weren’t paying attention, Jackson Darilek tried that same exact tactic, and he ended up going home that night eating every word he threw at me.” “You might want to call him up and ask him how it feels, because your about to feel the same agony of defeat at the hands of a man who’s nothing more than a “lead weight.” Lopez just laughs. 'All this time secretly thinking that I was just another someone, all this time secretly thinking that you could be me. Continue to think that, and you will continue to make a fool of yourself. You lost it Pete. But what I really liked about your latest attempt to make me out as trash, is how you claim that you should have been awarded the PRIMEtime title. I found that quite entertaining, especially since you have done absolutely nothing to earn it.” “And even after I contradict your theories Pete, you then turn around and start speaking of me. News flash. Peter Ventra stands no chance pinning the Apparatus on his best day. Lopez shakes his head. “So we’ve gone from important to unimportant to important again. I mean @#%$ Pete, if you want to keep contradicting yourself every time you speak, I might as well just let you talk against yourself the rest of the week, because you seem to be doing my job very well for me as it is.” Lopez winks at Ventra. “Don’t worry Pete, don’t think I don’t have anything good to say about you at all. Unlike Jackson Darilek, at least you know what you want out of PRIME.” “You’re going to realize, that I’m no bitch. I know the names of the shows I’m booked on, and I know how to defeat Peter Ventra. Since you’ve become such a headache, I’m going to make sure that down the line, when people are trying to take the PRIMEtime Title, nobody will be able to say I can’t do it on my own, because if they do, then I’ll be able to say that as soon as I got the chance, I took Peter Ventra and all of his bullshit…” “And sent it on a one-way trip to Anaheim Memorial Hospital.” Lopez isn’t laughing anymore. “If I were you I’d hit up the bars and try to drink these last few nights away, because before you know it, you will be on one side of the ring, and I will be on the other. And as soon as the bell rings, the myth of Peter Ventra will be exploited for the overrated pile of lard it really is when the...” Lopez mocks Peter Ventra. “Greatest player of all time...” He stops mocking. End. Cut. 7:03 PM. Marriott dining hall. We focus in on Tyler Lopez as he enters the hall and gazes among the crowded tables until he sees a table for 2 with a seat open. He recognizes the hair he was hoping to see tonight. Lopez heads quickly over to the table. He rushes to greet the man who bothered by Tyler’s hand, turns around to be revealed as... Absolutely nobody. Obviously the wrong person, Lopez mumbles an apology under his breath and continues to look around for someone he would know. Not recognizing anyone, Tyler turns and heads out the entrance, making his way back to his room when the blonde lady at the front desk approaches him yet again. “Excuse me sir, that same man came by and said something about there being too many people here, so he gave you this.” Lopez is handed a sealed envelope with the name “Tyler” on the front of it. He nods to the lady and heads for the elevator. He steps in and as the doors close, he opens the envelope and reads the note inside. The camera can’t zoom in on the envelope, but we can see a huge grin that fills Lopez’s face. Obviously happy, he folds the envelope up and stuffs it into his pocket. He turns towards the camera and nods his head. “Don’t think that I haven’t forgotten about you, Jackson.” End.
