Showdown at the Rusty Zipper

15 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/15/2007 It's been going on thirteen years now since Chuck became the owner and bartender at the Rusty Zipper in Arkansas. In that time he thought he had done seen everything. Marriage proposals. Three-legged dogs that drink bourbon. Best friends try to kill each other. Seventy-seven shades of vomit. And titties. Lots of drunk-ass titties. Yeah, nothing could faze old Chuck... until that one night. She was a pretty girl, Asian... just like the ones in the porn Chuck like to watch to settle down after a long night of slinging brew. It looked like she had a few in her already judging by the way she was swaying when she walked. The boys checking out her behind didn't seem to mind the sway. And Chuck didn't mind a customer that got an early start. He'd serve 'em, let 'em drown their troubles, and make sure they got home all right. Chuck was a good egg, he didn't judge ya and he watched your back. The girl got herself onto a stool in front of Chuck. There was something familiar about her. Was she one of the one from those movies? Chuck imagined her face covered with white and creamy man-mayonaise, but that wasn't it. Then he had an epiphany. "Ain't you that one gal on TV?" The Asian girl rolled her yeys. At first Chuck though she was gonna pass out, the he realized she was just annoyed. "Oh Mao... yes , I am..." Chuck was pleased to have a bonafide celebrity in the Rusty Zipper. "Well slap my cheeks and call me Rosie! It's Tila Tequila!" A pair of dark almond eyes glared at him. "Huh?" "Yeah, you're that gal with all them firends on Myspace! Like a zillion of 'em!" "I don't have any friends..." "Awww... don't get all long in the face. I'm sure you made some buddies on that show where them normal folks dance and take their clothes off." "I have absolutely no idea what in the hell you're talking about." "So you ain't Tila Tequila?" "No." "Well... would ya like a tequila?" "Sure. What the hell. I'm celebrating." Chuck got her a shot of tequila and she knocked it back faster that a sorority girl when the Girls Gone Wild crew shows up. He poured her another and she knocked that one back faster than an underage high school girl with a fake ID when the Girls Gone Wild crew shows up. Although she was buzzing pretty good, she didn't look to happy, especially for someone that said she was celebrating. Kindly old Chuck just had to know what was the pretty gal's story. Or at least get her hammered enough to bang her on a pool table. Chuck was cool with either one. "So what exactly are ya celebratin,' miss?" "I finally got rid of the bitch." "Oh. So you're one of them..." "That's right. I'm a wrestler." Actually, chuck was gonna say "lesbians." In fact, he already had this fantasy worked out with this lady here and that one butch chick that fingerbanged Angelina Jolie. But he could work with a female wrestler just fine. He poured her another shot of Tequila and the little lady knocked it back faster than Chuck's wife at his wedding before they got in the limo to take them to the hotel. "So who is this other filly you talkin' about?" "Karina Wolfenden. I had some trumped-up terrorist charges brought against her. Then I told her she had to put her career on the line against me or else rot in jail. And I won! Woo hoo!" She held up her glass for another shot. Chuck poured it and she knocked it back faster than Chcuk's wife's niece at his wedding before he snuck her into the coat closet. She motioned Chuck to get close. He did, even though her breath at that particular point in time could peel the paint off the walls. "And you know the best part? I lied! I had her arrested anyway! Yup, dragged away in secret after the show! I'll never have to see her again! Nyahahahahaha!" Suddenly, the gal collapsed face down and wept like Chuck's wife after she caught him with her niece in the coat closet. "Awww... why the waterworks, lil' filly?" She sat up, tears streaming down her face. "Now what am I going to do? My whole career has been based around my loathing for Karina Wolfenden. Now that she's rotting somewhere, I got nothing! Nothing! I wish I was back in China!" The gal collapsed onto the bar again, weeping. A little old man in an Aerican flag hat slowly walks over to her and taps her on the arm. "China!? I still got a piece of sharpnel in my keister from you people! That's why old Pops has to hover over the toilet when he takes a dump!" The Asian girl sits up and glares at Pops. "That was Japan, you stupid, shriveled scarecrow." "Oh, yeah..." Pops shuffles off. But then the girl feels a tap on her other shoulder. There's a middle-aged man with a shirt that says "Earl" standing there. "Well, you people are respnisble for my daddy taking a bullet in the knee over in Korea!" "Korea? Listen, you...oh. Wait. We did shoot at you people in that one. Here, let me make it up to you. HIYA!" "YAAAAHHHH!" Earl clutches his knee, which has just been karate chopped by the Asian gal. She snickers as she watches him jump up and down. "Now you and your daddy have matching limps. Barkeep!" Chuck is fond of walking, so he pours her another shot. She knocks the shot back, then grabs the bottle from Chuck and totally Lohans it. As she slams the empty bottle on the bar, music pours out of the old jukebox. Stand by your man Give him two arms to cling to And something warm to come to When nights are cold and lonely Stand by your man And tell the world you love him Keep giving all the love you can Stand by your man Stand by your man And show the world you love him Keep giving all the love you can Stand by your BLAM!!! A bullet whizzes through the air and hits the jukebox! BLAM! BLAM! BALM! Three more follow it, rendering the classic machine lifeless. A tall and skinny man standing nearby looks bummed, so Chuck comforts him. "Don't worry, Zeke! I'll refund your quarter!" Chuck turns his attention to the little lady with the smoking 9mm. "Now why you done go do a thing like that for?" After a few tries, she manages to put the pistol into the back of her little red short. It seems chugging a bottle of tequila has her three sheets to the wind. "I dunno... mebbe cuz I ain't got no man. Anlego... Angego... Alelo... DEVILLE! He didn't wan' me. Dekur Shavin sez he wan' some o' my poon tang, but he can' even win a match fer a date wit me. if he reeeeeaaaaaaally wanted t'tap my Beijing badonkadonk, he woulda ripped Dush's head off! An' you know wah happened t'the first guy I ever smoocked? He's dead!" "Uh, you didn't shoot him, did you, little lady?" "Naaaaah... broke his neck wit my thighs!" "That's hot." "Kinda. Yeah. That is hot! I'm hot! Thanks, misser barkeep! Yer all right fer a capipalmalist pig!" "Aw shuck, missy. That's the sweetest thing I've heard all week. So brings to to Arkansas all the way from China?" "Rasslin'" "Like... Jell-o?" "Nah... pro. I'm in PRIME. 'Numbah One By Debbinishun!'" "Ohy yeah. I used to watch that on the Playboy Channel. Now I watch FUSE cuz I hate to miss any softcore changing channels. So who you rasslin'?" "Some yutz named Pierce summin.' Pierce... Navelle. Hey! Doncha tink I would look hot wit a pierced navel? I gots killa abs!" To prove her point, she stands on her barstool and lifts her shirts. Sho nuff, she's got some killer abs. The fellas in the Rsuty zipper whistle their approval. "Y'ain't seen nuttin' yet! Watch dis! I'm a belly dancer!" The Asian girl begins gyrating her hips on top of the barstool. The boys hoot, holler, and egg her on. But suddenly she stops. Her face looks a somewhat unattractive shade of green. "I don' feel so goooooo..." And with that she falls off her barstool and onto the floor with a rather ungraceful THUD. Chuck scamper around the bar to check on her as the other patrons gather around. "Is she dead, Chuck?" "No, Pops. Just passed out. "Should we call her an ambulance, Chuck?" "Well, Zeke... I was thinking we would bend her over the pool table and assrape her." "WOOO-HOOOOOO!!!" The boys at the Rusty Zipper are so down with that. But amongst the sounds of their celebrating are a couple of metallic clicks, Chuck and Zeke look down and notice they each got themselves a 9mm pressed up against the family jewels. All the fellas raise their hands of in surrender. The suddenly sober Asian gal kips up and starts backing out of the bar. She stops when she spots the pool table. "You were going rectally violate me on this filthy thing?" Chuck gulps in fear. "Well... we was just weighing our options, ma'am." The Asian gal reaches behind her back. When she brings her hand out, it no longer has a gun in it. But it does have a grenade. She pulls the pin out with her teeth and tosses it onto the table. Chuck watches with terrors as the grenade rolls into a corner pocket. "Aw, shiiiiiiii..." BA-DOOOOM!!! There's an explosion of wood and green felt in the Rusty Zipper! When the smoke clears, the Asian gal is gone! Pops looks over at Chuck. "Whatre you gonna do now? Ya ain't got no jukebox and ya ain't got no pool table!" Steely resolves comes over Chuck's face. "The only thing I can do, Pops... the Rusty Zipper is goin' queer." A side panel opens in the wall and the bullet-ridden jukebox vanishes into only to be replace by a large fern a second later. The demolished pool table is lowered into a trapdoor. In its places rises a buff, shirtless DJ. A disco ball lowers as a rainbow of lights shine down.
