Date: 11th April [Voice over - the Las Vegas Times] Las Vegas, Nevada Sunset Blvd. Sonny Las Vegas, the land of sin, where dames and hot shots come to dine in their vast amounts of hard earned cash, the can-dos of sin spread the atrocities that attract all those nationwide for a little flesh and chips. Course what’s Las Vegas without the slot machines and rackets … well its dirt and extremely hot weather, but this dear friends is the place to make a name for you, to become a wheeling and dealing business entrepreneur with a misdemeanour for expensive taste and fast women. The flashy life of Las Vegas is plastered on every billboard as far as the eye can see. The perfect all American family with the white picket fence, the mass amounts of jobs available, and the allure of a buzzing nightlife, but this is advertising! All is not well in the City of Sin. Anthony Damascus sat inside the Lion’s den in a posh suit, his blond hair slicked back with an extreme amount of wax. A dancer laced his thighs, flirtatiously dancing to a harmonic beat on the sound system. The room is filled with flesh and men in business suits, enjoying the lavish surroundings of the Damascus Empire. Anthony "Snips" Damascus looks toward the bar, filled with a face he doesn’t recognise and that’s the problem. His bodyguard and local corrupt detective is vacant from his party – a definite no in Damascus’s eyes. Shoving the woman off of him he slips out of the chair and buckles up his trousers. "Where’s Andre?" The crowd doesn’t beckon to the seething words of a snake. He’d allow them their big headline and a night in lock-up, little did he know, Tax Evasion is harder to dismiss in court than fraud. Meet Anthony "Snips" Damascus, the big bad of Vegas, the man only feared by the name Snips and it wasn’t of Jewish faith. Your pinkies would be going to market as a warning with a pair of garden sheers, if you asked nicely, of course! Now, you only go to this man when you’ve lost your lease and wedding ring in a black jack game, but if you don’t pay up, you end up just another soul in the dirt that surrounds this blossoming Metropolis of Sin. Damascus’s bodyguard Andre Stinpolli was a hot-shot detective with an eye for money and a taste for drugs who recently ended up in the joint on a drugs beef, and Damascus, well let’s just say Vegas isn’t all that original, what worked for Capone would work for Damascus. The damper little gent now faces the judge’s hammer and my dear readers; it’s only a matter of time till another man decides to fill the big shoes of Damascus loans, and with every shoe to be filled, another remains open. ---- Washington, D.C. Before he could answer her, the music immediately switched to a tango beat and the dance floor came alive with the Spanish rhythm. Lavelle’s eyebrows rose as she grabbed his hand and pulled him onto the dance floor, she leant up to him. "Can you dance?" Suddenly Lavelle spun her. "I can tango, but I can’t dance to anything else." "This’ll do …" The beat swept her around, she followed his footing, eyeing the crowd as he spun and tipped her. The crowd struggled around them, his Latino dancing seeming to out-do the politicians and millionaires around him. He swept her up into his arms, his footing sleek and simple; counting the beats in his head he followed the room. As the crowd parted she found her target and wrapped a leg around Lavelle’s waist. He fell forward a little surprised but continued, but when his leg felt something metallic against it he raised his eyes toward Isabelle’s steely gaze. "What the hell are you -" Senator Rodney Hicks stood dancing with his mistress, gyrating his body to the music. Lavelle felt a clot rise in his throat, Isabelle wasn’t a private investigator, she was a hired hit woman. Before she could grab the metallic item he hoisted her and spun her, she swung sideways and off the dance floor. He followed as the music died, panting and out of breath. The strain on his leg and the pain in his chest were now evident as he limped off toward her. Isabelle turned venom in her eyes. "You idiot!" "Should’ve known everyone in Lillian’s family were corrupt. Why are you working when the Carlucci business is dead?" Isabelle grabbed his arm and dragged him into the ladies bathroom; he had no way of getting out of her grasp. "I’ve still clients and if I don’t do my job, questions begin to get asked." "I will not be a part of anything illegal. You can do this one alone, you understand." She gazed at him. "Viktor said you were trustworthy, I understand why." She spun her leg onto the sink and removed the silencer; he looked toward her with a shocked gaze. "How’d you get that …?" "Don’t ask a woman a question she couldn’t possibly give you a logical answer to," she said and patted his cheeks. "Thanks for the dance, hubby!" The door shut and he stood in the woman’s toilet alone. He waited till he heard the yelping screams of dismay and exited the bathroom, the silver wedding placed into his pocket. He moved with the crowd and into the night’s sky. His night with Isabelle Tandino wouldn’t be forgotten. Las Vegas was proving to be quite an experience. Loosening the tie he walked into the brisk night air toward the Bellagio, listening to the sirens in the distance.
