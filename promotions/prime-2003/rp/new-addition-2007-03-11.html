Date: 3/11/07 Shawn isn't feeling well lately. And let's face it, given his mindset, can you really blame him? The guy has made it his mission to strive to be the very best, and at his very best, he's been short of even mediocre. Making the top five of the Halo is drug down by two straight losses in weeks following, and be a streak of losses even before the car wreck. He came in with the fire of losing a little girl he'll never meet, and now, he's failed her too. He's failed himself. And he won't admit it, but he feels like he failed his wife, too. They're still living in the apartments over his gym, instead of having purchased a house and so on. Instead, he's blown opportunity after opportunity. That's what kept running through his mind when he tore open the punching bag down in the gym, anyway. There's a pile of sand in the floor he's got to clean up tonight, but when his wife shouted to him to hurry upstairs, he figured there was a good reason. She was right. “We've got company coming over.” “Wait, what kind of impolite...” “Your sister and Conner.” “Oh.” There's always that frantic movement, trying to clean up whatever you can lay your hands on when people are coming to visit. Shawn grabbed up a shirt and pulled it on, trying to get the living room back into a semblence of order. He grabbed the papers on the coffee table and shoved them into the magazine rack next to the couch. The empty glass went to the kitchen, where Nicole was shoving dirty dishes into the dishwasher. Shawn turned and dashed into the bedroom to straighten up and looked down to see the neglige Nicole had brought home the other day on the floor. Forget it, just gotta close this door. He dashed from there to the bathroom and tossed dirty clothes and towels into the hamper, then snatched clean towels out of the closet to hang over the racks. He dashed out, in bare feet, to the kitchen where his wife was starting the washing machine and looking around. This was really almost futile. Shawn and Nicole were good about keeping their place in good shape. But it's tradition to freak out when people are coming to visit. Can't break tradition now, can we? They stood there, looking at each other, breathing hard. “Did they just call?” he asked, breathing in through his nose and exhaling through his teeth. “No, they called yesterday,” she admitted, “I just forgot all about it.” “How long do we have? Did they give us an ETA?” “Yeah,” Nicole said, yanking a piece of paper off the fridge. The magnet hung on gamely, almost popping off at the swift grab, but staying put like a true champ. “Okay, they'll be here about nine.” Shawn paused, tilting his head slightly. “Nine? It's six-thirty, baby.” “I know, I didn't know how long it would take to clean up and run us through the shower.” Shawn smiled at her, wolfishly. She smiled back. It probably looked like something out of a cheesy sitcom, but they grabbed each other, kissing breathlessly. Shawn picked her up and carried her towards the bathroom. Shower, right? Now, something to interject as the narrator here. I'm not going to tell you about this little interlude because first, they're a married couple that deserves privacy and second, this isn't a geocities fed where you win by writing pathetic attempts at porno scripts. Suffice to say, when nine o'clock rolled around, they were both showered and dressed, but still having a hard time keeping their hands off each other. They sat on the couch, cuddled together, Shawn sitting behind Nicole, kissing her neck when a thought came to him. “Oh... crap...” he muttered. “What is it?” Nicole asked, laying her head back on his shoulder. “I didn't clean up downstairs.” “Why would that matter?” “You don't think a pile of sand would stand out?” “WHAT?” “Yeah... I kinda punched a hole in one of the hanging bags.” Ding-Dong. “Too late now,” she said, patting his cheek. They both pushed up off the plush couch and headed for the door out to the stairway. The gym smelled like usual: the mix of steel, sweat and forms of rubber from the floor mats and coating on the weights. Sooner or later, he was going to have to do something about that, but sooner or later does not mean now. They both walked to the side door of the building, right next to the stairs that led up to their apartment. It's a strange arangement, to have to go through your place of business to let guests in, but such is life. When they pulled the door open, there was a shout of delight. “Nicole!” “Caraline!” The two women embraced in a joyous hug and they giggled to each other. His tall, lithe wife of curly raven hair seemed an odd, yet fitting companion to his short, curvy sister with her blonde curls. They hugged again, probably the closest two sisters-in-law you've ever seen. Shawn reached past them to shake the hand of his old friend. Conner had been his freshman roommate in college, and the two had remained friends ever since. Nowadays, they didn't get to see each other as much, what with Shawn's road schedule and Conner's regular job, but you take what you can get. Now, bear in mind that Conner and Caraline had hidden their relationship for the first month from Shawn. Big brother complex, and all. Well, big brother with a slightly bad temper and the martial skills to remove various bodyparts with his bare hands. You get the picture. But Shawn was happy they were together; they fit. They accentuated each other's high points and helped each other out of low points. “Shawn,” Conner said, his blue eyes twinkling, “I understand things are going well for you. Top five in the Halo, huh, buddy?” Shawn tried to keep his face from falling. “Yeah, followed up by two losses.” He clicked his teeth shut as the two girls moved on into the building and towards the steps. The two of them stayed put, standing around and talking in the gym in that rugged, manly sort of what that all men do. Yes, you do. Okay, you don't, but everyone else does. Loser. “Dude, that doesn't matter. No one's going to remember that. Folks are going to remember how you did in the Halo.” “What, that I choked again?” Conner sighed. “I figured this would happen. You get too down on yourself too fast, my man. Look, you proved all over you can hang with the big dogs, you just gotta hit your stride.” “My stride?” Shawn said doubtfully. “Yeah, man, once you get settled into that mindset you get, these other guys will be running from you.” Shawn chewed the inside of his cheek for a moment. Mindset, huh? “Oh, and...” Conner's eyes passed over the ripped open punching bag. “We also brought you something.” “Yes we did, yes we did!” Caraline shouted, zipping back down the steps, with Nicole following with a confused look on her face. “Look, guys, I know you've been trying to have a kid, and I hate what happened. We thought it might be good for you guys to have something else, though. Keep you company when the other's not here, that kind of thing.” Shawn and Nicole looked at each other doubtfully. Something else? Keep them company? A snort and a sniff followed as Caraline walked up to them with a leash. A big, red bulldog with a splash of white between his eyes and a beautifully wrinkled face followed her in. It looked up at the married couple and dropped its mouth open to let a pink tongue hang out a few inches. “This is Samson!” Caraline announced excitedly. “He's about a year old and he's housetrained and we got him for you.” “Guys,” Shawn said. “Bulldogs are expensive and...” “And nothing,” Conner replied. “You guys have been so good to us and gone through so much, we wanted to do something nice for you. Samson's yours.” Shawn knelt down and snapped his fingers. The big bulldog trotted right up to him and sat down again. He scratched the dog's head and then under his chin. Samson looked at him with almost a content look in his eyes. Nicole leaned down, putting her hands on his shoulder. “Isn't he adorable?” she asked. Samson looked Shawn right in the eye... and Shawn nodded, smiling.
