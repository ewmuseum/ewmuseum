Stripped of All Else (RP for match w/Gamblev. Poteet/Winters)

28 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/28 fade in.'I sat up one night, and thought long and hard about my place in PRIME and my place in this sport. I questioned my relevance, and I pondered the dubious distinction that I had among the PRIME roster. I realized that in relation to my time on the roster, I had spent the least amount of time in the spotlight. Sure, I had an appearance here and there, but for the most part, I was a non-entity. I was filler. I was a warm body that rounded out a clusterfuck or a low card scratch match. I had spent years fighting to get my shot at The Big Stage, and once I arrived, I seemed to be stuck in the margins. As these thoughts began to coagulate in my mind, and I stared through blood-shot eyes at a city spread out before me, I came to a very important threshold. I was faced with a choice. I could either begin to walk the walk that I had spent so much time claiming I was capable of , or I could sputter and fizzle out as just another face that was unrecognizable and easily forgotten by the mouth-breathers, fanboys, and couch potatoes out in the Great Void once the cameras were pointed elsewhere. So, in that moment of truth, I decided to take a step back. I decided to rethink my motivations. I decided to walk away from the harsh lights, the surreality, the white noise, the staged chaos, and the booming tintinnabulations that had reduced my life to a constant film loop. For the first time in my career, I allowed myself to ask myself the questions that had been growing more and more haunting as my PRIME career unfolded before me. And I came face to face with the possibility that all of my copious accolades would be rendered worthless unless I could validate my present existence among my peers. I stared down the grinning face of irrelevance, and came to an understanding. If I was to be relevant, I would have to be feared as a threat.'a pause.'Stated simply; when I step between those ropes, I am stepping into that ring with a head full of questions and a 3-count to answer them. I am about to get back into that ring to prove to the fans, the guys in the back, and to myself that I am everything I claim to be. I am future of PRIME; I am the New Icon of Violence. I will, as I have done before, baptize the canvas with the blood, spit, and spinal fluid of those who oppose me. I will, as I must, fight with the ferocity, the desperation, and the blind aggression that has made my name one worth respecting, and my legacy one worth fearing. And in the event that either man dares take me lightly, I am hereby vowing to punish their arrogance harshly. The challenge has been answered. What is set to occur will erase the doubts from the minds of the skeptics, the ignorance from the willfully oblivious, and the questions of what desperate men like myself do to be recognized.'fade out.
