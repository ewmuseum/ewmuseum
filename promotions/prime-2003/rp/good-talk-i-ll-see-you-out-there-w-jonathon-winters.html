Good Talk, I'll See You Out There (w/Jonathon Winters)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Lately Standing atop of the embarkation platform, Jonathon Winters glared around in wonder at the desolation surrounding him and allowed an almost obligatory shiver. Snow clung desperately to everything in plain sight, painting a rather surreal picture for everyone unlucky enough to be caught up in it and Winters was just another victim. He’d made it all the way to his private jet, just to be informed by his fat, balding and generally incompetent pilot that they were going nowhere. By all rights he should’ve been angry, hell, at least mildly annoyed, but if this caused him to miss PRIME Revolution one-three-five, then he was kinda glad. Winters hated random tag matches, especially when he had to team with someone he didn’t like and let’s face it, Winters didn’t like anybody. “Some fuckin’ weather we’re havin’, huh?” The pilot asked, stepping out onto the platform and lighting up a cigarette in one smooth motion. Winters glanced at him for a second and grunted a response under his breath. “Global-fucking-warming!” The pilot exclaimed, between drags. “You know who’s to blame for this, don’t ya?” Winters shook his head, neither listening nor caring about what the man had to say. “Fuckin’ Communist’s!” He exclaimed. “Those red motherfucker’s have been cooking shit up since WWII!” Winters rolled his eyes and let his attention wander. His eyes grazed over the serene, yet chilling, picture painted before him only for it to spoilt by the instantly depressing image of rival’s Devin Shakur and Sonny Silver glaring out into the wintry July afternoon, each of them with a clear expression of anger plastered across each of their faces. “Well,” the pilot finally began, finishing his cigarette and casting it over the side, right down the shirt of some unfortunate baggage handler, “I‘ve seen Plains, Trains and Automobile‘s! I‘m gonna go book me a motel room before I gotta share one with some skinny white-haired freak and turn all queer and the likes.” Winters watched as the overweight pilot waddled off with purpose, attempting to tackle the steep metal chairs and failing miserably; he slipped on the icy first step and took a rather nasty tumble. Winters shook his head and allowed a chuckle; his day was just getting better and better. *** “Just this way, sir,” the airport appointed guide instructed, gesturing towards the small VIP express elevator needlessly. “Just just hit the button for the third floor and that should get you to the VIP lounge so you can wait this out.” Winters nodded an obligatory thanks and then shook his head as the guide minced away into the distance. Comfortably alone, he hit the elevator button and waited. However, the blissful silence only lasted so long. That thing about his day improving? Equally short-lived. “What the hell do you think this is?” The vaguely familiar and completely annoying voice questioned. “Brokeback? Take a hike.” Winters turned to see fellow PRIMEate Danny Ferguson approach, stalked by his two bodyguards. In the commotion, Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas barged passed the guide, knocking him on his ass. Winters sighed as the elevator doors slid open. “No good autograph seeking Gyllenhall wannab- Hey, hold that door!” Ferguson yelled, picking up his pace as he caught site of Winters, or more importantly, the express elevator. Jonathon considered the notion for a second. He could be civil and help a brother out - especially considering they‘re to supposedly team up on the next edition of Rev - but on the other hand, it would be much funnier to do nothing. Regardless, the doors begin to slide shut. Content with himself, Winters leaned against the back wall of the lift and thought about the first drink he'd order. However, his daydream is cut off before it starts, just as the elevator doors are cut off before they shut. A lone hand sticks trips the safety thingy, and the doors swing back open. Motherfucker! Winters silently cursed. “Hey. Hold. That. Door.” Ferguson questioned, even before Winters could see him. “Asshole.” Winters glared at him silently. Ferguson shook his head. “Gee, maybe you didn't hear me. Or maybe you're just a fucking clown shoes twenty four-seven! A real fucking hard-on.” Winters rolled his eyes. He didn‘t need this shit. “You getting in, or are you waiting for a red carpet?” Ferguson ignored him and stuck his head inside the express car, scrutinising the enclosed space carefully. “Small.” “Got my dick in here,” Winters told him, getting visibly annoyed, “so it’s big enough. Come on, I got complimentary alcohol to inhale.” Ferguson sighed and turned back towards his bodyguards. “It's only going to fit two of us, especially with your Volkswagen-sized asses.” Dametreyus sniffs off the lame humor. "Boss, I advise that you wait for the next elevator so that one of us may be at your side at all times." "Are you kidding? First of all," Danny scoffs, "that means I have to wait down here, which is dangerously close to plebe territory. I'm not just a P! I'm a VIP! I should be in a lounge specifically designated for VIPs! Secondly, then I have to wait at the top while whichever one of you takes a trip up by himself." Winters pushes 'Close Doors', but Danny puts a foot down on the track to block them again. Shooting him a look of death, Danny continues. "We all know that I can't leave Jim alone with alcohol and I can't leave you alone within 50 yards of a duty free shop. So I'd have to ride up with you, then ride back down and get Jim, then come all the way back up. So no, I'm going to ride up now with Cpt. Personality, and then you two can just follow." Dametreyus relictantly nodded and Ferguson stepped inside the elevator, promptly turning his back on Winters and leaning against the other wall. "You know I heard that, right?" Winters asks. "Oh, so you can hear now? Here I thought I was riding with a brick wall." Winters grinned and the doors closed. A Muzak version of that one Justin Timberlake song - you know, THAT one - serenades their chilly (pun!) ascent to VIP status. That is, until the car shook, rattled and juddered to a complete halt. “What the hell is this?” Ferguson questioned, his eyes wide with panic. Winters sighed and shook his head. "I don't think we reached our floor." "Oh no, I am not getting stuck on an elevator right now!" "Maybe if you click your heels and say it two more times, it'll come true," Winters sneers, sinking down to a seated position as he comes to terms with the sudden circumstance. “Motherfucker!” Danny fumbled around with the buttons and then located the emergency phone. “Hello? Hello? Anybody there? Hello?” Ultimately, he gave up and slammed the phone down. “Shit!” Danny sat down, too, but his was more like a pouting child jumping onto a bed - reckless and violent. The car sways and creaks a little as Danny curls up in the corner. Winters furrows his brow at the actor. "I don't thi-" "Just don't fucking talk to me." Winters gave a shrug and turned away. That plan suited him just fine. *** Winters checked his watch, only to remember that he had packed it in the carryon he left on his plane. The Timberlake song was on its 25th repetition by now, so he tried to work out the math in his head. Danny was still trying to get reception with his cell phone to no avail. "What the fuck am I doing?" Danny suddenly says, letting the phone drop to the floor of the car. Winters says nothing, trying to pretend he's doing something else...except there's nothing else TO do, so he took to counting the number of diamonds in the elevator's carpet pattern as a means of preoccupation. The lack of an enthusiastic audience does not deter Ferg. "I mean, seriously." This time, Winters looks up at him, not really sure if he's expecting an answer or not. He eventually bites and tries to offer one. "I- "I mean it's July and I'm stuck in an elevator because of goddamned snow, and do you know why?" Winters had been fooled by the pseudo-rhetoricals once already, so he wasn't going to bother with this one. Well, he wasn't until Danny turned and looked at him. "Do you?" "Well-" "Because I told Facey and Chandler that I'd hold my private plane until they made their connecting flights, so we could head out together. Because I'm fucking doing anything with Facey and Chandler again to begin with, dammit!" He slams his fist into the wall. Oh God, Winters thinks, this isn't going to become what I think it is. He's not going to do this to me... "Everything was going so well, you know?" Fucking hell, he is going to do this to me. "I had the movie coming out and I was headlining the cards, and I was going to Schwarzenegger it and crossover like a motherfucker, and then everything just falls apart and I start second-guessing my beef with Nova, he loses the belt, my whole plan falls apart, and then she tells me I can't keep doing this and then Allan quits and I can't quit because I don't know what to fucking do so I just fall back on what works even though it's stupid and I'm supposed to be better than that but I'm just so goddamn scared about everything and I just need some help and then this fucking elevator stops and GODDAMMIT!" Danny is to the point of sobbing now, and he punctuates his last few obscenities with some more punches to the elevator wall. Winters is equal parts mortified and livid that he stumbled into this heart-to-heart, and given his staggering lack of experience in that department, he isn't quite sure how to handle it. Eventually, he reaches an awkward arm out and pats the Superstar on the back. "I...uh...there...I mean....it's o...you know, it gets tough sometimes..." "Yeeeeaaaaah" Danny howls, in the middle of a breakdown right here, just feet from the VIP lounge. Winters shakes his head, not fully grasping that he has to put up with this shit, nor that he's going to be putting his faith in this man's hands in a day or so. "And, well, you just gotta get through it," he says, practically spitting at his own words, nearly nauseous at the delivery. "This Intense Title stuff hasn't made my PRIME career very fun, either. There's a lot of pressure..." With a sniffle, Danny looks up from his knee. "You...you work for PRIME?" Fuck that noise. Any remorse, even the fabricated kind, that he had shown Danny was now gone. This motherfu- With that, the elevator whirred to life, continuing the journey toward the lounge. Ferg immediately pulled himself up to a standing position and wiped his eyes. He couldn't look at Winters, which was probably good, given the whitehot hatred that was being channelled into him right now. Eventually the lift reaches the final destination and the doors ding open. "I'm very sorry," says the airport attendant standing before them in the lounge. "There was some ice in-" "You've got a serious fucking dust problem in that elevator," Danny growls, feigning sinus issues. "I want a scotch and some napkins, now." He immediately heads off to the corner of the lounge, not planning to speak to anyone else for a long time. Winters watches him go with unbelievable disgust. He saddles up to the bar as the keep walks over. "Snow in July, can you believe that? I mean, it's like the polar opposite of what you'd expect here!" Turning his eyes to the keep, Winters gestures for a bottle. He takes another look toward the corner where Ferguson disappeared. "Global fucking warming," he says.
