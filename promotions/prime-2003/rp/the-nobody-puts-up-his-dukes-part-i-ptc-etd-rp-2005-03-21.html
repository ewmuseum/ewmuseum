The Nobody Puts Up His Dukes, Part I (PTC ETD RP)

21 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/21/05 Character name: NovaFederation: PRIME The Nobody Puts Up His Dukes “You did what?!” Nova winces as his wife’s eyes cut into his soul like so many hot knives through the proverbial butter. “I signed up for the PTC Extreme Title Royale.” “Chris, why the fu…well, first of all, what the hell is PTC?” Ariel growled, her hands rested antagonistically on her hips. “Well, I’m an employee of PRIME, which is one wrestling federation,” Nova begins to explain, “there are other wrestling federations, and when a few of them decide to…” “Nevermind,” Ariel interrupts, “I don’t care. Why did you do it, Chris? In light of all the work you’ve put into PRIME lately, why pile this onto your plate? You’re never home, Chris. We have our family back, and I barely see you more than I did after we separated.” “I know,” Nova replies weakly. “You know? You know?” Ariel echoes angrily, “If you know, then why don’t you make more of an effort to be home with your daughter, instead of heaping more work on top of your already hectic schedule and jetting off in the middle of the night to God knows where?” Nova lowers his eyes for a moment, and Ariel’s shoulders slump as she sighs loudly. “This family can’t work without you here, Chris. I need you here. I’m tired of trying to keep this together by myself.” “I’m not asking you to do that,” Nova replies, “all I’m asking for is a little understanding here, Ariel.” “You are asking me to do that,” Ariel argues, “tell me, Chris. Tell me what I’m supposed to understand.” “You should understand that I’m not trying to duck out on my family,” Nova replies, his voice beginning to rise, “It’s just that this isn’t AW. I’m not King Shit here, Ariel. I’m just another name with no credibility.” “That’s not true,” Ariel counters, “you’re doing great in PRIME. You’re more than just a name there, I know you are. You wouldn’t put in so much time if PRIME wasn’t yielding results.” “I’m not talking about PRIME,” Nova says dismissively, “I’m talking about PTC. I’ve never been involved with something that has so grand a scope. I’m nothing there, Ariel. I’m not important, and it drives me fucking crazy. I’m not worried about PRIME. I’ll get my shot there, I can feel it. But in PTC, I’m not gonna get shit. No one is going to ask me if I would like a main event, or if I wouldn’t mind a title shot. If I want to get noticed, I have to take initiative and be ready to grab a spot when a spot is offered.” “So you decided to ‘grab a spot’ in the Extreme Division Royale?” Ariel asks. Apparently, Nova doesn’t hide his surprise well enough, as a small triumphant smile creeps over his wife’s face before fading back into a concerned frown. “I’m not stupid, Chris. I know what ‘Extreme Divisions’ require…more blood, more pain, more potential for injury than any other type of match. Do you just not think about the future? You gave away your right to ‘live for the moment’ when your daughter was born…Hell, by rights you should have given it away when you put a ring on my finger.” “I know what I did when I had a child, and I know what the fuck I did when I put a ring on your finger! Don’t patronize me!” Nova snaps. His wife recoils back, and Nova pauses to take a breath. “I’m sorry…I shouldn’t have yelled. But Ariel, I don’t have a lot of options…do you think I would have signed up for the Extreme Division if I had more options? The fact of the matter is, I can’t just stroll into PTC Headquarters and ask to bumped into the Global Title scene because my wife thinks the Extreme Division is too dangerous. There are already people lined up who have earned those spots. I saw that the Extreme Division had open spots and I jumped on it.” “But why?” Ariel asks, almost desperately, “that’s what I still don’t understand, Chris. What do you have left to prove?” “I have everything left to prove!” Nova retorts, “Ariel, I spent years running with the same crew, and I did a lot of things that I’m still very proud of…but the fact of the matter is, that crew is gone, and gone with them is the respect that I worked so hard and bled so much for. Now I’m surrounded by people who could give a rat’s ass if I crippled myself tomorrow.” “Then fuck ‘em, Chris,” Ariel says, putting her hand on Nova’s arm, “because I care. Your daughter cares. If no one else does, then that’s their problem, not yours.” “No,” Nova replies, “I will make them care, Ariel. That’s the only way to do it.” “Why, Chris?!” Ariel shouts, exasperation and anger mixing a hellacious verbal cocktail in her voice, “why try to impress these people?” Nova pulls a cigarette out of his pocket and lights it, almost in spite of his knowledge of Ariel’s disdain for smoking inside the house. “Because I see the way people treat Seymour Almasy. I see the way they treat Dr. Curiosity and Angelo Deville and Karina Wolfenden. I stand in the corner and listen to them talk about the battles and moments that have made PTC what it is, and I want it.” “So you’re risking your well-being and our future because you’re jealous over other people’s accomplishments?” Ariel replies. Nova bites down hard on his tongue to prevent himself from screaming at his wife. “Don’t reduce my aspirations to the level of petty jealousy. All I’m saying is that when I leave, I want to be remembered for something. Is that being jealous or self-centered?” Nova takes a drag from the cigarette and leans over the coffee table to reach the ashtray. “I’ve seen first-hand, over and over again, how short this sport’s memory is…I’ve seen guys bow out, and no one gave a shit…it was just an open slot on the roster for someone else to fill. I don’t want that.” “And I’ve seen grown men unable to lift themselves out of a wheelchair to play with their kids,” Ariel replies, “and I don’t want that. Your concern is for the longevity of your fame, and mine is for the baby girl who’s sleeping upstairs. I only hope that whatever recognition you receive is enough of a consolation prize years from now if you can’t walk your daughter down the aisle.” “Oh, can you be a little more dramatic, please?” Nova offers sarcastically, but Ariel just glares at him for a moment before shaking her head and turning to walk up the staircase. “Ariel, wait…” She disappears at the top of the stairs and Nova sighs before taking another draw from the cig and putting it out. “Damn it.” With relatively little fanfare, we arrive at the qualifying Royale for the contendership to PTC’s Extreme Title. I don’t know what the other’s motivations are for throwing their names into the hat. Maybe we each have different reasons for taking time out of our schedules to compete for the shot…or maybe we’re all here for the same reason. It’ll be a moot point as soon as we’re all in the ring. I do know what my motivations are, and for better or worse, they’re all I have as fuel to keep me going. Maybe it is just my ego…maybe I just want to be famous, to have the kind of praise heaped on me that I’ve seen others receive. But I have the sneaking suspicion that there’s more it than that… …I have to believe there’s more to it than that. I have to rationalize my decision to pursue this. “Nova! Over here, man!” Emilio Rage stands up and waves to the former 5-Star Champion, and Nova returns the signal with a nod before making his way over to Rage’s table. He takes a seat and pulls out a cigarette. “Starbucks, Rage? We’re hanging out at Starbucks?” “What’s wrong with Starbucks?” Emilio replies defensively, “it was close by, it’s not too hard on the wallet, and I could use a few cups of coffee.” “I dunno,” Nova says with a shrug, “I just feel like such a…tool.” “Oh, I’m sorry,” Rage replies with a grin, “I should’ve found some hole-in-the-wall diner in the city that no one has ever heard of…maybe with personalized murals on the wall or something. Say, I’d be careful wearing that shirt…I hear someone else in the entire world has the same one, and you’d hate to look like a poser, right?” “Touché, touché,” Nova admits, holding his hands up in defeat before taking his cigarette out of the ash tray and pulling off it, “so what’s shaking, man?” “At this point? My confidence,” Rage replies with a chuckle, pushing a coffee towards his friend. “I hear that,” Nova says, lifting the cup to his lips, “Jesus…” “Did you get an ear-full from Ariel?” Rage inquires, an amused smile making its way onto his bearded maw. “As predicted,” Nova says tiredly. “She’ll be fine,” Rage assures Nova with a confident grin. “Easy for you to say, buck-o,” Nova replies dryly, “I’m the one who has to suffer the consequences in the interim.” Rage shrugs and sips from his coffee. “Sucks that your first PTC match is gonna be such a cluster-fuck.” “Tell me about it!” Nova says, blowing smoke out of his mouth as he talks, “That would be my luck, wouldn’t it? I’ve never seen any of these guys before, and I’ve gotta find a way to outlast thirteen of them. I would stab a guy in the stomach with a meat thermometer for a bracket-style elimination tournament.” Rage nods. “Gonna be a lot of pressure.” Nova stares at him. “Thank you, Emilio, for pointing that out. I mean, it completely wasn’t obvious at all to me until you mentioned it…any other helpful facts? A statistical run-down of my chances of winning, maybe? Or a brief get-to-know-ya session on the accomplishments of my opponents, perhaps?” Rage holds his hands up defensively. “Just saying, you need to be ready for it. I doubt PRIME will host the match, and that means fighting on foreign turf, with foreign crowds, and foreign announcers and officials, against guys who have been involved in PTC longer than…” “Jesus, you’re giving me the fucking willies,” Nova interrupts, “It’s gonna suck. End of story. Can we maybe change the subject?” Nova and Rage sit silently for a moment, staring at each other. “This is a nice place,” Nova fires off awkwardly. “Yeah,” Rage replies, staring down into his coffee. “I, uh, I see you have a different-patterned bandanna on,” Nova points out astutely. Rage nods dispassionately. “Yeah.” “Where...um…where’d you pick it up?” Nova asks. “Okay, this is stupid,” Rage says, “it’s obvious what’s on both of our minds, and it ain’t my friggin’ bandanna.” “Wow,” Nova replies with a sly grin, “looks like somebody woke up on the wrong side of la cama this morning.” “Clever,” Rage says, “I see you replaced an English noun with its Spanish translation, a witty and subtle homage to my ethnic origin…where do you come up with this stuff?” “All right here,” Nova answers, pointing to his head. “Say, you got the time?” Rage asks. “If you’ve got the money, honey,” Nova replies with a wink. “It’s just a laugh a minute with this guy,” Rage says, rolling his eyes, “seriously, what time is it?” “It’s half past four,” Nova replies after looking at the clock on his cell phone, “why?” “Shit!” Rage curses, “I gotta run.” “Por que?” Nova asks, feigning a dramatically disappointed frown. “Press release for the Global Title match,” Rage answers, pushing back his chair and pulling his jacket on as he stands up, “I’m late already.” Nova lights another cigarette. “Yeah, well, I got stuff to do, anyway…ya know, press releases and photo shoots and things for the Royale…” Rage unsuccessfully tries to mask a snicker. “Well, I don’t wanna keep you waiting for all that.” “No, no, the brass wouldn’t stand for it,” Nova replies, “I mean, it’s just damn hard being such a hot commodity. I don’t think a lot of people realize the work that goes into being totally freakin’ awesome, twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week, three hun-” Nova stops as he notices Rage walking toward the entrance to the coffee shop. He opens the swinging glass door and turns back to meet Nova’s eyes. “You’ll be fine, man. You can do this.” Nova nods, and Emilio Rage lets the door close behind him. Rage’s words of encouragement are far from original…Nova has heard them from countless friends, fans, and co-workers in the seven years he’s been involved with professional wrestling… …but they still help somehow. Nova grins and takes a drag off the cigarette in his hand. Nova’s Abode, Detroit, MI… The door creaks loudly as Nova opens it, a long, low moan echoing off the walls of the room. Nova gingerly steps inside, but doesn’t close the door behind him for fear of the noise. The wind against the window outside makes for a soothing hum in the dark room…the soundtrack of serenity. Nova walks across the room to Lulu’s crib and stands over the railing, staring down at the little pooch of her stomach puffing in and out as she breathes calmly in her sleep. You’re the one person who believes, unwaveringly, in me, and you’re the one person I have the potential to let down the most. Nova bends down and puts his fingers through the slats in the railing to touch his daughter’s curled up fists. “I’m sorry,” he whispers. She stirs, but doesn’t wake up, sliding her head over on the blanket and emitting a high-pitched groan that sends spittle bubbling out between her lips. “You’ll understand some day,” Nova continues, “I wish I could stay home all the time, but I can’t.” He grins as his daughter clenches and unclenches her fists in her sleep. “Though if I had known how cute you’d be, I might have chosen a more family-friendly occupation.” As Nova stands up, his right knee pops audibly. He looks down at his leg, running a finger over the scar that he can feel through his jeans. His gaze shifts back to his daughter. I hope I’m doing this for the right reasons. My daughter, my family means more to me than some silly strap… …don’t they?
