The Lost Tapes: Chapter One

5 December 2005

Date: 12.5.05 The Lost Tapes: Sub-Chapter One The time was midnight. Most designate such a time of night as to which to garnish rest and rejuvenation for the next day’s journey, but deep in the heart of Queens, New York Midnight was opening time for one club in particular that went by the name of “The Rose”. Its owner? Sal Vaccini, one of New York’s most powerful crime bosses who ruled Queens with an iron fist. Sal wasn’t like most crime lords, though. He didn’t rely on violence and illegal cargo in order to garnish his claim as iron ruler, but instead he relied on this one production...his greatest ever. For this club drew so many guests that many of them had to be turned away at the door. Even an elevation in admission price couldn’t stop the populate masses of people. But what “The Rose” such a powerful and convincing draw? Simple. Gore. The Rose didn’t have a huge facility, as a matter of fact...there was no facility at all. Vaccini inherited his father’s famed basketball court, but holding little interest in the sport, he fenced the court with a cage and turned it into a fight club. They say that it takes money to make money, but not in Vaccini’s case. He didn’t have to spend a dime to get Club running...he was a heavy believer in the philosophy “Build it and they’ll come”. Three weeks later after claiming it an official fight club, the masses poored in from all over all the while Sal sat on his throne and watched the money pile up. What would make people want to pay just to have a fight sanctioned, though? You’d be suprised. Remember...this IS Queens. Various gangs used the fight dimension as a source of inauguration for their newest members. If they could get by The Rose, they’d be as good as in. Many gangs settled differences here, even family members squared off in The Rose. It was like an addiction to these people, one in which the government could not regulate nor tame. Sal had so many ways of getting around the law, whether it was legal loopholes that’d he learned in law school (Oh yes, Sal was a very smart man)...or simple bribery with his pile of cash, he always found a way. He wouldn’t be so lucky this time. The usual was occurring as two rival gang members stood in the center of the court, prepared to go at it. No one was allowed inside of the cage walls while fight was going on, so they all gathered around the cage like a herd of animals, banging and cheering all the while the two men slaughtered each other inside of the cage. There was no padding...the ground was pure cement. The cage was rusty and held loose sharp ends. It was straight street, and Sal loved watching every minute of it from his penthouse that sat behind the court. He sat on the balcony, watching as the barbarians charged each other. Obviously this battle held some sort of racial issue as the competitors consisted of a black and white man. All of the white guys were behind the white guy, and vice versa. Sal didn’t discriminate, though...as long as the money was green, he was happy. The black fist cracked the nose of his opposer, drawing blood from his nose which flowed and stain the cement below. He threw another right, this time across the cheek, nearly dislocating it. Another connected to thr right as blood began to pour from his mouth now. Finally, he grabbed the opposer his head and viciously DDTed him on the Cement grounding, evidently ending the contest. Sal smiled as the victor raised his hands in victory. “Some Fight, eh?” Sal jerked himself around and stared into the eyes of...who else? Vangelus Olsig. Olsig stood in the doorway which led to the balcony, his arms folded and his eyes narrow as if cutting a slice through Sal Vaccini. Sal’s facial expression told the entire story. The two had never encountered each other before in such a manner, but they’d definitely heard about each other and that was enough to draw the reaction from each. “May I help you, Olsig?” came the response from Sal. His deep toned voice echoed through the dreary night as he pushed himself out of the chair in which he had occupied and made his way to the face of Vangelus Olsig. The two maintained a staredown with each other for a moment before Vangelus Olsig finally spoke in repliance to Sal. “Yes you may...for years, now, you’ve run your little fight club illegally. Mind shutting it down...for me?” Vangelus asked, obviously trying to be the smartass we’ve all known him to be. Ugh...sorry, I forgot I’m supposed to be professional here. “Cute. So is this the government’s new brilliant plan to try and finally shut down my success? If so, please inform them that I expected more.” Sal stated, turning his back to Vangelus with his arms folded behind his back as he glanced at the cage where the next fight was already occurring. “Actually, my purpose for being here extends far beyond Government issues. My purpose for being here is something more personal, something of which you need not know.” Olsig responded, once again in the smart-aleck manner. “I’d advise you, though, to take the easy and less humiliating way of shutting down your little club here, though, because either way its going down.” Olsig told Sal with a more serious look on his face this time. “Oh really? And what makes you so sure? You gonna do a few wrestling moves on me? Huh? Gonna Suplex me? Gonna bodyslam me? Is that your little plan? Well save it, Olsig, because your idle threats mean nothing to me. I’ve toppled obstacles bigger than your little mind could even comprehend, so if you don’t mind ex-...” Suddenly, Sal stopped mid-sentence as Olsig extended a sheet of paper into the face of Vaccini which caught him off guard and left him in a state of awe. Sal snatched the file out of Olsig’s hand to find that it was not just a single sheet of paper, but yet several sheets all held together by a common paperclip. He turned away from Olsig and scanned the files with his mouth wide open and his mind questioning just how it could be possible. “H-ho..How did you get these!?” He shouted, jerking himself around to face Olsig. “You said you’ve beaten the government numerous times? Lets just say those numbers finally caught up with you.” Olsig stated as a smile crept unto his face, much to the dissatisfaction of his opposer. “I-I don’t get it....how could I owe the federal government 2.3 Million dollars!?” Sal shouted, still confused as to how this could have occurred. “It seems as if illegal activity is some sort of gene in your twisted family. Your father ran an illegal cargo operation, which gathered up half of that bill...and now you’ve added to the fire with this pathetic attempt at earning cash. You know what would be the real shame, though? The fact that the great Sal Vaccini has plotted numerous deaths, illegally taken in income without a valid enteurprenership license, AND illegally bribed federal officials...but yet he’s finally taken down, how? By Tax Debt. Such a nasty ending to an impressive saga.” Olsig stated, further boiling Sal whom threw the files unto the ground and stomped on them. “Oh, don’t worry...there’s plenty more copies where that came from.” Olsig stated before suddenly grabbing a briefcase full of copies of the Tax File. He opens the Briefcase and allows all of the papers to fly throughout the air, landing on the ground where the herd of people stand. “And now...your sheep will be able to learn of the destruction of their shepherd.” Olsig stated, dropping the now empty briefcase unto the ground. He then pulls out a chrome set of handcuffs out of his back pocket, and as Sal has his back turned to him watching the papers fly to the people below him, Olsig sneaks behind him and hooks his arms in one of the handcuffs. As he hooks the second, he speaks... “Vaccini, you’re under arrest for non-payment of Tax. You have the right to remain silent...anything you say or do can and will be used against you in the court of law.” And in one swift notion, the midnight sky served host to the destruction of the Vaccini empire. At the hands of Olsig. Winner of GTT. Universal Champion. Untangeable Record. Your achievements in the industry are comparable to known. Your name has been spread all across the nation as one of the most triumphed and recognized name in our industry. Many fear you because of this. Because of what you have achieved, they fear stepping inside of the ring with you. Because of idle threats, they fear encountering your presence. But the real reason they fear you is because many can't see beyond the veil. You call yourself the Devil's Don...but even Lucifer fell at the hands of Micheal the Archangel. Revolution is your falling point. Because for the first time in your illustrious career... You're stepping inside of the ring with someone who doesn't buy the hype. And Instead of recognizing you as some unbeatable force... I'll simply recognize you as an opportunity. An opportunity to shame the old and birth a dynasty of my own. An opportunity to tame the overhyped beast. An opportunity... To beat the unbeatable. “Good work, sir.” Chief Morris shook the hand of Vangelus Olsig as they watched the officers place Vaccini in his designated cell. Chief Morris was the head of the Queens correctional facility, and where he’d constantly failed in trying to bring Vaccini to justice, the man he’d hired succeeded...and that was all that mattered. Queens was now free of one the viruses that corrupted it. “Cut the shenanigans, Chief...I only did this so that I could obtain my father’s confidential documents...now hand them over.” Olsig stated, in no mood to play the “Friend Game” with the Chief. The Chief took a deep sigh and responded. “Okay, Mister Olsig...” He stated as he began to search through the file cabinets for the documents. “If you don’t mind me asking, why exactly are your father’s confidential documents so important to you...especially if he is deceased?” Morris stated, obviously clueless to the recent events that had shaped the life of Vangelus Olsig. “That’s none of your business...you mind hurrying with those documents, though? I sort of have a life to live, ya know.” Olsig stated...his attitude evidently apparent. It wasn’t the fact that the Chief was all in his business that bothered him...it was the fact that he had been used by the government, just to obtain those files. Finally Morris found the documents and closed the cabinet, handing the documents over to Olsig who snatched them out of his hand. “No need to be hasty, Mr. Olsig...you must tell me, though...just how did you manage to figure out that Vaccini owed 2.3 million to the Federal Government?” Morris asked. “It was simple...obviously the operation he was running was illegal, but the government could do nothing about it. Everyone knew that his father was largely in debt before he deceased, but hardly any knew that the court in which Vaccini was running his club was actually an inheritance and not a purchase, therefore the debts owed by his father carried over to Vaccini and also made his club a legal draw of debts. It’s a matter of knowing all the facts, my friend.” Olsig exclaimed as he turned and placed his hand on the door knob ready to make his departure. “Ah, I see....well, nonetheless we truly appreciate your very very courageous achievements.” Morris stated with a smile on his face. Olsig didn’t return the smile, but instead a snide remark. “You know, Chief...bribery is a felony. What you all did to me was against the law you so rightfully protect...I would expect a man of your position to recognize such.” Olsig stated, turning away and making his way out of the door. “Yes, Mister Olsig...but sometime’s you must bend the law in order to enforce the law.” Morris added, but by this time Olsig was already out of the door. He had no time for useless rambling... Because the Manhunt had ensued. You see me as no obstacle. You see me as no threat. Afterall, you've done it all. You've beaten the best in Karina Wolfenden and Brandon Youngblood and Magnum Wolf and a host of others... Afterall, you've achieved pinnacles that guys like me only dream of. Why should you be threatened by a force such as I? A mere man who hasn't achieved the global fame as you have. A mere man who hasn't achieved the pinnacles in which you have. A mere man... And nothing more. But heres in hopes that you come into Revolution with that same very attitude. Because then the door for exposure will be open. I will expose you as the faulty superstar you are. I will expose you of the hype in which your career has been built upon. Angelo Deville. Icon to Most. Mere man to me. Powers that be have brougt us together to open up the 84th edition of ReVolution... And in that opening contest, I will shock the world... How? By proving the immortal Angelo Deville to be... Mortal.
