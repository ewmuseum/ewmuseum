Colossus of PRIME: House Call

3 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 3, 2008 ((Thanks to [name withheld] for the use of Doctor Curiosity.)) David Walter Smith was a man with many...let's not say regrets, though they are things that make him sad. Let us simply say, then, that he is unable to understand much that has happened to him. Unable to understand that, despite being well-spoken, solid in the ring, and a hoss on a level Sonny Sil--err, Vince McMaho--err, CP Cantr--err, EXECUTIVES IN THE WRESTLING WORLD drool over, he'd never really sniffed success. The road to Colossus was incoming, and if he could get a match on it, it would likely be the biggest opportunity of his career. Surprising as it may be to some, UltraViolence is already out of his mind, rationalized away as a defeat to a man with chemical enhancement. Even so, it had been the source of the first praise as a competitor that he'd seen in some time, going one on one with the PRIME Choice at world famous Wrigley Field. Of course, his spur of the moment decision to leap from the top rope and splash Killean Sirrajin through an announce table probably had something to do with that. "They told be it'd be in the opening ReV montage, starting this week," he mused, to himself, proudly. He mattered, finally. It had taken a jump off the top rope, mind you -- not any of the entirely accurate things he was saying about Killean Sirrajin -- but he mattered. "At least PRIME management understands what a sham he is," Logic told himself, stretching out in the back of, for the first time in his career, a limousine. Indeed, in the wake of UltraViolence, things had picked up for the Pensive Punisher. A semi-main event against Dusk was in the cards for the coming ReVolution program, and he had finally met with PRIME merchandising staff, to discuss the possibility of a Logic t-shirt. It would probably sell maybe five shirts at best, but the thought of having his own shirt made Logic smile. With a quick glance at the seat next to him, Logic suddenly remembered why he was in this particular limousine. Seated next to him was a low level employee for the Disney Corporation, the entity that had once been paying his checks in the fans Wrestling organization. "It's good to see you again, David," the other man said. Logic didn't recognize the man, but he figured that he was once a member of fWo staff, reassigned elsewhere within the company once everything went south. "I told you a little bit about what we're planning over the phone, earlier, but I'll repeat it here for ease of clarity." Logic nodded once, and the Disney employee continued. "We're looking to make use of the massive fWo tape library. Other promotions are digging into their past for DVD with massive success. I see no reason why the fWo should be any different. We're starting off with compilation/history DVDs for some of the top talents, and we're also releasing the four Survivor IV competitions. That's where you come in." The Thinking-Man's Grappler made a face. "You want my comments on the competition, then?" "Yes. Your uncensored comments. Honestly, we had difficulty finding people willing to comment. A lot of the Survivors from your season have fallen off the face of the Earth, or said no to us. Really, it's going to be only you and one other. Ideally, you'll record us running commentary of each of the Survivor shows, some thoughts in a sit-down interview about the whole competition, and then we've made a bonus disk, with some of the fWo matches of the final three. For you, it's going to be your OCW United States Title match against Kellen Kinkade, your shot at the fWo Internet Title against Keith Scott Zimmerman, and your hardcore match against Lindsay Troy." Again, the big man nodded. "It all sounds fine," Logic began, "but where the Hell are we going?" Wincing, the employee gestured out into the distance, towards what appeared to be a giant...castle perched over the surounding area like a black widow spider. "Well, I guess you know as well as anyone, the only other guy we could manage to find to comment is...somewhat eccentric." "...no..." Logic's eyes fell to the castle, staring at it as the sinking realization that it wasn't Cinderella's set in. No, there was only one man who would build a gigantic fucking castle in the middle of nowhere. A man who Logic had once known quite well. A former fWo Cruiserweight Champion, and in Logic's new home of Primetime Central, a man whose achievements were quite revered. Doctor Curiosity. ============= If nothing else, Logic knew, the man with the faux-German accent was a very confusing and complicated individual. He'd known it during Survivor IV. Known it during the aftermath in the fWo. Even known it when he'd put on the ridiculous Professor Wisdom mask to try and get revenge on ze Doctor in XUW. "Vell, vell, vell!" said German accent boomed, as the three men sat in what...appeared to be a giant living room. "How do you like ze Living Quarters of Doom?" With a thoughtful nod, ze Doc took a sip from a mug of coffee, a contented expression streaking his face. A glance at the Doc was enough to bring Logic right back three years, to having to deal with the infuriating man every day of his life in Survivor IV." Logic noted the room's decor, which could best be described as "early Frankenstein." 'It is, err, lovely," David stated, in an effort to be nice. After all, by his own admission, Doctor Curiosity was retired, the master of the Running Cheat, the Shooting Star Cheat, and the Fruit-Based Cheat having opted to put his considerable, um, "talents" behind a new cause. "Vell, vhen I vas ze fWo Cruiserveight Champion, I realized that no vone could stand up to my might! With ze Curious Rules, I was unbeatable!" "Didn't High Flyer beat you?" Logic queried. "Zat did not happen, simpleton," the Doc retorted. "As usual, you are making t'ings up! Regardless, ze point stands! And so, I decided to go from Curious Wrestling to ze FAR more challenging pursuit of Curious World Domination!" Logic simply sighed, realizing that, could he lock Mega Job and Doctor Curiosity in an underground basement, Cask of Amontillado style, his life would be so very much easier. "And you, David. Vhat are you doing? Tending bar, or something else that a Neanderthal like you would engage in?" The desire to strangle was rising, but Logic forced it down. "Wrestling, actually," David replied. "For PRIME, in PTC." Curiosity scoffed. "You? In PRIME? Nein! Vhy vould PRIME ever hire a man such as yourself?" "It's true," the Disney employee chimed in, sheepishly. For about a split second, the face of one Doctor Curiosity softened. "Hah! About damn time you did something without ze help of ze Doc! I had thought zat you vere coming back here having finally realized zat you are most fit to be my new assistant!" David rolled his eyes. "You STILL haven't found a new assistant?!" "QUIET! It is not MY fault zat good help is hard to find nowadays!" The Doc's expression was somewhat sheepish. "Not zat it matters! It shall be easy to take over ze world! Vhy, my plan is already coming together perfectly! Ze Curious Mallet v. 12.0 is being created in a Third World nation as ve speak! It shall be sent to orbit ze Earth, and should ze United Nations not bow to me, I shall swing ze Curious Mallet, smack it into ze planet, and send Ze Earth onto a direct collision course with ze sun!" Logic looked at the Disney employee. Disney employee looked back. "Don't worry," David said, patting the employee on the shoulder. "He says things like this a lot." "Ze plan is flawless!" Doc exulted. "But now, onto ze matters at hand..." ========================= To be blunt, I simply do not understand this place. Men like Doctor Curiosity are revered as gods, and men like me are shunned. For months, years even, I thought it was the fault of the world. Only now do I understand the truth. It is my own fault. I have ALLOWED myself to become a joke. The fans Wrestling organization is dead. The only association I will have with it will be these DVDs and and the royalty checks they will pay me. PRIME is the present. It is my chance to erase the past, make it a footnote. I am six foot nine. I am almost three hundred pounds. I have every tool to be the greatest athlete the sport has ever seen. The PRIME Universal Championship will be mine one day. Until then, however, bodies must be left in my path. Bodies such as Dusk. Dusk is the beginning of my pathway to Colossus. The biggest event in PRIME's history will be where its largest superstar will make his mark. Where I will begin my slow, inexorable climb to this company's peak. At Colossus V, a Logical era begins. Quod erat demonstratum.
