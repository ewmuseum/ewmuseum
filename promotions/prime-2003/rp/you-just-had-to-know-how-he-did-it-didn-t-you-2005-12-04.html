Chapter I. “Are We There Yet?” Emilio did not like Mexico. In fact, he never had. Double-in fact, he had never even lived there before because he hated it so much... Even when he wrestled there. (He was housed in southern Texas.) So it was quite a bit odd, if not downright peculiar, to be gagged and stuffed in the back of a fast-moving van under the scrutinizing eyes of the INS as they attempted to 'deport' him 'back' to Mexico. Where he was, in fact, not even from. But Tyler Nelson had his connections, and those connections had their connection, so on and so forth until it reached the doors of the White House. And George Bush, the sensational genius he is, decided not to have anyone check in on the matter –you know, little things like “where is this guy from?”- took Nelson at his word, and had Emilio deported to Mexico. So as they headed South down a bumpy dirt road in the middle of the night, somewhere in the rural southern part of the United States, Emilio couldn't help but chuckle to himself. Quietly at first. But then a full stream of roaring laughter erupted from his lungs. The ridiculousness of the situation. The absurdity of the circumstances. They were enough to break a man. But Emilio? No. All too familiar was he with the ways of the cruel, cruel world. And as cruel as the natural elements were by themselves, throw in the intervening hand of someone with a psychotic cult of personality, a person such as Tyler Nelson, and you have a recipe for entertainment. A theater of the absurd, for sure. Distasteful. Cruel. Ironic. And yes, sometimes even unbearable. But entertaining nonetheless. …Mostly Because right then, for engaging in laughter, Emilio received a swift kick to the ribs for his troubles. The laugh was cut short as the air left his lungs in a hurry. As painful as it was, psychologically and physiologically, Emilio kept a stiff upper lip. Albeit a bloodied one. 'Shaddup!' commanded one of the INS men, giving Emilio a quick slap across the face. Emilio cocked his head to the side and spat a thick wad of blood onto the floor, warranting a grunt of approval from the onlookers. What Emilio wouldn’t give to wipe the snide grins off their faces… They were all dressed in matching black uniforms with the initials 'INS' engraved in white on the back. Besides the four in the back, there was a driver in the front and another man in the passenger's seat wielding a shotgun. But fear Emilio they did not. For the helpless figure of the former Alias Champ lie still with his hands cuffed behind his back and his head drooped solemnly in defeat. Or so they thought. But internally, Emilio was seething in rage much more than he was wading in a pool of self-pity. The pain...the pain he could deal with. The humiliation. Well, maybe even that. But the image that kept on reappearing in his mind of that arrogant, cocky son of a bitch, Nelson, that damn beaming smile he wore on his face as Emilio was gagged and dragged out of the arena…It was enough to drive a sane man mad. Or an insane man, such as Emilio, back into sanity. It was a wake-up call. It let Emilio know that obviously spent too much time out of the game. For this, he would pay dearly. It was merely hours ago when he stated, confidently, in front of thousands of fans in the arena and millions worldwide, that he would not take the bait. After studying incident after incident of Tyler Nelson and the way that he manipulated people, Emilio thought he knew him like the back of his hand. He thought he had him scouted well. He thought he had the former president of PCW dead to rights. So when he entered the arena, poised in his composure in front of tens of thousands of Chicago fans, he felt very satisfied with what he had said. That he would bring the fight to Nelson. Not the other way around. But he was wrong. And now he would pay. Dearly. For with Emilio now out of the country -and therefore, the picture- there was no telling what that evil bastard would do to PRIME. And just how much a shook up Hin See would let him get away with. With the likes of Richard Larkin, Tony Rolo, and Karina Wolfenden out of the picture as well, who knows if anyone could stop him. Or even if he could be stopped at all. But regardless, as the teetering SUV rounded another bend, swiftly taking the corner-which in cause threw Emilio back against the wall- the fleeting thought came across his mind that he should just give up. But that would never be the case. We should all know the stubbornness and rigidity of Emilio by now. Vampir, The Dark Age, the Alias Division, the Agents, Motoki, Latch Key Kid…they all turned out the same. So bowing his head again, ignoring the laughter of the INS agents as they watched their captive smack his cranium up against the side of the van interior caused by another swerve in the road, Emilio bowed his head in contemplation and began to scheme. He knew where his first call from prison would me made to. And to whom. Chapter II. The Trial. “Here goes nothin’,” Emilio whispered quietly. Stepping up to the podium, he raised his right hand and took the oath before cautiously taking the stand. Internally, Emilio’s stomach began to pull into tight rigid knots as the penetrating eyes of the graying old men focused in on him. They seemed to be scrutinizing every bone in his body, following every movement of his body like a sniper locked in on a weary target, just waiting for the moment to strike. Or maybe it was just him. It had been a very long and trying week for him. From convincing Hin See to allow him to face Nelson, to facing Deville instead, to getting deported from the country. Indeed, it had been less than a month back in his second foray in professional wrestling and already he found himself tired out. He was overcome with emotion now, distraught as the distrusting men unfolded there notes, adjusted their glasses, and prepared to grill him with questions. It was hard enough to convince Hin See to lend him a lawyer, temporarily, and double-so to get the lawyer to convince the feds to have this hearing.
