Insecurities - (CD Roleplay)

3 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-03-05 On weeks where nothing mattered, Nicholas would travel back to Pacific Avenue, by way of rented car, to visit his vacation home. An eyes-wide-open blue colored villa with an inground pool and jacuzzi to go with it. Area rugs made of mink and a stream of water that has the formations of an 'N' and a 'B'. Where maids will dust your furniture, your walls, your balls and television screens in payment of satisfaction to be within the walls of pure beauty. The outer walls decorated with bricks of gold with fountains planted around the lawn, spontaneously shooting spring into the secluded air. This represented all that he needed in life. It represented magic. Here comes a week where nothing mattered. Here comes Ozric strolling down Pacific Avenue, with his rented black Hyundai, in the realm of a priceless mood until he reaches his property. The villa, nothing but a run-down crack shack where the scattered paint chips make more value to the house than the lawn. The neighborhood, a blink away from being a legitimate 'hood', is home to crotch-grabbing, eyebrow-lowering, grill-representin', crunk-jumpers that even Nicholas, a man of massive size, locks his doors while going through. He lightly tapped the break until his car made a dead stop beside 42 Pacific - Ozric's home and only home as far as he was concerned. Opening the door, Ozric lets himself out of the car and walks until his feet touch the curb boundary. This wasn't home anymore. This is where Pablo Escobar is, he wasn't slayed afterall. This wasn't home anymore. Just another memory in this chapter book I'm endlessly reading. Turning around, Ozric jumps back into the car and shuts the door. This house did have an excessive memory card lodged into it. This was where Ozric ran away from his parents and ended up lost at the bottom of a bridge, frozen from the cold and drenched from the lake waters that he fell in. This was where his father cut his mother up like a salmon before Ozric stole the show by slamming into his father, shoulder-first, against the fridge, knocking it over along with his father. This is where he first tried drugs, had sex, and shot a mutant puppet in the eyeball with a pellet gun -- wait, that mutant puppet wasn't Ozric, was it? Yes.. yes it was. This was home in so many ways. He collected the rest of his gutted thoughts from one of the car rugs and accelerated down the street. God. Five times this week, Ozric. Five times you've come to this God-damn avenue. For what? Hopes that the motherfucker's going to be all daisies and good times again? Look, I don't want this anymore. I'm tired of listening to you and I'm tired of letting you get to me. You're no longer the voice I'm depending on. No? Seems to me I was the only thing helping you through most of your first tenure in PRIME. Those matches? The agony your body was being put through by being an amatuer with absolutely no background? That appeared well and good because of me, Ozric. I'm your savior. I'm your-- Don't say it. I don't want to hear that word anymore. If I needed his help, I'd ask for it. Now go find someone else to dry hump. Fine. I'll go pay a visit to Ignatius Lisiuex. He's certainly doesn't need my assistance as much as you do, but I'm sure there's a couple of screws and bolts laying around him that I can completely warp the fuck out of. Ignatius Lisiuex.. He had recieved a call from Toshiaki Motoki a few short hours earlier regarding information about Ignatius. There was an apparent 'distress' unfolding in the hands of Motoki and the PRIME administration courtesy of Mr. Lisiuex and he wanted to warn him to keep watchful eyes in the back of his head. Perfect. On top of his fight to eradicate the defects within, his mind wandering in the matter of a certain title gazing at him, and his home being nothing more than a block of waste, he had a charbroiled douche-bag on his heels. So, instead of heading back to his shady 'Days INN' hotel room on the tail-end of the Jersey Turnpike, he continued to drive harmlessly through .. harm. He took the proper precautions by locking his doors and rolling up his.. glass.. windows. Just before leaving his room was when the breaking-news special took flight on-air. Ozric was devouring a bag of a wasabi nuts when he nearly choked on one through constriction after hearing that Ignatius had abducted his own doctor, Dr. Silverman, in hopes to make a deal with the corporation's presidents. He's in California. There's no way he'd make the trip to Jersey just to bludgeon you to death. I mean, considering the circumstance, it'd be worth it if I were him. ..But you're not. Listen, I did these things only to make an example out of what we need to eradicate on this planet. You almost burned him alive. That's murder? That's jail time? That's what? An open door for a judge to consider the what? The death penalty. Therefore, you were THIS close to killing someone and getting nothing out of it but what? Your OWN death certificate. Maybe I've made a few wrong decisions in my life. So what? I've capitalized off of more than half of them. ..and I saved money on my car insurance by using Geico? Who gives a flying fuck about how many of the wrongs you've corrected, Nicholas? Let's be realistic. You can't go back in time and mourn for your rediculously sadistic ways. You can't take the root of being reborn because half of the PRIME roster is experimenting with that now. What do you have to do? ..I don't know anymore. I don't want to know anymore. Persist. Persist? What's the difference what people think now? You tried to kill someone on live television. There aren't many who are going to take condemnatory seriously, so why bother wasting your time with it? Let's go with persistance. Let's preach onward and rise to each occasion that pops its ugly, pimple-covered head from underneath the bleachers. This is 2005 -- Let's be modern and surrealistcally realistic. Persist - He thought to himself as he turned a sharp left onto a dark side-street before entering the onramp to the highway. He thought about this more than anything now. Slavon was right on so many different levels. Why bother wasting his time doing things that aren't going to make the situation better? What's the sense of opening your door to the protesters with a waving hand when you have two loaded AK-47s hung on the wall to your left? If you can get away with it, then why not? These people in attendance every night? The adolescent free birds that throw soda cans, gum wrappers and diaphragms? They don't like him in the least bit and could care less whether they ever did or not. Why not return the favor and become wickedly rebellious? Ozric slammed his breaks and made a screeching halt on a car scarce highway. Fuck Ignatius Lisiuex.. That's the spirit. What the else do you got? Fuck everyone that I've ever stood for. Fuck the smiles and cries. Fuck the fans, the PRIME administration, Vangelis Olsig, the Intense division, and most of all, fuck Ignatius Lisieux. You know what? I want him to make the long trip down here to bludgeon me. Afterall, this is what I've been waiting a year for. Let's give all these assholes, gazing at us with zombie-eyes and drooling mouths, a dandy show, Iggy. COME RIP ME APART, YOU FUCKING NOBODY! Right. Fuckin-A I'm right. No. A right to veer towards the next off-ramp. This is the Days Inn exit and you're drunk. I was never here inside the car with you and you've been driving for six hours straight. The car was now positioned in one of the various parking spots directly in front of the Days Inn hotel. Flourescent lights that boldly stated 'Vacancy' were what, Ozric thought, woke him up. He didn't feel tired at all, but then again, didn't really want to be awake, either. He opened his car door, exited and closed it. Walking down the pathway towards the entrance, he let everything escape him. His rant about tolerance and superiority had become just that, a rant. It vanished like an ice sculpture in a furious blaze of heat. Slavon was poisoning him. Convincing the weakened to do what's technically right, but unquestionably wrong. Fuck Ignatius Lisiuex? Fuck me.
