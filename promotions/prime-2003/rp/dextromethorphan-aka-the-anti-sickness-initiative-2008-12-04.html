DexTroMethOrPhan, aka, the Anti-Sickness Initiative

4 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-04-08 Hacking up a lung, Tony Davis rests on the living room couch to his humble abode, adorn in wet nap towels and blankets and chicken soup, wrapped up like a burrito. He stares, bloodshot eyed and weary, at the television, which blares out Kristen Bell’s voice as she narrates the popular tween show Gossip Girl. Which, awkwardly, is placed six inches away from his resting forehead, so much so he could headbutt the characters when he disagrees with their actions. He sneezes, a sneeze you’d be unlikely to forget, blaring like a foghorn. The splatter flies and sticks onto the plasma screen, just inches away. "Little..." Tony gasps for air, unable to finish his sentence due to his lungs giving out on him. He takes a few quick breaths before taking one long final one. He shouts, hoarsly, "LITTLE HELP~!?" Sighing as she enters, Mary-Lynn Mayweather hurries into the room carrying a tray of soup. She’s dressed down, red jogging pants with the phrase “Just a Butt” across her butt, like the fad trend going around these days. A red tank top completes the dressed down ensemble. She slides over to Tony's side and next to a pile of discarded soup bowls stacked as high as his couch, and sets her supper down next to them. She looks at Tony, who sheepishly looks up at her. "Where's Tammy?" Tony asks of his wife, coughing as he completes this thought. Quickly, he hacks up something from his lung and spits into an empty beer bottle. "She, uh," Mary-Lynn rubs the back of her neck. "She can't handle the spittoon." Mayweather gestures to the empty bottle of beer which has since been half filled with spit. Tony places it behind the couch, next to his disgustingly outdated soup bowls. Mary-Lynn takes this time to clean the plasma, wiping it down thoroughly. "She took Kate and the kids, took them out of town for a few days. Get you well, keep them well. You know?" Mary-Lynn sees a spot on the television which begins to mock her, and so she scrubs rather diligently to prove it wrong. "Never mind that we're the ones with the professional jobs having to go into work every week and put our body, NAY, our lives on the line." Mayweather successfully defeats the spot on the television, and stands to her feet in a sedated triumph. Tony blows his nose like a seagull squawking through a bull horn. Mayweather stares at the plasma, her job just so recently finished needing to be completed again. Slowly tilting his head back up to Mary-Lynn as he holds in a sneeze, Tony tries to say something but the sneeze keeps trying to come out. Davis breaths in short deep breaths, his neck craning back with each one, just inches from the cliff of the proverbial sneeze. On the fourth, Tony deeply exhales in relief, and sniffles once or twice. He then inhales again, and proceeds to cough into his own hands, a long spluttering series of coughs which soak Tony's hands in his own juices. "Ugh... Where's Fly?" Tony asks, reaching out for a tissue box that appears to be just inches away from his reach. That's when a large yellow boot slams into frame. The boot kicks the tissues lightly over to Tony, which allows him to have tissues AND not get up. Tony, relieved, looks up to see a man dressed in what he would describe as a yellow space suit, arms crossed in a pose that resembled one of those MTV Music Award Statues. "Oh God!" Tony deeply inhales and coughs, eyes bugging out. Pushing through his own pain, Tony croaks out, "I KNEW MY COLD WAS AN ALIEN PLAGUE!" He whimpers slightly. “And now we’re ALL doomed!” Jack Harmen, better known to the wrestling world as High Flyer, wobbles upright in what would be best considered a bio-hazard suit. Extra large glove like hands, a large astronaut like helmet, and a huge yellow suit are just a few of the fashion accessories that High Flyer has on his person this evening. QVC had a special. "Tony. Calm down. If Aliens were here, Max Hopper would be either defending us or getting us into more trouble. I just... I can't get sick, you hear?" Flyer's voice gravels out through a speaker. Flyer takes a knee next to Davis' couch, but slams it down onto the freshly prepared bowl of soup. The bowl flips over and spills all over the room, but Flyer doesn't feel a thing. Mary-Lynn sighs, huffing as she rushes out of the room to prepare more. "I'm in the Jewel in the Crown, maybe this week, maybe next. I don't know, y'know! I don't know!" Flyer swings his head from the left to the right. "Which is why I've been taking 18 tablets of emergenC, 5 or 6 bottles of NyQuil or DayQuil Cough a day, and decided this would be the most appropriate attire to go down to my son's elementary school and say hello." Mary-Lynn rushes back into the room, towel in hand. "Oh that's a horrible idea. You have the worst ideas ever. That may have been your worst idea yet. I mean, I’d say that if all your other ideas weren’t just as bad. You should stop having ideas. Now that’s a good idea." Flyer blinks. If you could see it through the mask, he blinked. "I feel like I'm a giant." Flyer says, holding out his hands a bit to stabilize himself. He tries to stand to his feet, but can only wobble to a seated-upright position. “Aren’t you hot in that suit?” Tony asks, blowing his nose after. “I feel wonderful…” Flyer says, pulling at his biohazard suit. "Why can't I pop my collar?" Flyer keeps grabbing at thin air. Mary-Lynn tilts her head to its side. She reaches over and grabs Flyer by his biohazard suit, forcing him to remain seated. "No, Jack. You're not leaving this room. You've got about 8 to 12 hours of my care ahead of you, you hear?" Mary-Lynn lets go, shaking her head as she turns and high tails it out of the room. "I swore, after my college boyfriend, never again!” “Jack?” Tony says, quietly. Flyer doesn’t respond. Davis tilts his head, trying to see Flyer through his suit. It appears as if Flyer has fallen asleep. “Fly?” Tony quietly asks again, this time leaning from his laying position for a clearer look. “Are you there?” Tony reaches over, and shakes Flyer’s biohazard suited shoulder. Flyer shakes awake, his head jerking around wildly. Slurring, “Isss my caar blewd up?” Jack slips out, his upper body falling against the couch’s arm rest as if he could no longer keep it upright. “I sought it blow up.” Flyer mumbles, head resting in his arm. “What was that!?” Tony’s eyes narrow in confusion. “What was what?” Flyer looks from side to side. “I… sought I heard a penguin. Or saw Anna Paquin. Or maybe a pumpkin?” Tony blinks. Flyer lowers his shoulders and his head, the suit slunking over in the process, pulling him down. “Oh… I… I misssss.” Flyer lets out a quick whale, tossing his head back, and falling to the ground in the process. He sniffles, at the same time Tony does. He wipes a tear from his eyes, or would if he wasn’t blocked by the biohazard suit. “Kate. WHY!” He winces. “I’m a fuck up, fucked up fuck up with fucked up philosophies and fucked up morals and fucked up, everything.” He raises just his head from the floor. “I blinded Ivy. I TOOK AWAY SIGHT.”He smacks the back of his head back down to the carpeted floor, letting out a thud. “I don’t deserve to see my kids…” His eyes go wide, pupils dilated. “I’m not seeing my kids right now?” Flyer points over to the corner, where a piano rests. Davis leers over, and then back. “Nope. No kids there. And I think your eyes are closed…” Davis pauses. “I’m sorry. I just had to take in the fact that I’m more lucid than you are and kind of deal with it on a personal level.” Flyer tilts his head to the side in childlike wonder. “Are you okay now? Davis narrows his eyes quickly. Before he can respond, Mary-Lynn Mayweather walks back into frame, holding a fresh bowl of chicken soup and a huge gallon jug of water. “Drink up. Eat up. We need you both ready, and preferably not tripping balls for tomorrow.” Flyer tosses his hand lazily up into the air, and it flops back down to the ground. “I’VE GOTTA SAY SOMETHING.” Flyer slurs, attempting to push himself up but putting no actual effort or energy into doing so. He promptly gives up. “I saw GOD! He was at the bakery.” Mary-Lynn rolls her eyes. Before she can say anything, Flyer continues. “I” Flyer quickly swallows. “I was five… and my dad took me aside on Christmas and said, ‘Son. I love you, you know that?’ And I remember thinking… I should.” He pauses. Mary-Lynn’s mouth opens to speak but Flyer continues over top. “I thought I was doomed to die the way my father did, repeating his actions throughout my childhood into my career, and I did everything I could to stop that from happening. And when I felt doomed, I felt my family slipping away to death’s door; I kicked Kate in the face.” Flyer swings his head low. “To save her… just to save her. And here we are, fine, alive, kicking, but… It was all delusions… it’s always delusions…” Flyer sighs. “I wish I was right. I wish I saved her. I wish I doomed myself. I’d rather been a martyr than a villain…” Flyer’s head swings skyward, a bit too far, so he’s looking up into the ceiling fan. “That’s what God told me.” With that, an eerie silence fills the air, and we slowly fade away from our uncomfortable scene.
