The Underground King (JITC RP)

13 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/13/08 The scene opens up at the studio of the 106 & park with their host of the show Terrance and Rocsi. Coming back from there number ten video. Fans are cheering and clapping in excitement. Holding up their hands up in the air, screaming. Then rocsi begins to speak as the cheers and screams die out in the background. “Okay, that was our number ten video, My Life by Game Ft. Lil’ Wayne. Now we have a great show today for you guy’s we have comedy’s funny man Katt Williams.” She say’s at the crowd erupts in cheers. “We have The King of New York PRIME’s superstar Jimmy “The Poster Boy” Bonafide. And the rest of our countdown. “Yes, that’s right. What a show we have.” Terrance say’s. “Right now we have our number nine video by Keyshia Cole. And when we return from our video, funny man Katt Williams will join us.” The crowd cheers as they cut to their number nine video. Off camera fans are still cheering, while Katt Williams walk back and forth through the crowd signing autographs and talking to his fans. The camera then fades to a commercial. -Five Minutes Later- We open up to Terrance and Rocsi getting ready to introduce their first guess today Katt Williams. “Okay, I know you guys been waiting and the time is here. Now he came out in Nick Cannon’s Wild N Out, We also seen him in Pimp Chronicles 1 & 2 it’s the funny man himself Katt Williams.” He say’s as the crowd jump to their feet, and begin to scream in excitement. Katt Williams then emerges out the back, threw the curtains fans begin to cheer and chant Katt Williams name as he walks on the stage wearing his green jacket, blue jeans and white air forces. He walks up to the couch where Terrance and Rocsi are already sitting down waiting for Katt to join them. He sits down at the couch and grabs the microphone that is next to him. The cheers fade, as Rocsi begins to speak welcoming Katt Williams to the show. “Hey Katt, welcome to the show.” “Thank you, it is an honor to be here.” “How are you doing, How is life treating you?” “I have to say, life has been treating me good.” He says as he grabs the chain that camron gave him a while back. Rosci giggles “So does this mean you are still with Dipset?” “Yes, I am. I’m not going anywhere.” “That’s good to hear, so do you have any new projects coming out?” Terrance asks. “Actually, yes I do. I am working on an album and have another comedy DVD coming out real soon. So look out for the pimp chronicles Pt.3” Katt Williams says with a smile on his face. “Very nice, so you been very busy lately.” “Yeah, real busy working on all those projects. But it’s going to be worth it.” “Yes, and we will all be on the lookout for those new projects. Katt would you like to introduce our number eight video?” “Sure no problem. Here is the number eight video Jennifer Hudson-Spotlight.” Fans jump to their feet, and begin to jump up and down and scream at the top of their lungs. The camera then fades out to the music video. Five minutes later they return from there video still sitting down on the couch as the fans cheers get lower. Rosci then begins to speak to Katt Williams about life on the road. “You are always on the road, does that have any effect on your family?” She asks him. “It sure does, I don’t get to see my kids as much and spend time with them. Every minute I get to see them or be with them I take it.” He says with a smile on his face. “It’s not easy always being on the road, you get home sick and miss your family.” “Yeah, I understand.” Terrance says. “Well, we have to go to a commercial break. But when we come back we will have Jimmy Bonafide joining us.” Rosci says. The crowd begins to cheer and chant Poster Boy’s name as the camera fades to a commercial. When they return, fans are cheering and chanting Bonafides name. BONAFIDE! BONAFIDE! BONAFIDE! “Okay, know our next guest is the king of NY. PRIME’S superstar Jimmy “The Poster Boy” Bonafide.” Terrance say’s as they claps and chant still continue. Jimmy Bonafide then emerges out the back, with a smile on his face. He is wearing light blue jeans white air forces, and a white shirt with a blue Yankee fitted. With a platinum cross chain, He walks to the crowd and greets them by shaking hands and signing quick autographs. He then walks up on stage with Terrance and Rosci and greets them. He sits down at the couch with Katt Williams, and they begin to talk to him. “Hey, how’s it going Bonafide. Welcome to the show this is your 1 st time on 106&Park isn’t it?” Terrance asks Poster Boy. “I’m doing good, thanks for having me here today.” Bonafide replies. “Well we are happy to have you here today. I hear you have a big match this revolution 178; you will be a part of the Jewel in the Crown Tournament with some very talented wrestlers. First I want to wish you good luck in your match.” Rosci says. “Yes, I will be in a tournament with some very talented guys. Guys I looked up too, and still look up too, I am just happy to compete with them and be in the same ring with them. From guys like Killean Sirrajin, High Flyer, Dusk, Tyler Rayne, Jason Natas, Bryan Dawkins, Jason Snow and other talented superstars. I just can’t wait.” Bonafide explains. “Nice, good luck once again. I can’t wait to see the outcome.” Rosci says. “Neither can i.” Bonafides says. “What do you like to do on your spare time Bonafide?” Rosci ask. “Well, I spend time with my wife and kid, and two dogs. I like to go to the gym a lot and work-out, I play a lot of sports. I write music, and have a couple of songs coming out.” Bonafide says. “Oh really, so you been hitting the studio and spending quality time with your wife that is good.” Rosci says. “You hear that girls, he isn’t single. He has a wife and kid.” She says. As the females in the crowd boo. “So you rap, Bonafide?” Terrance asks. “Yes, a little something.” “Do you think, we can hear something?” “Sure, I can spit a little something for you. I call this the underground king.” Bonafide explains. “Okay, well hit the stage and we will hit the beat so that you can start.” Rosci says. Bonafide gets up from the couch still with the mic in his hand. He walks over to the stage, then the beat begins to play, Fans begin to cheer and chant for Poster Boy as he begins spitting his verse to his song. ‘The Underground King’. “Clap your hands, yeah. I’m the underground king and I been crowned, the body’s underneath the house and they aint been found. The glocks and the burners stay stashed with the pounds. But I rather bust clips, till the body’s hit the ground. We can go round for round, block for block for the crown. Don’t matter to me, when it’s money over everything. Everything and anything I’m doing for the crown. I aint King but I pop for that crown. And I hunt niggas down, like I’m one of the best. Like the rest, with a vest. But I’m blessed. And I’m destined for the crown. Like pun was one of the best, leave you R.I.P like the rest, while your soul blows in the wind. Put to rest!” Bonafide finishes his verse and the fans erupt in screams for the King of NY. Poster Boy throws his hands up in the air, and begins to walk back to the couch where. Katt Williams, Terrance and Rosci are still sitting at. “Man, that was pretty good Bonafide. Keep up to good work. Know I hear you have some where to be so you will be leaving us.” The crowd begins to boo. “But thank you for blessing us with your presence and I’m sure this isn’t going to be the last time we see you here on our show. Good luck to you in your tournament, I hope you win and everyone will be cheering for you.” “Thank you, yes I have to go back to my hotel room and get on the road to revolution. But thank you for having me, I really appreciate it.” Bonafide says. “Before you leave can you introduce our next video?” “Sure, He is your next video Beyonce-If I was a boy.” The cameras begin to fade as they go to the video. -Scene end.
