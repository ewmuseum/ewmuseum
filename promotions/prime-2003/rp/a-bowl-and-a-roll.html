A Bowl and a Roll

undated

Date: Monday Monday, July 9, 11:30 AM Wrestlers have lives outside of the business. That’s the way it’s always been and that’s the way it always will be. Most experienced entertainers will attempt to keep their two lives separate. Some will succeed beyond their greatest expectations. Some... well, not so much. At least not on this particular day. “What the fuck do you mean NOT COVERING?!” Nicholas was trying not to fly off the handle, but he was not having great success. His stress level was quickly rising and it would hit him hard soon in his checkbook. He could feel it. “They just called this morning Nick. Apparently, it’s going to take many weeks, perhaps a couple months to get proper coverage stamped down on this with the paperwork required. You know Mom doesn’t have coverage through work, so she needs to pay for it now and be reimbursed later.” Hailey said with a tear in her eye. Nicholas’ sister was hugging their mother close, trying to calm the waterfall of tears coming from her eyes. Nitz however, was not showing much sympathy. Not that he wasn’t feeling terribly sorry for his mother having this cancer problem, but he was not outward with those kinds of feelings. “Ok, how much is this gonna cost me... and why can’t that lazy ass in the other room pay for it?!” He pushed off his perch on the stove, where he sat even when he was young. He stormed into the doorway to the living room to see his father passed out on the La-Z-Boy chair with a bottle in his hands and a ball game on. “Look at him, the lazy fuck can’t even hear me.” “Nick please, Mom is getting upset.” Hailey said, handing her mother a tissue to dry her eyes. But Nick was still fueling the fire so to speak. He roared back to the table where they were sitting. “Oh come on Hail... Mom knows that Dad is a lazy drunken bastard who refuses to get help. She has been working to keep this house and now she can’t. So now what? Who pays for this stuff here? Certainly not HIM!” He pointed back to the living room. He took a deep breath and ran his fingers through his hair. He came to sit right beside his Mom and gave her a hug. She leaned over and began to cry in his shoulder and he was trying to talk to her. “Mom... Mom listen to me. I’m going to help, lord knows I will. It’s lucky that I got this massive bonus otherwise we’d be all floating in a river of shit right now.” He looked into her eyes and told her he loved her, but as he hugged her again, he cast a menacing glance towards the living room. Hailey picked up on it and shook her head. She mouthed the words, “I know” to him and he nodded. Their mother pulled away and wiped her eyes again, thanking Nicholas over and over again. It was always his intention to help out, he just didn’t figure he’d have to carry the whole burden on his shoulders for the first month or two of treatment. The chemo was going to be the worst of all, but she would have to get other meds too that needed to be paid for. They weren’t cheap but Nicholas always believed in family before anything. His Mom came first. His sister came first. His Dad, the day he wanted to get help would also come first. “Nick, I hate to ask you this, but can you stay here this week and help me get Mom to her appointments?” Hailey asked. It was the straw that broke the camels back. He got up from his seat and a vein was popping out of his head. “You know I hate to be the mean person here, but someone has to say it. I’m shellin’out the dough for all of this to happen. How do you think I’m doing it? Sitting on my fuckin’ hands with my thumbs in my ass singing old McDonald?! Perhaps I should go pull up a dusty ol’ La-Z-Boy beside Boozer the Jack Daniels’ cock sucker in there and pull back half a bottle. Should I do that? Or should I go and do what I’m getting paid to do and bring some money back to help out?” He was overreacting but the stress was really getting the better of him. It was eating slowly at his mind the whole time he stood in the kitchen but now it finally released a shit storm of fury on his sanity and drove him over. He was right in a way, he was being asked for help financially and then he was questioned as to whether he’d skip work too. No workie, no monie... erm, money. Yeah, that’s it. “Dear dear please.” His Mother spoke. “No need to yell. I’m sure Hailey will be able to take care of things. I’m not an invalid.” As she finished, they heard a loud hacking cough from the living room, along with the sound of a breaking bottle. “Wha’ tha fuck is that racket in there?!” A drunk Cameron Donnelly yelled through coughs. Nicholas hit himself in the temples a few times, before going to kiss his mother and his sister. “I love you both, but I need to get outta here. Otherwise, I might go into that living room and vacate that chair.” He patted his Mom on the back. “I’ll come see you before I leave this week ok?” She smiled and he proceeded to leave, slamming the door a little harder than was perhaps necessary. “Mom, I can’t handle this on my own. I need to work too you know.” Hailey said while rubbing her Mom on the arms, but Trina cleared her throat. “You won’t have to my dear. Chad is flying in this week.” Hailey cringed. “It’s a good thing you didn’t bring that up while Nick was here. He’d have stayed to tear his brother apart.” She said, sighing deeply knowing that she would have to be around to deal with her brother Chad as well. “You don’t think I know that Hailey? Why do you think I told him to go?” She shook her head, wondering why her problems were having no success in bringing her family closer together. Meanwhile, Nicholas stopped half way down the drive way. He had his hands on his hips and was looking up into the cloudy sky. It wasn’t a bad mixture of his professional life and his personal life. He had bills to pay after all and his work was paying for it all. But in the end, his Mom would never expect him to step away from the job that he does better than anyone out there even if only for a few days. He knew that and he followed that with all that he was. What he needed was to create distance between himself and the reason for the stress. There was only one place to go where he could clear his mind. Mikey’s. Within twenty minutes, Nicholas was pulling up outside Mikey’s. The restaurant was unusually busy for mid-afternoon but he gave that no mind. He walked up to the chalkboard under the canopy to check out the daily specials that Mikey updated himself every day. Nothing really caught his eye on the board so he pushed the door open and walked inside. Immediately, the smell of cigarette smoke filled his nostrils, something that he never experienced before when stepping into Mikey’s place. It was a well known no smoking establishment yet someone inside saw fit to break some of the rules. He scanned the place closely until he spotted Mikey walking quickly to a table, throwing his arms in the air. “Listen man, you’ve been here before and you know it’s non-smoking in here. So put the damn thing out.” Mikey said with a rather concerned voice, noticing that a few of the patrons who enjoyed the no smoking atmosphere were getting up to leave their tables. “Look Mac, there is no law that says I can’t smoke in here so you have to serve me, so get to it.” The fat bastard said while laughing with his past her prime girlfriend/wife/fuck buddy. He was right in a way, but Mikey had put the no smoking rule in place for his numerous non-smoking patrons and the extra smoke would do no favors for the chowder and numerous other foods that could absorb the smoke. Mikey tossed his arms up again, knowing that the man was mostly right. But Nicholas was not going to have any of this garbage in his friend’s establishment. He broke through a row of empty tables and made his way around what people were still sitting in their seats. He approached the man and his lady friend and stood for one moment, winning a stare from the still smoking man. “What the hell do you want skinny?” Nicholas stood there for one moment, his leather jacket slipping off his shoulders slightly and parts of his hair out of place. The sunglasses he was wearing were preventing the man from seeing all the anger in his eyes. It wasn’t just anger from this situation, but it was his day that caused all of it. He needed to vent and right now this man seemed to be a great outlet. He leaned on the table with two hands and looked the man right in the eyes as his woman leaned back a little. “Look pal, read the fuckin’ sign. This restaurant is non-smoking and if you aren’t going to put the smoke out, I’ll have to remove your fat, smelly carcass myself and toss your wrinkled, overused love doll out along side of you. Do I make myself clear?” He was rather calm for a man threatening another, much bigger individual. The smoker began to laugh and so did his woman. This was not going to end well at all. “You?! You’re tiny ass is gonna toss me out? HA! Get outta here string bean.” The smoker said, blowing some excess smoke in Donnelly’s direction. “Ya skinny, beat it!” The confident yet still ugly woman added while lighting up a cigarette herself. No, Nicholas would not be able to keep his cool forever. “Last chance.” The couple sat there, waving Nicholas away with their hands. He nodded and walked away from the table. The couple laughed more, thinking they had defeated their enemies and ceremoniously blew more smoke into the air. Mikey was back in the kitchen putting lids of the numerous pots and pans in am attempt to keep the smoke away. Meanwhile, Nicholas calmly pushed the door open to the kitchen and took a quick look around the kitchen. He moved past Mikey and grabbed a previously used and still rather dirty cast iron pan from the sink area and looked it over. He nodded to himself this time and went for the other kitchen door that came out not too far behind the rebel couple. “Nitz, what the heck are you doing?” Mikey said as he watched his friend methodically make his way out of the kitchen, pan in hand. Nicholas manoeuvred around another couple chairs and tables towards the couple. The mans back was partially turned to him but the woman could see him approaching but could not see the pan by his side. She started to laugh again but Nicholas acted swiftly to bring the hard pan around the backside of the individuals head. The woman saw it at the last possible second but didn’t have time to react before the resounding clang rang out through the restaurant. Nicholas brought the pan down again, with the woman backing off and yelling, across the back of the man. He put the pan down on the table and grabbed the dazed man by his hair and shirt, dragging him out of the seat he was in. He wrapped an arm around his shoulder and began to stagger with the man to the front door. “I told ya didn’t I? Put the fuckin’ smoke out or I’d remove you from here myself. So your smart mouth has to spew some shit to me and I had to do what I had to do. I warned you to your fat face and you laugh at me? Fuck you buddy.” He pushed the door open, took the arm off his shoulder and quickly pushed the man to the ground. His knee scraped against the pavement and split open a little. Meanwhile, his woman friend pushed out past Nitz and went to her mealy mouthed love cushion. After checking him, she got back up and went to go scream at Donnelly some more, but he was having none of it. “I told your two chinned hero that I’d throw him out of here and I did. Keep flappin’ those cock suckin’ lips to me and I’ll live up to the other half and make sure you land right beside him bitch.” The threats were real but his calm tone put some real fear into his voice. Though he wasn’t the biggest man on the block, he talked a tough game and could back it up 95% of the time. The woman said a few more words and went to help her man. Nicholas turned to walk back inside and Mikey leaned out the open door. “And don’t come back again!” He added while quickly turning to follow Nicholas. The Ego took a seat near the kitchen and immediately threw his face into his hands, sighing deeply. “Nitz, what the hell was that all about? I’ve never seen you act that way in here before. That guy could have sat on you and made you disappear if you didn’t have that pan. What’s gotten into you?” Mikey sat down opposite The Ego and wrapped his dish towel around his hands in a nervous manner, wondering if the police would be alerted by the rebel couple just ejected from the premises. “It’s been a bad... no, very bad day. I told you about my Mom and her cancer before and now I have to pay for it all. That’s not what’s bugging me really. It’s the fact that Dad couldn’t give a shit about her. He is sitting on his piece of shit chair, drinking himself into a stupor like a fuck while she is fighting for her life. It makes me angry and sick.” He said while slamming his fists on the table, surprising his friend a little. Mikey wrapped the dish towel around a single hand and got up from his seat. He walked beside Donnelly and grabbed his shoulder, a gesture to show that everything will be ok. Nicholas shared almost everything with Mikey. They had met back in grade 2 and have been inseparable ever since. They told each other secrets, helped each other out when there was trouble and have basically stuck with each other for 15 years through thick and thin. Mikey lost his parents a couple years previous and inherited a lot of money. So being 22 and having his own restaurant came at a very high price. Nicholas stood by his friend in his time of need and he had feared that soon he would need the help next. He was confident his Mom would pull out of the cancer, but ones mind always wanders to the worst possible scenario. The key was that Donnelly was not friendly with many people. His family and friends, who basically amount to Mikey and his wife, were the only people he liked being with at all. In the wrestling business, Nitz had very few friends, and more than a handful of allies. He stuck with people in the business, but they were acquaintances, nothing more. He mostly kept to himself in the business, so his own goals were the top priority. Hear that Shakur? “Man, I know what would cheer you up even a little. A bowl and roll special.” Mikey said while he patted his friend on the back. “You can read my mind Mikey.” Donnelly said in a somewhat sad tone but he was starting to come around. The basics of a bowl and roll special is a bowl of the New England Special Clam Chowder and a homemade garlic roll with a special seasoning which makes it perfect for dipping. “And hey, it’s on me man. My best friend isn’t going to come in here and pay for something when he needs me.” He patted Nicholas on the back once more, winning a small smile. Not many people could win a genuine smile from The Ego and in the wrestling business that was more than true. His family and friends were the only ones who could make him smile. Sure, he may have an egotistical smile or a confident smile in the business, but never the emotional smile that means something. Mikey went back into the kitchen and Nicholas was left to himself to just sit and think. But that didn’t last long. His cell phone began to vibrate in the pocket of his coat and he pulled it out, seeing his parent’s home number. Perhaps his Mom needed something or perhaps his sister wanted a lift somewhere, as her car was in the shop. But when he flipped open the phone, he didn’t expect the gravely, drunken cough that greeted him. “Hey k... kid you there?” His father stuttered his words, the alcohol clearly evident in his voice. “Motherfucker...” Nitz thought after discovering who was calling him. “What do you want old man? How’d you dial the phone on your own?” He said in a cold voice. He loved his father... well... what used to be his father. He loved what his father was, a loving and caring man. In dealing with Trina's cancer, he turned into an alcoholic heartless person. “Now why do ya gotta talk to me like that son? I’m callin’ for a good reasom... reas... ya.” Nitz shook his head, thinking that his pops was out of booze and about to ask for some to be picked up. But what came next was not what he was thinking. “Boy, your family is gonna be hear next week. I heard them talkin’ in the kitchen and they said somethin’ about your brother comin’ here to stay for a while. Wednesday or somethin’.” “WHAT?! Are you talking about Chad? Are you sure?” “I’m pretty sure I know the names of my kids. There’s Hailey, Nicholas, Jack Dan, ummm Wild Turkey and Chad. No wait, Wild Turkey is good stuff... smooooooooth...” He was starting to drift off but amidst the names of different bottles of alcohol, he gathered the one important fact. His brother Chad was coming. He shut the phone, hearing only snoring on the other end. “That fuck... ARGH!” He yelled, winning more glances from the patrons who witnessed his earlier actions. Chad was not welcome in his mind, but there was nothing he could do about it. He’d be coming in Wednesday morning while Nicholas was away for PRIME. It was too well planned to be a coincidence. Chad knew that Nicholas hated him for taking all this time to call home and check on their mom. Nicholas received the bowl and roll from the waitress and he thanked her. He took a few spoonfuls of the hot dish and proceeded to rest his face in his hands once again. What a week it was going to be for The Ego. Wednesday, it was going to be the Universal Champion and the Southern Sheep Lover tasting the business end of his foot in PRIME. When he got home, his brother would eat the business end of his fist. Neither was going to be pretty.
