The Next Generation?

7 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/7/06 The Next Generation? Professional wrestling is such a tumultuous business; it’s not even funny. The right moves here, the attention of these fans there, and you could grow to be a legend one day. You botch one move, hurt a fellow worker in the ring, people start to question everything you do. Next thing you know, you’re wrestling the preliminary Dark Match while people go out and buy the merchandise of somebody that matters. But say, you make all the right moves. You accomplish everything that you want in your wrestling lifetime. People know your name, they know of all your accolades, they want their kids to grow up and be just like you. To those people, I ask you what’s left? What could you possibly do that you haven’t already? You’re famous. Everybody knows you. Future generations of wrestlers -- both the out-of-shape and the physically fit – they pattern themselves after you and the things you do in the ring that made you so great. What’s next comes the future. Do you have any kids that you want involved in this business one day to continue to uphold everything that you did before them? Sure, you do. You want your kids to grow up to have everything that you didn’t to ensure that their existence will be full of wealth and happiness for their hard work. It’s human nature for parents to want their children to surpass them one day. I have a baby girl, Gabrielle. I hope one day, she’ll enjoy what her dad did then look to continue his tradition of destroying everything in his path. Unfortunately, whether I like it or not, it’s not always easy for a woman to make it huge in wrestling. Not everybody is gonna grow up to become the next Karina Wolfenden, Tigera, or Sun Tzu. It’s harder out there for women being in a mostly male-dominated sport and that’s the ill-fated truth. Sometimes, I’ve wanted to have a son to mold into that proverbial next big thing. I wanted to teach him all the things that I didn’t learn until later on in my career so he can avoid the same mistakes and gain Hall of Fame status one day. The, it hit me. I’d found him. He was right there before me. His eyes had an undeniable fire that couldn’t be put down by the mightiest of rainstorms. His wrath burning brighter than the fury of the hottest star. The potential was there. But I was going to bring it all out. Kenjiro ITO: A wrestler barely alive… I could rebuild him. I have the technology. I can make him better than he was; better than he’s ever been. Better… stronger… faster… even more charismatic. I mean, I DO make the ratings spike, so helping some Asian with a modicum of wrestling talent isn’t gonna be THAT hard… STEP ONE: RESEARCH The laboratory was whirring with mechanical life while Mr. Silver plugged away at his Dell computer. Countless hours had been spent in this project. Important dates had been long since forgotten. Meals skipped. Shows TiVo-ed. (Seriously, how the hell was he gonna let himself miss Heroes and House? That’s some good shit right there.) This was his one shot to keep his destiny alive; no way he was gonna muck this up now. He spun his chair around and slid across the room to come to a complete stop near the operation table. A massive sheet enshrouded the cadaver currently placed on it. No doubt this was the product of Sonny’s research. He’d gone through his entire library. Seventeen-plus years worth of matches all from his highest point where he’d won the tSC Championship in Madison Square Garden to the lowest point of his career in OSW where he’d jobbed to Exceptional Dave Hurst and his misbegotten ladle. Eww. Pete thinks he had it bad with Silvio Rochelli? Pete: FUCK. YOU. AT LEAST YOUR GUY WENT ON TO BE SOMEWHAT RESPECTABLE IN RP COMPETITION! Me: :-( Non-sequiturs aside, Mr. Silver retrieved a pen dangling off his ear and jotted down a few more notes onto an empty pad. The ideas were flowing through his head every second, but he had to be careful with which ones he put in there. Obviously, making this new project of his racist against Japanese people was gonna be pretty fucking stupid. Against all others, I suppose racism would work. Sonny slid the chair across the floor until he came to a dead halt in front of his Dell. There, he continued to type up several more thoughts until he was satisfied with the end result. With a content sigh, he smiled and adored his handiwork. “Sweet.” With the successful creation of Mr. Silver’s brand-new Myspace account up and running, he turns right back to the cadaver on the operating table and sighs. “Shit… I suppose I should finish this up. I really don’t want to, but, eh.” He shrugs to himself then opens up MS Word 2003 in order to look at some of his notes collected over the past few months entailing how to keep his legacy going vis-à-vis his creation. In actual fact, it was a hastily done series of jotted notes that included the following: ITO IDEAS: MAKE HIM HATE EVERYBODY RANT ABOUT GREATNESS MAKE EVERYTHING INTO A BIG PRODUCTION GET BITCHES GET MILK. Milk? “Shit, I forgot to pick up Milk. Eme’s gonna kill me.” With a furrowed brow, he placed an elbow on the table to prop his head up in a half-assed effort to think. “I can’t let my legacy end here… think, damn it, think… OH, I KNOW!” One more note was added to the list of potential ideas for the creation of his newest protégé: GIVE HIM SUNGLASSES. SUNGLASSES = +1,000 BADASS BONUS. He moved over to the nearest drawer on his massive desk and dug around for several objects. Almost cartoony rummaging could be heard with garbage being tipped over, cats and dogs hissing and screeching at one another, and even an inexplicable car crash at one point before Sonny grabs a pair of sunglasses. With vigorous gusto, he hurried across the room and places the glasses underneath the cadaver. A satisfied smile crossed the face of the would-be professor before he turned back to his table and closed the research notes. Now we place the project into action. STEP TWO: TESTING The scene changes to that of a small empty arena. For a decent comparison, think the Hammerstein Ballroom located in New York City, home of the ECW shows. Standing in the front row is Sonny Silver, clad in a lab coat while jotting down notes. He needed a good base to work for his newest project and he’d be damned if he was going to be bothered by a bunch of stupid drunks. The prototype was gonna have to test his mic skills and his wrestling ability. He’d need to have it down PERFECT if he was to be the great protégé of Mr. Silver. Sonny reached into the empty seat next to him to procure a walkie-talkie. One of his students, Tim Spitfire, volunteered (See: Sonny beat him into submission, pinned him down, and farted in his face continuously until he complied with his commands.) his services to make sure the project worked to fruition. A skinny man with red shoulder-length hair and plain green tights stood in the ring, poised to go against the pet project of his trainer. “Send him out, guys! As a test, have him come out to something good.” Some stock Japanese music played. “NO, NO, NO!” Sonny shouted into the walkie. “Something that DOESN’T peg him out to be a stereotypical Jap with no charisma and generic black tights.” Some more music played. An instrumental of “Hot Dog” by Limp Bizkit. “WHAT IN THE NAME OF MR. SILVER AM I PAYING YOU FUCKS?!” A voice came out of the walkie-talkie. “You aren’t, Sonny.” “Shut up and give him something GOOD.” “Superstar II” by Saliva. “Eh, that’ll do, I guess. Out walked the prototype of ITO. Sneering to the mock crowd and listening to the sounds of pipe noise booing, The Cocky Osakan look-alike walks down to ringside and eyes the little punk before stepping over the ropes. Easily having half a foot over Tim Spitfire, he gets into the face of the skinny trainee who backs away in fear. Silver shouts some words of encouragement. “KILL THAT BUCK-TOOTHED FUCK! I DON’T KNOW WHY I EVER TOOK HIM ON AS A TRAINEE!” Timmy spoke up. “My dad paid you for the whole year up front.” “Oh… Right… Give him a mic to test his skills. THEN kill that buck-toothed fuck!” The young trainee gave ITO a mic, who was quick to snatch it from his hands. He stared at it for a moment while Sonny shouted instructions. “Okay, here’s the set-up, Mr. ITO. These New Yorkers are giving you crap. They’re jealous that they aren’t you in the ring and they’re jealous they aren’t paid to shitkick people for high income like you and I. Use that against them and verbally rip them a new one.” ITO raised the mic to his hands and laughed. “You stupid drunks. You stupid New Yorkers can give me crap, but the bottom line is that you’re just jealous; jealous that you aren’t in this ring right now and jealous that YOU guys aren’t paid to shitkick people for high income like me and the handsome man in the front row… I just used that against you and verbally ripped all of you a new one! HAHA!” He spikes the mic down – directly into poor Tim’s forehead. The feedback scratched over the PA and ITO went to work, pummeling the living Hell out of Spitfire with an elbow barrage. Timmy screamed out in pain as he was backed into the corner, bloody welts and horrible bruises quickly adorning his face. The young student started sobbing in between vicious blows and stomps to his stomach, something obviously different with the way that ITO was handling his business. “THESE ARE SUPPOSED TO BE FAKE WRESTING PUNCHES AND ELBOWS! AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!” >.> <.< “Yes,” Sonny said with shifty eyes. “Fake wrestling punches…” Just for that crack, ITO actually hit him HARDER. Each stomp delivered to the chest of Tim was robbing the kid of much-needed oxygen. Once the five or so minutes of stomping were finished, he picks up the near-lifeless form of Timmy and deadlifts him up, SPIKING him into the mat with the ITO’s Ill Awesomeness. The pin was academic, as ITO had just won his first match. Sonny stood up from his chair and raised his arms into the sky. “HERE IS YOUR WINNER AND THE FUTURE OF WRESTLING… KENJIRO ITO!” ITO raises a hand and stands triumphantly as he showed the invisible drunks and his scrawny opposition whose boss. This project was a success. The time for the final product was going to be nigh. Soon, construction would be complete and world domination… or something like it… would be his. Count on it. STEP THREE: THE FINAL PRODUCT MEANWHILE, AT THE LAB… “Who said that…?” Sonny questioned to the sexy narrator, who wasn’t going to respond because he had a fucking deadline to beat. When he would receive no answer, Sonny went back to typing hastily at his computer. He’d worked with the ITO prototype in the ring and the project was a success. His mic skills would be up to par with that of the competition and his mighty in-ring ruthlessness and efficiency were his key to dominance. He turned his attention back to the cadaver and looked at the equipment next to him. Robotic parts, a cybernetic eye piece for the right eye, an amped-up pair of legs. This was gonna be fucking awesome… the first wrestling cyborg outside of some shit James von Doom may come up with. Sonny grabbed the eyepiece and stared at it for a few seconds. With an evil grin and an even eviler laugh, he placed it back down on the table. “Young Kenjiro ITO: A wrestler barely alive… I could rebuild him. I have the technology. I can make him better than he was; better than he’s ever been. Better… stronger… faster… even more charismatic. He’ll be able to run at distances far greater than your average fat man going after the Ice Cream Truck. He’ll also be able to leap tall big man wrestlers in a single leap… good stuff.” Suddenly, the door opened behind him, revealing the form of a small, aging male with a completely bald head. Think Howie Mandel in twenty years. And only about five feet tall. The man tapped Sonny on the shoulder with an incredibly troubled look on his face. “Yes, Morty, what is it? You are interrupting very important research!” Morty MacDougal, Sonny’s trusted accountant, executed a heavy sigh before saying what he was about to say. “You know this incredibly awful parody that you have going on here and this cockamamie plan you have to turn this young Japanese man into a cyborg?” “Yeah, delusions of grandeur, what about it?” The tiny banker sighed again. “Well, Sonny, how should I put this to you… to actually go THROUGH with this kind of a project would not only be morally wrong, but it would be outright playing God with another man’s life, so I would suggest that you cease and desist this action immediately.” The Faux-Chairman of PRIME stared at Morty for a second as if he magically sprouted wings and flew through the ceiling. “No fucking way, Morty. This is awesome and my protégé’s gonna be something great.” He continued to look worried, so he put it in terms Sonny would understand. “Okay, that was plan A since I didn’t want to give you a coronary… this idea is financially… what’s the word I’m looking for… completely fucking retarded, sir. You don’t have six million dollars to spend.” That sound you just heard? Sonny’s jaw dropping to the floor. Once he pulled it back in, he folded his arms and looked at the body under the blanket. “Well, I suppose you’re right, Morty. I don’t really want to go that nuts with the money. What DO we have to contribute to this project?” “What’s in your pocket, sir?” Silver reached into his wallet and obtained only two things: 53 cents, an unused condom, and what appeared to be a string of dental floss. “I got this. You got anything?” “I’m afraid my paycheck has gone to Christmas this year, sir.” “Well… I SUPPOSE we could get him this far on money alone…” -------------------------------- The Puroresu Tiger (not of this "young lion" scheisse; that's just another term he equates with some charisma-less hack wearing black tights and using a dropkick as a finisher) is gushing from the nose.
