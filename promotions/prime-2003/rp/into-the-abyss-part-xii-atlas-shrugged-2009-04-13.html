Into The Abyss: Part XII - Atlas Shrugged

13 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13-04-09 You're the 7' Murder Show... He writhed on the bed, tossing the sheets aside and wheezing as sweat saturated him, glistening in the moonlight peeking through the half-closed curtains. A natural disaster that's claimed so many lives over the years... Gloria lay next to him, staring at the ceiling, sighing with worry. She had never seen him like this, always having been in a separate room such as their stay at Wyatt Manor. She understood the pain he was going through. Forget the Meanstreaks, Propagandas and John Mammoths. Hessian has outlived them all... Sitting up, she wiped her forehead. His soaring temperature had cooked her beneath the duvet and she too was sweating. The bedside clock. 04:27, he'd been tossing and turning for a good hour, and the thought of him suffering like this nearly every night filled her with guilt, that she should have the privilege of Buprenorphine and he should be left to rot on his own. That name echoes through the ages. It is barbarian, the true call of the wild... Von Kelsig rolled over, his massive paw striking the pillow where Gloria had lay moments before. She held a hand to her mouth to stifle the gasp, and running her hand through her mane got up out of bed and threw on a robe. Watching as he rolled over to her side, she sighed and retrieved the bed sheet from the floor. And yet...what lies here is not a Monster? Is this not the scourge of Man? No... Gently retrieving her pillow she took it with the blanket and dragged her feet across the carpet, sluggishly barging into the en-suite. Tossing the pillow into the bath she stepped in and wrapped herself in the blanket, snuggling into the cold ceramic. Here lies the broken hull of the great Titanic. The almighty Colossus has been reduced to rubble... She bedded down and drifted off, paying no mind to Von Kelsig's struggle as he grabbed the mattress and kicked, trying in vain to beat away the pain eating at his flesh. You are no beast, no longer a Monster among Man. You are Man. Nothing more, nothing less... ---- 7th January, 2007 "What're you doing in there?" Gloria rubbed her eyes and peered up. Von Kelsig was poised at the toilet with a hand down his shorts ready to expose and relieve himself when he noticed the covers and tuft of white hair poking out. Removing his hand he sat down on the toilet and rubbed his eyes with his forearm. "Cooling off, it was like sleeping in an oven last night." She yawned, stretching in the bath. "Sorry about that." Von Kelsig looked away, embarrassed. "It's OK, I understand what you're going through. You sure you wanna do this by yourself? It'd be so much easier if you just got proper help, heck I'd go with you if it made it easier." She sat up and leaned on the rim of the bath. He thought about the pain he endured, the restless nights followed by days of desperation. His muscles ached and his bowels turned like a cement mixer full of razorblades. Besides the fact he was no drug addict, the hunger that troubled him the most. Nothing quelled the pain; no meal or beverage or even physical pleasure such as a skin on skin thing with Gloria, at least in his mind. The greatest relief was imagining that dirty fluid coursing through his veins and that angered him. Knowing that that was the only solution besides more suffering helped in part to get him through the day, and he forced all those thoughts into the back of his mind, using the pain to push towards whatever the goal of the day was. "I don't know, Gloria. If I go into rehab it goes on my record, which gets checked when I apply for a job. How am I supposed to get work if that's tainted?" He held his head in his hands, staring at the floor closing his troubled eyes as Gloria pondered. "Gotta do something. I never wanna see the day when you're reduced to a scavenging junkie looking for a hit just to stop the pain......" Gloria mumbled dryly, pulling herself out of the bath and hauling the duvet behind. "It gets that bad?" Von Kelsig queried, standing up. "Trust me if they stuck Christ and left him on his ass he'd be scrounging for a hit too..." She quipped. "I'm bigger than Jesus!" He called as she closed the bathroom door over. "Sure Lennon, just do your business in there and let's get going!" She replied. Flipping the lid up, Von Kelsig relieved himself. The Beatles reference made him smirk. "Take a lot more to bring me down than this shit..." ---- "I had a real bad dream last night, Gloria..." Von Kelsig piped up, breaking the silence in the back of the cab. After leaving the motel, their next stop was Boston and Von Kelsig had been deliberating on whether to tell her about the dream. "Oh? What about?" She asked, turning away from the view of approaching skyscrapers to face him. "Reuben." Von Kelsig began. "He found us and...well...did a number on us...kinda rattled me a little." "Don't worry about that schmuck. We'll be rid of him once we're off the radar, we just need to find a place where he won't be operating." She assured, placing a hand on his shoulder. "Wherever that may be..." Von Kelsig stared off towards the city limits as the cab neared Boston. "Well..." Gloria began. "He's got Detroit, New York, Miami, Los Angeles...Dallas...a small set-up in Chicago." She studied him with those beautiful eyes. "That's only what I remember. He could have branched further afield by now." "What about Vegas? Surely he'd set up there?" Von Kelsig asked, sitting up as the driver pulled back the plexiglass window. "Where you wanna go?" "Take us to the Greyhound station." Von Kelsig called. "Atlantic Avenue it is, bub." The cabbie replied, closing the window and veering into the next lane on the freeway. "Reuben wouldn't go near Vegas, he's a one-vice man. If clients have the luxury of drugs and gambling there's a good chance he won't get his money. There's an element of gambling in most cities but Vegas is Vegas y'know? Plus it's a hot-bed anyway, Blanco's trying to break into the market in the U.S. He isn't looking to dominate...yet..." Gloria concluded. "Maybe we should head to Nevada, not Vegas, somewhere out of the way like...Lake Tahoe. Desert's a good place to lose yourself...how's that sound?" Von Kelsig asked, cocking an eyebrow. "Sounds like a plan. But what if-..." "Ah...now now." Von Kelsig cut her off. "If ifs and buts were candy and nuts.......uhh, we'd be fat. Just focus on getting to where we're going, hm?" He stroked her cheek and his eyes lit up as they drove through Boston towards the bus station. ---- 9th January, 2007 Great blood-red grasping vines wound up from the abyss swirl, stuck with razorblades that sliced the flesh as they intertwined on their ascent. Barbed wire tendrils tore at the walls of darkness, tearing them open and releasing a rotted corpse-like stench, while thick bile oozed from the wounds. He saw his feet dangling over the hole as the bleeding tentacles snaked closer. As they clawed through the air at him he could see their tips were dripping with a foul brown fluid, leaking from steel needles that glinted in the light from above. Try as he may he couldn't move as the vines licked at his feet and he felt the razor slicing his skin. His gut twisted agonizingly at the same time a mass of vines shot upwards, threatening to eviscerate him. With another contortion the limbs flicked up and latched onto his legs, pulling him off his invisible perch into the darkness. He yelled in horror, when suddenly a hand shot down from above and grabbed him. Looking up he squinted as a bright light shone down on the hellish creature made of steel flora, trying to pull him to the depths. With a great tug that nearly broke him in half at the waist, he felt the vines break loose and plummet deep below. He felt at peace suddenly as he was pulled back to safety... ...Until a single tendril shot back up, slashing at the wrist which held him and cutting through it like butter. All at once he felt himself falling hard into a swarm of vines saturated in that foul liquid. A sharp pain suddenly stuck him in his rear... "AARGH!!" Von Kelsig cried out and lurched to the side, slamming his head against a pane of glass. "What's wrong?!" Gloria was by his side, holding his shaking hand in her dainty grip. "I just had the weirdest goddamn dream..." He began, rubbing his head and sitting up. Looking out the window he saw a large billboard propped up in one of the many fields they sped past reading 'Rest Stop 5 Miles'. "Another? What about?" She asked, snuggling into him. "No idea...it was like I was falling into a big briar patch or something..." He croaked, clearing his throat and stretching. "Where are we?" He groaned. "We left Salt Lake City a couple hours ago. We'll be in Nevada in-..." "Move! Quickly!" Von Kelsig jumped to his feet and bolted for the bathroom. Gloria shifted quickly as he threw himself into the aisle, rushing into the toilet and slamming the door shut. She plopped back down in her seat, her eyes welling up as she stared out the window. The journey had been spent watching him constantly rushing to that damn toilet. The trip from Boston to New York had been peaceful, in fact they had spent it cuddling and sleeping off the restless night before. They barely spoke from New York to St. Louis after the second transfer. Other passengers who didn't notice anything suspicious began to wonder when Von Kelsig made his fourth trip to the bathroom in as many hours. Every time he emerged his eyes were a little further sunken and his skin a whiter shade of pale. The more miles they put behind them the more Von Kelsig suffered. The confined space of the Greyhound bus was certainly no sanctuary, and with only one bathroom for some twenty passengers he was humiliated every time he had to rush away to stop from vomiting on Gloria or worse, shitting himself in a public space. They blamed it on travel sickness, and after their next transfer at Denver he felt a little more at peace when the urges crept up on him. Still, the more Gloria saw the more she knew that the struggle was too great to fight on his own. With the rest stop looming she bit her lip and made a tough decision as Von Kelsig emerged from the bathroom, his lips a deathly shade of blue and his eyes bloodshot and a sickly shade of green. He slumped down into the seat as the bus pulled off the road and trundled along the tarmac towards a parking lot. Gazing out the window, Von Kelsig saw a gas station and a mini mall across the busy lot. "I could use fresh air..." He sighed, scratching his beard and rising back up out of his seat along with Gloria and everyone else. "It's one-forty, we'll be moving out again in twenty minutes folks. You know the drill." The driver commanded, opening the doors and letting the passengers filter out onto the black tarmac. Gloria and Von Kelsig shuffled in their midst, sidestepping where there was space until they were walking off on their own towards the mini mall. Gulping in the cool Utah air, Von Kelsig groaned and stretched his limbs as he walked, assuming a shambling style of goose step as he walked on. Gloria smiled softly as the fresh atmosphere seemed to rejuvenate him. "Hungry?" He asked, extending a hand out to her. "Yeah...how you feeling?" She asked cautiously, taking him by the hand as they approached the mall. "I'm OK. Don't worry, it's not like I'm going to go off and......and chase a hit..." He whispered to her as a family skittered past, drawn to the warm glow of a McDonalds sign within. "Course I'll worry! You don't deserve this and it won't kill you to get professional help!" She hit back, nudging him as they entered the mall and scanned the different shops and restaurants. "I don't NEED any help!" Von Kelsig snapped. His tone of voice startled her. He wanted to say something but the look in her eyes stunned him into silence. He never imagined a situation that would make him snap at her like that, but as the pain throbbed like nagging reminder he knew it was down to that godforsaken chemical. Even weeks after he was last injected he was still afflicted. "...I'm sorry Gloria, I didn't mean to-..." "No. Like you said, you've got a handle on it. I'm going to the bathroom, meet me outside McDonalds..." She muttered, taking off and leaving Von Kelsig filled with a hurting he had never experienced before. Disappointment. "I'm sorry..." He wheezed, skulking off towards McDonalds. Storming off, Gloria glanced back after a moment to see Von Kelsig heading off with his head bowed. A pang of guilt came over her, and as he disappeared inside she turned tail and bolted for the payphones hanging nearby. She scrambled for change in her pocket and inserted nearly a dollar before punching in a number. A moment passed before the pretentious tone like someone holding their nose filled her ear. "Information." "Yeah, I need the number for rehab clinics in or around Lake Tahoe, Nevada..." Gloria replied, clutching the receiver tightly in both hands and leaning over the phone. Her heart sank. ---- 10th January, 2007 The Greyhound reached Reno in the early hours, the long haul along I80 was over at last. After Von Kelsig apologized to Gloria, she accepted his remorse and spent the remainder of the trip relatively quiet while he spent all his time cursing the affliction and going back and forth to the toilet. Twenty miles outside of Reno, Gloria had given him the last of her Bupe which he guzzled happily. An hour later he felt no pain whatsoever, and though she felt the old pains creeping back gradually she sought solace in the fact he was OK. From the bus they jumped into a cab and started off for South Lake Tahoe, at Gloria's request. "Why the south? Why not west? It's that little bit further, you know..." Von Kelsig asked curiously. "It'd be a nice little place to settle down while we get our heads straight." A complete lie told through a smile so innocent it would straighten out a Catholic priest. "So be it." Von Kelsig replied, unaware. By the time the taxi rolled into South Lake Tahoe that evening, the tab had exceeded a hundred dollars and Gloria's system had reached its limits. "You got a place in mind?" He nudged her playfully as she slouched in her seat and clutched her gut. "Hm? Oh...nice little place, called Vista Bay." "Quaint." He replied nonchalantly as the cab turned at the corner of Roger Avenue into a gravel courtyard surrounded by tall bare trees and a little wooden fence. The cab parked up in front of the building. "That'll be one-twenty-nine-fifty." The driver called. Tossing him a hundred and fifty, Von Kelsig and Gloria stepped out. He retrieved their duffel bag and they took off for what Von Kelsig assumed was a quiet little hotel as the cab sped off. "Lovely. We'll rest up then have a wander tomorrow." He smiled, scanning the woods beyond the yard. "Sure." Gloria muttered, clutching her stomach as the front door to the 'hotel' opened and a man and woman stepped out, dressed in a black suit and blue polkadot dress respectively. "Welcome folks, you must be Gloria and Angelus!" The man called. "Wow, great service." Von Kelsig grinned. "Heh...yeah...but...Angelus, listen..." She struggled through the throbbing pain as they stepped onto the porch. "I'm John and this is Frances. We run Vista Bay, pleasure to meet you!" John welcomed Von Kelsig with a hearty handshake, while Frances gave Gloria a welcome hug. "Don't worry guys, we know it can be a little scary but we'll get through this together!" Frances said with a smile so bright it betrayed the dying sun. "I'm sorry?" Von Kelsig asked, dropping his bag and glancing at Gloria. "Angelus I was going to tell you but-..." "Come in guys. Once you're settled we can discuss the terms of your stay more thoroughly!" John smiled, grabbing the duffel bag and struggling with its weight. "Sure are a strong fellow, Angelus!" He chuckled. "What is this place?" Von Kelsig asked Gloria as they stepped inside. It hit him all at once. The staff swanning around, the reception area plastered with posters and pamphlets. John and Frances led them down a hallway past a few people who had the same look in their eyes that Von Kelsig recognized from the man in the mirror. "Gloria...what did you..." He uttered as they were escorted to their rooms. "I'm sorry Angelus. We need to do this." Gloria replied, bleary-eyed. "Welcome to rehab, we're going to get better. Together!" Von Kelsig zoned out. Voices muffled and vision blurred as he stared at Gloria in disbelief, her gorgeous big eyes closing tight as she burst into tears and grabbed onto him tightly. "Rehab...?" His heart sank as Gloria's had and his hopes dashed in a heartbeat. Gloria hung on, hugging him tighter as she cried while John and Frances tried to calm her down.
