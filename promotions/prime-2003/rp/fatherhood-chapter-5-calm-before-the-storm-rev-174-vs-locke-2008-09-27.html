Fatherhood - Chapter 5 - Calm Before the Storm (ReV 174 vs. Locke)

27 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 27, 2008 SATURDAY SEPTEMBER 27, 2008 A cooling breeze blew across the freshly-cut front lawn of the Sivec household, and Bryan Dawkins slowly limped toward the front door of the house. Dawkins was in bad shape. His right leg had been mangled, he thought his right shoulder may have been dislocated, and it was very likely that Bryan had some broken ribs. Honestly, it looked as if Dawkins had been in a car wreck, not a wrestling match. However, Bryan wasn’t letting that stop him from taking his weekly flight back to Hawaii to check on Nikkie. After undergoing tests in a hospital in Hartford, Connecticut all day Thursday, he finally caught a five and a half hour flight on Friday morning that took him from Hartford to Phoenix, Arizona. Bryan spent nearly two hours milling around the desert airport, grabbing something to eat and trying to stay out of the public eye before catching his second flight, a six and a half hour trip which took him from Phoenix to Honolulu, Hawaii. From there, it was a simple one-hour flight from Honolulu to Hilo, where he caught a taxi and stayed at his own home for once, something he hadn’t done in months. His parents were happy to see him, but he was only there for the night; he was going to spend the next day with Nikkie because the next few weeks were going to be critical for both of them. Dawkins finally made it into the house after a few moments, and Pam was there to greet him with open arms. She embraced him with a warm hug, being careful not to aggravate any of the injuries he had sustained earlier that week. Although hindered by the pain, Dawkins wasn’t feeling it so much, mostly due to the couple Vicodin he had popped a short time before arriving at the house. Pam let go of her future son-in-law and led him into the kitchen, where she poured him a tall glass of pineapple-orange juice and got herself a glass of water. “How you doing, honey? You look terrible.” Dawkins cracked his normal smile, not letting his unfortunate state rain on anyone else’s parade. Bryan just wasn’t like that. “I’m doin’ fine. A few bumps and bruises here and there, but I’ll get by.” A few bumps and bruises? That statement could probably be listed in the ‘Top 10 Understatements of the Year’ list for 2008. Dawkins had been beaten to a pulp by both Devin Shakur and Rhett Locke a couple of days back, but they couldn’t beat his cheery spirit out of him. “You always have, Bryan. And I’m sure you’ll have no problem getting by this time, either. You always find a way, honey.” Pam smiled and sipped at her water, watching her future son-in-law intently. The way he gingerly reached for his drink with his right arm frightened her, and his labored breathing didn’t help her nerves a whole lot. At one point, Dawkins caught her observing him, and tried to hide his struggle by flashing his pearly whites. Pam finally decided to break the silence after a few moments. “So how’s it feel to know you’re so close to becoming a father?” The new topic seemed to inject life into Bryan, who almost seemed to be in an injury-induced trance at times. He straightened himself up and seemed to speak with much more energy and charisma. “Oh, it’s exciting. It’s all I’m thinkin’ about anymore. Even when I’m out in the ring, doin’ my job, it’s like I’m doing my best ‘cuz I know it’s gonna help to give Nikkie and our son, your grandchild, a good life.” This was the Bryan that Pam knew and grew to love like a son. Bryan was smiling and cheery, but suddenly a hint of fear was present in his face. “…but it’s also kinda scary, too. I mean, I go out in front of thousands and thousands of people week-in and week-out, throwing my body around like I’m some sorta crash-test dummy, and it doesn’t faze me one bit, ya know? But put me in a room and say ‘Here, this is your son and wife. Take care of them and make sure they live the best life they can under you,’ and it’s scary. It’s alotta responsibility, and sometimes I dunno if I can do that.” Listening closely, Pam looked at Bryan, who was a nervous wreck now. This whole ordeal was doing a number on him emotionally and physically, and it was starting to become apparent. He was worrying about everything, when he only had to take things one step at a time. “I just don’t know. I love your daughter, Pam…I have for a really long time, and I’m completely sure of that. And I knew I wanted to have kids some day, but I wasn’t sure that I wanted them like…right now, ya know?” Pam nodded. “And I’m on the road nearly every week of the year with PRIME, and can only be home for a day or two in a row, max. I guess what I’m afraid of is that I can’t provide the kind of life for Nikkie and our son that I know they need and more importantly, deserve. I don’t wanna make them suffer because I’m doing somethin’ wrong.” Pam set down her glass of water and stood up, approaching Bryan. She held out her arms and pulled him in with another hug, this time reassuring him that everything was going to eventually turn out alright. “Bryan, you need to stop worrying. If you weren’t the guy that could provide my daughter with the kind of life she deserves, she wouldn’t have picked you. And just because you guys kinda skipped some steps in the relationship process doesn’t mean you guys are doing anything wrong. God just meant for you guys to take your lives in a different path than is ‘normal’ for most people. Just remember that in the end, things always happen for a reason.” Just as Pam was finishing up her pep-talk of sorts to Bryan, a familiar voice joined the conversation. ”Hey, get your hands off of my fiancée!” An eight-month-pregnant Nikkie stood in the doorway to the kitchen, her arm in a sling and resting on her oh-so-pregnant stomach. She made her way into the kitchen, kissing her mother on the cheek, and then giving Bryan the same greeting, only on the lips. She motioned with her good arm toward the door. “Ready to go, hun?” * * * THE SEASIDE RESTAURANT HILO, HAWAII After a short drive, Nikkie and Bryan arrived at one of the top restaurants in all of Hilo, the Seaside Restaurant and Aqua Farm. Bryan, being a perfect gentleman, or gentlebruh, if you will, held the door open with his good arm for his fiancée and mother of his unborn child. Bryan slowly followed his love, approaching the greeter, a young native of the island, maybe eighteen or nineteen years old, and smiled. “Can I help you?” “Yeah, I have a reservation for two for Dawkins.” Upon hearing the name, the father and oldest son of a middle-aged couple with two sons shot a look at Bryan, wondering if that was the Bryan Dawkins. I mean, what were the odds? “Oh, yes. Bryan? If you can wait just a moment, I’ll go make sure your table is ready. Feel free to take a seat right here and I’ll be back in a second.” With that, the young greeter swiftly walked into the next room to check on the status of their table. Bryan and Nikkie took a seat on a padded bench situated right across from the middle-aged couple. The father and son stared at the Flyin’ Hawaiian, mesmerized by the fact that they were going to dine in the same vicinity as their state’s wrestling hero. Bryan happened to catch them staring, and flashed a smile and a wink at the two, causing the father and son to smile back and wave. “Bryan? Your table is ready. If you’ll follow me right this way, I’ll escort you there.” Nikkie got up, slowly followed by Bryan, and exited into the other room. Before leaving, Bryan turned and flashed the “hang loose” sign to the father and son, causing their smiles to become even larger. The two exchanged laughs and began to talk about their encounter with their wrestling hero. The young greeter sat the two down at a small table in the middle of the main room of the restaurant and introduced them to their waitress for the night. She informed them of the restaurant’s specials for the evening, and took their drink orders. As she left, the two started to catch up. “So hun, what’ve you been up to lately?” “Oh, nothing, just shopping for clothes and stuff for the baby. You know, one of these times, you’re gonna have to make it home to shop with me sometime. I think I can only take so much more of my mother controlling the shopping cart at Babies R Us.” The two shared a laugh and continued catching up. “Yeah, I guess I’m gonna have to come shoppin’ for the little bruh. I wonder if they have anything shaped like a pineapple or with pineapples on it.” Nikkie rolled her eyes as their drinks were delivered to them. “What? I was serious!” “I know, hun…I know.” After ordering their entrées for the evening, which consisted of Paniolo PRIME Rib (no pun intended) for Bryan and a Grilled Teri Ahi Salad for Nikkie, the conversation started to get a little more serious. “Bryan, are you doing alright? I was worried when I saw what happened to you this week.” Dawkins wasn’t about to tell her that he had resorted to painkillers earlier in the day to get through the pain and discomfort, but he wasn’t about to tell her that since his encounters with Locke and Shakur, life had been a walk in the park, either. “Eh, I’m hurtin’, but I’ll get by.” I’ll get by. It seemed to be Dawkins’ motto anymore. The Flyin’ Hawaiian decided to flip the tables and start asking his questions. The important ones. “So are you ready to have our boy?” Nikkie smiled, and Bryan grasped her hands. “Yeah, it’s about time. I’m tired of carryin’ this little guy around with me. He’s not exactly light anymore, Bryan. And come to think of it, he doesn’t really like to sit still much anymore, either. Little bruh’s always kickin’ and pokin’ around in there. I can only imagine what he’s gonna be like when he gets out.” Just the mention of Dawkins’ unborn sun made the Flyin’ Hawaiian beam with joy…but also struck a certain amount of fear in him as well; but he wasn’t about to let Nikkie see that fear. He was going to be the strong one, and to this point in the situation, in his mind, he had certainly succeeded. “Yeah, it’s gonna be a good day, hun.” Bryan smiled and caressed Nikkie’s hands while still talking. “I know I haven’t been around much, but—“ Nikkie cut him off mid-sentence. “Bryan, it’s okay. I’ve been doing fine, just like I said I would be when we found out about this. There’s no need for you to apologize for doing your job and taking care of us. You’re out making the money for this family that’s going to grow by one in a matter of a month or so.” She leaned over and pecked him on the lips. “You’re making me so proud, going out there every week and giving it your all, even though you know you’ve got a lot of things going on here at home. And the great thing is that I know that when the time comes, you’re going to be there when the baby and I need you.” The speech gave Dawkins a chill down his spine. He shook it off and took a sip of his drink. “No, Nikkie, I was going to say that we have some decisions to make about the baby.” Nikkie’s reply was short and sweet. “Oh. Alright.” She smiled happily and sipped at her Diet Pepsi. Bryan swallowed his water and locked eyes with his fiancée. “Have you been thinking about any names for him?” “Yes, but I think we should hold off on this.” Dawkins looked confused. He understood that when people didn’t know the sex of the baby, it was better to just hold off and name the baby spontaneously, because otherwise you might get too attached to a name for a baby of one gender, and you get the other, messing up the entire name deciding process. “Oh?” “Yeah, I think we should just wait, because I really believe that when we see him for the first time, we’ll just know what his name is. Plus, since when do we plan anything?” Nikkie pointed toward her stomach. “This certainly wasn’t planned.” Although it was meant in more of a joking nature, Nikkie was right. After all, they had been through a lot, and most of it was far from being premeditated or planned for. So why should they change their ways and plan the name of their baby beforehand. Like Nikkie said—they’ll just know. Bryan smiled and agreed. “Okay then, next question. I know this is kinda like what I just asked you, but it’s a little different. How about godparents? I know mine were a big part of my life, and I want our little bruh’s godparents to be a big part of his life, too.” The expression on Nikkie’s face was a clear indication that she hadn’t given much thought to this. Bryan, however, had already picked out his boy’s godfather WEEKS ago. Since Dawkins made his debut in PRIME, Chandler Tsonda had taken him under his wing and helped him along. And now that Bryan was taking another important step in his life, he wanted Tsonda to be a big part of it as well. “You know, Bryan…I haven’t really thought about this, but I agree that it’s gonna be an important part of his life to have good godparents. Did you have anyone in mind?” Nikkie paused, and re-stated her question. “Wait, since you asked the question, you obviously have someone in mind…so spill it, hun.” Bryan had already asked Tsonda to be the godfather, but hadn’t told Nikkie yet. It was time to let the cat outta the bag, so-to-say. “You know Chandler?” Nikkie slowly nodded. “Tsonda?” “Yep.” Bryan smiled after confirming his choice. He was proud to have picked Uncle Chan to be a big part in the kid’s life, and Dawkins deeply hoped that Nikkie thought the same thing too. “Yeah, I think it’s a good choice. He’s close to you, seems pretty loyal, and from what I saw a couple of weeks ago, has a pretty nice body, too…” Nikkie’s voice trailed off into giggling, and Bryan began to laugh with her, before the last comment Nikkie made finally registered in his brain. Bryan’s laughter stopped, and he sported a look of confusion, making Nikkie laugh even harder, realizing that Dawkins just figured out what she said. Just then, their waiter arrived at the table with their food, prompting Bryan to ask one final question before dinner… “…he has what?”
