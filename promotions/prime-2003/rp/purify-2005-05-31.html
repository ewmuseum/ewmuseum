You train harder. Prepare for larger obstacles. Not for false goals. Not for false idols. But for the purity. To restore what you love so very much. And then, you begin... Again. *** 'Oh SHIT! The sky was black with an ambient lavender hue beginning to swallow whole the sky as the sun came. Soon, the sun would come and cover the valley, bathing the landscape in warmth. But the night sky still hung on for as long is it could, the somewhat frigid conditions of the Mojave desert lingering as night and day fought their twice daily epic battle. Beyond him laid the sand dunes that were ever present all across Mojave. Most people believe that the sand and the dunes signify a lack of life. That this place with the terrible heat variances and the lack of plentiful green vegetation underscored this as a dead zone. But is that not arrogance? Is that not self importance? These hills, with their immaculate ripples, are they not as beautiful as the Great Plains? Were the distant mountain peaks not alluring? Was the sublime tone in the skies not breathtaking? Is it not exquisite in the solidarity one feels in this setting? Digging his toes into the sand, he could not find the words to describe the utter beauty of the surroundings. The mountain peaks were far in the distance, keeping society out of reach. He could focus on his training here, only having to contend with these rolling sand dunes. Nobody could interrupt him here. Here, he was far away from the man made rules and the man made laws. He was away from their expectations. Here, he could perfect himself. Perfect his craft. He was like a monk, away from society to study and perfect himself for spiritual enlightenment. However, Brandon Youngblood did not seek enlightenment from any of the amount of deities that lay calm to the skies. Enlightenment was wrestling to him. He took this sort of time to perfect himself, to condition his body to the point where he could be considered one of the best conditioned athletes in all of sports. With his ranch home in the distance behind him, he could run through the sand dunes until the break of dawn, then begin his stretching exercises taught to him by one Mike Randalls on a random Squared Circle road trip. Then, it would be tape study of his opponent this week along with a hearty breakfast and fluids to replenish the amount he would surely lose in the desert heat. This week was different then any other in Brandon's career, however. As he pumped his legs forward through the icy sand, he could only feel a mixture of emotions. Anger. Fear. They collected inside his stomach and did not let loose their grip. This week, he needed to be perfect. He had to train harder than ever before. He had to do this. Brandon could not imagine what would happen if he were to fail at this chance. Before him was the demon's head, almost presented in a manner of 'what can you do to me?' He pumped his legs and arms forward, breathing in much needed oxygen as sweat began to pour from his entire body. The sun had begun to win the battle, the lavender hue turning into an ambient orange. It rose above the endless landscape of sand dunes and mountains, bringing with it the heat that gives no respite. Brandon continued running, the sand beneath his bare feet growing warmer and warmer with each step forward. He began to full bore sprint, his legs kicking up generous portions of sand, the sun reflecting off this glistening skin. He wore only black dojo pants with a white drawstring on the outside, the drawstring swaying with each movement. His expressionless face began to become more stressed, followed by sharp inhales and exhales of air. But he continued to sprint as fast as he could, lasting as long as it took for the sun to go from being a small dark orange hue on the horizon to a large yellow ball in the sky, beating upon the landscape with tremendous heat. In that moment, he collapsed to his knees, looking up to the sky as he tried to collect himself. No matter what he did, he could only think about Karina Wolfenden. She had always been close in his thoughts thanks to the realization of her true nature. While she was painted as something more, her nature was as twisted evil itself. But, she was the angel. The model of what people should be. The fans made that abundantly clear with their cheers for her. They made it clear by booing anyone who stood in her path. But her arrogance, her vanity, was it not part of the reason she fought meaningless war after meaningless war, yet still seem pure? Was it not her beauty and her grace that lulled people into serenity only for her serpent's tongue to lash them as if it were a whip across the bare back? To be exemplary is to care for others, but did she not care merely for herself? The Devil hath power to assume a pleasing shape. Was it really him that was the black to her white? Was it really a war against a principle, or rather a pestilence that needed to be addressed? Brandon did it the right way. He trained and shrugged off every single fork in the road. He overcame his personal issues. He considered himself a man of principle. He had his own doctrine of how life should be led. He thought that one must put their heart and soul...NAY...their essence into wrestling should they become one with the arena and the canvas with four ring posts. Was that not what was asked of everyone? Not that they necessarily succeed, but to give everything of themselves to what they do? To not do so is to taint yourself and taint what you are doing. To have success without giving everything, is that not almost criminal? She did not give of herself to the sport of wrestling. That is what Brandon wanted. For her to realize her true potential. Instead, she brushes the sport off as if it is nothing. She takes the accolades as if they are meaningless. Does she not understand how many men have fought for what she has been allowed to take? Does she not know the toils of those before and after her? Does she not know of those that have died wrestling trying to do it the right way? The only answer he could surmise is that she did not know. And she did not care to know. It was only right to make someone pay for their blissful ignorance. He was exhausted, yet picked himself up.
