Demons

2 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/02/06 It all revolves around a pain that most people cannot know. It surrounds itself with a resolve that will never resolve. It feeds itself with doubts and self loathing and pity for the idiots in the world who think that they have figured something out. These things that I feel today, tonight, hell it seems like one long nightmare, can only be summarized by trying to block out the different versions of myself long enough to hear a singular form speak to me. I can’t ever make out what they tell me, really, just that I am scared. Scared of what? I have really waited my entire life to find that out, but tonight it seems like I can finally see what is happening to me. I broke down and cried. I don’t cry. Rock Stars don’t cry. More importantly, “Electric” Eric Evans does NOT cry. You cant be a man in this world and allow your emotions to overtake you; at least that’s what the media and mass market America wants us to believe. Yeah, I felt my entire world slipping. Things I had known for over twenty years break apart with a single little phone call. The call itself was so insignificant in nature that I didn’t even bother to turn the ringer on my cell phone. It just vibrated silently, like it was telling me “Hey buddy, pick the phone up, it’s just a call from your mother.” I’ve received calls before from her when I was busy trying to work. She always had a problem timing her emotions properly. So I end up becoming the recipient of an errant call that changes my entire life. She doesn’t even know what she is doing to me, what she is causing in me, or what kind of pain she is giving me. Part of me wants to believe she doesn’t care. But I know she does. So I heard the voice through that static laced call, and my god damned piece of shit phone broke up a bit, but I heard that voice. I heard it loud and clear when it actually mattered. I have felt a lot of things in my life, some very good things, some that left me speechless in awe, some that not even the powers of speech itself could help me describe, had I the capacity to use them at the time. A long life of bountiful riches will do that for a guy. For the record, my definition of bountiful riches may not be the same as many other peoples. I define it as a love of life, family, friends, god etc. I have had these things my entire life. It was perfect, and I loved it. I looked at the people who had broken homes, and coming from a failed marriage when I was young, I thought to myself, man I really have it good. I have a dad who is not related by blood, but make no mistake, he is the only one I will ever truly call my father. God I love that man. He and I never got along until I figured out what actually made him tick. I always would take my mothers side, the side of impracticality and rash decisions. He always took a logical approach at things. I guess I could have been a little more sensible growing up…in retrospect. I stood there holding that phone to my ear, and I felt a creeping, burning sensation deep in my stomach. I was sickened. So much so that I left the locker rooms immediately and just headed straight to the one source of guidance I’ve always had. Jason Bass. I trust my compadre with my entire heart. He sees things that I, as a compulsive person, cannot at times. He feels things in a different way than I do. It’s not the sort of relationship that people can simply understand unless they have lived an entire life without a brother, and had to substitute someone for that void. I guess in that respect, I’ve always been alone, even though they never let me walk alone. My father, the only man in my life that I knew I could share the father and son bond with was slowly dying inside, and he didn’t even know it. The poor man who had worked 12 hours a day for 22 fucking years was away from home on a business trip. The man who had fed me, put a roof over my head, trained me from a boy to become a man was away on a god damned business trip. It wasn’t fair to him, and that’s what hurts. So I hung the phone up. What else am I supposed to do? I can’t scream in agony in the middle of a job. I can’t start smashing things just because I feel an absolute unchained fury inside of my heart, and every time that ol’ muscle pumps it delivers another shot of pain mixed with rage through my whole body. They all tell me not to be angry at the person, be angry at the act. I swear to fucking Christ if I saw HIM I would smash his god damned weasel head with a baseball bat. We aren’t talking the fake Sting foam bats either. I want to see the blood fly from his crushed face as I rip into it with a thick barreled Louisville Slugger. I want the bat to hit with so much force that it breaks in half, and by complete luck, impales that son of a bitch in the process. I’ve never felt like this before. I’ve never wanted someone to die…not like this. But I don’t want her to be unhappy, and I’m too damn smart to commit a cardinal sin against my lord. The only thing this leaves me with is that original pain, that original hurt, that original spinning darkness that I just can’t describe no matter how many pretty words I throw at it. Not even Karina or Angelo or Tchu could muster up enough delicate phrases or synonyms, or even antonyms to reveal what burns me alive. I don’t really care if Seek and Destroy beat The Alliance. I probably deserve a beating anyhow after what we did to Baltasar. It looks so fucking petty at this point to do what I did. How selfish could I have been to make a plan that ended a career? Really, The Alliance is right. We are self serving idiotic band mates who could give two shits about wrestling. I don’t think they are right in assuming Jason is the weak link though. That man has been there for me since I was a lot younger. We played a lot of shows and he keeps me on the level…most of the time. If there is a weak link in this group, I know what it is. I’ve never felt like this before. I hung my head, I hung my shoulders, I hung my heart. She betrayed me. No, that is selfish. She betrayed him. She betrayed my father, the only man I trusted with this title. The man I trusted over my biological father with this title. And the only thing I can think, as I sit here all alone, trying to put the jagged puzzle pieces together that make up the warped and twisted puzzle she has cast for us to figure out is this: Rock stars aren’t supposed to cry......Tell me then, why the fuck can’t I stop?
