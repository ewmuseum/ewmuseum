Step Aside or Get Put Aside

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Revolution 158 THE PROLOGUE This is a big, big match. This is exactly why Andrew Rossi came to PRIME. The roster has so many Superstars that every night is an opportunity to make his star shine just a little brighter. Also, it doesn’t hurt that SB doesn’t like Tony Rolo and wants a little revenge for the nutshot heard round the world at ReVolution 156. So as he’s sitting in his locker room, trying to get himself pumped up, SB can’t help but smile. He’s in just the situation he wants to be, and in a few hours he’ll take on the first ever PRIME Hall of Famer and get a chance to add one more marquee name to the list of former World Champions he’s pinned. What could be better than this? THE PROMO SB stands in a corridor, grinning like the Homecoming King with a pocketful of rubbers and a signed letter of intent to Notre Dame. His hair is PERFECT, teased to just the right balance of epic manliness and undeniable sexiness. The SB Oakley shades (red ones today) go right along with the Italian Flag bandanna tied around his right bicep. This t-shirt is sure to be a top seller soon – the front has an image of wrestling’s handsomest mug, and the back a shot of SB’s signature New York Nightmare with the Icon in mid-air. Simple, yet awesome. SB: People probably think I’m in over my head, don’t they? I mean, what the hell am I thinking starting with Tony Rolo? Wouldn’t it just be easier to start from the bottom and pick on fanooks like Posterboy or whatever the hell his name is? I mean, why in the world would I PICK Tony Rolo? After all, I did ask for this match before we even got out of Wichita. You see, I don’t care if you’re John Q. Nobody or Gene f’in Kiniski, you don’t kick the International Sex Symbol in the holiest of holies and not expect retaliation! SB starts walking, and we see that he’s at the arena, and it’s a bit earlier in the day then before judging by his demeanor. A few wrestlers are seen scuttling about down the hall, but their faces can’t be made out. SB: I gotta admit, I was embarrassed after my showing at 156. I got my ass pinned because I was too busy watching Natas and Dawkins try to show each other their cocks, and Douglas handed me my own. Then I catch my own motherfucking finishing room right in my fuckin’ grill-piece! And if that wasn’t bad enough this fuckin’ clod Tony Rolo bumps into me, I suppose still in delirium from swallowing Joey Troy’s near-poisonous load, and next thing you know he kicks me in the NUTS. IN MY FUCKIN’ NUTS? (nods) In my fuckin’ nuts. Seriously? I mean, I can see maybe taking a regular old cheap shot at me, but that was about the pussiest thing you could have done. I’d rather you fuckin’ poked me in the eye or tried to fuckin’ tickle me to death than strike the Triumvirate. SB: Just because you’re named after a delicious, caramel-filled treat doesn’t mean you can step to the Beautiful One. You see these hands? (SB holds his fists up) I’ve busted more motherfuckers than the FBI with these babies. You really want these bouncin’ off your dome, old-timer? What’d you come out of retirement for, to find out what it’s like to enjoy it the way that Hayabusa does? But hey, if it’s an ass-kicking you want it’s an ass-kicking you’ll get. SB always delivers, even on Sundays when Karl Malone takes off and misses free throws. SB: This isn’t a case of young vs. old, or Superstar vs. Unknown. I’m famous in my own right, and you’re only eight years older than I am anyway. But ask Brett Favre what a difference thirty-seven makes, and beyond. The game’s passed you up, Tony. I’m a step ahead of ya, I’m stronger than you, and as you yourself proved in Kansas – I’m tougher than you. I’ve been sitting back and watching guys like you cling to the spotlight for a long time, and you know what always ends up happening one way or the other? Rather than getting the torch passed to me, I reach out and take it from ‘em. This isn’t gonna be any different. You’re not going to pull off the wily veteran bullshit that broadcasters and ESPN wants you to believe exists. The better man always wins. And Tony? I’m better than you. SB mockingly and half-assedly salutes the camera, and gives a wink as it fades to black.
