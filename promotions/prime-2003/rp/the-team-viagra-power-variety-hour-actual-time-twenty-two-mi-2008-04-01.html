The Team VIAGRA Power Variety Hour : Actual Time Twenty Two Minutes

1 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 1st, 2008 Dear PRIME-ates, Fans of PRIME. It is with great reluctance that I tell you all, fuck you. A big fuck you. I'm leaving to go to Japan. Because my penis is HUGE over there. Yours Forever(I mean a week), High Flyer ** (Cut to High Flyer standing in front of a PRIME Banner, holding a bouquet of flowers.) HIGH FLYER: PLEASE! DARLING! TAKE ME BACK! (FADE OUT.)♦ NARRATOR: The following promotional material was brought to you by a grant from the Team VIAGRA April Fools Foundation, and from the proud Bumblebee Tuna Corporation, currently seeking a staff accountant for the next fiscal year. (FADE IN. Bumblebee Tuna Logo is on the screen. The bumble bee flies around. It looks oddly like stolen footage from Bee Movie.) *Jingle Singing* "Yum yum Bumble, Bumblebee Tuna I Love Bumblebee, Bumblebee Tuna Yum yum Bumble, Bumblebee Tuna I love a sandwich made with Bumblebee " (FADE OUT) (FADE IN. A seemingly large PRIME banner hangs. ZOOM OUT, and we reveal the banner to be a lone napkin, adorn with the PRIME logo. Tony Davis promptly takes the napkin to his nose and blows. He holds it out and exams his snot. Mary-Lynn Mayweather stops chewing on her hamburger, and contorts her eyes in this mixture of surprise and disgust. High Flyer looks on nonchalantly. He tosses down the small diner's menu.) TONY DAVIS: Why would you put your logo on a snot rag? (He is promptly ignored.) HIGH FLYER: I fucking hate April Fool's Day. Everyone wants to play the comedian. Y'know, when I superglue all the toilet seats at an arena, I have to pick up litter on the side of a high way for a bunch of weekends. But when you do it today? You're hilarious! It's a double standard. It's Dayism I tell you! And I will not abide! MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: I thought you'd love this. HIGH FLYER: It's like vampires during Halloween. Its such tacky shrill. I swear. This fucker uploaded a fake Lost Preair. It was fucking America's Next Top Model. I swore I wouldn't waste even a single kilobyte of illegal data transfer on the fucking CW's trite until they gave Veronica Mars a bloody movie. Not to mention that I swore that if I saw 90 minutes of reality television over the course of my life time I would blow my fucking brains out. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: How many minutes have you seen? HIGH FLYER: Let's just say I'm one episode of Survivor away from not surviving. (Mary-Lynn chomps on her hamburger. Tony Davis begins to play with the straw in his drink. Flyer rests his head on his shoulders, and sighs.) MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER(chewing): Hey, who you gonna vote for? (Tony Davis begins to blow softly into his drink, creating bubbles. Flyer shrugs his shoulders.) HIGH FLYER: (retort)Who are you gonna vote for? MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: Ron Paul. (Flyer and Davis both groan.) HIGH FLYER: That's like voting to waste your time. TONY DAVIS: I didn't know you were allowed to vote. (Mary-Lynn looks to her side, and narrows her eyebrows.) MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: I'm sorry. TONY DAVIS: It's just. We're so socially progressive. (Off their looks.) TONY DAVIS: Women's rights! YEAH! (Davis raises his hand for a high five that never comes. Mary-Lynn quickly bites on a French fry. Flyer leans back in his chair, realizing.) HIGH FLYER: So, it was pretty nuts when they passed that bill of suffrage five years ago. TONY DAVIS: Wow. Just think. Only six years ago a woman couldn't vote because they had a vagina. HIGH FLYER: Tony. Women's suffrage passed in the 60's. We haven't really advanced much since JFK got shot in his face. TONY DAVIS: Oh yeah. I went through a whole phase where I tried to figure out who shot JFK. HIGH FLYER: I remember. You kept stealing my time machine and setting it to 1963 without remembering to readjust the dial. *scoffs* I still think it was you who did the deed. TONY DAVIS: Hey. You can't prove anything about that faulty trigger. (Mary-Lynn sits there slack-jawed. Tony turns and raises his hand.) TONY DAVIS: *nonchalant* Check please! (FADE OUT. Here is where the TEAM VIAGRA theme song and opening montage music would play, if Team VIAGRA had a theme song. Wait. They do? Oh yes. That song that plays when they walk. I remember. Yes, but you see, we have lazy editors. And so, you just get an image, splashed on the screen which reads... "The Team VIAGRA Power Variety Hour : Actual Time Twenty Two Minutes." FADE IN. High Flyer, Tony Davis, and Mary-Lynn Mayweather all sit around Flyer's living room. Each has a cold refreshing alcoholic beverage in their hands. It is four in the afternoon. Clearly, they have nothing to do tonight.) HIGH FLYER: You think I could fuck Ellen Page? (Mary-Lynn looks disgusted.) HIGH FLYER: What, are you friends? MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: No... it's just... it's like I'm not even here. HIGH FLYER: You're there. You're obviously here. I mean, you're corporeal. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: And you use words no one else uses like corporeal! HIGH FLYER: HEY! I'm not the one with a tacky word a day calendar. TONY DAVIS: Uh... is it your time of the month? (Mary-Lynn turns, burning red. She steps just millimeters from Davis' face. Yeah. It's so close I went metric.) MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: IS THAT ALL YOU REMEMBER ABOUT THE FEMALE SPECIES? TONY DAVIS: Right now it is. It's all I can think about. I mean, you're so close, so very close to me right now. Like, extra close. Maybe too close. It's getting hard to breathe. Does anyone else feel light headed? Like you could fall unconscious at any mo- (Tony Davis slumps over in his chair. Mary-Lynn crosses her arms, obviously not fooled. Seconds later, Davis begins to snore to cover the illusion, and Mayweather shrugs, giving up. She quickly exits, slamming the door behind her.) HIGH FLYER: Hoo boy. TONY DAVIS: Where do you think she's going? HIGH FLYER: Strip club. (Tony Davis' ears perk up. He seems excited.) TONY DAVIS: Can we follow her? HIGH FLYER: You only want to follow her to the strip club. TONY DAVIS: So? HIGH FLYER: So? April fools? (Tony Davis lowers his shoulders, deflated.) TONY DAVIS: Well, now I understand your hatred of April Fool’s day. HIGH FLYER: Just don't bring it up to me again. (There's a long pause. Suddenly, the door to Flyer's apartment swings back open, with Mary-Lynn standing there in a huff.) MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: And just so you know, I AM on my period. (Immediately, she storms out again, leaving the rest of Team VIAGRA in a shocked silence. After a few moments pass...) TONY DAVIS: You know what holiday I've always hated? Groundhog Day. What I would give to take a shovel and just... BAM! HIGH FLYER: Woah. TONY DAVIS: You don't fantasize about knocking out Tom Arnold? HIGH FLYER: You mean Bill Murray. TONY DAVIS: Maybe. (FADE OUT.) (FADE IN. High Flyer and Tony Davis stand in front of a swaying PRIME banner.) HIGH FLYER: And that's all for the VIAGRA Power Variety Hour! It looks like we started a lot of things and didn't really finish much, did we Tony? TONY DAVIS: Sure not Fly. But that just leaves us with more to talk about next time! And we haven't even started on Global Warming! HIGH FLYER: Along with much more interesting topics, like BRITNEY SPEARS AND LINDSAY LOHAN, fighting to the death on the surface of the sun! Two women enter, which one would leave! TONY DAVIS: How to solve California's traffic problem. Nuclear bomb? Or bubonic Plague 2.0? HIGH FLYER: And what about the Dad from Family Matters. Why aren't you playing an old disgruntled cop in crappy independent movies? TONY DAVIS: We'll wait on your answer Carl Weathers. (Flyer lowers his head and shakes in disbelief of Tony. Slow fade out.)
