Life's up and downs.

Date: 9/30/09 REV 208 His life was like a broken plate of glass, the shards scattering about his feet… The bottle of half-empty Jack Daniels mocked him from the table. Peering at it in the darkness, Adam Garcia peaked over his shoulder at the alarm clock, which read 5:45a.m. Like clockwork, he reached for the lamp on the bedside table and turned the knob. Click, there was light, but only briefly. The bulb burned out just as fast as it illuminated. “Shit,” Adam muttered under his breath reaching for the light switch above his bed in the darkness. After the room was lit, he slowly rubbed the crust from his eyes, yawned, and stretched out his tired body. Today was the first day of his new life and he clearly could not wait for it to begin. It felt good for him to be able to get out of bed again, without any trouble, and even better because his head wasn’t hurting this morning. He yawned again smelling his own sour morning breath and headed straight for the bathroom. The sound of the running water surprisingly soothed him and though it was only a simple pleasure he smiled for the first time in months. That was until he looked up in the mirror at the stranger staring back. Painful memories tried to flood his mind, but Adam was now strong enough to hold them back. He knew however, that behind that innocent smile and buried deep down inside of him, there dwelled a monster. A monster so powerful and evil, that it ruined everything and everyone in its path. Snapping back to reality, he took a deep breath and pulled on the mirror opening the medicine cabinet which contained his tooth brush. “Oh, please, not today, Lord,” he prayed. He brushed his teeth and when finished, splashed cold water into his face, before proceeding from the bathroom. As he closed the door behind him a picture on the wall caught his eye. It was a portrait of he and his wife Veronica on their wedding day. Instead of smiling at what was supposed to be a joyous occasion, he just stood there. He stood there stroking the black hairs on his chin. “Hmm,” he said not taking his eyes from the picture. His eyebrows furrowed and his eyes clenched tightly shut. He took another deep breath, while continuing to stroke his chin, before finally shaking off the cobwebs. He walked back over to the table where the bottle of Jack sat and picked it up. Gazing at the fancy black label and with a heart filled with distress he said, “You were once my only true friend.” His voice cracked as if he were going to cry. “But now you’ve ruined my life.” And it was true. Jack Daniels was responsible for all the wrong in his life. It was the single blame for the downward spiral his life had recently taken and it was the reason why he’d lost everyth-… THUMP… THUMP… THUMP! There was a violent pounding at the door. Placing the bottle back in its position, Adam quickly stumbled down the stairs and headed toward the commotion. “Who could it be this early,” he thought. “Uhh,” he hesitated. “Who is it?” “Police, open up” a gruff voice immediately yelled from the other side. The unsuspecting voice caused the hairs on the back of Adam’s neck to stand on end. His blood boiled and his heart began to race. Butterflies fluttered within his empty stomach as he became a bit sick. Intimidated and with great haste Adam did what he was told. Two officers stared at him from the other side; fire burning in both of their eyes. “Is there a problem officers,” Adam immediately spoke up with a slightly suggestive tone. He grew concerned and began biting on his lower lip like he always does when he’s nervous. One of the officers stepped forward and began to push his way into the door. “Are you Adam Rivera,” he asked with a deep harsh voice and a strongly marked British accent. Adam gently scratched his face and slowly nodded. His eyebrows cocked and he squinted through his right eye as the sun crept through the door. He swallowed hard like he had an egg caught in his throat and began to panic. “Wait. What is this all about?” The second officer removed a pair of handcuffs from his pockets and started towards Adam. Reacting the best way he knew of, Adam put a hand in front of himself miming “stop.” He tried to remain calm and as polite as he could but he was so confused. “I think you guys are making a huge mistake here,” he pleaded. “Are you kidding me? What is this even for? Aren’t you guys supposed to flash your badges?” “Please turn around sir,” the officer holding the cuffs demanded. “We can do this the hard way or the easy way. I don’t want any trouble out of you. I know your kind.” “My kind,” Adam asked in a low guttural voice. “Is that what this is all about? Let me guess I fit the description, huh?” His Mexican accent started to come about as he grew agitated. “Young, dirty and mexicano? Is that what it is bruh? Ay holmes, you wanna see my green card?”” He resisted slightly until something inside of him told him to relax. The British officer tried to calm Adam down making sure however not to go against his superior officer. “Sir we are just following orders. Please, we don’t want any trouble.” The second officer began the Miranda rights as he handcuffed Adam. “You have the right to remain silent. If you choose to give up that right anything you say can-” the officer was interrupted. “Look, Officer Jackson is it? I know my rights. Now can you tell me what this is all about?” He squirmed from the cuffs being too tight. “Jesus man, do they have to be this tight?” “You’re under the arrest for the rape and murder of Olivia Jackson.” That was a curveball. “Woah woah woah,” Adam said screaming at the top of his lungs and slightly pulled away. “Rape? Murder? I don’t even know an Olivia Jackson. I’m telling you! You have the wrong guy! I have an alibi, I worked last night!” “How’d you know we were talkin bout last night?” Officer Jackson spoke up pulling Adam toward him by his unkempt brown locks. “Take it up with the judge,” he said as he pulled Adam away from his house and lead him toward the squad car. “Besides, I know about you Rivera. You have a record; 6 DUI’s, 11 alcohol or drug-related arrests. One in which you killed your own wife and kids! What makes you think we should believe you now? I hate your kind! They should bury you underneath the prison for all the trouble you’ve caused!” Adam blanked out. … The words burned him deep in his soul like drops of acid on his skin. It was all true. Adam killed his own wife and two sons six years ago, driving under the influence. He hit a guard rail. Not to mention the numerous illegal drugs he consumed over the years. Wrestlers in Mexico could usually get their hands on whatever types of drugs they wanted back in those days. He was a horrible person he knew. He hung with the wrong crowd and did things he wishes he can take back. But he can’t. However, he did his time; and he vowed to never drink or do drugs again. He even moved to America; leaving the legendary Mexican wrestling Garcia family behind. He had to not only get away from all the negative influences his motherland Mexico had to offer, but also left because of the shame he felt for casting such a dark cloud upon the renowned Garcia name. Not to mention the nightmares from the accident. He brought with him only one thing; the bottle of Jack Daniels from the night of the accident. Although he still copes with the demons daily, he turned his life around and was lead to PRIME by his brother Carlos Garcia formerly of The Trust Fund. It is the only thing he has now and it seemed like that too was now about to be taken away. … “Mr. Rivera,” the British officer uttered, shaking and snapping his fingers in Adam’s face. “Snap out of it!” Adam opened back up his eyes, his pale green retinas shining in the sunlight. For some reason he was uncuffed and back in his living room. “Wake up sir,” the officer pleaded. “Huh. What,” Adam finally responded. “Where am I?” The young officer cleared his throat and removed his hat. “I am very sorry sir,” he began. “But the real perpetrator with similar qualities of you turned himself in a few minutes ago. Right before you blanked out.” “Huh? Wait you mean to tell me-,” the young officer interrupted. “I know you’re upset and you have right to be but my partner and I truly apologize for the mix-up.” Adam scanned the room and noticed that the older officer was nowhere to be found. He nodded, the type of nod that says “I bet” and accepted the young officers apology with no ill feelings. Deep down he wanted to explode. He wanted to wipe the floor with him, but he realized that everyone was entitled to second chances. Just like he was given one; years ago. “Have a good day sir,” the officer bellowed from the distance exiting Adam’s home. He looked down at his watch and noticed that he was running late. Although the day had started off on the wrong foot things seemed to be changing; for the better that is. Adam Garcia was now a new man.
