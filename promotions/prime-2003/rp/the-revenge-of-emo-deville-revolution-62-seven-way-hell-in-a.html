Straddling his chest, Deville slapped him, forcing him to meet his torching gaze. “Leave her alone,” he ordered in a detached, rumbling monotone, as if he was somewhere else entirely. “If I hear about you giving that woman any more drugs – if I hear about anyone giving her drugs – I’m going to hunt you down and kill you.” The man murmured again, unable to form intelligible words, obviously protesting the unfairness of that, so Deville knocked him out cold with another shot, this one to the temple.
