Clydescapades

21 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 21st, 2005 In order to find the man who stole their Wookie, Clyde and James traveled through the darkest depths of Hell, enduring the pain of a thousand poorly executed promos about the killing of legends, and a rap about peanuts that didn’t seem to rhyme at all. Their journey took a thousand years to complete, by the end of which they had devised a plan to win their eight-man tag match at the upcoming PPV. That’s right. They spent a thousand years coming up with a way to win a single match. And they won’t even win it. I can pretty much guarantee it. If I were some sort of jealous douche that couldn’t put someone over without throwing a fit, I’d probably think Sebbsy hated me or something. Or I just sucked. And then I’d quit, and take my eighteen characters to another fed so I could feud with myself and get a fucking win for once. But no, I’m not like that. I simply chalk it up to the fact I handle comedy characters, and therefore they’re more apt to put over those in need of it. In essence, I’m the glue that holds this fed together, and every loss is one more coating applied to the foundation, keeping PRIME standing tall each week. Yeah, I’m pretty delusional. Which would explain why a thousand years in Hell is only like…twenty minutes in the real world. And there’s The Forsaken, finally figuring out a way to defeat Rampage. Screw Oxios. He’s the Marty Jannetty of the team. Except he sucks. And it’s not like Rampage is any better of a wrestler, but the only reason he gets the HBK comparison is because he puts over about as many people as Jesus Jr. If you’re not already at the top, you’re not getting his heat. And even then, he’ll get it back with some half-assed spear after the match. I don’t even know who the other two are. I think one of them lost last week. And the other one loses every other week. Come to think of it, I demand to win. There’s no way I’m putting over some guy who was possibly on Friends, and that dude who played Leprechaun. Anyway, I figure it’s about time I get back to the actual roleplay. I find it slightly humorous that I probably get further on these little blurbs that so consistently shatter the fourth wall than I do on my actual writing attempts, but if it works I’ll use it. Hell, I’d dress my little brother up like a twelve-year-old girl and let Sebs have his way with him if it’d get me a win. Sad. I can’t even buy a victory with sexual favors from children. But there both men stood, hatching a plan with which to defeat the only viable threat in the match, when a demon the size of Long Dong Silver’s penis stacked upon itself three times appeared, fire erupting around it. The breath left the bodies of The Forsaken, causing a great laughter to grow from within the enormous demon. “I have watched you from the moment you set foot in my realm. Tell me, why should I not kill you where you stand?” The demon’s eyes began to glow with a deep fire, and he towered over Clyde and James as both men took a step back. The demon waited for a response, but James wasn’t about to give Clyde the chance to get them both killed. “Uh…” Farwell stuttered, trying to think of a valid reason to be allowed to live. “I-I-I-…uh…w-we…” But Clyde didn’t take the hint. He never does. And he never shuts up. “I know who you are, the leader of lost souls! You can’t kill me! I’m immortal~!” Fucking Clyde. The demon wasn’t impressed, to say the least. Leaning over Walkins, the demon drew in air as he sniffed Clyde’s neck. “You smell like fear.” Clyde sniffed back. “You smell like cinnamon!” Farwell cursed Clyde under his breath, knowing the end was near. The demon, however, didn’t kill Clyde as James had expected. Instead, he withdrew, the fire in his eyes subsiding. “Do…do you really think so?” Clyde nodded, while James looked on in amazement, and the demon continued speaking. “I’ve been trying this new shampoo, and until now no one’s said anything about it…I thought my scalp was starting to get smelly again…” “Smelly like a fox!” The demon cocked an eyebrow. “Th-thank you?” Clyde grinned and shot him a thumbs-up. “You’re welcome, Mr. Cinnamon. But me and my friend here were trying to find the guy who stole my Chocolate Wookie. You wouldn’t happen to know where he lives, would you?” “You’re in luck, my young friend.” The demon smiled, showing off a row of fangs with blood spattered across them. “I just so happen to be the leader of lost souls, and it is my duty to know all those who reside within the bowels of Hell. I shall take you to him.” The demon turned and began to walk, prompting The Forsaken to follow. Trailing a few steps behind the demon, Clyde leaned over and whispered to James. “See, I told you he was the leader of lost souls.” “Shut up, stupid,” James shot back, eyeing the demon ahead of them. “It was a lucky guess. Besides, my mother always told me not to trust a demon.” “Didn’t she die when you were five or something?” James smacked Clyde in the back of the head for bringing up bad memories. “She also drank heavily and whipped me with an extension cord. What’s your point?” Some time passed between when they first left with the demon and when they arrived at their final destination. But I’m too lazy to go through all of it and actually add something to a roleplay besides shady description and one-liners found only in Just For Laughs’ Worst Of series. Instead, I’ll just say that you all missed out on the most amazing Forsaken adventure ever, and leave it at that. “I have taken you as far as I can,” the demon spoke, stopping suddenly in his tracks. “Beyond these fountains of molten sperm lay the ruins of what was once a great civilization. Within them, you shall find what you seek.” James was slightly curious, and decided to ask a question before the demon could walk away. “And…uh…why is it you can’t come with? I mean…you are the leader of lost souls, yes?” “Well, I…” The demon’s eyes shifted around, then lowered to the ground. “To tell you the truth, I’m just a temp. And those ruins house something more evil and vile than ten sequels to Glitter put together.” James and Clyde responded in unison. “Blasphemy!” “It’s the truth,” he sighed. “No one enters the ruins anymore, and no one leaves them. If I were you, I’d forget about finding who stole your Wookie, and get on with your lives.” “You know, that’s a good idea.” James grabbed Clyde by the arm. “Come on, we’re leaving.” And so they did. The end. What? There’s nothing left to read. Go home, dammit. … I’ll throw a rock at you. I swear to fucking God, I’ll do it! Ok, for those of you still with me, that wasn’t the end. I know, you wish it was. So do I. Quite frankly, I’m ashamed of myself for making you read more than five lines of this crap, but it has to be done. In order to have a chance at victory, a roleplay has to hit at least two thousand words. It also has to have about forty-five different colors in it, but I’ll let Youngblood handle that end. Credit to Geocities.com for those e-fed rules. So what happened to The Forsaken if they immediately left Hell when they were told? Well, it took another thousand years to get out of the place, but their perseverance brought them through. That, and Clyde’s rendition of Wheels On The Bus. Finally returning to earth forty minutes after they left, Clyde and James brushed the ash and dried semen off their clothes, exchanging glances with a few of the people staring at the smoke rising from their bodies. “What?” Clyde asked, staring at a little old lady. “Never seen something so hot before?” And then he licked his finger, touching it to his ass. As Clyde burned his finger on the extremely hot clothing and stuck it back into his mouth, James concocted a plan. “Clyde,” he said, leaning over to the little retard. “I think we can find the guy who stole your Wookie without having to go through those ruins.” “But how will we do that?” James pulled the note that was left in their locker room out of his pocket, showing it to Clyde. Clyde looked it over for a moment, then figured out what James was showing him. “I got it! We take this to a handwriting expert, and have them tell us who wrote it! Foolproof!” “Or,” James mused, “we could just go to the address he left on the back.” “Or that.” Now this is about the time I’d normally set you all free, and let you go about your day, while preparing to drag this out over another roleplay. But that’s not how I roll today. No, I’m gonna finish this here and now. So get a Coke, take a piss, and ready yourselves for part two of Clydescapades, entitled: Esclydepades! In order to help you along, I’ll make this more like WWE programming. It might make you feel more at home with taking a piss while someone’s trying to entertain you. Well, at least in their minds. Women’s wrestling matches…weak clotheslines…body slams that resemble inverted piledrivers…botched dropkicks deflating implants…crappier roll ups than the one Austin pulled on Owen Hart when his neck snapped. …Not Owen’s neck, you sick bastard. And if that wasn’t enough to make you want to go take a piss rather than read this shit, the next line will. Be warned: if you can be offended at all, don’t read ahead. Because this will offend you greatly. Greatly. Alright, here it com-AAAAAGGGHHHH!!! Someone…help…meeeeeeeeee… Ok, kids. I’m back. I had to go away for a while, but you can’t keep a good WARRIOR underground. He tried to bury me, but he should’ve gone deeper than six feet! Hogan couldn’t bury me, Vince couldn’t bury me, even HHH couldn’t bury me, so I’ll be damned if I let myself…hey, what is this? Clyde Walkins Super-Duper-Happy-Funtime-Don’t-Touch-Me-There-Or-I’ll-Scream-Picker-Upper? Hmm…wonder what it does. Hey, they don’t taste too ba-OH GOD! ULTIMATE WARRIOR going down… Thank God that’s over with. He’s back in the ground now, and with any luck we won’t be seeing anymore of Mr. Warrior. Anyway, since you’re probably done pissing by now, I’m gonna bring you back to The Forsaken. James looked at the address on the note, then the address on the building in front of him. “See, Clyde,” he boasted. “I told you there was a 123 Fake Street somewhere around here.” Clyde’s head hung. “Why do your fake-sounding things always turn out fine, but mine end up getting me shot or set on fire?” “Because no one likes you. Now be a dear and knock on the door for me…I’m gonna be standing around the side in case they start shooting…” James looked at Clyde, then immediately corrected himself. “Try to run away?” Walkins nervously approached the door, his hand trembling as it made a fist. His first knock attempt missed completely, and Clyde immediately turned and jumped off the porch, running around the side of the house to join James. “Did you knock, Clyde?” James asked, garnering a shake of the head from Clyde. “Go knock, dammit!” “But I’m scared…” Clyde responded quietly to Farwell’s shouting, and James finally just gave in, deciding to do it himself. Reaching the door, he slowly raised his hand, then brought it down onto the wood, knocking hard enough to cause an echo inside the house. Suddenly, a creepy laughter emanated from the doorway, causing the hairs of the back of James’ neck to stand up. “That’s it, I’m outta here.” James turned and started walking down the stairs, when the door slowly began to creak open. “You did it!” Clyde exclaimed in glee, wrapping his arms around James in a tight hug. Farwell simply pie-faced Clyde to get rid of him. “I didn’t do anything. That door opened on its own…” Both Clyde and James turned their heads to look at the open door, peering inside the house to see only white sheets covering old furniture. “This is looking a little too familiar for my liking,” Farwell whispered, subconsciously backing away from the house, causing Walkins to do the same. “Permission to run away from the haunted house?” Clyde asked. James didn’t take but a second to respond. “Permission granted!” And they took off back to their hotel, neither man slowing the entire time. Well, except for when Clyde said he really needed to take a piss. And again when James thought one of his lungs popped. But that was it. Back at the hotel, Clyde and James mulled over what just happened. “So,” James began, “to recap: we went to Hell, then came back to earth and found out where the guy lives that stole your Wookie, but we don’t want to go in his house yet, and at KOK-Eater 2005 we get to beat the crap out of Teh ‘Tardness, Chandelier Honda, and Frank McCourt. Clyde, anything you’d like to say to our opponents before we say goodbye to the folks at home?” Clyde paused for a moment, then nodded. “Ah, laddie. Ya’ll have yar pint!” James nodded in agreement. “That he shall, Clyde. That he shall.” And if you’re wondering about the reference to Frank McCourt and the pint, you’re a moron. Start reading something intelligent for once in your life, stupid. Wait…no. Don’t.
