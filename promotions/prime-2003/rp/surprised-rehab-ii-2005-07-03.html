Surprised? (Rehab II)

3 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd July 2005 Surprised? Surprised by what exactly? Merely six months ago Las Vegas’ golden boy had been reduced to a cabaret act alongside his fellow P.W.A. counterpart Nascosto Ireul Nerezza after the closure of the now forgotten Rush-Pro Wrestling. Or that he could be drawn back into the business by the tangible smell of wet ink smeared onto a crisp piece of headed note paper, One inscribed with, “PRIME WRESTLING CONTRACT” It had been a very long time since the Jester’s emerald eyes cast themselves over something containing the word ‘contract,’ not even the vultures who preyed endlessly on the unemployed circled. Those circling birds would much rather peck at the un-nurtured scrap of talent beckoning for an opportunity than nibble at the PRIME beef fillet that had been presented to them, ironically it was PRIME Wrestling that swooped in for the kill. The days of negotiating a contract worthy of signatures such as P.W.A. were a memory, in the same vain as all their accolades, this was to be a clean slate. It wasn’t just financial security, and they may not have mentioned it openly but the comic and vampire both craved to hear the sweet symphony of, “WHERE’S YOUR HEADDDDDDDDDDDDDD ATTTTTTTTTTTTTTTT?!?!?!?!?!?!?!” This accompanied by the thunderous applause of the live audience and the wails of agony were exactly what the Conniving Collective needed. It was this warmly welcome that fuelled the flames as the former five times PCW Tag Team Champion’s took it upon themselves to get to know the division up close and personal, making an unscheduled appearance during the, TeH Pwn versus Team Zambo bout at ReVolution 67. The four combatants could have been described by the word that opened this chapter, ‘surprised,’ but no-one was more surprised than Gaz “The Jester” Green. Six months prior to 22nd of June 2005 the Jester had no idea what the future would hold… ~ “During our last session you briefly touched upon your relationship with your family. You gave us an insight into your past, something you’ve concealed from the world…” “Look, if I knew my life before wrestling was thought to be so damn interesting I would’ve written an autobiography!” hissed the Jester in response. This was a sore subject, not just for the suppressed Gareth Green but the egomaniacal Gaz “The Jester” Green too. For the last eight years the Emerald Menace had tried to bury his past and here he sat listening to somebody excavating this information, his tongue was his spade and he was digging, real deep. His blood boiled like a cauldron. Not even Nerezza was aware of his partner’s sordid history, and it was for the best, had he known about this chink in the Jester’s armour he would’ve surely exploited it. Yes they are partners, in crime some would say but they enjoyed one upping one another and it was usually Green with all the ammunition leaving the vampire at the mercy of the Jester’s firing squad. “Okay!” injected the therapist having realised that more doors were closing rather than opening if he continued down this avenue of questioning. “Like you said in your first session, you opted to become a professional wrestler. So how did you break into the business?” Good move on the therapist. Very tactical, therapy is like a game of battleships or a technical wrestling bout, you feel out your opponent before slowly moving in for the kill. He took the conversation full circle, from a negative like Gareth Green’s relationship with his immediate family to a positive like his break into the profession. Lucky really, had he continued to press for information then the only break under discussion would be that of his therapist’s jaw one would assume. “Well squire,” began Gaz. “You don’t walk up to a director’s front door with a cocky strut and an arrogant attitude with a pre-prepared speech. Look Mr. Starr, may I call you Ron? Anyway Ronnie I’m Gaz Green, The Last Great Technical Wrestler and should you hire me you may come to call me the messiah, the second coming for I will save your company from pitiful buy-rates and embarrassing ticket sales. I will take your promotion to the moon and back quicker than it takes NASA to knock up another cardboard film set of the Luna landing. So what do you say?” he concluded jovially. “I was in the right place at the right time to tell you the truth. I was already enrolled in a wrestling academy and to pay the tuition fees I was working long hours in some grotty bar and grill, The Wisconsin Waterhole!” continued the former Primetime Championship Wrestling starlet, adding a roll of his emerald green eyes towards his former place of employment. “Interesting…” murmured his therapist quietly. “Anyway…” snapped the decorated veteran. Decorated veteran? This to describe a man on the brink of his twenty-sixth birthday, however, he is exactly that. Decorated in past accolades, the DWA Light Heavyweight Championship to the PCW Continental Title and veteran for giving eight years of service to this industry we call professional wrestling. “… Some Indy company called the Dark Wrestling Alliance were touring and hit town for a couple of days. As fate would have it, the owner, one Dave Time called in to sample Canada’s finest cuisine but that place was shut so he found his way to our cesspool establishment. And now for a world exclusive, doc. Most ‘wannabes,’ be it actors, singers or even wrestlers in my predicament would have gone with the clichéd waiter role and wormed their motives into the conversation, not me!” The therapist’s eyes motioned upwards from his notepad to rest upon the cunning smug face of his client. With an evil glint in his eye he began again, “Well squire, I’m a firm believer of the phrase, it’s not what you know but who you know and it just happened that I knew someone who would do anything for a bit of money… no questions asked. With a wad of Benjamin’s in my ‘friends’ back pocket he appeared to take a little dislike to our dear Mr. Time, and excuse the pun but Dave’s time was running out. However, instead of having a drunken brute’s fists raining down upon him, you could say lady luck was shining down upon him as everyone’s favourite bar attendant stepped in to defuse the situation with a little hold we wrestlers call, a cross-face chicken wing!” That’s right! While the only mastermind in wrestling Alan Kriegman was scheming and plotting in the illustrious PCW, another genius was masterminding his entry into the business. This story a little twist on what Gaz had originally told his partner of half a decade. Yes, the fundamentals were present and correct, there was a bar, an altercation and chance meeting but who other than those involved would’ve realised this whole situation was more false then an American footprint on the moon. “If I can speak openly…” said the therapist with caution. Gaz gave a wag of his head to acknowledge with ‘Yes’. “Your views and how you conduct yourself are, to be frank, puzzling. You feel great anger and spite when the topic of family is brought to the forefront of these therapy sessions but you take a sadistic pleasure channelling this anger onto an innocent. This being the case with this Dave Time, you basically lied and stole a place on his talent roster!” Some may refer to this incident as a nasty prank, but Gaz “The Jester” Green was two years away from even being a concept. You could say this was the planting of the seed, and how that seed flowered. “And…” was the response. “In this cut throat industry it’s a dog eat dog world and I happen to have bigger teeth than most. The most important thing is that this whole charade paid off because I managed to get my foot through the door. Dave was like thanks son; you showed some quick thinking kid… blah… blah… blah, anyway he invited me over for a try out before signing me to his circus act. The thing is Mr. Doctor Curiosity but you can’t just walk into a company with a name like Gareth Green, seriously, what fan with half a brain cell is going to buy ‘The Extremist’ Gareth Green or ‘The Second Coming’ Gareth Green? The latter sounds like a top shelf video!” He was right though, having been in the business five minutes he wasn’t worthy of such monikers and the best call of action was to place Gareth Green under a suitable guise. A persona that would propel him straight into a feud, and thus between them, Time and Green concocted the character “New-Blood”. Gaz was to play heel from the word go… “Between us I was to play New-Blood, a spoilt brat who felt that he was being treated unfairly. I was to play up to the idea that a youthful wrestler was far more marketable than a seasoned veteran, from looks and personality all the way to in-ring ability. With the character built from the ground up I was to debut in the hardcore division against one of the company’s old guard who’s contract was on the verge of expiring. I suppose you could say I was being groomed as his replacement and this match was the handing of the torch so to speak, personally I saw it as an opportunity to hurt a guy with no repercussions hanging over my head. It’s not like he would be getting a rematch…” the reminiscing came to an abrupt halt at the sound of the wailing alarm. - Ding. Ding. Ding. - “That was sixty minutes?” questioned the prankster. “I’m sure that alarm is rigged to sound every time I get to the good bits!?” he hissed, his eyes erect like daggers and focusing intensely on the intrusive device. “Well I’ll look forward to hearing these ‘good bits’ as you put them during our next session!” calmly concluded the therapist. As of now these episodes were nothing more than memories, nostalgic ones at that and Gaz could only look back but six months on he would be creating new ones, with the help of PRIME no doubt. Revolution to the Jester was merely a word he may have came across at some stage during his life, six months on it would be the show that resurrected his floundering career and he would show Seth Masters and Gabe Brown that it’s not only phoenixes that rise from the ashes.
