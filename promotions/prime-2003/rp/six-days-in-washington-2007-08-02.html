Six Days In Washington.

2 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 2nd, 2007 Wrestling is Fake. There are no real heroes in Wrestling. They're all a bunch of roided up freaks. That's what my mother and her high society friends always told me. For years, even when directly involved in the industry, I believed them. Six days in Washington swayed that belief. Six long days in Washington. --- Riding high in a black SUV, the unlikely duo of Vangelus Olsig and Machaiveli St. Romani found themselves headed to Seattle, Washington for a mega-event known simply as Colossus. "Colossus is being held in Washington." Vangelus stated. "...and that's where we're headed." "Just thought I'd reiterate it. The first time I told you, I felt a...disturbance." Silence "Eerily similar to the one I'm feeling now. What is it about Washington that makes your bottom lip curl like that?" "I'd...rather not talk about it." "We've got three hours to spare. I could use a good story." "I said I'd rather not talk about it. It's a...facet of my life that I've long since put behind me." "If you don't tell me, I'll start singing Paula Abdul's greatest hits again." He sighs. "Fine, Vangelus." "Make it good." "Upon leaving National Palace, Haiti, I found settlement in a city called Black Diamond, Washington. That's where I received my first taste of American wrestling. It didn't take long for me to rise up the ranks of a small independent promotion. Before I knew it, I was challenging World Champions. But something happened. Something I'll never forget. Something...tragic." --- MONDAY: DAY ONE "Neither joy nor sorrow is promised to the straddler, but to the saint, a peace that passes all understanding. I heard the preacher say that yesterday." His name was Salvador. Mr. Salvador A. Rumsfield, if you didn't know him personally. He was a wrestling promoter. A ruthless wrestling promoter who ran the base of his operations from within the confines of Black Diamond, Washington. Utilizing every asset available to his arsenal, he killed, cunned, and deprived many in order to construct his infamed Tri-State Wrestling. Yet, even in knowing this, the same three-hundred faithful continued to assemble together in order to cheer on their favored homegrown heroes. Salvador simply chucked it all up with no remorse. You couldn't help but hate the guy. "Yes, and while you were busy praying for the itsy bitsy children of the world, did you ever stop to think as to how you were going to handle our...situation." "Situation? What is this situation you speak of, Wendy?" Another thing concerning Salvador. He was a drunk. He loved to drink, and usually that same love led him to hurt someone dear to him. His daughter, Wendy, weighed heavy on his heart. Her mother...his wife...was killed in a fatal car accident some years ago. All he thought about was the girl. Intoxication mixed in with regret...it didn't surprise me that he'd call me by the wrong name, especially with it being her name. "Guess again." "I'm sorry...Francois. It's just the...stress, I guess." I sat comfortably in the leather chair perfectly positioned in front of Salvador's oak desk. He sat even more comfortably across, watching my every move. I was his top star and somewhat of an acquaintance...yet still he didn't trust me. I didn't take it personally...Sal trusted no man breathing. "That's funny. I always worked under the assumption that you stopped drinking." "Who said anything about drinking?" "The horrid stench on your breath pretty much says it all." His eyes glazed over. Something was preventing him from passing out. Maybe he knew that I would no longer stand for his intolerance. No longer stand for his recklessness. He didn't gave a fig about me personally, but on Sundays, I was his personal savior. I was the one drawing him those dollars that allowed him to finance Drug Lords all across the State. Every time I hit a hurricanrana, he drafted another Lord. Every time I attempted a suicide dive, he bought the Lord's kids a car in a gesture of good will. Every time I botched a move, he killed them all. That burden was the reason I never wrestled alone. "Dammit, Francois...does your altar-boy gimmick have an off-switch or what?" "No. Not when it comes down to preserving your life and that of your eighteen-year old daughter. She's graduating high school, Salvador, and the last thing she needs is a drunken father to weigh out the void that her mother left." "Wendy trusts me enough to know that I would never do anything to harm her in any way." I pushed myself from the confines of the leather Harrington branded chair in order to meet Salvador's daring glaze head on. He could feel a sermon stirring up from the inside of me. The glare in his bobbling pupils showed how desperate he was to avoid it. "Which is why it saddens me to see such trust blatantly violated. You need h-.." "Please, Mr. Francois...you said something about a situation, earlier?" I submitted. He was too passive in thought to comprehend anything I had to say anyhow. "How's this for a laugh. Remember last week when you promised both Andy and Dave, our current tag team champions, extra pay for replacing Beauty and Brawns in the Tag Team main event?" "Of course." "And how you backed out when pay day came?" "Backed out? Dear hell, I allowed them to win the belts! What else do they want?" "Apparently they wanted a lot more. They just quit." His lips curled in an odd manner. As if he wanted to scream, but forced himself not to. The news cut him. Deep. Like a 10 inch sword. Of course, he'd never admit to that, however. His stones were too precious to succumb to intimidation. Therefore, instead, he brushed it off as so. "Pssh...screw both of them. I still have my ace in the hole. When you and Monte face off, heads are sure to tu-..." His phone rang. He yanked it from within his coat pocket and scanned over the I.D. "It's Wendy. I'll be taking this. I trust you know the way out." Not because of Salvador did I leave, but because his daughter deserved as much time with her father as he could provide her. I'd feel cheap robbing her of that. Therefore, I initiated my leave. Or started to at least. "Wendy, honey I was just thinking of you." I heard a noise. Not a simple, passive noise that could be attributed to howling winds or stray dogs. No, this was a nose from within the building. Sounded like a muffled scream with a impactful thump followed subsequently after. "Of course, I'll be home tonight." I scanned the room, seeking some sort of relevance as to where the noise originated. The only thing I managed to find was a pair of sharp, beady eyes piercing through my heart. The belonged to Salvador. "No, sweetheart...don't you worry about that. Daddy's got it under control." I heard it again, only much more amplified this time. As a matter of fact, the estranged echo made it sound similar to a gunshot. Something was occurring upstairs. My conscious and my ears both said so. "Oh really? And how many A's did you receive this time?" I motioned for Salvador to come over near me. He gave a brush off wave. Or maybe it was a piss off wave. "That's great, sweetheart" I motioned once again, this time in a more urgent manner than the last. "One second, baby." He placed the phone on the desk and presented me with the most dastardly of looks. "Francois...you're still here." "I heard something." "I'm sorry. I didn't know the safety lock on my gun made so much noise." "Salvador, I'm serious. I he-..." There it was again. The same estranged echo that suggested a gunshot, only it came in pairs this time. "Did you hear that?" "Dammit Francois, you're hallucinating. It's probably just my security buffs playing house." "Probability says it's probably not. A man of your credentials would be far more cautious of such things, I'd assume." Salvador sighed deeply, but I could tell he was curious as well. If ever a man wanted a lesson in hiding his true feelings, he'd take from Salvador. I knew the man too well, though. I could read him like a book. "Honey, I'll call you right back, okay? Love you." Suddenly, Salvador reached underneath his desk, retrieving a chrome .22 glock that he pointed directly towards me. It was his own special method of intimidation. "I've got one bullet left. If this thing proves to be empty, you'll be full...of lead." "I'm your world champion. The promotor in you wouldn't stand for it. Now let's go." After equipping myself with one of Sal's spare glocks (and believe me...he had plenty of them), we both began our trek towards the outside of the office and up the stairs. I, myself, walked gently, but for a man of Salvador's size, the was a much more weighted task. It didn't help that he wasn't quite as convinced neither. We approached a split hallway with one path leading right and the other left. I turned to Salvador for guidance. "Both of these halls lead to alternate entrances into the conference room. You take the left, I'll take the right." Salvador advised. "I don't trust you. I'm taking the right”. It was true. I didn't trust him. Not as far as I could throw him. I can't speak for Sal's end, but on my side of things the hallway was completely empty. Still, however, I trekked along gently just as a pre-caution. Salvador obviously wasn't as cautious due to the fact that he reached the room far before I did. In turn, that also meant that he discovered the cause of the noise far before I did as well. "Good God..." I heard him belt out. "What's the m-..." I kicked open the door to find Sal staring down at the carpet. The blood stained carpet. The blood flowed from the veins of one Monte Black...the man I was set to face Sunday in a match that Salvador was billing as the “Matchup of the Millineum”. Tickets were already sold out, hype was exceeding an all-time high, and Salvador was prepping himself for the major pay-day. Something tells me that the death of Monte Black the wrestler instead of Monte Black the person was the cause of Salvador's surprise and sadness. He could care less about Monte Black as a person, but without Monte Black the wrestler, there'd be no Romani vs Black. And without Romani vs Black, there'd be no massive pay-day. That was enough to make Salvador cry. "No pulse." I stated after kneeling down to survey certain areas of Monte's body. "That means he's...." I shook my head yes. Call it pocus, but I literally heard Salvador's heart drop. "Wait, what's this? It's some sort of note.” Attached to the chest of Monte was a note. The handwriting was awful...nearly to the point of illegible, but still I managed to depict it's message. "What does it say?" "Says: The significant correspondents of this week will climax in the Roman Numerical equivalent of the Crumbling Dove minus the Costumed Liviu." "...the hell?" We both stood confused. The Crumbling Dove? That was the name of a famous statue located in downtown Black Diamond. And Costumed Liviu? The name of a local punk rock band. They weren't famous by any stretch of the imagination, but notable. How did the two connect, however? "This was premeditated, and I can't help but think that somewhere in this note there's a hint of more to come. We've got to notify the police." Suddenly, I heard a click. Following the sound of the click, I lifted my eyes to witness Salvador holding a gun to my face. "Put the phone down, Francois." "Salvador, we have to get hel-..." "I've got one bullet left, PUT THE PHONE DOWN!" Salvador feared the police. He was the most wanted innocent man in the city. In other words, the FBI knew he was involved in illegal dealings, but had no evidence other than word of mouth. They'd bite at the smallest opportunity to lock him up. This, by no means, was no small opportunity. This was big enough to send Sal to the chair. "Fine. Tell me of your grand idea, then." I stated, hanging up the phone prior to placing it back within my coat pocket. "I know a guy. Once worked for a currently disbanded government intelligence agency. Now he just sits and rolls in his royalties. He could depict the meaning of this note with his eyes closed. We'll pay him a visit tomorrow." "And in the meantime?" "You sleep here tonight. I can't risk you having a sudden 'urge' to alarm authorities." I sighed. I could have easily outran Sal, but the bullet was another story. "...And Wendy?" "She's at her aunt Tammie's. She won't mind staying another night. At least...I hope not." Salvador cared for Wendy. I couldn't take that from the guy. I guess the saying's true that even in the most hideous of demons, there's always a tiny bit of good. He scanned the body once more as tear began to formulate within his right eye. Before I knew it, he was on knees, weeping and wailing over the slain body of Monte Black. "My World Champion...My championship match...My massive pay-day...all lies in this pool of blood." I covered my ears. He screamed. "DAMNIT!" TUESDAY: DAY TWO "So ol' Monte Black was slain in cold blood, eh? Couldn't have happened to a nicer guy." This was Salvador's friend. His former name was Detective Frank Thomas, but now he's referred to by his middle name: Sleek. How he got that name is beyond me. He was quite the clever fellow, however. Once worked for a government intelligence agency that was disbanded due to negligence. Something tells me he had something to do with that negligence. I didn't trust him. "When Salvador described you to me, he failed to include your...horrid sense of humor." I responded in defense of Monte. "Who's the wise guy, Sal?" "My next World Champion." "You mean, would've been, right?" A small chuckle escaped Sleek as he took another puff of his cuban cigar. I could tell this was going to be a long and tedious process and therefore prepared myself for a tarry. "Here's the note left at the scene." Sal handed him the note in which he quickly scanned over. "Hmm...The significant correspondents of this week will climax in the Roman Numerical equivalent of the Crumbling Dove minus the Costumed Liviu." "We figured out the Roman Numerical equivilant part. The C, V, L, D, and I in both phrases represent Roman Numerals. when added up, they equal 666 and 667 respectively. Crumbling Dove minus Costumed Liviu...666 minus 667. That equals One. Whatever is about to happen next will happen in one day. Since the note was retrieved yesterday, that one day would equal today." I stated. Pretty decent work for an average joe such as myself. "And since it's obvious that the killer wants me dead..." Salvador trembled. He was afraid of death. That makes two fears. Death and the police. Both frightened him tremendously. "You believe you're going to die today." Sleek stated, finishing Sal's sentence. "Precisely." "Exactly what time did you discover the note?" "Time wasn't really of the essence, but I'd say it was somewhere around the 8:30 mark." He glances at his watch prior to glaring back at us with a hidden smirk on his face. If I didn't know any better, I'd say we were looking at the killer. "It's 8:20 now. That means, if they wanted to kill you...they'd kill you in approximately one day, which would be roughly around ten minutes from now according to your time estimate." "What should we do, then!?" Salvador began to panic. "I've got good news and bad news...pick your poison." "Bad news." "They ARE going to kill someone today." "Good news?" "That person is not you...yet." I interjected myself into the equation. "But that makes no sense, according to the riddle." "See, this is why you should leave this stuff up to the professionals. Whoever wrote this was either not very smart or purposely wanted you to decode the meaning of this 'riddle'. It's too simple. Despite that, however, he or she was smart enough to know that the letter "U" was not used in the Roman language and therefore was replaced with the letter "V". Therefore, every "U" in the phrases 'Crumbling Dove' and 'Costumed Liviu' would count as V's, and since V's equal 5 in Roman numerals, that'd add another 5 numbers to your count." "Meaning it'd actually be 671 minus 677. That's Six." "Nearly a week. Each day this week, he'll target someone in Salvador's life who corresponds with that day. Yesterday was Monday..." "And he killed Monte." "Yes, therefore I'm assuming that when he says “corresponding” he's referring to their name and the day's name. That means by Saturday, he'll have Salvador." 'Oh God...Why. Why does this have to happen to me. What did I ever do to deserve this?" “Look on the bright side, Sal. It could be Saturday if you're lucky.” Sleek's sarcasm wasn't benefiting Sal's panic in any shape or form, so I decided to offer my own piece of advice. "Maybe it'd be wise to...relocate for a while. Atleast until we're sure this fiasco is over." "No. No way am I going to allow some bizarro to run me out of MY city. Leave MY family behind. Leave MY promotion to die. NO WAY! Someone's picking a fight with me, and I'm going to give them exactly what they want. We're going to catch this crook and make his life a living HELL. NO ONE screws with Salvador A. Rumsfield!" Sal's phone rang again. It was Wendy. "Hello?" Sleek offered me a drink while in wait, but I declined. I still didn't trust him. "Oh, hey Honey." I could hear soft screams coming from the phone's speakers. Something was wrong. "Wait, what's the matter?" Sal began to turn red. Something was really wrong. "Honey, please...just slow down and tell me what's going on." Panic crossed his face. "WHAT? Are you sure?" His mouth stood agape. "How did this happen?" He took a deep sigh before running his hand through his rough and rarely combed hair. "Okay Honey, listen to me. Take the Navigator and head over to your Aunt Tammie's. I'm on my way to pick you up." Panic mixed in with fear. "Honey, just calm down and do as I say. I love you, you hear?" He was quite sincere. "It's going to be okay. Just get over to your Aunt's now." He hung up the phone. "What's going on?" I pondered. "Wendy found Teddy dead in the kitchen." "Teddy?" "My butler." "It's begun." I whispered to myself. Someone really did want to break Sal. They wanted to break him mentally and then physically. "Wendy's your daughter, correct?" Sleek asked. "Yes, she is." While taking a sip of his finest wine, Sleek presented the both of us with a suggesting glare. It took a while for Sal to come to terms, but he finally managed to decode the glare. "And tomorrow is...No. Hell no, not my daughter. That bastard won't touch her!" "Sal! Wait up" I yelled out. And as we both charged towards the home of Salvador A. Rumsfield, Sleek stood shaking his head behind. He smiled. "...My condolences." WEDNESDAY: DAY THREE "Daddy...I'm scared." The custom-built, million-dollar home of Salvador A. Rumsfield. Normally, a wise crack would follow soon after an introduction such as that, but not today. No, today, Salvador is the unluckiest man in the world. I feel bad for him. His daughter is slated to die and she doesn't even know about it. I attempted to persuade him to allow authorities to get involved, but he quickly declined. Salvador was more willing to fight the crook himself than allow any form of authority to become involved. With good reason, might I add. A man of his stature had to be on his heels at all times. Poor Sal. "There's no need to worry, honey. I'm here now and I'm...going to stay here." The two sat across each other, dining on a well-prepared meal. Sal couldn't even look Wendy in the eye. Every time he attempted so, all he could see was the face of Monte Black. The torture ridden through as much of his face that was left. The obvious signs of struggle. The anguish. He saw it all every time he looked into Wendy's eyes. "But if only you saw it, daddy. His brains were spilled all across the wall...on the floors...they even stained the bedsheets. And his face...the look on his face is what hurt me the most. It showed just how much pain he was in." He couldn't take it. "Alright, swe-..." "And his eyes. His eyes pierced through my heart like a sharp knife. I was afraid to touch him, but I had to close his eyes with my bare hands. It was surreal. It was gruesome. I've never seen so much blood in my life." He tried to stop her, but she continued. His stomach felt weak. "Baby, I u-..." "He was tortured daddy. He wasn't just killed, he was tortured. I pray to God I never have to endure something like that ever in my l-..." He glanced over at her... The killer was right behind her... "WENDY!" He leaped forward and covered her in his grasp only to realize that it was an illusion. He was seeing things. This scenario had him losing his mind. A deathly silence. She looked at him, he looked at her...for as about as long as he could stand. Taking a deep sigh, he placed a hand on her shoulder, pulling her in tightly and clinging to her with a hug. "I'm sorry, honey...it's just a touchy subject and I'd rather put it behind us. You'll never have to go through anything like that. I...promise you." "Thanks, Dad. I feel so safe when I'm around you. You'll never do me any harm." He hesitated. "No honey...I wouldn't." As moments of silence passed, Sal loosed the hug before making his way over to the medicine cabinet. It was if he was taking his last walk down death row. Maybe in a sense, he was. He need confirmation, though. Confirmation that she truly trusted him. "Honey, you do know that any and everything I do for...and towards you...is for your own good, right?" "Of course, Daddy...why ask that?" "I just wanted to be sure.” He slowly walked over to her, hands trembling and neck pounding with sweat. “Here's your medicine." "Thanks Daddy." She motioned to place the two pills into her mouth, but he stopped her, placing his hand on her forearm. "Honey..." "Yes?" He nearly choked up. "...I love you." "I Love you too Dad." She smiled. Gracious, what a smile. It all seemed to happen so slowly, but finally she managed to place the pills in her mouth, downing a tall glass of water upon doing so. Suddenly, she began to cough. "This tastes a bit different then usual." Without so much as a peep, Salvador turned his back towards her and stared straight forward...tears streaming down like an emerald water fall. "Daddy...what is this?" She began to cough heavily, dropping the glass of water thus leaving it to shatter all across the floor. A small piece tapped the back of Salvador's shoe, prompting him to peer down. In it, he could see her reflection. She was on bended knee, clutching at her throat as she coughed uncontrollably. "Daddy, I-...I'm cho-...king." He began to cry...hard. "Daddy...hel-...." He couldn't stand it. He fell to his knees and wailed like never before. "Daddy....h-..." He wanted one last glance at her, but couldn't bare it. "Dad-..." She hit the floor. Her pleas for help stopped. Her coughing stopped. Her breathing stopped. Salvador couldn't bare the thought of someone else taking his daughter away from him and torturing her unto death as they did the other two victims. ...So he did it himself. He broke down in tears. THURSDAY: DAY FOUR "Alright, Francois...sure you're up to this?" I wish Sal wouldn't do this. He was just forced to lay death upon his own daughter yesterday, and now he's acting as if nothings wrong. I can understand not wanting other to know your true feelings, but keeping it bottled in such a way will not result in flowery endings. Yet still, he insisted on doing just that. "As long as I can handle it my way." The plan was simple. Salvador didn't have many that were terribly close to him. We were set to use that to our advantage. Narrowing it down, Thursday's victim seemed almost guaranteed. It'd be his sister, Tammie. Sal had two sisters, but one died when she was nine. Before Sal was even born. That caused him and Tammie to stick close together. She cared for Wendy when Sal was away on business ventures. The two were close. Tammie was going to be the next victim. Sal and I were sure of it. "I don't want you flaking out on me at the last minute." While Tammie was away on the job, we snuck into her home and set up surveillance cameras in every major room. It wasn't a big home, so it was no tough feat. The plan was for me to camp out near the home while Salvador surveyed the camera shots from a distance. When the killer pounced, I break in and take him down. "I'm true to my word. You know a lot about that, don't you Salvador." I swallowed my words hard. "...Bad timing, sorry." "For what he did to my daughter, I want this guys head on a platter." "I'm not shooting to kill." "Think about it, Francois...i-.." "I'm not...shooting...to kill. I'll wrestle him down and I'll hand deliver him to you, but I'm not a murderer." "Fine. Make sure you keep my sister Tammie safe." I glanced at my watch. It was 8:29. "It's about that time. Tell me what you see." "She's removing her make-up in front of a mirror." "It's 8:30. He should be striking within the span." "She's removing her clothes. I'm not so sure I want to watch this." "Stay focused, Sal. What's going on now?" "She's slipping into a pink robe. Now the slippers. Wait, I just heard something." "What was it?" "I'm not sure, but apparently she did as well. She's peeking around the corner." "Do you see anyone?" "Not yet, b-...I just heard it again! It was louder this time!" I unlocked the safety on my gun. "I'm going in. Keep me posted." "She's peeking around the corner. She's...scared. Francois, please get in there and help her." I gently browsed around the corner and knelled in front of the front entrance door. "I'm moving in. Which room is she in?" "Upstairs bedroom. She just called her husbands name, but no answer. She's peeking inside of his bedroom now." With much force and anxiety, I plucked away at the lock. "I'm picking the lock." "Hold on...She's spotted something in the bedroom. She looks terrified." "What did she see?" "I-I...don't know. I'm switching over to the bedroom angle no-...Oh My God." "What? What's going on?" He didn't answer. I knew something was seriously wrong. "SALVADOR?" "He's dead. Her husband...Thommy...is dead." "Are you sure? How's she reeacting?" "She's crying uncontrollably. Something isn't right, though. There was a gun by the body. She now has it in her hand." The killer was more methodical than we originally assumed. I, myself, knew that Tammie was a nervous breakdown waiting to happen...apparently the killer did as well. He provided her with the gun, the bullet, and the temptation of suicide. For Sal's sanity, I prayed she wouldn't bite. "What is she doing with the gun?" "Nothing yet. Francois, please get in there." "Okay, I'm in. I'm heading up stairs." Quickly, I dashed up the stairs. I must've skipped at least six steps simultaneously, or so it seemed. "FRANCOIS SHE'S GOT THE GUN TO HER HEAD!" "WHICH ROOM IS IT!?" "SECOND DOOR TO YOUR RIGHT! FRANCOIS HURRY!" "I'M INSIDE...TAMIE, NO!" I saw her squeeze the trigger. The gunshot echoed throughout the room. Silence. "Did she..." "Salvador, I'm sorry." More silence. A deathly silence. "Salvador." I heard the phone drop. "Salvador, can you hear me?" The tone ended. It hung up. Sal was losing it. I could hear it in his voice. But maybe there was a method of finding this killer. The gun Tammie used...it was bound to have the killer's fingerprints all over it. And even if that failed, the serial number had to be in tact somewhere. Every gun had a traceable serial number. I picked up the gun. The handle was taped. No fingerprints present. Must've used a glove. I checked for the serial number. It was scratched off. This attacker knew what he or she was doing. SHIFT "That Friday, the attacker had it out for me." "They came after you?" "Yes...they tried. But I knew they were coming. I was the only person remotely close to Salvador who corresponded with the riddle." "Did they..." "They shot me, yes. But the properly equipped bullet proof vest planted underneath my trench coat jacket spared my life. Sleek was there. He ran up the stairs upon hearing the gun shots. The impact kept me down for a moment, leading him to believe that I was dead. He failed to confirm it. I'd later prove grateful of that fact." SHIFT FRIDAY: DAY FIVE Salvador found himself in the hospital. The stress had piled so high upon him that he eventually collapsed. I'm not sure whether it was the fear of death drawing near that did him in or the death of his sister. Whichever, it hit him hard. "Good Evening, Salvador." Sleek was back. Sal's first visitor. "Sleek? How much am I paying for this visit." "I'll tack it unto your never-ending tab." "...That's nice. But because me and you both know that you're hardly a man for casual visits, care to explain your real reasoning for being here?" Sleek took a deep breath before slipping his hands deep into his pockets. He was nervous, no doubt, but knew that a job had to be done. "Salvador...what I'm about to say may sound strange, but hear me out." "With all of these tubes attached to me, I don't think I have any other real alternative." "It's your turn." Salvador tilted his head, suggesting surprise. "...what?" "Friday's bullet has been placed. It's almost Saturday. It climaxes in six days...today's the sixth." "Whoa whoa, wait. Friday's bullet has already been placed? Quit speaking in parables, Sleek. What the hell is going on!?" "I di-..." "As a matter of fact, where's Francois? He'll give it to me straight." In came that awkward silence once again. Sleek gave Sal the same suggestive look as before. It didn't take long for Salvador to figure out that tonight was a Friday night. "No..." Sleek could not answer with words, all he could do was shake his head. "But h-..." "I met him at his home just as you requested. He went upstairs. Minutes passed, I got curious. Found him lying on the floor with three bullets in his chest. The window was open. I ran as soon as I saw the body." Sal's mouth stood agape. A cold sweat begin to break. He was on the verge of losing it. "You didn't...tell anyone...did you?" "Salvador, I'd hate to think that you were more concerned about your status with authorities than about the very man who tried his damnedest to help you preserve your life." "No...no. Of course not. Francois was a good man. I can't...I can't fathom it. Who is this person. Why do they have a vendetta against me. What did I ever do to anyone." Sleek grew timid. "Dammit, Salvador...be real with yourself. You've killed your own employees for a chance at making money. You've dealt with illegal Drug Lords, Kingpins, and even persuaded Judges with money. Your father told you that'd you have to pay before you put a bullet through his heart. David Michaels, owner of your biggest rival in All Star Wrestling, told you that you'd have to pay before you slit his throat. The city of Black Diamond told you that you'd have to pay after you swayed the Judge's decision with your almighty dollar. What haven't you done to people?" "I never professed to be a saint, Sleek."
