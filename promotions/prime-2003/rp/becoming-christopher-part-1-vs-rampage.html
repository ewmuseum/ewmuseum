Becoming Christopher Part 1 [vs. Rampage]

2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-5-2005 Becoming Christopher Part One Pain. That’s what I remember of that night in Canada. A pain like I had never felt before. I also seemingly remember seeing headlights heading right at me. And then the pain. After that, I don’t remember much. My memory is very iffy concerning that fateful night. The doctors all say that it’s traumatic shock amnesia. Maybe it’s a good thing I don’t remember much about it. After all, Boda had nightmares after being crucified by Vampir and The Dark Age. I’d hate to see what kind of nightmares the sissy would have had while recovering from damn near being killed for the third straight time. [Fade In] A dark room, with only the flickering of light emitting from a candle in the corner. A silhouette is forecasted on the wall opposite the candle. And even though a shadow is normally larger than its source, that is not the case in this instance, as sitting in the corner, next to the candle, is none other than Christopher. “Tropical Turmoil went exactly as I had envisioned. I played my cards just right, and they fell in order. First the fans believing Boda was returning. Dammit, that was hard enough to do, to pretend I was Boda. I played him for about 15 minutes, and I don’t like the guy anymore now than I did before.” Christopher stops talking and grabs his head. “GOD…. WHEN WILL THESE HEADACHES CEASE.” [Fade Out] Let’s see, what else do I remember about the night Boda died. I seem to recall Kano being there. And he was crying. Calling out for him. Some tough guy you are Jon. That’s why I blew you off the other night at Turmoil. I remember you being soft. The pain was immense though. Worse than the headaches I suffer from now. The ride to the hospital is a blur, except the occasional electrical shock which threw his eyes open. And when that happened, there again was Kano, crying like a little bitch. [Fade In] Christopher releases his head, the headache seemingly gone. The big man breathes deeply a couple of times. “Little bitch….. “ Christopher smiles, the glow from the candle throwing a shadow off his lips that gives him an evil grin. “I played Hoyt Williams like a little bitch. I opened myself to him, let him reach out and teach me. He’s the real reason I exist today. That sissy Boda was just going to retire. But instead, Hoyt brought me to life. Hoyt pulled me out of the darkness that was Boda’s soul. And now…. Now I am unleashed upon PRIME.” A crack of thunder erupts in the distance. Christopher stands and walks over to a window. Looking outside, he sees lighting in the distance. “Like that impending storm, I will wreak havoc in PRIME. And it starts this week with you…. Scott Mathews.” Christopher turns around and returns to where he was sitting moments ago. The flame of the candle nearly extinguishes itself as the big man sits. “For you, you have the unluckiest draw of them all in this Jewel in The Crown tournament. You have to face me.” Christopher leans his head back and chuckles. “But, I got a secret for you Scott. I don’t give a damn about winning this tournament. Boda would have cared. Boda would have cut a few promos about how this is ‘his time’. But we all know what happened to Boda. Don’t we. I’m not going to come out and tell anyone that ‘this is my time’. That’s not my style. I don’t give a shit about winning a tournament. But, I do have a desire to prey on weaknesses. And well, Scott, you’re going to experience that first hand this coming week.” The massive Christopher leans his head over to the side, looking out the window as more lighting and thunder explode outside. Suddenly, the big man starts to blink his eyes, then closes them, placing his hands on his skull again. “ARRRRRRRR…….” [Fade Out] Kano visited him a lot in the hospital. I guess after years of fighting, Kano wanted to be ‘brothers’ again. And that would probably have happened if I didn’t come into existence. All his career, Boda needed to leech off others in order to be successful. Not I. Hoyt’s teachings showed me that I don’t need to be second fiddle to anyone. That’s what I took away from his lessons at least. Hoyt was probably just hoping that I would side with him, to make him better. Hoyt thought he was making Boda better…. He had no idea he was creating Christopher. You see, while Kano visited Boda during rehab, Hoyt was visiting Boda also. Jon wanted to help Boda return to top form. So did Hoyt. Slowly Boda rehabbed. Slowly Christopher was born. [Fade In] Picking himself up off the floor, Christopher throws the chair across the room. “Fucking headaches. No one told me I’d be suffering from these a lot.” Collecting himself, Christopher dusts off his clothes, then sits in the other chair in the room. “Now, Scott, like I was saying… this tournament means nothing to me. I’m not in it to win it, I’m in it to prey on your weaknesses. I know you’re going to want to make a name for yourself in this tournament. And you’ll probably dedicate your performance to your father. Well, that’s the weakness I’m looking for. Your caring for someone, will make you vulnerable to me. The fact that you are as green as the Jolly Green Giant, will make you that more vulnerable. The mere idea that you are sitting at home, wondering how you are going to match up with Boda Version 2.0, will make you vulnerable. Because I don’t care about anyone, I’ve got more experience in the ring than you’ve got time stroking your little dick…… and I’m NOT a repackaged version of Boda….. I’m better.” Christopher grabs the candle from the table and walks to the middle of the room. Lighting outside the room lights up the inside. As the room brightens, we see a burn barrel in the middle of the room. “Inside this barrel, is the remaining remnants of Boda’s career. From PCW jobber, to PCW main event and World Champion. Hall of Famer. OSW World Champion and even Universal Champion of PRIME….. The entire career of Boda is represented and tossed into this barrel. Scott, like I said, I am more than a repackaged Boda. I am what laid dormant inside him for so many years. And tonight, before I come to ReVolution and prey on your weaknesses…. I want you to share in saying a final goodbye to the piece of shit that was Boda.” Dropping the candle inside the barrel, the contents erupt in flames. “One life had to end for another life to be born. And like the Phoenix rising from the fiery ashes, I have been reborn. Stronger, Smarter, more dangerous. Boda is dead…. I am Christopher…. And I’ll see you in Miami. The scene fades out as Christopher stands behind the barrel, watching the remnants of Boda’s career go up in flames. A sadistic smirk pierces Christopher’s lips as we fade to black.
