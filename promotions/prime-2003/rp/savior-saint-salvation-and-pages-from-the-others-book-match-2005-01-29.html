Savior, Saint, Salvation and Pages from the others' book (match)

29 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 29th Deliver me out of my sadness.Deliver me from all of the madness.Deliver me courage to guide me.Deliver me strength from inside me. Sarah Brightman's operatic voice echoed throughout the dimly lit room, fluttering around a small and warm source of light that bounced fitfully off of bright red locks... Hazelnut Coffee - rather, the chemically-balanced scent of it - filled the air and Amy smiled despite her awkward position. She was sitting in apparently uncomfortable jeans on the unforgiving, however carpeted, floor of the apartment she shared with her... her best friend. One arm was strewn across the coffee table with her head resting in the crook of that elbow as she watched the light of the flame dancing in the holder of the candle she'd just lit. This was surprising to her if not only because the candle holder had the word 'LOVE' glued onto it with sequins that cast a sihlouette around the small fire, reminding her how many burn scars both flames had left over her mere twenty two years. Alongside that burning sensation was an Indy Wrestling Insider magazine that had, by no accident, been left open and read over inumerable times. She wasn't interested at all in the current state of the business or what anybody thought of these 'rising stars'... something else had drawn her to pause when passing the newstand during her morning breakfast ritual. It had been the sight of herself that stopped her heart. For a wishing moment, her eyes had brightened at the prospect of a positive review when instead, all that greeted the redhead was one of her finer photos... overshadowed only by the 'Fallen Stars' headline printed bodly above. In the following pages, she was torn to shreds with the author using anything from drugs to sexual orientation to fitness to hair dye in an attempt to dismantle the rekindled redhead. She was labelled as a tiny taste of fame that wasted itself, now only infamous for the lines she could be seen sniffling up at only the seediest of bars. It cut deeply, very deeply, but she had managed to look past it for one simple fact: Two weeks sober. Fourteen days and about three hours now, she hadn't touched anything stronger than a Tylenol or Nyquil. This magazine didn't know shit about that, did they? No. She was out because she'd been hit on the head after she lost to Hoyt Williams of all people. Instead of letting him run out and slam her for her faults, PRIME allowed a separate attack... and she couldn't say she was ungrateful. It was a halfway decent cover up to sober up, but she couldn't even say she was doing this by her own will... Jeph was the driving force. Her closest friend, he was pushing her more and more each day - testing her boundaries by trying to force jogs and full breakfasts - but Amy was being herself and resisting as much as possible... just not enough to entirely ward off his golden-hearted measures. As far as matters of the heart went, though...well...nothing. It was only in that brief moment of thought that Amy realized the issue they'd been skirting; That admission and kiss in the church confessional and all of the nervous laughter they'd brushed the moment off with. 'Really, why am I romantically retarded? Am I just doomed to act too blind to date anything but pricks?' Even she couldn't understand why the topic went unmentioned for so long. She did have her excuses, what with the withdrawals and fits of illness, but that hadn't at all explained why Jeph was so silent. He hadn't spoken of himself all week. Hadn't really done anything all week, actually... whereas her week had been spent wondering how in god's name she was going to come out on top of a Revolution clusterfuhh-- ''Stop thinking about it,'' came Jeph's intrusion, ''You're letting fucking wrestling consume you, dude...that's not cool.'' ''It's my job.'' 'It's even starting to be fun again...' He was entering the room, having seen the little redhead half-asleep on the coffee table,''Then don't be like the rest of the world, leave your job at the office and keep that shit away from home.'' Amy cringed when she considered the consequences of doing the usual and not thinking this match through. Insult and injury were the inevitable outcomes...neither her ego or her bones could withstand another fracture. ''I have to plan it. I've lost to Hoyt once before, and I don't need that embarassment again. They're all dirty players,'' she sighed, ''I mean, The HIT...Vampir?! I know you hate this shit and never followed, but I've watched those two come up and that's one hell of a fight, not to mention their partners.'' Another sigh and she continued, ''My approach needs to change. Gotta avoid being clean about things...'' ''Well, you've got the dirty part down!'' The redhead slid away from the table and against the seat of the couch behind her with another scoffing expression, ''I'm serious, Jeph.'' His eyes rolled. For the first time, he saw the magazine that sat on the table Amy been hovering over and swept it off into the corner of the room with one aggressive swoop of his arm, ''And I'm not, so get with the fuckin' program, Ames! I can't believe I'm telling you to lighten up for once. Just forget about the damn match and...GOD, I don't know - step off your guard.'' For no reason she could find, Amy's eyes were tearing up slightly. Were they just dry? She was struggling with any straight emotions, and had been since going clean - having become far too accustomed to controlling and dictating her own feelings with foreign substances, cocaine serving as just one of many. Any serious conversation with Jeph always began with cruel humor or direct attacks, that was just the way it was...but she did NOT like having a voice raised at her. He knew that. ...So why, exactly, was he playing this game? She'd been concentrated to a fault, yes... but that didn't warrant a sudden aggression. Unless... ''Loosen up for what? You just want me to forget about this match and what, Jeph... deal with what I pushed aside so I could concentrate on not getting my ass handed to me again this week? Great idea. I'll just go back to dealing with the stress like I did in that church. Remember that, popping whatever I could into me until I went fuckin' comatose? Yeah, I'll get right on that so we can settle whatever's making you such a dick lately.'' Jeph's shoulders slumped. He looked at her with frustration, rage, disappointment... ''I know what you're onto, so go ahead and say it.'' A moment of stiff and awkward silence ensued. Amy was fidgeting with her shirt's long sleeves, rolling them up to reveal scattered tattoos as she shifted her weight up and onto the couch with a sigh. She paused a moment and close her eyes tightly, opening them to find Jeph in the same spot... arms still folded, expectant. ''Okay,'' she swallowed hard and didn't break eye contact, ''I'll leave wrestling until it's time to wrestle.'' ''And...?'' Sigh. ''...And we've both been thinking about it all week, so why don't you do the talking?'' Jeph smiled smugly and slid himself down onto the opposite side of the couch, resting his arms along its back, ''I take pretty pictures for a living...'' 'Here it comes. I backed off, so he'll play it up...' thought Amy, 'Blah blah blahblahblah...how'm I gonna cut this short?!' Meanwhile, Jeph was still talking. ''You beat the shit outta people - tell me which one of us would be better at confronting a subj--'' He was feeling cocky, so Amy's interruption came as quite a surprise. He'd expect to milk a reaction out of her, but found it fairly quickly when she leaned forward and kissed him. Part of her wanted to giggle when she felt the split that remained in his bottom lip, compliments of her foot the week prior... but there it was. Somewhere, far away from her consciousness, she'd been waiting to do that for a long while and when she withdrew, it was to give him a hug; Not exactly what was to be expected from such a normally openly sexual person. ''I like you too much for my own good,'' she grinned and spoke into his shoulder, her voice muffled by the fabric of his t-shirt, ''But I've got a few good chokeholds in store if you fuck this up.'' He squeezed her back, ''I only need one chance.'' When Amy pulled out of the hug and leaned back to her own section of the couch, she could see the breadth of her best friend's smile. They were both smiling. This was... unusual, to say the least. Amy Campbell wasn't used to smiling at anything but an unconscious, bloodied body. ''Things'll never be the same, y'know,'' she began nervously, ''I mean... kissing, sleeping together? That changes shit. You know...being more than friends and stuff, if one of us fucks this up, we're not gonna be best buds anymore. We won't be anything when this ends.'' Another unusual sight - Amy Campbell, nervous. Jeph hadn't seen this side of her in so long, it was actually refreshing... but served to leave a knot in his stomach at the same time. He was afraid of screwing up. He'd wanted this for so long now, how could he NOT be afraid? Instead of showing that fear, though, he took a page from Amy's book and completely ignored his brain's warning signs. Forget nervousness, be a man... right?! For her right now, you have to be. She's weak... possibly weaker than ever, especially struggling to pull through addiction. When Jeph realized he'd been thinking and NOT listening, he blinked. She was still talking... ''Amy?'' ''Mmhmm?'' ''Shut up.'' They both smiled. This was the start of something great... ...That is, if Amy survived wrestling this week and if he survived Amy this week. This relationship had come to a head the same way many things in Campbell's life did - it stumbled, stuttered and stood around awkwardly until somebody shoved it off the turnbuckle. Now, hopefully, it would fly. ...All of my life, I was in hiding,Wishing there was someone just like you.Now that you're here, now that I've found you,I know that you're the one to pull me through.
