When One Door Closes...

2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06-05-07 As Jasmine charged at the man in the ring, she ducked his clothesline attempt, and tripped his legs, taking him down hard with the Running STO she likes to call "Every Dark Cloud". She floats over and hooks the far leg, as the referee drops to the amt. His hand makes a strangely loud slam as he pounds the mat. "ONE!" Jasmine pulls his knee closer to his face as she grasps the cradle. "TWO!!" The sweat formed around Jasmine's brow as she strained to keep her opponent bundled, now feeling him trying to escape. Just one more second, just one more. -BAM!- "THREE!!!" Jasmine releases the hold as she stands up, and the referee pulls her hand into the air. She can hear the bell in the background, and in the distant part of her mind, she can hear the ring announcer calling her the winner of the match. She runs and jumps on the second turnbuckle, as she jets her hands into the air, and waves to the fans. Everyone is celebrating the victory as she can hear the people start chanting her name... JASMINE! JASMINE!... JASMINE!!... JASMINE!!! "Jasmine... JASMINE!!" She felt her shoulder shake as Rebecca shook her back into the real world. She shook her head a bit, and looked around as she noticed the classroom turning towards her, all eyes on the purple and silver hair of Ms. Drogan. "Did I fall asleep?" Rebecca just shakes her head in disbelief. She looks at Jasmine, looking embarrassed to even know her. "Fall asleep? You've been mumbling in your dreams this whole time. You just Knocked over the empty desk next to you." Jasmine turns, and sure enough, toppled over on the ground lays a vacant desk, obviously the victim of one of Jasmine's attacks. "I'm sorry, I just didn't get any sleep last night... I wasn't focused on the teacher and dozed off." Class moved on as usual, and Jasmine fought off her lingering feeling of exhaustion to stay awake in class. As they were dismissed, Jasmine gathered her things and headed towards her car. Rebecca tailed closely behind, trying to catch up to her roommate. "Jas! Wait up! Jasmine!" Jasmine stopped, mainly because she had reached her car, and was now depositing her things int eh trunk, but Rebcca's yell was part of it. "Sorry, Becca, couldn't wait up, still have half of a DVD to watch." Rebecca shakes her head, yet again looking more like a disapproving parent than a roommate. "Were you up watching those wrestling DVDs again? Jasmine, you have to let it go, you had your time, you did a great job, now it's time to make a career for yourself, and get an education." Jasmine shook her head, and cracked an almost sarcastic smile. "Becca, i was studying for Geometry last night. I only watch those videos on nights I have free time, don't worry. Just because I still enjoy a good wrestling show doesn't mean I'm going to drop school and go back to FUSE. I'm focused on school, 'Becca, I just need to learn to get more sleep." Jasmine grins at Rebecca as she jumps in her car, not really giving Becca a chance to respond. Before She could even process it, the next thing Rebecca heard was the engine of Jasmine's Chevy Cavalier, as she got ready to head home. ~*~ "ONE…TWO…THREE!!!" The crowd chants along with the referees hand, as the match comes to a close. DING! DING! DING! "SHE DID IT!" Scott Ambrose, FUSE's play-by-play guy announces, excited for Jasmine. "Are you kidding me?" Hunter Jones, color commentator, cries out. The crowd explodes as Jasmine rolls to the side of the ring, her mouth gaping. The referee holds three fingers in front of her face as he thrusts the CUSTOM Chaos Title forward in her direction. "Your winner of the match and NEEEEEEEEEWWWWWW CUSTOM Chaos Champion, "The Silver Lining" JASMINE DROOOOOOGGGGAAANNNNN!" The ring announcer, Skye yells. As Jasmine steps onto the second rope, holding the FUSE Custom Chaos title above her head two vertical lines appear by her head and everything freezes. The video stops playing, and we see Jasmine Drogan, watching the television, a longing look in her eyes. She hears a knock at the door, and rushes to flip the screen off, and lift up any text book she can find. "Come in!" Becca slowly opens the door to Jasmine's room, as she walks over to Jasmine, taking a seat next to her on her bed. "Whatcha ya doing?" Becca says with an overly innocent tone of voice. "Just studying, trying tog et ready for a test." Jasmine lies nervously as she looks at her friend. "Then why are you looking at -MY- Psychology book? You don't have that class until the spring." Jasmine chuckles nervously, as she puts the book down. "I'm trying tog et a head start?" she questions, as if Rebecca would believe her make-shift fib. Rebecca reaches over and flips on the tv, seeing the paused scene of Jasmine becoming the FUSE Custom Chaos champion. She looks at the look of pure joy on Jasmine's face as she hoists the belt high into the air. "What did it feel like?" Becca questions. Jasmine looks over at her, having put down the wrong text book. "What do you mean?" Jasmine couldn't quite put her finger on the question. Rebecca smiles as she explains. "When that woman announced you won the title? What did it feel like to have everyone there happy for you, and cheering as you won? What did it feel like?" Rebecca's seemingly simple question grew into something deeper. "It's nothing I can explain, Becca. When that referee told me I won, and handed me the championship, It was worth everything. It was worth leaving school, it was worth getting my face cut open by half the men I fought, it was worth every last minute of training I had endured. Standing there, knowing everybody wanted me to be their champion was possibly the greatest feeling I've ever had. I wouldn't trade it for anything in the world." Jasmine nodded as she spoke, almost as if agreeing with herself. She was beaming with satisfaction, all the while a small tear began to shore up in her eye. "Thinking about what I did that day, and then thinking about how I left all those people that were happy for me, it keeps me up at night. I keep thinking that I might be making a mistake. I keep thinking I should go back. I keep thinking I let people down." The smile fades from her face a bit as she says this, the reality of her actions setting in. She had left FUSE as Custom Chaos Champion, opting to pursue an education, rather than jump off the ropes, and bleed on canvas. She vacated the very same championship she was so happy to have won, and left FUSE high-and-dry, without a champion for over a month. She never forgave herself. "Jasmine, any of those people that were actually happy for you, that were actually cheering because they were glad you had succeeded, none of them could've been disappointed. They should be glad you're doing anything to make yourself better, and to further your life. If they were happy for -YOU- and not just the good guy in the match, then you didn't disappoint anybody." Rebecca placed her hand on Jasmine's shoulder as she turned off the tv, getting that image out of Jasmine's face. It wouldn't take it out of her mind, but she figured it might help a little. "Jasmine, listen, you decided that part of your life has passed. Don't be sad about it. Be glad you had the chance to live your dream. Now, you need to study, and focus on on the goal at hand. You're not going to get by sleeping during class. Come on, where's that smile I know so well? Come on, aren't you supposed to be 'The Silver Lining'? Look like it." Funny enough, Becca's little comments did cause a smile to pass across the face of 'The Silver Ling'. She grinned and nodded her head,agreeing with everything Rebecca just said. "Thanks Becca, I needed that. Now it's time to focus, and reach the goal." Jasmine smiled, as she picked up the correct book, and flipped to her homework. Rebecca, satisfied that she had pull Jasmine out of her slump backed out of the room. She smiled as she closed the door, leaving jasmine to her studies. ~*~ The next morning, Jasmine and Becca both woke at the same time, and Jasmine seemed to back to her old self. The two were laughing and joking, and Jasmine seemed focused on her European History test. The two shared some bagels and cream cheese, and Becca offered Jasmine a ride to their morning class. "No, I'll drive myself, I want to go by the book store and get some things for class. I'll probably come in a bit late, so don't worry about me." Becca looked a bit confused as she listened. She nodded her head, not really able to complain about missing the first few minutes of a class that doesn't actually get started until about half an hour in. Becca nodded and said good bye as she left for school. Jasmine watched the door close, and then went back into her room. She grabbed her things, oddly enough assembled into a small knapsack, as she pulled out a notebook. She leaned on the kitchen counter and scribbled something down on a piece of paper just before tearing out the sheet, and leaving it on the counter. She walked out the door and we could hear the sound of a deadbolt behind her. ~*~ There's a difference between "a few minutes late" and "not showing up at all." Rebecca rushed home, after class, worried sick about Jasmine. Her voice mail picked up after the first ring, signifying to Rebecca that Jasmine had either turned her phone off, or it had been broken somehow. She rushed through the door and threw her stuff to the side, trying to figure out what to do. As she put her books onto the counter, she noticed a piece of notebook paper, folded with her name on the outside. A sickening feeling flowed through her body. "Dear Rebecca, I'm sorry I had to lie to you, but you'd never let me go through with this if I told you. You were right, it is time to get focused, and aim for the goal. I have to do what makes me happy in life, because if I'm not happy, money and success mean nothing. When you get home from class, I'll already be in the air, on a flight to Houston, Texas. I made a few phone calls, and called in a few favors, and I've got a try-out for another wrestling company, called PRIME Wrestling, on Wednesday Night. Don't worry about me, I'll take care of myself. Again, I'm sorry i lied, but I know you'd be able to talk me out of this if I had told the truth. Remember if someone cares, they'll be happy that I'm doing what's best for me. Leaving this undone won't make life any better, and it certainly isn't best. Thanks, 'The Silver Lining' P.S. - You can watch, if you want. I'll be on FX on Wednesday night. It's called "PRIME Presents Revolution." I've got the opening match against someone named Sonny Silver. I hope you'll watch." Rebecca looked at the note in shock. She put her phone down as she sat on the couch, not knowing what to think. She shook her head as she dropped the note, and tried again in vain to reach Jasmine's cell phone.
