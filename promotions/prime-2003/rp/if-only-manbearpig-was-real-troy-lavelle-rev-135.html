If Only ManBearPig Was Real... (Troy/Lavelle, ReV 135)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Before ReV 135. Pick a day... Pierce Lavelle moved through security with a slight irritation flowing through him. Stepping around a group of tourists as they struggled with their luggage, he hoisted his carry-on over his shoulder and place a headset into his ear. The small blue light began to flicker and he pressed a button to answer the call. "Hello?" he said as he hurried through the sea of bodies. "Hi kid, where are you?" replied Tyler Rayne in a static-filled voice, barely audible. "Hello Ty, I’m at the San Francisco International airport, just got in. What’s up?" "Did you get a new PSP?" Pierce laughed and patted his back pocket. "Yeah I did, although the sales assistant wasn’t pleased when I handed back the other one. I said I was attacked by a ruler-wielding nun. I don’t think she believed me." He could hear Rayne laughing amidst the sound of car horns. "You buy a case for it?" "After what happened to the other ones, I think I had to!" Pierce grinned as he handed his drivers' license to a security official and moved through the corridor until he found his waiting area. He stopped suddenly and looked out the large bay windows in awe. There before him, snow dotted the runway, trickling off the windows and fluttering weightlessly in the air. "You there?" "Eh … it’s July, right?" inquired Pierce. "Yeah kid, it’s July. Why?" "Well … it’s snowing …" The small television balanced in the corner of the lobby alerted the room to a news bulletin. The large red and white letters belonging to CNN announced a comment from Al Gore. Pierce groaned, regretting wearing jeans and flip-flops, as he looked out to the white blanket draped across the landscape. "This freak snow storm in the San Francisco area is a clear example of the climate crisis the world is facing..." Al Gore began. "Rayne, I’m going to have to call you back. Tell Lisa I’m late 'cause of a snow storm!" "Like she’ll believe me!" "Well, charm her! You're good at stuff like that!" He grumbled, took the earpiece out and approached the boarding desk where an airline representative stood in front of him. "I’m guessing the flight to Port of Portland is going to be delayed?" "Yes sir, we have to wait for the snow storm to clear. Visibility is poor." Pierce sighed. "My boss is going to kill me." Before the rep could answer, he turned his back and spotted in the distance a familiar face, her eyes glued to the television with a displeased look as Al Gore continued his climate control narrative. Lindsay Troy could only shake her head in complete disbelief at not only the weather, but at the overzealous, twenty-something guy standing next to her. He prattled on to some unknown person on his cell phone, his eyes lit up like it was Christmas morning and all the presents under the tree were for him. "Dude! DUDE! It's freaking SNOWING here, man! Snowing in San Fran! And you'll never guess who's right beside me!" It was times like these that Troy wished she was blessed with superpowers that would make her invisible or, at least, able to blend in with backgrounds. Her height was always a dead giveaway for recognition, then her curly hair was next. Despite her more casual attire when hanging around the arena, she was always dressed smart for travel and today she was thankful she chose a pants suit and pumps over a skirt and peep-toed shoes, given the bizarre conditions outside. "DUDE! It's Lindsay freaking Troy! Oh man, she's friggin' tall in person!" Twenty-Something rambled, completely oblivious that Troy could hear every word. She wished he'd either move away to continue the conversation or just talk right at her instead of to 'DUDE!'. "I hear she's single now too...." That was Lindsay's cue to move out of the crowd, and she politely excused herself through the rest of the gathering gaggle. "You'd think I was a Beverly Hills Bimbo the way people are talking about this..." said her mind in a reproachful tone. With furrowed brows, Pierce dug his hands into his back pockets, feeling a little uneasy because in front of him stood a slightly annoyed Lindsay Troy. He knew her as a woman of respect, honor and the new queen bee around PRIME! He stopped walking when he saw her approach, making a break from the flirtatious man on the cell phone. "Lovely weather ... isn't it," he said, catching Troy's attention. "Oh..." her head jerked up and her eyes fell on Lavelle standing in front of her. "Yeah, crazy, huh? You'd think it was January up North." He smiled. "And I wore flip-flops. Thought that smart, being in San Fran." "I guess that makes it easier going through security. You don't have to have your shoes scrutinized down to the minute details. Not so helpful when traveling through snow, though." "Well I had difficulty. Couldn't get them off the damn official. He wanted an autograph. Blackmail! Then it would've been bare feet and snow and Lisa Tyler would be really screwed," he said with a smile. "I see you had your own little fan following you …" he indicated toward the enthusiastic man still on the cell phone. "Yeah..." she looked back over her shoulder to Twenty-Something, still yammering away. "I had gotten used to it, but lately people seem to be making a bigger deal over things. The Public Eye never shuts." "I never get used to it," he admitted. "I guess I should properly introduce myself since we've never actually talked. It's nice to meet you Lindsay Troy." "Ah, so we're doing this all official-like," she smirked. "Nice to meet you too, Pierce. Although, by extension, we have been introduced. Seems like one of your buddies can't keep my name off his tongue." "Well I thought I'd be polite. Buddies?! Are you talking about the three little pigs that have been ruining my life or the b-with-an-itch boss?" he asked, sitting down he ignored the CNN report and looked at the snow. "Lisa's not so bad. Then again, Lisa's a lot like me so I guess my tolerance level is higher than the rest of the roster's." She took a seat next to him and put her feet up on her rolling luggage. "I was talking about a relatively new friend of yours. Seems to think he's hot shit." Lavelle furrowed a brow. "Damn … you're as cryptic as Lisa. To be frank, she scares the crap out of me. I think she has it out for me." He paused momentarily. "You talking about Rayne or Sloan, cause neither have mentioned you to me … recently." "Sloan's got his head too far up Snow's ass to be concerned with lil' ol' me," Troy quipped. "I was referring to Rayne." Lavelle nodded his head, chucking his rucksack to the floor he removed the PSP from his pocket and smiled. "Yeah, I haven't noticed much, to be honest. I'm a little preoccupied with this. I will apologize if you have the wrong impression of me. Haven't exactly been Mr. Good Guy since all this started with Dusk …" he said and handed Troy his PSP. "If you get bored on the flight. Just don't ask me what all the buttons do!" "I'm one of the last people lately who should be the judge of the goodness in others." She examined the gadget in her hands before handing it back to Pierce. "I'm not much of a video game kinda gal. I can beat the Codemaster at Marvel vs. Capcom and that's about it. Four years later and he still hates me for it." He nodded his head and took back the PSP. His eyes wandered to the snow storm; it was coming down heavy. "So teaming with me isn't a problem then, what with you being sort'a friends with Dusk and Nova." "Please," Lindsay waved her hand dismissively, then resigned herself to looking out at the swirls of white. "I'm the Universal Champ and you've got the Intense title. It only makes sense that Lisa would put us together in order to smack around all the others. Besides," she let a toothy grin cut into her explanation, "I figure you're the best out of your blood-feud bunch to help me end Caesar's tag team win streak. 'Friendly competition' and all." Lavelle frowned. "Heck. Does this mean I have to actually work?" He smiled and looked around; the Gate had begun to fill. "Might actually mean you'll have to curse, too." "Shoot. Really? Cause I was trying to mend my rebellious ways …" he said, coyly. "Pierce, something tells me the rest of the guys in this tag match won't let you do that," Troy states with an innocent wink. Pierce laughed. "Oh. Hell. I guess I wasted money on the cuddle toy and chocolates for Dusk, didn't I?" he replied with a grin. "What about Ferguson and Nova? Something tells me it's not just going to be the fantastic four arguing." "They won't kill each other just yet. Both of them want to leave something in the tank for Colossus. I'm done trying to play mediator, though." He nodded his head. "True. Guess that leaves all the fun for us then, right…" he said with a smile. Suddenly a loud noise broke the silence, he eyes widened as he pulled out his phone. "Oh crap … It's Lisa. Quick. You answer. She'll never believe me that I'm stuck in a snow storm …" Lavelle handed Troy his ringing cell phone. She raised an eyebrow in confusion. "Come on. You're the respectful Universal Champion and I'm the big idiot that broke three plasma televisions two weeks ago," Lavelle pleaded, pouting his lower lip. Troy shrugged her shoulders, then flipped open the phone. "Lisa! Darling! How's my favorite boss in the history of ever?! " "Troy? Is that you? What the hell are you doing with Lavelle's phone?!" "The kids are well then, I take it?" "Knock it off, Troy. Where's Lavelle?" "Calling to check up on Pierce, Lisa? My my, I didn't know the two of you were dating. I guess you caught that 'Interviewer Flirting Bug' that's been going around the locker room..." Pierce snickered while, on the other end of the phone, Lisa Tyler fumed. "Unless you want me to dock your appearance pay seventy-five percent this week, you'll tell me where Lavelle is." Lindsay made the 'Scott Hall Scared Face' to Lavelle, who continued to snicker. "He's sitting right next to me at the airport, Lisa. Did you know that it's snowing out?" "I'm perfectly aware of the weather conditions. I just wanted to make sure he wasn't out turning it red with Shakur's, Dusk's, or Winters' blood." "Well, that wouldn't be very nice of him now would it?" "Just put him on the phone, Troy." Lindsay hands the phone back to Pierce. "Looks like she wants to chat with you after all." Pierce frowned and held the phone a slight distance from his ear. "Afternoon …" "Next time answer the phone yourself, Lavelle." "Well, see, I was busy trying to decide on whether to give Dusk a day treatment at a spa or a ticket home!" "I better have your word that you won't cause any trouble this week." Pierce struggled to hold back a laugh. "I'll be on my best behavior. I promise … I'm afraid you're breaking up …" "What?! The reception is fine." "WHAT?! I can't hear -" he shut the phone and let out a sigh. "I know … I have the mentality of a ten year old." Lindsay gave a knowing smile. "That still makes you mentally older than the majority of people we're fighting this week." Lavelle laughed. "You got an answer for everything, Lindsay. But what about Sonny?" Her eyes clouded over then, and if Pierce ever had to describe them to anyone else, he'd say that they turned as gray as the clouds from which the snow fell. "That answer will come in time," was all she said.
