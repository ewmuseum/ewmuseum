The Twelve Steps of Recovery: Step Two [Part 2 of 12]

11 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 11th, 2010 Intro……   There are 12 steps to recovering from addiction. These 12 steps have helped millions of alcoholics and addicts overcome the demons of addiction. I’ve addressed Step 1 and admitted I was powerless to the disease. Now we move onto Step 2 of the 12 steps and how it helped me overcome my addiction to drugs and alcohol.…… April 20th, 2008 I was entering the second week of my rehab stint, which meant I’ve taken and overcome the first step of AA. I was still having what many called ‘dope dreams’….. nightmares based on the withdrawals of the drugs. These dreams usually consisted of me being chased by a drug dealer, or me fighting for my life, or me in jail because I killed someone. And those were just the calm dreams. I believe it was my conscious talking to me. Many dreams made no sense at all, but had something to do with the drugs I was no longer taking. I was still having doubts about being at The Spirit of Life. I still didn‘t know how this small recovery center was going to make a difference in my life. Oh how I wanted to believe it, but I was still cynical. “Good morning sunshine” Rusty pops open the back door of our room. My eyes are barely open, but I look over at the alarm clock. 6:32 am “Damn Rusty…. Come on.” I plead for Rusty to leave me alone. I’ve been here a week, and Rusty has woken me up every day. But never this early. I pull the sheets over my head and try to ignore my roommate. “Come on big man….check this out.” I huff and sigh a few times. Finally I toss the covers off and roll out of bed. Scratching my balls, I slowly walk from my bed , pass the bathroom and out the back door. With a yawn and a ‘it’s to fucking early’, I plop down on the steel folding chair outside. “Open you eyes man….look at what GOD has created for you” Rusty pokes me, then points out towards the water. Now, as a child, I was taken to church every Sunday, did Sunday school, got baptized. But as I grew older, started making more decisions, I decided I didn’t need to go to church. I stopped believing in GOD. I didn’t believe GOD would have taken my father away from meat such an early age GOD would have allowed my brother David and me to have to grow up in a ghetto, where I had to protect him everyday. GOD wouldn’t make my mother struggle to support us, having her work 3 jobs so we could get by. “Yep….it’s beautiful!” I didn’t look, I was trying to dose back off in that chair. SMACK “What the fuck was that for?” I bark at Rusty. “Chris…. Come on man. Wake up, and look.” “FINE” Now, I’ve been all over the world. I’ve seen sites that the normal human being would only see via the internet. I’ve watched the sunset over the Tibetan Mountains. I’ve seen the sunrise over the Great Wall of China. I’ve seen a lot of sunrises over my 37 years of life. Why the hell should I wake up to look at this so called GOD created sunrise. What was going to be different about this sunrise? “Damn……sorry Rusty…. That is a very beautiful sunrise.” I am stunned by the beauty of the sun rising over Lake Ida just beyond the fence that borders the recovery center. There was a certain glow, one I’ve never laid eyes on before, coming from this sunrise. “But come on Rusty…. Isn’t it a little out there to assume GOD created this?” The collective ‘sighs’ from the other residents sitting on the back porch was rather loud. For this first time since I arrived the previous week, Rusty could sense that my belief in GOD was waning. The Spirit of Life after all is a Christian Men’s Recovery Center, with the core belief that GOD can cure your addiction to drugs and alcohol. Sure, they go over the 12 Steps of AA, and the 12 Principles of AA, but the main course taught here was the Alpha Program. And the Alpha Program hammered into each and everyone of the addicts that walked into this program that GOD was to do all, end all that would help each and everyone of them overcome their chemical dependencies. And, since it’s been some 20 plus years since I last went to church, I really was not a believer. “Come on man. How can you honestly say that GOD is the reason for that beautiful sunrise? Its beautiful because of the reflections off the lake and the glow of the sky. Besides, GOD’s to busy fucking up my life to worry about some sunrise.” I began my week late rant. Now, what followed, was more than a rant about not believing in GOD. It was about not wanting to truly be in rehab. Sure, I wanted Stacey and the kids back in my life. I damn sure wanted my career back. But what the hell was becoming a man of GOD going to do for me? He’d never been there before. I grew up with nothing. I had to make the best of what I had. I became a star basketball player. I became a legend in the square circle. GOD didn’t make me these things. So how am I to believe that GOD was behind this sunrise? “Come back in the room Chris…. Let’s go over the next step, since you’re skeptical as to what is GOD’s roll in everything.” Rusty says to me as he steps into room 7. I followed closely behind him, still yearning to crawl back into the bed and ignoring what was happening around me. Back then, 2 years ago, I was going through a rough time. My life was totally insane. Hiding the drug use. Lying to cover the missing money. The cancer really took a toll on me, and I wasn’t handling it the way a real man does. I became selfish, only caring about me. My family stopped mattering to me. I was going insane, and letting everyone around me be affected. I was paranoid all the time. I didn’t sleep for weeks at a time. I was depress and suicidal. “Insanity” Rusty blurts out as I plop my big frame on the bottom bunk. “That’s what your life has become because of the addiction. But there is an answer to ending it.” “Let me guess… GOD?” I replied sarcastically. Yes I wanted to kick the drugs and alcohol. Yes I wanted to return to wrestling, to become a world champion again and be the best in the business. But I was not believing Jesus or GOD would be the solution to my problems. “Chris…. Step Two says ‘Came to believe that a Power greater than ourselves could restore us to sanity’. Now think about your addiction. Think about what you did to feed that addiction and what comes to mind.” Rusty presses on. “That’s easy…. Insane. I mean come on man….. I would find every excuse to not be with my loved ones and go see the dope man. I would stay high for weeks and not sleep for the same amount of time. This addiction beat me like Meanstreak back in PCW. Sure, I have a gorgeous wife….but I was so insane, I would stick my dick in anything with a hole and two legs. I would crawl around the floor….. Imagine Rusty, a man my size, on all fours, scouring the carpet for crack when I was out of stash. That was pretty fucking insane. I stopped taking my thyroid medicine. Not because I couldn’t afford it, but because I didn’t give a flying fuck if I lived or fucking died. I feel a constant pressure of stress and anxiety at all times. So yes, Rusty… my life was insane…. So tell me how I can become sane again.” And what happened next, pretty much turned my belief of GOD around. Rusty walked across the room, and hugged me. Then prayed for me</> “Lord, brother Chris needs your help overcoming his addiction. He’s proven that his life, the life run by drugs and alcohol, has become a bed of insanity. He’s done things he knows he shouldn’t have done, but it’s not his fault. Hen needs your help, your guidance to overcome his addiction. Father God….please reach out and lead Christopher down the right path. Return sanity to his life. In heavens name we pray, Amen.” “Amen” I added. I hadn’t said Amen in 20 something years. And then, when I opened my eyes, something felt different. I instantly felt some sort of overwhelming feeling of relief. “You know what Rusty….. Maybe I was being a dick earlier. It’s just that this is all new to me. I’m sorry and I’ll listen from here on out.” I was feeling good for the first time in a while. “Maybe GOD can restore sanity to my life.” Rusty smiles and heads out of the room towards the dining hall. “You hungry?” “I can eat man.” JUNE 11TH ,2010 Two years ago I learned that faith in Christ could help my life become normal again. I had taken step 2 of the 12 steps. In a few days, I will find out how well finding that faith helps. For the last month, I’ve been your target. For some reason, be it our paths have crossed before, or your trying to make a name for yourself, you’ve picked the biggest son of a bitch in this company. You’ve caused my life to return to insanity. You’ve played mind games with me like no one else ever has. Come UltraViolence…… I WON’T NEED MY FAITH IN GOD TO RETURN MY SANITY…… YOU’LL NEED FAITH IN GOD THAT MY INSANITY DOESN’T HURT YOU FOR WHAT YOU’VE PUT ME THROUGH…. See you in Dallas.
