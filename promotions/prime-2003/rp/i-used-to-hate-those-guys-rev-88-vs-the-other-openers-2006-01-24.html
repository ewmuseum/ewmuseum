I Used To Hate Those Guys (ReV 88, vs. the Other Openers)

24 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/24/06 I’m doing it up old-school, just for you, Keeton. Consider it your last twenty-nine birthday presents. Here we are again, and if I may request a moment’s silence as we all enjoy how devilishly cyclical life can be sometimes. … I think that’s enough. Boy, oh boy! Been a while since I sat down to conjure up an old-fashioned smack-talk goulash for my opponents, or in this case really just opponent, to chew on. I think I still have the old recipe… Nova’s Smack-Talk Goulash2/3 tablespoon Sarcastic Whip-Cracking Wit¼ cup Historical FillerA light smattering of research (use your discretion)2 dashes $$$ex Appeal (do we have two dashes?)Add one-liners for flavor You’ll forgive me if it tastes a little stale, but it’s been in the fridge for a long time. Besides, even if I took 1000 years off, my milkshake would still bring more boys to the… …hmmm. I’m gonna go ahead and just veto that one, and we can all forget that I ever planned to go there. I’m digressing! Back to what I said above, about life and cyclicality and all that good shit. I’ve been here before, methinks. Indeed, the strangest feeling of déjà vu is overwhelming me at this moment, gripping at the back of my skull like one of those “extra mouth” things the Aliens used to suck the brains out of all of Ripley’s co-workers. No takers? Meh, all the better, really. The last thing I need right now is to head off on an Aliens tangent. Cyclicality. CYCLICALITY! Gotta concentrate. Feeling scatter-brained. I’ve been here before, staring down the barrel of a war of words with “The Future,” or “The Solution,” if you want, or even my personal favorite… “The Charismatic Vacuum that Makes Carrot Top Look Like Robin Williams, and Makes Baltasar Look Like Carrot Top”…Jake Keeton. I know. It’s a little long…but delivery is half the battle. Remember that, grasshopper. Yep, I’ve done this song and dance before, but none of you here in PTC-land would probably recognize me if I could somehow pull a Donnie Darko and pop us back in time a ways. Oh, you’d know it was me, I suppose…I still had the blue bandanna and the hair, though no beard. But I might as well have been a different person. Snot-nosed, awe-struck by the guys strutting around, comfortably situated at the top in this new promotion started up by Razor Blade, while I had nothing in my foreseeable future to look forward to except mercilessly trouncing the lower-card in opening matches of AW’s Insomnia. Okay, now you have to forget everything you think you know about the “lower-card.” PRIME doesn’t have one, not in the way I’m talking about. We have guys who aren’t going to compete for the Universal Title, guys who might compete for the Universal, and guys who are competing for the Universal Title. When I talk about AW’s lower-card, I’m talking about the guys who capitalize the 2nd, 4th, and 6th letters of their wrestling monikers. I’m talking guys who steal gimmicks from failed American Gladiators contestants that also happen to correspond to indigestion commercials (read: Acid, rEfLuX, CONGESTION). So there I was, in this dismal picture I’ve just painted for you, and I released my first promo. It wasn’t great. Looking back, it really sucked. But you have to start (again, in my case) somewhere. About a day passes after its release, and then lo and behold, Jake Keeton releases a promo, and I check it out, because Jake’s a big dog, obviously a contender, and I need to see what the hype’s about… …and then I see it. A couple of lines down… … …awww, shit. I’ve told this story before. Yeah, Jake Keeton insults me, I return fire, and my career takes off. Whoopedy-fucking-doo. I can’t really do this. I can’t sit down for the smack-talk sessions anymore. I can throw a few jabs out, and I do, but I’ve outgrown this, and I’m tired of telling the same stories every time I see an old face. “Ohhh, Jake Keeton done me wrong way back in the day!” Can’t harp on it. I’m done here…with this. I can at least switch gears. Gotta take inventory here. Gotta see what I’m working with. ReVolution 88. The “Random Draw” or whatever it is…and the magic 8-Ball saaaaays… Nova/Vetra/Gamble vs. Keeton/Baltasar/El Vice Alright, I’d be lying if I said I liked my chances. Vetra’s a sloppy douche, and his win/loss record reminds me of Tyson’s in the late 90s, except Vetra never had a golden age to look back on fondly and wonder what happened. Gamble? I got nothing, really. I don’t know the guy. I won’t count on him. Well, it’s a good thing we’re only pitted against one of PRIME’s premier tag-wrestling specialists in Baltasar, right? I mean, it could be worse. We could be pitted against one of PRIME’s premier tag-wrestling…um…specialists. Ouch. But does Baltasar know, DOES HE KNOW, that he’s up against the self-proclaimed King of the Friend-Or-Foe Tag Match? I bet it would keep him up at night if he did! Read ‘em and weep, my unfortunate opposition: - PRIME ReVolution 56 (3/2/05) - Friend or Foe Tag Match - Nova & Ozric Mortimer d. Big Poppa & Dani Furher via Ozric Mortimer's pinfall on Big Poppa- PRIME ReVolution 63 (5/02/05) - Friend or Foe Tag Match - Nova & Tyler Lopez d. Peter Vetra & Jackson Darilek via Lopez's pinfall on Vetra- PRIME ReVolution 68 (6/29/05) - Friend or Foe Tag Match - Nova & Vangelus Olsig d. Killean Sirrajin & Brandon Youngblood via Nova's pin on Brandon Youngblood That’s right…I dragged a win out of Tyler freakin’ Lopez. I can make this work! I somehow managed to grab Vangelus Olsig by his silly purple-streaked hair and wring a few drops of showmanship out of him like the article of wet laundry that he so often is, trumping then-champ Sirrajin and Brandon “I’m too sexy for my hair” Youngblood. Shit, man! I toot my horn a little more like I’m doing now, and I might actually convince myself that I can lug this metaphorical U-Haul of a team to the winner’s circle! I’M GETTING PUMPED! FUCK THOSE GUYS! I’M FREAKIN’ NOVA! I’M FREAKIN’…I’m freakin’… …I’m freakin’ out of breath is what I am. Anyone got a smoke? Anyone besides the guy offering me Pall-Malls? ... Fine, I’ll take a Pall-Mall. But I’m not happy about it. Hershey Highway Hotel, Hershey, PA 3:30 AM (-10pts 4 bad joke) Nova lies on his bed, staring up at the ceiling in the darkness and watching the stream of smoke from his cigarette glow randomly in the air as flecks of light penetrate the blinds over his window. He wants a joint. I don’t know about anything right now. I’ve been a ghost around PRIME lately. I don’t fit in anymore. I don’t feel like I’m contributing the way everyone expects me to. I used to get such a rush off of shows. I used to look back on a show when I was waiting out for a taxi or something, and I’d grin, fulfilled, knowing I had done something special, something tangible that would always be there as a monument to my time here. Now I get nothing. I don’t feel like I’m doing anything that I’ll want to see years from now when I’m searching for consolation for wrecking the twilight years of my life. I can sit backstage and smoke weed and kid around and everyone will laugh, and everyone will say “Oh, that Nova, he’s a character! He’s good, too, I remember some crazy shit he did back like last summer…” But if something doesn’t change, and soon, that’s all they’ll say. “Nova, he’s really good, he did some pretty crazy shit back last summer.” I want people to say “Nova, he’s really good, he’s doing some crazy shit right now.” But what have I done? I’ve done jack shit since making my gloooorious comeback except win an uninspired Main Event that was dwarfed by a much better sub-Main Event, and I’ve been beaten by Ozric Mortimer. That’s not exactly stuff to stop the presses over, you know? I can’t keep everyone laughing forever, at least not like this. There’s more to me than joints and dick and fart jokes… …or is there? What if this is all there really is to me? Maybe I don’t have this hidden poetic depth to me that no one’s really seen yet. Maybe I’m a gimmick. … I am a fucking gimmick. And I’m living on past glories. I used to hate those guys.
