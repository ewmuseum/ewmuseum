Rippa's Diner Girl

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Earlier today Veljumin looked across the diner at the waitress. Her dirty blond hair tossed around and revealed her greenish blue eyes, which was enough to catch his eye. She tended to her customers as the Swedish behemoth looked on from afar. “Hey Ripp!” Sitting across from the blond man was his redheaded partner. Veljumin jumped a bit and looked to his coworker with poise. Scraps: You lost or somethin’, kid? This is the big show! Garbage Bag Johnny. John Rhine. Rippa: Huh? Oh. I’m just tired, I guess. Didn’t really sleep much last night. Scraps: That shitface fucktard Korver. Ruby shot milk out of her nose. Scraps: Jesus, Rube! Watch the shirt! Scraps shot up, brushed the snot-laden spray off of his ‘WICKED WAYS’ t-shirt, and then proceeded to wipe his hands on the back on one man’s back. Scraps: Sorry, sir. You’re shirt, it’s open. I’m just gonna close it for you. The Irish Lion gave a coy smile to the unsuspecting customer who had received one of the worst saucing of all time. He turned back to Veljumin. Scraps: You didn’t sleep well? What the fuck, dude? We smoked that Gotti blunt. Rippa: My point exactly, Thomas. And... we come to this diner every day, all week. And we’re always high as balls. Ruby giggled again. Her eyes squinted to the point she looked like Tila Tequila. Then they sprang open as a righteous looking Banana split hit the table in front of her. Ruby: NOM NOM NOM Scraps: Oh hell yes! Come to Papa. The waitress stared down at Scraps’ hands and maneuvered the plate containing his Belgian waffle with strawberries and whip cream around them. She even played with him, moving it left then right, and then left again before setting it down. Scraps would have won an award for playing along so well. Rippa: Excuse me, miss. The waitress looked down to Veljumin. She set down his strawberry shake and then rested a hand on her hip impatiently. Veljumin swallowed his pride and looked up. Rippa: You have the greenest, most bluest eyes I have ever seen. Her facial expression didn’t change. Scraps and ruby watched on, awestruck to the point they forgot to keep chewing the food in their mouths. Rippa: And I love them. She raised an eyebrow and then the other. A soft smile began to creep over her face. Rippa: I love you! She sighed and then laughed. She pointed to the nametag that read Tina. Tina: Name’s Tina, Big Stuff. Leave your number. We’ll chat some time when I’m not a whore. She stamped her foot down on his foot. Except it was Scraps’ foot and sent him jumping into the table and smashing his knee into the table, as well. Rippa: No, no! You don’t understand. Ruby: NOM NOM NOM Tina: Yeah, yeah. I’m just some bimbo damsel in distress working a minimum wage job and you’re going to save me, right? Rippa: Hell no! A silence flew over the restaurant. Rippa looked around. Rippa: Look, lady. I just think you are the prettiest thing I’ve since I laid my eyes on those PRIME Tag Team Titles. Tina: Prime? TAG TEAM!? OH, HELL NO! Scraps jumped around on one foot more while Ruby just downed the Banana. Tina pushes Scraps over as he gets closer to her and he is slammed into another nearby table. Rippa: No, no, no! Not, TAAG TEAM. TAG TEEEAM. Ruby: NOM NOM NOM Rippa: I wrestle for a living. That is my job, my skill. Tina gave him another raised eyebrow. She looked down at Scraps who stopped grimacing for ten seconds to shake his head in affirmation. Scraps: We are. Swear. Please. Stop hitting me. Tina looked back at Veljumin. Rippa: That’s Scraps, my partner. I’m Veljumin, you can call me V if you like. Ruby is our CFO, CEO, and President. It’s alright. Tina folded her arms over her chest as she watched each of their every move. Ruby: NOM NOM NOM Tina: So... your not going to... rape me or anything, right? Rippa shook his head fiercely against what she had suggested. Tina: And... you don’t want me to have sex with you for money, right? Scraps’ eyes opened wide as he heard his favorite words, sex and money. Scraps: What’s up now? Rippa: No, ma’am. Tina, if I may call you Tina... here’s my number. Call me. Veljumin handed her his card and thought slightly about giving her one inspirational kiss to leave her wanting more. Instead, he just picked up Scraps and helped him out. Ruby: NOM NOM NOM Ruby was done with Banana split awhile ago she’s just been uttering retarded gibberish thanks to the constant Wake ‘n Bake going since 10am. Tina: Hey, V... Rippa turned just as he was about to leave. Scraps head bounced off of the metal doorframe and he groaned into Veljumin’s chest. Tina: You think you could pick me up at Ten? Rippa’s eyes lit up. But he had to play it cool. Rippa: You gots it, babes!
