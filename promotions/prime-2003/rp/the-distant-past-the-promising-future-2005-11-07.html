The Distant Past : The Promising Future

7 November 2005

Date: 07November05 The Passion Thoughts crumbled… Feelings confused… Passion gone? The Thoughts Left untold years ago, was a time when man would do anything in their power to receive what they wanted; not what they needed. Ways of hate, angst, anguish, torment, and trickery were varied by man in order for them to receive enough essentials to live another day. Two people with a strong sense of trust within each other were not friends, they were partners. Being misguided by a so-called ‘partner’ which resulted in progress for them only wasn’t even considered backstabbing; it was considered life. The past is a very important time to understand although many will claim differently; claiming that the past no longer influences how man acts today. If hardship was a lack of prior to the present, then poverty, sin, and other demeaning acts would not exist. Isn’t it apparent that the past has a direct connection of the way people move, the way people talk, and most importantly the way people act today? What happened in the past is far more important than whatever in the future may occur. Regardless if these thoughts can not be condensed into a completely true statement, they can be understood and trusted far more than possibly the Bible. What you do today or tomorrow will never really matter. What you did yesterday already has. The Very Short Story Early November could quite possibly be the most relaxing month out of any year in the city that could easy be told to have the greatest weather year round; San Diego, California. Never ‘too hot’, rarely ‘too cold’, and just enough rain year round to please the 1.2 million people that live there. The streets are constantly busy, but never overwhelming and the tall buildings lined by the metropolitan area enlighten any human being with that ‘America’ feeling that often becomes forgotten. Connecting the busiest of streets within the great city lies a world-class airport that directs and receives flights from practically any area around the world. This evening, the San Diego International Airport is where we will find Tyler O’Neill. A man who has not been heard of for over two weeks is found sitting in a chair waiting for what is seen to be Flight 2989 from the scrolling marquee above the check-in desk. Tyler O’Neill was last seen on Revolution 79 of PRIME leaving the building with a pretty lady by the name of Amy Cambell; better known by her superstar name as Aimz. Last week during PRIME’s biggest event since Tropical Turmoil, the new superstar was never seen and records show that he never even entered the building. Reasons for the absence is not entirely certain, but the fact he wasn’t on the card o Leaning back in his chair and arms crossed, Tyler stares forward; awaiting the moment to board his flight. Tyler glances at his watch and- “Now boarding, flight 2989…” Tyler in one swift move sits up, grabs his bag and makes his way to the desk. He slows down and nods to a middle aged woman and her son and lets them pass in front of him. Standing about seven people back, Tyler holds his ticket in his right hand and slings his backpack over his left arm. Within minutes Tyler walks up to a young man at the gate who takes his ticket and scans it under a computer. The man smiles and returns the ticket back to Tyler. “Enjoy Milwaukee.” With a disgruntled look of how fake happy the man was acting, Tyler nods his head and walks through the gate towards his flight. Revolution 80…just one more chance to shine.
