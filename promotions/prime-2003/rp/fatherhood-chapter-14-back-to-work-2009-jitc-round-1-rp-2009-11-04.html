Fatherhood - Chapter 14 - Back to Work (2009 JitC Round 1 RP)

4 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 4, 2009 The duffel bag sitting on the bed played the part of a basketball hoop, with a new item being tossed in its general direction every few seconds. Among the items being thrown were a razor, shaving cream, deodorant, cologne, boxers, socks, jeans, undershirts, t-shirts, and the like. Obviously, whoever was packing this bag was going on a trip. Onto that pile of belongings, white trunks with orange and aqua flowers delicately sewn onto them were thrown. A pair of old wrestling boots were tossed in that general direction. And topping the ensemble off was a pair of sunglasses. Not any ordinary pair of Oakleys, however. These sunglasses were different. These sunglasses were pearly white with orange and aqua lenses. These sunglasses were in the possession of one Bryan Dawkins. Dawkins walked toward the bag and stuffed the belongings in, checking off each item on a mental list as the item was packed. He zipped the bag closed and made his way toward the doorway, only to stop and quickly turn toward his dresser. Bryan dropped the bag and returned to the dresser to grab a picture frame. The frame had pictures of Nicholas Dawkins, his son, and Nicole Sivec, Bryan’s former fiancée. He slowly grazed his thumb across the picture of Nicole, prompting his eyes to water slightly. Taking the frame with him and placing it inside his bag, Dawkins walked out of his room and into the living room, where he was met by a going-away party of sorts. “Good luck, honey.” Sarah Dawkins rose from the couch to embrace her son; she was just getting used to him being around, and now he was leaving yet again. Watching Bryan leave was never the easiest thing for Sarah to do, because she watched her husband do the same thing for over 20 years. Her husband was also the same man who tried to keep Bryan and his son from being able to live their lives together. Sarah didn’t want Bryan to become the distant shell of a man that his father had become, and every time Bryan left the house, Sarah’s prayers began yet again. This was about more than safety for Sarah; it was about not losing her son. “Thanks mom. You know I’ll be out there doin’ my best for all of ya.” Mike and Pam Sivec both smiled at their would-have-been son-in-law as he sat down on the couch with his mother. After the child custody trial, things had become more warm and welcoming in the Dawkins and Sivec households. Everyone was getting along and it was just like coming home to a huge happy family. And to be honest, after what these families had gone through in the past year, happiness was exactly what was needed. “So how long will you be gone this time, Bryan?” While Sarah was hesitant to see her son go, Pam was the exact opposite. She knew that despite everything that had gone on, Bryan needed to return to a state of normalcy. And wrestling, well, that was Bryan’s state of normalcy. “Well mom, I’m thinkin’ I’ll ride this one out as long as I can. The Jewel in the Crown tourney starts next week, and I’m lookin’ to make it further than I did last year. I think after the injury I had over the summer, I’m lookin’ at everything a little differently. Ya know, I almost lost Nicholas this summer, and then damn near lost my job shortly after, so I’m just rollin’ with the punches.” “Like usual, right Bry?” Mike was the man’s man. A hunter, fisherman, and general jack-of-all-trades, Mike was there to be the father that Bryan never had. Mike was there to provide the fatherly support that Bryan needed over the summer, and Bryan was positive that Mike would never know how appreciative he was of that time and support. “Yeah Mike, like usual.” Bryan smiled, which was one of the best signs that he was back to normal. Even after winning the custody battle over Nicholas, Bryan wasn’t the Bryan of old, and it showed. He wasn’t smiling, upbeat, or the charismatic member of the family that he was a year ago. But now, after his injury over the summer, Bryan was finally beginning to live life the way he used to 12 months ago, and everyone around him was thankful for that. “So where are you gonna be for the next few months, Bryan? Near home?” After looking at the PRIME schedule, the roster will travel throughout the US, with no particular geographical theme to this arc. This means there will be many red-eye plane trips home for Dawkins, who will try to balance his work with being a good father to his just turned year-old son. “Well, the closest to home that I’ll be will be November 11th, when we’re in New Mexico. That’ll be the shortest trip home I take. But a couple weeks after that, I’ll be headed home from North Dakota.” Dawkins smiled and slouched back into the couch. “That’ll be a pleasant plane ride home.” There was a quick moment of silence before Bryan stood up and peered around the room. It was obvious he was looking for something, or someone, but it wasn’t clear what or who he was searching for… …until he crashed right into Bryan’s leg. “Nicholas!” Bryan picked up his 13 month old son and stared him right in the face. The two locked eyes for a moment, engaging in one of those father-son moments that could only be caught on camera to really see the beauty. Nicholas was the perfect blend of Nikkie and Bryan. He had Nikkie’s eyes, Bryan’s full smile, Nikkie’s perfectly proportioned face, and Bryan’s short, scruffy hair. One would be hard-pressed to find a more perfect duplicate of a mother and father in their child. “I can’t believe how quickly the little bruh started walking…and running! It’s like he skipped the crawling stage because he couldn’t go fast enough.” “Yeah Bryan, you’d be surprised how quickly Nicholas learned. He had his bumps and bruises along the way, but he’s like his dad. He never gave up and wouldn’t stop until he finally got it right. He’s a fighter, Bryan.” “…just like Nikkie. I still remember all of the times where we’d have an argument, or were competing for something. Even if it was something as simple as what color we wanted to paint the bedroom, or if we were playing Wii bowling, she’d never quit until she got things the way she wanted. I loved that.” “We all did, honey.” Pam’s mention of Nikkie brought a somber mood to the once happy, joy-filled room. She was missed by everyone, and Bryan took it the hardest. Knowing that she never saw her son was something that Bryan felt he would carry and take with him forever, because he felt overly responsible for the situation. For not being there for his wife, he felt as though he had failed her. And no matter how many people tried to tell him that it wasn’t his fault, he had never found it within to forgive himself. “But right now, we all need to realize that she’s with us every day, watching everything that we do, and I think we all know that she’s keeping her watch especially on Nicholas. Bryan, we all were hurt by what happened, but eventually, everyone needs to realize that Nikkie is still with us. And lucky for you, you have a son who is more like Nikkie than anyone else could ever be. And you need to love and cherish that, because you’re gonna have him connected to you until the day you die.” Bryan smiled and glanced and Nicholas again. He was fascinated with the Velcro attachment of Bryan’s duffel bag, and repeatedly opened and closed the handle. If for nothing else, at least the bag was good for Nicholas’ personal entertainment. Bryan picked Nicholas up and excused themselves from the room. “Mind if I have a few alone with my son before I leave?” With no objections from his family, Bryan and Nicholas made their way to the den. “So, little bruh, dad’s gotta leave for a while now. I’ll be back, but I have to go to my job.” Nicholas sat on his father’s lap, staring intently at Bryan. “You know what I’m sayin’, little bruh. I know a lot of people think you little bruhs can’t understand what us adults are sayin’, but I think that’s wrong. You know exactly what I’m talkin’ about, but can’t show it until the “right time”. It’s cool, I know how it goes. I was in the same position a long time ago.” Dawkins had his son’s full attention. “Bruh, look at you. Your mom would be so proud.” Bryan wiped a tear from his eyes. “Runnin’ around, drivin’ mom crazy. You’re doin’ me proud, little bruh. Only a true Dawkins would do that. I mean, tell me how many other one year olds are runnin’ around their house, tearin’ it to shreds. I don’t think you can, but lemme tell ya, little bruh. Not many. You’re a special little guy.” Out of nowhere, Nicholas reared back and smacked his father in the face, coming deathly close to his eye. “And strong, too. I guess that’s what I get for getting to close, huh? Sure are a little feisty, aren’t ya? Bruh, I’d love to take you with me, let me tell ya.” Dawkins sighed, looking at his watch, knowing that in a matter of minutes, he’ll be on his way to the airport to catch the PRIME caravan headed to New Orleans. “So daddy’s gotta go to work. I just need ya to know that every second of every day that I’m away from you, I’m thinkin’ about ya. You’re easily the most important thing in my life, and the only reason I keep going back to work is so that you can have a better life than I did, little bruh. Ya might not realize it now, but me going away to make money so that you can be happier than I was when I was younger is a good thing. I need ya to realize that I don’t like it either. If I could wrestle week-in and week-out here at home and make the kind of money I’m making out on the road, I’d do it in a heartbeat because I’d know I could come home and see my little bruh grow up before my eyes.” Dawkins sighed knowing that his time is running thin. “Sadly, little bruh, I can’t do that. If I did that, you’d have the same life that I had when I was younger, and I’d become the same bitter old man that my dad has become. And believe me, neither of us want that. You’re easily the thing that keeps me going every week, Nicholas, and I know that it’s tough now, but in the long run, this is gonna make Nicholas’ and daddy’s life a lot easier than it is now.” Nicholas was beginning to stir, and Bryan’s cab was supposed to be arriving in a matter of moments. With a heavy heart, Bryan picked his son up and embraced him, pecking him on the forehead with a kiss, and carrying him out into the living room to meet the rest of his family. “Time to go, Bryan?” Dawkins sighed, sitting Nicholas at his feet. Unaware of what was going on, Nicholas latched onto his father’s leg and peered up at his father with longing eyes. Apparently Nicholas just got what was happening, and it was evident that Bryan’s son was very against letting him leave. A tear welled up in Bryan’s eye and rolled down his cheek as he lifted his son up yet again. “Sorry, little bruh, but I gotta go.” With another kiss on the forehead, Bryan handed Nicholas over to his Sarah amidst a hug between the two. After a hug from Pam and a handshake from Mike, goodbyes were given and Bryan gave one last peck on the forehead of his son before waving goodbye. Once he was outside the door, Dawkins blessed himself and looked to the cloudy heavens, hoping that Nikkie was somewhere up there smiling down on him and hoping she thought he was making the right decision. And just like that, the clouds broke briefly, illuminating the fall foliage and sending a jolt of warmth to the earth. Bryan couldn’t help but wonder if that was a coincidence or Nikkie telling him that he was doing the right thing. Regardless, one thing was blatantly evident. Bryan Dawkins was back.
