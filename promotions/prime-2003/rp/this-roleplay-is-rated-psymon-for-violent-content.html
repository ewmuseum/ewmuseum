Up from my bed, always sleep on the left side, cross the floor OW! Damn shoes. I kick them off to the far wall. Must mean that I still wasn't sleeping alone. Man, that was a wild night. Never heard such moans coming from another person. You know how it is when you're moving with their body and there's every bit of shared pleasure. Then you hear, 'Yes. Keep going. Please.' Orgasm didn't come for like 40 minutes or so, but we were both into it every moment of the way. THAT'S how you know you've got total game. Pick myself up off of the floor, step into the bathroom. Have to open my eyes now, make sure I get my toothbrush and not the guest toothbrush. There's a note above the toilet. 'If you are a man or womanIt doesn't matter to me;But since I clean the bathroomI ask you to sit when you pee.' Ah Lupe. Never fails to be poetic, even when it's completely unnecessary and a bit embarassing. That's coming down. Brush my teeth, use the mouthwash. Now at least the top half of me smells somewhat decent. Turn on the shower water, wait for it to warm up. I'm already naked anyway, so that's something I don't have to worry about. The shower radio turned up, I listen to rock in the morning, helps me wake up. A testament to how much better I am than you; do you have a shower radio that's built to spell your name? No? Cinncinnati, Ohio With the sunlight coming in through the window, which he normally kept open during the rest of the year, James Picot sat in his chair. Smirk on his face, coffee mug in hand, he exuded the confident aura of a man who knew that he had victory within his grasp. And why not? Everything had come into place for the young federal prosecutor based in Cinncinnati, Ohio. The case that was about to make his career, that would solidify him as tough on all crime, great or small, was blown wide open, and he was so close to sealing it that he could taste the chardonay that he had planned for the day when the court would render a favorable decision. Upon his desk there was a dossier. Inside, all the information on the Cult of the Poison Tree. The same cult that had given one Scott Matthews so much trouble in the past. Not that it was any of Picot's concern; he couldn't care less about a legal small-fry like Matthews. For James, it was not a matter of petty crime, land disputes, and slander against a professional wrestler. Small-time court could handle that. No, he set his sights higher. 'The assistant investigator is here, James,' Sarah buzzed from outside the office. She'd always been incredibly casual with him, but they were friends for years before he became an attorney and she invested that $200 into a paralegal training course. Enjoying a slight break in the morning before hitting the workload fully, James had been expecting this meeting. It would be essential to his plans. 'Send him in,' he returned, swallowing the Hazelnut international blend he'd been swishing around in his mouth. Not two seconds passed before the door opened. A massive man met the eyes of the squirrely little James Picot. The 5'8' 143 pound federal prosecutor was sitting in his chair, right leg folded over the left. He'd have to look up at whomever walked through the door. So to see a man seven feet tall, if that, and well over twice his weight, stepping through the door, it made him do a half-spit take. He kept the fluid inside of his mouth, though it attempted to expel. 'Mr. Picot,' the giant greeted simply, formally. His eyes took in the room in a second. Fern in the two corners opposite the door, free chairs nearest. The office was sparsely furnished, with only a desk flanked by two chairs, and a computer. Though there were no filing cabinets, spare papers littered the desk. Picot needed a filing cabinet. James set down the coffee, stroking back his curly brown hair. 'Mr. St. James. Welcome. Please, have a seat.' St. James shook his head, standing in front of the desk with arms behind his back. A military man, his stance at ease. 'I'll stand.' The chairs weren't big enough to fit him. St. James was professionally dressed in a black suit, with blue shirt and black tie. One must look important to look be fully professional, and he certainly did look important. The Blackberry peeking from his pocket, notepad and pen in the coat pocket... The man looked like he had things to do. He was big, but he worked in the professional world, not in the sports world (as one would assume of a man by his size). James Picot had a separate file folder on his desk, set off to the right side from the Poison Tree dossier. He flipped it open, leafing through the papers. More of a formality of gesture, he had the resume information memorized. 'Titan St. James. Former IDF, immigrated to the United States and sought government work. You've worked with the FBI, only good things to say with you. You've shied away from active field assignments, instead choosing more desk-oriented jobs. Any reason for that?' Though Titan was listening, he didn't bother to make eye-contact. Instead, his gaze affixed to the pavilion across the street from the courthouse. Picot had his face down in the file until the last question. Without delay, Titan responded, 'I fought my battles back in Israel.' Simple and direct. James seemed to be expecting more. Usually government employees will be verbose about defending their reputations whenever possible. Picot liked that St. James let his work speak for itself. 'I'll come to the point, I've got work to do and so will you very soon. I've requested you for a special assignment.' St. James, unflinchingly, 'That being?' James leaned back in his chair. His white shirt had a light brown coffee stain at the lower left rib. By the end of the day the stain would be very difficult to lift. 'You don't know anything about the Cult of the Poison Tree, fo you?' Titan shook his head. 'Goons, really. They're not millenials, and their faith is little more than an offshoot of Santeria, or Ocho de Regla, whichever name you've heard. They're based in eastern Kentucky, primarily. Little group of thugs, they just mess with the locals. But they've recently gotten into more high-class crime. Drug trafficking and the like. I'm coming down on them hard, but the court won't be able to fit it into the docket for another month or so.' St. James remained patiently quiet, awaiting his assignment. 'In the meantime, I need you to put a key witness into protective custody. He's an outsider, but he's seen most of the the business. And from the reports I've read, they seem to have some particular bone to pick. Both as a witness to the state, and as a psychological trump to them, I need him to make it to the trial.' Picot handed Titan a picture. 'Name's Scott Matthews. Professional wrestler out of Pikeville. He bought some land they wanted, and that's where the disputes began. Since then, he's filed numerous harassment reports against them. They, on the other hand, have filed assault charges against him.' St. James studied it in but a second. 'This man is the witness? Protective custody would function more to protect the cult from him.' 'Don't underestimate,' Picot shook his head. 'As their business increases, they get more money. They can recruit better, get more violent individuals. The leadership remains the same, it's all a cult of personality. But more zealous, more dangerous individuals are coming into the fold. That's a big guy, but he's not invincible. If they come at him with weapons instead of just walking up on him at the bar, I've got a problem. And there's still the matter of his family; they get to them, they get to him.' St. James knew all too well what it was like to live in an environment in which women and children were just as much fair targets to the enemy as soldiers or adult males. He hated that. But, it was a guard job under protective custody. Titan wouldn't have to go after anyone. 'Get Matthews and his immediate family into to protective custody. Keep them safe until the court date comes up. That's it. I trust that you can handle it.' Pikeville, Kentucky Scott threw the bags into the car. The government would not allow him to take his Jeep, instead providing two black Ford Explorers for his family. Mother, sister, Aly, and Scott. Even the dog was coming with them. They packed their clothing, and any essential luxury items. They'd be under protective custody for anywhere from one month to two. 'Explain this to me again?' Lacey said. Scott told her, 'It's like house arrest for people who didn't do anything.' He huffed, not so much from loading luggage into the trunk of the car, but rather because he thought that her V-cut pink sleeveless tee and tight highcut jeans were entirely inappropriate. She was practically throwing herself at Titan St. James, not that the large FBI agent could care. He was constantly scanning the woods that surrounded the property. Cathy, the family matriach, wore a red dress that flowed down to her ankles. It was loose, but not too much so. The shoulders ruffled with shoes to match, she looked like a flamenco dancer. Titan assisted her in loading the SUV that he would be driving for Lacey and her mother. Scott, Aly, and the dog got their own vehicle. 'I just worry about how this'll affect work,' Scott said. He had to be taken away from everything. Home life and career-wise. PRIME would be without him. Vangellus would be without him. How would this affect his finances? He wasn't poor, had a little money saved up, but in the wrestling world, if you're gone, you're no good. Out of sight, out of mind. He knew that he hadn't connected with the crowd the same as K-Wolf or Nova or someone who would be fondly remembered during their absence. Would coming back be so simple? Aly, already sitting in the car, said, 'Don't worry. I'm sure Van will handle it.' Lacey came back from the house, having locked up. They'd be gone for a while. A single agent would be posted at the house in the event that anyone attempted to break into the premises. Scott closed the trunk to his vehicle, while Titan and Cathy were ready to go, engine running, in their vehicle in front of the one for Aly. 'We all ready to go?' Lacey cheerfully smiled. She wanted a break from her school responsibilities. The Christmas break wasn't long enough. That she hadn't had to return to school right away, she hoped Scott had messed with the Poison Tree cult sooner. Undisclosed Location Titan St. James pulled up in front of the lodge. They were somewhere far west of Pikeville, though still in the United States. Scott would have guessed in the Rockies, but he wasn't that great with his geography. Aly marveled at the rustic beauty of the two-story wooden cabin. 'It's like out of a Christmas Card,' she said, looking around the grounds. Lacey had put on her school sweatshirt, though she still had nothing covering her legs. She took the key from Titan, running hurriedly to unlock the front door. Titan helped Cathy out of the passenger side of the Explorer and went to get the bags out of the trunk. Scott's dog jumped out of the backseat, sniffing around its new home for the next few weeks. 'It's nice, but cold!' Lacey's teeth were already chittering, easily audible with 10 feet between her and the vehicles. Aly stepped in behind Lacey. 'Scott! Come quick!' Titan St. James was not concerned. He knew it was a secure location. Alyssa was calling him not in a tone of worry, but one of jubilation. Scott moved in behind her. His mouth dropped. 'Van!' Oxios was relaxing on the couch, watching television. 'Ey! Que pasa!' Now how was that going to be explained to PRIME? And how did Vangellus get there? Teh Greatness both out of action? What would become of their tag team standing in the federation? And would Olsig and Mortimer face Baltasar and Noble in a two-team Intense Rules match that would now suck without Rampage and VOX being a part of it? Scott Matthews, Titan St. James, and Vangellus Oxios all served to get the bags into the house while Cathy, Lacey, and their dog explored the household. Socializing and discovery. And no one was listening to the television. 'Tonight, on eXtra! Ladies, those dreams of being called Mrs. Vangellus Oxios are now crushed. We've got the exclusive photos of his triste with Queer As Folk actor Randy Harrison!'
