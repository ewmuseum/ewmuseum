Something In The Air Tonight

4 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 4th, 2005 Your breath catches in your chest, and you stand still as stone until her footsteps betray her. As you quickly jump out from around the corner, you suddenly realize she isn’t the one you were sent for. But that realization comes all too late. Her face devoid of color, she falls to her knees as her eyes roll into the back of her head. After pulling the sword from her back, you prepare to finish what you came for. Something dropped. Falling. Glass shatters. A scream. Silence. You hold a second innocent in your arms as she dies, but you had no choice. Your orders are to be carried out, by any means necessary. And at any cost. But her scream is muffled a moment too late, and within seconds you’re surrounded by armed guards, their blades pointed at your neck. Sweat drips from your brow as you take a fighting stance. “You will never take me alive. Ha-ha-ha!” You turn your own sword on yourself. “DREW CAREY!” “…Uh, Clyde…I think it’s ‘hari-kari’…” Pause. Pull back the view from the TV screen to James and Clyde sitting in front of it, playing some sort of ninja videogame. Not Shinobi or Tenchu, though. We don’t want any unnecessary lawsuits here. Let’s just call it…‘Shinochu’. I’m fucked if that’s some kind of Pokémon. Sitting next to James, Clyde turns his focus from the paused game, his character midway through gutting himself wide open, and the words SUICIDAL PANSY still visible on the screen underneath the options menu. Walkins: Listen, when you play the game, you can play it your way. But it’s my turn now, so we’ll play the game my way. Farwell: But it’s not! I got up to take a piss, and when I came back, you already killed three of my men! Walkins: Correction! I only killed two of them. An awkward pause ensues, with the two just staring at each other. Finally, Clyde breaks the awkwardness with…more awkwardness…and grins. Walkins: The computer killed the other one. Farwell continues to seethe. Farwell: Do you have any idea how long it took me to get to the last level? Walkins: Well, it says you’ve clocked in eight hours today. Farwell: Yes. Eight hours. And do you know what that means, Clyde? “You haven’t masturbated for three?” Having been sufficiently zinged, James Farwell whips his head around to see who’s coming up behind him. But in the process, a loud popping sound is heard. Farwell: Goddamn it! I got whiplash for you?! And what are you doing here, anyway? Skywolf: Nothing better to do. Farwell: Good enough of a reason as any, I suppose… Rubbing his neck, James turns back to the screen, but Clyde has other plans. Walkins: Wolfie’s here! And he brought presents! Throwing down the controller, Clyde spins around and runs right at Skywolf, but not before shutting the system off. Walkins: We don’t need you anymore! Farwell’s jaw slowly drops. Farwell: Eight hours…eight…freakin’…hours… Like a little kid, Clyde jumps up and down in front of Skywolf, who responds to the childish gesture with a questioning raise of the eyebrow. Skywolf: I…didn’t bring anything… Looking over to James for help, but seeing him still in a state of shock over losing almost half a day of his life, Skywolf shrugs and takes a coffee cup from on the table. Skywolf: Here you go, Clyde. Merry…something… Walkins: Yay! A coffee mug! Clyde grips the cup with both hands, holding it up to peer inside. Walkins: Hey, there’s something in here already… And since we don’t need to be rocket scientists to figure out what comes next, let’s just move on to the opening credits, shall we? “Super Hero” by Stereo Fuse Oh, why can’t you understand? I want to be just like Superman. The camera slowly moves from the feet to the head of Clyde, who just so happens to be dressed up as Clyder-Man, standing tall and proud on top of a building with his hands on his hips and his codpiece stuffed to satisfaction. I want to fly so high up in the sky. Be immortal, never gonna die. Clyder-Man kicks a super villain straight in the nuts, lifting him off the ground slightly. I want to be a super hero. I want to fly so high. Clyder-Man crouches on the top of a stadium housing a wrestling show… I want to be a super hero. Live forever and never gonna die. …And then subsequently takes on a few street toughs, spraying them in the eyes with blinding Clyder webbing, before ‘swinging’ off the roof and directly into a dumpster below. Oh, why can’t you understand? With my mask, I’ll be like Batman. Clyde, in his only appearance as a super hero other than Clyder-Man, steals the nipples from an unsuspecting thief and drops them into a jar filled with other nipples. With my powers, I can save the world. Drive a car and always get the girl. Clyder-Man and a reluctant Secret Jamesy Man jump into the Clyder-Mobile, which consists of an old Volkswagen made up of rejected parts from other cars. I want to be a super hero. I want to fly so high. Clyde stabs CGI Rampage in the sweet spot, but only after releasing the horror known as Gabeazard unto the world. I want to be a super hero. Live forever and never gonna die. Clyde hits the Clyde Crusher on Tyler O’Neill, proving I don’t suck as much as some might say. I’ll be a super hero. Not just a loser zero. I’ll be a super hero. Clyde ‘rocks out’ to the music. Basically, he’s just stomping his feet and pounding his fists on the air drums while staring at the ground. But I’ll be damned if he isn’t rocking his little heart out. Oh, why can’t you understand? I don’t want to be just a mortal man. From the rocking out comes the Robot, which Clyde executes and delivers to James. I want to fly so high up in the stars. Use my x-ray vision counting cards. James doesn’t sign for it. I want to be a super hero. I want to fly so high. Rehashed footage of Clyde repeatedly webbing the same guy over and over in the eyes. His reaction is priceless. I want to be a super hero. Live forever and never gonna die. More Robot. But rather than rejection, James simply mocks Clyde with a retarded version of his own, then walks away in disgust. I want to be a super hero. I want to be a super hero. I want to be a super hero. I want to be a super hero. The video fades out with the song, leaving an image of Clyder-Man standing atop a computer-generated image of the earth. Oh, why can’t you understand? I want to be just like Superman. Inside the waiting room of a hospital, Farwell and Skywolf sit across from each other, both men obviously bored out of their minds. Checking their watches at the same time, the two meet eyes for the first time in over an hour. Skywolf: I think my watch might be fast. What time do you have? Farwell: 11:45. Skywolf: Well, there goes my theory… Skywolf scans the room for anything with which to entertain himself, but finds nothing. Giving in to boredom, LSW twiddles his thumbs and quietly begins humming a familiar rhythm. Skywolf: Hmm…hmm…hmm…and you know the night is always gonna be here anyway… Fast-forward a few seconds, as a young male nurse helps wheel a bandaged Clyde out into the waiting room. Farwell & Skywolf: Skyrockets in flight…afternoon delight! The nurse stops in his tracks, and simply looks at James and Skywolf, who cut their singing short when they realized someone heard them. Like a deer caught in the headlights, the two move apart from their position of having their arms over each other’s shoulders to a new, less suggestive one. The nurse doesn’t appear impressed at the attempt. Male Nurse: Gay. And then he turns around, letting go of Clyde’s chair in the process, and heads back down the hall. A new silence ensues, before someone finally says something. Skywolf: …Who’s he calling gay? He’s a nurse, for crying out loud! Nurses are the bottom of the totem pole when it comes to gay. Farwell: Don’t you mean top-ohhh! I get it. James smirks and taps his finger to his nose. Meanwhile, Clyde just sits there, his face completely bandaged. Skywolf: Speaking of the bottom of barrel-shaped things…how’d he manage to get into GTT5 and you didn’t? Not to mention that I didn’t, either. Skywolf pauses for a second. Skywolf: If I didn’t know any better, I’d think someone had tampered with, or given away, our entry forms… Both James and Skywolf slowly turn their gaze to Clyde, who simply sits there the entire time, as still as if he were dead. Picking up a stick, James gives a few good pokes to Clyde’s ribs, but gains no response. Farwell: You don’t think he’s…you know…dead? Skywolf: If he is, he might have a better chance at winning the thing. Lord knows right now he’s more of a long shot than Stephen Hawking. Cut to a boxing ring, with Don King and Mike Tyson in one corner, and Stephen Hawking in the other. Decked out in red and white boxing trunks and gloves, no less. The bell rings as King gives Tyson a few more pointers, and Hawking rolls out to the middle of the ring. Hawking: BRI-ng it OOON, biiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiii- A frustrated look on his face, Hawking starts having a go at the stuck button, before a punch from Tyson knocks him and his wheelchair over. Back to The Forsaken. Now with 35% more Skywolf. Farwell: That one was a little lame, don’t you think? Skywolf: What? Farwell: Lacking impact, I said. Skywolf: What? Farwell: Punch. Skywolf: What? Farwell: Force. Skywolf: What? Farwell: You have no initiative. Skywolf: What? Farwell: Your cutaways are weak. Skywolf: What? Farwell: Of poor quality. Skywolf: What? Farwell: This prom-oh-ho-ho! I almost walked right into that one. Skywolf: What? Farwell: …You can quit anytime now. It stopped being funny after the second line. Looking back over at Clyde, Farwell’s expression turns to one of concern. Farwell: You know, it’s not like Clyde to be so quiet for so long. Do you think something happened to him in surgery, and the hospital isn’t liable as long as we don’t notice it here? Skywolf shrugs. Farwell: Thanks. You’re as much of a help as he is sometimes. Skywolf: I try. Sighing, James cautiously approaches the lifeless body of his tag team partner, suspiciously eyeing the bandages covering his face. A moment’s hesitation on Farwell’s part causes Skywolf to pipe up. Skywolf: Come on, we don’t have all day. Rip off those bandages and some loose skin, and let’s get out of here. Farwell looks back at Skywolf with an icy glare, before reaching forward and carefully taking hold of one of the bandages. He pulls slightly, and the sound of wet flesh being pulled away from adhesive tape echoes throughout the halls, causing both men to almost vomit. Skywolf: Oooh, that’s nasty… Skywolf leans over Farwell’s shoulder and watches intently as he peels away more of the bandages from Clyde’s face. Suddenly, a voice from behind them causes James to drop the bandages. Walkins: Why didn’t you wait for me? You know how much I like unwrapping mummies. Both James and Skywolf look at Clyde in surprise, seeing not a mark on him. Farwell: But…you were…and we…who the hell’s in this chair then? Turning back to the wheelchair, all three men watch as James pulls back the final bit of bandage, revealing… Farwell: Clyde?! ???: I’m Jake Hancock, you ass! Skywolf: But if you can talk, and you’re not Clyde…then why didn’t you say anything? And why are you his twin? Hancock: …I liked the attention… Skywolf: And the ‘looking like Clyde’ thing? Hancock: Shady Poser work? The four men share exchanged glances. Farwell: We-llll…it’s about time we hit the old dusty trail… Walkins: Yeah. This is more boring than the time I had to stand in line at that protest. Cut to Singapore, where a large group of protestors stand in front of a probable government building, holding signs and chanting chants while maintaining a candlelight vigil. On the outside of the group is Clyde, and deep inside is an ice cream truck. Clyde’s lower lip is bitten as he watches wave after wave of protestor stop for a delicious treat at said ice cream truck, all the while unable to get the snack he himself so rightly deserves. And then an idea suddenly pops into his puny mind. Walkins: You can all go home now! They killed him yesterday! A collective groan comes from the crowd, as they hang their heads and drop their signs and candles, immediately leaving the protest area. And, in turn, the ice cream truck. Never one to allow a plan to only come half-circle, Walkins runs up to the side of the truck and puts both hands onto the counter. Walkins: I’ll take a peanut butter ripple! Ice Cream Man: Sorry, sold out. Walkins: Dammit! Clyde slams his fist onto the counter, then shuffles off, dejected. Meanwhile, an official walks through the halls of the building with an aide by his side. Singapore Official: It looks like we have no choice but to comply with the demands of the protest- Turning from his aide, the official stops short as he opens the front door and sees no one standing there, but rather only dropped signs and toppled candles. Singapore Official: Quick! Hang him while they’re on their lunch break! And now back to The Forsaken. And Skywolf. But minus Jake Hancock, because now they’re back at the hotel. Convenient, eh? I thought so, too. Farwell: So how is it you came away from the entire ordeal unscathed, Clyde? Walkins: Well, you see…it’s a very complicated matter, which would take quite a while to explain. In fact, that’s why I was in the hospital so long. Farwell: I see… Walkins: I’ll explain it to you, if you’d like. I can sleep in tomorrow, so we can talk all night if need be. Farwell and Skywolf exchange panicked looks. Farwell: I would, but…uh…we need to go to the…ah… Skywolf: The vet! Because we…haven’t had our…uh… Farwell: Our…uh…our shots! Because we haven’t had our shots yet! Walkins: Wait a minute…something sounds fishy here… Skywolf and James look nervously at Clyde, then back at each other, both knowing they may be moments away from enduring the most brutal form of torture known to man. Walkins: If you haven’t had your shots, then why don’t you have rabies? And with that, all nervousness is lost. Farwell: …We do! We do have rabies! Skywolf: Uh…yeah, that’s right! We’re laden with the stuff. Farwell: Well, no time for chat. We need to go and get cured now, so we’ll see you later, Clyde. James pauses, then speaks in a low tone while staring Clyde right in the eye. Farwell: Much later… Clyde just smiles in return. Walkins: Have fun getting neutered! Skywolf shoots Farwell a nervous glance once more, but James calms him with a reassuring hand gesture. Skywolf: Hey! Why’d you flip me off? Ignoring Skywolf, James turns back to Clyde. Farwell: If I were you, I’d get ready for your match against Collins this week. Worry about Knight after. Walkins: Wait…I’m fighting Phil Collins?! And a knight of some sort now? Giving up, Farwell shrugs. Farwell: …Sure, why not. Motioning to the door, James opens it and ushers Skywolf out, following quickly behind. As both men escape into the night, they leave Clyde alone in his hotel room to ponder the upcoming challenges that lay before him. Walkins: All of PRIME is counting on me to win the Glowing Turdbuckle Tournament, as well as my millions of Clydermaniacs around the world. But they’re also counting on me to put an end to the music of Phil Collins forever, and with God as my witness I will strike down the plague of Genesis and show Phil that there is indeed something in the air tonight. Clyde pauses, reflecting solemnly for a moment. Walkins: Do you smell it, Phil? Do you smell what’s in the air tonight? Clyde pauses again, this time lifting an eyebrow. Walkins: Do you smell what the Walk is cooking?
