Could I

8 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/08/2005 “So you see…this is what finally broke me.” Lopez’s voice, cool as a desert wind. Up. Just the hum of a motor and nothing else. The car zips along a desert highway. We are looking at Tyler Lopez as if we were riding shotgun and looking at him trying to get himself under control. Periodically he will shut his eyes, and then open them a moment later and they will look clearer. He grips the steering wheel hard, so that his hands do not shake. Lives have been lost, realizations made, a great tragedy averted. But this is one of those times where you know it will never be the same again. Like lying to someone for the first time, only six million times worse. Like crossing a bridge, and then watching it fall down. The blood on Lopez’s shirt is drying, and the wound itches. He shifts uncomfortably, and Passion is concerned, but is afraid to speak. Lopez is muttering to himself as he pulls the shirt off and looks at the livid cut along his chest. “This is when I decided that it all needed to be burned.” He throws the shirt into the back seat. Again, Passion with much restraint does not try to help. She looks straight ahead, she has been with Tyler long enough to know when to not speak, this is one of those times. Lopez takes another deep and shaky breath. He is coming apart at the seems. Passion: Shall I drive. Lopez: NO! He shoots Passion an angry glance. “This is when I realized that I couldn’t be saved, and that the only thing I could do was to let go. You are everything, and I am convinced that you being with me would only cause more of this. Better to sever the ties. I do not deserve you, and you did nothing to deserve the curse that is me. I love you so much that I forgot what I was. And what I was wasn’t what you needed.” There are tears in his eyes. He is trying to blink them out, his face is flushed, he is sweating profusely. He feels everything guilt, anger, resignation, fear, panic, all the bad feelings and they are ravaging him right now. But he is holding it together. “I had finally reached the point where I had destroyed enough lives for something I wanted, that I felt wrong taking it. You are not to be won. And just because I love you beyond all things comprehendible, does not make me worthy of you. These are things that dawned on me slowly. I always said that I would give up the pursuit if I hurt you, but that moment passed and I missed it, and I was obsessed. Just another reason why you cannot belong to me This has been the worst day of my entire life.” The car skids to a halt. Lopez folds his arms on the steering wheel and buries his face in his arms and lets go. Lopez: I have to let you go Passion. He looks up. He’s another man, a vulnerable man. He looks at Passion, pleadingly, what does he want? Compassion? Who is he to want compassion? And he realizes this. Lopez: I will put my money into a trust. I want you to sell the house. I will provide what I can for you, while staying away, like I know I should. Passion: Do you think all this is necessary? Lopez: LOOK AT THIS BLOOD! Look…look at it. How could I continue on? When so much has happened to you because of me? You will be better away from me, and I do not say that for any other reason than it is the truth. I make people look at the truth, and I have to make myself look at it too. I am tainted. And you are not. And I would taint you, and that, would be too much for me to bear. It is best you forget about me. Passion is stunned. Lopez guns the engine again, and drives onward. He is still muttering to himself. Cut. Up. Lopez’s house. One last time. The car drives slowly up the windy driveway. This is where is all began. In a few days Tyler Lopez will appear as the Scientific Apparatus at Revolution and he will take back control of his career. But right now, oh if they had seen him then like this, the sharks would have been circling. “For what I had done. To her, who was blameless. These are all things I knew, of course, but never faced. But now, in this, my most weakest hour, I had to face them. I knew what I had to do.” Lopez slumps out of the car, Passion waiting for him, her eyes gleaming in the moonlight. “One more order of business, and this horrible day could end.” Lopez follows Passion up the walkway, and into the house. Cut. Lopez: Dad… Up. The old man stares at his son-in-law, Passion standing behind him. Lopez’s voice shakes again. It is dark in here, and after a moment of avoiding it, Lopez looks at his Father-In-Law. He sniffs and draws back as if struck for a moment. He has acted so shamefully, that he can hardly bear it. He squats down in front of his Father. Lopez: Dad…I….I’m so sorry. More tears, but he doesn’t sob. Lopez: I, they tried to kill Passion. I stopped him, she is alright. The old man exhales. Lopez: And thank you. Thank you a thousand times thank you Dad because she would be gone now if it weren’t for you. And after the way I have treated you…Dad I can’t say I’m sorry because it is so pathetically not enough. I can’t say it…because what I did was so beyond an apology and you never did anything but try to do right by me. I was poisoned Dad. I was poisoned and you saw it and you tried to save me. And I repay you with this torture. He looks up, his eyes shimmering. Lopez: I am sorry for this. I am sorry that I never appreciated you. That I let you down. That I became the man I became because you deserved better. Dad I am sorry that I never said thank you, and that I ever said bad things about you. I am so sorry for the awful things I said to you. And that… Lopez, on his knees before his Father, begging for forgiveness. Which, he already has. The old man, he’s got tears too. Passion stands as though she is about to break down into tears also. Lopez: I am sorry, I am sorry that you had to have me for a son. I am sorry I am who I am, and that you are like you are, and I am sorry that I hurt you, and that I liked it. Oh God, I am so sorry that I liked it. But I will not ask you to forgive me. You cannot forgive me. Thank you for everything that you have ever done for me, you are far more worthy of dignity than I am. I am sorry for everything. Everything in the world. Dad, I am sorry that I didn’t realize what kid of man you were because I was weak and stupid and blind. The old man cannot hug his son, and he wants to, and Lopez seems to read his mind. Tyler wraps his arms around the atrophied body of his Father and hugs him. The old man can’t feel his son’s arms, but it’s enough, it has to be enough. After a long time Lopez sets him down. He is back in some semblance of control. Lopez: Dad, I will ask you a question, because it seems like the only thing I can give you…. He looks into his Father eyes. Lopez: …Do you want to die? The man blinks twice. Yes. He is ready, he has suffered enough, and this could not possibly be a higher point. The old man wants to leave so that his wretched son cannot take any of it back. “And it was the only gift I could give him. The only thing I could do to show my gratitude.” Lopez smiles tightly, leans forward and kisses the man on his forehead. Lopez: Thank you Dad, I hope there is a heaven so that you can be there in peace. And he put his hand over the man’s mouth and nose. And the man’s breath became lighter, and he didn’t fight, he looked into his Son’s eyes.] And then he took his last breath. There was a death rattle. “I helped him to die, and I hope he died knowing that I regretted that we could not have been close. That he could not have been proud of me. I wanted him to be proud of me.” And Tyler fell back onto the floor. Cut. “Since that day, I have lived in a lie. A prison. I took my command, I took it because I had nothing else. I had nothing but the persona I had created for myself. But it has all been a fake. The bad things I do were because I had to, because I had to maintain who I was. Because who I am is all I have. That is my prison. No family. No real friends. Just alone with my infamy. My identity is The Scientific Apparatus, and the Apparatus always knows what to do. He always is in control. He always gets his man, or whatever it is that he desires. There is comfort in that, but nothing else. I am a slave to my reputation. And I have a hard time not being bored by it. I don’t so much care about the crowds. It’s just something to do while I wait to die. Or for someone to finally come and get me, and stop me. Sometimes I wish I’d break my neck. Sometimes I wish I felt like I was worthy of being happy. But you couldn’t possibly understand the pressure. Every night, I have to win, and every night I have to scare you. I have to do this because the people that have been waiting so long for me to falter would descend on me like wild dogs that were starved. I’m doing what I always did, because being evil is better than being nothing. But the secret is, I am not really that into it. If you watched, there were times when I just didn’t have the strength to do what I would normally do. I said a lot, I have done very little, and someday someone will notice. But for now, at least I contribute. But the Scientific Apparatus died in all but name on the day that all the rest did. Now I float. I am a fallen leaf in a river. I do what I have to do, go where I am pushed, to move forward. Because I know no other way. Someday I will find a way out of this prison. I don’t know what I’ll do when I find it, I have spent so long being what was expected of me, that I don’t know who I am. But I couldn’t possibly be worse than what I am. Could I?”
