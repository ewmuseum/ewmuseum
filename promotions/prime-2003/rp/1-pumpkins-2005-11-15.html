1$ pumpkins

15 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/15/05 People gave up on religion and now we live in a world where athletes are worth more then soldiers. About thirteen hundred American soldiers have died in the Iraq conflict fighting for a better world. Most of them have never had the chance to take drugs or destroy their lives, they died to young. They never had the opportunity to make good money and have a family as that was taken from them. Yet a wrestler takes a dirt nap and we’re supposed to care? I’m sorry I don’t as my life is to short to care about a sinner who helped destroy his own life. A godless society destroys the moral fabric of what it is we support. If there is no God, that makes us the top of the food chain. That makes us the individual the most important thing in the world. So we value what we see and touch. Since entertainment is a tangible and freedom is an intangible we grasp what we enjoy not what we need because we take it for granted. I do my best to bring the word of God back to soulless but it has become apparent that the world is becoming less and less interested. If you had the option between being a servant or being a king you instinctively would pick being the king. Yet how many people are truly ready to be a king? The first thing they teach an addict is to believe in a higher power. The reason this is done is because you can’t heal until you put the faith into others. If you’re the king you can’t ask for help but a servant can. The servant has somebody to look up to and can find comfort in their master. Mock religion, question it, and ignore it but my friends in the end you will answer to God. Regardless of who you are or what you have done. Man is that going to be a cold wake up call to those who don’t believe. But the point of this is not just to make sure you understand your faith but also to prioritize what is important to you. People mock Jesus and he died for your sins. Yet they weep for a stranger who entertained them who could gave two damns about them if they saw them on the street. Ironic isn’t it? I can talk until I’m blue in the face and you people still wouldn’t get it. I have spoken the word of God and have backed it up winning title after title. The Hindus believe ridiculously in reincarnation. They think if you live your life good when you die you will move up towards nirvana to a higher link on the food chain. So there was this guy Killean who lived a bad life and I killed. Yet he is reborn. Under Hindu thinking he would come back a step down on the ladder so maybe appropriately a rat. What ever he is he has returned and I’m not impressed. This is a guy who disgraced his dead wife by fooling with a child of a girl. His dishonesty telling people he would beat me is laughable. Not to mention his continuous sin of boring everybody around his with his yarns of yawns. (In walks Duke Williams carrying two pumpkins gleefully.) Duke Williams: Hey what are you doing? Hoyt Williams: Typing up my weekly address to the RISP a sermon on death and priorities. What the heck are you doing? Duke Williams: I stopped at the grocery store to buy some pineapple as I was craving the pineapple when I saw they were selling off all their left over pumpkins for a buck each!! I had a fifty burning a hole in my pocket so I bought the rest of their pumpkins! Help me take them out of my truck. Hoyt Williams: What are we going to do with 50 pumpkins? Duke Williams: I don’t know they were only a buck I can’t pass up a deal. We can cut them up and take the seeds out. Who doesn’t love pumpkins seeds? Hoyt Williams: I hate pumpkin seeds and these damn pumpkins are going to rot like the ring skills of Killean. Duke Williams: We can fucking throw them at people!! Let’s chuck some pumpkins at some old ladies by the retirement village. (Hoyt shakes his head in disbelief.) Hoyt Williams: Have you been drinking? Duke Williams: Yes, now lets get our asses to the Eleanor Roosevelt Retirement Community and bomb some bitty’s with agent orange. Hoyt Williams: Ok first off I’d never throw pumpkin’s at an old person. Second Pumpkins are huge! You would have to be close to them and if you were a flying pumpkin could kill them. Duke Williams: Then what the hell are we going to do with all these fucking pumpkins in the back of my pickup truck. Hoyt Williams: I’m not the one who bought them. Duke Williams: But they were only a buck, and I had a fifty to burn. Hoyt Williams: We’ve established that. Duke Williams: What about pumpkin pie? Lets make some fucking pies. Hoyt Williams: Fine, how do you make pumpkin pie? Duke Williams: Who the fuck knows! Hoyt Williams: Then we have a dilemma. Duke Williams: Hey don’t you have some tag match this week? Hoyt Williams: Yea Killean and some other guy. Duke Williams: Didn’t you run Killean out of PRIME? Hoyt Williams: He’s a cockroach they always return. Duke Williams: Hey!! Lets throw some pumpkins at him? Hoyt Williams: I’m going back to finishing this paper. You worry about your pumpkins. Duke Williams: I’m going to take a nap. =w=
