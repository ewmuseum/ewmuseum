Preggos are Annoying.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: CWMC Round 4b "You're leaving?" "I'm leaving." "You're leaving?" A hefty sigh escapes the mouth of the Illustrious Face-Eater. The first time, April spoke as if Adam's actions had not yet set in. It only took a moment to hit her, but once it did she slammed right into a brick wall. "Jesus Christ, YakBak much?" He replies while shoving a handful of masks into the dufflebag strapped at his side. "Adam!" "Don't worry about the bills; most of the shit takes about a month and a half to turn off. But once the cable and other less important shit goes out you might want to get a job, seeing as you're about to have a kid and all." He ignores the fact that she's in the middle of her third trimester. The tears begin to swell in April's eyes as she follows him around his room, watching as he puts more random shit in his bag, "I just—I just don't understand it..." She cries. "Why are you going?! Why now?!" ---- Why? Why? Tell me, Preggo, why have I been hanging around you for the past few months? Why have I opened every door you've walked through? Pulled out every chair you've sat in? Why the hell did I hold my tongue from unleashing every abrasive, demeaning cut-down that ran through my brain in response to all the stupid shit you've said? Because I'm nice? No, bitch; it's because I want to get laid. Helping to pick out 'onesies' at the BabyGap is hardly what I consider a hobby, and building craptastic cribs from Ikea is not my idea of a pastime. If there's one thing you need to get through you're fat, emotional head, it's that I'm not a nice guy. I'm not a nice guy. ---- "Because I can't stay here, April! I've got-" "What do you mean 'can't stay here'? What kind of bullshit excuse is that?" Turning from his collection of Scarface Action Figures, Facey reacts to her hostility in kind. "See? That's exactly what I'm talking about: all of the fucking time you go on with the drama! First you're tormented about your fiancé's whereabouts, now all you do is worry about your fetus! When does it end, April?! WHEN DOES THE DRAMA END?!" "You fucking asshole, Brock went missing and I'm pregnant with his child – and you expect me to stay poise? Are you dense? Are you seriously that selfish?" "Well what about me, huh? I've been waiting on you hand and foot for the past two months and what have I got to show for it?" Collecting his Tony Montana w/ Chainsaw Action Grip, he places it in the bag and scurries off. For a moment, April remains still. Her heart-rate is pumping so rapidly that she's finding her head getting lighter. Though she knows she should take a seat before she starts dizzying, she needs an explanation. "So you expected something in return." She stands in the doorway of his bathroom, watching him through the large mirror decorating the back end of the wall. Taking a slight break from gathering his toiletries, he returns April's gaze through the reflection. "Did you think I'd have sex with you?" Adam doesn't answer. He merely continues staring."This was never about being my friend, was it?" ---- Wait, what? Your.... friend? ...........me? Me..... be your friend? Us, being friends?........ That don't have sex? That's just-No. No, I can't.... No. Just, No, alright? No. ---- Facey doesn't answer her question in fear of the pain he might inflict. "Look, things were fun while they lasted but this isn't me. I can't keep pretending like I care about stupid shit--" "'Pretending like you care'?" Adam eyes widen—he knows he said the wrong thing. Instead of trying to cover his tracks, he decides to pretend as if it were never mentioned. He approaches the bookshelf in his room and scans the text on the spines, searching for something. "Look, I've got a higher calling, 'kay babe? I'm in an important wrestling tournament that I can't be losing focus of! Man's gotta stay on his toes, you know?!" "You've never trained for a match in your life!" "Well, not physically, no. But that doesn't mean some mental preparation'll do me no-good. EVERYONE knows that exercising the mind is the key to success, and there's no better way to do that than with a literary classic." While saying this, Adam pats a book he's retrieved from the shelf. April snatches it from him and examines the title. "The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past Strategy Guide for Super Nintendo?" ---- What am I supposed to do? Continue pretending to be something I'm not? I can't do that; I'm a man with comfort zones and am not willing to bend. The game has been played for as long as it can be, but it's starting to get tired. And when the end no longer justifies the means, it's time to pack it up. I've had enough of this I've had enough of you. ---- "See, you don't even get me!" He shoves the book into his bag and storms past her, out of his room. "This is a book about STRATEGY. Something I need to focus on if I plan on beating three fucktards this week, but YOU DON'T CARE ABOUT THAT! All you care about is your stupid baby's health, AND HE'S NOT EVEN BORN YET!" April grips Adam by the shoulder as he tries to walk away, spinning him around to a finger right in his face. "Don't you DARE say another fucking word about my child, you piece of shit." "Whatever," he scoffs before breaking away from her grip. "I'm outta here. I'LL GO FIND SOMEONE WHO APPRECIATES ME!" April watches Facey slam the door shut upon his exit, leaving her in a familiar state that she's never been able to grow found of. Once again, April Monday is alone.
