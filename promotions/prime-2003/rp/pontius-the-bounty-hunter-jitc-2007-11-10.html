Pontius The Bounty Hunter (JITC)

10 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-10-07 Life is full of lumps in the road, overdue movies, and bad egg sandwiches. In other words, shit happens, and sometimes said shit doesn’t flush so you need to get your hands dirty and the plunger wet. Even Jesus had a few bad, yet well publicized, days. Being a savior isn’t all about turning water into wine, or resurrecting the dead. Being a wrestler isn’t all about kicking asses and taking names. Being a presidential candidate isn’t all kissing babies and speaking in circles. No, sometimes life gets in the way of the who and why. For me it all started before my crusade to the White House even began. I should have learned from Nixon that even great men can be taken down by a cheap tape recorder, a malicious soul, and a phone. But being part human means I’m part flawed, and I trusted those who I shouldn’t have. You see, my trainer Burt Perlow was dating an Eskimo chick named Nanook. Now I have no problem with Eskimo’s, but I felt Nanook was an evil presence. I told Burt that if he wanted to keep working for me, he needed to dump the ice queen. Little did I know I was being recorded. Before I get to the tapes, let me explain about the timing of this disaster I call IceGate. For some reason, the idiots who run PRIME haven’t handed me my Universal title back. As a matter of fact, they keep booking me against people I have never heard of. So in order to trump the Enron type management, I need to do something big. One thing could do that, and that’s win the Jewel in the Crown tournament. I just entered the little tournament, and my first match up is against the kid from Home Improvement. At least I think it is, but because of IceGate I’m without my damn trainer to tell me these things. So now I’m stuck fighting the returning Jonathan Taylor Thomas not knowing anything about him. Why did he turn over the tapes now, during the toughest time of my life? Let me take you back to the phone call that, mind you, took place over eight months ago. I remember it like it was yesterday…. DATELINE: MARCH 5th, 2007 [The following is a recorded phone conversation between PRIME Hall of FAME member Hoyt Williams and his long time trainer Burt Perlow.] Hoyt Williams: Damn kids are outside throwing bean bags into a hole. They should be home studying the words of Hoyt Williams. What’s wrong with the youth today? Burt Perlow: I don’t know. Hoyt Williams: So where were we? Oh yea, that ice coon you're dating, I don't care if she's a Troy fan, a midget, or whatever. It's not because she's an Eskimo either, it's because we use the word Ice spook sometimes here. I'm not gonna take a chance ever in life, of losing everything I have worked for, for 2 years because some fucking igloo monkey heard us say ice spook, and turned us in to Tom Holzerman’s blog. Our career is over! I'm not taking that chance at all! Never in life! Never! Never! If Mary with Her Cherry was dating a nooker, we would all say 'fuck you!' And you know that. If Mary brought a chubby guy home ya da da... it's not that they're chubby fish eating folk, it's none of that. It's that we use the word nooker. We don't mean you fucking scum nooker without a soul. We don't mean that shit. But America would think we mean that. And we're not taking a chance on losing everything we got, over a racial slur because our trainer dates a girl like that. I can't do that Perlow. You can't expect Mary, Jesus, God, all them young kids to [garbled] because 'I'm in love for 7 months' - fuck that! So, I'll help you get another job, but you can not work here unless you break up with her and she's out of your life. I can't handle that shit. I got 'em in the parking lot trying to record us. I got that girl saying she's gonna wear a recorder!!! Burt Perlow: So I’m fired? Hoyt Williams: Not at all. You just can’t work with me as long as you’re with the niggloo princess. Burt Perlow: I’m sorry you feel that way. Hoyt Williams: It’s not easy being the King of Kings and the second coming of Christ. I have to make very hard decisions, and some of them may not be as good others. You know. [End phone conversation] So that’s where we left it, and I thought all was well. I figured he would hang out with this chick till he got bored, then he would come back to work. But like Britney Spears cleaning up and finding Jesus, it just didn’t happen. Life went on, and my bid to be the nominee for the Republican Party for President of the Untied States started. All was well in my life at the start of this week, well until Burt Perlow sold me out to the Holzerman blog for fifteen thousand dollars. He sold that tape of me using those horrible words for under twenty grand. As soon as the tapes aired on CNN, FOX news, PTC, my poll numbers dropped five percent. This whole thing reminds me of a tale from the old bible before my version was published by Judith Regan. There was this Jewish fellow named Judah who was good friends with Jesus. Sort of. You see, Judah was one of these guys who was only Jez’s buddy when he needed something done. Judah was a real-estate agent who would often flip huts and make a huge prophet (pun intended). Anytime this greasy prick would buy a house with bad carpentry, he would contract Jesus to fix things. Jesus, being just an alright guy according to the brother Doobies, would do a lot of labor for next to nothing. Jesus wasn’t very good at math and statistics, he was more of a thinker and a bit of a hippie. Well, Jesus told Judah he was retiring and closing his business “JESUS CHRIST LOOK AT THESE PRICES: CARPENTRY AND MIRACLES”. This upset Judah, because that meant he couldn’t use Jesus anymore. One night, while drinking at the Holy Roller bar and grill, Judah overheard this famous bounty hunter with a mullet named Pontius Pilate talking about finding Jesus. Judah still steaming about life, sold out Jesus for thirty silver coins which could be redeemed for ten plays on the jukebox. That is how Jesus got caught and crucified by his own friend. Now I understand the trials and tribulations of my brother, having lived them myself. With no where to turn, I only have one choice at redemption, and that’s to visit the King and plead my case. If I can appeal to the general public in the presence of the King, certainly I will be forgiven. I mean it’s my folksy charm that has carried me this far in life...why stop now. [The following is a transcript of Hoyt Williams on the 11/08/07 edition of Larry King Live on CNN. Hoyt is dressed in a nice suit and tie, a sad look on his face. While Larry is dressed in a red shirt with black suspenders, looking like a skeleton with aids.] King: Thank you for tuning in, we have a great show for you tonight. Sold out by his trainer, wrestler turned politician, Hoyt Williams, was caught on tape using racial slurs against Eskimos. How are you doing? Hoyt Williams: I’m still breathing Larry. King: Glad at least one of us still is. Tell me about this tape. Hoyt Williams: First off Larry, I have to apologize to you. King: I’m not an Eskimo! Hoyt Williams: No, I have to apologize to you for believing in me in the past. I let you down, and I let a lot of my fans down. I have nothing against anybody, regardless of race, religion, or belly size. My best friend is an Eskimo, and I visit his igloo all the time. King: So what happened here? Hoyt Williams: I was raised in heaven Larry, where we joke with each other. Eskimo angels come up to me and are like my Nigloo Hoyt what’s going on, and I would say it back. You know? That’s how we do it on the golden streets of heaven. It was a sign of respect, and never meant to offend or anger anybody. King: So why did you have a problem with your trainer dating an Eskimo? Hoyt Williams: Larry, it had nothing to do with what she was, but who she was. I felt she was evil, and I still feel that. They set me up! King: You were set up? Hoyt Williams: Oh yea, when I was in FUSE they sent this McKail guy after me trying to get me to say racial slurs on tape. They have been after me for a long time Larry. King: Do you hate the blog? Hoyt Williams: Very much so. King: What about your trainer Burt Perlow? Hoyt Williams: I talked to God last night on the 777-777-HOYT prayer line, and we had a good conversation. I need to be the bigger man here, and what can I say? He’s my trainer and I can’t hate him. King: Would you hire him again? Hoyt Williams: It would take some time, but I think so. King: What about the girlfriend; do you hate her? Hoyt Williams: I don’t trust her, but I don’t hate her. They needed money and they used me. I hate racial slurs and was wrong in using them. I have beaten up racists. King: Beaten up racists? Hoyt Williams: Oh yea, in the ring I have beaten up evil and vile racists. They are not men of God, but rather wimps. I hate racists. I was wrong in my words Larry, but never in my actions. King: Never in your actions? Hoyt Williams: I treat everybody the same. You can look at my entire history, and see that I've been nothing but respectful to every type of man who walks the earth. King: I understand that, not only are you running for president, but you’re also in a tournament in the rassling federation PRIME? Hoyt Williams: That is correct! Not only will I win the office of President, but I also will become the Jewel in the Crown. King: Has PRIME thought about taking you off the air? Hoyt Williams: I hope not Larry. I’m a good man who said the wrong thing. King: This coming week you are in a match against Joshua Taro Freedom. Hoyt Williams: I was a big fan of JTT when he was on Home Improvement. I hope he’s lifted some weights, because I want a challenge, not a wimp. By the way Larry, I’m going to dedicate this match to all the Eskimos out there. King: Wonderful, lets take some calls. Nookanana from Cold City, Alaska go. Nookanana: Hoyt you hate everybody why should we vote for you? Hoyt Williams: Because I’m looking to make the world a better place. If you vote for me, I’ll put more ice in your igloos and more fish in your belly. King: How do you compare yourself to Don Imus or Michael Richards? Hoyt Williams: Oh gosh Larry, I really can’t. Now that I have walked in their shoes, I realize I’m much more talented then them. So it’s unfair to compare us. King: We're all out of time, catch Hoyt Williams this week on PRIME, and me tomorrow night with Dr. Phil. Any final words? Hoyt Williams: Please forgive me, and I will take it out on this JTT kid in the first round of JITC. He will be crucified and saved in the name of all Eskimos. =w=
