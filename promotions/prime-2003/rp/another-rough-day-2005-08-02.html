Another rough day

2 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-02-05 Chris Collins stood before the mirror watching the many reflections shimmer on its surface. In an instant, memories began to drown his head embracing him with emptiness and darkness sending him into a trance. Reminiscing the thoughts of the past caused his lower lip to widen slightly over the top as two dimples formed on each of his cheeks. His reflection reminded him of everything he lived for. Love, hate, hope and despair. It reminded him of the times when he felt abandoned and thought that life was nothing but a lost cause. He continued to stare at the mirror looking into the burning eyes of his reflection before dropping his head into his hands. “How could I like this horrid reflection?” He muffled a question of himself. With both hands he reached for the sink pulling the handles of both the hot and cold water towards him. A few drops eased their way through the holes of the faucet before flowing out like a waterfall. Chris put forward two cuffed hands and sent water splashing into the pores of his face, in an attempt to wake himself. “Ah,” He moaned in a refreshing delight. “That feels much better.” His fluttered his eyes before pushing the two handles back into their original positions as the water halted. The water that lies in the sink like a fragile sheet of glass slowly made its way down the clogged drain. From behind him shards of light wash in through the cracks of the tightly closed door. Reaching over his shoulder he grabbed a white towel which hung from the rack near him and quickly used it to cover his face. Using both hands he worked through the warmth and softness to dry himself off. “Amber?” He bellowed into the darkness reaching and turning the doorknob exposing him to the light. “Is that you?” “Of course it’s me silly.” She giggled scaring him a bit. “Who else would it be?” She crept through the doorway from down the hall which lead to the kitchen and began to walk towards him. Relieved, Chris looked down at his watch. “It’s Five O’clock. What are you doing up this early?” A yawn forced from her mouth. “I couldn’t sleep.” She told him. “Besides, I have a few errands that I need to run today.” “Oh.” He nodded his head in agreement slightly dumbfounded. “Well I’m going back to bed.” “Okay.” She accepted. “Do you want some breakfast?” He shook his head. “No. I’m feeling kind of sick.” She placed her hands on her hips and tapped her foot lightly against the wooden floor. Her head slightly tilted and a right brow was forced to the tip of her hairline. In seconds the right brow lowered and was replaced with a simple shrug of the shoulder. “Whatever I think your just nervous about your match with Hoyt.” Chris gave a slight nod and turned around only to head down the hall. He stopped abruptly in front of his bedroom, pushed open the door and walked inside. The panels on the floor creaked like the tune of a piano each step he took. He stood over the fairly large bed for two and like a weightless feather he flopped to the mattress. He rolled over on to his back and with his left hand reached for the light switch turning it off. A FEW HOURS LATER “Get up you lazy you… get up.” Amber bounced up and down on the mattress waking Chris from a much needed sleep. He was tired and in too deep, he had been out for at least six hours. His eyes were red and tears slowly rolled down the sides of his cheeks. “Huh… what the…What’s going on?” “You’re late!” “I’m late for what?” He asked of her. “Your training.” With all his might and the use of his arms he slowly lifted himself up only to fall flat on his face and buried into the covers. He twisted his head in the sheets and rolled over on to his back breathing and panting deeply. Amber moved in and placed her hand on top of his head. He was covered in sweat and his body was extremely above normal temperature. “You’re steaming hot.” She told him. “You really aren’t feeling good are you?” “No. I’m having trouble breathing as well.” He took in one deep breath after another as if he were breathing through a coffee straw. “Don’t worry about it though. This always happens. I just need a couple of hours maybe days to rest and then I’ll be all good again.” “A couple of days?” She asked. “What do you mean this always happens?” “I don’t know.” He replied. “Ever since I was a kid. It happens at least once a month.” Amber slowly rose from the bed. She placed her feet back into the slippers that she had recently removed and walked over to the nightstand by the bed. “At least once a month? Ever since you were a child? You mean you never told anyone about this?” “No.” Chris replied. “I never thought anything of it. It’s just so common I’m sort of used to it.” “Chris, this could be serious.” She removed the covers from the bed and threw them down to the floor. Her eyes grew wider at what she saw next. “What the…” “What?” He asked. She held her mouth as if she were going to hurl. “Nothing it’s just. Your hands and feet they’re humongous!” He nodded. “Oh.” He took a breath. “That. Don’t worry about it. That’s normal.” “It’s not normal. It never happens to me.” She removed the telephone from its base and began to dial a few numbers. “I’m calling my uncle. He’s a doctor; he’ll know what to do.” She headed for the door. Chris tried to lift himself but Amber noticed. “No. You stay here and don’t make any sudden moves. I’ll be back.” Few Minutes Later Amber walked into the room her head down and the phone hanging from her hand. Chris looked up through the darkness. “Well what did he say?” “The good news is that you’re all right.” She told him. “After explaining your symptoms he believes that you may have sickle cell anemia.” “Sickle cell? What?!” To be Continued…
