Dealing with adversity

3 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 3rd ### Disclaimer: A lot of the earlier bits are mainly for background and shows Neil and Gideon off camera as well. By separating things into televised and non televised I’m going to be using the camera as a 3rd person to be introduced to the scenes when relevant. Neil Whittaker and Neil Fracas act similar but are different in many ways while Gideon will consistently be a ruthless asshole. ### Last week was the Alliance’s second wrestling match. They had the perfect plan. They had done the preparation and knew how they were going to deal with everyone in the ring. Neil and Gideon were in ring shape again and functioning well. It was going to be the night that the Alliance was going to put everyone on notice that the Alliance were in PRIME and they were going to be a force to be reckoned with. When the bell rang at Revolution 92 all those plans went down in flames. Neil and Gideon looked great. They functioned like a finely oiled machine and definitely made their presence known. They even managed to punish a few mistakes because they were ready with counters. Then it was all over. It came out of nowhere then the ref motioned for the bell to ring. The winner of the match Titan St. James. They never even saw it coming. There was nothing they could do to stop it. The thing that bothered them the most was that was the exact same strategy they had planned. Get a flash pin or submission so sudden that no one would even think to break it up. That was something they should have prepared for. They knew how dangerous Titan St. James was and knew he was a wrestler. He might not have had the grappling skills of Neil or the submission skills of Gideon but the man was ex-military and a very sharp man. There are always setbacks. This was a missed opportunity. Neil and Gideon could have taken a short path to a title shot. They were beaten by Titan St. James because quite frankly Titan was the better man. There is no sugar coating it and no selling it. Titan walked into that match as focused as they were. There was no luck involved by any stretch of the imagination. Both Titan and the Alliance were looking for that first mistake to win the match and Titan found the error first and he took the win. Can’t take that away. Man’s a pro and if they had to lose the match to anyone better to lose to a man like Titan St. James that has the same drive and work ethic. Time to deal with adversity. ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** It is 7 am in the morning on Tuesday. Neil is getting up and starting to get dressed. He turns and looks at Stephanie sleeping peacefully. He smiles thinking how tired she must be. She put a smile on both their faces last night and they didn’t get very much sleep. Have to check the wall though after she gets up though. There was a loud crack a couple times. Kinda thought the foam pad between the headboard and the wall would have held up longer then a week. Neil is snickering softly as he gets up. He’s a VERY lucky man. He grabs an old T-Shirt and his sunglasses off the dresser and unlocks the door as he goes to go for a morning run. He locks it again Ricky usually knocks before entering but why take chances. As Neil walks down the hallway the mansion is quiet. Tony and Bobby are likely fast asleep. Today is going to be a day they all relax and have a barbecue and watch a few movies. Thank goodness Bobby and Tony are around. When they were hurt a while back Stephanie took them in. She needed someone to look after the house while she was on the road wrestling and she liked them. They have been here for over 6 years and helped raise Ricky as a little brother. They have grown to think of Bobby and Tony as brothers. When both Neil and Stephanie were wrestling it was nearly impossible to take a baby on the road. They never had to worry about Ricky and to a man like Neil that means a great deal. If anyone ever messed with Bobby and Tony Neil would seriously hurt them and Gideon would make them disappear. He hears noise from Ricky’s room. He must be up early. Ricky hears Neil walking up to the door and looks up. Neil: Feel like going with me on my morning run Ricky? Ricky: Sure. Neil helps Ricky get dressed and gets ready to go. They have on their sneakers and Neil puts Ricky on his shoulders. Ricky giggles as they leave through the front door and Neil starts jogging at a brick pace. Ricky: Is Uncle Gideon still coming over dad. Neil: Yup. You know he wouldn’t miss it. Ricky: Can I ask you a question daddy? Neil: Sure. Ricky: Why are you and Uncle Gideon wrestling again? Neil has to think about that for a few minutes as he is jogging. Ricky seems a bit confused. How do you put something like this into something a 4 year old can understand when he doesn’t even understand himself. Ricky is sitting on his shoulders not understanding why Neil hasn’t said anything. Ricky: You aren’t mad are you? Neil: Nah I’m not mad. I’m just trying to figure out how to explain this. Neil continues to jog along thinking of the words but it isn’t easy. He can’t say he wants to beat the living f$%k out of these sports entertainment idiots that are killing the business. He can’t say that he loves wrestling because he told himself from the day that Ricky was born that he was never going to lie to him. How do you put into words how much wrestling changed his life. How if it wasn’t for wrestling he would have likely died in some bar fight hating the world. If it wasn’t for wrestling he wouldn’t have met Stephanie and found something worth living for. Or if it wasn’t for wrestling he would have Ricky on his shoulders and been part of a family. Neil: Remember when I tripped on a toy and fell in your room and how daddy was saying some very bad words that I told you never to say? Ricky: You were pretty mad. Neil: Remember when I told you to clean your room and how you didn’t want to do it because you thought everything was all right? Ricky: Yeah that was a lot of work and I wasn’t able to play until I was done. Neil: There are bad people that have made wrestling like that and it makes daddy and Uncle Gideon very mad. We don’t like cleaning up messes either. Ricky is giggling as they continue on to the 2 mile mark where Neil turns and heads back to the mansion. Neil speeds up and Ricky is screaming wheeeeee as they make their way back. Neil is running now and smiling. Life is good. No matter what happens in the ring Neil can always come home to what really matters. Time for a shower and who knows maybe even a quickie or two before Gideon comes over. ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** ***** It is later on that day. Gideon has arrived and he brought food as well as a few advance screeners for movies not even out in the theatres yet. They are in the Kitchen. Neil, Stephanie, Gideon, and Tony are preparing dinner. Bobby is looking after Ricky and keeping an eye on him so he doesn’t get into any mischief. Neil is preparing 3 trays of what he calls Fracas fries. For those of you wondering what they are Neil shreds up some potatoes and cuts up a variety of vegetables like green peppers, onions, carrots, and celery. The mixture is covered in garlic butter and cheese and baked. Stephanie is going to be cooking up the steaks which is a far cry from her earlier days when her cooking was met with a mixture of dread and fear. Stephanie is mixing up her basting sauce, which has barbecue sauce, Worchester, and dry garlic. Bobby is making up a pasta dish best described as lasagna with spaghetti noodles. Gideon is preparing a stir fry dish of mushrooms, onions, and green peppers. Luckily it is a very large kitchen with an electric and a propane stove. While they prepare supper they are shooting the breeze. Neil: What’s the plan for the movies? Bobby: Any action ones? Gideon: Several. Bobby: What about the Blood Rayne one. Gideon: Never mention anything done by Uwe Boll to me. Though I consider it practical that his prostitutes got air time. After all when you are directing a 40 million dollar loss you might as well get a few prostitutes. Neil: So what’s on deck for Prime? Gideon: Put in an appearance then go home. Neil: So basically we get paid for showing up and doing nothing. Gotta love modern day wrestling where even a corpse can put out the same work rate. Stephanie: If they are paying people to do nothing is it any wonder that wrestling is going down the crapper. Gideon: The Ultra Violence PPV is coming up. We should do something anyway. Neil: F$%k it. I’d rather spend time here with Stephanie and Ricky. Bobby: Why aren’t you guys booked? Gideon: Management doesn’t know anything about us. They are still sorting out the tag division now that they decided the belts are going to be revived. We lost a cluster F$%k match and now we are waiting for them to figure out what they are going to do with everyone else. Stephanie: Always hated those. Neil: Can’t knock it too much. Titan won the match and he did it the same way we planned on winning the match so it wasn’t like some jackoff fluked his way into the title picture. Gideon: Still annoys me that we lost. Neil: Annoys me too but it is one of those matches. Even wrestling a solid match and not making any mistakes doesn't matter because. Someone lost before they could lose to us. Gideon: Yup why it is so annoying. Stephanie: Good thing about matches like is that it reminds me why I’m glad I retired. Bobby: What movies we watching? Gideon: X-Men 3 and Lucky Number Slevin. One of the perks of having a movie studio is free swag. Neil: Have you watched them yet? Gideon: Nope. Stephanie: Good now we don’t have to worry about you annoying us with a “hey watch this” every once in a while. Gideon feigns looking hurt as he holds his chest. Gideon: You wound me. Would I do that? Neil + Bobby + Stephanie: Yup. They are all snickering as they continue preparing the food. Gideon looks in the fridge for anything else to toss in the stir fry. Neil has the trays of Fracas fries ready. Bobby is putting the finishing touches on his pasta dish. Gideon grabs a pot and dumps the stir fry out of the frying pan into it and then puts the pot into the fridge. They put the utensils into the washer and then clean up the kitchen. Neil: Which movie first? Gideon: X-Men Bobby: Lucky Number Slevin. Stephanie: X-Men. It should be all right for Ricky to watch too. Neil: Done. After deciding they put the food in the oven to cook while they are watching X-Men 3. They may have lost a match but there is more to life then wrestling and they have a lot of free time they can use while things get sorted out.
