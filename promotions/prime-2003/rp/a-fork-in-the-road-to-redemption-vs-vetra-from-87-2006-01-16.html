A Fork in the Road to Redemption ( .vs. Vetra from 87)

16 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16th January 2006 “Jesus Christ on a bi-… Ar- that hurts alright… Fu- yeah, that hurts too…” Ignatius Lisieux winced with the throb of pain and anguish as a currently faceless doctor went to work examining his post-Great American Nightmare injury collection. “So from the top-down, what exactly were you diagnosed with after the night of Sunday December 18th?” asked the female physician with slightly more than a raised eyebrow and a twinge of shock in her vocal tone. “Err… lemme see… severe concussion, wounded forehead, cuts to the temple and crown, split eyebrows, black eyes, trauma to cheekbones, broken nose – in three god-damn places! Err… missing teeth, busted lips…” Before Lisieux can finish the impressive run-down of his various damages, courtesy of Ozric Mortimer, he is cut off by the surprised expression and disbelieving interruption of his doctor… “Whoa, whoa… hold up there, all of that just on your cranium?!” “Yes ma’m, he was a big guy you know? A big… angry guy,” responded Ignatius with a perverted glimmer of fond remembrance of his epic battle at the last PRIME pay-per-view event. “…I thought wrestling wasn’t real?” semi-inquired the young doctor, without trying to sound too ignorant or too rude. “Wrestling isn’t real… but PRIME is,” retorted the French-Canadian with a shiny white grin. The doctor laughed at the sight of his fixed-up mouth, “I see you’ve had the tooth situation rectified already.” “Oh yeah, that was the first thing I had repaired, PRIME are actually offering me medical AND dental cover now that I survived Mortimer.” And there it was again, a grin that could literally blind anyone who got too close to it. The actual truth was that despite his Hollywood smile Ignatius Lisieux still looked something of a mess. The traces of cuts and bruises abound across the landscape of his face like faded craters on the lunar surface, his lips were still semi-swollen and his nose was still bent out of joint. Somehow, though, he still looked better than he had before his match with Ozric, perhaps because he was just wearing his underwear. “…Dare I ask what injuries you possess below the neckline?” the Doctor asked with a hint of playful reservation. “You may… I was kinda hoping you would, actually, chicks dig a tough guy,” Ignatius immediately backed-up this rather forward and impressively bold exclamation with a nervous laugh before being promptly shot-down anyway. “I’m a Doctor, not a “chick” – I just want to get you fixed, it’s what they pay me for.” Without a moment of wounded pride or regret the French-Canadian remained on-topic, “So do you think I can get back into the ring yet?” His eyes opened wide with the renewed hope of a return to action, but once again he was shot-down. “It doesn’t seem likely to me… now what else were you diagnosed with?” “…Sprained neck, dislocated shoulder on the right side, nursemaid’s elbow on the left side…” Lisieux continues as the Doctor hastily looks over the various aforementioned body parts to move along with the examination. “…Three broken fingers, these two here… and that one there… Some minor internal damage to the kidneys, three cracked ribs, a couple of bruised kneecaps and a sprained right ankle.” One deep breath later and it was all over, but Lisieux had seemingly left his doctor speechless. She reached up to her face to slowly remove her glasses before letting her flowing golden locks down and diving on top of the French-Canadian to straddle him, Ignatius’ wounded face was a picture of shock as he was engulfed with the passion of lust as the doctor kissed him powerfully and erotically. Lisieux wrapped his arms around her waist and then began to pull off her white coat, revealing a laced red brazier that was bulging with the size of her bazookas. She kissed downwards on Ignatius’ body, heading straight towards his crotch until he was interrupted by a voice calling out to him… “Mr. Lisieux… Mr. Lisieux are you alright? There you go, just grip onto my forearm there - we’ll get you back up here…” His eyes opened up to the real world to see the attractive doctor disappointingly fully dressed in her attire, and not kissing him but rather struggling to lift him back up onto the examination table. Momentarily Ignatius had regained control of his motor functions… aside from one prominent piece of activity in his underwear region - of course it did not go unnoticed… “Oh… oh my, excuse me one moment…” One flustered and exasperated leaving doctor later and Ignatius Lisieux was left to feel decidedly embarrassed and ashamed all alone, practically naked and sporting a boner, on a cold-as-hell steel examining bed wondering what the hell had just happened. “What the hell just happened?” he thought (told you so!) A few extremely long and uncomfortable seconds later an ageing male doctor appeared through the covered swing doors that led into the small room. His floppy grey hair rested on the rims of his chrome glasses and his face was long and full, capped-off with a huge, bulging nose directly in the centre of it. “Mr. Lisieux, my name is Doctor McLean and I’ll be continuing the rest of your examination… it seems that it happened yet again to Doctor Matheson,” he introduced himself without so much as a tone-change or a chuckle at the unfortunate event that had led to his arrival. “Oh God, I’m sorry… is she alright? I must have passed out or something, I honestly did not intend to cause any offence.” Lisieux was basically mortified, perhaps as embarrassed as he had been at any one time in his entire adult life, this was just too bad to be true. Doctor McLean ushered a forced smile before he spoke once more, “It’s fine, she will be alright… I suppose it’s just something she is going to have to deal with, I mean who wouldn’t like to just get up on that and go to town… I mean I could think of a few thousands things I’d like to-“. The sickening tangent was cut off as McLean finally looked at the shocked and disgusted expression being sported by the professional-wrestler sat semi-naked before him. The doctor’s voice had risen to a crescendo in his flurry of excitable fantasy, but now upon the grim realisation that he had let his perverted guard slip it returned to mono-tone. “Anyway…” he straightened his collar, “What seems to be the matter?” Lisieux rolled his eyes at the prospect of having to reel-off his insurmountable list of ailments to yet another physician, but he did it anyway, “From top-to-bottom: severe concussion, wounded forehead, cuts to the temple and crown, split eyebrows, black eyes, trauma to cheekbones, broken nose, missing teeth, busted lips… What? Yes I know it’s surprising forward-slash impressive, I’ve already been through this with the other one… I suggest you take a seat…” The doctor obliged as Ignatius continued, “Three broken fingers, minor internal damage to the kidneys, three cracked ribs, a couple of bruised kneecaps and a sprained right ankle. And yes, I know my teeth are all better, we don’t need to go over that again… what I really came here for was to find out if-“ Doctor McLean put his hands on his knees before rising back up to a vertical base and stroking his chin and pondering to himself, “I’m going to have to give you an once-over…” he muttered sinisterly (Cue another horrified expression on the face of the French-Canadian.) “My colleague told me you passed out from the pain when she attempted to rotate your shoulder, now as you might guess that isn’t exactly a great sign. Haven’t you had your arm in a sling already?” Lisieux looked slightly embarrassed again, “Well I was supposed to, but how the hell am I supposed to go through my workout routine with a sling on my arm? It just can’t work…” He was cut-off by the shocked interruption of Doctor McLean… “You mean to tell me that you’ve continued weight training with all of these medical issues?” interrogated the doctor with something of a severe tone to his voice. Ignatius was taken aback by the venom accompanying the question and meekly responded, “Well I had to take a week or so off because it was hurting so bad but then I was back in the gym, I need to get back up to my weight if I’m going to be back wrestling for good…” Doctor McLean looked immensely frustrated at what he was hearing as responded aggressively, “You need to heal before you can resume physical activity!” An awkward silence descended upon the small room as Lisieux immediately looked down at his feet, feeling slightly ashamed although also wondering if he had actually done anything wrong. The ageing doctor was staring intently at his patient, shaking his head, searching for a solution… “I really do not recommend that you return to your business until you are one-hundred-percent recuperated. These are a collection of highly dangerous injuries that you’ve gathered and you have not allowed them to heal correctly. If you get back into a wrestling ring in this condition then you’re nothing more than a ticking time-bomb.” Grim reality descended over the now-sullen Ignatius Lisieux. As a former PRIME Global Champion, a man who once sat atop the wrestling world, news like this was infuriatingly difficult to hear. For a man with the professional pride an ego of the French-Canadian it was so easy to ignore the advice of experts and to sugar-coat their words as nothing more than worst-case-scenario. This was a make-or-break moment for Ignatius Lisieux right here in the awkward silence of the Berwick Medical Centre in Albany. As far as he could see there were two paths to go down. Path A was to take the advice of the third physician he had seen this week, all of whom had told him the same thing, and take at least another four weeks off from active competition and training to recuperate fully from his numerous injuries and ailments and ultimately miss the Dual Halo at Culture Shock. Path B was to ignore the words of three of the best physicians in the country and find a doctor who would be willing to sign a waiver to allow him to resume wrestling activities with PRIME. He thought it could work if he took a course of painkillers before and after each match he was involved in. As Doctor McLean stared quietly waiting for the answer he expected – but didn’t hope for – another little something inside of him died at the thought of another young man succumbing to the tolls of professional wrestling. As an experienced professional, McLean had seen numerous cases of where someone in the industry needed substantial time off to recover from collected injuries but could not take the time off because of numerous factors. Maybe they feared being released or not having enough money if they were inactive for too long, perhaps they had too many immediate scores to settle with various colleagues, maybe some of them, much like Ignatius Lisieux, were just downright deluded and engulfed with professional pride and ego to take a backseat for a little while. Then the hammer blow came… “Screw this, I’ll find someone to sign a waiver to allow me to compete, I’m heading to Philadelphia for ReV eighty-seven immediately. Sorry for wasting your time and please apologise to Doctor Matheson for me.” Doctor McLean nodded solemnly before uttering a chilling retort, “I’m sorry to hear that Ignatius, unfortunately I get the feeling I will be seeing you soon.” “Yeah you’ll be seeing me all right, ReVolution eighty-seven, Wednesday night on Playboy TV, that’s where you’ll be seeing me..” Lisieux struggled pulling on his faded jeans as Doctor McLean exited the examination room with a bowed, shaking head. Lisieux looked at his back and paused for a moment of clarity as the chance once again descended to save himself from himself… but something inside of the French-Canadian forced him to ignore the voice of sanity pleading within for some rest. He slammed on his black Macbeth tee-shirt and fixed his watch and strap wristband back into place before checking his hair in a glass mirror and heading for the exit himself, he snapped on some black shades as he emerged in the cold winter chill of Albany, New York - his mind a flurry of activity. Maybe it was the Dual Halo at Culture Shock that prevented him from making the sensible choice or maybe it was just Ignatius Lisieux that presented him from making the sensible choice. Whatever it was, just as Doctor McLean had warned, Ignatius Lisieux was now a ticking time-bomb, just like so many wrestlers had been before him. What lay ahead for the French-Canadian seemed to be a lifetime of unrelenting aches and pains, drug and alcohol abuse, failed marriages and a whole heap of personal demons: he must have been the only one who didn’t see that. As the cool winter sun beat down on the damaged face of the former Global Champion, his only thought was one of where to find a doctor crooked enough to sign a waiver and write out repeat prescriptions for him over a long spell of time, however long PRIME wanted him. The downward spiral had already begun. Unless somebody was prepared to save him – because it seemed damn certain that he was not prepared to save himself – the name of Ignatius Lisieux would join the ever-growing list of great wrestlers who had died before their time.
