Knowing the More Important Things - Family vs. Wrestling (ReV 125)

Date: This Week Sometime Monday, April 16, 2007 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “No no Daddy.” Desiree’s weak voice cracked, trying to pull the toy horse back from her father and put it back into the play stable with all of the other animals. “But sweetie, you want to take this to Uncle Matt’s and Aunt Mary’s right? We need to pack it.” Was Killean’s reply, trying to reason with his young daughter. Sometimes, it was more of a task than reasoning with Chet Worth when he was still PRIME’s CEO. “All of them go.” Her broken English amused Killean, but packing all the toys that she wanted to take definitely didn’t. ”That’s a lot of damn toys.” He said to himself as he went to go get the bigger bag across the room. But before he got down to packing the gazillion toys that she wanted to take, he had to make her lunch first. Now was the chore of finding out what she wanted. “Alright baby girl, what do you wanna have for lunch?” He plucked her from the seated position on the floor and tickled her a little bit, winning a pleasant giggle. He walked to the kitchen and opened up the pantry for her to take a look at all the nice, yet healthy snacks to eat. She pointed to something, making little whining noises. Trying to teach her how to speak was difficult sometimes but luckily she was a pretty bright kid. “Use your words sweetie.” “Fruit cup!” She yelped, kind of startling herself with the volume in her voice. “That’s it? That’s not a full lunch. How about a peanut butter and jelly sandwich with some milk?” She clapped her hands and Killean turned to sit her in her high chair, strapping her in. He then went to gather her lunch. He would then play with Desiree and put her down for a nap. Yep, this was a typical day in Killean’s life when he was home. His daughter had become his main priority once again ever since his addiction and rehab time. He had lost his way in that sense, barely seeing her for a few hours every time he was back in town. It was sad that it took something so serious to snap him back to reality, not to mention almost orphaning his child. He certainly didn’t want her to go to Stacey as she was still probably as messed up as always. So he made it his goal to be the perfect father. Desiree went with him on the road, even though that was a lot of travelling for a small kid to endure. Luckily, she adapted to it quickly and even enjoyed the flying. She had seen more cities than most kids her age and that was not about to change anytime soon. Normally, after he put Desiree down for a nap, he would work out on his Bowflex for the duration. He rarely went to the gym anymore, unless he was away from home and needed to get a workout in before an important match. He knew he would hit the gym a couple of times before ReVolution 125 when he joins Matt in the ring against Tony Gamble and Brandon Youngblood. But his recent issues with addiction and a near death experience had put like into perspective even more for The PRIME Choice. What was really more important? Beating someone in the ring… or being a father to his daughter? Recently, he was trying to mix both, taking Desiree with him to events more and more. But he was silently wondering to himself how long he could keep that up. When would the time come where he said enough was enough? That was the main difference between himself and Brandon Youngblood. The arrogant fuck had time ahead of him and wrestling was his life, his death and his soul. Killean used to be like that, but those times are behind him. He was twenty-eight, a youth in the eyes of many. But to himself, he has recently felt that his wrestling career is winding down. Now that doesn’t mean he will be gone in a year. Heck, he could be around for another five years, but he knows that he won’t push it beyond what he wants to. When the time comes to call it a career and hang up the ass kicking boots, he’ll do just that and without hesitation too. Killean kind of thought the obsession that Brandon had with PRIME and wrestling in general was something that made a wrestler an actual superstar. So why did he have so much animosity towards the former PRIME outcast? It was simple really… he just didn’t like him. Couple that with the fact that he thinks he made PRIME what it was today and you have a recipe for disaster in the life of a Hall of Famer… especially an active one. But that was a story for another day. Killean had grown tired of Brandon and he wanted to end it at Ultra Violence. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tuesday, April 17, 2007 “Ok really, what is it that these kids see in this stuff?” Killean asked, sitting back in the recliner with Matt on the other side of the end table in a chair of his own. Sponge Bob was playing on the TV with Georgie and Desiree paying very close attention. “You’d swear there was some mind control technique or something huh? They’re glued to it.” Matt replied, passing a bottle of water to Killean that he had just recently grabbed from the kitchen fridge. He then passed a bottle of juice to Mary, who was lying on her side on the couch looking a little under the weather. “It’s not that…” She stops to cough a little, grabbing a Kleenex from the box. “It’s the colors and the funny looking characters. It doesn’t take much to entertain a child.” Killean looks at Desiree and sees that she is enjoying herself. Georgie is also having a good time and the kids seem to be communicating with their laughter instead of words. Funny how kids can do that too. Killean gets a sad look on his face and excuses himself from the family room as he moves out the front door to stand on the porch. Matt and Mary look at each other and with a nod, he is quick to follow his friend. Standing out on the porch, Killean takes a sip of his water as he leans against the rail looking in to the bright blue sky. The sun shines down, casting it’s reflection into the red sunglasses that Sirrajin is wearing. Matt opens the door and comes out onto the porch, a new can of Mountain Dew in his hand. He comes to stand alongside Killean. “What’s buggin’ ya partner?” Matt said, referring to them teaming together this week on ReVolution. Matt could sense when something was bugging Killean. After all, he used to be his psychologist. “I dunno man. One second I’m pumped to be in the main event tagging with you this week and the next I’m wishing that I could just stay home and spend all my time with Desiree.” He spoke quietly and took another sip from his bottled water. “I know how you feel.” Matt came to lean on the rail the same as Killean was. “I have some good weeks at home with Mary and Georgie. Back when I was off after the beating I took at the hand of FU, I wanted to stay home for good. I was ready to hang it up. But deep inside, I still had that hunger. Not to mention that I finally got Mary’s blessing.” “But see that’s the thing…” Killean said with a little bit of frustration in his voice. He got up from leaning on the rail and walked into the grass in front. “You got the blessing of Mary, something you had been looking to get for as long as I can remember. But what about me? What would Desiree say if she knew what he father was doing week in and week out? What would she think of me? I wouldn’t want her to think I was some brute… some savage that enjoyed getting into fights. Youngblood’s life blood is wrestling. I used to be that way, but now I… dunno.” He dropped his arms at his side, frustrated with his mind for dwelling on these thoughts instead of moving forward like he has done in the past. “Well let’s think about that. Think of Desiree being a teenager and you still being in the wrestling business. On one hand, she could think exactly how you think she would, thinking that you are a brute savage who loves to hurt other people and get hurt himself. She could reject you, which is a highly unlikely story. You would have raised her and she would have a connection to her father automatically.” Killean listened to Matt, wondering if Matt had gone through these same things with Georgie. “Second, she could look at her father as her hero. He busts his ass, goes far away to work and support his family and makes an impact on other people around the world. She listens to the cheers and knows that people love the show her father helps put on. Or third, she may not even care and look at it as her father is doing something he loves to do. Which do you prefer?” Matt took a long sip from his Dew as Killean stood, looking into the blue sky. He really didn’t know which he preferred, although he knew that the first choice was the one that he feared. Would it really be that bad though? He was sure Desiree would support him no matter what but no one could really say. She was too young to really know what her father did. Matt noticed that Killean was distracted and decided to leave him to his thoughts. “Our plane is leaving at eight. We got some ass to kick.” Matt said before disappearing inside the house, closing the screen door behind him. It was complicated, yet simple. Brandon Youngblood’s life was the game that they were playing. Killean loved the business, but had relaxed his stance in the past year. This would be Brandon’s downfall. Killean was comfortable with his position and Brandon always wanted more. Being comfortable was ok, but getting complacent was something that Killean wouldn’t do. He would go out there at 125 and at Ultra Violence and do what he does best and what he loves. He would wrestle, entertain the fans and he would win. But most of all, he was a family man supporting his daughter… the little laughs of Desiree enough to keep him going forever.
