Date: Ultra Violence AVANTGARDE. AVANTGARDE. AVANTGARDE. AVANTGARDE. AVANTGARDE. AVANTGARDE. AVANTGARDE. AVANTGARDE. AVANTGARDE. AVANTGARDE. AVANTGARDE. AVANTGARDE. It's the future. The big fight night. The fans have barely taken their seats when PRIME-Tron lights up with the JUST Wrestling logo!? ((CUT TO: Dirty blonde headed chick with a low cut V neck and a permanent pout to her droopy lips. She jiggles on the spot)) LADY: OK NOW THIS IS THE FIRST PRIME PAY PER VIEW FOR THE IMAGINARY MAN, SKYLAR MONTGOMERY! SO I WANT YOU TO ALL CLAP YOUR HANDS OK!? THE INDY FED THAT SKYLAR CAME FROM, JUST WRESTLING, DIED LAST WEEK BUT DON'T BE SAD OK, BECAUSE SKYMONT IS STILL HERE IN PRIME AT ULTRA VIOLENCE 2010!!! ((CUT TO: Sampled clip of ring announcer, Vince Howard, standing in the centre of the ring)) VINCE HOWARD: PUT YOUR HANDS TOGETHER FOR... ((RED)) *explosion sound* ((CUT TO: SkyMont with a brown paper bag over his face with two eyeholes cut out. "MAIN CHARACTER" T-Shirt)) MONTGOMERY: JUST IS DEAD! HOLY fucking mother of fucking holy hell heaven damn god, fucking, Just Wrestling is dead. TIM SHIPLEY!! (He points at us) It's not over between me and you. It's not over now and it will never be over until I say it is The End. WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO~!!!!! ((CUT TO: Dirty blonde)) LADY: HE'S A CULT ICON BABY!!! AND HE'S LIVE IN WRESTLING ACTION TONIGHT!!! HERE AT ULTRA VIOLENCE 2010! ((CUT TO: Skylar stands in the thick of a swampy forest, camo-faced so that his chin-length ginger hair looks like it's just orbiting a pair of levitating eyes)) SKYMNT: I don't hate you Shipley. Far from it. I... want you to, accept me. I need your acceptance, Tim. That's all I want. I don't want your attention. I don't want your time. I don't wanna fuck you up, leave you bleeding on the floor and I don't want you to stop screwing me over in backstage social situations or anything. I... just... want... your acceptance. Man. ((He flicks his fringe over one of the floating eyes and it disappears in the same way the frothing black portal and it's nosey pink tentacle just did. CUT TO: A baby pool of blood on the floor growing drip by drip. We follow it's vertical footsteps to it's source, the tip of a sharp grey razorblade, dipped in thin coating of blood that skis the edge. Holding the blade is him)) SKMNT: It's me. You know me. Skylar Montgomery from JUST Wrestling. R.I.P Just Wrestling. ((He sits on a lockerroom bench, wearing a Just Wrestling "Long Hot Summer 2009" T-Shirt, sunglasses and a handkerchief wrapped over his mouth like he's Michael Jackson or Jay Terror or something)) MONTY: But you see whilst Tim Shipley – the founder of that company - paraded around, trying to peruse his own dreams of being a fucking PROTAGONIST. Trying to. To fucking get over.... first in HOW. Where I admittedly followed him coz I was new to the PTC scene and whatever, I only knew about JUST and couldn't get into any other fed. But then he got me beaten up by some old HOW veteran guy, and all the commentators didn't put me over... Simply to screw me over. In the eyes of the fans. And what nobody understands is that he's trying to do it in the eyes of myself aswell. He's trying to bring me down. Lower my self esteem and make me believe something other than the fact that I am the protagonist of what is quite clearly a narrative about being in a narrative. He is trying to be my fucking GOD and I just can't allow that to happen. ((He adjusts his glasses and flexes his spine)) SKYMNT: He wants to kill Just Wrestling and bring back AWC? Oh now come on, we all know it's OBVZviously him behind that aswell! This guy is a scumbag, and I'm glad I'm in PRIME coz GCW is shit. Fuck SCCW, fuck them they have von Doom. But FUCK GCW! So Shipley wants to kill Just Wrestling, eh? Show he's powerful. Well how about I just destroy his precious AWC!? MUAHAHAHAHA!! Nobody even knows what I really look like – I could just turn up as a young newcomer and infiltrate AWC and nobody would have a clue. HAHAHAHAHAHA! But No no no no no, I can't be bothered to do that. You and whole little gay clique are all scumbags I don't even wanna give your storylines any attention. You CREATE the storylines... I star in them. But fuck that, fuck that I don't BELONG to you anymore, Shipley. No. No, now I'm here alone and I've got a chance to start writing my own story... now I've got a bit of "free-will", HAHAHA! You get what I mean, right? - SHITLEY!! You're gonna find out that no matter what you do I am the de-fucking-termined to prove that I am The Protagonist of This Narrative that I always said I was. ((He stands up, brandishing the blade he's been holding in his hand the whole time. He cocks his heads towards the camera. Looks at the blade then back to the camera again)) SKYLAR: And now I'm gonna go and kidnap that slut Roxie Knightly if I see her alone somewhere. Or maybe I'll just go and (blade) poke Garbage Bag Johnny a couple of times and put down GCW and AWC's biggest star with a few fell swipes. Or maybe I'll just put the blade away... in my shoe. And go to my match and kill somebody. ((Points blade at us)) SKY: Whatever happens you people will all see and know that Just Wrestling is dead. _ SKY: But it's Cult Icon lives on. ((CUT TO: black))
