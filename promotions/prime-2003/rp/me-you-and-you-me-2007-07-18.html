If you missed last week’s episode: not a businessman, a business, man. So after last week’s triumphant (would we have it any other way?) return, there is no surprise that Tsonda is parading around the PRIME headquarters. What’s strange, though, is the mini-bureaucracy traveling around with him. Back at hero central, Chandler Tsonda gulps and looks at Facey, threatening to give his leather chaps a nice urine-y finish. “Those,” he whispers, “are them?!” “They’re harmless, bro. They’re all family men, got wifeys n shit.” Right on cue, their respective boos enter the room.
