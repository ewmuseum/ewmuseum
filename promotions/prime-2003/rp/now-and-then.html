Tyler Rayne had won more championships in more federations than he ever cared to count. Titles and accolades and shiny pieces of gold had lost all meaning for him. The world of professional wrestling had become a violent and bloody playground throughout the years. Personal feuds and vendettas had long ago filled the black void that once hungered for such meager symbols of acceptance. Of excellence. Or so he had thought. Yet here he was, alone in a locker room reeking of sweat, piss and broken dreams… unable to pull himself away. Unable to tear his eyes from the prize. He’d given away his last World Championship. Winning that championship meant that he had gone through some of the best the business had to offer.
