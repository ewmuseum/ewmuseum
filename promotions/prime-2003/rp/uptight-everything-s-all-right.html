Uptight, Everything's All Right

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 27-29 Angelo Frazetti has been involved in pro wrestling since the early 60s. He’s trained some of the biggest stars in pro wrestling, but the one who has always meant the most to him is current PRIMEate Andrew “SB” Rossi. That’s why it’s totally understandable that he’s pissed off. As he walks down the hall leading to SB’s hotel room, the scowl on his face is absolutely glowing. It’s bad enough that he’s as old school as they come and gets regular heartburn from dealing with SB’s antics, flashiness, and overall tomfoolery. But this time, he’s gone too far. Angelo half knocks, half punches the door threee sharp times, and adjusts the collar on his dress shirt. He just got back from a wake – he didn’t know the guy too well, he was a promoter down in Tampa for a while and SB worked there for about two years. Which leads us to why he’s about to rip SB an asshole big enough to shit out a Buick. On the other side of the door, SB is lying – naked as the day he was born, Johnson covered by sheets – on the King sized bed in the middle of the room. Some chick is out on the patio having a smoke, and another girl, as naked as SB, lies beside him. We can hear Angelo’s knocking coming from the hall, but SB just lies there. SB: Fuck off! Angelo: OPEN THIS FUCKING DOOR! SB: Eh? That you, Ange? Angelo: Open that door you little prick! SB closes his eyes and puts his hands over his face. Maybe he just realized what time it is – 3:PM and he’s still in bed – or maybe he just realized he missed Fat Jimmy Blanco’s wake. Oh, it was heart attack by the way. Poor guy. He rolls over, puts on a pair of boxers, and walks over to the door. Before he can get it open, Angelo bursts in and smacks him upside the head. SB: OW! What the fuck was that for?!? Angelo’s eyes are bugging out of his head, and it’s all he can do not to unload on SB again. Angelo: Don’t you have respect for anybody or anything, you spoiled punk? SB: Look, Ange, shit got crazy last night. You know how it goes... Just then, the girl on the patio walks past them and kisses SB on the cheek, leaving a lipstick mark on his cheek. We don’t get a good look at her face, but Angelo does. Angelo: Was that…was that Paris Hilton? Did you sleep with Paris Hilton last night. SB: SHH! Paparazzi’s been trying to bust in all fuckin’ morning. And what the fuck do you mean sleep? You know I can’t sleep with anyone touchin’ me! Angelo: How is THIS keeping a low profile and focusing on wrestling? SB: Well, I was real secretive about it. I wore a hat and everything! STEAM comes out of Angelo’s ears. Well, not really, but you can picture it. Angelo: I hope you’re talking about a condom. SB: …What the FUCK is a condom? Angelo: You sicken me. But honestly, you really thought you could just go out on a date with Paris Hilton and bang her in some seedy motel WITHOUT attracting any negative publicity? SB: Why not, I bang celebritiesin shittier hotels in every city we go to! Sure, I’m usually in the B-list and the “before they make it” range, but every now and then Big Daddy reels in a bigtime winner! Don’t act like you weren’t slingin’ dick all over the Portland territories back in the day. They called you the Man-gelo for a reason! Angelo: That’s not even the fuckin’ point, I actually worked my ass off, unlike you! SB: Are you kiddin’ me? Nobody balances fuckin’ and fightin’ better than me. I’m practically Derek Jeter. SB bows his head slightly. SB: I don’t understand why you have to break my balls so much. All I did was a have a little fun! Angelo: You skipped out on Jimmy’s wake. Pretty much every fuckin’ guy in the business that didn’t already think you were a punk knows it now. What’s wrong with you? Why can’t you ever get your head out of your ass long enough to see how close you are to being the best wrestler in the fuckin’ world? SB: I thought I already was? Angelo: Yeah, when you’re not chasin’ tail, rasin’ hell, and gettin’ drunk and fighting homeless people. SB: A homeless PERSON. And he started it! Angelo: Let’s not get into this again. The fact is, you don’t focus enough to ever be a world champion. SB: I also don’t get enough fuckin’ title shots to be a champion. As in, I’ve NEVER had a World Title shot. So I don’t see where you’re comin’ from. Angelo: Do you honestly expect to get title shots the way you act? You had a chance to beat Tony Rolo, a Living friggin’ Legend, and you kick him in the balls instead? Are you retarded? SB: He had it comin’! And I’m not done with that prick anyway, that was just me sending a message. Angelo: And what message is that? That you fight like a girl and care more about getting “revenge”? This isn’t the schoolyard, son. This is serious business, and you kissed away a huge opportunity to beat a former World Champion. SB: Like I said – I’m not done with him. Angelo: Well, for this week, you are. SB: Oh, that so? Angelo: I paid a visit to JP Cantrell, and I told him starting at 160 I’m your new manager. SB: No. fuck that. You know I don’t like anybody out there at ringside. You’ll be second-guessing me so much I’ll end up wrestlin’ you instead! Angelo: Who said anything about coming down to the ring with you? I’m just taking more of an active interest in your career…so you don’t, y’know, fuck it up any worse than you already have. SB: Ah, come on! How could you go over my head like that? Angelo: Hey, I’ll head back home if you want. And you can go back to spending more time on Page Six than training. SB: (laughs) Give me a break, would ya? I work my ass off! Angelo: Yeah, I know. But we’re goin’ back to the old way. SB: Oh shit. Angelo: (smiling) Oh shit is right, buddy. I’m gonna work you till you puke every morning, and if you got the strength to go out and raise hell every night, then I’ll have work you just a little harder. And if you still wanna go out, fine. But you’re gonna have to earn your playtime from now on, kid. No more resting on your laurels; your laurels ain’t so great anyway. You want to be a champion? You’re gonna have to start acting like one. This is PRIME. This is the best promotion on the planet, and they saw enough in you to sign you to a contract. I think it’s time you stop banking on potential, and prove your worth. SB looks at Angelo, tight-lipped. He nods silently, and reaches out and shakes Angelo’s hand. Angelo pats him on the back and winks. Angelo: Now put some fuckin’ clothes on, we gotta catch that plane. (line) Two days later. SB is standing in front of a PRIME banner, dressed rather stylishly in a Giorgio Armani suit. His hair, which he’s been letting grow a bit longer than usual lately, is pushed back for maximum handsomeness. SB: You know, it’s good to be back in Minnesota. As far as I’m concerned, it’s still the wrestling capital of the world. And wrestling’s my middle name. It doesn’t matter if you put me on the first match of the night, the main event, tag team, battle royal – whatever. When you got talent that goes on for days, every night’s a treat for anybody in attendance. Now, tag wrestling isn’t my favorite most of the time. I had a partner that tried to actually kill me by throwing me down a flight of stairs and kidnapped by wife, but that’s for those fuckin’ Canadians to remember and me to forget. Fact is, I’m DAMN GOOD at it. I carried my team in the A1E Tag Team Tournament, and even survived getting shafted by my first partner to make it all the way to the semis. Not bad for a guy who hadn’t tagged regularly in 6 years, huh? So if you’re thinking “maybe there’s some ring rust?” Think again. My opponents are pretty well known. Pretty accomplished. Pretty tough, pretty talented. I don’t give a shit. You see this face? I’m the best, baby. They don’t come any better than the Italian Icon. You could the highest mountain or swim the deepest sea, but there will never, ever, EVER be another me! All this match is, is another chance for me to show that even the very best of PRIME can’t stand up to me when I’m at my best. Now, you may think you’ve already seen all I can do, but there’s more – so much more. I’ve shown little glimpses, just flashes in the pan. But here me right now. This week, nothing is gonna get in my way. I guarantee victory. Logic, buddy, I hope you’re good at coattail riding, because you’re gonna do a lot of it at 159. Consider this SB’s official notice to the rest of PRIME. You might think I’m another new face – but soon enough, I’m going to be THE face. FTB
