Living La Vida Borracha

28 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 28th, 2009 The old adage tells us, that when the going gets tough, the tough get going. Sometimes, however, when the going gets particularly tough, like when you're on the road wrestling professionally more than three-hundred days a year, the tough finally admit that they've grown long tired of going, and simply get drinking. And does there exist a better place to do so than a toga party? I think not. And you know who agrees with me? Apparently half the roster, because this raging inferno of toga'd excess is simply roaring. And we didn't start the fire, but at this point, we probably all know who did. Colby Korver. Hank Cobb. Duke Williams. Delta Upsilon Iota. Entering their den of iniquity, there are several immediate observations to be had. Firstly, the Tsonda Interns are here, and in full force. Manning the catering table and tending the nearby full liquor bar and keg cornucopia, the champ has apparently sent his minions in his place, as PRIME's Universal Title holder is nowhere to be seen. Granted, their initial numbers have diminished greatly, as evidenced by the pile of formerly inebriated and presently unconscious bodies stacked behind a couch at the back of the room. The impressive catering table on the left side of the room is replete with each and every bit of food a hungry college kid could dream of. From stacks of mozzarella sticks to bowls of steaming Ramen noodles, this smorgasbord will definitely not last long in a room full of overgrown adolescents and rapidly dwindling adult beverages. The only item in jeopardy at the moment, however, are under direct assault by PRIME's newest free agent signee. Hunter Sabuani: (unintelligible mumbling) Although impossible to decipher, Sabuani's grunts do apparently succeed in their intention, as his colleague Miss Madison signals one of the nearby interns for replacement wings. Hunter Sabuani: (more unintelligible mumbling) Miss Madison: Another dozen honey garlic please. Hunter Sabuani: (even more unintelligible mumbling) Miss Madison: Well of course they're free, Hunter. Don't be ridiculous. Hunter Sabuani: (intelligible nodding and smiling) Miss Madison: Make it two dozen. But now that we've dispensed with the food and drink formalities, there is another very important note to be...well, noted. There are a lot of girls. I mean, a LOT of girls. But one in particular has apparently distanced herself a little too far from the crowd. Miss Madison: Hey, you there. Yeah, you, Big Boobs Magee. Mr. Sabuani already has plenty of legs at his disposal, so why don't you take a few steps back. Go find Duke Williams or something. I'm sure he won't take much effort, or convincing. And right on cue, as if Cantrell himself was working his reality show production truck magic. Duke Williams: So who wants the first ride on Malice Mountain?!? Aforementioned Floozie: Pick me! Duke Williams: Picked. Now let's blow this pop stand before He comes after me for copyright infringement. In all fairness, Duke's last remark doesn't make sense unless you are intensely aware that his wardrobe consists of an open Hoyt Williams robe. And that's all. Go ahead and let that marinate for awhile, as the tour continues on. Leaning against one of the walls, captivating several members of the crew, is Bryan Dawkins. Sipping on a tall glass of what looks to be pineapple juice, Dawkins urges any who will listen to visit the islands. His islands. Bryan Dawkins: I'm serious about this, brahs, you got to get out there. Spring, Summer, Winter or Fall, just go. And tell them B-Dawk sent you. Panning further to the right, The Union members Jack Conway and Luis Gallegos have taken it upon themselves to round up several crew members as well, albeit for less "vacationy" conversation. Luis Gallegos: I'm telling you, you can't just take that kind of stuff lying down anymore. It's time to rise up and take a stand. Jack Conway: He's right, fellas. If you won't form a united front now, when will you? But enough trade talk, can I grab any of you guys another drink? And the third crowd of crew members, the one that easily dwarfs the other two? That exists entirely because of its uber-hot epicenter. And that epicenter, clad only with a few stitches and bits of fabric, is Mary-Lynn Mayweather. Her conversation is momentarily interrupted, however, as the crowd in front of her suddenly parts ways, although the gap is immediately filled. Hank Cobb: Ms. Mayweather, honored to have you with us tonight. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Well Hank, I figured that I could use a little break from work, and that you boys would probably be pretty likely to provide one. Hank Cobb: Well I appreciate that, ma'am. Now it looks like you're fixin' to need another one of them martinis, may I offer my services? Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Well you most certainly may, Mr. Cobb. But only if your services are better than your driving. Hank's mouth drops open as he struggles to formulate a response. His efforts are quickly rendered needless, as Mayweather offers a mischievous smile. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Just make sure it's dirty. Hank Cobb: Yes ma'am. As Hank the Tank heads to the bar, he passes by a large potted plant of some sort on the far wall, situated between two full size lockers. Although going about six feet, it is not exactly the kind of decor that stands out, considering its present surroundings. Unless, of course, you're Fusenhoff. Fusenhoff: (pulling a Tsonda Intern aside) Was this here when you got here?!? Cause I swear to you I don't remember it being here an hour ago. How much clearance do you think there is behind that thing?!? Two feet?!? Three feet?!? You seen anyone snooping around?!? Anyone at all?!? As quickly as the intern can shrug his shoulders, he scurries off even faster, nearly tripping over his toga and the tensed frame of Jay Phoenix. Glancing nervously to and fro, Phoenix is taking every possible precaution to avoid any...accidents. The mounting pressure, however, seems to be entirely too much, as Phoenix manages a slight smile and nod for the true master of ceremonies, before taking his leave. Phoenix is immediately replaced twofold, as his exit also marks the arrival of Wicked Ways. As Vejumin Rippa and Scrap step through the doorway, they are immediately greeted not only by a suddenly hushed room, but also by that very same master of ceremonies. Colby Korver: Ok, boys, let's make this as short as possible, (nodding to Rippa) something you should be pretty familiar with. Why are you here? Scrap: We heard there was an open party. Is that true? Colby Korver: Are you hear for a drink or a fight? Cause I've got boatloads of both. Your choice. Vejumin Rippa: Relaaaax. We are here to relax and have good times. Colby Korver: (unclenching his fists) That works. I'm sure we have some Absolut and Sam Adams at the bar. Vejumin Rippa: Thanks kids. Come ons Scrap. Scrap: (shooting Korver a heated glare) Later on. Colby Korver: Exactly. A thunderous pounding on the locker room door alerts the nearby Korver to a yet unknown presence outside. But before he can respond, the door is thrown open, revealing its mystery assailant. Tyler Rayne. And like that, this seemingly uncontrollable party comes to a screeching halt. As the party-goers turn their attention to the doorway, Rayne's arms remain crossed and his expression cold. With a sneer, he marches immediately to Korver and Cobb. Tyler Rayne: Someone told me there was a...disturbance around here. Figured I'd come check it out. Colby Korver: Rayne Man, come on. We need this. I need this. Tyler Rayne: This is a travesty. An absolute travesty. Long dramatic pause... Tyler Rayne: I am appalled by the fact that I can't remember the last time I did a keg stand. So...who wants to spot me? Hank Cobb: I gotcha. With nary a moment of hesitation, Hank the Tank scoops Rayne up and situates him directly above a fresh keg. After about twenty seconds, or twenty ounces, whichever came last, Rayne drops topside and drags his wrist across his mouth. Tyler Rayne: Perfect. Well...almost. The Underground Pimp closes his eyes, tilts his head back and raises his arms to the heavens. Tyler Rayne: RAYNE DANCERS!!! And with that, the party immediately shifts from overdrive to ludicrous speed as a dozen or so topless Rayne Dancers come pouring into the room. On roller skates. Tyler Rayne: Now I have a big match coming up, but you boys have yourself a good night. Hank Cobb: Will do, boss. Colby Korver: (pulling himself away from a pair of skaters, each with a great pair herself) Stop by after your match, the night is young...and so are they. Tyler Rayne: They always are, my man, they always are.
