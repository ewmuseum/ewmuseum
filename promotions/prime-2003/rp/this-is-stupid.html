This is stupid

Date: Now I'M sKYLAR mONTGOMERY. I stink and I'm comfortable with that. And I don't mean 'stink' in some sort of slang way, I mean there is a smell that comes from me. My friends try to comment on it to put me down, but I don't care. I never wanted to be a 'smelly person', like the token stinky kid from the weird family in school. And I am not one of those, I am so much more, which is why I'm perfectly comfortable with it. Excepting that I stink is just a small step towards excepting the whole of me. I'm better, more stinky and more hardcore than Garbage Bag Johnny, but that's a point not even worth elaboration. All men are stinky! That's the way they're supposed to be, and if they don't smell they're a faggot who's penis has been inverted by the confusion of today's society. This whole thing about things having to be sanitised and clean, and the correct smell being the smell of basically nothing, it makes me sick. Once I was getting some rice pudding from her kitchen cupboard when my cousin mentioned how stinky tripe is, and I came upon the revelation that the last avenue of sensual entertainment that hasn't been explored is smells. She said what about perfume, and I said yes, there is perfume. It makes me think... One day, humans will understand again and let their natural odours be free to spread anywhere and travel with them beyond the confines of their bedrooms. One day smell will be a form of entertainment too. After the world has ended. And life on earth is simple and primal again: governed equally by the core instincts of the five-senses. And then stinking will be cool. I've seen it so clearly over the past few days. Like I've found new puzzle-pieces and can begin to predict the final picture. Narrativium will be the main religion on Earth by this point. After the world has ended. Fuck James von Doom and 'Civilization'. Narrativium actually is going to be the main religion on Earth for the next Astrological Arc. And I'll be on a spaceship or another planet somewhere. It's obvious Aliens or some sort of Godlike beings come down every couple-thousand years to collect the surplus of us clever ones, and leave you 'real' humans to your own devices... of keeping Earth going round. There are obviously more than the five-senses and more than the stars we can see in the sky. I'm gonna see them all. And I'm not gonna have a bath for the whole time. Coz I'm The Imaginary Man, Skylar Montgomery. ...And I'm just a character in a story.
