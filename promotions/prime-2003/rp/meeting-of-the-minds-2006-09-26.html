Meeting of the Minds

26 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/26/06 Sitting backstage in the locker room a few hours before ReVolution 106, it became entirely clear to Kenjiro Ito why he’d done what he’d done, why he was now in the camp of one Mr. Sonny Silver. “God, this place is ripe for the picking right now. Colossus III is over. Nelson and his merry band of mediocre assholes are gone. The time’s right for someone with the moxie to grab this shithole by the balls to rise to the top.” That’s what Kenjiro Ito had thought before ReVolution 105. Apparently, so did Angelo Deville. So did Jason Snow. “Fuuuuuuuck,” the Japanese man managed, shaking his head. Figured that when he’d finally found a way to make his mark, the other vultures came circling as well. PRIME was supposed to be his kill, his and the Chairman of PRIME’s alone. But things never worked out as planned for Kenjiro Ito. After all, he’d figured he’d at least be the Intense Champion by now. He figured he’d come in and set PRIME on his ear. After all, he’d lasted a God awful long time in that silly Dual Halo contraption. Went out and beat the now dead Kyle Lamen. But after that, the wheels had come off, and ITO couldn’t figure out why. “Probably was enjoying the bitches too much,” he mused. Kenjiro did enjoy his bitches. And besides, PRIME was still paying him when he was sitting on his ass. It was the perfect gig. But even a man as soulless as the Bringer of the Ice Age got bored fast. After all, there was only SO much debauchery one man could find. And THAT was why, when enemy turned friend Sonny Silver had called him up a few weeks ago, he’d hopped on the first flight down to Honolulu to talk business with perhaps the only man in wrestling who could claim to be a bigger asshole than Kenjiro Ito. ------------------------------------------------- The Silver ResidenceFollowing Colossus IIIHonolulu, Hawaii “Fuckin’ hunk of junk. Stupid Hawaiians not carrying Japanese rental cars. Damnit, we were so close to owning these stupid rocks in the middle of nowhere, but no, the American aircraft carriers had to be away. That German guy was right. God DOES have a special providence for fools, drunks, and the United States of America…” Most of Japan didn’t like to remember World War II. Kenjiro just wished it ended differently. Still, he could take solace in the fact that his ride was over. He parked the shitty American car in the driveway of one Sonny Silver, a tight smile on his face. “Wonder what that bastard’s up to. It’s been a while since fWo.” Ito smiled, the memory of the second meeting between himself and Sonny Gabriel Silver still fresh in his mind. It was probably the last good match he wrestled, even though it had ended in a loss. He respected Silver’s abilities, but respected his innate prickishness even more. “Ah, it’ll be good to see an old friend again.” Are you SURPRISED the two wastes of sperm are friends? If so, die. The Cocky Osakan stepped out of his car, kicking the tire for good measure as he appraised Silver’s residence. “Not bad. Figured it’d be a bit bigger though. My place is FAR more GANGSTA than this is…ah well. I forgot, Sonny’s got the ball and chain weighing him down.” The concept of Sonny Silver being married was so ludicrous that it had to be true. “I wonder if he Yakuza kicks her before or after he fucks her…” Deciding to find out for sure, Kenjiro walked up the stone path to the front door. Dressed for success (or hopeful later scoring with Hawaiian bitches) in a sharp powder blue shirt and dress slacks, he knocked three times on the door. When that failed, he resorted to shouting. “HEY, SONNY! GET YOUR DICK OUT OF YOUR WIFE’S ASS AND ANSWER THE DOOR!” A few seconds later, the door opened, and standing there was a rather mad looking Sonny Silver. “You’re lucky she and my daughter are out,” the Son proclaimed, eyeing the Japanese man, “or I’d have cut you.” “Just busting your balls,” Ito replied, with a shrug. “Besides, you’re the fucker who called me here, and I’m right on time. I’m NEVER on time for anything, so consider yourself honored.” “If she was here,” Silver said, “you think I’d interrupt sex with her for YOUR gook ass? They’re out shopping, which means it’s my weekly Sit On My Ass And Drink Beer While Bemoaning the State of Wrestling time.” “Eh, what happened? fWo fire you for excessive cursing finally?” “Nah,” Sonny said, pushing the door open and allowing Ito in. “Place just stopped functioning. Not that it matters. It’s a sports entertainment shithole that I was selling my soul to. But they paid well. I’ve got two more mouths to feed now, so I gotta keep the cash rolling in. I was so desperate I considered working a…” Here, the Son shudders, a sad, humiliated shudder. “…independent show.” “Dear God,” Kenjiro exclaimed, sympathy for his friend in his eyes. “Go get another beer and get that horrible thought out of your mind!” “Think I will. You want anything?” “Damn straight I do,” Ito responded. “So long as it’s not that American shit. For the leaders of the free world, you fuckers make some SHITTY beer.” “Got Kirin, if you want. That’s what I drink now.” “ICH-I-BAN MOTHERFUCKERS!” Ito didn’t NEED to be hammered to be loud and obnoxious. It just sort of happened. Sonny returned with two opened bottles of beer, and handed one to his friend. “So,” Ito asked, taking a swig of the Japanese brew, “why’d you call me all the way out here to the middle of nowhere? Want to start ourselves a bounty hunting business now that Dog’s been put down, brah?” “As much fun as it would be to run down transients and physically abuse them in the name of God, no,” Sonny replied, shaking his head. “I’m thinking about getting back into the game. It’s been a while since fWo went tits up…and there’s nowhere else good to go. ACW shut down too, probably because that RPG fag was champion so long. And LoC….I’m not even going to get into it.” “So you want somewhere to go, eh?” Ito asked. “I’m not the guy to ask. Ever since Jolt went down the shitter, I’ve been doing next to nothing. Sitting on my ass in PRIME getting paid for doing shit. Damn racist bastards, don’t want to give main events to the Asian man.” Silver cocked his head to one side, as if mulling that over. “Hmmm, you’re still in PRIME. How’s the competition there?” “Shitty,” Ito responded. “Some retarded fat guy just took over ownership of the place. His name is Chet. I mean, seriously now. If you’re named Chet, you’re either a queer, a fucking fatass, or a queer fucking fatass. All the talent’s gone from that place. We’re left with people like Tchu and Killean Sirrajin. If I could be bothered, it’d be ripe for the picking.” Sonny Gabriel Silver was known for being a stiff striker and even stiffer shit-talker. But beyond the hard-ass kicks and hard-ass persona was the intellect of a wrestling genius. “Call me crazy, Ito, but I just got an idea…” Silver leans forward, whispering something into his compatriot’s ear. Almost immediately, Ito’s eyes go as wide as saucers, and a wicked smirk comes across his face. “Oh, that’s PERFECT!” Ito exclaimed, shaking his head. “I wish I could come up with ideas that good.” “That’s why I’m Sonny Silver,” the former Seattle native explained, “and you’re Kenjiro Ito. Sonny comes up with the ideas. Kenjiro curses a lot.” Ito nodded. “I like that arrangement. As a matter of fact, I think we should shake on it.” Both men went for the handshake. Both men pulled back at the last second, and laughed. ---------------------------------------------------------------- “Ah, that was a good day…” Kenjiro was back from his daydream, shoving a foot into its proper boot. It had been a while since he’d put the tights on and laced up the boots. Ring rust wasn’t a worry for Kenjiro; after all, even if he was rusty, he’d vociferously deny it anyway. Unless, of course, he lost, in which case the ring rust would be a ready-made scapegoat. “It’s time for ITOlution to go on the air,” he told himself, pulling on his other boot and lacing it up. “Just like it should have when I arrived here. I’ve wasted a fuckload of time waiting to be given my just do. As far as I’m concerned, it’s about damn time I take it.” Standing up, he looked at himself in the dressing room mirror. Thoughtfully, he stroked his bleached-blonde goatee. Just elbow pads, tape, and gloves left, and he’d be set to go kick Tony Gamble’s ass. “Time to show the Chairman of PRIME how mid-carder killing is done,” Ito cackled. In his view, he’d had lots of experience with the midcard. As Jolt Wrestling’s longest reigning Ambassador Champion, he’d beaten a veritable plethora of midcarders. Some fat, some short, some tall, some small, it didn’t really matter. A knock came at the door, and Kenkiro immediately went into full-on Ito mode, snarling in the knock’s general direction. “Whaddya want,” he asked, angrily. “I’m getting ready to make Gamble crap out.” “Just wondering if you finished my ring introduction for tonight,” Sonny Silver called, from the other side of the door. “After all, tonight is the Chairman’s debut…” “Yeah, yeah, don’t get your panties in a bunch. It’s a good intro, you’ll enjoy it. Have it up here in the ol’ noggin. Oh, before you go, we going looking for bitches after the show?” “Where are we again? Where we are is important. We don’t want to go out hunting for bitches in a dirty ass place,” Sonny explained. “I’m married. Can’t be bringing anything back home.” “Ah, makes sense,” Ito replied, not that he knew what it was like. “We’ll avoid the bitches tonight then. Get something to eat instead?” “Fine with me, CEO of Awesome. Now get out here, you’ve got a midcarder to maim, and I’ve got notes to take.” And with thoughts of cracking skulls in Kenjiro’s head, he made his final preparations. Ito’s Ice Age was done and gone. But the Age of Ito’s Ill Awesomeness was just about to begin…
