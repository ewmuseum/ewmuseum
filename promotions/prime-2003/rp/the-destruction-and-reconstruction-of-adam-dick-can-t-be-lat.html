The Destruction and Reconstruction of Adam Dick - 'Can't be late. Got a game to play.'

Date: Jazz/Spurs Game 5. Adam Dick waits impatiently outside the front door, watching the woman who literally threw herself at him only moments prior as she shuffles through the infinite amount of shit on her set of keys. She flips through them like a Rolodex, past the keyless remote to her Saturn SUV, and the plastic-encased picture of what could be a young niece or cousin, and the miniature Mouse Trap gameboard replica. A lot of it is unnecessary, like the lanyard that only serves to get tangled in the mess or the Betty Boop figurine with the purpose of taking up more space. Finally she chances upon the key she needs to unlock the deadbolt. With both of their heart-beats racing, they attempt to remain as calm as possible in the face of achieving their goals, long and short term alike. Adam is excited; too excited to notice the oddities that should be alarming him. However, he isn't excited enough to ignore the strange quality her 'home' possesses. She holds the door open for him, spitting in the face of rules old-fashioned. The action presses Adam to ask, "So, um.... shit, I know you already told me your name but I've already had a few drinks and things have been a bit hazy since I took probably three or four trips to the parking lot to get lit and mayb-" "Amy." she stammers as he enters, looking him right in the eye. "It's Amy Ackers." She'd love nothing more than to tell him the truth, but now isn't the time. He's still being baited. Such a risky misnomer is a dead give-away to the trouble lying ahead, but she holds confidence in Adam's stupidity. "Amy. Alright, Amy, I swear I'll remember it by the time the night's over." He smiles before turning back inside. With his back to her, "Amy" delivers him a piercing glare. Once they are shut inside she locks the deadbolt along with two additional fixtures that appear to have no way of unlocking from the outside. For a moment Adam stares into the darkness, nervously holding his hands out so not to collide with objects, until Amy flips a of couple switches near the door. "Anyways, ummmm, Amy? I gotta ask." "What's that?" She turns from hanging her jacket on a rack in the corner to see that he's grown a bit nervous. "Why the fuck do you live in a storage place?" Adam asks, his eyes adjusting to the narrow room that is surprisingly well-furnished. After a couple of re-calibrating blinks he is able to make out the carpeted floors and leather furniture, as well as a plump bed near the back. "It's only temporary." She says quickly. "I just got into a major meltdown with my ex-fiancé and pretty much had to get out of there right away. Since I didn't have enough time to find a roommate and can't afford a place myself, I just brought all my stuff here and... yeah, that's pretty much it." Adam doesn't realize it, but something isn't right with Amy. She isn't like most people. Unlike a normal person bringing company back to their storage-garage/home, she isn't embarrassed. When he stares across the room, Adam can't help but notice a material covering the walls with labels written across certain bare sections that read 'Insul-Foam'. A quick glance around the perimeter reveals this material covering every single square inch of the walls and ceiling. However, he is far more captivated by the mystery woman's story to inquire about what really matters. "Major meltdown? What happened?" he asks. "Well when I finally realized I didn't want to marry him it was a little too late to just back out. The date was two weeks away, so I figured the easiest thing to do would be getting him to break it off. So... I convinced him I was a lesbian and made him think I was having an affair with one of my bridesmaids. And then I left. "Wait – you dyked out with your bridesmaid?" "No." she scoffs. "I had a couple of good friends who were willing to help me out in a bind." Adam takes a moment, staring at her with a confused expression as if his mind were still processing the scheme. "So you aren't a lesbian?" They share an awkward silence. The whites in Amy's eyes grow bigger as she plainly states, "No, I'm not." "Oh, well that's alright too I guess. But just so you know, I'd be cool with it if you were." "Did you want something to drink?" Adam can't even reply with an answer before she heads to make him one anyways. "Sure. I'm gonna use the pisser real quick." "It's in the back." she points near the bed, where the walls in a corner of the room protrude to give one private access to the toilet. He jogs to the back to relieve himself, allowing Amy enough time to her lonesome that she bellows out a deep sigh. She enters the 'kitchen-area', which lacks everything that would make it a real kitchen, like a dish-washer or a stove and even an oven. All there is, is a sink, a refrigerator, and a microwave. Aside from a counter-top with storage shelves inside of them, that's it. Inside one of the cabinets, a small glass is removed along with a bottle of Crown Royal and a small, brown, unlabeled bottle that looks similar to vanilla extract. A flick of the wrist pours the entire contents of said bottle into his drink, filling the rest of the cup with whiskey and ice. Then the bottles fly back into the cabinet even before Adam exits the bathroom. She finds a glass of her own and pours some water, a bit of a change from what Adam was made. With drinks in hand Amy makes her way to the center of the narrow room, what has been fashioned to appear as the living-portion complete with a television and coffee table and couches. Adam, air-drying his hands via vigorous shaking, appears from the corner maintaining a look of intensity on his face. "I bet the second half has started." He says hopping into the empty seat next to Amy. "Jazz should be getting the big W." "It's probably almost over now. But come on, they're down three-to-one. Do you really want to watch the inevitable? Subject yourself to that kind of heart-break?" Adam doesn't answer, instead he takes a sip from his drink. The strength of it, as it hits the back of his throat, is enough to send a chill down his spine. He calls out "God damn, woman. What's in this?" "Crown and Coke." "It doesn't taste like Crown and Coke." "Did I put too much Crown in there? I can remake it." She attempts to take the glass from his hand. However, Adam's hubris comes into play and he pulls the drink away, taking another sip. "Chill out, lady. I can handle this shit." He begins searching around himself while the fire in his chest dies down. "Now where's the remote? We gotta put it on TNT." Amy attempts to mask her contempt through a "playful" slap on his shoulder that hits a little harder than it should. "Forget basketball! I can think of much more fun things that we can be doing right now..." The look in her eyes is a little crazy. Almost lustful, along with other more peculiar aspects. She tilts her head down with the faint form of a smile creeping across her open mouth, appearing as if she's about to pounce on her prey. Her look is provocative enough to make Adam gulp. The nerves force enough sweat out on the pores on the palms of his hands, causing them to glide down his pants when he attempts to wipe them dry. "Like what?" he finally asks. "Well, we could be getting know each other better... seeing how we just met." "You mean, like, talk or something?" Adam shakes his head while taking a sip from his drink. Of course, he makes a face again, forgetting how strong the alcohol really is. "I don't play that fag shit." "But we can make it a game! You know, like truth or dare." "Truth or dare?" Adam shakes his head while taking a sip from his drink, this time being cautious as it slides down his throat. "I don't play that fag shit." "COME ON!" Amy grabs his hand and starts waving it up and down as she speaks with emphasis. "It'll be fun!" Adam's face reads a bit skeptical, and honestly, whose wouldn't? It's fucking Truth or Dare. They're supposed to be grown—adult—but here she is, stooping to the lowest of lows, which is nothing she hadn't done before. Amy needs this now, more than anything, and once again would be willing to sacrifice more than her dignity to succeed. The look in his eyes tells her that she's all but lost him. So, she takes a rather precarious route. She decides to tell him the truth. "You think I'd suggest something as lame as Truth or Dare if I didn't have something in mind, Adam?" Okay, she decides to tell him an allusion of the truth. The 'take-it-or-leave-it'-stance piques Adam's interests. What the hell, fucking is he gay? Hell no, he isn't. Of course he wants to give in now. The look on her face is a practical permission slip for some serious face-fucking. But there's always been this quirk in his personality, ever since he began dating women. Through them all, from the girlfriends in high-school, to dropping out and slumming around, to the girls he'd take advantage of as an adult, to the closest thing he'd ever come to a real relationship with Ellis Nash; he's always been too fucking shy. It's an aspect that hasn't gone away with time, and it certainly isn't helping his decisions at this point. In fact, the only element guiding his decision is the matching immaturity of playing a game like Truth or Dare. Before he was simply relying on instinct. Now that the inevitability of sex is slapping him in the face, he's forced to react with thought. Because he knows that sex is a topic that's remained too awkward for far too long. "Fine, let's play." Adam shrugs it off with a sip. He's tricked his mind into believing that, despite the situation, sex is not inevitable. Instantly, Amy's face reads with glee. As if getting him back to her place wasn't enough, this seals the deal. Hook, line, and sinker. She performs a light clap while folding her legs underneath, enabling her to face Adam on the couch. Suddenly the moment becomes entirely more personal than Adam is used to. He attempts to feign a smile, but it doesn't stick. Amy starts, "Alright, I'll go first." "Why do you get to go first?" "Because I'm a girl, silly." "SO! I might be missing three toes but I'm not crying out 'dissability,' am I?!" "That's totally different." "IS NOT." As if it requires stating, Adam doesn't exactly exercise tact in front of women. In fact, he doesn't exercise tact at all. The apparent theme of the night: another deep sigh exits Amy's lips. "Do you want me to explain the rules or not?" "What rules? It's fucking Truth or Dare. Let's just get playing. You can go first." Amy allows Adam to notice the glare she directs towards him before her eyes roll to the top of her head. While she fathoms the possible outcome, the question is poised: "Truth or Dare?" "Truth." He replies without hesitance. The mental block placed on him moments prior apparently severed, as Adam attempts to take the route of 'selling one short.' Ever so slightly a smile creeps a cross Amy's face before she returns to the thinking position, with her hands on her chin and her eyes tipped back. "Alright, this one is easy; What do you do for a living?" The silence lingers a bit longer than it should until Adam conjures a suitable lie, "I, uh, sell weed." If only the lie weren't completely idiotic, he'd believe she's convinced. It just forced its way out of Adam's mouth before he could run it through the stupidity filter. For a moment, he fears she'll be disgusted. Instead she smirks a little bit, almost in disbelief. "Really?" Mmmm-Hmmm, Adam nods quickly, hoping that'd be the end of it. "Bullshit, you're probably just a big stoner--." "So, my turn, right?" An effort to avert the conversation and keep the game moving involves Adam faking a sudden interest as he rubs his hands together. "Truth or Dare?" "Ummmmm.... Truth." "Alright. Are you SURE you're not a lesbian?" Amy blushes a bit, "Very sure."Accompanied by rubbing his thigh slightly. "TOTALLY SURE?" "Yes, now shut up about it!" She says with another "playful" slap, this time leaving a bright red mark on his cheek. "It's my turn. Truth or Dare?" "Truth." "Alright. Now this is a good question; How far out of your way would you go to help someone?" What kind of question is this? Is this bitch insane, Adam can't help but wonder? No matter. After tonight, he'll never see her again. All he needs to endure is the night's crazy head-games and nothing more. With a shrug he says, "Um, not very far, I guess. I mean, I'd drop you off at the bus-stop if it was on the way to a Taco Bell or something." Amy says rather flatly, "Wow, that's brutally honest." "That's why I picked TRUTH. Anyways, Truth or Dare?" "Truth." "COME ON!" The fourth 'Truth' in a row. This is becoming even more boring than he thought. "You can't pick Truth again! Pick a Dare!" "Why don't we save those for later, when we start to gain some steam? I pick Truth." "Fine, why is your house so fucking weird?" "I already told you why! Now quit asking questions you already know the answer to!" This time, she attacks him with a barrage of slaps that he measly attempts to shield himself from. "Truth or Dare!" "Gosh! Dare! Stop hitting me, Christ!" Amy returns to her seat from the attack position, smiling as she stares into Adam's eyes. "Are you sure you want a Dare?" "WHAT THE FUCK DID I JUST SAY?" "Alright... I dare you to finish your drink." Adam stares at her for a few blinks. "Are you kidding?" "Finish your drink." After another couple of blinks, he begins scoffing as the glass raises to his lips. "No fucking problem." Gulp. Gulp. And another. Down it goes. "Ahhhhhhh." He lets out a refreshed exclaim, masking his face from the pain the best he can. "My turn, is it? Truth or Dare?" "Truth." "Fuck. Fine. Erm, are we gonna bone tonight?" The question causes Amy to burst out laughing instantly. "Excuse me?" "Well, y'know—." "You can exercise a little bit more tact than that, can't you?" After a moment of thought, Adam rephrases, "Will there be any penetration going on?" "You know, Adam. As a matter of fact, there will be." Contrary to what one would belief, she says this stone-faced. "Now, Truth or Dare." Not eager to pound another drink so soon, Adam opts to go with the alternative to give his stomach time to recuperate. "Truth." "Alright, this one is kind of like the last question; How far out of your way would you go to hurt someone?" Okay. Things are getting a little too weird, even for a person like Adam. "I don't understand the question." Her smile has grown sickeningly wide to the point that it creeps Adam out, despite how white her teeth may be. "Don't worry, we'll skip that one. For now. I'll ask you something a little bit easier instead." With a deep breath, Amy realizes her moment of truth. "Did you ever see that old TV Show, 'Andy Richter Controls the Universe'?" light bulb : turned on :: brain : smack-fucking-dab in the middle of realization Fuck. Fuck. Fucking Crapshit. With such a simple comment, Adam Dick's nerves get the best of him. How could he have been so stupid? How could he not notice who she is? Instantly, it feels as if his stomach had just jumped from the top of a tall building. Adam doesn't need to know the specifics because they are slapping him in the face as he speaks. He doesn't have any questions pertaining to the 'why' or 'what' because all of that is replaced with fear. The only thing he wants to ask is if she'd let him live. "Look, Amy--." "My name isn't Amy Ackers, you stupid, drug-addled piece of shit. It's Irene Fucking Molloy, but I can't say that out loud in public without eight pop-culture-obsessed assholes, WITH NOTHING BETTER TO DO THAN WATCH VH1 PANEL SHOWS ALL DAY, announcing that I'm the woman THAT LIKES TO FUCK MOTOR VEHICLES!" This is when the 'Vanilla Extract' works its magic. As if a spell were cast, Adam instantly slumps in his seat and his eyelids grow heavy. The pressing need to run for his life subsides, as does his ability to do so. "You... you – drugged – me?" His speech notably impaired. "Just a little bit of Promethazine to take the edge off. You've been a little up-tight lately, Adam. Too much for your normal self." "Look. I know this sounds crazy, maybe even a little unbelievable, but that wasn't me! I wasn't the one that--." "You think I haven't been watching you, Oh-So Illustrious Face-Eater? On the contrary, I've done nothing but with my free-time, which is all I've had since my life was RAPED. My career was DESTROYED before I could get it off the ground. ONE LOUSY SITCOM, ADAM! ONE FUCKING SITCOM BEFORE YOU MADE YOUR PRESENCE FELT. Now I resort to uploading my acoustic folk ballads on MySpace in some pathetic attempt to retain what fame I had—ALL BECAUSE OF YOU." She uncurls her legs and perks to her knees, taking an imposing stance that further instills fear in her frozen prey. "I'm TELLING you, that wasn't me!" Adam pleads. "That was someone els--." "Oh, you mean the OTHER Illustrious Face-Eater? The one you paid Violence Jack to set-up in your stead?" At first, it seems as if the revealed Irene is mocking him. But the follow-up question indicates to Adam that she knows much more than anyone else does. Apparently, she has been watching him for quite some time. "I know all about your little deal with the Black Sect of Wisdom, and how Bruce 'Violence Jack' Shanahan brain-washed one of his feeble-minded followers into a state-of-mind much similar to yours. I know that you didn't want to look like a coward for quitting PRIME after your first day, after Tchu decimated you with a steel chair, which is why you arranged a replacement in the first place. And I also know that since AWC has closed, you haven't been able to pay Bruce the amount you promised him. This is why you've been hiding for your life in small towns for the past year. To put it lightly, I know everything." "So you do know! Then you have to understand that it wasn't me, Amy! That wasn't me who got you drunk and then convinced you to stick that e-break in your vagina!" "You self-righteous cock-sucker, MY NAME IS IRENE! And you still don't get it. You forced this to happen. Your carelessness, your disregard for others. You've always been a selfish human being, Adam Dick, and it's always been in the worst ways possible. It doesn't matter that you weren't in the car that night. You are the true culprit. It was YOUR actions that caused all of this. And now you're going to get paid in full." In a last ditch effort, Adam uses all of his strength to break free of the sluggish trance he's stuck in... ...only to drop face-first to the carpet-covered floor in a heap of unsuccessful, drugged-out glory. "Wait a second." He slurs. "You said we were gonna have sex! YOU LIED!" "And you didn't, Mr. Weed Dealer?" Irene leaps up from the couch and begins to head to a chest that sits inconspicuously to the side of the room. From it she withdraws a few sets of hand-cuffs before returning to stand over Adam Dick stuck to the ground. "Technically, I didn't even lie. You asked if there was going to be penetration, remember?" As she flips him over, she takes satisfaction at the sight of the worried look now etched in his face. "And trust me, there will be." Awkward sex is now the least of Adam's fears.
