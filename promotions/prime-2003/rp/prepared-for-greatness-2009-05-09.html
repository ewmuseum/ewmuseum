Rippa comes to and looks to Ruby and Scraps who are both chanting, “Let’s-GO-RIPPA!” “Come on, Ripp,” Tom says. “Yeah, Veljumin!” Ruby supports. “We’re only going thirty-seven!” The big man looks down at the speedometer to see this woman, for once in his life, is correct about something other than his wrestling tights matching his boots. He looks around, investigating the situation. “Swedish Pigs! What is the limit?” Tom looks out the window just to see a God-given speed limit sign reading ’70 mph.’ The redhead throws his hands up, exasperated. “Seventy miles an hour, dude! Come on, Rippa! Where’s your head at kid?” The wet thud of Veljumin’s big boot splashes through a puddle as he runs through the night in search of his Rat. Thinking to who would be at the end of his meaty mitts by the night’s end, Veljumin runs stealthily, staying on task. His eyes light up as he spots something ahead. Something blond. “No, Velju-GAAAHHHH!!!” The young man ahead of him tries to plea but Veljumin’s other boot swings up, plants into his chest, and throws him some good number of feet backwards - smacking his head against the side of a brick building and rendering him unable to move. The enraged behemoth latches his big hand around the traitor’s throat and pulls him up off the ground. Nostrils flaring, Rippa smiles through his grinding teeth with a sick, sadistic flame burning in his eyes. “Marco…” Rippa’s ice blue eyes watch his onetime friend. The smaller man’s eyes squint around, never once making direct eye contact. That was one way Veljumin knew anyone was lying. They couldn’t stand the thought of picturing him angry. The smile fades from Rippa’s tense jaw as he jerks his hand abruptly. The body thuds to the ground. Staring in admiration, Veljumin watches Marco’s lifeless eyes staring right back at his leader. Rippa smiles, noticing his gang getting closer. Rippa turns to exit. “Holy shit! Marco!?” The first man arrives and stops in his tracks, halting to a skid with eyes wide open. He looks to Rippa who has made his way out of the area and back toward the dark alley he had come from. The next few men round the corner, looking on in confusion. One man steps up, demanding answers. “Rippa! Marco? You had to kill Marco?! He was the best man on the stree-“ Rippa turns his head without moving his body at all, glaring beyond the man to the crowd. A snarl spreads over his face, thinking back to when the pack was only 6 men. The Original 6, he thought, was now down to 5. Rippa looks back at the limp corpse, pointing lazily. “Then you deal with the rat.” Another man steps in. the same man that held the pipe to Tesson head was now glaring at Rippa with his weapon twirling among his anxious fingers. “How about I deal with you?” With a grunt, the man swings his pipe but misses by inches. Rippa, just like before, grabs the bar and spins around like doing a hammer throw – tossing the young man into the wall. With two hasty steps, Rippa hands clenches together creating a fist and capsizes the front three teeth in his lackey’s mouth. Another solid right hook leaves the boy groaning in agony, looking up at him. “Who the fucks are you to decide what happens around here!?” Rippa looks on at the bloodied boy, smiling a bit. He removes a pair of brass knuckles from his jacket pocket. He places them over his big hand, kneeling down to let the man see the answer. Veljumin watches a flower bloom in this man’s mind as he reads across, left to right. “Does that work for you?” Rippa sarcastically leaves the man with a few seconds left if life before smashing the brass bar into his face twice in rapid succession. The big, blond brickhouse stands to full height. He slowly looks back at the crowd. “Consider this our last night.” Rumblings begin. He raises a fist to silence the noise. “We’ve killed the rat, we’ve killed the weasel… we even got rid of the pig. Tonight, however, marks the first time I’ve thought to myself that you all are better than me. I am Rippa, leader of the Lost Angels. You are men with a life worth living. Marco was a good man. He just made one bad choice.” “We’ll never rat on you, Rippa!” Rippa grins at the honest attempt. He knows half of these kids try to get in with the gang so they don’t have to stay at home, watching their parents fight or work illicit jobs. He shakes his head. That wasn’t the mistake. He was doing the right thing, you know? Trying to better the streets is a noble cause. The mistake I’m referring to was even more serious than that, I’m afraid. Marco made the mistake of joining my gang.” BWAAAAAAAAAAAAA The horn sounds as Rippa punches into the heart of the steering wheel. “Jesus!” Ruby cries, falling to one side of the car as Rippa jerks the wheel in the opposite direction. With the car accelerating at a rapid rate, Scraps buckles his seat belt and begins laughing like a lunatic. Rippa shakes his head, smiling in the rearview mirror, “My head?! You’re questioning my head!?” “Let me tell you, gang,” Rippa grins wryly, “My head is right where it’s supposed to be. Locked and loaded on how much fun it’s going to be to rip the two teams everybody loves into itty-bitty pieces! That’s where my head is at! Wrapped around this BLOODBATH!” Ruby and Scraps glance to one another, clueless, and then look back to the maniacal driver. “Damn right!” Scraps jabs at his Swedish counterpart and driver, questioning the items of choice. “I looooove my bloodbaths.” Veljumin smirks, looking to his partner between traffic obstacles. “Then you won’t mind holding onto these then?” The big, blond meatball removes a pair of brass knuckles from his pocket and slides them over to Scraps. Scraps grin gets even bigger, nodding, “How fitting, Mr. Shark.” “Thank you, Mr. Lion,” Rippa responds systematically. The two share a brief, malicious glance and laugh. Ruby rolls her eyes and sits back in her seat. She knows what is coming but doesn’t want anything to do with how to stop it. Scraps and Veljumin Rippa are coming to the PRIME arena as Title Hopefuls. No longer newbies, or contenders, or even a tag team. Sitting back with her thoughts elsewhere, Ruby feels the flow of techno and high speeds warp her body in a trance of high hopes and wishful thoughts. Thoughts of grandeur that have Scraps and Rippa, each, with one half of PRIME’s Tag Team Titles around their waist.
