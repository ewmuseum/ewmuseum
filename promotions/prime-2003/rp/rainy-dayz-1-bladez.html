Rainy Dayz #1 - Bladez

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 5-6, 2007 DAY: March 6, 2007 CUE: "Rainy Dayz" by Raekwon ---- It's raining. He's changing. My man is going insane. Insane… Easton Hall stepped into the pristine lobby of the hotel, immediately shedding himself of a hat and shimmering black raincoat; a storm raged outside, the rain and hurrying pedestrians easily visible through the glass of the building's front. Underneath, he wore a black t-shirt with a yellow-and-green design emblazoned with the word Kylesa, and a sword strapped against his back. A bellhop took his coat without making much of it. Green eyes scanned the lobby, but nobody gave him or the blade he wore a second look. Telling himself that it would be alright, Easton made his way over to what seemed like the front desk, loosening the strap so he could belt it around his waist, letting the slightly-curved sword hang at his hip. "Hello?" Easton asked; he hoped they wouldn't expect him to speak Japanese. A woman standing at the desk looked up at him. She was pretty, with rouged cheeks and black hair pulled back behind her ears. Her eyes touched the sword at his hip, but did nothing more. "Hello, and welcome to the SRC Hotel," the woman said in a standard Japanese accent. "Do you have a reservation?" Easton frowned a bit, drumming his fingers on the marbletop counter. He should have expected a question like this, but he hadn't. "Someone sent for me," he said finally. "A Mister V.O." The woman's eyes lit up then and she looked Easton over. "Your name?" "Easton Hall." She glanced down, but it was obvious to Easton that she didn't have to; she knew his name immediately. Easton was more than a little suspicious, but he didn't say anything. "Ah, yes, a Mister Hall… Mr. V.O. has requested that you be escorted up to the 36th floor." She pressed a button on her desk and leaned to speak into it: "Suzuki. Murashige. Please escort Mister Hall to the 36th floor, as per Mister V.O.'s request." Easton watched her as she straightened up. "They should be here any min—" "What do you know about this V.O.?" Easton asked quickly. She stared blankly at him. "They should be here any—" "What do you know!?" Easton snapped, slamming his hand down on the counter. The woman jumped and her lower lip trembled just a bit, faced with the hairy, furious foreigner, but she did her best to keep her composure. "They should…" Her voice trailed off on its own accord this time. Easton twisted around to see two men standing behind him. One was taller, broader shouldered, with lighter skin, and the other was darker, narrower, with a gaunter face. Both of them were dressed in all black, hair neatly arranged, sunglasses over their eyes. "Suzuki. Murashige. Please, take Mister Hall up." Easton glanced back at her. "If you would please follow them," she instructed. "And have a nice stay at the SRC Hotel." Easton glanced back at the two and the smaller man nodded, gesturing for Easton to follow. The two men walked towards the elevators; Easton let them get three steps ahead of him so he could watch their backs. His hand drifted to the wrapped hilt of his sword and his eyes narrowed, but after one last look at the woman behind the desk, he followed. The three sets of footsteps heading towards the elevators were the only sounds in the massive lobby as they crossed it. Absently, Easton wondered if other people even lived in such a nice building; there had to be some. Suzuki and Murashige stood in the elevator cab, staring straight ahead. Easton stood on the outside, looking from one to the other for a bit before stepping inside. Suzuki reached forward, pressing the button for floor 36. Easton turned around, facing the front of the elevator. His eyes closed just as the steel doors slid shut. DAY: March 5, 2007 CUE: "Mr. Sandman" by Method Man ---- "Thank you," Easton said in halting Japanese, accepting the shallow cup of tea.with a slight dip of his head. He brought the bowl to his lips slowly, his eyes watching the girl who had served it to him. He took a short sip, trying not to slurp; he'd done this often enough, but he was always self-conscious. The girl smiled at him accomodatingly. Easton reminded himself that he was married. Easton couldn't even remember who had reccommended him to this tea house, but it was very nice. Typically, he stayed inside on his trips to Japan, but apparently someone had thought he might become tired of training and generally being indoors all the time. He'd have to try and remember this place for future trips. "I trust you like it?" she asked cheerily. Easton nodded and was about to take another sip when a harsh rapping came to one of the doors. She glanced up at the door, startled, before looking back at Easton. "You'll excuse me?" Easton nodded again. She rose and walked down the hall to answer the door. He followed her with his eyes until he couldn't see any further. He lowered his cup and let it rest on the table lightly. She reappeared soon, cradling a black, slightly curved object in both arms that Easton instantly recognized as a sheathed sword. Attached to the guard was a golden ribbon and a white card. The girl sank to her knees and bowed forward, extending the sword towards the Canadian. "For you." Easton bowed slightly as well, taking the sword with a quizzical expression on his face. He turned it over and over in his hands. He hadn't held a sword for several years. His fingers moved to the card and he pulled it close, just enough to see the Ordo Templi Orientalis lamen in gold dust on the front. Easton's eyes widened and he quickly thumbed the card open, eyes scanning the top half. Easton Hall, we know you are here. My Master wishes to meet with you. I suggest you not miss it; it's a unique opportunity to begin to understand secrets that I am sure you're interested in. Ascend to the 36th chamber. Mr. V.O. Easton's breath caught. DING! DAY: March 6, 2007 CUE: "Can It All Be So Simple" by Raekwon ---- The elevator doors opened and Easton glanced towards the floor number. 7. The hell? Easton was sure that Suzuki had pushed 36. The 36 button was still lit up. The elevator wasn't giving any evidence of moving, though. Easton glanced at the two guards once again before stepping forward. The room that the elevator had opened up into was bare, checkered tile on the ground and white tile adorning the walls. The wall was high and flat, completely white. Easton couldn't help but question what he was doing there. Just after he had taken two steps in, however, identical elevator doors on the other walls opened up and three others entered the floor, all armed. To his left walked a woman, tall and lean, with a long blade over her back; her hair was colored into a dark rainbow and her skin was pale. To his right came a massive mountain of a man, shaggy hair hanging down to his shoulders, a mean expression on his face. A heavy, wickedly-curved sword hung at his hip. Straight across from Easton stepped a leanly muscular man in all black, his collar coming up under his jaw, a thin sword at his side. After a few seconds of looking from one to the other, all four seemed to figure out what needed to happen. Four times as one, the unsheathing of swords rang through the room. Easton and Lean darted towards Big immediately, She sauntering towards Big slowly, leaving the other two to take the first crack. Easton brought his blade forward hard, but Big's block pushed Easton's balance off just a bit. Lean thrust his sword forward, but Big batted that one aside as well and then slammed the knife edge of his hand against Lean's face, catapulting him backwards. Big moves to take advantage, but She soars through the air, snapping her foot hard into Big's nose. She landed and turned around to see Easton slashing down, just in time to bring her weapon in the way. Both shards of steel sprang away from each other and the competitors twisted about, bringing their swords together once again, only to have Lean's sword strike in between them, locking the three for a split second. Easton then quickly twisted his grip and She did the same in the opposite direction, the force sending Lean into a brilliant barrel roll through the air. Lean landed and Easton backspun, swinging wide to catch the man's neck, but Lean ducked just in time. As Easton's hair flung out of his face and he turned, he saw a split-second of Big's intentions before tossing himself out of the way of a heavy swordstrike. Easton rolled on the ground, coming to his feet and twisting about, bringing his steel tongue diagonally over his front and just barely blocking a vertical attack from Lean. Easton felt himself being bent backwards, effort clear on his face as his guard was pressed into him. Just before he was going to be forced onto his back, Lean jerked and let out a cry of anguish, a burning line drawn across his back by She's sword. Easton spun backwards on the ball of his foot, slashing at their waists, but a ring of metal told Easton that She had blocked his attack. Easton and She locked eyes for a split second before both threw themselves into the air. Cartwheeling through the air with no hands in stereo, Easton and She exchanged attacks and parries before landing on their feet, ready to continue. At that moment, She seemed to sink out of view. Big's uppercut swing sent She tumbling to the ground, shoulder hitting the tile first, legs collapsing on top of herself. Big brought his sword back around over his head, a menacing roar booming from the depths of his stomach. Thinking quickly, Easton thrust his sword forward, but Big moved to the side quickly; still, he groaned as the edge of the blade cut across his stomach. The massive warrior didn't let this stop him, however, slamming the back of his elbow into Easton's face, sending the Canadian spinning through the air to a face-down landing on the unforgiving checkered tile. Easton began to push himself up, but he felt a foot slam down between his shoulderblades, forcing his chest back to the polished tile. His breath became labored, eyes darting around frantically. He felt a sharp line pushing against his neck and closed his eyes, knowing what was coming. He gripped his sword hilt, thinking of only one thing. Twisting his body to get his shoulder under him just a tad more, he rapidly struck upwards at the wrist of his assailant, the feminine yelp of pain telling him that he had succeeded. Easton rolled to the side frantically until he came to his back several feet away from She, bringing his legs up and kipping up to his feet. Just as the balls of his feet touched the ground, he felt a swift blade slashing diagonally across his back. The Canadian howled out in pain, twisting around to see a laughing Lean in a dropped stance before him, a line of blood across the edge of his sword. Glancing behind him, Easton noticed She ready to make some sort of rush. At the same time, Lean noticed that Big was standing on the opposite side. Easton and Lean's minds seemed to click into place as one at that instant and they charged towards each other, predatory yells lifting from their throats, swords ready. They closed, slamming their blades together with furious energy and then springing apart, spinning counter to each other. An astonished Big could only watch as Easton swung himself up into the air, spinning towards him and smashing him in the side of the jaw with a brutal kick. The disgusting triplet crack that accompanied Big's slow twist through the air told Easton exactly how hard he'd kicked him. As Easton began his descent, he knew Lean was coming at him. It was an instinctive thing. Easton's feet seemed to press against the ground slower than normal and Easton used the time to bring his blade back around, switching his grip deftly to hold the sword pointing behind him like a needle. A sudden pressure jerked his arm just as he set his arm into submission. A low, disappointed groan came from behind him, followed by the clatter of steel against tile. Easton yanked his sword from its intestinal sheath and twisted, kicking Lean in the stomach hard to knock him to the ground backwards. Easton spun about on his foot then, spinning his sword about rapidly above his head to sluice off the blood. Easton glanced at the three other warriors laying on the tile and sighed. He cracked his neck and turned back to the elevator slowly, bringing his eyes up to it. CUE: "Visionz" by Wu-Tang Clan Just as Easton eyed the elevator, he was greeted by the taller of the two guards hurling himself towards the Canadian, two kinves drawn. Tall stabbed at Easton rapidly, forcing Hall to step backwards repeatedly to stay out of their range. Easton brought the flat of his blade up rapidly, smacking Tall on the underside of his wrists. Tall's arms were thrown up into the air and Easton took advantage, booting the man in the midsection. Tall stumbled backwards, shaking his head. Wire flipped over his partner then, untucking his body as he came down to favor Easton with a flashing strike that the Canadian swordsman only barely blocked. Wire landed easily, spinning fluidly as he struck at the Dragon, forcing Easton to keep solely on the defensive. Finally, Easton found his opening, spinning about and bringing his sword low, slashing horizontally at Wire's legs. The other man leapt into the air, twisting and coming down until he was upside down, only the point of his sword on the floor. Easton only had a split-second to admire the man's agility and balance before the man flipped forward, sending the blade arcing up at Easton's face. The Dragon flung himself backwards, his fingers touching the ground before he managed to come down on his feet in a low squat. Easton slowly rose to his feet, but before he could think, Tall was in front of him, smashing his fist into Easton's chin. Easton cried out, stumbling backwards, and the punch was quickly followed by a knife cutting into his shoulder. Easton spun about with the momentum, though, bringing the end of his sword hilt hard against the man's temple. Tall soared a few feet before crashing into the ground hard. Wire took the advantage of the short opening in Easton's defenses, hurling himself forward and slamming his shin into Easton's stomach. Easton staggered backwards rapidly, trying to find his balance. Wire lunged forward then, thrusting his sword, but Easton blocked it with his own blade. Working his arm around, he forced open Wire's stance and fired a punch hard into Wire's nose, making it the other man's turn to stumble. Easton pressed his advantage coming at Wire with a high strike, but Wire deftly blocked it. The smaller man then tried to swing at Easton's legs, but Hall brought his steel down quickly to halt the attack. Easton and Wire locked eyes for a brief second before Easton flashed his blade back up, but just as Wire brought his sword up to parry, Easton kicked the smaller man's legs out from under him. Wire gasped as he collapsed to the ground, but before the man could respond, Easton brought his sword against Wire's throat. Glancing behind him, Easton saw Tall on his feet, both knives in hand. The man wasn't attacking yet, though, wanting to see if Easton was bluffing. The Dragon pushed the blade forward and Wire gagged a bit as he tried to shrink his throat away from the sword, dropping his own weapon. Tall took this as a cue to back down, taking a few steps backwards. Easton drew his blade away and, with a final look at Tall, walked back towards the elevator alone. Stepping inside, he turned around and pressed 36. The doors closed as Easton sheathed his sword, letting out a breath at the same time. CUE: "Bells of War" by Wu-Tang Clan Easton's eyes were hard against the door as the elevator rose through the SRC Hotel, the low hum preventing him from feeling too alone. He thought he might be shaken after an encounter like that; he was fairly sure people had been left dead. It didn't matter, though. He was going to get answers. Finally. Everything he had done here was necessary. He couldn't help but feel overwhelmed by such power, though. How much influence did this man he called his mentor really have? Was this an assassination attempt? The first three seemed to fight amongst each other as much as anybody else, but the guards… but if it was an assassination, why arm him? Was this some kind of weird test? If it was, Easton was prepared to deny whatever the man might ask of him. It all came down to Mr. V.O., however. Hopefully he could coax some answers out of the man. That was, if V.O. wasn't an assassin as well. If V.O. was another man sent to kil him… well, this was far too elaborate if that was all he was. No, V.O. had to have some answers and Easton would get them one way or another. DING! The doors slid open and Easton wasted no time in stepping through. CUE: "Rainy Dayz" by Raekwon "Mr. Hall," came a deep, practiced voice from across the room. "You came." The suite was a plush, modern place, cushioned couches seeming to sit everywhere, the color scheme white and gold. The voice's owner was standing on a raised platform; the opposite end of the suite was lifted by the way of three short steps. He was dressed much in the manner of the two guards, but his black hair hung down effortlessly over his back, and a sword was strapped against his hip. "Mr. V.O.?" Easton inquired. The elevator doors closed behind him, but he didn't glance back. "Yes," the man affirmed. "I must say, I don't know if I expected you to—" "Save it. Who's your Master? Why do you want me here? What's this all for?" Each heated question was accompanied by a step forward from the Dragon. He didn't take his eyes off of V.O. The other man laughed a bit, shaking his head as he approached Easton slowly. "I don't have anything to tell you," Mr. V.O. confessed. "You're bullshitting me," Easton growled. He drew his sword angrily and pointed it towards Mr. V.O. "You know what's going on, you bastard. You're connected with the OTO. Don't try to sell me shit." "I know nothing, Mr. Hall, I swear," Mr. V.O. repeated, holding up his hands. "But… he might know." Easton lifted an eyebrow. "Who?" "Him." Mr. V.O. pointed upwards and Easton's gaze followed. Above them was a glass ceiling. Standing on the other side was a man in black robes, looking straight down at the scene below. "In the Room of the Eight Diagrams." "Take me to him," Easton demanded, looking back at V.O. Mr. V.O. chuckled, drawing his own blade slowly. "Oh, Mr. Hall," he laughed smugly. "You didn't think it would be that easy, did you?" Easton narrowed his eyes, spreading his feet and dropping into a fighting stance. "It never is." Behind V.O., through the massive glass pane that formed a large section of the back wall, a flash of lightning and the peal of thunder reminded them that the storm was still raging outside. Gettin' through those rainy dayz… all lyrics from "Rainy Dayz" by Raekwon
