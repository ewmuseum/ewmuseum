Date: Who cares I’m counting down the days. The course of my destiny… that one last shot that’s going to catapult me straight to the top of this industry… I’m waiting to hear her say the words that I know somewhere within that river of pop culture references and dry wit, she wants to say. She’s aching to get her hands on me the very same way that I ache to get my hands around her throat. She wants to destroy me in the middle of my own hometown. Nothing would give her greater satisfaction than to see her try and defeat me once and for all, humiliating me in front of tens of thousands of people live and millions across the world on the grandest stage of them all. She took my hand, I took her pride and her shroud of invulnerability. No matter what that belt says, I proved her fallibility. Nothing… NO ONE… is far from my grasp. Not even you. You’re very right, Lindz. Revenge is a game not played by ladies. It’s also a game I play better than you. It will come in time. But… Until that day comes – the day she proclaims that she wants to tear me limb from limb -- I must bide my time. I need a distraction. I need something that will keep the mind sharp and keep me focused. Without focus, a man’s mastery to obtain an object of his desire is nonexistent. Luckily, I just happen to have a great way to relieve both frustration and retain focus. ~~~~~~ The foul stench of failure percolates through my nose while I look out at this wretched city of San Francisco. A cavalcade of brightness burns into my corneas when I acknowledge the sun, allowing its light to fully awaken my eyes to the maelstrom. Folding my arms across my chest, I observe the common folk of the city, going about their daily subordinate lives, and it sickens me. What have they contributed to society in order to allow them to live in the same atmosphere as I do? I spent the years of my life that they spent indulging their inner-delinquent learning about the hardships of reality. My accomplishments by the age of 27 are nothing short of legendary, and yet I am continuing to pay money for these destined middle class shits to live? Pull the plug now! Oh. What is troubling me? The stinging sensation still has not eviscerated from my body. No matter how hard my scowl is for society, there is one thing that I cannot escape about a couple of nights ago. The supposed end of my troubles. This one was yet again on my shoulders. At Overkill, Winters got my leg and managed to defeat me. At Revolution 131, that fucking bong loving Nazi dropped me on my head. At Revolution 133, that inbred redneck desecration of humanity gave me a Rebel Yell. I’m better than this, I know that I am. None of those motherfuckers have the resume that I have in this world. None of them are constantly roleplaying Fidel Castro to life’s Central Intelligence Agency. None of them have the collection of hatred from society upon their being that I do. I’m fucking death proof and I can’t beat anybody in PRIME? This shit has got to stop, and at 134 I’m going to make goddamn sure that it does. UPstarts, if I were you then I would get a good head start right now. Get out of the state. Get out of the country. I’m about to unleash a bomb of ferocity and you two hapless souls are the intended targets. I will make you bleed. I will break your bones. I will damage your lives. I will fuck you up worse than any horror movie that you watch with your inebriated ignorant fraternity douchebag friends. … I need to go and clear my head. Where in the fuck is my gun at? I’m going to the shooting range. ~~~~~~ PACIFIC ROD & GUN CLUB SAN FRANCISCO, CA – TUESDAY, JULY 17th Standard-issue shooting vest? Check. Protective Goggles? Check. Earplugs? Check. SIG P226 with 9.mm rounds? Check. Sonny finished going through the motions, making sure he was prepped and ready to go for the hour ahead. Ever since the day his father gave him a set of antique twin pistols for his 16th birthday, the strange fascination with the collection of guns and rifles only continued to grow. He had a room in his house (all unloaded with a daughter in the house, naturally, he isn’t retarded!) with racks mounted to each wall, containing some priceless trinkets and hand-me-downs from generations past. He was an encyclopedia of knowledge when it came to him and his precious firearms. When he wasn’t bogging himself down with his professional wrestling concerns, opening up an industrial-strength can of whoop-ass on some unsuspecting bull’s-eye was always the thing that allowed him to unwind his nerves. “Dude, you’re POSITIVE you’ve done this before?” Silver asked of his guest at the current time. “Do you really want me to recount all of the times that I‘ve popped a cap,” Devin Shakur fired back as he put the finishing touches on loading the magazine into his own P226. “Not to mention that I’m fucking Sun Tzu. What do you THINK we do in our spare time?” “Dunno, re-enact Full Metal Jacket? Sucky sucky, right?” A scowl emerged on Shakur‘s face and he shot it over at Sonny. “…You are lucky that I like that Old Skool Mic bit a lot.” When the gun was properly loaded, Devin cocked it back and went through his own mental checklist to make sure everything was on properly. Vest, protective goggles, the whole shebang. Covering one’s own ass was crucial before venting frustrations upon intended targets. Shakur started up the conversation once again. “So I take it by your verbal tirade last week that you are sick of being seen in an entertaining light?” “Little bit. I’m no longer going to be someone else’s bitch, Devin. Whenever people see me, the one thing that they think of is…that gimmick I used to do. I no longer want to be associated with tomfoolery or shenanigans. What people are going to see is the real me, the guy that loves to beat the everloving shit out of anything in his way. My time is not in the future, it is right now. You get me?” “I can definitely get behind that. The superficial frills of the stage are irrelevant in hindsight. You don’t need flash in order to get a championship. Last time I checked, flamboyance was never measured in our business, and it didn‘t hurt like a motherfucker when striking you.” A nod from Sonny Silver further allowed this strange relationship to continue on. Both men continued to go through this introductory period to one another, quietly analyzing each others verbal and body language. Shakur failed to indicate any kind of deceptive in Sonny’s words, all the seriousness that he is being presented is legitimate. Sonny concluded that Shakur, while on the surface he might be a communist and still trying to impress Sun Tzu, is someone who could be a valuable ally. “Thank you, Devin, for not making me regret inviting you out here. That having been said…” To make himself appear more badass than he actually is, Sonny cocks the gun once again, ready to do his business. “Who’s up for the ceremonious mass murder of targets?” An eerie, but very calculated smile forms across his face. “That’s the first thing I’ve heard in San Francisco that I actually like.” “You know, Dev, San Francisco is actually a very misleading city. Unless you happen to be a resident of this state or even an upstanding native of California, all you truly know about this place is painted horribly. You hear all these jokes in today’s media that portray each and every member of this sizeable city as some sort of frilly, rainbow-loving, pillow-biting degenerates who’ll do anything to get a quick dick in their ass. To me, good sir, it’s really quite sickening.” “Yeah?” “Indeed… They leave out the part where this town’s got more smog than Paris Hilton has STDs and the homeless people outnumber the people who DO lay down a job, like, 5-to-1. Government Cheese is gold around this shithole.” The brothers in dark comedic arms share a rousing fit of laughter before Silver adjusts the vision on his goggles and prepares to exercise his right to bear arms and all that other Second Amendment-y shit. He turns his head as both he and Shakur take their positions in their respective shooting booths. On the other side of the range, the targets travel down the line until they set themselves in perfect alignment to his trajectory. “Ready to do this?” Silver asked. “Does Tony Gamble not have the ability to see over the dashboard when he’s driving?” “Hypothetically or literally?” “Either or.” “Oh, yeah. My one-year-old daughter already knows how to say ‘I’m taller than you.” Both men adjusted their earplugs to block out the monstrous noises they were about to make vis-à-vis their guns and started to focus as the targets rested in place. Nothing but pure focus at this point. There seemed to be a lot of that going around lately. ~~~~~~ The bullets exited the gun with laser like precisions, mutilating the designated targets. Soon the process of death became academic for PRIME’s resident Commie Emo, and he went into a trance like state. He has been seen, much like his partner on his left, as one of PRIME’s running jokes for far too long. In one night, Sonny extinguished his former persona in pursuit of more illustrious pasture. Devin Shakur hoped that Revolution 134 would be the cessation of snide laughter in his direction. The man is capable of winning championship gold, but much like his girlfriend, one small nickname has overshadowed whatever accomplishment Shakur put his name upon. It made him sick to his stomach. Shakur did not care if it made every man, child, and woman in the Cow Palace vomit buckets, he was going to become the new slayer of dreams in front of their very eyes, with nobody capable of halting his rage but his own hands. Endless days of agony would be rectified in under twenty minutes. Shakur was sure that the new tag team wished to pull off the biggest upset in PRIME since Chainz defeated K-Wolf in his debut, but it would not happen. If destiny sided with the kids, Shakur would choke the subjective bitch until the spotlight was thrust upon him. Faint images of Eli Vanness and Felix Young came into focus for Shakur the very second that two bullets remained in his gun. Guess where they went? Right between the eyes both times. Dead fucking on. ~~~~~~ BANG! BANG! BANG! BANG! Each of the shots pierced their targets with relative ease. Even though the process itself of firing guns brought some kind of bizarre inner peace to him, right now he was merely going through the motions. The moving targets off the in distance continued to be blown clean through, one by one. More would pop up and more would be taken down. Such was the life of Sonny Silver. His career had been all about proving people wrong. Even when he first started in this business, he took a lot of shit because his father – the big, bad Richard Silver – got him a job in one of the federations he had a hand in supplying money to. He had to fight for every little thing he earned and for years, even sacrificed his own personal health and happiness to do it. There was little room for a love life when your first love consumed you and to this day, still had a stranglehold on every action you took. Inviting Shakur out here was a great idea, he decided. In order to synch properly as a tag team, the male bonding ritual was necessary. And since Devin seemed like the type that would pop a cap in somebody’s ass for looking at him funny, this seemed to be the correct setting to choose from. Devin’s intentions were true and he wanted to win and prove himself just as much as he did. This would be the very first time they worked together in any capacity and they’d have to face off against a couple of kids that at one point, were no different than they were when Sonny himself first started. Eli Vanness and Felix Young wanted to do nothing more than have a chance to succeed. Sonny couldn’t fault them for that. What he COULD fault them for was the fact they would be attempting to succeed at his own expense. No. No fucking way. Not this close to game time. A loss now makes it incredibly difficult to come back from. Eli, Felix, those boys better learn something right now. You can pick anybody else in PRIME to make a name for yourselves. “That shit isn’t happening to me,” he uttered before plugging another target right in the bull’s-eye. ~~~~~~ Now a different avenue was being explored by Devin Shakur at this particular point. Three figures manifested themselves through the eyes of Shakur, those prominent in his life for the better part of 2007. He could never get a moment to breath with those three wanting to encapsulate him in their attempt to garner more recognition for themselves. Why did they deserve to be on the stage that they were on? Jonathan Winters is horrified to take a step into the major leagues of PRIME. Dusk had to hide behind a maniacal Risen Star in order to avoid decimation at the hands of Shakur. Pierce Lavelle has been incapable of pinning Shakur’s shoulders for even three seconds. Shakur would desolate them not because he could, not because he wanted to, not because it would make him a two time Intense champion, but because he HAD to. This was seven full months of war culminating in one night of bloodshed. There would be no other way out but to come out on top. He could not quit no matter the circumstances. The last shots from his gun unloaded into the target at a breakneck pace, hitting the heart every time and not missing a centimeter to the left or right. There was no doubt, he was ready to claim his throne. ~~~~~~ The biggest show of the year. And not to worry, Sonny, it only took you twenty-one years to main event the biggest pay-per-view of a wresting promotion. You’re a very rare case, you know that? Most people that have your experiences in the business without numerous accolades are either famous jobbers or they’re stuck o n some internet show jobbing to people a lot younger than them without the chance to get back to the top. Most don’t get the chance to rise from the ashes multiple times to rise to greatness. You’ve been reborn again… again. BANG! BANG! BANG! Yeah, sure, you took those targets down with ease, but when it comes to the biggest stage of the year, what are you gonna do then? You know deep down inside your heart turning your back on the only connection to the past was a bad move. Lady scorned? Look at the cast on your arm and tell me you don’t believe it. BANG! BANG! BANG! Go ahead. Try and ignore me. You know you can’t. Everybody has doubt plaguing their mind, even the great asshole Sonny Silver. Nobody else may see it, but it’s there and it’s here right now. Come on, Sonshine. Don’t fail me now. You may get that chance yet. Don’t blow it or the only time people will know you is when they choke on the grand stage. They’ll say you pulled a Sonny. “Wait,” Silver said to nobody in particular. “Killean lost back-to-back years at Colossus. That’s twice as bad.” Okay, they’ll say you pulled a Killean. Do you REALLY want that to happen? “FUCK THAT!” BANG! BANG! BANG! BANG! BANG! Sure, that’s pretty funny stuff, but Killean? He had the belt twice during both those confrontations into Colossus. How many Universal Titles have YOU won? ~~~~~~ 15 targets out of 15? Success. Sonny put the gun down on the counter in front of him and removed the empty magazine from the P226. The first round of targets was complete, but the hour was far from over and his cast was beginning to bother him. Luckily, he could still hold the damn gun, but it was starting to itch underneath the stupid cast and he didn’t have a wire hanger to bend into place this time. To his right, Shakur had just completed the first round of shooting with the equal results. Those months spent pursuing Sun Tzu and learning a couple of her ways in the process turned out to be a very good thing. Both men took off their earmuffs and admired the other’s handiwork in putting bullets successfully in all their targets. “Damn, that’s some pretty nice marksmanship, dude,” Silver said, looking out at the field of blown-away targets. “I need to find me a gun-happy Asian to bang at some juncture when my eyesight and my mind starts going the way of the do-do. I’ll lose my hair and my beard before I lose my aim, dude.” “Yeah, it was worth chasing her for,” he noted, but his face changed to that of a slightly more grim expression. “Speaking of losing your mind, did I hear you scream something to the effect of ‘FUCK THAT!’ while you were shooting?” Looking off to the side, the former Mr. Silver grinned sheepishly. “Uh… no. I dunno what you heard, but I was too busy busting caps and kicking ass at this to speak. What about you, what the HELL were you thinking about the entire time you were shooting, dude? I mean, I can get pretty into this, but you seemed right at home here.” “The usual… gas prices sucking ass. Beating up frat boys. No better, no worse than anything this week. “Cool, cool. I’m gonna go buy some more rounds at the counter, you need anything?” “Nah, I’m good.” “All right, catch up with you in a minute.” Both men parted ways for a brief time as Silver took off his goggles and walked towards the gun counter to purchase rounds. The more, the merrier, he chuckled to himself as he patted his empty handgun. As he approached the counter, one of the fellow gun patrons already leaving the area failed to watch where he was going and bumped into him. “HEY, ASSHOLE! WATCH IT!” The kid – some American Eagle-clad prep -- looking no older than maybe twenty-one, backed away briefly and held his hands in the air to try and get this big, burly stranger to back away from him. “Whoa, man, relax! You sound like you need a chill pill or some booze or something.” Silver doesn’t take his eyes off the minor annoyance as he goes the opposite direction, back to a group of similar-dressed friends probably blowing off some steam of their own. Who knows, maybe fucking Abercrombie and Fitch sold out of their favorite fleece coats or something. Without thinking, he looks back at Devin and calls out to him. “Hey, Shakur!” “Yeah?” He shot back. “Remind me... our opponents… frat boys or something, right?” “Some shit like that!” Sonny handed the clerk some money for a box of 50 9.mm rounds and loaded a new magazine as he prepared for round two of venting his irritations. “Perfect.” Yup. Venting was fun.
