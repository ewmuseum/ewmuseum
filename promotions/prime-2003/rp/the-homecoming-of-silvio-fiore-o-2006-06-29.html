The Homecoming of Silvio Fiore-o

29 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 29, 2006 “Now are you sure you’re all ready-o?” The mood in the small room was seldom, as Father Vangelus Olsig prepared himself to take the grand march to view the body of the late Silvio Fiore along with Silvio’s family. Let’s see here...we have Uncle Shirley Fiore, Momma Francis Fiore, Cousin Crabcakes Fiore, Aunt Dandruff Fiore, Grandma Katie Fiore, and Grandfather Dillon Fiore. They all nod in approval to the question. “By the way...couldn’t you have dressed a little better-o? This is your loved ones funeral for Angry Pete’s sake-o...” One glance at the family who are all decked out in McDonald and Car repair man uniforms, Vangelus shakes his head in shame and bursts through the doors, set to enter the church. The family follows. While slowly making their way down the aisle, Vangelus recites the funeral precession as the family behind him weeps desperately. “All rise as we prepare to pay our respects to the late Silvio Fiore-o.” ...All 2 people in the congregation stand as told. “Ashes to Ashes...Dust to Du-...” Suddenly, Vangelus stops in the mid-stride and looks over to his left where he spots Vampir Nosferatu, desperately typing away on a laptop. “Ugh...What are you doing here-o? You don’t even know Silvio...” “Didn’t you hear? I’m making my return and I need some new material..” Vangelus stands bewildered as he shakes his head in shame and continues down the aisle, picking up where he left off. “Man whom is born of a woman shall surely die-o...that which i-...” Suddenly, non other than a hippopotamus-sized Buddy Kingfisher sprints up the aisle and grabs Vangelus Olsig, placing Olsig on his back and carrying the Funeral processor up into the pulpit, throwing him down feet first behind the podium. “What are you doing, foolio!?” Buddy yanks out a program of the funeral and proudly points to the bottom “It says right here ‘refreshments will be served’. I’m ready to coagulate my belly, so hurry the freak up!!!!!” Vangelus shakes his head once more as he pushes Buddy Kingfisher out of the way and begins the eulogy for the late, not-so-great Silvio Fiore. “People, people. We are gathered here today to pay whatever little bit of respect we have for Mister Silvio Fiore-o. Let me first take a moment to retract from my annoying alliterative dialogue (thanks Pete >=@ ) in order to recite a man whom we should all admire on behalf of mister Silvio Fiore...mister Dusty Rhodes. These are the words I’m sure Mr. Silvio would have stated if he’d in fact been alive today. Vangelus clears his throat” “I’ve wined and dined with Kings and Queens...and I’ve slept in alleys and dined on pork and beans. I’ve lived on the end of lightening bolts...and that’s funky. THAT’s the American Dream. I’ll make your heart shake, your liver cri-...” Suddenly, a big commotion breaks out in the front row as Vangelus runs down and attempts to break it up. What’s the commotion? Grandma Katie Fiore and Grandfather Dillon Fiore are fighting. “Security! Security! Get down here and get these two out of here!” shouts Vangelus With no hesitation, Head Security officer, Darryl, boldly walks down the aisle and picks up both Grandma Katie and Grandfather Dillon, carrying them both out with one arm. Vangelus shakes his head in shame and heads back up the podium. “Now...if we may continue-o. Silvio...well...let’s face it. He simply could not Walk-the-Walk-o. His level of suckitude was Copious and his chances of ever winning a match was Dubious-o. There’s a reason he and that fat Couch-Potato over there (*points to Buddy*) teamed up...because if either of them sucked any harder, they’d be glorified porn stars!” Buddy Kingfisher begins weeping after the comment as Uncle Shirley Fiore walks over to comfort him. “You see this–o? (*Holds up the Intense Championship*). Silvio thought that he could just walk right into PRIME-o and take this from me...but he couldn’t, because I am the best thing to ever happen to this division-o! As a matter of fact...” Vangelus jumps down from the podium and reaches into the coffin, yanking out Silvio’s body and placing it on the floor. He covers Silvio as Aunt Dandruff unknowingly counts the pin. One! Two! Three! The organ player plays Olsig’s theme music, “Faint” by Linkin Park, as Olsig raises the belt high above his head in triumph. “That’s my title defense for this month-o. ALL HAIL THE GREATEST INTENSE CHAMPION-O TO EVER LIVE-O!!!!!!!” “Excuse me...” speaks Vampir Nosferatu “Could you say that again? I didn’t quite get it...” “Okay...ALL HAIL THE GREATEST INTENSE CHAMPION-O TO EVER LIVE-O!!!!!!!!! Now that’s some shizzle for your Fizzle.” Vangelus places his foot on the chest of Silvio as he addresses the crowd once more. “All things are usual in the world again-o. Silvio is Dead-o....Vangelus Olsig is the best-o...and...” Suddenly, Uncle Shirley Fiore tries to help Kingfisher out of his seat, they trip and Shirley ends up on top of Kingsisher. Aunt Dandruff unknowingly makes the count again.. One! Two! Three! “...and Kingfisher jobs again-o. And now that I’ve used just about every theme word that Pete has assigned, I have nothing else to say-o. Burn in the fiery gates of hell, Silvio. You may all go to the back for refreshments now-o.” Kingfisher storms to the back as the scene fades.
