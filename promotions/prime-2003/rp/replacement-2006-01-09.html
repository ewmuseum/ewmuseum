Replacement

9 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-9-06 You are being replaced, even as we speak. All around you, the universe is closing to fill the void that is you. With the end of your life racing towards you...you...are...always...being...replaced. * * * * * Kyle Lamen walked out the front door of the Modern Osaka Grill across from the Galleria in downtown Red Bank, and he checked the collar of his black coat in the Red Bank winter chill. The sky was good and gray, and seemed pregnant with moisture. Shit. Rain. Almost on cue, a rush of icy rain collapsed on the town of Red Bank, immediately dampening Kyle and sending him running towards his apartment a few blocks away. The water splashed up around the cuffs of his jeans, and the odd car sped by, casting a fine mist of cold water into the air around him. That takes care of Modern Osaka, then. Naota and the other guys in the band get the restaraunt next year. They've got a good management guy picked out to take over for me starting next month...I think that takes care of everyone except Mom and Bekah. Kyle darted through the falling rain of the Jersey January, his blue hair becoming plastered against the crown of his head. With real life taken care of, at least for a moment, I can afford to think about ReVolution this week, and all the shit THERE that I have to take care of. I mean, thank God they gave me Fergo this week, even if it is only a tag match. If nothing else, I owe him a couple of good shots around the head, for what he did to me a few months back. He quickly dipped under an overhanging awning for a jewelry store, where a young couple was huddled, waiting for a car to pass by. He stopped for a second, shook his arms out, and went back into the rain. I really don't know much about the Face-Eater, other than he's good in a fight, but Danny proved he can take him out. Not that it matters, really...I just need someone to keep Tsonda occupied while Danny and I have it out. The sky opened up, and what was a medium-level rain turned into a torrential downpour, soaking the self-style eVolution of ReVolution to the core. Dunno what would happen if I manage to fuck this match up also. I mean, come on...that loss last week was awful. I come out, and say that I'm going to do things that mean something, and meanwhile, Damien Cruz takes our match, and I'm left looking like the asshole who can't back up his own words. Then, later on in the night, Chet Worth comes at me for NO fucking reason, and I almost get decapitated by X3, who I didn't even know was on the ROSTER here. On a scale from one to ten, that last ReVolution was like a minus-one. I lose again, especially at this point, and I turn up looking like Quartz the Digital Luchadore, or they make me put on the SDMJK mask again. It's a shame they gave me Facey as a partner instead of Arson. I'm gonna have to try and catch him backstage next week. I can't believe he showed up here. Between getting in touch with him, Facey, and trying to talk to that pile Worth, it's gonna be a long-ass night Wednesday... Finally, Lamen's long run slowed, as he approached the back door of Jack's Muic Shop. He fumbled in his jeans pocket for his keys, and jammed them into the lock of the strong metal door. He entered, closed the door behind him, and immediately started ascending the cramped stairs to his right. This is ridiculous. I'm wet, I'm pissed off...and I'm in desperate need of a sandwich. Oh yeah, and I'm dying. And I REALLY need that sandwich. Kyle climbed two flights of stairs, before stopping at his loft door, which held a note on it. LAST RENT PAYMENT FOR FEB-JULY 2006 - DUE AT END OF MONTH Too-fucking-riffic. I wonder who's gonna rent this place after I'm gone? I wonder if they're already looking for someone? He fumbled with the door, kicked it open, and hastily pulled the note off the front of the door while entering. And he didn't bother turning on a light or anything, the fairly open apartment was dark and gray in the midday light of the storm. Kyle solemnly made his way past the punching bag he had hanging up in the living room, with the CAT scan of his tumor taped to the sweet spot near the center. He gave it an angry look, but then focused his attention on the refrigerator, and digging out some turkey from the bottom drawer. This is bullshit. This weather is making me mopey. I need to get some music on, or plug in my axe, and get some energy going in this place. As soon as I get this sandwich safely in my stomach... He pulled some bread out of a bag, and grabbed the mayonnaise, when his pocket began vibrating. He immediately set his food products down. Be Sarah. Be Sarah. He pulled the phone out of his pocket, and the caller ID read 'Sarah McIntyre', the woman who was contractually obliged NOT to ignore him, but had been recently anyways. He kissed her at a New Year's Party, and since then, she'd disappeared. Thank god. He opened his Razr with a snap. 'Hey, Sarah. What's going on?' The voice on the other side was droll, and resigned. 'Nothing major, Kyle. The weather sucks, huh?' Weather talk. What's wrong with her. 'Yeah, I hope it doesn't interfere with our flight tomorrow...' Kyle asked, his voice touched with some nervous anticipation. The pause was long, and grew increasingly awkward. 'We need to talk about that. Kyle, I'm sorry. Kensuke's moved me over to a new client. You're going to ReVolution by yourself.' Shock. Still, sickening shock. If the turkey on the table could've cringed, it would've. The muscles in Kyle's stomach wrenched. Another awkward pause, another few seconds of dead airtime...before Sarah rushed to speak and her words came out tumbling all over one another. 'I'm going to work for Jiro and his partner in their new fed, they need help starting up. There's two of them, they're both barely eighteen, and they need someone to watch out for them.' Her voice edged away from sincerity and towards bullshit, but just barely. Kyle shook his head, some of the water flicking out of his hair and spattering against the walls of the apartment. The bread would have tiny wet spots. 'Come on, Sarah...I expect better than that from you. If this is about New Year's, then-' She cut him off with a quickness. 'Shut the fuck up, Kyle. This is NOT about New Year's...nothing is ABOUT New Year's. It's about my goddamned job. I do what I'm told to do and go where I'm told to go. I don't know WHY Kensuke chose to reassign me, but he did.' Her voice was angry and tired, almost breathy. Kyle stared out the window of his apartment, eyes boring holes into the tall brick building across Broad Street, the other half of Jack's Music Shop. His voice almost caught in his throat. 'You could've fought it. You could've told him you wanted to stay with me.' 'You're right, Kyle. I could've.' -click- And that was it. Sarah hung up on him, and it was that simple. They could be in the same area code, same town, same industry, and she'd passed him by. She'd replaced him with new clients. And it was that fast. The phone rang again, same number. The caller ID popped up 'Sarah McIntyre.' Maybe an apology? I owe it to her to apologize, to try and make this work. Even if it's as friends... 'Hey, Sarah..listen-.' The voice that interrupted Kyle wasn't Sarah's. It was a different voice. A guy's voice. 'Um, hey. Sorry, hit the wrong button I think.' -click- Kyle was left holding the phone, eyes wide and unblinking. Corners of his mouth falling. She's got someone else. I'm not just being passed by...I've already been replaced. In her job, in her life... He chucked his cell phone against the window, hoping for a shock of cold, wet air and the breaking sound of glass. Something to distract him from this raw, red feeling in his head, and a dark, black feeling all around him. Fuck it, is there anything left of me anymore? The glass and phone disappoints him, and all Kyle gets is a dull thump as the phone bounces off the windowpane, and clatters to the floor. The world around him isn't what's shattering...it's him.
