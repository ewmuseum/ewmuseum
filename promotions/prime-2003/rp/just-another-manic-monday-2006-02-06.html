Just Another Manic Monday

6 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/02/06 Waking up in a puddle of drool that stunk like a devilish cocktail of all the incarnations of alcohol under the sun wasn’t anything particularly new or exciting to Ignatius Lisieux. It had been another typically lonely night for the French Canadian, he’d been to a local watering hole to watch some live music and drink himself into a blissful oblivion before the week got itself firmly underway and he’d came home alone to collapse into his Motel 7 bed without a care in the world. “Beep… Beep… Beep… Beep… BEEEEEEEEEP!” “Dammit Williams, hush up, the boy needs his rest!” Ignatius stirred at the noises that came from both his left and right, face-down in his oh-so-cheap pillows the former Global Champion issued a strained nasal groan before slightly lifting his head from its resting place. “Shhh… he’s waking up.” Beams of light that bled through the nylon drapes shot into Ignatius’ eyes like bullets from a pistol, sending him to crash his head firmly back down into a dreary slumber. “Mom go away, I don’t wanna go to school today…” he uttered pathetically, harking back to what must have been a dream. Either ignoring or simply not hearing the loud snigger from his right shoulder, Lisieux grabbed the blankets and pulled them swiftly over the top of his head with another grunt. “HEY BOY! STOP HOGGING THE DAMN BLANKETS!” Shooting up like a startled deer caught in headlights, Lisieux rolled out commando-style from the bottom of his bed and adapted a pathetically cumbersome fighting stance at the foot of the room. “…Holy shit…” he blurted out, shocked at what was before him. Rubbing his eyes ferociously as though desperate to remove the projected image from his mind he turned away from his place of rest for a brief moment to shake the cobwebs out of his head and then back again… “Morning Champ!” Jesus Christ… Johnny Noble was laying on the bed… dressed in all white and with a dumbass smile etched onto his face. “Ah Sinner, I wondered how long you’d keep us waiting!” Hoyt Williams! Suited up in some kind of revolting red spandex that left a fairly small amount to the imagination… laying on the opposite side of the bed to where Noble was! “Oh… my… God…” Ignatius backed up into the wall and slumped down to a seated position on the horrible excuse for carpet underneath him as his two PRIME colleagues propped themselves up against the headboard, not seeming to notice how intrinsically wrong three men sharing a bed is… and this was Hoyt Williams here. “Say there Johnny… I think Frenchy here is a little confused!” Damn right Ignatius was confused… last he could remember was ambling back towards his motel room from getting bleary-eyed and melancholy in a local bar… and wait, that’s actually all he COULD remember of the preceding night. “You ain’t got nothin’ to worry about Iggy, it’s just me and Hoyt here.” Lisieux raised himself up and bolted for the window, checking to see what the state of play was outside… fairly bright on a misty, cold morning… standard fare for February. He looked back towards his bedside table, the digital display read “10:37” and then Hoyt piped up again… “What’s all this here, boy?” Looking accusingly towards the table where the clock rested, Hoyt raised his eyebrow at various pill bottles dotted around carelessly, not hidden from plain view. Although Lisieux was still too shocked with the whole scenario to answer himself, Hoyt’s apparent partner in crime spoke up for him… “Well, Ignatius here thinks he needs that crap to get along in life…” Hoyt’s eyes narrowed and he pointed a finger at his former tormentor, “All you need is the power of the Lord in your life, sinner, allow me to cleanse you! I will do so for an extremely small fee!” “Don’t listen to him, Iggy,” interrupted Noble, “Hoyt here represents the satanic side of your conscience… the part of you that attacked Black Angel and left Tony Rolo for dead, the part of your body that drove you back into PRIME on a mission of redemption…” “I’m basically your best part…” chuckled Hoyt in typical super-villain/Million Dollar Man fashion, “Anyway, don’t listen to this washed-up old has-been, this guy represents the part of you that allowed you to lose the Global Title to Rolo, to be humiliated in the first Dual Halo by Poet and to be burned like a martyr by the sinners in the Dark Age.” Struggling to comprehend just exactly what in the name of hell was going on, Lisieux sat once again slumped back against the wall of his motel room with his head in his hands, briefly rocking from side to side like some retarded nutcase, “So… I go to sleep alone and the next morning I wake up with Hoyt Williams to my right side wearing the tightest red spandex that I have ever seen… and on my left side there’s Johnny Noble… in a white dress…” Noble’s eyes widened in dismay at that comment, “This here is NOT a dress! This here is angelic garb! It’s standard heavenly fare!” He defensively stated. “Pfft! I don’t see why you get to wear the dress, I’m the Son of God in all of this… whoever cast this damn shenanigan is going straight to hell.” Hoyt’s fist clenched dramatically in the air as he spoke, but his apparent anger quickly subsided as boredom set in, “Anyway… are we doing anything today?” “Yeah Ignatius, what were your plans for today?” inquired Noble. Lisieux, still amazed at the scene in front of him but happy to play along with the deluded scenario for a little while longer, answered after a brief pause, “Well my plan was to catch some breakfast wherever looked good and cheap, hit a gym for a few hours and then head on out again later tonight.” “…Thrilling” concluded the sarcastic Williams. Noble shook his head, “That ain’t living, boyo, I’m here to set you straight and have you enjoying your existence again.” he proclaimed. Lisieux raised an eyebrow and nodded towards Hoyt, “What’s he here for, then?” Hoyt grinned back at him and then at Noble… “He’s here to make my job as difficult as possible…” replied Mr. Incredible - still wearing a white dress. “No, no… I’m here to show you another way, Lisieux, an easier way on the road to a fulfilling existence.” smiled Hoyt again. Noble looked at Hoyt with a condescending glare, “Who do you represent? Tell the boy here who you represent…” “The Lord, Jesus Christ, Hail Mary, Carpenter Joseph, John “The Baptist”, The Pope, Mel Gibson, and George W Bush, otherwise known as (cough) Satan.” responded Hoyt swiftly. “Who was that last one again?” “(Cough) Satan” “Who?” “(COUGH!) Satan” “So… Satan then?” laughed Ignatius, happy to be amused rather than scared by another “episode” in his life. “…Yes” conceded Williams, finally, with a meek response. Lisieux stood up and nodded towards the elder statesmen of the trio, “So, considering that you’re wearing a white dress and a halo… I’m guessing you’re here on behalf of the Big Guy?” “Baltasar?” jibed Hoyt… ”Yeah… I’m the angel on your left shoulder, sent by the G-O-single-D” nodded Noble. “He’s basically a Goody-two-shoes, with no testicles…” Noble scowled, “Would a goody-two-shoes do this?” Mr. Incredible zapped a finger towards his opposite number, who yelled out in screeches of pain as his red spandex leotard began shrinking further and further… Lisieux stood still in a state of shock as the previously-disgusting red garment became almost unbearable to witness as Hoyt Williams sat before him in nothing more than a pair of extremely tight devil-red hot-pants. Johnny Noble sat with an extremely satisfied grin on his face as Hoyt protested vehemently… “I refuse to be seen in public like this! Oh God it burns… Do I have one of those magic finger thingies!?” he inquired hopefully… “No, no you don’t…” responded Noble with another cheeky grin. “No fair!” came the reply, eerily reminiscent of a five-year-old. Shaking his head once again to try and drill some semblance of reality back into it, Lisieux was struggling to comprehend just exactly what was going on right before his eyes, “So why exactly are you here… is this definitely not a dream or some drunken episode?” “Certainly not!” exclaimed Mr. Incredible, “We’re here because this week you have been placed in an extremely delicate ethical dilemma. The wicked Tyler Nelson saw it fit to book you and Ozric Mortimer together, as a team, to take on Paddy O’Shea and someone else you know fairly well… Vangelus Olsig.” “The Artist Formerly Known as Dani Furher…” chimed in Hoyt with a proud smirk. “So, with that being said… it seemed necessary that you be aided in your direction for this conundrum by being contacted by both variations of your conscience. The “bad” side…” Noble directed a glance towards Hoyt Williams. Williams waved, “I prefer ‘misunderstood’.” “…And the good side… me.” Noble smiled again as Lisieux began to grasp the concept… “Okay, okay…” he pondered, “So I’m assuming Hoyt is here to tell me to crush Mortimer and Furher for what they did to me with the Dark Age and to crush Paddy… probably just because he’s Irish… and that you’re here to…” “…Help you find the best possible solution without resorting to ultra-violence.” interrupted Noble. “He does that a lot” observed Williams. “What?” responded Noble and Lisieux in stereo. “Interrupts people…” “No I-” “Yes, yes you do…” interrupted Hoyt with a sly wink. Noble wagged his magical finger a little as a warning to his opponent, causing Hoyt to reach protectively for his lunchbox, before turning his heavenly attention back to the messed-up and ragged French Canadian. “We can’t have you going out like that, why don’t you get yourself ready and me and Hoyt here will watch some Oprah.” “Oprah?!” criticised Hoyt aggressively, his voice burdening some definite disbelief. “Oprah is certified and acclaimed by the Heavenly Bureau of Education as a necessary learning tool for all apprentice angels,” responded Noble matter-of-factly, “…Now hush-up and enjoy, it might just do you some good.” Lisieux seemingly couldn’t stop shaking his head at the madness as he grabbed his washbag from inside of his travel bag and headed for the en suite bathroom. Turning back with the briefest of glances - just to check that Hoyt and Johnny were in fact still there - Lisieux wore a puzzled look as he pulled the door over behind him. Not wanting to waste any time on this manic Monday, he decided that there was no need for a shower (despite the fact that he probably smelled about as good as a second hand douche bag) and instead went straight about his business of teeth brushing. While trying to concentrate on brushing just like his hygienist showed him as a child he couldn’t help but be distracted by thoughts of the two pro-wrestlers who were playing a warped game of fancy dress in his bed. “Just what in the hell is going on here… am I dreaming!?” he thought to himself. One painful pinch on the soft skin of the underarm later and the French-Canadian thought it safe to say that this MUST be real if he could feel pain that bad, and then he was left to continue brushing while trying to find the answer to the problem. “I can’t still be THAT drunk can I? I mean hallucination-drunkenness is nothing entirely new… but eight hours later…” He gripped his brush between his teeth and then set about pacing up and down the cramped bathroom in as straight a line as he possibly could. “Nope… definitely not drunk…” A quick peek through the nook of the open door later only resulted in hearing a great big belly laugh from the hot-pant wearing Hoyt from something or someone amusing him on the TV, it basically did nothing to alleviate Ignatius’ fears of insanity. “Is this the craziest thing I’ve ever seen or done?” Funnily enough he actually had a hard time remembering a lot of the crazier things that he should probably never forget. Thinking Toshiaki Motoki was some evil genius out to get him, changing his name to Richard Larkin… but he certainly hadn’t forgotten about where he had been for the last year of his life… “Liberty Falls Mental Institute… that MUST have something to do with it!” Colombo probably wouldn’t feel threatened by this brilliant piece of detective work, but at least Lisieux felt appeased that the root of the problem may have been found. He was a big old cup of crazy and he had been that way since day one in PRIME, so he just figured he’d roll with the fact two of his colleagues were dressed like bad drag queens in his $20-a-night motel room.... …A few hours later on that manic Monday - after Hoyt Williams had convinced Lisieux to bleach his hair bright white and partake in a full-body wax before somehow persuading him that it might be a good idea to proposition a married woman with children - Ignatius Lisieux found himself sat over a cup of hot coffee with his two tormentors sat huddled on either side of him. “I can’t believe how much you’ve listened to him today Ignatius…” mused a disheartened Johnny Noble, still wearing clothing that looked suspiciously like a white dress, “Hair bleaching, attempted adultery, hair removal, more alcohol and those damn piercings… I suppose you at least agreed that you could do with some coffee inside of you.” The equally homoerotic inhabitant of red hot-pants sat on the opposite side of his victim with a broad, broad grin spread over his face and a proud glint in his eye. “Those piercings look great Ignatius, really old school Lisieux right there…” chuckled Hoyt. “Was there really any need to get six piercings in one sitting?” inquired Noble with a stern look towards both men sat beside him. “…Seven, actually…” humbly informed the French Canadian to his “good” conscience. “Seven!?” exclaimed Noble with a deep outburst of breath, “Oh Lord, don’t tell me…” “No, no!” laughed Lisieux, “Just my left nipple…” Johnny Noble still looked shocked and with all the aplomb of an ageing relative blurted out, “Jesus Christ on a bicycle! That’s nearly as bad!” Hoyt Williams was taking great delight in his devilish influence over the former Global Champion, despite everything the French Canadian must have heard about not taking advice from anyone looking gayer than Boy George he seemed intent on following the advice of his seemingly predominant “bad” side. “So are we gonna get you some tattoos done after this?” proposed Hoyt with a glint in his eye and a sly grin towards Mr. Incredible, “Or maybe we could go to the zoo and throw stuff at the penguins? I‘ve actually heard there‘s a really good brothel around these parts…” “That’s it… that’s enough… I’ve allowed you to get away with far too much today already, Mister. Because of all your little things we haven’t even had a chance to discuss Ignatius’ dilemma… it’s the whole reason that we’re here in the first place!” Noble slammed his heavenly fist down onto the table, causing Lisieux to jolt backwards in shock. “Booooooooorrrrrrrring!” faux-yawned Hoyt, before Noble wafted his finger once more, apparently silencing the Son of God in fear of spandex-tightening consequences. Johnny Noble, becoming increasingly frustrated, straightened himself up a little and removed the flustered expression from his face before turning towards Ignatius and placing his hand onto his left shoulder… causing something extremely Fantasia to happen… Lisieux jolted back in shock as his mind was transported to another time - pain and fear shot through the veins of his body like a piston as he struggled to make sense of his Noble-induced vision. Darkness shrouded his memory as Noble’s angelic voice wafted in his ears, pleading with the French-Canadian to find clarity amongst the madness, and then he saw exactly what Mr. Incredible wanted him to see… Looking down through widened eyes Lisieux saw Dani Furher, Tiamat, Shadow, Vampir Nosferatu and Ozric Mortimer crowding around the huge ReVolution 50 Christmas tree with sadistic smiles etched on each and every one of their faces and it all suddenly came flooding back. Hurriedly looking down the MVP became frozen in fear as flames began tickling menacingly at his toes. A tear began to drip from the battered and bloody face of Lisieux as he screamed out in pain and screamed out for somebody to help him. The burning started… his legs were roasting hot and almost numb with pain as the fire took hold of his wrestling boots, Ignatius’ screams became louder and more frenzied as tears streamed mercilessly down his cheeks. He fixed his glance upon Ozric Mortimer who bowed solemnly, along with the other members of the Dark Age, as a ritual chanting began - Lisieux couldn’t believe what he was seeing. More screams for help and of pain and anguish bellowed around the halls of the arena as the Dark Age suddenly scattered like scalded dogs… Out of the smoke that was surrounding the view of the almost-unconscious non-effigy emerged some angelic figures and all that Ignatius remembered at that point was the brief sense of hope as he saw the eyes of Boda, Emilio Rage, Killean Sirrajin and Paul Cain glowing through the choking smoke. Then it was all black. Lisieux gasped for a huge intake of breath as he regained control over his body, his limbs all shot out into tensed positions as he returned to his senses and sprawled himself out across the diner table. Johnny Noble placed his arms soothingly around the neck of his younger ally and raised his head up so that he may look at him and hear his message as the mood of the whole scene went from bad to worse… “You remember the hope, don’t you Ignatius? I know you remember it. That feeling that all is lost and that your time has come, you had it, it was taking hold of you… then you saw the glow, you saw the men who had come to save you from certain doom. I know you hadn’t remembered that before now, but you were saved by your colleagues Ignatius, not all men are of the same ilk as the Dark Age were.” Noble spoke swiftly, yet softly, determined to unleash more rays of hope into the life of the falling Ignatius Lisieux. “That was a dirty trick Noble…” scowled Williams, “…why did it take them so long to get there, Ignatius!? Why did you have to almost lose your legs before anyone came to save you!? They were all too busy elsewhere, that’s‘ why, too busy fighting their own battles to care about you.” Hoyt rose to his feet as he spoke, dragging the limp Ignatius up alongside him. Noble rose majestically from his seated position with a stare of pure distrust and pure venom towards the image of Satan that had a hold of Lisieux, he pointed his finger square at the forehead of the laughing Williams… to absolutely no effect whatsoever. “I think I’ve had enough of the whole finger-zapping thing, it’s a poor, poor tool… I can’t believe you thought you had that kind of power over me, anyway… This man needs to see the truth, you cannot blind him with fallacies of hope and justice… he needs to seek redemption himself! This week he has a chance to end this once and for all… Mortimer and Furher together in the ring, it’s a golden opportunity for revenge!” bellowed Williams towards Mr. Incredible. “A man cannot live with revenge as his only reason for breathing! It’s not humane, he’ll kill himself if he continues down the road!” responded Noble, the conviction to saving Lisieux ever so evident in his tone of voice, “You cannot expect a man to live for nothing but destruction and redemption, that is not the life Ignatius Lisieux was intended to lead.” Hoyt realised he was cutting no ice with Noble and swiftly turned his attention to the weaker Lisieux, “Crush them, Ignatius… destroy them both for what they did to you… destroy Mortimer for lighting the fire that almost killed you-” Noble cut him off frantically… “Don’t listen to him, Ignatius… don’t let him poison you into becoming one of his disciples, you will be no better than the Dark Age if you go down-”, Hoyt refused to be denied… “The time is here, Lisieux… the time is now… regain control of your existence, destroy them and take the Irishman with them…” Then something clicked inside of Ignatius with the mention of the fourth member of the match and his eyes shot open as he stared intently down at Hoyt Williams, the personification of the Satanic side of his personality… “What does O’Shea have to do with any of this? Why should he suffer, too?” Hoyt looked stumped and desperately tried to cover his tracks, “He is of no importance here-” Noble began smiling and laughing to himself at the realisation that Ignatius had seen the light, but Lisieux broke the mould as he turned towards him and fired an angry glare in his direction, also, startling Mr. Incredible into a subdued silence… Lisieux spoke with ember burning in his voice, a warning to the two men sat either side of him and to the rest of the PRIME superstars, “No man controls my destiny.” And then he awoke all over again. Waking up in a puddle of drool that stunk like a devilish cocktail of all the incarnations of alcohol under the sun wasn’t anything particularly new or exciting to Ignatius Lisieux. It had been another typically lonely night for the French Canadian, he’d been to a local watering hole to watch some live music and drink himself into a blissful oblivion before the week got itself firmly underway and he’d came home alone to collapse into his Motel 7 bed without a care in the world. “Knock… Knock…” Ignatius stirred at the noise that came from his left from face-down in his oh-so-cheap pillows the former Global Champion issued a strained nasal groan before slightly lifting his head from its resting place. “You awake yet? I thought you were gonna meet me at ten for a jog!?” Lisieux raised himself up and bolted for the window, checking to see what the state of play was outside… fairly bright on a misty, cold morning… standard fare for February. He looked back towards his bedside table, the digital display read “10:37” and then Noble piped up again… “Come on, come on… it’s freezing out here!” Lisieux swung the door open as the disgustingly awake and refreshed Johnny Noble bounded in through the front door right past him, dressed in an all-white tracksuit. Ignatius took a step back in shock at the sight of Noble, who was extremely eager to show him something, (no, not that, you dirty buggers)… “Check out this ghetto blaster thing, it means we can have music playing as we go… just you listen to this!” the excitement in his tone was easy to detect as he set the CD player down on Ignatius’ bed and pushed the “play” button before heading over to the window and drawing back the drapes, sending Lisieux sprawling back like a vampire in recoil from the light. There go the trumpets… “Damn I love this music… what a great way to start the day… Jesus Iggy it sure does smell like someone died in here, you gonna get ready or what?” Noble was bouncing up and down on his tiptoes in time with the music as Lisieux skulked towards the en suite to get himself ready. Noble’s attention turned to the bedside table and the numerous pill bottles that were strewn around, he shook his head to himself and reached over to observe just exactly what Lisieux had been taking. Noble looked down at the collection of painkillers and steroids with a sigh as his enthusiasm for the day ahead began to slowly seep away. “Gonna fly now… it’s so hard now…”
