Alone and Forsaken (PPV match rp)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Overkill John 11:23 'Jesus saith unto her, Thy brother shall rise again.' The promoter was a shred Jew named Moses who knew a Pay-Per-View white robe only event would sell if he pitted his two biggest names against each other in a brutal match. So a last man standing bout was booked between a governor turned flamboyant wrester named 'The Power' Pontius Pilate (Triple P) and a say your prayers, believe in yourself, and take your vitamins type baby face named 'The Savior' Jesus Christ. Sell it did, as it was wrestling’s biggest bible battle EVER. It started strong in the streets of Galilee with the crowd very much behind the savior cheering him on. A few lefts and rights were exchanged but unlike the tide of the red sea which was split, this battle quickly changed to a one sided brawl. The power of the Roman governor was too much for the sandal clad wrestler. Jesus was beaten like Pamela Anderson in a bikini walking through the streets of Iran. Jesus was whipped by members of Pontius's stable the Gladiators. He was spat on by fans that were sick of his goodie-goodie clean cut image. He was beaten with in an inch of his life by Triple P as blood streamed down his face. A crown of thorns was placed on his head before he was piledrived into the hard paved road. They thought he was down and out like a movie filmed in Beverly Hills. But Jesus's manager, slash valet, the lovely Miss Mary Magdalene poured a bucket of water over his head reviving the Savior. Unlike a modern day last man standing match there is no ten count, the battle goes till death. Pontius with his back turned celebrating with his Gladiators was shocked when Jesus tapped him on his shoulder and spit in his face. The expression on Triple P’s mug was classic causing the crowd to pop. Jesusmaina was running wild and with a quick kick to Triple P's crotch the Governor went down. Jesus wasted no time and dropped his patented leg over the chest of the fallen governor. A miracle had happened it looked as if Jesus was about to win as his stable the Disciples came pouring in from the crowd. But then out of nowhere Peter turned on Jesus hitting him in the head with a sledge hammer to the chagrin of Pontius. As that happened the Gladiators came down in full force taking out the disciples. Triple P was up and Jesus was dead. The men strung up his limp deceased body on a cross leaving him for the vultures to eat as the crowd started to disperse in displeasure. It was over or so the world thought but three days latter Jesus shrugged off the pain, got off the cross, and kicked Pilate's ass. Taking the victory and shocking the wrestling world. A few days latter Jesus retired and returned to heaven but the legend lives on to this day. This Sunday Hoyt Williams, Jesus's more talented and better looking brother takes on a last man standing match of his own. With a win Hoyt will not only beat two formidable opponents but he will forever create a legend that can not be taken from him. He will cement a name for himself not only in PRIME, but in wrestling history. Hoyt is a strategic thinker and he understands this is not only good for his wrestling career but also the biblical battle of good and evil. With an energetic smile and a baby blue suit Hoyt stands at the podium ready for his Overkill press conference scheduled for the media and the fans a few hours before the show. After taking a sip of God’s drink known as water the pontiff begins. Hoyt Williams: Greetings and Salutations both sinners and saints. Our culture, our environment, our world has become consumed by violence and drugs. We have gone from a civil nation of segregation and women in the kitchen to a barbaric land of females voting and disgust in the streets. Both of my opponents this weekend in the Main Event exemplify our current landscape. An overweight reporter in a tight suit and a small brown hat with a 'press' tag in it stands up and interrupts. Hoyt is obviously displeased with the chubby mans manors interrupting God’s champion. He shows his displeasure with a sigh. Reporter: Al Larcher here from the Wrestlers Digest. I thought Boda taking on the Universal Champion was the main event, has something changed? Hoyt Williams: Wrestlers digest? Looks like you eat the whole thing!! Fatty, do some jumping jacks for Jesus with me and lose some of that girth. By God man you’re huge!! Your body is your temple to God and let me tell you this chubs; yours looks more like an out house then a shrine. Now to answer your question I do believe there is a match after mine it's sort of a mirage to allow me to get out of the building with out the zillions of Hoyt's witnesses trying to touch me. Basically it’s a diversion to keep the sinners occupied while I leave the building. EVERYBODY knows the reason people are buying the Pay-Per-View is to watch God's champion, which is me, destroy a drug addict and a pain monger. Those two diseases should be thanking me for allowing them to be in the main event on such an important card. Aaron Wishnoff: Are you saying that your opponents don't deserve to be that high on the card? The gorgeous title crafted by the finest jewelers in heaven shines proudly on the shoulder of the champion. His teeth are as white as a Montana snow fall landing on the highest of peeks and he isn’t ashamed to show them in a champion’s smile. Hoyt Williams: Midcarders at best if you ask me. The only reason they are in the Main Event is because I'm in the match! Otherwise, a last man standing match between Mr. Can and Mr. Weed would be opening the show. Aaron Wishnoff: So it would be devastating if you lost? The savior chuckles as he looks at the man like he's nuts. Hoyt's confidence level going into this match is beyond high it’s astronomical as he doesn’t feel he can lose. Hoyt knows he destroyed TCM last month and Token Weed has been less then impressive since joining PRIME. Hoyt Williams: Are you on drugs? Have you been hanging out with Token and smoking that stupid sinner stuff? LOSE? ARE YOU NUTS? LOSE?? I'm Hoyt Williams and that's NOT going to happen. What we have here are two guys who are so lost not only in intelligence…not only in wrestling…not only in religion….but lost lambs of the world. I have promised God that failure is not an option. I am the strongest wrestler in PRIME. I’m thee most technical big man in the sport trained by the best the wrestling world has to offer. I’m fast and if you watched my last match with Mr. Can you’ll know I’m also as HARDCORE as it gets. Dave Hannity: Speed, strength, and technique are great in a wrestling match. I know you can dish out the hardcore nature… Hoyt Williams: Let me stop you right there for a moment. It’s not a hard core nature I’m dishing out it is THEE wrath of God. I am his son and his top general and it is my job to punish those who seek to hurt his world. If I can’t stop the evil this world will be washed clean just as it was during the time of the Great Flood. It’s my job to stop that from happening by any means necessary and with the grace of God. Dave Hannity: I stand corrected, but what I was getting at was while all the skills you clearly possess in a wrestling match are helpful, do you think you have the stamina and chin to last in match of this violent magnitude. Hoyt Williams: I can dish it out and I can take it. I’m going to be hit hard, beaten to brink of destruction, hammered with pains you and I can’t even image, I’m going to bleed, I’m going to fall, but as God as my witness and father I will get back up again. After all rising is a family tradition. For it is written for it is true according to the gospel of Hoyt. Marty Dean: If you win this what is your next goal. Hoyt Williams: The Universal Title will be mine with in the next two months. That is all gentlemen men. Hoyt is rushed to the back as flash bulbs from the cameras of the press blind the champion. A few moments later he finds him self in the Imperium lockeroom. Hoyt sits down on the sofa while tension builds silently with in him as he sits quietly reading. After a few hours of interpretation of Barry Goldwater’s masterpiece ‘Conscience of a Conservative’ fear starts to set in. Hoyt places the book down and looks at the clock. Two hours till his Last Man Standing Match where he will mêlée with two of the roughest, deadly, and most hardcore men the wrestling world has ever seen. Mary with her cherry is in a frantic panic as the man she loves could face injury or even death. She enters the room and sees the fear in Hoyt’s eyes something she has never witnessed before. The 5’6 blonde with the massive chest and hair to heaven sits next to Hoyt on the sofa weeping quietly. Mary: The limo is still here and I wouldn’t think any different of you if you left. Let’s go together that way we can live to see another day. Hoyt: You think I’m going to lose? Mary: This match means nothing to us. There is no title on the line why even bother. It’s an unjust battle and with out the support of the rest of PRIME I think you should pass on it. You can’t win this with out support from the allies and that isn’t going to happen. Leave with me if you love and if not well I’m going to leave alone because I can’t watch you fail. Hoyt: And if I don’t fail? Mary: I’m not going to be there to witness victory or defeat it’s either this silly fight or me. Hoyt: PRIME is my federation and as God’s son I must defend her values. I can’t allow a dope addict and a dope running around here corrupting all of Hoyt’s witnesses. This is my town and by God’s graces I will fight to keep it that way. Mary: I’m leaving. Are you coming?? Hoyt: I think I ought to stay. The champion’s comments are spoken soft and lack his normal assurance. The same assurance he had in front of the fans and the press a few hours earlier is now gone and replaced with doubt and fear. Hoyt knows he must fight the battle but he’s not sure yet how. Mary still pleads with tears in her compassionate eyes. Mary: I don’t understand you! This battle can be fought with out these stupid stipulations from these Satan spawns. Why most you fight two men who want to kill you. Hoyt: They want me to run and by adding this stupid stipulation they are forcing me to run. I’ve never ran from anybody before and neither has God. We’re not going to start now. Mary: I’m leaving you Hoyt Williams you’re too damn stubborn. Mary with tears in her eyes and pain in her heart storms out of the dressing room heading for the limo. Two paramedics are smoking in the parking lot as they watch the angelic lady enter the vehicle and slam the door hard. She is confused and hasn’t made up her mind yet if she will stay and watch and support Hoyt or move on as she disagrees with the violence. Paramedic Mac: How many stretchers we got here? Paramedic John: Just one. Paramedic Mac: We’re going to need at least two no matter how it turns out. You better call the hospital and get some reinforcements. The tick of the clock in Hoyt’s dressing room clicks away as the match is an hour away. Screams from inside the area can be heard as the event has already started. The option to leave is still valid but unlikely as Hoyt sits in a state of calm on the outside while emotions stir on the inside. There is no need to panic but concern has definitely peaked in his mind. A feeling he hasn’t felt since his early days as a rookie over comes him as he feels flutters in his stomach. The inter butterflies are active as a swarm in a field of lillies on a hot summer day. Hoyt’s fellowman Peter Vetra enters the dressing room moving Hoyt’s concentration from the clock and his stomach to his friend and trusted ally. Peter just finished a hard fought battle and the scars of war are evident on his weary body. Vetra: Hoyt I’d love to help you but I have to go. My plane leaves in a half an hour and there is no way I’m going to make it to the airport in time if I stay. Hoyt: The private Imperium Jet? Vetra: Los Diablo have it, and they are in Mexico. Hoyt study’s the face of Vetra for a moment as the realization that he has no support starts to sink in. Hoyt smiles and winks at Vetra to ease the anxiety slightly. He stands up and shakes his Imperium brother’s hand and give him a pat on the back. Hoyt: Great job tonight I’ll meet up with you in two days. Have a good flight. Vetra: I can stay, but I would be stuck here as there aren’t anymore flights tonight. Hoyt: No, go I’ll be fine. I’ll work something out I’m sure. The slam of the door exclaims the solitude Hoyt is feeling as he is left with out his love Mary, and the support of his brothers. He is the lone range and high noon is approaching fast. Hoyt stares sordidly at the clock like it’s his enemy. In many ways it is. Hoyt: Perhaps Mary is right and this battle is worthless. It’s not too late to flee the seen. God help me now. Hoyt walks across the street from the arena where an RISP church is in session and the pews are packed with parishioners. When Hoyt enters the church all the attention of the members turns to focus on Hoyt as the preacher stops preaching while Hoyt walks forward. Hoyt: I don’t mean to interrupt the words of my father but your help is needed. You see I’m in a battle for our virtuosity, beliefs, and welfare against the secularists and addicts of Satan. A battle that must be won in order for our message to keep moving onward and to force the plunge of our enemy’s evil. I give all the time but seldom do I ask for a return. Today is one of those rare occasions as I need back up in this last man standing battle against Satan’s addict and the devils bum. Parishioner Pat: Remember last year when we needed a new church?? Brother Hoyt biblically pillaged money from a black college to pay for this building. Or how about when sister Margaret started dating a Mexican Hoyt found it in his heart to make that fellow disappear. Then there was Billy who was getting fatter then Boda and Aimz’s ego combined. We all know how large that is!! Hoyt came here and put that lard ass Billy on a diet and look at him now he’s built like a rock. What has he asked from us? I don’t know about the rest of you but I’m with this man one hundred percent and then some. Parishioner Margaret: You mean he caused Juan to get deported? Parishioner Steve: Now’s not the time to talk about that Marge. I agree I think we need to stand with Hoyt on this one. Preacher Man Paul: Wait a minute here we’re not trained fighters or warriors. We are men and women of God who do our serving by spreading the word and helping Hoyt’s message multiply. Steve are you willing to walk out on your wife and your lard ass kid turned all state linebacker? Are you willing to risk your neck to help Hoyt and miss your kid’s games? How about you Pat you’re hardly a hundred pounds soaking wet. What are you going to add to Hoyt’s defense of our values? Hoyt I’m sorry we appreciate all you’ve done for us but your job as the savior is to protect us from such harm not put us in the middle of it. I can’t allow my parishioners to risk what they have in order to help you do your job. We can offer you prayers and support in a spiritual sense but to physically help you is impossible. Besides this is a meaningless match why even bother. We wouldn’t think any less of you if jumped into your limo with Mary and rode off into the sunset. Nobody expects you to beat those two hard core psychopaths of the Devil. Now leave us here as we have some praying to do. Hoyt looks out at the see of parishioners who are now avoiding eye contact with their white knight at all costs. Again PRIME’S Pontiff finds himself alone in a battle of damnation that will come down to ultra violence and an overkill of insane, possibly lethal combat. Alone and forsaken by God and by man with less then a ten minutes till his battle starts Hoyt leisurely walks back to the arena. His fear and solitude slowly turning into determination and rage with every step. Nobody thinks he can stop this evil and he himself even turned to doubt. Not very Savior like and the thought of those few hours of weakness begins to infuriate the champion. Hoyt’s lip starts to quiver as he talks out loud to himself. Hoyt: Nobody thinks I can take out the Rolo clone and the Dusty Rhodes meets Jim Duggan wannabe in a last man standing match? They don’t think this war is justified and with out the support of my allies I shouldn’t engage. I should just sit back and allow a pot head and a buffoon to taunt and disrespect me and my witnesses? I don’t fluping think so. I am the greatest force on earth and certainly PRIME. I’m greater then the champion of any title in this federation and more righteous then Jesus Christ himself. I am the be all and end all of wrestling ideology and power technique. Hoyt’s strut and posture goes from defeated soldier to a strong and brave power walk. His pace goes from lethargic to hasty as he marches back into the arena with the determination blazing in his eyes. His mind and mission is simple to seek vengeance at the will of God and destroy his enemy’s. No longer a desperado waiting for a train, Hoyt is now a gritty gun slinger ready for the clock to strike high noon to take down the bad guys and advance the causes of righteousness. The good guys always win and Hoyt is the man in white and the savior of souls. The Pontiff tightens his sparkling robe as he starts to head to the entrance way to battle the bad in a tide of biblical malevolence. He will be going eye to eye with two devils and spitting in their face. The time is now the mach is here. Out of the corner of Hoyt’s eye a pleasant surprise is spotted. The smiling yet tearful face of his love the Virgin Mary. With passion she comes running into Hoyt’s accepting arms. The embrace is short lived as the calls of the ring announcer and the jeers of the idiot infidel fans tear them apart. Although Mary can’t go with him to the ring he no longer stands alone. No words are spoken as he kisses her and enters the arena through the purple curtains the lead to his fate. “And he came to the ram that had two horns, which I had there seen standing before the river, and ran unto him in the fury of his power.”Daniel 8: 6 =w=
