...where the house ALWAYS wins

13 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13.09.2007 “Like a fart in the wind,” Walter began, throwing his last hundred dollar chip indiscriminately onto the craps table, “all my money is gone. Bastards!” The Montecliffe Casino; a place that could either make or break a man in a heartbeat and like most other casino’s a place where the house always wins. Also like most other casino’s, it was a valuable tool to be utilised by the world of organised crime, providing a potentiality to launder hundreds of millions of dollars on a daily basis and precisely why Jonathan Winters’ illustrious employer, Cornelius Stakiri, had a vested interest in the newly established gambler’s dream and why he wanted his dirty little paws all over it, before any of his competitors could maintain any kind of foothold. Winters shook his head and muttered, “Fuckin’ idiot…” “What?” Walter asked, innocently. “It’s not my fault! This game’s rigged!” As luck would have it, the gambling establishment was situated just off Atlantic City’s main boardwalk and close enough for Winters to participate in the much anticipated Roulette Match for the PRIME Universal Title at Dice, Dollars and Tragedy. The only downside to the whole deal was having Stakiri’s brain-dead nephew, Walter, to tag along for the ride; the world’s biggest wrestling fan, and imbecile, his uncle decided to be perfect company for Winters. It was Stakiri’s idea of a joke and revenge all mixed up into one nightmare of journey, but he couldn’t really blame him for it. After all, a couple months back Winters had betrayed his employer to keep him from dissecting the mind of the woman he loved, who held vital information about their enemies organisational structure, but that was another story for another time… The casino security guard standing right by Winters leaned closer toward him. “What the fuck is that moron doing? That money was supposed to last you all fucking night!” Before entering the casino, they’d been fully briefed by Stakiri about his man on the inside; the one helping them obtain an audience with Salvatore Barrone, the casino owner, and the first thing they were told was not to break the cover of their inside guy in case of any perceivable backlash. That said, Walter had all the memory of a dead goldfish with amnesia and was liable to fuck things up within the blink of an eye. The security guard wasn’t much better. Fuckin’ amateur! Winters silently exclaimed, shooting the security guard a stern look and dragging Walter up from the table. “What the hell are you doing?” Walter questioned angrily. “Just get me a little more money, I can win it all back no problem!” “Walter,” Winters began, pushing the much larger man towards the nearest restaurant ten yards ahead, “it’s quite obvious from your performance tonight that you don’t even know how to play craps, so no, I won‘t give you more money.” Walter’s face contorted with confusion. “Craps? I thought I was playing roulette…” Winters sighed and continued towards the restaurant, making sure the clown shoes of a security guard wasn’t following them. “So we got an hour to kill,” Walter said, glancing idly at the scantily dressed waitress walking by, “what do you want to do now? Hit the slot machines? I got a quarter in my pocket…” Arriving at the restaurant, Winters shoved Walters into an empty booth and cursed him under his breath. He was about to slide into the seat opposite him when someone grabbed his shoulder. “We got another problem,” the familiar voice of the security guard decided. Winters whirled around wildly desperate to determine whether anyone of any relevance had noticed them talking. Once satisfied, Winters shoved the security guard in a closed-off corner of the restaurant and, after a brief glance around, shot him a sombre glance; he was in no mood to fuck around today and he certainly was in no mood for fucking amateurs. “I know, I know, okay,” the security guard whispered, “but we do have a problem! I think Salvatore knows. I just found out he’s got connections and rumour has it he pays good money to know who’s in his casino. He won’t be patrolling the floor tonight; he’s sent his right-hand man to do it instead.” So wasting all of my precious time with these two fucking morons was for nothing? Winters angrily smashed his fist against the restaurant wall, forcing the security guard to jump back, startled. “Fuck…” “Oh Jesus!” The security guard exclaimed, gently massaging his forehead. Winters had noted how he’d grown pale and how the sweat now poured off him. “I just realised. If he knows everything about everything, then he may already know about me working with Mr. Stakiri! Oh fuck…!” Winters sighed and grabbed him roughly by the shoulders. “Calm down. If they knew about you, they’d have done something already.” The reassuring notion washed over the security guard like a cool ocean tide and finally he nodded in acceptance. “You’re right. “The question is,” Winters began, “what do we do now?” “What do we do?” The security guard asked, wide eyed and freshly agitated. “Are you fucking serious? We get the hell outta here, that’s what we do!” Winters shook his head. “No. We can still do this. We just need a plan, is all.” “Hey baby!” Walter brash voice interrupted. “Ever seen something this small!” “EEEEEEK!!!!!” The female voice shrilled. Winters turned to see Walter stood on the table with his pants around his ankles and the nice young waitress scampering back into the kitchen. Winters sighed. I leave him alone for two fucking minutes… “Fuck!” The security guard exclaimed, edgily. “Unwanted attention. I’d better go…” The security guard edge around him to take flight, but Winters placed a firm hand upon his shoulder and stopped him in his tracks. “What the hell are you doing?” The security guard questioned, eyes wild with fear. “Security’ll be here any minute!” A grin crept across Winters face. “Security’s already here.” *** “Seriously?” Walter questioned, his voice trembling. “Yeah,” the security guard responded. “He’s got a whole range of ‘em, different sizes and different styles. Doesn’t call ‘em hammers, he just calls ‘em ‘Finger Breakers’.” “And he likes to do it himself?” Walter asked. The security guard nodded. “It’s the highlight of his day.” “Oh man!” Walter exclaimed. “This was a bad idea, Winters. Get this guy to take us to Salvatore claiming we’ve been hustling’ the casino out money is a bad idea! We oughta just go back to New York and tell my uncle it can’t be done!” “Don’t worry,” Winters told him. “It’s probably not going to come to that.” Walter‘s eyes widened. “Probably…?” The security guard paused just outside of the ridiculously large doors, which were flanked at each side by too mean-looking bastards wearing all black and with CIA-style earpieces in place, connecting them to the casino’s security network. “What do you want, Randall?” The one on the right asked. “The boss ain’t seein’ nobody today.” “Really?“ The security guard said, pushing Winters and Walter reluctantly forward. “I’ve got something here that’ll really make his day…” The all-black security guard raised a speculative eyebrow. “Hustler’s?” Randall the security guard nodded. “Caught ’em trying to take down the craps table…” Both all-black security guards glanced at each other and nodded, then one whispered something into an hi-tech walkie talkie, whilst the other heaved open the doors. Being led into the great expanse that was Salvatore Barrone’s office, Winters had to stop and admire the man’s the taste. It was nothing like the cheapness that came as a prerequisite down on the casino’s main floor, it was more classical and considerably more tasteful; not entirely a difficult to do, hell, a burnt-down self-service laundry mat had more taste. The furniture all looked antique, the pictures all looked ridiculously expensive and the seemingly priceless vases spread across the entirety of the room just made him damned nervous. “What is this?” The man with the bald head and very expensive Italian suit questioned from behind his grand oak desk. It was Salvatore and he wasn’t seem happy. “I told you I didn’t want to be disturbed!” “I know sir,” one of the all-black henchmen ventured, “but we have a hustler and we though--” “A hustler, hey?” Salvatore interrupted. “Okay. Bring them to me and fetch me my collection of Finger Breakers will ya?” The all-black henchman scuttled off, whilst Walter nudged Winters in the ribs with the point of his elbow. “F-F-Finger Breakers? Oh Jesus!” “We’re not hustlers, Salvatore,” Winters announced. “We’ve actually come to deliver a message.” “Shut the f--” The remaining all-black henchman began, edging forward with sinister intent. However, a potent kick to the ‘groin area’ stopped in his tracks and Walter’s few obligatory kicks to the back of his head whilst down didn’t hurt matters either. His only tangible contribution to this whole damned disaster… “What the hell is this?” Salvatore questioned, standing from his chair outraged. “Do fucks know who I am? How dare you--” “Shut the fuck up!” Winters interjected. A wave of outraged washed over Salvatore’s face and momentarily stunned, his mouth was unable to formulate the adequate words to respond. “My message comes from my employer, Cornelius Stakiri, and he just wants you to know that you work for him now. Fifty percent of your monthly profit from this place goes to him and for this you’ll be granted his protection…” “B-B-But Stakiri‘s at war with a rival organisation!” Salvatore declared. “I don’t want to be involved in that, it could tear my casino apart at the seams!” Winters grinned. “It’s going to happen whether you stay neutral or not and you know it. This way, you get to back the winning team from the outset. It’s that simple. Besides, this isn’t a conversation - I’m just telling you how it is. Don‘t make me come back here.” Walter glanced around outraged. “That’s it? That’s all we came here to say? All of this, just to deliver a sentence and a few threats? With all the talk of finger breaking, I shit a brick back there! This was a waste of my time…” With that Walter stormed out of the office, walking in a decidedly funny manner and the faint sound of squelching following him the entire way. Winters sighed, muttering, “I never knew your time was precious…” He followed him out of Salvatore’s inner sanctum, keeping a distance all the way.
