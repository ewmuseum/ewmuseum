Date: Today How could your head feel full of warm water, but your body be frozen? Amy was shaking violently, soaked in her own icy sweat despite the warmth of the room. While the Karina Wolfendens of the world were warming up, Aimz had been laying in bed with a sneeze, cough and fever that would send anybody even a pound smaller to the doctor, but not Amy Campbell; No, she had to fight everything stubbornly - the doctors, at least. She'd given in entirely to the flu that had become her new arch nemesis...if not only because there was a small chance she'd get to sleep through that damned trip to the next ReVolution. I hate handwriting. If I wasn't too lazy to find my laptop, this would be a livejournal entry. Tomorrow I've got to prep my bike for summer. That's going to be fun if it's a nice day out. I love being outside for those rare nice days... doing anything makes me feel accomplished. When family at grandma's birthday got to be too much, I retreated with tail tucked firmly between my legs to her second living room and watched The Ashlee Simpson show. Seeing that hack on stage made me get the itch to play perform; Sadly, my opponent this week is only Token Weed. Essentially, this means I can do a few lines and step out to kick the snot out of the boy. Asylum? Does ANYBODY care anymore? His greatest accomplishments rank among street fighting some dyke I've already beaten. The guy came into PRIME and had all the game talk... but what happens? He loses. I talk, but I back it up and only lose when Negasonic Lovers step in unfairly... as opposed to Weed, who hides behind a stupid gimmick name that means jack to him and talks so much that he couldn't back himself up even if he HAD talent. I keep having nightmares. No, not about wrestlers. It's planes. Being stuck in airports. Foreign lands. Far from home. Far from comfort. Far away. Anxiety. I fear customs and immigrations officers. I wish I could fly past them when I go to the US. Is there a get out of jail free card? All I see is a ticket to Florida... -amy sorry about this. worst thing I've ever written. very blah
