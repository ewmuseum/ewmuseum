A Matter of Trust

20 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/20/07 "I'm still in shock," said Dogan, the large Turkish man, as he unpacked his suitcase. The man, Captain Suleimon's cousin and part of his entourage, hulked over his chair revealing a beer belly, the tell-tale sign of the deterioration of his physique from his wrestling days. "I'm still in shock that you've actually learned to trust this man with your back. He's not a Turk, so by all rights, you should think he's a retard." In the doorway of Dogan's hotel room stood the Captain, eating a pear. He bit into the fruit and chewed it down, the pause impregnating the air with a weird sort of silence. He swallowed and looked his cousin directly in the eye. "Dogan, I'm suprised you actually still tour with me after all the abuse I heap upon you. I mean, I don't know whether its because you really, really like me or because you're as dumb as an ox." "Actually, it's for the frequent flier miles." The sardonic look in his eyes got a little under Suleimon's skin, but he didn't show it. He's the one in control of the conversation, not his liberal-minded cousin. "Of course it is. You know, you're lucky you're blood and a good match strategist, or else..." "Or else what, Tariq? C'mon, let's not change the subject here. What's with the trust all of a sudden?" "All of a sudden? Pfft, if you had known..." "Yeah yeah, you keep me in the dark. Big flipping deal. But still, I know how you are with trusting people, and let me say, you make Steve Austin look like the welcoming type with the way you are." "Were..." ---- (For the benefit of those who aren't familar with Suleimon's AWC series of RPs, they were mostly done in the style of flashing back to Suleimon's early life as a wrestler in an indie fed known as Liberty Bell Wrestling. This RP is a continuation. ---- "Oh Jesus." Tariq sat in the back of the New Alhambra Arena, his head throbbing almost as intensely as the beating of a nervous heart. His head had taken quite the rocking during the night: two knucks shots and a powerbomb. His pride had taken the worst hit. He had been hustled out of a shot at the LBW Heavyweight Championship. He held ice to his head, and that made the swelling go down, but it didn't ease the pain of having a chance at glory stolen away from him by a meth addict only looking for the larger purse to get his fix. "It's alright Tariq..." His manager and mentor, Malik Rahman, tried to calm down his student, but Tariq was beside himself. "Oh Jesus... I'm going... I'm going to get him." "No you're not, not right now. You're going to sit here and keep the ice on your head so you don't get a concussion. Do you hear me?" Tariq's eyes became consumed by frustration, and he ceased his restlessness. The room's calmness lasted for a moment before James Hopkins, head booker of LBW, walked into the locker room. Tariq saw him and nearly dropped his bag of ice as he jumped to his feet. "I told you, sit the hell down. You needn't get excited." Rahman put a stern hand on Tariq's shoulder and set him down. "But..." "I said sit. No need in you getting hurt even further because you had to go and be a camel's ass." "He deserves to be angry," said James. "You're damn right he does, but staying calm is in his best interest right now. He's not going to get riled up..." Rahman's gaze turns from his pupil to the booker. "But that doesn't mean I can't." "Listen, Malik, you know I don't condone that kind of behavior, but..." "But what, James?" "But you know I can't just go out there and reverse referees' decisions like that. It looks bad." "So he's going to get a shot down the line soon, right?" "Well, that's what I came here to talk about. I've got a problem." Rahman and Tariq both raised their eyebrows at James. "You see, I've got like four or five guys already who're already established in the main event scene in addition to Josias..." "Who got there by treachery..." "We've established that, Malik, and I told you, unless I find him in the act of shooting up, I can't really do anything to him. My hands are tied. But it's not wholly bad news. I do have news of an opportunity for Tariq." "The Hardcore Title is overdone for him. I thought you and he agreed on that." "Well, we did, but that's not it. Two of my top tag teams got signed away on exclusive deals to other companies. Plus the other team just broke up earlier on tonight, so I'm really left with no tag teams left to challenge the Champions." Tariq sat up in his seat and raised his voice. "No, not again. You're not guinea-pigging me with some green bitch like you did last time." "No Tariq, it's not like that at all. I actually thought I'd let you pick your partner." "I'm still not sold on this, James. I mean..." Tariq started to sit up, but he got a rush of pain in his head. "My word, Tariq, sit the hell down! Do you want to spend the night in the hospital?" Tariq slumped back in his seat. "I'm just saying... all my backstage friends on the roster, the crowd loves them. They'd have to sacrifice their career to help mine. Plus it'd steer me more away from the title scene." "Not necessarily. Do you know who holds the Tag Team Championships?" "The Ironmen?" "No, they lost the titles before your match, and they're on their way to Japan. Garret Holden and Jericoholic Anonymous are the Tag Team Champions now." "Wait.... Holden, the LBW Heavyweight Champion?" Tariq's eyes lit up. "Yep. And JA's got some national caché to his name. Seriously, he's been all around the world, and he's an indie darling. Word on the street is that a bunch of national companies are looking at him. MBE's one of them, and they're the hottest new national promotion out there." Tariq sat back and thought to himself that maybe this wouldn't be such a bad opportunity at all. Finding a partner though, that would be the tricky part. The next day Tariq sat up in his locker room, the effects from the night before gone save a bump on his head. He was thinking about whom he could ask to be his tag team partner. Most of the heels on the roster really didn't like him. All the unsavory traits of their characters weren't really character traits at all. They all lived their characters. Tariq was unique in that he really did play into his character, but was a totally different person outside of the ring, away from the fans and the cameras filming for VHS tapes and this new technology known as DVDs. He still couldn't find a solution to his problem outside of asking one of his facey friends on the roster to make a turn. He sat down on the folding chair, put his elbow on his knee and made like the "Thinking Man." His thinking was interrupted. "Hey man..." Tariq looked up, and it was Barry Peters, his best friend in the locker room and one of LBW's up-and-coming popular fan favorites. "Oh, hey Barry. What's up?" "Nothin' much. I heard you were looking for a tag team partner." "Yeah, I am. I'm stumped for ideas." "Well, why didn't you ask me? I'd be down with hitting the tag ranks, man." Tariq's eyes went wide, as he couldn't believe the words he was hearing. "But you're over with the fans. They love you." "I know, but I need something different man. Besides, it's a chance to get a crack at the LBW Champion, man. A big chance to show that I'm a main eventer too! That I belong!" Tariq looked at him a bit funny. Barry gulped for a moment before cracking a laugh. "Of course, the biggest reason is because you're my boy, and I'd be more than happy to help you out." Tariq's look changed back to normal. "Thanks man." The two friends plotted things out over the course of that day. It was perfect. Tariq was scheduled to wrestle JA at the next show, to set things off for the tag feud. Barry would come out the moment Suleimon was in trouble to lose the match, nail JA with his patented Charging Elbow and then help Tariq beat down JA until Holden came to the ring, at which the two heels would turn their attentions to the double Champion. If their plan worked and they were able to fell both Tag Team Champions, they'd talk to the crowd. Suleimon would introduce the reason why Barry turned, which would be because he was tired of sucking up to the fans and that he wanted to get ahead. Tariq was uncomfortable with the obvious avenue, ie, having Barry denounce America because basically, it had already been done in the WWF, and he didn't want Barry to get himself killed walking out of the arena. They agreed to everything and had everything down to a science. The night of the card came, and it was a see-saw match-up that saw both men almost evenly matched. Barry was wondering if he was even going to get a chance to come out and make the plan work, but he knew that if worse came to worse, Tariq would start throwing the match. Surely enough, JA started to work towards finishing the match. Spinning heel kick, tombstone piledriver and then, his finishing touch. The Walls of Jericoholic, a submission move that was a homage to his mentor, Chris Jericho. That was Barry's cue. He came out of the back slid in the ring and drilled JA's blindside with his Charging Elbow, right to the Anglo Luchador's temple. They did a number on Jericoholic, nailing him with a spike piledriver and several boots to his gut. Just like their plan was laid out in their heads, Garret Holden came rushing out of the back. Tariq intercepted him first with a stomp to the head as he slid in the ring, and then he grabbed the Champion to his feet to deliver a more crushing move. However, he was intercepted... by his own tag team partner. Barry interjected himself and nailed Holden with his Barry Blast, a variant on the bodyslam piledriver. Barry started nailing Holden with a barrage of stomps, an attack so punishing that Tariq himself had to pull Barry off. Almost as if he was snapping out of a daze, Barry looked over at Tariq and nodded. Tariq called for a microphone and held it to his mouth. Before he could start talking, Barry snatched the microphone out of his mouth. "You people... this country makes me sick! Ptooey! I hate all of you! I have renounced my American citizenship! I am now pledging my allegiance to Turkey!" The crowd, stunned at first, started to rain boos down on Barry. Tariq just looked at him in shock, but soon realizing he needed to make the segment believable, scowled his face and applauded Barry. As they made their way back to the locker room, Tariq grabbed Barry by the shoulder. "Hey! HEY! What was the big idea back there?" "Back where man? I was just..." "We had a plan, we had a plan and you went off it." "Hey, relax, I was just doing what was best for us. Listen man, if we did things your way, the fans would still be cheering me. They wouldn't have bought that, and you wouldn't have gotten over as much. Now... not only do they hate me, they hate you for turning me against the country. Ain't that better?" Tariq scowled at him. "But we had a plan... now you're going to run the risk of having one of those fans attack..." "Hey, this is for you man. For you." A pregnant pause. "Why... why didn't you bring this up before?" "Because man... because I just like to fly by the seat of my pants sometimes." Barry walked off towards the showers, and Tariq went to the locker room. He found Rahman sitting there, smoking a cigarette, watching the monitor. "Quite the impressive debut for your faction." "Yeah right. He totally went off the plan. We practiced everything for two weeks, and..." "Oh, your plan must not have been that important if he decided he was going to change it slightly." "Slightly? First off, he took my spotlight away from me. We were supposed to beat down the Champion together, but he went wild! And then, he totally changed the reasoning for his turn." "Oh, but that reason was excellent." "It's been done!" "Tariq, let me let you in on a secret. Everything in this business has been done before. Angles are recycled all the time, but people still go for them if they're classics. Whatever your plan was before, it doesn't matter. The point is, he made the change and it got you over huge as a heel, right?" Tariq nodded. "And you went out there and you took what you wanted, that is, the first shot in your war against the Tag Team Champions. You didn't hold back, and you let them know you were on the attack. They're on their heels. That's a good thing, right?" "Yeah, I guess." "Good. Then your lesson learned is that plans don't always work out the way you want them to. Sometimes, it's best to just go with the flow, do what's spontaneous." "I guess.... I'm gonna go shower." Barry and Tariq reconciled quick. They had to; their tag debut was set to happen the next card, against a veteran team new to LBW called Helter Skelter. Before the match was set to begin, Barry caught up with Tariq in the entryway. "Hey man, could you do me a favor tonight?" "Yeah Barry, sure." "Could you try to let me get the pin tonight, if we're in a position to win? There's this hot girl in the front row, and I want to impress her." "You bang her yet?" "No, but after tonight, maybe I will man." Tariq smiled. "Never let it be known that I'm a cock-blocker." As "The Turkish March" cued up, both men walked out. Instantaneously, their "boys will be boys" smiles turned into crowd-hating, jeer-absorbing scowls, aimed on destroying the new addition to the LBW roster. Tariq started off the match with Eric Stacy, and they were off. Stacy got the early advantage, using his vast array of strikes to keep the quicker Tariq off-guard, but Tariq found a way to get his advantage. He countered a kick into an enzugiri. Then, right after, he bounced off the ropes, looking to deliver a leaping legdrop to the prone member of Helter Skelter. He felt a slap on his back. Turning around, he saw the ref signal a tag and Barry entering the ring. Blind tag. Tariq's look of shock took Barry off guard. "What?" Tariq just looked at him. "C'mon, you said it yourself, you ain't a cock-blocker." Tariq just went to the apron while Barry went to work. In between each move, Barry preened for the hot girl in the front row as Tariq got ever more frustrated on the apron. Not only did Barry take away Tariq's momentum, he was breaking character by flirting with an obviously white American blonde girl in the crowd. Barry's hold on the match slipped away slowly but surely, yet he made no attempt to tag out to Tariq. Finally, it looked as if Helter Skelter was going to put Barry away for good. A stroke of luck had it so that Barry accidentally took out the ref on an errant Irish whip, and it was here that Tariq would take matters into his own hands. He pulled a foreign object out of his trunks, a blackjack he kept there for emergencies such as this. He struck both Eric Stacy and Marc Jones with the weapon and put his dazed and confused partner on top of Jones, the legal man. The ref woke up, counted three and the match was over. Tariq and Barry won. After the match, Tariq hurried to catch up to Barry to talk to him... "Barry! Barry!" "Hey, Tariq! Good lookin' out man, I'm meeting up with that chick in the front row at the airport Holiday Inn. She likes the way I get out of sticky situations." "But..." "Hey man, we'll talk later." Barry hurried out the door, and Tariq was left frustrated. As they met up the next day, every time Tariq tried to bring up a complaint, Barry would change the subject. It would be a continuing pattern. They'd have a match, Barry would showboat and Tariq would have to bail him out. Barry would find some excuse to weasel out of discussion of the problems. Finally, the day before the big Tag Team Championship match, Tariq caught up with Barry. "Hey man, can we talk?" "Sure man, sure." "I have to ask you a question... why are you in this team?" "Wh... c'mon man, you know it's because I'm helping you out." "Really? Because every match through the last few weeks, I've had to bail you out because you seem to just want to showboat, to show your wares off. It's gotten us into a lot of trouble." "Hey... the way I see it... uhh, it... it... makes you look good because you get to bail me out a lot! Yeah..." "That's not going to work this time. I don't want to look good as much as I want to win matches. That's how you really look good. If you want to show your wares, you have to get your head in the game. You have to stop showboating, and you have to work with me instead of against me. Is that clear?" "Yeah man, yeah." "Can I trust you?" A pregnant pause. "Yeah." The match began with JA and Tariq. It was a mirror image of their singles match a few months before, back and forth. Tags were made back and forth, each team was working as a cohesive unit. It was back to JA and Tariq in the ring, and Tariq was getting a distinct advantage. He whipped JA off the ropes, but JA tagged out to Holden on the whip. Despite that fact, Tariq still had time to back body drop JA and nail Holden with a lariat. He was in control of both men. Victory was in his grasp. He picked up Holden and whipped him off the ropes. On the rebound... inverted Tiger suplex. The double Champion was nearly out. Tariq sensed imminent victory, so he hopped up to the top rope. Barry saw Tariq on the top, then saw Holden on the canvas. Barry leapt up and blind-tagged Tariq as he was on the top rope, then hurried in the ring to finish off Holden. The shock from the tag sent Tariq flying from the top rope onto the floor, crashing his head against the steel guard rail. Barry's last-second plan didn't go like he'd planned either. Holden countered with a drop toe hold, and then when Barry got up from that, WHAP~! Holden nailed him with his Imported from Japan uraken finisher. Barry was knocked out, and Holden got the pin. Tariq was woozy from his crash, but he looked up to see JA and Holden holding up the Tag Team Championships. It registered in his mind what had happened. He couldn't trust Barry Peters. He couldn't trust his best friend. If he couldn't trust his best friend, whom could he trust? The answer resounded in his head... No one.
