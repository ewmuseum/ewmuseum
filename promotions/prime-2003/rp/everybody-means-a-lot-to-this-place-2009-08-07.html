Everybody Means A Lot to This Place.

7 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/07/09 The scene opens at a podium. Various press are gathered around, and at points lazy photographers snap shots of the empty set-up, a PRIME banner drooping over two stand-up backboards wheeled onto an otherwise empty stage. Murmurs convert to a general buzz as Nova walks out onto the stage, clad in a suit coat, matching slacks, and a sentimental tie with bottles of bourbon on it. He approaches the mic stand on the podium. White-streaked blonde hair drapes his shoulders, and a brownish beard grazes his scalp. Purple bags hang under his eyes. Nova: “Um, thanks, everyone, for being here. Kinda weird, I know, but there are hidden purposes to everything, right?” (He laughs to himself.) Nova: “I apologize in advance for what is going to happen – or appear to happen – at Colossus VI, at the same time being humbled for my continued role in one of the greatest wrestling shows on the planet. It’s…it’s a complicated situation, y’know?” (Members of the press stare around at one another. They obviously don’t know.) Nova: “Maybe you don’t. I dunno. It’s meant an awful lot to me over the years, my time in PRIME. An awful lot. I, um, I might be going away for a while after this, a planned thing. I love this place. It’s still home. But it’s like I’m searching for something now that the industry isn’t good for. Like I have somewhere else to be. It’s been beautiful. I think this show will be a great parting gift. “Everyone’s been so gracious.” (The Risen Star walks away from the podium quickly, lighting a cigarette. He’s bathed in camera flashes. He disappears through the curtain, and no one’s questions get answered.)
