Going Back To Brand New #1

1 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st August 2005 Standing on the edge of the road Igor Arefiev and Constantino Zergolov looked at the place that gave birth to them as performers. It was the birth place of practically every world class circus act or troop that was currently touring the world. The Moscow School of Circus and Performing Arts stood magnificently before them in all its splendour. The snow and ice on the mountains behind the school added quite the picturesque feeling to it all. The high metal gates that were held up by the stone wall, gave way to the one of the grandest buildings that anyone could ever imagine. The grey stone was weathered from over a hundred years of standing tall with the gargoyles still looking as mean as the day that Igor and Constantino first walked in aged nine. To say they were nervous about returning was an understatement. They had been excellent students, some of the best but they just feared that the school had found about their new careers. Swallowing hard, Igor looked to his right to be met by the nervous look of his tag team partner. It was Arefiev’s idea to return to the school that they left just six short years ago. After only twelve months as full-time wrestlers, The Flying Vergomovs felt like shit. “We suck!” were the exact words of Constantino Zergolov as they returned to the locker room in Pontiac. Sweat rolling off his upper body, the Russian was fuming as he paced straight into the shower. Igor Arefiev followed close behind after they had battled for thirty minutes to not even manage one pin or submission. Arefiev was the feeling same emotion but he was less vocal about dragging their name through the mud. Colossus II was supposed to the swan song that would end the year perfectly for the young Russian tag team. Instead it was disastrous. They hadn’t won the titles. They hadn’t won the match. They hadn’t even managed one pin or submission. Igor walked into the locker room, slamming the door shut standing for a second in the tranquil locker room. Constantino had bee-lined straight for the shower room, and the only noise that Igor heard was the gentle crash of water on the floor of the shower. Alone, Igor sat down on the locker room bench elbows on his thighs and stared forward. He looked blankly into the wall, thinking. Alone, Zergolov washed down is body, his mind drifting away in his thoughts, thinking. The two Russians had joined the wrestling world with a huge bang. A couple of months of training in a territory and The Flying Vergomovs were allowed to burst onto the scene with a raw energy and unique style. Now in Pontiac, the pair were beginning to realise some home truths. Their wrestling knowledge was as basic as it was twelve months previous. They knew very little then. They had learned very little since. “We haven’t improved…” mouthed Arefiev, still staring straight at the same brick on the wall. “We haven’t changed…” mouthed Zergolov, his hands caressing his aching arms and back. Now for the normal wrestler that realises their skills are up to scratch, they’d check themselves in with a training facility. But this isn’t the case for the Russians. The Flying Vergomovs come from the school of though that they much be the best at everything and if they know they will never be the best, don’t bother. Igor Arefiev and Constantino Zergolov will never be the world’s greatest mat technicians. They will never have the power to lift a hummer over their head. They will never have the skills to twist and bend a person’s body to the point of breaking. However they can run around a ring with reckless abandon like no other, well maybe Horace Tully had a chance to match them but he’s gone from PRIME now. Therefore, their need to be at the forefront of crushing your body to beat the other, they have returned to school. They have returned to their school. Back outside the Moscow School, Igor and Constantino were still deciding if it was a good idea to return. They knew they would be welcomed back, as long as no one knew they were wrestlers. Still looking up at the gate, both men found it difficult to take that step forward towards the school. Fear of being exposed as wrestlers was overriding their capabilities to move. “BEEP! BEEP!” The sound of a car horn ripped both men from their thoughts as they turned around to a see a Russian car approaching them, and ultimately aiming for the gates. It was one of those Russian box-like cars. You know the type. The kind that nobody else in the world would drive but when you know that you could wake up to six feet of snow it is the only choice for a car. Turning around, Igor and Constantino tried to see who was driving as they moved out of the way. Reaching down they each lifted their suitcases and moved to the right, out of the way as the car crept up to them. The window slowly opened. So many thoughts jumped through the two Russians minds as the window opened. Everything they had worried about on the journey over was now coming to a point. Everything that they were nervous about when they decided on coming back was ready about to be realised. The window opened some more and sitting behind it at the steering wheel was a well-built man, in his forties with a bushy black beard. Grinning from ear-to-ear, it was obvious that not only did the driver know who the two men on the side of the road, but he looked pleased to see both of them. Igor and Constantino both hunched down to peer at the man in the car. “Good morning, gentlemen,” smiled the driver who the tag team instantly recognised as Igor’s uncle and their previous trainer, Tomas Arefiev. Igor instantly nodded his hello as his uncle as Constantino smiled in reply. Tomas continued to grin as he pulled up the car and the door swung open. “You didn’t inform us of your visit,” greeted the man that taught the pair everything we know. Igor and Constantino both sighed with relief as Tomas approached with his arms spread. If he knew they were wrestlers he would have mentioned it now. They relaxed a little as they embraced their mentor. “We didn’t plan to come until last Monday morning…” smiled Igor, welcoming the hug from his uncle, knowing that his place in the circus circles was still intact. After the hug, Tomas beckoned both men to get into the car. “I will bring you to main house…” beamed Tomas as he got back into his box with four wheels. Igor and Constantino clambered into the backseat with their suitcases resting on their knees, and enjoyed the thirty second lift to the main house of the school. “You look well,” smiled Tomas. Conscious that both men had retired from the circus last year after only five years as professionals and only did the occasional performance, he was impressed at how well they had kept themselves. Igor and Constantino both mumbled acceptances of the compliment, but still seemed a little nervous about being back in Moscow. As the car crept down the path of trees before pulling to a halt in the stony driveway outside the large house Tomas leapt from the car and hurried into the house. He was obviously eager to inform others of the return of Igor Arefiev and Constantino Zergolov. Sheepishly Igor and Constantino stepped out of the car, pulling their suitcases with them. Taking a couple of steps back, Igor looked up at the main house of his school. He had walked up those steps on his first day. He had walked down those steps when he graduated. The stairs had begun and ended his time in the Moscow school. He continued to look around him as he was joined by Constantino, staring around the whole grounds, reminiscing about the time they spent studying. Looking up at the grand house that stood above them, their attention was grabbed as a small group of their previous trainers emerged from the front door, lead by Tomas Arefiev. Walking towards The Flying Vergomovs came Tomas Arefiev, a couple of their former trainers, Ivan and Serge and Susanna Nicodemou, the woman that looked after the pair like her own sons when they were nine and were first away from home. Tomas and the two trainers stopped short of the pair but Susanna continued to approach. Her face was emotionless but Igor reached out his arms for an embrace. The three were really close. SLAP! The red handprint of Susanna’s left hand was left on Igor’s cheek as the embrace didn’t come. They knew. Tomas was family so he probably just accepted it. But Susanna wasn’t quite as nice. Zergolov prepared himself. He knew it was coming, but felt that Susanna was allowed to do it. She deserved that much. SLAP!
