Though the timeline does not particularly matter. When is not an important factor to the equation. You have. That is the important part. So the question becomes a matter of memory. Do you remember? Do you remember the scene in Top Gun where Val Kilmer spins the pen through his fingers with such ease that he can still spare the concentration it takes to gaze upon the dreaminess that is Tom Cruise? Of course you do. Now, for a second, let us take pause. If you do not remember this, or worse, if you have not seen Top Gun (like we presumed above), then you should hang your head in shame and exile yourself to the nearest ghetto of white thug wannabes, because you fail at life about as miserably as Jimmy Bonafide fails at using the English language. Back to topic. Val Kilmer. Pen trick. Pure awesome. You remember it. I remember it. More importantly… Tyler Rayne remembers it. The hero of the day has just pulled into a reserved spot in the back parking lot of the Columbiana Centre. His torch red 2010 Shelby GT 500 purrs at an idle before he kills the ignition and steps out of the vehicle. Combat boots land soft on the pavement. It is a stark contrast to his heaving limp from only a month ago. The improvement makes him smile. Tyler steps fully from the vehicle. The ends of his designer jeans (an early Christmas present from Chandler Tsonda) slide down over the laces of his boots. His hand reaches to the edge of his racing jacket. Fingers clasp around the open zipper, palm obscuring the top of the sharp angle red V on the otherwise black leather. It is an odd thing, to think sometimes that such an expression could be more powerful than it already is. He manages, though.
