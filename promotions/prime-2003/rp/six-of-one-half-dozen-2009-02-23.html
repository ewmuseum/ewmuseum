Six of One. Half-dozen...

23 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/23/09 He traced his fingers along the curve of her body. The feel of flesh on his fingertips never ceased to amaze him. Every detail… the goosebumps that rose from her skin, each tiny hair, the shiver from the cold air the filtered through the curtains of the open window… every detail. What his eyes couldn’t find in the black of the lightless bedroom, his touch saw it all. “Mmmmm” her soft, pleasant sound floated into the air as his fingertips grazed lightly across her cheek, and rolled across her bottom lip. Instinctively, he pulled his hand back, holding it, briefly, suspended nowhere above their heads, then gently placed it on her thigh. “Did I wake you?” “Was never asleep.” She propped her head up on her left hand, smiling softly at him. “Something on your mind?” “No.” “So, yes.” A half chuckle escaped the lips of both. “Lot’s of stuff.” “Like what?” “Work stuff… stuff with Dr. Ward…us… mostly Dave.” “Us?” “Just kinda crazy. A blind date turned into a few trips to the movies, and the next thing ya know… I guess this is getting sorta serious. Just wasn’t necessarily expecting it.” “Moving kinda fast, isn’t it?” “I dunno… I guess I just wasn’t expecting that first blind date to turn into much. Wasn’t ready for it.” “Kaiser…” Ellie brushed her hair out of her face with her hand, stopped and smiled, whether he could see it or not. “Yeah?” “…nothin’.” “What?” “Nothin’.” “C’mon, Ellie” “What’s bothering you about your brother?” “Huh?” It took a moment for his brain to run through the list he had rattled off a moment before. “...oh… it’s… I just feel kinda bad about how that whole thing went down back before the holidays. I don’t think we’ve talked since.” “You should call him.” “I left a message on his answering machine the other day… I dunno, just feel like I let ‘im down.” “Don’t know why. You did everything he wanted you to do.” “I lied to him.” Kaiser rolled over onto his back and propped himself up against the headboard. Reaching over to the night stand, he felt around for his cell phone, eyes still not fully adjusted, and pressed the button on its side, lighting up the phone’s display screen to check the time. “I don’t blame you. He over-reacted. Wasn’t that kinda why you lied in the first place?” “Yeah.” The clock on his cell said it was nearly midnight, and immediately his mind fast-forwarded a few hours to the sound of the alarm shattering his slumber. He had an early flight to catch and he needed rest as much as he needed Ellie’s confidence. “Maybe I should try and get some sleep.” “You tried to make him understand, Kaiser.” She reached over and placed her hand on his chest. “You tried.” “Not hard enough.” ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Monday, February 23rd The Office of Dr. Matthew B. Ward “Mr. Vashaun… please, sir!” He stopped his march towards the door, finding some sympathy for her plea, but his voice rattled the pictures on her desk and sent ripples across the surface of her coffee, doing little to ease her nerves. “I want to talk to ’im, Nancy!” As Kaiser boomed, he pointed towards the door in the far corner of the room. “This is the second time he’s cancelled on me. I had an appointment scheduled for this time-block… so I know he’s not busy!” “Mr. Vashaun, please…” the receptionist held out her hands, her fingers trembling in the presence of the volatile man. “Dr. Ward will reschedule an appointment with you…” “Like hell he will!” “I can assure yo…” Her sentence dropped, shot dead by the sound of a door unlatching. Both individual’s heads snapped towards the source of the disturbance. “It’s alright, Nancy.” Standing in the doorway, Dr. Ward smiled softly at his receptionist, then turned his attention towards his patient. Staring for just a moment, the doctor turned sideways, clearing up a path into his office, and extended his hand into the room, “Ok… let’s talk.” ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The sound of the alarm pulsed his brain through his left eardrum, causing him to jerk his neck, pulling a muscle. Grimacing, he leaned over and flipped open his cell phone, blindly pushing a series of buttons till the noise stopped. With a long, slow breath, he rolled over and propped himself up on his right arm, looking over her body. As occasionally was her habit, she had pushed the sheets and comforter down, despite the fact that it was probably not more than sixty degrees in the room. The beginnings of a smile curled across his face, and with his left hand, he ran his fingertips across her bare back, down her spine, then following up her side and along her arm. He loved just to touch her. A few soft sighs provided the soundtrack to his movements, letting him know that she was happy, and as he saw a smile of her own form as she slept, he kissed her shoulder before pushing himself out of bed. Stumbling around on the floor, he headed for the bathroom, grabbing a towel from the linen closet. Gently as he could, he tried to shut the door, but it was no use. “Hmmm?” Her inquisitive moan was abrupt, finding her senses more quickly than he had, and she pushed herself off the mattress just a bit. ‘What are you doin?” “I’m sorry, sweetie.” “Why are you up so early?” “Gotta get going.” Now that she was awake, he trudged back across the room, leaning across his side of the bed, kissing her softly on the forehead “I’m heading into the office early this morning to take care of a few things.” “Ok,” she lowered herself back into bed as he headed back towards the shower. In the quietest tone, while she slipped back to sleep, she yelled across the room at him. “I love you, Matthew Branson.” He stopped in the doorway of the bathroom and turned his gaze to her figure on the bed. “I love you too, Mary.” ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Monday, February 23rd The Office of Dr. Matthew B. Ward “So?.” Dr. Ward closed the door behind Kaiser, then made his way across the office. Turning and leaning against the edge of his desk, he folded his arms across his chest and waited. “Aren’t ya gonna sit down in your chair?” Kaiser pointed to the cushioned seat across from the doctor’s leather sofa. “I don’t plan on this taking long. You don’t even need to bother making yourself comfortable on the couch. This isn’t a session, Kaiser.” “It should be a session. It was supposed to be one… and I think I deserve a fuckin’ explanation!” “You know exactly what this is all about.” “That’s bullshit! Just because I’m not all buddy-buddy with your past friends in the business, doesn’t mean that…” “You assaulted her! I’m not asking you to hang out with Lindz… to go and have a beer with her every week after the show, Kaiser. You attacked her knowing full-well that she was one of my closest friends in the industry.” “Don’t you have a wife? Maybe oughta drop the hard-on for Troy, dontcha think?” “It’s called respect!” “Respect? How ‘bout me? So I did something that offended you… and you kick me to the curb? You’re just like my fuckin’ brother.” “Kaiser…” Dr. Ward pushed himself away from the desk, taking a step towards his protégée. “…I never said I was dropping you as my patient.” “What do you call canceling back to back appointments?” “If you still want my assistance, I’m still willing to help you. But not until you’re at a place where you’re going to listen to what I say and…” “You told me to do this shit!” “I told you to make a name for yourself! Not assault one of…” “Gimme’ a break! This coming from the guy who made his first big splash in PRIME by decking his old friend in the face with a steel chair. I did the same thing you did… for the same reasons.” “Following my mistakes isn’t what I had in mind when I agreed to help you.” “Wish ya would have made that a little clearer from the start.” “I'm still willing to see this thing through, but you’ve got to listen to what I’m trying to tell you.” “I tried.” “Not hard enough.” Kaiser soaked in the words, not uttering a response. For moment, he stood still, shoes stapled to the floor, and he began to bite his bottom lip, practically chewing his own flesh. Without any last remark, he walked out of the office, leaving the door open behind him.
