The nurses in the ward share similarly grave expressions, one watching the other while their sight stays planted to the floor. "Unbelievable," she says. Until now, he never even noticed the clarity of the sky and their notable absence of clouds. Despite nearing the end of their journey, the two are far enough outside of the city that their view is hardly obstructed by Phoenix' polluted atmosphere. Shad can't help but find himself irked when Adam reacts like this. It reminds him of arguing with a woman, his mother no-less, and the futility in trying to make a valid point. He can't make out anything specifically aside from the tricks that shadows love playing on the mind, unable to tell if anything really exists in the darkness. Then he hears the distinct struggle of a man and razor-wire, accompanied by the shuffling of a chain-link fence. "Hello? HELLO? DAMMIT." Shad cries as he slams his cell-phone to the ground, obviously fed up with T-Mobile's less than stellar reception. Even in emergency situations does the provider's reliability manage to fall through. With his last remnant of hope shattered into dozens of disappointing pieces on the dirt, Shad seems appears distraught to Adam who watches from the depths of a tree. At first, he wants to call out to his friend in an effort to help him, but his instincts cause him to hesitate. Adam thinks about that hesitation for a moment, and decides to remain quiet. After all, his chances of surviving increase if there's some other idiot wandering around out there—just waiting to be eaten by what can only be described as a vampire. 'Yeah, Adam thinks, Let the bastard die.' The prospect of Shad's sacrifice for his survival is appetizing- enough to make Adam's mouth water. He watches his friend with glazed over eyes, an almost blood-thirsty quality about him as he waits for the creature to descend on Shad and feast on his heart. He becomes engulfed in his anticipation to the point that he himself doesn't notice the heavy breathing taking place right behind him. That is, until he feels the warmth, moistness of the air grazing the back of his neck, causing the hairs to stand straight. The air forces its way out of his chest as fear suddenly swells up inside of him. Adam cannot even retain a grip on the branches he sits in. He's taken off guard to the point that he cannot regain his footing in time as he plummets to the ground, landing face first with an open-mouth filled with dirt. Shad leaps back with a startle until he sees Adam through the dust cloud, a sense of relief visible on his face. "ADAM! THERE YOU ARE!" Before Shad can attempt to reunite with his friend, the creature drops from inside the tree and onto the back of Adam. Its size is comparable to that of a small child, only the color of its skin and shape of its face prove it definitely is not. The odd looking creature, complete with pointed ears and teeth, hairless skin, and familiar beady yellow eyes renders Shad captivated, finally catching a clear view up close. Only the creature doesn't allow the boy to remain stunned for long. The instant it lands atop Adam's back do its skinny arms begin flailing—the pointed tips of the fingers digging into his flesh through the clothes that cover him. In an instant, his shirt is torn to shreds and his back is leaking blood while his cries fall on the deaf, cold ears of the merciless Chupacabra. "GHYAHHHKKKKK!" Adam painfully screams, the beast growing excited with every painful call that emanates from his mouth and inciting even more lashes to his back. Shad watches horrified; frozen in fear as his friend continue to be tormented. He sees the fallen hand-rake that Adam dropped in the scuffle, causing him to look to the house and find an even larger tool propped against the walls. He quickly runs and grabs a shovel before returning to the scene where Adam's being attacked. Taking a stance behind the feasting creature, Shad takes the stance he learned from Tee Ball all those years ago. With a swing that'd do well in making Griffey, Jr. proud, Shad drives the end of the shovel right into the dome of the beast. The impact doesn't send the small creature flying off of his friend, however. No, because apparently the little goat-sucker is more dense than he appears to be. In his hands, it feels like he just attempted to hit a street-light with a baseball bat as hard as he could. The jolt it gives forces the shovel out of his grip, and the Chupacabra only pivots in his position to gain a better view of his attacker. "KYRAAAAAAAAAAAAK!" it hisses from behind its crooked, barbed teeth with a lizard-like tongue swirling in its mouth. It takes every nerve in Shad's body not to piss himself at this moment in time, but he remains strong and keeps his eyes looked on the creature. Instead of leaping at him and tearing his face off, like Shad thought it would, the Chupacabra leaps from its position on Adam's back and back into the tree above. Seizing the moment as a chance to escape, Shad grips Adam's hand hanging loosely and pulls him to his feet, struggling to keep him moving as Adam remains almost lifeless. "Come on, Adam! You gotta help me out here!" "Shad? Are we reenacting scenes from The Bodyguard? You know that's my favorite movie..." "Adam, I need you to focus," Shad attempts to keep Adam moving, but his lack of energy proves to be more of an obstacle than he can manage. The two stumble to the ground, all the while Shad fearfully glances behind them and into the tree, making sure the Chupacabra stays away from them. Upon impact, Adam groggily speaks out, "I just want you to know, Shad, that even though we always make fun of you and make you the bitch of every situation that I've always respect you! Through it all..." He pounds on his chest twice with a fist. "Respect. And I just wanna say I'm sorry for being a dick to you." "That's fine, you can apologize later. Right now, you need to help me get the fuck out of here, Adam! COME ON, GET ON YOUR FEET!" Using the remnants of his strength, Shat fights through the pain in his hip and powers Adam to his feet, again jogging down the dirt path and back towards the freeway. Another quick glance behind them allows Shad to breathe in relief, his sight still absent of the creature that stalks them. "Think you'll be able to hold your thumb out?" Adam asks, causing Shad to turn towards him in question. His concerns are answered with a sight in the distance; the approaching pair of head-lights that navigate a dirt path off-shooting from the freeway and heading right towards them. "THANK GOD, ADAM! WE MIGHT MAKE IT OUT ALIVE!" The two painfully begin sprinting towards the oncoming vehicle, kicking up clouds of dust in its wake. They almost begin crying in joyous relief before they even reach the it, until the harsh cackle of the Mexican creature behind them anchors them back into the situation. Shad turns back to face the tree, finding the Chupacabra perched on the ground right in front of it. The creature sheds its fangs, releases a harsh howl, then begins galloping at full speed towards the boys. Shad turns back towards the car, a tear beginning to squeeze out his eye as he painfully forces his body to the limit. He runs as fast as he can but the hindrance of Adam on his shoulder adds difficulty. "Keep running, Adam." The pain in Adam's back begins to manifest, now. An ache in his spine that wasn't there before now throbs prominently, agitating him more and more with each pounding step. He knows he can't make it much longer, not even long enough to make it to the vehicle heading towards them. "Keep running, Adam," Shad says but it's easier said than done. He knows he's about to fall. He doesn't even think of his friend saving his life moments earlier. He doesn't even think of the fact that it was his friend who decided to go pick him up from Flagstaff and give him a ride to Phoenix when his original plans fell through. The only thought that lingers in Adam's mind is, 'Better him than me.' "Come on. Keep running, Adam," Shad continues to encourage his friend but they are useless. Adam's mind holds elsewhere. The impending grunts of the Chupacabra aproach them quickly, gaining on their position with every passing moment. At the rate they run, it would reach them before they even make it to the vehicle. "Keep running, Adam." Only Shad can't finish his sentence, this time. Adam elbows his friend in the face and drives his heel in front of Shad's legs, causing him to twist and crash to the dirt ground as Adam stumbles to safety. "AAAADAAAAAM!" Shad cries as the Chupacabra leaps atop of him, but Adam doesn't stop. He powers through the pain that permeates through his spite and continues to run, trying his best to ignore the pain-filled cries that emanate from behind him. The desperation in Shad's voice never causes Adam to think twice about turning back. Even as he pleads for his friend's help, begging for his assistance in ceasing the beast's vicious attack, Adam manages to keep his head turn. Never once does he regret his actions, leaving his friend behind to die, but that doesn't mean he's eager to watch. After just dooming a man to die, he's doing everything in his power to get away safely. Anything to escape the fate of hell, even if it's just one more day. "AAAADAAAAAAAAAAM!" Shad continues to call out, his voice distinctly growing weaker and weaker as the animal continues to eat his living body. Adam reaches the path of the headlights, now fixed right in front of him. He stumbles to his knees, waving in front of the lights as they rapidly approach him. Finally, he sighs in relief, safety has come. The truck will soon stop and pick him up, and he will be taken to a hospital where they can treat his wounds and give him some proper drugs. Then he will sell his story to Ripley's Believe it or Not and make millions of dollars off of a book deal. My Battle with the Goat Sucker, he'd call it. All of these unfounded thoughts of limitless joy flood Adam's mind as he's caught entranced in the oncoming headlights of the vehicle. The approach of safety never looked so beautiful. Only, the truck never stops.
