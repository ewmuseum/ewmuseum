Date: Ultraviolence To have absolutely no control is a strange sensation, as though you are a viewer in your own body. You are aware of everything that the body is doing and every feeling of guilt and regret but are totally powerless to do anything. Its a true feeling of helplessness knowing that once its all over you will be held accountable for everything. Yet you truly did nothing. It was a sensation that Claire had been feeling for sometime. Waking up in the morning in a warm sweat as though the previous day had been a long terrifying dream. However something about it had felt far too real to be a dream. Ever since had been in the meeting with Steve things had felt different, yet for all her efforts she could not recollect what had happened at all. It wad as though that entire day of her life had been systematically picked through and key information been removed. It was unnerving to say the least. Still that was the least of her problems she needed to get back into work. After her meeting with Steve she realised that she had been increasingly sloppy and slap dash when it came to Chris. In the last few weeks he had become distant and his performances were less than lacklustre. The missing component, or so she would have herself believe was her. Hell history itself indicated as such. When looking back at Meat's efforts in Just Wrestling she had been by his side the entire time, providing motivation and support for the promising young superstar.. Yet without her he had looked abysmal and uninspired. That had to be it. It couldn't have anything to do with the experimental drugs that were being pumped into his system on a daily basis. Which in turn provided Claire with her next problem. The company had noted Meat's behaviours and deemed them to be a risk to the programme, as a result a new serum had been designed to not only provide the enhancing effects of the original, but to also render the recipient almost totally susceptible to suggestion. If it wasn't too much like science fiction it could almost be compared to the super soldier serum from Captain America, but everyone knows that stuff like that doesn't exist, not to that extent anyway. When she had arrived at the hotel she had hoped that everything would go smoothly and at Ultra Violence the Meat from days of old would rise above the other newcomers and cement himself as one of the most brutal combatants in PRIME. However the second that she entered his room. That image was completely blown out of the water.. The man that stood before her was huge, a man who was essentially all muscle. He was far bigger than she could remember him being and yet for all the size that surrounded him, she couldn't help but think how weak he looked. The man who had once held her in his arms and made her feel like nothing would be able to harm her ever again, simply looked like a piece of Meat hanging from a butchers window. Looked quite impressive but very little substance to it. Also who knows exactly where that meat has been? It also didn't help that she had been sat on the edge of the bed for twenty minutes and not a word other than a grunt of acknowledgement had been shared between them. “Chris we need to talk. It's been too long.” The words left Claire's mouth as though she was about to break up with the man. Their relationship had always been on a variable of up's and down's,WE a mixture of love and hate. They were currently on a massive down swing which was apparent by the sneer with Chris shot out in her direction. If truth be told things had been bad for a long time. She knew why she was here to get things back to the way they were, to make Meat a dominant force in PRIME and the industry as a whole and what better way than decimating three other men in the ring and laying claim to the Intense Championship, which was currently around the waist of the murder show Hessian. It was a double edged sword getting the win at Ultra violence, but it was something that was expected of Claire and Meat. If she was going to go through with this, it had to be now. “Sorry I have to get ready for my flight to Minnesota.” The message although short and abrupt was perfectly clear. He had no interest in anything that the woman had to say. He was a man who was tired and broken and was looking forwards to just getting back to a normal life. He allowed himself to glance at Claire his bloodshot eyes and drawn face a far cry from the young energetic man he used to be. “I just think we need to catch up Chris, for the last few months things have been different between us.” Once again Claire spoke as though there was still a relationship to save between the two of them. Her methods may be questionable, her options though were extremely limited. What was being asked of her today was not something which she would have been able to ask Chris at the best of times. At least with the original serum they maintained a certain regulated level of control, with what she was about to do. He would be little more than a puppet. “No we really don't Claire. Simply because this is the end for me. This all this is a sham isn't it. My career, you my life. Do you really think I don't know what's happening to me? Do you really think that I didn't notice the difference in myself I'm not stupid Claire. I also know everything about you.” For a second the world stood still. For months the two of them had been dancing around each other, seconding guessing the others motives. Neither of them showing their cards at all and simply playing the game. However the level of physical and psychological punishment and cost for Chris had become far too much. Aside from the fact that he'd nearly died which some may consider a major occupational hazard, he had started to become lost in himself, isolated within the very depths of his soul. Everyone around him had suffered, those who he loved dearly and with all his heart had bore the brunt of his moods and elusiveness. Things were going to change and he was going to take his aggression and anger out on those who truly deserved it. Starting with the woman who had been the bane of his existence, since she fell into his life. The woman who he had been trying to finger since day one but covered her tracks too well. He knew what was happening and what she was involved in, but had no way of proving it. Not even he would be a prime example, blood tests came back as inconclusive and although all of the symptoms were there, there was no actual proof to back up the accusations. The only possible course of action now was calling her bluff. “About me?” Claire's voice was calm and controlled. A little too controlled though as though if she let slip her façade for just a moment the entire truth of the world would be revealed in her voice. What she didn't have control of however was her face, which had begun to glow a slight red crimson. She was aware of this and quickly lowered her head, not so quick though that Chris hadn't noticed and allowed himself a wry smile. “Don't play dumb Claire. We may as well get everything out in the open right now and go our separate ways, after all we have allegedly shared the least we owe each other is one modicum of honesty so spill it. Who the hell are you?” Whilst the smile remained on his face the tone of his words was far more serious, culminating in his final question to Claire almost being spat with venom. With every word he had advanced a little bit further towards Claire who was well aware and with every step he took forwards, matched in equal measure with one back. “You know all there is to know abo...... “DON'T FUCKING LIE TO ME!” It was a primal scream which came from the very pits of Meat's stomach. The months of pain and regret were beginning to boil away a fact which was evident as he slammed his fist into the wall, inches to the side of Claire's head. To her credit she didn't flinch nor scream. Her heart however was racing and her eyes were bulging with fear. As Chris glared down at her scanning every inch of her face begging her to break the act so that they could get on with their lives, Claire's heart was racing and for the first time in the years of knowing and working with Chris. She was scared of him. What was she to do though? If she told the truth and blew everything that she had been working towards, Steve would kill her., there was no doubt about that. What about Meat though? The Meat that she knew originally would not harm a fly. Now though she did not even know him, not really. The drugs had changed him into something irrational and more importantly unpredictable. The silence between them was beginning to fray as Claire decided on her next move. “I really don't know...” Claire's word and thought process were rapidly stopped when Chris lurched forwards and grabbed her tightly, spinning her towards the wall he had backed her up against, a firm forearm kept her body in place. Regardless of how much she struggled it was to no avail, his other hand grabbed a handful of Claire's hair pulling her neck back not so that it would cause pain, more a tutorial aid. If she didn't learn that this wasn't a game then things would take a rapid turn for the worse. “So help me god Claire I'm not a violent man, but the shit you have been pumping into me has turned me into one and unless you are brutally honest with me in the next five seconds I'm going to put your face through the wall.” There was no smile this time just pure rage. He felt the muscles in his body tensing up as wave after wave of anger swept over him an overwhelming sensation of losing control was beginning to strike him. And a very small part of his being liked it. “You wouldn't.....” When your face is millimetres away from a wall and you have no way of getting out of the predicament unless the person holding you there deems it applicable. Shooting off your mouth is not the smartest of ideas, but when you are scared you panic and say things that you normally wouldn't. The cool, calm and sophisticated woman that Claire personified to everyone else was rapidly being replaced by a frightened little child, who was minutes away from calling for her daddy. “Claire....do you really want to test me?” Chris waited patiently for an answer which never came. A bitter sigh of disappointment leaked from Chris' lips as in one swift motion he yanked back Claire's hair further formed a palm on the back of her head a thrust it forwards towards the wall. A shrill scream from Claire pierced Meat's ears, her eyes had instinctively closed and waited for the inevitable impact that for some reason never came. Instead her shaking body was turned around as she felt a large hand grab her face tightly. She tried to turn away but Meat, being considerably stronger than half of PRIME's roster, let alone a small woman didn't allow it and held her firmly in place. Leaning forwards he was almost snarling his hot breath could be felt on Claire's cheek who was still shaking in terror. “TELL ME THE TRUTH!” It was the final warning, there would be no more chances. “OK!” A shrill cry of defeat came from the psychologically broken Claire and with that Chris released his grip. Red imprinted finger marks were already beginning to form on her face. Claire looked up at Meat who had taken a few steps back and allowed her to cross and take a seat on the hotel bed and take a few moments to compose herself. A hundred thoughts were running through her mind, most notably could she make a dash for the door and live to tell the tale. It was not going to happen though she had been broken, all that was left was for her to talk. “So I will ask you again....who are you?” Chris' arms were firmly crossed against his chest as he paced up and down in front of her.. Already he was starting to feel bad about what had occurred but he was on forefront now and had to carry his advantage while it still carried some weight. “To be honest I'm not entirely sure any more.” “Don't play cute with me!” “I'm not! When you first came to me, it wasn't a meeting of chance. Everything had been pre-arranged. You were deemed the right height and build and that was it. You were the perfect specimen for the trial.” “The crap you have been pumping into me?” It was interesting for Chris to actually stand and hear everything that had happened and to know that it was the truth. For months he had been wondering about the intricacies of everything. Had he simply been a victim of circumstance or had it been something far more sinister. It was also in a strange way a relief. For the last few months his mind had been wondering and he had felt fifteen years older. He had grown to not trust his own thoughts and instincts so to have his doubts and worries clarified and given weight. The truth was finally setting him free. “Technically it was in powder form, I was slipping it into your food, as I'm sure you were aware by the end.” “Why me?” “As I told you, you were the right height and build, they were the only determining factors. It was my job to make sure you were put into a physical environment where we could see the changes that the drug implemented, based on your college wrestling background the story kinda wrote itself.” “....the drugs nearly killed me.” It was the one thing that had got to Meat in the entire process of everything. Sure the psychological element has fucked him over far than he would care to suggest. However that sensation of life slipping away and the world becoming a dark void was an image that haunted him in his nightmares. “It was at that point I wanted to stop I wanted to get away from everything, but the people I work for......you don't understand Chris they'd have killed me if I opposed them....they ….he did things to me.” It was almost a cry for help. Deep down Claire was wishing that he would ask the follow up question of what was wrong, what had they done to her. She knew it wasn't coming though. “And you did things to me Claire. You drugged me. Even after the heart attack you kept giving me that shit. You didn't care about me or what happened to me. You know what though, I was stronger than your shit. Whatever you were pumping into me Claire. It's over! After Ultra Violence I'm done I'm going back home to my wife and child. I've spoken to Lisa Tyler., she has given me the green light to leave.” Claire analysed the words for any trace of a lie but there was none forthcoming. In Meat's mind he was done. If he was going to form a career in wrestling he would have to have a fresh start. His wins in Just and PRIME were all tainted, he hadn't beaten his opponents. It was whatever was coursing through his veins. Of course he couldn't come forwards and admit to it as it would ruin his chance of work in the future. So for now he would just take his leave and train to become something better., cleanly. “You didn't tell her anything did you?” “Without proof? What do you take me for Claire. I'll give you credit. You were very good at covering your tracks, that was something I always wondered, exactly how you did that. Still I guess we'll never know now.” “So you just quit and gave no reason.” “Claire, you are safe there will be no investigations or anything.” “If you quit....you will have killed me Chris.” Her eyes burned a fierce sincerity “....Then I'll come visit your grave.” As did Chris'. Shaking his head at the mess of woman on his bed he couldn't believe he had ever been entangled with her. Even if it was all part of a grander scheme to catch her out. The fact that he had ever allowed himself to become sexually involved in a woman so dark and callous. “What like you will visit the grave of your child.” It was a strange and eerie silence that filled the room like an all encompassing fog. It hung there waiting to dissipate, but it never came and eventually had to be approached by Chris. A line like that did not go with out being responded to. “You're sick Claire what sort of person would even joke about that.” “Who says I'm joking Chris...did you not stop to think why she was calling constantly the endless voice messages that you ignored. For someone that professes his love for her. You sure have a funny way of showing it. “You're lying.” Chris could feel his chest beginning to tighten up as the all too real possibility of something having happened to his son was beginning to overwhelm him. His palms became more and more sweaty and could almost feel himself welling up. Yet through it all a low under brimming current of anger was beginning to boil again, but this time the safe guards were well and truly off. If what she was saying was the truth. “Look into my eyes Chris. The same eyes that you gazed into as you had sex with me, you know when I'm lying so tell me....am I lying now. Chris took the moment to do exactly that and looked deep into Claire's eyes. Trying to ignore the mental image that she had tried to imprint on his mind. A smirk of power slipped onto Claire's face and in a complete and total instant. Chris knew the truth. His son was dead. And in tat moment of realisation, he snapped and every ounce of restraint that had previously been a part of him. Every sinew and fibre of his being that told him what was right and wrong went out the window. He snapped forwards rapidly and wrapped a hand around Claire's throat, her smile quickly being wiped off her face. Hoisting her up Chris held her tightly, he feet flailing above the ground kicking Chris with little to no effect. “You bitch. WHAT DID YOU DO!” Even if Claire wanted to talk, she couldn't and all that she could muster was a low emanating gurgle and wheeze as she struggle frantically to get any iota of oxygen into her blood stream. Chris on the other hand was unrelenting and continued to scream in her face. “WHAT DID YOU DO TO MY SON!” Chris proceeded to throw Claire to the floor and threw an angry fist forwards which shattered through the wall, Claire screaming as it occurred fearing that the next blow would be coming her way. Chris pulled his fist out of the wall and silently watched a few trickles of blood seep down his hand and although his heart was broken and his heart crushed to the extent that every gasp of air felt like a blow torch to his lungs. In his hand he felt nothing. As tears seeped down his face, he turned an angry eye towards Claire who was already shimmying towards the exit. However stopped rapidly in her tracks as she saw Meat beginning to walk towards her, looming menacingly.” “The company I work for got wind that something was wrong. They could see that you were beginning to fight back so as a result. I was sent to see your wife to tell her everything about us. Every sordid thing that we did. I DIDN'T DO IT THOUGH. I COULDN'T” Claire screamed frantically as Chris lunged towards her once again grabbing her round the throat and lifting her up, pressing her against the wall. “YOU'RE LYING AGAIN.” “When I saw your wife in the state she was in, I couldn't do it. I was sent there to destroy your family. When I got there I didn't need to do anything. It had already been done. She needs you Chris.” Although the words were lacking in empathy or even the most remote portion of sympathy, the message behind them was true. Chris quickly dropped Claire and reached for his bag and made a beeline for the door. The only thing that was going through his head right now was his wife and how much he wanted to be with her. How had he been so stupid and not realised what was happening? All those calls, of course they meant something. She wasn't just being irritable she needed him. It is a long way to go that you hate yourself but at this moment in time. Chris wanted more than anything to take back everything that had happened. He wanted to cut out his heart and show it to her, just to show her that it was broken too. He hated himself that much. It would not help anyone though all he knew was that right now everything else was second fiddle. PRIME would have to wait. Ultra violence was simply not going to happen, he needed to be with his wife and to try and make everything better. He knew he couldn't make it right, but better at least would be a start. “I'm sorry Chris.” “What for?” “I'm just sorry.” Chris shook his head in disgust and opened up the hotel room door. As he did so he was face to face with a man wearing a simple black suit, his name of course with Steve, Claire's boss. Meat's eyes widened with realisation as he turned his head towards Claire. The pieces of the puzzle suddenly fitting together for easier than they previously had done. However in that once instance of head turning he felt a solid blow around the back of his head. Raising a hand tentatively he felt a damp wound as his eyes became glazed over.. Fighting against unconsciousness, the last thing that Meat saw was Steve standing over him cosh in hand grinning. ~@~@~@~@~ As Meat awoke he stretched aggressively, he felt as though he had been asleep for days. Also a pounding headache which was burning through his temples as though he had been on a drinking session with Oliver Reed. As he tried to cast his mind back to what had occurred the night before he was able to determine nothing as though his memory had been wiped, however anyone who has had a heavy drinking session has often felt the same. Trying to shake the feeling of grogginess he rose to his feet and stumbled out of the bedroom and came face to face with Claire who was pouring a glass of orange juice into an iced glass. “Morning Chris, I ordered you breakfast I hope that's alright. Want you big and strong for the big fight now don't we.” “Sure do Claire. So any ideas on how to go about this thing.?” That was the thing with Claire, she was always reliable and could be turned to in a moment of crisis. Truth be told he was somewhat worried about Ultra Violence and having to face off with three other men. Still with Claire at his side nothing could go wrong. Whilst getting lost in his own thoughts he realised that Claire had just been staring at him in silence. Something wasn't quite right about her, as though she was scared or had been shaken up. He had never seen her like that before and it was somewhat disconcerting. She was always so powerful and strong. Such an anchor whenever he needed her to be one. “What's wrong, you're just sort of staring at me like a baby staring at a cheeseburger. I don't have anything on my face do I?” “No...it just feels like I haven't seen you in a while that's all.”
