Well its not brillent but its a start.

14 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/14/08 The locker-room smells of faint urine, strong perspiration, male ego, and body oil all mixed equally. Doesn’t matter if it’s Boise or Moscow they all smell the same and after years of it every time I walk into a locker room it hits me with memories like freshly cut grass or the smell of your childhood bedroom. Some faces are the same, most are different and so young, and mine is familiar but now a bit faded by time. I was retired but I wasn’t happy, one can’t be happy without an identity. What I am is a wrestler and that is what I will die as. Well that and a historical love machine but that’s a tale for another time. One day back, one match, and look at me Ma I’m one half of the tag team champions. About fucking time. I know I can’t do it by myself any longer and that’s the struggle we face as we age. I’m not a guy who likes asking for help. As a matter of fact if you gave me the option of walking bare foot through a puddle of vomit versus asking for help my feet would be pretty nasty with chunks of corn sticking to them. But my personal struggles are not why you are reading my blog. You want to hear about the frat parties, the drugs, the women, and the shenanigans. Only two people hate shenanigans; prudes and protestants but they are the ones missing the fun so fuck’em. Well the kids threw me a party and let’s just say I partied like it was ’89 again and I loved every minute of it. I was dressed in an Elvis jumpsuit after banging some broad dressed as Jackie O (long story). Homeless “sweet” Pete my driver and mentor took me back to the frat house and was half in the bag when I walked in on a loud party full of kids dressed with popped collars and smooth skin. Williams: Shit the house is being overtaken by Metrosexuals!! Somebody get me a 1968 Chicago fire hose we need to rid this place of boys in perfume. Homeless Pete: I think it’s a party!?! Williams: But what the hell is that sound with all that pounding and repetition. Homeless Pete: Music. Williams: Oh that’s what they are calling music these days. Should we find the source and heal its sickness with some Credence or Waylon Jennings? Homeless Pete: I think the natives might revolt. Williams: I’ll take my chances. Chances were something I was used to, and like a fine women or a great drug I never got tired of taking them. It must have been an odd site on the flip side as all these young kids watching and pointing as a 6’6” fifty year old in an Elvis jumpsuit, cowboy hat, and homeless guy walks in their house, my house. Frat Boy: Holy shit Elvis is here, I thought you were dead. Williams: Funny I thought you were a women and I was just fantasizing about having rough intercourse with you. What the hell is a matter with you? I mean you got earrings, fluffy poofy hair, and pristine gym shoes am I in the right house? Frat Boy: I’m about looking good for the ladies. Williams: Thank god it’s socially acceptable for the ladies to experiment with other ladies hoss or you’d be shit out of luck. Frat Boy: Man, fuck you. Williams: I bet you would. Say stranger where does one get a drink in this place? Frat Boy: The kitchen’s in the back. Williams: To the back Mr. Pete, full throttle ahead. Sweet Pete: You think the kids today still smoke joints? Williams: By the smell of the room I’d guess some things never change. Sweet Pete: Far out. Pete like a blood hound wondered off looking for the source of the smell. I was tired and just wanted to sleep but the damn party was just getting started and decadence was on the horizon. I want to sleep, I want to give up, I want to just be warm and cozy without a care in the world or a match on the schedule. Not sure if its depression, age, or the season but I need to hibernate and wouldn’t care if I never woke up. No time to go emo it’s time to party the Malice Man is back and awake. Should have left sleeping dogs lying… Should have stayed out of the spot light... Should have just faded away... But all I am is a wrestler and the ring calls. Welcome home hoss. Time to party. =w=
