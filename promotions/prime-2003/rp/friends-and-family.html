Friends and Family

Date: ReV 176 THOOM!! Tyler Rayne’s right leg came up from the floor with unbelievable speed, rounding in an arc toward its target. The knee snapped forward, leg reaching full extension just before the moment of impact. His shin blasted against the heavy bag with shocking force. The reverberations of impact were enough to rattle the unshakeable Katterina Wylde. Her raven hair fluttered and porcelain brow furrowed with the strain of keeping the bag in check. Even with the Dark Angel’s sure grip upon the bag, chains rattled above, threatening to break loose from their moorings. Despite the ferocity of the attack, his foot landed lightly on the floor. It slid a few inches more upon contact, the counterbalance to the pendulum that was his left fist. As the right side of his body twisted back upon the recently placed foot, the left lunged forward, a vicious punch striking the bag from a complete opposite angle as the kick. Katterina planted her heels hard into the floor to keep steady. As his left arm retreated, the right one flashed forward. He took a step toward the bag to throw as much of his body weight as possible into the forearm attack. Katterina was shaken loose. The following blow, a jumping knee strike that rocked the bag straight back into her lithe and unbalanced body was enough to knock his poor sparring partner to her ass. "He seems a bit more… um… aggressive than usual." Drake Roberts watched from across the sparring room floor. His feet propped up in a chair just previously occupied by the mysterious assassin Milena. Tussled hair fell into icy eyes that wandered from the training session to the Asian assassin herself. She leaned against a banister, dark hair wound tightly against the back of her head. Sharp eyes lingered upon the aftermath. Tyler extended his hand to help the fallen Angel. Katterina gave him an earful for refusing to pull back. Drake waited a short moment, content with the opportunity to appreciate the lovely curves of Milena’s finely sculpted body. When the young man realized his comment would go unnoticed, however, he pushed himself from the chair, tossing the last peanut into his mouth before stepping up to lean against the banister next to Milena. He turned to smile, ready to ask the question again. Her eyes never left Katterina. "Seriously though, what’s his deal? He really that worked up about this tournament?" "It is not the tournament that concerns him. There is… something else." "Oh." Conversation with Milena had always proven a gamble. There were times when she was open. Receptive. Sometimes even flirtatious. Or perhaps he just hoped. Then there were times like these, when the woman was content to study the proceedings of those around her. She absorbed details and filed the most mundane of circumstances without comment or concern. Ever watchful. Ever vigilant. It was admirable, and, he assumed, an unfortunate side effect of being… whatever the hell it was she claimed to be. Her comings and goings were never more explained than those of Katterina, or Tyler himself for that matter. The whole lot of them were up to some shady business. Sharing secrets and playing in shadows. All well and good, if that’s what you’re into, but not exactly conducive to engaging conversation. "So he flew all the way to Japan to kick the shit out of Katt? Couldn’t they have just, I dunno, done that at home?" "The training? Yes. They could have done that anywhere. He did not come to Japan for physical training." Across the room, Katterina is reluctantly steadying herself against the bag. When the first blow comes, it doesn’t sound any weaker than the last. THOOM!! "Could’ve fooled me. So what did he come here for?" "Spiritual release." "This is Tyler Rayne we’re talkin’ about here. Doesn’t he just find a hottie from one of those wrestling shows to get his ‘spiritual release’?" Drake cracks a smile along with the joke. Milena turns to face him now, though not with the amused expression he had been hoping for. "He does have a reputation for such things, yes. Tyler comes here when he’s looking for peace. This is where he found his first wrestling gig. Where he first found love. This club was the first place he ever truly felt at home. In many ways, this is the beginning of Tyler Rayne. He still holds a connection to that fledgling innocence, in spite of the things that have happened since." "You mean like that girl dying? His fiancée?" "Yes. Like that." "Is that what’s got him so upset?" "No. It’s simply… that time of year. He always gets like this around this time." "So, what… he’s got a thing against Halloween?" "In point of fact, he rather enjoys Halloween. Any excuse to party… it’s November. His sister’s birthday is in November." "I didn’t know he had a sister." "You wouldn’t. He rarely talks about her." "They don’t get along?" "She’s dead." The young man falls silent. A lump in his throat prevents any sort of vocal response. The memory of his parents’ death is still fresh. Though months removed, sometimes it even still stings. "He would not care much for us discussing this." "Yeah. Sorry. I just… just wanted something to take my mind off the trial." "Do not fret upon it. Tomorrow it will all be over. The people who killed Summer will be sentenced. Slavers will be taken off the streets and justice will be served. Thanks to you. And your bravery. Never forget your part in this, Drake. Without you, this never could have happened. These men could never have been prosecuted. Many lives will be saved by these convictions." "That’s if they get convicted." "Have faith. The wicked always find retribution." "Easy for you to say. You go out and find your own retribution." "You have no idea what I do. You should hope you’ll never need to, either." "Why’s that?" "You are lucky in some ways, Drake. This trial is your chance at retribution. To see justice served. The system does not shine so willingly upon all of us. Sometimes justice is a long time coming. Too long, for some." Those eagle eyes narrow again toward the sparring match. The heavy bag forgotten, Katterina and Tyler now test each other hand-to-hand. Drake cannot find a proper response to her last comment. He also finds it supremely intriguing that, for once, her focus is not on Katterina at all, but intently fixed upon Tyler Rayne. ---- Katterina’s fingers slapped sharply against the damp skin on his forearm. He felt the tension of her contorted body beneath him. The struggle of her throat to find air. The fight was over. The Dark Angel lost. Tyler released the choke and stepped away. Katterina lay gasping on the floor for a handful of seconds while her lungs refilled with vital air. "Fuck’s sake, Ty. Ease up, will ya?" "You’re a big girl. You can take it." He plopped down on the floor next to her, both of them leaning sweat soaked bodies against the wall. Wet hair matted against the back of his head as he lulled to the side. His features lined with genuine regret. She refused to lower that dark scowl. "Sorry. I don’t know what’s gotten into me lately." "Besides an extra dose of asshole?" "Yes. Besides that." "You have been rather uptight lately. Lindsay still not giving it up yet?" "That’s not.. fuck you." "I’m thinkin’ you might try, she doesn’t cave soon. This has gotta be the longest you’ve gone without sex since, when?, your first pube?" "Funny." "I’m a riot. You could always just go for Brooks. You know she’s been begging for it." "You want back in that chokehold again?" "Yeah, yeah. You’re in love with Troy. I’ve heard it. Don’t get it. But I’ve heard it. You mind if I take a crack at Brooks then?" "Go for it. She’s probably hitting that experimental phase about now." "Mm. Fresh blood." "You sound like your father." "Ty…" "Sorry. It’s just… it’ll be four years. Four years since he killed Rena and I still haven’t… I don’t even know where the fuck to find him." "So that’s what’s got your balls in a twist." "You could be helping me, you know." "Helping you kill my father? Right, Ty. Sure. I’ll get right on that." "He’s a murderer." "He’s still my father." "So that justifies-" "No. It doesn’t justify shit. That’s if he’s even done any of the things you say-" THUMP!! His fist slammed quickly and violently into the wall. It shuddered beneath his fury. Tiny reverberations shaking their way down to Katterina’s spine. She stiffened at his sudden outburst, turned to face… and found herself staring quite unintentionally at his knee. The unexpected reaction had completely masked the fact that he’d stood up at all. Katterina pushed herself to her feet, finding rather disappointingly that she was already a half dozen steps behind. "Ty. Ty, please. Just listen…" "No. You listen." His finger was centimeters from her nose. His eyes pulsed with intensity. Passion. Fury. His scarred chest heaved and when he spoke, the voice came out in a gravelly, deathly tone. "Seven pints of blood. Seven fucking pints. Exactly. That’s what she lost, Katt. Tony found ‘er in the bathtub. Slit wrists. No water. Just an empty tub with her slit wrists and seven pints of her own blood to bathe in. You think that’s a fucking coincidence? You think my sister slashed herself like some sort of teenage tragedy? Fuck. You. He did it. You know gods damned well he did. I’m sure as fucking shit he did it. He killed my sister. Made it look like suicide. I watched him tear Miki’s throat out. Right in front of me. I fucking watched him. You wanna tell me I didn’t see that? You wanna tell me that didn’t happen? He’s a murderer, Katt. A remorseless killer. Father or not… I’m going to find him. And when I do…" "You’ll do what?" He smiled. Not the patented grin he’d used to charm the pants (literally) off of thousands of women the world over. Not that simple and alluring smile he’d become known so well for. No. This was different. It was a hollow expression. A fake little twist of the lips that belied more agony and regret than any frown or furrowed brow ever could have. "She was pregnant. You know that? They called me first. Let me know. Couldn’t have been more than a month or so. It was likely she never even knew. Tony never said anything. He didn’t even know. I asked them not to tell him. That it stay just with me. Even went through a fair bit of trouble to have it removed from the records. Better that Tony didn’t know. Se7yn didn’t just kill my sister, Katt. He ended the bloodline. She’s gone. And when I follow… that’ll be it. No more Rayne family. He took that away. I can’t… I can’t let that go. Four years now, she’s been buried. Four years and I’m no closer to catching him than I was the day after. That changes now. I’m going to hunt him. I’m going to find him. And I’m going to kill him. You decide how the fuck you want to deal with that. Friend." There was no chance for rebuttal. He turned his back to her. Marched out of the training room. Katterina stood fuming for a handful of seconds. She tried deep breaths. She tried finding a calm center. All she felt was anger. Undirected, unmitigated rage. At him. At her father. At the world. A hand landed gently, reassuringly upon her slender shoulder. She swatted it with a ferocious growl. Drake pulled his arm away, rubbing at reddened skin caused by Katt’s abuse. Milena slinked forward. She did not touch, but simply placed herself within Katterina’s line of vision. "Why’s he have to be such a prick?" "When’s the last time you remember Tyler entering a tournament?" "What?" "Just humor me for a moment. When’s the last time you remember Tyler willingly entering himself into a tournament or a battle royale or… any such nonsense of the like?" "I dunno. Years probably. He usually doesn’t go in for that bullshit. The fuck does that have to do with-" "Yet now he’s thrown himself into two. The TEAM thing and now this Jewel in the Crown. He’s defending one title and making a half-assed attempt at winning another. All this training to keep himself in shape… but I dare say these tournaments themselves are the training." "The point, Milena?" "He’s pushing himself. Harder than he has in years. Testing his limits. Exceeding them. Finding new ones. Why though? That’s the point, Katt. Why push so hard? Why even bother? He’s preparing himself. For Se7yn. For the battle to come. He was serious, you know. About finding him. Killing him. He’s preparing to do it. He’s marching to war. For the first time in many years… he marches alone." "That’s his problem, not mi-" "Don’t act like you don’t care. Just think about this. If you’re not there at his side… what family does Tyler even have left?" Katterina did not look her friend in the eye. Milena gave the thought a second to linger before she too turned to leave, ushering a reluctant Drake Roberts along with her. Katterina Wylde was left alone. She had no witty retort for Milena’s question. No snide remark to brunt the blow of the truth. She did not have an answer, for, quite simply, there just wasn’t one to give.
