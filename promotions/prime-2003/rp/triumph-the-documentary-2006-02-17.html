Triumph: The Documentary

Date: Feb. 17, 2006 ['Prelude”] Some things you believe without query… The sky is blue; the grass is green; bird’s fly. Some things must be proven to you… Ghosts are real; Heaven and Hell; Life in orbit But what happens when you hear a story that’s so surreal, yet so true… So complexed that it couldn’t possibly be based upon a true story. Some love stories are well known… Romeo and Juliet. Some are lesser known… Identity Crisis. But what happens when both of those stories are taken and shaped into a realm of reality. Let’s shape the realm of idealism for a moment. Bred on the outskirts of New Jersey…nourished by a band of tribulations. My dear mother…gone. My dear father…nowhere to be found. All I have left is this dollar and a dream. Through the broad daylight, society refuses to accept me as I am. They brush me under their broad assumptions. They see these scars that cover my external body and automatically scamper to their closet of assumptions. I seek a destined limelight that will never prosper. I seek diligently for the chance to be amongst the righteous and deserving, instead of the under-claimed and weak. I seek diligently for the chance to be an immortal figure amongst so many mortals. It’s an opportunity I’ve desired for so long…but have never experienced. A nation that’s statement is built upon a rock of hope and equality amongst the common mortal, but yet I see nothing of it. One Nation, Under God, with equal liberty and justice for all…all accept me. For I am not deserving of liberty, but instead of death. I have to taint my image so much so that people classify me as a species instead of a living creature with living feelings. I long for the chance to be normal, and also the chance for society to accept me as such. I long for the day in which they’ll peak beyond the veil of these scars and realize that I have feelings just as they do. But my feelings mean nothing to them…for to them I am nothing more than a thorn bush growing stagnated in a field of roses. But I’ve been told that faith is the substance of things to hope for and the evidence of things not seen…and yet in hearing this I stand here with a strand of faith, slowly blemishing at the hands of death. But why have I been annexed and outcasted by society, you ask? Maybe my outward appearance doesn’t fit into their spectrum of formality. Maybe my choices in life are of minimum merit. Maybe it’s just because of who I am and what I’ve become. I’ve been placed in a shadow, blocked out of people’s minds and classified as an under-being who would never amount to anything. While others have basked in the glory of silver platters and spoil systems, I have had to earn everything in my life. Beat as a child, punished for no other reason other than my father’s own enjoyment. I resemble Hester in the Scarlet Letter…disowned by her fellow people all because of one sin. In her case, the sin was adultery…in my case, the sin is being unusual. Being a living gimmick that only serves to entertain people for a matter of moments before they become bored and cast me out into the shadows. Yes, that’s what I’ve become…and it’s a role I have learned to accept. But why do I tell you this? Do I want pity? No. Pity is for the suffering and weak at heart. Though the name as well as the outward appearance begs to differ, I come to serve notice that I am a mortal just like everyone else. I bleed when I am cut just like everyone else. I partake of the same oxygen as everyone else and I walk the same plain as they do, too. But If society does not want to accept me as such, I’ll just have to prove it to them time and time again until I finally conquer that limelight and pedestal that I have so long dreamed of. My name is Vangelus Olsig... And this is why I cry... “Speaking of Casualties...how much is this paying” Black ashes decorated the creme colored carpet below, standing out as a disruption in an otherwise tamed atmosphere. A mild aroma migrated with a lemon scent, presenting a blissful atmosphere of various differences. Dave Gibson took one last heavy drag of the cigarette before disposing of its contents thereafter before sending a sharp glaze through the hazed pupils of one mister James Alexander in a sheer sign of fatigue and desperation to get through this night. “Just think of it as a favor for a friend...one who really deserves it. Shouldn’t that be payment enough?” questioned Alexander, fumbling with random mechanics in an effort to meet Gibson’s desire to end this thing as quickly as possible. “Right...I’ll take that as you saying my demands were met in full.” Gibson responded, taking a moment to adjust his attire which consisted of a sleeveless “Old School” shirt accompanied by faded blue jeans and workers boots. “Well, some of your demands were a bit exaggerated, for the most part I guess you could say they were met fully, though. Why’d you want to produce this so badly, though, Gib?” James asked. “Well mainly for the benefit of Vangelus. This thing with Ozric has him so disassembled to the point where he’s contemplated not even showing up in Tokyo. I want to produce as a symbol of triumph...something to remind him that he can conquer anything and anyone.” “I understand. Lets not waste time then...ready?” “5' “4' “3' “2' “...Go.” Tracer City Five years ago, that place was considered a haven. The people who populated the area were of the most elevated class, sporting the most decorated merchandise and claims. It was such a tight community as to where you couldn’t help but love it. Oh how quickly the tides change, though. What began as a safe haven from the dangers of life evolved into a city of chaos, ran and operated by various mobs and local crime lords. In a sense, I guess you could say that this very evolution from triumph to wasteland welcomed Vangelus into the city. Most should know by now that Vangelus is a security force, employed with police officials in an effort to defend justice. In my opinion, no single soul was better suited for the job than Vangelus. He had this certain aura about himself, even at his tender age, which made you fear him. As a matter of fact, that’s the only time I’ve known the kid to really get upset...when he was dealing with criminals. Tides grew too high for even him, though, forcing him into early retirement. Most of my respect for Vangelus comes through the sense that he has so much heart. The guy goes through a lot, but yet never stresses about anything. It’s a will power a lot of wish we’d had. The Justice department took a drastic shot when Olsig retired, though. That was five years ago, though...you’d think they’d adjusted themselves by now. I guess its true what they say about The more things change, the more they stay the same. The night began just like any other night for Vangelus. It should be noted that he’d been under deep depression for several weeks prior to this day. The love of his life, and girlfriend of six years Marissa Paige lived alone in Ontario, Canada...the two hadn’t seen each other for nearly three months. If you don’t take in anything else I say throughout this documentary, know solely that Marissa and Vangelus resembled every detail of a modern day Romeo and Juliet. Never in my life have I seen such a perfect match between two people. I mean, Vangelus was so fond of her that he didn’t even allow their relationship to be known to the public...he always kept it under wraps in fear that it would bring unwelcome exposure and/or drama. It seemed to work because the two never got into a dispute or argument over anything. There love for each other was so perfect that it almost seemed unreal. I wish I could show you, because words truly can’t even begin to express their love for each other. Marissa wasn’t just a key figure in Olsig’s life, though...she was a figure in all of our lives. She made her way to all the possible events she could and called every one of the guys at least twice a week. She was our inspiration and driving factor. Her impact was never seen, but all the guys can testify that it was truly great. I remember those Thursday night chats with her, one day after Revolution. She knew just how to make you smile and laugh, all the while maintaining focus. One of my most fond memories of her had to be when we traveled to Canada on the road to Colossus II. The main event was Vangelus versus Killean for the Universal Championship. Killean and Vangelus are great friends and that night was special because she was right there cheering both guys on. Memorable times with that girl, indeed. Three long months the two had been separated, though, and the impact it had on Vangelus showed through his facial as well as social expressions. I hate to say it, but it seemed to me as if his in-ring performance was slowly yet surely decreasing as well. A guy that’s usually active and up for a good time was replaced by a guy who sat around the house all day, watching black and white movies and raging through various bottles of Jack Daniels. Needless to say, we were all worried about him. Vangelus is so young and fresh to the business...he’s like our little brother. That’s what all of the guys saw him as because he was so young and new to the business. As big brothers and sisters, it was our duty to take care of him in times like those, though, which was the driving factor in our marching into Hin See’s office one faithful Tuesday afternoon. It took some doing, but we finally convinced See to give Vangelus a couple of days of vacation so that he could travel to Canada to visit Marissa. See is very strict when it comes down to business, but even he recognized the strong relationship between Marissa and Vangelus and the effect that it was having on Olsig’s in-ring performance. “Don’t play with me, brother...” Olsig stated after we informed him of the news. The expression on his face told the story, though, after it was confirmed that it was not a joke but yet very real. As expected, it didn’t take long for Olsig to gather his things and hop on the first flight out of the country. You can’t send a guy like Vangelus to another country without some support, though. Remember...he’s our little brother, you wouldn’t let your little brother travel that far without the proper safety precautions. In other words, we sent former PRIME competitor and veteran to the business, Jacob Cross, with him. “Can’t believe this is real, man.” Olsig stated in glee, as Cross smiled in response. It had to be somewhere near three o’clock in the morning, but yet Olsig was still awoken in a true sign of just how excited he was. “It’s real, dude...Not to extinguish your fire, or anything, but why is this so special to you?” Cross asked. He knew the impact Marissa had on not only Olsig’s life, but in all the backstage boy’s lives, simply because he was one of the those backstage boys...but I guess it’s a situation where, until you are placed in someone’s shoes, you can’t truly grasp or comprehend their true feelings towards a certain thing, or in Olsig’s case, person. “You know...it’s just like when you’re a kid and you have that special toy or bear that you hold dear to your heart. No matter what happens, you’ll always have that bear to turn to for comfort and salvation. Marissa was my bear and it felt as if she was stripped away from me...now I’m finally getting her back and...I don’t know...it’s just so...special. Plus she says she has a surprise for me, so that should be exciting.” He stated, smile broadening in an immense range. It was a familiar smile, but yet it seemed so special and unique. “I understand. I think it’d be best if you got some shut eye, though...don’t want Marissa welcoming you with bags, eh?” a small chuckle escaped from the mouth of Cross as Olsig gave a nod in approval and leaned his head back, closing his eyes in an attempt to garnish some much needed rest. The smile remains planted on his face...so young. Shift. Noon. Vangelus was expected to be arriving any minute and Marissa wanted the place to look as best as it possibly could. It’d been hard for her to fight the excitement and garnish any sleep, but deep down inside of her she knew that she had a husband on an airplane who was having the same trouble...it made her smile. As sweet of a woman you’d ever know, she was. After placing the last remaining plate on the table, she went over to the counter and grabbed a box that contained the special surprise she had in store for Vangelus...how special it truly was. When you think about it, it’s sort of hard to imagine two people who love each other as much as Vangelus and Marissa did, even though they were only dating. The two even shared Insurance together, but yet weren’t married. Vangelus once proposed to her, but she declined stating that she wasn’t ready for such a big leap in her life. Vangelus counted down the days to which they’d finally one day get married...with such a hectic schedule in the wrestling industry, though, it didn’t seem very likely to happen any time soon. Thump, Thump Marissa paused in place as she her the footsteps coming from downstairs. Someone was in the house...she not only could hear it, but she could smell it. A scent of blood caressed the atmosphere, causing Marissa to tip down the stairs in order to see exactly what was going on below her. She reached the bottom step, peering around the corner to find nothing. She glanced to her left and realized that she’d left the television on. Simple and sudden speculation told her that it was the television that was making the noise, and maybe something gone wrong with dinner that caused the smell...but with each step she took up those stairs, that sense began to faint. Suddenly, cold dreadful sweat began to creep down her face as she continued to ascend the stairs. Her breaths began to grow short and her body began to tremble. It didn’t take long before she clutched her neck with her hands and began to gasp deeply for breaths, but each one was as empty as the last. Something wasn’t right.... Her eyes rolled into the back of her head... Her trembling hands stopped profoundly... Breathing ceased to exist... And in one swift motion, her body went tumbling down the stairs, landing on the ground below with her boyfriend’s special surprise in hand....one that he’d never be able to truly appreciate for the sheer fact that it’s relevance ceased. Clamp “Baby I’m ho-...” Vangelus dropped to his knees at the sight of his deceased beloved. The first few moments were decorated with shock...the final...with screams and tears. Tears pelted the dead Marissa Paige’s face as Olsig held her in his arms, screaming into the empty abyss. In his hand he held the woman he could never stop loving... And in her hand was his special surprise... An engagement ring. Damn. Four Hours Before Revolution “I want to try something new in our match, tonight.” Revolution was fast approaching, dawning in on Peter Vetra and Daniel Pierce as they wrapped up ideas for their match. Pierce, also known as First Blood, was a veteran inside of the PRIME circle and so was Peter. Both men understood the business better than just about anyone else you’d find backstage. They were men who set examples for the new guys and didn’t mind putting others over themselves, which made them high commodities to the industry. “Well, if you don’t mind I-...” Suddenly, Vetra stopped in mid-sentence as he glanced down the hallway appearing to have heard some sort of noise. He brushes it off, though, and begins once again, but yet again he hears the noise resembling someone in heels running down the hallway. “Who is th-...” Cutting off Daniel Pierce, PRIME’s Interviewing diva, Lisa Tyler, burst unto the scene, tears masking her face as both Vetra and Pierce grabbed her, sitting her down and attempting to tame her in order to realize the situation that had her distraught. “Lisa, Lisa...calm down and tell us what’s wrong.” Pierce demanded, holding Tyler by the shoulders and mildly shaking her in an effort to calm her down. Her cries and screams drew attention from several of the crew workers standing around, causing them to pause in mid-activity and take notice of the situation at hand. “I-It’s Marissa....She’s....oh God, she’s dead!” Lisa blurted out before crashing into a state of tears, falling into the arms of Pierce as murmurs erupted among the surrounding crew workers. The expression on Pierce’s face told the entire story...Marissa meant something to us all. “Motherfucking DAMMIT!” erupted Vetra as he sent a cold fist spiraling into the nearby painted concrete wall. The impact of the shot alone made you realize how much it should have pained Vetra, but in his heart, just like everyone else’s heart, was an even sharper pain...an emotional pain that numbed any physical pain that could occur. “All I want to know is who the FUCK did this!?” Vetra blurted once more, taking the time to grab a lead pipe that happened to be leaning against the wall. The rage in his eyes clearly expressed his desire to lay waste to the scumbag who robbed us of one of our most precious and valued treasures. “Get a hold of yourself, Vet!” Pierce screamed out, releasing Lisa and grabbing Vetra by the collar in an effort to tame his tempered associate. “I know you want revenge...we all do, but Vangelus needs us right now. Lets tend to that first...THEN we will make the culprit pay” Pierce stated. The irony of it all was the fact that they didn’t even know if she was killed by a person...but a certain instinct takes over when you’re placed in a situation such as this. Marissa was in perfect health...as healthy as any of us, that’s for sure. That instinct told us that it was some sick soul, but right now that instinct needed to sit sideline. We had a friend we had to secure first. Shift. Wes Penthouse Brian Wes of Core Wrestling fame. Home of Caesar Vega It was a typical day for Caesar, otherwise known as Nova inside of the squared circle. Nursing his fatal injury suffered at Tropical Turmoil, he sat flipping through channels all the while taking the usual drag from his cigarette bud. The man was about as calm, cool, and collective as they came, but watching his fellow PRIME buddies compete inside of a ring he was forced to abandoned due to injury shook him a bit...nothing too noticeable, but definitely present. To watch Brandon Youngblood go on to King of Kings to battle Hoyt Williams in a Universal Title Shot that belonged to him...it no doubt stirred him, but there was nothing he could do about it. All he could do was brace the day in which he would return to the squared circle and reclaim that which was rightfully his. In the meantime...he’d have other issues to tend to. Burrrrrrr Nova’s cell phone vibrate rapidly, interrupting his deep concentration of the movie in which he was watching. Nova took one last drag of the cigarette before snatching up the phone and flipping it open, raising it up to his ear. “Yeah...” he greeted. It didn’t take long for his facial expression to shape after a couple of moment of listening to the person on the other end. You didn’t even have to listen to the person on the other end in order to realize what was being stated, all that was needed was the rapid expression on Nova’s faced that constantly changed as the news anchored in. “Are you serious?” Nova pondered, withdrawing the cigarette from his mouth and placing in on the arm of the couch as he concentrated his attention to the person on the phone. “Oh yeah...yeah, I’ll do that. Mister Paige’s Home I don’t know much concerning this aspect of the story, but I’ll tell you what I’ve heard. It was a Monday morning, two days after Marissa’s funeral. This Monday morning, though, he sought bigger issues, a possible explanation as to his absence, which is why he invited Vangelus over. He wasn’t as strong as Vangelus, so the tears weren’t fought back, but were instead allowed to flow like rivers. His mouth hung low as he watched the crimson blood ooze out of the chest of his father-in-law.
