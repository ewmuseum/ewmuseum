I Stand Before You Now, Truly Unafraid (ReV 103, vs. A Shit-Ton of Great Writers)

1 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/01/06 Nova closes his eyes and breathes deep, feeling the grassy earth of the open plain underneath his toes. He opens them, and unfortunately, the same scene presents itself as before. He stares across the plain. Several hundred yards away, five silhouettes rapidly gain clarity. Nova’s knuckles whiten as his grip on the handle of his warhammer tightens. No time to panic now. Strength is key. Visible confidence is crucial. The figures in the distance are fast approaching, and Nova can distinguish the individuals from one another. His eyes focus on a figure of medium height and heavy-set build wielding a long staff, appropriately out in front of the pack… …Chet Worth. Nova’s lip curls in disdain. The Worth’s ascent to power had promised shelter from the storm, order (in more than one fashion, coming from the OCD superstar) instead of chaos…respite from lawlessness. But like Isildur with the Ring of Power, the Worth had been corrupted at the point where the opportunity for good was at its apex. Chet aligned himself with the forces of evil, though even now at this critical moment Nova can’t for the life of him understand why. Once upon a time, the Worth waged his own personal, vigilante war against the people he leads into battle now, which is why the free world rejoiced at his coronation and heralded it as the dawn of a New Age. A chill runs up Nova’s spine. Perhaps this is the dawn of a New Age, but it could very well be an entirely different vision of the future than they had all thought. Blinded by hope, no one, not even the Rising Star, remembered that the Worth had always served himself and no other, to meet his own ends. Whatever motivations he has now standing across from those he might have championed, one can be sure that they revolve around the person Chet sees when he stares into a mirror. Despite it all, Nova doesn’t believe in his heart of hearts that the Worth is an evil man. But he can’t say the same for the figure who swaggers shortly behind him… Danny Ferguson. Nova can almost feel the heat where he stands from the sneer surely smeared across the face of Ireland’s worst export. The sword hanging from his right hand promises that blood will be spilt this day, though whether or not it will belong to Nova the Rising Star cannot speculate about. Danny was the driving force behind the assemblage of the coalition of evil that now makes its way toward Nova. He brought together like-minded individuals and led them in their campaigns of terror, unblinking blue eyes witnessing the carnage that followed with a sickening sort of pride. But the yellow streak running the length of Danny’s spine betrays his confidence swagger, and Nova knows it. Ferguson doesn’t have the heart of a warrior. He doesn’t have the stomach for battle. His famous vanity prevents him from plunging into battle with the abandon necessary to truly unlock his potential, opting instead to fight a reserved, defensive fight in order to protect his scar-less features. … But all of the words (and they are countless) used to describe the Illustrious Face-Eater, “reserved” is not one of them. The Eater of Faces dances around the group wildly, his chain mace bouncing and swinging around in tow. Smaller in stature, the masked menace resembles the classic side man, the Igor to a Dr. Frankenstein. Nova spits on the ground as he watches the loon approach. He has a special hatred for this one. To Nova, Facey is possibly the most dangerous foe on the other side of the field…because insanity has its fiery hot fingers gripped solidly around his mind. There is no rationale, no significant motive to the actions of the Illustrious Face-Eater, only whatever appeals to his violent, impetuous nature. Nova and Facey have met steel-to-steel outside the sanctioned environment of the battlefield, but at the outset of their only true confrontation, the Dick aligned himself with another to destroy the Rising Star, only to turn on his ally and spare Nova’s life. Nova resolves to himself that if he makes it out of this one alive, he’ll meet Facey on the field of battle like they should have long ago, and he’ll end his nemesis or die in the process. The cool, collected strut of Chandler Tsonda contrasts sharply to the Face-Eater’s manic prancing, and it belies other differences between the two A-Listers. Tsonda is cold, calculating, no less evil in his own way (or deadly) than his comrade, but more manipulative. Chandler Tsonda is a dangerous island, miles emotionally away from any other human being on the planet, the only bridge coming in the form of Eleanor Kannon-Hall, not surprisingly absent from the battlefield on this day. To Tsonda, other people are one-dimensional, flat images in his line of vision that disappear as soon as he stops looking at them. They don’t feel, or think, or matter, and that makes ALL of them disposable. Expendable. And the scimitar in his hand has rendered many, many of them expended. Finally, Nova’s eyes make it to the far left of the line of foes…to Karina Wolfenden, long sais twirling in her hands. Like Chet Worth, her self-concerned nature dictates her actions, and allegiances only go as far as they don’t compromise her personal goals. The way Nova sees it, the K-Wolf sold her soul to the Devil. Once the most powerful warrior in the land, Wolfenden suffered defeat at the hands of the Deville, a legendary fighter and close ally of the Rising Star. After that battle (and a battle it was), Karina fell slowly but surely from the echelon of the mightiest heroes back into the ranks of mere mortals. Her alignment with the forces of chaos can only be an attempt to claw back into dominance. The K-Wolf’s deflection doesn’t anger Nova in the same fashion that the Worth’s did, however, because of the latter’s ruined chance to effect serious change for the better from a position of power. The sound of a cough draws Nova’s attention to his right, and he smiles reassuringly as he stares into the face of young Silvio Fiore, the unlikeliest hero to emerge in this epic showdown. It was Fiore, standing 5’9” and weighing 164 pounds, who threw the gauntlet down. It was Fiore, unable to feel pain, who was so moved by the treachery of the A-List and their powerful entourage that he took action. It was Fiore, quiet and earnest, who issued the call for any heroes left in these trying times to unite and make a stand. Four warriors responded. All that stands in the way of more long months of terror is their fragile phalanx. Fiore shifts his feet nervously and tests the strength of his bowstring before returning Nova’s grin. The Rising Star turns his head to survey the rest of his team. Kyle Lamen stands unafraid, nunchaku dangling at the ready by his side. The group’s Doc Holiday, Lamen has the unique perspective of not fearing death because it lies in wait for him every day, always a step behind the Crown Prince of Punk Rock. ‘Chucks whirling, he’ll dive into combat with the reckless abandon that his nemesis Danny Ferguson lacks, and if he is wounded, he’ll die on the field like a warrior, tainted blood seeping into the earth, not in a bed like a sickly child. Ignatius Lisieux stands next to him, the cruel pentagram on his chest – a reminder of past evils since vanquished – obscured by a shield strapped to his left arm. His right hand holds a battered, dulled sword, once the sharpest, most feared weapon in all of civilization. Unlike Wolfenden or the Worth, Lisieux once fought ferociously for the forces of greed and power-mongering before finding it within himself to tear away and rebel. His body is a reflection of his sword, haggard from too many battles, too many stands against tyranny, too many dangerous enemies. Lisieux spread himself thin trying to protect the world, and now he’s a husk of his former vibrant self, his fighting abilities diminished. But the Eternal Sun also rises, and when Ignatius heard that heroes were converging to wrest control away from those who abuse it, the gleam of anticipation came back…the fires of passion were ignited again. That fire can be seen now in the eyes of the tired warrior as he gives Nova a solemn nod. Behind him, the massive form of Clint Poteet casts an intimidating shadow. Nova’s gaze lingers on him. Poteet’s place in the group is an odd one. The Worth’s coronation (and consequent dethroning of Tyler Nelson) spelled the end of Poteet’s reign as the Greediest Player in the Game’s right-hand man. The heroes cheered as Clint’s blood-stained axe was silenced. He holds that axe aloft now, eagerly awaiting his opportunity to cleave Chet Worth’s head in twain. Nova is sure that once that goal is accomplished, Poteet’s bond to the group will dissipate. He could very well turn on them. Nova would have denied his aid, if not for the decidedly lackluster turnout on the heroes’ side. As the space between the groups diminishes, cool strides become gaits as the villains charge. Ferguson points his sword out at Lamen, who pushes past Nova and runs, nunchaku whirling, to meet him. Silvio steadies his hand and fires an arrow that whizzes past the running Lamen. On target, it flies at the K-Wolf, who spins and with amazing dexterity deflects it with her sais. Danny meets Kyle and swings his sword. Kyle strikes with his ‘chucks, one wrapping around the blade, but Danny punches him in the face with his free hand and looses his sword, hoisting it up vertically to drive through his enemy’s chest… …but another blade intervenes, as Ignatius Lisieux knocks away the blow and shoulder-tackles Ferguson to the ground. The battle rages now, fully underway as the warriors do battle for the fate of the planet. Nova scans the chaotic scene, and his eyes fall on the Illustrious Face-Eater, laughing hysterically as he swings his chain mace overhead. With a war cry, the Rising Star charges into the thick of it, catching Tsonda in the stomach with a blow from the warhammer. Knocking him out of the way, Nova takes a step and then leaps into the air, hammer raised in one hand. Facey stares up at him, grinning wildly in anticipation of combat, and as Nova comes down, wind blowing his hair back, the shot fades out into white nothingness. PRIME REVOLUTION 103 MAIN EVENT THE A-LIST, CHET WORTH & KARINA WOLFENDEN VS. IGNATIUS LISIEUX, NOVA, KYLE LAMEN, SILVIO FIORE & CLINT POTEET
