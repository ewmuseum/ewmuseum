Roll Those Dice (i)

17 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th January 2007 He had to see her. He knew he shouldn’t… but fuck… when was GA ever within the realms of sanity. This was a dumb motherfuckin’ idea. The kind of idea that in the past saw him spend two, maybe three years in Fiji’s maximum security wing for a crime he did not commit. Was she worth it? Like fuck she was. Just another damn broad he was told. Except she wasn’t. In his eyes… in his dumb Fijian gaze… she was worth living for, worth fighting for, and if the worst came to it, he’d lay his life down on the line for her. The idiot was in love. Not that pussy wimpy bullshit, his was the Goo Goo Dolls – Iris kinda love. It was as pure as it was dumb, but there was the most noble of noble intentions imbued within his thoughts. He’d only ever sought to do good… sure he’d lost his way in life slightly – a term in ‘the Kill Zone’ (Fiji’s delectable institute for reforming criminals) would do that to even the most ardent of philosophers. It had sapped his soul, terminated his zest and grated every last ounce of human decency this once warm and mercurial character possessed. He had turned. There was no doubting that. Like the moment Anakin Skywalker turns evil… the moment you knew was coming… but just for those precious precocious moments you willed the outcome to be different. There was an impious inevitability about the change in Umaga, represented not merely by his renaming (a precautionary method against a vengeful father/psychopath), but by the mental stability of GA. His friends noticed more than most – hell, I had to put up with more psychobabble bullshit than someone with psychobabbling parents. It was hell on earth, and not even in the Mobb Deep sense. I didn’t know what to say when I heard the news. I mean… you hardly tell a 6’3", 287lbs Cage Fighting Animal no. Truth be told, I never would tell Umaga… or Gabriel… or GA… or whatever moniker he was currently under no. He was my brother, we’ve been there, seen it all, stood tall, and did it our way. Okay, so we weren’t related by any family, the blood coursing through his veins was not my own, but we shared an eternal bond that blood just couldn’t provide. "Brothers by chance. Friends by choice." There was more than choice here; it was more than a mere arrangement. It was the complete dedication of two people to the aid of another, I loved him like a brother, and that was good enough for me. I told him this was some crazy shit he was going to be involved in. I told him over and over. It was like a conversation with a brick wall. Trouble followed Gabriel like Blair follows Bush, through thick and thin, through hell, high waters, heavens and earth… it was like the maker had some sort of vendetta against GA. Even worse… like death had some sort of personal acquiescence of the fact GA survived when all else around him died and just now wanted to remember. Final destination… Fiji… the shores of Bua Levu. His awkward style was just that... the pure transformation of his jutting nature to a prose that made no sense... yet was perfectly formed. He was no James Joyce... but his stream-of-conscious heart on a sleeve life won plaudits... he was lost in a sea of confusion. He needed a raft... and she was just that. Things were fucked up. They always had been for Gabriel. You are a child. Your parents die in a horrific plane crash… yet you somehow survive. You are raised by the nomadic tribesmen of the Serengeti. Your grandparent takes you to Fiji a maladjusted fifteen year old. You grow up with no social skills. You fight. You fall in love. You lose. You lose in a big way. You get put away. You struggle. You cry. You find release in the arms of a wrestling federation. You join an Empire. Your Empire crumbles. You are in so deep you’re clutching at air as the tides subside over your now immersed head. What do you do? You run. And he ran. Ran back to what he knew… what he loved. The plains of the Serengeti. The cages of Eastern Europe. The waves of the Fijian Ocean. To… to her. Someone once told me that the definition of happiness was doing something you loved… and in doing something, that could be anything. Even a person. He was tired of running. Tired of being alone. Tired of living each and every day as if it was his last: not knowing whether some disgruntled Polynesian would crack his skull open due to some ill advised mafia fatwa. The only crime he’d committed was that of love. But. History tells us that love can be the almightiest of crimes. Romeo + Juliet, Troy… okay… not greatly based on history, but you can see my point. Ah well… I made the call… ---- It seemed like an eternity. He had counted down every second of every hour of every day, until the days merged with the nights and the nights the day waiting for this very moment. His once cropped head had been restored to its former glory, matted dreadlocks flowed over the collar of his Bathing Ape hoody. His jeans were of a relaxed fit, teamed with the same knackered and worn in Converse. The rosary beads still adorned his neck, and currently resided in between his lips. His face seemed weathered, but his eyes showed signs of new life. The icon of the MTV Generation, white ear bud’s slipped effortlessly into his inside pocket as the lyrics of Notorious B.I.G.’s 1970 Somethin’ caressed his brain. He was in his favourite place, the rock at the farthest point on the eastern tip of Fiji's most eastern isle. It was their place. How would he react? Lyric after lyric of BIG’s immaculate chubby flow massaged his senses… It had been too long? She’d have forgotten him? Would she even show? She had so much to lose… was he in danger…? All questions were soon put to bed. A hand upon his shoulder stopped his heart. Normally he’d whirl and unleash the fury upon whoever dare approach him but the hand possessed a deep warmth that could only come from that of a woman. "Lisa…" he peeled the ear buds from his ear slowly, not allowing the hand to lose its grip on his shoulder as if this were the last piece of human affection he would feel for a perpetuity. He turned slowly and was greeted with the tenderness and affection of her eyes; she bit her lips in an attempt to alleviate the tears welling up in her eyes. Her lip quivered as her eyes met his and shared a moment that not even the greatest love song could ever put pen to paper. She threw her arms around Afeaki’s thick Fijian neck and buried her face deep within his muscular chest as a burst of tears and sobs emanated from the Pacific’s premier beauty. He didn’t know whether to smile or cry, taking his arms and holding her tightly as she continued to sob into his chest. His lips parted as he tenderly left a kiss on her forehead, the moment of emotion too great for Lisa as she relented with another plethora of tears. She had missed him terribly, to have him here in the flesh was worth breaking any rules her father had laid down. She hadn’t seen him for almost four years. He looked down as her eyes met his, "Hi…" the words slipped slowly from his mouth, he flashed one of those movie star grins he’d discovered work wonders on photo shoots. Lisa smiled sweetly back, "Hi to you too mister." She nuzzled into his chest one last time before locking her hand in his: leading him toward the edge of the rock face. She perched opposite Gabriel, removing the hood from over his face. It was like she was looking at him for the very first time… "Mags…" the words ebbed from her mouth; it was painful for her… "I’ve… I’ve missed you so much. I can’t do this anymore. This whole entire life… let’s just go away… just you and me…" He looked deep into her eyes and wanted to chase cars with her, ask her if he just lay here, would she lie with him and just forget the world, but there would be no snow patrol today. He so wanted it to happen, but he knew the reality, he knew who her father was, what he could do and more importantly what he would do. "You know that isn’t possible Lisa." The fire. Let it burn. She let rip. "So why the hell have you dragged me all the way here, risking your life and mine for the sake of what? Tell me? You wanna fuck me huh? Then leave? Oh yeah… just run away like you always do from anything that’s hard. Well go on then… leave." GA turned away from Lisa… she knew him better than anyone. "You know what Gabriel. Someone a long time ago told me that if there’s something you love that’s worth fighting for, it should be worth every piece of your efforts, mind body and soul." He hated it when she quoted him. "That same someone also told me he’d never leave." Stick the knife in… "… told me that through everything on this planet they’d do everything within their power to hold me…" …twist it…" "All he ever was… all you ever are is one big empty promise." Stabbed through the heart. Lisa stood… ready to burst one last time… champagne supernova… "Lisa… wait." She turned slowly. "Let’s try." She looked at him quizzically… "Come with me to America. We’ll start again… just you and I. I could care less about your father or any of his goons. You’re right… all this time I’ve been running. I need you. I need to get back on track, get things in my life right again. I can’t do that without you… A smile crept across her face, "I… I didn’t mean those things…" Of course she did, but tough love was needed. "It’s okay Lis… but we really need to go." She nodded. The time was almost at the 11th hour… Afeaki smiled taking Lisa in his arm as they strolled back to Afeaki’s car. Time was of the essence… but for GA he’d do all the time coming to him just for one night in Lisa’s arms. This tale of unrequited love was slowly coming to a new chapter. How the fuck it would turn out was as good as anyone’s guess in Vegas. I mean… you take a chance right? Roll that dice. Or do you play it safe? Fuck. This. Shit. GA was a gambler. A fighter. He’d fight this… because when he looked at Lisa he’d give up forever to touch her… she was a beautiful iris whose tender affections could set his life in the direction it should be going. This could backfire… and horrendously at that. All that was certain though… was the tempestuousness of the journey. And if I’m being frank with you… it would soon seem a bad idea. Very bad.
