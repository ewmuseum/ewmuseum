Selling Out : Part Two - 'They Put My Name On A T-Shirt.'

March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March, 2005 Selling Out : Part Two - 'They Put My Name On A T-Shirt.' The model employee, Karina Wolfenden was not. With eyes still aching, not used to being open before midday, Karina Wolfenden staggered into PRIME's offices. Synapses still needed to warm up, causing her legs to trail a second or so behind her thoughts... leaving the usually vibrant K-Wolf looking more like an extra from Resident Evil. Wait, Shaun of the Dead, it was a better film. A yawn that spanned the lower half of Kari's face met the receptionist as she looked up from her monitor, instantly recognising the unmistakable, cascading lilac hair of PRIME's Negasonic Lupine. 'Mr Pham is expecting you at the sound stage,' her robotic tone drawled, sounding as if she was doing her best to mimic an answer phone. 'So he didn't spontaneously combust in the night?' the K-Wolf hopefully enquired, hands pushed together in prayer. 'The ground didn't swallow him up on the drive here?' 'Never happens,' the receptionist's colds words mumbled as her attention drifted back to her work. 'No matter how hard you wish...' Dragging her feet, and limply swinging her arms at her sides, Karina delved deeper into PRIME's home. Humble beginnings had blossomed with reputation, and the profit that stemmed from there. PRIME knew what people wanted, and it gave it to them hot and steaming every week, with no patronising or false advertising. It was sports entertainment, at its most (im)pure, and certainly at its most profitable. 'Oh, dear God,' escaped in a gasp as her jaw took the express elevator down to the floor. 'I've created a monster...' The sobering sight she was met with was Taun Pham rummaging through the top box of a sizable pile... pulling out a black t-shirt so cheap and then that you could almost see right through it. Marked on the box, like an accusation, was 'Karina'. 'I think I'm going to be sick,' she then gagged, striding over to her boss, a false smile needed to be embossed deep into her facial expression. 'Ah, if it isn't my new, model employee,' Pham met her with, opening his arms to the worker he'd hoped had turned over a new leaf. 'See how quickly the wheels of industry turn? No sooner had you signed your pretty little face over to u-' 'I'm sure the sweat-shop industry values your support,' Karina despaired as one of the shirts was handed over to her, the fabric nearly unravelling between her fingers. 'Well, what are you waiting for?' Taun questioned as the dollar signs chimed in his eyes. 'Try it on!' 'Are these safe to touch human skin?' she asked in retort, touching it with light fingertips. Looking out the tops of her eyes at Taun Pham, Karina folded to his visual gestures, and tentatively poked her head through the neck of the shirt. Her arms followed, before the top seemed to fall into place, little shape or style to it.... and even less comfort. 'Ugh,' the K-Wolf squirmed in her itchy discomfort, reaching around into the neck of the merchandise and pulling her undershirt between it and her pale, sensitive skin. 'What are these things? 70% cotton, 30% itching powder?' 'They're whatever the sweatshops have at hand,' the ruthless businessman informed his irritated star. 'The only percentage that matters is the profit. And with you showing this off on PTC TV, we'll be raking it in.' Rolling her eyes, Karina pulled the t-shirt down properly, then adjusted the collar. With hands dropping to her sides, Karina looked in the mirror, instantly deflating at the sight of herself in a low-grade, cheaply-produced top. The logo, possibly the only thing looking as if it took more than 2 minutes, was a simply graphic of her name, surrounded by various high-flying shots... with a rare picture of her barely smiling face acting as the cherry on top. 'Why would I wear a t-shirt with my own name on it?' the curious Karina asked as eyes narrowed, sending hands plunging down into her pockets. 'That's just a little too close to a name tag for my liking.' 'Because,' the short-of-patience Pham stated back in a fierce hush, 'for some reason that I'll never understand, people look up to you. And despite the fact that future generations having someone like you as a role model leaves be dreading the future, they have the disposable income to waste on something like this.' 'A mannequin would probably look a little more interested,' she apathetically critiqued in return. 'Oh, if I could have replaced you with a mannequin already, I would have,' her boss, far from the biggest K-Wolf fan in the world, was quick to assure her. 'But now you've signed over your likeness rights, and committed yourself to a whole year of promotional appearances, you're now my mannequin, billboard, and autograph signer all rolled into one.' 'Yup,' she regretfully sighed, having known full-well what her act had entailed. 'I'm ya whore.' 'We already have Aimz,' Taun chuckled back, enjoying bringing Karina to heel. 'No need for gimmick infringements.' 'Heh,' was all the K-Wolf grunted in response as her boss reached into his jacket pocket, retrieving a vibrating cell phone and gesturing for the K-Wolf to give him a moment. 'That hand signal means to go home, right? No? Meh.' Plunging her hands into the cheap t-shirt, forming a little pocket for her fidgeting digits, Karina stretched the garment down to her waist... almost turning the black transparent, such was it economic use for fabric. Stifling a yawn, she spun, trying to find something to occupy her fleeting attention. 'Fuuuuuuck,' she then groaned, having found it. Hands moved from shirt to face, finding the selling-out process frustrating enough, without HIM slithering in. The relationship between Karina Wolfenden and Angelo Deville was a strange one. In fact, relationship might not have been the right word... it was more of a connection, one that was meant to be from the very time they began to share a planet together. As verbal sparring partners, they kept each other's wits sharp, and their tongues sharper. In full-on 'Cash Cow' mode, she'd just left the Deville with an open goal, and an insultingly easy tap in. Almost oozing through the void between atoms, Deville seemed unaffected by anything around him. When it was hot, he didn't sweat. When it was windy, not a single hair blew out of place, and not even the most sudden and powerful of gusts appeared to ripple his suit jacket. Tarantino-movie music could be imagined to play as he strolled in, using the air around him as a speaker. 'Looking good,' Deville smirked as he walked past Karina, lingering his icy gaze on her humble chest, where the ill-fitting t-shirt hung awkwardly. 'Looks a little two dimensional, though...' Their verbal sparring usually managed to lift her, both jabbing words back and forth, ducking and weaving to land their retorts. But this time? Yeah, he'd found her looking a prize tool, and all she could do was take it on the chin. 'As I'm looking a prize fucking dork,' she sighed in defeat, 'and there's no way that I'll be able to deflect the VAST amounts of ammo you're staring at, you win this one.' 'Ah, Kari,' Deville dropped his head with, shaking it regretfully. 'The fun is in the challenge. C'mon, there's got to be an insult you've been wanting to land on me for a while... I'll even set it up for you. Just give me a lead.' 'Right now, all the insults I have are saved for myself,' Kari deflated his hopes with. 'You'll have to wait until next time.' With mock pleading eyes, Deville held his hands out to his sides. 'But, Kari, how will I possibly get through the day without your barbed words to hook into my very soul?' 'Look in a mirror,' she at least fed his craving a little with, 'I'm sure you'll find plenty of material.' 'Tease,' he beamed back. 'Tool,' she bid him farewell with, the connection between them fizzling out as he was called away by the PRIME entourage fussing around him. Lingering an icy blue gaze over his shoulder, Deville watched the K-Wolf as he departed, only for Pham to wander between their faltering visual connection, causing Karina to have to refocus hurriedly. 'Right, I have a feeling you'll like this one.' 'That would involve losing the shirt,' hopeful eyes prodded, 'right?' 'Well, of course,' her boss acted surprised that she'd even ask, 'if it can be sold, we don't want it staying on you!' With arms almost leaving a blur trailing behind them, Karina peeled the cheap t-shirt from her lithe body, before hitting an impressive three pointer back into the merchandise box. 'With this Seven of the Best thing coming up,' Pham then explained, 'we might as well milk your appearance in it for all it's worth. All your little, cheap, net-whining 'Indy' fans seem to be buying into it, so we might as well do a hype video for ReVolution.' 'We need to hype MORE?' Kari enquired with a cocked eyebrow. 'Well, since the crowd seems to get off their hands when you do your...' not the most knowledgeable when it came to wrestling moves, Pham struggled for the right terms. 'Spinny, jumpy things, we thought we'd just let y-' 'Already there,' the eager K-Wolf chirped, sliding in under the bottom rope of the old training ring that PRIME used for its promotional videos and segments. 'What's the routine?' 'Believe me when I say this,' his stern voice lectured, 'this will be the only time I ever tell you this. But, just do what comes naturally to you...' 'Bonus,' the enthused K-Wolf confirmed, showing an odd level of eagerness for promotional work. Stood dead-centre in the ring, her acute ring-awareness able to pinpoint the position, Karina took a moment to survey the ropes. Like Neo peeling away the facade of the Matrix to see code, all Karina saw were jumps, flips, and spins. No substance, just opportunity. No imprisoning cables, just tort launch platforms. It took her all of 5 seconds to forget that there was a camera on her. It was how she first prodded her head tentatively through the shrouding curtains of the wrestling business... spotted treating the ring as a play area while waiting for a friend to change, a wily trainer knew that her acrobatics show was enough to base an act around. When he found out that she could kick so hard it nearly tore the universe open, and took bumps for fun, he'd needed a bucket to catch his droolings. 'Play time,' Karina muttered gleefully to herself, eyes almost bubbling. In long, almost prancing strides, Karina made her way to the corner, before plunging her hands down onto the canvas. With the forces of friction between the worn canvas and her fighting gloves anchoring her, Karina pushed her seemingly never-ending legs up into a Handstand. Without even the most miniscule sign of strain tainting her serene expression, she held the pose for ten seconds, before prising her left hand from the canvas and turning through 360 degrees, not compromising an iota of balance. Letting herself gracefully topple backwards, Karina let the backs of her knees rest on the top cable, before hooking her feet under the top turnbuckle, locking them tightly at entwining ankles. Demonstrating the power encased in her limbs, Karina then used her legs' grip of sit up on the top turnbuckle, even crossing her arms in doing so to show that no extra leverage was needed. Both hands were then placed on the top turnbuckle, and her feet hopped up to join them, landing crouched, facing away from the ring. Not even looking back over her shoulder, Karina launched her slender frame up into a Corkscrew Moonsault, tightly wound body spinning too fast through the air for rotations to be guessed at. The loose legs of her trousers could be heard flapping as the turbulence slapped them back and forth. But despite that, and the blur of lilac hair obscuring her face as it wildly mimickrd her body's path, Karina managed to land flawlessly in a forward roll, putting her back on solid, yet characteristically light footing. Before her knees could even be seen bending, the K-Wolf launched herself into a backflip, again landing crouched on her feet, before exploding back up and sprinting towards the ropes. As if there were invisible beams criss-crossing the ring for Kari to spin her body around, she flipped forward, her feet plunging down the middle cable as she landed, which instantly expelled her back into the air. With a lilac trail left to dissipate in the air behind her, Karina landed tumbling backwards, rolling through onto her feet once more. Knowing what she was going to do a second or so before she even needed to think about it, Karina bounded over to the corner, leaping up onto the top rope, obtaining perfect balance upon contact between sneaker soles and cable tape. Then, with head bowed and arms held out to her sides, Karina walked the tight-rope across to the adjacent buckles, before dropping seated onto the ropes. After using an Arabian Press to return to her feet, Karina turned and grabbed the top rope in her gloved hands. Yanking back on the tightly wound cable, Karina let it propel her into the air, spinning around 180 to face the ring as her feet touched down. Feeling the give of the rope, Karina launched at the optimum moment, flinging her body back into a Shooting Star, easily rotating through enough to score another comfortable, crouched landing. Thrusting herself back up, Karina went seamlessly from crouch to backflip, managing to land gracefully on the middle rope, her legs resting back against the top cable for initial support. Bending her knees, Karina thrust her feet down into the rope, producing enough of a launch to fling herself off, high above the canvas. Plummeting down headfirst at the canvas, Karina extended her gloved hands once again, supporting a rolling landing that left the K-Wolf, typically, on her feet in the middle of the ring. Striding over to the ropes, Karina grabbed the top and leapt, sending her body in a perfect arc over the cable. Just as her feet went to touch down on the floor, her grip on the rope anchored her, allowing the K-Wolf to skin the cat, pulling herself back up and over. Without releasing the cable, Kari tucked her legs up and wove them over the middle rope, releasing her grip on the top rope, only to switch it to the other side, allowing Wolfenden to slither her body back up between the cables. With rapidly switching grips and fluid motion of her long, slender body, Kari treated the ropes as a figure-of-eight racetrack, before she finally dismounted and rolled back to her feet. Not showing even the slightest signs of dizziness, Karina turned and backed up against the ropes, feeling their plastic wrapping press into her back and thighs. Taking a deep breath, Karina closed her eyes, before the quick-reflex muscle fibres sent her launching forward. Taking the approach that a high jumper would, Karina curved her run, allowing her to plant her right foot down parallel to the ropes. Channelling all of her 157 pounds down through the limb, it then recoiled, thrusting her body up over the cable in an awe-inspiring Fosby Flop. As if every atom that comprised the building's air was crowd surfing the K-Wolf to the floor, she appeared to float down onto her feet, where only the slightest bending of the knees was needed to cushion the language. A content, yet subtle smile crossed Karina's pale pink lips as she nodded to herself, happy with each spot. Gloved hands scraped the stray lilac hairs from her face, revealing not even the most minuscule, apologetic excuse for a bead of sweat. 'So,' she exhaled snappily, turning to one of the cameraman whose job it was to keep up with her darting, mesmerising movements. 'Need anything else?' 'I'm good,' he muttered back, words losing potency due to his jaw being halfway down to the floor. Turning to the other cameraman, Karina's glowing, feline eyes prodded for an answer, only for the man's glazed-over stare to be lost in her. 'I love you...' Just as Karina's face took on a 'the whu?!?!' expression, the applause of Taun Pham grabbed her attention as he strode back over to her, coffee and script in hand. 'Nice... flexibility always goes over well with the male 18-24 demographic.' 'I'm...' she went to wise-crack back, 'I'm not going to even touch that one.' 'Anyway, I return armed with pure, verbal gold,' his words glorified, as his right hand proudly displayed the script. 'We've seen your moves do the talking, not it's time for your, well, talking to do the talking.' 'How do you mean?' Kari suspiciously mumbled back, looking to untangle the words to make an honest word. 'Well,' Pham went to explain, 'I think that PRIME would benefit from having its Seven of the Best competitor let the wrestling world know why they're going to bringing the prize home.' 'So...' she mused, seeing where it was going. 'You want me to stand in front of a camera, and talk about beating people who I don't really know well enough to comment?' 'That would be the jist of it,' confirmed her boss, having taken great delight in moulding the K-Wolf into a near-model employee. 'You want me to cut a promo,' she blurted out in mild disgust. 'Oh, yes,' Pham replied, the smile spanning from eat to ear as his hands rubbed together. 'You're going to cut a promo!' He couldn't quite hear what his resistant employee mumbled back under her breath, but it seemed to contain the words: kill, me, and now. With hands on Karina’s shoulders, Pham guided her over to the PRIME backdrop, much like a mother herding her reluctant child through the school gates. With an evil scowl locked and loaded on her face, Karina turned, her gaze threatening to melt the camera lens. 'Just stick to the script,' Pham urged, 'and we'll tell these people why PRIME is #1 by definition.' A loud, unrestrained sigh helped purge some of Karina's frustrations from her system, before she cleared her throat. 'Seven of the best,' Karina began, words dropping like stones from her lips as her eyes seemed to focus on anything but the camera. 'Seven of the best will pit 14 of PTC's best against one another...' From the sidelines, Pham gestured for Karina to perk up as, conducting her pre-scripted speech to accentuate the words. 'For some, this will be the hardest, most gruelling ordeal of their careers,' she continued, a spiteful glare burning into Pham, not that he cared about her unease. 'But being from PRIME, fighting top competition is what we do every single week. When you take on the best wrestling has to offer, the PTC events are mere exhibition matches, something to give us a nice little break.' Her words trailed off, almost disgusted to be reading such a disrespectful piece. Pham, however, seemed proud of his derogatory verbal artistry. 'So, Tsunami Oni, you'd better come with your sunglasses on,' Kari then read, eyes already rolling at the awful pun about to be forced from her lips. 'Because you'll be staring up at some mighty bright lights after a couple of kicks from me.' Off camera, Taun Pham chuckled, almost reaching around to pay himself on the back. 'Oh yeah, that was gold.' 'And to the other six that manage to get through their first round matches,' Kari continued to spew, just grateful that it was coming to an end. 'Get ready to learn that PRIME isn't just the name of the place... it's our standing.' 'Excellent,' Pham applauded as a cut was called. 'A little wooden on the delivery, but that script could make up for anything!' 'I need a shower,' the dejected K-Wolf spluttered, as if she had a nasty taste in her mouth. 'Oh, feel free, you're day's over now,' the beaming Pham congratulated. 'And just remember, as you're washing whatever grime you feel capitalism has left on you... every drop of water, every atom of steam, was paid for by the new line of quality Karina Wolfenden merchandise, available from our website, and all live shows.' His sales pitch left Karina gagging. 'Scratch the shower, I think I’m going to disinfect myself...' Next: Selling Out : Part Three - 'Of Guardian Devils & Crimson Angels.'
