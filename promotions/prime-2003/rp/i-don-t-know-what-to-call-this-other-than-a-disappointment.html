Date: Late. [Note: The following is taken from the hand-scrawled notes of legendary pro wrestling manager Reggie Delray in preparation for the publication of his memoirs. It should be noted, however, that the notes are never turned into an actual manuscript, due to the declining popularity of wrestling-related autobiographies and the absolutely horrendous undertaking that Reggie's handwriting, long-winded storytelling and fractured logic proposed to even the most skilled of editors.] Now any'ne hoo knows a lick 'bout Reggie Delray knows I have a innate 'bility to creayte champi[o]ns outta normal eveyday wrustlers. But it ain't jus as simple as sprinklin that queer dust and poof you got yourself a team. it takes some work, some kneeding and shapeing on the part of the manager, aka yours truely. Off the top a my head, I can name ya one pacific instance where I really had to flex thees mangerialistic muscles. It was something like 2005, an I was still workin' wit Dany Fergeson, an on this caucasian, he's teamin wit a fella named Jimmy Goodbye, or Farewell, or somthin like that, and another guy who I'm pretty sure was runnin the joint at the time. Well these dadgum fools couldn't walk a straight line if I's stapled they feet to a treadmill, if you know what I mean. They ain't on the same page. So this show where they's teamin is jus a few days away, and all I canremember bout they 'ponents is that two of 'em was women. So it shoulda been some kinda walking cake, or whatever the fuck that anal-logue is. Easyer than a ring rat who'd gotten a ride on the Zimbabwe Express, Coco and Dudu, or something like that.. Quick 'side in regads to that - I once saw the Express beat a curtain-jerker to deafth with they...'little tribesman.' Hand to God!!! I think 'little' qualifies for understatement of the year. My point is, they had really big dongs. Anyway, I knews a thing or eight 'bout team wrestling, 'specially the six-man matches. Everyone knows 'bout my work wit the ol' Midnight Train To Georgia, but around the same time the Train and I were tearin' stuff up, I was managing four different three-man teams to the Foggy Valley Wrestling six-man tag championships. That's a lot of math right there, so I don't care what my momma says, I's just as good at numbers as I is at Inglish. Well, you don't just pick any three men up off the street and bingo bango theys a team. So I knew that making three difrent men work t'gether was gonna be a challenge, but it ain't like I's pissin off a new bridge or nethin. Ferg's a Flair, which worked in his A-List unit, 'cause the Chinese guy was an Arn, and the third one...I don't know where he fits. Adrian Street, maybe? Ne'rmind, it's over yor head. But t' problem this time was the boss guy, Nelson, was also a Flair. Farewell was Tully if Tully sucked ass. So maybe he's more like 'Iron' Mike Sharpe. N' you can't jus have too Flairs and some jaberroni who can't carry Flair's jock strap. 'Cause there's two jock straps that need carrying. Wait, that's not how the metofore ends. I lost my place. What was I talkin 'bout? And why am I still writin' when I lost my place? So we held our first trainin' seshun at his gym Nelson bought. He didn' just rent it, he bought it! Sold it two days later 'fter he repainted and remodeled it! I told him I charged for mangerialist fees!!! We got the tree men in the ring with a couple of trainin dummies to work on. Plus we had an extradummie on the outside 'cause that Farewell guy brought his partner, who kept wearin' a crown and talkin' bout his wookie. 'Now you're used to having a kind of expert chemistry, right?' I asks to Farewell, on account of his partner sittin' right there with him. 'I don't think I've been more disconnected from another human being in my entire life,' he says. Ain't that some shit!!! So I asks him, 'Well then how do you explain the giant gold 'tag team' belts that you and your buddy brought in here with you.' 'Pfft,' says his patner. 'I let him carry that.' 'Bullshit,' Mr. Aloha says. Then he turns to me and just kinda shrugs. 'Blind squirrel finds a nut eventually.' 'Hey, not everyone plans to spend the rest of the day cursing their existence,' Ferg shoutd, point'ng at a clock. 'Can we get this show on the road?' So we get down to the practiss, and I gotta tell you, I was impressed with this Sayonara guy. No, not the Chinese one. The tag team guy. He knew his stuff in the ring. I ain't seen a man flow like that since ol' Dickie Suede retired in '67. And even Dickie had his problems, mostly on account of the alcohol and the childhood Parkinsons. Man couldn't drink a can a soda t' save his life. But likw I as sayin, this Farewell guy knew his stuff in the ring. Nelson could hold hishown, too. Te problem, as always, was Dany. He throws a punch like a monkey baseball picther, and I ain't talkin' bout one of them afro-mericans, so don't go round saying Reggie Delray is a raciss. Now there is plenty of men who couldn't get their techneek down an found ways round it. Gogeous George was one, and Horace Hogan was nother. But Dany made those men look like Lou Tesz. He usea coplain to me an his partners 'bout how he was an actor first and a wrestler secon. I used to tell him he shouldn't quit the day job!!! Anyway, Nelson's tryin ta ignore that Ferg works about as good as a wet turd when this Farewell character suddenly speaks up. 'And how are we supposed to win with that kind of ring work?' 'Excuse me?' Dany demanded, puttin' on this 'ready for a fight' look that he always used to signal his bodyguards. 'When was the last time YOU won a tag team match?' 'Uh, hello?' Farewell pointed over at his partner. I guess he'd been expecting the boy to hold up one of them shiny belts they won, but istead he was trapped under a barbell with 5-pound waits at eether end. Wasn't quite the visy'all he wanted. 'That would've been a lot cooler if he wasn't an idiot.' 'Bet you say that a lot,' Nelson says, and Farlwell nodded. 'Sure, but that doesn't mean he's any worse of a partner than you, sandbag,' he sad to Dany. 'What did you do, rent the Ultimate Warrior DVD before practice?' Dany lunged at him - quickest he'd moved all mornin' - but Nelson held 'im back, and I finally got involved. 'Gentlemen,' I says, 'I think it's about time that we ceased comparing our manlihoods and began preparation for the task at hand, which will prove quite formidable.' Ain't much to say 'bout the trainin. I mean, any man who knows a red cent 'bout the multi-man tag strategy knows what I had to do to get two Flairs and a potential Tully at championship cal'ber. We started out with the Boxcar Tambourine tekneek with a little bit of the Wandering Mongoose for Nelson, on account of him bein' the team captain. Then we spent a good three hours on the Lazy Susan, layed the foundation of the Yellow Submarine, and touched very lightly on the Plumber's Helper. You know, really rudimentry stuff. And we topped off the session with some good old three-man double teams. I intodoosed them to some classics, like the Asbestos Raygun, the Spinning Yugo and the Three-Foot Reverse Counterflip Thumbtack Special. Somewhere 'long the way, Farewell got fed up wit Dany's absolutely horrible work and started teachin' hisself. He show'd Fergs how to throw some real punches, how to use a sleeper as something other than a rest hold, and even taught the boy a suplex or two. An' first Dany was piss about it, but then he started warmin' up to the idea, 'specially after Nelson said somethin to him. Then they both became pretty intersted about Farewell's little teachin' clinic, even though they kept shooting looks over at Farewell's partner guy, who was still caught under that barbell and had fallen asleep. 'Say,' Dany says at one point, 'what do you say about makin' this more than just a one-time partnership?' Farewell stopped showin the move, and consecwenshally dropped some poor kid on the mat. 'I knew you'd pull this again.' 'Hey now, I can't be responsible for what the Chan Man says,' is what Dany replied. Parently his Korean friend had made some sort of pitch to Farewell in the past. 'But I can up the deal a little.' 'What am I now? Associate? Senior Advisor? Wrestling Maintenance Engineer?' 'Partner.' Farewell didn't say nothin, and Nelson just smiled while Dany kept talkin. 'We could use a fourth man, a...what'd you call it, D-Bag? Tooly?' 'Tully,' I said, shakin' my head and cursin to no one in particlar. 'Yeah, a Tully. The kind of wrestling mind to keep the rest of us on top when our star power gets us there. And you could use a new partner if you want to retain those tag titles beyond defense one. So I'm saying that if you train with me, you teach me how to beat your royal retard friend, the Columbo of professional wrestling, then I can challenge him for his two belts and win. Then you and I are the tag champs, I've got the big belt and the money and fame keeps rolling in.' Nelson was pretty happy with the suggestion, 'cause it was probably his idea. Farewell looked like he was considering it. He kept lookin' back and forth between his boy and Dany. 'So I should just give up on Clyde?' 'Come on, man - he's a midcarder with a lucky streak. You've got the makings of a bright young star!' 'I'm 47.' 'Forget the young part.' Farewell thunk some more. On the bench, his patner was snorin louder 'n my grandma when she had that nasal drip problem. After a while, he smile nd turned back to Dany. 'So I should drop Clyde because he's a dimwit whose professional ambitions aim much higher than where he actually belongs on the card, and whose ego inflates to epic proportions at even the slightest success, and whose perception of reality is sow skewed it's laughable?' 'That pretty much covers it, yeah.' 'Then explain to me how you're an improvement?' That's exactly how 't happened, I sware. You know, give er take a cuple lines. I ain't had the most photografic memry since my accident wit flash paper and the bottle rocket back at SuperCade '82, when the Midnight Train to Georgia won the CWA Tag Team Championships for the 32nd time. In any event, Id always been scrawlin my life down in prepration for this here autobografy, so I made sure to get my facts straight. Farewell got up and left the ring. 'Offer's still on the table,' Dany shouted after him. 'That means there's still time for me to come back and piss on it,' he snapped back, before shaking his partner on the bench and pulling that barbell off his chest wit one tug. The dude with the crown quickly sat and stretchd like a girl. 'You saved me!' 'You were asleep.' 'Asleep from a lack of oxygen to the brain!' 'That never stopped you before.' 'You saved this fair maiden from eternal slumber, and now I am forever indebted to you!' The partner grabbed Farewell by the arm like he was being excorted, and the Farewell guy stopped in his tracks. 'I'll murder you. Right here in front of everyone. I don't care about witnesses, because then I can go to jail and have several feet of cement and barbed wire to protect me from your reanimated corpse when you try to come back and continue harassing me from beyond the grave.' 'Have you been reading my diary?' Them two jus walked away then, and left Nelson and Dany to finish up the session. It was the typical Wabash Screwlily, a real 5-6 Dumptruck Gambit. We'd dropped one partner and had to make do with the two leftover, then hope the whole thing didn't collapse on itself in the middle of the match. I ain't been involved in an imploding six-man team since the summer of 1979 when Dick Suede was betrayed by the Mega Crusher and 'Thunderbutt' Tom Baskerville in the span of five minutes. Not pretty, let me tells you, and I wasn't about to let that happen again!!! Wit my help, and one of the punk kids filling in as Farewell, we reorganized the strategy a little and got Nelson an Dany working together. We wouldn't be t' prettiest team on the mat that night, but it was better'n nothing. 'Ventially, Dany left to go film something about deadly weapons, and Nelson went to...I dunno, do what rich people do. I stuck around wit the drawing board to work up some new strategies for the match. Plus the gym Nelson bought had a bathroom, and I didn't feel like payin' for a hotel.
