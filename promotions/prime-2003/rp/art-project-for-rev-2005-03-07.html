Art project (for ReV)

7 March 2005

Date: 3/7/05 “Yo, Michael? You around here somewhere man?” We can barely hear the tall, lanky figure’s shout over a loud rhythmic clanking noise as he makes his way through what appears to be some sort of junkyard. Towers of scrapped cars and useless appliances climb to the sky as the man makes his way through the labyrinthine passages between them. He cups his hands to his face and shouts again. “MIKE! WHERE ARE YOU, MAN?!” The clanking noise stops as a faint shout is heard in response. “Back here in the workshop, come on back!” The clanking noise starts up again as the sandy blond-haired man navigates his way through the maze of twisted metal into a clearing. We see the Trashcan Man, clad in jeans, t-shirt, orange gloves, and leather apron standing next to a furnace slamming a sledge hammer down onto a length of red hot metal. Sweat-sodden bandages are wrapped around his head and throat from the barbed wire strangulation he endured at last week’s ReVolution. He pauses to wipe the sweat from his face, a bright red from the heat of the open furnace next to him. He slams the hammer down on the glowing steel again, sparks flying up into his face, but in typical TCM fashion, he ignores it. He lifts up ths steel, frowning at it for a moment before tossing into a barrel of water. Steam flies out in a huge cloud as the Trashcan Man tosses the hammer aside in frustration. “Damn, if medieval blacksmiths could do it, why can’t I?” The lanky figure steps into the clearing with a chuckle. “They probably had some sort of Vo-Tech they went to back then, Mike.” Trashy turns with a quick grin to the man striding towards him and steps forward, hand extended. “Hey, Jason, didn’t know you were coming over! What brings you by?” They shake hands warmly, smiles lighting both of their faces. “I called your home but got your machine Mike, so I thought you’d be here. I was just seeing how you were doing after that match you had last week. Man that was brutal!” Trashy chuckles as he steers Jason into a large wooden shed. The flaking paint on the outside masks the well lit interior. Racks of shelves hold various well maintained tools, and a huge table dominates the center of the room. Papers are scattered all over the table’s nicked and scarred surface lacking anything resembling organization. “So Liz let you watch it, eh? She wasn’t upset that I bought you that year’s subscription to the Playboy channel?” Jason rolls his eyes for a moment. “She was a bit upset at first, but she knew why you did it. Then she started watching some of the other… programming.” His eyes light up as he grins like a cat who’s found the cream. “She’s been a bit more frisky lately. I’m a bit wore out, to tell you the truth.” Both men laugh as the hardcore legend makes his way to a small fridge in the corner. “Jolt?” he offers, holding up a bottle of his favorite hypercaffienated beverage. Jason shudders in response. “No thanks, Mike. I can’t see how you drink that stuff. Got any Diet Coke in there?” Trash bends over as he rummages through the fridge. “Yeah… I think I’ve got a few cans left of that six pack you brought over last month… here ya go.” He tosses a can at Jason who catches it smoothly. He pops the top as Trashy opens his bottle and both men drink. Jason idly leafs through some of the papers on the table in front of him. “Hey… this is nice! This your next project, man?” Trashy looks over at him. “The angel? Yeah, I’ve been trying to make a sword for him, but can’t get it to come out right yet. I’ll work on it later.” Jason nods appreciatively. “If it comes out like the draft you’ve got here, it’s going to be great. Very moving… I like how the barbed wire clings to his legs as he’s trying to fly off.” The hardcore legend shrugs. “Won’t have as much time to work on this stuff now that I’m working again.” He chuckles. “I think the world of art can do without my work for awhile, though.” Shaking his head, Jason puts the sketch back down. “Don’t sell your work short, Mike. Some of your stuff’s starting to sell for some pretty decent prices for the kind of work you do. You’re starting to get noticed by the Chicago art scene.” “Yeah… and they turn right around after finding out who I am.” Trashy grins. “Me just big dumb wrestler. What me know of art? I think they expect me to stick with fingerpainting or something.” Jason chuckles as he takes another swig from his Diet Coke. “Where’s the next show going to be?” Trashy fishes in his pocket for a crumpled pack of Malboro Lights. “Detroit.” He shakes one out and lights it with his trusty battered silver Zippo. Closing it, he leans back against the wall and takes a deep drag, slowly puffing out a cloud of smoke. “Gonna be a good show. This’ll be the first time I’ve tagged in awhile.” Jason frowns for a moment. “With that Kano guy, right?” At Trashy’s nod, he continues. “Didn’t you used to go up against him in PCW?” The pyromaniac shrugs. “That was waaaaay back in the day, when Tyler Nelson was running the show. Kano was working for him as a hired thug, pretty low rent too. He’s gotten better since then. I don’t think he’s going to try anything against me if that’s what you’re worried about. He’s got his sights firmly set on Boda. Los Diablos is pretty much someone to take out some aggression on, I think. I just hope he actually tags me in and lets me have a few shots beforehand.” He chuckles. “Kano always did have kind of a hot temper. But man does he ROCK~!” Chuckling himself, Jason checks his watch. “Wow… took longer to get here than I thought. I’ve got to get going.” He looks up at the hardcore legend. “Main reason I came over was to see if you were going to be at this week’s meeting?” A concerned expression crosses his face. “You haven’t been to one in a few weeks. Everything ok?” Trash nods slowly. “Yeah, I’m sorry about that. Things have been real busy lately, I haven’t had the time. But I promise to be there next week. I’ve got some promotional thing here in Chicago the PRIME brass want me to do, but after that I’m free all evening. So I’ll be there. Don’t worry about me, I’m holdin’ up fine.” Jason smiles. “I figured you were, I just wanted to make sure. Call me if you think you’ll make it to this week’s meeting, ok?” The Trashcan Man nods. “Will do, man. Give my love to Liz.” “Of course. Thanks for the soda, man. I’ll see you next week” With that, Jason walks out the door as Trash puffs away on his cigarette. I TOLD you she’d get into the hot girl on girl action if you bought Jay that subscription! Trashy laughs at Mr Smiley Face’s internal comment as we fade to black.
