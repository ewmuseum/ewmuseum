Missed connections (ReV 112, JiTC Round 2)

23 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/11/06 It was one of those mornings that began in the afternoon as Adam rolls, bleary-eyed, out of bed and falls straight to the floor, landing hard on his side. "Fuck…" After getting up slowly, he wanders over to the mini-bar, his head aching. He pulls the door open and relishes the cool air that flows out and over his sweaty face as he exhales; long and slow. Opening his eyes after the moment’s respite from the dull throb of his hangover he sighs – the vain hope of milk being left in there, post-White Russians, was dashed. In fact, the whole thing was cleared out, leaving the tiny bin provided in the second-rate hotel room filled with empty miniature bottles. "Fuck…" Holding his head between his hands Adam tries to sit on the edge of the bed but slides clean off and lands on his ass, hard. "Fuck!" He runs his hands through his hair as he returns to his feet, then stumbles through to the bathroom. After a struggle to find the light (why the fuck do they put the switch on one of those cord things anyway?) he finally grabs hold and pulls, blinding himself temporarily as the shiny white room, all tiles and mirrors, illuminates. "Oh Jesus…" Shedding the little clothing he was still wearing after tumbling into bed at Christ-knows-what time this morning, he steps into the shower and turns the heat up near-full before cranking up the power. "Ahhh…" ---- Half an hour under the water, an extended 4pm brunch and more than a couple hairs of the dog and Adam was officially awake now. Returning to his room he lifts the receiver on the room phone and gets himself an outside line. "Hello?… Yeah, I’d like to speak to Ms. Katy Goodhart, please… She’s an in-patient, yes… Yes, I know this treatment is meant to be centred in the, well, the centre but I can’t make it to the visiting times… No, I’m in a completely different state. I’d just like ten minutes to speak to her and see how she’s doing… No, I want to hear her tell me how she’s doing – what she feels, not what some nurse’s report says…" He reaches across to the bedside table and grabs a biscuit, shoving it in his mouth whole before trying to keep the crunching sounds to a minimum as he chews through it. "Mmhmm… mmhmm… Yuh, shuh… Mmm…" He swallows and looks round for anything else edible in the room, the three courses he’d just devoured an hour ago not leaving him quite as fulfilled as he’d hoped. "Well, technically I’m not a family member bu-… No, hold on a se-… Listen, I’m he-… Hello?... Hello?!" Lifting the phone away from his ear he stares at the receiver with incredulity, seriously not believing that bitch had just hung up on him. Shaking his head he clicks the base to end the call. He lifts his finger off the button to speak to reception again, getting himself another line to the Azure Acres Recovery Centre. "Yeah, hi – you just cut me off… No, I know that but I’m he-…Listen to me, for just one minute, please?... Legally I’m her ex-husband, but I’m the father of her children, we’re "co-habiting" again and I’m the one paying for her treatment, surely that …" He trails off and listens sombrely, his eyes fixed on the far wall, nodding slightly once or twice. "…I understand, bu-… No, I know, but can’t I… Are you sure? There’s no way…?" He sighs, closing his eyes and dropping his head so his chin nearly rests on his chest. When he speaks again his voice is quiet, taking on a solemn, resigned tone. "Well, thank you anyway." Allowing the receiver to rest on his shoulder Adam just sits motionless for a while, his eyes looking toward the floor, but remaining completely unfocussed on it or anything else around him. Finally he lifts the handset up and replaces it on the cradle. ---- Three hours later Adam staggers back up the stairs having drunk away first his hangover then the sheer anger and disbelief of his call to the Rehab clinic. A brief struggle with the key in his door is ended with a swift shoulder, tearing the lock out in the process. With a delicate push the door is closed and the Scot drunkenly lurches his way across to the phone again for another outside line. "Hullo?... Aye, it’s Adam, likes; how my weans doin’?" He sits down on the edge of the bed then falls back to lie flat on his back, his eyes slowly closing over as he continues. "Naw, ah’m no drunk – jist hud a few… Naw, seriously, likes… Oh, listen, Jean… Please – jist pit ’em on, ah’ve hud enough shite wi’ the cunts at the Rehab place the day…" His voice, thick with his native accent with all the drink in him, is slurred as he drifts in and out of full consciousness – his indignation at his sister-in-law not letting him speak to his kids about the only thing keeping him awake. "Jean, please – ah niver goat tae speak tae Katy, likes, they wouldnae let us cos I’m no technically family, likes… It cannae be that late, is it? Naw…" He stirs enough to lean over and look at the digital alarm clock sitting by his bed. "S’only hauf eight, likes – thur no in bed noo, surely… Aww, fuck, timezones… Whit time’s it therr, likes?" "Sir? Are you alright in there, sir?" "Aw, haud oan on a sec, Jean, some cunts fae the hotel ur hasslin’ us." He somehow manages to rouse himself from the near slumber he’d slipped into, swaying slightly as he gets to a vertical base. He walks across the room, giving the handset a yank that tears the phone clean from the wall socket when he meets some resistance from the cord. "Ah’m awright, aye, jist comin’…" He pulls on the broken door, letting it swing open to reveal the security guards ready to deal with a forced entry break-in. "Aye, sorry aboot yer door, lads – ma key widnae work, likes – jist gied it a wee shouder an’ it popped open, it’s cool likes." He smiles as sweetly as he can before he returns his attention to the phone. "Aye, Jean… Jean, y’therr?" "Sir, did you just say you broke down the door?" "Jean?! Let us speak to ma kids, ya bitch!" "Sir! What’s happening here?" Adam, looking ready to topple any second, cradles the receiver on his shoulder and turns back to face the guards, speaking to the one closest to him who’s reached a hand out to help steady the Scot. "Aye, it’s nae problem here, see ah was jist-" He doesn’t finish his sentence, instead opting to snap his head forward in a vicious headbutt that sends the guard reeling back into the wall behind him. Instantly his partner leaps on the Scot, pinning him to the ground easily while calling for back-up on his radio – Adam screaming all the while into the phone, almost oblivious to the guards until a well-placed swift elbow ‘neutralises’ him. ---- Near midnight, sobering up and feeling the effects of an elbow to the skull, Adam manages to get himself checked in a by-the-hour motel in an ever-so-slightly less salubrious district of town. The still-drying blood caking under his nose and marking out a wide crimson triangle on his T-shirt are completely unnoticed and/or overlooked at the check-in desk. "Cheers." Adam takes the key slid across the counter to him then heads round to his room, planning to liberate his luggage from his first choice hotel the next day. As soon as he locks the door, once inside, Adam sits on the bed and takes his wallet from his pocket. He rifles through until he unearths a crumpled sheet of notepaper with a number scrawled on it, tucked away in the very safest depths. Handset lifted, number dialled. "Oh, come on… please… answer…" With an expression nearing pain on his face Adam anxiously waits; just as his eyes begin to close in defeat his face lights up, a smile of sheer relief spreading across his face. "Iggy? Shit, man – I really need someone now… I- I just can’t deal with this life any more; Katy’s in Rehab, the kids are at her sisters – I haven’t spoken to any of them in so long, I’m just… alone." He sighs, dropping his head and rubbing the bridge of his nose. "I mean, just having someone to talk to on the road would help – I know things weren’t exactly peaches and cream when it was the two of us, but at least… yeah, that’s it exactly… Oh, fuck man – I just can’t deal with it; why is life so fucked up for us all? Did we just choose a shitty profession?" He looks up despondently, staring at the yellowed wallpaper and stained carpet. "Does everyone who lives on the road deal with this shit or are we an especially fucked up breed?... Seems everyone has something happening outside the sport, week-to-fucking-week, that affects them – does anyone have it easy?...Yeah, I s’pose… But, really – is it worth it? Is this job; the life, the gold, the chances, this fucking tournament… is it worth feeling this alone and isolated?... Iggy?" Adam falls from the bed, dropping to his knees and quietly sobbing as the phone falls form his hand and dangles on its cord, rhythmically banging against the beside table. As it hangs the voice at the other end of the line speaks again; "This number is no longer operational; the owner has now been disconnected, please try dialling again… This number is no longer operational…"
