Sitting here in this jail sucks (Breakup part II)

4 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/4/05 'Officer please escort Mr. Vetra into holding cell six.' The police sargent who is standing behind the counter which has just taken all my stuff told the officer who was dragging me around by my cuffs. There I stood as he told the officer to do this, my hands behind my back, all of my belongs in a plastic bag. Is this the end for me? I don't know but it surely seems like it. 'Let's go!' The officer who held me by the cuffs said abruptly and started dragging me to my cell. I quickly picked up the walking pace as he was hurting me as he pulled. Walking by five other cells, all of which were empty except for cell number one. The man inside was obviously drunk and a bit psychotic, as I walked I thought to myself do I really belong here? Speaking of being drunk my buzz had worn off in the last couple of hours as I waited for the bull shit to calm down. We passed cell block five and I knew the next stop for me was the small room. The officer opened the door and preceeded to take off the cuffs, once they were off I was pushed inside the cell where I hit the back wall and then saw the door slammed behind me. There I stood in a five by five square cell nothing, it was like staring at emptiness. The mono colored walls were mind numbing, the steel bed was the worst of it all, then I saw what was supposed to be a bathroom type section, that was just disgusiting, an aluminum toilet and an aluminum sink, not appealing at all. I took a seat on the bench that they called a bed and wondered how long am I going to be here? Staring at the walls I sat there thinking to myself what had happened this evening, I sat on the steel bench which was cold and thought of my actions; did this really need happen. I started to lean back on the steel bed to try and catch some sleep, it really wasn't happening, so I took of one of my shirts and used it as a make shift pillow, this will have to work for now... I slowly started to sit up, I wiped my face with my hands not knowing how long I was out for but still in a state of disbelief. I sat up and walked over to the window where I could see into the hallway, nothing was happening the bright flourecent lights gave me no idea of the time of day, there I stood alone and confused. I couldn't take looking out there anymore so I turned around and sat down on 'my bed' cupping my face in my hands. I just sit there thinking to myself... The four walls seem to be closing in, the mono colored walls are enough to drive any sane man insane. This should be the furthest thing from on my mind but it is hard to worry about anything else when all I can see is walls and flourecent lighting, I guess I can't forget about that security camera up in the corner making sure I am not killing myself or something. That right now is probably a feasible idea, but what would it do to everyone else? I mean sure I don't want to live but there are some people who actually want me to live. That is just not a good idea nor is it one I plan on acting out. So until then I need to just sit here not sure of what is going to happen to me and take it as it comes. That is almost impossible. I need something to take my mind of what is happening around me, this crazy man in the first cell who seems to have some sort of multi person disorder is not helping, but you know I have seen I never want to be here again, nor will I ever come back. I wil die before I return to this cell. I placed my hands in my lap no longer cupping them around my face, I looked up at the camera, nothing it doesn't move it doesn't make a sound all I hear is the noises I make, nothing else, complete silence. Not knowing what time it is coming up on I decided to lay down and try and get a little more sleep before I go further insane. If that is possible I guess you could say. I layed down on the steel bed took my makeshift pillow and closed my eyes, I started spinning in delirious circles til I feel asleep, this is the only place my mind was not going to drive me insane. What seemed like only ten minutes I awoke again tossing and turning the steel beds are not the same as my Serta at home. There I layed on the bed looking around the room at what was going on trying to figure out what was going to happen to me. It was not worth sitting here trying to figure out what was going to happen to me cause I had no control over it, it was her word over my word, bullshit is what it was. You have to love the American judicial system. There I laid on the bed thinking to myself again. I finally I had got ride of the depression and now all this shit happens, how am I going to react to this? I am not sure but the truth is I probably slip back into depression, showing the world what type of useless piece of trash I am. But what I am going to do with my life? Now I am going to be stuck in this jail not knowing what is going to happen to me, what is going to happen to Melissa, I know for sure that I never want to see her again but did what she say really happen or is it just some bullshit story she is telling people that is getting a little over exageratted. The time will come where she is revealed as the fraud she actually is. How is it possible she can be someone who made me so happy and with in hours she has made me so angry that I can not even think of talking to her again. She has done complete opposites on me and and now I am alone and depressed. But the real question is do I really need her? I sat up as the steel bed made my back hurt I look around the jail cell again wondering what would happen to me and everyone I loved. Then I noticed something on the far wall a telephone, I was not sure if I even wanted to try and contact anyone, was it worth it? Or was everyone I call going to yell at me cause at this point and time I was not ready for someone to just yell at me all night long. The first option was obviously the one I wanted to happen but I had a feeling most likely anyone I call right will just yell at me for hours. So there I sat thinking of many of different topics usually not any of them very nice, but finally something came into my head, the thought of my employment. PRIME wrestling a place who has been with me through out all of the different happenings in the last year of my life. I am sure they are going to be with me on this one. Then I just tried to think of happy thoughts, it didn't last to long as I started thinking of Jackson Darilek and the title I should be owning. PRIMEtime Title, the belt also know as Alias Title which should be around my waist as we speak, I was robbed and over and over again do I get robbed by the man. That is all well and fine as I think the time has come my built up aggression from this evenings events makes him a perfect target for all of the unchecked aggresion. I am sorry for him as this is not his fault but what does have to happen will happen and I rather take it out on him in the ring then out on someone else at the bar where I guess I could get arrested again, and like I have said before I am not coming back here, there will be people dead before my face ever sees a cell again. I took another long look at the phone should I try and call someone? But who would I call? I just laid back down on my bed wondering what time is it and stared at the mono colored walls and ceiling so boring, so mind numbing, now I can see why people hate jail.
