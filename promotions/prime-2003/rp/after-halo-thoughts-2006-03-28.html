After Halo Thoughts

28 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3.28 It’s been three weeks since anything has happened in PRIME. After the grueling Duel Halo what do you expect? Do you honestly think we were going to see another show right away? I mean we had people injured, we had others almost killed. Most people in the match we in there for a long time, the longest was over 83 minutes that is rather impressive. But then there were those who weren’t in for long at all, I mean look at Chris Collins I don’t think he even had a second actually in the Halo. Then of course we had those in the middle, where I was. I was in the match for just a hair over 35 minutes, now that isn’t much of an accomplishment, that actually should be considered as a defeat, one of which I’m by no means proud of. Going into this I felt like I had a shot, did I think I was going to come out the winner, hell no. However I expected to have a better showing, I expected to stand up and fight defend myself, but once again I let myself down, I’m not the wrestler I have aspired to be, but guess what? I am who I am and I am not about to change after twenty six years of being who I am. Now wasn’t I really bothered by my performance in the Duel Halo, the people who were expected to rise above the rest and perform did, and those like myself who were expected to show up and fail did as well. But my match with Illustrious Face Eater, Adam Dick is a completely different story. I was hoping to show him what I was really made of, I wanted to prove to him and the rest of the PRIME family I’m not the Bob Backlund or the Brooklyn Brawler of PRIME. I wanted to show my worth. I think that is precisely what I did, did I win? No, I didn’t winning wasn’t the only thing on my mind when that match was taking place, was it something I heavily wanted to happen? Of course but my mind president was none the less come out of that match with my head high, showing everyone I can compete, I am not just the walk over. Guess what I proved it, ask Illustrious Face Eater, he was expecting an easy win, he expected to land a punch or two and the match to be over. But the truth is that is far from the truth. I gave him a fight three weeks ago, and in due time I will give him a fight again. Don’t think I am just ending my quest for Pulse Gold here, don’t expect you have seen the last of Peter Vetra, cause that is not the truth, I will be back Adam, I will conquer my goal. My goal of having that Pulse Gold off your waist and once again around mine, time is in my favor Adam, I shall achieve it in time.
