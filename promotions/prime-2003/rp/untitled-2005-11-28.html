Untitled

28 November 2005

Date: 11-28-05 'Where’s my title?” Chris Collins had been asking that same question for days now. Ever since he’d been nailed with that Twilight Press a few ReVolutions ago, that question has continued to plague his mind. “Where is my Pulse Championship?” He’d ask. “Where, where, where?” There was only one answer to his question. …The Illustrious Face Eater. The name had brought a bad taste to his mouth. It hasn’t really been stuck in his head, but it would occasionally pop up throughout the day -- and when it did it would intensify him--. It even hurt him to look in the mirror. He just couldn’t accept the person that he was anymore? In fact, he didn’t even know who he was anymore. Who would? Losing to Adam Dick? Talk about low. “…Bitch.” He muttered in a low tone underneath his breath. Chris knew he was better than the Face Eater. If memory served him correctly, Dick was the same person he pinned to the mat just a few months ago in Core Wrestling for the Classic Championship. But that didn’t matter to him anymore. Core Wrestling. Pfft… Fuck Core Wrestling? Who needed them anyway? Chris wouldn’t have had the mentality to hate Core if this were just a few months ago. There was a time in his life when cW was his world. His pride and joy, and it were the only thing that would bring him happiness. Ever since he’d been released, he hated them. Oh well. You build bridges and you burn them. Besides, PRIME’s better than Core Wrestling… Honest! Wednesday everything was going to change. It was all going to come back to him in a big rush. The Adrenaline was going to flow through his veins again giving him a rush like no other. No longer would he lose to resident PRIME jobbers like Cimmerian and Chandler Tsonda –not that either man has beaten him-. No longer would he be wrestling at the bottom of the barrel in the promotion that he loved so much. Was he excited? I guess you could say that. He wanted the ‘Innovator of Offense’ inside of him to come back alive, but he wasn’t eager. Hell, it was just another ReVolution. Sure, he wanted to win. But, not THAT bad. He wasn’t going to die, or kill over it. Well, maybe just a few people.1) Illustrious Face Eater 2) Chandler Tsonda 3) Peter Vetra 4) etc. Okay, maybe a bunch of people. Why these guys? Simple, Collins hated them all. Illustrious Face Eater for being a dick and winning Chris Collins’ belt without his permission. Really, the Face Eater has been a thorn in Collins’ side from day one. There was just something about him that he didn’t like. Chandler Tsonda for getting in his way. Peter Vetra… well Peter Vetra has just plain sucked since day one. Talk about a waste of space. As well as everyone else that Chris hates. It’ll happen sooner or later.
