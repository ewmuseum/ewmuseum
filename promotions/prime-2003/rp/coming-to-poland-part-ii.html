Coming to Poland (Part II)

Date: Various Date: June 20, 2007 12:40 PM Location: Walczak’s Home Gdansk, Poland Walczak lies sound asleep on his king sized bed, his feet hanging off the edge. Even though it is past noon the sounds of Walczak’s snoring fill the room. He seems like he could sleep through the rest of the day, but then DING DONG The doorbell somewhat awakens Walczak making him stir around in his bed, but he doesn’t get out to make his way for the door. DING DONG DING DONG This time a succession of rings causes Walczak to lift himself to a seated position, but still cannot lure the big man out of bed. He returns back to his side this time covering his head with a pillow to avoid any impending noise. A couple seconds pass, and it seems that whoever was at the door gets the clue. DING DONG DING DONG DING DONG An endless stream of ringing now enters the house, and Walczak has had enough. He throws his covers off, puts on a t-shirt and walks angrily down the stairs. “I’M COMING STOP RINGING THE FUCKING DOORBELL!!!” Walczak’s eyes are filled with rage as he nears the door. He rips the door open fiercely, and sees a rather small man standing in front of him. The man wears a sharp navy pinstripe suit, with a white shirt underneath, and looks like a beating is coming his way courtesy of Walczak. However, Walczak just squints his eyes looking the man up and down. He focuses on the man’s hair which is loaded with blond highlights, and then looks up and down at the well groomed man in front of him. “Andrzej?” “Who the fuck you think it was?” A smile immediately appears on the face of Walczak and he sticks his hand out to the man bringing him in for a friendly embrace. The anger that had previously filled the head of Walczak has since dissipated, and he is overjoyed at seeing his old friend. “Come in Andrzej, do you want anything to drink?” “Yeah let me get some water.” “Water!? Water!? I haven’t seen you in over three years and you want a water? What happened you grow a pussy or something?” “No, you see I am not here for pleasure, I actually have to work make a living.” Walczak shakes his head in joyful disapproving of his friend’s choice. He heads into the kitchen as Andrzej makes himself comfortable on Walczak’s couch. A couple of seconds later Walczak reappears a bottle of water in one hand, and a protein shaker in the other. “You’re giving me shit, and you come back with that.” “I figured that if you weren’t gonna have some fun then I wouldn’t indulge myself, but if you’re so busy what brings you out here to see me?” “Well, I heard a rumor that you were back in town, and not incarcerated, and I just had to come here and see if it was true.” “Yeah, I should be coming back here more often now. I just can’t deal with being in the states for long periods of time, the shit gets to me.” “Can’t hack it in the US I see how it is.” “Fuck You.” The two men share a laugh, talking as if they haven’t been apart for more than a couple of days. Date: November 18, 1984 Location: Swiete Jadwigi Sieroniec The scene looks like that of any other school cafeteria. However, this is not just a school cafeteria, it is in fact the cafeteria of an orphanage. Outside of that the differences are not great. The room is filled with the chattering voices talking about their favorite subjects, sports, television, toys, and such. It is also easily seen that the kids sitting at the tables are formed into cliques. The popular boys sit with the popular girls, the smart kids sit with the smart kids, and the misfits sit with other misfits. However, there is one table where only one child sits. He eats his sandwich not saying a world, and not looking around at anything that goes on around him, a true outcast in a home for outcasts. We take a closer look at the boy and while he is young there is no mistake that this is Tom Walczak. As he takes a bite out of his sandwich he is hit in the back of the head with some foreign object. He looks around, but has no clue where the object came from, but quickly deduces it came from the popular kids as the table erupts in laughter. The laughter quickly spreads to the other tables as everyone enjoys partaking in the humiliation of Walczak. Walczak gets up and faces all those who laugh at him. “Who threw that?” “What does it matter to you? Freak.” An older boy stands up and answers Walczak. He has shaggy blonde hair, and is dressed fairly well for a boy in an orphanage. He has patches of hair around his face, and looks as he has yet to shave the first time showing his growth towards manhood. As he walks away from the table he is patted on the back by his friends, and a girl, who we can only assume is his girlfriend, roots him on as she stairs at him dreamily. “You got a problem with me throwing things at you?” “Not just you. I am sick of being picked on by everyone, I just want to stay to myself and be left alone.” Without saying another word the boy charges at Walczak. Walczak quickly moves out of the way and the boy charges into the table. As the boy backs away from the table the children form a circle around the fight hoping not to miss any of the action. “You should have just stayed put. Now I’m gonna make you pay for making me look stupid.” The boy once again charges at Walczak, but this time Walczak gets underneath him taking him down to the ground. He starts to unload fists sloppily towards the boy not connecting with many. The boys friends have seen enough, and they tackle Walczak off the boy, but before they can attack the sisters make there way into the center of the circle. They pull boys off the pile until they get to Walczak. “Tomasz! You are always getting into trouble. I have had enough of this, now get back to your table and don’t cause anymore problems. We will discuss your punishment later.” “But I…” “I don’t want to hear it. Now get back to your table and finish your lunch quietly.” Walczak bows his head once again being blamed for something he didn’t cause, but he is somewhat used to it by now. For some reason he has always been the odd one out in the orphanage, no friends, no family, even the sisters didn’t like him. As he got back to the table he pushed his sandwich away not hungry anymore after all that had just transpired. He sulks keeping his head down, and just waiting for the bell to ring signifying that lunch is over. Then on the opposite side of the table a boy sits down. “I want to be left alone, I don’t want to get into anymore trouble, so you can just go back to your friends and tell them to leave me alone.” “Those kids? No they’re not my friends. I’m new here.” “Well than what are you doing here? I sit alone I always have, and I’m kind of used to it by now.” “I’m the same way. My parents just got taken away. They told me they were taken away to work, but I know it was the Russians. Now I’m stuck here nowhere else to go.” “I’m not gonna make you go away, but you might not wanna sit here no one really likes me.” “No one really likes me either, so I think this seat will be ok.” Walczak lets out a slight laugh and looks across at the boy seeing his face for the first time. He sees the face of the first friend he ever had. While all those in Poland love Walczak now, it was not always the case. He used to be picked on just like he is in America, but when he became friends with Andrzej it was the first good piece of luck he had in his life, and he has thanked him for it ever since. Date: June 20, 2007 12:54 PM Location: Walczak’s Home Gdansk, Poland The two men are still sitting in Walczak’s living room. They seem to talk about old times as constant “Remember when’s” and “What about the time’s” are heard over and over again. Walczak finishes up his protein shake putting the empty mixer on the coffee table, and then again engages his friend in conversation. “Enough talking about the past, what are you up to now? You look all dapper, you some kind of big business man?” “I don’t know about big, but I did open up my own business about two years back.” “Really, so you’re some sort of hotshot tycoon now. What is it you do?” “Well, I had worked construction my whole life, and I saw that roads, and bridges didn’t get the specific repairs they needed. So I opened up my own business, and now I repair roads and bridges all across the north of Poland.” “Sounds like you got everything going for you now. The whole world at your feet, but what about the love life?” “Well, that’s what I came here to talk to you about. I just got engaged man, girl of my dreams, and we’re throwing a party to celebrate this Friday, I wanted you to come down take part in the festivities.” “Settling down? Didn’t expect that from you. What the hell I got nothing to do on Friday, I’ll be there.” Andrzej laughs joyously getting up to pat his friend on the back. He sits back down, and the two men continue to exchange stories of the past. Date: June 22, 2007 8:15 PM Location: Andrzej’s Home Sopot, Poland A taxi pulls up to a wraparound driveway outside of a rather large home. Walczak steps out of the back seat, and pays the man allowing him to keep the change. Walczak carries a bottle of wine in his right hand. He is dressed in khakis with a rather tight short sleeve button up with a nice gold tie that completes the ensemble. He grabs at his neck trying unsuccessfully to loosen the tight grip of the shirt. He walks up to the front door, and rings the doorbell repeatedly. Seconds later he is greeted by Andrzej. “No need to ring the doorbell like that.” “Just a little payback for before.” Andrzej ushers Walczak into his home, and Walczak hands him the bottle of wine. Andrzej loudly clears his throat getting the attention of his guests. “Ladies and gentlemen, it brings me great pleasure to introduce you to Tom Walczak, beloved wrestler of Poland, and my oldest friend. Treat him just as you treat me.” “That’s quite an introduction.” The guests clap and drown out Walczak’s voice. From among the guests a woman runs up to meet them at the front of the house. The slender woman has straight brown hair, and a nice tan, she is wearing a nice Carolina blue dress, and her neck is adorned with a dazzling gold necklace. She runs up to Andrzej kissing him passionately before turning her attention to Walczak. “Edyta, this is Tom Walczak, Tom this is Edyta, my future wife.” “It’s a pleasure to meet you.” “I have heard so much about you Tom, and I am glad that I finally get to meet you. Come down here and meet some of the guests.” Walczak follows both Andrzej and Edyta to the dining room where platters of food and bottles of liquor adorning the various tables. Most of the guests look at awe in Walczak as he passes by them. Making their way to the center of the gathering Andrzej pours Walczak a gin and tonic. “This is quite a party you got going on here.” “You know, just a few friends, business associates, nothing to big.” As Walczak is talking to Andrzej and sipping on his drink a man comes over to join them. He seems to be a well off business man, dressed in a nice, but simple navy suit. “Hi, Tom, my name is Lech Frankowski, I must say my son is a big fan of yours. He’s never going to believe that you were here tonight.” “I’ll be happy to sign an autograph for him, Andrzej do you have a pen?” “That’d be great. I must say, I don’t follow wrestling much, but I do like your act. You really make me believe that you don’t like America.” “Act? It’s not an act, I really don’t like America.” “Come on America is growing into one of Poland’s strongest allies, and they are not nearly as bad as some of the world makes them out to be.” “Say what you want, but you haven’t been there as long as I have, and been treated as badly as I have. Here is your autograph.” Walczak hands the man the autograph and continues to talk to Andrzej not saying another word to Lech. It hurts that even in Poland, my home country, people do not take me seriously. They think my message is only to entertain them. No matter, I cannot dwell on this here, I came to relax, and not to deal with work. Date: June 27, 2007 11:40 PM Location: Albuquerque, New Mexico A number of security guards, ushers, and other employees are seen leaving “The Pit” after just taking part in PRIME’s Revolution. After a couple of seconds we see Tom Walczak making his way to the parking lot. “HEY WALCZAK YOU SUCK.” “YEAH WALCZAK BIG BEAR WAS ABOUT TO ‘BUST THAT CHUMP’ YOU PUSSY.” Walczak sees two teenagers behind the guardrails. He shakes his head and continues on his way towards the car. Walczak just finished a convincing performance on his way to gaining the first five points in PRIME’s 5-Star Challenge competition. The two boys are obviously upset at the fact that their hero Big Bear Munson Monsoon, was one of the competitors that crumbled at the hands of Walczak. “WHY DID YOU RUN AWAY FROM BIG BEAR AT THE END WALCZAK?” Walczak seems to have had enough, and begins to turn towards the teens. As Walczak nears the kids the kids slowly back away from the guardrail. “Oh so now you are the one running away huh? You know I would have beat that punk Big Bear’s ass. No one in this competition stands a chance against me. They are all out of my league, so keep running because that’s the only way you or any of the competitors in this competition stand a chance of surviving.” Walczak knows the kids can’t hear him as they back away further and further now out of the parking lot completely. Walczak heads back towards his car awaiting the challenges that next week presents him.
