Lesson number one

4 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 4th 2007 "Lesson number one; this is why I am perfect…" Scott "Aryan" Brock opened his eyes to welcome the morning sunshine but instead was greeted by the dazzling white suit of Kevin Kassidy Kenwright. The huge six foot nine brawler wiped at the sleep in his eyes following his first night in Kenwright’s apartment to make sure he wasn’t seeing things that he really shouldn’t have been. "I said get up," snapped Kenwright as he watched his tag team and protege wriggling in bed. "No you didn’t," defended Scott as he rolled onto his front and checked the silver watch that lay on his bedside table. "What?" questioned Kevin, confused as what he didn’t do. "You never said get up," answered Aryan. "You just walked in here and said "Lesson number one… blah, blah perfect…" KKK stopped for a second. "What? No… I said "Get up. This is lesson number one…" ..." "I don’t think you did…" "I did!" "I don’t thin-" "You were asleep how would you know!" Aryan stopped. He decided against continuing the argument and inside sat up in the bed, dangling his feet over the plush carpet of Kenwright’s Manhattan apartment, one of the perks of getting a nice big payday from his old federation. "Now," started Kevin again, "get up. Today is lesson number one. I’m going to explain to you why we are perfect…" Aryan grumbled a whole pile of randomness before standing his huge frame up, towering slightly over Kenwright as he did, before slipping past and heading for the bathroom. The pair had met many moons ago while both working for random independent shows in the New York area. Kevin was fresh out of high school and had just left home to pursue his wrestling career when he met the dominating Brock at his third show. Scott was the guy that the New York Wrestling Foundation asked to take care of Kenwright before, during and after the show. Scott was basically in charge of making sure Kevin didn’t get lost or go AWOL. The pair struck up an unlikely friendship although never were at liberty to discuss their beliefs despite the numerous occasions that Scott’s drunken discussions headed in that direction. It wasn’t until five years later/two weeks ago that the pair realised that the other was a Christian, never mind the fact that both were white supremacists. It wasn’t until Scott Brock, paid a visit to Reverend Gregory Stephens of Harrison, Arkansas while working for a small promotion in the area. Scott was a devout reader of Gregory’s teachings for many years and it was something of a dream for Scott to meet the great man himself. But when he got there he noticed that Kevin was paying the same visit to the same house. Well, except that Kevin was visiting his dad. A brief, awkward silence facilitated by the fine Reverend was enough to answer all the questions of both men as they grew infinitely closer by the meeting. They had a friend in the multi-cultural business that is wrestling, and both were on the same page as far as pushing themselves to the top of that industry was concerned. "Do you remember the day we met at my house?" asked Kevin as he watched his now tag team partner coming out of the bathroom. Scott was wrapped in a towel and seemed to be on a completely different plant to the question, but after a brief second of realisation and thought, he nodded. "When I saw you there," admitted Kevin, "I was really disappointed to see you." Scott stopped. He had been casually pacing across the apartment towards the guest room but as Kevin finished his admission, the huge beast of man felt he should listen in. "I’ve seen numerous men come to visit my father. I’ve seen big and small men, rich and poor men, old and young men; and I’ve known none of them. They have arrived to our house. Spoke to my father about his preaching and then left, but when you arrived I have to admit I was saddened to see you there. And when you spoke I felt an uncontrollable urge to shut you the fuck up." Scott took a step towards Kevin. "I look at you, Scott and I’m disappointed. You have so much potential to be that member that you told my father you have always strived to be. In you, there is the possibility to be the finest solider in the white army… but to be honest, you’re all bullshit!" "WHO THE FUCK DO YOU THINK YOU ARE?!?" immediately snapped Scott. "WHAT FUCKING RIGHT DO YOU HAVE TO SIT UP THERE ON YOUR FUCKING HIGH HORSE AND LECTURE ME?!? JUST BECAUSE YOU’RE THE FUCKING SON OF THE FINEST MAN ON THIS PLANET DOESN’T COUNT FOR ANYTHING!!!" Kevin took a second to let Scott’s screams echo around the airy flat, before clearing his throat. "Oh I think it does," he smiled. "In my opinion it counts for everything. From the day I was born I have modelled myself on my father, whereas you have thought about him once or twice. Every single moment of my childhood was spent listening to my father, taking everything in. Every second I was thinking more and more like him, until finally I believed I reached his level. And what have you done?" "I have followed his teachings…" replied Aryan instantaneously. His tone was extremely defensive but that’s to be expected, seeing as KKK has had him on the back-foot for quite a while. "That is the biggest pile of horse shit I have ever heard." Scott was about to reply but he was cut off. "You do not follow my father’s teachings as strongly as I do. You are no where near as close as you think you are to being a true member of the perfect species." "What? How?" Kevin shook his head. "Just think about the amount of times that we have sat in a bar and I watched you get really drunk while smoking those horrible cigars. Every time I would sit in those shaky seats with you and all I could feel was pity. I was heart-broken, watching a good man poison his own body with that pigswill. That isn’t perfect. Your body is unclean. Your whole being is impure…" "Then maybe I shouldn’t be here," sulked Scott, still in the towel. "You are not a pure warrior…" Scott took a step towards the bedroom door. "But you will." But he didn’t take another step. "You are so quick to judge," smiled Kenwright as he walked towards his tag team. "You are so quick to throw in the towel and give up thinking everyone around you has given up. When you visited my father and me, I mentioned to him afterwards that you were a wrestler and we both agreed that you have the potential. Now I know we are tag team partners but if you are to become the pure warrior that you know you can become you really need to commit to it." Scott nodded. "There will be no alcohol. There will be no cigars. There will be minimal red meat. Our bodies will be treated with the respect and care that they deserve. Everything we eat will be examined thoroughly and only food cared for by the finest white farmers will be on our plates. My father has set up a weekly food package to be delivered by The Race Movement to whether we are in the world to make sure we aren’t poisoned. From now on, we are perfect. Together, we will be perfect. United we will strike down every impure being that stands in our way as we finally declare the United States of America as the pure and perfect nation that it deserves to be." Aryan nodded again. "To truly represent The Race in PRIME, you need to start living properly as a member of The Race; and it starts today… and it starts now." Kevin Kassidy Kenwright and Scott "Aryan" Brock were now inches from each other’s noses, following K3’s inspired speech. "Are you in?" asked Kenwright as the pair remained close enough to kiss. Scott nodded. He nodded only gently as if he had decided upon a "full nod" he would have surely head-butted KKK. Kevin smiled. "Good because I’ve already thrown out all of your old stuff." "Huh?" Scott was dragged out of his racist trance that Kevin’s preaching’s had set him into to realise that all of his worldly possessions were gone. "I have thrown everything out and bought you a new wardrobe of clothes to go with your new lifestyle." Brock immediately raced to the wardrobe in the room he had slept in and ripped open the doors. "Everything is white." Kevin timed his words perfectly to match the moment of realisation in Scott’s mind. But Brock didn’t explode like any other stereotypical big man would do; he simply turned on his heels and looked at Kevin. "My Guns N’ Roses t-shirt?" Kevin shrugged. "Gone." Then he exploded. And Kevin simply continued to smile. "I’m sorry," he added. "Our mission is not to accumulate possessions. Our mission is to make sure this world is that little bit purer when we leave it. And for that reason, you need to learn to live without your possessions…" Scott was silent. "Good." Kevin turned out of the bed room door. "Now get dressed, we have to get training for our debut on PRIME." Scott nodded. And when dressed, both in matching suits, Kevin lead Scott Brock from the apartment and to the New York street below. The pair received some bizarre looks as they walked but Kevin was oblivious to most of it. "They are simply jealous," smiled Kevin as he walked Aryan, slightly in his wake. "Millions and millions of Americans are currently going about their lives not realising the wonders that lie in front of them. They continue with the humdrum unaware that something better is possible. All they need is a wake-up call, a sign that everything is theirs for the taking… the perfect moment. And we are that sign. We will be that perfect moment. Our debut on ReVolution will be perfect. When we defeat Kurt Panzer and Kurt Steinheil in our debut match we will prove to the world our mission." The pair turned a corner in complete unison as more New Yorkers turned to glance at the two men decked out in matching white suits. "It will be a sign that we do not intend to fail in the same manner that Germany failed when they tried to cleanse the world. In 1940, Germany may have started their concentration camps but ultimately they failed. We will not fail. Our victory over Das Korps will send a subliminal message to potential warriors of our army. They will see our victory over the German nation as a declaration of intent…" "But surely, if Das Korps and ourselves are working towards the same goals we should look to work with them?" questioned Scott, interrupting Kevin’s mammoth speech. "No. We are cleansing the American country so that it only includes the American people. I do not want some German-speaking freaks part of our immaculate army." Scott nodded. "Come ReVolution, it will be their first lesson in who we are and we will prove to them, we are perfect…" Kevin stopped, and Scott finally realised where they had walked to. The Race were standing looking directly at the Brooklyn Bridge, and beyond it, Brooklyn. "And we will do it for the American race."
