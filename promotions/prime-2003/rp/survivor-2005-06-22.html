Survivor

22 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/22/05 'None of them scare me, and soon enough, I will force these lame son of a bitches to pay close fucking attention to me.' The voice of 'The Scientific Apparatus' Tyler Lopez. Lopez's voice is full of disrespect and mirthless amusement. We are inside a hotel. The curtains of this hotel room are drawn, they are white. We can see cracks of sunlight peaking around the edges and fading in the room. The windows are open outside the curtains. The wind jostles the white fabric and it bounces slowly and heavily. Lopez is sitting on a recliner in the living area of this suite. There is a half empty glass of what is probably bourbon on the end table to his left. His eyes are cool. He almost looks tired. 'When I saw my opponents names and it was placed with mines it the Alias Title Gauntlet match, it dawned on me that it would be you four men: Jackson Darilek, Peter Vetra, Paul Cain, and John Edwards that stand in my way of becoming the Alias Champion, I must admit, I was feeling a bit happy as well as a bit sad. One thing I love in this sport is crushing people’s desires. To see a grown man cry as he exits the arena gives me a feeling of pure ecstasy. It is moments like those that I live for. I’ve seen all of you fall flat on your faces at least once before, and it amazes me that any of you actually believe you have a shot at winning this match. See sooner or later, the time will come where one moment will shatter your dreams forever, shatter them to the point that wrestling in PRIME isn’t worth it anymore. A moment that will cause the all of you to realize you will never be considered one of the best in the history of PRIME.' Lopez had been pissed at the world these past two weeks. After losing his chance at destroying Jackson Darilek one on one and winning the Alias Title, and renewing the reputation that he was indeed an Apparatus, he was on a mission. He had started his voyage and planned on getting upon the road of success. He had a point to prove, he had to live up to the hype. He had to show his dominance, he had to show how much of a Apparatus he really was. 'Four moments. Moments that will transcribe all that is wrong with each of you. Moments that will symbolize Darilek for the gutless coward that he is; Vetra for the conniving disciple that he is; Cain for the hardcore chump that he is; and John Edwards for the nonentity that he is. Four moments that will epitomize why every time any of them hit the big time spotlight…they choke. I AM that moment, and I WILL bury each of your careers at the biggest stage of them all, placing your memories into the annals of PRIME history as four sorry son of a bitches that never had what it takes to be a TRUE champion. Face it, it's been a long time in the making. If I didn't make it clear to you, I really couldn't give two shits about any of you. If I end your career, I end your career, but honestly I won't be going out of my way. None of you are a problem for me because you aren’t worthy of being important enough to be considered a problem. See, every time you think you have one-upped me, you haven’t done a damn thing to me. That’s power. That’s not heart or desire or any other lame trait. That’s being powerful.' He had proven that he indeed could back the smack that he talked and he did it clearly by single handedly defeated two of the biggest superstars in PRIME, Vampir Nosferatu and Boda and would now finally see the top of the mountain. He will start fresh. And with him starting fresh has plans on a being on a streak, a streak that he plans on continuing for a very long time, a winning streak. 'For any of you to question if I can do this is easy. But Christ, I'm not gonna tell any of you, when I can show all of you. I am taken care of, I have been wined and dined, flown in jets and rode in limos and made more money than 99 percent of the PRIME. I live the top-quality life I have always wanted because I am a superstar. There is a simple understanding in this sport that as long as you take care of business, you are taken care of. I want to be taken care of, so I damn well make sure I take care of business. That’s my motivation, learn to live with it and don’t waste my time questioning it.' The ice cubes chink against the glass as he tips it back and takes a sip. He runs his left pointer finger around the cusp of the glass for a moment. He is talking as if he were addressing hopeless children. He is alert. He is firm. He is definite. 'And yes, for half an hour, and hour, however long it takes you all WILL have my full attention. Before stepping into the ring, in whichever order you all enter, you might want to thank your lucky stars, for you even reaching the level you are in today. What flaws are you going to try to exploit in this match? Are you going to try to convince the world I am arrogant? Are you going to try to convince the world that I am too abstracted in order to win this match? Hell, I wouldn’t even be surprised if one of you try to convince the world that Hoyt Williams really is God.' Lopez laughs. He didn‘t care for his opponents. Ever since he came in almost two years ago, trying to make a name for himself, Lopez became another name on the roster. He was looked over. He wanted the spotlight on him. He liked being in the center of attention. He wasn’t about to lose on the biggest stage of them all. He was victorious last year and was gonna be again. 'But I should focus on you Peter Vetra. Although you have gotten the best of me once, everyone does have an off night. See I’m simply here, like I’ve said a thousand times before – to win championships and make money. And I will accomplish those but only after I break you in half at Colossus because you are fucking harmless. You’re just a joke with a big mouth and not really worth wasting time on. Nobody gives a fuck who comes in second in this business. The bottom line is that you are not going to stop me, and in the end, you are going to be just like everyone else in this match, you’ll be left empty-handed… a loser. I have dealt with you, I know how you operate.' He stands, he is wearing his black slacks. And a white button down shirt. The room has the half light of a place with no light inside of a place of great light. Lopez's well formed torso is obvious as he walks on. He knew that the time for talking was over. It was high noon. He knew he was capable of winning this, but was he going to beat himself. The boasting and bragging wasn’t of interest to Lopez, it was about earning what should be his. He wasn’t going to let is slip away. He walks towards his private balcony and looks into the daylight. 'The next big thing. That’s how some refer to you Vetra. Its because of your head, not your potential ass wipe. You will never become that big thing, and it’s not anyone’s fault but your own. It isn’t for lack of trying. You believe that you are better than people that do nothing more than what you do. The difference between me and you Pete, I do it successfully, and I am that 'big thing'. You talk and talk and talk, and you honestly are just talking. I think I speak for the world when I say, if you are going to talk, then don't expect anyone to listen. And if you are going whine about things that are your fault, and things that you are guilty of, then don’t expect anyone to take you seriously. You are a fucking joke, you have been a fucking joke for a long time. And now here you are…bitching, and people boo you because you are a prick. Could you possibly be any more pathetic?' Lopez turns and stares at the camera, his voice more forceful. Lopez continues. He is convinced. He was back to his old ways. He has a plan in mind, a plan that no one would imagine him to do, a plan that could change the entire existence of PRIME as we know it, yet one thing was in his way. The Gauntlet Match. Dominance had to be present in this match, and Lopez knew that. 'Say what you will about me. I am who I am, and I have always been who I was. I do what I do, because it is what I am good at and I’d rather not do anything else. I don’t care about any f my opponents, anyone in the stands, or anyone in the locker room, I care about me and I think I have shown that. I see a hypocrite when I look at you Peter. I see a guy gloating about a championship he didn't earn. But don't fret, your opportunity will be taken away just as fast as it has been handed to you. This is YOUR fault. You know what will happen, you put yourself there anyway, that is not my problem. Sorry, I am not going to let anyone be a detour on my road to fame. Jackson, Paul, Peter, and John none of you are strong enough, and no one takes you seriously. And as long as the fans are around hating me, I will humiliate weaklings like you in front of them, and watch you agonize over it. Because you do care, you care a lot. You care, or you wouldn’t fucking be here. Go the fuck away.' He is stalking towards the camera now, the length of the room. In classic Lopez rant. Suddenly his voice is soft, and amused. His eyes take a glint like a predatory snake moving in. Lopez has a prophecy. He wants to glory, he will be the Alias Chamption. Nothing can come in the way of his future, his spirit. 'All of you know what I am going to do, and you can't stop it. My philosophy is very simple, hit hardest in the weakest spot, and do it over and over again until the target crumbles. I haven't tried to trick my insignificant enemies ever before, why would I start now? Acting like knights in shining armor won‘t get any of you anywhere. From what I see, none of you know diddly fucking squat about me, except that you won't beat me. Are you too stupid to know that the first step in beating an enemy is knowing that enemy? I know all of you better than any of you know me. I have you out numbered. Just because I haven't addressed what will happen in the ring does not mean I am not talking about the match. I talked about how stupid all of you are, does that not apply to what will happen? I talked about how your war is hopeless, does that not have something to do with our fighting? Must I spell it out?' He wants it, and he is going to do any and everything to get it No one can stop Lopez from his destiny. He won’t come up short. It was impossible to make everyone comprehend his thoughts, his emotions, he would have to use his actions to grab the attention of everyone: Fans, Wrestlers, and Staff. 'No. And you should know that. You should be able to read between lines. Come Colossus II, don't be shocked that I just laugh at you. There is a difference between being resilient, and just plain sucking ass. In the grand scheme of things you are all failures. I have been everyone's worst enemy since I can remember. And those people, with all the incentive and desire in the world couldn't take me. Stronger men proved weak when compared to my will. Speedy men found that they weren't that fast. And if you were smarter, you wouldn‘t even show up come Sunday. I’m not going to make this about who is going to win the match because those discussions often are very tiresome. You see, this started out being a battle of rights. The rights for one man to get what he has been after for over a year now while 4 others seek out the rights to be recognized. The right to be heard when he spoke, the right to be accepted in a world of neglection, the right to be seen as more then a trivial little bitch. And I…I think you know which one I am, and which one you four are.' He had begun to stoop depths thought unimaginable just to win. Lopez was starving, ravenous for a win, and starting Sunday he would feed his appetite. Tyler Lopez was more prepared than he had ever been before. 'I still think I’m going to win. I will admit to being confident, even cocky. I do think I’m a very talented wrestler who will beat 99% of the jackasses around here. I am a indispensable evil. I’m not asking anyone to like me, because that’s the point…you’re not intended to like me. I said it on before and I’ll say it again, this business is the success it is because of men like me. You have ever tasted opposition like me. This is the Scientific Apparatus. You’re all going to be in the back waiting for your chance to enter the match, your hearts will be pounding, sweat will be pouring down your faces, and most likely, you’ll be making a few trips to the bathroom. When you step out into that ring on the stage we will be on, the nerves and tension inside of you will be so great, beating me will be the last thing on your mind. All you’ll want to do is survive, the only problem is, I am the ultimate survivor!'
