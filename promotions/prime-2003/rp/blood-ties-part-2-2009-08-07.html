Date: 07/08/09 You can't choose your family. You are born with them and you die with them, normally a few generations down the line. However they are there for you. Whether or not they appear to care deep down they always will. You are their blood and their kin, their very essence flows through you with every single step you take in life. It was only a five minute walk. Enter the building, take the lift to the ninth floor and third door on the left. Yet Claire had been sat in the foyer for well over an hour. Her eyes glanced repeatedly every time the glistening lift doors opened, her chest tightening and an all encompassing fear grasping her soul. The foyer was strangely quiet. Normally it was filled with suits dashing around frantically, rushing from meeting to meeting with Starbucks Coffee permanently attached to their hands. They moved as though in slow motion, never taking the time to actually stop and realise how lovely their surroundings are. A three story fountain was the focal point of the foyer, gentle streams of water cascaded down the ornate design before merging with the rest of the liquid at the bottom in the crystal clear pool. Not even a thrown away penny was found to be thrown idly by the bottom. Some of the more aware workers would spend their lunches sat by the fountain, but today the foyer was absent of anyone, other than a lone receptionist who maintained a sombre resolve on her drawn face. Claire took the woman in, nineteen maybe twenty at a push but certainly no older. This company was no place for a woman like her, already the company was draining her. Where their should have been an attractive lively face with bright and vibrant eyes, there was just darkness. They say the eyes are the window to the soul and if that was the case, this poor receptionist had a lot of problems. A dull hum behind reception pulled Claire away from her thought train as she looked over anxiously at the woman. She is too far away to make out the conversation or the words that are being spoken. In her heart of hearts though, she knew that it was time. “Claire. They're ready for you now.” The receptionist had raised her voice to be heard as it reverberated around the foyer. She rose to her feet and straightened her suit before stepping towards the elevator. “For what it's worth. I'm sorry.” The receptionist had followed Claire's walk and noticed the slow calculating pace. Her high heels echoed through the foyer bouncing off the walls and attacking the senses. Claire stopped and let her head droop. As she sighed, a single solitary tear formed beneath her eyelid and rolled down her cheek, before dropping elegantly to the marble floor below. Without saying a word she pushed the button for the elevator and waited patiently. Sniffing as quietly as possible in an attempt to quell any further tears. As the doors opened she stepped inside before turning on her heel to face the foyer. The doors closed slowly as the receptionist gave a sympathetic smile, Claire nodded in solemn appreciation as she faded out of view. It was then and only then. In the solitude of the elevators, away from cameras and prying eyes that she allowed a small smile to form in the corners of her mouth. ~ ~ ~ Freedom. It's something that mankind strives to find. Whether in the physical or mental sense. To be physically free means having no ties, no bonds which hold you back in this mortal coil. Sometimes even those things that you spend your life striving to achieve can ultimately hold you back. Like a family. Who was it the decided that we are better off with those around us. Piggybacking on our existence in some fruitless escapade. It doesn't work that way in nature, when a lioness deems its young to be old enough to survive, she leaves them safe in the knowledge that they will thrive in the world, or perish as nature decides. Survival of the fittest. Which is exactly what life is about. Having people who aren't up to the challenge dragged you down. They would simply pose a threat. Like a mercenary who began to question his mission the night before the battle. Would he go crazy and be unable to define friend from foe or would he not lost the night? His fellow men sensing weakness, wanting to quell the dangerous thinking of a dangerous man. A glint of metal in the moonlight. Then silence. It may seem like a horrific action but when you break it down it's simply survival. You have to do whatever if takes to survive. ~ ~ ~ When he was left alone, that was when Christopher was happiest. Meat as the watchers of PRIME's weekly Revolution knew him, enjoyed the quiet moments in which he was allowed to be alone with his thoughts. It was during these precious moments that he came to clear and concise decisions. Including his latest idea, which although was not a world beater did have a fair amount of weight behind it. The idea had been mulling around Christopher's head for some time, yet at Revolution he had taken the step forwards and announced to the world that Colossus would be the last time they would see him. It was something that had torn him apart inside, but the truth was quite simple. He was a nobody in PRIME. What the hell had Claire been thinking to call him Meat? It worked in a two bit territory like Just, but this was the big leagues. Sponsorship deals and money that would enable him to be secure for the rest of his life. All he would have needed to do was keep injury free and climb the ladder slowly. There was no need for a rush after all he was young enough to have another twenty years in the ring if he so desired, but it was something that just wasn't to be. It was a shame really when he thought about it. At Revolution he had been given some screen time, he didn't know why exactly, maybe Lisa Tyler was too preoccupied with preparations for Colossus. The key thing though was that he had taken this moment to announce the end. After Colossus there would be no more Meat. It had been finalised that very day... ~ ~ ~ There is a lot to be said for freedom and nature is a perfect blueprint. However lions are not humans. Lions do not feel the longing for companionship, it is very simply a case of prolonging the family line. Humans are far more complex. The simplest emotion can cause a man to do the most irrational of actions. Lust has been known to kill a man and so has sadness. As a race we are not in control of our emotions, they simply guide us along the way and we follow them like perpetual slaves. Never truly seeing or understanding our captors, only knowing that one day they will be the end of you. ~ ~ ~ “I was told I would find you here, I've been trying to get in touch with you all day.” Chris allowed his eyes to survey the surroundings. To say the bar was run down would be an understatement. A broken jukebox whirred repeatedly as the machinery tried to slot the best of Huey lewis and the news into the player, smashed glass preventing it from doing so. With every step the tattered and presumably once intact carpet stuck to Chris' feet as he moved towards the vacant stool next to Claire. “Mind if I sit here” Chris asked, he could tell Claire was not herself and most notably drunk. The stale stench of alcohol coming from her, only slightly less than the aged odour the bar had developed through time. “Free country.” She didn't even look at him. “You know it's funny that, people say it's a free country but not really. Hell if I tried to pour myself a beer for free now I'd....” “Sit down. Shut up and drink.” Claire ordered as she indicated to the barman who waddled over, his stomach akin to one of the kegs in his cellar. Chris ordered a malt scotch, or as close as a bar of this calibre would be able to muster. “I never liked him you know, hated him in fact. But that doesn't change who he was. I respected him to the end of the world and back. I never wanted for anything and that fucking company of his took him from me.” Claire spoke but there were no more tears, she had none left. Now all there was, was the sweet embrace of vodka and attempting to block all all other feelings. “If there's anything I can do Claire, you know all you have to do is ask.” The words were sincere and heartfelt, what more is there to say at a time like this? However Claire had felt like she had been hearing them all do. Is there anything anyone could do? Yes they could find the one responsible and make them pay. “We're going to find the person that did this Chris. They will not go unpunished.” “I'm sure the police will....” “Oh no Chris, the police will be a lucky escape for the bastard. When I am through with the person responsible....” Claire shot Chris a glance, it was the first time she had looked at him since he'd sat down and to his shock, she didn't look too bad. Her eyes were a little blood shot, but there was no run make up and no puffy cheeks. Just a steely resolve and revenge on her mind. “....they will pay.” The barman returned with Chris' drink and placed it on the bar in front of him. The glass was unclean and didn't look as though it had been either in a long time. Reluctantly he took a large sip, which he found to be pleasantly surprising. The crisp liquid eased it's way down his throat and immediately began to warm his chest. Without even realising it, he had found himself staring at Claire. There was something about seeing her like this, it was tragic but refreshing. To see her so strong, even in the face of everything that had happened and yet so wounded. “And you...” Claire slurred as she pointed her glass at Chris who's eyes had not diverted from hers. “Why are you still here, following me around like a lost fucking puppy. If only you knew....” Claire pauses for a moment as she burps ethanol in Chris' face who winces slightly as the toxic air burns his eyes. “If only you knew the things I've done to you! Then you wouldn't be here!” Claire becomes more irate with every word. Chris said nothing and rose his tumbler to his lips, taking a long hard sip on the cool whisky that filled the container. A lone ice cube rolled around the glass as his eyes pierced hers. “Oh I don't know Claire, I might still be here.” “What...” Taking another sip of the whiskey, more for dramatic effect than Dutch courage he allowed Claire to simmer. She swayed less than eloquently on her stool as Chris leant in and brushed her hair away from her ear. He allowed his lip to brush the very crest of her ear, sending bolts of electricity between the two of them. A notable sigh of want and lust emanated from Claire's lungs. “I know everything.” it was little more than an expulsion of breath, the words barely formed. Yet they lingered, almost eternally in the air as Chris pulled himself away and allowed the world to crash down on her. But it didn't. Instead she simply smiled and sipped her vodka which was now nearly empty. She nodded to the barman who responded in kind and began to pour her a drink. “Then that just bodes the question. Why are you still here? Why are you putting your body and mind through this?” Claire asked as she ran her tongue seductively around the rim of the glass. This was time for her power play, sex. She had used it every single time in her life to get everything she wanted. Chris tried to maintain a level of dignity but the alcohol was slowly beginning to effect his synapses. As the alcohol began to infect his blood stream his lusts and wants began to over come him. “We both know the answer to that Claire.” Chris leaned back as far as he could without falling off the stool backwards. His voice was filled with realisation of years gone by, that if only everything had been this simple from the beginning “Then what are you going to do about it?” Claire asked as she took the drink from the barman without looking, her eyes transfixed on the man ahead of her. She took a slow calculating drink and leaned forwards slightly, exposing her ample cleavage to Chris. “Nothing...I am not your piece of Meat any more. I'm done chasing, I'm not about to be...how did you put it with such aplomb 'a lost fucking puppy'. What I want you to do now is kiss me.” Chris spoke with such authority that Claire almost lurched forwards immediately. “What?” Claire whispered as she pulled herself upright, trying to maintain some level of dignity, the one thought running around her head that she almost literally threw herself at a man. “You...kiss me. I am not going to move from this spot if you want this to happen, if you want to end the games. This is where it ends here and now. But you have to make that move, you have to make that choice.” The two sat in silence, staring at each other. For years they had played a charade, for years they had danced around each other for a multitude of reasons. But now it was an even playing field it was decision time. A decision that was clouded with the persuasion of alcohol and loss. Leaning forwards Claire closed her eyes and allowed her lips to embrace Chris'. She sighed deeply as she felt the connection surge through her body as Chris returned the kiss, his hand running through her hair gently caressing her. In that moment she wasn't alone. But the face of a woman who was burst forth into her mind. “Chris, I can't do this....your wife she....” Chris lifted a solitary finger to Claire's lips and stopped her mid-sentence. As she stared at the man who she longed would hold her tight and make everything feel better he leant in and once again whispered in her ear. “Claire...I told you.” He kissed her ear. “I know everything” Fin.
