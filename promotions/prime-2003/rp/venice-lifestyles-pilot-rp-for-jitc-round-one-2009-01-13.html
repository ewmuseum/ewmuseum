Venice Lifestyles - Pilot (RP for JITC Round One)

13 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 13, 2009 January 13, 2009 He thought the first step back on North American soil was the sweetest. Of course every step after months away was just as good as the one before, but he'll remember that first step. He didn't know what it was, but he didn't go back to Boston like he originally thought he would. After pondering the decision for days and delaying flight after flight, something pulled him to the western shores. He was never much of a surfer or a hot weather guy, nonetheless he was drawn to California and specifically, the Greater Los Angeles area. The sun beat down on the pavement as he exited LAX and pondered what to do from there. He had the clothes on his back plus a small bag containing some workout gear, a couple changes of clothes and some toiletries. Most of his available money was gone due to cancelling and changing flights repeatedly, but if his last second hunch worked out it would be well worth the money and effort. Nicholas Donnelly walked over to a payphone just outside the taxi area and picked up the receiver. He withdrew a credit card from his pocket and slid it into the slot. Much to his dismay, the phone would not accept it, claiming no charge could be applied to a card with insufficient funds. Fifty friggin' cents and he couldn't even spare that. He sighed and put the receiver back on the hook. He wiped the perspiration from his brow and bent down to unzip his bag. Digging through the bottom revealed some receipts for meals, cards of businesses that he never succeeded to gain employment at and various other forms of paper he really didn't need much. Finally he found a few miscellaneous coins and sifted through them in his hand. He found a quarter, a dime and three nickels. Not much else was left besides a few Mexican pesos and other Japanese coins, a rather unceremonious reminder of exactly where his travels had recently taken him. Donnelly zipped up his bag and glanced at a few of the passerby in the area. Most of them were staring at his dishevelled and perhaps quite dirty appearance. Indeed, his jeans were torn and rather filthy to the naked eye and his t-shirt was just a plain black color that had holes in the armpits. He had a change of clothes but they were not much cleaner or better for that matter. Travelling with such cheap and simple personal effects was certainly not fun. He inserted the coins in the payphone and dialed the number that he received in a message when he was away training. The line rang a couple times and just when he thought his prayers had been answered, a voice mail was all he heard. “Thank you for calling Mendoza's Gym and Training center. You've reached the voice mail of AnaLeticia. We're located at...” He continued to listen and quickly fought for a pen inside the confines of his bag as he listened. He found one and jotted down the address, pausing for a moment to shake the pen after it stopped writing. Finally he managed to write down the entire address. He turned for a moment to see someone else waiting for the payphone rather impatiently. The woman made a motion to her watch to show that she was in a hurry. Nitz however didn't take the gesture well. “I just fuckin' got here lady so you can wait for another minute.” He said with a stern look. His hair was long and greasy, hanging in his face and partially covering his eyes which stared daggers through the woman. She backed away a little and he turned back to the phone. “I'm in the office but away from my desk or on the phone at the moment...” He hung up immediately, collecting his note with the address he needed. He stuck his finger in the change slot, almost desperate to find some more coins to make another phone call if he needed. He then cursed himself for being so trashy as he left the phone booth. He gave the impatient woman one last stare and flashed her the bird, winning a disgusted look with a sharp inhaling sound. He smirked a little and made his way to the road past the taxi area as he surely couldn't afford even a short ride. He sighed, pulled his sunglasses out of his pocket and prepared for a hike around an area he wasn't even familiar with. |>~o~<| Donnelly never thought that he would run into a couple good Samaritans within the city limits. He was fortunate however to find a couple guys willing to give directions to the Venice Beach area just outside of the airport. He found himself to be even more fortunate when the two gentlemen agreed to give him a lift. To explain, Nicholas Donnelly is no idiot. He wouldn't just hop into a car with two people he doesn't know if he hadn't assessed the situation. He's in Los Angeles... that's the first hint to be extra cautious. Second, the two men looked middle aged, both no more than five feet nine inches tall and with Nitz being no slouch in the atheletic department, he wouldn't have an issue defending himself and most likely taking the offensive. Not to mention, what dangerous person in their right mind drives a 1997 4 door Toyota Corolla? That's right, nobody. Being realistic upon hearing the distance he would have to travel by foot, he graciously accepted the ride. All it took was some small talk, twenty minutes of patience and a thankful attitude and he soon found himself only two blocks from his destination in Venice Beach. A small fender bender blocked the road they were to travel down, so Donnelly excused himself from the vehicle, thanked the two men for the ride and the short discussion and hit the pavement for the final couple minutes of his journey. It had been years upon years since he last saw his childhood best friend Letty. He thought it was no more than fifteen years but he really hadn't seen her since he turned seven years old. At twenty-four, it was seventeen plus years now. He had known AnaLeticia since he was a tiny tot of only two. They grew up together and almost spent every waking our in the presence of one another. But a couple years prior to her departure from Boston, Leticia's parents went through a messy divorce. Messy because her Dad happened to be the faithful providing husband and her Mom became the lying, adulterous whore who was previously closeted in favor of the typical PTA assistant and soccer mom type. After two years of legal battles, all won by her father, the final decision forced Leticia to live with her Dad in his new home of Los Angeles, California. Her Mom was deemed unfit by the ruling judge to be a parents and only a short year after Leticia left, the Donnelly family learned of her long term incarceration for drug possession and intent to distribute plus some other miscellaneous charges that were never revealed. Turns out, just by the name of the fitness center alone, Letty and her Dad became much more successful on the West Coast. Nitz kept in touch with his childhood friend for two full years by letter and phone but soon after, they grew up, grew apart and the contact slowly ceased. Apparently during his time in foreign countries training and reconditioning, she attempted to contact him. She left the contact number of the club, leading Nicholas to the West Shores simply out of curiosity. His family knew nothing of his apparent return to New England and with his mind changed, he really had no plans or setups to cancel. So here he was, standing outside of the gym that his childhood friend had a major hand in running. He wondered what she would be like, look like and act like upon seeing him. He wondered if she would even recognize him. After opening the front door, he could immediately smell chlorine from the pool. He soon realized that there was a hot tub along with an indoor and outdoor swimming pool. He thought it strange, being born and raised in an area with snow in the winter time that a year round outdoor pool could exist. But it was January now and the weather was better than almost any summer weather found anywhere in the Northeastern United States. After kindly asking the fitness guru standing beside the counter of the whereabouts of his friend, he found himself taking a set of stairs down to the basement. So far, he had seen a state of the art fitness facility but he hadn't seen the crown jewel. Upon reaching the bottom of the stairs, the entire picture came into view. He set his eyes on what could have been the largest training center for fighting, boxing, kickboxing and perhaps even wrestling that he had ever seen. The entire basement has three full size rings along with what seemed like an endless supply of weights and other workout equipment. Perhaps the center seemed so large because mirrors covered most of the walls and gave the illusion that the building was bigger than it really was. His eyes darted around for a couple minutes, slowly making his way through the group of people but in reality, he had no clue what Leticia would look like. After thinking about his situation, he considered asking someone around him who Leticia was. One fighter pointed him in the direction of a somewhat butch woman holding a clipboard talking to a couple of people. He shook his head, not believing that cute little Letty from his childhood could look so different. As he made made his way over in that direction, the butch lady nodded her head and departed along with the group. Turns out he was mistaken and the fighter actually did point him in the correct direction. He soon was able to lay his eyes on a Latino Beauty, who quickly turned to the ring she was standing beside, urging the fighter on with such words as “Get those feet moving” and “Don't let him get close to you, use your kicks”. He knew immediately, from the deep brown eyes that seemed endless in the second he got to look at them to the rich tone in her voice. Seventeen years later, the two former best friends were once again in the same room. He walked up as she was still watching the fighters. He stood maybe two feet behind her, trying to figure out just what to say to her. Instead, she turned her head back a little not really looking at him or the ring in front of him. “You couldn't wear a better shirt for your best friend?” She then turned to face him and he was stunned. His childhood friend, the person he spent every waking moment with as a kid was all grown up. She was a woman and wow, what a woman. But he pushed those thoughts immediately from his mind, unable to think that way about someone he thought of as a family member. They looked as if they were going to share a hug, but they were both nervous. She froze in place and he hesitated. Really, they were both surprised they were seeing each other in person after so long. Nitz made a move to put his arm out and as soon as he touched her shoulder, she looked very unsure. There was a yell from behind him and before he could react, a massive hand found it's way around his throat and he was thrust forward into the apron of the ring. His eyes were closed and he couldn't get a look at his assailant. All he could hear was Letty chastising the seemingly giant man. “Vance! Stop it! Let him go!” The death grip was suddenly released, like she had the man under mind control. He inhaled quickly, dropping to the cold floor, his energy removed from his body. He opened his eyes a little, seeing a mountain of a man standing in front of him, offering a hand. Nitz debated slapping the hand away, but with the temper of his assailant he just didn't have the energy to fight back should it set him off. Instead he took the offer and was pulled back to his feet. The rest of the gym was already looking in their direction. “You'll have to excuse Vance. He's extremely protective. Long story.” Donnelly coughed, trying to clear the phlegm from his throat and open his airway. Finally he managed to speak, though he was unsure of what to say. “He's also a fuckin' psycho!” He said, following it all with a huge coughing fit. Finally, satisfied he was finished, he turned to look at the man known as Vance. He was suddenly wishing he didn't. The angry glare soon turned weak. He was looking into the eyes of an absolute monster. He was almost seven feet tall, probably three hundred fifty pounds of pure muscle and the tattoos were intimidating on their own. He was wearing a sweatshirt with a hoodie that had the sleeves removed. Vance was certainly a man who could have broken his neck on command if he chose. “People sometimes don't respect Leticia. I make sure that doesn't happen anymore.” Vance said in a flat tone, almost resembling the look on his face. He was stoic, barely emotional at all. “She saved my life before.” “That's also a long story.” Leticia said, kind of uncomfortable about the direction of the conversation. Nitz could see the apprehension in her eyes to continue so he quickly changed the subject. “Sorry for giving no notice. Was going to go home but I remembered the message you left me and decided to come see you.” He smiled a little, lightening the look in Letty's eyes. “I just thought you weren't supposed to be... ummmm... back yet but it's wonderful to see you!” She reached out this time and embraced her friend for the first time in many years. He returned the favor. “I just got back from Japan actually, so it worked out for the better.” He felt her tense at the mention of Japan, but he thought nothing of it. Leticia however let go of the hug and held Nitz at an arms length to look him over. “Couldn't get any worse by the looks of you Nicky.” Now it was his turn to feel tense. He wasn't used to dressing like a homeless person, but when a guy doesn't have much money and no gainful employment, shit happens ya know? He scratched his head, trying to find a proper excuse. Not being able to think of something acceptable, he was just honest. “Shit happens ya know?” See? “The job hunt hasn't gone well recently. That's why I'm back in the country.” “Well you've already met Vance. Vance, this is a friend from my child hood. His name is Nicholas Donnelly, but I just call him Nicky.” “And she's one of the few people who can.” He retorted, trying to cut off the Nicky talk from the start. “I know you man. Nitz Donnelly from PRIME a while back. I'll stick with Nick or Nitz.” Vance explained, reaching out a hand to shake and not to strangle this time. Donnelly accepted the handshake, not bothering to try to match the grip strength of the much larger man. “I'm Vance... Vance Raymes. Guys around here call me Van.” “Gotcha Van Ray. By the way, try and choke me again and your crotch will become a mess on my boot.” He laughed a little, winning a slight smirk from the big man. The joke, as demented as it sounded, was something of an ice breaker to the men. It actually made Leticia light up with a smile, forgetting quickly about the incident just a minute or two earlier. “Well, I'm glad you're here.” Leticia said, waving her hand to Nitz to follow her. “Let's get you cleaned up.” “Whoa whoa, you know I'm not one for handouts. Let's take it easy here.” “Where you gonna stay tonight then?” “Well... I uhhh...” “Thought so.” She smiled as she turned back to look at him as they walked. Vance began to follow. “Vance stays around a beach house that I use for guests. She's all yours for as long as you want.” “Alright, but where's he gonna stay then?” “I've got a trailer outside. Don't worry 'bout me.” “I see.” Nitz said, following Leticia up the stairs of the gym back to the main level. He wasn't at all comfortable accepting things based on his situation but he realized he didn't have a choice. Unless he was to go back home, he didn't have a place to stay. “We'll go get you some clothes, we have an account so no worries there. Then, make a stop by the grocery to get some food for the beach house and we'll look into the job issue for you.” “Ok this is all a little too much Letty. I know I walked in here looking a little worse for wear but come on, I'm not a charity case.” “It's not charity Nicky, it's what friends do for friends.” She said, smiling back at him again as she picked up her handbag from behind the front counter of the club. Vance stood by the front door with his arms crossed, looking outside and minding his own business. She giggled a little. “If you turn me down, I don't think I could ever forgive you for it.” “Funny. What's his deal anyway?” Leticia looked over at Vance, then back to Nitz. She nodded her head, understanding the question and answering in a quiet voice to prevent eavesdropping from the mammoth man. “Like I said, long story. But he works for my Dad at the condo now and he's been around now for over a year. The life saving stuff, I'll tell you later.” She checked her messages quick with the secretary and then made her way for the door. Vance held the door open for the both of them as they set out to the parking lot. Leticia pressed the button on her remote and the lights on a silver Land Rover flashed in the front row, closest to the front door. “Glad to see you made something of yourself after leaving Boston.” Nitz laughed, teasing his friend. He quickly found things returning to normal quite fast between them. Besides the opening nervousness when he arrived, it was gone pretty fast and it was like they were never apart. |>~o~<| Later That Night He had done many things with Leticia when they were kids. They tried to sell lemonade, they cut lawns together, they spent endless hours stuffing quarters into the video games at the arcade and they shared countless sodas from the local convenience store. But never before had he shared a “drink” with his friend. Obviously because of their age but still, there weren't too many firsts to be had between the pair who considered the other a part of their own families. He downed the shot of tequila fast as he could, satisfied with the burning in his throat. It was a welcome sensation given the fact he hadn't had a true drink in quite sometime. He then settled in with his old friend Jack Daniels, resting his arms on the table in front of him as Leticia came trotting back over to the table from the dance floor. Vance however was left out there. It was amazing how a guy could open up after about half a dozen shots and a couple beers. “Look at this guy, a completely different person after downing some booze.” Nitz said, staring in wonder as Vance let himself go to dance with a couple cute ladies who seemed to be half his size. “It's not often he's like this actually.” “So what's the deal now? He's out there and we're here. How'd you save his life?” “Well, about a year ago he began to show up at the gym and paid for a membership. For the first couple months he paid his dues, early actually. But then the third month came around and he didn't pay. He kept showing up like nothing was wrong and one day, he walked in stumbling like he was drunk. Turned out to be drugs. So I shelled out some cash for him and had him attended a rehab clinic. He never wanted to do drugs in the first place, it was more pressure from the guys he was doing work for. So he cleaned up, I got him a job with Dad at the condo and the rest is history. And before you ask about drinking being a gateway to drugs, he's one of the exceptions. Part of his job at the condo is constant drug tests administered and he's never failed since. He was never into drugs on his own accord.” “THAT was the long story?! It took like a minute to explain.” “Nicky, I could get into the gruesome details about how they forced drugs onto him by spiking his drink one night but that doesn't matter. What matters is that he's clean and he credits me for saving his life. I really don't think so but I don't fight it. Given that situation, he's become somewhat of a bodyguard.” She sighed, taking a long sip of her drink. She then smiled at Nitz, ultimately happy that he decided to come to the West Coast. “Gotcha. So listen, this won't be for long. I can't keep taking handouts from you.” “Nonsense. We'll get you some work and you'll be on your feet in no time.” “What makes you think I'm gonna stick around Letty? I never said I was gonna stay long.” “Gimmie a break Nicky. You wouldn't have come here with no money or barely any good clothes and passed up going home to a guaranteed place to crash if you didn't intend on staying for a while. We may be grown up now, but I can still read you like a book.” She was right. From the first time they laid eyes on each other earlier in the day, she read his emotions, his actions and his reactions. She knew he would fight the assistance to buy things, the offer of the beach house and even the offer of the job. Even as a kid, he would never accept help to do even the most simple things. He always like to earn his way in his circle of friends every step. Sure there were some exceptions places like the wrestling business laid out for you, but beyond that, he believed in self wealth so to speak. But right now, he didn't have a choice and at least it was someone he cared about helping him out. He knew he could always repay her later. “Guilty I guess.” He laughed, taking a sip of his drink in defeat. “You know me too well ya know that? It almost scares me sometimes. Even my own family can't read me that well.” “I am your family Nicky. You know that.” She placed a hand on his, the warmth immediately penetrating his skin, almost soothing his soul just by the simple touch. He nodded, his smile so bright she could have sworn he'd need surgery to have it removed. He felt like a kid around her again, only not... if that made any sense. Their moment was interrupted however when Vance made a beeline over to the table, trying cover his visible intoxication. “Someone called from the condo. Got some work to do for about an hour. You two be ok?” Nitz briefly considered making a joke about someone asking him to jump and Vance replying with the ever popular “How High?” but he thought the better of it. “We're fine Vance. Take it easy.” “Yeah, see ya outside the house later on Van.” Nitz said, sounding quite friendly with the big guy already. Vance grabbed his sweater and swiftly made his exit. Of course, this opened the door to Nitz for more comments in his absense. “Swear he was asked to beat the shit outta someone or kill some poor asshole or something.” “Heh, naw. Lots of my Dad's business partners stay at the condo during the week when they visit, so what better guy to stand security outside some conference rooms when meetings take place in the evening?” Leticia explains, winning the barkeeps attention with the wave of her hand, asking for a couple refills. “So that's what he does? Security for your Dad and stuff?” “Kinda. Vance has become a jack of all trades so to speak. Sometimes he's security, sometimes he's a driver and sometimes he delivers some important packages to my Dad's friends. All in all he keeps pretty busy and Dad pays him handsomely. Maybe I could get you in with Dad for some work too.” “Anything would do right about now. Just to get some cash flowing in. Being helpless isn't too shit hot.” Nitz replied as another drink was set in front of him by the bartender. Obviously, Leticia had a tab at the bar because not once did money exchange hands between her and the barkeep. “I gotta say Nicky, it's great to have you here, but you ain't seen nothing yet. You're gonna enjoy the lifestyle here in Venice Beach.” “Women in bikini's, good food, good booze and women in bikini's? I'm sure I could find a way to enjoy myself.” He had a pretty big smirk on his face, downing another large sip of his new drink. “Pervert. Let's finish these drinks and head out so you can get settled in to the beach house. Mark your territory as a man and what not.” “That's a great little beach house you got there by the way.” “Thanks, I kinda grew up in it for a couple years.” The small talk continued as they both put their empty glasses on the bar and set off to leave. A wave and a thanks by name from Leticia to the bartender confirmed his earlier thoughts. He could get used to the easy life in Venice. All he needed was good, steady work. Perhaps home was further away than he thought. |>~o~<| After putting away most of his new clothes and getting settled in to the bedroom, Nitz heard a noise from outside. He removed his watch and placed it on the dresser as he left the room to ensure it was Vance just tooling around in the driveway. Immediately after exiting through the side door, he saw the big man hanging some wet clothes on a makeshift clothesline attached from his trailer to the beach house. The line started coming loose from the house so Nitz reached up to retie it, ensuring it's stability. “Gone for a little more than an hour there Van.” He said, making some small talk. “Yeah, perks of the job I guess. Same with the clean clothes. Gotta look good around the executives.” Nitz nodded, leaving the clothesline alone after securing it. He made his way back to the door, not really having much to discuss with his new friend. Prior to offering a good night, he instead opted for a different route. “Gonna have a beer and watch SportsCenter. Wanna join me?” “Appreciate the invite Nick.” Vance nodded and put the last shirt on the clothesline. He followed Nitz into the beach house as Donnelly stayed behind to close the door. Vance found his way to the living room and Nitz grabbed a couple beers from the fridge. He walked into the living area and flicked on the TV with the remote before handing the big man his beverage. “So what brought you 'round these parts?” Now it was Vance's turn to try and make small talk. Nitz sat on the couch in the corner closest to the chair Raymes was sitting in and leaned back, breaking the seal on his drink and putting a foot up on the table. “Change of scenery and to see an old friend again is the basic answer. Won't get into the long explanation but let's just say I'm liking this place more and more every minute I'm here.” “How long you around for?” “Not sure. But home is seeming further and further away now. Probably seem like a whole world away once I tackle that beach scene tomorrow.” “You have no idea bro.” Vance and Nitz seemed to be getting along rather well, regardless of how they first met each other. Donnelly found himself to be forgetting about that incident more and more as time moved forward. He couldn't tell whether it was being back in Leticia's life again that made him so instantly comfortable or if Vance seemed like an easy guy yo get along with. But in reality, it didn't really matter. “Sure I'll be pleased tomorrow Van.” “You bet. Cheers Nick.” Vance leaned his beer bottle over to Nitz and given a second, Nitz tapped the neck his his bottle against his new friends. He smirked, took a long sip of his beer and leaned back to take in some hockey highlights. “By the way Van, that was some great dancing I saw to-” He was immediately cut off “Shut up.” “You bet... no prob Ricky Martin.” Nicholas Donnelly in Venice Beach. Imagine the possibilities.
