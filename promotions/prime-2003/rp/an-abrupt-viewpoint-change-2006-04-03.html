An Abrupt Viewpoint Change

3 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-3-06 Hahaha! I can’t believe she actually said that! Man, what a fuckin’ character! Oh, I’m sorry! I didn’t see you there. Pull up a stool. Kyle’s in the bathroom, and I’ve got a minute, so let’s chat. My name’s…well, I don’t have a name. But I’m thinking about adopting something cool like “Thor” or “Raven.” I’m a beer. Yep, you heard me right. I’m a delicious, frosty, cool Heineken. Now, I know what you’re thinking. How on earth could I be sitting here, listening to a beer talk? I know! I can’t explain it either! I’ve tried all my life to be a good beer. And it’s not all that hard, being a Heineken at all. Our family’s got a legacy, as it were. Or as it is. I forget which. So anyways…I’m sitting here fresh outta the fridge, and the bartender, Chuck pulls me out, and it’s go time. It’s the chance I’ve been waiting for my whole life! I put on my game face…Chuck gives me a quick little wipe down and pops my top, and I’m IN THE GAME. And sure enough, this spiky-haired kid is my drinker. We beers always wonder about our drinkers. I mean, after all…when we go to the great beyond (re: the stomach) we become one with our drinker. We’d like to get to know them before we get to be a part of them. Sometimes, if we don’t like the drinker (and I mean REALLY don’t like the drinker), we’ll try to commit suicide to save face. What? You think that you “accidentally” drop us? Not a chance, buddy. We’re jumping. So here’s this kid, like I said, probably just old enough to be legal, and he’s brought a few people to this bar to celebrate. This is cool, because typically, the only other way we get information about our drinkers is if they mumble to themselves, or talk to the bartender or other weird shit. But sure enough, I’ve got to be the luckiest beer in the bar…because I actually got a CELEBRITY! Sure, this guy isn’t a REAL celebrity, but he’s on TV. An older guy who ordered a Guinness from the tap made some comment about him starting out on cable TV next week, on a station called FX! You hear that, all you from the bottling plant back home!? I’M GONNA BE ON TV! Yep! He’s a wrestler. Which means that I’ll be a part of his system in a hurry, because you KNOW those wrestler types can drink! An athlete and a celebrity! Anyways, apparently Kyle (my drinker) and his friend Jason (with the Guinness) and a little Japanese girl named Miho came to the bar to celebrate a big victory in a wrestling match OVERSEAS! I’m gonna travel! And the guy’s a winner! Well, Kyle was just blathering on and on about how awesome it was to pin this Karina chick. Apparently she was really good or something, because Kyle made such a big deal out of it, like it was a once in a lifetime opportunity. And Miho (What a babe! She’s got the body of a pilsner, ifyaknowwhatimeanandithinkyado!) said something about how it was the first time Kyle’s gotten to lie on top of a woman for years. OH! GOOD-NATURED BURN! I tell ya, these people know how to have a good time! Except for the fact that Kyle didn’t take a single damn sip outta me! What? Do I have schmootz on my rim? I know he’s giving some attention to some loud dudes in the corner, near the pool table. Jeez, I mean, I know that we owe a lot to those guys, they take care of ten percent of us beers, but they COULD be a little more respectful to women. They remind Kyle of guys like Kenjiro Ito, and Chandler Tsonda? What the hell does he mean by that? Oh, they’re douchebags, with no regards for other people. I get it now. Now wait a second, the “Me love you long time” line to the pretty Asian girl? That’s really classless, isn’t it? AND THIS IS A BEER TALKING! So Kyle ran off to the restroom to cool down. I don’t blame him. Heck, I feel myself getting warm! (And flat) Great, now they’re paying even MORE attention to sweet little Miho. I can see Jason’s not really loving this… Oh, thank goodness, he’s back. It’s a good thing, too. One of those fraternity douchebags from Monmouth University is hitting on that Asian girl. Well, at least he’s picking me up now. FINALLY! MY DESTINY SHALL BE FULFILLED! Why’s he looking at that guy like that? You know…he’s pretty pissed off, this Kyle guy. I hope that- … Hey, wait a second, kid! No fighting!!! DON’T DO SOMETHING YOU’RE GONNA REGREAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!!!! crash!
