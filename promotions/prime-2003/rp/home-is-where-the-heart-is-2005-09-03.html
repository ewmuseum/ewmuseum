Home is where the heart is!

3 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd Sep 2005 Friday 30th September 2005. 5:24pm A huge gust of wind billows out and strikes everyone in its vicinity as a small private jet descends to the tarmac. As the plane lands the door flies open and a set of stairs descend from inside the plane, crashing down onto the floor with a loud crash. A few crew members and certain people we haven’t seen before begin to make their way down the steps and onto the landing strip below. A short time elapses before the man we all know as ‘The Enforcer’ pops his head around the exit. Mark is looking rather flash and more sophisticated than normal as he begins to make his way down the steps and onto the tarmac. He is wearing a suit and tie, instead of his trademark long, black trench coat and a pair of black Armani shoes. He also wears a pair of dark black sunglasses as he breathes in the air and lifts his arms up into the air, basking in it all. Blackwolf: “Ahhh….home. Boston, Massachusetts, there couldn’t be a greater place to be right now.” A small figure of a man begins to run across the screen, blocking our view of the 6’11’’ behemoth as he makes his way towards Mark and his group of companions. He comes to a halt as he reaches the group and curdles over trying to catch his breath. Small Man: “Sir…Is your name Mark? Mark Blackwolf?” Blackwolf: “Yes, yes it is. Can I help you?” The smaller man regains his breath and stands up straight to address the colossal structure stood before him. Small Man: “Yes you can. I am here on behalf of Chuck Liddell of the UFC fighting circuit. I believe you know him” Mark smiles at the thought of his past times with the Heavyweight Champion of the UFC. This was the reason he had come back home to Boston, days before he was scheduled to be on PRIME Television in a match with Vangelus Oxios…..the punk who decided…..actually we’ll get to that later. Yes the reason he had come back to Boston was because Chuck had invited Mark to be in the front row to watch his first live UFC show in years. Mark could not turn down the opportunity to meet up with some past friends and enemies from the UFC circuit. Blackwolf: “Yes, I know him well, why? Is he here?” Small Man: “No sir, but he has scheduled for a limousine to pick you up here. He said not to worry about paying the chauffeur, that is already taken care of and he wanted me to give you this.” The small man reaches into his top pocket and produces a small card which Mark takes from the man, looking at it through his tinted eyes. Small Man: “That’s a VIP pass to the UFC show tonight. With that pass you can get backstage and all your food and drink will be free. He told me that he wanted you to feel right at home tonight and that I was to make sure you received everything you desired.” Mark slips the card into his top blazer pocket as he places his hand down onto the small man’s shoulder, the weight of his arm almost forcing the man down to his knees. Blackwolf: “Now this is something I could get used to. So lead the way, let’s get this show on the road.” Small Man: “Sure sir, but could you please take your arm off my shoulder, its starting to hurt.” Blackwolf: “Oops, sure sorry. Didn’t mean to hurt you there. Now does this limousine have a TV? A fridge?” Small Man: “Sure sir, it has everything you could hope to find in a limousine of this quality. If you’d just follow me, I’ll take you to it and we can be on our way.” Mark shakes hands with his companions and the few crew members that had stepped off the plane, before following the smaller man across the tarmac and into the airport. The airport was small and rather claustrophobic, but that didn’t matter much to Mark, he was going to be back outside soon and on his way to the Fleet Center to see some first class UFC action. Mark had not been at a UFC event for years and he couldn’t wait to get back there and meet some familiar and less familiar faces. They made their way through the small airport and out into the parking lot, where a long, black limousine awaited them. The limousine looked like it was fourteen foot long at least, which is about the same size Subway Mark always orders, but that’s for another time. The driver’s door opens and a rather tall, balding man steps out and makes his way down to where Mark and the smaller gentlemen we’re stood. He was obviously the chauffeur as he opened the passenger door to allow Mark and the small man to enter the limo. Mark had never received such great hospitality before ever and it was something he would love to get used to. Mark and the smaller gentlemen entered the limousine and sat down on the long, couch like, leather seats as the door slammed shut behind them, leaving them alone in the back of the limousine. Blackwolf: “So what’s your name? How do you know Chuck?” The smaller man removes his jacket and throws it over the back of the seat. Small Man: “The name’s Jake. Jake Summers and I’m a representative of Chuck’s. I handle most of his business.” Blackwolf: “So Jake how is Chuck, is he doing well? I watched his match last week and couldn’t help jumping for joy at the conclusion.” Jake: “Yeah he’s doing fine, a little bruised and banged up, but that comes with the business, you know.” Blackwolf: “Yeah I do, got a few injures myself in my time. Its going to be great to see Chuck again after all this time. How long do you think it will take us to get there?” Jake: “Not long, half an hour, an hour at the most. In the meantime you might as well make yourself comfortable. Switch on the TV; lets see what’s going on in the world.” Jake hands Mark the remote for the TV as he leans back on the couch making himself more comfortable. Mark presses down on the remote, bringing the TV to life and the first thing that comes into view is a repeat of ReVolution from last week. Vangellus Oxios is in the middle of the ring, running his mouth when suddenly Mark hears his name. Oxios: “Mark Blackwolf is also in this thing. Really, Blackwolf, I'm not impressed. You were in UFC. Big deal. Why am I not concerned one bit by your presence here? Simple. It started with the UFC openweight tournaments. Who won those? Nobody as big as you. And going on through the events, you'd see that the big guy would get frickin' owned in combat. Sure, maybe they weren't going in against a 6'10' 400 pound guy or whatever. But they really didn't have to. You know what your problem is? Lack of ambition. I mean, hell, you went to UFC, after all……” Mark slams his fist down into the couch as he listens in to the verbal abuse spewing out of Oxios’ mouth. Mark hadn’t had time to watch last week’s ReVolution and he was now kind of hoping he hadn’t. Blackwolf: “Who the hell does this guy think he is? I’d love to see his sorry ass step into a UFC cage, then maybe he’d learn a thing or to, like shutting his mouth.” Oxios: “For that matter, I don’t think anyone's impressed by that Kuk Sool Wan, Man Sucks Wang... whatever you wanna call your style. Somehow, between UFC, which totally deals with beating a guy senseless, and PRIME, you came to the realization that you couldn't hit anybody here hard enough to win. So you added the Inverted Powerbomb to your repertoire. Yeah, I'm sure Liddell trained you all night and day to make sure you could pull THAT off right. How about we say this -- you've got no idea what you're doing in this ring, but thanks to being the lummox that you are, you've managed to fall into people and get wins that way. You haven't shown me anything that your typical big man hasn't yet. If you're special, prove it. And I'll tell you, it's not going to be done by beating a motivationally-challenged Boda and a Polish guy who's shouting 'See that, Gibson!' after every lariat he hits.” Blackwolf: “Man Sucks Wang?!? He’ll be sucking through a damn tube when I’m finished with him. I can’t believe he even has the balls to stand up in the ring and run his mouth off like this. Has he beaten Gibson? No he’s not. I’ve had enough of this shit.” Mark switches the TV off and falls back into the couch. His feeling of happiness at finally being able to go back to the UFC and watch a live event had now been clouded by the fact a small blonde haired freak had been berating him in the middle of the ring. The fact that Mark wasn’t there to answer back was another reason for his sudden anger, but Oxios had better well know that he was going to be there this week and he would shut him up for good. Jake: “Sorry, did you say something? I was kind of distracted. These gorgeous looking women we’re just walking down the road and it would have been distasteful of me not to be staring at their asses as they walked past.” Blackwolf: “Forget it. Let’s just get this over with can we?”
