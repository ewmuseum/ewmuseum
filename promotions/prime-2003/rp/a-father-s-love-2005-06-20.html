A Father's Love

20 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.20.05 ' Father's Day 2005 ' I was around the age of sixteen years old when it happened. My life had taken many ups and downs prior to that moment, but never on such as this. I’ve seen my family members slaughtered before a mass gathering of barbaric and blood thirsty mortals. I’ve seen my friends battered and whipped just because they were friends of mine, especially seeing as my families’ race was labeled as the “Outsiders.” I’ve even held the blood of my mother’s violated flesh in the palms of my hand. But none of those gruesome moments could compare to this very moment, as I stood before my dear father examining his helpless condition with there tender thought that there was nothing I could do about it. It hurt. Tears fabricated in my heart, beckoning and demanding to be released, but I couldn’t allow it. My father always told me when I was a little child never to cry. According to him, it only indicated fear inside of a man’s heart. He also told me that fear welcomed in reprisal from thine enemies. The latter was the least of my worries, but the thought of violating my father’s advice kept the tears from falling. I just stood there, watching my Father in a condition I thought I would never have to see him in. I took a step forward and held his cold and comatose hand in mine. “You always told me it would never be too late to tell you I love you…you were wrong.” I took one last look at my father and closed the casket. A tear assembled in my eye, but I quickly took my hand and brushed it off. I retrieved one of the red roses lying on my father’s casket to remind me of his being, and then turned away to face the crowd of Myrants and Tahj citizens. They were all followers of my father’s crusade, and they all gathered to pay their last respects. I took the hand of my mother and slowly walked down the red carpeted aisle, my hands trembling from the thought of never seeing my father again. Suddenly, I fell to my knees in the middle of the aisle in front of millions, with the burden weighing heavy on my heart. I allowed myself to cry. -- Years had passed since that day in which I laid to rest my beloved father. The man who brought forth my existence on this earth. The man who took me under his wing as an infant and cared for me, only to ditch me as I grew in age to pursue his career in politics…and yet I still loved the man. Loved him, not for the fact that he shared that same compassionate love for me…Loved him, not for the fact that he asked me to…but I loved him because he was a man of power, a man I admired, a man I never thought I’d have to live my life without. I stood before the oak dresser as I picked up the rose, with one of the thorns mildly poking me in the tip of my index finger. This very rose was the only physical remains of that day, and I cherished it with every fiber in my body. Over the years, the rose had tarnished a bit, but it still held up. It reminded me much of my father, a tarnished man who still held up no matter what situation arose. That was how I knew my father. I never knew him personally, which is sad for a son to have to say about his father, but from the stories I have been told, and from the articles I have read, I knew exactly what type of man he was. A hateful, Despiteful, and Envious man… And I loved him for that. A tear manufactured in the corner of my eye, but I quickly swiped it away, once again being reminded of my father’s words of being a weak man. I never wanted to be as such. My father was a strong man who knew not the meaning of fear or any other image reducing words. He was an authoritative and prevailing man, and my desire to be just like him is what kept me miles away from a pool of tears. I took the rose and slowly ascended up the lengthy staircase, every step reminding me of him. It was as if I knew him, but yet I didn’t know him. I knew what had been told to me through the stories told before me, but yet I didn’t know his motives behind committing such scandalous acts. I knew about the articles I read in the newspapers and the reports shown on television, but I didn’t know what he was thinking, or what drove him to such a state. I knew Czar Olsig…but I had no clue as to who my father was. Finally I reached the middle of the staircase and sat down, looking down at the living room floor below. This was his house, but yet I had inherited among his death. Every brick that assembled it and every particle of dust that hovered through the air reminded me of him. It was as if I just couldn’t get him off of mind. I apologize if I am carrying on a bit. It’s just the fact that he was such an inspiration in my life, but yet he never was there for me. He was never there to witness my first A+ in grade school. He was never there to watch me take my first shot in a basketball game, or hear my first performance with the school choir. While I was out, pondering over the identity of my father, he was out committing crimes weaved together by the oh so familiar themes of Greed, Sex, and the world’s greatest manipulator…money. I miss my father. I sat there on the middle step as I heard footsteps from behind. I turned around and looked up to see Elden walking down with a silver platter in hand that consisted of two cups of cappuccino. Elden was my father’s main assistant during his reign over Tahj, and quickly assumed the duties of being mine upon my ascension to the throne. He was an elderly man, standing strong at the age of 73. Age still did not take away his wisdom though, and his commitment to the Olsig family which picked up some thirty or forty years ago with my Grandfather, Sir Mussolini Olsig. He could whip up a mean cappuccino as well. Elden stood on the step above the one I sat on and placed his hands on my shoulder, scanning my bowed head and the rose that I held in my hand. He automatically knew what I was thinking. It was like this every father’s day since his death, and Elden had learned to cope with my endless mourning. He would console me with jokes and warm cappuccino, attempting to clear my mind of my past. That was what made Elden so special to both me and my family…his willingness to act as a leaning post in my time of need. “Elden…tell me something.” I stated as I continued to look at the rose before me. “Anything, Sir.” He replied, assuming that I would ask him for advice on avoiding stressful mourning all while cutting around the edge of the obvious situation. That always was the question on father’s day…but not this time. This time it was different. “Tell me about my father.” I stated. It seemed to catch Elden off guard as he withdrew his hand from my shoulder and placed it over his chest with his eyes widening in a “Shock” manner. He slowly took one step down and sat on the same step as me and began to ponder for the right words to say in a time like this. “…That’s a…That’s a very complex question.” Elden stated, fidgeting with his hands in a shaky and nervous manner. “Was he that bad?” I asked, my impression of him lowering a bit. “Not bad, Sir Olsig…more like difficult to comprehend. Your father had a lust for power. Without it, he felt very vulnerable. He stated on many occasions that if it wasn’t for the death of his father…he would have killed himself. I know these things because he confessed them all to me, forcing me into a promise to never speak of them to anyone. I have broken that promise, and I very well may be condemned for it…but my time is slowly decreasing and I feel it is important that you know of these things.” Elden stated. I could feel the emotion in his crackling voice. “How did my father rise to power?” I asked, curious as to how he strayed from killing himself in such a hurried manner. “Well as I stated before, your father always told me that if it wasn’t for his father’s death which ultimately allowed him to take over the throne that you now hold and retrieve the power he so desired…he would have killed himself. He was just that weak, or so he thought. He did the only thing he thought fitting…” -- It was the fall of 1967 and your father was a mere 32 years of age. His father…your grandfather, Mussolini Olsig, was in the shape of his life and was running the land of Taj like a pro. I can honestly say that it was the greatest period of time to be a citizen of this great land. Your father, Czar Olsig, saw this and grew jealous…therefore he sought a plan to overtake his father’s government and create one of his own. The November 1967, a serial killer invaded the Taj land who went by the name ‘Assassin’. Rumors had it that the Serial Killer was a Outsider who was sent over by King Mango, your grandfather’s biggest rival, to assassinate Mussolini. Assassin wore a black outfit accompanied by a black layered ski mask. He wore gloves and used a butcher knife in order to kill his victims. It was obvious that he was after Mussolini, because all of the murders centered around Mussolini. First it was the assistant, Mrs. Anne Daming, whose position I now hold. Then it was the personal security guard and close friend of Mussolini, Arthur G. Gutman. The next victim came in the form of Mussolini’s uncle who had been reported missing for a month prior. It was later discovered through a letter sent by Assassin that he was also a victim to the serial killer. The land of Tahj bowed on December 13th, 1967 when the Serial Killer struck once again…this time he had supposedly killed your father, Czar Olsig. This hit the nation hard, and hit Mussolini even harder. Two more murders followed with the victims being a close friend of Mussolini who grew up with him and Mussolini’s personal chef. April 15th, 1968 came and with it…the attempted murder of Mussolini. The serial Killer snuck into the home of Mussolini, but Mussolini managed to fight him off and ended up exhausting the Killer in what Mussolini reported as a one hour fight between the two. All of the commotion brought in security to the room where they took the killer and unmasked him. It was your father, Czar Olsig. Apparently he had faked his death back in December in order to put to rest all accusations that he could be the killer. The land asked “How?”. The response came when Czar admitted that the body in which they had buried was a wax copy. The nation then asked “Why?”…but no one was more shocked by the unveiling than Mussolini. Two days later, Mussolini died. One of the bystanders to the death stated that she saw Czar kill Mussolini, but Czar stated that the accusation was false. A lie detector determined that he was indeed telling the truth…but the woman went to her grave swearing that Czar was the killer of Mussolini. The land of Tahj wanted Czar executed for his actions, but seeing as there was no Tyrant to declare the execution, Czar, by law, was ascended to the throne for the mere fact that he was Mussolini’s son. Of course he wasn’t going to declare execution to himself…he had finally gotten what he strived for all of these years. Power. This upset many of the citizens in Tahj and half of the population fleeted. A quarter of the other half sought revenge. Years of a carefully planned plot ultimately resulted in the death of your father…which I’m pretty sure you are aware of. -- “So that’s how he rose to power, eh? I never knew how deceitful and greedy of a man he was.” I stated, my heart nearly dropping with the story that had just been told to me. I always thought of my father as a man who took power by the neck and strangled every bit of it out…not a man who killed for power. “Greedy indeed. If I know your father like I believe I know him, though…I must say that way deep down in the gallows of his black heart, there was a sense of love. A love for his family, a love for me…and a love for you, Sir Olsig.” He stated, attempting to console me in my time of need. “You really believe so, Elden.?” I asked, lifting my head for the first time to look into his eyes. Elden stood and patter me on my shoulder. “I know so, Sir Olsig.” He stated as he picked up the silver platter he had placed down earlier and mounted up the staircase, leaving me to look down at the living room floor where images of him flashed before my very eyes. I looked over to the fireplace where a picture of him holding me in his arms as an infant stood. “…Too bad it hurts too much to love him back.” -- I took the needle and placed the tip on my shoulder, pressing down as I allowed the fluid’s that the needle contained to enter into my body and takes its toll. The demon drug circulated through my veins, taking every aspect of pain and tenderness that my past had brought me and temporarily disposing of it, just for this moment…just for this moment of soothing greatness…this feel of ultimate success. I felt as if I had achieved something. But deep down inside, I knew that I had achieved nothing but a fabrication of depression, even if I was momentarily numb to it. Nothing else mattered at this moment…nothing but the drug. It had taken me and basically raped me of my pride, my dignity, my decorum, just for this moment of sin-filled ease. Just for this one moment, to be able to escape the abyss of life. Words could not describe my feelings right about now, but I didn’t care because speaking, or thinking for that matter, were the least of my worries. I just wanted more, so I pressed harder soaking the tube dry of every inch of its contents. It felt so good. Suddenly, I began to lose my breath. I released the grasp of the needle as it still stood placed in my arm. I grabbed my red neck and began to cough profusely, struggling for air or breath. I could not breathe…my eyes watered and my heart pounded as if it wanted to tear a hole through my chest and jump out of it. I didn’t know what to do…I was dying. My hand was shaking like a vibrator, but I still managed to grasp the needle and yank it out of my hand. My body and limbs grew so weak that I could barely move, but still I managed to throw the tube into the mirror attached to the dresser. Small pieces of glass shattered and crashed down on top of the dresser. I fell to one knee, still coughing and struggling for air. Finally I began to fall back, but I reached up and grabbed one of the pieces of glass from the dresser on my way down. My arm was so numb, that I desperately took the end of the glass and slashed a cut in my right shoulder. I could feel nothing, I just continued to struggle for air. Blood rushed from my arm as my view grew dizzy and I fell unto my back holding my neck. I took one last cough as my eyes closed. I was out. During my unconsciousness, though, I could hear voices…laughing. Familiar voices, they sounded like Needles Scharf, my alter ego. The man who vowed revenge on me for betraying him. The man who constantly told me that he was indeed the brains behind Dani Furher, and that my eagerness to stray away from him would not go unpunished. Of course, it was all in my head…but it seemed so real. His laughter…his screeching and heart piercing laughter. How could anything mortal produce such a thing. I heard a voice whisper…but it wasn’t Needles. I’ve finally got you. …It was him. -- It began at the PTC Global Turnbuckle Tournament 4 when I pinned your shoulders to the mat. It ascended two weeks ago when I did it again. Now I have been slated before you once again. But for what, I ask.. For respect? For Dignity? I raped you of your respect the first time I left you in a pool of your own blood. I raped you of your dignity two weeks ago when I left the almighty Hoyt Williams, battered in the center of the ring. This time, I'll rape you of your pride. I'll leave you descending into a state of Lunacy, screaming out to God for a salvation from the madness. I'll leave you broken in the center of that ring, with no motive for wrestling besides a paycheck to buy a loaf of bread. I'll leave you torn and lost without a light in sight. why? Because I can. Because through you, I will receive another title shot and take what’s rightfully mine. Because through destroying you, I will prove that I am superior to you and your legacy. I'll piss on your legacy and create one of my own. Because through you, I will achieve ultimate success. I am here for a purpose. You are here as a pawn in said purpose. I did it once. They called it a fluke. I did it twice. They called it beginners luck. Maybe once I defeat you this time... They'll finally realize that Vangelus Olsig is the real deal. Vangelus Olsig is the ram in the bush. Vangelus Olsig is a diamond in the rough. Vangelus Olsig... Is the shit. Get on your knees Hoyt... no...I wouldn't pleasure you like that. I mean get on your knees and pray. pray for hope. pray for salvation. pray that God, your self proclaimed father... have mercy on your soul. Because I can assure you I wont. fin.
