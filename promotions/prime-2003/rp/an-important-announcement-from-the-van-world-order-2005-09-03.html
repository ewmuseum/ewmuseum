An Important Announcement from the Van World Order

3 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/3/05 Seattle, Washington. In the United States. If you needed that clarification, stop reading right now and go jam a geography book up your ass. Gabriel Brown and Vangellus Oxios have, or rather had, a location there. A jointly owned apartment near enough to a wrestling gym where Brown could spend the better part of the week training Oxios to be a passable tag team partner. Van is a cool guy, if you can take the dragging of the fourth wall out back and the vicious beating of it with a lead pipe, but not what you would call a technically-proficient wrestler. Or even a technically-basic wrestler. In fact, even calling him a wrestler would be a stretch. Jeff Hardy looks like American Dragon when placed side-by-side with Vangellus Oxios. But to the point, it was time to close up shop. After all, the Oxios/Brown team had collapsed after Colossus II. The man formerly known as Invincible Gabe~! wanted to move on to other things, after scratching his tag-team-itch one more time. The problem was that he tried to recreate Invincibleness! without his close friend Scott 'Rampage' Matthews, with whom he'd come up in Pikeville, Kentucky. They were to have the same 'silly boy/straightman' dynamic, but it just didn't click the way Brown wanted it to. It was a momentary lapse in judgment for the normally astute tag team specialist Gabriel. He forgot that it was the personal chemistry between he and Scott that made their team work. Matthews was a good foil in terms of size, his ability to back up Brown by sheer force of will and strength alone. While he was a joke as Invincible Gabe, Brown could still take a great deal of punishment and every now and then might actually do a move worth hurting the opponent; but they legitimately feared the aptlt-titled 'Rampage'. When would he next go off? Who would be in the ring with him when it happened? Could the opposing team still prevail? That's what 'Teh Pwn' lacked when they came in. There was Gabriel Brown as the jack-of-all-trades, and then there was Oxios as the rookie partner who wasn't physically dominating. The team itself... it just didn't work too well. Its only win was in a tag team match that involved 3 other teams on its end. Chaos, with a 'W' in the win column without scoring the decisive victory. A shame. After the break up of that team, Oxios called on Brown's former partner Scott Matthews (it was Gabriel who contacted Van about teaming). And the torch, in a way, was passed to a new team. Only this time it was made up of two rookies, with probably five wrestling moves between them. Oxios as the funnyman as he'd always been, Matthews as the scary big man. They started off with some kinks to work out, losing to The Forsaken in the two teams's debut match. But then they came right back the week later, defeating Forsaken and The Flying Vergomovs in a triple-threat tag team match. And then the next week, Civil Disobedience fell to them in a #1 contender's match. Teh Pwn v2.0 seemingly had some chemistry there, which carried them through the match with the lackadaisical champions Los Diablos, getting them the tag title straps. Of course, twenty minutes later, they lost them to Civil Disobedience, wrestling two matches back to back. Now here was Sky High Oxios, completely closing the book on the Oxios/Brown by putting the Fortress of Pwnitude back on the housing market (Seattle's too gloomy and rainy anyway; I mean, if someone wants to live in that kind of weather, they'd move to England where there's at least quality programming and better people overall). And that included one final task done with the assistance of Richard Hargrove, Vangellus Oxios's webmaster/computer whiz. The 50+ year old hacker was coaxed into coming back into the service of Vangellus after The Whole Freaking SHO made him an offer he couldn't refuse (namely that Hargrove work for money). Rich was easy to get a hold of, he'd been using the Fortress of Pwnitude, living out of it after getting a key made without Van's permission. Imagine Oxios's surprise when he found pizza boxes scattered on the floor (billed to the owner of the Seahawks... HA!) and Richard passed out on the floor amid honey bun wrappers with Cheetos spores all over his mouth, fingers, and shirt. With little to do that chilly September morn in the apartment, most of the furniture already packed up so that Oxios could move his things back to Windsor, he had Richard earn his keep by logging into the PRIME website (quite illegally, of course, but that's how the Rich Man rolls) for a quick project. 'Is it done yet?' Vangellus asked, twiddling his thumbs while sitting on top of the Dell Precision 370 tower, the casing incredibly sturdy and easily able to support his full weight. Richard still didn't like this; after all, he respected Van's things. Except for that one time he got red-faced drunk and tried for 20 minutes, unsuccessfully, to find the flush handle Oxios's sofa after pissing all over it. With a final keystroke, Hargrove pressed the enter key and the confirmation popped up on the screen. 'Yeah,' he yawned. 'Your bio is updated now. Just like you dictated.' V.O. Burns, his 104-year-old body hunched forward and pumping blood at a breakneck pace of three beats per minute, cups his fingers together. 'Excellent....'
