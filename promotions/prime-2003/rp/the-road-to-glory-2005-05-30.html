The Road to Glory...

30 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/30/05 Finally the tag team titles have come home to where they belong over the shoulder of the massive masked men known as Los Diablos. Winning the titles has been the goal of these fine young men since they came into the PRIME federation and they have finally achieved that goal. It wasn't the typical easy road, in fact it was a road paved with many ups and downs. But isn't that the type of road that makes the eventually reaching your destination that much sweeter? I've heard it said many times, by many gringos, that winning championships in a big time federation on the North American scene is like winning the lottery. You feel like you've gained everything the world has to offer, the chance to be someon is finally yours, and being able to hold your head up with pride is forever more yours. I say that is crazy essa, cause it ain't the winning the titles that means anything. Sure winning the belts was the hardest thing for Los Diablos to do thus far in their young careers. Angelo Deville was able to defeat them more times than I'll ever care to remember but the boys kept coming back. They kept fighting, kept trying harder, and kept on attempting to prove that they could hang with anyone in the entire world. They did that and they did it long before they ever even won the titles. Winning the titles was the final piece in the first layer of proving that fact. Los Diablos is now the force to be reckoned with that I always knew they could be and should be. The time has come to see if they have what it takes for the next step in proving themselves and allowing themselves to keep their heads held high. Far too often in this business people believe once they have won the title, no matter which one it happens to be, that the fight is over. They think they are able to simply sit back and bask in the glory of being a champion and that some how simply carrying the belt will allow them to waltz right through the rest of their matches without even breaking a sweat. It's this type of fool who is caught blind in the middle of a busy intersection during rush hour. One moment they are champion, the next they are just some other person on the list of people who used to have something to offer everyone else. Don't believe me just ask Boda what it's like to be that type of wrestler essa, cause he knows first hand what it's like to think you are someone simply cause you win and title and then have someone else come along and knock you off the high horse. At the moment my boys are on that high horse. They are on the top of the mountain that they set out to climb up and take control of with the PRIME tag team titles now theirs to call their own. I'm proud of them, make no mistake about that gringos, but I also know they have a whole lot more to learn. They have a lot more to accomplish and the only way to do that is to quickly learn the golden rule. No not the one about he who has the gold makes the rules. Figures some foolish American would come up with a stupid idea like that and try to market it as actual fact. The rule they must learn is that nobody is suddenly going to lay down and let them win, simply cause they have some straps on their shoulders now. Those titles only make them even bigger targets then they already were in the first place. The boys have to learn, and it's a tough lesson for many to learn, that the hard work is just now beginning. Holding onto the titles is always far harder than the trip you take in getting the titles. Teams are going to come out of the wood work now, like last week on ReVolution, that wouldn't have cared in the world who Los Diablos was the week before. Without those straps my boys making a challenge like that would have meant little to nothing more to anyone, other than simply another chance to get in the ring and attempt to chalk up another win for their career. The titles change everything. Those gold titles, no matter what anyone wants to try and say, will lead greedy men down the whatever path is necassary to obtain them. A person needs look no further than Angelo Deville for one hundred percent proof of this fact. Last week, even after he was talking about simply walking away from the belts, he fought tooth and nail to retain them. He gave Los Diablos everything he had, once more, in yet another attempt to deny them from obtaining their goal. He even was willing to turn to the man that he neither trusted, nor who trusted Deville himself, in an effort to make sure that Los Diablos didn't take that gold from his waist. Karma can be a bitch though gringo. Make no mistake about this Deville I know you are quite the wrestler. You have taken my boys, Baltasar in particular, to the very limits of their abilities and beyond and come off smelling like a rose many times. You have proven your 'legend' to be everything that people have said and even more. I remember when you once told us that karma was a bitch cause we cost you the Dual Halo victory. You have been a thorn in our sides for a long time now, so I am an expert at this point on the subject. You spoke words so true that if God himself said them they wouldn't have been believed anymore than they are now. Guess now your very own advice is coming back to bite YOU on the ass this time. The road to glory is paved with good intentions. Every single person who ever steps foot into a wrestling federation has nothing but good intentions for themselves and their career when it all begins. They all come in wide eyed and ready to prove that they are the next person to notch their name among those who grace the honored and exclusive Hall of Fame of any given federation. PRIME is no different in the respect that they have seen their fair share of kids come in with a pocket full of dreams and a wish list of things to accomplish a mile long, only to turn around and disappear a month later cause the road to glory was far tougher than they could have ever imagined. I fear that my boys could fall prey to this type of behavior. I'm not talking about the leaving after a short period, but the allowing that tough road to break them and cause them to forget about why they came him. The last thing I want for them is to see them crack and become just another Tom,John, Dick, or Joe in the midst of the stars that are remembered like Startling, Rolo, English, and others. That vile road is plagued by those who would destroy Los Diablos and all they care for if it would net them their own personal gains. Now more than ever is the time for Los Diablos to prove themselves as the force I believe them to be. Today begins the walk down the long and winding road that leads them to immortality. Along the way it is going to be their job to watch the backs of their fellow Imperium brothers as they walk their own paths to greatness. The game has just begun... Scene begin The training area was dark,almost pitch black, as I sat there all alone in the dark. I came down here to train about an hour ago, but I haven't found the desire. No matter which machine I sat at or stood near I couldn't get the needed push to go into my daily routine. Instead I opted to simply turn out the lights and sit on this wooden bench near the door way and just stare. At nothing. The nearly complete darkness that engulfed my body seems to almost give me a sense of complete peace, like I've not felt in a very long time. I rest my head against the wall and just shut my eyes, allowing the darkness to over take me completely. All I can think about is how I can't seem to think about wrestling this week on ReVolution, no matter how hard I try to focus. 'It is not a good thing essa.' I say to myself in my dark solitude. For the first time in my career, specially with PRIME, I am sitting here unable to find one good reason to train, to prepare, to ready myself for the clear challenge ahead of me. As I sit in the dark I raise my hand to my face and rub it. In the dark I don't have the mask on that would normally be seen by the world. This is a private moment, one of those times when a man can simply sit back and reflect on what is going down in his life. For me it seems like my life is suddenly at a stand still. My career couldn't be in a better position at this point, with the tag team titles now belonging to Angelus and myself. Yet, still, I can't help but feel like this week isn't important. 'Hessian and TFV.' The names roll off my tongue as if they were nothing more than your average, every day, run of the mill gringos that no one cared to see. I'm no fool though and I realise that they are far more. Hessian is a force no matter who he faces and the man comes to the ring on a mission every single week. He has already done a number on Enrique before, even causing Angelus and myself to take notice. The Flying V's, as I like to call them, are no less dangerous. They are a potent tag team that would like nothing more than to take Los Diablos down in a six man, and prove they belong in the same ring with us...only with our titles on the line. That is fine by me, it isn't even the idea of them earning a shot at the titles that bothers me. Honestly I have no cause for fear of any team any longer. I've beaten the best that this sport has to offer in order to obtain that gold strap that meant so much to me. Finally I exercised the demon that was Angelo Deville. 'Angelo Deville.' His name rolls off my tongue with a perfect mixture of hate and respect. It is the perfect combination of admiration and jealousy that provides the hatred needed to fuel a encounter with Deville. It was never about believing myself the better of a man who has more than proven himself to be one of the top wrestlers on this planet. It was more so proving to the world, and more importantly to his arrogant ass, that I belonged in his leauge, his rings, and in the same breath when his name was mentioned. 'I've proven that.' I remind myself as I think about the title that I took from him our last encounter. The title means the world to me, but it wasn't really what proved my worth; it was icing on the cake. Taking Deville to the limit four times was the proof that I desired and craved so much. The titles almost, maybe even did, become an after thought in my obsession to prove that I was on his level, that he should respect my ability to take it to the limit in the ring. Whether he willingly will admit it isn't even the issue. I don't need him to say it to the world for me to know. It is a look you can see in the eyes of a man who you have faced off with on more than one occassion. After just a couple of times you can read whether the man respects who they are competing against. I can see that look in Deville's eyes every time we face now. It's there burning as brightly as our hatred for one another and the things we both stand for. 'Pride, honor, respect.' Three things that we both care a great deal about and it's all three of those things that make us drive to destroy one another that much more. To earn that which we don't have without defeating the other. I realise while I am sitting here and thinking about my fights with Deville why I can't get up for this week. Why the feeling to destroy something simply won't come to me. 'There is no Deville...no hatred.' I say to myself with almost a chuckle as if it is funny to realise that I could possibly blow a match this week, simply cause I don't want to view it as important as facing Deville. I can't have these kinda feelings and there is no place in this business for a man who will take an opponent like Hessian or the Flying V's for granted. They are not some pawns to be forgotten and take as a sacrifice on the board of wrestling's eternal chess game. 'Some how, in some way, I must make myself forget Deville and take my passion to everyone else.' I stand up and simply take in a deep breath of air. I know that I must focus and I have to make a statement with this match up. Allowing my fights with Deville the the things that define me... Will be the death of me. Scene Ends
