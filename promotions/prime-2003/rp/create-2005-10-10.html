Date: 10/10/05 An army of shadows. And I am the only thing that can stop them. Imperium. From the ashes of Killean Sirrajin has risen this...this curse. Hoyt Williams with the Universal Championship. Imperium having the power to make this company fall to its' knee's. Before, I would have been helpless like the majority of the roster here. Weak. Useless. However, one man changed that. Like Doctor Frankenstein, Peter Vetra created a monster. A monster that has since gone on to rip apart every single man or woman put in front of him in this company. Culture Shock. The controversy. Baffled by it, I was caught off guard by Peter Vetra. A man willing to try and make a name for himself at my expense. Thinking I could just roll through him like nothing, I underestimated him. I thought my skill set would make quick work of him. I was wrong. Bait a hook with my wife and he took it. But when you fish, there is the possibility that the fish will snap the line, and go away with your bait and hook. He took my prized hook. He took her from me. Made me play defense to his offense. And then, he defeated me. He ran me out of this company. You see, before then, I thought I could cruise here. The record showed it. My performance showed it. But that...what he did, it stuck with me. Fueled me. Getting my wife back, that was something I was willing to give up wrestling for. But I got back in. Back into PRIME. But even before that, I renewed my focus. Put wrestling front and center on my radar. Wrestling was going to become my life. I was going to train my body, hone my skills, push my mind to the brink of madness. All for the performance. All to knock down people who thought that I was a joke. That thought that I was an overrated prospect. Success followed. Karina Wolfenden? Tapped. Nova? Tapped. Dave Gibson? Tapped. Angelo Deville? So scared of the Gridlock he rather take a pinfall loss than tap. Tropical Turmoil, I stepped into that ring with five others. Five of the best this company had. And it was me who stood tall in the end. Stood tall and screamed as I became the number one contender for the belt I had coveted since the day I stepped foot in this company nearly one year ago. The circle is coming together fittingly enough. At Culture Shock, I could touch the Universal Championship. Pushed it against my blood soaked head and sighed as I had done what I had set out to do all those years ago. And then, in the debut of Angelo Deville, I was forced to pass out from blood loss. Not even looked at to see if I had a little bit of fight in me. The Dual Halo controversy and Peter Vetra who took advantage of it. The coming and going of Angelo Deville. I was run out of this company by him and Imperium. Gone for sure were my dreams of being the Universal Champion. I must thank Peter Vetra, even though my lifestyle obsession has caused a rift in my marriage. I must thank Peter Vetra for unleashing the beast that would bring his God to his knees. Hoyt Williams, the Universal Champion. Fitting how his closest disciple caused his downfall oh so long ago. At King of Kings 2005, I will reach skyward and pull down the Universal Championship. I will beat back the army of shadows who sought out to kill my dreams. But the cycle stops before the raging climax. Before I can bring Armageddon to David Koresh and his merry band of followers, it comes back to the Vetra. The mortar. The man who tried to make a name for himself at my expense and unknowingly unleashed Brandon Youngblood upon PRIME. I've waited for my chance at vengeance. Waited for a clear shot to rip you apart limb from limb, Pete. You see, it's evident that I won't be overlooking you again. What's the saying? Fool me once, shame on you? Shame on you for TRYING. Shame on you for bringing a bomb to a gun fight. You didn't kill me last time. Your mistake. You let me live. Left me an in. Let it fester inside me for all this time. You're not a disciple to me. You're not a sacrifice and you're not a message to Hoyt. You're a man I've been wanting to hurt for OH so long! The issue between you and me, it's more personal than any I have had. Not Nova, not Deville, not Wolfenden, not even Mike Randalls can lay claim to destroying me physically and mentally. The sad fact is, Pete, you've done nothing of real merit since that time. See, the biggest moment of your life was when you took me down. Knocked me down a few pegs. That was the greatest you could ever be. And you realize that. You realize that you got a shot in and the heavily favored opponent went down. But now, you will be going into Revolution 79 looking to repeat that feat. Force lightning to strike the same place twice. The ironic thing is, without me running out in the storm with a tin foil hat and an antenna clasped in my hands, you would not have gotten that ONE lightning strike. Hasim Rahman all over again. But you do realize that even thought Rahman knocked Lewis down and beat him, Lewis punked his ass out in the rematch in the fourth round. The underdog only gets so many shots. That's why they stay away from the giant they slay, because they know the giant will come back more focused than ever to knock them down. I know you want me Vetra. I know it because of your obsession all that time ago to end my professional wrestling career. You are a man who thinks that he is better than me. Someone who believes in himself above all others. Someone who actually proved it, giving fuel to their lunacy. The funny thing is, as much as you can pound your chest, I'm still here. I'm about to take the title away from Imperium. All you've done is made everything simplistic. This won't be a tune up match. And I don't care if I get hurt. I don't care if I break my leg. I will beat you to a bloody goddamn pulp, Peter Vetra, then put you in the Gridlock and make you tap. Make you submit. END our conflict. I'm bigger. Stronger. Faster. Tougher. Smarter. And I just want it more than you ever possibly could. I hate you more than you ever come possibly hate me. And I want more than ANYTHING to hear the sound of your bones breaking. Of your pitiful screams for mercy. You created me, Pete. You should be happy. Should be proud of your handiwork. You got inside my head and gave it a good stir. Are you scared Pete? Are you scared that the trail of bodies I've left behind because of you is about to come full circle? Are you scared that after all this, you'll just be a footnote to the greatest championship run in the history of professional wrestling? What are you going to say to Hoyt when he does tap? You should apologize. You should be praying beyond prayer that I die before I get into that squared circle. Sting once called himself a great white shark, and said he would chew Ric Flair up and spit him out. You won't get that satisfaction. I ripping you apart, and I'll let you die from the blood loss. Let the wolves feast on your entrails. And all these thoughts, they bring a smile to my face. The cycle is about to be completed. Tell me, Doctor Frankenstein, do you like what you see?
