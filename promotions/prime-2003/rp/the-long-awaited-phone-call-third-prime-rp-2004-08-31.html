The long awaited phone call........ [Third PRIME RP]

31 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-31-04 It’s been almost a month now. And not a day goes by, that I sit by my phone and wait for some kind of good news. I’ve become some kind of recluse as of late. Not wanting to go out, not wanting to leave the phone. I’m afraid that I’ll miss the call. “Sir, we’ve located your girls, and their just fine. We’ll have them home in an hour.” I anxiously await to hear that every waking moment of every day for the last month. But nothing like that has happened yet. That doesn’t mean the phone here hasn’t rang at all. My mother has called, worried to death about not only the girls, but me too. She knows the girls are my world. They are what keeps me grounded and surreal. Stacey, my ex and the mother of my two girls call, somehow blaming me through this all. Bitch. There has been one constant element with me this whole time. Athena. She has been a trooper through this all. Standing by me, taking care of me, loving me. But my heart is still empty at this moment. There are two holes in it. Mackenzie. Carleigh. I did get a phone call Monday, from PRIME’s Powers That Be. Apparently, I’ve been booked in this Jewel In The Crown tournament. Against Dragon Silver in the first round, this coming Revolution. Now, I remember the day after my girls were taken, that I had put in for time off, to straighten this out. And I do remember being told that I can take as much time as needed. So I have been wondering all week why I was booked in this thing. So I called the main offices, and told them……. “I’m in no way ready to come back to this. Book someone else.” After all, look at me. I haven’t worked out in a month, I’m unshaven, disheveled, a complete and utter mess. And that’s just the physical mess. Don’t forget the mind fuck that something like this plays on ones mind. My girls are innocent angels. Precious angels, that did nothing to anyone, other than melt my heart every time they kiss me good morning and hug me goodnight. And some piece of shit asshole motherfucker has taken them from me. So after calling back, PRIME told me that they’re sorry, they forgot, and that I won’t have to worry about my match. Now I can sit back, reclused in my house and ponder why my girls were taken from me. Dragon Silver….. you’re damn lucky that I’ve got personal problems to deal with. Good luck in the tournament. “Chris……..you have a phone call.” Athena pops her head into view. Boda stands back, taking his face away from the mirror he is staring into. Indeed, the big man looks like shit, after all, he’s has a lot on his mind. Who can blame him though. “All right, I’ll be right there.” Chris walks across the room he’s in, and walks into the hallway of his Orlando home. Picking up the hallway phone, he answers “Hello?” But he’s met with nothing but silence.” Hello… This is Chris. Can I help you?” Again, nothing on the other line. “All righty then, you’re done wasting my time. I’m hanging up now.” Boda starts to put the receiver down. “HANG UP AND YOUR KIDS WILL DIE” A sick, gravely voice yells from the other end of the telephone line. Boda yanks the receiver back to his ear. “WHAT?” Boda can not believe what he just heard. “Who are you? Where are my girls?” “They’re safe……. for now. Boy, do they miss you and their mother.” The voice penetrates Boda’s ears, like finger nails on a blackboard. “Listen, you piece of shit….. You hurt my kids, I’ll spend the rest of my life hunting you down, and when I find you, I’ll kill you myself.” The anger in Boda’s voice gets more prominent with each passing moment. “I swear to God……..” “THERE IS NO GOD. THERE IS ONLY ME” The voice on the other end screeches through the receiver. Boda punches the wall, putting a hole in the sheetrock. Athena pops into the hallway, wondering what just happened. Boda stands back, and takes two deep breathes. “Why, now, after a month do you decide to call me? Are my girls really okay?” Boda has momentarily calmed down. Athena moves in, but Boda puts his hand up, stopping her from coming forward. Athena turns and heads back into the living room. “Because, Chris……I’ve had no reason to call you before. But now, I do. I wanted to let you know the girls are safe…… so far. But that can all change, depending on your next move.” “My next move?” Boda seems perplexed. “Yes Chris. Seems you’ve decided to stay at home till this little kidnapping case is solved. Well, that’s not the way I planned this out. I want to see you wrestle. I want to see you get in the ring, and do what you do best….. fight. I want to see you fight for your girls’ freedom.” The man on the other side speaks, with an evil calm. “I want to see you in that ring, fighting like you want to save your girls.” “How do I know they are safe still? That you haven’t hurt them?” Chris almost cries out for the answers. “Show up at Revolutions, and I’ll show you that they are still okay. Don’t show, and you’ll not want to see what I can do to your girls.” And the line goes dead. Boda slowly hangs up the phone. Athena pops into the hallway. “Looks like we’re going to San Jose.” Boda still throws off a somber ambiance. “I’ll get to see my girls if I wrestle at Revolution. I guess this kidnapper is some sort of freaky fan, that’s trying to get too personal with me. So looks like I’m going to be in this Jewel in The Crown tournament after all. Guess Dragon Boy won’t be getting too far along in the tourney after all. This sicko wants to see me fight….. I’ll show him how he’s fucked himself up taking my girls…………..” “……………..Just hope Dragon Silver doesn’t take it personally.”
