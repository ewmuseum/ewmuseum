Date: Sometime Before Wednesday "You may think I’m nuts, but it’s totally different up here." "I don’t think you’re nuts Nicholas." I listened to her voice on the other end. The tones in her speech, the feeling in her words... it all was extremely soothing. It was different than what I was used to. Being in the world of wrestling, honesty was rare, almost that of a Blue Moon. She had to be one of the only people I enjoyed talking to, which is why I didn’t complain when she substantiated her claim of me losing my marbles. "Driving a short distance is one thing. You did it around here for the last series of events. But you’re gonna drive all the way across Canada and back." Yeah, she was right. As a matter of fact I was in the car at that exact time, wheels a turning on the cold pavement of Highway 55 in Quebec about one mile outside of Ayer’s Cliff. My satellite radio was working overtime keeping me sane for this leg of the trip. It would undoubtedly continue its magic for the other legs across the country. "I have been to many places in the world Rachelle. I’ve been from Japan to England and most places in between. I’ve been to Australia and even New Zealand. Heck, I’ve been to a few places in Africa and South America... Brazil is awesome by the way. But there’s this large land mass just north of the US and I’ve been to two cities. This drive will be the ultimate in concentration and relaxation. Shit... maybe I’ll even do what that rat fuck Blair wants and become a model employee." "Yeah I’m SURE that’s an option." The sarcasm in her voice was humorous, almost comical in its delivery. Like someone who has known me all my life, she knew the way I operated. I was nothing like a sneaky cheetah, crawling up on its prey to surprise it. Nor was I like the typical race horse, whipped into shape and earning money and respect for my boss so to speak. No, I looked at myself more like a shark in the meal infested waters of the business. I wasn’t sneaky because everyone knows I’m there with how outspoken I am. Yet I attack with poise, with confidence. Not to mention I am defiant until the end. The end would be when I say it is, with me leaving. I was not ready to leave PRIME. The GCW contract was nothing but a part of my plan, to push the hand of PRIME, make them listen to my demands. Now I have to put in some extra work, but it will all be worth it when the time comes that I sign a new contract and Blair, Tyler and the Board are kissing my dirty fuckin’ feet to bring in more money and more publicity for the widely known vessel known as PRIME wrestling. After saying my goodbyes to Rachelle, I returned the phone to my belt clip and continued my trek on the relatively deserted highway. I turned on the satellite radio and fell into the ultimate of relaxation, tunes ringing in my head and the world in front of my eyes. The miles were clicking off on the odometer and I found myself sinking into this world. |~o~| "You gotta be kidding me..." That was my only response to the ludicrous suggestion that was presented. This guy, some scrawny goof from the Head Office arrived in Montreal a bit early and needed to get some promotional work done for the Jewel in the Crown. He needed a poster boy and well I’d like to think I didn’t fit the description he was looking for. In fact, I thought I was the opposite from what he wanted. He didn’t seem to think so. "Blair told me to find you. He said you’d be perfect for the role." I almost threw up in my mouth hearing the mention of Blair wanting me to do promo material. It was the biggest insult after I was told I wasn’t good enough for a contract. He really was trying to turn me into the company monkey, working for nothing until I made a big enough splash to expect more. I felt like punching this guys teeth down his cock infested throat, but I thought the better of it. "Look.... Bill was it?" I put my arm around his shoulder and started walking, "Actually it’s Bert." He replied, trying to correct me. "Really it doesn’t matter what your name is Bob. What matters is that I’m not your guy... not even close. So why don’t you fix up that tie, clean those shoes and go running to find someone like Easton Hall for your promo. The guy looks like the Shaggy fuckin’ Dog, wouldn’t that appeal to some people who grew up in that era?" "I don’t think you understand Mr. Donnelly... the company wants someone with an attitude, someone with confidence to burn for this spot and you’re name was the first mentioned. Everyone thought it was perfect." He explained. "Everyone thought it was the perfect ploy to make me bow to their requests." I thought to myself. Obviously there was no fighting this. The Ego of New England was about to become a PRIME Billboard and needless to say, I wasn’t the least bit happy about it. |~o~| So here I was... my ass in a makeup chair and people all around me, trying to make me look the way they wanted. I was having none of it and the moment that someone went near my already flawless hair, I gave them a look that would haunt them the rest of their days. I wasn’t really an intimidating person physically, but I was sure they knew I could unleash a verbal hell on them that would make them hang themselves the same night in their basements. Plus these tools were your typical stuck up Frenchmen from Quebec. I found myself liking the fact that one event was being held here. "One minute Donnelly." A voice called out from behind me. Here it was, the time I thought would never come. I was to be in a PRIME Promo... I shook my head and got up from the chair. The makeup people barely did any damage to my already wonderful features and I wasn’t about to be turned into some makeup piece like some child’s doll. I shoved away their last minute makeup attempts and straightened my leather jacket, something they suggested I not wear. I nipped that suggestion in the ass quick as I could. "What’s this?" Bob or Bill or whatever his name asked when I approached the green screen. "I thought we agreed to lose the coat?" "You agreed and I didn’t. Let’s get something straight here... you want me in this crappy thing, it happens under my terms. Fuck your makeup, your professional tailors and your supposed master hairdressers. No one touches my hair or my clothes. Get me?" "Blair said you’d be difficult with this." He replied, the attitude was pouring from him in buckets and I really wanted to slap that smirk off his face. "Did he also mention that your Mom is a cum guzzling Queen that enjoys ass to mouth? If not, I will. Now go get that camera rolling so I can get the fuck outta here." I threatened him and it seemed to make him hightail his way across the studio. The scene was set. For the next ten minutes and five takes, I stood in front of a screen, talking to a damn camera and swinging a fist at it. I had one line... "To be King, by any means necessary." It was followed by a swinging punch, meant to shatter the CGI on the actual piece. It would be combined with other pieces from talent in the tournament and turned into a commercial. They wanted five different takes and afterwards, I wanted five different drinks. |~o~| I had hiked to the highest point in Montreal. Mount Royal had many hidden footpaths on its way to the top. I followed my gut and took a short cut and sure enough it paid off. I found myself looking straight into the Heart of Downtown Montreal. It was getting dark and I was up higher than most buildings, hardly a mountain, it would do for some relaxation and thinking time. I was a commercial, a slide show for Blaine Blair’s scheme to get me to straighten up and fly right. More often than not I found myself as a sucker for punishment. I stood, just gazing down at the city, the people roaming the streets. I heard car horns in the distance and saw lights flicking on and off in the cities tallest office buildings and apartment complexes. I was watching the world operate from a perch... a throne if you will. Upon reaching this point, I immediately took out my digital camera and took a few nice shots. They would make some great souvenirs and post cards for Rachelle. She had never been to Montreal and was always interested in what the place was like. I also thought about making a post card out to Easton Hall. Let me know how it turned out... "Dear Mr. Hall, I really don’t wish you could be here. It’s a great view of all the Frenchie Tools in the city and I am sure you’re mingling among them down there somewhere... receiving a ‘Merci Beaucoup’ from someone in the gay bar perhaps. But here I am, looking down on the world from my perch high atop the hill like a King on my throne, my peasants below. You are one of them, one who will never achieve the elevation required to become the Jewel in the Crown, the cream of the crop, the best PRIME has to offer. But I was born for this. So whether it is Montreal, Toronto, Winnipeg, Calgary or Vancouver you can bet I’ll be perched above you, one step higher with you at my feet. You’re used to being on your knees already, but when I win this tournament, you’ll be kissing my shoes instead of suckin’ my junk. Enjoy the view down there Skippy. Love, Nitz Donnelly P.S.
