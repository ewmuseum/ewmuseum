Date: Various After a long day of lectures and grading essay after essay on the grand failure of the Bay of Pigs invasion, the last thing Samantha Wade normally wanted to do was any more reading. Her nightly weekday routine was woefully simple, little more than a meeting with her couch, a squashy pillow and the latest fake news comedy stylings of Messrs. Jon Stewart and Stephen Colbert. If she was feeling adventurous, maybe she'd toss a bag of popcorn in the microwave. Normally, that was all Sam had the energy to do after going through yet another day in the meat grinder that was an adjunct professorship at Columbia. Turns out, teaching the best and the brightest had a nasty habit of turning her brain into smooth, creamy pudding without a speck of resistance. Tonight, however, was no normal night. For the better part of an hour, Sam's ever tiring hazel eyes had been locked on her laptop screen, her attention fixated on a series of e-mails she knew she'd find a way to save even when her hard drive went through its next factory mandated self-destruction. It was pushing 2 a.m., and she'd be dead tired for her 8 a.m. department meeting. So what? ***** From: [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] Subject: Morning Sam- Off for a couple of days, back home in Greensboro with no friends but my couch, season one of "Lost" on DVD and a steady dose of ice packs melting down the back of my neck to keep me company. As I'm sure you know, I could certainly do with better than that right about now. As for the ice, there's no need to worry (even though I know you won't anyway, Ms. Superior), I just tweaked my neck a bit at TV. Doc says its just a little soreness, says ice and Advil should do the trick. Personally, I can think of at leat one better solution, but seeing as you're stuck in the high strung world of academia and I've got a flight to catch in the morning, that's not really an option. Still, wish you were down here. I'm sure Carolina's a bit more relaxed than Manhattan this time of year, and even though you've only been back a few weeks, I can tell you need a break already. Whenever you need one, just give me a call. I'll be there to sweep you up for a romantic rendezvous to whatever exotic location PRIME is running in that particular week. Just think, an all-expenses paid getaway to lovely, scenic Detroit. Or Calgary. Or Oklahoma City. Or WACO~! Honestly, if I can just get you on a plane to come out to wherever I am, it'll be better than a week in the Caribbean. Well, maybe not, but certainly cheaper, at the very least. ... ... Okay, that's to give you some time to throw the tomatoes at the monitor after that horrible joke. But, as they say, gotta save money to make money, right? Hey ... hey, no more tomatoes! Miss you, and see you soon. -Troy ***** From: [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] Subject: I'm Leaving On a Jet Plane... Don't know when I'll be back again. Sam- Actually, as I'm writing this, I'm ON a jet plane, so the joke's not quite as perfect, but I'll leave semantics to you, Fancy Ivy League Professor Lady. Anyway, I'm headed out on yet another leg of the Troy Douglas PRIME World Tour, this time just outside of Detroit. And no, I don't think I'll have a minute of damn free time the entire trip. The book's on its way out, and both the PRIME PR folks and the boys at the publishing company have me so tied up with interviews and appearances that I'll be lucky if I remember to go through the curtain when my music starts playing. Of course, I've got a few thoughts to help me pass the time as I sit through a three-hour cattle call at Borders. The memories of that last Friday night, in particular, should be enough to keep a smile plastered on my face no matter how bad the old carpal tunnel acts up. Probably wouldn't be a good idea to be thinking about THAT when I'm on the air with the local ABC affiliate, though. Well, looks like we're about to hit a little weather, and the wireless signal might not be so great, so I'll send this along now. Still missing you. -Troy ***** From: [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] Subject: Hey again S- Don't think I'll ever get the hang of this iPhone deal. Trying to type with 1 hand while signing autographs with the other = VERY POOR IDEA. Was going to call you later tonight, but couldn't wait. Needed some sort of contact to break up monotony of 80 thousand autograph signings. Thanks for saving my sanity just by reading this. -T ***** From: [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] Subject: Bouncing off the walls Hey- You know, the one thing your parents never prepare you for is that there is absolutely nothing on Earth to do at 2:30 in the morning on a Tuesday. Honestly, if I see this damn AbCircle Pro infomercial one more time, I'm going to throw one of my wrestling boots through the TV, screw all the consequences. Truth is, I haven't been sleeping all that well the past couple of days. It's not my back, it's not the dreams again, it's ... Well, it's you. That might sound a little bit funny, but here's the thing. I slept alone for more than six years. Six years of tossing and turning, six years of nightmares and sleepless nights without anyone there next to me to turn to other than ghosts and memories. And then, you come along out of the blue, and finally there's someone next to me again at night, and it's the greatest feeling in the world. So now, to spend these nights alone on the road that I'd become so accustomed to is becoming an entirely new monster to cope with. So, it's your fault, Samantha Wade. You've got me addicted to you, and every second I'm away is like a junkie going through withdrawal. The good thing is, I know my fix is coming, and it's the farthest thing from bad that I can possibly image. Love you, Troy ***** Samantha Wade didn't even end up in her bed that night. She dozed off some time after 3:30, curled up on her couch, computer still glowing in her lap. She'd tired herself out from all the reading, but as she slept and dreamed, two words remained burned on the inside of her eyelids. Love you. She knew how tough it was for Troy to write or say those words. Just seeing them was reason enough for another peaceful night of sleep.
