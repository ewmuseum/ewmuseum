There Are No Second Chances In This Life

27 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/27/07 (occured in early June) The who's and the why's often bleed together in a cacophony. What is left is the senses, even through a whirlwind of haze, that seem sharp as they ever were. Everything has ascertained a certain scent. A flavor. A sharp sensation that wires the nerves. It alerts, always alerts. His mind was out of joint, but the feeling of pain washed over him, sending a universal shimmy for all the wrong reasons that resonated. A dull throbbing shot through his neck, mumbled moans and indecipherable jargon filling the air at a low key. The pavement smelled like ash. Smelled like piss. Smelled something foul. It was slick, but he did not know exactly from what. Had it rained just earlier? Reality was in discord, his mind trying to piece it back together on a table. By two old men who had not a care in the world about the pace they were traveling. Or so it felt that way, he thought. His internal monologue of the situation was not cogent. Singular cursings, filled with multiple periods to break them up, THAT is what was racing through his head in the twinkling twilight of his demise. "You remember what you did...you fuckin' shit bag?" A strong voice was cutting through the air, but his mind stayed dull. The sound was clear as day though. Big and black. Not a good thing. Not a good thing at all in this part of Los Angeles. "That nigga don't know shit. He fuckin' lyin' there like a scared little pussy ass muthafucka. Ain't even pickin' himself up. Ain't so tough now...is he?" He heard laughing of the deep belly kind. This was all a game to them...whoever THEM was. There was one fluorescent light bulb humming for the whole alley way. Brandon took his forehead off the pavement, his eyes blurry. The blood seeping from his head was slick, a migraine pumping into high gear as the dull pain in his neck began to creep idly by. "Dude is an ox man...you STILL took him down one shot. ONE SHOT!" "You gone soft nigga...or maybe all that fake shit you do make you seem like a tough guy. But yous a pussy. I know one when I fuckin' see one. You's a muthafuckin pussy." "Put it in him Shawntae! Before them fuckin' cops come." "No cops come down here dumb fuck. We in the jungle..." And they were right. Brandon Youngblood had only himself in this situation. An on screen tough guy, Brandon knew the odds were against you whenever you were being jumped in the universal sense. He rolled over to his side, looking skyward. He had been put on his back many times over the course of his life, but never was it as embarrassing as this. Nor was it ever as numbing. Staring up he saw the barrel of a gun, turned sideways, aiming right at his forehead. As if this was some sick Hollywood production, himself a mere bit player. Another moan escaped his lips. A kick hit him sharply in the ribs, followed by multiple people stomping down on him, peppering him with the heels of their expensive basketball sneakers. They parted for a moment. The sound of a gun being clicked, or a clicking noise from the back of a gun...he did not know. But it didn't matter because either way, it was a universal noise of being screwed. "Tyrell still can't walk no more you dumb guinea muthafucka!" That was coming from the man who stood over top of him with the gun slightly to the side. Of all the images Brandon had thought he would see last, the sight of a black man with a muscle shirt holding a gun fit for a cop killer was not on the list. Brandon's sharp tongue stayed firmly in place, not allowing him to get off a statement about this punk kid's choice of derogatory terms. That was survival instinct coming into play. "You think you gon' clean up the streets nigga? You think you a one man army? HUH? ANSWER ME YOU FUCKIN' SHIT BAG!" He didn't. He couldn't. His body was still in shock from the singular blow that hit him on the base of his neck. Had they tried it five years ago, he wouldn't have folded like a house of cards. On the flipside...he would be dead already. What was saving him was this taunting game. This sick sort of speech where the one with all the power dangled a carrot in front of the victim trying to get them to bite. And just when they would reach up to get it, to beg for their very existence, that is when it would happen Sopranos style, with the unexpected flash to black and dead silence. "Tyrell worked hard. Ain't his fault that cock suckin' slanted eyed tard couldn't pay his shit. And you gonna take it out on him? Who you foolin' nigga?" That was from outside his vision, from one of the army. "I thinks we gots a fuckin racist. Look at that fuckin' bald ass head of his." Again, from the distance. This one was different though. Sounded whinier. Like a prison bitch. "He's got a fuckin' jaw too." "Probably comes in handy when he's suckin' dick." Brandon wanted to know why the hell the guy over top of him could not seem to keep his gun straight as he spat down on him with that blast of his sexuality. "More places to put the cum so he can savor it." And the crowd laughed heartily while the principle stayed over him, unflinching. His gaze broke off for a moment, looking towards those Brandon could not catch a glimpse of with a nasty sneer. "Shut the fuck up faggot!" "Yeah dude...that was fuckin' gay." It was as if they were on their corner with Kentucky Fried Chicken, getting baked while breaking their own balls. Sirens. "SHIT!" "STOP!" They disappeared like street rats, back into hiding spots they learned about from growing up with the police constantly on their tail. And Brandon...he exhaled...resting his head against his own blood, nearly crying in joy that he had caught a lucky break on the river *** Was there a such thing as somber rage? His eyes burned the landscape, yet his face stayed still. His hands rested on the steering wheel, trembling in an anger fueled by fear. He was swallowing the chewing tobacco firmly in his cheek bit by bit, causing his throat to become raw. It was a brand new car he had treated himself to when he inked his new contract, thinking that he had finally turned a new positive chapter in his life. Free of all the dealings of the past. Free of the alcohol and drug pushing. But there were no second chances. Not in this life. A practical 08 Dodge Avenger, colored inferno red. Sporty, yet still even keel. How long had he stayed in the parking garage just staring at...at...he did not know what. IT had been mere hours ago. And now, it had been pieced together in perfect order. He should be dead. He should have a new lease on his life. Think positive... Think positive... Think positive... In his apartment was his beautiful love, completely unaware of what had transpired. He could nuzzle next to her and feel her warmth and thank whoever was out there that he still could. He could...he could... He punched, his fist colliding with the middle of the steering wheel. The blow was punctuated by the horn, but it did not stop him. He punched again, this time with a left, then with his right hitting the obsidian wheel. His breathing came in panicked yelps, his soul lit ablaze in emotion. He slammed his fists into the steering wheel over and over, trying to break it...punch through it...break his hands...ANYTHING to get him away from his own weakness. He could not control it anymore. Tears were streaming down his weather worn face, his voice catching in his throat in a most pathetic fashion. His knuckles were reddened. He did not care about his own well being anymore. Gripping at the door, he pushed it open and forced himself out. It took three spits to get the tobacco out from his cheek, but motes still lingered. His foot connected with the drivers side door, then again and again. He powered his fists down on it's hood, leaving dents aplenty. And he turned around from it, standing there in this listless place with the sun tickling the edges of the night sky. His face burned from his sobs. He promised, to silence that yawning fear, that restitution WOULD be paid. No matter the cost. No matter the cost.
