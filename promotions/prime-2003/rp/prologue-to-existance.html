Prologue to Existance

Date: Today The locker room of Kenjiro Ito was presumed by most to be a party site. Filled with booze and women, such a room would befit the image projected by the loud mouthed Kenjiro. It would likely come as a surprise to most, then, that in his locker room, Kenjiro Ito sat simply on a chair, dressed in his wrestling gear, partaking of a simple Kirin, listening not to the loud, driving rap music that he made his way to the ring with, but instead calming classical tunes. One looking at this scene might conclude that Ito wasn’t really the loudmouthed, woman insulting pig that he portrayed on television. And one…would have been absolutely wrong. You see, outward appearances often are deceiving. In the case of Kenjiro Ito, outward appearances are so overpowering as to be shocking. Kenjiro Ito the PRIME superstar, thus, in front of the camera, spews filth the likes of which few people can accept. People ask if this is the real man, or if it’s an exaggeration of his personality. The answer, not surprisingly, is both. Kenjiro Ito on TV is Kenjiro Ito turned way up. It is based on the beliefs of a man life has rejected, amplified for maximum benefit. What should concern you is not that Kenjiro Ito the wrestler exists. Any idiot can spout off hateful catchphrases to get cheap heat. Much rarer is the man who believes the stream of sewage coming out of his mouth. Much rarer is the man who believes in hate and misogyny. Much rarer is a man who is an utter waste of humanity. But, really, just looking at Kenjiro Ito and shaking your head doesn’t do anything. Kenjiro Ito exists for a reason. He wasn’t always like this. Once, he was a very kind boy, a loving boyfriend. He had a quiet existence in Japan, preparing for a career in baseball. But something happened, somewhere along the line. Something to bring him here. Something to make him die inside. Something to make him want to make everyone else to, too.
