The Offer

21 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 21, 2010 "You think he'll show?" The question had been echoing around his own mind for the past half hour or so, but Tyler Rayne had not bothered to voice the minor concern. Nor was he quick to provide an answer, though that in most part was due to the unsure nature of his response. It was a beautiful afternoon in San Diego, most of them were, and he was comfortable enough taking sips from his beer while waiting. Alex, however, seemed nervous. Not that she had much reason to be. Alexis Grant was a fine Federal Agent with a handful of accomodations and acknowledgements tacked to her file. She could be headstrong, and had had more than her fair share of disagreements with the Powers That Be, but in the end she knew when to cut her losses and file into line, and had proven herself an excellent field agent. The two of them had been in similar situations more than a few times. On most occasions, Alexis proved to be the cool and collected individual while Tyler sat impatiently waiting for the action. The reversal of roles amused him more than he dare admit. To her, anyway. "He'll be here." He spoke with more confidence than he possessed, but then again, he usually did. The two of them were seated across from each other, the small glass table between them filled with about half a dozen empty bottles. Tyler took another pull from the local brew, his gaze sweeping across the other customers seated out on the bistro deck, never allowing his eyes to linger too long on one face. He sat with his elbow resting on the back of the chair, beer never too far from his lips. He sat low, leaning into the back, comfortable and relaxed, right leg crossed atop the left. For all appearances, he was just another poor sap on a boring date with an uninteresting woman. Alexis played her part well enough, too, albeit her peformance was not enitrely on purpose. The nervous shifting of her dark eyes, the way she sat forward with elbows propped on the table, even the stern set of her jaw... it all spoke of an uncomfortable woman who knew she was losing control of her situation. This much seemed to be true, though the situation was less a date gone wrong and more an impatient wait for an international agent. There was an attractive brunette two tables from where they sat, tortoise shell glasses hanging low on her nose as she focused intently on the book in her hand. Jane Austin. How adorable. He shifted his position just enough that someone might notice, orienting himself more toward the brunette. Someone taking a look would assume, or so he hoped, that he was checking out the fine little literature fan. It was unlikely, he hoped again, that someone would realize this new position gave him a great peripheral view of the fellow reading the newspaper in the corner. The New York Times. Dated from the day before. "You gonna be OK, kid?" "Yeah. Sure. Just need to, uh... relax." "That's what the beer's for." "Not working." "So let's talk. What else is on your mind?" "Well, uh... fuck. You've got that big match coming up, right?" "Big show, dear. The match... not so much. There is a difference." "What's the difference?" "For PRIME, it's a big fuckin' deal. This is the show. What it's all about. Me facing the Ginger is... filler. At best. It's a meaningless match, in the big, grand scheme." "Then why are you doing it?" "Kid pissed me off. He's got no respect. Needs to learn some. Ass beatings are usually pretty good for that kind of thing. Plus, he left me busted a couple weeks ago..." Tyler's hand momentarily brushed across his forehead, fingers treading lightly over the seven stitches at the top of his brow. He noticed the man hiding behind the Times back there make a quick glance in their direction. His gaze was directed in Alex's direction. It was the second time he'd looked. "...and now I just wanna see him bleed. On the overall, though, it's not gonna be the thing people remember about this show. Small footnote in my career, but I'll likely forget about it in a couple months. The kid is just insignificant." "I don't get it. You go and do that shit all over the world, then come sit around in San Diego waiting to hear an offer from an Interpol agent. You'd think there would be enough excitement with one or the other. You'd have to be insane to want to do both." "I just wanna see what he has to say. Bastard's been waiting me out for months now, must think I'm worth something. I'm retired from all this cloak and dagger bullshit. Told you that." "Thought you retired from wrestling, too." "Yeah, well... fuck you." The man in the corner folded his newspaper, laid it down on the table, and left. Tyler smiled. "About fucking time." He turned himself back toward the table, noted the change in expression on Alex's face, and scooted his chair around the curve of the table to make room for their guest. The agent was a haggard son of a bitch. Nice suit, black and well fitted, which didn't seem to fit with the ruggedness of the man himself. His dark hair was slicked back, the streaks of white above both ears an amusing reminder of their first meeting. The man did not smile as he took his seat. He gave no indication of pleasure or even platitude in meeting with the two. The agent just continued to grimace, which seemed more like his natural demeanor than a purposeful scowl to put them off. "Mr. Rayne." "Rayne will do. I'm not my father." "Your father was a good man." "The fuck would you know about my father?" "More than you think. You've considered our offer?" "No. You haven't made an offer. You showed up a couple months ago with that whole Nick Fury schtick and then disappeared into the shadows. Figure whoever it is setting up these little meetings didn't mind waiting on me to come around... but none of that is an offer. What's the deal? In details." "You have skills that interest us. There are those within our upper echelons who have been debating about whether we should reach to you or not for some time now. Years, in fact. Your recent handling of the situation in Japan has persuaded the last of the holdouts that you could be a valuable asset, so--" "What situation in Japan?" "The truce you negotiated with The Jade Scorpion. Next to her, you are the most influential person in the Japanese underground." "I'm not a fuckin' criminal, asswipe." "I didn't say you were. You have a reputation that even the most dangerous woman in Japan respects. No one else could have made demands of that woman and walked out unscathed. Let alone managed to hold her to her word. She is a very ambitious woman. You know she's attempting to expand her empire to Bangkok?" "I've heard. Still waiting to hear about this offer, though." "We'd like you to come aboard as a special agent. You are quite a famous man, Rayne. Both with the criminal underworld and the general populace. You have a reputation, or three, that we could use to our advantage. We believe that there are certain situations you could defuse, or handle, better than our agents in the field. The work would be limited, and chosen quite carefully. Only assignments that fit your particular interests or talents would be passed along." "Yeah? And who gets to decide those?" The agent offered what must pass for his smile. It was an awkward and foreign expression. He pointed toward Alexis. "She does?" "I do?" "You do." "I..." "This isn't just an invitation for you, Rayne. We know that you do your best work with others, so we're willing to let you draft your own team. You choose the members. People you trust. Agent Grant will be transferred from the Bureau and act as liasion between Interpol and your cell. She will filter the requests we send along, and decide which would be best for your team to handle." "Quite the deal. Nice bait, too. Now if I refuse, it looks like I'm letting my friends down. Denying Alex a promotion and--" "Ty..." "No. This shit can go fuck himself. You wanna dick around with my life? Take a number and step to the back of the fucking line. Don't drag my friends into it and then try to dump their futures on my shoulders. Fucking cockbag." "They thought you might react this way. Which is why I was chosen. As I mentioned before, I met your father--" "And as I mentioned, that's bullshit. You speak about my father again, I'll bleed you here and now. Conversation is over. Be on your fuckin' merry." The agent sighed. He moved his left hand to pull gently at the edge of his suit jacket. The right hand moved inward, reaching for something hidden inside. The red dot of a laser sight appeared on the back of his hand. Just above his heart. The agent smiled again. Somehow it looked more natural here. Perhaps because of the looming danger. "The Japanese girl, I assume. The assassin? She's good." "The best." "We'd like you to take a look at something. If you're interested afterward, give me a call. If not... no hard feelings. The offer will stand for another month. If we do not hear from you by then, we will assume your answer is a no and proceed accordingly." The agent pulled his hand from out of his jacket. Clasped within is a manilla envelope, which he laid on the table. With a nod to Alexis first, then Tyler, the agent stood from his seat and left the bistro. Alex looked down at the envelope and then up at Tyler, who still stared down the direction in which the agent had left. "You gonna open it?" "No." "You're not even curious." "It's just leverage. Fucking burn it." Tyler Rayne slammed his beer down on the table and left. Alexis Grant sighed, realizing she just got stuck with a bit of a bar tab. She picked the manilla envelope from the table and sat, just holding it in her hand. Curious....
