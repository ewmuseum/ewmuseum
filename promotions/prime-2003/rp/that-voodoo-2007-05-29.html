That Voodoo

29 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/29/07 LOCALE: Jackson, Mississippi DAY: May 23, 2007 CUE: "Maxine" by Ghostface Killah ---- Easton eyed his ring finger, toying with the ring he had just slipped back onto it. He frowned, his thick beard seeming to turn all at once, a mirror of his general introspection. From across the round table, a heavy glass thudded down. "Your turn, E-meter," the scraggly-faced Johnny called out, a broad smile on his face. He belched, following it up with a sigh. "What're we drinking this time?" Alexander Shaw, who was sitting between the two PRIMEates, looked up from using the bottom of his mug to draw a circle in the table. The blonde-topped kid's eyes turned to the Dirtiest Dude in PRIME. "Heineken?" he offered. Garbage Bag Johnny's face contorted in rage. "Heineken!?" he snapped. "Fuck Heineken!" In unison, Johnny and Alex lifted their mugs into the air and cried: "Pabst Blue RIBBON!" They held the pose for a bit, letting Easton slowly raise his head. "So another round of Sam Adams?" the Canadian asked. "Boston Lager," Johnny replied, letting his mug sit on the table again. Easton nodded and slid out of his seat, pushing the ring fully onto his finger and making his way towards the bar counter. His steps were casual, not bothering to be hurried at the moment. Besides, he wanted to think, or rather, to clear his head. He wasn't the type to drink his troubles away and hanging out with those two wasn't likely to help him deal with his problems, so he wanted to take advantage of his time away from the table as effectively as possible. Jeanne had called him. The front of his brain was consumed with the call, scolding himself for not being around to talk to her personally, trying to grapple with the situation, what he should do. He had to see her, he knew that. He had to go back to New York and see her, but he couldn't right now. She needed to understand that. His lips were still pursed when he reached the bar. "Three Sam Adams," Easton said, leaning up against the bar in a cliché style, forearms down as the bartender made to get his drinks. He let his eyes slide closed, trying to gather himself. God, he hadn't heard Jeanne's voice in so long, and then just to hear her telling him that he had to come back, they had to talk, it was almost unnerving. He had wanted so badly to talk to her tenderly, and yet… "Hey." Easton lifted himself out of his reverie to see the seat next to him had become suddenly occupied. The woman that sat beside him gave him a tiny, suggestive smile, her thin frame leaned forward in an emulation of Easton's stance. Her dark hair was cut so that it hovered halfway down her neck. Her foot dangled idly and the fact that Easton noticed it made Easton want to break his own finger for being a traitor. "Hey," Easton replied, seeing no reason to be impolite. "I saw you tonight," she continued. "At ReVolution. Very nice performance." "I didn't perform anything," Easton said, shaking his head softly. Fuckin' ring rats. Easton watched the bartender, hoping that his drinks would be coming soon. The girl laughed softly, playing with her hair and leaning forward to catch Easton's eye. Easton didn't let himself look at her for more than a split-second. "I meant with Killean," she explained. "You didn't get scared or anything, didn't let him intimidate you. I like that." The three drinks were slid onto the wooden countertop and Easton took them, sliding the money to the bartender. "Thanks," Easton said, awkwardly gathering the mugs by the handles. "Always nice to meet a fan." "I'm Maxine," she said, her lips curling in a soft pout, but Easton just nodded as he turned and navigated back towards the table. He slid one of the frothing cups onto the table before easing himself back into the seat, passing the other mug towards his trainee and then sinking his beard into the foamy head of his own lager. They drank in relative silence for a while, Shaw and Johnny glancing out at the crowd and Easton's green-eyed stare pointed down at his mug. Finally, Alex let his drink down. "That girl," he said, nodding towards the dark-haired chick that Easton had just escaped. "She keeps looking at you, man." Easton rolled his eyes, setting his drink down. "Don't worry about her," he said. "She's just a rat." "Well, of course she's a rat," Shaw said. "Do I assume that means you're not gonna go for it?" Calmly, Easton lifted his fist up and rested his elbow on the table, locking eyes with his protégé. His golden wedding ring caught the dim light. "I'll leave a big gold dent in your forehead," Easton promised. Johnny laughed, taking a long swing of beer. Shaw just smirked. "Listen, I gotta use the john," Garbage Bag announced, sliding forward his half-full mug. He slid out of his seat and Shaw, glancing knowingly at Easton, followed suit. The two men parted ways after that, Johnny heading honestly towards the bathroom and Alex walking towards Maxine. Easton sighed, taking solace in his lager. Women had always been a curious subject for Easton, especially as he found himself largely unable to get one on his own merits. And yet, once he had found one, he found himself leaving her behind to tour the world, like any common wrestler's wife. He was hopeless. Absently, he brushed some of the froth out of his whiskers and glanced over to see Shaw excitedly chatting up the ring rat. She was pretty, but he wasn't about to let himself slip like that. Johnny sidled into his seat and eyed Easton curiously. "Something up, boss?" he asked, taking a long drink of his own beer. "Nah," Easton said, looking at the shallow depth of his remaining alcohol. "You ready to leave?" "Whenever," Johnny shrugged. Easton nodded and tilted his mug back, draining it into his throat. "Alex, we're heading out," Easton called, pushing himself up out of his seat. Johnny stood up, his head still tilted back as he chugged his beer down. After a second or two, Dirt McGirt the Third slammed his mug back down and burped loudly. "You okay to drive?" Johnny asked. Easton nodded and caught Shaw's eye, gesturing for him to follow. Followed by Johnny, Easton made his way out of the stuffy little bar and into the parking lot. The streetlights that lined the parking lot glowed like too-close stars, guiding Easton towards his car. He pulled out his keys and pressed the button to unlock the car doors, causing the headlights of the modest sedan to flood the street in front of it. Easton didn't break his stride, drinking in the sticky air as best he could. He was steady in his gait, lucky that he was experienced enough a drinker that a couple didn't stagger him. "Hey, Easton!" came Shaw's voice from behind. "Wait!" Easton glanced behind him and saw that Maxine was several paces behind his trainee, walking slowly to keep herself out of sight. "Get in the car," Easton told Johnny, pressing the keys into his hand. "Don't start it, I'll be there in a second." "Can do," Johnny said, nodding. "Or… cannot do." He blinked, continuing to make his way towards the car. Easton turned around, facing Shaw. "What is it?" Easton asked, dreading what was coming. "Maxine's coming with," Alex said and Easton squeezed his eyes shut, drawing in breath sharply. As soon as he spotted the girl, he knew she was coming. "No," Easton said firmly. "No, no, and no." The Canadian turned around on his heel, walking quickly towards the car, but Shaw hurried to keep pace. "Come on, Easton," Shaw pleaded. "It's just down to New Orleans. Being off TV entirely and not working any dates at freakin' all while training under you ain't exactly doing wonders for my libido, y'know." "Tough," Easton grunted. "You chose the lifestyle, you know what comes with it. This is PRIME, not Pussy-R-Us." "Listen, you know how it is," Shaw said, catching Easton by the arm. Easton turned around, emerald eyes blazing with indignation. He ripped his arm away from Shaw's girp. "I need this, man. Just let me do this, alright? I'll watch her, I'll keep her in line, all of it. Let her tag along." Easton glared at Shaw, his lips pursed. Goddammit, he was going to regret this. "Fine," he growled. "But I don't like this." "Thank you, man," Shaw breathed. "You keep her in line, hear me?" Easton jabbed Shaw in the chest forcefully. "Thank you, man." Easton turned around, marching towards the car. "You're running extra laps, man. I'm gonna work you like a dog." "Thank you, you son-of-a-bitch!" Shaw laughed before turning back to tell Maxine the good news. Easton hated being nice. LOCALE: New Orleans, Louisiana DAY: May 24, 2007 CUE: "Agony in Her Body" by Sage Francis ---- "Jeanne, I can't," Easton said. He sat on the edge of his bed in the hotel, his head inclined and phone pressed up against his ear. "I'm in New Orleans and… Jeanne, I… Responsibilities, Jeanne. I have responsibilities here." He did not want to have to talk like this to Jeanne. This wasn't a healthy road, not at all. "I want to, you know I do, but I can't. They need me down here. … but… Come on, Jeanne, you know that's not true." The feeling of emasculation was strong in Easton, but he couldn't help him. This was a woman he loved, a woman he had made vows to, and he was being incredibly unfair to her. He rubbed his forehead, feeling helpless as Jeanne ran him down over the phone. At least she wasn't as frail as before. He only hoped that it didn't mean she had found someone else in the interim. The door to his room closed and Easton's eyebrows furrowed curiously. Well, he supposed that Shaw was doing something in his own room. "Yeah, Jeanne, I…" He twisted around and his eyes fell on dark-haired Maxine, dressed in the clothes she was wearing the last night, though they sported a number of other wrinkles. His lips pursed. "Jeanne… Jeanne, I hate to do this, but I'm gonna call you back in a little bit. Jeanne… love you, Jeanne." He set the phone back down on the receiver, not exactly eager to have hung up on his wife, but needing to direct his full attention to what was before him now. "Hey, Easton," she said, taking a step towards him in a sultry, slinking manner. "I'm sorry, do I know you?" Easton snapped. "Mr. Hall. Please." He stood up, turning to face her and folding his arms over his bare chest. "Now explain to me exactly what you're doing in my room." "I just thought we could… chat a little bit," she answered, trying to be as cute as possible. Goddamn, sometimes it would just be easier to be a eunuch. Easton lifted an eyebrow at her, though, his face remaining stone. If only this bed was high enough to hide his crotch. "I've always been fascinated by what goes behind the scenes of wrestling." "Lots of nothing," Easton growled with finality. "You want more of an update? Ask Alex. Do not come in here uninvited again." "Can I have an invitation, then?" Easton lifted his jaw, his upper lip curling. "Get. The fuck. Out." Maxine pouted, a plastic hurt washing over her face, before she turned and did as he demanded. Shaw was obviously not a fan of coloring books. CUE: "Slow Down Gandhi" by Sage Francis ---- Easton watched Maxine leave Shaw's room from his own, closing the door behind her. He set down his magazine and rose from his seat, striding through the doorway towards his trainee. Savagely, he slapped the younger man upside the head. "Hey, what the fuck!?" Shaw cried out, reacting as if a bomb had gone off and clutching his head. He shot to his feet, eyeing Easton venomously. "What the fuck is right," Easton snarled. "What was that bitch doing in my room?" "You don't get to do that anymore!" Shaw protested. "What was she doing there?" "God, Easton, I don't know, I—" "I recall you saying you'd keep her in line." Easton snapped. "I wasn't aware the line extended around my personal space." "I was sleeping, Easton." Shaw rubbed his head, still fuming from the hit. "You can't hit me for shit that went on when I was sleeping." "You keep her with you," Easton demanded. "If I see her around me again, there'll be hell to pay." "Don't you have your own problems to think about?" Alex grumbled. Easton's eyebrows lifted dangerously, expecting Shaw to say something out of line and readying to beat the shitheel from end to end of this hotel room. "Like 129? You still gotta defend that belt, even if you hide the damn thing at the bottom of your bag all the time." Easton grunted, satisfied that Alex either didn't know or had enough sense not to say anything about his marital problems. "Let me worry about that shit," Easton snapped, turning his back on his trainee. "You worry about keeping Maxine on a leash." Easton stepped back into his room, slamming the door shut. It was true, he did have a big match on 129. Killean Sirrajin, Dusk, all three belts on the line, as well as the chance to supercharge Easton's career. Honestly, though, he was finding it hard to care about all that right this second. CUE: "Knuckleheadz" by Raekwon ---- "Take five!" Easton called, standing on the apron of the ring. He was dressed in his wrestling attire, a black t-shirt draped over his torso. In the middle of the ring, Alexander Shaw brought himself up off the canvas and ran his hand through his hair. He glanced up at the Canadian and Easton gestured for him to come over with a hand. Shaw shook his head tiredly, getting to his feet and walking towards Easton. Easton gave Alex's forehead a rough shove. "What the fuck is with you?" Easton demanded. "You're slipping." "It's nothing," Alex assured him. "Don't bullshit me, Shaw," Easton growled. "You losing sleep?" "It's Maxine," Alex admitted, laughing a bit. He leaned on the top rope confidentially, a braggart's smile on his face. "The woman won't leave me alone, she's all fuckin' over me." "Oh, and that's great, isn't it?" Easton snorted. "Fuck yes it is." "She's a rat, Shaw," Easton snapped. "You know she is." "I know." Shaw's eyebrows knotted together and he frowned at Easton. "So why are you gettin' all head-over-heels? You don't know this chick, Shaw, and chances are you're never gonna know her. Quit thinking with your dick, hear me?" Shaw grinned. "Hey, thinking with my dick got me this far, I'm willing to trust it." Easton rolled his eyes and shoved Shaw towards the ring. "Time in, everybody," he announced. "Hey, you guys, make sure to get Shaw in the brain!" He indicated his crotch and nodded to tell the others it was okay. Shaw groaned loudly. "Oh, fuck you!" Easton shook his head, leaning over the rope as Shaw grappled with his opponent once again. DAY: March 25, 2007 CUE: "Daylight" by Aesop Rock ---- Easton sat cross-legged on the roof of the hotel, his eyes closed as the dawn washed over him from the knees up. The feel of that slight warmth on his skin helped him get centered. This sort of early morning ritual had aided him so much in the past and in these trying times, he needed to get this sort of clarity on a daily basis. His breathing was regular, the oxygen filling his lungs and the feeling resonating through to his extremities. He felt a presence lower itself next to him and he opened his eyes, looking to his left. Alexander Shaw sat in a rough imitation of Easton's stance, dressed haphazardly in boxers and a wrinkled gray t-shirt. He sighed heavily. "So I'm cancelling my credit card." Easton nodded gravely. "What else did she get?" "Some membership cards," Shaw said. "Fifty bucks, maybe." "Sucks." Shaw snorted mirthlessly. "You're telling me." He sighed again, looking up and letting the sun's rays play over his face. "I just wish I could find her again." "Yeah?" "Is it bad that I want to play white trash husband with her?" Shaw asked. "I feel like throwing her down a flight of stairs would help let off a little steam." Easton shook his head, laughing a bit under his breath. "You gotta stick with what you know," Easton said, "and you're never gonna know a girl like her." "Yeah." Alex slowly lifted himself up and stretched. "I'm gonna go get some breakfast." "Go ahead." Alex turned on his heel and headed back towards the stairwell again. Easton had to talk to Jeanne, face to face. He had to head up to New York and it had to be soon. That wasn't going to happen for a little bit, though, and right in front of his face was a title match. He had to get past Killean and Dusk before doing anything else. This whole time, he was obsessed with what his wife might be thinking, but it wouldn't do any good. He knew he wasn't going to see her before Wednesday. He had to focus on what was coming first, now, take things as they came, just as he had always done. Stick with what ya know, right?
