Why He Fights (Cataclysm, Trojan Legion vs. the Dead Man's Hand)

17 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/17/08 Welcome to PRIME. Welcome to one of the sport’s great icons of progressive athletic entertainment, some would say its flagship promotion. I can say that “some would say” that because I pay no fewer than three people to repeat these claims to me on a daily basis (ha!). “Number One By Definition.” This time-honored slogan – mantra, really – is organic in its nature. This claim to greatness, often characterized as arrogance or bravado, must come from within the organization. It must be substantiated by the performance of the “moving parts” acting in concert together to produce something special. This is what we strive for each week, approximately forty-eight weeks out of every year. In order to reach the bar we set so high for ourselves, one concept remained illuminated at all times by our words and actions: loyalty. Our “product” – if it can be couched in such formal terms – cannot be replicated, only imitated; the reason for this is that every superstar who enters PRIME’s hallowed halls knows that this place comes first. There are no “side gigs” here. The concept is as foreign to our gladiators as a nine-to-five desk job. PRIME requires all that we have, all that we are. It accepts our offerings of blood and sweat like primal sacrifice, takes them from us and in turn amplifies our efforts, linking them and weaving the stories of our lives into a broad tapestry that testifies to the endurance of the human spirit and the most admirable qualities of our species: resolve, fortitude, fearlessness, triumph. PRIME offers you the possibility of immortality, having your name whispered in quiet tones of respect, sealing your legacy in the cement and mortar of the company’s foundation like construction workers in the columns of the Golden Gate Bridge. PRIME will give you everything… …if you will only do the same in return. Welcome to PRIME. - Hin See (deceased), PRIME Co-Owner, Forward to the PRIME “Wrestler’s Handbook” The first streaks of grey over the Bay Area horizon are too muted to penetrate the curtains of the hotel room, but Caesar Vega (known to wrestling fans worldwide as “Nova”) needs no solar alarm clock. Not this morning. Seated at the edge of the bed Nova slides his tights over his knees and up to his waist, the design a deep shade of blue partitioned erratically by silver lightning bolts that streak down both legs. As he bends to slip his right foot into a similarly-patterned black boot, the bed frame creaks loudly and he hears the sheets ruffle behind him. Thin, delicate arms snake around the Risen Star’s torso, and as Ariel pulls herself up, resting her head on Nova’s shoulder, her chin-length brown hair falls over her face. “Mmmm,” she murmurs, “is it time for you to go already?” Nova’s muscles tense up at the question; it might be couched in lackadaisical terms, but underneath the tones of sleepy resignation he knows what she’s really asking is “DO you have to go?” “Yeah,” he replies, his voice gruff and short, “it’s time to go.” He feels her pull away from him. Instantly they’re a thousand miles apart. His back is cool; the hotel A/C is blowing down his spine. “I don’t see why,” Ariel says in a low voice. “We’ve been over this,” Nova replies, rolling his eyes. Rays of sun are peeking at the edges of the curtains, and there’s a low sound picking up steam outside in the hallway. “I thought I explained…I thought you understood…” “I just still don’t get it,” she says, crossing her arms and staring intensely at him. “Some fight…with total strangers…wholly separate from your regular obligations? I just…do you love wrestling so much you’ll take ANY opportunity to be away from me?” Oh Jesus. “Ariel, that’s got nothin’ to fuckin’ do with it,” Nova snaps, feeling his patience ebb away, “and you know that. I told you. PRIME has been…it’s…” “Home?” Ariel asks softly. “…for four years, Ariel,” he continues, softer, “and now all this with another organization moving in, tossing the gauntlet down…I’m doing this for PRIME because I love PRIME. And I’m doing it for Chuck because I’m a member of the fucking Executive Cabinet, let’s not forget, and he…he needed the best people he had to go to bat against the Dead Man’s Hand. And I mean, hey…” Nova flashes the schoolboy grin, holding up his hands. “…I’m the fuckin’ best.” Ariel shakes her head, running a hand over her scalp. “Don’t do that. I’m not okay. This is not okay. This…,” her voice is heavy and tears glisten on the edge of her eyes, “…this is bullshit.” “Ariel…,” Nova starts, “Ariel, what…why are you crying?” “This isn’t about your stupid wrestling company or your stupid boss,” Ariel sobs, “this is about her.” Nova throws up his hands. “Oh my fucking God, here it comes…” “You’re doing this for her, aren’t you?!” Ariel spits with contempt, “You’d do ANYTHING if Lindsay Troy asked you to, but you won’t even look me in the face!” “Because you’re lookin’ at me like you hate me!” Nova shouts back. Sunlight illuminates the corners of the curtains, and the sounds out in the hallway have risen in volume to audible cheers. “How does SHE look at you?” Ariel says, almost pleading, “Tell me, Chris. Tell me what you see in her eyes that makes you nod your head and smile when she asks you to walk into Hell, to leave your wife and fight people you don’t know over issues you don’t understand! What does she have that I don’t, that I can’t get you to nail down a dinner reservation and you’re…you’re at her fucking beck and call!” “I’m not doing this, Ariel,” Nova replies quietly, standing up and pulling a black sleeveless shirt over his face. “This is stupid. I have to go.” As he moves towards the door the sounds of cheers outside amplify ten-fold. “NO-VA!! NO-VA!! NO-VA!! NO-VA!!” “I have to go, Ariel,” he repeats. “Chris,” she whispers, shaking her head, “don’t.” “NO-VA!! NO-VA!! NO-VA!! NO-VA!!” He takes a step away from the door, towards the bed. “Ariel…I love you. Not Troy. You are the most important thing in my life, more important than any wrestling match…” She slinks off the bed, clutching his arms and pulling herself close to him. She looks up into his face, her eyes searching his. “Chris, if that’s true…” Suddenly her eyes turn a deep blood-shot red and a grey pallor envelops her complexion as Nova’s jaw drops in horror, “…THEN WHY HAS IT BEEN SIX MONTHS SINCE YOU VISITED MY GRAVE?!!” Nova backpedals under her weight as her skin shrivels. Her hair falls away from her scalp like excess pine needles under a Christmas tree, and her joints sag as Nova recoils, crying out as he falls backward. He starts up out of bed. His heart is pounding. He swings his legs around the side of the bed and reaches over to open the door to the room’s mini-fridge. Removing a bottle of Bombay Sapphire gin he walks over to the table in the corner of the room and sits down. He flips open a metal cigarette case and removes a joint. He pours the gin. Down the hatch. He lights the joint. Almost seventeen months have passed since Nova’s ex-wife Ariel locked herself in the bathroom of the house they had shared in Detroit and consumed enough Xanax to fog a bull elephant’s memory…certainly enough to wipe the pain of their daughter’s death away and stop her heart in the process. Nova rarely dreams about her with any degree of lucidity. It’s probably been three months. As the Risen Star pours another dollop of gin into his glass, he prays it will be three more before it happens again. “Oh, fuck it,” he grunts, sliding the glass away and taking a pull from the bottle. … “WAKE UP, SHITHEAD.” The booming voice of Sonny Gabriel Silver shakes Nova from unconsciousness. He peers up at the bearded menace, who clutches the OLD SKOOL MIC~! in his bitch-slappin’ hand. “Ohhh, fuck!” Nova groans, clutching his head, “How do you get that microphone to just appear like that?! I know it’s a running joke, but for real, it’s implausible!” “Where I feel the need, there will be the MIC~! feed,” Silver answers cryptically, “now get the fuck up, Bong Boy. This hotel room stinks of gin and ass. Bad ass, like you haven’t showered in a while, not good ass, like you’ve been pounding snatch all day.” Nova watches as Silver releases the OLD SKOOL MIC~! It vanishes into the ceiling. “How the hell did you even get in here?” Nova asks. “Well the fat whore at the reception desk didn’t want to give me your room key, but then she talked to Mr. Franklin and worked it all out,” Silver replies. “You paid her to let you into my room?” Nova cocks an eyebrow. “Oh HELL no,” Silver snorts, “‘Mr. Franklin’ is code for my COCK, son, and after I mushroom-stamped the side of that bitch’s face she was TOSSIN’ room keys at me!” He steps back, cutting the air with jabs and a snap kick. “Now get your sorry ass up! Who’s ready to whoop that SCCW ass, huh? Is my dawg ready to whoop that SCCW ass like they stole somethin’ out the supply closet? Huh? Is my dawg ready to…” “Your ‘dawg’ feels like garbage,” Nova moans, shuffling a cigarette out of his pack, “all I feel like doing is crawling downstairs to the Liquor Depot and restocking on my sleep aids…” Silver eyes the nearly emptied bottle of gin. “You know they make Tylenol PM and shit to help with that?” The sorrowful expression visited on Silver by his longtime friend gives the Sports Entertainment Liaison pause. He pulls out a chair and plunks down, waving cigarette smoke away from his face. “Jeez, you know these are all ‘no smoking’ rooms, right? Okay. Talk to your boy. I’m looking into those big blues and I don’t detect that sticky-sweet stoner glaze affability. What’s going on, kid?” “Nothing I can’t handle,” Nova says with a sigh, “just…you remember the Fuck You days, how eventually I just felt like I was fighting to be fighting, with no idea why? Just…I dunno…just anger for the sake of anger?” “Yeah, I know a thing or two about that,” Silver says before looking down at his phone, “Hey, just a sec…Hello? FUCKIN’ A RIGHT THIS IS MR. SILVER, WHO THE FUCK IS THIS? OH YEAH? YEAH, GODDAMN RIGHT I ORDERED FOUR OMAHA STEAKS. THEY’RE READY? WELL YOU BEST HANG ONTO THEM SHITS, COCKSMOOCH, ‘CUZ IF I DRIVE ALL THE WAY…HELLO? Hello? Fuck him. You were saying…ummm…anger for the sake of anger, right?” “Yeah,” Nova nods, “it’s just…I dunno, I mean of course I’m down for the match, I’m down to rep PRIME and all that…I just can’t fight that same feeling of disorientation, wondering if I’m really any better than those fucks in the Dead Man’s Hand, if I’m just a mercenary for someone else’s fight.” “Well, A) maybe you aren’t better than those fucks in the Dead Man’s Hand,” Silver says with a shrug, “so you need to quit worrying about that shit ‘cuz it’s irrelevant. The sides are lined up, everyone’s got a reason, none of it matters, it just comes down to the fight. You want me to make you feel better about it? I can wax philosophical about pooooor Angelica Brooks and defending our home and all that shit. Or we can skip that and mutually acknowledge that we both have too much sac to sit around crying about motives. And B) you know why you’re doing this.” Nova’s chest tightens as Ariel’s words claw at the back of his mind: “This is about her.” “This is about her,” Silver says plainly, and Nova jumps. “Come on, man, what’s the fucking deal? Everyone knows you’ve been ‘in like’ with Troy for years. Hell, there’s a kind of compulsion in the way she can fix those eyes on you. Don’t forget, my career WAS Lindsay Troy for the better part of a fucking YEAR – much to my win/loss record’s chagrin – so I know what she can do to a person. You’re here, in part, because SHE wanted you here, and you love that. So don’t be such a prima donna about it.” Before Nova can open his mouth, Silver continues. “So in PART, it’s about that. But it’s about something else too, Nov. Pour me up one of those.” Nova dutifully divides the gin between two glasses and hands one to Sonny. “Guys like us…all we give ourselves is the business. Every day, the business. Every ounce of strength, to wrestling. So it comes as no surprise at the end of the day when ‘the business’ is all we have. You are here, my friend, in part because of who asked you to come…but also because THIS life…” Sonny pauses, scratches his beard, and meets Nova’s gaze with his best attempt at sympathy. “…it’s what ya got left, Nov. And that’s okay.” Silver clinks his glass off of Nova’s and knocks back the gin. He pushes back his chair and stands up, giving himself a shake. “Now come on. Let’s go get some shitty Mexican food.” As Sonny walks over to the windows and rips open the blinds, bathing the hotel room in the morning sun, Nova nods, going over his former stablemate’s words in his head. Sonny’s right. It’s not good, it’s not bad, it just is. Why does he fight? Because it’s what he has left. Because it was a Wednesday. And he wasn’t fucking busy.
