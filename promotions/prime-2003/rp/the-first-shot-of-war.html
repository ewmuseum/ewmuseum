Date: Props to John for helping with the ringtone Mindi Abair’s soothing saxophone resonated throughout Devin Shakur’s Lamborghini Diablo while he sped down a winding Raleigh road. Commie Emo always had a secret affinity for jazz music ever since his ears were first exposed to Alex Bugnon’s ‘107 Degrees in the Shade’ during 1992. New age music was alright, but on most evenings a glass of wine and a melody would soothe the roaring beast. Tonight was definitely one of those nights. Michael DeAngelis had just put Shakur out on his ass after failing to murder Tony Gamble in a timely fashion. No amount of explanation could dissuade the Don’s rage for Shakur’s ineptitude. Shakur was recommended by a mutual friend as "one of the best in the business" and instead was acting like an imbecile on national television. That kind of behavior constituted expulsion in DeAngelis’s world. Now Shakur was one of the hunted. The language used inside of DeAngelis’s office was symbolic of only one thing, war. With both sides backed by a volatile and intrepid army, only a couple of sands would need to drop from the hourglass before the casualties started. That was one field in which Shakur did not want to start out on the defensive. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Switching to the refreshing sound of "Will You Stick with Me" by Mike Phillips, Shakur’s mind went adrift, pondering what the love of his life Sun Tzu was indulging in. He hadn’t heard from her since leaving to meet DeAngelis in New York about a week ago. The last time they spoke she went on a twenty minute rant about how giraffe coitus lacked viscosity and left her with a sour taste in her mouth after watching it for seventy-five minutes on the Discovery Channel. She always spoke her mind, regardless of circumstance, and that was one thing that made Shakur's heart skip a beat every time he saw her. Now his mind was fixated upon engaging in some coitus of his own with the Chinese vixen. Oh mercy Mao, how beautiful she looks when she’s slithering down my body... Jolting him away from blissful thoughts of sweet Asian ass was his annoying cellular phone. "Oompa loompa dooba dee doo I picked a ringtone special for you. Who is that guy that thinks he's such a big deal... Sitting on two phone books to see over a steering wheel. He needs a shot of height viagra Before he gets lost in Troy's vagi...nah" Shakur flipped open the phone, eliminating the majestic sound of Oompa Loompas. Gamble: Greetings! Shakur: You sound guttural. Gamble: I just grabbed my package. Shakur: Exciting Gamble: Listen, I got a question…You haven’t seen the Cloverfield movie yet, right? Shakur: No, why? Gamble: Want to see the monster now? Shakur: Yeah, sure, I heard it was repulsive. Gamble: Oh, believe me, it was. Shakur: Disgusting Gamble: Check Shakur: Inhumane Gamble: Right Shakur: Utterly sickening. Gamble: …You want to see it or not? Shakur: Send away my man. Shakur waited the necessary moment to view the piece of cinematic history. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~` Piano keys from the car’s sound system quelled the Doberman pinscher’s barking in the backyard when Shakur pulled to a stop outside of his mansion. A living room light was visible from outside, making Shakur smirk a little on the inside. He quickly gathered all of his belongings and rushed inside expecting to see a jubilant and horny Sun Tzu. Instead, an explosion invaded the ground behind him and Commie Emo hit the deck faster than anytime prior in his life. When Shakur looked behind him, he saw his Lamborghini up in flames. The car that he was just in was now all over his driveway. Yeah, I'm still adding on this one.
