Road To Redemption

20 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/20/2005 Overkill. Nick: JACKSON just hit FINAL FLASH on TYLER LOPEZ! Richard: Told you he was going to come out the victor. Quick fade. “Never has Jackson Darilek been more lucky in his entire life.” Up. Welcome to the NEW Scientific Apparatus travel bus. Covered on the outside with Tyler Lopez logo’s, we pan to the interior of the new creation. The bus is filled with luxury rooms, a kitchen, a small living room, everything. It is one of the nicest specialty buses ever made, and is the current home of Tyler Lopez. Enter “The Scientific Apparatus” Tyler Lopez. Lopez is entering the living room where he plops down on one of the leather sofa’s. He is wearing shades. Tyler Lopez looks out the window at the passing mountains as they continue towards Buffalo, New York, site of the upcoming Revolution. Lopez asks one of the stewardesses on the bus for a glass of water, and as she gets it, he just sits there, controlled, unwavering. “Welcome Jackson.” The stewardess returns with his glass of water that Lopez quickly drinks. Even with the air-conditioning of the bus cooling him off, the 90% humidity outside isn’t helping anyone. “Before you even begin, let me be the first to welcome you.” Lopez snickers. “Nothing has been said. All I heard is the same thing that everyone else did. I was promised I'd have some fun.” Lopez just shakes his head. “PRIME was the greatest assembly of champions in the history of the Wrestling, and Jackson Darilek destroyed it. I stood in the ring at Overkill with destiny on my shoulders, but I always knew it was never about me. It was never about Tyler.” Lopez speaks slowly, careful of every word that comes out of his mouth. “And just like it always has been, I will finish it, once and for all. And when that happens, we will see what Jackson Darilek is made of. The generals are gone. The infantry is gone. And me Jackson?” Lopez smiles and gives the camera the thumbs up sign. “Good luck friend. You’re going to need it.” Lopez winks. Cut. Taped footage. Fade up to the Cow Palace in San Francisco, Ca., home of Overkill. It’s 1:00 a.m. in the morning, just an hour or two after the end of the PPV. We’re inside the arena in what seems to be a locker room. Lopez steps down off a bench and he just smiles. “Does I look bad? Because for as bad as I look, this thing I have right here makes it not even noticeable. Nice win out there tonight, Jackson you sure proved to be one tough son of a bitch.” Lopez grabs a bag and then walks down the back hallway of the Cow Palace towards another locker room without a nameplate on it. Lopez enters. We enter a room to find a make-shift office that was never seen during the Overkill broadcast. Tyler walks towards the desk and we notice a nameplate that sits on the desk. It reads... 'Taun Pham.' “I found this office after my match, hence I didn’t bother to make an announcement tonight. I figured I’d just wait until the evening was over.” Lopez just stares at the camera. ‘Now that I'm done, I thought I’d walk back to this office so I could discuss the future of the PRIME.” Lopez looks around the room. “I don’t know where I left it, but get ready for change and I'll go work out the issues. I’ve heard some say some pretty fucked up things about me, but now that I am forced...nobody could stop me now, I shall have the greatest region ever assembled.” Lopez turns towards the door. “No Jackson, I am not the Man. If I was the Man, you would have been gone from the PRIME a long time ago. So quit bothering me give me what is due, my rematch, and if you refuse... I’ll break your fucking arm.” And with that, Lopez turns and walks out the room. Cut. We return to the bus that Lopez was located earlier. We cut him off midstream to show the Lopez segment from earlier, but we now cut back to the same spot we were at earlier in the conversation. “And you know what Justin, you are going to have to be an example. Speaking about how degraded PRIME has become recently, let me give you a perfect example of why this happened.” Lopez turns to the camera. “It’s funny how you to choose to ignore anything and everything about your upcoming match with me, but I’d be pretty cautious if I were you. You see, you’re the type of filth that was used as nothing more than part of the numbers game. It was little worthless folk like yourself that were used just so that PRIME played the numbers game. As long as the core group of characters were still in play, pieces of tin could be used as replacements.” Lopez pauses. “Maybe PRIME should have gone with the tin after all. I mean that’s a good opener too.” He chuckles to himself. “You see Justin, you have a huge opportunity coming up, and yet you’re too busy. Lopez shakes his head. “This was the focus that was missing. This was the attitude that left. Maybe I was too busy being so damn good that I didn’t see it at the time, but you Justin Williamson are a waste of space.” Lopez clasps his hands and rest them on the table in front of him. “Whatever the case may be Justin, I’m the one you should be worried about. You’ve got a big problem on your hands. I just hope you can carry your own weight, because you don’t understand what you’re getting yourself into. You think I’m going to let you take this match? I can’t lose to you Justin, because after you comes Jackson Darilek. After you comes something that has been over a year in the making. And trust me, whether Jackson knows it or not, he’s going to give me exactly what I want.” Slight pause. “This is my road to redemption Justin, and you are a bug that just hit my windshield.” End.
