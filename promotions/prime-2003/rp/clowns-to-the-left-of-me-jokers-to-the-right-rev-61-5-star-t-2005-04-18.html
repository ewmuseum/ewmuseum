Clowns to the Left of Me, Jokers to the Right...(ReV 61, 5-Star Title Match)

18 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/18/05 “RAARH!!” Nova screamed as he planted his boot in Hoyt’s midsection, causing God’s Champion to drop his Bible, the gold-leafed pages fluttering before the tome landed on the mat with a loud thunk. A potent combination of rage and indignation clouded Nova’s brain. The fucking Bible. How dare this guy try to clock him with a fucking Bible. It wasn’t the apparent irreverence for the famous volume that got Nova’s blood boiling…hell, Nova had rolled his share of doobies with famous verses just for shits and giggles. It was…well…well, Nova didn’t know what it was. But it pissed him off something fierce. And he was gonna make this pompous asshole pay. Hoyt buckled over from the blow, clutching his stomach as his shaggy brown hair hung, soaked with sweat, down over his face. Nova picked up the Bible, staring at it. It disgusted him…it had no place in this match. It could have fucked everything up. Imagine that…something as simple as a book with the potential to ruin everything. Not this time. Nova turned to pitch it out of the ring…and then things got confusing. The fucking zebra. Ref getting in Nova’s face trying to indict him or something…what the hell? Where do these guys get trained, again? Pizza College? Fuck this. The ref wasn’t listening. Pushing him out of the way, Nova went for the ropes to seal this shit and end this circus show. All he wanted was a cab back to his hotel room so he could shower, watch people with shittier lives than his yell at each other on Maury, and pass out. DSD! Taste the rainbow, you preachy fuck! Three seconds later, and Nova heard the bell. But something was wrong. He was tired, and disoriented, and unaware of what had happened, but he had a bad feeling. He was on his back, not where you usually wound up when you won...and Hoyt was climbing to his feet. The DSD wasn’t the most deadly move in wrestling…in fact, Nova had long contemplated hanging up his antique finisher in favor of something a little more flashy, not to mention unique, for fuck’s sake. But no one ever got up that quick from it. And why was he still on the mat? Why did his back hurt so much? Why was that idiot ref handing Hoyt the 5-Star Title? … Oh, God. Oh, fuck. God’s Champion was now the 5-Star Champion. For the second time since his arrival in PRIME, Nova had lost. That was CULTURE SHOCK. That was the night everything changed. “You ask me if I want you to suck it,But I say nah, baby, I’ll just stick it in your LOVE BUCKET~!LOVE BUCKET! LOVE BUCKET! LOVE BUCKET! LOVE BUCKET!Oh yeah! (LOVE BUCKET!) Oh yeah! (LOVE BUCKET!) Oh yeah! (LOVE BUCKET!)” “Alright, that was Spermicide with their new hit single, ‘Love Bucket,’ off their comeback album, Catheter of Madness. I’m ‘Jammin’’ Jerry McCandless here at 104.5 WPTC ‘The Flame,’ and we’ve got a special treat for our loyal listeners out there today. Joining me in the studio today is one of PRIME’s own superstars, Nova, who will be co-headlining the show on Wednesday. Nova, how are you enjoying Orlando?” “Orlando? Um…it’s…it’s cool, I guess.” “You don’t sound so sure about that.” “Well, I guess I hadn’t really thought about it.” “What are you thinking about?” “I guess I’m thinking more about the match. I mean, I didn’t come to Orlando to play on the beach or go to Universal Studios.” “You mentioned the match.” “I very much did, ‘Jammin’’ Jerry.” “Tell our listeners about it.” “Well, it’s the Main Event on PRIME’s weekly show, ReVolution. I’ll be facing Hoyt Williams and Dani Furher for the 5-Star Title, which, along with the Intense Title, is a notch below the…” “Uh-huh. So are you gonna, like, body-slam your opponents?” “Body-slam? Um…you’ll have to be more specific. I employ a few differe-” “You gonna rip their heads off? You gonna break ‘em in half?” “Oh, now, let’s be serious, ‘Jammin’’ Jerry…I’m not gonna do any of tho-” “So do you ever feel weird, like, rolling around in nothing but a pair of tights with other muscled-up, oily dudes for ten or fifteen minutes at a time?” “I thought this was an interview about the show. And no, it’s not a big deal, really. Only assholes like you seem to be hung up about it.” “Whatever you say. So, Nova, you’ve got the big match tonight, right? For the…the, uh…” “The 5-Star Title.” “The 5-Star Title, that’s right! So what is that, exactly? I mean, like, in terms of the…the hierarchy of PRIME?” “Yeah, well, I was gonna say that before, but you interrupt-” “You think I could be a wrestler, Nova? ‘Jammin’ Jerry McCandless!” “It would take years of training, and at your…” “The Led Zeppelin Leg Drop! Ha, ha!” “Yes, hehe…I dunno. About the show, though…there’s some history between Hoyt and mys-” “The Deep Purple Piledriver! The Creedence Clearwater Clothesline! Ah, ha, ha, ha, ha…” “…right. The names can get pretty clever, no doubt. There’s actually a lot at stake for me, personally, in this mat-” “I saw the most ridiculous thing the other day on TV…I forgot the damn name, though. Bob, what was the name of that ridiculous show we saw?” “XCWF Savagery or something like that, Jimmy...” “Yeah, something like that. Well, it was just the damndest thing, I swear…there…hehe…there was a g(beep)damn 7’3” giant dressed like a caveman and he was fighting a Martian! Can you believe that?” “Yeah, well, some promotions don’t take it as seriously as others. You’ll be hard-pressed to find…” “Have you ever used steroids, Nova? I mean, I hope it’s okay to ask that…it’s just that in the middle of this huge baseball thing, it seems like a relevant…” “Can we get on-topic here? I’ve been trying to talk about the show for five minutes, but you won’t shut the f(beep) up about wrestling clichés long enough for me to get a word in edge-wise.” “Hey pal, I think I know what my own listeners like, alright? I don’t tell you how to sweat all over roided-out beefcakes, so don’t tell me how to run a radio program.” “Yeah? Well, I think you’re pretty rude, ‘Jammin’’ Jimmy...I’m getting out of here.” “Don’t let the door hit you in the ass on the way out, you goofball! Ladies and gentlemen, Nova is leaving the building! *Claps* Go give ‘em a DEATH BOMB for me, Nova! Ha, ha, ha! What a loser…” Stepping outside of the radio, Nova exhaled deeply, removing a cigarette from the pack in his pocket and lighting it. He slid on his sunglasses and took a seat on the steps. He hadn’t hit that obnoxious jerk, and he was proud of it. He had been trying to work on that. Hard to shatter that wrestler stereotype if you’re cold-cocking every asshole who insults or patronizes you, eh? But he had wanted to…so bad he could feel his temples throbbing from clenching his fists too tightly. Behind eyes of aqua played out a scene of savage violence. Nova leapt to his feet, heaving off his headset and running for the door. He locked it and jammed a chair underneath for added support. He then rounded on the strangely speechless ‘Jammin’’ Jimmy, who could only let out a tiny, terror-stricken yelp as Nova heaved him out of his chair, through the air, and onto his own desk back-first through a fax machine. “Still wanna be a wrestler, Jimmy?!” Nova screamed down at the hapless disc jockey before punching him in the nose, “oh, oh, excuse me…‘Jammin’’ Jimmy! Where’s that Led Zeppelin Leg Drop now, you dickhead!” Blood soaked through the back of Jimmy’s shirt as Nova grabbed him by the hair and lifted him up. He held him up by the face in front of the large glass window to the sound studio. “Whaddaya say, Jimbo? Wanna be a wrestler?” “Uhhg…pl-please…” “That’s a yes!!” Nova reared back before driving Jimmy’s head through the window. The glass collapsed around them, and Nova drooped Jimmy’s body across the sill before ripping the phone cord out of the machine on the desk and wrapping it around his fist until the phone rested security in his palm. “Okay, Jim! If you say so! Welcome to the world of wrestling!” Nova brought the phone down across the back of Jimmy’s head. “Welcome, Jimmy! Welcome, you snobby fuck! WELCOME!” Several more times, Nova swung the phone down across the back of Jimmy’s head until blood misted up into his face. Jimmy’s right leg twitched, shifting the shards of glass around it. Nova dropped the phone at last. Shaking the scene from his head, Nova flicked his cigarette and climbed into a cab headed in the direction of his hotel. Best Value Inn, Orlando, FL, Monday, April 18, 2005…11:46 PM. Nova throws back the covers and sits up, pulling his sweaty hair into a pony tail and grabbing a cigarette and a Bic lighter off the nightstand. He can’t sleep…he isn’t tired, and, as per usual, his thoughts dwell on the upcoming show…his thirteenth ReVolution. It always burned me, seeing Hoyt with the 5-Star Title. It was like a tangible reminder of my biggest failure to date…and the cockiness. God, the arrogance with which he strutted around, that joke of a God’s Title or whatever hanging over one shoulder, and my belt over the other. I had to watch, for over a month, through another PPV, as Hoyt paraded the 5-Star Title around like he owned the goddamn world because he beat me once…it seemed to shine brighter on his shoulder than it ever did on mine, probably because I didn’t have it any more and I remembered how badly I had wanted it in the first place. I could never shake it. That belt was mine. It belonged to me. It fit me better than him. The 5-Star Title was made for me. I won it by competing in a deadly maze of ladders and tables against PRIME’s upper echelon of talent at the time…Joshua Taro Freedom, Ozric Mortimer, and…*cough*Tyler Lopez.*cough*… …a high-intensity match-up for what was supposed to be a high-intensity title. Oh, what’s that you say, PRIME management? “Nova, you’re booked in another melee ladder match, and the competition will be stiff.” No problem! I handle that shit! “Nova, we booked you to fight in the crowd.” Good! I make it look sexy! “Nova, we need you to take it to the top rope.” One step ahead of you, Gerrard O’Riley, but thanks for asking! Ready the flash-bulbs! In the Dual Halo, I was one of the final six left in Halo One, joined by Aimz, Karina Wolfenden, Brandon Youngblood, Angelo Deville, and Emilio Rage. You could do worse for company than that. I’ve consistently stepped up my game for high-risk situations, unlike that bumbling oaf Williams, who wouldn’t know a moonsault if it hocked a loogie in his eye. I mean, at least I competed in a contendership match to see who I would defend against at Culture Shock. It wasn’t a defense, but it was something. By now, the 5-Star Title probably has an inch or better of dust across the top. We might as well make Angelo Deville’s luggage the 5-Star Champion for all good it’s doing around the Son of Duke’s waist. And would you take a look at this match? Main Event, ReVolution 61…Hoyt Williams vs. Nova vs. Dani Furher…for the 5-Star Title. Could the Stealer’s Wheel lyric be any more applicable than it is right now? The Imperium vs. Me vs. the Dark Age. I feel like the pepperoni lodged in the middle of an ideologically conflicted cold cut sub. The Imperium. What the hell are you guys celebrating? You’re the only ones who buy into Hoyt’s bullshit posturing. That doesn’t make you special, it makes you dumbasses. Los Diablos, you complain about the treatment and type-casting of your people, yet you have no problem sucking the dick of the biggest bigot in PRIME. I doubt you even have trouble sleeping at night knowing you’ve sold yourselves out to the very attitudes that you once despised so much and stood to reject. Pathetic. Peter Vetra, you’re the most contradictory and hypocritical of them all, bitching and moaning about the respect you haven’t been given, but what are you doing now? You’re hiding behind a safety bubble of your friends’ talent and masquerading it as your own. You’re still the same Peter Vetra, with or without your new buddies, still the guy in a neck-and-neck race with First Blood to see who can botch more title shots, and you made a big mistake last week way-laying me in the ring from behind like a coward. You’re gonna have to pay up on that one, kiddo…but good. You wanted to make this into something? Fine. You can dig a hole, and I’ll be more than happy to bury you in it. What else can I say about you, Hoyt? I’m ecstatic. I get the opportunity to take you down that I fucked up at Culture Shock, and I get to take back my belt from you before someone else gets the pleasure. Life is good. The Dark Age. … Wait, there’s nothing to say here. The Dark Age is a joke. I mean, come on. Boda was barely through his victory speech about selling his soul to the Devil for the Universal Title when he lost the damn thing. What has he done since then? He’s dyed his damn hair black. Look out folks, he’s making waves. Then we have Vampir Nosferatu, the man staking claim to two consecutive brutal victories over Tony Rolo, responsible for the ruin of Ignatius Lisieux, the apparent deaths of Paul Cain and Shadow, the destruction of Emilio Rage… …and last week, he almost pissed himself in fear as Hessian appeared at the top of the ramp. I’d say that doesn’t bode well for his followers. And then we have Mr. Furher, the mysterious one in the group. Now, I don’t mean mysterious as in cool mysterious, I mean mysterious as in it’s a complete mystery to me why someone who has been consistently thrown to the dogs when the Dark Age needed someone to get the shit kicked out of him is still associated with the group… …oh, wait. No, it’s not. I remember now…the Dark Age represents the only sliver of personality that Dani Furher has…it’s his lifeline. If he left the Dark Age, he’d just be Dani Furher. And let’s be honest…“Just Dani Furher” isn’t gonna bring in the ratings, is he? No, no he isn’t. So be ready, Dani, because when Hessian demands blood, Vampir’s probably gonna yank down your trousers, spin you around, bend you over, and leave you for dead. Just giving you advance warning, that’s all. Finally, we have First Blood…yessir, Daniel Pierce in the ring promises all the excitement of a condom in the garbage. Clowns to the left of me, jokers to the right, indeed… I have to make good on this match. There’s been a hum backstage about how I deserved a title shot, how I hadn’t really seen my due. If I lose now, what will that say about me? This is an obvious opportunity to either put up or shut up…the main event, the title I lost, all eyes on me. If there was ever a night not to fuck up so far in my PRIME career, this is it. Good thing I don’t plan on fucking up, I guess. No, I plan on punking those Imperium/Dark Age/Mickey Goddamned Mouse Club bastards way the hell down, and walking out of ReVolution 61 the way I walked out of ReVolution 50…the 5-Star Champion. I hold one record in this company, and on Wednesday, I’m gonna hold another one… …I’ll become the only two-time 5-Star Champion in PRIME’s history. On Wednesday, I’ll do what I couldn’t do at Culture Shock. On Wednesday, the 5-Star Title is coming home.
