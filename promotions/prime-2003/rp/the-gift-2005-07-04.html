The gift

4 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/4/2005 ~Everyone has a gift. Believe it or not, every single person has a gift in which they can do one thing really, really well. Some people can play sports really well, some people can gamble real well and make casinos like their own personal ATMs, and others can be a great public speaker and hold a crowd within the palm of their hands. There is a downside to having a gift, one day you will lose the gift. It is just a fact of life. One day you will wake up and not be able to hit that curveball, or speak with the ease you once could to a crowd. Then what do you do? What the hell can you do? Many people have difficulty adjusting once their gift is gone. This gift has been their life since as far as they care to remember and now their life as they know it is over. Let's be honest, Jon Kano is a legend. He had a gift in PCW and every wrestling company he was involved with up until then. He had the Midas touch, if you will. He was on top of the wrestling world, to an extent. While we are being honest, Kano's run in PRIME thus far has been less than legendary. He has feuded with Boda extensively, with neither man giving an inch and the feud ending in a stalemate. That is frustrating to Mr. Kano. His record hasn't been the best, but he has yet to lose cleanly in a single's match. He is still frustrated. A few years back, he would have grasped that final rung on the ladder and gotten the mark in the win column, regardless of the odds. It has finally come to the point where Jon Kano must reaccess his career and wonder if his gift is actually gone.~ *The scene opens up very simply. It is pitch black, save for a plain oak table with a single candle burning on it, to the candle's right lay an ashtray with several Marlboro Menthol Lights cigarette butts laying in it. Next to the ashtray, sits a glass with a dark drink in it. On that side of the table, sits the man in question of his own abilities of the past, Jon Kano. He is simply dressed, a royal blue sports jacket over his plain black shirt. His hair is pulled back tightly into a ponytail, exposing his expression of contemplation on his face. As he begins to take a sip out of his drink, his old friend and trainer, Mickey, sits down opposite of him. Mickey is just progressively looking worse and worse each time he appears with Jon Kano. His once vibrant and colorful face is now featureless and pale. His usually neatly groomed hair is white and wild.* Kano: Hey Mickey, thanks for coming. Mickey: Jon, it is my pleasure, as always. What is going on, it sounded important on the phone. Kano: Mickey, when did you realize you had lost it? *Mickey looks confused.* Mickey: It? Kano: The gift. Mickey: It was 1987. I was wrestling in a small northeastern fed. We had a show with about 2000 people watching. I can't even remember the name of the kid I was wrestling. It seemed like every thing I tried to do, came back and bit me in the ass. Everything I touched turned to liquid shit in front of my eyes. After that match, I packed my gear and never wrestled a match again. *Mickey pauses to take a deep breath before continuing.* Mickey: It was a mixed blessing, though. I opened up my school and taught so many people this artform of wrestling, including you, Jon. *Kano is busy fumbling around trying to light yet another cigarette. He finally gets it lit as Mickey stops speaking.* Kano: Mickey, I can't bullshit you. I think I may have lost my gift. I just haven't felt on top of my game really since I came to PRIME. It isn't ring rust, ring rust doesn't last this long. Mickey: Well, Jon, I don't think you can lose your gift this early in life. Hell, you aren't even thirty yet. You might just have a mental block or something else bothering you subconscienciously that is impacting your in-ring performance. *Kano slams his right fist down on the table.* Kano: I CAN'T AFFORD TO NOT BE ON TOP OF MY GAME. Do you know who they have me wrestling on ReVolution? Brandon Youngblood. This kid is the hottest up and coming star in PRIME. And, Mickey, if I am not on top of my game, I can't beat him. Mickey: Now listen here, Jon. I have known you since you were twenty years old, and you are my most successful student. You can beat anyone on this planet, when you are on. You are Jon Kano and you are a PCW Hall of Famer, at ReVolution, you must show why. *Saying this Mickey stands up and leaves very abruptly. Kano has a very confused look on his face.* Kano: Well shit. I hope Mickey is right. All I know is that at ReVolution, if I am not on top of my game, I can still find a way to win, I hope. Youngblood might end up being the sacrificial lamb. I hope not, I like this kid, but I have liked a lot of people that I have made pay the price with the Kanoplex and Kanospear. Youngblood is good, good as I have ever seen at this stage in his career. And if I continue to wrestle the way I have been lately, he will beat me. However if Jon Kano, 'The Man who Rocketh the Mosteth,' 'The REAL Legend' shows up, God have mercy on Youngblood, because I sure as hell won't. *The scene fades to black as Kano collects his cigarettes and lighter. He gets up from the table after taking the last drink from his glass.* ~Will Kano regain his gift? Did he ever lose it? I guess we will find out on ReVolution.~
