blessed are those enabled to turn their own cheek unto the man in the mirror

Date: 15 oct 08 “You’re REALLY gonna love what I’ve got planned for you, Brock. This is undeniably the greatest development in Just Wrestling’s history,” she barks at me proudly, like a Rottweiler with a chew toy. A chew toy it wants you to take and throw; but you know if you touch it you’ll lose the only hand you’ve got left. Jody Monroe was excitable, for lack of a better word, and she wasn’t that hard on the eye either, which was partly the reason I agreed to the meeting. See what they offered on a branding front, seeming as though I’d managed to garner some interest after the inaugural tour of Just Wrestling. Five minutes couldn’t hurt me. Push my foot onto her desk and lean back. She simply swats it off as she rises to her own feet and moves to the filing cabinet. Rummages around a bit then shoves a folio into my lap. Turns the cover and in big, fat red letters the words “Limbertek” shouts up at me. Look at her and she’s beaming like Crippen did when he found a dollar. “Tell me what you know about Limbertek,” she says to me, as if I should already know of them. Shrug at her, seems the only answer I got. She rolls with the punches and sits on the edge of the desk, folding one leg over the other and I hope for a Sharon Stone moment and the chance to let me have a little looksee at her manicured axe-wound. The thought never crosses her mind. She speaks about Limbertek. “Limbertek have been creating prosthetics for over 80 years, Brock. You know what a prosthetic is, right?” “The thing you wrap round your cock?” I joke. She ignores my jest. “Prosthetics are artificial limbs. Limbertek have the larges-“ “That’s me done.” Stand and spin on my heels, making a B-line for the door. The five minutes is up. “You haven’t even heard me through,” she explains. “This is a LUCRATIVE offer!” “Lucrative offer is askin’ me to hang from a cliff by my good arm while my balls are itchin’, bitch. THIS is disrespectful. I’d’ve expected more from an educated bitch like you. Turns out you’re just a money hungry whore that gets wet from dollar bills.” Her jaw gapes open widely as I grab the door handle. “PLEASE!” she shouts across the room. “Just hear me out.” Don’t look at her but stop to give her a moment to speak. “It’s not to get you on posters promoting limbs or doing TV campaigns about how you can open jars better with a Limbertek hand, Brock… it’s not like that at all.” “Pffft,” I manage. “I know what this is ‘like’, Jodie. Cold, hard cash. That’s what this is ALWAYS about, Miss Monroe. Now ‘scuse me fer bein’ rude, ma’am, but fuck you and the dick you rode in on.” Stomp. Stomp. Slam. That’s me. Business meeting over. - | s h e p | - The unknown force of the spoon guided my neck, twisting and turning my head with it’s flight path, mouth gaping like one of them clown mouthed sideshows at the carnival; I try to feed Adam some custard. But the kid ain’t interested. Like Stevie Wonder trying to play “Pin-the-Tail-on-the-Donkey”, there are pathetic shots at Adam’s mouth and custard blobs on his nose, cheeks and chin. “Dammit,” I mutter as the kid seals his lips shut tight. “How do you get him to eat this junk?” April pops her head round the corner, smiling for a change. How I longed for that smile. Missed it like oxygen. She just shrugs at me and sits at the other end of the table. Just watching. “With patience, Brock. So, I guess you’ve lucked out on the baby feeding skills department then.” Turn to protest, just in time to catch her cheeky wink. Missed that, too. My heart ached so bad and it didn’t so much as beat anymore, but dull thumped in my chest these days. Her wink made me miss a dull thump. Concentrating on the task I try again, this time the end of the spoon winds up in Adam’s eye and he squawks in protest. Shrug and toss the spoon on the table, push to my feet and throw my hand up in defeat. “Give up, Ape. Got nothin’. Kid ain’t hungry.” “I’ve NEVER eaten a thing through my eye, Brock.” “You keep that cheek up an’ yer eye might eat my fist,” I joke. She shakes her head in disgust but remains smiling the whole time, removing the squawking, custard-eyed Adam from the clutches of his high-chair. Her movements are so natural, second nature to her all this nurturing. The perfect mother. “I’m seein’ someone,” I finally brave the words to her. Stops her dead in her tracks. She twists a glance over her shoulder at me, trying to conceal her emotions. “That’s your business, Brock,” she pronounces, pausing with her thoughts for a moment. “But I hope she’s nice.” “What? No. Not like that. Fuck. For the angry. Anger, I mean. Seein’ a shrink group or whatever they call ‘emselves.” April’s ears prick up, her body language perks and she puts Adam on one hip. Reaches across and cups my neck in her hand, planting a wet, sloppy kiss on my cheek. “OMIGAWD! That’s the best news I’ve heard ALLLL day, Brock. Good for you,” she gawks proudly. Shake my head. Cup her head this time and plant a kiss on her forehead. Cup Adam’s head next and plant one on top of his. “Nah, it’s all thanks ta you, Ape. Made me see the light, so to speak, y’know? The angry took me over an’ I don’t wanna be like that no more. Just wannabe… well… I just wannabe Brock.” Her pretty mouth curves into a proud smile as she runs her hand ruffles me freshly shaved-to-scalp head, tapping my clean skull a few times. “Great to hear… and LOVE the new look. Very rogue-ish,” she remarks. It flatters me and I watch her leave the room. Didn’t feel like an intruder on my family’s life anymore, despite having to leave them to their own devices after my weekly visit. Planned on speaking with April about getting more access to Adam. Maybe even have him stay a night. Once I’d got the holes patched in the walls. Finally, I saw the kid as my son. - | s h e p | - “My name is Brock and I’m…” Face-palm myself, drawing deep on breath and courage. Feeling like an anti-climactic superhero in front of the town-folk; accepting a key to the city for saving an ice-cream sandwich. Gordon stands up, crosses the Ring of Trust and places his hand on my elbow gingerly. Almost TOO gingerly. Gives me a “tsk. tsk.” as if to tell me it was all okay. “Now, Brock, the first thtep ith admitting you have problem, mmmkay? We all done it before, mmmkay? Now it’th your turn, mmmkay?” he ‘mmmkayed’ at me. If he made that fucking noise again he might be lucky enough to brush his teeth by sticking his toothbrush up as his ass. Sigh heavily and run my hand over my smooth scalp. “My name is Brock. I’m am- fucking stupid. THIS is fucking stupid” Guy by the name of Randolph, with a beard near down to his belly and tatts anywhere skin ought to be, gets to his feet and crosses the Ring of Trust with his mouth running before he even gets to me. “Yeah, Brock, it’s stupid. An’ this here cunt, Gordon, is about as queer as they come. Bet everytime he farts people think he’s tryin’ to get their attention. PSSSST” mocks Randolph. “Know what I mean?“ “HEY!” Gordon tries to interrupt unsuccessfully. “But this works, man. Got me back with my wife and kids. Settled me down and I got a job again, right? It works. So fucken say it.” “Or what? You’ll get angry?” “Don’t test me, buddy. I’m not reformed YET!” “Okay,” I finally admit. “I’ll say it.” Randolph takes his seat again, in the Ring of Trust, landing on his ass with a “huff” of pride. Gordon holds his hands together tightly with a dumb smirk on his face, like a mother watching her kid walk for the first time. “My name is Brock and I’m an anger-holic.” A round of applause. My embarrassed ass falls into a cheap steel chair, back inside the Ring of Trust. This makes me feel like a natural woman. - | s h e p | - Get home and there’s a brunette standing at my door waiting for me. She’s dressed in a nice skirt suit, which is charcoal with a pink pinstripe. She’s etching toward 5”8” or so and her legs are damn nice. Cute little turtle shell glasses cap off the attire and I can feel warmth in my nethers. Her eyes light up when they lock with mine. “What’s the time?” I ask. Glances at her gold watch and throws the answer back that it’s around eleven-thirty. “Then what are ya doin’ here? I dunno who the fuck y’are so it can’t be a booty call.” Her face tells me she’s flattered and disgusted all at once, but she shakes her head and admits she knows it’s late. But it wasn’t reason for her visit. Tells me her name is Samantha Gideon. Tells me she’s the personal assistant to Jodie Monroe and Tim Shipley. “That s’posed to impress me? Guess yer here to try and get me to be the amputee poster boy for Limbertek, too.” “Yeah… kinda. And I wanted to meet you.” Stunned I do a cartoon-style double take as she coyly toes the gravel path with stiletto. “Meet me?” “Yeah. I’m a fan.” “Okay, but isn’t this a bit late for adulation? Unless we’re heading toward that booty call I was mentionin’ before.” “They wanted me to try and persuade you,” she admits. “Persuadin’ involve a bed, filthy monkey sex and a cigarette?” Samantha laughs and her pretty green eyes light up in the moonlight. “No. I’m just a fan and wanted to meet you.” Her gaze wanders down up and down my body. I watch it move across my shoulders and down my right arm to my non-existent hand, which is mimicked by my prosthetic. “You come to look at my hand?” Face reddens and she shakes her head nervously, brushing wisps of hair from her face to conceal her awkwardness. “No. Just…” she trails off. Courage ran out on her. “Just what?” “Did it hurt?” Her question left me even more stumped than my hand had. Eyes dancing in the moonlight as though she were anticipating my answer and hoping it to be that it did hurt. Stare at her like she were tearing the hearts out of kittens. “Did it hurt? Of course it hurt. It STILL fucking hurts. The fuck kinda question is that?” “I dunno, just a question I guess.” “Well, I guess you better get to steppin’ unless you got some secrets ‘bout how to beat Crip and Jake for me,” I suggest with eyebrows raised so expectantly she blushes and shakes her head. “Didn’t think so.” “Think you can beat ‘em?” she asks, in attempt to change the subject. “Dunno. Find out soon enough. I’m not gonna die wonderin’ though, am I? Would be nice to wear that belt. Haven’t worn one in seven years, since EWA.” She whistles as though stunned by the facts. “That’s a long time.” “Hopefully won’t be any longer. And I better rest up if I’m gonna get Crip’s bling. So nigh-nigh, Sam.” She passes by and leaves me unsettled as she glances joyously at my missing paw for one last time. The world felt differently to people unlike themselves whereas I just wanted to feel like Brock again. Getting Crip’s gold was that first stepping stone.
