War (Part 5)- Plans for the Future

2 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 2nd "It takes more courage to reveal insecurities than to hide them, more strength to relate to people than to dominate them, more 'manhood' to abide by thought-out principles rather than blind reflex. Toughness is in the soul and spirit, not in muscles and an immature mind."- Alex Karras May 2nd, 2009 Caleb, My name is Steven Connors and I am in dire need of your help. I'm a former detective of the Raleigh Police Department with loose association to the FBI. Recently, my badge was taken away because of departmental violations on a case that I am still working on independently. This case has taken my world and turned it upside down. Everything that matters to me has been stripped away. I have tried to fight against increasing adversity, but circumstances are crippling. Any resistance seems fruitless. It serves no purpose, because regardless of how hard I push, my enemies know how to push back harder, stronger, and faster. One of my enemies happens to be your father. The entire ordeal started when I was just a young man in 1991. My grandfather, Daniel Wilson, was a prominent detective working to bring your father's criminal enterprise down. After one of his dear friends was murdered by a henchmen, my grandfather decided to end the situation off the books. Needless to say, he failed and remained in debt to your father until his death. My grandmother was an unnecessary casualty, also falling because of your father's hand. My father and uncles went into the same line of work and ran into the same problems. Once they took your father's case, their lives were subsequently destroyed in a matter of months. They, along with the rest of the Raleigh Police Department, were conned into believing an illusion, one which allowed your father and his "family" to evade capture in June of 2006. In May of 2008, my team was called to a crime scene involving Devin Shakur, an adopted brother of your father. My brother, Larry, and I were on the verge of closure when your father decided to intervene and ruin the case, demanding that we stop investigating or face stiff consequences. Due to the nature of our jobs, we elected to continue with our investigation, establishing a new case by fusing your father's case with Devin's. As it stands today, I am the only member of my family that remains alive. My brother committed suicide after losing his wife and daughter to your father and Devin. I know it is my duty as a man to fight back and eliminate those who have done me wrong. But I can't. The last amount of willpower has been sapped from my soul. I don't have anymore strength left to pick up a gun and restore dignity to my family name. This is where I ask for your help. It is not in the nature of my family to negotiate with the enemy, but I don't have any other choice. I would like to have the chance to see a better tomorrow even if it is clouded in complete darkness. My request is for you to speak to your father and bring some morality into his warped mind. He is hellbent on scorching the Earth and I don't believe any law enforcement agency is capable of stopping or slowing him down. He has an army of soldiers willing to die for him, and none of their deaths will stop his goal of ultimate destruction. I know that you are a young man and what I am asking of you is a great responsibility. You have built a solid life for yourself despite being born with the last name Daniels. Believe me, if I felt that something, anything, would resolve the conflict before asking for your involvement, that resource will be exhausted. I don't see one. I am tired of living with death hanging over me. I am tired of living in the shadows of something I didn't create. The spirits of my elders haunt me at every turn, begging for me to avenge their deaths and bring this war to a close. So I ask for your help because I think you are the only person Christian might listen to. Signed, Steven Connors ---------------------------------- Christian Daniels pushed off weary legs and rose to his feet, clutching a bottle of Jack Daniels in his left hand. The burning sensation from the last swig lingered in his throat, so he waited a moment before letting the last drops enter his body. He took a moment to gaze at his surroundings, which constituted of nothing more than a dimly lit basement and the work bench he was just sitting on. The basement held a lot of history, considering it was the room where the Smith's had plotted notorious schemes on and off for the past seven years. Christian felt the need to concoct another scheme, one that would bring down the last remaining obstacle and ensure the collective freedom of his family. While the group suspected that Steven had gone into hiding and wasn't planning on showing himself again, Christian knew better. In his long and strenuous battle amidst the trenches of warfare, the first rule he learned was to never count an opponent out. Even if one iota of life remains, the battle must continue to be waged until they are one hundred percent dead. Christian also knew the Connors bloodline better than anybody, and a man with that last name would attempt some pretty desperate acts to keep himself in the game. A man like that wold never surrender even with his entire army decimated. It only meant Steven had nothing to lose and everything to gain. Showing weakness now would solve nothing and only give Steven new life when he needed it the most. Christian had come too far and risked too much to put the group in jeopardy now. Eliminating Steven was paramount, but thinking about the future also needed to be considered. If the warfare continued to be so public and bloody, federal involvement would soon find its way into the battlefield. If that were to happen, Christian would be on the defensive and have to waste immense time and potentially bodies to silence their attack. It was something he couldn't afford to let happen. The goal was to eliminate all of Devin's threats and get him out of the country, away from the furious eyes of law enforcement who would turn away from the traditional justice system to bring him in. Devin's PRIME contract expired on December 31st. While Christian hadn't discussed the matter in full with Devin, there was no way another contract extension would occur. The end of the year was the optimal target date for a complete exit. Devin already compromised the project by signing for a full year after the ankle injury, but anything after the calendar year would be unnecessary time. Just as Christian reached into his pocket to pull out his cellular phone, the basement door swung open. Michael Blaze made his way down the stairs and peered around the corner, spotting Christian next to the bottle of Jack. "What'cha doin, kid?" Michael said, grasping the rail and swinging himself onto the cold concrete. He leaned against the rail and drank from his own bottle of booze. "What I always do." "You aren't still fretting over Steven are ya?" "Kid ain't gone yet, despite how much ye'd like'ta believe so." Christian said, moving back over to the work bench and sitting down. "It's been over a month now and he hasn't done or said anything. You know how the Connors work better than any of us. They aren't the kind to waste time. What's he gonna have planned in a month that he can't plan in a week?" Michael replied. Christian nodded his head in affirmation, "Yeah, but I also know they're capable of actin' on a whim and catchin' us outta nowhere. Then what's gonna happen?" "Man..." Michael said, mulling over how he wanted to word the rebuttal. Christian wasn't the easiest individual to convince even if one was in his inner circle. "What does he got? Who can he turn to? If he goes to the feds, they'll lock him up for killing Brandon and Matt. No way they are gonna let someone like that off the hook, even to get us. His boys back at the PD wouldn't let him walk away either. He knows that shit is hanging over his head. He gonna call a mob boss? None of them want to fuck with you and the one who tried to, DeAngelis, hasn't been heard from in God knows how long. The kid tied the noose around his own neck this time. All we have to do is finish up and walk." Christian let out a deep exhale and slouched down into a thinking man's position. The rhetoric and logic had been shoved in his face over the past two weeks. Michael also figured their legal team was powerful enough to get Devin out of his PRIME contract after UltraViolence. The Biker didn't agree on any of the aforementioned. Christian placed a hand under his chin and kept his stare on the floor, "Mike, you know how much I hate fuckin' loose ends, right?" The gray haired stockbroker nodded in agreement. "A loose end was what got us in this bullshit'ntha first place." With a half frown, Michael nodded again. "All we gotta do is make sure the kid's dead. How long would a small program take to work on someone that fuckin' emotionally unstable? Two, three weeks, tops? Kid's a wreck, but he's still there. Say we bail, what happens then? He recovers, gets his shit together, gathers what he needs'ta, and brings our asses out of hidin' again. You think I'ma stand for that shit knowin' it was just one fucker standin' between us and the light at the end of the tunnel? Nah uh." Michael folded his arms across his chest and tapped his foot against the concrete, "What is your definition of a small program?" "We drop someone in his life, see where he's at in the head, and plot accordingly." "And you don't think that'll be suspicious enough?" "Not if it's a woman he won't." Michael thought for a second, finger in air for retort, and then dropped his head in agreement, "And all the while we're getting ready to bail?" "I'll be more than happy to take care of the little shit myself after our agent gets what we need." "Alright, if that'll make you feel better, I can run with that. Who are we going to get?" "What about Mal? She ain't got shit else to do but shop these days." Michael shook his head, "Chris ain't gonna let his wife get that close." "What about Rachel then?" Christian said. Michael chuckled and shrugged his shoulders, "Heh, she might have you killed if you make her go through with that." Christian gave a dismissive roll of the eyes, "I can handle'er. Let's just get this planned, call everybody back and get'tha ball rolling. I'm tired of thinking about what that prick's gonna do." "But you can't stop thinking about it." "When we're on a plane 5 miles over the Atlantic, I'll stop thinkin bout it." Christian said, walking past Michael and up the stairs. -------------------------------- Devin Shakur seldom discussed his personal life with anybody outside the Smith household. Ever since the first few weeks of his professional wrestling return in January, media appearances and general PRIME promotional activities were being thrown by the wayside. There were very few people Devin trusted to conduct an objective interview, and holding the carte blanche card allowed him much more freedom than other superstars on the roster. His relationship with Sun Tzu had never been on sturdier grounds. Most around the locker room. and even those in the house, suspected the relationship would fizzle out after a few months. How could two people with such alpha personalities be willing to lend each other a helping hand and play second fiddle if the situation called for it? The disdain for capitalist viewpoints brought them together, the sex kept them close, and the desire never to deal with others made them inseparable. Sunny came to accept and understand the battlefield of scars on Devin's body, and both agreed to distance themselves from the other's background unless involvement was absolutely necessary. With the death of Larry Connors, Devin felt safe enough to call Sunny and summon her from China. Her government business concluded in the middle of April and she was eagerly waiting for Devin's call. He decided to make the trip home for Sunny even more intriguing by claiming a big surprise was waiting when she got back. She tried every trick in the book to pry the information out of Devin, but nothing worked. Sunny wracked her brain for the entire eighteen hour flight, throwing out possibilities: Could they be moving in together permanently? Was he going to whisk her away on a romantic vacation? Buy her something so extravagant that she would fall even more in love with him? The actual answer was a combination of all three, an engagement ring. Ever since Tyler Rayne broke his ankle in October, the thought of making Sunny his bride was the ultimate fantasy. Devin was told that once business had wrapped up in the States, there would be a very low chance Christian would allow someone else into the fold at their new destination. He could tolerate Sunny and nobody else in the family had a gripe with her. They respected her because she kept to herself and didn't have a problem when it came to self defense. It also didn't hurt that she could put a bullet in someone's head and not blink out of place. From inside the jewelry store, Devin pushed a button on his remote and unlocked the car. He turned around and shook the owner's hand while Jason Hardy pushed open the door and walked out holding a briefcase containing the ring. They weren't going to move something so valuable in a plastic bag. Jason himself had never been married, or in a relationship that lasted longer than five months, but he knew a good deal when he saw one. The deal took $300,000 out of Devin's pocket, but Jason informed him through whispers that he had seen the same ring going for three times more at other high end stores. Devin and the owner exchanged pleasantries, both laughing at the joke that neither expected nor wanted to see the other one ever again. The owner walked Devin to the door before exchanging another handshake and going to his next customer. Devin strolled over to the car, watched his door rise vertically, and stepped down into the car. Jason shifted the briefcase over to Devin, who put it across his lap and adjusted the radio. Jason ensured both car doors were shut before speeding back to home base. "Man, I can't believe you are actually gonna do it!" Jason grinned, looking over at Devin and patting him on the shoulder. "Me either, really is the first step into the rest of your life." Devin said, looking down at the briefcase, "You really think she's gonna say yes?" "You ain't got nothing to worry about and neither do we!" Jason replied, exuding confidence, "Christian has everything on lock down so we can rest easy. All we have to do now is figure out how to turn from mobsters into wedding planners." -------------------------------------------- To Steven Connors: First, my deepest sympathies go out to you and your family. Coping with a death is a troubling experience upon any shoulders, but encountering such a great deal in a short amount of time is nothing short of unbearable. I applaud your courageous efforts to be strong in these troubling times, and nobody should be disappointed that you are seeking guidance to end the bloodshed. It is much better to end anything with words rather than with violence. I have followed your story rather extensively ever since it was suspected that my father was in some way related to Maria's untimely passing. I am also quite immersed in the history of your family since that time, and I know that each of your relatives were only doing what came natural to them as detectives. God will not judge them harshly for their actions if they have given their hearts to him in forgiveness. In regard to the request you have sent me, my father is a man that I would prefer to completely disassociate with. While the moral man inside of me says that he is a soul who is deeply disturbed and must be forgiven should he ask for forgiveness, the logical being in my body does not wish to delve into attempts at a recovery. I've worked through years of hardships and discrimination because of my father's reputation, and to indulge into the business now that I have finally established a distant lifestyle would be outlandish. However, the Good Samaritan in my heart cannot allow a man with your burdens to go unassisted. If you believe that a talk with my father is the cure for ending such mayhem, then I am more than happy to accommodate your request. My office number is [detail withheld]. I am here on most days helping out the church so there is a good chance you will find me. You may call me immediately upon receiving this letter or wait until your earliest convenience to set up an in person meeting to discuss specific details. God Bless You, Caleb Daniels Steven folded the letter and placed it back inside the envelope with a huge smile on his face. The wryness of his smile made him thankful that nobody else was around to see how macabre his intentions were. If all went according to plan, the sight of Christian's son would be enough to bring The Biker down to his knees, or at least thwart any goal of leaving the country within the month. The rest of the group would be under the assumption that Steven conceded defeat, and none of them will want for Christian to clash with his own son. Those notions would put Steven on the offensive and make the group a bunch of sitting ducks waiting to be picked off one by one. Steven looked over at his gun cabinet and got up to start loading the weapons. He would call Caleb in the morning to set up their first face to face.
