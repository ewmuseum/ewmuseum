Purpose A chance for self redemption A chance to truly make a difference in this world I’ll let life have me for this round. Tricks are damned life because I’ve got people with me this time… Oh dear Lord what are Christian and company going to think when I tell them this? "Our life is made by the death of others"- Leonardo Da Vinci Devin Shakur flicks the documents onto the bed, turns around, and once again stares back out into the night. "My life will now be made by your death Anthony…" His mind lucid as the day he was liberated. "It’s not like you have a choice in the matter…" And upon his face we find that eerie smirk that a certain superstar of PRIME is noted for.
