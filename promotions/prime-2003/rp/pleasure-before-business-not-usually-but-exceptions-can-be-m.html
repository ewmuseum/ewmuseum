Pleasure Before Business? Not Usually, But Exceptions Can Be Made

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: After ReV 89 and before CS The sound of bare feet softly stepping across a solid marble floor is heard in the pre-dawn darkness. What’s left of the moonlight offers only shadows for our view, making any recognition impossible. We can, however, see a shadow moving from right to left, the apparent owner of the footsteps we hear. The shadow stops and appears to stretch for a moment, arms reaching high into the air, then reaches out and pulls on what we will find to be a refrigerator door. The light from inside the fridge cuts into the darkness, revealing a luxurious kitchen decorated in immaculately bright white with restaurant quality stainless steel appliances. The large refrigerator door hides its intruder almost completely; except for the rather shapely female rear-end that pops out ever so slightly from around its edge. The black thong underwear accents the firm, tan derriere (which has no apparent tan lines) as its owner scavenges through the confines of the appliance. The clanging of glass-on-glass is heard as the huntress finally finds her prey…a bottle of Dom Perignon. She stands up, letting us catch only a glimpse of her beauty before the light from inside the refrigerator is extinguished by the closing door. Her brunette hair is pulled up into a make-shift bun, but mussed nonetheless as strands dangle down loosely here and there. A white button-up dress shirt, not really buttoned up at all, however, covers her torso and now hides the once revealed backside. Her body is extremely fit and her muscle tone is played up by the fading light from inside the fridge which casts shadows on her athletic-looking legs. The face of a model puts the icing on the cake of this exquisite form. Her brown eyes, long eyelashes, and full lips only accentuate what has already been a magnificent feast for the eyes. She almost looks familiar, but then again, maybe not. As the door on the refrigerator shuts, the woman does a graceful spin on the balls of her feet and shuffles around the kitchen some more, making her way toward an overhead glass rack. She reaches up and grabs two crystal champagne flutes, clinging and clanging them together as she wraps two fingers of one hand around each stem. Swinging the champagne bottle in the other hand, she merrily exits the kitchen area. She enters a grand foyer with a meticulously crafted chandelier hanging from the thirty foot ceiling. Starting up the spindling flight of stairs, we notice the shadows of several pieces of artwork adorning the walls white walls. She hums a fanciful tune of nothing in particular as she carefully makes her way up the first flight of stairs. As she reaches the second floor, the scantily clad woman makes her way down a long, dark hallway. At the end there is a door ajar where dim light flickers from inside. The champagne flutes lightly clang together, foreshadowing her arrival. A voice calls out from inside the room at the end of the hall. A voice that is certainly familiar and unmistakable. “It sounds like you found what you were looking for…” The voice trails off as the woman reaches the end of the hall. She leans against the doorway and nudges the door open completely with her foot, holding the champagne bottle and crystal flutes in front of her like a trophy. But the real trophy is Jenna Ryder, or at least that’s what Tyler Nelson would tell you after last night. The CEO’s seductress purrs as she enters the room, striding sensually toward Nelson’s large, round bed in the master suite of his palatial mansion. Tyler props himself up on his shoulder from a prone position on his side, his lower half covered by a money green satin sheet. Jenna: I found my way around. The candles scattered around the room have been burning for quite some time judging by the amount of wax melted at the base of each. Nelson’s room is bigger than some houses, complete with couches, chairs, dressers, and two big screen plasma televisions on opposing ends of the room. Various items of clothing are strewn about the room, giving more of a clue as to what has been happening for the better part of the night. The candle light flickers in Jenna’s eyes as she hands Tyler one of the flutes. The unbuttoned button-up shirt, obviously belonging to the CEO, just barely covers her ample bosom as she pops the cork off the champagne bottle and quickly drinks some of the overflow that follows. A small amount dribbles down her chin and onto her chest, running between her breasts toward her well toned tummy. Jenna giggles as she pours some of the bubbly into Tyler’s glass. Jenna: You can clean that up in a little bit. The CEO quickly accepts the invitation. Tyler: You better believe I will. I couldn’t possibly let that go to waste. Jenna finishes off Tyler’s glass and then fills her own. She sets the bottle down next to the bed and sits down on the edge, gazing down at the CEO. Nelson raises his glass to Jenna’s, gently tapping them together. Tyler: Cheers! Jenna: And then some… Both take quick pulls of champagne before Tyler sets his flute down on the floor. He plops down on the bed, lying on his back with his arms spread beside him. Tyler: You do realize that you’ve managed to keep me up all night when I have a lot of preparations to make before I leave for Japan. Jenna runs her tongue along her upper lip in a sexy, teasing manner. Jenna: So I did. I hope you aren’t angry with me. She bats her eyelids playfully at Tyler, who returns with a smirk of his own. Tyler: I thought you like it when I’m angry with you. Jenna: I do…. She bends down and plants a quick kiss on Tyler’s lips, then stands up. Jenna: ….but it will have to wait for another time. I have a flight to catch. Nelson props himself up on his elbows as Jenna turns and walks toward the couch where her dress is draped sloppily over the back. He watches her the whole way with a grin on his face, seemingly replaying the night’s events over and over in his head. She lets the CEO’s shirt slowly slide off of her shoulders and to the floor, revealing a circular shaped tattoo at the base of her back. She also exposes extremely toned and fit arms and shoulders. Jenna slinks herself into the tight fitting black one-piece dress that does her body some serious justice. Tyler: What’s on the agenda for today? Jenna finishes some final adjustments on her dress, smoothing out some creases, then tries to tuck some of the loose strands of hair up into her bun. Jenna: It’s gonna be a long day today. I’ve got a photo shoot, a magazine interview, and I have to shoot a couple of scenes for that movie I’m working on. The CEO rolls over onto his side and props his head up on his fist. Tyler: I trust you’ll be thinking about me the whole time. The arrogance with which he made the decree is dashed by Jenna’s answer. Jenna: Nope. The surprise answer was like a slap in the face, something Tyler wasn’t used to. His face grows kind of long, almost like a sad puppy dog. Tyler: Not even when you’re doing…. She interrupts before he can finish, this time turning to look him in the eyes. Jenna: Not even then. I don’t think about guys I’m involved with when I’m working. Porn is just a job to me, Ty, nothing more…nothing less. The CEO frowns. He’s still a little unsure of whether he wants to share this magnificent prize with anyone else. The fact that Jenna Ryder was an up-and-coming star of the adult entertainment industry was a turn on for him, but his greedy side still wanted to have her all to himself. An interesting quandary for The Greediest Player in the Game to find himself. Tyler: What makes me different from anyone else? The beautiful woman gets a mischievous glint in her eyes. Jenna: Besides all that money, you mean? Tyler: Well….yeah. Jenna walks over to the bed and crawls across it on her hands and knees. She nestles right up to Tyler’s face and puts her lips up to his ear. The sensuality in her voice sets his loins afire. Jenna: I actually want you, Ty. The ego can now re-inflate. The cocky, arrogant smirk returns to the face of the CEO as Jenna plants a kiss on his cheek. Jenna: Gotta run. Call me later. Tyler: I don’t call. You call me. Jenna: Fine then. She heads toward the door, grabbing an overnight bag from the floor along the way. She flings it over her shoulder and looks back over the same, winking at Tyler. The CEO simply nods his head as Jenna departs the room. He gets a satisfied look on his face as he leans back on the bed. Tyler: Tyler old boy….I think you’ve got yourself one hot piece of real estate there. He reaches over and grabs the champagne flute, taking one last drink before falling off to dream land. He had to catch at least a few hours of sleep before embarking on his journey to wrestling omnipotence at Culture Shock. The next morning… There was much to be done before heading off to Tokyo for his date with destiny. After a quick shower to freshen up, the CEO comes bouncing down the elegant staircase in his foyer, decked out in a black hand-made Italian suit, Italian leather shoes polished to a blinding shine, and a money-green tie over a white dress shirt to top off the ensemble. Nelson looks all business today, as his face really doesn’t emit any emotion at all. Reaching the bottom of the stairs, Tyler strides confidently toward the door where the butler is standing by to open it. The classically dressed man-servant pulls the large oak door open and allows Tyler to exit the mansion without missing a beat. He descends a flight of stairs to his limousine, where his assistant Mrs. Beasley awaits. The middle aged woman is dressed in a tan blouse with a dark brown scarf around her neck, along with a pair of brown pants which are similar in color to the scarf. A pair of wire-framed reading glasses rest on the end of her nose. Mrs. Beasley: Good morning, Mr. Nelson. Beautiful day today. It was a beautiful day, indeed. The temperature was in the mid-sixties and the sun was shining brightly overhead. Tyler: I’m not interested in the weather, Mrs. Beasley. The assistant frowns, mostly to herself. It was worth a shot, anyway. Mrs. Beasley: Of course not, sir. How was your evening with Ms. Ryder? The assistant pulls open the door of the limo for the CEO and he climbs right in. Tyler: Exhausting. Mrs. Beasley rolls her eyes before entering the limo after her boss, shutting the door behind her. Inside, Tyler slumps in his seat a bit, resting his head against the side of the vehicle. He rubs his eyes, seemingly trying to work the bags out from underneath them. Without even looking at his assistant, the CEO begins to bark out orders. Tyler: Send some flowers to her, and tell her that she’s coming to Tokyo with me. I’ll pick her up with the jet tomorrow afternoon. The assistant busily jots down some notes on a legal pad. Mrs. Beasley: Consider it done. She then shuffles through some paperwork in her attaché case, pulling a few sheets out and holding them in front of Tyler. Mrs. Beasley: Here are the final ratings numbers for this weeks ReVolution. Nelson reaches out for the papers, still rubbing at his eyes with his other hand and not able to see. He simply holds his hand out and waits for the papers to be put in it, which they quickly are by Mrs. Beasley. Tyler drops the papers in his lap for a moment as he finishes up the work on his eyes, then rubs his face. With a deep sigh he finally picks up the papers again and gazes upon them with dissatisfaction. Tyler: These numbers aren’t going up. He flips from page to page, seeming to look for better news and not finding any. The assistant tries to find the silver lining. Mrs. Beasley: They are staying steady, however. The CEO shoots her a scowl. Tyler: There are too many things collaborating against me in this venture, Mrs. Beasley. First, I have an imbecile as a champion who is about as credible in the wrestling industry as Rafael Palmiero is in baseball. Second, we are on a cable network that isn’t free to everyone and therefore we’re missing out on a huge demographic. I mean, I love Hugh Hefner and all, but we can’t survive with just the eighteen to fifty-five male pervert demographic. Third, I only have a handful of wrestlers on the entire roster that have any sort of cross-over appeal. Ferguson and Tsonda are blue-chippers, with a few others not far behind, but the drop-off after them is almost fatal. We need marketable wrestlers, not pot-heads and decrepit has-beens with a few jobbers sprinkled in for flavor. The CEO crumples the papers up in his hands and tosses the wad across the limo. Mrs. Beasley doesn’t even bat an eye and the miniature fit, obviously used to the outbursts. Tyler: I’ve got to get the title off of that goon Walkins, but now I have this idiot Tchu telling me that he’s not going to let that happen. I should just fire the both of them and be done with it. Mrs. Beasley: That might tend to cause more trouble than it’s worth, sir…legal issues and all. Tyler: Meh! There’s a legal loophole for everything. Nelson gazes out the window of the limousine as it heads toward downtown Dallas. Mrs. Beasley: Won’t it be more fun to keep them under your thumb? Tyler: Yeah, I suppose you’re right. She knows Tyler like a book, as well she should being his assistant for all these years. There have been plenty of rough patches along the way, but Mrs. Beasley is a tolerant person…especially when payday rolls around. She’s not greedy like Tyler Nelson by any stretch of the imagination, but everyone certainly does have a price and she gets paid handsomely for her patience. Mrs. Beasley: I’ve gone ahead and lined up airfare, hotel, and suites at the Tokyo Dome for executives from that have shown interest in putting PRIME programming on their networks. Tyler: Very good. I suppose we should set something up with Hef for when I get back from Tokyo so I can sit down and talk to him. Unless he can come up with a sweeter deal we might have to take our show elsewhere. Mrs. Beasley scribbles down some more notes on her legal pad. Mrs. Beasley: We’ve hired some extra security for the pay-per-view. Nelson rolls his eyes. Tyler: You’re not using the same place we always get these idiots from, are you? Mrs. Beasley: You’re not happy with Security-R-Us? Tyler: Those guys are a bunch of incompetent pussies. Terminate that contract and hire some good people. Maybe Poteet knows a few from the railroad that we can get. And I want a new head of security, too. This Sullivan Sawyer moron wouldn’t know security if it fucked him in the ass. Get me a list of potential candidates by the end of the day. More notes to scribble down on the legal pad. Suddenly Tyler sits upright in his seat, the devious smirk he’s known for slowly spreading across his face. Tyler: Wait a minute…..wait just one Goddamn minute! I think I just figured everything out, Mrs. Beasley. Mrs. Beasley: I knew you would, sir. Tyler: When I win the Dual Halo match, it’s stipulated that I get a wish, so to speak, of my choosing. All I have to wish for is to have Clyde Walkins forfeit the Universal Title and all my problems will be solved! The CEO is really loving this idea. Mrs. Beasley loves it too, since that’s what she’s paid for after all. Mrs. Beasley: That certainly sounds like the answer, sir. Tyler: It does, doesn’t it? The limo finally reaches its destination, the headquarters of the New and Improved Greed, INC, a sleek looking structure of mirrored glass. The vehicle pulls up to the front door where Clint Poteet is waiting out front. The newly crowned Tag Team Champion is dressed in his usual best, a Harley-Davidson t-shirt, black jeans, and a pair of black leather riding boots. Poteet pulls the door to the limo open, allowing the CEO and Mrs. Beasley to exit. Tyler: Morning, Clint. Clint: Sup, boss. Tyler: I trust you’ve recovered from the party I threw for you after ReVolution? A wide grin spreads across the face of the bodyguard, a rare sight indeed. Clint: Has the party stopped yet? Tyler: Heh…not if I have my way, which I usually do. We’ll give Tokyo something to remember when I win the Dual Halo at Culture Shock. Those tight-asses won’t know what hit ‘em. Clint: That’s what I like to hear, boss. Nelson looks over his shoulder to Mrs. Beasley as they enter the building. Tyler: Speaking of winning the Dual Halo, have all of my guests arrived? Mrs. Beasley: Yes, sir. All seven of them are in the main boardroom. Tyler: Seven? There are supposed to be eight. Mrs. Beasley: One of them declined the invitation, a Mr.- The CEO waves his hand dismissively. Tyler: I’m sure I know who it is. Fast forward to the executive boardroom, a spacious meeting room which is darkened except for a projector displaying an image of the Dual Halo structure. Situated around the long, thin, oval shaped table are eight high-back black leather chairs, each situated in such a fashion that the backs are obscuring who is seated in them. There is a low murmur of small talk going on, but nothing we are able to make out or discern who the voices belong to. After a few moments, the CEO strolls in with Clint Poteet lumbering close behind. The bodyguards stops and waits by the door, basically blocking anyone from entering or leaving. Tyler: Hello, gentlemen. The room becomes quiet as Tyler takes his place in front of the projector screen, the image of the Dual Halo structure superimposed over top of him. He squints his eyes a bit from the bright light, but holds his arms out to his sides nonetheless as he speaks. Tyler: This is what we are here to talk about. This magnificent structure, which I shelled out a fortune for, called the Dual Halo. I know all of you know the details of the match, so we’ll skip over that. What we are going to talk about is your roles in helping me secure victory. The CEO begins to pace in front of the screen. Tyler: You’ve all consented to helping me win in return for a gift of some sort, whether it is money, booking favors, what have you. The gifts will be weighted, of course, with the more lucrative gift going to the person or persons that help me out the most. I expect the competition to be fierce amongst you, as who wouldn’t want to be in the good graces of The King of the Nicaraguan Death Match. I know that moniker pretty much predisposes me to victory in an easier match such as the Dual Halo, but you can never be too prepared. Nelson stops his pacing and leans on the table, propping himself up with clenched fists. He gazes at the people in the chairs with a look that could probably kill, if allowed. Tyler: Keep in mind, in case you didn’t already know, that any treachery will be dealt with in the most uncomfortable and unpleasant manner I can come up with. He sneers as he stands back upright. Tyler: But enough about that. I’ve devised a fool-proof plan that we’ll need to follow which should bring me to victory rather easily. Each of you plays an integral part in this plan, so you if you need to take notes or something do so. The CEO proceeds to unfurl his master plan to the group as we fade out. You didn’t think you were going to hear the plan, did you?
