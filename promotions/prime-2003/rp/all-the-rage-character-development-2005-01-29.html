Date: 01/29/05 [DISCLAIMER: The following is the roleplay I used in the PTC Global Title Division Contener's match and is not to be counted for any kind of credit whatsoever, and is for the sole purpose of character development. Note, the failed html components are a result of the PTC forums having distinctly different settings...combined with the element that I'M TOO LAZY to do cool shit with the font like this with the rest of it...by changing the format...which, in actuality, would only take ten minutes. Sounds kind of fancy, huh?] It’s been five glorious months since Emilio Rage first stepped foot into a PTC building. Specifically, the blooming federation of PRIME... An institution that prided itself on being consistently on the better-half of the ratings list. At that point in time, however, in the days before Emilio came in, PRIME was going through a gargantuan slump. Former Global Champion, Rock Startling, was gone, as was Ian English. And not a moment sooner had Emilio set his boots into the ring, when none other than “The Specialist,” Tony Rolo, went on a rampage, taking apart Boda at the end of ReVolution, thus seemingly ending both of their careers. To make matters worse, Vampir Nosferatu had just entered the realm. This twisted soul brought with him the dangerous weapon of a lifetime of experience in destructive, crippling force, and as we’d soon see, the guns to back it up... He came with a focused, achievable goal, and a precision plan, laden with uncompromising hate. His goal: Destroy PRIME. Plain and simple. But before the fed could go down for the proverbial count, hope arrived in the form of a revived title division. At the end of last September, with the waning hope of PRIMEates around the world on the line, in came the Alias Title Division. More specifically, the highly-motivated, quick-hitting, and ratings-busting Alias Trio. This elite group was realized in the form of the great Dani Furher, Ozric Mortimer, and of course, future champion, Emilio Rage. Led by the homosexually-inclined Philip Everest, the division took precedence in the ReVolutions leading up to [I]The Great American Nightmare[/I], in a round-robin, PRIME-exclusive, five-man tournament. The ratings immediately ripped through the proverbial roof. After quickly tearing through the ranks, Emilio was able to dominate the tournament, going 3-1 and leading the division in points. All this, even while burdened by PRIME’s then-illegitimate, now-defunct co-owner in Toshiaki Motoki, and his cunning sidekick, Hetsu Onimushu. Rage was felled only once, and by the mammoth figure of PRIME’s resident clown-psychopath, Ozric Mortimer. Of course, only [I]after[/I] completing another match mere minutes before, in which Emilio defeated Cimmerian, knocking the kid from the tournament. It all cumulated in a battle of epic proportions at [I]The Great American Nightmare[/I]. By the end of the night -an historical night where Boda and Vampir Nosferatu would literally hover on the brink of death, driving each other toward oblivion, and where the legendary, victorious Black Angel would drive Munson from PRIME forever- a new champion was crowned. A “Ladder to Heaven” match was initiated by the division head himself, Mr. Everest. It included two forty-foot, giant pink ladders, tools by which the competitors had ro reach the titles tied forty-feet above their heads. In one of better PRIME matches in recent history, Mr. Everest set back in the comforts of his (gay-looking) chair as Ozric Mortimer speared Dani Furher forty feet down to the center of the squared-circle. And, well, Emilio Rage remained standing. He would soon reach up and take down the coveted title, and raise his hand in victory, crowned the first ever PRIMEal Rage Champion. The Alias Title division, a division in which the title holder gets to name the belt whatever he so desires, has henceforth risen to prestige paralleled by even the 5-Star and Intense Titles, once considered the most elite. And the PRIMEal Rage Championship, temporary as the name will be, will live on in the hearts and minds of PRIMEates around the world. Forever. This is all despite the fact that weeks before the Nightmare, Rage was bestowed the honor of being the number-one ranked wrestler in all of PrimeTime Central...an award that has now paid off...Possibly in gold. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ [B]Boston Commons. January 29, 5:46 PM[/B] “Now before we all start celebrating,” Emilio began as he peered into the audience, a living wave of brightly colored, smiling faces, “realize that I have my work cut out for me...” As his voice trailed slowly off, the level of excitement dropped suddenly, causing Emilio to momentarily bite his bottom lip... “You can do it Emilio! Rip their f***in’ heads off!” yelled out an excited, albeit drunkenly, man in a failed impersonation of Rob Schneider. The crowd popped in cheers, breaking the solemn ice as Emilio turned to raise a brow at Sullivan Sawyer. The chief of security, noteworthy for his fine work at ReVolution, was paid by PRIME to be the Alias Champ’s personal guard for his PTC bout. Sawyer could only chuckle at Emilio’s confused look though, the Nicaraguan not well versed in Wester pop culture. “Yes...Anyway,” Emilio began, clearing his throat, “I’ve got a long, long road ahead of me. I have three very worthy opponents in my contendership match-up, each of whom are considered to be justly ranked among the PTC elite.” He continued on in a respectful tone, although the foreground reeked of booing fans. “Mal Somers, the WWA 2004 Co-Wrestler of the Year. Justin Evers, an in-ring veteran and current Great Lakes Champion. And of course the charismatic, flamboyant, and in my opinion, very beatable...” Rage couldn’t help but cut a slight smile as the crowd popped, before finishing with, “...Nick Worrell...one of the hottest young men in not only CORE, but the greater PTC community as well.” He paused for a round of scattered boos, although many held Worrell in high regards as Emilio did, as a monumental competitor. “I have a full week’s worth of training to cram into several days. In PRIME, I have the Tag Team Tourney to worry about, and let us not forget that certain, malicious, hate-filled, go-to-momma-cryin’ group, led by Vampir Nosferatu...” Sullivan Sawyer had to step forward and subdue the crowd with a calming gesture as ill-intent chants began to echo forth from the rambunctious crowd, before Emilio added, angrily, “...the Dark Age...” The Rager stepped back and enthusiastically rubbed his rough mits together in the “let’s get to work” gesture, before stepping back up to the podium where he breathed heavily into the mic. The crowd slowly settled in. “Yes, as big as my dreams in PTC may be...all aspirations aside, do not believe for one moment, for one second in that eternal clock that ticks “life” in my heart, that I’ll forget who made it possible for me to be here...or who [I]almost[/I] made it so that I [I]wouldn’t[/I] be...” “Vampir...Furher...First Blood....and anyone both within or without the roster of PRIME, who thinks they can defy common decency and undermine our brand of wrestling...mark my words, that this is far, far from over! Those who threaten PRIME, or any of its innocent wrestlers therein, will be subject to the supreme punishment of MY law...at MY discretion! And just because I’m abroad to defend the honor of PRIMEates in the greater PTC community, doesn’t mean my heart does not lie at home where surely, after I have successfully completed my mission, I’ll be back to tie up lose ends...and tie knots in the throat of any man, or being, who stand in my way...” As his words built up to a crescendo, the roar of the crowd almost deafening, he took a last breath and added, “ Thank you, God bless, and goodnight!” The local news cameras, set up in various strategic places around perimeter, focused in on the wide grin of the PRIMEal Rage Champion as the Boston crowd erupted in cheers, tossing confetti left and right. Sullivan Sawyer stepped up to Emilio Rage with the look of a believer on his face. He extended his hand to one of the peacemakers in PRIME’s locker room -a notable trait, which made his job as chief of security all the easier- and slowly shook his hand. “You’re a good man Rage,” began Sawyer, as he peered directly into the Alias Champion’s large, brown eyes, catching the spark of determination which, for half a year, the rest of the PRIME roster has experienced, but not yet the whole world... “Just remember, this may be the beginning of PRIME’s first chance to bring back Global gold...but at our present rate, certainly not the last.” Emilio smiled as Sawyer nodded his head. “So don’t go overdoing it...But nonetheless, stand tall, sail true, and kid...bring one home for the boys!” Sullivan stepped back and clapped for the Alias Champ as the crowd burst into a another round of applause, sparked by the catalyst of Emilio’s theme music, as the overture of Bad Religion’s “The Empire Strikes First” began to carry its dark melody over the sound system. Before the rush of reporters could hone in on the poor soul, Sawyer pulled Emilio aside. “And come on, kid...a good buddy of Hin See has cordially invited you to a pre-celebration dinner,” said Sawyer in a matter-of-fact tone.. Emilio looked back, slightly surprised, before grunting in satisfaction as the bulky, bald figure of the security chief led Emilio backstage, leaving the buzzing of the crowd behind... ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ [B]Somewhere in Boston. 7:38 P.M.[/B] The press conference was set up by the local [I]Associated Press[/I] affiliate, directed by the producers of Playboy TV. Besides putting on a good public image, PRIME was proud to launch off the successful PTC career of one of its favorite sons with a proper going-away-party...or so it appeared. After an intense half hour of interviews and questioning from multi-media journalists from around the United States, Emilio was to leave with a few brief words to the crowd before heading out to a secret dinner, at a secret location, via an impressive limousine paid for out of the pockets of an unknown source...but it seemed, after the interview, probably related to Hin See... Hin See, of course, was the 45% owner of PRIME, sharing his stock in the industry with a certain feline by the name of Mr. Bojangles, the cat of former wrestling legend/promoter, Cecil Cassidy. (Yeah, it’s a long story). After Hin See’s son, Taun Pham, was tragically wounded at a PRIME event last year, consequently driving him into a coma from which he is yet to awaken, Toshiaki Motoki took control. Motoki, then assistant to Hin See, then took the reigns. Although it was later found that his contract was illegitimate after certain clauses had been slipped into the legal document, defacing his claim of control, Motoki continued to govern. Regardless of this legal loophole, and irrelevant to the fact that the overwhelming majority of the locker loathed the cunning Japanese businessman, Hin See was scheduled on the momentous edition of ReVolution 50 to sign over control of the company to Toshiaki Motoki...permanently. But as is often the case in this tumultuous promotion, things didn’t work out as they should have. Hell erupted in Canada that night, with the Dark Age, and the regiments of what was to become the Enlightenment -Boda, Paul Cain, Black Angel, and of course, Emilio Rage-, engaging in a roster-wide fight, shredding the legal paperwork in the ensuing chaos. On ReVolution 52, Hin See finally, after much deliberation, ended Motoki’s reign of terror once and for all, by embarrassingly firing him in front of the entire locker room...and stepped back to witness the following brawl. A brawl in which the locker would tear the once-dictator to shreds...starting with a sucker punch from your’s truly, Emilio Rage. So as Emilio quietly sat back in the leather seat of the limo, peering out onto the bright lights of the Boston skyline, he couldn’t help but grin, remembering the fuzzy feeling in the pit of his stomach as he popped the defenseless weasel... “So, ya hungry?” asked Sawyer, who set idly by, taping his bored fingers on the seat in front of him. “Yes...Do you know when we’ll arrive?” The PRIME chief of security shrugged. “I suspect very shortly. I mean, the guy made it sound like it’d be a pretty quick deal...” Emilio turned in his seat to face Sawyer, his heart sinking deep into his chest. “You mean it wasn’t Hin See himself?” “Oh no...he said he was one of his...advisors...” Emilio squinted hard, trying not to be impolite. “I know See’s intern pretty well, but that poor boy’s out on promo duty for the upcoming Tag Tourney...are you sure you’re not mistaken?” “Mistaken? Ha! If it really wasn’t Hin See, then how do you explain this limo?” As nervous as Emilio felt, he fell back against his seat, sliding down, and let out a deep, heavy sigh. “Yes, I suppose you’re right...” Sawyer just grunted back, turning to look out his window. As good a month as Emilio was having, something was just not quite right. Something he just couldn’t put his finger on... Oh sure, there was always the threat from the Agents. The kid-napping, drug-selling thieves, hired by his father to return Emilio to his home country. But their encounters had been few and far between the last several months, and Emilio had a feeling that after the recent altercations in Japan, they’d show a bit more respect and temperance...for the time being, at least. But with all that’s been going through his mind lately -engaging him in at every mental front, in thought and in dreams at night- it was hard for him to dig up all the monsters from his past, and peak into every corner where a demon may be sneaking up on him. Between wrestling here, wrestling there, facing evil, and going after PTC gold, time for reflection was on the back-burner. And so as they continued to drive down the streets, destination unknown, Emilio felt a sharp pang in his gut, as if there was something -something important- that he had overlooked... “Are you alight?” asked Sawyer, concerned. Emilio quickly snapped out of it. “Yes, Yes...Thank you, I’m fine.” But Sawyer reached over and put a heavy hand on Emilio’s shoulder. “You sure?” Emilio store back at the big man, dropping his head. “Yeah...” Suddenly, Emilio whipped his head up at the sound of a new voice. As the long black limo began to slow down, pulling up gently to a curb, the driver’s protective window rolled down. A heavyset, dark-skinned man peered back at Emilio, giving him a wink. “I assure you Mr. Rage, I’ve been driving for Hin See and his associates for many years...And although he’s been unreachable for mush of this afternoon, and it hasn’t been officially cleared, I had the front office run a background check...Apparently this guy has written checks to our company before, and seems quite friendly.” Emilio forced a small smile to come over his face, but remained internally unsure. The driver nodded his head politely before tipping his cap. “You should be in for quite a surprise.” A moment later, Rage stepped out of the car as the friendly driver graciously stepped back, holding the door open. Next, Sullivan Sawyer exited the vehicle, quickly adjusting the tie on his make-shift suite. He nodded toward Emilio, motioning toward a small Italian restaurant. “Feelin’ better?” “ I guess,” said Rage, furrowing his brow at the site of the elaborate restaurant. “It’s probably one of Hin See’s buddies here to give you a pep-talk...Who knows, it could be Boda, or any of the boys...a kickoff party!” Emilio smirks at the excited security chief. “Worse case scenario, the paparazzi.” “Of course...You’re right, of course.” But as Emilio stepped up onto the sidewalk, nodding confidently toward the driver as he stepped back in the car, his heart sunk deep in his chest... “Shit!” Emilio cursed under his breath, his eyes gone wide. A waiter, dressed in fine attire, bowed gently as a tall, light-skinned man edged his way past him and in through the doorway, flicking his long black ponytail... “Get!” Emilio took Sawyer by the arm and quickly shoved him back into the car. The driver looked back, confused... “Sir?” But Emilio glared back as he jumped over the hood and scrambled to the other side of the car. He quickly opened the passenger’s side door and jumped in. Rage reached out and tightly squeezed the driver’s wrist, peering into his confused, upset eyes. “Start the car...” demanded the champ. “Sir?” repeated the driver, smoothly, although quickly turning the key. He pulled out into the street frantically, shifting gears as Emilio tightened his grip... “Emilio...” called out Sawyer, upset in the backseat. But ignoring his friend, Emilio turned to the driver. “Are [I]you[/I] in on it?” “In on [I]what[/I]?” protested the driver, trying to remain as calm as possible. But Emilio bit his bottom look, snorting as he noticed a bead of sweat form on the frightened driver’s face. “Good.” Emilio said as he released the grip. The driver swallowed hard, focusing his eyes back on the road. “Pull in here.” “But...” “Pull in here!” Emilio demanded with a tone of urgency. Obediently, the driver drove into the small, empty parking lot... Emilio, knowing by the poor man’s look of utter confusion, could be trusted, gave him a reassuring pat on the back. He turned back to Sawyer and said. “Wait here. I’m back in twelve.” “Twelve? But...” “Twelve minutes! Yes...Just trust me.” And before either of Hin See’s entrusted employees could protest, Rage had already hopped out of the car, and quickly made his way up the street toward the restaurant from whence they’d just made their exodus... “Heh, what’s his deal?” asked the driver, more angered than concerned. Sawyer, knowing Rage pretty well, turned to give the man a scornful look. “I don’t know...But he’s a man of his word. If he says he’ll be back in a few minutes, he’ll be back in a few minutes...But...Well...” Sawyer sighed, letting the depressing thought drift out of his mind as one of PRIME’s finest drifted out of sight... [B]Moments Later[/B] “Big Ben,” Emilio whispered in a crisp, angered voice. With his fists balled up in tight, scorching balls, he quickly made his way up the street, cautiously ducking behind parked cars as he slowly crept up on the cryptic scene... Yes. Big Ben. The leader of one of Japan’s premiere cocaine dealers, right here in Emilio’s own city. It could only mean he had unfinished business with Rage, and the thought of what that business might [I]be[/I] sent shivers down the spine of the Alias Champ. With a Global Title Division match just around the corner, the last thing he needed was another gang war, or another involvement with the sinister, clandestine sect. Once before had they met. And once was enough when it came to these particular men. Emilio thought he had dealt properly with them before [I]The Great American Nightmare[/I], back in Japan where Onimishu -Toshiaki Motoki’s financier- and company had gunned them down, or at least disrupted their cell... But as luck would have it, he was wrong. And indeed, Emilio realized as he rounded the corner, squinting his eyes to make out the various familiar faces of the cartel, his past had yet again come back to haunt him... And at such a precious moment. At a moment in his life when things couldn’t possibly get any more complicated. It appeared, however, they had. The intrinsic nature of the PRIMEal Rage Champ had always been marked with the shade of black luck, but never on such a level as tonight... Cooling his mind, unwilling to risk his physical being with a rash move or wrong word, Emilio forced himself to remain in place, commanding his feet to stay put. They did. Cementing himself to the sidewalk, Emilio began to meditate, grounding himself. Moments later, a small grin swept over his darkened face, as he took in a deep breath, mind drifting off to thoughts of future glory and PTC Championships... Rage had heard from an interesting source a bit of dirt on WMW’s own Justin Evers, and some fascinating recent experiences. It appeared that while Justin Evers may be “The Heat,” Rage suspected it was merely from all the hot air in his Texan head. With his ‘69 Corvette and his odd friend in AWS Man -ninja fighting, nonetheless!- it makes one wonder if PTC wants a number one contender’s match, or an annual Psycho Bowl. Evers may have been the Great Lakes Champion, Emilio thought as he leaned up safely against a cement wall, but he’d be safer cutting an ice-fishing hole in any of the said Great Lakes, and diving into their depths at a heart-warming twenty degrees, than stepping into the ring with Rage... Besides, as big a load that Emilio had on his mind, he couldn’t imagine tossing such a load on his friend than Evers did when he tossed his cookies on AWS Man. Nor could, quite frankly, getting his ass kicked by a mere civilian with a minute case of road rage be a good omen of things to come for the WMW competitor. Cripes. Also, Mal Somers came out of 2004 as the WWA co-wrestler of the year...tied with his mortal enemy, Dave Harley...Pathetic? Some would say... I mean, think of Boda/Nosferatu... “Loveliest Couple, 2004,” could you imagine. Thought so.. Combine that with the fact that he’s a murder victim in the case of his own parents, and you’ve got one sick puppy. And the icing on the cake is his undying love for a [I]title[/I] -yes, we’re talking a strap- which would leave one to two notable conclusions. First, if he is ever convicted of he murder....with “Rookie” by his side, he could easily plead a fair case for insanity. Secondly, he has no business in being in this PTC match up... And what of the infamous Nick Worrell, one of CORE’s best and one if the fastest rising stars in Prime Time Central? As much respect as Emilio had for this man, one has to question his maturity. When you’re a premier pro-wrestling superstar and your father still has to baby you, there’s something wrong. When your love of the game is matched only by your love of fast cars, there is inherently something lacking in the field of dedication. But most importantly, if you’re a young, hot-shot athlete...you better back it up with a big bear of boots... Did Emilio believe Mr. Worrell can do this? Certainly. He’s proved himself time and time again. In the world of CORE, there’s no doubt. But shall the Little Prince be able to hang with the Big Dragons and go toe-to-toe with PTC’s best? Possibly...against two of them. But Rage has been there, done that, and the meal of victory is best served with a side of broken promise... Besides, is it possible for the mammoth, healthy figure of Emilio Rage to lose to a smaller, beat-up kid with brain damage and over two-hundred lifetime stitches? Nay. CORE’s 2004 “Surprise Wrestler of the Year” should be in for one hell of a surprise. As three of the other hottest rising stars in PTC come to step into the squared-circle with the PRIMEal Rage Champion, chew on this: If you play with fire, you’re gonna get burned. And if you play with Rage, you’re going to get killed. But the smile was soon torn off Emilio’s reflective face as he snapped out of his trance, peering down the street. Thoughts of future glory drifted humbly away as the seriousness of the moment came into being. “What’s this?” Emilio locked his eyes onto the unfamiliar set of new faces, two Americans, dressed in black business suites. But as Emilio began to wonder what the implications of these odd new figures might be, the hair stood up on his back, and he jumped back in fright, turning as a strange man latched onto his shoulder with a solid, heavy grip... “Psst...Hey!” the voice whispered as Rage whipped around. “Shh!” The man raised his finger to his lips, signaling for Rage to be quiet. For a moment, Emilio simply leaned back against the wall, pausing as his heart pumped poignantly in his chest. “Are you okay?” “Yeah...” Emilio shook his head as the adrenaline began to subside. He turned and faced the man, slowly looking him over. Emilio winced as he saw a bulge under the man’s shirt next to his belt. A holster. The man looked down, following Emilio’s gaze. He sighed. “Don’t worry, I’m friendly.” “That’s nice,” Emilio shot back, clenching a wary fist behind his back. The man, sensing the PRIMEr’s aggression, took a solid step back, acknowledging Rage’s territorial dominance. Emilio began to feel the tension subside, but then... “Boston P.D. I’m here to...” “What!?” Emilio yelled, cutting him short. The man’s eyes went wide as he reached over and clamped Emilio’s mouth, warranting a huge shove from the six-foot-seven wrestler. This rash move sent the cop back against the wall. “Shh!” The cop said, regaining his balance. He pointed back toward the street, reminding Emilio of the danger at hand. Emilio folded his arms firmly across his chest, waiting to see what this rat had to say... “Don’t worry. I don’t work for them...I’m a local...” Ne nodded in the motion of the restaurant, causing Rage to lean around the corner and give a quick peak. “You mean?” “Yeah,” whispered the cop, wiping something out of his eye, “they’re C.I.A.” “Thought so,” Emilio began, his chin quivering in anger. “But don’t worry, they’re...” Emilio cut him short, lifting the tall man off from his feet, and pressing him up against the wall like a rag-doll. “Don’t worry this...Don’t worry that...Pfft. Don’t worry,” Rage threatened, locking in the choke hold, “it’s only your neck...” But the man, with surprising speed and velocity, kicked out, catching Rage under the ribs. As the Alias Champ fell to his knees, gasping for air, the cop pulled out his gun and pointed it directly at the center of Rage’s head. “Don’t worry...the safety’s not off yet.” After catching his breath, Emilio couldn’t help but look up at the nifty cop with a bare grin, flipping him off. The cop smiled, and slowly shook his head, adding, “But it will be...if you don’t talk.” “Talk?” said Emilio, cracking his neck to one side before getting to his feet. All the while the cop kept his gun aimed steadily on Emilio’s head as the wrestler held up his arms, defensively. “It looks like the time for talk is over.” “Oh, it’s only just began.” “Oh, really?” Emilio retorted. “Then why...” “Shut up!” The cop moved in closely, gun still trained on Rage. “Do you want to know why your life’s in danger or not?” Emilio looked up, rolling his eyes in a manner that suggested “well...that’d be kind of nice...” The cop chuckled. Emilio leaned back against the brick wall, shaking his head. “First off, mind telling me how you got here?” Emilio looked up, surprised. “How I got here...well isn’t it obvious? A set-up! I mean, you, the C.I.A., the boys from Japan...Although,” Emilio began, rubbing his chin thoughtfully, “I see no reason for...” The cop cut him short. “Just as I suspected.” “What’s that?” “The set-up...I knew the feds wanted you in, but certainly not this bad...” His voice trailed off as a troubled look came over his wrinkled face. “W...wait a minute! Wait a minute, wait a minute, wait a minute!” Emilio got to his feet and held his arms out in front of him, motioning toward the cop. “You mean you had nothing to do with it?” “Me!?” the cop started, holding his arms up defensively. “I’m just after the truth.” “The truth?” “Yeah,” replied the cop, as a newly formed frown crept up on his solemn face. “Which leads me to my next question.” Rage store back, blankly. “You mind telling me why the fuck they’re using the mafia to lure you in?” “Well....I...I...Pfft! How the fuck would I know, mi amigo! You think I planned on getting trapped tonight. I mean, all I did was respond to a request my limo driver...got...some....uh...” Emilio paused to rack his brains for the words, “...some...uh...Mr. Johnson who wanted a dinner date...Thought it had something to do with my upcoming match.” “In...” the cop looked down, snapping his fingers until it came back to him. “In..P...PTC?” “Yeah. I’m a professional wrestler.” “I know that much, asshole!” the cop shot back, glaring at Emilio. “What I’m wondering is why the feds would be using such a volatile character in Big Ben to lure you out...Unless...” “Look, I don’t know who the fuck you are, or what you want from me. But I can tell you this....Whatever it is they’re accusing me of, I’m innocent! Okay? I...” Again, the officer cut him short. “Innocent? Innocent as in three counts of felony, six misdemeanors?” Emilio swallows hard, everting his eyes. “Crossing the border without receiving a proper visa....” “But...” “Receiving a proper visa...in the form of a FAKE drivers licence, bank account, social security fund, and birth certificate! ...Oh, and carrying a concealed weapon, assault and battery...times two, giving aid to international terrorists...” Emilio shook his head. “Whoa! Whoa! Whoa! Don’t think for one moment that...” “SHALL I continue?” Of little psychological will to argue, Emilio shook his head. “Good. Then we have an understanding.” “Understanding? You mean you get to stand here and play asshole, while I get to be taken to jail, and you make the front cover of the [I]Globe[/I] for getting who the feds couldn’t!” But the cop looked down at Emilio’s trembling hands and let out a sigh. “No...I mean, the thought did cross my mind, but it’s a bit more...complicated.” “Grr!...” “Get angry all you want, but just remember, son, that if it wasn’t for me, you’d be in the back of a C.I.A. car now, awaiting trial for federal prison!” “Then why,” asked Emilio, stepping up to the cocky young officer, “aren’t I in jail yet?” “Because I know your story...” Emilio looked up at the cop, biting his bottom lip. “The whole story...” “Ah,” said Emilio, trying to hold back laughter, humored by the fact that not even he knew the ‘whole story’, “which is?...” “Well...I’m working on it. But what I do know is this: You’re an ex-guerilla fighter from Nicaragua, who...unfortunately...has ties to the Communist Party. You’ve escaped the underground life though, but at the cost of personal security, risking your neck in public, wrestling in the US, with your father -who just happens to be the leader of one of the world’s most powerful drug cartels!- and his agents constantly on your back, and...” “Step father!...” “Yeah...Whatever. And you’re also a professional wrestler now, ten years of experience on both the Japanese and Mexican circuit. You’re currently involved in a promotion called PRIME, one of the highest-rated subsidiaries on the PTC market, and you’re on your way, soon, to an inter-promotional match.” “Ohh...” Emilio began, in as sarcastic a voice he could muster. “And,” began the cop again, “as I mentioned before, a criminal who broke international law, escaped into the U.S. without proper clearance, currently wanted on minor charges by the feds...who’re too reluctant to take you out publically, but seem to want you now because they think you know something about the murdering of a U.S. field agent in...” Emilio’s eyes went wide. He shook his head. “Wait a minute...” “I thought so,” said the cop, as a smile came across his face. “Yeah...I was trained at the School of America’s, class of ‘92....and yeah, I did take up arms against the Contras...and if one of the pens I shoved through the eyes of those guys as they attempted to burn down the village of my people, just so happened to be C.I.A....yeah, then maybe...” Emilio paused as his face reddened, the blood pouring into the veins of his dark face, tears forming in the corner of his eyes. “But I’m telling you, I’ve been clean for almost eleven years now, and the only time I caused trouble...” “Was when you had to.” “Exactly.” A soft moment of silence came about as the cop paused to think on what he just heard, his senses telling him to go on intuition, to go against the orders of the chief and trust this man standing in front of him... But the tension in the air was high, and as cliche as it sounds, thick enough to cut with a knife... On this night, it was cut by a bullet... “Look out!” The cop courageously ran forward, and with amazing strength, threw Emilio to pavement in the alleyway, just as the sound of a gun rattled off. Emilio covered his head in protection as the officer whipped out his pistol and turned. He dodged another spray of bullets as they bounced off the wall, shattering bricks and causing thick particles to rip into the air. Emilio began to cough. But the flame of the assailant’s gun, as he shot out with a (luckily) unsteady hand, was seen, and the cop turned and fired in the general direction...but his gun just clicked as the man drew in range... “Shit!” He had forgot to click it off safety... It was too late. Emilio got to his feet and turned to run, but logic held its place over his fears, as he knew the man would only gun him down like a dog if he tried to run, the tall figure approaching quickly from the alleyway... “Heh.” the man grunted, raising his gun to fire. Emilio blinked his eyes, waiting for the bullet to enter his skull... ...It never came. A shot rang out and the gunmen fell dead. The cool figure of another, quicker man came up behind him. “Starr!” shouted the new character. “Michaels!” The officer shouted back, quickly retrieving his gun. As the other man approached, Officer Starr (or so it appeared) turned to give Emilio a nod. “Emilio, my partner, Officer Michaels...Michaels, Rage.” Emilio rolled his eyes, angered by another wasted shot of adrenaline that would surely come in use someday when his life was in real danger... Regardless, he smiled and extended a hand to meet that of the officer’s. They exchanged a quick handshake. Michaels then stood back with a look of concern dawning his pale face. “I’ll get a med team back to evac in a few...But we’ve got to get moving, fast.” “What’s up?” questioned Starr, concerned. “They’ve given up on the trap....pretty much know he ain’t gonna show...” he turned, motioning toward Rage. “So they’re packing up...but want precinct 11 to do an area sweep...” “Shit,” said Starr, before asking, “and Big Ben?” Michaels simply shook his head. “Damn it!” Starr angrily kicked the ground with his boot. “So the deal they cut with that asshole murderer is so damn sweet that they gonna let him go, huh?” “Guess it was just part of the contract...” “Yeah, well fuck the contract!” For a moment, Starr kept back, trying to get control of himself. Finally, he turned toward Emilio and spoke. “Well I guess the gig’s up.” “Oh?” “Yeah...means you better get your ass out of here...quick...” “But...” “Don’t worry about that, I got it under control.” But Emilio store back at the fiery cop, unimpressed. “Trust me...you’re fine. They ain’t gonna grab a public figure like you until they can get you out of the spotlight, somewhere quiet....like tonight. So go back home and get some sleep.” Emilio still looked unsure. “Look,” said Starr, putting a reassuring hand on Emilio’s shoulder, “I know you’re all worried....but you’ve gotta focus on your daily life for right now...Worse comes to worse, I got...well...a contact, and will give you an ample warning if the feds plan to move...Otherwise, stay put. I got a man in the Gov’s office...be sure to push a few buttons, legitimize that little false identity you got there...Emilio...Emilio Ragerio...” Emilio looked back, frowning deeply. “Haven’t heard that name for a long, long time...” “Yeah, well you’d be best to forget it for a while...” “Agreed.” “Now,” said Starr, stepping up to shake Emilio’s hand, “scram!” “Heh. Try as he might, twiddling his thumbs as they made their way down the highway, the city lights at their backs, Emilio couldn’t help but feel a splinter of apprehension. Shrugging off all questions -and then support- from Sawyer and the driver, Emilio had kept to himself. He had a lot on his plate at the moment. An upcoming Tag Team Tournament, a PTC contender’s match, Culture Shock...not to mention the constant threat of defending his Alias Title, being trapped as the crowned warrior of the most bitter, contested division PRIME currently had going. All that, and this... With his very life on the line, his career hanging in the balance, it would be hard for Emilio to focus on his upcoming match. Or some would think. But realize what a life he has had, the things he’s accomplished while under great pressure, and what he’s been through... He’s lived in five different countries, spoken three languages, and dodged countless bullets over the thirty-four years of his ungodly existence. His mother, his father...his entire family and village, were burned before his very eyes as a young, helpless boy. And what had the world given him in return? Nothing. And to make nothing into countless opportunities, defying mathematical odds on the Einsteinian level, winning titles left in right in an environment where even the very owner of which (Motoki) said he couldn’t possibly survive, takes a wizard of supreme talent. But it ain’t magic. It’s will. The will to win, the will to succeed, the will to simply wake up every day and say, without reservation, “Dear God, thank you for allowing me another day in this cruel, miserable world without either hope or opportunity...for out of this miserable pit I will carve victory out of mountains of defeat, rip through the ebbing ocean tide, and tear down fences where they defy me...So thank you for Your Supreme help. But fuck you. I’ll survive.” ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The arena lights flashed on, burning his tired eyes...“Introducing last, from the unchartered territory of PRIME, weighing in at ‘underdog’...”
