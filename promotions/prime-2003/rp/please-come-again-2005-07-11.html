Please Come Again

11 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/11/2005 “Frequently in error, never in doubt” Fade in from black, to more black. Where we’re at is but a mystery, were we’ve been is simply history, but where we’re going is most important. What we’re doing now is staring into darkness, unlike the “new age” would lead you to believe, not every promo is a self-serving soap opera or slice of life, boring as it may be, some promos are simply meant to antagonize and demean. The voice you just heard was a familiar voice, a voice recognized with PRIME, a voice that is the “Apparatus” some would say of the PRIME, the voice of Tyler Lopez. I got that quote from a college professor in an old law class. It was applied to most lawyers then, but considering my opponent it fits well. In common terms it simply means, constantly speaking as if you know what you’re talking about, but constantly being wrong, and not really caring. I’d say that’s summed up my experience for the week. The sound of hard sole shoes walking on a hard floor can be heard, “The Apparatus” is moving for those who couldn’t figure it out. The funny thing about PRIME is even the oldest of veterans can learn from the strangest of sources, and I’ve found myself learning or at least agreeing with none other than a Peter Vetra. Vetra as the buzz world would have us call him, is not necessarily a man after my own heart, but on some points people should listen to him, he makes some valid some statements, few statements that I can concur with. You see Paul, or Mr. Cain, whatever your warped sense of self-likes to be called. The reason you hear the same old arguments from me, week after week and year after year, is because idiots who face me week after week, year after year, think you’re the first ones to invent the wheel. Then when I shoot you down and prove them wrong, they decide to avoid the issue all together. I say the same things, because you can’t disprove them, as displayed to you, I operate in Facts while you operate in Myths. More walking. For instance, you have the myth that all I’m going to bring up past accomplishments, when the fact is you’re the first one to do it to try and validate your self proclaimed superstar status. I mean honestly, you saying you’re the greatest is great, but what facts do you have to back it up? You’re a Alias competitor, well my lord, stop the presses, and there are NO other Alias Title contenders in PRIME. Lopez’s voice echoes a chuckle. Of course your claim is made that much more valid because you’ve wrestled the newly crowned Alias Champion, and god knows he’s accomplished so much as a singles wrestler. You see Paul, what you fail to realize is this isn’t the PRIME Debate team, this is professional wrestling were producing inside the ring is what counts. Now obviously you have been around the likes of Jackson Darilek too long, because even your arguments make no sense. More walking. So maybe you should hold onto the notion that Peter Vetra is something good to have on your resume, he did nothing but “win” the Alias Title, much like you want to “win” our match. You’re not a champion or anything close to it, you’re more like a trophy case, a walking and talking museum, who holds nothing nobody really wants to look at. Pause. Of course you’ll whine and cry about being beaten “fairly” after Revolution, right? Are you fucking kidding me, please tell me the almighty Paul Cain is not talking about fairness? Have you paid attention to the rest of the wrestling world for the last, oh, five to six YEARS? If we started talking about fair and unfair wins and losses, everyone would be undefeated, except for Darilek, he could find a way to lose to a dusty mousepad, but I’ll digress. You’ve walked in and made quite and impact, and as quickly as you done this, you're going to be forgotten. I’m the impact player and the impact taker. You’re not a champion, you just get lucky enough to get title opportunities and lose them just as quickly. Paul Cain expects us to sound the trumpets and lets have him run for president, with his return. Pause. Ask yourself this Paul, who’s really delusional here? A man that with his return got absolutely nothing he wanted accomplished? Or the man whose making one of the biggest impacts PRIME has ever seen? So honestly Paul, whose the crazy one? Lopez flicks on a light so we can assume, as the lights slowly turn on, and as they illuminate the room we view what if we didn’t know any better we’d call the offices that used to be Money, Power, Respect Inc. Headquarters. We’re inside a conference/trophy room, that shows pictures of all the Tyler Lopez's greatest moments, the day the founders, opened the building. Then White Tiger at a press conference holding the a World Title. And many other various artifacts that we won’t go into. On the far wall is a large class case, which holds all the former Title belts replicas of course, but they’ll do. Lopez dressed in a white sleeveless shirt and white slacks, hair laying down and the same style from the other day. Lopez walks up onto the glass case and smiles. Welcome to the former Headquarters of Money, Power, Respect Inc., none of you has probably been in this room, This room is the Championship Room. All the greatest members of the coalition that is MPR, and greatest wrestlers, are archived in this room, now no this isn’t some lame history lesson, I know you hate to learn Paul, god forbid you soak in any new information. I’m here personally for nostalgic reasons, and I just decided to bring a camera crew with me. I figured since wrestling will be the closest to greatness you ever get, I’d show you what actually being something in this sport means. Because being a name in this sport, a name that’ll never die is something that’s obviously escaped Peter Vetra, as he doesn’t even know the basics of the sport. For instance what’s called a “gimmick”. I was named “White Tiger” you fucking cum missile, because it was my gimmick, I was a rookie and allowed myself to be gimmicked. Upon becoming one of the greatest, I then named myself and I’ve been the “Apparatus” ever sense. Now even though I find it beneath me to argue such juvenile and lame facts with someone of your non-importance, I just couldn’t go without acknowledging your worthless presence. Because honestly Jackson, if I'm like the Movie “Pearl Harbor”, then that must make you like straight to video version of “Meet Joe Dirt”. In the Movie Theatres on Friday, in the video store by Saturday Night. Nobody went to see you, only idiots find you entertaining, and in the end you end up on the shelf where you belong, so please, as the bonafide and undeniable weak link, do us all a favor and go back to getting punked out like a three dollar whore, you’re just the weakest link…Goodbye! Lopez smirks. You see the fact is, Paul Cain is fighting a battle he can’t win. Because honestly, you have everything to lose, and nothing at all to gain. Unlike me, who’re entrenched in WRESTLING history. When I win, my legacy isn't in the least effected; you’re just another name on my “McDonalds resume”. And to be honest Paul, as you can tell, my resume has a lot more important notes on it than yours, which reads… ”Sexually touched by my daddy, sexually touched by my sister, Peter Vetra's bitch and sexually touched by him too, dumped into shit, PRIME Bitch because my big sissy says so”. So with that being known, who really needs to defeat whom? You can’t bring up myths and contradict yourself at every turn, and think you’re the greatest kid, it doesn’t work that way. Smirk. And why does it matter? Will you finally listen to our reasoning solve world hunger? Will it finally rid you of that god-awful genital wart on your lips? Will it bring you one step closer to; oh I don’t know, actually winning a title? What exactly will it do Paul? Nothing, it’ll do nothing, which about sums up your entire career up to this point, nothing. Why don’t you do us all a favor and take a note, and remain quiet, because obviously you're not smart enough to see this is a battle for the big boys, and contrary to what you tell yourself, that’s something you’re not. I'm the closest to greatness and prestige you’ll ever get to without having to pay the price for admission. So enjoy your time in the sun, because your tour is going to come to an abrupt end. I'm the Dynasty, and I'm the one making things happen. Pride smirks. But don’t fear, losing to me isn’t your right, it’s a goddamned privilege! So Paully boy, shake up your etch-a-sketch, and head back to your study notes, because the learning, just like your meaningless careers, has truly just begun. Thanks for playing please come again. Smirk. Lopez turns out the lights and we slowly fade to black.
