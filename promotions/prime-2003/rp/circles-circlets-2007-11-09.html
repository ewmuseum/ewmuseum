Date: 11-09-2007 "Why are you coming back to PRIME?" The woman seated across from Easton was pretty, deep tan, Hispanic, her black hair curled around her face because she was a new-age feminist, not a bull dyke, and he noticed that her legs were shaven below the half-thigh skirt she wore. How the fuck they could swing women into this fucking business, Easton would never know. He appreciated it, though. The sunset behind her set her skin on fire and he watched the sun for a bit, sitting placidly, his hands folded on the table. Their food hadn't come yet. Easton shrugged his shoulders and settled back into the wicker chair, spreading his feet square to his hips. He reminded himself he was married when his eyes dipped into the curves of her cleavage. He reminded himself he was married when he soaked in her exotic skin. Her smile never wavered. "Is it the money?" She lifted an eyebrow and they spoke to each other in small smirks. Easton's beard was as thick as it ever had been and his hair was a touch longer than when he had last been seen in the wrestling media, a few rebellious strands swaying over his face. His black t-shirt had a darker stain near his hip. "Yeah," Easton laughed. "It's the fucking money." "Don't cuss," she said, tapping the small microphone laid out on the table. "I hate editing." She lifted her eyes when the waiter came by and said "Gracías" as he poured water and ice into her goblet. Easton didn't roll his eyes. He reminded himself he was married. *** Jeanne hated this fucking music. Slim as always, jeans hugging to her hips, off-white t-shirt hugging to her waist, black hair hugging to her shoulders, she slid barefoot through the house, her lips pursed just a bit. Easton had taken the liberty of installing speakers throughout the house and now Sir Lord Baltimore raged everywhere, mid-70s Zeppelin ripoff artists, her toes tapping in the carpet and her arms wound around her stomach. She, the girl from Minnesota, had gotten him into the fucking Wu-Tang Clan, was responsible for his whole rap kick, and the first thing he does when he comes back to the house she's been living in for so long is start pumping through rock and metal and anything but bassy hip-hop. "Easton," she said quietly and he was there behind her, stiff hands on her shoulders, his nose and lips and breath in her hair. She let her eyes slide shut and tried to let herself love this song, leaning back into his chest so he could wind his arms around her shoulders and clutch her close. "Easton, what the fuck are you doing?" Easton grinned in her hair. "Going back to Canada," he said simply. "To work." "You're serious aren't you?" You got me so pumped up! You're feelin' alriiight!1 Easton nodded and pulled his hands back, fingers wrapping against her shoulders again, his lips pressing into her hair. "I am. Gotta do it." "You sure you're not still hurting?" She shifted her head a bit and glanced over at the wall which was as far as she could look back. She could feel his nose rubbing up and down against the back of her hair and let her breath bleed out from her slowly, her body slackening a bit. "Touring Canada?" "They are," Easton said. "It'll be good to see the family." "And Isaac." Easton chuckled into her hair at the mention of his brother. "Fuck Isaac." Jeanne smiled, her eyelids drooping slightly, her hair washing over her face like night climbing over the horizon without the flush of the sky. "Fuck Isaac," she mouthed. "I'm gonna go get something to eat." She brought up her hand slowly and gripped at his bicep, pushing herself back into him, trembling slightly, and he held her, his arms slipping around her waist, his chin and nose and lips at her neck. I treat you like a woman! You treat me like a man! I pump you full of looove, baby! Ohh, I can, now bring me up! Her toes curled into the carpet. *** "Nothing but falsehood needs the assistance of fame and place, of robes and mitres, of tiaras and crowns." -- Col. Robert G. Ingersoll (1833-1899) *** The woman interviewing him was named Deana Ramírez and she didn't have many publications to her name, just out of graduate school and in this stupid fucking business because of her father who she hated. She was not ploying Easton with her soft eyes or her curves so full despite her slimness, with her exotique. Easton was not interested. His eyes were whispering Robert Hanssens. She sipped at her water and waited for Easton to begin on a breadstick before dandling the clear perfect glass in her dark perfect hand. "Injured at Colossus," she said simply. "Lost your championship. Was that embarrassing?" Easton frowned, chewing slowly, missing the cunning on her face in his preoccupation with her breasts. He swallowed deliberately and lifted his face and mirrored Deana's amusement, running his tongue over his teeth but not cleaning them. "Little bit," Easton replied. "But those two guys hated my fu... nngh." He nodded towards the tape recorder and she stifled a laugh with the edge of her hand. "Hated my guts, you know?" "I do." "It was cowardly more than anything." "Do you want your revenge on them?" She met his eyes and her lips were twisted up in a smile but her eyes were stiff and sure. He sighed through his nose, wondering why they always goaded him like this. His teeth gritted behind his lips and he cracked his neck to the side. "I do," Easton said. "Given the chance, I'd step into the ring and I'd snap their necks. I'd break 'em in half. Show them how to really do things. Chainz is a big guy, a big truckin' monster." She flashed concern at him but his eyes were on the recorder. "But he's sloppy. Snow's got credentials, but he's cocky, he's stupid. When I come back, I'll put them out of this game. Not for three months. Not indefinitely. Definitely forever. Show them what it means to mess with me." Deana sipped at her water lightly, watching him. "So you're coming back for revenge." Easton lifted his eyes. "That's taking it too far." *** "Take me with you." She stood in the doorway, dark in the light from behind, stretched across it so he couldn't fail to see her when he looked back from sealing away clothing piece by piece into his dufflebag, but when he looked at her he knew she didn't mean it. "Don't protect me," she pleaded softly, not moving from the doorway, not swaying, the ball of her right foot poised and her heel lifted like the dancer she had never dreamed of being. Easton thought she was beautiful. He zipped up his bag halfway. "This isn't about that," he said, turning to face her but still on his haunches, leaning against the bed they had shared while he had healed, where she had cradled him until he could cradle her. "But I don't want you coming." "You wanted me to come before all this." "I said that," Easton said, nodding. "But don't. Understand." She tilted her head forward and her hair fell over her face and she was stiff like Medusa. "Remember I was with you when you were first a world champion. At your side. Helping you." Easton dropped his eyes a little bit, composing himself before he looked at her again. How to tell her that that her being there was a hindrance, an extra worry, no matter the joy. It was the problem with being gifted and the problem with finally being crowned. She was there, but she could not understand. "I know. This is different, though. This time I have to do it alone. Remember when I lost it." He could see her shiver all over. "Alone on the top. That's what they say." She nodded slightly. "That's what they say." When he was gone, this house would be full of RZA and Ghostface and Method Man but she would not, would not play "All I Need."2 *** Their food had not come. Deana rapped her fingers on the table and her foot bobbed under the table, every so often brushing against Easton's leg, but neither pulled away and neither apologized. He let his eyes meet hers. "See, the fans never meant anything to me," Easton continued, "which is a harsh thing to say, I know, but think about it. You start losing and they lose faith. It always happens. Fall into a slump and they're gone. That's why I've sort of... tried to do my best, done what I can on my terms, and if they like it, fine, and if they don't, well, you know." Deana smiled at the unspoken curse and Easton worked his mouth around the last morsel of greasy breadstick, wiping his hands on an oil-dark napkin. "No fanservice from Easton Hall," she said lightly, "but they loved you so much." "I appreciate it, but I never needed it. Never wanted it. There's a lot of cheese floating around about the power of the fans, which I think is ridiculous, don't you?" She shrugged and he let a snort of laughter float through his nostrils. "I did everything I did on my own. I didn't have a stadium around me when I just started out." "So what is this about?" Deana asked. She glanced up at a passing waiter and then he glanced up, but the waiter didn't stop or even notice them, which made her squeeze her lips and pause. She blinked. "It's not for the fans. It's not revenge. You're entered in the Jewel in the Crown tournament, so I can only guess it's the Universal Title." Easton kicked the idea around in his head a bit, stroking his fingers back through his hair. He leaned forward and placed his elbow on the table, resting his chin on his fist, glancing down at the basket of crumbs. "Yes and no. I want that affirmation, you know, but at the same time, I already know I'm the best the company has. I don't need the title. Just put me up against Nova or Troy or Silver, just once, let me rip 'em to shreds. Let me show you that I'm the best. I don't need the gold." "So why, then?" Easton leaned back into his chair and gripped the arms of it, his eyes never slipping back down to her breasts or to her arms or wishing he could see her hips. Her fingers crept forward on the table and he ignored them. He lifted his jaw importantly, defiantly. "Because I can." ---- 1 The song is "Pumped Up" by Sir Lord Baltimore.
